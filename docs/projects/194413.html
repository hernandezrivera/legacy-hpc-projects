Project Summary

The protection situation in South Sudan has been of a major concern since the start of political instability in 2013. Women and girls have been exposed to gender based violence and the vulnerability has been increased by the effects of COVID 19. Inter communal fighting, lack of referral pathways and lack of awareness on GBV services hinder service delivery of GBV services among the vulnerable and affected populations.
There is increasing concern on the number of increasing cases of gender based violence, sexual exploitation and abuse reported to GBV actors. Forced marriages remains quite high in South Sudan with young girls forced to drop out of school to get married. All this protection concerns leaves the vulnerable and affected populations in dire need of concerted efforts to reduce the protection risks among the vulnerable and affected populations.
TADO will there fore reduce the protection needs and especially cases of gender based violence among the vulnerable and affected population in Tonj East by robust community engagement geared towards prevention of gender based violence, risk communication and rehabilitation services for victims of gender based violence.
This project will ensure that the survivors of gender based violence get a survivor centered psychosocial support geared towards resilience building, empowerment and recovery. This will empower the survivors to improve their well being and fully recover from the trauma and psychological strain caused by gender based violence.
TADO will ensure that there is proper case management of the victims of gender based violence and establishment of robust referral pathways for cases that cannot be managed from the internally. Referrals to medical services, legal services and other case management services will ensure that the victims are supported to heal and improve thus leading to improved well being.
TADO will establish women and girls friendly spaces and train women and girls on key artisan trainings while giving group, individual or specialized psychosocial support to the victims to geared towards full recovery.
Activities geared towards reduction of GBV like training of women and girls on fuel efficient stoves will ensure that girls and women are not exposed to GBV services as they seek for fuel and especially firewood thus reduce the cases of gender based violence in the community.
Distribution of in-kind support for women and girls and especially dignity kits will ensure that girls and women are able to live a dignified life with reduced cases of gender based violence. TADO will also raise awareness on prevention of gender based violence and also ensure that the community is aware of risk behaviors that can increase cases of gender based violence in the community.
Staffs will be trained on community/frontline workers trained on GBV guiding principles and response services like case management, and psychosocial support to equip them with knowledge on provision of gender based violence services in the community.
TADO will also mainstream protection in all her Health, Nutrition, WASH, Emergency Shelter and Nonfood items, Education and Food Security programming to ensure that there are linkages and synergies in their programming and offer integrated services to the vulnerable and affected populations..
The response will have an overall target of 7000 beneficiaries with a disaggregated data of (Girls=2500, Women=2500, Boys=1000, men-1000) in Tonj North, and Tonj South. The project will target 1500 people with disabilities who are also highly vulnerable to protection issues to ensure disability inclusive protection programming.
A community centered-approach will be the focus, and TADO will work closely with the community stakeholders in mobilization, coordination and accountability to ensure that they receive appropriate GBV services.

1 - Needs

Protection needs in south Sudan continue to rise after years of protracted conflict that has traumatized the country compounded by increasing inter and intra communal fighting, massive displacements due to flooding for three years in a row and negative impact of COVID 19 Pandemic.According to Humanitarian needs overview 2022, 80% people are affected by multiple risks of protracted violent conflicts, climate change causing flooding which resulted in adverse food insecurity worsened by COVID-19 pandemic and increasing cost of living among the communities. Studies in South Sudan have shown that over 65% of women have experienced gender based violence at least once in their lifetime.(HPC South Sudan 2022)Tonj East, Tonj South are in IPC level 5 according to IPC analysis in October 2020 IPC report. This severe food insecurity has caused high protection needs as women girls and boys are highly stressed due to lack of food and therefore exposes them to gender based violence and other serious protection concerns as they move in search of food and other essential commodities.Intercommunal fighting and flooding in Tonj East county displaced over 20000 people. According to IRNA conducted in October 2021 in Tonj south County, women and girls face serious protection concerns including GBV due to displacements caused by flooding and intercommunal fighting among conflicting parties in Tonj East. (Tonj South IRNA Oct, 2021)Women and girls due to the conflict were exposed to sexual exploitation and abuse, domestic violence and are traumatized by the displacements. Coupled by these, displaced women and girls continue to bear the brunt of GBV that includes early marriages and unwanted pregnancies. A third of people displaced live in communities where women and girls avoid certain areas due to fear for their safety. Sadly, basic GBV risk mitigation measures around sanitation facilities remain extremely rare. According (UNMISS report 2021), Sexual violence including rape and sexual exploitation and abuse due to conflict Tonj East and South counties trend as major hotspot of conflict-related sexual violence.Referral pathways to the GBV services are lacking and communities are not aware of the available SGBV services. COVID-19 pandemic has worsened the mental health situation in South Sudan. In Tonj East and Tonj South counties are among the counties devastating leading to distressed Women, Boys, girls and men due restricted movement confining men and women at home for prolonged periods of time leading to increased domestic based violence. According to OCHA led multi agency IRNA assessment conducted in Tonj South, serious protection issues were identified after flooding and intercommunal fighting caused displacements in the communities. There were reported cases of gender-based violence, rape and communities were lacking shelter exposing women and girls to rape and other protection concerns including violence to women, girls and boys. The communities lacked awareness on GBV and there were no clear referral pathways to victims of GBV and other protection concerns. (Tonj East and South IRNA report OCT 2020). These GBV gaps needs emergency humanitarian response to ensure that women, girls, men and boys are able to access GBV services, build resilience and recover from multiple shocks of displacements, conflict, flooding and severe food insecurity.

• TADO will carry out service provision of survivor-centered psycho social support focused on healing, empowerment and recovery, through training of women, men, girls and boys with individual, group specialized PSS which include recreational activities-Games, livelihood services (Making door mats, designing bed sheets and Kitchen Gardens).• Training of staffs, community/frontline workers trained on Psychosocial support service provision like Counseling, giving referrals, GBV detection, Reporting and help desks services. • Provide appropriate GBV case management services including coordinated care and support to women, men, boys and girls through training community health workers, counselors, establish referral systems, reporting of suspected GBV/Protection cases and feedback mechanisms. • Establish and strengthen GBV referral systems and linking GBV survivors to available response services and support to women, men, boys and girls through establishing referral systems, re-enforcing and restructuring the already existing systems. • Set-up Women and Girls Friendly Space (WGFS) to provide services like Information sharing, livelihood skills, counseling, Peace-building at household level, Intimate Violence early detection awareness.• Carryout advocacy training of community leaders, Youths groups, women groups on Gender human rights, early detection of women rights, proper reporting protocols of women rights violation acts and legal aid available services and follow-up on justice. • Carryout awareness activities on GBV risk factors, mitigation measures and on how survivors can access support and services in line with the “do no harm” principle through training of community mobilisers on GBV risk mitigation and prevention measures-like Awareness on causes of GBV by Intimate partners, abuse of power, prevention measures include early warnings of GBV at household level, in the community and PSEA. • Provide capacity building trainings on various topics of GBV to frontline service providers like Basic advocacy tips and strategies, Gender/GBV referral pathways and how to established women and girls’ friendly spaces and COVID 19 awareness and protection measures/other related challenges it has on women and girls.• Distribute dignity kits to vulnerable and affected women and girls.

7.4.4. How will your project ensure feedback from the most vulnerable members (e.g. individuals living with disabilities, individuals who are not literate, women and girls)?

During project planning, TADO will give a main concern on applying and committing to Do No Harm principles through all project design, activities and results. The organization will save no efforts on follow these principles and mainstream the protection through this project life cycle, including consulting the stockholders, coordinating with partners and cluster, ensure safe and productive environment, providing equitable access to project services without discrimination and promoting the social cohesion. Ensure a comprehensive service mapping is undertaken so that risk mitigation and communication with communities can be proactive and informed; 2. Ensure consultation with communities related to location of protection services to understand relevant community perspectives; 3. Carry out safety audits for beneficiaries’ access to site locations. TADO will ensure that women and girls are an integral part of the design in terms of consultation of activities, older persons and persons with a disability (PWD) part of the design in terms of consultation of activities.

7.4.1. How will your project provide beneficiaries information about the project, feedback mechanism and/or complaint mechanism?

TADO will ensure that there is proper feedback and complaint mechanism put in place through out project implementation. TADO will have beneficiary accountability committees that will act as a linkage between beneficiaries and the organization. there will be complaint and feedback desks in the project locations and suggestion boxes that will be used to give feedback on the project implementation.TADO will also have focal persons in the project implementation sites that will be used to act as contacts for beneficiaries including the most vulnerable in the community and persons with disabilities.Through its social media platforms and emails, TADO will also give information on the project implementation and engage the youth on the deliverables of the project implementation.TADO will ensure that the beneficiaries are involved in project implementation from the beginning to the end ensuring that feedback and complaints are acted upon as soon as possible.

7.4.2. How will your project provide beneficiaries information about Protection against Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA)?

As TADO routinely Undertake PSEA orientation and briefing of its staffs and contactors, will be trained and oriented on the dangers of any related act of sexual exploitation and abuse to either the survivor’s and any other project beneficiary and cautioned to keep in mind a do no harm principle Confidentiality, Respect, keep safe and security and non discrimination of the beneficiaries, This will be held at all levels during project life. a) Confidentiality – information will be shared only with others who need to know in order to provide assistance and intervention, or as requested and agreed by the survivor. All actors will adhere to agreed protocols for sharing GBV-related information. b) Respect – actions and response of all project actors will be guided by respect for the wishes, the rights, and the dignity of the survivor, including treating the survivor in a non-judgmental way. c) Security and Safety – All project actors should be sensitive to the survivor’s needs for immediate care, while aiming to prevent further harm and/or distress. Any options should only be implemented with the consent of the survivor. d) Non-discrimination – All project actors shall always commit to providing services without discrimination based on ethnicity, clan, age, religion, gender, marital status, wealth, language, nationality, HIV status, political opinion, or other basis. The project will further be guided by the Humanitarian Charter, the Core Humanitarian Standards and Sphere Protection Standards, which require accountability of humanitarian actors to affected populations for the services they provide.

7.4.3. How will your project use the beneficiaries’ feedback to adapt project assistance?

TADO will ensure Setting-up appropriate mechanisms through which affected populations can measure the adequacy of interventions, and address concerns and complaints. Mechanisms for collecting feedback will use a combination of channels. Channels include active channels, where client perspectives are deliberately and systematically solicited (such as focus groups); reactive channels, where clients have more control over when and how they share perspectives (such as complaint lines); and finally, ‘open channels’ where feedback is solicited in an ad hoc manner, through ongoing interaction during implementation. TADO will strive and put affected populations at the center of decision-making processes that affect their lives so that services are choice-driven and client- focused. Special attention will also be paid to ensure the participation of, and consultation with, the most at- risk and/or marginalized members of society whose voices may not otherwise be heard. The project will also seek to support the development of self-protection capacities and assist people to claim their rights, including (not exclusively) the rights to shelter, food, water and sanitation, health, and education, TADO will ensure that women and girls are an integral part of the design in terms of consultation of activities, older persons and persons with a disability (PWD) part of the design in terms of consultation of activities.

8.11. Mitigation Measures.
