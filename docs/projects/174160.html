Project Summary

Despite the fact that access to fresh water is universal and human right of every individual, water has always been a prominent issues when it comes to Pakistan’s development challenges. Among other social, economic and political factors; and due to climate change, the increased frequency and intensity of extreme weather events like heat waves, droughts and floods, that Pakistan continuously faces, makes Pakistan significantly vulnerable to water associated challenges. COVID has exacerbated the existing vulnerabilities of people specially the most vulnerable including women, boys and girls, persons with disabilities and older people who bear the greater burnt of social inequalities as well as challenges triggered by a humanitarian situation.
Pakistan ranks at third place on the list of countries facing the problem of water shortages (International Monetary Fund, 2015) while report by Pakistan Council of Research in Water Resources (PCRWR), emphasis that the country is at the brink of reaching ‘absolute scarcity’ of water by 2025 (PCRWR/USPCAS-W, 2016). The societal impacts of water scarcity are far reaching and water is closely linked with economic and social development.
This project takes into account the WaSH needs of vulnerable communities factored in to combat impact of drought, floods, earthquakes and COVID-19 with special focus on women, girls, children, older people and person with disabilities who are more vulnerable due to their socio-economic status than others. Through this project, Islamic Relief Pakistan will specifically target integrated WaSH needs to those affected by shocks as per defined Specific and Sectoral Objectives for WaSH sector. The key focus is on WaSH needs raised from shock like Droughts, Earthquakes and Floods with special focus on COVID - 19 response as well as prevention.
Islamic Relief having its nationwide presence for over 2.5 decades and extensive experience in WaSH in humanitarian settings will target Balochistan (Quetta, Kila Abdullah, Musa Khel, Jaffarabad, and Musakhel), Sindh (Karachi, Badin, Thatta), Punjab (Rawalpindi), Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (Charsadda, Noshehra, Peshawar, Upper Dir) being HRP prioritized districts due to vulnerable for WaSH needs. The project aims to provide WaSH services with special focus of Provision of clean drinking water, sanitation and hygiene interventions in public and private health and education spaces, BCC, awareness and capacity building.
Following on HRP 2021 priorities and preferences, the entire action follow inclusiveness and ensure that participation of marginalized is given top priority. Gender, Age and disability will be one of preferences in execution while multiple vulnerability criteria will be one of the strategy to reach and access the most marginalized groups/ individuals.
The interventions are strictly aligned with the sectoral objectives of HRP 2021 and through these interventions, IRP will target a total of 80,200 individuals who will receives support and services through these WaSH interventions. The targeted individuals will include (Men: 31037, Women: 29915, Boys: 9624 and Girls: 9624 including 3208 persons with disabilities).
