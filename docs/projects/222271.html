Project Summary

WHO will strengthen humanitarian health coordination through the cluster approach, will enhance health information management, support disease surveillance, bolster epidemic and pandemic preparedness and response, and facilitate the delivery of essential health services to conflict-affected populations in Cabo Delgado.
By providing quality coordination the needs of the population will be addressed in a more efficient way, avoiding duplications by different partners, and helping solve any issue as it arises.
By centralizing the purchase and importation of medical supplies such as medicines and other medical equipment necessary for quality medical attention, WHO will support the MISAU directly and thru health partners to provide the medical care needed by the population.
Thru the support in coordinating the health partners, this project will intend to improve the quality of health services provided to the population in need of medical attention.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

WHO will deliver lifesaving and essential health services to address major causes of morbidity, including sexual and reproductive health, while providing care for physical injuries, disabilities, and mental health needs. The project also focuses on epidemic preparedness, detection, and response, aligning with the three core HRP objectives. Services will be provided in collaboration with Provincial Health Services (SPS), provincial Directorates of Health (DPS), District Health Services (SDSMAS), and other health partners through fixed health facilities and mobile health brigades. Targeted beneficiaries include all age groups—girls, boys, women, men, adolescents, the elderly, child- and female-headed households, persons with disabilities, and other vulnerable groups identified through assessments. The project will focus on the conflict affected Cabo Delgado province.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The project will implement the following priority activities across 15 districts in Cabo Delgado Province: Ancuabe, Chiure, Cidade de Pemba, Ibo, Metuge, Meluco, Mecufi, Montepuez, Mocimboa da Praia, Macomia, Mueda, Muidumbe, Nangade, Palma, and Quissanga:1) Health Cluster Leadership: Lead strategic planning and decision-making to ensure health needs are reflected in sectoral and multi-sectoral discussions. 2) Resource Mobilization Advocacy: Advocate for funding to support all health partners, emphasizing people-centered approaches, including Mental Health and Psychosocial Support (MHPSS). 3) Monitoring and Information Management: Collect, validate, analyze, and disseminate reliable health data for decision-making and monitor health cluster responses. 4)Health Services Functionality Tracking: Provide regular updates on health services functionality via the Health Resources and Services Availability Monitoring System (HeRAMS). 5) Essential Medicines and Supplies: Supply emergency medicines and fill gaps in the regular drug supply chain. 6) e-IDSR Support: Assess and monitor electronic Integrated Disease Surveillance and Response (e-IDSR) performance, addressing gaps and needs. 7) Disease Surveillance Training: Conduct e-IDSR training to improve outbreak detection and response for cholera, AWD, measles, malaria, malnutrition, and MHPSS. 8) Vaccination Campaigns: Support vaccination campaigns for cholera, polio, and measles, targeting vulnerable groups. 9) Lab Testing Capacity: Scale up laboratory testing and rapid diagnostic tests (RDTs) for cholera. 10) Lab Infrastructure Advocacy: Advocate for assessing laboratory infrastructure and equipment needs, enhancing infection prevention and control at health facilities.11) Service Delivery Support: Identify and address gaps in partners’ services through joint assessments and revitalized district coordination forums. 12) Mobile Brigade Services: Strengthen integrated mobile brigades to ensure population in need, including IDPs, access comprehensive health services. 13) MHPSS Strengthening: Enhance MHPSS services at health facility levels.These activities aim to address the health needs of conflict-affected populations effectively while ensuring comprehensive and integrated service delivery.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The protection risks for all the women and girls, boys and men, will be identified through the focused group discussion for each group. The accountability to affected people (AAP) approach will be included in all the integrated/join mission assessments by partners. WHO through the health cluster will work closely with the protection cluster to understand the protection concerns outlined in their periodic field visits. The findings will be used to ensure the participation of everyone in project implementation. Staff deployed to all locations will be sensitized on WHO non-discriminatory policy and all humanitarian core principles.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The proposed project ensures meaningful access to assistance by tailoring services to the specific needs of women and elderly persons, addressing barriers such as mobility challenges, cultural norms, and safety concerns. Mobile health brigades and community outreach initiatives bring services closer to underserved populations, including remote areas where women and the elderly face difficulties accessing fixed health facilities. To promote participation and empowerment, the project engages women and elderly persons through community consultations, focus group discussions, and involvement in health committees. These platforms allow them to voice their needs, priorities, and feedback, shaping the design and delivery of health services.Health services are designed to be inclusive and gender-sensitive, with dedicated provisions for reproductive health, mental health, and non-communicable diseases affecting older adults. Capacity-building for health workers includes training on age- and gender-sensitive care, ensuring that interventions are respectful, equitable, and responsive to the unique vulnerabilities of these groups.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The persons with disabilities will be identified through screening while health services are being provided. Responders will be trained on how to screen persons with disability and categorize them by age and gender and document them in the appropriate registers. Community health workers will be oriented on screening persons with disability for enrollment into the targeted health interventions such as mass vaccination campaigns and provision of health interventions.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project activities that may impact the environment include the generation of injection wastes during outreach consultations or measles, polio, and cholera vaccination campaigns. These activities have well-outlined mitigation measures or policies on waste management using safety boxes and incineration processes in place. The implementation of activities within this project will be conducted in line with protocols that protect the environment from the disposal of hazardous waste.
