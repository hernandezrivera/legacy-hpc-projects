Project Summary

Gender Based Violence is widespread in South Sudan, especially in Unity and Jonglei state counties that have faced unending episodes of sub-national conflict, widespread insecurity and perennial flooding, coupled with an increased number of returnees fleeing the Sudan crisis. These repeated crises have increased the levels of GBV, with 38% of communities surveyed in 2024 indicating GBV as the main protection risk. The GBV sub cluster further estimates that 2.75 million persons are in need of GBV services, including 1.59 million residents, 768,000 IDPs, and 396,000 returnees, yet 35% of all counties in the country have limited availability of core GBV services, making it necessary for humanitarian partners to prioritize GBV prevention, GBV risk mitigation, and GBV response strategies in priority locations.
In Leer, Mayendit, Panyijiar, Guit, Rubkona, Canal Pigi, Fangak, Uror, Pibor, and Pariang counties, cattle raiding conflicts, displacement by flooding, clashes over housing land and property, have further compounded the GBV concerns in these counties. Most of these counties are in IPC4 and IPC 5, bordering catastrophic proportions. A rising number of IDPs and returnees has worsened the GBV situation. Chances of women and girls being abducted and sexually abused or exploited are high, while intimate partner violence, physical violence, economic abuse are the order of the day. Women have invariably been traumatized by the repeated cycles of violence and are in dire need of GBV related services. This project will target 20,000 persons, mainly women and girls in Leer, Mayendit, Panyijiar, Guit, Rubkona, Fangak, Uror, Pibor, and Pariang counties of South Sudan.
Coalition for Humanity proposes the following activities:
1. Create awareness on GBV risks, available response services and timely reporting
2. Provision GBV prevention initiatives such as SASA and EMAP.
3. Provision of psychosocial support services for GBV survivors
4. Provision of specialized GBV services including case management, referral and legal aid
5. Distribution of dignity kits to adolescent girls and women at risk of GBV
6. Distribution of cash for protection to women and girls at risk of GBV
7. Supporting women and girls to start income generating activities by distributing start up kits
8. Capacity building of community leaders or humanitarian partners and social welfare staff on GBV
9. Establish and update functional GBV referral pathways at county level
The response will require 400,000 USD.

1. Needs

In Leer, Mayendit, Panyijiar, Guit, Rubkona, Canal Pigi, Fangak, Uror, Pibor, and Pariang counties, cattle raiding conflicts, displacement by flooding, clashes over housing land and property, have further compounded the GBV concerns in these counties. Most of these counties are in IPC4 and IPC 5, bordering catastrophic proportions. A rising number of IDPs and returnees has worsened the GBV situation. Chances of women and girls being abducted and sexually abused or exploited are high, while intimate partner violence, physical violence, economic abuse are the order of the day. Women have invariably been traumatized by the repeated cycles of violence and are in dire need of GBV related services. This project will target 20,000 persons, mainly women and girls in Leer, Mayendit, Panyijiar, Guit, Rubkona, Fangak, Uror, Pibor, and Pariang counties of South Sudan. Coalition for Humanity will deploy both static and mobile response to implement child protection activities and achieve the proposed targets across different counties. Implementation will be based on context and feasibility and will feature creation of awareness on GBV risks, available response services and timely reporting, provision GBV prevention initiatives such as SASA and EMAP, provision of psychosocial support services for GBV survivors, provision of specialized GBV services including case management, referral and legal aid, distribution of dignity kits to adolescent girls and women at risk of GBV, distribution of cash for protection to women and girls at risk of GBV, supporting women and girls to start income generating activities by distributing start up kits, capacity building of community leaders or humanitarian partners and social welfare staff on GBV, and establishment and updating a functional GBV referral pathways at county level. CH will regularly report implementation of GBV interventions on 5W's and resources mobilized on Financial Tracking Services.The response will require 400,000 USD.

2. Project activities

CH proposes the following activities:1. Provision of comprehensive case management services including material support – Coalition for Humanity will establish women and girls safe spaces through which it will provide case management to women in need of GBV Services, including material support, referral and legal aid.2. Provision of case management services through cash for protection initiative – Coalition for Humanity will distribute cash for protection to child headed households and families with children at risk of child protection violations. Conditional cash will be pegged on continued enrollment in school.3. Map and update referral pathways at the county level - CH will aim to map and update the referral pathway in each targeted county with a view of establishing gaps and developing guides that will help in referring and resolving protection concerns affecting children. 4. Provision of psychosocial support services for GBV survivors - CH will aim to provide specialized, structured and non-structured, community based mental health and PSS services for GBV survivors and women at risk of GBV. CH will train social workers in community based mental health and psychosocial support and deploy them to offer structured and semi-structured mental health and psychosocial support for children affected by different crises.5. Create awareness on GBV risks, available response services and timely reporting – CH will conduct awareness raising in the community to sensitize community members on gender equality, the negative effects of GBV, and the available redress mechanisms. Coalition for Humanity will reach people through radio talk shows, door to door and community public address systems.6. Provision of capacity building trainings for community leaders, humanitarian partners and government social welfare staff on GBV – Different leaders have different roles in the community. The project will train women leaders, youth leaders, opinion leaders from religious groups and local authority on GBV in emergencies. 7. Provision GBV prevention initiatives such as SASA and EMAP - CH will provide social behavior change communication mechanisms such as Engaging Men in Accountable Practices and the Start, Awareness, Support and Action (SASA) approach to prevent GBV at household and community level. GBV champions will be trained and tasked with elimination of the vice through peer to peer initiatives.8. Distribution of dignity kits to adolescent girls and women at risk of GBV - CH will distribute dignity kits to adolescent girls and women of reproductive age, and invest in the local production of sanitary pads through women and girls safe spaces, as part of psychosocial support.9. Supporting women and girls to start income generating activities by distributing start up kits - CH intends to empower women by training them on different income generating ventures and assisting them with start-up kits.
