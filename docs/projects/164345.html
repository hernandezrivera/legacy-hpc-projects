Project Summary

Mercy-USA for Aid and Development will support internally displaced persons (IDPs) and host community in Maarrat Tmasrin Sub-district in Idleb governorate with winterization support in the form of in-kind, eco-friendly biofuel, and stove kits (in line with SNFI activity 1.2 Provision of seasonal and supplementary NFIs and shelter assistance (e.g. winter clothing, fuel, winter-specific shelter upgrades; including cash/voucher for these items).
Total number of beneficiaries who will be targeted 21,183 individuals (Men 3,809- Women 4,454- Boys 6,563- Girls 6,357) by using with in-kind methodology for implementation.
Mercy-USA will target each household with in-kind 900 kg of olive pit fuel throughout the
winter months (November, December, and January) A distribution of 300 kg per month along with stoves for 25% of the target for new arrived IDPs depending on a preset selection criteria for the most vulnerable households.
On the other hand, Mercy-USA will target 50 HH (Men 48, Women 50, Boys 72, and Girls 80) in Dana Sub-district with shelter rehabilitation and repair for slightly damaged houses due to fighting on this area, in order to enhance safe access to shelter such as repair windows and wall holes without extreme damage taking in our considerations HLP issue prior to any house selection can be made by using contractor with cost no more than 1000 USD per HH.

4 - Needs

According to HNO 2020, roughly 5.65 million are People in need for shelter and 3.42 million are people in need for NFI assistance.In the north-west, there are critical levels of overcrowding putting significant pressure on overburdened communities, with few if any remaining shelter options available. In north-east Syria ongoing displacement coupled with a lack of safe or adequate shelter, and explosive contamination is a significant obstacle. Across Syria, levels of damage and destruction to shelters have not improved over the last year with 76% of all households listing at least one issue as compared to 2019 where 47% cited adequacy issues, thereby reducing the potential for sustainable return and undermining attempts to restore basic services. This lack of access to basic services including safe and adequate shelter increases the vulnerability of households and forces the adoption of harmful coping mechanisms such as selling assets or keeping children out of education, especially among female-headed households, households including youth, and those with functional difficulties.NFIs continue to be essential and life-saving particularly for those newly displaced of forced to relocate. While market access appears to be gradually improving, many life-sustaining, basic items continue to be unaffordable for people. NFIs become increasingly important during the cold weather months to ensure families can live in safety and dignity. Families report that they are in need of fuel, clothing, and power/heating sources. Over 60 per cent of people state a preference for receiving assistance in the form of cash or vouchers rather than in-kind as they can select specific items needed for their families.

5 - Implementation of activities

Regarding to Winterization activities: they will be implemented by the following steps: 1- Setup and planning 2- Conduct vulnerability Assessment for households in targeted locations 3- Procurement of Olive-pit fuel : This activity will be done in Turkey and in-line with our procurement and tender process 4-Contract with all transportation services: For delivery to distribution points and Households 5- Regular monthly distribution/delivery of 300Kg 6-Reporting 7- Monitoring and Evaluation throughout all implementation steps Regarding to Shelter Rehabilitation Activities: they will be implemented in line with Shelter Repair and Rehabilitation Guidelines (SRRG) which was launched by SNFI cluster Turkey hub in November 2018 by the following steps: 1-Program Design: by signing contract with contractor (Type A) for shelter repair and rehabilitation. 2- Community Selection: 1. Prioritise communities for assistance within Dana sub-district based on set of criteria including need, HLP documentation, access, security situation and most importantly slightly house damage 2. Verify communities prioritised for assistance with the Cluster. 3- Community engagement: 1. Identify local representatives for the selected communities in order to have good representation inaddition to forming shelter committee for accountability towards affected population. 2. Agree the scope of the Programme with the local authority and the community by signing MOU with local authorities, create and implement community outreach method. 3. Conduct community outreach activities to • ensure that information about the Program is provided to community members • allow relationships with the community to be established and developed; • ensure that community and household assessments can beconducted; 4- Beneficiary Selection 5-Agreement with beneficiary/Owner. 6-Quality Assurance 7-Completion of works.The most vulnerable people will be supported by heating assistance and dignified shelter
