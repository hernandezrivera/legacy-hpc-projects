Project Summary

The Darfur region of Sudan faces acute humanitarian needs gap, due to protracted conflict, inter-tribal tensions, the conflict has continued to displace populations, which has caused many households to be food insecurity category (phase3) the humanitarian situation exacerbated by harsh climatic conditions, with limited rainfall that negatively affect household food production. The population of North Darfur is constrained by a harsh environment, low access to services, protracted conflict, sporadic inter-clan violence, and an influx of refugees from South Sudan.
Korma Administrative Unit (El Fasher locality) and Kabkabya localities are located West of El Fasher , whereas Tawilla is in the South West direction.They are inhabited by several tribes: Fur, Arabs,Zaghawa, Tunjur, Berty, and others . The main economic activities are Agriculture, Trade and Animal breeding. The mention areas are the most affected by Darfur crisis since 2003 , which result in huge gap in basic service special in WASH, Health, Nutrition,Education, protection, emergency food and livelihoods opportunities . The project is aiming to address the education need for ( IDPs/Returnees children in particular ) ,through improving physical structures and learning environment in schools, by construction of classrooms, offices ,latrines, training teachers, PTAs and provide support & At-Risk youth, provision of school furniture including seats & benches , water tanks, nursery and recreational kits for pupils.
. The project is targeting 10,254 school children dis-aggregated to 4,567 boys and 5887 girls.

1 - Needs

The project is implemented in El Fasher (Korma Administrative Unit ) , Kabkabya and Tawilla localities , these areas are the most affected by crises in North Darfur since 2003 . ZDPO has conducted need assessment from 15th to 20th February 2022 in the three mentioned areas. The result showed that there is a huge gap in Education. The project will assess the situation in the context in which the project will be implemented, the needs is including the physical conditions of schools , education supplies, capacity building for teachers/students, PTAs and At-Risk youth, including pupils with special needs, water, sanitation and hygiene promotion gaps in schools, and recreational activities and awareness gaps including HIV/AIDs, incidence of teenage pregnancy, which hinders girls from accessing education.

2 - Activities/Outputs

The project is aiming to increase access to integrated basic education for IDPs , Returnees and the host communities in emergency contexts, beside building resilience and to provide durable solution for return or integration within the host communities ; it is intended to improving the learning environment, enhance child participation in over curricula activities, and decision making on matters concerning children in the schools and wider community, provision of learning space to 10,254 children (girls, Boys), through construction / rehabilitation of permanent, temporary classrooms and latrines and provision of learning material, school furniture, recreational and dignity kits, uniform and payment of incentive to volunteer teachers , additionally accelerated programs will be introduce to absorb out of school children and adolescents , the Parents-Teachers Association will be trained on school management to be able to prepare School Improvement Plans (SIP) , in the selected schools within the project area, also to enhance child and community participation on decision making processes in the targeted schools, Parent-Teacher Associations will be activated and children clubs established and trained on Child Right and participation. On the other hand teachers will be trained on child centered teaching methods and Education in Emergency.To improve child protection, will organize awareness sessions on child rights and protection as enshrine in the UN convention on the Right on the Child (UNCRC) and the other relevant Human Rights instruments. Child protection will be mainstream in the education system to create some kind of synergy between Education and protection and wider community protection, through strengthening the child protection networks, Community-Base Monitoring and Reporting Mechanisms to report exploitation and abuses, committed against children and adults, above all teachers will be provided training on positive discipline, a referral pathway developed to report cases of abuses and exploitation. To identify the protection needs, data collected will be segregated, by age groups, gender and disability to ensure inclusiveness and to design activities accordingly.

5.2 - Impact on environment/natural resources

The project will use Soil Stabilized Blocks (SSB) for the construction activities and use environmental sound materials for temporary construction to avoid cutting of trees and pollution , encourage tree planting in school and establish nurseries to educate school children, and raising awareness on environmental preservation , to minimize effects on climate change and combat drought & desertification, also improve hygiene promotion through construction of latrines to avoid open defecation and reduce increment of flies and other insects that spread diseases.

7.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will inform and interact with inter-agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including SEA and abuse complaints?

ZDPO would follow its code of conduct and the International Red Cross and Red Crescent Movement code on protection in general , with zero tolerance of sexual exploitation and abuse . Reporting tools and mechanism will be established to inform any incidence committed by staff or any person dealing with children or adults, base on the Child Safeguard policy and code of conduct signed by staff and contractors, organization staff and communities will be sensitized on GBV and a focal person will be recruited to report to the relevant UN agencies including UNICEF and UNFPA, through the referral pathway. Gender audits will be conducted to assess gaps that might need to be addressed, to ensure that women,children and Disable are protected and included in the project. ZDPO will strive to ensure safety, inclusiveness and protection of children in camps or voluntary returnees areas of origin and host communities. The organization will try to improve access to Education services , by reducing distances to minimize risks to going and back to school, by rehabilitating the schools which are functioning.
