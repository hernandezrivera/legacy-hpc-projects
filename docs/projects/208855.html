Project Summary

SAO aim is to intervene and implement an emergency shelter project to address threats exposure to rain, sun, and harms from fellow human being. The project will save lives of 1000 households who will receive 1000 emergency shelter kits that include wooden, standing poles, KG of rope, grass mats, bunch of bamboo sticks and plastic sheet. 1000 emergency shelter kits will procurement.
Outcome
The newly displaced populations in in Nyala south and North will increased access to shelter services to restore their wellbeing.
Output
1000 HH newly displaced population provided with adequate 1000 emergency shelter kits to improve their wellbeing and restore their dignity.
Activities
1.1 Conducting Identification of PSNs and vulnerable population registration, and verification of the 1000 HHs prior to the distribution of emergency shelter materials.
1.2. Conduct Procurement of 1000 ES kits
1.5. Conduct PDM after two weeks from the final date of construction
