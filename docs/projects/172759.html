Project Summary

The project will target vulnerable food insecure households by giving the cash transfers for food and soap. The soap is for them to practice hygiene practice. The target will include pregnant and lactating women,the disabled and elderly headed households.
To prevent further deterioration of living standards for acutely food insecure population, there will beprovision of agriculture support ensuring achievement of food security and resilience to repeated exposure to multiple shocks and stressors.
The resilience activities will target households which are labour endowered. Protection, education and safeguarding will be mainstreamed.

8 - Accountability to Affected People
8.1 - Describe how your project was designed in consultation with communities? How will you incorporate communities in project implementation and what feedback mechanisms do you have in place?

Save the Children will establish a safe, non-threatening and easily accessible complaints reporting and feedback mechanism (CRFM) that enables women, men, boys and girls, including even those most powerless, to make a suggestion or complaint. The CRFM system will be used as a quick and convenient way for beneficiaries to give feedback about our interventions through different means including bt not limit to using the SMS and phone calls to Deloitte Tip-offs Anonymous Call Centre. Wherever possible, the CRFM will build upon local structures and systems of addressing complaints in the community. All complaints will be logged on to an agreed database, analysis done and feedback will be done on the monthly basis and all feedback will be used to inform programmatic decision. SCI will follow up and give feedback to the beneficiaries. Depending on the type of the complaint, the follow up of the complaint could takes less than a day up to 2 weeks if other stakeholders are involved. An issues register will be kept for record of processes taken up to closure of the case.An inclusive, participatory approach will therefore be adopted, by which disadvantaged and vulnerable individuals will be represented to promote their full and equal participation.

9 - Environmental considerations
9.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Farmers will receive training on appropriate vegetative propagation techniques such as grafting, layering, budding and use of roots to raise new fruit trees from mature ones rather than using seeds. Trees raised through vegetative propagation are identical to the parent plant in terms quality of fruit, they also start producing fruits in one year, hence reduce the generation interval from seed to reach production. All participant farmers are expected to establish woodlots and orchards at their homesteads. Together with Forestry Commission and Environmental Management Agency, the project will organize conservation and aforestation competitions to create more demand for tree planting.
