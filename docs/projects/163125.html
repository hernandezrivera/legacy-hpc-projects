Project Summary

This project will assist 1000 newly displaced HHs in Afrin district (5000 individuals each month) with monthly food baskets for a period of 8-months. The total number of assessed HHs during the project will be 8000 HHs; 40000 individuals.
The targeted beneficiaries will be selected based on needs and vulnerabilities and the project will prioritize: (female-headed households, women and young girls, HHs including pregnant, lactating women, elderly, disabled people or people with chronic illness and injuries, focusing also on unaccompanied and separated children).
According to the recommendations of the FSL cluster, each food basket is 58 kg and contains about 1550 Kcal per person per day (for 5 people) for one month.

4 - Needs

After nine years of the Syrian crisis, At least 3 percent of the total population (6 percent of the PIN) are severely food insecure implying that they are unable to meet their immediate food needs and are having to resort to negative coping strategies. (FSS_HNO_2020_ OCHA)The number of PiN for 2020 has increased by 8 percent including about 9.8 million people are considered in need for aid and assistance; of which 569,477 people are estimated to be severely food insecure; 7.3 million estimated to be moderately food insecure while 1.9 million people are estimated to be at risk of food insecurity. (FSS_HNO _2020 _OCHA)According to the Emergency Situation Report of the FSL cluster, around 586,000 people, 80% of them are women and children, fled towards north-west Idleb and North Aleppo governorate since December 2019; of them around 49,211 people fled to Afrin district which is targeted by this project. (FSS Gap on Emergency Response Covering 01 December 2019 to 03 February 2020)Most of the IDPs in Afrin have been displaced multiple times and thus they are in dire need of food assistance to save lives. Most of the IDPs within Afrin district especially those newly displaced are experiencing a deteriorating humanitarian situation and extreme poverty with lack of income and restricted access to income generation opportunities making them unable to secure even their food for their children especially female-headed HHs and HHs including elderly, persons with disabilities of chronic illnesses. According to Sadad field surveys, IDPs’ HHs within Afrin district also were reported to follow negative coping mechanisms used to cope with lack of food are such as skipping meals, eating less preferred food, reducing meal size, reducing the number of meals. As a result of this, Sadad will respond to 8000 of the most vulnerable IDPs’ HHs through appropriate modalities and according to identified vulnerability criteria, with 8000 food baskets based on the FSL cluster recommendations

5 - Implementation of activities

All the project’s intervention and activities will be implemented by Sadad’s field staff under the direct supervision of Sadad management in Turkey office and in coordination with FSL Cluster. Sadad will also coordinate with other community-based organizations working in the same project’s targeted areas to ensure that targeted beneficiaries not receive any food baskets assistance from other agencies and avoid duplication.After signing MoUs with local councils and camps’ managements and contracting with the project’s staff, Sadad field teams will start collecting and preparing the updated new IDPs beneficiaries’ lists. Sadad will announce advertisements for tenders to After receiving prices offers and reviewing them, contracts will be signed with the supplier and then food baskets will be purchased inside Turkey, and shipped cross border to the target area. In the targeted areas there will be designated distribution points in each location in coordination with the local council in each area. Based on the Beneficiaries prepared lists, the targeted HHs will be informed of this location and the time and of distribution and Sadad staff will ensure accountability and gender measures are taken to ensure all beneficiaries have safe access to the service provided especially females’ beneficiaries; there will be separate distribution points for males and females and the distribution time will be in consultation with beneficiaries especially females’ beneficiaries. Sadad team also will ensure delivering the food baskets to beneficiaries with disabilities at their homes. Before and during the distribution process, MEAL officers will conduct assessments and surveys with the beneficiaries and ensure the accountability measures are taken to consideration including conducting FGDs, PDM surveys as well as monitoring the whole project activities and preparing reports.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

During the implementation of the project interventions, Sadad will ensure efforts to Do No Harm and mainstream protection concepts, especially of GBV and PSEA to ensure a safe and protective work environment for both the project’s staff and beneficiaries.The main protection risk during the implementation of the project activities especially when distributing food baskets comes from *sexual exploitation by humanitarian workers gatekeepers and potential other beneficiaries (risk of harassment) of women and girls; Impact: High; Likelihood: High; Mitigation; Sadad will establish a confident complaint mechanisms to ensure receiving beneficiaries’ feedbacks and complains; there will be a complaint boxes in the distribution sites, all beneficiaries will also be provided with the direct contact number (including Whatsapp) to directly voice any complaints, there will be also males and females M&E officers who will conduct face to face discussions with males and females beneficiaries as well as surveys and FGDs. Sadad will train the working staff on humanitarian standards and how to deal with beneficiaries as well as all the working staff will sign the organization’s code of conduct. *Risk of violence, harassment from beneficiaries among themselves or with humanitarian workers in the distribution points. Likelihood; high. Impact; high. Mitigation; there will be several distribution points ensuring these points are well-organized and a queue for females and another for males should be formed in the distribution site to prevent any harassment cases which might happen if only one queue is available. Sick people and pregnant women will always be given priority while distribution is taking place. There will also be a consultation with beneficiaries especially females’ beneficiaries on time and place of distribution.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumption1: The security situation within the project targeted areas is considered safe as the area is under the Turkish-control (olive Branch areas) and this will ensure the best implementation of activities in addition to the support from the local authorities to implement the project’s activities smoothly. However, there may be a risk1: disputes between the host communities and the IDPs might take place in terms of benefiting from the project’s activities. Likelihood; high. Impact; high. Mitigation: Sadad will coordinate with local councils and will conduct FGDs on the selection criteria and there will be printed leaflets about the project’s objective and the criteria and the distribution process. Food baskets will be in cooperation with local authorities, moreover, the field staff both in Syria and Turkey will be trained on Conflict Sensitivity and they will be able to approach their programming in a conflict-sensitive way. Risk2: large groups gathering around the distribution points could lead to physical harm and/or gender-sensitive issues arising from violence and coercion. Mitigation: There will be several well-organized distribution points with respect to gender differences and there will be sufficient time-period for distribution and the field staff will be trained on humanitarian standards and how to deal with different groups of beneficiaries.Risk3: Cross border closure between Turkey and Syria; Likelihood: medium; Impact: high. Mitigation: If the border-gate was closed for a long-period, Sadad will check for alternative suppliers from the local markets.
