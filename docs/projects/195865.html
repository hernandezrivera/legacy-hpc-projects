Project Summary

Under the CCCM cluster, the following interventions targeting all 100 camps, these camps have been selected according to a need assessment conducted by BINAA on Nov 21, ISIMM data, incident reports summaries, and MoMs of CCCM Cluster Meeting (these locations have no or weak emergency preparedness and (fire) response mechanism, the CMs structure in them are weak, the facilities don't have regular maintenance mechanism), in addition to complementary with other NGOs working in the campsites secured funds:
1. Contingency planning, Safety, and Security through providing/refilling 6200 fire extinguishers and 1550 first aid kits in 100 locations besides delivering training to the security points members (20 IDPs from each targeted camp will be trained about using the delivered items and about the best practices to avoid the fire hazard or deal with the fire incidents). each security point will include 4 fire extinguishers and a 01first aid kit and will be conducted in proper locations inside the camps by using the camp drawing which will be prepared before the intervention. the security point will be constructed by cement blocks, concrete floor, and roof with an iron door, the location of the security points will be published inside the site.
2. Site maintenance and emergency work through forming maintenance and care committees, the number of the committees' members will be calculated based on the camp size, BINAA will equip them with proper tools.
3. Support improvement measures to camp management structures and systems through delivering training to the camp management members in 100 camps.
4. Conduct a multi-sector need assessment in the targeted camps before the winter season to identify the gaps in these camps
BINAA will target in its plan 100 camps, according to the following:
50 camps in 2022 which are in most need for the project's activities especially "Strengthening emergency preparedness and (fire) response in IDP sites".
50 camps in 2023 with have active fire fighting points for 2022.

8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) is at the core of BINAA responses and all BINAA staff must comply with it. BINAA has a policy of zero tolerance for sexual exploitation. BINAA field team is trained on communication approaches, i.e., how to present the program transparently in a way that mitigates conflict, anticipating perceptions of unfairness or other issues that could create conflict, and managing expectations. BINAA recognizes that providing any material humanitarian assistance during a conflict in which needs are dire and resources are scarce has the potential to cause conflict and protection concerns for all actors involved. BINAA considers the different needs of the elderly, mobility-impaired, women, and youth in implementing the project activities to ensure equitable delivery of assistance by ensuring that the service is well provided to them. Effective systems are in place for preventing and responding to acts of SEA by integrating PSEA in BINAA policies and making sure that all personnel read and sign the code of conduct, and that all contracts with partners/suppliers/contractors include clauses on PSEA. BINAA ensures that complaint mechanisms for reporting SEA are accessible (WhatsApp, email, etc.) and that focal points for receiving complaints understand how to discharge their duties. taking appropriate action to protect persons from retaliation where allegations of SEA are reported involving stockholders in a timely and professional manner.
