Project Summary

The project’s role is to distribute agricultural inputs on most vulnerable farmers. Those farmers who own agricultural land and do have enough money to farm in Idlib regions including:
1- Distribute 500 basket of agricultural inputs including (seeds- pesticides – agricultural commodities – watering vouchers – harvest vouchers)
2- Establish 6 training courses for beneficiaries about best agricultural practices
Every HH will cost 832$ during the project

Needs

According to HNO draft report 2019, 9 million people in need. 6.5 million are food insecure and an additional 2.5 million are at risk of food insecurity.As per the same report, one from every 3 person in Syria suffer from lack of food security. Also, lack livelihoods sustainability or filling the gap between emergency aid and long-term aid will result in increasing of unemployment rate, economic dangers, and depend on aids, especially most vulnerable men and women.Support Syrian people to be independent and their ability to endure via creating and supporting make a living opportunity. Moreover, small projects must be supported, ability of production must be improved and linking them to value chain via supporting community services markets and economic infrastructure. Also, supporting production assets and veterinary care at once in conjunction with providing food assistance.Many families were forced to give up agriculture or cattle grazing or reducing production due to lack of inputs or price increase like seed and fertilizers. Also, lack of fuel, land and water is one of the most important obstacles.

Protection Risk Analysis

Risk of protection: There is a risk of discrimination if services are provided to specific groups or individuals and others are excluded. Probability: Average Impact: High Mitigation measures: 1. Ensure that there is a map showing the locations of the beneficiaries who are in real need of assistance so that communication with beneficiaries is proactive and based on knowledge. 2. Communicate with local councils to understand the views of the community 3. Educate the beneficiary community so that the person who does not receive the service knows that it is not needed and that there are beneficiaries who are more in need of it. Risk of protection: extortion Probability: Average Impact: High The employee responsible for the distribution of aid extorted women or children even men for personal needs before handing over aid or providing service to them and exploitation of the situation of disability and need. 1. Inform staff that this is contrary to regulations and laws and educate women and children in need to tell officials directly about any cases of extortion or harassment. 2. Use M & E and questionnaires mobilized by beneficiaries to control distribution and non-exploitation of those in need
