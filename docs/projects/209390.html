Project Summary

As lead agency for protection of forcibly displaced persons including conflict induced, UNHCR has been working in Sudan for many years on providing and coordinating protection of forcible displaced persons. In 2024, with expansion of the conflict in Sudan resulting in mass displacement and vulnerability, UNHCR plans to continue with the below to elevate the suffering of the forcibly displaced.
• Coordination mechanism is strengthened for Protection Monitoring; Support to Community-Based Protections Networks (CBPNs) and early warning systems.
• IDPs and conflict affected persons have access to tailored special protection responses through UNHCR and partner’ delivery coordination.
• Peaceful coexistence projects are implemented using area-based approach to programming.
The project will target 1, 340,929.00 million IDPs and returnees across the different states in Sudan.

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

UNHCR maintains zero tolerance towards sexual exploitation and abuse. The project teams will undertake mandatory training on the code of conduct and be continuously sensitized on the Prevention from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA). Further, the project staff and partners will be trained on the humanitarian and protection principles, including observing the elements of neutrality, impartiality, operational independence, and do-no-harm, and ensuring the highest standards of dignity in assistance delivery. UNHCR will undertake risk mapping and analysis to ensure proactive and reactive measures effectively mitigate the impact. UNHCR will use its capacities available in country and its long-standing partnership with national NGOs in Sudan to facilitate localization in programming, foster local ownership of the response, and pave the way for an exit strategy. UNHCR will ensure that due diligence on HLP-related issues is considered in site planning and development interventions. UNHCR will facilitate community consultation throughout the site development processes.

4.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will support Inter-Agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including Sexual exploitation and abuse complaints?

Through this project, UNHCR will aim for a more robust Accountability to the Affected Population (AAP) mechanism that places the affected population at the center of programming. UNHCR will adapt mobile approaches that involve deploying roving teams, setting up information desks, and providing feedback to the community. Where feasible, UNHCR will deploy static community resource centers that provide a one-stop information hub to the affected population in the gathering sites and the host communities. Further, UNHCR will ensure that persons with disabilities have access to all relevant information by preparing gender-responsive key messages in multiple and accessible formats. Key messaging and information dissemination will consider using local languages for a wider outreach.
