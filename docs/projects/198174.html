Project Summary

The project will actively engage in Child Protection and Inclusive education services for children living with disabilities, to prevent and response to neglect, abuse, poor education services, exploitation and discrimination that disabled children particularly faced in the society. We will provide case management services and PSS to 735 boys and girls in 4 LGAs of Adamawa,and livelihood support to vulnerable caregivers of the identified concerns in order to reduce the risk that disabled child faced as a result of begging for food by the street. Will organize community sensitization and awareness programmes to educate and train caregivers, CBCPCs, health workers and government school teachers on how Children with Disabilities communicate when they want to pass a message especially when they need help and some nonverbal communication that disabled child uses especially those having difficulties in the domain of hearing. Will train men and women on the effect of child abuse. The project will identify, manage and respond to all forms of discrimination against disabled children by reducing the stigma surrounding their abilities and promote child's participation and learning, mainstreaming to child's coping abilities, administering of case management service to UASC, and providing Psycho social services to fight the stigma surrounding the child's abilities. We will use braille for teaching the blind and sign language for teaching the deaf so that disabled children can communicate their pains, worries and possibly joy among other children. Persons with Disabilities (PWD) go through the most difficult hardship and acute poverty due to lack of good foundation and poor start in life, because none or fewer choices are available to them. They often experience barriers to the enjoyment of their basic human right and their inclusion in the society. Their abilities is overlooked,their capacity is underestimated and their needs is given a low priority.Out of school children are mostly the singled handed,one eyed,one armed,deaf, and blind.The primary beneficiaries targets are: 580 Children with disabilities,155 UASC. Indirect beneficiaries are 260 Men and women. The overall project will targeted 995 beneficiaries.
The purpose of these intervention is to respond to problems of mistreatment that disabled children and other vulnerable children faces as a result of conflict related issues,reduce their stigma and enroll them for inclusive education class. The Federal republic of Nigeria has been plagued by persistent conflict in the recent years which have claimed 300000 lives apart from the general impact of conflict.It has also contributed to an alarming increase in number of persons living with disabilities(PWD)and made more vulnerable.Adamawa State Statistician-General has declared that the National Education Data Survey (NEDS) reports for 2005,showed reported in Max Planck Institute for Demographic Research (MPIDR) database,0.4 percent death (over 17200) occurred in children and adolescent, 53 percent occurred in adolescent ages 10-19 and 47 percent among children ages 0-9,data corrected as of June 2022.In Adamawa state Nigeria, over 3400 children are malnourished, and this are vulnerable children and children living with disabilities.Furthermore, children living with disabilities are been deprived from fresh food and water, parental love and care,decent and nice shelter, quality education services and safe environment which lead some of this children to end up being found in another cities and towns as Separated or Unaccompanied children in search of care and support.Disabled children are used as income generating by other people and some caregivers, by forcing the disabled child to come out and show people his/her weakness and be begging people to contribute money for them to buy food to eat.During outbreak,CWD missed out crucial information about the pandemic and preventable measures as information is not provided in accessible format.

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

Africa Initiative for children with disabilities(AICD) will integrate strong feedback mechanisms designed to be accessible to all groups ensure people with disabilities,women,girls,boys,men and women.These will include regular FGDs and listening sessions,including session specifically with boys and girls living with disabilities,women and girls ,suggestion boxes at project sites,including within child Safe centers and protection information centers a toll-free hotline accessible to illiterate beneficiaries,with notices in local languages with the hotline number posted prominently at project side;and household interviews,which will reach beneficiaries served by mobile teams in hard- to -reach locations. Feedback is received through hotline handlers,who refer requests for assistance or feedback on programme quality to the relevant programme teams for action.Each point of feedback is documented and compiled in monthly reports,allowing management to track the status of response and follow-up. AAP will be added as a standing item in the PMC,joint monitoring,review meetings and selectively,in steering committee meetings,AICD will work to translate protection messages into sign languages to reduce the communication gap between those having difficulties from the domain of hearing and those with communication disorder so they can be aware and understand what is going on.Especially when there is new annocement of outbreak disease,the preventable measures will also be communicated to them. We will have a complain box in the community of intervention and field on related issues so that all persons with disabilities precisely children will communicate and drop . We will interpret information and messages to reach out children living with disabilities in sign or braille and any other means applicable and ensure communication appropriate,confidential and accountable protection services.
