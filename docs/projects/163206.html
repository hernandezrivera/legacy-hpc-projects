Project Summary

Women with disabilities all over the world experience multiple disadvantages due to gender and disability. Disability can mean any physical or mental impairment that substantially limits one or more of the major life activities. On a world-wide scale, women with disabilities are excluded from the paid labor market by 75%, and by up to 100% in developing countries. Majority of these women perform household work such as cooking, washing and taking care of children and relatives. Those who work often, experience unequal opportunities at their workplaces, including less pay for equal work (United Nations Enable [UNE], 2008). Additionally, according to the UN Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (CRPD), men with disabilities tend to have more years of education compared to their female counterparts. In developing countries, they experience “triple jeopardy” as they face discrimination on account of gender, disability, and geographic region.
JUSTIFICATION
Internally displaced women with disabilities possess great skills and creative talent within them (report from a survey conducted by FIED). When you visit centers, the height of creativity in the products they sell in order to earn a living is awesome. Despite all these, very little is done to nurture their creative talents or empower them to know that even though they are handicapped in one way or the other and internally displaced, they still have the potential to make their lives and community as a whole better. These internally displaced female handicaps mostly live in isolation due to cultural beliefs that some of these disabilities are curses and taboos so they tend to be shy and psychologically defeated. Also, in cases where parents have two or more children and just one has a disability, there is a tendency to abandon the child with disability and concentrate on those who are 100% physically fit. These ones with disabilities tend to depend on their parents and physically fit siblings who may not even be able to fend for themselves.
In order to break this cycle of poverty and dependence and ensure sustainable community development, these internally displaced handicapped women can be trained and set up to train other women with disabilities and set them as well. This is going to ensure sustainable community development as we will do the train-and-set up system as it is done in most unskilled labor training workshops. This also will ensure continuity as these handicapped women who will be trained, will come into agreement with us to form Self-Help Group under the supervision of the organization. This no doubt, will pass down knowledge to other people living with disabilities.
A total of 500 women living with disabilities(IDPs) shall be the main beneficiaries of this project. Beneficiaries of this project, shall be selected from three divisions in the SWR, Cameroon namely; Fako, Meme and Manyu. The beneficiaries of this project shall be disintegrated according to needs as follows:
- 300 women living with physical disabilities, 200 living with sensory impairment.
- A total of 200 of them will be selected in Meme, 150 in Fako and 150 in Manyu.
- 100 men living with disabilities will constitute part of the general target.
- Beneficiaries shall fall between the ages of 18 and 50.
- A total of 300 persons (IDPs) living with disabilities will be selected from the geographical areas and 200 persons (natives).
The main purpose of this project is as follows:
1. To form a strong network where empowered and independent female handicaps can make their voices heard.
2. To train at least 500 internally displaced handicapped women from the community on different handwork including tailoring, knitting painting, beads pastry making.
3. To increase their inclusion and participation in economic activities by involving their products in different trade centers. This will go a long way to build their self-esteem which had been trampled on.
