Project Summary

In order to strenghten the delivery of health care services to vulnerable groups in Rural Damascus, Madiarah & Misraba and surrounding is a priority for Qanawat Association, as the area has experienced harsh conditions, after eight years of war, siege and destruction, the health sector in the region was affected, and in light of the spread of epidemics and diseases.
Needs Assessment has emerged through the absence of medical centers that provide comprehensive services for all specialties with high quality and free of charge, serves the surrounding community through consultations and medical services and spreading health awareness in the area in which the center is to be established and the surrounding areas (Madyara & Misraba and surroundings) in which approximately 35,000 people live. The majority of whom are women and children, knowing that in those areas there are no medical centers.
Bearing in mind that the standard of living is low, and there is a lack of health awareness.
Based on the previous indications and in view of the urgent need, Qanawat Association has taken the decision to establish a medical clinic that provides the following services:
(Internal - Gastroenterology - orthopedic - cardiac - ophthalmic - Otology - Pediatrics - gynecology - neurological - dermatology - radiology - laboratory tests – emergency in addition to medication).
The medical clinic will cover the severe shortage in medical care and provide all health services free of charge, provide appropriate health awareness, reduce the burden of overcrowding on medical centers in Damascus governorate, and save the costs of beneficiaries moving to remote places to obtain services.
Noting that Qanawat Association has the expertise and experienced staff in medical field (medical referrals to hospitals & medical centers in Damascus) and in connection with most specialized doctors.
Based on the above, Qanawat association aims to establish a medical clinic that includes medical consultations and services in addition to medication free of charge, and spreading awareness of health culture in the targeted areas and surroundings; and this is the main objective of the project.
Expected outcomes:
- Provide free medical services and offer free medication with referral system.
- Raise health levels in society -targeted groups-.
- Distributing health raising awareness sessions brochures.

3 - Needs

The whole area with a population of over 35,000 has no medical care points. The population have highly vulnerable segments including women dependent families. Therefore, the establishment of a medical clinic for the provision of free medical services and free medication with referral system is a necessity. Taking into consideration the high costs of transportation and difficulties for the patients for proceeding to far medical centers. Source: Survey from Qanawat Team Work

4 - Implementation of activities

1. Renting the clinic and installing medical equipment. 2. Rehabilitation of the center in terms of furniture and equipment 3. Implementation of a transparent and fair selection mechanism for qualified medical and administrative staff 4. Opening the center and providing all medical consultations and referrals 5. Follow-up work by a team of volunteers and work on evaluating the services provided and the satisfaction of the beneficiaries with them to ensure the proper progress of work 6. Spreading the culture of health care and the principles of maintaining healthy habits (awareness) by distributing paper medical awareness posts7. Evaluating work and applying feedback by publishing patient questionnaires, collecting their opinions, and setting up a complaints box at the center and hot line.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

The risk of transmission of infection from any patient to another patient, such as corona disease, for example. Risk of electrical short circuit or fire. The risk of having a large number of children accompanying patients in the waiting room. Blackouts delaying the provision of a health service, which leads to a worsening of the patient's condition, the presence of a bad clinical condition that requires immediate referral to the hospital. Withdrawal of one of the medical center staff without prior notification. Work injuries, wrong medical diagnosis of a patient. Huge crowding in the center, which leads to the transmission of diseases Carrying out unhealthy habits and spreading them among children in the area.


8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

There is a complaint box and a hot line for complaints.


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Failure of a medical device, which will affect the delay in the medical service provided to the beneficiary until the device is repaired or re-calibrated. Errors in time estimates of project phases. Exceeding project budget A request to raise the wages of some of those in charge of the project, such as a doctor or an administrator. Increase in the exchange rate, which will lead to an increase in the costs of some supplies and equipment will be addressed as follows: Contracting with a medical equipment maintenance company and agreeing with it to send the specialized engineer and provide solutions immediately. Setting a time margin to avoid delays in the event of an estimation error. In addition to the previous proposed solutions to deal with risks, there will be: 1- Several meetings will be held with the supervisory staff during planning to benefit from their experiences in previous projects or projects under implementation to set a schedule on how and how to deal with risks that may occur: Mitigating/transfer risks avoiding the occurrence of risks 2- Contingency plan: A table in which there is an emergency plan will be relied upon to determine the mechanisms for dealing with each problem
