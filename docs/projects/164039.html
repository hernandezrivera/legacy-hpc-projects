Project Summary

This program aims to increase the resilience and livelihoods of the most vulnerable populations in Aleppo and Idleb, .
The first main objective is improving the food security of 800 vulnerable households’ women and men from host communities and IDPs by providing them agriculture inputs of summer vegetable crops, fertilizer, pesticide, and cash for irrigation. While the second objective is ensuring the source of income for 200 female-headed households through
support each HH with four sheep and vouchers of fodder and veterinary service. In accordance with the latest FSL report on priorities, responding to the need assessment that the RF team conducted in east Idleb and north Aleppo. This project aims to reduce the financial burden of vulnerable households and have access to one done through providing.
AL Resala Foundation (RF) technician team will follow up on the progress of agriculture steps through daily visits to beneficiaries’ land to provide the required technical information, which can enhance plants grow. in addition to providing awareness sessions for all 200 beneficiaries, which aims to enhance their knowledge about good agriculture practices, the best irrigation techniques and food processing of final products. Moreover, the availability of vegetable products which are the basic daily needs of HHs in
the targeted area will reactivate the local market and improve access, especially for women. Therefore, that mitigate the burden and risk of movement to farthest areas where the markets exist.
The second output of this project is increasing the resilience of 100 vulnerable femaleheaded households of host communities and IDPs with high priority to women and GBV survivor. Each beneficiary will receive (Four sheep, Fodder)

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumptions: We assume that none of the selected beneficiaries will leave their landsand all of them will use the provided assets in land service. Risk1: - This amount of cashfor irrigation may be used for another purpose. Mitigation1: - Distribute the cash irrigationvouchers after agriculture vouchers, which means verifying form beneficiariescommitment toward received items. Risk2: - Selling the voucher or replacing it withmoney from the supplier. Mitigation2: - RF will add penalty terms to contract if supplierre-procured or paid money to beneficiaries - Field team will present at redeem centers tomonitor the process. - We will apply the secret beneficiary’s mechanism to test thevendor’s commitment. Assumptions: We assume that none of the selected beneficiarieswill leave their places and all of them will use the received fodder to feed the receivedsheep. Risk1: - Selling all received sheep or some of them to the supplier or otherpersons. Mitigation1: -select the in need beneficiaries and raise their awareness aboutthe importance of keeping sheep and take care with them. -None distributing the fodderfor those beneficiaries who sold sheep. -Retrieve the remaining quantity of sheep fromthose who sold some of them and re-distribute to new beneficiaries. Risk2: - Selling thevoucher or fodder or replacing it with money from the supplier. Mitigation2: - RF will addpenalty terms to contract if supplier re-procured or paid money to beneficiaries - Fieldteam will present at redeem centers to monitor the process. - We will apply the secret beneficiary’s mechanism to test the vendor’s commitment - The beneficiaries who willsell the first batch of fodder, will not receive on the other batches, while the remainquantities will be divided equally to beneficiaries who did not sell.
