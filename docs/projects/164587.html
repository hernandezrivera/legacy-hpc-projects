Project Summary

The project is to fill the gaps in the FSL sector through Improving the food security status of assessed food insecure people through support self-reliance of affected households by protecting and building productive assets and restoring or creating income generating opportunities to save and sustain lives in the north of Syria through distribution of agricultural inputs,such as seeds,fertilizer,pesticide and equipment; and related training.
We aim to:
- Support wheat cultivation by distribute agricultural inputs,seeds,fertilizer,pesticide and equipment; and provide related training to 1,000 farmers
- Provide households with Negative Livelihoods Coping Strategy with Potato cultivation inputs (seeds,fertilizer,pesticide and equipment; and provide related training) to 2500 farmers
- Provide households with Negative Livelihoods Coping Strategy with olive cultivation inputs (seeds,fertilizer,pesticide and equipment; and provide related training) to 1000 farmers
- Support small-scale food production (horticulture,market gardens) by providing agriculture inputs to cultivate vegetables (home gardens and school gardens) to 2,500 farmers

4 - Needs

Based on results from a countrywide household assessment,an estimated 7.9 million people in Syria are food insecure and an additional 1.9 million people are at risk of food insecurity.” Some 70 per cent of the assessed households reported that food accounted for more than 65 per cent of their expenditure.More than 37 per cent of food insecure households reported having outstanding debt and 57 per cent of food insecure people had adopted negative coping strategies Of the surveyed households who were IDPs or returnees 82 percent reported having been displaced for more than 12 months.Of the displaced population,more than 760,889 people living in last resort camps are of particular concern to the sector as they have limited access to a diversified diet or income opportunities.With at least 69 per of the people living below $1.90 a day. Wheat production estimated to be at 2.2 million tons remains lower than the pre-crisis level.About 216,000 hactares of wheat and other crops such as barley,fruit trees,vegetables and grazing areas (17 percent of national areas planted) were affected by a number of shocks including floods,fires,hailstorms,high temperatures and plant diseases and pests specifically yellow rust.The recent operation peace spring in North East Syria could disrupt production in about 370,000 hectares returning to more than 36,700 farmers in Raqqa and Hasakeh.To date 60 percent of the above area is planted with rainfed wheat.The remaining 40 percent are unlikely to be planted owing to disruption of production activities potentially impacting on national food production Women and men each play key roles in food production,“however lacking ownership of,access to,and control of livelihood assets will negatively affect women’s food production.The lower production reduces not only women’s potential income,but also the availability of food for household consumptionHNO 2020

5 - Implementation of activities

Beyaz Eller will provide agriculture inputs(wheat,olive,potato,and vegetables' seeds and Fertilizers)Support small-scale food production (horticulture,market gardens) by providing agricultural inputs to cultivate vegetables(home gardens and school gardens)to vulnerable beneficiaries (people with disabilities,women and girls caregivers,elders,women headed HHs,IDPs,Poor members of hosting communities and young girls and boys in schools)-Efforts will be made to ensure there are no duplications or gaps.Cooperation with local councils,community leaders and the FSL cluster will take place via field meetings,e-mails-setting distribution plan from pre-agreed distribution points within communities.Distribution points will be chosen in coordination with the local council and will be walking distance from the homes of beneficiaries,.For beneficiaries who are not able to access the distribution points BE will provide door step delivery-Women and men of different ages will participate in awareness sessions and training,they will receive needed support monthly,Beyaz Eller will make sure all beneficiaries will have equal chances to access to food and awareness activities

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

In general,women are in charge of food selection,preparation and childcare. If they generate an income, this is most likely to be spent on food and the needs of their children.Although women play a crucial role within the subsistence agricultural sector,their work in producing food is often undervalued.Although women play an important role in the food security at the household and communal level, their potential capacity and contribution to a sustainable rural development and food security are often not recognized and due to existing inequalities between men and women not explored.Food access refers to access by individuals to adequate resources required for appropriate foods needed for a nutritious diet. Food distribution within the household can vary according to one’s gender.Although food may be available within the household,but due to hierarchy within the family,may be not as accessible by women compared to men. Also differences in risk and vulnerability between men and women can affect the accessibility to food. Risks-SEA by humanitarian workers male dominated,gate keepers and potential other beneficiaries-Risk of violence from beneficiaries among themselves/with humanitarian workers when huge onset occurring-Diversion of assets-Access to land/assets-Risk to secondary rights holders female HHMitigating-Establish a feedback mechanism-Recruit female staff and train them-Train staff on code of conduct-All staff contracts to include PSEA-Ensure distribution is well-organized.identification of sites/environment ; set up several distribution spots on sites-Staff work/support the local councils in preparing the beneficiaries list-Meetings with community leaders/gate keepers on targeting criteria etc-Set community feedback mechanism-Use well trained and gender balanced outreach team-Community based beneficiary selection-Integration of food assistance and livelihood projects-CB monitoring-Asset monitoring by CP

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Large numbers of people in need compared to the provided services,to avoid this risk,Beyaz Eller will select the beneficiaries depending on a criteria,and this selection criteria will be shared with local community,and the leaders of the community will participate in this process.Changes in prices in markets,so to mitigate this risk Beyaz Eller will buy the materials under fixed price condition with traders,delayed delivery of seeds from the preferred date of cultivation,finance issues or delay remittances in these cases the mitigation measures will be replacing the crop with other types of strategic crops that have not yet been completed and thus ensure that farmers receive the appropriate agricultural support for them.
