- Carrying out water trucking operations in areas that suffer from a shortage of (potable) water.
- Provision and supply of cleaning and disinfection materials
- Distribution of family hygiene kits
- Solid waste management (including workers and collection by trucks).

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The city of Al-Hasakah has been inhabited by nearly a million people. For five years, they have not had access to clean water. They buy the value of water with their income from water sellers in tankers. They suffer great suffering and depletion of resources.Syria continues to face a multitude of challenges that make it one of the world’s most complex humanitarian and protection emergencies. After twelve years of crisis, most Syrians live in a context of continuing humanitarian decline with 15.3 million people requiring humanitarian assistance, including 2.1 million IDPs living in last-resort IDP sites, with needs that keep increasing across all sectors. The country still has the largest number of internally displaced people in the world: 6.8 million people, and the largest number of people in need since the beginning of the crisis.
