Project Summary

The project aims to ensure that conflict-affected children and adolescents receive quality and conflict sensitive educational services to enhance their resilience. Secondly, communities have increased capacity to participate in school development and risk reduction planning and demand equitable access to conflict-sensitive quality education for all children and adolescents.
Although the Nigerian Government has made concerted efforts towards addressing the current challenges, such as the development of the universal basic education amongst other things, girls still lag behind in terms of education.
Many courses are being stereotypes as male-only domain while girls continue to struggle with courses in the social sciences as against the sciences. The completion rate for girls is also lower than that for boys due to societal expectations and stereotyping of girls to get married early before completion of school
The major risk factors behind these disparities are:
-Gender inequality and discrimination towards girls and less value placed on girls‟ education . Families prefer to invest in boys‟ education rather than girls
-Girls also lack confidence in themselves and awareness about their rights. These in addition, to lack of skills and low aspirations to actively participate in decision-making both at home, in school and the community
- Poverty leading to early marriages
-Violence towards girls e.g. sexual violence
- Lack of social protection for poor households
- High cost of education, (uniforms, hygiene kits, etc.
- Lack of Gender sensitive school curricula
- Teacher attitudes which push girls into humanities subjects instead of science-based or technical courses)
-Lack of appropriate school facilities such as separate girls‟ toilets
-Violence against girls (VAWG) perpetuated at schools by teachers and students which affects the well being, confidence and participation of girls in schools
-Inadequate pedagogical skills and professional teachers
- Limited involvement of girls in leadership activities and school governance
The intervention will target the communities living in affected communities in Borno,Yobe and Adamawa States
A total number of 30,00 beneficiaries from various schools and the host communities are targeted
The above target also includes: women, men, boys, girls, teachers, community leaders, and others

The intervention targets 30,000 people (women, men, boys. girls etc) within the target communities, IDPs, students, teachers, government officials, faith-based and community-based organisations, community leaders, security personnel, and the general public

Activities

The project will bring about change through the following:1- Provide educational materials and hygiene kits for vulnerable students 2-Train teachers and students on psycho-social education and support3-improved teaching skills for teachers4-Provide WASH aggregated facilities for students5-Construct toilet blocks and water tanks for easy access to water and hygiene needs6-Raise awareness of the importance of good hygiene (general and menstrual hygiene)
