Project Summary

NRC will ensure children and youth are able to exercise their right to quality, relevant education in a protective environment in NE Nigeria through Education in Emergencies and Youth programming. Vulnerable conflict-affected youth (ages 15-24 years) and out of school (OOS) primary-grades children (ages 6-14 years) will be targeted. NRC will prioritize those not accessing any kind of education opportunities who, due to conflict, have never been enrolled, have dropped out, or did not continue their education after completion of the primary cycle. At least 50% of those targeted will be female.

1 - Needs

According to the 2019 Humanitarian Overview (HNO), 7.1 million Nigerians are in need of humanitarian assistance in NE Nigeria. Of these, 2.2 million conflict-affected children and youth are in urgent need of educational services. This includes 731,000 IDPs, 640,000 returnees and 395,000 children in inaccessible areas. In inaccessible areas, many children, especially girls, are reported to be deprived of any education. IDP children are at higher risk of lacking access to education, and schools are not yet functional in several areas of IDP return. A 2018 OCHA report indicated that about 1,200 schools have been destroyed in Borno State since 2009, and are in need of rehabilitation. The destruction of large parts of affected towns, including education infrastructure, has put a strain on public services and has restricted access to formal schooling.

2 - Activities

Education in Emergencies: NRC will support conflict-affected children with Education in Emergencies services, including activities from the Better Learning Programme (BLP), NRC’s signature PSS toolkit. NRC will also provide formal school rehabilitation in communities where lacks of safe classrooms prevents access, including construction of latrines and WASH facilities. Where no formal schooling exists, NRC will provide construction of Temporary Learning Spaces (TLS), including provision of necessary Teaching and Learning Materials (TLM). Youth Education & TrainingNRC will promote youth education and empowerment through Youth Education Pack (YEP)a range of activities, providing various pathways to lifelong learning and ensuring that youth can live and work in dignity. YEP activities will include life skills, psychosocial support (PSS), basic literacy and numeracy, and vocational training. Activities will be youth-led, with beneficiaries conducting Youth-Led Labour Market Assessments to determine the best locations and activities for programming. To ensure active participation by young mothers and youth charged with the care of their younger family members, childcare services will be provided using the NRC Operational Handbook for Childcare Spaces.
