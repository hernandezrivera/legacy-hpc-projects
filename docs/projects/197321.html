Project Summary

Aleppo city, in particular, suffers from various issues related to security and services. These issues left most bakeries closed and/or damaged which in turn negatively impacted the affected population through:
1- Many of them became unemployed and lost their source of income, which made them unable to fulfill their families’ basic needs.
2- Significant lack of basic needs in the market, such as bread, which caused a spike in the prices.
3- Bakeries overcrowding as some of them are closed and/or damaged.
As a result of the above-mentioned reasons, the focus was on assessing the need for bread within the largest eastern neighborhood of Aleppo governorate, Hanano neighborhood and its nearby neighborhoods (Ard Al-Hamra, Sheikh Khader, Al-Haidariya, Jabal Badro).
AL-TA’ALOUF Association seeks to implement a project in the eastern neighborhoods of Aleppo governorate that aims to enhance the services capacity and infrastructure which in turn would encourage the safe return of IDPs to their residence by rehabilitating the bakery in Aleppo and equipping it with the necessary machinery and equipment for production to provide bread to the residents of Hanano and the surrounding areas. This project comes in line with the second allocation priority which is strengthening the resilience of the most critically affected people in the areas with high needs and poor inter-sector response and reach, improving the protection environment, tailored food assistance to the vulnerable families, livelihood opportunities, and sustained access to critical basic services.
In this project, we plan to provide the bakery with new production line and rehabilitate a section of the bakery to accommodate the new production line. In addition to providing it with the appropriate equipment to produce and provide bread. This in turn would facilitate obtaining bread for the people of the neighborhood, rid them of the long waiting hours as well as the high costs they pay to buy bread from the black market, and provide sustainable job opportunities for more than 20 workers. Moreover, the Syrian Establishment of Bakeries is committed to supplying the bakery with flour, fuel and all necessary raw materials once the production line is installed and put into operation.
• The project comprises of: Project Manager, Reporter, Monitoring and Evaluation Officer, Engineers, Procurement Officer, Accountant, Photographer, and Cleaner.

3 - Needs

Hanano bakery was one of the main bakeries that served Masaken Hanano neighborhood and other neighborhoods. It included three production lines with a production capacity of 36 tons/day. This bakery was subjected to severe destruction and soon became out of order. All attempts to re-operate the bakery failed.After visiting the bakery, AL-TA’ALOUF Association team conducted FGDs with a locals to acquire a full overview whether Hanano bakery’s fulfills the area’s need. The mentioned points were as follows:1. People are forced to queue for long hours to obtain bread due to overcrowding. 2. The elderly and people with special needs suffer in obtaining bread due to the hardship of queuing for a long time. Additionally, there is no designated queue to facilitate queuing for people with special needs.3. Many families, especially the elderly and those with special needs, are forced to buy bread from the black market for an amount ranging between 2000-2500 SYP, while it is sold for 200 SYP at the bakery.5. The bakery does not achieve the minimum protection standards, as there is no canopy to protect the beneficiaries from sunlight in summer and rain in winter.Based on the points above, the priorities and objectives of the ER&L sector, the information provided by the sector coordinator, we concluded that there is an urgent need to rehabilitate Hanano bakery and provide it with the necessary equipment for bread production. Through our project, this gap can be filled.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

•AL-TA’ALOUF helps those in need on the basis of equality and impartiality, regardless of race, religion, political opinion or gender. Furthermore, AL-TA’ALOUF devotes special attention to the needs of the most vulnerable groups, such as children, women and the elderly, and strives to promote the equal rights of women and girls.•AL-TA’ALOUF is committed to providing assistance to people in need wherever they are, as taking into consideration the key humanitarian principle of “Do No Harm” by preventing and minimizing any unintended negative effects on activities that can increase PoCs physical vulnerability and psychological risks. Through our project, we will minimize protection risks through the following:• Provide bread to reduce overcrowding• Separating the men from women in bread queues by installing queue railings to reduce GBV related risks.• Installing an external canopy above the railings to protect beneficiaries from weather elements during winter and summer.• Preventing the bread distributors from selling the bread bundle at a high price and exploiting the residents’ need.

9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk 1: Local ApprovalsMitigation measures: AL-TA’ALOUF will be responsible for obtaining the needed approvals and ensure effective coordination with local authorities prior to the commencement of activities. Risk 2: Lack of competent staffMitigation measures: AL-TA’ALOUF is capable to establish a group of competent staff to fully implement the project. Risk 3: the distance between the bakery and Aleppo city, which would pose a difficulty for staff transportation to their workplace.Mitigation measures: - Recruiting workers, professionals, and craftsmen from the residents of Masaken Hanano to save labour transportation costs.- Add the costs of project staff transportation. Risk 4: Fluctuating exchange rates resulting in the increase of prices of items and equipment.Mitigation measures: Purchasing the necessary items at the beginning of the project to avoid the later potential increase in prices.
