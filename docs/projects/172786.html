1 - Activities

 Revive/establish child led CPCs in all schools in the operational domains. Child led CPCs will act as watchdogs leading to early identification and resolution of child protection concerns. schools which were usually considered safe havens are no longer the same. There is reported increase in moral decay, bullying and sexual violations.  Raise awareness Child Protection and GBV in schools. Child Led CPCs will play a leading role in raising awareness in schools. Facilitate access to requisite services to survivors of SGBV. Facilitate monthly Child led CPC meetings and quarterly District CPC meetings to enhance child protection response mechanisms and strengthen referral pathway  Establish family parenting clubs and facilitate monthly meetings to discuss curricular of Child protection and GBV. DCA will work with Department of Social Development under the Ministry of Public Service Labour and Social Welfare to form parenting clubs in communities. Members of the parenting clubs will participate in resilience building activities. Provide training to community protection structures including Faith Based Organisations on PSEA, GBV and Child Protection.

8 - Accountability to Affected People
8.1 - Describe how your project was designed in consultation with communities? How will you incorporate communities in project implementation and what feedback mechanisms do you have in place?

The project was designed in consultation with School Heads in domains where DCA is currently operating, who indicated the need for structures to continue monitoring for child protection issues in schools . DCA is currently implementing CBT programmes in the proposed domains and have been regularly attending district protection meetings ,where the need to improve child protection response is a topical issue .DCA already has robust complaints and feedback mechanism which encompasses help desk , toll free number and a designated Complaints Handling Officer who ensure that all complaints are recorded and follow ups are done . DCA will work with community structures which include CPCs, CCWs, District Development Committees, Disabled persons organisations and other stakeholders to ensure maximum representation of the communities.1. Prioritizing safety & dignity and avoiding causing harmThrough prevention of unintended negative effects of the intervention which can increase people’s vulnerability to both physical and psychosocial risks. 2. Meaningful AccessThis can be achieved by arranging people’s access to assistance and services and paying particular attention to individuals and groups who may be particularly vulnerable or having difficulty accessing assistance and services. 3. AccountabilityBy setting up appropriate mechanisms through which affected populations measure the adequacy of interventions, and address concerns and complaints. This can be achieved through a complaint handling system such as Toll-Free lines, Help Desks, or suggestion boxes. 4. Participation and EmpowermentThe project will support the development of self-protection capacities and assist people to claim their rights to shelter, food, water and sanitation, health, and education. Sensitization Awareness campaigns, distribution of IEC materials will be advantageous in ensuring that community members receive adequate information.

9.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

DCA will priorities resilience building activities that will have positive impact on the environment . Nutrition Gardening – DCA will encourage use of compost manure in nutrition gardens Manufacturing of reusable pads- Reusable pads will reduce the amount of waste in the environment Hub based mushroom farming will be deliberately designed to consider impacts on the environment and the following are some of the considerations factored in the assessment:1.Mushroom farming aids in minimizing waste accumulation through use of waste for farming.2. Absorption of toxins through the mushroom life cycle.3.Provision of food (alternative source of protein)4. Mushroom farming provides manure for organic farming which in turn reduces the use of fertilizer that destructs natural ecosystems.5. Bio conservation- the land is not degraded since mushroom farming is done in a room not on direct ground.6.Water conservation by use of wastewater for watering other plants.7.There is integrated food production system where the spent mushroom can be used to grow other crops 8. Soil conservation by use of spent substrate as mulch and for water
