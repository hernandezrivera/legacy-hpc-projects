Project Summary

The main objectives and activities of the project:
1. Ensure that all victims of gender-based violence have access to legal services by providing legal aid.
2- Raising the community’s awareness through messages and awareness programs presented through local and state radio stations and adopting different awareness methods.
3- Improving the quality of services by training service providers for survivors of gender-based violence.
4- Training of paralegal
Target areas:
The project targets six states as follows: (Darfur and Khartoum states, River Nile state, Red Sea, Gezira, Gedaref, Blue Nile, South and West Kordofan)
It also works as a cooperating organization in the field of providing legal aid services to, women victims of violence and children, among a team of distinguished lawyers and human rights defenders. It also works on community legal awareness on the previous issues.
She has years of experience in the legal field of gender-based violence.
It is also a part of the monthly GBV Sub-Sector meetings and has a thorough knowledge of GBV.

1 - Needs

According to the recent study (Voices from Sudan) carried out by the United Nations Population Fund and the Unit to Combat Violence against Women, we have designed our program according to the results of the study. We will target (areas affected by violence) by providing legal aid to survivors of violence based on social awareness, in addition to raising awareness on Gender-based violence focusing on vulnerable people such as children, domestic workers, people with disabilitiesAccording to a study of Voices from Sudan, the most areas that we will target are: (Darfur and Khartoum states, River Nile state, Red Sea, Gezira, Gedaref, Blue Nile, South and West Kordofan). There are higher rates of domestic violence, sexual violence, forced marriage, and circumcision in these states. female
