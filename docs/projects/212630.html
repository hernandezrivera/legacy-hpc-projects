Project Summary

Through the proposed interventions HIHFAD is planning to continue the provision of general and specialized health services through a network of health facilities into a comprehensive modality including Primary health services with 6 primary health centers and secondary health services with 5 hospitals 3 Maternity and Pediatric Hospitals and 2 general hospitals. The mentioned facilities are also putting into consideration full access to people with disabilities through the prepared infrastructure of those facilities to ensure access without any difficulties. In addition, HIHFAD will continue operating 3 rehabilitation centers to enhance the quality of life of people with disabilities by the provision of physiotherapy sessions and a solid referral mechanism to other healthcare facilities if needed. Adding to the previous interventions HIHFAD focuses from a comprehensive perspective on providing specialized health services connected to the hospitals and outpatient services such as the dialysis unit at Armanaz Hospital and operating a Tuberculosis center which is the only of its type in Idlib governorate and prosthetics /orthotics Center to serve people with amputations.
HIHFAD has been implementing MHPSS activity since 2017 in NW Syria through IntegrateMHPSS services into health facilities including providing mhGAP consultations and distributing psychotropics through 15 mhGAP-trained doctors working in health fixed facilities.
Also, Individual Psychosocial Support sessions are provided using structured interventions (problem management plus PM+, thinking healthy program) through well-trained PSWs who have the psychological academic background working in fixed facilities and rehabilitation mobile teams as a part of a comprehensive rehabilitation approach including 3 specialties (physiotherapy, nursing, and psychosocial support).
All project staff will receive PSEA sensitization training sessions before the activity's implementation, and staff will work to share information with the beneficiaries during the project activities implementation about PSEA and reporting channels/mechanism, the awareness materials including child-friendly materials will be provided.
HIHFAD Developed PSEA IEC materials to be used in health facilitates, In addition to dissemination of the reporting channels as posters with all distributed items in the project, in addition to integrated Complaints against PSEA in complaint leaflets, a hotline number specific to PSEA cases, where this number is held with the Female PSEA Focal points. HIHFAD field PSEA focal points received advanced training sessions about complaints handling mechanisms to follow up according to PSEA network SOPs. Furthermore, a PSEA Field Prevention and Mitigation Checklist on the project sites will be implemented by the M&E Team periodically.
The field Monitoring and Evaluation (MEAL) team, consisting of both male and female members, is responsible for collecting information on project activities, assessing performance quality, gathering beneficiaries' feedback, and sharing reports with the Turkey MEAL team.
Data collection and indicator measurement processes are designed to be comprehensive, with indicators measured using disaggregated data, including different gender and age groups. The sample size would be calculated using a 95% confidence level and a margin error of 5 % of the target BNFs. All information will be collected and submitted digitally through the KOBO toolbox.
For health activities, Health Information Systems (HIS) will be utilized as sources of verification, in addition to field visits to health facilities.
During our visits to health facilities, patient exit interviews will be conducted to determine the appropriateness of services and satisfaction levels, considering the diversity of beneficiaries by gender, age, and vulnerability.Additionally,we will offer continuous and advanced learning opportunities to maintain a high level of efficiency and ongoing development
