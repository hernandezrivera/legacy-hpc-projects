Project Summary

The project is designed to target 6500 MAM children including PLW in Jere and Mafa LGAs of Borno state to address critically poor nutrition conditions moderately acutely malnourished children under five years old and pregnant and lactating women (PLW), severely food insecure households with poor hygiene practices and behaviors. Also, Sirri Care Foundation will target to provide hygiene education aimed at behavior change. Strengthen Community members’ capacity to carry out family MUAC screening and self-referrals for sustainability. The project will collaborate and engage care givers and community members through participatory IYCF in line with the nutrition sector guidelines for management of child acute malnutrition, while respecting COVID-19 protocols and enhance GBV risk mitigation through the dissemination of COVID-19 and GBV messages.

1 - Needs

The prolonged conflict situation in the north east has made it almost impossible for the populations to access farm land for agricultural activities to build their resilience. This has had direct impact on the nutrition levels of the affected population. The situation has been exacerbated by the outbreak of COVID-19 pandemic that has brought negative impact on the overall socioeconomic situation as well as increasing insecurity. According to Global acute malnutrition (GAM) levels in children aged 6-59 months is on the increase across the BAY states with Borno state rates above the emergency threshold at 13.6 per cent as at September 2020 (HNO, 2020).Also, the IPC Acute Malnutrition analysis 2020 indicates that, inadequate breastfeeding, poor food consumption patterns and high prevalence of diarrheal, lack of attitude towards good hygiene practices and culturally-tailored believes of community members on children feeding are the immediate causes of acute malnutrition in Borno state with several cases of children under 5 MAM, and SAM. The Nutrition sector need analysis for 2020 indicates that, a total number of 1,531,882 persons are in need. 613,909 of this number in Borno state. In Mafa, the total number of MAM is 4002 against SAM 2452 and PLW 5568 while in Jere, MAM 19059 against SAM 26819and PLW 43114. It can be observed that the rate of malnutrition among pregnant and lactating women (PLW) is higher than that of children MAM and SAM, thus, there is need for the prevention of malnutrition targeting PLW and children under 5. To prevent the children under 5 MAM, there is need to reach vulnerable groups (0-59), (6-59 months), 12-59 months, 6-24 months, PLW with multiple micro-nutrient supplementation. In order influence behavioral change and food consumption attitudes, within the communities, there is need to form groups of mothers to mothers and fathers to fathers to strengthen their capacities on IYCF good practices (cooking and eating demonstration). There is also need to reach new mothers and caregivers with COVID-19 and hygiene promotion key messages.

2 - Activities

Act1: Promote optimal Infant and young child feeding (IYCF) practices through community-based support groups. Act 2 Formation of mothers to mothers groups and fathers to fathers groups. Act3 dissemination of COVID-19 and hygiene key messages and counseling on IYCF good practices targeting new mothers and caregivers. Act 4 Awareness rising on exclusive breastfeeding and complementary/ supplementary feeding targeting PLW and caregivers. Act5 Awareness rising on GBV prevention in nutrition services Act 6 Training of hygiene promoters to reach new mothers and caregivers with hygiene promotion including exclusive breastfeeding. Act7. training of caregivers, mothers and fathers on IYCF keys messages (cooking and eating demonstration) using locally sourced food. Act8 Training of mothers to mothers groups and fathers to fathers groups on screening of malnourished children using MUAC.
