Project Summary

In the first three months of 2021 the number of people in need of urgent humanitarian assistance in Ethiopia increased from 19 to 23.5 million, compared to the end of 2020 (HNO).
Conflicts, desert locust invasion, cyclical climatic shocks including floods and droughts, as well as the socioeconomic impact of COVID-19 are the key triggers of increasing humanitarian needs. Ethiopia ranked second in the International Crisis Group’s list of conflicts to monitor in 2021and 18th out of 191 countries for its exposure to hazards, vulnerability, and lack of coping capacity (2021 INFORM).
Since July 2020, attacks by unidentified armed groups (UAGs) have cumulatively displaced more than 300,000 people in Metekel.
In December 2020, the Ethiopian Human Rights Commission reported that about 500 people lost their lives in the last five months, including 207 individuals being killed in one single day. The violence in Metekel included burning of houses, casualties and other serious violations of human rights.
Due to the persistent insecurity, limited humanitarian assistance is being provided; according to the regional Disaster Risk Management Commission (DRMC) there are huge gaps in the provision of food, health supplies, water supply, shelter/NFIs and protection.
The Amhara Regional Disaster Preparedness and Food Security Programme Office reported that more than 50,000 displaced people from Metekel Zone are currently living in Chagni Ranch collective site in Awi Zone. Recent IDPs returns are taking place toward Benishangul Gumuz region
This project will provide effective services in Education and Protection reaching 2,350 people in need living in Gigel Beles town Metekel Zone, Benishangul Gumuz Region.
- Education: Imagine1day will Enhance emergency-affected school aged boys’ and girls’ access to equitable, safe, and inclusive education by establishing temporary learning spaces and implementing Accelerated School readiness and Accelerated Learning Programs for the IDP and host community children in Metekel Zone.
- Protection: The planned protection activities will be contributing to the cluster objectives one by ensuring provision of safe, accessible and inclusive Protection services for crises affected people, especially vulnerable groups such as women, children and people with disability/ reduced mobility, are identified, referred, and addressed, with functional complaints and feedback mechanisms in place.
Imagine1day will identify existing protection risks, GBV/CP identification and case management; Mental Health and Psychosocial Support (MHPSS); GBV risk mitigation and awareness. In order to strengthen humanitarian actors on protection mainstreaming a capacity building training will be provided on basic protection principles and the prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse (PSEA) the service mapping will be conducted for the referral service for those who needs multi-sectoral needs.
