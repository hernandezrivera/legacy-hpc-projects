Project Summary

This is a humanitarian health project in an emergency, fragile and low capacity context. The purpose of the project is to ensure that no child dies from a preventable cause, and all children reach their full potential in health and well-being in the 8 targeted districts. The strategy emphasizes the importance of multi-sector approaches to enhance child development and address underlying causes and determinants of poor health outcomes. The project involves empowering communities by building capacity of local community health actors to engage for social and behavior change, strengthening targeted community health system by building capacity of management and health providers in emergency preparedness and resilience. They will help to sustain progress and to increase the resilience of both delivery systems and communities to absorb and recover from external shocks; the project will also advocate for every child rights to health in the affected communities through data capture, evidence generation and use, evidence will be based on quantitative (household/facility survey and birth registration) and qualitative data sources (focus groups and interview)
The project involves training and working together with community health actors that is 150 community health mobilizers, 40women group leaders/ 30 youths for life group leaders, 30 nurses/skilled personnel,8CBOs, and 4focal point for communication change social norms that have implications for health, that is, newborn care, household hygiene practices, prevention and management of epidemic prone diseases; engage with health care providers, to reduce the stigma around certain health conditions especially HIV/AIDS, contraception, EWARS, monthly mobile clinics.
Promote community dialogue and education including regarding the rights to health of all pregnant and lactating women and children, and equip them with clear action steps in relation to immunization and antenatal consultation; project will used community health actors to develop compelling and contextual messages to promote prevention and care seeking behaviors.
4. MAIN OBJECTIVES :
The aim of this emergency health intervention is to contribute to decreased morbidity and mortality rates among IDPs and their host communities in the affected districts in the northwest region of Cameroon. This will be by improving access to basic curative and preventive health care services for vulnerable IDPs and host through provision of primary and secondary sexual and reproductive, mental health services, GBV targeting the most vulnerable women and children.
5. SPECIFIC OBJECTIVES
• Strengthen capacity of 10 project staff on specific emergency health care intervention by UNICEF/WHO team (malaria, PMTCT, EWARS, pneumonia, Mental disease (stress, depression, post-traumatic stress), diarrhea and referrals of GBV cases and unaccompanied children) for 4 days
• Maintaining emergency primary and secondary health care services through provision of basic equipment, drugs, medical supplies, essential package of reproductive health services (MISP) equipment and supplies including those to maintain PMTCT and psychosocial care through community-based mechanisms.
• Supporting functioning health facilities in IDP locations, and increasing provision of care via mobile medical units in remote or underserved pockets of 8 health Districts in the NW region of Cameroon
• Empowering communities. The project involves training and working together with community health actors that is 150 community health mobilizers, 40women group leaders/ 30 youths for life group leaders, 30 nurses/skilled personnel,8CBOs, and 4focal point to engage for social and behavior change;
• All stakeholders obtained feedback on project results (successes and challenges)

Besoins / Needs

Improved access to basic curative and preventive health care services for vulnerable internally displaced and conflict affected populations in targeted areas through provision of primary and secondary, sexual and reproductive, GBV and mental health services targeting the most vulnerable women and children. - Consultation of children 0-5yrs for 3 killer diseases (Malaria, diarrhea and acute respiratory infection and PLW - Treatment of children 0-5yrs and PLW - Distribution of vitamins, deworming drugs and malaria drugs - Referrals - Health education and promotion messages - Identify IDPs under trauma, stress and provide them with psychosocial support Improving access to sexual reproductive health services and assisted delivery - Provision of new born kits, delivery kits - Providing ANC cards - Identify GBV cases and refer to the necessary partners - Provision of IPT, Fansidar, Iron and Folic acid - Provision of Vitamin A to postpartum mothers Improve epidemiological surveillance/ Vaccination coverage - Strengthen a disease EWARN (early warning) surveillance and response system based on a comprehensive risk assessment of communicable diseases - Identification of loss to follow up children - Vaccination of children during mobile clinics - Sensitization
