Project Summary

The project aim at: (i) improving access to education for boys, girls and CWD 5-18 affected by the crisis and deprived from the right of education due to conflict; (ii) supporting teachers and members of schools communities affected by the crisis to detect risky situations for students, increasing their skills in communicating with children to reduce the effects and consequences of trauma and/or stressful events.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Pemba City, Mecufi District, Metuge District 1. Outreach activities and support to Back to learning campaign: The campaign will focus on identifying IDPs school aged children and OOSC with disabilities or special needs to be enrolled, sensitizing communities on the right of CWDs to fully access their rights (including the right to education). 2. Provision of emergency learning and teaching materials together with hygiene supplies: basic stationery will be delivered to students who will be indicated by teachers and pedagogic directors of the schools according to the needs. Pre-identified criteria are: children with specific needs, newly enrolled students (not enrolled in 2021), students attending the first 3 grades of primary education, 3. Train trainers and teachers on EiE, psychosocial support and psychological first aid including provision of focus sessions with student and teachers on prevention of VAC,GBV and PSEA: improve the quality of teaching and the ability of teachers to deal with complex situations in their classrooms as children have experienced a significant academic gap, they are often traumatized and are continuously exposed to protection risks. The training enable teachers to detect risky situations for students, increase their skills in communicating with children on problems that they do not usually discuss with adults, reducing the effects and consequences of trauma and/or stressful events. 4. Children participation field test: Children in selected schools will be able to be informed on key questions that describe the quality and relevance of the ongoing response. Tools and selection criteria will be agreed with the Education Cluster on the basis of the results of the pilot test implemented by Cluster coordinators (UNICEF,SCI)

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Gender focal points and girls’ Clubs, will work together on screening, supporting and referring cases of girls and boys at risk of dropping out of school, early marriages and other forms of vulnerability. Regular meetings will be promoted to address issues of potential vulnerability and/or child protection risks.Schools’ Councils will be constantly involved through the promotion of workshops about tracking mechanisms. Finally, at district/provincial level, the involvement of the referral system and the multi-sectorial team in the screening, support and referral of cases of different risks will be put in place with the collaboration of the different actors in the area.In general, the whole project forsees the meaningful involvement of all the principal actors (local and international) that will allow a easier and smoother access to the assistance as well as the participation of the beneficiaries in all the proposed actions. In particular, institutional capacities of multi-sector teams in terms of mechanisms for identifying, supporting and following up cases will be strengthed; regular meetings for the improvement of synergiens among multi-sectorial team will be guarantees (clusters, planning sessions, joint studies and research, etc.); finally, community members will lead the planning and decision-making process, through locally established committees/structures.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

The project has been designed in consultation with communities and relevant local authorities where We World-GVC is implementing its ongoing programme. Needs and vulnerabilities have been identified along the current year with the constant envolvement of DPE, SDEJT and School Councils whose members belong to schools and to local communities. The project will therefore adopt a protection mainstreaming approach in all the components and activities envisaged. In particular, with respect to accountability, mechanisms for measuring the adequacy of interventions and collecting / responding to problems or complaints will be made available to beneficiaries.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

People with disabilities will be identified according to the following strategies:- Involvement of the multi-sector team for the definition of indicators’ criteria for the identification of people with different disabilities;- Production of an instrument for collecting data on indicators of people with disabilities, taking into account the criteria of defined indicators, with the involvement of the multi-sectorial team;- Training enumerators to collect data from people with disabilities who are both included and excluded from the formal education system;- Adaptation of the activities in order to include completely people with disabilities.
