Project Summary

The Emergency Telecommunications Cluster (ETC) supports humanitarian activities by ensuring that humanitarian actors have access to vital telecommunications services in common operational areas. These services enable them to coordinate and deliver assistance to beneficiaries more effectively and safely.
ETC will provide coordination platform and Information Management for partners, maintain, enhance, and expand the ETC services in common operational area in Syria and Turkiye. ETC will provide technical support and develop technical capacity of national IT personnel of humanitarian organizations. Given the shortage of equipment in the local market, ETC will ensure sufficient contingency stock of equipment and system in the country. ETC will continue to assess the needs of humanitarian actors and partner organizations on the ground.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The summary of the main activities and objectives of the Emergency Telecommunications Cluster (ETC) in Syria and Turkiye in 2024 as follows:- Provide coordination platform and Information Management (IM) for key partners to deliver high-quality and cost-effective ETC services, such as security communication, data connectivity, power supply, and technical support, to enable and support humanitarian actors and operations across Syria.- Maintain the security communication and solar power system in common operational areas across Syria.- Upgrade the equipment and system in the main Security Operations Center (SOC) to enhance the coordination of security communication in Syria.- Upgrade the security communication system to enable interconnection and integration among the UN common operational locations.- Improve the connectivity services in selected common operational areas and ensure their high availability and redundancy.- Expand and upgrade UHF Radio Networks at targeted areas in Turkiye to support humanitarian operations into Syria.- Maintain and update the license/approval of the security communication system according to the authority’s requirement.- Conduct tailored technical capacity building workshop for targeted humanitarian personnel in Syria. - Ensure the availability of the strategic stock of equipment and system in the country to meet the immediate need in existing or new common operational areas.- Provide technical support and expertise to the entire humanitarian community related to Emergency Telecommunications.
