Project Summary

Through this HRP, IOM will provide first-line emergency services in the form of rapid needs assessment and community engagement to respond to the emergency needs of affected population. Ability to respond will be based on logistical capacity, funding, and accessibility. The IOM CCCM mobile team will develop referral pathways and early warning systems in cooperation with local authorities) to equip populations to better cope with natural hazards and mitigate against displacement.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

To demonstrate that displaced people are central to decision making that affects their own lives, including in relationship to environmental risks and disaster response, IOM will take measures to ensure a clean and healthy environment to enhance the dignity of displaced populations living in Sofala and Cabo Delgado Province. CCCM field staff will facilitate an essential engagement between IDP, host communities and government counterparts, The CCCM Cluster role will be to bring these measures to scale in other settings, whether the displacement site (camp) is urban or rural, the geographical setting, camp size, stage in camp lifecycle will influence the prioritization and technical environmental action. Furthermore, this proposed action aligns with the UN Secretary-General High-Level Panel on Internal Displacement Action Agenda “to identify concrete recommendations on how to better prevent, respond and achieve solutions to the global internal displacement crisis”. Following the release of the High-Level Panel’s report in 2021, the Secretary-General developed an “Action Agenda on Internal Displacement”, focusing on the same three areas and with a particular emphasis on durable solutions to internal displacement, in particular through development-focused approaches.IOM intends to contribute to the sectoral objectives through the following interventions:1) Continued and adapted provision of critical CCCM activities as well as critical care & maintenance activities 2) Deployment of emergency CCCM teams to deliver sustainable mobile responsespromoting self-governance, community ownership and resilience in hard-to-reachlocations following triggers at the ICCG-level.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

• Conduct Information dissemination and awareness raising to disaster-prone communities to have increased ability to prepare for and respond to natural hazards at sub-national levels.• Support national authority to establish safe Evacuation Sites for IDP movement during emergencies including evacuation plans and build basic infrastructure (lights, latrines and communal centers for selected five evacuation centers.• Strengthen human capital and capacity in provincial/district disaster management departments.• Improve strategic information management on recurrent emergencies, risks and disasters; and Revisit the scope and focus of current contingency plans• Enhance the capacity of disaster-prone communities to increase their ability to prepare for and respond to natural hazards through training and awareness raising

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

• Participatory Assessment and satisfaction surveys: regularly conduct participatory assessment and satisfaction surveys on specific aspects of living standards such as services, behaviours of the service providers including elements of procedures of reporting issues of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (SEA) and use the findings to inform decision-making.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

• Community Engagement and Consultation, - Create opportunities for community members to actively participate in decision-making processes related to AAP initiatives and encourage feedback and suggestions from the community to shape the direction of these initiatives. IOM will involve displaced community, returnees, and vulnerable members of host community in decision-making processes related to service provisions. Seek their input on improvements and changes, ensuring their voices are heard and considered in the planning, implementation, and monitoring of the services.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

In order to reach all segments of the displaced community, IOM CCCM implements an outreach CFM modality in order to adopt a proactive approach in the collection of beneficiary feedback. Community mobilizers are trained to receive cases in a sensitive manner in order to make the community member comfortable submitting feedback, complaints and requests on humanitarian services and assistance. Regular community engagement meetings are held between CCCM teams and representative governance structures, including elected community leaders, women and youth forums, as well as persons with disability committees – ensuring that any and all consultations are representative of traditionally marginalized groups. All communication and community engagement strategies, including beneficiary satisfaction surveys, will include an inclusion lens.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Through this project IOM will demonstrate that displaced people are central to decision making that affects their own lives, including in relationship to environmental risks. By piloting the CCCM checklist on clean energy and environment, IOM will help to identify environmental risks that could potentially impact the health and well-being of people in humanitarian settings and support other humanitarians to take a rights-based approach to environmental interventions in displacement sites.Through the improvement of environmental conditions, IOM will demonstrate that collaboration with environmental and development actors can lead to improved conditions in humanitarian setting. For example, it will identify where there are gaps in services or where management practices could be improved and make these changes to realize an environment that is physically, socially and culturally appropriate.
