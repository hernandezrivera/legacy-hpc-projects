Project Summary

The project aims to serve the city of Deir ez-Zor with a health dispensary providing medical consultations (obstetrics/gynecology, internal medicine, pediatrics) and therapeutic medications, in addition to 3 mobile medical clinics targeting the rural areas east and west of Deir ez-Zor governorate. The project targets 50,000 individuals from all segments of society including children, males, females, elderly, and people with disabilities of all ages who require medical intervention and treatment.
The idea of the project came from the difficult health situation that the province is suffering from, especially in remote rural areas, in addition to the lack of health centers and effective projects in this field. This shortage became apparent recently when people fled from the eastern areas of the Euphrates River due to ongoing armed conflicts there to the western areas of the Euphrates River. The few health centers with limited capabilities were unable to meet even a part of the medical and treatment needs of these residents, and they even failed to contain the infectious diseases brought by the displaced population to the host community. The health campaigns and initiatives led by associations and organizations there did not suffice to meet all the medical and health needs, despite their significant efforts, Therefore, the presence of mobile medical clinics that are regularly available, equipped with doctors and medications, is an essential necessity to significantly reduce suffering and provide immediate relief as one of the tasks of these clinics, in addition to their work in providing comprehensive medical and therapeutic services to cover all areas of Deir ez-Zor province.
The project also contributes to relieving the burden on the residents in the countryside of Deir ez-Zor province who are forced to travel long distances to access medical services and medications from health centers located in the city of Deir ez-Zor, putting significant pressure on these centers and adding extra burden on the residents, in addition to the high transportation costs and the need to spend long hours to access the necessary medical services.
The significant shortage in clinics and health centers poses a major concern for the residents, and that is why the project seeks to intervene through its health clinic and mobile clinics to save the lives of the residents and alleviate their suffering in light of the high prices of medication and transportation, as well as to relieve the pressure on the health centers operating in Deir ez-Zor city.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Project Objectives:In the context of the mentioned health situation, a series of cost-effective interventions can achieve a goal that has become a dream for many needy families in remote areas, provide them with assistance, preserve their dignity, and even save their lives.- Providing and delivering medical services and therapeutic drugs to 50,000 people in need of medical intervention in Deir ez-Zor Governorate during the project implementation period (9 months).- Providing guidance and health awareness services to 50,000 people in Deir ez-Zor Governorate during the project implementation period (9 months).Project Activities:1- Providing medical consultations and therapeutic medications to the residents of Deir ez-Zor Governorate through a health clinic located in the city of Deir ez-Zor equipped with three specialized doctors, physician assistants, a health awareness officer, in addition to an administrative staff.2- Providing medical consultations and therapeutic medications to the residents in the countryside of Deir ez-Zor Governorate within their residential areas through three mobile clinics, each equipped with a general practitioner, a physician assistant, a health awareness officer, and a data entry personnel. The entire cities, villages, and towns in the countryside of the governorate will be covered according to a specific timetable.3- Referring critical cases requiring specialized medical attention by the mobile clinics to the project's health clinic after scheduling a specific appointment and facilitating their access to the center.4- Referring cases requiring laboratory tests by the health clinic to a contracted laboratory to provide free tests for the beneficiaries.5- Disseminating health awareness messages within the health clinic and through the mobile clinics to educate the residents about proper health practices.
