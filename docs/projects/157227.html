Project Summary


Sudan face an increasing challenges of displacement and conflict of which education and protection needs among the most urgent ones. The project is designed through using a multi-sectorial interventions in a complementary manner in each locality. The goal of the project is reducing mortality among conflict affected communities by providing emergency protection and education assistance with a WASH and nutrition components in their three outcomes. It targets SD (Sharg Jabal Marra locality), BN (Damazine, Roseries, and Krmuk), SK (Abujubaiha, and Alabassiya), and WK (Alnihood). The project targets directly 33,820 conflict affected people (2,325 men, 13,525 women, 8,985 girls, 8,985 boys) in these areas to safe lives and protect them. The project is adopting a strategy targeting different gender and ages groups and using cash transfer in line with the 2019 strategic approach of HRP. This project targets 36 basic schools (14,400 pre- school and primary students) in 7 localities (7,200 girls, 7,200 boys) to increase access to inclusive and protective lifesaving education and improve the quality of education by improving learning environment and providing psycho social support to ensure protection. It consider gender, AAP, environment and nutrition mainstreaming. The planned activities for the two sectors are designed to fill the gap in the target areas where NIDAA has ongoing projects. The education activities proposed in the project are in line with the HRP activities which includes construction of TLSs, provision of WASH package, EiE training for teachers and PTAs and awareness raising for community members, nutrition support and grants (gardens and management costs). Protection is addressed in areas served by the target schools as NIDAA is adopting the Protection minimum standards in humanitarian action including GP, CP, GBV standards, principles and approaches, hence, selected activities in this project are strengthening CBPNs, awareness raising, and psycho-social support, child protection, IDTR, GBV safe referral pathways (Case management CMR, psycho-social, legal, etc) through the women safe spaces, MRE, monitoring and reporting mechanisms. The Identification of the children in need will be based on the vulnerability profile which will be done in collaboration with the existing CBCPNs, community leaders, SCCW and the NGOs operating in the area.

Needs

Education and protection needs continues to increase in particularly in the newly accessed areas such as Jebel Marra, and BN, besides the area affected with the last year flooding in WK and SK. According to the 2019 HNO the areas of Abujubaiha in SK has a population of 201,095 with people in need of protection of 13,752, and for Education of 9,752; Alabassiya locality in SK has a population of 31,174, with people in need for Protection of 7,618 and for Education 5,079; in SD state, Sharg Jaball Marra locality has a population of 14,785, with people in need for Protection of 2,189 and for Education of 1,459; in WK state, Alnihood locality has a population of 336,262, with 12,522 people in need for Protection, and 8,348 in need for Education; in BN state, Damazine Locality has a population of 317,906 and 16,241 people in need of protection and 10,827 for education; Kurmuk has 165,618, with 10,731 for protection and 7,154 for education; and Roseries 161,295 with 10,575 in need for protection and 7,050 for education. All these 7 localities are prioritized as highly in need. In WK, Alnihood locality a joint mission has visited the area to assess the need after the floods, there is 13 schools (8 for boys and 5 for girls) damaged (4,798 boys, 2,103 boys). With a great need for protection services. In Sharg Jabal Marra locality. According to the HRP, Children of displaced communities are adding pressure to existing education facilities which cannot accommodate new students due to limited space and resources. In Jebel Marra localities, access to education remains low where enrollment for basic education stands at 40 per cent on average. Moreover, many more households cannot afford schooling costs. so, children are in risk of dropping out of school, becoming exposed to additional risks and violence and child labor in particular girls. NIDAA has an on going protection project of SHF in 7 villages in SD, however the need for protection and education in the other areas is a must. People who were severely affected during conflict among the population of the target areas according to official data in Sudan. As in most conflict situations women and children are the greatest victims of suffering and GBV in Sharg Jabal Marra NIDAA has established 1 CFS and will establish 7 that needs upgrading. Also 1 women center and 2 will be established providing protection services to 43% of the population. 19% only are provided with birth certificates. Some children are separated from their families and move unaccompanied, and many of them became orphaned and/or disabled, subjected to different types of violence in particular sexual violence for girls and child recruitment for boys. The access to natural resources (wood and water)- which is scarce- put women in particular girls in the risk of being subjected to sexual violence and abuse. Although data is disputed and reporting is poor, evidence show that many women and children experienced traumatic events.

Activities

Education:1\Provision of teaching, learning and recreational materials to 36 schools for14,400 students (7,200 girls and 7,200 boys);2\Construction of 72 semi-permanent classrooms in 36 schools (8 in Alnihood,8 in sharg Jabal Marra, 4 in Abujubaiha, 4 in Alabasiya, 3 in Aldamazin, 3 in Gaisan and 3 in Alkurmk localities) for 3,600 students (1800 girls, 1800 boys);3\Provision of seating for 72 classes in 36 schools for 3,600 students (1800 girls, 1800 boys)4\Provision of 36 gender sensitive latrine for 6,480 students5\Capacity-building for teachers in EiE, PSS, Nutrition and Environment for 210 teachers in 7 localities;6\Mobilization and participation of communities and children in school-based activities such as school clubs and Nutrition, environment and GBV awareness programs (14,400 students and 720 PTA).7\Provision of schools meals in coordination with WFP school feeding program.8\School grants (cash grants) for 36 schools to support schools running costs and school farms and gardens.Protection SD,SK,WK:1\Identification, registration of 70 UASCs in the target 4 localities, girls/boys/ and children with special needs in collaboration with CBCPNs and SCCW. provision of UASCs reunification kits.2\Formation and establishment of 7 CBPNs (140 persons with 50% W) and AAP system.3\Train CBPNs targets 140 (70:70 F\M) covering child protection, IDTR, GBV safe referral pathways (psycho-social, legal, etc), MRE, M&R mechanisms.4\Train 210 volunteers (50% M) as Psycho-social and CP promoters, will be linked with CBPNs and the SMoSW and SCCW. 5\Establish 3 CFS and upgrade 4 to multipurpose community centers offering child protection services such as PSS, MRE, case management services (referrals, integrated services, child and adolescent led initiatives) Each serve 500 children (3,500 (1,750 girls 1,750 boys)6\No of 7 Women’s Safe Spaces for 7,000 W serve as case management and referral service points 7\Conduct sensitization sessions for GBV and MRE 7,000 80%W
