Project Summary

This project will contribute to improved sanitation and good hygiene practices among Vulnerable populations among IDPs and the urban poor in Afgoi district. This project will focus on the ​distribution of complete hygiene kits to 4000 households, training of 50 community workers on handwashing with soap, good hygiene and proper sanitation and training of MOH staff and a selection of a cross-section of IDPs on handwashing with soap, proper hygiene, sanitation, and cholera prevention.

Project Activities

1. Distribution of complete hygiene kits (2400g Soap, 35 pieces of 10 strip Aqua tabs, 20L Jerry Can, 20L Bucket, Sanitary Pack, and 100 Water Purifiers per family) to 4000 families.2. Training of 50 Community Workers on WASH Cluster approved good handwashing with soap and proper hygiene, sanitation and cholera prevention.3. Conduct daily, weekly and monthly awareness-raising and community hygiene promotion at the facility level and within communities​.

Mitigation Measures

1. Ensure the location and access routes to WASH services are safe. QRCS will Identify central locations and bring services closer to vulnerable populations in IDPs and host communities. For example, QRCS will use mobile outreach teams to provide services to populations living further away from static WASH facilities to reduce their risk of exposure to protection issues.2. Consult women, men, boys, girls, persons with disabilities, chronically ill, older persons, pregnant and lactating women, and marginalized persons to collect accurate information about their specific needs and preferences for WASH center location, design, and services.3. Ensure all health staff, implementing partners and volunteers working with affected populations understand, sign and adhere to a Code of Conduct stating their commitment to respect and foster humanitarian standards and the rights of beneficiaries, including the confidentiality of patients.4. Ensure all staff, implementing partners and volunteers in direct contact with patients, health staff and beneficiaries have received training in the prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse (PSEA).5. Engage the community and committee representative to play an active role in identifying solutions and in the decision-making processes that affect them, so as to promote a sense of ownership, build their self-esteem and improve the relevance and sustainability of the response.6. QRCS will ensure that the ​distribution of humanitarian support is done in a safe space and location with adequate security that does not compromise the safety of beneficiaries as much as possible. On Disability inclusion: -QRCS will ensure people with disabilities are actively sought, mobilized, and engaged. Especially, where available, disability advocacy groups, organizations, and networks owned, led, and run by people with disabilities will be engaged to recommend qualified candidates for suitable staff positions. They will also be engaged in the registration of people with disabilities so that the exercise can be done in a dignified manner and without exposing them to risk.
