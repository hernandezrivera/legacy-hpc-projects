Project Summary

The project will address community needs in camps in Al-Dana sub-district camps in Idlib governorate. The need emerging for integrated protection interventions, women's protection & empowerment interventions.
This project is designed on a community-based approach by involving beneficiaries in the design phase, involving communities in awareness campaigns for IDPs in camps,
empower women to start up their small businesses to serve and address the needs of their communities. The total direct beneficiaries of the project are 800 women and girls. Coordination is already established with the management of the camp based on the MoU already signed between POINT and local authorities. Further coordination will be with NGOs and CBOs working in the camp.
The project is drawn according to our 6 years of expertise working in camps, empowering women and youth, developing protection service providers and humanitarian workers' quality and impact, and enhancing women's skills to manage women's centers and lead activities in Sarmada camps. POINT has established more than xx women and PWDs committees in the targeted camps in Dana based on community participation and a volunteering approach. The committees were led by women.
The project aims are:
1. Providing protection services of PSS, GBV, and referral pathways for women and women with disabilities. The main purpose of these activities is to support them restore their balance psychologically and mentally and help GBV and PSEA survivors overcome trauma impacts.
2. Equipping women and women with a disability with vocational, and life skills, starting up small business training, and mini-grants to implement those businesses, in order to restore and enhance their dignity by positioning their selves positively in the community as active members and decreasing their dependency on their husbands or relatives.
3. Participating in declining child labor by decreasing women's reliance on their children as major or minor income sources.
4. Raising community awareness of women, women with disabilities, and young girls on several topics (including but not limited to) parenting skills principles, early marriages, women's rights, and many more to increase their resilience.
making protection monitoring for each camp.
All of that will be achieved by:
a. Hire GBV, PSEA, and PSS referral pathways specialists for the 2 women's centers. and refer health and nutrition cases to related sector service providers.
b. Conduct awareness sessions led by women, women with disabilities, and young girls on PSS and GBV.
c. Make 10 protection monitoring surveys every month in each location.
d. Provide vocational, skills training for women, women with disabilities, and young girls in the two centers
e. Provide women, women with disabilities, and young girls based with support and information about starting their small business based on a curriculum approved by International Labor Organization ILO
f. Award 100 women with mini-grants and follow-up, coaching sessions to implement and sustain their established small businesses.

8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

POINT is strongly committed to protecting children and women from abuse, a commitment that is reflected in their Child Protection and PSEA Policies and is a member of PSEA network and follows the guidelines. Gender is mainstreamed in the design of proposed project, ensuring that the needs of women are considered and properly addressed. During the beneficiaries’ selection phase, specific emphasis will be put on women-headed households and their specific vulnerability to protection concerns and reduced access to employment opportunities due to their gender, ensuring women have equal access to the provided services. POINT will closely monitor protection risks throughout the implementation period and will ensure coordination with Protection Cluster partners.distributing PSEA brochures and contact details of the PSEA network to ensure in addition to explain the ways of contacting with the PSEA focal point in case of any PSEA situation happened to ensure that all beneficiaries are aware how to contact with PSEA focal points POINT trains its field staff to safely identify and refer protection concerns to the correct unit(s) and external actors to reduce risks and support safe and dignified access for all affected populations. Ensuring that staff members are trained on following do not harm principles in interventions and signed a code of conduct, receive PSEA training, and have a reporting system


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Protection risk: There is a risk that the number of women engaged in the projects is less than targeted. Likelihood: Medium. Impact: High Mitigating measures: 1. Ensure our community outreach workers mobilize project communities and explained the effects of planned activities. 2. increase coordination with camp management, local authorities, other CBOs, and NGOs working in the same locations to call women to participate. Protection risk: military clashes within AL-Dana preventing the center from operating. Mitigationmeasures: 1. Suspend activities till peace is recovered. 2. coordinate with POINT programs to resume providing services in nearby safe areas once safe is taking a long time to be restored POINT has experience in successfully delivering various kinds of projectdespite the ongoing conflict. Additionally, we have designed security plans for the team and participants according to different scenarios. Our primary priority is the safety and security of the beneficiaries; therefore, we will conduct safety and security assessments prior to implementation and update the safety plans accordingly. An accessible accountability mechanism will be used in the project, known communication lines as well. Do no harm Standards will be applied at all stages of implementation and the project’s life cycle. In order to do that, POINT will conduct baseline assessments to ensure that none of the project activities will harm the community in any way.
