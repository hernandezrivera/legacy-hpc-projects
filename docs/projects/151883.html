Needs

Due to the armed conflict, the stagnation of the economy and the humanitarian crisis in the Donbass, the socio-economic situation of large families, families with children with health restrictions has significantly deteriorated in the Donetsk region.High unemployment, low social benefits, modest earnings of the adult population living in the region do not allow many parents to adequately satisfy the needs of their children, including in the field of education.This project is aimed at ensuring of equal access of children in the region to multidisciplinary education and the creation of conditions for the full development of children, regardless of their characteristics, academic performance, language of communication, psychophysical capabilities. To achieve these goals, within the framework of this project, it is planned to create an educational platform, on the basis of which children from these families will be able to receive additional training and individual development services, as well as spend their time with health benefits.The project will take into account the needs of boys and girls, as well as the special needs of children with disabilities and chronic diseases.However, as long as the children are engaged in educational and recreational activities, their parents will be able to receive advice on the formation of an adaptive developmental environment for children in the family, as well as purchase sets for entertaining games and interesting activities with children.Apart from this, within the project provides the providing of legal protection and assistance to families with children with disabilities. Counseling such citizens will allow them to develop an action strategy for resolving a problem situation that arose during the execution of primary documents (birth certificates, passports, MCC certificates), rehabilitation or treatment of a child.

Activities & outcomes

1. The formation of a project team consisting of teachers, educators, psychologists, trainers, lawyers, and medical professionals who have the industry-specific competences to support and implement the project.2. The creating on the basis of the Donbass Development Center (DDC) a platform for additional multidisciplinary education and active recreational activities for children.3.The formation of children's groups consisting of boys and girls, including children with disabilities and children with chronic diseases, and conducting multidisciplinary classes with all children, contributing to their intellectual and physical development.4. The conducting of orientation thematic seminars for parents on the formation of an adaptive developmental environment for children with disabilities in the family.5. The conducting of legal counseling for parents whose children are engaged on the basis of DDC educational platform.6. The providing of legal assistance to a family with children who have health restrictions in solving problems related to registration of papers, treatment and rehabilitation of children.7. Analysis and synthesis of the results of the implementation of this project and preparation of a report about implementation of activities.Monitoring the quality of implementation of planned activities and feedback from residents of the region on their satisfaction with the results of assistance will meet the requirements of the regulatory document ‘Suggested Actions for Cluster Coordination Groups to Strengthen Accountability to Affected Populations and Protection in the Humanitarian Programme Cycle’ (the IASC on Accountability, September 2016).

Indicators & targets

Children will receive educational services and comfortable conditions for active recreation and leisure, including those with disabilities :# of children benefiting: 840 including 420 girls and 420 boys. Adults receiving legal assistance: # of people benefiting: 2770 people, including 1,610 women, 1,160 men, among whom 100 are internally displaced persons (IDP).
