Project Summary

Since 2009, the Boko haram insurgency forced millions of people to become food insecure with millions of people estimated to need humanitarian assistance in North east Nigeria, according to the 2022 humanitarian Need overview (HNO), 8.4 million people are projected to be in need in the BAY states 58% of which are children, women and girls.
The challenges facing these populations are enormous, millions of which are fighting food insecurity with little or no source of livelihood. The conflict makes the general humanitarian situation complex and it becomes more complex in Borno states with the recent closure of IDP camps by the state government that bringing about movement of population from one place to another.
The recent IOM DTM No 262 shows that 1329 new arrivals with 218 screened for MUAC with 9 identified to be at high risk with Many partners reporting increased cases of malnutrition in children. The 2022 HNO shows that 3.0 million need Nutrition services 50% of which are children. Children and women who are acutely malnourished, have weaker immune systems and are therefore vulnerable to frequent infections that may overburden the health system with an estimation of 320,000 children expected to suffer from SAM and will need highly specialized nutrition services which will require increasing coverage of the OTP program centres in all accessible LGAs and wards to strengthen identification and referral of severely malnourished children.
The ongoing return of people back to their former communities has created the need for continued WASH assistance and already existing WASH facilities are stressed in these selected proposed LGA’s, the continued influx of people back to these LGA’s increases the risk of outbreak with history of frequent outbreak of cholera during raining season, there is need to support these LGAs with safe Water, sanitations infrastructure, hygiene education, WASH NFI and cholera prevention kits to respond effectively to ensure safety and dignity of these population.
With the current challenges at hand, there is need to build a stronger and more efficient Rapid response mechanism that strengthen the spotting of outbreak, identification of SAM cases using mass MUAC screening to ensure a quality response is administered to high risk cases within 72 hours. FRAD propose to implement this project to deliver an integrated robust Rapid response to meet WASH and Nutrition needs of this population through and integrated approach to provide lifesaving activities to address WASH and Nutrition needs of these hard to reach LGA’s.

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

To ensure good accountability to affected population mechanism is put in place, FRAD will deploy three (3) different methods to collect feedback from the community we serves for them to take ownership and lend their voices through opinion for them to be heard and acted upon.Help Desk: FRAD will have a help desk in all its projects implementation sites with personnel from the community that understand the local language as help desk assistant to attend to the communities we serve.Toll Free Line: FRAD shall deploy the use of toll free line for beneficiaries and community members to call and express their grievances, suggestions and feedback at all times. the number of the toll free line will be visible on all our visibility materials like, banners, shirts etcSuggestion Box: FRAD will position a suggestion box at the community in a location where it is safe and accessible by everyone including persons with disability. Focus Group Discussion: FRAD on quarterly basis will conduct FGD with beneficiaries and project stakeholders to gather feedback and act on those feedback received immediately.
