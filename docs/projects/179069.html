Project Summary

The project is aiming to provide educational and vocation training to youth and women in Noth-West of Syria,
the project will target the most vulnerable women to produce vocational skills like Crochet and computer skills. The appropriate professions and training will be delivered according to women's qualifications.
270 women will be targeted, in " Idleb, Afrin, Azaz, Albab, Jarablous and Tal Abaid".
Women who received Crochet training will be able to produce crochet pieces and MAPs will use the crochet to advocate for certain issues, "MAPs Lebanon already has this network with activates in Europe and advocate through the crochet there", the income from selling the crochet will support those women after the project end.
In addition, a set of training will help the resilience of youth and families will be launched, that will include:
1- Robotic Coding training.
2- Programming languages training.
3- Media content & design.
4- Digital marketing.
5- Freelance jobs.
This training will target no less than 2,430 BNFs, in the same previously mentioned regions, in NWS. This training will be followed by an internship with either NGOs offices inside Syria, as volunteers and/or private sector if available, all the training and the internship will be paid with small incentives.
All women and men who received multi technological cal training will be able to either find a good position in NWS or start their own business which will not only be targeting the local market but any other area as freelancers.

4 - Needs

The situation of people in North-West Syria is alarming, the recent displacement movement in last year, with the already huge number of IDPs, and according to the report of "Humanitarian responders,2021" make the IDPs consist around 47% of the total population, this meaning 47% have lost their jobs their homes and all or most of their capitals, the situation was further deteriorated dur to both Covid-19, and the huge infatuation of Syrian pounds, which decrease the purchasing power of people inside NWS. According to the last HNAP, The average value of daily per capita income is around 0.66$, which is much lower than the defined average of extreme poverty "$1.9 per day", according to the report, (66% MHHs, 67% FHHs) of interviews mentioned that they suffer from lack of employment opportunities. (46% MHHS, 54% FHHS) of respondents said that there is a lack of skills. MAPs already have a great experience in providing training in its main branch in Lebanon, and now it's in the process to replicate these experience in North-West of Syria to make actual employment opportunities and increase the capacity for youth to be able to build their own future.

5 - Implementation of activities

MAPs through past experience and the ongoing activities implemented in NWS and FGD that were conducted with women from different ages and has determined the sets of training. MAPs will community mobilize and advertise for the training activities. after receiving the list of candidates, MAPs will filter the list according to a two-set of criteria, 1 the vulnerability criteria, "MAPs" will try to focus as much as possible on women who are the most vulnerable. 2nd set of criteria is the qualifications, some training doesn't need any qualifications, and some do. MAPs then will use/rent centers depending on locations and the previous availability of MAPs centers, equip the center and recruit the staff, and start providing the training. the training for sewing and crochet will be for 3 months, while the computer skills and technological innovations, will vary from 1-3 months, according to each training. An incentive will be paid during the training period "10$ per month" and a certificate will be delivered to all trainees,the crochet group will continue for additional three months to produce crochet pieces, each crochet group will consist of 15 women and can produce 500 pieces each month, those women will be paid by MAPs during this period and according to marketing the crochet, those women could engage for a longer period. while the trainees who attend the different technological training, will have an internship with NGOs offices inside Syria, or with the private sector "taking advantage of MAPs MOUs with different stakeholder.PWDs will be encouraged to participate in the training and there will be a minimum quota for them to ensure their engagement, the center will be equipped in order to facilitate their access.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk: There's a risk of discrimination in some areas where there's a variety in ethnicity. likelihood: medium, Mitigation measures: the selection process will be managed and supervised by the program administration in Turkey, Complaints/Feedback mechanisms are put in place and are accessible to beneficiaries, Risk of SEA or GBV can be noticed during the implementation of training activities, likelihood: low, Mitigation measures: ensure that all the staff is oriented and attended MAPs policies for PSEA, and all the staff signed a code of conduct containing a clause for PSEA, and this is well explained as part of the recruitment process, and make sure that the PSEA compliant system is in a place and are effective and rapidly respond to any abuse. Risk: the presence of negative consequences from protection, gender and social perspective, likelihood: medium, MM: a proper contextual analysis of the target areas as a necessary input into planning and programming of activities, This would inform programming with a view to avoid any probable inequities and disagreement and to support targeted initiatives promoting collective action by the community members.

7.1 - Please list any risks related to sexual exploitation and abuse that have been identified during the project design phase and any risk mitigation and prevention measures that have been adopted and built into the project

The risk of PSEA is existed mainly between the trainers and trainees, without mentioning the risk between the project administration with either trainers or trainees, the likelihood is medium. the mitigation is the presence of PSEA policy and its mainstreaming in all other policies and administrative procedures. the training of the MEAL staff to deal with this kind of issues in the field, the orientation sessions, and the refreshment training that conducted during the project lifetime, in addition to the strong compliant and feedback mechanism that MAPs will include to be notified ASAP when any PSEA accident happened, including engaging senior-level management to react. and finally the singing of all staff on the code of conduct that has a PSEA clause included and explained for each staff.

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

The risk of threats on safety and security from bombing or airstrike, likelihood: low, MM: 1)MAPs will sure to do a security risk assessment before renting any place for training, and will make sure to implement th activities in a safe and secure area. 2) in case of any threats part of the activities will be conducted as virtually as possible, while the rest will be postponed till the situation is clear and secure. the risk of stopping the activities due to new Covid-19 regulations, likelihood: high, MM: MAPs will also have a plan for conducting the training virtually if it was required in the new regulations, in addition, to take care of all safety procedures like masks, social distancing, place capacity, etc. The risk of aid diversion, the likelihood is Medium, the MM: Providing training to staff on relevant international and donor laws, and relevant policies and on measures to be taken to prevent forms of financial abuse such as fraud, theft and loss. A personal security issue for Cash transfer, the likelihood is medium, MM: 1) Transfer all the cash for staff through their own PTTs accounts, transfer the cash that will be delivered to trainees through multiple PTTs account to minimize the amount carried by one person to the minimum. There's a risk of tensions between BNFs groups, likelihood: low, MM: this will be overcome by equally participating of all BNFs groups in the mentioned training.
