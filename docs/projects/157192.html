Project Summary

The project will target IDPs and vulnerable host communities in BN (Gissan, ELdamazin), WK (Elnuhood, Abye- Muglad) & SK (Kadugly) to serve access to basic services, life-saving, quality education and to promote protection and self-resilience to the affected IDPs. Because the situation became worse Due to continues conflict and current deterioration in Sudan economic situation and local level tribal conflicts have so far resulted in repetitive displacements in the last couple of years.
This project will focus on multi-sectorial component WASH, Education, and Child protection These interventions will be summarized as follows:
WASH Component: The project will ensure improved access to safe drinking water and hygiene promotion by implementing the community promoters software activities for 18,680 IDPs (4,670 men, 4,670 women,4,670 boys, and 4,670 girls) and 20,608 affected host community (5,152 men, 5,125 women, 5,152 boys, and 5,152 girls) in Kadugli locality in South Kordofan state through rehabilitation of Non-functional hand pumps, rehabilitation of 2 water yards and installed with solar energy, training of mechanic and provision of toolkits. The project will ensure access to improved suitable sanitation facilities through CLTS where communities will be trained on the CLTS approach.
Education Component: The project targets IDPs and the host communities focusing on children 2000 child (1020 girls & 980 boys) 140 PTAs and teachers( 72 Female & 68 male in South Kordofan and West Kordofan to increase access to inclusive and protective lifesaving education and improve the quality of education for boys and girls. in addition to strengthening community based education and child protection system as well as target with rehabilitation the gender-sensitive classrooms that is risky for the student, and construction of new classrooms to facilitate hosting the huge number of school children, the project will also have intended to rehabilitate and construct of school latrines specially for girl’s schools . To consider gender in latrine construction AORD will guidance civil engineer during the design and building stage to build each latrine in a separate area for girls and boys to consider gender sensitives and privacy
Child Protection component: AORD targets in this project Damazin with 1100 children ( 561 Girls and 539 Boys and Giessen 815 children (416 girls & 399 boys ). in existing CFS in Damazin Bau, Gessian , and Kormuk the project will strengthen and support the refresher training of the existed 2 community-based child protection networks in Damazin and Gessian and link with the SWCC to create the feedback mechanism, FGD and case management also conduct Family Tracing and Reunification for the study separated and unaccompanied Children moreover mobilization of community members to engage and interact within the CFS through community meeting to enhance child protection issues and Daily sensitization on prevention and referral of child protection issues including working with men and male youth to support in compacting harmful practice and violence against women . 100 older adolescents’ children (60 girls & 40 Boys) will be supported by life skills training. supporting of exciting child protection facilities (CFS) and integrate education and child protection in both CFS and schools, establishment a very tide like between the social workers and surrounding schools and train the CBCPN and link them with PTA and health workers to complement their efforts,
The project will be implemented in close coordination with the State Ministry of Education, State Ministry of Social and Welfare, State Council for Children and Welfare (SCCW), Water environmental sanitation unit, MOH and HAC at State level. The project will also coordinate with humanitarian Actors in the targeted areas.

Needs

Due to continues conflict and current deterioration in Sudan economic situation resulting from lack of access to basic services and the war in the Blue Nile resulted in a major humanitarian crisis in the southern part of Blue Nile, affecting some 250,000 people that were displaced. Then, in 2015, the Government controlled areas of Blue Nile, an estimated 56,000 were displaced (24,000 were relocated from 12 villages). Addition to that joint assessment conducted by OCHA and HAC in September 2018 reveal’s that in Giessen and ELdamazin localities which is target area for the Project.There are more than 200 hosts in each community and equivalent number of IDPs. Most communities reported that the IDPs did not have access to basic infrastructure. parents report that their children are working when they should be attending school. They are most often helping in the fields (planting, weeding, harvesting), collecting firewood and fetching water. They are also involved with transporting produce to market or loading building materials. Children reportedly spend between 1-2 hours per day at these tasks. this critical situation needs the intervention in CBCP.
