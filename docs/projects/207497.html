Project Summary

The project aims to enhance the livelihoods of internally displaced persons (IDPs), returnees, and host communities in the North West Region of Cameroon by promoting sustainable animal farming (Poultry and Pig farming). By providing training, resources, and support, the project seeks to empower participants to engage in animal husbandry as a means of generating income, improving food security, and fostering community resilience.
Key Objectives:
1. Provide training and capacity building in animal husbandry techniques, including livestock management, healthcare, and breeding practices.
2. Distribute essential resources such as livestock, feed, and veterinary supplies to participants to establish and sustain animal farming enterprises.
3. Promote gender equality and social inclusion by ensuring equitable access to project resources and opportunities for women, youth, and marginalized groups.
Expected Outcomes:
1. Increased household income and economic stability for participants through the sale of animal products such as meat, and eggs.
2. Improved food security and nutrition for vulnerable populations through increased access to animal protein and related products.
3. Enhanced environmental sustainability through the promotion of responsible animal farming practices and natural resource management.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The need for the project "Improving Livelihood for IDPs, Returnees and Host Communities in the North West Region of Cameroon through Animal Farming" arises from the challenging circumstances faced by internally displaced persons (IDPs), returnees, and host communities in the region. Several factors contribute to the necessity of this initiative:1. Economic Vulnerability: Many IDPs, returnees, and host communities in the North West Region of Cameroon lack sustainable sources of income and livelihood opportunities. This economic vulnerability hinders their ability to meet basic needs and impedes their long-term prospects for financial stability.2. Food Insecurity: The ongoing displacement and resettlement of populations have disrupted traditional livelihoods and agricultural practices, leading to food insecurity among affected communities. Access to nutritious food, particularly animal protein, is limited, impacting the health and well-being of vulnerable individuals and families.3. Limited Livelihood Options: The prevailing socio-economic conditions in the region have constrained the availability of viable livelihood options for IDPs, returnees, and host communities. Diversifying income-generating activities, such as through animal farming, is essential to create sustainable livelihoods and reduce dependency on external aid.4. Community Resilience: Strengthening the resilience of communities affected by displacement and conflict is crucial for their long-term recovery and sustainable development. By promoting animal farming, the project aims to empower individuals and communities to build resilience, foster self-sufficiency, and contribute to local economic growth.5. Sustainable Development: Encouraging sustainable agricultural practices, such as animal farming, aligns with broader development goals, including environmental conservation, economic empowerment, and social inclusion. This project addresses the need for sustainable solutions that benefit both the current and future generations in the North West Region of Cameroon.In light of these pressing needs, the project seeks to address the economic, nutritional, and social challenges faced by IDPs, returnees, and host communities through targeted interventions that promote animal farming as a pathway to improved livelihoods and community well-being.
