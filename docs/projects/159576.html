1 - Needs

For five years, during the conflict in the Donbas, the population of the region is faced with its negative impact on the formation of civil society. This happens in two ways: firstly, the increased rate of migration, including forced migration, causes people to lose their life-long social ties. Secondly, because of the negative psycho-emotional background on NGCA there are conflicts in communities. The victims of these circumstances are three million inhabitants of the conflict zone, who face numerous difficulties every day. These include the destruction of infrastructure, a critical drop in material support for the population, lack of funding and qualified specialists due to the outflow of the population from the conflict zone.Public relations were also broken, and social and legal support to the population was extremely inadequate. Public associations and associations of citizens are fragmented and limited in capabilities and critically limited in capabilities and competencies. The issues of registration and re-registration of public organizations and associations of citizens are not legally regulated; such organizations do not have state support. People without public support are in extremely constrained material and psychological circumstances due to the ongoing conflict, which leads to an annual reduction in the population of NGCA of 30 thousand people.One of the most pressing problems is the lack of legal, recognized by Ukraine local governments. In this regard, the formation of civil society institutions comes to the fore. Local communities are the form of people's organization that is most informed about their own needs. Based on this, it is proposed to create a resource center based on the CO CF “Donbass Development Center”, which could provide comprehensive assistance by advising, redirecting , provide social projects and self-employed in need of space, the Internet, equipment, computers, trainings, assistance in finding funds and partners, as well as self-employment opportunities.It is also planned to carry out trips to consult and implement support programs in remote areas near the contact line, whose residents do not have access to the Internet, social projects or to receive legal services on a regular basis.It is necessary to include emergency assistance in the project.This project will allow giving a wide category of citizens of Donbass the possibility of self-realization, development and effective integration into society, and will reduce the outflow of the population from the region. Among the categories of citizens whom it is planned to provide support on the basis of the resource center are pensioners with minimum monthly assistance (150 people, men - 61, women - 89), families with children with special needs (100 people, men - 43, women - 57), low-income people (100 people, men - 42, women - 58), people with special needs (150 people, men - 62, women - 88).Also, it is planned to support IDPs living in collective centers, unemployed, single women raising minor children, children without parental care, persons without permanent residence or having lost documents. This equals 1,000 people (men - 447, women - 553).

2 - Activities & outcomes

The proposed project includes the following activities:Selection of suppliers and partners of the program, conclusion of relevant contracts;Coverage of the project on the information platforms of the organization;Development of methodological materials for training, preparation of consultation plans;Trainings and consultations (in total, it is planned to hold at least 240 hours of trainings for 600 people (247 men and 353 women) and at least 4 hours of consultations of various directions weekly);Activities of the resource center aimed at providing free office space, providing office equipment and equipment for individuals and social initiatives;Formation of a mobile team to provide on-site consultations (legal, employment or self-employment, psychological counseling and others);Consultations: on-site, in-patient, or through any communication channel (hotline, receiving and processing applications through social networks), for at least 300 people (144 men and 156 women);Selection of program participants to provide resource support and verification of their needs;Purchase and transfer of necessary goods, excluding food, hygiene, medicines, on the basis of individual requests (it is planned to provide assistance to 300 people ( 150 men and 150 women), including 50 children under 18 years, 250 people from 18 to 59 years.Monitoring (intermediate, final) to assess the effectiveness of intervention (assistance) in each case. Mechanisms of feedback and accountability to the affected population will be implemented through the use of the organization's website, social networks, trust boxes, telephone lines, messengers, the work of visiting specialists, beneficiary interviewing in accordance with the norms of Accountability to Affected Populations.Evaluation of the implementation of the program, with the formation of the final monitoring report.
