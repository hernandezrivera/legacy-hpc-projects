Project Summary

This project is aiming at protecting HLP Rights and SBGV respond by addressing issues of Housing, Land and Property (HLP) rights and Sexual Gender Based Violence including illegal occupation of HLP, absences of legal knowledge of HLP rights, HLP based communal conflict, limited provision of legal information, counseling and lack of sustainable and innovative community based HLP dispute resolution mechanisms, legal representation in the court of law, legal counselling and legal aid . Therefore, this project seeks to strengthen sustainable local mechanisms for protection and enhancing access to justice for HLP affected persons and SGBV cases taking into consideration; accountability to affected population, gender and conflict sensitivity. The project will be achieved through;
(1) Raising awareness on HLP and SGBV and the law for urban and outreach communities;
(2) Provision of legal support and legal counselling and response to assist IDPs and Returnees to reclaim their land and properties and survivals of SGBV in the legal procedures to get Justice and compensation,
(3) Provision of free legal aid and legal representation in the court of law for Persons of concern HLP affected persons / SGBV survivals in addition to case management and
(4) Supporting community based alternative dispute resolution mechanisms and
(5) Training of local authorities and institutions staff in HLP and dispute resolution and ADR plus training of SGBV frontline workers on SGBV guiding principles and response
(6) Supporting SGBV survivals with livelihood support in term of Cash and Voucher assistance with SGBV program. The sensitive nature of HLP and SGBV issues in the project locations, RFAO offers to establish community mediation groups and provide accessible mobile and static legal aid services. These holistic interventions will be implemented within a period of Twelve (12) months to ensure and achieve positive impact on the lives of the beneficiaries.
