Project Summary

Sudan has been in crisis for years and the fighting between the Sudan Armed Forces (SAF) and the Rapid Support Forces (RSF) escalated in April 2023.
This has led to mass displacement, with over 6.1 million people forced to leave their homes. Sudan currently faces the most significant internal displacement and child displacement crises in the world. The ongoing fighting has made it difficult for humanitarian assistance to reach those in need, causing dire shortages of food, water, fuel, communication, and electricity. The 2024 humanitarian project will focus on life-saving activities such as protection, WASH, and food security. The project will target internally displaced persons and host communities in River Nile and South Kordofan states and also work towards preventing conflicts, promoting peaceful coexistence, and ensuring sustainability.
JASMAR will provide assistance to vulnerable IDPs and host communities in line with the sectors strategic objectives, and the proposed project targeted total of 14,950 most vulnerable IDPs and host community members . and below proposed intervention
For the General Protection response:
Targeted 3,590 IDPs (2,145 Men- 1,245 Women - 80 Boys – 120 Girls ) and will deliver the basic Protection services (formation of Community based structures ,awareness session and Capacity building for service providers on protection issues and Provide Multipurpose Cash Assistance for most vulnerable HHs )
For the Child Protection response:
Targeted 2,120 IDPs (210 Men - 610 women – 550 Boys -750 Girls) children and will deliver the basic Child Protection services (Case Management and referral services, awareness session, Capacity building for series providers on children protection issues and provide assistant for PWD) and JASMAR will ensure the community’s active participation to prevent and respond to child protection issues.
For the Gender Based Violence response:
Targeted 2,520 IDPs (485 men -1,485 women – 125 boys - 425 Girls _ and will deliver the basic GBV services (GBV Case Management and referral services, GBV awareness session ,Capacity building for service providers and non GBV service providers as GBV risk mitigation activities )
The project includes WASH activities in school (availability of potable water by water tanks)
The WASH Response: Targeted 4,620 IDPs and host communities, will deliver the basic WASH service, and will including schools.
For Food security and livelihood:
The proposed activities targeted 2,100 include provide Agriculture support services including improved seeds (staple and cash crops and vegetables), including hand tools for 1,000. provide emergency livestock, inputs, and veterinary services – including vaccination, treatments, and extension services to 1000 HHS. Provision of goat restocking for 100 HHS (50 Femal HHS) this activity will be implemented in south Kordofan state mainly Habila and Elrif Elshargi localities .
JASMAR have operation, presence and good knowledge of targeted communities in South Kordofan River Nile states , and already coordinate the project activities with other humanitarian actors and forums (sectors FSL , WASH ,CP and GBV) and respective government departments in the project sites .
JASMAR implementation strategy will be direct implementation which include community orientation ,mobilization and engagement.
