Project Summary

Mercy Corps aims to strengthen our protection interventions using a gender sensitive lens to support the overall protection cluster objectives towards the provision of quality integrated protection services. Mercy Corps will focus the intervention on vulnerable adolescent girls and boys and young women utilizing community-based approaches taking into considerations the existing resources in the communities. Informed through a conducted multi-sector needs assessment in September 2023, Mercy Corps will expand on its commitment to integrating protection outcomes into other sectoral interventions including cash, food security and livelihoods, WASH and social cohesion. Mercy corps will implement projects through 2 different but complementary modalities: direct implementation and though local partners in north east Syria. Mercy Corps projects aim to ensure vulnerable adolescents and young people exposed to acute and/or protracted crises are safe, protected and more resilient and have the capacity to address root causes of inequalities. This will be achieved through 1) Opportunities in livelihood and resources including cash grants or cash assistance; 2) Individuals, couples, and community leaders are supported to unlearn harmful values and social norms and to adopt new values and attitudes and become more committed to equality and diversity 3) Adolescents and young people who are survivors and marginalized understand their rights, exercise self-efficacy 4) Adolescents are supported to to work together and communities on building a peaceful coexistence, through peacebuilding practices and violence risk reduction. These interventions will complement existing sectoral programming where Mercy Corps or its partners are operating in and will not be standalone. Mercy Corps will also continue to ensure protection mainstreaming is embedded across all our activities and programming. Mercy Corps staff and partners are trained in protection and gender mainstreaming principles and approaches, protection risks analysis and Safeguarding/PSEA; Mercy Corps community accountability feedback and response mechanism are designed in consultation with communities and feedback received are use to track red flags; community awareness sessions to inform the communities of their rights and entitlements, complaints and feedback mechanism and the code of conduct of all individuals providing aid.
