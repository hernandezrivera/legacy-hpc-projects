Project Summary

This project aims to contribute to sustainable development and food sovereignty in Cabo Delgado. It focuses specifically on strengthening local capacities for food sovereignty (including the promotion of organic agriculture, increased agricultural production and crop diversification, women's economic empowerment and nutrition education).

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

This project aims to contribute to the sustainable development of livelihoods in communities whose population depends mainly on agriculture, strengthening their capacities to increase production, crop diversification and food sovereignty: (R1). Economic empowerment of women, strengthening their capacities for income generation and strengthening their economic enterprises. The specific objective focuses on strengthening the local capacities of rights holders, the population most affected by the situation of poverty and food insecurity in Meluco, Quissanga and Metuge, Cabo Delgado. To achieve the proposed objective, work will be done with local communities to strengthen the livelihoods and capacities for self-sufficiency of the target population in the prioritised areas (production, diversification, marketing, nutritional education), contributing to the sustainable production and use of food. Overall, 11,202 people (5,869 men and 5,333 women) are expected to benefit directly.

This is why the project foresees the following activities; focused on capacity building for the increase, diversification and use of production. Similarly, capacity building of rights-holders in nutrition education will contribute to the in nutrition education will contribute to a better use of production, improving food security and sovereignty. ACTIVITIES: A.1.1 Training in sustainable agricultural practices; A.1.2 Creation of community nurseries and seed banks; A.1.3 Promotion of organic farming in groups of 60 members; A.1.4 Crop diversification and distribution of agricultural inputs to families; A.2.1 Form and establish local self-sufficiency groups with groups of 60 members each, all of them women; A.2.2 Develop business training, entrepreneurship and business management courses.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Protection mainstreaming is an integral part of AeA’s design strategy, this alongside the participatory methods that AeA use to collect information help greatly to identify the risks posed to each demographic. Staff working in the field are aware of the do no harm principle. AeA prioritises preserving safety and dignity in the design of any installation, infrastructure or the planning of any event.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

Ensuring that there is equitable access for all and encouraging participation in order to empower those we are helping. A multi-sector risk analysis for each component of the project will be made to ensure that all risks are considered and mitigated. As an example early marriages, which are a frequent practice on the sites. In these cases a previous analysis will be carried out and provide extra support for those large families and/or having children under this risk. In addition, AeA will closely coordinate with protection actors in the field in order to attend referrals about GVB or other child protection cases to assure the security, dignity and comfort. In addition, a risk assessment and safety walks will be carried out prior to the intervention in order to identify the safe areas and potential and current risk in the community for the different population groups in order to adapt the design of the action.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Throughout the project implementation, households with PwD will be prioritized for assistance, receiving additional support, if needed. Considerations such as functional diversity and other special needs will guide the adaptation of tailored activities. In the context of the activities implemented in this project, specific training will be provided with a focus on addressing the needs of PwD. This training will incorporate elements that highlight the unique requirements of individuals with disabilitiesand how to effectively respond to their needs and fostering their safety and dignity.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Measures are foreseen to mitigate the negative impact of project activities on the environment in line with the relevant institutional policies of AeA and its local partners. AEA will adopt an agroecological approach while implementing the farming activities, contributing to the preservation of the environment and natural resources, while also enhancing the provision of a number of ecosystem services. AEA will include biological pest control methods that have fewer negative impacts on the environment. Climate change and environmental consideration will be part of the farming training curricula. The project will promote the strengthening of the local buying and selling circuit in terms of raw material and input supply, and will contribute to a reduced demand for external resources, the use of which impacts on the environment.
