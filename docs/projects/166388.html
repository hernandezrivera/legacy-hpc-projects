Project Summary

Under the Emergency shelter/NFI response Program for COVID-19 affected Rohingya refugees, Caritas Bangladesh (CB) aims to ensure that refugee families of Camp-13 & Camp-19, can live in safety and with dignity during COVID-19 Pandemic. The proposed project includes the delivery of NFIs and emergency shelter kits, including labor support to repair the shelter for households in-home care and with elderly family members. With this funding, IOM will provide the required NFI and emergency assistance shelter kits, which will be distributed by CB, including delivery for families in Camp-13 and Camp-19. Caritas will handle transportation and associated cost and ensure targeted households are receiving the NFI/Shelter materials by delivering to their shelters. Besides, CB will also assist families with repairing damaged shelters during monsoon after conducting a rapid assessment of damaged shelters.CB will hire locally trained skilled laborers from the Rohingya communities to support families during shelter repair. Upon delivery of NFI materials, CB will create awareness on hygiene promotion, and households will be informed about the purpose, use, and Quantity of NFI kits that are being distributed. Since older adults and people at home care are the most vulnerable for COVID-19, necessary precautionary measures will be taken to ensure their well being while in-home care. To reduce the footprint in the camp, the proposed support will be provided mostly by Rohingya volunteers with minimal supervision from CB staff in the camps. CB engineers will be on the field for shelter rapid damage assessment, to supervise shelter repairing activities, and to provide technical support to the laborers and volunteers during shelter repairs. CB will make surer all volunteers and teams are trained before the implementation of the project on precautionary COVID-19 measures, sectoral guidance on distribution & construction site safety recommendations by the Shelter/NFI sector, code of conduct of CB, Safeguarding Policy and PSEA. CB will ensure "DO NO HARM" principles are followed in the project and will incorporate the existing complaint response mechanism in the monitoring, evaluation and learning system. CB will closely work with HEALTH partners, site management team, and protection partners through our protection team with matters related to child protection, GBV, PSEA. CB is the catchment focal point person for Camp-13 and Camp-19, which will give us an additional advantage in terms of coordination with different actors in the camp, which intern will contribute to the smooth execution of the project and immediate response during emergencies. CB has reliable field-based staff and volunteers in both camps and will hire additional volunteers for timely completion of planned activities for Rohingya refugees. Also, CB is committed to mobilizing the team in short notice during emergencies and the need for immediate response.

Needs

Following the Bangladesh government announcement of essential activities to be implemented in camps in light of COVID-19 shelter related supports have been on hold, which makes it challenging to distribute construction materials in anticipation of the monsoon season. As part of COVID-19 prevention measures, families are advised to be in-home care or in isolation in medical facilities if they have symptoms of being affected by the virus. With monsoon coming and families being in self-quarantine, the need for emergency shelter assistance is more than ever. The primary targets in this project are older people and families in-home care which are not in a position to collect shelter assistance kits from and repair their shelters. CB will support affected households in delivering the shelter assistance kits and repair their shelters. Given that the refugee families will stay for an extended period in home care with elderly members, additional NFI materials will be required to keep the older people in a relatively comfortable situation during home care. In the presence of family members with COVID-19 symptoms, during homecare, they have to stay separated in a room, and without sharing any household items with the rest of the family. NFI Kits such as blankets, floor mats, and solar lights will be needed; specific NFI needs of the families will be determined by sector. Considering the vulnerability of older people for COVID-19, CB will make sure the delivery of the NFI kits for targeted households to their shelters.
