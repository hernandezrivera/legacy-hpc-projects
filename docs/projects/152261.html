Project Summary

Water Sanitation and Hygiene is a fundamental basic human right and most prioritised in conflict situation about 1.6 million people are affected in the insurgency in Borno state so many people are hosted in camps as internally displaced person (IDPs) and host communities.
Therefore, the need for adequate and qualitative water followed with provision of standard Sanitation facilities and Hygiene are key to the affected women, children and their families otherwise disease and death will follow. Women and children are most vulnerable in crisis and emergency situation and can fall prey and are abuse and exploited in trying to access these WASH facilities. Measures must be put in place to prevent and mitigate any form of abuse and exploitation that may occur in trying to access WASH facilities.
This project will aim to provide equitable access to safe water and sufficient quantity for drinking and domestic purposes and also safe sanitation. It will also address adoption of basic personal hygiene among the affected people of concern.
Monitoring and maintenance of these facilities will also be conducted.

Needs

Since the beginning of the year, 2.9 million people across Borno, Adamawa and Yobe states are in need of safe WASH intervention. In addition to responding to urgent water and sanitation needs, organisations providing WASH services have continued to respond to cholera and acute watery diarrhoea (AWD) outbreaks in the three states. In Borno State, the AWD outbreak, is a WASH related disease continuous to escalate, although the WASH and Health Sector are responding to the outbreak, there is still need for more provision of safe water and sanitation services followed by promoting good hygiene practices to the affected population. For sustainability of the project, volunteers will be trained for WASH related disease outbreak response and also to monitor and maintain WASH facilities in their respective communities and to ensure equitable distribution and access to all gender and age group.

Provision of safe water and sanitation services to the affected people.Coordinate for promotion of safe hygiene practices.Strengthen capacity for WASH related disease outbreak response in collaboration with health.Monitoring and maintenance of facilities in camps and in host communities with focus on women and girls accessing.
