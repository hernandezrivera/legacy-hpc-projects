Project Summary

This project addresses urgent needs with regard to food security and livelihoods of newly accessible locations in South Kordofan State, in El-leiri, Rashad and Talodi localities. The selected localities are in urgent need of emergency food security interventions (agricultural & livestock inputs and services), income generating activities and natural resource management. This will be followed by selection of 7050 HH (42,300 people,6,345 men, 15,228 women, 8,418 boys and 12,309 girls) of the most vulnerable households among new and protracted IDPs, returnees and most vulnerable and destitute host communities as direct beneficiaries. Selection criteria such as number of children under 5, women/children headed households, food insecured households with more than 6 members, households with malnourished children, disabled, elderly men and women, landless (for IGAS), chronically poor and minority groups will be part of the selection criteria. Beneficiary (300 HHs) selection for goats restocking will focus on female-headed HHs, HHs with malnourished children, vulnerable HHs with children under the age of 5 and elders; tools distribution (for 6,300 HHs) will focus on vulnerable farmer IDPs and returnees who have lost their production assets due to conflict and displacement; stabilized soil block machines provision and training (150 HHs) will focus on returnees, vulnerable host communities and IDPs men and youth from the targeted communities including persons with disabilities; fuel efficient stoves training will target mainly vulnerable women, girls and youth from IDPs, returnees and host communities including persons with disabilities.

Needs

Displacement and refuge created heavy burden on both IDPs and host communities. The majority of the IDPs have been unable to re-establish the livelihoods they practiced before being displaced. They were farmers, pastoralists as well as agro-pastoralists and those who lived near urban centres practiced other crafts and trades. Regarding food security, the IDPs said they are finding it difficult to provide enough food at house hold level because of steep increase of prices in the market. For example, the price of Sorghum, which is the main staple food crop has doubled compared to the same time last year. Inter-Agency Assessment mission conducted and information compiled based on the inputs provided by partners on the ground including; government authorities, affected communities/IDPs and agencies (12-26 Oct.2018)A total of 10,206 people (4,591 Male and 5,669 Female) are living as Internally Displaced Persons (IDPs) in El Leri according to HAC. Only 50% of the displaced are registered as some of the areas where IDPs live have been inaccessible. El Leri has 38,882 people which was further boosted by the arrival of 14,000 refugees from South Sudan form 2013 to-date. Care International Switzerland, Save the Children (CIS) CONCERN and Al Manar have humanitarian response activities in El Leri Locality. They support health, WASH and nutrition as well as providing cash grants to South Sudan (SS) refugees and some vulnerable IDPs to meet some household expenses.Rashad locality is inhabited by 95,919 people (22,874 HH). Currently there are 22,324 IDPs composed of 3,576 households living in displacement in Tandla, Kajmala, Kaboos and Rashad city. They are comprised of 12,520 females, 9,794 males and 4,702 children under five years of age. Presently Mercy Corps Scotland (MC-S), Global Aid Hand (GAH), Sudanese Red Crescent Societies (SRCS), Alsalam Organization for Rehabilitation and Development (AORD), SOS Sahel Sudan have offices in Rashad. Other partners such as INGOs, UN agencies and NNGOs operate remotely. They are providing food, health, WASH and nutrition services to the IDPs and some vulnerable community members. Talodi locality has 13,968 IDPs (2,328 HHs) mainly living in five locations, four of which are inside Talodi Town, and one in Al Tugula Village, 16 kilometers east of Talodi Town. The IDPs were displaced from Mandi, Angarto, Al Gardod, Mafloua, Kindirma, Al Salamat and Taboli which experienced several attacks by both SPLM-N and the Sudanese Armed Forces (SAF). Most of those villages are no longer under government control. Some people from these villages initially fled to Talodi and Om Dual village which had been evacuated and later moved to AlTugula. The displaced people did not form displacement camps but were absorbed into existing villages by relatives and other people in Talodi. The total population of Talodi is 48,862 people, including IDPs.

Activities

- Provision of quality assorted crop seeds (Sorghum, sessame & legumes) and vegetable seeds to 6,300 farmer HHs (37,800 people: 6,010 men, 13,608 women, 7,182 boys and 11,000 girls): Under this activity each HH will be provided 20 USD worth package of assorted seeds including: Sorghum, sesame, water melon, okra and tomato seeds, pigeon pea seeds and chick pea seeds will be provided to the targeted beneficiaries in consultation with the communities in Rashad, Eleri and Talodi to select the suitable package. Procurement of assorted seeds will take place in Khartoum through tender process including certified suppliers. - Provision of 6,300 tool kits (consists of hoes and rakes) to 6,300 HHs or100% of beneficiaries targeted by seeds distribution.- Formation and training of Village Agricultural Committees (VACs) in seeds management, storage, pest control, community mobilization and distribution of agricultural inputs:- Extension training of 300 HHH farmers in good agricultural practicesLivelihood:- Provision of goat package (4 She, 1 He) to 300 female-headed most vulnerable HHs (1800 people: 288 male, 640 female, 342 boys and 530 girls):- Procurement of 15 SSB machines for 150 HH (men & youth) 900 beneficiaries , divided in 15 groups of 10 beneficiaries each one machine for a group. - Arrangement of TOT of 30 HHHs men and youth, (on molding mixture, materials ratio, production, maintenance and operation technologies of Stabilized Soil Block machines.- Provide training of 30 HHHs, 180 beneficiaries on small business management 2 from each SSB machines group.- Arrangement of TOT of 300 women and youth HHHs (1,800 people: 288 male, 666 female, 342 boys and 504 girls) on molding mixture, materials ratio, production, and maintenance and operation technologies of Fuel Efficient Stoves.
