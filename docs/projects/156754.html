Needs

The conflict in Syria grew out of what started as the civil unrest in 2011. Since then several million of civilians have been displaced, some have sought refuge in neighboring countries others have undertaken the journey to Europe in search for a newer, saver home. Specifically to Aleppo, around 30% of HHs miss at least one member (Humanitarian Needs Assessment Programme for Syria (HNAP) June 2018. Amongst the civilians that remained in Syria 6.1m have been displaced, some of them already several times due tochanging front lines (HNO2018). In 2018 1,250,956 Syrians returned, the top sub-district that welcomed returnees were Jebel Saman, Aleppo (MNM, Dec 2018). 89% of them returned to their own house, often needing to restart their lives there completely. In this fluent, uncertain situation children and women bear the burden of the consequences of the conflict. Many families live in dire needs and parents struggle to provide, because of lost livelihoods or unemployment. This significantly reduces parent's ability to provide for themselves and for their families. In the meantime, savings are depleted after several years of conflict and poverty is on the rise. In Jebel Saman there are an estimated 1,012,815 PiN, of which 65,720 count as food insecure. The severity level for the food security sector in the district is 4.

Implementation of activities

Dorcas will select vulnerable households and support a person / household to improve their opportunities to provide for the family by themselves, options are vocational courses combined with distributions of livelihoods assets, input to improve options for businesses that have an established business plan. The project is implemented in an urban setting and is fully funded.For women the pilot that we are about to evaluate focused on hairdressing and sewing, for men, DAI focussed on support to small entreprises and supported them with additional know-how and machines to serve customers better. As a first step DAI will conduct an internal evaluation and planning exercise and then decide to determine the income generating activities formally. For now it is possible to confirm that entrepreneurship support was very promising and also the sewing training showed good results. To offer more advanced sewing might be a suitable strategy for the future programming cycle.
