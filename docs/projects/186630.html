Project Summary

The overall aim of this project is to provide support and motivation by provision of quality school sandals and scholastic materials in the move to improved access to safe and inclusive learning to IDP girls and boys in through life saving alternative basic education (Integrated Qur’anic Education and accelerated learning programs) and inclusive Education for 4000 conflict affected boys, girls and children with impairment (IDPs, Returnees and Host community) in Monguno, Dikwa LGA of Borno State

1 - Needs

Monguno town is one of the major towns in Northern Borno that is accessible but have experienced unceasing attacks from the Non State Armed Groups( NSAG) especially at end of the year 2018 the whole Northern part of the State down to the fringes of Lake Chad have seen increased campaign from ISWAP which led to a mass exodus of people school level girls and boys included, from Baga, Kukawa, Kekeno and Marte into Monguno according to UNOCHA their numbers is placed to be 122,000 IDPs in Monguno, this has also add up to the over stretched dysfunctional education system existing in the town, the town is characterized with a lot of children (boys and girls) on the street, without access to education ( quality not in question here) As at March 2020 school enrollment stands at 11,132 children, hence the need for support and motivation of this kids with quality school sandals and Scholastic Materials, the workability of this has been proven by the Pilot shoe distribution let by Mon Club in Monguno late 2020.In the same vein, with the move by the State government to resettle IDPs back to their original homes, of which Baga, Kukawa have since been resettled though with access challenge, Marte relocation is ongoing, the need for Scholastic materials and School Sandals especially Temporary Learning Space (TLS) that might be springing up in this new settle communities cannot be over emphasized.

2 - Activities

The need in the education sector is so enormous, distribution of quality school shoes and scholastic materials has proven to be a huge source of motivation for children in the schools, this pilot activity was conducted in 5 schools targeting 1000 pupils, when the M & E unit of Mon Club international conducted an assessment, school attendance has increased immensely, few sample of kids interviewed attributed it to the new beautiful school sandals, we hope to do more to revolutionized the Northern Borno School attendance Monguno, and Dikwa as our Focus.The modalities used to target this pupil was thought from motivational concept perspective; pupils that comes to school early were targeted to encourage punctuality and commitment, vulnerable children were also part of the beneficiaries, relying on information received from the school teachers of parent that can’t afford uniforms and schools shoes, the Education assistant carrying out the activities too observed the pupils without uniforms and shoes deemed as vulnerable made up the targeted list at the spot.

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

Protection and accountability to affected populations are an integral part of all Mon Club programing. Protection will therefore be mainstreamed into every phase of the project cycle. To ensure that protection is effectively and efficiently mainstreamed while the complaints and feedback mechanisms are properly managed, a provision for protection staff has been included in the budget. The protection officers will be responsible for the following tasks:o Leading and updating context and protection risk analysis and diversity mapping; o Conducting joint protection risk analysis assessments with other stakeholders including safety audits with a specific focus on risk factors by AGDM;o Monitoring and communicating changes in the protection environment within which education programs are implemented in synergy with existing protection/CCCM programming in the project locations where Protection Monitoring is an ongoing activity;o Advising on the implementation of mitigating measures in operations and appropriate adjustments to ensure inclusion of most vulnerable and at-risk categories; o Mapping and communicating protection referral pathways to their respective teams. This activity is particularly pertinent as conducts case management activities under a number of other complementary projects who conduct and share monthly service mapping exercises; o Contributing to the design and implementation of approaches related to information provision, community consultation, feedback and complaints mechanisms; o Ensuring good practice in data protection and privacy;Working as part of an integrated program team to translate protection mainstreaming into concrete actions like awareness raising. For instance, Protection staff will use the waiting time at food distribution activities, to raise awareness on GBV, Child Protection, Social Cohesion and Human Rights issues (as pertinent per area of interventionProtection staff are fully dedicated to supporting the integration of protection principles. In order to ensure that this is done, the protection officers will be based in the LGA where activities are to be implemented.

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

The project will have a community based complaints and response mechanism, a dedicated staff will be in charge of this complains, the complaints will be evaluated individually and attended to, will only be closed when sorted out, complains will take the maximum of 15 days to be resolved. The CFM Assistant will also work along side the project management committee resident in the communities, to provide feedback, they will act as project pulse checkers, feedback will be use to re-evaluate. A dedicated phone line for call-in at anytime is provided to community members for feedback.
