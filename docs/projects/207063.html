Project Summary

The proposed project aims to strengthen the effectiveness of CUAMM's integrated humanitarian nutrition intervention in Cabo Delgado by - Supporting availability and access to the comprehensive treatment of acute malnutrition, both MAM and SAM, for under 5 children and PLWs;
- Timely identification and referral of malnutrition cases through existing programmes (i.e. Integrated Mobile Brigades);
- Supporting the integration of the acute malnutrition treatment and any related medical complications in existing health system.
CUAMM will adopt a human rights-based and people-centred approach, implementing tailor-made and flexible activities to respond to the most acute needs of the population.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The proposed project builds on the good results obtained thanks to the actions carried out in 2023, aiming to reinforce them and to strengthen the collaboration with local authorities in order to maximise their involvement and ownership of the action. The activities will be implemented in an unstable security and humanitarian context. The security situation in Cabo Delgado province, despite recent improvements remains, indeed, volatile and 2.3 million people are reported to be affected by the conflict, of which 2 million remain in need of life-saving and life-sustaining humanitarian assistance. Recent months have also been characterized by an important increase and growing trend in returns to the districts of origin while the number of IDPs remain high, with 850.599 individuals displaced in Northern Mozambique. Civilians continue to bear the brunt of the armed violence: conflict affected populations suffer from lack of food and water, inadequate sanitation and health services, insufficient shelter, and numerous protection risks. According to WHO/UNICEF Joint Malnutrition Estimates 2023, over 570.000 children under the age of 5 are facing moderate acute malnutrition (MAM), while over 256.000 children suffer under severe acute malnutrition (SAM). Moreover, serious violations of IHL and human rights conventions by all parties to the conflict have been reported, including SGBV, psychological trauma, killings, abductions and the forced recruitment of children into armed groups. In this framework of action, CUAMM adopts a conflict sensitive approach, considering its structural and social basis in designing the project's activities. Furthermore, being Cabo Delgado prone to epidemics for food and water born disese (cholera, thyphoid fever. etc.), CUAMMadopts a flexible approach in order to respond promptly to new crisis, affecting also the nutritional status of the population.The intervention proposes an integrated approach towards nutrition aiming at addressing the most urgent needs of the most vulnerable categories, i.e. children U5 and PLWs.Thanks to this proposal, CUAMM will reach 18.360 beneficiaries, disaggregated as follows:SADDAge:- children (<18 years): 7200- Adult: 11.160, of which 3.960 PLWsGender:- Male: 6.912- Female: 11.448.Considering the status of the beneficiaries, the present intervention targets: - IDPs: 7.827;- Host communities: 10.533.Due to the complexity of the humanitarian crisis in Cabo Delgado, CUAMM considers it fundamental to direct its activities towards all people in need, regardless of their status, paying particular attention to their level of vulnerability.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The project will be in line with the cluster objectives, implementing the following main activities:- support the improvement of mobile brigades with integrated nutritional screening;- support the improvement of Integrated Community Case Management;- support the strengthening of the provision of micronutrients supplementation;- support the screening, referral and treatment of severe acute malnutrition (SAM) and moderate acute malnutrition (MAM) in children under 5 and PLWs;- support the Sensitization and delivery of key messages on Infant Young Child Feeding Counselling thanks to the effective deployment of mobile brigades.The provision of care will be enhanced through technical guidance and supportive oversight, while monitoring will be conducted via an improved data collection and analysis system, designed to facilitate informed decision-making with the aim of improving the current situation.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

CUAMM designs its projects in close cooperation with local authorities to ensure buyin and active participation of local actors. Project activities are implemented through participatory approaches, ensuring that community members and beneficiaries are actively involved in the development and implementation of every project components, thus ensuring that their needs are effectively met. Prior to theimplementation of all activities and regularly throughout the project, information meetings will be promoted at community level and with government services to ensure support and accountability. CUAMM, in coordination with the Cluster, will promote the establishment of effective project community feedbacks and complaints mechanisms, enabling beneficiaries and community members to provide feedbacks on CUAMM activities in a safe, non-threatening and accessible manner. Community feedbacks and complaints could be collected at service delivery points through focus group discussions with beneficiaries and community members. In addition, CUAMM will continue to promote access to 'Linha Verde' as an effective inter-agency complaint, feedback and information hotline, including for sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA).

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The project's activities encourage meaningful access to assistance thanks to the implementation of tailored integrated interventions strenghtening both the demand side and the supply one. On the demand side, CUAMM will identify the difficulties, challenges and barriers encountered by the different groups of population in accessing assistance. Togheter with the beneficiaries and the local authorities, common, accepted and feasible meaures will be identified and put in place. In addition, community awareness activities will be carried out in collaboration with local civil society organisations and activists to develop better attitudes towards gender and disability among community members and leaders, and to ensure that women and girls are fully aware of the services available at the nearest functional and operational centres. On the supply side, CUAMM, in collaboration with the local authorities and the cluster, will contribute to facilitate the referral and follow up system for critical and complicated patients from communities to specialised services. In collaboration with local CSOs and activists, CUAMM will produce accessible IEC materials and participate to inclusive awareness campaigns promoting the active participation of disadvantaged groups. i.e. women, girls, people with disabilities and elderly persons. Finally, considering the inequalities affecting women and elderly persons in participating to the activities, CUAMM will implement specific measures to facilitate their active involvement and engagement in the intervention.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

CUAMM analyses the barriers, constraints and difficulties encountered by various demographic groups, impeding their equal access to humanitarian services and assistance. Specific attention will be given to identifying the barriers faced by individuals with disabilities (PwDs). A protection map will be developed by gathering community feedback and conducting an assessment of other partners/institutions. This will aid in identifying vulnerable groups and situations that may pose a threat to intervention goals. The map will assist in maintaining a focused approach, preventing the squandering of resources, and prioritising key areas and issues. In carrying out the initiatives, CUAMM will embrace and uphold a human rights-based and people-centred strategy to cater to the particular requirements of individuals with disabilities, encompassing women, men, girls, boys, and the elderly.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The proposed programme aims to minimise its impact on the environment through sensitising nutriton workers and community nutrition workers on the safe disposal of consumables, particularly personal protective equipment. Moreover, the project willprioritise raising awareness for environmental and personal hygiene, sanitation, and prevention/control of disease outbreaks for both host and displaced communities at the community level. During the project implementation, CUAMM will promote the integration of activities that involve vehicle movements, aiming to reduce the number of cars and motorbikes travelling to the same location and thereby decreasing fuel consumption. Additionally, we will ensure the careful monitoring of fuel storage and management to prevent any potential leakage. Waste products are hazardous to the surrounding environment. It is imperative to engage in discussions with the local health authorities on how to safely handle the products resulting from the drugs store dejunking. Regular exercises should be carried out to avoid wastage being left in the field, potentially contaminating water sources or cattle feeding spaces.
