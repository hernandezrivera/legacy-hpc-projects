Enhancing IDPs site in North West Syria through strengthening of Fire mitigation, care and maintenance, management and coordination

Basic Info

Project Name

Enhancing IDPs site in North West Syria through strengthening of Fire mitigation, care and maintenance, management and coordination

Start Date

01/01/2024

End Date

31/12/2024

Project Summary

This project aims at providing the following services to IDPs sites:
a) Care and maintenance ten IDP sites including the following:
1. Train 500 IDPs (youth, women, and men) on tents and road maintenance and connect them with the recruitment committees for CFW activities of care and maintenance in the camps.
2. Conduct care and maintenance for ten IDP sites during 2024. The major work will be through technicians and labor to conduct light to medium maintenance services such as for electricity, water, and sewage network in the camps. Shafak will use the voucher modality or CFW as a redemption method for the recruited staff from the camps. Camp selection would be through identifying the camps that are at risk of floods or have had the effects of floods previously, in addition to applying the vulnerability criteria where camps with higher numbers of women, elderly, children, and disabled persons would be prioritized.
b) Strengthening emergency preparedness and (fire) response in IDP sites through establishment of fire and safety warden committees through training of 240 IDPs on camps management in ten IDP sites. Fire and safety warden activities will include fire extinguishers and fire blankets provision in addition to providing TOT for the camps fire committees and training for camps residents.
c) Implement tailored livelihood activities designed to enable HHs to restore their assets and leave IDP sites for better solutions and develop spontaneous exit strategies through the provision of income generation activities and TVET training for 300 HHs (1,500 individuals) in three camps in Harim and Azaz districts to enhance HHs resilience and their capacity to restore their assets and leave the camps during 2024.
d) Develop and strengthen the capacity of camp management actors on camp governance and protection mainstreaming through the training of 120 representatives of camps on CCCM principles.
The scope of work has been limited to training of IDPs, Care and Maintenance and camps committees' formation activities, and fire safety activities as they are the top priorities in camps in order to mitigate the effects of crisis floods and fire incidents. Yet, Care and Maintenance activities are still top priorities out of these activities. During the project, Shafak intends to communicate with the stakeholders like camps managements, beneficiaries, local authorities, and donors through reporting on the displacement, floods, and fire incidents and creating advocacy messages to support IDPs in the camps in addition to the meetings. Shafak normally has with the camps representatives during signing MoUs and communication channels for the beneficiaries like hotline and complaints boxes, and WhatsApp.
Shafak keeps tracking of incident reports and displacement reports issued by CCCM and closely monitoring the displacement and incidents on the ground (bi-weekly basis), which are reported to CCCM. In case of underfunding for these activities, camps at risk with more vulnerable groups like women, the elderly, and the disabled will be prioritized.
