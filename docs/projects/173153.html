Project Summary

In a survey conducted by WES , South Darfur , it is found that Access to improved water sources for the state population as per 2020 census was 49.9% (urban: 80.5%, rural: 43.7%) with the average daily per capita consumption of 12.1 l/c/d (urban: 41.7, rural: 7.3 l/c/d) as of 2020.
Access to household safe means of excreta disposal for the state population is estimated to be 37% as of 2020. The state’s Localities were ranked into three zones to reflect the HH sanitation services availability ranging from fair (3 Localities) to critical
About 70% of the state’s IDPs have access to improved drinking water
Access to adequate sanitation for the state’s schools is estimated as 27% and for the state’s health facilities is estimated as 63%.
The state’s water, sanitation and hygiene sector major challenges were found to be:
• Deficiencies in the institutional setup, structures and policy instruments.
• Overlap and fragmentation of responsibilities among the sector stakeholders.
• Significant shortfall of qualified staff, equipment and supplies.
• Inadequate information system.
• No coordination body at state level for the promotion of the sanitation component.
• Inadequate consultation with communities during the project planning and implementation phases.
The state is highly affected by the protracted armed conflicts over the last years which resulted in displacement of population and restriction on access to many places.The State’s IDPs are mainly staying in camps in different localities particularly around the big towns in the state such Nyala,
Access to improved water sources as per 2008 census was49.9% (urban: 80.5%, rural: 43.7%). Based on the estimated output of the different functioning water facilities, the 2010 average per capita consumption is estimated at 12.1 l/c/d (urban: 41.7l/c/d, rural: 7.3 l/c/d)
Based on the number of existing latrines in the IDP camps (17,789 HH latrines for the total IDP population of 297,069), the average access to the adequate sanitation for the IDPs is estimated as 35.9%
After a rapid assessemnt and meetings with WES South Darfur, we found that the Health, Hygeine and water situations in the targetd areas is deteriorating very fast. According to the Inter-Agency Assessment, 2000 House hold latrines in Kalma IDP Camp, used by 6000 femals and 5000 females, need maintenance while 400 new latrines need to be digged. to be used by the camp residents No Agency did come up to treat this issue and people are defacting in the open.
It is clear from the rapid Assessment conducted by the Applicant that The Hygeine Situation is deteriorating in in Shawaya and Aradaiba IDPs camps in Kass Locality The garbage is in the open, Citizens unaware of the Least standards of cleanliness and Health Education is unheard of. In Kass Town, host community for more than 26000 IDPs 14000 of them are females, ,and itself is arecovery area with 45000(6000 children, 15500 femles and 21500 males) , WES constucted two Mini-Water Yards and both the yards ceased to operate, for the locals are unable to afrford the operating and maintenace costs. .
Interviewing a sample of The women in the targeted camps Kalma, Shawa and Aradiaba, we found that they are the ones usually fetch water and throw away the garbage, targeting the female community would be of utmost importnace to increase their wellbeing and of the population in general .
The female and males needs in the camps are the same as it is clear from the interviews and meetings . women headed households are more affected by the water and hygeine situation in the camps.
AOSCD with its vast experience in Darfur, in The fields of constructing latrines, Hygeine and health Campaigns and Digging of water wells is seeking to address the above issues in the 3 mentioned ares through tailor designed activities planned in the project

1 - Needs

This project is addressing Sustaining and expanding access to WASH services for vulnerable populations affected by conflict or natural disaster in Sudan, ensuring mitigation of environmental impact and Strengthen disaster preparedness in Sudan by building the capacity of communities, government and partners to anticipate and respond to critical WASH needs interventions in areas of high risk. South Darfur State is negatively impacted by the conflict that began in 2004. . It has disrupted the normality increasing vulnerability of an already fragile population and thereby potentially increasing the Risks of the locals. The unstable security situation, coupled with limited access basic services made the health situation very risky specially for the Displaced, This project intends to improve the Water sanitation and hygiene with special emphasis on IDPs camps Locations through provision of essential package, upgrading capacity of communities and locals and mainstreaming environmental protection Women headed households are among the most vulnerable groups. Women represent 47% of the state population, about 35 % of the total labor force in the public and private sectors and 55 % of agricultural labor and are generally regarded as subordinate to their male counterparts, have less voice, less autonomy, fewer opportunities and lowered self-esteem from childhood to old age. Scarce resources are less likely to be directed to their needs. The deprivations and suffering they face are not fully taken into account, and the significant contribution they make to household is undervalued. AlthoughAOSCD Organization was one the very few agencies that has been on the field since the eruption of the conflict, working in the very localities that are targeted by this action, we still lack the adequate resources and funding to meet the extreme needs of theIDPs, Returnees and locals What is worsening the situation is that no immediate funding is at sight and our internal reserves wouldn't cover the proposed activities. Even the Government support in the targeted locations which was provided, is far from enough

2 - Activities/Outputs

In Kalma Camp , The project will conduct Hygeine Campaigns expected to reach 10000 Males and 10000 females Garbage Cleaning campaigns, conduct House Hold Visits for awareness and follow up, jerry Can Cleaning campaigns . The project also will organize 20 Group Discussions on Health Education - 10 discussions for males and 10 for females and Rehabilitation of Water Schemes for 4 sources While in Shawaya IDPs camp , The project is planning Rehabilitation of 200 House Hold latrines and Digging of new 50 New Latrines In Aradaiba , The project is planning the Rehabilitation and Maintenance of 2 Mini Water Yards and Rehabilitation of 12 Hand Pumps The project will help strengthen the hygiene base of affected communities. In each of the targeted areas, the project will train 100 Community members leaders, Ministry staff and HAC staff and LOCal NGOs and CBOs ( 50males and 50 females) on Sanitation and Hygeine services and Emergency Response Preparedness A separate training would be conducted for 10 persons in each location (5 males and 5 females ) on Water Management The project will help reduce the vulnerability of the affected communities to climate change and desertification through environment protection practices TrainingsThe project will strengthen the capacity of local leaders and ministry departments to support WASH activities and reduce or prevent the impacts of disasters as well as monitor the WASH Situation on regular basis. A sensitization workshop and trainings will be organized to achieve this.
