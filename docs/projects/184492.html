Project Summary

The target area for this proposed project will be in Garbaharey and Elwak of Gedo region jubaland Somalia with target population of 30,000 drought affected people through enhancing WASH activities. These regions are accessible and ARD is currently doing WASH and health Activities. In collaboration with the local authority, partnership with main water vendors in rural villages in Garbaharey and Elwak districts, ARD will provide emergency water voucher to the 10,400 (15600 men, 2,600 women, 3,120 boys and 3,120 girls) targeted beneficiaries including people with disabilities and marginalized communities in the targeted districts, which they can redeem for water at the specified water vendors. Provision of reliable and sustainable access to environmental sanitation through construction of latrines in IDP camps from the target districts. Distribution of 80 sanitation sets to 6 IDP camps, in order to improve the environmental sanitation of the targeted locations. Conducting comprehensive hygiene promotion campaigns focusing hand washing with soap practices to 15,000 men, women, boys and girls through visiting house to house, health facilities, and nutrition centers. ARD will also train 24 Community Hygiene Promoters (CHPs) shared equally men and women, selected from the target communities and IDPs, so that in their turn they can educate the entire population to change their behaviors

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

ARD sets targets for female inclusion in all activities and project staff ensures these are met; communities including VRCs are sensitized to this requirement prior to implementation.ARD is already mitigating this risk by implementing programmes in the target area and having a good relationship with local authorities and communities. ARD also employs extensive community consultation and feedback mechanisms to mitigate this risk.The established government structures responsible for security issues in the respective district is functioning. In case of deteriorating the security of the target location .ARD has a longstanding presence in the target locations where it implemented different projects. During its presence, ARD established direct relations with the community leaders/elders and local authorities to promote humanitarian principles, gain access, enhance program awareness, monitoring on issues including complaints/ feedback about operation within the framework of the new complaint/ feedback system. ARD also maintains proper balancing between different clans/ sub-clans, including service provision, contracting for services or goods or in staff recruitment. These measures reinforce local acceptance and protection of the staff by the communities, local authorities and other interest groups.ARD will identify the risk of conflict in the project area and immediately avert this risk. ARD will closely engage the local leaders, government officials and the community to sort out any possible threat. At all times, the Do no harm principle will be applied to prevent harm to the staff, clients and community.
