Project Summary

Specific Objectives :
 Children are trained to live in love, justice and truth with respect for the dignity of the human person.
 To assist children from ten (10) to twenty (20) years rendered helpless by the socio-political crisis to be self sustaining.
 Children are prepared for specific jobs or careers that will make them self-reliant
Project Summary:
Overview:
There is no opportuned and timely moment as this in the on-going socio-political crisis which has turned into an armed conflict between government forces and separatists in the Northwest and Southwest Regions of Cameroon. From the birth of the crisis in November 2016 till up to date, children of school going age and students in various institutions of learning have been held hostage by the crisis. We the Tertiary Sisters of Saint Francis (hereafter TSSF) Cameroon Province based in Shisong, see it expedient to give our charism of Education another face lift. This is a God-given moment to intensify the education and formation of consciences of children in the light of the Gospel of Christ. The various abuses, violence, discrimination, wanton destruction of property, terror with horror and random killings are symptomatic of a much deeper problem embedded at every level of society – the education of the mind at the detriment of education of the heart and body
Effects:
Series of unrests and violence have continued unabated in most of our towns of north west and south west Regions of Cameroon. There has been loss of human life, destruction of property of citizens, gross abuse of human rights as demonstrated by eye witness accounts. The school system has been paralyzed and dysfunctional. Children are growing up in frustration because the dreams of their lives have been shattered by those they were looking up to.
Conviction:
Nothing surpasses the greatness or dignity of the human person. Our young ones deserve the right to their dignity and right to education. The future of our Cameroon lies in our children. Their formation in good conscience and education will therefore be our priority. Our children are living in danger. We want a safe future for them as well as continuity as the aged are passing out. The quality of family life is impaired as children not going to school have become problem children. The damage needs to be repaired through holistic education.
Action:
Since we are not permitted to use our existing school structures especially in Kumbo, the TSSF have created a Child Friendly Space to accommodate children in order to help them develop a set of values which can serve as a guide to behaviour so that they can be of help to society that is already at the verge of hopelessness. This child friendly space carries out activities such as ICT, Guidance and Counseling, Sewing and Embroidery, Food Science, Home Craft, General Knowledge, and Moral Education. Its goal in the first place is to provide a social stamina to children in the midst of the crisis to cope with the unbearable trauma that has haunted them for long, in this stage of growth where their dreams have failed to be realized.
Justification:
It is in the light of these vulnerable children that we table for multi-purpose financial assistance. With a shattered economy, the children have the eagerness to learn, to have a trade, to be self-reliant but the means to pay for services are not there.
Your assistance will help us to give meaning, purpose and direction to the lives of these children. Your aid will be a timely gesture to ease families already stretched out by poverty and the crisis.
Conclusion:
It is our firm belief that from experience, and information, we are convinced of the necessity for action to more than ever before be involved in the education of children, traumatized by the crisis

1 - Besoins / Needs

1. BENEFICIARIESThis project will benefit Seven Hundred (700) children in villages around Shisong, Djottin and Nkar in Bui, and three hundred (300) in Njinikom in Boyo, Mambu Bafut in Mezam and Akwaya in Manyu Divisions, totaling One Thousand (1000) who are under the care of the Tertiary Sisters of St. Francis. 2. NEEDS Their needs vary based on the availability of provisions necessary to carry out a particular exercises or training. Their needs include: tuition, sewing machines, exercise books and pens, computers, internet services, counseling, sewing material, house wiring equipment, beads, twain for handicraft work, velvets material and yara wool for embroidery, needles/pins, sporting equipment, charts, gas cookers and covid-19 kits. 3. ACTIVITIESIn the light of the forgone effects of the armed conflict, the TSSF have not remained indifferent to the plight of the underpriviledged children and the trauma brought by the ever widening of COVID-19 pandemic. - ICT (Office Automation Secretary)- Basic Computer Maintenance- Electricity (House wiring)- Sewing/Embriodery/beads making- Website Building (HTML)- Counseling and Guidance on daily basis- Sports and Games- Practical Sensitization and Prevention of Covid-19- Formal Teaching and Learning (Njinikom, Bafut and Akwaya)- Attending of Meetings, Seminars and Refresher Courses.- Education on Environmental protection 3.1 Guidance and counseling There is a great out cry for regular guidance and counseling as things are seemingly out of control. Counseling needs to be administered regularly and systematically to curb the emotional stress of war in children by experts. Many are traumatized because they have lost families members, witnessed their homes being torched down with fire. Counseling exercise is handling stress, drug or substance related disorders and serves as a code of conduct to minimize its incidence. Peer group conflicts and delinquency are handled. The TSSF continues to brave all odds to reach out to the needy children in the suburbs, organise seminars on how to live in times of war. 3.2 ICT Training CentreThe TSSF have not remained indifferent to the needs of the children rendered helpless by the school blackout especially in Bui. They deemed it expedient to create a child friendly space to keep the children in focus. One of the activities carried out in this centre is the training in ICT skills. There are limited computers with 20-25 children crowding over one computer. This makes practice and learning difficult. 3.3 Regular Meetings Four coordination, monitoring and evaluation, meetings will be held before the end of the project. These meetings will seek to give advice to actors’ progress towards targets. 3.4 Monitoring/Evaluation  There shall be periodic data collection of underpriviledged children in areas within the scope of this project.  The number of children on counseling roll and actually handled. There shall be periodic visitation by project coordinator to monitor incidence of child abuse, sexual harassment and availability and use of resources.  Monitoring if there are incidents of violence, drug abuse as spilt over of the crisis, Work performance of all involved in the execution of the projet shall be monitored 4. EXPECTED RESULTSOn completion of training the children shall be:• Competent• Self-fulfilling• Self reliant• Critical in thought and objective in action• Potentials are developed and weaknesses subdued• Less active in crime action 5. CONCLUSIONSince the TSSF have been identifying with the sufferings, despair, poverty and hopelessness affecting children in the Anglophone Cameroon the epicenter of the armed conflict, irrespective of religion, gender, race and political affiliation, they have condemned and denounced these ills and have called upon the people to rise above tribal lines to justice and reconciliation, to take up their responsibility in order to attend peace and development.
