Project Summary

Mercy-USA will target 4,500 HH with USD$100 one-off emergency cash per HH (MPC). This intervention is aligned with FSL cluster and CWG to ensure the value covers the full SMEB.
The fund for this project is secured to be implemented during 2021
Mercy-USA will respond to any emergency across NW Syria with coordination through FSL, SNFI, WASH and CWG cluster to avoid any overlap. This emergency includes but is not limited to displacement, flood, fire, major storms, and other forms of emergency that may arise.
Mercy-USA will conduct vulnerability assessment and verification before the cash distribution and will ensure the response to be implemented within 7 days from the emergency to ensure effective response is in place
Mercy-USA will have contract with Financial Service Provider (FSP) to cover all targeted locations for timely response to any emergency, so the FSP has the capacity for cash distribution with huge amount
Locations will cover broad area in NW Syria at the district level: Ariha, Harim, Idleb, Jisr Ash-Shugur, Afrin, Jebel Saman where Mercy-USA has full access, even though we will focus on four sub-districts: Idleb, Maaret Tamsrin, Jandairis, and Darkosh

5 - Implementation of activities

Mercy-USA conducts rapid cash feasibility assessment in area for newly displaced (market accessibility, functionality, risk analysis), while engaging with local authority, host community; and coordinating with other humanitarian organisations planning responses in same area to avoid overlap and ensure complementarity. Following beneficiary verification and determination that MPC is most timely and appropriate modality, Mercy-USA distributes 100 USD in cash per HH along with pre-vetted contracted Financial Service Provider (FSP)•Targeting:-Eligibility:Newly displaced HHs (30 days or less) from new emergencies (weather-related, fire, attacks)-Vulnerability: (coordinated) blanket approachData protection: All BNF data are kept in separate server where FSP has no access, under supervision of Mercy-USA program management only.Community sensitization to MPC targeting criteria, scope. CFM is done by project team, banners and posters before distribution CFM channels: WhatsApp, hotline, email, complaints box and in person.Mercy-USA coordinates all activities with: Local Councils (LC), IDP and host representatives and MOU is signed with respective LC per community or camps.The team prepares Payment Sheets for approval by Project Coordinator. Once verified by Mercy-USA staff, beneficiaries receive MPC through FSP with monitoring from Mercy-USA M&E team. Beneficiaries sign Payment Sheet after verifying authenticity of USD banknotes and amount. By end of aid delivery, project team completes distribution progress report, including total amount of MPC distributed and currency, with both FSP and Mercy-USA staff signing to certify completion of distribution. Mercy-USA M&E team conducts post-distribution monitoring after 1 week

7 - Does the project include a PSEA strategy ?

Yes

7.1 - Please list any risks related to sexual exploitation and abuse that have been identified during the project design phase and any risk mitigation and prevention measures that have been adopted and built into the project

SEA Risks: • Overcrowded during the cash distribution which may cause SEA • Mixed distribution lines for males and females • All distribution staff are males • The cash distribution is not accessible to all gender groups • No shared clear information about timing and amount of cash• No clear procedure regarding loss of card• Taxation on the MPC assistance • No complaints and feedback mechanism (CFM) in place Risk mitigation: • Ensure extend distribution days to avoid overcrowding • Separate lines for males and females in all distribution process• Ensure gender balance in the hiring process, ideally to be 50% of the team are female • The cash distribution sites will be chosen to be accessible to all gender groups especially female HH and disables • Clear information about timing, cash amount, and procedures about loss of card of the distribution will be shared with all beneficiaries beforehand and will be existed in the banners in the distribution sites • Sensitization about the scope of the project to all communities with highlighting “This assistance is Free” and this statement will be printed on the beneficiary cards. • Different CFM channels will be in place such as WhatsApp, hotline, email, phone as well as physically by MEAL team. • PSEA awareness materials (such as posters and pamphlets) and reporting hotline will be visible to beneficiaries at the distribution site

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risks:-Markets are not functioning which may affect the use of MPC-General daily security threats including robbery and looting.- Macro level conflict related threats including aerial bombardment, advancement of hostile actors, shifting alliances, border closure, and Chemical attack.-Danger to staff and beneficiaries including conflict between beneficiaries.- Delays in fund dispersal- Exchange rate fluctuations between USD and TRY and SYP-Uncontrolled Spread of COVID-19 pandemic in NW Syria which may cause complete lockdown-FSPs offering over-the-counter services do not have sufficient capacity to meet higher caseload-FSPs forced to reduce staff and office hours, limiting over-the-counter service-Limited trading; market shutdown-Critical items unavailable due to market closures, increased demand and hoarding Mitigation:-Regular price monitoring and markets assessment to ensure this intervention will not cause harm to the local markets-Currency is USD and in case of huge currency fluctuation, Mercy-USA might increase the transfer value in coordination with CWG or change the modality-Clear steps have been taken to mitigate risks between staff and beneficiaries including gender-based violence.- Project staff may be dismissed at any time in the event of endangering beneficiaries or other staff, and this will be clearly outlined in each employment contract.- Consider market-based programming, supporting critical markets/traders (actors across the supply chain, including small retail shops) traders directly, by providing them capital to augment their stock of essential goodsFSP vetting will be done in coordination with donors to exclude any FSP listed in the sectioned list of OFAC for US treasury, SAM, UNSC
