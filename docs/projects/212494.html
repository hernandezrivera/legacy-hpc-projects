Project Summary

"Daraa governorate has been severely affected by the crisis especially the health system which is heavily disrupted in addition to a large scale of destruction of civilian infrastructure, major gaps in basic services – especially electricity, water and sanitation networks.
The targeted locations under this project which are newly accessible areas: Daraa Al balad and Ash-Shajara are classified as 3 on health severity scale where an increased incidence of infectious diseases such as diarrhea, upper respiratory infections, lice and scabies where noticed. It is estimated that Daraa Al Balad hosts 107,213 people in health need inclusive of 15,856 Spontaneous Returnees and over 30,000 IDPs. Averagely; Young to middle age children constitute: 6.9% female and 7.4 % male, Adolescent: 8.6% female and 7.3 % male, Young adults: 6.8% female and 6.6% male. Middle –age group: 3.9 % female and 4.6 % male. Elderly: 2 % female. The estimated percentage of people with disability is 23.6%. While Ash-Shajara hosts approximately 41,387, off these 22,763 in health need. Averagely; Young to middle age children constitute: 10.6%female and 7.9 % male, Adolescent: 3.4% female and 3.6 % male, Young adults: 6.8% female and 7.9% male. Middle –age group: 3.3 % female and 3.4 % male. Elderly: 0.5 % female and 4.9 % male. Furthermore; persons with disabilities are disproportionately affected by the crisis. They face barriers in accessing support and life-saving services and face multiple and intersecting forms of discrimination and marginalization.
"The primary objective of this project is to significantly improve the quality, accessibility, and efficiency of healthcare services in Daraa. This will be achieved through the following key goals:
Medical Training and Staffing.
Public Health Initiatives: Implementing community health programs focused on preventive care, health education, and addressing prevalent diseases and health issues specific to the Daraa region.
Emergency Medical Services: Strengthening emergency medical services (EMS) to provide rapid and efficient response to crises, with a special focus on conflict-affected and hard-to-reach areas.
Medical Supplies and Equipment: Ensuring a steady and reliable supply of essential medicines, medical equipment, and consumables to all healthcare facilities.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

"In line with the national health sector priorities and strategic health sector objective in terms of increasing access to life-saving and lifesustaining, equitable health services for those most vulnerable and in need in areas with high severity scale; Khotwa Assocaition aims through this project to :1) increasing access to health care through the provision of comprehensive and integrated essential health services to cover the health needs of vulnerable populations in Daraa Governorate, including medical consultation and medication on Reproductive, Child Health (RMNCH), as well as continuity of treatment for people who live with non-communicable disease . As well as increasing awareness on primary health core subjects, 2) Improving access to Trauma care management through providing rehabilitationservices.3) Improving access and delivery of MHPSS services.To that end;Khotwa Association shall strengthen the health services delivery through sustaining the functionality of the previoulsy established Family wellbeing center in Daraa Al Balad. The center will provide integrated health services of primary health care and MHPss as well as secondary health care on referral basis and strengthening the provision of rehabilitation services to people with disability as follows:The center will resume the operation of the three clinics operating on 1 shift covering medical consultations and medication on internal andchronic diseases, child health, RH and gynecology. As well as MHPss services will be provided in the center through trained community socialworkers trained on WHO guidelines and tools and other evidence- based protocols. Noting that WHO will strengthen the capacity of the healthand community care providers to enable them providing evidence and community based culturally appropriate kind of services.The MHPss services include:Provision of group psychological intervention sessions.Provision of counseling group sessions.Recreational activities.Provision of raising awareness sessions on MHPSS / GBV topics / Domestic violence.Provision of individual psychological counseling sessionsProvision of interventions related to the Problem management programProvision of interventions related to Physiological First Aid/ First Line Support.Conduct Community Initiatives to improve hosting community status.Conduct MH campaigns at the community level linked with the WHO agenda.Detected patients who require diagnosis investigation and advanced medical treatment under secondary care will be supported on a referral basis to the nearest Laboratories and Radiographic centers in the area where the Khotwa Association will have an agreementto facilitate the referral process as well as to pre-contracted hospitals. Similarly; people with disability either those who have amputated limbs as a result of the current crisis will be supported on a referral basis to specialized medical centers in close coordination with the trauma technical unit at WHO/ Damascus.
