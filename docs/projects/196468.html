Project Summary

The proposed intervention meets the needs of vulnerable groups especially IDPs in settlements and the increasing demand of the overburdened communities due to the continuous displacement movements.
The project activities will be in line with strategic objective SO1 by providing life-saving and life-sustaining humanitarian assistance to the most vulnerable people with an emphasis on those in areas with high severity of needs.
MWL will implement three main activities targeting 9240 HHs (46200 persons) 9702 Men/ 10626 Women/ 12474 Boys/13398 Girls) including 1360 PWDs. The project seeks to improve the vulnerable affected- populations’ life conditions by rehabilitation the houses for both IDPs and host communities, building concrete shelters as dignified shelter for the IDPs, and providing life-saving and life sustaining shelter & NFI assistance as follows:
1. MWL will conduct medium rehabilitation for the houses (1000$ each) targeting 1000 HHs (5000 persons) in Dana and Maarat Tamasrin sub-districts.
2. MWL will install RHUs in Azaz district, Aghtrin sub-district targeting 240 HHs (1200 persons).
3. The third intervention targets 8000 HHs (40,000 persons) of the affected population within 20 camps in Afrin, Dana, and Maarat Tamasrin sub-districts by delivering seasonal and supplementary NFIs assistance including winter clothing, stoves, and fuel. Each household will receive a voucher of 200$ and prioritize its needs of these items accordingly.
During the beneficiary’s selection, MWL will consider prioritizing the following:
• Households headed by female, child, disabled, or elderly,
• Households with severely ill or disabled members
• Children below 5, elderly, pregnant or lactating women, with special attention to those recently displaced or living overcrowded.

3 - Needs

Based on HNO 2022, safe, warm and secure shelter remains out of reach for many vulnerable families across Syria, a consequence of a decade of hostilities which has severely impacted the housing stock. With over a third of the overall population living in substandard, damaged and/or inadequate shelters, shelter needs are particularly pronounced during winter. Of the 6.92 million people estimated to be displaced across Syria, 3.37 million live in inadequate shelters with some form of damage67. Over 2 million IDPs live in last resort sites, the vast majority in Idleb and Aleppo Governorates, in particularly inadequate, temporary shelters such as tents and makeshift shelters which provide limited protection against the elements and require frequent replacement and/or repair. Poor shelter conditions, overcrowding and limited to no access to basic services such as electricity, education, water, sanitation/waste disposal and health services in last resort sites significantly increase protection, GBV, and public health risks, including COVID-19. Long-standing needs of an estimated 6.9 million IDPs remain staggering, particularly for over two million people in 1,760 informal settlements and planned camps, often hosted in inadequate shelters and with limited access to basic services.

Coordinate with Shelter/NFI and CCCM clusters for ensuring no overlapping in the targeted camps.3. Holding focused groups discussions with older persons, and women in order to inform them of the project goals, increase awareness to the project activities and coordinate beneficiary selection process to reach the most vulnerable persons and ensure impartiality. 4. MWL will conduct the houses Rehabilitation for 1000 HHs. Starting with beneﬁciaries’ selection process, verification, qualiﬁed engineers will assess the houses.5. Installing 240 RHUs with interior facilities, MWL will identify 240 of the most vulnerable IDP , who live in inadequate shelter or tents and verify their info through field visits. The units will be served with all necessary interior infrastructure and facilities. MWL will evaluate all the activities through its MEAL team to measure the satisfaction and impact on the BNFs and targeted communities. 6. Provision of seasonal and supplementary NFIs including heating fuel, MWL will receive the beneficiary lists and pay visits to a random sample of beneficiaries to verify they meet the project vulnerable criteria. The modality of delivering the assistance is restrict voucher, cash and in-kind.
