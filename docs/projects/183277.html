Project Summary

This Project is designed to provide life-saving nutrition services in poor neighborhoods with high density of Lebanese, Refugees and Migrants in vulnerable situation through both community and facility-based interventions including:
1.Infant and young child feeding (IYCF) practices optimization, micronutrient supplementation distribution, and reporting of blanket distribution of Infant formula
2. Screening, referral, and treatment of malnutrition (SAM without complications and MAM) among children under 5 and PLW
The project will contribute to saving lives of the most vulnerable people, children under 5, in addition to PLWs by providing coordinated, accessible nutrition services and informative data to derive recommendations needed to inform the nutrition sector response in the country.
The project will target 41,237 people (Host communities, Syrian Refugees, PRS/PRL and migrants) in Akkar and North Lebanon disaggregated as follows: 15,956 Pregnant and Lactating Women (PLWs) & 25,281 Children under 5 (13,002 girls - 12,279 boys).
19 localities will be targeted through the support which will be provided to 8 non-supported Primary Healthcare Centers (PHCs) in high populated areas in the targeted Cadasters in the North & Akkar.
6 outreach staff (3 IYCF counselors and 3 Community Health Workers) will be based out of each PHCs to provide visual screening, referrals and IYCF services for PLW and children under 5. These outreach teams will work in parallel and direct coordination with the selected health facilities in the targeted areas.
Staff will assess the nutritional status of children under 5 and distribute nutritional supplies to them as needed. Additionally, IYCF services will be provided.
Moreover, an assessment of the nutritional status of children aged 6-59 months in order to generate information for an improved response by conducting a SMART survey. The SMART survey will be conducted at national level across all 8 Lebanese Governorates + the camps. The overall objective of the survey is to conduct a nutrition assessment representative at the national and governorate level to determine the nutrition status of children under 5 years of age, maternal nutrition status for pregnant and lactating women as well as critical IYCF practices. The survey results will be used to derive recommendations needed to inform the nutrition sector response in the country.
A pre and rapid coordination combined with referrals will be put in place whenever the team identifies any sign of abuse or violence among children under 5 and or mothers. All cases of violence against women and children will be referred to the pre-identified health facility and signaled to the relevant sector.
This response will adopt the safety recommendations in the COVID-19 context.
Coordination with the nutrition sector, UNICEF, MoPH and IOM will be ensured throughout the project. Mercy-USA will also coordinate with IOCC and Al Midani and other stakeholders to avoid any overlap of activities, and to ensure a wider coverage of targeted populations including migrants, and to ensure a harmonized coordinated response. The Migrant Presence Monitoring exercise being finalized now by IOM will be shared with Mercy-USA which will give a good understanding about migrants’ geographical distribution.
This project will empower mothers and HH members through training them to screen their own children for malnutrition by teaching them how to use color-coded Mid-Upper Arm Circumference (MUAC) tapes.
Hence this project will contribute to sector specific objectives through:
- The provision of life-saving nutrition services in high needs and remote locations through both community and facility-based including screening, referral, treatment, micronutrient supplementation and infant and young child feeding (IYCF);
- An assessment of the nutritional status of children aged 6-59 months in order to generate information for an improved response by conducting a SMART survey.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

19 localities in Akkar and North governorates will be supported with preventive nutrition services (at community and facility levels) through outreach teams that travel from the following 8 PHCs. Moreover, 8 healthcare facilities have accessible regular quality to SAM and MAM curative services for all PLWs and children under 5 in the targeted localities. Staff will assess the nutritional status of children under 5 and distribute nutritional supplies to them as needed. IYCF services will be provided through:- IYCF sessions- Counseling PLWs & caregivers of children under 24 months of age and providing messages on IYCF recommended practices to mothers who have malnourished children- Promoting timely initiation of breastfeeding, exclusive breastfeeding and introduction of safe, age appropriate and nutrition diversified complementary food (including related hygiene messaging)- Provision of Skilled Infant and Young Child Feeding (IYCF) counselling and support to for breastfed and non-breastfed children under 6 months of age- Distributing micronutrients and nutrition supplies for the PLWs and children - Reporting breast milk substitute distribution to the cluster Moreover, an assessment of the nutritional status of children aged 6-59 months will be conducted in order to generate information for an improved response by conducting a SMART survey.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

Protection mainstreaming will be maintained in all project phases. Activities are designed to ensure safe & equal access to inclusive & non-discriminatory provision of nutrition services. Mercy-USA understands the breastfeeding topic sensitivity in the targeted community, which will be taken into consideration during the hiring process to ensure a gender balance as much as possible. Moreover, beneficiaries will be clearly informed that all nutrition services are free of charge, and the day, time & location of the community services will be discussed with community members & leaders to determine the safest most accessible location to allow access to service with dignity & without exposure to harm. Mercy-USA will identify any sign of abuse or violence among PLWs & children, all cases will be referred to the sector. Full adherence with the Do No Harm principle will be ensured and PSEA & GBV issues will be dealt with firmly. All project staff will be trained on PSEA & GBV prevention.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

The project's activities are designed taking into account considerations for gender & different needs of women, boys, girls and PWDs, opportunities for other groups to give feedback and influence the program design in a manner that meets gender needs. Mercy-USA conducted an assessment during the design phase to engage all affected groups.Individuals, particularly those considered vulnerable such as women, children and disabled, will be provided with the necessary information and resources to ensure access to the complaint mechanisms. Mercy-USA will dedicate a WhatsApp number as well as a complaints email address to receive all feedback/complaints from beneficiaries. The person making the complaint will be kept informed of any progress and his/her identity will remain confidential. Mercy-USA will conduct monthly satisfaction surveys which will be reported quarterly using a random sampling of beneficiaries (including PWDs). Results will be shared with the relevant program department.
