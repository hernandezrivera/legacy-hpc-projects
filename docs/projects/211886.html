Project Summary

As reported by HNO 2024, there is 5.9 Million malnutritioned population out of which 0.544 Million are Pregnant and Lactating Women (PLWs) in dire need for nutrition assistance as reported in January 2024. In Afrin sub district alone, the rate of malnutritioned PLWs and CU5 is a staggering over 25%. The access to health facilities is limited by PLWs unaffordability to reach and / or follow nutritional advice.
Another major factor playing a vital part in infant health is lack of awareness of young child feeding practices. This coupled with lack of mother support groups puts a vast majority of PLWs in severity scale of 4 and 5. Amongst all PLWs, a vast majority (90%) reported unaffordability to cope with nutritional needs. This is complexed by social issues such as underage marriages, resulting in malnutritioned PLWs in age group of 15 – 17 years. It is reported that 25% of the PLWs in age range of 15-17 are malnutritioned. This age groups have no coping mechanism except begging for food or picking food from garbage. This was observed during implementation of programs previously in Afrin sub district.
Building upon the previous program, the proposed action will provide response to 2000 PLWs, mostly IDPs. We plan to focus on 1 sub-district (Afrin). The planned programming intends to be achieved through an integrated program of assistance focused on providing
targeted, CVA, hygiene kits, coupled with awareness sessions and creation of Mother Support Groups. Nutrition Response (2000 PLWs age range 15 - 40) is a holistic approach to ensure best use of assistance
provided.
Modality – The planned activities will have a repeated support for a period 10 months through our voucher program. The voucher program will be complimented by 4 awareness sessions each month for a period of 10 months. As implemented previously,
150 Mother Support Groups will be created to support beneficiaries as well as nonbeneficiaries. There will be soft interventions on healthy cooking and hygiene for PLWs. Linkages will be formed with health centers and other health actors for provision of micro
nutrients. The selection of beneficiaries will be based on our already developed vulnerability criteria taking into consideration protection issues as well.
Data Collection and Monitoring - The monitoring will be through our well defined M&E systems. Monitoring will be diligently executed starting from Verification, Distribution Monitoring, Post Distribution Monitoring and Satisfaction Surveys. The data will be collected online, primarily using tablets and Kobo tools. We deploy several tools to include Excel and other tools to analyze and report. A robust system is in place for complaints receipt and redressal, with dedicated teams available for handling and resolving complaints. A robust system of lodging complaints is already in place through communications tools such as WhatsApp, SMS and email. Further the complainant can also lodge a complaint via hard copy and drop it in the drop box placed at our office locations.
Reporting – We will ensure prompt reporting on all of our projects through regular filing of donor as well as 4W reports and ad hoc reporting requirements.
CVA Support Systems – A fully automated and online Cash Voucher System developed internally and utilized for last 8 years will be deployed. The system is flexible to use paper vouchers or cards and can be of single or multiple use. To complement our voucher
programs, we have framework agreements with Fund Service Providers that provide cash distributions through formal arrangements. Due to this, our response time is short. The program will be implemented by Big Heart.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

It was observed during our previous programming that needs for PLWs and infants varies from individual to individual. Providing pre-determined food baskets do not result in optimal decrease in malnutrition. Big Heart in collaboration designed a CVA program for nutrition outcome, which was implemented end of 2021. The same approach was used for our FSL programs subsequently in 2022 and 2023, providing food vouchers (CVA) to PLWs. The programs resulted in optimal utilization of resources and improvements was reported. It is of prime importance that PLWs are given the flexibility of choice in nutritious food intake according to their need. This is only possible through controlled CVA intervention. Building upon our previous successful programs, this program will support PLWs in age range of 15-49 years and infants 0-59 months. The program will use its tried and tested CVA approach to counter malnutrition in PLWs and infants age 0-59. A total of 2,000 PLWs and associated 1,000 infants will be provided assistance of USD 100 each month for 10 months. To ensure intended use and avoid aid diversion, of the CVA, the beneficiaries will be able to redeem vouchers at shopkeepers selected by Big Heart, for food (fresh and dry), supplements, infant formula. Coupled with CVA, awareness session will be held for all selected beneficiaries in IYF and utilization of nutritious food. The assistance will be provided to pregnant women who have completed the first trimester of pregnancy. A strict criteria of vulnerability will be applied to include malnutrition status, earning of the household, and any disability.
