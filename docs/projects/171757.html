Project Summary

Given the conflict, insecurity, drought, and floods an estimated 2.6 million people have been displaced in Somalia. The majority of people have self-settled in over 2,000 sub-standard IDP sites in urban and peri-urban areas across the country. People displaced to these sites are living in precarious conditions and are not having their basic needs met due to inconsistent service provision or exclusion from accessing humanitarian support. 85% of the sites are informal settlements on private land and about 74% of them are in urban areas according to the Detailed Site Assessment (DSA) of the CCCM Cluster.
Sanaag, Sool and Bari regions are categorized among the worst drought hit areas with high people in need, the impact of current locust elevation has worsened the situation in those regions particularly the already frangible IDPs, while impacting their livelihood incomes and generally the living situation. Humanitarian interventions have been recorded minimal in those areas for the last due to shirking humanitarian funding in Somalia, hence this has resulted so many IDPs in those areas to migrate to the areas they think receive more humanitarian assistance. Moreover, community structures in the IDPs in those regions got destroyed and currently most of the IDPs have less systems and corporation. Safety issues and protection challenges has been recorded including Physical and mental assaults, GBV, other forms of violence, neglect and other abuses and hazardous coping strategies. Continues displacement as result of the frequent drought have been also recorded in those regions. Majority of the new IDPs and the protracted ones always live in private settlements with no protection, no livelihood, inadequate access to basic services and have no proper land.
In lieu of the above needs in those region OTP is aiming to contribute CCCM cluster effort of strengthening safe access to multi sectoral services at site level through improved site management and coordination, improve living conditions of displaced people through site development, care and maintenance, strengthening community self-management and access to information and working with IDPs towards leaning durable solution.
Specific activities which OTP will in place under this application are include, Set up of CCCM coordination structures in collaboration with appropriate government counterpart, Ensuring through CCCM coordination that relevant responders are mobilized towards providing relevant sectorial assistance to those identified as very vulnerable, Conduct Detailed Site Assessments, Establish/update master list of sites, Coordinate/ conduct intention surveys, Monitoring service delivery at site level to minimize overlaps, Provide capacity building to existing governance structures/focal points, Together with other clusters and partners, as well as the authorities, coordinate the establishment/ efficient use of referral pathways for both protection (including SGBV) and assistance/service delivery to persons with special needs, Provide training on-site maintenance to camp committees & Support community led site maintenance activities to ensure upkeep of sites (cash for work, site maintenance committees).

Project Activities

The proposed activities under this application are as following, establishing 3 CCCM coordination structures one in each region, establishing CCCM mechanisms/structures in 50 sites, training of 100 people on CCCM, conducting detailed Site Assessment in 10 districts, training 50 government officials on CCCM, in placing CCCM mobile teams in 10 districts, site improvement through cash for work targeting 300 HHs, devolving safety improvement plans for 10 districts while also improving their safety. Establishing information centers in 50 sites, supporting 5 sites towards self-determination regarding their future and providing on on-site maintenance to 50 members of camp committees. HLP and information awareness raising interventions targeting 150,000 will convened as well under this project

Reaching long term solution for the displaced people in Somalia always takes longer than expected as it requires joint efforts for all partners, governments and other stakeholders, therefore, OTP will work towards securing a long lasting solution for the displaced people in Somalia through being active in the important meetings or fora where durable solutions is discussed so that both IDPs and refugees will must likely get a permanent solution in the near future. OTP will closely work with local authorities by advocating the rights of the people so that jointly all the needs or longer terms solutions for the displaced people are addressed collectively through durable solution working groups or related forums so that partners and other stakeholders will be able to secure lands which are public so that these lands can be given to the displaced people and permanent shelters are constructed as well as ensuring rights of these vulnerable people are respected and they will have chance to live a dignified life
