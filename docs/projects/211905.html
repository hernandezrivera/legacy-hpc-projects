Project Summary

According to OCHA's HNO 2024 Statistics indicate increased exposure to ongoing hostilities, particularly in the Northwest. limited access to the Services; Displacement, which often separates children from family members, including primary caregivers, is disrupting the education of 2.45 million children aged 5-17 years out of school, increasing protection risks reported by parties to the conflict. Children and primary caregivers also suffer Vulnerabilities associated with direct risks of the worst forms of child labour, disability, physical/psychological injury, family separation (including anticipated separation due to COVID19), child recruitment, and child marriage. The project aims to reduce the above-mentioned protection risks for displaced children and the host community in northwestern Syria in Al Dana area within the CP Center of the AHF, in line with the minimum standards of CP, as the center will provide multiple services in the field of CP from through it:
1. Awareness campaigns through awareness teams targeting women, men, and children for CP issues
2- Implement CM services that address the vulnerabilities of children and primary caregivers associated with direct risks of worst forms of child labour, children with disabilities, physical and psychological injury, severe neglect, family separation (including expected separation due to earthquake), child recruitment, and child marriage. These services will be provided through referrals from the community protection committees and through the monitoring team, according to joint procedures between the authorities.
3. Provide (CVA- ECF) to cover protection gaps for children if the service is not available within the communities
4. Capacity building of CP workers of Case management..
5. Implementation of mental health and psychosocial support activities within the AHF Center for war-traumatized children.
6. Provide parenting sessions for primary care providers
The center will serve the most vulnerable children and Sexual differences between children will be taken into account by providing male and female case workers to provide protection services in a manner that takes into account gender differences. Protection services will also be directed at 50% for male children and 50% for female children. All activities are aligned with the 2024 Humanitarian Response Plan as well as the Strategic Objectives of the Protection Cluster. Services will be provided for one year.
.The project will focus on mitigating protection risks for children with disabilities by providing them with protection services, integrating them into society, and educating the community about the mechanism of dealing with this group., and all activities will be implemented according to Recommendations of the Protection Cluster and the WHOto the necessary preventive measures to protect beneficiaries and humanitarian workers.
The project will ensure the accountability of the affected population and stakeholders through coordination, participation, transparent accountability, complaints mechanism, feedback mechanism, and worker competency monitoring.
Reports will be prepared and coordinated with the Child Protection Sub-Cluster (5Ws, Case Management Reporting Tool) to report on project activities
The monitoring plan will ensure that:
1. Monitor the quality of the implemented activities and compare the action plan using case management monitoring and evaluation tools and report any issue Case management monitoring (recording - evaluation - intervention plan - closing the case and ensuring access to the service) while maintaining confidentiality and privacy of the case and that the names are encrypted
2. Track and collect project indicators to measure project progress at the output level
The project is designed to reduce protection PSEA,
The project is designed to strengthen GQ to include women and girls as an integral part of the project's activities at all stages during its tenure.
