Project Summary

"The inhabitants of makeshift camp are living with enormous health suffering because limited access to medical center, hygienic sanitation and safe water. To solve the health sufferings, RPN has taken to initiatives the project with a view to aiming continuation of existing Health Post (HP) in order to provide medical treatment under guideline of Health Sector. Assessment Team of Health Sector was deployed to Cox’s Bazar and findings revealed that the needs and gaps are still exists especially access to critical services. Thereby, in order to fill the gaps RPN with the support of Islamic Help, UK and Humanity Rises, USA and YY Doctorlary, Turkey will continue the existing two Health Post in Camp 8E, Block B-10, Balukhali, Ukhiya and Camp 19, Tanjimarkhola, Ukhiya also provision of including host community people. Main objectives of this program are to improve access to comprehensive primary health care services of Rohingya communities as well as host communities, with special emphasis on maternal and child health and nutrition's services, adolescent reproductive health and family planning service. The project will support primary eye care service with diagnosis facilities, provide refraction, screening of cataract and also provide spectacles through mobile eye unit. Subject to available of fund RPN will arrange eye camp. RPN will take this initiative with consultation and coordination with Mobile Medical Technical Group (MMTG). RPN will delivery pure water to house hold level in order to reduce water borne disease. RPN will delivery 50 thousand litter pure water through the water tanker everyday. Necessary health infrastructure has been built in a suitable place considering short distance traveling of Rohingya women and kids. Pharmacy has been established within the health post compound. The patients take medicine from pharmacy showing their prescription. During medicine delivery the patients gains knowledge about personal hygiene practice through leaflet & booklet. Family Planning Counselor visit door to door for providing counseling service and contraceptive. RPN will take this initiative with consultation and coordination with Sexual & Reproductive Health Technical Group (SRHTG). Midwife provides pregnant women antenatal and prenatal care service that come to clinic. She gives the necessary supervision, care and advice to women during pregnancy, labor and the postpartum period. She helps to decrease maternal mortality rate and pregnancy related sufferings. Our medical team will served the host community twice in a week. Treatment providing place will select consult with the local community.
Subject to available of fund RPN has willing to extend the facilities of health service for both community, Rohingya & Host."

1 - Identified Needs

"Since 25 August 2017, as many as one million forcibly displaced Rohingya populations entered into Cox’s Bazar district of Bangladesh from Myanmar and living with enormous sufferings especially limited access to health facilities. This number added to the total of 300,000 members of the Rohingya community who were residing in Bangladesh prior to the new influx. Now about 1.3 million Rohingya people are living in Cox’s Bazar district, particularly in Teknaf and Ukhiya upazilas. These refugees are now living in thirty-four different camps, and among Bangladeshi host communities. The camps have become the largest and fastest growing refugee camp in the world, where approximately 1.3 million people are living extremely close to each other without access to basic health care services. Because of close living to each other they are in extreme risk of corona virus. Anytime it may increase and become outbreak. In addition, Rohingya influx has been creates an extra pressure for Bangladeshi society. Also, it has been creating additional pressure on health system of Cox’s Bazar. Primary health care services remain limited due to lack of quality service provider in the camps. Newborn care is poor, especially at community level and referral linkages need to be improved. Family planning needs remain unaddressed, particularly for the eligible couple and support are required to strengthen neonatal care, family planning and mental health counselling. Due to shortage of adequate health facility Refugee are in grate risk. There is urgent need of quality health service provider including necessary drugs, diagnosable facilities and family planning and mental health counseling. Providing health service through establishing a health post may be the effective way to reduce the burden of health suffering of Rohingya.On the other hand, five years ago, the host community highly welcomed the Rohingya in a view to humanitarian ground but at present it is being to be burden for them. So, tension is being increasing between both parties. Day by day the situations are going out of control. So, it will be better for every stakeholder (The Rohingya & the Host Community as well as the local administration) if we can engage the host community successfully with the program activities that will help reduce health vulnerability and will be able to minimize their extra tension. Health Sector also has given priority to host community engagement into the health program activities. "
