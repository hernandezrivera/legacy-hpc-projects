Project Summary

The project will target the IDP’s and Host Community in the targeted locations in order to improve the safe, adequate and sustainable access for these service along with the improvement for the public health and environment to avoid any outbreaks caused by contamination of the drinking water and lack in the SWM which might cause an outbreak in leishmaniasis either cholera. In this project the rehabilitation for water system, sanitation system, and restoration for the SWM system. Moreover, rehabilitation for water system will target increasing the efficiency for the water station and network by conduct the needed maintenance, replacement and extension for the existing infrastructure and water station as well along with establishment for water ow meters in order to re-activate the tariff system. Rehabilitation for the sanitation system will target maintenance for the sewage network to repair the leakage points to avoid the mixture between drinking water and wastewater along with conduct needed extension for the network in order to ensure that all shelters and settlements are connected to the sewerage network to ensure the best practice for wastewater disposal along with install for rain drainage in the targeted communities and collective centers to avoid ods which caused by heavy rain. The Solid Waste Management restoration will cover distribution for the containers in the targeted areas along with providing the needed equipment, trucks, and tools to the local council/ authorities to collect and transfer the solid waste to the related landfill which will also be rehabilitated. Distribution for Hygiene kits (WASH NFI supplies) will be considered for the IDP’s in Camps and communities three times during the project duration the in 2021 (104.932 beneficiaries) will receive a hygiene kit (WASH NFI's) based on the need along with consideration for age and gender in each family along with hygiene promotion session for each family, where the main topic will be COVID 19 pre-cautionary measures.
In addition, with collaboration with MENTOR initiative prevention training and capacity building sessions for the vectors which causing leishmaniasis will take place in order to improve the capacity for preventing leishmaniasis. Nevertheless, during the implementation phase, BINAA considers all the pre-cautionary measures against COVID 19.

4 - Needs

According to the CCCM Cluster, from 14 January to 3 March, more than 152,700 IDPs were affected by the flooding, with one death and three injuries reported, as well as some 27,700 tents destroyed or damaged. Many people also had food and household items damaged or destroyed during the floods. This affected at least 418 IDP sites, and access issues were reported due to the flooding. The flooding increased the need for tent replacements, food, non-food-items, plastic sheets, multi-purpose cash assistance, ground leveling, winterization and graveling support, and the provision or repair of WASH services. According to 2021 Needs and response summary report for Syria:*The immediate response priorities for WASH are to sustain uninterrupted provision of comprehensive WASH services for 1.9 million people living in IDP sites across NWS and NES. In NWS alone, 630,000 IDPs in 429 camps urgently need continued humanitarian WASH assistance in addition to Mainstreaming COVID-19 response throughout all regular WASH activities with focus on ensuring appropriate water, sanitation, and handwashing standards in IDP sites. since the beginning of the Syrian crisis, the infrastructure has been targeted badly with limited access to conduct the needed maintenance or expand the current infrastructure to create a suitable environment for human life. furthermore, this situation not only affects the random camps and make them relying 100% on emergency activities such as water trucking and septic tank desludging but also affected the community who was depending on constant WASH services via water network and sewage network due to lack in the fund to conduct the required maintenance or to implement the normal expansion of the network.

5 - Implementation of activities

As BINAA has been supporting 143 camps with WASH activities during 2020,through this proposed project plans BINAA is welling to continue provision of critical WASH services to 104,147 (boys 19.218,girls 17.860,men 34.040 ,women 33.029) IDPs in 144 camps and informal settlements in Idleb and Aleppo through the following key interventions;1-Provision of safe water 35 l/p/d on daily basis needed.2-Wastewater desludging of WASH facilities3-Solid waste collection.4-Procurement and distribution of consumable hygiene kits. 6-Conduct RCCE interventions and COVID19/hygiene awareness messaging in IDP camps through FGD.7-Water quality monitoring, to ensure safe water.8-As part of BINAA strategy for the provision of sustainable services, BINAA will shift from water trucking to more sustainable water system by constructing groundwater tank along with the rehabilitation/extension of the existing water network, where the technical study will be made in each camp to implement proposed activity. Compounded with the establishment of cost recovery mechanism that will generate adequate resources necessary to operate the systems such as (fuel, chlorine, spare parts, and operational cost of service unit staff); and ensure the cost of management is collected as tariff for self-dependence.•9-Water system rehabilitation and restoration: The water supply system in Atareb will be restored through the rehabilitation of four main water stations, repair of networks, and expansion of the water network. In addition, the work will include the establishment of Water Units (WU) to manage systems operation and maintenance. Compounded with the establishment of cost recovery mechanism.10- sewage system rehabilitation.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

- Protection risk: The risks of GBV can happen because of overcrowding in distributionpoints, or may happen after distributing while returning home, which may lead to harmsome women and may cause domestic violence. - Likelihood: Medium. - Impact: High. -Mitigating measures: 1- We will not distribute in a late time of the day. 2- We will notchoose distribution points far from the residential complex, also we will distribute in eachvillage separately. 3- During distribution, we will divide the location into two sides, one sidefor women and the other for men. 4- Establish a confidential complaint mechanism, withtwo phone numbers: one for men and other for women. - Protection risk: The risks ofexploitation of beneficiaries by the field workers. - Likelihood: weak. - Impact: High. -Mitigating measures: 1- We will choose the field team carefully, who have high behavioralqualities. 2- Establish a confidential complaint mechanism, with two phone numbers: onefor men and other for women.
