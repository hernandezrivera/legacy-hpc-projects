Project Summary

The project seeks to provide WASH-related activities, and integrate with it other running livelihood projects in Dolow District maximize impact and increase the reach. The project will entail the provision of sustainable sanitation-related skills to enhance the resilience of the at-risk IDPs and host communities. This will be achieved providing water through water vouchers system to at least 3000 affected IDPs, train 20 WASH specialists on the use hygiene management systems, undertake hygiene campaigns targeting the primary and secondary schools in Dolow, capacitate 100 persons on water and resource management skills to increase response use of water as a scarce resource responsibly, chlorination of water 10 points and continually monitor so as to eradicate chlorine residual effect and conducting CLTS program to 5 nearby villages and 1 IDP camp. furthermore the project will carry out campaigns on basic hygiene, sanitation and skills on proper domestic waste disposal management system to avoid acute watery diarrhoea (AWD) and other waterborne diseases. While at it the skills will also include the provision of sanitation kits to the at-risk community.proper use of the latrines.

Mitigation Measures

Planning for the project adopts Do No Harm approach. This means the programs are implemented in such a way that there is close ordination with the local administration and other stakeholders to ensure that the beneficiaries are not subjected to any form of risk. Additionally, GEWDO programs are done in the morning hours to provide the beneficiaries with adequate time to travel to their homes.
