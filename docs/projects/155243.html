Objectives

Ensure effective, sufficient and continuous provision of life saving water and sanitation services for targeted men, women, boys and girls Ensure that all targeted women, men, girls and boys have the means and are encouraged to adopt individual and collective measures in order to health seeking behaviors and to mitigate public health risksEnsure that all WASH assistance promotes the protection, safety and dignity of targeted people, and is used equitably to men, women, boys and girls

Needs

In NPM round 12, it was determined that In Nayapara bathing and washing facilities do not meet protection standards (57% have no locks, no lighting in 93% feature insufficent lighting). Most locations report severe or extremelly severe water needs while Water together with cooking fuel is the most mentioned need. The REACH assessment of April 2018 stated that 79% of the households faced access propblems due to distance and/or cuing time while 52% employed negatrive coping strategies safe drinking water is scarce.The high ground water level disqualifies this camp for simple pit latrine. Feacal sludge management remains an issue this includes desludging activities as well as sludge management sites.

Activities or outputs

Upgrading 100 latrines, to bring these upto minimum standard, through construction of septictanks. Installations of lightning at wash facilities like bathing cubicles and latrines 150. Continue distribution of Hygiene top up kits, and MHM kits to 4000 HH. Continue Hygiene promotion with 20 hygiene promoters covering 2000 HH focussing on M&O of facililities as well domestic hygiene and solid waste management. If required decomissiong latrines, and desludging of latrines and establishment and operations of one FSM site.

Key project/performance indicators

Proportion of target population with increased access to sanitation services (latrines with handwashing facilities) that are improved-80%Percentage of target population that have improved theit hygiene knowledge, attitude and practices in at least 3 topics compared to the baseline-80%
