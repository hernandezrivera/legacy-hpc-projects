Project Summary

The project interventions aim to address the area of Darfur's urgent needs for water, sanitation, and hygiene. The overarching goal is to increase equitable access to safe water in order to meet the basic needs of the population and to reduce morbidity among the most vulnerable populations. The project is designed to provide WASH support for 54, 000 people from different community segments (IDPs, returnees, vulnerable residents, and people with disabilities) in 9 localities in Darfur.
RPDO will provide life-saving and life sustaining WASH services that will increase households' access to safe water, adequate sanitation facilities, and improve their knowledge of hygiene promotion through installation of water hand pumps, construction of institutional latrines, distribution of hygiene kits, provision of Menstrual Hygiene Materials for women and girls, and also through community capacity building.
 For this project, RPDO will emphasize the importance of protection, ensuring the safety and dignity of populations affected by conflict and displacement, and maintaining a "do no harm" approach, while also ensuring accountability toward affected populations through the equal participation of all community members, including people with disabilities.

1 - Needs

The water, sanitation, and hygiene (WASH) situation has deteriorated as a result of the exacerbating economic crisis, which has affected IDPs, returnees, and vulnerable residents, especially in Darfur states. The project targets nine localities in three states, which are North Darfur (Kutum, Mellit, and Tawila), South Darfur (Ed El Fursan, Kubum, and Rahad El Berdi) and Central Darfur (Nertiti (West Jebel Marra), Rokoro (North Jebel Marra), and Umm Dukhun). The selected areas are all of high severity ranked with high population and PIN. In the locations, there are more than 370,000 IDPs PiN and about 320,000 returnees PiN with an overall WASH target of about half a million people. The project will provide WASH support for 9000 households where more than 30% of these HH fetch water directly from surface water sources. Furthermore, the sanitation and hygiene status in these locations are catastrophic. As a result, the project will provide institutional sanitation facilities and hand washing facilities. The project will also focus on hygiene practices and WASH capacity building in these areas to mitigate the risk of the spread of water-prone diseases, malnutrition, AWD, and all other hygiene and water-related risks.

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

This project addresses protection and safety concerns by ensuring that proposed WASH services meet the needs of all, especially women, girls and people with disabilities. Gender-differentiated approaches will be considered by:- Consulting women and girls when selecting water point locations and ensuring short distances to water points. -Gender segregated and separated institutional latrines with Menstrual Hygiene Management (MHM) components and lockable doors.-Disabled-friendly institutional latrines with ramps, handrails, and seats.
