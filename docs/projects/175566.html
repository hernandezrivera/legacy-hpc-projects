Project Summary

CCM will contribute to (i) alleviating the suffering of vulnerable people especially school aged boys and girls - protecting their dignity ; (ii) resilience - safe teaching/learning, quality education; (iii) contributing for HR protection and reduction of inequality - reference, accountability, child protection and gender-sensitive basic services.
In this context, CCM intends not only contribute to ensuring that these (i) students receive basic learning materials, but also to ensure that schools are provided with cleaning kits and the learning spaces/schools are clean and covid-19, cholera, malaria and diarrhea's free, becoming thus in centers of social transformation and promotion of the dignity of children (boys and girls), (ii) guaranteed their right to inclusive and quality education, (iii) promoting psychosocial, moral and spiritual support.
In general, 3000 beneficiaries (75% of women, including students, teachers, technicians and support staff, and educational institutions/services and social support, school board members, parent and guardian groups and other related groups).

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

CCM, as a religious and humanitarian organization, intends with this initiative contribute to ensuring the children and teachers human dignity by promoting, respecting and protecting the right to inclusive and quality education for all.The impacts of Cyclone Kenneth are still felt through learning spaces/schools situation, that have been destroyed (partially or totally), still do not offer decent conditions for teaching/learning. Moreover, the COVID-19 pandemic has made evident the weaknesses of water and sanitation systems in education units, permising/risking the lives of thousands of students and teachers. Therefore, hundreds of learning spaces/schools do not offer the necessary conditions to welcome children and teachers and provide a safe environment, both for the teaching/learning process as well as shelter form communities that, due to insecurity or cyclones, are forced to leave their land or households. In this context, CCM intends not only to contribute to ensuring that these (i) infrastructures are safe and resilient, but also to ensure that the accommodation for displaced families/learning spaces for boys and girls are centers of social transformation and promotion of their dignity, (ii) guaranteed their right to inclusive and quality education by providing necessary learning material for boys and girls, (iii) making the learning spaces/schools safe spaces by promoting hygiene and sanitation.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

(i) Conduct a basic study on learning spaces/schools, teachers, pupils (boys and girls), primary and secondary education and learning infrastructures, partners, similar initiatives, relevant support units and assistance and basic needs of the target groups;(ii) Advocate for the children (boys and girls) registration and the provision of gender-responsive public services (GRPS approach);(iii) Distribution of basic learning materials to boys and girls;(iv) Provide cleaning kits to schools;(v) Provide to learning spaces/schools with handwashing stations.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Providing humanitarian assistance to communities affected by cyclones and terrorist attacks, especially disadvantaged/marginalized/vulnerable ones is the first step towards long-term protection and rehabilitation. Therefore, CCM will ensure the support package to the target group beneficiary of the action (boys and girls), through response mechanisms in order to mitigate the negative (including but not limited impacts of prostitution, COVID-19 and other diseases, with alternatives of access to resources and income). To ensure that women and children do not experience harm/injury resulting from the implementation of the project, CCM will work closely with communities by engaging their members in sensitization and awareness sessions, particularly to advocate for the rights of women, children and people with disability and raise their awareness of the risk of trafficking.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

In addition to CHS and the Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) policy, CCM will ensure that beneficiaries, all personnel and partners are trained on these protection and gender issues to reduce the risks of sexual abuse and the rights of disaster victims. However, CCM will explore formal partnerships with protection actors to ensure referral mechanisms for affected populations (target group) as well as local CS networks to empower and foster participation especially women and girls. Since the project aims to contribute towards turning the schools and learning centers centers of social transformation, for CCM the knowledge/awareness of people's rights, the change of consciousness and elimination of stereotypes, harmful habits and customs that constitute to a large extent structural causes that perpetuate the cycle of poverty and violence, idleness and poverty, discrimination and abuses of rights, will be priorities , through effective empowerment and capacitation, especially of women and girls, in this learning cycle that the project itself represents.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The project will make the thorough identification of people with special needs through the existing database, as well as through visits to displaced people households outside and inside the centers, in the communities in a situation of vulnerability for survey. To highlight that CCM works in networking, which will facilitate the identification and location of people with special needs and advocate, above all, for the purpose of protection and assistance in any circumstances, and access to all public and private institutions and services (GRPS). The ultimate goal will be to propose measures that predict, prevent or eliminate potential risks for people with special needs, reduce the frequency and consequences of possible incidents of violation of their rights.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Throughout all project scope, CCM will ensure that environmental protection aspects are considered priorities in planning and implementation processes. The Project will take into account improving education, raising awareness of the need to protect the environment and human and institutional capacity on mitigation, adaptation, impact reduction and climate change alertness (prior information system), as well as increasing knowledge for conscious and participatory action in order to make communities more resilient and adapted to climate change. CCM will focus on clean up campaigns actions, that comprises of garbage collection and treating inside and outside the learning spaces/schools, tree planting and environmental education as an incentive for school age children to read, through the story tale about environmental care that arouse their curiosity, interest and activism about the environment.
