Project Summary

The intervention in the project area is based on expanding existing camps in order to raise the capacity to absorb more of IDPs due currently military actions, project will target 20 camps in several areas in Northwest of Syria (A’zaz, salqin, Maaret Tamsrin, Harim, Sarmada, Dier Hassan).
Before starting the activities, we will take into consideration the HLP issues if necessary by making both the camps’ managers and the landlords sign consent forms which expressly provide the permission to conduct the project activities without any legal consequences.
the activities will be implemented according to the agreed criteria in CCCM cluster.
The key administrative and critical humanitarian infrastructure will be developed including admin offices, distribution points, and warehouse, safe spaces for women and children, supplying camps with necessary safety equipment, firebreaks & fire points, in addition to support the camps' management by providing trainings and supplies, and supporting forming the committees inside camps, in addition to contingency planning.
- Providing camp operational and regular maintenance activities for camps including repairing of lighting, fence and other camp facilities, and graveling of communal areas and roads and replacing damaged tents, especially after heavy rains. cleaning of sanitation and communal facilities, providing fuel for running generators and other maintenance cost.
Balad will take over the camps management for the first 3 months, in parallel with training and preparing the camps management committees to take over the management for the rest of the project duration. Balad organization will train the camps management committees on the necessary skills that will facilitate the care and maintenance activities including reporting tools and respond during emergencies.

4 - Needs

Due to recent hostilities in Idleb, Hama, and Aleppo, tens of thousands of civilians have fled from in southern Idleb governorate. Displacement happening in winter months is further exacerbating the vulnerability of people in need. Many of those who fled are in urgent need of humanitarian support.According to CCCM cluster reports the number of IDPs from 1 December 2019 to 2 February 2020 has reached 586000 people, 80% of them are women and children. And they fled their homes without taking with them what they need during displacement, they've gone to safer areas, but these areas already crowded with displaced people over the past months, and most of them are living with host families or in temporary shelters,Also according to statistics of HNO 2020 the number of people in need of shelter has reached 5.65 M, and 3.42 M in need to non-food items -the economic pressures are considered as the most challenges for IDPs- Balad team in have implemented rapid need assessment in many locations in A'zaz, salqin, Maaret Tamsrin, Harim, Sarmada, Dier Hassan to assess main needs of IDPs.The main findings of this RNA indicates that the newly IDPs are in serious need of assistance, and don't receive sufficient support from organizations, the main needs are shelter, non-food items, WASH services, food and cash assistance.

5 - Implementation of activities

Intervention is based on expanding existing camps to absorb more IDPs due currently military actions, we will target 20 camps in several areas in NWS (A'zaz, salqin, Maaret Tamsrin, Harim Sarmada, Dier Hassan). before starting project's activities, we will take into consideration HLP issues if necessary by making both camps’ managers and landlords sign consent forms which expressly provide permission to conduct project activities without any legal consequences, ithe activities will be implemented according to agreed criteria in CCCM cluster. Project's activities: -Establish CM committees. -help development for committees members selection. - training the CM members on camp managment. - Help in development for each committees member - Establishment of contingency planning with fire fighting system and evacuation plan.- Providing camp operational and regular maintenance activities for camps including repairing of lighting, fence and other camp facilities , and graveling of communal areas and roads and replacing damaged tents , especially after heavy rains. cleaning of sanitation and communal facilities, providing fuel for running generators and other maintenance cost.-Establishment of feedback/complaint (AAP) mechanism- Reporting and monitoring the provided services in camps.-Conducting needs assessments in camps for all services provided (3Ws) tool.- Through trainings and sessions , raise awareness of the CM and IDPs communities on protection mainstreaming imitative and risks related.• We will ensure that principle of Do No Harm and Sphere principles will applied in all project's activities, taking into consideration gender sensitive• As well as staff will be males and females, all of them will sign code of conduct and will receive training about it.Balad will take over camps management for first 3 months, in parallel with training and preparing camps management committees to take over the management for the rest of project duration.
