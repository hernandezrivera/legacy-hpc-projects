Project Summary

the situation in project location can not be isolated from the whole status in the country. a variety of factors, including decades of marginalization of the periphery by central elite, lack of investment and development, and general poor governance have led to a number of separate, but similar.crisis and conflicts in different areas of the Sudan. each in varying stages of evolution. Together, these conflicts have had a devastating effect on the country.
More recently,the tribal conflicts in the project location in South Kordofan state, has seen tens of deaths and over hundreds thousands of people displaced. the main cause of this conflict is competition over the land and resources, particularly the gold and water sources.as well as natural disaster such as flood and influx of refugees from the neighbor South Sudan. the most vulnerable categories are children (girls, boys) these protracted conflicts has driven displacement, separated children from their families and left thousands of children vulnerable to grave violations, including child recruitment.Children and adolescents in affected and vulnerable communities have been exposed to high levels of risks and vulnerabilities, including being associated with or recruited by armed groups. Poverty, lack of employable skills and opportunities, family pressure and shortage of social services were listed as root causes of child recruitment. CDF proposes to provide crucial protection and reintegration services for children associated with armed conflict. the project aims to provide a training of armed forces on childrights,development of standard operating procedures on reintegration of children associated with armed groups/forces, strengthening of community-based child protection
networks, as well as life skills and vocational training for adolescents at-risk of recruitment. so many adolescents and youth will start their own business after receiving such training and supportive ‘start-up kits’. the project targets IDPs and returnees children that estimated (16964- 7068) subsequently (HNO 2022). Moreover, the project suggests to establish Entrepreneurship center to train girls and boys how to run business. this is after completion of the vocational training. the main purpose of this is to prevent them from economical exploitation. where the children manage their project themselves.simultaneously they will provide training for other out of school children, together they will constitute the general assembly of the center which be register in the Ministry of Social Welfare. for cash management CDF will open an account bank in the name of the center. the center involve, car mechanic, car electricity, blacksmithing,food processing, sewing, perfume,handicrafts.the project contributes to peace building between the components of the community that the economical relation will be the alternative than that based on the tribe and ethnic aspect.

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

CDF understand how to work in seeking collective protection outcomes for people affected by crisis. This is achieved by protection considerations underpin all humanitarian interventions during all stages of the project cycle; that individual rights are respected as part of programming; and that potential protection risks are identified from the outset and mitigated. therefore protection is a shared responsibility that is at the forefront of humanitarian action and that requires a system-wide approach to addressing severe and widespread protection risks. Accountability to Affected Population is particularly important throughout project duration.
