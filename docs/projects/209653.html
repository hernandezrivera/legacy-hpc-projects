Project Summary

SDI implements integrated protection services to prevent, mitigate, and response to harm, exploitation, and abuse for the most vulnerable boys, girls, men and women in northern Syria in Idleb governorates. Under this program, we will manage one protection center and 4 mobile teams. Mobile teams will support the static facility to provide services to those not able to access to the safe space, raise awareness in the community and conduct assessment. Mobile teams will also be ready to respond and provide emergency response services to IDP displacements and any influx in IDPs. The objective of this project to enhance the prevention and mitigation of protection risks and respond to protection needs through supporting the protective environment in Syria, by promoting international law and through quality principled assistance.
SDI will establish reporting mechanisms by develop clear and confidential reporting mechanisms for instances of child abuse. This could involve a helpline, community centers, or trusted individuals within the community. SDI team will reach to 10,000 beneficiaries.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

SDI will train staff, and community members on child protection policies, procedures, and best practices. Provide specialised training for those who work directly with children. Also, Conduct awareness campaigns and educational programs to raise awareness about child rights, protection issues, and available support services. This may include workshops and community outreach activities. Implement safeguards to prevent potential risks to children, such as background checks for staff and code of conduct policies, and safe recruitment practices. Our child protection case management services target the most vulnerable, including unaccompanied and separated children, children associated with armed forces and armed groups, child survivors of sexual violence, children involved in the worst forms of child labor, children suffering psychological distress and child-headed households
