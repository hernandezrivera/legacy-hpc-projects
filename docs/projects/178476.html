Project Summary

Bread is consdided as a basic and indispensable material for the Syrian people with the collapse of the Syrian currency, the bundle price of bread was changed 5 times. Bread securing became difficult for many of the displaced, especially with Covid 19 epidemic Spread which forced people to stop working. many families depend on daily work to provide food and bread for their children But the Covid-19 epidemic prevented that ,so we work as humanitarian organizations to alleviate their suffering, and therefore we present a proposal for distribute free bread project for the IDPs in northwestern Syria according to the following criteria:

4 - Needs

The continuing situation in northwestern Syria, specifically the Idlib governorate, has affected the population, in a situation characterized by constant displacement and movement. Not only is the civilian infrastructure damaged and degraded, the population's coping mechanisms are overburdened and many are unable to cover basic needs. . According to the reports of the Camp Coordination and Camp Management Group, the number of internally displaced people in Idlib Governorate is increasing more and more. Moreover, accommodation capacity has decreased in northern Syria and reception centers are overcrowded. . The overcrowding situation in northern Syria is making the situation worse and the needs there are more than the capacity of the humanitarian actors, especially the need for shelter, which is a top priority at present.According to HNO2021 sources, which clarify the number of people who are in need of food security and through the needs assessment made by the MATTAR team MATTAR organization team assessed the needs of 10 camps for the displaced, unsupported with bread, and gave the names1- Widows and orphans (462 HHs) needs 595bread Bundle2-ALBEREE (94 HHs) needs 161bread Bundle3-ALHAJREE (54HHs) needs 88bread Bundle4- ALKANDOUSH (157HHs) needs 272 bread Bundle5- LAJLEKOUM (30HHs) needs 49 bread Bundle6-IKHAA HAMA (120HHs) needs 208 bread Bundle7-ALKORA (252 HHs) needs 374 bread Bundle8- GAITH &RAHMA (290 HHs) needs 379 bread Bundle9-ALDYAA 9 (75 HHs) needs 105 bread Bundle10- ALSAROUT (271 HHs) needs 388 bread Bundle

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk 1: Implementation of the durable activities is stopped by regular clashes, violence, or an upsurge in criminality including theft or kidnappingMitigation 1: MATTAR will monitor the security developments and reported criminal violence along its main delivery routes and in its areas of operation. If an increase in violence is noted, alternative routes and distribution points will be used to mitigate against the possibility of an armed group putting any pressure on the beneficiaries or consider alternative areas/activities under the planned intervention with the approval from HF and Cluster Risks 2: A fees might be collected by the local council in return for receiving humanitarian assistance leading for Aid diversion. Moreover. Conflict and tension in the community might happen between the project team and non-beneficiaries since they believe they are marginalizedMitigation 2: MATTAR will ensure the humanitarian nature of the project activities by communicating the project scope and selection criteria with the beneficiaries through verbal communication and distributed brochures that the humanitarian assistance is free of charge. In case the aid diversion happened, MATTAR will immediately suspend the activity and report the incident to OCHA as per MATTAR policy. Furthermore, The project team will involve the stakeholders from the beginning of the project to facilitate the activities and getting their support
