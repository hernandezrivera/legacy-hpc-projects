Project Summary

Adolescent girls face poorer health outcomes, likely to be infected with HIV, socially isolated, less likely to complete secondary school and have fewer income-generating opportunities.
The armed conflict has imposed 271164 IDPs in cities and girls 51% (64165) boys 49% (62663). Many of the girls rapped, coerced, abducted and used as sex toys by armed groups/militia, sexually abused in crowded homes hosting IDPs. Early marriages, teenage pregnancies and mothers resulting from school boycott. More than 30% of vulnerable adolescent girls and teenage mothers have become orphans as a result of killings in the crisis and are heading households. 229teenage mothers have been registered in our organization and about 30% reported the authors of the pregnancies have been killed. This leaves children to engage into survival sex, boys joining NSAGs as survival strategies. Sexual exploitation is on the rise. IDPs are exploited by their host families. A cycle of poverty and Misery persists.
The above effects are due to;
Limited knowledge on life skills, knowledge and participation particularly IDP girls who are coming from the remote areas and access to such information has been very limited. However, some vulnerable girls expose to such information hardly have complete package as they mostly attain 1 or 2 days workshops not covering the full package in a continuum for better apprehension.
Vulnerable girls are not exposed to income generating activities opportunities and not very competitive for such opportunities.
Limited uptake of appropriate essential life services either before or after exploitation such as Emergency Contraceptives (EC), Post Exposure Prophylaxis (PEP), Psychological first Aid (PFA) etc., legal redress, shelter, food and Non-food items etc.). This is largely due to the vulnerability of the girls.
Parents/critical adults to girls have limited knowledge on comprehensive sexuality education (CSE). Parents take sex education for moral education. Religious institutions see it as immoral and girls particularly Adolescents’ navigate through these challenges without parental support and denied public spaces. Even girls that actively participate in child friendly or youth led groups in religious institutions are groomed only in religious doctrine and not on life skill protection and participation. All these denials abandon girls at their tipping point to experience physical and emotional changes, noticing sex drive, much societal expectations etc. with severe resulting negative effects.
The purpose of this proposal is to reduce the rate of adolescent girls’ exploitation and abuse including those in IDP host communities. This will be achieved through: adolescent friendly theatre therapy center to increase the knowledge of vulnerable girls on life skills, knowledge and participation. Increase the knowledge of parents/critical adults to girls on CSE and positive parenting. Increase girls’ uptake of essential life-saving services/products and place very needy girls on cash for work model and provision of start-up kits to them.
The target include
Unaccompanied adolescent girls, some heading households and often get involved in sex for money activities as a survival strategy.
Separated adolescent girls living with relatives are exposed to incest in the host home and most often exploited or serve as domestic servants.
School adolescents making efforts to access education often kidnapped sexually abused
Out of school girls in vocational trainings exposed to sexual exploitations as most of them are ignorant of SRHRs and services.
Married adolescent girls likely to face multiple intimate partner violence
Pregnant adolescents with no author of pregnancy.
Teenage mothers have high tendencies of further sexual exploitation and abuse in the community.
The parents and the critical adults need to be informed of girls’ challenges and to give approval and fully equipped with knowledge to manage girls under their parenthood.

1 - Besoins / Needs

Adolescent’s girls at their tipping point experience physical and emotional changes (noticing sex drive, much societal expectations and others) and need to be accompanied toward safe adolescence. Unfortunately, they are abandoned at this stage e.g. Cameroon teenage pregnancy is 28% and Kumbo East Health district recorded 60.75% teenage pregnancies in October 2018. About 300 rape cases reported from Jan. 2017 to Dec. 2018. Parents/critical adults to girls are ignorant of CSE/positive parenting for girls who are supposed to be the first educators to them. This leaves the girls alone and they face increasing HIV prevalence. CAMPHIA 2017 statistics show HIV in adolescents at 3%. This situation is likely to double Cameroon Early marriage is 30% in 2018 (UNICEF) and hence increasing teenage pregnancies increases child marriage and illiteracy. The vulnerable girls are less exposed to employment opportunities given most of them are unskilled hence poverty and misery perpetuating a cycle of poverty The high prevalence in teenage pregnancies, early marriages, HIV is supper high way to complicated maternal and child mortality. Vulnerable girls experience limited uptake of appropriates life -saving services (EC, PEP, FP etc.) and access to other services like PFA, Legal, Psychosocial support, shelter food, economic Empowerment given they are less exposed to safe spaces for information and knowledge. E.g. 6 months of HIV Free North West project managed by Cameroon Baptist Convention Health Services (CBCHS), we have recorded about 229 pregnant women and 75% are teenagers unable to access Ante Natal clinic and even where to have humanitarian support.Running equipped adolescent-friendly theatre therapy center offering two weeks training on life skills, knowledge and participation to vulnerable girls identified and classified. The practical asset value (bargaining power, autonomy and control over decisions) building sessions (trust building games, drama and sketches on negotiation with girls) develop self-confidence and esteem to say no and stand by it. Hold theatre therapy sessions for SGBV survivors to tell their stories, heal from it, and cause others to learn. A magazine of an inspiring curated collection of SGBV stories and images of transformation and resistance shall built from this theatre therapy Entrepreneurial/financial Education will be provided to teenage mothers, married and pregnant adolescents giving skills and attitudes that adopt; good money management practices. This builds a strong link between economic independence and health behavior given that girls have a strong relationship between power and money in relationships. Girls shall be exposed to essential life-saving services, trained on psychological first Aid (PFA) to be able to access these services and also link their peers. Hence increase number of SGBV survivors immediately receiving services. Training parents and critical adults to girls on CSE, positive parenting and communication approaches of this subject to adolescents will permit them adequately educate adolescents to navigate emotional and physical challenges successfully. Design information, Education and Communication materials that support sensitization/ advocacy campaigns on GBV prevention/response and where to go for essential life-saving services increasing child protection in the community.Organize adolescent girls’ conferences/fairs to show case their potentials and inspire others to do same. These initiate public speaking skills Place very needy and vulnerable girls in short term skill building trades and put them on cash for work model such they take care of their basic needs and upon graduation are provided with start-up kits in the trade they have acquired practical skills. This empowers them to live independent lives free of exploitation in any form. Engage girls to celebrate international day of the girl child, African child, 16days activism campaign world AIDS day.
