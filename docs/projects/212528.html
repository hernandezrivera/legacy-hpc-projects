Project Summary

In Syria, and as per HNO 2024, 16.7 million people need humanitarian assistance (70% of the total population), it is estimated that 6.8 million people in Syria will require shelter support and 6.1 million people (8 per cent decrease from previous year) will require NFI support. Additionally, years of conflict resulted in damaging vital community infrastructure in NES hindering access to basic services education and health. In Raqqa,
Residents in informal settlements and Collective centers live in unsafe and undignified spaces exposing them to serious protection risks. The recent escalation has forced IDPs to resort to unfinished/abandoned shops and buildings that lack facilities which has exacerbated families’ vulnerabilities.
- Implementation of activities:
To respond to these needs, DCA is providing (secured funding) shelter assistance in informal settlements (Ifs) in targeted communities. This includes distribution of quality tents and shelter kits to protect vulnerable families from harsh weather conditions in both summer and winter.
Informal settlements- that didn’t receive any shelter support in the last 3 years will be prioritized based on the dashboard of Ifs working group (WG). Based on the technical assessment and the degree of damage, tents with significant damage will be replaced and tents with medium or light damage will receive shelter kits with high-quality materials specially designed for tent types in informal settlements with guidance provided in the installation phase.
DCA will also identify community infrastructure (not secured) to be rehabilitated to enable safe access to IDPs, and host communities to basic services. This may include schools, primary health centers and other soci-economic infrastructure that were affected by the conflict and in collaboration with Mine action team who will be providing clearance support in case of suspected contamination prior to the rehabilitation. This integrated response aims to respond to the complex humanitarian needs in Raqqa.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

In addition to distributing shelter and NFI kits, DCA is looking for opportunities to extend its interventions to the informal settlements. DCA also aims to build mud houses in targeted informal settlements to be a center for CBP (community-based protection) activities. The community will build these units there with C4W activities and thus improve livelihoods. In additional to the interventions in IFS, DCA also proposed an integrated shelter response to rehabilitate public infrastructure in conflict affected communities in Ar-Raqqa and Deir-ez-Zor. The key infrastructure will be rehabilitated to provide access to vital services such healthcare and education. Prior to the rehabilitation, these facilities will be surveyed and cleared by DCA mine action team to ensure safe access for both the population and humanitarian interventions, if needed. In case of suspected contamination or evidence of ERW, DCA will also do clearance and based on the need. The proposed intervention will target vulnerable, conflict-affected communities in NES, including IDPs, returnees and host communities. Targeted locations will be selected based on consultations with the technical working groups. DCA will also engage local stakeholders and communities through focused group discussions to incorporate their feedback in terms of priority areas and level of needs prior to the implementation, and after selecting the locations and community liaison, DCA will conduct the HH Assessments and Repair Plan development; Contractor support to execution of repair plans; technical Follow up. All projects will be concluded with a Post-distribution Monitoring (PDM) survey after a month, to assess the efficacy and impact of the activities. HHs will be selected based on vulnerability, livelihood and access to essential facilities.
