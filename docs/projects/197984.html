Project Summary

The number of vulnerable children continue to grow with the the prolonged armed conflict ravaging the Northwest and Southwest Regions of Cameroon. Children living within the conflict affected communities continue to be the most vulnerable and most affected by this conflict. The number of child Rights abuses and violence against children continue to be on the rise. Caritas Kumbo as a child protection actor wants to scale up child protection services and offer a comprehensive approach in preventing violence against children while offering protection to victims of abuse and violations.
These vulnerable children include Unaccompanied and separated children, child survivors of Gender Based Violence, neglected children, children orphaned by the armed conflict, children from families that have been greatly victimized by the war (victims of burnt homes, children in families whose sources of livelihood have been destroyed, children in displaced families).
Of children in crisis affected communities where Caritas Kumbo has intervened, 10-12% of the children fall in one or more of the above listed categories. of this percentage of children, 90% of them do not have access to basic education, basic health care, basic meals and other basic needs like clothing. In their vulnerability and lack, the girls are mostly abused sexually and end up living in trauma, diseases and unplanned teenage pregnancies. Recruitment and use of girls by parties to conflict has been going on unreported and the consequence is seen in the number of teenage pregnant girls who are not ready to open up on authors of the pregnancies due to the fear as they have been threatened by the perpetrators.
There is still a lot of ignorance on issues surrounding child protection and access to safe channels of reporting violations and abuses against children.
Through this program, Caritas Kumbo has as objectives the following.
- Train and equip more Community Based Child Protection Mechanism members to carry out awareness raising for child protection, Prevention of GBV and identify and refer vulnerable, abused and violated children for comprehensive case management and assistance.
- Train and equip more Child Protection Monitors from to monitor and report grave violations against children.
- Offer comprehensive services or referral of affected children for other services
- Mainstream COVID-19 prevention in all activities and services
- Mainstream PSEA in project communities through community education and awareness raising
- Mainstream AAP through Complaint and feedback mechanisms and other safe reporting channels

1 - Besoins / Needs

The overall need of the targeted children is Protection. Given that children are abused or affected in different ways, we are earmarking a series of multi-sectoral needs-based interventions for the targeted children. These are the following; MHPSS: 2,500 children are targeted in 5 affected communities. (Melim, Wainamah, Ntaba, Ntubaw, and Tatum). All the children in the conflict affected areas especially those in the hot spots have lived varying traumatic experiences from minor to very severe cases of trauma recorded in children. These children need to be accompanied with Mental Health and Psychosocial Support services and individual support to severe cases in order to enhance a healthy state of mind so that they can grow normally. These children also need to be empowered on their Rights as children, Life skills training to empower them on how to ensure their own protection and how to access help if in need. - Protection from further abuse and violence: The need for Community Based Child Protection Mechanisms. The project will target and train 60 community members and strengthen them to set up and maintain Community Based Protection mechanisms for children to prevent further violence. Other protection needs will be addressed through; - Needs based assistance to identified vulnerable children and SGBV through counseling and trauma care, placement in Vocational training centers, support with food, NFIs, health assistance, educational support, birth registration (establishment of birth certificates), livelihood support to families of vulnerable children.
