Project Summary

Since the start of April 2023 conflict in Sudan humanitarian needs have dramatically risen. As of 17th May OCHA estimates that 24.7 million people are in need of humanitarian assistance, with more than 700.000 people internally displaced across the whole Country as of April 2023.
Violence and destruction of infrastructure is preventing millions from accessing food, medical and other non-food supplies; it also resulted in the serious disruption of basic services in Water, Hygiene and Sanitation, as well as Health and Education.
The scarcity of drinking water, the disruption or inexistence of sanitation services and waste management are likely to further increase food insecurity, the spread of diseases and protection concerns, especially among women. In addition, lack of water and conflict over resources is likely to further exacerbate conflicts existing since decades between different communities.
RCDO and Ahbab identify the provision of WASH services as one of the needs to be urgently addressed. Newly displaced populations and host communities are identified as a priority target group for this intervention. Among these, women are a group of particular concern and some activities are designed to address their needs.
This project aims at providing emergency WASH assistance, with a special focus on women and girls among conflict affected communities, while also contributing to more sustainable ways of water supply in areas most in need.

1 - Needs

Since the start of April 2023 conflict in Sudan humanitarian needs have dramatically risen. As of 17th May OCHA estimates that 24.7 million people are in need of humanitarian assistance, with more than 700.000 people internally displaced across the whole Country as of April 2023. Violence and destruction of infrastructure is preventing millions from accessing food, medical and other non-food supplies; it also resulted in the serious disruption of basic services in Water, Hygiene and Sanitation, as well as Health and Education.The current emergency further worsen the already dire effects of the protracted crisis faced since 2003 conflict and the military takeover in October 2021.Water scarcity, lack of hygiene services and poor or non-existing waste management are likely to severely impact the already fragile food security and health conditions of the most vulnerable groups among displaced and host communities. In the present situation, women and girls are particularly exposed to abuse, attack and ill-health, and the inaccessibility of hygiene and menstruation hygiene items affects their dignity, while also increasing their vulnerability. In addition, since decades lack of water in vast areas of Sudan has been one of the main causes of conflict, which contributed to exacerbate the already existing fractures between different ethnic and socio-economic groups (e.g. farmers and herders). The current hostilities and the deterioration of security, combined with water scarcity are likely to reignite existing tensions between different communities and groups.RCDO and Ahbab have operational presence in various states of Sudan. The present project responds to needs in WASH and protection identified in Khartoum, Kassala, AlGedreif and West Darfur.

5.2 - Impact on environment/natural resources

The project aims at addressing one of the main environmental problems in the country such as water scarcity through the rehabilitation of damaged water points. The maintenance of existing water points (e.g. handpumps) also ensures the maximization of assets, thus contributing to reduce waste and maximize financial resources.The project also aims at improving waste management and pollution through the distribution of sanitation kits. The training for community committees on waste management will also focus on topics such as waste disposal.

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

The project targets specifically women and girls and aims at enhancing their protection.Hygiene kits and hygiene promotion campaigns will be specifically focusing on gender sensitive WASH. Menstrual hygiene items such as sanitary pads aim at facilitating women and girls affected by conflict and displacement and even more exposed to distress during menstruation. The rehabilitation of water points will also support women and girls protection since they often carry the majority of the responsibility for collecting water and domestic cleaning. The increase availability of functioning water points will increase proximity and decrease the risk of harassment.
