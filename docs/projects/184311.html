Project Summary

Reoccurring conflicts between AS, Ahlu-Sunna Wal-Jameca(ASWJ) , Galmudug and Federal Government hosts urban IDP communities have been a prevalent issue in Guriel, Hobyo, Mataban and Galkacyo expelled families from Laas-anood and ceergabo from Somaliland authorities , due to high competition over scarce resources. Waves of evictions have increased vulnerabilities of displaced populations in Mudug and Galgaduud Regions especially women and children, with many of them living without adequate Cccm Intervention for IDPs in the area. The threat of eviction is always imminent. which increases IDP vulnerability to both security/protection concerns therefore, the lack of CCCM interventions activities's are highly needed by the IDP's and the Host Communities in Galgaduud and Mudug Regions, it is foreseen that populations will continue to need CCCM services .It is anticipated that increased conflict, frequent climatic shocks and the continuation of the triple threat will lead to further displacements which will strain existing sites and lead to the formation of new sites. KAF will continue to provide services in areas where the cluster is currently active and consider the expansion to new areas if necessary. This project aims at improving the status of approximately 16,500 IDPs individuals and 3,000 vulnerable host community members in Galkacyo, Mataban,Hobyo, Dhusamareb, Goldogob in Mudug and Galgadud regions through delivery of quality community centered CCCM Intervention services, the project also aims to train 200 camp management committee on CCCM principles and governance .KAF will Register 800 beneficiaries for CFW in the selected IDP camps each will receive 50USD, Training camp leader’s on-site management to strengthen their capacities, Establish roving CCCM teams for site monitoring and community engagement, Furniture community centers for tables, chairs if not exist by any of 30 settlements, Identification and support of community structures and encourage participation of all different segments of the displaced population and, as relevant, from among the host communities and local authorities, Provide effective and practical collaboration and coordination of humanitarian interventions.
There is funding gap for cccm intervention activities in mudug and galgaduud regions of somalia, Only 2 of the 37 IDP sites in Guriel are being supported with CCCM services,31 idps sites out of 72 sites in mudug KAF has CCCM intervention track record and is a an active member in the cccm, therefore , it has geographical expertise in galgaduud and mudug regions. Some 22%of IDP sites in Guriel have been served eviction notices within the last three months and 67% of the sites are at high risk of flooding, Many families do not have access to CCCM interventions. however, Displaced people are at risk of eviction due to the lack of formal agreements., KAF will coordinate Site coordination/management – coordinate or lead improved provision of humanitarian assistance in IDP settlements, Communication with affected communities, including complaint and feedback mechanisms, community sensitization/mobilization, ensuring participation of vulnerable groups (women/youth) and that the woman committees are maintained and functional, Information Management . The flash update report on the situation in Guriel, Galmudug As of 26 October 2021 stated that More than 100,000 people have been displaced to 28 nearby villages, as well as three new sites. Of those displaced, about 1,800 people have reportedly arrived in Mataban town, Hiraan region where they are staying with relatives and friends, and are in need of CCCM Interventions Activities.

1. - Projects activities

KAF will continue and expand its existing site coordination and management in Mudug and Galgaduud Regions Specific activities includes: (a) Site planning and preparation to increase Mudug & Galgaduud Regions POC3 existing capacity (b) Site coordination/management – coordinate/lead improved provision of humanitarian assistance in IDP settlements; (c) Communication with affected communities, including complaint and feedback mechanisms, community sensitization/mobilization, ensuring participation of vulnerable groups (women/youth) and that the woman committees are maintained and functional; (d) Information Management – Through KAF, supporting KAF CCCM activities, cluster’s DTM, as well as additional surveys/assessments as necessary and agreed upon with the cluster. Variations in gender, ethnic origin, physical ability and age that affect vulnerability and coping strategies are identified and taken into account for planning to prevent their situation from deteriorating even further.As a cccm Partner KAF will comply with data protection regulations and ensuring safe data management of sensitive beneficiary core personal data, Additionally, contingency plans are developed and put in place to ensure KAF can quickly adapt to and respond to changing humanitarian needs on the ground. This project aims at improving the status of approximately 16,500 IDPs individuals and 3,000 vulnerable host community members in Galkacyo, Mataban,Hobyo, Dhusamareb, Goldogob in Mudug and Galgadud regions through delivery of quality community centered CCCM/NFI’s services, the project also aims to train 200 camp management committee on CCCM principles, governance and site maintenance skills to manage their settlements in Galkacyo and Galgaduud . KAF will Register 800 beneficiaries for CFW in the selected IDP camps each will receive 50USD, Training camp leader’s on-site management to strengthen their capacities and Support community-led site maintenance upkeep of sites (cash for work, site maintenance committees), Establish roving CCCM teams for site monitoring and community engagement, Furniture community centers for tables, chairs if not exist by any of 30 settlements, Identification and support of community structures in the IDP settlements and encourage participation of all different segments of the displaced population and, as relevant, from among the host communities and local authorities, Provide effective and practical collaboration and coordination of humanitarian interventions. Ensure that relevant responders are mobilized towards providing relevant sectorial assistance to those identified as very vulnerable, update service mapping of partners operating in sites e.g. sectors and service providers at site level to ensure that there are no gaps or duplication of activities, Ensure the relevant CCCM intervention activities , specifically regarding evictions, Conduct RCC COVID-19 awareness sessions for IDP populations to emphasis physical distancing procedures and local authority policy minimizing the number of individuals while targeting community members, religious leaders, camp management committee members (CMCs) and not leaving behind persons with disabilities (PwD), Conduct site verification exercise to take stocks of IDP populations disaggregated data while trucking number of persons with disabilities living in sites for program inclusivity, Together with other clusters and partners, as well as the authorities, coordinate the establishment/ efficient use of referral pathways for both protection in safety audits (including SGBV) and assistance/service delivery to persons with special needs, Establish/reinforce communication mechanisms per geographical area/sites i.e. Communication/ information centers in coordination with local authorities and CCCM actors) and finally to Carry out community consultation awareness raising and social mobilization.

2.1.1 - What is the justification for using cash or vouchers instead of in kind?

As you know most households idps and host communitie in somalia hinge on, cash to some level, when humanitarian aid is given in kind, households are often compulsory to seek cash in ways that may be disadvantageous to longer-term maintenances, for example selling productive assets, or giving children to the care of relatives. Evidence shows that cashmoney transfers reduce the negative coping strategies used by beneficiaries. Motivation of the local markets, cash-based interventions may benefit more than the direct recipients of cash. Local traders and service providers, transport workers and suppliers and transporters of goods may all benefit from households having money to spend as our perception view and expertise in the humanitarian set-up.

2.1.2 - Explain how the project design considered market functionality?

Assessments Conducted by KAF indicates that the IDP’s and Host Communities beneficiaries request during in the assessment that Extend cash assistance and provide vocational skills trainings to the displaced, therefore KAF will Register 800 beneficiaries for CFW in the selected IDP camps each will receive 50USD, Training camp leader’s on-site management to strengthen their capacities and Support community-led site maintenance activities to ensure the upkeep of sites (cash for work, site maintenance committees)so as to reduce the negative coping strategies used by beneficiaries.
