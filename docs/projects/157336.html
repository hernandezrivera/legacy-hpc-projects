Project Summary
Most children of displaced communities are adding pressure to existing education facilities which cannot accommodate new students due to limited space and resources. the education intervention will be provide safe learning protected space, Rehabilitation of 4 schools 8 classes total 32 classes and construction of two informal classes in two schools with blocks, resetting for 10 classes and one kinder Garden per school, 24 latrines holes 14 hole for girls &10 hole for boys and 4 water points and backage of hygiene facilities(points, Basins, pipes and hygiene club for children to increase access to education for the most vulnerable disadvantaged children especially girls, also provision of recreational and educational material to children affected will improve their psycho social and emotional well-being of girls and boys, capacity buildings of education providers and child careers on choosiness support children to improve their inter communication skills programming strengthens children’s coping mechanisms to mitigate the effects of war and displacement. This model focuses on wellness, effective child friendly communication, interpersonal and community resourcefulness, displacement and protracted crisis. Forming school clubs and raising awareness of children education providers and stockholders and community on hygiene promotion and child protection will reduce the negative impact of displacement and poverty and provide prevention of disease in conditions of high density living such as residence settlements. Provision of adult education and community awareness programs will support informal education either by helping in constructions of schools or help in school activities. Tree nurseries and trees will be established in all safe learning spaces and school’s fences. School planting of seedling ’, including children in planting and reusing grey water hand washing facilities for extra water need, awareness sessions for children and teachers combine with cleaning campaigns Special awareness will be provided on the health implications. In all training and activities, the gender issue will be considered awareness campaigns against early marriage and VAW will be conducted BPWVO believes that education is the key for
those who cannot avail themselves to available education opportunities due to economic, political, social or geographical reasons and always invest in girls BPWO staffs are trained to work in difficult and emergency situations,

Needs

According to 2019, education in Sudan target 0.7 million people, out of which 99 per cent are children and youth, and one per cent are adults the project targeted people in need of education intervention. Even before the conflicts there is low enrollment particularly in targeted area and school aged children mostly female in White Nile have their education disrupted by early marriage and child labor The targeted areas are Algabalain locality Juda Administrative unit with their surrounded F ORGAN of vulnerable residents/ vulnerable poor people of concern. Intervention target 900 beneficiaries of different ages 600 pupils ages from 6-14 years ,420 girls (120 in two mixed schools , 300 in two girls schools) 280 boys in 2 mixed schools in addition to other 150 youth in informal education age 15- 18 years and 50 children age 4-6 for kinder garden, most of them are temporer famers and shepherds The affordability of school fees is one of the root causes that hinder access to education. Other factors are existing education are the inadequate security and emergency situations such as floods and the long way, which discourage them to send their children to expose them to harassment. As a result, children are in risk of dropping out of school, becoming exposed to additional risks and violence and child labor, critical gap in teachers in operated schools which are overcrowded and classes have more than 100 children, the dropout rate among girls is far higher than boys. This is due to the high rate of early marriage and social norms that do not support girls’ education. The illiteracy rate among residence is 80 %. The quality of education has deteriorated. Only less than 25% teachers have received trainings. The school environment has been affected by the inadequate sanitation facilities associated with poor access to clean water, and poor hygiene

Activities

1- Rehabilitate of 4 schools targeting 32 classrooms.2- Construction of 2 informal classes in two schools3- Construction two kinder garden with recreational material. 4- Constriction of gender sensitive 24 hole latrines 14 for girls and 10 holes’ latrines for boys with environmentally friend hand Wash facilities in the schools. 5- Provision of 2 water points in each school for safe drinking water supported by TLS.6- Establishing one hygiene club, & 10 hygiene promotion campaigns.7- Provide teacher assistance course training for 5 teachers including methods of teaching the new curriculum.8- Train 100 pupils in each school in environment protection activities and support them with plant seedling to plan their schools. 9- Formulation of 4 environment friends’ societies.10- Organizing 12 enrollment campaigns to promote girls’ education.
