Project Summary

GOAL will support the provision of comprenhensive health & nutrition services in 17 Primary health facilities across South Kordofan (Habila, Abukarshola, Rashad, and Talodi localities including support for Stabilization Center in Talodi and Habila) and 17 clinics in North Darfur (Kutum and Al Waha localities), in areas with high numbers of confict-affected households (including newly displaced persons, long-term IDPs, vulnerable host communities, and some returnees. GOAL will conitnue support to rural and hard-to-reach facilities in these Localities.
Services include Reproductive Health, EPI, early warning and outbreak response, support and training for health staff, clinic running costs, provision of drugs, medical supplies and equipment, and support for the Locality Health Department, including the Health Information System. GOAL also provides community health through Care Groups, community health promotion, defaulter tracing for RH and OTP services, and
the Nutrition Impact and Positive Practice (NIPP) approach to prevent and treat Moderate Acute malnutrition. GOAL will ensure comprehensive care in supported facilities, and GOAL will continue to
closely coordinate through the Ministry of Health and the Health and Nutrition Sectors in El Fashir, Kadugli and Khartoum, and will also engage in ongoing coordination with other partners in each locality to ensure no overlap. GOAL provides a package of lifesaving primary healthcare services in all supported facilities. PHC services include curative consultation, provision and dispensing of essential drugs, managing injuries, EPI to increase the immunization rates in children and pregnant mothers, nutrition screening for children under 5, OTP for management of SAM, reproductive health services, disease surveillance, outbreak response, and health education. Our ongoing programme in Sudan has taught us that an integrated programme works best, with linkages between health, RH, GBV, community health, and nutrition activities, especially at the community level and in defaulter tracing. Midwives play an especially critical role, both distributing Clean Delivery Kits which encourage skilled deliveries, and also playing a critical role in identifying and referring SGBV cases through GOAL’s existing RH referral pathway, which allows for anonymity and protection of survivors. GOAL’s extensive network of nutrition workers, volunteers and NIPP circle members will also support to identify protection and GBV cases and do appropriate referrals. GOAL also supports referral of complicated SAM and RH cases and ensures provision of medicines and support for staff in rural areas where SMoH has not deployed health workers. On-the-job capacity building, mentoring and formal training for SMoH seconded staff is provided as part of the facility-level support, where needs are identified.

1 - Needs

There is significant vulnerability of the overall population due to lack of access by government services, and ongoing insecurity. MoH presence is thin due to low capacity and proximity to rebel-held areas in SK and rural, hard to reach ar. Prior to GOAL intervention, only 50% of health facilities were functional and most of these were providing only limited services. Through the support of other donors, GOAL has in place seconded staff from MoH, however, these staff need regular refresher training (including on IMCI, reproductive health and SGBV, and facility management) to enable them to effectively provide health services in these localities. GOAL requests staff to be seconded by Ministry of Health in the health facilities that it supports, to ensure qualified staff, build long-term capacity, and promote sustainability. The health status of the populations of Talodi, Habla, Rashad, and Abukarshola remains critical with diarrhea, malaria, and acute respiratory infection (ARI) regularly affecting large numbers of people. GOAL proposes to provide health services through a total of 34 PHC facilities in Sudan, including 17 in North Darfur and 17 in South Kordofan (specific locations above at the beginning of health sector), covering a catchment population of 314911 vulnerable people, while also supporting the locality health department (LHD) in the localities where these facilities are located: Kutum and Al Waha in North Darfur; and Talodi, Rashad, Abukarshola, and Habila in South Kordofan. All PHC facilities will provide a comprehensive package of primary health care services (see more in Basic Primary Health Care sub-sector design). All facilities will support the overall health system and locality health department through weekly early warning reporting, supporting EPI campaigns, linking with community health activities to promote use of care and positive health, hygiene, and nutrition behaviors. Through community-based midwives and community nutrition volunteers (CNVs), GOAL will be able to provide reproductive health services and nutrition screenings and defaulter tracing in the community. GOAL will ensure referrals are in place particularly for EMOC, Severe Acute Malnutrition, and SGBV cases, and all referred cases will have costs covered by GOAL and results monitored back at the PHC. GOAL ensures IPC in all supported health facilities and training for communities, staff, and health system stakeholders. GOAL will provide PPEs to staff. Furthermore, all PHC have latrine and incinerator. Minor rehabilitation will include repairs of doors, windows, locks, and basic servicing of electricity and other minor maintenance and repairs, to ensure that medicines and supplies can be stored securely and safely, and that the facility overall can function properly. For health and nutrition interventions, GOAL closely coordinates activities with both South Kordofan and North Darfur State Ministry of Health (SMoH), and regular meetings will be set up with the relevant LHDs, referral hospitals, and sector partners. GOAL submits weekly EWARN reports from all the supported clinics to the State MoH and WHO and attends the weekly coordination meeting at State MoH level. GOAL program managers in Kadugli and El Fasher facilitate dialogue with State MOH, UNICEF, UNFPA, WHO and other stakeholders at State level. At locality level, GOAL works with LHD staff to implement activities and oversee quality of services. This includes also ensuring effective reporting to the Health Information System and that data is available, as well as ensure that facilities and localities are regularly submitting EWARN reports
