Project Summary

This Project derives from the Cooperation Programme between UNICEF and the Ministry of Basic Education. It aims to improve the learning environment in 22 schools (70 classrooms) from 03 subdivisions by acting on two levels. Firstly, the project intends to alleviate the overcrowdness of classrooms and provide additional infrastructures for the enrollment of out-of-school children. Secondly, the project will improve access and utilization of quality WINS infrastructures by constructions of 86 latrines and community mobilization and training.

Besoins / Needs

The target population consists of pupils and out-of-school primary going-age children, teachers and members from schools' participatory structures.The project targets 22 primary schools in 03 communities from Fotokol, Koza and Kolofata in Mayo Sava, Mayo Tsanaga and Logone et Chari divisions. It intends to construct and equip 70 classrooms to provide children in overcrowded classrooms with more adequate learning conditions, and also accommodate out-of-school children. The project will also construct 86 sexospecific and inclusive latrines equipped with handwash mechanisms and 22 waterpoints to further improve the learning environment and make it friendlier for girls specifically. 10,350 children of which 4,140 girls are expected to benefit from the infrastructures which will be created by the project.Besides, 770 teachers of target division will be trained on how to integrate WINS and MHM related themes in pedagogical practices and lessons. 330 members from 22 school participatory structures will be mobilized and engaged on good practices of WINS and MHM; for the benefit of communities.
