Project Summary

Throughout this project, PUI will support vulnerable Lebanese, PRL, migrants through health-tailored activities designed to strengthen the resilience of households with vulnerable pregnant/lactating women. This project was designed based on PUI’s technical expertise and long-term presence and acceptance in the area of intervention. Furthermore, PUI will also respond to cholera alerts through prevention and response by supporting 2 cholera treatment units (CTU)/cholera treatment centers (CTC)/PHCCs in Cholera hot spot areas in addition to 8 contracted hospitals. Furthermore, PUI will raise awareness at community level while distributing as well oral rehydration salts (ORS). The 2 CTU/CTC/PHCCs will be equipped with medical and nonmedical items to stabilize cases and refer them to the hospital or discharge them home. This project will also support HR, fuel additional costs, waste management, and light rehabilitation works to make the centers’ flow adjustable to this alert. Monitoring activities will be carried out throughout the life of the project in order to guarantee the compliance of the activities with agreed objectives and targets. A detailed monitoring and evaluation plan will be developed at the beginning of the project by PUI’s MEAL unit together with the technical staff. Data collection and storage tools will grant the highest level of data protection and confidentiality, allowing a systematic disaggregation per sex, age, and disability and providing automatic reporting features. Programme monitoring will combine different methodologies, based on qualitative and quantitative approaches, including surveys and regular field visits. Patient Satisfaction and Post Intervention Surveys will be conducted at least twice during the project implementation period in order to gather feedback from pregnant women on their perceptions about the quality and the outcomes of the services received, their feelings about topics such safety and accessibility as well as the patient centered approach supported by PUI. In specific circumstances preventing direct access to the project areas, such as COVID-19 related movements restrictions decided by the GOL or civil unrest, measures will be taken to shift these activities to remote modalities (phone calls, reduced sample sizes, online FGDs, etc.).

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

A PRA was conducted and mitigation measures were identified: provision of capacity building for health staff on safeguarding policies, FCRM, Safe Identification and Referrals and PFA; info sharing and awareness for PoC on existing FCRM to strengthen accountability; provision of SHR services by female staff to ensure female benfs’ access to services and respect of social and cultural norms; provision of services to all groups/nationalities and clear communication on criteria of assistance to avoid inter-communal tensions; provision of services at community level to reduce risks for PoC to be exposed to violence and abuse while accessing PHCC; IEC material distributed by CHVs is gender, age and developmentally appropriate, is in multiple languages and is distributed through different access points to enhance accessibility to information and to reach at-risk populations; consultations with community take place during the entire project to identify additional risks and mitigation measures.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

To ensure that projects implemented by PUI in Lebanon are responsive to the population’s needs within targeted areas, PUI involves the affected populations in all stages of the project cycle. During implementation, PUI will follow the recently revised FCRM Standard operating Procedures to gather feedback from beneficiaries, using multiple channels. All beneficiaries’ feedback/complaints are addressed and used to improve program implementation, provide timely response, and enhance transparency. Informed consents will be taken from any person of concern and strict confidentiality rules will be followed. Beneficiaries are informed of all available feedback mechanisms throughout the implementation of the project. PUI uses the Referral Information Management System (RIMS) to effectively track, follow-up and monitor referrals across sectors. Moreover, PUI will ensure the accessibility of the FCRM to migrants in order to prevent language barriers.
