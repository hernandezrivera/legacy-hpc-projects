Project Summary


The project aims to access to formal and informal education safely
for girls and boys at school age affected by crises (5-17 years)
By providing intensive education for 840 students who were cut off from learning in order to enroll in the appropriate stage of study for their age.
The project is establishing tents in camps
Supported by the block and it contains the necessary school equipment (blackboard, chairs, table and chair for the teacher in addition to stationery materials)
that have received new numbers of displaced people in the recent period in the north of Syria and Preparated it to receive children (3-17 years, girls / boys) In the following areas:
(Toum - Afrin)
And support this educational process as required,
And based on the recent recommendations of the Syria 2021 Humanitarian Needs Overview,
After ten years of conflict, it is critical that formal and non-formal education services are approached as a long-term investment by not only children and their families but by aid actors
Via
Back-to-Learning (BTL) campaigns and present the curriculum through an intensive curriculum
So that the student can join in his Class in the nearest formal school After the student has completed the intensive curriculum within 3 month
Or 6 months in the sense of delay that happened to him as a result of displacement and displacement
Note: tents' numbers were specified according to statics that made by our team in the
mentioned areas during the third month of the current year.
4 tents will be established in Afrin, two for primary, two for secondary (male-female), comprising 120 students at one stage,
And through two successive periods, which means 120 * 2 = 240 within 3 months
3 tents will be established in the Toum Camp, one for primary, two for secondary (male-female), 90 students at one stage, And through two consecutive periods, which means 90 * 2 = 180 within 3 months.
One class includes 15 students in one lesson, and there is a rotation between males and females, so that the total number is 30 students per period within the same class.
The recommendations in order to avoid infection with corona will be taken into consideration through awareness leaflets, maintaining the spacing between children, and training teachers and administrators on these techniques.
The curriculum for this process will be an science curriculum, which shortens the teaching of the academic year to a period of three months
Will be contracted with
33 teachers (male and female) will be appointed to secondary school, 8 (male-female) teachers for the primary stage.
5 (male-female) mentors.
4 (male-female) administrators.
Notice:
All teachers and administrators are from the same camps and targeted areas
Our organization is working to secure funding for this project through continuous campaigns, and we had previous experience in recent years through the Accelerated Education Project in Kafr Naha, before the military campaigns were launched against it.
The curriculum that was adopted is the (Ummah) curriculum that Abrar Organization has adopted since 2017

5 - Implementation of activities

The project works to reach 840 formal education through non-formal education through the Umma curriculum, in which the curricula used in northern Syria have been intensified, so that the school year is shortened to three months, which has been adopted by Abrar Organization since 2016, - Conduct Back-toLearning (BTL) campaigns Through 6 awareness campaigns Emphasis will be placed on the role of education in the revival of society, in addition to the emphasis on the role of Abrar Organization in supporting enrolled children by placing their families so that they have priority in food baskets and in the aid that will be obtained from the organization.Establish, expand and rehabilitate new tentes (ln Project Summari) - Provide students with learning materials (stationary, pens, notebooks), to 840 students (malefemale) depending on the stage of their studies. - 50 teachers will be enrolled in a training course to deal with the temporary and non-formal classes. - 50 teachers (Male / Female) will be provided with monthly incentives for6 month - Provide textbooks to children The curriculum will be provided to 840 students during the project period in cooperation with the Directorate of Education and Local Councils - classroom activities involving the distribution of some gifts (according to the gender of the bene¦ciary / male or female) once a month, The registration regulations, weekly and monthly reports, in addition to the results of the final examinations and then follow-up of students after conducting an exploration of information for them in public schools will be evidence of the progress and development of the project throughout the period of the project.
