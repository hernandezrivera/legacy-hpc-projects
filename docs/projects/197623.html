Project Summary

This project seeks to ensure that targeted conflict affected populations of the North-West and South-West Regions of Cameroon receive various Protection and response services from our Protection Monitoring Personnel.
During the implementation of this project, COHESODEC will be establishing and supporting 14 community based complaints and feedback mechanisms and will as well be enhancing advocacy through training of 130 civil society advocates and 100 local and national authorities on disability inclusion based on the IASC guidelines for people living with disabilities to be included in national programs.
Moreover, this project seeks provide targeted protection assistance to 130 most vulnerable people (persons living with disabilities to be included in social and economic support to livelihood through a productive economic inclusion program for persons living with disabilities including training and support to set up various forms of income generating activities and offering conditional cash transfers.
The project shall also be providing legal assistance to some 14,000 crisis-affected people on the protection of their rights through sensitization sessions focused on their rights and obligations while also ensuring that 100 crisis-affected people received legal assistance.
Furthermore, COHESODEC shall be conducting protection monitoring in crisis-affected areas involving 7 regular protections monitoring activities, ensuring at least 130 referrals to specialized services and ensuring that at least 5,000 crisis-affected persons are covered by protection monitoring activities.
Finally, COHESODEC shall be providing targeted protection assistance to at least 100 of the most vulnerable conflict-affected people (men, women, boys and girls, PWD. The project will most importantly be strengthening the process of issuance of civil documentation to 1,500 conflict-affected people ensuring that they benefit from civil or identity documentation support and 50 awareness-raising sessions on civil documentation.

1 - Besoins / Needs

In the implementation of this project, COHESODEC will be requiring the collaboration of other protection stakeholders such as: community leaders, setting up a 24/7 call-in lines and suggestion boxes to ensure adequate support to the community based complains and feedback mechanisms.Moreover, COHESODEC will require logistical support to organize advocacy training sessions for civil society actors and local/national authorities centered on advocacy for people living with disabilities to be included in national programs based on the IASC guidelines. Also, COHESODEC will support targeted protection assistance to the most vulnerable people (persons living with disabilities in the form of social and economic support to livelihood through a productive economic inclusion program for persons living with disabilities including training and support to set up various forms of income generating activities and offering conditional cash transfers.Furthermore, the project shall ensure the provision of legal assistance to crisis-affected people on the protection of their rights through sensitization sessions and ensuring they effectively receive legal assistance. Also, the project will conduct protection monitoring activities in crisis-affected areas in targeted vulnerable communities. We shall ensure the provision of targeted protection assistance to the most vulnerable people (men, women, boys and girls, PWD.Finally, COHESODEC in the implementation of this project will solicit the services of high court judges, Police Services and local councils to ensure the issuance of 1,500 civil documentations to conflict-affected people. Also, COHESODEC will be producing hand-outs, brochures and posters for awareness raising campaigns on the importance of civil documentation.
