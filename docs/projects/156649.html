Project Summary

SEMA as an active organization in Both Health and protection cluster intend to respond to the high need of GBV services through this project in several modalities as follow:
1- SEMA has successfully integrated GBV services in its RH facilities, and intend to provide this service in it’s all other facilities from different levels (PHC, BemoCK,….) which are 14 facilities.
2- SEMA plans to conduct responsive and preventive activities to respond to the needs of conflict-affected families by existing 3 WGSS in (AFRIN: Sharran )AND(Jisr Alshogur: Ain Al-Bayda, kherbet Aljoz ) and planning to establish new one in (Afrin, Bulbul).
3- SEMA aims through this project to continue responding to the urgent needs of the to the new IDP’s in Jisr-Ash-Shugur through outreach teams, delivering a package of integrated, comprehensive protection services including GBV services.
4- During this year SEMA will assess the current practices in monitoring and evaluation methods used in the Syrian response with collaboration with another partner in GBV sub-cluster as a first phase, to be followed by developing a harmonized tool to measure the impact of the services conducted.

Needs

After 8 years of continuous conflict in Syria, Gender-based violence (GBV) remains a major protection risk, particularly in forms such as domestic and family violence, child marriage and the fear of sexual violence and harassment. The lack of understanding for the need for GBV services in some communities remains high, indicating a need to strengthen GBV awareness, referral pathways, as well as outreach and prevention activities. Disruption of traditional safety nets and the reduction of livelihood opportunities, in addition to sudden displacement, put many women and girls at risk of various types of GBV that affect their lives in different ways, aggravating protection concerns such as psychosocial stress, limitation of movement in some areas of the country, situations and potential for increased recourse to negative coping mechanisms. This context highlights a continuous need for increasing the availability of specialized GBV services.In reference to the HNO 2019 assessments which have indicated that there is gender-based violence need in Syria, SEMA is implementing GBV programs by Women and Girls Safe Spaces in the following Subdistrict (Afrin and Jisr Al Shogour). Particularly sexual harassment, early/forced marriage and domestic violence (including intimate partner violence and other forms of family violence against women and girls) continue to pervade the lives of women and girls in Syria inside and outside the home, resulting in very few spaces where women and girls feel safe. The length of the crisis and deep-rooted social norms, in conjunction with the mounting lawlessness in some areas, are normalizing this violence, resulting in the detrimental psychosocial wellbeing and the continued erosion of women’s and girls’ rights. The restriction on freedom of movement of women and girls also inhibits their access to services.

Implementation of activities

Also provides a place where women can access confidential services which is :- Providing GBV specialized services to including case management and PSS support Provide activities empowering women and girls and GBV prevention activitiesEnhance the referral pathways.Prevention and response activities targeting adolescent girlsReach out for both men and boys through GBV awareness raising activities Capacity building foe the team including, Training of case managers on SOP and Case Management • Training of WGSS staff on WGSS minimum standards using relevant curriculum• Provide supervision to GBV service providers, particularly case managers through case discussions and learning sessionsPSEA training will be conducted for staff in both facilities to insure accountability to affected population. coordination with other organizations working on the emergency response. - coordinating with other partner in the cluster for effective response in case of emergences .- In coordination with the GBV sub- cluster and clustrer members, SEMA will assess the current tools used to monitor and evaluate the impact of the GBV services in northwest Syria in the first half of 2019 and during the next phase, M&E consortium led by SEMA in cooperation with the GBV sub-cluster will develop harmonized tools to monitor and evaluate the impact of delivered services.-SEMA’s M&E department will develop a plan for monitoring and reporting includes: daily reports about the activities conducted written by team leaders or persons who are responsible for conducting the activities; on a weekly basis the center manager with help of administrative and technical staff will compile the daily reports and write a weekly report that can be shared with project management team; on a monthly basis, the project manager, with M&E and finance staff, will compile the reports from the center in one monthly narrative report that can be shared with organization management .SEMA has established effective accountability mechanisms in all of its work to the extent possible for each activity, integrate a beneficiary inputs and feedback mechanism into project design and implementation. Active engagement with beneficiaries, including community organizations, women and youth, will inform the assessment of vulnerabilities and ensures accountability to the community.- Project staff will be trained on accountability, including best practices, to provide safe and accessible opportunities for feedback.Beneficiaries will be made aware of their rights and accessibility in regards to services provided by SEMA, and enable beneficiaries to provide feedback on the project activities to SEMA staff and receive follow-up on these issues.- As a result, SEMA will continue to implement a full-fledged monitoring, evaluation and accountability system for all components of the proposed project. Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk 1 : Changing the security situation that interfere with implementation of the project. Mitigation Strategies: We will establish a response plan that can be activated according to the situation. Designing the activities in a flexible way where we can change the way of conducting the activity according to the situation so as to achieve the wanted results. and we can focus on more suitable activities like doing PFA. Stopping the activities for short period. moving the center to another place if deemed necessary after negotiation with OCHA.. Risk: The local authorities decide to prevent us from implementing the project or part of it. Mitigation: as preventive measures we will coordinate with the local authorities and negotiate with them about the best way to implement the project and discuss the importance the project that will help. . Risk 3: changing in the attitudes or priorities of the population that will affect their willingness to benefit from the project activities. Mitigation strategies: Will work on awareness raising throughout the project period and will get the feedback from the beneficiaries about the services and adapt accordingly . Risk 4 : Due to lack of experienced people in the targeted communities, IDP may be recruited based on their previous experience. Mitigation Strategies: equality in opportunities among host community and IDPs will be ensured throughout selecting people who are residing in the targeted area regardless of being IDP or from host community. Job vacancies advertisements will be shared with the local councils. Risk 5 : Risk of triggering sensitivity with the local vendors during the procurement and purchasing materials from outside the targeted areas. Mitigation Strategies: All materials that are available in the targeted area should be purchased locally in accordance with SEMA procurement policies. Risk 6 : Risk of demands on case management fund by people who are not in comply with the required standards. Mitigation Strategies: The influence of the local council and community leaders will be utilized in order to justify that this amount of money is not relief assistance and it should be in accordance with specific criteria to target the fragile categories.
