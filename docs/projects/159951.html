Project Summary

Despite above average Gu rains in certain areas of Somalia, the delayed rains have prevented the population from planting crops in time for harvesting and has resulted in poor harvests. Cities and major towns are still the most favorable destinations for the majority of the affected population that lost their livelihoods. Despite numerous efforts to reach rural and unreached communities humanitarian aid remains largely concentrated in urban areas. Insecurity and protracted drought still do not allow safe and dignified returns for the affected population, while eviction continue to be a major protection issue for displaced populations and prevents work towards local integration. Self-settled sites remain exposed and more prone to hazards related to site layout such as fire and floods, but also other protection concerns related to overcrowded spaces and lack of privacy. Even in urban settings, humanitarian assistance is provided in a discontinuous way, with poor quality service delivery or the absence of exit strategies.
Camp management remains key to ensure coordination of service provision, underpinned by reliable, strong and regular information management, in order to ensure identification of gaps, advocacy for the needs of affected population, as well as monitoring of evictions risk and service delivery to minorities and marginalized groups. The participation of affected populations is lacking in many aspect of the delivery humanitarian assistance. In response to this, camp management is crucial to empower affected populations through dissemination of information on delivery of humanitarian assistance and in supporting inclusive and representative community governance structure. Moreover, it monitors service delivery by other partners. In order to reach displaced sites in rural or unreached areas, whenever security conditions allows, ACTED will adopt a two layers approach. Mobile teams will visit sites weekly in urban areas, providing a full range of camp management activities whereas for rural areas, the team will mix remote site monitoring with in person visit (every one or two months). The teams will collect information on needs, provide support through capacity building of governance and Communication with Communities (CwC) while focusing on quick impact projects for the community to account for the absence of service providers.

Project Activities

Coordination and Information management:Coordination will aim at monitoring service delivery and standards in the sites, identifying gaps and needs and advocating for them, while maximizing impact of partners programming for available resources. Coordination will be underpinned by reliable, up to date information management on population data and service delivery. Information products produced and circulated with partners, clusters, local authorities and communities will include: 3Ws, service mapping, site profile, site monitoring databases, eviction risk monitoring (in coordination with HLP) and CCCM snapshots. Apart from site partners, ACTED will actively coordinate with the CCCM and other National and Sub National Clusters, including HLP colleagues responsible for Areas of Operations and local authorities. Site Care and MaintenanceACTED will provide tools, skilled training supervision and capacity building for site maintenance and site improvements through cash for work activities according to a plan developed jointly by Camp Management Teams and the communities. The plan will also integrate and include activities for GBV risk mitigation, fire risks mitigation and flood management. Moreover, the plan will take into account the elimination of existing physical barriers for disabled people to access services. Communication with Communities and Community ParticipationACTED will map existing governance structures and their integration within the communities. This will be used to determine the inclusivity of the different available services and the level capacity building required for community governance structures. Communication with communities and community participation will include an integrate and multi sector Complaint and Feedback Mechanism for the sites while increasing community awareness on rights, services available and risk mitigation through outreach activities. ACTED will also focus on supporting communities to implement community led projects targeting humanitarian priorities identified directly by communities. GBV IntegrationGBV will be actively integrated in CCCM interventions. Safety audits, in particular, will be implemented to raise awareness on GBV risks and vulnerabilities with the humanitarian community. GBV risks identified through safety audits will be mitigated through site maintenance activities, a strong focus on women empowerment and women participation, as well as coordination with GBV partners to disseminate the knowledge of existing referral pathways for partners and the community. Durable solutionsCamps and camp like settings are just temporary solutions to displacement, therefore the project will link to durable solutions through coordination with existing durable solutions forum at consortium, at the local and national level. ACTED will strengthen the cooperation with HLP and local authorities for preventing eviction risks and negotiating stable land tenure agreements as a first step to achieve local integration.

Mitigation Measures

Through regular physical presence in the sites and service delivery monitoring activities, ACTED will ensure that principles of impartiality is respected by partners working in the sites and that beneficiaries are prioritized only on the base of needs. If community governance structures in place are not inclusive, they will be integrated so that marginalized groups of the population will also be represented. Community Governance structures and communities will be empowered through capacity building activities and dissemination on information on services available as well as rights of the affected population .An integrated and multi sector CFM mechanism for the site will give the chance to every individuals to present complaints, including reporting discriminatory delivery of humanitarian assistance. Training conducted by ACTED in CCCM have specific modules on Protection and GBV mainstreaming and through pre and post training test ACTED will be able to measure if improvement of knowledge occurred. If community governance structures in place are not inclusive, they will be integrated so in order to equally represent different ages and gender of the affected population. Community Governance structures and communities will be empowered through capacity building activities and dissemination on information on services available as well as rights of the affected population .An integrated and multi sector CFM mechanism for the site will give the chance to every individuals to present complaints, including reporting discriminatory delivery of humanitarian assistance. The project will have a strong GBV integration component, with focus on safety audit and women participation as well as mitigation of GBV risks faced in the sites. Training conducted by ACTED in CCCM have specific modules on Protection and GBV mainstreaming. Through small project grants for the community, ACTED will targeted youth and women to implement community led project to address residents needs as identified by young and female residents. ACTED will target new arrivals and evicted communities with emergency and immediate cash assistance: this will provide at least short term relief to HHs in serious distress because of displacement, often connected to widespread violence, especially in south central Somalia. New arrivals will also referred to specialized protection partners for immediate protection assistance, following referral pathways in site, and to other services available, such as nutrition and health. Though outreach workers and camp management mobile teams, ACTED will target with new arrivals with information dissemination activities, to make them aware of the services available, and will ensure that their needs and views and represented within the community governance structures. ACTED will regularly report new arrivals and new sites to the National and Sub National Cluster. Whenever possible, ACTED will try to expand on-going activities in order to target newly formed sites not receiving assistance. When not possible, ACTED will advocate for new funds and ensure to report to other humanitarian actors the most urgent needs of the sites.

Please provide further explanation

In areas where evictions are less rampant and public land is available ACTED will promote transition to local integration as durable solutions, particularly through: -promote and conduct consultation with affected population on intentions; -participate in durable solutions forums at the local level (and advocate for their creation), inclusive of all the stakeholders , with the local authorities playing a key part in the planning for durable solutions-advocate for land and resilience services for affected population -support and promote capacity building initiatives for local authorities, to enable them to cope with displacement waves -in urban context, adopting a settlement approach that looks into integrating displaced population into existing services without discrimination, social safety net or links with development actors-integrate CM and Shelter component and, once land is secure with a stable formal stable agreement, built sustainable transitional shelters with active involvement of the local population -link construction of Transitional shelters to other existing long term and resilience building interventions -with its Community Participation approach, ACTED will build community capacity working towards community self-management and future handover of camp management activities to the communities themselves.
