HF (previously known as AHA) will provide shelter support through rehabilitating of 800 households living in Idleb and Aleppo governorate and NFI distribution for IDPs for 1250 HH

HF will target HHs that have been partially destroyed due to continued damage as a result of aerial/land attacks in addition to general dilapidation from poor living conditions
(HF will verify that by Local Councils and members of the host community that reside in those homes). In addition to the repair of small damages in walls, doors, broken windows, locks and deteriorated plumbing.. Mainly, shelter type grade 2 as Residential housing (single or multiple-story buildings), partially damaged or unfinished. Also, this activity will be implemented through two types of contract modalities, the first is through contractor for the for procurement and transportation of materials, coordination of works, including hiring of staff, health and safety practices as well as a commitment that the work is completed on time in addition to the mass works. In addition to that we will provide NFI kits for 1750 families of IDPs in those areas. The project will be targeting most vulnerable HHs especially women heading HH based on the previous implementation of our work; we focused on reaching them as one of the most vulnerable HH of the communities..

4 - Needs

In 2020, the number of people requiring shelter assistance remained substantial, with 5.88 million people in need. This is attributed to the loss of capital, destruction of housing infrastructure and the limited response. The systematic targeting of social infrastructure including civilian homes has been a major driving force behind mass internal displacement. Families deprived of adequate shelter provision suffer from a wide spectrum of fundamental issues that erode their quality of life, continue to deprive them of essential resources, diminish their physical and mental protection and reduce their overall resilience/ability to respond to conflict-related events. ongoing economic limitations will negatively impact household resilience across all aspects of basic living standards, further reducing families’ ability to pay rent, address shelter issues, meet their basic needs– potentially causing households to resort to harmful coping mechanisms HNO2021 In addition to poor sanitation, exposure to hazardous environmental conditions and lack of safety, families endure psychological instability that arises from lack of privacy within degraded homes, HF had conducted a needs assessment for most of the sub-districts in targeted locations, where we received many appeals from LCs, our team headed to the location and accomplish the rapid assessment where some findings of the surveys were: It showed a great and urgent need for: - rehabilitation for houses for the IDPs and host community in addition to the NFI kits
