1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project aims to improve the life conditions of families displaced by the conflict, host communities, returnees and communities located in the areas affected by food insecurity and cyclones. Inkind and CBT aims to give support to the most needy ones reporting constraints in carrying out livelihoods activities. Very often recently displaced individuals found themeself in such a situation at the arrival in a new and safer place. The program foresees also CFW aimed at building up basic infrastructure for agriculture (e.g. irrigation interventions, watercourse rehabilitation, barns, wells, animal fences etc.) for host community and community affected by conflict and cyclone, as well as maintenance interventions in the relocation sites. The families in the conditions to cultivate their own land will be also trained on sustainable agricultural techniques by AVSI agronomists and will receive seeds and tools. Specific plots will be Idenitied and used for demonstrations so that to scale up sustainable agricultural techniques. Every activities of distribution will be assessed through a post-monitoring form elaborated through Kobotool. Other livelihood activities implemented to contrast food insecurity will be: animal farming (goats poultry and egg production), pisciculture and subsistence fishery.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

1.1 Cash-based Transfer for IDPs, host communities, returnees and cyclone affected communities. 1.2 Cash-for-Work (CFW)to build infrastructure for agriculture, farming and rehabilitations of relocation sites targeting IDPs, host communities, returnees and cyclone affected communities. 1.3 Training on sustainable agriculture techniques 1.4 Seeds and agriculture tools distributions in host community for IDPs, host communities, returnees and cyclone affected communities.1.5 Training on specific livelihood activities (Animal farming, egg production, pisciculture and fishery)1.6 Livelihood kit distributions in host community for IDPs, host communities, returnees and cyclone affected communities. 1.8 Literacy, numeracy and financial training 1.9 Post-distribution monitoring

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Potential harms and risks may arise, for instance, either when the lists of beneficiaries selected for the distribution activities are not double-checked and then the products ends up in the hands of wrong beneficiaries; or when the intended beneficiaries sell the products received from the distributions or give them to a third party/person in exchange for personal favors not related to improving the food security of the target population, or there is not a coordination with other implementing partners at cluster level to avoid duplications . To avoid this, the list of beneficiaries will be verified by AVSI staff before the distributions to ensure that the beneficiaries meets the vulnerability criteria. Moreover, the distributions will be monitored through post distribution monitoring activities through questionnaires elaborated with kobtool. The access to assistance that does not depend directly from the implementing partner is also promoted at the SDAE and DPASA level. By coordinating closely with the local authority, AVSI will advocate for increasing the assistance to people in need whenever deemed necessary.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The project itself is based on the strong participation of the beneficiaries for the activities implementation. Before the beginning of the project, focus group activities will be planned to understand which are the beneficiaries expectations concerning project impact. These meetings will be organized and held in close coordination with local leaders and the existing structure at the community level. Firstly, community’s opinion on how to adapt the general aspects of activities implementation and coordination according to local culture, customs, beliefs and capacity, will be taken into account and incorporated into the work methodology. Secondly, it will be created a specific project hotline for feedback and complaints related to the project and it will be promoted the Linha Verde. It is worthy to note that AVSI has adopted an internal policy on accountability to Affected People on the March 23th, 2020 based on the IASC Commitments on Accountability to Affected Populations (CAAP).

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Distirbutions of seeds, tools, livelihood kits and cash-based transfer will be insured for the swath of the population having disabilities. During the identification of the beneficiaries in close coordination with local SDAEs, Acção Social (INAM), and reference cluster partners, special attention will be given to the most vulnerable, including persons with the disabilities. Accordingly, the post-distribution monitoring form will be elaborated so that to swiftly track individuals with disabilities and therefore check regularly the assistance provided to them. Moreover, it is worthy to note that very often people with disabilities are discriminated within their own communities. This can be a compounding factor when people with disabilities arrived in a host community, as in the case of IDPs. AVSI Foundation will ensure the implementation of sensitization sessions throughout its activities to ensure that these people are supported by the community and given special attention when coordinating and planning activities with local authorities and partners.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

AVSI will follow the policies and rules contained in the AVSI Environmental Policy. Environmental considerations include the use of locally sourced materials. Agriculture trainings will link environmental health with agricultural productivity, stressing the importance of water conservation and the effects of excessive charcoal production. The environmental sustainability and appropriateness of all agriculture activities, including seed varieties and land and water usage, will be ensured through regular consultation with FAO and the relevant country and state authorities
