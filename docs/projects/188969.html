1 - Needs

Protracted communal conflict of tribal nature instigated by divided political opinions, cattle raids and revenge killings in Unity state coupled with the effects of climate change causing perennial flooding since 2019 are the key diverse to humanitarian crisis and emergencies in Leer, Mayendit and Rubkona. (IPCs ,2020,2021)indicate these counties as food insecure, poor health condition and livelihood, effects to education due to hyped population displacements due to floods and violence.(HPC 2021) shows an estimation of 5 million people are in need of protection intervention of which 2 million require gender-based violence (GBV) interventions and 2.7 million are in need of child protection(CPAoR). 426,000 people have been affected and displaced by floods while in Unity state (koch, Leer,Mayendit and Rubkona payams were the most affected. Floods have exacerbated the vulnerability of communities, with many people displaced by the moving onto higher land areas within their county and planning to return home once the flood waters recede. Koch, Mayendit, Leer and Rubkona counties are characterized by sporadic inter-communal violence, cattle raids, displacement/migration, flooding and volatile prices on local markets, leading to market collapse, and exacerbating the humanitarian crisis. These shocks and stressors contribute negatively to the lives of the girls, boys, men, women and children with special needs. Since the beginning of the year, Unity State has experienced major intercommunal conflicts/tensions that resulted in the suspension of humanitarian services in some location. The most devastating conflict led to 10 fatalities in February 2021, when the youth from the boma of Jahjah in Koch County raided cattle from the neighboring Koch county. During the Initial Rapid Needs Assessment IRNA- May 2021), the population reported a number of GBV/CP incidents including rape, physical assaults, and burning of houses and foodstores and livestock , with knowledge of county government. As such, there has been a rise in Child protection risks. This is evident from the information retrieved from the CPIMS,CPAOR (Jan- August, 2021)weekly caseload reports which shows about 2000 are separations, Unaccompanied, Stressed, traumatized, abducted , forced marriages, psychological/emotionally abused, denied resources/opportunities . Anecdotal information from the communities also indicates that there are a growing tensions over land for settlement between the host community and the IDPs in Leer town and pillieny Payam, Rubkona in Kaljak and Nhialdil, Mayendit in Tutnyang, Malkual,pabuong and Thaker, Koch in mirmir, Jaak,bang and Jahjah payams child protection issues are emerging between the IDPs and host communities. These shocks and stressors increase protection concerns sexual abuse affecting girls and women, while boys are forcefully recruited to an official armed groups in Rubkona, Bentiu Pocs and other parts of UNITY state. Besides the political tension between Government and IO pose insecurity in the state more so, as south Sudan approach election in 2023. Koch, Mayendit , leer and Rubkona counties experience every year the capacity of the local government to respond remains very weak and needs strengthening. Since the formation of the Revitalized Transitional Government counties have not received equitable resources to respond to emerging shocks, as witnessed in the aftermath of the ongoing floods across the country.

7.4.3. How will your project use the beneficiaries’ feedback to adapt project assistance?

The project will ensure accountability to Affected persons through ensuring feedback and accountability mechanisms are integrated into project strategies, monitoring and evaluations, recruitment, staff inductions, training and performance management, partnership agreements, and reporting. it will also provide accessible and timely information to affected populations on organizational procedures, structures, and processes that affect them to ensure that they can make informed decisions and choices, and facilitate a dialogue between an organization and its affected populations over information provision. Feedback and complaints mechanisms such as the AAP committees, CBCPNs, suggestion boxes, and other community structures and leadership will be streamlined, appropriate and robust enough to deal with complaints about breaches in policy and stakeholder dissatisfaction.

7.4.4. How will your project ensure feedback from the most vulnerable members (e.g. individuals living with disabilities, individuals who are not literate, women and girls)?

A community based centered approach will be used where people with special needs with specific age and gender will be consulted during project planning, launching and kickoff of activities to ensure that there's a clear understanding of the deliverables and stakeholders in the project activities, In concern with security coordination with UNMISS, local leader s and youth groups will be done through engaging them in all project activities and ensure resources , job opportunities are distributed equally among them, In terms of price dynamics, UNIDOR will ensure that all cash is converted using the central bank rate. for effective service delivery UNIDOR will ensure that activity planning, procurement and logistics are done timely to avoid challenges of floods in rainy seasons. In coordination UNIDOR will ensure that all project activities will be coordinated through CBCPN, Child protection cluster at state level, Technical workings groups and all areas in protection AOR. UNIDOR will ensure timely reporting and information will be managed through information management system focal persons.

7.4.2. How will your project provide beneficiaries information about Protection against Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA)?

Through trainning on PSEA core concepts, Through community awareness messages, Including in languages, Interpretation, talking walls and visibility material which carry key messages of ZERO Torerance on PSEA. Engaging CBCPN and ensuring refresher sessions on PSEA are conducted monthlly.

7.4.1. How will your project provide beneficiaries information about the project, feedback mechanism and/or complaint mechanism?

The project is grounded on building/strengthening community-based mechanisms to prevent, mitigate and address protection risks. The project will build engage children through CFS and awareness in schools on child right and responsibility,sexual gender base violence targeting adolescent boys and girls and Covid 19 awareness. The service providers including Teachers, police and women leaders and community health workers would be trained on caring for crisis ,violence survivors including survivors of sexual gender base violence, CAAFAG. The project will work with community protection committees including women groups. Improve care and protection of children in their communication. The project will also work closely with children, women, men, volunteers in community-based protection networks (CBPNs), children’s clubs, local leaders, and influential people (e.g., community leaders and religious leaders) to identify and analyze the causes, scope, and patterns of protection risks that affect children in their communities. Action plans will then be developed to prevent and respond to identified protection concerns. The action plans will hinge on local resources, such cultural leaders, caregivers, teachers, and community leaders. The plans will then be implemented under the leadership of local leaders and influencers in the community to mitigate protection risks, improve the care protection of children and improve their well-being. UNIDOR will work with communities to build a mechanism for monitoring, identification, support, and referral of vulnerable and risk children for support services. CP and basic services mapping will be conducted to inform the development of referral pathways through the CP Sub-cluster and GBV Area of responsibility technical working groups. The referral pathway will be disseminated to children, their caregivers, teachers, and community leaders. This will provide a link for vulnerable and risk children and their communities to essential CP and basic services. Hence, will build local resources and strengthen local capacities for care and protection of children of children to ensure sustainability. As a community based organization, the organization enforces community initiated response and project ownership. Community participation in this project will be emphasized, through which they will gain knowledge and understanding of response mechanisms.
