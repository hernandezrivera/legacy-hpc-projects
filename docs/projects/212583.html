Project Summary

This project aims to respond to 10,200 HH with a total of 51,000 individuals disaggregated roughly as(10,200 men, 10,200 women,15,300 boys, 15,300 girls)for the most vulnerable groups IDPs and host communities in Aleppo and Idleb governorates, and prioritizing households headed by female, child, disabled, or elderly, then households with severely ill or disabled members, children below 5, elderly, pregnant or lactating women; with special attention to those recently displaced or living overcrowded.
The project contributes to providing life-saving and life sustaining shelter & NFI assistance through the following activities:
- Distribution of 2,200 NFI kits for 2,200 IDPs households (11,000 individuals disaggregated by gender: (2,200men, 2,200women, 3,300 girls, and 3,300 boys) in Afrin, Jandaris, Albab, A'zaz, Atareb, Daret Azza, Suran, Aghtarin, Harim - Dana - Kafr Takharim - Salqin - Maaret Tamsrin sub-districts.
- Distribution of 2,200 winterization kits/vouchers to 2,200 HHs (70% IDPs and 30% host)/ 11,000 individuals disaggregated by gender (2,200men, 2,200women, 3,300 girls, and 3,300 boys) in Afrin, Jandaris, Albab, A'zaz, Atareb, Daret Azza, Suran, Aghtarin, Harim - Dana - Kafr Takharim - Salqin - Maaret Tamsrin sub-districts.
-Rehabilition of 400 damaged houses for 400 HH, 2000 individuals (rehabilitation of 200 houses/minor repairs with an average cost 1000 dollars and 200 houses/Moderate and Severe with an average cost 1500 dollars)in Al Bab, Jandairis, A'zaz, Aghtrin, Mare', Jarablus, Harim, Dana, Salqin, Armanaz, Jisr-Ash-Shugur and Ariha).
- Construction\Installation of 300 temporary dignified shelters with related infrastructure for 300 HH disaggregated by gender (300 men, 300 women, 450 girls and 450 boys) in A’zaz, Aghtarin, Suran, Al Bab and A'rima sub-districts.
- Implementation of site levelling, gravelling, installation rain drains, and tents ground isolation for 8 sites reaching out for 1,400 IDP HHs (7,000 individuals) in Afrin, Jandaris, Albab, A'zaz, Jarablus, Ghandoura, Suran, Aghtarin, Armanaz - Idlib - Sarmin - - Maaret Tamsirin sub-districts.
-Installation/replacement of tents for (2,000 HH,10000 individuals) in Albab, A'zaz,Dana, Armanaz and - Maaret Tamsirin sub-districts.
- Implementation of HH level improvements (Tent gravelling) for 2400 HH (12,000 individuals) in
A'zaz, Jarablus, Al Bab, Ghandoura, Suran, Aghtarin, Armanaz - Idlib - Maaret Tamsirin-Dana sub-districts.
Needs assessment was conducted early this year to identify the project locations.
In all activities QC considers the protection of the beneficiaries, so the distribution centers and the shops provide the assistance will be prepared with functional firefighting equipment, brochures of safety and security practice will be distributed to beneficiaries, moreover, female staff will be recruited and trained to ensure more protection from exploitation and sexual abuse for girls and women and to deal with any GBV cases may encounter during the project.
Most importantly QC pays high attention to people with disabilities, elderlies, and people with special needs and for those assistance will be delivered to home.
Housing, Land and Property (HLP) rights of shelter sites, shelter beneficiaries, and house owners will be considered throughout all stages of shelter repair interventions, and agreement will be signed to guarantee the rights of the beneficiaries. Coordination with SNFI cluster and other active members is essential to ensure geographical separation and avoid overlapping/duplication.
