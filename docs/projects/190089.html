Project Summary

For more than three years, the province of Cabo Delgado in northern Mozambique has experienced attacks by Non-state Armed Groups (NSAGs), causing over 700,000 people to be internally displaced. Attacks have increased in severity and frequency since they began in October 2017, with continued increases in violence and subsequent displacement in 2020 as well as a significant rise in internal displacement following launched attacks in Palma Sede from 24 March 2021.
Vulnerable populations including women, children and the elderly are particularly impacted. In addition, humanitarian service delivery faces challenges. In the face of constrained humanitarian access, it becomes increasingly important to address the highest priority needs effectively and efficiently with a targeted approach. Quality and robust information is required to support effective inter-cluster coordination integrated response missions, and to enable partners to implement critical humanitarian response activities This project enables IOM’s Displacement Tracking Matrix (DTM) to establish IDP verification and registration efforts for the purposes of humanitarian response. In developing a centralized and regularly maintained digital platform – the proposed project is designed to maximize operational impact and support more effective and efficient resource mobilization, whilst strengthening partnerships with the wider humanitarian community. Beneficiary verification and digital registration for the purposes of humanitarian response will additionally support accountability over resource mobilization/allocation across humanitarian partners through
The overall objective of this project is to strengthen accountability and targeted humanitarian response through list activities:
1. Scale-up of IDP verification and registration across sites and host communities, including the provision of technical support in mapping and identifying IDPs hosted in urban settings. Technical support will be provided for purposes of planning for humanitarian assistance
2. Development of Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), inclusive of updated Community-Feedback mechanisms, revised Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) messaging and protection referral pathways-to fit the evolving context. Technical Assistance to verification/registration will also be adapted to align with minimum standards outlined in Verification/Registration Checklists drafted by the, including improved technical assistance in registration of conflict-affected populations (in line with Checklist outlined by Cabo Delgado Technical Working Group).
3. Expanding training sessions to government technicians (INGD, SDPI), local leadership, the local community, relevant committees, and relevant non-government stakeholders, including newly established NGOs active in Cabo Delgado), among others
To distinguish the different kinds of exercises in this project
• Verification is used to refer to a preliminary exercise whereby beneficiary identity is confirmed
• Registration of verified caseloads via digital platform (BRaVE or SCOPE)

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The DTM unit also has a rapid deployment roster of some 30 previously assessed staff members that can be activated on short notice. The roster includes persons trained in biometric registration including data entry and operations. IOM works under the IOM DTM global methodology. All information management/DTM projects comply with IOM Data Protection Principles, and respect of ethical and confidentiality considerations in all stages of data processing, including data collection, management and dissemination. The IOM Data Protection Principles ensure the systematic application of safeguards aiming at preserving the right to privacy of IOM beneficiaries. Data processing and analysis will integrate gender analysis and do no harm principles.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

IOM strengthens monitoring of DTM activities, through a monitoring tool to gather information directly from beneficiaries during DTM exercises to improve DTM responses and ensure that IOM is held accountable to affected populations.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

For all beneficary verification and registration activities, IOM will coordinate closely with protection partners on the site to ensure families with particularly vulnerable members, such as physically disabled and elderly, will be fast-tracked in the verification and registration process. Crowd control will be cognizant of those with identified vulnerabilities, or who arrive at the site after travelling long distances, and convey protection-related concerns to protection partners.
