Project Summary

Below average rains across most of Somalia during the 2018 Deyr October December rains followed by harsh weather conditions during the dry season and poor performance of the Gu season April to june 2019, has led to worsening drought conditions in many parts of the country. Food Security situation is still fragile owing to impacts of past devastating drought that nearly culminated into famine exacerbated successive seasons of below normal rains has significantly impacted to sustained food insecurity, limited access to safe water and sustained critical malnutrition rates among children and pregnant lactating women in Middle and Lower Juba, Gedo and Lower shabelle Regions (FSNAU Post Gu Technical report of July 2019).
The above plight calls for timely life-saving Food security intervention to enable the worst drought , displaced, returnees and vulnerable host communities' men, women boys and girls in the project targeted locations that are yet to recover from impacts of past devastating drought to meet their immediate food security needs through conditional and un-conditional safety nets as well as earn income in form of cash for work to sustain their family food needs in order to mitigate already precarious food insecurity conditions and further restore their livelihoods and strengthen their resilience with suitable food security support programmes to cope with the current and future shocks. The project targets 28,800 persons (14,861 females 13,939, male, 2880 boys and 2880 girls). limited access to safe water and persistent critical malnutrition rates among children aged under 5 and PLWs and families that were badly affected in Middle and Lower Juba, Gedo and Lower shabelle Region, Somalia with GAM ≥15.0% classified as stressed and crisis IPC 2-3 (FSNAU Post Gu Technical of 2nd July 2019).The situation is compounded by trade disruptions in parts of South and central regions of Somalia due to military action and related insecurity as well as currency depreciation that has impacted to high food prices in the local markets hence exorbitant food prices that is affordable to the vulnerable host communities, IDPs, returnees (men, women, boys and girls) whose purchasing powers is completely eroded having lost their livelihood assets to flooding, past severe drought and successive seasons of below normal rains. The affected vulnerable host, IDPs, returnee’s families are living precariously under very poor conditions and rely on marginal unreliable livelihood strategies under diminishing coping mechanism. There are affected families that moved to the IDP camps in the neighboring regions seeking humanitarian support while some men and women have moved to the market centers in search of manual jobs. JDO has proposed this intervention in order to provide and strengthen timely life-saving Food security intervention to enable the worst drought affected families in the project targeted locations to receive safety nets and earn some income in form of cash for work to sustain their family food needs in order to mitigate already deteriorated food insecurity conditions and further restore their livelihoods and strengthen their resilience to cope with the current and future shocks. The project will use cash modalities for some activities this will be so where markets are fully functional.

Project Activities

1. Provision of food vouchers, conditional and unconditional cash transfers as well as e transfers to 1800 families most vulnerable and displaced women and children headed households and those with chronic illnesses and the disabled in the target locations2. Rehabilitation of 5 water catchments, farming lands/rangelands, canals and 3 feeder roads in the targeted locations in Gedo, Middle and Lower Juba, and Lower Shabelle Regions on cash for work scheme to benefit 3600 vulnerable and displaced families 3. Provision of hay/fodder to the most vulnerable and drought affected 2500 pastoral families in Gedo,Lower Juba,Middle Juba and Lower shabelle Regions of Somalia, 4. Provision of emergency veterinary services (animal vaccination and treatment) to 3200 most vulnerable and displaced pastoral families in the targeted villages in Lower Juba, Middle Juba, Gedo and Lower Shabelle5. Distribution of farm inputs (seed package) to the 2800 most vulnerable and displaced farming households in the Four Target Regions6. Provision of tractor hours to 1800 farmers in Lower shabelle, Middle and Lower Juba Regions

Mitigation Measures

Risk of exclusion of female, elderly and children/children headed households that are very vulnerable and more deserving from the project as beneficiaries of conditional and unconditional cash vouchers, cash for work, farm inputs, hay distribution and veterinary services. Mitigation measure: Conducting a comprehensive mobilization and sensitization campaign with the project staff, elders, religious leaders IDPs committee member, women heads of households and regional government representatives to outreach the targeted communities and ensure their awareness of the project and identification and selection of beneficiaries in a very transparent manner through a committee consisting of the mentioned stakeholders to ensure that actual targeted beneficiaries are selected for the project. Furthermore, the selection committee should also have a representative of the targeted groups for selection (female, elderly, children/children headed households)
