Project Summary

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

This proposal is the result of CUAMM's collaboration with the Provincial Management Teams of Cabo Delgado and the District authorities of targeted districts (Metuge, Ancuabe, Chiure, Montepuez, Balama, Mueda, Ibo) to meet the exacerbation of needs for IDPs and host communities. The health system has been weakened following the shocks that the Province has suffered in recent years: cyclones in 2019/2020, consequences of the humanitarian emergencies that is causing poor living conditions for almost 700.000 IDPs and 600.000 host communities, Covid-19 epidemic still ongoing, cholera outbreaks. The following components will be implemented: - reinforce the existing health facility close to IDPs target sites;- support the delivery of life saving health services in IDPs sites: temporary clinics and integrated mobile brigades will be put in place in the highest populated/hard to reach areas in the targeted districts;- A referral pathway, especially for GBV survivors/U5 and vulnerable groups that need emergency services will be put in place. Beneficiary of the action will be 399.124 people, 31,5% will be adult male, 36 % women and 32,5% children.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The project will be aligned to Health Cluster specific objectives, namely 1. To provide accessible essential health services to targeted populations, focusing on main causes of morbidity and sexual and reproductive health;2. To provide quality care for people with physical injuries, disabilities and mental health needs;3. To prepare for, detect and respond to epidemic prone disease outbreaks. The project will work at different levels: - At Health Facility level: Cuamm will ensure essential lifesaving services to the most vulnerable groups especially providing additional health workers (especially in Health Centres close to IDP sites), medicines, medical supplies, etc;- At IDPs sites: Cuamm will support the realization of integrated mobile brigades and, where required, temporary clinics (in particular in IDPs sites) in order to expand the coverage and increment (in term of quality and quantity ) of health services, especially antenatal and postnatal care, U5 vaccinations, U5 screening. Attention will be given to GBV cases, to be promptly identified and carefully handled, considering not only physical but also other aspects. Patients will be given adequate clinical attendance and then referred to the closest health center/ Hospital/specialized centers for psychosocial and legal support and follow up when required. - Staff training and sensitization as well as advocacy for all needed supplies constant availability will be ensured. The quality of provided care will be strengthened through technical assistance and supportive supervision and monitored through an enhanced data collection and analysis, functional to informed decision making process to improve the current situation.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The project does not directly address inequalities or protection threats, however it will be implemented paying particular attention in ensuring the provided services will be accessible to everyone, without discrimination, especially to the most vulnerable and usually neglected as women, children, displaced.As highlighted in the last Complaint and Feedback Mechanism Monthly Report (CCCM Cluster Mozambique – September 2021), a total of 199 cases were filed. 11% of all cases were related to the Health sector: all the cases were complaints about the lack of health services at site and the long distance to the closest health center. Bringing services closer to the people by adopting mobile brigade strategy or sustaining facilities in the remote areas will be one of the ways to minimize the barriers certain categories of people are facing when looking for assistance. Special cases as patients victims of SGBV or patients with disabilities will be carefully handled, to ensure their dignity and privacy will be respected and to facilitate their access to adequate and comprehensive care, provided with the required expertise and competencies. The complaints feedback mechanism is designed to offer the affected population additional avenues for accessing information, registering complaints, providing feedback and enhance transparency.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

CUAMM designed its projects in close cooperation with local authorities, in order to ensure intervention endorsement and local actors’ active involvement. CUAMM staff on the field ensure a constant communication flow and open discussion on each aspect of project planning, implementation and readjustment. CUAMM has technically supported the District and Province authorities in establishing and leading a coordination and consultation mechanism involving all stakeholders operating in the area, including health staff and beneficiary population. For each HF, there is a Village Health Committee, whose members, chosen within the community living in the facility catchment area, are in charge of monitoring and reporting about HF functioning and responsiveness to people's needs, providing continuous inputs on how to improve the given services. Supervision visits at HFs and Coordination Meetings constitute regular opportunities for communities, staff and various stakeholders to interact and to contribute to Health System development. CUAMM Community Feedback and Response Mechanism aims to establish accessible channels to receive and respond to feedback and complaints from beneficiaries/patients of the programs and partners. Maintaining an open channel of communication with beneficiaries and partner’s aims at integrating opinions, suggestions and concerns about the program implementation, improving transparency and ensuring a response to any case of sexual exploitation and abuse.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The attempt of bringing services where people live is functional to put them in conditions to access what they need, limiting the risks connected to the movements in such an unstable environment and overcoming access constraints related to women domestic cores and to family/social power dynamics, disability or mental issues. Attention will be done to GBV services at health facilities and in temporary clinics: the intention of making health services an entry point for GBV victims to access adequate assistance and the same will be for people with disabilities. CCM already did an assessment of disability persons in some of the IDPs sites. Integrated mobile brigades and temporary clinics will be done in IDPs sites in places easily reachable also from people with disabilities and as closest possible to beneficiaries. CUAMM is already training health staff on these topics and IEC materials. A protection map will be defined through the feedback collected at community level and making reference to other partners/institution assessment so that identifying vulnerable groups and situations that might endanger the goals of the interventions and maintaining a focused approach, avoiding wasting resources and addressing the priority sites and issues.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

TThe proposed programme will limit its impact on the environment by ensuring appropriate sensitization on healthcare workers as well as community health workers on safe disposal of consumables, especially PPEs.In addition the project will focus on sensitizing host and displaced communities on environmental and personal hygiene, sanitation, disease outbreaks prevention/control at the health facility and the community levelDuring the project implementation CUAMM will encourage: - integration of activities, implying movements by vehicles, to limit the number of cars and motorbikes going to the same destination and, consequently, the consumption of fuel and petrol; - careful monitoring of fuel storage and management, to avoid leakage and waste and hazard for the surrounding environment; - discussion with local health authorities on how safely handling the products resulting from the drugs store dejunking regular exercise to avoid all wastage to be left in the field, contaminating water sources or cattle feeding spaces.
