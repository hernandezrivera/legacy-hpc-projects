Project Summary

Bonyan Organization designed its SNFI intervention based on the analysis of humanitarian needs (MSNA), and need severity for SNFI assistance, as well as to be in line with SOs of HRP and SNFI Cluster.
The project will address SNFI needs for totally 31,500 INDVs (15,435M/ 16,065F – 2700%PwD) of the most vulnerable people of IDPs, HC and RTRNs in NSAG controlled areas in Aleppo and Idleb.
First component will contribute to provide lifesaving and life-sustaining shelter and NFI support targeting 42,600 INDVs. BO will respond to the emergency or high severity situations; especially in hard-to-reach areas, last resorts, and informal settlements. BO will conduct assessment survey in order to collect SNFI needs data (with keeping in mind the speciﬁc needs of different age and gender groups), in addition to collect any other necessary data (i.e., market assessment or security assessment).
As for the response to NFI needs, BO will conduct the following:
• Provision of core NFIs for 7500 INDVs including mattresses, blankets, cooking utensils, solar light, plastic sheets, and kitchen kits
• Provision of seasonal and supplementary NFIs for 10000 INDVs including winter clothing, shoes, and heating fuel
Likewise, BO will respond to shelter needs through:
• Repair of tents for 1500 INDVs
• Installation of new/replacement of existing tents for 2000 INDVs
• Distribution/ installation of emergency shelter kits/materials for 2000 INDVs
• Implementation of HH level improvements for 1000 INDVs
• Implementation of site level improvements/infrastructure for 5000 INDVs
Second component will contribute to reinforce an enabling protection environment and cohesion of communities by improving housing and related community/public infrastructure targeting 2500 INDVs. To achieve this, BO will conduct the following:
• Rehabilitation of damaged or unfinished housing for 2500 INDVs including installing partitions, installation of doors and windows, wiring, plumbing works, insulation and painting
Throughout the project, BO will select the target communities based on scoring and prioritizing system in coordination with SNFI Cluster, then will engage the selected community in all phases of the project. Also, BO will ensure that the support will be provided to the most vulnerable people through transparent process based on clear vulnerability criteria (HLP veriﬁcation will be included). Principles of Do No Harm and AAP as well as humanitarian standards are incorporated in the project design and will be ensured during the project timeline. Also, MEAL team of BO will conduct ongoing monitoring of project activities to verify achievement of indicators and to correct any deviation.
