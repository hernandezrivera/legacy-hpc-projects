Project Summary

The Syrian crisis has left many with little or no access to education, especially in Deir EzZor Governorate. The absence of quality education led many children to leave school at
an early age. Thus, these children might be affected by child labor, early or forced
marriage, domestic violence, and abuse. These negative coping mechanisms are widely
reported across Deir Ez-Zor Governorate both in local communities and IDP informal
settlements, representing protection risks for children, and a serious barrier to access
education.
The project aims to provide ALP (Accelerated Learning Program) curriculum to out-of-school children and pupils that are studying at a level behind their age group. The project
will increase the chances of 800 vulnerable children, in two schools within the Sur subdistrict, to be re-admitted to the educational system and accordingly prevent protection
risks related to education dropout. According to Education Sector Findings HNO22, 25% of school-aged children are less likely to attend any learning form.
The project will work towards meeting three objectives of The Education Cluster. The
rehabilitation of the two schools, procurement of teaching materials, and the provision of
ALP curriculum to out-of-school children as well as students that are behind their age
groups will ensure better access to Education. Furthermore, the teacher’s training and
inclusion of coaches in the two schools will ensure the provision of education quality for
the targeted children.
After completing the ALP cycles, children will be re-admitted to the correct level of
education and will be able to continue their courses in formal schools.
Monitoring :
As a part of the M&E plan AFH MEAL team will conduct a baseline, endline evaluation, and quality of participation with the targeted students in the selected locations/ schools. The baseline will be followed with couple of light monitoring surveys based on the project needs. AFH staff will conduct regular field visits and exit interviews to observe anticipated and unanticipated changes and communicate with community members.AFH MEAL team will track all project documents to be used in measuring the project indicators and reporting in the database platform. Regular meetings will be conducted with partners and other stakeholders on a quarterly basis to jointly analyze and reflect on project monitoring data in order to produce action items with assigned responsibilities AFH MEAL team will conduct a follow-up evaluation with the students after the end of each cycle and reflect the findings with partners and other stakeholders to generate appropriate recommendations and inform learning agency.

3 - Needs

The Syrian crisis has left many with little or no access to education. The situation is worsein the Deir Ezzor governorate due to the full or partial destruction of educational facilities,lack of qualified teachers and weak capacities, and the very limited resources of the localEducation committee. Just after the security situation in Deir-Ez-Zor relatively improved,the Education Committee focused its efforts on rehabilitating schools and re-admittingchildren into the educational system without taking into consideration of their academiclevels or age groups. Furthermore, the education quality provided was very mediocre dueto the lack of qualified teachers in the area. These factors have resulted in many childrendropping out of school as a result of poor educational quality or the fact that they arestudying with much younger age groups which often creates frustration among thesechildren. Consequently, children being out of school are more exposed to negative copingmechanisms and protection risks such as child labor, early or forced marriage, neglect,and abuse. AFH is proposing this intervention out of its belief that the right place forchildren in school and that no child must be deprived of this universal right regardless ofthe causes. According to Education Sector Findings HNO22, 25% of school-aged children are less likely to attend any learning form

4 - Implementation of activities

1_Rehabilitation of two schools in Deir Ezzor_ NESAFH will start by conducting rehabilitation activities in the targeted facilities to make thelearning space safer and more appealing to children. Furthermore, 2_ provides an accelerated learning program for 800 children who are attending school but lack the right academic level for their age group and therefore fall behind compared to their peers. We also target out-of-schoolchildren with the aim to provide them with the necessary support to be readmitted to the school where they normally belong as for the youth interventionAFH will provide equipment, stationeries, and teaching materials that are required for the execution of the education activities. 3_increase capacity building the number of trained teachers in the area.The organization will also recruit teachers and ensure that allof them receive essential training at the beginning of the project in accordance with INEEinternational standards. Therefore, the registration of students will begin with thedevelopment of defined criteria for selecting the most vulnerable children. Following that,all registered students will have diagnostic tests to determine their level. After that, AFHintends to provide two cycles of educational activities in 2023, each lasting four months.Finally, after the conclusion of each cycle, AFH will follow all graduates to ensure theyresume their formal education.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

1-Learning facilities might be attacked by parties of conflict or by communities. Attackers may arrest teachers or students, intimidate school staff/students, or military use of the school, arson, theft, or looting. Formation of a local committee within each school composed of community leaders in the area in addition to the school's neighbors, the school principal, and some teachers. Their mission is to protect the school's property from theft and fire, in addition to appointing guards for the school from the same area. 2- Acts of sexual harassment conducted by boys on young girls in latrines or unsafe places. AFH will ensure to rehabilitate sanitation facilities in a way that provides privacy and gender segregation to protect girls from any SG or harassment risk. 3- Services not matching the needs of people with disabilities. in this regard, AFH will make sure that all the facilities are PWD-friendly including classrooms and sanitation facilities. 4- Learning facilities or their surroundings are structurally unsafe or do not provide sufficient protection from the elements (rain, snow, cold, dust, etc.) Unsafe learning spaces and insufficient/non-existent WASH facilities contribute to children not attending learning facilities. In summer, provide ventilation in all classrooms. In winter, safe heating is ensured in all classrooms. In addition to ensuring the cleanliness of sanitary facilities and the availability of water therein.

9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

The beneficiaries might reject the education services and have fewer children to benefit from our program. The mitigation plan is to reach out to target children and children who are out of school by holding meetings with students' parents and community leaders, introducing the project, explaining the advantages, and encouraging them to join the schools and the ALP program. •Security risks, the contingency plan in case of a minor security threat includes the temporary suspension of the program and the temporary closure of the school until the security situation goes back to normal, during which an emergency plan will be developed based on the delivery of distance education through WhatsApp groups. •Withdrawal of American troops in the SDF-controlled area, a contingency plan in case of an imminent advance of GoS in the areas where the program operates involves, closing the AFH once, and facilitating the evacuation of the staff that wants to leave the area, despite the high impact of the risk the accordance of the event is low.
