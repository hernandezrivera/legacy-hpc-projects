Project Summary

According to AYUUB Multi-Sectoral Assessment report on June 2019, Marka and Afgooye were among the most areas hosted highest IPDs movement, mainly escaped from ongoing security operation and airstrikes in Bariire and Aw dheegle areas of Lower Shabelle and to the protectorate IDPs of 2016/17 evicted by AS around Marka, suffering for multi response, shelter response is one of the most needed component for the affected populations, an estimated of 9000 IDPs fled to Marka and 10000 IDPs to Afgooye corridor IDP camps lacking the basic of humanitarian assistance.
There for The Project deliveries Shelter component of ESKs and NFIs to the new arrival IDPs affected by the man made and natural disasters mentioned above and resulted in the high IDPs influx to safe areas in Merka and Afgooye corridor for seeking better protection and shelter.
The project targets 2500hhs (15000 people) in Marka and Afgooye districts of Lower Shabelle region, The response will majorly focus on ensuring that persons affected by conflict have protection from the weather and privacy through
a) Provision of emergency shelter and NFIs to 2500 HH (equivalent 15000 individuals) by getting Shelter Cluster Stander NFI kit comprising 1 Plastic Sheet, 3Blanket, 2 Sleeping mat, , 1Kitchen Set, 1 Mosquito nets, 1 Solar Lumps, 2 Cherrycan
b) Provide cash assistance Support to 2500 HH (equivalent 15000 individuals) to construct their ESKs
c) Community Response and Feedback Mechanism (CRFM) 90% of the beneficiaries will have access.
d. Two Post distribution Monitoring activities in the two districts will be conducted to evaluate how the project met with the beneficiary’s expectation and data recording 90% of the beneficiaries Will be targeted.
For the beneficiaries’ selection and registration,AYUUB will hold consultations with community members to jointly determine the targeting criteria, identify the most vulnerable and register beneficiaries for the NFIs and Shelter. AYUUB will prioritize the Most vulnerable people in the Targeted area . AYUUB will also target host communities to ensure that other vulnerable people within the target locations are also assisted based on needs and also avoid conflict between the host community and displaced people, the Host Communties are among in the IDP Camps.
We Consider Protection in our Shelter activities, during beneficiary identification we select beneficiaries with Specific needs, Marginalized communities, Female headed house hold and families with orphans, also during our community training we also ensure representation from all the above-mentioned groups.

Mitigation Measures

1b. Protection of civilians: The area where the project implementation will be either forced into temporary displacement because of land dispute or conflict, Since all the Shelter interventions will be channeled through cluster approach and involvement of relevant local authorities, thus the impact of armed conflict and violence will be mitigated through proper coordination, advocacy at national and international level, media and referral mechanisms. 2a. Exclusion and marginalization: The Federal Government of Somalia has committed to women’s empowerment and eradicating violence against women, recognizing that without institutionalizing the equal rights of women, the country will never be able to stabilize and develop in a sustainable way. Somali women and girls continue to bear an unequal burden of the hardships occasioned by poverty, conflict and clan-based culture, which promotes strict male hierarchy and authority. This is further exacerbated by cultural limitations on the role and status of women in Somali society. Women with disabilities are often marginalized on account of their disability and their gender. They may be more vulnerable to poverty and social exclusion, and often have limited social, political and economic opportunities and lack of access to basic services. Women with disabilities may also be at greater risk of sexual and physical violence and abuse. Girls with disabilities often experience discrimination, for example in education and family life. This project will ensure engagement of people, especially women with disabilities, and the elderly at the co-management level and will ensure that programs and interventions do not generate negative side-effects. The beneficiary selection will be conducted in a participatory approach with the community leaders and the local authorities that will identify the most vulnerable people. Women and Child headed households will be given special priority. 2b. Exclusion and marginalization: Targeted assistance will be provided to the most vulnerable groups including women, girls and boy. Beneficiaries will be selected through a community-based approach, which will identify the most vulnerable based on certain criteria as female or child-headed households, elderly, disabled, large families and the chronically ill. The beneficiary selection will be conducted in a participatory approach with the community leaders and the local authorities that will identify the most vulnerable people. Women, girls, boys and Child headed households will be given special priority. 4b Protection mainstreaming: Beneficiaries risk exposure to violence during registration/distribution: The project will include Women, girls and boys in consultations and decision-making processes for the project and during beneficiary identification and registration will be selected beneficiaries with Specific needs, Marginalized communities, Female headed house hold and families with orphans, and the people in need (can’t afford) but do not fall in these categories will be prioritized by the setting up an other criteria and will be discussed with local Authority and community representatives. also during our community training we also ensure representation from all the above-mentioned groups

Please provide further explanation

The areas being targeted in Merka and Afgoye districts, Lower Shabelle have been secured which gives an assurance that the activities will be implemented without any interruption. Insecurity in general is inherent in Lower Shabelle Region, in overcoming the chances which might lead to insecurity we have security access information from the local administrations and own security assessment out of outstanding presence in Lower Shabelle for many years. We have unrestricted access to any of the locations including where our projects are being targeted. AYUUB has it’s own policy of risk Management and will apply it for the risk Management, a risk mitigation plan is designed by AYUUB to eliminate or minimize the impact of the risk events.AYUUB is also developed risk checklists based on experience from past projects. These checklists can be helpful to the project manager and project team in identifying both specific risks on the checklist and expanding the thinking of the team. The past experience of the project team, project experience within the Organization, and experts in the Organization can be valuable resources for identifying potential risk on a project.After the potential risks have been identified, the project team then evaluates the risk based on the probability that the risk event will occur and the potential loss associated with the event. After the risk has been identified and evaluated, the project team develops a risk mitigation plan, which is a plan to reduce the impact of an unexpected event. The project team mitigates risks in the ways of Risk avoidance, risk sharing, risk reduction, risk transfer and each of these mitigation techniques can be an effective tool in reducing individual risks and the risk profile of the projectRegarding risks related to movement to these IDP Camps in the two districts , AYUUB staff has so far mitigated the risk by discussing AYUUB staff purpose in advance with community leaders and putting into place a security protocol consisting in monitoring and evaluating the security situation before movement and check in calls to the Field Coordinator who is the person in charge of the security of the staff. Risk of fraud and corruption will be minimizing by the delegation of duties and sharing of responsibilities, as well as monitoring visits and quality control of activities implemented by the Head of program. AYUUB HR Manager will conduct a training on AYUUB Policy on Code of Conduct.Beneficiaries will be provided with AYUUB hotline number to refer complaints. A strong feedback mechanism will enhance the capacity of AYUUB Staff to prevent challenges human errors thus improving risk management in relation of the delivery of services.Review of the project achievements (including lessons learned and good practices) will be promoted periodically in order to identify risks and avoid and/or mitigate them in future actions
