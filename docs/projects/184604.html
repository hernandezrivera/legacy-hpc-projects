Project Summary

In order to prevent the recurrence of famine risk, it is critical that WARDI will not only meet people’s immediate humanitarian needs but also scale up its activities aimed at reducing risks and vulnerabilities and building resilience to future shocks and stresses. To this end, WARDI would like to upscale its WASH activities in Banadir region, Hirshabelle, and Southwest states of Somalia, where we have in good presence and implementing health, nutrition, WASH and protection projects to strengthen coherence and complementarity between humanitarian and development efforts, and ultimately aim to reduce needs and vulnerabilities to a point where humanitarian action is no longer required. Thus, Under this project, WARDI proposes to improve access to emergency and sustainable safe water, sanitation facilities and services and promote good hygiene practices in an effort to improve the health status of 28,000 crisis and disaster affected communities and IDPs including people with disabilities and minorities. Lack of access to safe and adequate water and sanitation facilities coupled with poor hygiene knowledge and practices put these communities at risk for waterborne diseases and loss of life particularly women and children. Key activities that WARDI would like to implement in the target locations include:
WATER: (1) Provision of emergency safe water to new arrived 2,000 IDPs or IDPs affected by floods through voucher for three months; (2) provision of sustainable safe water through rehabilitation of 2 boreholes equipped with hybrid system, rehabilitation of 26 shallow wells with hand pumps; construction of 6 water kiosks supplied water through piping system to schools and health posts; chlorination of the rehabilitated 28 water sources; capacity building training to 28 WASH committees and 56 well operators shared equally men and women on operation and maintenance. As cross cutting, WARDI will conduct DRR and conflict resolution mechanisms workshop to 100 community members.
SANITATION: To improve the sanitation of the IDP camps and institutions, WARDI will construct 4 twin block and gender segregation institutional latrines in 2 school and 2 health facilities and 200 gender separation IDP latrines with lockable doors and hand washing facilities, special consideration will be given girls/ women privacy and people with disabilities' special needs.
HYGIENE: To reach more than 28,000 crisis affected communities and IDPs, WARDI will train 20 CHPs to carry out comprehensive hygiene promotion and Covid-19 awareness campaigns to educate the community better hygiene promotion practices including hand washing with soap practices through visiting house-to-house, schools, feeding/nutrition centers and health centers; in this project, WARDI will provide 1,500 HP kits to 1500 households and IDPs who are at risk of AWD/cholera and malnutrition diseases.
The proposed response took into consideration the impact of the crisis on women, people with disabilities, minorities and children in terms of access to WASH services; it proposed specific actions to prevent risk of gender based violence and hence will contribute to greater gender equality. WARDI top management will monitor the quality and quantity of the WASH intervention during the project implementation and its completion. In this proposal, WARDI will organize series meetings with the community stakeholders including women, people with disabilities, minority groups and most vulnerable people in the community. The aim of the meetings are to give the community the picture of the project (objective, outputs, indicators and targets). The community will select the locations of the project within the target districts. WARDI will also inform the participants the complaint mechanism and hotline telephone for their satisfaction/non satisfaction of the services delivered for Accountability and transparence purpose (AAP).

1. - Projects activities

Project objective: To reduce people’s WASH needs, risks and vulnerabilities and increase in their resilience. The project is designed to strengthen coherence and complementarity between humanitarian and development efforts. I. Water: (1) Provide emergence safe water to 2,000 new arrived IDPs affected by crisis, each HHs composing 6 people will receive 45 l/day as SPHERE standard for 90 days through voucher, benefiting 12,000 (1800 men, 3000 women, 3600 boys, 3600 girls, including people with disabilities and minorities. (3) 26 flood proof shallow wells with hand pumps, benefiting 13,000 people (1950 men, 3250 women, 3900 boys, 3900 girls including people with disabilities and minorities). (4) Construct 6 water kiosk supplied water through piping system to 6 IDP camps, benefiting 3,000 people (450 men, 750 women, 900 boys, 900 girls including people with disabilities, and minorities. (5) conduct training to 28 WASH committees from 56 water sources; each committee consists of 7 members (4 men and 3 women) on better water source management, HHWT use, and maintenance of water facilities. 84 well operators will also be trained on O&M and collection of water revenues for the sustainability of the water source. II. Sanitation: (1) Construct 4 gender sensitive twin block latrines with lockable doors and hand washing basins to 2 schools and 2 health facilities, benefiting around 3000 school attendants and health facilities visitors. The action should support the easy use of children and disabilities. (2) Construct 200 flood proof and gender sensitive IDP latrines with lockable doors and hand washing facilities to increase women/girls’ dignity and avoid the risk of GBV against women and girls. 15% of the latrines are people with disabilities and child friendly latrines. III. Hygiene: (1) Train 20 CHPs (15 men and 15 women) on best hygiene and sanitation practices, hand washing with soap. (2) Conduct comprehensive HP and mobilisation on AWD/Cholera and Covid-19 mitigation measures to 28,000 (4200 men, 7000 women, 8400 girls and 8400 boys). 2 KAP surveys will be done at the inception and the completion of the project. (3) Distribute 1500 HP kits to 1500 HHs/IDPs affected/risk AWD or malnutrition diseases. Each HP kit contains (200 aqua tabs, 5 soaps of 800mg, 1 Jerry can, 1 bucket and 3 female sanitary materials).
