Project Summary

COHESODEC seeks through this project implementation to ensure that conflict affected children in the North-West and South-West Regions are absolutely protected from various forms of abuses often perpetrated against children. The project will be targeting a total of 13,585 Children who shall be prioritized during this implementation. The project will be implemented in vulnerable target communities within the following sub-divisions: Bafut, Bamenda 2, Bamenda 3, Bali, Tubah, Batibo, Mbengwi, Konye, Kumba 2, Kumba 3 and Mbonge of the NW/SW regions.
During the implementation of this project, COHESODEC will be providing mental health and psychosocial support services to children and their caregivers involving 90 functional child friendly spaces! Also, COHESODEC will be identifying 200 unaccompanied and separated children and ensuring adequate alternative care arrangements and reunification of 45 of them with their respective families while also providing mental health and psychosocial support services to 4,500 children and their caregivers ensuring they have accessing to mental health and psychosocial support.
Moreover, COHESODEC will be providing appropriate case management service to 800 children at risk of violence, abuse and exploitation during humanitarian situations with child sensitive case management (multisectoral services such as psychosocial support, education and family economic empowerment) while also identifying and implementing measures to prevent the risks of violence, abuse and exploitation of children during humanitarian situations involving 900 parents and caregivers through key measures of sensitization of community leaders to prevent family separations in humanitarian context.
In addition, 1,800 children and caregivers will be informed about risks related to explosive remnants of war while 800 caregivers (women, men) will also be sensitized on positive parenting. COHESODEC will also be providing interventions for the prevention and mitigation of risks and response to GBV affecting children involving 1,000 children accessing GBV risk mitigation, prevention or response interventions.
Finally, COHESODEC during the implementation of this project will be providing support to issue birth certificates to children affected by humanitarian situation-affected children as well as ensure that 300 children affected by the humanitarian situation receive support through the establishment of their birth certificates while ensuring that at least 2,500 persons sensitized on birth registration including building the capacities of 90 Community-Based Child Protection Mechanisms.

1 - Besoins / Needs

During COHESODEC’ response in the implementation of this project, we shall need financial support to remunerate Facilitators of the 90 functional child friendly spaces. Also, the project will need the services of mental health and psychosocial support to ensure the provision of mental health and psychosocial support services to at least 4,500 children and their caregivers.Moreover, COHESODEC will need the services of CP Case Workers who will ensure that 200 unaccompanied and separated children gain access into family-based care and appropriate alternative services while making sure that at least 45 of these of unaccompanied and separated children are reunited or in contact with their families.COHESODEC will also be needing the services of CP Case Workers to effectively respond to 800 children facing risks of violence, abuse and exploitation during the ongoing humanitarian situation, and will ensure 900 parents, caregivers and community leaders are sensitized on key measures to prevent family separations following the humanitarian situation. The project will also ensure that 1,800 children and caregivers are effectively informed about the risks related to explosive remnants of war as well as 800 caregivers (women, men) sensitized on positive parenting. Finally, during the implementation of this project, COHESODEC will also be needing the services of GBV specialists to ensure that at least 1,000 children are accessing GBV risk mitigation, prevention and response interventions. Also, COHESODEC will need the cooperation of the high courts and municipal councils of Mezam, Momo and Meme divisions to ensure that 300 birth certificates are established to children affected by the humanitarian situation as well as sensitize 2,500 persons on birth registration while also needing the services of CP Case Workers to ensure that 90 community-based child protection mechanisms are effectively functional.
