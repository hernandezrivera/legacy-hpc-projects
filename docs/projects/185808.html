Project Summary

Northeast Nigeria is considered to be the least developing region in Nigeria as a result of indebt poverty with millions of children out of school. The recent Boko haram crisis have brought essential services to standstill. markets are attacked and business have been brought to standstill with no modalities of continuity due to the crisis in the region, after decade of struggle by business owners in the region and the high risk involve in doing business, it has become more worsen with the development of Covid-19 pandemic which made the whole situation worse with thousands of businesses short down due to low knowledge of business owners on digitals skills and online presence.
Borno State have been educationally behind in terms of education and technology compared to the southern states in the country. The project will be an opportunity to create awareness and advance the use of digital skills and digitalization of businesses by individuals and SMEs to grow their markets and sells across the country to improve and increase household income.
The project will also focus on social cohesion to ensure lasting peace in the areas of return, by increasing community skills on conflict sensitivity and the use of dialogue to address grievances.

1 - Needs

The humanitarian crisis in Nigeria’s north-eastern states of Borno, Adamawa and Yobe (BAY) have been persisting for over a decade now the continuing conflict severely affect millions of people who will remainvulnerable to displacement (new or continued), economic hardship, unemployment, and hunger. the crisis which started as local conflict eleven years ago has turned into a regional armed conflict with civilians, mostly women and children bearing the brunt of brutal clashes and attacks. 1.9 million people in the Lake Chad Basin areas of the neighboring countries of Cameroon, Chad and Niger are in need humanitarian aid. Nigeria remains the worst affected by the conflict with 8.7 million people in need of assistance in the BAY states in 2021, which has become one of the largest and most severe humanitarian crises in theworld today. MSNA classifications indicate that more than 2.2 million people face extreme degree of needs and more than 4 million have severe needs in the BAY states. In the LGAs that have extreme needs, 83 per cent of the populations require some kind of humanitarian assistance, as do 62 per cent of the populations in LGAs with severe needs. The people in need are divided into three groups—IDPs, returnees, and host communities—all with different types of needs according to severity levels. Based on the inter-sectoral severity analysis, IDPs are more vulnerable than returnees and host communities. Overall, 65 per cent ofthe IDP households have reported at least one kind of vulnerability having a female-headed household, a family member with mental or physical disability, pregnant girl or woman in the household; a child separated from them; or being or having a married child. It is evident that The current Humanitarian crisis exposed millions of people from the crisis region to hunger, joblessness, little or no access to decent means of livelihood, with majority of the people that have been impacted by the crisis women and girls. The covid -19 pandemic have further increased business and household vulnerabilities their by increasing business collapse, unemployment and difficulty in accessing daily jobs for people to earn even daily income to carter for family needs. the pandemic have further resulted to low access of humanitarian to the vulnerable population their by reducing livelihood interventions that increase household income. The current returning of IDPs to their home town by the Borno state government is a call to action to action as thousand of returnees might have possible land dispute and other conflict dynamics that may arise during return. As the conflict is still very difficult to predict, more and more populations flee conflict, disaster, and insecurity, refugees and internally displaced people (IDPs) are forgoing traditional camp-based resettlement settings in lieu of greater perceived economic and social opportunities in cities andurban areas.

2 - Activities

Establish Peace Clubs through Training of Trainers: Training of Trainers will be conducted for 15 community Peace Club 5 in each LGA Targeting 3 LGA. in the area of return on Peace building and Resilience Peace Club will be represented and monitored by the following people. youths, women, and supervised by key community stakeholders. The peace club members and leaders will advocates for institutionalizing peace in the state and LGA of implementation. 5 day training will be delivered to selected peace clubs leaders and community peace clubs ambassadors. Social Cohesion Activities in Peace Clubs:Following the training of Peace Club Leaders, (peace ambassadors, community leaders and influential community members), FRAD will support these leaders to establish peace clubs in their community of Return, and consider, together with boys and girls in the peace clubs which activities can be implemented to promote an agenda of peace both within and between peace clubs and the wider community. Community Strengthening InitiativeCommunity consultations will be carried out in a participatory manner with men, women, boys and girls by FRAD by Community Mobilizing Officers. These consultations will identify key needs that can be addressed through 15 actionable community strengthening initiatives that FRAD will support by providing small grants. Within these consultations, community ownership will be identified and promoted to ensure the initiatives chosen are related to the deeper recovery and resilience in communities identified by participants. Participatory Community Theatre20 Participatory community Theatre 10 performances will be delivered in this project, This will better enable target communities to understand the impact violence and involvement in armed groups has on their community progress. These performances will trigger discussion around peace building, entrepreneurship and business creation awareness Establishment of community Owned Empowerment Centers - Following the inception meetings, FRAD will hold meetings with key community stakeholders. FRAD would work with key stakeholders to identify a location within the community (i.e. an existing town hall, community center, or central location) to establish the empowerment center. Life and Financial Skills Trainings FRAD Foundation will support 12 life and financial skills trainings with selected beneficiaries for empowerment aspect of the project. The training would increase participants knowledge and skills in literacy and numeracy, financial management, micro-enterprise development, agro-business skills, simple financial record keeping, management and leadership, group dynamic and leadership according to the needs of the women involved. Artisan/ apprenticeship placement and skills LinkagesThis aspect will link beneficiaries to artisan to learn skills in their area of interest for a certain duration of time, after the Artisans certifies the participants successful Start up Kits will be given to the beneficiaries to start business. Capacity Building for CSOs, government partners and other identified stakeholders.FRAD Shall conduct training Targeting 200 participants to build their capacity on digital skills to improve their quality of work. Training on Entrepreneurship and Digital skillsThis activity will be Targeting youths women and girls, the activity will train youths on entrepreneurship using various components like business development, design of business plan, digitals and all other packages their will help and support them start a business. this activity will be a continues one it is a structured activity with different levels Community Agro cash for work.these activity will be implemented in areas of return, where youths are engage in agro cash for work activities in agricultural businesses.

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

To ensure Protection mainstreaming, the FRAD Foundation will use a consultative approach to implement the entire project, Key assessment will be conducted to identify if women structures exist in community and establish women action group in communities where such those not exist. other key individual to mainstream are also youth, boys and girls. All intervention of the project will ensure that community are aware of targeting criteria for every aspect of the project, and banners carrying information in local language for the project will be placed at strategic locations in the community where many population will see and be aware of the project modalities.All community stakeholders, FRAD staff, volunteers and contractions and all other persons that will work either directly or indirectly under these project will be trained on do no harm and PSEA and also sign FRAD foundation PSEA policy. A Robust mechanism will be deployed for complaints and feedback ranging from help desk, suggestion box, toll free lines and Radio programming, this is to give community and project participants an avenue to give and say their complaints and have it addressed.
