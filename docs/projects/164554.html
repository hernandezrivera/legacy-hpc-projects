Project Summary

The Project aims to continue supporting pre-existing Youth and community center in
Northern countryside of Aleppo, the center provides several activities in the mean of
empowering members of the community especially youth and increase access to
moderate values with enhancing their resilience and increase the positive engagement
and involvement within their community.
SOH carefully conducting activities in which helps reaching for the previous principals,
the main activities of this project is :
- Provide 16 Life skills trainings (Langues, administrative skills, Managment skills,
leadership and computer) for 400 youth male and female (16-24) years old.
- establish two networks/groups from youth (male and female) from the age of (18-25)
and provide them with civic education trainings.
- fund and support to two initiatives developed and conducted by the two
networks/groups .
- provide 12 cultural lectures/ seminars at the center .

4 - Needs

Depending on the needs assessment conducted by SOH, NGOs and the UN agencies, allof the outcomes and recommendations stat that focus should be shifted towards youth.Space of Hope have been working in Education for the past 4 years, trying to help youthto continue their study but unfortunately the absence of universities for some areascaused many adults to stop going to schools. The situation led us to think aboutvocational training for those who need to be offered an income generating solutions.Vocational Training workshops will be providing essential knowledge for Adults toencourage them to build their future which will keep them away of military groups (forboys), and of early marriage (for girls). Aside of the vocational training we will providecultural & social learning, which would help our communities to think properly about thenecessity of being responsible towards their country, and how important is saving peoplelives while you can but not knowing how to make it. If we check all educational activitiesduring the conflict, we can notice the weakness and shortness in developing civiliansspecially those who are in low level of Income. No opportunities nor realistic solutionshave been used to save those people’s future. We believe that our civic educationalefforts can improve others opportunities as well (others=similar initiatives), and will givebetter understanding for the uneducated people to become responsible & collaborated,and the most important thing: people will learn how to pick their choices of building theirfuture away of extremism.

5 - Implementation of activities

depending on the experience gained by implementing similar kind of projects, and takinginto consideration the pre-existing canter, the first step will be conducting needs assessment in coordination with the local authorities to determine the specific vocational and soft skills needed for the youth in the area of implementation. preparation and development of activities with specific work plan will be the phase two of the project, beneficiaries will be chosen and have a role in these phases, the center will be basically rehabilitated and fully equipped with the needed materials . a schedule/program for the center will be set and be followed closely by the staff and the activities will start accordingly .monthly reports will be prepared and submitted with closely work of the M&E staff. the activities that will be provided is :-phase one :conducting needs assessment in coordination with the local authorities to determine the specific vocational needed for the youth in the area of implementation (one month) - Provide vocational training (based on the outcome from the first step ) for 562 youth male and female (16-24) years old.-provide advanced business skills and life skill for 54 selected participant from the 562 youth who has vocational training (the participants will meet the established the criteria for this advanced training)and provided with vocational kits - establish two networks/groups from youth (male and female) from the age of (18-25) and provide them with civic education and leadership skills training. each group made of 9 participant - fund and support initiatives developed and conducted by the two networks/groups to increase the cohesion between the IDP's and hosts communities . each group two initiatives
