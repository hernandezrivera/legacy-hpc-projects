Project Summary

In the Humanitarian Response Plan (HRP) project. TAS will have commitment to education and protection in crisis-affected communities in Northwest Syria (NWS).
The project addresses the urgent need for quality education in crisis-affected areas. TAS's Humanitarian Response Plan (HRP) in Northwest Syria focuses on uplifting vulnerable students and establishing secure learning environments through innovative methodologies through catch-up programs to accelerate learning for those who have fallen behind, and literacy and numeracy initiatives to improve foundational skills.
The project incorporates a thorough protection risk analysis and addressing risks, one of the risks such as the potential lack of qualified PSS providers in the targeted location, with a medium likelihood and high impact, mitigation strategies are essential. To mitigate this risk, teachers and education personnel will receive comprehensive training to define the aspects and activities related to providing PSS sessions.
The security situation or authorities/groups in control of project areas, with a medium likelihood and high impact. Mitigation strategies include closely monitoring the security situation in coordination with partners, civil authorities, and relevant stakeholders, ensuring staff members are trained in safety and security.
TAS integrates CP activities in targeted locations, benefiting individuals, including people with disabilities. Activities focus on case management for at-risk children, support within schools, and PSS programs in Child-Friendly Spaces.
TAS integrates a robust Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) strategy into the project, emphasizing the importance of safeguarding vulnerable populations ensuring smooth implementation and effective outcomes.
Back-to-Learning campaign is one of these essential activities that can play a pivotal role in re-engaging students’ post-disruption, emphasizing education's importance and restoring educational infrastructure. Simultaneously, TAS provide school disinfection mitigates health risks, crucial during pandemics like Covid-19, promoting safe learning environments, Besides, TAS is providing rehabilitation in conjunction with infrastructure improvements, the project includes provisions for providing schools with essential furniture, including desks, chairs, whiteboards, and other instructional materials, and in response to security concerns and threats to the safety of students and staff, TAS is committed to providing schools with safety and security equipment as well.
For promoting inclusivity, TAS Establish gender-sensitive and disability-sensitive Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene (WASH) facilities in addition to solar power installations that can improve school infrastructure sustainability. TAS is also supporting operational costs covering maintenance, including cleaning materials, stationery for school personnel, and fuel for heating. Alongside providing supplies and teaching materials.
Central to the project is the investment in teachers and education personnel, enhancing their capacity to shape student learning experiences positively. TAS prioritizes gender equality, social inclusion, and the psychosocial well-being of students, alongside fostering community ownership through school-based accountability mechanisms. Furthermore, TAS ensures student safety, particularly amid pandemics, and promotes gender-sensitive and disability-sensitive approaches to education, fostering social inclusion and equity within the sector.
Additionally, the project includes provisions for providing teachers and education personnel with monetary and non-monetary incentives.
As part of the project, TAS will provide children with recreational materials, to encourage active engagement and enjoyment promoting recreational activities.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The project emphasizes: The modality of education intervention for both formal and non-formal education programs within TAS encompasses a range of initiatives aimed at providing quality education opportunities in conflict-affected areas like Afrin, Al Bab, and Idlib. Specifically, TAS includes Manahel schools in this proposal.Formal education programs will focus on supporting existing schools, ensuring access to curriculum, trained teachers, and safe learning environments. This includes efforts to rehabilitate damaged school infrastructure, provide essential educational materials, and train teachers to deliver quality instruction.Non-formal education programs will complement formal schooling by offering flexible learning opportunities for children who have missed out on education due to conflict-related disruptions. These programs will include remedial classes to address learning gaps, catch-up programs to accelerate learning for those who have fallen behind, and literacy and numeracy initiatives to improve foundational skills. Rehabilitating educational facilities to enhance the learning environment and promote inclusivity.TAS will ensure inclusivity for children with disabilities by providing accessible facilities, specialized teacher training, adaptive learning materials.Over 60% of the project funds will be dedicated to teacher stipends, ensuring sustainability, and recognizing their essential role in delivering quality education. Teacher Support and Incentives: Providing teachers with monetary and non-monetary incentives and professional development opportunities to enhance educational quality. Non formal education and remedial classes are offered during summer for all children and youth as inclusive summer clubs, ensuring access for all students to bridge educational gaps throughout the academic year. This approach promotes continuity of learning, addresses knowledge deficits, and fosters holistic development, ultimately enhancing academic outcomes and student success.Commitment to Principles: The project remains committed to upholding humanitarian principles, promoting human dignity, and advocating for the rights of crisis-affected populations.Overall, the project represents a holistic and multifaceted approach aimed at addressing the complex challenges of education and protection in crisis contexts, with a strong emphasis on inclusivity, resilience-building, and sustainable development.In conclusion, the Humanitarian Response Plan (HRP) project executed by TAS represents a holistic and multifaceted approach to addressing the complex challenges facing education and protection in Northwest Syria (NWS). Through a comprehensive set of activities, including training and coaching initiatives, learning circles, and community engagement efforts, TAS seeks to build the resilience of individuals and communities, foster culture and learning, and contribute to the sustainable development of NWS.
