Project Summary

The objective of this project is to enhance access for services for GBV survivors and strengthen GBV coordination through improving capacity of service providers, strengthening of referral pathways and provision of material and livelihood opportunities for women and girls.
The specific objectives of this project are:
• Facilitate access to comprehensive multi sectoral GBV services (medical, legal, psychosocial) for 6000 women and girls
• Enhance the capacity/and mobilize 200 PSS counsellors; 150 health workers to provide timely, quality and confidential PSS and rape treatment
• Promote mobilization of women and girls to access services through the establishment of 15 safe spaces for women and girls (WGSS) to reach 3000 women and girls
• Strengthen referral pathways for dissemination and access to services reach 2000 women, girls, boys and men.
• Provide 2,000 dignity kits and 36 post rape kits for women and girls and treatment of rape
• Support 200 women and girls’ survivors of GBV with skills and grants for livelihood
• Establish 15 new coordination mechanism/and enhance 12 existing ones in rural locations in Puntland, Somaliland and FGS
• Build capacities of 90 GBV sub cluster coordinators for gender responsive data generation, utilization and utilization.
• Support the mainstreaming initiatives on IASC Guidelines for cluster programming on GBV for 300 cluster-coordinators at national and sub national levels

Project Activities

• Support 10 GBV one stop centers through which survivors access multi sectoral services and other GBV response services including Clinical Management of Rape (CMR) and medical assistance, legal aid, Psychosocial Support (PSS) and counselling, material assistance and safety planning for female and male GBV survivor.• Procure and distribute 50 post rape treatment kits for treating GBV survivors• Procure and distribute 2,000 dignity kits to vulnerable women and GBV survivors. • Support capacity building/and mobilize 300 PSS counsellors to deliver cultural appropriate, age and gender responsive PSS service.• Organize refresher trainings/and mobilize for 150 health workers on provision of treatment of rape, collecting and preserving forensic evidence for prosecution of perpetrators • Support training and mobilization of 100 police and community police on management of GBV survivors and collection/preservation of evidence• Support referrals and legal aid support for 150 GBV survivors • Support the development and update of referral pathways and dissemination to communities to reach 2000 persons• Establish 15 women and girls’ friendly safe spaces• Maintain the GBV sub-cluster coordination system in the country.

Mitigation Measures

Risk 2a)The project will endeavor to ensure maximum consultation with marginalized communities in all phases of the program. The project will ensure inclusion of marginalized individuals in community-based structures to ensure their priorities are tailored in to the program.Risk 2b)UNFPA and its implementing partners will ensure consistent sex-disaggregated programming to ensure men, women, girls, and boys are targeted adequately. UNFPA uses the “Leave no one behind” strategy to maximize the inclusion of men and boys in its programming. The project will use tracking mechanisms for the reports to ensure the target beneficiaries reported are the same as those planned.Risk 3a):UNFPA will use community-based committees composing of all segments of the community including marginalized/minority groups. UNFPA partners will closely work with community committees to select beneficiaries for the project.
