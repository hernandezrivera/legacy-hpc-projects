Project Summary

The Education in Emergency's Rapid Response Project (EiE-RRP) aims to provide children with adequate educational solutions and activities through remedial and follow-up/catch-up classes with flexible modules and courses via face-to-face and distance education; also to provide essential training and on remote/ distance education and improve teaching practices for teachers who are working with the national education system as well as teachers working in non-formal educational systems with the focus to harmonize catch up and remedial activities in non-formal education. The project will prioritize distance education and capacity building of teachers, catch up classes, WASH activities, and food distribution in southern Libya's educational facilities.

1 - Needs

Due to COVID-19's pandemic, formal and non-formal educational centers in Libya remained closed in order to limit the spread of the disease; this measure resulted in leaving students out of school for 8 months. This closure of educational facilities prevented vulnerable boys and girls from accessing essential services such as remedial classes and psychosocial support. In many cases, those educational facilities were the only access points for vulnerable boys and girls, children with disability, refugees’ and migrants’ children to receive the essential services. Distance education is recently considered in Libya as acceptable modality of teaching; therefore, teachers who are working in formal and non-formal educational systems need customized and special support to successfully imperilment their distance classes. Our mission is to minimize the negative impact of disruption of formal and non-formal classes through creating opportunities for access to education and simultaneously protecting vulnerable boys and girls affected by the conflict and following proper safety procedures and precautions to protect children and teachers during COVID-19's pandemic.
