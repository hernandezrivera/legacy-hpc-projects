Project Summary

The idea of this program is to support vulnerable Syrian families and their children to have improved food security and livelihoods as well as improved nutrition status, As well as and reduce the use of negative coping strategies that impact the wellbeing of children. Through nine month the project is designed to cover animal herders, farmers and food processors through: 1. Providing householders with the essential needs to make them able to grow up their lands through provision of agricultural inputs (such as seeds, fertilizers and some agricultural tools 450 HHs). 2. Improved access to irrigation . 3. Establishment of kitchen gardens 150 HHs (ﬁrst part: hens, chicken coop, chicken concentrated fodder, vaccines and vitamins, the second part: micro- garden seeds), 4. Support cow herders with concentrated fodder and animal drugs (250 HHs). 5. Then enhance their knowledge and practice through extension service and training program related to the speciﬁc activities. 6. As well as create work opportunities through cash for work activities to rehabilitate the productive assets 50 workers (each worker will receive 10 $ per working day (8 hours)). 7. Targeting the households for restore/establish/expand small –scale business activities through capital infusions (rehabilitate some small dairy products factories after conducting survey for all factories which found in project area, in addition we will support the interest families in dairy products processing with manufacturing equipment and some other tools 100 HHs), and capacity building by training them on entrepreneurship and business practice related to their aspects. 8. Rehabilitate or set up (5) small factories. Based on these activities we will target 950 HHs as a direct beneﬁciaries in food processing, agricultural and livestock activities as well as 50 beneﬁciary from CFW activity and rehabilitate or set up 5 small food processing factories. Expected Results: • Improve food security and strengthen resilience of vulnerable households affected by conﬂict and displacement. • Livestock breeders (Male and female) in targeted locations are supported through the provision them with fodder. • Increase the animal production that led to increase herder income through providing them with fodder for their animals will increase animal productions of milk and meat to be sold the surplus in local markets. • Contribute with child nutrition, and also maternal nutrition as a woman’s nutrition during pregnancy and lactation has a direct effect on her children through increasing in production (Milk, yogurt and other products). • Economic and income generating options for targeted communities explored and supported based on their commodities through supporting in food processing equipment. • Create new job opportunities through CFW activity. Beneﬁciary Selection criteria: The beneﬁciary must be 65% Host Community (HC), 35% IDPs. In total the Minimum 25% women, 15% People with Disabilities (PWD). • Beneﬁciary has a land/space suitable for home-gardening. • Priority to be given to vulnerable households including widows, female headed HHs, Person with disability or have household member with a disability, and households with elderly family members (50+). • The herders who have up to 25 animals or 5 cows. • Willingness to participate in training related to the provided livelihoods kit. • Not eligible if they do not plan to plant crops for the following reasons: no interest, not enough time, not suﬃcient labor.

4 - Needs

According to HNO2020; Compared to last year, the PiN for 2020 has increased by 8 per cent, demonstrating the continued impact of the protracted crisis on the food security and agriculture situation of the country.., it is appeared the problems which face the agriculture and livestock sectors, we can summarize it: 1. High prices of agricultural inputs such as fertilizers, seeds and pesticides. 2. High prices of fuel. 3. Absence and lack of source of alternative energy. 4. High prices for irrigation. 5. The diﬃculty of importing feeds، medicines and vaccines. 6. Worries about the spread of communicable and infectious diseases for inadequate vaccines. 7. The cost of feed, production, and veterinary care. 8. The diﬃculty of securing green feeds, especially for cows due to lack of irrigated areas. 9. Poor feed composition due to lack of control over the feed produced and on entering the area. Food processing and dairy products: • In target area the diary product factories not available and there are much of products exposed to mess up. • Simple household units for the production of dairy products with very little production capacity and do not meet the technical and health conditions • Food industries for domestic consumption of the household • The Olive mill( oil extract)There are several mills, this year that work but did not cover the need and based on Rapid need assessment was conducted in Qabasin and Bazzaais by IACO team, Qabasin is a town located northeast of the city of Al-Bab in northern Syria. It is administratively part of the Al-Bab sub district, in the Al-Bab District of the Aleppo Governorate. The population is approximately 69,422 / 960,000, distributed in the city of Qabasin and the villages of about 25 villages. The majority of the population works in agriculture and animal husbandry, with a total area of about 18,000 hectares. Common crops in the region are cereals, legumes and fruit trees.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

1- Area retaken by government of Syria: the likelihood of risk occurring is Low, the effect on achievement of activities probably led to STOP the activity, the risk management and mitigations through Predicting the situation before evacuation. 2- Rising of conﬂict within target area: the likelihood is Medium, probably to Delay some of the activities, the mitigation methods through Secure our ﬁeld team, prepare security plan 3- The Airplanes of Syria government attacking the crowded locations : the likelihood is medium, the effect on activities led to suspend the activities immediately. the procedures of mitigation: We must to regular the queue of beneﬁciaries and prevent the crowded through identify the beneﬁt HH the time to come to distribution point, then consider distributing door to door if conditions permit. 3- Car Bomb or motorcycle or Suicide: the likelihood of risk occurring is medium, the mitigation procedures Delay the activities The Syrian Free Police carries out intensive security patrols on a 24-hour, making it less likely that any risks or threats will occur, Information gathering and situational awareness, proper security advisories, ﬁrst aid training, medical plan 4- Armed Robbery: the likelihood of risk occurring is Low, the effect on activities achievement led to Delay the activities, the mitigation procedures: Awareness the people on any robbery process through local council, mosques, and farmer union and market owners so we can reported through walki- talki.
