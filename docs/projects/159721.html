Project Summary

The critical situation that people are living under in West Bank including area C, and the inability of the Palestinian Authority to work in the mentioned location has put the Palestinian people under difficult life situation due to their lack of adequate standards of living. Continues cracking down by Israeli occupation practices and settlers against Palestinian people living in area C and close to hot zones that face continues violations by settlers such as; preventing people from accessing their lands through closing check points, destroying agricultural lands and uprooting planted trees, destroying agricultural roads, and confiscation of lands. All these aggression and violation deteriorate life in area C and the its villages
The project aims at enhancing the resilience of resilience and protects the agricultural livelihoods of 330 food-insecure families including 423 women, 427men, 334 male children and 335 female children, while the percentage of the elders between these families is 7%. The project will target the Tubas (Bardala , Ein el Beida , Hammamat al Maleh , Kardala , Khirbet Atuf), Qalqiylia (, Falamya, Jayyus ,Al Mdawer, Azoun, Ras Atyeh ,Qalqiliya , Wadi ar Rasha). Through three activities; rehabilitation of Greenhouses, Rehabilitation and expansion of water carrier lines, and rehabilitation of water reservoirs.
The project activities will also respond to the different humanitarian needs of the most vulnerable farmers (women and men), to improve their living conditions by increasing the productivity of their agriculture lands, and enhance their resilience in their communities. Due to PARC experience and previous studies shows 87% of women are working in agriculture as unpaid work, PARC will give priorities to HH headed by women. Additionally, women help to improve the living conditions of their families, taking into consideration that more than half of the population in Nablus and Qalqilya are females.
The intervention will be aligned with the third Strategic Objective of the 2018 2020 humanitarian response plan.
According to the Strategic objective, Palestinians are vulnerable to rights violations, especially in relation to their livelihood, with which they struggle due to the prolonged crisis of the OPT. One of the main concerns of the Palestinian population is the impact of protection threats and violations on livelihoods, due to the fact that these communities rely heavily on agriculture and livestock as their main source of income. The lack basic agricultural needs and resources to improve the infrastructures are causing many lost to the farmers on the production in terms of quality and quantity.
Also, it will address the Food Security Sector objectives: “the resilience, food security and the productive capacity of vulnerable households are restored/enhanced and livelihoods protected”. This will be achieved through the rehabilitation of greenhouses and irrigated open fields and water carrier lines. This will allow the farmers to improve the production and the access to a basic need like the water for the lands.
PARC’s for the last 36 years has worked on strengthening the agricultural sector profitability and holding the occupation accountable towards Palestinian rights. In the last 3 years, PARC rehabilitated 320 dunums of greenhouses, 65 Km of water carrier lines and 500 dunums of irrigated open fields. At the same time, this proposed intervention will respond to the Ministry of Agriculture´s Strategic Objectives through improvement of the agricultural infrastructures that will allow enhancing the contribution of agriculture to food security and it will ensure and enhance the farmers` resilience.
The project’s main interventions are built on PARC’s humanitarian response approach and previous FSS experience in the area to include the followings:

1 - Needs

A need assessment was developed in full participatory approach at community levels; this project was developed in the year of 2018 through collecting the needs from the field in April 2018 and update on April 2019 by PARC staff. Through the resubmission process of the project PARC had reshaped the project out of the result of the need assessment conducted on 8,9,10 of sept 2019 by PARC with full coordination and consultation with key stakeholders at institutional level (working NGOs and related governmental ministries). This update was done through full participatory approaches: conducting a central workshop in the targeted locations with participation of 22 persons (March 11 2019) , from village councils’ representatives, farmers, women center, agricultural cooperatives. Additionally PARC had conducted field visits for 12 locations in Qalqilya, Salfeet, Nablus, Tubas and Jordan Valley. A specific criterion set for the selection of the project activities as follows: to be located at area C, close to conflict (threat of confiscation, close to settlements), the number of dunums benefited from the rehabilitated greenhouses and the water lines. While the beneficiaries should be selected in accordance to social and economic situation in addition of occupation threats to include the following measures; number of family members, priority is given to families headed by women, the availability of disability cases, threats of confiscations and to make sure that the family is not receiving similar services from other institutions to avoid duplication. The meetings and analysis relied upon PARC’s experience, field knowledge, and lessons learned arising from the implementation of many years’ similar programs. Finally; the need assessment will be conducted in order to start implementing the differentactivities. This will allow PARC put all the efforts in the more specific and urgent needs,choosing which activities will be implemented to each farmer. Beneficiaries will beselected in a transparent and participatory way based on clear criteria. In order to targetthe most vulnerable farmers, application forms will be filled up by the farmers includingdifferent data such as average income, land location, own eland area, exposed tonatural/man made disaster or water prices for irrigation. Prior to any projectimplementation, the organization will strictly coordinate the final definition and selectionof localities and beneficiaries with line ministries offices, and with any other organizationworking in the same area and sector of activities. Activity 1: Rehabilitation of 100 dunums of green Houses benefiting 100 families. The GH will be selected according to thefollowing selection criteria to reach the most marginalized group. The project has aspecific questionnaire that should be filled through the beneficiaries. This will be throughgiving priority for those families who have the less income salaries comparing to thenumber of the family members, headed by women, high number of family members, ifthey have students in the university, if they have disable people in the family, giving thepriority for lands located in area C since the majority of the project is implemented in areaC, and giving less priority for families that received similar services from other institutions.The high priority will be given for people who are suffering from natural disasters, andtheir sole and main income is from these GH only. In addition, a special consideration willbe given to those families who are not able to rehabilitate their greenhouses due todifficulties in their economic situation rather than families who could rehabilitate theirhouses from their own income resources. This rehabilitation will include differentactivities depending on the needs of each farmer: Tubas Governorate • Plastic roof andsides ‘for GH. • Side protection of greenhouses. • Replacement of the deterioratedirrigation systems with new regulated irrigation networks • Rehabilitation of wood withgood specifications for greenhouses. • Rehabilitation of ponds beside the greenhouses tobenefit from the harvesting of water from green houses in some cases. The rehabilitationof these greenhouses will increase the protection of the livelihood of the farmers, reducingalso their vulnerability and food insecurity. Activity 2: Expansion and Rehabilitation ofwater carrier lines: Qalqiliya Governorate 10.5 km of water carrier lines will be expanded andrehabilitated, benefiting 330 families. The first step will be to select the beneficiaries andto identify the specific needs of each of them priority will be given to the lands located inArea C, Surrounded or behind the wall, high percentage of leaking water, after that, thetender will be prepared and announced then the water carrier lines will be rehabilitated.After the implementation of these activities in the open irrigated lands; the farmers will beable to increase the productivity of the land and to decrease the amount of water lost withthe old irrigation system. Activity 3: Rehabilitation of 1 water reservoir: 1 metallic waterreservoirs will be rehabilitated, this reservoir provides water to lands inside area C, this activity willorganize the access of water to farmers increase the number of irrigated areas, andprotect land from the confiscation risks.

9 - Protection mainstreaming
9.1 - Do No Harm

A monthly meeting will be conducted at community level to update beneficiaries and communities about project development and challenges, which will enable beneficiaries to provide recommendations and highlight lessons learned. As most project activities will be carried out in sensitive Area C, a comprehensive risk analysis and security incidents will be documented, discussed and updated in a participatory approach with the target population. Consequently, mitigation measures (such as low profile methodology of activities by beneficiaries) will be carried out to reduce potential risks adhering to the principle of Do No Harm. Also local committees will be trained in documentation and dissemination of HR violationsPARC is committed in assuring accountability through the HAP policies adopted by PARC, the main accountability principles will be available and monitored during any intervention implemented by PARC include: participation, information sharing, enabling complaints and "do no harm" ,environment protection and gender mainstreaming.

9.2 - Equal and impartial access to aid

- PARC will ensure that the assistance is provided in times where control is limited (at night, during week-ends) in coordinated times with VCs and CPCs. - PARC have a field office in Jordan Valley and Nablus, Jenin where local coordinators for its projects in Area C are mobilized to mitigate access risk in locations where security incidents are likely to occur. - When needed, possible alignment of PARC internal security and procurement policies and guidelines are obtained to ease delivery of items, engineering works as well as contractor’s management. - PARC have been working since 1983 in the Jordan valley including the target communities and established robust trust and collaboration with local communities, CBOs, VCs, CPCs as well as donors and stakeholders, including for SHELTER projects, so they will be made aware of any problem with ICA or IF in the communities in real time. - PARC is a focal point for protection mainstreaming in the Shelter Cluster which enables us to briefly be engaged with updates related to overall security situation together with OCHA and other stakeholders humanitarian alert systems for WB. - PARC will be using all above access to information to support and facilitate when needed, all the times project team, contractors and materials to the project sites.
