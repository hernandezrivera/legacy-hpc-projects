Project Summary

The overall goal of the project is to provide enabling institutional, technical, and environmental conditions that would improve livelihood and food security in a sustainable manner, therefore, reduce economic vulnerability of local communities in general, and the relatively disadvantaged households, women and unemployed youth in particular. The actual target will be determined based on the results of baseline survey to be conducted at the planning phase of the project. In addition of other forms of groupings and associations like cooperatives, small business groups, and specific product associations, households of the targeted groups are dealt with as productive or economic units during the project interventions.ar.

5.1 - Impact on environment/natural resources

It will be incorporated throughout each phase of the project. Farmers and Herders will learn improved practices to make the most out of their land whilst ensuring its sustainable management and continual fertility. Communities will be trained on equitable and sustainable use of resources. All established committees will be trained and well represented, ensuring broad adoption of sustainable practices. The successively sessions of the agriculture extension will be the driving forces for raising the awareness of the targeted to sustain their related environment.

6 - Protection mainstreaming

3=Yes, room for improvement

6.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

the Protection and rehabilitation of natural pasture . Where the grazing areas are degraded and encroached by farmers, encroachment of animals onto farmland is more likely, and this leads to crop damage, confrontations, retaliatory burning of pasture, and so on. Pastoralists need rehabilitation of their grazing areas and mutually agreed routes along the grazing lands which they can move their herds, with defined resting areas. Key areas of grazing land that have become degraded along the recognized livestock corridors will be rehabilitated through reseeding and the arrangement of community agreements to leave them unused, to recover.
