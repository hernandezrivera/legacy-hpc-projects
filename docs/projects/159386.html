Project Summary

NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS under this project will undertake activities such as: Selection of drought affected households of vulnerable community of peri-urban and IDPs chosen through registering families/HHs with malnourished children who have been discharged from the nutrition sites to also benefit through provision of Family ration to 1000 drought affected HHs; provision of farming inputs to vulnerable riverine and agro-pastoralist farmers to 1000 HHs giving consideration to women headed HHs; provision of restocking to 1000 pastoralists who lost their animals during the drought; Rehabilitating primary and secondary irrigation canals and Establishment of community led Early Warning Early Action systems; Strengthen the monitoring and supervision activities in order to optimize the project implementation. The overall goal of this project will be to provide lifesaving assistance to most affected households in Peri-urban and IDPs integrated into host communities in food crisis in the identified regions i.e Galgaduud and Lower Shabelle in order to prevent death due to starvation.

Mitigation Measures

NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS is an independent, neutral organization ensuring humanitarian protection and assistance for victims of armed conflict and other situations of violence, natural disasters such as drought and famine. It takes action in response to emergencies and at the same time promotes respect for international humanitarian law and its implementation in national law. NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS is guided by a code of conduct to ensure that displaced people are treated with the humanitarian principles of humanity, neutrality, impartiality and independence. As such the highest regard for the protection of human life and health will be provided to all people needing and seeking help with clear policies and procedures in place for NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS staff and partners to endure that not protection risk is incurred. In the event of any exposure to protection risk, NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS will follow its laid down procedures to mitigate those risks to ensure protection is provided at all times to all in need. Displaced populations, where by natural or human-made disasters are at a high risk to be excluded from accessing the humanitarian support they need the most due to multifactorial issues some of which include segregation of minority groups, stigma, exclusion due to lack of knowledge or information sharing. Taking cognizance of these risks of exclusion that displaced populations face, NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS will actively seek an all-inclusive policy in order to reach all those in need especially in the areas NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS is working. NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS will recruit some of its project staff from displaced populations especially for positions such as community workers and Community Resource Persons in order to ensure IDPs get inclusion in accessing and benefiting from all our projects and will also ask for their participation in project design, implementation, monitoring and evaluation. NWO will coordinate with the the Food Security Cluster and the Cash Working Group.NWO will ensure that the distribution of humanitarian support is done in a safe space and location with adequate security that does not compromise the safety of beneﬁciaries as much as possible; NWO will ensure all staff and volunteers have received training on protection; Engage the community and committee representative to play an active role in identifying solutions and in the decision-making processes that affect them, so as to promote a sense of ownership, build their self- esteem and improve the relevance and sustainability of the response; Ensure all the staff and volunteers working with affected populations understand, sign and adhere to a Code of Conduct stating their commitment to respect and foster humanitarian standards and the rights of beneﬁciaries and NWO will also consult women, men, boys, girls, persons with disabilities, chronically ill, older persons, pregnant and lactating women, and marginalized persons to collect accurate information about their speciﬁc needs and preferences, location, design, and services. Populations displaced by conflict and violence face a lot of problems especially humanitarian need and lack of life-saving services. NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS will work to ensure the provision of access to basic life-saving to all populations in need in our areas of interventions without discrimination. In this regard, NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS will also work within the humanitarian context and coordinate with other actors to ensure the severe impacts of armed conflict and violence is reduced. NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS works very closely with communities and local authorities, ensuring that local laws, customs and culture are adhered to, and avoiding action and advocacy that may be perceived as offensive. Rigorously apply ‘do no harm’ principles. Programme informed by regular conflict analysis and understanding of politics at different levels.

Please provide further explanation

NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS will work the Government in order to build a resilient and responsive system infrastructure. NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS will focus both on the everyday routine challenges that front line staff face on a regular basis as well as focus on sudden shocks such as disease outbreaks. NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS will work to build the leadership capacity of district and regional state representatives and in this regard will target Government key managers, field staff, community members as well as Community Resource Persons so as to empower them to solve problems at their level and build relationships and social networks. NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS will focus on strengthening existing social networks to provide access to multiple resources available in the community and forms of value such as community knowledge and reputation that can build and restore resilience to the existing systems. NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS will use the model of everyday resilience building in the systems of the Government through a combination of absorptive, adaptive and transformative strategies that authorities, staff and community members can use to adopt the system in responding to strains and shocks. This will help in producing a strengthened and more resourceful resilient system infrastructure. NWO will coordinate with the the Food Security Cluster and the Cash Working Group. NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS will train the Local Authorities, field staffs, resource persons and community members on how to deal with sudden shocks such as outbreak of diseases. All the different strata of the community system will be taught how to manage such disease outbreaks at different levels in order to build a resilient and responsive infrastructure into every level. NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS will use the funding from donors as well as its own funds to ensure adequate resources and strong government system infrastructure is built to contribute to generating a resilient and responsive system in Galgaduud and Lower Shabelle regions.
