Project Summary

The protracted crisis in the North East Nigeria has disrupted means of livelihoods of women, men with a large number of displacements. Of this number, women and children account for 70 %. This crisis has led to a scarcity of staple food in the local markets driving a high rate of malnutrition among under 5.
The Director General of the World Health Organization (WHO) declared the outbreak of the coronavirus disease 2019 (COVID-19) and on 30th January 2020, a Public Health Emergency of International Concern (PHEIC), and on 11th March 2020 as a global pandemic.
On 27th February 2020 the Federal Ministry of Health, Nigeria announced the confirmation of the first case of Corona virus disease in Lagos State, Nigeria. In the same communication the Honourable Minister of Health announced that the Multi-sectoral Coronavirus Preparedness Group led by the Nigeria Center for Disease control (NCDC) has immediately activated its National Emergency operations Center . Since then, in less than 2 months, Nigeria has reached more than 50 cases across the country. Among the immediate measures to curb the spread and respond to the pandemic are the recurrent lock downs, promotion of social distancing and self- protection, strengthening the health system and preparedness of quarantine sites. The lockdown has drastically impacted the nutritious status of IDPs and people living in H2R locations.
In order to limit the spread and reduce morbidity and mortality among vulnerable groups of under 5 pregnant and lactating mothers/caregivders, AHA shall implement IPC protocol and a blanket supplementary program among vulnerable group of Under 5 / lactating mothers in the host communities in Adamawa and Yobe States.

1 - Needs

The outbreak of corona virus pandemic has lead to series of lock down with a direct impact on trade, means of livelihoods, lack and rising of prices of staple food in the local markets. This has exacerbated and deepened the precarious nutrition status of vulnerable group of IDPs, single female headed households with low income and daily laborers. This health pandemic has lead to food insecurity with reduction of number of daily food consumption and negative coping mechanism among women to meet up the daily food needs. The myths sustained by community leaders, religious leaders on corona virus, the belief on certain food in response to corona virus coupled with the weak health system, insufficiency of wash facilities both in health and nutrition sites, quarantines sites for effective prevention and response still contribute to reported cases of death among infested persons. In addition, there is a lack of health equipment, nutrition supplies and preposition of core preventive kits for self- protection, health and nutrition workers.

2 - Activities

-Training of CNMs on preventive measures, means of transmission of corona virus, signs of infection and referral pathways.-Pre-positioning of detective material (thermometer) and core hygiene kits at the entrance of nutrition and quarantine sites.-Provision of self- protection equipment for CNMs and nutrition workers-Provision of Wash services and items for prevention and response to covid-19- Training of mothers / caregivers on self- screening at households and referral for mitigation of the spread of the virus.- Supplies of nutritious food to vulnerable groups of under 5, pregnant and lactating mothers/ caregivers in Host communities.- Training of CNMs on permanent and regular hygiene promotion among lactating mothers/ caregivers.
