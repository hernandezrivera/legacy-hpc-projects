Project Summary

Due to the complexity of the response to the COVID-19 pandemic in Bangladesh, there is a a need to strengthen coordination, information management and analysis capacity in six areas - (1) joint assessment and reporting tools; (2) cash and voucher assistance; (3) community engagement (4) coordination at local level and localization; (5) information management and (6) monitoring and evaluation.
