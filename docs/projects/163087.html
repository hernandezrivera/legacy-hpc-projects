Project Summary

According to the Zimbabwe Poverty Atlas of 2015, Binga and Gokwe South have a poverty prevalence of 85% to 96%, Chipinge and Uzumba Maramba Pfungwe 73% to 84% and Gokwe Town 61% to 72%. The 2019 WASH severity rating indicates that Binga, Gokwe South and Gokwe Town have a WASH severity of 4; UMP and Chipinge 3. In the recent years, there was inadequate basic sanitation and safe water for drinking for the five districts. The government could not provide adequate WASH services due to financial constraints hence the need for intervention in the stated areas. Binga, Gokwe –South (including Gokwe Town) and UMP experienced drought for three consecutive years whilst Chipinge was hit by cyclone Idai, early 2019, that destroyed WASH infrastructure. The drought and the cyclone negatively affected service delivery in terms of access to safe water, safe and dignified sanitation as well as access to hygiene enabling facilities. Mvuramanzi Trust has the capacity to intervene and mitigate the challenges faced by the communities in the mentioned five districts.
The main aim of this intervention is to reduce or counter the negative effects of drought and cyclone during the year 2020. The project will target communities and Institutions (Schools and Health facilities) and will ensure that at least 209,784 children (boys and girls), men & women are reached with access to adequate and quality safe water; sanitation, health and hygiene messages and NFIs. The coverage and targeted people in need are as follows;
 Matabeleland North Province
- Binga ( 62,297 beneficiaries targeted)
 Midlands Province
- Gokwe South ( 72,300 beneficiaries targeted)
- Gokwe Town (13,546 beneficiaries targeted)
 Mashonaland East Province
- UMP ( 37,508 beneficiaries targeted)
 Manicaland Province
- Chipinge ( 24,133 beneficiaries targeted)
This will be achieved through rehabilitation of water points, Installation of solar water reticulation systems, Rehabilitation/repair of urban water reticulation systems, construction of headworks, Distribution of NFI kits to Schools and Health facilities, Distribution of NFI kits to vulnerable people in the community, Strengthening and training of Extension workers and Community Based Volunteers on Hygiene promotion, Dissemination of critical hygiene messages; and Improve capacity for Operations and Maintenance in 5 Districts (Chipinge, Binga, Gokwe South, Gokwe Town and Uzumba Maramba Pfungwe). Nutrition gardens will be set up around these water points to enable schools and Health facilities to provide nutritious food for their supplementary feeding programs. These supplementary feeding programs will ensure continued high attendance and improved health of pupils; thus improve the level of education in the community as a whole. The targeted communities including schools and health facilities are in drought and cyclone affected districts with a WASH severity ranking of 3 to 4. The project will work closely with the Civil Protection Unit (CPU) and District Water and Sanitation Sub-Committee (DWSSC) at district level to ensure construction of climate resilience structures and Social Welfare to support on proper targeting of vulnerable households. The Community and School Health clubs will act as vehicles of behaviour change. To ensure sustainability, the project will also embark on capacity building training's for builders, pump mechanics, water point committees and school health teachers.
