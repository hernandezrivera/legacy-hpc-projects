1 - Needs

Sudan is home to 786,524 South Sudanese refugees of which 65% live out of camps in host communities. UNICEF is responsible for meeting their WASH, Child protection, Education , Health and Nutrition needs. 57,081 Ethiopian refugees fled conflict in the northern regions of Ethiopia, and around 120,00 are predicted to join in 2022. Alongside UNHCR, UNICEF is ready to respond to the needs of these refugees and the host communities that support them.

2 - Activities/Outputs

These activities will be implemented as part of UNICEF's response. For SSRs these are focused on out of camp, refugee and incorporated into Host community activities across UNICEF led sectors. These will be implemented through government partners, international NGO partners and national NGO partners
