Project Summary

To deliver vital nutritional aid to Pregnant and Lactating Women (PLWs) and children under five (CU5) and in an effort to improve the nutrition status in targeted sub-districts (Al Bab, Aghtrin, Suran, Jarablus, Daret Azza, Mhambal, Armanaz, Kafr Takharim, Salqin, Bennsh, Idleb, Maaret Tamsrin, Darkosh, Jisr-Ash-Shugur) in Idleb and Aleppo governorates. The Catchment Population in the targeted locations is about 1,924,443
PAC project in NWS will employ a qualitative approach, aligning with CMAM, MIYCN and CVA guidelines. The program targets 149,328 children 0-59 months (boys and girls) and 85,488 caregivers, adolescent girls and PLWs, focusing on females (74% girls/women), with limited male participation mainly in awareness-raising activities. 10% of the targeted BNFs are PWD. PAC will reach Inter IDPs and Host Communities across 14 sub-districts through the following service modalities in addition to continuing our role in monitoring CMAM and MIYCN indicators across NWS
Supporting Nutrition Surveillance: PAC, will continue nutrition surveillance activities, implementing community-based surveillance, including anthropometric measurements and other data collection on monthly bases along the year in addition to conducting annual SMART survey to be used as advocacy tool
Providing CMAM and MIYCN nutrition services (as listed in indicators section) within in 2 PHCs and 1 Maternity hospital as an integration effort with Health services
Supporting Outpatient Therapeutic Feeding Programs (OTP): PAC will support 9 OTP centers in areas with high Global Acute Malnutrition (GAM) rates. These centers will provide CMAM and MIYCN services, ensuring access to necessary nutrition supplies
Deploying Rapid Response Teams (RRTs): 12 RRTs will deliver multisectoral services, integrating CMAM and MIYCN programs. These RRTs will be linked to OTPs. They will provide health messages, hygiene promotion, and child protection cases referrals, particularly in crowded camps and communities
Providing Nutrition Cash Voucher Assistance CVA to PLWs/ children under 23 months in several locations linked to our OTPs and PHCs and conduct necessary referrals to nearest stabilization centers for SAM cases
Integration with other sectors though:
Health Services through Screen, referral of CU5 and PLW with medical problems to the nearby health facility, disseminate awareness of self-hygiene messages, Cholera. and Risk Communication, and Community Engagement (RCCE) related messages. Identification of zero dose children and conduct necessary referral to SIG teams
WASH sector: Support hygiene promotion by raising WASH awareness by providing Key messages (Hand Washing, Food Processing, and access to clean water)
Protection: Identify visual signs of abuse/violence against childU5/PLWs during Nutrition screening
Integration with FSL services through conducting referrals of PLWs enrolled in CVA for Nutrition services who didn't receive General food assistance
Regarding the monitoring activities, the team does the following:
Technical Indicator Monitoring: MEAL team collaborates with program team to design an indicator tracking table to ensure activity tracking accuracy during implementation. Then, they track progress of activities against monthly targets. conduct KIIs and FGDs with host community to assess project's impact, measure the level of satisfaction
BNFs Follow-up: Conducting periodic interviews and satisfaction surveys to collect feedback from BNFs after receiving services and conducting post-verification to measure effectiveness of services and their impact on BNFs and to ensure data accuracy
Service Delivery Site Monitoring: Evaluating application and mainstreaming of humanitarian work standards at service delivery sites, like mainstreaming general protection principles, safeguarding and protection against SEA, GBV considerations, and AAP. Also, ensuring the provision of supportive services like water and sanitation services
AAP activities: Feedback and Complaints
