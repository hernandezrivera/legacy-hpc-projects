Project Summary

Ethiopia has experienced an unprecedented high number of conflict-induced displacements across Oromia, SNNP, Tigray, Amhara, Afar and Benishangul-Gumuz regions. The persistent high number of internally displaced persons (IDPs) coupled with multilayered complexities of the displacement and inadequate humanitarian response have resulted in high levels of unmet humanitarian needs.
Conflict induced displacement have become a common phenomenon in recent years in Western and Southern Oromia zones, particularly in the Wellegas and West Guji zone.
According to rapid inter- agency need assessment conducted in April/ 2022, revealed that many IDPs needs access to shelter and are forced to sleep in overcrowded spaces including emergency shelters or communal sites in Sibu Sire woreda and both Gida Ayana and Bule Hora woreda are also extremely in need of humanitarian responses. In response to these challenges AIDRO designed a project to provide ESNFI and cash for rent, Conduct HLP due diligence, distribution of ES- Repair kit, ES, NFI for households displaced as a result of conflict in Sibu Sire, and Gidda Ayana woredas in East Wellega zones and Birbirsa Kojowa Woreda of West Guji zone.
This proposed project will respond to the need of the most vulnerable among the conflict- affected IDP population, returnees and host communities residing in Sibu Sire woreda targeting 15,081 (2742 HHs) individuals with ESNFI and cash for rent supports for displaced as a result of conflict and living with the host communities for 10,557 (1919 HHs) and cash for rents for 4524 individuals (822 HHs), Gida Ayana woreda 4,444 individuals (808 HHs) with NFI- for 1486 individuals, 262 people with disabilities, and ES repair kit for 1975 individuals from this 348 ES repair kit is for people with disabilities, Emergency shelter kit for 970 (176 HH) 146 for people with a disability, Birbirsa Kojowa- ES-repair kit for 4254 individuals, HLP due diligence and building back better and other technical support activities are were planned in this projects.
The target area is selected in accordance with the Ethiopian Emergency Shelter/NFI cluster prioritization depending up on the need and response gap in the intervention area. The overall project objective is to ensure access to Emergency Shelter, NFI, ESNFI items for IDPs and host communities in targeted woredas in order to ensure the safety, well-being and dignity of the affected population and planned to be implemented for six (6) months, AIDRO will assure close collaboration with the ES/NFI cluster, concerned government bodies, and beneficiaries in implementing and involving them in the decision-making process of the proposed project.

3.2 - How will your project work with relevant local & national actors (e.g. CSOs, advocacy groups) in support of effective design, implementation and monitoring of the response, as well as ensure their meaningful participation in supporting this response

AIDRO will coordinate well to deliver this projects in the proposed location b/c AIDRO has well-structured program execution and monitoring mechanisms at different structural levels which is designed in a way that enhances its efficiency and effectiveness in responding to the emergency needs of the community. AIDRO’s presence as operational in the proposed areas comprises professionals which are supported by the zonal cluster leads and regional leadership. AIDRO head office and zonal level office will be responsible for the direct implementation of the project. Zonal level Project Officer and Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability, and Learning (MEAL) Project Officers will lead the monitoring and evaluation of the project under zonal project coordinator. Head office MEAL Unit will spearhead all monitoring activities using the emergency contextualized Simple Measurement of Indicators for Learning and Evidence-based Reporting M&E system.The regional and zonal leads also work in harmony with the technical leads and units at the head office. This project will apply participatory monitoring approaches that engage all concerned bodies and beneficiary representatives. The process engages the beneficiary representatives, local authorities and other stakeholder from the onset of the project with special emphasis on screening. Joint monitoring with the involvement of ESNFI shelter cluster, progress and performance will be tracked and a range of data collection methods of both qualitative and quantitative will be applied. A comprehensive monitoring framework will be developed at the start of the project to ensure the existence of a well-planned monitoring schedule, partner’s involvement, required resources allocation, and supportive tools for data collection and analysis prepared.Alliance for Integrated Development and Relief Organization will maintain a strong working relationship with other implementing partners operating in the target regions to avoid duplication of effort and active participant of national ES/NFI coordination groups with relevant Government and non-government stakeholders to discuss priorities, share best practices, and make decisions on issues of common purpose. AIDRO will coordinate and closely work with protection partners / GBV response partners/ in the project location and regions to ensure gender mainstreaming and protection under this ES/NFI programs and achieve the targeted indicators and activities.

3.6 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members using age and gender appropriate, safe and confidential channels?

AIDRO ensures the participation of community members throughout the life cycle of the project. It will establish clear mechanism that allows the community members to provide feedback on the project implementation. The mechanism will ensure comprehensive and confidential system set up. This project is designed by engaging community members through their representatives such as community leaders and religious leaders, concerned government bodies and other partners. During startup of the program, information about the program, beneficiary selection criteria, and information on their entitlement, the beneficiary’s right to raise any complaints and complaining mechanisms will be provided for beneficiaries and will continue to be communicated to promote transparency. Site and beneficiary selection will be take place after consultation meetings held with government stakeholders, cluster representatives and representatives of the affected population. Different meetings will be held with community members to provide information and discuss on planned interventions from beneficiary targeting, verification, prioritization, distribution process, to informing the rights and entitlements of recipients. AIDRO works to integrate inclusion of people with disability in all cycles of the project. It will ensure the participation of this groups in beneficiary selection committees, complaint handling committees and monitoring framework. It will also organize consultation on planned projects with people with disabilities and understand the barrier to access to assistance. Feedback mechanisms will be designed to adequately record issues faced by persons with disabilities and staff is empowered to act on these suggestions. The reporting mechanism will involve assigning focal person to record and follow up on any complaints received. Locked complaint boxes will be prominently displayed and regularly checked to collect feedback and to take appropriate measures. Information sharing and awareness creation about accountability to affected population will be held during distribution by using different printed IEC/BCC materials. Complaint handling and Beneficiary selection committee along with responsible government stakeholders and AIDRO project staffs will involve handling any concerns or complaints that arises from the beneficiary and act accordingly. The committee will be comprised of women, people with disabilities, elderlies to ensure maximum community participation. Aside from the committee, AIDRO will also set up alternate ways for feedback and complaint mechanism to promptly respond to concerns of beneficiary and adjust implementation. Mechanisms to receive this complaint will consider the preferred mechanism of the community but will mainly use Help desk, community feedback suggestion boxes, provision of local stakeholders of contacts of AIDRO staffs and selected focal representative of the community. AIDRO will use the standard checklist on incorporating accountability to affected population in the humanitarian programme cycle developed by the Global protection cluster to ensure that AAP is integrated into all cycles of the project. AIDRO will also ensure the collection and utilization of sex and age disaggregated data that adheres to recommended age groupings and AIDRO project staffs and community volunteers will be trained on humanitarian accountability and its importance to mainstream them on ground in every level of the project.
