Project Summary

ALIGHT’s proposed Livelihood support Program for Disaster (covid19 impact and drought) affected Population is an emergency and recovery building project aimed at promoting conditions for improved access to food for vulnerable households and increasing Government services to Returnees, IDPs, farmers, Pastoralist dropouts & Agro-pastoral Population in Jubaland, Banadir regions and Somaliland. The project intends to contribute to the HRP 2022 of ensuring increased access to food security for 100 IDPs, 150 returnees households, 100 agropastoral farmers, 100 pastoral drop-out affected of recent drought and extreme vulnerable non-displaced households. Specifically the project will contribute towards; (i) Increasing HH capacity to produce own food and vegetables with adequate nutritional value for Target HHs. Farmers and Agro-pastoral communities in Jubaland have increased access to farm input to enable recover to household own production capacity ; (ii) vulnerable returnees and IDPs have Improved access to Government lead and managed services through increasing capacity of Government to provide extension services to small farmers and kitchen gardens will be done. In addition Strengthening capacity of JRIA & JCC to assess economic impact of COVID-19 & drought on Small IGAs owned by vulnerable population Returnees, refugees, IDPs and Resident households and develop support plan.; (iii) Vulnerable households (Returnees, IDPs, pastoralist dropout and residents (host)) living in Jubaland, Banadir and Somaliland have improved capacity to recover from the impact of recent shock by having access to food with nutritional value through provision of livelihood Assets, cash based response promoting Kitchen gardening intervention and cash grant support for small IGAs owned by returnees (targeting Vulnerable households headed by women and disability persons.
Key activities include;
 Provision cash grant to 100 returnees households (80% female headed household, elderly people and disabled families)
 Provision of Livelihood Asset (donkey + cart) (80% female headed household, elderly people and disabled families
 Capacity and facilitation for Government to provide service to vulnerable population
 Provision of farm input (seeds, fertiliser)
 CFW and Cash grant to support covid19 affected IGAs (70% female headed household, elderly people and disabled families
 Restocking of drought affected pastoral dropouts
Beneficiary targeting will be done through community based approach to ensure inclusive and protecting minority and vulnerable groups in the community. ALIGHT (ARC) believes that targeting the right beneficiary is critical in realization of project goal and in order to do it right, prior to beneficiary identification process, ARC will first begin conducting intensive community sensitization and raise community awareness on the project, assessing clan dynamics in project area will be done during need assessment. To protect rights of minority groups, ARC will ensure Project management committee is all inclusive with representation of minority groups.

2.1.2 - Explain how the project design considered market functionality?

Market design Alight has conducted an initial market assessment (conducted interviews of key traders and beneficiaries) at the proposed sites before deciding on the use of market-based programming. Alight has also reviewed the recent joint market assessment report done by REACH in Somalia and has found out that essential hygiene items and construction materials are locally available in major markets and beneficiaries can access them if the given support in terms of the vouchers supports. Alight has also established that markets are integrated, interconnected, and functional in the proposed target areas (Mogadishu, Kismayo, Sool, and Sanaag). In the event that, certain locations will have no functional markets and therefore not feasible to do the voucher-based support, Alight will be flexible to switch to in-kind based distribution. Alight will conduct continuous monitoring of the local markets and collect information related to pricing change, effects of seasonality, etc. which might affect the suitability of the preferred mode of delivery. Justification for use of commodity vouchers over In-kind distribution The project has proposed the use of the MBP Programming approach particularly the use of Commodity vouchers in the delivery of hygiene kits and sanitation materials to targeted beneficiaries
