Project Summary

This support for the JRP 2020 project, aims to enhance the COVID-19 response by ensuring targeted COVID-19 affected households have access to a range of basic non-food items (NFI) including floor mats, blankets, solar lights, 3mm rope, mosquito nets and tarpaulin. At the same, this project will also ensure that shelters damaged due to the monsoon receive emergency resistant materials to improve and maintain shelters. In addition, the shelter mobile team will be trained on IOM's code of conduct, PSEA (Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) and COVID-19 distribution guidelines (e.g. maintaining proper social distancing while distributing NFI). The Shelter team will distribute NFI to the families in home-care and quarantine centres. A brief orientation (compliant with social distancing measures) will be conducted. Similarly, emergency response activities would also include a brief orientation to the beneficiaries. This trained shelter mobile team would ensure that any shelter repair, upgrade or new construction undertaken is aligned with construction site safety recommendations in light of COVID-19 by the S-NFI sector. The above mentioned activities will be undertaken in Camps 8W, 9, 10, 11, 12, 18, 20, 20 extension, 22, 23, 24 and 25.

Objectives

1. Ensure that families affected by COVID-19 are supported with specific NFI, home delivery of NFI and implementation of emergency shelter response, including material delivery. 2. Mitigate the risk of exposure to the virus for the elderly population, by providing home deliveries and specific NFI support items, and implementation of emergency shelter response, including material delivery.

Needs

Need of Refugees: With limited access to construction materials and shelter support put on hold prior to the monsoon season, it is highly likely that damage caused to the shelters during the monsoon season will increase. This will be exacerbated with potential increase in the number of people that are unable to leave their shelter to collect the materials and repair their shelter because they are in home-care or in medical facilities. The need for emergency shelter response during the monsoon season, for implementation of shelter repairs as well as delivery of shelter materials will increase. Furthermore, refugees in quarantine facilities and the ones in home-care will need to be supported with NFI. In addition to NFI distributed previously, there will be a need for additional items to support with home-care such as blankets, floor mats, solar lights or other. This kind of support will be especially needed as the person with symptoms should use a separate part of the shelter and several items could not be shared. Elderly are especially vulnerable and would need home delivery of items, NFI items to make them more comfortable when in home isolation as well as implementation of any emergency shelter support.

Activities or outputs

1. Delivery cost of NFI to households in home care and to the households with elderly members and those in quarantine facilities 2. Delivery cost of shelter materials and implementation of emergency response to the households in the home care and for the households with elderly members
