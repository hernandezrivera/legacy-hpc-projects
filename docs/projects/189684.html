Project Summary

This humanitarian response intends to address dire needs of communities affected by conflict and natural calamities through provision of emergency WASH NFIS, NUTS, Health, improve dignified sanitation facilities, and mitigate protection/GBV risks for the persons affected in Baliet, Koch, Panyijiar, Panyikang, Fangak, Malakal Akobo and Ayod counties Canal Pigi counties of Uppernile, Jponglei and Unity states. Hope Restoration South Sudan(HRSS) and other humanitarian partners and in coordination with UN OCHA, conducted IRNA between September to October 2021 in Fagak and Canal Pigi counties confirmed that, almost 80% of the total population were displaced from their homes to the highest grounds by flooding and experienced few protection concerns. Due to flooding and conflict communities experienced inadequate infrastructures and malnutrition affecting women and children U5 in Baliet, Panyikang, Fangak, Malakal Akobo and Ayod, Canal Pigi counties. IRNAs and households surveys conducted by Hope Restoration South Sudan (HRSS) and other humanitarian partners in Panyijiar and Ayod, found that thousand households were displaced to the highest grounds without access to the dignified sanitations, facilities, WASH NFIS few protection on walk to water sources. There are no hygiene promotion sessions and open defecation increases up to 80% in Canal Pigi and Fangak. Humanitarian Need Overview (HNO) underscored that, 6,159,886 people will face WASH services shortage in 2022. Disasters associated to nature and civil war plus communal violence, exacerbated by the cattle raiding and revenge killing, continued to be worsening and left communities with nothing. However, this becomes a matter of humanitarian concern and requires humanitarian intervention. Women, elderly persons of 60+, persons living with disability and children are compromised to high risks of water borne diseases: Malaria, Typhoid, cholera and e.tc. The project beneficiaries are 3577 HHS translated to 21466 individuals and can be broken down as follows: Akobo =1322 Koch=2131 Ayod=3217, Fanagak=2761, Panyijiar=2213, Malakal=,1231 Panyikang=3113,Pigi=2143, Baliet=1203, Ayod=2132
This humanitarian situation in the counties mentioned above, needs intervention that cut across all sectors to reduce suffering of persons affected by conflict and natural disasters. Civil war and natural catastrophes or flooding, have paralyzed Community coping mechanisms and weakened other viable options for living. Sanitation infrastructures both institutional and communal broke down submerged, while remaining few infrastructures can’t accommodate the evolving needs of the affected persons. Women and girls are attacked by gunmen or bandits on their way to water source and when fetching fire woods, exposing them to the few risk of GBV and its associates.
Hope Restoration South Sudan (HRSS) will address the needs of affected communities through provision of WASH NFIS; households water storage; jerry cans, buckets with taps and without taps; household water filter tablets; Aqua tabs, water filter clothes and PUR sachets; construction and rehabilitation of sanitation infrastructures in nutrition facilities and health facilities to reduce vulnerability of women and children U5 admitted for SAM/MAM in Baliet, Fangak, Akobo, Malakal, Panyikang and Ayod counties. Recruit and train training community hygiene promoters increase community awareness through hygiene messages and sessions in public places, health schools, and nutrition facilities. Mitigation of GBV risks against women and girls through GBV awareness. Establishment or strengthening of Complaint feedbacks desk to track protection concerns and other satisfaction issues about response mechanisms and services felt. Hope Restoration South Sudan(HRSS) will form and train Accountability to the affected population committee (AAP) committee to boost community participation or participatory accessibility of the of population groups in decision process.

1 - Needs

Displacement of populations and the aftermath looting of their properties among many others abuses, complicated humanitarian situation in Baliet, Koch, Panyijiar, Panyikang, Fangak, Malakal Akobo, Rubkona and Ayod. Women and girls have been attacked by gunmen on their way to the water points unmasking them to few protection and GBV risks especially in Canal Pigi and Fangak counties. They are harassed, raped, abused and exploited. While most of these perpetrators normally go at will. The judiciary system in most parts of locations targeted is paralyzed by many factors including availability of small arms in the hands of bandits or unauthorized agents seem to undermine legal redress. Communities in Baliet, Koch, Panyijiar, Panyikang, Fangak, Malakal Akobo, Rubkona and Ayod have experienced displacement associated to natural disasters or flooding and conflict. The flooding started in June and intensified in the midst of September, which triggered displacement of thousand households and destroyed WASH infrastructures. This left many households vulnerable. Also women children became malnourished, as revealed in the FSNMS report 2021). Heavy rainfalls coupled up with the floodwater from the River Nile and its tributaries, submerged many villages, whereas WASH infrastructures both sanitation and water infrastructures, including households latrines destroyed and caused pollution to the human environment resulted to the diarrheas' and water borne other diseases, water storage items; jerry cans, buckets and without taps absolutely lost during the displacement. Hope Restoration South Sudan (HRSS) and other humanitarian agencies on ground and in collaboration with UNOCHA, conducted IRNA in Canal Pigi and Fangak counties, between Sept and October 2021, and underscored that 80% percentages of the population had been displaced by flooding and other driving factors highlighted, deteriorating WASH conditions of the communities in regards to WASH services. FSNSM conducted in 2021) proved beyond reasonable doubts that, 39% of the total population in Pigi and Fangak, practiced Open Defecations (OD), number of attacks by gunmen on the routes to the water points, considerably reported to have threatened women and girls. In light of this contextual analysis, humanitarian situation in counties mentioned in this statement, communities living conditions are confronted by multiple problems: malnutrition in women and children U5 prolonged by poor feeding and lack of WASH infrastructures. There is a Shortage of Households water storage and water treatment tablets such buckets with taps and without taps, jerrycans, Aqua tabs, PUR sachets and water filter clothes. Poor hygienic conditions both in camp like and camp setting. There are few sanitations or latrines and water infrastructures in most locations. Hope Restoration South Sudan (HRSS) will address needs of conflict and disaster affected persons in areas targeted, through multi-sectoral approaches; WASH health, NUTS , WASH NFIS such as households water storage; buckets, jeer cans and households water treatment tablets to improve water storage and treatment systems to the women and children admitted for SAM/MAM and other affected household in project locations. Distribution of soaps to increase hygiene practices and reduces diseases. Construction and rehabilitation of latrines or sanitation infrastructures, installation of hand washing stations in nutrition and health facilities in all locations. champion hygiene promotion by training community hygiene promoters in all locations. Hope Restoration South Sudan (HRSS) will establish or strengthen complaint feedbacks desk in efforts to track beneficiaries’ feedback on concerns related to satisfaction or protection and establish AAP committee to enhance community participation or population groups in the humanitarian response. This involves community awareness training to beneficiaries, conducted Gender Based Violence GBV by female WASH assistant.

2 - Project activities

Activity 1.1.1 : Community consultative meetings: Hope Restoration South Sudan (HRSS) and other static partners, will conduct community consultative meetings with community members drawn from the population groups to participate in the consultative meetings in a pursuance to establish vulnerability and identify the project beneficiaries. Population groups represented in these consultations, will comprise of : Local authorities, women leaders, representatives of religious groups, representatives of persons living with disability, representatives of persons living with chronic illness or HIV&AIDS, persons without effective community links, members of affected households, chiefs, teachers, These meetings, will inform the project team to understand the community dynamics. These community leaders, will serve as focal and entry point to the project locations. Hope Restoration South Sudan (HRS) will use these consultative meetings to mobilize and sensitize community about the project scope. Meetings are also mandatory to justify the core pipeline request, which is very crucial to WASH cluster and core pipeline agencies for the purpose of Core pipeline approval. Activity 1.1.2: Formation and training of AAP committee: Hope Restoration South Sudan (HRSS) will establish, train Accountability to the affected population AAP committee in all locations. This committee members work as focal points to the vulnerability locations. It helps the project team to identify need and address vulnerability also establish project beneficiaries. Activity 1.1.3: Establishment and strengthening of complaint feedback desks: Hope Restoration South Sudan (HRSS) will establish or strengthen existing complaints feedback desks in all location of the intervention. Complaint feedback desk will inform the team about the level of satisfaction and dissatisfaction served from the targeted population or population groups or response modality. Activity 1.1.4: Distribution of households water storage minimum packages, water treatment tablets to the women and children admitted for SAM/MAM and other affected persons and fetching water from untreated sources: Hope Restoration South Sudan (HRSS) will conduct distribution of households water storage and water treatment table; Aqua tabs, PUR sachets and water filter clothes to the affected households in the areas highlighted and to the women and children admitted for SAM/MAM in Canal Pigi county, Fangak, Ayod, Malakal, Panyikang, Rubkona and Baliet counties and other locations targeted in the project such as; Koch and and Panyijiar counties Activity 1.1.5 :construction and rehabilitation sanitation facilities latrines in health and nutrition facilities: Hope Restoration South Sudan (HRSS) will construct and rehabilitate 45 latrines in efforts to improve access to the dignified sanitation and elimination practices of Open Defecations along with its predisposing factors. Activity 1.1.6: Installation of 45 hand washing stations in nutrition facilities: Hope Restoration South Sudan (HRSS) will install (45) hand washing stations in Baliet, Panyikang, Fangak, Malakal Akobo, Rubkona and Ayod 5 each location Gender separated in attempt to improve of affected persons, and in response to reduceCOVID-19 transmission. Activity 1.1.7 : Recruitment and training of 90 community hygiene promoters: Hope Restoration South Sudan (HRSS) will recruit and train( 90 ) hygiene promoters with 10 promoters drawn in each location total (90) promoters, to conduct regular hygiene promotions in public places such as: Nutrition facility, health facilities, markets and schools in compliance with COVID-19 WHO protocol Activity 1.1.8 :Awareness sessions with population groups and partners in all locations: Hope Restoration South Sudan (HRSS) will conduct training to increase awareness of community representatives etc in all areas targeted by the project, in order to identify vulnerability and understand needs in most apparent manner.

The Complaint feedback desk, will inform the team about the level of satisfaction and dissatisfaction served during the response. This includes response modalities, selection of distribution sites, Time and date. The safety audits and tools employed. whether the installation of handwashing facilities was installed and with soaps chlorinated water in the distribution sites in response to COVID-19. Hiring crowd controllers to separate the queues between men, women, Girls and boys in efforts to mitigate the GBV concerns against women and girls receiving WASHNFIS. This means distribution sites shouldn't be planned to take place near the military barracks or locations proximity to the armed groups. Therefore the feedback will be used to strengthen the referral pathways and make them clear. We will use the feedback to include the beneficiaries in the design and delivery while considering the culture, political dynamics, language barriers
