1 - Activities

Proposed Objectives 1. To build livelihoods resilience for communities to unforeseen shocks 2. To disseminate information on health and hygiene mainstreaming COVID19 response and gender3. To impart skills and knowledge on COVID19 pandemic mitigation options Outcome 1: Messages are clear and relevant:Examples of interventions include: • Consolidate and disseminate behavior change messages through podcasts, pamphlets /fliers ensuring messages that target women as well as men across all wards • Facilitate Health and hygiene promotion to all project wards • Resuscitate 120 health clubs as well as capacity building for 150 village health workers in all the project wards Outcome 2: People have the resources to practice good hygiene: Activities• Training and support with starter up material grants for 30 youth groups for locally-based manufacturing of detergents, sanitizers, and home-made masks.• Technical support for farmers to establish hygiene enabling facilities (tippy taps, footbaths, rubbish pits).• Procure and distribute materials for repair of 40 boreholes (e.g. hand pumps ) • Procure and install a minimum of 1 standpipe per garden and irrigation site to enable abstraction of safe water for domestic use by project beneficiaries and surrounding communities. Outcome 3: Key workers stay safe and healthy:Activities• Support for 20 ward-based clinics with PPE, safe water, ablution, and solar lighting • Procuring and distributing PPEs (soap, hand sanitizers, and masks) for health workers, farmer markets representatives and maintenance committee teams• Procure and distribute safety equipment to support the 320 key community activists (lead farmers, community representatives) to stay safe while continuing their rolesOutcome 4: Food production and distribution is maintained and reaches the most vulnerable:Food production is maintained at levels that ensure food security especially for the vulnerable Activities• Vouchers for production input starter packs for 400 farmer groups and Income Generating Projects (IGP) also targeting youth groups (these packs will include seeds, fertility amendments, poultry feed)• Vouchers for small scale post-harvest handling equipment for 30 farmer groups (e.g. solar driers)

2 - Indicators

1. 40 000 people (8 000 HH) in approximately 40 wards of rural Chimanimani and Mutare receive and use the right behavior change communications on COVID-19 hygiene and social distancing, based on Ministry of Health and Child welfare (MOHCW) and WHO best practice. 2. 40 000 households in 40 wards of rural Chimanimani and Mutare have access to and use soap, water, hygiene, and cleaning products to maintain good hygiene. 3. Systems are in place to protect 280 farmer markets representatives who visit the market place to deliver produce and procure inputs4. Systems are in place to protect 320 key workers providing rural health and lead farmers who offer maintenance support to irrigation sites as well as the extension workers to enable their work to safely continue 5. 12 000 farmers receive seeds and inputs to enable food production and post-harvest handling to reduce losses to continue
