Project Summary

Through this project, PUI contributes to improve living conditions of affected population through responding to their needs of basic services, in line with SO1 Provide lifesaving and life sustaining shelter & NFI support & SO2 Contribute towards the resilience and cohesion of communities and household by improving housing, and related community/public services, infrastructure and facilities.
In full coordination with stakeholders and in line with HNO 2020 raised needs and shelter severity scale, PUI field team conducted assessment visits between November 2019- January 2020 where they highlighted that needs in Deir Ez Zor (Deir Ez Zor city), Aleppo (Jabal Samaan), Rural Damascus (Qatana-Darayya), Rural Lattakia (Rabea-Qastal Maaf), Daraa (Izraa-Daraa), Homs (Mahin) and Hama (Suran-Harbanifse,-Asalamyeh) are still high. As for the current change of context, the possibility of access to new locations and the willingness of many displaced families to return back to their original areas, PUI still aims to target those areas although it and other partners have already conducted different types of shelter activities in several locations in the mentioned governorates during the last three years.
Consequently and through this project, PUI plans to cover the most essential shelter needs for 6,000 vulnerable individuals, desegregated to males, females, boys and girls targeting IDPs and returnees through rehabilitation of damaged houses and emergency shelter solutions in the abovementioned governorates.
A total of 1,000 partially damaged houses (approximately 5,000 individuals) will be targeted under the shelter long-term solutions (owner oriented response) and 1,000 individuals (200 displaced families) will be targeted under collective shelter rehabilitation.

4 - Needs

During assessment phase, PUI team in coordination with local partners observed that households cannot carry out necessary repairs to their homes, most frequently because they cannot afford the materials or professional assistance required to carry out the rehabilitation works, as well as due to a lack of skilled labour.The overall impact of the lack of income and high prices resulted in a big number of households living in rented accommodation not being able to afford rent costs, leading to increased vulnerability.Highlighting that potential displacement from different areas of conflict are expected, especially from northeast areas to safer areas such as Aleppo, north Hama and Deir-Ez-Zor, hence needs for emergency shelter response are included in PUI 2020 programming. . PUI will coordinate with the municipalities, Governorates and MoLAE to identify the shelter needs of each area. The response could include rehabilitation of collective shelters, light shelter rehabilitation and repair of damaged houses.During the selection of beneficiaries, PUI will give priority to the FHH, elderlies and people with disabilities, considering gender-segregated needs. PUI will identify the needs of the shelters in consultation with the owners and beneficiaries in order to bring the living spaces as per the guideline and minimum standards agreed by the shelter sector.After selecting qualified contractor, PUI monitoring team will visit the shelter on regular basis to ensure the work is implemented as per the agreed specification and standards.PUI will explain the scope of rehabilitation to the beneficiaries and local stakeholders and rehabilitated apartment will be handed over to the beneficiaries in the presence of PUI monitoring staff and local partners.

5 - Implementation of activities

PUI will contribute to SO1 Provide life-saving and life-sustaining shelter and NFI support through the implementation of rehabilitation of collective centres and transitional shelters(one shelter in Tal Daman / Aleppo,1,000 IDPs with budget of 160,000 USD).Through this intervention, PUI will rehabilitate the shelter with the following works: Civil works (temporary wooden doors, windows and partitions) Sanitary works (maintenance or creation of WATSAN facilities) Electrical works (maintenance of the electrical network, LED systems) Any civil/sanitary works required for persons with disabilities or elderly populations; Safety/protection woks for the common spaces in the building (roof parapet, staircase door +handrail + windows) and additional specified items for people with special needs or elderly populations. In addition, PUI will contribute to SO2 Reinforce an enabling protection environment and cohesion of communities by improving housing and related community/public infrastructure through the implementation of:• Support to sustainable repair/rehabilitate housing and related community/public infrastructure and facilities to owners/tenants/returnees/host families through rehabilitation of damaged houses (Rehabilitation of 1,000 Partially damaged houses: 1000 x 3000 = 3,000,000 USD). Based on the Minimum Standards and Guidelines, through the intervention PUI will rehabilitate: (1) Kitchen (1) Bathroom (2) Additional rooms selected by the household Communal Works within the building including: Closure of all facade opening (i.e. windows) within the unit, but using varied materials, protection works (Main Door, Door for the roof, Fence for the stairs)

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

1.Eviction from camps/transit sites/spontaneous settlement due to lack of approval from property owner/conflict on property ownership-Likelihood:High-Impact:High-Mitigation measures:Work closely with local authorities to ensure there is a written approval from owner for specified period to use the property to develop the site/Advocate with local authorities to facilitate approval for spontaneous settlement and to find alternative accommodations/sites for those at risk of eviction.2.Increased risk of inter or intra-community tension due to varied assistance packages, unclear beneficiary selection criteria or poorly timed distributions leading to a feeling of discrimination-/Likelihood:High-Impact:Medium-Mitigation measures:Standardise assistance package with guidelines followed by all sector partners/Ensure beneficiary selection criteria is widely communicated within a community/Engage with community leaders, to raise awareness in the community/Establish complaint mechanism at community level.3.Crowded living conditions and lack of lighting and gender segregated sanitation facilities can lead to increase in GBV and sexual exploitation-Likelihood:High-Impact:High-Mitigation measures:Advocate for provision of alternative accommodation/Provision of gender segregated mass shelter and facilities in case of large displacement/Work closely with protection and other sectors to mitigate any issue/Extending consultations with all groups of population including women, girls and PWD.4.Safety of beneficiaries/communities are at risk due to structural integrity of buildings and/or the close presence of ERWs to potential work sites-Likelihood:High-Impact:High-Mitigation measures:Qualified personnel to assess the building structure/ Repair/rehabilitate the buildings only if it is structurally safe/ Work closely with parties involved in ERW clearance to ensure that the area is free from ERW/Orientation on ERW risks and asking to report on any suspicious object found during work.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk: Local population does not accepts PUI´s intervention/ Mitigation measures: PUI will ensure the full engagement of the community leaders and local stakeholders in project design and well introduce the benefits to the targeted communityRisk: Late return movements/ Mitigation measures: PUI follows a multi sector integrated approach in programming to support the community resilience in a multi dimensions.Risk: Delay and change in the approval process/ Mitigation measures: Close follow-up with the stakeholder and take anticipatory approach.Risk: Volatile security situation/ Mitigation measures: close follow-up with the stakeholder and locals to anticipate in advance any deterioration in the security situation/conduct security training for the newly recruited staff to raise their awareness/ensure the circulation of the security SOPs within the staff.Risk: Fluctuation in the exchange rate/ Mitigation measures: Constant update and discussion with all stakeholders of the project to anticipate all possible scenario and mitigate the effect of the SYP fluctuation on the project.Risk: Lack of qualified contractor/ Mitigation measures: Conduct constant survey in order to expand the list of qualified contractors working with PUI and adopt Framework Contract mechanism.
