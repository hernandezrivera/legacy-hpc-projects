Project Summary

The proposed intervention meets the needs of the vulnerable groups especially IDPs in settlements aligned with strategic objective SO1 by Provide life-saving and life-sustaining humanitarian assistance to the most vulnerable people with an emphasis on those in areas with high severity of needs. MWL will implement the project in 12 informal camps in Afrin sub-districts. The project aims to respond to 2100 HHs with a total persons 10,500 disaggregated roughly as (2,205 Men/ 2,415 Women/ 2,835 Boys/ 3,045 Girls) including 500 people with disabilities of the most vulnerable conflict-affected IDPs, and host communities.
MWL will take into account to prioritize the following:
* Households headed by female, child, disabled, or elderly,
* Households with severely ill or disabled members
* Children below 5, elderly, pregnant or lactating women; with special attention to those recently displaced or living overcrowded
by providing life-saving and life sustaining shelter & NFI assistance through the following activities:
1- Provide 2000 HHs (10,000 persons) with seasonal and supplementary NFIs assistance including winter clothing, stoves, and fuel in Afrin sub-districts camps. Each household will receive voucher of 150$ and prioritize its needs of these items accordingly. The distribution will take place in 10 camps as following:

4 - Needs

Based on 2nd allocation paper 2021, the situation in NWS remains grim, with 3.4 million persons in need of humanitarian aid. Out of the total 4.4 million persons residing in NWS, over 70 per cent are considered people in acute need of life-saving assistance. Compared to 2020, the number of people in need has increased by almost 20 per cent. Since the inception of the war, a majority of the IDPs have experienced repeated and protracted displacement and see their living conditions worsened by flooding and other harsh weather conditions. Women and children are disproportionally affected and overrepresented in the statistics. Families are crammed in flimsy tents in valleys that flood, or on rocky hillsides, which makes for unhospitable living.MWL's MEAL team conducted need assessment in 12 camps in Afrin sub-district using key informative meetings with camps managers and focused-group discussions with people living in camps. Out of findings, all the winterization materials considered as priority for them as: 39% needs heaters works with pomace, 29% are in need for NFI items such carpets, blankets, and tents, 18% in need of heaters works with diesel, 13% in need of winter clothes.
