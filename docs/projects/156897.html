Project Summary

The project aims to assist the affected people on northern of Aleppo governorate (Afrin-Al Bab- Aghtrin - Mare' - A’rima Sub-districts) as following:
1. Provision 36000 cooking gas cylinders for 3000 households (18000 individuals), where each household will get one cooking gas cylinder on monthly bases for 12 months.
2. Provision 800000 liters of heating fuel (diesel) for 8000 households (48000 individuals), where each household will get 100 liters of diesel during the winter.
3. Rehabilitation 500 shelter units for 500 households (3000 individuals) depending (HLP) and Shelter guideline.
The activities 1 and 2 are relevant with Sector Objective 1, while the activity 3 is relevant with Sector Objective 2.

Implementation of activities

1. Provision 36000 cooking gas cylinders (1.1.1 Provision of core and essential NFIs):Field team will coordinate with local councils as well as will conduct the verification visits to select the most vulnerability 3000 households (18000 individuals), priority will be given to female-headed households which should be 15% at least of total beneficiaries.Each household will get one cooking gas cylinder every month for 12 months. SRD will procure cooking gas from local markets inside Syria depending on open tender.Indicators:No. of people whose cooking gas needs are met.% of female-headed households from total beneficiary households.% of NFI distributions have separate lines for men and women. 2. Provision 800000 litres of heating fuel-diesel (1.2.1 Provision of seasonal and supplementary NFIs including heating fuel):SRD will coordinate with SNFI cluster and local councils as well as will conduct the verification visits to select the most vulnerability 8000 households (48000 individuals), where each household will get 100 litres of diesel, priority will be given to IDPs households which should be 80% at least of total beneficiaries.SRD will procure heating fuel from local markets inside Syria depending on open tender.Indicators:No. of people whose heating fuel needs are met.% of IDPs households from total beneficiary households.% of winter clothing kits distributions have female staff present. 3. Rehabilitation 500 shelter units for 500 households (3000 individuals) depending (HLP) and Shelter guideline (2.1.1 Rehabilitation of damaged houses):SRD is implementing partner of OCHA and has eligibility to submit proposals in GMS.SRD has implemented three shelter rehabilitation projects, one for OCHA and two for WVI-T.SRD plans to submit rehabilitation project proposal on the HF allocation of 2019 to rehab 500 shelter units that will match the new allocation paper of SNFI cluster.Field team will select the shelter units that meet the HLP requirements, then technical assessment of shelter units will be conduct to prepare the BoQs and select the contractors as well as activate the Cash for work component through hiring the workers to contribute on repair works, in other words, some rehabilitation works will be done by contractors as construction and carpentry works, the others by workers such as plumbing and electrical wiring.Since the technical assessment takes time, it is planned to start repair before the assessment is completed, in order to invest the time well, where work will be done simultaneously in parallel between assessment and rehabilitation.Indicators:No. of people assisted with durable shelter solutions.% of shelter interventions that incorporate requisite HLP components, analysis and/or activities.% of shelter interventions that consider privacy for women and girls.No. of construction workers employed.

Protection Risk Analysis

Prioritise safety & dignity and avoid causing harm  Ensure the location of and access routes to distribution points and services are safe with enough space for orderly distribution. Ensure the mode and frequency of distribution minimises safety risk – be aware that beneficiaries may face theft, intimidation, sexual assault and extortion whilst taking their rations home. Ensure the ration size is appropriate for beneficiaries to be able to carry home with no additional physical or financial burden and without being dependent on others to carry it for them. Design and construction of shelters must preserve the safety and dignity of the affected populations: Take into account the land tenure arrangements of IDPs, including statutory/legislative and customary access rights to land and inheritance rights. Advocate with land and property owners and local authorities to avoid eviction or disproportional rising in rent. Provide solar lamps/torches for each household in settings where lighting of communal areas is not available. Avoid community tensions, by providing the local/host community with access to camp services and assistance in situations where they are experiencing similar shortages of non-food items. Keep lists of beneficiaries and personal data confidential and safe from unintended use - apply effective data protection measures. Work in coordination with protection actors to put in place measures to prevent and respond to physical/sexual violence arising due to crowded shelter conditions. Promote equitable and impartial access for all Collect and use data disaggregated by sex, age and diversity to effectively inform programming. Ensure that assistance and services are reaching the most vulnerable - Identify and prioritise the most vulnerable groups in the community and prevent discrimination or exclusion of marginalised groups (refer to Vulnerable Categories guidance note). Ensure the location, distance and access routes to the distribution points is accessible to all members of the community, including the most vulnerable groups. Ensure that NFI packages include suitable items to cover the specific needs of women, girls, older persons (eg. sanitary pads, adult diapers) and provide additional clothing and blankets for the frail and weak.  Make infrastructure adaptions to shelter structures to make them user-friendly to persons with reduced mobility (eg. persons with physical disability, older persons). Assist physically impaired groups transport shelter materials and assemble their own shelters. Issue ration cards to female-headed households, child-headed households and unaccompanied children in their own name.  Consider the gender balance of distribution teams and committees to ensure that there are enough women available as a contact point for women in the community. Establish mechanisms to give priority to those who cannot stand in line for long periods, such as older persons, persons with disability, unaccompanied children, pregnant and lactating women. Ensure that beneficiaries know how to obtain shelter and NFI assistance, where and when the distributions will take place, what they will get and for how long it should last and how to use the items. Ensure accountability to affected populations Be transparent with the affected populations by providing them with accessible and timely information on selection criteria for targeted assistance, organisational procedures and processes that affect them. Set up accessible, confidential and well understood feedback mechanisms for suggestions and complaints with a view to improve programming, understand community perceptions, promote beneficiary empowerment and assist in detecting misconduct. Eg. place feedback surveys in NFI kits, feedback box at distribution site, phone lines.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

1. Provision 36000 cooking gas cylinders (1.1.1 Provision of core and essential NFIs):The Risks and Assumptions:SRD plans to procure the gas from local market vendors, but the source of gas will be from Turkey, the closure of the border if happens will deplete of the market and inflate of the prices. Mitigating Measures:- Monitoring the situation of cross border and the movement of goods by coordination with logistic cluster.- Ensuring that there are reserve stock of gas cylinders (at least for one month) in SRD warehouses taking into account safety and security conditions.- Find alternative sources of cooking fuel in local markets- Conduct a strong and constant market monitoring before starting any local procurement- Keep the prices of the market monitored with short and periodic frequency. 2. Provision 800000 liters of heating fuel-diesel (1.2.1 Provision of seasonal and supplementary NFIs including heating fuel):The Risks and Assumptions(1):Diesel fuel available in local markets is poor quality, and its combustion when used in heating causes the emission of toxic and harmful gases.Mitigating Measures:- Contracting with reputable,well-known suppliers in local markets.- Our field team will conduct periodic and surprise tests prior to purchase and distribution to ensure fuel quality and conform to specificationsThe Risks and Assumptions(2):SRD plans to procure the diesel from local market vendors inside Syria ,the indirect purchase of conflict associated resources as diesel fuel might involuntary contribute to conflict.Mitigating Measures:- Conducting (Anti Terrorism) check for suppliers before contracting them.- Monitor the lines of supply of the fuel and track the source down and ensure fuel is not procured from parties involved in the conflict- Explore more eco-friendly and conflict-free available fuel or hear resource types. 3. Rehabilitation 500 shelter units for 500 households (3000 individuals) depending (HLP) and Shelter guideline (2.1.1 Rehabilitation of damaged houses):The Risks and Assumptions(1):Eviction from shelter due to lack of approval from property owner and/or exposing beneficiaries at risk due to lack of HLP documentation for the shelter rehabilitated. Mitigating Measures:- Abide by the HLP guidance document that was developed by the SNFI Cluster- Do not proceed with the Shelter rehabilitation unless with the consensus of the owner, proof of registered HLP ownership, verified ownership claims. If this won’t be available, feasible, refer to the HLP Due Diligence Guidelines.- Include HLP caveat into the MoUs or agreements signedThe Risks and Assumptions(2):Risk of conducting rehabilitation of building with poor structural integrity.Mitigating Measure:- Conduct a thorough shelter assessment and infrastructural technical assessment of the buildings that are aimed to be rehabilitated- Coordinate the interventions with local authorities that might be aware of security issues in the neighborhood.
