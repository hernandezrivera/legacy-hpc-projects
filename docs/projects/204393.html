Project Summary

WASH is one of the most important sectors in humanitarian field and by provision of services will open the path for other humanitarian services, Sudan is one of the countries, that facing a big problems in its security situation which caused different obstacles in providing humanitarian assistance for vulnerable populations, since the last conflict and clashes between RSF and SAF on 15th of April 2023 in Khartoum and different States that resulted in a widespread destruction of public facilities, properties, losing of lives and deterioration on Security situation. Also, the life cycle is stopped due to lack of the basic services,
Ongoing hostilities have severely hampered efforts to mobilize and respond. Humanitarian access and protection of civilians are critical pre-requisites for the scale up of the WASH response. Safe access will be negotiated for technicians and engineers to water treatment/supply sites (facilities) for repairs or rehabilitation. The looting and damage to the premises of tech-nical institutions and civil society organizations has and will significantly impact the capacity to respond. Vehicles, drilling rigs, offices and equip-ment have been lost in Darfur states. Funding gaps, non-functional telecommunication and banking systems, inflation, fuel shortages, and displaced humanitarian staff have severely hindered operations. Maintaining the extensive water supply network will be challenging due to inability of the Government’s Water Supply Units to generaterevenue. Additional funding and solid negotiation expertise will support the implementation ofthe response.
Due to the usage of heavy weapons including air strikes and fighting in the residential areas, water stations, electricity stations, petrol stations, telecommunication and other public and private services providers were either damaged or significantly interrupted . At the start of the conflict in Nayala the fighting was heavy and the main market has caught fire and destroyed.
South Darfur was one of the affected States that suffered from the armed conflict and this affection resulted in looting of properties, Women’s Violation, killing of civilians and thousands wounded, among them (women, children, men, youth, and elderly people, PSN). Since then, almost two months and there is no change in the situation therefore the assumption of high risk is remained because of escalation of the two conflicting parties. The project interventions will empower the General protection services in South Darfur state; Elgossa Charity Organization for Rural Development (ECORD) is proposing to improve the provision life-saving activities and access to safe environment for people, thus increasing accessibility of affected population to live with dignity. This in turn will ensure a smooth and continuous flow, while keeping people away from the danger caused by conflict and expose to explosive bodies. The project proposed interventions will enable Elgossa Charity Organization for Rural Development (ECORD) to obtain the key objectives of protection services in emergency situation through integrated interventions targeting vulnerable communities in Beleil, Nyala Shimal and Nyala Janoub Localities. ECORD believes that tackling issues through strengthening the capacity development of existing structures while increasing awareness among communities, and enhancing monitoring and linkages and integration to service provision for individual, households and community levels.
Through this proposed sector interventions, ECORD is hoping that by using a combined approach to solve the challenges faced by communities in Beleil, Nyala Shimal, and Nyala Janoub in South Darfur State, ECORD will provide much needed lifesaving services, and set a stable basis for their sustainability through community outreach, appointment and finally adoption of key lessons for better self-reliance. ECORD will contribute to the WASH main Objectives in emergency.

1 - Needs

According to Sudanese Development Call Organization (NIDAA)Rapid assessment report for conflict in Sudan between the Sudanese Armed Forces(SAF) and the Rapid Support Forces (RSF) , on 22nd of May, 2023Sudanese Development Call Organization (NIDAA) .Water is still a challenge, till this day after five weeks of the conflict there are many areas in Nyala of capital South Darfur State without water (Nyala Shimal and others Areas). The drinking water is not safe as the stations needs maintenance. There is huge amounts of waste in the streets and garbage besides there are still in many areas unburden dead bodies (mainly soldiers) could not be reached due to continuous fighting, which resulted in offensive odor, and dangerous sanitation hazards, in particular with the coming rainy season. Many water stations suffered from the electricity cut, people bought fuel by community support to make it work from time to time, but still there is great need for maintenance and electricity supply. at least 700 people civilians have been killed and over 5,500 have been injured because of the conflict between SAF and RSF, as of 13 May. Active fighting is taking place in densely populated areas violating International Humanitarian Law principles of discrimination and proportionality without due consideration to the safety of the civilian population. There have been widespread indiscriminate attacks targeting civilian in restructure including markets and hospitals, as well as looting of homes, belongings, and humanitarian offices/assets in different parts of Sudan. Criminality is reportedly increasing. This portrays the breakdown of the rule of law and protection of civilian mechanisms. Similarly as the situation is terrible in Khartoum and may States, also South Darfur state is affected in the ongoing armed conflict and clashes and as a result of the fighting many civilians properties have been looted, Organization’s Offices and stocks were totally looted, governmental institutions were destroyed, which made the situation of humanitarian actors very difficult to provide services to vulnerable and most affected population, moreover there were civilians displacement from their locations to other locations, the displacement spread to include some South Darfur localities such as Beleil locality and other areas, which caused the population to be in IDPs camp and other left their homes to other safety places.Moreover Base on HRP to that WASH needs have increased significantly in urban centers, which were very thinly covered under the 2023 HRP initially. The bulk water treatment and supply systems that can reach a million people are not functional in South Darfur State and other conflict-affected states. Existing displaced persons, including the most vulnerable residents, and an increased number of displaced people in new locations, including international borders, area matter of significant concern. The already limited water sources are under extreme pressure due to increased population concentration in new IDP concentration points, which carries inter-communal tensions and conflict risks; skyrocketing cost of fuel has resulted in a300 to 400 per cent increase in the operation of fuel-reliant water supply systems as well water trucking. The rainy season will further heighten the risk of outbreaks in the coming months. Water-borne diseases and epidemics are a threat due to the deteriorating WASH situation, including no waste disposal services, water safety, poor hygiene, disruption of the supply chain, and access concerns. Also the clashes resulted in spreading of reckless shootings that killed many civilians and other were seriously injured, in addition to houses damages and burning of properties, all these factors added the load on the economic situation which resulted in deterioration of the most vulnerable and affected population’s life, during the conflict there were assessments have been conducted by some NGOs and the situation of South Darf

2 - Activities/Outputs

So, for 6 months- Provision of cleaning tools, PPE, disinfection and operational support for environmental cleaning at health/nutrition care facilities (6 labour plus cleaning kit includes PPEs).
