Project Summary

The intervention will target food insecure households in selected districts in IPC level 3 and 4 where Plan International has presence. This will be achieved through the provision of monthly electronic cash transfers which will enable targeted households to purchase food by distributing an equivalent to US$10 for a household that has one member and USD$9 per member of a household that has two or more over a period of five months as per the cash working group recommendation. A total of 20 000 beneficiaries will be reached out to from an estimated 4000 households across the districts. Working with the Ministry of social welfare who has the database of vulnerable child headed families and the CPCs, deliberate targeting of the child headed families will be done with assistance from the community leadership. This will ensure that they are not left out as they may be attending school. Proxies will be allocated to the make sure that the children are not skipped by information. The transfer value is expected to enable households to purchase cereal, pulses and oils for their basic food needs. Electronic cash transfers distributions will be done through the mobile money modality. To ensure that beneficiaries maintain their food rations in this economy of high inflation, the interbank rate will be used for the budgeted hard currency to cater for the price changes. Post distribution tools will be developed to determine the effects of the food insecurity situation on the rights of the children especially girls as well as the benefits of the project implementation on bridging the game. The Post distribution Monitoring will be done two weeks after each distribution cycle to assess utilisation and monitor and other protection issues arising from project implementation. Cash transfers will be carried out for a total of four months taking the season to the month of April where the next agricultural harvests are expected to take on the food requirements of the targeted households. The post distribution will validate the level of satisfaction of the targeted beneficiaries from the intervention by measuring the ability to purchase desired balanced diet food items from the distributed cash. Market monitoring will also tack the market performance following cash injection into the community to ensure that value for money is achieved and beneficiaries are able to access all the food items they desire at the expected costs. In a bid to improve the nutritional status for young children, the project will undertake borehole rehabilitation or drilling and support nutritional gardens targeting vulnerable households. Training on agronomic practices will be conducted as well as cooking practices that preserves nutritional value.

1 - Activities

The project will carry out the following activities;1. Provision of monthly electronic cash transfers. An equivalent to US$10 for a household that has one member and USD$9 per member of a household that has two or more will be distributed over a period of four months as per the cash working group recommendation. A total of 20 000 beneficiaries will be reached out from an estimated 4000 households across the districts were Plan is currently working. Distributions will be done via mobile phones. Market monitoring will also track the market performance following cash injection into the community to ensure that value for money is achieved and beneficiaries are able to access all the food items they desire at the expected costs. To ensure that beneficiaries maintain their food rations in this economy of high inflation, the inter bank rate will be used for the budgeted hard currency to cater for the price changes2.Rehabilitation or drilling of boreholes to establish and support nutritional gardens. This activity will levarage on the already existing developmental projects that Plan International is already carrying out in the selected districts. Plan will support establishment of nutritional gardens either at schools or in communities and support with training of beneficiaries on climate smart agronomic practices. In addition cooking practices that retains nutritional value of food will be conducted.
