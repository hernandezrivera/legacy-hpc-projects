Project Summary

The project aims to increase access to safe water supply, hygiene promotion and sanitation improvement services aimed to provide health and contribute to reducing mortality and morbidity rate caused by high incidences of water borne diseases outbreak in the Prone and vulnerable communities in Morobo county through new boreholes drilling, repairs and rehabilitation) increased household rural water treatment using Pur/Aquatabs, hygiene promotion sensitization at household and institutional levels, improved safe sanitation practices awareness, disinfection, WASH NFIs support and monitoring the gradual progress in hygiene and safe sanitation practices.

1 - Needs

Based on the Food Security and Nutrition Monitoring System (July/August 2018); 29% of the population only has access to a borehole, tap stand or water yard within a maximum 30minute distance without facing protection concerns; 90% of the population at least practices open defecation, either because they do not have access to or are not accustomed to using a basic sanitation facility; 13% of the population only has access to WASH NFIs such as Jerry cans, soap and mosquito nets; 75% of the population reported households with members that had been self-diagnosed with a water-borne illness in the in the previous two-week period; and 5.7million most vulnerable are in urgent need of WASH services (2019 HNO/HRP). To reduce the mortality and morbidity rate caused by high incidences of water borne diseases outbreak specifically cholera and diarrhea in the identified prone and vulnerable communities. To improve hygiene status, general health and reduce the risk of waterborne diseases among the vulnerable population through hygiene promotion, scaling up improved drinking water source use and consumption and reduce level of open defecation at household levels through safe human waste disposal methods. To ensure the distribution of water treatment supplements and training of under served households with safe water sources on safe/proper water chain management to reduce the risk of water borne disease outbreak. Recruitment of local volunteers, training and creation of hygiene awareness messages and sessions will ensure increased practices of appropriate hygiene behaviors.

2 - Activities

The project shall ensure the drilling of 10 hand pump boreholes and repairs and rehabilitation of 30 broken hand pumps/boreholes to serve 10,000 people (Internally Displaced Persons and returnees in Cholera prone areas) with safe clean water, and establish and operationalize their Water Management Committees to ensure the sustainable use and management of water infrastructure. Scale up hygiene promotion activities through the training of 50 voluntary hygiene promoters from within the communities to sensitize on proper hygiene awareness and practices, and ensure the sharing of improved hygiene knowledge and information to ensure households adopt good practices and have improved health.
