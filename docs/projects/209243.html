Project Summary

Across the five Darfur states the overall humanitarian and protection environment is extremely worrying. As the escalation in violence between Sudan Armed Forces SAF and Rapid Support Forces RSF Spreads though Sudan.
Its Known that Sudan conflict ignited on 15th April 2023 among Sudanese Army Forces SAF, those divided into two parts SAF & RSF Rapid Support Forces, in A capital of Sudan Khartoum left a huge damages and more that 3.3 million based on UN-OCHA have been displaced across the country millions of Sudanese were exposed to internal & External Displacement some were being seeking for refugee in neighbors countries, Egypt, Libya, Chad, South Sudanese, IDPs and refugees were looking for a safe areas where can feel away of death, injuries, this clashes of two pats SAF & RSF destroyed all Sudan premises, Sudan system as a country, using of heavy weapons, Airplane fighters inside Khartoum directly shooting to civilians across city left thousands of death, injuries forced displacement, children, women, men were being forced to seek other safe place inside Sudan states in Aljazeera, Sinnar, Damazin, Kassala, Al Gadarif Port Sudan, Shandee, Atbara, While this conflict became more affection Darfur states as one of the fragile and margin region since 2003 up to 2023 over than 20 years living in exceptional and critical situation, the extension of this conflict again affected the region in Alfashir, Al Guniena, Zalingi, Nyala and Al Daien with huge damages left in Death, injuries and force migration to close nighbor countries Such as Chad, Libya and same repeated inter displacement to East Darfur state. lack of services such food security, health services, due to critical security deterioration in the region and limited humanitarian services due to continuation fighting and blocked of airports across Sudan except port Sudan represent the capital of Sudan have access to receive humanitarian assistances then will coordinate with local government to dispatch those through tide covey from Port Sudan to other limited areas.
Humanitarian Aid commission in East Darfur was reported a massive influx of internal displaced persons in the state, according to the assessment conducted in July 2023 by WVI indicated.
more than 83008 people arrived to ED state, 37353 children, 29053 women and 16602 men based on inter- Agency assessment done led by World Vision Organization WVI and 15 of partners were participated from different UN OCHA, INNGOs, and NNGOs the report reflected the negative situation that IDPs living and suffering a lot and lack of humanitarian services, chronic illness, mal-nutrition, diarrhea , malaria, Typhoid, diabetic and other illness within women, children and men, the limitation food security in ED and burnt of big food storage contributed to shortage of food, ALsawaid Alkhadara Organization strive to provide services to those vulnerable communities in East Darfur state in particular, Al Dain & Alfardos localities to address their needs in protection, these affected community expected to face challenges, issues and violations such GBV/SGBV, rape cases, discriminations, exploitation, harassment, abuse, neglecting and Unaccompanied And Separated UASC Children and un- employment and all forms of violations as long as they had forcipate to leave their home/town. Through this angle Alsawaid Alkhadara Organization will dedicate efforts by proposed activities to response to the community needs in coordination with stakeholders in ED.
minimum protection standards should require in supporting those based on the needs identified through assessment protection issues mainly the vulnerable community needs to be aware of Child protection, formation and train protection networks, Sextual Gender Based Violence SGBV & Gender Base Violence GBV through capacity building should be addressed, awareness raising for child protection networks on referral pathways, community feedback, case management and Unaccompanied And Separated Children UASC

4.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will support Inter-Agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including Sexual exploitation and abuse complaints?

First and foremost, AAO is accountable to the children it serves. As such, child participation is a core guiding principle in all AAO interventions. It is in fact a basic child right which should be upheld at each stage of program me implementation. Children who will benefit from this intervention, their families and the communities will be involved in a series of consultations and dialogues with AAO and its implementing partners to influence the implementation of the proposed intervention. For example, AAO will ensure transparency, communication and feedback to the beneficiaries, consultation with beneficiaries, including community leaders and representation of different groups (women, youth, and ethnic minorities) during the selection of sites for construction of any infrastructure. Also, AAO will closely monitor the implementation of the intervention through its staff based in the field offices and in Eldaein, who will be conducting field visits. In 2015, AAO adopted PSEA, received a training on PSEA. Regular AAO internal as well as external audits will be carried out to ensure procedures are followed
