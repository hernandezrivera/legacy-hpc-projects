Project Summary

SOS Sahel Sudan has significant experiences in WASH interventions, (water Sanitation and hygiene program) in the project areas through its partnership with different donors in the tow targeted States (North Kordofan and North Darfur). Particularly in the two targeted localities of Shiekan in NKS state and Alfashir in NDS where the gap concerning WASH services is still huge which need more efforts and interventions. SOS Sahel proposes for this project is to support crisis-affected vulnerable populations to have equitable and affordable access to essential water for drinking and domestic use through provision of WASH services. Likewise, the project intervention's purpose is to address critical community needs through improve equitable access to improved sanitation and hygiene promotion services.

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

SOS Sahel Sudan in its implementation approach follow do no harm in all levels of the project planning and intervention phases to ensure safety and security of the target community and other stakeholders as well. This is why it's very essential to grantee the free of conflict around any kind of proposed water/health facilities before actual implementation, and this process done in collaboration with the local government and target community. Likewise, all services and goods should be provided to the beneficiaries with dignity and equality based on agreed criteria to avoid bias-based conflicts. SOS introduced electric column with solar lights in each constructed or rehabilitated water yards to protect people from the potential threats like snake bite when collecting water at night. SOS has safeguard policy and protection is part of it, so protection all the stakeholders is mandatory issue when planning or implementing the project. the project will orient all the stakeholders about their rights, responsibilities and duties and to hold them free without any kind ofinfluence when they participate or offering project associated ideas. Furthermore, women and girls will be consulted in the location of water points to ensure their security and safety. Also, security information will be shared with all stakeholders for more protection. Community adaptive complaint mechanism will be established as well.

4.1.1 - How will the project use community feedback to improve and adapt activities? (open-ended/free text, required)4

The project will adopt bottom up approach through which community opinions and feedback will highly appreciated as they considered as the very essential for the project improvement and sustainability. This is why SOS Sahel will collect community feedback with respect to all community different groups then analyse such information with good documentation for proper utilization. The community feedback will be collected through monitoring visits, community face to face meetings, complaints mechanisms, participatory joint needs assessment etc. Phone calls and field visits report are one of the most important communication means using for feedback collection. The documented community feedback addressed through field staff to SOS Sahel management and accordingly joint decision will be made by the project in consultation with the target community to improve the planned activities as appropriate.

4.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will support Inter-Agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including Sexual exploitation and abuse complaints?

In collaboration with the IDPs and host communities, line ministries, and humanitarian actors working in the two states, the SOS Sahel intervention team will work together and closely with a high commitment to safeguarding the target community to minimize the potential violations, including PSHEA (prevention and Protection against Sexual Harassment, Exploitation, and Abuse). SOS Sahel has a code of conduct on PSHEA signed by all staff members, including those with temporary contracts, temporary contracts, and volunteers. At the beginning of this intervention, SOS Sahel will conduct orientation for volunteers and target the community about PSHEA to ensure their commitment. SOS Sahel will ensure that intervention activity complies with Do No Harm principles and that humanitarian assistance does not harm anyone or create tension within the community. SOS Sahel will continuously assess the situation on the ground and involve the community to actively participate in the Early Warning Prevention Mechanism, which will be an integral part of the implementation. Women, youth, elders, and children will be a priority in the protection activities to strengthen project mechanisms and create an enabling environment for project beneficiaries to submit their complaints and report issues related to protection in each target location. Also, those particular beneficiaries will be highly considered through agreed selection criteria to ensure no one of them is left behind in terms of accessing the provided WASH-related goods and services. More awareness of all stakeholders about their roles and responsibilities ensures good ground for accountability.
