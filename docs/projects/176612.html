Project Summary

The goal of the MontePuez and Ancuabe WASH projects is to ensure that 37,200 people and surrounding communities in Montepuez District (Mapupulo-Sede, Nikuapa, Nacate and Matuto 3) and Ancuabe District (Najua A, Nanjua B, Kujapani and Natove) have access to portable water, sanitation services that are effectively used, including consistent hygiene practices and to prevent the spread of Covid19 among the IDP and host communities
Project objectives:
1. Provide inclusive and gender responsive life-saving water; sanitation; and hygiene services, items, and facilities for vulnerable populations affected by conflict, including displaced populations and affected individuals in difficult to access areas
2. Promote protection of populations and prevent conflict and gender-based violence through provision of inclusive and gender responsive water; sanitation; and hygiene services, items, and facilities for vulnerable populations, including hosting communities
3. Provide emergency WASH services, including continuity of existing services, to prevent and mitigate the impact of public health emergencies and prevent disease outbreak
Key Activities:
1. Conduct a rapid emergency assessment on WASH
2. Drilling of boreholes in IDP camps to provide portable water
3. Rehabilitation and disinfection of existing water points and support for water committees in communities surrounding the IDP camps.
4. Construction of new water sources for most vulnerable communities surrounding the IDP camps.
5. Promotion activities for self-construction of household latrines and garbage pits in long-term resettlement and returning areas (including the provision of sanitation supplies for most vulnerable households).
6. Distribution of point-of-use water treatment materials / products, including training and monitoring on proper use at household level
7. Dissemination of WASH related messages through mass communication media
Project Indicators
1. A total of 37,200 people with access to sufficient quantity of water
2. A total 37,200 people with access to appropriate sanitation facilities
3. A total 37,200 people reached with hygiene supplies, services, and messages
Targeted geographical locations
Montepuez District (Mapupulo-Sede, Nikuapa, Nacate and Matuto 3), Ancuabe District (Najua A, Nanjua B, Kujapani and Natove)

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Protection activities will be integrated into all the proposed intervention areas. The project will identify barriers in accessing assistance and services for vulnerable groups and take practical actions to maximize access for beneficiaries

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

Consultations will be done with affected populations (not only community leaders) to define needs and priorities. Updates on the project will similarly be shared with the affected populations through Community Feedback mechanism. Affected populations will be informed in advance of selection criteria, date/time, and process. All requests for responses to the Linha Verde mechanism will be responed to within 48 hours of receipt from the WASH Cluster.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Consultations will be done individuals with individuals living with disabilities and the elderly to identify specific adaptation needs for infrastructure. Infrastructure will be adapted for these needs.

Adherence to Host-country Environmental Regulations: All related engagement efforts will adhere to applicable host-country environmental regulations and policies during the implementation of the proposed interventions.
