Project Summary

To provide WASH interventions to the affected families and households in Adaado district. The project will aim at ensuring provision of clean water for drinking and household use, proper sanitation infrastrucutre and training, provision of wells for fecthing water, establishment of latrines to ensure hygiene
The project targtes households and communities vulrenarable to lack of access to water sanitation and hygiene

1. - Projects activities

Emergency water supply with temporary distribution systemsFOO will establish emergency distribution of water through the use of water boozers. Affected regions will receive water regularly on a temporal basis before a permanent solution is established like drilling of water and installing hand water pumps or solar powered water pumps Household Water Treatment and Safe Storage (HWTSS)FOO will provide required materials for treating household water. This will reduce the prevalence’s of water bone disease within the households Well disinfection and chlorination;FOO will ensure the chlorination of and disinfecting of water and water bodies used for drinking like ponds and swamps. This will reduce the chances of spread of water bone diseases Extension/rehabilitation of existing water supply system;FOO with the locals will rehabilitate existing water supply systems like repair of the existing hand powered pumps, rehabilitation of rivers, streams to ensure steady supply of clean water for drinking and household use Provision of water containers for transport and storage;FOO will provide water containers to households to ensure better storage of water in case of shortages and provide steady supply of water for drinking and household useMonitoring of water quality at HH level and/or community level;Through its team FOO will be monitoring the quality of water at household level to ensure that households have clean waterLocate water sources in visible, central locations and not more than 500 meters from settlementFOO will establish water sources near to households and in central places to reduce the risks of women and girls encounter during the time of water fetching Introduce accompaniment arrangements to reduce threats to the safety and dignity of women and-FOO will train and introduce companies that will see the threats of girls and women during water fetching are reduced. This will include fetching water in groups and accompaniment with household boys and men Drilling of boreholes where water accessibility is difficult There are some regions in the project area of implementation where water accessibility is very hard due to drought and sometimes floods FOO will do water accessibility survey and establish boreholes in the areas found to be highly vulnerableTrain and establish handwashing systemsFOO will train households in terms of hand washing so that to reduce prevalence’s of cholera and diarrhea Establishment of latrinesFOO will carry our community assessments and establish latrines in central places within the households. This will reduce open defecation hence reducing prevalence’s of cholera and diarrhea
