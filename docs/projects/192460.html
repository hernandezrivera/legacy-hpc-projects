Project Summary

Within the framework of the ERP 2021-2022, Al Shifaa in consultation with their local community has jointly developed a project to Provide a life-saving and health response and prevention services for the most vulnerable Lebanese, Palestinian Refugees from Lebanon (PRL) and migrants in the 5 Palestinian camps and 10 gathering in the South Governorates.
The proposed intervention will take place in the Governorate of South, particularly in Saida and Tyre districts, covering 5 Palestinian camps. In addition, the project will target 10 Palestinian Gathering of South (Bourj Chamali, Al Bass, Rashdieh, Ein el Helwi and Mieh Mieh).
The locations were identified based on the presence of AL Shifaa. Also, following the consultation with the Popular Committees and PHCs in the five Palestinian Camps. Furthermore, the previous prevention and outreach activities within Al Shifaa in Palestinian camps were factors that supported the identification of the locations and pointed out the areas with the highest medical needs.
The specific localities were also selected based on Al Shifaa ability to access and areas where projects are ongoing have more than 13 years of constant presence and working experience. The primary purpose of the project is to provide a life-saving response in a context of significantly increased needs due to the complex humanitarian crisis in Lebanon. Al Shifaa and local partners have identified the vulnerability criteria through needs assessments, Monitoring Reports, and analysis of the recent referrals but also used as a baseline the data from the medical specialized services of the previous and/or ongoing projects that target partially the medical concerns of Lebanese and PRL populations in the Palestinian camps. As a response, the project is proposing to target the most vulnerable individuals with the following criteria: 1. Kidney and dialysis patients 2. People with disabilities 3. The elderlies are exposed to poverty, malnutrition, hidden malnutrition, and socioeconomic situation in Lebanon. 4. Thalassemia patients 5. Female single-headed households with children.
This project will provide a specialized response to lifesaving, including the provision of equipment and tools for people with disabilities, diapers for elderly and paralyzed people, as well as moving dialysis patients from Tyre to Sida by Al Shifaa ambulance, and medication. The project will have a holistic approach, including Community-based awareness sessions in medical centers and home-based. and distribution of Dignity Kits within prevention activities. Moreover, specialized response services related to health cases will be provided such as medication, testing, and referrals to the relevant and needed services under other programs. Al Shifaa will conduct Community-based sessions, Parenting sessions for caregivers who have patients' children and community initiatives as well.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

The internal regulation, policies, and Code of Conduct also the protocols for supporting people in need will be implemented as followed: 1. Staff will be trained on and commit to policies by signature, 2. Policies and reporting mechanisms will be explained to beneficiaries, 3. Consult individuals on their needs and preferences to access the reporting mechanisms, 4. Three independent focal points monitor the implementation of the policies, receive, and address the complaints, 5. Al Shifaa will continue active participation in the PSEA national network, 6. Ensure an active and clear appeal process with the program team and the communities, 7. Recruitment of females and male will ensure access to beneficiaries of all gender, 8. Al Shifaa, that is specialized in disability, will ensure the active and tailored participation of people with disabilities in the protection risk mitigation plan.8. Al Shifaa specialized in health care access, is well known in the community, and participate

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

[ Al Shifaa ensures that beneficiaries engage with the project planning, design, implementation, and M&E and learning practices. All beneficiaries will be encouraged to work with Al Shifaa in the phases of the project cycle: in delivering messages, identifying vulnerable people and families, and sharing general data on trends and gaps. Through close contact with the communities, Al Shifaa ensures access to relevant and clear information about the program. This information includes the project plans and outcomes, staff roles and responsibilities, contact information, beneficiary selection criteria, and entry points. Feedback around the quality of service received and any faced challenges from the beneficiaries will be collected through outreach activities, focus group discussions, and satisfaction surveys to improve the activity's implementation. Feedback and complaints of beneficiaries will be collected through feedback boxes at PHCs, hotlines, and email addresses.
