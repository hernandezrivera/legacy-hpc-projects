Project Summary

The project proposed will be implemented in two departments of the Extreme North Region for a period of 12-months, in favor of internally displaced populations (IDPs). The implementation strategy is based on Cash Based Assistance (CBT) to the IDPs in two subdivisions in Mayo-Tsanaga (Mokolo and Koza), through a, local mobile operator, the assistance targets 4000 PDIs: 1460 boys, 1360 girls, 440 men and 740 women; while in Logone et Chari, the In-Kind assistance (distribution of food in kind) will target 10,000 IDPs in 4 districts (Logone Birni, Fotokol, Makary and Blangoua), i.e. 3,558 boys, 3,397 girls, 1,117 men and 1,928 women.
General objective: The main objective will be to reduce household vulnerability to food insecurity most especially the Internally Displaced Populations (IDPs), who are the most affected by the crisis in the Lake Chad Basin.
Specific Objectives will be:
- To provide input support to household farmers in order to increase their agricultural productivity
- Supporting and accompanying households farmer in the implementation of agricultural production activities.
The main results will therefore be to provide in kind assistance through the distribution of almost 1950 tons of food within a period of 3months (which is the planting season) in order to strengthen the nutritional status of these populations and to strengthen the local economy through resilience activities, which including the CBT modality.

1 - Besoins / Needs

In the past years, the situation of insecurity, coupled with other natural hazards specific to the Far North Region, (high rainfall causing flooding ; locust invasions; Boko Haram insurgent group, ...), have caused the reduction of Cereal production which is the main reason for food insecurity in the Region. The movement of these displaced person has lead to the lost of almost all their properties and resourses, reducing their autonomy over food security. The properties lost are mainly their agricultural products and livestock. Men, who own the majority of the land are seriousely affected by the lost of their property. Women too have not been left out as they too have also been heavily affected, as they equally dominate the agricultural sector in this region, represent 71.6% in the informal agricultural sector.Multisectoral evaluations of partners working in the localities of Logone et Chari reveal high rates of food insecurity, which is a major concern for the increase in global malnutrition rates and the persistence of chronic malnutrition in children under 5 years of age. The situation of Pregnant women and lactation mothers are just as much of a concern. These indicators is a very critical situation which also account for insecurity of which almost the entire region is subjected to. Adding to this, the low level of education of the population and poor food hygiene practices, which is one of the main reasons to nutritional consequences that are causing serious problems in the Far North Region which has the highest national poverty rate (13.6%) in the country.Due to population growth of around 2.7%, the number of poor people is significantly increase in this Region of the country. According to the November 2019 SMART nutrition survey, the prevalence of global acute malnutrition (GAM) in children under 5 years of age is 5.2% including the SAM rate which is 1.4%, constituting an alert situation. It is therefore important to strengthen the resilience of populations through agricultural production activities to improve the nutritional situation of the targeted localities. The current Covid-19 pandemic has further put these populations in difficulties.In order to maximize farmers' chances of production towards their empowerment AHA will collaborate with WFP to protect the seeds by distributing food during the cultivation and harvesting phases. Activities The different activities will include,1. Identification and registration of beneficiary households; 2. Distribution of food seeds (millet, sorghum, beans, groundnuts, etc.) to the beneficiaries registered households; 3. distribution of farming tools to households in the said locality.4. Training of facilitators using the Farmers Training Approach(FSA); 5. Setting up the agricultural fields in collaboration with the community ; 6.Technical support to farmers in the implementation of the agricultural production activities 7. Establishment of the distribution program based on CBT. In addition in the summary specifying the group of beneficiaries, the duration of the results, and the duration of the results
