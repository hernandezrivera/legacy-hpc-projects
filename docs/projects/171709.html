Project Summary

In response to the growing humanitarian crisis in southern Somalia, SOS Children villages is proposing the following interventions to expand lifesaving child protection services to vulnerable populations in middle Shabelle, Bay and Bakool regions . The complexity of the operating environment requires multifaceted approaches that are tailored to each location including; Strengthen and capacitate community-based child protection mechanisms (CBCPM) to prevent and mitigate CP risks through multi-layered, community based psychosocial support interventions to reduce vulnerabilities and risks to children by building a protective environment at family and community levels in the targeted districts. The project support for the effected individuals include direct support of identified cases through conducting case management including referral, Mental health and psychosocial and counselling support. In addition to this, unaccompanied and separated children in the intervention targeted districts will be reunified with their caregivers/guardians and those children who do not have caregivers/guardians will be supported through community-based foster care placements. In addition, the proposed action will ensure inclusion of person with disabilities, mainstreamed in every action of the project, and as well carry out awareness creation on the rights of children and person with disabilities. Child protection frontline staff, Child safeguarding and Child protection focal points, community volunteers as well as staff from local government authority will be capacitated on child safeguarding, PSEA and code of conduct to ensure that all interventions are safe for children and that they can identify child right violations and take appropriate measures to protect and safeguard children.
SOS CV will expand protection prevention and response by providing services in locations that such protection needs are not addressed. The proposed intervention is designed to Increase prevention and response services to children including those with disabilities have access to life saving case management, Mental health and psychosocial support (MHPSS) services and protections to reduce risks of abuse and violence. The protection monitoring will be mainstreamed into the proposed intervention to improve the overall context analysis and integration of protection principles in the intervention that will be scaled up to respond the identified needs. Enhance understanding and analysis among humanitarian actors on the protection context and needs. SOS CV Somalia will utilize existing internal emergency response capacity teams to provide timelyeffective and efficient protection responses during the project period.
Responses to address the recent needs and violence in these target regions are current priorities within the Protection Cluster in Somalia. The proposed interventions are centrally located within all of the 2021 protection cluster objectives. The proposed Protection will respond to the needs of vulnerable populations including women and girls affected by the recent floods, drought and conflict and will build on existing SOS emergency responses that are already in place in these target districts including Hudur, Baidoa, Jowhar and Balcad districts. These locations are very dispersed and there are few
organizations providing Protection services and SOS CV has established health facilities that are equipped to offer primary health care at present. SOS CV will utilize internal emergency response capacity to respond to this emerging crisis and provide case management and MHPSS, information, protection monitoring and risk mitigation activities services in these target districts.

1. Strengthen and capacitate community based child protection mechanisms (CBCPM) to prevent and mitigate CP risks through multi-layered, community based psycho social support interventions to reduce vulnerabilities and risks to children by building a protective environment at family and community levels.2. 2. Peer to peer awareness raising will be led by CBCPMs, youth and community volunteers’ networks to prevent child protection risks,3. identify vulnerable children in need of support such as case management and emergency child protection care (i.e. alternative care or basic PSS activities)4. Identify and support children at risk and child survivors including unaccompanied and separated through providing case management services that meets their unique needs, which include family tracing and reunification services, alternative care arrangements.5. . Strengthen and capacitate Project frontline staff and community volunteers to support the case management process, through identification of children at risk, making home monitoring visits and offering peer support to caregivers.6. The nature of the mental health support will be based on each case individual and group need assessment. 7. Support to caregivers and families (MHPSS, positive parenting and other kind of support) of children facing protection risks in order to enhance their capacity to care for and protect their children.8.
