Project Summary

Through the project RADA will implement a school meal programme in the North-West Region targeting school children in the most crisis affected divisions. The project aims to provide school meals to 5,000 children to encourage and promote school attendance, performance and healthier living. A consequence of the crisis in these regions, many schools were closed leaving thousands of children out of school for many years mainly in the targeted divisions. With the improvement observed in the security situation in the areas and thanks to the humanitarian support from UN agencies like WFP, UNFPA, and NGOs many children have been able to go back to school.
The project will consist of providing daily meals using locally produced food to all the children for a period of 6 months during the current academic year 2022/2023 thus contributing to improve their nutritional status, academic performance as well as to protect them from enrolment by the terrorist groups and reducing the high crime waves that we are already experiencing in this region. RADA with availability of funds will provide selected nursery primary schools and supply them with the locally produce foodstuffs bought from local suppliers and farmers. This contributes to the increase in local production, promote market access to small farmers while promoting and improving schooling, attendance and performance. Each school feeding Programme committee controls the reception and issue out of food stock as well as request for new stock when the old ones are about to finish on time. This project is strictly to provide foodstuff to schools and sensitization for their pupils and this will mean that other resources like wood, water, warehousing and cooking will be provided or taken care by schools authorities, parents teachers associations and the communities at large. During the implementation, special attention will be paid to children with disabilities. Their food will be collected and served in at their sitting place.
Various actions will be put in place to support the implementation of the project. Capacity strengthening sessions will be organized on school feeding management, food storage, gender and Protection, for all stakeholders involved in the school’s management including other partners operating in the targeted areas. Sensitization will involve school administrations, Parents teachers’ associations and local meetings on nutrition aspects using locally available food.

1 - Besoins / Needs

The recent crisis in Cameroon has amplified the displacement of many households’ dwellers from their homes to host communities or relatives in safer areas increasing the burden on these families as far as education and food are concerned. The education sector was heavily impacted with schools closed and 100,000 children out of schools in the Far North, North, Adamaoua, East and North West and South West regions. Thanks to Government efforts to maintain security especially in the divisions, school children are progressively going back to schools and few schools have reopened. Some displaced families are returning to their villages and children are attending school but still hesitant and demotivated. Parents have limited income sources; they are mostly busy reconstructing destroyed farms and properties but they are still fearful with sporadic attacks. Most of the parents’ activities include income generation to sustain households 'feeding, some returnee families are benefiting from humanitarian assistance. Children highly need food support to keep them at school also to improve their nutritional status, improve their academic performance as well as to protect from being enrolled or used by terrorist groups. The food assistance at school will encourage them to attend classes and stay in school throughout the school year. The school meals will serve as an incentive to maintain them in class, this alongside various other education assistance and psychological support provided. Gender mainstreaming will be taken into consideration as well as disability inclusion by providing some reason accommodation to these nursery and primary school pupils. We shall also make available room for PPEs to combat COVID-19 and other ailments.
