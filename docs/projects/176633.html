Project Summary

This project aims to address the current gaps (according to the 2021 HRP) in returnee response and emergency shelter/NFI needs identified by the Emergency Tracking Tool (ETT) of IOM-DTM Unit, by:
a) Reinforcing the capacity of national and local authorities on emergency response
b) Creation of Early warning system and Strengthening the rapid assessment capacity
following natural disasters, including monitoring of IDPs
c) Distribution of Emergency Shelters and NFI response to returnees from the United Republic of Tanzania and people affected by natural disasters, including cash-based intervention to support during construction process
d) Providing Transitional Shelters and Repair Shelter kits to the most vulnerable
households returning from the United Republic of Tanzania and/or affected by natural disasters, including cash-based intervention to support during construction process
e) Reinforce Emergency Communication with Community mechanism in country,
specifically the Emergency Hotline (109) hosted in the premises of the Burundian Red Cross.
In addition, this project will reinforce the IOM’s Emergency Event Tracking system (EET) for rapid assessment following natural disasters and other displacement events, including the development of an emergency response team dedicated to assessing life-saving shelter needs of returnees.
The project, as well as the Emergency Event Tracking (EET) will covering the 18
provinces of Burundi, namely Makamba, Rumonge, Bubanza, Cibitoke, Kayanza, Ngozi,
Ruyigi, Rutana, Gitega, Bururi, Mwaro, Murumvya, Karusi, Cankuzo, Muyinga, Kayanza, Bujumbura Mairie, Bujumbura Rural.
It will be carried by active partners from the Shelter/NFI sector in country, following sector standards and a harmonized approach, in collaboration with relevant sectors when relevant.
