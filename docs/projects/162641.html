Project Summary

The project interventions are intended to lead to increased food security & strengthen community resilience to climate shocks &conflict through enabling sustainable increases in agricultural and livestock productivity & adopting alternative livelihoods which will decrease competition over natural resource. The ultimate beneﬁciaries of the project are mainly the farmers and pastoralist, IDPs, returnees & poor residents in Blue Nile, North Darfur and Kassala states (around 580790 people). Agricultural tools, drought resistant seeds & seedlings, vegetables and horticulture will be provided to increase household food production. Promotion of climate smart & resilient crops & livestock production systems, protection of livestock based livelihoods in pastoral & agro pastoral community through provision of emergency livestock assistance, livestock watering, restocking with goat & poultry; awareness raising on the important of healthy diet in addition to women training on food processing for malnutrition, these inputs will build the capacity of the targeted communities to achieve their own food security & improve their livelihoods by increasing their production & income to better able them to withstand future shocks, droughts & other natural disasters. Solar powered women farms will enable produce quality diversified food items for household consumption and income & will reduce work load compared to currently practiced irrigation methods. Establish women saving & loan groups & support it with clean fuel (LPG cylinders & stoves) to establish revolving funds thus reducing wood collection work load & negative health impacts of indoor pollution. Process of women farms will be supported by nurseries, business management & agro processing trainings, linkage to markets & solar lights for safe gathering and mobile charging. The project will support assets protection and increase the ability of the target communities to decrease post-harvest losses & increase their resilience to shocks through provision of cash for work & cash for assets. The project will facilitate regeneration of ecosystem through a range of activities that aims to increase access to water, pasture and forestry which in the past directly contributed to conﬂict between farmers and pastoralists. Training will be provided on the management of these resources and environmental conservation techniques. The project will support the formation & reinforcement of farmers’ groups & community based associations strengthen their institutional capacity and consolidate their links to government service providers & other actors; to facilitate delivery of other project activities. For example, the training & technical support will be provided through these groups. The Project will apply participatory approaches for prioritizing & planning community needs to ensure that community planning & service delivery is tailored to local needs. More over the project will arrange to raise community awareness on themes other than production of the value chain products, including gender, protection & environment. As with elsewhere in the three targeted states men & women have different and unequal access to agriculture and livestock production inputs and technology (women represent about 49% of the total population yet are responsible for more than 65% of agriculture and livestock livelihood activities); therefore the project will ensure that everyone in the affected community women, girls, boys and men have a voice in the development & implementation of the project & have access to opportunities that the project gives. Restocking with livelihoods kits, vegetable growing & marketing will enable women and youth to better engage in income generating activities and production process. The project will be run in environment friendly pattern, saving natural resources & increase target population awareness about environment issues through discussion & orientation sessions.

1 - Needs

Food security: According to the newly launch Global Humanitarian Overview; 9.3 million people—nearly one in four—will need assistance in 2020. The latest IPC results June-August 2019 estimated that 5.8 million individuals (mainly in the Darfur states, the Kordofan states, Kassala, Red Sea and Blue Nile) are experiencing Crisis or worse levels of food insecurity (IPC Phase 3 and above) and need timely lifesaving food and livelihoods assistance in Sudan. Out of these, around 1 million individuals are facing Emergency levels of food insecurity (IPC Phase 4). However, estimates by WFP show that more than 58 per cent of the country’s population may not be able to afford the price of the local food basket and is facing continued food access challenge. Moreover, higher production costs are anticipated to continue increasing the already high cereal prices, which are not expected to decline significantly. FEWS net reports on food security 2019 highlighted that eleven out of Sudan’s 18 states have recorded global acute malnutrition rates (GAM) above the World Health Organization’s (WHO) emergency threshold of 15 per cent, with the highest rates found in eastern Sudan.Security: The security situation in the country has improved and IDPs have experienced more freedom of movement. Despite this however, IDPs—particularly women and girls—still face high protection risks and continue to be harassed, and occasionally killed or raped. On the other hand,in the first three quarters of 2019, the International Organization for Migration (IOM) registered 14,500 IDPs and 111,500 returnees in six states in Sudan. The highest number of returnees registered was in North Darfur (44,500 people). Returns have been recorded in all Darfur states, most likely due to the improved security. Institutions: The limited organizational, networking and advocacy capacity of CBOs & national NGOs is a major constraint to their playing a more decisive role in coordinating & effectively delivering humanitarian support to the affected populations. Women lack adequate representation & have low voice in decisions for their communities. Strengthening the capacity of these institutions in various areas will enable them to plan, prepare & effectively respond to future food insecurity and livelihoods hazards. It worth noting that, Practical Action has extensive experience through different projects in developing the organisational, institutional & technical capacities of civil society organisation and government institutions. Environment: Climate change and related natural disasters (floods, droughts, and dry spells) have a strong bearing on the socio-economic situation of communities and households and can contribute to the number of people requiring humanitarian support. Environmental degradation (for example of land and forests) and unsustainable use of natural resources are both contributory factors and consequences of the humanitarian situation in Sudan. The degradation of natural resources has forced the majority of the rural population to overgraze range lands, expand cropping in marginal lands to compensate for low yields & cut trees for ﬁre wood & charcoal making for sale as coping strategies. People have also become involved in conﬂict & competition over dwindling natural resource base. All these activities will continue to further degrade the environment unless efforts are exerted to support, conserve and restore the natural resources.

5.1 - Impact on environment/natural resources

The project will be run in environment friendly pattern, saving natural resources and increase target population awareness about environment issues through discussion and orientation sessions. The project will support solar irrigation system which will reduce hazardous gases emission from burning fuel for water pumping in addition communities' women and men committees will be trained to establish forest nurseries utilizing waste water to start community forests around water supply points. On the other hand, the project will encourage farmers to adopt agro-ecological farming practices.

3=Yes, room for improvement

6.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

Practical Action –and partners implement set of policies that protect staff, community and all who are involved in performing any type of activities, contact with these organizations. For example PA implements code of conduct, gender, protection against sexual exploitation, anti-fraud and bribery, whistle blowing policies. In particular protection of children and vulnerable adults policy governs own staff and Partners staff by signing statement indicating that abuse of any kind (financial, material, psychological, discrimination, sexual, neglect or exclusion) for vulnerable adults and children is unacceptable and recognizes the organization responsibility to safeguard their welfare by protecting them from any form of abuse. Behavioural controls also commits all related to the organization work including partner organizations and stakeholders to show understanding when dealing with sensitive issues including cultural differences and human diversity, impartiality according to race, culture, gender, disability, religion or beliefs. Practical Action is also committed to treat with full seriousness any accusation of abuse, investigate and to take necessary protective arrangements when such cases are reported. The link with whistle blowing mechanism that protects those who provide information on abuse by established safe communication channels to access all levels of the organization management hierarchy by direct contact, email, phone or any other feasible communication channel. Beside, all project implementation processes will participatory and consultative ensuring voice and reflection of interests of all indiscriminately and adoption of fair selection criteria of beneficiaries in consultation with community organizations that are oriented to the principles of selecting the most vulnerable and do no harm. PA will provide training to build the capacity of its own and partner staff to ensure application of protection mainstreaming throughout the project implementation process with emphasis on misuse of power relation, respect to dignity and privacy of all, prevention of sexual abuse, child and adult protection
