Project Summary

In order to ensure the basic needs of the needy, the most vulnerable persons in safe water supply, a number of specialized measures are planned to improve water supply, sanitation and hygiene services in settlements located in the 0-20 km zone from the contact line (NGCA):
Providing social institutions (kindergartens, schools, hospitals, territorial centers, boarding houses for the elderly and disabled) with reverse osmosis system and drinking fountains with filtration, to reduce the risk of recurrence of cases of mass water poisoning, which were previously recorded in the region, as well as increasing the economic sustainability of the most vulnerable persons, forced, for example, bottles of drinking water to buy children to school;
Provision of hygiene kits to the most vulnerable categories of the population affected by the conflict;
Installation of boreholes and / or pumps, if necessary, on the basis of popular social institutions of the most water-deficient locations in the area of the contact line.

1 - Needs

The territory of Donbass has always been water-scarce, however, with the beginning of the military conflict, the problem came to a qualitatively new level, in terms of regular damage fragile water infrastructure, disrupt communications, degradation of public property and large-scale disruption of supply of vital reagents for the operation of the supply system.This situation has already led to mass disasters, such as the hospitalization of 230 children with tap water poisoning in Makeyevka at the end of 2018. Moreover, there are a number of settlements, especially in the area of the contact line, where water supply is generally partially or completely absent.The crisis of the municipal water supply infrastructure takes place against the background of the ongoing trade blockade of the region, which preserves the state of collapse of the local economy, mass unemployment and falling incomes, especially among the neediest target groups, which, often, it is not possible to provide their basic hygienic needs.Assistance in water supply, sanitation and hygiene will reduce the risk of disease (purified water will reduce the incidence of diseases caused by microorganisms and chemical compounds contained in poor-quality drinking water), will save on hygiene and sanitation products, which will allow to cover other basic needs.It is planned to install drinking fountains with filtration and reverse osmosis system in 60 social institutions (20 educational, 20 social, 20 medical), develop 10 boreholes connect 20 pumps that will provide access to water in water- deficient locations.People with disabilities receiving minimal social benefits, need additional support. Disabled people can not take care of themselves, so they have to seek help from others. This category of the population requires diapers for adults, which they are forced to buy on their own, despite the fact that the benefit on average is 3000r. ($50).), and a pack of diapers (10 ps.), which is enough for 3 days-300 rubles. ($5.) In addition to persons with limited mobility, a similar need for hygiene kits, including diapers, exists in families with children, especially in vulnerable households (single parents, large families, guardians), due to unemployment and lack of funds for the purchase of such goods. Among the respondents, 23% of families do not have the financial capacity to cover basic needs, including food and medicine, 9% of respondents are forced to sell their previously accumulated property.Distribution of hygiene kits for families with special needs and large families is provided by the project. The project involves the preparation and maintenance of a database of applicants selected on the basis of incoming applications.

2 - Activities & outcomes

Activities should be carried out to provide social institutions with drinking fountains with water filtration:1. Conducting preliminary monitoring in social institutions in need of drinking filtered water. 2. Selection and coordination of objects. 3. Preparation of estimates for the purchase of necessary goods, determination of the required capacity of the system for a particular social institution (depends on the number of people who are served in social institutions). 4. Installation and connection of fountains with osmosis system. 5. Post monitoring and reporting.As part of the water supply component, it is planned to:1. Preliminary monitoring in water-deficient areas. 2. Selection and coordination of objects. 3.Hydrological works: the choice of location for the arrangement of boreholes taking into account the geological features of the site; hydrological factors; location of other objects. Checking the source location for compliance, providing access to the drilling rig and other special equipment for site maintenance. 4. Preparation of estimates for the purchase of necessary goods. 5. Control over the implementation of hydrological works. 6. Selection of objects that require the installation of pumps to ensure the supply of water. 7. Purchase of pumps of necessary capacity. 8. Post monitoring and reporting.As part of the hygiene kits component, it is planned to: 1.Collection of applications, preparation and approval of the list of beneficiaries, preparation and maintenance of the database. 2. Preparation of the list of goods included in the hygiene kit. 3. Selection of suppliers and purchase of hygiene kits. 4. Distribution of hygiene kits. 5. Post monitoring and reporting (intermediate, final).
