Project Summary

Significant increase in numbers IDPs in the informal settlements/IDP camps in Baidoa and Burhakaba is attributed to intermittent conflicts in Middle and Lower Shabelle Regions coupled by serious Deyr 2019 and Gu 2020 flooding and impacts of past successive drought season that almost culminated into famine. The IDPs (men, women, boys and girls) as well as the elderly and those with disabilities are living precariously in the unplanned settlements under very poor living conditions that expose them to violence, exploitation and other forms of abuse coupled by serious risks and threats to their safety and security besides threats of forced eviction. Their level of vulnerability has been worsened further by recent impacts of Covid -19 containment measures that subjected them to increased forms of discrimination to access humanitarian assistance. Of particular concern is the fact that children/boys and girls endure multiple protection risks and violations of their rights including family separation, sexual and physical violence, killing and maiming, psychosocial distress, child labor, early and forced marriages, widespread recruitment and use of children, forced and protracted displacements, limited access to humanitarian assistance due to insecurity and/or discriminatory practices and exploitation particularly among women and young girls as well as those with disabilities. The mentioned plight calls for urgent protection measure to ensure protection of the targeted vulnerable IDPs and marginalised host communities. The project targets a total of 18600 persons (7738 women, 7142 men, 1860 boys and 1860 girls) with appropriate protection services including including Provision of specialized MHPSS and victim assistance services to individuals and persons with disabilities and older persons, provision of protection services during eviction, conducting eviction monitoring services, provision of counseling as well as medical support for children, building capacity of case workers, provision of material support and access to essential services for other protection victims and child survivors, Prevention and Response services for unaccompanied and Separated Children.

Project Activities

1.Capacity building of 40 case workers through training on Psychosocial support, clinical care and safe and ethical referral protocols in Baidoa and Burhakaba Districts2.Provision of protection services to 7500 persons (men, women, boys and girls ) including those with disabilities during eviction in IDP settlement in Baidoa and Burhakaba Districts.3.Provision of specialized MHPSS and victim assistance services to individuals and persons with disabilities and older persons in Baidoa and Burhakaba.4.Conducting eviction monitoring services to prevent disputes and mitigating risks of forced evictions by ensuring security of tenure agreements in IDP settlements in Baidoa and Burhakaba 5. Provide immediate protection specific responses to incidences of abuse/violation through both static and mobile outreach service delivery and material assistance alleviating their worst effects or preventing their occurrence including a) Survivor-centered (psychosocial support, medical assistance, legal aid, and physical security) and case management and addressing needs of 3500 survivors of GBV by provision of dignity kits to uphold their dignity and well-being in acute displacement situations to the project targeted locations.b) Family reunification and case management, including psychosocial support to overcome trauma and reintegration, for 5,000 survivors of child-rights violations including unaccompanied and separated children in Baidoa and Burhakaba Districts.7.Provision of material support/life skills training and business support for 2000 GBV survivors8. Provision of recreational activities for 2500 children (boys and girls) and adopt the child friendly environment in Baidoa and Burhakaba Districts9. Setting up of Child Protection Desks in Camps with Child attendants at standby for children by guiding and advising the children on appropriate action to ensure their safety and rights in Baidoa, Burhakaba

Mitigation Measures

1. Risk that beneficiaries are exposed to violence during registration/distribution as a result of the distance to or location of humanitarian assistance and service: Mitigation Measure : Enhancing security at the project sites with guards as well as ensuring centrality or more distribution centers of humanitarian assistance to limit distance and time that exposes women and girls to GBV incidences when accessing assistance.2. Risk of women, girls and boys exposed to sexual violence, including sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA), when accessing humanitarian assistance. Mitigation Measure: Ensuring that all staff and gate keepers sign PSEA code of conduct and adhere to it3. Risk of no access to or awareness of protection assistance or referral services for survivors of protection incidents (i.e. GBV, child protection, evictions, explosive hazards, etc.)Mitigation measure: Conducting awareness on protection assistance for survivors of protection incidences to sustained impacts4. Risk that marginalized groups, persons with disabilities, women, boys and girls, do not participate in consultations or decision-making processes for the project or are excluded from humanitarian services/assistance/activitiesMitigation measure: Conducting comprehensive awareness for persons with disabilities, women, boys and girls on importance of their inclusion and participation in decision making processes as well as involving them in the project from commencement to completion of the project including monitoring excercises5. Risk that beneficiaries are not accessing/using complaint and feedback mechanismsMitigation measure: Establishing beneficiaries complain and feedback mechanisms and conducting awareness targeting the beneficiaries for utilization of the same
