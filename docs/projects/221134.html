Project Summary

The Project titled, “Strengthening Emergency Preparedness and Child Safeguarding in Conflict-Affected Schools in Cameroon’s North West and South West Regions,” seeks to improve the safety and well-being of school-aged children in crisis-affected areas. This initiative will build the capacity of 300 Teachers, Parent-Teacher Association (PTA) members, and educational authorities across 40 schools (20 in the North West Region and 40 in the South West Region) through targeted workshops and trainings to better prepare for emergencies, implement safeguarding practices, and reduce disaster risks. By equipping educational stakeholders with critical skills and resources, the project aims to create a safer, more resilient learning environments that protect children and support their right to education in crisis conditions

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The protracted conflict in the conflict affected North West and South West Regions of Cameroon since 2016 has disrupted the education system, leaving schools highly vulnerable to security threats, natural disasters, and emergencies. Over 1033,00 estimated school -aged children have been directly impacted (Cameroon Education Cluster 2023-2025 strategic plan), with many lacking the essential support systems to ensure their safety and well-being in these volatile settings.Assessments by the Cameroon Education Cluster (Join Education Need Assessment March 2024) and the Pan African Institute for Development-West Africa (PAID-WA) indicate that the vast majority of schools (77%) in the affected regions lack emergency preparedness plans, and 75% of schools are limited in their capacity to train students and staff on child safeguarding and emergency response. Schools are often not equipped with disaster risk reduction measures or safeguarding protocols, making children increasingly vulnerable in times of crisis. Further, household surveys reveal that 78% of parents and community members lack awareness of emergency preparedness or safeguarding strategies to protect their children in the event of a disaster or security incident.This project seeks to address these gaps by building the capacity of 300 teachers, including: Parent-Teacher Associations (PTAs) members, through targeted workshops and training, participants will gain essential knowledge and practical skills to establish emergency preparedness plans, promote child safeguarding, and implement disaster risk reduction measures. By addressing these urgent needs, the project aims to create a safer, more resilient environment that protects children and supports their right to education, even in crisis conditions.
