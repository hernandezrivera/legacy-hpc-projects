Project Summary

Considering the current COVID-19 pandemic, NFI support will be provided to households with "home delivery" modality in order to avoid chances of Coronavirus spreading in the community. With this modality, social distance will be maintained perfectly.
During any emergencies such as fire, flood, land slide, etc. HEKS will assess the needs through Rapid Damage Verification (RDV) process in order to determine the category of support required. emphasis will be given to HHs with elderly members and HHs in home care
These interventions will be implemented in close cooperation with IOM and Shelter sector. IOM will provide required NFI/shelter materials as an in-kind contribution through common pipe line system, whereas HEKS/EPER will ensure the whole implementation of the project activities. NFI support will be provided to HHs in home care and HHs with elderly members.
Currently HEKS/EPER is implementing TSA in camp 15 with support from IOM, also Having become the Shelter/NFI sector catchment focal point for camp 14,15 and 16 allows HEKS/EPER to expand its working area to two more camps, HEKS/EPER is responsible for shelter emergency support in all three camps.This project will be directly implemented by HEKS/EPER’s own team that has significant experience and knowledge in programming including in the proposed project location. Considering the situation of COVId-19 HEKS/EPER will arrange trainings for staff and volunteers regarding the personal safety isue and social distancing.
Project implementation process will be shared with majhi's and emam before stat, entitlement of each HH will be shared through this meeting, after distribution each HH will be visited by trained community volunteer for dessiminating awareness messages and collect their feedback, also one complain desk will be introduced where community can make their complain.
Community volunteer will be male and female both, so that female beneficiaries can make complain or share their openion during the implementation period,Also volunteers and stafs will be oriented on PSEA and child protection, gender sencetive language will be used.
PDM will be conducted after completing the distribution,during PDM age and gender specific data will be collected.

Objectives

1.Ensure that families affected by COVID-19 are supported with specific NFIs, home delivery of those NFIs, home delivery of LPG and delivery as well as implementation of emergency shelter response 2.Mitigate the risk of exposure to the virus for the elderly population, by providing specific NFI support, home delivery of NFIs, home delivery of LPG and delivery as well as implementation of emergency shelter response.

Needs

Due to the limited access to the shelter support, shelter repair and construction materials prior to the monsoon are the main reason of the shelter’s damages which highly increased during monsoon. As a result, implementation of shelter repairman as well the materials distribution needs to be increased during monsoon or before monsoon. Additionally, the refugees who will be in quarantine and those who will be in home care also need be supported with NFIs. At the same time, there will be a necessity to support additional items like blankets, floor mats, solar lights or others to the person in home care from previous NFIs distribution. This sort of assistance will be required for those who will be suspected and for them a separate unit will be needed and some of the items might not be provided. Home delivery support must be needed especially for the elderly and physically disable person who are most vulnerable including additional items which could make them more comfortable during their home isolation care as well the implementation of any emergency shelter assistance.
