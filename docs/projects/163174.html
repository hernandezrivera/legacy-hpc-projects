Project Summary

MICHAFOUND aims at reducing the problem of shelter within some IDPs in the SWR of Cameroon. Targeted population is at 78.
These IDPs are as a result from the current Anglophone crisis ongoing in Cameroon for the past three years.
This project aims at providing accommodation thereby providing monthly rent for 2020 only along side mattress, blankets, buckets, pots, stoves and other utensils.
IDPs related to shelter as a problem based on data collected shall be called to secured venues and donations shall be made as planned.
Project execution will be done chronologically that is from one location to another for proper monitoring. Proposed locations are Buea, Limbe, Mutengene and Tiko.
Feedback will be collected in-house through short paper surveys for general reporting. This shall rebuild livelihood and play positively in the minds of the vulnerable.
Total budget is at 35,589.88USD beginning 01/01/2020 – 31/12/2020

Besoins / Needs

The results of the Shelter/ NFI and WASH Joint Needs Assessment conducted by REACH in cooperation with locally based Non-Governmental Organizations, indicate that an overwhelming 95 percent of key informants Interviewed confirmed that the affected population in the SW/NW regions are in dire need of shelter and NFI assistance. Similarly, it was revealed during the assessment the depletion of the economic resources, added to the insecurity, are the two key reasons that explain the mentioned needs. Findings of the assessment also indicate that IDPs living in precarious self-settled conditions, in makeshift shelter in forests, as well as in agricultural infrastructure not appropriate for this purpose, are increasingly exposed to ill-health, including malaria and respiratory diseases. Living conditions of the most vulnerable host families, their hosted IDPs and the displaced population renting accommodations are gradually worsening due to the overcrowding and very limited access to sanitation facilities. Moreover, cohabitation problems have been reported extensively by interviewed IDPs.Miracle Charity Foundation based September, 2019 data collected shoes 78 families out 1940 had problems of shelter. Therefore, for us to fully utilize collected data, these 78 families with an average number of 5 IDPs shall receive 12 months paid rent alongside mattresses, blankets, buckets, pots, stoves and other utensils. Approximately, 78 households shall receive an average of 434USD for total aid in value.
