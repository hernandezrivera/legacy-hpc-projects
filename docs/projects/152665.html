Project Summary

This Project intends to achieve the Cluster Objective 2: Food production for the most vulnerable households to protect the erosion of livelihoods through provision of agricultural seeds, farm tools and equipment and fishing inputs. The project will be come handy to complement the other sectors such as Nutrition, Health, WASH, Veterinary and Protection ( Prevention of Sexual and Gender Based Violence and Child Protection) hence leading to a more integrated and holistic approach of interventions.
The main purpose of the project is to establish a strong nexus between the already existing humanitarian response and Resilience Programming by setting in place structures and assets for Sustainable Agriculture for Economic Resiliency. The priority areas will be to increase food production and access to food within the four counties of Mundri East, Mundri West, Mvolo and Nagero, reduction of post-harvesting losses and safeguarding animal health, restoring and strengthening of livelihoods for communities in stressed and emergency phases alongside integrated actions e.g in WASH, Nutrition and Health, incorporate women's needs, voices and rights, strengthen Livestock Vaccination and Treatment which we are already implementing in Mundri East, Mundri West and Mvolo Counties, Capacity building, skills and knowledge development and community engagement and to improve Accountability with complaint and feedback mechanisms by ensuring inclusion of the vulnerable host communities, Internally Displaced Persons ( IDPs) , the elderly among other targeted groups.
The targeted Households for this project include: 1,667 HHs in Mundri East, Mundri West and Nagero Counties. This translates to a total of 10,000 beneficiaries in the four counties.

The project activities will include increasing emergency food production for both main and dry seasons, provision of seeds, farm tools and equipment to the identified and registered targeted groups and beneficiaries, distribution of Vegetable Kits, Fishing Kits, and Crop Kits, reduction of crop pests and diseases e.g. Fall Army Worms Surveillance, detection, prevention and eradication, expansion and strengthening of livestock vaccination, disease prevention and treatment.
The overall situation and level of acute malnutrition in the targeted four counties attributed to severe food insecurity, poor access to health and nutrition services, high morbidity, extremely poor diets and poor sanitation and hygiene is high. Therefore, the project will be vital in changing the situation and setting ground for more sustainable projects in the future.

Needs

According to the latest Integrated Food Security Phase Classification ( IPC Analysis for September 2018 to March 2019), the key findings shows that over 330,000 of the total population in Western Equatoria State are under Phase 2 ( Stressed), 280,000 are in Phase 3( Crisis) and 60,000 people are in Phase 4( Emergency). Narrowing these findings to the four counties of Mundri East, Mundri West, Mvolo and Nagero Counties, the food security and acute malnutrition levels attributed to reduced food production, poor access to health and nutrition services, high morbidity, extremely poor diets and poor sanitation and hygiene is very high. Insecurity and inaccessibility to the farming areas by the population are the main underlying causes of food insecurity and increased malnutrition in these areas. In September this year, there was a Initial Rapid Needs Assessment conducted by Multi-Agencies in Mundri East and West Counties in coordination by UN OCHA. The key findings showed that physical access remains limited in most areas especially in Bangolo, Lakamadi, Minga, Lozoh and Siringiwa.The road security in these areas have remained unpredictable making humanitarian access and delivery of services to be a problem. For Nagero County, there was fresh conflict between the SPLA IO and Government troops that led to displacements. There are an estimated 1,540 IDPs and others moved to Tambura County. Livestock diseases are very rampant within the four counties due to movement of animals from neighboring counties of Terekeka, Wau and Jur River. Controlling the disease becomes difficult for the Host Communities as they don't have enough vaccines and treatment drugs. We intend to fill this gap by strengthening the already existing vaccination and treatment activities in the targeted areas. Constant surveillance of Livestock Movement and Reporting of any outbreak of the zoonotic diseases will be our key priority. Rift Valley Fever disease is of great concern to the farmers in Western Equatoria State as their livestock are vulnerable due to movements. Agricultural production in the four counties has gone down due to reduced rainfall. From June to September 2018, there was reduced agricultural food production and increased inflation in the market. This affected the purchasing power of the communities. We intend to capacity build the farmers on the various ways of reducing post harvest losses by improving their storage facilities and linking them to the markets for the surplus just in case.The targeted community members have limited sustainable agricultural activities due to the prolonged conflict among the armed groups within the counties. Mundri East and West being hardly hit by the continuous attacks among the SPLA IO groups and Government soldiers in the peripheries of the counties.Lean season from September to December is on.Though recently signed Peace Deal has improved movement of people and commodities, the prices are still very high in the few functional markets. There is great need of packaging and branding of the honey produced by most of the farmers in these areas. They produce natural honey but lack skills on how to harvest, package and brand for easy marketing to generate income. We intend to fill this gap by providing basic skills and knowledge on Apiculture and Aquaculture to the targeted women and youth groups in the four counties. We will also link them to the market through their cooperative societies and groups for faster sales. There are cases of Sexual and Gender Based Violence among the community members, early girl child marriages and exploitation. This is an area that we plan to emphasize on to fill the gap of what has not been covered by the other implementing partners. Mundri East, Mundri West, Mvolo and Nagero Counties have so many isolated groups of the Elderly people in the community. We have seen this need and our plan is to identify, register and support them.We will equally incorporate PLWDs.

Activities

The Planned Project Activities will include:1. Identifying and Registration of the Project beneficiaries by visiting households and engaging the community leaders.2. Conducting a one week training to all the identified farmers on good agricultural practices e.g. Land preparation, planting, weeding, harvesting and storage.3. Implementing the Seed Fairs after proper Community Mobilization and Sensitization.4 .Distribution of Crop seeds, farm tools and equipment during the main season and dry season through community participation procedures.5.Identify and Register the Fisher Folk, Women and Youth groups to be supported by the Fishing and Vegetable Kits.6.Distribution of Fishing and Vegetable Kits to the identified and registered farmers.7. Conducting Post-Distribution and Post-Planting Surveys to confirmed whether the seeds, tools and equipment are being used by the farmers well and the progress of the planted farms.8. Conducting community based participatory exercises to identify the enterprise choices through technical, financial, environmental and social feasibility of all the planned enterprises.We will conduct continuous training for enterprise groups on enterprise development, gender and Prevention of SGBV, Nutrition, HIV/AIDS,Group dynamics and Management.9. ACEM will support the formation and strengthening of community-based seed producers, hides and skin enterprises.10. We plan to establish market linkage activities for enterprise groups for various enterprises including honey, vegetables, Shea butter, hides and skins, fish, poultry and livestock products.11. We will be setting up and monitoring small grant marketing groups12. We plan to establish seed banks for seed producers and link them to Input Trade Fairs ( ITFs)13. We plan to pilot warehouse receipt system for grain producer groups and link them with The World Food Program ( WFP) Purchase for Progress ( P4P) programme.14.We plan to support the formation of agro-based dealers ( engaged in seeds, tools, equipment, veterinary drugs, fishing gear) and strengthening through training in agro-dealership.15.We will distribute relevant inputs, equipment and tools and provide training on utilization, operation and maintenance of such equipment.16. We will provide intensive technical, hygiene and food safety and business training to the different identified women, youth and men groups.17. ACEM will conduct community based participatory planning exercise to identify major production constraints, training needs, prioritize interventions/ technologies to address the constraints.18. With partnership with other Stakeholders, we will facilitate market assessment to identify accessibility of agro-inputs19.We will identify potential community facilitators( lead farmers, village facilitators) within the communities in the four counties.20. ACEM will distribute relevant inputs, equipment and tools to pilot the different intensification and climate technologies.21. We will provide both methodological approach and technical trainings to facilitators on topics such as micro-irrigation, soil water management, integrated pest management and seed production.22.We will facilitate cross learning actions among different farming groups locally and establish and document knowledge management systems all the identified groups.23. ACEM will establish groups to support in sustainable fishing through fisheries co-management training of local fisher folk and local leaders as per the Ministry of Livestock and Fisheries Guidelines and Regulations and FAO's Technical Guidelines for Responsible Fisheries. 25.Identification and training 80 Community Animal Health Workers in the four counties of Mundri East, Mundri West, Mvolo and Nagero Counties.26. Distribution of Livestock Vaccination and Treatment Kits to the trained Community Animal Health Workers27. Provide training on disease control and surveillance, pasture management, improved animal feeding practices & breeding
