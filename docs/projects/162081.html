Project Summary

DRC became the co-lead of the SMSD sector in 2019 and is a strong actor in the sector with vast activities in Site Management and Site Development in Rohingya Response Crisis. DRC plans to continue to co-lead the sector in 2020 as one of the main INGO representatives, providing guidance on SMSD issues and coordination efforts within sector actors and also with government counterparts with the objective of maintaining the added value as an INGO co-lead that understands the issues and gives voice to NGO/INGO or on-the-ground partners that are funded by UN and other donor agencies. This project aims to build on DRC's existing Site Management and Site Development achievements from the begining of the Rohingya crisis in Bangladesh. DRC will target 225,092 vulnerable Rohingya refugees living in camps 2W,2E, 5,6,8E, 8W, 11 and 12 in Ukhiya. DRC will also target additional 200 people from host community in Ukhiya. DRC aims to achieve this goal through the provision of quality, coordinated site management and site improvement assistance, drawing upon the support of DRC's proven expertise in Protection. With this project DRC will continue the investment into engineering solutions as well as the programmatic emphasis on household's involvement in the site improvement and DRR, with a focus on safety and inclusion. DRC is proposing complementary Site Management activities in 2W,2E, 5,6,8E,8W, 11 and 12 and site improvement activities in 2W,2E, 5,6, 11 and 12 only.

Objectives

SO1. Continue to support the Government of Bangladesh Camp in Charge Officials in managing the camps and strengthen their capacity while ensuring equitable and safe of access of refugees, including women and girls to standardized and monitored assistance and protection, against agreed standards, coordinating multi-hazard emergency preparedness. SO2. Holistically upgrade and improve the settlement areas and their immediate surroundings, through community-participatory and mid-term macro planning, linked to construction and maintenance works that enable safe, dignified and resilient living conditions ensuring access is for women, girls and children. Ensuring the centrality of community led decision making. SO3. Strengthen field level coordination, by facilitating joint initiatives on macro settlement and establishing community consultation and dialogue mechanisms. Assessing social cohesion dynamics with host communities in close vicinity of camps.

Needs

While significant improvements can be seen in the coordination and management of camps across DRC's AoRs, a harmonized approach to community action planning is required in order to address the continued gaps stemming across all sectors, notably protection and site development. In particular, the J-MSNA results indicate that almost a quarter of refugees indicated that they were not consulted regarding the services they received, and an even greater number responded that language barriers and jargon were contributing barriers to their interactions with aid workers. In terms of safety and security, refugees reported feeling unsafe in markets, latrines, and at water points, and concerningly, refugees highlighted insufficient access to infrastructure overall. In order to collectively respond to these needs, DRC's SMS-led community groups, volunteers, and the RRRC staff require additional capacity building efforts. Despite the positive impact of site improvement activities to the settlements, the vulnerability of the congested and overpopulated camp settlements, combined with environmental hazards including flooding and landslides and extreme weather events, require continual site improvement works. 44% of refugees reported insufficient camp's infrastructure, 51 % of refugees is facing physical challenges accessing their shelters (MSNA 2019). Households have already taken initiatives to provide drainage or mitigate potential for landslides on the hillsides, and this needs to be capitalized on and scaled up, coupled with the ongoing construction of bridges, steps, pathways, retention walls, and ongoing maintenance of solar lighting, to enhance the physical safety of the site and facilitate access throughout the targeted area.
