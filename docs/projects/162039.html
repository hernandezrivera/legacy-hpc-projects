Project Summary

The objectives of this project is to create livelihood opportunities for the Rohingya Refugees and host communities by providing portable skill packages including training,inputs support, support to agriculture,livestock & fisheries with market linkage.The underlying goal is to improve the food production system, access to food and increase income of Rohingya refugee and host communities.The project will cover total 11000 HHs from 2 Camps (14 & 15) and from 2 host communities (Palong Khali and Raja Palong union). Among the targeted Rohingya beneficiaries (5000), 70% will be female. Regarding the targeted host community beneficiary (6000), the male and female proportion will be 50:50.

Needs

Needs of the Rohingya refugees: The Rohingya Refugees are solely dependent on humanitarian assistances given by Govt., UN, and NGOs. Poor diet, lack of education and livelihood opportunities, precarious health and WASH conditions continue to remain a challenge to Rohingya refugees’ livelihoods and raise serious protection concerns. According to the recent Refugee influx Emergency Vulnerability Assessment (WFP, May 2019), 88% of the overall Rohingya population remains highly to entirely relying on lifesaving assistance. The assessment confirms that the coping patterns of Rohingya population have changed: after having depleted their assets (jewellery, savings) an increased share of households sold part of the food assistance received for cash (at least 40%) and/or borrowed money to meet their food and other essential needs (70%). A significant number of refugees have thus been forced to employ diverse coping strategies to meet their needs. The adoption of these negative coping strategies is higher this year in comparison to the same period last year. As a result of constraints such as lack of land for own food production, very limited opportunity for income generation, movement restrictions, and limited access to cooking fuel and energy sources, the refugee population remains highly dependent on food assistance. Concern Worldwide conducted a rapid assessment across 3 camps (14,18 and 18) in June 2019, where Rohingya refugees were asked which specific assistance they would like to receive in order to improve dietary diversity - and this was cross referenced with observations and secondary data. Rohingya refugees highlighted a need for provisions to undertake home gardening, small scale poultry and fish farming; which are noted as a viable opportunity to enable an increase in their intake of vegetables and animal source proteins which is presently difficult due to the costs involved for inputs. Needs of the host community: The food security of host community has been worsened by diminishing arable land, high cost of production inputs of agriculture, livestock, fisheries and exacerbating harsh climate like drought, heavy rainfall which in turns decreasing the food production, jeopardise the livelihood pattern and increase food insecurity. In addition, refugees have now occupied the land for habitation that were previously used by host communities. As a result, the crops production has dramatically reduced. Simultaneously host community people have lost work opportunities to cheaper refugee labour market. The increasing impact on host communities' livelihoods is contributing to social tensions and resentment between the local population and refugees, particularly over natural resources such as land, water, fish, firewood and labour. The need to support local communities' livelihoods under the guidance of Bangladesh authorities is paramount and will have a key role in mitigating tensions.
