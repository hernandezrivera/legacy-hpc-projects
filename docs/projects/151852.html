Project Summary

The Overall Objective is to improve the living conditions and resilience of vulnerable populations (including migrants, refugees, Internally Displaced Persons, returnees and host communities) , in particular in the Municipalities most affected by migratory flows, by enhancing access to basic WASH services.
Main areas of intervention will include:
- rehabilitation of WASH services (water access and provision, sewage system, latrines and toilets)
-Hygiene promotion campaigns, hygiene kits distribution, implementation of vector control system in targeted detention centers
-Technical Assistance to DCIM and municipalities on how to promote inclusive and coordinated service provision

Needs

Significant needs in water supply, in particularly drinking water, most of the water networks or water treatment plans, if exist are damaged and require significant renovation, moreover, issues with the wash facilities, most of the wash facilities are not functioning neither accessible and in urgent needs for rehabilitation or construction. Furthermore, damaged sewage networks inisde the detention centers, and poor knowledge about basic hygineic practices and vector controls.

Activities

GVC plans to renovate/construct the WASH facilities (latrines, showers) inside the targeted detention centers,host communities , plus rehabilitation/construction of the water supply and sewage network inside. Conduct hygiene promotion session in addition to hygiene kits distribution.Implementation of Vector control system
