Project Summary

The projected goals of our intervention are derived from the evident economic situation of those residing in the targeted villages. Most of the current residents, regardless of country of origin, live well below the poverty line, and suffer its associated negative effects, as the crisis continues to develop and escalate. To this end our intervention will aim to;
To strengthening child protection community-based network and provision of community-based PSS services of the protracted Lebanese, Palestinian, and Migrants crises and to better respond to the needs of these population.
SPHERE Building Tomorrow is proposing a holistic approach, under the umbrella of protection implemented through an ecological model, to provide the targeted groups from children, youth, and adults the “Know-How” of communication and exchanging dialogues with non-violent manners and respecting mutual cultural values. The designed curriculum includes three main modules of a total of 12 sessions addressed to children between 8 and 14 years old (divided into two groups), and 7 sessions (for adults) based on protection key-messages and combine GBV, PSS, life skills, and inclusion sessions (with beneficiaries and staff) among recreational activities (art craft and art, etc.…), with community-based PSS and intergenerational activities addressed to children and adults, located in Akkar- North Lebanon (Massaoudieh & Hissa, Tal Maien, Cheikh Ayash, Koueikhat & Koleiat)
The total number of the targeted population is 464 children and adults (400 Children & 64 Adults), distributed as below:
82% Girls, 82% Boys, 18% Women,18% Men
54% Girls & Women and 36% Boys & Men
88% Lebanese_10%Palestine_2%Migrants.
Children aged between 8 and 14 years old will attend 2 classes in two shifts, respective to each of the four centers located within the specified area. The children will be attending 12 sessions (3 to 4 hours/20 days) and distributed three times per week each group. These sessions will be ended by 2 days of intergenerational activities based on valuable topics of GBV, PSS, life skills and inclusion.
The eligible adults/ youth will benefit from a curriculum of recreational and handicraft activities predefined for 7 sessions (Seven days/once per week).
Sessions for both children and adults will be held to give both the opportunity to debate and display several arguments while respecting the mutual and different point of views. In parallel, the technical team will closely be working with community facilitator/Volunteer and orient them to deliver in their turn our action key-messages to their communities, ensuring sustainability of our action and its objectives and properly responding their needs. Moreover, to ensure that the children and adults are benefiting from this service, they have to attend 70% of the curriculum.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

Outcome 1: To formulate a capacity development response for the staff and community leaders , that addresses those capacities that could be strengthened and optimizes existing capacities: Management, PSS, GBV concepts and advocacy, Outreach process, leadership, etc.Activities: Needs assessment will be conducted in the beginning of the project with the team to identify the topics and deliver the staff with capacity building activities. The targeted population will be selected based on the vulnerability assessment analysis and required criteria Outcome 2: 464 Boys, girls, men and women marginalized, vulnerable &/or at risk of violence have access to an integrated package of quality prevention and response services in Akkar Activities: Children (Boys and Girls) and adults (women and men) with protection needs attend designed technical curriculum (Protection topics, PSS and GBV) on the Capacity Building Training managed and delivered by SPHERE Building Tomorrow from a managerial and technical levels to, SPHERE Building Tomorrow staff, facilitators, and targeted populationOutcome 3: To promote the social cohesion between the the 464 targeted communities alongside with the intergenerational concept by designing, planning and implementing the ceremonyActivities: Children, youth and adults attend a "graduation" at the end of the training to promote social cohesion and intergenerational concepts throughout recreational, art/crafts activities and interactive games.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

• Fuel Crisis and Electricity Interruption: At least one week before the activity, we should ensure that the fuel and gas availability and the transportation for the beneficiaries' is covered to ensure their safe arrivals.• Special attention should be given to prioritize service provision to children with a disability, and females headed-households• COVID 19 Pandemic & PRP: We will apply and commit to the COVID 19 & PRP International measurements or apply remote modalities when deemed necessary.• Safety and Security Conditions: We are ready to reschedule the timeframe of the training or adjust/having access on alternative placements relevant to the training requirements and individuals to ensure individuals safety and secure environments.• The low participation of men participation in CP activities has been a common issue in social work and that has been addressed by proactively seeking to sustain the equivalency between the targeted female and males in our selection process

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

SPHERE Building Tomorrow will conduct a preliminary outreach/vulnerability assessment to benchmark the level of awareness and need in its selected beneficiaries'. We will then employ a month and half re-assessment matrix to track progress as well as verify whether the project is achieving its outcomes on individuals and collective level (pre-posttests, assignments'), as well as conducting a final assessment to track outcome achievability.All activities will be documented with possible audio and video recording, as per the consent of the attendees, to ensure the sustainability of the visibility. All documentation will be retained for the purposes of tracking and transparency. SPHERE Building Tomorrow is committed to providing a final report detailing the project phases and all actions taken throughout the commitment phase, as part of its M&E process.
