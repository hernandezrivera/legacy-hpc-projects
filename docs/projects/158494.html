Project Summary

UNICEF will re-establishing access to educational services for 152,000 school-aged children and play-based activities for 30,400 3-5-year-old children in the most affected areas of Manica, Sofala, Tete and Zambézia; and 15,000 school-aged children and played-based for 2,500 3-5 years old in the most affected areas of Cabo Delgado and Nampula provinces. UNICEF will provide schools with teaching and learning materials, including school- in-a-box, chalkboards, learner kits and ECD kits. The ECD kits target pre-school population in the catchment areas and Child Friendly Spaces (CFS) in partnership with the child protection section. In these locations, they will benefit from play-based activities, bringing a sense of normalcy.
