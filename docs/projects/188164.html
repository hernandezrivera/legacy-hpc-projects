Project Summary

As the Syria crisis approaches its tenth year, the health system remains heavily disrupted. While attacks on health care in 2020 fell by 67 per cent compared to 2019, the COVID-19 pandemic, coupled with economic downturn in the country, has pushed the health system to the brink. By mid-2020, more than 7.78 million people in 100 sub-districts were living below three critical emergency health standards and a localized household survey in North-west Syria found crude and under-five mortality rates were below international standards. With just 58 per cent of hospitals and 53 per cent of Primary Healthcare Centers (PHC) fully functional, the COVID-19 pandemic has exacerbated weaknesses in the health system.
According to the HNO 2021 statistic, during the spread of the Covid-19 virus, women and girls were exposed to more risks, including access to health services, particularly reproductive health services.
There are increased patterns and number of people with disabilities. Close to ten years of crisis have triggered immediate suffering and at the same time severely undermined the ability of communities to recover.
Al-Ameen seeks to meet the needs and ensure continuation of health basic services prioritizing most vulnerable people and have a strong emphasis on women and girls, people with disabilities and older persons and protection.

4 - Needs

10 years of crisis have caused huge damage to the Syrian population eroding its resilience and causing immense suffering. The needs are now gigantic. 4.3 Million people are now in need of health assistance. The project will be implemented in order to address the most urgent needs of the communities of Northwestern Syria in particular.Al-Ameen provided the mentioned health facilities with the necessary equipment and furniture, improved their buildings through maintenance and rehabilitation, put systems and protocols in place, and enhanced their linkages with the referral networks. After this investment in infrastructure and capacity building of the staff, Al-Ameen is now able to provide more efficient and cost-effective health services, without the need to procure major equipment or rehabilitation, and with a focus in the next phase of improving the quality and enhancing systems and integration with other services.People with disability have higher prevalence of multiple chronic conditions, and disability status is documented to be associated with disparities in health care.More than one in four individuals (28 percent) aged 2 and above have disabilities. The rate of persons with disabilities inside Syria is 10 percentage points higher than the 18 percent average of lower income countries and signi􀂦cantly higher than the global average of 15 percent. Also, over a third of the population (37 percent) in NES have disabilities.

5 - Implementation of activities

By supporting these facilities, the provision of PHC services as per the Essential Health Service Package (EHSP) and service delivery modality recommended by the health cluster to provide preventive and curative health services will be secured. Al-Ameen targeted HFs will receive support in the form of payment of salaries for HF staff (medical and non-medical), monthly operational costs, professional training and monitoring, provision of pharmaceuticals, and other medical and non-medical commodities (including equipment, medicines, and consumables). Al-Ameen will collect health data from the HFs monthly and share it accordingly. All targeted HFs are part of the EWARN network and report weekly bulletin to EWARN regularly. Provision of primary health care services as per the (EHSP) and service delivery modality recommended by the health cluster to provide preventive and curative health services. The facilities are supported directly by Al-Ameen, and they are of different levels according to the EHSP and provide all PHC services according to its levels.MEAL Plan:Al-Ameen applies the following MEAL tools:Facility assessment tool and sharing of suggestions and recommendations with the team.Beneficiaries satisfaction surveys.SAFE complaint mechanism: focused on maintaining the complaint response system, by ensuring the feedback channels are in place (complaint Box, WhatsApp and email)Al-Ameen applies IPTT to ensure that the project progress is in line with the work plan.

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

The project staff will mainstream protection principles in all components which include making sure that all beneficiaries have purposeful access to services prioritizing the safety and dignity of them, ensuring non-discrimination and 'do no harm' in the delivery of the services and pursuing a meaningful engagement of the stakeholders, and ensure that confidential reporting mechanisms are in place to address any potential abuse. The project will ensure reaching the most vulnerable persons and provide secondary health care services for them or refer them. The following considerations will be taken in all activities:1-Facilitate the access of the women and girls to the facility in a safe manner, without being exposed to abuse or security risks.2-The facilities are located in easy-to-reach, close, and safe areas in order to avoid any potential risks.3-Ensuring the availability of clean and well-lit toilets in the facility.4-Having a trained and qualified team that is committed to the code of conduct and PSEA principles and works on not to violate these principles knowing that there are disciplinary actions in place. The needs of people and children with disabilities were taken into consideration. The facilities will be provided with measures that the needs of PWDs such as rumps, handrails. Al-Ameen will hire localized and gender-balanced staff, and supervision mechanism will put in place to enhance the quality of services. Referral will be done for complicated cases.


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Abuse or harassment by admin or medical staff towards patients and HH members. Probability: low. Risk severity: high. Mitigation measures: - Recruitment of female CHWs and limited the recruitment of male outreach workers. Organizing teams in two CHWs per household visit and conducting random supervision visits by supervisors; - Training staff on PSEA, code of conduct and ethical data collection; - Ensuring the commitment of staff to Al-Ameen’s PSEA, Child Safeguarding and Code of Conduct policies; - Contracts including a strict term of dismissal towards any misconduct of SEA; - Maintenance and enhancement of Complaint and Feedback mechanism including hot lines for receiving reports and allegationsof SEA misconducts; - Regular surveys conducted by MEAL team to monitor the conducted visits. Surveys include question about team members behaviors, misconducts and their respect of privacy; - Allegations are seriously investigated, where aid workers are subjected to contract termination and further disciplinary actions if allegations were proven. Awareness of GBV and PSEA issues will be incorporated into project activities and beneficiary satisfaction will be verified during household and facility visits which will include female employees to facilitate collecting suggestions/complaints from female beneficiaries especially in relation to GBV and PSEA. All of the CHWs under this project are trained on the PSEA policy during their initial induction.


11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumption 1: Beneficiaries have access to centers to receive services.Risk: people have no access because of security issues.Mitigation: Suspension of the work until release of risk.Assumption 2: COVID-19 prevention and response measures are in place to protect staff and beneficiaries.Risk: Lack of funding of personal protective equipment (PPE), staff and beneficiaries do not comply with the instructions.Mitigation: Encourage staff to take 2 doses of covid 19 vaccination, providing sufficient amounts of PPE and disinfection materials, raising awareness about IPC measures for COVID 19.Assumption 3: Persons with disabilities will be able to attend the HFs. Risk: lack of access for persons with disabilities, or access with being exposed to more harm because the places are not appropriate for their needs.Mitigation: provide the needed transportation to ease the persons with disabilities access to the centers.Assumption 4: Security situation will not deteriorate.Risk: shift in power balance, increased airstrikes, and proximity to ground battles severely compromising access and implementation.Mitigation: Continuous monitoring of the situation and provision of services where accessible. Set a response plan and design the activities in a flexible way where we can change the way of conducting the activity according to the situation so as to achieve the objective, temporarily suspending activities.
