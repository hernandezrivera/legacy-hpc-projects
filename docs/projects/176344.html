Project Summary

Initiative for Peace Communication Association (IPCA), a proactive national leading child protection in emergency programming is undertaking a child protection project with an aim to provide inclusive structured and non-structured static and mobile case management and psychosocial services to vulnerable girls and boys including children with special needs from the IDPs, Host communities and Returnees population in Yei, Lainya, Juba Rumbek North and Duk County.
This project key activities include, i) strengthening and establishment of 18 CFS in Yei, Lainya, Juba Rumbek North and Duk County, ii) provision of static and mobile community-based case management and psychosocial support services to vulnerable girls and boys, iii) provision of dignity kits to school going adolescent girls and child survivors of SGB, iv) establishment and strengthening of 12 community-based child protection networks at County level, v) establishment of ten child protection committees at the Payam level, vi) conduct data collection on grave violations of child rights, establishment and strengthening of community-based child protection referral pathways in Yei, Lainya, Juba Rumbek North and Duk County, vii) conduct sexual reproductive health education to adolescent girls & boys.
This project expects to provide vulnerable with comprehensive case management and psychosocial support services and mitigate risks for children in Yei, Lainya, Juba Rumbek North and Duk County.
The project will collaborate with the Ministry of Gender, Child and Social Welfare, UNICEF, TdH, TiTi Foundation and SSUHA health pooled funded project for clinical case management at hospital and health centers. This project duration is 12 months at a cost of USD$200,000.
The protracted violent conflict in Yei, Lainya, Juba Rumbek North and Duk County continues to negatively impact on the protection environment for children and their families. Displacement and lack of critical social services for children means a compromised childhood for many children in Yei, Lainya, Juba Rumbek North and Duk County. An Inter-Agency reports for Jonglei, Lakes and Central Equatoria State presents urgent needs for child protection lifesaving services for survivors of rape, sexual assault, child labor, CP-GBV, separated families, psychological distress, physical assault, forced child marriage or psychological/emotional abuse. The needs assessment report also presented need to expand awareness on explosive remnants, sexual reproductive education and outreach activities on child rights and data collection on grave violation of child rights based on The Child Act 2008 and UN Security Council resolution 1612 and 1888. Based on this emergency child protection needs, IPCA is responding with child protection prevention and emergency lifesaving services which include comprehensive Case management, structured and non-structured psychosocial support packages, establishment and strengthening of community based child protection structures, expand awareness on explosive remnants, sexual reproductive education, creation of space for children with focus on girls and children with special needs, child labor, recruitment of children into armed forces, safety Audit, referrals, post rape services, forceful marriages and other inclusive CP-GBV services in all the ten Payams of Yei and Kajo-keji counties Yei, Lainya, Juba Rumbek North and Duk County.

7.4.2. How will your project provide beneficiaries information about Protection against Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA)?

This project will mainstream sexual exploitation and abuse campaign across project activities given the grimly reported egregious widespread rape cases and related violence in the country. Through this project IPCA outreach activities will reverberate far and beyond informing local partners, vendors, stakeholders and beneficiaries of clear reporting lines with IPCA and other sexual exploitation abuse taskforces across South Sudan. Besides, the agency through this project will spearhead advocacy against sexual exploitation and abuse. The project staff will sign IPCA SEA code of conduct to hold them accountable of their actions throughout the project implementation. IPCA has identified prevailing perpetrators of conflicts and will take leverage of its other sector projects on access to justice and rule of law as well as peace building and conflict mitigation to aggressively campaign for national policies that mitigate and eradicate sexual exploitation and abuse. In addition, IPCA organizes a series of dialogues, workshops and public lectures on protection at national and state level to generate ideas and host of issues on sexual exploitation and abuse. Furthermore, we will advance actions on women empowerment, gender discrimination and Sexual Exploitation and Abuse, by spreading the word that “one victim is too many”. Empower women and girls to better understand and effectively prevent, respond, report cases of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse and other forms of sexual gender based violence and to reflect the belief that sexual gender based violence isn’t just an issue involving a crime committed by a perpetrator against a victim, but one in which the rest of us also have a role to play.

7.4.4. How will your project ensure feedback from the most vulnerable members (e.g. individuals living with disabilities, individuals who are not literate, women and girls)?

This project needs assessment, planning and design had incorporated needs of the IDPs population in this project locations by sex, age and vulnerability, so that each group of beneficiaries get assistance based on their needs. The key IDPs concerns identified in the project needs assessment are distance from schools, health facilities, water points, cultural norms, religious norms, mobility for people with disability and sexual exploitation and abuse of beneficiaries seeking for employment and humanitarian assistance. This project will pay close attention to needs of each group and will also coordinate with other partners to refer other issues which could not be immediately addressed by this project.

7.4.1. How will your project provide beneficiaries information about the project, feedback mechanism and/or complaint mechanism?

The project will establish project management committee which will include community leaders, beneficiaries and disadvantaged groups and conduct monthly project management committee meetings to include beneficiaries views in the project implementation

7.4.3. How will your project use the beneficiaries’ feedback to adapt project assistance?

This project will establish several platforms (community project committees’ monthly meetings, stakeholders’ monthly meetings, beneficiaries’ working groups as ways to share information with stakeholders and collect feedback on the project. It will establish gender-sensitive beneficiaries’ help desks at community level and establish phone lines and emails and the social media for beneficiaries to raise complaints directly to the leadership of IPCA at the main office. This project will conduct participatory joint M&E missions that will include focus group discussions to create a space for the beneficiaries to share their ideas and concerns with the partners, stakeholders and IPCA senior management.

8.11. Mitigation Measures

The project needs assessment captured broad protection risks including security access, presence of explosive objects and violence against women, girls and other vulnerable, when seeking for services like water, education, markets, health, farmlands and humanitarian aid. Such assessment is conducted to inform the project of the protection risks that girls, women, men, people with special needs and other disadvantaged groups face in the target project locations. This project will design risk mitigation strategies that best address needs of specific groups identified during the participatory assessment. Above all, IPCA will utilize its project logical framework development expertise in simulating a comprehensive log frame that underlines its key components, including assumptions and risks to determine the most appropriate project risks to be mitigated.
