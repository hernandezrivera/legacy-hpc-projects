Project Summary

This project will be implemented in Al-Hasakah Governorate in (Al-Hol Camp)
With the aim of providing support for life-saving and sustainable non-food items to the
displaced and refugees in Al-Hol camp, especially during the winter in the years 2024-
2025, because it will provide them with protection from rain and weather factors,
especially since these families cannot secure their basic needs.
For this reason, the project is of great importance because it provides protection and
warmth from weather factors and the harsh cold of winter.
Through this project, a plastic sheet will be distributed to each tent inside the camp,
distributed as follows:
4267 plastic sheet for Syrian family tents.
5407 plastic sheet for Iraqi family tents.
That is, the total number of plastic sheets that will be distributed in 2024 is 9674, and the
same number during the year 2025.
The specifications of each plastic sheet will be:
Size: 4x6 m, Weight: 4800g ±5%, Made of woven high-density polyethylene (HDPE) black
fibers fabric laminated on both sides with low density polyethylene (LDPE) coating.
Reinforced rims by heat sealing on all sides, Provided with aluminum eyelets or
equivalent on four sheet sides of the single sheets, Color: White sun reflective on both
sides, gray bands. "
Sham El Khair Foundation will implement the project in accordance with quality and
quality control requirements for the entire distribution service and for non-food
specifications in order to meet the required needs.
The project will apply mainstreaming protection through the principles of do no harm and
non-discrimination in project design, implementation and monitoring. Priority will be given
to the safety and security of the distribution site, in addition to meaningful access to
people with disabilities, with the possibility of the distribution team going to people who
are not able to reach the distribution site.
Risk Management:

- Enforce reporting mechanisms embedded in anti-fraud policies

Accelerate the completion of tenders and the implementation of contracts with a permanent analysis of prices in the market

Likelihood: Medium
