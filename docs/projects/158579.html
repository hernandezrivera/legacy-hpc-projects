Project Summary

Include all that apply to your program (only the activities below approved):
• Distribution of point-of-use water treatment materials / products, including training and monitoring on proper use at household level.
• Rehabilitation and disinfection of existing water points affected by cyclone / flooding and re-establishment / support for water committees.
• Construction of new water sources for most vulnerable communities / resettlement areas.
• Installation and management of temporary sanitation and bathing facilities in accommodation centres / transit camps and / or institutions.
• Promotion activities for self-construction of household latrines and garbage pits in long-term resettlement and returning areas (including the provision of sanitation supplies for most vulnerable households).
• Community and household level hygiene promotion activities in accommodation centres, return communities, and related institutions.
• Procurement and distribution of WASH related non-food items, with focus in most vulnerable families / households.
