Project Summary

PALRI through its role as CCCM partner managing Gubio IDP Camp, will continue to address life­saving needs of IDPs in Gubio LGA by expanding its coverage to IDPs in Camp-like settlement, responding to new displacements, improving and upgrading basic services in sites, and finally, supporting the overall humanitarian response. Through this project, PALRI will address the Sectors overall objective of improving the living conditions inside IDP sites and facilitate the immediate and targeted delivery of services to the
displaced population.
PALRI will continue to support emergency life­saving interventions of the wider humanitarian community through the maintenance, and management of humanitarian camps in Gubio LGA. To immediately address needs and gaps identified through the need assessment conducted. PALRI will facilitate and utilize the assessment conduced to support current management and coordination to respond to increasing need the IDPs and camp-like settlement.
Throughout the project, the needs of women, girls, men and boys of all ages will be addressed all throughout the planning, implementation and monitoring and evaluation of the activities. Through the CCCM and regular site monitoring, needs and gaps will be reported and shared with partners and all stakeholders including all disaggregated data. Particular attention regarding needs will be paid to women and girls who also
have to content with GBV and protection concerns within camp and camp-like settlement. Relevant consultations will be made with the Protection cluster and GBV subcluster,
particularly in site development, site management, to enhance PALRI’s response to address the special needs of the IDP population.

1 - Needs

The CCCM Cluster prioritized activities through assessments and reports from the field, and through consultations with the humanitarian and beneficiarycommunities. Needs and gaps are identified through management and coordination with partners, conducting need assessment and findings during sector meetings. Based on these findings, the sectoridentifies priority locations and activities that the sector can support and carry out. The needs have been identified and explained in the detailed need assessment conducted by PALRI. The number of beneficiaries has been calculated through the sector site tracker process. This analysis has been proven to be effective at estimating the numbersof people in the sites. Data captures certain categories of vulnerabilities (including female headed households/disabilities/unaccompaniedminors or elders). The information calculated is disaggregated by age, se and disability. This data serves as a baseline for all organizations operating in camps and camplike settings and informs needs assessments that inform the provision of assistance against SPHERE standards that PALRI holds itself accountable to. This processis paramount for detailing gaps and mobilizing resources for meeting these standards for the benefit of IDPs. All of these assessments and tools capture specifically the gender associated needs which are described below.Findings during need assessment conducted by PALRI in GUBIO LGA Camp Coordination & Camp Management (CCCM)- The settlements ranges from informal settlement to more camp like settings (scatteredsettings).- Settlement is arranged based on local community leaders and location of origins(communities from Gubio are clustered together).- Population data is based on estimation and KII/FGD (Biometrics not captured by anyhumanitarian partner). As per estimated information there about 6596 HH, (Malari has 900 HH,Bazam has 2,200 HH, Gubio II Primary School has 540 HH, & Malamsuri has 2,956 HH). Shelter/NFIs- Based on observation, majority of displaced IDPs lived in inadequate shelter (with sheltermaterials ranging from makeshift, clothing’s, school buildings and locally assemble zinc.- Families in Gubio LGA particularly in (Bazam, Malari, MalamSuri, & Gubio II PrimarySchool camp) either lived in informal settlement or spontaneous settlement. Whereasothers, constructed dwellings with mud walls and a thatched roof.- Majority of the IDPs have lacked standard NFI kit.- Over 60% of the shelter & tarpaulin were damaged.- The IDPs were desperately in need of shelter kits to repair their existing shelters. WASH- There are ten boreholes available in Gubio of which 6 are functional & 4 were damagedrequires minor repairs/maintenance.- Due to unclear demarcation of the sites, the clarity of the borehole being in the sites is hardhence IDPs with Host Communities use same source of water.- Each IDP fetch less than 15L per day due to low yield of the water facilities.- House to house hygiene promotion campaign to mitigate hygiene risk behaviors need to beconducted. Health- Malaria, Typhoid fever are the major illnesses affecting returnees and IDPs in the camps.- Increased supply of basic drugs, mosquito nets and supplements are most needed at present.- Insufficient clinic in Gubio LGA. 8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

This project will contribute to addressing lifesaving needs through camp coordination and camp management, in line with the aims and objectives of the CCCM Sector. This CCCM supported intervention is consistent with the basic humanitarian principles of humanity, neutrality, and impartiality. The project will support the delivery of current essential lifesaving services to continue protecting the lives of the most vulnerable groups in the escalating protection concern, particularly women, and children in the emergency situation. This project operates with the understanding that activities will take into account equity principles that promote the protection of women and girls. This CCCM project also take into consideration cross­cutting issues, and at all stages of the project cycle, PALRI practitioners work with experts from Health and, WASH, amongst others, to ensure that programming is effective, targeted and making the most of key resources and staff for the benefit of IDPs. This approach is only possible due to the emphasis PALRI places on working directly with beneficiary to ensure effective communication and interventions. This reduces overlap and duplication and provides resources where needed the most.

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

The propose activity will ensure the involvement of all target groups and beneficiaries at all stages of the project. Feedback mechanism will be implemented in the communities based on the desire and ideas gathered from the beneficiaries using FGD/KII. The mechanism will allow community members to provide feedback on the activities, about service rendered and challenge face during the implementation process. PALRI has existing suggestion box place in strategic position in GUBIO IDP CAMP for collection of feedback from the displaced communities. PALRI has presence in the community and a designated feedback mechanism through meeting in the IDPs in GUBIO LGA. PALRI will conduct post intervention monitoring and evaluation to ensure proper feedback.
