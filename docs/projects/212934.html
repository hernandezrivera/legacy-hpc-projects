Project Summary

The project aims to increase resilience and enhance the community's access to basic services and livelihood opportunities in the Qadi Askar area in the Jebel Saman district in Aleppo. The initiative embodies three key outcomes: supporting basic services, increasing livelihood access, and strengthening social cohesion. Through a multifaceted approach, the project endeavors to install street lighting, remove debris, provide livelihood support, and empower communities, thereby fostering sustainability and inclusivity. This intervention will contribute to increasing safety and security in the community, enhancing community vitality, improving health and the environment, and creating opportunities for economic improvement and new job opportunities. It will also empower households in the community to achieve self-sufficiency and promote social cohesion and community participation.
At its core, the project resonates with the essence of recovery and renewal. Strengthening access to basic services includes lighting the streets through the installation of 20 solar-powered lighting panels. Beyond lighting, this initiative increases safety, security, and a renewed sense of community vitality, especially after dusk, while it will promote a safe feeling among the community members who move into the community during the evening. Concurrently, addressing environmental concerns becomes paramount, with plans to remove 5000 cubic meters of debris and 3500 tons of waste, thereby alleviating economic burdens and health hazards that mainly may cause in the dark evening where there is no lights in the streets.
Yet, the project's ambitions extend far beyond infrastructure. Recognizing the intertwined nature of livelihoods and community well-being, it ventures into economic empowerment. For 100 individuals, a lifeline emerges in the form of short-term Cash for Work opportunities, offering not just financial support but a chance to reclaim dignity through meaningful engagement. Moreover, 130 aspiring entrepreneurs receive tailored training and financial grants, heralding a new era of self-sufficiency and enterprise. Through vocational skills training, 70 young beneficiaries will find pathways to employment, underscoring the project's commitment to long-term prosperity.
The project will also equip 30 young volunteers with advocacy skills and community engagement tools to ensure community empowerment and ownership of the project. This will support the volunteers in mobilizing their peers and neighbors around common goals and initiatives, whether organizing community clean-up campaigns, advocating for improved access to services, or facilitating dialogue on pressing issues, the project will support the community to create their ERL plan and led the ERL process in their community.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The proposed project for the Qadi Askar area in Aleppo City adopted a comprehensive and participatory approach to address the pressing needs of the community, adhering to the three outcomes outlined in the call for proposal. Through a series of interconnected initiatives, the project aims to address local community-identified challenges by empowering beneficiaries, enhancing basic and social services, and fostering social cohesion, ultimately contributing to a revitalized and resilient community.The proposed project for the Qadi Askar area in Aleppo city is designed based on the priorities and needs identified by the community itself. The project team also coordinated with the ERL subsector in Aleppo to obtain additional perspective and a comprehensive understanding of the community's situation. The project's needs, intervention logic, and activities were thoroughly discussed and validated with the ERL subsector in Aleppo. The ERL subsector expressed strong support for the project activities and goals. They emphasized the project's potential to address the critical needs of the Qadi Askar community and contribute to its revitalization and resilience.The proposed project demonstrates a strong commitment to complementarity and collaboration in addressing the needs of the Qadi Askar community. It seeks to leverage existing efforts, enhance coordination with relevant stakeholders, and encourage broader engagement from other actors to maximize its impact, ensure alignment with local priorities, and promote sustainable development in the area.Currently, there are no active actors in this area other than UNDP, as per the feedback of the ERL subsector. Therefore, the project will build upon UNDP's past and ongoing efforts to demonstrate a continuous commitment to addressing the community's pressing needs. UNDP's previous interventions in the area have focused on addressing critical infrastructure needs, enhancing basic and social services, and fostering community resilience. The project team will maintain ongoing coordination with UNDP and the ERL subsector to ensure alignment with broader recovery and development goals. This coordination will involve regular communication, sharing of information, and joint planning to maximize the impact of interventions and avoid duplication of efforts. The project will also collaborate closely with the municipality and governorate to facilitate debris and waste removal, disposal, and street lighting. This collaboration will ensure that the project's interventions are aligned with local priorities and regulations and that they contribute to the overall development of the Qadi Askar area.
