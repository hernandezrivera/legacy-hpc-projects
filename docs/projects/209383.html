Project Summary

The World Vision implementation for 2024 is majorly guided by the Humanitarian Needs overview focusing on the current Sudan Crisis that has generated plenty of Nutrition needs country wide. World Vision will continue its operations in South Darfur, South Kordofan, East Darfur and Blue Nile. Looking at the nutrition severity scale, most of the localities of these states still leave a lot to be desired and hence the need for intensified nutrition care and support. Due to the conflict that is persistently looming, World Vision has for now had presence in Al Gezira state and is open to extend its emergency and fragile nutrition lifesaving interventions in any other state that turns out to be in need due to a population explosion due to IPDs and returnees.
World Vision is operating on the guidance of its current Sudan Crisis management framework that was develop to ensure World Vision advocates for more funding from a number of donors to focus on the looming current highest displacement population on the content. This as seen through the PIN lens is affected availability of food and affecting mostly children under the age to 5 years and pregnant and lactating women and girls. World Vision has set to prioritise nutrition interventions that are globally approved as lifesaving in IPDS, returnees and Vulnerable host communities in Sudan.
This FY2024, World Vision affirms its commitment to invest in nutrition information management system through the timely provision of 4Ws and other nutrition data to the Cluster. This includes the fortnight needs for the coverage, supplies and any human resource capacities and gaps. Further still strengthening the Community nutrition systems that promote early warning and response system for key glaring nutrition situations in the states where WVI operate. This information includes the IYCF-E updates through nutrition volunteers, Mother support Groups, community Health workers, nutrition workers. This will be integrated with the health management and information system to ensure 100% complementarity at the household level.
World Vision Current operational sites are struggling with the effects of war and hence nutrition supplies, medicines and consumables are very inadequately procured. World Vision further appreciates the role played by the current inflation that has hiked all prices of nutrition supplies, further affecting the Human resources for nutrition care, and secondment rates. And hence is factoring all these in the current suggested lifesaving interventions. The elimination of barriers when setting up an OTP or a MSG will be key to ensure availability of supplies for Nutrition preventive approaches and those that contribute to curative strategies.
World Vision will roll out IYCF-E and CMAM interventions a project level. To ensure Robust IYCF-E, at the PHU level, there will be strengthened guidance on the BFHI principles while at the community level there will be availability of messaging for good practices including complementary feeding. Care of children below the age of 5 will be at the forefront ensuring that prevention of the onset of any form of malnutrition.
