Project Summary

Humanitarian and rehabilitation needs have increased in the Tigray, Amhara, and Afar regions due to the violence in Northern Ethiopia. Kilbati/Zone-2 is one of the zones in Afar significantly impacted by the escalating war along the border with Tigray, resulting in many people being displaced within the woreda and zones and also to other neighbouring woredas in the Zone.
Multi-agency assessment was conducted on the 9th and 10th of February 2022 where ANE was part of the mission. The assessment report revealed that more than 294,000 people have been displaced from the Zone's six Woredas, according to a recent humanitarian situation summary from the regional Disaster Prevention and Food Security Coordination Office (DPFSCO). Erebti, Berahile, Koneba, Berahle, and Dalol Woredas are currently hosting these displaced people.
In Berhale woreda, more than 53,590 persons displaced so far within the different kebeles of Berhale and to the neighbouring Afdera and Kori Woredas. Every IDP site (collective Center) is seeing an increase in the number of IDPs.
The assessment report revealed that IDPs from the conflict areas are fled to Different and other rural kebeles of the neighboring woreda with no resources as the rebels are entering into the city suddenly. As a result, they do not have time to collect food & non-food items and other basic tools at home for their children, elders and themselves while leaving their original place. All displaced persons are currently living in collective sites, with their host community as well as majority of them living in open spaces without basic shelter and household NFIs. Emergency shelter kits and household NFI items like Kitchen sets, bedding sets, women and children closes, jerrycans and soaps are mentioned as priority needs among the displaced people. ANE has proposed to target and reach 87,867 most vulnerable conflict affected IDPs in Awra, Hanruka, Abaala, Kunneba, Megale, Chifra and Kori woredas to provide ESNFI and Returnee package kits assistance.
According to the multi-agency assessment recommendation, emergency shelter construction (Tarpaulin sheet, wooden pole, rope) materials and household NFIs and returnee packages are very much needed by the affected conflict induced IDPs. Under this project plan, ANE proposed to reach a total of 87,867 most vulnerable conflict affected IDPs and Returnees.
