Project Summary

The proposed project for 2022 will contribute to the Borno development plan.
Project will focus on providing non food item (NFI) and emergency shelter kits for the
recently displace people due to insurgency, food and fire outbreak. A multi sectorial rapid
assessment will be conducted with in 72 hours from the alert received to identify
emergency needs of women, girls, boys and men including disable people. 45,000
individuals (10100 men, 13800 women, 7000 boys and 14100 girls) will be benefiting from
NFI and emergency shelter.

1 - Needs

According to the OCHA report October 2021, the continued influx of new arrivals into already overcrowded camps and communities has increased the risk of illnesses spreading, particularly cholera and COVID-19. CCCM and shelter partners are struggling to secure additional land to expand and decongest the camps. Some 3,400 households are in dire need of shelter repair kits across camps and host communities due to the impact of floods and windstorms. Over 6,300 households are in reception centres that are equipped to provide temporary housing for new arrivals for a maximum of two weeks. Over 67,000 complaints received hinged on NFI shortages, especially blankets, mats and also cooking utensils. The Sector site tracker tool recorded multiple gaps, especially the lack of NFI and shelter repair kits, across several camps following floods and windstorms. Efforts are underway to address the most critical gaps based on available resources and capacity.

2 - Activities

Distribution of NFIs and Shelter kits will be done together with hygiene kits proposedunder WASH sector to help women, men, boys and girls maintain their dignity duringhumanitarian crises. Preserving dignity is essential to maintaining self-esteem andconfidence, which is important to cope in stressful and potentially overwhelminghumanitarian situations. Supporting women’s self-esteem and confidence also assiststhem in providing care and protection to their children. Generic hygiene kits will helppeople improve cleanliness (with items such as jerry can, soap, sanitary materials) andwhilst NFIs and Shelter kits are similar to get access of shade and daily routine kitchenutensils hygiene in hostile emergency. All kits are designed in partnership with RRMoperational group with affected communities to ensure the most appropriate items areincluded, and distribution is carried out in a manner that minimizes the risk of GBVagainst women and girls. Kits will be distributed in kind considering facts that IDPs andreturnees will not be in position to buy, secondly proposed NFI/Shelter and Hygiene Kit’sitems will be not available at local market also limited market accessibility in remoteareas. Kits help target group by: • Contributing to the psychosocial and physical wellbeingof women and girls, by including items such as intimate wash and pads, and improvingmobility of women and girls by providing specific sanitary items. • Contributing to theprotection of women and girls, by including items such as flashlights, so that women andgirls do not have to walk in the dark. • NFI/Shelter kits will enable HHs to build shade andto cook food for family, and household items for use such as mates for sleeping will behelpful. Activity 01: Distribution of emergency shelter kits Emergency shelter kits will bedistributed to the recently displaced IDPs to ensure their safety and security. Project teamwill coordinate with shelter and NFI cluster to support the affected population. A total of500 shelter kits will be distributed only for targeted most affected HHs in a location whereshelter and NFI partners are not able to immediately access the locations. The shelter kitswill consist of two tarpaulin sheets, one rope, umbrella nail packs, hammer, and wood. Atthe same time, AAH will coordinate with shelter cluster partners to support long termsolutions for shelter. Activity 02: Distribution of emergency NFI kits The NFI kits will bedistributed to the recently displaced or returnee IDPs to ensure that they have access tobasic non-food items. A total of 2000 CCCM/NFI kits will be distributed, and the kitincludes three plates, three cups, four cooking pots, one kitchen knife, three tablespoons,three blankets, three sleeping mats, foldable mattress, seven laundry soaps, seven bathingsoaps, four reusable pads, two 10l and one 20litres jerry cans, one 2l kettle, two 10litresbuckets, one 10litre basin, two mosquito nets, two serving spoons, one carrying bag andone solar flashlight.
