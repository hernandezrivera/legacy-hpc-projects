Project Summary

Project Summary (maximum 50 words) The project targets 450 vulnerable households (150 hh/district) with temporary shelter facilities in 3 Districts of Bikita, Buhera and Chipinge rural at a cost of $404,470. Implementers commit to prevent miconduct and safeguarding beneficiary rights with consideration of COVID 19 dictates. 1 - Activities

Key Activities: Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting. (maximum 250 words) In partnership with relevant clusters in all the 3 Districts of Bikita, Buhera and Chipinge e.g. Shelter, WASH and Health clusters the following activities will be carried out:- Employ participatory and consultative approaches in conducting rapid needs assessments in communities affected by Cyclone Idai, the current drought and anticipated floods to verify, prioritise and update registers of households in need of temporary shelter. This is to ensure the appropriateness of the planned assistance, and ensure strong ownership by beneficiaries of project activities. Subsequent needs assessments will be conducted in recognition of the anticipated La Nina phenomena.-Enhance training of field operations staff and communities on COVID 19 and CHS.-Train community members on constructing temporary shelter facilities.-Provide temporary shelter to dispersed 450 households. This project is already complementing previous shelter support under the ACT Alliance project.- Adopt shelter standards from previous projects which were certified by the Department of Public Construction and National Housing.-Enhance Safeguarding and Accountability mechanisims to reduce risks Communities will be made aware of Complaints and Response Mechanisms (CRM) and safeguarding which will also contribute to less harassment of women and girls.-Attend coordination meetings.-Carry out monitoring and evaluation of the project to feed into reporting.

8 - Accountability to Affected People
8.1 - Describe how your project was designed in consultation with communities? How will you incorporate communities in project implementation and what feedback mechanisms do you have in place?

The project will use consultative village meetings to reprioritise the deserving cases in need of urgent temporary shelter. These meetings will be conducted through the coordination of local leadership represented by traditional leaders, the councillor and any other relevant committee e.g. DRR structures. In all meetings at least 65% should be women. Most of the beneficiaries will be women, children, elderly, the disabled and those living with chronic illnesses not in a position to gainfully provide for families. CA and partners use the help desk, toll free lines and suggestion boxes that are put up at each activity . Responses will always be given there and there were possible or reffered to relevant stakeholders. mechanisms will be strengthened by CA and partner with dashboards consolidated and shared on action taken. COMPASS, a digital feedback mechanism with direct link to Head Office will be shared, this is managed by focal persons at community level to promote safeguarding and accountability. Local authorities will facililitate a coordinated response. The project will establish synergies at district level with other CSOs to address complementary needs of the community members. Mapping of other stakeholders and activities in the same communities will be conducted. This approach will be promoted appropriate package of interventions by different stakehlders.

9 - Environmental considerations
9.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

CA and its partners are committed to the protection of the environment by integrating environmental considerations into the planning and implementation of all development initiatives, regardless of their sector of focus. Effects of social and environmental vulnerabilities will be reduced through building capacity of communities and institutions to prepare and respond to disasters through effective civil protection capacities from government. The response will identify possible remedies to environmental rehabilitation, and will ensure that no negative environmental impacts will come out as a result of the interventions. Contamination of the environment will be reduced through safe disposal of residual building materials , garbage collection and safe hygiene behaviour. There is the WASH and Shelter cluster experts to be consulted specifically on proper mounting of tents ensuring safety and WASH programs. It is expected that the project will have a positive impact on the environment since it utilises limited resources from the environment and there is going to be proper excreta disposal reducing improper disposal of human waste thereby improving hygiene standards in turn contributing to enhanced livelihoods through reduction of communicable diseases due to overcrowding.
