Project Summary

This project is designed to address the assessed needs of 22,000 individuals of protracted is IDPs in Bileil camps are coping with the situation displacement due to the long settlement in th camps in Kalma, El salam camps, Hijer Tono, Sani Affendu and Bileil town, who represent 24% of the target beneficiaries in the project. Huge number 59,810 of returnees from different camps in Bileil and Nyala town to the Locality to several areas in Bileil Locality represent 68.5% of the total target of the project. Vulnerable host communities and 11,700 agro- pastoral and host communities who represent 8.5%. Great Family Organization (GFO) has a good knowledge Buram and Bileil localities, GFO is now co-camp Coordinator in Kalma camp with OCHA since 2014 and also the first National Coordinator of El Salam Camp in 2007 when settling the camp and also working in Buram for longtime in emergency intervention including NFIs and FSL and education. the project is designed to target 70% and above women 15% men and 13% youth both gender and 2 % children Headed HH.
The project aims to seek for FSL resilience and emergency based interventions for the conflict affected returnees, IDPs and the host communities in accessible locations: Hijer Tono, Sani Afendu, Eshma, Bileil town villages in Bileil locality and Buram town Assunta, in Buram locality to improved access to agricultural and livestock inputs to affected 27,090 HH (135,460 individual). For agriculture, the project target 18,702 HH (93,510 individuals) 70% women, 20% men, 15% people with disability and 5% children headed HH and Elders over 70 years are provided with sorghum and millet seed crops for seasonal plantation to increase means of livelihood as sorghum and millet are the main food of Darfurians. 22,000 vegetable farmers (23%) among them 80% women 15% men and 5% are given vegetable seeds and tools to produce complementary food items and raise the economical capacity of the HH for livelihood sustainability. 60% of the farmers for both crop and vegetable are provided with agricultural hand tools: hoe, plough and rakes (13,000 pieces). 210 Community Extension workers (CEW) are trained to increase beneficiary production and productivity for certain sustainability in the area.
The intervention addresses livestock needs to strengthen livelihood of the pastorals 11,700 who represent 2% of the total targeted, sharing the same areas. they own 120,000 livestock: 90,000 cattle, 12,000 camels and 28,000 sheep and goat will be treated and vaccinated through training of 60 CAHWs where 15% women 75% to maintain animal treatment and vaccination. In this intervention 300,000 doses of vaccination will be provided including Anthrax, Sheep Pox, BQ PPR and 60 CAWHs’ kits and veterinary drugs will be given to CAWHs to reduce morbidity and mortality of the livestock and increase animal production and quality healthy food and also avoid border diseases during its seasonal movement from the South to the North and so on.
The project also contributes to cooperate with other project to enhance peace in the areas through creation of village Community Development Groups Peace Process where 25% women are part of the native leaders’ meetings to solve farmers/ nomads problem and has a strong voice in the activities of the project.
2,500 female HH are provided with 4 sheep for restoring to improve self-resilience. to contribute to women empowerment 400 women are targeted to be trained to make efficiency stove to reduce desertification (Decreasing firewood) 420 youth 60% male, 40% female are trained in environment shelter construction (SSB).
All project activities will be supported technically by the line Ministries of South Darfur and incorporated with the HRP, DRR, and DO NO HARM matching to this project.

1 - Needs

The needs reflected in the project are based on different surveys and assessments carried out by several FSL actors: Sudan Comprehensive food security Assessment, WFP for Food Security Monitoring System (FSMS) 2018, FAO for Crop and Food supply Assessment Mission; The IPC comparison between June-August 2019 and January-March 2019 to October 2018, and WFP Market Monitor August 2019, beside the recent information and observation collected by our NGO and State FSL sector shared with partners in the FSL Working Group in South Darfur. It is also based on IPC June-August 2019 valid up to 2020. All these assessments and surveys indicated that the food security situation in Bileil and Buram localities has worsen because of the increase of cereal prices and the low income of the population, these price are expected likely to continue increasing despite of the good rate of rainy rain fall of the last season due to the increased price of the agriculture inputs and the unexpected amount of rain and duration of the rainy season continued up to October 2019, that spoiled about 30% of groundnut's harvest as a cash crop and about 10% of the sorghum and the millet, beside the birds and insects witch destroyed about 2% at the beginning of the harvest. All this shows the rapid increase of price of cereal, the sorghum and vegetable where people are dependent on.The number target beneficiaries of the protracted IDPs in Bileil Locality are 22,000 (4,400 HH) in Kalma, El Salam and El Sireif camps 70% are women and 2% are children headed HH and 28% men, the permanent returnees from Nyala camps and other Bileil Locality are 43,500 (8,700 HH) in the villages named: Hijer Tono, Sani Afendu, Bileil town in Bileil and Buram town, 65% women and 35% men and the sedentary PiP as host community are 11,700 ( 2340 HH) in the localities where by 70% women and 40 men.Protracted IDPs and returnees lost their opportunity of food assistance and their livelihood deteriorated and will decrease further if the project did not intervene timely. To address the needs of the affected population in Phase 3 according to the IPC of June-August 2019 and the VAM food security analysis indicated that: 43,500 returnees, protracted IDPs and host vulnerable host communities in Bileil and Buram localities in need of immediate rainy and winter season crop and vegetable seeds and tools. In Bileil locality, inter-agency assessment indicated that the need of vegetable seeds and tool for the rainy and winter is an immediate and urgent,; beside the animal vaccination and treatment belonging 11,700 pastors in Buram locality and a minority in Bileil. They have two main nomadic road crossing it from the South to the North twice a year. In Buram people are depend mainly in groundnut as a cash crop. Millet, sorghum are the essential part of the food, melon is he only fruit they have. No valley suitable for vegetable production in Buram except Okra which can be planted in the sandy ground. In Bileil all categories of people are very fragile as the area witnessed very long confrontation between the GOS and the Opposition. The area is became very poor in term of green belt, trees are all cut for firewood and construction, our intervention will target 6,000 women for Efficiency Stoves and 1000 youth both sexes for SSB to decrease dependency on wood for shelter construction and also. In Buram the project will mainly focus on livestock production by training 60 CAHWs (40 in Buram and 20 in Bileil) with provision of Kits and vaccination and treatment drugs, establish two veterinary centers to be equipped to expand animal vaccination and treatment to avoid border diseases, support extension services to improve crop and animal production, awareness for farmers and agro-pastoralist to change their poor cultural practices, support opening animal route to avoid confrontation and build resilience to sustain livelihood system.

2 - Activities / Outputs

Activities/outputs 1.1 -Provision of life-saving emergency agriculture and livestock/veterinary inputs, extension services and kits during or in the aftermath of a shock - Provision of crops seeds to 8,700 HH (43,500 farmers) and tools to 70% returnees, IDPs and host farmers, 70% female and 30% male where 1,600 HH in Buram and (11,400 HH) in Bileil. Amount of seed ration 10 kg of millet, 10 kg of sorghum and 20kg of groundnut per HH. - Provision of tools to crops production to 45% with animal plough and 55%% with hoe.-To supply vegetable seed to 1800 HH in Bileil with 20 g Okra, 10 g tomato, 15 g cucumber, 25 g of onion and 30 g of melon per each HH.- Provide 100 g melon seeds and 20 Okra to 1,600 HH where 70% are female- Provision of hoe to 1,600 HH for vegetable production in Buram.- Provision vaccination services to 130,000 livestock segregated by type: 90,000 cattle, 30,000 goat and sheep and 10,000 camel in bileil and Buram.- Refreshment training to 60 CAHWs with provision of vaccination and treatment kits and drugs. to reduce animal losses (pest and animal diseases control, animal vaccination and treatment) and food preservation techniques- Conduct and disseminate 4 food security and livelihoods related assessments pre and Pro harvest.- Conduct capacity building of our NGO staffs and government institutions to improve FSL preparedness and response during emergencies 4 times during the running of the project.2- Building, restoring and maintaining community assets through food or cash for asset programs to crisis effected communities with food insecurity levels.- Conduct 12 awareness raising sessions in living together among farmers and pastorals and exchange commodities by creating common market 4 time a year in 2020.3- Provision of income generating support and vocational trainings- 1,000 HH of most fragile returnees 80% female receive small grant to complement their opportunity of life where 15% of them are people with disability and elders- Provide vocational training to 900 individual: 600 female and 260 men and 40 disabled.
