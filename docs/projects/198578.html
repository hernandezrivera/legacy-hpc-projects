Project Summary

This project will put strong focus on accountability, community participation, mobilization and engagement, a community-based approach strategy through protection lens, will be the bedrock of all activities and services in camps, camplike settings and host settlements.
It is expected that, the project will enhance displacement management in camps, areas of return, and out of camp sites to ensure equitable and dignified access to humanitarian services, assistance, and protection mainstreaming for IDPs through CCCM responses. IDPs needing site management services with inclusive and representative governance structures will receive appropriate support and to contribute to raise awareness among the affected population in the selected communities of Borno, Adamawa and Yobe to be more resilient and to exhibit positive coping mechanism towards the humanitarian crisis.
The project will also enhance displacement management in camps and out of camp settings to ensure a protective environment through an effective flow of information to facilitate coordination and quality of integrated service provision. It will also enhance resilience and improved ability of communities and local partners to cope with displacement and ensure local ownership and self-governance through inclusive participation, gender mainstreaming and engagement of displaced persons at all levels.
Salient Humanitarian organization will ensure that Service providers will deliver quality services in a safe, dignified and in a confidential manner to the affected beneficiaries as referred by us. GBV survivors will be identified and provided with appropriate recovery support. The project will strengthen co-existence between the returning persons and host communities by addressing the root causes of conflict, innovative solutions to improve security using alternative dispute resolution. Salient Humanitarian Organization will also look beyond the physical structures of safe spaces and in person help desks but also venture into establishing virtual safe spaces to handle issues of safeguarding, GBV and other forms of harm.
The project will be focused to reduce vulnerability and promote coordinated multi-sectoral approach to strengthen life-saving protection in selected sites in Borno, Adamawa and Yobe states through camp coordination and camp management, provision of NFI assistance and advocacy for shelter rehabilitation/ repairs in camp and camp-like settings and host communities.
SHO aims through this project to continue and expand its existing Shelter/CCCM/NFI sector services, to mitigate impact of the protracted crisis and identified encumbrances, reduce vulnerability and address the accumulated and projected needs for shelter solutions, and NFI needs for IDPs, including persons with special needs in the category of IDPs, returnees and most vulnerable members of host communities in Borno, Adamawa and Yobe states.

1 - Needs

Based on the 2023 HNO analysis, achievements of the CCCM actors in 2022 and looking at the financial considerations, the target population for CCCM interventions in 2023 is over 1,557,731 individuals at Borno, Adamawa and Yobe. There is a strong need to enhance resilience and improved ability of communities and local partners to cope with displacement and ensure local ownership and self-governance through inclusive participation, gender mainstreaming and engagement of displaced persons.On this premise, Salient Humanitarian Organization seeks to expand its Community based approach solutions to meet the needs of over 150,000 individuals within 10 proposed LGAs of the BAY states

2 - Activities

Activity 1: Registrations and information management; SHO’s dedicated site management team will be responsible for the registration of all direct beneficiaries under the scope of this project to build a database. These beneficiaries will include all persons found at the LGAs selected for operations. SHO will conduct regular demographic data collection, will conduct frequent partner and service mapping, and will continue the facilitation of consistent analysis of needs, gaps, and trends. The information doctored from the sites will further inform the sector about the trends of displacement, needs and response. This activity will be a direct and repeated service delivery.Activity 2; Persons supported by complaint and feedback mechanisms (CFM)SHO will establish CFM channels such as Helps desk, Complaint registers, Suggestions boxes and Hotlines. The CFM channels will be to collect, analyze, share, refer and take action on complaints and feedback from beneficiaries on humanitarian services deliveries. SHO will have designated CFM team that will be responsible for ensuring proper confidentiality is established, complaints are responded to and there is a feedback loop. This activity will be a direct and repeated service delivery.Activity 3; Community engagement and participation:SHO will support the formation of community leadership through Age, gender and disability diversity to ensure active participation in humanitarian services deliveries, support and advocate inclusion of all Community’s interest.SHO will support existing structures to be more independent, support the women committee, SHO will commit to the setting up of safety mapping committee as a functional part of the CMC (Camp Management committees).SHO have routine meetings with these committees to improve their understanding of their roles and responsibilities to their communities at large. This activity will be a direct and an in-kind service delivery.Activity 4 Care and maintenance of camp infrastructure;SHO will distribute site maintenance materials and tools (Wheelbarrows, Rakes, Hoes, Diggers and Shovels ) to promote better living conditions r maintenance of site infrastructures, communal buildings, drainage, fire stations within the IDP sites and to enhance durable solutions, repairs of utilities such as faucets/Tap-heads, Communal buildings, installation of fire stations across all sites of response, minor repairs for shelters etc.these tools and equipment will be given to the camp maintenance committee members who would be charged with the responsibility to assist in the facilitation of camp/site maintenance which will in turn promote participation and enhance accountability.SHO will ensure that these tools and equipment are given to the community maintenance committee members who would be charged with the responsibility to assist in the facilitation of site maintenance to achieve active participation and enhance accountability.Activity 5 Site management support;SHO will conduct daily monitoring of interventions, responses, and services provided by other humanitarian bodies at the BAY states. Through our site facilitators, Community Management Committee, and community members, we will able to identify gaps in all communities and make proper advocacy of identified gaps through the referral pathways on site that will be set up or improved.Based on access to location and community size, SHO will respond to the CCCM needs of the people through mobile approach or Dedicated approach depending on the access and security context.SHO will ensure routine coordination meeting where needs, responses and Gaps can be identified and share with all appropriate quarters. This activity will be a direct and repeated service delivery.Activity 6 Capacity building;SHO will conduct a routine capacity building sessions for Site facilitators, Community leaders, Camp Management Committees and Community members about the roles and responsibilities of CCCM.

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

Salient Humanitarian Organisation will at all times liaise and coordinate with partners on site to call to mind their mandate which requires the incorporation of all principles of protection so as to prioritize safety and dignity, avoid causing harm, enhancing meaningful access, for accountability to the project beneficiaries and enhancing participation with the intention to empower the project participants. The involvement of humanitarian workers in acts of sexual exploitation and abuse is a grave violation of our responsibility to do no harm and to protect people affected by crises. Sexual exploitation and abuse violates universally recognized international legal norms and standards. It brings harm to those whom humanitarian agencies are mandated to protect, and jeopardises the reputation of these agencies and their ability to provide protection, therefore, Salient Humanitarian Organisation will in all areas of operation activate kin attention to monitoring and reporting PSEA modalities that will help in identifying and handling PSEA cases accordingly. Although States have obligations to respect, protect and fulfill rights related to SGBV, a growing backlash caused by the activities of insurgents has weakened political, financial and psychological support for protection concerns of the displaced persons living in camps and host communities. These barriers relate to availability, believes, accessibility and cultural norms as well as the quality of the services provided. Entrenched social norms in Borno and gender inequality around women and girls, means women behaviours are controlled and they may be stigmatised for being violated. Due to their age, teenage girls’ ability to express herself over violation may not even be respected but rather, damned. All these make it difficult, and often prevent young people and other minority groups from accessing services having to do with SGBV and SEA. Salient Humanitarian Organisation will on a weekly basis create door to door awareness and carry out mass sensitizations to open the eyes of the Internally Displaced Persons to Sexual and Gender Based Violence among divergent topics affecting the PoCs, the Persons of Concern will also be taken through the intricacies of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse and how to report cases regarding these concerns. At the management level, SHO already has policies (PSEA Policy, Gender Policy and Child Protection Policy) that are assented to by all staff and by implication, the policies are binding on all and the organization does everything possible to educate her staff concerning these policies and how to mainstream them while in their places of primary assignment. Succinctly, to make easy the reporting of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse, Sexual and Gender Based Violence, SHO will provide telephone lines, help desks, complaint boxes and at certain occasions conduct interviews and Focused Group Discussions to gather information concerning SGBV and PSEA among the IDP population.

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

SHO has feedback mechanisms ranging from suggestion boxes, telephone lines and one on one contact with any Person of concerns.SHO has established a Complaint and response mechanisms currently in all areas of operation – a formalized process providing a safe confidential, accessible, transparent and effective channel for beneficiaries and other relevant stakeholders to raise concerns, register complaints, call for investigation and make suggestions. SHO will systematically investigate and follow up on complaints in a timely manner. The purpose of the CRM and relevant contact information (phone, email, physical address etc.) is communicated to beneficiaries through complaints boxes wherever relevant, on CCCM activities as well as on distributed materials and / or at distribution sites. Staff will explain the purpose to beneficiaries. Received complaints are categorized according to level of sensitivity and severity and responded to in accordance to SHO’s Standard Operating Procedures for Programme-related Complaints Response and Feedback Mechanism. SHO will inform beneficiaries and the broader community on the selection criteria as well as on the distribution process in order to avoid the creation of tension. SHO’s Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability and Learning (MEAL) mechanisms provide a two-way communication channel through which beneficiaries and affected populations can give feedback and complaints, which will enable SHO to adjust its intervention accordingly and address issues directly in a time-effective manner.
