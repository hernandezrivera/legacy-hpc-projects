Project Summary

(i) expanding humanitarian access to deliver life-saving assistance and protection in Diffa, Tillabery and Tahoua a complex operating environment where the security situation remains volatile; (ii) Better coordination of the response in other parts of the country during peaks of malnutrition, food insecurity, epidemics and flooding to update needs and highlight gaps; (iii) establishing an efficient humanitarian coordination mechanism in Diffa, Tillabery and Thoua (iv) optimizing the cooperation with the Government of Niger and development actors trough the operationalization of the humanitarian / development nexus, capitalizing on the willingness of government of Niger to take the lead on humanitarian issues while considering how to further support them in terms of capacity building; (v) ensuring adequate funding for humanitarian activities in all humanitarian sectors, including by working closely with donors towards more support and funding tools to national responders, and the scaling up of cash-based programming; (VI) conduct discussions for a possible revision of the coordination mechanisms after the 3 years strategy and support national NGOs to be better organized in view of their representation in the HCT .

Needs

Throughout the year 2019, it is expected that food insecurity and malnutrition will remain a major concern in Niger. Insecurity is likely to persist, especially in the western region of Niger, particularly alongside the border with Mali, as well as in the East, at the border with Nigeria and Lake Chad, where sporadic attacks of Boko Haram are expected to continue.Cyclical natural disasters would continue to affect the lives and livelihoods of high numbers of vulnerable communities in the country. The health situation may remain characterized by the prevalence of diseases with epidemic potential such cholera, measles, meningitis, hepatitis E.The number of refugees and returnees seeking refuge in Niger from Nigeria can stay in the same proportion. With ongoing military operations and attacks from armed groups at the border with Mali and Burkina Faso, as well as intercommunity conflicts in Tahoua and Tillaberi regions, it is expected that more Malian refugees and Nigerien IDPs will be hosted in Niger while the number of refugees from Nigeria will remains at its level of 2018. In addition, some migrants may continue to pass through Niger to reach European countries through Libya or Algeria; however, the outgoing flux will slightly lower compared to the incoming flux. Considering the above context, OCHA will have to respond to the following needs:• Need to work towards the setup of a more strategic coordination framework that affirms the leadership of the Government over the implementation of the humanitarian response in the framework of the New Way of Working. This will entitle the design of new humanitarian mechanisms that take into consideration the need to advance the humanitarian / development nexus at the national and regional level• Need to adapt the information products to the needs of various actors including the government and development and humanitarian actors• Need to ensure coherent communication on the humanitarian context, needs and challenges • Need to support informed decisions making process by the leadership, namely HC and HCT.• Need to reinforce mechanisms for the mainstreaming of accountability and resilience throughout the programme cycle and into cluster strategies• Need to reinforce coordination mechanisms to consider cross-cutting issues• Need to ensure that the Protection of civilians remains at the center of the humanitarian response• Need to ensure that PSEA is consider in all activities
