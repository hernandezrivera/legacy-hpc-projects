Project Summary

Cyclical climate shocks, including drought and flooding, which is challenging traditional rural livelihoods, as well as ongoing conflict, together contribute to growing internal displacement in Somalia, with an estimated 2.6 million women, men, girls and boys currently displaced. Between January and August 2020, conflict, flooding and lack of livelihoods contributed to the displacement of 893,000 people across the country. IDPs comprise 30% of Somalis in IPC 3 and 4 requiring lifesaving assistance in 2021.
Agricultural and livestock production in riverine, rain-fed, agro-pastoral and pastoral areas in Somalia have been affected by various shocks. Most recently, the Gu 2020 seasonal flooding in the South and desert locust in the Northwest negatively impacted crop production. Conflict and insecurity in Lower Shabelle and Middle Juba continue to disrupt productive activities.
Alta Juba relief and rehabilitation organizations aims to provide unconditional food assistance to flood displace people in lower-Shabelle region particularly Afgoye district n Somalia. The puprose of this project is to provide food assistance to flood affected households in Afgoye district in order to prevent malnutrition and starvation among the affected house holds in Afgoye district. Our target are the flood affected house households in Afgoye district.

Mitigation Measures

1.Risk that beneficiaries are exposed to violence during registration/distribution as a result of the distance to or location of humanitarian assistance and servicesMitigationA-Ensure the location of and access routes to distribution points and services are safe with enough space for orderly distribution.B- Ensure the mode and frequency of distribution minimises safety risk – be aware that beneficiaries may face theft, intimidation, sexual assault and extortion whilst taking their rations home.C-. Ensure the ration size is appropriate for the beneficiaries to be able to carry home with no additional physical or financial burden and without being dependent on others to carry it for them.D-. Avoid community tensions between displaced and host communities by providing the local/host community with access to camp services and assistance in situations where they are experiencing similar shortages of food, assets and inputs. 2 Risk of interference from external stakeholders in the impartial and needs-based selection of beneficiaries thus negatively affecting social cohesion and sparking intercommunity conflict over access to resources MitigationA- Identify the power dynamics in the area. Who has access to food, assets and inputs? Who is able to influence decisions on food, agriculture and livelihoods?B- Ensure that assistance and services are reaching the most vulnerable - Identify and prioritise the most vulnerable groups in the community and prevent discrimination or exclusion of marginalised groupsC-Ensure lack of documentation does not exclude individuals from accessing servicesD- Make sure beneficiaries know how to obtain food assistance and livelihood opportunities, where and when the distributions will take place, what they will get, how long it should last and how to use the items. 3. Risk that beneficiaries are not accessing/using complaint and feedback mechanisms MitigationA-Be transparent with the affected populations by providing them with accessible and timely information on selection criteria for targeted assistance, organisational procedures and processes that affect themB-Set up accessible, confidential and well understood feedback mechanisms for suggestions and complaints with a view to improve programming, understand community perceptions, promote beneficiary empowerment and assist in detecting misconduct. Eg. place feedback surveys in food baskets, feedback box at distribution site, phone linesC-FSL staff and relief committees should be representative of all groups within the community (e.g.gender, age, ethnicity, socioeconomic group, disability) and all staff and members should receive protection mainstreaming training.D-Rotate distribution teams so no one team constantly visits the same settlements.
