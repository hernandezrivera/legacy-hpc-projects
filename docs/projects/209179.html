Project Summary

This project aims to provide conflict-affected civilians in Sudan with the information they need to make decisions for their own safety, protection and rights.
It includes the setting up of a Signpost instance, a responsive information platform – including a website and social media channels – that provides urgent information for people in crisis and direct responses to questions and concerns from affected people. A range of digital content will also be produced and disseminated through Signpost, based on the key questions and concerns people have, and targeting potential protection risks and issues that emerge for civilians. The purpose is to reduce barriers to accessing services and to expand access to life-saving information, guided by the specific needs and questions that community members have.
The other project component is social media listening, rumor tracking and analysis, and providing that data and analysis to the humanitarian community. This supports broad humanitarian efforts for accountability and participation through the dissemination and greater understanding of community information needs gathered through a robust social media listening methodology. Internews will also gather, track and analyze rumors in order to identify any potentially harmful misinformation and to support the development of humanitarian strategies to counter such misinformation.

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

This project aims to mainstream protection and engage in safe programming across design and implementation. It will do so in the following ways:--SOPs will be developed to ensure that staff engaging (digitally) directly with people in the affected community are fully trained on psycho-social first aid and how to make safe and confidential protection referrals. This will be done through working closely with agencies offering specific protection services as well as relying on Internews experience, policies and sector-wide best practice.--Participation is the core of this project - social media listening collects feedback and data from social media platforms and is a form of listening to the affected population. In addition, the project will engage directly with individuals in the community through online two-way communication including through social media channels, websites and phone/SMS contact. This participation will guide the project design in an iterative fashion, ensuring that there is a constant internal feedback look using data and feedback collected from community members, including broken down by age, gender and disability status.--This project aims to contribute to the broader accountability of the affected population through providing analysis of community feedback to humanitarian agencies. This will support the humanitarian response to be more accountable to the specific needs of affected people.--Internews has developed tools and methods for safe programming specifically for programs focused on communication and information interventions and will be using these throughout the project. These include a risk assessment template and process that will be used for all relevant project components, to ensure any potential harm is minimized.

4.1.1 - How will the project use community feedback to improve and adapt activities? (open-ended/free text, required)4

Communicating with communities is the core activity of this project, both through social media listening and through the two-way communication components of the Signpost program. All the data that is collected through both of those processes will be analyzed through the lenses of age, gender and disability (where possible, in digital engagement there is a more significant margin of error in terms of detailed data disaggregation). All this data will be used to influence management of the project in an iterative fashion - trends in needs, questions, concerns and feedback by different groups in the community will be used to tailor content to their specific needs. Ongoing assessment to understand the information ecosystem will also be engaged in, in order to ensure that the project uses people's preferred platforms, channels, languages and forms to communicate crucial information. This analysis of the information ecosystem will also be shared with the broader humanitarian community.

4.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will support Inter-Agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including Sexual exploitation and abuse complaints?

One core activity of this project is to package all the community feedback data, including rumors and misinformation, and share in accessible ways with humanitarian agencies through the coordination system and bilaterally as needed. These bulletins and presentations are aimed at contributing to the broader accountability of the affected population through providing analysis of community feedback to humanitarian agencies. This will support the humanitarian response to be more accountable to the specific needs of affected people and add to existing complaints and feedback mechanisms. The Signpost program also gives an additional method of community outreach to humanitarian agencies, through being included in the service mapping that is available online and through contributing to the production of specific content that meets community information needs in their areas of expertise or programming.All project staff will be trained on referral systems for sexual exploitation and abuse complaints and will set up a direct pathway to appropriately and safely refer any complaints to the appropriate mechanisms. Community engagement in this project will be primarily through digital means, and any staff who are involved in this engagement will be trained, and given regular refreshers, on PSEA standards for prevention and response.
