Project Summary

According to the draft 2024 Humanitarian Needs Overview, statistics indicate increased exposure to ongoing hostilities, particularly in the north-west. Limited access to services and displacement, resulting in economic deterioration, widespread poverty and lack of livelihoods, destruction and loss of housing and property, shortage of natural resources, continued spread of the pandemic, exacerbation of protection needs and increased reliance on passive aid and cooperation mechanisms. Restrictions on movement affect communities due to a variety of issues.
Older people, people with disabilities and chronic diseases face a set of challenges unique to their individual circumstances, especially in areas affected by conflict or multiple displacement, as well as the earthquake that struck the region in February 2023. They face stigma and social isolation, and people face an increased risk of loss of civil documents, land and property, and earthquake events.
. The project aims to reduce the aforementioned protection risks for the displaced and the host community in northwestern Syria in the Dana area and the surrounding camps through the presence of a protection center that provides services to the community and is considered a starting point for awareness teams. The project will also provide the following points:
1. Awareness campaigns through awareness teams to educate the community about the dangers of GP
2- Implementing C.M. services that address the weaknesses of the elderly and PWDs. Who will be monitored by case workers, community protection committees and service delivery workers through referrals to the primary service provider, in accordance with interagency procedures.
3. Providing individual protection assistance (ECF- CVA) to cover protection gaps when the service is not available within communities
5. Implementing MHPSS activities within the Foundation’s centre for people suffering from psychological disorders and psychological distress as a result of the war
6- Legal attention to beneficiaries by verifying ownership of homes, lands, and civil documents to ensure the authenticity and credibility of the papers
7- Holding legal awareness sessions on CSD and HLP: The sessions will focus on CSD and HLP, the importance of civil documentation, the steps and processes for obtaining civil documents, recording life events, explaining the risks associated with not obtaining civil documents, in addition to providing information about administrative procedures and the risks associated with them and Increase legal awareness among local communities about HLP & CSD issues.
8- Providing social integration activities for PWDs in society through peer-to-peer sessions for PWDs on a regular and recurring basis every 15 days, which helps in exchanging coping mechanisms. Facilities for PWDs will also be rehabilitated to improve their access to basic services and educate their families and the community. A mechanism for integrating people with disabilities through various seminars at the Primary Health Care Corporation Centre, which will be presented by community workers.
The project will ensure accountability of affected populations and stakeholders through coordination, participation, transparent accountability, complaints mechanism, feedback mechanism and monitoring workers’ efficiency.
The project is designed to enhance GQ magazine to include women and girls as an integral part of the project
The monitoring plan will ensure that:
1. Monitor the quality of the implemented activities and compare the work plan using monitoring and evaluation tools and find out the extent of beneficiaries’ satisfaction
2. Track project indicators to measure the monthly progress of the project at the level of outputs and activities (surveys, records, photos...),
The project aims to reduce protection from PSEA abuse, by signing a code of conduct for protection from PSEA and establishing complaints mechanisms
