Project Summary

The conflict in Syria had a catastrophic effect on the country's economy. The country witnessed market collapse, inflation, devaluation, looting and destruction.
The lack of job opportunities and funds are among the main challenges facing most of the 6.2 million internally displaced people in Syria, as well as many host communities.
In a context more related to Homs governorate, the governorate has been significantly affected during the last period, and with poor security conditions and limited access to resources, livelihoods have been significantly affected and its productivity has decreased, food and the daily essential needs prices have increased significantly, which has led to a major problems In food insecurity, malnutrition and poverty.
In addition to the migration of men and young men or joining compulsory service, a large number of families headed by women have emerged, forcing them to work in harsh conditions and expose them to abuse in some cases.
In the context of the efforts that been made by Al Birr Association to improve the economic situation of the population, and help them obtain income-generating sources.
Al Birr Association works to provide vocational training and livelihoods grants. Where AL-Birr studies the needs of the region through in-depth interviews with key people in those areas such as the village head and the mayor as well as the information obtained from the association’s branches and its medical and relief points deployed in the area.
the Association proposes to support the residents of the countryside of Homs by livelihoods grants that suit the nature of the region and the population, as for the country sides a grants related to poultry raising to produce white meat, and eggs production, Agricultural grants to support the agricultural production by providing a small irrigation network, seeds and fertilizers needed for cultivation of 600 m2, Small factories for cheese and dairy production, Tools and supplies for some occupations such as blacksmithing, wood and aluminum carpentry, sanitary installations, shaving and decorating for men, sewing and embroidery, hair dressing and decorating For women, in addition to providing vocational training for the same professions.
Regarding to the city areas, it is proposed to provide grants of tools and supplies to the needed professions in the city and provide vocational training in some professions that creates a job opportunities for the trainees.

4 - Needs

The conflict in Syria has affected societies and families significantly, which has led to declination at the economic level, livelihoods, as well as an acute shortage of basic services. The dimensions of the crisis have expanded to be more complex and affected the ability of individuals and families to adapt to daily life conditions.All this was accompanied by the declination of the purchasing power of the Syrian currency. Bearing in mind that 70% of the population lives in areas that suffer from extreme poverty, and they are unable to obtain their basic food needs or even their Needs from non-food items.In a context more related to Homs Governorate, The area has been severely damaged during the last period, and with poor security conditions and limited access to resources, The livelihood sector has been severely affected.Most of the population has their own means of livelihood and it is either destructed or damaged and they were forced to sell it to obtain food or they left it to rust due to the Inability to use it during the crisis and difficulties of reactivating it. Because of the migration of men and youths or their enrollment in compulsory service. A large number of families headed by women have appeared, forcing them to work in harsh conditions, and subjecting them to abuse, which show us the need to enable the affected population by the means of livelihoods to reach a good level of financial security.

5 - Implementation of activities

Through the project:1- The youth and in-need people will be trained by specialized trainers on a number of professions that is needed in their areas according to the nature of each region and they will be able to integrate into the labor market and become productive individuals in their societies.2- we will support those who lost their productive assets by new ones and that will help them start again

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

One of the potential protection risks during the implementation stages of such projects is the occurrence of some cases of harassment or exploitation by employees or workers to the beneficiaries. Therefore, Al-Birr Association ensure that its entire staff signs a strict code of conduct document to ensure they act properly.2- The possibility of exploitation cases or even child labor may occur in granted small projects, as for that, the association ensure that the beneficiaries signs a code of coduct stipulating that no exploitation activities will be carried out using the provided grant.3- a potential protection risk, is that some children might try to enroll in one of the vocational training courses but for that Al-Birr sets a clear criterias for selecting beneficiaries that consider age, ability and desire to work.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

1-One of the potential risks during the implementation stage of the project is -the fluctuation of prices of the needed materials and equipment. Therefore, AL-Birr will sign frame agreement with the suppliers at the beginning of the project to guarantee that all the needed supplies and materials will be purchased with fix prices during the whole period of the project. 2- delay in delivering of the seasonal agricultural grants: the management staff of the project will study the critical path of the project and ensure that all services will be delivered in the right time and place.
