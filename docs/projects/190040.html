Project Summary

The FSL program is part of a multi-sector program, whose global objective is the reduction of morbidity and mortality for conflict-affected populations in Cabo Delgado through targeted multi-sectorial support across Nutrition, WASH and Food Security and Livelihood (FSL) and integrated emergency response. The program targets the entire province of Cabo Delgado, with a focus in Mueda, to assist 30,000 beneficiaries

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project aims to support longer-term improved and sustainable food security and nutrition outcomes through community asset creation, behavior change, and/or strengthening of capacity. ACF proposes the provision of food vouchers to IDPs and host community households in need of emergency support in Mueda. The reduction of malnutrition rates will be targeted through a hybrid approach based on farming support and complementary nutrition trainings to women on healthy dietary practices for vegetable preparation, child-feeding practices and marketing to increase household income, thus providing additional access to more nutritious foods. A community-based approach (with the involvement of activists and CHWs network) will constitute the backdrop of outreach efficiency and enable the identification of vulnerable groups that will benefit from livelihoods and agriculture activities. The project will assist 30,000 beneficiaries in Mueda district, including both IDPs and host community members.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

1. To support longer-term food security and nutrition outcomes through community asset creation, behavior change, and/or capacity-strengthening, ACF proposes the provision of food vouchers or in-kind food to IDPs and host community households in need of emergency support. ACF will carry out an assessment to gauge the severity and to identify the most vulnerable households to food insecurity. The assessment will also identify households with malnourished children and/or pregnant and lactating women. The identification process will take place through outreach activities, such as mobile clinics and community-based activities.2. The second objective will include the following activities:- ACF will carry out a number of community improvement projects with a focus on the construction of public spaces, including rehabilitation of roads, markets facilities, health posts etc. that will be implemented by community members through skilled and non-skilled labor;- Activities in the agriculture sector will focus on providing urgently-needed inputs that promote increased agricultural yields and strengthen post-harvest management so that food reserves are more plentiful and predictable. - Provision of access to Business start-up kits and/or cash grants for groups composed of IDPs plus members of the host community aimed at integrating traditional safety nets and promoting social cohesion.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

ACF will mainstream gender and protection into the humanitarian response in several ways to reduce the vulnerability of IDPs and host communities, as well as mitigate the risks they face. For instance, the voices of women and girls will be enhanced throughout to promote their safety and access to humanitarian services. They would actively participate in decision-making on subjects such as community-based activities, health services.The project will promote linha verde service and foster its use among the beneficiaries. Promotion activities will include the scope of the service, costs and practical examples. The project will consult with affected populations (not only community leaders) to identify specific needs for vulnerable groups. Updates on the project will be shared with the affected populations through the available feedback mechanism including community meetings. The project will be monitored by the MEAL department, which will also support it in a risk analysis in view of the situation close to the beneficiaries, in order to guarantee respect for accountability, the PSEA policy, the staff's code of conduct and the policy on child protection.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

ACF will work with the community engagement and accountability to affected population (CE/AAP) working group towards adopting standardized tools and jointly implementing strategies. Needs assessments will be conducted jointly with community leaders and key focal persons representing specific groups like women, adolescents and the elderly, and will apply methods like direct observation, key informant interviews and focus group discussions. Their views and recommendations will be included in the project design and implementation. At the local level, project and facility management committees will include community representatives. The provision of business start-up kits and cash grants to joint groups will contribute to integrating traditional safety nets and promoting social cohesion with financial and social empowerment. More women will be encouraged to take up roles. Complaints will be monitored at service delivery points on a regular basis using complaints boxes and interviews with beneficiaries coupled with a feedback mechanism.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The implementation of the inclusive approach will be guaranteed in a transversal way to the project. Inclusion, on the implementation of all project activities will include a follow-up on the "triptych of inclusion", thus taking into account the aspects of gender, age and disability.For the inclusion and advocacy activities, coordination within the Disability working group will be ensured with the Cabo Delgado protection sub-cluster, OCHA Pemba, Cabo Delgado and Maputo disability working groups. ACF will implement the WGs recommendations, and guarantee equal access to services for all.Key messages disseminated during training sessions with health/nutrition staff and filed visits will emphasize the importance of achieving equivalent health outcomes for both girls and boys, and PWDs. As part of their onboarding process, ACF staff will be trained and sensitized on the importance of narrowing the gender gap, the inclusion of PWDs and shifting attitudes towards gender-based violence, exploitation and harmful societal and cultural norms.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Specific sessions will be implemented during the sensitizations activities: importance of waste management and its impact on the environment and health, importance of water source and water conservation, importance of care and feeding practice in a sustainable way.Rehabilitation activities will promote the use of local material and sustainable structures.
