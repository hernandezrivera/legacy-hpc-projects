Project Summary

The aim of the intervention is to provide emergency shelter assistance (MINOR REPAIRS AND REHABILITATION) to 236 UNITS AND 8 BUILDINGS INHABITED BY 922 INDIVIDUALS FROM the affected populations (irrespective of nationality) in Burj Hamoud, Sin El Fil, AND BACHURA AREAS. Based on the needs assessment, in line with the sector recommendations, CISP will determine whether the HH falls within the low, moderate or severe categories of damage. It is estimated that for low and moderately damaged HHs, CISP plan to conduct the MINOR repairs.
FOR THE SEVERELY DAMAGED HHS, CISP PLANS TO CONDUCT REHABILITATION WORKS IN ORDER TO CORRECT THE STRUCTURAL ELEMENTS THAT HAVE BEEN DAMAGED (COLUMNS, BEAMS, BALCONY, ETC...)

1 - Needs

On the fourth of August the city of Beirut was hit by a devastating explosion which flattened most of the city’s port, damaging neighborhoods and buildings across the capital, leaving at least 140 dead, 5,000 injured and 300,000 displaced. The blast was caused by the accidental ignition of large amounts of ammonium nitrate stored in a warehouse inside the port. Streets were littered with rubble from damaged buildings and blown out windows and doors, it is estimated that half of the Beirut and Mount Lebanon homes are unable to reside within at the current time. The explosion impact area reached 6km from the blast epicenter with what can be categorized as ‘severe damage’, 10km with ‘moderate’ damage and up to 24km with ‘light’ damage. This event adds to an already critical situation faced by Lebanon in terms of a COVID-19 pandemic and a severe economic and financial crisis. Based on the shelter sector and Lebanese Red Cross (LRC) lead assessments, the damages can be classified as follows:a) Level 1 - light damages: the needs in this category include replacement of broken glass, broken doors, locks, collapse of false ceiling etc. b) Level 2 - moderate damages: similar to level 1 needs, but here the household is either not habitable and/or habitable with safety and security of the premises compromised. c) Level 3 - severe damages: the buildings are deemed structurally unsafe, thus making the property inhabitable. In almost all cases the property inhabitants have left the property and temporarily sought shelter elsewhere, and they may need financial support to cover rent.

2 - Activities

IN THIS BASHOURA AREA, CISP WILL UNDERTAKE MINOR REPAIRS FOR HHS DETERMINED AS LEVEL 1: (64 UNITS) AND LEVEL 2 (16 UNITS), INCLUDING, BUT NOT LIMITED TO, REESTABLISHMENT OF WINDOW FRAMES, GLASS INSTALLATION, DOOR FRAMES, DOOR INSTALLATION, INSTALLATION OF TEMPORARY PARTITIONS, FIXING OF FALSE CEILINGS, AND MINOR PLUMBING/ELECTRICAL WORKS AND CONNECTION TO ELECTRIC GRID. SIMILARLY IN THE AREAS OF BURJ HAMOUD AND SIN EL FIL, CISP WILL TARGET 156 LEVEL 1 DAMAGED UNITS WITH THE SAME KIND OF MINOR REPAIRS.FURTHERMORE, FOR THE 8 BUILDINGS DEEMED AS LEVEL 3 IN BASHOURA, CISP WILL CONDUCT THE NECESSARY REHABILITATION WORKS IN ORDER TO REPAIR THE STRUCTURAL DAMAGES (E.G. COLUMNS, BEAMS, BALCONIES ETC.) IN TOTAL CISP WILL COVER 236 UNITS AND 8 BUILDINGS (220 LEVEL 1; 16 LEVEL 2 AND 8 LEVEL 3) REACHING AN ESTIMATED 922 INDIVIDUALS.
