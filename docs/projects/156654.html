Project Summary

Through the project, BARAKA helps the educational environment in the areas of Grapes and the Bab in order to bridge the gap of need in those areas, which have suffered from a long struggle between the state organization and the opposition factions backed by Turkey
Baka will coordinate with the directorates of education in these areas to provide a part of the educational support through which the organization hopes to reach a good educational level through:
- Support five schools in the areas of Jarablus and Afrin fuel and heating supplies to alleviate the suffering of students in the winte
- Rehabilitation of water and sanitation facilities in 10 schools in Bab and Tripoli areas
- Support 3500 students in winter clothing to support the children in the mitigation of the cold winter cold for winter temperatures in those areas

Needs

With the beginning of the military actions inside Syria in 2012 and the increase of the displacement movements in the conflict, the social and economic conditions of the Syrian people deteriorated. All sectors were affected by the current conflict. All of the educational institutions were subjected to harassment and marginalization. The schools were closed for years and imposed appropriate approaches to the policy and strategy of the dominant force in these areas.In the Grapes and Afrin areas, all the approved curricula were abolished and the military policy of the state organizations in Graves and the People's Protection Units in AfrinAnd after the control of the opposition factions supported by the Turkish government on the cities and towns of Graplas and the door in addition to pride in the first military operation in late 2016 and Afrin in the second operation in the beginning of 2018 became relatively safe areas of these areas have seen the return of refugees from Turkey and displaced from the armed conflict In these areas to settle in their lands in addition to receiving displaced people from the eastern countryside of Aleppo, Raqqa, Deir al-Zour and many cities. Several camps were built to accommodate these numbers after the displacement of Homs, Ghouta and Deraa, which increased pressure on the education sector, which generated an increase in the needs of the sector.Education committees formed by the local councils in cooperation with the Turkish Education began to open some schools to restore the educational process but below the required level despite the intervention of some organizations in this aspect of the restoration of educational facilities and the distribution of student supplies and others, but does not constitute 15% of the concrete need.There are problems in the education sector is a phenomenon that can not be ignored and summarized in the need for teachers and students of the necessary educational requirements of education and luxury requirements. In addition to a shortage of repairs and water supplies and sanitation of bathrooms and water and others in schools. The study showed that 16% of the water and sanitation facilities in schools need simple repairs and 10% need complete repairs. There is also a lack of heating at the current time, especially as the winter approaches and the low temperature in these areas near the northern border near Turkey. Also with the entry of winter there is an urgent need to cover the needs of students for winter clothes that has become a burden on their people and To the influx of prices and the low income of the family in these areas.We see from the above that the state of the educational system and its establishment does not live up to the average level and is not acceptable for educational processes in neighboring countries and needs urgent interventions and cooperation from all sides to return to the promotion of these schools and educational system
