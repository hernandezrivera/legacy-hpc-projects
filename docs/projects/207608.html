Project Summary

This project aims to provide life-saving services GBV support to vulnerable women/girls in highly conflict affected divisions in the North West Region. The project will be focusing on preventing various forms of violence perpetuated on women and girls such as domestic violence, rape, sexual violence, physical violence, discriminatory practices, forceful and early marriages and other harmful practices and tradition that violate the rights and dignity of women and girls in society. The project will be targeting women of all ages especially from 18-59 years, girls below the ages of 18, albinos, women and girls with disabilities.
The project will also be targeting women (married) undergoing domestic violence in two divisions (Mezam and Menchum) highly affected by the ongoing armed conflict. Domestic violence is a major violence affecting women and girls in these divisions which needs greater attention. Also, some girls are being violated by parties to the conflict (State and non-state armed groups) with beatings for refusing to abide to certain conditions while in a relation. This is also caused mainly by the behavior/perception of the people of these divisions who strongly believe the woman should be subjected to the man before she can do anything. Therefore, this project will using behavioral change of target through intensive counseling of couple, families on how to live a happy life through the respect for one another’s rights in the home, workshops, seminars, trainings on ending GBV, GBV talks, drama displays and continuous sensitization and awareness raising through community radios, social media etc. Focus will also be on reducing the GBV risk on women and girls through engagement of survivors in income generation activities
This intervention is also coming to give women and girls the exposure, promote their rights and empower them to feel valued in society and see themselves equal to any other human being. Also, information regarding survivors will be treated with confidentiality by competent and trained GBV task force.
This project will :
• Carry out advocacy, campaigns, targeting traditional rulers, community leaders, women, men, and youth groups, religious leaders of different denominations, family heads, to sensitize them on the ills of GBV.
• It will also carry out case management, counseling, psychological and psychosocial support to survivors, referrals to other partners for better intervention.
• Community sensitizations of target population in all the four divisions will be carried by trained community mobilizers or community focal point persons who have a good mastery of the culture, language and other social aspects of these target communities and villages.
• This sensitization will entail communicative, educative, learning and awareness raising messages with the objective of creating self-guilty and self-consciousness in the minds of perpetuators, institutions of these acts of violence.
• Provision of small sale support for survivors to startup income generation activities so as to minimize exposure to being violated again.
• Training of community mobilizers on GBV globally and specificities within the four divisions emphasizing on the causes of GBV, psychological first aid to survivors, and referral of worst GBV cases to service providers for better intervention.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The project titled is crucial for several reasons:Humanitarian Crisis: The armed conflict in the North West Region has led to a humanitarian crisis, resulting in the displacement of thousands of people. This has created a vulnerable population at high risk of gender-based violence (GBV).Protection of Vulnerable Groups: The project is essential for protecting the rights and well-being of internally displaced persons (IDPs) and host communities, particularly women and children, who are disproportionately affected by GBV in conflict settings.Addressing Trauma: GBV can have long-lasting physical, emotional, and psychological effects on survivors. By minimizing the incidence of GBV, the project aims to address and mitigate the trauma experienced by individuals in these communities.Promoting Sustainable Development: By addressing GBV, the project contributes to creating a safer and more stable environment for the affected communities, which is essential for sustainable development and recovery from the impact of armed conflict.Human Rights and Dignity: Every individual has the right to live free from violence and fear. This project is essential for upholding the human rights and dignity of the affected populations, ensuring that they can live in safety and security.In summary, the project is needed to protect vulnerable populations, address trauma, promote sustainable development, and uphold human rights and dignity in the face of armed conflict and its associated risks of GBV.
