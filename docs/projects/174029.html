Project Summary

As per RNA 2, 9% of the schools are fully damaged and 41% schools are partially damaged in district Kachi. Similarly, all the schools in district Quetta were closed due to COVID 19 from March 2020 to September 2020. The schools were reopened for two months and closed again on November 26, 2020 due to ill-observance of the set SOPs.
The current project proposes a rapid need assessment (RNA) to reassess the total number of students affected from floods in district Kachi and COVID 19 in district Quetta as per requirement / measurement scales of the severity.
The project will establish schools/ temporary learning centers each for boys and girls in both the focus districts as per need of the district According to the assessment of the RNA. Similarly, repair of fully damaged and partially damaged schools will be done.
Communities and PTSMCs will be mobilized through awareness sessions, seminars, dialogue and inclusiveness in the implementation of the project activities. In all the activities, secondary education department will be taken onboard and the activities will be done by involving community members for ownership and sustainability.
In case of closures of the schools, temporary arrangements of learning centers will be done, and alternative ways would be sought out to ensure the continuity of learning and engagement of students in a child friendly environment. Teachers would be trained on the concept of child friendly schooling. Additionally, teachers would be trained on distance/e-learning/alternative learning modalities. Hygiene and safety kits will be provided to students of the selected affected schools
All the stakeholders will be trained on SOPs for safe reopening of the schools
Beneficiaries:
1- 8000 students (60% girls and 40% boys) from Quetta and Kachi districts will benefit from the project;
2- 200 teachers (60% female and 40% male) will benefit from in terms of capacity building programs;
3- Total 8 child friendly schools will be established in both districts to cover up for the fully damaged schools in flood affected areas;
4- Total 20 schools will be repaired / rehabilitated which are reported / confirmed to be partially damaged during floods;
5- Additionally, awareness sessions will be conducted areas including child friendly environment, Covid-19 prevention / SOP implementation, safe re-re-opening of schools, distance learning modalities, protection rights of children, women and girls, teacher trainings on developed TLM and some other capacity building and sanitization trainings will benefit approx 8,000 students, 200 teachers, 2400 Families / Households, 224 PTSMC members. Project programs are designed to account for PSEA, gender balance / protection issues and inclusiveness of disabled members of society.
6- 28 Schools to be provided with clean Drinking water facility;
7- 28 Schools to be provided with Foot paddle hand washing unit to promote safe hygiene practices and covid-19 spread prevention at school level;
