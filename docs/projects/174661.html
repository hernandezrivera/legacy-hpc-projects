Project Summary

COHESODEC within the framework of its emergency response efforts seeks to improve on
the deplorable conditions of I.D.P's and target host communities with regards to nutrition in the North-West Region of Cameroon in 2021.
COHESODEC's interventions in the nutrition sector as usual shall ensure gender equality, protection mainstreaming of all and ensure accountability to affected populations. Moreover, the project will ensure gender equality by making sure at least 50% of both sexes-(females and males) do benefit equally without any discrimination or bias and marginalization during the implementation of project activities. Furthermore, protection mainstreaming will be prioritized to ensure victims of GBV, abused children and people whose rights have been violated are prioritized and given center stage. In addition, accountability to affected populations shall be upheld in the implementation process of this project. This will be done by putting in place a call line where all beneficiaries can call in to table their complaints or appreciate the services being rendered as well as a locked and confidential suggestion boxes to permit beneficiaries drop in their feedback regarding the project.
The ongoing conflict which has badly affected a significant proportion of the population of the North-West Region for over four years and counting has categorically necessitated the relevance of this project in the target communities. The major objective of the project is to significantly improve on the lives of the affected populations as well as host communities especially involving Pregnant Women and vulnerable children whose vulnerability to the adverse effects of the crisis and negative impact on a significant proportion of the population cannot be over-emphasized.
Our major target in this response will be to reach out and support with regards to nutrition
care of at least 7,300 persons in need who have been badly affected in various areas and ways by the ongoing conflict through the implementation of a micro-nutrient supplementation program for malnourished children under five years of age. The essence of this activity is to significantly stamp out malnutrition from among this vulnerable age group and to prevent growth retardation among the targeted children.
COHESODEC's activities regarding the implementation of this project will ensure absolute support for community engagement to make affected populations change their behaviours towards the quality and good practices of balanced diets offered to young children as well as ensure micronutrient supplementation for adolescents and pregnant women.
COHESODEC interventions during the effective implementation of this project will be
centered in the following health districts and health areas;
>Bafut health districts;-(Mankwi, Mundum, Nchum, Manji, Njibujang, Njinteh, Mankanikong, Obang, Mambu, Agyati, Mforya, Akossia & Buwe-Bukari),
>Bali health district;-(Njenka, Wossing, Ntanfoang, Bosa, Bawock, Mantum, Sang & Naka'a),
>Bamenda-(Alamandum, Nkwen, Ntanka'a, Atuakom, Azire, Ntambag, Alabukam, Ntinkak),
>Santa-(Njong, Awing, Buchi, Menka, Mbei & Ndapang),
>Tubah-(Bambui, Bambili, Babanki Tungoh & Sabga),
>Ndop-(Bamunka, Baba I, Bamali, Bamunkumbit, Baligashu, Babessi, Bafanji, Bamessing, Bambalang & Balikumbat),
>Mbengwi-(Nyen-mbo, Guneku,Zang Tabi, Njah Etu, Njembeng, Nyen, Acha, Munam & Mbemi) and >Batibo-(Guzang, Ashong, Anong, Ambo, Wumugi, Bessi, Enyoh, Bifang, Diche, Wumudeck, Mbunjei & Baramban).
These health districts and health areas mentioned above, it should be noted are among the most
affected communities in terms of nutrition deficiency among the population following this ongoing conflict in the North-West and South-West Regions of Cameroon.

1 - Besoins / Needs

The assessed needs the project seeks to engage in to improve on the deplorable conditions of I.D.P's and target host communities with regards to nutrition are aimed at achieving project outcomes. COHESODEC's target in this nutrition response will ensure at least 7,300 persons in need are effectively reached through the implementation of a micro-nutrient supplementation program for malnourished children under five years of age. The expected result of this activity will be to significantly stamp out malnutrition from among 75% of this vulnerable age group of children as well as prevent growth retardation among the targeted malnourished children. The activities of this project will also ensure absolute support for community engagement to make affected populations change their behaviours towards the quality of feeding offered to young children as well as ensure micronutrient supplementation for adolescents and pregnant women. The expected outcome of this activity will be to ensure that at least 75% of young children, adolescents and pregnant women change their behaviours and feeding habits so as to prevent malnutrition in their respective communities. COHESODEC interventions during the effective implementation of this project will be centered in the following highly vulnerable health districts and health areas; >Bafut health districts;-(Mankwi, Mundum, Nchum, Manji, Njibujang, Njinteh, Mankanikong, Obang, Mambu, Agyati, Mforya, Akossia & Buwe-Bukari),>Bali health district;-(Njenka, Wossing, Ntanfoang, Bosa, Bawock, Mantum, Sang & Naka'a), >Bamenda health district;-(Alamandum, Nkwen, Ntanka'a, Atuakom, Azire, Ntambag, Alabukam, Ntinkak), >Santa health district; -(Njong, Awing, Buchi, Menka, Mbei & Ndapang), >Tubah health district; -(Bambui, Bambili, Babanki Tungoh & Sabga), >Ndop health district;-(Bamunka, Baba I, Bamali, Bamunkumbit, Baligashu, Babessi, Bafanji, Bamessing, Bambalang & Balikumbat),>Mbengwi health district;-(Nyen-mbo, Guneku,Zang Tabi, Njah Etu, Njembeng, Nyen, Acha, Munam & Mbemi) and >Batibo health district;-(Guzang, Ashong, Anong, Ambo, Wumugi, Bessi, Enyoh, Bifang, Diche, Wumudeck, Mbunjei & Baramban). These health districts and health areas mentioned above are among the worse affected communities leading to nutrition deficiency among these populations following the ongoing conflict in the North-West and South-West Regions of Cameroon. Moreover, the project will ensure gender equality by making sure at least 50% of both sexes-(females and males) do benefit equally without any discrimination or bias and marginalization during the implementation of project activities. Furthermore, protection mainstreaming will be prioritized to ensure victims of GBV, abused children and people whose rights have been violated are prioritized and given center stage. In addition, accountability to affected populations shall be upheld in the implementation process of this project. This will be done by putting in place a call line where all beneficiaries can call in to table their complaints or appreciate the services being rendered. Also, COHESODEC project teams on the field will often move around with a locked and confidential suggestion box to permit beneficiaries drop in their feedback regarding the project. This feedback mechanism to be put in place will ensure improvements in subsequent project activities.
