Project Summary

CRS is implementing the Somalia Health, Protection and Nutrition project in Benadir, Lower Shabelle, Bay, Gedo and Galguduud regions of Somalia. The project aims to serve 125,000 individuals. CRS works with three implementing partners: Save Somali Women and Children (SSWC), SOS Children’s Villages Somalia (SOS), and Trocaire Somalia. The project works to increase access to Health, Nutrition, Protection and WASH services to reach the following objectives:
1. Target populations access comprehensive primary care and have reduced morbidity.
2. Malnutrition levels in young children (<5) and pregnant/lactating women (PLWs) are decreased.
3. The risk of GBV against vulnerable groups (including children) is reduced and survivors of GBV access comprehensive services.
4. Vulnerable populations access clean water and have improved hygiene.

Mitigation Measures

The project responds to 1b by instituting mobile clinics that provide medical services in IDP camps so that people are not required to leave their homes for lengthy periods to access basic services. The project addresses 2b by providing services specifically targeting women and their children, such as prenatal care, child-friendly spaces, and mother-2-mother groups; each of these is a source of feedback from the respective constituencies. In order to mitigate the barriers for beneficiaries to report concerns (4d) the project has made toll-free hotlines available for those seeking to give positive or negative feedback. Posters have been printed in Somali and Mai languages in order to make beneficiaries aware of their right to give feedback, confidentially if they so choose. Implementin partners are also actively learning with each other to find the best approaches to sollicit feedback.
