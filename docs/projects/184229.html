Project Summary

The Project proposes protection services that comprises of Child protection case management services through reporting of violations affecting the newly displaced people affected by the ongoing military operations, airstrikes and floods in SWS, and supporting these IDPs with Child friendly spaces, psychosocial support (CP), referral responses for GBV cases affecting individuals to the services and facilities in the areas.
The project will also enhance the activities with community mobilization targeting the various community members, respecting gender equality inclusion, disabled children, adolescent children and other special needs as orphans. READO will provide Family tracing & reunification (CP) to the separated children during the displacement and providing Child friendly spaces service awaiting to reunify with the caregivers and parents.
The Project targets 2333HHs or 14000 individuals (4200 girls, 2940 boys, 4480 women and 2380 men in Bay- (Baidoa, Bardaale, Dinsoor) and Bakool-(Waajid, Xudur). The project in collaboration with Child Protection sub-cluster will be delivering CP Referral and Response services, Community based CP Awareness activities in the IDP camps and furthering child safeguarding activities by other programs and agencies in the locations targeted.
