Project Summary

Based on the findings from J-MSNA 2021 published by ISCG, this project will address the needs in WASH sector for both targeted blocks and host communities. The timeframe of this project is proposed one year and that will enable Tearfund and its partners to contribute on achieving the objectives set by ISCG WASH sector through addressing the needs of the targeted FDMN and Host population in Cox’s Bazar. Tearfund will engage local and national NGOs for implementing the project. As primary local partner COAST Foundation, CCDB & World Concern will implement this project funded by Tearfund and respective donors. Tearfund believes and promote in localization and capacity of local and national partners at the beginning of response. This project will support the implementation of localization agenda in Rohingya response. One of the Tearfund’s top focus on WASH will be implemented by three partners in several blocks under Camp 1E, 1W, 04, and Raja Palong, Ukhiya. As a result of its ongoing WASH interventions, one of the partners (CCDB) has been assigned as WASH Focal Organizations by UNHCR in Camp 1E, block- D and Camp 1W, block-G.
The project has been consulted with communities especially during designing phase through different need assessments. All interventions will follow ISCG-WASH sector and working groups’ guidelines. Beneficiary data has been disaggregated by age, gender and disability for informed decisions and decrease barriers to accessing services. To address most vulnerable people WASH needs the project will target women, children, elderly and persons with disabilities. The project also focusses on regular, sufficient and dignified access to WASH facilities for all age and gender groups through its planned interventions.
The project’s major activities includes; repair Tube well/water points, Installation of community water points in host community, repair latrines and bathing place, Latrine installation, Regular de-sludge Latrine pit, repair & maintenance of Faecal Sludge Management point, strengthen WASH management committee, Collect waste regularly from the targeted blocks, Drain repair, Maintenance of secondary waste dumping places, HHs and community waste bin distribution, Construction of waste dumping points, Family Hygiene Kit Distribution and Hygiene promotion session and Disinfection materials including safety materials for community people.
Tearfund and its partners are already active in coordination meeting at WASH sector in Coxsbazar and Camps. This project will keep coordination with WASH sector on regular basis while implementing. Meanwhile, 4Ws, Sitrep and other required reports will be shared for better coordination.
Tearfund will ensure the Accountability to the Affected Population (AAP) conducting community consultation meeting, involve them in project design and monitoring and influence on project activities. Tearfund and its partners will organize workshops at community level. This activity has been proposed to inform and engage affected populations and stakeholders in decision making process of the project. Besides, Post Distribution Monitoring (PDM) will be carried out by hiring enumerators after distributions. This activity will help the project to know the community perception, feedback, further recommendations and impact of the distribution in the community. At the initial stage of the project, a baseline survey will be conducted to know the status of community people and how the project will support the most effective ways. Later end line surveys will be conducted to compare the change made due to project intervention and what is the status of targeted outcomes.

1 - Identified Needs

The proposed project has been designed considering the needs suggested in JMSNA 2021 report, and several need assessment reports by partners. Activities, methodology and target populations take into account the most need endorsed by sector and WASH actors. As per recent J-MSNA access to safe & functional latrines' (23%) and access to improved drinking water (have been identified as one of the priority needs, which Tearfund's will also address under WASH activities. In addition, Tearfund and its partners has also conducted an assessment to identify the needs in August 2021 especially in Tearfund existing camps. According to the findings of that assessment, it has showed that 49% families have not enough hygiene materials (like soap, reusable cloths masks, etc.) and more than 49% reported that they don’t have easy and safe assess to clean toilets, 43% respondents also indicated that there is scarcity of water in camps, 37% respondents stated that dirty environment and 28% said that drainage system is not working due to maintenance properly in camps. Host communities also reported the lack of awareness and hygiene promotion.
