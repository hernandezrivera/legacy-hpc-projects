Project Summary

This project is an on-going project, under LHF-OCHA on GMS (LEB-21/DDA-3604/SA2/E/NGO/20726) funded from 14 January 2022 till 13 August 2022, we are applying for an extension for four months and a half, with the same activities.
By the proposed project Al-Fayhaa association focuses on implementing education to children at risk in North Lebanon and Akkar. Since late 2019, the prolonged school closures due to anti-government protests and the Covid-19 emerging situation, have deprived 1.3 million children from receiving normal education, while about 700,000 children have received no education at all. Thus, a remedial program will target 1200 children over two cycles (100% Lebanese and Migrants, 60 % in Akkar, 40 % in T5, 50 % boys, 50 % girls, 3 % disabilities) enrolled in public schools from grade 1 to grade 6 and who are at risk of dropping out due to their poor academic performance and achievement results. Children from Grade 1 to grade 6 will get interactive remote learning support for 4 months each cycle in all the subjects they need help with, especially the major subjects of the Lebanese curriculum (30 sessions of Arabic language, 30 sessions of foreign language, 40 sessions of mathematics, 40 sessions of science, and 20 PSS-SEL sessions).
The first cycle aims to provide homework support to school children. The second cycle aims to provide retention support to school children to support them after the learning loss, due to the gaps in learning after Covid19, to enable them in the upcoming school year.
The proposed education program will support 36 children with disabilities with technologies devices (tablets) among them 18 children with disabilities will be provided with special needs assistive devices such as hearing aids, wheel chairs, walkers and others to empower their access and retention in education. In addition, all the 36 children with disabilities will be provided with an average of 16 therapy sessions based on their needs. The program will also focus on providing 960 caregivers with individualized face to face home-schooling support and Psycho social support and social emotional learning , during cycle 1.
The 960 caregivers will be supported with 3 food packages, over 3 months, during cycle 2.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

Risk 1: Staff get infected by Covid-19 Risk1 mitigation plans/actions: Take precautions by always wearing a mask and keep social distancing, prepare urgent action plan Risk 2: SEA allegations Risk 2 mitigation plans/actions : All staff are required to Sign AL-Fayhaa code of conduct and CP -PSEA policies, drivers must be accompanied with at least a volunteer when transporting beneficiaries to any of the programs' activities, inform beneficiaries of Al-Fayhaa PSEA hotline.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

During project implementation awareness sessions for caregivers will be implemented throughout the execution of Retention homework support program. A supervisor will be responsible for any means of communication with parents of affected population. This will act as effective channels for mutual communication to enhance project sustainabilityBased on AFA policy a tool for feedback will be kept, where individuals can fill in feedback surveys & submit confidentiallyEducational specialist, inclusion officer & therapists will execute regular virtual classroom observation to monitor implementation of courses, provide feedback to facilitators on their used strategies & refer individuals as needed. Moreover, within design of the project we believe feedback from beneficiaries is key to sustainable outcomes & possessing greater impact.
