Project Summary

The ongoing “Protracted Armed Conflict” in the two Anglophone regions of NWSW Regions of Cameroon, has resulted in increased movements of Anglophones (Children, Women, Men, Older people & PWDs). IDPs/ host communities have the right to life with dignity, protection, security, and humanitarian assistance on the basis of need, without discrimination and avoid exposing them to further harm.
The project shall be implemented in 9 Sub Divisions in hard to reach communities with a total population of 769,976 inhabitants of 12 communities of Momo (Ngwo, Oshie, Andeck), Mezam (Agyati, Mankwi), Menchum (Weh, Wum central, Befang) & Bui Divisions (Mbiame, Sop, Ngomrin, Nkar) of the NWR of Cameroon.
Aim:
This project aims at Improving IDPs establishment of birth certificates by 30th June 2021.
The IDPs Children/families and host communities need an urgent humanitarian intervention for life-saving and alleviate suffering by supporting the affected populations to meet their basic needs by improving child protection access to the following;
-Number of community-based child protection mechanism functional
-Number of conflict-affected children receiving birth certificates
-Number of persons sensitized on birth registration
These above needs can be achieved by;
-Build the capacity of Community-Based Child Protection Mechanisms.
-Provide support to issue birth certificates to conflict-affected children
TARGETS:
-This project is targeting affected IDPs children/families & host communities of Gender, Age, Disability and Diversity mainstreaming, i.e. (60% IDPs, 10% PWDs & 30% Host Communities) towards birth registration.
FEASIBILITY:
-Materials for are available and goods for the needs of family reunification, foster parents, case management kits and hygiene kits are available in the local markets in the suburbs will be prepositioned in dry season because in rainy season roads are almost impassable
-Insecurity is one of the main constraints. Partners’ acceptance in the target area is crucial for smooth implementation of project.
