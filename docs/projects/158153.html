Project Name

Provision of psychosocial support; activation of protection mechanisms to prevent and respond to violence, exploitation, abuse and neglect against children; and support access to birth registration.

Start Date

01/04/2019

End Date

31/05/2020

Project Summary

1) Mobilize affected communities around the protection and wellbeing of all children and provide access to affected girls and boys of various ages, including children with disabilities, with psychosocial support, opportunities to play and acquire contextually relevant skills.
2) Provide child protection case management services to child survivors of violence, exploitation, abuse and neglect, including specialised support for children with disabilities.
3) Mobilize affected communities to identify and refer children affected by violence, exploitation, abuse and neglect to formal and community-based inter‐sectoral support services.
4) Strengthen capacities of service providers, including social welfare, police and justice sector actors, to re-establish prevention, referral and response services.
5) Identify entry points for birth registration and issuance of identification documents for children who have either never been registered or have lost birth registration documentation to facilitate access to essential services.
