Project Summary

The project purpose is to improve the wellbeing, resilience and a protective environment for girls, boys and women through providing integrated specialized child protection and GBV services, targeting IDPs and vulnerable host communities in South Kordofan, Blue Nile, East Darfur and South Darfur States, The project intervention will be through multiple specialized CP and GBV services which include case management and referrals, Psychosocial Support, provide alternative care for Unaccompanied and Separated Children, provide support for children with disabilities, community based approach through strengthen community based structures, provide life skill and parenting skills, provide support to GBV survivors and link them to livelihood services, community mobilization to prevent and respond to exploitation, neglect, abuse and sexual harasement.

1 - Needs

About 15.8 million people - 23% of population in Sudan will need humanitarian assistance in 2023, which increased by 1.5 million compared to 2022. from 15.8 million about 11 million need emergency assistance for life threatening needs related to critical physical and mental wellbeing which is increased 21% compared to the previous year. associated factors increasing the vulnerability in Sudan which raising the number of People in need, these factors are criterial political situation, Economic crisis, which is resulting in continuous inflation, also affecting delivery of public services, in addition to inter- communal conflict in areas of Blue Nile, Darfur and Kordofan, beside floods creating internal displacement of about 3.7 million people in Sudan, above 70% are conflict areas based on IOM verification. The main challenges facing CP and GBV programming in conflict areas are lack of qualified technical staff to provide high quality services, capacity of government line ministries, Funding challenge which affect provision of comprehensive services to meet needs of vulnerable girls, boys and women, furthermore insecurity in conflict areas caused difficulties to access CP and GBV PIN, in addition to of challenge lack of referral pathway services, food insecurity and lack of safe spaces. all these challenges increased vulnerability and protection threats to girls, boys and women. in this project World Vision targeting to save Lifes and improve the wellbeing, resilience as well provide a protective environment for boys, girls and women in IDPs and vulnerable communities in Blue Nile, South Kordofan, East Darfur and South Darfur states through providing high quality CP and GBV services by respond and prevent violence and mitigate risk through community-based approach which aim to establish and reinforce community-based structures and capacity of communities to prevent family separation and empower girls and women. The project will provide support to disabled girls, boys and women so that address their specific needs. also, it will support Unaccompanied and Separated Children through alternative care, moreover the project will focus on case management strengthen referral system through trained staff as well will build capacity of communities by provide life skill, parenting and vocational trainings for girls, boys and women. Empowering vulnerable girls and women the building community capacity and provide start capital for IGA to mitigate risk and vulnerability. to provide high quality CP and GBV services and strengthen coordination the project aims to build capacity of project staff and partner staff beside building capacity of government personnel, social workers and community animators.

provide comprehensive high-quality services for vulnerable boys, girls and women from IDP and vulnerable host community in four conflict affected states of Blue Nile, South Kordofan, East Darfur and South Darfur.the vulnerable boys, girls will provide with specialized child protection services aimed to achieved through out one; improved wellbeing, resilience and protective environment through provide sustained structured Psychosocial Support by specialized trained staff, as well life skills and literacy classes inside the safe spaces, Parents and caregivers of vulnerable girls and boys supported with family strengthen, and positive parenting programs, in addition build community capacity by establish, reactivate community based child protection networks, build capacity of CP staff from the organization, partner, line ministries and community social workers to provide high services.CP output 1 is focus on needs of vulnerable girls and boys addressed by providing integrated specialized services through identification and registration of Unaccompanied and separated children and support them with specialized services and placed them in alterative family care, provide case management for GBV survivors and other child protection violation survivors, integration of CP in other programs, community mobilization and raise awareness.Output 3; Is CP risk factors are addressed through assess and identify child protection root cause to mitigate risk and reduce vulnerability. GBV program is focusing the activities of:Conduct community awareness raising on GBV, Establish, support and run women centers, support women and girls with startup capitals for IGA, Establish and support Community based networks, conduct training for community members on GNCV prevention and response, conduct training on GBV for non GBV service providers, conduct training on GBV for specialized service providers, conduct community-based awareness training sessions on GBV, Conduct large scale public community awareness campaigns, provide specialized GBV services, provide dignity kits, conduct GBV assessments.
