Project Summary

This project aims to continue the delivery of CMAM services within the existing health system in Khartoum South Kordofan, East Darfur States, through the provision of full package of treatment and prevention of acute malnutrition among boys & girls U5 as well as providing Healthcare and treatment for SS/HC. The project targets acutely malnourished children (SAM with or without complication and MAM) through treatment. Improve the capacity of MOH staff and community volunteers to provide quality services to mitigate the risk of food insecurity and reduction of malnutrition. The project will enhance the need analysis of the nutrition situation through conducting regular community screening of cases, involving the communities in tracing SAM/MAM children and refer them to the H/N centers. Mother to mother groups where a group of mothers with malnourished children, grandparents group) to help empowering Mothers to solve issues related to the nutrition with focus on infant and young child feeding(IYCF) practices and knowledge and coordination with relevant sector (for multi-pectoral nutrition activities).Acute malnutrition and the underlying causes of malnutrition will be managed through participatory nutrition education activities using the Nutrition Impact & Positive Practice approach (NIPP) through targeting predominantly mothers and fathers of moderately malnourished children.Community screening of cases will equally target boys and girls & depending on their nutritional status they will be referred to the OTP or NIPP circles based on the admission criteria.
Mainstreaming child protection through reflecting in the assessment, design, monitoring and evaluation of the project. Girls and boys of all ages and their caregivers, especially pregnant and breastfeeding women and girls, have access to awareness about women and child rights for the prevention of malnutrition Gender, HIV/AIDS, important of education and the environment are mainstreamed throughout the project activities. The ultimate goal of the project “save the lives” of acute malnourished children U5 as well as provision of medical services to South Sudanese in targeted states. The following activates planned to contribute in achieving this goal: Screening for 16,000 under 5 children for SAM detection in H/N Centers, Admit, trace and treat 8000 SAM children in Khartoum state, Referral of 1600 SAM children to inpatient care who develop medical complications, Admit, and treat 2000 MAM children cases, Develop 80 IYCF mothers support groups from the communities of 800 mothers, support group providing basic health education in an interactive participatory manner during series of sessions.Provide 10,000 individuals (5000 in KRT,3000 in ED,2000 in SK) with awareness raising sessions including breast feeding, complementary feeding, immunization, hand washing, using of mosquito bed nets, distribution of oral dehydration salts, distribution of soap, importance of education,HIV/AIDs and GBV. Conduct mobile OTP, to reach beneficiaries at the inaccessible areas during rainy season. The nutritionist and community health promoters conduct home visits to the beneficiaries within the catchment areas to readmit the defaulted children in the program. Provide Training in CMAM for OTPs technical staff (6 staff per each OTP) Provide Training for the Community Health Volunteers in CMAM in the targeted locations. Provision to medical supplies and equipment (drugs, diagnostic kits, .etc)for the centers in, Provide Medical treatment to 37,000 SSR in the targeted states (KRT30,000, ED4000 and SK3000), out of the cases total of 7400 people with serious medical conditions that require assistance will be referred, in terms oftreatment at specialized health facilities/ hospitals, Provide Training in Primary Health Care for heath technical staff, Conduct number of Mobile clinics for treatment and referring especially those with handicapped to reach the extremely vulnerable individuals.

Activities

The project is targeting SS with 80% portion and 20% for HC with focusing on CMAM and supported by local authorities and communities in the geographical areas where the proposed intervention targets as well as providing health services to conflict affected people. At the community level, children will be screened for SAM by the Community Health Promoters (CHPs) and referred to CMAM outpatient care centers for further screening and for admission into the Outpatient Therapeutic Program (OTP) and checking for childhood illnesses. Health and nutrition education sessions at the centers level and community level will implemented towards increasing family knowledge and adoption of key nutritional practices, essential family practices, and encouraging health and treatment seeking behaviors in a timely manner to complement delivery of life-saving services, which will contribute to the prevention of future episodes of acute malnutrition.Nutrition Impact and Positive Practice (NIPP), and mother support groups of IYCF mechanism will be adopted aiming to provide the complete package for treatment and prevention of acute malnutrition. Two approach includes communication for behavioral change, towards child feeding, cooking demonstration using fuel-efficient stoves, and establishment of micro-gardens to encourage diet diversity towards providing high-quality care to rehabilitate MAM children (discharged from the OTP) to prevent their lapsing into SAM,During the rainy season, extensive mobile clinic to reach vulnerable people in their areas will be implemented, Providing the medical treatment and referral for cases to be treated either in center or to be referred the Hospitals for the targeted beneficiaries (boys, girls, women and men) even PNS and elderly.To ensure the sustainability of the services capacity building will be provided for MOH seconded staff, Community members and Health care medical staff.
