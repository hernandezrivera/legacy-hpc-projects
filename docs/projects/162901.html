Project Summary

An Inter-agency Assessment (IA) Mission took place on 17 – 27 June 2019 in Abu Kershola Locality, South Kordofan State. The team assessed 19 villages out of the 20 targeted villages and four settlements in Abu Kershola Town. Tommy Village was initially targeted but was later excluded after because it has no civilians. It was the first time for an IA assessment mission to access and assess the humanitarian needs in Teiri, Aldeinat, Alfait Um Abdalla and Khor Aldaleeb villages which are considered front line to areas outside government control. The assessment team had six UN agencies: OCHA, UNHCR, UNICEF, WFP, WHO, UNMAS; seven international NGOs, namely; Goal, CIS, SCI, WVI, IRW, MC-E and VSFG and four national NGOs namely, GAH, Pancare, Mubadiroon and FPDO. In addition to government representatives from HAC, Social welfare,Talodi locality has 13,968 IDPs (2,328 HHs) mainly living in five locations, four of which are inside Talodi Town, and one in Al Tugula Village, 16 kilometers east of Talodi Town. The IDPs were displaced from Mandi, Angarto, Al Gardod, Mafloua, Kindirma, Al Salamat and Taboli which experienced several attacks by both SPLM-N and the Sudanese Armed Forces (SAF). Most of those villages are no longer under government control. Some people from these villages initially fled to Talodi and Om Dual village which had been evacuated and later moved to Al Tugula. The displaced people did not form displacement camps but were absorbed into existing villages by relatives and other people in Talodi. The total population of Talodi is 48,862 people, including IDPs.
The assessment team visited six communities in Talodi, during the focus group discussions, the IDPs prioritized health, Water, re-establishment of livelihoods, Education and NFIs in that order as their key humanitarian needs.
water is freequently reported as a main humanitarian needs in tha area. Unprotected water sources are used to meet the water needs at the household level throughout the locality. Those interviewed by the IA team complained that the water was insufficient and of poor quality. 12 out of 14 mini water yards and 57 out of 119 hand pumps have broken down. Most of the open wells in Abu Kershola Town are contaminated because of proximity to latrines and the water sources and household latrines have a similar depth. Prior to this assessment, water from 23 sources in Abu Kershola Town was tested and found to be contaminated according to the local water and electric sector and locality health department. There is no regular water quality testing and some communities reject water chlorination at the source. Conflict over access to water sources was reported.Regarding access to water in Talodi , only 45% of total needs are covered by the available water. In summer the gap increases when the haffirs dry up. In Al Tugula IDPs area, the water is contaminated, has color and smells bad. This is attributed to a 2013incidence of a mine collapsed killing unknown number of miners whose bodies were never recovered. The ground water passes through the cave systems in this mining area. tha project aims to respond effecyively through; • Rehabilitation of all broken hand pumps, mini water yards and drilling of new water sources at Umbair village.
• Immediate disinfection of +-28 hand dug wells by chlorine to reduce spreading of E. coli.
• Construction of emergency household latrines and building new school latrines for schools which have none.
• Provision of drinking water source for schools in affected areas.
• Chlorination of drinking water at main water sources and distribution points.Provision of cleaning tools at health offices at admin units to conduct regular cleaning campaigns
• Provision and distribution of chlorine tabs (0.33 and 1.67) in Abu Karshola and Conduct quality test /Construction/operating of 9 drilled WY.
• Rehabilitate six broken hand pumps
• Conduct CLTS for latrines construction. in Talodi
