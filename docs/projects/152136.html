Project Summary

To reduce the moderate malnourished idps/new arrivals from being severe malnourished
Emergency response to the new arrivals.
To provide cooking fuel to new arrivals who are able to cook for themselves.
To act as first responder to the new arrivals.
To feed the new arrivals for 120 hours in the reception center.
To provide follow ups and referrals from therapeutic feeding program.
To reduce mortality and morbidity risk in children under 5 years.
Protection mainstreaming adherance during the program.
To compliment the effort of WFP food distribution program.
Beneficiaries: 35,000 beneficiaries across five (5) Local government Area.

Needs

As of 15th of April 2018, the estimated number of the influx in the border towns is around 15,000 individuals.The new arrivals cannot afford NFI, cooking utensils and firewood to feed themselves before they could settled with the new environment. It takes time ,while some are starved due to harsh conditions encountered on their way, which is why the contingency measure to receive the new arrivals especially In Kind Food assistance as in wet feeding need to be taken into serious consideration.Through this project, Salient HO aims to expand its wet feeding program and also emergency response program to better serve the IDPs at the time of arrival and intend to expand to other liberated locations. Since Salient has been the service provider of last resort in wet feeding in Ngala. Salient intend to cover all geographical locations. However, priority will be given to the five (5) LGA's across Borno State namely – Ngala, Banki, Rann, Pulka, Bama and Mongono. And to create a preposition of food assistance where there is influx, the pipeline in that corridor will be serve promptly. Accordingly, the program is design to provide meals that are cooked and are ready to eat upon arrivals after undergoing screening.

Activities

- Registration and issuing of beneficiary card - Provision of adequate Wet feeding for New Arrivals for Five days.- Incorporate Protection Mainstreaming- At the field level, protection monitors are charged with ensuring that effective systems are in place for preventing and responding to acts of sexual exploitation and abuse. - conducting of post distribution monitoring per feeding cycle- Set-up appropriate mechanisms through which affected populations can measure the adequacy of interventions, and address concerns and complaints. , to ensure accountability to the affected population- Engaging the community volunteers in cooking their food with the supervision of cook.- Providing mobility to the new arrivals from screening area to RC.- cooking demonstration on how to prepare CSB++- On site ratio,provide 2100 kcals of energy per person per day.- To provide cooking fuel to new arrivals who are able to cook for themselves.
