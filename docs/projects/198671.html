Project Summary

ActionAidNG plans to implement the project around the HDP Nexus to begin to unpack and operationalize this concept in Northeast Nigeria. The project activities will unpack some of the core ideas around the Nexus, support community, state and national structures in the efforts to popularize and operationalize the HDP nexus across Borno, Adamawa and Yobe State.

1 - Needs

The volume, cost and length of humanitarian assistance over the past 10 years has grown dramatically, mainly due to the protracted nature of crises and scarce development action in many contexts where vulnerability is the highest. For example, the crisis in the NE of Nigeria has become protracted requiring innovative ways of working such as the Humanitarian Development Nexus. There is very little knowledge of the HDP among actors who are not as keen about coordination as well. The operationalization of the HDP nexus has also progressed rather slowly in the NE as there is lack of clarity from most actors as to how to proceed. Communities who are benefitting from intervention largely do not understand or know how to engage productively with HDP actors that guarantee long term sustainable and peaceful development.
