Project Summary

The project aims to respond to and prevent violence and abuse against conflict-affected individuals with a focus on children and adolescents in Damboa, Dikwa, and Ngala in Borno state. Through this Project Mercy Corps seeks to improve the wellbeing and strengthen the resilience of conflict-affected children and adolescents in need of protection and other services across the three LGAs. The project will target to provide Essential and profession case management services for child and adolescents, age appropriate Mental and PSS for children adolescents and their caregivers. Mercy Corps improve protective environment for children through strengthened community child protection response mechanism , building community actors capacity and provision of age segregated child and adolescent friendly spaces. Mercy Corps intents to respond to urgent protection needs of children in Damboa, Dikwa and Ngala through
- Provision of essential case management services in Damboa Dikwa and Ngala for 6000 vulnerable and at-risk children and adolescents. Mercy Corps will focus on ensuring children with urgent protection needs are identified and receive timely, age and gender- appropriate services.
- Ensure there is a safe and an environment for children affected by the crisis in Damboa, Dikwa and Ngala through Community-based Mental Health and Psychosocial Support (MHPSS). Mercy Corps will establish Child and adolescent friendly spaces allowing these groups access to psychosocial counselling, support networks, life-saving information, and referrals to other complementary services. Mercy Corps will also provide PSS support to parents and caregivers through parenting courses to help caregivers develop skills to cope with crises.
- Strengthening community based protection mechanisms for children and caregivers. In collaboration with peer agencies, civil society organizations, community leaders, and local governments, Mercy Corps will organize training for and build the capacity of partners to support existing structures within communities that keep children safe. MC will conduct awareness-raising activities on prevention of violence against children and on child rights for community members.

1 - Needs

An estimated 5.2 million people are projected to be in need of protection assistance in 2019. New, ongoing conflict-related displacement, including the influx of IDPs, refugees and returnees, into overcrowded and under-serviced camps and settlements in some LGAs, mainly in Borno State (Pulka, Damboa, Gwoza, Monguno, Bama, Dikwa, Kukawa, Ngala). The protracted conflict, recurrent floods, and a resulting upsurge in internal displacement have led to a highly complex crisis of protection in Northern Nigeria. Protection violations and concerns are both a root cause of the crisis and a grave consequence, resulting in a myriad of acute and evolving protection concerns affecting communities, households, and individuals. Of particular concern is the situation of conflict affected populations including those in hard to reach areas and displaced and marginalized populations who face an elevated risk of protection violations, notably pervasive gender based violence (GBV), child rights violations including child recruitment and early marriage, and forced evictions. Returning refugees and mixed migration dynamics expose individuals to various and distinct protection concerns.Adolescent girls and boys continue to face specific risks. Girls are at risk of GBV, including domestic violence, forced/early marriage, and exploitation and trafficking. These risks are exacerbated by various factors including cultural practices, insecurity within the camps, limited opportunities for self-development and self-reliance for families, and inadequate access to education. Adolescent boys face heightened risk of forced labour and exploitation, as well as social conservatism and isolation. They also remain vulnerable to substance use issues and harmful behaviors against adolescent girls in a context of inactivity and lack of opportunities. Due to the emergency, adolescent girls and boys face changing roles within the household, with girls having to take on more caring responsibilities, and boys taking on work. With no access to formal education, idleness, potential awareness of parental distress combined with uncertainly of the future, it is vital to engage youth in meaningful and empowering. 2 - Activities

1. Addressing the needs and vulnerabilities of women and girls - Mercy Corps will implement an integrated programme that focus on empowerment of women and girls through multisector interventions. This will focus on providing life-saving, coordinated, multi-sectoral services and systems including life skills, literacy, and livelihood activities for vulnerable women and girls, with integrated psychosocial support. - As a result, women and girls begin to access and take control over their economic opportunities, giving space for more autonomy and control over their lives and decision-making which is critical on addressing protection risks for women. Access to multisector services gives women and girls the resources, opportunities and support needed to recover from the violence and risk to violence they are exposed to – 2.
