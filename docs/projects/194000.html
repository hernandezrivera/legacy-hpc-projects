Project Summary

The project primarily focuses on supporting the hosting community, IDPs and vulnerable individuals in North Kordofan State through enhanced access to basic services including safe and adequate water supply, promotion of sanitation and hygiene situation for resident communities. The project activities and results in terms of, efficiency, effectiveness and relevance to the essential needs of the community will be addressed by the project staff.
The project justifications, planning, formulation, activities, implementation process and outputs will be relevant to the needs of the communities in the project areas. The technology option for construction rehabilitation and upgrading of hand pumps compatible and well matched with the technical guidelines and specifications developed at the national level by WASH sector. The option of improved traditional latrines is technically feasible but relatively expensive and the majority of the community cannot afford such option.

1 - Needs

Thirty two percent (32%) of water brought for home use is raw stuff: untreated and unfiltered and prone to contamination. It comes directly from unclean sources; 16% from hafeer/turda with unfiltered stagnant water; 9% from shallow hand-dug wells. About 65% of water brought for home use is sourced from hand pumps (35%); from deep boreholes18%; and, 10% from water filtering stations, also not immune to contamination by multiple users, sharing the source with animals. The fact that 22% of water brought for home use is carried by male-and-female children under-fifteen years of age who could mis-handle the water, thus allowing for risk of water contamination. These are observations uttered in different occasions during group discussions with village residents. A similar amount of water, 22% brought for home use, is carried by adult males, whereas adult women bring home 39% of water for home use. The average amount of time spent on walking to the water source is 35 minutes. This is probable walking distance of 2.5 Km., carrying water containers: 5km to-and-from source Women and especially young boys and girls frequently take on important roles in pitching water for their families due to which their expose to numerous protection risks, exploitation, enslavement, rape and other forms of abuse and Sexual and Gender Based Violence (SGBV). The availability of the water at door step will help in reducing the protection issues for girls, boys and woman groups in the targeted areas.
