Project Summary

This education response project is targeting protracted conflict and displacement affected school age children from most vulnerable IDPs, Returnees and Host communities who have been displaced due to a protracted crisis over the past one year or more years, compounded by inter-clan, inter-tribal violence, cattle raiding and flood disaster in 2019. The project will reach up to 3,700 targets of which 3,288 (1,973M, 1,315F) will be primary school and ECD pupils and 412 teachers and SMCs/PTAs. The project will address three key areas of education in emergency with the following objectives: Increase access to safe, protective and inclusive learning environment to crises affected boys and girls (3-17yrs) in IDP center, Returnees area and Host Communities, To Improve the quality of Education through better education services for school aged children and Improve the response capacity of communities and education actors to mitigate impact of emergencies on school aged children in in Twic East, Duk and Uror counties.
The project will ensure durable solution in education sector through building capacities of customary institutions, SMCs and PTAs, local administration, women and youth groups, . The project will ensure the sustainability of its outcome through working together with customary institution, line departments, SMC/PTAs, women groups, youth groups, and local administration from beginning of the project. At the end proper handover of the project will happen. This project will contribute to objective 1, 2and 3 of Education cluster of South Sudan

1 - Needs

At country level in South Sudan due to humanitarian crises, protracted conflict, tribal clashes, poverty, and deteriorated livelihoods, there has been interruption of education and poor protection or complete absence of protection for school age children (3-17 years age). The children affected by this type of interruption reaches 3 million. Jonglei State is the most affected state with aforementioned problems related to education. According to Counties RRC offices, in Jonglei state in Duk , Uror and Twic East counties are hosting about 32,000 IDPs and about 11,000 Returnees related to conflict displacement and returning from refugees and IDPs. The school age children of the IDPs and Returnees in dare need of education, however the existing challenges on teaching learning material, access to education due to lack of teachers and learning space, protection concerns in some areas has been disbursing. In Uror the education sector is almost dying. In addition to that from June 2019 to October 2019 there has been incidence of flooding in the three counties. There was multiagency assessment of flood disaster in Twic East in June 2019 that come up flood affect people 8722, however recent record is in October shows that the people affected raised to 29,259 and multiagency assessment in this October 2019 in Duk confirmed that 1870 HHs (12,342 individuals) were affected by flood. The flood case damage/collapse on Tukuls (grass hatched houses) , damage household item, pit latrines collapse ,Schools pit latrine and toilets collapse ( for example in Twic East alone six toilets collapsed in six schools), in Twic East 24 schools flooded , in Duk one ECD center closed because of flood, there are some temporary learning spaces collapsed, school dropout and absence increased in all counties. Furthermore the damage of flood extended to agriculture crop, water point and livestock, these have direct impact on school age children in terms of going and attending schools. Of the 26 primary schools operational in Duk County prior to the 2013 conflict, only 18 schools reopened by 2016-18 with 8 schools remain deserted of which 6 schools were destroyed during the conflict. While only two Early Childhood Development centers are operational in 2019, among which one is closed temporarily due to flood. In Uror County, of 21 primary schools operational prior to 2013 conflict, only 13 schools are reopened by 2017/2018/2019. There are no Childhood Development Centers functional in 2019. In 2018 and 2019 the Education activity in Uror did not showed any change. In Twic East County of 39 primary schools operational prior to 2013 conflict, 37 has reopened by 2016-18, however 4 Primary schools from Lith payam are operating in Kongor payams as displaced schools. In Twic East County as of 2019, there are 8 ECD centres which are operational.

2 - Activities

Objective 1: Increase access to safe, protective and inclusive learning environment to crises affected boys and girls (3-17yrs) in IDP center, Returnees area and Host Communities in Twic East, Duk and Uror, counties • Establish four temporary ECD centers in the three counties (connected to primary schools and Church)• Rehabilitate three temporary learning space of 4 classes each in three primary schools• Establish gender segregated 4 Pit latrines with Washing facilities in four ECD centers • Establish five gender disaggregated pit latrines in flood affected schools • Train pupil on sanitation and hygiene• Support 4 ECD Centers with ECD kits that includes teaching and learning educational materials (textbooks, exercise books, pens, pencils, chalks, blackboards etc.)• Provision Dignity kits for 1200 school girls (Africa’s)• Establish 3 women advocacy groups to create awareness and advocate for the girl children to stay in school• Provision of teaching learning materials (Scholastic materials) for 12 schools Objective 2: To Improve the quality of Education through better education services for school aged children in Twic East, Duk and Uror counties • Support 4 ECD center pupil psychomotor growth by provision of recreational material• Training of 92 teachers (12 ECD teachers and 80 primary school teachers) on pedagogy, and life skill, and handling of children with psychological support• Identification and support of 12 incentive ECD teachers with salaries• Support 8 support staff (cleaners and security guards) in five ECD center with salaries Objective 3:Improve the response capacity of communities and education actors to mitigate impact of emergencies on school aged children in Twic East, Duk and Uror counties• Train SMCs/PTAs from 16 Primary Schools and 4 ECDS on School Management and Governance• Train SMCs/PTAs from 16 Schools and 4 ECDS child safe guarding and psychosocial support, GBV, Sanitation and hygiene, and environment • Train 12 ECD teachers, 80 primary teachers, 8 support security guards on code of conduct• Establish child help desks in all the targeted primary schools to report and refer cases of SGBV and other forms of violence and abuse of children.
