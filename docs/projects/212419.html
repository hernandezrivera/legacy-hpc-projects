Project Summary

Many Syrian governorates, especially the countryside of Daraa Governorate, the Lajat region, the Damascus countryside, the Jaramana region, and the Aleppo Governorate, Jabal Samaan region, suffered from many harsh events that they witnessed as a result of the war, which led to Displacement and destruction, in addition to the earthquake that occurred in the Aleppo Governorate, which had negative and harsh effects on all levels. Some of them also suffered from hosting affected communities during the war, as school buildings belonging to government institutions were affected, and many of them went out of service, which led to the emergence of a large segment of children who dropped out of education. Those at risk of dropping out, and those with poor educational attainment, especially the age groups from 6 to 17 years, including those with special needs. The educational institution is also unable to improve the educational situation, due to the severe shortage of educational staff, the major logistical shortage in school buildings, and the lack of an environmental health education.
During the presence of the EPDC teams in the mentioned areas and the implementation of some projects, they monitored and evaluated the significant damage to the education sector through coordination with key persons and focused sessions for the members of the targeted community. Therefore, it wants to intervene in those areas by supporting dropouts - non-formal education and working to return the dropouts to their schools and continue Educating them through implementing educational activities that include carrying out return-to-learning campaigns, repairing and rehabilitating classrooms, providing schools with furniture, such as desks, chairs, blackboards, and other educational supplies, providing children with school supplies such as stationery, school bags, and appropriate nutrition classes, and providing teachers with educational materials and resources. Children will also be provided with non-formal education programs and lessons in reading, writing and numeracy. Motivating teachers with cash rewards in order to provide the education service in an optimal way, in addition to providing a specialized and non-specialized psychological support program for beneficiary children and involving them in the case management program according to need and standards and benefiting from its services. In addition to training courses. It aims to gain sufficient experience to optimally provide the project services and objectives. Governance and accountability mechanisms will also be supported and established at the school level to support the educational process. All potential risks, such as discrimination and inequality in society, especially gender-based violence, will be studied. Sessions will be held for stakeholders and caregivers to promote the principle of gender equality. To provide services to all the target segment without discriminating against anyone because of their differences. As for service provision sites and ease of access by beneficiaries, there is a trained field team capable of reaching groups that cannot reach service provision centers. The risks that may occur and may be beyond the control of the work team will also be taken into consideration. It will be dealt with through effective coordination with key people and sector coordinators, and the presence of a safe alternative work plan for the team, such as weather conditions and infectious diseases. It will be taken into account that all services are accessible to the project beneficiaries. We will conduct a systematic procedure for the employees working in the project through courses aimed at protecting the beneficiaries, having them sign the protection and safety procedures for the beneficiaries, and activating the reporting policy when there is any type of violation of the policy of sexual Harassment, assault and exploitation of the beneficiaries throughout the life cycle of the project.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The response strategy comes from the presence of sufficient experience through the implementation of similar projects in the field of supporting non-formal education in different regions in the past years, and its positive impact measurement, in addition to the urgent need of the targeted communities, given that the targeted community in the Daraa countryside is from the remote countryside and The residents of this region are characterized by poverty, and most of their income depends on agriculture and livestock raising, in addition to being affected by the crisis. As for the Damascus countryside community, the Jaramana region, it is considered one of the communities hosting the crises, which witnessed a movement of displacement from neighboring communities, and thus the phenomenon of educational dropout of children appeared there.
