Project Summary

Covid-19 pandemic, coupled by Gu 2020 seasonal flooding and sustained conflict in Middle and Lower Shabelle and Bay Regions has impacted to increased numbers displaced populations in IDP camps where they live in informal and unplanned settlements under very deplorable living conditions. Women and Young Girls' condition are worsened by increased exposure to violence, exploitation and discrimination and other forms of abuse worsened by very serious risks and threats to their safety and security. Of particular focus are persons with disabilities face increased risks of exploitation, abuse and discrimination while accessing assistance meant for them hence strong need for their inclusion in the project right from the beginning of the project throughout the implementing period including monitoring, till completion. Its against this background that the project intends to provide timely and appropriate protection services to increase protection of boys and girls and vulnerable displaced and marginalized women as well as those with disabilities. The project targets total of 9190 women including GBV survivors those those with disabilities.

Project Activities

1. Building capacity of 90 case workers on survivor centered approaches (SCA) and psychological first aid (PFA) as well as referral protocols and through training. 2. Provision of protection services (provision of alternative settlement, protection from destruction of property and physical body harm) to 4500 persons (women and girls) including those with disabilities during eviction. 3. Provision of Psycho-social counseling as well as medical support for 1500 children and 4800 GBV survivors 4. Provision of material support and access to essential services for 4500 other protection victims including those with disabilities and child survivors at the project locations in Middle Shabelle, Lower Shabelle and Bay Regions.5. Providing training and Material support to 3500 Women to GBV survivors in Middle Shabelle, Lower Shabelle and Bay Region, Somalia

Mitigation Measures

1. Risk that beneficiaries are exposed to violence during registration/distribution as a result of the distance to or location of humanitarian assistance and service: Mitigation Measure : Enhancing security at the project sites such as guards as well as increasing distribution points for assistance to limit distance covered and associated risks by women and girls2. Risk of women, girls and boys exposed to sexual violence, including sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA), when accessing humanitarian assistance. Mitigation Measure: Ensuring beneficiary feedback and complains mechanism in place in addition to all staff signing PSEA code of conduct3. Risk of no access to or awareness of protection assistance or referral services for survivors of protection incidents (i.e. GBV, child protection, evictions, explosive hazards, etc.)Mitigation measure: Conducting awareness on protection assistance for survivors of protection incidences4. Risk that marginalized groups, persons with disabilities, women, boys and girls, do not participate in consultations or decision-making processes for the project or are excluded from humanitarian services/assistance/activitiesMitigation measure: Conducting comprehensive awareness for persons with disabilities, women, boys and girls on importance of their inclusion and participation in decision making processes and the entire project to ensure their active involvement right from the project inception throughout during project implementation including monitoring exercises till completion.5. Risk that beneficiaries are not accessing/using complaint and feedback mechanismsMitigation measure: Establishing beneficiaries complain and feedback mechanisms and conducting awareness targeting the beneficiaries for utilization of the same
