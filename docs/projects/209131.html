Project Summary

On April 15th, 2023 a war started between Sudan Armed Forces (SAF) and Rapid Support Force (RSF) in Sudan particularly in Khartoum and Nyala South Darfur. As a result of that armed conflict which last for more than 7 months in Nyala, people of Nyala town and neborghring localities are heavily affected by huge number of IDPs. According to primary rapid assessments of NNGOS Initiative, IOM-DTM and others local authorities' information a number 1,765,340 people fled from Nyala Shimal to different localities: Bilail, Elsalam, Edel Fursan, Niteiga, Kass and Nyala Janoub. Others headed to outside the State
This project will target IDPs and vulnerable host communities who directly affected by the ongoing conflict. a total of 7,860 Households are targeted by the project in the 4 localities: Nyala south: HH 3,500, Nyala North: HH 1,500, Bileil protracted IDPs who are affected by flood and the current war: HH 2,220 and Edel Fursan IDPs who fled from Nyala and the tribal conflict between Salamat and Bani Halba in Kubum: HH 2,100
The selected locations are the most vulnerable and mostly affected by the current armed conflict in Sudan with no actual humanitarian assistance. The project will support these beneficiaries to protect themselves and save their life.

3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

The project commits to address and mitigate protection risk in the targeted areas through focus and empowering on most vulnerable affected people through taking lead and planning in the project on, risk facing women, children and disables, be given priority during the project cycle.
