1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

In this project, the goal will be to offer 200,000 sexual and reproductive health services to the population victimized by natural disasters and armed conflicts, with a focus on the population of vulnerable women and girls from areas of difficult access and the population with disabilities disaggregated by district, sex and by age

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The priority will be to maintain the tents for the provision of sexual and reproductive health services in the resettlement and reception camps, through the allocation of a maternal and child health nurse in the tents.Working in communities with CHW to promote and prevent diseaseCreating demand in communities through sensitizing communities on SSR issuesIdentification and referral of cases of GBV, premature marriages, and safe abortion for referral and follow-up

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

AMODEFA has its own established safeguard policies to ensure that both employees and beneficiaries are protected and provision is made.the action of services in a minimum environment of security and confidentialityIn all fields where services are provided, the existence of conditions of security and confidentiality will be ensured with the minimum number of tents,The selected districts will be assured that there is no threat of armed attacks.in case of identification of the beginning of armed attacks or risk of natural disaster, AMODEFA will guarantee the evacuation of the personnel

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

AMODEFA has already been implementing projects in the province of Cabo Delgado for four years.Before this project start AMODEFA identified the specific necessity of different groups of age and seGirls, young in vulnerable situation have priority to be attended in all services offered by mobile brigades The CHW focuses on raising awareness, informing and educating these populations.The data collection sheets have disaggregations that allow feedback on specific activities in this population

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

All program activities carried out by AMODEFA must be inclusive.For this project AMODEFA aims to ensure awareness, information in the community about the rights of people with disabilities to SRH services, CHWs should generate demand not only from the general population but specifically from vulnerable and marginalized populations.The activities in the community will be continuous, prioritizing care in static health facilities and mobile clinics for people with disabilities.for people with disabilities with barriers to accessing the services offered by this project, they will be served through mobile clinics nearby their communities

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The experience of previous projects in the resettlement camps, the provision of health services involves different partners, AMODEFA identified a very big challenge in the treatment of hospital waste generated in the tents, including the disposal of cutting waste to the environment with all risks.For this project, AMODEFA intends to budget a value for the proper treatment of the waste generated not only by AMODEFA but also by other service provider partners.on the other hand, AMODEFA intends to disseminate the findings of the supervisions to other partners to sensitize them on the issue of treatment of hospital waste generated in the tents of resettlement centers
