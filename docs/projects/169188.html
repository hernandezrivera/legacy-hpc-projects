Project Summary

Medair has already been involved in the Shelter response through distributing 2144 emergency shelter kits to affected households. The project will re-establish secure shelter conditions for households affected by the Beirut port explosion through conducting minor repairs and rehabilitations of houses/apartments; providing safety and security for the families and protection from the elements. The project will also rehabilitate common areas and take special consideration of the accessibility needs of People with Specific Needs (PwSN). Medair aims to partner with local initiatives and NGO's to help build capcity through this project (in line with the objectives of the Shelter cluster).

1 - Needs

The LRC assessment found that amongst respondents 26% of households had major damage, 27% having medium (not closable, repairable), 29% having minor damage (closable and repairable) and 18% having no damage. In terms of the type of damage, 26% had damage to the balcony, 19% had damage to a ceiling, 17% had damaged columns,11% had damaged beams, 49% had damaged window frames whilst 88% reported broken windows. Windows, doors and ceilings are therefore the key needs (LRC WASH DANA, 9th August 2020). Shelter conditions are therefore not adequate, lacking safety, security, privacy and dignity. Affordability and availability of construction materials are the two main barriers for households to conduct repairs; identified by both ACTED and Save the Children’s rapid needs assessments (August 2020). Many Lebanese will not be able to afford the cost (particularly as materials may need to be imported) with pre-existing vulnerability due to the economic situation. 89% of LRC surveyed households have no savings, with 38% having no source of income in the last 30 days.

2 - Activities

Activities under Strategic Objective 1 include minor repair of residential and non-residential buildings using either contractor or cash modality. Medair's shelter team will undertake technical assessments in houses/apartments within its zones of operation and will assess the needs for minor repairs or rehabilitations. For Strategic Objective 2 activities include rehabilitation of residential buildings and communal areas. Medair will also ensure that these rehabilitations take special considerations of PwSN accessibility requirements. Under objective 3 of the shelter logframe activities include currently exploring options for implementing community support projects, discussing potential options with partners, and capacity building of national institutions and organizations on shelter response. Following guidelines and recommendations of the technical working groups.
