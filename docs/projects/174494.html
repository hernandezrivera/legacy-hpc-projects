Project Summary

Pak Women will select COVID 19 affected areas in consultation with health departments Mardan and Tor Ghar. Pak Women will map out human rights defenders and community leaders both male and female. Ten groups will be formed with male and female units. Members will include youth both boys and girls. 10 FGDs will be organized in 10 identified areas, the purpose of the FGDs will be to identify the forms of GBV, hurdles in reporting of GBV cases and hurdles in provision of GBV services. 20 awareness sessions will be organized for community men and women to provide them information on GBV received from FGDs and sensitized them on the issue. 10 sessions will be for male community members and 10 for female. After awareness sessions units members and participants of the awareness sessions will start identifying and reporting of GBV cases to the project as well as to the police. The project will address immediate GBV issues and empower women to take a stand against violence and rebuild their lives. The community-based approach for protection against GBV will strongly convey the message that a life free from violence is a fundamental human right. Women leaders of the community groups will not only facilitate identification and reporting of GBV cases but also advocate in their communities against domestic violence, sexual harassment, child marriage and cultural forms of GBV. The project’s community outreach will ensure gender equality, social and disability inclusion. 10 counseling sessions will be organized with the help of expert female counselors. Pak Women will provide total 1200 dignity kits to GBV survivors, 1000 women and girls and 200 women/girls with disabilities. Dignity kits will help women and girls by: Providing information about hygiene, reproductive health, GBV related issues, and services though the inclusion of health and protection information; will help Improving mobility of women and girls by providing specific sanitary items. For dignity kits Pak Women will print leaflets in local language Pashto. Pak Women will map out institutions and organizations in project districts, whose are providing support and response services to GBV survivors. Pak Women will form districts level a referral partners networks (RPNs), RPNs will comprise of community support services, Police, Lawyers, National Database and Registration Authority (NADRA), CSOs, Psychiatrists, women organizations etc. and will organize one day orientation and Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) signing event. RPNs will meet on monthly basis and Pak Women team will connect RPN with 10 community groups formed by Pak Women. Community groups will start referring of GBV cases for different type of services directly, services could include reporting of case to the police, complain to Jirga system, health treatment, advance psychological counseling, registration of NIC, free legal aid, assistance for micro enterprises development, micro credit etc. Pak Women will facilitate the whole process, linkages development between community groups and referral partner at very start of the project is very much needed for sustainability of the referral mechanism. Pak Women will also identify very deserving 50 GBV survivors by following a pre defined criteria and will provide them economic kits to set up their small enterprises, Pak Women will provide them micro enterprises development orientation. Those 20 women will also link with Business Development Services (BDS) i.e. training, micro credit, grant, material provision, markets etc. Pak Women will ensure participation of women/ girls with disabilities, transgender and minority women participation in the project. 30 women/ girls with disabilities will provide care packages and dignity kits. 30 men and boys with disabilities will involve as male allies and they will also sensitized on GBV. Pak Women will monitor and track the project progress to ensure successful implementation and completion of the project.

1- Project Activities

OUTPUT 1. Gender-responsive community outreach groups formed and orientated for GBV identification and referral, consisting of women human rights defenders, teachers and community leaders. Activities: 1.1 Formation of community 10 groups of human rights defenders and community leaders. Every group will have two units, one male and one female, each unit will have 5 members, so total 50 women and 50 men leaders will became members of the groups. Groups will meet weekly, will identify GBV cases from their respective communities and will refer to referral partners. Formation and orientation of community groups will complete in first month of the project.1.2 20 FGDs will organized, 10 with male and 10 with female community members. Purpose of the FGDs will be to identify GBV types, hurdles to GBV cases identification, reporting and referrals. On the basis of findings of the FGDs, Pak Women will design awareness sessions and will also design and print leaflets for awareness sessions, dignity kits and referral partners.1.3 20 Awareness-raising and sensitization sessions will be organized, Units of community groups will be responsible for arrangement of the sessions, identification and invitation of community members and making follow up with them after sessions. All participants will provide referral forms and they will sensitize to refer GBV cases to the community groups, community groups will further refer cases to referral partners. 1.4 20 GBV counseling sessions will be organized. Pak Women will hire services of female Psychologists and they will conduct GBV counseling sessions jointly with groups of five women and also with individual woman if needed. All GBV survivors’ history will be taking on a specified format approved from UN Women. Pak Women team will be responsible for follow up with all GBV survivors, who undergo through counseling and will prepare monthly reports of the progress.1.5 Pak Women will provide 1200 dignity kits to women and girls GBV survivors. Dignity kits will comprise of items i.e. information about hygiene, reproductive health, GBV related issues, and services though the inclusion of health and protection information, sanitary items. The items list will be approved from UN Women. OUTPUT 2. GBV survivors facilitated through referral to shelter homes or other GBV redressal services. Activities: 2.1 For improving accessibility and services for responding to GBV survivors during the emergency, Pak Women will map out referral partners, will form a referral partner networks (RPNs) and will provide orientation to all partners. 2.2 Motivational sessions at door-step with GBV survivors, to encourage them to seek GBV support.2.3 Pak Women will link RPNs will community groups to encourage women to seek support for GBV. Community groups will refer GBV cases to RPNs and will make regular follow up. Community groups will provide weekly reports on status of referred GBV cases. OUTPUT 3. Women GBV survivors facilitated to become financially independent through livelihood support. Activities: 3.1 Pak Women will identify 50 most deserving GBV survivors by following a pre defined and approved criteria. 50 Economic Kits will be provided to GBV survivors to set up small businesses. 3.2 Support women businesses by provision of information on services available for grants and loans to set up micro businesses. Linkages will be developed with business support services and micro enterprises development and strengthening orientation will be provided during monthly visits. OUTPUT 4. PWDs accessed with GBV messaging and provided with care packages and dignity kits. Activities: 4.1 200 women/ girls with disabilities survivors of GBV will be identified and will ensure their participation in FGDs, awareness sessions and GBV counseling sessions. 4.2 60 care packages and dignity kits will be provided to women/girls with disabilities.
