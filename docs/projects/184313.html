Project Summary

According to the Somalia Humanitarian Need Overview for 2021, up to 66% of the population in need of protection services in Somalia are children who continue to be exposed to protection threats of recruitment, family separation, abuse, neglect, abduction, exploitation, and violence. 2.3 million children, including 10% of children with disabilities, need immediate child protection services. Compared to the 2021 analysis, the estimated number of people in need increased by 25 per cent in 2022.
The ongoing clashes and heightened tensions between Ahlu Sunna Waljama’a (ASWJ) and Galmudug forces backed by Somali national forces have triggered massive displacement in Guriceel, Galgaduud region. A recent interagency joint assessment conducted in October 2021 in Dhusamareeb and Guriceel Districts shows that 100,440 people have been displaced in Guriceel and have arrived in 28 rural villages where access to basic services is limited. Among those displaced are 1,005 unaccompanied minors and 2,009 people with disabilities and health related complications. Protection concerns reported include fear of SGBV, child recruitment, child recruitment (boys) in armed forces, forced marriage and presence of unaccompanied minors (UACs) in areas of displacement.
This child protection and GBV project aims to close the gap between the vulnerable children and women and available services in Cabudwaq, Cadaado and Dhusamareeb districts in Galgaduud region and Galkaacyo and Hobyo districts in Mudug region. 150 caregivers will receive psycho-social support and positive parenting messages to improve relationships with children under their care. Individual case management will be provided to at least 60 children at risk including those with disabilities. Also, 100 identified separated children will be reunified with their parents and the unaccompanied children placed in safe alternative family-based care. This will be arranged through support of the cluster and other CP and GBV actors through information-sharing and capacity building workshops. 150 health care providers, CP actors, including local authorities, will be trained on child safeguarding measures and PSEA. A further 70 CMCPMs will be trained on identification and referral of children at risk in their camps to available CP services. Awareness raising on child protection issues will be conducted by the CISP outreach team targeting the 13,500 people in host communities and the IDPs settlements in the area. GBV activities include provision of dignity kits, menstrual hygiene kits and solar lamps to 1,200 vulnerable women and adolescent girls to promote their well-being and safety in their communities. Harmonized messaging on GBV, including FGM will be developed and shared with approximately 6,500 members of the target communities.
The project will promote gender mainstreaming and inclusion of minority clans, older persons, and people with disabilities during implementation. Activities will be conducted in locations that are accessible to the most vulnerable populations (women, girls, persons with minority clan affiliations, PLWDs and older persons); mobile case management to reach remote areas that hinder vulnerable groups to access basic services; and involving families of PLWDs during project awareness activities (including those with mental health issues). Gender equality and equity will be considered in selection and involvement of project beneficiaries. All data will be disaggregated by age, gender and disability and shared with CP and GBV AoRs to improve programming.
To monitor and evaluate the activities, CISP will develop quarterly, and monthly work plans to ensure planned activities are implemented, all data is collected using cluster approved tools (3W, 4W and 5W matrices) and updated in the CPIMS+V2 and GBVIMS for improved coordination. CISP will closely coordinate and work with other partners to provide services to the affected population and share information with CP and GBV AORs regularly.

1. - Projects activities

Child ProtectionActivity: 1.1: Conduct awareness sessions targeting 150 caregivers on psycho-social support, positive parenting, forms of child protection and GBV, and available services for reporting, or seeking assistance to improve their relationships with the children under their care, including those with disabilities. Activity 1.2: Identify, document, trace and reunite at least 100 unaccompanied and separated minors with appropriate caregivers. CISP will further strengthen mechanisms for registration and receiving information and for the active tracing of immediate family members and relatives through building capacities of child protection committees. Case management procedures for follow up of children who have been reunified with their caretakers will be strengthened and IDTR coordination improved.Activity 1.3: Provision of individualized case management that meets the unique needs of at least 60 identified boys and girls at risk, including adolescents and children with disabilities.Activity 2.1: Capacity building of 150 child protection actors (social workers, community volunteers, government officials, field coordinators) and non-child protection actors on PSEA and child safeguarding measures.Activity 2.2: Capacity strengthening of 70 CMCPMs on how to identify children in need of child protection services and referring cases of children at risk in their camps and communities to available CP services.Activity 2.3: Community engagement awareness raising activities on key child protection risks, social norms, harmful practices, and related information on CP services and mitigation measures. This activity will aim to reach at least 13,500 community members in the project intervention areas.Gender-based ViolenceActivity 3.1: Provision of dignity kits, menstrual hygiene kits and solar lanterns for 1,200 vulnerable women and adolescent girls to promote their well-being and safety in their communities.Activity 3.2: Community awareness campaigns for 6,500 women, girls, boys and men (including those with disabilities and from minority clans) on key GBV and FGM messaging, available GBV services and prevention and mitigation of GBV and FGM.
