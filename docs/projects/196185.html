Project Summary

In response to the WASH infrastructure requirement in North-West Syria (NWS) of significant support and substantial investments and the Most of the population in NWS are relying on alternative to piped water and often unsafe water supply modalities to meet or complement their water needs, Space of Peace is aiming to enhance the long-term WASH infrastructure and ensuring sustainability of water stations in Jisr Al Shogour community. To this aim the planned intervention will respond to the needs of 49800 individuals (8300 men, 11700 women, 10900 boys, and 18100 girls and 800 IDPs)- through the following strategies: Rehabilitate the water system in Jisr- Al Shogour community ensuring the functionality of the water network and there were Two Water stations SoP will Ensure the two water stations are connected to the public power grid for sustainability. Secure low-cost power source by installing solar power systems in the two stations ( Southwest station and North station).
A cost recovery system will be established in the community by supporting the O&M cost for 6 months in two stations ( Southwest station and North station) in Jisr Al Shogour and for three water stations in Ariha Community. to ensure a smooth exit strategy. afterward, Space of Peace will conduct end-line survey for the water station using the tool by WASH cluster.
While Space of Peace will be responsible to provide and install the Solar power system in Southwest station and North station).
Because of the spread of the cholera outbreak in northwestern Syria and the lack of water and its pollution, the Space of Peace organization emphasizes providing awareness and hygiene sessions to Population and providing the necessary materials to avoid infection with cholera and infectious diseases that are transmitted through polluted water. Therefore, a team of hygiene promoters will conduct these sessions, which will include All people in the community include the vulnerable, women, teenage girls, and PWDs.
The proposed intervention will include the establishment of WASH user’s committees at the community level. Space of Peace will encourage women and adolescent girls to be part of those committees. The purpose of establishing the WASH user’s committees is representing the vulnerable and marginalized groups in decision making at the community level and empowering them to raise their voices. In the other hand the WASH user’s committees will be the core for Risk Communication and Community Engagement (RCCE) modality that will be used in control the spread of Cholera, COVID-19 at the community level and hygiene promotion as well. ة
Additionally, Space of Peace will implement a safety audit questionnaire on a quarterly basis to inform recommendations across intervention for improving the security of women and girls in the targeted community and measure general improvement conditions over time. 3 - Needs

The lack of water and WASH services in northwestern Syria has a great impact on communities and camps, in addition to the outbreak of cholera caused by pollution water. Through assessment of the needs in the places we reach, the needs were identified in the Jisr al-Shughour community in the Idlib governorate. It was found that there is 49,800 Population from IDPs and hosts need access to water services and access to clean water. The project will work to respond to these critical needs by rehabilitating the water network in the Jisr Al-Shughour community to ensure the functioning of the water network. Ensure that the two water stations are connected to the public electricity network in order to ensure sustainability. Securing low-cost energy sources by installing solar energy systems, a cost recovery system will be established in the community, and water, sanitation, and hygiene committees will be established and distribution of Hygiene kits. Space Of Peace organization has an Implementation Project in 2022 to rehabilitate the water network and drinking water stations in Ariha in Idlib Governorate and provided the Ariha Al- Jadida and Al-Omran stations with a solar energy system to reduce the cost of water, which benefits 50,476 Population from the IDPs and Hosts, and it needs to support operational costs, and these operational costs have been included in this project.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

The intervention have identified several protection risks (in-line with WASH clusterprotection risk analysis framework) and set a strategy to mitigate them: -Unequaldistribution of services inadvertently empower or strengthen the position of armedgroups or other actors. Mitigation:Establish strong links with authorities and operatorsto promote equity and “do no harm principle” all the time; Organise joint planningculture and agree on minimum standard; Include community and authorities intargeting; Ensure sensitive information is handled with care. -Risk of theft of WASHrehabilitation/construction materials such as generators and construction materials(during delivery/storage/ after installation) by armed groups and used in conflictactivities. MoUs to be agreed with local authorities/councils to ensure local ownershipof materials and rehabilitated/new facilities. Mitigation:Promote cost recoverysystems for longer term maintenance and ownership to infrastructure to promotecommunity ownership. -Health risk associated with construction works and exposureto chemicals. Mitigation: Ensure staff/partners/contractors involved in constructionworks or working with chemicals wear appropriate PPE (Personal ProtectiveEquipment) and follow established safety regulations; Conduct/repeat safety trainingfor staff/partners/contractors.

9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumption 1: Flexible in-site implementation, when certain areas are inaccessible.Risks: Application of humanitarian principles where the risk of aid diversion caused by armed groups exists although thus far not reported officially, SoP will report for any possible aid diversion incidents.Mitigation measures: Ensure proper coordination with the local councils and detailed written agreements are made with local authorities prior to the implementation. Assumption 2: All materials needed for the rehabilitation are available and in sufficient quantity.Risks: As most items and materials for water system rehabilitation are imported from Turkey, any closure of the border crossing points with Turkey, will result in a lack of such items and materials.Mitigation measures: SoP will ensure to launch of procurement directly after the signature of the award for this project. In addition, contractors’ in-house stock capacity will be considered a key criterion for selection during the bidding process to ensure bulk deliveries.Assumption 3 (Cholera ): Water quality within the acceptable range.Risks: Water quality fluctuationMitigation measures: FRC at consumption point should be > 1 mg/l after a contact time of 30min to be monitored on a daily basis. Since the chlorination will be done at the water station level, The chlorine concentration will be adjusted to ensure FRC in the range of 0.2-0.5 mg/l, and above 0.5 mg/l at the user level in the cholera outbreak.
