Project Summary

This project is seeking to respond to the critical health needs of crisis affected population in Rubkona, Manyo and Baliet counties of South Sudan.
The project will respond and address the critical health needs of 202,415 people in Rubkona, Manyo and Baliet counties identified as intersectoral prioritiy1 and 2 locations and aligning to the 2025 health cluster severity levels 3 and 4. Intervention and services to contribute to achieving the cluster objectives and support the 2025 HNRP’s (Humanitarian Needs Response Plan) strategic objective of ensuring Vulnerable crises-affected people have reduced morbidity and mortality through equitable, safe and dignified access to life-saving assistance to meet their needs.
The plan focuses on delivering life-saving health services and kits according to humanitarian standards.
GOAL seeks to support the needs at the different levels of health services delivery through support to PHCCs, and PHCUs, integrated medical mobile outreaches as well as complement BHI activities in the three counties.
GOAL approach will include integrated facility WASH activities in the health facilities with the aim of improving the IPC standards to WHO Minimum standards.
Under the proposed project, GOAL will address acute health humanitarian needs of crises-affected vulnerable populations in the 3 counties. Rubkona, Manyo and Baliet counties are faced with complex
health emergency compounded by high levels of food insecurity and malnutrition (IPC 4-5), high risk of or current disease outbreaks(measles, hepatitis E and Cholera), poor child and maternal health coverage, lack of functional WASH in facilities and community.
Key activities proposed include delivery of integrated health services, strengthening of surveillance and reporting, IPC improvement, capacity building of CHD and facility staff on relevant topics, support for last mile delivery of medical supplies from core pipeline and integrated facility WASH in close collaboration with National and State MoH and CHDs and other relevant local authorities.
These activities will be complemented with coordination with relevant clusters -health, nutrition, WASH, protection and GBV sub clusters, support to documentation and use of HMIS as well as data analyses and sharing of lessons. Additionally the project will ensure that services are inclusive and reach diverse populations and capacities in a culturally sensitive, accessible, and responsive manner.
GOAL's strategy of Resilient health will guide implementation to ensure the response and outcomes are sustainable and promoting durable solutions in line with the "From Crisis to Resilience" GOAL Global strategy.

1. Needs

South Sudan continues to face severe health crisis due to multiple factors. About 8.9 million people in the country need one or more forms of humanitarian assistance, especially in the counties most affected by floods, conflicts, IDPs (Internally Displaced Persons), returnees and disease outbreaks. The health system in South Sudan is unable to cope with the emergencies and provide life-saving services. aces many challenges, such as lack of funding, staff, medicines, and equipment. The people suffer from hunger, violence, and poor health. Women, children, the elderly, and people with disabilities are the most vulnerable groups. They have limited access to health care and face high risks of death and illness.Through this effort GOAL is seeking to reach 106,971 crisis affected individuals with critical health services in the counties of Rubkona, Manyo and Baliet. This includes people who are displaced within the counties, refugees from neighboring countries, and recent returnees from Sudan. They need interventions to prevent and respond to disease outbreaks and other public health emergencies. They also need support from other sectors, such as nutrition, water, sanitation, hygiene, and food security. They also need community health services and engagement to bridge the gaps in accessing health care.The current health status of the population is poor with Malaria being the leading cause of death and illness, affecting half of the population. Measles vaccination is low, and outbreaks are frequent. Cholera and Hepatitis E have also been reported in the target counties. In the three counties, as is limited functionality of health facilities, capacity and poor coverage, including recent closures of health facilities due to reduced funding, have made it difficult to meet increasing health needs worsened by floods, food insecurity and conflict. Women, children, the elderly and PWD are particularly vulnerable to the limited access to health care. The lack of medicine and skilled staff as well as equipment, high transportation cost, insecurity, floods, long distance to health facility and high cost of health care are the main barriers to accessing health services. Only 49 per cent of the population can access health facilities in one hour’s time. Sixty-six per cent of the population has difficulty accessing health services, with additional barriers faced by women and girls.

2. Project activities

Key proposed activities for the project will be :1. Implementation of Integrated disease surveillance and response in close coordination with WHO, state MOH and County health departments in addition to Support CHD to develop EP&RPs and also to set up early warning systems. 2. Support the improvement of waste management, including the construction of incinerators in PHCCs, PHUs and Hospitals.3. Support infection prevention and control through the provision of supplies, capacity building of staff, improving the WASH in health facilities.4. Support last mile delivery of Vaccines and other medical commodities and supplies from the core pipeline5.Capacity building of health care staff and in critical skills to be able to provide RMNCAH services.6.Support RCCE at the County level to conduct community engagement as part of emergency preparedness, during crisis and or disease outbreaks particularly vaccine preventable and water borne diseases(Cholera and hep E) and during the recovery period to prevent relapse.7.Strengthen community-based event surveillance and the timely investigation of any alerts of public health importance8. Procure and distribute emergency drug supplies to PHCCs and PHCUs as to provide life saving treatment to conflict affected population.9. Support delivery of health services in facilities and through outreaches, including EPI services, clinical consultations and Skilled birth attendance10. Support minor rehabilitation of facilities infrastructure to ensure full functionality.11. Support integrated MHPSS and S/GBV service delivery in supported facilities
