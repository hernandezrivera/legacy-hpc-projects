Project Summary

Humanitarian Need Overview in Syria; Waking up in Syria today means looking at a bleak future ahead of you. Syria faces a multitude of challenges that make it one of the world’s most complex humanitarian and protection emergencies. The country has lost 42 places on the Human Development Index; most Syrians face a context of continuing humanitarian decline. The country still has the largest number of internally displaced people in the world.
Overall humanitarian situation in the targeted area: after the 12-year war in the region and the almost complete destruction of infrastructure, especially health centers and due to the return of the displaced people who are still displaced from it for several years, the region lacks basic services, especially health. As 75% of the area is destroyed, and we find in the OCHA reports that the risk rate in the area reached 4.
The targeted areas were determined based on the reports sent by OCHA.
This project was developed to alleviate the repercussions of the war and to improve the situation of the most vulnerable people through several services, the most important services are:
• Providing primary and secondary medical and trauma services to all vulnerable groups
• Reducing the mortality rate
• Raising the level of health awareness
• Providing medical services such as consultations, and medicines for free to children and their families.
• Increase access to health services for vulnerable children, adolescents and caregivers through referrals and direct services.
Justifications and objectives of the project:
Due to the association's long experience in implementing such projects, as a large part of its activities and services provided within the health sector, in which it achieved great success and distinction through its centers and projects with several organizations, including the World Health Organization, which confirms its experience and ability to implement such projects with the highest possible quality.
And also, the association has access to Ain Al-Fija because it’s already having access to Al-Ghouta and most of the Rural Damascus areas.
