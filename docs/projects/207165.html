Project Summary

Following the escalation in violence which resulted to the ongoing armed conflict in the North West and South West Regions of Cameroon today, many humanitarian problems have crop up among which is child protection issues. Due to displacement and the fragile communities or areas of habitation, many children are continuously been violated and abused daily. This Project targets conflict affected children in conflict affected communities in the North West Region and aims at making sure all children are protected from various forms of abuses perpetrated against children in these communities as a result of the ongoing conflict. This project targets a total of 5,245 Children. To successfully address this problem, the project will:
1) Built the capacities of 25 Community-Based Child Protection Animators on protection monitoring and reporting.
2) Establish 300 birth certificates for affected children
3) Educate at least 1000 persons on birth registration.
4) The project will ensure that 300 children have access to GBV risk prevention, mitigation and response
5) the project will also ensure the identification and implementation of measures to prevent risks of violence, abuse and exploitation of children during this humanitarian context.
6) This project will also ensure that 1097 parents, caregivers and community leaders are sensitized about child protection risks in targeted Communities of the North West region.
7) the project will ensure that 825 UASC are accessing family-based care or appropriate alternative services and reunified or in contact with their families.
8) The project will allow us to provide children at risk of violence, abuse and exploitation in these humanitarian situations with child sensitive case management.
9) Provision of psychosocial support to children and their caregivers.
10) Provide beneficiaries with PSEA measure by providing accessible safe channels for SEA reporting.
Therefore, this project is quite timely and of great need now as there is an increasing violation of children's rights in many communities within the North West Region.
