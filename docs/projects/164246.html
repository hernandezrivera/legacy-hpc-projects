Project Summary

MWL will implement an integrated protection project which covers four sub-districts: Afrin/ Albab /Harim /Idleb, by 4 community centers, and mobile teams based on the huge and enormous needs in those areas after the last wave of displacement.
The project aims at creating a safer and more secure protective environment by providing an integrated set of General protection, Child Protection, Gender-Based Violence, and Mine Action services.
Regarding trageted Gender and Age Groups, the CWDs, Orphans, separted children, adolescents girls, Widows, Children Headed House hold to be prioritized, focusing on the highest severity of vulnerable groups to respond to these group’s needs, taking into consideration GAM.
In order to ensure full inclusive intervention provided to the targeted communities, MWL has ensured a high level of coordination with other organizations which are already working in the same location.
MWL will target 2000 beneficiaries consists of four main components:
The First component is delivering general protection services to the host and IDPs including the following activities: awareness-raising, psychosocial assistance, targeted support to persons with specific protection needs and other community initiatives, through community centers and mobile teams
The second component covers child protection issues targeting children, youth, men, and women include the following activities:
sustained child protection programs, parenting programs, awareness-raising, and community events to prevent and respond to child protection issues, case management and training for men and women to raise the capacity about protection concept
The third component focuses on GBV issues to decrease and confront any GBV concerns and topics through awareness raising using the GBV SC awareness raising toolkit. In addition to GBV response services, prevention &empowerment activities, and training for staff and community actors on GBV SOPs Referral and Risk Mitigation, and GBV awareness Toolkit for 7 days
The last and fourth component focus on Mine Action activities:
Provide Risk Education sessions in addition to train people to conduct these sessions, conduct contamination surveys and Provide victim assistance services in areas affected by explosive hazards.
Workshop and meeting with community leaders and key community members will be implemented throughout the assessment, planning stages, implementation and close out of project. In addition, consultations and awareness raising will be implemented with the communities people prior to project implementation. Interviewing key informants during the assessment phase and Focus group discussions FGDs will be voluntarily conducted after getting the consent with several beneficiary groups. The expected outputs of these FGDs will be ideas, suggestions or recommendations that can be integrated into the project design so as to improve the provided services.
The need assessment has been conducted in coordination with Local Councils, community key members, Camps leaders, and stakeholders. All the topics of protection awareness activities will be determined depending on the need assessment results
Conducting FGDs based on beneficiaries consent for the Community-based Child Protection Networks (CPN) in targeted locations to get their feedback along the project life cycle. MWL will involve all relevant community groups and stakeholders of child protection within these committees. These networks will support MWL in building good relationship with beneficiaries and the whole community.
FGD tool will be conducted voluntarily on monthly basis to measure (B,G,CWD) satisfaction about the provided activities along the project implementation and get their feedback from multiple perspectives

4 - Needs

MWL collaborated with the local councils in All locations to do a needs assessment. Selected communities have a large number of IDPs, MWL has selected the target location in coordination with CP and Mine Action coordinators to ensure that prevent overlapping and select the most need in those locations.The initial findings indicated that: forced and early marriage, economic exploitation by intimate partner, and family members, community, denial of work and education, and physical violence are the most common types of GBV across the 4 locations; while the reasons why GBV continue to happen are deterioration of families’ economic situation and lack of livelihoods which put the family under psychological and financial pressure and lead to different types of GBV including early marriage, economic exploitation, and violence.According to Sit Rep8 OCHA: protection risks have increased with the ongoing displacement, lack of shelter options and sub-standard living conditions in areas of displacement, specifically related to GBV, CP. Overcrowding in IDP sites and makeshift shelters and a lack of WASH facilities places women and girls at particular risk of GBV.According to Voices 2020: In NW Syria, women reported on well-functioning safe spaces and mobile teams with psychosocial support. The spaces are for women the only place to spend time away from their home. W&Gs access to GBV services is restricted because of cultural and social norms as well as movement restrictions linked to violence and harassment referring to FGDs conducted in Kelly WGSS.Trainer of GBV training will continue supervising the trainee work through field visits and WhatsApp group for backup information. GBV case manager will supervise the caseworkers using CM supervision tool, survivor satisfaction survey, follow up checklist and other recommended as per the IASC CM guidelines. MWL will send regular reporting to the protection/GBV cluster in addition to 4Ws

5 - Implementation of activities

Project activities will be implemented inside a community center (safe and accessable for CWD) using the appropriate curriculum and materials like PSS activities for children and adults, parenting skills, and GBV activities in the safe space for women, they can leave their children in the PSS room to exploit and benefit from their time inside the center. Case management activities for women and children will be delivered in a separated and safe room to ensure the cases privacy.GBV component will include:- Outreach activities and Awareness raising using GBV SC awareness raising toolkit. - GBV response services including CM- PSS activities - Emergency protection services by mobile teams including dignity kits distribution- Training for staff and community actors on GBV SOPs Referral and Risk Mitigation, and GBV awareness Toolkit for 7 daysThe project will support the protection community networks to launch protection initiatives, Advocate for duty bearers and key stakeholders to inform and enhance the response to protection risks, Build technical capacity targeting humanitarian actors and community members.MWL mobile teams will be responsible to raise the awareness between beneficiaries in the community focused on the most important protection issues and concerns covering: Risk Education, awareness sessions about GBV and child protection topics in addition to conduct open days and eventsAll these activities will focus on the highest severity of vulnerable groups and individuals through case management, identify, refer and use emergency fund when needed.These activities conducted in conjunction with M&E process to arrange and monitor the activities smoothly by creating a workplan the beginning of the project and well be revised on weekly basis by arranging a regular meeting with project manager and other relevant focal points. Tracking on the plan will be done to make sure that there is no deviation affecting the sought project performance.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk: The overcrowding and lack of privacy in multi-household, lack of doors and partitions for sleeping and changing clothes can increase exposure to sexual harassment and assault. Tensions linked to overcrowding may lead to an escalation of partner violence and other forms of domestic violenceMitigation: a) advocate for appropriate and adequate household rehabilitation to be provided considering privacy. We will include women, girls with disability and other at-risk groups to determine such things as if criteria are in place to determine how shelters are being allocated; if they are built for safety and privacy; and if shelter materials and shelter-related NFIs are being distributed in areas that are safe. B) safety audits with elderly, disabilities, women, girls, boy and men should be organized will be organized. R: There is a risk of discrimination or perceptions of discrimination if a service is provided to specific groups or individuals at the exclusion of others. M: 1. Ensure a comprehensive service mapping is undertaken so that risk mitigation and communication with communities can be proactive and informed; 2. Ensure consultation with communities related to the location of services to understand relevant community perspectives; 3. Carry out safety audits for beneficiaries’ access to site locations. R: Women might face challenges accessing the WGSS due to child-rearing responsibilities.M: Day-care will be available at the WGSS to ensure women’s participation at the vocational training initiatives.R: Privacy concerns along with confidentiality and safety might prevent women & girls to access GBV services. Program and field team ensures confidentiality principles are in line with global standards, data protection protocols.M: Staff is sensitized around attitude in terms of sensitive service provision through GBV guidelines and SOPs training. Supervising and Monitoring on-site visits will ensure privacy, confidentiality, and safety measures.
