Project Summary

Beirut Port explosions killed more than 178 persons, with more than 6,000 injured. The number of
people missing is reported to be more than 30. Thousands of patients remain in hospital for trauma and
burn treatment, while others remain in ICU.
The influx of patients comes as many intensive care units (ICUs) in Beirut were already approaching capacity due to a growing COVID-19 caseload. Ministry of Public Health data indicates that Beirut has one of the largest concentrations of identified cases nationwide.
Through this intervention, Islamic Relief Worldwide is planning to support the affected hospitals and PHCCs with the medical consumables (including PPEs), medical supplies, medicines, and medical equipment to ensure medical services are provided with the quality required, and to enhance the people protection against COVID.19
Islamic Relief has an experience of more than 6 years supporting Syrian, Palestinian refugees, as well as host community in Lebanon through the compensation into the health sector. IR provides primary and secondary health care assistance for vulnerable groups in static centers and through mobile clinics.
IR equipped private and governmental hospitals, for instance, the Kidney Dialysis units-to target most vulnerable beneficiaries with Free of charge medical services.
In response to the Beirut port explosion, Islamic Relief Worldwide has already secured funding for these interventions and already started to contact the affected medical centers (hospitals and PHCCs) since the explosion took place in order to record the needed materials/ equipment, list of needs were received by IRW and are under analysis to be procured and provided to the targeted centers/ hospitals in coordination with Health cluster and MOHP.

1 - Needs

The damages caused by the explosions to medical facilities compound the burden on the health system in Beirut. Six major hospitals and 20 clinics sustained partial or heavy structural damage. A preliminary rapid assessment within a 15-kilometre radius of the explosions, has revealed that, out of 55 medical facilities, only half are fully operational and around 40 per cent have suffered moderate to serious damage and need rehabilitation. The collective capacity was reduced by 500 hospital beds and at least 160,000 vulnerable people residing in the catchment area and who rely on them for medications and basic health care were affected. Medical equipment, supplies, medications and personal protective equipment were destroyed. There are major concerns regarding the resurgent spread of COVID-19 are due to the high risk of exposure for health workers due to overcrowding of emergency departments; shortages of personal protection equipment (PPE); high risk of accelerated community transmission; increased population vulnerability coupled with poor compliance to preventive measures and anxiety; and reduced number of beds and ICU capacity.Procurement of PCR tests and community-based surveillance among newly displaced groups is critical, as is the distribution of PPE and disinfection and hygiene supplies to vulnerable groups.

2 - Activities

Islamic Relief will identify health centers (PHC & Hospital) for needs and will address the following gaps according to a specific criteria:- The provision of essential medical supplies, consumables and medications (including PPEs) that adheres with the list of essential items mentioned in the "request of medical consumables for the health center" that apply to the MoPH on a quarterly basis (every 4 months).- Support the medical facilities affected with the medication/ drugs needs.- Support the medical facilities with general supplies and medical equipment to ensure medical services are provided with the quality required.Islamic Relief will work with the health sector and the MoPH PHC department before initiating distribution to ensure close coordination and avoidance of waste and duplication. Lists of commodities and initial needs assessments conducted bilaterally with 7 PHCCs and 2 hospitals will be fully shared with relevant actors.
