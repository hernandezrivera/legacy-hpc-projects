Project Summary

The overall objective of the program is to provide immediate emergency primary healthcare services through mobile clinics to the drought affected population in Buurdhuubo district. The Project will also entails provision of treatment to the venerable women of reproductive age and children <5 children boys and girls. The project will improve the social mobilization of the preventive healthcare services through provision of monthly health talks to the caregivers as well as disease surveillance in the area through sharing of weekly epidemiological report to the health cluster. It will also provide outpatient consultation to the vulnerable boys, girls, and women of child bearing age as well as men. Through this RAAS will intend to capacity build the project staff and community health workers on effective integrated management of childhood illness as well as community case management hence improving the staff capacity
The project will be coordinated with Jubaland regional Ministry of health and Gedo sub cluster health for information sharing and feedback it will principally take community as one of its main agenda driving for the implementation and will ensure better coordination for sustainability of the services.

Needs

An estimated 2.6 million people are displaced in Somalia.it has the world’s highest child mortality rate (World Bank, 2017). For a child born in Somalia, risk of dying is highest in the neonatal period (Neonatal Mortality Rate 39/1,000), the first 28 days of life, and approximately 45 per cent of child deaths under the age of five years will occur during that period. From the end of the neonatal period and through the first five years of life (under-5 mortality rate of 127/1,000), the main causes of death are pneumonia, diarrhea and malaria. Across this age range, malnutrition is an underlying contributing factor in about 45 per cent of all deaths from all causes. Measles affected over 6,600 children up to August in 2018 and the recent cases of vaccine-derived (cVDPV) polio underlines the continued risk posed by overall low routine vaccination coverage (average of less than 65%) in Somalia. The high incidence of communicable disease is especially concerning within the food insecure population, and leads to worse health outcomes in malnourished children and adults, combined risk, experience a high prevalence of GAM (16.7%) and SAM (4.1%) as well as a low measles vaccination coverage (45.4%).The lack of comprehensive and reliable epidemiological and disease information from all regions has made it very difficult to ascertain the extent of gaps in service provision and burden of disease across the country. Women of child bearing age die as a result of complications during and following pregnancy and childbirth, even though most maternal deaths are preventable, Malaria remains a key public health problem affecting the communities living in Burdhubo Gedo and the Malaria Indicator Survey (MIS) 2014 reported a prevalence rapid diagnostic test (RDT) rate of 5.4% in this region ranking among the highest regions that recorded (12.9%). Malaria also constitutes the leading cause of morbidity and mortality affecting mainly women/children within the facilities (MCH and OTPs)

Activities

1. Provide basic consultation on obstetric services through ANC and PNC consultations for 4000 PLWs in Garbaharey and Buurdhubo district Gedo region.2. Established outreach mobile clinic to improve referral of complicated cases to nearest hospital in Garbaharey town.3. Provide EPI services through routine vaccination at the facility, outreach services for 8000 children under five years.4. Provide quality drugs and equipment to support the facilities as well as face lift in the two facilities.5. Train and supervise 20 health care providers and 10 community health workers on quality health services such as emergency management, and public health promotion in the project sites.6. Capacity build 20 project staff on IMCI and EMOC in Garbaharey and Buurdhubo district.7. Provide vaccine preventable to children under five boys, girls and pregnant and lactating women in Buurdhuubo and Garbaharey district RAAS health facility 9. Provide improved emergency obstetric care services in Buurdhuubo and Garbaharey district.
