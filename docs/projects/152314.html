Project Summary

The proposed activities under this project will enhance UNHCR's outreach and response to vulnerable groups affected by the conflict, which will contribute to strengthening their resilience, mitigating protection risks and boosting their coping mechanisms through the provision of Core Relief Items (CRIs), None-Food Items (NFIs) and shelter interventions. This is to enhance access to basic needs and services for UNHCR persons of concern in Libya especially the most vulnerable ones.

Needs

Due to the on-going political and economic instability, access to basic needs and shelter are top priority need in Libya.33% of IDPs in public shelter settings were reported to be in unfinished buildings. In addition, 26% were reported to be in informal settings such as tents, caravans, and makeshift shelters and 14% in schools. Another 14% were residing in other public buildings, 10% were residing in deserted resorts, and the remaining 3% were reported to be squatting on other peoples’ properties. To promote return and address the immediate shelter needs of IDPs, there is need for rehabilitation and/or construction of housing for those IDPs returning, considering the fact that years of ongoing conflict have taken a toll on infrastructures and housing in conflict neighborhoods. Provision of shelter assistance is therefore key.UNHCR’s interventions at disembarkation points and detention centers in Libya has as its primary objective to save lives, provide basic emergency relief and ensure the early identification and immediate protection of UNHCR persons of concern, mainly refugees and asylum seekers. Basic emergency assistance takes the form of distribution of a variety of core relief items (CRIs) tailored to the specific needs of the persons of concern, and include women kits, baby kits, winterization kits, and other kits adapted for special needs.

Activities

Under this project the following 2 main activities will be implemented: • Shelter materials and maintenance tool kits provided: UNHCR’s shelter tool kit includes various items to support the construction of different types of basic shelters, particularly transitional shelter and other basic structures. It is designed for family distribution, one kit per family and includes tools and hardware only. Under the current project, the shelter tool kits will be distributed among the IDP and returnee population with shelter needs. IDPs in need of shelter assistance will be identified based on a criteria that takes into account access to areas of return, intention of return and the voluntariness of the return. • Core relief items provided: UNHCR will procure CRI/NFI kits to be distributed to refugees and asylum seekers at disembarkation points, detentions centres and community development centres in Libya as well as to vulnerable IDP returnees and host communities. The distribution of such CRIs/NFIs do not only provide much needed emergency relief, but also assist UNHCR in accessing specfic detention centres and disembarkation points, where access would otherwise be more restricted. In addition to the distribution of tailored kits such as women kits, baby kits, winterization kits, and other kits adapted for special needs.
