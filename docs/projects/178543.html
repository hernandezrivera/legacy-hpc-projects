Project Summary

The project aims to distribute 100 Food Baskets for "food insecure people" displaced to Al-Bab Sub-district (monthly distribution for 9 months), Priority will be given for households with specific vulnerability criteria (e.g. HH headed by women, People with special needs)
The Selection process will be coordinated with FSL Cluster and Al-Bab Local Council and will start in March 2021, Food baskets will be standardized according to FSL Cluster technical recommendations.
Needs Assessment was carried out for 100 Households during Ramadan month (April 2021), which reached many neighborhoods inside Al-Bab city.
The targeted beneficiaries according to the needs assessment are:
- 94 IDPs / 6 HC (Total 100 families)
- 25 HHs headed by female / 58 HHs with PSN / 17 HHs under poverty line
- 98 Men / 106 Women / 146 Boys / 141 Girls (Total 513)

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Protection Risk/Threat 1: Risk of theft of Food baskets or inputs given, Likelihood: medium, Impact: high, Mitigating Measure: Agencies provide feedback and accountability mechanisms to different groups of users; work closely with all partners on the ground, Monitoring: Agency ﬁeld monitoring and consultation with communities; Proper stock management system in place. Resources: Dedicated staff member and good storage management.PSEA Risk/Threat 2: beneficiaries sexually harassed or abused in exchange for Food baskets, Likelihood: low, Impact: high, Mitigating Measure: Consult community throughout program cycle to determine the safest mechanism for handing over the food baskets, Monitoring: Enhance communication: (i) distribute brochures with Food baskets including hot-line numbers for reporting PSEA incidents (ii) avoid 1 to 1 connection with beneficiaries (SOH front-end team will consist of 1 male and 1 female at least), and (iii) provide training for SOH field staff, and ensure they commit to humanitarian principles and code of conduct signed after recruitment.Protection Risk/Threat 3: Exchange process for Food baskets at a lesser value may expose females to harassment by buyers, Likelihood: medium, Impact: medium, Mitigating Measure: Consult community throughout program cycle to determine the reasoning behind the sale/exchange of assistance and solicit their feedback with regards to solutions, Monitoring: Enhance data collection: (i) align PDM tool with global standards; where possible, (ii) collect qualitative data through FGDs with different segments of the community; and (iii) consistently review and address contents of feedback mechanisms.

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

The project took into consideration the following risks (beyond the project's control):1- Mass displacement from Idlib to Aleppo based on conflict escalation (SOH contributes actively with local stakeholders during crises and has plan B to shift operation to Turkish/Syrian borderline).2- Markets may be un-accessible because of insecurity (modalities will be changed if necessary according to local security conditions/challenges).3- Some humanitarian actors may conduct uncoordinated distribution for households targeted by our project (coordination with the local council and FSL cluster will take place before implementation).
