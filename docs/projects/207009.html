Project Summary

The overall objective of the project is to contribute to ensuring dignified living conditions and to enhance the self-reliance and resilience of the most vulnerable population affected by the ongoing conflict and natural hazards through the provision of safe, equitable, inclusive, and gender-responsive life-saving and life-sustaining assistance mainstreaming safe programming attuned to the protection risks and needs of the most vulnerable groups such as women and girls. The project has two main components:
1) Life-saving assistance: ACF will focus on providing support for the most vulnerable population to satisfy their basic needs. ACF will be providing first-line survival assistance for basic needs such as food (either in kind or in cash/voucher, with a flexible methodology adapted to the specific context of each community that will take into account the market functionality and capacity, governmental restrictions on cash assistance, and beneficiaries preference among others) with a focus on newly displaced persons and returnees.
2) Life-sustaining assistance: ACF intends to protect, re-establish, and support the livelihoods including diversification as well as provide access to life-sustaining essential services to the affected population with restoration activities focusing on areas of return and diversification in areas of displacement. ACF will provide emergency livelihood support such as farming/fishing inputs, and complementary training (including life skills) and Income Generating Activities (IGAs) to enhance their self-reliance and resilience through agricultural production, access to income, and diversified livelihoods. Rehabilitation of essential and strategic infrastructures (e.g., markets, roads, schools) through cash for work will be prioritized targeting young adults and other HHs not targeted with the other life-sustaining interventions and creating temporary employment.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

All the below listed activities (with the exception of Mueda where only life-sustaining activities will be carried out du to the high presence of other actors providing food assistance as per coordination and agreement with the Food Security Cluster) will be conducted in the following underserved and/or hard-to-reach areas: Meluco, Macomia, Quissanga, Mueda, Muidumbe and Nangade districts. The proposed priority activities undertaken by the project are:1) Life-saving activities:- Food and basic needs provision: provision of first-line survival assistance for basic needs to newly displaced persons and returnees.2) Life-sustaining activities:- On-farm livelihoods protection and restoration: provision of inputs and training for vulnerable households engaging in agriculture, livestock, and fishery.- Off-farm livelihoods protection/restoration and diversification: provision of inputs and training for vulnerable households engaging in off-farm Income Generating Activities (e.g. petty trading, tailoring, carpentry, blacksmith, masonry, food processing/preparation, handcraft).- Provision of temporary employment: Cash for Work schemes will be implemented to both provide vulnerable households with employment opportunities and to rehabilitate/construct essential and strategic infrastructures damaged or destroyed during the conflict and natural hazards (e.g., markets, feeder roads, schools, returnees' housing).

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The project will mainstream protection, addressing the protection risks and needs of affected people with the aim of safeguarding their dignity while strengthening the protection environment through collectiveand multi-sectoral action in line with commitments to gender equality principles, the centrality of protection, and PSEA across the humanitarian response. ACF will promote inter-agency collective action on the centrality of protection by ensuring implementation of mitigating measures, provision of complaint and feedback mechanisms, PSEA and child safeguarding focal points and disability inclusion. Moreover, ACF has already started the preliminary steps for conducting a gender and protection analysis to highlight specific gender issues in the intervention area and strengthen gender and protection activities, with an analysis of protection risks and existing vulnerabilities, distribution of roles and responsibilities including empowerment and decision-making power within the household, etc. ACF will complete the gender analysis and protection risks assessment in the initial phase of the project implementation, and will disseminate main results and observations to inform project design and intervention activities. ACF will also closely work with Protection Cluster to be supported on protection referral.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

ACF will ensure that at beneficiary targeting stage, community committees with equal representation of all community groups (including women, elderly and people with disability) will be formed for the initial self-targeting process. ACF will also advocate and propose to the community committees to utilize beneficiary selection criteria that includes gender and age vulnerabilities. In addition, during the needs assessment, ACF will assess also the specific needs of the different groups and accordingly tailor the assistance. The time, location and implementation approach of the project activities will be defined in consultation with the beneficiaries and according to their needs, particularly of women, elderly and persons with disability, to ensure their participation and access to assistance. When the assistance will entail lengthy activities (e.g. Cash for Work, training), ACF will consult the vulnerable groups to ensure that no harm is caused; for example it will be taken in consideration to create childcare space where women with children will leave their child to play or be cared for while doing work; and women, elderly, disabled will be considered for clerical work and other light tasks to implement.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The project will identify people with disabilities since its targeting phase. Community committees will be formed and have equal representation of all community groups including people with disability. The communities, in all their different groups thus including people with disabilities, will be consulted to define appropriate strategies and implementation approaches for removing any barrier for participating to the project and access its assistance.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

An environmental impact assessment NEAT+ will be conducted before the project inception. In addition, ACF will adopt an agroecological approach while implementing the farming activities. Agroecology contributes to the preservation of the environment and natural resources, while also enhancing the provision of a number of ecosystem services. In parallel, ACF will promote use of biological and cultural pest control methods (including Integrated Pest Management, home made biopesticides and biofertilizers) that have fewer negative impacts on the environment. Climate change and environmental consideration will be part of the farming training curricula.With reference to the rehabilitation/construction of infrastructures through Cash for Work, a feasibility assessment will be conducted at project inception which will also assess the possible impact of the works on the environment. The study will also provide solutions to anticipated problems to avoid environmental harm, and accordingly measures will be put in place.
