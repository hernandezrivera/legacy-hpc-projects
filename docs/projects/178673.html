Project Summary

Due to military operation in the area, many of the resident population fled their
communities. Nevertheless, after the end of the conflicts in the regions, the local people
started to return to their communities. Recent reports declared that more than 300
households came back to Tell Abiad from Turkey through Turkish cross-border gate.
Latest feedback refers to a total of 25,000 population; 7000 of them are IDPs coming
from surrounded areas.
The responsible committee of the Water Hygiene and Sanitation – Tell Abiad local
council started to conduct check up on the existing water stations to assess their
technical situation. Two water stations provide the city with water. The first one is the old
water station, which consists of three pumps; each is linked to one well. The capacity of
the three wells is 100 m3/h, 75 m3/h, and 75 m3/h. This station provides the city with
80% of the total need for water. The new station is the new one that consists of 1 pump
related to a well of 100 m3/h capacity. The new station provides the city with 20% of its
total need for water. Nevertheless, the two stations are currently damaged and in need of
immediate rehabilitation for the submersible pump, electric transforming panel,
generator, and the control panels. Additionally, water pipelines must be maintained.
Furthermore, the challenge of obtaining the financial resources to operate the stations
still exists. The quality of water is compromised due to the lack of quality assurance
procedures. In general, the two stations are not 100% functional, which leads to a gap
regarding the smooth and safe access to water in Tell Abiad city. In other words, the core
problem that must be addressed is the miserable situation of the two water stations in
Tell Abiad city.
Assessment of the 2 water stations has shown the follows:
Station: Old station, No of wells: 3 well, Wells capacity: 100m3/h, 75m3/h and 75 m3/h,
Number of high tanks: 2, High tanks capacity: 200 m3 and 100 m3, Operating through electricity.
Station: New station, No of wells: 1 well, capacity: 100 m3/h, No of high tanks: 1,
capacity: 300 m3, Operating through electricity.
The project aims to improve safe access of the target population to drinkable water. This
aim will be reached by rehabilitating the water stations in Tell Abiad city to address the
core problem that was presented in the previous paragraph. The project will last for 10
months and will start on 01/03/2020.
The primary approach of this project is delivering development services to the affected
population in Tell Abiad city through the rehabilitation of 2 water stations along with
raising-awareness sessions.
The project will last for 6 months, maintenance and rehabilitation activities will be fulfilled. phase 2 will include the supervising of operation resources to function the two water stations which will be run by electracy.
The project will serve more than 25000 population and will prioritize the most vulnerable
groups in the target communities. The cross-cutting issues like gender, accountability to
affected people, and exit strategy will be taken into account in this project. WHOSD will
coordinate with other humanitarian actors and stakeholders in the area in order to
integrate the proposed WASH services with other humanitarian services.
WHOSD has guaranteed access to the target area through its team in Tell Abiad. WHOSD
was part of last meeting with the Turkish governor of Sanliurfa city in which the Turkish
authorities guaranteed access to Tell Abiad for humanitarian purposes
WWhite Hats Organization will be responsible for the water works, and coordination will be made with other authorities working in the region for the rest of the services, as the garbage removal service is carried out by the local council and the sanitation network is good.
An awareness hygiene promotion campaign, especially within the COVID 19 pandemic, will include all city residents.

4 - Needs

total population is about 25000, out of them, there are 7000 IDPs. Turkish Government announced that the cross-border gate between Akcakale city in the south of Turkey and Tell Abiad in northern Syria was opened forhumanitarian organizations based in Turkey and for Syrian refugees willing to return totheir communities in Tell Abiad. From a humanitarian perspective, the situation isdeteriorated in terms of available services, especially the prioritized ones. Acoording to arecent needs assessment carried out by WHOSD in Tell Abiad; the prioritized servicesnowadays are related to access to health services, availability of food, and availability ofdrinkable water which in turn led to undesired behaviors like using clean water forcooking and reduce the number of baths weekly. The key informants prioritized issuesrelated to the low level of hygiene in the community during the needs assessmentinterviews. In addition, most of the prevalent diseases are related to the existed poorpublic health within the city, especially, diarrhea, fever, and gastroenteritis. Some peoplepointed out some concerns related to accommodation. The demographic figures in TellAbiad are not so different from other areas in northern Syria. The females represent 55%of the total population. In fact, 55% of the community population is under 18 years oldwhich might refer to some households led by youth or non-adults. The needs assessmentreport shows that there are 185 females and 295 male with a disability. The deterioratedsocio-economic status in the city is challenging the access of the target population to theavailable services in the area.

5 - Implementation of activities

1-1- Rehabilitate the two water stations in Tell Abiad city. 1-2- Repairthe water pipelines linked to the target two water stations in Tell Abiad. 1-3- Operate thetwo water stations in Tell Abiad for three months under the support of WHOSD incooperation with the local water authorities. Phase two activities: 2-1- Install chlorineinjection pump for every target water station. 2-2- Carry out chlorine tests FRC every weekfor the drinking water from the target water stations. The FRC tests will be carried out onthree levels; the first one is in the container, the second one is after the chlorination, andthe third one is in the households. The rehabilitation description: The project aims toincrease the daily per capita share and sustain the water supply service to the city by: 1-Rehabilitation of (Tell Abiad old water station): • Purchasing and insulting submersiblepump 70 hp • Purchasing and insulting generator 460 KVA • Providing and insultingcontrol panels for two wells • Maintenance of electricity transformer • Maintenance ofgenerator • Purchasing and insulting chlorine Injection Pump 2- Rehabilitation of (TellAbiad new water station): • Purchasing and insulting battery 200 mAh. • Purchasing andinsulting Chlorine Injection Pump • Purchasing and insulting generator 225 KVA 3-Maintenance of the water network within the city, including the maintenance of pipes,valves and manholes.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

1-Risk: Risk of theft of WASH rehabilitation/construction materials such as generators and construction materials (during delivery/storage/ after installation) by armed groups and used in conflict activities.Likelihood: Low . Impact: High.Mitigating Measure: MoUs to be agreed with local authorities/councils to ensure local ownership of materials and rehabilitated/new facilities.Promote cost recovery systems for longer term maintenance and ownership to infrastructure to promote community ownership. 2-Risk: Improper storage of hazardous supplies (chlorine and other) materials) - Possibility of explosion, human casualty and asset destruction.Likelihood: Medium . Impact: High.Mitigating Measure: Ensure proper storage of supplies with clear provision of care and safety consideration. Make sure that clear instruction for use is available for chlorine products primarily for household use. 3- Risk: Potential exposure to explosive hazard (IED/UXO/ERW) during assessment/ rehabilitation of wastewater infrastructure (WWTP, sewer networks).Likelihood: Medium . Impact: High.Mitigating Measure: Work closely with Mine Action actors in the area and conduct assessments/rehabilitation works only in areas declared as cleared; Conduct safety training with stuff and contractors; Add explosive hazard awareness material to your programming.
