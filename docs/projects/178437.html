Improving safe access to quality formal and non-formal education services in Northwestern and eastern Syria with preventive measures of Covid-19.

Project Summary

This project is going to contribute to the education cluster and HRP objectives through improving the safe access of 50,901 children (25687 boys & 25214 girls) host and IDPs including 491(0.01) % CwD in 79 schools located in high severity locations in Idleb, Aleppo and Raqqa governorates to quality formal and non-formal education services by providing a package of education services that target the gaps and needs of the most vulnerable groups of hosts and IDPs children in 7 sub-districts in the camps and off camps.
This project will help 46065 (22957 boys & 23108 girls) school aged 6-17 years old to access formal education using the SIG curriculum and 4345 children(2476 boys & 1869 girls) of law achievers at the risk of drop out of schools and Out of schools children to access non-formal education(1975 children in catch-up & 2370 in remedial) including 491 children with disabilities (254 boys & 237 girls) with preventive measures of Covid-19 during 2021 through establishing 100 new classrooms in tents , conducting light repair to 376 classrooms and wash facilities taking into consideration the needs of CwD and gender sensitive issues , providing 60 schools with school furniture to increase the absorption capacity , conducting 79 back to Learning campaigns in 79 different locations targeting 7900 beneficiaries(3950 children and 3950 caregivers and parents)to reach out to the children dropped out of schools and re-enroll them and increase the awareness of parents and caregivers towards the importance of education and CP basic rights , providing 111,672 liters of heating fuel and 600 heaters to 79 schools , procuring and distributing 50901 school bags and education kits to 50901 children in 79 schools ,providing 79 schools with operational cost(drinkable water - cleaning tools & materials - buffet materials- stationery) to work properly , providing transportation for 491 CwD in 79 schools, providing 79 schools with safety equipment(first aid kits and fire extinguishers), paying the incentives to 2072 teachers(1170 males & 902 females) and supporting staff working in the 79 schools in formal and non-formal education , providing light professional development package including training on (Child Protection Principles and Child Safeguarding –Integration of Children with disabilities in Education Response - Modern Teaching Methodologies– Education in Emergencies ) , providing 79 schools with recreational materials , printing 4345 copies of non-formal textbooks , providing teaching materials kits to 79 schools , providing 50901 children in formal and non-formal with school-based psychological, support conducting safety training to the children and teachers ,provide cleaning and disinfection materials kits to 79 schools ,provide 52973 PPE kits to the teachers and children in 79 schools ,provide awareness raising sessions on the preventive measures of Covid-19 to 50901 children , 2072 teachers and 7900 parents and caregivers.
The affected population will be involved in all the stages of the project planning , designing , implementation , Monitoring , Evaluation where there will be FGDs , PMDs after each distribution, satisfaction surveys on the provided services and distributed items and field monitoring visits , discussions , coordination , consultation and meetings with the parents , teachers , children and local education authorities in the schools on choosing the best appropriate ways for implementation of activities, provision of services and maintain high levels of accountability to affected population during after COVID-19.
In order to make sure that suggestions and complains of the beneficiaries are heard during and after COVID-19, there will be posters clarifying the objectives of the project with Facebook, what’s up, email, accounts will be posted outside and inside the schools (After re-opening the schools) and on the WhatsApp groups to the caregivers and children in the distance learning in COVID-19 as plan(B)

4 - Needs

Basing on the HNO 2021, an estimated 6.9 million people (97% children) need humanitarian education assistance, 94% of school-aged children are living in areas with sever, extreme or catastrophic education conditions mainly concentrated in Idleb and. Basing on “Schools in Syria report” that published from ACU and 14 of Education Cluster members in March 2021, 78% of school don’t have adequate quantities of cleaning materials and soap, 50% of the enrolled students don’t have textbooks ,22% of the non-functional schools are partially damaged. Basing on the need assessment that SCA conducted in 7 sub-districts in Idleb, Aleppo and Raqqa Govern-orates in January 2021, the key findings were: 1-There are 433 functioning schools, 78 schools out of them need support2- There are 46065 children (22957 boys & 23108 girls) enrolled in formal education in 78 schools 3- There are 2072 teachers working voluntarily without incentives in the xxx schools 4- There is a need to establish 100 new classrooms in tents in 3 sub-districts 5- 376 classrooms need light rehabilitation in the 60 schools 6- The wash facilities in 54 schools need light rehabilitation 7- 79 schools need support with heating fuel and heaters during winter 8- All the children in the 79 schools need support with school bags and stationery kits 9- The 79 schools need support with running cost (drinking water - cleaning tools & materials - buffet materials, stationery for education personnel)10- 491 CWD need transportation to reach the schools 11- The 79 schools need support with safety equipment 12- 80% of teachers in the 79 schools need capacity building training 13- The 79 schools need recreational materials 14- The 79 schools need support with teaching

5 - Implementation of activities

This project is going to help 46065 children (22957 boys & 23108 girls) school aged children host and IDPs to have access to quality formal education opportunities using the SIG curriculum and 4345 children(2476 boys & 1869 girls) who are late in their education and children dropout of schools to access non-formal education(1975 children in catch-up & 2370 in remedial) including 491 children with disabilities (254 boys & 237 girls) with preventive measures of Covid-19 during 2021 in 79 schools through establishing 100 new classrooms in tents , conducting light repair to 376 classrooms and wash facilities taking into consideration the needs of CwD and gender sensitive issues , providing 60 schools with school furniture to increase the absorption capacity , conducting 79 back to Learning campaigns in 79 different locations targeting 7900 beneficiaries(3950 children and 3950 caregivers and parents)to reach out to the children dropped out of schools and re-enroll them and increase the awareness of parents and caregivers towards the importance of education and CP basic rights and child safe guarding , providing 111,672 liters of heating fuel and 600 heaters to 79 schools during the winter , procuring and distributing 50901 school bags and education kits to 50901 children in 79 schools ,providing 79 schools with operational cost(drinkable water - cleaning tools & materials - buffet materials- stationery) to work properly , providing transportation to 491 CwD in 79 schools, providing 79 schools with safety equipment(first aid kits and fire extinguishers) , paying the incentives to 2072 teachers(1170 males & 902 females) and supporting staff working in the 79 schools in formal and non-formal education through direct supervision

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Protection Risk: Abusing or exploiting the children by teachers or other supporting staff Mitigating Measures: 1- SCA will make sure that PSEA policies are in place, practiced and monitored 2- 2- SCA will ensure that targeted communities and staff have access to safe and effective reporting systems including when they will get response Protection Risk: Teachers and other supporting staff participate in physical punishment or bullying of childrenMitigating Measures: 1- All the teachers and supporting staff in the schools will sign on a code of conduct on physical punishment and bullying 2- The parents and the children, teachers and other supporting staff in schools will be encouraged to report any issues to physical punishment or bullying Protection Risk: Children are over-exposed to the risk of, or experience, harm by teachers, school personnel or organization staffMitigating Measures: 1- There will be Child Safeguarding policy signed by all the project staff , teachers and supporting staff in the schools and monitored on annual bases 2- SCA will provide sufficient safe and clean bathroom facilities that segregated basing on sex and age Protection Risk: The schools don’t provide sufficient protection from the elements (rain, snow, cold, etc.)Mitigating Measures: 1- SCA will do rehabilitation to the wash facilities and classrooms including Painting, blacksmithing, Electricity, building works, building works for CWD)2- SCA will provide safety and evacuation training to the children and teachers in the schools on monthly bases and explain the safety and security plans

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumption: All the planned schools are accessible safely without any restrictionsRisk : The schools are no longer accessible due to the change in the maps of control MM:1-SCA will monitor the situation on the ground closely 2-SCA will track the movement of the teachers and children and coordinate with the local education authorities to accommodate them in the new destinations to access their education after displacement Assumptions: 100 classrooms in tents are established and 376 classrooms are rehabilitated in 79 schools as well as provided with furniture and safety equipment without the interventions of head of the camps or any concerns related to HLP. Risk: The intervention of some camps' authorities in rehabilitation works and some land owners aren’t giving the permission to establishment of new classrooms MM: SCA will discuss these activities in advance with all the relevant stakeholders and will coordinate closely with the local education authorities (Directorates of Education in the areas of operations and will keep them updated on the progress of activities in schools) Assumptions: The CwD are enrolled and attending the schools where all their needs are met and there are no barriers that prevent their access to schools in the classrooms and wash facilities. Risk: The CwD are facing some barriers that prevent their access to learning facilities in entrances of classrooms and wash facilities. MM: SCA will consider the needs of CwD when assessing the needs of rehabilitation in the classrooms and wash facilities and the M&E team will monitor the implementation of their needs and the Engineer will give much focus to their needs in the designs, implementation and receipt of works from the suppliers.
