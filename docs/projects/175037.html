Project Summary

According to HNO 2020, The conflict in Syria has created and exacerbated large-scale humanitarian needs around water, sanitation, and hygiene (WASH) for the country’s population. Women are disproportionately affected by a lack of access to WASH services and facilities due to both biological and social factors. Women and girls have specific needs around menstrual and reproductive health, which if unmet can have wide-reaching adverse effects on health, maternal and infant health, The escalation of hostilities from 1 December 2019 to 31 January 2020 resulted in the displacement of nearly 520, 000 people mainly from southern Idleb and western Aleppo governorates, 80% of displaced are women and children. Many of the people displaced have been displaced several times, intensifying existing vulnerabilities. More people have moved in the interim period; through this project, under (ASO)1-(ASO)2 Objectives IHR will implement six interviews:
1-Installation 20 latrine units, the latrine to set up includes 5 cabins. This allows to meet the condition of health standards. 4 cabins toilets one of them for PWD’ the fifth one will be path room. 3 Water tank 1m3 should be fixed above each latrine set.
2- Supply and distribution 22680 m3 of water via trucks to 4,200 BNF most affected population in three Camps in (Maaret Tamsrin, Afrin) districts attached locations .
3-Installation of water tanks and water points (4 tanks capacity of 45m3 will be received from Ihsan RD or other UNICEFs partners warehouses if possible,
4-Supply safe water through trucking or connection to existing systems, by contraction of water network with four main points in each camp to facilitate the water distribution, in addition to that this network will be ready in future when we get chance to arrive the general network to the camps
5-Hygiene promotion training for community mobilizers for hygiene promoters, increased hygiene awareness and adaptation at household levels and community participation, organized communities to practice hygiene behaviors and this training will target the population in Camps and community with high priority for PWD, women and GBV survivor.
6) Aligns with training activity, IHR depending on wash cluster recommendation will Distribute of WASH NFIs and hygiene kits and IEC to affected population (700 Family Hygiene kits, Related to all above intervention IHR will considered the PWD, Women headed household, GBV survivor and sex segregated public latrines based thorough consultation with the users; separate queues for men and women while distributing hygiene kits and ensure the presence of female staff during distributions. mitigation measures to prevent Sexual exploitation and abuse including from construction workers/landlords, in camps, outside of camps, for the shelter rehabilitation or for free rental activities, etc.
its worth to mentioned that related to locations selection IHR will coordinate with cluster and partners in Afrin and Mareet Tmesrin districts . To avoid any tension between IDPs and HC ,IHR will target 10% of host community
IHR is required to develop their project with a strong risk management strategy.

4 - Needs

The IHR assessed the needs in 2 communities Maaret Tamsrin and Maaret Elekhwan in Mareet Tamisrin subdistrict, as well, one community in the Afrin subdistrict. The locations selected according to received feedback and latest updates from the CCCM cluster about the IDPs movements. Interviews were done with key informants’ individuals form IDPs and local councils in 2 subdistricts Afrin and Maret tamarin. However, 20 interviews were held; 11 with local council members, 9 with representatives of IDP groupsMost of the IDPs in assessed locations were from Ariha, Ma’arrat An-Nu’man, Saraqab and Jarjnaz.39% arrived a week before the assessment date, 36% arrived 1-2 weeks before, 15% arrived a month before and 10% arrived 1-7 days before the assessment date. The newly IDPs in the studied areas have reached 89,000, representing 18,390 families, 47% of the IDPs are males, 53 % are females. Children represent 45% of them. For children between 0-17 years, 41 % of them are under 5 years old and 59% of children are in the school-age (between 5- 17 years). the elderly is about 5 %, while 3% were households headed by disabilities. regard to the humanitarian assistance access, the respondents confirmed that there are no constraints caused by host communities to access of humanitarian assistance to the affected newly IDPs. In addition, 54 % of respondents confirmed that there are no constraints hinder IDPs' access to assistance, but 38% said that there was a lack of IDPs' information about available aids, and the rest respondents have no idea During the last 30 days. The drinking water main source was; 80% of the interviewed KIIs mentioned that women and men have not to access clean and safe water, the most challenges are high price of water, lack of storage capacity of water, and the far distance to water sources.

5 - Implementation of activities

The funds for this project will be utilized in order to achieve the priorities as outlined in the allocation paper, and in line with the Syria Response plan. The proposed activities will be, Covering wash, protection and safe water services of the most vulnerable HHs through the most appropriate modality for six months. Further more this project will participate in establishing sustain water networks in the camps. The program was designed in a manner that enables providing wash and protection services to women, children, youth, PWD and men, as in the proposed areas of intervention. Furthermore, due to the context in which the IDPs local population lives under, access to main wash and protection services. The design of the project also enables the empowerment of the local community and IDPs through improvement and development of the wash sector and improvement the protection services for children, women, disables and Adults.Additional that this project will contribute to reactive the local markets through purchasing the project items Therefore, IHR is planning to construction 20 latrines units, water network in three camps, distribution 700 hygiene kits for 700 families from IDPs in camps and non-formal camps.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

With better access to Protection, Wash and clean water for the entire population, women and girls will not be forced to travel long distances to access water thereby decreasing protection risks that are associated with such activities. As this program is designed to rehabilitate water networks, At the end of construction works, people live in targeted formal and nonformal camps will have equitable access to safe and adequate Protection, Wash and clean water as well as improved sanitation. In case of emergency response, separate male and female latrines will be constructed with full respect to privacy and culturally appropriate. furthermore, in each Latrines’ unit one of Toilet will be for PWD. Furthermore, all Child, youth women well be targeted through protection activities in this project. IHR will take in consideration included the PWD and women in recruitment procedures for the contingency program, the distributions of Hygiene and water kits will be door to door in some cases, to ensures that female-headed households will be reached for a much more equitable distribution, and through separate lines for men and women in cases of the door to door distributions is not possible.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

The access in the Maaret Tamsrin sub-district, north is available for the IHR team. The targeted community is located about 25 kilometers from the Turkish border.. The security situation a little bit stable comparing with the location near M5. So IHR had developed clear contingency plan to deal with all security situations The access in the Afrin sub-district, is available for the IHR team since the IHR has office and IHR has officially work permission and registered to operate in Afrin from Turkish authority and has a strong collaboration with the local authorities and has implemented many projects in Afrin and there are many ongoing projects in this area with signing cooperation protocols with a project-related directory in Hatay Turkey.Afrin located in Aleppo governorate, it is under Turkish control so its considered safe and stable To secure high security level IHR will keep coordinate with Local council and Turkish authority IHR will advertise on tenders and contract with vendors of same intervention area. This aims to ensure the access for all items in the targeted location easily with minimal risk.. The security instructions will be clear for all field staff to implement in case of an emergency change in the situation. As the security standards will be implemented in the field to ensure the safety for staff and beneficiaries, and evacuation if necessary. The targeted beneficiaries will be from IDPs and host communities to avoid tension between communities’ members and achieve positive intervention.for GBV risks, the IHR has female staff professionals dealing with GBV cases. those staff will be a presence at all implementation stages. their responsibilities; explain the feedback mechanism for people, alert the team on the need to adhere to the ethical standards of humanitarian action, be close of people to monitor any GBV case may appear during work and send according to referral pathway.
