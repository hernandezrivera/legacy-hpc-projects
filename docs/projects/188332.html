Project Summary

Food security remains a serious challenge due to continued localized hostilities, new and protracted displacements, increased numbers of recent returnees and the sustained erosion of communities' resilience after almost ten years of conict, in addition to, loss the value of local currency with rising the food prices continuously.
ONSUR’s proposed intervention seeks to support of vulnerable IDPs in camps through improving access to bread in these camps. The intervention will contribute to cover the FSL gaps in Salqin, Akhtarin, Azaz and Afrin camps.
The project (ongoing project) included one key activity of humanitarian intervention which is supporting the IDPs through distributing daily free bread bundles 800gr for HHs (1 bundle 1-4 members, 2 bundles 5-8 members,3 , 3 bundlesmore than 9 members).
-8500 daily free bread bundles for 4500 families in Salqin camps through 24 months.
- 9000 daily free bread bundles for 5000 families in Aghtrain and - Azaz camps through12 month.
-3200 daily free bread bundles for 2000 families in Afrin camps through12 months.
ONSUR will be committed to the FSL cluster and WHO recommendations on prevention measures related to COVID-19 especially during the distribution of bread and agricultural inputs and conduction of trainings. In addition, protection cluster recommendations will be taken into consideration to avoid any GBV/PSEA risk.

4 - Needs

The crisis in Syria has entered its tenth year, with seen the further exacerbating the food security and livelihood situation of families across the country. Food security remains a serious challenge due to continued localized hostilities, new and protracted displacements, increased numbers of recent returnees and the sustained erosion of communities' resilience after almost ten years of conict, in addition to, loss the value of local currency with rising the food prices continuously. HNO 2021 indicated that the estimated sector beneficaries for 2021 of food insecure people has increased to 14.2 million. ONSUR’s proposed intervention seeks to improve the resilience of vulnerable IDPs in camps through improving access to bread in these camps. The intervention will contribute to cover the FSL gaps in Salqin, Akhtarin, Azaz and Afrin camps.

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

Protection risks will be analyzed throughout the project life-cycle. According to theproject activities, the following protection risks and mitigating measures are presented:1)- Risk of exploitation or abuse by staff members/service providers if access toservices Mitigating measures: Communicate regularly and clearly to the communityabout what services are available and who services are for. Provide regular training tostaff and service providers for code of conduct and PSEA. 2)- Risk of doing harm orperpetuating unintended consequences to girls, and boys (and their caregivers)through the provision of specialized child protection services if service providers arenot adequately trained/ services are of low-quality. Mitigating measures: 1. Ensure training packages are in line child protection minimum standards and consider remotemanaged context 2. Services should not be provided by any staff who have not received and demonstrated proficiency in training modules. 3. Service mappingsshould only include services that have been determined to provide safe and qualityservices. 4. Establish consultation with communities and ensure feedback mechanisms are in place


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

ONSUR strongly considers gender aspects at every step of its project design andimplementation. A gender analysis was included in this project’s needs assessment. Atevery stage of the program, men and women will be included engaged, withconsideration to the “do no harm approach”, especially those that are influenced oraffected by project performance. Furthermore, ONSUR proposes by our project toengage women in decision making, particularly regarding the location of projectactivities. ONSUR will ensure rigorous follow up through monitoring and evaluation ofprojects. In addition, Rigorous M&E frameworks will be implemented to record genderdifferences in project implementation and results. Information will be disaggregatedaccording to gender and results will be shared at the cluster and community level toassist in decision-making. ONSUR will never tolerate any abuse by anyone towards awoman or a girl.


11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumptions:ONSUR has good experience by support bread, we have good access for camps and good relationship with this camp administrators, numbers of IDPs families and their details in targeted camps are known for us.- Bakeries are available, transportation is available, Furthermore, there is positive interaction with bread distribution from IDPs.-Fuel for operate bakeries are available in prohect area. Risks: 1- stopping the bakeries due to technical issue, ONSUR will contract with two high capacity and modern bakeries in project area 2- issues related to quality of bread and weight of breadMEAL team will monitor the performance of these bakeries, and follow up all complaints from bread beneficiaries3- Change the number of IDPs inside campsONSUR will update the numbers of IDPS monthly and update the beneficiaries list, if there are decreasing in numbers of beneficiaries, ONSUR will distribute the extra breads on other camps with coordination with FSL cluster, If there is increasing in numbers of beneficiaries ONSUR will try to cover them by private donors4- Covid -19 issueONSUR will strictly adhere to the cluster and WHO recommendations on prevention measures related to COVID-19 (wearing mask, social distance, increase the numbers of distribution points) during the distribution
