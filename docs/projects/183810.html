Project Summary

The proposed project is intended to complement the development interventions, most likely will be closely linked with the ongoing projects in Kordofan, the proposed activities will be linked with the WFP funded integrated project and will target vulnerable communities in Al Rahad Locality, North Kordofan State, Sudan. An estimated 10,850 persons will be reached by the proposed interventions which will aim at Increasing Access to and utilization of improved sustainable water facilities for vulnerable targeted populations in Al Rahad locality North Kordofan State.
Planned activities for drilling and installation of 6 hand pumps, upgrading of 3 HP to MWY with solar energy and hygiene awareness including C-19, will cover SSR, IDPs from South & North Kordofan and host communities who are still have gaps for water supply and water sources, these resources are far from their settlements.
This project will also aim to promote awareness among the host community, refugees & IDPs through provision of information, education and communication (IEC) materials for COVID19 prevention which will mitigate the possibility of infection amongst the community as a whole. The distribution of hygiene/sanitary kits - will be tailored to specific group needs as age/gender, face masks, hand sanitizers, soap and IEC materials will help the community. Refugees & IDPs and in particular women and children to adhere to WHO and MoH standard preventive measures to reduce their risks of transmission.

As the inadequate access to safe water supply services represents one of the great challenges faced by the local communities. 6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

IRW guided by humanitarian protection policies, will mainstream protection of served populations and ensure human rights are respected in accordance with international law and all implemented and delivered activities seek to minimize the negative impacts of services, to avoid increasing the harm or risk to vulnerable populations in conflict or natural disaster settings. The planned activities undertaken through this intervention, will ensure: I) responsive, by preventing or stopping violations or abuses of rights; ii) remedial, by ensuring that violations are rectified, including through access to justice and reparation; or iii) environment-building, by promoting respect for rights and the rule of law.The protection strategy will be achieved and promoted as guided by humanitarian actors by: I) providing basic material needs to people suffering from the humanitarian consequences of displacement, violence, etc.; ii) advocating with authorities to facilitate people’s access to basic services and livelihoods, based on humanitarian principles and the spirit of international law; and iii) ensuring that the assistance they provide does not exacerbate the risks to which people are already exposed. Moreover, protection will be a key feature within the intervention. In all the awareness session woman and girls will be sensitized about the protection issues and to reduce the risk of such incidents. For example, facilities and water points will be constructed in safe locations that minimize risks of various types of GBV. Pitching water in most part of remote areas in Sudan is the responsibility of women and young girls. However, this project will spare women an ample time and give them the opportunity to carry out other activities and participate in the development process of their communities, state and country. Hygiene promotion will be also be gender sensitive, with topics applicable for girls and for boys.

7.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will inform and interact with inter-agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including SEA and abuse complaints?

Islamic Relief has a system of handling complaints both at the country and field levels, to ensure that the complaints of the target people are properly handled and feedback on the complaints are timely provided through appropriate channels. Complaints’ phone numbers and email addresses are displayed in all sign boards. Community mobilisation activities will be conducted to make the community aware of their entitlements and mechanisms on how to report complaints. IRS engages the community during all phases of the project cycle, where IR staff continuously explains to the communities the process of complaint and feedback mechanisms. Communities are also encouraged to directly communicate with project officer if they want to reflect on any concern or issue of the project. There are identified focal points for complaints receiving in all offices of IR Sudan and they are provided with dedicated numbers to register complains from the field and provide timely response to the complainer. The Project Officer will be is the focal point.
