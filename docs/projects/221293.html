Project Summary

within the context of armed conflict (internal displacement of persons, shut down of schools, hospitals, poor sanitation, water scarcity, inferior water quality, inappropriate hygiene) and food insecurity that has to be addressed for over 437,000 people are internally displaced across the affected areas as cited by David Malan of the local Norwegian Refugees Council Office in an interview with Africa Renewal that this concept note is developed. it is hoped that the project will increase the quantity of safe drinking water for vulnerable households in the Mbengwi and Bamenda II sub divisions by the construction/rehabilitation of water catchments, water storage tanks and the extension of pipelines plus the placement of 40 stand taps in needy communities

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The project seeks -To improve upon the quality of drinking water in Mbengwi and Bamenda II subdivisions. -To reduce the rate of water borne/related diseases affecting these vulnerable populations. And -To improve upon the child protection and sexual reproductive health for teenage girls and women.This will be done through the construction of concreted water storage tanks of 2000 meter cube, the rehabilitation of water catchments and the extension of pipelines with 40 water points to needy communities in these subdivisions.
