Project Summary

The project aims at reducing the number of people in sever acute food insecurity, Protect and restore their livelihoods as well as related food and income sources and further enhance their food consumption levels and strengthen their household dietary diversion and increase their coping mechanism through provision of seasonally appropriate agricultural inputs and technical support through training in rural and peri-urban settings in Kismayo and support their household and community resilience to shocks, by rehabilitation their livelihood assets. The project targets 18000 persons (7488 women, 6912 men, 1800 boys and 1800 girls in Kismayo, Lower Juba Region, Somalia. The mentioned plight is occasioned by very fragile food security situation that has been impacted by cumulative effects of below-average rains experienced across most parts of Somalia during the 2018 Deyr (October-December), followed by harsh weather conditions during the dry 2019 Jilaal (January–March) season and the delayed and poor performance of the 2019 Gu (April-June). These consecutive seasonal poor performances have been exacerbated by limited recovery from the extended impacts of the severe 2016/2017 drought whose impacts are still prevalent.

Project Activities

1. Provision of food vouchers, conditional and unconditional cash transfers, as well as conditional Transfers to most vulnerable 1200 families and displaced women and children, headed Households and those with chronic illnesses and the disabled in Kismayo to increase their food consumption and Household Dietary Diversity2. Rehabilitation of 6 water catchments, farming lands/rangelands, canals and 6 feeder roads in the targeted locations in Kismayo on cash for work scheme to benefit 3600 IDPs, returnees and vulnerable marginalized host communities to increase their coping mechanisms as well as their food consumption and Household Dietary Diversity3. Provision of hay/fodder to the most vulnerable and drought-affected 1500 pastoral families in Kismayo 4. Provision of emergency veterinary services (animal vaccination and treatment) to 1200 most vulnerable and displaced pastoral families in Kismayo 5. Distribution of livelihoods inputs livestock, fishers and agriculture/farm inputs (seed package) to the 1200 most vulnerable and displaced farming households in Kismayo
