Project Summary

This project will provide protection services for the most critically vulnerable Lebanese population, including women, men, girls, and boys, working both to prevent protection violations as well as respond to needs. It will address GBV needs through provision of services including psychosocial support and case management. It will also address child protection needs through protection case management, focused and non-focused psychosocial support, and recreational activities. The project will address legal needs for GBV and CP cases through provision of legal services, including legal counselling and payment of legal fees, for individuals both in and out of detention through partner organization, MSD.
The project will operate through Tabitha-run community centers in Batroun, North Gov.; Aley, Mount Lebanon (3 centers in Aley city, Choueifat and Bayssour) in addition Chouf through the intervention in the SDC of Chehime; Achrafieh, Beirut ; and through the mobile unit in the Bekaa Valley in addition to greater Beirut area (Metn and Baabda) that Tabitha is planning to target through the proposal submitted to OCHA lately. These centers and mobile unit contain Tabitha’s community outreach workers, a psychologist, and social workers who maintain a presence within targeted communities. Cases taken up by these social workers may come from multiple sources- referrals from other NGOs and CSOs, cases identified by community outreach workers, cases identified through individuals or family members already participating in Tabitha’s community center protection services, etc. Tabitha also partners with another Lebanese NGO, Migration Services and Development (MSD), that specializes in legal services and has access to individuals in detention facilities. MSD lawyers also maintain designated offices within Tabitha community centers.
Tabitha will also coordinate its work with the stakeholders of each area, for Achrafieh and Greater Beirut, Tabitha will coordinate with Makhzoumi & TDH Lausanne following the recommendation of the sector.
Case Management will be implemented through the use of the social work methodology including individual vulnerability/needs assessments (through interviews and home visits when possible), a network of outreach workers (selected and trained among vulnerable refugees/host communities) to facilitate trust and access to isolated groups on a timely and safe manner. Tabitha follows the national protection working groups, all the team is trained on the usage of the national SOP for GBV and CP, using the recognized referrals pathways to ensure cases that require specialized follow-up are referred to be assessed, specialized service providers. MHPSS services will be made available for those attending programs at the community centers in all the locations- this includes the elderly, children, and those who have experienced severe trauma and abuse. A licensed psychologist is present in all locations and animators/facilitators are trained to facilitate group PSS sessions, especially for children. The legal assistance program operates using a holistic approach, whereas both lawyers & social workers provide services to those in need of assistance. Beneficiaries are referred to or approach Tabitha community centers for issues related to civil documentation or access to justice. Services provided include: support with employer or family issues such as divorce; child registration and birth registration; marriage certificates; documentation for the continuity of education; renewal of documentation to stay in Lebanon; or organization of documentation to return to Syria. As the beneficiaries for the legal program are vulnerable conflict affected households, often cases of GBV and CP, cases are referred from social workers either within Tabitha, or from the referral pathway system established in country. With homelessness rising, legal support is also needed for rental and eviction, another service of MSD.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

Under objective 1, Survivors and individuals at risk have access to quality specialized GBV services and measures are in place to prevent, mitigate, and respond to GBV, the project will provide non-focused PSS services. In order to respond to GBV cases, Tabitha will provide focused PSS, and case management and legal services. Under child protection (objective 2), Tabitha will provide case management services, focused and non-focused PSS, and will engage with communities to raise awareness on CP related issues as well as recreational activities. All of the proposed activities below will take place in the different centers that Tabitha runs whether in Aley, Chouf, Beirut, or Batroun community centers or via mobile teams as needed in addition to Metn and Baabda as suggested in OCHA recently submitted proposal. Tabitha will establish a community based mechanism to reach the most vulnerable, isolated and hard-to-reach children in the communities and specific attention will be given to (a) children with disabilities, (b) children engaged in worst forms of child labour, (c) children in conflict or in contact with the law and (d) children survivors of physical and sexual abuse and who are in need of protection (e) children at risk of not attending school, undocumented children, etc. Through the referral map of interagency coordination, outreach modality, and community based volunteers, and the case management team Tabitha will work to get access to the above mentioned groups.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

This project will base the intervention on the national SOPs using vulnerability/needs assessments (through interviews and home visits), a network of outreach workers (selected among vulnerable refugees/host communities to facilitate trust building and access to isolated groups). Tabitha mainstreams protection in all its activities which allows maximization of the protective impact of the project through ensuring that the activities target the most vulnerable and enhance safety, dignity, and promote and protect the human rights of beneficiaries without contributing to or perpetuating discrimination, abuse, violence, neglect and exploitation. . Activities create a space for discussion and peer mentoring which allows different groups to reflect on their similarities rather than differences. Project activities are designed to include any critically vulnerable individual within the population, regardless of their gender identity, disability, race, religion, or socioeconomic status.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

Accountability towards the served population and stakeholders is key to Tabitha’s humanitarian approach. Tabitha has already well established feedback and complaint mechanisms, this mechanism will be established at each site so children, adults, and anyone else who wants to is able to share their opinion, feedback and/or complaints. Tabitha builds its programming based on feedback and the needs of the community; this is achieved utilizing Tabitha's community centers which are well-established, known, and trusted within each community. Tabitha has successfully participated in a series of coaching/mentoring sessions on PSEA & safeguarding integration done by ABAAD in partnership with OCHA, LHF and the Lebanese National PSEA Network.
