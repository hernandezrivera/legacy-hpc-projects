Project Summary

The North West Region of Cameroon, over the years has had series of challenges in the educational system. Low literacy rates, restricted access to high-quality education, and lack of trained teachers especially in rural areas. The socio political crisis, worsen these situation with the closure of Schools, high displacement rates, and limit ed educational resources. Many children are bound to perform poorly due to this surrounding issues, numerous ghost towns and limited number of teachers who have little or time to properly follow up children performance in school. The project intends to tackle the problem of low literacy rates among Cameroonian primary and secondary school pupils in Mezam and Bui division in the North West. The project's main goals are to give teachers training, access to high-quality literacy resources and tools, and evidence-based literacy instruction. About 1,000 pupils and 50 instructors will be reached by the project, which will be conducted in 20 primary schools in the area.
The project's goals and objectives are in line with the Sustainable Development Goal 4 (SDG 4): Quality Education of the UN and the Cameroon government's National Education Strategy. Improved student performance on literacy tests, more teacher competency and confidence in literacy education, and easier access to high-quality reading tools and materials are all anticipated results of the initiative.
