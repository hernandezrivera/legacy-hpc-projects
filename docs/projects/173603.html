Project Summary

The Water, Sanitation and Hygiene (WASH) Sector will support the crisis-affected population, which includes conflict-affected people currently living in the IDPs and returnees in the Localities of Bendisi and some of the Jebel Marra areas, people at risk of epidemics, and other vulnerable residents to maintain and gain access to WASH services using multisector approaches. The WASH sector is addressing the urgent and demanding needs of the people and localities mentioned above. The targeted localities will receive hygiene promotion messages, focusing on handwashing with soap through household visits and campaigns, construction of emergency latrines in the camps, and provision of clean drinking water through rehabilitation of broken hand pumps in and around new settlements and new hand pumps in the camps and areas of returnee’s settlement
This project is designed based on Central Darfur State WASH sector 3Ws information and assessment results in targeted localities, which shows that, targeted localities that has a high priority and been suggested for WASH intervention because there is an increase of IDPs due to continue confilect in Jabal between SLA & returnees in Umdukhun and Bindis and this led to the pressure on capacity of available improved water sources. People still collect water from wadi and hand dug wells which are not safe for both drinking, cooking, and personal hygiene. Poor personal hygiene practices are observed among both children and adults, there are broken and dirty water containers, zero sold waste management, garbage, trash, animal dung, children feces all are been disposed in an open environment such as around houses and open water points such as wadi. Open defection is practiced in the localities. Furthermore, limited availability of school latrines, with many schools not having any latrines and other coeducation schools are without segregated latrines for both boys and girls, there is also lack of school WASH facilities that are accessible for children and teacher with disabilities, there is lack of awareness on how girls can manage their menstrual hygiene in school. Therefore, JMCO proposed this intervention to increase access to safe water, as well as improve hygiene and sanitation practices of Returnees and host communities including IDPs in targeted localities through provision and improvement of WASH facilities in schools, rehabilitation of nonfunctional water sources, increasing sanitation coverage, as well as general hygiene and sanitation promotion, These are considered as the most priorities for intervention, this project targets the communities with high needs of WASH service, hygiene and sanitation

1 - Needs

Jabal Marra Charity Organization for Rural Development (JMCO) has been actively supporting people who are affected by the continuous armed crashes and displacement from the Jabal Marra region. This is first time that JMCO submitting direct project to work for WASH project, JMCO has been active partner for UNICEF in WASH , Education and Child Protection sectors. The main focus of this project is to support the displaced population in both Central Darfur (Bindis , Rokero , Um Dukhun , Golo and Zalingei localities) by providing sanitation and water services. The key target population of this project is, however, not only the people displaced by Jabal Marra conflicts. In Central Darfur, the main focus is on the returnees from camps and neighboring countries and host communities. This project aims to increase and improve the access to water, sanitation and hygiene to 100 communities in the targeted localities of Central Darfur and 20 communities per each locality. The targeted beneficiaries are 64,218 including returnees and host communities including IDPs. The activities proposed include improving access to the quantity and quality of water through the rehabilitation of 10 hand pumps, installation of solar operated submersible system to solve the issue of water and water-related diseases, all people will have safe and equitable access to a sufficient quality of water for drinking, cooking, personal and domestic hygiene, construction of 20 segregated school latrine for both boys and girls, with gender consideration to help girls stay at school rather than going home, there will be provision of WASH facilities for washing hand in schools. In addition, there will be provisions of water tanks well installs with taps for accessible for both children and teachers with disability, conducting menstrual hygiene education awareness through coordination with MoH at locality level to help the adolescent girls in school able to manage their menstrual hygiene, conducting CLTS in 100 communities, conducting sanitation mapping for communities in 5 localities of Central Darfur, training of CLTS facilitators in 5 localities, support SMOH in the establishment of sanitation units at locality levels, and training of hand pump mechanics , including formation and establishment of WASH information management system at state level and locality level to grantee WASH information update on monthly basis
