Project Summary

The intervention focuses on supporting conflict-affected Syrians in North West Syria by implementing Livelihoods and Rehabilitation (ERL) initiatives. These efforts aim to enhance the resilience of the community by providing sustainable livelihood opportunities and rehabilitating essential infrastructure, such as water systems and communal facilities. The target beneficiaries include vulnerable populations, such as displaced individuals and host communities, with the goal of promoting stability, self-reliance, and long-term socio-economic empowerment in the region. Overall, the project seeks to help these communities withstand and recover from the impact of conflict, ultimately fostering a more resilient and sustainable future.
Provide skills training workshops and vocational programs tailored to the identified needs and capacities of beneficiaries, with a specific focus on adolescents and youth.
Facilitate access to resources and tools necessary for income-generating activities, such as small business grants or microfinance support, prioritizing youth-led initiatives.
Offer mentorship and coaching to support adolescents and youth in establishing and managing their livelihood ventures effectively.
Monitor and evaluate the impact of livelihood interventions on beneficiaries' economic well-being and resilience, with a particular emphasis on assessing the involvement and empowerment of adolescents and youth.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The objective of the intervention is to bolster the resilience of conflict-affected Syrians in Northwest Syria through Livelihoods and Rehabilitation (ERL) interventions. Specifically, the project aims to provide sustainable livelihood opportunities and rehabilitate essential infrastructure to enhance the community's ability to withstand and recover from the impact of conflict. The intervention targets vulnerable populations, including (IDPs) and host communities, residing in conflict-affected areas of Northwest Syria.
