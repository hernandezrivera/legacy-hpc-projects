Project Summary

The project is an economic empowerment for youth, targeting 2000 person (1000:M – 1000: F) between 18 – 40 years in four sub-districts: (Dana, Maar Tamsarin, Al-Bab, and Afrin ).
The starting point of the project will be Labour market assessment and youth needs assessment: We will conduct contextual analysis on the structures and systems that shape adolescent and youth’s economic opportunities and constraints, identify deprived youths’ rights violations and their speciﬁc deprivations and needs in northwest of Syria, to inform the specific project design.
The project consists of four main components:
- The First component is delivering capacity building and vocational training for 1000 Syrian youth in targeted locations. Through these vocational, we will facilitate and enhance youth opportunities to get the necessary skills to be involved in the market. Vocational and income training that MWL is proposing include: Computer Programming Techniques, Computer maintenance, Cell Phone maintenance, Administration assistance, Painting, Skincare and makeup, Sewing.
- Second component is Entrepreneurship where youth entrepreneurs club will targeting groups of 500 youth in the 4 targeted sub-districts, who have business ideas, or recent projects needing to develop. The club will offer practical training in various aspects of entrepreneurship, During the club's activities and workshops, the participants will design and develop their own project plans. Every circle period of a club, a Demo Day will conduct in the presence of a jury of trainers who provided training workshops, and experts in entrepreneurship, Winning projects will be selected for each round of the project. A financial grant will be given to the owners to help them start the project.
- Third component is an internship program for building the capacities in targeted locations for 300 youth who graduated recently or in the final year. Where we will conduct agreements with some institutions, and organizations, to host and train groups of graduates, undergraduates and postgraduates’ students in their offices, and business. The process of internship program will be by distribution of trainees to the departments in the institutions and firms according to their specialtiesو with the appointment of a supervisor from every division directly to each trainee to provide him with guidance, training and mentorship and asking him to perform some tasks. MWL will cover the costs of internal training, in addition to offer some incentives for participants (Volunteer allowance) to encourage them to be committed for attending the internship program. The internship period will be around 2-3 months, it will be completed with evaluation report for every participant and certificate, that he completed and achieved the internship program objectives with providing the participants with recommendation letter for facilitate their employment in the future. Moreover, we will conduct some training courses for those internship program’s participants. These courses will be about teamwork, administrative skills, soft skills like communication skills. These training will enhance the capacities and skills for the participants.
- Fourth component will be a training program for 200 adolescents (in secondary schools), on discovering their professional leanings and talents, and selection academic and vocational specialization. This will serve the objective of this component to prepare youth to succeed in their academic and professional life.
- Fifth component is launching social initiatives by the trainees to enhance the social cohesion and activate the role of youth and adolescents in public affairs.

4 - Needs

MWL as a leader of Youth and adolescents Task-force, have designed needs assessment questionnaire targeting youth and adolescents inside Syria includes the main aspects related to youth and adolescents between (18-40) year, which was conducted in four sub-districts(Dana,Maar Tamsarin, Al-Bab, and Afrin ). This questionnaire sent to the members of CP cluster to apply with youth & adolescents inside Syria from local and host communities to measure their needs, social problems,interests in different areas, in terms of social participation in public affairs, and their feedback about the programs that aiming at empowering them. Where the process aims at measuring the opinions of youth and adolescents towards the protection and empowerment activities that NGOs have conducted with target groups during the Syrian crisis.1222 questionnaire were collected in the targeted subdistricts.MWL have analyzed the data and prepare the report of findings.The need assessment mentioned the high gaps in the youth needs where 81 % of the respondents indicated that they aren’t received any training in campaign management and community initiatives, 72 % of them have the desire to launch volunteer initiatives to serve the community, and regarding the youth male/female participation in the community life, (85%, indicated that the participation is middle.the most problems that have faced the youth were (the weakness of the work opportunities, and security situation), the most important problems that the community suffering were lack of adequate employment opportunities, continuing conflicts, and lack of encouragement and involvement of young people.Crises in Syria have been accompanied by the exacerbation of existing harmful cultural norms, increasing the vulnerability of adolescent girls to gender-based violence such as harassment, sexual violence and child marriage.The rate of youth unemployment in the region is one of the highest in the world, especially regarding female youth unemployment.

5 - Implementation of activities

The project activities will be designed in consultation with the targeted youth groups through focus group discussion sessions, KI interviews and questionnaires to determine the inputs and outputs of the project from beneficiaries.Following the assessment, MWL will be working on designing the training materials, the selection criteria for trainers, and training participants.The project will create a sustainable environment supportive of entrepreneurship by establishing entrepreneurial clubs, connecting young entrepreneurs in the targeted areas, and enhancing communication between service providers, suppliers, and entrepreneurs.During the training, the trainers will provide the trainees with the opportunities to apply what they have learned during the training theoretical sessions, through practical internship sessions, In addition, MWL will be conducting small workshops with training participants on how to set up their own small business and how to contact with labor stakeholders to get a job. opportunity. To connect those trainees with the labor market stakeholders.The internship activities will provide graduates, university students and skilled youth to gain practical experience, sustainable jobs, and strong market access through institutions, organizations, factories, enterprises, and professional workshops that they will be distributed to.The project will stimulate and support youth and adolescent groups trained to design and implement social and economic initiatives and Lifeskills will be provided to the project's participants about teamwork and soft skills.launching social initiatives by the trainees to enhance the social cohesion and activate the role of youth and adolescents in public affairs.All project interventions: Vocational training - Entrepreneurship training - Internship are aiming at empower Syrian youth in the economic field according to their interests
