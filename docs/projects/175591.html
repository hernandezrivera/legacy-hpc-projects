Project Summary

The project plans to assist the most vulnerable population displaced by violence and host communities in Cabo Delgado province through actions aimed to recover their agricultural capacities looking to ensure their food security livelihoods trough the provision of seeds and agricultural tools in Metuge, Pemba, Montepuez and Ancuabe districts. 1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project aims to contribute to the food security /livelihoods care of 6000 families in need in the districts that have received internally displaced persons (IDPs) displaced by violence. Districts of Metuge, Ancuabe, Montepuez, Chiure, Balama and Namuno. The objectives of the project are focused on several core activities such as the distribution of seeds and agricultural tools, considering two types of seed distributions: one very urgent for a first planting immediately in January to support to vulnerable food insecure population, and two more following the established planting seasons. In addition, we will have the coordination of the district directions of agriculture, so that the distributions and the work of supervision with the needy families will always be done in the company of extensionists, allowing us to travel to the different communities. The distribution of agricultural tools: this would be carried out in a general way after a census has been taken in the camps for displaced persons and also in the host communities that have received displaced persons, for which it is necessary to coordinate with community leaders, the INGC, WFP and FAO, with the agricultural directorates, and where appropriate with the CCCM. Also, there are various activities related to the rehabilitation of infrastructure improvements, small warehouses silos, to improve storage conditions and management of crops throughout the year.Metuge district : 588 HH – (2.940 Individuals) Montepuez District : : 588 HH – (2.940 Individuals)Ancuabe District: : 466 HH – ( 2.328 Individuals)Chiure District: : 465 HH – (2.327 Individuals)Balama district: : 417 HH – (2.083 Individuals) Namuno district: : 416 HH – (2.082 Individuals)Direct Target 14.700 individuals. // Indirect target 40.000

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The joint and coordinated work between local authorities, Cluster Leading international Agencies (UN) and the beneficiaries themselves is essential for a successful, flexible and continuously developing risk analysis and management and screening it. For this reason, will be carried out a previous identification of potential risks , which will also include a risk and management analysis , through the implementation of a collaborative coordinated work system between the beneficiaries, district and provincial authorities and with the different clusters involved in the project (CCCM, Food security Cluster (WFP), Protection Cluster (UNICEF)). These results will be contrasted with the previous risk analyses identified by the Mozambican authorities in charge for the assistance to the displaced people (INGC), as well as the district administrators. Above all, it will be taken maximun precautions to ensure the safety of the women and prevent any kind of harassment or assault. The empowerment of people will be one of the crosscutting goals that the project aims through recovery of agricultural skills, and involvement of people within the activities themselves, making them part of the decision making process, and not just recipients of the activities, making people the protagonists of their own well-being.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

At the beginning of the project, in addition with the baseline of food security/livelihoods needs, it will be conducted an initial survey to gather beneficiaries needs and expectations, and agreed them on the activities planned and ensure that their approval some crucial points such us where to organize the distributions, activities, etc. Project activist and extensionists will use the regular monitoring visits to maintain an ongoing dialogue with the beneficiaries and inform about the results and coming activities. Quarterly meetings will be held with local leaders and communities in each planned place, to evaluate together the progress of the project and collect complaints, suggestions, and comments. At this time it is essential to take advantage of the fact that many displaced people arrive at the resettlement camps previously organized in communities, which means that exist a real possibility of talking with local leaders and involving them in the dialogue with the beneficiaries. The project will take advantage of this circumstance to facilitate the management of accountability in conjunction with the reception of individual complaints collected by the activists during their family visits. Finally, will be carried a second survey to analyze and communicate the project results, measures taken, gaps and lessons learnt.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

People with disabilities will be located and incorporated into the activities as priority beneficiaries through the implementation of a baseline census of the beneficiaries at the beginnig of the project conducted among project promoters , community leaders, and the collaboration of other national and international partners directly related to the work with vulnerable people. will be placed an special emphasis on the active incorporation of these persons in the activities, being prioritized to receive livelihoods items (tools, seeds) and join the promotion activities. At the same time, will be developed a map of existing barriers to identify and eliminate these.Finally, will be carried out a permanent screening of people with disabilities and special vulnerability during the visits of the promoters to the families and with the collaboration of community leaders.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Respect for the environment will be ensured throughout the project, avoiding any type of environmental pollution, especially regarding with the use of pesticides, forest degradation, and the contamination of aquifers. The planting, cultivation and harvesting of crops will be monitored by the district's agricultural authorities to ensure work that preserves the environment and does not create unnecessary damage to crops by introducing crops that are unsuitable, prone to pests, not very resistant to climatic changes or that worsen the quality of the soil. It will be included in all health promotion activities as a cross-cutting element of environmental conservation and environmental health, highlighting the impact that environmental degradation has on people, and why it is so important to avoid deforestation, preserve water sources and what measures can be taken to respect in daily life.
