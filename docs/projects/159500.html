Project Summary

Save the Children (SC) intends to propose sustainable solutions and expand the provision of life-saving WASH services to drought, flood, AWD and conflict affected populations in target regions in Somalia through the provision of access to both sustainable and life-saving water, sanitation and hygiene services in target communities, schools and health/nutrition facilities. The proposed project is also designed to ensure equitable access to WASH services considering people with disabilities and the elderly, safety of children as well as dignity and privacy for women and girls. Coordination will be maintained with relevant clusters including Health, Nutrition, Education, Protection and WASH actors at national and state levels. The project will also contribute to the resilience and life-saving approaches of the target community and health systems ensuring participation of beneficiaries in the project cycle.
In terms of centrality and mainstreaming of protection, the project targets to reduce the risk of violence against women, children and vulnerable groups while accessing water and sanitation facilities by providing gender segregated facilities and establishing the facilities within 500 meters’ distance of settlements while also providing solar lights for latrines for safe access at night. The project will also address gender issues by collecting gender and age disaggregated data. SC will analyze this data to identify gaps, provide gender sensitive interventions and ensure participation and empowerment of women. SC will ensure protection during access and utilization of services, to reduce risks further.
Accountability to affected populations will be ensured by involving communities, information sharing and engaging them to participate in the complaints and feedback system.
The proposed intervention also fits under the WASH sector specific objective to provide life-saving and also restore sustainable access to WASH services while also reducing the violence against women when accessing services for both IDPs as well as affected populations.
The proposed project activities will include construction of strategic water sources, pipeline extensions and distribution systems, provision of operation and maintenance tools, construction/rehabilitation of sanitation facilities in the target villages, IDPs, schools and health/nutrition facilities, trainings and dissemination on hygiene best practices through IEC and distribution of hygiene kits.

Mitigation Measures

SC will avoid community tensions between displaced and host communities by providing the local/host community with access to services and assistance in situations where they are experiencing similar shortages of water and sanitation services. SC will consult both displaced and host communities about water and sanitation needs and assess whether assistance could cause tension, harassment or conflict between communities. The organization will engage both groups during community mobilization, project sensitization and will ensure that all groups are represented in any project structures. SC will use vulnerability criteria to enroll access of people to the services, which will be made very clear during community engagement. Communities will be given sufficient information about the project including how they can access, selection criteria, selection process and entitlements. SC will sensitize communities on how best they can provide their feedback about the progam and therefore establish safe and accessible CRM. Beneficiary data will be segregated sex and gender but will keep lists of beneficiaries and personal data confidential and safe from unintended use - apply effective data protection measures. Work in coordination with protection actors to put in place measures to prevent and respond to physical/sexual violence arising from services provided. SC will also work closely with the child protection department to sensitize and train communities and local community structures including project committees on issues around child protection and child safeguarding. Furthermore, SC has Child safeguarding, child protection, fraud, anti-bribery and anti-terror financing policy as well sexual harassment policy, therefore, the organization will train all its staff (current staff are already well trained), partners and suppliers. SC has clear internal procedures of reporting of such cases, however, SC will provide clear, safe and accessible mechanism of reporting in any of such cases. Land tenancy is a major issue and IDPs are repeatedly affected by evections and humanitarian partners denied permission to construct WASH facilities as the land is not owned by the IDPs. SC will work with local authorities and community leaders to allow humanitarian partners to provide WASH facilities in the target settlement areas by creating dialogue for communities hosting IDPs. In this very fragile and dynamic context in Somalia. IDPs and minority groups are also marginalized. Clans who believe are better and more powerful take the lions share if not all of the resources. Host communities or gatekeepers in the IDP camps may sometimes manipulate IDPs. In both IDPs and host communities, families headed by categorically vulnerable and weak individuals including the elderly, children, women and disabled are marginalized and their rights are taken by so called more physical and/or societally powerful. The project will deliberately prevent risk of exclusion of most vulnerable and marginalized groups (based on gender, age, minority clans, IDPs, returnees, persons with disabilities, etc) by taking the following:• Awareness raising and sensitization: SC will start sensitizing stakeholders including community leaders and local authorities on the objective, processes and targets of the project. The organization will than meet people in target communities in a public places where we will ensure all those who can attend come to the meeting. In these meetings, information about the project including objective, entitlements, duration, targeting criteria, targeting approach will be shared. • Project relief committee: after sensitization, communities will be asked to establish project committees. To do this, communities will be asked to establish them in public and endorse the committee in public. The committee will represent all different groups and age brackets (excluding children but youth), and any other groups including women and minority. SC will sign an M

Please provide further explanation

Due to the protracted nature of displacement in Somalia, past assistance to IDPs has mainly focused on sustaining humanitarian responses with limited efforts in supporting IDPs to become self-reliant. Hence the proposed WASH response will go beyond provision of access to safe water supply and restoring of WASH infrastructure and services, and should strive for a change or transformation, which will prepare the affected communities to face future hazards. Water, sanitation and hygiene as a critical need and priority during the emergency, early recovery and longer term recovery efforts in Somalia. The recovery measures to be undertaken by the project will be creating of sustainable WASH facilities as well as community and institutional capacity building. The project is designed in such a way that while providing emergency lifesaving interventions, it will also provide longer term sustainable solutions for affected populations. To ensure the emergency interventions are linked to durable solutions, the project includes construction, rehabilitation and upgrading of water supply systems and promotion of community based sanitation interventions which will be provided to the affected populations with durable solutions and increase resilience of the communities to mitigate the impact of future shocks.
