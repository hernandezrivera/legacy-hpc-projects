Project Summary

This project is designed to provide integrated intervention in education and protection and increase safe and equitable availability and access to in-person and home-based formal and nonformal education within project's period, 2023 Binaa aims to target (7200 students, aged 5 - 17 years) crisis-affected children and youth (3593 girls, 3607 boys including 258 PwDs ) in 4 schools in Ma'arrat Tamasrin and Aghtrin community) in addition to 6 schools in 8 targeted camps including 4 new established camps as follow: (1 camp in Haranbush,1 camp in Salqin),(1 camp in bhorta in Aghtrin) and (1 camp in Bazagha) addition to Yasin camp and Omran Camp which were identified by Binaa rapid need assessment as the nearby targeted camp in Harnabush with no access to education services which can be provided with education services through the school in Harbabush camp, as well as two camps Besnaya and Alqima camps which Binaa has 2 established schools there but they have no secured support for the ongoing academic year.
the project will target two grades, the first grade from 5 to 12 yo, and the second grade from 12 to 17 yo.
Through support the provision of formal education to 4359 children (using The Interim Government curriculum, according to the existed educational approach), and Providing 2841 children with non-formal education programs including remedial classes, Accelerated Learning Programs (ALP), catching up classes, literacy and numeracy classes. Conduct 4 Back-to-Learning (BTL) campaigns in targeted communities.
Providing students with catch up classes will support them and increase their ability to move to formal education by helping them to raise their education capabilities and Compensates the educational gaps using the curriculum B, there will be diagnostic tests to check the level of students at the end of each cycle, and verify that they will be able to enroll the formal education.
Disinfection of 10 schools and learning spaces, where the schools will be held in two shifts , taking into account surfaces disinfection between shifts as Covid-19 mitigation measures, in addition, Providing personal protective equipment (PEE) to (students,
teachers and education personnel)to mitigate the risk of Covid-19.
Establishment, and rehabilitation of 100 classrooms (in line with the dignified shelterو some of them will be constructed with concrete and others will be constructed with sandwich panels). Providing 5 schools with school furniture, including desks, chairs, and blackboards. Improve and construct gender-sensitive and disability-sensitive WASH facilities for 10 schools to provide an adequate environment for
7200 children. Providing 10 schools with safety and security equipment and systems, Providing maintenance and running costs for 10 schools. Providing 225 teachers with teaching materials in formal and non-formal settings. Providing 600 children with school transportation support (for students who are in nearby camps and CwDs).
Provide 350 children with school-based psychosocial support and 300 children with life skills programs
The project aims to enhance the quality of formal and non-formal education for children and youth through providing professional development to 150 students.
personnel, including psychosocial support, subject knowledge, pedagogical skills, classroom management, positive discipline, life skills, technical and vocational training, life skills and citizenship education, remote teaching pedagogy, education in emergencies (EiE), national/INEE (Inter-Agency Network for Education in Emergencies) minimum standards, Covid-19 related knowledge, codes of conduct and anti-bullying, mine risk education,
safety/security protocols. Provide teachers and education personnel with incentives and teaching resources.
BINAA will take into consideration all the needed procedures to harmonize the participation of the CWD in all the project's activities.
BINAA will conduct cholera awareness campaigns in all targeted schools.
