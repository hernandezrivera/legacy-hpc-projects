1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project seeks to uphold the basic human rights of refugees and asylum seekers living in Mozambique, ensuring access to basic services, and paving the way for safe and dignified access to durable solutions. In 2022, UNHCR will maintain and strengthen its leadership role in managing the protracted refugee situation. In order to achieve this, asylum procedures need to be strengthened, including individual registration, the issuance and renewal of identity cards and naturalization procedures. UNHCR will support refugee communities in the prevention and response to GBV and SEA and offer quality child protection services - including those provided increasingly by the authorities - as well as supporting refugee youth, women, the elderly and people with disabilities. This project will also support the transition towards local integration through livelihood opportunities that enhance self-reliance, while providing support to individual repatriation requests and enhancing the capacity of the authorities to conduct registration, issue individual documentation, manage mixed movements and maintain the asylum space. UNHCR will continue to provide quality education to camp-based refugee children, including through the Vodafone Foundation-funded INS Programme, as well as primary health care for the camp population. Therefore, UNHCR with its partners will undertake: 1) Registration and documentation of asylum-seekers and refugees, including urban refugees; 2) Protection services for persons with specific needs, including support to persons with disabilities provision of case management, psychosocial support, legal assistance and resettlement; 3) Promotion of livelihood opportunities to increase self-reliance and support local integration, identification of durable solutions for Maratane residents; 4) Ensure access of Maratane residents and host community to basic services including education, WASH, health care and shelter.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

With the objective to prevent, mitigate and address the protection risks of affected populations in priority areas as well as provide multi-sectoral assistance and empower refugees, asylum seekers and host community through livelihood and self-reliance, the refugee response will have the following activities: 1) Community outreach to refugees and asylum-seekers ensuring they are registered and that two-way communication between them and UNHCR/partners ensures protection risks are monitored and necessary support provided;2) Support in documentation and legal assistance of refugee and asylum seekers including mapping exercises as-needed to study documentation needs; 3) Protection monitoring to support access to services, identification of protection gaps and incidents, advocacy with Government for refugees inclusion in national services;4) Protection services for persons with specific needs, including persons with disabilities: UNHCR will undertake case management of refugees and asylum seekers with specific needs , including GBV survivors and children at heightened protection risk, and will provide specialized services such as psychosocial support, legal assistance;5) Develop livelihood opportunities: UNHCR will conduct life-skills and vocational training to enhance self-reliance of refugees in Maratane and support local integration while facilitating the host community’s access to livelihoods, education, and health care envisioning a cash-based component;6) Solutions: Support voluntary repatriation upon request alongside resettlement and complementary pathways for relevant profiles and local settlement (including naturalization) for those who may opt to reside in the country; 7) Enhance accountability to affected populations (AAP): awareness-raising & training activities for community structures, community representatives, women, girls, men and boys of concern as well as support to existing complaints and feedback mechanisms (CFM), including LV (Linha Verde, the inter-agency humanitarian hotline) and suggestion boxes, while developing new channels to foster the communication with groups of different age, gender and diverse background.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

UNHCR’s workforce and partners are trained on humanitarian principles and the key elements of the centrality of protection: do no harm principle, safety and dignity, effective access to services and assistance, accountability to affected populations and community participation and empowerment. UNHCR’s focus is on the quality of protection services. In line with its AGD policy, UNHCR’s GBV policy, the Best Interests Procedure (BIP) guidance and guidance on case management, UNHCR and partners will ensure the respect for culture and religion, a survivor-centered approach, the best interest of children and the respect of the individuals. UNHCR has developed SOPs for humanitarian assistance and has adopted vulnerability targeting criteria based on the specific needs of individuals. UNHCR also uses a global tool for classifying the level of risk of individuals (Heightened Risk Identification Tool) to assess the level of risks according to which protection and assistance services are provided. Capacity building will be provided to implementing partners in order to create awareness and ensure understanding and implementation of protection standards. Referral mechanisms and pathways will be strengthened by ensuring that persons of concerns have access to services and are aware of the different options and lines to follow when intending to access them. UNHCR plans to undertake participatory assessments with all the stakeholders in Maratane. This exercise will be implemented using the AGD approach in view of including all groups. Through the participatory assessment, ideas, aspirations and wishes of the persons of concern will be included into UNHCR programming. Through focus group discussions and meetings with community leaders, feedbacks on concerns made by persons of concern will be shared.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

UNHCR with partners plans to provide targeted assistance based on needs, taking into account vulnerability criteria and the new standard specific needs codes in order to fill gaps in assistance while continuing to work on disability inclusion – thus strengthening relationships with local disability actors, and strengthening their capacity where possible. UNHCR registration data and protection assessments include disaggregated data on persons with disabilities. UNHCR also conducts specific consultations with persons with disabilities in order to assess their protection needs, and their challenges in the community. Home visits conducted by the implementing partners motivators as well as strenghtening the capacity of the implementing partner in specific needs case management will serve to improve the identification of PSN in the communities. UNHCR will continue work in mainstreaming protection in all the sectors of humanitarian assistance in Maratane. Capacity building sessions will be conducted to partners as well as the beneficiaries to improve the protection situation of the settlement and ensure persons with specific needs and persons with disability more specifically have access to humanitarian assistance.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

As part of its community-based protection strategy, UNHCR ensures that the refugee and asylum seeker communities are sensitised and engaged on the protection of the environment, emphasizing their responsibility for the local environment where they settle. As part of the community initiatives that will be supported in 2022, UNHCR will consider joint eco-friendly projects with refugees, asylum seekers, and host communities.Additional efforts to sensitize and promote green practices among the communities will be implemented throughout all interventions. UNHCR will improve the overall living conditions in the settlement by supporting waste management interventions and the use and promotion of climate-resilient agricultural practices, such as the use of biochar to support water retention in the soil. For the waste management component, UNHCR will take informed decisions based on an assessment concluded at the end of 2021. This assessment will provide qualitative and quantitative data on the waste produce in Maratane. The results will provide a solid basis to plan and implement interventions in the settlement and surrounding communities that promote the overall community environmental wellbeing.Livelihood interventions focused on capacity building and transfer of technology will ensure that the persons of concerned engaged in agricultural activities have access to technical training and extension services to increase productivity in rain-fed crops. UNHCR will also engage artisans in Maratane to make use of dry waste to produce products and generate income.Moreover, the National Energy Department programme “Energy for all”, financed by the African Development Bank (AfdB), will target Maratane and its host communities, providing adequate power stations and cabling to enable access to energy, which shall have substantive impact in using less wood charcoal.
