Project Summary

In a bit to ameliorate on the deplorable living conditions of affected populations of the NW/SW Regions of Cameroon, COHESODEC in the implementation of this project hopes to intervene in three-(3) vulnerable divisions i.e Mezam, Momo-(NWR) and Meme division in the South-West Region. Vulnerable communities identified as beneficiaries of this WASH sector project in 2022 are found within the following target sub-divisions: Bafut, Bali, Bamenda 1, Bamenda 2, Bamenda 3, Santa, Tubah, Batibo, Mbengwi, Konye, Kumba 2, Kumba 3 and Mbonge. It is worth noting that this project seeks to directly affect the lives of 45,600 vulnerable people by ensuring they have access to WASH services and Needs which will for a long time significantly improve on their lives and social wellbeing regarding Water,Sanitation and Hygiene.
Construction and rehabilitation of 20 water points (solar powered boreholes) to enable some 10,000 persons in these target vulnerable communities have access to constructed and rehabilitated water points. The project will also be ensuring that 5 of the 20 solar powered boreholes are constructed in health centers to enable these community health facilities gained access to safe drinking water points. This project will ensure the construction of 34 gender-sensitive latrine cubicles in IDP sites / host communities of the target sub-divisions as well as the construction of 25 gender-segregated family latrine cubicles in IDP sites and host communities for a minimum of 1,850 beneficiaries.
Furthermore, the project will enable COHESODEC to distribute a total of 650 WASH kits to boys, girls, men, women, families, PWD and old people. These kits shall include Menstrual Hygiene management Kits to 250 women and girls, 400 kits to households involving a total of 2,600 malnourished children, 1,300 WASH kits to affected people who shall receive WASH supplies such as washing soap and aquatabs. The WASH kit intervention of this project will ameliorate on the living conditions of 3,900 beneficiaries in target communities!
Moreover, the project will be promoting safe hygiene and sanitation practices amongst vulnerable people reaching out to 10,000 people along awareness raising campaign and the promotion of good wash practices, ensure that at least 10,000 affected people know how to provide feedback and complaints to humanitarian actors and at least 10,000 affected people reached through sensitization of GBV along WASH activities!
In addition, this project seeks to ensure the support of solid waste management in IDP sites through the construction of 6 waste pits and the distribution of 40 waste bins in target vulnerable communities earmarked for this project.

1 - Besoins / Needs

This project seeks to address the WASH needs of the population most especially vulnerable IDPs & host communities of the target areas. COHESODEC in a bit to promote WASH activities through the construction of solar powered boreholes in Bafut, Bali, Bamenda 1, Bamenda 2, Bamenda 3, Santa, Tubah, Batibo, Mbengwi, Konye, Kumba 2, Kumba 3 & Mbonge sub-divisions of Mezam, Momo and Meme divisions of the North-West and South-West Regions of Cameroon. The project will require; specialized technicians who shall be sub-contracted after a tender process ensuring they have a proper mastery of earmarked project activities with regards to borehole drilling, latrine construction, the promotion of Sanitation and Hygiene practices in affected communities, the distribution of menstrual hygiene kits to women and girls will also constitute a major priority. These menstrual hygiene management kits which shall include sanitary napkins, soap, bleach and hygienic towels among others for women will be distributed to vulnerable women and girls in affected communities of the North-West and South-West Regions of Cameroon. In the course of implementing this project, COHESODEC will require the services of expert companies in the Construction and rehabilitation of water points (solar powered boreholes) to enable some 10,000 persons in these target vulnerable communities have access to constructed and rehabilitated water points. The project will also be ensuring that 5 of the 20 solar powered boreholes are constructed in some vulnerable health centers to enable these community health facilities gained access to safe drinking water points which will go a long away to stamp out terminal outbreaks like cholera, dysentery and typhoid which have in the past claimed so many lives in these communities. This project will also require the services of an expert company that will be engaged in the construction of 34 gender-sensitive latrine cubicles in IDP sites / host communities of the target sub-divisions as well as the construction of 25 gender-segregated family latrine cubicles for a minimum of 1,850 beneficiaries. Furthermore, the project will require COHESODEC to purchase through suppliers after a bidding process a total of 650 WASH kits for boys, girls, men, women, families, PWD and old people. These kits shall include Menstrual Hygiene management Kits for 250 women and girls, 400 kits for households involving a total of 2,600 malnourished children, 1,300 WASH kits for affected people who shall receive WASH supplies such as washing soap and aquatabs. The WASH kit intervention of this project will ameliorate on the living conditions of 3,900 beneficiaries in target communities! Moreover, the project will be promoting safe hygiene and sanitation practices amongst vulnerable people reaching out to 10,000 people along awareness raising campaign with the use of handouts, posters for the promotion of good wash practices, also ensure that at least 10,000 affected people know how to provide feedback and complaints to humanitarian actors and that at least 10,000 affected people reached through sensitization of GBV along WASH activities are effectively transformed! In addition, this project will require a contractor after a bidding process to ensure the support of solid waste management in IDP sites through the construction of 6 waste pits and the purchase and distribution of 40 waste bins in target vulnerable communities earmarked for this project. In a nutshell, COHESODEC will need campaign handouts and posters to reach out to communities with awareness raising massages which promote safe hygiene and sanitation practices among vulnerable people in targeted communities in the earmarked communities of the NW/SW regions of Cameroon.Also, for COHESODEC to support solid waste management in IDP sites, the organization will need 40 waste bins to put at the disposal of communities and equally construct 6 waste pits where the waste bins can be regularly emptied.
