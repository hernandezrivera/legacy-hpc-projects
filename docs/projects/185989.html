Project Summary

The conflict in the South West contributed to growing humanitarian crisis with approximately 2.3 million people in need of humanitarian assistance in 2020, a significant increase compared to the previous year. Continued insecurity, displacement, lack of access to farmlands, and restriction of movement have severely disrupted household’s capacity to meet their basic food needs, compounding their vulnerability to shocks, leading to reliance on negative coping strategies with associated protection risks. State & local authorities’ capacity to provide protection for local populations has been greatly reduced, with 700,000 displaced persons and 450,268 IDPs within the South West and North West regions, as insecurity continues to be the primary driver of displacement .Access to clean drinking water is also one of the most urgent needs for both the IDPs and the host population in the South West. Despite the efforts of the humanitarian community, it is estimated that in 2020, a total of 1.6 million people (25% adult women, 51% children, mostly girls, 4% elderly women and men) need assistance to access water, sanitation, and hygiene services (WASH). In the case of Lebialem it is estimated that over 57000 persons have been displaced either into the bushes or to the neighboring West region with some going to the Littoral and even the Centre. Over the past months, many persons have returned to the project area of Alou, Wabane and a hand full in Fontem. However, basic survival systems including WASH have been heavily affected and overstretched leading to a high risk of water borne and hygiene related diseases. Therefore this project has been conceived to improve sustainable access to water, hygiene and sanitation services and facilities to conflict affected populations of Wabane , Alou and Fontem Sub Division in Lebialem Division.

1 - Besoins / Needs

The violent socio-political conflict in the North West and South West regions is in its Fifth year and has affected thousands of people who are internally displaced, some living in bushes and the rest are returnees with nothing to begin life with. The situation in Lebialem Division of the South West region tends to be unique as very little and no interventions in many villages have been done there by international or local NGOs. Thousands have escaped to farm settlements across the division. The upper part of the project area north of Alou subdivision is covered by M’mockmbie village with quarters like Atsombie, Mendia, Kongemeh, Atrah, Mbegle,Mbingang where there is a concentration of the population (over 8,000) that has either remained there throughout or are returnees from the neighboring West region where they had fled earlier in the conflict.Our last field trip to the area was in late August 2021. We noticed that there was relative calm, and the returnee population were carrying on with their farming activities. This situation is ongoing as our contacts on the field assure us that farming, marketing activities and schools in some parts of Alou and Wabane area are going on unperturbed. In this context access to basic WASH services is very difficult and the concentration of people in single households is not helping. There are equally many small streams that cut through the villages in the lower parts of M’mouckmbie down to Njenawung to Ndumbin. The majority of the villages get drinking water from small streams and springs. A few villages are however, provided with pipe borne water. As for latrines low-income households do not have appropriate ones and there is need for some to be constructed in Alou market which will serves other neighboring villages in Alou and some villages in Wabane subdivisions. These activities will be supported by sensitization on proper hygiene practices targeting the general population of the area. Emphasis will be laid on the respect of COVID-19 barrier and physical distancing measures prescribed by government and the WHO 3. Project Goal, Objectives, Activities PROJECT GOAL: To improve sustainable access to water, hygiene and sanitation services and facilities for conflict affected populations of Alou ,Wabane and Fontem Sub Divisions in Lebialem Division. Objectives:- Build the capacity of community members to sustainably promote good hygiene practices in their community- Community members adopt safe hygiene practices that prevent them from WASH related diseases- Households have basic items to enable them practice good hygiene while maintaining their dignity The Project will ensures:o The community is effectively informed about the project deliverables and actively participate in project implementation decisions. o The completion of assessments geared towards determining WASH needs in the community and collection of baseline data to inform project performance. o Transparent profiling of project beneficiaries based solely on need identified as per the agreed upon criteria. o Effective implementation and monitoring of project activities to ensure high quality delivery of project goods/services.o Collection of client feedback and lessons learned. Planned Activities• Stakeholder meetings• KAP baseline/endline survey and wash assessment • Hygiene promotion• Purchase of 500 WASH and NFIs kits• Validation meetings for beneficiaries selections• Distribution of 500 WASH kits and 500 NFIs• End of project evaluations meeting
