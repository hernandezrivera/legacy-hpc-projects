Project Summary

Providing Shelter and NFI emergency assistance for the most vulnerable IDPs, returnees and host community families in conﬂict-affected areas of North East Syria, reaching a total of 900 conflict-affected individuals. Through the provision of shelter rehabilitation in host community of private shelters.
The NGO undertake shelter activities to support recent returnees, particularly in Ar Raqqa, Tabqa, Kobani and Manbij, living in unsafe and inadequate housing. This will be done through undertaking essential repairs of 150 partially-damaged or unfinished homes as well as ensuring a minimum level of privacy, dignity and access to WASH facilities for each household. This will benefit an estimate 900 individuals.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

As there continues to be a decreasing number of partners supporting Syrians, inadequate provision of shelter (5.9 M people are in need of shelter support (HIP, 2022), and 34% of people live in ‘sub-standard’ shelter (MSNA, 2022)), and an alarming food consumption score, the sectoral intervention will provide winterization NFI assistance to IDPs households in Camps and informal settlements, emergency NFI assistance for emergency-affected populations, and access to shelter solutions to both IDP and returning populationThe purpose of provision of winter NFI assistance is to improve the safety and wellbeing of households by reducing exposure to harsh climatic conditions while also reducing the burden on the families’ limited income assistance. Emergency NFI assistance will provide lifesaving /life-sustaining NFI support to emergency-affected populations.The program will utilize vouchers in areas where there are no functional markets or access to income opportunities. Voucher modality will also be utilized in informal settlements due to significant debt, lack of employment opportunities, and demonstrated need for winterization support.NFI interventions will continue to focus on informal sites where IDP households with high food insecurity levels reside, limited or not productive assets, and limited NFI items. To ensure coverage and avoid any overlap, the proposed interventions in this sector were mapped out in coordination with the SNFI WG, active NFI assistance partners and the Humanitarian Affairs Office.The Emergency NFI Kits, will be distributed in the event of an emergency with the hygiene kits and RTERs to ensure a comprehensive package of support is provided in emergency lifesaving situations.The Shelter assistance will be provided through the rehabilitation of unfinished/partially damaged buildings, which will reduce exposure to harsh climatic conditions and improve their safety and wellbeing. The program will coordinate through the Shelter Working group to select the districts in Ar-Raqqa, Ain al Arab, Menbij and Al-Thawrah city. Selection will be made first on the basis of the state of the shelter itself, prioritizing first those households that are residing in shelters that do not have a single, sealed room with basic WASH facilities (i.e. those shelters furthest from the SPHERE standards). Next those shelters with only a single habitable, sealed room will be selected. A vulnerability assessment will be conducted to ensure that only those households that lack the financial means to conduct the rehabilitation themselves, single headed-households, families with a person living with a disability and elderly will be prioritized for assistance. This activity, therefore, has two principal targets; A) To support returnees across targeted districts by providing safe, secure and winter-proof shelter through essential repairs of their own partially-damaged homes, B) To improve the quality of shelter for IDPs living in an inadequate private shelt
