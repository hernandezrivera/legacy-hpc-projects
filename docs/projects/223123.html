Project Summary

Six years into the conflict, more than 670,000 people remain displaced, and 68% of displaced children are out of school. The continuous displacements, insecurity, the increase in grave child violations, and looming climate hazards are adding complexity to the ongoing protection crisis in Cabo Delgado. The project proposes to support 440 children, 375 adolescents and 295 adults, particularly teachers affected by the conflict - to access safe, inclusive, and quality education and child protection services. HI team will deliver above all child protection and psychosocial support activities working closely with other education in emergencies partners to ensure a holistic response to the needs of children with disabilities. HI will also work in partnership to layer its interventions to ensure the most vulnerable out-of-school girls and boys, including children with disabilities, catch up on their learning and are enrolled/retained in formal schooling. The key activity related to access concerns the delivery of assistive devices for children which disability might be stopping him or her to access education. Quality of education will be improved through Teacher Professional Development adapted to needs of teachers in the crisis contexts. Schools with the highest severity ranking, identified in the cluster’s EiE Intervention Triggers methodology, will be supported in priority. In close collaboration with Child Protection Area of Responsibility, HI will also strengthen school-based protection, community-level protection and case management services to ensure comprehensive protection of the most vulnerable girls and boys, focusing on children with disability.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

HI intervention is targeting the most vulnerable groups, informed by the severity and vulnerability criteria used by the cluster in the design of the HRP. The activities will target on one hand, a total of 815 children and adolescents from host communities, internally displaced or returnees; and on the other hand, 295 adults, teachers and education officials. Please note that with the budget proposed by the Education Cluster (TOTAL = 151,700$), it is NOT realistic to reach the targets proposed by the Cluster for HI intervention. First, please be aware the cost of a non basic assistive devices is around 50 000 MZN; then, it's important to understand that HI intervention in physical rehabilitation is NOT an Assistive Distribution but: identification of people in need, medical diagnositc, assistive devices together with physical rehab sessions, follow up and maintenance of the AD. With a total budget for activities of less than 100 000 USD, the target should be drastically reduced in order to offer to the beneficiaries a real service - of quality - in physical rehabilitation.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

HI project includes two key activities, one targeting in and out-of-school children with disabilities and the other targeting teachers and education officials:1. Teacher Training on MHPSS, GBV/VAC, Inclusive Education in Quissanga, Mocímboa da Praia, Nangade, Muidumbe, Mueda, Meluco 2. Provision of Assistive Devices Macomia, Mueda, Quissanga, Muidumbe, Nangade, Meluco, Mocimboa da Praia, Ancuabe, Pemba, Ibo, Mecufi, Metuge Overall, the implementation of the projects will rely on strong partnership with Education and Child Protection implementing partner and coordination mechanisms that will support the referral of Children and Adolescents in need of assistive devices, as well as any follow up needed in terms of case management to address other needs.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Protection is mainstreamed throughout all the activities. HI due to its missionality, is very sensitive to identify protection risks, and set a referral pathway to address them. The active participation at the protection clusters, allows to identify risks and coordinate with other partners for an effective and meaningful access to services for the most vulnerable. Furthermore, HI conducts a risk assessment , to identify potential risks ( a. Breach of Safeguarding policies and Code of Conduct; b. Sexual exploitation, abuse and harassment ; c. Breach of Child protection policy; etc) For each risk identified HI set’s up a package of mitigation measures, these include: All staff will receive refresher training on the Code of Conduct and Safeguarding policies, including PSEAH and child protection, and will sign revised documents in their personal files.; community members will be consulted on the design, adaptation and implementation of the complaints mechanism, where appropriate, and will be consulted on the handling of complaints including by confcall sessions; The recruitment and selection of staff to work directly with children will include other child protection elements throughout the process (e.g. child protection mentioned in the work description, reference checks, interview questions, etc.).

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

Activity implementation will be operationalized under a people based approachNA. The dialogue with targeted groups and community members throughout the implementation is crucial to ensure the understanding of overall and specific needs and also the awareness raising on key factor for inclusion. Community engagement and participation is one of those key factors.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

This is project designed specifically to address the needs of children and adolescents with disabilities. HI’s mandate is to prioritize persons with disabilities as the main target population throughout all the activities and ensure a meaningful participation. HI identified, assess, provide direct service and refer for those services not covered by HI, to ensure that persons with disabilities will receive the adequate assistance to respond to their specific needs. A referral pathway is very needed to ensure a comprehensive response. stakeholders a follow-up system will be set up to receive feedback from beneficiaries referred and confirm if they received the adequate assistance. Beneficiaries will be informed about their right to equitable and safe services and assistance as well as on the selection criteria. HI will take into account the factors and barriers limiting the participation of beneficiaries to make sure all have equal opportunities

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project activities are planned to have a limited environmental impact. For the teacher training activities, the use of disposable materials will be avoid and the team will use participatory methodologies instead. Additionally, the selection of the supplies to be provided to children and adolescents will take into account the environmental practices from supplies and the criteria of the use of durable materials, quality and long lasting devices will be prioritized
