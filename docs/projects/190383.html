Project Summary

The project supported by ECW MYRP aims to improve learning through equitable access, crisis sensitive and quality education for out of school displaced children to assist the government’s efforts to mitigate the negative impacts of the crisis on out-of-school children in target woredas and support education institutions and community structures to strengthen their institutional resilience by helping build their capacity to plan and manage education in humanitarian situations.
Teachers, facilitators, education personnel and PTSA will be trained on PSS and referral path way to prevent and respond to child protection issues. In addition, the already established referral system in the existing schools will be strengthen and establishment will be carried out in the newly targeted schools to promote children’s participation specially girls. Further, identified cases will be referred to world vision child protection team to respond to immediate need and those who need additional support will be linked to available service providers. To ensure these WV has already established and working relationship with stakeholders at district level.
