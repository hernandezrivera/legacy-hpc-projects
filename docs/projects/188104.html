Project Summary

The project design is based on the following:
This project aims to increase the food security and enhancing the livelihoods of 11600 HHs IDPs and host communities in western rural of Aleppo and Idleb Governorate through:
1- wheat value chain activities:
Inputs supplies: 1200 of wheat farmers will be provided with agricultural inputs for 10 donum of wheat through ( Azote Fertilizer, fuel voucher for supplementary irrigation and harvest voucher )
-Marketing Phase: ONSUR will purchase 1 MT of wheat from each supported farmers
-Processing Phase: ONSUR will contract with mill to grind 900 MT of wheat. Also, ONSUR will contract with bakeries to bake 720 MT to produce 828 Ton of bred. 21148 free bread bundle (800g/ for each 4 person of family/6 days a week) will distribute to 9200 IDPs HHs in Salqin camps for 52 days through two months.
- ONSUR will sift the rest of purchased wheat seed (300 MT) to produce 270 MT of clean-good seeds. The seeds will be then sterilized and distributed among other 1200 wheat farmers during next season agricultural seasons. Each farmer will receive 225 kg of seeds to cultivate 10 donum in project areas.
2- Support olives value chain activities in western rural of Aleppo and Idleb Governorate:
Inputs supplies phase: 200 of olive farmers of will be provided with olive seedlings in Idleb. Also, ONSUR will distribute olive Inputs for other 600 of olives farmers ( manure of animals for to produce organic fertilizer (compost), 2 types of pesticide vouchers (insecticide, and fungicide), in addition to 2 fuel vouchers for supplementary irrigation, Additionally, ONSUR will contract 200 seasonal workers (women) of IDPs to harvest olives for 600 HHs. Also, ONSUR will contract 50 seasonal workers (men) from IDPs, to olives for 600 HHs.
Processing Phase: ONSUR will contract with presses to extract the oil of olive farmers beneficiaries.
-Marketing Phase: ONSUR will support the marketing of olive oil through purchase of 2 cans (32 kg of olive oil) in the ongoing harvest season
2- support vegetables farmers
ONSUR will support 200 vegetables farmers by distributing vegetables input (seeds, fertilizers and pesticides) Idleb to support generation income for vegetables farmers.
3- Support livestock breeders:
ONSUR will distribute livestock (40 cows for 40 cow breeders and 600 sheep for 200 sheep breeders (3sheep/sheep breeders)) in western rural of Aleppo and Idleb to support generation income for livestock breeders. ONSUR will identify, verify for target beneficiaries according to eligibility criteria (Families who has enough experience of livestock breeding) in addition to, vulnerability criteria.
ONSUR will be committed to the FSL cluster and WHO recommendations on prevention measures related to COVID-19 especially during the distribution of bread and agricultural inputs and conduction of trainings. In addition, protection cluster recommendations will be taken into consideration to avoid any GBV/PSEA risk.

4 - Needs

According to Humanitarian Need Overview (HNO2022), Syria continues to suffer from multiple and complex socio-economic difficulties, characterized by a weakened local currency, a devastating water scarcity witnessed during 2021, the knock-on effects of the Covid-19 pandemic, and a poor 2020/2021 agricultural season that resulted in significant agriculture production losses. The protracted crisis, now heading into its 11th year, is exacerbating food security and livelihood needs for many families across the country, further eroding the resilience and recovery prospects.ONSUR has developed rapid need assessment According to the KII with local councils, there is a large amount of land that the farmers have planted with wheat.All the surveyed farmers reported that their main difficulty is the high price of fertilizersand lowtheir quality. Also, high costof fuel and the high cost harvest lack of harvest equipment. Additionally, for technical support, not all the surveyed farmers received agricultural technical support.

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

Protection risks will be analyzed throughout the project life-cycle. According to the project activities, the following protection risks and mitigating measures are presented: 1)- Risk of exploitation or abuse by staff members/service providers if access to services Mitigating measures: Communicate regularly and clearly to the community about what services are available and who services are for. Provide regular training to staff and service providers for code of conduct and PSEA. 2)- Risk of doing harm or perpetuating unintended consequences to girls, and boys (and their caregivers) through the provision of specialized child protection services if service providers are not adequately trained/ services are of low-quality. Mitigating measures: 1. Ensure training packages are in line child protection minimum standards and consider remote managed context 2. Services should not be provided by any staff who have not received and demonstrated proficiency in training modules. 3. Service mappings should only include services that have been determined to provide safe and quality services. 4. Establish consultation with communities and ensure feedback mechanisms are in place


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

ONSUR strongly considers gender aspects at every step of its project design and implementation. A gender analysis was included in this project’s needs assessment. At every stage of the program, men and women will be included engaged, with consideration to the “do no harm approach”, especially those that are influenced or affected by project performance. Furthermore, ONSUR proposes by our project to engage women in decision making, particularly regarding the location of project activities. ONSUR will ensure rigorous follow up through monitoring and evaluation of projects. In addition, Rigorous M&E frameworks will be implemented to record gender differences in project implementation and results. Information will be disaggregated according to gender and results will be shared at the cluster and community level to assist in decision-making. ONSUR will never tolerate any abuse by anyone towards a woman or a girl.


11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

These areas selected by the project are considered the most fertile areas to produce wheat in Northern Syria.- ONSUR has good experience in distributing agricultural input - Based on a market assessment by ONSUR showed that the markets are functioning and, agricultural input is available in the local market.-ONSUR will hire two agronomists (engineers/ experts), who will follow up the stages of the wheat production cycle and accompanied by related guidelines and conduct technical training.There is social acceptance for the project activities Risks and mitigations:- Risk: The inability to meet the needs of all people in the project area;Mitigation: Transparency with local authorities and community and inform them very clearly of the selection criteria.- Risk: Corruption and fraud by the traders and providers; Mitigation: clear criteria to select vendors, MEAL department will monitor for the local market. M&E staff will move between vendors' shops, monitor the voucher replacement process, and check the availability of agricultural inputs in accordance with quality and quantity agreements. Beneficiary will sign a list containing the redemption of voucher according to the specifications mentioned in terms of quantity and quality.- Risk: Beneficiaries sell the input; Mitigation: awareness sessions to the beneficiaries on the benefit of these inputs to increase their production of wheat. - Risk: Drought. Mitigation: ONSUR will distribute fuel for supplementary irrigation.
