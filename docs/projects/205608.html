Project Summary

Due to the high influx of returnees from Ethiopia and Sudan into the county of Nasir, which adds to the already dire humanitarian situation and worse nutritional outcomes for the host community and IDPs who have continued to face the brunt of violences, floods, high market prices, and devasted social infrastructure, there is need to provide live-saving preventive nutrition services. UNKEA has vast experience in the implementation of nutrition interventions in county of Nasir for the last 12 year and well-built community trust and understanding. UNKEA understands from these experience that most of the effects of the various shocks can be better leveraged at the community level through deliberate efforts put to address some of the root causes of the shocks.
UNKEA proposes to support the delivery of nutrition interventions that are targeting the proliferation of sectoral integration, and prevention of acute malnutrition through social community level behaviour change and communication strategies, supporting of community structures that enhances service delivery and family level interventions specific for nutrition status improvement for both children and mothers. These efforts will help to contribute to the reduction of nutrition specific and related sectoral outcomes in Nasir County.
UNKEA already has set up community level structures such as BHIs, HHPs, Mother’s Support Groups, and other child support groups that shall be used to deliver the project. This project will specifically implement the activities below that are part of the nutrition cluster targeted activities in the county of Nasir.
1). Lactating women, breastfeeding women, and caretakers of children 0-23 months counselled on MIYCN 17415. UNKEA will support the achievement of this through community mobilization using MSGs who shall be formed and oriented to mobilize, screen, and refer PLWs and children from households to the service sites. The MSGs and care groups will help house to house mobilization of PLWs and support peer-to-peer encouragement.
2). Number of nutrition surveys conducted that include key nutrition indicators. UNKEA will support the conduct of nutrition surveys in Nasir County where UNKEA has strong community linkages. Surveys such as Mass MUAC screening, family MUAC screening, FSNMS, and others will be fully supported in this project. This will end the challenge of community’s support during the conduct of surveys or assessments in Nasir County since the community structures who shall be trained shall be responsible to reach the last village during surveys.
3). Number of MYICN Barrier Analysis conducted. UNKEA will support the conduct of community level analysis of possible barriers to MIYCN services that help in the prevention of acute malnutrition.
4). Proportion of girls and boys admitted with SAM who are tested positive for malaria, and Proportion of girls and boys admitted with MAM who are tested positive for malaria. UNKEA through the health program will deploy necessary staff in all nutrition sites who will support the screening of every child who is admitted to nutrition program for malaria and treat those found positive with antimalaria drugs. This will prevent the poor outcome of the nutrition intervention when a child has underlying medical condition.

1. Needs

Women and children in South Sudan continue to face the brat of continued violence and displacements that has led to loss of properties, livelihoods, economic meltdown, and worsened the health and nutritional status of vulnerable groups such as women and children. This is made even worse for individuals who are at higher risks such as children, disabled persons, female gender, and other minority marginalized groups. Women and children in South Sudan continue to face the unacceptable high levels of acute malnutrition in all parts of the country especially in Upper Nile state where the population face man made and natural disasters e.g., extensive flooding, draught, violence, and extraordinarily high market prices of goods coupled with unavailability of goods and services in the markets of all counties. The existing poor health system such as infrastructure, equipment, staffing, and medical supplies makes the already dire situation in all counties worse as common childhood ailments such as Malaria, cough, pneumonia, and diarrhoea among children under the age of 5 years become the top killer diseases. The low levels of EPI and WASH coverage in all the parts of the state worsens the nutrition outcomes for all children under 5 years and PLWs. The coverage of the essential nutrition supplements such as VASD are so low that their benefits are not being realized adequately. According to the IRNA reports for Nasir, the already dire humanitarian situation has been further aggravated by the influx of returnees from Khartoum, Sudan and Gambella, Ethiopia following violence in the 2 countries specifically in those areas where they were as refugees. RRC and IRNA reports of August 2023 have indicated a sharp rise in the number of returnees estimated 5,188 individuals in Nasir. These returnees have occupied any available structure in the county such as schools, churches, and others have incorporated into host community. These returnees add to the already dire states of the IDPs within Nasir County, this exacerbates the health status of the population.Already the Nasir continue to suffer from displacements due to devastating floods, communal violence and economic difficulties that have led to loss of properties. GAM rates in Nasir have GAM rate of 26.4% which is in the crisis level. Nasir is in IPC 4 for acute malnutrition level. The morbidity and mortality rates for children under 5 years in Nasir is at 102/1000 live births. The nutritional situations in Nasir County need a robust intervention to impede the escalation of humanitarian situations that would lead to deaths and disabilities among children and PLWs that are related to acute malnutrition. The lead nutrition partner for treatment of acute malnutrition is struggling to ensure community participate and support the end of venoms that cause acute malnutrition at the household level which requires a well-sort-for attention to details of daily lives of community members. This bit is not well demonstrated in the treatment of acute malnutrition modalities of nutrition intervention delivered to the community of Nasir. UNKEA has vast experience, cohesion, understanding, and is well accepted even in the last village. An attribute which UNKEA through delivery of developmental and humanitarian services to the communities, and establishment of various community structures. UNKEA therefore proposes to support the implementation of Nutrition preventive services in the community through direct community engagement and support the integration of health and nutrition services in all the nutrition sites through provision of health services to acutely malnourished children and PLWs. Such services shall include training of nutrition workers, screening and treatment for diarrhoea, malaria, pneumonia, LLTINs distribution and others that are under the mandate of UNKEA. These shall help to leverage synergy in holistic service delivery to the population.

2. Project activities

UNKEA proposes to implement live-saving nutrition interventions in Nasir County through community engagement to support the delivery of nutrition services in both outreach and fixed health & nutrition facilities in the community. Principal activities shall include the following in each county. Obj1: Prevention of malnutrition: MIYCN counselling for PLWs & caregivers of 0 – 24-month children; conduct of VASD campaign for children 6-59 months; BSFP services to 6-24 months & PLWs. These shall be supported through mobilization of the community to support the activities in their areas and ensure all targeted children and PLWs are reached. Obj3: Nutrition reporting, monitoring, analysis: conduct FSMS, SMART surveys, and BNA analysis for MIYCN; UNKEA will support community level structures and groups who will work with the treatment implementing partner to ensure that these objectives are reached. Obj4: Integration of nutrition with other sectors: testing and treating SAM & MAM children for malaria; screening and treatment of diarrhoea; distribution of LLIN to SAM, MAM & PLWs; Establish kitchen gardens in nutrition sites and households; As the lead Partner for health in Nasir, UNKEA will support the realization of health and nutrition integration through screening for and treatment of common childhood illnesses, provision of health commodities to PLWs and children in Nutrition programs. At community level UNKEA will lead the role out of kitchen gardens to support dietary diversity in preventing acute malnutrition at household level.
