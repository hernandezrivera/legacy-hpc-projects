Project Summary

This project will involve strengthening the DPS capacity in the 3 provinces (Cabo Delgado, Nampula and Niassa) to detect, investigate and respond to outbreak prone diseases in the districts/communities with special attention to vulnerable populations such as IDPs and the host commnities.The project will also involve responding to epidemic prone diseases e.g cholera through procurement and distribution of cholera kits, IEHK kita and training of Rapid Response Teams at the districts reporting cases and theose most at risk.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project aims at strengthening disease surveillance for early detection adnd reporting of cases for prompt response.The key target groups are IDPs and the host comunities in the ditricts already reporting cholera cases(Ancuabe, Metuge,Chiuri,Monetepuez & Pemba ) and Acute watery diarhoea(AWD) i.e Balama, Namuno, Macomia and those which are most at risk including where there are no functional health facilities(Macomia, Muidumbe,Mocimmoa de Praia and Quissanga)'Other key targt groups include men,boys,women,girls, elderly and people living with disabilities

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Key activities include:1)Procurement and distibution of Cholera and IEHK Kits2) Training of districts Rapid Response Teams and their deployment to districts reportng cases(Ancuabe, Montepuez, Macomia,Namuno,Balama, Metuge, Pemba,Ibo, Chiuri,Quissanga, Mocimboa da Praia, Muidumbe)3.Strengthening disease surveillance system by training cummunity based surveillance system4.Strengthening case investigation and analysis too identify the chain of transmisssion3)Support lab capacity through trainings of lab staff and provision of lab reagents as well as support for sample collection&transportation

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The health cluster is working in collaboration with protection cluster to ensure that all the procedures pt in place are followed .WHO has also put in place monitoring mechanisms to identify potential risks and mitigation strategiesto ensure there is no harm

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

The project will be implemented through the health system through which those who require assistance are easily identified and have access to the assistance.WHO is working with the health authorities and as the technical adviso to the health administration both at the district and the provincial evel, it has a responibility of ensuring that those who require assistance receive it.Through community engagement during humanitarian response and training on community diseases surveillamce, disease prevention and control measures, communities are being empowered to report any suspected cases for early detection and response.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Through community engagement ,people living with disabilities will be identified and any barriers to their access to assistane will be removed .The will have access to case management at the health facilities or at the Cholera treatment centres where they will receve opportunities to be admitted if need be.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

To control and prevent epidemic prone diseases like cholera or AWD, environment plays a major role.Proper waste management, properly managed sewer system and clean waterare key in controlling or preventing these disease which are now a major problem in Cabo Delgado.people are taking contaminated water which is not fit for human consumption which is as a result of poor hygien, poor waste management and degredation of environment.WHO through the Health cluster is workng with partners to support the districts and the province including communities in ensuring that the environent is safegurded.
