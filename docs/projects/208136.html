Project Summary

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Education Emergency Response in Cabo Delgado is an education project to respond more education challenges faced by girls and boys in condition of returnees in north of Cabo Delgado. The project has boys, girls in school age and teachers in four district were affected by conflict. The main objective is to promote a right for inclusive and safe education in returnees communities

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

TLS will be set up in Quissanga and Ancuabe (One TLS for 1 District)Distribution of Kit for leaners in Quissanga (500 students) Macomia (1000 Students) Mocimboa da Praia (1000Students)Provide school enrollment in Quissanga 1500 Kids, Ancuabe 2100 Kids, Macomia 1100 Kids, Mocimboa da Praia 800 KidsEstablish Catch Up Clubs and remedial class to enroll in Quissanga 720 Persons, Macomia 360 Persons, Mocimboa da Praia 720 Persons and Ancuabe 900 PersonsProvide training with PSEA for Staff of project and stakeholders 400 targeting 400 in 4 districtProvide VAC, GBV for girls clubs in 800 girls in 4 districts (Quissanga, Macomia, Mocimboa and Ancuabe
