Project Summary

MWL will implement an integrated protection project which covers four sub-districts: Afrin/ Albab /Harim /Idleb, by expanding the protection interventions in 4 existing community centres, and mobile teams based on the huge and enormous needs in these areas.
The project aims at creating a safer and more secure protective environment by providing an integrated set of General protection, Child Protection, Gender-Based Violence, and Mine Action services
Regarding targeted Gender and Age Groups, The project targets the most vulnerable groups in the targeted communities so the CWDs, Orphans, separated children, adolescents girls, Widows, Children Headed Household to be prioritized, focusing on the highest severity of vulnerable groups to respond to these group’s needs, taking into consideration GAM.
In order to ensure full inclusive intervention provided to the targeted communities, MWL has ensured a high level of coordination with other organizations which are already working in the same location.
MWL will target 2000 beneficiaries consists of four main components:
The First component is delivering general protection services to the host and IDPs including the following activities: awareness-raising, psychosocial assistance, targeted support to persons with specific protection needs and other community initiatives, through community centres and mobile teams
The second component covers child protection issues,the overall objective of CP intervention is to improve access to specialized child protection services and to child-friendly spaces by targeting children, youth, men, and women include the following activities:
sustained child protection programs, parenting programs, awareness-raising, and community events to prevent and respond to child protection issues, case management and training for men and women to raise the capacity about protection concept
The third component focuses on GBV issues to decrease and confront any GBV concerns and topics through awareness-raising using the GBV SC awareness raising toolkit. In addition to GBV response services, prevention &empowerment activities, and training for staff and community actors on GBV SOPs Referral and Risk Mitigation, and GBV awareness Toolkit for 7 days
The last and fourth component focus on Mine Action activities:
Provide Risk Education sessions in addition to train people to conduct these sessions, conduct contamination surveys and Provide victim assistance services in areas affected by explosive hazards.
Workshop and meeting with community leaders and key community members will be implemented throughout the assessment, planning stages, implementation and closeout of the project. In addition, consultations and awareness-raising will be implemented with the communities people prior to project implementation. Interviewing key informants during the assessment phase and Focus group discussions FGDs will be voluntarily conducted after getting the consent from several beneficiary groups. The expected outputs of these FGDs will be ideas, suggestions or recommendations that can be integrated into the project design so as to improve the provided services.
The need assessment has been conducted in coordination with Local Councils, community key members, Camps leaders, and stakeholders. All the topics of protection awareness activities will be determined depending on the needed assessment results
Conducting FGDs based on beneficiaries consent for the Community-based Child Protection Networks (CPN) in targeted locations to get their feedback along the project life cycle. MWL will involve all relevant community groups and stakeholders of child protection within these committees. These networks will support MWL in building a good relationship with beneficiaries and the whole community.
FGD tool will be conducted voluntarily on monthly basis to measure (B,G,CWD) satisfaction about the provided activities along with the project implementation and get their feedback from multiple perspectives

4 - Needs

Syrian conflict enters the tenth year, with families and communities continuing to experience violence, displacement, poverty, and consequences of the Covid-19. Data from 2021 HNO show that overall, 11.1 million people are in need of humanitarian assistance. 6.1 million remain internally displaced. Inadequate living conditions in makeshift settlements or overcrowded sites, particularly in the northwest, as well as loss of livelihoods, property and assets, compound protection risks, including risks of gender-based violence (GBV) and CP concerns, children at risk of killing and injury, recruitment and use in hostilities, torture, detention, abduction, sexual violence, attacks on Armed conflict, economic deterioration, displacement and COVID-19 all have disproportionate impact on girls and women in Syria, which manifests different forms of gender-based violence (GBV), especially early/forced marriage, domestic violence, sexual harassment violence, and psychological and/or physical abuse (HNO 2021).Based on HNAP report, population in NW Syria reached 4,297,891 as 1,584,186 in Aleppo and 2,713,705 in Idleb. High occurrence of child labour preventing school attendance by obliging them to leave school or requiring them to combine school attendance with excessively long and heavy work as clarified in Protection Monitoring Interactive Dashboard (65% of interviewed in Dana, 70% in Maaret Tamsrin, 74% in Afrin) sub-districts. Based on Multi-sector need assessment (MSNA 2021) the early marriage considered as negative coping strategy to respond for worsening economic situation of families since overall early marriage percent of interviewed (60% in Maraat Tamasrin for adolescents aged 12-17, 95% in Dana, 16% in Afrin) sub-districts
