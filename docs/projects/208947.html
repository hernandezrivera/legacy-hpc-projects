Project Summary

After the outbreak of war in Sudan on April 15, 2023, the ongoing clashes led to the sabotage of infrastructure and the destruction of public facilities such as hospitals, health centers, schools, universities, etc., and the residents of the inflamed states fled to safe states due to the seriousness of the security conditions and to escape death. The war left the country in a very deteriorating humanitarian situation, there were no sources of food, drinking, or treatment in the heart of the conflict areas, and the people of Sudan remained in search of a livelihood and a desire for security. Therefore, the need for housing, food, clothing, protection, health, and environmental sanitation emerged as the basics of humanitarian intervention to try to reduce people’s suffering and bridge gaps.
As YPCB is one of the organizations that have good enough experience in the field of protection, the YPCB wishes to intervene through this project in the IDPs shelters in Sennar State, Singa, Abohjar, and Aldender localities, by building the capacity of the protection networks that already exist in the state and create more network if needed, provide psycho-social support, through these activities YPCB wish to mitigate risk and make the IDPs shelters, secure and safe place, and that all IDPs across the line could stay there peacefully, in the other hand and through the need assessment survey that was conducted in Sennar state by YPCB, food, and nutrition immerged as a top priority for the IDPs, there for YPCB planned to add another sector to this proposal and provide food nutrition for the IDPs. This project targets a total of 500 beneficiaries, with a total budget of 70,000 USD, in a period of 4 months. Starting on 1 Feb 2024 through 30 May 2024.
