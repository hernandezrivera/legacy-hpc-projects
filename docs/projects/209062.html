Project Summary

Following the outbreak of war on April 15th in Sudan, the capital Khartoum, Darfur, and Kordofan states are heavily affected leading to biggest displacement of crisis of in the world with more than 6 million were displaced and more than 1 million have moved outside of the country. The conflict resulted in disruption of the health system at Khartoum which was the healthcare hub for the entire Sudan before the war. The displacement overwhelmed the already poor health system in the countryside by the influx of the IDPs, whom themselves are not able to afford the cost of the healthcare services. Up to the time of writing this article Blue Nile has received more than 121,000 individuals from Khartoum and Gazira states in addition to more than 130,000 IDPs from 2022 intercommunal conflict took place at the region. these IDPs spread over many locations among the urban settings and north Damazine and Ar Rusiris towns either among their relative households (the majority) and (few) in concentration settings e.g., schools.
Blue Nile region was classified among category 3 in IPC indicators for food security and livelihood, adding GAM rate 12.29% and SAM 2.84% (from the last S3M data available 2019), in addition after the inflex of the returnees from South Sudan, IDPs from Blue Nile conflict in 2022 and new arrived IDPs from the April 15 conflict has expanded the need and the gap for nutritional interventions. Up to now there has been no active response taken to address the gap, particularly the one resulting from arrival of new IDPs from April 2023 conflict, those who are mainly concentrated in the urban settings and areas North Damazine and North Ar Rosairis.
The SRCS is planning to respond to the nutrition gap by integrated CMAM and IYCF/MYCN with health and WASH to address the urgent needs, also will mainstream gender to al its intervention.
In response to this situation, the SCRS is planning to intervene in the IDPs concentration sittings, and while they are mainly located in Damazine and Ar Roseyris localities, the SCRS is targeting those high IDPs concentration community in the to localities by integrated CMAM program (SAM, MAM and community outreach)
Sudanese Red Crescent Society (SRCS) is currently responding to the crisis by cash and voucher assistant to the newly arrived IDPs from April 2023 (up to now the SRCS reached 1.200 HHs) which is funded by the GRC, also distributed 1,200 food baskets, which is funded by the ICRC. Moreover, SRCS is running the healthcare intervention at the gathering settings in Damazine and Ar Rusiris towns from its private funding.
SRCS is planning to maintain the healthcare services at the gathering points and extend it to outsides where IDPs are concentrated.
The purpose behind the proposed intervention is to:
- Prevent excess mortality and morbidity resulting from various diseases, health, and nutrition conditions.
- Provide lifesaving health and nutrition interventions to those affected by the conflict and their host communities (treatment of common diseases, RH, treatment of CU5 illnesses and malnutrition).
- Provide mental health and psychosocial support at the community and household level and at the individual level.
SRCS-BN branch is planning to response to this crisis by maintaining and rehabilitated of the existing water supply facilities Rehabilitate/repair /upgrade from water power system to the water solar system for (2) water yards and Repair/rehabilitate of (10) hand pumps not working in the region targeted15,000 beneficiaries, with improvement of the health situation in the gathering areas by provide, (15 ) collecting garbage’s medium size, health cleaning tools, which it is already available at the main market in place, Cleaning campaigns, hygiene awareness specially for puberty girls, vector control campaigns, distributions of hygiene kits and dignity kits targeted (5,000) households.
