Project Summary

The project will address community needs in 8 Sarmada Camps (Alforqan, Gitaa Alrahma, Aleppo south country, Blouzia, Alwafaa, Basnat Amal, Khaled Bin Alwaleed, and Alimdad) and in Daret Azzeh SD in Aleppo. The needs emerging children remedial classes to catch up their missing knowledge and resume their learning process with their peers. The project will target conduct remedial classes to 2880 out of 21416 children in both project locations. Of 2880 targeted children 1610 will be girls and 1270 will be boys. The project will reach 4000 children through back to learning BTLs campaigns.
This project is designed on community-based approach by involving beneficiaries in design phase, involving community in awareness campaigns of remedial classes importance.
A coordination is already established with Saramda camps MoU already signed between POINT and local authorities and camp management. Further coordination will be with NGOs, CBOs working in camps camp; Coordination is also already established with local authorities Daret Azzeh.
The project is drawn according to our 5 years expertise working in camps, and off camps empowering education actors developing education quality and humanitarian workers quality and impact.
POINT partner in this project Bonyan has rich experience as well in conducting remedial classes and create curriculum that is approved by education cluster. POINT has been working for 5 years in camps including Samrada camps.
The project aims at:
1. Providing remedial classes for students aged between 8-15 years. This to support them restore missing knowledge and level up with their peers. The curriculum to be used for the remedial classes is Short version of Syrian Interim Government SIG curriculum.
2. Enlighten children students with "I DEAL" courses as supportive materials to provide them with practical and discovery interest.
3. Participating in declining child labor by encouraging parents resume sending their children to schools.
4. Raising community awareness of importance of education process
All of that will be achieved by
a. create 8 non-formal education centers in 8 Samrada camps and one center in Daret Azzah
b. provide materials and remedial classes to two groups of children a group aged as (8-12) and a group aged as (12-15)
c. equip children students with "I DEAL" life skills course to increase their passion toward knowledge
d. conduct two rounds of remedial classes, each round last 3 months, each round will have groups, each group will have 20 children.
e. equip the learning centers with Child Protection specialists to provide needed services and referrals.
Targeted children will be reached through back to learning BTL campaigns conducted in the targeted areas and by conducting placement test to identify children students levels and grades.
To achieve project objectives provide non-formal education, POINT and Bonyan organizations will conduct catch up classes, remedial classes, back to learning BTL campaigns reaching 4000 children in targeted areas, Parent Teachers Associations PTAs, and literacy numeracy.
All teachers will be trained on how to teach and deliver both the short version of the Syrian Interim Government SIG curriculum and "I DEAL"
In the learning center
The project will be conducted with coordination with:
- POINT and Bonyan organizations other programs and projects of CCCM, Protection, and Livelihoods
- WASH cluster members working in the target area to support POINT and Bonyan conduct the rehabilitation for WASH facilities in the learning centers
- Protection cluster members working in the target areas to support POINT and Bonyan in refereeing cases of Child Protection, GBV, PSS, health and nutrition

Implementation of activities

The project activities will be implemented as the following 8 Sarmada Camps in Idleb and in Daret Azzah, Aleppo: First component, "Provide children and youth with non-formal education programs (including catch-up classes, SLP, remedial education, literacy and numeracy classes)"POINT and its partner Bonyan will provide remedial educations through 8 centers in 8 camps and 1 center in Daret Azzah. This support children students catch-up their missed classes. also the classes teaching methodology is designed on fun learning approach. POINT will target 4000 children reached by back to learning BTLs campaigns and will target for learning 2880 children students from targeted communities aged between 8 to 15. The students will be divided into two group aged first one aged 8-12, second aged one 12-15. The first group will receive all subjects of Arabic, English, Science, and Mathematics during three months. The second group will receive subjects of Arabic, English, Science, Physics, Chemistry, mathematics for three months. POINT will provide remedial classes for 144 groups. Each group consists of 20 children students. Second component, "Conduct Back-to-Learning (BTL) campaigns (media outreach, awareness raising activities, community mobilization)"POINT and Bonyan have a rich experience in education and community mobilization. Both organizations will conduct BTL campaigns to reach 4000 children and meetings for parents teachers and students. Parents and their children students will be invited to join classes to encourage parents and students attending remedial classes. POINT supports "no lost generation' in this context and to encourage students joining the remedial classes by conducting awareness sessions for them and their parents. We will conduct 18 campaigns targeting all community members to encourage children join the remedial classes. Third component, "Provide students with learning materials (stationary, pens, notebooks), school bags and school in a Carton kit in formal and non-formal settings"POINT and Bonyan have improved non-formal curriculum and learning materials that is based on student-center classroom settings, and have fun and learn approach. The supporting materials are I DEAL course. That course provides life skills knowledge to students. These materials are approved by education cluster. All subjects will be trained staff and qualified teachers. Also, in each center there will be child protection specialist to deal with any child protection case. Fourth component, "Provide professional development to teaching staff (child-centred and protective pedagogy, active learning, self-learning, life-skills, Curriculum B, ECCE, New Curriculum, psychosocial support and referral mechanisms, Education in Emergencies (EiE) and national/INEE minimum standards (MS))"POINT and Bonyan will provide teachers with training on short version of Syrian Interim Government SIG curriculum, INEE, and child-centered classroom approach. The teachers will be trained as well on "I DEAL" curriculum. This will provide teachers with beneficent knowledge to provide students with qualitative teaching and learning outcomes.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk 1: number of children engaged in the projects is less that targeted.Likelihood: Medium. Impact: HighMitigating measures: - coordinate with Parent Teachers Associations PTAs - Ensure our community outreach workers mobilize project communities and explained effects of planned activities. - Increase coordination with camp management, local authorities, other CBOs, NGOs working in the same locations to call parents to support the project Risk 2: military clashes within Daret Azzah SD and/or Sarmada preventing students access to learning centersMitigation measures: - coordinate with Parent Teachers Associations PTAs - Suspend activities till peace is recovered. - coordinate with POINT and Bonyan programs to resume providing services in nearby safe areas once safety is taking long time to be restored Risk 3:Risk of long distance and young children walking alone. Children may get lost, be attacked or lose interest in learningMitigation measures: - establishment of complaint mechanisms in school- Training of partners and local teams on Child safety, development of schools specific child safety policies POINT has experience in successfully delivering various kinds of project despite ongoing conflict. Additionally, we have designed security plans for the team and participants according to different scenarios. Our primary priority is the safety and security of the beneficiaries; therefore, we will conduct safety and security assessments prior to implementation and update the safety plans accordingly. And accessible accountability mechanism will be used in the project, known communication lines as well. Do no harm Standards will be applied at all stages of implementation and the project life cycle. In order to do that, POINT will conduct baseline assessments to ensure that none of the project activities will harm the community in any way. Additionally, POINT will conduct security risk assessments and safety and security reports in order to ensure the safety and security of project beneficiaries.
