Project Summary

Due to continuation of recent hostilities in Syria, tens of thousands of civilians have fled from governorates Idleb, Aleppo, and Hama to Northern Idleb and Aleppo which are already densely populated of IDPs having previously fled to these areas. Also displacement in winter months is further exacerbating the vulnerability of people in need. Many of those who fled are in urgent need of humanitarian support. In light of the high population density and the dependence of civilians on alternative sources such as water trucking, which are absolutely unsafe, new concerns arise from the spread of diseases due to the lack of water, especially among children. The most urgent need currently is the safe drinking water, in addition to personal hygiene items, and sanitation facilities (latrines).
BALAD is planning to implement and provide WASH services to about 15000 of newly IDPs in camps.
Balad has a good experience in providing WASH services due to implementation of two WASH projects in 37 camps in Idlep (more than 30,000 BNFs), in addition to providing other services ın camps such as installing tents and water tanks and distributing NFI kits.
The project aims to increase the number of water taps according the number of IDP families, the distribution of water trucking will be on daily basis for 5 months.
Project will ensure water is safe by testing water samples in sources, trucks, and in household level.
Project will work on WASH facilities by construction of latrine in accordance with the standards of allocation WASH cluster and sphere taking into consideration gender sensitive by building separate blocks, safe accessible, lighthouse and its doors provided with income locks, also taking into consideration persons with disabilities by building latrines and showers that suit their needs.
In addition to desludging all septic tanks in the targeted camps once a week for three months
Project also will provide containers for solid waste, communal garbage bins and small garbage bins for each tent, also trucks will periodically collect garbage and transport it to designated places to be processed by sorting the contents so as not to damage the environment and surroundings.
Project also includes conducting hygiene promotion sessions to raising awareness about personal hygiene, the campaign will be done by trained team on HP principles and presentation skills. Throughout session team will distribute materials printouts and stickers in public spaces in targeted camps, in addition to distributing hygiene kit for every family.

4 - Needs

According to HNO 2020 the number of people in need has reached 10.7 M, 3 M of them in acute need, access to sufficient and affordable safe water, adequate sanitation, solid waste management and/or hygiene supplies remain a challenge for newly displaced populations and IDPs living in sites, in particular those in informal or overcrowded sites. Also based on the reports submitted by WASH and CCCM cluster in coordinated with the rest of the cluster working in the humanitarian field and based on the priorities of the new allocation, it was found that the areas of NWS are received a large number of people because of the current military campaign. Balad has conducted a rapid needs assessment in many camps in several locations as A'zaz subdistrict (severity 4), Dana subdistrict (severity 5), and Maaret Tamsrin subdistrict (severity 4) according severity scale of HNO 2020, the main findings of this RNA indicates that drinking water is the most needed intervention in camps, followed by the need for sanitation facilities and personal hygiene items.In spite of all current interventions from the humanitarian organizations and other actors, there are still a lot of needs and gaps.

5 - Implementation of activities

The project intends to provide basic WASH services for about 15000 IDPs (2500 families) in camps of NWS that hosting newly IDPs due recent military campaign, who are unable to access these services and are fully dependent on the WASH services provided by INGOs/NGOs.At 1st month we will prepare and finalized all engineering studies, MOUs, conducting procurement & contracting processes. We will contract with contractor for constructing WASH facilities within camps (Blocks of latrines & showers and blocks of latrines & showers for persons with disabilities by rate of one toilets for every 40 person), construction of blocks will be done simultaneous with construction of septic tanks until end of 3rd month.Also desludging all existing septic tanks once a week for 3 months.During 1st month of project we will working on increasing number of water taps to cover all targeted camps (1 tap for every 10 HHs),From beginning of 2nd month until end of project safe water will be supplied daily by rate of 25 L/person.During 2rd & 3th month we will distributing hygiene kit (1kit for each family).Installation of solid waste containers (100 L capacity), provision of communal garbage bins (100 L capacity) and periodically collect garbage and carry it to a designated place. As well as will be forming Hygiene Promotion teams and train them on HP principles and presentation skills, HP team will conduct Hygiene Promotion sessions in public spaces in camps for 4 months, and distribute HP materials during sessions.During project implementation, project technical team will look for sustainable solutions and consider the possibility of implementing them, such as connecting to a water network if it is close to camps and implementing cost is similar to cost of trucking water, or securing operational cost of wells in or near camps instead of trucking water.The possibility of extension of close drainage networks to camps will also be studied instead of construction of septic tanks.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Balad takes all aspects gender, diversity, and age into account, women and girls are consulted to ensure they feel and are safe, especially when using WASH services constructed at their preferred places, the female staff is recruited in order to mobilize women and girls from diverse groups and to facilitate feedback from these groups.Protection Risk Assessments:Activity 1: Supporting solid Waste Management Protection Risk/Threat: Movements of garbage trucks in congested.Reliability of services.Likelihood: High.Impact: low. Mitigating Measure: Consult camps admins (separately women and girls) regarding SWM services modality/ frequency, truck routes, and schedule.Activity 2: Water trucking.Protection Risk/Threat: Unequal distribution of services inadvertently.Likelihood: Medium Impact: High.Mitigating Measure: Establish strong links with camps admins and to promote equity and “do no harm principle” all the time; Organize joint planning culture and agree on the minimum standard. Activity 3: Provision of Hygiene kit.Protection Risk/Threat: Gender insensitive contents verities of kits with inconsistent items. Likelihood: Low Impact: HighMitigating Measure: Harmonies and standardize gender friendly.Activity 4: Maintenance of bathrooms and toilet and increase its number.Protection Risk/Threat: Not accessible for all genders. Likelihood: Low Impact: HighMitigating Measure: Building separate bathrooms (male, female) and lighthouse, equipped with indoor locks, increasing the number of bathrooms for girls and equipping bathrooms for the disabled children in order to respond to the needs of their motor abilities. Access to water and sanitation services will be available to all population.Ensure participation of women in decision-making processes (siting, design, and management of WASH facilities). and Include women and girls in user-satisfaction surveys, as both respondents and interview.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Balad conducts a security assessment for its projects by receiving information on security situations from its network of local councils, camps admins & civil society activists, and by remaining continuously apprised of security situations through international community' bulletins. For emergency relief projects, when a security situation arises that threatens safety of staff, staff are redirected to nearby, safer location until area is safe enough to conduct activeties Risks and mitigation measures1: Extreme location of camps in rugged areas impedes arrival of trucks & materials. lowMitigating: Local suppliers of trucks and contractors have appropriate vehicles to access and execute required works and activities2: Ownership of camps land. lowMitigation: All papers & permissions on matters related to land are available at camp administrationRisk 3: Movements of water trucks in congested, camp like settings, low.Mitigating: Consulting community regarding location of distribution points, delivery routes and its schedule; Avoiding location of water points in narrow streets, congested areas; Establishing clear routes and schedule for water trucking that use wider access routes4: Movements of desludging trucks in congested, camp like settings; Reliability of services.Mitigating: Consulting community regarding desludging services, truck routes & schedule; Establishing clear routes and schedule for desludging trucks that use wider access routes, ensuring workers wear appropriate personal protective equipment and follow safety regulations.5: Proposed locations receive more IDPs, and the provided services are not sufficient (especially the quantity of water) to meet needs of all residents in targeted camps. Medium. Mitigating: Increase quantities of supplied water according SPHERE standards by transfer any available surplus in budget lines to item of water supply with the consent of donor, and establish water quantity tracking & monitoring system involving BNFs.
