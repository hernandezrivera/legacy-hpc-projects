Project Summary

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The Project will be realized in Ibo District; Ibo District has been heavily affected by cyclone Kenneth, moreover, due to the situation of insecurity in the coastal area of Cabo Delgado Province is now facing high influxes of IDPs looking for safer places to settle down. The fluxes of IDPs observed are also causing cholera outbreak mainly in Matemo and Quirimba islands (Ibo District) The intervention will target:- In Ibo District both host community, affected by the cyclone Kenneth, and IDPsThe objective of the project is to support with improved access to safe water, sanitation and hygiene facilities and a safe and healthy environment, contributing to reduce the risk of transmission of WASH related diseases, about 30,000 people (of which about 10,000 IDPs and 20,349 people from hosting community: 9,640 men, 10,184 women and 13,758 children) The project will support:The rehabilitation/construction of the supply systems, water connections, RWHS rehabilitation, construction of latrines (whether is possible pour flush) in the health centres of the District;The IDPs community with the construction of new water points (wells) near the relocation areas, the distribution of NFI to the most recently arrived familiesBoth host community and IDPs facing cholera outbreaks with the construction of sanitation and bathing facilities , distribution of certeza, latrine’s construction triggering activities and hygiene promotion campaign (focus on waterborne diseases and Covid-19 prevention)The most vulnerable families/families with disabilities in the construction of appropriate sanitation facilitiesTogether with the construction/rehabilitation of water points, training /revitalization of water committees will be carried out in order to give sustainability to the intervention.The triggering campaign for construction of latrines will be carried out together with the training of local artisans for the construction of slabs. Whether “emergency activities” (such as emergency toilets and bathing facilities construction, washing stations installations) are done, Istituto Oikos will be responsible for the maintenance and decommissioning. The kits will be distributed with the current COVID-19 exposure risk taken into consideration such as distributions at HH level and/or social distancing measures enforced during mass distribution. Whether distributions are foreseen, Istituto Oikos will conduct post distribution monitoring and share the report with UNICEF for improvement of items in hygiene kits. Target groups: - 20,000 people from the host community- 10,000 IDPs

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

- Rehabilitation of water supply systems of the health centres of the District- Construction of new water points equipped with hand pumps in new settlements where IDPs are concentrated and training for water committees- Distribution of point-of-use water treatment products in case of cholera outbreak, including training and monitoring on proper use at household level- Construction of emergency latrines, hand washing points and bathing facilities in relocation areas or in case of cholera outbreaks- Promotion activities for self-construction of household latrines and provision of slabs locally made by trained artisans- Construction of latrines for most vulnerable families, people with disabilities and child-headed families- Community and household level hygiene promotion activities (focus on waterborne diseases and covid-19 prevention)- Distribution of NFI (family/dignity kits) to IDPs and vulnerable families, including community engagement sessions and sensitisation on safe use and disposal of materials- Installation of community hand-washing station to prevent the spreading of Covid-19 in community’s key-points

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The main risk of the project is the difficult acceptance from the part of the beneficiaries of the adoption of a certain behavior which are not yet included in social behavior of the local community (as using latrines).The project identified the following solutions:● The introduction of the “new” behaviors will be made after consultations with the principal stakeholders and community leaders ensuring a better acceptance from everyone. ● Involvement of the community leaders on the diffusion of the messages. In fact, they have significant potential to influence the cultural norms. ● Holding single-sex, age segmented focus discussion group sessions on constrains to their behavior change.● Valorizing traditional knowledge and local productive culture and keeping in consideration taboos, preferences and construction patterns.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

This action is designed according to the expressed need of target groups assessed during periodical focus groups, surveys and other participatory methodologies during years of presence of Oikos in the area. Furthermore, participation of target groups is mainstreamed during the whole project implementation. The action focuses on working in close collaboration with the community utilizing their internal resources and valorizing traditional and cultural knowledge. This will facilitate the acceptance of the intervention by the community and will guarantee its participation. The project will create spaces for interaction, such as public consultations, focus groups and community data collection, to identify constraints and ways to better production techniques. It will make the results more reliable, fostering ownership and capacities. All the activities will include specific sessions to discuss the issues and new habits promoted to enhance the full comprehension and adopting.The Districtal service for infrastructures SDPI in the selected district for implementation of the project activities will work with assistance from the WASH cluster and active partners in Cabo Delgado and utilize existing feedback mechanisms such as Linha Verde and Linha Fala Criança for which specific pre-formulated questions and replies already do exist. All requests for responses to the Linha Verde mechanism will be responed to within 48 hours of receipt from the WASH Cluster.Therefore, the DPOPHRH, SDPI and partners will be aware and can follow up about any comment on the distribution or availability and quality of the services and products provided, and of specific requests for information.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Local Institutional stakeholders will participate in the beneficiaries’ selection to ensure to include the most vulnerable households, including people with disabilities. Further, consultations will be done individuals with individuals living with disabilities and the elderly to identify specific adaptation needs for infrastructure. The support offered to the vulnerable families/people with disabilities will take into account the special need, adapting infrastructure to the specific needs.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project will take into account some environmental considerations that can come up during the NFI distribution (such as the production of waste) or linked to the construction material provenience.The project identified the following solutions:● During distributions activities, the population and other beneficiaries will be taught the correct way to dispose the waste produced (such as plastic bags and packaging) in a way that would not be prejudicial to the environment.● All the construction material will be provided assuring its sustainable provenience and avoiding to encourage the illegal traffic of wood, in order to prevent the exploitation of the resources of the QNP (Quirimbas National Park)● The project will avoid to promote material coming from non-renewable resources and it will ensure a minimal use of non-re-used material to reduce mass waste production in refugee accommodation sites and host communities.
