Project Summary

The Covid 19 pandemic which has become a global issue has hit the world Nigeria inclusive. The lock down order and restrictions on movement has confined people to their homes which makes it difficult for children to cope with. This is because they are vulnerable to domestic abuse which may likely happen. These children often feel relieved when they are at school with their colleagues but now that they are at home, they may experience violence which may lead to trauma.

1 - Needs

The Covid 19 pandemic which has become a global treat has hit Nigeria. Adamawa State is one of the states that has the virus. Children have been forced to stay at home because of the restrictions in movement. These children may face psychological problems that is why Life at Best Development Initiative want to intervene with series of PSS activities to help them cope with the situation.

2 - Activities

1. Identify strategies for providing PSS to children, especially those under quarantine2. Training of community volunteers as case workers and case managers 3. Provide targeted support to interim care centers and families including child headed families and develop child friendly messages on covid 19 in local languages4. Provide financial and material support assistance to families whose IGA is affected5. Work with traditional and religious leaders to sensitization programs
