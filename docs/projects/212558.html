Project Summary

Throughout 2024, Violet Organization is planning to implement comprehensive SNFI interventions by applying a variety of modalities such as in-kind and ash to address the emergency needs of the different groups with the most suitable modality.
The proposed project will effectively contribute to addressing the life-saving and life-sustaining response through the provision of shelter and seasonal non-food items as well as NFI assistance to the most vulnerable and IDPs which will help to avoid negative coping strategies. the planned activities for 2024 are the following activities
1. Provision of essential and Core NFI items for 2000 HHs through the provision of nonfood items by modality
2. Distribution of seasonal and winterization assistance to 25000 households through cash, modality.
3- Installation of 2000 tents in camps.
4- Support 850 HHs with cash for rent.
5- Rehabilitation of 300 partially damaged and unfinished houses with solar system.
6- Provide capacity building for 150 humanitarian workers
To ensure no overlap will occur, Violet coordinated with its partners who are funding Violet's projects and only submitted the activities that are not reported by other partners. Moreover, Violet will ensure close coordination in both fields and Turkey levels and through the SNFI cluster to ensure a coordinated and effective humanitarian response. However, Violet coordinated with its donors/partners including CARE and SCI to design the HRP and took into account their submissions. So Violet doesn't have any overlap with the other intermediate donors.
Violet requires 8743000 to implement the above activities. Unfortunately, we have only 10% secured funds for 2024 so far.
