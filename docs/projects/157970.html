Project Summary

This project aims to address one of the biggest gaps in humanitarian emergency response capacity in Burundi: the lack of pre-positioned stock to implement a rapid response in case of emergency. To support the needs of internally displaced persons (IDPs), returnees, host communities and crisis-affected communities, this project proposes to (1) create a shelter and non-food item (NFI) pipeline that would pre-position both shelter and NFI kits in case of a major event; 2) reinforce the Displacement Tracking Matrix (DTM) Emergency Event Tracking system, 3) provide basic food needs to affected populations; and 4) improve access to safe water and prevention of diarrhoea by providing critical WASH-related information to crisis-affected populations, prioritizing the most vulnerable women and children. These four components together will create the capacity to respond in case of an emergency in a way that respects the minimum standards of quality.
Outcome 1: Improved life-saving assistance and emergency shelter and NFI goods are made available to populations affected by disasters
Outcome 2: Natural hazards are regularly identified through the DTM Emergency Event Tracking system
Outcome 3: Increased ability of crisis-affected populations, internally displaced people (IDPs) and returnees in targeted areas to meet their basic food needs
Outcome 4: Improved access to safe water and sanitation facilities and prevention of diarrhoea by providing critical WASH-related information to targeted populations
