Project Summary

To increase equal access to safe, adequate and sustainable water supply and to ensure improved sanitary and hygiene conditions and to prevent and reduce WASH related diseases among host community, returnees, IDPs and in (Pibor, Nyirol , Nasir, Fashoda, and Manyo) Counties in 2019.

Needs

Pibor: Assessment report conducted by REACH showed that Food security has further deteriorated in Pibor and it is possible that more than 20 percent of households are in Catastrophe (IPC Phase 5). The report showed that 24.3 percent of households reported a Household Hunger Scale of six, indicative of Catastrophe (IPC Phase 5) and a significant increase from already severe hunger reported in late 2017. In October 2017, the prevalence of global acute malnutrition (GAM (WHZ)) was 26.8 percent (22.8-31.2), somewhat below the Famine (IPC Phase 5) threshold of 30 percent. GAM rate expected to increase if SMART survey conducted. Urgent humanitarian assistance is needed in this county to save lives and to control water borne diseases. This project target 5926 people in need in Pibor. Nyirol: Nyirol County is classified in IPC 4 an emergency threshold. There is dire need for immediate humanitarian assistance. Most of the available WASH facilities are in deplorable conditions and are broken down. Flooding is also a severe problem in this county farther increasing the burden of the already vulnerable populations. GAM levels in this county is way above the emergency threshold of 15% with SMART survey conducted in June 2017 indicating that the GAM levels were at 26.1% in Jongolei state in which Nyirol county lies. This therefore calls for integrated approach to address these needs. This project will ensure provision of clean and safe drinking water to children admitted with SAM and MAM in OTP centers and stabilization centers. The project will ensure that the mothers of the children admitted are supplied with hygiene its and trained on how to use them. There will be also sensitization on the minimum WASH package activities to the health/nutrition workers in the county to ensure that the messages are passed to the community. WASH facilities in the nutrition centers and stabilization centers will be rehabilitated to ensure that the objectives are well met. There will be rehabilitation of Latrines in OTP centers and health facilities to ensure that the already malnourished children do not conduct other WASH related diseases which will further deteriorate their health. CRADA will also work with other actors in Nyirol to ensure that the most vulnerable populations are well served. Target people in Nyirol are 53590.Nasir: According to UNICEF report, Nasir County reminds critical and of high priority following ongoing insecurity that has led to the displacement of 37,000 households to Jikmir, Makak, Wangding and Nyatot. UNICEF, through implementing partner Nile Hope, is providing access to safe water and sanitation for the displaced population. Therefore, WASH services are needed in this County. CRADA will target 54,746 people in need.Fashoda: Following the intensified fighting in March- April 2017 in Kodok, and the consequent take-over of the town by SPLA-IG, part of the local population fled the town and the neighboring villages to seek refuge in Aburoc (Fashoda county) while some continued northwards to Sudan. During and immediately after the violence, looting of properties and raiding of cattle were reported, leaving the town empty of civilians and in a desperate situation. According to OCHA 2 , in April 2017, an estimated 25,000 people were displaced to Aburoc from various previously IO controlled locations, including Kodok. Five months later, in September 2017, the Local Administration was re-established and it started advocating for safe returns to the area. this project is targeting 33329 IDPs and host community in Aborouch/ Fashoda County.
