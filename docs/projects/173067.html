Project Summary

UNICEF as a global sector lead in WASH , Education and Child Protection started to support the respective sectors in Libya within humanitarian coordination architecture
The funding requested within this programme will be supporting the following coordination forms in terms of the sector coordination as well as providing the required information management and documentation support.
WASH as well as IPC (COVID-19 Coordination )
Child Protection Subsector ( including support to PSEA and GBV sub-sectors)
Education Subsector (including support to JTCC coordination - as a joint secretariat for the Basic Services Pillar)

1 - Needs

With the protracted conflict , COVID-19 pandemic, the coordination of the humanitarian assistance has never been important and relevant , as it is in 2021. At the same as the government of Libya is moving ahead with peace reconciliation processes , an important opportunity is provided to support the government authorities with the development of the national development priorities . UNICEF supported sectors are very well positions (building on the comparative advantage of the UN agencies in the country) to support the line ministries of Education , Water and Sanitation and Social Affairs to improve and coordination the basic service delivery with the support of the international community

2 - Activities

To Coordinate WASH , Education and Child Protection Services Provide the required Information Management and reporting capacity to UNICEF supported sectors Build the capacity of the sector partners on the essentials of the humanitarian coordination, monitoring, evaluation and follow up

5 - Mitigation measures
5.1 - For each risk mentioned under 4 above, please describe the ways you plan to mitigate, prevent and/or address these.

4.2 , 4.3 :-Close and direct cooperation and engagement with the project beneficiaries, within the framework of “accountability to the affected population”. This will include - regular interaction with the targeted communities by the programme staff, - strengthen monitoring by the programme team as well as third party monitors , - creation of the “complaint box”, - active engagement with the media (especially social media – facebook) to explain report of the progress of the project implementation taking into account the feedback from the beneficiaries in the form of humanin interest stories 4.6 , 4.13 : the WASH response has incorporated/mainstreamed the GBV in the programme activities through the below mitigation plans- advocacy and insurance that there are gender disaggregated sanitation facilities that are inclusive of the cultural sensitivities- WASH supplies distributed to the females –( in a priority matter)- inclusion of the hygiene products that are required for the use of the females - complaint box made available at all the sites for the reporting/feedback- strengthened monitoring by the programme staff as well as third party monitors 4.15: UNICEF is a sub-sector lead in the PSEA at the inter-agency level , together with the UNFPA- UNICEF has developed and has distributed the PSEA material- All the sector partners staff has been guided to take PSEA training - UNICEF has undertaken PSEA traning of all its partners - UNICEF has conducted PSEA assessment of all its partners . All the high risk partners (mainly national NGOs) have been requested to lower their risk to medium by the end of January 2021
