Project Summary

This project in partnership with CARE aims to address the emergency and urgent needs of vulnerable populations in the northwest of Syria, through coordinated, multi-sector rapid response and support in Idleb and Aleppo Governorates. Through the hospital and the primary healthcare centre in Azaz city in Aleppo governorate; Sexual and Reproductive Health (SRH) and protection/GBV services are to be provided to community members in selected locations, as well as referral services through the ambulance system that is functioning in selected locations in Aleppo and Idleb governorate. Also, this project aims at disseminating Covid-19 awareness messages through trained Community Outreach Workers’ (COWs) teams.
To strengthen its response to the complex and multispectral needs of survivors, Violet will integrate GBV response into the health facilities under this project. The GBV intervention in this project aims to "Survivors of GBV and individuals at risk of GBV have increased access to safe and confidential GBV services through AMAL Hospital and PHC" in Azaz city.
The GBV staff in AMAL Hospital and PHC in Azaz will target women, girls in particular, including women and girls who live with disabilities, and also the elderly women.
Violet will ensure to conduct a risk assessment and analysis to understand the safety and accessibility of the services and develop clear and targeted recommendations and action points while establishing GBV services. Also Violet will strengthen privacy and confidentiality for all women who come for health services through infrastructure improvements, and look at the representation of females in clinical and administrator positions in the health facilities and recruit dedicated GBV staff to be located in the health facilities and PHC. Violet will develop GBV Case Management Standard Operating Procedures including GBV CM tools, Data Protection Protocols, and update the GBV service map that is available by the GBV Sub-Cluster in NWS and update the relevant referral pathways for multi-sectoral support in the operating area. Violet will do refresher training on the already existing protocol on CMR and ensure compliance with it, for Doctors and Midwifes are already exist in AMAL hospital. Also, ensure/improve educational materials available to clients on topics related to GBV in the health facilities. Vıolet will collaborate with the GBV SC to participate in the case management coordination efforts to benefit from inter-agency capacity development opportunities and already developed tools related to case management and data management.
1,350 women, girls, and men will be targeted by Violet GBV intervention within Amal Hospital and PHC, starting from train health facility medical and non-medical staff on GBV core concepts and safety referral. Whereas the GBV staff will be trained on Community-based Protection, PSEA & CSG, and PFA, and in-depth cases management to GBV cases workers and PSS counsellors, targeting a total of 120 staff members.
GBV Case Workers will provide specialized GBV services to survivors and the at-risk cases based on survivor-centered GBV Case Management to a total of 100 women and girls cases.
Violet MHPSS counsellors will provide cultural and age-specific individual and group mental health and psychosocial support sessions targeting GBV survivors and at-risk cases in a total of 850women and girls.
Violet Young Mother Club facilitators will provide YMC cycles and sessions for women and girls to address the holistic needs of pregnant adolescents and first-time mothers, targeting 180 women and girls. Moreover, 100 women and community committees members will be trained on GBV basics and Safety Referrals, and Enhancing the community acceptance and support for GBV issues and needs.
Violet will use the GBV SC M&E Toolkit to 2nd edition 2021 to monitor and evaluate the implements and reports in line with the inter-agency guidance.

4 - Needs

Based on HNO 2022 the people ın need is 7,319,965 M, 52.3% women, 2.3% men, 36.7% girls, 4.6%boys, 4.1% elderly, 28.5% disability. Women and girls are subjected to increased denial of economic resources and education, movement restrictions, exploitation, forced and child marriage, intimate partner and family violence, technology facilitated violence, and physical, psychological, emotional, sexual, and social violence.19% of HH mentioned that women and girls feel unsafe in certain areas. 71% of communities mention that child marriage is an issue for adolescent girls. Male partners, male family members, and men and boys in general are the most common perpetrators. Patriarchy, institutional gender inequality, discrimination and social exclusion uphold and compound GBV. Older women, divorced and widowed women and girls, and women and girls with disabilities, face heightened social policing, deprivation of inheritance and resources, 57% of communities mentioned that widows are at risk of exclusion from receiving humanitarian aid and services.The impact of GBV on women and girls is tremendous and long-lasting, aggravated by victim blaming, social stigma, and social isolation. Seeking GBV and health services remains the safest, and often the only, way to seek support and improve their wellbeing. The protracted crisis, economic deterioration, and COVID-19 collectively exacerbate GBV risks. These increase the violence women and girls experience, and diminish their resilience.

10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Violet targets PSEA risk through a) training on PSEA for staff, BNFs, vendors; b) integrating PSEA in AAP with safe and accessible complaints mechanism; c) appointment of PSEA focal points in each location to handle reports of misconduct, d) support to survivors through case management, e) regular information of affected community on their entitlements.Potential risks:1) Misconduct of staff against child safeguarding principlesMitigation: initial and refresher training on CSG responsibilities, behavior training for staff, assign focal point for CSG, child-friendly AAP sessions including CSG information and how to raise complaints, zero tolerance on misconducts and rapid action after investigation.2) SEA misconduct from project staff to parents or childrenMitigation: provide information sessions to parents (sex-segregated if preferred) on expected behavior from staff and humanitarian workers, explanation of complaints mechanism, availability of AAP staff face-to-face for sensitive complaints.3) Request of SEA for selecting BNFs to the servicesMitigation: clear information on the provided services in terms of quality and quantity and that nothing is expected in return in any case, emphasize on complaints mechanisms, provision of multiple channels to raise complaints, referral to inter-agency hotlines as alternative. Violet will conduct FGDs/KIIs to ensure participation of women and girls during all phases of the project, especially in design of the activities and monitor


11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumptions: Border crossing between Turkey and Syria remains open for humanitarian assistanceRisks: Indiscriminate attacks on civilians and health infrastructure (IED,VBIED). Intensification of the conflict resulting in shifting frontlines, mass population displacements or drastic changes of the context. Exodus of professional staff to address the risk of instability, contingency plans are made and include predominantly the emergency evacuation of staff and equipment and shifting the services to where the community moves. Security team carries out regular security assessments of facilities and closely monitors developing security situations to determine if facilities remain open and continue to provide full range of services or limited emergency services during periods of increased hostilities.Assumption: The COVID-19 situation is still increasing Risk: Access loss to GBV servicesMitigation: Violet is addressing by improving the hygiene and wash protocols and materials in the facilities. Decrease the number of beneficiaries in the group sessions, and keep the social distance in both group and individual sessions. Resume the activities through an online approach in-link with all data protection protocol and respect confidentiality, with providing internet cards and use additional resources if needed by other projects, sectors, and donors. Keeping the donor updated with any additional fund required.
