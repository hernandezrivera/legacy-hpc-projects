1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Within the context of Cabo Delgado, AIFO and relevant partners will carry out an integrated action Cabo Delgado Province in order to promote inclusive and accessible PwD protection, to encourage community disability mainstreaming and to endorse the rights of PwD. The action will respond through service providers’ capacity building and through services delivering to PwD and their families. AIFO will strengthen community protection mechanisms already put in place since 2021 in the Districts of Montepuez, Pemba and Metuge through Community structures and Social Action Technicians capacity building on PwD protection and case management. The project will create linkages and referral paths between community structures and relevant institutions, optimizing lesson learnt from previous experiences. The action aims at creating an enabling an inclusive protective environment in the communities of intervention.Before starting all trainings and case management activities, AIFO will conduct a rapid assessment, involving DPOs, community members, Civil Society Organizations and local authorities, focused on services auditing to determine their accessibility and propose jointly agreed and simple solution when they are not fully accessible. Through this exercise, potential risks of exclusion from protection mechanisms and of harm will be identified and duly addressed.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The action is addressed to contribute overcoming protection risks within communities, with a specific focus on elderly persons with disability and female and young girls with disabilities. This last group is one of the most vulnerable groups, because they face double discrimination: the first related to gender and the second one related to disability. As a consequence, female and girls with disabilities are more at risk of direct violence, and they are more excluded from basic services, such as health, education and MHPS (Mental Health and Psycho-Social Support), also because services are poorly adapted to their specific needs. The action will put emphasis on gender and gender/disability specific needs and vulnerability, addressing them with a specific attention in the content of training, sensitization and awareness activities, both at community and government level. The gender/disability focus will take into account also the specificity of vulnerability according to age.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Target beneficiaries of this action are precisely PwD, who are particularly vulnerable among communities, living ostracized and excluded from social life. They will be identified by community structure members and community leaders duly trained and using the Washington Group Short Set of questions (re-adapted to community understanding). PwD, in particular women and girls, are often subject to sexual and physical abuse, exploitation and neglect. They are often excluded from education and not provided with the support to help them develop to their full capacity. The most vulnerable and discriminated are children with albinism, psychosocial and intellectual disability (myths and superstitions have generated the common belief that persons with albinism have magical aptitudes, and their body parts have become instruments for witchcraft practices). The exclusion of PwD is exacerbated when they belong to another disadvantaged group, such for examples the IDPs. The action will contribute to identify (rapid assessment) and remove (capacity building) attitudinal and social-cultural barriers which prevent the full development, participation and protection of PwD, providing local authorities and service providers with specific knowledge and means to guarantee assistance and support to PwD.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project will be implemented in the respect of AIFO Environmental and Sustainability Policy, included in AIFO Manual of Operational Policies- Code of Ethics and Conduct 2020. According to the essential points of this Policy, an environmental assessment will take place and will include representatives of people with disabilities, strengthening environmental measures that increase accessibility and break down existing barriers.This action does not have a negative impact on the environment while mitigation and protection risks within communities, as part of the Disaster Risk Reduction framework, will tackle community preparedness to natural disasters – as the geographic area is exposed to this catastrophic natural events. The action will Include representatives of vulnerable people with disabilities in the preparation and implementation of disaster risk reduction plans to limit their impact on the most vulnerable.
