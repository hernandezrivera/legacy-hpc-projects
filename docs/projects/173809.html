Project Summary

This response will prevent and rehabilitate women and girls survivors /at risk of trafficking in persons (TIP).
the response will prevent and respond to trafficking through series of capacity building of law enforcement officers, protection officers on victim identification, prosecution and GBV prevention respectively and awareness campaigns on social norms and beliefs. in addition, AHA shall rehabilitate survivors of trafficking by setting up/ rehabilitating 5 women friendly spaces(WFSP), provision of reintegration services through Psycho-social support and socioeconomic empowerment as well as voluntary prosecution of traffickers.

1 - Needs

The Gender-based violence (GBV) assessment conducted by AHA in March 2020 in 5 IDPs camps in Gwoza through focus groups discussions, key informant interviews, in 100 households of low incomes revealed that: 55% of single female headed households from 100 assessed households lack livelihoods opportunities as a result of social norms and disruption of livelihoods by Covid-19. women survivors are at risk of trafficking face several forms of exploitation mainly sexual exploitation and abuse(SEA). 100 interviewed women reported that they were proposed negative livelihoods opportunities ( searching of firewood) in neighboring bushes at the risk of being abducted by insurgents. 5 incidents of sexual exploitation and abused were reported as result of limited access to food. survivors were single female headed households of 25 to 45 years old with several children. they were sexually abused in the bushes as they were trafficked for harvesting groundnuts in villages. the identified perpetrators were intimate partners known before living within the community; they were traders and community heads. the means of transportation were traffickers old cars, and the harboring places were hubs in bushes. 10 % of survivors presented symptoms of distress, and anguish. the exposure of women and girls to sexual exploitation and abuse as the most common forms of exploitation of trafficked persons is to grow, as 55 % of women and adolescent girls in Gwoza as women have not yet recover means livelihoods after lifting up of movement restrictions and opening of businesses. distortion of supply chain caused by the lock down and movements restrictions of pandemic of ccovid-19 and prevailing insurgent attacks limited farmland to military trenches. women access to socioeconomic opportunities in Gwoza is 27% compared to their male counterpart 63%. There is need to improve women access to income generation opportunities. in addition, there is domestication of women and girls in Gwoza and the North East: 60% of women are mostly engaged in cap making businesses, tailoring, baby making, domestic chores while male are involved in POS businesses, selling of materials. There is need of raising awareness on social norms and beliefs to curb gender inequality and improve women access to socioeconomic diversification. Furthermore, there is need to provide tools and build capacity of law enforcement officers to identify women and girls with symptom of trafficking, trafficking network, while also building capacities of judiciary on prosecution.
