Project Summary

A. Needs
The ongoing conflict in Syria, which is soon entering its fourteenth year, has inflicted enormous destruction and unimaginable losses on the Syrian people and other areas of their lives. The number of people in need of assistance in the country is constantly increasing. Socio-economic conditions are rapidly deteriorating in Syria, with the local currency continuing to depreciate and inflation spreading, eroding real wages, deteriorating livelihood opportunities and rising extreme poverty. The electricity sector is also in a tragic state due to the destruction of a large proportion of electrical infrastructure, power plants and networks, in addition to the high prices of oil derivatives and the difficulty of obtaining them in light of the crisis, wars and sanctions imposed on the country.
The areas of Al-Duwadiya and Al-Jahman suffer from a complete loss of electricity in light of the struggle to repair the electrical system due to its high costs and the economic sanctions imposed on the country. Local residents rely on primitive means of lighting, such as lamps and alcohol.
The areas of Ma'arrat An Nu'man and Abul Thohur suffer from power outages to the streets, main and secondary roads, homes, and shops, in light of the difficulty of repairing the electrical system due to its high costs and economic sanctions. Residents of the area suffer from panic and fear when leaving at night due to the lack of road lighting. The risk of car theft incidents is also higher at night.
B. Intervention and Implementation of Activities
 Provide vocational and technical training courses for a period of 3 months through a cadre of specialized trainers to 75 individuals to work in these fields with work bags at the end of the training.
 Lighting houses (100 Families) in the areas of Dwadiyeh and Jahman.
 Road lighting in Ma'arrat An Nu'man and Abul Thohur : install environmentally friendly, easy-to-install and high-luminous solar panels In poles, (60 lighting poles).
C. Protection Risk Analysis
Abuse and exploitation in exchange for service delivery/high impact/mitigation: Dissemination of protection policy among beneficiaries and staff and provision of the possibility of complaint.
Tensions and conflicts between beneficiaries as a result of variation in their choice/medium impact/mitigation: clarification of distribution mechanism and provision of the possibility of complaint.
Physical injuries and trauma during installation of solar systems/high impact/mitigation: awareness of safety procedures with the provision of safety tools.
D. Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse Strategy
Al-Birr Association in Idlib adopts a code of conduct and a protection policy that all employees and volunteers in the association adhere to, and prohibits any behavior or procedure that indicates sexual exploitation, harassment or abuse, and does not tolerate any behavior or behavior that may affect the dignity or privacy of the beneficiary.
The Society has help and protection offices that receive any complaint in accordance with confidential procedures that take into account the safety and security of the individuals submitting the complaint. Providing training related to protection policies and procedures for individuals and beneficiaries if necessary.
E. Risks, Assumptions and Mitigation Mechanisms
Equipment theft/medium impact/mitigation: Appointment of a guard, installation of surveillance systems and constant security scores.
Difficulty of access of trainers and staff/medium impact/mitigation: selection of a cadre from the residents of the area.
Fires and natural disasters/high impact/mitigation: provision of extinguishers and emergency exits.
Material damage when transported and stored/Medium impact/mitigation: provision of standard conditions in trucks and warehouses.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Intervention and Implementation of Activities 1. Vocational training in the city of Ma'arrat An Nu'man: The area was chosen due to the relatively large return of the population. The project aims to provide vocational and technical training courses for a period of 3 months through a cadre of specialized trainers to ensure access to the needs of the market, and it is sought to fully prepare 75 individuals (50 men - 25 women without a breadwinner) to work in these fields with work bags at the end of the training. 2. Lighting houses in the areas of Dwadiyeh and Jahman: The two areas were chosen due to the relatively large return of the population. The project includes the provision of solar energy systems for 100 families classified among the most needy. Each system includes a 550W solar panel, a 40A charge regulator, and a 150A battery. 3. Road lighting in Ma'arrat An Nu'man and Abul Thohur : The two areas were chosen due to the relatively large return of the population. The project aims to install environmentally friendly, easy-to-install and high-luminous solar panels on poles, in the main streets and public places (30 poles in Abul Thohur area and 30 lighting poles in Ma'arrat An Nu'man).
