Project Summary

The Goal of the Broadening Emergency and Graduation Efforts (BEGE) - Borno Program is to deliver lifesaving, life-sustaining interventions and enhance adaptation of 524,719 displaced, returned and host households to shocks and systemic constraints within crisis-evolving Borno State.
the sub purpose is the enable 9,000 people from the most food-insecure, conflict affected IDP, returnee and host households have access to sufficient, nutritionally adequate, and diverse foods.

1 - Needs

To enable 9,000 people from the most food-insecure, conflict affected IDP, returnee and host households have access to sufficient, nutritionally adequate, and diverse foods.

2 - Activities

BEGE Borno will provide unconditional Food Assistance via the E-voucher system to 1,500 vulnerable households in Damboa (IPC 3 -- Crisis) host communities, particularly because of the gaps in targeting people in need, as well as the high impact of the 2022 flooding. Over the last two years, Mercy Corps has been working to reduce food aid dependency in Damboa and BEGE Borno offers a pipeline for effectively transitioning these households.
