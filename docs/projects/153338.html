Project Summary

This project is targeting conflict affected host community, returnees and IDPS living in Yei , Morobo, Kapoeta North and Renk counties who are at high risk of gender based violence,death, injury and life threatening diseases like HIV/Aids, STIs and reproductive health related illnesses aggravated due to violence. The primary purpose of the project is to prevent protection risks and to provide timely response services to men, women, boys and girls affected by conflict .The target population is 12200 individuals broken down as 1000 boys and 4000 girls, 6200 women 1000 men. The project will be implemented in a period of 12 months and is costed at a budget of $ 440,000 .CIDO will implement this project directly in the target locations in collaboration with the target population, the local administration ,line ministry and partners on ground to achieve maximum project results. The project activities are designed to prevent and respond to GBV. This are; Case management / Individual PSS, community Outreach and safe referrals. GBV risk mitigation, Operations of WGFS, engagement of women , youth and adolescent girls and boys in GBV programming. The project dedicates 30% of the budget to cash based interventions. It is expected that by the end of the project period, lives of vulnerable women and girls will be saved, they will have increased access to services and positive life skills and positive coping mechanisms entrenched in them. It is anticipated that boys and men will be better informed on GBV prevention mechanisms in emergencies and will make meaningful contribution to the population needs of safe and, dignified lives.

Needs

UNHCR needs assessment report of July 2018 indicates a displacement of 60-70% of the population in Yei town . Over 163,000 boys, girls men and women from Ture, Lasu,Otogo and Mugwo payams fled to Uganda and Congo while an estimated 50,000 individuals have fled to the capital city of Juba. The remaining population 30-40% ( 46000-61300 individuals) live in urban Yei and can be found in Hai Tarawa , Lomoko,Hai Nyakaba, TTC and Hai Gabat suburbs. This number includes Some of the IDPS originating from Morobo , Mundri and Maridi. Located on major routes from Sudan to Paloich and an important crossing to the West Bank of the white Nile , Renk has been at high risk of conflict-related displacement. According to REACH assessment report of hard to reach areas, The county is hosting both IDPs and repatriated population as the area is percieved as a transit site and returnees have not been able or willing to return to their areas for origin. Abayok boma in Renk is sheltering about 5,000.Renk county is an underserved location with absence of GBV partner and services. HNO projections for 2019 indicate that border towns like Morobo county are expected to receive an influx of returnees while Kapoeta North is expected to receive returnees from kenya. Currently CIDO is providing GBV services to 800 returnee individuals composed of women, girls, men and boys on transit to Morobo The Renk Report further highlighted protection threats targeted at civilians -including killings, abductions, sexual violence, forced recruitment and mistreatment by armed groups.There is widespread trauma among men and women because of the existing protection threats .There is need to liaise with partners to ensure girls, women boys and men access psychosocial services within the GBV minimum package per IASC guidelines. There is need to enhance coordination with local authority, frontline service providers to strengthen local protection mechanisms and orient them on protective emergency response for both adult and child survivors .This will ensure that beneficiaries adopt positive coping mechanisms.

Activities

• Activity 1: Conduct Comprehensive case management (Psychosocial support (PSS), Safe referrals). CIDO will engage competent and qualified case workers to provide case management services in line with the IASC guidelines for GBV. Referrals will be channeled through referral pathways in each location. Case workers will follow up with survivors and support the M&E staff to establish levels of client satisfaction in regards to access to services in order to fully meet their needs. • Activity 2: Provide lifesaving information including information on available services; This activity will be coordinated with other sectoral partners to ensure agencies deliver accurate and harmonized messaging. For example this can be done through establishment of referral pathways. Messages will be translated into local languages and disseminated with respect to age and cultural sensitivities of the targeted beneficiaries• Activity 3; Support Women & girls friendly spaces programming, including strengthening integration with child protection to support child survivors; CIDO will build on the current work at the existing WGFS to replicate in other proposed lcoations and ensure continuity and sustainability of the existing WGFS. Apart from lifeskills , and PSS services ,the WGFS will provide a platform for women and girls’ peer engagement and information sharing on critical aspects like SRH. In coordination with child protection partners , CIDO will design appropriate approaches to deal with Child survivors• Activity 4: Provide Legal awareness, assistance and counseling for survivors (including training police and justice authorities); CIDO will build on its pro bono legal services and scale up provision of legal services to SGBV survivors. At the same time police and justice authorities including traditional justice mechanisms will be trained on the South Sudanese legal framework• Activity 5:Establish women's safety committees: CIDO in coordination with other protection partners will encourage communities to establish committees to promote safety nets and advocacy initiatives for the protection of women and girls rights• Activity 6: Conduct Community awareness raising and sensitization on women’s rights: CIDO intends to establish a multi sector taskforce to be tasked with community awareness alongside male champions .The taskforce will deliver life-saving information and messaging on key protection threats affecting women• Activity 7:Engage female and male youth as agents of change for gender equality through grassroots campaigns.- The youth will be encouraged to join available protection mechanisms like the existing protection committees to provide messages at household level on GBV prevention and response.• Activity 8:Coordinate with child protection partners to provide lifeskills and SRH education to adolescent boys and girls; The project will adopt a modality of providing comprehensive case management to child survivors and provide mentorship to adolescent girls and boys • Activity 9:Conduct training of male and female community leaders on Peacebuilding and peaceful co-existence ; The training will achieve the objective of mitigating imminent conflict among returnees, host community and IDPS as a result of competition over scarce resources, land and property ownership. • Activity 10: Provide conditional cash programming to GBV survivors: CIDO will use an internal criteria to provide restricted cash vouchers in Yei and Budi counties to support GBV survivors access livelihoods .

Are messages context-specific or/and tailored?

Yes

Briefly describe how

This project activities has been tailored to fit the South Sudanese context in respect to various community cultural values. The project has therefore factored in gender and age appropriate activities to address needs of different sexes and age groups. Secondly CIDO is keen to ensure that the appropriate staff ( male and female ) are engaged to deliver the planned activities depending on sensitivities related to culture across the beneficiaries’ target locations. The project give allowance for separate and confidential discussions with different community groups (including gender and age disaggregated groups)

Protection from sexual exploitation and abuse

Yes

Briefly describe how

CIDO has a functional policy on PSEA . The policy has been signed by all signed by all staff in respect to the 6 core principles of PSEA.The respective project staff leading this project will be guided by the standard operating Procedures (SOP) of PSEA and where applicable will use the community based complaint Mechanism to address cases of SEA. CIDO will ensure that PSEA IEC materials are used and are visible in all designated areas of service delivery. In collaboration with other partners and I recognition that CIDO is an active protection partner, efforts will be consolidated to conduct capacity building of frontline workers on PSEA and extend applicability of SOP to other NGOs at grassroots level

Centrality of Protection in humanitarian action
Protection Risk Analysis?

Yes

Briefly describe how

CIDO being an active Protection Cluster partner has conducted a protection Mapping and risk analysis exercise of the targeted beneficiaries. Both exercises provided essential information on groups and individuals who are at risk, the reasons why they are at risk and how, while taking into account the specific vulnerabilities that underlie these risks. The project therefore take into consideration specificities and unique needs for men, women, girls and boys, and groups such as internally displaced persons, older persons, persons with disabilities, and persons belonging to sexual and other minorities and most importantly how to mitigate the identified risks.

Barriers to Access?

Yes

Briefly describe how

In reference to the risk analysis exercise, it is noted that some beneficiaries could be falling under the elderly, Physically handicapped or under Persons Special needs (PSNs) categories. Under such circumstances, the project activities are designed to give priority to safety and dignity of all men, women boys and girls and prevent negative coping strategies and mitigate risks including issues of discrimination in regards to accessing services. Through regular coordination meetings, partners on ground will be encouraged to train the frontline workers on disability mainstreaming and also report and share protection concerns for persons with specific needs.

Addresses inequalities or protection threats?

Yes

Briefly describe how

In regards to addressing protection threats ,CIDO will be guided by the four principles of South Sudan HCT Protection Strategy.(2015) i.e • Prioritize safety and dignity, and avoid causing harm,• Equity and Meaningful access, • AAP • Participation and EmpowermentCIDO will be guided by the protection mainstreaming tool kit (PMT) to address protection inequalities. The goal is that potential protection risks are identified from the outset and mitigated.CIDO will conduct protection assessments and risk analysis, stakeholders mapping, and needs verification with clear AGD data to understand context dynamics, protection risks, vulnerability level and inform respective partners depending on needs, access, safety, and gap findings. This will be done through focus group discussions, key informant interviews, meetings and HH surveys with women, men, children, institution workers, community leaders, within IDPs, host community and returnee population. CIDO will also conduct awareness raising by use of IEC materials for protection specific messages.as well as advocate for other sectoral partners to conduct NFI distributions at HH level to address needs of people with disabilities, elderly and chronically ill. CIDO will be promoting equality through recruitment of men and women on various level of activities as deemed necessary.
