Project Summary

Currently (July-September 2021), an estimated 2.2 million people are classified in Crisis (IPC Phase 3) and Emergency (IPC Phase 4) and need urgent humanitarian assistance. This number is expected to increase to 3.5 million people in Crisis (IPC Phase 3) and Emergency (IPC Phase 4) between October and December 2021 due to multiple risk factors. Below average rainfall during the forthcoming October to December Deyr season could worsen the current situation and could potentially evolve into a major drought by early to mid-2022.
The lack of essential WASH services means a serious public health risks and preventable deaths. The Somali communities, in particular those living in Hirshabelle region of Somalia. The project targets the most vulnerable populations including IDPs, returnees and marginalized host community members (women,men, boys and girls) including those with disabilities and the elderly that are discriminated from accessing essential WASH services.The project shall promote the equitable participation of both men and women in the planning and decision-making processes, this will ultimately help to ensure that the entire affected population has safe and easy access to water supply, sanitation facilities and services, and that such services and facilities are appropriate.
The main objective of this intervention is to provide safe drinking water and to improve on the sanitation aspect of the residence in the target districts by distributing water guards and water tracking systems. We also intent to reduce the transmission of and exposure to water borne diseases (AWD/cholera) amongst targeted at risk and vulnerable IDP populations. This shall be achieved through the provision of access to safe water, adequate and appropriate sanitation facilities and services as well as promoting hygiene awareness practices. The project also aims to ensure that the targeted communities are sensitized, mobilized on best practices to prevent water and sanitation related diseases (AWD/cholera). The program will see to it through the staff that we give special consideration to people living with disabilities and for the tracking as well as other entitlement we will ensure that we adequately reach out to them through the community workers. The latrines will have special consideration to people living with disabilities.
RRP will seek to rehabilitate and restore the damaged latrines and enlighten the society on the best response to the recurrent damage and loss of property that has been experienced over time in the region.

Risk that beneficiaries are exposed to violence during registration/distribution as a result of the distance to or location of humanitarian assistance and service: Mitigation Measure : Enhancing security at the project sites by hiring security staff as well as ensuring that WASH services are located at places within clearly defined WASH cluster sphere standards 2. Risk of women, girls and boys exposed to sexual violence, including sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA), when accessing humanitarian assistance. Mitigation Measure: Ensuring all staff sign PSEA code of conduct and adhering with it as well as establishing and strengthening SEA reporting procedures for its effectiveness 3. Risk of no access to or awareness of protection assistance or referral services for survivors of protection incidents (i.e. GBV, child protection, evictions, explosive hazards, etc.) Mitigation measure: Conducting awareness on protection assistance for survivors of protection incidences 4. Risk that marginalized groups, persons with disabilities, women, boys and girls, do not participate in consultations or decision-making processes for the project or are excluded from humanitarian services/assistance/activities Mitigation measure: Conducting comprehensive awareness for persons with disabilities, women, boys and girls and ensuring their inclusion and participation in decision making processes as well as active participation on all project phases/stages right from initiation throughout implementation including monitoring, till completion 5. Risk that beneficiaries are not accessing/using complaint and feedback mechanisms Mitigation measure: Establishing beneficiaries complain and feedback mechanisms and conducting awareness targeting the beneficiaries for utilization of the same with frequent organizational review.
