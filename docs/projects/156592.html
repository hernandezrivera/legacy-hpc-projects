Project Summary

The Humanitarian Needs Overview indicates that 92,000 children are at risk of health status deterioration and death due to acute malnutrition. poor feeding practices increase serious risks to children’s’ health and wellbeing. Without appropriate treatment of acute malnutrition, a malnourished child will be 4-11 times more likely to die than well-nourished child. About 128 thousand pregnant and lactating mothers are already acutely malnourished which subject them and their children to diseases and death. Therefore, INSAN CHARITY is proposing nutrition project to ease the suffering of people in the displacement areas in Idleb govern orate.
The project seeks to offer humanitarian response and lifesaving nutrition care services to internally displaced population, IDPS in the camps and host communities affected by the conflict in Almaara and its rural , Ariha , saraqeb .
The intervention will specifically target vulnerable populations such as women, particularly of childbearing age, PLW, infant and young children, and children under five years in areas classified as 4 severity according to nutrition cluster statistic.
This project consists of 12 community health-nutrition workers who carry out their activities in two different methods :
1- community level ( IDPs and host community ) is targeted with the following activities :
a) IYCF practice counseling for Pregnant and lactating mothers and caregivers (through home to home visits) and IYCF practice promoting ( group sessions in community gathering where mothers as well as other family members are targeted including men to insure maximum impact )
b) malnutrition screening
c) follow up and referral according to CMAM program
d) distribution of nutrition supplementary supplies at household including high energy biscuit, plumpy doz and micro-nutrients / vitamin supplementation,
2- The nutritional facility ( Al-Salam Center ) which focuses on
a) malnutrition screening
b)distribution of nutrition supplementary supplies
c) IYCF good practice promoting (group sessions)
d) treatment of malnutrition
e) referral
11077 pregnant and lactating women, and 30433 children under five years will benefit of this project

Needs

4.7 Million girls and boys under the age of five years and pregnant and lactating women are at risk of undernutrition and in need of comprehensive curative and preventive nutrition services to continue during 2019. Around 92,000 children under the age of five years are expected to suffer from acute malnutrition during 2019, out of which 19,000 girls and boys are at risk of death due to severe malnutrition if not treated appropriately. Without appropriate care, acute malnourished children are eleven times more likely to die than well-nourished children. 865,295 girls and boys under the age of five years suffer from micronutrient deficiencies. It is estimated that 1.6 Million mothers will require maternal nutrition services and skilled support for optimal infant feeding and caring practices. Chronic malnutrition was a problem in Syria even before the crisis and increased rates have been observed recently. The increase in chronic malnutrition potentially reflects long term inadequate dietary intake, including inadequate micronutrient intake, as well as repeated infections in younger children, poor feeding practices due to low rates of exclusive breastfeeding in the first 6 months of life, and inadequate complementary feeding in terms of diversity and frequency. Maternal and child malnutrition is impacted by deterioration in security in some areas such as Idlib and rural Aleppo, increase in needs among returnees and internally displaced people, as well as coverage of nutrition programs such as in underserved North-East Syria and northern Rural Aleppo, including Afrin. - According to IYCF practice assessment that INSAN CHARITY nutrition team has done through ( provision of life saving nutrition services ) in May, June and July 2018 In 3 different areas ( Maaret Annuman, Ariha ,saraqeb ) in Idleb governorate , the assessment shows a very poor IYCF practice among mothers , and the major four indicators of IYCF practice ( early initiating of breastfeeding , exclusive breastfeeding for first 6 months , introducing proper complimentary feeding and continue breastfeeding to two years ) unaware by most mothers .- According to malnutrition screening done by CHWs team in project (provision of life saving nutrition services to IDPs in Idlib) in May, June and July 2018 in 3 areas in Idleb( 4582 boy and 4304 girls were screened ) the result of acute and moderate malnutrition was ( 34 males – 60 females ) And 1589 PLW were screened the result of moderate malnutrition was 100 cases- Families of children with cases of acute or moderate malnutrition are facing a very limited option when seeking treatment with very limited number of PHCs which provide that kind of treatment and follow up in the target areas ( families are less willing to put the effort to seek the treatment when the health facility is far)

Protection Risk Analysis

Violence against Health Facility and nutrition services Providers: Mitigating measures: - Applying the designated de-confliction mechanism for health facilities, - Continued reporting on attacks against healthcare.risk of GBVMitigating measures: - involve women and other at risk groups as staff and leaders in the planning,design,implementation and monitoring of nutrition activities.- include a case worker as part of nutrition staff who is specialized in in GBV case management. - place posters and other GBV messages in nutrition service delivery points.-Engage males ,particularly leaders in the community,as agents of change in nutrition outreach activities.risk of PSEA: Mitigating measures: - A PSEA policy describes standard of behavior for organization’s staff and representatives, specifically forbidding sexual exploitation and abuse, and inclusive to accountability measures. - Effective community based complaints mechanisms, including victim assistance. - Effective and comprehensive mechanisms should be established to ensure awareness raising on SEA amongst personnel, with continuous training on the topic. - Effective recruitment and performance management of all humanitarian staff. - Provide information to communities and people affected by crisis about the organization, the principles it adheres to, how it expects its staff to behave, the programmes it is implementing and what they intend to deliver.provide training for nutrition staff on issues of PSEA ,GBV and women's/human rights.
