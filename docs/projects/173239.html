Start Date

End Date

31/08/2021

Project Summary

Address the basic needs of displaced families and improve the protection and education outcomes for Colombian children and their families affected by the armed conflict in Tumaco, El Charco, and Barbacoas in the department of Nariño
O1: Strengthening children and communities’ resilience and coping mechanisms in areas affected by armed conflict and enhancing community based protective mechanisms.
O2: Boys and girls with urgent protection needs are identified, receive psychological first aid and are referred to relevant services.
O3: Out of school children and children at risk of dropping out, including those with disabilities, are supported to access quality, safe learning environments through provision of flexible, non-formal education.
O4: Teachers and other education personnel are supported to deliver inclusive, child centered education and develop skills to support children´s psychosocial well-being.
O5: Internally displaced families affected by conflict are provided with emergency assistance to address their immediate needs
