Project Summary

Based on the need assessment report conducted by SR in 3 camps in two sub-districts (Maaret Tamsrin and Dana) located in Idleb governorate Jan 2024 mentioned that all KII interviewees answered that there is a challenge in accessing clean and safe water for children and persons with disabilities (PWD) due to a lack of household water storage capacity.This affects their daily lives, such as a lack of drinking water and its impact on personal hygiene, 67% of interviewees confirmed that there were challenges in accessing clean and safe water for women and men.When SR asked them about why accessing clean and safe water is a challenge for women and men, they said it's due to a lack of household water storage capacity. 33% of the respondents mentioned that more than 50 individuals are sharing one toilet. In other hand All KII interviewees said that light for latrines, separated latrines for males and females, and extending a sewage network are the most urgently required sanitation interventions
Aiming to improve access to WASH basic needs in Northwest Syria, this project will achieve the following:
-Improved water quantity and quality leading to access to safe and sufficient water for men, women, girls, and boys from the IDP in the target camps.
-Improved sanitation systems leading to access to safe disposal of solid waste and wastewater for men, women, girls, and boys from the IDP in the target camps
SR will provide lifesaving activities to 135 camps in NWS.In other hand, SR will identify 5 water systems (both in NWS and NCS) in need for rehabilitation and water network extension. The activities of this project is summarized below: Water trucking, Provision of 2000 water tanks, Rehabilitation of 5 water stations, 5 Water network improvement, Operational cost for 5 water system inc (Electricity bills, chlorine, salaries for operators, connection with electricity grid.etc),Desludging, Maintenance, repair and installation of 200 latrines including PWD, Solid waste management, Provision and distribution of 2000 garbage bins,5 Sewage network improvement, Rehabilitation of WASH facilities in 5 health centers including medical waste management and incinerator,Rehabilitation of WASH facilities in 10 schools, Hygiene promotion activities and distribution of 5000 HKs
35L/P/D of potable water for drinking,personal and domestic use as per WASH cluster recommendation will be provided either through water trucking or rehabilitation of water system. The supplier will chlorinate all water trucks and distribute it into the water tanks not less than 30 minutes after adding the chlorine and the field team will collect a water sample from the provided trucks before starting the distribution to ensure that the FRC is matching the standards
2000 water tanks with capacity of 500L will be distributed in the target camps.
SR will rehabilitate 5 water systems using of solar systems approach for operation. Along with improving 5 water network and connect them with the rehabilitated (or existed) water station, beside that, SR will provide operational cost(Electricity bills, chlorine, salaries for operators, and connection with electricity grid)
The wastewater will be desludged from the septic tanks to the nearest public sewage network. SWM and installation of 200 latrines including PWD will be provided along with 2000 garbage bins 100L
5 Sewage network willbe implemented
WASH facilities in 5 HC including medical waste management and incinerator will be implemented, these centers are supported by Health in SR. and rehabilitation of WASH facilities in10schools.
Hygiene promotion activities will be flowed by KAP survey after identifying the gaps. Environmental factors are central to this action. Sludge disposal will align with the sewage system endpoint, ensuring proper waste management. Opting for a solar system at the water station emphasizes environmental friendliness. Simultaneously, regular boreholes inspections will monitor water levels for sustainability
