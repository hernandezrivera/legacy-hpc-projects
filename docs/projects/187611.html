Project Summary

This project seeks to improve the livelihood situation for the olive farmers in Idleb countryside three sub-districts of Kafr-Takharim, Armanaz, and Jisr Ash-shughur through conducting the integrated olive management . where the general character of the targeted area is characterized by olive cultivation as a main source of income.
the project will target 1500 olive farmers throughout during two consecutive seasons all of them from small ownership between 5 -8 donum.
First season 2022 will target 750 farmers by providing the agricultural inputs, intercropping, services and trainings of olive trees with estimated budget 413,500 USD$.
And in the next season 2023 the project will target the other 750 olive farmers with the same inputs, intercropping services, and trainings. with estimated budget 413,500 USD$.
The project concentrate on four main activities:
- Providing the agricultural inputs (fertilizers, harvesting tools, pruning tools).
- Providing legume seeds for the intercropping between olive trees.
- providing the agricultural services of the olive trees (tillage, harvesting service, pruning service).
- providing the agricultural training beside the agricultural guidance for the olive farmers.
the total direct beneficiaries of the project are: Olive farmers 1,500HHs. Unskilled worker (3,000)HHs as a harvesting worker with total workdays 30,000-day work. Skilled worker (750) HHs as a pruning worker with total workdays 15,000-day work.

4 - Needs

The estimated sector PiN for 2021 – of food insecure plus those at risk of food insecurity – has increased from 9.8 million to 14.2 million. The evidence highlights an alarming deterioration, caused by a combination of factors including currency depreciation and associated loss of purchasing power, considerable hikes in food prices, large population displacements, lack of livelihood and income generating options, losses in agricultural production and assets, all compounded by ongoing conflicts and the COVID-19 pandemic.In North-west Syria, the widespread conflict in 2020 led to loss of farming and cropping land, which limited crop production, which is being worsened by high agriculture input prices.Amal and through conducting RNA found the following:There is need to support the olive value chain starting with olive farmers, who need the inputs and services, which cost more in compere with the production and small properties.There is need to provide Machines (Tractor, plow, grinder) for olive farmers to overcome the plowing cost (which usually conducted with two cross tillage’s 4 time in year), and to grind the pruning residues.Provide agricultural extension and training especially in the field of managing pruning residues, integrated olive pest management, building olive farmers associations for information exchange, support collective marketing, and conduct services collectively to decrease the cost of production.

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

The project will engage the target beneficiaries in the design process, planning and activities implementation. So, the project targeted both host community and IDPs in the all project stages.the beneficiaries are from both women and men with priority for the following families: orphan, female headed household, family with disability person, elderly, huge number of children under five. the project will consider gender issues during the training session by recruiting a female for training, large hall, training time, ..etc.same thing during input distribution to allow women receive the inputs and service in appropriate way. the project will conduct complaint mechanism to receive and solve any issue in manner time.the percent of the targeted women in the project consider the activities nature so the unskilled workers will be mostly from female.


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

The project will follow strict procedure during the implementation periods, by distributing place and time to appropriate the activities nature and gender, women will receive the inputs in the first half of the day and second half of day will allocated for men. the project staff will include female to insure smooth reach for the targeted women, and to avoid any kind of sexual exploitation and abuse
