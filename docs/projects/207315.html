Project Summary

The proposed action aims to improve access to quality health services to girls, boys, adolescents and Youngs affected by lake chad crisis in the far north region specially IDP, Refugees and host communities. The objectives of this intervention is to ensure increased access to information about SRHR for adolescents through strengthening the community protection mechanism, provide support to adolescents and young who are disproportionately affected by emergencies and crises due to their development, dependence on adults, limited participation in decision making, lack access to SRHR information to achieve their sexual and reproductive health and rights; organise the capacity of service providers and health structures about adequate attitudes and skills on adolescent- and gender-responsive SRH service provision; support parents and caregivers as well as members of the project's target communities to be able to access quality care, information and services for their children in accordance with gender standards; ensure that children, adolescents and young people receive comprehensive, high-quality sexuality education in safe and non-violent learning environments. By Considering the socio-cultural factors and gender inequalities in the Far North region, the project plans to increase advocacy efforts to promote the rights of children, especially those of young girls who are exposed to forms of discrimination and gender inequality. The project targets both younger adolescent girls (10-14 years) older adolescent girls (15-17 and 15-24 years) affected by the conflict.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The most felt needs in the health services can be grouped as follow:- The non-existence of reproductive health and sexual rights services for young people and adolescents in existing health facilities. - Lack of confidentiality in reproductive health and sexual rights services for young people and adolescents; - Constraints linked to the patient circuit and the treatment process. - Ignorance and lack of awareness among boys and girls of the importance of urological and gynecological examinations; - The need for training providers in health care and sexual and reproductive rights; - The need for reproductive health kits (delivery kits, Caesarean section kits, dignity kits, family planning kits, etc.).- Poor reception by healthcare providers; - Incomplete service packages. To meet these needs, several activities will be implemented, including the following:- Activity 1. Assessment of existing health structures for adolescents' and young people's access to sexual and reproductive health (SRH) services. - Activity 2. Support health facilities with SRH-related supplies and products important for adolescents and young people.- Activity 3. Rehabilitation of health facilities in particularly remote and conflict-affected settlements (in selected health facilities, ensure that there is a safe space for adolescents to integrate nutrition and SRH topics and services).- Activity 4. Establish a referral system for adolescent- and youth-friendly, gender-sensitive SRH services, including GBV and nutrition services in target communities.- Activity 5: Train staff in the existing adolescent life skills training program, including adolescent and youth-friendly sexual and reproductive health services (+ referrals to public health centers). - Activity 6. Train and supervise female community health workers (CHWs) on sex education to ensure that correct information on sexual and reproductive health and rights is communicated in a way that is appropriate for adolescents, and not harmful). - Activity 7 Organize sex education sessions with adolescents, young people and parents, including community and religious leaders.- Activity 8 Organize dialogue sessions with parents, including religious and community leaders and traditional healers, on issues of SRHR and GBV among adolescents.- Activity 9 Development and distribution of updated IEC materials for use by health providers to increase adolescent access to SRH services (first step: mapping of existing IEC materials available and validated by Ministries of Health).- Activity 10 Train Plan International staff and local partners involved in SRHR activities on "Conversations that Matter" to support alignment of staff values and attitudes with Plan International positions on SRHR.
