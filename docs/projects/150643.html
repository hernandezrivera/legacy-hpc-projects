Project Summary

The project aims to enhance safe access to inclusive early education which was extreme negatively affected by the current situation in the Gaza Strip.
The project will target 15 KGs in south Gaza (East Khanyounis, West Khanyounis and Rafah) and will work through a set of activities toward:
- Enhance appropriate and safe learning environment for pre-schoolers.
- Strengthen emergency preparedness capacity of KGs based on UNICEF Emergency preparedness Guidance-Note 2016
- Improve the psychosocial situation and trauma response capacities of vulnerable children.
The project will last for 24 Month targeting 15 KGs in south Gaza and it will be implemented in a partnership with 3 local partners.

Needs

The situation of Gaza Strip characterizes a complex protracted crisis that is not traceable to a single acute shock but rather is a constant state of high vulnerability driven by a combination of inter-related factorsThe economic situation in Gaza has deteriorated since the salaries cut for the PA employees and Gaza government in ability to pay its’ employees except 40% of their salaries causing deteriorating of the economic situation and increasing the rates of poverty; as an impact the Education system was one of the most affected educational operate In 2018 the Protection cluster estimated that in Gaza 295,913 children and 190,279 caregivers would be in need of psychosocial support. The series of intense exchanges of fire in recent months had devastating PSS consequences on the population.Based on Reality of kindergartens in South Gaza needs assessment conducted by EducAid in October 2017 and updated in February 2018 targeting 46 KGs in the southern area of Gaza Strip the results showed a significant decrease in the number of enrolled children in KGs compared to the last 2 years: the decrease reached around 20%; 60% of which is related to girls’ enrollment. Especially those enrolled in KG1, recent update done in September 2018 showed a new decrease of the number of enrolled children reached 9.8%, 54% of which is related to girls(30% in KG1), in addition only 23% of the children completed KG fee for the 1st semester, 59% pay less than 50% of the fee, 18% paid nothing The assessment also highlighted as an impact of the financial crisis significant absence of educational resources for KGs, high demotivation among teachers and education personnel; affecting negatively teachers’ performance and their ability to improve students’ learning outcomes. In addition to the significant decrease in parents’ and community involvement in supporting development of their children, in ability to fulfill their children educational needs like uniform and materials, worrying indicators of potential increase in the dropout rates, increase in the negative coping mechanisms among teachers and parents as an impact of the financial crisis. In light of the above and according to the recent study implemented by IRPAL which mapped Gaza Strip most vulnerable localities in terms of, Education, Protection, WASH, Shelter and Economic vulnerability, it was found that Rafah and KahnYounis are the highest in the overall vulnerability 68% and 65% respectively• Reality of kindergartens in South Gaza needs assessment findings were presented in the Education cluster in 2017

Activities

2.1.1 Identification of children at risk (dropout) agree with KG Exempt them from fees compensated with what the project will provide the KG poorest children2.1.2 Conduct winterization activities focus in providing children in need with winter clothes, shoes and bags in order to allow them to attend KG with high protection during winter.Project’s staff in collaboration with the KG principals, and KG committee will identify girls and boys to be targeted in 2.1.1 and 2.12 based on specific criteria will be developed by project steering committee2.1.3 Provision of educational materials: in addition, the project will support the KGs with educational needed materials to put into practice teachers’ trainings skills2.2 Provide essential and basic emergency rehabilitation to KGs to ensure that school environments are safe for students.2.2.1 Provision of upgrading activities to targeted KGs based on KGs needs including rehabilitation, furnishing and outside toys.In order to allow the adoption of adequate inclusion practices and their proper implementation, KGs will benefit from structural/non-structural rehabilitation works including WASH facilities to upgrade the KG premises and facilitate with a special focus on interventions promoting the accessibility of KGs for CWD. KGs will also be supported through the purchase of furniture and outside toys responding to the main needs identified during the initial assessment.Implement by: EDUCAID 2.3 Supporting conflict affected KG children with disabilities (hearing and visual) with assistive devices.EducAid will support CWD (hearing and visual) enrolled in the KGs with needed assistive devices. The children will be identified through hearing and visual screening all KGs children.Implement by: EDUCAID and Al Amal for Rehabilitation 3.1 Support the most vulnerable kgs in Gaza in responding to emergencies through structured preparedness packages and post emergency responses based on UNECIF Emergency Properness guidance-Note 20163.1.1 establishment of an emergency committee (composed by 2 KG staff, 2 parents and 2 community members) in each KG to provide KG with support during emergency situation. And train the committees on the National emergency plan in Education sector.3.1.2 Trainings of KG principals, MOE representative and KGs’ emergency committee members on Risk analysis, scenario definitions, preparedness action, child protection principles and EIE to support the establishment /improvement of a child friendly and protective environment, definition of child friendly safety practices and contingency plan in case of emergencyImplement by: EDUCAID3.2.1 ToT training will be provided by educational experts from CEIS (Italian–Swiss educational center) to experienced educators who will then train all KG teachers, Kg principals and specialized educators on child protection principals and psychosocial support of children in emergency situations through the Diamond Kite Project (DKP) approach.3.2.2 Realization of coaching activities for structuring psychosocial support interventions through the approach of Diamond Kite Project within Kgs .Implement by: EDUCAID and PECEP3.2.3 Conduct psycho social support for children and cwd through recreational activities led by experienced animators, girls and boys will be supported in expressing their emotions and discovering their abilities and potentialities. The recreational activities will be carried out based on Play Bus methodology which developed by EducAid and implemented through several interventions in Gaza the main was funded by OCHA.Implement by: Al Amal For Rehabilitation3.2.4: Awareness raising for parents who will be subjected to 12 sessions based on COPE methodology.Implement by: PECEP3.4.5 Organization of drama activities in kindergarten: the activity will involve mothers, teachers and children of targeted kindergartens.Implement by: TDP* referral pathway will be followed to refer children in need for deep PSS.
