Project Summary

This project will capitalize on Migration Services and Development's (MSD) vast experience providing services and humanitarian support to migrants and refugees across Lebanon over the past several years. In particular, MSD has been active in supporting migrants since the 2019-2020 political and economic crisis began in Lebanon, which has left many vulnerable migrants homeless, without work, and with an increased risk of protection based violations. MSD is also supporting refugees cases by providing them legal counseling, and legal assistance and representation mainly GBV and CP cases. The project will cover protection services for 300 of the most vulnerable migrants beneficiaries, including protection case management, psychosocial support, and legal guidance, support, and representation. The project will also support with voluntary repatriation legally and through provision of tickets for 200 individuals. In addition, the project will provide humanitarian assistance in the form of NFIs and food parcels to an additional 300. Finally, the project will engage in preventative measures like awareness raising and community outreach to reduce the number of migrants who become victims of trafficking and other abuses under the Kafala system with a target of reaching 4000 individuals- this will also include information on legal services. MSD is currently operational across the entirety of Lebanon, with permission and access into prisons to support migrants both in and out of detention; for out of detention cases, work is centered in Beirut, Mount Lebanon, and the North. MSD provides aid through its community outreach staff, social workers, and lawyers who are trusted and known within the community. MSD also has strong relationships with other stakeholders, such as embassies, consulates, and Lebanese governmental agencies including general security to support with legal issues and voluntary repatriation.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

This project falls in line with the overarching sector goals for migrants. Under strategic objective 3, "To enhance timely, unhindered, and equitable access to protection assistance for migrants", MSD will provide legal assistance, including awareness, counselling, and legal aid to migrants vulnerable to exploitation and abuse, and will provide protection support, including MHPSS, to persons facing or at risk of abuse. This work will be carried out by MSD's social workers and lawyers who have access to migrants both in and out of detention centers and prisons. Under the sector objective, "provide multi-sector lifesaving assistance to migrants in vulnerable situations", MSD will also provide food parcels and NFI kits, in line with sector guidelines, to the most vulnerable. Finally, MSD will support the sector initiative of voluntary repatriation for migrants who wish to return to their home country.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

Reducing the risk of protection violations and maintaining trust and confidentiality is of utmost importance to MSD, particularly due to the sensitive nature of much of the work surrounding legal cases. MSD staff members work with individual cases to determine vulnerability & needs, conducted through interviews and home visits, including those in prisons. MSD staff members work to ensure that the activities target the most vulnerable and enhance safety, dignity, and promote and protect the human rights of beneficiaries without contributing to or perpetuating discrimination, abuse, violence, neglect and exploitation. Cases are referred to MSD through partners and other stakeholders including embassies and consulates, or found through ongoing work. MSD accepts cases regardless of gender, ethnicity, religion, or any other factor.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

MSD maintains a strong monitoring and evaluation system. MSD has an already developed accountability system including PSEA which allow affected populations to address concerns and complaints. MSD also rolls out its standard code of conduct that is linked to the beneficiary feedback mechanism to ensure that suggestions and complaints can be received by the organization. Such input will be dealt with in a structured manner as outlined in MSD’s work process regarding beneficiary accountability. Beneficiaries are informed of the result of their input after the validity of the input was evaluated and in case of sexual exploitation and abuse or child abuse allegations thoroughly investigated. MSD has complaint boxes with posters to explain their purpose, and employees always explain to new beneficiaries about this process. In addition to the complaint boxes, MSD makes sure to display the code of conduct, the CSG policy, and children's rights conventions in all its locations.
