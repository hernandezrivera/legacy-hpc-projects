Project Summary

We will focus here in this center on displaced children (60%) and host children (40%).
We will coordinate with health centers in the area to refer children to them when needed.
We aim to provide a safe space for displaced children that includes recreational activities as well as educational services for children.
Child-friendly spaces are a safe environment to support children’s rights and well-being under emergency circumstances. Through the Protection Sector of MIDAD foundation provides protection to children by creating safe spaces through which they can participate in organized play, adaptation and self-expression activities. The safe spaces raise awareness about risks to children, mobilizes community capacity to begin the process of providing a healthy and safe environment for children, and provides information on the various services and support available in the region.
There are several reasons why the Child Protection in Crisis Program is critical. Children are the most vulnerable groups, and their dependence on adults for care leaves them vulnerable and vulnerable.
Child protection activities have a positive impact on society, as these activities are designed to target children, and those groups are responsible for protecting children from all kinds of violations.
This Centre is going to target at least a hundred child in the area.

4 - Needs

Safe Spaces for children, including Child Friendly Spaces (CFS), are interventions used by humanitarian agencies to increase children’s access to safe environments and promote their psychosocial well-being. In fact, the Syrian children, after ten years of the war in Syria, the children have suffered the hardships and displacement that they have suffered, which negatively affected their psyche.There are great needs for low-level psychological support activities for these displaced children from different areas, as well as for the members of the local community itself.In addition to the children not feeling safe from the chaos in the areas, in addition to the aerial bombardment sometimes, which led to the emergence of a feeling of fear and terror among the children.It was necessary to provide a safe, child-friendly space that includes recreational activities for children to distance them from the atmosphere of war and psychologically rehabilitate them from what they suffer, in addition to educational services in the kindergarten stage.

5 - Implementation of activities

Approximately 200 children from the displaced and host community in the area will be targeted.Transportation will be provided for these children to the center five days a week, and there will be an activity coach and a social supporter for the children to help provide the activities on a daily basis.
