Project Summary

Support the humanitarian community in implementing the Humanitarian Response Plan (HRP) 2024 in Burkina Faso and the clusters in implementing their actions, programs, and interventions throughout the year. A further escalation was observed in 2023, with a more significant toll on human lives and vulnerable populations with a massive increase in Hard-to-reach Areas (Sahel, North, Centre Nord, East, West, Cascades, Haut Bassin, and Southwest). The Logistics Cluster will coordinate all necessary efforts to deliver assistance to these areas where people are the most vulnerable. In 2024, humanitarian logistics actors (grouped in the Logistics Cluster and supported by UNHAS and The Logistics Cluster Lead Agency WFP) will aim to support humanitarian actors in implementing their actions, programs, and interventions.
In terms of Coordination, the cluster will undertake, in collaboration with key partners, the following coordination activities:
- At the national level, the cluster will maintain representation in decision-making forums, including the Intercluster Coordination Group, CMCoord, Access Working Group, etc., and conduct bi-monthly coordination meetings.
- At the regional level, the Logistics Cluster will participate in the meetings of the regional ICCGs and will be in the main hubs of Ouahigouya, Kaya, Dori, and Fada. Information Management The logistics cluster will continue to ensure the provision of information on operational issues for storage and transport) and (ii) access issues (provision of access constraint maps and ad hoc recommendations if necessary). Other tools will be provided, such as SitReps, contact lists, infrastructure, and logistics services assessments. The logistics cluster has begun to establish five operational hubs in Ouagadougou, Ouahigouya, Kaya, Dori, and Fada. In addition, it will prioritize its resources in 2023 and 2024 to support the efforts of partners who target the most affected populations.
- Temporary storage: the logistics cluster offers a temporary storage service in the capital and the regions, open to all humanitarian organizations in Ouagadougou, Ouahigouya, Kaya, Dori, and Fada. In the regions and remote areas, the Logistics Cluster will facilitate the deployment of Mobile Storage Units (MSUs) or smaller units to benefit partners who request and manage them.
- Road transport Subject to physical and safe accessibility, the logistics cluster will also be able to coordinate/consolidate partners' requests for humanitarian cargo and facilitate access to a dedicated fleet of trucks operationalized by WFP and the private sector to support partners' projects’ implementation.
- Inter-agency humanitarian cargo to Hard-to-Reach Areas. A system for providing logistics services will be set up between the Logistics Cluster and the humanitarian actors who will use the service, which will include a cost-recovery procedure.
–The Logistics Cluster coordinates the delivery – by air – of humanitarian aid to areas that are difficult to access for certain items/sectors identified as priorities: Water, Hygiene and Sanitation, Food Security, Shelter, Health, and Nutrition. Helicopters, operated by UNHAS, carry out rotations to priority geographical areas. The defined areas served and the goods that can be chartered are established based on priorities defined by the Humanitarian Country Team and the Intercluster Coordination Group.
Training The Logistics Cluster will facilitate access to training aimed at developing the capacities of logisticians present in Burkina Faso in various fields of humanitarian logistics (supply, warehouse and inventory management, transport, integrated management of humanitarian corridors, etc.). In addition, it is essential to increase the number of logisticians experienced in emergency response and humanitarian coordination mechanisms.
