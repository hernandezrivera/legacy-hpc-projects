1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Caritas Pemba will help a total of 3000 households, internally displaced people. The programme will help people to improve living conditions by implementing food security and livelihood projects for the displaced population, both native and returnee, in order to restore dignity to the host communities in Cabo Delgado province.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The project will be implemented in the districts of Ancuabe and Chiure, and aims to promote agricultural activities through the production and productivity of food and cash crops, without forgetting the distribution of agricultural inputs, technical assistance and monitoring of activities to guarantee the expected results, which is access to food.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The risks will be identified at field level and the potential risks to be identified and mitigated (locations where the project services will be provided; gender balance in the project; the type of product or service to be provided. )-Light and facilitated access to people: areas or locations accessible to all beneficiaries including people with disabilities will be selected and conditioned to ensure non-exclusion without exclusion. Gender balance in the project: this component ensures that the project has complete data, for example: it is easier for a woman to talk to another woman to report something that is bothering her than to talk to a man, and this prevents cases in the community.Service to provide: identify based on the needs of the community, and avoid giving a non-food product without explaining how to use it.Promote talks in the community explaining the project and all the possible services to be made available, this prevents misperceptions and creates risks during implementation.Always work in coordination with local leaders, who will help to identify risks and mitigate them.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

Caritas is committed to integrating protection and gender into all programmes to promote safety, dignity and meaningful access, especially for those who may be most vulnerable and marginalised, including individuals with disabilities and the elderly. Caritas applies a framework to support the integration of protection principles into humanitarian activities. The project will be implemented based on the Global Protection Cluster approach and the principles of ensuring access to impartial assistance and services, especially for the most vulnerable and at-risk groups; accountability to beneficiaries; supporting the development of self-protection capacities; and ensuring "do no harm" by adapting activities to mitigate identified protection risks.Finally, during a monitoring visit, beneficiaries will be consulted on the degree of satisfaction and will be informed to donors and local government.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

As it is considered an inclusive project, people with disabilities will be the focus of Caritas, including the elderly, and will be the highest priority in the distribution of supplies to ensure greater security and protection of members, especially women who are the most vulnerable group. If there are people with disabilities and they have some leadership skills and the ability to do some work, such as registering, organising beneficiaries, writing reports, they can be included in the team at work.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The focus of the project is the construction and repair of shelters, which aims to deliver tarpaulins, construction material, in short, the entire shelter kit, depending on the situation of vulnerability. Field teams will be trained to explain to target communities how to keep the environment clean, avoiding littering in inappropriate places, as well as promoting the use of latrines, the use of local material such as timber/wood or bamboo and promoting debates to ensure the well-being of society as a whole.
