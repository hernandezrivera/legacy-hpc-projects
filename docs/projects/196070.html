Project Summary

Muzun Org (MHD), aims to continue providing GBV,CP,GP services in the Community centers established in Idleb -Harim (Dana,Sarmada,Qah,Salqin ) & Aleppo- Jarablus -Albab (Ghandorra ,Alaree ) to ensure that a Affected Population (IDPs and Host Communities) in those areas have access to information and GBV ,CP,GP services in their community, the number of CC is two each CC includes a WGSS aims to provide a Safe spaces where women, girls can socialize and strengthen previously fractured social networks in addition to polarizing men and boys especially PWD and elderly to the Centers also, the activities in the center will include the following:
1- GBV Activities-Awareness raising according to GBV awareness toolkit-designating trained protection points from the community that we will train on Sops, basic principles of protection and mechanisms for identifying and referring GBV cases -enhance the mitigation of the risks of GBV by training its teams working in all sectors and other humanitarian organizations 'teams working non protection on the basics of GBV and the identification and referral -train MHD teams on GBV SOPs and case identification procedures for GBV survivors and how to refer them specialized GBV service providers and GBV Awareness Raising Toolkit training developed by the GBV Sub-Cluster-establishing two WGSS inside the community center - provide GBV response services including identification and referral for GBV cases.
2- CP activities: PSS activities-providing parents skills programs for women and men - awareness raising and community events to prevent and respond to child protection issues for adult and Children according to child protection toolkit- child protection training for MHD team project 20 employees in each center with line child protection minimum standards, MHD will target for all this activities the children with PWD ,youth ,separated and unaccompanied in addition to orphans boys and girls focusing in all activities on the Children with Disabilitites, by making the CCs friendly for the CWDs, by having the CFSs in ground floor, having ramps, etc.. and all of the activities of the CP will be carried out by MHD trained team, in addition to mobile teams reaching to distanced areas and camps, also in the CCs MHD will have a front desk to provide information for Caregivers on the map services, and services providers in the different areas, in order for the caregivers to find all the services which they need, which might not be provided in MHD CCs.
3- GP activities:-Emergency response to new IDPs arrivals (PFA, receiving and orienting activities, Protection monitoring ,GBV cases referral, sharing information-provide awareness raising session for protection issues-provide individual assistance for specific protection needs ,MHD team will be trained on how to integrate PWD into CP, GBV, programs, in addition to rehabilitating spaces to be more suitable for receiving PWD, such as providing a wheelchair accessible WC in the centers and providing transportation.
This project will serve 25,000 Beneficiaries as direct beneficiaries in three sub clusters ( CP,GBV and GP IN tow governorate (Idleb & Aleppo ), ( Harim ,Jarablus ,Albab ) Districts and many sub -district and communities.

3 - Needs

Based on the needs assessment carried out by Muzun and reports of the Protection Sector and the GBV Group, the needs were identified to carry out this project. Also,looking at HNO report, we note in NW Syria: the deterioration of the economy, the spread of poverty, destruction, loss of housing and property, long and multiple cycles of displacement, poor living conditions, family separation, and the collapse of family or community support. Structures exhausted the adaptive capacities of individuals and communities and generated high levels of psychological distress. As a result, people resort to harmful coping mechanisms (including child labour, child recruitment, various forms of exploitation and child/forced marriage) while over-reliance on humanitarian aid persists.There is a risk for children without official birth certificates and CWDs, Elderly people are at risk of neglect, bullying by relatives, and lack of access to medical services.And based on the Voices from Syria 2022, In 2021, women and girls reported facing intimate partner violence to a significantly higher degree than in previous years, In the case of adolescent girls, physical and emotional violence and restriction of movements within the home are also perceived to be on the rise.reports of early and forced marriage as well.

8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

MHD staff have a duty to report any allegations of behavior that might be abusive or exploitative towards beneficiaries or staff. This applies equally to allegations concerning staff from another organization. Any Code of conduct breach related to SEA requires sensitivity and discretion in order to avoid harming those involved any further.1-Investigating PSEA whistleblowing, management suspicion, complaints from staff or beneficiaries, notifications from other agencies, police, or from external audits.2 ConfidentialityEfforts will always go into prioritizing the protection of the Survivor, his/her family members, witnesses, complainant(s), and any other individuals who might be affected by an allegation or complaint.All staff involved will aim to avoid causing any further harm during and following an investigation.3 Investigation Structure: are carried out by the Turkey office, A committee is formed of 3 people inside Syria to verify the information and submit a report to the Turkey office.The decision is taken by the Executive Director in consultation with the project manager and the HR Director in accordance with PSEA Policy.4 ReportingConfidentiality, informed consent, and the wishes of the Survivor of sexual exploitation and abuse will always be given priority.5 Recording and Reporting SEA Complaints with PSEA Network and appropriate measures must be followed to maintain the confidentiality of all data gathered
