Project Summary

This project is ongoing and its fund is secured. Strengthen the access of 180 most vulnerable households in Afrin to Basic Needs through 3 rounds (@ 3 months each) of short-term income generation via CfW rubble removal/irrigation canal cleaning. (Payment is attendance/daily based)
The selection of cleaning and light rehabilitation of irrigation canal linked was based on the feedback from olive harvesting scheme that IMDAT team implemented in 2021, and it is directly linked with FSL priorities. Farmers were identified as one of the main affected groups by the crisis, as lack of proper governance structure in place resulted in losing the access of farmers to subsidize inputs and the damage of agricultural infrastructure increased the burden on farmers where the production of their crops recorded a dramatic increase. The output of this result, 1.2.1, will help farmers in overcoming the irrigation challenge they face and will help them reduce the operational cost, relatively, on farmers ‘shoulders. The main source of water for these canals is Medanki dam which store water from Medanki lake that is located Sharan sub-district in Afrin district.

5 - Implementation of activities

180 households will be selected based on an application to work in 3 rounds each round three months (6 months rubble removal/irrigation canal cleaning and 3 months rubble removal/city cleaning). Each beneficiary will receive 100 USD on a monthly basis based on the last agreed SMEB value by the Food Security Cluster. The number of allocated beneficiaries under this result was developed in close coordination with agriculture office in Afrin. It is expected to help 200 farmers from this result, and clean around 20 KM of secondary canal. IMDAT will (1.1) Coordinate with the Agricultural Office to select irrigation channels that need cleaning. The application process will start by publishing an (1.2) Online COVID sensitive announcement followed by Verification (COVID sensitive) of application requests based on vulnerability and eligibility criteria (100% of selected applications). The program and field team will (1.3) Set work-plan and form the supervisor and beneficiaries’ teams. the CFW team leader will work on (1.4) Providing training to the CfW participants and distributing CfW tools. Then the selected teams will (1.5) Start the Working Cycle (3 months each). After each month, the payment will be issued for all the BNFs by (1.6) Verification of attendance and distribute payment tokens for the beneficiaries, (1.7) Cash disbursement. Finally, the M&E team will (1.9) Conduct PDM and end line assessment.

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

Based on lessons learnt from previous projects and to avoid causing any tension between the old IDPs, host community and newly IDPs, IMDAT team will keep monitoring the percentage of selected beneficiaries to ensure that around 20 to 30% of the selected beneficiaries are from host communities, if applicable, with maintaining the selection based on needs and vulnerability.Inclusion and protection risk analysis and related mitigation measures will be ensured throughout the project cycle at various stages and vulnerable groups participate equally and meaningfully in the project activities. The IMDAT team will do all the efforts to include families with special needs, people from marginalised groups at the different activities of this intervention. Further, host communities will be informed, consulted and included to reduce tensions between host communities and IDPs.
