Project Summary

In 2024, 16.7 million people (8.4 million female, 8.3 million male) are assessed to need humanitarian assistance across Syria, up from 15.3 million in 2023. Of the 16.7 million people in need, 5.5 million are displaced, including over 2 million who live in last resort sites. The reports show a general decline in the ability of the population to access livelihoods, as well as the inability of the population to meet their basic needs.
According to HNO 2024, people in Syria are sliding deeper into poverty and finding it overwhelmingly difficult to make ends meet, In
2024, 16.7 million people are expected to require assistance, the largest number ever since the beginning of the crisis in 2011.
The economic situation has deteriorated sharply due to insecurity and the deterioration of currency prices, which led to 88% inflation as mentioned by WFP minimum food basket price index, respectively. Furthermore, household income has been decreasing, which resulted in a widening gap between income and expenditures of 60%. Ultimately, due to many circumstances (mainly economic), 85% of households were unable to meet basic needs in 2022. More than 12.1 million people are estimated to be food insecure (69% of households have taken on more debt since mid-2021). 70% of households rely on buying on credit and 56% rely on borrowing to cover their basic needs. Additionally, more than 2 million children are out of school.
This project proposal aims to enhance youth access to livelihoods and job opportunities in northwestern Syria. The project will achieve this through two integrated activities that aim to create a qualitative shift in accessing new markets , taking advantage of the advantages of remote work for clients in other countries.
Project Activities:
1. Building soft skills capacity for youth:
• This activity will focus on developing essential soft skills such as communication, teamwork, problem-solving, and critical thinking.
• Training will be delivered through a combination of workshops, interactive sessions, and mentoring.
2. Vocational training in digital skills:
• This activity will equip youth with the technical skills needed to succeed in the digital economy.
• Training will cover topics such as web development, graphic design, social media marketing, and data analysis.
3. Awareness raising on freelancing and accessing new markets:
• This activity will inform youth about the opportunities available in the freelancing market and how to access them.
• Workshops and seminars will be conducted to provide youth with the knowledge and skills needed to find and secure freelance work.
4. Providing financial grants to support youth in establishing their own livelihoods:
• This activity will provide financial support to youth who have successfully completed the training and are ready to start their own businesses.
• Grants will be awarded based on a competitive selection process.
5. Business Development Training:
• This activity will provide training to youth and women on business model development, financial management, and other essential business skills.
• Training will be delivered through a combination of workshops, interactive sessions, and mentoring.
6. Linking Graduates with Employers:
• This activity will connect graduates with potential employers through job fairs, networking events, and online platforms.
• Internships and apprenticeships will also be facilitated to provide graduates with hands-on experience.
• Daily stipends will be offered to trainees during their internships to support their living expenses.
