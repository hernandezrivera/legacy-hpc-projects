Project Summary

Proposed interventions will provide lifesaving humanitarian assistance and protection to the most vulnerable people, emphasizing underserved areas with high severity needs.
Minber Alsham is planning to respond to 23,017 IDPs in 12 camps and 149,450 in 5 communities (Jalma, Kafr Safra,Jandiris,Bafloor,TalAlKAramej)to ensure an improved, sustainable access to WASH services and supplies, we will target 12 camps and 5 communities.
In line with the WASH cluster strategy, the project will participate in reduce the massive reliance on the costly non-sustainable direct provision water trucking, desludging etc, towards a more cost-effective, cheaper, and more efficient response. We will connect the targeted camps and informal settlements to the existing nearby water and sanitation system.
The project aims to rehabilitate the water network and power it with a hybrid energy system.
We aim to ensure improved access of host communities and IDPs in four communities in Afrin and Harm sub-districts through rehabilitation and operational support for the water system, we will rehabilitate four water stations divided into three water stations in three villages Kafar Safra, Jandairis, Jalma in Afrin district, and the fourth one will serve the Islamona camp Harim district.
Totally(149,450 individuals)will receive safe water services from this activity by coordination of the LCs to ensure sustainability after completing the rehabilitation and the maintenance of the equipment installed, we will support the establishment of a Water Management Unit(WMU)to support and ensuring the efficiency of the project after closing.For three months, also will support the running costs for the four stations(operations cost and tests of the flow of the well).In parallel, WMU members will receive technical training on operation and maintenance activities and implement a cost recovery mechanism. The collecting fees by the WMU after completing the project will be used to purchase the diesel for the generator and the spare parts.
We will establishment a sewage network reduce the huge reliance on the septic tanks and desludging and construct communal water tanks in targeted camps and connect it with water sources through construction of water supply network from available sources for all 12 camps for reduce reliance oncostly non-sustainable direct provision water trucking and .
Besides, the project will be including rehabilitation of the sewage systems in two communities(Tal Alkaramaej and Bafloor)that is gradually deteriorating due to damage and lack of maintenance and implement a new system to meet the critical need after the residents' numbers doubled in targeted camps the we will provide basic WASH and Hygiene services, adequate drinking safe water via water trucking(35 liters per capita per day)while assuring the water's quality through chlorination and tested.To address the gap in latrines numbers in the targeted camps by constructing 17 new latrines, gender-sensitive block in(Alzaitoun and Kortek camps).The awareness sessions will be integrated with distribution HK to displaced families in targeted camps. Along with this service, we will provide protection activities (Awareness Raising sessions, PFA, and PSS) through two mobile protection teams of 4 persons each.
The same beneficiaries who receive the Hygiene kits and Hygiene promotions sessions will receive the protection sessions. Other integration services will be delivered through this intervention like solid waste management, cleaning the latrines by hiring cleaners from both genders, constructing high elevated tanks, and relying on gravity distribution to the water points inside camps.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

1-Risk: Risk of theft of WASH rehabilitation/construction materials such as generators and construction materials (during delivery/storage/ after installation) by armed groups and used in conflict activities.Likelihood: Low . Impact: High.Mitigating Measure: MoUs to be agreed with local authorities/councils to ensure local ownership of materials and rehabilitated/new facilities.Promote cost recovery systems for longer term maintenance and ownership to infrastructure to promote community ownership. 2-Risk: Improper storage of hazardous supplies (chlorine and other) materials) - Possibility of explosion, human casualty and asset destruction.Likelihood: Medium . Impact: High.Mitigating Measure: Ensure proper storage of supplies with clear provision of care and safety consideration. Make sure that clear instruction for use is available for chlorine products primarily for household use. 3- Risk: Potential exposure to explosive hazard (IED/UXO/ERW) during assessment/ rehabilitation of wastewater infrastructure (WWTP, sewer networks).Likelihood: Medium . Impact: High.Mitigating Measure: Work closely with Mine Action actors in the area and conduct assessments/rehabilitation works only in areas declared as cleared; Conduct safety training with stuff and contractors; Add explosive hazard awareness material to your programming. 4-1- Risk: Sale or exchange of NFI kits at lesser value.Likelihood: High. Medium: High.Mitigating Measure:Consult community throughout program cycle to determine the reasoning behind the sale/exchange of assistance and solicit their feedback with regards to solutions.

7.1 - Please list any risks related to sexual exploitation and abuse that have been identified during the project design phase and any risk mitigation and prevention measures that have been adopted and built into the project

MSO has PSEA, Code of Conduct, Protection and Child Safeguarding policies and mechanisms to ensure every effort is made to protect all program participants. MSO s PSEA Mechanism is designed around four core components: Awareness Raising (for staff regarding power dynamics, risk and vulnerabilities etc.), Prevention (awareness regarding the existing controls which are in place and how PSEA is prevented), Reporting (how participants can report and what are the specific steps which need to be taken regarding SEA cases) and Response (how and what IHR will do when cases of SEA are reported). A similar mechanism is available for CP where staff is familiar with approaching and dealing with cases, suspected cases, child abuse, exploitation, or violence.
