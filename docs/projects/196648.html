Project Summary

The project aims to improve the food consumption for 900 HHs (4500 Individuals) the needy families from IDPs, returnees and host communities in Idleb governorate- Harm district, Dana sub-district.
The project will target the most vulnerable Households, with regularly food basket within 8 rounds of distribution, in average one food basket/family/month.
According to WFP and FAO recommendation, the food basket price is 65 USD$ and it contains the main food material (rice, bulgur, vegetable oil, green lentil, red lentil, chickpea, bean, pasta, tomatoes paste, and the other essential components)
The project will coordinate with the local councils and the worked organizations in the field to target the most needed communities and avoid any overlapping.
the project criteria are considering the needs, with priority for household headed by women, divorced, pregnant and lactating women, elderly, disability, and families with huge number.
Where the MEAL departments will design tools to identify the beneficiaries based on the project criteria, and conduct monitoring throughout all the project stages, and submit the MEAL and PDM reports to evaluate each round of distribution.
The project targets 900 families /4500 individuals/ of them 700 HHs IDPs and 200 HHs of Host communities, with an estimated budget 658,000 USD$. The fund to run the project is still unsecured and Amal is still seeking to secure the fund.

3 - Needs

The food security situation in Syria has drastically deteriorated since last year. In 2023, an estimated 15 million people will need some form of food and agriculture assistance, representing 68 per cent of the total population. At least 12.1 million people are estimated to be food insecure, including all 2 million people living in camps who are deemed to be 100 per cent food insecure, and 2.9 million people are estimated to be at risk of food insecurity. Above that the high risks of spreading the cholera disease, which has a direct consequence on vulnerable communities, increasing morbidities and mortalities, (UNOCH-HNO, 2023). The WFP remote food security monitoring and recent food security and livelihood assessment in Syria (FSA, FSLA) estimates that seven in ten Syrian families is food insecure with the highest concentration in Idleb (74 per cent).Amal finds and through Rapid need assessment FGD, there is a massive need for food support in Dana district, where hundreds of thousands of families from IDPs, Returnees and host community sever food insecure.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

The project will engage the target beneficiaries in the design process, planning and activities implementation. So, the project targeted both host community and IDPs in the all the project stages.the beneficiaries are from both women and men with priority for the following families: orphan, female headed household, family with disability person, elderly, huge number of children under five. the project will consider gender issues during the beneficiary selecting, verifying and basket distributing by inserting females in the project staff, coordinating with the community leaders, and families before and during activities implementation.The project will set and apply complaint mechanism to receive and solve any issue in manner time.However, the project may face the following risks:- Price changes and materials availability. Mitigation: Procurement materials in the proper time in one patch, buy from nearby or distant markets.- Control/ interfere person/s or groups in the processes of registration, verification, contracting, and distribution. Mitigation: AMAL will check the sanction lists for all BNFs, suppliers and workers, Distribute the Food basket only to the eligible BNFs.- Sexual exploitation and abuse to the beneficiaries/staff. Mitigation: Establishing a confidential complaint mechanism, train staff on the code of conduct and code of conduct in all staff contracts Address any SEA complaints


8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

The project will follow strict procedure during the implementation periods, by engage females in the project staff, and engage all stakeholders with targeted families in all project activities especially the distribution process.the project will select office inside the community near the public facilities and main road for easy access.the project will recruit a qualified MEAL staff to ensure high quality of monitoring, evaluating, and transparency.All these steps will conduct by the project are to avoid and prevent any kind of abuse or sexual exploitation.All actions are based on AMAL’s policies that ensure compliance. As such, all project staff at HQ and in the field attend awareness sessions regarding gender equality principles and are required to adhere to the code of conduct and gender policy by signing the related documents. Furthermore, the recipient will adhere to the GBV code of conduct whilst on programmatic level the complaint and feedback mechanism installed by the partner is monitored which ensures follow ups on safeguarding issues.
