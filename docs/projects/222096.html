1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project “Integrated approach of Education in Emergency” has been designed based on Terre des Hommes Italy (TDHIT) active participation to the Education Cluster and its past and current interventions. The project aims to address critical education needs in emergencies in conflict-affected areas of Cabo Delgado. In line with the priorities of the Education Cluster, the project activities focus on the Palma area. The intervention strategy includes training teachers on topics that facilitate the resumption of the educational process (inclusive education, education in emergencies, etc.) and providing educational and hygiene kits for children and adolescents.Key target groups:- 35 teachers (15 from host communities and 20 returnees) will benefit from training to improve the quality of teaching in emergency contexts.- 1200 youth (adolescents) will benefit from Alternative Education activities, such as life skills, vocational training, literacy, and numeracy and/or 430 children will benefit of the Provision of Teaching and Learning Supplies

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Teacher Training on TICC, MHPSS, GBV/VAC, Inclusive Education, DRR:The activity will focus on training of teacher on GBV and education in emergency in the Palma district. The activity will involve 35 teachers (15 from host communities and 20 returnees) Alternative Education: lifeskills, vocational, literacy and numeracy:The activity will focus on providing 200 youth with alternative education training based on the specific needs identified in the area of intervention. Specifically, priority will be assigned to training aiming at promoting income generating activities trough vocation training. The activity include the provision of small start-up kit that will be defined during the training sessions. Vocatrional skills training will be reinforced by lifeskilsls and soft skills training that are foundamental to allow youth to cope with the stress and trauma deriving from the external situation and invest their attention and capacities on productive activities Provision of Teaching and Learning SuppliesIn order to facilitate access to school to children returnees, the project will provide most vulnerable children with school and educational material. These kits will be aligned to the education cluster experience. Beneficiary selection will be based on predeveloped criteria and will see the involvement of school and community reoresentatives in order to ensure the transaprency of the process. At the end of the project 430 kits will be distributed Provision of Hygiene/Menstrual Hygiene SuppliesIn line with facilitating a safe return to school, the project includes the distribution of 1,000 hygiene kits to an equal number of female beneficiaries. The distributions will be carried out in accordance with the guidelines of the cluster, and the kits will consist of standard materials used in such cases, including informational flyers on sexual and reproductive health. Beneficiary selection will be based on predeveloped criteria and will see the involvement of school and community reoresentatives in order to ensure the transaprency of the process. At the end of the project 1000 kits will be distributed.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Gender inequality can reduce participatation: there is a need to ensure that girls have access to education and that there are no gender biases in the curriculum or teaching practices. The project team will address those cultural practices underlying this difference by campaigns on relevant topics (GBV, early marriages, etc.). - Violence: Palma has experienced violence and conflict in recent years. This can clearly have a negative impact on education, leading to dropouts and discontinuity in learning. The project will address the trauma that students may have experienced and provide a safe and peaceful learning environment, trying to recover learning gaps - Financial: The fluctuation of exchange rates can result in a decrease in the value of funds received for the activities. However, TDHIT will have a well-developed contingency plan that outlines how to deal with unexpected events such as funding shortfalls or operational issues. - Extreme climatic events and natural disasters: phenomena such as cyclones and floods are more and more frequent in Mozambique. The project will carefully monitor the climatic alerts. - Operational: There are supply chain risks, such as delays or disruptions in some educational or recreative materials. This risk can be mitigated by well-advanced planning, establishing strong relationships with suppliers, and having backup suppliers in place. Risks related to monitoring and evaluation, such as the lack of accurate data or the failure to use data to improve the program, will be mitigated by investing in robust M&E systems and ensuring that program staff are trained to use e collect data. Political: Changes in government policies or community leadership can have an impact in the programme implementation. To mitigate this problem the programme is aligned with the national education priorities. One of the possible political risks is including Schools being used as center for election and election census (prior to election day) which could affect and reduce even more the learning time.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The project was designed with the participation of the main stakeholders in the intervention areas in such a way as to be able to ensure its implementation without encountering resistance from local authorities and interested communities. these actors were consulted in identifying the beneficiary schools and the spaces where the needs in the area are greatest. To ensure meaningful access to the services provided by the project a series of measures will be implemented: - publicization and dissemination of the activities: the activities will be promoted in the intervention communities through communication campaigns that include information meetings with local authorities and the populations of the communities, door-to-door visits by project activists, involvement of school teachers, distribution of information and awareness material and on the beneficiary selection criteria. - selection of beneficiaries based on the real needs identified both during the project writing phase and during the implementation of the activities. In particular, the project staff, through an information sheet, collects the main information necessary to have the profile necessary to identify the beneficiaries of the action - identify schools as the nerve center of the activities: according to a consolidated methodology by TDH IT, schools are the starting point of the activities.- Collection of feedback from community actors and beneficiaries on ongoing activities: Particular attention is dedicated to a meaningful participation and empowerment of women. Women and girls are the most at risk. IDP sites and host community locations lack safety conditions and basic assistance, leaving women and girls exposed to ongoing risks of GBV. At the same time, for girls school participation and access to education is exacerbated by cultural barriers that do not see school as a priority. To ensure full access for girls to school activities, families will be made aware of the importance of school participation and the minimum conditions will be provided to ensure the participation of children, both girls and boys, in activities.

6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?The inclusion of people with disabilities is transversal to the proposed intervention. TDHIT has long experience in Mozambique and around the world in the inclusion of people with disabilities in its projects, always trying to put PwDs at the centre, from project design to its implementation, possibly with the direct involvement of PwDs associations. 7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

For the production of children’s activities, both recreational and educational ones, the use of recycled materials will be incentivized: not only will this stimulate the creative process of children, but also their green consciousness and their basic understanding of the anthropogenic pressure on the environment. The project will raise awareness about the causes and impacts of climate change and provide students with the knowledge and skills to adapt to its effects.
