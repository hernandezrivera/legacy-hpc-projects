Project Summary

The main objective of the project is to improve access to nutritious and diversified food at household level through climate smart agriculture, home gardens, small livestock, food processing and income generation through small businesses. Participating households will receive training on vegetable production at household level through modern techniques and environmental friendly way like the use of organic fertilizer and other pest control systems such as pheromone traps as opposed to pesticides. To ensure optimum utilization of the food from the vegetable gardens, Concern will support beneficiaries through participatory cooking demonstration sessions. Food processing, preservation of various types of foods and promotion and small business will improve household income. Vulnerable women will be trained on vegetable production and how to run small businesses.
The project will ensure dissemination of key information on climate smart agriculture, training on livelihoods targeting the most vulnerable population including pregnant and lactating women (PLW), husbands of the PLW, elderly people.
Activities includes:
i. Support households to grow seasonal vegetables to improve dietary diversity.
ii. Facilitate participatory cooking sessions to develop innovative nutritious recipes
iii. Support small livestock production and increase household income
iv. Capacity building for the small businesses to improved household income
v. Ensure cross-cutting issues including gender, disability, AAP etc. "

1. Needs
1 - Identified Needs

According to REVA-5 2022, by the end of 2021, 95% of Rohingya households are moderately to highly vulnerable and remain entirely dependent on humanitarian assistance. In the host community, 52% of the population being moderately to highly vulnerable. Inadequate food consumption in Rohingya camps still 45% and 51% of Rohingya households cannot afford the minimum expenditure basket (MEB). In addition, Infant and young child feeding practices are sub optimal as indicated by only 26.4% of the children meeting the minimum acceptable diet. Own production for vegetables in very low in both the camps and the surrounding host communities. The assessment shows a minimum income level in camps as well as in the host community. Specially, women are more vulnerable physiologically and economically as they have less opportunity in the involvement of income generation activities (IGA) and cannot take decision or contribute in the decision-making. The project will emphasize on the involvement of women in IGA as the will be the primary target beneficiaries. They will get necessary training, inputs and other technical support throughout the project. To increase awareness regarding women empowerment, husbands will be involved in the project through regular awareness session, meeting etc. As the project will be implemented in Cox Bazar a disaster-prone area, project will implement climate smart agriculture for the sustainable and environment friendly agriculture production. Organic farming method will be used to ensure less effect on environment and the households will be trained on other forms of pest control without the use of pesticides. The project will support the needs in both the host and camp communities and this might contribute to co existence between the host and the Rohingya communities.

2. Rationalization
2 - How is your project aligned to the Rationalization Principles (in particular reference # 3 - Adopt an integrated approach, # 4 - Ensure predictability and continuity)?

The project will take an integrated approach focusing on nutrition sensitive agriculture home and model gardening and small livestock rearing and small business support including food processing, preservation and selling to improve households' incomes. These activities will be implemented as an integrated approach, as the beneficiaries will be selected from the primary groups, all of which will get awareness session on nutrition sensitive and food security. Some beneficiaries will get direct support and others will get the knowledge through sessions, ongoing activities, demonstrations gardens etc. Both in the camp and the host communities linkages and referals will be made with nutrition specific activities, and other intervention especially on protection related matters. In both camp and host, the home gardening activities, along with the session on home gardening enhances knowledge and practices to ensure vegetable garden in the household premises that can be used beyond the life cycle of the project. Beneficiaries receive session on seed preservation to grow different types of vegetables without support from the project in future. For the host community, input support to most poor and needy households to start / restart small level business, which enable them to generate income at the household level and support their families. The gains of this project will be sustained by the beneficiaries as they retain the skills and continue the business by themselves with limited external support. The activities in the host community (vegetable production and livestock ) are implemented in collaboration with the relevent government authorities to ensure linkages and continued support even after the life the project. To continue this type of activities, Concern continuously builds the capacity of local partner.

3. Cross-cutting Themes
3.1 - Protection

Concern uses a protection, gender and safeguarding risk tool to identify protection risks based on available evidence and community self-protection measures. Concern proactively identifies mitigation measures with the intention to prevent those risks from occurring. Concern will design the activities in line with the need and suggestion of the community. During the implementation, regular session and mobilization meetings will be conducted with participation from the targeted community. Complaints and feedback response mechanisms (CFRM) for beneficiaries and non-beneficiaries is in place through CRM box, hotline number and direct feedback during session and meeting. To ensure protection mainstreaming, all staff and volunteers will be trained on the Concern code of conduct, Program Participant Protection Policy, Child Safeguarding Policy and anti-Trafficking in Persons Policy all of which provide a comprehensive safeguarding framework for all of Concern’s programs.

3.2 - Gender

This project will ensure an increased focus on gender mainstreaming. Concern will train all staff on gender equality, prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse led by Concern’s Gender Coordinator. To ensure gender mainstreaming in the projects Concern will ensure: - Inclusion of women, men, elderly, children as beneficiaries align with the different needs, interests, capacities and vulnerabilities. - Pregnant and lactating women will be primary beneficiaries for the provision of support, which will empower them for the decision-making and contribution. - Women and men (and girls and boys, as applicable) participate equally during the implementation, monitoring and evaluation of the project. - The project will also establish husband groups to engage them on maternal and child nutrition

3.3 - Age including child safeguarding

The project targets pregnant and lactating women (18-59 Y) and elderly (60+ Y) as the primary target beneficiaries. In addition, husband of the pregnant and lactating women, mother in laws are the secondary beneficiaries. Women, elderly and person with disability gets support for the agriculture, livestock, and food processing and small business activities. Concern has strong child safeguarding policy, which ensures safe participation of child in the project as indirect beneficiaries through other household members and ensure ‘do no harm’.

3.4 - Diversity

Concern promotes diversity in its every program and project. In this project, Concern will ensure inclusion of the beneficiaries for different age, gender, ethnicity, religion etc. During the design of the project, Concern target different age specific group as the beneficiaries and identify the vulnerability among them. One of the primary target of the project activities is to empower women and ensure their participation in the family and the society. Concern emphasize to include people from different ethnicity, class, religion, languages etc. This project has one specific outcome to reduce Nutritional Rickets, so that, Concern and its partner SARPV readily identify person with disability (including Rickets) and provide treatment and rehabilitate them. In addition, person with disability gets priority during the trade beneficiaries’ selection.

3.5 - Accountability to Affected Populations (AAP)

As part of AAP Concern will have initial meetings with the community and other key stakeholders to introduce the project its objectives and key activities. Concern will also share detailed information on Concern, code of conduct, program activities, entitlements and their rights. The CFRM system has complaint boxes in activity sites/offices, hotline number, email, and local help desk. All feedback/complaints provided by beneficiaries through the accountability mechanisms will be recorded and resolved. During the monitoring and evaluation of the project, community will be engaged such as in the outcome harvesting, impact analysis and for reprogramming.

3.6 - Does your project have any DRR/DRM elements and/or environmental issues and/or climate change mitigation measures? If yes, please explain.

The project considered DRR and other environmental issues during its design and will be aligned during the implementation period. As part of environmental protection, the project will provide agricultural support to the beneficiaries for the organic production of crops by incorporating organic fertilizer (compost), organic pest control mechanism (pheromone trap) etc. The project introduces different climate resilience method of farming and vegetable gardening to cope up from the risk of disaster. Concern also has plan to response in any natural disaster through this project and maintain close coordination during the preparedness for disaster response.
