Project Summary

The proposed activities under this project are designed and planned according to the needs assessed during Altamayoz work in those locations and the needs are well observed and documented through data collection and, in addition to depending on the facts and statistics driven by HNO, HRP which indicated that In 2024, 16.7 million people (8.4 million female, 8.3 million male) are assessed to need humanitarian assistance across Syria, up from 15.3 million in 2023.
Syrians are getting poorer as the economy continues its freefall. Since February 2023, the Syrian pound (SYP) has lost about half of its value against the US dollar (US$)
High inflation, currency depreciation, and increases in commodity prices continued to drive humanitarian needs in Syria. This resulted in an increase in poverty, reliance on humanitarian assistance, and ignore the health side.
The situation is characterized by an increasing number of people turning to basic services which are not there, making them more vulnerable, including individuals with disabilities. .
Recurrent disease outbreaks, waterborne diseases, a prolonged drought and water crisis, vaccine-preventable illnesses, and food insecurity are contributing to to rising mortality and morbidity, including increased rates of malnutrition and increased humanitarian needs. Rural Damascus, Governorates have gone from acceptable to stress Global Acute Malnutrition (GAM) levels.
Evolution of People in Need at health and Nutrition sector was 17.7 million at 2022, 21.2 at 2023, and expected to be 20.6 at 2024, 4,224,620 people of them is at Damascus and Rural Damascus.
this project aims at supporting health and nutrition status of vulnerable people in Rural Damascus and Aleppo. This will be done through comprehensive package of services in the fields of health and nutrition that includes providing medical consultations in internal medicine, pediatrics and gynecology, provision of medicine, referrals to surgical operations (through a well-controlled referral system that has been approved by several UN agencies), supporting normal delivery and caesarian sections, supporting physical rehabilitation, MHPSS consultations, health workers training, screening for malnutrition, provision of micronutrients to those in need, support PLWs with Nutrition CVA for nutrition outcomes. activities will be implemented in Aleppo- Jabel Samaan, and in Rural Damascus as a complimentary services with other services that Altamayoz provides in the same locations (Ein Elfijeh, Madaya, Markaz Darayya, Duma, Arbin, Babella, Kisweh, Qudsiya) noting that Altamayoz is implementing nutrition and health services in part of those locations in Rural Damascus (Arbin, Babella, Qudsaiya,) under SHF fund which will end in 14 March 2024, and Altamayoz is intended to continue providing those services due to the huge needs accompanied with hard economic situation. the target under Nutrition sector is 20000 including children under 5, PLWs, boys and girls children. the target under health sector for all services is 101500. People with disability will be prioritized and will be additionally supported by MHPSS services
AlTamayoz is planning to run 5 centers and 4 mobile teams in the targeted locations noting that Altamayoz has very good experience in running PHC centers and mobile teams as it is providing health care services through PHCs in partnership with UNICEF since 2014 and has implemented Mobile team modality to IDPs sites and to returnees during East Ghota response in 2018 under a grant from WHO, and through providing RH services in partnership with UNFPA.
Activities implementation will assure avoiding duplication as there is a strong database used for validation, crosscutting and statistics. AlTamayoz will also coordinate with DoH, SARC, and other actors in Aleppo and Rural Damascus to assure services complementarity and avoid duplication.

A PSEA policy that describes standard of behavior for staff, that forbidding SEA 3.Ensure awareness-raising on SEA amongst personnel Altamayoz has PSEA strategy that is introduced to all staff membersAll staff has to sign a Code of Conduct related to protection from sexual exploitation and abuse (PSEA).
