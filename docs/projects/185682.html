Project Summary

This project will be implement in Whykong Union, Teknaf to ensure regular, sufficient, equitable and dignified access for affected host communities to safe water for drinking and domestic needs. Also to ensure the change of potentially dangerous behaviors through participatory hygiene promotion activities and distribution of hygiene items with particular focus on contagious diseases.
1. Water supply; Installation of 10 DTWs (or small scale solar-powered piped water networks) in Whykong Union to provide safe water to the beneficiaries. Water quality will be tested quarterly and the result will be submitting to DPHE. To strengthen of Operation and Maintenance system in each community themselves, we’ll form ‘user committee’ for each facilities and provide training of management and maintenance. Also to make a system of ‘user committee’ to collect user fee and to manage a system of water facility maintenance and management by users.
2. Hygiene Promotion and Epidemic Control; Promotion of hygienic practices through community participation to improve overall WASH situation. Critical supplies of hygiene items (soap, water containers, Menstrual Hygiene Management materials, etc.) will be distributed, together with community education and mobilization activities targeting all age and gender groups at the targeted households. Engagement of communities in O&M of WASH facilities, waste segregation and preventing COVID-19 disease transmission will provide. A real time service monitoring and community feedback mechanism will be maintained to ensure a timely response for any WASH-related health risk. WASH non-food items will be procured and prepositioned to respond to any emergency.
*Note, support costs are higher because it is a small project.

1 - Identified Needs

During the dry season in Teknaf, the depletion of water sources has been cited as one of the causes of increased community tensions, and ensuring the quantity and quality of drinking and domestic water is an urgent issue. Eighty-eight percent of the people in the county get their drinking water from wells, but 23% of them said that even if their wells are 60 meters deep, they are not able to secure sufficient quantity and quality of water.Based on our needs assessment in ward 5 in Whykong Union, there’s 25-35% of villagers said that access enough and safe water all year round, also there’s still exist households which far from Dug well to fetch water around 40 mins in targeted area. Particularly in hills and areas where poor households live, access to safe water is still severe, and installing DTWs requires more depth and is more expensive than installing regular tube wells. Moreover, NGO/CBO installed DTW or solar-powered piped water networks installed, however, some facilities become private or once it will be broken, there's no maintenance by users and leave it. From these matter, we identify to create system to maintenance and management of user committee for sustainability operation and management. Also importance of manner of use public facilities and set up rules which decide by user committee.

Actiity1: Water supply; Installation of 10 DTWs (or small scale solar-powered piped water networks) in Whykong Union to provide safe water to the beneficiaries./ 10 Water facilities installed according to the standards of the DPHE, Number of people using the same Water facilities: 90 people in 15 households, water quality testing clear DPHE standards 10 DTWs. Activity2: Organize and training of well user committees for Water facilities./ user committee is organized for each Water facilities, users committee participate training session of management and maintenance&repairing of Water facilities, all Water facilities are properly maintained to use all year round, 150HH. Activity 3: Hygiene Promotion and Epidemic Control; Promotion of hygienic practices through community participation to improve overall WASH situation./ The incidence of waterborne diarrhea among households using Water facilities is reduced by half between the start and end of the project, 150 HH.
