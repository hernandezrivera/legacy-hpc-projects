Project Summary

This proposed project aims to reduce the morbidity and mortality due to COVID-19 outbreak in Bama , Dikwa, Ngala and Magumeri LGA among beneficiaries and communities, and nutrition services providers and mitigate the negative impact on the nutritional status of children , pregnant and lactating women and service providers wellbeing by adopting the various guidelines including IASC guidelines , Nigeria National guidelines , Global Nutrition Cluster guidelines and others recommended by UN Nigeria in establishing and running of various OTP and stabilization centers where children with severe acute malnutrition will be managed.
INTERSOS will implement an integrated Nutrition, WASH and Health program in order to limit human-to-human transmission, including reducing secondary infections among close contacts and healthcare workers, preventing transmission amplification events, strengthening OTP centres. The focus will be on infection prevention and control household and OTP levels, risk communication, community engagement and counter misinformation, and integration of WASH activities with nutrition activities.
INTERSOS will work with the Communities and other implementing partners within Bama and Magumeri LGA in early detection of COVID-19 cases in sites and surrounding communities.

1 - Needs

The humanitarian situation is expected to worsen due to the COVID 19, catalysed by the on-going stressors experienced by the health systems including non-functional health facilities, lack of health care workers, limited knowledge lack of previous experience on COVID 19 response and capacity to manage complicated cases of COVID- 19. Expanded food insecurity, massive population displacement, limited access to WASH infrastructures, and restricted humanitarian access, the COVID-19 pandemic is a major threat to the nutritional status of young children and mothers.Closure of all land borders leading to a disruption of food supply that, in turn, will increase food prices and exacerbate food insecurity; Compromised quality of care for acute malnutrition treatment if hospitals and health staff are overwhelmed leading to increased mortality; Suspension of IYCF and mother support groups, cooking demonstrations, mass MUAC screening, and MNP distributions due to prevention measures that do not permit gathering of people; Reduced coverage of curative and preventative health and nutrition interventions due to restricted movements and decreased geographical access; Suspension of nutrition programs due to national directives or measures, or complete unavailability of health system and/or health workers.

2 - Activities

INTERSOS is continuing its nutrition activities in both LGAs through the provision of aglobal package of CMAM. INTERSOS will adopt the nutrition sector recommendedguidelines on programing of nutrition interventions in COVID 19 context to implementactivities at the stabilization center and thirteen INTERSOS supported OTPs in Magumeriand Bama LGAs. INTRESOS response will be guided by the 3 main scenarios in regards toCOVID preparedness and response; 1. Preparedness – activities to be implementedbefore the widespread of the COVID 19 outbreak, focusing on prevention, needs analysisand preparing for the worst-case scenarios. 2. Scenario 2 (most likely) – This is based onan infection rate of 10% in Borno and 5% in both Yobe and Adamawa. The 10% in Borno isinformed by the high concentration of IDP camps in the State. 3. Scenario 3 (worst case)– This is based on an infection rate of 60% in Borno and 25% in both Yobe and Adamawa.The scenario is based on an assumption of widespread and uncontrolled communityinfection Screening, identification, and referral of children 0-59 months with acutemalnutrition (Mother/Family MUAC) INTERSOS will adopt the Mother/Family MUACapproach for all community screenings based on the Nutrition Sector Mother/FamilyMUAC guidelines, provide adequate training for Community Nutrition Mobilizers (CNMs)on COVID-19 prevention and Provide Community Nutrition Mobilizers (CNMs) withpersonal protection equipment (PPE) including face masks, hand sanitizers etc.Treatment of children 0-59months with severe acute malnutrition INTERSOS will continuewith the treatment of SMA cases without medical complications at the OTP centers. Thehealth workers will screen and triage all patients seeking services at the facility accordingto NCDC case definition. Suspected COVID 19 cases will not be treated at the OTP center,the surveillance team will be notified, patient will be isolated while awaiting referral to thetesting and treatment center designated for the management of COVID 19 patients withSAM in Maiduguri. INTERSOS will reduce the exposure of patient, care giver and thehealth worker by using MUAC only for anthropometric measurements in children for bothadmission and discharge criteria: o Admission criteria - <115mm(RED) or Oedema + oDischarge - > 125mm for 2 consecutive visits and no oedema with no medicalcomplications. The frequency of follow-up visits will be reduced to 2 weekly for thechildren with uncomplicated SAM by increasing the take home ration of RUTFs. Thecommunity nutrition mobilisers will deliver all the treatment of SAM withoutcomplications using a limited/ no touch simplified treatment approach. INTERSOS willalso provide hygiene kits adequate for the duration of restriction of movement applyingrecommended IPC measures while avoiding mass gatherings. MAM identified will bereferred to actors implement TSFP and BSFP within the catchment areas. Infection,prevention and control (IPC) in nutrition service provision sites- INTERSOS will set uptriage and screening area at the entrance of the health facility, all persons accessing thefacility will be required to wash their hands at the entry with soap under running water,screen for temperature and briefly interviewed on COVID 19 symptoms and casedefinition criteria. Social distancing will be ensured at the waiting areas and theconsultation rooms, patients matching with the case definition for suspected COVID 19case will be counselled on the importance of isolation and COVID 19. The patients will beheld temporary at the holding area which is well ventilated will referral and surveillancenotification process is commenced. All MUAC tapes and equipment used at the facilitywill be disinfected appropriately according to the NCDC guidelines. Waiting areassurfaces will disinfected with O.5% chlorine solution regularly.
