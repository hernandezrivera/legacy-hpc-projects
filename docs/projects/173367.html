1 - Needs

The SHPDO WASH Section will support the Areas of the crisis- Zone affected population, which includes conflicts people at risk of epidemics and floods, and other vulnerable residents to maintain and gain access to WASH services using Harmonize approaches. about 467,000 people via areas of SHPDO Intervention they will receive WASH Services in Darfur Region and Kordofan , Blue Nile ,Aljazera States the Target vulnerable residents. These vulnerable residents are prioritized based on the highest severity of needs related to WASH borne epidemics, floods, food insecurity, undernutrition situation, COVID-19, access to WASH services.

2 - Activities/Outputs

1. the 467,000 People in crisis-affected population will have access to basic water services. Under this objective, interventions will ensure Sef drinking water from an improved source with a collection time Reducing , including queuing. People targeted with basic water services will benefit from new installations ,Rehabilitation operations of the existing handpumps, water yards, water treatment plants, protected wells, and hafirs in the areas of the SHPDO intervention. 2. the 467000 People crisis-affected populations will have access to adequate Basics Needs , protected, and gender-sensitive and Vulnerability people the sanitation services. 3. The 467000 People of the crisis-affected will have access to the hygiene promotion services, water quality, environmental health interventions of SHPDO Operation Areas
