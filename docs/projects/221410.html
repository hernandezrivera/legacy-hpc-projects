Project Summary

The Coastal Aqua-Shield: Strengthening Climate Resilience of Water Infrastructure in Vulnerable Communities of Southwest Region of Cameroon aims to improve the reliability and safety of water infrastructure in communities impacted by both conflict and climate change, such as Limbe, Tiko, and other coastal areas. The purpose is to rehabilitate and upgrade water systems using climate-resilient designs, integrating green infrastructure solutions like enhanced catchment areas and wetlands to manage stormwater, reduce erosion, and protect water quality. The project targets community engagement and inclusive planning to foster sustainable water management and resilience against environmental and conflict-related challenges.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The Coastal Aqua-Shield project will address several key assessed needs in the vulnerable communities of Southwest Cameroon: Restoration and Protection of Water Infrastructure: The project aims to rehabilitate and reinforce damaged water infrastructure to ensure reliable access to safe water. Conflict and extreme weather events, such as floods and landslides, have disrupted and damaged water systems, making it essential to restore these facilities using climate-resilient materials and methods. Mitigation of Water Contamination: Heavy rainfall, poor drainage, and waste management issues contribute to water contamination. The project will integrate green infrastructure solutions like restoring catchment areas and wetlands to prevent erosion and stormwater runoff. This approach helps maintain water quality and minimizes public health risks, including waterborne diseases such as cholera. Increased Community Resilience: By engaging local communities in planning and decision-making, the project will promote inclusive and sustainable practices. This participatory approach ensures that infrastructure improvements align with community needs and contribute to resilience against future climate impacts. Policy and Sustainability Support: The project aims to influence local policies that encourage climate-resilient infrastructure investment and sustainable land management. This will facilitate long-term maintenance and protection of water systems, ensuring continuous service and reducing vulnerability to climate-related disruptions.
