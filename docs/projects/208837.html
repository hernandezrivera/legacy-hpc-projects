Project Summary

This project aims the provision WASH essential/emergency services for the most vulnerable communities (IDPs and Host communities) in order to contribute to the improvement of public health and support the WASH sector efforts in the reduction of mortality and morbidity rates among the displaced people in North and South Darfur. The focus of this WASH programming interventions will be delivered in conflict affected communities and institutions- including schools and healthcare centers. The program will also cover WASH in emergencies, to respond to the needs of people displaced by conflict or other types of shocks the selected localities in North and South Darfur. More than 323,000 vulnerable groups of people (women, children, youth, men and the disabled) will benefit. HDPO through this project will provide life-saving water, sanitation, and hygiene (WASH) services to the most vulnerable individuals with the goal of preventing loss of life due to dehydration or disease outbreaks. The response will include an emergency supply of chlorinated water, construction of water storage and distribution points, and the eventual repair and rehabilitation of available water sources. Necessary sanitation interventions such as emergency latrines, permanent latrines renovation, and solid waste management systems will be established accordingly. HDPO will also provide supplies like; soap, buckets, water purification tablets and menstrual management hygiene kits will be distributed in conjunction with risk communication and hygiene promotion messaging.

4 - AAP & PSEA
4.1 - How will the project share information and collect community feedback? Please select as many as relevant.
4.1.1 - How will the project use community feedback to improve and adapt activities? (open-ended/free text, required)4

Community members and project beneficiaries will have different communication preferences due to a range of reasons such as literacy, trust, culture, disability, other access factors, etc. The project will offer a variety of ways to allow the community members and beneficiaries to share their feedback and complaints, including different face-to-face feedback channels as well as the possibility to submit feedback and complaints in writing (and anonymously). This will helps build trust with community members/ beneficiaries and ensure that they feel comfortable and safe sharing their questions, feedback, suggestions, concerns or complaints. Feedback channels will be chosen in consultation with community members & beneficiaries including women, men, girls, boys, youth, older people, and people with disabilities and people living in remote locations, among others. The community members and beneficiaries communication feedback preference will then be monitored.
