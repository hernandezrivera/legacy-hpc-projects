Project Summary

In this Human Response Covid 19 Addendum Plan, Save the Children plans to reach out to 175 000 boys and girls including those with disabilities in 440 schools in 8 districts with a budget of USD 3 000 000. About 55 needy schools will be supported in each of the 8 districts. The districts include Mbire, Rushinga, Gokwe North, Gokwe South, Binga, Matobo, Beitbridge and Matobo. This is in the background of a massive Covid 19 pandemic that has ravaged all countries of the world. Zimbabwe to date has recorded 51 Covid positive cases, including 4 deaths. The Country has tested a total of 31 589 by the 19th of May 2020. Globally there has been 4,978,691 confirmed cases, 327,543 reported deaths and 1,886,259 recoveries by the 21st of May 2020. The threat of the pandemic has resulted in children closing their first school term prematurely to reduce the risk of the disease’s spread among children. To date children have been out of school for over 65 days, this has resulted in a lot of anxiety among care givers and students themselves. Save the children is making this application to increase its capacity to respond to the Covid 19 pandemic in a big way through the cluster coordination structures. In its response Save the Children hopes to conduct the following activities a) Disinfection of schools b) procurement of masks, sanitisers, disinfectants, soap, and buckets etc. c) Teacher training on conducting the teaching and learning process in the Covid 19 era. d) procurement of thermometers e) Training of health masters f) school catch up programmes g) School based hygiene competition h) supporting schools to meet the WHO guidelines and i) Psychosocial support activities for teachers and students. In all these activities girls will have equal opportunities to participation including equal access to benefits from the products of the projects. Programmes will be conducted in such a way that accommodates boys and girls especially those living with disability are reached in times where they are available to participate. All barriers for children to benefit or participate will be considered in the design of activities. There will be high participation by vulnerable boys and girls and their teachers and communities in the project. Accountability mechanisms will be set to ensure that the programme is conducted in a transparent manner.
Save the Children notes that It is imperative for children to have a safe return to school. Children should be afforded the opportunity to continue learning in school but only when it is safe to do so. As shown by the activities above Save the children’s 8 months programme will work to ensure that schools are ready to receive children when the government makes the pronouncement on the opening of schools. To begin with teachers some of whom will be health masters need to be trained on how to manage the new school environment. The training will be on the symptoms, prevention, mitigation and management of Covid 19 in a school environment. SC will support schools to maintain the highest standard of hygiene. This will include sanitizing and disinfecting classrooms very regularly, temperature checks, isolating children with signs and symptoms of a fever.
Schools will be supported with strategies to ensure that children have a safe social distance to reduce the transmission of Covid 19 in schools. Most of the trainings that will be conducted will be done online so as to reduce traveling for teachers and children, psychosocial support will be another important product in this project. Due to the prolonged absence from school children and their care givers are now under so much stress and trauma from deaths that may have happened In the family. The project will reach out to marginalised boys and girls including children living with disabilities.

1 - Activities

Objective 1: To ensure that school surrounding are safe and conducive for learning for boys and girls including those living with disabilitiesActivities:• Disinfecting schools - schools will be disinfected before children come and also twice or once a day depending on the availability of resources.• Procurement of hygiene kits for schools that include masks, sanitizers, disinfectants, soap, and buckets etc - these will be absolutely necessary to keep the virus at zero in schools. school cleaners will be trained on how to apply the chemicals and how they can also keep themselves very safe. All children will be education on very high precautions and hygiene measures.• supporting schools to meet the WHO guidelines - the WHO and the MoPSE guidelines will be yard sticks of progress in the fight against Covid 19 in schools SC's M and E department will work with schools to ensure that this is adhered to. indicators and milestones will be set and support and compliance to them monitored regualrly• School based Covid 19 hygiene competition - the school competitions are just to test children aware of the information on Covid 19. This will be done at school level high adherence to social distancing. This could be an art competition, poems etc• Training of health masters - teachers will be trained so that they can support children better armed with the correct knowledge.Objective 2: Promote emergency sensitive inclusive and sustainable teaching and learning processes in the schools.Activities:• Teacher training on Covid 19 and emergency sensitive teaching - this will help teachers be sensitive of the time as children were affected differently.• Teacher training on psychosocial support in the Covid 19 context - this will help support children during this difficult times• School catch up programmes - this is to help children catch up as they have lost a lot of time out of school. 3: To conduct adequate monitoring and evaluation services to support the Covid 19 Response in schools.Activities:• Conduct support monitoring visits to district offices and schools - this is to support school measure progress towards goals. • Weekly and Monthly reports on the progress of schools towards national and WHO guidelines adherence - this will help to produce documentation to be used in assessing progress.The activities above will be implemented in about 55 schools in each of the 8 districts where the project will be done. Save the Children (SC will be working with the structures of the MoPSE from the national level, provincial level, district level, cluster level right down to the schools. Teacher training will mainly be done through online meetings, the cascading methodology and the cluster approach. this will help minimize the risk of the risk of transmission of Covid 19. in each school a school coordinator will be chosen to be responsible for the coordination of the Covid 19 response at school level. With support from the school administration, a check list and the M and E support team indicators will be tracked to see that all project activities are being done and are achieving the desired results. in this project there will be a lot of procurements and deliveries. These will be done at school level to ensure that all children receive the intended projects for the school.. The school administration and the school coordinator will be trained on stock management so that stocks of things like sanitizers are stored somewhere safe and used economically and for the intended purpose. Accountability mechanisms will be set to ensure that corruption and fraud do not reduce the benefits to children..
