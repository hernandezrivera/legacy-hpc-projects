Start Date

01/01/2024

End Date

01/08/2024

Project Summary

The Project will consist in
- Training of 6community Mobilizers, 6-Supervisor and 150REP facilitators in peace, CP, fight against hate speech, On the running of REP Center, Identification and Referencing of children with special needs
- Allow 3000children and adolescents to have access to Primary education through courses in safe place with registered leasons followed up by trained facilitators.
- Identify children with special needs and prepare a file for Referencing
- Purchase of learning materials for 3000beneficiaries
Purchase of teaching materials for 150 facilitators
- Distribution of Learning Materials to 3000 beneficiaries children (4-17)
- Mobilisation of community and Local authorities for ensuring of protective learning environment for children/learners.
