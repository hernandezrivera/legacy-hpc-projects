Project Summary

For the past five years, Lebialem Division has experienced severe stagnation in education due to the socio-political crisis in the North West and South West (NWSW) regions of Cameroon.
Based on the operational presence of Queen Fogang Foundation (QFF) in this division, situation reports, needs assessments has in several occasions been carried out in the area of education to better comprehend the reality on ground. In Alou Sub division, numerous cases of boys and girls within the ages of 5 to 14 do not have access to school because of the lack of educational facilities. The crisis has left most of the school structures burnt, dilapidated or abandoned. The only existing formal Learning center in Alou is Catholic Primary school Kongho in Nwametaw Fondom which is over populated and also very far from most of the communities identified in this part of Alou Sub Division.
Different tools for data collection and analysis such as community Mapping, Key informant interview, Focus group discussions, sex and disaggregated data, field observation and individual story were all used in the different communities of Alou to better understand the education plight of the people.
The purpose of this project is to renovate some dilapidated classrooms in the only existing formal school center in Kongho found in Nwametaw Fondom, provide benches to comfortably accommodate the over populated children, encourage social distancing and make it conducive for learning. The two abandoned catholic schools in Nwangong and Ndungadet Fondoms needs to be renovated, benches provided and teachers recruited to enable boys and girls with no access to education to gain the opportunity of going to school. The beneficiaries for this pilot project are boys, girls, persons with special need within the ages 5 to 14 in part of Alou Sub Division, Lebialem.
Education is a sustainable development goal and a fundamental human right which is seriously experiencing setbacks in Lebialem Division.

1 - Besoins / Needs

This proposal addresses the education needs of boys, girls and persons with Special Needs of ages 5 to 14 in Alou Sub Division of Lebialem. Some of the needs entails: Encourage more education centers in areas of proven possibilities ( Example, the case of the two abandoned catholic schools in Nwangong and Ndungadet Fondoms) to enable boys and girls with no access to education go back to school. Renovate the dilapidated classrooms and assist with benches in the ONLY existing Catholic Nursery and Primary school center in order to accommodate the over-populated boys and girls and encourage social distancing. Provision of COVID 19 preventive materials to limit the spread of the pandemic Encourage the recruitment of teachers to ensure efficiency and effectiveness Provision of Basic didactic materials to facilitate the teaching and learning process Provision of emergency basic need assistance like food for the boys and girls in school
