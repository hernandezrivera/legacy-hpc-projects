Project Summary

Recurrent climatic shocks particularly serious floods, severe drought conditions, acute water diarrhea/ cholera outbreaks and protracted crises, exacerbated by effects of COVID-19 and devastating impacts of intermittent conflict and insecurity has impacted to circles of displacement with very high number of IDPs at the settlements in Baidoa and Burhakaba Districts, Bay as well as in Banadir and Lower Shabelle Regions where vulnerable IDPs and marginalized non displaced women, men, boys and girls including those with disabilities live in congested unplanned settlements where they are exposed to high risk of violation of their rights. Their plight is compounded by increased exposure to violence, exploitation and discrimination to humanitarian assistance and other forms of abuse worsened by very serious risks and threats to their safety and security as well as negative coping strategies that expose them further to rights violation at the settlements. Of particular concern is the fact that women and girls continue to face various forms of Gender based violence while children/boys and girls endure multiple protection risks and violations of their rights including family separation, sexual and physical violence, killing and maiming, psychosocial distress, child labor, widespread recruitment and use of children, forced and protracted displacements, limited access to humanitarian assistance due to insecurity and/or discriminatory practices and exploitation. Furthermore, children/boys and girls are burdened with responsibilities at home that deny them opportunities of attending to schools while young girls are subjected to forced and early marriages whereas those with disabilities face additional risks of exploitation, abuse and discrimination, isolation and denial of the assistance provided hence need for their increased protection. It's against this background that the project has been formulated to enhance and ensure appropriate and timely protection services to children/boys and girls as well as those with disabilities in the project targeted locations in Lower Shabelle, Banadir Regions and Baidoa and Burhakaba Districts. To ensure durable solutions, the project will strengthen existing protection services by putting into account distinct protection needs of children/boys and girls as well as those with disabilities by consulting with them and including them in the project activities right from the project design face, throughout the implementation period including regular monitoring till project completion hence sustained impacts. The project targets total of 7600 beneficiaries classified as 2530 women for General Protection and 2200 boys and 2870 girls for Child protection services including those living with disabilities.

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

1. The project implementation will ensure inclusion of minority and marginalized groups such as minority clans, IDPs, women, boys and girls, the elderly and Persons with disabilities in the consultations on the project right from the commencement and ensuring they are part of key decisions made at different stages of the project implementation. This will ultimately ensure that all needs are addressed by the project and cultivate a culture of project ownership through their commitment and participation in the project.2. Protection of women and girls from sexual violence by ensuring increased access to protection Services through timely reporting and provision of appropriate protection responses. The project will in addition ensure access to targeted beneficiaries by working closely with the community structures such as elders and local administration/Federal Government State's security apparatus. The project will moreover ensure security of the staff by ensuring that staff are recruited from the local community and are well acquainted with the local communities and the associated risks. Where necessary, they will be provided security by the security staff and the local police system as well as the Somali National Forces in case of of highly volatile situations. Furthermore, the project will be implemented in a very accommodative manner involving the targeted beneficiaries and the host community to mitigate any feeling of discontence by host communities that may result to any form of conflict from the project implemented activities. In addition, the project will be implemented under very strict adherence with PSEA policy by ensuring that women and girls and boys and those with disabilities are fully aware of the project activities and the expected benefits to them to mitigate any form of sexual exploitation and abuse by the staff and community leadership strucutures during the project. Moreover, the project will ensure that complaints and feedback mechanisms are in place for easy access by the targeted beneficiaries and other community members for timely reported.
