Project Summary

To manage the sustainable development under climate change, Burundian populations have long adopted practices to adapt to disturbances of climate change such as: staggering sowing to prevent any loss due to climatic hazards; planting crops more resistant to climatic extremes; conservation of crops and resources genetics and the practice of zero grazing for livestock farming. The Government and NGOs have initiated development projects in response to the degradation of natural resources and the environment and to deal with irregularities rainfall.
In addition to the enormous efforts of the Government, several technical and financial partners of Burundi and Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs) both national and international have invested, for years, in the development of watersheds and the fight against erosion to protect productive capital and hydro-agricultural infrastructure downstream from runoff and flooding. In 2013, the government has elaborated National Strategy and Action Plan on climate change. The objective of the climate change strategy is to strengthen Burundi's capacities and resilience to face the challenges of climate change.
As far as Ripple Effect is concerned, from 2014 through its projects, it has significantly participated in climate change adaptation and mitigation. Different actions such as tree plantation for reforestation of uncovered lands, sensitization on importance of zero grazing and providing crops that tolerate scarcity of rain or drought were undertaken. Farmers are supported to install an appropriate rainwater harvesting technology and capacity building to be able to produce even during the time of drought period and coping with climate change. In its upcoming initiatives, Ripple Effect Burundi is willing to continue support farmers who are in emergency of food insecurity as the farming season 2023 A was disturbed due to the irregularity of rainfall to enhance them to cope with climate change effects. The main fucus is providing agricultural inputs specially seeds that tolerate drought such as sweet potatoes with orange flesh, Irish potatoes, and vegetables seeds for marshland farming. The project will also support farmers with farming kits for emergency for the revival of their agricultural production aftershocks. The project will support farmers with conditional cash transfer. In addition, they will be assisted to initiate income generation activities to restore their livelihoods aftershocks to enable them to be resilient.
The project will target Batwa communities, the most vulnerable families or groups with low-income, disabled people and elders as these are more affected by climate change effects. Gender and social inclusion will be taken into account during all the processes of the project implementation to reinforce intercommunity cohesion.

La cohésion intercommunautaire auquel l'organisation Ripple Effect contribue actuellement; se fait dans le cadre de la promotion de genre et l'inclusion sociale. 4 - Cochez si le projet contribue à la préservation et restauration de l’environnement impacté par les interventions humanitaires

Yes

4.1 S’il vous plaît ajouter plus de détails pourquoi vérifié cette option

Le projet envisage les activités liées à la préservation et à la restauration de l'environnement impacté par les interventions humanitaires. Ces activités seront la sensibilisation et la mobilisation à la reduction de la production des gaz à effet de serre ainsi qu' à la promotion de l'agriculture durable. La régénération de la structure et la texture du sol par la plantation des arbres agroforestières et forestières seront la priorite dans la mise en oeuvre du projet. On insistera également à la promotion de l'utilisation des engrais organiques et réduire les engrais sythétiques. Quant à la protection des plantes contres les maladies et les ravageurs, on mettra l'accent sur la lutte integrée qui consistera à l'utilisation des produits phytosanitaires naturels et à la lutte par les pratiques biologiques tout en réduisant ou en supprimant complétement l'utilisation des produits chimiques qui nuient les etres vivants dont l'homme.
