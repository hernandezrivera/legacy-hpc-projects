Project Summary

Because safe overland and air transport options are limited, WFP will continue to run the United
Nations Humanitarian Air Service to enable humanitarian workers to reach most areas. The
service provides regularly scheduled flights to 50 destinations each week, with special flights for IRRM teams, special visits and emergency evacuations. It trains civil aviation personnel and
supports an air traffic management project to improve the safety of South Sudanese airspace.

Needs

UNHAS is the only common air service provider for the humanitarian community serving deep field locations, providing safe, effective, and efficient air transport to over 55 destinations on its regular schedule by helicopter and fixed-wing. South Sudan has poor and/or non-existent surface transport infrastructure, making air service the only viable alternative to access locations with the most affected populations. With no viable commercial alternatives to reach remote locations, UNHAS remains the only common service provider for the entire humanitairan community for the delivery of light humanitarian cargo and humanitarian workers to deep field locations. Over 90% of the airstrips in the country are unpaved. Only UNHAS has the aircraft with the right capacity to offer a common service that reaches these locations. Moreover, UNHAS as a common service, supports Inter-Cluster Working Group (ICWG) Rapid Response Missions and assessments to hard-to-reach locations.

Activities

Operate regular flights for the humanitarian community and conduct regular needs assessments to provide enhanced access to beneficiaries and project implementation sites through safe, effective, and efficient air services.
