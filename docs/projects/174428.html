Project Summary

The project is aimed to protect 2000 girls against child marriages through creating awareness among 2700 communities on legal knowledge to stop child marriages as well sensitization on girls protection through education in three districts of Sindh province of Pakistan . The project will engage adolescent girls and boys on promoting SRH and life skills education, mental health and positive pubertal development to reach 4000 girls and boys . the project will identify 100 needy girls to continue their secondary education who otherwise are vulnerable to the child marriages . The project will engage health providers to address GBV through their counseling and motivational influence to the people . The health and well being camps will provide face to face counseling to 900 girls and boys on health issues and mental health by the peer friends and health providers. The Community based protection mechanism will be established and strengthening to run girls protection campaign.

1- Project Activities

The project is aimed to contribute towards the already launched project to curb early marriage through complementing SRHR education component to reach 4000 adolescent girls and boys to prevent from early marriages through developing peer educators for awareness-raising on health literacy specifically SRHR. The project will reach out 2700 parents/communities ( 1350 families) through SRHR, health well being and mental health camps and counselling to change behavior and prevent girls against early marriages and towards access of girls to health facilities and information related to RH in 90 villages of three districts of Sindh province of Pakistan. 180 adolescent and youth peer educators will be trained through 6- Three days long cascade workshop on leadership skills , RH and peer education skills. The trained youth will deliver sessions of the topic in their respective areas with fellow male female students in separate sessions in communities and will youth will also deliver sessions with male female parents on worst impact of the child marriages and improve health care system. The project will contribute towards the goal through additional and more effective strategies to leave positive impact on the lives of the girls and will prevent them from GBV. The 100 girls in need of transportation support to continue secondary education who otherwise would be vulnerable to child marriages and violence. The 90 Community Based girls protection systems will be established , trained and strengthened to sustain the impact of the intervention. The 90 Health providers will also trained and sensitized for their positive role to address GBV mainly child marriages .
