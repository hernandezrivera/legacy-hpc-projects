Project Summary

Needs

Provision of a humanitarian air service in Chad. Logistics services are essential to facilitate the humanitarian response in Chad through transport of actors and humanitarian assistance. United Nations Humanitarian Air Services (UNHAS) provides this service to organizations involved in the humanitarian response in Chad.In Chad, road infrastructures are inadequate and often in poor condition, and long distances between the capital and the intervention zones combine with insecurity to make access to many humanitarian intervention sites difficult.In addition, many of these areas, particularly in the east of the country, become completely inaccessible by road during the rainy season (at least from June to September) due to floods and the official closure of many roads.In these conditions, air transport is the safest and most reliable way for humanitarian workers to travel safely from N'Djamena, Abéché, Goz Beida to more than fifteen localities in the South, Center, East and West to deliver humanitarian assistance.

Activities or outputs

The objective is to maintain a continuous humanitarian air transport service adapted to the needs of humanitarian actors. In addition to scheduled and scheduled passenger transportation services, UNHAS conducts medical and security evacuations according to requests.Since 2016, UNHAS has opened regular lines to new destinations in the countries of the Lake Chad Basin, such as Maroua (Cameroon) following the demands and needs of the humanitarian community. UNHAS also facilitates high-level visits that are critical to supporting the humanitarian community in its advocacy efforts. In 2017, the frequency of flights was reduced to eleven destinations out of nineteen in total to cope with underfunding.The use of services divided by organizations is 44% for NGOs, 53% for UN agencies and 3% for government counterparts, the diplomatic corps and the donor community.UNHAS uses a fleet of four aircraft mainly based in N'Djamena, Abéché, Goz Beida and Jet in Cameroon. These devices are strategically positioned in these localities and exploited to respond effectively to the humanitarian needs of the country. UNHAS plans to carry a total of at least 24,000 passengers in 2018. For 2019, UNHAS forecasts to carry up to 1,500 passengers per month in 2019.UNHAS will continue to provide critical access and increasing frequencies to the implementation sites in 2019 to the emergency areas of the Lake region and to the South region, including access to airstrips on the basis of ADHOC.In order to effectively serve the humanitarian community, the UNHAS Chad fleet includes three fixed-wing aircraft - a Dash8 (based in Abéché) and a C208 Caravan (based in Ndjamena and Goz Beida). The ERJ135 (based in Yaoundé) is also used to strengthen the UNHAS Chad network. For 2019, UNHAS will continue to rely on security networks to obtain information to mitigate threats to the security of the operation. UNHAS Chad will continue to provide air services to NGOs, UN agencies, donor representatives, the diplomatic community and implementing humanitarian partners, with the necessary access to rapidly reach remote or inaccessible beneficiaries.For 2019, UNHAS will maintain its current weekly schedule and operational model despite the recent presence of a local start-up airline. So far, this had no effect on UNHAS. Nevertheless, UNHAS considered all the main risks associated with the project and presented mitigation measures for each possible risk. In addition, building the capacity of international and national staff, NGOs and key authorities is a major component of UNHAS's footprint at the local level. As part of the broader efforts of the aid sector to develop a better aviation architecture in UNHAS 'areas of work, the service will continue to build the capacity of national and international staff, NGOs and authorities as a major component of the local level. UNHAS will continue to implement activities aimed at advancing the transfer of knowledge from aviation-related core disciplines and transferring skills towards strengthening national authority. UNHAS will continue to seek funds towards improving key airfields whereby rehabilitation works are planned for FARCHANA, IRIBA, and BAGA SOLA* (* pending approvals).
