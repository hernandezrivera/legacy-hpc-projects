Project Summary

Malnutrition, forced displacement, and health emergencies are recurrent in the Tahouaand Tillaberi Regions of Niger due to structural crises, poverty, and insecurity. TheHumanitarian Response Plan for Niger foresaw a further deterioration of the humanitariansituation in 2019. The document estimated that more than 716,000 people in the tworegions will needed assistance. This includes the 42,000 people currently displaced, andmore than 55,500 Malians refugee who already live in the two regions. In 2020, similarnumbers are probable.
UN agencies in Niger in concert with humanitarian partners have created a ‘RapidResponse Mechanism’ (RRM) to prepare for sudden emergency situations that require themobilization of lifesaving humanitarian assistance.
The RRM is an operational, programmatic and partnership model designed to enhancecapacity of Sectors to respond in a timely, coordinated and standardized manner to theneeds of populations made vulnerable by displacement, disease and/or natural disaster inhumanitarian/emergency settings.
It is meant to address:
- the most acute vulnerabilities;
- to access the most complex zones (insecurity);
- to access areas where few actors are present.
The intent of the RRM is to deliver aid within 72 hours of the report of an emergency.
