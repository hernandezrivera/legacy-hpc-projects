Project Summary

According to Humanitarian Need Overview 2020, it is estimated that 6.5million children and youth from pre-school to secondary school-age (in and out of school) and 6,569,803 who are in need of education (severity 3-6). 86% of these people in need have acute and immediate need for humanitarian education assistance (severity of 4-5-6).
A Joint Education Needs Assessment for Out of School Children (JENA) in December 2019 was conducted within 112 communities; 77% (86 cities) of which were cities and towns and 23% (26 camps) were regular or random camps; 7 camps in Aleppo governorate and 19 others in Idleb governorate. According to the statistics of the IMU of ACU who produced the JENA report, the number of children between 6 – 18 years in NW of Syria within the assessed areas reached 1,712,468 children; and according to JENA 66% (1,130,229 children) are attending schools and 34% (582,239 children) are out of school.
As a response to the aforementioned factors, Basmeh and Zeitooneh (BZ) and is seeking to provide nine schools in Idleb governorate with educational assistance to enhance the schools’ performance in terms of educational quality for the duration of nine months, the assistance includes: IT equipment, learning materials, incentives to students (graduation ceremonies, certificates and gifts), PSS activities, staff’s salaries, and heating fuel.

4 - Needs

According to Humanitarian Need Overview 2020, it is estimated that 6.5million children and youth from pre-school to secondary school-age (in and out of school) and 6,569,803. A Joint Education Needs Assessment for Out of School Children (JENA) in December 2019 was conducted within 112 communities; 77% (86 cities) of which were cities and towns and 23% (26 camps) were regular or random camps; 7 camps in Aleppo governorate and 19 others in Idleb governorate. According to the statistics of the IMU of ACU who produced the JENA report, the number of children between 6 – 18 years in NW of Syria within the assessed areas reached 1,712,468 children; and according to JENA 66% (1,130,229 children) are attending schools and 34% (582,239 children) are out of school. Based on OCHA’s 7th situational report in January 2020, As of 28 January, due to the unfolding situation in Ma’arrat An Nu’man, Saraqab, Ehsem and Mahambl, education have been suspended, impacting the learning of 59,231 school-aged children and showing a 36% increase of displaced school-aged children in impacted areas. A total of 199 schools are out of operation, impacting 59,231 children and 3,703 teachers: 135 schools in Ma’arrat An Nu’man and neighbouring communities, impacting48,649 children and 2,704 teachers; 23 schools in the communities of Ariha, impacting 5,681 children and 384 teachers; and 41 schools in the communities of Saraqab, impacting 4,901 children and 615 teachers. BZ conducted a needs assessment in nine schools in Idleb governorate in Armanaz, Idleb, Teftnaz, and Harim sub-districts:1- Ajyal Alghad School2- Jeel Alnasr School3- Biret Armanaz School4- Ahmad Izzedine Alhomsi School5- Haj Jumaa School6- Quneitra School7- Faroukiyeh School8- Nihad Fanary School9- Nihad Fanary School The conducted needs assessment for all the above-mentioned schools showed sever lack of heating materials, stationery and salaries.

5 - Implementation of activities

The project will be achieved by implementing the following activities:1- Needs Assessment: This step will be conducted prior to the implementation phase2- Recruitment: Recruiting Project Staff3- Items Procurement: Based on B&Z's procurement policies and procedures4- Items distribution that includes: a. Providing classrooms with IT equipment such as computers, projector...etc. b. Providing incentives to students such as presents, Certificates of Appreciation, graduation’s celebration. c. Supporting the school’s staff with salaries. d. Providing heating fuel. e. Providing Students with students’ kits. f. Providing teachers with Educational kits. g. Establishing Child Safe Spaces, and PSS services. 5- Monitoring and evaluation: This step will be conducted during and post-project implementation. Before initiating the implementation process, BZ will conduct a comprehensive educational assessment to recognize other implementing partners that are working in the same field. Furthermore, BZ will establish a bilateral communication with the cluster to fill any potential service gaps and prevent any duplications.During the project implementation, Basmeh and Zeitooneh’ staff will ensure compliance on Accountability to Affected Populations (AAP) and on Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA). Under the proposed project, BZ will remain accountable to affected populations through open dialogue and feedback/complaint mechanisms provided to these populations, as follows: - Boxes will be checked periodically, and the response will be generated within a week (except urgent complaint)- Using social media and mobile applications to receive complaints from beneficiaries.- collecting gender and age-disaggregated data from project implementation and impact, which will be analysed and routinely reported on while project activities are monitored for improvements in self-reliance as well as beneficiary satisfaction and resilience of both women and men.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

1-Support the provision of formal education to children and youthRisk -Changes in the security situation, particularly in project target areas, affects the safety/mobility/ displacement of beneficiaries/staff and results in being unable to participate in the project. This includes the risk of targeted attacks and bombing of schools/ communities and the risks of mines, airstrikes and crossfire. -Child safety incidents in school due to bullying and overburdened teachersMitigation Measure: -Closely monitor the security situation in coordination with partners and relevant stakeholders and conduct a security assessment. They will ensure that staff members and teachers are trained in safety and security best practices, including mine risk education.If and when the local security situation deteriorates: a) Immediately inform internal emergency focal point on the situation; b) Coordinate with partners, cluster and hibernate/suspend the operations if necessary; c) Examine the possibilities of adopting different modalities to implement the project. All important documents will be saved as soft copies, so that they will not get lost due to an attack. Decisions are taken in advance whether hard copies will be destroyed or not. -Communication with donors to accept flexibility and support relocation of activities to safer spaces whenever needed.2- Provide students with school supplies in formal and non-formal settingsRisk-Increased risk of theft from children receiving kits -Increased risk of inter-community tension due to varied assistance packages across education sector members -High fluctuations in prices of items needed (if procurement happens inside Syria) -Lack of suitable markets 3-Provide maintenance and running costsRisk-Risk of children being out of school due to unavailability of school furniture upon completion of school rehabilitation and construction of prefabricated classrooms -Risk of not having enough WASH facilities for boys and girls.

Assumptions:- Locations, where the project activities will be conducted, are secure and accessible.- beneficiaries are positively engaging in the activities (Students and education staff attend the schools) Risks:- Security Situation: Military operations close to schools- How to mitigate: Keeping up to date with the military actions/movements and inform the education staff accordingly Train the education staff on safety and security procedures.
