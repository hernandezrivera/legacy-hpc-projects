Project Summary

The project is a response to the finding of rapid multi-sector joint assessment of Gu,/Hagaa floods in Afgooye, South west state on 23th July 2020. the project will target the underserved stratum of the population in Afgooye district to provide health services through two fixed health facilities and two outreach services in Afgooye district. Outreach teams under many circumstances provide this crucial contact with the underserved stratum of the population (IDPs, vulnerable host communities, minorities, etc) Their role is partly in improving access to available resources, but it is also important in fostering behavior change and, more generally, in supporting caring practices. KAAH intend to Implement and improve primary health care intervention, especially in poor communities, which require a combination of community and facility-based activities, with support from central levels of organization. Outreach teams with health promoters may also play a part in mobilizing social demand for services, targeting specific areas with low accessibility and specific biological groups—generally women and children— within those areas, followed a bottle neck analysis exercise of our program (primary health care). the project will use fixed and outreach services to reach the hard to reach areas, as well as areas, where previous outbreak of epidemics (AWD, measles, COVID 19 etc) has occurred. <5 children and pregnant women will have access to VPD (vaccine preventable diseases) as part of fixed and outreach activities , treatment of common diseases, as well as ANC/PNC. Infant & young child feeding promoter: Promote infant and young child feeding promotion through home visits and campaign targeting pregnant and lactating women as well as men. KAAH will ensure that most vulnerable displaced and disaster affected women. Girls, boys and men have increased and equal access to primary health care through fixed and outreach services.
The project direct beneficiaries will be 21,105 people living in Afgooye town and surrounding villages which affected by the floods, including IDP population and host communities in Afgoye district. Following targets will be reached during the implementation period of this project: Two primary health care facilities and two mobile services in Afgoye district will provide emergency health and at least 5 out of essential components of BNSP to the target population. An estimated 844 pregnant and lactating mothers will have access to quality Antenatal care, skilled birth attendant and postnatal care. A total of 1,900 pregnant and lactating mothers and 844 new born infants will receive appropriate antenatal and postnatal care. An estimated 844 under 1year children will receive appropriate vaccination, and 4,221 under five children will receive two VAS and deworming tablets. An estimated 4,400 under 5 children will receive appropriate care for the three common childhood illnesses (malaria, diarrhea and pneumonia). A total of 15 health professionals will be trained on integrated management of childhood illnesses. However, the indirect beneficiaries of this project are 173,000 people (including 30,000 IDPs) in Afgoe district.

Mitigation Measures

Risk 1 Mitigation:1. Project will ensure the location and access routes to health facilities and services are safe.2. Organization ensure that all staff, implementing partners and volunteers in direct contact with patients, health staff and beneficiaries have received training in prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse (PSEA).3. In cooperation with protection actors, enhance the capacity of community health workers to monitor,report and refer protection cases (such as abuse and exploitation) in accordance with standardoperating procedures. 4. Organization will install lights outside and on the access route to the facility. Risk 2 mitigation:1. Project will ensure that assistance and services are reaching the most vulnerable - Identify and prioritize the most vulnerable groups in the community and prevent discrimination or exclusion of marginalized group.2. project will ensure the location, distance and access routes to the health facilities and services are accessible to all members of the community, including the most vulnerable groups.3. Project will introduce special arrangements for persons unable to access health facilities eg. mobile health services, transportation/taxi services, facilitate accompaniment arrangements (e.g. youth groupsassisting the elderly/persons with disability; community networks).4. Provide training to male and female health professionals to work with persons with such disabilities,including on how to ensure proper confidentiality and informed consent.Risk 3 Mitigation:1. project will Set up accessible, confidential and well understood feedback mechanisms for suggestions and complaints.2. Project will Set up mechanisms for submitting feedback that do not require the beneficiary exposing themselves to project staff. 3. Organization will respond to complaints, regardless of whether corrective measures can/need to be put in place. 4. Project will organize awareness raising sessions so that people know how it works.
