Project Summary

The project aims to scale up integrated field nutrition, child protection, health and hygiene promoting services through 16 Rapid Response Teams in coordination with the respective clusters in high severity communities and IDPs hosting areas where the project will target the IDPs camps in Dana through 2 teams, Al-bab through 2 teams, one team in I’zaz, 4 teams in Jisr Shugur, one in Harim, 4 in Salqeen and 2 in Maaret tamesrin/ Maret El Ikhwan sub-districts.
Each team, consisting of a nutritionist, and 3 community health workers and one data entry will introduce the following integrated services:
Nutrition: Provide IYCF-E counselling for mothers with moderately malnourished children, distribute micronutrients for preventing of anaemia and malnutrition among children, pregnant and lactating women, disseminate messages (IEC/BCC) on IYCF-E recommended feeding practices, and monitor distribution and use of breast milk substitutes, support health/nutrition inclusive education and promotion refer the needed cases and follow up the referred cases. Besides, they will distribute breast covers for PLWs to encourage them feeling more privacy and support the exclusive breast-feeding.
Protection: identify the children with disabilities; cases exposed to abuse or violence via the visual signs on the children under five and PLWs bodies to refer the discovered cases to the child protection CP and GBV case management teams of SEMA or other NGOs working in the nearest areas according to the service mapping
Health: Screen and refer CU5 and PLWs with danger signs and Medical problems, where the referred cases will receive the medical care under the supervision of professional doctors such as paediatrician or general practitioner.
WASH: support hygiene promotion and raise the awareness through providing key messages about the hand washing and food processing. Hygiene kits will distributed to the most needed beneficiaries.
Messages about the RH module will be provided by the CHWs for PLWs. Besides, 1000 Breast covers will be distributed to the PLWs to encourage them and to raise the awareness about the importance of exclusive breastfeeding.
The nutritionist will provide consultations, referrals and monitor the upload of data before the data entry uploaded the data directly through KOBO link to the Information Management Unit IMU in the main office in Gaziantep; while the community health workers CHWs will screen the children under five years CU5, and PLWs (pregnant and lactating women), provide messages, and distribute brochures about the importance of the exclusive breastfeeding to raise the awareness in the targeted locations.
After finalizing the recruitment process, the staff will be trained on the protocols and templates, in coincidence with conducting Communities (Camps) Mapping by CHWs under supervision of the supervisor to prepare the detailed work plan. Then the home visits and group awareness session will take place based on pre-prepared activities schedule.

4 - Needs

Based on 2019 Humanitarian needs overview report, “more than 91,811 boys and girls are acutely undernourished. If malnutrition isnot prevented an additional 146,898 children under five years will become acutely malnourished”. The last rapid need assessment conducted by the organizations working in NW and NE Syria between October 20th and 23rd, 2019 and under supervision of the nutrition cluster in Gaziantep, Turkey, the GAM rate was 3.61%, MAM was 2.94%, SAM was 0.66%. Over 92% of the surveyed areas did not contain hand-washing basins with soap, while soap for hand washing is not available in 35% of the camps surveyed. As for the latrines locations, the assessment showed that 17% of the camps were without latrines, while 57% had latrines between tents.Concerning wastewater disposal, the results of the assessment indicated that 38% of wastewater was disposed of in pits and 20% in the streets.Moreover, SEMA conducted a multi-sectoral needs assessment in many locations in NW Syria in Sep 2019. The respondents reported that 70% of the community are in need of nutrition and childcare services in Harim (Dana) while 67% in Shamarekh (Izaz) and 53% in Bab Al Hajr (Al Bab).The assessment showed that 44% of people in Harim (Dana) get sick from drinking water, while in Shamarekh (Izaz) the proportion was 27% and 22% Bab Al Hajr (Al Bab).

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

The project staff will mainstream protection principles in all components which include making sure that all beneficiaries have purposeful access to services prioritizing the safety and dignity of them, ensuring non-discrimination and 'do no harm' in the delivery of the services and pursuing a meaningful engagement of the stakeholders, and ensure that confidential reporting mechanisms are in place to address any potential abuse. The project will ensure reaching the most vulnerable persons and provide nutrition, and WASH services for them, or refer them to other service providers. This project is designed to boost the dignity and living conditions of the targeted beneficiaries who will include the most vulnerable populations; including women-headed households who are generally more exposed and vulnerable to protection risks.The following considerations must be taken into account in this project:1- Facilitate the access of the women and girls to the supported units safely, without being exposed to abuse or security risks.2- The RRTs will be located in easy-to-reach, close, and safe areas to avoid any potential risks.3- Having a trained and qualified team that is committed to the code of conduct and PSEA principles. The team works on not to violate these principles knowing that there are disciplinary actions in place.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

 Risk 1: - Temporary cutting of cross border accessMitigation: - SEMA will ensure the availability of supplies and buffer stocks in field warehouses and will be able to supervise the service provision through field offices and teams. Risk 2:- Displacement waves from or to areas of implementation and other war-related risks (, air bombardment, shelling, bombed vehicles, theft, kidnapping, fire, etc):Mitigation:SEMA through a network of facility-based Liaison and humanitarian access focal points, will monitor the situation on the ground and send immediate feedback regarding security alerts and beneficiaries movement, and programs teams will work through a mobile team approach to reach the newcomers and displaced people in site. Safety equipment for facilities and warehouses, guards, personal safety training and evacuation plan orientation will be in place especially for facilities staff working in higher grade if risks.
