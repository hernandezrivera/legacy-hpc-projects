Project Summary

The post Gu 2019 nutrition assessment findings showed marginal decrease in the median prevalence of Global Acute Malnutrition (GAM) of 13.8% down from 14% of post Gu 2018. However, currently , 1 million boys and girls are affected by acute malnutrition. The health indicators in Puntland are among the worst, there is high maternal and infant mortality rate, low coverage vaccine preventable diseases, high malnutrition rate both Global Acute Malnutrition (GAM) and Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM) above threshold(15% and 3.5 % respectively ). Acute watery diarrhea (AWD)/Cholera are among the most serious cases.The AWD disease poses a huge threat to millions of people, especially those living in crowd displaced camps. Maternal mortality rate in Somalia is one of the highest in the world with approximately 730/100,000 live births and in Puntland, only 30 percent of people have access to health services and one in five children die before their fifth birth day. The proposed project will provide lifesaving primary health care services to drought/AWD affected people through continuation of four fixed health facilities and scaling up one health facilities, two mobile clinics targeting 60.000 beneficiaries including (18,000 girls , 15,000 women, 18,000 boys and 9, 000men ) in Nugaal and Sool i regions in Puntland. KAALO is active health cluster partner in Putland for more than eight years. KAALO is currently implementing SHF project in Sool region . According to our HMIS data , a total of 5200 affected people (1275 women, 1679 girls, 1441 boys and 705 men) reached with primary health care services between June -September 2019. The project objectives and activities contribute to the reduction of morbidity and mortality among affected population. KAALO will provide essential primary health care services including basic maternal and reproductive health services in our health facilities and outreach services in hard-to-reach areas. In addition , community health workers will work with community , conduct active case management and referral of server cases to health facilities. In collaboration with health cluster in Puntland , KAALO will support outbreak prevention, control and response interventions at community level.

Mitigation Measures

Garowe and Bossaso host the largest internal displaced people in Puntland and estimated about 300,000 people. The displacement is driven by drought and conflict. These population live in overcrowded settlement and number are increasing with a limited services available. As result it has put pressure on existing services resulting increased incidence of communicable disease and other protection concerns such as violence. KAALO will apply adopt gender dis-aggregated community consultation that will allow women and girls to raise their concern and take appropriate prevention measures to address their specific concerns and prioritize their needs. KAALO will scale up an integrated health and protection services to fill these gaps. KAALO will ensure the capacity of local community and resilience improved. We will focus on both prevention/curative services and building local community structure and system that will enable community response to risks and sustain basic services in long run. Access of women and adolescent girls to the health services are the main risks facing at target population. long walking to the general hospitals increased exposure and vulnerability to risk such as gender based violence ,discrimination , limiting access to health care and information, and impacting on health outcomes. To overcome these challenges, KAALO will provide transportation services and mobile clinics in IDP camps close to their settlements. this will reduce the long distance that affected women will walk for health services. We will also provide awareness education on available services ,mapping the risk areas and prevention measures on GBV.protection mainstreaming : The absence of protection mainstreaming in both humanitarian and development sector has increased risk of GBV and in particular, sexual violence remains a critical protection concern for women and girls at target locations. Both state and non state armed men are the largest perpetrator group, (50%) of sexual violence and majority of cases were physical assault, accounting 50%, of the cases. To mitigate the impact of armed conflic and violence , KAALO work with the government and UNCHR in prevention and response to violence and GBV. KAALO is working with UNCHR in provision of legal aid services to refugees and asylum seekers and the provision of GBV services including medical, livelihood and psycho social support
