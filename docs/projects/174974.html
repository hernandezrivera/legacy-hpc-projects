Project Summary

This project adopts a multi-sectoral approach, aiming to address some of the most pressing humanitarian needs of floods affected families in districts Dadu and Tando Muhammad Khan in Sindh where they are living in terms of food insecurity, damaged shelters, unhealthy environment, including poor WASH and the associated risks of protections, as well as limited and infrequent access to cash. Concern and its partner Thardeep Rural Development Programme (TRDP) jointly well placed to implement this programme, given their extensive experience in livelihoods, shelter, and WASH restoration programming.
Projects will enable poor and vulnerable households including students (girls and boys), women, health facilities staff, and community members including (women headed families, widows, women and persons living with disabilities), who are affected by 2020 floods to restore WASH infrastructure and hygiene awareness. The project will target approximately 20,000 individuals through WASH in Dadu and Tando Muhammad Khan districts of Sindh province.

1- Project Activities

Rehabilitation of communal water sources (hand pumps) - Drinking water supply schemes (mainly hand pumps) will be rehabilitated/constructed. Through consultation with community and household assessments, this activity will target vulnerable households in the target community whose water source has faced damages due to floods and there is no nearby other safe water source of drinking water, while simultaneously ensuring to adhere to Sphere and WHO standards. Rehabilitation of water and sanitation facilities in schools - Damaged/non-functional water and sanitation facilities within schools will be rehabilitated which will not only provide adequate and safe drinking water but also a healthy and comfortable environment, which will help in improving children health and will boost educational attendance and achievement. Distribution of WASH NFI kits to flood affected vulnerable families - The programme is addressing provision of hygiene items such as soap and jerry cans for most vulnerable HHs to improve access to water for drinking and domestic use and provide soap to practice hand hygiene hence prevent themselves and families associated public health risks. Distribution of Mini Dignity/ MHM kits to flood affected vulnerable female beneficiaries - Menstrual Hygiene and Health (MHH) management requires easy access to safe, private water and sanitation facilities, along with appropriate menstrual materials and supplies, proper disposal and waste management and basic information on menstrual hygiene which is however not focused during post-disaster relief and recovery operations. Distribution of disinfection kits to schools and health facilities - Concern will work through the Department of Health, District administration, and TMAs to reach out to those facilities for disinfection purposes. Concern will distribute disinfection kits to targeted facilities, which contains Automatic Spray Machine, Chlorine, PPE kit, and Industrial Safety Long Rubber Rain Boots. Distribution of WASH related IEC materials at household level - IEC material will be developed and disseminated among HHs for better understating, this IEC material will be used in community based, and school based hygiene sessions. Interactive hygiene promotion sessions - In order to create awareness and minimize the after effects of disasters, interactive hygiene promotion/ BCC activities will be carried out making the people resilient against public health risks. The action planned to conduct hygiene promotion activities with in communities and schools (subject to opening of schools). Hygiene promotors of the implementing partners along will conduct interactive hygiene promotion sessions with men, women, and children separately in the targeted communities under WASH sector. This will help to change behaviors, promote good hygiene and sanitation practices among the target audience and in return will contribute to health, hygiene and sanitation related goals of SDGs. On average, 10-15 persons will participate in the session keeping and following COVID-19 SOPs. Besides session with communities, special sessions will be organized for schoolchildren to improve their knowledge attitude and practices regarding personal and school hygiene, considering them as agents for change for their families, peer, and entire community. All the session will be conducted in small groups considering COVID SOPs while sessions will be focused upon personal, environmental, HHW storage and treatment and COVID-19 preventive measures.
