Project Summary

This project will provide protection assistance in Kafr Taal ( community in Atareb subdistrict/
Jabel Saman district), Akhtrin and Arshaf ( are a communities in Aghtrin subdistrict/
A’zaz district) and Kafrantin (is a community in Daret Azza sub-distirct/ Jabel
Saman district) all in Aleppo governorate, through CP services in (CFSs) and community
based protection center.
The several displacements from different areas to the north of Syria caused a huge influx
of IDPs to western and northern country side of Aleppo as well as Idlib governorate.
There is a huge need for protection services and especially CP and YEP programs in
many areas of northern Syria and till now there is a huge gap in the coverage of the
community based services due to many reasons related to funds and lack of capacities
and most of the established centers are suffering from the cutting in the fund.
Therefore SOH chose to help in providing spaces for children and adolescents and to
help those affected groups where children and adolescents will be able to feel physically
and emotionally safe.
Safe in this context refers to the absence of trauma, excessive stress, and violence and
express themselves without fear of judgement or harm, where they can build their social
networks, receive support from their peers, and learn something new.
The proposed activities are:
- Outreach activities including (awareness session, life skills and PSS): Outreach Team
will target households, community establishments, schools, and any reachable places
that are considered suitable for such activities.
- life skills for adolescents: The proposed activities will use the protection cluster
developed YEP life skills guidelines.
-The centers will offer recreational activities for children and adolescent boys and girls.
-The centers will provide parenting sessions to discuss many issues related to protection
of children and their rights.
-Capacity development for the centers staff on CPMS, PSS, PFA, self care and case
management.
-Vocational courses for adolescents, administrative skills, languages, computer skills and other vocations.
-There will be 2 case managers at the centers where they will receive cases from other
activities. They will do case study, basic counseling and referrals to other services e.g.
Psychologist when needed.
