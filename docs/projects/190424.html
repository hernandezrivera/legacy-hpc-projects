Project Summary

The purpose of the project is to provide access to safe and protective education services for
girls and boys internally displaced, returnees and in host communities in conflict affected
woredas of five Zones.
The project targets 340,363 girls and boys living in Debre Berhan, Kewet, Shewa Robit, Menz mama, Menz Gera and Efretana Gidem in North Showa Zone, Kemise, Bati, and Artuma Fursi in Oromo Special Zone, Dessie, Dessie Zuryia, Kombolcha, Kuta Ber, Tehuladere, and Kalu woreda in South wollo zone , Meket, Gashena, Woldiay, Kobo, Habru Woreda, Wadla, Bugena, Raya Kobo in North Wollo Zone and Gazgiebela woredas’ in Wagimra Zone.
Out of school aged children, children affected by the conflict and with protection concerns, facilitators/teachers, PTAs members, CBO leaders and communities receiving learning and protection services are the direct beneficiaries. Hence over 6 months 4,700 (50% girls) children will access ASR, 9000(50% girls) children will follow ALP/APLP, 50,139 displaced/returned and affected community out of school children will have access to quality O-class program in government schools through training for teachers and provision of teaching and learning materials. In general, 292,524(50 % girls) children receiving formal and non-formal primary education and their schools will be linked to child protection services.
The project will benefit 106583 (50% female) Vulnerable children and Care givers/parents and 1,600 teachers in need of psychosocial and emotional support by providing access to mental health and psychosocial support services.
The project will also Provide 1,600 School principals, teachers, Woreda and Zone Education Personnel; a TOT on Mental Health and Psychosocial Support (MHPSS) and SEL for three days to cascade down to 13,258 teachers in 757 schools in the five targeted zones
Accordingly, Imagine1day designed a project named “ Education and Child Protection support for out of school IDP/Returnee and conflict affected children and children at risk through ‘Bete’ program with an integrated Accelerated Learning Programme (ALP), case management and psychosocial support in the five Zones of Amhara Region” . The project is expected to generate innovative models of connecting stakeholders and partners particularly the communities, project implementers, donors and beneficiaries for sustainable education development.
Imagine1day will employ strategies to create systemic lasting results, by focusing on building the leadership and technical capacity of project staff and key stakeholders, who will influence the sustainability of this project.
This project deals with creating access to learning for out of school aged children through accelerated school readiness and accelerated primary learning program, providing protection service, deliver skill development, and organizing capacity building program for facilitators/teachers, PTAs, CBOs and other stakeholders.
The project inputs include financial, human and material resources. The total cost of the project is estimated to be ETB Cash: 127,580,933 (One hundred twenty seven million five hundred eighty thousand nine hundred thirty three birr) The community contribution will be at a free will of the community members and committee selected by the community consisting PTAs, Kebele Chairman and District Education Office, Women youth and children office are responsible to mobilize out of school children to learning centers. The project will employ a total of 165 people including Amhara region program director, Zonal CP and EiE program coordinators, capacity building officer, Regional EiE program manager, project M&E officer, 31 social workers, 34 community social workers, 3 CP program officers, 5 Cp and EiE program officers, 57 ASR facilitators, 176 ALP facilitators. permanent and temporary staff personnel that will support the project.
