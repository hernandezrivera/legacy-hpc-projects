Project Summary

Christian Aid (CAID) COVID-19 Food Security and Livelihood intervention targeted in Camps 15 and nearby Palongkhali Union covering 24,000 Rohingya and Host community vulnerable people. The objectives of this project are to protect livelihood of vulnerable Rohingya and host community from the scourge of COVID 19 and provide food assistance along with inputs support including hygiene kits, PPE and awareness related message among the Rohingya and host community specially persons with disability, children, women, and old age persons to cope with the COVID 19 situation.
The underlying goal is to provide support to farmers of host community & Rohingya to ensure that the food system is not at risk due to COVID-19 transmission and to provide input (including cash) support to the farmers of host community in resuming livelihood by providing food production assistance in order to stabilize income and minimize the impact of COVID-19.
Keeping in mind the objectives, Christian Aid proposing Immediate intervention to keep the food and agriculture market functional maintaining the health standard and guidelines and protect people from transmission of COVID-19. Short term intervention: Identify & ensure Food Security essential and critical services and supplies, Supporting the safe handling of food and transportation upto consumer, Support the farmers and food handler for safe and (COVID)contamination free food, ensure agriculture and livestock services are provided maintaining safety in a continued manner, organize awareness raising campaigns, Communication with Communities. Medium and long-term intervention: ensure resume livelihood process by supporting either in cash/ inputs in production mechanism and ensure market functionality. Need to Support along the value chain of agriculture, livestock, fisheries, food based business and off farm trade that the livelihood process resume.
To ensure quality of program design and implementation, Christian Aid will strengthen and reaffirm its community-based participatory approach in assessment, design, implementation, monitoring to Cash/inputs based livelihood and food security programme underpinned by rights-based and inclusive programming principles, by mainstreaming protection and strong accountability as cross-cutting themes and prioritizing the needs of the most vulnerable and hard to reach.
In this connection, Christian Aid will promote engagement with PwD, widow women, young people through community consultation meetings, assessment of individual livelihood, Complaints & Feedback and Response mechanism.
The project will adopt a comprehensive M&E Plan based on a detailed implementation plan to monitor and evaluate activities in proposed camps and host community. Christian Aid being a CHS certified humanitarian agency on Quality and Accountability is fully aware of the key guiding principles and standards such as Sphere standards, and Minimum Standards for Prevention and Response to GBV in Emergencies.

Needs

"The lockdown due to COVID-19 resulting in economic implications specially to marginal who were above the poverty level falling below the poverty line due to daily loss of income as wage basis in micro income generation economic activities. Daily losses of income adversely impacted on Food Security of the children, widow women, PwD, old age person in the society. This is not exception to the People of Plaongkhali Union whose livelihood mostly depends on agricultural options with chain of agricultural production centre which shut down due to perishable item, buyer’s movement restrictions and shut down of mini food processing shop. This negatively impacted on daily wage and loose of unsustainable job. Closure of agricultural market and lack of inputs impact on less production and hence less consumption, selling, buying and profit have sharply fallen. Moreover, returnee migrants, PwD, ethnic minority are the major victim of current COVID-19 situation.
