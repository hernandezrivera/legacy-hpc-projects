Project Summary

The food security of most of the rural populations of Somalia have already been adversely impacted by the poor performance of the 2021 Deyr rains, followed by a drier and hotter than normal season, rising food prices, loss of livestock, and increased indebtedness. Large-scale and sustained humanitarian assistance, coupled with localized rainfall during the 2021 Gu season have modestly improved the overall food situation in rural areas, livelihood promotion and social inclusion services to the poor families with innovative solutions through restocking livestock and cash for work activities for the low income families to ensure immediate economic recovery to enhance their purchasing power while rehabilitation targeted water catchments, the project also will enhance the emergency livestock assistance through improved capacity of Community based animal health workers.

The food security situation in most rural livelihood zones in southern Somalia continued to deteriorate through mid-2021. Poor harvests at the end of 2020, limited access to alternative income sources, limited livestock holdings, and rising food prices have led to severe food insecurity and forced substantial displacement of rural populations towards IDP settlements Elwak Bardhere as well as urban centers.

1. - Projects activities

To provide cash for work activities for the low income families to ensure immediate economic recovery to enhance their purchasing power while rehabilitation targeted water catchments. To provide livelihood promotion and social inclusion services to the poor families with innovative solutions through restocking livestock for 250 HHs.4000 vulnerable Internally Displaced households in Elwak of gedo Region have increased access to incomes adequate to purchase minimum food basket through timely distribution of unconditional cash transfers, 60% of target beneficiaries are extreme vulnerable female headed households.. Provision of Unconditional cash transfers to 4000 IDP households living in Elwak and Bardhere and its surrounding villages. To improve protection and recovery of livelihood production sources contributing to HH access to food and income of drought affected agro pastoral and farming community in gedo regions. This will be done through; (i) Provision of seasonally appropriate agricultural farm inputs (certified quality seeds and fertilizers) to 1500 beneficiaries for two seasons.(ii) Provision of emergency livestock assistance through improved capacity of Community based animal health workers to improve livestock owner’s access to quality drugs, treatment & diagnostic skills and services among 10 agro pastoral villages in Targeted district and linking private agro vets to quality drug stores. (iv) Provision of small generators and low cost hey making machine (such as money-maker hey pallor) to improve local fodder production and management among agro pastoral communities in Elwak and riverine farmers in Bardher Districts.
