Project Summary

The project title increase access to safe water, promote sanitation and hygiene services in South Kordofan, West and Central Darfur States, start from 1st January 2021 to 31st December 2021 period of 1 year. There are humanitarian needs for construction of new and rehabilitation of households, public and communal latrines which are gender sensitive and consider disability and age in the same localities mention blow. The hygiene promotion was identified as a priority for targets with at least 1 hygiene promoter for 500 persons. This project will targeting 24000 people where 8000 people in each states. Women and children are the primarily the ones who fetches water among IDPs, returnees and vulnerable host communities in Central, West Darfur and South Kordofan States. Long distances to water points expose them to potential physical and sexual abuse within this context of instability. Lack of latrines at the household and community level has resulted in increased risk of across contamination and the spread of illnesses, special consideration in design of water sources for disable, children, elderly and lactating women for easy access. The loss of trees affects soil fertility, contributes to erosion and reduced groundwater level which resulted to degradation in the environment.

1 - Needs

HNO 2019 indicates that the impact of the conflict in Central, West Darfur and South Kordofan States has been widespread displacement with some 1.86 million IDPs (male 911400 and female 948600). HNO 2020 indicated about 7.6 million people need water, sanitation and hygiene services. These people includes IDPs, returnees, non-displaced host communities with limited access to WASH services. The most affected people groups are IDPs, returnees, non-displaced host communities and refugees. 1.7 million IDPs and 291000 returnees are in dire need of WASH support. 5.3 million people lack access to improved water sources, 6.2 million people lack access to improved sanitation and 7.5 million people lack access to improved hygiene services in South Kordofan, Central and West Darfur States.Across Sudan, WASH remains an urgent humanitarian priority.Surveys done at IDPs camps indicate that water service varies from 7.5 to more than 20 liters per person per day with an important number of them are still below the global SPHERE standards. This involves prioritization of water use within the household and resorting to use of unsafe drinkable water. A: Neutral impact on environment with NO mitigation

5.2 - Impact on environment/natural resources

Cutting of tress for construction of pit latrines at household's level affects the environment where loss of trees affects soil fertility, contributes to erosion and reduces underground water level which resulted to degradation in the environment. Upgrading of water sources to water yards requires power. SORC will mitigate by introducing technology of using solar panels for energy to pump water avoid air pollution, disseminate environmental messages to raise awareness during training and campaign on benefits to support sustainable use and maintenance WASH facilities. SORC further will encourageuse of environmental friendly materials for constructions and rehabilitation latrines where compensation will be done by planting additional trees. Campaign of cut one tree plant 5 seedlings will be key message to restore the environment.
