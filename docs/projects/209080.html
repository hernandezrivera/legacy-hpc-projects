Project Summary

The situation in White Nile is a grave humanitarian crisis with more 297,000 refugees prior to the conflict. An additional 144,000 refugees have arrived in White Nile from Khartoum as of the end June UNHCR/COR verified 89,244 individuals. 10 refugee camps were established prior to the crisis and one refugee settlement (Abu Dolou) will be established to host the secondary displaced refugees from Khartoum, there are also returnees from South Sudan after the cessation of the country who are currently in the localities adjacent to South Sudan. Last year the tribal conflicts in Blue Nile resulted in population’s movement whereby 10,000 people fled from Blue Nile. The war in Khartoum has also led to large influxes of IDPs from Khartoum and other western state to White Nile, today the caseload of IDPs in White Nile is 507,066 according to Humanitarian Aid commission report, 421,113 hosted with families and 85,951 hosted in 227 gathering centers across the nine localities of the state. Still the influxes of IDPs from different states is on progress especially when the conflict erupted in Wadani Medani in Alazira State recently. White Nile is also experiencing annual flooding specially in Algabalian, Alsalam and Tendalti localities. There are about 618,419 people 19.9% of the total State’s census—in crisis and above levels of food insecurity for the projection period: October 2023 – February 2024, according to the latest Sudan Integrated Food Security Phase Classification (IPC) analysis. Based on the first findings of the Cro Suply Assessment Mission it was found that the area harvested represents on 30% of the area cultivated in the rainfed areas which indicates that the cereal balance sheet is going to show gap in the food.
The project aims to enhance food security and livelihood of (16,767) farmers and agropastoralists (IDEPs, Host communities and non displaced) through the availability of agricultural inputs and animal health services, thereby improving the agricultural production and productivity and minimizing livestock losses resulting from diseases and ensuring food and nutrition security for vulnerable pastoral communities. The initiative will primarily focus on five localities of White Nile state namely Elgabaleen, Elsalam, Rabak, Kosti and Tandalti localities. The purpose is to restore and improve the food and nutrition security of 50,000 vulnerable pastoral household (250,000 individual) through provision of agricultural inputs, veterinary medicines and vaccines and providing the appropriate capacity building in Elgabaleen, Elsalam, Rabak, Kosti and Tandalti localities of White Nile state. Special attention shall be given for women-headed households and people with disabilities.

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

The project staff will do their best to ensure that their activities target the most vulnerable, enhance safety, dignity, and promote and protect the human rights of the beneficiaries without contributing to or perpetuating discrimination, abuse, violence, neglect and exploit. Criteria will be determined to ensure that the beneficiaries selected without any bias. The protection could be mainstreamed by including protection principles in the general project management and as core principles in delivering humanitarian aid. Moreover, it could be mainstreamed by incorporating protection principles into project cycle management through protection mainstreaming indicators for the sector.The Organization as a humanitarian actor shares an ethical responsibility for mainstreaming protection across the humanitarian response. The Project staff would be responsible for ensuring that activities within their respective sector and to be carried out with a “protection lens”, and in particular for ensuring that their activities integrate protection principles. The organization will work closely with the Protection's Sector Lead to ensure that the staff has supported with the suitable protection tools and packages.
