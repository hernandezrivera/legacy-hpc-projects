Project Summary

The Libya INGO Forum exists to serve the needs of its members and to promote the voice of international NGOs in the Libya humanitarian response. Under this project, the Forum will build upon its current work to improve operational coordination and facilitate the sharing of best practices among INGOs operating in Libya, lead collective advocacy effort towards the Libyan authorities, donors, governments and UN agencies, as well as represent the best interest of the NGOs in the coordination fora. The Forum will provide support to its membership (currently composed of 15 INGOs operating in Libya), and to humanitarian organisations seeking to start operations in Libya.

Needs

Operating in Libya remains a challenge for most of the INGOs, which prompts the need for inter-NGO consolidated approaches in 2019. In a recent survey conducted by the Forum, members have expressed a need for increased INGO coordination on operational matters, administrative-related issues, security matters, and advocacy for improved humanitarian space and response. In addition, a number of new INGOs seeking to start operating in Libya have recently requested support from the Forum.

Activities

o Provide a platform for coordination and info-sharing between INGOs, through monthly general meetings as well as through dedicated administrative/finance, security, logistics, and advocacy working groups. o Set-up a Video Teleconference system in INGOs offices in Tunis, Tripoli, Misrata and Benghazi o Develop a practical guide for NGOs operating in Libya that addresses legal, administrative and logistics-related issues and highlights red-lines and best-practiceso Organise events and meetings, as deemed needed by the membership (i.e. meeting with lawyers, security companies, etc.) o Foster common positioning and represent the best interest of NGOs in coordination forao Conduct and lead collective advocacy efforts for enabling humanitarian space
