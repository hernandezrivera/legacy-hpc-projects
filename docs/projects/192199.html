Project Summary

According to USAID’s latest report (April 26, 2023), the cumulative effect of rainfall failure over 3 consecutive seasons has left about 8 million people in need of immediate humanitarian aid and resulted in the death of about 1.5 million livestock in Ethiopia’s Oromia; Somali; Southern Nations, Nationalities, and Peoples’ (SNNP); and Southwest regions as of April 2022. Because of their predominant pastoral and agropastoral livelihoods systems, Borana zone of Oromia and Liban Zone of Somali regions are among the most affected of all.
The proposed action aims to provide most needed assistance to most affected people in Moyale district of Borana Zone and Dekasuftu district of Liban Zone. The key intervention areas are: food support in the form of conditional, unconditional cash transfers, and general food supports; provision of supports in the form of agricultural inputs and services (animal vaccination, vet drugs, and bush-thinning); and WaSH (particularly pond rehabilitation). The Project lasts for 4 months that is, May 15 to Sep 15, 2022. As main stakeholders, the project will engage concerned local, regional, and federal partners through project appraisal, and monitoring and evaluation. For actual project implementation, particularly beneficiary targeting, DAE will work with the existing committee systems (Humanitarian Committees located at district, kebele, and village levels and also complaints handling committees parallelly structed along with the first committee system). In addition, during the project implementation process key stakeholders will be communicated and share feedbacks through progress reports.

1. - Projects activities

Generally, the proposed action will target a total of 10,000 households with the different agricultural and livestock support inputs listed below. Whereas equal number of beneficiaries will be targeted on both sides of the boundary as much as possible as planned, 3800 households will receive all supports (both livestock and crop inputs). The three major project activities are:• Agricultural seeds support to 3800 households, 1900 in each district• Provide basic agricultural tools to 3800 households, 1900 in each district• Provide drug, per-diem, logistics and vaccine support for animal vaccination campaigns in the two districts, thereby addressing the needs of 10,000 households
