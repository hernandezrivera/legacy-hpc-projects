Project Summary

The project is aimed at bridging shelter/NFI gap that exists in the IDP camps in Abudwak owing to the fact that a number of old and newly arrived drought and conflict affected IDPs have no access to shelter and are living precariously in deplorable conditions in crowded congested structures with other families that have their own shelter. The targeted IDPs (women, girls, men, boys, the elderly and people with disabilities) are living in small makeshift structures made of rugs, recycled old garments and used hard boards in the IDP camps. Although the structures are providing them with basic physical protection and extreme climatic conditions, their privacy and dignity is quite compromised while they are exposed to increasing protection and health risks. Furthermore, limited access to basic services, exposes them to heightened protection risks. The project intends to improve living standards strengthen protection needs and enhance the dignity of IDPs (men , women, girls , boys, the elderly and people living with disabilities) living in deplorable conditions without proper shelter in IDP camps in Abudwak where they will be provided NFIs to improve their hygiene conditions and enable them channel their meagre resources to other basic needs. The project targets a total of 16550 persons.

Project Activities

1. Provision of Emergency Shelter kits (ESKs) to 1250 vulnerable newly displaced IDP families in the IDP camps in Abudwak2. Distribution of in kind NFI kits(Plastic sheet, Blanket, Sleeping mat, Kitchen sets, Mosquito net, Torch, Jerry Cans) to 1500 Vulnerable IDP families in IDP camps in Abudwak 3. Conducting Post distribution Monitoring exercise for the NFI kits distributed and Emergency Shelter kits (ESKs) constructed in IDP camps in Abudwak4. Conducting capacity building training for 180 vulnerable host community members and 180 IDPs/ male and female in Abudwak on life skills to enhance their resilience and strengthen their coping mechanisms.
