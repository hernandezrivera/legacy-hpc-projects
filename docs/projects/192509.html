Project Summary

This Education project funded by the Lebanon Humanitarian Fund and the ADRA Network, is to provide 430 Lebanese and Syrian boys and girls from ages 6 to 14 years old, and who are at risk of dropping out. Geographic targets include 165 children in Mt. Lebanon and 165 children from Baalbek. In addition, at least one community event will be organized each month by the Inclusion Officer and the Outreach Officer, and at most times, another local organization will jointly host an event such as local NGO Himaya – which ADRA has worked with for over two years. To ensure increased energy and ability to focus during learning, ADRA will collaborate with Lebanese NGO ShareQ to provide healthy and quality snacks to each child at the center, every day for 10 months.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

ADRA conducts PSEA/GBV and Social Inclusion activities such as awareness sessions approximately weekly and monthly. These are usually done in partnership with other organizations specialized in this such as Himaya, Save the Children, ABAAD, IMC and AMEL

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

Ensures the inclusion of all affected populations, especially children with disabilities. We ensure persons of concern are included.Non-discriminatory and equity is mainstreamed throughout all the activities to both students and parents/caregiversADRA ensures the beneficiaries access to protection and assistance programmes.ADRA promotes transparency, feedback and communication from the beneficiaries, and learning adaptation for the affected populations.
