Project Summary

Jami Al Hakeem Foundation (J-HF) will be targeting a total number of 5000 beneficiaries with the total percentage of (60% women/girls and 40% men/boys) where they will be reached with risk communication messages on the prevention and control of waterborne diseases and also 4 Health facilities will be supported with capacity building to be able to provide Clinical Management of rape services and specialized Mental health services across camps and host communities in Girei, Yola North and Yola South LGA of Adamawa state. 5000 Information Education and Communication-IEC Materials will be printed and distributed to beneficiaries who will be reached with risk communication massages. The IEC materials will be developed in Hausa, Fulani and English languages in collaboration with the Risk Communication Pillar of the Health sector and the Adamawa State Ministry of Health. 10% of the total number of beneficiaries will be focusing on people living with disabilities.
Jami Al Hakeem Foundation in collaboration with the Risk Communication Pillar will develop preventive majors through jingles/messages on water borne diseases which will be aired consistently on Radio Station with coverage reaching Girei, Yola North and Yola South LGA respectively. Jami Al Hakeem Foundation will also procure and distribute small transistor radios and batteries to the 1000 vulnerable household heads during the door-to-door risk communication to ensure that they listen to the prevention and control messages aired through the radio stations.
Jami Al Hakeem Foundation through its ongoing Community Based Psychosocial support (CBPSS) and Life skills project in Girei, Yola North and Yola South LGAs has identified the critical need for mental health psychosocial support, especially for adolescent girls and survivors of sexual assault. Jami Al Hakeem Foundation will be fully operational in all the LGA during the intervention
Jami Al Hakeem Foundation will implement in IDP camp and host communities; in Yola north and Yola South, while in Girei LGA will carry out motorized risk communication in 10 camps and host Communities

1 - Needs

At present, more than 2 million persons are displaced in North East Nigeria. The majority of these reside in informal camps and host communities where the provision of basic amenities were not initially planned. According to the World Health Organization (WHO), one in five displaced persons are affected by mental health disorders. Their right to their mental health and needs are challenged because they live under difficult circumstances. This in turn causes social isolation, stigmatization and economic losses among those affected. Study Aim This study aimed to analyze the mental and psychosocial support response to the mental health needs of displaced persons in North East Nigeria. By examining evidence-based guidance and highlighting success stories of Mental Health and Psychosocial Support (MHPSS) in other settings, gaps in the current interventions will be identified and recommendations provided to policy makers and providers of MHPSS in the region. Methodology A desktop review has been conducted. The Inter agency standing committee intervention pyramid was utilized to analyze the MHPSS response to the mental health and psychosocial needs. Results and Conclusion Given the MHPSS Sub working group’s coordination of activities in the region, there has been a considerable degree of response to the mental health needs of IDPs in the North East. However, the lack of basic needs such as food still creates psychosocial challenges. Additionally, adequate of coverage of MHPSS to the majority of IDPs residing in informal camps and host communities is not documented. To help translate success stories of MHPSS interventions to the context of the North East, a strong focus on solid leadership, holistic interventions across the pyramid and high quality training is needed to meet the mental health needs of displaced persons irrespective of their settlement

2 - Activities

J-HF will Set up LGA based project implementation committee in Yola North, Yola South and Girei LGAs Respectively • Consult with community stakeholders, parents, and caregivers and notify them of the availability of service in the selected LGAs •Sensitize community stakeholders, parents, caregivers, heads of households, and adolescents on reporting sexual or suspected sexual assault to the MHPSS/Counselling Facilitators in the Health Facilities • Conduct training for MHPSS/Counseling Facilitators deployed to Yola North, Yola South and Girei LGAs. • Carry out MHPSS/Counseling for boys and girls reported or identifies to have been sexually assaulted in the Health facility. • Identify and train community-based Cholera Health Educators in Yola North, Yola South and Girei, LGAs. • Deploy trained community-based Cholera Health Educators to engage identified and profiled vulnerable households heads.•Follow-up to monitor behavioral change amongst reached household heads and members of their household. • Consult and develop Cholera prevention and control messages in collaboration with the Risk Communication Pillar, Adamawa State Ministry of Health, the community leaders/people and Nigeria Centre for Disease Control (NCDC).• Engage Media (Radio Stations) on the continuous airing of developed jingles in Hausa, Fulani, and English.• Distribute printed IEC materials (English, Hausa & Fulani) to target individuals in camps and host communities reached with awareness-raising outreaches in Yola North, Yola South and Girei, LGAs.• Follow-up to monitor changes in behavior post-awareness

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

By integrating every strategy with the protection principles, Jami Al Hakeem Foundation will mainstream protection (GBV & PSEA) throughout the execution of this project and advance meaningful access, safety, and dignity of both beneficiaries and of employers and volunteers. Safety, decency, and avoiding any kind of harm to both beneficiaries and employees are priorities for Jami Al Hakeem Foundation. Additionally, Jami Al Hakeem Foundation will also make sure that the target population has equitable access to the service, free from impediments and prejudice, in accordance with their needs. Jami Al Hakeem Foundation will pay attention to people and groups who could be especially vulnerable or have a hard time obtaining help and resources, including those with disabilities and GBV survivors who have been identified. Jami Al Hakeem Foundation will integrate GBV Risk Mitigation efforts to reduce or decrease GBV risk to beneficiaries. Jami Al Hakeem Foundation will work consciously in compliance with the Sphere Humanitarian Core Standards and Imperatives. All approaches will align with the principles of protection as we remain committed to ensuring that this project does not expose beneficiaries to more risk in compliance with the DO NOHARM Protection Principle and will continue to raise awareness to the target population that humanitarian assistance is free and never conditioned on sexual favors as well as to be informed on how to access the appropriate complaints mechanisms, and support to set up in the community if SEA occurs. Also, Jami Al Hakeem Foundation will be intentional about maintaining an environment where there is zero Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (SEA).

In target LGAs, Jami Al Hakeem Foundation will create or improve new or existing procedures for complaints and feedback.
