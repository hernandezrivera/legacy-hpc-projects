Project Summary

many years of conflicts were enough to weak the Syrian citizens and create execrable humanitarian situation. The degree of resilience and positive coping mechanism of the affected population have reduced due to the protracted nature of the conflict. The economic and social situation in Syria continues to decline and severely affect everybody especially those who have been displaced from or returned to their homes. Statistics reveal that two-thirds of Syrians are living in extreme poverty, unable to meet basic food and non-food needs. The main reasons for poverty are loss of property and jobs, loss of access to public services, including health and clean water, and rising food prices.
According to HNO 2023, Nearly 85 per cent of the overall population reported a deterioration in their ability to meet basic needs, demonstrated through a significant increase in the inaccessibility of NFI items, such as clothing, mattresses, and blankets.
Coupled with the deterioration of shelter integrity, increasing shelter deterioration of shelter integrity, increasing shelter adequacy issues (i.e. limited electricity/lighting and poor insulation from heat/cold) households are exposed to harsh weather conditions with a high risk of loss of life, particularly during winter. Adequate shelter conditions and access to basic NFIs is vital for maintaining good physical health, mental well-being, and to sustain learning and educational opportunities.
The purpose of this project is to provide winterization items to affected people living in target areas.
If the commodities in local markets available, the distributions modality in this intervention will be through vouchers for winter items, Otherwise, it will be in-kind according to sector standards.
The program aims to provide in-kind assistance and essential equipment that are urgently needed and not financially available to the population. The main goal of the program is prevention, reducing protection risks, and avoiding resorting to harmful adaptation mechanisms to suit the specific circumstances of families.
Recent natural disasters that have occurred in the past two years in Tartous Governorate, starting from the earthquake disaster to the flooding of the southern region, have exacerbated the effects of the long crisis, leading to new displacements and worsening conditions for the displaced people in the governorate. Therefore, the most needy families that meet the following criteria will be targeted: 1- Internally displaced families, returnees, and the host community. 2- Beneficiaries who meet the criteria based on vulnerabilities, including but not limited to: women heads of households, persons with disabilities, individuals with severe mental disorders, elderly without family or community support, children at risk, unaccompanied or separated children from their families, etc.
Humanitarian guidelines that must be maintained include: 1- Respecting the confidentiality and privacy of individuals and families at all times. 2- Respecting the confidentiality of information and sharing it only after clear and explicit consent. 3- Respecting the wishes, choices, rights, and dignity of the individuals receiving support. 4- Conducting home visits at convenient times for individuals and families and informing them in advance. 5- Ensuring non-discrimination in all interactions with individuals and families throughout the assessment and service delivery period. 6- Applying principles and laws to prevent abuse and sexual exploitation. 7- Respecting the separation of women and men during implementation and distribution operations and providing appropriate facilities for persons with special needs.
Monitoring and evaluation will be conducted three months after the delivery of the items only once to assess the response to the specific needs, evaluate its impact on the well-being of the beneficiary and their family, and determine if there is a need for further assistance.
