Project Summary

The project aims to contribute to the sustainable food security of 5,000 (direct project targets) and 6,788 (indirect project targets) poor rural populations in the district of Sodo Bu'ee, SNNPR, Siraro and Fentale Districts of Oroma region, Ethiopia. Through its interventions, target households in the 27 Kebeles of the three districts will be able enhance and sustain emergency food production through Introducing drought resilient agricultural material and better cropping practices and DRR awareness of most vulnerable community groups; Enhance the livelihoods of vulnerable households through asset building and income generation activities.
The major activities of the project revolve around food security, prevention of hunger/building resilience, innovation in Agriculture, and family-based economic strengthening.
Key activities include, preparatory and activities to kick of the project, provision of improved seeds and fertilizers for selected target groups and associated trainings; provision of small ruminants and associated technical supports, Provision of supplementary livestock feed to vulnerable households; different trainings for the target communities and woreda stakeholders, training target communities and organizing them into VSL groups and supporting women with other IGAs and cross cutting interventions.
The project is planned to be implemented over 18 months period and entails 350,000 USD.

Unlike some other stakeholders such as donors and government agencies, the people whom ChildFund aims to assist have very little power to hold us accountable for our actions.
