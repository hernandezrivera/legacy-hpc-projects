Project Summary

This project is in line with the Food Security and Livelihoods (FSL) sector objective (SO1and SO2)and the GBV sector’s Strategic Objectives (SO1 & SO 2), part of the revised HRP 2023, which was done mainly to respond to the current conflict in Sudan(15 April 2023) and due to this emergency situation the cost of the revised 2023 HRP will only focus on lifesaving activities., The project activities are designed to improve access to lifesaving assistance for the food-insecure and provide protection for IDPs affected by recent crises besides the IDPs project will target returnees and host communities who are vulnerable to Food security, & Gender-Based Violence in South Kordofan. This project targeted 7,319 60% of them IDP and segregated as follows: ( 3,660 Women HHs, 1,097 men, 1,098 boys, and 1,464 girls) aiming to improve access to lifesaving assistance for the food-insecure and help affected women and girls face an increased risk of GBV including sexual violence during displacement due to the current crisis post-15 April 2023. Specifically, Al buram, Kadogli, and Ar reif Ash Shargi localities. in Kadogli and Ar reif Ash Shargi. and Al buram locality.
The planned interventions contributed to HRP -FSL SO1 – SO2 improving the food security status by providing an integrated multi-sectoral lifesaving response, including protection assistance to save and sustain lives and GBV. Provide life-saving specialized quality GBV services, including CMR, case management, and psychosocial support, and reinforce referral pathways. (With a focus on women and girls with disabilities and adolescent girls) GBV objective 2 GBV Enhance strategies for community resilience through the empowerment of women and girls and strengthened community-based interventions GBV objective 3 Promote GBV risk mitigation into all aspects of the humanitarian response
This intervention will be through the following activities:
For Food security and livelihoods:
Agriculture support services including improved seeds (staple and cash crops and vegetables), including hand tools for 4,120. provide emergency livestock, inputs, and veterinary services – including vaccination, treatments, and extension services to 1090 HHS. Provision of goat restocking for 1090 HHS (600Femal HHS). Provision Cash/Vouchers Based Transfer (Half Ration) to 2695. vulnerable HHs, 899 Al buram locality, and 898 Kadogli, and 898 Ar reif Ash Shargi localities for HHs from each locality.
For Gender Base Violence
Women, adolescent girls, and at-risk groups on the move and crossing borders due to the current conflict in Sudan(15 April 2023) are exposed to increased risks of GBV and exploitation GBV Protection interventions will help them access proper and safe assistance, in terms of community awareness-raising through campaigns on GBV targeting 3,199 women, men 60% of them women from the three localities, and community leaders. Establish Women Centers. With operational support to run the center. Provision of 3000 dignity kits. This project aims to reduce and prevent gender-based violence and support targeted beneficiaries, especially women and girls to improve their livelihood and food security status and increase their capacity to prevent and respond to Gender-Based Violence as a tool for campaigning against Gender-based violence, confidence building, and skills development.

1 - Needs

Clashes between the Sudanese Armed Forces (SAF) and the Rapid Support Forces (RSF) that erupted on 15 April 2023 increased the violence across the country and caused tremendous human suffering, accordingly, Sudan HRP 2023 has been revised and updated response strategy of the initial 2023 HRP regarding the scale-up of lifesaving multisectoral assistance and protection services in line with HRP Strategic Objectives (SO) 1 and 2, including related implementation approaches and costs. The proposed project is an Integrated Multi-sectoral Lifesaving Assistance project that covers FSL and GBV interventions offering lifesaving and protection services, which will be critical to ensure privacy and safety, mitigating GBV risks, and safeguard displaced populations from harsh environmental conditions. this project is designed to be implemented in South Kordofan states, in 3 localities; Al buram, Kadogli, and Ar reif Ash Shargi. The main planned interventions are under two sectors, FSL, and GBV. This project aims to increase the capacity of affected displaced People especially women to prevent and respond to Gender Based Violence through food and livelihood activities as a tool for campaigning against Gender-based Violence, confidence building, and skills development. This project will improve the beneficiary’s living status and livelihood condition for the most vulnerable, including social cohesion support in the three localities in South Kordofan State( targeted 7319 people). In Sudan, especially in the conflict area’s Gender-Based Violence (GBV) is a crucial concern within the different communities affected by intercommoned conflict and irruption of the 15 April 2023 war. Women, girls and vulnerable people, and people with disabilities in these communities face an ever-increasing risk of GBV. Due to the stigma, Survivors of GBV lack adequate access to quality specialized lifesaving services, reporting mechanisms, and legal/justice interventions. Women and girls try to protect themselves from violence in ways that will limit their freedoms and opportunities to get their businesses. Moreover, JASMAR designed this project activity under HRP strategic objectives O1( Lifesaving) and O2 ( protection ) and in line with sector strategic objectives

2 - Activities/Outputs

The project activities are designed in a community-based approach to contribute to eliminating suffer of 7319 IDP returnees and host communities (60% of them are female-headed HHs) were provided with agriculture support services including improved seeds (staple and cash crops and vegetables), hand tools and extension, and GBV services.For this intervention, the planned activities are as below: This intervention aims to provide food security and livelihood to improve their living status protection services. The planned activities are the provision of agricultural inputs and services including improved seeds for 4120 Farmers. The project will also provide emergency livestock inputs and veterinary services – including vaccination, treatments, and extension services for 1090 HHs the project will also support the livelihoods of 1090 beneficiaries (600 female-headed HHs) through a goat restocking program. These inputs will build the capacity of the targeted IDPs, returnees, and vulnerable host communities to achieve their own food security and improve their livelihoods by increasing their production and income. Provision Cash/Vouchers Based Transfer (Half Ration) to 2695. vulnerable HHs, 899 Al buram locality, and 898 Kadogli, and 898 Ar reif Ash Shargi localities for HHs from each locality.Raising awareness in the Community through campaigns on GBV targeting 3,199 women, men from the three localities, and community leaders. The project will build the capacity of 10 local actors, government bodies staff for each locality, and health cadres (nurses, midwives.) on GBV, its type, consequence, and actions to prevent GBV, reduce GBV incidents, and mitigate its negative impact. Influential people such as religious leaders, community leaders & native administration will also be trained on GBV and engage in dialogue to reduce and prevent GBV. The project will provide psycho-social support to victims of GBV and facilitate their referral to relevant specialized competent institutions. The project will also distribute 3000 dignity kits to the most vulnerable women to ensure that women’s menstrual health is not compromised. Women centers will be constructed in each locality to run women and girls-oriented activities. With operational support to run the center.
