Project Summary

the project is supporting education process in new liberated area (Mosul) through The following activities.
First proposed activity will be a quick rehabilitation of 10 schools (windows, doors, washbasin, electric). Distributing of teaching and learning materials will also vital, in addition to supply basic materials to schools (water stores, cleaning tools, and furniture). Promotion life skills, lifesaving messages and peace education programs in the schools, and support the training of teachers and other education personnel on PFA, PSS, child- centered, protective and interactive methodologies; and classroom management. Psycho social support training for students and teachers and the children individual case management at school level. Finally, supporting the conﬂict affected children, adolescent and youth (3-17) to teaching and learning materials and cash assistance to cover transportation costs.
BOHD registered in the NGOs Directorate in Baghdad, and also NGOs department in Erbil.

Needs

Our teams have visited East Mosul, and conducted rapid Education needs assessments on the ground and collected data about education gaps and needs by interviewing Mosul governorate council members, education authorities, Directors of schools, teachers and students. They found many schools need quick rehabilitations (including windows, doors, electric systems, washbasins, toilet, water tanks). We contacted the Ninewa Directorate of Education in Mosul who informed us where the re-opened schools were that need many services, particularly restoration of windows, doors, and water stores (tanks). The details of school assessments are attached in documents section, and was used to inform our project design.The needs identified in the 10 school buildings were:235 windows need replacing;40 doors need repairing or replacing;35 classrooms need new furniture;32 student toilets need plumbing maintenance or replacing;50 water tanks need rehabilitating.120 students need transfer fees.200 teachers need transportation fees.220 of orphan students need cash for education.45 teachers need incentives.10 schools need a culture of tolerance and coexistence. Learning supplies needed for 5400 students.100 of children (boys/girls) needs to receiving individual case management at school level.5400 of children (boys/girls) needs to receiving Pyschosocial Support 140 of teachers,10 social workers needs to trained on positive discipline, school codes of conduct, PSEA, gender based violence.

Protection Risk Analysis

team could be in insecure areas and the security situation in East Mosul deteriorates; construction teams cannot source the necessary tools and equipment for rehabilitation works.Teaching and student kits available children and teachers require more specialized support aside from PSS, so referrals will need to be made.

Mitigation Measures

Protection measures:Coordination with the competent security authorities. Using collaboration and management based on results (results management) Surely the collaboration and active participation of all participants of the program( targeted people, the organization, related institutions and host people ) on decision making process , the process of describing the problem and setting suitable solutions for it, managing and analyzing the out puts of each activity in order to put interim goals toward achieving project goals. The organization uses applying conflict-sensitive approaches in the whole project as the following: 1. Circumstance analyzing period: Participating of basic factors in analyzing, preparing a map for factors, decision making according to the level of analyzing( total, middle, partial) discussing the exodus plan with the partners. 2. Project designing period: Locating the probable effects of the project on the social groups according to the principle of ( Do No Harm), sanctioning if it is necessary to modify one of the project aspects, registering the risks on regional block, planning for new activities to reduce the risks keenness, modifying the budget. 3. Implementation period: Informing unrelated groups with the project, modifying circumstance analyzing, measuring the interaction between the circumstance and the project. 4. Monitoring and Evaluation Stage: verifying hypotheses related to specific risks, deputing employees transparently, ensuring that investments are fair and transparent, to ensure the security of workers in the conflict. 5. Exit phase: reviewing the exit plan during all phases, consulting with the partners and informing them about the exit plan.
