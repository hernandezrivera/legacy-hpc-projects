Project Summary

The project of distributing food baskets, breakfast meals, and charitable vouchers during the month of Ramadan is one of the permanent and ongoing projects of the Shabab Alkhair Association, through which comprehensive food baskets are provided to families registered with the Orphan Relief Office, the Social Assistance Office, after studying the case and assessing the needs and severity of vulnerability. The program targets single women (widows and divorcees) registered in the Orphan Sponsorship Office and female breadwinners, in addition to the elderly, families of individuals with disabilities, and the poorest families. They are classified into categories, and they all live in the city of Homs and its countryside.
Due to the deteriorating economic situation, high prices of basic foodstuffs, and lack of job opportunities, the number of families registered with the relief office increased to (5,000) families. The average number of members of one family is six, who need to secure the basic nutritional needs of individuals.
The food basket distribution project, which the association self-finances, does not only aim to distribute primary food items, but also aims to protect women, the elderly, and people with disabilities from the risk of exploitation and exposure to any sexual assault, and to reduce child protection risks such as child labor and school dropout due to their economic situation. Which leads to child labour. Providing the basic needs of individuals, namely food, and reducing the risks of malnutrition and diseases that may result from a lack of nutrients necessary for health.
