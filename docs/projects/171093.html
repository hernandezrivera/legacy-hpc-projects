Project Summary

MARDO urgently calls for aid to safe and dignify lives and livelihoods among the extremely vulnerable households in the IPC 3 and 4 drought-hit, poverty ravaged, under-served and under-funded but formerly hard to reach and access in Bakol region. A rapid scale up is needed given the surge in the number of people in IPC 3 & 4 which most likely increase through December 2019. Without humanitarian assistance, the people facing crisis and emergency food insecurity (IPC 3 and 4) worsen into huge food consumption gap and engage in irreversible coping strategies contrary to UNFPA 2014 population survey which says less than 17% region’s population live in the urban, for some time now, the migration from rural areas to urban increased due to the drought, the humanitarian sanction imposed by Insurgents and the IDPs in Rabdhure district area of accessible and the vulnerable drought affected HHs in Elbarde districts of Bakol region. Thus, there are very many IDPs, poor urban and rural dropout vulnerable HHs of whom their situation is very grave as the assessed in August 2020 by MARDO. Adhering to humanitarian vulnerability categorization/ranking and specifically targeting, The Unaccompanied children, Older Persons, Persons with disability, Single Women, Single Headed-households, Persons with chronic illness or psychological condition, Large family and/or family with babies, toddlers or children attending school, Persons at risk (to be identified by protection officers); MARDO identified over 1,000 vulnerable HHs in Rabdhure area of accessible and Elbarde towns. Based on this, MARDO proposes 12 months unconditional cash support for 800 most vulnerable households among these. According to FSNAU-FEWS-NET- Post-Gu-Technical-Report released - 2 Sept 2019, the number of people facing acute food insecurity in Bakool region are 168,000 persons of which 53,000 are in crisis/emergency. This is an increase of 116% when compared the total reported in February this year by FSNAU, where 78,000 persons only were food insecure in the entire region. in this report, the humanitarian situation in Bakool is not expected to improve in the near future and the number of people currently facing emergency will most likely increase from 8,000 (Sept 2019) to 26,000 till December this year. Malnutrition remains the major problem persisted over time in Somalia with high levels of Global Acute Malnutrition (GAM) well above emergency threshold of 15% (NCC - Detailed Input for HNOHRP 2019 and the humanitarian situation continued to deteriorate because of the poor rains in Gu, causing widespread food insecurity exacerbated by large-scale movement of people in Bay and Bakool regions. MARDO, in agreement with the national/humanitarian response plan and drought intervention strategy on Food Security Cluster, adopts the unconditional transfers (food assistance and cash) and conditional transfers (cash-for-work for small-scale infrastructure (75 KM feeder-road). Cash-based intervention addresses the low-purchasing power per person and per household in Rabdhure area of accessible and Elbarde district of Bakool region accessing functional money. Cash dignifies the beneficiaries; used for buying food, drugs, vegetables, fruits and milk, re-stocking livestock and acquiring veterinary related drugs and equipment, paying off debts, paying school-fees and increases the credit-worthiness among the economically vulnerable persons. The proposed intervention (conditional and unconditional cash transfer) will increase the target beneficiaries’ access to food through self-purchase nutritionally rich-food: The program will also improve the local market through injection of considerable amount of money into the local economy as the unconditional cash beneficiaries will purchase their food requirements from the local markets.

Project Activities

For the past seven months the number of persons facing humanitarian crisis only in Bakool region increased 116% - i.e. from 78,000 persons to 168,000 persons (FSNAU-FEWS-Reports – February and Sept 2019) and will most likely continue increase till December 2019. In addition, leaving in war-torn country where all the basic social services have been frozen for so long and covering the 100% needs of these people have always been impossible, MARDO put more effort to systematically select the most vulnerable drought affected households from the returnees, IDPs and the drought affected population in Rabdhure area of accessible and Elbarde districts of Bakool region – SWS of Somalia. Reaching the people who are most in need of assistance is central to the humanitarian mandate and is reflected in the humanitarian principles of humanity and impartiality by ensuring both access to protection and assistance in safety and dignity. In a humanitarian emergency, people with disabilities, IDPs, women-headed households, unaccompanied minors are often among those most in need of assistance as they are at heightened risk of violence, exploitation or abuse. These groups and among others also face discrimination due to significant barriers in accessing needed humanitarian assistance and these are the concerns that the proposed program aims to intervene through improving household access to food and other basic needs which are the factors determine the vulnerability and nutrition level. The program entirely aims at improving households’ purchasing power and access food varieties hence elevate the malnutrition level. In addition, MARDO has identified in recent assessment that the unconditional cash beneficiaries can purchase the food varieties they need from the local markets and, as a result, the program will also revitalize the local market by injecting a substantial amount of money into the local economy Moreover, the following include the program specific target beneficiaries; What is the justification for using cash or vouchers instead of in kind?

In humanitarian settings, aid agencies often assist populations by transferring resources, usually using cash, vouchers or in-kind aid, such as food rations, shelter materials, seeds and tools and kits of household items. The use of cash or vouchers to replace in-kind aid is most pronounced in food assistance programming, but it is increasingly being considered for emergency shelter, education and many other kinds of programming. Several manuals and reports discuss the relative advantages and disadvantages of providing aid in-kind, giving recipients cash that they can use to meet their needs as they think best, or giving them some form of voucher, which makes the assistance available through a local market system but restricts to some degree what recipients can buy or where they can make their purchases. The choice of how to transfer assistance – whether through in-kind aid, cash or vouchers – to people in crises may be an important decision. In order to constantly improve practice, the humanitarian community needs evidence about the diverse impacts of different choices. In order to ensure that the best decisions are made, evidence is also needed about the decision-making processes that lead to these choices. However, evaluations of these interventions have rarely examined this choice, and no explicit guidance exists for evaluators on how to examine the decision or the decision-making process. This guidance is a first attempt to fill that gap, with the aim both of encouraging evaluators to give more explicit attention to the choice of transfer and also providing some assistance on how this can be examined.

Explain how the project design considered market functionality ?

The feasibility of cash and vouchers rests on the expected future ability of appropriate price and quality7 markets to respond to increased demand if cash or vouchers were provided, Access of beneficiaries to markets and not simply on the state of the market before any intervention. Decision making should include consideration of possible measures that could be taken to address any market constraints (e.g. addressing constraints facing traders, such as capital, credit, storage and transport). Seasonal, regional and global trends can all influence prices. Possible price rises caused by external factors do not rule out the use of cash and vouchers, if they can be managed through the programme design. There is no consensus on when a comprehensive assessment of markets is necessary when a ‘light’ assessment makes sense or the extent of analysis required in areas with well-functioning markets. If in-kind assistance is purchased locally or regionally, the ability of traders to ensure timely supply at an appropriate price and quality may be critical. The potential impacts on markets of different types of transfers should also be considered.

Mitigation Measures

MARDO always considers gender equity from the planning to implementation through the monitoring and the evaluation of projects. A gender dimensions based on understanding of women, girls, boys and men's different needs, roles, responsibilities, capacities and risks are also put into consideration throughout the project period. Generally, with the aim of promoting community participation and engagement on the project activities, MARDO forms a group of committee members from the community named Project Community Committees (PCC). During the formation of the PCC, the project considers gender proportion by ensuring all categories of the community are represented in the project committees. In this regard, the members from traditional leaders, elders, women and youth groups, religious leaders and disable groups (including physical handicapped and hearing/visually impaired people) and the local authority will all be represented in the project community committees. Therefore, the intervention will ensure equity through equal selection representative from all categories of the community in the consultation, decision making and capacity development. MARDO will collect gender-disaggregated data by sex and age of relevant data related to Nutrition activities. MARDO will ensure that all segments of the drought affected population including women have equal access to humanitarian assistance and that targeted support to advance gender equality is based on a gender analysis. The program will meet the distinct needs of women, girls, boys and men to generate positive and sustainable outcomes. MARDO shall apply gender segregated community consultation meetings enabling women and girls as well as the disables to address their specific concerns, prioritize their needs and appropriately inform the development of proposed activities. MARDO shall Endeavour to provide equal chance, inclusiveness in participation of minority and majority community members as well as disabled groups in the district and be able to transparent enough in terms of employment, capacity building, empowerment, and resource sharing hence anticipated to foster comprehensive community engagement in the target locations. As part of inception activities, MARDO will collect information on areas of concern in regard to both vertical and horizontal equality approach. MARDO will be protection conscious, particularly, during the selection and payment of the program beneficiaries.
