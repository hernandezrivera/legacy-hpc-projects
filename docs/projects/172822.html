Project Summary

This Project aim to support the population identified by the WASH sector HRP in area of omdafog locality whom currently estimated to be about 5107(2553 Adults Females, ,2042 adults males, 512 children) people in needs. This project also will provide water and sanitation service as per WASH sector standard and strategies.
This project will provide wash awareness and preparedness for targeted host communities and refugees in moshag sub locality ,SAO will also construct 5 hand pumps in Um dafog locality.
SAO will also provide hygiene kits to targeted estimated beneficiaries and health centers

1 - Needs

Um dafog , is one of the localities in South Darfur affected by the tribal conflict during recent years and therefore.The communities have moved away from their own villages for quite long time then their access to water and other resources also negatively affected Over the past two years, the general humanitarian situation gradually became critical as the communities are increasingly suffering from different types of humanitarian gaps, such as water shortage for example especially from February 2020 update due to funding gaps for SAO regarding WASH and project In Um dafog which resulted negative impact within the local community. and There is high demand for water, Sanitation and SAO is planning to implement this project to cover hygiene services. therefore there are 5107 both host communities and refugees people identified by the WASH sector HRP in area of Um dafog locality and this conclusion was drawn from numerous field trips and inter agency missions. SAO aims to provide access to proper WASH facilities for the targeted population, mainly focusing on the host communities and refugees and women at a risk death during water collection due to the far distances between the camps and the water sources. Child safety is also a concern, not only because they are often with their mothers but also because they are exposed to feaces (outdoor defecation) which in the long term could lead to diseases and an increased risk of mortality. In addition, children are more likely to be affected by water-related diseases. been reporting hence the project will focus on rehabilitation of the water sources beside the hygiene promotion actives as well health kits distributions in healthy centers and refugees targeted from moshed sub locality.There is need on focusing on water hand pumps construction in Um dafog locality such that water accesses can be near instead of long distances except the farming activities mixed with pastoralists to prevent waterborne diseases awareness programs have to be carried out by SAO.
