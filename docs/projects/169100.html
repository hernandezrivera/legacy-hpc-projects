Project Summary

The August 4 blast is expected to exacerbate vulnerabilities and translate into rights violations in several of the affected neighbourhoods, including housing and labour rights. The blast area is home to many migrant, refugee, and disadvantaged communities which are at risk of falling between the cracks when it comes to recovery plans and reparation. To safeguard the rights of vulnerable groups, UNDP seeks to support:
- Legal aid desks operated by the Beirut Bar Association in 8 of the affected areas, as well as through the Bar's website. These desks provide survivors of the blast including vulnerable groups with legal counselling, information, and referral to relevant services;
- In partnership with OHCHR support the dissemination through various channels of facts/information sheets/pages tailored to specific legal and human rights related issues pertaining to the blast;
- In partnership with OHCHR support efforts of the National Human Rights Commission to safeguard the rights of the most vulnerable by conducting field visits to affected areas and communicating on key issues;
- Advocate for policy revisions/adaptation in order to ensure to protect the rights of vulnerable groups.
Close coordination with OHCHR will be ensured for the development and dissemination of rights related information, and complementary provision of legal aid services, including referrals of individual cases
