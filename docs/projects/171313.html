Project Summary

The proposed project aims at reaching out to 3,400 individuals (1,00 girls, 1,000 boys, 500 adolescents, 400 persons with disabilities and 500 old-age persons) in Banadir, Somalia with protection services that include Case Management and Referral Pathways, Community-based Services, MHPSS, Specialized Services, mine action and victim assistance services. One of the key interventions of the project will be search and identification of the abandoned girls and boys and those released from armed forces and groups reintegrated with their families/communities and provided with adequate care and services. The project will be implemented through 12 months period and will cover the protection needs of both IDPs and non-IDPs. The project will kick start with assessment and mapping of protection gaps and service. From very onset of the project, HA will develop a profound working relationship with the communities in hard-to-reach areas to collect data on the movement of the population, identify protection issues, and analyze needs and workout case management, referrals and other to the relevant services. The major aim is to fast track the most convenient and appropriate response, coupled with high-impact interventions to hard-to-reach areas of the country, this ensures that the most disadvantaged persons of concern benefit from the project. The project will ensure to initiate collaborative sessions with the policy makers and strengthen cross sectoral and interagency linkages. This can pave the way for improved service and protection networking and include children, adolescents, persons with disabilities and the old as the key target groups for improved access to preventative and responsive protection services.
Up to 66% of Somalia’s population comprise of children under the age of 18 years. They endure multiple protection risks including recruitment into the armed groups resulting into grave violation of their rights. HA Somalia will ensure to implement projections from the national taskforce and work closely with the relevant ministries to help curb limited access to the most urgent humanitarian assistance. Somalia has 15% of the population living with disabilities; this group lacks access to the most immediate and basic protection services.
HA Somalia will ensure all basic information is accessible to persons with disabilities, help capture centralized data on persons with disabilities, register the particular protection risks they experience, and barriers they face to access services, make protection services fully accessible and ensure reasonable accommodation measurements are in place for them. Further, HA Somalia will to bring together organizations of persons with disabilities to allow them adapt interventions, information and communication.
HA envisions a collaborative multi-stakeholder exercise that will aim to provide relevant data and analysis of the protection needs and prospects for durable solutions. To achieve this objective, HA plans to use the Inter-Agency Standing Committee (IASC) Framework on Durable Solutions for Persons of concern. An integrated institutional framework for the identification, inclusion and support for physical and rights-based protection would offer a galvanized approach to enhancing resilience.

Project Activities

Needs Assessment and Mapping of Prevention and Response services- HA will conduct needs assessment and mapping of protection gaps and services available. Based on the findings and analysis together with the protection cluster, HA will start implementation of the proposed project.Case Management for Child Protection for children recruited into armed conflict (CAAFAC): HA will establish case management and referral services to the children and adolescents in compliance with the protection cluster prioritization criteria. The children that have lost both their parents and those whose parents cannot take care of their children will be identified through the community committees; the alternative care arrangements will be worked out and arranged. During the mobilization and capacity building process, HA will identify abandoned children and search their parents if they are alive for unification and the community committees will have leading role for this task. HA will provide capacity building sessions to the parents on skills for positive parenting and family strengthening to acquire maximum emergency resilience as well as integration of children recruited into the armed groups while giving them the maximum care they need. Best Interest Procedure—BIP: HA will employ BIP to provide best case management to the children. It will include conducting BIA to understand an individual child’s best interests and taking them the foremost while considering the case management options. Once BIA had been conducted, HA will go for BID exercise by engaging the individual boy/girl/adolescent what is the best option for him/her. BIA and BID will be conducted engaging the experienced team and other specialized stakeholders.Persons with Disabilities: HA, through the community committees, will identify persons with disabilities and tailor its interventions focused on them so that they can also proactively participate in all the interventions and where required they will be referred to the actors that can provide them assistive devices. Where required, HA will provide them with assistive devices and other special requirements they need so that children and adolescents and the old age persons with different disabilities will proactively participate in the project interventions.Community-Led/Based Approach and Specialized Services: HA will launch mass mobilization campaign with the IDP and non-IDP communities to sensitize and educate them on protection. Community committees will be formed, terms of reference for those committees will be developed and they will be trained on prevention and response, MHPSS and mine action services at community level. Besides, specialized MHPSS and victim assistance services will also be provided to at-risk-persons including persons with disabilities and the aged persons.Engagement of Leadership: Human Appeal will, based on its long-term working relationship on matters of protection with existing community leadership hierarchies to help in the initial identification of the persons of concern. In most of the Somalia context, community protection working have in-depth understanding of the protection needs within the community and HA will reinforce their capacities and skills in prevention and response, protection risk mitigation and quicker response at communal level. Gender Mainstreaming: HA will ensure inclusion of the most vulnerable persons, including girls and boys, adolescents, persons with disabilities and the old both IDP and non-IDP, being members of the community committees for decision making and leadership roles, recipients of services and owners of the project beyond its life span.Coordination and Networking: HA envisions a collaborative multi-stakeholder exercise that will aim to provide relevant data and analysis of the protection needs and prospects for durable solutions. To achieve this, HA plans to use the Inter-Agency Standing Committee (IASC) Framework on Durable Solutions for Persons of concern

Mitigation Measures

Mitigation Measures Risk 1: Human Appeal will conduct a detailed assessment and mapping of protection gaps and services and all the project interventions will be around its findings. There will be massive mobilization, awareness raising and education campaign at the start of the project where all the information related to different protection services will be shared with the communities and specially the target groups. HA also will provide case management, MHPSS, mine action, victim assistance and specialized services. HA will develop referral pathways and facilitate the target groups to access specific services each of them requires. HA in its all programmes strictly adheres to PSEA Standards and will ensure there is strict compliance to these standards while implementing the proposed project. HA will also ensure strict adherence to Protection Mainstreaming Principles including: i) safety and dignity in implementation, ii) meaningful access to services, iii) participation and empowerment of affected communities to the services provided, and iv) accountabilityRisk 2: The proposed project will follow inclusive approach and all the vulnerable groups will be encouraged to participate at all stages of the project cycle management. Persons with disabilities, women, girls, boys, and old age persons will be part of the committies and participate in all the consultations and the decision making processes. Boys and girls and children with disabilities will have equal benefit from the project interventions. The project will use Washington Group Questions during the assessments and tailor the interventions so that the persons with disabilities can easily participate in and benefit from the project.Risk 3: There will be robust Complaint/Feedback mechanism in place and everyone will have access to it. There will be sessions with the beneficiaries on how to register complaints/feedback. Contact numbers will be displayed at central places, female case workers and other female staff focal points will be nminated and communicated to the beneficiaries to share their complaints/feedback. A clear complaint redressal mechanism will be in place and the beneficiaries and other key stakeholders will be informed of the mechanism

Please provide further explanation

The project will fully support all mechanisms intended to assist individuals with protection needs. The environment where the intervention will be carried out will be stable and can allow for more important resilience programs to be implemented. The project is intended to support all efforts by the local administration and the federal line ministries to take responsibility on the protection of the affected population. More so, the project puts more emphasis on the inclusion of the structures of the community-they are made to be project. Since it is set to benefit them, their valuable inputs and integration of ideas to the core and clear processes is vital. All protection responses set out in the project are recognized and will ensure to address all concerns to the satisfaction of the affected populations
