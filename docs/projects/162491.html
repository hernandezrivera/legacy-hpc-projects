Project Summary

The project overall goal is to improve food and nutrition security of most vulnerable displaced, returnees and host households in the Goundam circle. The project targeted areas of intervention include pastoral and agro-pastoral communities located in the Goundam circle, including: Alzounoub, Douekire, Gargando, Kaneye, Raz-El-Ma, Tilemsi and Tin Aicha. These communities have been identified through SI Multisectoral Diagnostic conducted in Timbuktu region. The MD conducted in December 2020 in Goundam and Diré circles identified food and nutrition insecurity pockets of vulnerability. Moreover, within the framework of OFDA funded project, SI -with the support of Timbuktu Regional Directorate of Hydraulics (DRH)- conducted a diagnostic of Goundam water points from December to January 2020. This diagnostic identified 16 water points in need of rehabilitation, among which 6 will be rehabilitated as part of the “Jiguisigui” project in 2020. The proposed integrated approach will maximize the expected impact on targeted populations.
The proposed intervention is targeting 7 most food insecure communities in Goundam who are not yet supported by other stakeholders. 4 out of the 7 identified communities are similar to communities targeted by “Jiguisigui”. The proposed intervention will leap on the water points rehabilitation to enhance pastoral and agro-pastoral livelihood activities. The combination of both projects will act directly on roots causes of food and nutrition insecurity including: access to food and livelihoods, prevention of water-borne diseases and nutrition awareness.
As part of its « do no harm approach”, SI has developed a vulnerability approach strategy and will target most vulnerable households regardless of their status (displaced, returnee or host). Indeed, SI latest diagnostic highlighted that given the similar vulnerability levels among the targeted communities between IDPs and host households it was essential to adopt an inclusive approach. Moreover, the proposed vulnerability approach will prevent further social tensions that have been fueled by massive movements of populations during the past years. SI's intervention strategy focuses on 3 objectives below-described:
Objective 1: To ensure most vulnerable households capacities to meet their basic food and nutrition needs.
Outcome 1: Direct and immediate improvement of the food security of most vulnerable populations through unconditional and non-restrictive cash transfers (CT).
Objective 2: To enhance most vulnerable households’ resilience capacity towards shocks through an integrated livelihood response.
Outcome 2: Improved most vulnerable communities’ livelihoods through support to agricultural and pastoral income generating activities
Objective 3: To raise awareness among most vulnerable communities towards nutrition best practices
Outcome 3: Improvement of malnutrition prevention through the implementation of training, screening and awareness raising activities
