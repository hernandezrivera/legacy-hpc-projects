Project Summary

To address the multi-sectoral needs, the project will provide a different and context based assistance that includes: ERL, FSL, WASH and Protection (CP). The project will take place in Aleppo (Jabal Saman and AZAZ) and in Idleb (Harim). SARD will conduct comprehensive need and market assessment to identify the needs, demands, and gaps of careers, jobs, and vocations. Thought out the process, SARD will ensure that women and PwDs are well represented in the data collection process (be it FGDs, KII, Surveys) which will reflect the needs from their own perspectives and in their words. All project data will be disaggregated by Gender and disability. This includes designating a certain target for women and SARD will do its best to meet 5-7 % of the target.
For ERL , the project aims to achieve the following outcomes: 1. Essential socioeconomic services and infrastructure are revived and restored through the creation of short-term work opportunities and rehabilitation work ( markets, Schools, roads, sewage, drainage) 2. Access to inclusive livelihoods and entrepreneurship support is enhanced through vocational training (VT) 3. Local small businesses and entrepreneurs are supported 4. Social cohesion among IDPs and host communities is enhanced.
As for FSL, the project will address the wide range of multi-sectoral needs, SARD’s intervention is comprised of the following: emergency and life-sustaining food assistance ( 100 $ of cash for 8 rounds through e-card) , linked with livelihood restoration opportunities ( IGA, 1200 $) as an exit strategy. As well as the provision of emergency one off MPC (150$). Cash will allow the assistance to be more effective and efficient in areas with accessible markets allowing for a higher marginal utility at the recipients’ end. Additionally, cash also has a multiplier effect which is expected to boost and stimulate the local economy
For WASH, this action aims to focus on more sustainable solutions at the community level, in which 5 water networks will be rehabilitated/upgraded to assist up to 1,500 families (7,500 individuals).in addition to rehabilitation/upgrading of 5 sanitation and sewage systems, targeting 1500 families (7.500 individuals ).Essential WASH NFIs are also part of this project since HK are a household-level WASH intervention recommended for use during emergencies, Covid-19 and cholera outbreak responses, and other crisis contexts; 3000 families (15.00 individuals) dispersed in open fields and informal settlements are anticipated to benefit from this service. Water and sanitation facilities also fall under this action where 6 facilities are planned to be rehabilitated (3 schools and 3 health facilities), benefiting up to 9.000 individuals (direct and indirect). The rehabilitation of public infrastructure facilities (water/sewer networks, schools, and health facilities) is anticipated to involve 50 percent of the local population and 50% of IDPs.
CP: SARD intends to support unaccompanied and separated children (UASC) and children at risk of separation through the extension of its specialized and unspecialized child protection services, which specially target sensitive issues such as family separation, child marriage, child labor, neglect, and violence.
CP services will be two-fold: prevention and response to the separation of children from vulnerable families. The preventative part is ensured through CM of children at risk of separation, living with vulnerable families, after a thorough assessment, a strategy is identified based on the individual needs while tackling the risks of the family as a whole. Response to separation will be carried out by the services provided through an Interim Alternative Care Center (IACC) for UASC. The critical situation of referred and/or identified children, who have mostly been living in the streets for a period of 3 months - 2 years, require immediate attention and is a real life-saving intervention encompassing all aspects of the child's life.

3 - Needs

latest HNO indicated that 14.6 million people in need, 5.34 million are Internally Displaced People and over two million reside in 1,760 last resort sites such as informal settlements and camps, planned camps and collective centres, the vast majority in Idleb (69%) and Aleppo (22%) governorates, with three sub-districts – Dana, Maaret Tamsrin in Idleb, and Azaz in Aleppo - alone hosting more than two-thirds of all IDPs in last resort sites in the country, 70% of them facing food insecurity and 42% in need of winterization support. In 2022, SARD’s conducted a YNA (Yearly Needs Assessment) which observed the needs in all sectors . For FSL, Findings showed the main challenge relating to food access pertained to the high price of food items in local markets, as reported by 78% of households, the low quality of available food (36%), and the lack of variety of goods in the local market (14%). As for ERL, the draft 2023 HNO for ERL indicated: As socioeconomic deterioration continues, Early Recovery and Livelihoods needs are expected to continue rising. For WASH: 2023 HNO for WASH indicated: Access to WASH services is compromised by prolonged decay of the existing systems, caused by damage over years of conflict, wear from long-term functioning at high capacity, limited or no maintenance, the continuous drain of technical staff, weak system management and poor natural resource management.

4 - Implementation of activities

With its unique galvanization approach, SARD will address the multi-sectoral needs as described. The assistance will be provided and range from emergency food assistance to restoring livelihoods, to early recovery to meeting wash and protection needs to the most vulnerable affected population. For WASH: Under its First Line Emergency Response (Ruslt1), SARD will target the most vulnerable IDP families scattered in the open fields in informal settlements with rapid emergency WASH assistance, including Hygiene kits for each family for three months. Compositions of the hygiene kit will be designed based on cluster recommendations, discussed and agreed upon with people of concern. Under result 2, SARD focuses on more sustainable solutions, looking at community level rather than the family level. Activities under result 2 include Rehabilitation and extension of existing water and sewage networks. Rehabilitation of water and sanitation facilities in schools and health facilities, SARD technical team conducts site visits and studies to prepare BoQs. Site works are implemented through contractors under close supervision by site inspectors. For PRO: A child-rights based approach will be adopted in all phases of the project cycle: design, implementation, and M&E. Children aren’t seen as mere recipients of humanitarian aid in general, but through this project, their participation is fostered, starting from an open dialogue and the formal consent from the child to open the case.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

For ERL, FSL and WASH within current context, the most vulnerable categories, such as women headed HH, PwD, may be excluded from accessing the service due to the socio-cultural barriers.Likelihood: medium, Impact: HighMitigation Measures. a) SARD increase visibility for self-referral to ensure inclusion of the most vulnerable individuals. b) SARD applies a scoring system to all selected beneficiaries in order to categories them according to their vulnerabilities. c) PWD friendly tools such as ramps and handrail will be provided, complaint response mechanism (CRM). PWD will be considered by designing tools and conducting focus group discussions FGDs. For PRO: Risk: Perpetuating stigma for children and their families while providing CM and/or parenting program or by providing low quality service and risk of physical safety of CP workers if perceived to be interfering with family concerns. Likelihood: Medium - Impact: High Mitigation Measures: SARD team is trained with materials that are in line with CP minimum standards and uses service maps that includes only quality services.Risk: Breaching the confidentiality of the UASC at the IACC. Likelihood: Medium - Impact: high Mitigation Measures: SARD sensitizes the staff and partners regularly on the accessibility and confidentiality policy of the IACC and holds data confidentiality to the highest standards.


8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

1) Risk: Beneficiaries of SARD services may be targeted for sexual exploitation/abuse by a staff member.Likelihood: low, Impact: highMitigation: Staff is aware of the PSEA policies and undergo regular refreshing training. In 2023, strengthening external knowledge and compliance with safeguarding policy, including PSEA, is also an institutional objective at SARD, through organising surveys and trainings of service providers associated with SARD.2) Risk: Beneficiaries of SARD services do not know, or do not trust, complaint reporting mechanisms (CRM).Likelihood: low, Impact: highMitigation: SARD is the reinforcing established complaint mechanisms to facilitate reporting of abuse, including referral pathways, to ensure a quick response in case of any incident. The objective is to make access to several channels easier for beneficiaries, to raise issues or concerns.3) Risk: The socio-cultural context in the area and the gender gap may be conducive to acceptance of abuse of power or sexual harassment.Likelihood: low, Impact: HighMitigation: awareness raising conducted within the communities will help to challenge gender stereotypes and patriarchal norms, and reinforce the idea of accountability towards affected population within SARD staff.


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

For ERL and FSL and WASH Risks -Persistency/return to negative coping mechanisms, such as the employment of children. Mitigation: the project will purposefully target households at risk of child labor or where child labor cases are present, with FSL, ERL opportunities. Moreover, the child labor cases will benefit from protection support in the context of the project. For PRO: Risk to physical safety of child protection service providers if perceived to be interfering in family-based concerns Likelihood: Medium, Impact: High Mitigation: staff conducting parenting program are trained on how to touch upon sensitive matters without triggering any reaction, beneficiaries are sensitized on the materials and consent is taken prior to implementation.
