Project Summary

Cox’s Bazar hosts the largest refugee camp in the world with more than 855,000 Rohingya refugees in an area of approximately 24 square kilometers. More than ten cases have been confirmed in different upazilla under Cox’s Bazar district, which is growing daily. Consequently, considering the increasing rate of confirmed cases, huge risks and threats have been foreseen for Cox's Bazar district and especially in refugee camps because of the congested settlement and population density.The outcome of this project is to decrease the likelihood of public health risks and outbreaks through the construction and management of safe water and sanitation facilities, distribution of WASH NFIs and delivery of hygiene awareness messaging.
Given that, IOM WASH is planning in the framework of this project to work together with implementing partners in 13 camps including some host community under Teknaf and Ukhiya upazilas to reduce risks and vulnerabilities related to COVID-19 and strengthen the WASH related responses. During preparedness mode, the procurement of hand washing stations for communal places (distribution centers, health post, SM hub, CIC office, religious centres, markets, etc.), soap and personal protective equipment (PPE) for operational staffs and volunteers will be undertaken while scaling up health promotion (including hand washing station management). In high alert mode, activities will focus on continuous dissemination of COVID-19 related messages: IEC materials & demonstration and handing hygiene material door-to-door. In emergency mode, the block/sub-block level volunteer-led WASH O&M team will be activated to continue regular operation and maintenance of WASH facilities including fecal sludge and solid waste management in the absence of humanitarian workers. Disinfection of water and sanitation facilities will be continuing during the emergency/critical mode.

Ensure the change of potentially dangerous behaviors through hygiene promotion and distribution of hygiene items with strong focus on highly contagious diseases, for all refugees, affected or vulnerable communities of the district.

Needs

13 cases (till 27 April) have already been confirmed in Cox’s Bazar district. Considering the spontaneous development of COVID-19 situation, huge risks and threats have been predicted for Cox's Bazar district and specially in refugee camps because of the congested settlement and population density. Cox Bazar hosts today more than 855,000 Rohingya refuges, in an area of approximately 24 square kilometers, with a poor living and hygienic condition. Furthermore, limited resources and necessary WASH infrastructures have the potential to exacerbate the overwhelming spread of the virus in a very short period within these communities. To prevent the disease transmission, improved handwashing facilities with soap and COVID-19 related hygiene awareness and practices along with the operation and maintenance of WatSan facilities are being considered as the key activities within WASH responses which are needed to be strengthened and continued.
