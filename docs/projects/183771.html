Project Summary

This project seeks to strengthen the protection and resilience of 49,490 refugees and vulnerable persons including persons with specific needs (PSNs) and the host community in East Sudan- Kassala and Gadarif states. Building on the efforts of 2021 as leading SHF,UNHCR Protection partner in East Sudan, ALIGHT will engage with and leverage community-based preventative and protective interventions towards delivering protection services to the affected populations. This will be implemented through existing coordination and collaborative initiatives that prioritize the inclusion of population of concern with specific protection needs and girls and women, among others. The range of services will include prevention and response to Gender Based Violence, (GBV), Protection of the population from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (SEA), legal information, counseling and assistance, community-based protection, support to PSNs and general protection for refugees and other persons of concern including the host community.. A harmonized set of comprehensive activities with local authorities and humanitarian actors will ensure that tools in place are utilized to make meaningful reductions in GBV and other forms of abuse and general protection concerns. The project will jointly implement community mechanisms to address GBV using community- based protection approach to GBV prevention and response and protection of the affected population.
To appropriately respond to the needs of the targeted population, 2022 Alight’s strategy will be to continuously identify PSNs and other extremely vulnerable individuals on arrival at reception centers and in the camp and ensure that errors of omission, non- inclusion are minimized. The key activities to undertake will include; identification of PSNs, individual case management and follow-ups, Psychosocial Support, ensure advocacy for equity and equality in service provision.
ALIGHT will continue to work closely with SHF, UNHCR, partners and community structures to have a better understanding of the protection challenges faced in communities, and will provide appropriate multi-sectoral tailored responses, as well as strengthen the community’s capacity so that they are at the center of their own protection and are able to address protection concerns that may be affecting them. The Refugee leadership committees which are the government-supported leadership structures in the camps, and other community structures will be strengthened through capacity-building (trainings) and other support to facilitate them to fully perform their functions in the best interests of the communities they represent. Trainings will focus on roles and responsibilities of refugee leaders, Sudan laws, human trafficking, code of conduct, humanitarian principles, gender equality, gender roles, roles of Government in protection of refugees, women’s participation in leadership and referral pathways.
For health and nutrition, the project has been designed to improve the health and nutrition status of 111,792 refugees in Kassala, Gedaref and East Sudan as well as est. 22,387 host community members. The project will provide an integrated package of primary health care including maternal health and child health, community health, nutrition and mental health and psychosocial support through primary healthcare centers and referral mechanism for emergency cases in need of secondary and tertiary health care. ARC/Alight will ensure capacity building of health workers and community structure members based on gaps assessment. Alight will also ensure the capacity improvements of primary health care centers in terms of infrastructure, equipment and materials as well as maintain the supply chain of essential medicines and medical supplies. ARC/Alight will ensure its interventions are aligned with Sudan humanitarian plan as well as refugee response plan through coordination and collaboration with UN agencies, Federal and state MoHs and other stakeholders.

The health system struggles to handle the multiple emergencies affecting the country. Health needs are exacerbated by the deteriorating economy, conflict, floods, and disease outbreaks. In September 2020, WHO classified Sudan as grade 2 acute health emergency (CRRP, 2021). This requires to reinforce epidemiological surveillance for timely informed interventions. The supply chain of essential medicines and medical supplies remains inconsistent and the pandemic of COVID-19 worsen the situation due to movement restrictions and ability to import with a need to sometimes opt to local procurement for lifesaving. In terms of nutrition, Children under five and pregnant and lactating women (PLW) are the main groups of concern as their nutritional needs are relatively high and deficiencies readily manifest when nutritional needs are not met. Under nutrition exacerbates morbidity and mortality risks especially for children, and cause physical and mental underdevelopment including cognitive development and a weak immune system in the long term. The floods and inflation have negatively impacted the food security thus increasing the risk of malnutrition. There is a need to continue to promote best maternal infant, young child feeding practices while reinforcing screening for timely enrolment in different programs. There is a need for Alight to ensure refugees and host community access to quality primary health care through increasing the capacity of PHCCs to respond to population needs.

2 - Activities/Outputs

In regard to GBV prevention and response, ALIGHT will employ a Compassionate survivor-centered approach to case management focusing on a holistic approach that provides clear, coherent pathways for survivor care as well as quality psychosocial support and counselling, working with staffs that are trained to provide safety, security, confidentiality, and prioritize the wishes of the survivor. Interventions will include strengthening community-based structures that provide support to survivors of GBV, Sexual Exploitation and Abuse, and to perpetrators who are choosing non-violence through fostering community activism, providing comprehensive case management services, including intake and assessment, referrals, safety planning, and case follow-ups.Other related activities to include capacity building- trainings for partner staffs, communities, stakeholders etc on GBV prevention and response, awareness raising, working with men and boys as champions of change, support to youth activities including sports and drama, dialogues on GBV, provision of basic legal information and counseling, strengthen Community Based Protection Networks, strengthen FRRMs, peace building, mediation and conflict resolution activities and mainstreaming GBV in other sectors e.g. in WASH, Health & Nutrition etc in abide to reduce the risk of GBV and improve the quality of response. Alight will leverage on the existing protection facilities- the women center & protection desks with upgrade. Alight through its professional and capacitated staffs, will provide specialized MHPSS to all refuges and host population while aiding recovering, and reconstruction of their communities. This will be done through provision of individual PSS sessions, outreaches for group therapies and awareness raising. In this regard, Alight will establish community -based spaces to serve as community outreach posts in abide to increase service coverage. These spaces will also serve as hubs for tailored Art Galleries, awareness sessions, trainings and distribution of IEC materials on MHPSS. Support to PSNs, ALIGHT will continue to strengthen Services for persons with specific needs through active identification of PSNs, provision of in-kind assistance as well as access to MPCA, offer Psychosocial support, case management for those with heightened protection risks, support referrals to PSNs to access appropriate multi-sectorial tailored services as per their specific needs. The project will promote and support participation and inclusion of PSNs and other vulnerable persons in community activities, leadership and service provision. Alight will employ the Rights Based Approach while promoting the rights and dignities of the affected population, AGD mainstreaming approach to ensure the project responds to needs of all irrespective of their Age, Gender, Diversity and Disability backgrounds. The project will ensure inclusion of all in project needs assessments, implementation, monitoring and evaluation, this will be aimed at strengthening capacities of the affected population to respond to their specific needs, as well as enhancing ownership and promoting sustainability and continuity. To reduce morbidity and mortality rates, Alight will continue provision of life saving primary health care services following key primary health care pillars: patient consultations, maternal reproductive health, child health, community health awareness, capacity building, health information systems, pharmaceutical, and maintenance of facilities infrastructure. These services will be provided in an integrated approach with Alight WASH sector which will support provision of environmental health, sanitation and hygiene services including provision of household latrines, waste management and disposal, community awareness and provision of hand washing facilities Alight will also support youths through creation of employment opportunities, life skills trainings and IGAs creation for their livelihoods.

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

Alight places protection at the center of its programming by undertaking efforts for immediate and life-saving activities throughout the duration of a crisis and consistently working with the affected communities to strengthen community-based protection. Alight employs strategic, comprehensive, and collective approach to protection in order to enhance the overall ability of our staff to analyze, prioritize and respond effectively to the protection needs of the affected populations including the risks and consequences of violence, abuse, coercion, and deprivation.ALIGHT will coordinate and support other organizations in project locations to incorporate protection principles and to promote meaningful access, safety and dignity while delivering proposed interventions. ALIGHT will ensure that the following elements are taken into consideration during service delivery; • Meaningful access- Pay special attention to women and girls from marginalized groups and those with special needs and ensure they are included in all activities and other services. In addition, while selecting suitable location for the services, the voices of the vulnerable groups will be prioritized to address barriers related to discrimination and access due to distance.• Prioritize safety & dignity and avoid causing harm-As much as possible, Alight will prevent and minimize unintended negative effects of the program that is likely to increase people’s vulnerability to physical and psychosocial risks.• Accountability-Alight will place a strong emphasis on receiving feedback from the individuals and communities that we seek to serve in order to improve ownership and the quality of our programs. A feedback/complaints response mechanism that both receives inputs from beneficiaries and regularly provides feedback will be utilized during this project.• To ensure that the needs of people with disabilities are taken into consideration, ALIGHT will use its technical team from WASH sector for designs of its facilities striving to create safe and friendly structures; easily accessible and accommodating to individuals with special needs. ALIGHT will adhere to the set of guidelines that are an intrinsic part of our program design paradigm. These include the IASC/ Global Protection Cluster Guidelines for Integrating Gender-Based Violence Interventions in Humanitarian Action and the Humanity and Inclusivity (HI) guidelines on design of interventions for people with disability. Illustrative actions. ALIGHT will ensure that inclusivity and appropriateness in GBV programming include:• Latrine doors and cubicles at the facilities will be wide enough to ensure access to those with limited mobility and their caregivers where suitable; handles and other utilities to support mobility will also be installed to facilitate their use. • Involvement of community volunteers to help the older people and those with disabilities to find their way into the facilities and back to their homes.

7.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will inform and interact with inter-agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including SEA and abuse complaints?

In regard to AAP;Alight will employ the AGD policy that will be implemented through community based, participatory approaches that are already well established in the refugee camps. The Project will ensure that participation, feedback and complaints mechanisms are integrated in all strategies and plans all throught the project cycle. This is to ensure the project decisions and actions are responsive to the expressed priorities, needs, capacities and views of all persons of concern irrespective of Age, Gender, Diversity and Disability backgrounds. This is aimed at ensuring meaningful participation, enhanced communication and transparency, improved feedback and response. ALIGHT will ensure a balanced boys, girls, women and men in all project activities with relative 60% female and 40% male. Data for beneficiaries will be disaggregated by gender, age groups and location, as the project activities are tailored for different groups in line with developmental stages, needs and vulnerabilities and basing on relevant parameters appropriate in reverence to heightened protection risks of the persons of concern. The Project will ensure at all persons of concern have equal and non- discriminatory access to protection, assistance and solutions, this will build on their resilience and recovery.The project will engage much better with boys, girls, men and women while in their safe spaces like the women & girls centers, children and adolescents friendly spaces, youth centers. Activities like awareness raising, dialogues, will be conducted in those spaces and in the communities, provision of specialized MHPSS, comprehensive case management processes, specific needs and vulnerabilities identification and culturally sensitive coffee corners and Art Galleries'.Alight will maintain effective feedback systems ( including comments, suggestions and complaints) using a variety of communication channels that are accessible to all persons of concern and that are appropriate for both sensitive and non- sensitive feedback e.g. use of complaints boxes, toll free hot lines. In regard to PSEA;All Alight staffs have committed to and signed PSEA policy, Child Protection policy, Code of Conduct as well as the engaged community based networks Alight is a member of National and State levels PSEA task force and regularly coordinates with other partners in relevant PSEA forums. Under the Project, we will conduct Safeguarding and SEA risk assessments to mitigate any potential or identified risks of SEA. Alight has contributed to Inter Agency Action Plan for the Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse and will ensure Zero Tolerance - No Excuse to SEA in all its operations and project. Jointly with the PSEA task force, we will develop relevant SEA IEC materials and distribute them in communities, raise awareness on SEA, strengthen community based complaints mechanisms, enhance reporting and ensure appropriate measures are undertaken for investigations.
