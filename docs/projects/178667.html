Project Summary

RI aiming to establish and support two child friendly CFS spaces in Azaz subdistrict in addition to four mobile team, RI already has present in the area through health , nutrition and GBV service and working on providing integration approach to response for the most vulnerable children in NWS through by adding community based and specialized child protection services.
in line with CP Minimum Standards RI , will provide specialized child protection considering what shall be in the best interests of the child through mobile outreach, counselling, referral, and case management services addressing boys and girls and caregivers’ vulnerabilities that are related to disability, physical/psychological injuries, severe neglect, lack of appropriate care, family separation and immediate risks of child labor, child recruitment and child marriage. Active family tracing will be implemented for cases need reunification and follow up visits to ensure reintegration is going according to plan.
RI will provide community-based child protection services through deploying outreach team to work on awareness raising on child protection concepts targeting men, women , girls and children and referral for identified children at risk to specialized services within the CFSs. In addition, RI will provide parenting skill and structured psychosocial support in the CFSs targeting men and women , as well as child protection monitoring in coordination with the CP sub-cluster in addition to facilitating the establishment of two community committee one of them composed from men and women and the other from adolescent boys and girls in the targeted areas and ensure appropriate training . This Committees will be responsible for raising awareness on CP concerns in the camps as well as identification and referral of children at risks to response services provided at the CFS.
RI will conduct a service mapping to ensure safe referral pathway in place. Moreover, RI will mobilize their health, nutrition, physiotherapy, specialized mental health services to build internal referral pathway to contribute for family strengthening approach. Supervision mechanism will be put in place to support caseworkers to provide safe, ethical and competent case management services.
All activities will be implemented while adhering to strict COVID 19 prevention measures such as reduced numbers of participants in group activities, social distancing and provision of PPEs for both staff and beneficiaries.
The project target is 5500 including 300 men, 500 women, 2350 boys and 2350 girls. The team will be trained on inclusion approach to ensure they have the knowledge and skills to include children with disabilities in the project activities through outreach and center based activities , in addition , all the rehabilitation works will be done to ensure safe access for children with disabilities and their families including rehabilitating the toilets to provide them with measures that fit the needs of children with disabilities such as: rumps, handrails in addition to transportation and other needed works based on coordination with the inclusion taskforce and CP SC . Community engagement through CPNs leading consultation with communities and ensure feedback mechanisms are in place. In addition RI will secure friendly child feedback mechanisms through suggestion boxes , review disks , satisfaction survey , FGDs and other channels to ensure boys and girls with their age categories are able to reach the feedback mechanisms .

4 - Needs

According to HNO 2021: Deepening poverty continues to fuel harmful coping mechanisms and strain the capacities of families and communities to protect children, particularly adolescent girls and boys: child labour, including its worst forms is reported in NWS . Access to basic rights are further jeopardized for children without official birth certificates and children with disabilities. Many of whom also suffer marginalization, stigma and discrimination, whilst facing heightened child protection risks. Additional CP risks reported are Separation from caregivers as a persistent issue across Syria and Psychosocial distress among children . COVID-19 has increased risk factors that drive the intensity and frequency of the aforementioned CP risks and vulnerabilities, whilst also undermining partners’ detection mechanisms.Based on these findings and the information gathered through child-protection-situation-monitoring-dashboard first quarter of 2020 conducted by the child protection monitoring task force in 22 communities in Azaz district, the main needed services for children, apart from the need to ensure children access to education opportunities, are: focused non-specialized psycho social services (according to 94% of KIs interviewed by the CP monitoring TF). parents (or other caregivers) in need of specialized services (86.5%). 60.39% of KIs reported children engaged in type of work that prevents them from going to school. 67.42% of KIs that reported children living in households with persons with disabilities.

5 - Implementation of activities

The project aims to support two CFSs , the establishment will consider field coordination and consultation with children and parents on the accessible location in addition to consultation on the activities timing , CFS staff and outreach teams will be hired and trained on CPMS , CP case management and CP community based approach . all supplies should be provided to the CFS including the PSS toolkits for children and adolescent boys and girls . transportation will be provided for children in remote area and for children with disability especially for the referral for more specialized services. Child protection networks CPNs will be established based on the volunteers from parents shared in the awareness raising sessions . the CPNs will operate as entry point to identify children at risk and refer to more specialized service will be provided within the CFSs, case management will be provided by trained and supervised team. Supervision will be provided remotely and physically to CP staff to ensure providing quality services and in line with the CPMS. Parenting sessions will be conducted in the CFS and on the community level.MEAL approach includes monitoring, evaluation, accountability and learning which is supported by technical expertise in gender, age & disability, child protection and conflict sensitivity to ensure consistent and accurate flow of information and data in relation to Gender, age and disability. A range of tools will be used to gather data in order to verify indicators, also to identify gabs through triangulate findings and tracking issues, the tools are highlighted below: activities report , pre and and posttests , satisfaction survey , FGDS and Standard observation and spot-checks forms.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk of doing harm or perpetuating unintended consequences to girls, and boys (and their caregivers) through the provision of specialized child protection services if service providers are not adequately trained/ services are of low quality. Likelihood: high. Impact: High . RI will mitigate the risk by : 1. ensure training packages are in line child protection minimum standards and consider remote managed context 2. Services should not be provided by any staff who have not received and demonstrated proficiency in training modules.3. Include a practice element to training modules that can be overseen through remote mentorship arrangements to allow for practice prior to service provision.4. Service mappings should only include services that have been determined to provide safe and quality services.5. Establish consultation with communities.Risk of exploitation or abuse by staff members/service providers if access to services or information are linked to a fee or limited to/excluded for specific persons/groups. Likelihood: medium .Impact : High . RI will mitigate the risk by 1. Communicate regularly and clearly to the community about what services are available and who services are for. 2. Provide regular training to staff and service providers for code of conduct and PSEA.3. provide awareness raising session on PSEA for boys, girls and parents 4. Provide child friendly feedback and complaints mechanisms .

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumption: The CFSs and are located in relatively safe areas. Risk: Armed Clashes, arrest and detention, shelling/artillery, VBIEDs/IEDs. mitigation: staff to contact the security focal point and alert all staff to be prepared for immediate evacuation/hibernation if possible. Activate an emergency plan.Risk that existing traditional practices and community structures conflict with child protection principles (e.g. child rights messaging is perceive as a threat) .Ri will mitigate the risk by Training of some staff as key resource persons to act as trainers/ facilitators ; 2 Regular engagement / communication with communities through the CPNs and community leaders.Risk : some high risk CP cases not identified or reached by the CP team in the targeted area , RI will mitigate the risk by establishing the CPNs , Hotline for informing the CP team on the most vulnerable cases in addition to conduct the awareness raising session by a qualified team able to deal with identification directly. In addition , RI will ensure consultation with communities and neighboring communities related to location of services to understand perspectives of communities are all understood and addressed.
