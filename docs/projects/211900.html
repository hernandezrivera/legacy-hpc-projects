Project Summary

The project aims to contribute to continuity of essential life-saving health services tonsure equitable access of people in need and improving quality of services through supporting convergent primary health care activities through activation of the pending pre-existed health facilities in Al-Bab district with HeRAMS code (B0202051). and emphasis focused on five key vulnerable groups: displaced persons, children under five years (U5), women of reproductive age WRA (15-49), older persons (60+) and people with disabilities affected by the crisis in northern Syria. Amal Organization will continue to contribute to the improvement of the health status of pregnant and lactating women (PLW) and children under 5 years by strengthening lifesaving program through integrated services in the proposed PHC Centre. The project consists of a comprehensive primary health care center providing services though policlinic and specialty clinic as the following:1-Genecology and reproductive health clinic, through this clinical department there will be a gynecologist and midwife who will provide reproductive health care including pre-natal care, post-natal care, prevention and appropriate treatment of infertility, treatment of reproductive tract infections, and family planning counselling. 2-Pediatrics and Nutrition clinic3-General and internal clinic 4- ENT clinic 5-NCD package services 6- MhPSS mental health and Psychological Support services through centered based and outreach activities7- providing essential and therapeutic medicines 8- Nutrition screening and referral for U5 children and PLWs. Amal will coordinate with WHO cluster and local health partner (Health directorates and other Health actor) beside to other stakeholders (local authority and beneficiaries through planning implementation and evaluation of this project to ensure better health services responses and strengthen the health network at the targeted area. Moreover, dealing with covid-19 cases in terms of Coordination, planning, and monitoring, on the other hand Infection prevention and control, protection of the health workforce, and therapeutics and coordination of vaccination campaign, moreover coordination with Cholera taskforce for the needed actions points regarding awareness sessions and other related activities. Moreover, Amal is coordinating with, DHIS2, EWARN for surveillance report and nutrition sector related of assessment and management of management of moderate acute malnutrition beside to management of severe acute malnutrition. Amal will continue cooperation with GBV sub cluster and MPHSS technical work group to coordinate of psychosocial support PSS activities designated at the beginning of the project. Amal is committed to robust MEAL (Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability, and Learning) and set the Indicators Performance Tracking Table (IPTT) which will be developed and tracked using the internal M&E Workplan developed at project start-up). The main database to insert and track the beneficiaries will be designed as well on Access Microsoft with ensuring clear gender markers, sex, age, disability, and displacement disaggregation will be considered). Amal will identify the timelines for data collection support information follow and analysis of progress against targets. MEAL personnel will conduct regular spot visits to the operation area on a weekly basis. Also, during the monitoring field visits, MEAL staff will use a standard center checklist to be filled in monthly to identify any challenge/gap in the center. Moreover, beneficiary satisfaction and accountability surveys will be collected, and the findings will be shared monthly. More qualitative data will be collected by conducting Focused Group Discussions with the community. The MEAL team will share a follow-up report with the program on the same day of the visit to follow up with the monitoring observation. Also, will observe COVID-19 precautions
