Project Summary

The project will take place in Aleppo and Idleb governorates in several districts which are:
Afrin, Azaz, Jarablus and Ghandora, Harim in multiple communities:

Babka C1021
Hezri G0703144
The aim of the project is to deliver nutrition services within nutrition, and child-mother friendly spaces settings.
The target population is reproductive-aged women, PLW, and U5 children.
The scope of activities will be mainly nutrition services (CMAM and IYCF), and raising-awareness.
The target facilities are IDA PHCs in Mreimin, Al Bab, Jarablus, Hezri, and Zoghra, IDA MCs in Babka, and two hospitals which are the Hope and Bab Al Salameh Hospitals. Nutrition services will be delivered in the child-mother spaces. According to secondary sources of information, the majority of current and recent IDPs are vulnerable as they are women, elderly persons, and U5 children.

4 - Needs

According to the cluster bulletin for September 2019, nearly 3 million people face the threat of an intense battle in a densely populated area, with the potential to result in a large-scale humanitarian disaster. Humanitarian actors continued to implement and update response plans, including preparedness training for chemical attacks, strengthening referral systems and pre-positioning essential supplies. Furthermore, the catchment population in north, northeast, and northwest of Aleppo (Euphrates Shield and Olive Branch areas) is reaching more than 1,350.000 people, from which up to 60% are IDPs who came in the last 4 years through several waves of displacement and are living in dire conditions affected by lack of basic needs. The situation is even more constrained due to the limited number of humanitarian organizations that can operate in those areas. The severe gaps in healthcare provision and the limited response of agencies operating in those areas call for an improved response based on PHC strengthening. This project builds on strategic nutrition services set-up in order to work towards long-term solutions in nutrition services provision for Aleppo and Idleb governorates that can meet with the increasing numbers.

Non-discrimination. Gender, disability, age, are emphasized in data collection and data analysis throughout the project period.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

This project proposal has been designed on the basis of needs assessments consisting of interviews with key informants within the target population. The project staff will be hired whenever possible from the target area itself, ensuring intimate knowledge of the context and good acceptability of the staff by the community. IDA has incorporated beneficiaries' feedback into their existing feedback system, through suggestion boxes at each PHC, which are collected and reviewed by the M&E field officer on a monthly basis. The M&E Officer will obtain direct patient feedback during the monitoring process. All patients feedback will be registered in complaints tracking sheet, the tracking sheet will easily show the statues for each complaint (such as category, time cycle, decision, extra), the beneficiary will get the result of his/her complaint if possible( since the anonymous complaints are acceptable). IDA team are trained on service provision according to humanitarian principles, as well as, IDA will implement meetings with camps management, Local Council and community leaders as well as with Health Directorate to know the affected population suggestions and needs to improve the services provided. Prohibition of Sexual Exploitation and Sexual Abuse (PSEA) IDA continues to stand firm on zero tolerance for (sexual) misconduct. Therefore, IDA and the contracted sub-partners are obliged to create and maintain an environment that prevents sexual exploitation and sexual abuse. Managers at all levels have a particular responsibility to support and develop systems that maintain this environment. The partner should ensure that systems are in place to collect and effectively manage feedback from communities
