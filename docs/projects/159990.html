Project Summary

This project intends to strengthen CCCM site management structures in Banadir, Lower Shebelle and Gedo Regions with increased coordination among the service providers within the settlements and host community to streamline humanitarian service delivery. The project shall also ensure that the population can access key services through ongoing advocacy to the partners with a special emphasis on people with specific needs. Through this project, NoFYL shall work in close coordination with the local authority to strengthen the existing governance structure and fill in the identified gaps while encouraging the active participation of both IDPs and the host community’s leadership. The project shall further promote building a sustainable relationship between the host communities and IDPs through strengthening the leadership capacities of IDPs and the host community. The project aims to improve living conditions and reduce protection concerns in Banadir, Lower Shebelle, and Gedo Regions, encourage free movement of IDPs and partners around the sites.
Through this project, NoFYL will address the cluster’s overall objective of improving the living conditions inside IDP sites and facilitate the immediate and targeted delivery of services to the displaced population. Cluster coverage will be expanded through NoFYL capacity to carry out assessments and monitor the displacement conditions in target locations where there are currently no CCCM coordination structures in place or no IDP assessment of any kind having been conducted in the last one year. Coverage will also be expanded through coordination meetings with camp committees and camp leaders.
The cluster’s primary monitoring tool, the detailed site assessment (DSA) will be utilized to gather baseline information on multisectoral issues and trends on displacement and monitor areas with camps and camp¬like settings and where there is a likelihood of displacement because of the ongoing conflict, flood/natural disaster. To immediately address needs and gaps and monitor areas where new displacement is reported, NoFYL will draw on field monitors to strengthen response capacity.
NoFYL will implement emergency site improvement projects to minimize risks of exclusion and high incidence of protection violations in IDP sites, as well as achieving durable solutions. NoFYL will also conduct settlement planning training for settlement leaders; create coordination structures and SOPs for emergency displacements; conduct capacity building training for government actors and relevant stakeholders; establish/reinforce community mechanisms for communication channels among others. Gender and protection will be mainstreamed in all program activities. Variations in gender, areas of origin, physical ability and age that affect vulnerability and coping strategies are identified and taken into account for planning to prevent their situation from deteriorating even further. Additionally, contingency plans are developed and put in place to ensure NoFYL can quickly adapt to and respond to changing humanitarian needs on the ground. Lastly, NoFYL will develop systems to strengthen community self-management to promote durable solutions for displaced people in sites.

Project Activities

Activity 1.1.1 Set-up and organize a sectorial IDP community self-management committees in each identified target location by CCCM Workers. This includes identification of committee members, structuring of committees, and the development of reporting/referral lines. Activity 1.1.2 Implementation of service delivery monitoring and coordination in the target locations. This will also be embedded to regular safety audits conducted by outreach workers to ascertain protection risks and how they can be prevented. Camp audits will also monitor service delivery, gaps, needs, by NoFYL working closely with self-management structures to build capacity for community monitoring, with NoFYL supporting communication and advocacy with partners and Clusters. NoFYL will ensure regular coordination with humanitarian agencies, working closely with Protection to ensure Do No Harm approaches are realized. Exact structure of partner coordination is to be defined with the CCCM Cluster to ensure integration with local actors and sustainability. Activity 1.1.3 Capacity of community committees will be enhanced through a 3 days training on safety audits to ensure they can conduct camp audits on their own and report them to relevant coordination bodies and cluster focal persons. This will ensure sustainability. Activity 1.1.4 Service mapping and monitoring is conducted on an on-going basis and feedback is share with Cluster leads and cluster members in a timely matter. NoFYL will coordinate monthly updates of the service mapping and disseminate to the partners and communities. NoFYL's outreach worker team will be strengthened in order to better ensure a robust service delivery monitoring mechanism, of which data will feed into humanitarian service delivery decision making. Outreach scope will be extended to cover nearby host communities whose members have access to the humanitarian services. NoFYL had previously in the ongoing SHF funded projects conducted service mapping with GBV&CPWG members in the 17 districts of Mogadishu, this will scale up and update the previous mapping to include CCCM and WASH facilities. Activity 1.1.5: Establish/reinforce mechanisms for communication of services available in the target locations/camps, this can be achieved by establishing communication/ information centers in coordination with local authorities and protection actors • Community outreach teams leading community awareness campaigns will also enhance the community knowledge on available services and referral mechanisms by disseminate key information regarding the settlement including information on services. Activity 1.1.6: Embed accountability structures within programme and Communication with Communities (CwC) activities implemented in IDP camps in the target locations. This will be conducted through Information dissemination through community committees and NoFYL Outreach Workers, with NoFYL facilitating regular meetings between community leaders and humanitarian partners. An information desk will be operational in all sites, staffed community committee representatives or NoFYL field staffs. A Complaints and Feedback Mechanism will be run in the field centers and at child friendly spaces run by NoFYL. Activity 1.1.7: Support community-led site maintenance activities to ensure the upkeep of sites through cash for work activities, site maintenance led by the site maintenance committees in 4 IDP settlements. 200 persons in each of the target districts will be targeted for the conditional cash for work, beneficiaries who will support in site maintenance through digging drainage systems, pit-holes for garbage collection, bush clearing and clear pathways. Each person will be paid 50$ for 2 months. 200 persons. (50 persons from each of the 4 camps (30 Female 20 Male)

Mitigation Measures

Protection mainstreaming: Risk of dispute on land ownership/land tenure in targeted project areas: Mitigation measure being the following;1- Assess the ownership of land if governement owned or private before establishing any facility on the land.2- Engage local authorities on the allocation of public land with lease of over 5 years to ensure long term sustainability of projects3- On private land ensure agreement is signed with the land owners for a lease of over 5 years to establish community structures like community centres in an IDP camps.4 - Engage directly with the owners of the land rather than gate keepers to understand land tenure processes.5 - Validate with the regional authorities tenure documents produced by private land owners to ensure claimed land owners are known before proceeding in any engagement. Protection mainstreaming: Beneficiaries risk exposure to violence during registration/distribution, mitigation measures being as follows;1- Seperate the process of registration and distribution, never register beneficiaries at the same time conducted distribution.2- Engage the authority for clearance and provision of security during the distribution excersise.3- Do not consuct distribution in an open ground, use community centers or fenced compound to restrict access to only listed beneficiaries.4- Where distribution is conducted at the agency's office, provide transport allowances to beneficiairies back to their camps or homes.5- Use cash/food vouchers where beneficiaries can visit a supplier anytime to receive their share instead of open distribution. Protection mainstreaming: Barriers for beneficiaries to report concerns through complaint and feedback mechanisms, being mitigated through the following means. 1- Establishing free hotlines and informing the community of the services2- Establishing community committees who will oversee self management of the camps and raise any concerns to relevant agencies.3- Map out available services and establish focal points for the different sectors, inform the community of these focal points.4- Community outreach and awareness campaigns to inform the communities of available services.
