Project Summary

This project is planned to target 3 localities mainly Kadugli, Abbassiya, and Abujebah in South Kordofan State. It aims at contributing to HRP 2019 outcome 2 through provision of general protection services for persons with special Needs (PSNs) through community based protection, capacity building of CBPNs, women centers and government counterparts and direct assistance to PSNs.
While the GBV services component focus on GBV prevention and response to non-survivors mainly women and girls at risk and GBV survivors. The prevention activities includes; strengthening GBV facilities eg. women centers, awareness rising/mobilization, vocational and life skill training and livelihoods means While the response activities focus on referral to medical, psychosocial support and legal Aid.
The general protection component activities includes strengthening community based protection through establishment/rehabilitation of meeting spaces, women centers, and support to 270 members CBPNs, 50 members of women centers with the support and 125 paralegals to provide needed protection services for 6845 PSNs of which includes 300 PSNs disagregrated by gender and age are planned to receive mobility devises, 100% of GBV survivors and 420 female headed household for livelihoods support, 5430 through community projects and provision for Personal hygiene kits for women and girls of reproductive age.
Secondly; referral will be done with the support of 120 members of CBPNs, 30 social workers who will conduct routine assessment/case management for 4500 PSNs including UASC, women at risk and SGBV survivors for referral to specific service providers.
The GBV component targets Percentage (100%) women at risk, both non survivors and GBV Survivors women, girls, boys and men related to either child Marriage, FGM, domestic and sexual violence are referred, Psychosocial support, legal aid services and % of households selected for vocational life skill as a preventive measures to GBV.
In project will be implemented in coordination with protection sector and sub-sector coordinators (GBV & General Protection), and technical departments at line Ministries eg. MoH, planning, and Social Welfare and protection Unit (Family and child protection Unit, SCCW, etc . to strengthen the referral system.
For gender mainstreaming, given the traditional context, many women, especially among the IDPs, returnees and host community face systematic restriction as majority limited participation, For this reason we will make every effort to promote the involvement of women as well as provide survivors with opportunities to participate in the project implementation as agent of change, ASSIST will also consider inclusion of UN Declaration on elimination of all forms of violence against women while ensuring due diligence for all staff, contractors etc.
We will conduct gender mobilization, sensitization and awareness rising at community level which will continue to be built into training program, and all employers involved with the project must obey employment laws related to gender and protection of women, girls and children with social security risk and engaged in child labor.
Accountability to affected populations; We will make information available on the need for participation, consultation and use feedback mechanisms which will determine beneficiary preference for project interventions and feedback mechanism will be developed. AAP mandates that we incorporate measures to ensure that the voices of the beneficiaries at all levels are integrated from the design phase, implementation and through to the evaluation phase. grievance mechanisms are an integral part of this system and will be incorporated along with checks to ensure accountability such as focus group discussion, spot check on implementation and key informant interviews.

Needs

According to the results of the inter-agency assessment conduct in Kadugli, Abbassiya, and Abujebah locality in South Kordofan State on 31 May- 1 June 2017, 4-8 February 2018, and 10 May 2018 respectively shows critical protection findings; Personal safety and security, family separation, women issues, separated and unaccompanied minors, lack of social services welfare, cases of persons with special needs, and non functional community based protection structure. The devaluation of local hard currency and high inflation rate >70% have led to dramatically increase in prices (between 200% and 300%) which have affected persons with special needs to access basic services and increased vulnerability of PSNs households. Cases of persons with disabilities, including children, were reported in all the areas visited by the assessment teams. However, no figures were provided or breakdown of various vulnerabilities such as chronic diseases, elderlies, unaccompanied minors among others. From reports received, the team estimates that at least 10% of the entire IDPs\Returnees populations are vulnerable. Separated and unaccompanied minors were reported to be living with members of the extended families and lack proper child welfare staff/services. Most caregivers told the team that the main source of stress revolved around the day-to-day survival including lack of food, lack of water and lack of adequate and appropriate shelter for their charges. While parents and peers were identified as the major support groups for children, a high number of children are out of schools and working and/or living on the streets, in some cases children are sent by their parents or caregivers to work as cattle herders for long duration away from immediate family. Family Separation and Women issues: Almost 60% of the visited IDP’s are women and children. Community members stated that the numbers of child and women headed households are high. Information was not available on the actual number of unaccompanied minors due to lack of proper registration and population statistics. It is estimated that no less than 40% are female-headed HHs. The actual number may be more and also, they are not evenly distributed in the assessed area. Despite a majority of the IDPs saying they did not fear for their personal safety, cases of violence against women (Rape) were report but the alleged cases were not reported to the police for appropriate legal process and medical procedures but were resolved using traditional structures. FGM and girl child marriage are widely practiced in the locations visited by the team. In some instances the girl child is withdrawn from school even before attaining eighth grade to be married off. Informants reported several cases of physically and mentally challenged children and adolescents. Most of these cases have never been treated or examined by a specialized physician. Psychosocial distress is not identified and appropriately handled.

Output 3. 100% of GBV non survivors and survivors have access to GBV prevention and mitigation • Rehabilitation and support for 8 Woman Center as a community base GBV facility for eg. Psychosocial support and referral • Conduct 80 GBV awareness rising on prevention and reporting for girls, boys, women and men• Vocational training, livelihoods empowering 425 persons men and women.• Life skill training for 470 women at risk and GBV survivors in boys, girls, women and men. Impact on environment/natural resources

The project has neutral impact on the environment because most of the activities are focused on community-based protection, prevention and responses to protection needs of persons with special needs, GBV services. However as the project is part of HRP 2019 responding to the new and protracted IDP situation and returnees movement which have resulted to excessive tree cutting for Shelter construction and wood worsen by the expansion of desert resulted to depletion of forestry resources making it difficult for PSNs to access materials for shelter, and firewood for heating and lighting in the night and risk women and girls collecting firewood from the nearest access point 10-15 km. Therefore, as part of mitigation measures, we propose provide Solar system for lighting in the night, cooking and introduce fuel efficiency stove (FES) alternative to use for firewood to reduce vulnerable women and girls through cash based intervention to purchase solar heating, lighting lamps Units, butane gas instead of firewood and promote an integrated protection, EN-NFIs, vocational and life skills livelihoods support and introduce concrete cement poles instead of tree woods/poles, and use soil stabilize block for women center construction and minimum of wood and grasses.
