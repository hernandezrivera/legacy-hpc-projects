Project Summary

According to HNO 2024 findings, almost 16.7 M people are in need in Syria, this number witnessed significant increase in comparison to the previous year 15.3 M, this mainly related to the Feb 23 earthquake disaster and its consequences on the economic situation where Syrians are getting poorer as the SYP continue its fall which since the earthquake lost half of its value against USD. Also, by Sep 23 SMEB value is greater than income average in NWS in 23%. TAS has created the proposed project as a contribution to reduce the food insecurity and inability to meet the basic needs among affected HHs in NWS. This will be achieved by the following interventions:
Regular MPCA distribution:
TAS will target 2000 HHs (12000 individuals, 2200 men, 2800 women, 3200 boys, 3800 girls) in Idleb and Aleppo governorates by Regular 8 rounds of Multi-Purpose Cash, MPC to the most vulnerable HHs, from both IDPs and HC, without access to general food assistance (GFA). The MPCA approach enables vulnerable households to access an adequate and basic needs supply by providing cash assistance, allowing them to choose needed items that align with their preferences and cultural practices. TAS will implement MPC for 8 rounds based on the guidelines of CWG and after coordination with relevant clusters, ensuring an effective response. TAS will distribute e-vouchers to the targeted HHs where each HH will receive one voucher with value of 100$ that will be redeemed each round for 8 consecutive rounds/months. The redemption process will be via FSP that TAS previously contracted with, and the project staff will ensure the distribution and redemption process to be in safe and well-equipped sites and accessible to all BNFs also for those who could not reach the distribution/redemption sites due to their special difficulties or disabilities, TAS will ensure providing the adequate transportation for them. This intervention will mainstream protection especially (Do no harm) throughout the project cycle by considering the different needs of men, women, children, adolescents, adults, middle-aged adults, elders, and PwDs.
One-off MPCA distribution:
TAS will target 4000 HHs (24000 individuals, 4400 men, 5600 women, 6400 boys, 7600 girls) in Idleb and Aleppo governorates by providing one-off MPCA, MPC to the most vulnerable HHs, from both IDPs and HC, without access to general food assistance (GFA). The MPCA approach enables vulnerable households to access an adequate and basic needs supply by providing cash assistance, allowing them to choose needed items that align with their preferences and cultural practices. It is worth mentioning that TAS will give priority for those affected with any recent emergency, either natural or security one. TAS will implement MPC for only 1 round based on the guidelines of CWG and after coordination with relevant clusters, ensuring an effective response. TAS will distribute paper vouchers to the targeted HHs where each HH will receive one voucher with value of 150$ that will be redeemed only for one time. The redemption process will be via FSP that TAS previously contracted with, and the project staff will ensure the distribution and redemption process to be in safe, well-equipped, and accessible sites to all BNFs, also for those who could not reach the distribution/redemption sites due to their special difficulties or disabilities, TAS will ensure providing the adequate transportation for them. This intervention will mainstream protection especially (Do no harm) throughout the project cycle by considering the different needs of men, women, children, adolescents, adults,middle-aged adults,elders,and PwDs.
