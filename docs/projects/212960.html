Project Summary

The overall goal of the project is that more vulnerable children are better protected by humanitarian action and resilience-building initiatives in North West Syria.
The project is designed to significantly enhance the protection and resilience of vulnerable children in Northwest Syria through a comprehensive approach that addresses immediate protection needs and builds long-term resilience. The project's cornerstone is to provide structured PSS and establish Child-Friendly Spaces that offer a safe environment for children to learn, play, and recover from trauma. These initiatives are coupled with concerted efforts to prevent GBV, an acute risk in crisis settings, through targeted awareness-raising campaigns, advocacy interventions, and referral services tailored to the needs of those at risk.
A critical component of the project is the provision of case management and referral services, ensuring that children and their families have access to essential services that address specific protection needs, including PSS, referrals, and material assistance. This is complemented by community-based protection services that reach out to individuals with tailored assistance, focusing on both child protection and GBV issues.
To ensure the sustainability and effectiveness of these interventions, the project places a significant emphasis on capacity building for project staff and local partners. This involves training on a wide range of critical issues, including foundational child protection principles, organizational child protection policies, community-based child protection interventions, and specific training on addressing GBV. By equipping local actors with the necessary skills and knowledge, the project aims to promote ownership and sustainability of the response to protection needs in the long term.
Moreover, the project includes specialized training for trainers, ensuring that knowledge and expertise in protection mainstreaming and community-based protection approaches are disseminated widely among humanitarian workers and local/national authorities. This strategic focus on training and capacity building is intended to strengthen protective systems at all levels, enabling more effective prevention and response to child protection risks.
In addition, the project will build the capacities of the project staff to effective protective systems are in place for preventing and responding to child protection risks.
The project will ensure raising the awareness using key messages on prevention and responding to child Protection Risks and implementing a better parenting programming for children's caregivers to strengthen the family and caregiving environment.
