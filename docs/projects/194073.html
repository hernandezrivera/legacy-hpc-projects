Project Summary

the project will provide protection and WASH services for effected people. the project will provide PSS, protection monitoring,GBV services, Dignity kits as well as hygiene promotion services for most people who affected by ongoing conflict between Sudan Army Forces and Rapid Support Forces in target area.

1 - Needs

n 2022 , Sudan has witnessed a combination of inter communal conflict, political crisis, inflation crisis, deterioration of security situation, flood , outbreak disease as well as impacted of COVID19 pandamic these factors led to effect on humanitarian response and provide services for most effected population around country. According to HNO 2022, estamited about 14.9 million people in need while about in need of general protection is 4.5 million, child protection 3.2 million, Gender Based Violence 2.7 million and WASH …(OCHA source). HNO indicated that, there are lack of access to civil documentation services for displacement, lack of physical safty, lack of access to WASH and hand washing with soap and water, children protection services as well as Gender Based Violence. Based on protection sector HNO 2023,Persons in Need (PiN) is 3.2 million of whom 56% are children, and 22% are women. While Person on Need(PiN) of IDP in Central Darfur is decreased to -7%, returnees increased to 196% and resident decreased to -13%. In East Darfur PiN of IDP is decreased to -27%, returnee remaining to 17% and resident decreased to -1%. In South Darfur PiN of IDPs continue decreased to -13%, returnees remaining to 16% and residence decreased to 16%. In West Darfur State PiN of IDPs are remaining 6%, returnee remaining 12% as we as residence remaining 98%.on 15 April 2023 conflict between Sudan Army Forces and Rapid Support Forces has been took place and still continue in several locations and cities across Sudan including khartoum, South Darfur Nyala, Central Darfur Zalingei, North Darfur Elfasher and North Kordofan Elobaid. according to OCHA nearly 1.4 million people have been forced to flee their home and are now displaced inside and outside of country, add to that at least 730 people have been killed and 5,500 people have been injured. the reports indicated that, Gender Baes Violence and sexual violence are increasing add to that food, water and fuel have increased by 40 to 60 per cent in conflict affected area.this project urgently response to provide and deliver General protection, Child protection, GBV services for most affected people as well as hygiene promotion activities to meet their basic needs.
