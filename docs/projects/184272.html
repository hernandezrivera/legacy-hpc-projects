Project Summary

The Somalia Education Cluster Needs Analysis Note October 2021 estimated 3 million school aged children (45% girls) are in need of humanitarian assistance to be enrolled and retained in schools.(2,419,059 are from non-IDP families and 667,891 are from IDP families). Learning has been disrupted in schools including Puntland due to concurrent shocks such as drought and floods, Covid-19 pandemic and the ongoing conflict. These environmental shocks have resulted to destruction of classrooms/WASH facilities, children not being able to attend schools because of lack of school fees, uniforms and scholastic materials due to financial constraints experienced by their families,lack of safe drinking water and lack of teachers to attend to children with special needs. The proposed interventions by PWMDO will support 8,300 children in schools whose learning have been disrupted due to the above mentioned shocks in 7 districts within Puntland representing 9.5% of the gaps identified in the 2022 Somalia HNO. PMWDO anticipates to reach 50% girls and approximately 10% CWD or special needs. PMWDO will target 373 learners in Bossaso,2,600 learners in Gaalkacyo, 2,800 learners in Galdogob, 1,020 learners in Iskushuban,307 learners in Jariiban and 600 learners in Qandala ensuring at-least 50% or more are girls and prioritizing CWDs in the process(anticipating total of 830 CWDs). Beneficiaries’ selection will put into consideration children whose learning has been disrupted due to COVID-19 ,drought,floods,cyclone,locust infestation and conflict. Response will aim to;
1. Support EiE for 220 Educators and 8,300 crises affected children and youth have access to a safe and protective learning environment including TLS construction of classrooms (previously suspended due to COVID-19) with recommended spacing or rehabilitation with child friendly and inclusive way (making them more CWDs friendly) maintaining four (4) WASH facilities (separate for boys and girls) per district.PMWDO will also record and report through cluster mechanisms (including CP MRM if applicable) the number of learning facilities damaged and/or attacked. Through coordination, EiE will work with school children and youth to access protection activities as per the Integrated Education-Child Protection Response Framework such as MHPSS.
2. Vulnerable children and youth are in safe learning space that promotes personal well-being and social cohesion through;
a)Ensuring 8,300 boys and girls benefit from emergency teaching and learning materials(TLM)
b)Facilitating teachers supported with emergency incentives in schools enrolling vulnerable children for up to 220men and women with 50% to be women.
c)Providing 8,300 vulnerable children(50%girls) with access to safe drinking water and hygiene awareness sessions
d)Supporting schools with access emergency school feeding to maintain enrollment of the 4,150 girls and 4150 boys.
e)Directly targeting atleast 830 CWDs to benefit from inclusive education through mobilization awareness
3. The project will also support capacity building to deliver effective and coordinated education in emergencies preparedness and response within the education system in areas of operation through; training on inclusive, protective and socio-emotional teaching,learning and PSS within schools (110M and 110F). PMWDO will work with Community Education Committee members to enable them support school management, CP initiative and safety in schools. Still on capacity building, PMWDO will work with Education Cluster partners and MoE staff on emergency preparedness, response planning and coordination (20M and 20F).
The project focus on cluster targets based on PIN of 2,599 in Bandarbeyla; 27,444 in Bossaso; 33,017 in Gaalkacyo; 7,940 in Galdogob; 2,893 in Iskushuban; 4,037 in Jariiban and 2,203 in Qandala. As well as Education staff or personnel of 396 in Bandarbeyla; 4,182 in Bossaso; 5,031 in Gaalkacyo; 1,210 in Galdogob; 441 in Iskushuban; 615 in Jariiban and 336 in Qandala

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

1. For the risk of exposure of displaced boys and girls to violence, caused by living in highly insecure settlements, PMWDO intent to mitigate by unloving children’s families or caregivers when determined to be in the best interests of the child, in addition to supporting safety audits while coordinating with CP AoR. as much as Somalia remains volatile conditions in Puntland selected region (and districts) will focus on the capacity of CECs to help disseminated relevant information including risks. Where beneficiaries come from volatile locations, emphasis will be on alternative care in coordination with CP AoR standards for kinship of care by extended families. Safety audit exercises are aimed at analyzing real-time risk participation of children in the process ensure the perceptive and relevant information are contextualized to child friendly messages to disseminate so that child can avoid getting at risk 2. For the risk of exposure of violence as a result of distance to and location of humanitarian assistance/services or indiscriminate attacks from armed actors, the PMWDO mitigation include ensuring access of beneficiary to service mapping (which can be explained through awareness sessions) and by cluster coordination with members to ensure even coverage of district with people in need. By ensuring multiple response in different locations this risk can be mitigated. Additionally, PMWDO history of operation in the location (Puntland) predisposition it to be able to use its community entry strategy to involve locals in supporting safe and compassionate models in community friendly mechanisms by soliciting help from CECs and School managements to ensure enrollment does not contribute to the problem but rather focuses mobilization to enhance safety
