Project Summary

The project aims at providing the entire population of Gaza, including women, men, boys and girls of all age groups with access to safe drinking and domestic water that is free from biological contamination by ensuring over 99% disinfection efficiency of all water system in the Gaza Strip for six months until CMWU are able to secure additional resources in future. This includes: 284 water wells, 55 water reservoirs, 26 water lifting and booster stations, 2,987 km of water networks and carrier lines, 48 small-medium sized brackish water desalination plants (50m3/day to 1000m3/day), and three connections of Mekorot water supply across the Green Line with Israel with average total annual supply capacity of 10MCM.
These water facilities are distributed across 25 municipalities of five governorates and will all be targeted in this project except for the two seawater desalination plants as they have special arrangements and already covered by disinfection materials and chlorine.
This will be achieved through the following set of activities:
ACTIVITY #1: Supply sufficient quantities of the following water disinfection materials/chemicals:
- Sodium-Hypo chlorite-(10-12%active Chlorine) for water disinfection of all water facilities (284 Water wells and desalination plants and water networks)
- Hydrochloric Acid 33% for 48 desalination plants
- Caustic Soda 50% for 48 desalination plants
- Anti-fouling Agent, Antiscalant, Sodium Hexa meta Phosphate 40% for 48 desalination plants
- Sodium Metabisulfite for 48 desalination plants
The provision of these chemicals is essential to the operation of the desalination plants, and contributes significantly to the improvement of water quality for women, men, boys and girls in Gaza.
Human Appeal will procure the above items and devise a delivery plan in consultation with CMWU to ensure its adequate and efficient use for water distribution, and the operation of 284 water wells and public desalination plants. Chlorine and chemicals will be procured from private sector without the need for prior coordination; as they are not 'dual use' materials.
Supplied quantities of water disinfection materials will be sufficient for period of six months during which CMWU will continue to secure resources needed to cover the rest of the year.
ACTIVITY #2: Carry out regular and consistent water quality testing for six months
Samples from 580 locations across the Gaza Strip will be tested for water quality and/or residual chlorine level on a monthly basis to monitor water quality & ensure safe water delivery to the population of Gaza. All test results will be logged into the monitoring database to inform actions required for water safety plans.
ACTIVITY # 3: Supply spare parts, equipment and maintenance necessary for water disinfection systems
Regular preventive and corrective maintenance will be conducted for all water disinfection systems in the water facilities across the Gaza Strip and any faulty chlorinators, disinfection dosing pumps or other parts will be replaced to ensure effective functioning of the water disinfection system. This will be carried out through:
Provision of chlorinators and its associated spare parts for the operation and maintenance of 284 water wells in the Gaza strip;

1 - Needs

By end of February 2020, CMWU will completely run out chlorine and water disinfection materials for all municipal water wells, reservoirs, booster stations, brackish desalination plants and water networks across the Gaza Strip.In the past, IsDB, Islamic Relief, and recently UNICEF have covered the cost of chlorine and disinfection materials. However, as of end of February 2020, there will be no funding and no commitment by any donor to cover this critical activity while CMWU cannot afford this cost alone, increasing concerns of further deterioration of water quality. As winter is approaching, the need for chlorination and disinfection chemicals is a top priority to prevent the outbreak of diseases. There is also a high need to carry out essential maintenance of the water disinfection system including dosing pumps, chlorinators and supply essential spare parts and fittings required for the proper functioning of water disinfection systems in the Gaza Strip. This is critical to ensure water safety and appropriate levels of chlorine and other disinfection chemicals in water supplied through network. Unless a longer-term agreement is reached to supply water disinfection materials and chlorine in Gaza, this intervention is considered an urgent humanitarian need to keep the minimum level of water services and water quality. Otherwise, over 2 million people of Gaza will be at serious risk of water-borne disease outbreaks. According to recent CMWU figures, the overall collection ratio has declined from 36% to %31 in 2018 due to overall deteriorating humanitarian situation. This makes CMWU unable to maintain minimum quality water supply without external donor support. The present water quality monitoring outcomes demonstrated that water supply system in the Gaza Strip relies mainly on severely deteriorated and contaminated natural coastal aquifer which is seriously affected by seawater intrusion and infiltration from wastewater leaks and agricultural fertilizers. PWA Water Status Report of 2018 states that more than 97% of the water abstracted from the only available natural aquifer in the Gaza Strip is unfit for drinking use and is far below the recommended international drinking water quality standards e.g. WHO and PWA guidelines.The Gaza strip suffers from a critical lack of safe drinking water. Ground water is the only source of natural drinking and domestic water available to households and businesses in the Gaza strip. Contaminated drinking water poses a significant protection concern and risk to public health, PWA’s latest baseline study highlighted the correlation between poor water quality from municipal wells and diseases such as renal, diarrhea diseases, hepatitis A, meningitis, parasitic, and typhoid. The most impacted children between 0 - 5 years (59% of cases). The water supply and distribution system in the Gaza Strip covers 98% of the entire population. Gaza Water Supply System consists of 284 water wells, 55 water reservoirs, 26 water lifting and booster stations, 2,987 km of water networks and carrier lines and 48 small-medium size brackish water desalination plants (50m3/day to 1000m3/day).These facilities are distributed across 25 municipalities of five governorates and will all be targeted in this project except for seawater desalination plants as they have special arrangements and already covered by disinfection materials and chlorine.

2 - Activities

* Supply sufficient quantities of the following water disinfection materials/chemicals:Provision of sodium hypochlorite, disinfection materials and chemicals to water facilities in the Gaza StripHA will provide 413 water facilities located in five governorates of the Gaza Strip (North Gaza, Gaza, Middle Area, Khan Yunis and Rafah) with the following essential disinfection materials and chemicals: - Sodium-Hypo chlorite-(10-12%active Chlorine) for water disinfection of all water facilities including (284 Water wells, 55 water reservoirs, 26 water lifting and booster stations and 48 desalination plants) - Hydrochloric Acid 33% for desalination plants- Caustic Soda 50% for desalination plants- Anti-fouling Agent, Antiscalant, Sodium Hexa meta Phosphate 40% for desalination plants- Sodium Metabisulfite for desalination plantsThe provision of these chemicals is essential to the operation of the desalination plants, and contributes significantly to the improvement of water quality and prevention of outbreak of water-borne diseases such as diarrhea, especially among children under 5 years old across the Gaza Strip.HA will procure the above items and devise a delivery plan in close collaboration with CMWU to ensure its adequate and efficient use for water distribution, and the operation of 284 water wells and 48 public desalination plants. Chlorine and chemicals will be procured from private sector without prior coordination; as they are not dual use materials.Supplied quantities of water disinfection materials will be sufficient for period of six months during which CMWU will continue to secure resources needed to cover the rest of the year. HA will develop a distribution plan, taking all safety and protection procedures into account during all stages of delivery, transportation, storage and handling. As there is no storage capacity within Gaza to store large quantities of Chlorine, HA will arrange a delivery plan of chlorine on a monthly basis. However, the rest of disinfection materials will be stored according to standard operating procedures for safe storage of chemicals. * Carry out regular monitoring and consistent water quality testing for six monthsSamples from 580 locations across the Gaza Strip will be tested for water quality and/or residual chlorine level on a monthly basis to give a realistic representative result of all water distribution system in Gaza. All test results will be logged into the monitoring database to give historical reference and inform the monthly disinfection reports. This regular water quality testing is important to detect any potential biological contamination that may occur and prevent the spread of contamination. If contamination is recorded, immediate action will be taken by CMWU and MoH and the Water Quality Working Group will alert the cluster to ensure that men, women, boys and girls take extra precaution and hygiene standards.* Supply spare parts, equipment and maintenance necessary for water disinfection systems for one yearRegular preventive and corrective maintenance will be conducted for all water disinfection systems in the water facilities across the Gaza Strip and any faulty chlorinators, dosing pumps or other parts will be replaced to ensure effective functioning of the water disinfection system. This will be carried out through: Provision of chlorinators and its associated spare parts for the operation and maintenance of 283 water wells in the Gaza stripHuman Appeal will coordinate with the Water Quality Working Group and Area Focal Points to inform other similar projects such as Oxfam’s Water Safety Plans (WSPs) and other relevant interventions. As detailed in the GAM website, this project addresses water quality and seeks to prevent the outbreak of water-borne diseases. Therefore, the project targets the entire population of the Gaza Strip regardless of gender, age groups or other forms of diversity.

9 - Protection mainstreaming
9.1 - Do No Harm

HA and CMWU will ensure 100% safety of staff and communities during the implementation of the project. First, chemicals and Chlorine will be transported and stored in line with the highest safety standards. CMWU will provide preventive and corrective maintenance to chlorinators and dosing pumps and water disinfection systems to ensure that accurate levels of chemicals are used in the treatment/disinfection process. HA will ensure that this project does not have any negative impact on the environment or society. On the contrary, the project contributes to preserving the natural water resources and reverses the deterioration in water quality.

9.2 - Equal and impartial access to aid

The project aims to disinfect drinking and domestic water for the entire population of 2 million people in the Gaza Strip regardless of their age, gender, disability or other forms of diversity. The project targets all public desalination plants across the Gaza Strip, including desalination plants in the marginalised and vulnerable areas. All women, men, boys and girls in the Gaza Strip will be targeted in this project. Human Appeal is committed to humanitarian principles of impartiality, neutrality, independence and humanity. HA will ensure that no one is left behind and that everyone in the community has equal access to support based on need only. All the population of the Gaza Strip will have meaningful access to water supply that is free from any biological contamination. Women and men in the community will have the means to report any issues related to quality and quantity of water and CMWU staff will respond to all community concerns.
