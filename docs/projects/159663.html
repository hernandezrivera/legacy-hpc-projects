Project Summary

CPD intends to protect the well-being of 31,200 (5,200 HH) women, girls, boys and men who are newly displaced IDPs and vulnerable host communities in Galmudug, South West, Banadir and Hirshabelle States of South Central Somalia through provision of combined emergency shelter and NFI kits. The organizations also intend to identify and register them using modern KoBo collect digital technology that will break the data down by gender and age in order to better plan for their assistance. A mutually agreed criteria will be used to prioritize the most vulnerable members of the IDP and about host communities in getting assistance first. A market assessment will be carried out in each of the districts to assist in deciding the modality of assistance to the beneficiaries. The IDP’s will be provided with standard NFI kits that will include plastic sheets, blankets, jerry cans, sleeping mats and kitchen sets. They will also be provided with standard ESKs that will include plastic sheets, supporting poles, nails and a rope for tying down the structures. These relief items will be provided to the beneficiaries’ vouchers to redeem at traders’ shops that have gone through a transparent suitability selection process and have signed an agreement with CPD. Trainings will be conducted for the beneficiaries on construction skills and assisted in erecting the shelters.
CPD will agree with the beneficiaries and the traders on the list of items the IDP’s can choose from. The beneficiaries will be flexible to choose from these items up to the value of the voucher according to their own priorities of needs with the amount allocated to each adhering to the recommended Shelter Cluster standards. This will allow both gender, who have different needs to choose what is appropriate for them. The flexibility will also achieve a higher degree of ownership. Post Distribution and Construction monitoring exercises will be carried out to ascertain the effectiveness of the activities.
CPD will ensure that there is gender balance in the committees in the IDP. Especially women and children that head households will be encouraged to represent their interests in the committees. They shall integrate informal leadership in the camps including elements that have usually conflicting interests with the potential of violence against individuals or groups. All the activities will be done in consultation with the beneficiaries or their representatives ensuring that DO NO HARM principles are adhered to.
