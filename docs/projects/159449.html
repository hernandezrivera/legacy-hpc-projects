Project Summary

The longstanding humanitarian crisis has been aggravated by the impact of four consecutive poor rainy seasons, including in conflict-affected areas, triggering large-scale new displacement and increasing protection concerns. The drought is exacerbating existing vulnerabilities, particularly among women, children, elderly, persons with disabilities and marginalized communities. Displacement from rural to urban areas has a significant impact on the demographic make-up of the country, leading to urban overcrowding and putting additional pressure on scarce resources and services in cities and towns. This is also increasing the risk of disease outbreaks due to limited access to safe water and poor sanitation, and overburdened health services. Malnutrition rates have increased and inter-communal tensions over access to water and grazing lands are on the rise due to drought and uneven rainfall across the regions.
In 2020, DKH will continue to expand its existing site coordination and management activities in Banadir, Hiraan, Galgaduud and Mudugud with specific activities including site planning and preparation to increase existing capacity, site coordination/management – coordinate/lead, improved provision of humanitarian assistance in IDP camps; Communication with affected communities, including complaint and feedback mechanisms, community sensitization/mobilization, ensuring participation of vulnerable groups (women/youth) and that the women committees are maintained and functional; additional surveys/assessments as necessary and agreed upon with the cluster. DKH and the Implementing Partners (IP) intend to achieve an effective and efficient coordinated humanitarian response in situations where IDP and host communities are forced to seek refuge in camp. DKH, through the IPs, will contribute to the reduction of barriers to assistance, support the formation and capacity building of Camp-Management Committees (CMC), ensure access to basic services, enhance the accountability to affected populations, mitigate risks of SGBV, fire and flooding, and strengthen community participation and empowerment through consistent engagement with the camp management committees. The proposed intervention is a joint initiative by DKH together with IPs to sustain relevant replicable and scalable solutions for basic and protective service delivery.
The intervention aims to facilitate and support the provision of emergency, lifesaving assistance to IDPs through coordinated site management and rapid response. DKH has coordinated with the Camp Coordination and Camp Management(CCCM) Cluster in the project sites to ensure that activities address cluster identified needs and gaps. Throughout the project, DKH will communicate on progress, challenges, and lessons learnt with other stakeholders on a regular basis.
In 2020, the main objectives will be:
Objective 1: Strengthen safe access to multi sectorial services at site level through improved site management and coordination;
Objective 2: Improve living conditions of displaced people through site development, care and maintenance;
Objective 3: Strengthen community self-management and access to information for displaced populations.
The project targets reaching 94,000 beneficiaries in more than 55 camps sites in the target locations.

Mitigation Measures

3a.~mitigation 1)The project will enforce community participation in all levels of the project and will ensure effective safety and security measures are in place. CMCs will coordinate to establish community patrol groups, where possible, to support prevention of forced recruitment, looting, GBV and other threats stemming from displacement. The CMCs will enhance their camp system of monitoring the movement of persons and assets entering and leaving the camp. 2)The project will work in coordination with protection actors and CMCs from the onset to put into place measures to prevent and respond to protection concerns including physical/sexual violence arising in camp settings 3)The project will ensure that camp assistance and services are reaching the most vulnerable. Displacement has more impact on specific categories like women &children headed households, the elderly, persons living with disability and other marginalized groups. The project will ensure that the formed CCCM committees are all-inclusive and represented in CFW activities 4) The project will locate recreational spaces for children and youth to play and advocate for more community cultural/sports events to be conducted to foster a spirit of unity and avoid the children and youth going to play in remote areas where danger could be lurking 5) The project will enhance the already existing complaints and feedback mechanisms to ensure that it is accessible, confidential and well understood with a view of understanding the community perceptions, promote beneficiary empowerment and assist in detecting misconduct.3c.~mitigation 1)The project will empower the community to identify areas in and around the camp that could be potentially unsafe or a risk of violence, especially GBV f.i. around the bathrooms, latrines, routes to school, health center or water points. These spots will be monitored regularly and lighting provided and also household solar lamps will be provided to these households 2)The CMCs involved in organizing and coordinating the camp activities will also try as much as possible to ensure that the space arrangements, camp structures and distance between dwellings respect the privacy and cultural norms between men and women and minimize risks on women and girls of exploitation and abuse. 3)The project will ensure the camp staff and committees consider gender balance to ensure that there are enough women available as contact points for women and girls to repost cases of potential and actual sexual violence activities in the community. 4)The project will ensure camp managers, staff, volunteers &anyone involved in camp management activities understands, signs &adheres to a Code of Conduct stating their commitment to respect and foster humanitarian standards and the rights of beneficiaries. 5)In cooperation with protection actors, the project will enhance the capacity of camp committees to monitor, report and refer protection concerns such as sexual violence against women and girls. The project will also set-up camp referral systems to provide an appropriate response.3D.~mitigation1)The project aims to work with CMCs in maintaining peaceful coexistence between camp and host communities with inclusion of host communities in decisions on site location and planning. 2)The project will not give a blind eye to any existing friction between different groups inside the camp, through engagement with the committees ensure camp layout adequately avoids fueling tensions 3)In cooperation with committees &local authorities, the project will raise awareness of camp rules and sanctions for infractions to minimize safety threats.4)Identified information on power dynamics will be used to inform of any existing barriers to peaceful coexistence within the communities and support in identifying who is able to influence decisions 5)The project will establish appropriate, transparent and sustainable mechanisms for meaningful dialogue with all stakeholders to ensure community buy-in

Please provide further explanation

DKH understands that a gradual, often long-term process of reducing displacement-specific needs and ensuring a coordinated and timely engagement of different actors within IDP camps is vital for safety, liberty or health of the IDPs. It`s on this basis that DKH through partners will support strengthening of CMCs to play a greater role in day to day running of the camps. The interventions will also support early recovery for pastoralists that lost their existence and displaced. The durable solutions will ensure that IDPs live in safe and secure camps, help CMCs solve housing, land and property issues; support post-conflict reconstruction; and come up with workable remedies for violations suffered.To achieve these, DKH and the partners shall set up effective feedback mechanisms to monitor the process of supporting durable solutions and determining what remains to be done to achieve durable solution. Assessments conducted will include a gender analysis and take into IDPs who have special needs or might be marginalized within the displaced population. Credible complaint mechanisms for IDPs and other affected populations will ensure that concerns are brought to the immediate attention of the responsible national or local authorities through the relevant channels.
