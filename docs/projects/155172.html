Objectives

The project primarily addresses the impact of the refugee crisis on the livelihoods and natural resource base of the host communities. The program aims to seize opportunities for income generation in the growing market for food products created by the refugee population, while also creating alternative livelihoods opportunities in environmental protection and rehabilitation.

Needs

For food security and nutrition enhancement two distinct approaches have been identified: (1) first approach targets individual, small-scale agriculturalists and groups that are not currently in a viable position to enter into commercial or contract farming, yet still require food production assistance to improve upon their current livelihoods strategies. These farmers will receive appropriate inputs and technical support to bolster food production, safe food storage and management. Nutrition awareness will also be included. (2) second approach targets agricultural groups with higher potential for commercial or contract production. These farmer groups will see investments in their technical capacity to reach and benefit from the ongoing market expansion from the increased demand. These groups, given proper organizational and technological development support, will have a high possibility of success given the opportunities at hand in the current context. The agro-ecological characteristics of the district are challenging and climate change is affecting gowing conditions, the Wood Fuel Supply and Demand Assessment – Forestry Impact Study conducted in June and July of 2017 established, that the refugee population consumes on average 1 kg per capita per day and used an average above-ground biomass of forest in Bangladesh as 128 tons/ha. As a typical football pitch (approximately 1 hectare) is a commonly relatable area for the general population, we estimate the equivalent of 4 to 5 football pitches per day of forest loss. Aquifers have been drained and are not being recharged due to forest and vegetation cover loss. FAO's contribution to the JRP is part of a multi-year plan of investment into the Agro-Forestry Sector in the district to be implemented alongside government partner - Ministry of Environment, Forestry and Climate Change.

Activities or outputs

FOOD SECURITY AND NUTRITION ENHANCEMENT AND LIVELIHOODS SUPPORT • Increase of food production within host community agricultural groups and individual farmers, including targeted production of high-demand and high nutrient crops to supply the refugee market; • Provision of quality agricultural inputs at local level including distribution of seed, plantings, irrigation and production technology; • Extension and Nutrition Behaviour Change Communication materials for target groups and individuals; ENVIRONMENTAL REHABILITATION AND MITIGATION OF LANDSLIDES AND FLASH FLOODS • Land stabilization and erosion control measures in high priority areas (threas to water sources and agriculural lands) and reforestation of damaged ‘hot spots’ are technically planted with trees, shrubs and long-rooted grasses (1,000 hectares); • Development of water management plans and model for Water User Groups and high-efficiency water control systems established (22 targeted areas); • Watershed rehabilitation plans are produced/approved which feed aquifers supplying the Ukhia and Teknaf sub-districts, with concrete implementation plans (22 targeted areas); • Maintenance support for planted areas through Community Co-Management Group strategies (CFW)

Key project/performance indicators
