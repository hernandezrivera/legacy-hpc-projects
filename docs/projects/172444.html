Project Summary

Community engagement and accountability to affected people (CE/AAP) is critical to ensure that women, men, adolescent, children representing different identity groups and social strata are meaningfully engaged in the process of delivering humanitarian assistance from the onset of the response/programming. In Pakistan, various initiatives to enable community members to participate in humanitarian action have been established and rolled out by humanitarian community, however meaningful participation and response to the specific needs of affected people particularly vulnerable groups remain a challenge. Several aid agencies reported that there is a major gap to address corrective actions in the response/programming due to a lack of collective system including centralised community feedback data and referral mechanisms, and as a result the concerns and priorities of the affected people are not properly met (based on a series of consultations with a wide range of aid providers conducted by OCHA in January and February 2021). To address this challenge, a coordinated approach to AAP is required to allow humanitarian actors to systematically listen to community inputs (from the design phase) and make a response-wide course correction throughout the programming.
This project aims to strengthen system-wide accountability in Pakistan to ensure that affected communities are regularly informed about different services available to them, have access to express their specific needs and raise concerns, and influence as well as guide the decision-making process in the operations. This project will build on and strengthen the existing structures within the government (national and provincial assemblies) and humanitarian organisations, and establish a robust system (collective platform) to amplify community voices in response. Further, this project will leverage the inclusion of vulnerable and marginalised groups and deliver strong advocacy on behalf of affected communities to improve their living conditions.

1- Project Activities

(1) Establish and convene a coordination system for AAP that brings key stakeholders (government agencies and humanitarian community) together at various levels to develop an effective and coordinated approach for accessible sharing of information with affected people before, during and after emergencies and ensure communities’ meaningful participation in the process. (2) Develop and share analysis for consistency and clarity in providing information and advice to affected communities by working with national and provincial assemblies (including Elected Representatives) and stakeholders, ensuring the voices of affected communities are accessible to policy and decision makers. (3) Advocate for inclusive engagement strategies across all humanitarian action including supporting humanitarian responders to prepare for and integrate the views of affected communities into programme decision making. (4) Provide technical support to humanitarian responders to improve community engagement strategies and practices in preparedness and response. This includes matching technical expertise across AAP WG members for shared learning and capacity building.
