Project Summary

(i) Provide effective coordination by strengthening linkages between the various coordination and working group levels from ICCG in the capital and intersectoral working groups in the regions to HCT level engagements with the Government; (ii)Promote humanitarian access in a complex operating environment, where the security situation remains volatile; (iii) Strengthen civil-military coordination at the regions and capital levels to improve response preparedness, rapid and safe delivery of humanitarian assistance, and foster enhanced predictability of civil-military coordination processes and platforms; (iv) support country-led identification of strategic HDP collaboration priorities/collective outcomes, which will inform the multi-year Humanitarian Response Strategy framework (2023-2025) and the humanitarian, development, and peace action based on a shared understanding of the nexus, and be reflected in the UNSDCF and the National Development Plan; (v) advocate for principled humanitarian action by relevant authorities and sharing its coordination expertise based on global best practices. This will include an enhanced interface and support to the Government of Niger, especially the Ministry of Humanitarian Affairs and the Prime Minister's Office, capitalizing on their willingness to lead on humanitarian issues; (vi) Enhance partnerships with existing and new humanitarian donors through improved bilateral and multilateral engagement; (vii) Increased accountability of the humanitarian system to meet humanitarian needs.

1. Besoins
1 - Besoins

OCHA will have to respond to followings needs: 1) respond to the humanitarian crisis and continue advocating for humanitarian coordination mechanisms to be adapted to ensure that they are fit for purpose to respond to the above challenges. 2) Also, to strengthen its deep field presence in areas of acute humanitarian needs if the security and access situation allow. (Set up a new antenna in Torodi). Through its main office in Niamey, OCHA will 3) promote and facilitate an inter-agency / inter-sectoral coordination of humanitarian action and response. 4) support the Humanitarian Country Team (HCT) and the Inter-Cluster Coordination Group (ICCG), 5) organize joint assessments, develop response plans, lead the planning process, and co-chairs working groups, e.g., Multisectoral cash transfer, CMCoord, humanitarian access, AAP etc. 6) OCHA will also spearhead public information, advocacy efforts, humanitarian planning and resource mobilization. 7) to support national and local capacities in coordinating humanitarian assistance and advocate for the greater involvement of development actors in addressing structural issues, 8) The need to ensure access to humanitarian workers to provide adequate and timely assistance to those in need.
