Project Summary

The main project goal is contribute to reduce food insecurity and improve livelihood of vulnerable IDPs, Returnees, and host community in Kadugli, Abujebaba, Dilling and Toladi locality South Kordofan and Kurmuk and Gissan locality in Blue Nile State to achieve HRP outcome 2 and 3 during the period of 12 months.
The projects expect to target 50500 beneficiaries. 4500 Households (M/F) (22500 Persons) new IDPs, returnees and vulnerable host community will be provided with crop & vegetable inputs and agricultural tools and 1350 Households (M/F) (6750 Persons) agro-pastoralist targeting 99900 heads of livestock to be supported with livestock, veterinary inputs and extension services.
3500 households male and female (17500 Persons) provided with climate smart vegetables for winter season farming, 1650 households (8250 Persons) gender and age disaggregated provided with livestock, veterinary inputs and extension services, 3500 households (17500 Persons) provided with post harvest techniques for food and crop storage, & processing facilities, and 1600 (1600 Persons) households (M/F) provided with labour, market information, capital and vocational training in livestock, Crop and vegetable production to promote Income generation activities.
The Voucher system is design to meet the specific needs of rural men and women, for instance by providing vegetable seeds, small ruminants and poultry to women to address their livelihoods needs. Beneficiaries will receive vouchers that can be redeemed for goods and services (e.g. seeds, tools, livestock, animal feed and veterinary supplies and services) at selected shops. it includes the following process; beneficiaries Identification, determine type Select local suppliers (paper, electronic, etc.) and value of voucher and select local suppliers, distribute vouchers to beneficiaries, beneficiaries exchange vouchers for goods and/or services and suppliers will be paid upon verification
ASSIST included the cash voucher system because it promote a diverse range of seed varieties based on farmers’ preference and it increase agricultural production and productivity and enable small holder farmers to ensure seed security, livestock production and animal health as farmers and livestock producers have access to quality input, goods and services.
The cash voucher will be both unconditional especial for beneficiaries that meet the criteria eg. trained and cash is disbursed without the need to fulfil any particular condition. Conditional cash transfer beneficiaries have to carry out certain actions or meet specific requirements, such as attending a training, adopting good agricultural practices.
Gender mainstreaming, Accountability to affected populations are part and partial strategy adapted for this project to ensure women are involved, participate, have equitable access to resources and decision making and access to employment, income-generating activities, gain skills, and cash for building agriculture and livestock assets while consulting them for feedback, complains grievance handling to determine beneficiary preference at design, implementation and evaluation phase which are an integral part of this system with checks and balance through spot check and key informant interviews.

Needs

According to FEWSNET Food Security report June 2018 to May 2019, the devaluation of local currency, high inflation with an average rate 68.9%, the scarcity & high prices of fuel have led to dramatically increased in prices of food by 150-200 percent in 2019, agricultural inputs & labor, Animal supplementary feed/folder and veterinary services. The Limited purchasing power further expected to affect ability to access food from the market, reduced food availability & diet diversity intake, which could negatively affect the nutrition status of the vulnerable household in Kadugli, Abujebaba, Dilling and Toladi locality in South Kordofan & Kurmuk and Gaissan locality in Blue Nile State. In addition, the heavy rains in 2018 that caused severe flooding in Kurmuk and Gissan locality in Blue Nile State have damaged houses, productive assets & sorghum crop although some vulnerable households replanted fast germinating sorghum to reduce severe risk of food insecurity in 2019. 60% of the vulnerable affected populations are women and children & at least 40% of households are headed by women or children with 65% have food stock enough only for 3 months, 25% have for 5 months, and 10% having for one year. 95% take two meals per day & will reduce to one/day as their harvest depleted. People displaced and returnees in recent months receive limited aid assistance.VAM May 2018 food consumption score for Kadugli, Abujebaba, Dilling and Toladi locality indicates 51%, 41%, 39%, and 38% are food insecure, 41%, 53%, 46%, and 58% are at borderline between food insecure and food secure, only 8%, 7%, 15%, and 5% are food secure, 16%, 3%, 11% and 7% are poor and on average only 42%, 59%, 54% and 59% have acceptable consumption score respectively.IPC October 2018 to March 2019, issued January 15, 2019 indicates that 33 localities are estimated to be IPC phase 3 including 15%, 17% in Phase 3 and worse in Kurmuk and Giassan locality in Blue Nile and Kadugli, Abujebaba, Dilling and Toladi locality in South Kordofan State between Oct-Dec. 2018 respectively. However, IPC projection for January to March 2019; Blue Nile and South Kordofan have a caseload of 135195, and 227640 in phase 3 and worse with total of 36748 (4%) and 35106 (3%) in phase 4 in Blue Nile and South Kordofan respectively. WFP monitoring market updates December 2018 indicate that, the sorghum and goats prices increased significantly by 160 to 283 and 56 to 259% respectively compared to 5- years average. More than 20% of population have poor food consumption score due to depleted of food stock, rising food prices, poor purchasing power, limited livelihood options, low income-level. 13% of vulnerable households are food insecure with average GAM rate of 10.5% in South Kordofan and 24.5% in the Blue Nile, and 0.4% are with acute malnutrition with the situation expected to be worse in SPLM-N-controlled areas. Gender mainstreaming, AAP is part and partial strategy adapted for this project.

Impact on environment/natural resources

Degradation in the environment due negative livelihood practices such as tree cutting for firewood & charcoal resulted into deforestation at alarming rate which influencing rainfall amount and contribute into increased soil erosion & desertification and reduce soil fertility resulting low production and long dry spells. Extensive livestock practices such as irregular movement and overconcentration poses significant threaten to already fragile ecosystem, which further undermine the agricultural productive and pastoral capacity. In addition the conflicts between pastoralists, agro-pastoralists and crop farmers are widespread and rooted in disputes over ownership and use of natural resources.The project will contributes to increasing the resilience of selected vulnerable households (IDPs, Returnees and host community) in South Kordofan and Blue Nile through provision of climate smart crop and vegetable seeds, provision of post harvest techniques for storages and processing, formation of farmers groups, Village agriculture committee (VAC), training Community animal health workers, provision of vaccination and treatment inputs for livestock considering digging dumping sites for proper disposal of vaccine waste, demarcation of livestock routes, water catchments, and support agro-business in farm and off farming production to improve food security through climate change adaptation measures sustain agricultural and livestock production. Training women on Fuel-Efficient Stoves to reduce use of wood. Secondly; extension services support for farmers to improve their knowledge land management practices in agriculture and livestock production. The project will strengthen structures and capacities within the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry, Fisheries and Animal Resources in strengthening agriculture and livestock extension service in Kadugli, Abujebaba, Dilling and Toladi locality South Kordofan and Kurmuk and Gissan in Blue Nile State. This facilitates the implementation of measures focusing on sustainable natural resource management at selected locations of the project with the support of the extension agents/workers.
