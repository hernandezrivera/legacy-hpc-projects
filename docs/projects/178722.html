Project Summary

The planned project will increase the self-resilience in the targeted communities by supporting most vulnerable households with sustainable livelihood through providing under-conflict doable income generation sources, The project will assist internal displaced and host communities households with 200 milk cows for 200 HHs to be as an income generator, supported with practical vocational shorth-term training about cow breeding conducted by senior vet trainers, trained new bread winners will be supported throughout 6 months with a close veterinarian backup by providing vet care and treatments field by expert veterinaries, moreover the project will strengthen the created livelihood by supporting cows with the required fodder for 6 months. In addition to the provided livestock the project will support the wheat value chain in the communities with high severity by providing 200 most vulnerable farmers with wheat
cultivating inputs for 1 hectare, the following inputs will be provided: 250 Kg/he seeds and irrigation vouchers for 50 mm irrigation depth, 20% of another vulnerable farmers yield will be procured to supply a complement QRCS project with flour. QRCS also will support mid-scale agricultural practices by supporting 400 most vulnerable farmers who planting 0.5 hectare with legumes by providing seeds, fertilizing materials, and irrigation vouchers for 200 mm of irrigation water, and supporting 200 most
vulnerable farmers who have olives trees by providing services, fertilizing materials, and irrigation vouchers for 200 mm of irrigation water.

4 - Needs

Syria remains one of the world’s most complex humanitarian emergencies characterized by ongoing hostilities which have killed ‎hundreds of ‎thousands of people, triggered one of the worst displacement crises of our time, and led to the widespread ‎destruction of civilian and ‎agricultural infrastructure, including homes, water supply and irrigation systems. Today, 13.4 million ‎people in Syria are in need of ‎humanitarian assistance, The Syrian pound is in virtual freefall having lost 78 per cent of its value ‎since October 2019, while price increases ‎for staple goods are at an all-time high. The COVID-19 pandemic has accelerated this ‎economic downturn by further reducing already sparse ‎income-generating opportunities in a context where 50 per cent of the ‎work age population is now estimated unemployed, Substantial ‎damage has also been sustained by water systems, furthermore, ‎Needs related to agricultural livelihoods therefore remain ‎considerable.Farmers also suffer from limited access to markets due to ‎physical and economic constraints, limited access to finance, ‎damaged infrastructure such as irrigation canals, limited access to ‎relevant early sewage networks and irrigation canals (HNO, 2021) ‎‎(Advanced Copy)‎ According to (HNAP, Feb, 2021) Aleppo is the highest governorate regarding to population density with 1.2 million IDPs, about ‎‎8,600 IDPs ‎arrived it only in February 2021Wheat ‎crop production as a strategic food pillar in Syria has significantly damaged by the drought in 2020/2021 season as a ‎result of rainfall ‎shortage, during wheat growing season Arrouj plain in Jisr-Alshughur subdistricts received only 62% of the ‎annual average of the rainfall. ‎‎(GALI, 2021)‎

5 - Implementation of activities

The project intervention will be throughout: - Providing 200 milk cows in addition to fodder and vet materials for 6 months. - Providing in-kind 50 MT of wheat seeds for 200 farmers, with fuel vouchers. - Providing in-kind legumes seeds and fertilizing materials for another 200 farmers, with fuel vouchers. - Providing agricultural services, and fertilizing materials for 200 olives farmers, with fuel vouchers - Purchasing 160 MT of wheat yield from 200 wheat farmers. Project implementation cycle will start from coordination with the communities representatives and local councils, performing livestock market assessments to evaluate cows, fodder, and seeds availability. Beneficiaries selection will be built according to their vulnerability and need prioritizing displaced families affected by last escalation with the focus on female/children headed HHs and disabled persons, the project has been designed to mitigate the conflict consequences on adolescents as well by included them in the targeted beneficiaries. Qatar Red crescent society will implement the project by Syrian expertise through an infield team with wide knowledge of the Syrian context and belong to the targeted communities, the field team will comprise: vet experts, field, logistics officers and workers, all members have high awareness and long experience in the humanitarian service providing and had been trained on GBV, PSEA, doing no harm, protection and security aspects.Beneficiaries selection will be as a result of an interactive process will start with introducing the project plan, beneficiaries criteria and targets to the targeted communities in order to mainstream them in the project.Trained M&E team will be assigned/hired locally.

Assumptions: Timely disbursement of funds Risk: The delay in funds which will have an adverse effect on the start of activities Likelihood: low Mitigation measure: implementing low cost activities Assumptions: Conflict does not affect access to markets Risk: equipment price fluctuation, Fuel price fluctuation, tightening of the siege making it more difficult to procure materials, Likelihood: Medium Mitigation measure: using equipment available in the local markets near the project area, importing fuel from Turkey considering ‎price ‎differences, contracting with suppliers at the beginning of the project, requesting project reprogramming as plan B ‎ Assumptions: Coordinating with representatives of target communities ‎ Risk: Lack of coordination and synergies with representatives of target communities ‎ Likelihood: Low Mitigation measure: involve focal persons from the local council to coordinate with the beneficiary elders ‎ ‎ ‎ Assumptions: no serious military actions in the project area ‎ Risk: Security and access are disrupted to key irrigations ‎ Likelihood: Medium Mitigation measure: suspending activity implementation, close coordination with QRCS safety and security specialist, Use of ‎‎alternative ‎measures for project supervision including Remote Asset Monitoring to carry out spot checks in the target areas , ‎‎changing implementation ‎location, extending the project period
