Project Summary

The project is located in DeirEzZor "DEZ" governorate and consisted of three key
component areas: 1-preparing currently out-of-school children(boys, girls) to return to formal schools, 2-securing equitable access to NFE -FE provision for children and youth in DEZ, and 3- supporting TVET for Youth girls and boys. The project will reach 2900 children per year (Boys, Girls)in the age of 7 - 17 including children with disability CWD under formal and NFE and 150/per year of youth who are between 18-24Yrs. the targeted schools will be four: two of them are preparatory and two are primary. 5 ‘Back to Learning’ campaigns/per year will be undertaken as well as project information dissemination. all the three-component include comprehensive supporting environment services for education quality which are: providing textbooks, stationery kits for all targeted students, minor rehabilitation work, providing schools furniture and gender and disability sensitive wash facilities, potable water, electricity, and fuel winterization. This work also forms part of the recruitment strategy for the emerging Parent/Teacher Associations, to secure ongoing community support and participation.
For OOSC, NFE services will be provided in each of the project locations. based on children needs assessment, two cycles of SLP per year, targeting children with minor gaps or more than 2 cycles according to their education needs, therefore likely to transition to formal education for the coming academic year. Under the NFE we have: school feeding program, PSS program and PSS recreational kits for schools, teachers incentives. Additional ongoing support will be provided throughout the academic year once children have transferred to formal schools; this will support their retention in this provision through remedial classes and homework support as appropriate. Children will be identified by the placement test according to needs/age. the project includes mobile education modality which is tailored to provide education due to the absence of an education environment to provide education or establish TLS or home-based centers with mobile and fixed teachers to cover OOS children who are above 12 and under 18 from boys, girls. security and safety plans will be established and resourced at each location. For FE-NFE: Professional development and capacity-building to enhance the performance and quality of education personnel for 90 persons per year under CSG, positive discipline, peer coaching and SEL, and provision of textbooks of the SEL. The project includes doing a pilot for SEL in the formal and NFE schools by the trained teachers and providing all needed materials. The project includes supporting youth from boys and girls and improving the socio-economic situation by supporting NF TVET school in DEZ, by establishing one new center and targeting 100 students, equipping it, provision of all needed staff and educational materials for the five professions determined through market assessment. And support the graduated students with startup vocational kit and incentives. TVET will be for male and female from Youth 16-24Yrs, including highly PWD, women headed HH and girls under risk of Early marriage, 2 NF-TVET cycle per year. The center includes the provision of life skills sessions and PSS. Through all these components, the project will therefore improve the access to and the quality of formal and non-formal education NES. Finally, all the COVID 19 preventative measures shall be taken into account while implementing the project activities. the children will have participated through monitoring program and feedback mechanisms, complaint boxs, and classrooms level initiatives for more engagements in activities, reflection and taking actions on other hand as kind of response for their needs and inputs.
