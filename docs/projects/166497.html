Project Summary

Access to learning and education for all is one of the fundamental areas that need urgent attention in this response. In response to this pandemic, Government and international organizations are working toward strategic ways in promoting access to learning for all in all communities despite the social distance and some COVID-19 protocols, learning has been impacted with low and limited children having access to learning.
To this effect, CATAI is implementing Digital learning innovation with the focal point of meeting with the strategic objective of the response strategy of the COVID-19. The affected population within almost 6 IDP camps will have access to learning through digital tools with the solid Aim of retaining children in learning.

1 - Needs

Nigeria, and indeed the Northeast is a conflict prone area with deep seated multiple crisesof religious tensions. These conflicts, largely due to poverty, unemployment and illiteracyhas displaced over 1.8 million people and put over 4.8 million in crises, emergency andfamine1 and resulted in one of the worlds’ worst case of humanitarian crises. Women andchildren are the most affected vulnerable groups (both in Internally Displaced Persons(IDP) camps and local communities in the Northeast and other states in Nigeria). Poorhealth conditions, Transactional sex, human trafficking, early/forced marriage and drugabuse have been reported among IDPs. A large number of children have become streetbeggars. Lack of livelihood opportunities for young people makes them vulnerable torecruitment by groups like Islamic state for West African Province (ISWAP) popularlyknown as Jama’at Ahlas sunnah lid-Dawah wal-Jihad Boko Haram. There have been reportsof an increase in violent crime and drug abuse, all these serving as coping strategies forsurvival with increase in out of school and children without opportunity to attend school. Center for Advocacy Transparency and Accountability initiative (CATAI) hopes to establisha Teaching-Learning space (TLS) for affected population to teach school age children basicsof literacy and numeracy as a strategy in giving them basic knowledge on how to read andwrite and to also give them opportunity to be enrolled in school . 450 Children from theElmiskin camp will be taught basic literacy skills in both formal and non-formal schools.This will enable them to be active in the post insurgency and the resettlement processes.All these activities will follow with an online presence with the hash tag #Educatechild onall our social media platforms.

2 - Activities

The project’s global objective is to increase access and continuity of quality, accessible and inclusive education for crisis-affected female and male children and youth (aged 3 – 18) in Borno state The first expected results for this general objective are that out-of-school crisis-affected children and youth are provided with a flexible and mobile response to increasing access to functional alternative education solutions in emergency contexts (Output 1.1). This will be measured through the number of TLS constructed, the number of children and youth receiving individual learning material, and the number of classrooms supported. For this output, the targeted beneficiaries are 1200 pre-primary to school-aged IDP children and 480 youth/adolescents.The second expected result is that crisis-affected out-of-school girls and boys are provided with increased opportunities to access a formal, better-quality, and functional education and learning environment (Output 1.2). This result will be measured through the number of trained community committee of education. The targeted beneficiaries for this output are 5 349 school-aged children and 12 community committee of education.The third expected result is that crisis-affected girls and boys are supported in accessing quality education through an adequate, inclusive, safe, and child-friendly learning environment to reduce drop-out risks and mitigate the impact of the crisis on their well-being (Output 1.3). And this expected result will be measured through the percentage of learning spaces adapted to host recreational activities. The targeted beneficiaries for this last output are 1 890 pre-primary to school-aged children, 42 community teachers, and 12 community committee of education. The proposed project aims at targeting 5 649 pre-primary to school-aged children and 300 youth, will support 12 community committees of education (CEC), and train 42 community teachers. • CATAI will lead community awareness on the importance of schooling with a focus on girls’ access to education: indeed, to increase the demand for education, CATAI will sensitize affected communities (host, displaced, returned) on the need to enrol children into school with a focus on girls.• CATAI will distribute 1200 individual school kits in order to alleviate the issue of education costs for the most vulnerable families and improve the learning environment for girls and boys. • Light Rehabilitation of most affected existing classes & learning spaces in Camps to address the urgent needs for functional learning spaces, damaged or worn-out classes and spaces will be rehabilitated. CATAI will look at the opportunity to involve the community through enhancing WASH facilities in existing learning spaces which will be rehabilitated to ensure access to the minimum WASH package.• Accelerated education programs and remedial courses for crisis-affected children. To facilitate transition from non-formal to formal system, CATAI will implement a 3-month accelerated curriculum in TLS next to or attached to existing schools to facilitate reintegration into the education system of children who have been out of school for 3 months or less. Also, certification will be provided following the program taught in TLS to facilitate reintegration into formal system after 1 year. This will involve working closely with local schools when existing as well as teachers and authorities to facilitate transition and delivery of adequate documentation if required.

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

CATAI will ensure that all project sites identified for the implementation of the project meet protection standards. CATAI shall work closely with SEMA , EIEW, Bulamas and the SBMCS in the respective IDPS Camps and host communities to ensure that the learning facilities are at close proximity, safe, clean and meet protection and COVID-19 IPC standard. Ensuring proper hygiene facilities in the respective IDP CAMP, the availability of water supply and additional shade where applicable shall be the role of CATAI to ensure. All learning space shall be done in a manner that will ensure conduciveness.. CATAI shall ensure that all staff, volunteers and service providers assigned to carry out tasks within the implementation of project activities sign CATAI’s PSEA policy and child protection Policy in addition to code of conduct. This is to ensure the maximum protection of women, girls and boys from abuse and exploitation throughout the project. Each Camp shall have a Complaint and feedback posts where beneficiaries as well as other observers can channel their complaints and concerns about the implementation process. CATAI shall ensure that proper sensitization is made before, during the implementation of the project to ensure that IDPs and host communities understands the categories what the process of the project entails and how their rights will be protected. The Community local leaders(Bulamas) shall also be engaged throughout this process to ensure a clear understanding and proportional target is met among the IDPs and host communities population so as to avoid one region of the Camp benefitting more as compared to another. SEMA and other camp management bodies shall be involved throughout this process.Constant sensitization shall be done especially on how and where beneficiaries can channel their complaints and protection concerns throughout the project implementation. CATAI shall ensure that beneficiaries are fully aware of where the complaint and Feedback Mechanisms focal points are located in the respective communities to submit protection related concerns and issues. communities shall be educated on how to send in their concerns and get feedback as all levels of the project
