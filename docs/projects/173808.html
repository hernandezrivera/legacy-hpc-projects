Project Summary

In 2020 education is the most affected sector in Pakistan due to a series of emergencies. From COVID-19 to floods and again the second wave of COVID 19 affected the learning of children at schools. Pakistan was already struggling to improve its net enrollment because of the high ratio of out of school children. This situation was further become worsen with the closure of schools due to COVID 19 and in some areas damages to schools because of the flood. As per government education response plan, COVID-19 has added 140 million more children to the existing 22 million out of school children (55% are girls). As an effect of school closure and due to other secondary impacts of COVID-19 the already enrolled children are also vulnerable to drop out mainly due to the poor economic situation of families and loss of the source of livelihood due to COVID 19 and floods. Save the Children (SC) research on the Impact of COVID 19 on children shows that children in rural the area learning has decreased from 20% to 15% due to COVID 19 with girls reporting relatively higher reduction in learning. In Sindh, the situation is more vulnerable where the ratio of school children is already high with high gender disparity. And the recent floods along with COVID 19 have put the children at stake to miss their schools as well as vulnerable to drop out. Schools were already short of health and hygiene facilities while the floods damage the poor existing facilities living the schools with no WASH facilities, which protect the children from infection and ensure their safety. The existing education system in the province is also not capable to support the continuation of children learning in wake of a school closure or damages due to floods.
SC has experience in implementing robust and ready to implement successful interventions to cope with the current situation that has minimized the children learning losses and reduced the number of days to be missed. Responding to this situation SC will implement a holistic model that can respond to the need of children in both natures of emergencies by keeping education continue as well as making the community including children aware and resilient to these crises.
SC will intend to work with the School Education and Literacy Department in four districts “Dadu, Badin, Hyderabad, and Tando Muhmmad Khan” of Sindh province that is most affected by COVID-19 and floods in terms of schools damages and learning losses. In particular, SC will;
1. Establish 8 Temporary Learning Centers to provide learning spaces to damaged school children especially focusing on girl child
2. Rehabilitation of 240 schools including COVID 19 focused facilities prioritizing the girls’ schools
3. Capacitate and where needed activate 240 School Management Committees by developing COVID 19 and Flood resilient School Improvement Plan ensuring the women/girls participation
4. Enhanced teachers skills on distance learning and infection prevention models
5. Campaigning for implementation of safe to schools SoPs through media sources by airing messages in 4 targeted districts
6. Scaling up SELD started distance learning in 4 target districts, as well as pilot SC, developed an online application for Education in Emergencies
7. Provision of learning material for 36,000 children in schools to fill the economic loss occurred
The above holistic approach will serve the purpose of continuation of children's education and support the teachers and parents with the knowledge, school rehabilitation, and material. This will enable all the stakeholders involved in the provision of education for children and prioritize their safety. Target groups will include;
1. School-age children
2. Parents/community members
3. Teachers
4. SMCs members
Based on this holistic model and reach to multiple beneficiaries from project activities. SC aims to reach 0.4 million direct beneficiaries including male and female children, parents, teachers, SMC and community members.

1- Project Activities

In the proposed holistic model, activities are designed on basis of needs highlighted in different studies undertaken for floods and COVID 19 context. Below is the description of each activity: 1. Engagement with government line departments.- SC is already working closely with Education, NDMA, social welfare and health departments with NOC to operate across Pakistan. At start of project, SC will engage with relevant district authorities to on-board them about project activities. 2. Establish TLC to provide learning spaces to damaged school children especially focusing on girls'.- SC will establish 2 TLCs in each district to meet the education continuation need of the children from damaged schools. These TLCs will be equipped with all teaching and learning material for teachers and students along with health & hygiene requirements of children.3. School rehabilitation of damages including COVID 19 focused facilities prioritizing the girls’ schools and girls focused facilities- SC will conduct a detailed assessment of damages to these schools by involving the education department and SMCs to get a clear picture of damages to these schools. SC using its previous experience for working with SMC, and will involve these SMCs in developing and implementation of these School Development plan developed based on detailed assessment. Girls' needs regarding education and facilities will be prioritized in particular.4. Capacitate and activate SMCs by developing COVID 19 and Flood resilient School Improvement Plan ensuring the women/girls participation - Based on previous experience, SC will train these SMCs on School-based disaster risk management (SBDRM) through contextualizing its existing SBDRM manual to COVID 19 context. These SMCs will also be capacitated on developing the School Improvement Plan included in the SC global School Health and Nutrition COVID 19 contextualized model.5. Enhanced teachers skills on Remote Learning/distance learning and infection prevention models by using multiple purpose gadgets/tools- Due to the increased importance of distance teaching, the project will develop a consolidated training package on the new approaches and capacitate the teachers on this package to enable them on the teaching requirement of children in pandemics like COVID 19. Female teachers will be prioritized due to the increased need for girl's education in the target area and a higher ratio of out-of-school girls in the province.6. Campaigning for implementation of safe to schools SoPs through media sources by airing messages and material and equipping schools with infection prevention material to keep the children safe- In the midst of COVID 19, SC innovated the remote community mobilization approach by using different social media channels in midst of COVID 19. All these initiatives will be used to get aware of the people in Safe back-to-school SOPs, increase awareness on COVID 19 and encouraging children and parents to send back children to schools by taking all safety measures to discourage drop out.7. Scaling up SELD started distance learning in target districts, as well as pilot SC, developed an online application for Education in Emergencies. - SC has developed a distance learning model for Non-formal education centers in Sindh and is working on a remote learning application for education in emergencies. These on-going initiatives will be aligned with SELD online education initiatives and will be brought on the scale in target districts.8. Provision of Teaching in Learning material for children in schools to fill the economic loss occurred by emergencies- SC will develop a package of learning material for each child of these affected schools. This package will include learning material and aids that children can use in schools as well as at home. The package will also include the COVID 19 material, children can increase their knowledge about this infectious disease and prevent themselves and their families.
