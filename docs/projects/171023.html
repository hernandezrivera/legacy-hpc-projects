Project Summary

This Response will target among the vulnerable 2.7 million people that internally displaced and host communities that requires assistance in the greater Somalia/land. DRC will target over 3000HHs(18000 beneficiaries) in both livelihoods with conditional and unconditional cash transfers. The injection of cash is meant to increase the purchasing power for target households with limited access to food commodities that are available in the local markets. The provision of cash will be a dignified means of supporting beneficiaries as they allow them to make their own choices in terms of the types and quantities of food to purchase. Under cash for work activities component, community members will be engaged to rehabilitate productive community assets to restore them to usable condition. The community members will identify and select assets that are not in proper working condition and those that have been damaged by human activities or natural calamities for rehabilitation/restoration. Possible cash for work activities will be bush clearing, erosion control, digging latrine pits, road work to improve access to markets, basic services and other work to preserve community assets and recover from effects of drought. Women and men will be equally involved in determining the final gender appropriate cash for work activities. Care will be taken to select activities that allow a high percentage of women to participate. Work norms will be established to ensure a reasonable quantity of work but allow women time to carry out their family responsibilities. The Danish Demining Group will complement DRC’s efforts and provide mine risk education to the population in target areas.Acoountability to affected population (AAP) will be applied Additionally, unconditional cash transfers covering at least 100% of MEB will be provided to HHs that do not meet the cash for work criteria and do not have an available adult laborer within the family to be involved. Examples include female-headed households where all members of the family are children except for the caregiver

Project Activities

Provision of farming inputs (spade, wheelbarrow, panga, shovels,seeds ,fertilizers) to support irrigation and increase agricultural production for small scale subsistence farmers from asset poor HHs and minority people who lack the necessary collateral to access credit.. This activity will target 40% female headed households and People with Disabilities to reduce negative survival mechanisms including livelihoods that increase their risk of violence and exploitation. Provision of Cash for Work activities to generate sufficient income for households in Emergency and Crisis to access at least 75% of the cost of the MEB while maintaining and rehabilitating communal assets. DRC will target 60% female participants and People with Disabilities . Gender roles and cultural context will be a key consideration, so that women can undertake activities that are consistent with Somali cultural tradition. Provision of unconditional cash transfers to vulnerable households and those at particular risk of exclusion from accessing livelihoods to increase their access to food, and strengthen purchasing power for essential household items and services. These activities will target at least 50% female beneficiaries and People with Disabilities . Provision of unconditional food vouchers to vulnerable households in urban areas in emergency and crisis in order to increase access to essential food and non-food items through supply of selected quantities of dry food items from selected vendors on a monthly basis. The project will target the most vulnerable persons including boys, girls, men and women and People with Disabilities . At least 50% of the targeted households will be female headed and will be given at least 100% of MEB. Specific food that is culturally and religiously appropriate to the beneficiaries may be provided on a monthly basisDRC will integrated COVID19 messaging in all above activities in order to protect elderly and persons with underlying issues

Please provide further explanation

project will contribute to durable/ resilience of vulnerable populations against shocks as part of FSC objectives. This will include protecting and conserving natural resources on which lives, and livelihoods depend, such as water, soil, land and forest resources. The activities will be directed at repairing and effectively managing small-scale productive infrastructure to mitigate shocks (e.g. river de-silting and embankment repair to prevent seasonal floods) using a combination of machinery and human works as appropriate. As well, life skills training activities will be prioritized to increase household income for the vulnerable and food insecure households. Community-based preparedness, surveillance and early warning systems/networks will contribute to prevent damage or losses to crops, livestock and coastal fisheries
