Project Summary

Enhance accessibility and delivery of a comprehensive and integrated package of health care services to alleviate the tension and suffering of the most vulnerable groups in Latakia, Jablah, and Al-Qurdaha sub-districts in Latakia Governorate.
2.2. Objectives/ goal:
- Reduce morbidity and mortality rates through securing accessible, timely-mannered, and quality services.
- Delivery of life-sustaining, coordinated, and equitable health services to all.
- Alleviate tension of health care disparities in Latakian rural and suburban areas and enhance meaning in the lives of the most vulnerable communities.
- Enhance community ownership and patient safety in vulnerable communities to ensure an inclusive, whole-of-society approach to health system recovery, inter-sectoral collaboration, integrated programming, and proactive engagement with humanitarian donors.
2.3. Expected outcomes: type of service(s) and the beneficiaries such as:
- Access and provision of primary health care services, including medical consultations, medications, and diagnostic investigation, are strengthened to 104,856 beneficiaries.
- RCCE: Health promotion and education sessions on PHC issues are enhanced through spreading key health awareness messages to 28,080 Beneficiaries.
- Advanced medical treatment under secondary care is boosted on a referral basis to 288 beneficiaries.
- Provision of assistive devices and trauma management services for people with disabilities is reinforced to 300 Beneficiaries.
- Provision of MHPSS services at the community level is strengthened through a community centre and two mobile teams to 101,520 beneficiaries.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

In line with national health priorities in terms of the necessity to increase access to equitable basic healthcare services for the most vulnerable groups especially after the recent EQ disaster and currency depreciation, the Syrian youth council aims to strengthen the functionality of its operational PHC center to keep meeting quality basic health care services and cover the increasing needs of Al Daatour as a vulnerable host community and its surrounding affected by EQ, also to address shortage of PHC services in Al-Qurdaha and Jablah districts and transportation and out-of-money pocket through outreach medical teams. The basic health needs of the targeted affected population will be supported through the following interventions:Primary health care:Under those activities of the project, SYC will enhance the delivery of primary health care services by strengthening the functions of its 3 clinics that operate 6 days a week for 6 hours per day outside of governmental working hours as agreed with DOH from 2 p.m to 8 p.m to increase accessibility for governmental employees of the lowest wages to access appropriately since they form most of the residents. Static medical point in Al- Daatour consists of 3 clinics: Pediatric, Internal Medicine, Gyneacology/RH, where all clinics will operate daily with mhGAP trained doctors also making referrals to a contracted psychiatrist within the project.SYC teams are trained on WHO and DoH related training in communicable diseases programs, Rh, and nutrition and SYC shall procure the identified needs of medicines and medical supplies as per its procurement policy under the principle of the best value for money. Deployment of a medical mobile team to Al-Qurdaha district in Latakia governorate consisting of an internist, a gynecologist, a pediatrician, a nurse, a midwife, a pharmacist and Health educator targeting the following communities that are being targeted by the MMT supported by WHO for another 4 months from now, but the intervention will lead another vehicle for the first two months and expand scope of services in winter, also sustain the main team for the remaining time of the year ( Snaibleh, Shemberti, Al-Senqalin, Bzermo, Al-Dlaibat, Al-Shouq, Shnboutin, Al-Amoud, Fourshat, Al-Arkoub, Nineh, Bsout, Bisseen, Bsherieh, Owainet Al-Reehan, Al-Ktalbeh, Jermati, Ghlmaiseh, Bqelioun, Al-Qalmoun, Al-Jbeiryyieh, Marj Mouerban, Al-Mroush, Btalaa, Al-Kallouryyieh, Ras Al-Kallouryyieh, Al-Khzaimyyieh). All coordinated with DoH
