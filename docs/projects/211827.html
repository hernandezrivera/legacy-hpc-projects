Project Summary

Needs:
Twelve years of unrelenting conflict in Northwest Syria have inflicted a devastating toll on its people, displacing over 2 million residents, with 1.8 million confined to congested and often unsanitary IDP camps. This vulnerable population faces a multitude of hardships, including a rapidly deteriorating humanitarian situation in 2023. The recent earthquake, coupled with worsening food shortages and the most significant escalation of hostilities in four years, has pushed them towards the brink. Food insecurity runs rampant, with many households struggling to access essential and nutritious food due to limited income, disrupted livelihoods, and skyrocketing prices. These dire living conditions, coupled with the recent rabies outbreak, pose serious health risks. Traditional coping mechanisms have been disrupted, leaving families with limited options to meet their basic needs, often resorting to risky strategies to survive. This project aims to address these critical and urgent needs by providing targeted food security assistance and emergency rations, empowering vulnerable households to prioritize their immediate needs and rebuild their lives with dignity and resilience.
Implementation of Activity:
- Objective: Reducing food insecurity and inability to meet basic immediate needs, through monthly food security assistance, emergency ready-to-eat rations
The objective will be achieved through providing food security assistance, and emergency food assistance. The program will achieve its purpose by improving food security, reducing reliance on risky coping mechanisms, and enabling current and newly displaced HHs and vulnerable communities to simultaneously meet their immediate multifaceted food needs with dignity and flexibility. This will further support the overall program goal by addressing lifesaving needs and promoting self-resilience leading toward early recovery.
- Targeted BNFs: The project aims to target 30K HHs with monthly cash for food distribution.
Locations: in Aleppo (Azaz, Al-Bab, Jarablus, Jabel Saman) and Idleb (Idleb, Ariha, Harim, Jisr-Ash-Shugur).
- Modality: E-card, where each beneficiary will get 65 USD per round for 8 rounds
- The selection criteria are:
1- Families with no regular income / limited income
2- Two or more family members unless one elderly living alone (above 60)
3- Families living in the targeted area/community/camp
4- Targeted families are not from the contracted vendors (for vouchers)
