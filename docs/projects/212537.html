Project Summary

Mihad team is operating three PHCs in Idlib in Barisha (G0703043!Barisha PHC Unit) and Aleppo in Bab Al Hajar (B0202027 ! Bab alhajar) and Kafr Kalbin (B0204104 ! Kafr Kalbein PHC) with the delivery of primary health consultations with the integration of nutrition services. The aim of the intervention is increasing the availability and affordability of primary healthcare, including integrated SRH, nutrition services and protection services. Through its output 'Integrated essential primary healthcare and nutrition services through one PHC are provided through 4 PHCs', the intervention aims at delivering 32,400 medical consultations (8,262 consultations for girls, 7,938 for boys, 9,720 for women and 6,480 for men) in the three facilities mentioned previously. As far as protection is concerned, it will be integrated in the PHC in Barisha, mainly focusing on GBV case management, related referrals to other services and PSS sessions. The protection staff is trained on case management, safeguarding and receive regular refresh sessions to be able to deliver appropriate and safe protection services. Following the 13 years of conflict and the recent escalation of violence started in October, populations and especially women are exposed to protection risks including GBV-related ones. Also, social stigma may refrain women anf girls from accessing GBV services while they are exposed to this risk. Regarding primary health, the intervention aims at providing the following services according to the EHSP developed by WHO and health partners: child health package, reproductive health package, non-communicable diseases package, communicable diseases package, nutrition services, providing lab analysis and medicines, referrals to different levels of healthcare services. After several years of conflict, combined with a struggling economic situation in the area, in addition to limited support (WFP), the nutrition and health status of the population in NWS is deteriorating in addition to difficulties of access to services. In this context, the project aims at improving the accessiblity of essential health services.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Mihad team operates three Primary Health Care (PHC) facilities in Idlib and Aleppo, namely Barisha (G0703043!Barisha PHC Unit), Bab Al Hajar (B0202027 ! Bab alhajar), and Kafr Kalbin (B0204104 ! Kafr Kalbein PHC). These facilities provide essential health services and nutrition consultations. The intervention aims to enhance access to primary healthcare, including reproductive and nutrition services, and protection against gender-based violence (GBV). In Barisha, GBV case management and psychosocial support sessions are integrated into PHC services. The project targets 75,600 medical consultations, addressing the health needs of women, men, girls, and boys. The region faces challenges due to prolonged conflict, economic hardship, and limited support, exacerbating health and nutrition issues. The project seeks to mitigate these challenges by improving access to essential health and (nutrition consultation to 22,680 people). Regarding primary health, the intervention aims at providing the following services according to the EHSP developed by WHO and health partners: child health package, reproductive health package, non-communicable diseases package, communicable diseases package, nutrition services, providing lab analysis and medicines, referrals to different levels of healthcare services. By utilizing three RRTs teams, the project aimed to reduce the risk of malnutrition in three communities (Kaljibrin, Kafr kalbin ,and Barisha) in NW Syria through combined preventive and curative approaches. Both RRts and nutrition teams in the fix facilities with provide CMAM and IYCF programs to children Under five of age and PLWs/cargivcers of children under 2 years of age.
