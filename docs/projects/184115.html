Project Summary

White Nile State is one of the least developed states in Sudan with an estimated in Sudan with an estimated 41% of the people living in acute poverty of less than $1.25 per day (HRP, 2021). The latest Humanitarian Response Plan estimates that 1.1 million people in the state are in need of humanitarian assistance.
The proposed project is an integrated project that will cover thematic areas of Education and WASH for more than 11,600 person (vulnerably host communities ) (6,000 women, 2,800men, 1,180 girl and 1,620 boy) from Kosti and Algabalain Localities in White Nile state. It targets 10 communities and more than 11,500vulnerable people in targeted areas with below breakdown:
WASH response : targeted 7,600 ( flood -affected people ) and will deliver the essential WASH services in the affected communities by floods.
Education response: targeted 4,000 ( (49% girls and %51 boys and from total targeted children 1% children with disabilities) through improve quality education and other services by construction and rehabilitation of classroom and provision of furniture , direct support through cash grant for HHs and incentive for school teachers and response to COVID-19 pandemic through deliver of awareness sessions establishing referral pathway and other protection servicers(Psychological and social support) ,hard copies and training on COVID-19 school protocols and distribution Non medical mask for children ,teachers and school staff.
The project will provide sustainable assistance through multispectral interventions that promote resilience among affected populations and strengthen human security in targeted locations.
JASMAR have operation ,present and good relation with targeted communities in White Nile state, will coordinate the project activities with other humanitarian actors and forums (sectors Education , WASH ,Nutrition and Health sectors ) and respective government departments in the project sites . JASMAR implementation strategy will be direct implementation which include regular community orientation ,mobilization and engagement.

The Education Sector has identified 2.7 million conflict and disaster-affected school-aged children in need to access quality basic and secondary school education. This project will targets 4000 children’s from host community - in White Nile state in Kosti locality and targeted AL wadi primary school for girls hosting about 2030 and AL wadi primary school for boys about 1910 children and all of them between (7 to 13 years old) .Abosharif community one of communities affected directly by economic crisis and COVID -19 in this community 2 primary school serving more than 4,500 children from 5 areas in Kosti locality near to Abosharif community which lead to crowding in some school and other activities .JASMAR in targeted localities responses to the identified needs are in line with the Education sector response strategy (Learning environments are safe, protective and responsive to the needs of vulnerable children including children with disabilities).. This project proposal is made relevant to link with the emergency response, as well as to focus on life-saving assistance in ongoing humanitarian responses. Moreover, it is linked to WASH clusters partners' response which mainly focuses on dignified access to critical basic WASH services in affected areas and is also linked directly to fit the humanitarian situation and priorities of affected people by flood in Aljabalain locality, The proposed target location the flood-affected areas . The proposed interventions will be in line with the WASH sector strategic objectives to address the prioritized sectoral needs of the most affected people host communities in Aljabalain a locality of White Nile state.
