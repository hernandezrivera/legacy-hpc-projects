Project Summary

The project aims to target:
Total beneficiaries 4000 and in the camps in Azaz, Al-Bab, Dana, sub-districts.
Through the main following interventions:
- Work on providing integrated protection services focusing on societal approaches. Systematic PSS services, PFA, adolescent community initiatives and PSS activities for newly displaced children will be provided, focusing on persons with disabilities in all activities.
- We will target community actors to provide them with an awareness of CP issues and will strive to enable them to contribute to reducing protection risks in their communities.
We will contribute to empowering women and girls through awareness sessions, and through vocational training for those in need.
We will provide positive parenting programs, through integrated programs for children and caregivers.
- There will be case managers to point out children at risk of protection and work to mitigate these risks and link them to services. We will create mobile teams that provide beneficiaries in schools, camps, PFA, and PSS and contribute to identifying cases of children at risk.
- At the beginning of each project, Takaful hold a meeting with the local community and local actors, ensuring their participation in the project, explaining the project goals and taking their suggestions and observations to develop project activities and implementation. Takaful will coordinate with the Protection Cluster and with other organizations in the same sector and field to reach the appropriate beneficiaries and avoid any duplication.

4 - Needs

According to MSNA 2020, the Protection issues and needs in Syria should be understood in relation to the impact of nine consecutive years of crisis. The protracted nature of the crisis continues to create increasingly complex and inter-connected protection issues and needs that affect all population groups, in an ever-evolving environment. 13.6 million people in protection needs, 54% of them adults, 32% children 5-17. In addition to physical destruction, the protracted and multiple cycles of displacement have contributed to a steady erosion of coping/protection mechanisms of individuals and communities, generated high levels of psychological distress to the most vulnerable populations. All of which adversely affect the community especially women and children. Needs Takaful carried out a protection assessment in Azaz, Afrin. It was found that the critical problems that affecting girls are early marriage, sexual violence, sexual harassment and restriction of movement with a rate of 64%. 33% of the KIs said that female children are exposed to economic exploitation and the males children 22% according to key informants.
