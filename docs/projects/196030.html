Project Summary

The IDPs of Ras Al-Ain are in dire need of winter materials, especially winter clothes for adults and children, in light of the high prices and the inability of the IDPs to buy as a result of their loss of the most important necessities of living and work for more than ten years, and their loss of livelihood sources, lands, shops and blinds as a result of displacement from Ras Al-Ain.

3 - Needs

Since September 2019, the date of influx of local families from Ras Al Ain to the secured southern areas including BICA office which moved its office and based in Hassakeh city, Moreover, BICA as of its mandate is responsible geographically for Ras Al Ain humanitarian response intervention, and responsible to develop a geographic information system covering Ras Al Ain areas, BICA has immediately, established ad hoc assessment committees and set up a network of staff with local knowledge managing data collection from immediate site visits, which considered the most trusted sources. And despite the field difficulties, BICA conducted a number of field assessments targeting the displaced families, where they, through direct observation from the field, visited the entire families at their different locations both at collective shelters and host communities (More than rural locations including Tal Tamer town and Hassakeh city and aldrbasia ). As well, The association communicates with displaced families through a net account and telephone link created by the Association for this purpose. 7 - Protection Risk Analysis

Security and safety risk: the security is unpredictable although the areas of intervention is enjoying calm security situation, Likelihood: low. Impact: medium. Mitigation: BICA closely monitors the changes in the overall security situation in targeted areas and adjust accordingly its operations, adapting its protocols, systems and contingency plans, to minimize safety and security risks for its personnel and beneficiaries, and ensure safe transportation and storage of goods. BICA engages community leaders, authorities in order to determine the most suitable and safe locations for distribution, and to ensure that all target beneficiaries are informed including people with disabilities about distribution dates and locations. BICA ensures that distribution points are accessible to the most vulnerable including women and persons with disabilities. BICA will also keep consulting with OCHA and WASH Sector on monthly basis about the implementation and monitoring of action plan. In addition, contractors, service providers and suppliers may do not complete required activities due to fluctuations in security situation.
