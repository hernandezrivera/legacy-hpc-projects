Project Summary

The effects of pandemic have deepened vulnerabilities for the most marginalized around us and children happen to be one of those segments. Pandemic driven lockdowns have either reduced or cut off children’s access to schools, recreational activities, their friends and psycho-social support services around the world. In the face of Covid-19, UNICEF has warned against the continued risk to the well-being of children due to school closure. Similarly, the prevalence of GBV during Covid-19 has increased manifold and has put women at highest risks of suffering and mental stress arising out of longer hours of unpaid care, losing their income, domestic violence, the fear of Covid-19 infection, among other factors, has added to significant mental stress, anxiety and depression among women and girls. The proposed intervention therefore is conceptualized to promote protection and safer environment for children (girls and boys) and for women/survivors of gender based violence in ten districts from four provinces of Pakistan having shown high recurrence of child rights violations lately.
Overall goal of the intervention is to create safer and responsive environment for children in Pakistan.
Objectives
1. To provide safe spaces to children where they feel protected
2. To strengthen community to take action against child right violation and gender based violence
3. To enhance capacity of duty bearers for improved child protection mechanism
Organization will adopt the mobilization, capacity building and advocacy strategies to achieve the objectives. Providing safe environment for children will be the first preference of intervention. The Child Friendly Spaces (CFSs) will be formed in all target areas. These CFSs play a vital role to give children & women psychosocial support. The CFS will also establish the referral mechanism to support the maximum vulnerable children and women in project area. In 50 Union Councils of 10 districts of Pakistan, safer atmosphere for children & others prone to GBV shall be promoted through establishment of 50 Child Friendly Spaces (CFSs). Approximately 100,000 children & 5,000 women will be directly benefited. The CFSs will be run by trained well orient staff. The intervention will also build capacity of the areas active people on the children issues and GBV. Approximately benefit 100,000 children (50% girls and 50% boys) and 5000 women vulnerable to abuse and GBV will be benefited from these centers. Additionally, children and women attending the CFSs shall also be oriented upon safe behavior related to Covid-19 while SOPs on protection from Covid-19 shall be strictly maintained and advocated inside the CPCs, within the community and dealing with all stakeholders. For creating a sense of ownership and engagement among the communities for prevention and reporting, group Community Monitoring Committee (CMC) will be formed to monitor and report the child sexual, physical or mental abuse and GBV cases. For this purpose, a mobile application will be developed to record the case in time. The affected women and children will be support through referral mechanism. Different groups of society will be oriented to impact of child abuse and GBV. The CMC shall be set up in each community where there is a CFS. The Communities will be fully engaged through a string of activities for greater awareness on child protection issues and basics of GBV. Behavior change communication material shall be developed in national and local languages for wider dissemination. The orientation and advocacy on the issues shall also be promoted through observing international days (20 November) and International Day of the Girl Child (11 October). Trainings on GBV and Child Protection with duty bearers will be held. Equal gender representation in staffing and committees shall be ensured and cultural sensitivities in engaging the communities shall be adopted. Gender, environment and inclusion are cross cutting issues and will be addressed.

1- Project Activities

1- Staff hiring: A total of 217 experienced staff will be hired 2- Core Staff Orientation Workshop: Two days orientation workshop will be organized at national level bringing together a group of thirty (30) newly recruited staff including District Managers and senior level Provincial Staff with an objective to build an orientation of core staff on the project. The core staff shall subsequently orient their respective staff in the districts. 3-Staff Orientation Workshops: 10 District level two day staff orientation workshops will be held. All District level staff will be oriented on the project and major concepts of child protection and gender based violence. 4-Setting up of 50 Child Friendly Spaces in 10 districts: As one off activity, 50 Child Friendly Spaces (CFSs) in 10 districts across Pakistan shall be set up.5- Operationalization and maintenance of CFSs and related services: CFSs will be made fully functional after staff orientation, recreational activities, psychosocial support, referral mechanism and community engagements6-Formation of 50 Community Monitoring Committees (CMC) to monitor and report child abuse: Community engagements will be ensured by formation of CMCs comprising 10 to 12 persons in order to create awareness among people and to monitor and report the child abuse and GBV cases.7- Ten (10) Two day Training workshops for CMCs: Two day District level training workshops for 30 participants shall be conducted on concepts of Child Rights Convention (CRC), child abuse, prevention and protection, community role in eliminating child abuse, monitoring and reporting of child abuse. 8-Three-hundred (300) Awareness Raising Sessions with Community: A total of 300 awareness raising sessions will be conducted within Community on impact of child mental, physicals and sexual abuse with parents, teachers, shopkeepers, hotel and small scale business owners. These sessions shall be exclusively tailored for the said group to minimize child abuse in schools and workplaces, around 6000 persons will be engaged in these sessions. 9- Three-hundred (300) Awareness Sessions on GBV Prevention, Existing Laws and GBV Addressing Mechanism: Around 6000 persons (men and women) within communities in the target districts will be oriented on GBV and existing laws. 10- Development of a Mobile Application: A mobile phone application shall be developed and deployed in the field to report GBV and Child abuse cases. This application will be introduced in community awareness sessions. The app will help community to report child abuse and GBV incidents, after fact finding by staff, these incidents will be shared with concerned departments for necessary actions. 11- Fifty-five (55) International Children Day Activities: To mark the importance of 11th October the Girl Child Day and 20th November - the International Children Day, fifty-five (55) activities such as walks, press conferences, community level seminars, speech competitions will be conducted in target areas. The children and girl child issues will be highlighted during these activities and messages will be conveyed to approximately 2750 persons directly. 12- Fifteen (15) Exhibitions of Crafts and Paintings Produced by CFSs: The crafts & paintings made by children in CFSs will be exhibited on district and provincial level with an objective to highlight the children skills as well as to assess trough art the emotional and psychological conditions of children in the context of COVID-19.13-Development of Awareness Raising Material: One (1) poster and two (2) booklet will be developed to raise awareness among communities about child rights and existing laws against violence against women and girls. A quarterly newsletter will be published to highlight the CFSs and project activities. 14-Ten (10) Capacity Development Workshops with Duty Bearers and CSOs in each district on GBV and Child Protection mechanism will be held.
