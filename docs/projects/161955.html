Needs

More than 950,000 Rohingya refugees have fled violence in Myanmar and sought safety in Cox's Bazar, Bangladesh. 52% of the refugees are women and there is a high prevalence of GBV including sexual and physical violence, intimate partner violence and emotional abuse in the camps. The fear and reality of abduction, harassment, and sexual violence have severely limited women's mobility and ability to access critical information and other essential GBV services. Sexual violence is leading to unwanted pregnancy, unsafe abortions, stigma in the community, and sexually transmitted infections and HIV. There is a significant gap in 24/7 health services including basic emergency and neonatal care. Other needs include clinical and psychosocial support for survivors of sexual violence and intimate partner violence; unhindered access to basic SRH care such as full range of contraception methods, menstrual regulation, safe delivery, and emergency obstetric and newborn care.

Activities or outputs

"Through health service points (PHCCs, BEmONC centers and IWCs), IRC will provide the following services: health consultations including the provision of triage for mass casualties; emergency resuscitation, stabilisation and referral to secondary care facilities; first aid; treatment for priority communicable diseases including diarrhoea, acute respiratory infections and malaria; emergency deliveries for pregnant women; emergency neonatal care; Clinical Management of Rape; MHPSS; syndromic management of STIs/STDs; Short term and long term Family Planning methods/Contraceptives, Menstrual regulation with medication; Post Abortion Care (PAC). Local level coordination will be done through participation and capacity building of government health officials.The project is targeting beneficiaries as per services provided the project. Where PHCC will be located, the project targets all the population of the camps for the refugee communities. The RH and BEmONC services are targeting adolescent girls as well as women of reproductive age.The targeted PHC, BEmONC services are located in different camps and some are middle of the host and refugee communities, which will be accessible by all age groups, gender categories and refugees and host community members. The PHCs have gender segragated toilets and other serivces in the centres that have privacy and confidentialities to respect the gender norms. The project design is informed by community engagement, collection of feedback, and assessments. The community leaders (majhi), local camp authorities (CIC) and local administration (sub-district and district administration) will be involved throughout the project. The IRC will develop a detailed M&E plan in line with the Logic Model and Performance Measurement Framework of this project. M&E data and information on beneficiaries will be disaggregated (by sex, age and disability and other exclusion factors). The percent of women ages 15 to 49 with a live birth within a given time period who attended antenatal care (ANC) four or more times during their most recent pregnancy and it will be measured through creating unique ID for each patients. Client’s (OPD and IPD patients) voluntary feedback will be collected through bi-monthly random survey (close ended questionnaire) to assess individual satisfaction.Teknaf and Ukhia is naturally prone to cyclones and storm surge and the camp settlements are in hilly jungle, which is highly prone to land slide. IRC will strengthen it’s capacity on DRU (Dispatch and referral Unit) in order to support effective coordination of MMTs across the health sector. DRU will be located at the field level Emergency Operations Centre (EOC) as a means to enable real-time field level response, enabling a rapid response and maximization of available resources. Additionally, IRC will coordination with other sector actors with the cyclone preparedness program; development of security and evacuation plan and suspend operation temporarily in an extreme/forced measure in consultation with the donor. IRC will introduce safe waste management and disposal measures of medical wastes from it’s service points (e.g PHCC, BEmONC, IWC) to mitigate adverse environmental consequences including contamination of surface water sources and that may bring additional health hazard risk to the communities.
