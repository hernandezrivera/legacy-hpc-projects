Project Summary

Facilitating the provision of Quality Essential Primary Health Care Services to IDPs, Returnees and Host Population affected Humanitarian Crises in Northeast Nigeria (Adamawa State), is aimed at supporting primary health care facilities selected LGAs of Adamawa State through the procurement of essential drugs and hospital equipment and distribution and mobile health care services in targeted communities.
LESGO will strives towards ensuring that continues the delivery of essential health care services across the three states. Health sector response always provides services to any population age and demographic group. Health services are provided in different standard packages for diverse age group like under-five children, pregnant and lactating women, older men and women with chronic health conditions. For outbreaks, the response health sector will continue its collaboration with WASH, CCCM, Shelter and other sectors for a more coordinated response for timely containment of an outbreak. For issues related to children suffering from acute malnutrition with medical complications will be coordinated through joint programming with nutrition sector partners. Same joint planning and joint monitoring mechanism will be promoted for integrated programming across the sectors.
Joint Health and WASH response to an AWD/cholera outbreak will be strengthened in line with the Joint Operation Framework (JOF) in the state. Collaboration with WASH sector will contribute to contain the outbreak through water chlorination community awareness, the establishment of ORS points and behavior change communication on the use of latrines and prevention of open defecation in camps and host communities.
The provision of life-saving and life-sustaining health services will be driven by a combination of minimal and comprehensive packages, including maternal and child health services, sexual and reproductive health services, services for gender-based violence survivors, management of malnutrition with medical complications, management of non-communicable diseases, mental health and psychosocial support and the strengthening of referral services. Working in support of State Ministry of Health in the State, Health Sector partners will enhance surveillance for epidemic-prone diseases by strengthening and expanding the Early Warning and Response Surveillance System (EWARS). The epidemic outbreak preparedness, prevention and response capacities will be further strengthened for timely rapid response to avoid any major public health threats of disease outbreak. The sector’s Sexual and Reproductive Health (SRH) Working Group will coordinate partners to ensure that SRH related needs such as maternal and neonatal health (including basic emergency obstetric care), HIV and GBV-related health issues are addressed in a timely manner. Health partners will work through the Health Rapid Response Team to provide a standard package of quick response support and supplies to populations affected by a disease outbreak, natural disaster (mainly floods) or medium to large-scale population movement (both IDPs and returnees) in areas where partners are not present or where the needs exceed existing capacities.
Health sector response will look more for health system recovery activities in LGAs where the security situation is stable while areas with more IDPs presence the humanitarian health response will continue. The priorities will be the mix of humanitarian and developmental needs to ensure access to quality health services in a sustainable manner.
