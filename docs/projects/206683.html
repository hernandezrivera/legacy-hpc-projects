Project Summary

This project seeks to ensure that targeted conflict affected populations of the North-West and South-West Regions of Cameroon benefit from various Protection and response services/assistance from our Protection team.
During the implementation of this project, COHESODEC will be establishing and supporting 40 community based complaints and feedback mechanisms and will as well sensitize 2,000 IDPS, returnees and host communities on peace building, peaceful coexistence and conflict resolutions and social cohesion.
Moreover, this project will be providing targeted protection assistance to 250 of the most vulnerable people (persons living with disabilities to be included in social and economic support to livelihood through a productive economic inclusion program for persons living with disabilities including training and offering conditional cash transfers.
The project shall also be providing legal assistance to some 300 crisis-affected people on the protection of their rights through sensitization sessions focused on their rights and obligations. Furthermore, COHESODEC shall be conducting protection monitoring activities in crisis-affected areas, ensuring that 15,000 persons of concern covered by protection monitoring activities, ensuring that 400 protection incidents are reported through protection monitoring and that at least 300 referrals to specialized services are made.
Finally, COHESODEC shall be strengthening the process of issuance of civil documentation to some 1,000 conflict-affected people ensuring that they benefit from civil or identity documentation support and 25 awareness-raising sessions on civil documentation as well as ensuring that 800 conflict affected persons benefit from targeted protection assistance. This project will most importantly be advocating for people living with disabilities to be included in national programs through conducting 3 training sessions involving civil society organizations working in the domain of disability inclusion based on the IASC guidelines.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

In the implementation of this project, COHESODEC will require funds and will be working in close collaboration with other protection stakeholders such as: Community leaders, Religious leaders & Influential community members alongside its staff team. The organization will set up a 24/7 call-in lines and suggestion boxes to ensure adequate support to the community based complaints and feedback mechanisms. Moreover, COHESODEC will support targeted protection assistance to the most vulnerable people (persons living with disabilities in the form of social and economic support to livelihood through a productive economic inclusion program for persons living with disabilities including training and offering conditional cash transfers. Furthermore, the project shall ensure the provision of legal assistance to crisis-affected people on the protection of their rights through sensitization sessions and ensuring they effectively receive legal assistance. Also, the project will conduct protection monitoring activities in crisis-affected areas in targeted vulnerable communities. We shall ensure the provision of targeted protection assistance to the most vulnerable people (men, women, boys and girls, PWD). Finally, COHESODEC in the implementation of this project will solicit the services of high courts, Police Services and local councils to ensure the issuance of civil documentations to conflict-affected people. Equally, COHESODEC will be running community radio programs using the most widely listened community radios as well as producing hand-outs, brochures and posters and carrying out awareness raising campaigns on the importance of civil documentation as well as sensitize IDPS, returnees and host communities on peace building, peaceful coexistence and conflict resolutions.
