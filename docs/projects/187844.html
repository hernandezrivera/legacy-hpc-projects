Project Summary

The livestock sector is an important sector in the region because it is one of the most important pillars of food security in the region. Poultry is one of the branches of this sector, poultries have effected by the wars that took place in the region. The instability that prevailed and the closure of roads and crossings led to a significant increase in the prices of fodder, vaccines, power and other requirements of production, thus causing great losses in this sector and a very high rise in meat prices. 55% of the poultry houses are existing in Raqqa compared to Deir Ez-Zor and Al-Hasakah governorates. During the war, many poultries farms were exposed to shells, explosions or stealing tools and equipment in some poultries, which caused partial or complete destruction in some cases, which caused them to leave the market, and consequently a decrease in the number of operating poultries farm in the region. This caused high increasing in prices in north east of Syria due to big gap between great demand and the lack of supply of chicken meat, especially with the high number of IDPs and population. Therefore, we believe that rehabilitating poultry houses and providing appropriate support to their owners to reactivate them will contribute to decrease the rise in prices and will enhance opportunities to support food security in the region In addition to contributing to providing more job opportunities and reducing unemployment rates. We think that Providing the necessary and qualitative support to the poultry, such as rehabilitating the damaged poultry, providing the necessary work equipment, and providing chicks to the poultry that stopped working due to the financial deficit, vaccines, medicines, and fodder, will contribute to increasing production capacity and improving the labor market. Nawat conducted two project to support poultry sector supported by ALSP. Last years a new problem caused a great suffering for poultries owners, it is lack of electricity. Euphrates dam is the alone source of electricity in Raqqa, Turkey decreased amount of water of Euphrates River, which comes to Syria; this caused very lack of electricity.so that people depend on generators to get electricity which caused great rise of prices for everything. breeders have forced to use generators, they are suffering from the very expensive cost of fuel and maintenance. Breeders requested to install solar energy because the power outages caused losses and suffocation for chicks due to lack of ventilation or respiratory diseases due to cold and sudden temperature drop. Solar energy will enable them solve thier proplems and sell their chickens in lower prices. Due to the interventions of Nawat, the number of registered poultries increased from 89 to 147, this will contribute in closing gap between demand and supply and will contribute in stopping rising of prices.
Project overall goal:
Providing comprehensive support on livelihood activities for families affected by crises in Raqqa governorate and supporting the revival of professions and businesses in order to achieve sustainable livelihoods for families and communities, achieve economic and social recovery and support food security.
The objectives:
1- Expand the use of solar energy in agricultural sector.
2- Preserving environment through decreasing the use of fuel.
3- Providing job opportunities in the region because of the increase in operating poultry farms, which were stopped because of power.
4- Rehabilitation of poultry farms to raise their production capacity.
5- Creating a kind of economic competition that will contribute to the development of the poultry sector.
6- Providing job opportunities in the region because of the increase in operating poultry houses and the increase in production quantities.
7- Raising the knowledge level of the beneficiaries by providing them with the appropriate veterinary guidance to increase their productive efficiency.
8- Breaking the monopoly and reducing prices.

4 - Needs

Energy in all its forms is the main demand of the population in recent years, 80% of the population in Raqqa and northeastern Syria works in agriculture, where they need a great deal of energy for their work. Due to the crisis in Syria, the demand for energy has increased and its prices have doubled due to the war. ALL breeders use Fossil fuels in their work, but all poultry farmers suffer greatly from the cost of fuel. Through the projects implemented by the Nawat organization, several meetings were held on the energy problem with the head of the Poultry and Breeders Association, where there are great difficulties in securing diesel and coal as a result of their high prices. Nawat organization also met with representatives of livestock in the local authority who showed their inability to provide fuel. One poultry farm needs about 2500 liters of fuel per month . Breeders get part of the fuel at a reduced price, but this quantity is not enough, so they have to buy the rest of the quantities at high prices, and this leads to a doubling of prices products. Solar energy is the ideal solution for them to secure stable energy sources for them, and thus stability of production and prices. IN this project and through and through 2022/2023,Nawat plans to targeted 150 poultries farm and support them with required items submit to them

5 - Implementation of activities

1- Survey of targeting areas and identification of poultry houses that are working and other that stopped working. 2- Determining the needs for the rehabilitation of each poultry farm. 3- Preparing the support materials and presenting them to the breeders, such as feeders, drinkers, heaters, tanks, spray system and solar energy 4- Follow-up rehabilitation for poultry that need it. 5- Providing the necessary consultations that serve poultry houses. 6- submit requested reports.
