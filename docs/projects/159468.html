Project Summary

The protracted military conflict in eastern Ukraine continues to have a negative impact on the quality and access to education at NGCA. The most vulnerable are education facilities located at NGCA and along the contact line. Many of the schools lo-cated in these locations were partially or complete-ly destroyed. Renewed hostilities often disrupt the learning process, which has to be suspended to ensure the safety of students and teachers.
According to the de facto authorities (information provided by the Ministry of Education), 434 educa-tion facilities located in the territory of the Donetsk region not controlled by Ukraine need building ma-terials, of which 335 buildings need to be restored to restore heating and water supply systems.
It should be noted that the reduction in funding in the educational sector also provoked the deterio-ration of the vital infrastructure. A prolonged lack of repair led to the failure of water-wire and heat-ing systems, roofing and insulation coatings. As a result of this, many schools and kindergartens do not heat well in winter and cannot maintain suffi-cient temperature conditions in the premises. This leads to the temporary closure of schools and the postponement or extension of the winter holidays during the onset of severe cold weather, which negatively affects the quality of the educational process.
Also, insufficient funding leads to the fact that many education facilities feel the need to update school furniture, sports equipment, textbooks and teaching aids.
The access to quality education and the socio-economic recession, which occurred as a result of the rupture of socio-economic ties between Ukraine and uncontrolled territories, make it diffi-cult. In many households, there is virtually insuffi-cient cash to cover school expenses. Often, the purchase of the necessary stationery is an over-whelming article in the family budget. Most often, such difficulties are experienced by single-parent families and large families, families raising children with special needs.
The project aims to improve access to safe educa-tion facilities that provide quality-training services by carrying out repair and rehabilitation work in education facilities that are directly or indirectly affected by various factors caused by the protract-ed military conflict. In addition, project activities are aimed at maintaining a high-quality educational process through the distribution of personal learn-ing kits and ECD kits, as well as the distribution of school equipment and sports kits.

1 - Needs

In accordance with the Strategy of the Education Cluster, at the beginning of the 2018/2019 academ-ic year, the NGCA of the Donetsk region had 1,151 education facilities (522 schools, 524 kindergartens and 105 vocational schools). 226 education facilities along the contact line and the NGCA need repair and rehabilitation work. Due to the limited access to remote locations, it is impossible to make a reliable assessment at NGCA, therefore we are forced to take into account data from various sources, including information from the de facto authorities and the results of our own monitoring and evaluation. It should be noted that the Fund’s analysts make a survey once every three months in separate locations and conduct system monitoring in the locations where the or-ganization operates. Monitoring is carried out through focus groups, personal questionnaires, expert interviews. Also, information about the needs of the Fund’s specialists comes through the organization’s groups on social networks and through the telephone hotline. Also, if it is neces-sary to conduct monitoring in a remote location, the Fund’s specialists have the skill to collect re-spondents and conduct monitoring via the Inter-net.According to the de facto authorities (information provided by the Ministry of Education), 434 educa-tion facilities (schools, kindergartens and vocational schools) in NGCA need building materials for the repair and rehabilitation, of which 335 education facilities in NGCA need repair and rehabilitation systems water supply, water disposal, heat supply.In addition, 682 education facilities also need to provide and / or update sports equipment.555 education facilities have a need for updating and / or re-equipment of classrooms.

2 - Activities & outcomes

As a result of the project activity, it is planned to implement the following activities and achieve the following results:1. Repair and rehabilitation of education facilities affected by the conflict. Reconstruction of educa-tion facilities in connection with a conflict may in-clude schools directly damaged as a result of shelling or hostilities, as well as restoration of school infrastructure to reduce the existing risks associated with conflicts and leveling the indirect impact of the conflict on education facilities through the destruction of key infrastructure (wa-ter supply, heating, electricity, etc.) and economic recession in areas affected by the conflict2. Purchase and distribution of equipment be-tween preschool education facilities directly or in-directly affected by the conflict3. The purchase and distribution of Sport kits be-tween education facilities directly or indirectly af-fected by the conflict.4. Purchase and distribution of of personal learning kits. This activity will be aimed at children with spe-cial needs.5. Purchase and distribution of ECD kit.In order to provide a full cycle of assistance to edu-cational facilities, the provision of sports kits can be distributed in the same facilities in which repair and rehabilitation work was carried out (provided that the existing needs for such assistance are con-firmed). Due to restrictions for certain types of activities at NGCA, namely the inability to accredit programs containing a soft component (building the capacity of teachers and parents, supporting distance learn-ing, providing psycho-social support), this project proposal contains only 2 components. In the event of a change in the situation, the fund team is ready to begin the implementation of activities that will achieve other goals of the Education Cluster.
