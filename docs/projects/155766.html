Project Summary

Inclusive humanitarian support to the conflict affected populations at places of return in North Waziristan, Khyber and Orakzai Districts, through integrated livelihood interventions, with an effective mainstreaming of DRR and protection.
The livelihood strategy of IR is anchored on agriculture start up support (crop inputs), livestock packaging, rehabilitation of agriculture infrastructure and off-farm livelihood support through the provision of cash grants and imparting livelihood skills among youth, disable and female entrepreneurs. As important component of organizational approach, IR will facilitate strong linkages with line department and district administrations for technical backstopping and leaving strong bonds for imparting a sustainable exit strategy.

Needs

The complex emergency in the northern parts of Pakistan (Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and tribal belt) surpasses 10 years in conflict since 2008. Local residents from the tribal districts were displaced in different parts of the country due to security operations and resided in designated camps and off camp setting mainly in the settled districts of KP. As per updated factsheet of FDMA and UNHCR, there were 471,126 families displaced from different geographical locations of the tribal belt since 2010, where 97% of these displaced families were returned to their place of origin till October 2018. However, the numbers of displaced families might be much more if the un-registered families were counted which for some administrative constraints couldn’t able to register themselves (40%). The return happened mainly to North and South Waziristan, Khyber, Orakzai and Kurram districts during 2017-18, while the process was completed in the rest of tribal districts. The socio economic profile of the region remain unstable throughout the conflict period; household vulnerabilities multiplied manifold and livelihood patterns were largely disrupted during the displacement period. Public sector institutions and humanitarian community emphasized every possible effort to make the repatriation and return process sustainable. The economic conditions of returnee population is not qualified enough to sustain their livelihoods, especially where the basic services and amenities in the return areas need extensive rehabilitation. The in-depth FSL assessment in 2017 revealed that the prevalence of food insecurity is yet 24% among returnee population, where the incidence is much higher in North Waziristan and Orakzai districts. Over 11% of the people yet adopting emergency irreversible strategies in their routine chores. The household expense is over 30% higher than the income level. The vulnerability level is much higher among non-registered TDPs, especially the female headed households and families with disabilities. IRP has refreshed a rapid assessment round in October-November 2018 in Khyber, Orakzai and NW districts, the findings are not far different from the assessment rounds of 2017. The assessment also revealed that 14% of households in the community is headed by female and 15% of households is headed by an elderly person (above 60 years) along with quarter of all households has at least one person with a disability. Study result reflected that 69% families have not enough money to buy food in the market. Livelihood pattern has been reportedly changed in displacement and thereafter in return from productive livelihood sectors (9% reduction in farming) to occasional unskilled labor (26% increased) for sustenance. Lack of resources and limited family income were quoted the main reason behind reduction in agriculture activities. Though the livestock keeping intensity is reasonable at the HH level, however livestock needs are more prevalent (40% quoted feed and 25% medication) in the return areas. The average household income is between 9000 - 15000 PKR however average income decreased by 40% due to limited livelihood opportunities in the places of return. The percentage of women in income generation activities is restricted to home boundaries, only 4% respondents have reported affirmatively in this category. The food security assessment of 2017 and the findings of Islamic Relief in the 3 districts outline an uninterrupted and immediate food security and livelihood support to returnee population in North Waziristan and Orakzai districts while emphasizing medium term support in Khyber community in addressing their chronic and structural issues of rehabilitation. Islamic Relief is planning to target its livelihood strategy in Khyber (40%), North Waziristan (40%) and Orakzai District (20%) accordingly during 2019. Islamic Relief is already present in Khyber and North Waziristan while expanding its operation in Upper Orakzai area.
