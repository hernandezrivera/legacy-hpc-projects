Project Summary

Livestock is one of the most important sources to support a balance of food security, one of the most important reasons for our interest in this issue is to maintain and support the food security sector, the special reasons are: the lack of veterinary clinics and the absence of special vaccines for these animals. Some animals must take 6 vaccines each year, and therefore a large number of animals died due to the lack of vaccines and the absence of animal medical centers, and the preservation of human beings, and this is our main goal
Our project is a mobile veterinary clinic equipped with complete supplies that move to the villages and provide vaccines for all types of animals. Under the supervision of specialized and experienced veterinarians, there will be a daily schedule to visit the villages and provide the necessary vaccines. Our project will be one of the most important projects in the region and it will maintain the main food security in Syria and the project will benefit in general from 3,000 families.
Objectives:
1- Contributing to support the food security sector
2- Contributing to improving the livelihood of 3,000 HHs dependent on livestock for their livelihoods
3- Providing vaccines to a large number of animals
4- Maintaining balance in the food security sector
5-The project will contribute to support the food sector in the livestock department significantly
6-The project will benefit 3,000 families, equivalent to 15,000 direct beneficiaries
7-Reducing food insecurity and the food source of animals
8-Contribute to achieving balance for food security in northern Syria
9-Provide vaccines for 400,000 sheep
8,000 cows
and about 80,000 of ducks + 80,000 of chickens + a large number of fish
10-Protecting beneficiaries from eating sick animal meat and reducing disease
11-Contributing to reducing the death rate among animals by providing the necessary vaccines
