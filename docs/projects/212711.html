Project Summary

Muzun for Humanitarian and Development has developed this project to strengthen community-based child protection services by providing operational support to established child-friendly spaces, supporting mobile outreach teams delivering PSS services, and conducting case management activities. The second objective is to strengthen the capacity to respond to child protection concerns by focusing on training caseworkers, caregivers, and teachers. The project will benefit 2500 people, including girls, boys, women, and men over 11 months, children aged 5-17, and women and girls of all ages are the primary targets of this program, with a focus on the most vulnerable and at-risk individuals.
This proposed project will tackle child protection concerns in several communities in northern Syria, including Azaz and Salama in Aleppo Governorate, and Ariba, Kafr Jales, and Kelly in Idleb Governorate.
The approach includes a comprehensive set of activities focused on child protection:
Establish a child-friendly space, offering PSS sessions to 1250 children aged 5 to 17, extending support to 100 children via case management services, conducting a Child Protection Awareness Raising Campaign, and providing case management training for case workers.
Conducting a Child Protection Awareness Raising Campaign: Trained outreach workers will regularly conduct awareness sessions in child-friendly spaces and outreach locations. The sessions will be tailored to address specific child protection concerns and will adhere to established child protection guidelines. The target audience includes 550 men, 700 women, 550 boys, and 700 girls.
Establishment and Empowerment of Community-Based Child Protection Committees: Collaboration with local authorities, community leaders, and stakeholders will help identify and engage community members to form child protection committees. These committees will receive comprehensive training on child protection laws, recognizing signs of abuse and neglect, reporting procedures, and effective communication with support services.
Enhancing Parenting Skills: Awareness-raising sessions on positive parenting and child protection will be provided to parents and caregivers of school-age children. The target is to reach 400 men and 600 women.
Providing Specialized Child Protection Services: A trained caseworker will identify and register at-risk children using child-friendly spaces, mobile psychosocial support, and awareness-raising activities. They will conduct assessments and develop individualized case plans based on each child's specific needs and risks. This activity aims to target 300 children, with 150 boys and 150 girls.
Provision of Psychosocial Support (PSS) Activities: Non-structured recreational activities will be organized to support the psychosocial well-being of girls, boys, and children with disabilities. Child-friendly safe spaces will be created in Azaz in Al Harameen Camps.
Comprehensive Training Sessions for CP Staff and Teachers: 25 Capacity-building training sessions will be conducted to enhance the knowledge and skills of child protection staff and teachers. The training will cover various topics such as identification and referral procedures, child protection in emergencies, child safeguarding practices, gender-based violence prevention and response, and mental health detection and referral.
Through these activities, the project aims to achieve positive changes by improving awareness of child protection issues, establishing community-based protection mechanisms, providing psychosocial support, delivering specialized services for at-risk children, and enhancing parenting skills. The activities align with global standards and principles for child protection and aim to address the underlying causes of child protection issues in the targeted locations.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The proposed project includes activities to address gender disparities and promote gender equality. It focuses on enhancing the parenting skills of both male and female parents/caregivers, promoting positive parenting practices, and improving parent-child relationships. The provision of specialized child protection services takes into account the specific vulnerabilities and risks faced by both boys and girls, ensuring equitable access to support and protection. The designed project focuses on equity by targeting vulnerable and at-risk children, including unaccompanied and separated children, children with disabilities, and those at risk of or experiencing psychosocial distress, violence, abuse, neglect, and exploitation. The proposed project demonstrates a commitment to sustainability by focusing on capacity development, including staff training, technical assistance, and support to strengthen programmatic and financial management. The program aims to ensure the sustainability of its interventions beyond the program duration by building the capacity of local stakeholders and community members. Furthermore, the emphasis on awareness-raising and training sessions for parents/caregivers and community members contributes to the sustainability of positive parenting practices and child protection efforts within the community.
