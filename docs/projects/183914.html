Project Summary

The proposed actions have been designed as a part of an overall Integrated Program
aiming to respond to WASH, Health and Protection needs for the Southern and Western of
Libya, Sebha, Murzuq, Ghat, Ubari, Aljfara, Azzawya and Tripoli mantikas.
- The health component will be focusing mainly on the rehabilitation of primary and
secondary public health facilities by upgrading their basic services through the supply of
the needed equipment, commodities and/or refurbishment. The support provided will
strengthen the capacity of the health care facility staff through the delivery of personnel
capacity trainings and workshops for e􀂨cient and inclusive management, operation and
maintenance of the upgraded facilities. The project will highlight the inclusion of the most
vulnerable groups in the areas such as IDPs, Refugees, Migrants, women and children, by
facilitating their accessibility to the facilities and include staff training related to GBV and
protection. Furthermore, awareness campaigns will be conducted among the targeted
local community populations on inclusive access to basic services and key protection
information regarding communicable diseases like the COVID 19 as well as Non-
Communicable diseases. The awareness campaigns that are to be conducted will not
cover the health staff but will target 4000 community bene􀂦ciaries with health-related
knowledge, however they are not covered by any indicator in the Project Module Platform
- The WASH component (separated HRP proposal submitted to the WASH Sector) will
ensure safe and digni􀂦ed access to water and sanitation through the rehabilitation of the
WASH facilities and infrastructures in targeted urban communities, focusing as well on
providing children and all people with COVID-19 assistance (regular disinfection, regular
provision of PPE to medical staff, awareness raising campaigns).
- The protection components (separated HRP proposal submitted to the Protection Sector)
integrated into above responses will enhance protection through safe identi􀂦cation and
referral of people in need of specialized protection and multi-sectoral services, based on
outreach to individuals, households and community levels focusing on vulnerable groups
such as children, women, PWSN.
