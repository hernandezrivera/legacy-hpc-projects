Project Summary

IhsanRD is dedicated to a comprehensive program targeting the Shelter/NFI and Protection (HLP) sectors in the earthquake-impacted regions of NW Syria, focusing on the sub-districts of Armanaz, Salqin, Jisr-Ash-Shugur, and Jandairis. These sub-districts exhibit unique demographics and pressing needs, necessitating a multi-faceted response strategy that harmonizes these three sectors.
Our approach integrates shelter rehabilitation, MPC for winterization, and protection activities. We plan to support 500 HHs (2500 individuals) in Jisr-Ash-Shugur and Armanaz sub-districts to rehabilitate their moderately damaged homes. Simultaneously, we will provide winterization support to 1,100 HHs (5500 individuals) to ensure liveability during the rehabilitation process and the harsh winter. This aid targets larger families with four or more children and households with no income or earning less than $20 per month, considering the financial strain and the prevalence of poverty in these sub-districts. These households will receive multi-purpose cash for winterization of 150 USD per HH in three-rounds from Nov 2023 to Jan 2024. The intervention will occur in Salqin sub-district and will be determined before the winter campaign and after coordination with the SNFI cluster and other humanitarian actors to avoid any potential overlap.
Intersecting with our Shelter/NFI objectives, our HLP initiatives aim to fortify these sub-districts' resilience targeting 16,150 BNFs through (12,000 legal awareness sessions, 1,000 awareness raising campaigns, 1,200 legal consultation and 450 cash vouchers for legal protection and 1500 on HLP documentation safeguarding). In addition to 800 referrals to legal services, legal audits, establishment and support 2 legal networks, 250 cash voucher protection assistance, eviction support activity including recreational and PFA to 280 BNFs and establishing 2 protection committees.
Our focus recognizes the distinct vulnerabilities of certain groups. With female-led households making up 5% and child-headed households comprising about 2% in the sub-districts, we will strive to ensure these households have access to necessary resources and legal / protection support and are included in the shelter rehabilitation process.
All our interventions are built on a thorough needs assessment – Annex 1, allowing us to address the unique protection and legal needs of women, men, boys, girls, and persons with disabilities effectively. Our harmonized approach encapsulates our commitment to a comprehensive solution, ensuring resources are effectively utilized, and sub-districts are served holistically.
Moreover, humanitarian recipients will be selected based on specific eligibility and vulnerability criteria. BNFs Selection Criteria. Attached (Annex 2), considering the specific needs of PWD and the Elderly.
In doing so, IhsanRD remains committed to fostering lasting change in these sub-districts by responding to their distinctive needs with transparency, accountability, and efficiency, always in line with donor preferences.
