Project Summary

Improving the resilience of the population affected by the conflict in the East of Ukraine through the organization of agricultural coworking for self-employment of the needy population and assistance in improving the technical component of the agricultural segment.
Organization and conduction of training in agricultural technologies: vegetable growing, horticulture, cattle breeding, beekeeping, processing of raw materials. Providing legal advice for course participants.
Provision of agricultural equipment to support vulnerable households affected by conflict.

1 - Needs

The Donbass region has always had a high potential in the agricultural sector due to the quality of its soils. Chernozem soils provide an opportunity to provide food for population at affordable prices, or to attract the population to work in the food sector, allowing them to become self-sufficient. According to our sociological data, 23% of families do not have enough money for basic needs, including food and medicine, 21% can satisfy their needs for food and medicine, but can no longer buy clothes, 48% are not able to make savings. 9% of respondents are forced to sell their previously accumulated property, 7% - to reduce basic health care costs. Thus, even outside the target group of the project, among the affected population, including those without additional vulnerabilities, except for the place of residence, 44% of the economic situation can be described as critical, and 92% - as economically vulnerable. 22% of households do not have a job as a source of income for any of their family members, another 29% have unstable sources of income and have to constantly look for normal jobs while working. The situation in rural areas is aggravated by the reduced mobility of respondents (who have significantly fewer opportunities to simply take and temporarily leave a private house, which requires constant protection and attention, unlike an apartment) and their isolation from more mobile and large-scale urban labor markets. On the other hand, it is life on earth that allows these people to have sustainable opportunities for self-sufficiency, if they would be provided with the necessary equipment and competencies for the development of their subsistence economy, which this project aims to solve.Within the framework of the project it is planned to give the needy the necessary knowledge and skills, to analyze the history of successful projects. The training process will affect the areas of agricultural technologies: vegetable growing, horticulture, cattle breeding, beekeeping, processing of raw materials; as well as teaching to serve and sell their products profitably by mastering the disciplines of advertising and marketing. We will analyze the models of farming construction that use hydroponic plants, pasteurizers, oil presses, butter churn, incubators, cheese making devices, granulators, brooders, the sheep’s clippers etc, with a visual demonstration of this equipment. It is especially important to give to needy the possibility of situational use of equipment: the ability to use the free Internet, computer and printer, designer services, Internet telephony to access marketing efforts, purchases in the foreign market, etc.The project also provides legal support to the self-employed. In addition, the coworking space can be used by third parties to engage the beneficiaries in their projects, allowing them to find a job that will meet their needs. The coworking will support the self-employed at the stage of implementation of their business plans, and one of the most important areas of work will be the analysis of their success stories.According to the interim results of the coworking activity, small peasant farms with the most socially vulnerable owners (living in the zone of 20 km from the contact line, with disabilities, elderly dependents and other risk factors) will receive support in the form of equipment. Such an approach can motivate beneficiaries to participate in the program, increasing its effectiveness.The scheme of a joint equipped workspace, with the possibility of gratuitous use of various small-scale equipment in demand in agriculture, is provided due to the episodic nature of these works (for example, devices for occasional cooking of cheese, incubators and brooders for growing poultry or chainsaws, for cutting dry trees, followed by their use for firewood) and opportunities with less resources, to provide an opportunity for life support for more The number of people.

2 - Activities & outcomes

1.The following activities are planned as part of the training component: 1. Conducting research to identify the most priority areas for the formation of training courses, as well as persons interested in participating in the program. 2. Development of methodical materials, formation of plans and schedules of courses (vegetable growing, gardening, cattle breeding, beekeeping, processing of raw materials).3. Announcement of the information campaign on the start of agricultural training courses. 4. Drawing up groups of people willing to participate and belonging to vulnerable categories of citizens affected by the conflict. 5. The theoretical component of the course, during which at least 200 people will be trained (21 hours). 6. Practical component of the course (at least 8 hours of practical training on the provided equipment).2. As part of the agricultural coworking component it is planned: 2.1. Conducting research to identify areas for the organization of agricultural coworking, as well as persons interested to participate in the program. 2.2. Selection of specialists, suppliers and partners of the program, conclusion of contracts. Formation of the list of equipment, purchase and installation of equipment. 2.3. Work of agricultural coworking daily from 9 to 18, the services of which will be able to use at least 100 people (50 men and 50 women). Beneficiaries will also have the opportunity to make use of computer equipment, Internet access for training, meeting space, processing equipment, which is freely.2.4. Consultation of beneficiaries by specialized specialists on work with the equipment, safety control. Publication on the website of the organization of the photo report and history of the project.3. As part of the agricultural equipment transfer component, the following activities are planned to support vulnerable households:3.1. Selection of program participants, verification of their needs. At least 500 households (including those trained) will participate in the program. 3.2.Drawing up the list of necessary goods for ensuring agricultural activity of the population, purchase of goods (greenhouses, beehives, seeds, forages, pasteurizers, incubators and others). One case will be $ 500.3.3. Safety briefing before transfer of equipment of greenhouses, refrigerators, pasteurizers, incubators. Equipment transfer.

3 - Indicators & targets

Reducing the risks to the population due to food shortages in areas located in the zone 0-20 km from the contact line and affected by the conflict, strengthening the material situation to provide themselves and their families with food. Objective 1. Conducting courses on agricultural training in order to improve the financial situation of vulnerable groups of people, as well as to increase the quality indicator of agricultural direction. It is planned to involve 200 people, 107 of whom are women, 93 – men (including 18-59-114, elderly 59 – 86 people) to participate in training in the agricultural sphere. Objective 2. Implementation of agricultural coworking, during which 100 people will be involved, 50 of them-women, 50-men (18-59-56 people, elderly 59 - 44 people.) Objective 3. It is planned to transfer the necessary agricultural equipment to 500 households with at least 1000 inhabitants, of which 560 are women and 440 are men (18-59-570 people, elderly 59 - 430 people). Consultations for working with equipment: 200 beneficiaries, 125 -women, 75-men (18-59-115 people, elderly 59 - 85 people.) Beneficiaries are involved in the project at every stage: at the stage of project development, when the donor provides this opportunity, surveys and analysis of the needs of target groups are conducted in accessible ways, project activities are formed based on specific needs and aimed at solving specific problems. At the stage of project implementation, during verification and monitoring with a wide range of different forms of feedback, the need to adjust activities to achieve the goals is identified, at the stage of post-monitoring, satisfaction with the results of the project is determined, for planning further activities.The organization has mechanisms for filing and handling complaints in the form of a box in a prominent place in the office of the organization, a postal form on the organization's website and social networks, a telephone hotline, by mail, by e-mail. Also, there is a possibility to address the needs of the organization through various communication channels for individuals and legal entities, from such appeals formed a database of beneficiaries, numbering at the moment more than 3000 people in need, every month the organization receives more than 200 applications and appeals from beneficiaries.Members of local communities participate in the development of social projects, in which DDC participates with the help of its donors. Mechanisms for involving residents in joint humanitarian activities are gradually introduced, for example through the reception and issuance of second-hand, or the involvement of volunteers. Students are actively involved in the work of the organization for training practice, according to the results of which some of them are employed in the structure. Crowdfunding campaigns aimed at solving local problems are carried out in some cases.
