Project Summary

In response to the most pressing needs in the most underserved areas in Syria, several cities and towns will be targeted in Hasakeh, Deir Ezzor, Raqqa, Rural Damascus, and Aleppo governorates with WASH assistance in light maintenance and rehabilitation of water supplies, sewage and solid waste collection and disposal.
The targeted governorates inherently suffer from severe weakness in the delivery of all basic services related to water, sanitation, and solid waste management. With the on- going crisis, depleted assets hindered local authorities to respond to the immediate and humanitarian high needs in this area. Additionally, the communities in the target areas suffer from seasonal outbreak of water borne diseases and Leishmania which put thousands of inhabitants at risk.
Through this project, UNDP will timely intervene to curb the impact of poor services and ensure access to clean affordable water, reduce water related diseases leishmaniasis incident through waters supplies, sewage networks rehabilitation, solid waste collection and removal, insecticide spraying and awareness-raising campaigns to promote the healthy and hygienic environment in the target areas and to help provide decent living conditions for their communities. 3 - Needs

According to HNO 2022 , access to suficient and affordable water, adequate sanitation, solid waste management for affected communities is dire. Furthermore, according to WASH Sector the needs of affected populations of WASH services and maintain WASH facilities and infrastructure, will continue to be high in 2022. Years of crisis andinsuficient funding to respond to WASH needs have had cumulative effects on water, waste management and sanitation systems across the country. As a result, the need for supporting water systems, sewerage and solid waste management systems will continue as a priority, with focus on overburdened communities, areas with high return ratio and areas highly dependent on water trucking. The primary WASH need across Syria remains access to quantity of safe water as a high need. Despite efforts to restore or maintain water systems’ functionality and countrywide support to water treatment processes still up to 42 per cent of surveyed households are relying on alternative and often unsafe water sources to meet or complement their water needs. Moreover, the findings of the rapid assessment conducted by UNDP field team within targeted areas in 5 governorates indicates that there is an urge need to provide wash services in waters supplies and sewage networks rehabilitation and solid waste collection and removal, and awareness- raising campaigns to promote healthy and hygienic environment in target areas and tohelp provide decent living conditions.

4 - Implementation of activities

The project will be implemented under direct supervision of UNDP field staff in targeted areas. preparatory phase to set up project team to implement the required tasks, in parallel verify inputs required to undertake planned activities, then prepare the technical specs and launch the procurement process. Procurement will be undertaken by UNDP. some activities will be implemented by private sector, others will be implemented by local workers mainly from the targeted communities. UNDP will monitor Installation of equipment to ensureefficient operation of the rehabilitated supplies. UNDP will coordinate with local municipalities in targeted areas to complement their work and cover shortage and with all partners in the sector to ensure complementary and synergy of roles needed to maximize the impact of the project and to ensure an integrated package of services to help strengthen the Stability of local communities. technical committees will oversee project implementation, and conduct periodic site visits to track progress against indicators and ensure that it is undertaken as per the work plan and in compliance with required technical specifications. The committee includes representatives from UNDP project management team, local municipalities and local communities. quarterly 4ws will be prepared and shared with WASH sector and OCHA Field Teams in targeted governorate shall conductregular site visits. UNDP Damascus will conduct two field missions to track progress.

Mitigation: Establish strong links with authorities and operators to promote equity; engage community to ensure equal targeting Risk: theft of WASH rehabilitation materials during after installation. Mitigation: agreement with local authorities to sign an o cial receipt to ensure local ownership of materials and rehabilitated facilities Potential exposure to explosive hazard during assessment/ rehabilitation of wastewater infrastructure. 9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Programmatic: Cross-border organizations don’t coordinate activities with sector Mitigation: organizations will be informed through OCHA of the project's target areas and duration. In the case of an intervention by a cross-border organization, the project will move its workers Political: increased violence Mitigation: taking into consideration the criticality of the UNDP intervention in the field, the risk is acceptable. UNDP have a larger footprint in the area to shift resources, implementation set up will be flexible enough to shift geographical focus Operational: in Hasakeh, there is more than one authority on the ground so any unplanned intervention may indicate bias and raise sensitivities among society or dominant political forces Mitigation: a geographical targeting mechanism was developed that considers diversity and balanced representation ,the adoption of precise targeting criteria that emphasize neutral image of interventions Operational: difficulties in obtaining required approvals from local authorities Mitigation: prior communication, consultations and obtaining preliminary approval Adopt clear and balanced geographical targeting criteria as well as accurate selection criteria for the targeted population and to focus on the importance of the project to local communities. Operational: Difficulties in procuring equipment Mitigation: clear and accurate technical specifications on the equipment will contribute to rapid processes
