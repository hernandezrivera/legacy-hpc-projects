Project Summary

The project supports the most vulnerable populations in Idleb and Mahmbal subdistricts.
It aims to enhance the resilience of affected people through integrated early recovery
interventions. 2550 HHs (3875women, 3775 men, 3800 boys and 3820 girls) will benefit
from the activities of this project. This aim will be achieved by supporting the value chain
of wheat in Sahel Al rouge area via light rehabilitation of new 3 wells including connected
irrigation channels in the area in addition to rehabilitation of the roads to cultivable lands
and farms in addition to rehabilitating the roads around the bakeries. Capacity of farmers
will be also enhanced by training on strategic thinking, business management and
marketing, cash for work scheme may enhance a feeling of inequality and discrimination
among different segments of the target communities. In order to avoid an escalation of
any tensions, the project will clearly inform the selection criteria to the target communities,
and let the communities understand that the vulnerable households are supported
through the project, not only land owners. In order to enhance the social cohesion in the
targeted areas, the project will ensure outreaching IDPs, including IDPs who are renting a
land for and who are able to participate in cash for work or families what have persons
with functional difficulties. Women headed households (whether IDP or from the host
community) as priority in the selection criteria as they tend to face limited income
generating opportunities, compared with their male peers. Community will be engaged
throughout the project cycle and HIHFAD will build on the already existing beneficiary
committee in Sahel Al Rouge and will make sure that it includes people from the host
community and the IDPs in addition to women in order to reach all groups in the targeted
area.
HIHFAD will coordinate with both Education Cluster, Field Education Directorate and Local
authorities to select the list of schools which is already running and needs for
rehabilitation and then HIHFAD will select 15 schools, where HIHFAD already did same
activities in 2019, where HIHFAD rehabilitated 5 schools in Afrin Area in collaboration with Education cluster, Field Education Directorate and Local authorities, HIHFAD will use a
field contractors and will enhance the cash for work for IDPs to complete the rehabilitation
of the Schools.

4 - Needs

The ongoing crisis in Syria has severely affected the lives of people, leading to majordisplacement, loss of livelihood opportunities, made people (host community anddisplaced people)depend on negative coping mechanism. According to HNO 2021 morethan 13.4 million are in need of humanitarian assistance. The impact of hostilities in thelast five months is particularly severe on women and children who constitute 76% of thepopulation in north west Syria, of which over 51% are children. REACH intercluster NeedsAssessment reported that the high number of IDP arrivals to assessed communities in north-west Syria within recent months has posed several challenges to available services and livelihood opportunities within the region.Likely due to strains on stable livelihoods, certain protection issues have become more prevalent. Child labourwas reported by KIs in 51% of assessed communities, through which many children are likely exposed to various forms of abuse and exploitation. Many people have no or insuficient incomes to cover their basic needs in Northwest Syria. Moreover, 10% suffer from the physical difficulty of accessing possible jobs due to factors such as damaged roads or a lack of transportation,(REACH Initiative). HIHFAD also conducted a needs assessment in Al Rouge plain in November in order to identify the exact needs of the people and their difficulties. Interviews showed that farmers face various types ofchallenges such as the high harvesting and sowing costs in all assessed villages with70% out of all interviewed farmers, followed by high fertilizer prices with 65% and highseeds prices with 65%. Concerning access to markets, 40 % of the farmers said that theyhave difficulties reaching the markets.

5 - Implementation of activities

HIHFAD filed team will conduct a light rehabilitation of 22 KM of roads to cultivable lands and farms in addition to rehabilitating 3 KM of the roads around the bakeries using a casual labors from both IDPs and locals form the same community to provide a short term job opportunities, all of the suitable jobs for PWD will be dedicated only to PWD during the implementation of this stage. HIHFAD will conduct a package of training for farmers on strategic thinking, small business management and marketing, also HIHFAD will re-contact the local education authorities and Education Cluster to re-update the list of the schools in northern part of Aleppo which need for rehabilitation in addition to keep coordination with UN-Education Cluster, then HIHFAD field team will revisit the 15 schools to put the final study taking in consideration PWD needs in all of these schools (suitable entrance and toilets) for the rehabilitation before tendering "also this activities will be conducted through local suppliers and will use cash for work for the IDPs and locals".

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

In order to support the vulnerable households, the project ensures 10% of the beneficiaries to be female-headed households, via all of the project's contracting phases and will announce for that and share the feedback mechanism in the communities to ensure the non-discriminations, also HIHFAD MEAL team will encourage the beneficiaries to use the feedback mechanism and to keep claiming for their rights and feel free to complain on our serveries and on others as well.HIHFAD organization will implement the accountability pillars in all its programs, which include quality compliance, complaints and feedback mechanism, information sharing, consultation.Based on the fifth criterion in the basic humanitarian standards, The complaints and feedback received through the complaints channels are received and transferred to the relevant department, and the complainant's data are dealt with in complete confidentiality and the complaint is classified based on the classifications approved by the organization and the time required to respond to the complaint.A monthly accountability report is prepared accompanied by statistics that express the numbers of beneficiaries that have been reached, disaggregated by gender.HIHFAD provides all staff with an induction training on the protection principles so they become oriented enough to provide the services in a dignified and safe way for the beneficiaries.The project will pay attentions not to increase child labour and out of school children due to the project.HIHFAD field prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse (PSEA) assistant will ensure the participating beneficiaries are informed PSEA approaches as well as aware of anonymous reporting mechanism.

7.1 - Please list any risks related to sexual exploitation and abuse that have been identified during the project design phase and any risk mitigation and prevention measures that have been adopted and built into the project

Persons with disabilities are at higher risks of neglect and exclusion from the families and the service providers. Women and girls with disabilities are two times at higher risk of neglect and exclusion from the families and the service providers, to compare with their peers without disabilities all of this makes the persons with disabilities in higher risks of SEA . Females with disabilities remain most invisible groups in the Syrian society, they may not leave their houses, and not participating social events in the communities hugely isolated. Thus, the project will ensure outreaching house-bounded females by ensuring appropriate gender balance within the team, so ensure our access to this group. HIHFAD provides all the hired staff an induction training on the protection principles, PSEA and GBV so they become oriented enough to provide the services in a dignified and safe way for the beneficiaries. A focal point for PSEA and GBV would be appointed per area. HIHFAD has a pre-established feedback system and safeguarding reporting channel that supports the ability of the targeted populations to submit their complaints, comments, and suggestions in a beneficiary-friendly and easy-to-access way. The system provides diverse options for the convenience of the beneficiaries to deliver their feedback including WhatsApp hotlines, feedback boxes presented at all HIHFAD supported facilities in addition to the direct feedback

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Regarding the personnel Risk Assessment for the field team and the Management and the Resources for the Risk: 1-IEDs and explosive Devices: this Risk is common all around Idleb and Aleppo Governorate, targeting vehicles and military personnel significantly. Mitigation Measures : -Avoid crowded places. -Avoid peak times for movements. -Avoid military points as possible. -Keep 200 meter as a safety distance from any military cars and suspected cars. -Using the main roads only and after check with HIHFAD security team. -Improved internal information management. -Maps ofthe safe roads (HIHFAD security Team provides them on Daily Bases). -SMP / SOP / CP in place and permanently updated. 2-Problems with /among the Beneficiaries: the probability of this Risk is low , but it could happen at any time. Mitigation Measures : -HIHFAD had a good experience in this kind of projects. -HIHFAD explained the project very well to all beneficiaries in this project. -HIHFAD has well-trained Team on the ground. -people in the area are familiar with kind of projects. -Good relationship with the people on the ground. 3-Robbery of the Staff : according to the security incidents data in the area , the probability of this Risky is very low. Mitigation Measures : -Activities may be split into several small ones.4-Aid Diversion, Cash for Work transfers: the probability of this Risky is moderate.Mitigation Measures : -Select the Cash for Work beneficiaries based on the selection criteria in collaboration with the local entities. -handover the rehabilitated roads and schools to the related local entities based on clear contract and with specific use terms. -pay the wages directly to the workers based on signed timesheets and original IDs.
