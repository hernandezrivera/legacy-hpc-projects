Project Summary

WARDI planned to propose a 12-month quality lifesaving curative nutrition care services aimed at supporting communities in Banadir, Lower Shabelle and Hiraan region. The project intends to conduct facility and community-based screening for severe acute malnutrition for children aged 6-59 months and pregnant and lactating women (PLW) and provide treatment of acute malnourished children 6-59 months (2640 SAM cases) at the outpatient therapeutic center attached to the health facility to curb Malnutrion in the targeted districts. Further, micronutrient supplementation to PLW mothers and children 6-23months for treatment and prevention of malnutrition, Routine indivialual IYCF counselling services will be conducted for PLW mother attending the facility; children aged 6-59 months will be supplemented with vitamin A, capacity building of health facility and community workers(Boys,girls,men and women ) on appropriate IYCF counselling services to mothers attending the facility, conduct training for the facility workers as well as community nutrition workers(50 male and 50 female ) on integrated Management of Acute malnutrition(IMAM) Guidelines to ensure basic nutrition package are provided. WARDI will closely work with other agencies in the region to better coordinate humanitarian interventions and prevent duplication.

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

WARDI will ensure all minority and majority as well as well as disable groups are involved in the implementation of the project and be able to transparent in terms of employment. Community nutrition workers selection and training will also consider gender sensitivity including men and women. All this will ensure gender and equity issues are addressed among the community members. WARDI will collect gender-disaggregated data by sex and age of relevant data related to Nutrition activities to ensure they have equal access to quality nutrition care services. WARDI will ensure that all segments of the affected population including pregnant and lactating women, children aged 6-59 months have equal access to Nutrition assistance. The minority and marginalized communities who are at risk of being neglected/ excluded are also given quality and equal nutrition care services to prevent malnutrition. WARDI will also advocate community participation in the implementation of the program. This will help in getting complaints and feedback responses that enhances effective decision making and promote accountability to the affected population WARDI proposed project is designed in way that prioritizes the rights, safety and dignity of the beneficiaries. The target communities are fully involved and aware of the roles of each stakeholder where each sees the other as partners and equals. The proposed project will not discriminate against any beneficiary and will enable equal and impartial assistance and services for all. Targeting of vulnerable children and adults will be done on basis of their specific needs. The staff will be inducted on their duties and responsibilities and particularly the rights of the beneficiaries to ensure there are no any form of patient exploitation at the facilities and WARDI offices. Watertight mechanisms will be put in place to ensure served populations voices are heard and can file their complaints without being victimized.
