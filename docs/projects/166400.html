Project Summary

An unannounced lockdown is being carried out by the Government of Bangladesh in Cox's Bazar district as well as across the country to curb the spread of the strongly infectious epidemic COVID-19. Since, COVID-19 is a strongly infectious epidemic; the only way to get rid of this disease is to stay at home; avoiding public gatherings as much as possible with maintaining proper personal hygiene. The only goal of this project to aware of beneficiaries on COVID-19 pandemic through proper hygiene promotion so that it could not spread out as well as change their hygiene behavior by providing life-saving measures on COVID-19.

Objectives

To change the Hygiene behavior of both Rohingya and the host community through proper hygiene promotion, distribution of hygiene items as well as personal equipment with special focus on highly contagious diseases.

Needs

Awareness on COVID-19 as well as total hygiene through proper hygiene promotion, hand washing facilities, hygiene kits and disinfection facilities for both Rohingya and the Host community.

Activities or outputs

1. House to House hygiene promotion on COVID-19 pandemic and total hygiene. 2.Installation of hand washing facilities and community level. 3. Distribution of hygiene kits just as: Soap, Mask. 4.Disinfection of every WASH facilities. 5.Regular maintenance of hand washing facilities and others.

Key project/performance indicators

1. Both Rohingya and Host community will be able to protect themselves from COVID-19 and other contagious diseases. 2. Changing the handwashing behavior of Beneficiaries. 3. To reduce contagious disease.
