Project Summary

This project seeks to ensure a better GBV prevention and response through community approaches which creates significant impact in target vulnerable communities. The project will be targeting a total number of 20,184 conflict affected populations in vulnerable target communities affected by the ongoing crisis!
The target divisions involved in the implementation of this project includes Boyo, Bui, Menchum, Mezam, Momo, Manyu and Meme divisions of the North-West and South-West Regions include the following: Fundong, Jakiri, Kumbo, Fungom, Menchum-Valley, Wum, Bafut, Bali, Bamenda II, Bamenda III, Santa, Tubah, Batibo, Mbengwi, Mamfe, Kumba II and Kumba III localities.
In implementing this project, COHESODEC is committed to ensuring the following prevention and response activities:
> Ensure capacity building for 4 women led organizations-(WLO) on GBV prevention, response and coordination in the humanitarian context while ensuring that 20 women are trained from these organizations and making sure that 100% of these WLO, received resources to implement GBV activities.
> Strengthen GBV prevention and response activities at community-level including sensitization of 72 conflict-affected communities, local authorities, traditional and religious leaders making sure that at least 18,960 people are reached-out to during awareness raising and sensitization activities at the community level.
> Conduct 6 safety audits and risk and vulnerability mapping for GBV,
> Strengthen through training the capacities of 67 GBV Service Providers in Case Management including MHPSS,
>Provide holistic support to 684 survivors of gender-based violence to enable them regularly access mental health or psychosocial support services, receive medical support, 29 Post Rape Kits, 69 GBV survivors receive legal assistance and 130 GBV survivors are referred to other service providers for service provision.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

This project will need the services of experts from within and without COHESODEC in the provision of capacity building to women led organizations (WLO) involving GBV response, prevention and coordination in this humanitarian context. This project will also require financial resources to produce sensitization materials and pay for radio airtime with which to strengthen GBV prevention activities at community-level including conflict-affected communities, local authorities, traditional and religious leaders through outreach sessions conducted at community and during awareness raising and sensitization activities at the community level. Furthermore, the project shall require financial resources to conduct 6 safety audits and risk and vulnerability mapping for GBV while also strengthening the capacity of 67 GBV service providers in case management including MHPSS. Finally, COHESODEC in the implementation of this project will require financial resources to provide holistic support to survivors of GBV, ensuring at least 684 GBV survivors access mental health or psychosocial support services, receive medical support services, supported with 29 Post Rape Kits and 69 GBV survivors receive legal assistance while over 130 GBV survivors get referred to other service providers for service provision.
