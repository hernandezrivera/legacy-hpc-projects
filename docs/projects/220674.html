Project Summary

The proposal aims at strengthening the capacities of organizations and communities in anticipatory action and rapid
response. This will be done through enhancing analysis, assessment and coordination capacities of these respondents in
multi hazards crisis affected areas in Colombia. The proposal is aligned with ECHO HIP priorities and prioritized sectors:
protection, health, EiE, and SAN. EVIDEM will provide a collective response, from leading IM and analysis actors
addressing identified gaps in assessment, analysis, and information management (IM).
Through IM service provision, via a humanitarian information support team, iMMAP will provide timely, needs based
support, open to all actors. Drawing from existing data sources, iMMAP Y 3iS will address gaps, in close collaboration with
partners, providing needs-based IM products and services to OCHA, the HCT and NGOs, while empowering and
strengthening information management capabilities at the community level.
IMPACT Initiatives (IMPACT) will provide coordination, information management and assessment support to the
humanitarian community through its inter-agency initiative REACH, with in-country operational support from ACTED.
Within the action, the objective of REACH and its proposed activities is to improve the quality of strategic decision making
within the humanitarian coordination structure, and enhance the programmatic decisions made by the targeted individual
organizations.
ACAPS Colombia will provide independent analysis and technical support to contribute to a strengthened humanitarian
response creating a robust evidence base for strategic, programmatic and operational decision making. ACAPS analysis
products are multi-sectorial in scope, and integrate descriptive, contextual, in-depth anticipatory analysis, using
human-centered approach to understand how diverse population groups in complex environments are affected by multiple
risks.
