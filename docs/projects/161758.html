Project Summary

The proposed intervention as titled above is closely inline with the Early Recovery and Livelihood Sector Strategic Objectives 1: Support Livelihood stabilisation; Sector Strategic Objective 2: Promote inclusiveness & Peaceful co-existence among IDPs, Returnees and host communities.Indicator: Number of people participating in social cohesion or community security related activities(awareness, training etc.).
Number of community reconciliation and Peace-building efforts undertaken to support coexistence in areas of return and Sector Strategic Objective 5: Increased capacity of local partners, communities to identify, analyse and respond to early warning signs of violence conflicts and disaster.
The project proposal aims to provide comprehensive support for GBV Survivors and people at risk in some returned areas of Adamawa and Borno State: Construction of Child Friendly Spaces (CFS) and Safe Space/Houses for Psyco-Social Support Activities.
In line with the Sectors’ objective to promote inclusiveness and peaceful co-existence among IDPs in HC, returnees, and host communities, the proposed CFS /Safe spaces will be set up to support and protect children and adolescents. The objective is to restore a sense of normalcy and continuity to children whose lives had been disrupted by the insurgency. PSS, recreational activities and informal education will be carried out in the centres. Interaction of the IDPs, returnees and host communities will reduce tension and increase togetherness among them while Stigma and discrimination will also be cautioned against. Depression and possible trauma will be identified, adequate PSS provided to Survivors of GBV and to those at risk.
The proposed 'Safe Houses' in Mubi metropolis and Gwoza will enable patronage of people mostly survivors of GBV from other surrounding Local Government Areas like Mubi North, Maiha, Michika and Madagali.
Safety and security of GBV Survivors and those at risk is very paramount hence it is proposed that two of it be constructed in Adamawa and Borno States. Many times Survivors do not have safe house or places to stay which placing them at risk of further harm.
The house is to be constructed, fenced and properly secured to ensure the Survivors are secured from further harm. While there, the necessary PSS, Skill acquisition can be provided and any physical injuries attended to. The House is also a platform to disseminate useful hygiene, health-related talks and other key messages to the inhabitants by skilled and experienced Social Workers.

1 - Needs

The proposed Child Friendly Space (CFS) will be used to provide various Psycho-social Support Services to the GBV Survivors and those at risk. The proposed Safe Houses, when constructed, will give temporary shelter to Survivors of GBV who have problems of shelter and accommodation. While there, they will be protected from further harm, be provided with adequate PSS, acquire skills for economic empowerment, some basic literacy and be mentally stable before being integrated into society.
