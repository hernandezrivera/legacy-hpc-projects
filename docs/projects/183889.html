Project Summary

The project seeks to provide life-saving and resilient in WASH, Education and Protection and FSL targeting 3000 Beneficiaries of returnees, host communities, affected by conflict or recurring natural disasters in three administrations ( Alyaha, Korbia, and Um marahid) of Umbaru locality North Darfur State, , 80% of the populations are vulnerable farmers and shepherd living in extreme poverty, food insecurity or earning their living by informal economy, workers in the fields, grazing and some marginal work. The project will provide lifesaving hand wash facilities, gender-sensitive latrines, and hygiene proportion session and sanitation items for 1800 and Education facilities with improved hygiene practices in times of COVID 19 pandemic, distribution of hygiene kits and (Person Protective Equipment PPE) to education facilities, Conducting Safety Education workshop, children learn to assert their rights, understand and respect their bodies, and stand up for the rights of others. also works with school staff to ensure that schools are safe for both girls and boys and that teachers are equipped to respond in cases of abuse and violence, processors package leaders with reflect mechanism overlapping with Reflect methodology which support the community to study early warning and work together in solve the problems with their own resources. GBV will be addressed by establishment of three temporary women’s safe spaces in selected accessible gathering points based on the consultation with women and girls as well as community leaders with child safe space, to provide different services to 1500 GBV survivors in the three administrations, strengthen the referral pathway and 100 women will be economically empowered in a studied Protection component where FSL vocational training and live stocks will be provided within this component, to mitigate GBV and to sustain food security BPWVO will conduct awareness sessions on gender Based violence and public community awareness campaigns,

6 - Protection mainstreaming
6.1 - Does the project incorporate protection principles and promote meaningful access, safety and dignity for the beneficiaries?

4=Fully in place

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

Through this intervention, BPWO is committed safeguard Right holders from violence and discrimination and is aimed at obtaining full respect for the rights of the individuals. BPWO will implement the project in impartial and non-discriminatory ways that promote the safety, dignity and integrity of the people receiving assistance during assessment, design of project and delivery of services. The project team will ensure all elements of accountability to affected population to be captured through extensive engagement and consultation with the community. Project is designed to inform all displaced about the availability and location of services. The project ensures safe and equal access to inclusive and non-discriminatory provision of services. The intervention will also carefully apply Do No Harm approach to avoid any negative impact that it might bring to the area or the targeted communities. Manage information in a sensitive manner so that the security of informants or others who may be identifiable is not jeopardized. The assistance and protection measures will not undermine local capacities for self-protection as well as support the efforts of the affected population and local communities to find security and restore dignity. Project intends to respond to the root causes of the protection concerns in the area. To achieve this, BPWO embraces community based protection approach, for building capacity of local communities, service providers and government officials. Their capacity will be enhanced in analyzing & finding solutions to protection problems, inclusive of reducing threats& risks, reducing vulnerability of affected population and increasing their capacity to cope with protection problems following exposure to shock and disaster.
