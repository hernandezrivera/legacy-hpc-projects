Project Summary

The present GBVS project targets to support community members in Askira Uba and Damboa LGAs with water supply, sanitation facilities for proper disposal of waste and solid fecal matter, and hygiene promotion. The project will ensure protection of communal water sources through hygiene support and water chlorination, as well as creation of WASHCOMs among community members in Askira Uba and Damboa LGAs of Borno state. The project will also support the provision of real-time data on the mapping and functionality of water and sanitation and water facilities in the LGAs of Damboa and Askira Uba. Through this, the project will facilitate the provision of real-time and verifiable data to aid decisions for the GBVS and other Sector partners including IMC, who serves as cluster lead in Askira Uba and Damboa. Finally, the project shall carry an Anticipatory Action component, where sanitation facilities rehabilitation materials shall be made available given the recent history of extreme floods in Borno, and the real potential negative impacts of climate change, especially as it affects conflict-vulnerable populations in the target LGAs.

1 - Needs

The present GBVS project aims to address critical WASH needs in Askira Uba and Damboa LGAs of Borno State. The specific identified needs include:Access to Clean Water• Communities lack a sustainable and safe supply of drinking water.• Many areas experience water scarcity, with insufficient or deteriorated water sources.Sustainable Sanitation Facilities• Insufficient access to proper sanitation facilities, including showers and latrines.• Widespread open defecation due to lack of sanitary infrastructure and poor knowledge of effects.• Unsafe disposal of human waste leads to contamination of water sources.Hygiene Promotion• Poor hygiene knowledge and practices, resulting in high rates of disease transmission.• Limited awareness and practice of handwashing with soap and water.• Low levels of personal and environmental hygiene awareness in targeted communities.Disease Prevention• High prevalence of waterborne diseases such as cholera, diarrhea, and dysentery.• Need for improved water quality and better sanitation to curb disease spread.Community Participation• Inadequate community involvement in planning and managing WASH infrastructure.• Limited local capacity for sustainable management of water and sanitation facilities.Gender and Social Inclusion• Vulnerable groups (women, children, and persons with disabilities) face barriers in accessing WASH services.• Sanitation facilities are not always inclusive or accessible to all members of the community.Sustainability and Flood Preparedness• Project gains need to be sustained beyond the intervention period.• Communities remain vulnerable to flooding events, highlighting the need for adequate flood preparedness and mitigation strategies.

2 - Activities

The response activities under this project are designed to directly address the identified needs and include:1. Water Supply Activities• Rehabilitation of Existing Water Sources: Repair and upgrade non-functional water points to ensure sustainable access to safe water.• Drilling and Installation of Water Supply Systems:o Install hand pumps, motorized pumps, and gravity-fed systems.• Provision of Water Purification and Treatment Systems: Distribute water purification kits and support water treatment infrastructure.2. Sanitation Activities• Construction of Sanitation Facilities:o Build latrines and showers, including ventilated improved pit latrines (VIPs) and school pour-flush toilets.• Promotion of Healthy Sanitation Practices:o Community sensitization campaigns to eliminate open defecation.• Support Fecal Sludge Management: Partner with UNICEF/MC to ensure effective fecal sludge management systems.• Provision of waste disposal systems and Waste Management: Construction of waste disposal areas, and provision of collection, transportation and disposal mechanisms. 3. Hygiene Promotion Activities• Community-Based Hygiene Education and Awareness: Conduct hygiene promotion sessions to improve practices.• Distribution of Hygiene Kits: Provide kits containing soap, toothbrushes, toothpaste, and menstrual hygiene materials among other Sector Standard kit contents.• Establishment of Handwashing Stations: Set up stations equipped with soap and water in community hotspots.• Sensitization on Food Hygiene: Educate communities on proper food handling and storage techniques and other forms of hygiene promotion.4. Anticipatory Action for Flood Preparedness and Response• Procurement and Pre-Positioning of Materials:o Stockpile water repair materials, hygiene kits, and sanitation supplies in anticipation of potential flooding.• Community Flood Preparedness:o Conduct training and awareness campaigns to prepare for flood-related WASH risks.o Develop emergency response plans in collaboration with local authorities.5. Community Empowerment and Sustainability• Capacity Building: Train community members to manage WASH facilities sustainably.• Community Engagement: Involve communities in project planning and implementation to promote ownership and long-term sustainability.
