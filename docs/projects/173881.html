Project Summary

The proposed project seeks to initiate an intervention to strengthen good hygiene practices to prevent diseases (Cholera) in Girei and Michika LGAs respectively while mainstreaming protection as a sub component of the project to protect women, adolescent girls and children against Gender Based Violence in the target locations.
This project also seeks to establish and strengthen the capacity of hygiene promoters\volunteers in the target communities in order to raise awareness on the need for good hygiene practices. The provision of good hygienic conditions is essential in preventing and protecting human health during all infectious disease outbreak including Covid 19. Therefore good hygiene practices such as maintaining good menstrual hygiene, frequent hand washing with soap, shaving and general good health practices will be promoted. This will help in preventing human to human transmission of pathogens. Therefore the hygiene promoters will be saddled with the responsibility of raising awareness on maintaining good hygiene practices . Consequently sensitization to women and girls on menstrual hygiene management will be crucial to the project.
Furthermore sensitization on GBV prevention, community engagement and capacity building on GBV will be conducted.
The purpose of the proposed intervention is to raise awareness on the need for maintaining good hygiene practices and strengthening of GBV services.
The proposed intervention will target 2000 beneficiaries(women and adolescent girls) in Girei and Michika LGA respectively

1 - Needs

Due to low awareness on personal hygiene in Girei and Michika LGA's, the need to train more hygiene promoters became necessary. Furthermore the covid 19 outbreak in the state has also increased the need for maintaining good personal hygiene . Therefore, the proposed project will address through its activities to raise sustained awareness of the need for good personal hygiene. The project has also included providing PPE's and hygiene promotion kits to be distributed in the target communities. Critical feedback and lessons learnt from previous engagements was; a need to strengthen the capacity of the hygiene promoters in Girei and Michika communities. More so the proposed project mainstreamed protection of women and children against Gender Based Violence this is necessary because of the increase in the number of cases of Gender Based Violence cases in the target location due to the negative effects of Covid 19, this has made life difficult for families with low income therefore parents can no longer afford to provide the basic necessities for their families. Consequently this has resulted in an increase in the cases of domestic violence.

2 - Activities

other necessary convincing detailsTo secure the support of major stakeholders and buy in advocacy and sensitization will be conducted. This activity will be key to community entrance, this will enable the organisation the opportunity to identify major stakeholders who will play a vital role to the project implementation and sustainability. Activity 1- Establishment and training of male and female hygiene promoters in Girei and Michika LGA's respectively- this will be achieved by identifying hygiene promoters and volunteers resident in the community. They will be trained on personal hygiene which includes menstrual hygiene, health talk and mainstreaming of Covid 19 prevention and response. These volunteers will be responsible for carrying out sensitization and focus group discussions on personal hygiene. Activity 2- Hygiene promotion leaflets\ flyers distributionInformation Education and Communication materials will be developed and disseminated during sensitization this will enable beneficiaries access to user friendly awareness guide. Activity 3-Hygiene promotion house to house visitSequel to the training of Hygiene promoters and volunteers, a house to house visit will be conducted to sensitize the households on the importance of maintaining good personal hygiene while distributing IEC materials to sustain the awareness.Activity 4- Hygiene promotion focus group sessionsFocus group discussions proof to be an effective means of passing information to the beneficiaries. in this regard, The hygiene promoters and volunteers will organise a monthly focus group discussions. key stakeholders such as women, adolescent girls, lactating mothers, and children will be in attendance. this will give us the opportunity to collect relevant feedback on the project. Activity 5- Monitoring of hygiene promotion activitiesRoutine monitoring and evaluation will be conducted to collect both qualitative and quantitative data. This will help the organisation to assess the progress of the programme and also identify challenges that might occur during the implementation of the project. Activity 6- Initial hygiene kit distribution Hygiene kits and PPE materials will be procured and distributed in the target community. PPE's will be kept in an identified social places where there constant gathering such as schools, markets and religious placesActivity 7- Post distribution monitoring Post distribution monitoring will be conducted after each distribution activity to ensure compliance with good personal hygiene and covid 19 prevention protocols. Activity 8 sensitization on GBV prevention, community engagement and capacity building in the target communities Activity 9 Training of Non specialised service providers on GBV prevention services
