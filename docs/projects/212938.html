Project Summary

Mercy-USA aims to support the current urgent needs of stabilization in communities that have maintained minimum security stability, and show the potential of progress toward early recovery; to re-activate the livelihood of the vulnerable people and improve the economy for people in Idleb and Aleppo, Mercy-USA seeks through this project to support vulnerable IDPs population and host community members to contribute towards increased resilience of vulnerable households through sustainable and restorative socio-economic interventions, targeting300 beneficiaries (150 males and 150 female, 50% HC & 50% IDPs), as below: Beneficiaries with disability will be encouraged to participate in the project activities based on their activities will be supported in coordination with ERL cluster will target males and females between the ages of 18-50, who are currently residing in the target community and camps supported by Mercy -USA projects. Mercy-USA proposes to shift the current project BNFs who have previous experience in livelihood activities by supporting them to restore their livelihoods. Each HH will receive a start-up value of USD$500 as first installment, then 500$ on two installments separately. (total: $1500).Beneficiaries would begin generating income 4 months from the initial project start and would generate over USD$1500 in an 8-month period.
BNFs will be registered base don eligibility and vulnerability criteria, the focus will be for those who are on the borderline FCS and have medium to high rCSI. The BNFs have to attend technical and management training.
Eligibility Criteria per profession will be:
One of the beneficiaries of the food basket distribution project in the previous and current year.
2. Males and females between the ages of 18-50.
3. Background with food processing, and vocations
4. More than five years experts in the selected vocation.
5. BNF are willing to commit to the conditions and full duration of the program which is more than one month.
6. BNF are not engaged in regular-job (daily or seasonal included).
7. Only one member of each household enrolled at any one time.
8. Trainees who would like to apply for the following professions: blacksmith, sewing, electric maintenance, mobile phone maintenance, carpeting and woodcraft, mechanical maintenance of vehicles and trucks and hairdressing amongst others: Must have a minimum level of knowledge about the mentioned professions and showing documents like certificate or reference from the previous work. Vocational Training Vulnerability criteria (House headed by a woman, HHs with more than five members. Beneficiaries will be both IDPs and host households with 50% each with total 300 persons direct Beneficiaries i.e. 300HH (1500 individuals) will be benefitted indirectly:
• Jandairis sub-district: 150 HH, 750 individuals (Men 105, Women 128, Boys 277, and Girls 240)
• Idleb and Maaret Tamsrin sub-district) 150 HH,750 individuals (Men 105, Women 128,Boys 277, and Girls 240) Beneficiaries with disability will be encouraged to participate in the project activities based on their ability to start their own business, Mercy-USA team will provide technical and management training to all BNFs includes people with disability.
