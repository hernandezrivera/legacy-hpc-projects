Project Summary

The project aims to support the most affected population in northwest Syria with RTE's package, food basket and bread for 5 months. In this context, RTE's package will be distributed for the first 1 month, food basket will be distributed for the next 3 months and bread will be distributed for the whole 5 months. The start date of the project is planned for March 10 and the first distributions will start on April 1. In the interim period, preparations for all activities will be completed. The distribution will be finished at the end of August and the rest 9 days will be closure of the project.
The areas to be covered by the project will include Aleppo>Afrin>Jandairis villages, one of the areas most affected by the earthquakes on and after February 6, 2023.
Beneficiaries will be identified through registration and verification procedures in coordination with local councils. In addition, all lists will be verified by M&E teams by involving communities. depending on specific criteria I added in plan fields , 850 families will be selected.
Food Security and Agriculture/Livelihoods Sector - Humanitarian Response Plan 2023-24 guidance for RTE's package and food basket will be considered and fully implemented. According to that; minimum 2100 kcal per person per day Ready to Eat Rations (RTERs) for 5 days for a family of 5/6 will be provided. In case of households less than 5, kit content will be divided accordingly for a family size larger than 5/6 provided two kits per household. As for food basket, at a minimum 70% of the daily kcal intake which is around 1550 Kcal per person per day in a monthly family food ration. Calculation will be made on a reference family size of 5/6 for a period of 30 days.
RTE's package will be provided by IHH as a contribution to the project.
IHH will provide 156 MT of imported Turkish flour for bread production as a free contribution to the project.
IHH will import an average of 8 MT of flour every week, which will be stored in the bakeries' warehouse.
IHH will produce 1760 bundles of quality bread weighing 800 g each day, which will be distributed to 800 H.H. for 26 days a month for up to 5 months.
Each ton of flour will produce 1440 bread packages.
The bread distribution method will be depending on specific criteria I added in plan fields to cover HHs in the targeted camps based on the following distribution criteria:
HHs of 1-3 persons will receive one bread pack.
HHs with 4-6 persons will receive two bread packs.
HHs with 7-9 persons will receive three bread packs.
HHs with 10 or more individuals will receive four bread packs.
IHH will be responsible for importing, transporting and storing 156 tons of wheat flour and contracting the bakery(s), as well as preparing RTE's package (in-kind) and food basket. IHH will be fully responsible for preparing the beneficiary list and distributing the bread to beneficiaries on a daily basis and monitoring the distribution process. IHH will conduct on-site control monitoring for bread distribution

0 - Project Summary (extended)

This project aims to provide essential food assistance to the most affected population in northwest Syria, particularly in Aleppo, Afrin, and Jandairis villages, following the earthquakes of February 6, 2023. Through the distribution of Ready to Eat (RTE) packages, food baskets, and bread, the project aims to alleviate food insecurity and support affected households for a period of 5 months. Overview:The project spans 5 months, beginning on March 10th and concluding at the end of August. It comprises the distribution of RTE packages for the first month, food baskets for the subsequent 3 months, and bread for the entire 5-month duration. Beneficiary selection, distribution, and monitoring activities will be conducted in coordination with local councils and communities. Key Components: Ready to Eat (RTE) Packages: RTE packages providing a minimum of 2100 kcal per person per day for 5 days will be distributed to targeted households during the first month. For households with fewer members, kit contents will be adjusted accordingly. Food Baskets: Food baskets meeting at least 70% of the daily kcal intake (approximately 1550 kcal per person per day) for a family of 5/6 will be distributed monthly for the following 3 months, considering a reference family size. Bread Distribution: Quality bread, produced using imported Turkish flour provided by IHH, will be distributed to households for 26 days a month throughout the 5-month duration and the MEAL process will include Quality control measures may include regular inspections of the baking process to maintain consistency in size, texture, and taste of the bread. Additionally, adherence to hygiene standards in handling ingredients, preparation, and storage is essential to prevent contamination and ensure bread safety. The selection criteria for beneficiary households are as follows:1- The household should not be receiving assistance from any other organization.2- The household must comprise four or more members, and the absence of the father is required.3- In cases where the father is present, he must be aged over 60, and no male member above 18 should be residing in the household.4- if the father is present and below 60, but the household consists of more than eight members.5- there must be a member in the household with a disability, accounting for more than 50% impairment. Implementation Responsibility: IHH will be responsible for importing, storing, and distributing wheat flour, as well as producing and distributing RTE packages, food baskets, and bread. The organization will also oversee beneficiary selection, daily bread distribution, and monitoring activities. Expected Outcomes:Food Security: The project aims to mitigate food insecurity among earthquake-affected households, ensuring access to essential food items for their sustenance.
