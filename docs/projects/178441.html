Project Summary

Bonyan designed its inclusive education interventions to contributes to addressing the actual education needs in Syria and bridges the gaps in the education sector and comply with the strategic objectives of both the HRP and Education Cluster by improving the availability, access, and quality of education guided by an analysis of the severity of needs in accordance with assessment findings and key indicators.
Likewise, Bonyan 2021 Education program will focus to bring children back to formal education pathways through variety of interventions ensuring that they have access to lifelong healthy learning prioritizing vulnerable children including adolescents/youth, girls and boys living with disabilities, in hard-to-reach locations targeting total of 30,000 children (14900G/14100B, 5-17 years old) including 3500 CwD (1800G/1700B) with strong coordination with relative authorities (Turkish MONE, LC, ED) in Aleppo, and Idleb Gov
** Obj.1: Scale up safe and equitable access to formal and non-formal education for crisis-affected children aged 5-17 years
Starting with back-to-learning campaigns targeting children with their parents to raise awareness of importance of education, address parents’ concerns, and outreach for the established education services including distribution of School kits for children, teacher, and schools including hygiene and recreational kits. Total target is 21,500 children (10900G/10600B – 2500 CwD, 5-17 years old) and their parents and surrounded communities.
Schools and desk maintenance activity will be conducted to expand the capacity of the educational facilities to host more children, provide equity services for both females and males, and children with disabilities and provide better education (rehabilitation of gender separated WASH facilities replacing doors windows whiteboards fixing the walls painting) targeting 50 schools~21,500 children and 2500 desks~7500 children.
Providing operation costs for 5 ECCE targeting 1,250 children (650G/600B, 3-5years old), and 15 formal education school for 11,250 children (5750G/5500B – 1600 CwD, 5-17 years old). non-formal education is tailored by age and needs to fulfill the gaps for each targeted age and gender, 15 NFE centers for 6000 children (3000G/3000B – 600 CwD, 8-12 years old) to provide literacy and numeracy along with PSS sessions. And 15 NFE centers for 3,000 children (1500G/1500B – 300 CwD, 12-15 years old) to provide Remedial program. Both FE and NFE centers will be provided with equipment, student and teachers kits, learning materials, operational consumables, heating during winter season, and sanitization materials with enforced COVID-19 measurements
As education is one of the very basic children’s rights, Bonyan is planning to provide access to education for children with disabilities by provide targeted assistance within the schools in order to encourage these children to continue their education and integrate them with their peers
** Obj.2: Enhance the quality of formal and non-formal education for children and youth aged 5-17 years within a protective environment
Building the capacity of 1000 teachers and educational actors on child centered, gender-sensitive inclusive education, codes of conduct, anti-bullying PSS, referral mechanisms, EiE, and INEE. Targeted training will be conducted for the in-school protection supervisors, and coordinators to improve project quality and compliance with protection principals and improve the established referral system
Bonyan will provide the schools incentives for the teachers and education actors along with teaching resources and supplementary kits during the whole academic year. And will integrate structured PSS life skills and citizenship programs within school activities.
A complete MEAL framework will be utilized for the project that include direct observations and field visits, surveys, FGDs, PDM along with establishing feedback and complaints mechanisms, and in-place PSEA reporting chan

4 - Needs

According to HNO 2021: 6.9M are PiN for humanitarian education assistance in which 97% ~6.7M are children and 15% ~1M are CwD. in 2020, an estimated 2.45M children were out of school and 1.6M were at risk of dropping out. Due to the cumulative effect of the conflict, the closure of learning facilities, and/or reduced learning hours due to COVID-19. Bonyan conducted NA to assess education and protection needs in 7 sub-districts in Idlib governorate and participated in Joint NA conducted by 12 NGOs with ACU~313K Child (61%) in the assessed areas are dropped out. 17,740 are children registered in schools but not attending66% of the school aged boys (~156K boys) are dropped out schools, boys (5-10 years old) have the highest dropping out ratio (~91K) followed by age segment (11-14 Years old) (~44K). 67% of the school aged girls (~157K girls) are dropped out schools, girls (5-10 years old) have the highest dropping out ratio (~83K) followed by age segment (11-14 Years old) (~46K).2563 CwD reported in NWS (Idleb and connected countryside) and 229 CwD in TalAbiyad23% of the operated schools include CwD and all schools are not adequately ready to provide education to CwDPoverty and Displacement were major factors for children dropping out of schools for all ages, in addition to that teachers are not teaching due to the incentives are not paid, and lack of the supported schools leads to unaffordable cost of children’s parents for the children’s education.The most important Children needs: (books, stationery, clothes, PSS sessions and remedial classes) in addition to protective materials in the spread of COVID19The most important teacher’s needs: Salaries, educational materials, trainings

5 - Implementation of activities

the project will link NFE with FE school by scaling up of equitable learning opportunities, enhancing the quality of teaching and learning, ensuring safe and healthy learning environments, and strengthening education at community level30 NFE center (Literacy and Numeracy and Remedial) targeting 9,000 children (4500G/4500B, 8-17 years old) will operate on cycle bases two learning cycles (5 month each). cycle includes BTL events at the beginning, and children transition to formal school at the end. 5 months education cycles achieve a notable progress on children education status. if there is a child still require additional courses, will be reenrolled in the next cycle 15 FE targets 11250child(5750G/5500B, 5-17 years old) (SiG curriculum) this schools will be able to accept the new enrolment of children transitioned from the NFE center. Schools will undergo Inclusion program for children with disabilities by increasing schools’ capabilities through assigning inclusion supervisor, and structural modifications to school building.FE and NFE will include Daily sterilization for COVID-19 Consideration and structured PSS sessions 625 classrooms in addition to disability and gender sensitive WASH facilities in 50 schools will be rehabilitated BTLs will be conducted to increase awareness among parents on the importance of education. outreach teams will conduct awareness-raising activities to support enrolment, and sustain community support to education services in and around schools and build the resilience of communities to find solutions to local needsProvide professional development for 1000 teachers (480M/520W) on child-centered pedagogy, active learning, PSS, referrals, integrating CwD, EiE INEE

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

1. Violence against education facilities and education providers - Mitigation: Learning facilities should have contingency safety plans and safety protocols in place with enough resources (walkie-talkies, basic firefighting equipment, basic first aid supplies, flashlights, exit signs, etc.) and Contingency safety plans and safety protocols are regularly practiced by students, teachers, and caregivers 2. Teachers or other education personnel participate in physical punishment, act violently, bully and neglect students. Or Students participate in bullying or act violently towards other students or towards teachers3. Students are not sufficiently protected from adults or older students while present in a learning centersMitigation: Learning facilities should have an enforced code of conduct, Principals, teachers and caregivers should be made aware of positive discipline methodsTeachers and school personnel trained on prevention and reporting of violence, physical punishment, and bullying. Sufficient safe and clean bathroom facilities should be provided. These should be sex and age segregatedChild Safeguarding polices are part of the program and regularly monitored and corrective action takenStudents, teachers and other education personnel should be provided regular PSS 4. Insufficient support from, or undue influence by, members of the community or local authorities – Mitigation: The community should be consulted and listened to through the life of the project Organizations should work with local education and other relevant authorities and community members to develop a positive relationship. Aid workers should be trained on appropriate and respectful engagement and interviewing technics
