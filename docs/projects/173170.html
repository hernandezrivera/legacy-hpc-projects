Project Summary

The food insecurity and livelihood of the targeted communities in Shattaya and Kubum Localities in South Darfur State are categorized in two: vulnerable Protracted IDPs and host community.
Using the results framework of WFP/VAM pro harvest of 2019/2020, the statistics of the project target areas of focus for the most vulnerable population in phase 3 to 5 are as follows: GFO an partner WVI conducted 3 assessments on Food security and livelihood and we together implement two FSL projects on going until now in Edel Fursan a neighboring locality. 3 gathering villages of IDPs, Host community mainly in Kubum and Shattaya localities:
- Kubum town: IDPs gathering location HH 1409 (7,045 individuals) 62% female and 38% male including children of both sexes.
- Vulnerable Host Community targeted by the project: HH 1380 (6,900 individuals) 54% female and 46% male
Shattaya town: IDPs HH 1171 (5,855). 60% female and 40% male including children.
- Host community in Shattaya: HH 998 (4,990 individual) female 57% and male 43% including children.
* Total population of the target Host communities Male, Female, Children under 18 and Elderly in both localities is 11,890 individuals
* Total population of the target IDPs Male, Female, Children under 18 and Elderly in both IDPs gathering in the two localities is 10,900 individual
* Total Beneficiaries are 22,790 (5,558 HH}.
* Target: 22,790 vulnerable populations are food insecure and have limited access to livelihood. Other obstacles these population experienced on daily basis includes livelihood and coping strategies opportunities, unexpected increase of food prices and reduce of employment opportunities for physically capable youth. Security and sustainability of this community is secured by the living acceptance between the host community and the IDPs. IDPs are given land for cultivation and that reduces the dependency of IDPs on external humanitarian services. They lack basic skills that will translate to income-generating and other livelihood opportunities necessary for effective recovery, return and reintegration especially affecting women and youth). Settlements are scattered within the locality which poses a further challenge for the provision of services.

1 - Needs

Shattaya and Kubum localities in South Darfur neighboring Central Darfur in the Western side of South Darfur. Location was called GATE of fled displaced person from the conflict areas. Displaced took refuge as IDPs in (Dagadussa village) Shattay locality, Kubum and Um Labassa villages on their way to Nyala camps.The needs reflected in the project are based on different surveys and assessments carried out by several FSL actors: Sudan Comprehensive food security Assessment, WFP for Food Security Monitoring System (FSMS) 2019/ FAO for Crop and Food supply Assessment Mission. The and WFP Market Monitor for 2020, beside the recent information and observation collected by our NGO and State FSL sector shared with partners in the FSL Working Group in South Darfur. All FSL/nutrition information and surveys indicated that the food security situation in Shattay and Kubum localities has worsen because of the unexpected increase of crop and animal prices and the low income of the population, these price are expected likely to continue increasing despite of the success last rainy season. Increased labor price of the agriculture inputs and transportation affected the market and capacity of people, particularly Vulnerable IDPs and host communities. The community in these locality consumes millet and sorghum beside ground nut and vegetable in the valleys (wadi). All this shows the rapid increase of price of cereal, the sorghum and vegetable where people are dependent on. The number target beneficiaries of the protracted IDPs in Shattay Locality are 7,000 (1400 HH), Kubum 9,000 (1,800 HH), desegregated in men 30%, women 55% and youth and Children 15% headed HH. In Kubum locality 1,404 HH host communities are semi pastoral they are always looking for animal treatment beside animal vaccination. They have one main nomadic road across the two localities from the South to North South to the North heading to around West Jabal Marrah where they spend the rainy season, twice a year. In Shattay Millet, sorghum, vegetable including tomato, okra are the essential part of the food, melon and sugar can and cucumber are the cash crop.In Kubum where and old protracted IDPs is located the categories of people are very fragile as the area witnessed humanitarian withdrawal since 2007 NCA, WFP, UNICEF WVI, GFO was all working in the area The area is became very poor in term of green belt, trees are all cut for firewood as IGA and construction, our intervention will target 600 women for Efficiency Stoves and 400 youth both sexes for SSB to decrease dependency on wood for shelter construction and also. In the two localities, the project will mainly focus on livestock production by training 30 CAHWs (15 in Kubum and 15 in Shattay) with provision of Kits and vaccination and treatment drugs, establish two veterinary centers to be equipped to expand animal vaccination and treatment to avoid border diseases, support extension

2 - Activities/Outputs

- Provision of crops seeds to 1800 HH (9,000 farmers) and tools to 70% host communities and protracted IDPs where 65% female and 25% male and 15% youth where 1,200 HH in Kubum and 600 HH in Shattay. Amount of seed per beneficiary farmer 15 kg of millet, 20 kg of sorghum and 20 kg of groundnut. - Provision of tools for crops production to 40% with animal plough and 60%% with hoe. -To supply vegetable seed to 1,200 HH in Kubum with 30 g of Okra, 20 g of tomato, 20 g of cucumber, 40 g of onion and 30 g of melon per each HH. - Provide same amount of seeds to 600 HH in Shattay - Provision vaccination services to 1400HH belonging of 100,000 livestock segregated by type: 60,000 cattle, 30,000 goat and sheep and 10,000 camel in Shattay and Kubum - Refreshment training to 30 CAHWs with provision of vaccination and treatment kits and drugs to reduce animal losses (pest and animal diseases control, animal vaccination and treatment) and food preservation techniques - Conduct and disseminate 2 food security and livelihoods related assessments pre and Pro harvest. - Conduct capacity building of our NGO staffs and government institutions to improve FSL preparedness and response during emergencies 2 times during the running of the project. - Building, restoring and maintaining community assets through food or cash for asset programs to crisis effected communities with food insecurity levels. - Conduct 6 awareness raising sessions in living together among farmers and pastorals and exchange commodities by creating common market 4 time a year in 2021. - Provision of income generating support and vocational trainings - 500 HH of most fragile host community 70% female receive small grant to complement their opportunity of life where 15% of them are people with disability and elders - Provide vocational training to 100 individual: 50 female and 350 men and 15 disabled.
