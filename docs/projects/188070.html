Project Summary

According to the proposed interventions, Aoun will directly contribute to the Shelter Sector’s objective 2 of Syria by supporting the sustainable rehabilitation of housing and related public infrastructures in Homs Governorates.
Proposed locations were identified according to the severity of the needs, their level of accessibility, and the experience of Aoun. The comprehensive, multisector and multiyear
approach was the determining element in the selection of the locations for 2022 and 2023, with the aim of promoting the sustainability of the interventions, community selfreliance and resilience.
In line with the results of consultations with the local community and relevant authorities, and taking into account sector priorities, the proposed Aoun intervention will contribute to
building the resilience of 1,000 individuals in 2022 and 1,000 individuals in 2023 affected by improving housing conditions and related public infrastructure for vulnerable populations through:
(1) Rehabilitating 100 damaged houses in 2022 and other 100 in 2023, (50 Alqusoor-50 karm shamsham) areas.
(2) Providing and installing 100 shelter Packages (doors and windows) in 2022 and other 100 in 2023, in Alqarabees area.

4 - Needs

The targeted areas are those affected by the crisis, and their people are displaced to neighboring areas and wish to return to their homes.According to HNO 2022 draft, It is estimated that 5.92 million people in Syria will require shelter support in 2022, where a total of 38 percent of the overall population lives in substandard, damaged, and/or inadequate shelter. Furthermore, 54 percent of IDPs live in substandard, damaged, and/or inadequate shelter, and 55 percent of returnees live in substandard, damaged, and/ or inadequate shelter with nearly 80 percent stating inability to meet their basic needs. With the improved security situation, the severity of the need for shelter support. - There are infrastructures in the target area that help families quickly return to their homes.- Those returning to their homes in the targeted areas live in damaged apartments that lack the basic necessities of life.- These apartments need wooden work, sanitary works, and electrical work.- A preliminary assessment of the situation was made through field visits to the community.- Coordination with the bodies responsible for the rehabilitation work, which is the directorate of the local administration in Homs. Aoun, through this coordination, was informed of the need in the affected areas

5 - Implementation of activities

Aoun will implement the project based on the following steps1- Receiving the names of families accepted for the home rehabilitation process from the governorate within the project implementation areas2 Field needs assessment on the ground for apartments3 Selection of the most vulnerable cases (Female-headed households; Families with elderly/chronically ill members PwDs; Families with children under 2 and under 5; Families hosting IDPs and Large families more than 7). 4 Upload accepted names for government approvals5 One window to ensure that the family does not benefit from previous rehabilitation services6 Announcing the tender to start repair work7 Renovation work begins8 Receiving apartments and handing them over to families under the supervision of government agencies and the donor organization
