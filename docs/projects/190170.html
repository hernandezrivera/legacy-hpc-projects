Project Summary

The project aims to contribute to reduce morbidity and mortality by increasing durable access to safe water and access to life saving basic water and hygiene supplies, through distribution of water treatment emergency kits and in the longer term through the rehabilitation of a water point in Adet and payment of water bills in Axum IDP sites, as well as improving access to hygiene supplies and knowledge of correct hygiene behaviors for conflict affected population through distribution of WASH kits and through hygiene promotion sessions.
This project was started before 2022

3.6 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members using age and gender appropriate, safe and confidential channels?

SC will establish a FRM with multiple community-friendly channels, including bothreactive (hotlines, suggestions box) and proactive (community satisfactionconsultations, FGDs, Post Distribution Monitoring) channels andposters/advertisements to ensure that participants understand their entitlements andknow how to raise concerns as needed. The FRM channels introduced in the projectlocations will allow targeted communities, including children, women, andmarginalized people, to safely and confi dentially report serious complaints (includingchild safeguarding concerns, sexual abuse and exploitation concerns and breaches ofthe code of conduct). SC will manage complaints in a timely, fair, and appropriatemanner that prioritizes the safety of the complainant and those affected at all stageswith confi dentiality. SC’s MEAL Team will analyze and present the information fromFRM to Senior Management to inform decision-making. SC’s MEAL team will workwith the MEAL and Accountability offi cers at fi eld offi ces to close the feedback loop, communicating with communities and children about the actions that have been takenbased on their feedback.
