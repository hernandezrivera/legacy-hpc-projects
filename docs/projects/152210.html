Project Summary

JDF will be providing food security and livelihood support for IDp's in Mongonu LGA, this includes provision of livestock for 400HH, (goats and poultry),Livestock is considered a potential source of livelihoods and nourishment in northeast Nigeria. Every identified household will get 3 female goats. 100 families will get 4 goats (3 females and 1 male), after undergoing a livestock training to ensure they fully understand how to carter for the livestocks and know the economic importance of keeping them, preventing them from slaughtering them immediately for food.
As part of this effort JDF seeks to carryout SEED DISTRIBUTION PER 50 KG for 400HH, In order to promote improved state of nutrition among beneficiaries and strengthening local food security system, JDF will support IDP's with seeds for starting farms, putting into consideration the kind of crops that are permitted to be grown in monguno, JDF also seeks to provide TRAMPOLINE for MICRO GARDEN BED, which will be given to the beneficiaries of food from CAID who do not have access to lands or live in camp settings, with a total target of 400 HH, JERRY CANS FOR WATER will also be given to 400 HH, This support will be given those families who would get tampolin and micro garden bed package. Clean Cooking Stoves400HH: In order to promote safe and smoke free cooking, every identified household will receive an improved fuel efficient, smokeless cook-stove. Overall we will also be providing training respectively for beneficiaries and ensuring we complement the efforts of other actors working to provide food support for IDP's in Monguno, we will ensure that the most vulnerable are selected using the selection criteria that would be jointly developed, JDF will also continue to work closely with the food sector group and the energy sub sector to ensure this intervention follows set standards and remains relevant.
JDF will ensure protection is mainstreamed into this project by
1. Consult men, women, boys, girls, the elderly, and persons with disabilities to understand their needs and
preferences for location, design, and methodology of assistance. Direct observation and discussion groups with representatives of the community to identify the adaptions that are needed for the most vulnerable
2. A complaints feedback mechanism will be established with a view to improving programming, assisting in
understanding beneficiary and community perceptions, promoting beneficiary empowerment and assisting in the early detection of problems such as targeting, misconduct including sexual exploitation and abuse, diversion and fraud.
3. JDF will Identify, support the role, and strengthen the capacity of local authorities with responsibilities in the food Security Sector. Where possible, train local Food Security committees consisting of beneficiaries can do identify problems for themselves. Make sure beneficiaries and staff know where to refer or report incidents of rights. in order to ensure protection mainstreaming throughout the project about 2% of the total budget will be kept aside to conduct such activities.

Needs

Monguno has over the years been affected by the humanitarian crisis in the north east, giving rise to various international and local agencies/organization providing support for registered IDP'S within the town, there have been food support by christianaid Nigeria and other agencies but plans/support to ensure sustainability which this project also targets or hope s to achieve has not been considered yet by agencies working in mongonu, this gap we seek to fill as we gradually shift from humanitarian response to ensuring full recovery and sustainability. Providing these support will ensure that IDP'S are strengthen economically to meet their own needs, feed healthy and reduce exposure to protection concerns. all of this will be done in close collaboration with Christian aid and food sector group. IDP's that relocated to monguno from other parts still live in camps, and solely rely on support from different organizations, the lack of access to land is also a major challenge for IDP's hence those who desire to carryout agricultural activities like farming cannot, we therefore hope that the provision of micro gardening beds will help them grow fresh vegetable and other crops that will be used to build their nutritional intake. new arrivals in camps have little acess to fuel for food and need to go far to ensure they gather fuel, making it them open to different forms of attacks, providing fuel efficient stoves will go along way in ensuring reduced attacks and protection concerns in camps. Putting into consideration the need to be accountable to the affected population JDF will implement in accordance with the Core Humanitarian Standard (CHS) – on quality and accountability anchored on 9 commitments that organization and individuals involved in humanitarian response can use to improve the quality and effectiveness of the assistance they provide. The single core standard has been devised to clarify the responsibilities of aid workers, make the implementation of humanitarian standards simpler and easier and contribute to better humanitarian response. The coherent and easy –to-use standard will be put into practice and make a difference in the lives of crisis affected communities/persons. Special attention will be given to vulnerable population (Pregnant and nursing mothers, elderly and persons living with disabilities during project activities) For this response project, specific activities have been built in to consult and encourage active participation of the target beneficiaries. These include the following: 1. Community entry meetings to introduce/present the project 2. Consultations on the location of women and girls’ safe spaces, activities included in the safe spaces, and community awareness-raising activities. 3. Establishment of a complaints mechanism 4.Promoting women’s empowerment by offering safe income alternatives, building knowledge and fostering independence.5. Ensuring women and men (girls and boys as appropriate) have equal say in decisions affecting the project

Activities

JDF will be Distributing livestocks (goats), Poultry birds, micro gardening equipment, fuel clean stove and jerrycans for water. the project will be targeting new arrivals in camps, female &child headed house holds, IDP's with large house holds, Food beneficiaries of Christian-aid, IDP's with no source of income, aged women with large house holds,in-order to achieve this JDF will after carrying out beneficiary identification and registration,applying the set criteria. after which vouchers and cards will be issued for easy identification during scheduled distribution. Jireh Doo Foundation (JDF) is committed to ensure transparency, accountability and effectiveness in all its development efforts, projects and programs, as such, Jireh Doo Foundation has a robust monitoring system defined at all stages of the intervention. The action will be monitored by JDF using a results-based monitoring, evaluation, accountability and learning (MEAL) framework at the beginning of the project considering the project log-frame and work plans as well as agreed standards and donor and cluster guidelines. JDF will use Indicator Tracking Cards (ITCs) for the results of the proposed action. JDF program and monitoring staff will monitor activities through regular visits to target sites, accompany them in the training sessions. At Field level - Programme implementation would be overseen by a Project Manager based at Maiduguri. All field level staff will meet and discuss the progress, the operational difficulties being faced by them and the possible solutions and arrangements that needs to be made to make the programme effective. The National Coordinator of Jireh Doo Foundation will be responsible for the overall project management, financial management, donor compliance, reporting as well as coordination among other humanitarian response. Regular meetings of the Project Management Team to review the progress of the activities, outputs and achievements. JDF programme and monitoring staff will maintain overall responsibility of the monitoring of the project. Participatory monitoring tools are in place including KAP, beneficiary cards, Regular monitoring visits will be conducted by Jireh Doo Foundation while carrying out activity based monitoring, with any short comings being corrected immediately within the field. JDF will seek to have quarterly joint participatory monitoring with team members drawn from JDF. JDF finance staff will monitor and ensure effective financial management, verifications, counter-signing the sheets and overall financial management oversight throughout the action. Through the monitoring activities and the implementation of the actions on the project, JDF will document lessons learnt during the implementation and share these with the sector group. During the course of implementation JDF will conduct project review meetings to assess what has worked well and what has not and try to promote the good practices as well as re-strategize to address the challenges. JDF will maintain a monitoring and evaluation information data set that will be populated periodically. Situation Reports will be produced on a regular basis to share implementation progress. The following will implemented over the course of the project: 1. Monitoring visits to project locations will be conducted on monthly basis 2. A toll free line will be provided to get feedback from intervention communities. 3. Monthly FGDs will be conducted in intervention communities to get feedback, the groups involved in the FGDs will include male, female, young, old and people with disabilities. 4. Feedback from these processes will be analyzed and used for learning. 5. Real time reviews will also be organized during the project duration. 6. JDF will utilize the GBVIMS for statistical reporting of anonymized, disaggregated data in a safe and ethical manner. 7. JDF has a staff code of conduct and child protection policy and will ensure staffs do not exploit Beneficiaries
