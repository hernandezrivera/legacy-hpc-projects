Project Summary

This project aims at improving access to safe schools (education facilities) that provide quality education services to conflict-affected learners in a protective learning environment. This will be achieved through:
- Conducting repair and rehabilitation of conflict-affected education facilities;
- Procurement and distribution of equipment to conflict-affected education facilities.

1 - Needs

The armed conflict in eastern Ukraine continues to impact children, educators and the whole education system, especially in the areas along the ‘contact line’. In other areas, there are widespread evidences of the cumulative effect of the conflict for 735,000 children and teachers learning and teaching in 3,500 education facilities. For many children and youth, access to education is occasionally disrupted and their wellbeing and learning is affected due to prolonged fear, conflict-related distress and trauma and for teachers, their ability to teach. Children, adolescents, and young people are among the most vulnerable categories due to very specific risks they are exposed to in their everyday life. In relation to the ongoing conflict, the socio-economic situation has also significantly deteriorated in Donetska and Luhanska oblasts. Communities and institutions located in the region are excessively underfunded from the government’s side.According to the results of the latest Education Cluster school survey, 84% or a total of 771 education facilities reported that they need rehabilitation for some of the infrastructural components indicating roof (46%) and windows (45%) as a top of priority. Moreover, 40% or 374 education facilities reported need in rehabilitation of heating system. This information is in line with our preliminary assessment conducted during 2018-2019 which claims that problem-related to the heating, water piping and insulation are still considered as ones with the most acute needs. There are 113 education facilities need conflict-related rehabilitation out of which 77 education facilities are located within 20 km of the contact line and 81% or 747 of education facilities indicated that they need replacement of some school equipment. Some 75% of education facilities reported the availability of indoor space where children can engage in sports activities, but 45% of them or 298 stated that this space (usually sports gym) needs repair or rehabilitation.Based on the needs revealed during the survey, the areas of 0-20 km from the contact line of Luhanska and Donetska oblasts. For instance, in Marinskyi rayon only 3 facilities had been damaged and fully repaired, leaving 16 facilities, which were damaged during the conflict, only partially renovated, the majority of which (12 facilities) were located within 5 km of the contact line. If learning conditions are not improved, it will make people resort to the negative coping strategies depriving children of universal right to obtain a proper education. According to the Safe Schools Declaration, education is fundamental to development and to the full enjoyment of human rights and freedoms. Education can help to protect children and even can alleviate the psychological impact of armed conflict by offering routine and stability and can provide links to other vital services. It is crucial to guarantee that proper learning conditions are equal to boys and girls, taking into account their gender-specific differences and inclusive approach.

2 - Activities & outcomes

Output 1. Rehabilitated educational facilities: roofing, windows installation, heating systems repairs, repair of classrooms and gyms, etc. arche noVa will conduct rehabilitation works in school and pre-school institutions in order to improve the learning conditions, which will positively influence educational process in general as well as pupils and teachers’ well-being in particular. The works will be based on a detailed needs assessment, during which needs of direct beneficiaries will be taken into account and they will have a chance to influence the project implementation. The active participation of women, girls and boys is critical to ensure gender issues are addressed. Children and young people can play a powerful role in the planning of learning spaces as they often know more than adults about gender dimensions as well as different risks they might face in school. The identified risks will be taken into consideration during conducting the works. For instance, ensuring good visibility in educational spaces by maximizing the number/size of windows and doors as well as installation of additional lightings might prevent risks of GBV issues. In order to facilitate inclusion of the beneficiaries, a comprehensive feedback mechanism will be implemented. It will be designed ensuring safe access for women, girls, men and boys. Output 2. Procured and distributed equipment - procurement and distribution of equipment to conflict-affected education facilities. Providing support with modern interactive equipment will be based on the facilities` needs, revealed during focus group discussions involving pupils, school management, education committees, parents and teachers. Potential list of equipment could include equipment for practicing during learning of cookery, hairdressing etc., and also computers, furniture, sports equipment. This will lead to ensuring of safe and comfortable learning conditions for students and improving their competences, giving them more possibilities for development in future.
