Project Summary

In a bit to ameliorate on the deplorable living conditions of affected populations of the NW/SW Regions of Cameroon, COHESODEC in the implementation of this project hopes to intervene in five-(5) vulnerable divisions of the North-West and South-West Regions i.e Mezam, Menchum, Momo-(NWR), Meme and Manyu divisions-(SWR). Vulnerable communities identified as beneficiaries of this WASH project in 2024 are found within the following targeted localities: Fungom, Menchum-Valley, Wum, Bafut, Bali, Bamenda I, Bamenda II, Bamenda III, Santa, Tubah, Batibo, Eyumodjock, Mamfe, Kumba I, Kumba II & Kumba III. This project shall ensure that beneficiaries have access to WASH services and Needs which will for a long time significantly improve on their lives and social well-being regarding Water, Sanitation and Hygiene.
This project shall ensure the construction of 20 water points (solar boreholes), construct/rehabilitate 150 stand taps to enable some 10,000 men, women, boys and girls to be provided with sustainable access to safe drinking water in the above target vulnerable communities through either construction/rehabilitated water points. The project will also ensure that schools and child friendly spaces gain access to 3 safe drinking water points as well as be ensuring that 1 other solar borehole is constructed in a health center to enable these community health facilities and schools gain access to safe drinking water points.
This project will also ensure the construction of 15 gender-segregated community latrine cubicles built in IDP sites and host communities of the targeted localities as well as ensure the construction of 15 gender-segregated family latrine cubicles in IDP sites for a minimum of 1,080 beneficiaries. Also, COHESODEC will through this project ensure the construction of 9 latrine cubicles which are gender & disability sensitive, construction of 6 latrine cubicles in health facilities and the construction of 3 latrines in schools.
Furthermore, the project will enable COHESODEC to distribute a total of 10,000 WASH kits to boys, girls, men, women, families, PWD and old people. These kits shall include Menstrual Hygiene management Kits to 5,000 women and girls and 5,000 WASH kits distributed to vulnerable households to ameliorate on the deplorable living conditions of target beneficiaries.
Moreover, the project will be promoting safe hygiene and sanitation practices among vulnerable people also reaching out to 10,000 people along awareness raising campaign-lines ensuring the promotion of good WASH practices and also making sure that at least 10,000 affected people know how to provide feedback and complaints to humanitarian actors.
Also, the project shall ensure an emergency WASH needs assessments, surveys, studies and others as follows; conduct 3 emergency WASH needs assessments in affected areas-(Baseline, mid-term evaluation and an end-line assessment), conduct 3 KAP studies 5 divisions of the North-West and South-West Regions.
Finally, this project shall also ensure the support of solid waste management in IDP sites through the construction of 10 waste pits, the construction of 12 bathroom cubicles in IDP earmarked sites for this project and the distribution of 30 waste bins in target IDP sites.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

In the implementation of this project, COHESODEC will require the following; >Specialized technicians shall be selected and sub-contracted after a tender process ensuring they have a proper mastery of earmarked project activities with regards to borehole drilling and latrine construction, >Specialized suppliers shall also be selected and contracted after a tender process to supply WASH kits and hand washing facilities as per COHESODEC procurement policy. This will be done through a rigorous tender process to ensure that only the best suppliers are retained and contracted to do the job. The WASH kits will be comprised of the following items: (Plastic pail with cover, Plastic dipper, Laundry Soap x4, Bath soap x12, Toothpaste x2, Toothbrush x5, Nail cutter x3, Sanitary Pad packs x3, Malong x2, water purification tablets-( aqua tabs). >As far as the promotion of safe hygiene and sanitation practices among vulnerable people is concern, COHESODEC shall recruit, train and use 10 sensitization contracted social workers as well as contract a printing press to produce hand-outs for awareness raising campaigns and hygiene promotion of good wash practices and sensitize the vulnerable populations on the provision of feedback and complaints to humanitarian actors. Moreover, COHESODEC shall after due process contract a specialized contractor to construct waste pits and the supply of waste bins. Finally, COHESODEC shall recruit specialized experts as well as train data collectors in the 5 targeted divisions of the North-West and South-West Regions to ensure effective data collection for the WASH need assessments, surveys and KAP studies.
