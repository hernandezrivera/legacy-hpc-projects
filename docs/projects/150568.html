Project Name

Start Date

01/01/2019

End Date

31/12/2019

Project Summary

Overall Goal : Ensure all sick children get the right to learn in Gaza Strip
Objectives:
1- Establish Catch-Up Classes (School Class) for children with kidney failure and other chronic diseases inside main hospitals in Gaza Strip.
2. Provide psycho-social support to vulnerable children inside the hospitals
3. Provide compensate classes for temporary illness children inside the hospitals.
Target group:
More than 600 sick children with chronic diseases (specially kidney failure) and their ages from 6 to 18 years, and 30 teachers for different subjects.

Needs

The diseases are one of the most reasons that lead children to dropping out from school or hinder the completion of the studying and effects on the their goals in the future. However; when the child is cured of the disease; a long term rehabilitation prgramme and many years for recovering his/her education situation which was lost during illness. So that; the child unable to continuing life as usual as peers, and cannot achieves ambitions and dreams, especially chronic disease in childhood which causes dropout from school, lose many scholastic years, and loss right to learn. Many diseases required long treatment within the hospital, the most influential is children with chronic kidney failure, which requires several years in treatment and dialysis for several days in the month, and leads to absence for many school's days, dropout, or inability to continue studying compared with their peers, that's adversely effects their mental and physical health, and their families' life. In case the child have kidney transplantation and healing from kidney failure, the child cannot return to the school life and continuing his/her education. In addition, there are other diseases affecting on children such as cancer, blood diseases, gastrointestinal diseases, etc., which also require treatment within the hospital for intermittent or long-term, and may cause dropout from school, and lose many scholastic years, and weak educational attainment. As self-assessment with cooperation with MOHE, MOH, and UNRWA on December 2017, we found that total of 1160 child with chronic diseases (588 boy under 15year, 491 girl under 15year) , so that from the total, 388 child (202 boys, 186 girls) with kidney failure, 437 child (229 boys, 208 girls) with cancer , and 338 child (189 boys, 149 girls) with blood diseases, and there are too many children visits hospitals for short term treatment, and lost many scholastic days. After that assessment, the MPC implement a pilot project with partnership with MOHE & MOH which aim to establish School Class As self-assessment with cooperation with MOHE, MOH, and UNRWA on December 2017, we found that more than 388 child with kidney failure, 437 child with cancer , and 338 child with blood diseases, and there are too many children visits hospitals for short term treatment, and lost many scholastic days. After that assessment, the MPC implement a pilot project with partnership with MOHE & MOH which aim to establish School Class inside Rantisi Hospital (governmental hospital) in Gaza on December 2017, to provide catch-up class for every child takes treatment on it. This Class is certified , special books contents, and full supervision by MOHE and all students gets accredited certification from MOHE to complete educational stages. This projects targeted only children under 15 years, served 30 student as essential, and more than 40 different child every month. Consequently, more than 200 child gets catch-up classes during 4 months only. The children above 15 years take their treatments in different governmental hospitals in three main areas such as Gaza & North Gaza , Middle, and Khanyounis. So that; over 1100 child needs catch-up classes in all hospitals. All those children need direct intervention and response to recovering their education situation and support them during their illness, and provide to them catch-up classes even in the hospitals or during dialysis hours for the kidney failure students and assistive classes for other children inside the hospital , and gets their "Right to Learn", and as SDG goal-4 targeted "sure that all girls and boys complete free, equitable and quality primary and secondary education leading to relevant and Goal-4 effective learning outcomes" and " to eliminate gender disparities in education and ensure equal access to all levels of education and vocational training for the vulnerable, including persons with disabilities, indigenous peoples and children in vulnerable situations".

Activities

The idea of the project is to provide catch up classes for 600 illness children ( 45% girls) (kidney failure as essential ) those children resident on the hospital for treatment for more than 15 days in each month, so that, most of them dropout from schools and they can't Activities in detailsActivity 1-Establish Catch-Up Class (School Class) inside 3 main governmental hospitals in Gaza StripThose classes will provide 4 basic educational subjects and exams as realistic school class and contains two classroom, for the children stay for longtime inside the hospitals, whose full or partial dropout of school because of their illness such as kidney failure , cancer, blood diseases, and gastrointestinal diseases. This Class is certified , special books contents, and full supervision by MOHE and all students gets accredited certification from MOHE to complete educational stages with cooperation with UNRWA and MOHE.So, every child resident in the hospital for a longtime will take this classes by especial teachers , and will get psychological support, also support child mother from the specialist, this will help the child and his/her family to be acceptance for child situation and the child be able to complete the his educational stages as in real school and gets the needed certificates. This classes will provide all the needed tools, books, and equipment's for teaching, and customize especial room inside the hospitals. More than 90 child (40% girls ) will enroll in this classes and takes lesions as School Class. This is a response activity. Activity 2- Provide compensate classes in the chosen hospitals.The compensative class will server children whose stay inside the hospital for short time (or have emergency disease), and they already enrollment in schools. So that, this class will help them to continue their lessons as their peers in their schools and provide psychological support. Those children will take the needed subjects as their schools study schedule in different hospital departments or in the catch-up class.The compensate class will server 510 children (55% girls) whose stay inside the hospital for short time ( about 40 child (55% girls) each month) , and they already enrollment in schools. So that, this class will help them to continue their school's subjects as their peers in the school. Those children take the lessons in different hospital departments or in the catch-up class. This will help children to be able to complete the studding in their schools as normal and didn't lose more time in understand the lost lessons. This is a remedial activity. Activity 3: Training and capacity building for 30 teachers (70% females) to conduct the catch-up classes with the children inside hospitals Classes. The training will be on (New teachers course) and (methodology and planning the lessons courses) with cooperate with the Ministry of Education and UNRWA . This training will improve teachers skills in dealing with illness children, and learn new methodologies in teaching. This is a remedial activity. Activity 4: provide more than 3 psychological support session for 200 children (50% girls) ( 85% ages from 6 to 15 year) who are suffering from kidney failure, cancer, and other chronic diseases this will helps them to dealing with their illness as normal, and coexistence with it. Most of children frustrated, hopeless, and unwilling to learn, after the sessions, they will accept to return to studding through the Catch-up classes in the hospital and be able to communicate with their peers, teachers as normal. This will helps child mother to support, and provide assistant for her child. She will believe on her child abilities to continue the education as other children, and helps her child to achieving his/her ambitions. This is a remedial activity.

Indicators

1- More than 90 (45% girls)(90% ages from 6 to 15 year) child (specially children with kidney failure) takes catch-up classes in 4 basic educational subjects, takes monthly and quarterly exams, and complete their educational stage (Certified form MOHE) inside 3 main governmental hospitals (Rantisi, ELShifa, and European hospital) in Gaza strip.2- More than 510 child (55% girls) (85% ages from 6 to 15 years) takes 1530 compensate classes (3 subject for each child ) and catch-up classes inside 3 main hospitals(Rantisi, ELShifa, and European hospital) in Gaza strip.3- About 30 teachers(70% females) have the ability to implement the catch-up classes inside the hospitals Classes, and to learn all illness children as they trained on dealing with children with Kidney failure or other chronic diseases.4- More than 200 (50% girls) ( 85% ages from 6 to 15 year) children gets 600 psychological support sessions during the project period.5- Three hospital Classes certified as school class implements the catch-up classes to supports all illnesses children inside the targeted hospitals.6- Increasing in children numbers who enrollment in Catch-up classes inside the hospitals.7- Families and hospitals staff helps in supporting children in their educational sessions.8- At least 20% of secondary school children success to have their high school (Tawjihi) certificate and continues their higher education.9- Many request form other hospitals and families to establish new classes in the other aerie's in Gaza strip.
