Project Summary


The conflict between SAf and RSF in April 15th began in Khartoum and extended to the other states , after nine months Sudan facing one of the fastest unfolding crises globally, with unprecedented needs emerging in such a short period, The internally displaced people (IDPs) are originally from 12 states, with the majority - about 3.7 million people (61%of all IDPs post-April) - from Khartoum.
This is a community early recovery project aims to positively improve living conditions and life-sustaining basic services of vulnerable populations in Khartoum after war,the impact of the food security and livelihood ,socio-economic well-being of targeting total of 11,200 returnees ,IDPs, and Urban poor HH, Bahry, Al Salam West and South Ombada, Sharq Elneel, and Haj Yousif localities. 60% of which are women/G, 20% Youth, 5% elderly and disables, 5% children without parental care, in Khartoum State.
BPWVO will enlarge their awareness and sense during conflict, and inflation crises through direct distribution of cash base intervention and support them to access the main live saving services through dividing women groups guided by resistance and changes committees in 7 localities in Khartoum.
Distribution of direct fund ,training in food and sgro- procecing ,REFLECT Methodology which will help them to advocate to solve their problems, reduce vulnerabilities, increase the diversify of income to access to the local labor market and create an enabling environment for livelihoods in the target localities as well as prevent negative coping., It will work on strengthening the protection security and increase the economic capacity of households, facilitating skills and development training through certified training institutions based on Reflect Methodology(as one of fitting literacy-peace building tools), setting to help more people and make a bigger impact. These empowering women and girls as well as boy’s programs will help to end cycles of gender-based violence. The three populations IDP, host communities and refugees live in extreme poverty and illiteracy, children girls/boys are dropped out of schools are subjected to abuse, child labor, trafficking, beggary, homeless, wrong practices and other offenses,. Protection for children and Women is needed by equipping skilled, education, counseling, medical care, small business loans, and other programs that empower women and girls as well as boys helping to end cycles of gender-based violence to meet the aspiration of childhood and fill the gabs, spread awareness and apply principles proclaimed in the charter of UN & international, regional and local covenants appeal to decision makers BPWVO will be engaged with key actors that could influence changes in the community, the multi-pronged strategy will keep the whole community active, and campaigns facilitation that sensitize Leaflet and prepare host communities to sustain the project .
Objectives:
The project aimed to reach 22,000 individuals in five localities in Khartoum by providing them FSL and protection services . The project also considered the returnees (after War Ends)
Do no harm approach will be used to ensure the safety and dignity of the beneficiaries and additional services will be provided for vulnerable groups such as prioritizing children, PWD and single headed households for assistance

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

Through this intervention, BPWO is committed safeguard Right holders from violence and discrimination and is aimed at obtaining full respect for the rights of the individuals. BPWO will implement the project in impartial and non-discriminatory ways that promote the safety, dignity and integrity of the people receiving assistance during assessment, design of project and delivery of services. The project team will ensure all elements of accountability to affected population to be captured through extensive engagement and consultation with the community. Project is designed to inform all displaced about the availability and location of services. The project ensures safe and equal access to inclusive and non-discriminatory provision of services. The intervention will also carefully apply Do No Harm approach to avoid any negative impact that it might bring to the area or the targeted communities. Manage information in a sensitive manner so that the security of informants or others who may be identifiable is not jeopardized. The assistance and protection measures will not undermine local capacities for self-protection as well as support the efforts of the affected population and local communities to find security and restore dignity. Project intends to respond to the root causes of the protection concerns in the area. To achieve this, BPWO embraces community based protection approach, for building capacity of local communities, service providers and government officials. Their capacity will be enhanced in analyzing & finding solutions to protection problems, inclusive of reducing threats& risks, reducing vulnerability of affected population and increasing their capacity to cope with protection problems following exposure to shock and disaster.
