Project Summary

According to Voices 2023, Gender-based violence (GBV) continues to be a central component of the Syrian humanitarian crisis, and persistently affects the lives of millions of women and girls. Perceptions of increased risks of intimate partner violence (IPV), domestic violence, family violence, forced/ early marriage, technology facilitated GBV (TFGBV), sexual violence, and exploitation both in public spaces and in the domestic environment. Women and girls as well as GBV experts and service providers shared the vital, lifesaving importance and need for Women and Girls Safe Spaces (WGSS). and the continued increase in levels of GBV have no doubt taken their toll on the well-being and resilience of women and girls. Consistently, women and girls, as well as GBV experts, shared that rates of suicide were also increasing amongst this group.
SEMA through this project intends to provide both preventive (GBV awareness raising, empowerment activities, life skills, AMAL initiative, and Recreational activities, ) and responsive (Case management including Focused PSS, PFA, CVA, and referrals to other specialized services).
SEMA will focus on enhancing integration between GBV and RH in both GBV and health-delivering points, using 2 types of modalities (static, and mobile) for this purpose in all locations
SEMA will support 2 established WGSS (Sharran, and Atmeh camps), and will provide integrated GBV services in health facilities (Ariha, Al-Bab, Idlib, and Atmeh) as well as in community centers at Bulbul and Salqen. Key strategies will be taken into consideration during the implementation:1- Provide specialized GBV services to survivors with supervision, all cases will be uploaded to GBVIMS+, Providing Individual case funds using the GBVSC sops,
2- Provide activities empowering women and girls and GBV prevention activities including an Awareness-raising toolkit, life skills activities, and vocational training.
3- Providing and enhancing specialized adolescent girls programming, including life skills programs and AMAL initiatives.
4- Support women and girls with disabilities to reach service delivery points (both static and outreach).
5- train humanitarian actors (non-specialists) to reduce the risks of GBV and refer them to specialized services.
6- Support women-led organizations inside Syria to expand their GBV technical knowledge and GBV programming
7-conduct Safety audits in camps
8-Dignity Kits distribution
