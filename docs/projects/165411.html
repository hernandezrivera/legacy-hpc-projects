Project Summary

This project is designed to provide and improve the vocational training and supporting women and youth-led community projects, also to empower the capacities and abilities of the youth ages 18-30 years for both gender with deep focusing on IDPs in Aleppo, Idlep and Ar-Raaqa Governorates.
We aim to support them to build, plan, and manage their future in a realistic environment that helps them to be more involved in the community by providing them with self-employment package of services, raising their self-confidence, and their psychological and mental behaviour and providing the needed of the qualified technical cadres for the local community, civil society organizations and other civil local actors which able to participate in the process of reconstruction and development also to compensate for the lack of vocational training to develop the expertise of youth by providing them with technical expertise in order to become those youth the technicians who are able to work in the society. Furthermore, the relevance of this project is the extent to which the project’s objectives are consistent with the area’s current development priorities expressed in livelihood services, vocational training to IDPs and Host Community members.
Supporting women and youth-led community projects activity will take place after each training course as a course requirement to have applied what they have learned during the Life Skills course. Following each life skills course, Silk Road will request from beneficiaries to integrate themselves in small volunteer separate groups which will implement a community initiative in the targeted location.
Silk Road will Organize school’s cleanup campaigns, provide minor rehabilitation for schools and other activities such as:
1- Re-establish market access and reconnect neighbouring communities.
2- Improving community water and sanitation facilities, rehabilitating schools, reconstructing gardens and painting public buildings.
3 - Remove rubble from city streets and panting sidewalks.
Silk Road will train and equip women and youth volunteer or through the Cash-for-Work activity ( Slight support) to provide temporary employment for public projects to the most vulnerable segments of a population targeted through this project. Furthermore, the training will include best practice for each specific training subject, and explain what safety risks they may face in their work and how to mitigate them (This will be explained during the vocational courses and before implementing community projects activity).
One of the ultimate goals of this project is to provide the targeted beneficiaries with the tools and resources to work towards self-empowerment and sustainability by providing each trainee with, Knowledge, expertise and full equipment kit to enable him/her strongly enter the labour market.
This project, in addition to providing youth people with the necessary expertise in certain sectors, it will create business opportunities that are not present in the targeted area, such as maintenance of computers and electricity, building skills, electrical maintenance skills, Carpentry and blacksmithing skills.
We will apply specific training courses to serve targeted people and enable them to have the required technical experience and make them ready to be an active part in the society and local market and have a stable economical source of living, as the length of the course is 3 months.
Add to the above that, Silk Road expect that the local community will facilitate project activities and from our side, we will motivate them to implement similar projects and activities, attract their attention to the importance of vocational training and involving them in counselling as well as we strive to support trainees and providing favorable conditions for them and encourage them to ensure the success of the project depending on the extent of their interaction, commitment to the program, and seriousness and commitment of qualified trainers.

4 - Needs

During 2019, the conflict escalated between the different parties in large parts of the country in both northeast and northwest of Syria where the heavy conflicts reflected a negatively impact on the whole region in most sectors such as education, WASH, main services, and economic situation of the population furthermore the future of a whole generation is threatened due to the retreat of educational process level because of the lack of expertise and low attendance and permanence and interaction, as a result of the high costs of the training, the bad economic situation that prevails in the area, the significant shortage of learning and training tools. On the other hand, the recent heavy military operation by Syrian government forces in Aleppo and Idlep generated a massive IDPs flow to the northern areas near the border. The humanitarian situation across northwest Syria, particularly in Idleb and Aleppo governorates continue to deteriorate at an alarming rate due to ongoing violence, which has a devastating effect on the four million people in northwest Syria. According to OCHA, from 1 December 2019 to 2 February 2020, some 586,000 people fled from their homes in northwest Syria as a result of ongoing hostilities. Many of them have been displaced many times throughout the Syria crisis, eroding the resilience of families and communitiesThe resilience of communities, families and individuals who have been displaced many times as a result of hostilities is eroding. Families continue to flow from the border areas and clashing lines to the safer areas in Tel Abiad, Ras Al Ain. Youth society which equals 50 to 60 % of total population between males and females “ordinary people or with disability”, majority around 67% Couldn’t complete their high school or go to the university and for this category and due to the lack of technical experience, they have so tight job opportunities to live with very poor payments and temporary periods with high-risk work nature.
