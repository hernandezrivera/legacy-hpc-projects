Project Summary

To ensure the provision of reliable connectivity and security telecommunications services for the humanitarian community. The ETC will also provide dedicated coordination and Information Management services to ensure a swift and coordinated response and avoid duplication of efforts. As part of its Services for Communities (S4C), the cluster will assess communications needs of affected communities and technical needs of local community radio stations in the affected area where feasible.
