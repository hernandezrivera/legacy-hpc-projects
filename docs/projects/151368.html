Project Summary

The project will support the immediate recovery of drought affected people in Lower Shabelle region through provision of cash and food assistance and restoration of livelihood opportunities, while helping vulnerable people and building resilience through sustainable intervention. The project also connects to cover the need to enhance the productive capacities and coping mechanisms of displaced populations, in a way that promotes social cohesion and mitigates tensions. The project will struggle to build the capacities of community structures to be able to respond to disaster early warnings and all these will be tended towards improving food security and building community resilience while fostering disaster risk reduction. The primary focus will be to increase and diversify sources of incomes among targeted communities in with focus on sustainable sources. The project will Endeavour to build capacities of community to be able to cope with the changing times devised by Climate change; these would be realized through trainings and cash for work in the form of rehabilitation of community infrastructures. The aim of the project will be to alleviate chronic poverty and reverse vulnerability to drought shocks thus promoting self reliance in economics, social and cultural needs of project area populations as well as enable them live in harmony with their neighbors. Cash Programming will be conditional in nature and will be done in form of Cash for work; identified beneficiaries will participate in the rehabilitation of community infrastructure, and earn the cash needed to purchase food and other basic items and services. Community Capacity Building is intended to ensure that communities have the skills and knowledge needed to plan for and cope with future livelihood shocks.
One of the primary objectives of the project is to build and fully equip the Communities which will be durable solution and will promote self sufficient, the Community will run directly and look after the implementation of each project, AYUUB will seek collaboration with the ministry lines both Federal and state level and focus will be paid on system strengthening to ensure sustainability and enhancing capacity particularly with focus on strengthening and self sufficient in emergencies preparedness, response planning of the This strategy will support the Ministry of Agriculture in Emergencies and engagement of community to realize effective response and fundraising upon the termination of the project. In addition the response will continue to invest in community mobilization of the local people and local authority as an integral part of broader programme. Needs

As per the HNO –Oct 2018- The sustained food security responses in prioritized areas and livelihoods including Lower Shabelle region following high levels of food insecurity reported for the previous seasons, has contributed to preventing the worsening of the food security situation. Had it not been for the sustained food security assistance during the first half of 2018, these livelihoods would have been classified one phase worse. Of note was the rapid scale up of humanitarian assistance in response to the needs triggered by flood and cyclone in 2018.The acute food insecurity and malnutrition is one of the most persistent humanitarian challenges in Somalia despite concerted efforts to address them. The mix of chronic and acute food insecurity together, necessitates a sustained and multifaceted response.According to FSNAU, about 5.3 million Somalis (43% of the total population) have encountered acute food insecurity since 2011. This number has dropped to 4.6 million (34%) in Gu 2018. and the total number of acutely food insecure people has decreased by 25% between Gu 2018 andGu 2017. The population in IPC 4 and 3 also show a significant reduction (50%) over the last two years. AYUUB is targeting 32,000 beneficiaries Female: 17,420 Male: 14,580, Children (under 18): 17,940the most vulnerable HHs in Merka, KM50, Qoryooley, Awdheegle and Kurtunwareey districts of Lower Shabelle region through providing lifesaving and life sustaining integrated multi sectoral assistance to reduce acute humanitarian needs among the most vulnerable people and to prevent further deterioration food security situation.AYUUB will determine Rural livelihood support to restore own food access, prevent further displacement, and enable early return or local integration or settlement elsewhere for the willing and able, Urban livelihood support to improve own food access and facilitate integration in the same location or elsewhere.

Activities

Activity 1: Identify and register 8900HHs with malnourished children, lactating women, pregnant women, elderly, sick and new arrived IDPs, identified by community leaders and women groups to prevent a deterioration of the situation and specifically to avoid people in phase 3 from slipping into phase 4 or 5. Activity 2: Distribute un/conditional cash transfer to Merka and KM50 IDPs 1700 HHs; each household will receive US$70/month through mobile transfer for a period of three months. Female headed HHs will constitute approximately 60% of the beneficiary HHs. Activity 3: Rehabiliate 4 main canals, excavate 4 water catachments and Provide tractor hours in land preparation representing 4500 HHs to restore own farms and promote crop production in Merka, Qoryooley and Kurtunwarey small scale farmers. Activity 4: Distribution of emergency agricultural inputs packages containing land preparation support through canal rehabilitation and seeds (Maize, vegetable seeds, and cowpeas seeds) and farm tools to pastoral 15000HHs (8250 Female and 6750 Male) in Marka (2500 HHs), Qoryooley (4200 HHs), Awedhegle (2500 HHs), KM50 (2300 HHs) and Kurtunwarey (3500 HHs) district of Lower Shabelle, since they urgently need food and seed to secure GU harvest.. Activity 4: Creating temporary job opportunities to (1000 persons) youth groups to use their own small business movement by giving loans, train farmers and communities by conducting capacity building, training, and knowledge transfer on disaster risk management

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

For the food security projects, there will face no more risks at the time of project implementation, very few risks will be met during distribution and cash transfer which may lead theft of distributed items such food and electronic cash transfers, AYUUB will take its role to make awareness to the beneficiaries and will also practice call for confirmation accordingly. Information from various sources will be used to assess and monitor the progress of the Project throughout its implementation.

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

AYUUB procedure is to satisfy the targeted population without challenges by practicing electronic methods, The implementation arrangements will setup accordingly for delivering the assistance. AYUUB will be transparent with the affected populations by providing them with accessible and timely information on selection criteria for targeted assistance, organizational procedures and processes that affect them. Set up accessible, confidential and well understood feedback mechanisms for suggestions and complaints with a view to improve programming, understand community perceptions, promote beneficiary empowerment and assist in detecting misconduct. Ensure all FSL staff, implementing partners and volunteers working with affected populations understand, sign and adhere to a Code of Conduct stating their commitment to respect and foster humanitarian standards and the rights of beneficiaries. AYUUB will provide clear information to the distribution actors and affected population explaining that beneficiaries do not have to pay or provide services/favours to anyone in exchange for receiving food assistance or being selected for livelihood projects.

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

The Project area is under full control of SFG, therefore, chances of occurrence for armed conflicts are very limit, but respecting the overall security trends in the country, there can be unanticipated armed conflict, that can be triggered by any means, but AYUUB, by employing its experiences and skills it has for this field of Food Security and following its Risk and Mitigation Policy in place during Project Implementation which can meaningfully contribute in the safety and access of the beneficiaries and the staff as well. AYUUB will fully and keenly put a work-plan in plan that can serve for the beneficiaries in accessing the services in a safe mode, such delivering the CASH through the modern electronic mode, safe and access to the beneficiaries, reaching the support in the home, accurate and accountable to the contractors, AYUUB will be in close collaboration with ground stakeholders, fronted by the Local Authority, recruiting security guards, crowd controllers, mobilizing the community on services and how it can help the overall community direct and indirect ways, AYUUB in applying its Risk assessment and mitigation policy will do mobilization to both beneficiaries and project staff in the respective area when need calls.
