Project Summary

This project aims to prevent morbidity and mortality among women and children in the priority districts affected by drought. Procurement of essential medicines, commodities and supplies is an important aspect of the response activities by UNICEF. This will enable a targeted 3,042,251 people to have access to health services ensuring there are no stock outs of essential medicines in all health facilities. To reduce the under five mortality rate, UNICEF will conduct capacity building trainings on case management and proper referrals of childhood illnesses among Health Workers and Community Health Workers and procure and preposition vaccines for routine immunizations and the Solar Direct Drive(SDD) Refrigerators within these districts. In addition UNICEF, will conduct activities to reduce the proportion of children born without assistance of a skilled health personnel. These include, conducting capacity building for Basic Emergency Obstretric and newborn care (BEmONC) and Emergency Triage and Treatment (ETAT) to health care workers as well as training CHWs on proper referrals to health facilities for safe delivery among pregnant women. In addition UNICEF, will support and compliment the Ministry of Health and Child Care with real time data collection from the health facilities using a Rapid Pro System

1 - Activities

Activities that will address the needs identified include, 1. Procurement of essential medicines commodities and supplies to enable people to have access to health services ensuring there are no stock outs. 2. In order to reduce the under five mortality rate, UNICEF will conduct capacity building on Health Care Workers and Community Health Workers on case management and proper referrals of childhood illnesses. 3. In addition, UNICEF will Conduct measles vaccination catch up campaigns in these districts to reduce the chances of any outbreaks. 4. UNICEF will also procure Solar Direct Drive Fridges to all health facilities for cold chain maintenance of all routine vaccines. 5. To reduce the proportion of children born without assistance of a skilled health personnel, UNICEF will conduct capacity building on BEmONC and ETAT among health care workers and train Community Health Workers on proper referrals to health facilities for safe delivery among pregnant women.6. In addition, it is important to provide incentives to skilled health workers in order to retain them at health facilities through Result Based Financing (RBF). 7. UNICEF will support and complement MOHCC data collection using the Rapid Pro inorder to collect real time data from the health facilities 8. UNICEF and MOHCC will conduct monitoring visits on the response activities in these affected districts

2 - Indicators

The success of the project will be measured through;1. Number of people with access to health services in the affected districts2. Number of children 9 -59months vaccinated against measles in the districts4. Number of Health Facilities with SDD Fridges procured and installed 5. Number of Health Workers trained on BEmONC and ETAT6. Number of CHWs trained on early identification and proper referral of pregnant women for ANC 7. Number of Skilled health workers receiving incentives through RBF 8. Number of HWs trained on Rapid Pro
