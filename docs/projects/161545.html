Project Summary

the proposed Hope 360 initiative for peace project is aimed at scaling up timely and equitable access to quality basic and vocational educational empowerment, through community mobilization, sensitization and advocacy, and capacity building. The project is targeting to reach 5500 IDP, out of school children which are in the age (4-17 years) in Nguru, Machina local government area of yobe state and Jere Local government area of Borno state and Adamawa State. 2500 children within the age bracket 4-7 years will be targeted for sensitized on basic education while 3000 children within the age bracket 8-17 years will be targeted on sensitization on both basic and vocation education children, living with disability will be mainstreamed into both. The proposed project is schedule to run for one year successful.
The project objective are.
1. To increase children access to quality basic and vocation educational through orienting education and help the children to developed knowledge, skills values and behavior needed for sustainable development to the local government Nguru, Machina of yobe state and Jere local government Borno state
2. To create awareness, sensitized an advocate to the same local government to benefit of quality basic and vocation education.
3. To incrase the safe and equitable access to sufficiency quality of education empowerment and equipped the children on various vocation through quality vocation educational capacity training.
Project Justification
In the past 5 years it is estimated that over ten thousand children in Nguru, Machina Local Area of Yobe state, Jere Local government area of Borno state are out of school children and have no access to basic and vocation education thereby living the children moving round the street and area hence increase the number of violence within LGA’s. this is due to fact that the children within this locality feel abandoned and neglected and no emergence response have been taking to solved the issue or meet to their need.
Considering the increasing number or nature of the out of school children and will still be increasing if emergence response of Nguru , Machina and Jere are not met. Gearing towards the objectives of the project Hope 360 Initiative for Peace with the HPC Fund Grant will focused on providing timely emergence response services to Nguru and Machina LGA of Yobe state which are of high risk of increasing number of out of school children follow by Jere LGA of Borno state in other to restore peaceful co-existing and development.
