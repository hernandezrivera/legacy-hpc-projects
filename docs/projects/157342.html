Project Summary

Civil conflicts & 2018 Floods has continued to compound the already fragile humanitarian situation in most parts of the SK and BN, worsening the already reeling situation from the protracted economic situation. Civil wars in Sudan & 2018 flooding remains one of the drivers of displacement and especially in the south Kordofan and BN States in Sudan. During 12th - 26th October 2018, UNOCHA UNICEF, WHO, UNDP RRR, UNHCR, COR, HAC, ARC, SRC and GAH conducted inter-agency assessment mission to assess the situation and needs of IDPs, Returnees and most vulnerable host communities. According to the inter-agency assessment report, there are more than 21,456 people, (3,576 HHs) in SK and majority are women and children in different age groups among the Returnees. Rashad village hosting 5,000 Returnees ( 833 HHs) returned from Alsaraf, Tendimin, Kalouba, Tagya etc. , 22,324 IDPs (3,576 HHs) from Tabdoun, Tindimin and other surrounding villages, Talodi hosting 13,968 IDPs (2,328 HHs) from Angarto and neighboring villages and Eliri hosting 38,882 IDPs from Warin with total of 14,000 SSR. Blue Nile is hosting about 22,000 IDPs in Bau and Geizan. There's 3,576 IDP HHs in Rashad, 12,328 IDP HHs in Talodi, 1,200 IDP HHs in Eliri in need of urgent NFI/ES Support. The shelter situation remains worse and especially in the hard hit areas mentioned above as 2018 floods swept all the makeshift shelters and especially in the IDPs settlements in Eliri, Rashad & Talodi in SK where most of the IDPs are using makeshifts. Need for provision of NFIs/ES has continued to increase as most of IDPs/Returnees are now residing in congested settlements where they have lost all their household items. Most of the infrastructure has been damaged by the flash floods and this includes shelters and latrines leaving them prone to diseases and a lack of protection for women and children, Prices of commodities have also gone up rendering the IDPs powerless and with no alternatives means of survival .Hence the need to scale up provision basic services like provision of ES/NFIs and construction of shelters. The deplorable shelter conditions and there's continuous pressure from the communities who have to share the already scarce resources.

Activities

GAH will Conduct consultative meetings with the community leaders and women, local and govt officials to discuss different interests and perspectives that converge in the locations where IDPs/Returnees have settled or are expected to settle, or are expected to benefit from the ES programs, such as this proposed program. • Select locations and beneficiaries of the ES construction. Resolution of tenure issues and site planning, including GPS mapping. This will be a participative process involving stakeholders within the community planning and rehabilitation committee (50% women) • GAH will provide technical assistance in design of emergency shelter that meets the spheres standard and as per the sector guidelines. GAH will provide vouchers to the owners to be able to secure skilled labor with the pre-selected small contractors that GAH is familiar with. GAH will only work with contractors if working with skilled IDPs/beneficiaries is not possible. Unskilled labor such as excavations, loading, unloading, and transport of materials will be expected from all able-bodied beneficiaries • Form, orient and expand CTG where they exist, consisting of local technicians and engineers, providing authorities with up-to-date information and mapping of developments in different settlements. The CTG members will be referral focal points for returnees/IDPs and will provide information to help them access services and protection available in the area. • Identify households with multiple dimensions of vulnerability according to standard UNHCR guidance and assess materials needs.Assessment will include consultation with local authorities and IDP settlement representatives and women to determine process for equitable distribution and secure locations for distribution events to mitigate risk of a security incident against staff.Registration will be conducted, with beneficiary ID cards. Conduct distribution events of standard UNHCR NFI kits to the targeted.GAH will conduct on-site and PDM.

Impact on environment/natural resources

This proposed project intends to respond to the situation by promoting long-term solutions to the needs of IDPs and vulnerable host communities. The project envisages to achieve this by contributing to the stability and resilience of communities to promote local economic development. The project will take into account gender specific needs as well as needs of other vulnerable groups while applying interventions. It will seek positive environmental footprint through the application of the best practices for natural resources management and conflict sensitivity. The project also realizes that there is a need to bring the local private sector actors to augment to the local economies and build resilience and absorption capacities of the targeted communities. due to the fact that the building materials are of flammable GAH will include fire fighting training for community volunteers and support the settlement with fire fighting tools.
