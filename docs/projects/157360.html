Project Summary

This project designed to deliver quality education services to most effected populations including DPs ,returnee and host community at Nertiti and Rekero in center Darfur state with targets estimated about 2350 children , the project aim to Construct 8 classrom in 2 primary school in( Nertiti and Rekero), establish 2 Nursery School in targeting areas, this consider a new initiative linkage between education and environment activity , the purpose of it is encourage students to participate actively to mitigate the impact of climate change as well as increase awareness of students on environment effect in our life through session learning, inaddtion to different activity as following :-
Activity1 :Construction 8 classrooms in 2 schools (4 classroom in Rekero , 4 classrooms in Nertiti) , as semi-permanent classrooms using environmentally friendly (Stabilize Soil Block SSB) and durable materials to conserve the environment by reducing wood use in construction
Activity 2 :Provide Gender sensitive WASH facilities to 2 schools (1 in Rekro , 1 in Nertiti ) , the WASH facilities including hygiene promotion ,gender sensitive hand washing facilities and gender sensitive safe drinking water point .
Activity3: Establish of 8 child clubs in 2 school (1 Primary school in Rekero ,1 primary school in Nertitti), the child club focusing on life saving ,SSP, arts, arts therapy ,drama, song ,sport ,peace ,protection ,hygiene promotion ,discover children talents and leadership (each activity will be conducted 45 day each club target at least between 50 to 60 child.
Activity4 :Conduct 12 session on role of children to actively participating in school hygiene campaign, the campaign will be conducted in 2 primary school (1 in Rekero ,1 in Nertiti), each campaign at least targeting 670 children.
Activity5 : Establish of 2 Nursery schools (1 in Rrkero,1 in Nertiti) to produce 100000 trees in one year( 50000 fruitful tree,50000 shade tree), the nursery schools will provide schools and community with trees to mitigate the negative impact in schools environment will contribute to reduction the impact of climate change and reduce carbon dioxide in the air . as well as provide raise awareness about impact of cut trees .
Activity6 :Provide and distribution of education in emergency material's to 2 school( 1in Rekero ,1 in Nertiti) including students packages, equipment blackboard, recreation material's for children (1200)
Activity7 :Conducted 2 (5 days) workshop PATs to strengthening capacity building 0n EiE ,school ,management .international child convention ,child right Sudanese child law, each workshop will targeting 25 teachers. From 2 primary school(1 Nertiti,1 Rekero )

Needs

Darfur considered one as big humanitarian operation receiving humanitarian assistance in Africa and pervious in the world ,since conflict started in 2003 between government of Sudan and movement oppositions , the Education around Jebel Marra witnessed deterioration and this made the gaps in Education sector at Nertiti and Rekero because most of people Displaced currently refer to UNICF almost the children of host community and IDPs who out of school ages estimated about 35 % to 45.according to HNO indicated that, the total population in Nertiti is 227,060 divided 128,041,retunee is 18,552, total vulnerable resident is 55689 Basis on ministry of education Data and assessment conducted , there are a lot of classrooms constructed from local materials it will damaged due rain and earth at rain season, there are shortage in education materials in emergency situation , the teachers have lack of knowledge they are need training , in the Rekero and Nertiti the schools need to be construction from fixed materials, the WASH and hygiene promotion need to enhanced because it will keep children safe from AWD and other disease as well as SSP, According to inter –agency rapid assessment which was conducted 2004 by WFP, UNICEF, OCHA, WHO, HAC SRC, and SPRC . the assessment found that, there were only 4 basic schools and 2 secondary schools . between 12-17 November 2016 , WES, HAC, MoE and UPO conducted multi-sectorial needs assessment in Nertiti and Golo, the assessment key result is , there are 51 schools I the localities with 22,000 childern (8500 grils ,13,500boys), no leering materials, there are 13,000 have not seating , PTA reported very weak, SSP needed and Low quality of teaching skills been observed , lack of trainings on EiE and lack of skills and knowledge for teachers. On period between 25 up to 29 April - , Inter-Agency Assessment Mission consist from UN (OCHA, UNICEF, WFP, UNHCR and IOM), INGOs (CRS, DRC and WR), line Ministries (SMoE, SMoSA, SMoE), WES and HAC conducted needs assessment to Rekero tow and Gimzan village in order assess the IDPs ,host community and returnee , the key result assessment found that’s, the functional schools within the affected villages were 4, with total of 1,280 (671 boys, 609 girls), only 8 temporary classrooms was built with local materials and only two classrooms have benches for sitting and no Education materials provided .there are lack of wash facilities on Rekero and Gimaza village, new classroom construction needed. For those needs which pointed , Almasheesh for Peace and Development Organization (APDO) full committed to provide education services for most effected vulnerable population especially children to enable then having access to quality education services . APDO will coordinate with line ministers, local authority and all stockholder to implementing this project with maximum standard .
