Project Summary

The project seeks to promote the access of food, water and shelter for 2300 H.H living in 11 IDP camps in Kaxda District- Mogadishu. The project works to improve food and shelter access through cash based support in a period of 6 months while also project covers the fee to access sustained water in the camps. The purpose is to safe and sustain the lives of IDPs living in the Kaxda district with the provision of integrated emergence package in a period of 6 months.

Needs

An estimated of 2,300 H.H people (around 9,200) have fled their homes in lower and middle Shabella, regions in search of safety and security due to natural disasters, armed conflict and escalating violence since 2016 and settled in 11 IDP camps in Kaxda. The overall number of IDPs living in Mogadishu are said to be nearly half a million persons mainly living in the two districts of Kaxda and Deynile. Among the identified 2,300IDP H.H, 87% were displaced in the past 12 months because of the recent conflict between the government and armed groups in both middle and lower Shabelle. Findings of RNA carried out by human appeal shows that Provision of immediate food rations, clean drinking water, basic shelter/NFI items and Psychosocial support to the new comers are identified as the most priority for the time being.According to the RNA , access to food remains challenging as 72% of the people showed no access to food since they arrived while only 16% reported that they received ONE time shot humanitarian assistance in June this year while 4% reported to engage domestic work. Also water access is challenging where people are reported to go for distances to fetch water. In addition to the distances, people have to pay higher prices to access and that is the limiting factor in place. The difficult existing in water access is exposing people to the vulnerability of water porn diseases like AWD.While few IDPs (less than 5%) have tents provided by humanitarian responders, the vast majority still live in makeshift hat shelters built of sticks, plastic sheet, straw, mud, scraps and whatever else is available. Threat of eviction and inability to afford the cost of rented accommodation was cited by 87% of the interviewed Households . The top priority shelter-related NFIs reported by IDPs were mattresses, coverlets, kitchen items, solar lights (as the electricity is not available at all inside the IDP camp) and plastic sheets or tents to protect their makeshift shelters.

Activities

To address humanitarian gabs existing in Kaxda IDP camps project provide immediate life saving and life sustaining integrated emergence support to the 2,300 IDP H.H living in Kaxda district. The project will work on:1. Provide multi-purpose cash-based support to cover the immediate needs of food and shelter in a period of 6 months. Unconditional cash with the value of $100/moth/HH will be transferred through available banking structure on monthly bases. Beneficiaries will be mobilized and engaged through all the process starting from planning and selection to the final evaluation and their input will be used. All protection concerns will be addressed accordingly on time with the help of the community. Also people will be mobilized on the efficient usage of the allocated transfers. A field based team will be hired to work with the targeted community on ensuring that beneficiaries are receiving the intended amount and using it according to the project objective.2. Provision of sustained clean water in the 11 IDP camps where target beneficiaries are living in. Project will pay the fees of drinking water and allow families collect water on daily bases. Water will be reached inside the camps for protection wise. A minimum of 60 litre/day/family will be provided. Voluntary base water management committee that is selected by IDP community will be formed to manage the daily collection of water.3. To improve sanitation and hygiene and also protection concerns, people will be trained and mobilized on maters concerning the wellbeing of the IDP community specially water management and hygiene promotion practices.

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

People living in the IDPs are vulnerable to a numerous risks including exclusion of services as there is no legal framework in place in the ground that gives protection and voices. According to the findings of rapid need assessment by human appeal (HA), 60% identified that access to services is limited only for those who have links with the camp leaders. Eldery and disabled people in the camps are not able to get assistants as they can't go out to collect. To ensure that the right people are included in the project beneficiary, project team will work very hard with the communities and there is process in place in QC that puts emphases on targeting where beneficiaries participate to approve the lists based on the agreed selection criteria. In this regard, this ensures proper targeting and avoids the risks of exclusion. targeting will be done in public with the presence of the target groups. Also people with special needs will be given special arrangement that keeps them access the assistants. Further on that vetting process will be done in the camps where all people in the camp can access and contribute the process. Camp leaders along with the project team and members from the local government will direct the meeting.

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

According to the assessment, gender based risks have been identified. GBV cases were reported by the community in the IDP. The majority of incidents are reported to being perpetrated with impunity. Many of the protection risks facing displaced women and girls are the same or similar with all women groups in Mogadishu IDPs. This project will address risks related to the gender through employing accountability to affected population framework. During planning and implementation, people will be engaged and consulted the way forward and impacts that may come up. special consideration will be given to the women and girls as they constitute the most vulnerable groups in the society. Also during distribution and targeting special consideration will be given to women and girls. Also protection concerns relating water collection will be addressed in the designing of water collection points. water management committee will be trained on ensuring safe collection of water. Also project will try to engage with local authorities to ensure risks related to the gender are mitigated through community policing.

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

Project is providing assistants to the IDP families to enable them life in dignified way, feel safe and not to go in in to the negative coping mechanism. Among the negative coping mechanism that this project preventing is joining armed groups to better manage income sources for their families. During the time frame of the project, target families will receive monthly cash support to cover the existing basic need in the family and this will promote families specially youth groups stay away from joining armed conflicts.Also to mitigate risks related to inter-conflict and violence between IDP community to include the benefit, a clear set of selection criteria will be shared with all before selection exercise started and the final list will be shared with all to ensure acceptance by the community.
