Project Summary

In terms of supporting and enhancing the Health system in Syria, and in particularly in Homs governorate, Rescate had stepped up to rehabilitate Deir Baalbeh health center.
The main objective of the project is to increase access to life-saving and life-sustaining coordinated, equitable health services for those most vulnerable and in need, through enhancing the health system capacity to support continuity of health care, and strengthen the community resilience, through sustaining and improving the delivery of primary health care services addressing chronic diseases, child and maternal health, ultimately providing life-sustaining humanitarian health assistance with an emphasis on those most at risk and in need.
Rescate plan lasts for one year of integrated interventional work based on a participatory approach with all involved and beneficiary’s parties, mainly with Ministry of Health.
Initially, the rehabilitation of the infrastructure of the buildings of the aforementioned health center, which includes; Construction work and cladding, WASH, Sewage work, Mechanical and electrical works.
The second phase is equipping the health center with primary health care medical equipment and office supplies, (furniture).

Needs

Humanitarian Situation Overview in Syria (HSOS) reported top 3 priority needs in assessed communities in Syria: Healthcare, Food Security, Livelihoods. The Humanitarian Response Plan (HRP) of the Health Sector stated that: Throughout 2018, health needs in Syria continued to be exacerbated by access constraints, damage to health facilities, and insufficient human resources. Many areas that witnessed escalation in hostilities and changes in control are suffering from disruption of critical health services, access constraints, and an increased need for humanitarian interventions. The health system struggles to respond to trauma and rehabilitation needs. High numbers of trauma-related cases continue to be reported across Syria. Attacks on health facilities and health staff has remained one of the main drivers impeding access to health care, impacting the functionality of the health facilities themselves as well as the availability of personnel needed to provide the necessary care. From January to September 2018, the Health Resources Availability Monitoring System (HeRAMS) reported that 46 per cent of health facilities across Syria were either non-functional or partially functional. According to the global Surveillance System for Attacks on Health Care (SSA)6 - to track attacks in Syria, Used by the World Health Organization WHO; Syria now accounts for a staggering 70% of all reported attacks on health care facilities documented by WHO worldwide. In the first half of 2018, there were more attacks on health care in Syria than for the whole of 2017. Hundreds of thousands of Syrians are being deprived of essential health care due to the continuing destruction of the country’s hospitals and clinics.(30) Health centers out of (220) in Homs are out service, addition to losses in the private hospitals, Deir Baalba is one of the areas of the city of Homs in Syria. The neighborhood consists of two sections, first Deir Baalba district, and consists of four neighborhoods are the Deir Baalba North and South, Hay Al Bayada and Al-Kasra district, Deir balaba is located in the north-east of Homs on the road to a Alslmia city. The second section is the agricultural land of Deir Baalba. The population of Deir Baalba was estimated at 85,000 (2011). The number of returnees up to date is 17,000, and the residing population at 15000, whereas IDPs from other areas are 2000 individuals. Number of cases of people with disability 100 cases, Percentage of men is 40%, and percentage of women and children is 60%. At the end of intervention, at least a population of (34000) will benefit directly from the facility as they are in dire need to health care.

Protection Risk Analysis

In order to ensure protection mainstreaming, Rescate applies the “Do No Harm” approach, to strengthen the role of the health system in addressing violence, in particular against women and girls, and against children, Rescate through rehabilitating and strengthening the health services is trying to address the VAW Violence against Women because: - Abused women more likely to seek health services, - Violence is an underlying cause of injury and ill health, - Most women attend health services at some point, especially sexual and reproductive health. The clinics will cover RH, Children, and general health care which will assist in alleviating the risks of mal-health in the communities nearby. Confidentiality will be ensured for all patients and full care at equal footing will be ensured in cooperation with health directorate in Homs.Rescate will present the organizations core values, mandate and vision to all stakeholders during the allocation of the project. Rescate will also ensure active involvement at all times of the Ministry of Health and other stakeholders, ensuring that the activities are accessible to everyone for examination and inspection. Rescate will ensure mitigation measures are taken in consultation with other partners from the sector so nothing will go wrong during the implementation of the project. Rescate will provide the services in consultation with stakeholders and we will coordinate with relevant partners to ensure do no harm.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Rescate in collaboration with the Ministry of Health/Directorate of Health in Homs will ensure that: health services are accessible to women and children (girls especially), Implement the Minimum Initial Services Package of reproductive health in emergency situations (MISP) within the rehabilitated health facilities, Motivate and support the medical staff, Involve and inform the community. Initialize awareness program about violence against women.Rescate through enhancing the health system and coordination with MoH/DOH-Homs will, ensure that all staffs are aware of and abide by medical confidentiality, ensure all staffs are aware of standards in consulting with children, Disseminate and inform all partners on codes of conduct for staff, Provide staff at health centers with clear protocols and sufficient supplies and equipment. Rescate will focus throughout the project period on how to:- Ensure appropriate, non-offensive, non-stigmatizing ways of making people aware of sexual violence and HIV-related services, and how to Make the community aware of services available at the health center including HIV-related prevention, care, support and treatment services (e.g. prevention of mother-to-child transmission services), and finally, how to ensure men’s access to health care and counselling including HIV-related services, and provide them with information about women’s reproductive health and about the health risks to the community of sexual violence.
