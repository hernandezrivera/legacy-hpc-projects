Project Summary

The International Organization for Migration (IOM) in Chad has been tracking and monitoring complex mobility dimensions since 2014. IOM’s Displacement Tracking Matrix (DTM) was first activated in 2014 to respond to the influx of Chadian returnees in the southern part of the country as a result of the Central African crisis. In 2015, the DTM was further activated in the Lac region to respond to the displacement crisis resulting from the Boko Haram insurgency. In 2017, another component of the DTM was deployed in Northern Chad to track and monitor migration flows, whether these were intra-regional or towards Europe. Since the beginning of 2018, IOM Chad has observed steady and increasing economically motivated migration flows through the north of Chad bordering Libya, presumably resulting from limited economic opportunities, on ongoing food security crisis, and imposed austerity measures. In order to continue providing crucial information on displacement and migration dimensions, IOM’s intervention will focus on three pillars: i) mobility tracking and stability index; ii) emergency tracking and iii) flow monitoring.

1 - Besoins, Activités et Resultats Attendus

This programme will contribute to the continuation of IOM Chad’s Displacement Tracking Matrix (DTM) activities in the country. Information provided by IOM is being used by the humanitarian community to inform live-saving humanitarian actions, specifically in the Lake Chad basin and northern Chad and provide baseline information for the nationalization of the Global Compact for Migration which feeds into the transition towards development, specifically for communities affected by forced displacement. IOM’s multidimensional DTM allows the provision of accurate and timely data informing decision-making processes for both humanitarian and development actors. To this end, IOM has been collaborating closely with the World Bank, the Lake Chad Basin Commission, the African Union, and humanitarian partners in providing data related to mobility dimensions in Chad to inform the multi-annual assistance framework of the World Bank, the Africa Union and Lake Chad Basin Commission stabilization strategy of the Lake Chad Basin, the Regional Stabilization Facility, the United Nations Development Assistant Framework, the Humanitarian Response Plan, the Humanitarian Needs Overview, and the Chadian National Development Plan.
