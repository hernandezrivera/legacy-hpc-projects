Project Summary

This project will aim at increasing timely and equitable access to safe water, sanitation, and hygiene services to 73,400 (women, men, girls, and boys) emergency-affected populations in Eastern Lakes State and the Eastern Equatoria States. HELP-eV has been responding to the ongoing humanitarian crisis since the onset of the conflict in the country both in conflicted-affected states and noncrisis states. The project objectives are;
- Ensure provision to affected populations with safe, equitable, and sustainable access to sufficient quantity of water for drinking, cooking, and personal and domestic hygiene (15 L/p/day).
- Ensure that the targeted populations have access to safe, sanitary, and hygienic living environment through the provision of sanitation services that are secure, sanitary, user-friendly and gender-appropriate.
- Ensure that affected populations have reduced risk of WASH¬ related diseases, or negative impacts on nutritional status, through access to improved hygienic practices, hygiene promotion and delivery of hygiene products and services on a sustainable and equitable basis.
HELP-eV will support WASH Cluster response in locations of critical malnutrition in Eastern Lakes and the Eastern Equatoria States by ensuring the provision of a minimum standard of improved basic WASH services in the areas. The activities include; comprehensive rehabilitation of hand pumps, distribution of WASH NFIs, Improving latrines, and hygiene promotion campaigns both at institutional and community levels HELP-eV will ensure that WASH services, specifically in regards to latrine construction, incorporate the minimum safety and privacy considerations with use of the WASH Cluster Latrine Checklist, developed with the GBV Sub¬Cluster. HELP-eV will work with WASH Cluster and NFI/ES, Logs and other clusters to ensure adequate supplies are strategically pre¬positioned in the field to respond to new displacement and potential disease outbreaks through the 2019 rainy season. HELP-eV will maintain its active participation in the WASH Cluster meetings both at the state and National levels where ideas and project updates are shared, and monthly WASH reports are submitted. HELP-eV will encourage the active participation of the local community including women and children in the implementation of the proposed project. Through Consultation of local authorities at all levels, women, men, boys, and girls in the selection of the project areas within the States based on the identified needs. Hygiene promoters including hand pump technicians will include both host community, returnees and IDPs men, women, boys and girls in the targeted project sites in the county. The local authorities, host community, and IDPs men, women, girls and boys will be involved in the design of the community and institutional sanitation facilities putting into consideration the need for privacy for females for the case of latrines and bathrooms construction. This project is expected to complement current Help-eV WASH activities in the locations and will integrate with our Nutrition and FSL activities.

Needs

In the 2019 HNO, it is estimated that the number of people that will require emergency WASH services has increased by 7% as evidenced by the improved WASH data collection in support of REACH. In South Sudan, women and girls face an increased risk of harassment and sexual violence during the collection of water and use of the few functional sanitation facilities and lack of access to menstrual hygiene products. IDPs in non-camp settings and returnees also have added pressure and stretched to the capacity of the host community in utilizing the scarce WASH services. Access to a safe water supply is constrained by long distances to collect water and frequent breakdowns. 48% of families in hard-to-reach locations reported that the closest source of safe water is farther than 30 minutes away. The distance to a water source has a known correlation with the volume of water available to the household, and in these conditions, practicing positive hygiene behaviors, like handwashing, becomes a significantly lower priority than using the water for drinking or cooking. According to JMP data, only about 30% of the population have access to a functional improved water source within 30 minutes of their dwellings without facing GBV and protection concerns. The remaining population thus go to extreme lengths and take a lot of time to access water from protected sources with some relying on surface water or unprotected water sources.Most of the communities in South Sudan practice open defecation as they do not have access to or are not accustomed to having basic sanitation facilities. Access to WASH NFIs for South Sudanese is limited as only about 15% can access them due to the effects of the conflict on the economy which has limited WASH commodities in the markets and also the population lacking the purchasing capacity due to the high cost of materials. High levels of insecurity and malnutrition in the country combined with poor access to WASH services and NFIs has had an adverse impact on the health of the vulnerable communities. In 2018 Help-eV through its WASH interventions in Eastern Lakes and Eastern Equatoria State has been able to evidence and get community feedback that there are still huge WASH needs and this is expected to rise with incoming returnees due to the recently signed peace process that is expected to improve the security situation in the Country.With this project, Help-eV is looking to provide essential services to the affected population of 85,600 of which 25,679 will be children under 18, 55,641 and 4280 adults. This population will include 4,280 IDPs, 2568 Returnees and remaining comprising of host community.

Activities

Help-eV WASH activities will focus on:1. Access to safe and sufficient water increased for families of malnourished children, PLWs, and IDPs in host communities through the following activities:a. Rehabilitation of water points based on SPHERE guidelines ensuring safe, sufficient and equal access to men, women, boys, and girls.b. Establishment/reactivation, capacity building and provision of materials to Water User Committees, with women representation at least 50% in all the target areas; includes capacity building of PMA, RWSS, local institutions, and other stakeholders to better support communities during emergencies.c. Provision of water treatment products and water quality testing2. Knowledge of appropriate hygiene and sanitation practices promoted for families of malnourished children, PLWs, and IDPs in host communities through the following activities:a. Hygiene promotion sessions on good hygiene practices to men, boys, women, and girls through awareness sessions at village and nutrition sites level, and door to door sensitization.b. Promotion of combined PHAST/CLTS approach at households while addressing accessibility, safety and security issues for women, girls and people with specific needs (disabled, chronic disease).c. Distribution of hygiene kits and WASH NFIs to affected people, including personal hygiene & MHM materials for women and girls.d. Training of community hygiene promotion volunteers on key hygiene and sanitation promotion practices and provision of IEC materials.e. Organization of training and peer to peer sessions with mothers of malnourished children to promote good hygiene practices (prevent worms, diarrhea, malaria).3. Access to adequate, safe, secure, dignified and gender appropriate sanitation services improved for families of malnourished children, PLWs, and IDPs in host communities through the following activities:a. Construction of household latrinesb. Promotion of combined PHAST/CLTS approach at households while addressing accessibility, safety and security issues for women, girls and people with specific needs (disabled, chronic disease).c. Construction/rehabilitation of sanitation infrastructure (latrines, hand washing stations) in nutrition sites, including accessibility, safety, and security for women, girls, disabled persons; includes the provision of waste management facilities.for women, girls, disabled persons; includes the provision of waste management facilities.
