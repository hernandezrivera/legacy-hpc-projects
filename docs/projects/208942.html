Project Summary

Plan international through this project will provide life saving and emergency health services in 21 health centers through out 21 localities across the 4 targeted programme areas: White Nile (Ad Diwaim, Aj Jabalain, As Salam / Ar Rawat, Guli, Kosti, Rabak, Tendalti, Um Rimta), North Kurdufan (Sheikan), Kassala (Madeinat Kassala, Reifi Aroma, Reifi Gharb Kassala, Halfa Aj Jadeedah, Reifi Nahr Atbara, Reifi Shamal Ad Delta, Reifi Wad Elhilaiw, Gedaref (Al Mafaza, Al Galabat Al Gharbyah - Kassab, Gala'a Al Nahal, Madeinat Al Gedaref, Al Qureisha).The project targets 103,000 individual in White Nile, Kassala, North Kurdufan, and Gedaref. Among them is 27,000 above 18 years old female 21,000 male above 18 years old, 30,000 Female age below 18 years old and 25,000 males under 18 years old. The targeted localities selected from PIS operation areas determined by the HNO severities of health needs, where significant proportion of population lacking access to basic services due to unavailability and poor coverage of outreach services, and also from the area affected by armed conflict or received IDPs from conflict areas. The project link to Humanitarian Response Plan 2024 Strategic Objective 1: Provide safe, timely, principled and gender-responsive life-saving humanitarian assistance to the most vulnerable groups to reduce mortality and morbidity in areas with high severity of need or at risk of a further deterioration of critical humanitarian needs. And to Strategic objective 3: Provide safe, equitable, dignified and unhindered access to critical basic services and livelihoods opportunities for the most vulnerable groups to prevent further erosion of their coping abilities. The health response will be aligned with health cluster strategy, Sector Objective 1: Support prioritised quality health services to PiN with focus on strong primary health care. Focus on accessible areas prioritizing pre-identified facilities, including existing emergency obstetric and new-born care facilities, to support with a comprehensive package of services (including operational cost, fuel and WASH in health facilities). In addition, the project activities will link to Sector Objective 2: Strengthen emergency preparedness, response, and all-hazards emergency risk management. Through reactivating the disease surveillance system and reporting and supplying the static vaccination centres with the provision of needed supplies will be a priority. By support provision of emergency life saving health services in the targeted areas in the 4 targeted states and reduce the impact of the conflict on the health system by strengthening the capacity to respond timely to different emergencies and health out breaks by provisions of quality health services, including treatment of communicable & non-communicable disease and trauma , reproductive health services, support of EPI coverage enhance the clinics infrastructure provision of essential drug and medical supplies, capacity building of health services providers to response timely during the emergency and conduct mobile clinics .Plan International will address the urgent needs by support provision of free accessible quality lifesaving health services in the targeted localities we will also work with health authorities across our programme areas to ensure availability of life saving medications& medical supplies ,build capacity of healthcare workers to promptly respond to health need of the targeted population ,we will also ensure improve the infrastructures of the health facilities ,enhance the reporting mechanism by expand the reporting capacity of the early warning systems, Strengthen emergency preparedness& Support health authorities to carryout timely response to emergencies and disease outbreaks. The project identified and prioritized the most at-risk individuals in the project areas as project participants, the common thread however, was that they were selected from IDPs, and the host communities in the project areas
