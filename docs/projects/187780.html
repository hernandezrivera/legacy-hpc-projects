Project Summary

This project is designed to provide integrated health, nutrition, food security, and hygiene promotion services in 96 different communities in NWS in Idlib and Aleppo for 2022&2023.
The proposed project will reach 175,000 people (75.000 PLWS out of them 2000 PWD) and 100,000 CU5 (52,000 G, 48,000 B) out of them 5000 CWD) through supporting 6 Nutrition centers, and 25 nutrition teams / RRTs in addition to establishing 20 mother-baby areas. The nutrition centers and RRTs will provide IYCF and CMAM services through different modalities and will conduct the following center-based/outreach activities:
IYCF; Breastfeeding and lactation counseling, complementary feeding counseling will be provided for mothers and caregivers of children under two years, also a Provision of USD$20 top-up cash voucher for identified households to ensure they have access to recommended diet and nutrition for a period of 6 months.
CMAM; Screening and treating malnutrition cases (SAM and MAM) for PLWs and children under 5 that do not show signs of any medical complications, those with medical conditions are referred for support to the appropriate field partners, also distribute micronutrients for PLWS and children to address the micronutrient deficiencies among the PLWs and children. Distribute fortified blended foods to prevent stunting among the children in the targeted areas.
Moreover,20 mother feeding spaces will be established in 20 different camps in Maaret Tamsrin and Harim where the lowest proportion of Exclusive Breastfeeding is found that provides a safe space for lactating mothers to breastfeed their children. Each space will have 1 nutritionist and 2 CHWs who will be responsible for providing IYCF sessions and MUAC screening.
Also, this project will involve empowering mothers and HH members through training to screen their own children for malnutrition by teaching them how to use color-coded Mid-Upper Arm Circumference (MUAC) tapes and check for Odema.

4 - Needs

SMART survey conducted Jun 2021 in Idleb and Rural Aleppo showed an increasing rate of GAM by MUAC from 0.9% in 2019 to 4.7%, while the GAM rate by WHZ increased from 0.73% in 2019 to 2.5%, both differences by MUAC and by WHZ were statistically significant, this increase maybe can be explained by the wave of displacement started in 2019 and the economic deterioration due to the inflation and the decreasing of the purchasing value of the Syrian pounds, all these causes had been exacerbated by the Covid-19 outbreaks that led a lot of people to lose their work. Also, the prevalence of stunting was found to be 24.5%.The prevalence of anemia was found to be 46% among children 6 – 59 months, while the prevalence of anemia among WRA was 53.85%.Maternal Malnutrition continues to be a major public health problem in NWS especially in areas affected by displacements where 10.8% of Women of Reproductive Age (WTA) are malnourished, while this rate was higher between PLWs 12.4% According to the nutrition, surveillance report had conducted in September 2021 that included 9 districts, 35 Sub-district, and 50 spatial points in the northwest Syria surveillance team had reported.The overall proportion of acute based on MUAC in northwest Syria, GAM (global acute malnutrition) rate was 1.6%,SAM (severe acute malnutrition) 0.2%,MAM (moderate acute malnutrition) 1.4 %, MAM among PLWs 9.9%. Stunting was %33.15 according to the facility surveillance system which is considered extremely critical

5 - Implementation of activities

25 RRTs and 6 nutrition centers will work in parallel and in coordination with the available health facilities in the target areas. This response will provide integrated health, FSL, nutrition, child protection, and hygiene promotion.-Screen PLWs and children under 5 and refer the ones with dangers signs and medical problems to the available health facilities.-Assess and screen nutritional status among PLWs and children under 5 and distribute nutritional supplies to them.-Treatment of malnutrition cases among children and PLWsAlso, a pre and rapid coordination combined with referrals will be in place if the team notices or identifies any sign of abuse or violence among children under 5 and or mothers. All cases of violence against women and children will be referred to a pre-identified center.-Provision of USD$20 top-up cash voucher for 1000 pregnant and caregivers to ensure they have access to recommended diet and nutrition for a period of 6 months. This response will adopt the recommendation of the cluster in COVID-19 context to minimize the risk of infection for staff and beneficiaries Also, this project will involve empowering mothers and HH members through training to screen their own children for malnutrition by teaching them how to use color-coded Mid-Upper Arm Circumference (MUAC) tapes and check for OdemaAll reports are collected on a daily basis using UNICEF's COBO tool. 10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Mercy-USA will maintain protection mainstreaming in all project steps as follows: • List of PLWs and Children under 5 will be identified, including disabled and chronically ill children and mothers.• Monitor unaccompanied and separated children admitted into nutrition programs and coordinating with staff in terms of defaulters; • The project is designed to identify any sign of abuse or violence among PLW and children, all cases will be referred to pre-identified centers• A confidential complaint and protection reporting mechanism system will be designed and beneficiaries know where to refer or report incidents or violations.•Mercy-USA has different channels for information dissemination which includes but not limited to WhatsApp groups, IEC materials, fixed posters and banners •PSEA messaging and awareness-raising will be done with beneficiaries through one or more of the following (track and report):- PSEA posters will be distributed to beneficiaries- PSEA banners will be placed at the project site/community center (or other service centers)- PSEA videos will be shared with beneficiaries on social media platforms / played at service centers- PSEA messaging/videos and hotline information will be shared with beneficiaries through SMS/WhatsApp messaging• All project staff will be trained on GBV prevention and response in emergencies, in order to ensure full adherence with Do No Harm principle with strict dealing with Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (SEA) and GBV issues
