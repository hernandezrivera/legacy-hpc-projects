Project Summary

The ongoing emergency humanitarian crises in the NW and SW regions of Cameroon continues to deteriorate with increasing displacement and attacks by belligerent groups. Before the crisis, nutrition has never been considered in Cameroon especially in the NW/SW. The Multi-Sector Needs Assessment (MSNA) conducted in August 2020 in the NWSW regions showed low coverage of nutrition services in the assessed villages highlighting the need to scale up nutrition services. Only 25 percent of the assessed villages reported availability of nutrition services/response. While 65 percent of these villages reported the availability of nutrition screening, only 24 percent reported that the treatment of severe acute malnutrition (SAM) was available. Access to basic health services is a more challenge due to insecurity and persistent attack on health care services. Food insecurity, poor sanitary conditions and repeated displacement which are major indicators of malnutrition is likely to deteriorate this 2021. Rapid emergency food security assessments (EFSA) conducted in October 2019 revealed a precarious situation with a proxy GAM of 5.1% and 7.1% in North West (NW) and South West (SW) regions respectively. Cumulatively, 840 SAM cases have been identified and treated in 2020 reflecting 33 percent of the cluster target for 2020. 50% of IDPs in the North West are food insecure(52,080 people) with 13% being severely food insecure (13,650 people). SHUMAS intervention will be to improve the nutritional status of children under 5 years, adolescent girls and boys 5-9 years, pregnant and lactating women affected by the crisis in 6 health districts in NW and SW regions through micronutrient supplementation, BSFP, Iron and Folic acid, homemade fortified food, IYCF sensitization, screening for malnutrition and referrals.
The main objective is contribute towards the reduction of Morbidity and Mortality linked to life-threatening forms of malnutrition in boys and girls under 5, adolescent’s girls and pregnant and lactating women in 6 health districts in the North West (Kumbo West, Ndu, Kumbo East, Ako and Nkambe) and South West (Mamfe) Regions of Cameroon for 12 months.
We will target the following health areas per district; Kumbo West (Meluf, Kitiwum, Kumbo CMA, Kikiakom, Kumbo urban, Nkum Nkuv, Mbveh and Melim; Kumbo East (Noi, Nkar, Wasi Ber, CMA Jakiri, Mbokam, Wainamah, Sop; Ndu (Mbiyeh, Ndu Urban, Ntumbaw) Nkambe (Kungi, Binka, Nkambe Urban, Dumbu, Misaje), Ako (Berabe, Ako, Abuenshie, Akwaja) and Mamfe (Bachuo Akagbe, Mamfe, Tali).
And the specific objectives will be;
• To promote infant and young child feeding in emergencies for Complementary Feeding (including breastfeeding) in 15,000 care givers in 6 health districts in NW/SW for 12 months
• To provide micronutrient supplementation programmes for Children under five years (4000 M, 6000F) support in 6 health districts in NW/SW for 12 months
• To provide BSFP supplementary ration to 3058 children 6-23 months and 1985 PLW givers in 6 health districts in NW/SW for 12 months
• To provide micronutrient supplementation programme for 2000 adolescent and 1985 Pregnant Women in 6 health districts in NW/SW for 12 months.
• To provide Integrated management of severe acute malnutrition in children 6-59months in 6 health districts in NW/SW for 12 months
• To Support Community engagement for Behaviour and social change in 6 health districts in NW/SW for 12 months
We are the cluster Co-Lead for the NW/SW regions of Cameroon and also SAG for NW.
