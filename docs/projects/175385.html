Project Summary

The project will provide life-saving and life-sustaining assistance to 5.000 children from IDPs and host families, ensuring continuous access to quality and inclusive primary education in WASH safe schools in host communities, in Nampula city.
The project will focus on contributing to the following HRP education cluster objectives:
• Ensure all displaced children in host families or accommodation and relocation centres have access to education services;
• Ensure displaced and other vulnerable children and those affected by cyclone Kenneth and COVID 19 have access to quality and relevant education and protection services, including through distance education;
• Facilitate all children in the safe return to schools or TLS.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project aims at providing continuous access to quality and inclusive education for 5000 primary education school children aged 10-18 from displaced and host vulnerable families), in Nampula city, targeting the following locations (bairros): in the following “bairros”/ communities: Nampaco, Namicopo and Namutequelia Expansão (3 EPC schools).The project will contribute to the following education cluster HRP objectives: • Ensure all displaced children in host families or accommodation and relocation centres have access to education services; • Ensure displaced and other vulnerable children and those affected by cyclone Kenneth and COVID 19 have access to quality and relevant education and protection services, including through distance education; • Facilitate all children in the safe return to schools or TLS.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Community Participation: • Facilitate, in coordination with DPEDH/SDEJT, the creation of a reference group formed by existing School Councils Commitees, members from host communities as well as members from IDP families to establish mechanisms for mitigation of any conflict that may arise and to set up jointly a complaint and feedback mechanism;• Consult and revise, with the reference group, the prioritization of the education activities, particularly for girls, and capacitate them to achieve the goals of the education plan with support of community; Access and Learning Environment:• Organize distance education sessions via community radio and through support of teachers and parents/caregivers for IDP students with focus on literacy and numeracy (before re-opening of the schools);• Facilitate support sessions for IDP students to gain coping skills to promote their own health and protection and prevent risk of COVID-19 infection, based on the Coping with COVID-19 methodology;• Facilitate parenting sessions to provide parents/caregivers a better understanding of the crisis on the children, promote self-care practices and positive parenting skills and accompanying their children on studying; • Provision of hand washing stations with soap to the schools hosting IDP students and rehabilitate water and sanitation facilities, with privacy for girls to manage MHM;• Equip schools with cleaning package and train relevant school staff in cleaning schedules and techniques to manage hygiene within the school environment;• Provide supplemental home study when schools are reopen, through distance education to complement the reduced time of teaching due to COVID-19 prevention measures and increased number of students enrolled; • Provide learning materials to IDPs students and other vulnerable students among the host communities; • Support the development of MHPSS skills among teachers and ensure cascade of coping skills among the children; • Raise awareness among school communities and communities at large on harmful gender norms, SGBV through radio and community sessions;Teaching and Learning:• Provide teaching materials and recreational kits for PSS support to host schools;• Provide teachers with capacity and resources to continue to support their students via distance and community radio;• Train Education personnel and teachers according to their needs on EiE, PSS, CPIE.The project will be implemented in Nampula city in the following “bairros”/ communities: Nampaco, Namicopo and Namutequelia Expansão (3 EPC schools).

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

• The project will identify and understand the IDPs capacities and coping mechanisms through a community-based approach, which will allow us to be more aware of the broader political context, to recognize and understand changes in roles and power relations between women and men, young and old, and majority and minority groups, and to work with leaders and all members of the community to ensure respect for rights and equality. This will help us to ensure that our interventions do not undermine community structures which can provide protection and support.• By working closely with the community, the project will be better informed about the protection risks faced by different groups, depending on their age, gender and background.Collaboration with government, national and international non-governmental partners is also essential to provide protection and assist persons of concern in claiming their rights to humanitarian assistance.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

• A consultation process will be carried out through meetings with stakeholders and with the relevant government authorities, aiming at clear identification of IDPs in host families, as well an assessment of education needs among the students mechanism that Plan International uses include suggestion boxes, community engagement meetings, participation in meetings, surveys and evaluations, help desk, and the national emergency green line “1458”. In all these methods, we ensure the mechanisms are child friendly. These child friendly mechanisms promote gender and age-appropriate, inclusive, safe and confidential ways for children and young people to receive information, provide feedback and meaningfully participate in influencing humanitarian programming. A reference group will be created, formed by existing School Councils Committees, members from host communities and members from IDP families, which will be critical to establish mechanisms for conflict mitigation and to set up complaint and feedback mechanism.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The project will follow the guiding principles of working IDPs with disabilities. These include Rights-based approach, with actions targeting persons with disabilities informed by the human rights principles and standards codified in the UN Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (CRPD). These include principles of nondiscrimination, respect for the inherent dignity, autonomy and independence of the individual, gender equality, respect for difference and diversity, and respect for the rights of women and children. The identification of people with special needs will be done through data regularly collected and shared by INGC. The project will validate the existing data through direct engagement with IDPs management committees.To remove barriers to their access to assistance, the project will include people with disabilities, their families and caregivers in all community-based participatory assessments, provision of training and awareness-raising programs on their rights, to empower them to participate in community affairs and decision-making processes.Particular attention will be given to promotion of the inclusion of people with all types of disabilities in community leadership positions (e.g., through quotas/reserved seats/awareness raising). The referral group formed will be consulted to ensure the children with disabilities will be integrated in the project.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

We will work closely with the schools and community structures to assess and mitigate any environmental risks that can also pose a risk, such as the water provision and sanitation, waste management. We will also identify potential environmental impacts and necessary mitigations particularly related to the WASH rehabilitation in the schools. We will also monitor any natural hazards, such as floods and in coordination with relevant stakeholders prepare a contingency plan to mitigate and manage potential risks and reduce people’s vulnerability.
