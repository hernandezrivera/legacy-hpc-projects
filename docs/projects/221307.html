Project Summary

This project focuses on addressing key humanitarian needs in the NWSW regions of Cameroon, particularly for displaced, returnee, and host communities. The intervention aims to improve conflict resolution mechanisms related to housing, land, and property (HLP), enhance economic livelihoods, and improve access to basic services, including water, sanitation, and hygiene (WASH).
The project seeks to address those most urgent humanitarian needs in the NWSW regions of Cameroon through a comprehensive approach that combines legal empowerment and comunity dispute resolution, economic support, and improvements in basic services. By strengthening conflict resolution mechanisms, enhancing livelihoods, and improving access to water, sanitation, and hygiene, the project will contribute to building resilience and improving the overall well-being of affected populations. 1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The proposed intervention seeks to improve social cohesion, build acceptance and tolerance towards displaced persons by host communities and vice versa, in favour of fostering safe access to housing land and property, WASH services and food and livelihood opportunities for vulnerable persons amidst crises. Crisis in the Northwest and Southwest regions of Cameroon, have led to influx of IDPs pendular movements, with communities struggling over limited lifesaving resources and properties. Consequently, there are high rates of community disputes over housing land and property rights, and the most vulnerable are left unprotected. Moreover, the displacement affected populations struggle to gain access food and livelihood, and there is a high pressure by host communities, IDPs and returnees over limited WASH services available in the community. NRC proposes a holistic response to the abovementioned needs by delivering an integrated response initiative, building on previously established community social cohesions centers.The intervention will specifically aim to: - Strengthen targeted communities' Capacity to Prevent and Resolve Conflicts Related to Housing, Land, and Property (HLP). - Improve the Economic Stability and Livelihoods for Affected Communities- Improve Access to Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene (WASH) Services
