Project Summary

This program aims to enhance the food security for IDPs and host in NW Syria who have been affected during approximately 13 years of conflict and the affected people by the earthquake, around 8236 HHs / 43650 individuals will be benefited from this program who meet the vulnerability and eligibility criteria (IDPs, PWD, Poor, elderly, head by child or women).
The interventions contribute to help the most vulnerable people to alleviate their suffering from the lack of food security, and secure sustain private income through supporting several modalities as follow:
Cash Vouchers Assistance:
This intervention has been allocated by SCHF to be a part of multisectoral project. 325 HHs/1625 individuals IDPs and host will be benefited from this project for 5 rounds starts in Jan until May, HA team will support those people through a food voucher in value 65 USD to discharge it for the most nutritional food items in Qourqeena S. district market. The proposed items provided as a BOQ from CWG cluster.
Food Basket Distribution:
The intervention will support 2000 HHs/ 10000 individuals with a food basket distribution which contains 20 nutritional food items as (proteins, carbohydrate, phosphor, etc.), this will be distributed in April 2024 for one round in Ma'arretmisrin camps.
Free Bread Distribution:
The proposed intervention aims to target 711 HH / 3665 IDPs with daily free bread distribution that will be designed according to the FSL cluster guidelines. In Ma'aretmisrin (Kelly) camps, the allocated bread bundles around 135000 will be distributed for the camps during 6 months starts in Jan until Jun 2024. HA team considering the criteria of BWG cluster that the sufficient allocation of bread bundle per a person should be 200g daily.
Monitoring mechanisms starts from data collection and verification passing by monitoring bakery working and distribution process, PDM conduct after each month of distribution.
Cash for food:
This project designed to be a cash distribution modality for 850 HHs / 4250 individuals in Idlib camps. Each family will be benefited with 50 USD for one round make them able to procure their needs of food items.
Cooked Meals distribution:
This intervention allocated to be a cooked meal distribution in Ramadan 2024 for the people who are fasting supporting them with nutritional meal as (rice, chicken, salad) 3200 HHs / 16000 individuals will be targeted through this project. HA team will contract with a kitchen to provide the hot meals to the beneficiaries received to the camps with good and healthy status.
Olive Supply Chain:
HAT will implement a saplings distribution in 5 sub-districts in Idlib governorate, this allocation funded by HA UK to support the food security through olive cultivation in NW Syria in targets:
1- Increase the olive production in NW Syria.
2- Contribute to increase the green lands in NW Syria.
52,500 olive saplings will be distributed in the first half of February in (Idlib, Harem, Ma'arretmisrin, Bennish, Salqine) for 1050 farmers whom owned at least 2.5 donums of lands, each HH will be benefited by 50 saplings to plant by himself. HAT conducted a need assessment and registration process in the mentioned areas. This intervention will secure a good livelihood income to the beneficiaries after 3-4 years by selling the olive crops when it starts fruiting and contribute to enhance the food security for the families.
Wheat Value Chain:
HA team planning to support 100 farmers in NW Syria with the harvest stage through vouchering process. A voucher value will be around 100 USD to be paid for the harvest supplier for one hectare to each farmer.
In all implementation, HA team considering the data collection and need assessment from the field, after that verify from the beneficiaries. The post monitoring process is one of the most important activities which conduct by MEAL staff after the implementation has been done. HA programmes team try to apply the clusters' criteria in all projects.
