Project Summary

This project aims to provide life-saving support to contribute to the reduction of morbidity associated with malnutrition and prevent livelihood depletion of conflict affected newly displaced persons through responding to immediate food security & livelihood and protection needs of the target population at Nyala south and North of South Darfur state State, through improved access to agricultural and livestock inputs and provision of livelihoods support for 5,000 individuals ( males :1000 females 2000 children 2000 ) is from destitute host communities, IDPS, returnees and nomads. The intervention aims to address immediate FSL needs through direct provision of agricultural and livestock inputs including: provision of community-based veterinary services including vaccination and vouched treatment of 20,000 heads of livestock belonging to 4,000 individuals in the 2 localities, to contribute to the beneficiaries’ livestock assets protection and reduce morbidity and mortality rates. Increased animals movement due to large scale displacement of good and affected populations both animals and humans at risk of diseases,. In this activity: 40,000 doses of animal vaccines will be provided, including: sheep pox, PPR, HS, and BQ, CAHWs kits and veterinary drugs will be provided to CAHWs for animals’ treatment. Provision of quality assorted seeds mainly legumes including: chick pea, pigeon pea and vegetable seeds including: okra, watermelon and tomatoes to 5,000 vulnerable individuals, in addition to provision of hand tools including: hoes and rakes, this activity aims to enable beneficiaries ensure self-reliance, increase their dietary intake and diversification and will place special focus on women who are involved in farming activities in targeted areas. To enable the targeted communities to withstand current crises and improve their resilience; restorative livelihood activities and Income Generating Activities (IGAs) will be undertaken including: capacity building in small scale business management and 250 women will be divided in 5 groups comprising of 50 in each and will be targeted by training on manufacturing of fuel efficient stoves (FES) and provision of start-up kits, FES decrease firewood consumption hence reduce the time spent by women collecting firewood and their exposure to abuse and SGBV in remote areas, in this activity also promotes environmental conservation as a result of reduced firewood extraction, and reduces health and safety risks associated with using traditional three stone ¦repackages whilst mainstreaming gender and environmental protection. IGAs target the most vulnerable women and girls to create self-employment, improve their income and to further contribute to women empowerment.

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

The project is designed to ensure that beneficiaries safely access their services, withoutcausing harm, and promoting meaningful access, accountability and participationof beneficiaries in the provision of FSL assistance. If some individuals, for example,older persons or persons with disabilities, cannot access the services , the project willensure that special arrangements are made to bring services for them. It should not beassumed that friends and family will do it. Activities must not discriminate against anygroup and must be performed in such a way that they cannot be perceived as doingso. Consider whether women and men may have different capacities to access cashcompared with in kind resources. Activities should promote and help protect and therights of people who have historically been marginalized or discriminated against,such as certain castes, tribes or women and girls in some societies. Interventions withdisplaced beneficiaries should not be at the expense of the local host population;assistance should be provided to both groups where possible

4.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will support Inter-Agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including Sexual exploitation and abuse complaints?

The project will ensure accountable and cost-effective interventions that aim to strengthen beneficiaries’ resilience capacity to current humanitarian situations and maximize the impact toward food security and nutrition outcome. The proposed project activities are fully integrated targeting beneficiaries in the denned geographical locations identified by different clusters, in-order to ensure synergistic effect to improve overall well-being of affected populations.Through implementing accountability to the affected population with effectivefeedback and response mechanisms to measure the adequacy of the servicesprovided and satisfaction of the target population and to effectively and timelyaddress valid complaints. SAO adopts Core HumanitarianStandard (CHS), Humanitarian Accountability Partnership (HAP) guidelines and Interagency Standing Committee (IASC) Task Force on Accountability to AffectedPopulations (AAP) in setting standards, building capacity, communication,participation, monitoring and reporting on compliance, addressing complaints anddevelopment of strategy to improve the quality of partnership on principles ofaccountability. IASC commitments with regard to AAP are incorporated in this planincluding governance and integration of feedback and response mechanisms intoprogram design, maintaining transparency and dialogue between the organization andthe affected population, participation, and involvement of the target population inproject design, monitoring, and evaluation of this project. All the while mainstreamingprotection and considering the safety and confidentiality of the target population. SAO focuses on assessing and addressing the needs of all within the communities,including men, women, boys, girls, elders, persons with disabilities among othervulnerable and or marginalized groups. Community consultation meetings andorientation sessions will be held at the beginning of the project to verify needs, gaugeacceptance, ensure that the project is well understood by target communities and raisethe communities’ awareness with regard to their participatory roles and responsibilities, and the responsibilities of the project partners towards them.
