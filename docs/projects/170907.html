Project Summary

Decades of prolonged conﬂict and insecurity, drought and massive displacement in Mudug and Galgaduud regions have placed women, girls and children in extreme vulnerability and at increased risk of gender-based violence (GBV), during the 2020 dyer rains Mudug region was one of the most affected, with very high levels of displacement and food insecurity. Children are being used for a range of military purposes: ground combat, assassinations, intelligence collection and logistical support. Female and child-headed households remain at risk of sexual violence, abuse and exploitation. For CAAFAG, it is estimated that there are between 3,000-5,000 CAAFAG in Somalia, but these are only estimates; actual numbers could be larger. Grave violations against children are expected to continue with the ongoing military offensive and prolonged conflict. While the humanitarian response has stabilized ﬂoods and famine affected populations, perpetual external shocks limit the population’s access to basic services causing ﬂuctuating levels of vulnerability. As a result, women and children are forced to adopt risky and sometimes harmful coping strategies, including collection and sale of ﬁrewood and payment of debt through forced marriage. Pressure for survival intensiﬁes the potential for intimate partner violence. Furthermore, the modalities of aid assistance have sometimes exacerbated vulnerability and risk of exploitation. WISE is proposing the following interventions to lifesaving GBV and protection services to at risk population in Galmudug state of Somalia. The proposed interventions are designed to increase women and adolescent girls’ access to life saving GBV case management psychosocial support services and protection to reduce risks of GBV, integrate protection monitoring and mainstreaming into the GBV intervention to improve the overall context analysis and integration of protection principles in the intervention that will be scaled up to respond the identiﬁed needs. WISE will utilize existing internal response capacity to staff these teams to provide timely, effective and eﬃcient GBV and protection response during the project. This will provide much needed humanitarian services in mudug and Galgaduud to areas without GBV and protection services.
Since early 2020 through WISE’s existing programming, and in line with protection cluster ﬁndings, WISE has noted increasing and targeted attacks against women and girls with growing levels of sexual violence perpetrated. These are linked to the escalating insecurity, militarization of political leadership and mobilization of armed groups. Girls are particularly vulnerable to multiple forms of violence and have limited support systems and access to information. Based on the programming experiences in Galkayo, Dhusamareb and Adaado the majority of girls are out of school, experience growing levels of sexual violence abductions, sexual harassment, early and forced marriage, sexual exploitation and abuse and have restrictions placed on their movement by their families. 400 GBV cases were reported by women and children over the last 10 months (GBVIMS, December 2019-August 2020); 39% were rape and 48% were physical/domestic violence. Over the last year WISE has been building capacity in case management and provision of support to rape survivors However, due to post-rape kits pipeline gaps no rape survivors were provided with post rape treatment, there are currently no safe shelters or material support for survivors to access services. It is necessary to improve the quality of care by; reception of GBV cases, referral services and material assistance and safe shelter support. Communities, authorities and humanitarian actors lack awareness of GBV risks and consequences and have limited practical involvement for GBV prevention and response.

Project Indicators

This project aims at addressing protection concerns of the most vulnerable and human security for the persons of concern. The project provisions anticipate improved protection of the persons of concern against domestic, sexual and physical violence through varied and yet inter-related effective protection solutions. In course of the proposed project WISE will ensure that direct protection services and coordination to the people of concern. The mechanism that the implementing partner will use for strengthening is S/GBV Sub-cluster actors’ communication, participant and sometimes host the GBV sub-cluster meeting meetings in Gaalkacyo, participate and share information of interventions with protection cluster in Gaalkacyo,Adaado and Dhusamareb regions of Mudug and Galgaduud and at strengthen PC cluster in Galgaduud regions to ensure mechanism of information sharing among the concerned clusters of project are active and soured/circulated all information to all actors. Key strategies to achieve the proposed project objectives include: Reduced vulnerabilities + increased capacities is equal to reduced risk Working with communities, influential actors, opinion leaders, religious leaders to ensure their support, Activist, camp management, diverse ethnic, youth and women groups Providing training to volunteer reporters and GBV focal points as well as trained GBV case workers Awareness rising on negative effects of S/GBV and FGM prevention and response. Inculcation of social values in youth, women and girls Creating enabling environment for open discussions through inclusion and participation Participation in protection cluster meetings and consultation with other stakeholders Effective Coordination of community mobilization and responses, and networking
