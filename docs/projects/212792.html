Project Summary

WASH Needs:
As of December 2023, Syria's WASH crisis impacts 13.6 million people in urgent need. Prolonged conflicts, inadequate maintenance, energy shortages, and poor water management have severely damaged Northern Syria's WASH infrastructure. These challenges disproportionately affect marginalized populations, including people with disabilities. The multifaceted crisis, marked by conflict and climate challenges, particularly damages water and sanitation systems. Diminishing international funding and minimal development support exacerbates the situation, while recurrent disease outbreaks, waterborne diseases, prolonged drought, and a severe water crisis contribute to rising mortality and morbidity rates. Limited access to clean water and inadequate sanitation elevates the risks of diseases like cholera, scabies, lice, and leishmaniasis. Since August 2022, a cholera outbreak has impacted various governorates across Northern Syria, with 211,327 suspected cases, and 26 cholera deaths.
Implementation and intervention activities:
1.The organization will continue its emergency water trucking provision in 8 informal settlements in Ar-Raqqa , where HLP issues prevent the implementation of alternative solutions to water provision and 36 collective centers in Al Hassakeh .
2.The organization will conduct regular water quality monitoring of FRC, PH, and conductivity levels at Source, communal, track and Household level in line with the water supply chain.
3.In Raqqa, the organization will also conduct bacteriological tests at water source, communal tank and household level.
4.In Tel Tamer district, Al Hassakeh, the organization will support to establishment of 2 WASH committees, one for informal settlements in villages and one for collective centers.
5.In Ar-Raqqa, the organization will continue its pilot Market Based Approach water trucking in 7 informal settlements.
6.In Ar-Raqqa, the organization will support one Water Treatment Unit in Hattash informal settlement. The organization will provide the chlorine every month during the whole project as well as maintenance services or light repairs as the needs arise.
7.In Ar-Raqqa, the organization will provide maintenance and cleaning of communal and household water storage infrastructure in the targeted informal settlements.
8.In Al Hassakeh , the organization will provide 2 water stations in Tel Hamees sub-district, with water system rehabilitation and solarization.
9.In Al Hassakeh , the organization will conduct comprehensive technical assessment and will select the proper solar system size based on accurate KPIs.
10.The organization will provide support to the Tel Hamees water department to enhance their operation and maintenance capacities.
11.In Ar-Raqqa, the organization has identified two municipalities for support to improvement of the waste collection system and desludging activity in two informal settlements.
12.In Al Hassakeh gov, the organization will provide waste bins at communal and household level.
13.The organization will continue to operation checks on sanitation infrastructure including latrines, showers and handwashing points in 12 informal settlements in Ar-Raqqa and 26 collective centers in Al Hassakeh
14.The organization will provide existing sanitation facilities 10 informal settlements in Ar-Raqqa governorate with a desludging service.
15.In Al Hassakeh and Ar-Raqqa governorates, the organization hygiene promotion activities will be accompanied by soap and/or jerrycans distributions.
16.In Ar-Raqqa and Al Hassakeh governorates, the organization will distribute water tanks to facilitate safe storage of water and limit the spread of water borne diseases.
17.In Ar-Raqqa governorate, the organization will distribute two rounds of Menstrual Hygiene Kits in 12 informal settlements.
18.In Al Hassakeh governorate, the organization will provide essential hygiene items to households residing in 26 collective centers, focusing on the most vulnerable communities.
