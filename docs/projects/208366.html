Project Summary

Nigeria has been identified as a source, transit and destination country for VoTs subjected to forced labour and sex trafficking .Internal trafficking occurs within Nigeria mainly for purposes of domestic servitude, exploitation and abuse. While international or cross-border trafficking occurs for purposes of forced labour, domestic servitude and sexual exploitation.
As define by UN, Human Trafficking is the transnational Organized Crime as “the recruitment, transportation, transfer, harbouring or receipt of persons, by means of the threat or use of force or other forms of coercion, of abduction, of fraud, of deception, of the abuse of power or of a position of vulnerability or of the giving or receiving of payments or benefits to achieve the consent of a person having control over another person, for the purpose of exploitation.
In North-East Nigeria, where a large number of IDPs in camps and at Host community in Borno and Yobe state, has exposes the vulnerability of IDPs to Trafficking in person and other forms of coercion.
Child protection will include Prevention, Protection, Rehabilitation and Reintegration.
TARGET; IDPs in camps and Host community in Borno and Yobe state.

Plan fields

1 - Needs

Enrich Initiative will step up response in Borno and Yobe state. IDPs are reliant on the provision of basic services by humanitarian actors. Need based assessment, vulnerability screening was conducted in coordination with concerned stakeholders to inform the planning and design of activities forthcoming "with the intention to create awareness support on trafficking in persons and to ensure informed, evidence based planning and implementation capacity at community level across sites and host communities in Borno and Yobe state. Enrich Initiative prioritizes activities by assessment and report from the field and through one on one dialogue with the humanitarian and beneficiary communities. Needs and gaps are identified in deep fields visits through coordination with partners, referrals and findings are communicated through reports, sectorial meetings, coordination meetings.

2 - Activities

Targeted beneficiaries: IDPs in camps and Host Community.Locations: Borno and Yobe state.Activities: Engagement with relevant key stakeholders, including local authorities, in order to prevent or discourage or possibly bring an end to the menace of trafficking in persons, Smuggling of Migrants and neglect of Child protection. Survivor of Child exploitation and abuse, trafficking and smuggling, are profiled and referred to. Provision of assistance, including cash-based assistance to Survivor of Child Abuse and Exploitation, Trafficked persons and smuggled migrants. Raising awareness on how traffickers deceive people and the negativity of Child neglect . Conduct awareness campaigns targeting women, men, girls and boys on GBV prevention and risk mitigation strategies including PSEA. Conduct regular community mobilization and awareness raising on consequences of GBV. Establish and support community-based protection, strengthening participation of women’s groups and women-lead organizations. Provide livelihood programs for women and girls.
