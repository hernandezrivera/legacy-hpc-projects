Project Summary

The proposed project is proposed to strengthening Health care response system of Borno state to have capacity to response Coronavirus outbreak that have reach the state. The project is aimed at contributing to the Cluster Object two through supporting 200 young women and girls headed households and special needs persons, vulnerable men and boys through cash for work, to engage them on environmental sanitation and waste disposal activities, Emergency employment opportunities for the rehabilitation of community infrastructures, such as community marketplaces, community drainage and water supply systems, and small earth dams, cleaning of blocked drainages, to ensure that a reasonable level of environmental cleanliness is achieved and enable them to cope with COVID 19 response and prevention. GOALPrime Organization Nigeria shall provide livelihoods skill training and livelihood skill tools to empower 100 vulnerable young women, girls, and boys across IDPs and Host communities of MMC Jere, Kala Balge and Damboa LGA.
GOALPrime shall mobilize 1000 young boys and Girls to educate them on the Coronavirus prevention massages through social media communication platforms, engage, and support to participate on community social cohesion activities through community COVID 19 risk and prevention communication across their respective IDP camps and host communities in the target LGAs. GOALPrime shall mobilize the educated 1000 young boys and girls in different groups to carry out Household COVID 19 prevention communication with printed COVID19 prevention IEC materials across IDP camps and communities in the target LGAs to reduce the wide spread of coronavirus and mitigate stigmatization of the Coronavirus survivors among family and friends in the communities targeting to reach 4000 households across IDPs and communities in the targeted LGAs.

1 - Needs

The early recovery sector partners understand that the continuation of enforced lockdown in cities and towns will result into having negative impacts on people’s lives more especially vulnerable groups such as IDPs, female-headed households, women, children, persons with disabilities as many of their livelihood(food stocks) would only last for few days or months which will necessitate adopting severe coping mechanisms further undermining lockdown measures and public order. The already overwhelming humanitarian needs especially in BAY state where the majority of the IDPs are located, any outbreak of COVID-19 would indeed become catastrophic therefore, a coordinated and multi-sector response is paramount.

2 - Activities

1. Consultation with the project stakeholders across the project locations.2. Mobilization of 1000 young boys and girls for COVID 19 prevention communication across IDP camps and communities of the targeted LGAs.3. Creation of WhatsApp group platform for the strategic engagement of the mobilized 1000 young Boys and Girls to educate and equip them with right information on COVID 19 response and prevention massages.4. Development of appropriate awareness message on coronavirus prevention and referral for schools factoring in the language and visual impairments5. Printing of developed appropriate awareness message on coronavirus prevention and referral for 200 schools6. distribution IEC Material on COVID 19 Prevention Massages to the grouped 1000 mobilized and educated young boys and girls across Communities and IDP camps in MMC, Jere, Kala/Balge LGAs for household COVID19 risk and prevention communication. 7. Consultation with community/Camp leaders which include women leaders, camp chairman, and secretary, young girls and boys parents and care givers to map out community/camp infrastructures that need rehabilitation and how to select 200 young women and girls headed households and special needs persons, vulnerable men and boys to engage them on cash for work activities across the project targeted LGAs.8. Profiling and selection of 200 young women and girls headed households and special needs persons, vulnerable men and boys for Cash for work activities.9. Profiling and selection of 100 vulnerable young women, girls, and boys for livelihood skill training and livelihood skill tools empowerment across the target LGAs.10. Engagement of the selected 100 vulnerable young women, girls, and boys on livelihood skill training of their choice for the period of three months11. Procurement of livelihood skill tools for 100 vulnerable young women, girls, and boys that were trained on their choice of livelihood skill. Biweekly Community/IDP camp infrastructural rehabilitation activities (based on the need of the IDP camps and Host community)14. Coordination of the Household COVID 19 prevention communication by the mobilized 1000 young boys and girls to reach 4000 households across the targeted LGAs.15. Community/IDP camp situation update by the mobilized 1000 young girls and boys through the created WhatsApp platform.
