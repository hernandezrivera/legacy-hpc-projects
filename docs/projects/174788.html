Plan fields

1 - Needs

Humanitarian actors and agencies lack safe transport to and adequate storage options in key areas of operation across Borno State, Nigeria. Additionally, partners require assistance with facilitating access and the free movement of cargo throughout Borno, and lack access to centralized logistics information.

2 - Activities

The Logistics Sector will continue to offer humanitarian organisations access to common storage in six locations in 2021: in Dikwa, Damasak, Banki, Bama, Ngala, and Monguno; while UNHAS will continue to offer a fixed-wing air service between Maiduguri, Yola and Abuja, as well as helicopter transport, medevac and evacuation services throughout Borno State. Logistics Sector will continue to hold regular coordination meetings and produce maps and information products for humanitarian partners, while continuing to provide training and equipment loans to humanitarian partners. Lastly, the Logistics Sector will continue to provide consolidation and facilitation of movement notifications for cargo and trucks throughout Borno, as well as providing advice and coordination for access related issues.
