Project Summary

This project aims at providing the following services through establishing two normal deliveries centers in Rural Damascus (Al-Nashabiyyeh and Al-Zabadani):
1- normal Deliveries.
2- Gynecology clinical consultations.
3- Awareness raising sessions regarding (RH, CD and NCD) and medical consultations (Internal Diseases, Gynecology and Pediatrics) through two mobile teams affiliated to both centers (one each).
4- provide home medical services in addition to tele-medicine and medicine delivery for patients of chronic diseases due to Covid-19 pandemic impact on health facilities and on the cost of medical services to be afforded.
5-Distribution of hygiene kits.

4 - Needs

This Project aims through its services to (1) address humanitarian needs in undeserved areas, as based on the severity of needs and the current humanitarian response. (2) support mitigation of the socio-economic impacts stemming from economic and financial crisis in addition to restrictive measures to prevent COVID-19 due to the high increase of medical services cost among others Gynecology consultations and Normal Deliveries. along side with continuous depreciation of Syrian Pound, had its toll primarily on affected population where prices of basic commodities and food increased significantly. Also, the country is witnessing an increasing number in Cesarean operations the project will encourage normal deliveries which is recommended by WHO and Ministry of Health. In Al-Zabadani area there is 10 medical health centers (6 is partially working, 4 is out of service) served 13.556 persons during the first quarter of 2020 (HeRAMS, Q1 2020). In Al-Nashabiyyeh area there is 22 medical health centers (6 is out of services, 15 is partially working, 1 is fully working) served 43.610 persons during the first quarter of 2020. Moreover these two areas are witnessing a considerable returning movement. Moreover, HNO 2021, Health sector stated that people in need was estimated at 12.4 M of which 6.4 M are female (page 74). Regarding geographically needs, the health sector severity scale reveals that areas of catastrophic need are primarily located in parts of Aleppo, Idleb and rural Damascus in 2021, while areas of extreme need have remained primarily in north-west and north-east Syria.... . (page 75 HNO, 2021) this is the base on which NFRD select the locations. Ref: Who WoS consolidated HeRAMS, Q2020.(page 22,HRP 2021)

5 - Implementation of activities

1-Approvals to be obtained from ministry of health, ministry of social affairs and labor and ministry of foreign affairs. Usually it takes a long time to get theses approvals and they are essential to be obtained before the start of implementation. 2-Receiving the two public medical centers affiliated to ministry of health in both locations, if possible. 3-Renting two centers in both locations Al-Nashabiyyeh and Al-Zabadani. 4-Rehabilitation of two centers. 5-Equipping two centers with office furniture. 6-Equipping the centers with medical equipment. 7-Recruitment of the suitable staff for two centers. 8-Provide awareness raising sessions on RH, CD and NCD in both centers in addition to distribute hygiene kits. 9-Provide medical consultations through two medical mobile team in two centers. 10-Provide obstetric consultations and normal deliveries in two centers.Moreover, NFRD will do this through its partnerships and cooperation through: 1-Cooperation with National NGOs Such as: Saed Charity, Institute for Palestine studies, Lamset Shifa, Al-Qanawat Charity, SSSD and Masahat Foundation. 2-Cooperation with volunteering teams such as: Y-Peers and JCI. 3-MoUs signed with Governmental bodies such as Moh, MoE and Ministry of culture. In addition to MoSAL as a supervising ministry. 4-Benefitting from projects in progress carried out by NFRD funded from WHO, UNFPA, OCHA and UNICEF in Rural Damascus. 5- NFRD makes its best to maintain the continuity of the project through exit strategy to make sure that the services is still provided to beneficiaries of vulnerable groups, even if were reduced in number.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

The project aims to provide healthcare services but the 2 mobile teams of both centers will be responsible for raising awareness sessions against GBV issues, NCD, CD and RH.All conditions for patients privacy and data secrecy are kept in mind and in practice. Moreover, conditions regarding drugs storage will be followed. Both locations witnessed war actions, but now the situation is stable and a considerable number of returnees is coming back. As a result the need for Medical Health services will be increased. If, God Forbidding, the locations was under attack of any kind or the staff we will follow SOPs regarding Syria Humanitarian Notification System and Surveillance System for attacks on health care facilities.

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

1- One big risk in running normal deliveries center is the need for an ambulance , the mitigation of this situation will be through contacting health authorities in the area to provide the ambulance when need arises and as quickly as possible.2- the delay in receiving approvals from relevant authorities.3- prices and costs due to exchange rate fluctuation, to some extent.Nour Foundation will do whatever is possible to avoid the potential negative impact on the project.4- Bad weather in winter (for example snow-times) may affect the work of mobile teams which may affect the targeted number of the awareness sessions.Risk management measures: we will focus on working in good weather days also we will choose prepared places for the sessions and activities such as schools and government buildings.
