Project Summary

This project aim to provide services such as strengthen protection system and enhance hygiene promotion for most effected people including children from IDPs ,returnee and host community in south Darfur (Beleil, Murshing and kass locailities ), central Darfur (Zlingei Nertiti and Golo ),with targets estimated about 25000.
