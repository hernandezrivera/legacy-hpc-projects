Project Summary

The project provides food security assistance through the distribution of food baskets to households affected by the conflict in ( Ar-Raqqa ) and Afrin, Syria.
The food baskets will secure food security for the internal displaced households for 3months, benefi ciaries will be selected as per the vulnerability criteria set by Al Ameen (AHS ), the selection criteria include:
1- House headed by a woman, child or elderly.
2- HH and IDPs with Disabilities “one of the householder members”.
3- Host communities and IDPs living under poor shelter conditions (i.e. destroyed housesor no shelter), and/or living in collective centers “vulnerable groups”.
4- HHs with more than fi ve members.
5- HH and IDPshas at least 1 Pregnant/lactating woman.
6- HH and IDPs has three or more kids under 10 years.

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

Although all Syrian population has been heavily affected by the current crisis, due totraditional local norms, women are exposed to additional protection related risks. Alltypes of violence are cited as occurring in opposition areas of Syria, with domesticviolence, sexual exploitation and child marriage as top three. While sexual exploitationis somehow new, child marriage and domestic violence are often normalized andingrained in the culture. Women and girls are exposed to structural and societal risksas the lack of safe spaces and public transportation, limited freedom of movement,poverty, unemployment, displacement, stress associated to war, gender role change,lack of documents. All these poses enormous pressure, men feeling they failed inprotecting and securing a good life for their families. With men perceiving women as athreat to their control due to gender roles change, domestic violence has increased inSyria. Whereas men actions are explained as a result of stress, women are oftenblamed for provoking the violence or allowing it to happen. Parental violence againstchildren has also been reported as increasing. Displacement and living in camps arenoted as being particularly unsafe environments for women/girls due to the lack ofprivacy (in tents or in latrines), overcrowding and mixing of people, poverty, fi nancialdesperation, and lawlessness.
