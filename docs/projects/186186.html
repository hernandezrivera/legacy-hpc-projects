Project Summary

Through this project, Caritas Kumbo sets out to Improve access to quality education in identified communities within the Diocese of Kumbo (Bui and Donga-Mantung divisions of the North-West Region of Cameroon. This will be achieved through improving access to complementary packages that will enable and enhance quality delivery of services by the teachers and consequently improved access to and performance of the students and pupils.
We set to reach out to a minimum of 4,000 children in nursery, primary and secondary schools (3,000 in 17 Nursery and Primary schools and 1,000 in 08 secondary schools).

1 - Besoins / Needs

Education is a fundamental right of every child. This right has been denied thousands of children across the North West and South West Regions of Cameroon in general and Bui and Donga-Mantung Divisions of the North West Region in particular; for over five years now. This gruesome denial of children from the right to education is as a result of the ongoing political crisis which eventually degenerated into an armed conflict. Schools have been completely shut down on the most parts of the aforementioned area, which is co-terminal with the Diocese of Kumbo, since the onset of the crisis in November 2016. It is no doubt that there are thousands of children who have not had access to any form of education, not to mention formal education. The issue of school resumption still remains very critical as there are still very varied opinions regarding the operationalization of schools within the area. However, a lot of advocacy has been going on at different levels, precisely the Church and her partners, the results being the operationalization of several schools within the localities, specifically the mission schools (Catholic, Presbyterian and Baptist Schools, both basic and secondary levels. That notwithstanding, the war has continually rained varied other effects on the populations including children, rendering access to the schools in areas where schools are operational rather challenging in the following main aspects- With the continuous abduction, torture, maiming or killing of teachers or demand for huge ransoms, teachers continue to live under a lot of stressful circumstances. This poor psychological status affects the quality of their services as teachers. - As the war prolongs, children are increasingly subject to psycho-social challenges that continue to impair their learning abilities. - A majority of the parents cannot afford fees talk less of basic school supplies for the children. As a result, the schools cannot afford to cater for the teachers put in place to teach the children as well as purchase the needed didactic materials. This leads to poor deliveryThe Social Welfare/Caritas Department of the Diocese of Kumbo, with an outstanding field presence both in the pre-crisis period and during the crisis has easy access and acceptance in the communities. Based on this strength, Caritas seeks to contribute to improving access to and enhancing quality education by addressing the following needs; 2. Targets Caritas Kumbo and partners through this project targets to reach out with the following interventions- Mental Health and Psycho-social Support (MHPSS) to 216 teachers drawn from 17 basic education institutions and 08 secondary education institutions- Support with minimum school kits (books, pens, pencils, rulers etc.) to 3,000 children in Nursery and Primary Schools and 1,000 children in secondary school - Support 30 schools with basic didactic materials and infrastructure (chalk, board rulers, flip charts, bold markers, etc) to enhance the teaching/learning process - Provide MHPSS and support materials to children (building blocks, balls, drawing books, colored pencils etc)- Carry out WASH promotion and MHM promotion in target schools while providing emergency water supply options for the target schools or communities (rehabilitation and extensions or water purification)
