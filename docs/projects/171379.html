Project Summary

IOM WASH is proposing to contribute to the continuation and restoration of access to safe and equitable basic WASH services to overall 380,000 beneficiaries from vulnerable IDP and host-communities in multiple regions in Somalia.
Specifically, the project will target 228,000 Nondisplaced people (children 107,160; men 41,040; women 36,480; elderly 9,120 and disabled 34,200) and 152,000 displaced populations (children 71,440; men 27,360; women 24,320; elderly 6,080 and disabled 22,800).
IOM will rehabilitate 50 strategic water sources for multi-use that will provide continued, equitable, safe, sufficient and sustainable water to regions, targeted by the cluster, prone to natural disasters.
IOM further proposes to construct 3,000 new emergency sanitation facilities (following cluster specifications) targeting populations without access to basic sanitation and hygiene.
The project will also strengthen the capacity of the Federal Government of Somalia and Federal Members States, community leaders and water committees/technicians for better coordination with government authorities and communities and sustainability of the constructed/ rehabilitated WASH infrastructures. In addition, the project will nurture community participation in good hygiene practices through training and community sensitization campaigns. All trainings, awareness raising and capacity building activities will follow national guidelines on COVID-19 preventive measures, e.g. on physical distancing, to avoid mass gatherings and minimize risks of exposure.
The project is aligned with protection mainstreaming to ensure safety and dignity of affected populations without causing harm. Affected populations and all relevant stakeholders including men, women, boys, girls, elderly and people with disability will be involved in decision making throughout the project activities/cycle.
Furthermore, the project will be a part of an IOM integrated emergency response program and resilience building, where possible, efforts will be made to integrate IOM health, nutrition, shelter, CCCM and protection activities.

Project Activities

Activity 1.1: Continued provision of equitable, safe,sufficient and appropriate basic water supply (inclusive of trucking/voucher system) to 159,600 people (55,860 girls; 47,880 boys, 31,920 women and 23,940 men) in vulnerable settlements and Communities. Activity 1.2: Rehabilitate 50 strategic water sources for multi-use to provide continued, equitable, safe, sufficient and sustainable safe water to 144,400 people (50,540 girls; 43,320 boys; 28,880 women and 21,660 men) Activity 1.3: Rehabilitation and improvement of 45 communal water berkads to provide safe water to 76,000 people (26,600 girls; 22,800 boys; 15,200 women and 11,400 men). Activity 2.1 Construction of 3,000 emergency sanitation facilities (with internal locks) targeting 90,000 people (31,500 girls; 27,000 boys; 18,000 women and 13,500 men) who do not have any access for immediate provision of basic sanitation and hygiene. Different age and gender groups (Women, girls, men, boys), persons with disabilities, older persons and marginalized individual will be consulted to collect accurate information about their specific requirements and preferences for location and design of sanitation facilities. Activity 2.2: Support 10 villages to become solid waste free through community led cleaning/disposal campaigns approach by training 200 community mobilisers and leaders (100 men and 100 women, 20 per location) in vulnerable settlements and communities Activity 3.1: Recruit and train 600 (300 women, 300 men) community members and hygiene promoters on key health risks related to water, sanitation and hygiene. Persons with disabilities and marginalized groups will be consulted and included in the training. Activity 3.2: Disseminate key hygiene messages, including COVID-19 preventive messages, and hygiene kits, through hygiene promotion campaigns targeting individuals (girls, boys, women, men), groups (children, women, men), households and institutions (schools) conducted by trained hygiene promoters and mass media communications to benefit 380,000 people Activity 4.1: Provide capacity building to 500 (250 men and 250 women) water committees, local technicians, community leaders and local authorities on proper operations and maintenance of water services. Activity 4.2: Provide capacity building on solar water solution, water quality testing and water quality and monitoring to 120 officials of the Federal Government of Somalia, Members States, regional and district level.

Delivery Mechanism

Voucher - Paper

What is the justification for using cash or vouchers instead of in kind?

Voucher system is cost-effective and timely since the transportation and logistics costs is lower. It allows beneficiaries greater choice of what they want to spend on and upholds their dignity by eliminating long queues at water points. Voucher system further allows money to be spent locally which will induce beneficial multiplier effects on the local economic activities by increasing production in other sectors such as agriculture and livelihoods.

Explain how the project design considered market functionality ?

Through voucher system for hygiene kits and transportation of water, it is expected to generate demand for products and services which will strengthened and catalyze local market growth. When required, market assessment will be conducted, to see how the local market can be strengthened effectively.

Mitigation Measures

1. Risk that marginalized groups do not participate in consultations:a. IOM will gather data (age, sex, diversities) of communities and beneficiaries for effective and informative programing. This will help identify vulnerable and marginalized persons for planning, inclusion purposes and engagementb. A systematic outreach process will be done to pre-register marginalized/minority vulnerable groups to avoid exclusionc. Efforts will be made to ensure that information regarding provision water will be disseminated to all members of communities (including women, girls, boys, men) through appropriate channel of communication such radiod. An inclusive feedback mechanism that will include all beneficiaries will be put in place which help evaluate selection criteria and overall services gaps.e. In all water collection points, a mechanism will be established that will give priority to those who cannot stand in line for long periods, such as older persons, persons with disability, unaccompanied children, pregnant and lactating women, so that they wait for less time. 2. Risk that particularly women, girls and boys are not included in consultations and decision making processes:a. Women, girls and boys will be consulted during programme design to gather accurate information about their specific WASH requirements and preferences for location and design of WASH facilities. Issues of privacy and access to persons with disability will be discussed. b. Since women and girls often fetch water they will directly be engaged to contribute on decision about safety of the location of and routes to the water collection point.c. Efforts will be made to include women in water committees and community hygiene promoters so that they contribute to the overall program implementation at community level.d. The project will hire female staff members at project location to ensure that women and girls will be free talking to them and provide feedback mechanism 3. Risk of women, girls and boys exposed to sexual violence, including sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA), when accessing humanitarian assistance or violence during/after registration or distribution:a. Water locations will be put in central, visible places and not more than 500 meters away from settlements, following consultations with women and girls. This will help to protect them from risks of all forms of GBV when accessing servicesb. Separate toilet (and bathing) facilities with internal locks and secondary enclosures will be constructed for women and men with clear labels such pictorial/signs to ensure that they are used by intended sexc. In setups where threats are anticipated frequency of hygiene kits distribution will be modified and door-to-door distribution considered, to avoid sexual abuse and extortion while carrying kits. d. Coordination will be done with Shelter/NFI partners to put solar light installations at WASH community centers to deter any risk of violence against women and girlse. Distributions of water will be done during day light to allow beneficiaries who often women and girls reach home early. Please provide further explanation

Despite the recurring of droughts, flooding and Acute Watery Diarrhea (AWD)/Cholera in the country, there are a multitude of opportunities to build upon existing Somali structures and innovations to increase the overall resilience of the population. The project design and implementation will take into account environmental protection to avoid over-exploitation and contamination of the environment and sustainability. This includes design and installation of the water pumping system based on pumping test to avoid depletion of aquifers. Constructed and rehabilitated boreholes will be equipped with elevated water tanks, distribution systems with household connections where possible and hybrid energization systems. Latrine construction/rehabilitation will use standard designs to avoid contamination of ground and surface water. The project will be linked to and built on ongoing resilience WASH program in close collaboration with government institution, local communities to ensure stable and safe environment. IOM will continue to strengthen the capacity of staff from Federal Government and Regional States. WASH committees will be established and a cost recovery system introduced to ensure proper operation and maintenance of water sources, community ownership and ease of access for all population groups. Private enterprise has thrived in many areas of the Somali economy, and the private sector accounts for a large proportion of WASH service providers, the project will promote private Public partnership (PPP) approach to ensure sustainable and affordable WASH services. All the project activities are also linked to and are contributing to the National Development Plan.
