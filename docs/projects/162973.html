Project Summary

Landmine (LM) and Explosive Remnants of War (ERW) pose a significant threat to the safety and security of IDPs, returnees, at risk populations and humanitarian actors in South Kordofan and Blue Nile state. Women and children are particularly vulnerable. According to IMSMA monthly report for July 2019 SK and BN states have the higest number of land mine casualties in the entire Sudan with 645 in SK and 449 in BN survivors of land mines /ERW. Furthermore in South Kordofan and Blue Nile delivery and access too much needed humanitarian assistance is complicated due to high ERW/mine contamination levels. The project aim to reduce the risk posed by LM/ERW, by deployment of two Multi Task Teams (MTTs) , tow Manual clearance teams (MCTs) and tow MRE teams in Blue Nile and South Kordofan to release 300,000 Square meter of land contaminated by land mines and Explosive Remnants of War . Build and Increase knowledge about dangers of UXO and ERWs among 60,000 beneficiaries from affected communities in Blue Nile (Kurmuk , Baw , Gissan localities ) and South Kordofan (Dalanj, Habila, Dalami and Abu Jebiha localities ) by contamination of ERWs, to enable them to identify and adopt measures that reduce the risk posed. The released land will be handed over to 76,614 (16220 men, 18340 women, 20487 girls and 21567 boys) IDPs, returnees, at risk population and humanitarian workers in SK and BN free of LM/ERW. JASMAR works in coordination with protection sector/MA sub sector in Khartoum/ South Kordofan and Blue Nile, in collaboration with National Mine Action Centre (NMAC) for tasking and technical support and SHF for financial support, Humanitarian Aid Commission (HAC) to facilitate the permits and engagement of men and women’s from affected communities throughout the project cycle.

1 - Needs

According to the Information Management System Mine Action (IMSMA) November 2019 report, in BN Blue Nile there are : 16 Dangerous Areas, 4 Suspected Hazardous Areas, 4 Mine Field still opened. In South Kordofan SK: there are 47 DA, 44 SHA, 48 MF based on IMSMA November 2019 report, the contamination in South ,west Kordofan and Blue Nile state has directly affected the daily life activities of over 205,712 according to HNO report for 2019. The landmine /ERW contamination impeded the socio economic development of the area as inhabitants are unable to utilize this land for agriculture, grazing & blocking accessibility of roads. Women and children are particularly vulnerable either as direct victims or as part of families who become even further marginalized economically, socially & psychologically. Although women and girls comprise a smaller percentage of the total recorded casualties they are particularly vulnerable either as direct casualties during daily livelihood activities, cultivating land, attending animals, collecting water and firewood or as part of families who become even further marginalized economically, socially & psychologically. Women and girls face additional discrimination, including abandonment, limited access to medical services, Injured (handicapped) a female’s chance of marrying is likely to be affected, the responsibility of caring for the ill or disabled of children and husband adds heavily to the workload of women. This indicates the necessity for landmine clearance/TS/ and MRE interventions. To reduce the risk posed by LM/ERW, JASMAR two Manual Clearance Teams (MCT) and tow Multi Tasks Teams (MTT) conduct land release operations to releases 300,000 Square meters of land and 18 Dangerous Areas/Suspected Hazardous Areas from Landmine and ERW contaminated land and JASMAR tow MRE teams through Mine Risk Education will enable 60,000 IDPs, returnees & people in host communities to identify and adopt safety measures that reduce the death and injuries posed by landmines/ ERW in BN, SK.

2 - Activities / Outputs

Activities Activity 1: Recruitment of Staff Activity 2: Logistic preparation for MCT, MTT and MRE teams. Activity 3: Training sessions for new teams and refresher training for the current JASMAR MCT, MTT and MRE teams. . Activity 4: Tasks and Deployment of the 2MCT, 2 MTT and 2 MRE teams in Blue Nile , South Kordofan states. At least 8 tasks issued by NAMC and completed by JASMAR teams. Activity 5: Land release operations including Non Technical Survey (NTS), Technical Survey (TS) and clearance of mines/ERW (MC) at least 190,000 sqm cleared and released . Activity 6: QA and Field Monitoring visits. Weekly QA conducted by TLs and 8 QA & monitoring visits conducted by Mine Action JASMAR Operation manger Activity7: Coordination with project stockholders & other Organizations in project area. Activity 8: Provision of community Base Mine Risk Education (CBMRE) intervention. 80 local volunteers trained and equipped with mine risk Education package to conduct awareness to their communities: Activity 10: Direct Mine Risk Education sessions to the mine affected communities, IDPs and at risk population in BN, SK states. 60.000 benefit from direct MRE sessions in SK & BN states. Activity 11: Environmental awareness and tree planting: The project staff will comply with JASMAR Code of conduct regarding the respect and saving human dignity of beneficiaries. Through regular meeting the CL will ensure the consultation/ participation of the affected communities in prioritizing the clearance and technical survey process, during operations and handover of the land. CLO ensures that women opinions on clearance and technical survey prioritization are heard and valued. CLO will liaise with, collect and share information from a wide variety of sources (e.g. communities, local authorities, Ministry staff, other NGOs & INGOS) using meetings, interviews, mapping and other participatory activities. JASMAR will respond to the feedback provided by communities through the Orientation & consultation meetings. JASMAR will make use of the physical presence and expertise of JASMAR in the fields of GVB, HIV/AIDS to raise the awareness of beneficiaries about the danger .

5.1 - Impact on environment/natural resources

The teams will adhere to environmental conservation during LR operations by, the location of Central Demolition Side (CDS) to consider the environmental aspects. Items found during the clearance will be destroyed with no impact on environment, avoid burning/cutting trees & vegetation; if necessary mitigate the damage by adopting the control measures according to JASMAR SOPs, NTSG & IMAS 10.04 & at the end of the operations all items removed for camping purposes will be returned to the original situation before intervention
