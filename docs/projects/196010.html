Project Summary

The proposed intervention is designed to be integrated with dignified shelter activities to ensure better living conditions to most affected people,emphasizing on those in areas with high severity of needs and cholera affected areas.International Humanitarian Relief (IHR) is planning to respond to 9102 (Men, Female, Boys, girls),IDPs in3camps and 26667 in(Maydan Afrin,Basuta,Burj Abdallah,Deir Ballut,Nisriyeh,Bayada-Jandiris,Haj Eskander,Hamam-Jandiris, Moatrem,and Orm Eljoz)community to ensure an improved,sustainable access to WASH services, Sanitation,and supplies which leads to provide safer living conditions against cholera. Parallel to the WASH cluster strategy which emphasizes on reducing the massive reliance on the costly non-sustainable direct provision water trucking, desludging, etc.,towards a more cost-effective, cheaper, and more efficient response.The proposed project aims to rehabilitate the water stations targeting the entire system and power it with a hybrid energy system and equip them with a proper chlorination systems.IHR aims to ensure improved access of host communities and IDPs in ten districts and three camps in Afrin and Ariha sub-district through rehabilitation and operational support for water system.IHR will rehabilitate Midanki, Basuta, Deir ballut and Nisriyeh water stations. Parallel to that,IHR will rehabilitate Orm Eljoz community water stations in Ariha (Idleb governates),which serves Qamue camp too.To ensure sustainability after completing the rehabilitation and the maintenance of the equipment installed,IHR will support the establishment of a Water Management Unit (WMU) to support the Local Councils of both sub-districts.For 3 months,IHR will support the running costs for the stations (including oil and fuel for generators and tests of the flow of the well).During this time,WMU members will receive technical training on operation and maintenance activities and implement a cost recovery mechanism.The fees collected monthly by the WMU after completing the project will be used to purchase the diesel for the generator and the spare parts as needed.IHR will provide basic WASH and Hygiene services, adequate drinking safe water via water trucking (40L/p/day) while assuring the quality of the water through chlorination and testing.Besides,the intervention will include rehabilitation of the sewage systems in Moatrem community that is gradually deteriorating due to damage and lack of maintenance.We will implement a new sewage system with associated treatment to meet the critical need after residents’ numbers have doubled. To ensure the full response for PWD,we will provide special tanks for all PWDs as it was the most required service in our PWD’s need assessment which was made in the three targeted camps case by case.Besides, we will distribute hygiene kits (CVA modality) specially designed for the targeted PWD’s in addition to constructing special latrines for PWDs where needed.The awareness sessions will be integrated with distribution HK to displaced families in targeted camps it will include sessions on cholera preventive actions to be done.The distribution of HK will reinforce and increase the population’s awareness level in targeted camps to prevent the spread of cholera.Other services will be delivered through this intervention like solid waste management services, cleaning the latrines by hiring cleaners M&F.IHR have a potential projects in same location.Therefore, this project proposal includes a comprehensive package and need to extend mentioned activities.All activities have been coordinated with present NGOs and camps management to ensure complementarity and avoid overlaps.

3 - Needs

IHR made a rapid needs assessment for in10 communities, 3 camps with a sample of people in each camp and community (73 individuals:41M,32F). IHR assessment results indicated that:In off-camps:52%of respondents stated that water amount is not enough. The respondents recommended many aspects that should be supported to improve the reality of water, hygiene, and sanitation in the study sites; maintain the station of Al-Nasriya community equipped it with solar system, as this station supports 4 neighboring villages. Renovation for the station building of Maydan Afrin, including provision of submersible pump with generator and a hybrid system. Providing sanitation activities in Moatrem, and establishment of solar system in each of Basuta and Burj Abdallah.In camps: There is a permanent failure to clean the septic pits. Most respondents stated that the camp residents have no access to cleaned WASH facilities, in addition to some problems in the facilities infrastructure. Garbage is disposed of in public dumps in Muhammadiyah and DeirBallut camps. Providing operational support for the stations in each of Muhammadiyah and Deir Ballut camps. Providing 25 garbage containers for each camp, and maintaining the sewage network (expanding lines, cleaning septic pits). Providing large-scale WASH services and activities in Al Qamou camp

8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

IHR has PSEA, Code of Conduct, Protection and Child Safeguarding policies and mechanisms to ensure every effort is made to protect all program participants. IHR s PSEA Mechanism is designed around four core components: Awareness Raising (for staff regarding power dynamics, risk and vulnerabilities etc.), Prevention (awareness regarding the existing controls which are in place and how PSEA is prevented), Reporting (how participants can report and what are the specific steps which need to be taken regarding SEA cases) and Response (how and what IHR will do when cases of SEA are reported). A similar mechanism is available for CP where staff is familiar with approaching and dealing with cases, suspected cases, child abuse, exploitation, or violence. IHR has delivered numerous stand-alone Protection, Safeguarding and PSEA projects in the NW and others where protection was a cross-cutting component, guided by Protection principles. As part of this intervention, by coordination with the different clusters IHR will develop a service map that identifies all Protection and other service providers within the operational areas; this tool will help further mainstream protection through the Program as all staff will have access to this resource.This means that al the projects activities will be monitored and reported through our PSEA mechanism, protection service mapping, safeguarding. This makes it easy to follow up any breaches to the SEA or Safeguarding issues.
