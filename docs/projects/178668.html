Project Summary

throughout 2021, Violet Organization is planning to implement comprehensive food security interventions applying a variety of modalities (GIK, Cash, and vouchers) to address the needs of the diffrent groups with proper modality.
the proposed project will effectively contribute to life-saving and life-sustaining response through improving food consumption, dietary diversity and avoiding negative coping strategy
1. RTE distribution for the newly IDPs
2. MPGs distribution for the newly IDPs
3. Regular Food baskets distribution
4. Cash for food distribution
5. restricted commodity vouchers for food distribution
5. Cooked meals provision for the COVID-19 positive confirmed cases admitted in 3 CCTCs in NWS
Moreover, the project will contribute to improving the livelihoods of the most vulnerable and value chains through income generation activities as following activities:
1. Cash for work for olives harvesting
2. vocational training related to Food processing
3. Business grants for food processing
4. Agriculture inputs provision through restricted commodity vouchers distribution
5. Provision of GAP training for the farmers
6. Provision and Installation of solar powering irrigations systems
7. livestock extension services awareness sessions, and consultation

4 - Needs

According to the HNO 2021, Syria remains one of the world’s most complex humanitarian emergencies characterized by ongoing hostilities which have killed hundreds of thousands of people, triggered one of the worst displacement crises of our time, and led to the widespread destruction of civilian and agricultural infrastructure, including homes, schools, health facilities, water supply, and irrigation systems. 13.4 M people in Syria are in need of humanitarian assistance – a 21 percent increase compared to 2020 – with needs increasingly being exacerbated by economic decline According to the needs and response summary report, The continued conflict and the economic downturn have deteriorated the living conditions of people who are already in need of humanitarian assistance. On top of that, the massively reduced purchasing power and financial unaffordability for food and other goods have pushed further parts of the population in high need of assistance. the same report indicates that 12.4 M people are food insecure, of whom 1.3M severely food insecure. The food basket average price was increased by 236% between Dec 2019 and Dec 2020. 51% of the HH income spent on food in Sep 2020 leaves little room to meet other basic needs. 28% of HHs adopt emergency strategies to cope with increased food prices including child labor and early marriage, selling property, migrating due to lack of food. According to the response and needs summary, More than 90 percent of the Syria population is now estimated to live below the poverty line and Food security remain the first priority need for 71% of the population, and Livelihood assistance is the priority of 50% of the population

5 - Implementation of activities

through the project at hands, Violet aims to address the food security needs of the crisis-affected groups through the implementation of the following activities 1) provision of RTEs for 3000 HHs, 2) provision of One-off MPGs (100 USD) for 15000 HHs, 3) provision of regular food baskets for 1000 HHs for 8 distribution rounds4) provision regular food assistance for 5200 HHs for 8 months; 1700 out of them will receive cash assistance (60 USD). 2500 will receive restricted commodity vouchers (60 USD) for food and 1000 will receive regular food baskets. 5. provision of Cooked meals for the patients admitted to the CCTCs Additionally, Violet will contribute to improving the livelihoods of the most vulnerable and crises affected groups through the implementation of the following income generation activities: 1. 2200 beneficiaries will participate in Cash for work for olives harvesting for 1 month (20 days)2. 300 beneficiaries will receive vocational training focusing on Food processing3. 100 vocational trainees will receive Business grants to allow them to establish their own business. 4. 300 farmers will have improved access to Agriculture inputs through the provision of restricted commodity vouchers5. 300 farmers will have improved knowledge through the Provision of GAP training6. . Support economic/productive infrastructure through Provision and Installation of 10 solar powering irrigations systems7.provision of livestock extension services awareness sessions, and consultation

Impact: HighMitigation: Violet will ensure the supplier is aware of the CP policy through the mandatory CP awareness session to all suppliers, an associated provision will be added in the supplier contract to ensure the suppliers are aware and accountable3- the risk of COVID-19 spread in the targeted community especially on those at risk.Likelihood: High impact: High Mitigation measures: Violet will ensure all teh preventive measures are applied during the implementation, elderly and people at risk can stay at home and delegate someone on their behalf to collect their assistance, if the People at risk doesn't have anyone to delegate, violet filed staff may deliver the assistance to his door

7.1 - Please list any risks related to sexual exploitation and abuse that have been identified during the project design phase and any risk mitigation and prevention measures that have been adopted and built into the project

1- sexual exploitation and abuse through humanitarian workers. As the staff will interact with the most vulnerable groups of the community, the power dynamics between staff and BNFs can be exploitedLikelihood: Medium. Impact: HighMitigation measures: Violet trains all staff on PSEA, raises awareness of BNFs, and informs on available complaints mechanism. The M&E team will include post-monitoring and PSEA topics in supervision sessions to identify any misconduct. Any complaints, allegations, or rumors will be elevated on the country director level and investigated immediately2. GBV can be compounded by overcrowding during the distribution activities:Likelihood: Medium. Impact: HighMitigation measures: ensure the ques of both men and women are separated during the distribution, ensure the female staff is presented in the distribution site to address the women-specific needs, consultation with women, girls, boys, and men should be organized.

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

1- Logistical operations are affected by the security situation like transportation, storing the assistance- Likelihoods: medium- Impact: High- Mitigation: The safety and Security team will study all possible scenarios for the storing and transportation and the distribution will be HHs to HHs not in one place 2- inability to respond to the IDPs within the first phase of their displacement- Likelihoods: Medium- Impact: High- Mitigation: Violet has an IDPs tracking initiative by setting communication channels with all LCs in the whole community which usually hosts IDPs, then once an emergency occurs, the LC will inform Violet focal points to respond immediately 3. tension might occur between the Host community and IDPs. - Likelihoods: High- Impact High- Mitigation: Selection criteria will be clear with no chance for personal judgment taking into consideration the participation of the local communities as references and the IDs information. MoUs will be signed with all local councils to clarify the project's scope of work which will facilitate the processes in their communities and apply the selection criteria transparently 4: COVID-19 transmission as a result of the action- Likelihoods: Medium- Impact: High- Mitigation: adjusting the activities and applying the preventive measures including sterilizing the Distribution sites, wearing the masks during the distribution, screening the BNFs temperature before getting into the distribution site and supplying the distribution point with adequate hygiene solutions, minimizing the number of BNF at the distribution site and setting different distribution sites at once to avoid the overcrowding.
