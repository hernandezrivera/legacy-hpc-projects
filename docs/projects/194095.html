Project Summary

IDPs in South Darfur State remain the most vulnerable people in the Greater Darfur due the old humanitarian situation and the new confrontation of the sides of the Government troops. According to the humanitarian assessment reports issued by Un Agencies, INGOs and NNGOs during the period of 2022, IDPs in camps settlements in and around Nyala town and the localities still lacking physical protection including housing. The recent inter-government war between SAF and RSF occurred in April 2023 in all Sudan, made the life situation of IDPs critical. More than 600,000 IDPs in the 6 camps of Nyala and about 450,000 citizen moved from their houses to seek refuge with their families or elsewhere in the South part of Nyala and other localities in South Darfur such as Kas, Edel Fursan, Mersheng, Bileil and Elsalam.
The project will target those who are vulnerable based on needs identified by need assessment and verification process conducted by NNGOs initiative, HAC and State Ministry of Health and SMoWF conducted on May 14th and 15th, 2023 in Nyala city and the surrounding localities: Kas, Elsalam, Edelfursan, Bileil, Niteiga targeted HH 30,613 (153,613 persons) including 67% female households, 7% Children households and 26% male. This assessment report is shared with OCHA staffs, IOM and other humanitarian Agencies which still have staffs in Nyala.

5.2 - Impact on environment/natural resources

NFIs for humanitarian are mainly plastic, including plastic sheets, Jerrycans, plastic mats impacted badly in the environment. During the distribution, beneficiaries are given awareness on how they will use in items and where they should put them when they are useless to avoid environment pollution. For the emergency shelter, the project avoid using cutting trees for construction, it's designed to use either Sustainable Soil Block (SSB), or iron materials. Beneficiaries are aware of all these environmental challenges before receiving the services
