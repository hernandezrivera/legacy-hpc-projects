Project Summary

The advent of COVID-19 global pandemic on the 31st December 2019, in China has threatened human survival across the world. On 30th January 2020 WHO declared the outbreak a Public Health Emergency of International Concern (PHEIC) under the International Health Regulations (IHR 2005. WHO declared COVID-19 a global pandemic on 11th March 2020. As of 16 May 2020, a total of 4,534,952 cases of COVID-19 were confirmed across the globe; 307,108 deaths and 1,633,185 recoveries. Zimbabwe had 42 confirmed cases, 4 deaths and 13 recoveries.
Despite the lower number recorded for Zimbabwe as compared to the neighbouring South Africa (13,524 cases) and Zambia (668 cases), the country is ill-prepared to respond to the COVID-19 Pandemic. The macro-economic situation is underperforming, and basic services provision such as health care, food and water are inadequate. The country’s health system is currently under distress with shortages of critical staff, medical supplies and personal protective equipment (PPE).
Potential hotspot areas for the pandemic are overcrowded areas with high density of people and inadequate water supply services. The Tropical Cyclone Idai induced displaced areas and host communities in Manicaland Province are exemplary of these potential hotspot areas. In this regard, Zimbabwe Council of Churches (ZCC) is appealing for funding to implement a COVID-19 emergency prevention project for displaced and host communities in Chipinge and Buhera Districts to enhance sensitization, information dissemination, handwashing, and COVID-19 preventive measures to IDPs and Host communities. The proposed intervention is targeting 10,000 people with Risk Communication and Community engagement, and Infection Prevention and Control activities.

1 - Activities

The proposed intervention will aim at interrupting the spread of COVID-2019 through the implementation of activities under the two (2) broadbased pillars which are Risk Communication and Community engagement, and Infection Prevention and Control. Activities that will be undertaken under the Risk Communication and Community Engagement pillar includes COVID-19 awareness raising through the distribution of COVID-19 educational materials (fliers, posters, t-shirts), erection of billboards with COVID-19 messages and training of Village Health Workers and Local Church pastors on COVID-19. The Infection Prevention and Control activities will include distribution of personnel protective equipment, handwashing tapped buckets and soaps as well as the repair/rehabilitation of water points to improve access to safe water for IDPs and host communities. The project will be implemented in coordination with line government departments, local authority, shelter cluster humanitarian organisations and local leadership. The organisation will attend all coordination meetings that will be called by government departments, other partners and the Shelter Cluster.

2 - Indicators

The success of the proposed intervention will be measured by the following 2 indicators: 1) Number of IDPs and host communities reached with risk communication messaging, 2) Number of IDPs reporting access to COVID-19 preventive measures
