Project Summary

The project aims to provides an integrated assistance to an estimated 29,794 IDPs in Montepuez and Balama districts through the delivery of CCCM service. AVSI will ensure that basic conditions are met and community structures established and/or supported with an Age Gender and Diversity approach mainstreamed;engaging IDPs and host communities.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project aims at targeting 29,794 displaced individuals, living in the relocation sites of Ntele and Nicuapa within the districts of Montepuez (approx. 9.729 F, of which 1.500 girls, and 413 elders, approx. 9.332 M of which 1.557 boys and 318 elders) and Balama living in the relocations sites of Angalia, Mavava and Impire (approx. 5.260 F, of which 833 girls, and 242 elders, approx. 4.931 M of which 843 boys and 184 elders).AVSI, in partnership with UNHCR is the actual CCCM in Ntele and Nicuapa sites (Ntele, Nicuapa) , benefitting 32.100 IDS, 15,633 males and 16,467 females.Building up on AVSI solid and multisectorial presence (CCCM, WASH, Protection, FSL, Nutrition, Education, Shelter/NFI, Energy) in the area, which would allow an area based and multi-purpose response for IDPs and Host community members

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

In particular, the project will contribute to cluster objectives, through 3 main strategies:i) Guarantee integrated CCCM services in terms of protection, data management, community participation and engagement, through much as possible on site approach, promoting inclusion of the most vulnerable groups in within camp leadership;ii) Continue and expand the process of mainstreaming on CFM utilization initiated in 2021, with the purpose of “giving voices” to beneficiaries needsiii) Site structural organization, planning and development, leveraging on AVSI experience in participatory urban development matured through 2019-2021 years of experience in the municipalities of Maputo and Pemba. Main identified activities are: 1.1.Supporting camp administration and governance structures promoting the inclusion of women and youth committees1.2.Mapping all stakeholders-5Ws (Who/What/Where/When) and facilitate a clear and agreed division of tasks/ ToRs of service providers in the given sites1.3. Establishing a registering and monitoring service within the camp/site 1.4 Training of government structure and camp/sitegovernance structure on basic CCCM tools and standards1.5 Supporting community-led initiatives to enhance participation of marginalized and vulnerable groups in activities 1.6. Conducting a multi-sector household surveys to identifies and communicat multi-sectoral needs of IDPs2.1 Reinforcing/setting Complaints and Feedback Mechanisms (CFM)2.2 Capacity building of camp/site governance structure members and community in PSEA, Protection, GBV prevention and community engagement principles2.3 Awareness raising on usage of CFM and Linha Verde within IDPs community 2.4 Promoting referral pathways2.5 Service monitoring surveys and satisfaction surveys 3.1 Assessing shelter needs/risks of IDPs in targeted areas3.2 Conducing technical insfrastructure and urban assessment serving the camp/site development and re-organization 3.5 Post- settling and camp management monitoring3.6 Support and train camp committees on contingency planning and DRR within the camp/area

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The project will ensure protection mainstreaming process in order to esure DO NOT HARM approach, promoting meaningful access, safety and dignigy in all actions. In order to guarantee this principles all the staff will be trained on the principles and procedures in place and will sign the reference documents such as the Internal Code of Conduct, the Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse Policy (PSEA) and the AVSI Children Safeguarding Policy documents as well as the AVSI lead protection mainstreaming checklists. In order to avoid conflicts between IDPs (new or extended) and host communities, targeting for individual assistence will be done in consultation with the communities, defining together the criteria of vulnerability and ensuring that all the different groups are represented in the consultations. This will also ensure the use of the principles of impartiality and neutrality: no distinction of nationality, race, religion, social condition and political affiliation will be made and no sides will be taken in any dispute. Protection monitoring will be garuatee through routinely monitoring access to services by women, girls, boys and men through spot checks and discussions with communities, and promptly address the obstacles to equal access

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

This project will follow AVSI Foundation SOP described in the AVSI FOUNDATION ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY (DIGE 11/2020) on ‘feedback and accountability mechanism’. Newly recruited and old staff are always oriented and re-oriented on the need to seek the views of communities concerning program implementation. Beneficiaries and the population at large is also made aware of the forms available to provide suggestions and complaints. Furthermore, the project can leverage on the harmonized CFM promoted by the CCCM cluster and already in use by AVSI personnel since October 2021 that allow a punctual and harmonized management of complaints and feedback. AVSI Foundation will carry out customized multi-sector household surveys and ad hoc studies to shed lights on the condition of special and vulnerable groups. The results will be presented and validated with camps district representing all communities in the camp as well as SPDI and local government, as a means to establish a productive fact-based conversation with affected populations.In AVSI’s experience, this has proven time and again to be particularly productive, as not only people’ concerns and priorities are taken into account, but, more importantly, solutions in line with the specific contexts and people capable to support such initiatives are identified. Example of these practices could be found at: https://assessments.hpc.tools/assessment/c891c32a-7c60-4aa9-bf6d-a8b48a84730ahttps://reliefweb.int/report/south-sudan/multi-sector-household-survey-rumbek-north-county-lakes-state-june-2021The capillary and continuous presence that AVSI has in the communities targeted by the intervention (AVSI has more than 80% of field based staff, including senior positions) ensures that such fruitful cooperation with project beneficiaries continues throughout the project’s implementation.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

In the present project proposal screening, surveillance and participation of people with disabilities have been included as key activities and indicators. During their implementation, the detection of people with disabilities will be reported (disaggregated by gender and age) and given special attention. This translates into prioritizing the abovementioned target population as to the assistance for Shelter, NFI and Protection, especially for those cases having motor disabilities to which AVSI Foundation will provide logistics support.AVSI Foundation will, when possible, organize specific studies on the inclusion and conditions of persons living with a disability and will utilize such studies to devise inclusive strategies on round table with PwD as well as local communities. Examples of such work in similar context can be found at: https://assessments.hpc.tools/assessment/e3f17126-d1f5-4a78-80a4-99dea7c46e69AVSI Foundation will ensure the implementation of sensitization sessions throughout its activities to ensure that these people are supported within the community and given special attention when coordinating and planning activities with local SDSMAS and partners
