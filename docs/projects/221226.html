Project Summary

The project aims to improve access to food, enhance livelihoods and strengthen the resilience of vulnerable and food-insecure communities affected by the crisis and climate change in the Far North region. This project will focus on the deployment of food assistance through the distribution of food and cash transfers conditional on participation in activities, as well as on strengthening the technical capacities of beneficiaries. The project will support 40,000 vulnerable people (60% women), including small farmers, internally displaced people (IDPs), returnees and local populations affected by the crisis and climate change in localities in the Far North region. To implement the activities, Community-Based Participatory Planning (CBPP) sessions will be carried out in collaboration with local community members (women and men), decentralized technical services of key ministries and partners to identify the real needs and priorities of local communities that will help affected populations develop their own response strategies and strengthen their resilience. This people-centred approach ensures the provision of effective solutions with beneficiaries and reduces reliance on relief.
The project's objective will be achieved through the implementation of two major interventions: food assistance for early recovery (30,000 targeted beneficiaries) and food assistance for resilience (10,000 targeted beneficiaries). Food assistance will be provided using two modalities: cash transfers and in-kind food distribution. The project will provide six (06) months of food assistance to vulnerable households participating in early recovery activities and 10 months to those participating in resilience-building asset creation activities. Early recovery activities will focus on the socio-economic empowerment of beneficiaries. Asset creation activities are grouped around three priorities: climate adaptation and mitigation; infrastructure rehabilitation; and food system resilience.
WFP will work closely with government line ministries, cooperating partners and communities to ensure project implementation and sustainability. Nutrition, gender and protection issues are taken into consideration at all stages of project design, implementation, monitoring and evaluation. A gender and protection risk analysis will be carried out, and the most vulnerable will be targeted, including female-headed households, pregnant and lactating women, and people with disabilities. Project sites will be located in safe areas and at a convenient distance from beneficiaries' homes. To support successful project implementation and ensure sustainability, capacity-building sessions will be organized for all stakeholders on gender and protection policy, project management, monitoring and nutrition.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

This project will be one of a series of interventions that WFP Cameroon is implementing as part of its Country Strategic Plan (CSP) 2022 - 2026, in collaboration with partners to support the Government of Cameroon in achieving Sustainable Development Goal ODD 2 "Zero Hunger". The intervention will provide livelihood support to targeted groups, including the creation and regeneration of productive assets, construction or rehabilitation of climate change adaptation infrastructure, value chain development and market support for smallholder farmers. The aim of these activities is to meet the immediate food needs of vulnerable individuals and households, while helping them to strengthen their livelihoods, reduce the risk of natural disasters (mainly droughts and floods) and build community and household resilience to present and future shocks, through sustainable market access (diversification of income sources) and self-sufficiency (breaking dependence on humanitarian aid).Interventions will focus on the creation of assets linked to climate change adaptation, the restoration of community infrastructures and the resilience of food systems. The assets created will enable households affected by climate shocks, conflict-related displacement or economic shocks to cope with the disruption created by these shocks, diversify their livelihoods and return to their previous or better situation.The project targets food-insecure populations affected by climate change, as well as small-scale farmers, including refugees, internally displaced persons, returnees and host populations. To implement this intervention, the WFP will sign agreements with partners (international and local NGOs) to carry out activities in the field. Payment instruments (a telephone and a SIM card) will be distributed to beneficiaries to receive cash transfers conditional on participation in asset creation activities. Inputs, materials/tools and equipment will also be provided to beneficiaries for the proper execution of activities.WFP will support solutions aimed at removing bottlenecks to make food systems and value chains more productive and inclusive for women, youth and marginalized populations, as well as more environmentally sustainable and better able to provide healthy and nutritious diets for all, particularly in the event of shocks.
