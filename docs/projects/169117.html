Project Summary

Anera has been able to promptly respond to the health needs after the Beirut blast through receiving shipments from US worth $12 million including over 56 tons of full of cardiovascular, diabetes, neurology, respiratory and ophthalmology medications, as well as roughly 200,000 N-95 masks, tens of thousands of exam gloves and protective coveralls, and wound care supplies. medicines, medical supplies, and personal protective equipment from US. Anera was able to facilitate this shipment quickly despite the myriad funding, logistical and political challenges in Lebanon because the connections and partnerships made in Lebanon over the last 40+ years. Anera worked directly with the American Task Force for Lebanon (ATFL), the Afya Foundation, FedEx and others to ensure the quick delivery of these critical items. This shipment is Anera’s first of 24 expected medical donation deliveries in response to the blast.
As of now, Anera’s team expects more than $ 50 million USD of donated medicine and medical supplies to arrive to Beirut in the coming few weeks. In the next month, Anera will be sending, from the US, UK and Canada, 12 shipments – nine by air and three by sea – to Lebanon, with another 12 air and sea shipments in discussion. Anera’s total medical donations during its last year fiscal year budget was worth 80 million USD, with only $ 12 million USD of the total going to Lebanon. Anera’s team expects a marked huge growth in its medical donations to Lebanon in responding to the current crisis and to the ongoing COVID-19 and economic crises. While this growth is needed and responds to acute, critical the urgent needs, Anera’s Medical Donations program will be growing five times in its volume in less than two months.
Through this project Anera is requesting support in responding to the blast to comprehensively cover the assessed needs correlated to the health sector. The project aims at upgrading RHUH warehouse which is allocated as a storage and distribution point for the medical donations received by Anera’s RHUH’s main warehouses are being utilized to store the medicines received from the damaged Karantina warehouse. The project will target as well the rehabilitation of St. George Hospital PHCC which was moderately damaged. The hospital has secured resources from 14 projects to rehabilitate the majorly affected hospital, but have not received donations to rehabilitate the PHCC. Through this project, Anera will contribute in 50% staffing support to the PHCC, and the community health center, along with (3 months) short term supply of essential chronic medicines, and medical supplies to the PHCC which is in high need of human, and medical resources to cover the access of the high number of patients. Anera predicts that after this initial 3-month period, the center will be able to sustain operations at full capacity without heavy support.
Anera is in continuous coordination with the MOPH, the health sector actors, and is in the process of being part of the national MHPSS taskforce with the national mental health program. Any distribution of mental health drugs will be in accordance of the drug list, and in coordination of NMHP, and the chronic medicine program at the PHCC level. Anera’s strong M&E procedures will be put in place to ensure the project effectiveness.

1 - Needs

St. George Community Health CenterThe Qatari Red Crescent donated a field hospital set-up to St. George Hospital, which is not currently operational due to lack of staffing, latrines, medicine, equipment and supplies. With the support of the project, St. George Hospital management is planning to utilize the set-up to serve as a community health center to serve a wide catchment area (including Achrafieh, Rmeil, Mdawwar, Marfaa, Bourj Hammoud and beyond). Initial estimates indicate that this center will serve at least 700 patients per month. The center will provide free-of-charge health services that benefits all populations, including Lebanese, refugees, migrant workers and others. Services include family medicine, internal medicine (including Cardiology), Ophthalmology, ENT, General Surgery, Psychiatry, Minor Surgery, and a patient monitoring/treatment unit.The RHUH hospital had been already operating on limited resources due to the economic crisis and the dual impact of the blast and COVID-19. These combined factors encouraged Anera and other organizations to support the hospital by providing increased quantities of much needed medicines, supplies and equipment. Additionally, the hospital had been suffering from budget shortages that limited the ability to maintain available storage space. In order to expand the safe and efficient storage capacity of RHUH, especially with the predicted need for even more medicines and medical supplies in the next 3+ months, the hospital voiced to Anera an immediate need to upgrade secondary storage spaces that can accommodate additional stocks of medicine and medical supplies in the short term, but will also be available for longer term use. The stocks include the high amounts of medicine delivered by Anera. Based on the assessments, the warehouse needs around 5 ACs, Surveillence Cameras, Security Alarm, and fire security. These installations are essential for quality assurance, and are part of the monitoring procedures to ensure the safe and secure storage and distribution of medical donations. Anera has secured at least 70 tons of high quality donated medicine and medical supplies and needs support in covering the cost of immediate shipping, storing and distributing the assistance to hospitals and PHCCs. Donations respond to gaps in chronic and acute medicines, cancer medicine, mental health medicine, medical supplies, SRH supplies, and disability aid tools and supplies. Anera’s team expects more than $ 50 million USD of donated medicine and medical supplies to arrive to Beirut in the coming few weeks. In the next month, Anera will be sending, from the US, UK and Canada, 12 shipments – nine by air and three by sea – to Lebanon, with another 12 air and sea shipments in discussion. Anera’s total medical donations during its last year fiscal year budget was worth 80 million USD, with only $ 12 million USD of the total going to Lebanon. Anera’s team expects a marked huge growth in its medical donations to Lebanon in responding to the current crisis and to the ongoing COVID-19 and economic crises. While this growth is needed and responds to acute, critical the urgent needs, Anera’s Medical Donations program will be growing five times in its volume in less than two months. This highly justifies the need to cover the shipment costs of the high amounts of medications.

2 - Activities

Activity 1: Rehabilitation of St. George's PHCC and Upgrade of RHUH Warehouse - UPDATE 30 NOVEMBER 2020: this activity will not implemented during the project period. Activity 2: Support St. George Community Health Center The project will support the temporary community health center with the installation of latrines, as well as equipment, medicine, and medical supplies. The center will serve at least 700 beneficiaries per month with a free-of-charge range of medical services including medical/surgical specialties, Obstetrics and Gynecology and Mental Health. The number estimates are provided by the Saint George hospital and are expected especially with the delivery of the free of charge service to patients from different nationalities. Activity 3: Provision of Essential Medications and Medical Supplies:Anera's procurement team will lead a fast-track competitive procurement of medicines, medical consumables, and reproductive health supplies sufficient to St. George PHCC's and Community Health Center's needs for at least 3 months. Chronic medications will be delivered in coordination with YMCA to be supplied to St. George PHCC through the regular channels of the MoPH Chronic Medicine Program. Other supplies will be delivered directly to the PHCC.In addition to locally-procured medicines and supplies, Anera will provide large quantities of high quality medicines and medical supplies to PHCCs and public and private hospitals in Beirut. Additionally, donations will include disability aid devices and supplies, psychiatric medicines and SRH supplies. Distribution to PHCCs is implemented in partnership with the MoPH Chronic Medicine Program (YMCA). Distributions to hospitals are made from RHUH secondary warehouse, which Anera will operate in coordination with the Higher Relief Committee. Only pre-approved varieties and quantities are shipped to Lebanon, as Anera does not facilitate shipping universal/preset packages but rather responds to local demands (itemized review with recipients). All shipments are imported in line with the Lebanese rules and regulations and shipments are approved by MoPH.Activity 4: Provision of human resources (physicians, nurses, midwives) support to St. George PHCC and community health center: The project will contribute to 50% of the staffing cost of physicians, nurses and midwives for three months to strengthen service provision capacities of the PHCC and community health center. This will be provided in the form of a grant to the PHCC after reviewing staff contracts and pay scales. Activity 5: Provision of medical equipment: The project will provide basic medical blood pressure machine, EKG machine, autoclave, and defibrillator., and an ultrasound for obstetric/gynecological imaging.M&E: - Regular visits and spot checks by Anera to RHUH warehouse, the PHCC, and community health center to ensure the effective implementation of activities - Monthly reports will be requested by the hospitals to monitor the distribution of medications, and the delivery of services at the community center - Patient Satisfaction through surveys
