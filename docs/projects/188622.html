Project Summary

The project is mainly about providing monthly unconditional cash assistance for people in need in Homs city to cope with the huge economic inflation and the deterioration of the economic situation and obtain their basic needs of food or non-food items.
the project will identify the most in need families in the city through the help of the association database and the coordination with the most active social actors in the city.
about 1000 families in Homs city will receive a cash assistance of 40 $ on a monthly basis for 1 year to enhance their resilience through the current harsh economic situation that the country are going through.
the transfers will be done using money transfers company spread in the city.
this kind of assistance will be provided to the most vulnerable families and it will save their dignity and help them purchase their basic needs freely like any others.

4 - Needs

Ten years into the Syria crisis, humanitarian needs are deepening. Ongoing insecurity and the compounded impact of displacement, combined with worsening socio-economic conditions characterized by sharp currency depreciation, record-level price increases and cuts in subsidized goods have prompted an almost 20 per cent increase in the number of people in need compared to early 2020. 82% of Syrians report a significant deterioration in their ability to meet basic needs since August 2019 and 65% of Syrians report currently being unable to meet the basic needs of their household. Homs governorate have more than 800,000 PIN 20,000 of them are in a acute need for any form of assistance. ( 2021 NEEDS AND RESPONSE SUMMARY) therefore the Association is aiming at providing as much as possible of direct cash assistance to the most in need families in the city to help them meet their monthly basic needs and mitigate the devastating effect of their increased poverty due to the current economic situations.

5 - Implementation of activities

1- the association will identify & register the most in need families around the city through the help of local charity organization and its own database. 2- cash transfers to families on a monthly basis through money transfer companies. 3- continuous monitoring of the families to detect the project impact.

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risks: 1- There is a risk of discrimination or perceptions of discrimination if a service is provided to specific groups or individuals at the exclusion of others Likelihood: Medium Impact: High Mitigating measures: Al-Birr will provide its services to most in need families with no discrimination, clear criteria will be considered to choose the beneficiaries according to the socio-economy study. 2. Inflation: likelihood: medium. Impact: High. Mitigating measures: Al-Birr will sign contracts and make agreements with some vendors in advance to provide basic materials with low prices to PINs in order to ensure stability in prices as much as we can. In addition, Al-Birr may increase the cash amount according to variance in prices.3. decreased capacity of FSP. Likelihood: medium. Impact: high. Mitigating measures: Al-Birr will contract with more than one FSPs in order to lower this risk, and lower load on each FSP.
