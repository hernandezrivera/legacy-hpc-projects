Project Summary

The targeted area suffers from many problems as a result of the difficult conditions, especially after the crisis, and strange cultures were born in the region, including the marginalization of women and their isolation from the outside community. It is located in places where military conflicts occur, regionally and globally, and the aforementioned areas were more affected as a result of the concentration of armed groups during the period of their presence in the region
Based on the assessment of the targeted area and on focused discussion sessions with women and girls, we believe that it is necessary to intervene and work to support women over 18 years and girls over 14 years in the proposed area in order to reactivate their role in society and secure their freedom. We believe that this is done by providing a humanitarian response. To combat gender-based violence, reproductive health and awareness through implementing activities (pregnancy care and pregnancy control - providing medicines - family planning methods - psychosocial support - vocational training - recreational activities - awareness sessions possible to target men and teenage youth - case management for survivors of violence) and its purpose is to provide a comprehensive response to survivors of violence and rape who are at risk and in dire humanitarian conditions
These reasons were the motivation to seek to provide our services and to request partnership from the United Nations Population Fund, Based on the above, we suggest that the targeting areas be:
Tal Hamis town - one team
Jazaa town and its countryside - one team
Al Yarubiyah Town - one team
The following services will be provided through the project proposal because the targeted area is in dire need of them:
• We will work to provide quality integrated reproductive health services, integrated primary health care with a focus on reproductive health and psychosocial support will be provided through medical teams, in addition to improving referral mechanisms to secondary services. Particularly for the people affected by the crisis, including host communities and displaced populations.
• Distributing hygiene bags that preserve women’s dignity
• Distribution of medical medications to beneficiaries
• Our Foundation, through its teams, will contribute to prevent and respond to gender-based violence, with a special focus on vulnerable women in humanitarian settings.
• Through this project, our Foundation will work on Strengthened capacity of community leaders and young people to advocate against gender-based violence, including early and forced marriage.
