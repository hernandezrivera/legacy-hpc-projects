Project Summary

Increasing number of GBV cases in Hudur and Wajid are constant for IDPs and Drought affected people, Many cases of GBV were underreported due to a culture of silence and fear of consequences towards the survivor from society such as stigma, isolation and revenge attacker is an issue in Gender Based Violence (GBV) which is a pressing problem, not only because it violates human rights but also it poses a challenge to public health, economic and social development. High rate of GBV are caused by the weakness and the lack of adequate capacity to prevent and respond, with situation of Wajid demanding an urgent intervention. Traditional leader are the main providers of justice in Wajid and Hudur Given the weaknesses in the formal justice system most of the population relies on customary laws and on traditional elders to adjudicate their problems. Traditional elders and Sheiks can be unsympathetic to GBV survivors. They often reach solutions that are problematic from a protection perspective. Traditional elders are generally older men, with deeply ingrained patriarchal views. They often seek restorative solutions, which promote community harmony, rather than solutions to hold perpetrators to account Despite the existing GBV prevention and response mechanisms in Hudur and Wajid are still needs stepping up for prevention, Response and coordination mechanisms. The little efforts are faced by a countless of obstacles which if not tacked might lead to erosion of the gains already made. As well as the existing challenges on the ground concerning prevention and response of GBV towards protection of IDPs, Returnees, drought affected people and Host Community. ARD intends to promote and protect the dignity of most vulnerable people in IDPs and Host Community through ensuring adequate prevention and mitigation of GBV cases as well as facilitating timely multi sectoral response to SGBV survivors through linkage with health services and referral for better counseling and treatment of the survivors

Project Activities

1.1 Increase provision of legal aid counseling and representation as well coordination mechanisms of Hanano Centre for GBV response. - Provision of legal assistance including representation and legal counseling to the GBV survivor, evicted people or those at risk of forced evictions. ARD will work law enforcement agency(Police/local administration) to harmonize and strengthen coordination between the clan elders and traditional judges to ensure GBV survivors are provide decent justices 1.2 Strengthen GBV response through established one-stop centre in Wajid and Hudur for survivors that can access complete package of service including basic psychosocial support, Medical, legal aid representation, Safe Shelter, Livelihood and Dignity kit. 1.3 Provision of Psychosocial counselling and Legal Advice for GBV survivors in Hudur and Wajid at established Reception and transit centres to identify person with concern and supported as well 1.4 Establishment Community conflict resolution mechanisms in Wajid so to strengthening and engaging community participation through community conflict resolution mechanisms in IDPs through media programs. 1.5 Improve Capacity of Wajid and Hudur Police officers on GBV case management so to enhance better protection and on timely response of GBV cases.

Mitigation Measures

ARD will engage project stakeholders at all stages particularly during the implementation stage of the project where concerned stakeholders including project beneficiaries, local authorities, justice institutions, law enforcement departments, relevant line ministries in both state and federal level will be involved to maximize the response toward the above mentioned problems. ARD is member of Protection Cluster Groups, Gender Based Violence Working Group (GBVWG), Child Protection Working Group (CPWG) and the network of legal aid providers in Somalia. And also ARD Participated monthly and quarterly meetings organized by different line ministries and UN agencies. These groups have various monthly meetings of which ARD is invited, in these meetings, ARD will table and share update information about the project and its impact as well as the challenges, particularly ARD will undertake various visits in police stations, central prisons and the attorney general office and will have meetings with the administrations of each of these institutions to address the problems that this project is intended to solve
