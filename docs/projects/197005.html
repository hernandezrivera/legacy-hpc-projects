Project Summary

The main objective of the project is to provide access to basic and improved livelihoods over a 2023 in order to bring about more profound change for 75,000 IDPs, returnees, and overburdened at-risk groups in the severely neglected sub-districts of the areas listed below than is possible with short-term projects in Ashara, Deir Ezzor, Abu Kamal, / Deir Ezzor Governorate.
The consequences of the conflict and the ongoing cholera and covid-19 are particularly severe, as many parts of different governorates have been turned into hard-to-reach areas where people suffer from lack of basic needs. This is due to the enormous destruction, including socio-economic infrastructure cut off from access to markets, loss of livelihood and income, increase in unemployment rate, lack of essential goods, and decreasing purchasing power and sharp devaluation of the currency. In addition, food security continues to deteriorate due to declining crop yields and economic and climatic shocks. This reconstruction and livelihood support will improve the livelihoods and socio-economic conditions of 75,000 people in areas with high displacement rates, and expand livelihood interventions to mitigate the impact of recent economic deterioration. This is done by strengthening resilience through building capacity for unemployed youth; and providing income-generating activities. Distribution and installation of solar systems, rehabilitation of power grids and rehabilitate roads and health centers. Also, irrigation canal to be supported. It should be noted that the project is consistent with HNO 2023and the Sector Strategic Objective for the ERL (SO3) and the priorities.
Strategic Objective 3: Increase the resilience of affected communities by improving access to livelihood opportunities and basic services, especially among the most vulnerable households and communities
The intervention areas may be increased according to the increase in the areas that will be under control during 2023.

3 - Needs

According to HNO 2023 and conducted assessment, an estimated 734,824 people are in need of Early Recovery and Livelihood Support in Deir Ezzor (74% of total population).77% of households report that they are unable to meet the basic needs of their families. 65% of available basic socioeconomic infrastructure is damaged. The vulnerability of the population has worsened compared to last year due to the impact of the Cholera pandemic. In addition, the overall poverty rate is estimated to be close to 80%. In addition, the informal exchange rate has increased to 5500SYP/USD. Unemployment is estimated at 65% of the working age population and over 60% of youth, and the income deficit of households where the head is a person with a disability, including female-headed households, is estimated at nearly 74%. 70% in need of electricity for livelihoods and high rate of debt as coping mechanism. Therefore, RSRP aims to reduce the loss of livelihoods and improve the socio-economic resilience of affected households. Priority will be given to scaling up support to people with disabilities and women with their own households to improve access to livelihoods, as well as in high-need areas where access to basic services is most limited, where there are high levels of destruction, and where there are spontaneous returnee/high influxes of IDP’s

4 - Implementation of activities

RSRP's responses are in line with the sector's response strategy which follows the needs-based approach, identification of PINs under HNO2023at sub-district level and adherence to the strategic objectives (SO3).RSRP proposes the activities mentioned in the project cost section. The main pillars followed to achieve the proposed responses in coordination with MoLA, STD, ERL sector and other relevant stakeholders are-Get approval in coordination with STD -The recruitment process. Publicize registration for soft skill training through local community and social media.-Identification of1000 beneficiaries in the targeted areas based on selection criteria that consider female HHs, people with disabilities without income, HHs without any income sources, and needy youth. The registration process is conducted through the registration form on KOBO. Procurement Process. Identification of 25000KW solar panels to be generated,15km of power grids and roads will be rehabilitated, 20km Irrigation canal to be supported and health center to be rehabilitated -To ensure quality, regular field visits will be conducted and field staff will report and compile progress of ERL initiatives through submission of 4Ws regularly.-Accountability to the affected population will be ensured through the establishment of a grievance mechanism and regular evaluation of feedback.
