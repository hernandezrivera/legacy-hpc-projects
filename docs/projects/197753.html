Project Summary

During our intervention on Association of Civil Society Organizations on Malaria Immunization and Nutrition ( ACOMIN) Project in the communities of Demsa and Ganye LGAs. We discovered that a lot of Gender Based Violence eg Wife Battering, Denial of Resources, Forced Marriage, Decision Making, Freedom of Speech etc are traditionally and culturally acceptable and we integrated awareness creation/sensitization on GBV and the communities that these intervention took place is yielding positive response but the communities are limited where by the community leaders are urging us to extend the intervention to other communities within the LGA. Also, there are numbers of Boys and Girls out of school and we hope to give them life skill education to be useful to themselves, their Families and communities in general. Therefore, this intervention hoped to reach Four Hundred and Sixty Three (463) Persons in Demsa and Two Hundred and Forty Seven (247) Persons in Ganye. Total number of Persons hoped to reach is Six Hundred and Ten (610) where we are going to consider the stipulated target by severity. We shall also consider social inclusion where by people living with Disabilities shall be considered.

2 - Activities

Due to Traditional/Cultural Beliefs, Wife Battering, Denial of Resources, Forced/Early Marriage, Decision Making, Freedom of Speech, Right to Education are not seen as an Offence. Therefore, this Intervention shall build the Capacity of Men to Respond Positively thereby Reduce if not Eliminate Gender Based Violence in the Communities of Intervention. The Violence Against Persons Prohibition (VAPP) Law has been passed and accented in the state and need Awareness Creation for the Public to know and Utilize it. There shall be Life Skill Education for Girls and Boys who are out of School to become useful to themselves, families and the Community in general.
