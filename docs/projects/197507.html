Project Summary

Through this project, Mehad aims to provide 30 health facilities in Northern Aleppo with solar Photovoltaic (PV) Systems with the following expected results: • Installing solar energy systems to power thirty health facilities in Northern Aleppo
o A total of 30 health facilities have been provided with solar energy systems; three (3) hospitals and 27 PHCCs;
o A total of 30 health facilities in Al Bab, Azaz, Jarablus, have full access to solar PV systems; and
o A total of 30 solar PV systems have been installed, tested and are ready to secure power.
• Strengthening the capacity and raising awareness of the LCs, FAHD, and key stakeholders (NGOs) about the importance of using renewable, safe energy and managing the solar PV systems.
o A total of 30 staff from 30 health facilities attended the technical training about the solar PV systems; and
o Ten (10) individuals from LCs, communities, FAHD, and key stakeholders (NGOs) have attended the technical training on solar PV systems.
• An impact study is reported as an outcome indicator to show the effectiveness of the installation of the solar PV systems.

3 - Needs

Mehad conducted the technical survey between 17/9/2022 and 24/9/2022. During this period, 30 health facilities were studied and technical studies were prepared according to the field technical assessment. The technical advisory body (consultant) was selected based on technical experience in previous projects with the UOSSM organization (projects to provide the primary care centers with solar energy systems where technical studies were prepared for 20 care centers and 5 secondary care centers). The facilities are equipped with sustainable solar energy systems), as well as work experience with SRTF. Each facility was studied separately by conducting a direct technical study on the activities of each center by appropriate technical methods, in addition to interviews with the management of each center to get the needed logistical information and information about the need of the centers and the working times (8 hours or 24/7), and as the main result according to the technical and administrative study: 29 medical facilities need a complete solar energy system, and one facility in need of restoration of the existing solar energy system. The time required to recover the full financial value of the project is on average three years, according to the initial consultant's assessment.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

In general, the IE will adhered to the following ‘Do No Harm’ principles:• No Risk of Parallel Public Entity;• A project as a Social Unifier, not a Social Wedge; and• No Negative Economic Impact on the Local Economy. The IE abides by the ‘Do No Harm’ principle in its programming, utilising standards and procedures according to its Accountability to Affected Population (AAP) Framework. The Framework ensures the principles of transparency, participation, Complaints Response Mechanism, learning and adaptation, and Preventing Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) are adhered to. The IE, through the AAP Framework, ensures that affected populations have access to timely, relevant, and transparent information about the IE and the proposed project. In the design phase of this project, the IE team will review the feedback received from the beneficiaries and the selected facilities staff. For years, the IE has promoted, advocated, and practiced humanitarian principles (humanity, neutrality, impartiality, and independence) through its activities. Cooperation and close coordination with the Health Directorates, local councils, and other significant stakeholders contribute to this. In addition, the IE follows other procedures and mechanisms to ensure its accountability to the affected population, including the "Do no harm" principle. PWDs and all gender groups can participate in the trainings proposed either on community committees, partners, or project team.


8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Mehad is an active member of the PSEA network and it is committed to protecting beneficiaries from sexual exploitation and thus it is very serious to adhere to the PSEA standards and to make it inclusive to accountability measures.To achieve that, all staff of the proposed projects will be required to sign a PSEA code of conduct at the contacting stage after being fully oriented on PSEA and the available complaints mechanisms. On the other hand, posters and banners about PSEA will be adopted and visible for beneficiaries in all project facilities and the PSEA videos published by PSEA network will be shared on Mehad social media accounts i.e: Facebook.Mehad will raise the awareness of the beneficiaries at the levels of facility and community on how to recognize SEA cases and how they have the full right to report any of these cases through the PSEA network complaint hotline/ email which Mehad make them visible for all beneficiaries in the health facilities. Also, the PSEA focal point will conduct frequent training to the project staff on PSEA standards and the best practices to achieve high engagement between service providers and beneficiaries. Mehad is aware of the importance of the presence of female employees to conduct and follow PSEA activities, particularly when approaching female benefices. PSEA focal point will ensure the mainstreaming of PSEA standards and policy in all project activities.


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumptions: -The border between turkey and Syria remains open for humanitarian assistance and staff. -The security situation is stable to provide the services and there are no major movement or displacement occur or a change in the controlling map between the military forces.-The local health authorities keep supporting the provision of services through the project. -The staff of the project are competent to provide assured quality services.Risks:Overlapping in the provision of the services: Mehad will make sure to maintain high coordination levels with the health cluster and local health authorities. Through these coordination channels, Mehad will exchange information and ensure all parties are updated.Cross-border resolution is not renewed: Mehad will keep advocating with other partners in all possible platforms on the criticality of the resolution renewal. Mehad will also participate in all workshops conducted to discuss the contingency plan in case of a non-renewal scenario.Lack of health workforce availability: Mehad will announce the project job vacancies on all possible and relevant recruitment platforms and will also reach the health directorates to validate their credentials. Risk: PWDs and women may not be able to directly be involved in project implementation due to its nature "PV systems installation" however, all groups can participate for example: female engineers and PWDs may work as site engineers and supervisors i.e., vacancies will be open for all
