Project Summary

The proposed action will provide basic life support to persons with injuries, persons with disabilities and elderly health, rehabilitation and psychological needs through combination of designed activities.
The proposed action follows multidisciplinary and holistic approaches to better respond to most affected populations who suffer from lack of or/and difficulties to access to services. The project will build collaboration with 5 local service providers and organizations through partnerships. It will cover the Gaza Strip and Area C of four governorates of West Bank. The project will support the deployment of outreach and center-based teams to assist the vulnerable beneficiaries through the development of admission and exclusion criteria. These criteria will be based on the socioeconomic conditions and through coordination with local referral bodies to enhance trauma pathways and improve networking among action’s stakeholders. The project will also work on building capacity of partners and other stakeholders.

Needs

According to WHO and the Ministry of Health in Palestine, since the start of the ‘March of Return’ demonstrations, 191 people have been killed. Similarly, the number of people injured stands at 20 833, out of which 10,762 required hospitalization. Out of those hospitalized 5,048 (47%) were casualties of live ammunition gunshots injuries, 1,845 were children (17%). An astonishing total of 6,702 limb injuries have been registered since the beginning of demonstrations representing the highest type of injury of the caseload. No less than 1,200 injured people have been identified as in need of limb reconstruction. The injured will require up to 7 surgeries and extensive rehabilitation and treatment for up to 2 years. With the overwhelming number of casualties, hospitals are not able to provide the required comprehensive care and rehabilitation for persons with injuries which make them susceptible to disability. Cases of early discharge and pending list of persons with injuries in urgent needs of rehabilitation are rampant. This situation has also very negative impacts on the psychological state of the caregivers in addition to the injured themselves. It has also direct consequences on the situation of persons with disabilities at large, considering the overall lack of services and the current focus on victims of demonstrations. In early September 2018, HI implemented a rapid need assessment in Gaza. A total 259 persons with injuries and person with disabilities were consulted to identify gaps in the provision of health and rehab services. The survey identified lack of outreach services for the bedridden or insufficient capacity to provide outreach services compared to the needs. For example, 67% of the surveyed persons face difficulties to access center based programs and 49% don’t benefit from home-based programs. Lastly, 92% of the surveyed explained that the rehabilitation treatment is usually too short. Further investigation involved consultation of 12 DPOs and SHG, who revealed the following: persons with disabilities were neglected recently due to the acute crisis of Great March of Return, becoming even more vulnerable; and the continuum of health and rehabilitation care to persons with disabilities has deteriorated. In West Bank, many communities, particularly in Area C and Bedouin communities faced difficult access to basic health and rehabilitation care/services due to insecurity, restrictions on movements and checkpoints and violence. In the oPt, there is a big gap in comprehensive rehabilitation services using multidisciplinary approach and inclusive services. Poverty is also a factor in play; affected individuals consider seeking treatment but cannot afford the services or the associated cost of transportation. Furthermore, lack of accessible infrastructure, services and own habitation leave people with disabilities even more vulnerable, and isolates them further. Finally, inequality between males with disability and females with disability in accessing rehabilitation services is still a major aspect of concern that needs immediate actions.

Activities

HI, present in oPt since 2006, responds to the needs and demands of persons with disabilities/persons with injuries and other vulnerabilities. HI is an active member in Health and Nutrition cluster, trauma working group, disability working group, international disability group, and ICRC rehabilitation quarter group. HI initiated a local network in 2009 in the Gaza Strip, to respond adequately to crisis causalities. This network became a dominant body of exchanging referrals among local service providers and eased access to service for whom unable to reach it. Within this project, HI will work on strengthening referral pathways among the members of the local networks in addition to the professionals on the frontlines of UNRWA and Ministry of Health primary healthcare responders. In both Gaza and West Bank, the project will support home-based services and center-based services within rehabilitation centers and deployment of outreach services to health care actors. The project will make sure that gender equity is respected Among the mechanisms, tools and of the whole process of intervention. Expected Result 1: Persons with injuries and people with disabilities have increased independence and have improved access to basic rehabilitation, health, and psychosocial services through a multidisciplinary outreach and center-based services in line with the continuum of care.1.1 Provision of outreach and center-based services of wound dressing, physiotherapy, occupational therapy and psychological support for 750 persons with injuries and 750 persons with disabilities in Gaza and WB targeted governorates. Gender will be considered at the level of beneficiaries during phases of identification and provision of services; and at the level of project team – rehabilitation teams will be composed by two third of females staff. 1.2 Training and follow up of at least 1,500 family members and caretakers on physiotherapy, occupational therapy services and psychological activities with persons with injuries persons with disabilities in Gaza and WB targeted governorates. 1.3 Purchase and donation of 550 assistive devices for 180 persons with injuries & 370 persons with disabilities as per their identified needs (in Gaza and West Bank’s targeted governorates. The assistive devices will support the functional independency of persons with disabilities and enhance their participation in the community. The project will focus on the women and girls with disability who usually have limited access to such devices thus their participation in the community limited as well. 1.4 Support independence through reasonable home adaptation works for 50 persons with injuries/persons with disabilities to enhance mobility and daily life activities in Gaza strip. 1.5 Technical support to partners’ rehabilitation teams though monthly technical trainings, quarterly exchange meetings and monitoring field visits in Gaza and West Bank’s targeted governorates. Expected Result 2: Support continuum of health and rehabilitation care through referral and coordination mechanisms. 2.1 Technical support and mobilization of healthcare actors (UNRWA/MoH in Gaza, and mobile health clinics in West Bank) on beneficiaries identification and rehabilitation needs, and on prevention of secondary complications of impairment. 2.2 Collaborate with key rehab and health actors in the targeted areas (UNRWA/MoH in Gaza, and mobile health clinics in West Bank) to improve referral pathways coordination. The technical support and mobilization will include tools and mechanism to facilitate the identification and referrals of women and girls with disabilities. 2.3 Provision of financial support to vulnerable people in need of acute center-based health and rehabilitation services in West Bank’s targeted areas. These services are related to disability complications through covering fees and transport costs to overcome financial barriers to access services to which they are referred.
