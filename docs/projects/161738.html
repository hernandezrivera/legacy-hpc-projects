Project Summary

The prolonged armed conflict in the North East in Nigeria has resulted to the destruction of basic infrastructure and social services such as markets, schools and wash services, limiting access to farmland which has declined livelihoods of IDPs, returnees and host communities.One in four households in the North East lack access to income generating activities as a result of the conflict while, 650 public building were totally broken-down.
In 2018, one million IDPs remain in host communities while 1.6 million have returned to their home areas.unprecedented displacements continue to stretch the capacity of the already vulnerable facilities with per-existing inadequate capacity to withstand and deliver basic services, thus limiting access to livelihoods opportunities to vulnerable women, in which access to income generating opportunities is limited to smaller and less secured plots of land , less access to physical inputs, less use of labor,and less access to extension services, but earning and producing more than male farmers.This unfortunate situation of imbalance in access to infrastructure and services has led to tensions between IDPs, returnees, and host communities. The extreme damage of infrastructures and services, both at LGAs and community levels perpetuates the continued marginalization, deepens the under-development in the North East.Poverty index in Nigeria was at 76,3 % in 2010; while access to basic infrastructure such as WASH, Health facilities. of the overall people in need of early recovery assistance,1 million are IDPs in host communities, 1,5 millions IDPs and returnees. and 2.9 millions in host communities

1 - Needs

The protracted crisis in the North East of Nigeria, has disrupted livelihoods, destruction of basic infrastructures and social services. many schools, health and WASH facilities were broken down by the insurgents in the three most affected states by the insurgency. there is restriction of movement to due to insecurity, limited access to farmland, destruction of health facilities ( General hospital Gubio), limited access to SC ( stabilization centers ) for SAM with medical complications. the assessment carried out by AHA , in some localities in Damboa and Askira/Uba in Borno State showed that women have fewer livelihoods opportunities compared to men. As a matter of fact, women and girls are limited to smaller business, such as cap making. kitchen garden, yet, they earn and produce more than the male.

2 - Activities

Act1 restoration of water facilities through construction/ rehabilitation of nonfunctional water points. Act2 maintenance of water points through distribution of Aqua-tabs ( chlorine) Act3 capacity building of community volunteers on maintenance of rehabilitated/constructed basic infrastructure ( water point). Act4 providing environmental sanitation: - employment through cash for work for solid waste management, cleaning of drainage - distribution of sanitation tools( wheel barrows, gloves, brooms) - capacity building of local authorities on solid waste management disposal. Act5 rehabilitation of electric system in local government departments through provision of wires. Act6 distribution of office furniture to ensure re-opening and functionality. Act7 hygiene promotion, and key messages Act8 distribution of hygiene kits ( buckets, soaps, 2 jerry can, 1 for fetching water and the other one for storing water to the most vulnerable households) and cholera preparedness accessories.
