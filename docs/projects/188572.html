-The cost per student is 82 USD.

-The total persons that Al-Ameen organization reached (all the Sectors) in 2021 is 360830 Beneficiaries.
Al-Ameen organization will support formal and non-formal education for 12000 (5-17, 6500 boys, 5500 girls) who are dropping out or at risk of school drop-out, in addition to 380 (212 males and 168 females) education personal through supporting schools and educational centers that have ended fund or will end in 2022, in addition to providing the dropped-out children with non-formal education. This project is critical due to its significance in mitigating social risks that might lead to educational gaps if they were not dealt with immediately. These risks include early marriage, recruitment into armed groups, and child labor.
the interventions of the project will be as the following:
1- Al-Ameen organization will support formal education in 20 schools where those schools include 8300 students (4700 boys and 3600 girls) and their fund has ended or will end soon. The intervention minimizes the risk of students dropping out, where monthly incentives to teachers will be provided, as they don’t have any alternative fund.
2-Al-Ameen will support the educational process by supporting 3700 Out of School Children (OOSC), (1900 boys and 1800 girls), (65% IDPs and 35% HCs) with non-formal education in 15 schools through catch up and remedial classes.
3- 380 Teachers (212 males and 168 females) will be provided with incentives, as they don't receive any financial support from any other NGOs.
4-monthly operational costs, and heating materials to face the cold of winter.
5-PSS weekly sessions will also help children, who spent a long time away from school, ridding of the harsh experience of war conditions and motivate them to continue their studies at school.
6- The 20 targeted schools will be equipped with safety and security tools, also, the project will involve 195 children with disability, who drop out of school, and overcome the difficulties that they may face through providing them with transportation service in addition to providing schools with tools for children with disability.
7- Teaching materials will be distributed to Schools.
8- education kits will distribute for students, teachers, in addition, recreational kits to facilitate PSS sessions.
9- at the beginning of the project Al-Ameen organization will conduct a meeting with the local community and local actors to ensure their participation in the project, explain the project goals and take their suggestions and notes to develop the project activities and implementation.
10- AlAmeen organization will coordinate with the Education cluster and with other organization who works in the same sector and field to reach the right beneficiaries and avoid any duplication.
11-since the wash facilities are playing big role in increasing the access to the schools, the project will give this issue high priority and will take in our consideration the gender issues and the segregation between the toiles of boys and girls, in addition, supporting the schools by ramps, hand drills and the toilets for the disabilities.
-the project will contribute to increasing the quality of education in the targeted schools by training the teachers (males and females) in several topics like EIE, PSS, Child protection and teaching methodologies.

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

Although all Syrian population has been heavily affected by the current crisis, due to traditional local norms, women are exposed to additional protection related risks. All types of violence are cited as occurring in opposition areas of Syria, with domestic violence, sexual exploitation and child marriage as top three. While sexual exploitation is somehow new, child marriage and domestic violence are often normalized and ingrained in the culture. Women and girls are exposed to structural and societal risks as the lack of safe spaces and public transportation, limited freedom of movement, poverty, unemployment, displacement, stress associated to war, gender role change, lack of documents. All these poses enormous pressure, men feeling they failed in protecting and securing a good life for their families. With men perceiving women as a threat to their control due to gender roles change, domestic violence has increased in Syria. Whereas men actions are explained as a result of stress, women are often blamed for provoking the violence or allowing it to happen. Parental violence against children has also been reported as increasing. Displacement and living in camps are noted as being particularly unsafe environments for women/girls due to the lack of privacy (in tents or in latrines), overcrowding and mixing of people, poverty, financial desperation, and lawlessness. The WoS Protection analysis reveals that 17 sub-districts in Idleb and 23 In Aleppo are facing severe.
