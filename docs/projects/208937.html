Project Summary

Alight will support community-based approaches that enhance protection for all Persons of Concern and strengthen community-based protection structures (CBPNs, Child Protection Committees, Women groups..) which will identify protection concerns, raise awareness on protection-related issues, respond to issues at the community level where appropriate, and refer to relevant partners based on need. Alight will continue to build their capacity and ensure they are operational. This project includes life-saving intervention for the affected population, including case management for PSNs, GBV survivors, UASC and other children at risk, PSS for adults, GBV survivors and children, including children with disabilities, cash or in-kind assistance depending on the case, provision of dignity kits. Alight and its partners will improve monitoring, identification and reporting of protection incidents and analyze trends including grave child rights violations, including through the use of focus groups and specific protection assessments. Alight will support timely and targeted responses to protection incidents, information sharing, and awareness-raising along with other protection measures to mitigate protection risks and respond to actual protection incidents and concerns. Alight intends to establish and support temporary Women Centers and Child Friendly Spaces as entry point for protection activities, identification of cases, awareness raising and PSS and recreational activities. Protection help desks will be established as well with the support of MEAL department, to increase the access to information and specific assistance for the affected population, as part of the efforts of FCRM and AAP. Moreover, the proposed interventions are in line with the Sudan Humanitarian Response Plan.

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

In all protection interventions, ALIGHT will consider persons ages, gender, and other characteristics for better assessment of their risks, capacities, solutions and provide meaningful services to all without discrimination but rather inclusion for a stronger response. Effective accountability and protection programming will pay attention to diverse and intersecting forms of structural oppression, inequalities, and equitable provision of information, services, and assistance to men, women, boys and girls. ALIGHT will train the project staffs, volunteers and protection networks on the essentials of AGD approach and its importance in protection programming. ALIGHT ensures protection measures and prevention of exploitation and abuse (PSEA) across its workforce and partners. Additionally, ALIGHT ensures that its long-term, temporary, and volunteer workers and implementing partners working with affected populations understand, sign, and adhere to a Code of Conduct stating their commitment to respect and foster humanitarian standards and the rights of beneficiaries. To eliminate exploitation risks, clear information about intervention procedures is provided to prevent any form of exchange for assistance.

4.1.1 - How will the project use community feedback to improve and adapt activities? (open-ended/free text, required)4

The primary aim of collecting feedback is to capture the voices and sentiments of those directly benefiting from the project initiatives. By delving into the perspectives and experiences of the beneficiaries, ALIGHT aims to gain invaluable insights into the quality, accessibility, and effectiveness of the provided services. Through this comprehensive feedback mechanism, the organization intends to validate the extent to which the interventions align with the diverse needs and expectations of the target population. By proactively seeking input from the beneficiaries, ALIGHT continually aspires to refine and enhance its services. The assessment's findings and recommendations will be pivotal in shaping future initiatives, ensuring the organization remains aligned with its communities' evolving needs and aspirations. ALIGHT recognizes that beneficiary satisfaction is an essential indicator of the project's success, as it signifies its meaningful impact on individual lives. Through this diligent assessment, the organization demonstrates its commitment to transparency, accountability, and the continuous improvement of its interventions, fostering a harmonious and responsive relationship with the communities it supports.

4.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will support Inter-Agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including Sexual exploitation and abuse complaints?

The Project will ensure that participation, feedback and complaints mechanisms are integrated in all strategies and plans all throught the project cycle. This is to ensure the project decisions and actions are responsive to the expressed priorities, needs, capacities and views of all persons of concern irrespective of Age, Gender, Diversity and Disability backgrounds. This is aimed at ensuring meaningful participation, enhanced communication and transparency, improved feedback and response. ALIGHT will ensure a balanced boys, girls, women and men in all project activities. Data for beneficiaries will be disaggregated by gender, age groups and location, as the project activities are tailored for different groups in line with developmental stages, needs and vulnerabilities and basing on relevant parameters appropriate in reverence to heightened protection risks of the persons of concern. The Project will ensure at all persons of concern have equal and non- discriminatory access to protection, assistance and solutions, this will build on their resilience and recovery. Alight will maintain effective feedback systems ( including comments, suggestions and complaints) using a variety of communication channels that are accessible to all persons of concern and that are appropriate for both sensitive and non- sensitive feedback e.g. use of complaints boxes, toll free hot lines.In regard to PSEA; All Alight staffs have committed to and signed PSEA policy, Child Protection policy, Code of Conduct as well as the engaged community based networks Alight is a member of National and State levels PSEA task force and regularly coordinates with other partners in relevant PSEA forums.
