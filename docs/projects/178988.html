Project Summary

The project aims to implement a number of activities in the city of Idlib during a 10-month period to meet the legal needs of the most IDPs and the host community in HLP and CSD, as well as training, counseling and outreach. Activities will be implemented as follows:
-HLP Support due diligence by conducting verification of private home ownership that benefits from IOM interventions in the rehabilitation of unfinished buildings / repair of damaged homes in northwestern Syria.
- Legal awareness sessions on CSD and HLP: The sessions will focus on CSD and HLP rights, the importance of civil documents, steps and processes for obtaining civil documents, recording life events and explaining the risks associated with obtaining civil documents.
- Individual legal advice on legal issues and inquiries related to CSD and HLP. In addition to providing information on administrative procedures and associated risks, the legal team will obtain information about HLP rights in the areas of return and provide legal advice on contracts and agreements.
- Legal assessments to collect detailed information about CSD and HLP needs, grievances and concerns, focusing on gender perspectives and legal documents issued by the governing authorities everywhere. This information will feed into the legal awareness sessions provided.
-HLP / CSD Trainings for humanitarian workers to provide customized capacity-building training courses on HLP and CSD related topics. Specialized training will target humanitarian workers who implement legal programming to ensure full compliance with the Cluster Protection and HLP TWG Guidance directives. The second set of courses provides an overview of CSD and HLP issues including definitions, procedures, conflict impact, available services, and referral to legal or auxiliary services; and HLP due diligence.
- Legal research reports documenting the results of family surveys
The project will be implemented in the center of the institution and through mobile teams where AHF has the ability to reach the region efficiently and with high quality. The project is designed to support the scientific process of preserving civil and real estate related documents
The project is designed to target all age groups of the host community and the displaced, as the project takes into account persons with disabilities and the elderly
The project will provide free services to all people without discrimination.
