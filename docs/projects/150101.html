Project Summary

PYU implemented two need assessments in the 5 villages of Eastern cluster of Salfit governorate one on( 2 and 5 /10/2017) and the other on September/2018 ,no changes were recognized, these need assessments were done by interviewing civil associations, rural men and women and local councils In each targeted village, they are under occupation and suffer from poverty, water shortage, settlements violation In addition to other agricultural and food problems that lead to high levels of Food insecurity 27.43 % food insecure in the cluster (DirIstya 22.35%, Kifl Haris 24.41 %, Haris 41.17 %, Marda 22.35 %and Qira 41 .17 %) / atlas poverty , these villages have the capacity to produce crops especially olive but it is constrained by limited access to land and water; in addition to the high cost of water and limited access to fertilizers and other inputs which are imported through Israeli suppliers, in these villages Women's activities are highly vulnerable and generally concentrated in unpaid and informal work. Women's have limited access to resources and markets that increase poverty, the project is designed to improve the livelihood conditions of the target areas through creation of jobs and income generation opportunities during the implementation of the project as well as at the long term, and improving the socioeconomic status of these communities, by protection and rehabilitation agricultural lands and improving water irrigation access, that will be coordinated directly with local councils and MOA in order to have commitment and ownership levels necessary to maintain sustainability and continuity which will also create jobs for community members.
So the main aim of the project is: To improve socioeconomic conditions of farming families in East Salfit Cluster through: Enable 2743 marginalized farming families to access and utilize productive land and water efficiently in East Salfit Cluster and increased the local production of olive and other plants to 293040 kg/year in addition to increase harvested water to 2160 m3 and improve the access to 30138 donumn land, this project is a multi year for 3 years, in each year same activities will be implemented in different areas according to the MOA Priorities in addition of that for this year 2019 Salfit exists on the priorities of HRP 2019 priorities-FSS-WBws with top priorities of rehabilitation of agricultural road lands and water sources , that linked with FOOD SECURITY CLUSTER OBJECTIVE 1: Livelihoods of vulnerable households are protected, and their resilience, food security and productive capacity restored/enhanced, through FSS activity: Rehabilitation and provision of productive assets and inputs, for farmers, herders, breeders, and fishers, including promotion of technical capacities and small scale production units, and using the BBB (Build Back Better) approach for increased resilience, Promoting local production and Improved sharing of responsibilities with line ministries.

Needs

This project will be implemented in 5 villages (Eastern cluster of Salfit governorate) in WB. Salfit is a center of 25 villages ( 75% area C, 18.8% area B and only 6.2 % area A) it is under occupation and suffers from a lot of restrictions and fragmentation practices that lead to reduce the livestock and agricultural activities and to high percentage of poverty ( Salfit exist on the priorities of HRP 2019 priorities-FSS-WBws with top priories of rehabilitation of agricultural road lands and water sources) , The Eastern Cluster with 66727 donums (32% from Salfit Governorate area)on 2003 in accordance with Minister of local government decision to raise the capacities and improve services to five villages (DirIstya, Kifl Haris, Haris, Marda and Qira). About 60% of the cluster’s area is agriculture, and about 40 % of the cluster’s land were confiscated by Israeli Occupation to establish settlements and build Separation Wall (east Salfit cluster strategic plane). PYU implemented two need assessments one on( 2 and 5 /10/2017) and the other on September/2018 but no changes were recognized by interviewing civil associations, rural men and women and local councils In each targeted village, which are under occupation and suffer from poverty, water shortage, settlements violation In addition to other agricultural and food problems that lead to high levels of Food insecurity 27.43 % food insecure in the cluster (DirIstya 22.35%, Kifl Haris 24.41 %, Haris 41.17 %, Marda 22.35 %and Qira 41 .17 %) / atlas poverty , these villages have the capacity to produce crops especially olive but it is constrained by limited access to land and water; in addition to the high cost of water and limited access to fertilizers and other inputs which are imported through Israeli suppliers, in these villages Women's activities are highly vulnerable and generally concentrated in unpaid and informal work. Women's have limited access to resources and markets that increase poverty, food insecurity, and malnutrition especially female farmers, but male farmer’s activities are the main and formal work for them men’s have the access to resources and markets. PYU recognized that most families are in need to access to their lands and resources especially rural women whose should be more active and highly motivated in the farming activities they play an effective and substantial role in the agricultural development process.( (approximately 35% of paid working women are employed in the agricultural sector) these Site visits and rapid assessments were conducted after full coordination with MOA that this cluster is within their strategy for intervention and priorities, and to identify immediate interventions needed It was clear that access and lack of agricultural roads are the major reasons behind abandoning some lands and decreasing their production. Drought and scarcity of water was also a major problem that adversely affected agricultural production so they are in need to rehabilitate their land and protect it from confiscation, as well as lack of knowledge of modern agricultural techniques that will be done by MOA., this project is designed to improve the livelihood conditions of the target areas through creation of jobs and income generation opportunities during the implementation of the project as well as at the long term, and improving the socioeconomic status of these communities, by rehabilitation agricultural lands and improving water irrigation access.So the main aim of the project is: To improve socioeconomic conditions of farming families in East Salfit Cluster through: Enable 2743 marginalized farming families to access and utilize productive land and water efficiently in East Salfit Cluster and increased the local production of olive and other plants to 293040 kg/year in addition to increase harvested water to 2160 m3 and improve the access to 30138 donumn land, it’s a multiyear for 3 years project with same activities in different areas according to MOA.

Activities

Palestinian Youth Union coordinated with MOA to implement project activities and improve capacity building for farmer’s men and women, selection criteria will target farmers who have land titles and the provision of these documents will be a precondition to proceed for signing the contracts. Further, involving the intended beneficiaries will positively impact the design and planning of this project each benefited farmer will contribute with 20% from the financial cost even by money or work , this will be done in full cooperation with MOA and Local Councils this project will increase local production of olive and other plants to 293040 kg/year in addition to increase harvested water to 2160 m3 ,it will be a multi year project for 3 years, in each year the activities will be implemented in different areas according to the MOA Prioritiesall these linked with FOOD SECURITY CLUSTER OBJECTIVE 1: Livelihoods of vulnerable households are protected, and their resilience, food security and productive capacity restored/enhanced, through FSS activity: Rehabilitation and provision of productive assets and inputs, for farmers, herders, breeders, and fishers, including promotion of technical capacities and small scale production units, and using the BBB (Build Back Better) approach for increased resilience, Promoting local production and Improved sharing of responsibilities with line ministries,before implementing any activity PYU will contact with FSS and MOA to be aware about other organizations working in the same area to avoid duplication or gabs. The following outputs and activities will be achieved:Output 1: 296 dunums cultivated land will be Increased. This will be supported by opening or rehabilitating agricultural roads to improve access according to the MOA priorities through the following activities : Activity 1.1: 144 dunum of land will be full rehabilitated: (DirIstya 40, Kifl Haris 40, Haris 20, Marda 20 and Qira 24) Activity 1.2: 152 dunum of land will be rehabilitated: (DirIstya 40, Kifl Haris 40, Haris 20, Marda 22 and Qira 30) Activity 1.3: 13200 m stone terraces will be constructed in 296 donums rehabilitated agricultural lands Activity 1.4: 6512 plants and seedlings will be planted in 296 donums rehabilitated agricultural lands Activity 1.5: 14800 m fences will be established in 296 donums rehabilitated agricultural lands will be constructed Output 2: Access and availability to water for irrigation will be increased .This output will be achieved through the construction of 36 water cisterns and conveyance systems. This will provide facilities to collect water 2160 m3 of water from rainfall, through the following activities : Activity 2.1: 36 water cisterns, capacity 60 m3, will be established for 296 donums rehabilitated agricultural lands Output 3: Improved access to cultivated and newly rehabilitated land covering 296 dunumsAgricultural roads will be constructed to aid farmers with limited access to their cultivated land. The work involves excavation and layering of base coarse aggregates. Estimated outcomes include 23 km estimated agriculture roads in place, allowing access to farmer’s especially rural women to 30138 donumn land through the following activity :Activity 3.1: 23 Km agriculture roads will be established in 296 donums rehabilitated agricultural lands.Output 4: responsibilities with MOA will be improvedFSS Activity: Improved sharing of responsibilities with line ministriesActivity 4.1 200 farming families’ especially rural women will be trained by MOA to Increase their awareness towards natural resources and introduce best agricultural practices and technologies to increase land productivity. The training will promote beneficiaries role in their communities as agents of change. Activity 4.2: audit report will be done at the end of the project.
