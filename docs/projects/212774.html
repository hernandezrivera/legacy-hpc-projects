Project Summary

According to HNO 2024, " 13.56 million people need of WASH assistance, of whom 51% are women and girls, 44 % are children and 17% have a disability. Almost 1.5 million more people are in acute need" .
By the end of 2023, during the implementation of one of Saed's projects within several camps in the Dana district, observations made by the project team, alongside feedback gathered through feedback mechanisms and monitoring activities, revealed a significant gap in the WASH sector in 9 camps. Consequently, Saed's team conducted an initial assessment of the needs of these camps through:
Technical assessment: This assessment relied on direct observation by technical staff resulting from field visits to each of the 9 targeted camps, as well as interviews with camp managers.
Conducting focus groups with 3 groups representing the population divided by age and gender, including women, children, the elderly, and persons with disabilities.
Here is a summary of the results:
- Total number of beneficiaries:16032 IDPs (3,367 men, 3,527 women, 4,649 boys, and 4,489 girls), including 159 persons with disabilities across 9 camps.
- All internally displaced persons receive drinking water intermittently through tankers. There are two public wells on-site that can be utilized to establish a water network.
- There is a gap in water and sanitation networks in all locations (5 camps lack any form of sanitation, while 4 camps rely on technical pits for wastewater disposal).
- There are no solid waste management services in any of the camps.
- Most displaced persons in these camps struggle to sustainably access personal hygiene materials due to poverty.
- All camps are established on public land, and there are no HLP-related issues.
This proposal aims to provide life-saving humanitarian assistance in the field of water, sanitation, and hygiene (WASH) for 16,032 internally displaced persons (3,367 men, 3,527 women, 4,649 boys, and 4,489 girls), including 159 persons with disabilities, in 9 camps in Idlib Governorate, Harim district, Dana sub district.
Additionally, this proposal seeks to enhance personal and public hygiene and reduce the spread of diseases transmitted by insects such as Leishmaniasis, as well as COVID-19 and cholera. This will be achieved through a range of integrated activities, including:
* Provision of safe drinking water and water quality monitoring through:
- Establishing water networks and connecting them to water stations, considering WASH, SPHERE, and AISC guidelines (activity duration: 6 months in 2024).
- Supporting the operation of water stations for 12 months (3 months in the first year and 9 months in 2024) to provide water to displaced persons at a rate of 35 liters per person per day.
* Provision of sanitation services and general public hygiene through:
- Construction and improvement of sewage networks in the camps and connecting them to the main networks (activity duration: 6 months in 2024).
- Solid waste management and daily transfer to main landfills for 12 months (6 months in 2024 and 6 months in 2025).
We believe that these interventions will significantly improve the living conditions and hygiene standards for the displaced population in the targeted camps.
