Project Summary

The project will strengthen community referral pathways through building their capacity of case workers on service provision to survivors of SGBV. The population which shall be targeted by this project is 3000 persons in Wau, 3600 persons in Terekeka, and 3000 persons in Fangak respectively. The Community based capacity building will promote synergies of local GBV actors to reduce SGBV cases. Local judicial officials will be empowered to provide acceptable judicial procedures, offering justice to victims and perpetrators. Legal aid services will be provided especially for victims in detention without trial, with SGBV survivors benefiting from redress mechanisms, including on transitional justice. SGBV mitigation will further be strengthened through the establishment of local Women and girl’s friendly spaces (WGFS) structures to assist GBV refugees to assist providing security and protection. The project will further work closely with community building capacity of volunteers in providing Psycho social support services to those who are psychologically stressed due to stigma and fatigue. Creation of awareness raising to protect women, men girls and boys from any harmful cultural norms eg forced and early marriage communities will be enhanced by strengthening judicial law and referrals. WAO shall build the capacities of the stakeholders to have knowledge on human rights in relation to their cultural and customary laws. Engagement men and boys in preventing and responding to GBV cases through EMP programme for initiating Behaviour change in the communities will be enhanced. Hygiene education sessions shall be provided to pupils and Teachers in Schools to improve the learning outcomes and change dominant social norms. The law enforcement shall be capacitated in good practice on to apply law enforcement in preventing and responding to cases of sexual gender based violence in the communities.
