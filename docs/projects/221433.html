Project Summary

The project will focus on an emergency WASH response that includes prepositioning and distribution of life-saving WASH supplies (family hygiene kits/dignity kits/cholera kits), rapid rehabilitation of water points and water committee trainings, construction of emergency latrines, construction of inclusive emergency latrines (PLWD), promotion of critical hygiene and sanitation, and the promotion of menstrual hygiene management. The target is 20.000 people affected in resettlement center and in host communities in Ancuabe and Chiúre.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project will focus on an emergency WASH response, targeting 20,000 people in resettlement centers and host communities in Ancuabe and Chiúre. This included internally displaced persons (IDPs) and non-displaced (host community) populations The project aims to improve access to sustainable and quality WASH services, encouraging behavior change around safe water consumption, handwashing, and sanitation. These efforts are designed to reduce the risk of water-related diseases, particularly malaria and cholera, by fostering the adoption of safer hygiene and sanitation practices in affected communities.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The priority activities will be: 1. Distribution of familiar hygiene and dignity Kits/cholera kits. 2. Post-distribution monitoring3. Provision of the emergence sanitation and hygiene (construction of emergence latrines)4. Rehabilitation of water points with Afridev hand pumps5. Promotion of behavior of change activities related to hygiene and sanitationThese activities will be implemented depending on the demand and safety in Ancuabe and Chiúre.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

Promoting gender equality is important for HELVETAS to protect and provide assistance to people affected. Humanitarian response will contribute to gender equality by effectively identifying and responding to the distinct and differential needs, capabilities and priorities of women, girls, boys and men in all their diversity. The project will ensure women’s full and effective participation and equal opportunities for leadership at all levels of decision-making, especially at Water and Sanitation Communities and Sanitation Groups. The project will engage men and boys, urging them to become champions of gender equality, as well as empowering women and girls to fully participate in the life of society are fundamental for making a change. Older people should be afforded the possibility to become actively engaged in the development process and in society so that their skills, experiences, wisdom and knowledge can be used for the benefit of all. The active participation of older persons is based on providing older persons with the opportunity to continue contributing to society beyond their roles in families and in the community.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

HELVETAS and its partners will do assessment to identify the people with disabilities. The assessment will be during the implementation of the activities at level of household with coordination of the local authorities. Persons with disabilities have the same need to access other basic services as any other affected person in emergency response. Ensuring inclusion of persons with disabilities during emergency response must be considered a core component of principled and effective humanitarian action. It is based not only on the humanitarian principles of humanity and impartiality, but also on the human rights principles of equity and non-discrimination. Women, older and disabilities are a specific target group in terms of care givers and decisions makers in households. The project will address activities to remove barriers to their access to assistance in special related to WASH, including needs of inclusive WASH infrastructures.

7 - Environmental considerations
