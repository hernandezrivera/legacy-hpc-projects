Project Summary

1. Needs: The camps project identifies a critical need for mental health support among its residents. The displaced population is likely to experience various mental health challenges due to the circumstances leading to their displacement, including trauma, stress, and uncertainty about the future.
2. Intervention, Implementation of Activities: The intervention involves implementing a comprehensive mental health program. This may include individual and group counseling sessions, psychoeducation workshops, and creative therapy activities. Trained mental health professionals will be engaged to facilitate these activities, ensuring culturally sensitive and trauma-informed approaches.
3. Protection Risk Analysis: A protection risk analysis assesses potential risks to the mental health program participants, considering factors such as vulnerability to exploitation, violence, or discrimination. It aims to identify and address potential risks to ensure the safety and well-being of individuals seeking mental health support in the camp.
4. PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) Strategy: The project incorporates a PSEA strategy to prevent and respond to any instances of sexual exploitation and abuse within the mental health program. This involves implementing clear policies and procedures, conducting training for staff and beneficiaries, and establishing mechanisms for reporting and addressing concerns related to sexual exploitation and abuse.
5. Risks, Assumptions, and Mitigating Measures: Risks associated with the mental health project may include potential resistance or cultural barriers to mental health services, inadequate resources, and challenges in maintaining confidentiality. Assumptions may include the willingness of the community to engage in mental health activities and the availability of qualified staff.
Mitigating measures could involve community engagement initiatives to address cultural concerns, securing additional resources through partnerships or advocacy, and implementing robust confidentiality protocols. Ongoing monitoring and evaluation will help identify and address emerging risks and challenges throughout the project's implementation.
