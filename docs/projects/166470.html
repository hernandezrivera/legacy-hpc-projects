Project Summary

This quick food security intervention that will be deployed by GREENCODE in increasing the procurement of more Hygiene kits relevant for COVID-19, Printing of adopted IEC materials and more batches for training because of social distancing. Increase number of persons in need.
Also, the project will provide emergency food package both in-kind food and cash for food for some fo the most vulnerable especially those in host communities that can not be reach by the Borno and Yobe states Palliative food support.

1 - Needs

The emergent of COVID-19 in the world and the in Borno in particular, this has brought an untold hardship to the lives of already displaces persons. Some of whom have not been enable to access food due to the lockdown which has restricted their daily movement in and out of the respective camps and host communities.Similarly, the restriction of movement and fear of contacting the Corona virus has made many to stay at home and most of them have been depending on food aid from humanitarian actors and the lockdown has affected the flow and delivery of the food aid and they can not engage in any economic activities to generate or gain income.Also, considering the congested and poor hygiene in some locations/settlement of IDPs in Borno couple with the spread pattern of COVID, as most of the people in Borno does not believe in the Virus as such are not aware of the preventive methods. In view of the above, there is an urgent need to embark on COVID-1 prevention messages and outreach to the IDP locations in camps and host communities, procure and distribute COVID-19 preventive kits, as well as embark on in-kind distribution of food and cash for food based on FSS Food basket calculation.

2 - Activities

The following activities will be undertaken1). Community mobilisations (visits and meetings)2). Identification,selection and training of community COVID-19 Prevention Promoters3). Adoption, Production and distribution of IEC materials on COVID-19 Prevention messages4). Construction and distribution of hand washing station and soaps during in-kind food distribution and Cash based transfer.5). Identification, selection and registration of food in-secured for In-kind food and cash for food for the most vulnerable IDPs in camps and host communities especially nursing and lacating mothers, persons with disabilities, child and women headed households.6). Routine monitoring of the FSS COVID-19 Response activities.
