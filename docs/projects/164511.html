Project Summary

This project aims to empower 160 youth beneficiaries by providing them with easily applicable skills: industrial and technical education programs within practical training that will enable them to find job opportunities and develop their own small businesses. Also, it will create 20 job opportunities for qualified engineers and engineer assistants from Atmeh town which will help them improve their families' economic situation.
We already have a well-equipped training center in Atmeh (with support from Expertise France), which includes two training rooms and four labs for mechanical and electronic training.
Afaq Academy intended to conduct Four specialized programs (250 hours each):
1.Mechanics and automotive electricity
2-Electronic software and maintenance of computer & mobile
3-Electricity & Industrial Automation Program
4-Programmed operating machines & industrial drawing
Four categories are launched into four different programs at the same time so that the total number of beneficiaries is 40 trainees (10 each program) within one stage. Each program needs 4 months; one for advertising, registration, and exams and 3 months of training.
Each trainee will receive a transportation compensation 25$ for each month of the program.
The trainee in each program receives a program relevant kit of equipment costing $35.
The kit of the electrical and electronic programs contain:
1. Electric Soldering Iron tool
2. Tin Wires
3. Test Pencil
4. Duct Tape
5. Pliers
6. Wire Cutter
7. Cutter Knife

9. Plastic Toolbox
The kit of the mechanic program contains:
1. screwdriver set
2. grip pliers

these kits will contribute to helping the trainees during the training period and after when working freelance maintenance men until they find the best work opportunity.

4 - Needs

Atmeh town did not mainly have adequate basic educational facilities, as well as vocational education facilities. Like the rest of the Syrian areas outside the control of the regime, it lost thousands of young competencies so the idea of the project was to try to save what can be saved and to light a candle in Syria's dark day. We have conducted a study in Atmeh and its camps to identify training needs and develop the concept of the project.The study included four main tools:1. Group discussions.2. Interviews.3. Field visits.In the study, the team gave a presentation about the Afaq Institute for Industrial and Technical Sciences in Atmeh, asking for more ideas to improve it.Key findings:- There are very few vocational training centers in and around Atma town offering specialized technical training- Providing shorter intensive specialized pieces of training- Making use of the available equipment to focus on specialized pieces of training- Providing advanced computer training that can lead to job opportunities rather than only basic software pieces of training- There are many qualified trainers in the mechanical, electrical and electronic fields but many of them work in other jobs and sometimes below their level of education due to the lack of relevant training centers or appropriate opportunities.The project justification- Reducing unemployment.- High rates of population growth in the region, population inflation and IDPs in large numbers- Low levels of education and the absence of an industrial project- The available resources are wasted considering the inability to invest the human resources of the youth- Youth joining the military factions, which reflects on civil society- The deviation of youth to crimes and thefts to meet their needs in the absence of available alternatives- Reducing Immigration- Lack of qualified industrial cadres to deal with industrial machines- Absorbing the energies of youth and their personal aspirations

5 - Implementation of activities

To respond to the above-mentioned problems, Afaq Institute for Industrial and Technical Sciences targeting the young group (f/male) in the first place, through industrial and technical educational programs within practical training to provide them with experience and skills and enable them to enter the labor market to be productive and able to improve their living situation and support themselves and their families.Afaq Institue is based and fully equipped where the institute contains classrooms and laboratories (electron- electricity - industrial automation), in addition, to control workshops (hydraulic - antenna - metal formation), also, the institute's management contracts workshops of external programmed operating machines to increase the experience and efficiency of trainees.Afaq Academy intended to conduct Four specialized programs (250 hours each):1.Mechanics and automotive electricity2-Electronic software and maintenance of computer & mobile3-Electricity & Industrial Automation Program4-Programmed operating machines & industrial drawing-The trainee in each program receives a program relevant bag of equipment costing $35The mechanism of work and the trainees selecting criteria:1. Announcing each program on social media and local channels.The selection process includes the following criteria:The applicant must have a preparatory education certificateThe applicant must pass a test on the basis of mathematics, physics, and general culture.The age group should be between 19 and 30 yearsThere is a priority for displaced applicants and people who are missing the breadwinnerEach specialty program lasts 3 months and 1month for advertising, registration, and examsFour categories are launched into four different programs at the same time so that the total number of beneficiaries is 40 trainees (10 each program) within one stageThe project will contribute to securing 160 professional seats to join vocational and industrial education in Atmeh.
