Project Summary

The Project targets post war traumatized populations in hard to read areas in Raqqa Governorate (Maadan, Saqba, Debsi Afnan, Debsi Faraj, Journiyeh) that are deprived of food security for the difficulty of arrival of humanitarian help and for the lack of resilience. Selected population (6500 families) living in precarious post war conditions in Raqqa will receive each month for three months a standard family food ration and each week for this same period 5 bread bundles.
Ar-Raqqa governorate’s current context is characterized by the recent offensive against ISIS. Insecurity represents the primary hazard for residents of Raqqa City and surrounding areas and is the main barrier for those seeking to return. IDPs represent between around 34% of the population in addition to many new arrivals recorded in Jurneyyeh, Al-Thawrah, Karama and Maadan sub-districts – in formal (transit) camps, informal settlements and host communities. The situationhas improved as the control changes in most of the governorate, including Raqqa City. However, a certain level of instability is remaining, due to high levels of contamination and further political instability. Market functionality is still being affected. Conversely, food access is a major limiting factor, due to high food prices, reduced food production and low income. However, humanitarian support is absent is limited in areas with restricted access . Our Project will target those populations from Mansura - Tabqa to Debsi Afnan. With the unfolding of our activities that consist in providing food security to the most deprived and vulnerable beneficiaries among the population (women households, children, people with disabilities, elders, IDP's, returnees), the beneficiaries will be able to enter in early recovery opening the way to resilience. Our Project is in compliance with F&A guidelines recommending : Scale-up in-kind food assistance for the most hard-to-reach areas impacted by the conflict. We will target Raqqa, Sabqa, Maadan on the East and Debsi Afnan and its surrounds in the west, serving both IDPs and vulnerable host communities, and maintaining Food Assistance in other parts of the governorate through a tight targeting mechanism.

Needs

Following the FSS' HRP and Response priorities "Reliance on food assistance is expected to continue throughout the coming year and access to a diversified diet will remain a key need to improve the nutritional status of the acutely food insecure people.After the liberation of Raqqa the population is trying to return from the camps where she was scattered. The returnees and the IDP's that are dwelling in the ruin of their houses are in bad need for a direct emergency food assistance. The region that has been selected and assessed (south and west of Raqqa) is receiving a daily arrival of IDP's but is not yet covered by a steady humanitarian relief aid. The people are in need for everything and especially for food security as the first emergency need. People live in squatted house, makeshift camping or destroyed houses. The markets are hardly accessible to the pocket of the majority of the population as free circulation in the targeted areas is yet very limited. Many children and teens show signs of malnutrition. The burden of a woman head of a household is to provide enough food for her family. In a moment were early recovery is beginning very slowly, it is important to help this population with temporary food security. MSJM will strive to take into account the needs of women, men and children in the implementation of the Project. The assessment is done respecting the segregation of age and of gender by MSJM who is dwelling in those hard to reach areas. Giving special attention to segregate the needs by age and gender.

Implementation of activities

MSJM has a steady experience on assessing, procuring and distributing food security. We can also obtain special permissions to dwell in hard to reach areas. Our previous activities in hard to reach areas helped us improve our methods following risk mitigation and the no harm principle. To prevent a rush on the humanitarian goods by the population, we will secure collaboration arrangement with stakeholders(local leaders and communities etc) to be helped to distribute monthly vouchers for the food rations and weekly vouchers for the bread bundles. We will perform a swift distribution moving in the targetted villages and stopping for each 50 houses to avoid the rush. We will listen to the complains or to the desires of the beneficiaries and will try to honor them in the limit of the possibility of the Project. Protection Risk Analysis

The dwelling in Raqqa and surrounds is not secure. The PIN are in dire need and they can bypass the administrative measure of the distribution.Mitigation measures : always dwell with local authorities and representatives of the beneficiariesRegister and distribute coupons BEFORE showing the convoyDistribute in a closed place where beneficiaries can enter in small groups not exceeding 25 at a time. Our Project as well as all MSJM policies abide to the NO HARM principle as we fully respect the freedom of each person and we will never use coercion. The proverbial humanitarian approach of MSJM staff will be as always full of respect, joy and profesional excellence. We will build good relations with the notables that represent the different social grouping: the heads of families and the mayors. The Project will be under accountability of the affected population A complain box will be opened in each mobile unitM&E activities will rely on interview of the beneficiaries. The M&E officer will give special attention to any sign of discrimination, to any appeal about GBV or inequality to orient it to the protection sector of our organization The interviews with the beneficiaries will not be limited for reporting but also for hearing their voice as the best source of information on the outputs of the Project.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Explosive devices (mines, non detonated bombs) are the most acute risk in Raqqa Province. The other Risk is the proliferation of bands and of instability especially in the night time. The lack of electricity, the vacuity of the roads makes the campaign very dangerous. We will take the utmost security measures to avoid dwelling in regions with those risks. Never move after the sun set. Avoid to be isolated in a place that is isolated.
