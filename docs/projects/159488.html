Project Summary

The project aims to increase food availability and food access for drought affected communities In Baidoa and Qansah.Dhere District to address humanitarian needs for women, men, boys and girls in South West State of Somalia by providing life saving and life sustaining assistance to people in need while prioritizing the most vulnerable. The project will also restore and strengthen livelihoods mainly through strengthening the local production system and capacities, in order to build resilience with focus to agro-pastoralist and agriculture based women households ho are engaged in food production so communities have sustained access to food security in spite of recurrent shocks..

Project Activities

1. Provide unconditional transfers to populations in emergency including internally displaced people and people in crisisduring the lean season. Deliberate effort will be made to target women and people with special needs and wouldconstitute at least 70% in this category. beneficiaries would be segregated into gender and age during implementation. 2. Provide livelihood specific and seasonally appropriate inputs for agro-pastoral and agricultural households in emergency, crisis and stressed. This would constitute about 90% of the target beneficiaries. women farmers and IDPs who are engaged in faming activities would be given priority. Data of beneficiaries would be segregated into gender and age. 3. Provide conditional transfers to restore and build community and household productive assets and capacity. Thetransfers would be mainly cash for work or for training and would constitute about 70% of the beneficiaries. Training willentail environmental, and other good agronomic practices. Women who can participate in such activities would be given priority and work norms would be adjusted to reflect the reality on the ground and situation of women and men.

Mitigation Measures

The Project During displacement in emergency period there are many risk related to exclusion based on the displacement such risk factor are, child labor, sexual exploitation, discrimination and poor documentation on the specific needs of the people at risk. To mitigate this, URRO will however, put in place strict policy that will protect the right of women and children in emergency and conflict status, it will ensure all its staff are capacity build on child right, women, vulnerable groups and protection mainstreaming in its livelihood and food security activities, it shall also advocate community awareness in its implementation of livelihood through equal representation of community relief committee and village elders responsible for selecting the most vulnerable household as well ensure no harm policy within its implementation of the program. URRO will strive to adopt most reasonable project implementation methodology such as mobile outreach registration of beneficiaries in the far village to enable take services to the most vulnerable with less mobility status, through this, URRO will achieved handsomely on wider coverage to its target population. URRO will ensure to protects its beneficiary information and as well adopt reasonable program location that will ensure access and safety of the beneficiary. The following are some of the risk factors facing the target population such factors are, discrimination against race,sex or gender employment opportunity, sexual exploitation in exchange of relief food as well as poor documentation, monitoring and reporting within the program implementation. URRO will however, mitigated such factors without hinderance,it intends to provide equal opportunity for employment to all these qualified ad within the local areas as first priority and based on their skills, it shall also ensure both men and women boys and girls have equal access to project information and services in the target areas, through this program URRO will ensure its staff and community members were capacity built on “DO NO HARM” principle of neutrality as well as disseminates project information to the community members through consultative forums and in particular religious institution such as mosque. This will facilitate more inclusive of both gender and various community members hence create an atmosphere of more responsive coverage among the target group. URRO will ensure the safety and dignity of its beneficiaries by providing regular monitoring and reporting of the new challenges and opportunity in consultation and coordination with other stakeholders and community key point. The following are main risk factors that might impact negatively on the beneficiaries during implementation such factors are poor timing during distribution of foods or agricultural input to the beneficiary which may fall late in the day hence susceptible for theft, cross fire, intimidation as well as sexual exploitation among poor women and girls beneficiaries, it may also risk of beneficiary by registering one main clan against other dwellers in settlement through collusion with project staff hence this might create community tension in this case conflict might a raise thus dangering the safety of these vulnerable population. Also leaking of vital beneficiary data might be risk associated with arm conflict and violence this is because in the instance militia groups get the beneficiary details they might intimidate and force to surrender the little foods provided thus risking the life of the vulnerable women, men, boys and girls population. In mitigating the above URRO intends to establish strict beneficiary data protection for safety purpose as well as facilitates equal representation among the target community in selection of community village committee that will directly work with URRO food security teams. Staff and community capacity shall be build and ensure staff signed code of conduct
