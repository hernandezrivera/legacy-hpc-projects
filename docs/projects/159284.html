Project Summary

There is dire need to sustain the provision of services, which is currently declining since HDC has been under the gap and has affected effective services in 8 facilities, thus exposing the populations to significant health risks and making it hard to access quality health services. Similarly ,we have been providing CEmONC services in Bardhere Hospital through support of UNFPA and Ministry of Health (Federal Government) and came to an end in June 2019 further causing gap. To also ensure increase access to services, we intend to support CEmONC services and secure access to referral services in Elwak District hospital where only BEmONC services are currently available though with no funding support. Populations around Elwak are forced to travel hundreds of Kilometers to access health services or referral services and in many instances have to cross borders to Wajira nd Mandera of neighbor country or BaardhereCEmONC servicedue to limitation of services.
Since 2019, we took over management of Berdale MCH previously under Swisso Kalmo where are currently providing IPTCS services vices to the communities (host)and the IDPs/persons displaced most pf whom were affected by drought and conflicts. There are many health needs in Baidoa as estimated populations of displaced populations stand at 323,000 persons in 48 IDP camps. The health needs are alarming due to the high populations and recurring shocks, including constant disease outbreaks and common illnesses. We are seeking to enhance the support of services through Berdale MCH which sits close proximity with IDP camps and convenient to access services which will focus in strengthening access to quality health services to the most vulnerable populations who are forced to travel far and wide to seek medical facilities as well will cover hard to reach communities who are still inhibited to accessing services due to insecurity in some locations.
This project seeks to reach over 48713 person (6029: girls; 5565: boys; 26083: women and 11036: men)) through provision of health services in 4 districts including Bardhere, Elwak, Doolow and Baidoa to populations including IDPs and hard to reach communities , conflict and drought affected people by ensuring access to Universal Health Care covering primary and secondary health care through 8 facilities, 2 in Bardera District which is Bardhere District Hospital, Ali cato MCH/Habala Adey IDP,4 facilities in Elwak which include Elwak District Hospital, Dhamasa MCH, Geriley MCH and Husqurun, one facility in Dollow which is Doolow MCH and Berdale MCH in Baidoa through the provision of services including outpatient consultations for both males and females (boys and girls included), child health services, immunizations for both children under five and Women of Child Bearing Age; Reproductive health services including Family planning promotion and integrated method mix counseling and service delivery, Focused Antenatal Care (including complete physical examination of the mother and her foetus, systematic screening for syphilis, urine analysis; hemoglobin as well as establishment of delivery plan including referrals when appropriate, Sexually Transmitted Infections and HIV Testing and Counselling which shall enhance Emergency Obstetric and Neonatal Care Services. We intend to provide the BEmONC and CEmONC services through Bardhere District Hospital and in Elwak Hospital, where we are currently providing similar services in the former facility. Reproductive health campaigns shall also be conducted in Baardhere and Elwak District to improve access to sexual health services.
Five mobile outreaches shall also complement the services in static facilities to ensure adequate and equitable access to health services in Bardhere Hospital, Doolow MCH, Elwak District Hospital, Geriley MCH, and Berdale MCH to reach the vulnerable populations including IDPs.

Please provide further explanation

We have designed the interventions to take care of protection environment, linkages with the government/community priorities , sustainability , access to to services among the displaced communities.The protection environment has been addressed by ensuring even the most vulnerable community members can be accessed in the hard to reach areas designed through the outreach services. The interventions are highly linked with the government /community priorities especially envisaged in Health Sector Strategic Plan II (2018-2021 which envisages the pillars in health sector as well as health cluster objectives. The interventions seeks to provide long terms gains to health sector and realizing health sector resilience through approaches which seeks to enhance access to Universal Health Services while considering the most vulnerable populations including children, women and displaced populations which anchors the sustainability.Designed in the activities include comprehensive and effective approaches to reach displaced populations including hard to reach populations through outreach/mobile services as well as visibility and awareness to the services accessible through the interventions. Most of the facilities which will support the interventions are in hard to reach populations and within reach and access of the displaced populations.
