Project Summary

The overall project goal is to Under the partnership, increase access to quality learning environment, support with the education and Child Protection system strengthening as well as strengthening the Prevention and Response to Violence, Abuse and Exploitation of children in schools through Child protection, GBV/PSEA services for Conflict-Affected Children Across 10 schools in MMC and Jere LGAs of Borno state, 5 Schools in Michika and Madagali LGAs of Adamawa State and 10 Schools in Damaturu, Potiskum and Bade LGA of Yobe State.
The expected outcome is that Conflict-affected children and adolescents including Women, Parent/Caregivers have increased access to quality protection and response services in safe and healthy environment. NADA will through community and stake holder’s engagement ensure that all children have access to quality and inclusive basic education, mental health and psychosocial support and vocational opportunities for crisis-affected girls and boys in safe learning environments in North East Nigeria as well, ensured the capacity development for SBMC, CBCPC, Teachers and Parent of Children on CDRR, Risk/Psycho-education, protection and GBV/PSEA reporting mechanism. The organization will support in re-establishment of all school’s structures including protection and GBV/PSEA reporting mechanism in schools across the targeted schools within the BAY States

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

NADA recognizes the different needs of men and women, and thus the proposed project will be implemented in an inclusive and gender-sensitive manner prioritizing women and girls’ participation in project activities and ensuring that issues related to women reintegration are addressed. Applying learned lessons from the context and previous project, NADA found that most of the project participants were men since they were more likely to be engaged with armed groups. NADA will address gender issues with the adult stakeholders engaged in the project by targeting women to participate in project activities and ensuring gender-related topics are included in the topics on radio programs and community awareness creation.Directly in line with UN equity-based approach, this project will address factors contributing to discrimination and inequality for women and girls in the target communities. NADA will work with community stakeholders and the government to ensure that women and girls have the opportunity to exercise their rights in the communities especially HLP rights irrespective of their gender, ethnicity, religion, or any other basis used for marginalization.

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

This project is designed in consultation with the community stakeholders and based on lessons learnt from our previous implementation in the target LGAs. NADA shall use a people-centered feedback mechanism to gather opinions, and complaints of the community members and beneficiaries on its project to inform decision-making for quality project implementation. However, lessons learned from the approach will be used to form a LGA level project committee that will include all community groups' representative and stakeholders to improve the participation of the affected community in the project implementation process. NADA project team will conduct an LGA inception workshop with the already existed community-based project structures to discuss the project deliverables and the community-level needs and how this project will contribute to resolving the needs. NADA will also use a feedback mechanism through focus group discussions to gather perceptions of the project. beneficiaries and community members on the services rendered which will form part of the discussion in the project implementation review meeting with the community-based project committees at the end of the project. NADA will be intentional about maintaining an environment where there is zero Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (SEA) and have trained PSEA focal person who is solely responsible to receive and respond to any SEA-reported cases. NADA will sensitize the LGA level project committee on PSEA and NADA's established protocol of reporting PSEA and will continue to remind the committee in every meeting about the PSEA and NADA's established reporting procedure.
