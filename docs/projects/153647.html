Project Summary

This CP project will be targeting 7,500 vulnerable children, Directly and Indirectly from IDPs , Returnees and host community from Kajokeji County of Central Equatoria state with significant impact aiming at Supporting children and young people affected by armed conflict and will contribute to the Cluster Objectives 1$2 in the HRP 2019. SCA has been implementing Child Protection projects in Kajokeji since 2012 by providing Psychosocial Support through Child Friendly Spaces and Family Tracing and Reunification at Kangai Way Station for Returnees Children after the Boarder Conflict which forced many South Sudanese to flee from Moyo District of Uganda. In 2017 SCA managed two projects in Kajokeji funded by RRF-IOM and UNICEF Providing Child Protection and Gender Based Violence Response to the IDP Populations (Children and Women)in Logo, Kerwa and Ajio IDP Camps of Liwolo Payam, in County, through the provision of comprehensive Case Management, Family Tracing and Reunification, Facilitating access to Child Friendly Spaces, Psycho-social Support, strengthening resilience and developing healthy and safe coping mechanisms to reduce the risk of children and adolescents resulting into abuses and Violations of their rights. SCA planned to improve protection of conflict affected Males, females under the age of 18 years and internally displaced children, especially separated and unaccompanied children who will be better protected through the following activities 1. Comprehensive case management 2. Strengthening the established community-based Child Protection mechanisms 3. Improvement of the established Referral pathway for GBV and CP 4. Provision of alternative (Poster) care for vulnerable Children 5. Conduct Family Tracing and Reunification. 6. Mainstreaming/Integration of CP activities into Education, WASH(Partner,ARC to support with Hygine and Sanitaion services/Materials), Health(ARC$TRI-SS to suport with Medical ) SE/NFI( Titi Foundation $SPEDO to Support with Shelter $ Non Food Items and FSL( Titi Foundation ,SCA $Diocese of Kajokeji to support); and 7. Conduct awareness raising to prevent and respond to child protection issues. 8. Train care givers on good parental skills to Poster parents 9. Train community based Child Protection and Multi Sectoral Focal Points /service providers.

Needs

As the conflict in South Sudan enters its fifth year in 2018, the humanitarian crisis has continued to intensify and expand, on a costly trajectory for the country’s people and their outlook on the future. The compounding effects of widespread violence and sustained economic decline have further diminished the capacity of people to face threats to their health, safety and livelihoods.People in need of assistance and protection number 7 million, even as more than 2 million have fled to neighboring countries.The situation of the Children has deteriorated over 2017, with continued incidents of recruitment, abuse, exploitation and other grave Violations having directly affected about 100,000 Children since the beginning of the conflict. More than 19,000 Children are estimated to have been recruited by armed actors, up from 17,000 in 2016. A total of 16,055 unaccompanied, separated or missing children have been registered in South Sudan since December 2013. Destruction of Schools and the departure of teachers from many affected areas severely impact access to education, with 2 million Children out of School-More than ever,(2018-South Sudan Humanitarian Needs Overview).The above mentioned Scenario(Recruitment,abuse,exploitation,etc)is faced by Children in Kajokeji too (RNA- Korijo IDP Camp 17-10-2018 Report) where there are 40, 294 IDPs, out of which 69% are Children i.e 27,718( Boys-13823.Girls-13895) with urgent needs for Provision of Psychosocial Support, Continuation of the Family Tracing and Reunification to add on the 59 caseload SCA has already in the CPIMS, awareness raising on protection Concerns, Case management and other Child Protection concerns.Therefore, because of the Signed Peace Agreement, we hope the IDP will soon be disengaged/Repatriated as well as Returnees populations within and from neighboring Countries will move to their respective Villages and Homes in 2019, which will enable us to provide protection services through the Community based Child Protection Mechanism approach to the targeted Children in need. The degree of protection risks and women vulnerability is high. With this project,Sustainable Children Aid (SCA) will directly support the Protection Cluster framework for theHRP 2019 – specifically the Child Protection Sub-Cluster by- Conduct awareness raising to communities on Child protection (Child Rights, Land Mine awareness, etc ) - Conduct Additional Identification, Documentation Registration of UASC for FTR process. - Conduct follow up and support to Vulnerable Children through case management and referrals to specialized services (Health, Legal, etc) Providers operating in Kajokeji.- Provide training to social workers on PSS and PFA in the respective communities on Child Protection in Emergencies and case management - Conduct continuing Family tracing and reunification (FTR)of UASC - Support some Identified vulnerable children and place some of the UASC in alternative care – Identify additional Community Based child protection focal Points and Mult Sectoral Focal points on CPiE - Provide parental skills Training to caregivers and some community members . - Provide structured community-based psycho-social support activities to the children using local Play materials through the existing community structures. - Awareness raising on prevention and response to Child Protection issues through campaigns.
