Project Summary

The main objective of the planned action will be to build households’ coping capacities by providing livelihoods assistance, increasing food production, and building productive assets for more sustainable livelihoods, in line with the HRP FSL cluster objective 2: Enhance emergency food production through complementary vegetable and crop seeds and fishing and livestock support. The project aims to support 5,000 HHs of Cueibet County (1250 Males, 2,500 Females, 500 Boys and 750 Girls) in Lakes State through direct provision of seeds, tools and fishing kits for main. The households will be supported to increase their immediate food stock and have their knowledge of basic food production enhanced to boost their food preserve and stores to sustain them through the lean period. Beneficiaries will be encouraged to reserve seeds for the following planting season using both known traditional and conventional methods. 2500 HHs (1250 Males, 625 Females, 375 Boys,250 Girls) from the payams bordering Tonj (Maluopec, Ngap and Duony Payams) having access to water bodies will be supported with fishing kits. The selected households will be further disintegrated into vulnerability cadres to include people with disability, lactating and expectant mothers and elderly people. The selected fisher folks shall be strengthened on better fish harvesting and handling techniques. They shall be trained on environment friendly fish catching methods (correct net sizes and hooks), fish hygiene, preservation, packaging and marketing. 2,500 HH (1250 Females, 625 Males, 375 Girls,250 Boys) will be provided with vegetable kits. Income from the sale of surplus vegetable produce will be used to purchase alternative food requirements such as Dura (sorghum) and also supplement their HH dietary needs. Farmers will receive basic training on improved agronomic practices with regards to improved vegetable production, utilization and the nutrition aspect through the existing primary health care centres. These trainings will be primarily targeted towards pregnant and breastfeeding women who remain at high risk of malnutrition under stressed conditions. Livelihood assistance in terms of delivery of emergency livestock vaccination and treatment will be provided to support vulnerable livestock owning households in the Cueibet pastoral community.Vaccination and treatment will be conducted using the Community based Animal Health Service (CAHS) approach. These exercises are carried out in accordance to the annual vaccination plan/calendar issued by the Ministry of Livestock, Animal Resources and Fisheries (MLARF) & FAO which is based on region specific disease seasonal calendar. These activities involve mass vaccination and case treatment of predetermined populations and numbers of animals. Incidental interventions are carried out on consultation with State Ministry of Animal resources and Fisheries upon approval of action on confirmed disease outbreaks. Vaccinations are only carried out on animal population basis (accompanied by de-worming in wet-land areas); but treatments are carried out on herd and cattle camp basis, targeting clinically active cases. The activities are carried out by trained Community Animal Health Workers (CAHW) under supervision of Animal Health Auxiliaries (AHA), Stock Persons (SP) and a veterinarian

1 - Needs

The humanitarian crisis in Cueibet County, Lakes State emanates from political conflict, poor farming practices, poor livestock management and land use practices. The September 2019 IPC classifications considered Cueibet as IPC Phase 4-emergency. The high levels of acute food insecurity continue to be driven by the cumulative effects of the cyclic inter-communal conflicts, heavy reliance on unpredictable and poor rainfall performances, associated population displacements and prolonged years of asset depletion. In the Nile River bordering areas of Lakes State, cyclic communal conflict caused enormous destruction to productive assets, loss of human life, and local population displacement. Additionally, conflict has impacted on households’ access to other food sources, such as wild foods, fish, and livestock products. The on-going national economic crisis has in turn significantly reduced households’ purchasing power. Other significant drivers include the prolonged dry spells at critical stages of crop growth, flooding, and dry spells. An impact survey conducted by VSF Germany on the PROMISE project in Cueibet in April 2019 revealed that 80% of the households had access to agricultural land but utilization was inadequate due to poor agronomic practices as well as limited access to livelihood inputs with only 2.3% of the households applying post-harvest handling techniques. The survey showed that 61.5% of the households experienced high livestock mortality from preventable diseases. Milk production was a meagre 2.5litres/cow/day which is insufficient to meet HH requirements. Through the project, livelihood assistance by provision of agricultural inputs (seeds, tools and fishing kits) aims to support the selected households displaced by conflict to begin restoring their lost agriculture or fishing livelihoods, thereby enabling them to have increased food availability through personal production, as well as a small income for increased food access. The households will not only increase their immediate food stock but will have their capacities enhanced to build on existing local strategies and preserve thereby store food to sustain them during the dry seasons. Further livelihood assistance in terms of delivery of emergency livestock vaccination and treatment will be provided to support vulnerable livestock owning households in the Cueibet pastoral community.

6.6 - Will beneficiaries' rights be legally recognised?

Internally, VSF Germany has a several policies in respect of the working environment in South Sudan and includes chapters on Child Protection and Protection of Women that recognizes the vulnerability and susceptibility to common vices . Affirmative action on equal opportunities and access to basic rights is active. Frequent analysis of beneficiaries dimensions especially on agro-pastoral livelihoods are carried out by project managers to inform interventions designs. Such programming measures include ensuring inclusion of all beneficiaries in Community Dialogue meetings and workshops where important decisions are made, and also feedback forums; program design that ensures equitable allocation of resources to livestock over which women and children have decision rights – poultry, sheep and goats; dis aggregation of data by gender; education of women and children of potential harm from zoonoses and untrained animal health services. Through established Community Dialogue procedures, VSF Germany staff and supported extension workers undertake consultations with community leaders and representatives from boma level to county levels to determine and prioritize their needs, equitable use of the available resources, social protection mechanisms, safety and security considerations, feedback and complaints mechanisms, and also management of services for vulnerable members

6.5 - Includes perspectives of displacement-affected people?

The high levels of acute food insecurity continue to be driven by the cumulative effects of the cyclic inter-communal conflict that has disrupted livelihoods and impacted on households’ access to other food sources, such as wild foods, fish, and livestock products. the conflict has continued to cause enormous destruction to communal productive assets, loss of human life, and caused enormous local population displacement. The action will promote activities that will protect the displaced population from fall further in vulnerability bu building ion the resilience. The most important shocks after the prolonged conflict are related to food insecurity or access to food. Access to food is either through own production or purchase form the market. In this regard, the project will strengthen capacity for increased food production and also on-farm income generation to counter high food prices. the other type of resilience that the action will stimulate is protection from climatic shocks – this type of shock has seriously affected food production due to changed cycles of drought, dry spells, irregular rainfall as well as accompanying crop pests and diseases. The project will support and promote adoption of appropriate climate smart production techniques and technologies

6.2 - Is the project linked to government/community systems?

The project will collaborate with the local government at county level and its structures below; and also traditional leadership to strengthen and establish official procedures that will support project strategies throughout its duration and beyond. The project will as well collaborate with local civil society and community based groups to build capacity of local structures to enable them to continue facilitating resource mobilization, management and advocacy. It will collaborate with traditional leadership at community level which is also recognized in the law. This shall strengthen the community engagement and capacity building. The project will also facilitate linkages and functional relationship between beneficiaries and private production input suppliers and service providers. This is to enable economically feasible and sustainable access to the same providers. Finally the project will collaborate with Relief and Rehabilitation Commission – to ensurer smooth implementation of the project and legal compliance.

6.3 - Is the project aligned with national objectives?

The goal of enhancing the food security of vulnerable households is aligned with the priorities set by South Sudan and complies with the Agriculture Sector Policy Framework 2012–2018. The action adheres to the South Sudan Ministry of Agriculture and Food Security polices and strategies as prescribed in the Comprehensive Agricultural Master Plan (CAMP). extension service delivery is paramount to increased to food production hence the National Agriculture and Livestock Extension Policy (NALEP) is key for the project expectations. The project will employ the Livestock Emergency Guidelines and Standards (LEGS) delivery approach which uses the technique of Community Dialogue Meeting (CDM) and Community Dialogue Workshop (CDW) to secure effective and popular participation of all stakeholder and beneficiary representatives in all stages of the intervention.

6.1 - Is the protection environment sufficiently safe/stable?

The ever degenerating food insecurity in Cueibet is increasingly driving greater numbers of household members to other locations and adopting some coping practices for survival purposes. Presently, the IDP settlement and most population groups comprise women, children, youth, men and to a lesser extent the aged. Normally traditional village administration structures include women leadership which represent the interests of women, children and girls. Through established Community Dialogue procedures, VSF Germany field staff and supported the community extension workers undertake consultations with community leaders and representatives from county level to villages to determine and prioritize their needs, equitable use of the available resources, social protection mechanisms, safety and security considerations, feedback and complaints mechanisms, and also management of services for vulnerable members like restraining of the cattle for vaccination and treatment and exemption from cost recovery if any.

6.4 - Does the project include sustainability/handover?

The project support will include sustainable agricultural assets and individual skills that will secure resilience against common household shocks. positive coping mechanisms to environment and climatic shocks will also be addressed. The following actions will secure progressive exit of the project:• Nutrition – skills and knowledge development in nutrition and hygiene, through continued sensitization, mobilizations and training on dietetic matters • Food security – introduction of locally appropriate time and labor saving tools and equipment, climate smart production techniques and improved income earning livelihood activities; . Linkages- with the local institutions to ensure smooth exist Appropriate skills and Technologies – the technologies and skills proposed in the action are affordable and can be locally sourced even at farm level. As mentioned above, the planning and implementation of the whole project is through active involvement of the beneficiary, local authority, County and State level Ministries of Agriculture and Forestry, Livestock and Fisheries with aim of building capacity and smooth handing over by the time when the project ends. Therefore, the exit strategy of this project will be deeply embedded with the key stakeholders of the project at the beginning. County Agriculture Department (CAD) and County Livestock Department (CLD) will be involved throughout the project cycle in addition to attending and co-facilitating training.

8.18 - Please indicate how the risks selected above will be mitigated.

In the present context of conflict and insecurity, access to water for domestic use, and firewood by women are significantly constrained. This is further compounded by increased absence of male heads of households. The program will limit the amount of time taken from women’s busy schedule through fore-planning with women, and advocating for prioritization of intervention process for female and old persons headed households to limit the possible extra time that women and girls may need to spend outside the home as a result of the program interventions. One of the major limiting barriers to the minority groups in this project location is male dominance and females exclusivity to community issues due to their busy house chore schedules. The project will limit the amount of time taken from these women’s busy schedule through prior-planning with women, and advocating for prioritization of intervention process for female and old persons headed households In the present context of conflict and insecurity, access to water for domestic use, firewood and market for animal products by women are significantly constrained and highly exposes them to risks of exploitation.The programs intends to enhance equal and safe participation of women through training in basic community health skills that will encourage them to part of the community resources caretakers. The project will be monitoring for discrimination, abuse and exploitation with particular attention to women, children, older people, people with disabilities and minorities. The project monitoring will focus on knowledge, attitude and practices of beneficiaries in regard to potential harm from animal husbandry practices, animal products and animal health activities. The project will find out about discrimination, exploitation and abuses on beneficiaries by service provider and people in power within the program value chain. There will be evaluation of safety, dignity and security measures employed by project.

9.14 - How will you project ensure feedback from the most vulnerable community members?

Community Dialogue procedures have incorporated beneficial customs of treatment of aged people which gives them maximum respect and preferential treatment. The case of people with disability is championed during the same sessions and equitable treatment procedures are ensured. Such actions include physical support with animals during vaccination and treatment, modified cultivation implements to suit their postures . Agricultural and veterinary inputs and services are highly sought in the pastoral communities of South Sudan, much so in the present context where survival and sustenance are significantly dependent on crop farming and risk of losses of livestock from disease are high. Through established Community Dialogue procedures, accountability is progressively established at the state level, then county level and further to the villages through popular participation and all round representation. The more responsive and effective traditional feedback and complaints mechanism is adopted. However, parallel mechanisms are in place to validate and interrogate the system, especially in respect of responsibility and accountability of personnel of partners and the organisation
