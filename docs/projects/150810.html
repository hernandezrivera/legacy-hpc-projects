Project Summary

The project is aligned with 2017 resilience and recovery framework and will contribute to strengthening of health systems ability to withstand and cope from recurrent shocks and disasters. Its purpose is to build preparedness and response capacities of health authorities at all levels and health cluster partners providing services to Somali communities.
Project interventions include;
1. Supporting finalization and endorsement of All hazards and specific contingency and response plans.
2. Training of 100 senior level managers from health authorities on public health risk assessment using STAR tools and development of contingency planning
3. Training of 100 senior officers from health cluster partners on public health risk assessment using STAR tools and development of contingency planning.
4. Provide epidemic prone diseases case management training to 500 staff selected from health facilities located in hots-pot areas.
5. Undertake functional reviews of health emergency units of federal and state levels - Streamline structures, TORs and reporting mechanisms

Needs

Prolonged conflict and social unrest had led to collapse of national institutions including health sector. Somali communities face a significant hazards and conditions that result in overall poor health and well being of individuals. Recurrent droughts, flooding and disease outbreaks had further weakened health systems ability to provide services to affected communities. As result of these hazards,over 2.6 million are displaced within the country. Health authorities and NGOs implementing services have got limited preparedness and response capacities to support a timely lifesaving health services mainly during disease outbreaks. Limited health facilities have been trained on case management for epidemic prone diseases. WHO conducted public health risk assessment in Sept 2018 using standard STAR tool and 5 key hazards were identified and prioritized. These include AWD/Cholera, Measles and conflict. A draft response plan had also been developed. Specific contingency plans will be developed for each hazard to ensure preparedness measures are put in place.

Activities

How is the project linked to durable solutions/resilience?

The project is expected to contribute to resilience an recovery framework through supporting health sector preparedness and response capacities. It will be mainly conducted in Mogadishu and state headquarters which are relatively stable compared to regional and district level. The project is linked with and supports the implementation of pillar no. 9 of phase II Health sector strategic plan 2017-2021 (Preparedness and Response Capacities). The development of such capacities contributes both short and long term interventions through training of health workforce.
