Project Summary

• Our project aiming at establishing a training center within 6-month providing appliances and technical means required. This training center will accommodate (2775) trainee, as it will provide training on various programs related to working skills required in the local market, companies and organizations. This training will lead to develop personal skills integral to technical skills the trainees already have. Moreover, we will monitor training results, set guiding sessions for trainees after the training ends.
There are a lot of training programs will offered to the trainees:
- Effective communication skills
- Writing resume, cover letter, and motivation letter
- Negotiation and conviction
- Job interview skills
- Making a good presentation
- Leadership and teamwork skills
- Creativity and problem solving skills
- Time and priorities management skills
Description of the problem/ problems need to be solved
Local market suffer from lack of staff possessing personal skills qualifying them to jobs market. This lack is the main reason for the gap between employers and unemployed qualified workers.
Result/ project’s expected results
Improving the level of trainee’s personal skills
Trainee’s ability to market their skills and capabilities efficiently in jobs market.
Community awareness/ targeted beneficiaries
Targeted sector includes a lot of experiences and capacities that needs to be enhanced. They also need to focus on personal skills, especially after the large wave of displacement that affected various regions. Actually, that sector found themselves without any educational or qualifying prerequisites in the targeted region.
The countryside of north of Aleppo contains a combination of evacuees and residents. The project will take into account both groups, taking into consideration community integration via the engagement of both groups in the same training courses.
Project’s added value for the targeted sector
The ability to market personal and technical skills and investing it in jobs market and companies.

Needs

• As per humanitarian needs over view of 2018 issued by the United Nations: 9.8 million people urgently need early recovery and support livelihoods. Three out of four people do not participate in revenue producing activities.Actually, securing livelihood one of the most important challenges facing citizens in Syria. More than half of the jobs numbering 5 million job before the crisis have disappeared. That situation affected unemployment rate of 53% and reaching 75% of youth in the age group 15 to 24 years.• Considering that there is a gap between the outputs of education and vocational training and the needs of jobs market, the level of graduate’s efficiency has declined. They graduates are not able to provide the expected quality, thus education has become an economic and social issue.
