Project Summary

Mercy Corps aims at improving the food and nutrition status of 23 975 vulnerable households (119 875 individuals) in Mudzi, Mutoko, Mutare and Buhera Districts of Mashonaland East and Manicaland Provinces. The project will scale up delivery of rapid urban social assistance through cash transfers to 2,000 most food-insecure households in “hotspots” of Mutare City. MC will leverage on current work under the VALUE project in Buhera and Mudzi to scale up interventions in Mutare and Mutoko, not currently covered by interventions. The project will target the most food insecure households (IPC phase 3+) especially marginalised households without access to either cereal or alternative coping strategies. The project proposes rehabilitation of dip tanks, capacity building of extension staff and farmers, distribution of crop and livestock input packages through a voucher system, distribution of small livestock to vulnerable households, increasing access to affordable livestock feed in rural areas and scale up of Cash based transfers in Mutare City. Project will also train farmers on climate smart agriculture, crop pest management, post-harvest handling, storage and processing.

8 - Accountability to Affected People
8.1 - Describe how your project was designed in consultation with communities? How will you incorporate communities in project implementation and what feedback mechanisms do you have in place?

Mercy Corps (MC) will make use of and strengthen its established Community Accountability Reporting Mechanisms (CARMs) designed to empower individuals to provide feedback and complaints. MC will ensure that all staff receive accountability and safeguarding training facilitated by the MC Zimbabwe CARM Adviser. Project staff will conduct participatory consultation meetings at project conception to sensitise community members on the available feedback mechanisms. CARMs that include help desks, suggestion boxes and Community Accountability Focal Persons will be utilised as well as the existing MC integrity/toll free line. The toll free feedback will be handled by the CARMs advisor and shared with trained project CARMs focal persons to lead the handling and response. A CARMs database will be set up and maintained throughout the program. In addition to that public address scripts and information on project criteria will be prepared and shared by all project participants including the toll free number.

9 - Environmental considerations
9.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Through our implementation Mercy Corps will engage key stakeholders such as the Environmental Management Agency (EMA) to ensure capacitation of local communities on environmental issues. The project will promote Climate Smart Agriculture techniques which actively contribute to positive environmental change by providing farmers with the means to increase agricultural productivity while at the same time improving soil fertility, reducing erosion and abating the overuse of chemical inputs. Mercy Corps’s focus on rehabilitation of existing defunct water points presents an alternative to other water program approaches that simply install boreholes throughout target zones without consideration of the impact on the underground water resources. Where necessary the project will conduct Environmental Impact Assessments to inform Asset creation activities.
