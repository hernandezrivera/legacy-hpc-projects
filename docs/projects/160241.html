Restoration of buildings and provision of materials for life support in emergency conditions of the population in the winter period in the zone of the contact line in the East of Ukraine

Basic Info

Project Name

Restoration of buildings and provision of materials for life support in emergency conditions of the population in the winter period in the zone of the contact line in the East of Ukraine

Start Date

01/01/2020

End Date

31/12/2020

Project Summary

Comprehensive assistance to those in need, aimed at providing them with a temporary roof over their heads if they cannot live at their old address due to violence, as well as various, flexible assistance in the restoration of their households.
Restoration and repair of bomb shelters and roofs in housing construction, issuance of life support kits in emergency conditions in winter, issuance of vouchers for construction equipment and tools, provision of rent-free tools for the population affected by the destruction of housing, with additional criteria of vulnerability (unemployment, disability, old age, the presence of minor children, chronic diseases) in the 20 kilometer zone of the contact line in the Donetsk region, establishment of a temporary residence (up to several months) for emergency accommodation of IDPs, destroyers and victims of CGBV.

1 - Needs

There are more than 300 settlements, which are home to more than 1 800 000 people, situated in the 20-kilometer zone from the demarcation line. 3332 deaths among the civilian population in the Donbas were recorded, more than 7 thousand were injured, more than 50 thousand civilian homes were damaged or destroyed, according to the report of the UN human rights Monitoring mission, from April 2014 to June 9, 2019. We can talk about at least 14 thousand damaged and in need of reconstruction of households in the NGCA. During 2016-2019 experts of "Donbass SOS" talked to 227 persons whose real estate was damaged or destroyed during the conflict in Donbass. The survey showed that the majority of respondents were forced to solve their housing problems by their own. Among the respondents, 46% are people of retirement age.In second semester of 2019, the situation in Donbass region was escalated. Regularly recorded damage to housing, power lines, boiler stations. There is a constant threat to the lives and health of civilians living near the line of contact. In the conditions of regular attacks, there is an acute issue with the maintenance of working conditions and the restoration of shelters and bomb shelters, where civilians can take refuge in case of danger. Residents also have a constant needs in building materials and tools to repair their homes. Such repairs are critical to the ability of civilians to live in that households in the winter. In case of damage, civilians also need emergency assistance in the form of emergency survival kits.According to local administrations, there are up to 1,500 shelters in NGCA, which does not cover the residents needs in shelters from shelling. Existing shelters are often in disrepair.Gender-based violence remains a major problem. According to the survey of the "East Ukrainian center of public initiatives", there are 200 cases were revealed facts of violenc out of 276 interviewed persons. The victims of violence were both men and women; both girls and boys. Women were more likely to be victims of rape and sexual intimidation, and men were more likely to be victims of torture with elements of sexual violence. The project involves the purchase of materials and external work (in the case of social institutions and disabled people whose homes are destroyed), necessary for the restoration of shelters and bomb shelters, the issuance of vouchers for construction equipment with consumables, as well as tools, the provision of free rental tools for the population affected by the destruction of housing, with additional criteria of vulnerability (unemployment, disability, old age, the presence of minor children, chronic diseases) in the 20 kilometer zone from the contact line in Donbass, also, the issuance of emergency survival kits to residents, consisting of means of heating, lighting, cooking.It is planned to involve unemployed residents living in the 20 km zone from the contact line in the Donbas and other vulnerable groups from among their neighbors in the work on the restoration of buildings with limited involvement of technical experts and payment of work of inhabitants.In addition, in the framework of the planned arrangement of temporary residence (up to several months) on the basis of the DDC for emergency IDP, destroyed and victims of CGBV; issuing the necessary documents for placement in hostels for refugees, registration of the statement in law enforcement, etc.The implementation of the program will help to increase safety for life and health of civilians, reduce the number of victims of shelling, improve the comfort of residents and their financial situation. Will support of victims а emergency situations, which will reduce the number of refugees and IDPs, as well as to realize the basic rights of those in need, such as roofs over their heads and safe environment.

2 - Activities & outcomes

The proposed project includes following measure:Output 1Procurement of materials and equipment, repair and restoration of shelters and bomb shelters. It is planned to carry out repair and arrangement of 15 shelters in schools, hospitals, kindergartens near the contact line, 1500 civilians from surrounding areas are supposed to be beneficiaries of the project. The cost of the case for one shelter is $ 5000.Output 2Provision of procurement of materials and equipment, repair and restoration of roofs, Windows and doors of houses damaged as a result of hostilities near the contact line (locations within the zone of 20 km, among the most vulnerable, disabled beneficiaries affected by the destruction of housing during the fighting, additional criteria of vulnerability are disability, old age, the presence of minor children with single parents, chronic diseases. It is planned to repair 250 houses, in which 550 people live. The cost of the case per household is $ 500.Output 3Issuance of vouchers for the purchase of materials and equipment for the repair of buildings damaged as a result of hostilities near the contact line (locations within the zone of 20 km). Beneficiaries will be able to use vouchers in large local networks of building materials distributing. This activity will be implemented based on the experience of using electronic voucher systems in other projects. It is planned to issue 1000 vouchers with a face value of $ 200, beneficiaries of at least 1800 people.Output 4Purchase and issue of tools and equipment for the restoration of residential buildings damaged in the course of hostilities. It is planned to lease tools and equipment for 200 beneficiaries from among the unemployed living within the zone of 20 km from the contact line for the period of the program, after which the equipment will remain on the balance of the applicant organization, and this activity will become a permanent form of service, until the complete loss of functionality of the equipment.Output 5.Provision of emergency life support kits to victims. It is planned to issue 400 kits, the beneficiaries of the project will be at least 1000 people. The cost of one kit of 200$.Output 6.Arrangement of temporary residence (up to several months) on the basis of the DDC for emergency accommodation of IDPs, destroyers and victims of IDP. The beneficiaries of the project will be 50 participants.
