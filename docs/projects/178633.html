Project Summary

Mercy-USA proposes to implement this project with the following three components:
1) 4,766 in-kind monthly food baskets distribution per SPHERE and FSL country level recommendations of micro and macro nutrients and a minimum intake of 2,100 kcal per person.
2) Up to 15 distributions per month (once every other week) of bread for IDPs living in camps due to limited access to bakeries by removing flour from their monthly food baskets;
3) Pre-positioned, emergency aid of 8,000 Ready to Eat Rations (RTER) distributions for newly displaced IDPs. All components of the project would be implemented in the sub-districts of Idleb City, Maaret Tamsrin, and Jandaris with support to 1,000 HH each within and outside IDP camps.
This project is ongoing and fund is secured from July 2020 until June 2021.
During 2021, the targeted beneficiaries will receive 6 rounds out of 9 rounds planned while 3 rounds during 2020, so the budget is deigned only for the activities which will be implemented during 2021

4 - Needs

The crisis in Syria has entered into its 10th year, with 2020 having seen the emergence of new and complex socioeconomic difficulties that are further exacerbating the food security and livelihood situation of families across the country. The situation in Syria has drastically deteriorated with an estimated 14.2 million people in need of some form of food and agriculture assistance, representing 68.6% of the total population; Based on a countrywide assessment of 36,484 households and technical reviews and consultations, at least 12.4 million people are estimated to be food insecure, of which 1.3million people are considered severely food insecure. Furthermore, all 1.7 million people living in camps are deemed to be 100% food insecure, and 1.8 million people are estimated to be at risk of food insecurity . The results show a sharp increase from last year, with an increase of 4.5 million people facing acute food insecurity in 2021 compared to 2020. Whereas in 2019, 39% of the total population was food insecure, that figure is now almost 60% in 2020. The number of severely food insecure people has also increased from 3% (0.6 million) of the population in 2019 to 6% (1.3 million) in 2020. (2021 HNO FSA) Based on the sector-based Food Security and Livelihood Assessment (FSLA) 2020: Food Security Index (CARI) % Severe + Moderate : Maaret Tamsrin : 59%Idleb: 51%Jandairis: Reassessing while it was 35% in 2020This highlights the need for food assistance in the targeted locations

5 - Implementation of activities

This project will be implemented by Mercy-USA field staff under the direct supervision of Mercy-USA management from the Turkey office Mercy-USA will attend all FSL Cluster meetings in Turkey, submit 5W's, along with regular information and updates about the project, and the region. Efforts will be made to coordinate with any humanitarian actors on the ground to ensure duplication is avoided. Field staff will coordinate with local councils to emphasize neutrality, and adherence to humanitarian principles while ensuring all stakeholders are informed in the area. When possible, temporary staff will be hired from the beneficiary community to maximize efficiency and impact while building local capacities. - Food Baskets will be purchased inside Turkey, and shipped cross border to the target area. - Food baskets will be delivered to designated distribution points in each targeted location in coordination with the local council in each area. - Beneficiaries will be informed of this location and Mercy-USA staff will ensure protection measures are taken to ensure equitable access particularly for women, and young girls (separate lines, distribution during daylight hours, household delivery, etc.) - ***Household distribution will take place for those vulnerable beneficiaries who cannot receive their allocation at the distribution point such as disabled. - Distribution schedule will be determined during the vulnerability assessment process in order to reasonably target the most vulnerable households first. - The program coordinator will oversee the project, compile and submit reports, and coordinate with the donor to ensure full compliance.
