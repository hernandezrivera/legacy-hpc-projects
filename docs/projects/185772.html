1 - Needs

Violent activities of Boko Haram and the armed conflict since 2009 have deeply affected North-East Nigeria and the lives of 15 million people across the region. Over 20,000 people were killed and 2.2 million were forcibly displaced. The North-East states of Borno, Adamawa and Yobe host 70% of those displaced, with regional spillovers around the Lake Chad. Affected communities have experienced severe damage to their social fabric, extensive destruction of infrastructure, and significant socio-economic basic needs that remain largely unmet. The 2016 Recovery and Peacebuilding Assessment (RPBA) estimated multi-sectoral recovery and resilience-building needs at nearly $6.7 billion. IDS will support the response to the acute humanitarian and forced-displacement crisis in Borno states by implementing a surge of high-impact, early recovery interventions (Training and Support IGA, Village Saving and Loan Activities (VSLA), Infrastructure rehabilitated and Reconciliation and social cohesion) to address urgent needs and rebuild the affected communities in the region.Violent activities of Boko Haram and the armed conflict since 2009 have deeply affected North-East Nigeria and the lives of 15 million people across the region. 9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

IDS gender officer is responsible for the collection of feedback and reports directly Project Director . Reports can be made through IDS GBV Helpline: 08172702242 or info@ids.org.ng, The phone number and email address that receives and responds to enquiries, complaints, feedbacks and reports of safeguarding issues. This phone number and email address are managed by the trained staff to maintain confidentiality and promote the rights of the complainant. IDS have a complaint and feedback box in all areas of intervention where individuals, community members and beneficiaries can anonymously drop their complaints, feedback or report any safeguarding concerns. The complaint and feedback mechanism focal persons manage the boxes at the community level while the gender officer manages them at the organizational level, IDS protection manager, gender officer and complaint and feedback mechanism (CFM) focal persons in communities of intervention are trained to receive, handle and manage reported safeguarding cases, these people are also trained to recognize and respond to safeguarding issues. All reports are treated as soon as possible efficiently and in confidentiality. Reports are assessed by the protection manager then case management for the survivor (if need be), the investigation is launched and the outcome of the investigation is used to make decisions and also used to inform the disciplinary action to be taken against perpetrators. Information on the investigation is shared on a need-to-know basis and with the consent of the complainant. The Email and complaint box mechanisms can be used for anonymity, the whistleblowing policy (procedures) protects people with serious concerns.
