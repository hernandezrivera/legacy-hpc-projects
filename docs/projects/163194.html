Basic Info

Project Name

Provision of primary health care to IDPs and host populations in the health districts of the Far North region

Start Date

01/01/2020

End Date

31/12/2020

Project Summary

Ensure continuity of provision of primary health care, at health facilities and communities, for pregnant women and children under 5 in health districts hosting IDPs in Far North region of Cameroon
Activities or outputs
Training and monitoring of service providers and community health workers
Provide treatment for conditions that have a significant impact on the chances of survival in maternal, newborn and child health such as pneumonia, diarrhea and malaria
Indicators and targets
37,000 households received LLINs
76000 children aged 6 months to 15 years vaccinated with RR
87.676 children under 5 treated at the clinical and community level
60 health care providers and 120community health workers trained in Integrated Management of Childhood Illness (IMCI)
Preventive campaigns for measles
Distribution of LLIN
Promotion of essential family practices for vulnerable populations
Data collection and use for action
