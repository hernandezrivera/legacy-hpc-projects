Project Summary

Somalia continues to experience protracted crises and emergencies, ranging from conflicts, and natural disasters and this has undermined the national educational system. Save the Children’s HRP 2020 project will be implemented across Somalia/Somaliland, focusing on the areas that are affected heavily by natural disasters, hosting huge numbers of IDPs, and areas prone to emergencies. The project will target 22,523 (11,262girls and 11,261 boys) emergencies affected school going age children from 95 targeted schools including. The project will have a strong focus on gender equity in order to address the discrepancies in access and learning outcomes between girls and boys in Somalia due to deeply ingrained gender norms. In Puntland, the HRP project will be implemented in the districts of Galkayo, Garowe, Dangoroyo, Gardho, Eyl, Jariban and Lasqaaroy. In South Central Somalia, the HRP project will be implemented in Kismayo (Jubaland), Hiraan (Hirshabelle), and Baidoa (Bay region. In Somaliland, the project will target Buuhoodle, Caynabo, Ceel Afweyn, Laas Canood, Taleex, Zeylac and Burco.
The project will focus on providing access to and equitable and quality education in a safe and protective learning environment for boys and girls affected by emergencies and crisis through carrying out enrollment drives to bring children back to school and to retaining those already in school. Provision of teaching learning materials, training and incentivization of teachers; provision of improving the learning environment through construction and rehabilitation of classrooms and TLS will ensure that children effected by emergencies are able continue with their education in a safe and conducive learning environment, and teachers are motivated to continue teaching learners affected by the emergencies. The construction will be carried out in both new and existing schools in order to address overcrowding due to displacement influxes and increase in new learners. Provision of school feeding programs for learners and clean drinking water will improve the health status of the learners and help retain them in school. The project will also implement activities aimed at strengthening the capacity of the Ministry of Education and the education cluster among others. In order to address the attitudinal and physical barriers to children learning in a safe and protective learning environment, Save the Children will carry out community sensitization and dialogue to mitigate all abusive practices towards children, teachers will be trained on inclusive, safe education and protection focal points will be established. The project will also map out protection services and establish referral mechanisms in addition to Child Rights clubs. Due to existing norms and beliefs in Somalia, children with disabilities are subject to neglect, abuse, stigma and discrimination, negative attitudes and exclusion at all levels of the education system. Girls in particular face double discrimination on account of their disability and gender and as a result, they are highly vulnerable to abuse and exploitation. The project is therefore targeting 1,126 (563 girls, 563 boys) disabled learners.

Mitigation Measures

In order to address the identified risks, Save the Children will undertake the following measures;1. Students and parents will be sensitized on exploitation and abuse, including sexual abuse and how to report any cases should they arise. This will be carried out in a confidential and culturally sensitive way to avoid further stigmatization due.2. Save the Children will consider safety risks associated with road blocks, busy roads, exposed wires and dangerous materials on the ground. 3. Save the Children will minimize and be aware of the distances children have to travel and their mode of transport to ensure that children are not exposed to protection risks on their way to school.4. All classes will take place during the day giving children sufficient time to travel home before nightfall to avoid security risks particularly for girls. 5. Play areas will be situated in in clearly visible and safe locations in addition to asking children directly (and especially adolescent girls) about the safety of their travel to and presence at school. 6. SC will have separate sessions with girls and boys to gain a deeper understanding about the barriers they face in addition to holding discussions with groups of children that could face added difficulties.7. SC will advocate for enrolling female teachers at all levels. If there are not enough qualified female teachers, we will have female teacher aids accompany classes and club activities.8. Save the Children will avoid community tensions, by providing the local/host community with access to camp services in situations where they are experiencing similar shortages of basic education services and facilities. 9. SC will also consult both displaced and host communities about education needs and assess whether assistance could exacerbate tension, harassment or conflict between the two communities. 10. Encourage the employment of female teachers and teachers with disability to build a more inclusive environment with tolerance and respect by reducing the stigma and challenging stereotypes and gender norms in a culturally sensitive way11. Collect and use data disaggregated by age, gender and diversity to effectively inform programming.12. Ensure that assistance and services are reaching the most vulnerable - Identify and prioritize the most vulnerable groups in the community and prevent discrimination or exclusion of marginalized groups 13. Save the Children will continue to advocate for equal access to education for girls and sensitize the community about the value and benefits of girls’ education.14. The targeted communities will be sensitized and involved in the beneficiary selection criteria to ensure transparency and accountability to our beneficiaries. This will be complemented by our confidential and well understood beneficiary feedback and complaint mechanism.15. All SC staff are trained in Child Safeguarding and Sexual harassment in addition to signing a legally binding code of conduct as part of their employment contract. In addition, all SC service providers are screened and sensitized on the same.16. SC will construct gender segregated, private and secure latrines and we will ensure that the toilet/latrine design accounts for children (e.g. size of drop hole may present a safety risk for children).
