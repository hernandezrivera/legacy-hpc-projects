Project Summary

AHF through this project aims to increase access to life-saving and life-sustaining humanitarian health services for the most vulnerable and in need; strengthen the health sector's capacity to prepare for, detect and deliver a timely response to disease outbreaks, including Cholera & COVID-19, and strengthen health system capacity to support continuity of care.
Through the planned intervention, AHF will support the operations of its facilities on different care levels to continue the provision of PHC, SHC, and THC services as follows:
- PHC services through: Atareb PHC with an attached mobile clinic, Salamah PHC with an attached mobile clinic, Kherbet Eljoz PHC, Kafra PHC, with EPI supervision project in Idleb, the estimated number of beneficiaries from PHC services is 317,750.
- SHC through Sham Children's Hospital in Sarmada, Sham pediatrics hospital in A'zaz, Sham surgical hospital in Tal Elkarameh, Arraei Hospital's Pediatric department, and Sham ambulance system in many locations across Idleb and Aleppo a part of the central ambulance system, the estimated number of beneficiaries from SHC services is 235,412.
- In addition, providing specialized health services includes rehabilitation services, hearing aids, assitive D. to PwDs through 2 centers in Tal Elkarameh and Sarmada, and 1 Dental mobile clinic in A'zaz, the estimated number of beneficiaries from the specialized health services is 19,888.
- Outbreaks response will be maintained through the COVID-19 department (stand by) at Alsham Internal medicine hospital in Dana and Cholera integrated services with the same hospital and Sham Children's Hospital, and our ambulances for outbreaks, the estimated number of beneficiaries from AHF's outbreak response is 6,365.
The mentioned facilities are also putting into consideration easy access to people with disabilities through the prepared infrastructure of those facilities to make sure of full access.
As always, AHF will have the previous interventions fully coordinated with all health actors through the Health Cluster whether through the data in 4Ws, HeRAMS and DHIS2. or through its constant participation and active membership within the TWGs like SRH, PHC, and Non-Communicable Diseases, SIG, Rehab, referral, Supply Line, Cholera TF and their related groups, and on the ground, AHF constantly coordinates with the HDs.
Before the project implementation, the M&E team will make regularly collect the required data that help in making enlightened decisions. Monitoring and evaluation of the quality of the service provision through analyzing the collected data based on the Log-Frame, the project will be monitored and evaluated through three levels:
1) The operational, for which the project manager is responsible.
2) The technical, for which the technical team is responsible.
3) The objectives, for which the MEAL team is responsible.
Monitoring tools:
• Performance Indicator Tracking Tables (PITTs), issued on a monthly basis.
• Satisfaction surveys among beneficiaries and stakeholders will be analyzed and reported to stakeholders.
• Project Evaluation Report, by the end of the project term.
The project activities will be coordinated with other actors including partners through the TWGs meetings and health cluster meetings, in addition to the regular meetings with HDs, so we have full coordination with all parties working in the same field, and for the community within the project areas we are coordinating with the local councils through regular visits will be conducted by the ambulance system administration manager.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Through this project, AHF will work on the recruitment and provide the salaries to the HFs' staff, carry out general maintenance for the HFs and support the operational costs, and provide capacity building program to staff members (1,045 medical and nonmedical) of the facilities covering many topics like IPC, PFA, Cholera, NRP, NICU, BLS, ACLS, ATLS etc., Provide PHC services according to revisited EHSP including CD, NCD, child health, and SRH, provide trauma, surgeries, and in-patients services and specialized centers including ICU, PICU, NICU, maternal and child health, and Cholera integrated servicess. In addition to procuring medical/non-medical supplies for the HFs, Raising awareness sessions, health-promotion, to the community through CHWs, Provision of emergency referral services (Emergency and outbreaks response), Providing physical therapy sessions and prostheses through 2 rehab centers, Implementation of IPC program in all HFs, Sustain supervision of EPI services, and Provision of PSEA and safeguarding training. Coordination of all the activities with IHD for Idleb, and with AHD, Turkish health Authorities in Afrin and Azaz, technical coordination with Health cluster, WHO, and with partners working in the same areas. During the project implementation, AHF's Meal unit will contribute to improving decision-making and management, by keeping the project on track to achieve the outcomes and objectives and by benefiting from prev. lessons learned.
