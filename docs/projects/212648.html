Project Summary

Chronic malnutrition remains high, so nutrition interventions concentrate not only onidentification and treatment but also on the prevention of malnutrition, illness, and death. In this regard, the response will target vulnerable mothers and caregivers to optimizeprompt and appropriate infant and young child feeding (IYCF) and care practices throughnutrition and health education and IYCF counseling for health and nutrition staff.Capacity building will include comprehensive training in IYCF and community management of acute malnutrition (CMAM) best practices. Local capacity building will focus on prevention and strengthening community health workers, including scaling upthe family MUAC approach and caregiver referral system. Multisector coordination will involve the nutrition sector collaboration with the health sector to improve the health status of mothers and young children and reduce the risk of disease.
The project will cover April 2024 till Dec 2024 and will aim to deliver facility and outreach-based nutrition services:
CMAM:
2.1Outpatient therapeutic program (OTP): includes Severe acute malnutrition (SAM).
2.2Supplementary feeding program (SFP): includes Moderate Acute Malnutrition (MAM)in all supported health facilities.
2.3Stabilization Center (SC): includes Severe acute malnutrition (SAM) with complicationsin Bab Al Salam and Hope hospitals.
IYCF:
· Exclusive breastfeeding for six months.
. Monitor distribution and use of breast milk substitutes.
· Enter complementary food after six months old.
· Begin breastfeeding during the hour after delivery.
raising awareness services: At the community level, the IYCF and CMAM facilitators willdistribute messaging that cover topics on Family health, NCDs, CDs, EPI, Lifestyle,Nutrition module, and make referrals to the unique services at the nearest HFs as neededaccording to the WHO referral guidelines.
the services will be provided within 24 OTP, 24 TSFP both services are provided inside PHC centers, 2 SC, 4 MBAs, and 4 rapid response teams in eight districts of Aleppo and Idleb governorates;Afrin, AlBab, Harim, Azaz, Jarablus, Idleb, and Jabal Saman targeting reproductive-aged women, PLWs, and U5 children of the host and IDP communities.
The RRTs activities:
- Within the CMAM program: Screening, referral and follow-up children 6-59, pregnant and lactating women who are malnourished.
- Within IYCF-E program: Provide emergency IYCF counseling for mothers with moderatelymalnourished children. Distribute micronutrients for preventing of anemia andmalnutrition among children and pregnant, lactating women. Disseminate messages(IEC/BCC) on IYCF-E recommended feeding practices.
- WaSH: Support hygiene promotion
- CP: Identify visual signs of abuse/violence against childU5/PLWs during Nutritionscreening.
The services will be provided through fi xed facilities and outreach in Kafr Karmin, Azaz,Soran, Bab Iman, Bab Noor, Ekdeh,Shamarekh, Al Bab, Jarablus, Al Rayyan, Zoghra, AlSalam, Afi eh, Hezri, Kelli, Mariamin,Dana, Afrin, Bulbul, Medanki, Bza'a in addition to 2hospitals (M. Wassim Maaz and Hope) and1 MC in Azaz, 4 child-mother spaces in Azaz camps and 4 rapid response teams in Azaz, Jarablus, Al Bab and Afrin districts.
The target groups will be children U5, and pregnant and lactating women including beneficiaries with disabilities and as well as caregivers of the children U5. The project response is committed to a holistic approach with enhanced coordination among nutritionactors within and across sectors to achieve a reduction in life-saving and life-sustainingneeds. the services will be guided by the severity of conditions in the targeted areas.
the project will target 97,056 benefi ciaries including 72000 children U5 and 25,056 PLWs.the children U5 will 50% will be male and 50% will be female, 70% IDPs and 30% Host. ThePLWs will be 100% female, 70% IDPs and 30% Host. of the total benefi ciaries 10% will bepeople with disabilities.
