Project Summary

To contribute to improved and equitable access to quality lifesaving services for management of Severe Acute Malnutrition in need in selected LGAs across Borno, and Adamawa States
Strengthen preventative nutrition services for vulnerable groups focusing on supplementary feeding activities, appropriate infant and young child feeding practices, micronutrient supplementation and optimal maternal nutrition.
40,000 Caregivers/Pregnant and Lactating Women (IYCF counselling, Health Talks, Sensitisation)
Set up 150 support groups and conduct 100 food demonstrations across implementing states
Support 6,000 children 6-23 months on Micronutrient Powder (MNP)

Caritas Nigeria is an active member of the Nutrition in Emergency Working Group in both Federal Level at Abuja, chaired by Federal Ministry of Health and Borno and Adamawa states which are chaired by the State primary health care Development Agencies. We have formally notified the Borno State Primary Health Care Development Agency (SPHCDA) and Ministry of Health as well as held meetings with the Directors of SPHCDA on the forthcoming interventions. We have already established good working relationship with the state nutrition officer and the respective Nutrition Officers in-charge of the target LGAs as well as established working partnerships with some PHCs within these LGAs.

Needs

From April 2017 to date, Caritas Nigeria has been implementing CMAM and IYCF in Sothern Borno and Northern Adamawa region. Our target LGAs of implementation in this region include: Hawul and Askira Uba LGAs in Borno State and Michika, Madagali and Mubi North LGAs in Adamawa states. Caritas Nigeria has been working to promote access to services for the treatment and prevention of malnutrition among vulnerable groups, focusing on IYCF in emergencies, and pregnant/lactating women, for improved health for both Mother and Child. Our choice of these LGAs is due to the high prevalence of SAM cases noted in these LGAs during our field assessments and field observations during our ongoing project interventions in the targets locations, as well as locations as agreed for our interventions by the State Nutrition Sector Working group, and as indicated on Nutrition Sector Humanitarian Response Plan of 2018, as areas with caseload.Mubi-north and Madagali according to the nutrition HRP for need assessment, records high needs for SAM, Caritas has just moved in in these areas to help reduce the burden of malnutrition in the community, has only been operational for three months, during which high number of patients have been enrolled on the MNP and OTP program. To ensure quality of project implementation, there is need for project continuation to ensure project transitioning and hand over of activities to the community through the services of the community health workers engaged in the project
