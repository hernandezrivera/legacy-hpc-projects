Project Summary

Natural disasters and conflicts have a profound impact on children’s psychosocial well-being and development. Exposure to violence, disaster, loss of, or separation from, family members and friends, deterioration in living conditions, inability to provide for one’s self and family, and lack of access to services can all have immediate and long-term consequences for children, families and communities and impair their ability to function normally and be fulfilled. Without support, this subjects communities to trauma, entrapment in cycles of violence and other effects of unaddressed trauma. Trauma, if addressed, is passed on to the next generation as a story, but if unaddressed, it is passed on to the next generation as trauma to be experienced again. Such analysis is where the crux of this initiative is based on. To provide Psychosocial support (PSS), including trauma counselling and psycho-social first aid, as a vital step on the road to recovery and building resilience to children and youth and their caregivers. The initiative addresses five P’s of child protection in that; it gives children and youth the necessary knowledge and resilience enhancing their ability to respond to future violence (Prevention); it enables them to be at the forefront of issues which affect them (Paramountcy); enhances community partnership in child protection; reinforces community protection mechanisms and enhances parental responsibility.

In these clubs, children and youth will meet to enhance social connectedness and they will be connected with relevant stakeholders and leaders from their community who will give them help in solving any problems they may face, for example, child protection issues, child abuse, making communities safer, etc.)At least 3000 people (children, youth and caregivers) to be offered one on one counselling.Build capacity and knowledge in local relevant organisations such as VHWs, CCWs on GBViE and PSEA Please note that Singing to the Lions is a psychosocial support tool which is targeted at enhancing the capacity of communities to be resilient in the face of fear and violence in their lives.
