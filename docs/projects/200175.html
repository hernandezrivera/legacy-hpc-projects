Project Summary

The aim of the project is to provide legal consultancy and legal representation of the victims in order to get their rights, in addition to providing quality services such as case management , PSS and mental Health support for 200 women and girls in the area of the North specifically in the area of Tripoli- Zgharta- Menieh- Tebbeneh. This project will be in coordination with the relevant Municipalities in the selected areas and the different actors working under protection in the North. There will be a close coordination with the GBV working group in order to identify the beneficiaries through the placed pathway. Moreover, this project was designed according to the identified needs, where access to justice for victims of trafficking is or unavailable or very limited in addition to quality services. HDA has an experienced team that provides quality services for GBV victims in general and victims of trafficking especially in addition to the legal services. HDA do provide prevention and response services for victims of GBV. Our lawyers will provide legal representation for the victims and our social workers and psychologist will be providing the case management and the mental health support for them. We will be using our premises and some safe spaces in the relevant municipalities or other community centers in the areas where we usually provide our services.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

-Legal Consultancy to 200 women and girls, this will be provided by our lawyers-Legal representation to 70 women and girls where our lawyers will be accompanying victims to the court. -PSS to 100 women and girls that will be provided by our case workers.-Case management to 70 women and girls that will be provided by our case management workers-Mental Health Support to 50 women and girls and this will be provided by our psychologist.-Gender equality awareness sessions will be provided to 200 women and girls in the different areas and empowerment activities will be provided in the safe spaces of the relevant municipalities at the community level in the selected areas for 100 women and girls victims of trafficking.Our activities will be provided in our premises and in other safe spaces that we usually provide our services, in addition to the premises of the relevant municipalities where the project will be implemented. The outreach of the beneficiaries will be through the placed pathway and in coordination with the protection actors in the area in addition to our beneficiaries who we identified during the implementation of previous projects and not being provided with the needed service.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

HDA has a PSEA policy that is set and all the employees who works in HDA including those who will be implementing this project has signed the policy and they are aware of it. The project will be respecting the confidentiality of the beneficiaries and will be working with DO NO HARM with all beneficiaries without any discrimination. The project will be inclusive since it will be having beneficiaries from different background: Refugees IDPs and hosting community and PWDs. Moreover, there will be a complain system where beneficiaries can put their complains if any.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

The project will ensure accountability for affected populations through including them in everything step of the project from the designing step till the closing of the project. We will be sharing with them every information needed, and we will be conducting FGDs and interviews with the beneficiaries in order to get their feedback and what we will be doing to improve the performance of the activities and their impact.
