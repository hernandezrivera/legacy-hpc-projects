Project Summary

In 2024, UNICEF will increase the convergence and quality of its programme for most affected populations in the most vulnerable regions, addressing underlying risks to vulnerability to reduce humanitarian needs by enhancing access to critical services with an integrated package of health, nutrition, education, child protection and WASH. Improving child protection services remains a priority, and UNICEF's systemic approach include tackle child protection issues: birth registration and identity, case management for at-risk and vulnerable children including victims of violence, human trafficking, and unaccompanied and separated children, and psychosocial support and mental health care for children and caregivers.
In this project, UNICEF's key intervention will be: i) Strengthening the capacity of sub-national child protection system to carry out case management of vulnerable children.3 Ii) Provision of community mental health and psychosocial support services. iii) Access to a safe channel to report sexual exploitation and abuse. iv) Alternative care measures for children and adolescents and family reunification. v) Support community platforms on empowerment, participation, behaviour change actions and social norms linked to the protection of children and adolescents.
The area of implementation will be in 54 municipalities in District Capital, Amazonas, Apure, Bolivar, Delta Amacuro, Miranda, Sucre, Tachira, Zulia and La Guaira.
