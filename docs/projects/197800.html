1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Taking into consideration existing needs of IDPs populations, the project aims to reach 15600 Households living in the districts of Montepuez, Balama, Namuno, Metuge, Pemba, Ibo, through provision of 840 shelters, and 15600 non-food items life-saving assistance for most vulnerable crisis affected population.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

1. Evaluation, Identification and Selection of the HHs utilizing vulnerability criteria in case of need of prioritization. 2. Shelter assessment (safety audit -if not done recently by site planner), shelter checklist); 3. Community focus group to share and define construction standards as well as main need and possible solutions more fit to the camp/environment 4. Capacity building and on site support to local artisans – done mostly with technical and continuous support on ground. 5. Procurements of Material for shelter construction or NFI. 6. Selection of IDPs engaged in construction as local artisans, promoting inclusion and involvement of women and people with disability/members of HHs who host a person living with a disability. 7. Shelter construction – will be done involving as much as possible the affected population and preferably with local material or locally procured material. 8. NFI kits distribution, which includes eventually items received in-kind. 9. Monitoring – shelter construction, satisfaction, promotion of best practices 10. Complaint mechanisms setting in line with AVSI AAP policy and possibly with harmonized cluster/partners tools

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The project will ensure protection mainstreaming process in order to ensure DO NO HARM approach, promoting meaningful access, safety and dignity in all actions. In order to guarantee these principles all the staff will be trained on the principles and procedures in place and will sign the reference documents such as the Internal Code of Conduct, the Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse Policy (PSEA) and the AVSI Children Safeguarding Policy documents as well as the AVSI lead protection mainstreaming checklists. In order to avoid conflicts between IDPs (new or extended) and host communities, targeting for individual assistance will be done in consultation with the communities, defining together the criteria of vulnerability and ensuring that all the different groups are represented in the consultations. This will also ensure the use of the principles of impartiality and neutrality: no distinction of nationality, race, religion, social condition and political affiliation will be made and no sides will be taken in any dispute. Protection monitoring will be guarantee through routinely monitoring access to services by women, girls, boys and men through spot checks and discussions with communities, and promptly address the obstacles to equal access

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

Gender equality and women’s empowerment are preconditions for effective and sustainable development and the enjoyment of universal human rights. The project will ensure IASC GAM within the different phases of the project: - Need Analysis set, in order that all and the right people are understood and counted - - Tailoring and adapting the interventions according to gender and age - Ensuring a gender and aged balanced participation on the feed-back process The project shall make ensure gender equaility and inclusion criteria are used. AVSI promotes and encourages active participation of women. As such, the project has a strong commitment to ensure the full participation of women in decision-making and implementation of its programmes including in beneficiary selection, implementation, and monitoring to the greatest extent possible within culturally appropriate contexts. Gender will be mainstreamed throughout the entire project. For all constructions, community members, and women in particular will be consulted on the proposed models, as well as on the structure’s orientation and placement; women with previous knowledge on construction practices will be prioritized in the selection of artisans. All artisans and daily workers will receive a basic PSEA training before initiating construction, provided by trained AVSI protection personnel. The project shall collect and report program monitoring data which includes sex- and age-disaggregated data of beneficiaries and will ensure that such informations are used purposely to improve all groups and age inclusion, participation and satisfaction. This project will follow AVSI Foundation SOP described in the AVSI FOUNDATION ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY on ‘feedback and accountability mechanism’. Newly recruited and old staff are always oriented and receive refresher trainings to stress the importance of the opinions and views of communities concerning program implementation. Beneficiaries and the population at large is also made aware of the forms available to provide suggestions and complaints. This project will adopt a community-based and participatory approach. The selection criteria will be developed in consultation with the communities and will aim to upgrade living-conditions within a settlement and when possible through the construction of new shelters. AVSI will conduct the assessment in KOBO according to the agreed criteria, which will be reviewed and verified by the local authorities and the representatives of the communities. In AVSI’s experience, this has proven time and again to be particularly productive, as not only people’ concerns and priorities are taken into account.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

In the present project proposal screening, surveillance and participation of people with disabilities have been included as key activities and indicators. During their implementation, the detection of people with disabilities will be reported (disaggregated by gender and age) and given special attention. This translates into prioritizing the abovementioned target population as to the assistance for Shelter, NFI and Protection, especially for those cases having motor disabilities to which AVSI Foundation will provide logistics support. AVSI Foundation will, when possible, organize specific studies on the inclusion and conditions of persons living with a disability and will utilize such studies to devise inclusive strategies on round table with PwD as well as local communities. AVSI Foundation will ensure the implementation of sensitization sessions throughout its activities to ensure that these people are supported within thsere community and given special attention when coordinating and planning activities with local SDGCAS (Serviço Distrital de Genero, Crianças e Acçao Social) and partners.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

AVSI aims to promote a sustainable and healthy environment in order to improve the wellbeing of the community. Given the experience of AVSI Mozambique in energy efficiency-related projects, methods to save charcoal and wood during cooking will be also promoted so to sensitize the target population on preventing deforestation. This in turn will positively impact family finances by incurring savings on wood and charcoal. In addition, , the use of local materials for construction will be prioritized. This will help promote an environment-friendly approach by trying to increase awareness in the affected communities. When possible, local procurement of shelter materials will be conducted to ensure a positive contribution to local economies and reduce enviromenal overheads associated with transportation. At the same time, however, technical quality is often varied and at times below the required standards because of: - Limited capacity to deliver bulk shipments of local products - No authority for quality certification of wooden products or manufactured products - Limited industrial capacity and many small suppliers on the market.Moreover, with strong community engagement and participation on rehabilitation activities, the project will put specific attention regarding the site selection and sourcing of materials, favoring the use of local and renewable resources and where possible promoting good environmental practices. Furthermore, AVSI can rely to an internal Policy on environment on May 11th 2020.
