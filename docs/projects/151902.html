Project Summary

The main target of DIBAL 360 NIKAO Foundation is to use Vocational/ skills acquisition training, coaching, mentoring and other access to income generating activities to support livelihood stabilization and income recovery which will be targeting conflict-affected youth and vulnerable women. The project will also provide employment opportunity and empowerment to beneficiaries in the selected LGAs of Borno state in the North east of Nigeria, thereby contributing directly to the ER & L Objective 2: (Support Livelihood Stabilization and income recovery).

Needs

Over the years, there has been a severe conflict that seem to be unending in the northeast of Nigeria; this led to a number of families and household been displaced from their comfortable residential communities, which also contributed to the deaths of persons within the northeast region and the gross resultant recession from the displacement of this communities.The continuing lack of safe livelihoods risks contributing to ongoing cycles of violence, fueling grievances and further undermining the stretched coping capacities of impoverished communities. Massive investment in livelihoods opportunities is needed, combined with short- and long-term approaches, to prevent a deterioration of the situation.According to the ER response plan; it was established that restoring livelihoods could include short-term employment creation, providing market-based skills, including vocational training, micro and small enterprise recovery that will be targeting youth and women. In order for DIBAL 360 NIKAO Foundation to respond; it becomes essential to address negative coping issues which include borrowing food, buying food on credit, selling of productive assets, borrowing of cash from others and spending savings; with all these listed therefore, empowering vulnerable youth and women with vocational skills training becomes crucial for people to be self-reliant.

Activities

The intended activity will basically be; to provide vocational skills training to the most vulnerable victims of the recent insurgency in selected LGAs of Borno State; thereby empowering the beneficiaries so that they can be independent and cater for immediate household needs.In vocational training: DIBAL 360 NIKAO Foundation vocational training response plan includes three flexible approaches that can be selected depending on the context. In the urban areas, DIBAL 360 NIKAO Foundation will identify the most vulnerable in all the IDP camps/ Host communities and get them enrolled into the organization’s vocational training Centre (which is managed by DIBAL 360 NIKAO Foundation). DIBAL 360 NIKAO Foundation will now use the expertise of artisan to train 10,000 conflict affected adolescents, young people and vulnerable women to undergo tailoring, leather work (making of belt, Shoe, Purse, bag, etc.), bead making, Kanuri native cap knitting and bamboo weaving.2. In apprenticeship: in areas where DIBAL 360 NIKAO Foundation training Centre are not available, we will explore the option of apprenticeship where beneficiaries will be placed to learn skills from local artisans and this will be monitored by the organizational monitoring team. DIBAL 360 NIKAO Foundation will set up mobile vocational skills trainings for locations where neither training Centre (Institution) nor apprenticeship opportunities are available. DIBAL 360 NIKAO Foundation will also train formal instructors to work with conflict-affected adolescents, youth and women. For rigorous employability training, especially in parallel technical training and apprenticeships, DIBAL 360 NIKAO Foundation will offer training and mentoring to enhance the ‘employability’ skills of adolescent girls/ boys and women to increase success. At the end of the training, NIKAO Foundation will empower all the beneficiaries with start-up kits/ machines so as to establish their small scale enterprise of choice to make earnings and be able to find alternative livelihood income.Finally, Dibal 360 NIKAO Foundation will use its appropriate tracking too; in monitoring the performance of beneficiaries and to also check for sustainability of their business.
