Project Summary

This project is proposed to save lives through the provision of Gender responsive and Conflict sensitive Water Sanitation and Hygiene Services to Populations affected by emergencies in Yobe State, Northeast Nigeria. Since the WASH facilities already existing in IDP camps are overstretched due to the severity of the situation, this project will provide and improve access to water and sanitation facilities by constructing and rehabilitation of new and existing boreholes, respectively. The project is targeting to reach 65,000 individuals individuals across Bade, Bursari, Gujba, Geidam, Fune, Potiskum, Machina, Nangere, Tarmuwa and Yusufari LGAs in Yobe State. The targeted live-saving interventions include construction and rehabilitation of boreholes and latrines as well as targeted demand creation process using the Community
Led Total Sanitation (CLTS) model. The project will provide immediate hygiene replenishment materials
to vulnerable individuals. The action will also include conducting water quality testing and monitoring to facilitating feasibility studies.

1 - Needs

Due to the protracted humanitarian crisis in the Northeastern states of Borno, Adamawa and Yobe (BAY), which has caused displacement of populations and destruction social infrastructures, including water and sanitation facilities, over 5 million people are estimated to be in critical need of Water Supply, Sanitiation and Hygiene Assistance. The heavy flood witnessed during the 2024 rain season has further esclated the situation with several water and sanitation facilities destroyed in camps and communities. This has also led outbreaks of cholera and other water borne diseases disproportionately affecting the population in affected areas across the three states.

YOPCODI will ensure that safety, dignity, accountability and do no harm procedures are implemented and respected during the entire period of implementation. YOPCODI has its own robust strategies, policies and procedures in place to ensure the protection of children, youth and adults from sexual exploitation and abuse, and sexual harassment. These measures are designed to safeguard beneficiaries, staff, volunteers and partner representatives from harm, ensuring that activity sites and workplaces are a safe environment - free from abuse, exploitation and discrimination. Applying the same approach that will be followed for child safeguarding, We will ensure that everyone has adopted and apply all necessary policies and procedures to protect children, youth and adults (including staff and associates) from sexual exploitation and abuse, and sexual harassment. This will include ensuring access to incident reporting mechanisms, affording protection to individuals under whistleblower policies, and referral of survivors to appropriate assistance services in line with the UN Victim Assistance Protocol, including MHPSS and health services.

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

YOPCODI will work within IASC framework for Accountability to affected Population that enforces provision of information, involvement of the target community/persons in decision making process related to the humanitarian response and setting up of complaint mechanism and feedback loop during the implementation of the project. Therefore, complaints and feedbacks generated through such channels will be used to make necessary adjustments to program delivery. At inception of the project, a meeting with state and LGA level and community stakeholders including persons with disability, women and girls to explain to the beneficiary groups and government partners the project deliverables, their roles and expected results of the project, and the change the project aims to support. To ensure that the targeted beneficiaries’ views are captured, YOPCODI will promote the use use of local languages during engagements with community members, to give them a sense of belonging and encouragement to provide feedback. This will create continuous communication with communities to inform them of about the programme (at inception), gather and timely respond to feedback including dissemination of findings of assessment conducted during the project implementation. YOPCODI will also adopt community participatory approaches in planning, implementation and monitoring of the project, including setting up of effective Complaint response Mechanisms appropriate to the culture, norms and values of the population we serve.
