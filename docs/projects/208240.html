End Date

31/12/2024

Project Summary

WFP aims to meet the food and nutritional needs of vulnerable populations in crisis-affected areas, including refugees, internally displaced people, returnees and host communities, through a package of unconditional food and nutritional assistance in these priority areas.
In 2024, WFP plans to provide food assistance through food modalities to around 350,000 vulnerable people.
