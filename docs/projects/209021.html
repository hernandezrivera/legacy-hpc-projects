Project Summary

The project aims to reduce the suffering of conflict affected population focusing in vulnerable people like children, women and PWD, and support them through provision of life saving quality nutrition services which will be integrated with health, WASH & education, as integrated multisectoral project to be implemented in Plan International operation areas. Plan international through this project will provide life saving and emergency nutrition services in 21 health & nutrition centers through out 21 localities across the 4 targeted programme areas: White Nile (Ad Diwaim, Aj Jabalain, As Salam / Ar Rawat, Guli, Kosti, Rabak, Tendalti, Um Rimta), North Kurdufan (Sheikan), Kassala ((Madeinat Kassala, Reifi Aroma, Reifi Gharb Kassala, Halfa Aj Jadeedah, Reifi Nahr Atbara, Reifi Shamal Ad Delta, Reifi Wad Elhilaiw ),Gedaref (Al Mafaza, Al Galabat Al Gharbyah - Kassab, Gala'a Al Nahal, Madeinat Al Gedaref, Al Qureisha).The project targets 197,600 among them is 105,000 children, 33,600 PLW and 120 health workers. The project link to Humanitarian Response Plan 2024 Strategic Objective 1: Provide safe, timely, principled and gender-responsive life-saving humanitarian assistance to the most vulnerable groups to reduce mortality and morbidity in areas with high severity of need or at risk of a further deterioration of critical humanitarian needs. And to Strategic objective 3: Provide safe, equitable, dignified and unhindered access to critical basic services and livelihoods opportunities for the most vulnerable groups to prevent further erosion of their coping abilities. The nutrition response will be aligned with nutrition cluster strategy, Sector Objective 1: Life saving preventive and protection intervention focusing among under-two. In addition, the project activities will link to Sector Objective 2: Treatment of Severe and moderate among under-fives and PLWs, and Sector Objective 4: Evidence generation, situation and response monitoring. Plan International will support and work to address the needs in project targeted locations in the area where there are vulnerable communities who are affected by armed conflict, the aim is to improve the nutrition status of children and PLW and enhance their survival and resilience. This project is aimed to reduce the mortality and morbidity rates and the impact of poor growth due to malnutrition among under five children and pregnant & lactating women through provision of lifesaving prevention and protective intervention for children and PLW. The project will implement life-saving and prevention activities which includes early detection of malnutrition among U5& PLW referral and regular MUAC screening at community and HH level, IYCF messages distribution, formation of MSGs, Strengthening and scaling up breastfeeding program, provision of referral cost of SAM cases with medical complications as well as management of acute malnutrition for children and PLW, in addition, to provision of support and transport the supplies from SMOH level to targeted nutrition centers, and capacity building to the services providers on IYCF, CMAM, and surveillance and reporting system. The project team will also ensure coordination with other interventions. The project will deliver these activities through community services adopting the sector objectives and approaches, besides integrating some of the WASH interventions into nutrition centers to ensure the minimum IPC is applied.
