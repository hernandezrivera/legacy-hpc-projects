Project Summary

The overall objective is to ensure that humanitarian response in oPt contributes to gender equality and addresses and advocates for the needs and priorities of women, men, girls and boys equitably and effectively.
The project supports gender mainstreaming of the multi year Humanitarian Response Plan for oPt for 2018 2020 with particular focus on the following:
Supporting advocacy and response to the needs of women faced with specific vulnerabilities including women at risk of displacement in Area C, female IDPs in Gaza, women victims of GBV, women working in agriculture, and women with disabilities.

Making available qualitative and quantitative data to inform the multi year humanitarian planning cycle

Enhancing coordination mechanisms with Women NGOs to ensure their effective engagement of women and girls as participants and right holders in the humanitarian response
Provide ongoing support to Cluster Gender Focal Points to capacitate them to better strengthen clusters' work on addressing gender needs and priorities.
The project responds to the HRP Strategic Objective 1: The rights of Palestinians living under occupation, including those living under the blockade and other restrictions, are protected, respected and promoted in accordance with IHL and IHRL; and Strategic Objective 2: The basic needs of vulnerable Palestinians living under occupation are met through the provision of essential services, in accordance with the rights of protected persons under IHL.

Needs

UN Women’s strategic partnerships with OCHA and humanitarian partners have led to positive achievements in engendering humanitarian action. The UN Women and OCHA first Joint Action Plan implementation (2015-2017) demonstrated that humanitarian partners are increasingly focused on advancing gender equality in humanitarian response by mainstreaming gender in cluster response plans and projects developed for potential funding under the HRP. While progress has been made and several results can be shown, it is important not to lose the momentum. A protracted crisis like the one in Palestine can result in progress being diluted rapidly without sustained efforts to build on the foundations achieved. Therefore, there is a strong need to continue to enhance the partnerships and gender mainstreaming of humanitarian action further. For This purpose, UN Women and OCHA have developed the second joint action plan (2018-2020) which was endorsed in July 2018. The second joint action plan was developed to ensure that progress with regards to engendering humanitarian action is maintained and accelerated. Through this project and in cooperation with OCHA and the Humanitarian gender group (HGG), UN Women proposes to contribute to the implementation of different components under the joint action plan particularly including: a) addressing gender specific capacity needs at a cluster and inter-cluster levels, b) producing gender analysis to inform humanitarian planning, programming and response, c) solidifying the partnership with women’s organizations to address humanitarian needs and gender-based vulnerabilities, d) improving the participation of women and girls as right holders and affected population in humanitarian planning and response, e) and strengthening the gender focus in advocacy messaging and events/initiatives.

Activities

CSS Objective 1: Humanitarian response is implemented through effective coordination mechanisms and supported by the timely allocation of humanitarian pooled funds Output 1.1: Strengthened gender focus in humanitarian action through the implementation of UN Women and OCHA joint action plan (2018 2020) Activity 1.1.1: Provide technical and financial support to clusters to produce knowledge products focused on gender as relevant to cluster members’ needs (including assessments, guidebooks, gender responsive evaluation etc) (specific focus on 2 clusters / year) Activity 1.1.2: In consultation with HGG members, produce a gender analysis (The Gender Alert brief) that covers gender needs and priorities under each cluster and that to inform HNO and HRP development. Activity 1.1.3: leverage partnership with local NGOs, especially Women's Organizations to support the involvement of women and girls as right holders in humanitarian planning. Activity 1.1.4: Organize Humanitarian Gender Group meetings with GFPs and cluster coordinators on a quarterly basis, to address and agree on key actions and deliverables for HGG members to strengthen gender mainstreaming in cluster planning and response especially during the HPC cycle. Activity 1.1.5: In partnership with HGG, organize trainings that support the roll-out of GAM and ensure that technical support is provided to cluster members through different means/methods (including GAM clinics) Activity 1.1.6: Support the organization of advocacy initiatives that highlight the needs of women faced with specific vulnerabilities including women at risk of displacement in Area C, female IDPs in Gaza, women victims of GBV, women working in agriculture, and women with disabilities.

Indicators

The beneficiaries of the project will include key humanitarian actors such as the clusters, cluster members and civil society/women’s organizations as (duty bearers) and women and girls as right holders who will benefit from gender sensitive humanitarian response. UN Women will ensure regular monitoring of project activities through applying internal monitoring and reporting mechanisms.
