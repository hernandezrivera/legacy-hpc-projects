Project Summary

The project will provide basic nutrition services to children under five and pregnant and lactating women. The project aims to contribute to the reduction of acute malnutrition (severe and moderate) rates and underweight prevalence among children under 5 years while improving the nutritional status in pregnant and lactating women and increasing beneficiary households’ knowledge on health and nutrition. The project will ensure services to all eligible beneficiaries disaggregating beneficiaries by gender and age taking into account special needs such as disability and other vulnerability. Complaints and feedback mechanisms will be in place to ensure accountability to affected populations including help desks, hotlines, post and process monitoring

1 - Identified Needs

According to the preliminary results of the fourth round of the SMART survey (October 2019), GAM rates are still 10.9 percent in the camps. Due to COVID-19, it has been difficult to conduct other assessments, however programme data suggests that cases have increased. This might also be due to modified admission procedures in place due to COVID-19. In addition, anaemia rates are still quite high in children and PLW. Results from food security assessments still show that there is lack of dietary diversity. With the anticipated second wave of the COVID-19 pandemic, it is expected that malnutrition will be on the rise. In addition, according to the 2019 coverage survey in the camps, though the coverage had increased, it had still not reached SPHERE standards. COVID-19 related restrictions have made community mobilization difficult meaning that there could be more children who are not being reached with services and efforts to prevent and treat and malnutrition should be sustained.
