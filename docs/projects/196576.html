Project Summary

Nearly 85% of the overall population reported a deterioration in their ability to meet basic needs, demonstrated through a significant increase in the inaccessibility of NFI items, such as clothing, mattresses, and blankets. Coupled with the deterioration of shelter integrity, increasing shelter adequacy issues (i.e. limited electricity/lighting and poor insulation from heat/cold) households are exposed to harsh weather conditions with a high risk of loss of life, particularly during winter. Adequate shelter conditions and access to basic NFIs is vital for maintaining good physical health, mental well-being, and to sustain learning and educational opportunities. Thirty per cent of the population rely on either communal or private generators, exposing households to high fuel costs and driving a need for quality winter clothing. Consequently, basic items are out of reach as families’ average monthly income ‘v’ expenditure gap reaches 60 per cent. The number of people in need of NFI support is expected to increase in 2023 with a projected worsening of the economic situation. (HNO 2023)
The in-kind and voucher assistance project aims at providing the most needed basic materials and equipment which are not affordable by IDPs, returnees, and affected host communities. The main objectives of this program are prevention of and reducing protection risks faced by persons of concern while mitigating their resort to harmful coping mechanisms through the provision of NFI materials and equipment in rural Homs especially the eastern rural and some areas in the northern by providing life-saving NFI. In addition, it will provide cash-based voucher to help theses families in getting needed fuel for winter as subsided.
The in-kind assistance will be delivered through the community centers as a one-off type of assistance based on identified needs through different means (including registration at the community centers , home visits, etc.).
All in-kind materials will be delivered at the association's centers, and cash vouchers for heating fuel will be delivered to all beneficiaries.
AL-Birr will ensure non-discrimination in all interactions with the individuals/ family throughout the course of assessment as well as in all service provision.

3 - Needs

According to (HNO 2023) A higher percentage of female-headed households (71 per cent) reported food as an unmet need than households headed by men (58 per cent). This is followed by the need for NFI items (50 per cent) and livelihood opportunities (45 per cent). Another significant finding worth highlighting is that more than 3,000 households (nine per cent) reported disability specific needs as an unmet need.The need for non-food items (NFIs) (clothing, blankets, sanitation and hygiene items, fuel, and school supplies) is another top unmet need reported by 45 per cent of households compared with 21 per cent last year. While some NFIs are not available, access to NFIs is mainly impeded by lack of affordability of items, largely due to socio-economic circumstances. Al-Birr is planning to implement this intervention in high severity scale areas in Homs Governorate eastern and northern rural areas and homs city.According to its need assessment for households registered by Al-Birr during first half of 2022, Al-Birr Association has a large data for NFI need people, where 4,500 families need urgent intervention to secure heating materials and winter clothes before the onset of winter. Al-Birr has the experience of working in those areas especially since Al-Birr has several branches in both eastern and northern rural of Homs.
