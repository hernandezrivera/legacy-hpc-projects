Project Summary

Needs.
According to Rural Damascus Health Directorate: the population of the targeted locations of the project (Duma – Arbin) is 297,602 people including, 130,637 children under 18 years.
The targeted locations residents are mostly returnees and IDPs, suffering from low level of equitable and quality health care services resulting high level of health and psychosocial problems and chronic diseases and disability.
Moreover, the health actors have limited interventions in those locations.
The severity scale in targeted locations by WHO is /3/.
Mosaic has the proper experience and ability to reach to those people in need for the project services easily.
• The bad economic situation in the region.
• Lack of public services and satellite infrastructure in the region (availability of water - electricity - sanitation -.....)
• The birth rate in the region, the spread of premature sex, and the spread of gender-based violence (violence against women - violence against children - child labor...)
• Increase the unemployment rate
• Environmental pollution due to demolition, displacement, and demolished human structures...etc
• Increasing rates of new diseases (due to environmental pollution), lack of water (lice and scabies in abundance), nutritional problems (poor economic situation), especially among newborns, and chronic diseases.
• A relative increase in disabilities in the region: approximately 3% of the population, ranging from war wounded to congenital causes and chronic accidents.
Intervention.
• Provide medical consultation including medicine and referrals.
• Identify and refer children aged 0-59 months and PLWs malnourished cases.
• Provide MHPSS group sessions and individuals.
• Provide Health Education group sessions and individuals.
• Provide ANC services to pregnant women including referrals to virginal or cesarean deliveries.
Implementation of activities.
• Health and MHPSS services will be provided through one PHC and two MTs.
• The above package of the interventions will be conducted in two sub-districts that administratively belong to Rural Damascus governorate (Duma – Arbin) sub-districts.
• Total and breakdown of beneficiaries are provided below.
Protection Risk Analysis
The most important protection risks in the intervention areas:
- Inequalities in access to services provided
Exposure to physical violence and gender-based violence
- Exposure to assault or exploitation
- Hard to reach
Risk mitigation measures:
- Consultation sessions with residents on mechanisms to mitigate potential risks,
- Clear Reporting mechanism published in working sites including Hotline and Complaint box.
- Conducting awareness sessions on protection risks and mitigations
- Training project staff to acquire the proper knowledge of PSEA rules and regulations and its application with regular refreshments.
- Monitoring and Evaluation.
