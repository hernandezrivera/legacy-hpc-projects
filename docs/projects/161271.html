Project Summary

Sustainable Children Aid-SCA has been implementing GBV programming since 2016 funded by IOM-RRF and UNICEF and currently managing One(1) Women and Girls Friendly Space with funding from IsraAID.
Kajo – Keji County of Central Equatoria State have experienced multiple displacements and insecurity due to political rivaling within the SPLM and other factions. To Provide immediate lifesaving assistance to address the urgent protection needs, SCA will; 1. Train and support medical staff on clinical management of rape (CMR) and survivor centred approach, 2. Conduct Comprehensive case management to GBV survivors, other vulnerable children, persons and survivors. These activities are linked to protection cluster objective 1 and HRP 2020 Strategic Objective 1. They aim at addressing immediate lifesaving protection needs of the most vulnerable women, and girls in Kajo – Keji. By the end of the project, a total of 400 most vulnerable women, and girls especially GBV survivors, other vulnerable children, persons and survivors are expected to benefit from comprehensive GBV case management services. To Enhance prevention and preparedness programming to mitigate risks and strengthen resilience, SCA will; 3. Establish Women and Girls friendly spaces to enhance accessing of GBV services to women and girls, 4. Train front line service providers on GBV, referral pathways, and survivor centred approaches and psycho social first aid, 5. Establish community based protection networks to provide protection assistance and psycho social support to women, girls and vulnerable people affected by the conflict, 6. Conduct Safe referrals, including information dissemination on available services, women’s rights, 7. Conduct door-to-door outreach activities and awareness raising campaigns on GBV risks, referral pathway, and key GBV messages and other cross cutting issues, 8. Conduct regular safety audits and develop community based women’s safety committees, and strengthen participation of women groups in humanitarian and other community meetings, 9. Procure and distribute Dignity Kits including hygiene and protection items (reusable menstrual pads, underwear, multi purposed cloth, Torch and Soap), 10. Participate in protection needs assessments and Coordination of Protection meetings.
These activities are linked to protection cluster objective 2 and HRP 2020 Strategic Objective 2. They aim at preventing, mitigating, and responding to protection risks including GBV of the most vulnerable women, and girls in Kajo – Keji. By the end of the project, 20,000 women and girls are expected to benefit from GBV preventive and resilience building services and messaging. To Enable durable solutions for IDPs and other affected populations, SCA will; 11. conduct peacebuilding and peaceful co-existence activities including; building capacity of local women groups, women individuals, and women leaders on participation in meetings, mediation and peaceful resolution of conflicts, dispute resolutions, conflict mitigation and so on. 12. Engage the women, and girls on Income Generating Activities (IGA) and livelihood for protective purposes for persons of concern including GBV survivors, other vulnerable persons and survivors, and youth using Cash-Based interventions (CBI) for example embroidery of bed sheets and beads, bakery, distribution of Fuel Efficient Stoves (FES). These activities are linked to protection cluster objective 3 and HRP 2020 Strategic Objective 3. They aim at providing durable solutions to GBV survivors, other vulnerable persons and survivors, and youth and support at-risk communities to promote and sustain their resilience to recurrent shocks in Kajo – Keji. By the end of the project, 200 women with leadership and decision-making responsibilities in community-level peace and security initiatives and 300 GBV survivors, other vulnerable persons and survivors, and youth benefits from IGA through cash-based intervention (CBI).

1 - Needs

In Kajokeji, Central Equatoria state like other states affected by the conflicts in South Sudan, Rape and sexual violence among other GBV types have been perpetrated on women and girls during crisis yet opportunities to access basic life-saving services have been limited. The need for multi-sectoral humanitarian response to address the multiple needs of the population and scaling up of existing services to meet the growing demand of most vulnerable and conflict affected population therefore becomes eminent. (OCHA:Fact finding mission to Kajo-keji-August,2019).Sustainable Children Aid-SCA has been implementing GBV programming since 2016 funded by IOM-RRF and UNICEF and currently managing One(1) Women and Girls Friendly Space with funding from IsraAID. Kajo – Keji County of Central Equatoria State have experienced multiple displacements and insecurity due to political rivaling within the SPLM and other factions. (IRNA, June 2019). The IPC, August 2019, indicated that 182,012 affected people in South Sudan will require urgent protection needs with 29,996 of them are in Kajo – Keji county (OCHA, 2019). In Kajo – Keji, there are rampant cases of domestic violence due to lack of basic needs as they fail to manage stress (psychological problems), cases of rape against women and girls are common as well and the perpetrators are men (IRNA, Kajo Keji, June 2019). The above needs overview is severe and therefore require urgent humanitarian interventions including the GBV. The degree of protection risks and women vulnerability is high. With this project, Sustainable Children Aid (SCA) will directly support the Protection Cluster framework for the HRP 2020 – specifically the GBV Sub-Cluster by: providing immediate lifesaving assistance to address the urgent protection needs through comprehensive case management to GBV survivors, other vulnerable children, persons and survivors; enhancing prevention and preparedness programming to mitigate risks and strengthen resilience of women, girls, GBV survivors, youth through establishment of Women and girls friendly spaces, conducting psychosocial support services (PSS), Safe referrals, including information dissemination on available services, women’s rights, establishment of women’s safety committees, strengthening participation of women group in humanitarian meetings; enabling durable solutions for IDPs and other affected populations through peacebuilding and peaceful co-existence, Income Generating Activities (IGA) and livelihood for protective purposes for persons of concern including GBV survivors, other vulnerable persons and survivors, and youth using Cash-Based interventions (CBI).

6.6 - Will beneficiaries' rights be legally recognised?

Beneficiaries in this project will legally be recognized as they will have an opportunity through the feedback mechanism, to provide thoughts and ideas on how best services and assistance should be provided. SCA will be accountable to the beneficiaries as services provided will be done in a manner that does not cause any harm to the affected population as part of the DO NO HARM principal. Further, services/assistance provided will be culturally acceptable and affected people, irrespective of their gender or age, will be engaged in influencing humanitarian planning process more than just being passive recipient of aid.

6.5 - Includes perspectives of displacement-affected people?

The project will undertake needs assessment to further determine the perspective, needs and challenges of the affected population that will in turn be in cooperated into all phases of the project life cycle, from planning to monitoring, evaluation and reporting. The project will also ensure that planning and programming are relevant and responding to the needs of affected people, and processes and programming are gender and age responsive and there is proper monitoring, accountability and reporting on gender and age markers in accordance with IASC Global Gender commitments and framework

6.2 - Is the project linked to government/community systems?

Yes, the project will be linked to the community in Kajokei and the local government in the County. This is to ensure that the project falls within g the local framework in terms of collaboration with the relevant department and coordination among the actors within the county. For the community leaders who are the custodians of the project on behalf of the community members they will work hand in hand with SCA to ensure that they understand all the concepts and activities in the project so as to take over and sustain the project when SCA leaves the scene.

6.3 - Is the project aligned with national objectives?

The project is directly linked to achieving Protection Cluster objectives 1, 2 and 3 derived from South Sudan Humanitarian Response Plan 2020, a strategic planning policy document used to ensure an evidence-based response in line with a common strategy to minimize gaps and duplications in an effort to best meet humanitarian needs. The project will further be linked to South Sudan National development plans as well as Integrated Food Security Phase Classification (IPC) reports.
