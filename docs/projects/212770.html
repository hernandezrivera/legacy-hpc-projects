Project Summary

According to HNO 2024 by the concentration of IDPs has remained consistent over the last few years, with over half of all IDPs found in 10 of Syria’s 270 sub-districts. Almost half of all IDPs (3.4 million) live in north-west Syria, including 1.1 million in the sub-district of Dana (Idleb Governorate). There are almost 1.9 million IDPs living in over 1,525 last resort sites across north-west Syria, an increase from the 1.7 million and 1,421 sites recorded in 2022. The income-expenditure analysis shows that Syrian households are struggling to afford basic needs. September 2023 in north-west Syria household income fell 23 per cent short of covering the Survival Minimum Expenditure Basket (SMEB)
The project aims to target 6500 HHs in both Idlib and Aleppo governorates, aiming to ensure that 4500 of the most vulnerable HHs near markets receive emergency multipurpose cash (MPC) and are able to cover their most urgent needs immediately following a shock; this will be a timely response immediately following displacement caused by conflict, climate-related shock, or other types of natural or man-made disasters and will targeting 3000 HHs with one-off MPCA equal to 150 USD and 1500 HHs with three rounds MPCA equal to 150 USD per round. moreover, the project aims to ensure that 2000 HHs Most vulnerable HHs near markets receive repeated multipurpose cash (MPC) and are able to prioritize a variety of basic needs, reducing reliance on negative coping strategies over time, and this will be a longer-term frequency of MPC designed for the most vulnerable HHs, with the aim to reduce the reliance in negative coping strategies and

seeking a longer-term impact and sustainability and will target 2000 HHs with 8 rounds of MPCA; each round will be 100 USD it's worth mentioning that the number of rounds and transfer value designed based on the CWG recommendation, the modality of the transfer will be cash in hand through the financial service provider, taking into consideration the market assessment and monitoring will be maintained by Shafak through the intervention to avoid any harm and ensure the appropriateness of the intervention, Shafak will ensure reaching the different vulnerable groups following an inclusive and targeting approach and will avoid blanket distribution to ensure the effective utilization of resources to reach the most vulnerable HHs.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The project aims to target 6500 HHs in both Idlib and Aleppo governorates, aiming to ensure that 4500 of the most vulnerable HHs near markets receive emergency multipurpose cash (MPC) and are able to cover their most urgent needs immediately following a shock; this will be a timely response immediately following displacement caused by conflict, climate-related shock, or other types of natural or man-made disasters and will targeting 3000 HHs with one-off MPCA equal to 150 USD and 1500 HHs with three rounds MPCA equal to 150 USD per round. moreover, the project aims to ensure that 2000 HHs Most vulnerable HHs near markets receive repeated multipurpose cash (MPC) and are able to prioritize a variety of basic needs, reducing reliance on negative coping strategies over time, and this will be a longer-term frequency of MPC designed for the most vulnerable HHs, with the aim to reduce the reliance in negative coping strategies andseeking a longer-term impact and sustainability and will target 2000 HHs with 8 rounds of MPCA; each round will be 100 USD it's worth mentioning that the number of rounds and transfer value designed based on the CWG recommendation, the modality of the transfer will be cash in hand through the financial service provider, taking into consideration the market assessment and monitoring will be maintained by Shafak through the intervention to avoid any harm and ensure the appropriateness of the intervention, Shafak will ensure reaching the different vulnerable groups following an inclusive and targeting approach and will avoid blanket distribution to ensure the effective utilization of resources to reach the most vulnerable HHs.Shafak has extended experience and capacity to implement the cash-based intervention, including trained staff, in-place SOPs, and past experience; in addition to the access, community acceptance, and reputation on the ground, framework agreements with multiple financial service providers, Moreover has the ability to manage the CBI related risks including the Financial risks, Coordination risks, Diversion risks, Markets-related Risk, Feasibility Risk and this will be in line with Northwest Syria Cash and Voucher Assistance Risk and Mitigation Matrix. Furthermore, Shafak has PSEA and Child safeguarding policies and a dedicated focal point to ensure dealing with PSEA and CSG related risks and issues.Shafak MEAL team will conduct several activities such as the monitoring of all project phases, verification and PDM using the harmonized PDM tool.
