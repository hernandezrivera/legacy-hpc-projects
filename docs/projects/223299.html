1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

This Current Project, intend to accompany the IDPs in trauma healing through MHPSS ; Child Protection and provide training and support to the very poor through sustainable livelihoods development (vocational skills training and income generating projects).Jesuit Refu-gee Service (JRS) Mozambique’s work in the Maningane Settlement Area in Chiure District, Cabo Delgado province, primarily focuses on sustainable livelihoods , particularly women and children aged 3 to 17 among IDPs and host communities. After a successful rst phase (2023–2024), JRS is now consolidating its efforts to deepen impact in these areas, supported by holistic services that include education, child protection, and mental health. The target population is Internally CLOSEDisplaced Persons and Host Communities, with a greater effort to assist IDPs in percentages of 60% and 40% of the Host Community. For the Child Protection component, the target group is Bene ciaries under 18 years of age, of both sexes; For the Mental Health and Livelihood component, the target group includes both children and adults, with greater focus on girls and women at risk and/or survivors of GBV.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The Priority activity for the project are fundamentally: *Child Protection Through 1. Familiy and Tracing Reuni cation for Separated and unaccompanied Children; 2. Case Management,( Identi cation and registration of cases of children with different vulnerabilities such as GBV, Lack of identi cation documents, orphan, Neglect, Sexual abuse cases, exploitation and child labor, Kidnapping, CAAFAG, Etc) 3. Strengthening the Child Protection Community Committe; 4. training of foster parents; 5. Provision of reintegration KITS; 6. Provisio of dignity KITS; 7. establishment of reporting mechanisms; 8. Community awareness on Child Protection; 9. strengthening of reference Groups, through Training workshops and support with technical material; * Livelihood and empowerment on income generation and life Skills for girls and Women; Creation of saving Groups. * Mental Health and Psychosocial Support through Fixed and Mobile CFS. However, the de ned geographical area for action are in Cabo Delgado Province, Chiure District, communities of Maningane and Chiure Velho. but also the Organization aim to operated and expand intervention to other District as it is needed.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The Project propose to identify Potrncial Risks Through taking JRS internal Policies and Donor Policy, Apply the Internal conduct Code ( anti-harassment, anti-corruption, anti-fraud, anti-harassment policy) internal safeguard policy (PSEAH, SEA) and also application of the Mozambican labor law in force in the country.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

According to some researches in Mozambique, women and the elderly are one of the social groups most at risk and cyclically suffering abuse, therefore strategically the project intends to bring an interventional approach led by the community, particularly looking at the emancipation of girls and women, at the same time looking to create mechanisms for elderly participation in the so-called of decisions, and as an integral part for monitoring and evaluating project impacts and sustainability, for past present and future generations at the community level.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Through community awareness raising, consultations with groups and people with disabilities, peer groups, application of inclusive approaches in all areas and stages of the Project. and partnership work with Child protection partners, Government and community members as well, working with disability and inclusion approaches.
