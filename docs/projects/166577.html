Project Summary

The lockdown encourages households to safely stay in their homes and this may guarantee safety from COVID-19 but not from violence against women, girls and children. Trócaire and partners anticipate an increased in demand for shelter services and psychosocial support as some perpetrators may take advantage of the vulnerable groups. It is against this background that we will put in place measures to disseminate information on available GBV services as well as basic COVID-19 hygiene tips.
