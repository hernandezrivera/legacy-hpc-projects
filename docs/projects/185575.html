Project Summary

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
The on-going “Protracted Armed Conflict” in the North West/South West (NWSW) Regions of Cameroon has resulted in increased displacements of people/incooperated with COVID-19 pandemic.
OCHA Sitrep 5 Nov 2021: 2.2 M Affected people in NWSW; 1.6 M Targeted for assistance in NWSW; 573.9K IDP or 383.6K Returnees (former IDPs/refugees) in NWSW; 68.6K Cameroonian refugees in Nigeria. Sources: Humanitarian Needs Overview – 2021/Humanitarian Response Plan – 2021/Multi-sector needs assessment (MSNA) NWSW, OCHA - August 2021 MSNA in West/Littoral regions.

Affected population are in need of improved sanitation facility to schools, insufficient HWFs/learning materials, school fees, schools/communities sensitized on COVID-19 prevention.
DEMOGRAPHIC INFORMATION: 08 Subdivisions in rural, urban, semi-urban in Nkambe, Misaje, Ndu, Ako, Nwa, Batibo, Widikum/Bamenda II in 3 Divisions (Donga Mantung, Momo/Mezam) in the NW Region in Cameroon.
Targets Schools: 28 Schools in 16 communities in Donga Mantung: PS Ako; CBC Nwa; PS Ndu; (GPNS Njema, PS New Town, CBC, GPS Binshua, GSS Nyanyi, GPS Kungi, GBPNS/CS Binju) Nkambe; CS Misaji. Momo Division: GS Kuruku, GS Angei, GS Elum, GBS Diche I/GS Diche II)/Mezam Division: AMBAM Quality BNPS-Ntarinkon, CBC Musang, Joan of ARC NPS, MaLuc NPS, CS Small Mankon, CS Alakuma, PS Azire, CS Alamatu, Petina NPS Azire/GBS GMI Bamenda. Project target: 10,000(dissag. by gender, age, disability).
AIM:
To ‘improve access to learning environment with basic sanitation facilities, hgyiene services and learning material to vulnerable IDPs by 31st December 2022’
They needs an emergency humanitarian assistance for live-saving/alleviate suffering. IDPs/host communities have the right (to life, dignity, protection, security/to receive humanitarian assistance) on the basis of need without discrimination/avoid exposing them to further harm/leave no one behind/prevent them from PSEA.

Provision of these priority needs will address the physical/mental well-being/living standards of these IDPs through these ACTIVITIES;
-Community awareness of the prevention of COVID-19.
-Construction of school and training infrastructure (classrooms, latrines, water points, temporary learning spaces).
-Purchase & distribution of learning materials (bags, books, pens, pencils, etc.).
This lead to;
Schools/education services serve as a platform for accessing basic Water, Sanitation, hygiene/health promotion for crisis affected children/communities. Vulnerable children/teachers in schools/communities access basic teaching/learning materials for improved quality EIE by 29th February 2023. Reduce mortality by 75% /morbidity by 75% of people affected by crisis.
BENEFICIARIES:
Affected population (IDPs/host communities) of infants, girls, boys, women, men/PWDs of Gender, Age, Disability/Diversity mainstreaming i.e. 60% IDPs, 10% PWDs /30% Host Communities.
METHODOLOGY: Sensitisations.
STRATEGY OF IMPLEMENTATION:
Through awareness raising campaign (school-to-school/door-to-door sensitization)/radio talks, organised workshops, stakeholders, community’s mobilisation/engagement. Inclusively school children, IDPs/the communities are consulted/participate in the conception of the entire project cycle as decision-makers/local management. Rapid HH Survey/schools, community FGDs/report from community. MINEDU, MINSANT/Municipal Council. NB: Target group (3-18 years) for latrines/learning materials, sensitized on COVID-19 prevention (all gender, age, disability groups from 3>60years) in the 3 Divisions mention above.

SUSTAINABILITY:
Trained CPCs, Schools administration on how to treat/reuse squatting slabs once the pits are full. Installation of HWFs/preventing COVID-19 is medical needs for wider population. Ensuring education continuity with learning materials.
JUSTIFICATION:
Latrines reduce GBV cases that are latrines related. Marginalized/vulnerable groups of children are not assisted with learning materials/fees schools.
