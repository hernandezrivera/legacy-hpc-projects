Project Summary

As a result of the ongoing crisis in Syria for years, the population is still suffering from the consequences of this crisis, especially in the targeted area (Host community and displaced people in camps in Al-Hasakah Governorate). What has increased the suffering of the population currently in that region is the renewal of armed clashes between the parties present on the ground, and according to another Reports issued by the Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs expect an increase in the number of displaced people, and thus an increase in the suffering of the population, especially children.
In addition to the deterioration of the economic situation in general as a result of the decline in the value of the Syrian pound due to the continuous rise in exchange rates in the parallel market and the lack of sources of income for most families in that region as a result of the crisis.
This, alongside deepening rates of poverty, has strained the capacities of families and communities to protect children and adolescents. Child labor is frequently observed, as adolescent boys in particular seek to generate additional income in support of their families. Further, in such a resource constrained and insecure environment, girls face significant pressures to enter early into marriage.
internally displaced and returned children are particularly vulnerable to increased protection risks, including physical violence and sexual exploitation. Deteriorating living conditions, frequent exposure to violence and repeated displacement has profoundly impacted children and adolescents’ psychological well-being. There has been a significant increase in the proportion of girls and boys displaying signs of distress.
Millions of children and adults live in areas contaminated with ERW or IED’s putting their physical safety at immediate risks, and also hindering access to school and the delivery of essential services and humanitarian assistance.
Sham Al-Khair Foundation is one of the main partners of UNICEF, OCHA and the World Food Programme, through a number of projects in different geographical areas as well as in camps. We have employees in the organization
They have high experience in managing and implementing projects, and we have a specialized department with great experience in providing child protection services.
The project will be implemented in the following camps: Twaina (Washokani) + Al-Tala’i In Al-Hasakah Governorate
The following areas will also be targeted: Tal Brak - Jazaa - the western countryside of the city of Qamishli In Al-Hasakah Governorate
