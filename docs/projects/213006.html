Project Summary

After over a decade of conflict, repeated displacements, and economic decline, the humanitarian crisis in Northern Syria affects every sector, area, and population group. Needs remain high and widespread, with almost three-quarters of households reporting they were unable to secure basic necessities. people in Syria saw their situation worsen when a series of earthquakes hit northern Syria and Türkiye on 6 and 20 February 2023.
In 2024, 16.7M people are assessed to need humanitarian assistance across Syria, up from 15.3M in 2023. Of the 16.7M people in need, 5.5M are displaced, including over 2 million who live in last resort sites.
The erosion of basic service capacity has continued, in a context of barely any development investment.
The most common barriers to accessing livelihoods were lack of money (does not cover costs of living) in 87% of communities assessed, followed by lack of job opportunities (79%), and lack of job opportunities that match people's skills (44%).
The project aims to provide Multi-Purpose Cash Grants (MPGs) response for most vulnerable familise to help them cope with the harsh conditions and secure their needs in a dignified way, by distributing three rounds of cash grants with a total value of 150 USD per round per family . The project and the grant amount were designed to allow the beneficiaries to cover their needs of food, NFI and WASH since this cash modality will give them flexibility in choosing items they need and to give the project the ability to meet all changes in beneficiary needs.
GAN seek to target 3000 HHs (1500 (3750 male, 3750 female, 3378 under 18, 4122 above 18, 375 people with disability) of them in 2024 and the others in 2025), the beneficiaries will be selected in close coordination with the local councils and civil authorities with 100 % verification based on the vulnerability and eligibility criterias, the project will prioritize the women-headed households. IDPs as well as the households with children, disabilities, pregnant, and lactating women, GAN will use the
GAN could cover as much as can of the geographical areas in Aleppo, such as Afrin, Azaz that have overcrowded IDPs to reach 1500 vulnerable and newly HHs in urban, rural or even camps. Since GAN, through its ‘on the ground’ operation team and its prior experience in Idleb will be able to access the locations and markets during the project implementation.
Due to the lack of the banking system in northern Syria, GAN will use MTA agent who will be responsible for delivering the MPG's amount to the beneficiaries in the different locations.
MEAL team will be existed to observe the process and raise the beneficiary awareness to the complaint mechanism. - PDM to monitor receipt, use and impact of assistance provided Following every distribution round, GAN will be using the Harmonized PDM tool developed by the CWG.
Prior to the distribution, a rapid market assessment in terms of availability of the food items, prices, and accessibility will be conducted to make sure the local markets are functioning and to ensure all BNFs have good and equal accessibility to the different goods and services.
