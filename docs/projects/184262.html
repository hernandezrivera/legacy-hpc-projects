Project Summary

The project aims to provide the enabling capacity of the Logistics Cluster through its lead agency and strengthen the humanitarian community's logistics capacity to deliver timely, agile and reliable assistance. The project aims to facilitate operational coordination, generate decision support products through information management and, whenever resulting from a systemic gap, facilitate access to common service to key locations across Somalia as well as access to storage, sea and road transport services, where required.

1. - Projects activities

This project serves as the support capacity of the Logistics Cluster and it aims to provide logistics coordination, information management,and – whenever a systemic gap is identified - facilitate access to common logistics services (air, road, sea transport as well as storage) on behalf of the humanitarian community and in response to shocks. Based on its mandate and capacity WFP – as the cluster leader – intends to support the humanitarian community in addressing the projected demand for humanitarian assistance by providing logistics support and enabling required augmentation of logistics capacity whenever needed. Moreover, this Project aims to strengthen the logistics capacity of the humanitarian community and support the use of preparedness and planning in order to better allocate logistics resources and increase operational efficiency. In order to achieve these goals, the project is designed as follow: 1. Facilitation of access to common services (air, road, sea as well as storage) using available resources such as commercial contractors for humanitarian organizations responding to the needs of populations.2. Ensure of asset utilization is maximized by providing continuous updates and facilitating planning requests to service users, through the Logistics Cluster coordination cell.3. Assess gaps and redesign common services whenever the humanitarian situation changes to enable rapid response by users.4. Ensure organizations have access to coordination tools to ensure efficiency generation and synchronization of logistics activities.5. Support efforts for preparedness, planning and advocacy on behalf of the humanitarian community.6. Provide timely and relevant logistics information to the humanitarian community to ensure logistics decisions are informed.
