Project Summary

In this project, UOSSM aims to provide life-saving integrated nutrition services by supporting 13 Rapid Response Teams (RRTs) in Idleb (Dana and Ariha, Maaret Tamsrin, Salqin, Qourqeena, Badama,) sub-districts and Aleppo (Afrin sub-district) and Raqqa (Sululk subdistrict) 13 health facilities are located in the targeted areas and one RRT will be linked to each health facility, RRTs will target PIN Children under five (CU5) and caregivers, including pregnant and lactating women (PLWs) and people with disabilities. The proposed activities will be for 12 months during 2023, each health facility include OTP, daily target (OTP,RRT) is 75 beneficiaries(50 CU5,25 PLWs)so total beneficiaries is 75 daily beneficiaries*26working days*13 locations*12 months= 304200
within the project period. RRTs’ main activities will include:
• Strengthening early case finding and treatment for SAM cases without complications in case supplies are available from UNICEF and treat MAM cases in case we will receive supplies from WFP, in case we don’t receive supplies from UNICEF and \or WFP SAM, and \or MAM cases will be referred to other partners' health facilities in the same community,
• Providing screening, treatment, follow-up, and referring complicated malnutrition cases to the nearest hospitals to ensure the completion of the CMAM program cycle.
• Referring beneficiaries with dangerous signs to the health facilities for treatment.
• Providing emergency IYCF counseling for mothers with moderately malnourished children and micronutrient distribution for preventing anemia and malnutrition among children and PLWs.
• Through RRTs, providing integrated nutrition services with hygiene promotion.
• Screening and referring CU5 and PLWs with signs of abuse/violence to GBV case management and women and girls’ safe space (WGSS) centers to receive services according to their needs.
• FMA (Family MUAC approach) will be achieved through:
1. train mothers and household members on acute malnutrition using video and pictures and practical demonstrations on how to measure MUAC based on colored codes and check edema and training will be conducted at all points of contact with the mothers or caregivers. Community or OTP/TSFP site
2. During the training, the CHWs will counsel the mothers on optimal infant feeding and caring practices as well as the provision of MNS.
3. Distribution of MUAC tapes to mothers or caregivers after the training
4. Follow up the trained mothers to be sure that their measures are correct and treat SAM and MAM
According to UNICEF and Nutrition cluster, protocols, to enable RRTs to deliver integrated nutrition services with high-quality UOSSM aims to conduct IYCF & CMAM training during the first quarter and conduct GBV training for all members of RRTs' staff. On the other hand, UOSSM’s M&E team developed the monitoring plan so that the M&E team monitors the implementation of project activities in the field through field visits, conducting beneficiaries’ satisfaction, controlling services quality, following up the progress of activities, and achieving indicators on a monthly and a quarterly basis. Nutrition programmatic adjustments will be implemented in the context of COVID-19 to mitigate the risk of infection while ensuring the continuity of life-saving nutrition services. Furthermore, RRTs will be provided with PPE during the delivery of services.also deliver cholera awarness messages to beneficiaries
