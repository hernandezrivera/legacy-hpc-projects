Project Summary

Syria is one of the world’s most complex humanitarian emergencies laded with ongoing hostilities, triggering one of the worst displacement crises in modern history, resulting in widespread destruction of civilian infrastructure, including limited access to WASH
facilities leading to WASH insecurities. As reported by HNO 2024, over 2.4 million people are highly dependent on humanitarian
WASH assistance in Aleppo. Given the fact that most IDPs need support and are not in a position to sustain even simple hand washing without support it is imperative to have a sustainable intervention, which at a minimum will provide basic water facilities. Building upon the previous program, the knowledge and practice, we have planned integrated programs for 2024 as follows:
Water Station Support: During the past years, Big Heart was one of the organizations implementing WASH activities in Aleppo/Afrin. Big Heart identified the water stations by conducting RNA in Afrin and close coordination with the LCs and other organizations
working in that same locations, in addition to reviewing the information provided by ACU for the water stations in Afrin. Therefore, we have identified 9 Water Stations (Sheikh Alhadid, Karababa, Masikan, Tobal Mahmood, Afrin, Suran, Marae'e,
Al-Bab, Ar-Rae). While designing the programs, we focused on integration and sustainability. The plan is to solarize all 9 stations to reduce dependence of public power. This is a critical factor in sustainability as Cost Recovery Mechanisms are rudimentary and at time nonexistent making it difficult to operate the water stations.
Further complexing the situation is outdated equipment and major/minor repairs required to water infrastructure, including pumps and generators. The planned programming will address all needs, including installation of solar power, replace non-functioning pumps,
civil works to water reservoirs and rehabilitation of networks where required, thus reaching out to approximately 500,000 beneficiaries under 9 water stations.
Hygiene Promotion: The planned programming has integrated hygiene programs built into it. The programming will include provision of hygiene kits, awareness sessions and monitoring of hygiene practices at the community level.
Cost Recovery Mechanisms: All of our WASH programming has CRM as an integral part built into it. All possible economic factors have been taken into consideration while designing the CRM. A dedicated team is available to design and commission the CRM, enabling water stations to operate without external assistance.
Modality: The planned activities will be implemented without any sub partners and will be through direct contracting. The programming will also support the stipends for water stations staff and limited supply of fuel, while solar systems are being installed on test basis, and other material required for optimal operations of each water station. The target is to provide water at 40 ltr/person/day. We aim to achieve 90% satisfaction levels.
Data Collection and Monitoring: The monitoring will be through our well-defined M&E systems. Monitoring will be diligently executed, starting from Verifications all the way to Satisfaction Surveys. A robust system of lodging complaints is already in place through
communications tools such as WhatsApp, SMS, email, and via hard copy and drop in the complaints boxes.
