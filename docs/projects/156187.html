Project Summary

The main objective of Program is to provide access to a Child Friendly Space community center as referral point for under-18 years old with mental stress symptoms and their parents through establishing a quality safe space for children that promotes their access to the essential activities and life skills and raise the awareness of the targeted community. The activities will be provided to the dis-aggregated by group of children age and appropriate accordingly; art, cinema, music, reading club, theater club, computer language, robot and sport club. In addition, the center will contain psycho-social services which include Case management and follow-up, Group-PSS sessions and Individual PSS. Moreover, the parents of the children will be involved in the center by providing them awareness raising session, self-care activities, promoting healthy mind, body and mentality, developing life skills and relationships, marriage, healthy relation between the women and their husbands as we as PFA and parenting care. Furthermore, capacity building will be provided to the medical and educational staff in Bennsh sub district.
The direct beneficiaries during center access: UP to 4890 children divided to 4395 beneficiaries of child protection programmers and 495 children dropout of school,17 from MASRRAT trained on child protection issues .
The classification of age:
• Group A, 6-9 Years-old;
• Group B, 10-13 Years-old;
• Group C, 14-16 Years-old;
• And Caregivers (mothers).
• Gender: choosing children by gender must be balanced and suitable to the community customs.

Needs

Bennsh Community is a big community with large number of inhabitants and internally displaced persons and considered accessible for the organizations. According to the HNO (Humanitarian need overview) 2019 reported an assessment undertaken in Syria identified large proportion of children displaying signs of psychosocial distress. Children were deemed to be suffering from abnormal level on a wellbeing index designed to gauge levels of distress with no significant difference between IDP and host community student.The children suffering from fear, anxiety, sleep disturbance, loss of motivation, panic attack, aggression, stress, with draw, anger, mood swings.which indicate low level of wellbeing, acute or severe stress and high potential for post-traumatic stress.Bennsh sub-district is considered in a high need for child protection support as there was not an official support for children, there has been a few number of organizations operating in this location and providing educational services which is limited to access some of the actual needs. In the current conditions of instability in Idleb, MASRRAT is looking forward to implementing psychological and social recovery and unexploded ordnance (UXO) awareness campaigns, as well as providing vocational training to support stability for a wide range of young people in Idleb governorate. list of needs assessment sources:- Key finding of our project 2018- HNO (Humanitarian Needs Overview ).- HRP (Human resource planning ).

Protection Risk Analysis

Protection risk: sexual exploitation and abuse / child safeguarding Likelihood: Medium. Impact: High Mitigating measures:Masrrat has an absolute commitment to the safety and protection of all children, and preventing harm to beneficiaries. We recognise risks faced by beneficiaries and children, and the difficulties they face in reporting harm they experience by the organisation.We will not tolerate our staff, partners or associates to engage in or support any type of sexual exploitation and abuse or child abuse. The principle of ‘best interests’ will guide our actions in response to concerns and breaches of this policy.Masrrat understands that, in order for the PSEA/Child Safeguarding policy to be well understood and effectively implemented, it is essential that there are high levels of awareness regarding the policy and that staff and others are clear, confident and competent in putting the policy into practice.Disseminate and promote the policy widely. Copies of the policy will be made available and accessible to all stakeholders, both in hard copy and electronically.The policy will be distributed to partners and any other relevant parties beyond Masrrat to ensure that the policy commitments, principles and practices are publicised and that everyone is clear about Masrrat position in relation to PSEA and safeguarding children.Briefings and trainings will be incorporated into standard induction processes for all staff, and at the annual staff meetings/workshops. Those staff in direct contact with beneficiaries and children must have a full briefing on the policy, and how it applies to them and the work they are undertaking, prior to working directly with beneficiaries and children.Protection risk: Suitable locations for the delivery of activities project:Likelihood: Medium. Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk (1): Changing the security situation that interfere with implementation of the project.Likelihood of Risk Occurring: Medium.Effect on Expected Results: high.Risk Management Strategy/Mitigating Measure: We will establish a response plan that can be activated according to the situation. Designing the activities in a flexible way where we can change the way of conducting the activity according to the situation so as to achieve the wanted results. and we can focus on more suitable activities like doing PFA . Stopping the activities for short period. moving the center to another place. Risk (2): Security issues being sometimes unstable in the project’s areas such as faction armed fighting.Likelihood of Risk Occurring: Very high. Effect on Expected Results: Very High. Risk Management Strategy/Mitigating Measure: According to lessons learned from previous projects, it was found that while conflict occurred, access could be restored within a few days. A cautious approach that plans for these types of delays is expressed in the project work plan. Risk (3): There is no coordination between organizations, especially inside Syria, where most of organization opening the same activities in the same time: Likelihood of Risk Occurring: medium. Effect on expected Results: Very High. Risk Management Strategy/Mitigating Measure: MASRRAT would be conducting more meetings and coordinate with those organizations. Risk (4): changing in the attitudes or priorities of the population that will affect their willingness to benefit from the project activities.Likelihood of Risk Occurring: medium. Effect on expected Results: Very High. Risk Management Strategy/Mitigating Measure: MASRRAT will work on awareness raising throughout the project period and will get the feedback from the beneficiaries about the services and adapt accordingly.
