Project Summary

The project endeavors to address the compounded challenges resulting from more than a decade of conflict and a devastating EQ in Feb 2023, which inflicted widespread destruction upon homes and infrastructure in NW Syria.
Building upon these circumstances, Amal aims to address the critical circumstances through 2 interventions:
1) The 1st intervention: Establish small residential neighbourhood(s) to address the long-term needs for dignified shelter among affected families. This neighbourhood will prioritize the most vulnerable groups, including widows, orphans, or PWDs' families who struggle to secure adequate shelter. By providing protection from cold, floods, snow, summer heat, and the threat of sexual harassment, this initiative aims to ensure the safety and protection of these marginalized groups.
To address these concerns, Amal has devised a comprehensive plan to secure dignified shelter for 100 families in NW Syria, involving the following activities:
The construction of 100 shelters will include the following steps:
- HLP verification.
- Land Preparation.
- Sewage Network: SN8 polyethylene pipes will be installed with thorough inspections to ensure integrity and smooth wastewater flow.
- Dignified shelter Construction: Foundations, columns, walls, and roofs will be constructed according to approved plans. Internal space will be optimized for ventilation and lighting, with electrical and water lines installed. The final stages include plastering, painting, and tile installation.
- Service Units: Essential facilities such as schools, mosques, and gardens will be established within the neighborhood, centrally located for easy access by BNFs.
Furthermore, the project will provide Non-Food Items (NFI) to the selected families, offering immediate relief during the transition period. Beneficiaries will be chosen through a transparent selection process based on vulnerability criteria, ensuring assistance reaches those most in need.
Selected families will receive NFIs such as blankets, mattresses, and hygiene kits will be distributed based on BNFs' preferences and cultural considerations.
The distribution process will be carefully planned to ensure fairness, efficiency, and transparency. Distribution points will be established for beneficiaries to collect their packages, with robust registration and verification measures in place to prevent misuse. Beneficiary participation will be encouraged throughout, with feedback mechanisms in place to address concerns.
This comprehensive approach aims to alleviate financial burdens, enhance living conditions, and promote the recovery of earthquake-affected families, contributing to their overall well-being and resilience.
Amal Organization plans to establish dignified shelter in Suran and Jisr-Ash-Shughur, 100 vulnerable families (500 individuals) will be benefit from this component.
2) The 2nd intervention: Provide life-saving assistance through winter vouchers. The winterization vouchers contribute to meeting a Minimum Winter Needs for one family, The distribution of the winter vouchers will be through vouchers to be redeemed through the money-transfer agency for 180$ USD. The beneficiaries will be selected based on a set of vulnerability criteria with a focus on the newly IDPs women-headed households, elderly, orphans, pregnant and lactating women, and other hardship cases like those who are in dire need of Shelter. Amal Organization has a technical voucher and cash Standards Operation Procedures SOP in place to deal with cash and vouchers in such risky and sensitive context, and this SOP is in line with the cash working group guidelines as well as all the international NGOs who have Cash-Transferring Programs.
Amal Organization plans to implement this activity in Azaz, Afrin and Kafr Takhrim, 1,200 HHs (6,000 individuals) will be benefit from this component.
