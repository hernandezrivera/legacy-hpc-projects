Project Summary

This project aims at ensuring access for conflict-affected boys and girls to the safe, inclusive and enabling learning environment in 15 education facilities (schools and kindergartens). This will be achieved through conducting repair and rehabilitation of education facilities as well as distribution of equipment, incl. furniture, IT, etc.

1 - Needs

Children affected by the armed conflict in eastern Ukraine face persistent threats to their physical and mental well-being. Attacks on education facilities and continued disruption of schooling have a profound impact on the learning process of nearly 660 000 boys and girls. Education system in Ukraine has not been reformed throughout recent decades, mainly due to a lack of adequate funding. As a result, most of the education facilities in the region are insufficiently equipped and require rehabilitation of basic infrastructure. According to the latest available data provided by Education Cluster, some 84 percent of the education facilities in GCA of Donetska and Luhanska oblasts required some form of repair, including basic infrastructure, primarily to the roof (46 percent), windows (45 percent), WASH facilities (45 percent) and heating systems (40 percent). In addition, 81% of these facilities indicated need in some school equipment. Besides, according to the Needs Survey in Government Controlled Areas of Donetska and Luhanska oblasts, conducted by the Education Cluster in 2020, the conflict impacts teachers’ and school staff's wellbeing and their ability to work effectively which is recognized by school administrations in 58% of schools in rayons along the LoC. The situation deteriorated with the COVID-19 outbreak and full lockdown of the education system in both GCA and NGCA at the beginning of 2020. Limitations of using distance learning modalities left thousands of boys and girls on both sides of the contact line without possibilities to access education.
