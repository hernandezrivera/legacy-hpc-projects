Project Summary

The Proposed innovation “Sustainable Multipronged Cost Effective Solutions-SMCES” effectively solves the challenges being faced due pandemic (covid-19) and increase the enrolment, retention rate and contribute to improve quality education. The prudent action strategy of project will contribute to increase the enrolment rate and as well as improve the retention rate of the students which ultimately turned into quality education .
Multipronged Cost Effective Solutions-MCES, innovatively are to be put in place targeting manifold results. To address lacking enrolment, miserable low retention rate and lower learning outcomes, by providing understanding based quality education. In doing so, special focus will on girls education. As an action strategy, this project will be carried out in the four low ranked districts (Sialkot, Gujranwala, Multan & Lahore) of Punjab - where Bunyad- BLCC is already operational with good will in these area .Some of the MCES in mind, detailed ahead are reproduced here in,
• Well planed / designed capacity building of teacher leaders training programme on child friendly lines.
• Linking stakeholders (Teachers ,Students, Parents) with e-education solutions (TV, Radio, Mobile, Whatsaap, Education Websites etc)
• Customized/ simplified education based on Bloom Taxonomy and Outcome Based Education (OBE).
• Involve multi stakeholders for ownership and active support
• Scaling up and continuation of Bunyad “Mobile Rickshaw Library” activity
• Multifaceted Promotional UPE campaigns, linking with relevant departments
• Establishment of low margin schools introducing Bunyad Accelerated Education Program.
• Systematic School Education Improvement Planning.
• Capacity building of key stakeholders especially Education Department for PPP and like minded NGOs in the project area.
• A complementary high expertise core team will design and will manage the project all critical stages. (440 School Improvement Plans, 1200 primary school teacher trainings and 88000+6000 Beneficiary Students and Mothers 30 mother schools and 30 community base schools in each four districts. For sustainability, micro edu social entrepreneurship idea will also be promoted among many other initiative in mind to be discussed to project donor leadership.
• Social Mobilization - Social Dialogue and Consultation with stakeholders at upstream(district) and downstream(community) level will serve to spread the message against challenge of drop out and put in order the communities into purposely organized forums at Village, UCs, Tehsil and District level.
• Activating The Media-The project team will build relationship with local print, electronic and social media to highlight the issues concerning to out of schools OOSC and report on the interventions relating to children’s development and education initiatives .

At District level we will create a networking with Districts and Teshils governments and area notable and politician, NGOs operating in education, development practices and business entities of experience. One to two educational consultants are in mind to put on board particularly to improve the quality and to make the education process child friendly .Its is our well considers belief that if we are to improve quality aspect of education the most important stakeholder is the teacher, as students come to school happily and the parents sending with interest . Teacher training will be especially designed and customised according to bloom taxonomy and Outcome Based Education –OBE.At the head office a core team of two to three person with high expertise will allocated to the project in addition to the contribution that will given by selected persons at the head office . Catalyst facilitators and Mobilizers will be attached to the schools where interventions will be scale up. 2 - Project Indicators

By the end of the project there will be an educational model developed on proactive lines to serve as bench mark for quality education at primary level for replication towards mega goals set by SDGs 2030 for imparting education to all between age group to 5 to 16 , mandatory requirements of GOP ,25 A and section 37 of constitution of Pakistan . this model will focus on followings • 100% enrolment to target area with zero percent tolerance to dropout. • Quality education for improving learning outcomes in reading, writing, (comprehension and understanding based) and arithmetic and relationships . When end results will be compared with baseline data. • Develop 440 School Improvement Plan in each target district.• Project efforts will be inclusive to especially take care of girl’s education.• 8800 children to be enrolled and benifited from project interventions.• House hold profile to be prepared for in-depth of education situation • Established 120 Mother education schools to Provide basic literacy to 3000 illiterate mothers for reinforcing project goal by improving enrolment and retention. • Established 30 Community Based schools (where there is no schools ) covering 3000 out of schools children, where they will be prepared to mainstream in formal schools .• Out 10 Community Based schools, 5 will be sustained with support of community. • Communities of the target areas have been sensitised to improve the children enrolment and retention in targeted schools.• The learning achievements of the target age group will be enhanced up to the standards set by the education department• The learning achievements of the target age group will be enhanced up to the standards set by the education department
