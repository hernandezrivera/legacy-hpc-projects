Project Summary

a. Needs:
The ongoing conflict in Syria, now entering its fourteenth year, has caused immense destruction and unimaginable losses to the people of Syria and all aspects of their lives. Protection indicators have tragically declined due to the war and its consequences. Syrian women are among the most affected segments of society, facing a continuous state of violence, displacement, and limited access to basic rights. Thousands of women and girls have lost their lives, while others have suffered varying degrees of injuries. The battles and the February 2023 earthquake have caused temporary or permanent disabilities.
The suffering of women in Abul Thohur's region is exacerbated by the prolonged conflict, displacement of residents, and widespread destruction in all sectors and prevailing misconceptions in the local community. Women who have lost their husbands and providers have taken on economic, social, and educational responsibilities, becoming both "mother" and "father." Some girls are forced into early and forced marriages to sustain their livelihoods due to poverty, hunger, and deprivation in some families. Most girls and women suffer from ignorance and illiteracy due to the war and its consequences, leading to their unawareness of their rights and the absence of an active role within the family and society. Many of them become victims of domestic violence, marital abuse, and persecution under various pretexts. Women in Abul Thohur have shown resilience in the face of the harshness of war and are now in urgent need of support, compensation, empowerment of their rights, and status.
b. Intervention, Activities Implementation:
- By providing a mechanism for responding to gender-based violence against survivors of gender-based violence and women and girls at risk and enabling access to them.
- Training humanitarian actors in the field of gender-based violence from both males and females to enhance response to gender-based violence through a comprehensive approach focusing on victims/survivors.
- Empowerment activities for women and girls including building opportunities in life skills and employment.
- Establishing easily accessible safe spaces for women and girls.
- Conducting activities against gender-based violence to mitigate its risks.
c. Protection Risk Analysis:
Repetitive and periodic exposure of women to gender-based violence increases the risk of lacking safe spaces and unhealthy environments for families and communities, leading to physical harm and visible psychological effects that require long periods of treatment. Early marriage poses a significant risk of expanding ignorance and sometimes leads to an increase in divorce rates and causes serious physical conditions.
d. Strategy for Protection from Exploitation and Sexual Abuse:
Al-Birr and Social Services Association in Idleb adopts a code of conduct and protection policy that all employees and volunteers must adhere to, prohibiting any behavior or action indicating exploitation, harassment, or sexual abuse. The charity does not tolerate any behavior that may compromise the dignity or privacy of beneficiaries. The charity has assistance and protection offices that receive complaints according to confidential procedures that ensure the safety and security of complainants while providing training related to protection policies and procedures for individuals and beneficiaries as needed.
e. Risks, Assumptions, and Mitigation Measures:
Physical Harm: Case management - Psychosocial support services
Early Marriage: Awareness sessions
Exploitation and Harassment: Educational sessions on the risks of exploitation and harassment
Difficulty accessing mental health services: Community center offering multiple protection services against gender-based violence
Prevailing customs and traditions: Initiatives with training for community leaders
Marginalization of women: Support and empowerment courses for women and girls

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The project aims, alongside its activities in supporting and protecting women from gender-based violence, to distribute dignity kits to women and girls affected by crises and conflicts. The dignity kit includes (laundry detergent, soap, shampoo, sanitary pads, toothpaste, toothbrush, women's underwear, slippers, towels, sanitizing wipes, toilet paper, solar-powered flashlight, feminine hygiene products, health awareness guide). The dignity kit aims to help women and girls preserve and enhance their dignity.
