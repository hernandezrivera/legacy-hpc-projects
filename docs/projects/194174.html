Project Summary

The proposed interventions of WASH will contribute to reduce the morbidity and mortality through increased availability of potable water that meeting the standards, adequate hygiene and sanitation for 70,500 war affected people including ( IDPs, Returnees and host community) living in 8 localities in Central Darfur and South Kordofan, mainly in (Gharb Jabal Mara, Shemal Jabal Mara, Azum,Wadi-Salih and Zallengi in CDS and Abujubaiha, Alabassya, Rashad and Abukershola in SKS). The intervention will focus on construction, rehabilitation and upgrading of mini-water yards and rehabilitation of broken hand pumps, implementation of CLTS, vector control, massive hygiene awareness through awareness campaigns and home visits and distribution of hygiene kits. The intervention will capitalize on engaging and building the capacity of the targeted communities in various sanitation and hygiene, in addition to management and repair of water sources to ensure long terms sustainability. As well the intervention will strongly focus on streamlining the protection aspect and building resilience all over the design and the implementation phases, notably women, elders and children will be the main targets.

1 - Needs

The WASH assistance will response to the dire needs of the war affected population in Central Darfur and South Kordofan states, including IDPs, returnees and host communities. These two states are classified as the most affected areas in Sudan, due to the prolonged conflicts and communal clashes exacerbate with the continued floods and epidemics, that have resulted in severe needs to life saving services such as WASH. Based on NEF assessment and the humanitarian needs overview, the population living in the targeted locations have limited and below standards availability of water, very poor hygiene and sanitation services that contributed to severe rates of morbidity and mortality. The project will support improve access to potable water within a reasonable distance to the affected households, adequate hygiene and sanitation service that will contribute to improve the general health conditions and the wellbeing. Provision of water supply will reduce the frequency of water borne diseases cases, while it will enable the communities to improve personal hygiene practices in addition to the wellbeing. The designed sanitation and hygiene activities will help the communities improve awareness, practices and positive behavior change that reduces the morbidity and mortality rates.

2 - Activities/Outputs

The project will identify nonfunctional water points and will conduct rehabilitation of existing water yards and hand pumps, upgrading of hand pumps to mini-water yards as well drilling and construction of new mini-water yards in areas that lacking safe water sources. The project will work closely with the Water Corporation and the community plat forms, such as Water Sanitation Committees who will be trained in water sources management, Water safety plans and hand pump mechanic training to ensure the sustainability of the water points, furthermore, the project will organize general hygiene awareness campaigns using different mechanisms including media and market based approach to raise the hygiene awareness, as well implementing the CLTS approach to reduce open defecation practices, coupled with massive community engagement to raise their skills and knowledge. in addition to that the project will assist the community to conduct general clean up campaigns in the main public places, such as schools, water points and health facilities. The project will ensure massive community engagement process, where beneficiaries will be participating in all the project interventions to promote ownership. The project will use market based approach, through establishing mobile community water point repair who will be able to response to cases of water point repair needs, linked with community tariff systems to ensure sustainability. NEF will strongly seek streamlining the resilience along with the WASH interventions to ensure sustainability and the wellbeing of the targeted population, that will be done through the massive capacity building activities and linking for instance availability of water within reasonable distance and how the community member would use the credits time that they use to fetch water in other economic activities, as well the formation of community based water points repair teams linked to the tariff system, and this can leverage with establishment of small trades such as solar and hand pumps spare parts shops, promotion of local artisans on latrines design who can enrich the local technical labor needs. As well strengthen the Water Sanitation committees in a way that to create community based emergency response plat forms, that can be linked with basic services providers to response to emerging crises, such as floods and epedimics.

6 - Protection mainstreaming
6.1 - Does the project incorporate protection principles and promote meaningful access, safety and dignity for the beneficiaries?

4=Fully in place

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

The project design includes, updated curriculums that incorporate protection to be used across all the capacity building activities, as well within the water supply construction, all the designs of the water sources are updated to consider the protection aspect in terms of distance of the water source, easy access to the tap stands for those with special needs, elders and pregnant. As well the project will engage the communities at the early stage to identify and assess the vulnerabilities related to protection main concerns, so it can be mainstreamed in the project implementation.
