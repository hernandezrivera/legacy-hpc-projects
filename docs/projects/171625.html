Project Summary

The project is aimed at increasing ensuring utilization of quality lifesaving primary health care services including during emergency to reduce excess morbidity and mortality due diseases outbreak attributed to climatic shocks and common intermittent conflicts. The project is further aimed at improvement of physical and mental wellbeing and case management for gender based violence among crisis affected IDPs,returnees and vulnerable host communities (women, men, boys and girls) in Bay and Lower Shabelle Regions, Somalia through appropriate medical case management and referral services whenever applicable. The project will be implemented through the following activities: Provision of Essential PHC (EPHS) clinical care, Child health care, Emergency and trauma care, Communicable disease treatment/conducting immunization on common disease outbreak including EPI, NCD treatment, STD / HIV treatment, TB screening and treatment. The project will in addition ensure surveillance to support to early detection for timely response to disease outbreaks through Rapid response teams (RRTs), monitoring (EWAR) in addition HCW Training on trauma, MCM, case management, emergency management, provision of medical supplies including essential drugs. Furthermore, the project prioritize safety of Health Staff and patients from Covid-19 by provision of PPE, safety masks and regular hand-washing. To ensure comprehensive utilization of the health services, the project will ensure awareness at the health facilities in the project locations through mobilization and sensitization meetings and forums, Community STD / HIV treatment and prevention from GBV cases. The project targeted beneficiaries are 6592 Children under 5 years, 1518 children under one year, 7356 WCBA and 2518 Mothers.
The service modality: The project activities will be conducted by the project staff at the Health facilities/MCHs run MCH by Degsuf Foundation while outreach services will be provided by the the outreach teams.
The special population - people with disabilities will be reached and supported by the project since they will be consulted and their special needs included in the project right from the project design phase a well as throughout the implementation including project monitoring where any necessary adjustments to meet their needs will be done. Furthermore their views/opinions will be put into consideration during the project implementation.

Mitigation Measures

1. Risk that beneficiaries are exposed to violence during registration/distribution as a result of the distance to or location of humanitarian assistance and service: Mitigation Measure : Enhancing security at the project sites by hiring security staff as well as ensuring centrality of health delivery services to limit distance covered by vulnerable women and girls to mitigate dangers of GBV that do occur when they cover very long distance to access the services and end up getting to their homes under the cover of darkness2. Risk of women, girls and boys exposed to sexual violence, including sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA), when accessing humanitarian assistance. Mitigation Measure: Ensuring all staff sign PSEA code of conduct and adhering with it as well as conducting awareness on SEA and the reporting procedures that doesnt expose the victims to the perpetrators.3.
