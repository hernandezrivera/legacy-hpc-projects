Project Summary

ADA through this interventions in the stated localities (Gedo, Galmudug, Sool, Lower Juba and Lower Shabelle region of Somalia) will work towards Strengthen and improve access to essential lifesaving health services aimed at reducing avoidable morbidity and mortality, reduction of maternal and child morbidity and mortality among and strengthen emergency preparedness and response capacity at all levels in order to mitigate and respond to communicable disease outbreaks in an efficient, coordinated, and timely for crisis affected and host populations in Gedo, Galmudug, Sool, Lower Juba and Lower Shabelle region of Somalia. As a way of working towards provision of Integrated life-saving primary health care services aimed at reducing the burden of morbidity and mortality; We will improve access to health care systems by providing health interventions to the residence within the target population to the nearest proximity. Health care services through consultation and management of childhood illnesses with an aim f reducing maternal and neonatal mortality will be given focus. ANC and BEmONC with referrals of the CEmONC cervices will be provided to the pregnant mothers.
Staff and the community will be equipped with adequate information to manage emergencies and outbreaks that may arise from the dynamic natural and conflict related concerns. EDEWS reports shall be shared with the in line MOH and the cluster including proper coordination so as to respond to emergencies like AWD/cholera, measles, and some haemorrhagic fever outbreaks.
ADA staff will be equipped with trainings on ANC and BEmONC, IMCI, Emergency response preparedness and CHWs equipped with skilled on community management and targeting. Community dialogue will be key in adherence with the AAP framework.
The project staff per health facility will be comprising of 1 clinical officer, 2 qualified nurses, 2 mid-wives, 2 auxiliary nurses, 2 vaccinators and 2 Laboratory assistants, in the main central pharmacy we will have 1 pharmacy assistant with 2 separate store.
ADA will ensure that services are provided in safe and protective environment adhering to a do no harm standards. Mainstreaming of cross cutting issues like protection, accountability to the targeted population as well as avoidance of discrimination of the less fortunate or those with terminal illnesses. ADA will give equitable services to both the HIV and TB cases without biasness. Mental health services will be introduced to the health facilities with none existing services but for complicated cases we shall refer for comprehensive support.
ADA will therefore ensure equitable service delivery is offered to the beneficiaries within their reach to reduce on the morbidity and mortality rates in the area.
Proper coordination with partners, the respective focal point representatives as well as the zonal and the national team will be adhered to avoid overlap.

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

The target population is at risk of displacement that may pose human rights violation. The Protection Risk Analysis highlights the extremely difficult situation in IDP settlements and collective centers across the country, in light of growing displacement, due to drought and conflict and continuing protracted and secondary/multiple displacement situations (including the particular challenges facing refugee returnees who get displaced again). The IDPs in these displacement locations frequently live in undignified and hazardous circumstances, where they face multiple protection risks/threats such as, unlawful evictions, overcrowded and unsanitary environments with limited access to basic services, exposure to explosive hazards, increased risk of Gender Based Violence (GBV), negative coping mechanisms such as child marriage and child labor, and tension with the host community. Children and young people (mainly male) in displaced situation, particularly those from Al-Shabaab controlled areas face risks of arbitrary arrest once out of those controlled areas, and therefore live in fear and sometimes isolation. Lastly, IDPs continue to struggle to end their displacement and pursue voluntary, informed, safe, and dignified durable solutions based on their individual and household needs. By utilizing the reach, capacity, and weight of the whole humanitarian system, the safety, dignity, and well-being of these displacement affected populations (including the heavily burdened host communities) can be strengthened.

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

Somalia faces challenges when it comes to gender equality due to the dynamics surrounding the cultural aspects, not leaving aside the age exclusion when it comes to activities prioritization. In this case ADA has gender policy that ensure that when hiring staff that women are given 50% consideration in different positions. In addition, the project target populations are segregated into men, women, boys and girls hence giving emphasis to all gender groups in the community. Gender considerations will be taken into account throughout the assessment, planning and design of the interventions and significant consideration will be given to pregnant women, mothers, and women of child-bearing age. In the provision of health and nutrition promotion campaigns, messaging will be targeted for specific gender and age groups and as appropriate the events will be organized separately. Equitable and gender-sensitive participation and decision-making approaches will be adopted throughout the project, including women’s participation in the community nutrition committees and access to gender-specific health and nutrition information. Community consultations through focus-group discussions will take place during the project implementation, and stakeholders will be involved in decision-making processes to tailor the project to the needs of different gender and age groups. The target population according to the needs raised is aggregated to both sex. During the health service delivery health concerns and health code of ethics shall be adhered to where every boys and girls will be given equal rights to health care services.

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

Volatile risks in Somalia context with service delivery has been very fragile. ADA will focus of the humanitarian response and provision of protection services has been accessible thus forcing IDPs to move to find assistance or in drought impacted communities in accessible, less conflict prone areas. Areas with active conflict, will be benefiting from all measure of the health humanitarian response, have seen minimal protection engagement through the efforts of some highly motivated individual at the community. ADA will engage further in conflict affected areas through various actions including but not limited to engaging the various gatekeepers and the local authorities and the IMAMs.
