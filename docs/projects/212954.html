Project Summary

In the prolonged conflict that has engulfed Syria for a long period, the repercussions extend beyond the direct challenges of violence and displacement. The ongoing war has led to countless issues, with the disintegration of infrastructure and population dispersal being a major concern. Among the various consequences, the impact on public hygiene awareness has become alarmingly evident.
The consequences are particularly severe in NES, according to estimates from the HNO for Al-Hasakah Governorate, which indicate a significant number of people in urgent need of assistance. Al-Hasakah Governorate emerges as a pivotal and worrisome focal point.
In recent months, external attacks have further exacerbated the already deteriorating situation. The destruction of essential infrastructure not only results in the disruption of basic services but also leads to the deterioration of healthcare systems and waste management. This is one of the main reasons contributing to the emergence of the problem of personal and public hygiene. Additionally, other factors have worsened the issue, such as the scarcity of water resources in the region. Furthermore, the prevailing insecurity, as a result of the prolonged conflict, forces people to prioritize survival, making hygiene a secondary concern. Moreover, the absence of clear guidance exacerbates the situation, as many individuals fail to understand the importance of personal hygiene and its positive impact on public health. With financial constraints hindering access to basic needs, individuals are forced to overlook hygiene issues, further exacerbating the prevailing challenges faced by families, adolescent girls, and women. Consequently, they become vulnerable to diseases and the accumulation of environmental chaos.
A recent report by the organization Reach sheds light on the severity of the situation in Al-Hasakah Governorate in winter 2024 and reveals statistics that confirm the urgent need to address the hygiene crisis. Here are the translated statistics mentioned in the report:
- The percentage of lack of awareness of personal hygiene among the population of Al-Hasakah Governorate reaches 58%.
- The spread of communicable diseases attributed to the lack of effective personal hygiene practices is alarmingly high, reaching 26%.
- The cost of personal hygiene materials has increased by 55% before the start of the conflict, exacerbating the challenges faced by the population.
According to the gloomy estimates from the Overview of Needs in Syria, 49,542 families in the city of Al-Hasakah are in need of assistance, indicating the urgent need to increase awareness of hygiene practices.
Against this backdrop, civil society organizations are striving to address the multifaceted challenges facing communities. Recognizing the urgent needs in Al-Hasakah Governorate, Al-Khaboor Association has designed its activities in line with the 2024 Humanitarian Response Plan. The association aims to provide essential personal hygiene supplies to women, while promoting a sense of dignity and well-being through awareness sessions and the development of a community that prioritizes basic hygiene principles.
Intervention Logic:
Activity 1: Distribution of hygiene kits and awareness sessions on personal and public hygiene for families in Al-Hasakah city.
This activity aims to develop a community that prioritizes basic hygiene principles and integrates them into their daily routines. The association will provide awareness sessions for 2,000 direct beneficiaries in Al-Hasakah city,
The association will also distribute household hygiene kits to them. The hygiene kit will include essential items such as laundry detergent, broom and dustpan, mop and bucket, liquid soap, toothpaste and toothbrush, shampoo and conditioner, garbage bags, hand sanitizer, disinfectant solution, paper towels, gloves, insect repellents, and air fresheners.
