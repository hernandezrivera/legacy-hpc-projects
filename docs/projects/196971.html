1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The aim of the project is to provide WASH assistance for IDPs and native population of the host communities in Pemba and Mecufi districts. It forsees the implementation/rehabilitation of water and sanitation facilities with specific focus on gender and disabilities, sensitization campaigns about water-related diseases and strengthening the partners’ coordination for a better implementation of the WASH actions in both districts.Mecufi DistrictTarget beneficiaries: 2,500 (60% women, 40% men)

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

A1-WASH sector coordination established in Mecufi: coordination in the WASH sector among all partners operating in the areas will be necessary. Sectoral meetings will be held in the two district, involving local authorities and local/international partners that work in the area. A database will be realized and updated weekly. WW-GVC will lead the process and share data with the partners.A2-Access to safe water of appropriate quantity and quality is guaranteed to 2500 IDP in Mecufi districts: in partnership with the district authorities (administrations, SDPI and SDMAS), an evaluation of the updated necessities will be elaborate and the host communities where to construct/rehabilitate wells will be selected. The process will be lead by WW-GVC’s civil engineer. A3-Access to appropriate sanitation and hygiene facilities is provided to 2500 IDP in Mecufi districts: in partnership with local authorities, an evaluation of the necessities will be put in place in order to select the host communities where to build VIP latrines integrated with MHM and facilitate access for people with disabilities. A4-Dissemination of information on safe water, sanitation and hygiene and NFI to prevent spread of WASH related diseases in Mecufi districts: sensitization campaigns in the host communities and district schools will be implemented through communication material (flayers, graffiti (“paredes falantes”)) and artistic movements (dances, theatre, games for kids).

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

All the activities will include gender consideration approach, in particular: the coordination meetings (A1) will involve WASH and gender experts of all the partners; the access to safe water and sanitation and hygiene facilities (A2-3) will take into account all the orientations coming from Provincial WASH, Protection and Education Clusters and most of all from the Gender Working Group (in which WW-GVC constantly participate through its gender specialist). Finally, the sensitization campaigns (A4) will consider specific information to be addressed to girls and women. The constructions (wells and latrines) will be build in safe areas to facilitated the access to women and girls in order to limit the risk of harassment and sexual attacks. In particular, latrines will be distinguished in different blocks from men and women and will be insert MHM. On the other hand, wells will be build in areas near the communities so women and girls will not have to walk a lot of hours, also after the sunset. Finally, all the material that will be distributed (hygiene kits, sensitization material, etc.) will take into account the gender approach and will be selected in collaboration with the different Provincial Clusters.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

WW-GVC is already present in the target district so it was facilitated in the design of the project, already knowing the basic necessities of the population. Moreover, the organization is in constant relation with the local authorities (WW-GVC leads the Mecufi WASH Cluster) allowing to have an update vision of the population needs. This relationship allows WW-GVC to have official data and information about the number of IDPs, host families and host communities, following the ongoing changes that, due to the situation, are very common and most of the times cause disrupted knowledge of the reality. All the data is collected distinguishing information about sex, age, status (IDP or native), origins and actual location of each project’s beneficiary.The areas where the wells and latrines will be built will be selected and agreed in collaboration with the local authorities as well as with the target communities. The project’s staff will visit these areas at least three times at week to guarantee the right execution of the constructions. WW-GVC will put in place the Green Line’s service, adequately advertised with visibility and communication material. Complain boxes will also be present each time WW-GVC’s staff will be in the field.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The first stage of the project will the identification of population’s updated needs as well the final definition of beneficiaries. This will include the distinction of IDPs and native population discriminated by age, sex and disability. It is common knowledge that many times people with disabilities are hided by their families or communities. At the beginning of the project, WW-GVC will put in place both an arising campaign about the project and a broad data collection that will include all its beneficiaries.For what concerns the hygiene facilities, both the water points and the latrines will be built in order to be accessible to people with disabilities, with slopes and structures with enough space for the movement of wheelchairs. Latrines will be equipped with grab-bars and larger doors that will consent its simple use. All the communication and visibility material used for the sensitization campaigns will be developed including specific focus for people with different kind of disabilities.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

For the building of hygiene facilities, contractors will have to take a geophysical study in order to certificate the right position of the water points as well as of the latrines, avoiding the contact between the water table and contaminated water. The wells will be provided of discharge channels in order to avoid water stagnation and the development of water-related diseases. Latrines will be build using local material and taking attention to the particular soil. As a matter of fact, sandy soils will need a more specific technique to avoid them to sink. In general, all the actions will have a minimum impact on the environment and everything will be done to avoid it. The process will be lead by the WW-GVC’s civil engineer.
