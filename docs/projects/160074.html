Project Summary

Since Somalia doesn’t utilize rainwater by harvesting means including durable dams, eﬃcient subtraction of underground water, water scarcity persists in large parts of the country. Deteriorating Sanitation conditions of displaced people signals the importance of a collective efforts to end open defecation and maintain existing latrines through comprehensive hygiene and sanitation promotion. The ongoing conﬂicts coupled with natural disasters continue to reduce the resilience of communities, trigger displacement and impede civilians’ access to basic and humanitarians’ access to those in need. Resilience of pastoral communes is getting weaker as well as agricultural communities due to lack of comprehensive support that enables them to move forward. Communities in South Central Somalia has been a host for disasters including that of water scarcity caused by recurrent droughts. However, Zamzam Foundation has embarked on a strategy that contributes to the wellbeing of affected communities who are in need through provision of suﬃcient and clean water and provision of healthy sanitation facilities and raising and promotion of communities' awareness to hazards of misuse of the sanitation facilities and negligence of good hygiene practices to deter and prevent diseases. However, ZZF will remain accountable to the beneficiaries, local authorities and respective donors on the quality and quantity of the humanitarian intervention being implemented for the benefit of the target communities. Accountability mechanism will be enforced during project implementation.

Project Activities

The project is tailored to the WASH assistance in a way that alleviate the suffering of children of girls and boys being sent to harvest water for their families. This project eases access of water to the community so that girls, ladies will not be jeopardized to harassment and protection risks. The following are activities the project will deliver:1. Provision of durable clean water in highly needed communities by drilling 3 Strategic boreholes in highly water stressed areas in South-Central Somalia. 2. Construction of 15 hand pump wells for affected communities in low water table areas in Somalia. 4. Rehabilitation of strategic boreholes in Hiiraan, Middle Shabelle, Lower shabebelle, Galgadud and Bay & Bakool regions in South and Central Somalia to minimize risks, that might jeopardize protection of women and children associated with emergency breaks for the boreholes’ accessories eg. generators, pumps, pipes etc. 5. Improvement of sanitation and hygiene status of displaced and stressed communities in Somalia by provision of sanitation facilities (318 flood proof latrines) and by promotion hygiene of affected and host communities in emergency and post emergency times and provision of hygiene sanitation kits 500 HH in Somalia. 6. Hygiene promotion activities at schools and health centres in Somalia using modern and friendly approaches. 7. Chlorination and disinfection of communal water sources, health centres in emergence context.
