Project Summary

ADRA has been working in Sudan for over four decades, working in providing both direct relief and development long-term projects. Currently ADRA Sudan is present in 5 out of the 18 states in Sudan; Khartoum, White Nile, Blue Nile, West Darfur, and Central Darfur. ADRA In WDS, had implemented about 10 WASH project through DANIDA fund Targeting 60,000 beneficiaries in Geniena , Krienick , Jabal Localities, in WDS. ADRA had rehabilitated 12 Hafirs and drilled and rehabilitated more than 120 HPs, benefiting and serving about 150,000 IDP and host communities. In BNS ADRA was one of the first responders when the civil conflict broke out in 2010. Through its work with communities, ADRA was successful in reaching remote and war-torn villages in BNS and providing lifesaving assistance.
For this submitted project ADRA Sudan will target WAR affected populations , The IDP and returnees in the three targeted states WDS, CDS, and BNS. The project will target to fulfill the needs and gaps in shelter , NFI, and WASH for the IDPs and Returnees. for shelter, NFI, the project will provide shelter materials and NFI which mainly local materials, wood, Bamboos and reed matts, and Trapline. plus the project will also address the WASH services needs like rehabilitation of the non working Hand pumps, and will also drilling new bore holes with Hand Pumps, will also target to improve the water yards to include operating with solar than using diesel. also the project will enhance the sanitation and hygiene condition for the targeted groups by coordinating with the local partners and the Government line ministries to adopt CATS program for newly IDPs and returnees groups, and encourage CLTS programs for old groups of returnees and IDP. The project also focus on to construct institutional latrines in the targeted areas in the three states in schools, health centers and markets. Moreover the project will target the youth groups male and female to train a team of hygiene promoters, to enhance good hygiene practices through hygiene promotion for IDPs and returnees people.
The project will target localities with overburden of IDP and returnees and which are expected to receive more number of IDPs and returnees. in WDS the project will work in Algeniena and krenick and Jabal Moon localities, in CDS the project will target Azoom locality, in BNS the project will work in the SPLMN areas and localities like Kurmuck and Bao , in areas , Yaboos in Kurmuk locality, and Olu in Bao, these area will be targeted because of high needs in shelter NFI and WASH services. The shelter and NFI, will be provided to the newly returnees from the war affected areas, while WASH service like rehabilitation of water supply devices (15 Hand Pumps , 4water yards ) will be rehabilitated for all groups of IDPs and returnees in all of targeted areas the three states WDS, CDS, BNS.
ADRA’s WASH assessment found many water sources in WDS CDS and BNS were damaged as a result of conflict, affecting approximately 20,000 households, over50% of which are IDPs. Field observations show that often, WASH infrastructure—especially hand pumps—is destroyed as a result of intercommunal conflict.
WASH and Protection. Women and girls have limited options to access latrines, either going to a distant neighbor or using open fields with no privacy. With rapid inflation and soaring food prices, parents may send their children to work, exposing them to unsafe migration, trafficking, and sexual exploitation. Female IDPs still face high protection risks and harassment, and are occasionally killed or raped; between December 2017 and January 2019, UNAMID documented 192 victims of gender-based violence (GBV). Women and girls remain the primary collectors of water and sometimes must travel more than two kilometers each way for water collection—over four times the Sphere standard of 500 meters. The impacts of GBV are exacerbated and prolonged due to a lack of quality response services and reporting mechanisms.

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

While providing WASH services and facilities, ADRA Sudan will consider girls' and women's security and safety. Their engagement will be critical while identifying water points and new water sources to save women. Gender Analysis and protection Mainstreaming.the conflict WDS , CDS BNS. has had an adverse impact on the majority of communities and tribes however, the effects have been different for women, men, boys, and girls. Women and children make up 60% of the displaced population in Sudan; women and girls represent 25% and 30% of the population, respectively. In addition, Sudan ranks 139 of 155 in the UN Gender Inequality Index, and the culture in BNS WDS, CDS is more severely patriarchal than the rest of the country. Women and girls are targeted for GBV and other forms of abuse— while fetching water or collecting fire wood for cooking. Displaced women and children are the most vulnerable and at the greatest risk of being exposed to violence and GBV during their movements to and from markets, for water and wood collection, and for farming activities. Water collection is done by girls, women, and young boys, from ages ranging from as young as six up to 60 years. Due to restrictions on access to services and to movement, many household members are opting to move at night when they can.Though older women have a heightened ability to engage in community leadership, women face the following cultural or socioeconomic barriers to community participation and leadership:• Women engage in farming and herding, but the income goes to men.• In public gatherings, women and men sit in separate areas, with women sitting with their backs toward men.• Women are usually not allowed to complain or air their concerns in public.• Women are not allowed to move outside the home without permission from men.• Additionally, domestic violence and rape are unfortunately common, but cannot be openly discussed and are rarely reported, causing increased psychological trauma.ADRA Sudan will promotes female participation at all stages of the project cycle. Both male and female community members will be consulted during the design of the project through focus groups as well as needs assessments. ADRA Sudan uses its knowledge of the context in the project area, as well as consultations with local staff, in the design of gender-sensitive and tailored approaches. Equal female and male representation in Water User Committees (WUC) . Females will be encouraged to openly voice their needs and concerns through regular segregated meetings facilitated by the female Water User Committee members. Since females—especially young girls—are the main water collectors, they will be consulted prior to the rehabilitation of water taps. During distributions, separate queues for women will be established, and females will be consulted on the most appropriate time and place for distributions. Tailored awareness methods will aim to be inclusive of different segments in the community.

7.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will inform and interact with inter-agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including SEA and abuse complaints?

ADRA Sudan will ensure coordination with the WASH Sector at both the federal and state levels. ADRA WASH Technical Specialist represents ADRA Sudan in sector meetings at the national meetings, while the Project Manager/WASH Coordinator attends the WASH Sector meetings at the field level. All project activities and targeting were developed based on the gaps identified by the sector through sector reports and meetings. Prior to the project conception, ADRA conducted a joint meeting with OCHA, HAC (Humanitarian Aid Commission of Sudan), SPLA-N, WES (Rural Water Authority & Drinking Water Authority), and UNICEF at the field level, whereby the activities and villages where identified. During implementation, ADRA will ensure regular reporting to the WASH Sector. ADRA will coordinate with different national and international partners in the area, such as World Vision International (WVI), International Organization for Migration (IOM), and ADD (Addition for Disasters Assistance and Development).As WASH is closely tied to Health & Nutrition, ADRA will collaborate with International Medical Corps (IMC) and RI Blue Nile to engage community nutrition/health volunteers and Infant and Young Child Feeding (IYCF) groups in assisting in both the design and the dissemination of hygiene information. Health stickers and posters will also be presented to PLW when they visit the health facility, as they are one of the high-risk groups.In this time of the second COVID 19 wave , ADRA will take in consideration all Covid 19 measurement getting feed back from the affected people IDP and returnees in the project targeted areas. also while collecting the SEA and abuse complain which will be preserved as top secret. because it harm the dignity of the beneficiary if unfolded by Adra staff or by health worker.
