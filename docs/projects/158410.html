Project Summary

The focus of the project will be on individuals and Households (HHs) returning home and/or individuals and HHs being relocated to help with the transition of individuals in a protected and inclusive manner and to ensure protection structures exist and are strengthened for both the host communities and those being relocated. Activities will focus on 3 of the 5 Child Protection (CP) minimum response package interventions for Mozambique as defined by UNICEF and supported by the protection cluster: (Protective communities, Awareness raising, and psycho social support).
Goal: To strengthen child protection mechanisms and accountability systems for children and their families impacted by the cyclone.
Objective 1. Community based protection mechanisms strengthened
Outcome: Child Protection Committees strengthen
Activities:
• Carry out a rapid assessment and mapping exercise to understand the level of capacity of the existing host community child protection committees
• Training of child protection committees and community and religious leaders on basic case management, alternative care and child protection community mobilisation
• Remobilize and train the existing child protection committees.
• Train relocation site child protection committees and new households entering the community
Outcome2: Community structures for child protection strengthened
Activities:
• Case management (Referrals of children to specialized services, including MHPSS and health services) and direct support to children at risk not eligible under UNICEF project priority criteria
• Child protection trainings for parents, teachers, community and religious leaders
• Training of services providers (teachers, health workers, INGC) on child protection and referrals pathways
• Conduct Awareness Raising to reach out to the wider community.
Objective 2: To strengthen the Community system on psychosocial support and care for children and their parents/caregivers including people with specific needs and accountability systems.
Outcome: Psychosocial Support services strengthened
Activities:
• Training of community structures and stakeholders on psychosocial helping skills
• Train the community on referral pathways in order to access the psychosocial support services
• Conducting recreational activities, Psychosocial and life skills including peer support sessions to adolescents on self-protection, hygiene and sexual reproductive health
• Organize peer to peer support sessions in schools
• Psychosocial referral network for adolescent girls - alarm systems and lighting
• Conduct Foster care training and awareness session on child protection, child rights, parenting and PSS training for foster care
Outcome 2: Accountability systems strengthened at community level.
Activities:
• Dissemination of safe guarding policy and child protection code of conduct to communities and children
• Rapid protection monitoring, including those with disabilities and the elderly
• Set up the Complaint and Response Mechanisms and training
• Train staff and volunteers on Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse
