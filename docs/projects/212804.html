Project Summary

Objectives/ goal:
To reduce morbidity and mortality rates as well as mental conditions among the most affected population in Hama Rural, and build community-based health capacity in the targeted areas.
Overall health situation in the country
The health situation in Syria is not much different from the other life sector situation, as the Syrian health system has deteriorated, like the rest of the government sectors, as a result of the war and repeated attacks on medical facilities and health workers. The fragile health system is also experiencing additional stress as a result of continues outbreaks from COVID-19 to Cholera followed by the repercussions of earthquake and aftershocks.
The weakness of the health system, the lack of medicines & medical supplies and the migration of health workforce especially doctors have had a prominent role in the deterioration of the health situation in Syria. The infrastructure of basic health services such as hospitals and health centers are in poor condition, and requires intensive maintenance, and some of them need rehabilitation to provide the minimum level of service provision, where at least 14.6 million people need health assistance.
Across the affected communities in Syria, there are reported shortages of services at the primary and secondary health care level due to increased load and backlog of surgeries, shortage of health human resources, overuse/disruption of medical equipment, and shortage of medical supplies.
Based on the need analysis, AKHS-S aims to provide MHPSS, Chronic Diseases and Childcare services to respond to the urgent needs of the targeted affected population through supporting the following interventions:
I. Primary Health care:
Under this project, AKHS-S is going to strengthen the delivery of NCD primary health care services via boosting the functionality of the following health facilities:
o Establishing 7 medical mobile teams (MMTs), each team composed of: 1 Team leader, 1 Internist (Trained on mhGAP) , 1 Pediatrician, 2 Nurses, 1 PSS, 1 Community Health worker and 1 data-entry assistant.
200 communities will be targeted (20 monthly field visit for each team) as following:
This medical teams will provide about 40,320 outpatient consultations, 3,360 MH consultations, and 80% of cases will be included in the referral system when needed. (will be explained later in the summary).
Also, the mobile medical teams (MMTs) will conduct home visit for elderly and PWDs who are unable to access the services in the provision site of MMTs.
7 First Line Supporters PSS will provide MHPSS services inclusive of group sessions, raising awareness on MHPSS topics, Family counseling, Individual Services, individual psychological counselling sessions.
II. Secondary care:
Patients who require advanced diagnosis investigation or surgical intervention under secondary care will be supported on referral basis. Beside that, MH cases who need Behavioral and medical therapy will be also included in the referral system.
The network of contracted medical service providers may include the Aga Khan Medical Center in Salamiyeh, the Syrian Medical Association M.R.I center in Hama, and some private hospitals in necessary cases.
III. Community building and developing capacity:
 Training heath workers on mental health topics including mhGAP, Problem Management Plus (PM+) and Psychological First Aid (PFA).
 Training heath workers on infection prevention and control (IPC).
 Training community health volunteers on different health topics including.
The goal of forming trained community health volunteers is for:
a) Following up services for cases which need homecare visits especially for elderly and PWDs.
b) Conduct awareness sessions about MH, NCDs and childcare messages which will sustain even after the project completion.
