Project Summary

3,675 children (1,911 boys and 1,764 girls U5 admitted for SAM treatment)
25000 people (M/W) sensitized on IYCF in emergency
Activities or outputs
• Caregivers of children aged 0-23 months are supported to adopt recommended infant and young child feeding (IYCF) practices, including both breastfeeding and complementary feeding
• All children aged under five years in affected areas are screened regularly for the early detection of severe wasting and other forms of life‑threatening acute malnutrition and are referred as appropriate for treatment services.
• All children aged under five years suffering from severe wasting and other forms of life-threatening acute malnutrition in affected areas benefit from facility and community-based services (Fix or mobile) that provide effective treatment.

1 - Besoins / Needs

The March 2021 SMART/SENS survey, conducted by the Ministry of Health, with the financial and technical support of UNICEF and UNHCR has shown:• A relative stable situation related to acute malnutrition for host population in the Esat region with 4,5% Global Acute Malnutrition rate• A high prevalence of acute malnutrition among Central African refugees (12,5% in sites and outside of sites), which has deteriorated overall compared to 2016 (9,6% and 8,8% respectively).• A very high prevalence of stunting everywhere, with a maximum of 1 in 2 children affected in the refugee sites and camps.The project will continue to strengthen the nutrition assistance to the CAR refugees and reinforce the preparedness plan for early action for new arrivals. For the Central African, the main needs identified are to strengthen the analysis of the nutritional situation of the populations and their access to community nutritional care services (outpatient and hospitalization) while ensuring the strengthening of supply chains. Also, prevention aspects will be take into account with the strengthening of good dietary practices for infant and young child feeding in emergency situations.The nutritional response, with the participation of various humanitarian and development actors, will be carried out in the following areas:-Strengthening of the nutritional surveillance system and food security through surveys and screening;-Improve the management of severe acute malnutrition with or without complications in the Inpatient and Outpatient treatment facilities;- Promotion of good infant and young child feeding practices (IYCF)
