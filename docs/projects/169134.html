Project Summary

1.1Purpose:
The Appeal will enable MS response to the most immediate educational needs and time-critical early recovery needs arising from the Beirut Port explosions in Mount Lebanon governorate (Sin El Fil, and Bourj Hammoud)
1.2 Duration of the project: 2 Months, Sep-Oct 2020
1.3 Areas of Intervention: Bourj Hammoud and Sin El Fil
Based on OCHA’s Lebanon Flash Appeal Report, 14 August 2020, Bourj Hammoud is considered as a very high risk area, and Sin El Fil as high risk area
MS Education Experience in Bourj Hammoud and Sin El Fil:
RACE Designed Programs: Literacy and retention support for Lebanese and displaced children and youth:
Since 2016, the Mouvement Social teams worked on two programs developed by the Ministry of Education and Higher Education to cater for the needs of vulnerable children and youth: The Basic Literacy and Numeracy Program and the Retention Support Program
Basic Literacy and Numeracy Program
The program:
• Targets the most vulnerable: This program targets the most vulnerable children who are willing to return to school but are out of school and children who need a safe space to be in
• Driven by needs: Program activities are continuously tailored to cater for the specific needs of each child
• Comprehensive: Program activities are comprehensive, targeting each child’s development as a whole
• Ensures further opportunities: Children are able to continue their learning in school (when possible)
Retention Support Program
The Retention Support Program works with children who are already enrolled in first and second shift schools in grades 1 to 9. The program aims to provide children with the support needed on academic and psychosocial levels to prevent them from dropping out of school. The program:
• Targets vulnerable students: This program targets children who are enrolled in first and second shift schools and are at risk of dropping out
• Driven by needs: Program activities are tailored to cater for the specific needs of each child
• Comprehensive: Program activities are comprehensive, targeting the child’s whole development
1.4 Target:
5,000 affected students (Bourj Hammoud: 2,800 students, Sin El Fil: Approx. 2,200 students)
450 affected education personnel (Bourj Hammoud: 250, Sin El Fil: Approx. 200 Education Personnel)
1.5 Project Activities:
1.5.1 Rapid Needs Assessment on Formal Education/Non-formal Education (FE/NFE), including of the impact on infrastructure and program
Target: approx. 5,000 affected students, 450 education personnel
1.5.2 Cash for children at affected schools to support families with education related expenses:
700 children will benefit from conditional cash assistance-Scholarship
The fund for this activity is available
1.5.3 Psychosocial Support (PSS) training for teachers and education personnel, as well social emotional learning (SEL) activities in close coordination with the Child Protection partners
Target: approx. 450 education personnel divided into 23 groups, 20 participants in each group
1.5.4 Remote learning support (e.g. equipment and connectivity) to educational institutions not ready to fully function due to explosion damages and rehabilitation works
Target: approx. 400 teachers

2 - Activities

1. Rapid Needs Assessment on Formal Education/Non-formal Education (FE/NFE), including of the impact on infrastructure and programThe purpose of the education rapid needs assessment is to collect information quickly to inform decisions regarding design and delivery of an education program that supports access to quality educationSpecifically the purpose of the rapid needs assessment is to: • Describe the dynamics of Beirut explosion as they relate to education programming • Describe education capacities and gaps • Inform prioritization of education program parameters Phase 1: Assessment Planning (Setting Assessment Parameters, Hiring Specialized Staff, Gathering key resources), Timeframe: 2 WeeksPhase 2: Data Collection and Analysis (Creating and implementing data collection work plan, Analyzing the data), Timeframe: 2 weeksPhase 3: Reporting (Writing up the results, recommendations, Sharing the findings), Timeframe: 2 weeksTime needed to complete the assessment: 6 weeks And will take into account:Education Level: primary and secondary educationEducation Type: Formal and Non-Formal Education, Technical-VocationalEducation Providers: Public, semi-private, and private schools, Community-Based learning Centers (LNGOs, INGOs)Learners: In and out of school children, specific groups (disabilities, displaced) Key informants interviews-meetings will be conducted with education cluster leads, LNGOs, INGOs, ministry of education, teachers, specialized services organizations 2. Cash for children at affected schools to support families with education related expenses:700 children will benefit from conditional cash assistance-Scholarship. The fund for this activity is available 3. Psychosocial Support (PSS) training for teachers and education personnel, as well social emotional learning (SEL) activities in close coordination with the Child Protection partners:Approx. 450 education personnel will be trained on PSS, Remote PSS, Social Emotional Learning SEL, PFA, Early identification and Safe ReferralThe 450 education personnel will be divided into 23 groups, 20 participants in each oneTimeframe: 2 weeks, each group will attend 7 training days:PSS and SEL: 4 daysRemote PSS: 1 dayPFA: 1 dayEarly identification and Safe Referral: 1 dayThe training will be developed and conducted in close coordination with MS Child Protection Training Modality: will be identified (Remotely or Face-to-Face) according to the evaluation of the situation-COVID 19 prevention and social distancing 4. Remote learning support (e.g. equipment and connectivity) to educational institutions not ready to fully function due to explosion damages and rehabilitation worksAs mentioned above, approx. 400 teachers in Bourj Hammoud and Sin El Fil are affected by the explosion. In order to facilitate the remote learning, all the teachers teaching in the affected schools will be provided with: 1. 400 tablets with internet connectivity-bundle (1 tablet/teacher)Selection: all teachers teaching in the affected schools (Approx. 400 teachers, 20 schools) 2. The 400 tablets will be provided with internet connectivity-bundles valid for 1 year Internet bundles will be provided in kind: MS has a special offer from Alfa company (60% lower price) 3. 60 laptops (3 laptops per affected school)4. Online library5. Communication platforms subscription All the activities will be implemented in close coordination with Education sector lead, and relevant MEHE units
