Project Summary

The project will support the affected population in communities within Madagali and Michika LGAs with early recovery and livelihood starter packs, such as agricultural inputs, conditional cash grants to identified cooperative groups for business start-ups, training cooperative groups on business management skills, training of farmers on farming best practices.
The formation of these cooperative groups is because of the prolonged humanitarian crisis that has affected the level of socio-economic cohesion within the LGAs, especially the vulnerable IDP and returnee households. Furthermore, these cooperative groups will be used to facilitate circulation of cash based socio-economic activities within the project locations.
The beneficiaries of the project include the returnees, IDPs in host communities, vulnerable groups, women and youths in the communities. They will benefit from various training and skill acquisition after an in-depth and thorough baseline assessment. The project seeks to also integrate and link the beneficiaries with other opportunities outside the project scope. They will be linked with existing markets and organized private sector, government agencies that will serve as off takers/markets and by advocating to the government to invest more on critical social economic activities and infrastructures in the two project locations.
Resilience of the community members within the areas will be enhanced by putting up an early warning system in place at the community level and strengthening the coping mechanism. The number of direct beneficiaries of the project is 1,000 HHs. 700 HHs will receive agricultural inputs and training on best farming techniques, while 300 HHs will benefit from cash grants and training on business skills and group formation.

2 - Activities

Beneficiaries under this intervention are in two categories: Agricultural input beneficiaries and Cash grants beneficiaries. Agricultural inputs beneficiaries will receive seeds (vegetable and cereals) and extension services for a period of six months where they will be taught farming best practices. This activities are in line with the 2020 Early Recovery strategy; SO 2 - Support livelihood stabilization of the affected population.Conditional cash grant beneficiaries will receive skill acquisition, given business management training and given start up packs to enable them set up their businesses. This activity is also tied to the 2020 Early Recovery strategy SO 2 - Support livelihood and stabilization of the affected population.Lastly, cash beneficiaries will be grouped into cooperatives; this will help propel access/linkages with opportunities outside the primary scope of this project that is linking with the organized private sector, existing market and government agencies. Advocacy, in form of sensitization, mobilization that aims at strengthening synergies that will encourage increase in investment in critical socio-economic infrastructures that will improve livelihoods of the affected population in both project locations. This activity speaks to SO 1 and 2 of the 2020 early recovery strategy.
