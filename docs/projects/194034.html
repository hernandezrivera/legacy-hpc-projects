Project Summary

The project is based on human rights awareness-raising program piloted by CRW in 2017- 2022 in Sudan (– Darfur states Khartoum –lagawa -sounut & teama west Kordofan and a garrison town in an area that has experienced shifting divisions between GoS- and SPLA/M-control. GoS, SPLA/M and military/security forces not only gave approval to grass-roots human rights education but also engaged pro-actively in the training and began to work constructively with civil society groups to talk about and then take action on identified concerns - a significant breakthrough. The pilot allowed CRW to define its methodology, learn critical lessons (especially in how to create spaces conducive to multi-stakeholder dialogue on sensitive issues) and proved that such an innovative, multi-stakeholder approach to human rights and peace building culture could be implemented successfully in the Sudanese context.

This project will therefore build on the proven model developed allowing not only a wider, deeper capacity-building component and the forging of constructive and lasting partnerships between all key local stakeholders in Khartoum.
