Project Summary

The overall objective of this response plan is to increase access to quality nutrition services in targeted woredas both for IDP and host communities. The Nutrition response will support health centers and health posts to improve access to and quality of IMAM services (TSFP, OTP and SC) and provide quality emergency nutrition services for the most affected and marginalized groups, regardless of their status (returnees or IDPs and host communities). To improve access to quality therapeutic feeding center, SCI planned to establish /strengthen OTP and SC in existing health facilities in targeted areas. To ensure access to community in hard to reach area, the project support mass nutrition screening campaign on monthly with logistic, finance and technical support. To enhance referral of identified SAM cases, the project support referral mechanism with provision of fuel for ambulance and with covering perdiem for ambulance driver. The nutrition action will promote optimal IYCF-E practices for U5 children and PLW with a focus on exclusive breastfeeding, appropriate complementary feeding and promotion of micronutrient supplementation both at facility and community level through strengthening individual counseling, establishment of M2MSG and mass awareness creation.

1. - Projects activities

1. Support Treatment of severe acute malnutrition with medical complications in stabilization centers (inpatient treatment of SAM)The response planned to establish/strengthen SC in the existing health facilities through equipping health facilities with SC kit and other supplies. The project build capacity of Health workers in targeted health facilities on management of complicated SAM cases, infection prevention and other related topics through formal and informal training. Save the children will deploy experienced health professionals (CMAM burse) to provide technical support and improve quality of malnutrition treatment in those targeted health facilities. The action will increase coverage and quality of malnutrition treatment services in targeted health facilities. The mother will be supported with cost for referral and food during their stay in health facilities. 2. Treatment of Severe Acute Malnutrition without medical complication at in Outpatient Therapeutic Programme (OTP) sites. Save the children planned to establish/strengthen OTP service in in the existing health facilities for treatment of uncomplicated malnutrition through equipping health facilities with OTP kits and other supplies. The project build capacity of Health workers and HEW in targeted health facilities (OTP) on management of complicated SAM cases, infection prevention and other related topics through formal and informal training. SCI will also deploy experienced health professionals (CMAM burse) to provide technical support and improve quality of malnutrition treatment in those targeted health facilities. The action will increase coverage and quality of malnutrition treatment services in targeted health facilities. 3. Support Management of moderate acute malnutrition among children under 5 years and PLWsSave the children planned to support MAM treatment service among children under 5 years and PLWs through technical support, capacity building of health professionals and FDAs, coordination of distribution, transportation of supplies, cover financial cost for FDA and logistic support. The fully deployed TSFP officer will provide technical support for FDA to ensure dissemination of proper information on cooking/preparation, hygiene and other related key messages. Through liaising with WFP and provision of logistic and technical support SCI will increase capacity and coverage targeted woredas to identify and treat moderate Acute Malnutrition (MAM) in their respective woredas. 4. Support Promotion of IYCF, community mobilization, Vit.A supplementation, deworming (CHD) and mass nutrition screeningSCI supports mass nutrition screening by providing vehicle, and other supplies, technical support from Experienced health professionals to improve coverage and quality of the service . The project will Support Vit.A and deworming (CHD) and mass nutrition screening bi annually and least 85% of the children U5 and PLW will be addressed in the IDP host community in the districts. During mass nutrition screening and IYCF-E counseling identification of BGV will be integrated. 5. Promote optimal Infant and Young Child Feeding in Emergencies (IYCF-E)The project will support MOH in the integration, planning and implementation of IYCF-E activities through capacity improvement trainings, equip health facilities with supplies needed for IYCF-E services provision, establish breast feeding space, support and strengthening community mobilization activities pertaining to IYCF using HDAs, MtMSGs and FtFSGs. The response will train HW and HEW from on IYCF-E, infection prevention and other topics using the recently released national IYCF-E guideline to improve quality of the serve. Awareness creation on code of conduct for formula feeding and its monitoring will be integrated in all nutrition activities in targeted area. This IYCF- E activity targets at least 85% of caretakers of children under five years care takers/mother in the project period

3.6 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members using age and gender appropriate, safe and confidential channels?

Save the Children strives to ensure transparent and participatory processes throughout the project cycle, during needs identification, project development, implementation, and monitoring and evaluation. Feedback from community members, including children, on the quality and accessibility of project resources and services is collected regularly through participatory monitoring mechanisms including community feedback and response meetings, focus group discussions and suggestion boxes. Appropriate and confidential feedback/complaint mechanisms will be strengthened in consultation with beneficiaries to enhance Save the Children’s ability to protect beneficiaries from various forms of exploitation and abuse. Established CRM committees resolve complaints related to beneficiary selection and manage feedback from beneficiaries throughout the project. Existing project/context-specific accountability systems will be established, and the project will have a detailed accountability plan. CRM training will be provided for technical project staff and committee members on complaint/feedback management, information sharing, and accountability standards to ensure sensitive complaints are met with confidential investigation. All responses will be delivered verbally, in writing, and recorded. Accessible and timely information about project deliverables and progress will be shared in local languages and through familiar formats easily understood by children and communities. Details about Save the Children’s vision and mission, objectives, source of funds, staff code of conduct, confidential complaint will be shared.
