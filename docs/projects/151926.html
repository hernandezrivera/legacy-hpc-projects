Project Summary

At least 6000 girls and Boys in north East Nigeria are estimated to have been recruited, abducted or held by Non State armed groups. Approximately, 50% of these children are survivors of sexual and gender based violence.
LABI intends to identify the children who have suffered grave violations with the non state armed groups and have lost hope for survival. These children will be integrated back to their communities and the adolescent girls and boys will be given access to livelihood activities.
Furthermore, LABI intends to provide psycho social support for the children and to also strengthen referral pathways for the children.
I. To provide basic psychosocial support to children affected by the insurgency, and their families
II. To provide child protection case management services to unaccompanied, separated and other children with protection concerns
III. To strengthen the capacity of community structures including religious, community and local groups/CBOs to address child protection concerns and risks.
IV. Women and girls empowerment to enhance Child protection activities and response
 Recruit and provide child protection, case management and other relevant training to case workers, FTR officer and Facilitators/community volunteers

Needs

From the needs assessment conducted by LABI at Maiduguri, Bama, Chibok and Jere, it was discovered that the numbers of vulnerable women and children is high due to the displace persons brought in by the military.Psychosocial support services for children.Currently, children in Borno State North east Nigeria, are facing a lot of challenges, among others are, limited access to schools, burden of care of family members especially those that have lost parents to the insurgency are either staying with aged caregivers or as child headed households, child labour, sexual abuse and exploitation, and abuse of drugs among others. For children who are associated with Boko Haram (parents or family) there is a needs to reintegrate this children, the threat of stigma, discrimination and rejection remain high, even with the current return efforts. With almost all children having been directly affected, or witnessed the death of loved ones, the need for psychosocial support to children remains great. There is a needs to strengthen the resilience and wellbeing of children and adolescents through community level organized structuredThere is a need to mobilize communities around the protection and psychosocial wellbeing of their children.LABI have identify the need opportunities for children to play, acquire life skills and receive social support and strengthen their resilience

Activities

 Reintegration of children who have suffered grave violations.  Foster care for foster parents  Care givers in vulnerable conditions are supported with psycho-social support and life skills  Training youth mentors, community volunteers CAAFAG&,GBV on PSS (including the use of resources such as Adolescents kit) on Child protection,  Mobilize and create awareness about the intervention among parent and children  Set up CFS and adolescent centers in the 4 targeted program LGAs (Locations) Hold monthly meeting with CVs& Youth mentors  Facilitation to community volunteers/youth mentors Identify and refer children requiring further support and specialize services  Provision of supplies including play, recreational, educational and interactive materials and equipment Establish sport and cultural leagues and facilitation of inter-group competitions Set up of adolescent discussion group Age and gender appropriate recreation, social, learning and creative activities including storytelling, games, reading, art and drama Periodic maintenance of the CFS and adolescent centers including fencing and amenities
