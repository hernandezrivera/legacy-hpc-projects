Project Summary

Project Objective: To enhance the capacity of agricultural assets of vulnerable farmers in the southern governorate of the Gaza Strip who are subjected to humanitarian crises to restore livelihoods.
Special objectives:
1. To increase agricultural assist productivity of 360 Dunums of open field holed by (at least 10 female farmers and 170 males' of the headed household farmers).
2. To rehabilitate agricultural water wells and Install a 75 kW solar power for three wells in the targeted area (at least 3 female farmers and 57 male of the headed household farmers).
3. To provide 9000 clubs of water storage by rehabilitating 100 ponds (at least 5 female farmers and 95 males of the headed household farmers).
The project's objectives match the HRP 2019 (part of 2018-2020 HRP) oPt, Strategic objective country #( 3): The ability of vulnerable Palestinians to cope with the protracted crisis, including environmental threats, is supported, while solutions to violations and other root causes of threats and shocks are pursued.

Needs

2 million people living in Gaza Strip are witnessing a continuous decline in their living conditions. Their food insecurity is directly linked to households’ inability to purchase food available on the market. Food security situation in the Gaza Strip continues to be characterized by high levels of food insecurity, with ranges of 40-47% HHs being food insecure for the last few years. This condition is also driven by the increased rates of unemployment, which exceeded 50% in the second quarter of 2018, for the first ever. This results in increased poverty levels, which is one of the main determinants of food insecurity. It also increases the level of aid dependency, especially on food assistance. The situation has been worsened by the funding crisis for agencies funded by USA funds, especially UNRWA, considering that approximately 66% of the population in Gaza Strip are refugees. The funding crisis is clearly represented also by the FSS HRP funding appeal shortfall, with only 24% of the overall FSS budget being funded as of mid-year 2018. The continuous salary crisis for civil servants of both the PA and the de facto government adds new layers of the population in need for immediate assistance, including food assistance. If the situation continues, it will lead to further increase in poverty rates, where 53% of individuals in Gaza Strip are reported living below the poverty level based on their consumption pattern, as reported by PCBS for 2017. Moreover, the long-lasting energy crisis has put an additional burden on farmers who already suffer from an increase in agricultural input costs accompanied by low purchasing power by households, Al Nakheel assessed humanitarian's needs using several methods; semi-structured interview executed with headed house females farmers (random samples), field visits to some owners of the water wells, three focusing group discussions were conducted. In Deir Al Balah, Khan Younis Rafah, with participation of MoA, women' associations representatives, 18 small headed household farmers (4 females and 14 males) with different ages and marital status, Some of them are landowners or land hold and some of them work with their relatives in their father's or grandfather land ownership. Participants concluded their needs as follows: 1.(95) % of the participants focused on low productivity of open fields due to man-made and natural disaster. They focused on the necessity of increasing their land production capacity as well as protecting their plants from stray dogs and loafers. 2.(80)% participants indicated agriculture water availability and accessibility issues as an urgent need due to the high price of cubic meters of water (about 2.5 NIS for one cubic meter). 3. All the interviewed agricultural water wells' owners complained about the high cost of operating their wells by fuel. 4. Participants focused on the necessity to rehabilitate the greenhouses to be more effective. 5. Female-headed households participants complained form that food insecurity among them; even they have some production assets like house backyard. They expressed their needs for agricultural water accessibility to produce fresh food to match their family's needs. 6. Females focused on their rights to benefit from agricultural assets of their fathers or grandfathers - equal with the males. They expressed their urgent need for Al Nakheel association to design the project activities to sustain the livelihood of target the beneficiaries.Notes: According to information released by the General Directorate of Planning and Policy- Ministry of Agriculture, the number of female farmers who are holding the agricultural assets is equal to 5 % of the number of farmers in Gaza Strip.

Activities

This intervention aims to increase the production capacity of the agricultural assets owned by the small male and female farmers who are most vulnerable to humanitarian crises in Rafah, Khan Younis, and Deir Al Balah. The activities are designed in coordination with MoA and FSS to eliminate the duplication of the beneficiaries from other agriculture projects, which may be implemented in the same place, this project will target women farmers who own or rent or hold agriculture assets from their fathers or husbands.Moreover, in the implementation stage, the coordination with MoA will be in each stage.Activity # 1: Rehabilitation of agricultural assets for 180 farming households (at least 10 female farmers). Al Nakheel will create opportunities for vulnerable and poor farmers, two dunums for each farmer to rehabilitation 360 dunums( 100 Rafah, 160 Khan Younis, 100 Middle Area), through tilling the soil in addition, to fertilize the soil by natural manure on 6 cubic meters per dunums and extension of irrigation network for each dunum and providing vegetable seeds. Output 1# 360 dunums of high productivity soil and 1800 tons at least of vegetable. Activity # 2: Rehabilitation of water wells. Partial Rehabilitation for operating four agricultural wells to improve the efficiency of discharge water and installing the renewable energy for the agricultural water wells. (1 Rafah, 2 Khan Younis) each well will serve 20 farmers; this activity will target 60 farmers (at least 3 female farmers and 57 males of the headed household farmers). Outputs#2: improving water access to 300 dunums with low cost. Activity # 3 Rehabilitation of 100 water ponds, The plastic water harvesting ponds rehabilitated with plastic sheeting, shadow net, and safety fence ( 25 Rafah, 50 Khan Younis, 25 Middle Area) – 100 farmers (at least 5 female farmers and 95 males of the headed household farmers).Outputs: # M3 of water storage will be provided
