AHA will target HHs that have been partially destroyed due to continued damage as a result of aerial/land attacks in addition to general dilapidation from poor living conditions (AHA will verify that by Local Councils and members of the host community that reside in those homes). In addition to the repair of small damages in walls, doors, broken windows, locks and deteriorated plumbing.. Mainly, shelter type grade 2 as Residential housing (single or multiple-story buildings), partially damaged or unﬁnished. Also, this activity will be implemented through two types of contract modalities, the ﬁrst is through contractor for the for procurement and transportation of materials, coordination of works, including hiring of staff, health and safety practices as well as a commitment that the work is completed on time in addition to the mass works. In addition to that we will provide NFI kits for 2000 families of IDPs in those areas. The project will be targeting most vulnerable HH especially women heading HH based on the previous implementation of our work; we focused on reaching them as one of the most vulnerable HH of the communities..

4 - Needs

Till 2019, 870,343 IDPs are residing in Aleppo governorate with total population of 1,3 million are in need for shelter and NFI assistance in only Aleppo rather than bigger number if we considered Syria as a whole, where almost have of them reside in Aleppo and Idleb. The systematic targeting of social infrastructure including civilian homes has been a major driving force behind mass internal displacement. The failure to rehabilitate deteriorated homes and secure stable shelters for families has perpetuated the challenges of internal displacement and for host communities rather than deescalating the crisis in a time when the overall security situation has stabilized as a result of the cessation of hostilities. Families deprived of adequate shelter provision suffer from a wide spectrum of fundamental issues that erode their quality of life, continue to deprive them of essential resources, diminish their physical and mental protection and reduce their overall resilience/ability to respond to conﬂict-related events. ongoing economic limitations will negatively impact household resilience across all aspects of basic living standards, further reducing families’ ability to pay rent, address shelter issues, meet their basic needs – potentially causing households to resort to harmful coping mechanisms HNO2020In addition to poor sanitation, exposure to hazardous environmental conditions and lack of safety, families endure psychological instability that arises from lack of privacy within degraded homes, AHA had conducted a needs assessment for most of the sub-districts in Afrin and Idleb, where we received many appeals from LCs to see the bad condition of the houses, therefore, our out-reach team headed to the location and accomplish the rapid assessment (document with narrative report will be issued in March) where some ﬁndings of the surveys were: It showed a great and urgent need for: - rehabilitation for houses for the IDPs and host community in addition to the NFI kits
