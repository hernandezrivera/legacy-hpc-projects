Project Summary

In non-protected displacement settlements or camps, the IDP, returnees and conflict affected population lacks access to essential lifesaving NFIs kits and often times unable to support themselves through communal connections or coping mechanisms due to limited resources. This project designed for the IDPs, Returnees and host communities found in an open settlements and war affected villages of EES. It will address issues of vulnerable households with old shelters, damaged NFIs that’s places IDPs and returnees’ families at risk of climatic conditions such as direct rains, hit and coldness in addition to human sickness which’s related to poor hygiene and lack of dignified NFIs kits. The purpose of this intervention is to deliver lifesaving assistance and social protection messages to the project beneficiaries through distribution of S/NFIs and training awareness on Housing Land and Property (HLP). Before distribution, LSDO will conduct FGD and KIIs with community representatives and local leaders in Ikotos and Budi areas. The project staff will be trained on identified issues in regards to HLP including receiving claims from individuals or the community-based complaints members and how to report the feedbacks in order to improve the level of beign Accountabile to Affected Population (AAP). The project will be for the period of 12 months from 1st January to 31st December 2020, it will focus on direct distribution of S/NFIs in kind using a quick response mechanism to reach out a total of 10,000 beneficiaries including of a robust shelter's most vulnerable households in the project locations. LSDO will incorporate the multi-sector approach during field work for selection, registration and verification of beneficiaries to receive direct S/NFIs kits and shelter materials as well as rehabilitation of the shelters for the most vulnerable families. LSDO will be in charge for delivery of direct S/NFIs as a roving partner jointly with other cluster team and Site Focal Persons (SFPs) present in the targeted project areas. For the robust shelter LSDO will engage IDP, returnees and host communities to use local materials for erecting dignified shelters to the identified persons such as elderly persons and Persons with Disabilities (PWD), widows and young children headed households especially in the areas where local construction materials available.The beneficiaries will be lactating and pregnant mothers, old age and disable persons, households with certain health complications and unaccompanied children. The project team will ensure timely delivery of assistance through rapid response approach to the targeted locations. The response teams will distribute items in 14 days per location/State and Post Distribution Monitoring (PDM) for 3 days per targeted area. The monthly, quarterly reporting and the end of project report will be shared with the Shelter cluster and the donor. The project team will be in close consultation with the cluster Working Group (WG) for identifying gaps that will be informed largely through the multi-sector agencies’ assessment missions. In order to achieve holistic and integrated NFI/shelter response, LSDO will also coordinate its field operation with other NGOs doing Food Distribution, WASH, Education and Health rapid response. Distribution sites will be identified by the local communities and they will be advised to consider the minimum standards of protection, for example; the sites will not expose women and girls to any risk of Sexual and Gender-Based Violence (SGBV), places with shades and not at long walking distances from the village or settlement. LSDO will ensure that, the distribution sites have designated areas of ease with hand washing facilities and the beneficiaries will be encouraged to bring their own drinking water in cases safe water sources are not available in nearby the distribution site.

1 - Needs

LSDO participated in the field assessment needs analysis conducted in Bungu and Ganji payams of Juba county as roving partner member of S/NFIs cluster last month of September/2019 together with the team from NNGOs members includes; Caritas, IsraAID, PAH and Africa Life Aid (ALA).The results or evidence of the assessment needs analysis was shown level of severity, because the host community are sharing their shelter and resources with the displaced population including copying mechanism which have been eroded due to sharing with IDPs and returnees. The project considering the targeted counties that in line with the S/NFIs sector Need analysis of 2020 Humanitarian Needs Overview (HNO), like Ikotos and Budi in EES as a hard to reach areas but it would be on a joint missions effort, where Relief and Rehabilitation Commission (RRC) & Join Boarder Monitoring and Verification Committee (JBMVC) will be ask on clearance procedures for a convoy trucks to the project locations. LSDO will prepare and submit the Request for Services to the Logistic Cluster (LC) for the prepositioning of the S/NFIs kits to Ikotos and Budi using Juba to Kapoeata route by land site transport, while LSDO will provide vehicle to its own project staff. Elderly, physically incapable, pregnant women and others who require special attention will be prioritized during distribution. LSDO will complement the distribution with awareness on social protection issues through Community Communication and Engagement (CCE) approach and also will organize practical demonstrations (drama group) to improve the understanding and proper usage of distributed items. LSDO will use peer monitors (local leaders, women and youth representatives) to monitor and report claims of any marginalized community group or persons through county authorities and to LSDO protection officer for the timely solution across the targeted locations in order to complement the assurance of being accountable to the beneficiaries or affected population.

2 - Activities

Cross-check the sector needs analysis in line with South Sudan HNO 2020 relevant to the project locations. Conduct assessment on HLP issues in Ikotows and Budi Counties. Train project staff and community leaders and members on AAP & CFMs approaches. Submit to Logistic Cluster the Request for Services (RFS) of landsite transport, to transport or deliver S/NFIs kits to the Project areas. Acquire clearance letter from RRC and JBMVC for land transport convoy. Carry out selection, registration and verification of beneficiaries list in collaboration with the local authorities and community representatives. Conduct distribution of S/NFIs kits to the beneficiaries. Establish peer monitors and community claim committee. Conduct rapid monitoring and post distribution monitoring (PDM) at project locations. Engage local communities on mobilizing local construction materials to erect dignified shelters to the most identified vulnerable persons with the host community. Conduct regular and consistent reporting to agency, donor and cluster as required. Conduct awareness raising campaigns on social protection issues, hygiene practices, Food and nutrition.
