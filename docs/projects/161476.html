Project Summary

Unity State emerged as the most highly contested area with the worst humanitarian crisis since the start of the conflict. The communities had to move to very remote areas most which were swamps to escape the fighting and all forms of livelihood initiatives like agriculture were abandoned, livestock raided and used as food for the armed groups leaving the population unable to meet the most basic food and nutritional needs especially of the children, lactating mother and pregnant women.
The USA factsheets released in July 2019 reported over 17000 returnees came from neighbouring Sudan between April and June out of which 2000 reportedly settled in Leer County alone. The same report indicates that the driving force for their return is the perception of relative calm in their original counties and insufficient basic services like health and education for their families in Sudan.
Reports from RRC officials in Mayendit County indicate that many returnees are living in Markuel, Luom and Dablual in Mayendit County. Reports from the assessment undertaken by Mobile Humanitarian Agency (MHA) indicate that 8000 returnees were recorded in Adok and Thonyor Payams in Leer County in June 2019 of which 20% are children. The same report mentioned 5831 returnees in Dablual in Mayendit County alone. As the report states, these returnees do not have anything left to re-establish themselves apart from expecting handouts from the humanitarian actors in the area.
This project will target the 17,000 returnees in Southern Unity who are in need of humanitarian assistance and protection and rebuild their livelihoods. The fact that food security situation is very acute, households rely on nature for their survival in terms of alternative food sources. This will not only provide the much-needed supply of protein foods but it will act as a source of income when due training is given to the fisher flocks to learn simple fish preservation. The presence of the water bodies will also be essential in establishment of the demonstration farms for vegetables by providing irrigation water. It should be noted that Christian Aid and its partner UNIDOR will also train beneficiaries on psycho social targeting the nutrition voucher and unconditional cash vouchers beneficiaries.

2 - Activities

Community mobilization and engagement: This project will facilitate detailed project information sharing through an established community-based committee using an agreed criteria. This is aimed at ensuring clear and strong relationship with the community through continuous community communication and engagement (CCE) throughout the project cycle, by soliciting and sharing feedback from different stakeholders in the community, the project, outcomes, quality and performances will be enhanced. Unconditional cash transfer - food security objectives: Cash transfer for 600HHs. In close coordination and collaboration with the local authorities, community committees, enumerators and other partners on the ground. Beneficiaries targeting, and registration will be done in accordance to the standardized FSL cluster tools and methodologies and all registration forms will be shared with both the cluster. During selection and registration, high priority and attention will be given to the most vulnerable members of the IDPs, Returnees and Host Community, in consideration of the most elderly, pregnant and lactating mothers, people with special needs or physical impairments, persons with no sources of livelihoods and community links. Verification process will be accompanied by issuing of tokens.Conditional cash transfer - nutritional objectives: Cash to 400 lactating and pregnant mothers including caregivers for children under 5. Each household will receive conditional cash value of $50 per to supplement their dietary nutritional needs that they will not access from their gardens. The conditional cash distribution; a mother or a caregiver will receive the cash on condition that at least there is well-established garden and verified by the project agriculture technical trainers before distributing the cash and that the cash is used for procuring the other missing nutritional foods that they need in the diet. Emergency food and livelihoods support: Provision of vegetable seeds and tools to 900 HHs and fishing Kits to another 1,000HHs. Selected seed package will include Kudra, eggplants, okra and tomatoes with a molado and watering cans. While the fisher forks will get fishing hooks and 4 rolls of twines. Capacity building trainings to of lead farmers and fishers. The project will offer technical trainings to all inputs beneficiaries in groups. For instance, lead farmers will be trained on nursery bed establishment and vegetable production, pests and diseases management, while for fisher forks post-harvest handling and preservation measures. Other key skills gap identified include raw planting, transplanting of vegetable seedlings, mulching, thinning, staking and crop rotation.
