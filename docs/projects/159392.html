Project Summary

Implementing of the programme of the services provision through well coordinated, sustainable network of shelters and their support care model unified and applicable for IDPs and groups of IDP classified in accordance to their special needs, status, gender and other criteria of vulnerability. We offer to create shelters based on our country experience, especially in the conflict affected area, which we have got while established network of shelters for GBV/DV survivors. It wil be 4 shelters, 2 per each oblast: in Kurakhove and Bakhmut for Donetsk oblast, and in Novoaidar and Popasna for Luhanska oblast, so all of 3 areas 0-5km, 5-20km, 20+km would be covered.
Shelters are part of the continuum model of care for GBV survivors, which includes mobile teams of socio-psychosocial support operating in Donetsk and Luhansk region with total coverage of more than 6000 clients per year, day service centers (crisis centers), shelters as safe place (providing housing, basic consumables, medical assistance, first need consultations and referrals, some other support). The model foresees close collaboration with local state social services, police, centers for pro bono legal aid, healthcare facilities, educational institutions, local authorities and partner organizations.

1 - Needs

As the armed conflict in the east of Ukraine lingers, GBV, be it in the form of rape, sexual exploitation, human trafficking or domestic abuse, remains a major concern and represents a significant risk in crisis-affected areas. According to recent researches, the various forms of violence including threat with weapon, psychological violence, sexual violence, intimidation ( and domestic violence, which are faced by IDP - women and girls. International experience suggests that the current context of the armed conflict, combined with widespread social and economic stresses, is fertile soil for a further increase in violence against women and girls. At the same time, in conflict-affected areas local authorities’ and civil society’s capacities have severely degraded, many women’s protection centres or organizations in the conflict areas were destroyed or closed. Therefore, the project addresses the issue of GBV in the armed conflict and post-conflict settings through strengthening of multi-sectorial coordination, protection and prevention systems and enhancing access to legal, health and social-psychological care services for survivors of GBV.

3 - Indicators & targets

The results of the project and its success are supposed to be measured by 1) effectiveness, 2) impact, 3) efficiency and 4) perspectives/sustainability. To assess achievement of the main project outcome we will apply quantitative and qualitative measures of evaluation. We have created reliable data collection, storage, analysis and disclosure Management Information System (MIS). In terms of quality we have also system of indicators, methodology for their identification and assessment. We’ll assign our qualified monitorinf&evaluation specialist to the project. In the course of implementation of the internationally funded projects we have gained experience in procuring and supervising sub-contractors, for example in logistics, organization of trainings and purchase of goods and services, PR services. UFPH applies tender procedures elaborated by our American founders and based on internationally recognized standards and techniques. Transparency, cost efficiency and impartiality of concluded sub-contracts is available for our partners upon request in our Progress Reports and accountancy records. In terms of financial evaluation UFPH is financially transparent non -profit organisation. It undergoes financial audit every year. This audit is completed by auditing LLC KYIVAUDIT. Also, each international organisation financing projects implemented by UFPH conducts its own audit according to the schedule agreed. For this purpose, the financial personnel of UFPH is fully available and committed. Specific measures to ensure accountability to the affected population:- For shelter beneficiaries: detected clients quantity, clients located in the shelter quantity, clients under case management quantity, сlients under group program (number if referrals), rehabilitated persons quantity, gender & age disaggregation, type of violence disaggregation, quantity of services provided by lawyer and psychologist, distributed emergency kits quantity. Also UFPH designed a special mechanism for receiving a feedback from our direct beneficiaries to ensure the quality of the services provision. There is a complex procedure, that is proscribed in organization policies: procedures of complaint submitting and solving, anonymous feedback forms, special available at every shelter location complain/proposal boxes, opened and disseminated contacts of key UFPH staff to approach them directly with any comments on services provided etc. - For beneficiaries who receive services via training UFPH designs an incoming/outgoing questionnaire (tailored for each activity individually).- For information campaign: subscribers of UFPH FB page (with age/gender disaggregation according to Facebook Page Admin Tools), Facebook shared publications quantity, final presentation attendees quantity, events organized in cooperation with Social services centers for family, children and youth attendees, related media publications quantity (if any).
