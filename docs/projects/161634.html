1 - Needs

IOM DTM Assessment Report in August 2019 Reveals that in camps and camps like: 96% per cent of displacement sites (up from 95% in the last round of assessment in May), toilets were described as ‘not hygienic’, while toilets were reported to be in hygienic conditions in only 3 per cent of sites (down from 5%) and none were reportedly non-usable. In Borno, 97 per cent (up from 96%) were reported as not hygienic. The situation in Borno camps and camps like needs urgent attention. However, 97 per cent of host community sites (down from 98%), toilets were described as not hygienic and good in 3 per cent of sites (up from 2%). Unlike the scenario in camps and camp-like settings, hand pumps are the main source of water in 54 per cent (up from 52%) of sites where IDPs are residing with host communities. Borno had the highest reliance on piped water supply, with 70 per cent of sites in that state using piped water as a water source. In fact, in Borno the main source of drinking water was piped water in 70 (down from 75%) per cent of sites, followed by hand pumps in 17 (up from 15%) per cent of sites and water trucks in 8 (up from 6%) per cent of sites. In 22 per cent of sites (down from 23%), piped water was the main source of drinking water, followed by protected wells (9% - down from 10%) and unprotected wells (8%). Other common water sources include water trucks (5% - down from 6%) and springs (1%): DTM REPORT ROUND 28 - AUGUST 2019

2 - Activities

Identify key influencers (e.g community leaders, religious leaders, health workers), networks (women’s groups, youth groups, community health workers, social mobilizers for polio, malaria, HIV and trusted channels of communication (local media, radio, social media, hotlines) to strengthen or establish a system for community engagement at all community and Camps level levels Community Base Approach Door to Door Hygiene promotion and Awareness raising using key messaging on Social distancing practiced as much as possible during distribution of WASH NFIs, water collection points, queues at latrines and bathing facilities also on how to prevent Covid-19 infection and stop disease transmission through regular hand washing with soap after sneezing/coughing, before collecting water, covering mouth and nose with flexed elbow or tissue when coughing and sneezing – avoid close contact with anyone who has fever and cough, attention to multi touch surfaces (doors, chairs, tables, phones etc), existing resources will be used to develop/adapt communication materials (infographics, posters, video clips), including for outreach through mass media. Also specific Messaging targeting children, parent/caregivers, pregnant Mothers, Elderly, people with disability, depending on how covid 19 impacted such group of people and their Families, this would include, Key stakeholders and at-risk groups, such as health care providers (e.g. public, private, traditional practices, community health workers – for case detection, management, referral, and infection prevention and control). Strengthen community-based mechanisms (hotlines, surveys, rumor tracking) to inform the response. Involve traditional media and social media monitoring, local communication systems and networks. To build and develop the capacity of community based level operation Management and hygiene promotion volunteers, Setting up of hand washing stations in strategic and public places within the camps and Distribution of hygiene kits, soap and cleaning supplies for latrines, home and health facility disinfection. Provision of WASH services in health facilities to enhanced water supply, sanitation as well as adapted management of medical waste. Providing improved access to safe water supply by daily chlorination and monitoring residual chlorine level at water pointEnsure provision of safe water supply from sources to consumption by promoting community level water safety plan Sensitization to mitigate on open defecation practice in the camps/ Host communities during Covid 19 through participatory community base Approach of latrine maintenance acceptable to the community members. Hygiene promotion to promote and encourage good hygiene practice, gender equality using participatory community base approach Conduct risk information study of camps/ communities identify risk practice
