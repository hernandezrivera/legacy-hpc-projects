Project Summary

The project aims to-
1. Number of people (dis-aggregated by sex and age) in settlements benefiting from safe water to agreed standards and meeting demand for domestic purposes.
2. women, men, children in settlements (both refugee and host community) who are benefiting from functional latrines and waste management system to agreed standards.
3. Facilities are better prepared in terms of water, sanitation and hygiene service availability and safe management and disposal of wastes.

Objectives

Objective 1. Ensure effective, sufficient and equitable provision of life saving water and sanitation services for targeted men, women, boys and girls. Objective 2. Ensure that all targeted women, men, girls and boys have the means and are encouraged to adopt individual and collective measures increasing health seeking behaviors to mitigate public health risks for the well-being of the affected population. Objective 3: Ensure that all WASH assistance promotes protection, safety and dignity of targeted men, women, boys and girls with focus on sustainable resilience-building approaches.

Needs

In the initial days of response due to immediate and urgent nature of demand, a large number of latrines were constructed in Camp-15 to meet the immediate need of newly arriving Rohingya people. Many of these latrines were not aligned to the sectoral standards and global WASH cluster guidelines. Even though some latrines that fulfill construction standards are often not functional, as they are already filled up. There are insufficient capacities of faecal sludge management sites in Camp-15 as well as in other camps and no systematic and safe approach to desludging in the camp. Current desludging is done manually, which has an inherent risk of further contamination and health hazards for the community and the workers. The WASH Sector has declared faecal sludge management as a high priority concern. HEKS refrains from constructing more latrines in the camp but instead will ensure by desludging dysfunctional latrines, that people have again access to these facilities.In the early phase after the influx of Rohingya solid waste management was only defined as a secondary priority as health risks are much higher for wastewater. As the crisis continues and high investments are being done in sanitation, it has been agreed by the WASH sector in Camp-15 that also solid waste management needs to be started.Considering the still very problematic situation with many non-safe toilets in Camp-15, the WASH Sector has prioritized hygiene promotion, which has not yet been scaled up as required. In Camp-15 most people have access to some form of hygiene messages, but more efforts are required to really change hygiene behavior.

Activities or outputs

1. Faecal sludge management plant2. Bathing cubicles3. Hygiene promotion4. WASH in school (Blues school)5. WASH in health care facilities

Key project/performance indicators

1. The environment of the camps and the hygiene practices of Rohingya have become more hygienic.2. Number of older women and men who access latrines and bathing facilities.3. Number of people practicing hand washing with soap before handling food, cooking and eating, and after defecating and changing diapers.4. Number of Schools with a Basic level of water and Sanitation service.5. Number of Health Care Facilities that improved their Sanitation service from No Service to Limited.
