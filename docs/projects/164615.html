Project Summary

SKT through this proposal aims contribute in the comprehensive WASH response that required for IDPs across all locations including in camps and communities and led by the cluster, by focusing on the newly displaced people in the camps hosting IDPs from previous displacements and newly arrivals who are unable to access these services and are fully dependent on the WASH services provided by INGOs/NGOs in addition, to preparation for any sudden influx of IDPs.
The proposed project has been designed based on a needed assessment report, and meet with cluster objectives and allocation paper activities and priorities.
The proposed project will be implemented by SKT directly and will work on the provision of basic WASH services in camps hosting IDPs for new arrivals who are unable to access these services and are fully dependent on the WASH services provided by INGOs/NGOs (in close coordination with CCCM cluster. SKT will cover 9678 IDPs divided into; 4254 female and 4170 males. The new IDPs are 72% of the total served IDPs.
This project has been designed based on a need assessment report and will work to provide the following services;
1-Water trucking: 43549 m3, which is 25 liter per day per individual.
2- Water storage tanks: 1862 tanks with a capacity of 200 liters
3-Water purifying agents: 200 liters per month, as well as, testing through Pool tester, and DPD
4-Jerry cans: 1862 cans with a capacity of 20 liter
5-Hygiene kits: 1862 kit to distribute.
6- Latrine and/or showers construction: 81 unit to construct
7-Latrine and/or showers repair/maintenance and desludging septic tanks: 143 unit to maintenance and 1228 m3 of septic to desludging.
8-Solid waste management: 6030 m3 to transport.
9-Provision of communal garbage bins: 75 bin will be distributed
10-Hygiene promotion activities: 6 community members to implement hygiene promotion group sessions and community management for WASH facilities in 11 camps for 6 months.
this project will serve 11 IDPs camps and informal settlements located in Harem district as follows; and will cover 1862 HH.
SKT has an ongoing WASH project in the first 3 camps mentioned above, however, this project will end on the first of September.2019. SKT aims to keep running these services in these camps, in addition to the other 8 camps and informal settlements to ensure access to life-saving WASH services for old and new IDPs.

4 - Needs

According to OCHA, Humanitarian situation across northwest Syria, particularly in Idleb and Aleppo governorates continue to deteriorate at an alarming rate due to ongoing violence, which has a devastating effect on the four million people in northwest Syria.From 1 December 2019 to 2 February 2020, some 586,000 people fled from their homes in northwest Syria as a result of ongoing hostilities. Many of them have been displaced many times throughout the Syria crisis, eroding the resilience of families and communities.This latest emergency compounds an already dire humanitarian situation for people in northwest Syria who have been made vulnerable by years of crisis, violence, economic downturn, and multiple displacements.The humanitarian situation in northwest Syria continues to deteriorate at an alarming rate. SKT team conducted a rapid needs assessment in 11 camps in Idleb governorate, and it showed from this that the increase of families and the population inflated within a geographical location are limited and increased the needs of WASH sector.That assessment confirmed that these camps have limited access to WASH services. The current consumption is between 15 and 20 liters per day per person. Currently, the IDPs obtain 100% of their water through trucking from private wells, for which they spend about 35-40% of their income and savings. There are also no community-level waste bins or municipality trucks used in waste collection and most of IDPs bury or burn garbage. The same assessment showed there are inadequate numbers of latrines due to an increased number of IDPs in camps.

5 - Implementation of activities

SKT will implement activities as follows1- Water supply: SKT plans to provide water supply for a maximum of 6 months in response to displacements of IDPs. This will be achieved through water trucking and provision of water points (water storage tanks with taps) in camps and informal IDPs settlements. At each water point, chemical analysis will be conducted to ensure that the water points meet the national water quality and SPHERE standards. 2- hygiene kits. emergency Hygiene Kits are utilized in response to emergencies in support of newly displaced HH. Hygiene kits are designed to provide an initial kit containing both consumable and durable hygiene items for essential personal and household hygiene. Kit's content has been adapted to be appropriate for a family of six with basic hygiene and dignity items. To complement the kits' distribution, SKT will also undertake hygiene awareness activities and specific messages tailored to the context such as identification, prevention, and treatment of diarrhea; proper handwashing procedure; potable water safety; human/solid/household waste disposal; Leishmaniosis identification and prevention; and oral hygiene. 3- WASH sanitation and latrines: The design of bathing facilities and latrines will take into account cluster guidelines in addition to the needs of all users, including women and girls, particularly seeking to minimize protection risks, such as SGBV. Measures include ensuring the locations of bathing facilities are recognized by women and girls as safe places where they are not at risk of sexual violence, facilities are well-lit and can be locked from the inside, separated entrances for females/males, placement of shelves and sinks near latrines to encourage their use for handwashing (especially during menstruation). 4- Solid waste management and Provision of communal garbage bins:SKT will implement these activities according to cluster guidelines and will ensure they do no harm the population.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

1- Inaccessibility to target areas due to insecurity, large scale military offensives, or change in territorial control. SKT would suspend, relocate activities to areas where access is upheld; shift program resources to other areas.2- The safety and security of staff and beneficiaries are compromised. SKT uses rigorous security monitoring mechanism carried out by SKT staff and triangulated with information available through other sources, including the International NGO Safety Organization (INSO)3 - Borders are temporarily closed. SKT would suspend activities; advocate with UNOCHA and local authorities to facilitate access and operations; purchase locally if possible; eventually resort to remote Cash-based programming in extreme cases after OCHA and cluster approval4- Aid is diverted for personal or political gains. SKT mitigates aid diversion through a comprehensive set of rigorous management policies SKT would suspend activities; carry out investigations, advocate for dialogue for return/replacement of diverted aid; re-sensitize actors; reallocate funding to alternative target areas
