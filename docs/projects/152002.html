Project Summary

Going to the Tenth year of the crisis, over 7.7 million people are still in need of life saving assistance and protection, and 1.83 million people are internally displaced across the Northeast (DTM, August 2018). Despite some areas becoming more stable and accessible in 2018, thousands of new arrivals have been recorded in recent months, largely coming from hard to reach areas as a result of insecurity and military operations. Recent displacement to already-crowded areas has a compound effect on severely stretched services. Currently 41 formal sites across 12 LGAs in Borno are classed as in a ‘high congestion’ status, with 285,000 individuals above camp capacity (OCHA Situation Update, September 2018). In this context, protection needs remain severe, especially for vulnerable groups, children, and adolescents with protection concerns. More, for the 2019 Humanitarian Response Plan, the total population of children in need have been projected to be 3,138,643 while the HRP target is 1,505,647 children (564, 454 boys & 699,414 girls) and caregivers (133, 608 women and 108, 171 men).
From January 2019, GOALPrime Organization Nigeria is proposing to carry project that focuses on providing child care services to the vulnerable children in 10 Adolescents Friendly Spaces (Camps and Communities) in high priority LGAs of Gwoza, Monguno, Jere, MMC, Bama and Dikwa. The project target to reach 10000 community people with age, gender and culturally appropriate information on child care and child protection; identify and support 15 Community Youth centres, 1500 conflict affected Adolescents with integrated Adolescents Kits for Expression and Innovation in Emergencies Life-Skill programme; 260 conflict affected school children with integrated iDEALS Module Life-Skills Programme in schools; Train 40 school teachers on the implementation of the iDEALS Module and 100 Caregivers of Former CAAGs and SGBV Survivors on Life-Skills (Parents DEAL) as well as Post-re-unification Livelihood Support.
GOALPrime Organization Nigeria shall ensure that the grouping of the Adolescents are age, gender and most importantly, culturally appropriate. The adolescents shall be grouped into younger adolescents (10 - 14yrs) and Older Adolescents (15-18yrs) while for the iDEALS Module, beneficiaries shall be grouped into Children (11-15yrs) and young people (16 - 18yrs). GOALPrime Organization Nigeria shall also ensure that 10% of the target beneficiaries are those with special needs (Disable Adolescents).
GOALPrime will build on the existing community and government structure to develop a community base working and efficient support service to children and adolescents in camps and host communities of priority LGAs. GOALPrime will work in close coordination with the United Nations Children Fund which it has been supporting in the Capacity Building of Adolescents Kits and iDEAL Module Facilitators in Borno and Yobe States in 2018; as well as in the Roll out of the integrated Adolescents Kits Programme for Former CAAGs in the Borno State Rehabilitation Centre. GOALPrime will set up 12 community based child protection committees and trained them to ensure child protection minimum standards are maintained. To prevent and respond to family separation of children, the project will organize a community based sensitization of the families to mitigate family separations by providing information to family and children on how to prepare for mass movement as well as teaching children relevant family information.
GOALPrime shall implement this project with the lens of gender, taking into account how the conflict has affected the different gender dimensions such as girls, boys, women, men and the disabled. This will inform and justify the rational for our beneficiaries targeting. GOALPrime shall also deliver the relevant capacity building to both staff and beneficiaries that will foster the mitigation of Sexual exploitation and Abuse to Zero level all through the programme cycle of this project.
GOALPrime shall ensure access to Child Help Desk sessions in the Adolescents Centre. The project will also identify child headed families, women headed families and other vulnerable family and empower them with economic support and support other family to ensure their children remain in the school.

Needs

The protection and well-being of 2.6 million children and adolescents (1.4 million girls and 1.2 million boys) in the north-east remains seriously at risk mainly due to the complex and protracted conflict. The conflict in North-East Nigeria has exposed threats to the mental health and well-being of children. More than 2 million girls, boys and caregivers require psychosocial support services due to the severe distress, hardship and displacement. In addition, adolescent boys and girls require basic life skills support to help them cope better with the threats and challenges occasioned by the conflict. From the 2019 Humanitarian Needs Overview Data, the Child Protection Sub-sector working group has projected the total people in Need (PiN) in this project priority LGAs (MMC, Jere, Monguno, Bama, Dikwa and Gwoza) to be 937,780 while the people targeted for the 2019 HRP in these six priority LGAs of this project is 517, 625.In LGAs like Gwoza and Bama, out of school children have limited access to formal education hence the need to have them engaged in community Based Life-Skills Psychosocial Support Programmes using the iDEALS module translated in their primary languages of Bura, Hausa, Glavda and Kanuri. In Jere, three of the formal camps are classed as highly congested, and new arrivals are frequent (over 3,300 in 2018). Despite the large number of partners present in Jere LGA, there is still a need gap of over 57,000 people in need of Child Protection services (Majority of them being Adolescents and young People).Strongly putting the aforementioned context realities into consideration, there is immediate need for not just conventional Psychosocial support services but integrated Psychosocial Support Services with both Life-Skills and Livelihood Skills training as key components of the package. GOALPrime Organization Nigeria shall be using a community based approach to deliver both the Adolescents Kits and iDEAL Module Life-Skills Programme in target six LGAs.Furthermore, the conflict has not only disrupted the social fabric but it has also negatively affected the ability of parents to appropriately care for their children. At least 150,000 caregivers of vulnerable children, particularly female-headed households, foster careers and single caregivers with several children, will require psychosocial or socio-economic support to assist them to better cater for children in their care. Therefore, GOALPrime Organization Nigeria shall also target about 100 caregivers of vulnerable children with Life-Skills on good Parenting and also Livelihood Skills training and support to strengthen their resilience.
