Project Summary

This project is aiming at increasing access and strengthening capacity of PHC to provide emergency lifesaving health care in Leer, Mayendit, Koch and Panyijiar Counties through the provision of mobile response, PHCUs, RRMs and PHCCs with integrated responses. After the cessation of the most deadly attack to Mayendit north and Leer County between April to July 2018, the security situation has been relatively calmed in southern unity, however due to the massive displacement caused by April fighting, the local community lost more times in preparing and planting their farms, hence there was poor or no harvest in 2018, and for this reason, southern unity is expected to face a serious starvation which will lead to malnutrition increasing chances of morbidity and mortality rates in 2019. This project proposal will be targeting beneficiaries of 20, 627 individuals in Leer, 20,627 in Mayendit, 5,157 in Koch and 5,156 in Panyijiar counties.
This project will benefit all gender groups including, 28,599 men, 30,982 women, 33,564 girls and 30,982 boys across all the project catchment Counties.
Predictions from IPC findings show that 8 counties which include Leer and Mayendit may go into catastrophic levels which might further trigger diseases.
A SMART Survey conducted by UNIDO in Nov 2017 reported GAM rate at 17.9% and SAM at 3.5% in Mayendit county, while in a reference made to SCF SMART Survey conduct in April 2017 shows a prevalence of malnutrition rates among children in Leer county is high – estimated GAM prevalence of 20.1% (15.6-25.5 95% C.I.) and SAM of prevalence of 5.0% (3.2- 7.7 95% C.I.) based on weight-for-height/WFH z-score; this is alarming and classified as ‘Critical’. IDSR report of week 30 in Aug 2018 indicated a proportional morbidity at 67.9% for malaria, AWD, 9.3% and ARI with 8.8% while the same diseases are also leading in mortality rate, malaria with 55.6%, AWD 14.0% and ARI with 12.8% across the Country. UNIDO report on EWARS in the IDP site at Leer TPA has always shown a 100% in health cluster weekly IDSR report. The routine EPI services in Koch, Nyal and Leer TPA is progressing well with Penta3 coverage of 84 in Koch PHCC. Although due to insecurity, the entire County of Mayendit, most payams in Leer and Koch County are not benefiting yet from both routine and outreach EPI services. The provision of this primary health care service shall include OPD curative consultation in 7 mobile sites, 6 PHCUs, 2 RRMs and 2 PHCCs with integrated responses. The Seven mobile sites are Meer island, Tuochriak island, Tuochnhialchang island (in Leer County) Dhorleak and Dongol island (in Mayndit County), Gany and Gony (in Koch County) while the static sites are Bow, Dindin and Pillieny, (in Leer County) Pabuong and Dablual, (in Mayendit County) Duong and Majak (in greater Nyal of Panyijiar County). The project will support one stabilization center at Mayendit PHCC to treat SAM cases with medical complications. We will also support the psycho social and basic mental health services through updating/on-job training targeting nurses, midwives and clinicians on clinical management of sexual violence using SGBV treatment protocol and to offer the first aid such as protection and care for the survivors.

Needs

Mayendit, Leer, Koch and Panyijiar counties are located in the Central/Southern part of the former Unity state where the conflict inflicted enormous destruction both on the health care service delivery system and the general livelihood of the local people. After the cessation of the most deadly attack to Mayendit north and Leer County between April to July 2018, the security situation has been relatively calmed in southern unity, however due to the massive displacement caused by April fighting, the local community lost more times in preparing and planting their farms, hence there was poor or no harvest in 2018, and for this reason, southern unity is expected to face a serious starvation which will lead to malnutrition increasing chances of morbidity and mortality rates in 2019. This project proposal will be targeting beneficiaries of 20,627 individuals in Leer, 20,627 in Mayendit, 5,157 in Koch and 5,156 in Panyijiar counties. This project will benefit all gender groups including, 20,215 men, 21,040 women, 5,260 girls and 5,052 boys across all the project catchment Counties. UNIDO report on EWARS in the IDP site at Leer TPA has always shown a 100% in health cluster weekly IDSR report. The routine EPI services in Koch, Nyal and Leer TPA is progressing well with Penta3 coverage of 84 in Koch PHCC. UNICEF has installed a cold chain system in Leer TPA (Ghanbatt base) which was meant to serve the entire County of Leer and northern part of Mayendit County, unfortunately due to persistent insecurity in the areas, only few children and mothers in Leer TPA and town are benefitting from the routine EPI services. There is also a great need to strengthen and improve the HIV/AIDS & TB services in emergency, psycho-social supports and mental health services at the health facilities and the communities’ levels in addition to focus on GBV/CMR since much of the population has been subjected to numerous atrocities which includes rape, torture, killings and many other human rights abuses. UNIDOR program data and monitoring visit reports submitted to clusters continue to show great needs for comprehensive health intervention in this areas, and if funded we will ensure our mandate to serve the beneficiaries is upheld. UNIDO`s Strategic Response:This project will maintain and improve the existing essential emergency PHC services through providing BPHNS and emergency referral services by Mobile Response approaches at the communities levels in the islands and with focus on maternal and child health services (MCH). In collaboration with nutrition, Protection and WASH sectors.

Activities

1. Mobile teams: Conduct OPD consultations at facilities/mobile clinics levels. Do community awareness on the importance of health facility delivery by skilled birth attendants and refer pregnant to attend ANC services at health facility. Collect and submit a quality and timely weekly EWARS/IDSR reports to health cluster/WHO. Encourage mothers to bring their children for vaccination mainly with 3 doses of pentavalent. Conduct community consultative meetings to identify needs of the affected populations. Ensure mentally disturbed persons received mental health and psycho-social support. 2. RRM: Participate on UNICEF/OCHA led IRNA rapid response teams. Train 30 health workers trained on integrated Health, WASH and nutrition response. Train 30 health workers trained in emergency preparedness and response. 3. PHCU: Conduct OPD consultations at facilities/mobile clinics levels. Conduct normal deliveries at PHCU level. Compile and submit a quality and timely weekly EWARS/IDSR reports to health cluster and WHO. Ensure mothers bring their children for vaccination with 3 doses of pentavalent. Conduct community consultative meetings to identify needs of the affected populations. Ensure mentally ill persons received mental health and psycho-social support. 4.PHCC/ Upgraded PHCC: Conduct OPD consultations at the PHCC Conduct normal deliveries at the PHCC by skilled birth attendants. Compile and submit a quality and timely weekly EWARS/IDSR reports to health cluster and WHO. Ensure mothers bring their children for vaccination with 3 doses of pentavalent. Conduct community consultative meetings to identify needs of the affected populations. Ensure mentally ill persons received mental health and psycho-social support.
