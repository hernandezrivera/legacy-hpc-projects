Project Summary

The National Syrian Project for Prosthetic Limbs (NSPPL) aims to provide a platform to serve and support amputees inside Syria lead an independent and autonomous life. As of December 2017 based on the findings of the World Health Organization and Handicap International more 3 million people in the Syrian Arab Republic were living with physical injuries and disabilities (WHO and HI 2017). There is very little stable accessibility to medical and healthcare facilities. UNOCHA Situation Report #5: Recent Developments in Northwest Syria (as of 8 January 2020). NSPPL centers in Al Bab and Idlib provide critical support for this most vulnerable population to thrive and prosper. Of all Prosthetic and Orthotic patients served 70% are amputees the other 30% suffer from paralysis, war related injuries and non-communicable disease and conditions. The philosophy at the core of NSPPL is one to provide a house of healing and opportunity as such NSPPL focuses sustainability and service by educating local talent to serve as technicians and become care providers to those in need. NSSPL uses a holistic approach that provides sociological, psychological and physical rehabilitation. The NSSPL staff fully recognize the wounds and the scars suffered by our patients with are not merely physical they run much deeper and require a great deal of care and attention to help an individual achieve a sense of normalcy and agency. At the NSSPL we are interested in growth the growth of all of our beneficiaries the patient population as well as the personal and professional development of the staff. The project goals are two fold we focus on people with impaired mobility as well as training Prosthetic and Orthotic technicians to serve them. We provide prosthetic limbs, orthotic devices, and assistive devices, side by side with the appropriate psychological and physical rehabilitation.
Our targets are the Syrian local society and internally displaced people in the north of Idlib city and countryside and the northern Aleppo countryside, Al Baab. We serve children just a few days old born with congenital and non-communicable diseases to those who are suffering from war related amputations and paralysis. We serve and support beneficiaries regardless of age, race, religion, gender, sexual orientation, political affiliation, military service, sectarian affiliation.

4 - Needs

9 years of conflict in Syria, created a high number of disabilities especially the Impaired mobility, (Amputations and Paralysis).From this point, the idea of our project has been launched, to establish a Physiotherapy center that Provides Prosthetic and orthotic services side by side with training a new generation of P&O technicians.The center Provides P&O (prostheses and orthoses), PT (physical) therapy, PSS (psychological) support, social support and, if possible, also free transportation.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Assumptions1: the target areas are stable and no large-scale military operations are anticipated, procurement and shipment roads/lines are stable, and relations with the Ministry of Health and Ministry of education, and local authorities are accessible. Risk 1.1: Fighting between military groups in the area. Risk 1.2: Unexpected or bureaucratic barriers through the local authorities during project implementation.Risk 1.3: A new wave of displacement to the area. Risk1.4: deterioration of the general security situation in the project area. Risk 1.5: Duplication in services among the targeted locations. Risk 1.6: Difficulties in transporting the raw materials to the projects inside Syria.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Mitigation 1.1: Monitor the security situation on a daily basis, suspend, or evacuate depending on the severity of the situation. Mitigation 1.2: Maintain relationships with local authorities during the project implementation, with abiding with humanitarian principles and JOP. Mitigation 1.3: Follow-up of the security situation in the area and Integration with other stakeholders to provide emergency services. Mitigation1.4: Closely monitor and follow up security situations and coordinate with the education cluster and follow the instructions issued to maintain the safety of the project and its employees. Mitigation 1.5: NSPPL will coordinate with the health, protection, and education cluster and the active NGOs who work in the same target locations in the designing phase. In addition to the Education and Health Directorates to avoid any kind of duplications. Mitigation 1.6: Store materials inside Syria in a safe location.
