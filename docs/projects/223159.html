Project Summary

This intervention aims to promote food security and improve the livelihoods of farming families in the districts of Ancuabe, Mecufi, Macomia, Nangade , and Mocimboa da Praia in Cabo Delgado province, with a special focus on families displaced by conflict and host families. The primary objective is to equip farmers with good agricultural practices (GAP) and support income-generating activities, enhancing the resilience and sustainability of these families, who are affected by climate shocks, food insecurity, and social instability.
The intervention includes training in GAP, such as the use of quality seeds, soil management, pest control, irrigation techniques, and crop rotation. The focus on sustainable agricultural practices will increase productivity, improve product quality, and ensure stable food production. In addition, promoting income-generating activities like agricultural diversification and agro-processing will help families reduce dependence on a single source of income, creating multiple revenue streams and broadening economic opportunities.
The intervention also addresses the inclusion of women and youth, who play critical roles in agriculture but often face barriers to accessing resources and training. Gender equality and providing youth with opportunities in agriculture will be priorities, ensuring all community members are equally benefited. Furthermore, displaced families will be supported with access to agricultural resources and training, and will be integrated with host communities, fostering social cohesion and rebuilding support networks.
Ancuabe, Mecufi, Macomia, Nangade , and Mocimboa da Praia were chosen for the intervention due to their high vulnerability, exacerbated by the security crisis and climate challenges. With a clear focus on both displaced and host families, this intervention aims not only to improve agricultural production but also to create a more stable, inclusive, and prosperous environment, contributing to long-term resilience for affected communities. By empowering farming families, strengthening community support networks, and promoting sustainable practices, the intervention aims to make a significant impact on the improvement of livelihoods and food security in Cabo Delgado province.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

In many rural regions of Mozambique, particularly in Cabo Delgado province, agriculture is the primary source of livelihood for most families. However, various challenges such as limited access to modern agricultural knowledge, vulnerability to climate change, food insecurity, and the socio-economic impacts of conflict have put significant strain on the agricultural sector. These challenges are exacerbated in districts like Ancuabe,Mecufi, Macomia, Nangade e mocimboa da praia, where farming communities face increasing hardship, especially as conflict-induced displacement affects the stability of local populations. This project aims to enhance food security and improve the livelihoods of 12,000 beneficiaries providing training in good agricultural practices (GAP), promoting income-generating activities, and supporting community resilience. The focus will be on smallholder farmers, displaced families, and host communities. By equipping these groups with the skills and resources necessary to increase productivity and diversify income sources, the intervention will help strengthen the economic resilience of the population and reduce vulnerability to food insecurity. The overarching goal of this intervention is to improve food security and enhance livelihoods in the target communities by providing support and resources that strengthen agricultural production and create alternative income streams. • Increase the productivity of smallholder farmers, displaced families and host communities through training in good agricultural practices and access to appropriate farming tools, seeds, and techniques. • Encourage families to diversify their income sources through the introduction of alternative livelihoods, including small-scale livestock farming. • Ensure that farming families have access to sufficient, nutritious food by promoting sustainable agricultural practices that ensure stable and diverse food production. • Provide specific support to displaced families, integrating them into the local economy and promoting their participation in agriculture and income-generating activities. • Strengthen the social fabric between displaced families and host communities by building trust, cooperation, and mutual support. As the key target groups are the smallholder farmers, displaced families, and host communities. Together, these groups will benefit from the project’s efforts to improve agricultural productivity, enhance resilience, and promote sustainable livelihoods. Smallholder farmers represent the majority of the agricultural population in Ancuabe and Macomia. These farmers often rely on traditional farming methods and have limited access to modern agricultural knowledge, resources, and markets. Many of these farmers face challenges such as low crop yields, poor soil quality, inadequate pest management, and vulnerability to climate change, which often results in food insecurity.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

To mitigate potential risks and avoid causing harm, into account the current conflict situation in Cabo Delgado the project incorporates several safeguards: The project will ensure that all activities are conflict-sensitive, meaning they will be designed to avoid exacerbating tensions between displaced families and host communities. Activities will promote inclusion, respect for local cultures, and peaceful coexistence. Community dialogues and peacebuilding initiatives will be central to fostering understanding and cooperation. The project will continuously monitor the security situation in both Ancuabe, Mecufi, Macomia, Nangade e Mocimboa da praia. Activities will be adjusted if necessary to ensure the safety of beneficiaries and staff. For example, training sessions and distributions will be organized in secure areas, and travel will be planned according to local safety conditions. All care will be taken to ensure equitable resource distribution among both displaced families and host communities, avoiding exacerbating existing inequalities. The project will engage local leaders and community representatives throughout its implementation to ensure activities are culturally appropriate and locally accepted. This will help avoid any misunderstandings and ensure that interventions are aligned with the needs and priorities of the communities.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The proposed project prioritizes meaningful access to assistance by ensuring that all beneficiaries, especially vulnerable groups such as women and the elderly, can fully participate in the activities. Outreach efforts will be tailored to reach these groups, ensuring they are not excluded from training, resources, or decision-making processes. The project will utilize community leaders to help identify and engage women and elderly persons, ensuring they have access to information, support, and the resources needed to benefit from the project. Women will be empowered through targeted initiatives that provide them with leadership roles in agricultural activities, access to income-generating opportunities, and specific training on agro-processing and entrepreneurship. The project will also facilitate the formation of women’s groups to ensure they can collectively address challenges, share knowledge, and access resources that improve their economic standing. Special focus will be placed on ensuring women have equal access to training and materials, reducing the gender gap in agricultural productivity and income generation. For elderly individuals, the project will offer tailored support by considering their physical limitations and specific needs. This includes providing assistance in accessing agricultural tools, resources, and training that are adaptable to their capabilities. In addition, the project will encourage the inclusion of elderly persons in decision-making processes and community dialogues, ensuring their voices are heard and their traditional knowledge is valued. By fostering an inclusive environment, the project aims to create a platform for both women and the elderly to participate actively in the improvement of their livelihoods and communities.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The project will identify people with disabilities through close collaboration with local community leaders, who have knowledge of individuals with disabilities in the target areas. A comprehensive needs assessment will be conducted to identify the specific barriers faced by people with disabilities, such as limited mobility, lack of accessible facilities, or difficulty in participating in agricultural training and income-generating activities. To remove these barriers, the project will ensure that all activities, including training and resource distribution, are inclusive and accessible. The project will also adapt to meet the specific needs of people with disabilities, offering assistive devices where necessary. Additionally, the project will raise awareness about disability inclusion within the community to promote acceptance and understanding, reducing stigma and discrimination. By actively engaging people with disabilities in the design and implementation of activities, the project will empower them to fully participate in decision-making processes, ensuring that their needs are adequately addressed and that they benefit equally from the project’s support.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project is committed to minimizing its environmental impact while promoting sustainable agricultural practices. It will primarily focus on enhancing local agricultural productivity through techniques that improve soil health, conserve water, and reduce environmental degradation. The introduction of sustainable practices such as agroecology, crop rotation, and organic farming will help preserve the natural environment, enhance biodiversity, and reduce dependency on chemical inputs. To mitigate potential negative environmental impacts, such as soil erosion or water depletion, the project will implement conservation measures like, small-scale irrigation setups, and erosion control techniques. Additionally, environmental assessments will be conducted at the onset of the project to identify potential risks and ensure that the planned activities do not harm local ecosystems. Regular monitoring of environmental conditions will be carried out to track the effectiveness of these measures. Furthermore, the project will encourage environmental awareness among beneficiaries, emphasizing the importance of sustainable land use practices. Training will include topics on environmental conservation, waste management, and the responsible use of natural resources, ensuring that the community adopts practices that contribute to long-term environmental sustainability.
