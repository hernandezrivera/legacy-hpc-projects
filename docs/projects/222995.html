Project Summary

1. The overall objective of this project is to Reduce excess morbidity and mortality; prepare, prevent, detect and timely respond to epidemic-prone & endemic diseases, driven by climatic shocks and conflict among IDPs and non-displaced, in Terekeka and Juba Counties. to strengthen health system recovery and coping mechanisms among Vulnerable persons 14,857 (male 7428& female 7428) due to pressing circumstances including insecurity caused by intercommunal and cattle related conflicts, climate chan flooding and drought, stress of food insecurity and essenti service gaps. both counties have inadequate health infrastructure, underdeveloped and limited capacity of the health care systems, such as a shortage of qualified human resource for health, and inconsistent medical supplies, this exposes the vulnerable communities to negative coping strategies, (dependents on traditional doctors, excessive use of local herbs and late health care seeking behaviors) The project will establish mobile health to serve hard-to-reach locations where health services are disrupted.
a. the mobile health clinic teams will be involved in conducting OPD consultations including diagnosis, treatment of common communicable, management cases of SGBV (Clinical Management of rape) referrals of cases for further management, Nurses will provide basic nursing care, taking patient’s vital signs, dressing of wounds, establishing Oral Rehydration Corner and carry out vaccinations of children. The Lab Assistants will carry out malaria tests using RDTs in the current setting. The Dispenser’s role is to issue drugs to patients as prescribed by the Clinical officers.
b. Conduct Epidemiology disease surveillance, this will be done through general Health education and mobilization for routine, immunization, screening of under 5 for malnutrition with MUAC and lactating women for malnutrition, including rapid testing for malaria for under 5 and pregnant women as well as Screening for Tetanus vaccination of pregnancy, vaccination needs to all under 5 and TB Screening,
c. Communicable Diseases ONO AID will work to prevent, detect, and respond to infectious diseases including case management. In addition, the services are carried out through the mobile clinics’ sites. National MOH treatment guidelines will be used for case management. RDT Para-checks for malaria and syphilis will be done at mobile outreach sites. Weekly reporting on data collected to Health Clusters at both State and National levels on common morbidities like Malaria, Acute Watery Diarrhea, Measles, and cholera reporting.
d. Mother and Child Health. ONO AID will provide maternal and child health care activities guided by MISP and BPHNS of the National Ministry of Health. At each mobile clinic site, a midwife will provide ANC, skilled community deliveries, post-natal care within 7 days, family planning, STI/HIV prevention and management, and GBV prevention & management. ONO AID will ensure that other commodities like trauma and rape kits for the management of rape cases, family planning commodities, and Mama Kits are distributed, the midwife will conduct counseling on the birth preparedness plan and do screening of pregnant women for malnutrition during the ANC services and those found malnourished referred for nutritional services at the respective targeted Payams. immunization will be done twice a week every Monday and Thursday until the project period.
