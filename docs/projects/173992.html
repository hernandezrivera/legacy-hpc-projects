Project Summary

Initiative for Development and Empowerment Axis (IDEA) having valid ATW and MoU with EAD Govt of Pakistan. The proposed intervention aims to enhance the self-reliance and livelihoods of target populations (Afghan refugees in 4 RVs in District Kohat) through the specialized assets distribution approach. IDEA is already delivering services in the fields of livelihood, education, in RVs at Kohat and Mansehra. The project aims to benefit 600 refugees Households by providing specialized assets, development of market linkages, capacity building on enterprise development and 50,000 individuals through RCCE awareness in Kohat. Thus the total beneficiaries will be 66,200 refugees.
IDEA will implement the proposed project from its regional office at District Kohat and will target the most economically vulnerable households (POR Cards Holders) identified through poverty score. The Project will have separate PMU, staff and bank account. Prior to the start of the implementation of the activities, IDEA will provide orientation staff to ensure that the project objectives and implementation methodology as well as standards of quality, conduct, financial management, procurement and reporting are fully understood. “UN-OCHA“ will be invited to participate in the orientation sessions to provide guidance as needed.
Duration: 08 months
Selection and Implementation Process:
The selection criteria for targeting refugee will be; unemployed daily labourers, low income families, a household headed by women/child, having PwD and chronic diseases with no source of income, with more than three children. IDEA will also include families with children involved in labor, unaccompanied, separated and orphaned children, children that are at risk of or have experienced abuse or neglect, and adolescent girls, their caregivers and communities, in the relief packages assessment. The project team will conduct the detail assessment and the beneficiaries will be selected through poverty score card. The list of the selected beneficiaries will be shared with UNHCR for further verification and removal of duplication. IDEA team will also make sure the proper follow-up with the beneficiaries and will provide assistance in developing market linkages and providing technical assistance in establishment of different enterprises. Beside this IDEA will also develop the capacity of the Community Resource Persons and Shura members on the specialized enterprise development, the trained personals will also provide assistance to the beneficiaries in the development of small scale businesses. Further for ensuring the sustainability, IDEA will train each beneficiary on the utilization of specialized asset and increase their production.
M&E:
M&E officer will be inducted under the entire guidance of M&E Manager for ensuring regularly monitoring of the progress, process, quick impact of the planned activities, etc. and will provide support to project team in proper reporting to capture the relevant and required information.
Complaint mechanism will be established, whereby all the participants and end beneficiaries will be encouraged to record their feedbacks/complaints directly to the project team on the spot, put complaint in written in compliant boxes to be installed in the project area or record their compliant through phone or sms. Confidentiality will be ensured, proper response will be given to the feed backs from beneficiaries and the whole process will be recorded. The beneficiaries will be selected in a transparent way as per the agreed criteria without the influence of all stakeholders.
Coordination
IDEA will maintain close coordination with the existing partners of UNHCR working in the livelihood sector, CAR, UNOCHA office through Cluster meetings, progress review & joint field visits. Monthly progress updates will be shared with HRP. IDEA will also share the details of beneficiaries with for removal of duplication in future.
