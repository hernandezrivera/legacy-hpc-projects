Project Summary

The project aims to contribute to the reduction of the vulnerability of IDPs and host communities hosting IDPs, affected by conflict in Macomia, by strengthening access to safe water, sanitation facilities and hygiene promotion, through a strong community-based strategy to access the most vulnerable people in hard to reach areas.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

ACF will implement water, hygiene and sanitation activities through activists (community volunteers) and dedicated community mechanic teams (including women, volunteers and local hand pump mechanics), as most of the areas in Macomia are hard to reach due to the volatile security. ACF will work in collaboration with the Serviço Distrital de Planeamento e Infra-estruturas (SDPI) and the District Services of Women, Social Affairs and Health (SDMAS) to identify the activists, the volunteers, and the mechanics that will be trained in Ancuabe in the initial stages of the project. The support to the community mechanic teams and SDPI to do rehabilitation of existing water points (hand pumps on borehole) and re-mobilizing the water committees aims at having a sustainable management of the water points at the community level.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The project will improve the sanitation conditions of relocation sites by building 100 emergency sanitation facilities (latrines and bathing areas) including hand washing stations. This will be done through contractors who are identified with SDPIs’ support. Activists will promote proper management and cleanness of the facilities at community level and will provide some materials to support the community. Regarding sanitation for IDPs living in host communities, a subsidized CLTS approach is preferred, where displaced communities will build their own family latrines and bathing area.ACF will train activists in order to promote safe and appropriate hygiene and sanitation practices to IDPs and host communities. Use of certeza for water treatment and soap for hand washing will be promoted and distributed together with the hygiene kits (targeted distribution based on vulnerability criteria defined with the community) to ensure safe drinking water and handwashing with soap at household level. Activists will also train community mobilizers and leaders in order to replicate the messages within the community. ACF will identify and train a community mechanic team, in coordination with the SDPI. Women will be strongly encouraged to participate, as well as voluntary community members. The team will also be composed of local hand pump mechanics. Together with a local contractor and the SDPI, ACF will identify and rehabilitate 10 water points.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

ACF will mainstream gender and protection into the humanitarian response in several ways to reduce the vulnerability of IDPs and host communities, as well as mitigate the risks they face. For instance, the voices of women and girls will be enhanced throughout to promote their safety and access to humanitarian services. They would actively participate in decision-making on subjects such as community-based activities, health services.The project will promote linha verde service and foster its use among the beneficiaries. Promotion activities will include the scope of the service, costs and practical examples. The project will consult with affected populations (not only community leaders) to identify specific needs for vulnerable groups. Updates on the project will be shared with the affected populations through the available feedback mechanism including community meetings. The project will be monitored by the MEAL department, which will also support it in a risk analysis in view of the situation close to the beneficiaries, in order to guarantee respect for accountability, the PSEA policy, the staff's code of conduct and the policy on child protection.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

ACF will regularly consult affected communities and key stakeholders to ensure that the program is designed in a way that is responsive to the needs and preferences of the affect population. All hygiene distributions will include items for menstrual hygiene management as per the WASH Cluster standard hygiene kit. The location of WASH infrastructure will be done through consultations with the elderly, the disabled and women and girls to ensure that these groups feel safe in using these facilities.The project will promote linha verde service and foster its use among the beneficiaries. Promotion activities will include the scope of the service, costs and practical examples. Accountability will be also guaranteed by a rapid and proper response to the feedback received by linha verde.Post distribution monitoring surveys will include also specific questions related with sexual abuse and harassment related with distributions.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The implementation of the inclusive approach will be guaranteed in a transversal way to the project. Inclusion, on the implementation of all project activities, will include a follow-up on the "triptych of inclusion", thus taking into account the aspects of gender, age and disability.For the inclusion and advocacy activities, coordination within the Disability working group will be ensured with the Pemba protection sub-cluster, OCHA Pemba and Maputo disability working groups. ACF will implement the WGs recommendations, and guarantee equal access to services for all.Key messages disseminated during training sessions with health/nutrition staff and filed visits will emphasize the importance of achieving equivalent health outcomes for both girls and boys, and PWDs. As part of their onboarding process, ACF staff will be trained and sensitized on the importance of narrowing the gender gap, the inclusion of PWDs and shifting attitudes toward gender-based violence, exploitation and harmful societal and cultural norms.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Specific session will be implemented during the hygiene promotion activities: importance of waste management and its impact on the environment and health, importance of water source and water conservation. By rehabilitating water points and supporting water management committees for the repair and maintenance and providing sensitization to hygiene with tailored activities (WASH’Em), the project also promotes sustainability.
