Project Summary

The entire world is grappling with the unprecedented spread of COVID-19. The novel Coronavirus (COVID-19) was first reported from Wuhan City, Hubei Province of China, on 31 December 2019, and was reported as cases of pneumonia with unknown cause. The disease was declared a global pandemic by the World Health Organization on the 11th March 2020; As of the 20th of May, there have been 4,9M confirmed cases and 323,000 deaths globally .
Zimbabwe has not been spared by the COVID-19 pandemic. On the 20th of March 2020, Zimbabwe recorded its first case, the second case was recorded on the 21st of March in Harare which was followed by the death of the same on the 23rd of March. As of 20th May 2020, Zimbabwe tested 1,291 suspected individuals and 46 of these tested positive for COVID-19.
Despite few confirmed cases recorded, it is important to put in WASH measures to reduce the spread and Infection Prevention and Control (IPC). Improve awareness of COVID-19, safe hygiene and sanitation practices in high risk communities and access to WASH facilities in Health Care centers/ Isolation centres supporting COVID-19 response. This will be achieved by capacitating health facilities for IPC through provision of basic water and sanitation facilities, setup of handwashing stations in public spaces, distribute hygiene kits as well as promote hygiene in communities. The communities through local stakeholders have been engaged to understand their needs and the activities required to address these. The project will deliberately target households with young children and children with SAM and MAM and people living with disabilities who are in need of hygiene to reduce exposure to more diseases such as Covid 19, as well as ensure beneficiary feedback mechanism during and after implementation.
