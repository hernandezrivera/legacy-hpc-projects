Project Summary

PUI’s FOCOS strategy programming continues to be undertaken in a multi-year strategic way, with 2020 representing the sixth year of implementation of PUI’s integrated-area based approach (IABA) in Syria. Through this approach, PUI make sures to create synergies between the different interventions implemented in areas in need in order to respond to the needs of the Population of Syria as a whole and to help them to go back to their pre-crisis standards of living, to enhance self-sufficiency and resilience in the long run and to promote social cohesion.
In line with SO1 and SO2, this project aims at meeting the basic WASH needs of the conflict affected population in targeted locations by enhancing their access to safe and adequate water supply and to adequate sanitation services, while fostering sustainability through the rehabilitation of community infrastructures

4 - Needs

According to the 2021 HNO, 12.2 million Syrians are estimated to be in need of WASH support. To better understand the needs on the ground, PUI obtained information from primary and secondary resources in targeted locations, and identified an acute need for WASH support; in many locations debris are blocking the smooth movement of population, basic WASH infrastructures are out of services due to destruction and lack of local municipalities support to secure spare parts, People are relying on expensive water trucking or unsafe sources for water consumption to meet their needs. Sewage networks also are partially damaged. People are using septic tanks to dispose waste water, and are collectively renting tanks that draw the water from those septic tanks to a proper disposal location. This is not only very costly for vulnerable people but those tanks are also flooding in the streets, exposing the population to health hazards and the spread of insects and rodents, which is negatively impacting the community public health, in addition to the health risk of mix between leakage sewage water and ground potable water in points of damages.

5 - Implementation of activities

Rehabilitation of water supply systems: to ensure that all individuals targeted under this activity have safe access to clean, sufficient and adequate water supply. The work will include:1. Civil works including the excavation, backfilling and paving works in the damaged area where the broken pipes will be replaced2. Installing new water pipes (different types, lengths and diameters)3. All needed accessories.4. Water tree connections for each building.Rehabilitation of sewage systems: to improve the sanitation conditions of the populations living in the targeted area. The work will include: 1. Maintenance & rehabilitation of manholes.2. Execution of new rain water manholes.3. Execution of rain water pipes.4. Cleaning of manholes.5. Cleaning of sewage pipes.6. installing new sewage manholes and sewage pipes if needed.Garbage and Debris removal: The objective of the intervention is to ensure that populations in targeted areas are living in safe and healthy environments, thus reducing public health risks. The work will include:1- Debris / garbage removal from streets and roads: Accumulation of the debris / garbage in the streets using shovel (wheel loader) Filling accumulated debris / garbage in the trucks and removing away to the outside landfills.2- Debris removal from building: Accumulation of the debris inside the apartments Removal of debris from the buildings, accumulating in front of buildings, subsequently removing the accumulated debris away to the outside landfills using wheel loaders and trucks. Water trucking: to temporary response is to ensure safe supply of clean water to relieve the suffering of beneficiaries and improve health and hygiene conditions for individuals living in the area

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk: Exposure to SGBV due to movements of water trucks in congested communities (Likelihood: High, impact: Low). To mitigate this PUI will consult communities (separately men and boys and women and girls) to adapt the location of distribution points, delivery routes and schedule; to avoid installing water points in narrow streets and congested areas; to establish clear routes and schedule for water trucking, to avoid school areas during the time children travel to/from school; to conduct safety training with truck drivers/operators.Risk: child labour among contractors, (Likelihood: High, impact: High) Mitigation measures: to ensure proper registration system for staff/partners/contractors’ labourers; add zero tolerance disclosure on child labour in the contracts; check and register civil documentation.

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumptions; No curfew or lockdown due to COVID-19 pandemic, Stable security situation , Consistent humanitarian access , No major fluctuation of Syrian Pound, No mass movements of populations , Availability of material in the local market and finally acceptance of community. Risk: COVID-19 new waves and lockdown (Likelihood: medium, impact: high) Mitigation measures; 1) Good coordination with the concerned stakeholders to anticipate such measures. 2) full adherence to WHO recommendations. Risk: shortage of Fuel (Likelihood: medium, impact: high) Mitigation measures: rely on private transportation means, more activate remote management and decrease the site visits, Risk: Quality and/or quantity of water not adequate (Likelihood: High, Impact: High) Mitigation measures: to ensure provision of safe water and monitor water quality including water source, water trucks Risk: Potential exposure to explosive hazard (IED/UXO/ERW) (Likelihood: Medium, Impact: High) Mitigation measures: to work closely with Mine actors and conduct works in cleared areas only; to conduct safety training with staff and contractors - Risk: Theft of WASH rehabilitation/construction materials by armed groups and used in conflict activities ( likelihood: low, Impact High ) Mitigation measures to sign MoUs with local ministries to ensure local ownership pf martials & facilities to promote cost recovery systems for longer terms maintenance and to promote community ownership .
