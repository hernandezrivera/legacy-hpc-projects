Project Summary

An estimated 2.9 million people are displaced in Somalia (2021 Humanitarian Needs Overview). The displaced population’s coping mechanisms have been affected over successive years of drought, floods and conflict, leaving them vulnerable to shocks and inhibiting a sustainable return to places of origin. Of the total displaced population, 2,241,328 million people are living in IDP sites with the most severe needs (2022 CCCM Cluster PiN). An estimated 336,199 (15%) of them are persons with various disabilities while 47% of the displaced are women. IDP sites lack dignified living conditions, suﬃcient access to basic services, land tenure and adequate resources to sustain their populations.
A large proportion of humanitarian aid in Somalia targets IDP sites and camp-like settings that accommodate some of the most vulnerable populations in the country. The coordination of site-level activities, in addition to general service monitoring and site management support, largely relies on CCCM partner expertise. Consequentially, given the sheer volume of IDP sites in Somalia (over 2,400 IDP sites), there is a pertinent need to support the coordination of CCCM actors addressing priority needs within districts/regions of the country. The CCCM cluster actively works to ensure that partners are delivering services in a coordinated manner that fosters high service quality.
Moreover, the cluster plays a vital role in developing the capacity of government, humanitarian and development actors working to explore sustainable solutions for IDPs living in camp-like settings. Lastly, the cluster’s role in inter-cluster coordination is imperative through highlighting, analyzing and raising key trends in displacement patterns while monitoring the quality of services at the site-level.

1. - Projects activities

This HRP project aims at maintaining national and sub-national cluster coordination responsibilities with IOM’s continued co-chair role. Under this project, IOM will provide 1 national CCCM Cluster Coordinator, and1 national CCCM Cluster Information Management Oﬃcer, in addition to 3 sub-national focal points (Kismayo, Gedo and Baidoa). The CCCM cluster will continue to play an active role at the national level and via the Inter-Cluster Coordination Group (ICCG) by advocating for improved living conditions in IDP sites and strategic fundraising decisions allowing for partners to target IDP sites and populations articulated within the sectors’ HRP. Furthermore, the CCCM cluster will strive to improve both intra-and inter-sector coordination at the sub-national level through the technical support of cluster focal points in seven geographic states within the country. These initiatives will link with durable solutions programming when possible, aspiring for the achievement of durable solutions. Through the CCCM cluster team, partners will be supported with a range of high-quality programmatic tools, which will be revised and updated on a needs basis. Additionally, capacity-building support will target both CCCM partners, local authorities and other humanitarian/development partners with the aim of enriching the understanding of CCCM principles, capacity to implement in line with these principles, and establishing a clear exit plan for the sector. Under these activities, the cluster will promote localization measures to strengthen the participation of national partners. The information management oﬃcer will assist in compiling data to form baseline data for functional and verified IDP sites, in addition to actively contributing data for the establishment of the overall IDP population within Somalia which is done yearly. Furthermore, the information management oﬃcer will create information products that visualize some of the important data categories captured by partners such as needs/gap analysis, complaints feedback mechanism trends and site verification updates. Lastly, the CCCM cluster will look to streamline durable solutions activities within the overall CCCM approach for Somalia to operationalize the humanitarian/development nexus.
