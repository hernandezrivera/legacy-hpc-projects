Project Summary

This project will address the educational needs of Rohingya Refugees in Camps 13 and 16. As per the report of Education sector dated 29.10.2019 show that over 2137 children aged 3-14 are out of education in these camps due to shortage of learning facilities and need to be established 22 Learning Centers across these camps. This project aims to provide basic education to 1000 Rohingya refugee children in Cox’s Bazar (4-5 years old: 350; 6-14 years old: 650) through Temporary Learning Centers (TLCs), with capacity development initiatives for their teachers and facilitators to deliver high quality teaching and learning to the children. Community participation will also be ensured through Learning Center Management Committees, which will include representatives from the Rohingya Community. Learning Center Management Committee (LCMC) will meet monthly basis. For awareness raising among the parents on rights of education, importance of education, WASH, early marriage, CRC, all are equal etc , Parents meeting will conduct bi-monthly every learning Center. ISDE has ongoing 10 learning centre at the camp 13 and under this project it will continue for further and its need to repair and maintenance the existing learning centre. Teaching materials will also be ensured from education cluster and local sources as per guideline of ISCG Education cluster.

Objectives

[1] Expand and strengthen immediate access to equitable learning opportunities, in a safe and protective environment, for crisis-affected refugee and host community girls and boys aged 3-24 years old. [2] Provide quality inclusive education to refugee and host community girls and boys aged 3-24 years old, aligned with Education Sector standards, and increase teaching-related professional development opportunities. [3] Ensure refugee and host community ownership and active participation and engagement in the education of girls and boys aged 3-24 years old.

Needs

Despite significant progress made these last two years in providing safe and systematic access to learning opportunities, 69 % of the households reported at least one 5-17 years old aged child not attending any learning opportunities (JMSNA, 2019). Furthermore, an alarming 89% of the adolescents and youth aged 15-24 years old don’t have access to any educational activities.Although 71% of the community (REACH/ENA 2019) consider that the education situation has improved in the camps over the last 12 months, children and parents are reluctant to pursue their education, especially as the Learning Competency and Framework Approach levels 3-4 are yet to be approved and around 30% of the children currently enrolled in level 2, will be enrolled at the level 3 in 2020.But as per the latest report of Education sector on education gap analysis in refugee camps show that over 2137 children (minimum 50% female child) aged 3-14 in Camps 13 and 16 are out of education due to insuﬃcient learning facilities. ISDE will address these critical gaps in education in these refugee camps by operating 10 safe and equipped learning centres with WASH facilities for 505 female child and 495 male children. Basic education for children aged between 4-14 years and awareness rasining session for parents and local community will be priveded under the proposed project. This project will address the needs of children and parents/caregivers in camps by incorporating participatory approaches, beneﬁciary feedback mechanisms, and alternative delivery methods tailored towards persons with speciﬁc needs into programme design and implementation (as per Inter-Agency Network for Education in Emergencies (INEE) standards. An additional needs assessment will establish the exact needs of the different communities. This will be followed by regular assessments that assess whether the programme is meeting the population’s needs and promoting accountability to the affected populations. This will be complemented with regular monitoring of enrollment rates, retention, teacher`s attendance and improvements in learning outcomes.

Activities or outputs

1. Repairing and Maintenance of Learning Canters in Rohingya Camps; ISDE has 10 learning centre at Rohingya camps. The existing LCs are need to repair and maintenance for further use. The LCs required regular maintenance works. ISDE will renovate the LCs as per need and WASH facilities will ensured. 2. Provision of high-quality learning and educational Materials for Rohingya children at learning center (LC); Necessary Education and extra curriculum activities materials will be supplied as per guidelines and instruction by the ISCG Education cluster and RRRC office. 3. Collection and distribution of learning materials to Rohingya children: ISDE will develop teaching learning material (like bag, Khata etc) for learners and also collect some materials (like Books, reading materials) from Education Sector for distribution among the learners ; 4. Formation of Learning Center Management Committees-LCMC (with inclusion of women) and arrangement of community engagement activities (including monthly progress review and feedback sharing meetings); 5. Foundation and Monthly refresher training for Teachers and Safeguarding training for staffs and Learning Centre Management Committee-LCMCs: ISDE will arrange 07 day long foundation training for all teachers and staff. Monthly day long refresher training will organize in every month for develop skill of teachers. ISDE also arranged a day long safeguarding training for teachers, staff, member of Learning Center Management Committee-LCMC and parents under this project; 6. Bi-Monthly Parents meeting: ISDE will arrange parents meeting in every learning center on bi-monthly basis for ensuring community participation, raise awareness on education, UNCRC, personal health and hygiene, gender based violence etc issues; 7. Coordination with Sector and GoB department: ISDE will coordinate with Camp In Charge ( CiC), Upazilla and District administration, education sector and other stakeholders regularly to keep updates the progress of the learning centers and ensure their participation in the project activities.
