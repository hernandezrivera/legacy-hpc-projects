1 - Needs

This project is designed for the IDPs to Ensuring better services and assistance in accessing vital facilities such as drinking water and water used for washing and conducting some sessions onawareness of the importance of personal hygiene and how to contribute tocreating a more healthy environment and how it is possible to provideassistance to some of them through awareness of the dangers that threatenthe level of personal hygiene, aims at this type of Activities to enhance thefeeling of individuals that they are playing an important role in society, byinvolving them in maintaining general hygiene and this leads to increasedintegration and interaction in the environment, which provides them with theappropriate living conditions for living.

2 - Activities

- Rehabilitation of water facilities in collective and safe shelters, and/or other priority areas - Water quality monitoring - HY promotion - PSS activities - Safe evacuation training - Provision of NFIs and HY kits - WASH technical supplies- Fumigation- Solid waste management
