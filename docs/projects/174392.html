Project Summary

Following the devastating torrential monsoon rains in the Sindh province, many areas in the province are reported to be flooded with rising water destroying households’ homes, livestock and belongings. The Sindh Government announced a state of emergency on the 25th August 2020, with 20 districts declared calamity-affected. One million people are affected according to the PDMA, Sindh, 30 percent of whom are estimated to be in need of food assistance based on the findings of the rapid needs assessment conducted in August, 2020 by the Sindh UN provincial team on the request of the PDMA Sindh in 9 districts of the province including 5 rural districts and 4 districts of Karachi. Sanghar district is among the worst affected districts in Sindh province in the light of recent flood assessments conducted. According to one of the rapid need assessment carried out by Sindh Rural Support Organization (SRSO) in Sanghar district in September, 2020, 87,643 households are affected by the floods in the district which corresponds to 17% of the district population. A total of 7,972 houses are fully damaged and 6,509 are partially damaged by the floods. 627 livestock are reported to be killed including 592 goats. Agriculture crops including cotton, rice, chilli and sugarcane are destroyed partially or completely in affected areas with crop damages reported up to 90 percent in some areas. In the wake of the recent floods, SIF conducted a rapid needs assessment in the flood affected areas in the province in September 2020. The immediate needs of affected populations in terms of life saving assistance include:
• Food packages for affected population for support up to 3 months.
• Emergency cash based interventions for affected population (unconditional cash assistance).
• Safe drinking water.
• NFIs (jerry cans, water cooler, plastic sheets, utensils, mosquito nets etc.).
• Emergency shelters and debris removal from houses, roads and pathways.
• Fodder for livestock and vaccination.
• Awareness regarding health and hygiene, COVID-19, mother and child health care, etc.
• Winterization kits for the displaced households as the season is changing and winter has started. as
The situation after the floods in the district is expected to continue market disruptions, adversely affect agricultural activities and worsen the food security situation of the communities.
Tharparkar district is facing a chronic drought situation due to significant reduction of rainfalls during the past couple of years. This has negatively impacted both agriculture and livestock conditions in the area, increasing the stress on local population to meet the demand for basic resources for their survival. For instance, in the Daheli sub-district, 34% of the households own 3-6 acres of land and cultivate their land during the monsoon season to ensure their livelihoods. 66% of the households do not have their own agricultural land and grow cereals (guwar, millet) and beans on a sharing basis along with seasonal vegetables and are subjected to variable rainfall. Due to a prolonged drought crisis, the water resources have dried up, which has adversely impacted the agriculture productivity. In addition to this, land owners do not have the capacity to grow crops on their own land as it is high risk and high invest in the prevailing drought situation. The recent locust infestations in 2019 and 2020 also aggravated the situation of agriculture in the area.
The summary of the latest situation analysis report in post COVID-19 in District Tharparkar, Sindh conducted by SIF in July, 2020 is as follows:
• The current monthly household income is estimated as PKR 7,851 (USD 47.044 @ 1 USD=166.887 PKR) in Tharparkar during the assessment. The household income is far less than the international poverty line per person two dollars per day.
• 91% of the respondents in the households said that COVID-19 and lock down situation impacted the employment situation of their household members.

1- Project Activities

The proposed project aims to address the food security and livelihoods needs of the families affected by floods, the COVID-19 situation and drought in Sanghar, Mirpurkhas and Tharparkar districts of Sindh.Under the proposed project, SIF aims to provide immediate support for food security, livelihoods, and agriculture and livestock services through the following activities:1.1. Provision of un-conditional cash grants to disaster affected families:Unconditional cash grants will be given to 3,000 families for the monthly food assistance for initial 4 months. This will give privilege to the affected families to access the preferred food items of their need from the nearest market. This cash grants will also strengthen the local market economy in the target areas through circulation of cash among local communities.1.2. Provision of un-conditional cash grant to women handed, orphan, elder’s person with disability.At least 500 women headed, orphans, elders, persons with disabilities and extremely vulnerable households will be prioritized for the provision of cash grants. Cash will be transferred in three equal tranches of PKR 9,000 in totalthe following will be the criteria for selection of beneficiaries:For immediate recovery needs, lifesaving relief food assistance will be provided to address their immediate hunger needs and nutritional requirements. The cash transfer support will protect and enable the restoration, protection and access to livelihood assets.2.1. Provision of agriculture support:The majority of the families have their agriculture land and they have been cultivating their lands before disaster hit, but currently they are not cultivating their agriculture land due to lack of inputs (equipment, seeds, knowledge) and due to financial constraints. Agriculture support will be provided to 500 potential families who have cultivable agriculture land in their possessions. The farmer families will be assisted with agriculture inputs as per identified needs including but not limited to seeds, tools, land preparation, etc. The agriculture package will be of PKR 18,000/ targeted family2.2. Provision of kitchen garden support:Kitchen garden package support will be provided to 1,000 vulnerable women. This kitchen gardening support will include both training and tools kits. This will also include rehabilitation and preparation of land for kitchen gardeningThis activity will also provide an opportunity to women to work inside their houses and contribute to food security and economy of the family. The fresh vegetables produced will be consumed at household level that will contribute to the nutrition as well as savings of the family3.1. Provision of economic support:Livelihoods grants will be given to 50 youth for developing small enterprises in their respective villages. Trainings on enterprise development will be given to these youths for improving business development skills.3.2. Carry out tree plantation campaign:Afforestation campaigns will be carried out with the support of farmers, women, and agriculture department to discourage deforestation and promote forestation. Local fruits/ trees plantation will be promoted to plant 4,000 fruit/ local trees. It will be carried out through cash for work activities with 100 beneficiaries.3.3. Build linkages development:Capacity building of line departments and linkages development with the targeted communities will be implemented to ensure sustainability and extensive support in rehabilitation process for enabling the targeted communities in adopting best livestock management practices. The line department especially agriculture department, livestock will be trained and link with the communitySIF will maintain close coordination with district administration, and other line departments to ensure smooth and successful implementation. SIF will ensure close coordination with humanitarian and development actors working in the area to avoid any duplication and build synergies.
