Project Summary

The WASH response will be implemented for population affected by the conflict which started on 15th April 2023. This action will target IDPs, as well host communities affected by the war in South Kordofan, West Kordofan,West Darfur & Pot Sudan. A total of 3000HHs will be reached due to the WASH response. This will include rehabilitation of existing hand pumps, water yards, construction of emergency toilets, hygiene promotion at the community level with disemmination of key hygiene messages. These intervention will be complemented with Solid waste management and vector control.

1 - Needs

Since the beginning of the armed conflict between the Sudan Armed Forces (SAF) and the Rapid Support Forces (RSF) in multiple locations of Sudan, including Khartoum, thousands of people have fled the capital city. Almost one million people have been newly displaced by the violence, adding to the 3.7 million people internally displaced prior. Before the latest escalation, an estimated 15.8 million people were in need of humanitarian assistance. This number is now estimated at 21 million. Port Sudan, as the main port of the country, has witnessed a significant influx of displaced persons of different social conditions and nationalities. Concern has been operational in three states West Darfur, South Kordofan and West Kordofan. Given the above Concern will be implementing a WASH response in all three states along with a possibility of expansion in Port Sudan.

2 - Activities/Outputs

In South/ West Kordofan WSK, West Darfur and Red Sea states the WASH response will be implemented the following activities. Construction/rehabilitation of 4 water yards, rehabilitation and repairs of 50 handpumps. Rehabilitate and construct 50 communal latrines incamps and other communal locations including Construct 100 handwashing facilities, 100 emergency latrines including washing facilities, of 4-5 stances or in sharing with families, including vector control and solid waste management (WSK,WD & Port Sudan). Provide 2000 women with sanitary materials (all states) - Conduct vector control and solid waste management activities (all states).

5.2 - Impact on environment/natural resources

The project will not have direct significant impact on the environment. The project will ensure proper training and awareness raising for the hygiene promoters on environmental protection particularly related to proper waste management (segregation, storage and incinerating medical waste). The project will also provide basic facilities to supportappropriate segregation and disposal of medical waste according to best practices. As per any rehabilitation planned, the use of locally available materials will be suggestedto reduce the environmental footprint and increase cultural sensitivity. During the rehabilitation of facilities, Concern will ensure that renovation debris are properly handled during and after the renovation.
