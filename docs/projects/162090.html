Project Summary

Currently, Allama Fazlullah Foundation (AFF) implementing 15 community based Learning facilities in 9 different camps following education sector standard guideline and using GIEP materials. AFF seeks to facilitate access to equitable quality education for children aged 3-18 yrs. adolescents and youth aged 15-24 years through the community based Learning Facilities within existing madrasas across the camps. The project aims to do a year end assessment in all the existing learning center to assess the student’s ability as well as promote them in appropriate CFA level. AFF will renovate all the selected classrooms to ensure child friendly and learning friendly environment in the classes. Additionally, AFF will enhance the learning experience for adolescent girls and boys through adolescent and youth engagement intervention within the madrasas. The project plan to recruit, train and deploy teachers, procure and distribute adequate teaching-learning materials among the teachers and student.

Needs

As Per the Education Sector gap analysis in July 2019, about 17% of the children age 6-14 and 95% of adolescent and youth aged 15-24 are not accessing any form of learning. Moreover, as per Education Need Assessment (ENA) 2019, the students who are attending in the learning center among them around 10% girls and 16% boys are not attending due to attending in the madrasa. So, the project aims to create a scope of ensuring standard secular education for around 13% non-learning center gong children age 4-16. The learners of those madrasa have no standards curriculum and materials as well as receiving very basic secular education. The major obstacle to access to meaningful education for the refugees children of the madrasa remain not having access to a standard learning framework no clear pathway to progression, no accreditation and certification. Moreover, to ensure access for all the children is still remain great challenges due to having space constrain. To have a best collaboration with community structure likely madrasa will be the best option to mitigate the space constrain. Further, due to a lack of trained teachers, unavailability of qualified teachers, very limited access of female teachers in the madrasa, teaching is largely rote are significant challenge in the refugee camp settlement. For the learners to succeed, they must obtain academic skills as well as life skills such as Disaster Risk Reduction (DRR), psychosocial wellbeing, health and hygiene promotion, as well as awareness on vital elements on environmental protection, sexual and reproductive health issues. As such, in addition to pedagogical training, all the teachers require training on DRR, psycho-social support (PSS), Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) and Child safe guard principles.

Activities or outputs

Currently AFF implementing education program in 9 different camps that includes; camp 3, 4, 5, 10, 11, 12, 13, 20, and Kutupalong registered camps. AFF ensure access of 1451 children through 15 community based learning facilities. AFF is an active partner of education sector and playing critical role in sector level coordination meeting through leading discussion with religious leaders in the camps, and participating in different sector organize meeting and workshop. Setup and upgradation of Community based Learning facilities: To mitigate the space constrain as well ensure secular education for all the madrasa students in indicated camps AFF seeks to setup 23 new community based learning facilities along with upgradation of existing 15 community based learning facilities. The facilities will assure access to education sector standards secular education for around 3776 children age 3-18 yrs. Additionally, the project will establish gender segregate and accessible WASH facilities for all the children including disable children in all the selected community based learning facilities. Teachers Continuous Professional Development: Well trained and supported teachers are motivated and better prepared to support the students. Due to lack of qualified teachers and most of the instructors/teachers have no previous teaching experience nor are professionally trained, teachers must be equipped in pedagogical contents as well as other cross cutting issues. The project shall target 76 teachers,2 technical officers and 7 school supervisors. AFF will also ensure that the newly recruited staff develop their ability effectively managed their classrooms through the foundational training. Additionally, the project targets; all the teachers and facilitators, technical officers and school supervisors for training programs aimed at sensitization on life-saving interventions such as DRR, PSS, and PSEA. Community engagement: AFF will form 50 Community education committee consisting of 7 members in each. All the community members will be trained on learning center management, disaster reeducation management and psychosocial support. Additionally, the project will organize monthly meeting with the committee members, Joint monitoring visit, program sharing session, FGDs to identify the gaps and revision of program activities in consultation with the community. AS AFF has long experience in working with religious institution, it planning to utilize its strength to engage madrasa/religious leader in education. The project will organize mixed group meeting with religious leader, community leader and parents to create awareness among the parents in education.
