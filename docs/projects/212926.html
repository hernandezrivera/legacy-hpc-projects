Project Summary

Project Summary: The "Empowering and Protecting Vulnerable Women and Girls: GBV Services Project in Northeast Syria" by Salam Organization aims to make a significant impact on the lives of vulnerable women and girls in the region. Through this project, Salam Organization will deliver comprehensive GBV services to address the urgent needs of those affected by gender-based violence. The project focuses on empowering women and girls and providing them with the necessary support to break the cycle of violence and discrimination. Key project activities include conducting awareness campaigns to promote gender equality, conducting training sessions for service providers, establishing safe spaces for survivors, and providing essential services such as counseling, medical care, legal support, and economic empowerment opportunities. By working closely with local communities, stakeholders, and partners, Salam Organization aims to create a safe and supportive environment where women and girls can access the services and support they need to heal, rebuild their lives, and thrive. This project underscores Salam Organization's commitment to promoting gender equality, protecting the rights of women and girls, and creating a more inclusive and secure society in Northeast Syria.
Needs: the implementation of the "Empowering and Protecting Vulnerable Women and Girls: GBV Services Project" in Raqqa, Deir Ezor (Busira sub-district), and Hassakah is crucial based on the Humanitarian Needs Overview (HNO) 2023 and the severity of the situation in these areas.
High Incidence of GBV: The HNO 2023 highlights the alarming rates of gender-based violence (GBV) in these sub-districts, indicating a pressing need for targeted interventions to address this issue. Vulnerable women and girls in these areas are at a higher risk of experiencing various forms of violence, including domestic violence, sexual assault, and exploitation.
Limited Access to Services: The severity of the situation is further exacerbated by limited access to essential GBV services. Many women and girls lack access to safe spaces, counseling services, medical care, legal support, and economic opportunities, leaving them without the necessary support to escape abusive situations and rebuild their lives.
Protection of Vulnerable Groups: The project aims to prioritize the needs of the most vulnerable groups, including internally displaced persons (IDPs), and marginalized communities. These groups face additional challenges due to their displacement, increased vulnerability, and limited access to basic services. Empowerment and Resilience Building: By implementing the project in these sub-districts, Salam Organization seeks to empower women and girls, enhance their resilience, and provide them with the tools and resources to overcome the impacts of GBV. This includes promoting gender equality, raising awareness, and providing comprehensive support services tailored to their specific needs. Collaborative Approach: The project emphasizes collaboration with local communities, stakeholders, and relevant actors to ensure a comprehensive and sustainable response to GBV. This approach aims to build the capacity of local organizations, engage community leaders, and foster community ownership of GBV prevention and response efforts. By addressing the identified needs in these sub-districts based on the HNO 2023 and severity of the situation, the project strives to make a significant impact in empowering and protecting vulnerable women and girls in Raqqa, Deir Ezzor, and Hassakah, ultimately contributing to the overall well-being and safety of the communities in these areas.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Mainstreaming Monitoring and Evaluation (M&E): Effective monitoring and evaluation are crucial for the success and sustainability of the project. To ensure that M&E is effectively integrated, the following steps will be taken:Data Collection: Relevant data on project activities, outputs, and outcomes will be collected using standardized tools and methodologies. This will include information on the number of beneficiaries reached, types of services provided, and feedback from women and girls accessing the services.Regular Monitoring: A dedicated M&E team will be responsible for monitoring the implementation of project activities. They will conduct regular visits to WGSSs, mobile units, and other project sites to assess progress, identify challenges, and provide support where needed.Quality Assurance: The M&E team will ensure the quality and adherence to established standards in the delivery of GBV services. They will work closely with service providers to maintain high-quality support and address any gaps or issues that may arise.Outcome Evaluation: Periodic evaluations will be conducted to assess the impact and outcomes of the project. This will involve measuring changes in knowledge, attitudes, and behaviors related to GBV, as well as assessing the effectiveness of the services provided.Mainstreaming M&E throughout the project, Salam Organization aims to ensure accountability, identify areas for improvement, and make evidence-based decisions to enhance the overall effectiveness and impact of the GBV services being delivered.Protection Risk Analysis:1. GBV Risk Assessment: A thorough assessment of the specific GBV risks and vulnerabilities in the targeted sub-districts of Raqqa (Sabkha and Thawrah), Deir Ezzor (Busira), and Hassakah (Shadada) will be conducted. This assessment will consider factors such as social norms, power dynamics, displacement, and access to basic services. It will provide critical insights into the prevalence and forms of GBV in the project areas.4. Risk Mitigation Strategies: Based on the identified protection risks and vulnerabilities, appropriate risk mitigation strategies will be developed and integrated into project activities. These strategies may include community engagement and mobilization, awareness-raising campaigns, capacity-building initiatives, and the establishment of referral mechanisms to ensure that women and girls can access timely and appropriate support.
