Project Summary

The Digniti project aims at improve living conditions of IDPs by setting up a cash transfer system between donor citizens in Cameroon and IDPs in the Far North region. NRC will enable donors to donate at their own discretion and give beneficiaries simple mobile phones to spend the funds on their most pressing needs through mobile money. Main activities will be provisions of multipurpose CASH.
Targets are both men, women, boys and girls in the most vulnerable households, located in hard to reach area.

Besoins / Needs

(IDPs) and host communities in the Far North region of Cameroon need access to food security and others basic service like shelter, education and water. The 2019 Humanitarian Response Plan (HRP) has identified 4,3 million people in need of humanitarian assistance in Cameroon of which only 2.3 million have been targeted within the HRP for a total budget of 299 million USD. Only 9,1% of this total is planned to be channeled through the cash transfer modality, leaving less chance for beneficiaries to meet their needs. Although NRC is providing assistance in the following domains: Shelter/NFI, WASH, Livelihood and Food Security, Education and Information, Counselling and Legal Assistance, including cash for food, the needs are still huge in the Far North region. The humanitarian situation in the country is in addition increasingly sensitive. In its 2019 list of most neglected crises in the world, NRC has ranked the Cameroon crisis number one, due to the lack of funding, lack of media attention and political neglect.On the other side, there is an estimate of 10.7 million citizens working with good revenue in Cameroon. A survey conducted in 2018 by NRC showed that 72,13 % of these Cameroonians would be willing to donate to displaced fellow citizens through cash transfers, if an efficient, sustainable, and transparent system was in place. NRC will support households basic needs through this project with provision of multipurpose CASH. NRC will seek to get Cameroonians citizens support to humanitarian activities through local funding.
