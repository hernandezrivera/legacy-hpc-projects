Project Summary

In line with the South Sudan HRP and cluster objectives 1 and 2, the project will reach 11,111 (1,700 Men, 2,975 Women, 1,721 Boys and 2,104 Girls) affected people in Renk County of Upper Nile state. The project will prioritize and target SAM/caretakers at nutrition sites, nutrition facilities with poor WASH conditions and communities with high GAM and no access to safe water as well as refugee returnees at the transitional site in Renk. Women, children, the elderly, and Persons with special needs will particularly be targeted in this project from both the vulnerable host communities and the refugee returnees because they are disproportionately affected during crises such as floods, and conflicts which have been proven to have great linkage to disease outbreaks and malnutrition. For example, Malnutrition is linked to poor mental health and psychosocial distress among children and caregivers especially women. Reports has indicated that the returnees are severely without access to WASH services, age of households also have strong link to access to WASH services with HH headed adults aged between 18-60 years are more likely to have access to WASH services compared to the young and the elderly headed households. Gender also plays a significant role in the hygiene status of households with women headed households slightly having more hygiene knowledge across South Sudan. The activities proposed will focus on Contributing to reduce WASH-related morbidity and mortality through WASH integrated programming with Nutrition, and providing access to WASH services for the refugee returnees and vulnerable host communities affected in Renk. The project will therefore target these most vulnerable groups through inclusive assistance, and will ensure that protection, gender and conflict sensitivity is mainstreamed in the proposed activities. Protection will be mainstreamed throughout the project cycle to ensure beneficiary safety & dignity, avoiding doing harm as result of the intervention, providing meaningful access to all sets of beneficiaries and ensuring all groups are actively involved and participate in the project in an inclusive manner.
Cordaid has been responding to the increasing humanitarian needs in the area through local partner Africa Development Aid (ADA) providing WASH, FSL and Health assistance and is fully operational in the area with an office at Malakal and a sub-office at Renk.

1. Needs

Nearly, 300,000 people, mostly South Sudanese have returned to South Sudan since the Sudan crises started in April 2023. More people are expected to arrive as the fighting continues. The arrival of the returnees has driven up humanitarian assistance needs, increased competition over the limited resources and has contributed to an increase in households facing large food consumption gaps. The situation is further exacerbated by flooding which has of recent displaced over 2,800 people destroying houses and properties in Renk. Besides the floods, poor infrastructure and lack of roads are major challenges in Renk. FEWSNET projected some households to likely remain in catastrophe IPC 5 phase as result of the South Sudanese returnees and disruption to trade flow with Sudan. The protracted negative impacts of conflict in South Sudan, expected below to significantly below average harvest, and the large-scale influx of South Sudanese returnees as result of the on-going conflict in Sudan will remain the greatest concern in Renk. According to recent Inter-Agency Assessment report in Renk there is high need for clean water, excreta disposal facilities and integrated hygiene awareness that promote appropriate practices. There is also high rate of acute malnutrition among children under 5. This has been attributed to the poor WASH practices as one of the factors that contribute the underlying causes of malnutrition in the County. As result, Renk has been ranked in phase one (1) of extreme intersectoral severity phase. Women, children, the elderly, and Persons with special needs will particularly be targeted in this project from both the vulnerable host communities and the refugee returnees because they are disproportionately affected during crises such as floods, and conflicts which have been proven to have great linkage disease outbreaks and malnutrition. For example, Malnutrition is linked to poor mental health and psychosocial distress among children and caregivers especially women. Reports has indicated that the returnees are severely without access to WASH services, age of households also have strong link to access to WASH services with HH headed adults aged between 18-60 years are more likely to have access to WASH services compared to the young and the elderly headed households. Gender also plays a significant role in the hygiene status of households with women headed households slightly more hygiene aware across South Sudan. Units SAM/caretakers at nutrition sites, nutrition facilities with poor WASH conditions and communities with high GAM and no access to safe water Will also be prioritized for targeting.

2. Project activities

In line with the overall HRP objective, the project will prioritize cluster objective 1 (CO1) and cluster objective 2 (CO2) to contribute to the HRP strategic objective 1and 2 respectively. Gender, age and Persons with Disability (PWDs) will be prioritized in the response while working in a coordination especially with other implementing partners in Renk for complementarity because during crises, different genders, age groups and PWDs are disproportionately affected. In addition, SAM/caretakers at nutrition sites, nutrition facilities with poor WASH conditions and communities with high GAM and no access to safe water will be targeted and prioritized. Together with the implementing partner Africa Development Aid (ADA), the following activities will be implemented to ensure the cluster objectives 1 and 2 are achieved.CO1 Activities include;- Distribution of Dignity/Hygiene kits among SAM/MAM children and caregiver families- Hygiene Promotion sessions conducted at Nutrition sites- Distribution of WASH minimum kit for SAM/MAM caregiver families- Installation of village pump for sites near river or water pond. CO2 Activities include;- Conducted the safety audit for installation of village pump.- Construction of safe water source- Water Quality Analysis- Conducted safety audit for construction of emergency latrines- Construction of new Latrine at transitional site- Hygiene promotion session at HH level through door to door visit, at mass gathering and dissemination of IEC material and using mass media for hygiene promotion- MHM kits distribution- Distribution of WASH Emergency Kit (buckets, aquatabs, PuR Sachets, filter cloth and soap) during Emergency response for new arrivals from Sudan to the Transitional site
