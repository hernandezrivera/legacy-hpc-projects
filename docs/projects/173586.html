Project Summary

This project will be implemented in three target states (Blue Nile, White Nile, and South Kordufan). The overall aim of the project is to contribute to reduction of mortality and morbidity among the affected persons (internally displaced persons, returnees, and host community members) through provision of integrated Nutrition ,Health, and WaSH emergency and recovery interventions to vulnerable men, women, boys,and girls in the selected states during the year 2021. The total population targeted by the project is estimated at 74,732 individuals including children under-five years of age, pregnant and lactating women. The nutrition proposed interventions aim at improving the nutrition status of the target population and reduce the nutrition related mortality and morbidity rates among the most vulnerable groups of the population, especially children under five years of age ,pregnant and lactating women (PLW) at the selected geographical areas (Blue Nile, White Nile and South Kordufan) through provision of timely and comprehensive preventive and curative nutritional services. The main activities of this project include: 1. Supporting nutrition screening for children under-five and PLW, 2. Supporting management of MAM cases in the TSFP, 3. Supporting referral of malnourished children from the community to the TSFP, 4. Following up the MAM cases and defaulters tracing.5. Providing micro-nutrients supplementation to the children in need. 6. Supporting nutrition counseling on infant and young child feeding.7. Supporting capacity development of the care providers , community based and civil society organizations staff.
The total budget required to implement this project is estimated at 267,500 USD.

1 - Needs

According to Humanitarian Need Overview (HNO) 2020, the total number of peopleestimated to be in need of humanitarian assistance is more than 8.6 million with anincrease of 40% compared to 2019. This increase in number can be justified by severalfactors such as large scale population displacement, influx of refugees and climate andsocioeconomic conditions across the country. Host communities and residents areestimated to be 1.94 million which represent 23.2% of the total people in need ofhumanitarian assistance in Sudan. Considering the type of the need, there are 7.8 millionpeople are in need for basic health services; 59% of them are female, 63 % are childrenand 8% are older people. It is estimated that 2.2 million are women of reproductive agewho are in need for comprehensive reproductive health services. In White Nile The level ofglobal acute malnutrition (GAM) in the target communities of White Nile is 10.30 (S3MII)these communities have very limited access to nutrition services. According' to the IAassessment, this is largely due to the influx of refugees out camp. The inability of thesehouseholds to produce food has made it a top household expense. The cost of food hasalso increased more than 60% since October 2018, exacerbated by the currentmacroeconomic crisis, and the majority of vulnerable households are unable to engage inwage labor. The lack of access to food has had direct effects on children. The SMoH WNreported> 30% of children under five (CU5) were stunted and 8.55% moderated wasted and1.75% severe wasted in 2018 (S3M), while the under five mortality 65.8 (MICS 2104)Households are engaging in negative coping mechanisms, including consuming low qualityand less expensive food, decreasing the portion sizes at meals, eating fewer mealsin a day, borrowing food from neighbors, and, to a lesser extent, harvesting immaturecrops and collecting wild foods. In 2019 the SMoH WN reported more than 89,000 childrenunder five and 93,000 pregnant/ lactating women with malnutrition and in needed toassistance aid. In Blue Nile the level of global acute malnutrition (GAM) in the targetcommunities of Blue Nile is 7.66 (S3MII).ADD proposed in 2020 to targeted 8 localities in Blue Nile, and White Nile.This project is part of a multi-sectoral program (WASH, Health & Nutrition) provided tobeneficiaries by ADD in the targeted areas. This approach ensures synergistic effect tomaximize impact and hence achieve the aim of the project, and based on nutrition needs& gap the project will be focused on IYCF and nutrition treatment of SAM & MAM cases atboth facilities and community levels.
