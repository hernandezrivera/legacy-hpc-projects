Project Summary

Whilst COVID 19 is the devastating emergency which the world and Zimbabwe are battling to contain, the virus has spin off effects which have worsened the emergency. The virus has different impacts on women, children, the poor and other vulnerable citizens. The rapid spread of the disease has overstretched health facilities on a global scale. There is however real fear that the virus could cause turmoil in the developing countries due to the poor state of health facilities. Zimbabwe health sector is almost non-existent and is experiencing a protracted crisis spawned by lack of funding, strikes by doctors and health workers for living wages and lack of equipment. Zimbabwe will not withstand this shock. Its citizenry can be decimated by this novel virus if not contained.
The emergency is also worsened by the economic crisis facing the country, characterised by basic shortages of food, fuel, money foreign currency. The result is that the mandatory government lockdown has affected the most vulnerable and poor citizens who and struggle for a living daily and survive hand to mouth. Social distancing, a key instrument of slowing down the spread, is proving difficult in the country as most of the citizens are scrounging and hunting around for basic commodities like bread and mealie meal.
This project Intends to:
• Promoting general infection control, training health care workers on improved WASH, integration of non-COVID19 health services with local solutions (efficient, cost-effective and sustainable).
• Essential healthcare services integrated with health promotion and prevention .
