Project Summary

To help promote access to education and social inclusion for the most vulnerable Lebanese and refugee population in Lebanon.
Access to quality education and enhanced social welfare services for the most vulnerable Lebanese and refugee population is improved in North Lebanon.
Expected Results: Levels of access, attendance, participation, safety and inclusiveness of public schools for vulnerable Lebanese girls and children and refugees have improved; School readiness and performance of vulnerable Lebanese girls and children and refugees has improved; National systems (SDCs) in charge of providing protection, social welfare and specialised health services (including mental health services) have strengthened their capacities to ensure services to children and their families; Psychosocial wellbeing of vulnerable girls and children and their families has improved.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

Rehabilitation and equipment of schools, organization of remedial classes, BLN, training of teachers, case management, FPSS, SEL, and school clubs.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

Beneficiaries and other stakeholders through the FCM are able to submit suggestions, complaints and recommendations; they would receive an effective, efficient and structured response. Information would be capitalized and tailor programs and interventions based on new knowledge gained through community participation. Feedback and complaints are treated according to their nature and seriousness. For the most serious cases - relating to violations of the COOPI Code of Conduct, allegations of sexual exploitation and cases of abuse and threats - COOPI has ad hoc procedures. Female staff will be involved in the project to receive complaints (social workers, facilitators, case managers…).

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

The project at all levels is based on a participatory approach, involving all the stakholders in the decisions. The project implementation areas and target groups were identified on the basis of local needs without discrimination related to religion, social or political background and gender. The identified families will be adequately informed about the proposed activities and their opportunities through preliminary awareness activities. COOPI will ensure proper communication and transparency with beneficiaries and stakeholders at all levels by using different culturally appropriate tools and languages to make them accessible to different groups. It will provide accessible and timely information to affected populations about programs, organizational procedures, structures and processes that affect them to ensure they can make informed decisions and choices. COOPI will set up a feedback and complaint mechanism, culturally appropriate and accessible to beneficiaries.
