Project Summary

During this project, TGH will contribute to provide appropriate assistance in areas with high needs for shelter support and NFI support to protect the most vulnerable people from harsh winter and to support return movements. In line with the Shelter Sector’s strategic objectives 1 & 2, TGH will contribute to reinforce an enabling protection environment andcommunity cohesion by improving housing through addressing the urgent shelter needs in Idleb and Rural Damascus governorates.
This intervention will focus on the rehabilitation of damaged houses for the conflict-affected newly returnees in Khan Shaykun, Maarat An-Nu'man, Hajar Aswad and Babella localities, who are facing cumulative challenges, including the large destruction level of housing. Seasonal winterkits will be distributed in Shabaa and Babella localities (Rural Damascus). HNO 2024 in Syria states that 6,8 million people are still in need of shelter support; and 6,1 million requiring NFI support. In Idleb and Rural Damascus governorates, the newly accessible areas are witnessing one of the largest percentages of displacement and return cases in the country. Idleb and Rural Damascus were identified as in severe need for shelter support according to HNO 2024.
The intervention will foster synergies with TGH Education and WASH activities that are currently taking place in Khan Shaykun. Adding a Shelter component by rehabilitating damaged houses in targeted areas, will further foster early recovery.
In December 2023 & January 2024, TGH conducted multi-sectoral assessments in Khan Shaykun, Maraat An-Nu'man, Hajar Aswad, Babella. These assessments were done in coordination with the local authorities (municipality and governorate representative) and enabled TGH field team to design detailed sketches and BoQs foreach HH.
Through this project, TGH will address the immediate shelter needs of the most vulnerable returnee families. A total of 250 partially damaged houses will be rehabilitated in 2024 in Khan Shaykun, Maraat An-Nu'man, Hajar Aswad and Babella and 350 in 2025 in the same localities, which will benefit 250 households (1,250 individuals, 618 women and 632 men) in 2024 and 350 households (1,750 individuals, 865 women and 885 men) in 2025. Through this intervention, TGH will ensure safety, privacy and dignity standards in the rehabilitated houses and TGH will supply and install HH level solar electricity system to meet basic HH level energy needs. In 2024, TGH will distribute 250 winter kits (Winter Clothing kit/Fleece Blanket) for 250 households (1,250 individuals, 618 women and 632 men) in Harbanifse, Shabaa and Babella and 350 in 2025 in the same localities, targeting elderly or female-headed households and disabled persons. TGH always adjusts its distribution modalities (through door-to-door visits) to ensure that the most vulnerable effectively benefit from the intervention.
The project therefore aims to strengthen the resilience and cohesion of vulnerable communities by improving housing. Repair and rehabilitation of damaged houses will improve safe and decent living conditions of vulnerable conflict-affected populations (returnees’ families) selected based on vulnerability criteria (women-headed HHs, familieswith children under 5, elderly, with a member chronically ills and/or with physical/mental disability). This will be done in close coordination with the local authorities and MoLAE, with whom a previous MoU had already been signed by TGH in June 2020. This is expected to facilitate the approval process for this project. The primary beneficiary list with 250 HH has been acquired by TGH and the approval process was already started through a meeting with Idleb governor, SARC Idleb Head of branch in order to secure the needed approvals in timely manner.
