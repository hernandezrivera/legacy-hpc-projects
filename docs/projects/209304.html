Project Summary

This project is in line with the Food Security and Livelihoods (FSL) sector objective (SO1and SO2)and the GBV sector’s Strategic Objectives (SO1 & SO 2), part of the revised HRP 2023, which was done mainly to respond to the current conflict in Sudan(15 April 2024) and due to this emergency situation the cost of the 2024 HRP will only focus on lifesaving activities., The project activities are designed to improve access to lifesaving assistance for the food-insecure and provide protection for IDPs affected by recent crises besides the IDPs project will target returnees and host communities who are vulnerable to Food security, and gender-based Violence in Blue Nile. This project targeted 11,104 % of them IDP and segregated as follows: ( 5,552.00 Women HHs, 2,220.80 men, 1110.4 boys, and 2,220.80 girls)aiming to improve access to lifesaving assistance for the food-insecure and help affected women and girls face an increased risk of GBV including sexual violence during displacement due to the current crisis post-15 April 2023. Specifically, Ed Damazine, Ar Rusayris, and Al Kurmuk, localities
The planned interventions contributed to HRP -FSL SO1 – SO2 improving the food security status by providing an integrated multi-sectoral lifesaving response, including protection assistance to save and sustain lives and GBV. Provide life-saving specialized quality GBV services, including CMR, case management, and psychosocial support, and reinforce referral pathways. (With a focus on women and girls with disabilities and adolescent girls) GBV objective 2 GBV Enhance strategies for community resilience through the empowerment of women and girls and strengthened community-based interventions GBV objective 3 Promote GBV risk mitigation into all aspects of the humanitarian response
This intervention will be through the following activities:
For Food security and livelihoods:
Agriculture support services including improved seeds (staple and cash crops and vegetables), including hand tools for 2747 individuals 824 per HHs. provide emergency livestock, inputs, and veterinary services – including vaccination, treatments, and extension services to 1090 individuals 218 per HHS. Provision of goat restocking for 1090 individuals 218 per HHS Provision Cash/Vouchers Based Transfer (Half Ration) to 2695. vulnerable HHs, 899 Al, Ed Damazine, 898 Ar Rusayris, and 898 Al Kurmuk localities for HHs from each locality. The total of individuals who benefit from this is 6,532.
For Gender Base Violence
Women, adolescent girls, and at-risk groups on the move and crossing borders due to the current conflict in Sudan(15 April 2023) are exposed to increased risks of GBV and exploitation GBV Protection interventions will help them access proper and safe assistance, in terms of community awareness-raising through campaigns on GBV targeting 3,199 women, men 60% of them women from the three localities, and community leaders. Establish Women's Centers. With operational support to run the center. Provision of 3000 dignity kits. This project aims to reduce and prevent gender-based violence and support targeted beneficiaries, especially women and girls to improve their livelihood and food security status and increase their capacity to prevent and respond to Gender-Based Violence as a tool for campaigning against Gender-based violence, confidence building, and skills development.
The overall goal of this project is to reduce and prevent gender-based violence, improve the livelihood and food security status of targeted beneficiaries, and enhance their capacity to prevent and respond to GBV. Additionally, the project aims to raise awareness, build confidence, and develop skills to campaign against gender-based violence.
