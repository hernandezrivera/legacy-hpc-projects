Project Summary

The project will provide comprehensive WASH services for affected people in northwest Syria living in IDP sites and host communities. The project will focus on providing emergency WASH services in the short term and provide a sustainable services in the long-term to the affected people in Aleppo and Idelb governorates.
Shafak through this project will provide emergency WASH services for 240,000 individuals living in camps.
In addition to conducting rehabilitation, maintenance, and upgrade for WASH facilities and infrastructure to link the remote sites (camps and neighborhoods) with sustainable WASH services. Focusing on the rehabilitation of the infrastructure will ensure the sustainability of access to WASH services for 315,985 people and maintain the efficiency of the infrastructure to be able to receive more subscribers and avoid any damages which might be very difficult for the local authorities to fix it.
The project will comprise the following components:
a) Rehabilitation of 15 water pumping stations including equipping the stations with all electrical equipment and install renewable energy systems to reduce the running costs. In addition providing monthly running costs for the stations (fuel, chlorine, engine oil, and incentives for workers) and apply a cost recovery system.
b) Provision of WASH emergency services for 240,000 individuals living in camps including water trucking and water quality monitoring, water containers, construction of emergency latrines and maintenance, desludging of septic tanks, and solid waste management.
c) Rehabilitation of sewage networks to connect the remote sites, schools, and health centers (isolation centers) in addition to replace the damaged sewer lines.
d) Rehabilitation of water networks to connect the remote sites, schools, and health centers(isolation centers) in addition to replace the damaged water lines.
e) Supporting the local operators and municipalities to continue the garbage collection and disposal of the waste from 20 communities, schools, health centers and surrounded IDPs sites. This support will cover providing fuel for garbage vehicles, and maintain/repair of the broken ones in addition to support worker’s incentives.
f) The hygiene promotion will implement awareness sessions about the improvement of hygiene practices and behaviors.
g) 60,000 hygiene kits will be distributed for the IDPs and host communities who don’t have the capacity to secure hygiene materials.
Shafak will ensure support the innovative solutions as possible in its proposed interventions. Shafak will apply solar-powered systems for operating the water pumping station and support the hybrid system to reduce the operating costs in the water pumping station. Moreover, Shafak will develop the design of septic tanks and support construction water treatment plants where there is no possibility to link the camps with the sewage networks.
The proposed intervention directly contributes to Syria's HRP strategic objectives, through providing WASH emergency services and provide sustainable access to WASH services through linking the remote sites with WASH facilitates and improves the infrastructure in the host communities.

4 - Needs

The scale, severity, and complexity of the humanitarian needs of people in Syria remain extensive. This is the result of continued hostilities in localized areas, and new and protracted displacement, according to HNO 2021. 13.4 million people in Syria are in need of humanitarian assistance. Moreover, the effects of the COVID-19 pandemic entrench existing needs and proliferate new ones across the population of northwest Syria.WASH systems have suffered from damages owing to hostilities, years of functioning at high capacity, limited or no maintenance, the continuous drain of technical staff and poor water resources management. these factors pose significant public health risks that have harmful cascade effects on communities in terms of inequality to access to the WASH services , therefore, WASH infrastructure requires significant support and investments. According to the HNO report, 36% of the general population relying on alternative to piped water and often unsafe water supply modalities to meet or complement their water needs, at least 70% of sewage is discharged untreated and at least half of the sewerage systems not functional, and about 29% of garbage inappropriately disposed of. Also, the WASH infrastructure and service provision in many parts of Syria require significant repair and operational support including capacity building for operation and maintenance of infrastructure in host communities with high ratio of IDPs and returnees,

5 - Implementation of activities

The WASH response will reach 555,985 people, 60% of them are IDPs. Shafak will provide comprehensive WASH services through implementing the activities directly by their teams in the field. A comprehensive Emergency WASH services will offer access to WASH services through the following activities: ●Provision of WASH life-saving services in camps: Shafak will provide potable water through water trucking in alignment with the water quality monitoring process and install water storages. With regards to sanitation, Shafak will construct latrines and rehabilitate the damaged ones, desludging of septic tanks, support the solid waste management and install garbage bins. ●Rehabilitation of sewage/water networks, Shafak will connect the camps, promote neighborhoods with sewage and water networks in addition to replace the damaged parts.●Support the water pumping stations by providing/repairing electrical and mechanical equipment, equipping the boreholes, upgrading the power sources in the station by installing solar-powered systems. ●Establishment of the cost recovery system, through gradually supporting for the water pumping station through secure fuel, oil, chlorine and cover the workers' salaries.●Solid waste management; Shafak will support 15 municipalities to maintain garbage collection and removal from host communities and surrounded camps, in addition, to rehabilitate landfills to ensure dispose of the garbage with no harm to the environmental; ●Hygiene promotion; Shafak will distribute 60,000 HKs for the most vulnerable people and implement hygiene promotion awareness sessions.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

The WASH sector will ensure a “Do No Harm” principle, and has carried out a Protection Risk Analysis as part of its sector strategy development. Potential protection risks that may arise from the implementation of the WASH sector strategy and related activities, along with a description of the relevant mitigation measures and resources required for monitoring those risks have been identified. The risks might include inequality in accessing services, work safety, gender-based violence (GBV) and exposure to explosive hazard . Regarding emergency services delivery, the sector will ensure that SPHERE/sector agreed standards are strictly followed and adapted to the context during each part of project implementation, especially during emergency sanitation infrastructure implementation phase when the risk of GBV is higher. WASH partners will take adequate measures to minimize exposure of the most vulnerable groups (women, adolescent girls, children, people with disabilities, female-headed households) to greater protection risks, and particularly to GBV. WASH staff will be trained on key GBV concepts and referral pathways to properly support survivors. Women, adolescent girls and people with disabilities will be consulted during the design, implementation and monitoring phase of each project, with a need to put the necessary mechanisms in place to facilitate feedback from users.
