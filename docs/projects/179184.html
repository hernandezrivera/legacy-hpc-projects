Project Summary

This project aims to strengthen productivity and profitability for livestock holders through an improved access to quality assets and inputs in Aleppo (Afrin district- Jandairis sub-district), Idlib (Idlib district – Idlib and Maaret Tamsrin sub-district) though distributing E-voucher to the livestock holders to be redeem it against animal feed from the participant local suppliers. total of 216 livestock holders, 1,296 individuals (518 female and 778 male) (will be supported with approximately 2,175 sheep and cattle. The E-voucher animal feed will be distributed within winter. number of rounds is three and each round will cover the needs for two months starting from November 2021 to April 2022. The animal feed will be Concentrate Feed
Targeted beneficiaries will be both IDPs and host households with 50% each, the animal feed quality will be based on the livestock technical working group under the FSL Cluster support. Also, the beneficiaries will receive training through two different approaches, first one is through training of trainers (ToT) of the local authority and focal point and as well as through livestock farmers field school, to make sure the livestock holders to receive not only the necessary knowledge but also in practice. Brochures and leaflets will be distributed to the targeted beneficiaries.
Eligibility Criteria are:
1- Livestock holders with maximum of 20 sheep or 2 caws,
2- No or low source of income,
3- Do not receive similar type of assistance,
Vulnerability criteria are:
1- Women-headed households,
2- Elderly headed households,
3- IDPs,
4- Households with disable members,
5- Household with more than 6 members,
The project will be implemented from September 2021 until May 2022, so we set the duration only for the time tin 2021 to be aligned with HRP guidance

4 - Needs

According to the HNO 2021:•The agriculture sector, which was once a cornerstone of the Syrian economy, has been severely impacted, with half (i.e. 50 per cent) of national grain requirements met through imports.•According to Food and Agricultural Organization (FAO), high cost and limited availability of quality crop and livestock production inputs, damaged irrigation infrastructure across the country, degradation of livelihoods and income earning opportunities, coupled with the direct and knock-on effects of Covid-19 and climate-induced shocks continue to drive more people into food insecurity. The livestock sector in NW Syria is considered one of the potential and good opportunity for livelihood recovery from a protracted crisis that has lasted for more than 10 years. The number of livestock decreased significantly, part of the IDPs who displaced from Saraqab and Ma`arat al-Nu`man moved their livestock in Feb and March 2020, however, those IDPs and even host households tend to sale part if not all their livestock which is considered as productive assets to cover the households need and as well as they cannot cover the feed and healthcare for their livestock which is considered as negative coping strategy which is high in NW Syria. As well as the limited land available for farming in general and low interest in forage crops, high cost of animal feeds, most of the farmers rely on the rainfed farming with the low rainfall in winter 2021 and lack of water for irrigation, all these challenges force those livestock holder to cope negatively.

5 - Implementation of activities

Mercy-USA will identify the community following the coordination with the FSL cluster to make sure the high need is prioritize and no overlapping with other organization, then Mercy-USA field team will meet with the local councils to discuss the project objective, targeting criteria, timeline and MoU will be sign, then Mercy-USA field team will advertise the project in the targeted locations with cooperation from the local councils, different meetings will be held with the community representative at this stage with both male and female participants, then the registration will take place based on the eligibility and vulnerability criteria mentioned above, Merc-USA M&E team will monitor the registration process and also verify 100% of the registered households. Market assessment will be conducted early of the project, quality will be measured based on the mix percentage and quality of the raw material, supplier will be selected based on Mercy-USA procurement policy, selected supplier will be trained by Mercy-USA contracted E-voucher service provider who will train the animal feed suppliers and provide them with the necessary equipment’s. Price will be monitored regularlyMercy-USA will distribute E-voucher to the targeted beneficiaries, each beneficiary will receive PIN code and leaflet on the E-voucher redemption process and list of the participant list of animal feed suppliers with list of animal feeds and price. Beneficiaries are free to redeem their E-voucher at any supplier’ center at ay time, Mercy-USA M&E team will monitor the E-voucher distribution and redemption process, and PDM will be conducted after each round. Mercy-USA technical team (male and female) will train the beneficiaries through FFS and TOT

7.1 - Please list any risks related to sexual exploitation and abuse that have been identified during the project design phase and any risk mitigation and prevention measures that have been adopted and built into the project

SEA Risks: •Overcrowded during E-voucher redemption to support livestock which may cause SEA•Mixed redemption lines for males and females •All distribution staff are males •Redemption site is not accessible to all gender groups •No shared clear information about timing and amount of redemption •No clear procedure regarding loss of card/voucher•Taxation on the assistance •No complaints and feedback mechanism (CFM) in place Risk mitigation:•Ensure extend redemption days to avoid overcrowding •Separate lines for males and females in all redemption process •Ensure gender balance in the hiring process, ideally to be 50% of the team are female•The redemption sites will be chosen to be accessible to all gender groups especially female HH and disables•Clear information about timing, amount of cash, and procedures about loss of cards/vouchers of the distribution will be shared with all beneficiaries beforehand and will be existed in the banners in the redemption sites •Sensitization about the scope of the project to all communities with highlighting “This assistance is Free” and this statement will be printed on the beneficiary cards.•Different CFM channels will be in place such as WhatsApp, hotline, email, phone as well as physically by MEAL team. • PSEA awareness materials (e.g. posters and pamphlets) and reporting hotline will be visible to beneficiaries at the redemption site• Use PSEA organizational and field checklists•Report any SEA case to PSEA network
