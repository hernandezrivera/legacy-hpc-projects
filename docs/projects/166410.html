Project Summary

Through its Camp Coordination and Camp Management services (CCCM) in Northeast Nigeria, INTERSOS seeks to improve protection services & camp coordination in 4 LGAs.
The intervention will impact 41,563 IDPs in Banki, 74,792 in Damasak, 52,978 in Ngala and 90,577 in Monguno camps, reaching a total of 259,910 internally displaced persons (IDPs).

Associated response plan

Nigeria Humanitarian Response Plan 2020

Plan fields

1 - Needs

A: Risk Comms & Community Engagement -Protect, assist and advocate for vulnerable IDPs, migrants, refugees and host communities, through dissemination of critical key Health and WASH messages to the communities, and awareness raising on COVID 19 preventive measures. B: Point of entry work, & Coordination -Enhance and support surveillance, including active case finding, case investigation, contact tracing and surveillance at Points of Entry (PoE/Reception Centers), to mitigate risk and contain the spread of the COVID-19 pandemic and decrease morbidity and mortality in Northeast Nigeria. C: Logistics -Ensure availability of logistics needed for prevention of vulnerable IDPs & households from risks of increased congestion during COVID-19 pandemic, and strengthen out of camp approach.

2 - Activities

A1 Support awareness against COVID 19, & information campaigns carried out in translated local languages. A2 Training of camp volunteers to facilitate the upscale awareness, prevention and referral on COVID-19 A3 Ensure access of humanitarian assistance to vulnerable populations, through establishment of Communication Centers to facilitate Out-of-Camp coordination and mobile site monitoring B1 Provide support to improved reception capacity by providing quarantine spaces in the entry points and designated camps. B2 Provide lifesaving NFI support to prevent exposure of IDP households to Covid-19 via lending essential non-food items to each other. B3 Improve reception center management by actively sharing POE information for rapid detection of imported cases, comprehensive and rapid contact tracing, and case identification. C1 Provide lifesaving logistics solutions including soap, hand washing stations, face masks and PPEs for health staff, and strengthen out of camp approach for IDPs and host communities during COVID-19 pandemic. C2 Provide support to the decongestion strategy for dangerously overcrowded parts, innovative shelter solutions for repair of damaged shelters, and fencing, to mitigate exposure of IDPs and host communities to COVID-19 pandemic.
