Project Summary

The critical situation that people are living under in West Bank including area C, and the inability of the Palestinian Authority to work in the mentioned location has put the Palestinian people under difficult life situation due to their lack of adequate standards of living. Continues cracking down by Israeli occupation practices and settlers against Palestinian people living in area C and close to hot zones that face continues violations by settlers such as; preventing people from accessing their lands through closing check points, destroying agricultural lands and uprooting planted trees, destroying agricultural roads, and confiscation of lands. All these aggression and violation deteriorate life in area C and the its villages.
470 vulnerable families (2162 capita); including 543 female, 538 male, 426 Female children, 425 male children, 42 elderly through improving the food security of vulnerable farmers through land rehabilitation 6 locations located in Jerusalem and Bethlehem governorate on area C.
The project activities will also respond to the different humanitarian needs of the most vulnerable farmers (women and men), to improve their living conditions by increasing the productivity of their agriculture lands, and enhance their resilience in their communities. Due to PARC experience and previous studies shows 87% of women are working in agriculture as unpaid work, PARC will give priorities to HH headed by women. Additionally, women help to improve the living conditions of their families, taking into consideration that more than half of the population in Jerusalem and Bethlehem are females.
The project’s main interventions is built on PARC’s humanitarian response approach and previous FSS experience in the area to include the followings:
Activity 1: this activity includes:
1. Rehabilitation of rehabilitation of 200 dunums of lands at household level, This Intervention will benefit 50 families. Different sub activities will be implemented for each beneficiary depending on the needs: Light machinery work for leveling and rock removal, Rehabilitation and construction of the stony retaining walls to protect from soil degradation and to increase water retention capacity, fencing to protect lands and crops from wild animals and livestock animals.
Activity 2: Rehabilitation of 12 Km of agricultural roads. This intervention will protect and improve accessibility and utilization of the served agricultural lands. Agricultural Roads rehabilitation includes several sub activities: machinery work , basecours, culvert, and construction of stony retaining walls
Once the project is approved, there will be inception phase where a series of actions will be done. Firstly, PARC will announce the project and will establish local committee per each location. Secondly, announcement and selection of end beneficiaries Thirdly, tendering and contracting. Fourth, complaint mechanism (HAP) will be elaborated from day number one. Fifth, handing over and exit strategy, PARC, MoA and the local committees will design an exit strategy per location and included in the final report.

1 - Needs

One of the biggest impacts in this year 2019 has been the risk of confiscation of agricultural lands by Israel. Farmers needenhancement of the resilience and support to rehabilitate many lands in order to avoid therisk to be confiscated. One of the main factors that differentially affects women is linkedto restrictions on access to property, particularly land. Women, by law, have the right toreceive 50% of the inheritance that a man receives, this being a law that is alreadydiscriminatory in itself. However, in many cases due to social pressure, tradition andcustom, women reject this part of their inheritance. This practice is especially problematicif we take into account that households headed by women have higher rates of foodinsecurity. On the other hand, as far as employment is concerned, women in the WestBank constitute only around 17% of the active population. To this must be added that 5%are dedicated in the informal sector and that 60.2% occupy unsafe, unstable andprecarious jobs. PARC through its work on similar project funded by OCHA had detected the need to target Bethlehem and Jerusalem, due to its resistance in a conflict area including; suffer from the surrounding Israeli settlements, separation wall, highly exposed tonatural disasters specially the floods that destruct the agricultural roads and lands andlocations with small agricultural holdings, lands with a difficult access and lands exposedto destruction by wild pigs and livestock. The collected needs were verified also with Ministry of Agriculture, Jerusalem Governorate has beenidentified to be targeted in the project proposal for the high need for rehabilitation.While number of applications were submitted by beneficiaries through other projects shows the need of targeting Bethlehem area. PARC had conducted number of field visits on the 16th and 17th of September 2019, to verify the needs that collected on June 2019 to ensure the raised needs by the beneficiaries During these visits, PARC met with the local councils, farmer’s committees and thecooperatives to update and verify the information, needs about the agricultural areas thatcan be rehabilitated, during these meetings and visits we have noticed that there is morethan of 300 dunums needs to be rehabilitated, according to the most needed lands wehave selected 200 Dunums for this intervention. On the 17th of September 2019, aworkshop was conducted to talk about the needs of the farmers, and where the localcouncils, 15 representatives of Pioneer farmers at the location, Farmers committee andCooperatives participated. The results previously identify were discussed, being detectedthe following needs as the main ones: lack of material resources for rehabilitation ofagricultural lands, damage to agricultural land due to winter floods, the spread of wildpigs and livestock that destroy agricultural crops, grazing at the agricultural lands, regularharassment by the occupation and settlers and the difficulty of access to agriculturallands, in order to meet this need, PARC through this project will add the rehabilitation of 12km of agricultural roads due to the lack of organizations working in this intervention in the targeted areas. In addition, one of the common needs was the high unemployment rate they face.In the specific needs for women, they mention the particular needs they have in relationwith land as mention before.During the previously mentioned process of detecting the needs, PARC coordinated with relevant institutions working in the area, to find that Oxfam through a consortium are implementing a land rehabilitation intervention targeting 300 dunums at Bethlehem area. This intervention will not affect the designed project due to the high need recorder to that area, adding to this the designed project will target lands belongs to people who are unable to pay community contribution, rather than OXFAM project that target those people who are able to pay the community co

In relation to the preparation of the project, during the first phase of identification, PARCwas involved in the coordination mechanism promoted by the FSS. Due to that, PARC will be able to achieve the third objective: FSSactivities are planned and implemented in a coordinated manner, and partnership with keynational ministries is strengthened. After this analysis, PARC will be able to knowin which stage is, for example, the different lands that will be rehabilitated and which oneare the specific activities to work on. The selection criteria that will be used will take intoconsideration different socio-economic data, also, the criteria will prioritize women andthe particular needs: lands that are close to settlements and the separation wall, level ofincurred damage of the infrastructure, level of food insecurity, priority to female-headedhouseholds (The percentage of female-headed households was 11.7% in the West Bank),priority to lands owned by women, number of members in the family disaggregated orlevel of incomes of the families. In order to target the most vulnerable farmers, applicationforms will be filled up by the farmers including different data such as average income,land location, owned land area, exposed to natural/man made disaster or water prices forirrigation. 9 - Protection mainstreaming
9.1 - Do No Harm

A monthly meeting will be conducted at community level to update beneficiaries and communities about project development and challenges, which will enable beneficiaries to provide recommendations and highlight lessons learned. As most project activities will be carried out in sensitive Area C, a comprehensive risk analysis and security incidents will be documented, discussed and updated in a participatory approach with the target population. Consequently, mitigation measures (such as low profile methodology of activities by beneficiaries) will be carried out to reduce potential risks adhering to the principle of Do No Harm. Also local committees will be trained in documentation and dissemination of HR violationsPARC is committed in assuring accountability through the HAP policies adopted by PARC, the main accountability principles will be available and monitored during any intervention implemented by PARC include: participation, information sharing, enabling complaints and "do no harm" ,environment protection and gender mainstreaming.

9.2 - Equal and impartial access to aid

- PARC will ensure that the assistance is provided in times where control is limited (at night, during week-ends) in coordinated times with VCs and CPCs. - PARC have a field office in Jordan Valley and Nablus, Jenin where local coordinators for its projects in Area C are mobilized to mitigate access risk in locations where security incidents are likely to occur. - When needed, possible alignment of PARC internal security and procurement policies and guidelines are obtained to ease delivery of items, engineering works as well as contractor’s management. - PARC have been working since 1983 in the Jordan valley including the target communities and established robust trust and collaboration with local communities, CBOs, VCs, CPCs as well as donors and stakeholders, including for SHELTER projects, so they will be made aware of any problem with ICA or IF in the communities in real time. - PARC is a focal point for protection mainstreaming in the Shelter Cluster which enables us to briefly be engaged with updates related to overall security situation together with OCHA and other stakeholders humanitarian alert systems for WB. - PARC will be using all above access to information to support and facilitate when needed, all the times project team, contractors and materials to the project sites.
