Project Summary

As the Anglophone crisis continues to deteriorate, more population are being displaced which has led to an increasing population in need of humanitarian assistance. According to the recently conducted Cadre Harmonise assessment (October 2019), an estimated nine hundred and sixty-eight thousand people in the NWSW regions are identified to be food insecure and considered to worsen as current assistance provided are at small scale compared to the fast-increasing needs. The current people in need identified under the physical and mental well-being humanitarian consequence is significantly higher than the three hundred and thirty thousand from last year which is mainly due to the deteriorating security situation with weeks of imposed lockdowns, lack of access to farmlands, population displacements and locations being inaccessible. The most needs are in the North-West region while security situation remains fragile in both regions and the IDPs population group in the bushes and inaccessible areas remain the prioritized group of people to receive assistance. Because host families especially those in relatively safe areas have been receiving frequent influx due to displacements, the host community population are also becoming more vulnerable. Markets are either non-functional or overstretched in some areas leaving in-kind assistance as the option in these areas however; cash/vouchers are being implemented in small scale. The aim of the project is to provide food assistance to 1000 Internally Displaced people in the Littoral regions of Cameroon through Cash based transfer.
The project will provide cash-based transfer to IDPS in the Littoral regions where markets are functional
. The project activities will consist of -
• Recruitment of project personnel
• Training of project personnel by the food security cluster on programs
• Confirmation of need assessment already carried out in the targeted communities to select the most vulnerable beneficiaries.
• Market analysis and selecting the most appropriate supplier.
• Capacities of beneficiaries will be strengthened on Livestock, crops, vegetable production and marketing. Further training will be held on Cash Base transfers.
• Monthly cash transfer will be conducted.
• social cohesion, psychosocial support and counseling activities will be enhanced
• Regular and periodic monitoring will be carried by project personal, feedback committees and the food cluster
