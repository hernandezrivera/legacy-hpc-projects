Project Summary

In Syria, the food processing sector was one of the economic sectors and a significant source of income for many people in rural areas, particularly women. The studies show that domestic food processing, both the agricultural and livestock sectors, were significantly affected during the crisis period. To achieve sustainable food security for women, the International Humanitarian Relief (IHR) proposed a food processing support project. It aims to empower 80 women (HC & IDPs) through providing the required inputs, equipment, material and tools, in addition, to train them to produce manufactured food products to be sold out of season, which can achieve a high financial return and secure the life requirements at non seasonal months of the year. Meanwhile, support the food processing value chain through create women association from supported ones and train them to lead the process and ensure the sustainability of the process for years.
To address those activities, IHR is going to rent four houses for nine months (two at Kafr Nobol sub-districts, Idelb and the other in Azaz sub-district, Aleppo governorate) to be as training centers. The houses will be provided with the required tools and equipment to be used during processing the foods. The raw material of fresh vegetables (tomato, cucumber, pepper, jut mallow, olive,...etc.) and milk will be procured through a transparency procurement process, with taking consideration the season of each kind of plant.
During six months of food processing, the selected women will be trained on principles of food processing for the most common products (tomato paste, pepper paste, dried foods, dairy products and pickles). Using the gained knowledge they will the producer for that period. Therefore, the knowledge of targeted women will be enhanced through theoretical training on the best practices of food processing, new used techniques of food manufacturing scope, and marketing.
During training, the most active women will be selected by IHR team to be as food processing women association. They will attend training about business management to build their technical capacity and empower them to manage the center by marketing the produced foods, capital management in a way that ensures the continuity of the next season and gives the participants women their share in the financial return equally. To address that, IHR will develop suitable ToR for those associations.
At the end of the project, the assets will be handover to food processing women association, to be used in the interest of all members, therefore achieve sustainable sources of income for those vulnerable women. In additional to introduce the association member for all stakeholder of the community to facilitate their access to a market in future and in order to share that successful experience of community engagement and women empowerment, perhaps to be the nucleus of expansion to include for largest project include the most vulnerable population of society

Needs

The humanitarian crisis in Syria continues to deteriorate as the war moves into the eighth year. The estimated number of people in need as per the most recent Humanitarian Needs Overview topped 5.89 million (HNO, 2019). Since the most humanitarian agencies are responding to those affected populations through emergency projects, the IHR conducted need assessment to find out the needs of people and addressing it. The assessment has been conducted by IHR M&E field team in several communities of Al Maraa district, Idleb governorate and Azaz district, Aleppo governorate. Through focus group discussions and key informants with female and other community representatives. Tow location were proposed for intervention, Kafr Nobol sub districts, Idleb and Azaz sub district, Aleppo governorate. Which are under opposition control, and relative stability. According to assessment results, the most needs of women are income generating opportunities. Which can keep her dignity and reduce the dependence of man. As we are going to target rural area, the most suitable and required livelihoods opportunities were food processing support projects which have decreased critically due to some reasons relate with the inability of the rural woman to purchase the materials and the relevant equipment because its need to the power resources “electricity and fuel”. All of that negatively affected on households’ life, especially woman. In this sense, that kind of projects proposed. Whereas, the women already practiced it during their daily life. Which encourage us to recommend those kinds of project to empower women by facilitate their access to their basic needs from projects can achieve good income, therefore, support their food security and increase the resilience. From the other hand, the raw material of food processing is available cheaply for several months at a year, so processing it and keeping it out of season, ensure the basic food for households, in addition to possibilities of selling it with high prices. That process can achieve good value chain of agriculture and livestock products and reach the access from community members for it.

Implementation of activities

The IHR is going to hire staff, most of them female to facilitate the deal with women beneficiaries. Engaging with community, IHR field will hold introducing meeting with representatives of local authorities to explain the project objectives and responsibilities of both parties, which can be documented by signing MOUs at targeted communities. During that stage, the project will be advertised in public places of targeted communities, with clear description of selection criteria to ensure that only vulnerable ,GBV survivors and interested women will be selected. Using application form the interested women can apply to benefit from support and the survivors will be come through referral pathway in the target areas . Once the primary list of beneficiaries be ready, the M&E team will verify from 100% of potential beneficiaries to confirm their eligibility. In parallel with beneficiaries’ selection, the procurement team will advertise of transparency and competitive tender according to IHR policy and procurement procedures. Using a developed Request for Quotation (RFQ), the technical specifications will be detailed to ensure that high quality items are purchased under supervising of procurement team, evaluation commit will evaluate the offers and select the winner supplier based on quality test’s results that program team are going to conduct on the all required items. Once the beneficiaries selected, they will be informed with training place and dates. According to the season of each kind of vegetables, IHR team will prepare training plan to extend for six months, starting at June. The technical training will be on stages, each one will be related with kind of final product that women are going to produce, like yogurt stage, tomato paste stage, piper paste stage,…etc. however, one day before starting any stage will be specialize for theoretical training to explain the implementation instructions, emphasize the importance and scientificity of every step, following up the health rules during processing and packaging to ensure high-quality production. In addition to a detailed explanation of the best methods and new used techniques to produce and store the dairy and vegetables products. Training place will be provided with required tools, raw materials and equipment and electricity for turning the machines one. In addition to the plastic bags to keep the final product in. the supplying of raw material will be according to center and women capacity. Which aims to produce high-quality products from fresh vegetables daily. It’s worth to mentioned that the marketing phase will start after two weeks of starting training phases To ensure sustainable value chain of agricultural and livestock food products. IHR team will monitor the progress of work inside those centers and provide the required support like more linking with market. In addition to select the most active women to be members of food processing women association. Their basic responsibilities are leading the process after the project be finished, then continuing activities implementation at the next seasons. However, those members will get on business and finance management and marketing training to ensure succession project. Finally, this project aims to empower the women through creating sustainable sources of generating income, reduce the depending on the man and raise the level of representation in society through more engaging with markets.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumptions:- We assume the all beneficiaries will attend the theoretical training sessions and will commit toward practical during six months. - We assume that association members will keep assets and continue the work as by fair distributing of money to all members.Risks:1 - Risks: the beneficiaries try to sell some of raw materials during producing. Mitigation: the process will under IHR control, and buying materials will divided through several batches of each item. 2 - Risks: the beneficiaries sell or give the assets to other parties. Mitigation: for one year, IHR team will continue monitoring the progress of work and association activities. During that period the IHR will meet the members to show the positive impact that that can achieve for their HHs. 3 - Risks: management board control with profit and don’t distribute it fairly. Mitigation: IHR will prepare ToR and will explain it clearly for all members, which include on possibility of changing the board by election through each six months, and when it is needed to avoid keeping the decision under one person.
