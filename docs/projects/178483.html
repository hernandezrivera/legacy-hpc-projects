4 - Needs

Many years of the crisis in Syria have heavily disrupted the health system, leaving only 58 % of hospitals and 53% of PHC's fully functional, in addition to more than 7.78 million people in 100 sub-districts were living below three critical emergency health standards, a localized household survey in 2020 in NWS has found that crude and U5 mortality rates were below international standards. Moreover, the COVID-19 pandemic, coupled with the economic downturn in the country, has caused additional pressure on the already disrupted health system, with the self-reported compliance with preventive measures remains below 50 %, and laboratory capacity and access to testing are also below recommended levels. According to HNO, the health sector PiN has increased three percent with the largest absolute change seen in Idleb Governorate where the PiN increased by more than 200,000 people, which is classified as an area with catastrophic needs according to the health sector severity scale. According to HNAP report on March 2021, Idleb sub-district has received the highest number of IDP's during this month, according to the same report, health care services have been reported as a priority by 41% of the residents in the sub-district, 45% of the IDP's, and 20% of returnees, the same report has mentioned that all Idleb sub-district population is at high risk of CPVID-19, and the COVID-19 health services are less sufficient. Chronic shortages of advanced diagnostic technology prevent full functionalization of health facilities and heavily impacting the provided services, Orient MRI center is the only center providing MRI services free of charge in NWS, making it very important not only for Orient, but for the health System in NWS.

5 - Implementation of activities

Orient medical complex is already up and running, all the staff required to provide the health care services are available, the center provides primary health care services according to the EHSP, the primary health care service will be integrated with additional services provided in the center, including physical rehabilitation services, mental health services, nutrition services throughout facility-based lifesaving, curative nutrition services through early identification, and treatment of acutelyU5 children, and PLW’s, integrated with IYCF services, and community based integrated nutrition, health, and protection services throughout 10 RRT’s, in addition to the advanced diagnostic services provided by Orient MRI center, to increase the accessibility of beneficiaries, a mobile clinic will be also linked with the center. All Orient facilities will be provided with a triage system, to ensure to minimize the risk of infection for staff and beneficiaries, in addition to strict IPC measurement and distribution of PPEs to all the staff. Monitoring will be conducted at every stage of the program through the collection and analysis of routine data on a continuous basis throughout the life of the program. Data collection tools are appropriate and tailored to the program's needs. A feedback mechanism will be established to affirm that all beneficiaries have the right to complain and provide communication channels to the communities to voice their suggestions and provide their feedback about our intervention. in addition to contributes to improving the quality of our programs.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

-Violence against Health Facilities and Healthcare Providers: Orient will report any attackon health facility, and support any efforts to highlight protections challenges facinghealth workers across Syria. -Gender-Based Violence GBV: Hiring of female staff to increase access to health services for women and girls, training gynecologists, midwives, and nurses on the clinical management of sexual assault and rape, identification of GBV cases, and safe referrals, and link the health facilities with the social services to ensuresafe referral pathways between both of them. -Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse PSEA: Orient is an active member in the WoS PSEA network, all the staff have been trained on PSEA on all health facilities, all the recruitment process includes PSEA in the criteria of selection and all the staff should sign Orient code of conduct, and PSEApolicy.-COVID19 spread among health workers as well as among beneficiaries: All Orient facilities have been provided with triage tents and other infection prevention and control measures, in addition, all the staff has been trained on the IPC, Orient will insure to provide PPE equipment to all the staff.

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

- Changing front lines, changes in the parties controlling the implementation areas, and significant population movements as they flee shifting conflict areas: Orient regularly monitors the local situation, and Context-specific Safety and Security Management Plans will be in place, and regularly reviewed and updated. - Risks due to the COVID-19pandemic, that may result in lack of access of beneficiaries to the project sites or even the suspension of project activities: relevant program adaptations have been developed and are currently in place, in addition to strict IPC measurement. - Interruption of WHO supplies line if the UNSCR was not renewed: Orient is supporting all advocacy efforts about the importance of the UNSCR renewal and works closely with the health cluster and other partners to develop a contingency plan to mitigate this risk.
