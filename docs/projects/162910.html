Project Summary

In South Kordofan, Blue and White Nile, South Darfur and Khartoum states, GAH conducted needs assessment in November 2019 to review the humanitarian situation. The assessment covered the localities of Provision of WASH services to the vulnerable communities in El Damazine in Blue Nile, Dimso in South Darfur, Abujubeha in South Kordofan, Bantiue and Om Bada in Khartoum States in Sudan. The rainy season has affected all Sudan states significantly this year, heavy rainfall during the month of August that occurred in different areas resulted in massive flooding, there by affecting most parts of the various states throughout the Country. According to the findings of the assessment reflected that there humanitarian needs in these areas, 50% of those people who are without access to basic water services are considered in severe need, 40 to 60 percent of assessed boreholes and 70% of the hand pumps are destroyed, people are traveling long distance to collect water (30 minutes to 8 hours), 39% of the Sudanese population have access to limited sanitation services as per the revised S3MII. 23% of those people who are without access to limited sanitation are assumed to be in severe needs, 80% of the assessed population are not practicing proper hand washing practices during critical times, In addition, only 4% to 20% of the assessed households have latrines and the remaining percentage are practicing open defecation including the IDPs, host communities and SSR. They suffer from lack of enough safe drinking water and basic sanitation facilities and inappropriate hygiene practices. According to the State Ministry of Health (SMoH), the international NGO (INGO) and the World Health Organization (WHO) report, lack to Safe drinking water, poor sanitation and inadequate personal hygiene practices the major causes to Acute Water Diarrhea (AWD) and Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM) to children in the targeted Areas.
This confirmed that water quality is a public health issue among the targeted communities. The planned intervention is needed to contribute to reduce morbidity and mortality associated with WASH related diseases among IDPs, host communities and vulnerable groups, especially children under 5 years and pregnant/lactating mothers and elderly. Additionally GAH conducted assessments in 2019 to rural targeted areas to identify the most critical WASH needs and there is urgent need of WASH services as well as other needs like health and immediate food and non-food items assistance. In 2019, based on focus group discussion GAH collect information in targeted areas and localities, The main finding of these assessment are summarized as follows, The main assessment findings in WASH sector highlight needs for (i) safe drinking water by repairing the existing Hand pumps, Haffirs and upgrading some to mini-water yards, (ii) support sanitation/hygiene including school sanitation/hygiene, and (iii) explore options for new water supply sources. The socio-economic situation in the targeted areas had been broadly affected by the conflict in South Sudan and Civil wars in Darfur as the entire displaced populations sought refuge in villages within the targeted areas putting additional pressure on existing available WASH facilities, hence posing a threat of possible conflict outbreak among different communities, people currently depend on unprotected open water sources for drinking water and these sources are not protected, and water declines significantly during the dry season.

1 - Needs

According to the assessment conducted in 2019 by GAH staff there is 240,293,358 total populations in El Damazin locality. Those persons affected by the conflict in 2011 have been displaced for around 7 years. They can be found mostly near Damazine town living either within host communities or in settlements adjacent to host communities. Those relocated in 2015 have a different perspective regarding their circumstances and medium term prospects. Those having experienced 7 years of displacement are less likely to consider a return to their homes of origin. It is conceivable that they would prefer settling where they are. Those that have experienced a shorter period of displacement would prefer to return to their homes of origin but are unable to for safety reasons and livelihood concerns.The IDPs that have been displaced between 10-20 kilometers from their homes of origin may continue to cultivate their former fields (where these can be accessed safely) all the while maintaining their families in the relative safety of the displacement settlements.In South Kordofan state there was 200000 total populations in abujibiha, 35000 of them IDP’s, 160170 host community and most of them suffer from access of drinking clean water and they need to provide them by WASH services. Moreover, 3998 the total population in Dimso South Darfur which is 900 of them host community, 2300 Refugees and 798 others. The gaps and need is very highly in WASH and people suffer from shortage of water services and hygiene.Furthermore, Khartoum one of the most affected areas by flood recently in 2019, which was hits various areas and people lack to basic life services. The total population is 56668 host community and 10000 refugees in Bantiue settlement area and scattered to 8 smaller location affected by flood, they lack to safe and clean drinking water and poor of hygiene practices and sanitation as well as Om Bada locality one of the most affected area by cholera which is the children was most infection rated.Needs assessment missions conducted by GAH during 2019 in targeted locations concluded that there was an overwhelming need for all sectors, especially Nutrition, Health and WASH. It was cited the need for safe drinking water and AWD mitigation training and convenient sanitation locations as key priorities. WASH urgent needs: 1.Sustainability of water services in South Kordofan, Improved Environmental health, Behavior change through hygiene promotions and improved environmental health (Vector Control) in Bantiue and Om Bada in Khartoum, construct defecation facilities for the communities in Dimso in North Darfur State and improved defecation facilities in El Damazine in BN. 2 - Activities / Outputs

Rehabilitation of 40 Hand Pumps, Distribute jerry cans to reduce water contamination which will minimize water related diseases, implementation of CLTS for the construction of latrines for HHs and schools with community participation to eliminate open defection, Complementary systemic hygiene promotion with water and sanitation facilities such as hygiene awareness, training sessions for community hygiene promoters for water, sanitation and hygiene activities as well as to training them in the PSS service to cases management and referral pathway, supporting materials, Construct 50 HH latrines in line with WASH sector and sphere standards, Construct 1 Segregation latrines in schools for students and teachers, Construction of 2 hand washing facilities for student, Conduct 10 hygiene awareness sessions for community, Conduct 10 cleanup campaigns inside school and 10 for community, Construction of 1 teacher latrine in school (F/M), Procure and distribute 2 Water tanks for student for Drinking water at school, Distribution 8000 hygiene kits to vulnerable women, Construct 50 household latrines. (Consider 10 child-friendly latrines included), Provide 16 water points (water tanks), Rehabilitate 50 communal/public latrines, Conduct 80 awareness raising sessions on how to proper treat drinking water points and best hygiene practices, Conduct 80 cleanup campaigns, Provision 500 lights (solar energy light) at latrine and shelters, Ensure participation of the Refugees and IDPs in WASH activities (CLTS), Maintenance of the existing non-functioning tap stands, Chlorination and conduct training on use of recommended household water treatment technologies, Train 50 Hygiene Volunteers on improved hygiene and sanitation practices, and follow-up of volunteer’s hygiene promotion activities, Conduct Health, hygiene and sanitation education sessions at HC and in strategic community places, provide 10000 soaps and water container (Ibrig) for hand washing. Girls and women of menstrual age who are in the initial phase of emergency will be provided with 1000 menstrual hygiene materials, Distribution of 2,000 hygiene and water treatment kits to 250 households, Printing and distribution of 2,000 hygiene promotion leaflets and posters through HH visits, campaigns, and media coverage, Conduct 3 mass clean-up per location, Conduct WASH activities (5 schools), Construct of 10 hand washing facilities.

6.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

Global Aid Hand will ensure the ease of access by the targeted population to service without any constraints such as discrimination, reduced mobility or other constraints.
