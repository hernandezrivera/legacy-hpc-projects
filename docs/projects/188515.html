Project Summary

Domestic Violence is the most common type of gender-based violence.
In most contexts, domestic violence is used to describe intimate partner violence. This can encompass sexual, physical, emotional and psychological violence (Voices from Syria 2021).
Women are the majority of victims, but both men and women can be perpetrators or victims and also children or grandparents (Mosaic's yearly need assessments report 2021).
The project is based primarily on Increase the capacity of GBV actors to respond and prevent GBV throughout training and empowering groups of youth, men and women from areas of need on a set of skills to be partners in reducing domestic violence through:
-Build the capacity of GBV non-specialist to respond, prevent and mitigate GBV and then propose community initiatives to reduce domestic violence and implement them in areas of need.
-Participation in a wide media campaign.
-Participation in dialogue seminars to strengthen their belief in the fight against violence.
-Creating a map of services and informing people in areas of need about the approved hotlines and protection centers.
Training the target group will contribute to transforming it into an actual partner in raising awareness and spreading knowledge, a large part of whom may be abusers, even indirectly.
-Provide humanitarian life-saving specialized quality GBV services, including case management, psychosocial support and establish referral pathways by opening of 5 main family counseling centers (Lattakia and rural of Damascus) and a 8 units (a room for counseling in each Mosaic's center): in which group awareness sessions about domestic violence are held for local community families, group sessions for parents who suffer from one family problem, individual sessions for a family member to help assess the case and develop a plan of assistance, individual sessions for families of vulnerable groups in society (caregivers of the elderly, widows and divorced women, children with special needs), awareness sessions for adolescents and youth.
-Conducting coordination and partnership with a number of official departments and associations to provide services for people with special needs and addicts and their families:
-Directorate of Health in Lattakia.
-Directorate of Health in the countryside of Damascus.
-Specialized counseling Center for Diagnose and Rehabilitate People with Disabilities and for Psychological and Social Consultations (Lattakia).
This is because addiction problems and mistreatment of people with special needs are the most important causes of domestic violence.
Mosaic Association's work for years in areas of need to spread issues of gender-based violence and support education has helped prepare the environment to accept awareness topics and prepare a wide segment to be part of the proposed training.
In the second year, the project must include livelihoods to help alleviate the economic burdens that are causing the violence.

4 - Needs

The areas of Al-Ramil Al-Jnoubi, Al-Datour (Lattakia), Harasta and Mesraba (Rural of Damascus) are poor and densely populated areas (600000 in Lattakia city), (160000 in Harasta and Mesraba) (HNO 2021). The areas in Lattakia have hosted the displaced since 2013, some of whom are still settled there until today, causing a severe shortage of basic services. While the areas in the Rural of Damascus were destroyed and their residents displaced. The harsh conditions that the population has been exposed to have increased violence, especially domestic violence. 2 of Mosaic centers supported by UNFPA are working in Lattakia to reduce GBV. 3,000 survivors of violence received our services during the last three years (800 women received case management, psychological counseling and psychosocial support services). There are 5000 women still waiting to receive that services (Mosaic's yearly need assessments report 2021.Mosaic centers hold services to 1800 survivors of violence during the last two years in Harasta and Mesraba. There are 10000 women still waiting to receive that services (Mosaic's yearly need assessments report 2021.Mosaic has been working in the targeted areas for at least 5 years, and has a database and survey of all needs in them. It also updates this data annually.

5 - Implementation of activities

-Mosaic will find a team of trainers to train groups of young people, men and women, on team building, building community initiatives, and masculinity, provided that the duration of each training is three months. The groups present community initiatives aimed at reducing domestic violence, and distinctive initiatives are adopted and implemented. -This is accompanied by a media campaign that sheds light on issues related to domestic violence and converges with government efforts that work in the same line, and also contributes to informing people of hotlines and protection centers.-Seminars are also held, attended by the trainees, to crystallize their initiatives and deepen their understanding of the issues at hand, with the participation of clerics, academics, government officials and representatives of international organizations.-A map of the services provided by all parties is prepared for circulation.Opening of 5 main family counseling centers (Lattakia and rural of Damascus) and a 8 units-Conducting coordination and partnership with a number of official departments and associations to provide services for people with special needs and addicts and their families.
