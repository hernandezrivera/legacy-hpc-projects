Project Summary

One in every five of these children with SAM and one in every 15 of the children with MAM are at risk of death if their malnutrition remains untreated. The continuous influx of returnees and the camp overcrowding is suggested to continue to increase the risk of outbreaks including cholera, hepatitis E, meningitis and measles which ultimately contribute significantly to the immediate causes of malnutrition. However, the ongoing outbreak of Acute Watery Diarrhoea (AWD) in Borno state is also assume to be a potential threat to the concerted efforts in place to curb malnutrition in Borno state.Investment in nutrition programs in Nigeria has not been commensurate with its contribution to child mortality. It is worthy of note to say that there won’t be meaningful economic growth if nutritional health, food security and livelihood are not prioritized as a matter of policy in Nigeria. In spite of the efforts by humanitarian actors to mitigate the problem of acute malnutrition in Northeast, more still needs to be done through the Community-based Management of Acute Malnutrition Program.
The aim of this program therefore, is to strengthening preventive and treatment of Nutrition activities in Bama and Gwoza Local Government Area, such that it would be community driven to reduce Malnutrition in the LGA.

Needs

The main goal of the project is to provide a live-saving intervention that will prevent the death of children due to malnutrition and contribute to reduction and prevention of malnutrition in host communities. Specific Objective:• To improve access and utilization of nutrition and basic health services in primary health care facilities in accessible communities.• To improve the knowledge and practice of members of communities on good nutrition and key household practices. • To encourage hygiene and good sanitation based practises in host communities.• Improve coordination and integration of nutrition specific intervention with other multi-sector nutrition sensitive interventions at the community level.

Activities

Children 6-59 months of age would be screened using MUAC tape, identified and refer to the Outpatient therapeutic program OTP in the community.Pregnant and Lactating women PLW would also be screened for malnutrition, identified and referred to the OTP/Health Facility.Mothers, Care givers and community members would be sensitized on awareness and mobilizations on key IYCF practical approaches
