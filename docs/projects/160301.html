Project Summary

The key drivers for integrated WASH needs in the current context are the IDP population, GAM rates and displacement putting a strain on existing water sources and increases WASH needs such as to break the vicious circle of diarrheal diseases and high malnutrition rates which are also linked to poor WASH conditions as exemplified in the diarrhea-malnutrition cycle prevalent in vulnerable populations.
ASCO’s WASH response will target beneficiaries groups who are most at risk of diseases outbreak, notably, children under 5 years of age. As well displaced populations with limited access to safe water and improved sanitation and Food insecurity with IPC level 3 and 4.
The project will focus on 2020 WASH objectives and consequently the lifesaving WASH needs to the affected population in Aweil East, South and Wau Counties. ASCO will respond on integrated programming with other sectors nutrition, health and protection coping with the unanticipated scale and spread of the cholera outbreak, responding to the protection crisis GBV to provide services for survivors of GBV and dedicated services for women and girls with protection concerns.
WASH will integrate with nutrition to ensure the OTPs and SC has safe water and soap for washing hands after visiting washing facilities, children with SAM in the OTPs sites provided with WASH kits. Ensuring soap for mothers with children of SAM and MAM cares provided in nutrition sites. Ensuring water is treated with aqua tabs and availability soap in hand washing stations.
Starting from the OTPs/SC will target the couple mother or the caretaker with malnourished children until the household level aiming to break the vicious circle of diarrheal diseases and addressing small stock open defecation affecting the young children.
ASCO will ensure the affected beneficiaries access equitable safe water, sanitation, hygiene promotion and prevention of malnutrition through provision of sanitation services to Children admitted for SAM/MAM treatment with WASH minimum package of activities at household level that are secure and appropriate to IDPs, returnees and vulnerable host communities.
The differential needs, concerns and priorities of women and girls, men and boys of different ages and disparities will be addressed basing on the four main cluster objectives as follows:
Integrate WASH in nutrition response through famine prevention minimum package. ASCO will respond by ensuring children admitted for SAM treatment discharged with WASH hygiene kit from functional OTP/SC and admitted for SAM/MAM treatment with access to safe water at OTP/SC. WASH response at household and community levels for displaced population and host community in the areas with highest malnutrition rates GAM coupled with highest famine risks.
Integrate WASH in public health response to control outbreaks at wider community level. ASCO will respond by ensuring WASH-related diseases vulnerable population provided with access to safe drinking chlorinated water and provided with functional hand washing facilities by soap or 0.05% chlorine running.
Provide timely and sustainable, equitable access to safe water, sanitation and hygiene for IDPs, host communities and returnees. Provide safe drinking water to communities and nutritional sites OTPs and SC from protected water point till the appetite test for SAM and MAM.
Mitigate WASH-related gender based violence. ASCO will respond on objective four by ensuring women and girls with safe water and access in secure location agreed after GBV risk analysis through focus group discussions with women and girls, conducted by female staff with safe sanitation and hygiene facilities access in secure location agreed. Provide safe water and access in secure location to women and girls with after appropriate risk analysis
The project will target 24000 beneficiaries that of constitute of Women 7500, Girls 6000,Men 5000 and Boys 5500 to undertake different roles and activities of the project.

1 - Needs

Based on the needs assessment on South Sudan WASH Cluster dashboards and IRNA assessments the selected counties face multi-context crisis: violence and conflict, cattle raiding, food insecurity, economic crisis and the collapse of healthcare and social services are key drivers.Increases in acute malnutrition is attributed to severe food insecurity, widespread insecurity, displacement, poor access to WASH services, high morbidity, extremely poor diets and poor sanitation and hygiene.The overall WASH situation is magnified by the endemic nature of cholera and other water borne epidemics Aweil East, Aweil South and Wau Counties which disproportionately affects children under 5 years. High malnutrition rates are strongly linked to poor WASH conditions exemplified in the diarrhea-malnutrition cycle which is prevalent in vulnerable populations. Need to ensure the OTPs and SC has water and soap for washing hands after visiting the latrine, children with SAM in the Nutrition centers need to be provided with WASH kits. Soap and hygiene kits for mothers with children of SAM/ MAM cares provided in nutrition sites, no garbage in the nutrition sites and WASH messages provided in the OTPs sites by ensuring water for appetite test is treated with aqua tabs and availability of soap and water in hand washing stations.The remaining coping abilities have been further eroded by prolonged food insecurity, economic collapse and disease outbreaks especially cholera. The widespread lack of safe drinking water, sanitation facilities and poor hygiene practices has left a large proportion at risk of preventable WASH related diseases.Open defecations is a common practice. The consequences of lacks of access to clean drinking water, poor sanitation, and inadequate basic health services would likely cause serious water borne diseases and increases the prevalence rate of cholera outbreaks and WASH related diseases. Cholera transmission contexts levels for displaced population and host communities is at highest risk.IDPs remain among the most vulnerable groups with the most acute and vulnerable populations found in this counties with limited basic WASH needs. ASCO will provide timely equitable access to safe, water, sanitation and hygiene for IDPs, host communities and returnees. WASH needs are also closely linked to Nutrition, protection and health needs. ASCO will respond to enhance WASH in Nutrition sites, which both serves to keep children boys and girls in nutrition sites and schools to prevent further outbreaks. Displaced women and girls continue to be disproportionality affected by protection related WASH issues with GBV occurring due to insufficient and unsafe access to water and sanitation facilities.Hygiene and sanitation situation with low standard sphere of WASH. Provide latrines with locks and design improvement agreed through focus group discussions conducted by female staff with women and girls to mitigate WASH related GBV risks, Lack of latrines in many nutrition sites, residential areas, public places such as schools, markets and health facilities have compelled the communities to practice open defecation which is a health concern.
