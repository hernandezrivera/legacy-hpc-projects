1 - Besoins / Needs

The persistent crisis which has emanated to a prolonged armed conflict in the NW and SW regions of Cameroon has had a negative impact on access to primary health care. This is due to insecurity and persistent attacks on healthcare and this constituted a huge threat to the availability of essential health care to the affected population. In 2021, cholera, measles, yellow fever, and monkey pox epidemics were recorded in the SW region. Numerous attacks on health facilities, targeting both infrastructure and health personnel, were recorded throughout 2021, thus leaving many health facilities non-functional and leading to a reluctance of health personnel to work in these insecure areas. this scenario has persistently increase with these communities in Mbonge, Ekondo titi and Tombel health Districts. Armed attacks and shooting on civilians have also increased in places where adequate surgical assistance is not available. Equitable access to health care and epidemiological surveillance urgently needs to be strengthened in the Mbonge, Ekondo titi and Tombel health Districts. Moreover, the few available health facility operational in the South West region are mostly within the urban setting, faraway from the communities leaving inhabitant already affected by the crisis not able to reach the facility in case of an illness and has thus referred to traditional herbs for treatment. The stress from the crisis has led to the death of many inhabitants who as a result of the crisis has lost the livelihood and cannot cope within the context of the crisis. Mobile clinic activities shall support communities by taking the both preventive and curative health care services to the different communities hence reducing the inaccessibility of healthcare to communities. This will together with the decentralized model of care support the entire strenthen the entire health system and ensure reduction An inadequate surveillance and primary health care in the South West region have account for the increase in outbreaks of cholera, Monkey pox and measels in ekondo titi health district. It is therefore important to increase healthcare provision in communities faraway from functional health facilities to aid in arresting epedimic prone diseases and help safe the lives of this affected population. Primary health care shall be provided by the use of mobile clinic and the community decentralized model of care to help improve on accessibility to curative and preventive medicine. The following activities shall be considered; - To improve timely access to basic curative and preventive health care services to affected population through general consultation of 12,000 patients. - Improve access to primary health care to 5000 children less than 5 years , 1000 pregnant women through consultation and treatment for 3 killer diseases. - distribute of 1000 LLINs to affected population and sensitize community on the proper use of the nets. - Provide emergency delivery kits to 500 pregnant women in Ekondo titi Health District. - Sensitize 30,000 inhabitants in crises affected communities on HIV, Diabetes, Cardiovascular diseases, Hypertension, water borne diseases and epidemic prone diseases essential preventive family practices. - Sensitize community members on best menstrual hygiene practices - Recruit and train 10 project staff to ensure project staff- Liaise with all the district medical officer on a monthly bases to improve on monitoring of project activities and ensure quality provision of healthcare services. - Recruit and train 100 Community health Workers to improve on the decentralized model of care- Share monthly reports on the project outcome with Health Cluster lead. This project shall go a long way to reduce excess morbidity and mortality of inhabitants in the Ekondo Titi, Mbonge and Tombel Health Districts.
