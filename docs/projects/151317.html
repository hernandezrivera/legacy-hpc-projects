Project Summary

The main objectives of this intervention are to:
* Improve access to essential healthcare needs and scale up responsiveness to epidemic prone disease outbreaks of vulnerable populations in Marka, Awdhegle and Qoryooley districts of Lower Shabelle region
*Scale up mobile services in the same districts in order to provide access to health services to the displaced and hard to reach population affected by armed conflict and other disasters.

Needs

More than half, (56%) of the communities in lower shabelle region does not have access public health facilities and the region faces increased risks of epidemic prone, endemic, vaccine preventable and other diseases due to protracted conflict and community displacements. * The health sector is experiencing shortages of supplies, essential medicines and skilled human resources in key healthcare facilities and for outreach activities. Health facilities lack the required infrastructure and in some instances, are damaged and closed. Others have been appropriated and thus unavailable to provide the much needed effective surveillance services or serve as referral mechanisms, especially for maternal obstetric complications. The health situation in the three districts targeted will most likely worsen. In these locations, malaria, diarrhea, respiratory infection, disease outbreaks (e.g. measles, cholera) and poor maternal health outcomes are still among the top causes of mortality and morbidity.

Activities

- Provision of immunization services to the under five children and PLW.-Provision of OPD services to boys,girls,women and men.-Provision of ANC and PNC services to the pregnant and lactating women.- Provision of deliveries though skilled birth attendant.- AWD/Cholera prevention and treatment.-Social mobilization and community education.- Staff and CHW training.

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

The population of Lower shabelle faces clan conflict and other militia groups might be a threat to the local population. The project will ensure inclusiveness of all social groups, clans and local authority in its implementation and will closely work with the Somali forces and South West Administration to mitigate the impact of armed conflict and violence during the implementation of the project.

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

SHAHRO will ensure the affected population including women have equal access to emergency health assistance in the targeted support to advance gender equality is based on a gender analysis. SHAHRO shall apply gender segregated community consultation meetings enabling women and girls to address their specific concerns, prioritize their needs and appropriately inform the development of proposed activities, measures are also put in place to maintain gender balance in terms of staff recruitment. Equitable and gender-sensitive participation and decision-making approaches will be adopted throughout the project, including women’s participation in the community Health committees and access to gender-specific health and nutrition information. Community consultations through focus-group discussions will take place during the project implementation, and stakeholders will be involved in decision-making processes to tailor the project to the needs of different gender and age groups. Furthermore, data will be dis-aggregated by age and gender in all recording and reporting
