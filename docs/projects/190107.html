Project Summary

The project aims to provide emergency support and livelihoods early recovery actions in pastoral and agro-pastoral households in seven selected woredas of Somali and three woredas of Oromia regional states. These regions have been affected by a number of natural and man-made problems including drought, flooding and desert locust infestation. These natural disasters have also been complicated by man-made problems such as resource-based conflicts and politically motivated ethnic clashes along regional borders. The target regions host a higher number of IDPs and millions of people with chronic food insecurity and malnutrition. Drought has especially been more frequent and severe over the last few decades leaving little space for recovery of degraded rangelands and livestock production in the lowlands. In response to the dire humanitarian needs of 37,200 selected households located in hot-spot woredas, namely Dolobay, Moyale (SM), Qada Duma, Bokolmayo, Dolo Ado, Adadle and Gode woredas in Somali region and Miyo, Moyale (OR) and Yabelo woredas of Oromia region, VSF Suisse proposes to implement the following key actions which have been prioritized by the Agriculture Cluster, HRP 2022. The emergency livelihoods support and early recovery interventions aim to enhance the availability of animal feed, animal health care, supply of seeds and related agricultural input, facilitate strategic destocking and restocking and income diversification such as IGA. The proposed actions will be implemented following the international and national good practices as outlined in the National Livestock Emergency Guideline and the global Livestock Emergency Guidelines and Standards (LEGS). Animal health services will be implemented following Community-based Animal Health system involving CAHWs and PVPs linked together to achieve sustainable supply of veterinary drugs and equipment. Based on a case-by-case consideration, guided by specific assessment results, those households in extreme destitute will be considered for voucher-based animal health service provision and feed supplementation. These interventions will directly contribute to food security, nutrition and resilience of communities affected by conflict and climate-related challenges. It sustains core agricultural/livestock livelihoods and support early recovery.

1. - Projects activities

The emergency livelihoods support and early recovery interventions include: Enhancing the availability of animal feeds through supply of commercially available animal feed mix and introduction of innovative feed solutions such as hydroponic fodder production; Communities will also be supported with emergency seed and farm tools provision to revitalize normal agricultural/livestock practices and fodder production; Animal Health interventions will be supported through training and equipping of CAHWs and linkage with PVPs; The project will also facilitate strategic destocking and restocking activities based on the ground reality in terms of the market functions and climate disaster forecast; The action will also support income diversification through establishment and training of income generating groups (IGGs), with a focus on women groups who will be linked up with local financial service providers (FSPs). Support to capacity building of local government office to predict and respond to climate disasters through skill trainings; Support to traditional early warning, information and climate disaster response system;

VSF-Suisse will proactively participate on the different platforms both at National, Subnational and District level to share the experience and learn form partners. Also engage partners at the implementation areas during the project launching and to align plans, to ensure harmonization of the approaches and to ensure synergy and complementarity. At grass roots level, it will essentially engage local/traditional institutions and community-based structures such as Pastoral Field Schools, Farmer Field Schools and Farmer Market Schools as entry points and localizing the humanitarian and recovery activities. They will participate in identification and articulation of problems in order to put vulnerability in context and advocate preferred solutions through this project. These structures offer excellent opportunities to effectively engage women and men equitably in economic and livelihood and as well as humanitarian interventions. VSF Suisse will also seriously comply with relevant gender markers.
