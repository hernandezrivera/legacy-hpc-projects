Project Summary

The project comes in light of the people displacement, which took place during the Syrian crisis in many areas, including the Rural Damascus
A lot of families in these areas don’t have the ability to repair their damaged homes, the role of the association here is to assess the number of damaged homes in every area of Rural Damascus including the percentage of damage.
So, this project is created to service these families with rehabilitate their houses, as it aims to secure the returnees to save their dignity and their right to live safety in their areas to enhance their ability to resilience, that is related to the Shelter sector objective No.2 .
During 2024, the association aims to rehabilitate 200 residential units for the IDPs. This is in continuation of the association’s plan, which it began two years ago, to rehabilitate 827 residential units, 220 of them have been completed till now.
The implementing activities including Structural repairs (minor concrete repairs for walls and roofs), Installations of the Sanitary- Water (plumbing works), Installations of Electricity & Solar Power, Carpentry works (doors and windows installations) and Finishing works (flooring and painting). The required activities vary across the homes depending on the level of damage.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The conflict affected on the residential sector more than others, as it destroyed a large part of the infrastructure and real estate properties during the Syrian crisis. In Rural Damascus, the number of destroyed units reached to 40,259 units, while the partially damaged units reached to 103,794 according to World Bank statistics.The returnee’s movement began about 4 years ago, which reach to 65%, but due to living conditions, they faced difficulties in repairing their homes.Where the official estimates from the local municipalities of Rural Damascus, and based on the restoration requests submitted to the association that the number of partially damaged houses reached to 827 houses, where the association rehabilitate 220 of them, while the remaining (607 houses) were distributed as follows, noting that the percentage of damages houses ranged between 40 -70%: Maliha (Maliha and Shabaa), Babella (Babella,Sidi Makdad, Beit Sahm, Aqraba, Sayyeda Zeinab)During 2024, the association aims to rehabilitate 200 residential units. Beneficiary families will be selected according to priority criteria all of them from IDPs, after that the engineering inspection will be conducted on the selected residential units, the quantities of materials needed for rehabilitation will be estimated, and a contractor will be contracted. During the implementation period, periodic monitoring reports and the final evaluation report will be prepared in cooperation with the engineer supervising the project.On the other hand, the project will take into account all safety and security standards to protect workers from potential risks during operations.The project comes in the context of securing integrated and sustainable protection for families, including protection from the risks of assault and sexual violence. This is done by securing adequate shelter and housing that meets the basic living standards for the returnees. In addition, the project ensures aspects of technical protection for the rehabilitated houses in terms of installing doors and windows equipped with safety locks. Equipping the house with alternative energy sources (Solar Power) to provide night lighting and protection against potential risks.Also the project is expected to face several economic and logistical risks during implementation, which will be processed according to the previously prepared risk matrix.
