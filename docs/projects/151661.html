Project Summary

The project will benefit 59,015 boys, girls, women and men from IDP camps, returnee and host communities across Somalia through various child protection interventions. Key interventions will include strengthening community based child protection structures to prevent, respond and refer cases of violence against children , providing case management support and referral services to children with protection concerns including SGBV, providing FTR services to UASC, and preventing of physical and humiliating punishment by rolling out the parenting without violence program. Efforts will be made to ensure equal participation of minority groups like excluded clans, less participating groups like men, female, and people with disability.

Needs

Increased protection risks and violations are common in situations of drought, mostly as a consequence of the disaster and ensuing displacement, but also due to the increased stress disaster creates in a community. The majority of those affected by humanitarian emergencies are children. In times of crisis, children face increased risk of all forms of violence and exploitation. Emergencies both exacerbate pre-existing protection concerns and create new ones. The range of protection concerns faced by children in humanitarian contexts manifest in various forms such as dangers and injuries, physical violence and harmful practices, sexual violence, psychosocial distress and mental disorders, children at risk of being recruited by armed forces and armed groups, child labour and unaccompanied and separated children. However, child protection risks should not be seen as discrete, but rather interconnected and compounding. Save the Children carried a child protection assessment in April 2017 which showed that 85 per cent of respondents in the study areas reported that they were aware of children being separated from their households due to the current drought. The study also revealed that children and parents had exhibited changes in behavior following the onset of the drought. In addition, increased incidences of violence against children such as rape, beating and sexual molestation were reported by more child respondents than adults. The reasons for increased violence against children were reported as children were exposed to more risks at the workplace, or due to living in unprotected shelter. There has been an increase in the number of separated and unaccompanied children, child labour, child and early marriage because of the threat of recruitment due to clan conflicts, forced evictions, and cyclone attacks in 2018. The conflict between Somaliland and Puntland for example left many girls, boys, women and men in dire need to services including protection services.

How is the project linked to durable solutions/resilience?

The project will work to strengthen community based mechanisms (CWCs and CECs) to protect children from violence, abuse and exploitation. The project will also work closely with government and local authorities. These will be involved in mobilization, selection of beneficiaries and in the project decision making. Sustainability will be built into the project by establishing and strengthening community base mechanisms and involving local leaders in the project, it will be possible to sustain actions even when the project ends. Communities will also be supported to manage the CFS.

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

Local leaders may select beneficiaries based on clan favoritism: To mitigate this a clear beneficiary selection criteria will be jointly developed local leaders and community representatives to ensure transparency and selection of beneficiaries will also be supervised by SC staff.

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

There is likely to be low male engagement in some project activities and few female engagement for children activities. The project will utilize the influence of local leaders by working closely with them to woe men to participate in project activities. Girls and boys will consciously be selected to participate in activities.

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

Some beneficiaries maybe overly distressed with the burden due to conflict and violence experiences: The project has designed psychosocial programs to support children and their caregivers to cope with impact of armed conflict and violence.
