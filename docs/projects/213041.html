Project Summary

ONSUR through this project intends to mitigate the damage caused to the environment by substituting the water trucking modality for providing water to the 461,000 individuals under the following activities
Activity 1. (Repair, rehabilitation, and operational support to water systems): 461,000 individuals
Activity 2. Rehabilitation of sewage systems in NWS. 69378 IDPs and host communities
The first activity will benefit the people who live in 22 different communities affiliated to Idleb/Harim, Ariha, and Idleb the city districts rely on the water trucking service, and on diesel-operated water networks to survive with water network modality by re-establishing the already existing water network which consists of 5 sequenced water stations located in Idleb /Jisr Al Shugur/ Jisr Al Shugur/ Jisr Al Shugur in an artificial lake called Ayn az Zarqa. It is an existing system that can cover significant areas. According to the database shared by the Assistance Coordination Unit (ACU) and the WASH cluster dashboards revealed that these communities can be supplied with potable water by rehabilitating the already existing water stations in Ayn az Zarqa. These communities and their adjacent scattered camps are in sever need to WASH services like hygiene kits, and hygiene promotion. Idleb/idleb/idleb /idlebreceives water supply as the following: 1)60% (Seiger project implemented by Goal) water network: water derived from boreholes which are affecting the groundwater level and consumes diesel which is a high-cost methodology. 2)40% water trucking. 240,000 individuals live within this city regardless of the daily visitors who also need water shares that will be considered within the project. Remarkably, the water shares are not sufficient. The water station re-establishment project will be implemented on two phases (10 months and 6 months). The second aspect of the project is covering the open main sewage channels in Idleb/ Harim/ Dana/ Sarmada which continue to Aleppo/Jebel Saman/Atareb/ Batbu is expected to be implemented through 8 months. According to the latest HNO and the feedback of the local Authorities in NWS, there is a remarkable issue resulting from the open sanitation channels especially, the main outfalls-the discharge points which cause hazardous impacts that have leaked into the soil and groundwater and the food chain.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

According to the HNO of 2024 the prolonged Syrian crises, let the situation of the internally displaced persons IDPs exacerbate, and the demolishment of the already existing water facilities like damns and water stations caused by the striking earthquake which occurred on the 6th of February 2023 in NWS and Turkey. In addition to the absence of strategic and coordinated humanitarian responses in NWS which caused even more damage to the environment on many levels. The excessive use of the energy resources and the groundwater in NWS led to the drop off the levels of the ground water within the region, Additionally, the randomly implemented WASH projects (sanitation systems without taking into consideration the discharge points) The same practice is being applied by all the NGOs by contracting with the local vendors who discharge the desluged sewage fluids to the adjacent streams or rivers caused the contamination to the water sources and the ground water as well which increased the level of the water borne diseases like cholera and leishmaniasis.
