Project Summary

The current anglophone crisis has led to many untold challenges faced by families in the Northwest and Southwest region. UNICEF in their 2022 report for Cameroon has reported that they are many children in need of educational assistance. This need has become so urgent due to the burning down of school buildings, and displacement of parents from their comfort zones. Also, most parents to these children are women who can barely feed or take care of their children’s school needs. The project primary goal is to improve the education outputs for boys and girls, primarily in nursery and primary schools. This would specifically also include interventions designed and carried out to address social and cultural bottlenecks of girls and boys at the household level, to be able to attend school, promote inclusive education activities at the learning institutional level and address underlying socio-economic factors that burden the families of the children. The project will also do distribution of school kits (learning, teaching materials), Capacity building of teacher in Inclusive Education and PC, and Construction of school latrines.
The project target the most vulnerable children including out of school children who have not attended school regularly, children who drop out in the middle of the school-term, children with physical and learning disabilities, girls who are not able to attend due to family socio -cultural priorities and are at high risk to child marriage, and children that are poor where families depend on their children to contribute to the family income and household needs. The project will ensure that; children and their families and caretakers have improved socio-economic conditions at the household level; girls and boys receive education cash grants; children with disabilities gain access to inclusive education and feeding for nursery school kids will be subsidized. Project will focus on children who are victims of inequality, specifically girls and those living with disabilities in the targeted areas. Capacity of schools’ administration and parents’ teacher associations will be strengthened. This project lasting for 9months will directly impact the lives of more than 2,000 children in more than 30 schools.
