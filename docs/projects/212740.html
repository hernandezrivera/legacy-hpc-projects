Project Summary

In 2024, 16.7 million people are assessed to need humanitarian assistance across Syria, up from 15.3 million in 2023. Of the 16.7 million people in need, 5.5 million are displaced.
After 12 years of hostilities and persistent economic crisis, people in
Syria their situation worsen when a series of earthquakes hit northern Syria. As Syrians grappled with these shocks, economic indicators
took a further downturn, increases in commodity prices continued to
drive humanitarian needs in Syria. This resulted in an increase
in poverty and reliance on humanitarian assistance (HNO, 2024)
This project aims to increase the food security and enhancing the livelihoods of IDPs and host communities in western rural Aleppo and Idleb Governorate based on the following activities :
A- Free bread distribution
ONSUR will Support 35,000 HHS 160,000 individual of IDPS and host communities by daily free bread bundles (800g/ for each family of 4 people / 6 days a week (except for Fridays) in rural Idleb, rural Aleppo and Raus Al-Aien for twelve months
B- Food basket distribution:
ONSUR will distribute 2000 food basket for 2000 HHs of the most vulnebility families of IDPs and host communities families in rural Aleppo and Idleb camps regularly for eight months. The beneficiary selection stage will consider the vulnerability degree and will give priority to food-insecure families, especially women headed families.
The Calculation of food basket based on (family size of 5 people for a period of 30 day).
The food basket will be at a minimum 70% of the daily kcal intake which is around 1550Kcal per person per day in a monthly family food.
C- E-Voucher assistances
ONSUR will support 2000 HHs, 10,000 induvial of the most burnability families of IDPs and host communities families in rural Aleppo and Idleb camps regularly for eight months by E-Voucher assistances for six months.
ONSUR will strongly committed to integrating a gender-sensitive approach through all stages of project management.
ONSUR has zero tolerance for any kind of abuse and exploitation against women, girls, and children/ by the project beginning, ONSUR will conducted PSEA training for field staff inside Syria.
All ONSUR Staff will attend the prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse (PSEA)
ONSUR will be committed to the FSL cluster and protection cluster recommendations will be taken into consideration to avoid any GBV/PSEA risk.
The S&S department will follows up on any development in the project area, conducts a daily risk assessment of the area, and informs the administration of any possible scenarios and how to deal with them, including evacuation and contingency plans and training the field staff on all expected scenarios.
ONSUR will coordinate closely with the I/NGOs that work in the project area under FSL umbrella to avoid overlapping
