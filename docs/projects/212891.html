Project Summary

After years of the long war and with the Syrian crisis approaching its thirteenth year, compounded by additional crises, the heaviest of which was the destructive earthquake that struck southern Turkey and northern Syria on February 6, 2023, leaving thousands dead and tens of thousands injured, exacerbating fragility in the community and expanding the vulnerable population in the northwestern region of Syria. In a related context, the Ataa Humanitarian Relief Association has been able to provide emergency response services across all humanitarian sectors to hundreds of thousands of beneficiaries during the war years. Gradually, it shifted towards community empowerment after establishing a fundamental asset base and implementing several projects that enriched its experience in empowering the community, paving the way for the development phase by activating the role of the Early Recovery Program, providing high-quality education, psychological support, and mental health services.
Ataa has established three vocational and technical training centers for youth in northwestern Syria in Atma, Al-Bab, and Jarablus. The construction of these centers adheres to high standards, providing a safe and suitable educational and training environment for both genders, in addition to focus on the inclusion of PWDs by providing adapted courses and accessible centers with technical supervision allows easily accessible to all segments of society, and enhance the positive impact on PWDs and their impact on their famileis.
Atma center is fully equipped to host project activities, while Ataa will work on equipping both Al-Bab and Jarablus centers through this project, providing services to a total of 1400 male and female beneficiaries.
Summary of the planned intervention:
Aligned with the main project goal of empowering communities and individuals to become active and productive members of their societies, Ataa will deliver the following services within its centers:
• Atma Center (Idlib Province) - Jarablus Center (Aleppo Province):
• Establishing criteria for selecting beneficiaries published on Ataa's platforms and in project areas during the beneficiary registration application phase. These criteria include:
• Literacy
• Knowledge of technology and computer usage
• Residency in the project area
• Commitment to training hours to progress to the next stage
• Trainees from both genders
• Internally displaced trainees within the project sites
• Passing a pre-test for each applied specialization.
• Equipping and furnishing the Jarablus center with the necessary materials, equipment, and tools to create a safe and inspiring environment for trainees and staff.
• Providing high-quality digital training focusing on developing the skills of male and female youth, enriching the labor market with distinctive skills, and enhancing the resilience of the targeted communities by increasing the number of active individuals and income earners, especially given the demand for these skills locally and internationally. Ataa will also enable beneficiaries to benefit practically from the freelancing platform established within the same center, applying the acquired skills and launching their projects as a trial phase. Specialized training areas include: digital marketing, programming, website design, voiceover, artificial intelligence skills, instant translation, and content writing, contributing to the development of employability skills in the digital innovation world.
• Providing specialized training in entrepreneurship to center trainees, giving them the necessary knowledge to start their projects and identify the goals, challenges, and risks associated with project launch.
• Accepting project proposals from trainees as individual projects based on the digital training and entrepreneurship training provided, with project proposals adjudicated based on technical criteria developed and approved by the Early Recovery sector in response to northwestern Syria.
