Project Summary

Climate shocks including drought and flooding as well as ongoing conflict contributes to internal displacement in Somalia, with an estimated 2.6M men, women, girls and boys who are currently displaced. This people/ IDPs are now living in IDP camps with lack of enough basic services including shelter, The 2020 Gu’ Season flooding has caused several displacement, damage of properties, housing, farmland and social amenities and also Massive displacement especially in the river rine areas of Middle Lower Shabelle areas.
This areas are also prone to conflict crises particularly in South Central Somalia where part of it is under al-shabaab control hence contributes to more displacement and increase the need of the IDPs who were already in need 0f basic services, this also contributes to the protection concerns of vulnerable groups e.g. women, girls and people with specific needs and also people with disabilities.
Through this project, Juba Foundation aims to address the humanitarian needs of the displaced IDPs and poor host communities in poor shelter conditions in south central Somalia.
The project will focus on improving the lives & dignity of IDPs and poor host communities across Somalia through the provision of locally purchased Non food items (NFIs) Emergency shelter kits (Esks) through in-kind distribution where feasible and if there is functional market with shelter and NFI materials cash or voucher can be an option. But at the moment Juba Foundation is Flexible to use either cash or in-kind Distribution.
For the beneficiaries’ selection and registration, juba foundation will give consideration to the female headed Household. People with specific needs among other households. The host communities will also be given consideration with selection of 20 – 30 %, similarly 15% will be selected from persons with disabilities in each project.
Juba Foundation will ensure the affected population will have full protection and improve their dignity and living conditions through the provision of appropriate emergency shelter kits (Esks) for each household will receive ESK( type 2 with a size of 4.4m x 3.6m) depending on land availability. For the provision of NFIs each household will be provided with one NFI kit as per the cluster guidelines along with the Esks.
The proposed intervention will focus on providing of Esks (1,500) and NFIs (1,500) that consist of 1 plastic sheet, 3 blankets, 2 sleeping mats, 1 kitchen set, 1 solar lamp, 1 mosquito net and 2 jerricans. That will total up to 9,000 Individuals.
Juba foundation will train the camp leaders and camp committees on selection of beneficiaries, HLP issues, site planning to ensure all shelter and NFI projects meet the required cluster standard.
