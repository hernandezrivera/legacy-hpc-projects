Project Summary

The proposed project will improve psychosocial wellbeing and social cohesion in the conflict-affected areas of Mopti and Timbuktu regions. HI will target a total of 16,000 direct beneficiaries in 56 communes of the regions of Mopti and Timbuktu, including 3,681 people in need of PSS, also benefiting from IGA (184 beneficiaries), kits (920 beneficiaries) and rehabilitation of safe spaces (2,945 beneficiaries). In addition, HI targets 484 community focal points, 560 members of local protection committees, 216 members of local peace committees, 8,400 beneficiaries of direct information and awareness raising sessions on social cohesion and 2,700 beneficiaries of community projects (in 18 communes). HI estimates that 83,390 persons will be reached through mass media information campaigns on social cohesion and peaceful cohabitation.

1 - Contexte

The central and northern regions of Mali have been the main theaters of the military and inter-community crisis that Mali has been experiencing since 2012. These regions are both the main departure and hosting areas for 287,496 internally displaced persons (IDPs). According to OCHA, 3,300,000 displaced people and host populations are in need of protection . In Mopti region, there are 113,043 IDPs, (54% women; 58% children). In Timbuktu region, there are 43,617 IDPs (56% women; 59% children). Women are at heightened threat of domestic violence, with 49% reporting to be victims and of those more than 68% are unable to access a care service, including psychosocial care . More recently, a UNFPA investigation on the impact of COVID-19 on gender-based violence (GBV) in Mali warned that the pandemic could increase women's exposure to GBV by 49%.From November 9-13, 2020, HI organized focus groups in four communities of Timbuktu and Mopti in order to collect the necessary information. Community leaders and women highlighted the psychological impact of violations on victims; "you see people with psychosocial troubles and no one has time for them." In the 2020 Humanitarian Needs Overview, mental health is identified as one of the vital needs of the displaced, returnee and host populations in Mali4. In 2018, the Mopti region was ranked in second position as an area of high prevalence of mental disorders, with 385,650 cases, reporting the absence of formal support mechanisms and lack of national resources in the field (only 3 clinical psychologists in Mali).
