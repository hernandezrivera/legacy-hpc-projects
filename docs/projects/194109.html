Project Summary

Since the 15th of April armed conflict spiraled between the SAF and the RSF leading to mass displacement, huge causalities and complete collapse in the basic service sector( health, water, energy and food) . According to the 2023 HNO the four most significant risks identified are conflict, disasters associated with natural hazards, disease outbreaks, and economic deterioration. UPO focuses on maintaining an inclusive, sustainable and accepting implementation environment that results in community collaboration and ownership . and due to the recent war the bad situation has become worse for the already vulnerable refugees, this project aims to 1) Increase access to lifesaving basic service to the
most vulnerable South Sudanese refugees in Darfur 2) Enhance the community capacity to
prevent risks, neglect, violence, trafficking and ensure effective protection for the at risk
and most vulnerable refugees/returnees populations. 3). Provision of livelihood coping
mechanisms to the most vulnerable CAR refugees to enable them produce to meet their
food demand and increase self reliance.4) provision of WASH ,Protection, health and education services to
the refugees in Eastern Sudan.

1 - Needs

since the WAR breakout in 15, April, 2023. thousands of refugees left Khartoum due to insecurity situation ad lack of services, thousands reported staying in Eljazeera state. the Ethiopian refugees in Gadaref still inadequately supported, the humanitarian situation is bad and the living status requires urgent supports in all sectors. South Sudanese and COR refugees continue to be in urgent needs for assistance in diverse sectors and since the beginning of the year, almost 152,000 South Sudanese refugees have arrived in Sudan, bringing the total number of arrivals to some 400,000 since conflict began in South Sudan in 2013. although large response is ongoing, there is a significant gap in WASH, Education protection, livelihood assistance. In conflict contexts, women and girls bear the brunt of the violence and brutality as rape, sexual abuse, sexual exploitation and other gender-based violence are common. The forms of violence and abuse encountered by minors including child marriage, child labor, child recruitment by armed groups, unaccompanied and separated children and child trafficking. Women and youth are also the most dynamic in seeking day to day opportunities for income to cover partial need of their demand which has not been satisfactorily met throughout this period; this exposes them to risks, violence, and neglect. Provision of livelihood coping mechanisms is urgently needed to help the women, girls, boys to meet their demands and contribute to self-reliance. It was observed that, most of the family’s male and females are sharing houses with their extended relatives and accommodated in shelters built partially from local materials (bamboo, sticks and grass) but lacking preventive materials that protect them from rain and fire , therefore there is an urgent need for ES and NFIs. Refugees living in Umrakuba, Anteneitba are in desperate situation and lack of urgent supports not limited to water, education and health. Increased caseloads of CAR refugees in Umdafug ,Alradoum areas, with the majority settled in temporary sites are posing a health risk in each of these areas, . UPO will integrate GBV risk reduction strategies into the implementation of the activities through the consultation of women in locating resources and services, as well as including them as members of the decision-making committees that govern these resources and services
