Project Summary

The project will provide holistic life-saving and life sustaining nutrition intervention to scale up current nutrition intervention through establishing static and mobile outpatient therapeutic program sites in Badahde and Kismayo districts hence provide active community case finding, screening, admission and treatment of severe acute malnutrition cases boys and girl <5 children in the targeted internally displaced person . Southern Aid shall enhance referral services to the affective and most complicated cases to the nearest stabilization center in Kismayo and Badadhe town to enable provide it adequate attention to the severely malnourished children in the internally displaced person. Southern Aid shall also provide routine immunization, deworming and vitamin A supplementation services to <5 children boys and girls as well as multiple micronutrient to the target beneficiaries. It shall provide integrated management of acute malnutrition and infant and young child feeding program and basic service for nutrition packages training packages to its technical nutrition staff as well as community health workers to enable them deliver effective management of the IMAM programs in the target Internally displaced person in the target district. Southern Aid (SA) will be able to provide integrated management of acute malnutrition training package training packages to its technical nutrition staff as well as community health workers to enable them deliver effective management of the integrated management acute malnutrition programs in the target internally displaced person in the target district .The project will integrated with food security cluster in which Southern aid is also intending to undertake the project in the same area hence improving the life of the vulnerable population both Internally displaced person returnees and drought affected pastoralist in the target area.

Project Activities

1.Provide therapeutic and supplementary treatment to 2,000 severely acute malnutrition to vulnerable boys and girls in Kismayo IDPs, returnees and Host community as well as Badhaade district. 2.Provide treatment to 1,000 moderately malnourished pregnant and lactating women in Kismayo and Badhaade district Lower Juba regions. 3.Provide basket supplementary to 1,000 pregnant and lactating mothers and 2,000<2 years boys and girls in Kismayo and Badhaade district. 4.Improve project staff capacity through provision of integrated management of acute malnutrition (IMAM) and basic EPI training to 30 staff (20 male and 10 Female). 5.Improve referral of severely acute malnutrition for effective an urgent management of severe cases with medical complication targeting vulnerable under five boys and girls in Kismayo and Badhaade district.

Mitigation Measures

The project targets to intervene in Lower Juba more specifically Kismayo IDps,Badhade and Afmadow district where there is a large number of internally displaced person as well as returnees from the boarder Dadaab refugee in Kenya however, this different diversity in the target areas may create conflicts or community tension among the already weak IDPs, returnees and host communities on resources sharing, to mitigate this Southern aid will enable to provide equal and transparent project information and target beneficiaries hence reducing the community tension. Also lack legalize land for constructing permanent health facilities is a big challenges and may create animosity between the various stakeholders, where such issues raise Southern aid will ensure closer coordination and information sharing as well as government guidelines on the best way possible as well engage the community for durable solution.Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and agePoor beneficiary data dis-aggregated on needs may challenge to enable reach the actual people in needs, to mitigate this Southern aid will strive to conduct pre- intervention assessment as well as provide mobile clinic to the far rural areas with limited access to enable save the life of the vulnerable groups.Location and distance of the health facility may be challenge for the boys,girls and pregnant and lactating women in emergency , SA shall be able to ensure the health facility is closer to the beneficiary to avoid any risk related such as sexual violence/abuse and explosion of war remnant. Unfriendly health facilities infrastructure will discourage the disable and physically challenge members of the community, to mitigate this SA will ensure all health facility are up to date and health cluster standard required ensuring further on gender sensitivity twin latrine hence friendly to all user including boys and girls.Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violenceProject staff capacity will be built on prevention of sexual exploitation among the vulnerable women, girls and boys as well the staff will sign code of conducts, Southern aid will ensure to promote all-inclusive consultation and feedback within the project staff as well as the community to avoid any gender based violence among the displaced and returnees. It will also ensure to prioritize the most vulnerable among the target population giving more consideration on women, boys and girls.
