Project Summary

The general objective is to upgrade women headed shelters especially those who have people with special needs and chronic diseases in the eastern region of Khan Younis governorate in the areas of Khuza'a, Abasan al kabera, and Abasan al jadida, by supporting marginalized families in rehabilitation their houses and upgrading their current housing status .
This intervention responds to the HRP strategic objective #2 (improve their access to essential services ) and to Shelter Cluster Objective #2 (support to vulnerable households exposed to harsh weather and protection concerns )

Needs

The poor economic and physical situation, the division and the large number of political parties and conflicts have negatively impacted the Palestinian people in the Gaza Strip, specifically the governorate of Khan Younis, in the eastern regions, and also affects their access to the basic needs (housing, education, health services, etc.) and their inability to develop their families. And their communities because of the increasing numbers of the population and the limited space available for housing construction and lack of employment opportunities and low level of household income. According to statistics provided by the Palestinian Central Bureau of Statistics, the population of the Khuza'a area is estimated at about 13,950 inhabitants, Bani Suhaila area with about 77,000 residents, and the Abasan al jadida area with about 7800 inhabitants, while the population of the Abasan al kabera is estimated at about 25,000. The proportion of females in each of these regions is 49% of the total population The results of the FGDs with a number of dilapidated households showed that in 20/3/2018 , There were at least 1,000 houses in need of upgrading and rehabilitation, of which 300 were already registered with the Society. Questionnaires were filled out by the researchers. Based on the results, the 73 poorest families were selected. In addition to that, it was found that the interviewed households suffered many problems including racked sealing, damaged windows, unavailability of valid rest rooms, and different problems that affect the health and security of households, furthermore, some of the interviewed householders Through field visits by researchers and questionnaires, 73 needy families were selected to rehabilitate their homes especially female households reported that they face different privacy issues in their houses due to the unavailability of valid doors, and windows. As for the safety of children in their houses, the results of the FGDs showed that many households believe that their houses are not safe for their children, since some of them reported 1- ground is not made of concrete .2- Water leaks from Zinko coated surfaces in the winter and insects enter homes in the summer3- The walls are cracked with winter water and insects in summer 4- bathrooms are unhealthy moist filled with bacteria and insects 5- Kitchens are grounded in sand and have no doors and water jacks that are unusable and no shelves which may transfer different diseases to their children. The project in hand aim to rehabilitate and upgrade the most affected women headed houses, the targeted number of houses for the project is 73 house , these houses are suffering from very bad infrastructure, not suited for living, and violates the privacy of women and the children rights to play and live in a safe environment. *poor HH with no continuous source of income and do not benefit from the MoSA aids. **conflict affected HH: are those who were affected by the war, and have received an amount of money to upgrade their houses, however this amount of money was not sufficient for the required upgrade

Activities

The project is mainly aimed at the rehabilitation and upgrade of dilapidated houses (women headed houses), and the owners of these houses cannot afford to rehabilitate or upgrade their houses due to their poor economic conditions. The project targets a total of 73 women headed houses, distributed among targeted areas The information obtained through the field visit of the worn out houses is verified by filling out questionnaires by an engineer and field researcher 1 preparing a detailed list of Target group as per shelter cluster minimum standards. 2 employment a qualified staff to determine the exact needs of the targeted houses, 3 Association will preparing Tenders and offers for the required resources The average cost of shelter upgrades for each HH is about $ 2000. In general, the project will have direct and indirect benefit on at least 511 people, including 254 women and at least 306 child, 135 adolescents of both sexes aging from 18 59, and 70 elderly people of both sexes The design of rehabilitation works will take various beneficiary needs into consideration such as : Privacy concerns of women and girls at each HH individually or through special focus group discussions Needs of people with disability and elderly Upgrading wash facilities ( kitchen , bathroom and toilets ) as well as protection from external threats including weather extremes Walls, holes and holes shall be sealed with cement Repair damaged windows and reuse old windows Lift the non-concrete roofs to ventilate the house when using heating stoves The inner walls are covered with a cement layer to cover the holes Rehabilitation of the bathroom with a shower and an Arab toilet in case of preference of the beneficiary Restoring the facilities of the residential unit (shower, bathroom, kitchen, ramp entrance) for persons with disabilities and safely leaving in case of fire discuss with beneficiaries on the best way to reduce the disturbance during construction and rehabilitation works The project will be implemented through contractors driven approach by The tender will be announced in the official gazette on the official working days for the implementation of the project activities through the receipt of a tender letter to the suppliers described in 1- Information about the supplier2. Information on the tender3. Invitation to participate in the tender4. General and special conditions5. Table of Quantities. The association maintains a strong monitors and evaluation and learning system which includes a suitable complaint mechanism to respond to the beneficiaries claims . this includes : Capturing the situation before and after intervention to show improvement, and to show the effect of the project interventions on HH through communication with beneficiaries get lessons learned and beneficiary satisfactory through post intervention surveys The data provided by the target group will be validated by different field visits conducted by specialized and trained field researchers Periodic follow up visits will be conducted to ensure that the project implementation is as planned The association will allow beneficiaries to submit their complaints and recommendations throughout the project duration The project interventions will be closely monitored by specialized team, and the progress will be compared to needs assessed to determine whether the intervention meet the needs beneficiaries The association will conduct different interviews and FGDs with the targeted groups upon the completion of the project to assess the project success and to identify achievement By filling out questionnaires and field visits with the target group to identify, document and monitor the level of satisfaction with the service provided Through the Complaints Fund and the Proposals Fund Periodic achievement reports will be submitted and analyzed to assess whether there are any gaps, in order to effectively response to these gaps
