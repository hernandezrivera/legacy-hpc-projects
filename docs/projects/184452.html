Project Summary

• Wajid district Pastoralists & Agro pastoralists communities in Bakool region has been seriously affected the impact of the current Somali climate crises 7 Conflict which resulted prolonged drought & endless displacement, the district is used to host hundreds of IDP’s those majority of them have fled from the close & remote villages and presence / live in the district for a long period with less access of livelihood stability from the humanitarian organization based in the region; however approx. 24% of these IDP’s & returnees has arrived recently due to the past GU and the ongoing Dayr rainfalls failure.
The majority of these drought & Conflict affected populations including (IDPs, Returnees from the neighboring countries, host communities) are agro-pastoralists and pastoralist with limited access to their local farms production and livestock hearing that is being hit by the drought which made their livelihood extremely difficult to sustain.
CCCM and IOM's rapid assessment indicated that IDPs in Bakool are lacking basic needs and Basic food, education due to the prolonged life stable instability. As well as to their traditional livelihood type of agriculture and small livestock rearing lost, IDPs are left with no means to sustain their life while limited humanitarian assistance barely meets their basic needs. Although women headed households make up the majority of IDP population in Wajid / Bakool region, they have limited access to income and livelihoods and rely mainly on casual labor. 70% of The climate crises hit IDPs spend have no household income to manage the daily food of their children / families, . Around 90% of the IDPs rely on restricted expectation of humanitarian assistance or otherwise can die for a hunger, In order to support the vulnerable IDPs in Wajid, there is an urgent need to provide alternative livelihood assistance such as (Food voucher, Cash assistance, vocational training including life skills based on the market demands to increase their household income and decrease reliance on assistance) by giving priority to the women. Relief Emergency to the targeted districts and communities will not only increase their income level in the short term but they will also contribute to their transition towards durable solutions
• To improve diet diversity at the household level through improved access to affordable nutritious food
• To provide immediate household access to food and increase purchasing power to the most vulnerable populations in emergency throughout the year and to people in crisis during the livelihood lean season by providing monthly food vouchers and unconditional cash relief/transfers
• To restore community productive assets through provision of livelihoods inputs and develop alternative livelihood strategies for vulnerable population, realized via provision of farm inputs, vocational training, temporary job creation and daily stipends for maintainers of productive assets.
• To Improve food access to school going children in vulnerable communities by
• providing them with daily meals to prevent further food insecurity and malnutrition for the school children
in this project, WWC will target 400 HH’s / 2,400 individuals those will get Food voucher through Cash assistance with a period of 6 months, each HH will earn 70$/ Month for family food survival in this critic situation.
