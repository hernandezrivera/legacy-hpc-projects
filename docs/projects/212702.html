Project Summary

The project aims to provide essential non-food items (NFI) to vulnerable populations affected by conflict in Aleppo and Homs, as identified in the Humanitarian Needs Overview (HNO) 2024 and the Multi-Sectoral Needs Assessments (MSNA) conducted in October 2023. Dorcas will align the distribution of NFIs with sector-specific items recommended by relevant authorities.
Identification of beneficiaries will be conducted through community centers in Aleppo and Homs, targeting vulnerable populations such as those affected by the earthquake in Aleppo who haven't returned home, large families, families with disabilities, families with newborn babies, and older persons. Additionally, the project will target vulnerable cases in Homs through activities conducted at the community center in Al Waer.
In Homs, Dorcas will identify sub-districts that did not receive support previously and will follow the guidance of the sector to ensure equitable distribution. Moreover, the project will emphasize protection considerations, ensuring that vulnerable populations, especially women, children, and persons with disabilities, are prioritized in the distribution process. Protection mechanisms will be integrated into the operation to safeguard the dignity and safety of beneficiaries throughout the distribution process.
