Project Summary

The project aims to increase access to education and ensure retention in schools for 3,310 boys, 2,516 girls (of these include 310 boys and 272 girls living with disabilities) from IDP and vulnerable communities while working with the community in Gedo Region to shape durable solutions to education dropouts and other access constraints. This project will be implemented in Belet Hawo, Luuq, Dollow and Garbaharey districts with the aim to address a lack of access physical, safe and inclusive learning facilities for primary school children, a lack of quality learning materials, a lack of trained teachers, economic barriers including a lack of menstrual education and facilities for girls. To achieve this aim, Trocaire, working with a local education partner, will seek to address pending gaps in its areas of intervention by providing the following: Teacher incentives to 74 teachers across four districts (this will complement ongoing teacher incentives to 106 more teachers); provision of teaching and learning materials to 18 schools; renovation of 20 gender friendly latrines for 6 schools; rehabilitate 17 learning spaces; hold monthly hygiene promotion sessions across 18 schools with distribution of dignity kits to 1300 girls; hold bi-monthly protection promotion sessions in schools identification and training of 36 Child protection focal points, two in each of the target schools; establishment or reviving of Child Clubs; conduct teachers training on pedagogy for the 74 new teachers with refresher trainings for the 106 existing teachers; school fencing for 10 schools; conduct management trainings for 36 school heads and their deputies as well as 33 female and 75 male CECs across schools of intervention; and training of key stakeholders on accountability.
Protection mainstreaming will be ensured by creating a safe learning environment that encourages positive dialogue on inclusivity in schools, tailored service provision especially for girls, and collective monitoring of protection outcomes in schools. Child protection activities will be incorporated to further tailor services to the needs of children. For instance, protection promotion sessions will include structured recreation activities where children will freely express themselves, child protection focal points will be identified and trained to support children, and cluster- advised trainings for teachers incorporated for improved management of school environment.
This project is based on the gaps and needs identified within Trocaire’s internal monitoring activities within its integrated Humanitarian programme across Education, WASH, Protection, Health and Nutrition sectors, complemented by the findings of the Education cluster, and the Joint National Multi-Cluster Assessment conducted in July 2020. This project is aligned to the 2021 Education Cluster objectives; ensuring that 5,826 emergency and crisis affected children have access to safe and protective learning environment; that these same children are engaged in life-saving learning that promotes well-being and social cohesion, and strengthening the capacity of teachers and Community Education Committees to deliver effective and coordinated education. The project will complement Trocaire and other partners’ ongoing efforts, more specifically addressing the gaps within Trocaire’s education programme in Gedo, while extending education services to under-served districts such as Garbaharey.

Project Activities

Trocaire aims to support schools with the following activities: • Payment of teacher incentives to 74 teachers (15 in Beled_Xawa, 20 in Luuq,15 in Doolow and 24 in Garbaharey for 12 months. This is in addition to the 106 teachers already in place. This will be USD 110 for teachers and USD 120 for head teachers. • Renovation of 20 gender friendly latrines for 6 schools in Garbaharey district. Four doors (2 separate for girls and 2 separate for boys) will be renovated complete with water system, handwashing facilities installed near latrines. These will also include disposal buckets especially in latrines used by girls.• Provision of teaching/learning materials to 18 schools. • Printing and distribution of COVID - 19 prevention/response IEC materials to the 18 targeted schools. • School fencing to 10 schools to ensure safety of children from trespassers (for both girls and boys), danger emanating from armed conflicts, dignity for girls as they visit the latrines, school land demarcation and for school trees/environmental conservation. These are 4 schools in Beled_Xawa, 4 schools in Dollow and 2 schools in Luuq. • Guided monthly protection promotion sessions held in schools with the schools CECs, teachers and pupils’ representatives to enhance and inculcate the need to ensure gender inclusivity, girl-child rights, etc. • Identification and training of 36 Child protection focal points, two in each of the target schools. Once identified, they will be trained by Trocaire’s protection team on PSS First Aid and made aware of the existing child protection services referral pathways.• Establishment or reviving of Child Clubs. One teacher and one CEC member will be trained to facilitate discussions within these clubs mostly to focus on children’s rights, anti-bullying and conflict resolution. • Establishment or strengthening of existing Feedback and Response mechanisms in target schools. Teachers and pupils’ representatives will be trained on the various Feedback and Response mechanisms that exist, and be guided to identify those that will be suitable for use in their schools. • 216 hygiene promotion sessions (1 session per school every month for all the 18 schools for 12 months) held in schools, including distribution of dignity kits to 950 girls. • 6 community outreach sessions (bi-monthly) once in every 2 months held with the communities and led by CECs to strengthen parental and caregivers’ knowledge and skills to support children in school enrolment, as well as educate the community on their responsibility to protect and support children and ensure their safe access to and at schools as per the Safe Schools Declaration;• Three Teachers’ training on pedagogy, teacher Code of Conduct, basic child protection, psychosocial and school management- to 74 new teachers, with refresher trainings to existing 106 teachers. • Three School management training for 36 (18 head teachers, 18 Deputy headteachers) to equip them with the requisite skills of school management with a focus on COVID 19 prevention and control in school environments, child and teacher management, accountability in schools, stakeholder liaison, resource mobilization and financial prudence. • Six CEC trainings for the 18 schools CECs for 108 CEC members (6 per school). The trainings will be conducted to build their capacity on COVID 19 prevention and control, school infrastructural safety and maintenance, accountability, resource mobilization, school development planning, child protection and gender. • Training of 3 DEOs on accountability in schools during joint visits. • Rehabilitation of 17 existing learning spaces, including construction of ramps and wide doors to improve access for children with physical disabilities.

Mitigation Measures

Risk 1Likelihood: MediumImpact: HighSchools will be fenced and staff trained on child protection. The school supplies will be distributed in schools and girls encouraged to move in groups and report any fear/suspicion to the established Trocaire or any other available Feedback & Response Mechanism. Trocaire’s interventions are restricted to accessible schools in both rural and urban areas, and involvement of CECs, DEOs and communities is prioritized in identifying schools to be targeted. This ensures safety of children while accessing schools, and reduced protection concerns to and from their homes. Risk 2 Likelihood: MediumImpact: HighWhile protection referral services remain novel in Gedo Region, Trocaire’s protection team has prioritized protection services, including the strengthening of existing referral mechanisms to ensure women and girls receive assistance in a timely and safe manner. One key activity planned for year 2021 is to link schools to this referral mechanism, and train CEC focal persons (one per school) as a referral point for school- going children. To mitigate against this risk, Trocaire shall strengthen school girls’ clubs and support their in-house forums on a monthly basis as a platform to discuss common challenges and sharing the same to the school management (the headteachers/deputy headteachers) and the wider protection stakeholders for improved service delivery/ risk mitigation. In addition, girls will be exclusively targeted with sanitary kits and will have their own segregated WASH facilities to minimize risks associated with abuse and promote access with dignity. Risk 3Likelihood: LowImpact: HighRenovation of existing classrooms to incorporate ramps, wide doors for wheel chair users, incorporating training on Special Needs Education (SNE) inclusion for teachers and CECs.Triangulating data on how many boys/girls are living with disabilities and enhancing representation and participation in all school programmes. These include class activities through teachers, during core-curriculum activities and when seeking feedback on the quality of service delivery by Trocaire through client satisfaction surveys, weekly/monthly routine monitoring by field staff. Inclusion of girls and women in consultations and decision-making processes throughout the project cycle is ensured by encouraging meaningful participation of women and girls in CECs, school clubs, and other school leadership structures. Girls are actively consulted in peer meetings, school meetings and club meetings. Their views are also collected from regular monitoring sessions, where participants are selected in a representative manner. Mitigation measures against exclusion of children with disabilities:Trocaire will aim to work with teachers, CECs and other local community structures to undertake the following steps: - To develop capacities of CECs and other local stakeholders (DEOs, Headteachers, teachers, parents) to take part in the broader plan of disability inclusion.- Increase the number of trained teachers in schools by targeting 15 new teachers in addition to continuing support for 106 more teachers;- To identify systematically girls and boys at heightened risk at the start of the project and ensure their needs are regularly assessed using participatory approaches. Reference will be made to and technical support sough from Humanity & Inclusion in this segment of beneficiary identification and triangulation of needs at the onset. - To design and implement protection responses and assistance programmes jointly with girls and boys at heightened risk and within their schools.- To develop partnerships and referral mechanisms with other protection and assistance actors (including national partners) who have relevant expertise and adequate capacity to support girls and boys at heightened risk.

Please provide further explanation

- Is the protection environment sufficiently safe/stable to enable durable solutions?While the protection environment in the target districts cannot be said to be completely safe to implement long-term durable solutions, opportunities to enshrine sustainability exist through the community structures that Trocaire works with. That is why Trocaire will work closely with the CECs, the teachers and the community at large to increase ownership of the project and create a conducive environment for them to learn, manage and serve as advocates of quality and equitable education service delivery within their communities. Trocaire will also be working with local education partners, who are present in Gedo, to further encourage collaboration and coordination in delivery of services. This will ensure Value for Money and collective efforts in empowering the community and the children in Gedo to advocate for education rights with the local and regional authorities. Trocaire’s larger education programme incorporates capacity building of District Education Officers, who are encouraged to work closely with community structures in identifying education needs and jointly advocating for education resources for all children. - Does the project support a government initiative or policy that protects the rights of displacement affected communities? Yes. Trocaire promotes the rights of displacement- affected communities as spelt out in the National Policy on Refugee- Returnees and Internally Displaced Persons. Some of the principles that Trocaire promotes include: Protection during displacement by working closely with communities to ensure inclusion of children from IDP communities; the right to an adequate standard of living by providing integrated humanitarian services across its larger humanitarian programme; purposefully including children with disabilities and working with communities to create a favourable environment for them; training communities on importance of inclusivity and working closely to eliminate discrimination tendencies; and coordinating all efforts with government structures at the local, district, regional and national levels, and with other NGOs, especially through the education cluster. - Does the project leverage on existing community structures, systems and existing networks with a clear benefit for the acceleration of processes of local integration return or resettlement? In order to encourage local integration of IDP communities in Gedo Region, Trocaire has been working closely with school heads, teachers, and most importantly CECs to build their capacity on peaceful co-existence and encourage sharing of resources. In the targeting process, Trocaire has been coordinating closely with other cluster partners to ensure inclusion of IDP children in schools. Some of the challenges include discrimination of IDP children from accessing schools. Therefore, working closely with school communities, Trocaire has been monitoring the mpact of inclusion trainings through school calendars, and encouraging less profiling of children, unless it is based on assessment of the protection risks different categories of children face. The District Education Officers are also supported to participate in the process through on-the-job capacity building during joint visits. - Will the entitlement of beneficiaries to services/utilities provided be legally recognized?Yes. This project is informed by a rights- based approach in provision of education services. Trocaire recognizes that every child has a right to education, and therefore Trocaire will work closely with all stakeholders to ensure that Children of Gedo enjoy this right. It also seeks to achieve SDG 4- Access to Quality Education, and therefore is designed to achieve objectives under SDG 4 as spelt out by Somalia Government in the 2020-2024 National Development Plan. Trocaire shall prioritise partnership, accountability and inclusivity in delivery of education services.
