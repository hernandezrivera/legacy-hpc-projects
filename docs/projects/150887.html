Project Summary

This project is aimed at improving the lives of 22092 (3682HH) newly displaced individuals (10604 women, 7070 men, 2651 girls and 1767 boys) living in precarious crowded settlements, without sufficient access to basic services, with poor shelter conditions that raise protection and health concerns. This will be achieved through provision of emergency kits and NFIs to the most vulnerable IDPs in Nimcaturabi and salama2 camps in Daynille, Boqolson and Macin Alle camps in dharkinley and Balsar and maslah camps in Afgoye.
The target population (women, girls, men, boys, the elderly and people with disabilities) living in the IDP camps reside in small makeshift structures made of rugs, recycled cloth and card boards. These structures are insufficient in so far as providing the basic physical protection, privacy, dignity, or protection from extreme climatic conditions
Included will be returnees and IDPs living in deplorable conditions and in need of emergency shelters and NFIs. The most vulnerable men, women, boys ,girls and the elderly will be prioritized during the beneficiary selection process. Save Somali Women and Children will Introduce special arrangements for persons who have difficulty accessing the distribution point or unable to carry the kits. Save Somali Women and Children will directly implement the activities.

Activities

1.Distribution of appropriate NFIs to the most vulnerable 12594 HH (men 4030, women 6045, boys 1008 ,girls 1511) through cash and vouchers . The activities will include selection and registration of the most vulnerable individuals. Priority will be given to women, female-headed households, children, child-headed households, the elderly and persons living with disabilities. This will be done with the aid of camp leaders and camp committees. Subsequent to the selection of the most vulnerable Beneficiaries, the NFI kits are going to be purchase through competitive bidding considerations will be put on quality and pricing. Vendors will then be selected from within the selected target areas. Vendors will be selected in a public and transparent process in which small traders will be encouraged to participate. Save Somali Women and Children will ensure the location of and access routes to distribution points and services are safe with enough space for orderly distribution. The distribution centres will be central and the distribution will take place in daylight hours giving beneficiaries sufficient time to travel home before nightfall. Save Somali Women and Children will Introduce special arrangements for persons who have difficulty accessing the distribution point or unable to carry the kits.2. Provision of emergency shelter to the most vulnerable 9498 HH (men 3039, women 4559, boys 760 ,girls 1140). The beneficiaries will be the similar ones provided with the NFI kits. Priority will be given to women, female-headed households, children, child-headed households, the elderly and persons living with disabilities. This will be done with the aid of camp leaders and camp committees. Subsequent to the selection of the most vulnerable Beneficiaries, the emergency shelter kits will then be distributed to the beneficiaries.

How is the project linked to durable solutions/resilience?

The distribution of emergency shelter and NFIs to the to the most vulnerable affected population of the this project is itself the beginning of contributing to the durable solution which is to make people resilient to the shocks. Save Somali Women and Children will engage the government and the communities on this project which are all currently working on a durable solution in the IDPs. Continuous dialogue with the local authorities will take place from the onset of the response through durable solutions. SSWC develop exit Strategy to help resolve tension that may arise after the withdrawal of the food assistance program since all stakeholders are involved.

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

There is expected risk of conflicts between the host communities and the selected vulnerable beneficiaries from the IDP community. The distribution of NFIs and emergency shelter kits may aggravate the tensions between IDPs and host communities and increase risks of exclusion and marginalization, or even trigger violence against IDPs.Save Somali Women and Children will include the vulnerable host community members as part of their beneficiaries. the host community will also be included in the sensitization and planning meetings.

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

There is a risk of insecurity which may result to Gender Based Violence in the process of accessing distribution points and returning to the IDP camps.Save Somali Women and Children will ensure the location of and access routes to distribution points and services are safe with enough space for orderly distribution. The distribution centres will be central and the distribution will take place in daylight hours giving beneficiaries sufficient time to travel home before nightfall.People living with disabilities may have difficulty accessing the distribution point or unable to carry the kits.Save Somali Women and Children will Introduce special arrangements for persons who have difficulty accessing the distribution point or unable to carry the kits.More so proper targeting will be followed to ensure equitable distribution of the resources to all.

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

Beneficiaries may be at risk of insecurity while accessing the assistance and service provided by the project. Save Somali Women and Children will ensure the location of and access routes to distribution points and services are safe with enough space for orderly distribution. The distribution centres will be central and the distribution will take place in daylight hours giving beneficiaries sufficient time to travel home before nightfall.
