Project Summary

According to the latest humanitarian need assessments in Syria in 2021 , the number of food insecure people has increased approximately 45 percent from last year which reflects the deteriorated situation in NW-Syria as a result of the ongoing war. While the fact in the ground is harsher as the households in the conflict areas from their homes daily from seeking security in the safer areas which is mainly crowded by the IDPs. Also, inflation and currency depreciation pose a major threat to food security and livelihoods in Syria as well as the unemployment rates are believed to be at 50 percent, with only 16 percent of women working in the labour force.
The project aims to meet the food needs as an urgent humanitarian response in Aleppo province- Suran subdistrict through distributing of 1000 food baskets to 1000 households for eight months sequentially. The beneficiaries in the intervention will be mainly from IDPs with a percentage of the host community of the most in need households in the target areas including the newly displaced in order to contribute to strengthening their ability to tolerate the situation through providing food assistance that meets their basic needs. The food baskets will provide the families with 2100 Calories which is equal to 100% per person per day as Sphere standards. The project location will be as the following:
Aleppo Governorate – Azaz District – Suran Subdistrict.
This intervention will target the families which meet the selection criteria and at the same time we are going to focus on the families that have not received any food assistant during the last three months. The food basket’s weight is approximately 80 Kg. Thus, the food baskets will be distributed to the IDPs to their living places. In addition, the food basket will be procured inside Syria in order to support the local markets

4 - Needs

According to the latest HNO 2021, The situation continues to deteriorate drastically with an estimated 14.2 million people in need of some form of food and agriculture assistance, compared to 9.8 million in the previous year. The food insecurity PiN has risen by 45 per cent in the last year and at least 12.4 million people are estimated to be food insecure, an increase of 57 per cent compared to 2020, of which 1.3 million people are considered severely food insecure. Food prices in Syria have increased dramatically in 2020 and WFP’s national standard reference food basket price in November 2020 was 251 per cent higher when compared to November 2019. MASRRAT conducted many visits and needs assessment in Azaz district in Aleppo rural. Wherein, the results showed that the IDPs suffer from the general shortage of basic food items. Also, all the surveyed people assured that food is the biggest need for them, in addition to shelter and especially the IDPs people who live in the random camps, informal camps and uncompleted or uninhabitable houses in Suran subdistrict where, there are four random camps Suran region that suffer from difficult living conditions in these camps. Where it is considered one of the most necessary needs is Providing the vulnerable food insecure populations with monthly food assistance through appropriate modalities to most vulnerable households.

5 - Implementation of activities

At the beginning of the implementation of the project, MASRRAT's team will sign a Memorandum of Understanding (MOUs) with the local council in the area. The process of engaging the Local Council will start with advertising about the project in the communities of intervention, explaining the mechanism of selection, and the number of potential beneficiaries under this project. Advertisements of the activity will be published and will include selection priorities. The application form will include questions about basic personal information of the family. Data collection of the beneficiaries will be conducted at the beginning of the project (in the preparation month). The data will be verified based on the criteria of selection. Delivery of food baskets will be according to paper voucher and according to distribution lists for 8 months. The project will follow the procurement policy of MASRRAT, as the food items will be procured from local markets in Syria.The team will distribute the baskets to the families for eight consecutive months. MASRRAT M&E team will follow up implementation of the activities, where the team will verify the potential beneficiaries based on the specific criteria to have the final lists after that the team will monitor packaging of baskets in the suppliers warehouse, distributing of baskets, conducting of PMD and following up the complaints if existed.The food basket will provide the family with 2100 Calories which is equal to 100% per day as per Sphere standards. The targeted locations will focus on the crowdy IDPs in the random camps in Suran area who did not receive any food support during the previous three months.

Risk management process designed to include all of project life.
