Project Summary

The action is targetting vulenrable groups: migrants in DC, internally displaced population and hosting communities that are suffering from the deterioration of the acces and quality of health care services in the intervention areas. The activities will include the adaptation of the service delivery according to the needs of the specific groups: deployment of mobile health teams delivering integrated medical services including MHPSS, reproductive health and SGBV care, referrals for cases who will need specialized care, community mobilization and awarness raising regarding the most common health issues as well as reinforcing the current capacities at fixed PHC structures. The quality improvement will include capacity building of the health professionals, implementation of standard procedures and rehabilitation and equipment of the structures to garantee that the services reach the standard requirments (including universal standards and Libyan authorities standards). PUI will also work on reinforcing the health system capacity to respond to emergencies, it will include prepardness to disease outbreaks, mass casuality events and mass displacement of populations.
This action will be directly delivered by PUI teams in close coordination with a range of actors and agencies; MoH , LRC , DCIM, UNHCR, IOM, UNICEF, OCHA, UNFPA, ICRC

Needs

Vulnerable populations dont have access to health care services, because of geographical obstacles, economic, social rejection or lack of understanding and information about the availability of the services.Facilities witnessing frequent stock outs for the essential drugs because of the weakness of the stock management system.Data collection system is very poor leading to problems in forcasting the needs, Health Facilities buildings are deteriorated and equipment are missing or not functionnalInfection protection and control procedures are not implemented leading to putting the health care users at risks.Lack of emergency response mechanism in case of specific hazard; mass displacement, disease ourbreak or mass casuality events; the response mechanisms should include coordination body, contingency stocks and pre-established procedures that are disseminated amongst the different actors

Activities

Run mobile health teams delivering a range of medical and protection services including integrated primary health care, MHPSS, reproductive health and SGBV care . Perform trainings of the care givers in the supported facilities on managerial skills including : IPC procedures, stock management, data collection and waste management, more clinical trainings will also be delivered according to the needs in terms of case management.Refer patients that need specialized care including payment of health expensesRehabilitate and equip health facilities to insure that they meet standard requirmentsCreation of coordination mechanismsCreation of emergency response plans, Prepositionning of contingency stocks
