Project Summary

The project will be implemented in Idlib- Harim-Dana district, where the education severity scale for PiN is 4/ extreme in total. 356588 children (3-17) at school age and 11886 educational personnel are 3-5 on need severity scale. The estimated number of beneficiaries of WATAN 2023 project is 23094 (16719 formal education students - 3750 non-formal education student - 2000 ECE children – 625 schools’ staff), with a total number of 590 CwD. Estimated # of disability beneficiary: 2.5%. This project will support the educational activities in 25 schools in Idlib city, Harim, and Al-Dana districts (camps) – Idleb. The project contributes to the education cluster and HRP objectives through: 1- Project will increase safe and equitable availability and access to in-person and home-based formal and non-formal education for crisis-affected children (aged 3- 17 years). 2- Enhance the quality of formal and non-formal education for children (aged 3- 17 years) within a protective environment. In 2023, The project will target 25 schools through: 1- Rehabilitation of 200 classrooms, facilities and sanitation services to access healthy clean drinking water and segregated toilets. 2- Providing 25 schools with running cost, supplies, etc. 3- Providing 16719 students (5-17 years, 8125 boys & 8594 girls) with formal education and PSS activities and stationery. 4- Providing 3750 (1688 boys and 2062 girls) low-achieving and dropped out of school students (5-17) years, with non-formal education (remedial and catch up classes) and stationery 5- Providing 2000 children (3-5 years, 1100 girls/900 boys) with ECE for 9 months at the same schools. 6- Providing 625 school staff (344 male, 281 female) with capacity building and monthly incentives. In case of spreading of Covid 19 pandemic and the suspension of work at schools, schooling will depend on hybrid education approach ( face to face and via WhatsApp and other electronic channels based on cluster guidance for COVID-19 response, in addition to visiting the school several times by week, considering the rules of social distancing, preventive and personal protection measures. WATAN will distribute Personal Protection Equipment to the targeted schools including masks, gloves, etc. as a contribution from the WATAN side in the project. The project will ensure provision of safe drinking water and water for sanitation facilities at schools. Any Cholera cases among students and teachers will be dealt with according to cluster and educational authorities measures. Raising awareness sessions about cholera and Covid19 will be conducted with students and educational staff to decrease infection possibility. The project will increase the boys’ and girls' students with disabilities by providing schools with the necessary ramps, tools and equipment’s to be appropriate with disabilities and will train the staff on CwD inclusion education. The affected population will be involved in all the stages of the project planning , designing , implementation , Monitoring , Evaluation where there will be FGDs , PDMs after distributions, satisfaction surveys and distributed items and field monitoring visits , discussions , coordination , consultation and meetings with the parents , teachers , children and local education authorities in the schools on choosing the best ways for implementation of activities and maintain high levels of accountability to affected population during after COVID-19.WATAN (MEAL) Team will be responsible for developing a monitoring methodology that will include the relevant indicators to track project progress and different tools and steps: Collecting information and other relevant data. ii-Conducting observation checklists in the schools. iii-Collecting complaints and feedback. Iv-Conducting evaluation surveys (interview-BNFs) v-Conducting monitoring interviews with the head of schools. The project is not funded yet.

3 - Needs

Based on HNO2023, ACU 2022, PiN Severity reports and WATAN assessment of the educational services at 25schools at Dana sub-district, the findings were:15% of children are out of school. No preschool learning services for(3-5)CH. Lack of access to secondary education. Schools not able to accommodate CwD continue to limit their access to education. CGs could not afford education services or children working to support HH. Schools continue to be unconducive due to lack of school furniture & supplies, insufficient heating and WASH facilities. Teachers need provision of incentives and capacity building to ensure quality of education. So, 25 schools have been selected by WATAN according to these needs:1-Schools are not supported, and CH are at risk of drop out.2-the schools include host and IDPs pupils. 3-Those schools have (8125 boys & 8594 girls) enrolled in formal education. 4-There are about 3750 low-achieving and dropped of school pupils who need non formal remedial classes. 5-There are 625 teachers and education personnel working without incentives. 6-The 25 schools do not provide ECE for 3-5 children. 7- 200classrooms and wash facilities need rehabilitation to keep boys and girls privacy. 8-Those schools need provision of running costs, education material, and other hygiene and protection supplies. 9-children need bags and stationery kits. 590 PwD need ramps,direction signs, and welcoming schooling environment through PwD inclusion training and sessions for teachers and CH.

Conduct 25 Back-to-schools campaigns. 3. Provide 16719 children (5-17 years, 8125 boys & 8595 girls - 425 PWD) with formal education. 4. Provide 3750 children (5-17 years, 1688 boys & 2063 girls- 100 PWD) with non-formal education with same time with formal Education. 5. Support 2000 children (3-5 years, 1100 girls/900 boys) to enroll in ECE in same schools in summer months. 7: Launch the teacher's recruitment process focusing on both Male and females including PWD. 8. Provide all staff 625 (281 female & 344 Male – 37 PWD) desegregation (500 formal, 100 non-formal, 25 ECE) with incentives and training them on Active learning, Classroom management, and Inclusive Education. 7 - Protection Risk Analysis

1.Risk:Teachers, edu personnel participate in physical punishment, act violently, bully and neglect students. Mitigation: Teachers will be trained and monitored on prevention and reporting of violence, physical punishment and bullying and the practice of positive discipline & Child Safeguarding policy. Recruitment of teachers should evaluate candidates soft skill competencies & pedagogical skills that ensures enrolling vulnerable girls, IDPs & PwD2. Risk: Discrimination against PWD, IDPs & girls at schools. Mitigation: WATAN will enforce an inclusive education approach to include all the beneficiaries in the targeted schools3. Risk: The schools don’t protect the children from flood & rain. Mitigating: WATAN will do rehabilitation to the classrooms and wash facilities.4. Risk: PwD, girls& female teachers may face SEA due to insecure and inaccessible wash facilities. Mitigation: Sex and age Segregated toilets & provision of door locks. Wash facilities will be accessible by PWDs through provision of ramps, special seats for PWD.5. Risk: COVID 19 and Cholera: Not adherence to preventive and IPC related measures can increase the risk of disease spread. Mitigation: monitoring of adherence to preventive measures.6. Risk: Female teachers and girls at schools may also face harassment and abuse. Mitigation: Train staff on Do No Harm, (PSEA), and child safeguarding. sign and adhere to code of conduct that respect and uphold the rights of beneficiaries and PSEA.


8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Risk sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA), including harassment, against women and girls. Risk Management Strategy/Mitigating Measure: Mainstreaming PESA in all the phases of the project When hiring the staff of the project and education personnel in the schools through signing the PSEA policy and code of conduct for all staff and education personnel in the schools, conducting PSEA sessions for all staff and, providing internal reporting mechanism on PSEA, and clear reporting channels that are accessible for all staff and All WATAN staff including field staff that is working directly with beneficiary are regularly trained on PSEA and reporting channels. providing orientation for beneficiaries PSEA reporting Mechanism, through multiple channels (lines, Posters, email, Focal points) and MEAL Dept will: 1- Pay a visit to schools and ensure the safety of children and staff. 2- All beneficiaries will be able to contact WATAN through complaint and suggestion email and phone numbers to follow up on all complaints. 3- All feedback from the community will be discussed in the monthly meeting.


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

WATAN through conducting this project considers These main risks:1-Air attacks and indirect shots. Likelihood: Low Impact: High. Mitigating measures: Project location is in a relatively safe area because of its closeness to the Turkish border, so the probability of safety threatens is low. In emergency cases, WATAN will provide all schools with safety and security tools to ensure the safety of schools and staff inside the project location.2-Intervention by local authorities. Likelihood: Middle Impact: High. Mitigating measures: The project will coordinate with two main entities: local authorities and other organizations. WATAN will sign an MoU with ED to clarify targeted schools, planned activities, and interventions and take the authorities recommendations into account to achieve project target and community benefits.3-A lack of skilled teachers. Likelihood: Middle Impact: High. Mitigating measures: Training teachers on formal and non-formal education curricula.4-Children dropping out of formal education to attend Non formal Education centers. Likelihood: Medium Impact: High. Mitigating measures: Identifying specific criteria to all implementing partners for accepting children into the program.5-Cholera spreading in the targeted areas. Likelihood: Medium Impact: High. Mitigation measures: the WASH facilities especially the water and sanitation ones will be sanitized, disinfected periodically according to Health and Hygiene authorities protection measures.
