Project Summary

The project seeks Upgrading of IDP sites to ensure safe and dignified living conditions for the most vulnerable people in northwest Syria, with a focus on appropriate preventive measures to alleviate the effects of flooding and prepare for winter.
The project seeks achieving long term as well as short term objectives, in terms of rehabilitation of essential access roads and infrastructure that ease the service access to IDPs sites in all seasons.
Through the maintenance/rehabilitation of access roads, the the physical access for water trucking and food basket distribution as well as medical services will be ensured. Local rapid deployed teams in the targeted communities will be established to report the occurrence of incidents and conduct/supervise the maintenance works.
The project will create short-term work opportunities for the most vulnerable IDPs through Cash for Work Modality within the rehabilitation works.
The project targets 12 Km of road rehabilitation on long and short term scale.
150 CFW short-term work opportunities will be created for duration of 36 days.
Targeted locations are Quorqeena, Maaretmisrin and Dana dub-districts.
HLP validity of rehabilitated roads as well as the guidlines of the CWG for daily wage will be applied.

4 - Needs

Almost ten years of crisis in Syria have continued to erode the socio-economic foundations of the Syrian people [HNO 2021]. Gaps in infrastructure continue to be a real concern, made worse by flooding incidents. The SNFI Cluster continues to advocate for improved infrastructure implementation in IDP sites and camps [Situation Report, OCHA, 2021]. As the informal camps are spreading on a large scale in Idleb and Aleppo governate with many of them are located within the valleys, water streams, and agriculture lands, they are exposed yearly to floods and other factors affecting them negatively and cause ruins in roads and infrastructure. In such contexts, delivering the basic assistance is becoming more needed. Therefore, any impediments in the access roads will cause delay in reaching the BNFs at the right time, which could lead to loosing lives.The WASH Cluster in agreement with the Shelter Cluster and Syria Cross Border Fund will address the issues of gravelling of roads and construction of proper drainage to safeguard infrastructure and make roads passable to deliver humanitarian aid. 5 - Implementation of activities

The short term intervention will be implemented as follows:1. Developing Contingency Plan.The CP will be designed for the high risky regions and those that characterize higher probability that a disaster or emergency situation may occur such as flood and snow.2. Proposing Action Plan and rising the awareness by IDPs for the potential risks.The plan will include determining actions, time frames , deploying teams and volunteers in order to address the needs of IDPs when the crisis occurs. There will be net of volunteers and read-to-operate contractors and machines. 3. Conducting long-term agreement with service providers in different areas and sub-districts in order to be ready for the road blocking.4. Implementation of the emergency response through the deployed teams, procured contractors and volunteers. The long term intervention will be implemented as follows:1- Assessing the flood-prone areas that are exposed to floods in last three years and havent been targeted by rehabilitation works. Additionally, the access roads that characterized obstacles and impediments in access will be assessed. Accordingly the designs and detailed BoQs will be delivered in line with the road rehabilitation guidance recommended by ERL cluster and created TWiG. Additionally, the Guidelines of Flood mitigating measures will be applied.2- The rehabilitation plan will be finalized and tendering process will be carried out.3- CFW modality will be applied during the implementation phase.
