1 - Needs

1) The presence of Ukrainian armed forces and armed groups in populated areas increases the risk of sexual violence against civilians. The deterioration of the economic situation, particularly in conflict-affected regions, combined with the destruction of community ties caused by the conflict and displacement, have compelled some people to use harmful survival strategies and coping mechanisms that may increase the risk of sexual violence and trafficking; 2) National legislation and legal practice regarding the prosecution of sexual violence is limited and is not fully in line with international standards and practice. Due to a number of gaps in legislation and a lack of capacity, acts of sexual violence are often recorded by law enforcement as other crimes such as bodily injury. Lawyers, police officers, prosecutors and judges lack knowledge of how to document, investigate and consider cases of conflict-related sexual violence, as well as with regards to allegations of ill-treatment and torture. Consequently, victims of sexual violence are often confronted with inaction from state authorities or armed groups who exercise control over certain areas; 3) According to the analytical report “Gender-based violence in the conflict-affected regions of Ukraine” by the Ukrainian Center for Social Reforms published in 2018 there is no common practice to seek assistance from specialized institutions in cases of violence. Amongst the main causes for not applying for the assistance are unawareness of the availability of the services, absence of proper institutions in communities and mistrust in service personnel; and 4) According to the data within the ‘Emergency protection-based support to conflict affected older women and men in the GCAs locations of Donetsk and Luhansk Oblasts’ presented by HelpAge on 19 of September 2018, 20,5% of older people aged over 60 were subjected to at least one type of violence and abuse. Of them 96,9% reported emotional and psychological abuse, 0,9% reported gender-based violence, 0,4% - physical violence and 0.2% reported sexual type of violence. Among the elderly who subjected to at least one type of violence, share of women were 75,9%.

2 - Activities & outcomes

Outcome: Conflict-affected community members in the Donbas increased their capacity to prevent and respond to Gender Based Violence Output 1: Conflict-affected community members in the Donbas are better informed on GBV issues, including the importance of gender sensitivity in family and community activities, through community-led activities Activities for output 1:1) Conduct training on GBV-related issues for the community members;2) Provide consultations with the community members for the development of community led initiatives;3) Open a call among the targeted communities to apply with community-led development initiatives to share the information about gender and GBV issues;4) Select community level development initiatives through the competitive selection process;5) Monitor the implementation of community led-initiatives. Output 2: Local officials from police, social services and other relevant stakeholders in targeted communities are better prepared to provide relevant services regarding the GBV issues Activities for output 2:1) Select local officials to participate in the trainings on gender-based violence;2) Select expert trainers in line with IOM HR procedures;3) Training materials on GBV for local authorities developed by the GBV Sub Cluster partners published to be disseminated among the training participants; and3) Carry out trainings developed based on training materials prepared by the GBV Sub Cluster partners for selected of local officials on gender-based violence issues. Output 3: IDPs and other vulnerable persons are better informed on the GBV though the awareness raising campaigns and events. Activities for output 3: 1) Outreach to IDPs, conflict affected population and other vulnerable persons through information sessions, round tables and workshops and other targeted prevention efforts; and2) Carry out an awareness raising campaign to inform on the right to a life free of violence and about the existing help lines and complaint procedures. Output 4: Conflict-affected community members in the Donbas are provided with targeted PSS support focusing on GBV issues, including the importance of gender sensitivity in family and community activities, through community-led activities Activities for output 4: 1) Psychosocial support through individual and family counselling sessions;2) Group counselling sessions focusing on addressing specific challenges including GBV, coping with stress, etc.; and3) Community-level psychosocial activities, including but not limited to community dialogue sessions, art therapy sessions, workshops on coping with stress. IOM is also an active member of the Humanitarian Country Team (HCT), Inter Cluster (ICWG), as well as an active member of the Early Recovery, Shelter/NFI, Wash, Protection Cluster and the Cash Working Group, among other technical coordination working groups. Under the current project strong cooperation with the GBV Sub cluster and actors providing assistance to GBV survivors is foreseen. Activities will seek to take advantage of a gender expert with experience working on GBV issues.

Output 2: Local officials from police social services and other relevant stakeholders in targeted communities are better prepared to provide relevant services regarding the GBV issues - # of trainings on gender-based, domestic and conflict-related sexual violence for local officials organized (Target: 30 trainings);- # of training participants (Target: 750 participants, including 450 female and 300 male) Output 3: IDPs and other vulnerable persons are better informed on the GBV though the awareness raising campaigns and events - # of round tables conducted involving local authorities and representatives of target communities (Target: 15 round tables);- # of individuals that actively participate in the round tables (Target: 375 individuals, including 225 female and 150 male)- # of awareness raising campaigns on GBV related issues (Target: 15 campaigns); - # of leaflets with the information about GBV related issues printed out and distributed (Target: 30,000 leaflets);- # of persons covered through awareness raising campaigns (Target: 40,000 individuals, including 24,000 female and 16,000 male, out of whom 8,000 children, 24,000 adults, 8,000 elderly). Output 4: IDPs and conflict-affected community members in the Donbas are provided with targeted PSS support - # of beneficiaries who attend PSS activities with GBV focus (Target: 375 individuals, including 225 female and 150 male).
