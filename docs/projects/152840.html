Project Summary

Mundri east and West counties in western equatoria state has registered increasing cases of malnutrition across all demographic cadres in 2018 as compared to previous years.With the recent signing of a new peace agreement in south Sudan between the government and the rebel group,majority of the people who had been displaced from their households and villages as a result of the crisis have started to return to their original residences,security has reportedly improved across the two counties and people allowed to move freely with few incidents reported since June.There is huge need for scale up of nutrition services within the region so as to be able to reach a majority of the population that had been left out due to insecurity as a major accessibility issue. ACEM is currently operating at 50% coverage within both counties but hopes to scale up to above 85% with funding availability.Many households remain food insecure as there has been no adequate opportunity to cultivate bearing in mind that Mundri east and west are largely dependent of subsistence agriculture as their main source of livelihood.Coupled with the current economic crisis that has greatly affected overall household income ,the food security situation is worrisome as observed during the 20th round of the FSNMS which revealed and increase in number of food insecure households,an increase in proportion of households with poor consumption from 43% to 67%, and limited coping mechanisms.
In line with the nutrition cluster priorities,this intervention seeks to improve the nutrition situation and prevent malnutrition related mortality through scale up and provision quality life-saving management of acute malnutrition for the most vulnerable and at-risk under-five children and pregnant and lactating women,increased access to maternal infant and young child nutrition preventative services and increased access to integrated nutrition services in mundri east and mundri west counties.The project will target children under five years and pregnant and lactating women as the most vulnerable groups. ACEM will also consider beneficiaries with TB and HIV/AIDS and nodding disease that lie within the admission criteria.
The major activities under this intervention are;(but not limited to) treatment of severe acute malnutrition among children 6-59 months,which will target 1,852 children for admission (900 boys and 952 girls).
Treatment of moderate acute malnutrition among children 6-59 months. At least 3,210 children will be admitted in TSFP (1,500 boys and 1,710 girls)
Treatment of malnutrition among pregnant and lactating women with 2,940 PLWs being targeted for admission and treatment.
Training at least 50 health and nutrition care providers on management of acute malnutrition using the approved CMAM guidelines for south Sudan
Provide quality counselling on MIYCN services at nutrition sites and in the community which will see at least 9,137 caretakers of children 6-23 months reached for counselling through individual and group sessions.

Needs

Its been two years since onset of the crisis that affected greater equatoria region causing high level insecurity.The communities in mundri east and mundri west counties are still coming into terms with the violence that changed the entire context of the once bread basket of South Sudan, after populations were displaced,livelihoods destroyed and insecurity crept in.Agriculture, being the main source of livelihoods was greatly affected over the years as there has been limited access to cultivation land due to insecurity as well as displacement of the cultivating population. As much as the recent SMART surveys conducted in 2018 April in both counties of Mundri east and Mundri west revealed a GAM rate of slightly above 5.1%,Mundri has seen a rise in admissions as compared to the same time in the previous year 2017.According to the recent IPC report for September 2018, western equatoria was classified under IPC phase 3 -crisis phase.As per 20th round FSNMS,there is a more worrying food and nutrition security situation in mundri east and west with 24% and 17.4% classified as severely food insecure ,41.7% and 34.1% HH registering poor food consumption ,22.6% and 11.8% HHs with low dietary diversity scores,78.6% and 68.1% HHs registering moderate hunger scale respectively .There is also an increase in proportion of households adopting to emergency coping strategies.The current malnutrition situation in mundri east and west counties is likely driven by the prevailing insecurity that has affected food production and accessibility to beneficiaries,protracted economic crisis characterized by hyperinflation and depreciation of the South Sudanese pound which has had an effect on household income thus affecting food availability at household level and access to markets,soaring food prices leading to reduced purchasing power thus affecting food availability at household level ,high cereal crop deficit owing to the fact that Mundri population is highly dependent on agriculture as the main livelihood source with cereals like maize and sorghum forming main part of their diets as a staple food .Other factors affecting the malnutrition prevalence in mundri east and west counties include; poor access to health services,increased morbidity especially among the under 5s,poor diets consumption and poor hygiene and sanitation.Western Equatoria ,is also well known for its high prevalence of HIV and AIDS in south Sudan,Men,women ,boys and children living with HIV/AIDS and TB in Mundri east and Mundri west counties experience multiple stresses trying to cope up with the disease effects coupled with inadequate food,access to proper healthcare and other important services thus exposing them to risks of malnutrition.Most affected are pregnant and lactating women and children under 5 years.During the crisis,many girls were forced to drop out of school due to reasons such as insecurity and displacements,many schools were not functional as well.As a result ,there has been increased cases of teenage pregnancies thus teenager girls remain vulnerable to risks of malnutritionACEM at the moment is providing nutrition services in 20 out of the possible 40 facilities/nutrition sites in Mundri east and Mundri west.Through the SSHF fund ,ACEM hopes to scale up and improve service coverage as security and accessibility continue to improve with the signing of the new peace deal,so as to reach more beneficiaries with nutrition services.Facilities currently operational include;Mundri ,Gulu,Mbara,Amadi,Bitti,Kotobi,Karika,Bari ,Garie,Ledingwa,Bangolo in mundri west and Lakamadi,Kasiko,Kediba,Doso,Wandi,Buagyi,Mideh,Mariba,witto,minga in mundri east.ACEM hopes to scale up by 20 more facilities ,9 in Mundri west i.e-moba,Tore-wandi,Groubalow,Diko,Naini,lockua,Kulundu and mandi, and 11 in Mundri east namely;Agyi,Bekenye,Magyiri,Singiriwa,Wirrow,Movo,Lanyi,Lozoh,Gabat Doro with support from the cluster and SSHF funding

Activities

The following activities will be carried out under the implementation of this project in order to realize our objectivesTreatment of severe acute malnutrition among children 6-59 months .This will be done in line with the CMAM guidelines.Screening will be done at facility and community level and children found to be having SAM basing on Oedema,MUAC or WFH cut offs will be admitted basing on the criteria stipulated within the guidelines.Children with SAM with medical complications will be admitted in the stabilization centers in lui hospital and mundri PHCC while those without medical complications will be admitted on RUTF and monitored till discharge to TSFP.ACEM will seek to admit at least 912 children in mundri west and 940 children in mundri east.Treatment of moderate acute malnutrition among children 6-59 months .Basing on the CMAM guidelines,treatment for MAM cases will be done under TSFP program .Admissions will be directly from community or facility screening and also children discharged from OTP for continuum of care.This will target 1562 children in mundri east and 1648 children in mundri west.Treatment of malnutrition among pregnant and lactating women,identified through screening.only women who are visibly pregnant and those having children less than 6 months of age will be screened and those fond with a MUAC less than 23cm will be admitted in the treatment programTrain 20 health and 40 nutrition care providers on management of acute malnutrition using the CMAM guidelinesMonitor availability of core pipeline supplies at nutrition sites through routine update of cluster supplies tracking matrixProvision of quality MIYCN services in nutrition sites and at community level through well trained MIYCN counselors as per the south Sudan MIYCN guidelines.The counselors will provide MIYCN counselling at facility and community level to caretakers of children less than 24 months ,children admitted under SAM or MAM as well as the PLWs. Mother to mother support groups will be formed to enable learning through experience sharing from peer mothersTest and treatment of malaria among children with SAM aged 0/6-59 months. ACEM will test and treat malaria among children admitted in Mundri and Lui stabilization centres.Integrate nutrition into WASH,FLS,health and other existing programs at community level.ACEM will work closely with the health partners on ground to ensure that all children being treated for malnutrition are screened and treated for malaria and that children 0-65 months are linked to the existing health services such as growth monitoring,deworming,vitamin A supplementation,mosquito nets distribution,Immunization and treatment of common childhood ailments,Provide WASH NIF kits to children with malnutrition and install hand washing facilities,clean toilets for the beneficiaries use at nutrition sites,conduct safety audit/assessment at nutrition sites to identify and address women and children’s protection/safety needs through protection actors,linking households with malnourished children and PLWs to existing livelihood programs in the community.Accurate and consistent reporting on activities to the nutrition cluster through the NIS system at 100% rateReporting on treatment performance outcome ; cure rate @>75%,defaulter rate @ <15% ,death rate at <10% for both MAN and SAM managementAccurate and consistent Reporting on funding secured through the established FTS system by OCHA
