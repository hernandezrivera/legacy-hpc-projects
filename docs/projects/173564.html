Project Summary

The proposed project will support the ongoing WASH sector activities in Borno state by providing a comprehensive WASH approach to implement complete WASH packages and accountability to affected people in the hotspot locations of Gwoza LGA in Pulka Ward specifically in (Camp C and sector 5 in the host community), Konduga LGA, Jere LGA, MMC, and Mafa LGA.
MI will provide WASH life-saving services through improving access to safe and sufficient drinking water supply, safe/adequate and dignified sanitation facilities, hygiene supplies, and information to mitigate risks related to public health with mainstreaming of protection, gender equality for vulnerable IDPs, returning refugees, and host communities that meet the differing needs for men, women, boys, girls, and persons with disabilities. Activities will also include cholera preparedness and response, construction of water and sanitation infrastructure to newly-arrived IDPs, operation and maintenance of existing WASH infrastructure including water supply systems, latrines, and bathing facilities, solid waste management and distribution of hygiene kits, which are in line with the WASH sector strategy.
Malteser International will continue to provide adequate measures to curtail the spread of the Coronavirus through the distribution of hygiene kits, provision of specialized leg-pedalled handwashing stands, Sensitization/Mass Messaging of populace across all demography in camps and host communities.
The targeted beneficiaries for this response will include IDPs, host communities, returnees, and most vulnerable segments from the communities such as female-headed households, elderly persons, and persons with disabilities who will be identified and prioritized in collaboration with authorities and community leaders.

1 - Needs

Malteser International identifies humanitarian needs and responds accordingly by relying on the primary source of information through conducting its own needs assessment and secondary source of information from the Humanitarian Needs Overview conducted by UN-OCHA as well as relevant information from WASH cluster humanitarian gaps mapping and multi sectoral need assessment. According to the Humanitarian Needs Overview of 2020, Borno state continues to be amongst those areas with the highest levels of vulnerability in Nigeria with millions in need of assistance. In the absence of durable solutions, the situation of IDPs depends heavily on continued humanitarian assistance, permanent returns of IDPs to their areas of origin are hindered by the prevailing insecurity situation.In June 2020, Malteser International conducted an end line KAP survey to evaluate the impact of WASH activities on knowledge, attitude, and practice regarding safe water sanitation and hygiene amongst the targeted population in relation to the WASH activities previously implemented which will be used as a baseline survey for the 2021 project. The result from the KAP survey shows; beneficiaries have access to a water supply as much as 10l/p/d which is below the sphere standards of 15l/p/d in respect to emergency context. WASH needs remain overwhelming in both IDP camps and host communities, where people solely rely on water supply schemes constructed by INGOs. The quantity of water is insufficient either due to the limited number of water schemes installed or the non-functionality of the existing schemes with the functional systems either stretched or incapable of providing services in a consistent manner. These shortages are partially caused by the influx of new IDPs into camps or host communities who have been displaced as a result of the ongoing crisis, female-headed households are mostly affected in such instances. Families cope by reducing water consumption, which relatively increases risks to their health.In areas of hygiene and sanitation result from the survey shows only 30% wash their hands during five critical times and only 40% of the total respondents wash their hands with soap due to unavailability of soaps. There is a high need for additional new latrines especially for the newly arrived IDPs to maintain the ratio of 20 persons per drop hole, many existing latrines are in urgent need of rehabilitation and Operation and Maintenance (O&M) since many are vandalized by the harsh weather during the rainy season and consistent use.Relating to the aforementioned needs, MI will construct full water supply solar-motorized schemes within Borno state for newly arrived IDPs to improve access to safe and sufficient water supply. In other operational areas including Konduga, Jere, Mafa, MMC, and Gwoza LGAs, MI is proposing under this action to rehabilitate hand pumps/motorised system, upgrade existing hand pumps to solar-motorized systems, provide hygiene supplies and dignified sanitation facilities, and build capacities of community members to operate and maintain water systems. As the Coronavirus continues to spread in an unpredictable manner around the world, the impacts of the pandemic will extend further than that on human health as it will also impact on existing services. With the on-going humanitarian crisis in the north-eastern part of Nigeria where thousands of displaced persons take refuge at camps rendering them vulnerable to the spread of infectious diseases. The number of people in need of urgent assistance in north-east Nigeria rose from 7.9 million at the beginning of 2020 to 10.6 million since the onset of COVID-19. Borno is among the worst affected states in Nigeria. MI has proposed measures to curtail the spread of the virus by the distributing of hygiene kits, introducing physical distancing during distributions, setting up handwashing stands and sensitization/mass messaging of populace across all demography in camps and communities.

2 - Activities

Water supply activities: 1- Construction/Upgrading/Rehabilitation of water supply systems; including drilling of boreholes, installation of hybrid motorized pumps, installation of water tanks and solar panels, construction of tap-stands and laundry aprons.2- Operation and Maintenance of existing water supply systems through the provision of Spare parts/tool Kits and capacity building training for communities to improve access to safe water supply 3- Conduct periodic/monthly Water Quality Testing through the provision of Water Quality Testing Kit (H2S strips and Pool Testers) to test Free Residual Chlorine and Coliforms bacteria to ensure water quality. Sanitation activities: 1- Construct/Rehabilitate gender-segregated latrines/bathing facilities to eliminate open defecation and reduce public health risks.2- Support Operation and Maintenance (O&M) of gender-segregated latrines through the provision of latrines cleaning kits to WASH Committees to improve proper hygiene practices.3- Support Drainage Control and Solid Waste Management through the provision of Sanitation tool kits to WASH committees.4- Support Desludging of latrines in IDP camps across Borno state. Hygiene Promotion: 1- Conduct new/refresher hygiene promotion training and sessions to new/existing Hygiene Promoters, and provide Information Education and Communication (IEC) to aid Hygiene Promoters to facilitate the dissemination of hygiene messages.2- Distribution of Emergency Hygiene Kits which include soap, jerry-cans, sanitary pads for the new arrival. 3- Distribution of Hygiene Replenishment Kits (sanitary pads, soap) to ensure improved personal hygiene.4- Conduct Post Distribution Monitoring (PDM) to monitor effectiveness, sustainability, quality, quantity, and use of hygiene items distributed in hygiene and replenishment kits. Cholera Preparedness and Response:1. Deployment of Cholera response team and Provision of Cholera outbreak kits.2. Conduct General hygiene awareness to contain the cholera outbreak. Corona Virus Prevention Activities: Malteser International will proactively engage in a number of activities and practices that is essential for protecting human health and preventing human to human transmission of the Coronavirus during the outbreak. Activities and practices such as; 1. General Sensitization/Key Messaging: A team of Hygiene Promoters will be tasked with the responsibility of creating awareness and passing key messages in IDP camps etc. on ways to prevent the spread of Corona virus, basic underlining symptoms, and what to do if one is infected. Messaging is to be conducted at the household level while maintaining appropriate distancing and avoiding gathering people for mass messaging. 2. Distribution of Hygiene Kits: In line with sensitization and awareness campaigns, Malteser International will distribute hygiene kits consisting of soap, sanitizer etc to beneficiaries at IDP camps and host communities for the purpose of improving personal hygiene thereby reducing the risk of human-to-human transmission of the Coronavirus. Additionally, in order to minimize the risk of exposure of staff and beneficiaries during distribution Malteser International has revised its hygiene kits distribution SOPs by actively managing the flow of traffic while maintaining the required social distancing at waiting areas, providing a handwashing station at the entrance and exit of distribution sites. 3. Malteser International has prioritized the distribution of handwashing stations and soap, alongside HP messaging posters at certain locations such as; latrines, water collection points, distribution site, entrance to camps etc.

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

Malteser International will adopt the IASC guidelines to mainstream Gender in its response and the Handicap International guidelines on the design of interventions for people with disabilities. To ensure Gender considerations is mainstreamed in our programming, MI will utilize recommendations from MI’s assessments, assessments from other partners, and Gender Marker Analysis. The following steps will be taken into account while designing and implementing the program to address sexual exploitation and abuse and participation of women, men, boys, and girls in consultations about the design of programs/services disaggregated by sex and age;1. Mainstreaming program interventions to make sure that they are gender-sensitive through the strategies, proposals, analysis, activities, outputs, and outcomes. 2. Gender consideration in Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability, and Learning: A gender-sensitive approach to MEAL will be adopted to ensure that WASH program is having intended impacts to meet the needs of women, men, boys and girls while addressing sexual exploitation and abuse.3. Ensure that exploitation/abuse based on gender and age disaggregated data is collected and analysed for reporting and immediate action.4. Setting up separate women and men’s committees in Focus Group Discussion (FGD) to provide spaces for the participation of and accountability to the community about the design, implementation, information sharing, and opportunity for feedback. 5. Spaces will also be provided to allow adolescent girls and boys to be consulted. 6. Application of the Gender Marker through the project cycle from assessment to evaluation.7. Women and girls will also have opportunities up to 60% to participate in WASH committees and hygiene promotion volunteers. Through timely and effective humanitarian response, Malteser International will mainstream gender consideration with an aim to empower women and girls in decision-making through consultation and effective participation in program assessment, planning, implementation, management, and monitoring of WASH intervention. Malteser Nigeria will also strive to reduce risks from exposure to GBV and protection issues through creating a safe, dignified and friendly environment for women and children while ensuring the WASH facilities such as latrines are safely accessible to women and girls by constructing segregation walls, constructing outside ramps and internal support handles in designated latrine cabins for ease of accessibility to elderly persons and people with physical disabilities.

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

Step 1. Secure organizational commitmentI. MI will build an organizational culture that is supportive of accountability to communities and ensures that MI staff, consortium members, and local partners understand and are committed to Malteser’s FRM minimum operating standards.II. Secure budget and resources (including staff time) to set-up, run, and continually improve the FRM.Step 2. Define the scope and scale of the FRMI. Decide the geographic scope of the FRM.II. Define and create referral pathways for feedback and complaints which are beyond the scope of what MI can address. Step 3. Consult with communities and analyse the context I. Consult with community members to understand their preferences for channels to provide feedback and complaint and receive responses, including preferences around how to provide sensitive complaints, particularly for women, children, and other vulnerable groups.II. Make all possible efforts to include the full spectrum of community members paying particular attention to different levels of ability, inclusion, and power dynamics.III. Take into account contextual information arising from other project implementation processes such as monitoring, community scorecard activities, and gender analyses as well as broader contextual knowledge. Analyse the context considering logistical, cultural, and organizational factors which will impact on how well different channels will work for different types of feedback and complaints. Step 4. Select a variety of channels I. Using the results from Step 3 and prioritizing the channels preferred by community members, select multiple channels to ensure that all community members (regardless of age, gender and ability, including the most marginalized) will have safe and easy access to provide all types of feedback and complaints and receive a response. Take into account physical, social and cultural access barriers identified in the consultations such as mobility, literacy, gender norms etc.
