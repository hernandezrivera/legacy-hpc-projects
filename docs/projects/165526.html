Project Summary

This project aims to address the current gaps on shelter needs identified by UNCHR
Protection Monitoring teams, by: Providing emergency shelter and wash assistance to
returnees from the United Republic of Tanzania through the voluntary repatriation;
Four (4) types of assistance will be provided:
(1) Emergency shelter repair kits (material and tool kits), including Construction and
Rehabilitation of VIP latrines
(2) Grants for rental/host assistance
(3) Hygiene sensitizations
(4) Support refugee returnees to obtain documentation on the land and/or house
property titles
The assistance will be determined by an evaluation of houses by IOM’s shelter team. The
response will be based on the results of the evaluation and prioritize, by vulnerability, the
households that do not have shelter or that are creating a significant burden on host
families.
Targeted areas: The locations of the shelter response for returnees are Rutana, Kirundo,
Cankuzo, Ruyigi, Muyinga, and Makamba.
