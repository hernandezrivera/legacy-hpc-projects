Project Summary

As the Syria crisis enters its ninth year, the humanitarian situation remains alarming across northwest Syria where the effects of conflict continue to have a devastating impact on the lives of 4 million people with an estimated 2.7 million people in need of humanitarian assistance. The years of conflict have eroded people’s coping ability across resident and displaced communities, making timely and principled provision of humanitarian assistance for them all the more critical;
With the latest escalation of violence in northwest Syria, civilians in Idleb governorate are again suffering from the devastating consequences of hostilities. Since 16 December, aerial bombardment again intensified in southern Idleb, affecting large population centres such as Ma’arrat An-Nu’man and Saraqab as well as towns and villages in their countryside, which further accelerated displacement from the area that began in November
This project aims to monthly distribute 3000 food baskets in Dana sub-district which are located in Idleb, Harim, Dana.
Ataa will distribute 3000 FRs for 12 months and there are 14400 RTEs will be distributed in emergency situations on three rounds.
project phases will be as the following;
Maar Tamsarin there will be 1800 FRs for 1800 HHs (IDPs) and 9400 RTEs for new IDPs.
In Dana, there will be 1200 FRs for 1200 HHs (IDPs) and 5000 RTEs for new IDPs.
This project aims to reduce hunger by providing access to food to the population affected by the crisis especially people living inside camps in Dana sub-district to have access to life-saving food to meet their basic food needs, all year round.

4 - Needs

According to OCHA report dated 10/10/2019 “last Situation Report #13: Recent Developments in Northwestern Syria as of 8 October 2019” 2 million internally displaced people in northwest syria, 4 million people in northwest syria 51%children and 25% are women. Moreover, 2.7 million people are in need of humanitarian assistance in northwest syria. According to the 2019 Humanitarian Needs Overview (HNO), 5,898,489 million people will be in need of urgent humanitarian assistance in Syria. In Harim, Dana (population 821,640), there is a total of 266,199 people in need. The food consumption score breakdown is as follows: 8.6% poor; 26.2% borderline; 65.2% acceptable. In the coming period, significant displacements are expected in Idleb and surrounding areas. This will cause an increased need for a multi-sectoral response to mass displacements and immediate needs of host communities and IDPs, especially emergency services.According to the latest monthly IDP Sites Integrated Monitoring Matrix, there are 313 registered IDPs sites hosting 437,747 IDPs in northwest Syria. In Idleb, there are 276 sites with 302,547 IDPs. The majority of the IDP sites are by running beyond their absorption capacity at 400 per cent. The recent large waves of displacements put immense pressure on the already overpopulated sites and created additional housing land and property considerations.Nine years into the crisis, the main triggers of food insecurity in Syria remain insecurity, financial and physical access to food, and depletion of livelihoods.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

One of the possible risks during the implementation of any project is the intervention of military factions or civilian entities in humanitarian work. As part of the management of this risk: The Safety and Security department depends on the strategy of achieving community acceptance during all phases of projects and building a trust relationship with the local councils and community leaders by clarifying for the local authorities the organization and project in the area and signing a MOUs at the beginning of each project. The S&S department is committed to all humanitarian principles, especially neutrality and impartiality, and it strictly follows the JOPs principles, and work hard to prevent any kind of intervention by the controlling armed groups or any other de facto authority. for warehouse security, Ataa will purchase fire extinguishers and install cameras in there and will increase the number of warehouse guards. The S&S department follows any developments in the project area, conducts a daily risk assessment of the area and informs the administration of any possible scenarios and how to deal with them, including evacuation and contingency plans and training the field staff on all expected scenarios. The safety of field staff comes first, especially with regard to the risks to human workers from arrests, kidnappings or even direct targeting. The management of risks related to the humanitarian worker begins by training humanitarian workers to commitment to the humanitarian principles, especially neutrality towards the parties to the conflict, in addition to specialized training in the person security and staff movement (movement clearance) in addition to effective communication during movement and the provision of all security and safety equipment. The safety of employees begins with the selection of their workplaces. Therefore, the Safety and Security Department is careful to choose the centres that achieve highest levels.
