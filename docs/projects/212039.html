Project Summary

Needs:
The Al-Zir irrigation (Sixth Irrigation Sector) in Deir ez-Zor covering 14,500 hectares of agricultural land across various residential complexes, the project relies on2 lifting stations, canals to distribute water from the Euphrates River, to get rid of excess water 85 vertical drainage wells containing pumps operated by an electric motor created. However, out of 85 vertical drainage wells, only 8 are operational due to equipment failure and infrastructure damage.
A field assessment conducted by the Bedaya Organization in 2023:
Soil Salinization: 2,400 hectares of irrigated agricultural land became salinized and rendered out of service (about 16% of the irrigated area).
High Groundwater Levels.
Primitive Irrigation Methods: Farmers resort to primitive irrigation methods, such as flood irrigation.
Decrease in production by up to 50% in 2,400 hectares of irrigated agricultural land.
The infrastructure of some irrigation channels was damaged as a result of the rise in surface water levels.
About 500 farmers’ homes located close to agricultural lands where water levels rose were cracked.
Intervention
To mitigate the great challenges for the population in NES, and to support the agricultural sector to improve the water and economic conditions in the region to be more flexible and able to face economic and environmental pressures and adapt to the scarcity of natural resources, Bedaya organization proposes a project that supports farmers in the region to find solutions to the problem of soil salinization, low production, and inability to Farmers cultivate some of them and its activities are:
1- Improving agricultural drainage techniques to get rid of the accumulation of salinity in the soil, by rehabilitating 10 drainage wells to withdraw excess groundwater.
This activity includes:
- Reactivating 5 agricultural drainage wells by installing submersible pumps (Saline ground water) that operate on solar energy.
- Reactivating 5 agricultural drainage wells by reconnecting them to the electrical network.
2- Involving farmers and the local community in agricultural development and effective management of available agricultural resources to contribute to the sustainability of eliminating soil salinity.
This activity includes training 45 farmers whose lands are located near activated drainage wells. The training includes several aspects:
Modern irrigation technology, crop management, monitoring and measuring irrigation water consumption, soil management practices, effective use of available resources such as organic waste and biodiversity.
The project will provide solutions to the problem of soil salinization on the one hand, and train farmers to use water resources sustainably, in addition to educating them on the subject of organic agriculture.
The project will support farmers in the Al-Zir irrigation area and will provide information to transition to sustainable, environmentally friendly and more efficient practices.
The implementation
project activities involves many stages, starting with the signing of a MOU with the Deir ez-Zor Civil Council and the Agriculture and Energy Committee.
Preparing a detailed plan for the project, holding consultative meetings to explain objectives, selecting drainage wells for rehabilitation, notifying relevant authorities.
Selection Training Beneficiaries (45 farmers) are selected based on predetermined criteria. Meetings are held with beneficiaries to clarify expectations, and a pre-assessment is conducted to measure their knowledge level.
Preparation for Wells Rehabilitation and Farmer Training: This stage involves receiving sites for 10 drainage wells, holding group training workshops for farmers, installing pumping sets using electrical and solar energy, and establishing experimental model for organic conversion systems.
Trial Operation of Rehabilitated Wells for 48 hours.
