Project Summary

Almshannaf District is located in the eastern part of Sweida governorate, and after the security conditions became stable the affected populations returned to their villages but the effects of climate change pushed them to leave their homes
again
The total number of inhabitants is 28,599 individuals distributed in 15 villages
The inhabitants depend in their livelihood on agriculture and livestock
The Effects of Climate Change on Agriculture and Livestock
Hot summers
Rainfall deteriorated
Desertification of lands
These conditions have caused their livelihoods to deteriorate because the pastoral and agricultural lands have decreased, and the needed water for both of them has deteriorated
Need assessment mechanisms
meeting with community members
FGDs with the community
surveys
Communicating with the relevant authorities
Analyzing the current conditions
Deteriorating conditions of agriculture
Displacement movement from villages to cities
Internal, domestic and intimate partner violence has increased
Increased cases of school dropout
Analyzing the problem
The above-mentioned conditions were a result of water shortages, desertification, increased prices of fodder, and low financial ability to invest in agriculture
The solution proposed by the community is to provide egg-producing chickens
Project objective: To help improve food security and living conditions and prevent and mitigate protection risks
Objective (food security): Support the self-reliance of affected households by providing urgent and time-sensitive emergency agriculture inputs for protecting and building productive assets and restoring or creating income-generating opportunities to save and sustain lives
Objective (Protection): Enhance protection: Enhance the prevention and mitigation of protection risks and
respond to protection needs through supporting the protective environment
The activity
Distribution of agricultural inputs – The inputs will include seeds, fertilizer, pesticides and equipment, and chicken distributed ( 20 chickens for each POC)
Training on the best ways to raise and propagate poultry to face climate change and weather conditions
Specialized veterinarians are contracted to provide training to the beneficiaries.
Implementing awareness sessions on the dangers of gender-based violence and forms of violence, in addition to awareness sessions on the dangers of child protection, including neglect, employment, and school dropout.
BACC has a PSEA policy that requires all of its employees, volunteers and contractors to commit to the PSEA and 6 principles of zero tolerance, prohibition of sex with children, prohibition of sex for money, prohibition of sex with beneficiaries, reporting any suspected case of sexual exploitation or abuse and creating a SEA-free environment. Moreover, the association plans to implement sessions to raise the awareness of the community about their right to receive assistance for free and without any form of SEA, in addition to their right to report any such behaviour and the secrecy the association guarantees for their complaints

Via the Facebook page
Risk Management
We will ensure that the purchase process is completed from the public sector, the General Poultry Organization, with basic conditions related to the quality and age of chickens and vaccines, with a contracted consultant supervising all delivery and receipt and training the beneficiaries, also we will distributing a sufficient quantity of the necessary medicines, vitamins and fodder, in addition to distributing crops to be grown by the PoC to preserve On the feed mixture necessary to complete production process in an ideal manner.
