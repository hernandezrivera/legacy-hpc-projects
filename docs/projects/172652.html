Project Summary

International Medical Corps UK proposes to implement an emergency nutrition project which will target 22,383 pregnant and lactating women (PLWs) and 25,867 children under five (CU5) years at risk of malnutrition. The response will include intensive community level screening, including campaigns, acute malnutrition treatment, infant and young child feeding in emergencies (IYCF-e) as well as participation in cluster meetings at the sub-national level.

1 - Activities

International Medical Corps UK will implement the same activities in all the proposed high-risk districts for malnutrition. The activities are as follows:1. IMC will conduct capacity building of the Village Health Workers on Screening and referrals for acute malnutrition 2. IMC will conduct capacity building of MOHCC health workers (nurses) on inpatient treatment of SAM with clinical complications and outpatient treatment of SAM without clinical complications.3. IMC will support the capacity building of MOHCC health workers on IMAM 4. IMC will conduct IYCF-e training for MOHCC health workers and Village Health Workers 5. IMC will build the capacity of Village Health Workers to conduct support group/ peer counselling 6. IMC will build the capacity of Village Health Workers to conduct community cooking demonstrations7. IMC will participate in the national and sub-national cluster coordination meetings for coordinated nutrition emergency response in the proposed districts (Lupane, Tsholotsho, Gwanda) 8. Consult communities on intervention issues utilizing community based platforms in the districts, and ensure timely feedback and response to complaints9. Data quality assurance for routine nutrition information

2 - Indicators

Through achieving the main outputs and outcomes:These will be:● Emergency-related capacity building activities for facility-based and community health workers (CHW) for active screening and treatment of acutely malnourished children through both in-patient and outpatient services● Strengthen reporting, information management and utilisation of nutrition data at district and sub-district levels for CHWs and health facility staff● Build capacity of care givers and mothers of children below two years to provide optimal nutrition and care for infants and young children in emergencies through counselling, as well as community structures (e.g. community support groups) with emphasis on supporting and protecting breastfeeding● Strengthen coordination of the nutrition response through the cluster system at sub-national level● Ensure accountability to affected populations IMC will have set project indicators and success will be measured against these indicators ie the targets being reached for each of the indicators.

8 - Accountability to Affected People
8.1 - Describe how your project was designed in consultation with communities? How will you incorporate communities in project implementation and what feedback mechanisms do you have in place?

International Medical Corps UK recognises that people affected by disaster or conflict have a right to receive protection and assistance to ensure the basic conditions for life with dignity. These individuals also have the right to provide feedback and to receive an appropriate and timely response per CHS Commitment 5: ‘Communities and people affected by crisis have access to safe and responsive mechanisms to handle complaints.’ International Medical Corps UK also draws guidance from the Community Based Feedback and Response Mechanism, Zimbabwe Country Program Procedure. International Medical Corps UK conducted a Desk Review of secondary sources in October 2020. International Medical Corps-Zimbabwe will conduct community consultations at baseline and use findings to establish and train community structures responsible for receiving, responding and documentation of feedback and complaints on their roles and responsibilities. IMC UK will raise awareness verbally or in written form in the community regarding how they can provide feedback. The feedback and complaints register will be used to log in all feedback and complaints received through community preferred mechanisms. These will further be entered in Excel to ensure proper data management. Complaints will be responded to either verbally or in writing within 30 days. International Medical Corps UK will gather contextual information at baseline through community consultation. Examples of mechanisms used by IMC UK in other programs include help-desks, suggestion boxes and mobile phones. The project will identify the risks and barriers towards the disabled by having discussions with disabled as well as able bodied people within the community to explore the kind of mitigation measures to address the risks and barriers faced by the disabled in the community

9 - Environmental considerations
9.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project will encourage care group participants to use eco-stoves (environmentally friendly stoves made from clay that require a small quantity of firewood, emit less smoke and cook faster). This will contribute to reduced deforestation and minimize air pollution.
