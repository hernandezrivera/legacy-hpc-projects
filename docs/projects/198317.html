Project Summary

INTERVENTIONS AND PURPOSES: The proposed intervention has been designed to respond to the dynamic and recurrent crisis affecting vulnerable communities in Borno state IDP camps.
(1): Strengthen community governance structures at camps. This will be a continuous process over the life of the project.
(2): Effective Accountability through Communication and Community Engagement (CCE). Also to be continuous throughout the life of the project.
(3): Capacity development of the affected population, on protection, sanitation, hygiene and risks of fires, diseases, flood and wandering around the safe environment.
(4): Site improvement activities.
(5): Preparedness culture development. Fire outbreaks preventive measures and also floods.
(6): Governance structure improvement. This is going to be achieved by involvement of all age group, for equality.
(7): Advocacy: Shelters, WASH, FOOD and other sectors where there are gaps.
(8): Manual registration of returnees, displacement, mortality and natality.
(9): Camp profiling updates.
(10): Provision of tools for sanitation and maintained the culture, and discourage open defecations
(11): Training of Staffs, volunteers, women and men community leaders, and youth leaders.
(12): Contingency and emergency preparedness plans.
(13): CCCM support and activities proper throughout the life of the project.
TARGETS: returnees and Displaced communities around Nganzai, Jere, Damboa and Maiduguri LGAs.
(14) Protection Mainstreaming.
(15) Complaints and feedback Mechanism (CFM) and Sensitization.
(16) Training on PSEA.
(17) GBV awareness raising.
(18) Safety mapping.
(19) OUT OF CAMP CCCM.
(20) Shelter management.
TARGET: IDPs in Camps and Outside Camps.

2 - Activities

Presently, IDP population and the most recent assessments, which foundthat over 47% of the displaced people are living in camps or camp-like settings. According to the recent DTM assessment, approximately 2.25 million IDPs currently residing in camps or camp-like settings, including reception centers, require CCCM (DMS). This takes the current vulnerability of the IDPs into account by comparing the current site facilitation in the camps against the preferred adequate site facilitation of one site facilitation team to per 15,000 individuals in camps. While the government is working to relocate IDPs, those who return are anticipated to face House, Land, and Property (HLP) challenges (inhabitable houses due to heavy damages, secondary occupation, missing ownership certificates, or entirely not having a home to return to) and may end up in camps in the areas of return; and as such, they should be planned for as a temporary contingency because they are still vulnerable and lack basic means to sustain themselves, particularly the most socio-economically vulnerable group, child and female headed households. Improving living conditions of IDPs in the camps, is part of an integrated approach with all sectors, ensure displacement management in camps, areas of return and out of camp sites to ensure equitable and dignified access to humanitarian services, assistance, and protection mainstreaming for IDPs through CCCM response. Strengthening self-reliant livelihoods for IDPs Enhance resilience and improve the ability of communities and local partners to cope with displacement and ensure local ownership and self-governance. In order to have a more qualitative, efficient and accurate respond, PPGW will implement a comprehensive CCCM intervention which will involved the following key activities;1: IDPs in need of site management services with inclusive and representative governance structures are supported.2: Effective Accountability through communication and community Engagement (CCE). For a guarantee unhindered access to direct feedback loops for all IDP residents, PPGW will continue to manage the complaints and Feedbacks Response Mechanism.2: Community led Improvement and Maintenance. PPGW CCCM team will closely monitor sites, and will conduct regular assessments on site conditions, in order to identify urgent site maintenance and upgrades required to protect the safety and well being of IDPs.3: Contingency planning and Emergency preparedness. To strengthen protection of IDPs against critical risks, PPGW CCCM team will implement mitigation, preparedness and response strategies against rapidly onset emergencies within the target IDP sites. Based on PPGW experience managing camps in around Borno state LGAs, anticipated risks include fire outbreaks, Sanitation issues, diseases out break and flooding.4: Hygiene promotion. Through the WASH committee. In order to promote good hygiene practice and strengthen IDPs first line resilience against outbreaks of diseases PPGW will conduct hygiene promotion awareness campaign in all location we are covering5: Camp Coordination and Camp Management. Strengthening the community Governance structures.6: Capacity Building/ Training: of Site managers, Volunteers, Women and Men leaders, and Youth.7: Ensure Protection mainstreaming and that staffs and volunteers adhere to PSEA. 8: Sensitizations and Campaigns; flood, fire, complaint and feedback mechanism, GBV and Safety on site.9: Increase visibility.10: Coordination with partners, LGA authorities and community leaders.11: Set up follow ups with partners.12: Advocacy!!!13:Provide sites with active CFM mechanisms including referrals to ensure well-coordinated CCCM assistance, reducing protection risks and strengthen accountability to affected persons.
