Needs

Regarding to the Humanitarian Bulletin Sudan Issue 01 | 24 December 2018 – 27 January 2019, an estimated 2,200 people (about 440 families) arrived in Otash internally displaced persons (IDP) camp near Nyala town, South Darfur, between 13 December 2018 and 7 January 2019 from East Jebel Marra locality, according to the government’s Humanitarian Aid Commission (HAC) and the International Organization for Migration (IOM). The displaced people had initially fled their homes in Gubu, Saboon Elfagor and Kawara villages to caves near Erri, Krobandra, Reimo and Kidila villages. They have had no access to shelter, water, food, health or security services. Their arrival to Otash IDP camp is their second displacement. The newly displaced people have informed humanitarian partners about these details after their arrival in the camp as they were not previously accessible when they were initially displaced in mountainous villages. With the current economic situation and increase in prices they are in dire need of humanitarian assistance. AOSCD conducted rapid assessment survey to Kass on 17 February 2019 to tee the gaps of ES/ NFIS and we find some of 5,640 individuals need an argent intervention and 3,440 new arrival IDPs are sating in Kass IDPs cam due to our own rapid assessment to Kass IDPs camp on last February 2019 they need an argent intervention to protect them especially from lack of ES/NFIs.An estimated 2,200 people (about 440 families) arrived in Otash internally displaced persons (IDP) camp near Nyala town, South Darfur, between 13 December 2018 and 7 January 2019 from East Jebel Marra locality, according to the government’s Humanitarian Aid Commission (HAC) and the International Organization for Migration (IOM). The displaced people had initially fled their homes in Gubu, Saboon Elfagor and Kawara villages to caves near Erri, Krobandra, Reimo and Kidila villages following fighting between the Sudan Liberation Army – Abdel Wahid (SLA-AW) and government forces between May and June 2018. They have had no access to shelter, water, food, health or security services. Their arrival to Otash IDP camp is their second displacement. The newly displaced people have informed humanitarian partners about these details after their arrival in the camp as they were not previously accessible when they were initially displaced in mountainous villages Due to that, AOSCD , (RDN ) national Organization as the Camp coronation focal point, and the International Organization for Migration (IOM) and the UNHCR, conducted Rapid Assessment Survey to Kass on 2/2/2019, to identify the gap of humanitarian need and verify the new arrival from Jebil Marra and some area surrounding Derbat IDPs camp, the assessment was took about two weeks, according to the last South Darfur Population Working Group Figure (DPWGF) which taken place in 8th February 2016, the result of assessment in Kass locality was 26,398 households, About 131,990 Individual, tin Kass IDPs.
