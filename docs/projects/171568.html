Project Summary

The protracted humanitarian crisis in Somalia is among the most complicated around the globe. Ongoing armed conflict and insecurity, with recurring climate shocks, has resulted in protracted economic vulnerability across the country as well as fragmentation and weakening governance structure. Violence and limited access to humanitarian assistance in hard-to-reach areas propel large number of Somali moving toward urban and peri-urban areas, straining the limited resources of the host communities. Food security and nutritional needs remain high in all of Somalia.
According to the UN,Without sustained assistance, up to 3.5 million people in Somalia could face severe food insecurity through September 2020.COVID-19 control measures and the associated worsening of socioeconomic conditions are further eroding the protection environment in Somalia.Desert Locust affected rural livelihoods in Northern and Central regions and presents a risk (serious) to the South. The 2020 Gu seasonal flooding (river flooding) contributed to loss and damage to property, housing, farmland and infrastructure.
WV is proposing an integrated assistance to rural and peri urban populations,including IDP populations with livelihoods assets support,cash assistance to help them rebuild their livelihoods and become more resilient to frequent shocks in Somalia.There is a possibility of the food security and nutrition situation worsening for the most vulnerable HHs in rural areas , as populations who are currently in IPC 2 are likely to deteriorate to IPC 3, and IPC3 to IPC 4 if they lack assistance.
To prevent further deterioration of productive assets and mitigate the need for HHs to engage in negative coping strategies to meet basic food needs, WV will target IPC 3 and IPC 4 populations in the proposed regions.
WV will engage project participants during selection of infrastructure to rehabilitate under the CfA activities to ensure that only community owned structures are prioritized and targeted to ensure sustainability and continued ownership of the structures after rehabilitation. In order to ensure that the assets rehabilitated are well maintained and remain operational beyond the project period, the project will put in place the following measures: 1- The project will only target to rehabilitate infrastructure (e.g. feeder roads, check dams and water catchments) that is important to the community and contribute to improving HHs food security and self-reliance. 2- The project will put in place community project management committees and train them on their roles and responsibilities, technical & managerial skills and operations & maintenance of rehabilitated assets to ensure they continue applying the same beyond the project life. 3- The project will be horizontally linked with relevant government institutions and line ministries where they exist to provide technical support and oversight for selected CfA activities. This will ensure continuation of support from the government and other prospective donors beyond the project period.

Project Activities

The objective of this project is to preserve and enhance the livelihoods and food security of vulnerable households while minimising the public health risks brought on by COVID-19. The economic impact of the pandemic is as significant as its health threats, and the proposed interventions will maintain economic activity and household incomes, especially for women.Cash Transfers:The project will support vulnerable rural and IDP communities through short-term income support. Cash for work mechanisms, to rehabilitate and build key livelihood assets, will kick start the local economy and livelihoods of poor households while laying a foundation for longer term food security and environmental resilience. By targeting women for cash for work and training support, the project supports women’s economic empowerment and increases the financial, social and economic resilience of households.Support Own Food production:To improve HHs own crop production, the program proposes to provide 6000 targeted HHs with a restricted seed and tools voucher of $40. The voucher will be redeemable from identified seeds/inputs suppliers who are recognized by the ministry of agriculture as certified seeds supplier. The program will engage with Growth, Enterprise, Employment and livelihood project (GEEL) program to identify certified seeds providers operating within the targeted districtsMeet Immediate Food needs:WV proposes assistance to meet immediate food needs for the affected households.In this regard, the project intends to provide the selected HHs with unrestricted cash to purchase food to enable them to meet their daily food need at HH’s level. To achieve this, the program intends to target at least 90% of HHs with conditional cash transfers. The cash will protect households from other shocks and reduce the use of negative coping strategies that undermine long term livelihood sustainability and enable the households to shoulder more economic risks. World Vision feels that the conditionality will be very crucial in enabling the communities build assets that can enable early recovery while considering the type of shocks effect the affected population is emerging from. The program will support the community for a maximum of 6 months, and it is expected that the proposed activities will enable the targeted HHs to graduate with the capacity to use the created assets to produce own food or enhance their absorptive capacity.The cash for asset activities will be undertaken on communal land and this is considered as a great contribution from the community. It is a crucial contribution that is the enabler to the project success. The assets created on communal land will be accessible by everyone including the project non-participants thereby enabling the project to create suitable assets. Other contributions include the fact that the project management committee are volunteers from the community. Targeted communities will also contribute other material e.g. sand and stones that are important to the achievement of some of the proposed activities like construction of check-dams, stone lines and gabions. Communities will also be responsible for provision of security at CfA sites. Conditional activities work will be carefully planned to ensure alignment with gender considerations, cultural sensitivity, safety considerations, community access to tools, technical skills and their applicability. What is the justification for using cash or vouchers instead of in kind?

Voucher or cash distributions are the most appropriate modality in all the targeted areas, because there are functional markets. Low purchasing power of vulnerable local inhabitants dampens market incentives and limits the incentive of traders and wholesalers to expand markets. Through the provision of food vouchers, this project will assist in stimulating these markets

Explain how the project design considered market functionality ?

WV conducted a Rapid Assessment of Markets (RAM) in all the target locations to understand the market situation and provide a snapshot of market functionality, capacity and availability of different commodities across the target locations. The Assessment revealed that all districts had functional markets with the capacity to adequately serve the communities. Subsequent market monitoring and analysis done monthly in all the target locations using a structured tool, observation of markets and reference to secondary sources showed that different scenarios were experienced that in one way or the other impacted the market situation leading to the different trends exhibited in the market with regard to the availability and pricing of commodities.
