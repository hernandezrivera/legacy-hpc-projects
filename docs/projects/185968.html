Project Summary

COHESODEC through this project will be targeting a total of 1,995 intervening in communities in the following sub-divisions; Bafut, Bamenda 2, Bamenda 3, Bali, Tubah, Batibo, Mbengwi, Konye, Kumba, Kumba 3 and Mbonge to significantly ameliorate the living conditions of these conflict affected populations of the North-West and South-West regions of Cameroon through the following activities: -
>Organize 2 awareness raising sessions on negative impact of excessive usage of fossil fuels to health and environment with 20 internally displaced women,
>Organize 2 training sessions on management of individual/group IGA involving IDPs in 11 communities,
> Ensure the provision of 33 kits to 12 IGA beneficiary communities,
>Organize 2 support sessions to ensure the provision of psychological support to IDPs and returnees in 11 communities in order to limit their psychological distress and improve their coping mechanisms,
>Organize 1 intercommunity dialogue involving 11 communities to strengthen social cohesion,
>Organization of 1 training session on vocational skills involving 10 people targeted,
>Organize 1 cash disbursement session for business involving 10 people/beneficiaries,
>Construction of 8 water points and rehabilitation of 4 water points,
>Construction of 2 health centres and rehabilitation of 4 health centres,
>Organization of 3 awareness raising and capacity building sessions involving 11 communities and authorities in the prevention of GBV particularly sexual violence and the provision of appropriate support to 30 victims,
>Identification of 15% of households in need of income support involving 105 persons,
>Organize 4 training sessions with 25 CBO leaders in affected areas in disaster risk reduction and planning,

1 - Besoins / Needs

In the course of implementation of this project, COHESODEC will be needing hand outs and posters to raise awareness on the negative impact of excessive usage of fossil fuels to health and environment with internally displaced women. The organization is also going to be organizing 2 training sessions on the management of individual/group IGA with 11 IDPs Communities.Moreover, during the implementation of this project, COHESODEC will also distribute 11 kits for IGA to beneficiaries of 11 target communities. While also ensuring the provision of 2 psychological support sessions to IDPs and returnees in order to limit their psychological distress and improve on their coping mechanisms.COHESODEC will also be organizing 1 intercommunity dialogue to strengthen social cohesion involving participants of 11 communities. The project will also ensure the organization of 1 training session on vocational skills involving 10 targeted people involved. Furthermore, in the implementation of this project, COHESODEC will be disbursing cash in 1 session for business to target vulnerable populations while also ensuring the Construction of 8 water points and the rehabilitation of 4 water points as well as the rehabilitation of 4 health centres and the construction of 2 health centres in target vulnerable communities of the North-West and South-West Regions. In addition, COHESODEC will be organizing 3 awareness raising and capacity building sessions with communities and authorities in the prevention of GBV particularly sexual violence and provision of appropriate support to victims involving 30 participants on the prevention of GBV particularly sexual violence and the provision of appropriate support to victims as well as identify 15 households in need of income support which will enable at least 105 persons benefit from this assistance.Finally, during the implementation of this project, COHESODEC will in 4 training sessions be training 25 CBO leaders in affected areas on disaster risk reduction and planning to ensure their resilience.
