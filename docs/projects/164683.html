Project Summary

The rationale of this area-based housing project is derived from experience gained from previous pilots and shelter projects, and in light of Shelter Sector’s evaluation and interventions, as well as challenges emerged during the last years, concluding that
• Targeting individual housing units scattered across cities, reduces the desired impact of emergency response to enhance stability and social cohesion in areas,
• Restoration of services and infrastructure, simultaneous to rehabilitation of living space, is a key factor in reviving the affected neighborhoods and improving stability,
• Community (and municipality) involvement in project identification and planning processes is a prominent element in emergency assistance, as it serves to mitigate possible tensions arising from perceived or real discrimination in selection processes. It is however essential to manage expectations of beneficiary populations, thus to boost positive impact of outcomes of delivered assistance,
Objectives:
Through the area-based housing approach, UN-Habitat aims to boost sustainable access to housing, restoration of essential infrastructure and access to cadastral services to displaced-, host- and returnee- populations in selected neighborhoods in Rural Damascus, Daraa, Deir-Ezzor, Hama and Aleppo. In practice, the approach consists in rehabilitation of roads, sidewalks, and debris removal in damage-affected neighborhoods, which are prioritized through ‘Neighbourhood Action Plans”, developed through the municipality-led MTOS (Municipal Technical Office Support) program. The holistic notion to serve entire conflict-affected, vulnerable communities through improvement of essential infrastructure and related services, under the objective to revive neighborhoods, is at the core of this project
Project Components:
Based on the municipalities’ neighborhood action plans, the project targets to:
• Light repair and maintenance of roads (mainly the Asphalt layer) in residential areas
• Rehabilitation of sidewalks (including tiling, and replacement of damaged curbstones)
• Debris removal
• Provision and installation of solar street lighting
• House connection activities including connecting buildings with public networks of sewage and drinking water
• Support to local cadastral departments to restore its functions and to provide services in regards of ownership and tenure documentation to all citizens.

4 - Needs

After 9 years of crisis, the scale, severity, and complexity of needs across Syria remain deep and far-reaching.An estimation of 5.65 million people are in need for shelter assistance (20% increased from 2019), and 3.8 million are in areas of severity 4 or higher (HNO SYRIA 2020 Draft). The shelter response requirement goes beyond humanitarian response and the shelter sector members will not be able to meet all the needs.Furthermore, a significant number of IDPs were able or would like to return to their communities of origin, increasing the need to more stable infrastructure to move toward more sustainable solutions for those able to return. Simultaneously, a variety of ownership issues, e.g. destroyed premises of institutions, damaged, lost or incomplete records, etc., present further challenges to the situation, especially that rehabilitation of damaged houses is only pursued following confirmation of relevant tenure documents.Besides the criteria in HRP, the selection of targeted locations is based on*Being in line with the Shelter Sector and HRP 2020 strategic priorities and objectives, as well as severity of needs*Being priority locations identified through detailed field assessments and consultants with local experts and municipalities (MTOS)*Complementarity with ongoing projects already through UN-HABITAT resources and donor commitments*High numbers of people in need, vulnerable and expected returnees in target locations*High complementarity with area-based interventions in WASH, Protection, and ERLThe targeted sub-district locations are:Sub-district DumaSeverity 5PIN 27,174Status Planned Sub-district HarastaSeverity 4PIN 67,311Status Planned Sub-district: Dara’aSeverity: 4PIN: 58,480Status: Ongoing (60% funded) Sub-district: Deir Ez-zorSeverity: 3PIN: 31,733Status: Ongoing (60% funded) Sub-district: Jebel SamaanSeverity: 4PIN: 491,774Status: Ongoing (60% funded) Sub-district: MuhardaSeverity: 3PIN: 6,758Status: Planned

5 - Implementation of activities

Implementation will adopt the following sequence: • Develop the Municipality’s action plan identifying the priorities and areas of intervention through an area-based approach aimed to target specific geographical areas characterized by complex issues, through an integrated, multi-sector, and participatory approach, where communities’ representatives, international organizations, ministries and public departments, are jointly identifying priorities in a comprehensive manner that takes into account the interdependencies between the identified issues• Form a community committee to guide through project implementation, in coordination with Municipalities and local authorities. This will take place through the well-established neighbourhood initiative, where the local community has a significant supervisory role in terms of projects’ selection and implementation • Detailed assessment for the activities in priority neighborhoods• Tendering process, awarding and contracting• Implementation• Completion of work and handover to the relevant stakeholders • Support the Cadastral Department to restore basic cadastral services in the target areas through rehabilitation of damaged cadastral services departments and establishment of new service centers in locations where there are emergent needs. in addition to regular reporting to the FTS, all activities and indicators will be reported to the sector through regular 4W mechanisms. UN HABITAT has developed monitoring mechanism including site visits reports, progress reports, inspection tools and financial tracking tools. All these tools and templates will be utilized during the implementation to enhance the monitoring process. The Municipalities will have key role in monitoring the implementation through the technical committees that shall be formed to supervise the work and issue the official handover reports, also the implementing partners are required to provide regular reports showing the progress on the ground.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk/Assumption: Non-consideration of projects on grounds of national/local spatial development plansMitigation:• Municipality-led, stakeholder inclusive, transparent processes for project identification;• Capacitate municipalities and technical departments to lead assistance and recovery projects in their constituencies, through project- management-oriented capacity building programs, provision of adequate workspace, IT and technical equipment• Align assistance activities on the basis of requirements, situational circumstances and municipalities’ spatial development plans through a collaborative, consultative process• Advocate for intermediate solutions for affected communities in areas unlikely to be developed on short term. Risk/Assumption: Delayed assistance to vulnerable and people in need due to cumbersome processes to approve programs, projects, locationsMitigation:• Close coordination with the specific ministries and other related public departments to expedite processes• Diligence and due consideration of time aspects in project planning processes, incl. anticipation of possible refutation• Selection of project locations with high concentration of vulnerable / at-risk groups but with little likelihood of being contested Risk/Assumption: Quality, speed and effectiveness of assistance programs/projects is negatively affected by poor, resp. inefficient governanceMitigation:• Close coordination with the resp. bodies in the ministries and other related public departments to identify and design projects based on municipal priorities and action plansCapacitate municipalities and technical departments to lead shelter projects in their constituencies, through project- management-oriented capacity building programs
