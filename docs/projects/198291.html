Project Summary

Portrait of Lake Child Initiative is seeking a grant to establish non-formal learning centers across Gujba, Gulani, Damaturu and Tarmuwa in Yobe State. The project aim is to reduce number of out school children in yobe state, increase access and encourage enrolment for all out of school girl children to primary up to secondary education, through community mobilization, sensitization and advocacy, and community outreach and livelihood intervention. PLCI’s strategy was designed to enroll 2500 out-of-school girls from across the aforementioned LGAs. Two learning supervisors and two learning facilitators will be selected each across the four LGAs. The learning supervisors and learning facilitators will be trained by instructors under the coordination of Better Education Services Delivery for All (BESDA) at the state universal basic education board (SUBEB) to give extra moral lesson to the selected beneficiaries lasting for 9 months with an ultimate goal of mainstreaming into formal school system.

2 - Activities

Our mission is to mobilize, motivate and sensitize non-school going girls; hawking, street begging, or act as beggars’ aid, that are between the age of 6-18, from economically and socially backward communities of lake chad region. Total LGAs : 4 LGAs (Damaturu, Gujba, Gulani, Tarmuwa)Number of Beneficiaries: 1000Project Period: 1 YearsGirls, Women , Religious Leaders, Traditional Leaders, Academics, Politicians, CSOs, CBOs, NGOs, INGOs and UN System, Security Agencies, Students and Media PLCI’S activities and strategies are given below:- • Identification of areas and beneficiaries (Survey); so that at least 250 out of school girls are picked from each LGA and each potential beneficiary is identified. This will be followed by data validation by the monitoring teams who will rule out any beneficiary duplication with any other programme in operational area. • Training of ad hoc staffs (Training and capacity building): PLCI will follow a periodic training cycle for each staff member. Trainings will be organized at regular intervals starting with foundation training at the initial stages and then at every quarter. The main objective of the training is to develop instructors’ and supervisors’ capacities on building and effective delivery• Involvement of parents, community and formal schools (Community mobilization and organization): This is done in a very planned and effective manner. Each parents and community members will formally involve. The projects are routed, monitored and facilitated at the grassroots by the project team.• Enrolment is after 2 months of rehabilitation, girls will join formal schools. For all this PLCI will work closely with the neighborhood schools and the government system. Classes is proposed to hold inside government school facilities to familiarize children with the formal school environment, teachers are oriented and parents are told to visit the schools more often. • Support through child and family sponsorship and other such mechanism to support long term pursuance of the goals of the children were also projected.
