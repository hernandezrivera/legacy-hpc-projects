Project Summary

With the objectives to mitigate the socio-economic impacts of the pandemic, the project aims to support through multi-purpose cash assistance which will be provided for the most vulnerable beneficiaries receiving individual case management, targeting 1,000 families emergency cases based on vulnerability assessments.

1 - Needs

Vulnerable and fragile families are the most affected by the socio-economic impacts of the pandemic. Their children are therefore facing financial barriers to access basic social services, including food security. Families accessing the Bayti centers, supported by UNICEF, are among the most vulnerable. These families already receive regular case management support but lack financial resources to ensure access and use of basic social services for their children.

2 - Activities

The key activities include the following:- case management by social workers complemented by,- cash assistance for children facing high vulnerabilities and financial barriers to access basic social services,- screening and targeting in line with Libya CMWG framework,- grievance and complaints mechanisms
