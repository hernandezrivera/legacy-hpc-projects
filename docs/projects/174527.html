Project Summary

The intervention providing holistic response in order to breaking the silence on GBV in hard to reach communities of three division (Fako, Manyu, Ndian) in the south west region is the process of mainstreaming GBV into the ongoing performance base financing (PBF) health care project model that covers this three division. These ongoing (PBF) health project has already engage partnership with grass root operational CBO in this communities to monitor and evaluate the quality of health care delivery services patience received in hospital and health centre quarterly, meaning this CBO master this grass root communities and often come across GBV violation cases but lack the competence to carry out holistic intervention. With regard to humanitarian response in this grass root communities GBV have been silence as this grass root communities have not benefit from any intervention. As a result of this reason, IYEC a UN ECOSOC Accredited grass root NGO working in this PBF health care project in the three division came out with this project to break the silence and make GBV a history in this communities by creating a coalition of these grass root CBOs working in the PBF health project and build their capacities on the holistic response to GBV in these communities in order to mainstream GBV in their PBF health care project since they master this communities and have been working in this grass root communities even at the peak of the crises. The objective of this project is to build the capacity of frontline service providers (CBO, Community leaders) in these grass root communities on the holistic response to GBV Psychosocial First Aid and Case management, PSEA etc, in these three divisions of the south west region of Cameroon. The target beneficiaries will be women and men, persons with disabilities, young girls and boys, internally displace persons (IDPs) and returnees. IYEC plan to intervene in Fako division ( west coast crick, Limbe III crick,Tiko crick), Manyu ( Akwaya, Tinto, Eyumudjock), Ndian (Toko, Mondemba,Ekondotiti, Bakassi Mbenmong, Akwa, Kombo itindi). In the project IYEC will encourage IDPs in these communities to return to their place of origin in order to increase the number of returnees’ in these communities

1 - Besoins / Needs

After an assessment carried out by IYEC the project will address the following needs: Provide holistic support to people at risk of GBV including survival, strengthen legal and medical assistance for GBV survivors in conflict affected hard to reach areas, strengthen GBV prevention activities at community level including sensitization of conflict affected communities, local authorities, traditional leaders and religious leaders, strengthen the capacity of operational organizations, local authorities, line ministries including security forces and police on the holistic response to GBV, conduct safety audits and risk and vulnerability mapping for GBV survivors, carry out education and sensitization on PSEA, provide basic vocational training and economic support to women and girls including dedicated livelihood activities to women and girls victims of sexual exploitation. As a result of the above need, IYEC a UN ECOSOC ACCREDITED grass root organization came out with this project, to partner with UNFPA to implement this project in order to make GBV a history in hard to reach communities.Activities: IYEC plan to carry out the following activities to implement this project-Provide holistic support to people at risk of GBV including survival-Strengthen legal and medical assistance for GBV survivors in conflict affected areas-Strengthen GBV prevention activities at community level including sensitization of conflict affected communities, local authorities, traditional leaders and religious leaders.-Build the capacities of operational organizations and relevant line ministries and local authorities including security forces the police on holistic response to GBV.-Education and sensitization on PSEA in hard to reach communities-conduct safety audits and risk and vulnerability mapping for GBV survivors-Provide basic vocational training and economic support to women and girls including dedicated livelihood activities to women and girls victims of sexual violence.Expected result of the project-The number of people who will benefit from holistic support to GBV will increase.-Legal and medical assistance for GBV survivors will be improve as a result of the project-GBV will be mainstream to operational focal point in hard to reach communities-Hard to reach communities will be sensitized and educated on GBV-GBV will be highly prevented in hard to reach communities-There will be accountability towards survivors of GBV-There will be increase education and sensitization on PSEA in hard to reach communities- The capacities of people, local authorities (traditional ruler, security forces) will be build on holistic response to GBV in conflict affected areas-SGBV victims, women and girls will benefit from vocational training and economic support so that they will become self reliant.
