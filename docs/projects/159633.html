Project Summary

HINNA women organization is proposing to provide protection of gender based violence through prevention mechanism of Multisectoral response and provision of quality service through provision of quality gender based violence services which will bring positive impact to the GBV survivors in Daynile, Dharkenlay and Kahda districts of Mogadishu and Baidoa district of Bay region, this HINNA’s project intends to strengthening the protection of GBV services in the two regions especially where gender violations is rising and response of quality GBV services is limited, through this Project HINNA will objectively target a total persons of 6386 which represents (912 Households) based in the IDP settlements and poor vulnerable host communities of the target districts in Mogadishu and Bay regions.
The overall objective of this project is to improve access to quality GBV services to the GBV survivors and poor vulnerable households as well as decrease Gender Based Violence harassments and protection risks through community education, empowerment and access to legal and justice services, medical aid, and improving human dignity as well as community basic services through increasing the knowledge of GBV Mainstreaming in Banadir and Bay region
During the project implementation HINNA is planning to target with a total beneficiaries of 6,386 individuals compromising of 3,670 women, 1, 421 girls, 754 boys and 541 men beneficiaries from the different roots of the communities (IDPs, Host Communities and Returnees) in the project target locations.
Accordnigly to HINNA capacity and knowledge of implementing GBV services in that last 5 years with SHF and other UN agencies including UNFPA, HINNA will strengthen it’s work which is improving the protection of GBV and Provision of quality GBV services the communities in Mogadishu and Baidoa, through this project’s life HINNA will work its four (4) GBV stop centers in Daynile and Baidoa HINNA will be supported GBV service accessibility and livelihood project, to prevent and reduce gender-based violence incidents in targeted area HINNA will utilize behavior change strategies through community sensitization and Engagement with Religious Leaders, Focus Group Discussion and awareness rising in Mogadishu for the project participants, HINNA will provide Referral pathway to GBV survivors, Procurement and Distribution of Dignity kits to 1000 GBV survivors, Distribution of Solar lanterns to 1000 GBV survivors, Basic Medical and Psychosocial support counseling 2388 of GBV survivors, Legal Aid Support Services 2388 of GBV survivors, Psychosocial support training to 40 health workers and GBV service providers, Tailoring Skills Training to 300 GBV survivors and Poor marginalized women and girls, Psychosocial support training to 40 health workers and GBV service providers, Referral Pathway training to 40 health workers and GBV service providers, Business Livelihood skills training to 300 GBV survivors and Poor marginalized women and girls, GBV and FGM Awareness raising campaign to 30 GBV survivors and 30 host communities, GBV and FGM training to 30 health workers and GBV service providers, International Day of Zero tolerance of Female Genital Mutilation (FGM), International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women, 16 days Activism to 40 persons from the different sectors of the community, International Women’s Day 60 persons from the different sectors of the community and Establishment of Women Friendly Spaces.
