Project Summary

A. Activities:
1. Water Station - Restoration / Rehabilitation / Repair /Expansion
2. Provision of hygiene awareness sessions at household level facilitated by gender-balanced teams
3. Water Tank (Elevated - Ground) - Rehabilitation / Repair /expansion
4. Water Network - Rehabilitation / Repair /Expansion
5. Rehabilitation / construction of WASH facilities in schools ( Handwashing , latrines , water tanks , pipes)
B. Targeted Locations:
1. As-Sweida
2. Daraa
3. Ar-Raqqa
4. Deir Ez-Zor
5. Al-Hasakeh
6. Homs
7. Hamah
8. Idleb
9. Rural Damascus

C. Budget:
6,503,180 USD
D. # of Beneficiaries
Total: 657,985
Vulnerable Residents: 470,188, Retunee: 1528, Refugee: 322, Final IDPs: 185947, Disability (avg.pwd/hh): 82032, Female: 360084, Male: 297901, Young children < 5: 99890, Children (5-17): 225403, Adult 18-59: 296611, Elderly >59: 26477.
E. The proposed activities focus on increasing access to water in many communities that have limited access to water (Ex: 4 hours every month). In addition, it works to provide clean access to water; and water, sanitation, and hygiene services for the communities to improve the level of health and reduce water-related diseases.
The proposed activities ensure to following solutions that are environmental wise where it can be implemented, such as providing solar power systems.
The proposed activities were coordinated on the national and sub-national levels to ensure the activities meet the needs.
