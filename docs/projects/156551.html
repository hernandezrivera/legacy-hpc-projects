Project Summary

In line with the Protection Cluster priority areas, MASRRAT will provide critically needed Protection services to an estimated 35550 vulnerable people in Jabel saman district, south rural Aleppo, North Hama.
Masrrat implements integrated protection services to prevent, mitigate, and respond to harm, exploitation, and abuse for the most vulnerable boys, girls, men and women in in Jabel saman district, Idlib, Northeast Syria.
We have been implementing Protection, Child Protection (CP), Gender-Based Violence (GBV) in Aleppo and Idlib since June 2015. Currently, we provide protection services in Atareb,Suran and Bennsh through 6 centres, including Child-Friendly Spaces (CFS), Women and Girls Safe Spaces (WGSS).
The project will provide:
-Specialized and non-specialized protection services through static facilities and mobile teams, including PFA, PSS, awareness raising activities, referrals and case management
-Distribution of in-kind protection materials in coordination with UNFPA and UNICEF
-Community-based protection initiatives, such as Women’s Committees - comprised of active female beneficiaries – taking what they have learned out into their community and mentored by our GBV staff, and Child Protection Networks.
legal awareness-raising sessions, counseling or assistance, including awareness sessions about civil documentation and HLP issues.
- Advocacy with duty bearers and key stakeholders to inform and enhance the response to protection issues during success stories and reporting.

Needs

According to the HNO 2019, people in need in Jabel saman district reached an overwhelming number of 1,052,505 people, with women and girls accounting for 51% (536,777) of the affected population while children accounted for 42% (442,052). Protection is the most needed service in Jabal saman district . where most of them individuals in need of one or more protection services.According to HNO 2019, recruitment and use of children by armed actors continue to be reported. Children, particularly adolescent boys, are used in combat roles as well in support roles. more so than ideology, continues to be a major contributing factor. Family and community influence, indoctrination, abduction, arrest and coercion of children are also factors.According to HNO and HRP 2019 the protection Needs Overview has stated that many issues related to protection have been the main concern. Also pointed out that the Early Marriage, Child Labor and Child recruiting have been common issues spread across.Creating awareness about protection issues related to children and armed conflict, including post-conflict situations and the specific needs of internally displaced children.Also, to raise awareness about women's issues, including the participation of women in peacekeeping. to raise awareness men about issues related to women and peace and security.During empowerment and support the women and girls to be meaningfully engaged as stakeholders and decision-makers like becoming one of local council member.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk (1): Changing the security situation that interfere with implementation of the project.Likelihood of Risk Occurring: Medium.Effect on Expected Results: high.Risk Management Strategy/Mitigating Measure: We will establish a response plan that can be activated according to the situation. Designing the activities in a flexible way where we can change the way of conducting the activity according to the situation so as to achieve the wanted results. and we can focus on more suitable activities like doing PFA . Stopping the activities for short period. moving the center to another place.Risk (2): Security issues being sometimes unstable in the project’s areas such as faction armed fighting.Likelihood of Risk Occurring: Very high. Effect on Expected Results: Very High. Risk Management Strategy/Mitigating Measure: According to lessons learned from previous projects, it was found that while conflict occurred, access could be restored within a few days. A cautious approach that plans for these types of delays is expressed in the project work plan.Risk (3): There is no coordination between organizations, especially inside Syria, where most of organization opening the same activities in the same time: Likelihood of Risk Occurring: medium. Effect on expected Results: Very High. Risk Management Strategy/Mitigating Measure: MASRRAT would be conducting more meetings and coordinate with those organizations.Risk (4): changing in the attitudes or priorities of the population that will affect their willingness to benefit from the project activities.Likelihood of Risk Occurring: medium. Effect on expected Results: Very High. Risk Management Strategy/Mitigating Measure: MASRRAT will work on awareness raising throughout the project period and will get the feedback from the beneficiaries about the services and adapt accordingly.Risk (5): Communities don’t accept the outreach activities approach and conducting an awareness session.Likelihood of Risk Occurring: high.Effect on Expected Results: high.Risk Management Strategy/Mitigating Measure: continue hiring a man in his staff from the local authorities who has strong empowerment in the community and good communication skills to convince the men through awareness raising sessions and coordinate with local council in the targeted area .Risk (6): Airstrike shelling: Likelihood of Risk Occurring: medium. Effect on expected Results: Very High. Risk Management Strategy/Mitigating Measure: The risk of Security situation (only heavy shelling) and The risk of the local council buildings being targeted by airstrikes are a very low probability according to The historical information for 7 years ago.
