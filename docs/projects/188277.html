Project Summary

The project will target the IDP’s and Host Community in the targeted locations in order to improve the safe, adequate, and sustainable access for these services along with the improvement for the public health and environment to avoid any outbreaks caused by contamination of the drinking water and lack in the SWM which might cause an outbreak in leishmaniasis either cholera. In this project the rehabilitation for water system, sanitation system, and restoration for the SWM system. Moreover, rehabilitation for water system will target increasing the efficiency of the water station and network by conducting the needed maintenance, replacement, and extension for the existing infrastructure and water station as well along with establishment for water ow meters in order to re-activate the tariff system. Rehabilitation for the sanitation system will target maintenance for the sewage network to repair the leakage points to avoid the mixture between drinking water and wastewater along with conduct needed extension for the network in order to ensure that all shelters and settlements are connected to the sewerage network to ensure the best practice for wastewater disposal along with install for rain drainage in the targeted communities and collective centers to avoid ods which caused by heavy rain. The Solid Waste Management restoration will cover distribution for the containers in the targeted areas along with providing the needed equipment, trucks, and tools to the local council/ authorities to collect and transfer the solid waste to the related landll which will also be rehabilitated. Distribution for Hygiene kits (WASH NFI supplies) will be considered for the IDP’s in Camps and communities one-two times during the project duration the in 2022 and 2023 (104.932 beneciaries) will receive a hygiene kit (WASH NFI's) based on the need along with consideration for age and gender in each family along with hygiene promotion session for each family, where the main topic will be COVID 19 pre-cautionary measures. In addition, each family will receive 20 $ via cash voucher two times per year in order to purchase their needs from the hygiene items, BINAA considers all the precautionary measures against COVID 19. A wastewater treatment plant rehabilitation will take place in Al-Rai, reaching over 40.000 beneficiaries in Al-RAi and Waqf communities by conducting rehabilitation work for Al Rai wastewater treatment plant which has been established by the Syrian government in 2011, in parallel with the wastewater treatment plant in Al Rai, implementation/rehabilitation of the sewage network in Al Rai & Waqf communities will take place to link both communities to the waste after treatment plant.

4 - Needs

According to HNO " 55% of off-camp households who reported using water trucking as their main water supply modality spent more than 5% of household’s income on purchasing water only, while the combined costs for water and sanitation services should not exceed 5% of a household’s income. The overall estimated average expenditure on WASH services and supplies (water, hygiene items, garbage removal and desludging)", in addition, "Investment in sanitation infrastructure also becomes critical, as at least 70% of sewage is discharged untreated and at least half of the sewerage systems are not functional. In addition, according to ACU Ewarn system, Al-Rai sub-district has been considered as a severe area related to waterborne disease, where during 2019, only 3702 waterborne disease cases have been reported, however, the number of the reported cases have been escalated to be almost doubled during 2021 to be 6772 cases. Moreover, HNO mentioned "IDPs living in displacement sites are often fully dependent on humanitarian assistance, and those in informal sites often experience worse WASH conditions making them particularly vulnerable. Also, newly displaced populations face specific WASH needs and require emergency WASH response. Women and girls, people living with disabilities (PLWD) and the elderly, together with female-headed HHs face more constraints and various protection risks in accessing WASH services".

5 - Implementation of activities

As BINAA has been supporting 164 camps with WASH activities during 2021,through this proposed project plans BINAA is welling to continue provision of critical WASH services to 104,147 (boys 19.218,girls 17.860,men 34.040 ,women 33.029) IDPs in 164 camps and informal settlements in Idleb and Aleppo through the following key interventions; 1- Provision of safe water 35 l/p/d on daily basis needed. 2-Wastewater desludging of WASH facilities 3-Solid waste collection. 4-Procurement and distribution of consumable hygiene kits. 6-Conduct RCCE interventions and COVID19/hygiene awareness messaging in IDP camps through FGD. 7-Water quality monitoring, to ensure safe water. 8-connecting 18 camps to water network by which will be operated by using renuable power sources..• 9-Water system rehabilitation and restoration, the water supply system in Atareb will be restored through the rehabilitation of four main water stations, repair of networks, and expansion of the water network. 10- Rehabilaiton works for the sewage treatment plant in parallel in Al Rai with connecting the communities by rehabilitation works for the sewage networks. 11- providing cash vouchers twice per year for the IDPs family, one voucher with total cost of 20$ per family. In addition, each family during each round will receive 10$ to buy their needs of hygiene items, and 10 $ as cash assistant, in total each family will receive 20$ via cash voucher for two rounds.
