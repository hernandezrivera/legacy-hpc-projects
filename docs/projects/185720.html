Project Summary

This project aims to support the most seriously affected and vulnerable categories in Rohingya community. The project complies with shelter criteria and non-food items (NFI) for the Rohingya Refugees and Host community. The project covers 400 new Mid Term Shelters in camp 6, 5 for the Host community and maintenance of 1200 existing shelters in camp 5, 8W, 17 and 18. We also distribute NFI for 3000 RR through Voucher modality and 500 HC through in kind distribution. In the construction of New MTS, we ensure the design and quality provided by ISCG and we will supply bamboo after getting treated from the suggested place. For the repair and maintenance, we collect the data from HH to HH and we can find the needs where R&M is needed. This is how our participatory activity also be met and a relationship with the community will be developed. In the provision of shelter activities, we also involve women, older people, people with disabilities and other forms of gender diverse population. Women will be invoved in basket production, older people will be involved in bamboo mat production and people with disabilities will be involved in distribution point. MSI also will make their Project staffs get trained in The Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) with the help of ISCG's PSEA team.

1 - Identified Needs

As per the Joint Multi-Sector Needs Assesment (J-MSNA) represent a continuation with 2019 and 2020 findings, when: 81% and 69% of households, respectively, reported issues with their shelter. 32% and 28% of households, respectively, reported not having made improvements despite have reported issues with their shelter. This is why Multi Serve International (MSI) wants to take this project with importance. So, according to J-MSNA, we have found that there are needs of New MTS construction in Camp 6. This is why MSI has taken initiative to build 400 New MTS in Block B, D and E of camp 6 in 2022. And also there we distribute NFI through voucher modality. On the other hand, we have constructed 1200 shelters in diffarent camps in 2021. In camp 5, MSI has built 500 shelters (block A,B,C and D), 200 shelters in camp 8W (block A,B,C), 200 shelters in camp 17 (block A,C,E), and 300 shelters in camp 18 (block B,C,E). So, we will repair those when it is needed. As we have project in those camps we decided to distribute NFI among those households and hope a community relationship will be developed with them in participatory activity for HH level site improvement. Again, we build 5 shelters in the host community and distribute NFI among 500 hoseholds. We also involve women, older people, people with disabiliyies and other forms of gender diverse population in our projects. MSI hopes, this is how at least we can make a contribution to fulfill the need assesment and objectives of JRP will be met.
