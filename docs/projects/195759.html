Project Summary

As the Syrian crisis continues in its 11th year, the scale, severity and complexity of humanitarian needs remain extensive. Depending on the database of WHO 2021, it has been showed that there is a shortage of services in the Health sector in Rural Damascus as a densely populate area, mainly regarding the COVID-19 outbreak. This area is in need to primary and secondary healthcare.
GOPA-DERD suggests to establish a healthcare center in Rural Damascus with focusing on providing comprehensive primary healthcare services targeting the most vulnerable population with special focus on women, children, elderly and people with disabilities in addition to the reproductive health, besides to spreading the health awareness through a specialized programs about personal & general hygienic behavior, pregnancy, breastfeeding & proper nutrition, awareness sessions of cancer & COVID-19 precautionary measures, and the common medical mistakes & diseases in the society ( mis-use of medicines, popular therapies,… ).
Additionally, equipping one center with the internal medicine clinics and the specialized gynecological and pediatric clinics to provide the primary services and patients’ checkups In Rural Damascus -Jaramana . Accordingly, referral the patients for lab tests and radical investigations along with providing them with required medicines, in addition to referring the life-saving surgical cases to specialized surgeons to implement the surgical operations through specialized surgeons staff.
Currently, GOPA-DERD has a successful experience in this field, whereas it has been implemented around 800 of lab tests, X-ray, CT scan, and MRI, in addition to 1100 surgical operations in various specializations ( neuronal, orthopedic, gynecological, urinary, ophthalmological, and general surgeries and cardiovascular interventions ) in Rural Damascus ( Jdaideh, Sahnaya and Ain Terma ) in 2021.
Through this project, GOPA-DERD is keen to continue supporting health services in the countryside of Damascus in 2022 and later in 2023.
GOPA-DERD has a qualified team that has a sufficient experience and a capacity to carry out advanced awareness sessions in GOPA-DERD affiliated centers in rural Damascus areas ( Harasta, Quatana, Jdaideh, Sahnaya, Jaramana, Dwelaa, Kafar Batna, Ain Terma, Arbin, Mleha ). Through these centers, the GOPA-DERD’s team through out this project can refer cases which need medical assistance to Jaramana Center.

3 - Needs

GOPA-DERD has developed a beneficiary registration system that is used by all relevant staff in all centers in Rural Damascus and has also been adopted by UNHCR Syria, to ensure that standardized and unified procedures are followed to enter beneficiary data into the Beneficiary Relations Management System (BRMS). Through one-on-one interviews, GOPA-DERD’s staff determine a beneficiary’s category (Vulnerable, IDP, Affected, Refugee returnee, Poor, PwD, etc.) and they are ranked by level of need on a special scale. The potential beneficiary must show either a family book (issued by the government) and/or some other form of identification with a photo. Beneficiaries are then registered in GOPA-DERD’s BRMS, then issued a “Family Card”, which has a bar code that allows staff to immediately access and track the type of relief assistance given to the beneficiary households.-Based on Health sector the project implementation areas are classified as 3 on Severity Scale list which is the highest level on the list .-According, this project will be one of the HNO Health Response Priorities" Ensure access to quality, life-saving and life-sustaining health services across all levels of the health system – community, primary, secondary and tertiary. Essential services comprise surgical and trauma services, reproductive health, child health". Need assessment report conducted in Q4 2021- by GOPA-DERD can be provided upon requested.

Referral of CT scan for 150 patients. Referral of MRI for 150 patients.- Secondary Health Services: Referral of general surgeries for 200 patients. Referral of gynecological surgeries for 200 patients. Referral of orthopedic surgeries for 50 patients. Referral of ophthalmology surgeries for 100 patients. Referral of urinary surgeries for 150 patients. Referral of ENT surgeries for 75 patients. Referral of medicines for 1000 patients. - Primary healthcare services including medical consultation for 3000 patients in the Jaramana health center.- Provide 200 Health raising awareness sessions through outreach medical team for 5,000 beneficiaries. These sessions will be implemented in the GOPA-DERD's community centers, which are spread in the rural Damascus.

8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

All project activities will use a rights-based approach to provide a protective environment that promotes equity and non-discrimination, reinforcing the basic social and human rights of participants while taking into consideration the cultural, historical and social specification of targeted populations.Also, all our volunteers have already received a package of training including: psychological first aid, non-violent communication, protection induction, basics of SGBV, first aid, PSEA and risk management in case of any emergency, as well as other basic information that enables them to act towards any person in need with total respect and most importantly to deliver assistance while protecting the dignity of the person.GOPA will ensure the “Do No Harm” principle by carrying out a Protection Risk Analysis as part of its strategy development. In some urban and crowded areas, humanitarian and beneficiaries may face a GBV abuse and Sexual exploitation, GOPA will make sure that its staff is well trained on SGBV basics. Moreover, GOPA had developed a misconduct internal policy to be fit with the humanitarian works in all sectors and locations.It is very common that the children would be exposed to any kind of harassment or abuse, GOPA will ensure providing gender-segregated facilities to avoid such cases.
