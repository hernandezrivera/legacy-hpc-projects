Project Summary

Provision of Basic Wash Needs for the most vulnerable 100 Lebanese Households and 100 migrant workers Sheltering in grey areas (Palestinian camps adjacent areas), in the context of the socio-economic situation is designed for a duration of 10 months starting from March 2023. 480 HHs will be supported with six rounds of Wash kits sufficient for a family of 5 individuals. The identification of the most vulnerable 200 HHs is based on the Naba’a database and matches the targeted vulnerabilities/ 2022 first standards Allocation strategy paper, where;110 families including pregnant and lactating women,173 headed families by a single elderly with one or more children,120 single female-headed households,77 Households with four or more dependents (children at risk). 900 individuals ( 450 Lebanese and 450 Migrants) will be receiving Wash kits, disaggregated by gender as follows; 90 men, 180 women, 360 Girls, and 270 boys. Wash kits will be sanitized and then handled door-to-door to the eligible 200 families in order to reduce the spreading of COVID-19 among the refugee community in case a distribution point is set. The wash kit paired with the food kit will be distributed during months 1 and 3 to the following:
- Elderly kit: twice for 80 families who are headed by a single elderly
- Baby kit; twice for 42 families including pregnant and lactation women
- Hygiene kit; twice for 78 households including female-headed households, and families with children at risk

An HHS satisfaction survey is a useful tool to measure the level of satisfaction of HHs

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

Naba’a team is well oriented and trained to recognize and detect warning signs of people at risk of violence or victimization, including signs of abuse, neglect, domestic violence etc. The team prepared to address problems and challenges that are faced might create an abusive environment. In case of suspected abuse, Naba'a’ is under full obligation to report to authorities. In addition, the beneficiaries will be well oriented to Naba'a complaint mechanism in order to facilitate receiving it and act accordingly. The estimated percentage of females benefiting from the intervention are approximately equal to male, the distribution method will be based on a door-to-door system to avoid exposing people to transmitting the virus if collected at a distribution point, and this will help us to avoid exposing people to additional risks through this emergency intervention.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

beneficiaries and affected populations have been involved in the needs assessment during March 2021 titled “The Implications of the Economic Crisis in Lebanon on Food Security” outlined through conducting focus group discussions and questionnaire as well. currently the project team are working on the formation of a steering committee composed of; Popular committees, and UNRWA ( due to the fact that the current approved LHF intervention is located within the Palestinian camps , while this part which is complementarity will be beyond the borders of the camps and the steering committee will be enlarged through adding other active members from local community and municipalities. This will facilitate the communication with different stakeholders without doing long process of communication, while INGOs whom are actively working in all locations will be kept updated and promoted to activate referrals.
