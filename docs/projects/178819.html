Project Summary

Project summary:
The project aims to provide life-saving assistance and improving living conditions of IDPs and host communities in aid distribution, sector. Aid distribution component support vulnerable families with low/no income, poor housing conditions, severe disability or other special needs, and no external support for the procurement/supply of food or essential items in informal settlements. The distributions include 5 types of assistance: monthly food parcels along 3-4 months for 1600 vulnerable households (8,000 beneficiaries), monthly bread bags along 3-4 months for 3,000 vulnerable households (15,000 beneficiaries), , . These distributions intend to provide target groups life-saving assistance.

4 - Needs

In the province of North west Syria, 3.4 million people are in extreme and catastrophic need (HNO 2021)Humanitarian needs are exacerbated in northwest Syria, especially in winter, as communities are at risk from extreme weather conditions and potential floods. There is an urgent need for food support, including heating, winter clothes, blankets, food, livelihoods and winter water. Without proper winterization, people in need may resort to a negative coping measure that is either fatal or degrades family assets. The total number of internally displaced people in the government of Aleppo is estimated at 990,000, and 2.7 million people are in need of humanitarian aid, including 1.5 million in urgent need of humanitarian aid . food aid is among the top three priorities for internally displaced people. Aleppo Province: The total number of internally displaced people in Aleppo Province is estimated at 990,000. In Aleppo province, 2.538 million people are in need of humanitarian aid, of which 1,012,000 are in urgent need of humanitarian aid (HNO 2019, 12/2018).

5 - Implementation of activities

Distribution of 21000 food parcels to households in 17 communities/informal settlements The food parcels contain: Rice, bulgur, red and brown lintel, sun flower cooking oil, olive oil, margarine, bean, check peas, sugar, zaatar, tuna in tins, tomato paste. The food parcels are distributed monthly. Tendering/requests for quotations are done every 6 months. It is planned that selected families will be given food parcels for the period of 3 months. After this time, new families will be selectedDistribution of bread 950,000 bread bags l/1 kg) to households in 5 communities/informal settlements (Afrin, Maydan Afrin, Ma'btali, Sharan, Kafr Janna) . It is planned that the beneficiaries will get bread for a period of 3 months. Then new families will be selected. The bakery/oven will be contracted for 15 days only. The bakery is baking and packing the bread. .

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Security situation will get worse and thus the distribution of humanitarian goods will be impaired. medium Distribution of humanitarian goods is done by our local staff who are in regular contact with the local councils in order to assess the risks/security situation on site. Local and international security manager assess the security situation on a daily basis and give recommendations/orders to staff members. Fraud/corruption medium Strict adherence and control of procurement guidelines and anti-corruption measures; strict control of project administration and finances; clear distinction of tasks, responsibilities in finance, logistics and program department.
