Project Summary

The proposed multi- years project by GREENCODE under the Early Recovery and Livelihoods Sector is aligned to the ERL Sector Strategy and the 2016 Buhari Plan developed by the President Committee for the North East Initiative (PCNI) by contributing to its priority intervention on Rehabilitation, Relocation & Resettlement.
In this project will Support Livelihood Stabilization and Income Recovery through the pomotion of micro and small enterprise recovery through short-cycle business-management training, cash grants, access to microfinance schemes and coaching and other income-generating activities targeting particularly women and youths;
Also, the project will support the process of providing emergency employment opportunities from the rehabilitation of community infrastructure for instance thre rehabilitation of Tanbang community primary school, destroyed by the insurgent in 2014.
This project is contribute directly to the ER&L Strategic Objective 2: Support livelihood stabilization and income recovery.

Needs

The long protracted violent conflict in the states of Borno, Adamawa and Yobe states in the north east has left many untold hardship and displacement.Livelihood of the displaced and host communities have been totally destroyed and leaving the residents in hunger and in the state of hopelessness.The report of the recently conducted multi-sector need assessment on livelihood and economic recovery indicate inadequate level of access to basic services and public infrastructures, whereas the demand for available resources is on the increase.In some locations, community members are gradually returning home and there is high need to restore basic facilities such as health care, water, sanitation, education and income generating activities.These are already content in the ER&L sector plan which is also aligned to the 2016 Buhari Plan developed by the President Committee for the North East Initiative (PCNI) by contributing to its priority intervention on Rehabilitation, Relocation & Resettlement. The ability to access basic services, produce goods and participate in the local economy has been significantly hampered.GREENCODE in close collaboration with ER&L sector is proposing this project to stabilize local communities through the provision of support in livelihoods restoration, security and basic service. This will in turn lay the foundations for sustainable development.

Activities

The following activities will be implemented;1). Community mobilisation: This will involve project staff visiting and holding consultation meetings with the community leaders on the project and also to get their buy-in2). Identification, selection and registration of beneficiaries: This will be done in close consultation with the ER&L sector, community leaders in setting the selecting criteria, while GREENCODE will select enumerators from the community, train them on the use of Smart phone in data colelction and upon finalising on the data collected each selected beneficiaries will be given a registration card.3). Beneficairy consultation on prefered micro and income generating activities: GREENCODE will meet each of the selected beneficiary to know his or her preferred vocational skills areas based on the pre-design options by GREENCODE. After which they will be grouped based on their trade area.4). Skill Acquisation trainnig for women, men and youth for 5 months on their preferred vocations: Every beneficairy will be enroll to undergo a 5 months trade/skills training with a skills centre close to them, while project will support with local transport5). General mirco enterprise management skills: This will be 2 day simplief training for all trainees on how to manage their business and it will include simple recordin keeping, saving and investment.6). Training on community saving and loan scheme (CSLS): This will be a 5 days community level training that will expose the beneficiaries to simple culture of working in teams/group, saving and loaning to themselves. A middle level profession in the enterprise development feild will be hire to conduct the traiing. 7). Establishment of 6 CSLS (3 per LGA): Upon completion of the training a CSL assocaition will be set up and simple kits given by GREENCODE to each assocaition. while GREENCODE will continue the monitoring.8). Procurement of Micro income equipments and tools: Some Micro income equipment such as sewing machine, wwaving and milling machine etc will be procured by the project and distributed to each beneficiary that have sucessfully completed the 5 months compulsory vocational training as a start-up capital for their livelihood restoration.9). Seed capital for the trainees: In addition to the income generation kits, the project will also provide the trainees with small cash/money for them to use and purchase the initial materials for example- Thread, cloths for those sewing.10). Rehabilitation/renovation of Tanbang primary school: The project will rehabiltate 5 classrooms of destroyed primary school in Tanbang which is a community not accessible and is like 10km from Askira Uba town. Upon rehabilitation, it wil provide hope and education for children that has stayed out of school for over 4 years now.11). Organise community outreach on Protection on GBV, child protection, mine awareness and peaceful co-existence: The will include trainng of selected communty stakeholders that will continue the education/awareness even after the end fo the project. While and during the period of the project, GREENCODE will be organising community outreach event on the protection issues (GVB, sexual abuses and mine awareness ) using audio visuals and other IEC materials.12). Routine monitoring and post distribution monitoring: GREENCODE will be conducting routine monitoring of all project activities and post distribution monitoring as well.
