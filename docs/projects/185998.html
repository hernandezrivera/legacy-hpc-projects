Project Summary

The crisis in the NW and SW regions has increased the vulnerability of the population. Families face insufficient means of subsistence, likelihood and welfare resources as they are forced displaced far away from their home and from available services with women and girls facing the risk of denial of resources. In September 2020 some 409,173 people were reported displaced within these Regions with 360,547 considered as returnees. Female population count for 48% of the total displaced population, children 44% (51% girls and 49% boys), and older people for 12%. Due to a decrease in economic activities and destruction of the social safety nets, women, girls are adopting negative coping mechanism such as survival sex, child labor, early marriages. Sexual violence is on the rise. There is a need to step up holistic GBV prevention and response services. The purpose of this project is to contribute in the provision of life saving interventions and restoring human dignity for at least 10,000 indirect people affected by crisis and alleviating hardship and suffering by strengthen resilience of at least 700 direct people especially women and girls.
The project will provide holistic Psychological Support Services to people at risk of GBV or GBV survivors. COMINSUD plans to provide support to 700 persons (500 women and 200 men) in the North West Region. Strengthen legal and medical assistance for GBV survivors in conflict affected areas with referred to legal assistance and received medical support including Post Rape Kit. 450 GBV (400 women and 50 men) with medical assistance in the Region Conduct safety audits, risk and vulnerability mapping for GBV to reduce risk and vulnerability to the occurrence of some GBV incidences and enhance mitigation measures. COMINSUD will conduct 10 safety audits, risk and vulnerability mapping for GBV.
The project will to reach out to 30,000 persons with information to strengthen GBV prevention activities at community-level. Provision of basic vocational training, economic support and livelihood income generating activities and start up support to 200 women and girls who are victims of sexual exploitation, place 50 in vocational training centers.

1 - Besoins / Needs

Over 50,000 persons in need of psycho social support services, with a need to prevent over 25000 women and girls including those living with a disability from exposure to GBV, as well as provide response support to over 3500 women and girls survivor of GBV and those involved in other negative coping machanisms. This project will contribute in providing: Adequate access to GBV lifesaving and humanitarian assistance services, likelihood and welfare resources for displaced families as well as host community members to enable them reduce their vulnerability. Support for women, girls, PLWD and families so that they adopt healthy and positive coping mechanism in the face of the crisis. Strengthening the protection framework against SGBV and enhancement of response systems for survivors of SGBV. Holistic GBV management services with strong referral pathway with assistance for medical, legal, shelter and economic empowerment support to GBV survivors. Project strategy Train 12 case workers on case Management respecting GBV Minimum Standards. Create and provide support for the functioning of 5 safe spaces for women and girls and 3 adolescent and youth friendly spaces and carry out community outreach to identify and connect people in need to service points. Procure and distribute 1,000 dignity kits to vulnerable women and girls and provide support for shelter, and other basic protection needs to at least 700 persons. Produce and distribute in collaboration with 30 actors involved in GBV service delivery 5,000 copies of the referral pathways, and support the functioning of a good referral system to benefit 1,000 GBV survivor for legal and medical support including Post Rape. Train and support 12 GBV Focal Points to conduct safety audits, risk and vulnerability mapping for GBV in 12 sub divisions. Create awareness and identify vulnerable women and girls, organize trainings on managing income generating activities, provide 200 of them with start up support for income generating activities, place 50 in vocational training centers and as well as and carry out mentoring to all 300 beneficiaries
