Project Summary

The education project will be implemented, in partnership with First Step Together Association-FiSTA, and will aim at providing vulnerable Lebanese, PRLs, and migrants with equitable and sustainable access to specialized educational and mental health services. In particular, individual and group interventions and community-based support will be provided to increase children’s retention in schools, limit dropout rates, and re-integrate children with disabilities (CwD) in inclusive education systems in Northern Lebanon. The strategy supports the implementation of MEHE’s 5-Year Plan on General Education using Inclusive School Programs.
Children’s educational outcomes will be tackled through targeted and personalized specialized interventions (i.e., psychomotor therapy, speech and language therapy, special education intervention, and psychotherapy), and community interventions by engaging and supporting their teachers and caregivers. Using this holistic approach, the project will improve the learning capacities and behavior outcomes of 180 CwD between the ages of 4 and 18. Early screening, specialized education, and MH services will be provided for mild to severe cases of children with learning difficulties (LD) and attention-deficit hyperactivity disorder (ADHD), in addition to neuro-developmental disorders.
Restart Center will provide individual psychomotor, speech and language, and psychotherapy services, while FiSTA will provide special education group intervention sessions, allowing an easy transition to the classroom setting.
Furthermore, to complement the above, 110 educators in formal and non-formal education settings will benefit from a capacity-building program through the provision of awareness raising and training sessions to support educators working with CwD, supporting inclusion. Thus, Restart will target 60 educators working with partners implementing NFE programs (BLN, ECE, retention and homework support), while FiSTA will target two schools to provide a capacity building program to 50 teachers/supervisors from early childhood education (1st, 2nd, and 3rd grade) to ensure early screening and intervention.
FiSTA and Restart will also target 180 parents of referred children through the provision of a parental engagement program. Within this program parents will benefit from parental guidance sessions for children benefiting from individual and group interventions. FiSTA will also aim to provide 180 parents with awareness raising program tailored to their needs to ensure that parents understand their children’s case and have enough skills and tools to best support their children (i.e., children benefiting from speech and language therapy will benefit from sessions related to speech delays and practical ways to support their children).
Moreover, Restart will ensure that the 180 identified and/or referred children benefit from recreational, psycho-social support (PSS) and socio-emotional learning (SEL) components, through group interventions. Common objectives for children will be identified and accordingly, sessions will be tailored and provided by the needed specialist(s). Through this activity, children will be supported to cope with stress-inducing events and gain other interpersonal skills. The aim of this activity is to increase resilience, learn to express themselves, and learn to identify and protect themselves from dangerous situations (i.e., harassment), promoting the protection of children who are at higher risk of abuse, neglect, and ill-treatment by their siblings, parents, peers, and teachers through advocacy of child rights. Moreover, identified needs and objectives will be tackled using music and art therapy.
RESTART and FiSTA will mainstream prevention and mitigation of risks of GBV and child protection into all activities. Children with protection risks will be safely identified during activities carried out by the partner will be referred following the inter-agency referral mechanism.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

Restart and FISTA will ensure the most disadvantaged children with mild, moderate, and severe learning difficulties and disabilities have access to inclusive education in schools. For children who are not eligible for registration in the public school system, Restart and FiSTA will refer to NFE. Activity 1: Provision of preliminary assessment sessions and specialized services, including individual psychomotor therapy, speech and language therapy, special education intervention and psychotherapy 180 children between 4 and 18 with mild to severe LD and ADHD, behavioral problems and neurodevelopmental disorders. A combination of face to face and remote therapy sessions will be used in addition to clinical pre/post tests to track progress and impact against therapeutic objectives.Activity 2: Capacity building program including engagement program to 180 parents (2 full day trainings), info sessions to 2 parent committees (2 sessions each), and awareness raising sessions 50 FE (2 full day trainings) and 60 NFE educators (5 sessions).Activity 3: Provision of a Recreational and Social Emotional Learning (SEL) program to 180 registered children will aim at increasing resilience in light of the crisis in Lebanon and increasing knowledge on how to identify risks, and protect themselves against dangerous situations (i.e. harassment) and tackle common objectives through different approaches including music and art therapy (3 to 5 sessions per group of 10).

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

The project will also incorporate the principles of Do No Harm with policies including: - Code of Conduct- Zero-tolerance Safeguard policy: Protection from sexual harassment, exploitation, abuse and child abuse (drafted and approved) and PSEA related SOPs - Protection concerns will be addressed through referral flow mechanisms, internal and interagency, ensuring beneficiaries and staff have immediate and equal access to needed services.- Anti-Fraud – Anti Corruption Policy- Anti-Bullying / Anti-Discrimination- Whistleblower and Anti-retaliation- Implementation of contracts and partnership agreements, requiring partners and subcontractors to adopt policies that prohibit and take action for SEA- Age Gender and Diversity Mainstreaming approach used to support diversity of childrenThe project will ensuring all beneficiaries have medical services and treatment and promote the inclusion and access to services of all boys, girls, men, women, and hard to reach vulnerable populations.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

Restart will apply a participatory approach with children, specialists, caregivers and educators in the design, implementation and evaluation of services, treatment plans, and capacity building programs. Participatory assessments are used when planning sessions to identify gaps/needs, tailor sessions accordingly and implement program adaptations. The program will also adopt a rights-based approach, promoting full inclusion at all levels of education and equal opportunity rights of CwD to access inclusive education and quality services to support development. Restart will enhance the access to hard-to-reach vulnerable populations by informing other organizations of the proposed project and how to access services. Restart has been an active member in the education and CP working groups and sector meetings since 2016, working with different NGOs and INGOs in line with the interagency SOPs. Community-Based Complaint, Feedback and Response Mechanism (FRM) will be used and addressed.
