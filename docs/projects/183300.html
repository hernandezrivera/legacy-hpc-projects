Project Summary

For 2021, the current crisis in Lebanon has had significant impact on all communities, increasing household’s vulnerabilities, limiting access to basic items and services, with women and girls being disproportionately affected in terms of reduced protections and increased risk of GBV. Global studies, Concern’s recent context analysis study on Lebanon (April 2021) and our current programming continue to confirm that women, girls and PWSN are disproportionately affected in terms of increased risk to GBV and access to services. Concern Worldwide will work with Arcenciel who are a Lebanese based non-profit NGO. The present action aims to address the major needs and gaps identified for GBV survivors and PWSN, by proposing an effective, sustainable and gender inclusive response, which has been designed to tackle the identified problems (Concern, Washington Survey, March-April 2021) at community, safe spaces and rehabilitation centers
1:Case management is an approach for addressing the needs of an individual who is at risk of harm or has been harmed. The person and their family are supported by a caseworker in a systematic and timely manner through direct support and referrals. Case management provides individualised, coordinated, holistic, multisectoral support for complex and often connected protection concerns. The views and decisions of the individual and, where required, their family direct the case management process.
2:Cash for protection is an intervention whereby cash and voucher assistance (CVA) are used as the modalities to address individual or household (HH)-level protection needs. Cash for protection is designed to achieve specific protection outcomes on the basis of an individual case. Consequently, cash for protection can be used in situations wherein an individual and/or HH is at risk of immediate harm or not necessarily time-specific. The provision of cash for protection is identified within the case management services provided by Concern Worldwide. Concern Worldwide is proposing to include transportation fees to those who would be financially incapable of attending the centers otherwise.
3:Based on the Washington Survey questionnaire conducted by Concern Worldwide in Akkar between March and April 2021, 163 out of 1,200 families have a family member with a disability and limited income or access to resources to provide them with the services they need. Arcenciel offers a range of paramedical services (oxygen therapy, physical therapy, occupational therapy, psychomotor therapy, speech therapy, psychological consultations) at their facilities to help and facilitate the life of people with disability, promote their independence.
4:Concern will focus on building capacities of partner staff on GBV risks and safe identification and referrals, to strengthen the integration between health, GBV services and the comprehensive care, support and protection GBV survivors might need. Further to this, Arcenciel will provide training to hired and volunteer rehabilitation staff on gender and age sensitive, PWSN rights, CP and GBV core and concepts, safe identification and referrals of cases in the rehabilitation center. By providing integrated services within the rehabilitation center, the project will ease access to critical GBV and rehabilitation services (for the most vulnerable groups and PWD) and streamline support for identified needs.
5: Mental health and psychosocial support (MHPSS) support aims to protect and promote psychosocial well-being, prevent mental health disorders and treat such disorders when they occur. MHPSS describes a wide range of activities carried out by the Concern. The MHPSS response includes, but it not limited to, psychosocial support groups (sharing experiences with other individuals/families), group sessions and community outreach to share information and develop life skills and individual consultations.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

1: Concern will provide GBV response services (focused PSS, case management) for 120 individuals. Two qualified Case-Workers under the supervision of a Case-Management Supervisor will be based in the identified rehabilitation centre for continued support. Concern will trigger referral service provision as required to based on security or discovered needs through the case management process2: 80 persons under the focused PSS will receive a one time payment and 30 persons, with a focus on PWSN, will receive cash assistance for protection with a for 6 months following SOPs of the sector.3. Disability Services will be provided by Arcenciel in the form of Physiotherapy, Psychotherapy, speech therapy, occupational sessions or any other related therapy to 80 individual including PWSN referred from Concern. 4: Trainings will be provided to rehabilitation staff (from Arcenciel) on gender and age sensitive, PWSN rights, CP and GBV core and concepts, safe identification and referrals of CP and GBV cases in the rehabilitation center5: Outreach will be conducted by 3 volunteer under the oversight of Concern to 1,000 individuals. To assist in achieving this objective will be using peer-to-peer approach. To ensure engagement, Concern will provide dignity/hygiene (SRH) kits to 1,120 all programme participants.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

Protection risks for targeted populations–this will include protection risk analysis, including identification of protection threats and of safeguarding risks as well as appropriate mitigation measures, regular review and adaptation of risk mitigation measures as necessary, ongoing coordination with relevant protection actors to identify trends and patterns of threats in areas of operationsCorruption-Concern will maintain well established financial and support systems that are transparent and accountable as well as implement robust monitoring and evaluation systems which include frequent, un-announced verification of programmes assets and outputs.Community tension–This will be managed by building on already established networks with local authorities and communities, particularly as the project is targeting Lebanese families effect by recent economic crisis who were not targeted in previous years. Concern will invest in sensitization processes at community level.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

Concern seeks to ensure that communities are not only involved in decisions that directly impact their lives, but also able to raise feedback and complaints should we fail to fulfil our commitments to them. Concern uses a combination of channels for collecting feedback and complaints; Concern’s Complaint and Response Mechanism (CRM) will be shared at the beginning of the process and at reach of the whole duration of the action. Concern CRM Focal persons will also make random and regular calls to the beneficiaries to inquiry the satisfaction with programme implementation and share concerns about improvement. This platform will be used to develop monthly CRM report for review and constant follow up for improvement.
