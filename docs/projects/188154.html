Project Summary

This project aims to provide winterization assistance for the most vulnerable IDPs in NW Syria as follows:
1- Provide 2000 HH with olive-pit fuel (750 kg per HH) divided to three rounds as per Shelter NFI guidance.
2- Provide 200 stoves for some households who will receive olive-pit fuel and don't have appropriate stove to burn the olive-pit.
3-Provide NFI kits to 500 HH, each HH will receive one NFI kit as winter top up as per Module 5.3 in the SNFI guidance which includes : 2 plastic sheet 4*6, 2 Jerry Can 10lt, 1 Solar Lamp, 4 - 5 single Mattresses, 4 – 5 single High Thermal Blankets, 6 sqm Carpet
Total Number of targeted beneficiaries : 12,500 individuals (1,500 Men, 2,250 women, 4,250 Boys, 4,500 Girls)
Targeted locations at the sub-district level : Dana- Harim-Maaret Tamsrin- and Daret Azza sub-districts

4 - Needs

According to HNAP Mobility and Needs Monitoring MNM Syria -December 2021:4.57 millions are total population in NW Syria from them 2.8 millions are IDPs Based on WOS SNFI Severity PIN file: in the targeted three sub-districts (Dana-Harim-Maaret Tamsrin, Daret Azza) 1.6 million are total population from them 1.3 million are IDPs and 906 k are in need for NFI assistance with high severity Score HNAP figures show: IDP needs for the NFIs in Dana 59%, Harim 72%, and Maaret Tamsrin 54% As per Daily Emergency Needs Tracking weekly bulletin: 8 November-14 November 2021:Percentage of newly-arrived IDP without sufficient fuel for heating 73%Households without bedding 14% Based on all mentioned figures, there is high need for winterization items including fuel, stoves, bedding, and plastic sheets

5 - Implementation of activities

This project will be implemented based on the following steps:1-Need Assessment 2-Selection of the targeted camps 3-Registration and verification of the beneficiaries 4-Procurement process 5-Coordination with all stakeholders including SNFI cluster through winterization plan and 4Ws to avoid any possible overlap6-Coordination with the camp management for distribution time and dates7- Distribution process: *Each Household will receive 250 Kg of olive pit fuel on monthly basis for three months total 750kg*200 HH will receive appropriate stoves *Distribute NFI kits for 1500 HH, each HH will receive one kit as winter top up 8- Monitoring and Evaluation across all stages

10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Sexual Exploitation and Abuse Risks: 1- Overcrowding in the distribution sites 2-Mixed distribution lines between males and females3-No CRM mechanism in place 4-No distribution monitoring in place which might cause discrimination or abuse.5-No qualified staff on the humanitarian principles Mitigation Measure:1-Allocate specific time for some beneficiaries to avoid overcrowding 2-Dedicate one distribution staff to organize the distribution process3-Separate distribution lines for males and for females4-Dissemtinate Complaints response mechanism information including complaints number to be accessible to all beneficiaries and take action immediately especially for the sensitive complaints related to SEA and refer them directly to the specialized NGO in the area.5-Enough monitoring staff during the distribution process 6-Conduct special training about the humanitarian principles to all projects staff in the beginning of the project


11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risks:1-Low quality of purchased items including olive-pit fuel2-Increase prices of items due to inflation3-Shortage of required items4-Delivery delay of the purchased items from vendors5-Delay in the fund dispersal from the private donors Mitigation measures:1- All items will be aligned with the shelter NFI cluster guidance and this will be highlighted in the conditions book during the tender process and the technical analysis will be given accordingly.2-Where possible, all items will be purchase and contracts will be signed with USD to avoid any price increase. 3-Maan may use local procurement in Syria or regional procurement in turkey to avoid shortage of items.4-Maan will allocate enough time to the items delivery, so the procurement will be done before winter to ensure timely response.5-Maan may use the internal fund until the committed fund arrives from the donors to avoid any delay in the winter response
