Project Summary

Bahar has crafted its Food Security interventions to precisely address the food requirements in Northwest Syria while aligning with the strategic objectives of both the HRP and FSL Cluster. This approach is informed by an analysis of the severity of needs using assessments and key indicators. The Food Security program by Bahar prioritizes time-sensitive interventions that not only address immediate needs but also lay the groundwork for sustainable recovery and a swift transition to long-term development.
1. Distribution of Ready-to-Eat (RTEs):
Content of Assistance: Emergency food assistance provided to 2,000 newly displaced households.
Modality: In-kind assistance with ready-to-eat meals.
Indicative Weight is 4 Kg and Value is 12$ for each kit.
2. Distribution of Regular Food Baskets:
Content of Assistance: Food baskets provided to 11,200 households over eight rounds.
Modality: In-kind assistance with staple food items.
Indicative Weight is 47 kg and Value 65 $
3. Distribution of E-voucher Assistance:
Content of Assistance: E-vouchers provided to 4,800 households over eight rounds, allowing families to purchase food items of their choice from local markets.
Modality: Cash-based assistance through electronic vouchers.
Indicative Value: 65$ Determined based on the local market prices of essential food items.
4. Agricultural Value Chains:
Content of Assistance: Support for 15,500 households in agricultural value chains, including provision of seeds, tools, and training to improve farming techniques.
Modality: In-kind assistance with agricultural inputs and training.
Indicative Value: Varied based on the type and quantity of agricultural inputs provided.
5. Rehabilitation of Bakeries:
Content of Assistance: Rehabilitation of four bakeries and training for bakery management and staff.
Modality: Technical support and training.
Indicative Value: Cost of rehabilitation and training programs.
6. Cooperative Establishment:
Content of Assistance: Support for the establishment of two cooperatives.
7. Monitoring and Risk Mitigation:
Monitoring: Regular monitoring of project activities and outcomes to track progress.
Risk Identification: Identification of potential risks such as logistical challenges and funding shortages.
Risk Mitigation: Development of mitigation strategies to address identified risks, including contingency planning and resource mobilization efforts.
8. Coordination with Stokholders: Coordination with other humanitarian organizations involved in emergency response to ensure efficient targeting and avoid duplication of efforts.
Collaboration with the FSL Cluster and other humanitarian actors to ensure comprehensive coverage and avoid gaps in assistance.
Coordination with local markets and vendors to ensure availability of essential food items and fair pricing.
Collaboration with agricultural extension services, farmer cooperatives, and other stakeholders involved in agricultural development to maximize impact and sustainability.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

BO has developed Food Security interventions to address the actual food needs in Northwest Syria while aligning with the strategic objectives of both t HRP and FSL Cluster, guided by a comprehensive analysis of the severity of needs in accordance with assessments. The projects are designed to support the fundamental and urgent food requirements of vulnerable IDPs and HCs Targeted Beneficiaries: The project targets vulnerable populations facing food insecurity, including families living in poverty, communities affected by conflict, and regions experiencing environmental degradation. By focusing on these specific groups, the project aims to reach those most in need and address the root causes of food insecurity.Additionally, the project will extend its focus to newly displaced individuals. The project enhance the food security status of assessed food-insecure individuals through lifesaving and life-sustaining food assistance, encompasses the following:Objectives and Goals:Reduce food insecurity and malnutrition among vulnerable populations.Improve agricultural productivity and resilience through targeted interventions.Build the capacity of communities to sustainably manage food resources and respond to crises.Activities:a) Distribution of Ready-to-Eat (RTEs): Providing emergency food assistance to 2,000 newly displaced households.b) Distribution of Regular Food Baskets: Distributing food baskets to 11,200 households over eight rounds. Bahar will target beneficiaries with food basket distributions.c) Distribution of E-voucher Assistance: Providing e-vouchers to 4,800 households over eight rounds. Bahar will target beneficiaries with E-vouchers allow families to purchase food items of their choice from local markets, promoting local economies and preserving dignity.d) Agricultural Value Chains: Supporting 15,500 households in agricultural value chains aims to enhance agricultural productivity and resilience among smallholder farmers. These interventions include providing seeds, tools, and training to improve farming techniques and increase crop yields.e) Reh.of Bakeries: Recommending the Reh. Of four bakeries and initiating training for bakery management and staff. This initiative will include technical business support covering compliance with minimum bread and nutritional requirements, equipment maintenance, environmental health, food safety regulations, as well as business and financial management training and mentorship.h) Cooperative Establishment: Supporting the establishment of two cooperatives.i)BO will conduct regular monitoring of project activities and outcomes to track progress. Conducting surveys and assessments to measure the impact of interventions on food security indicators. Stakeholder consultations and feedback mechanisms to identify challenges and areas for improvement.BO will Identification of potential risks such as logistical challenges, funding shortages, and mitigation strategies to address risks.
