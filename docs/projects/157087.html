Project Summary

This proposed project aims to sustain, expand the provision of improved WASH, food security and livelihood, and Education services to conflict affected IDPs, returnees and vulnerable host communities in Blue Nile State (Al Kurmuk, Damazine, Rosaries and Geissan localities) and Central Darfur State (Nertiti and Golo in Central and West Jabal Marra localities). The project will address the WASH, FSL and Education needs of 47,190 in Blue Nile state in addition to WASH and Education needs of 76130 persons in Central Darfur state.
In Blue Nile State, this project will: - enhance and sustain access to improved drinking water and basic safe sanitation through drilling and installation of new HPs; rehabilitation of nonfunctional HPs; training of HP mechanics and provision of hand pump maintenance tool sets. The WASH activities will reduce overcrowding at host community water sources that are partially utilized by IDPs – i.e. the intervention will contribute to improved social cohesion between the IDPs and host communities. Aiming at enhancing access to safe basic sanitation facilities for the IDPs, especially those will be affected by AWD, the project interventions will include construction of school latrines; building the capacity of communities through training of WASH committees on water resources management; establishment and support of sanitation clubs; conduct of sanitation awareness campaigns; provision of hand washing facilities; training on CHAST approaches; and water quality monitoring and testing. Hygiene promoters training will be key part of the project to facilitate safe knowledge and practices transfers to end beneficiaries towards sustainable behavior change and adoption of good hygiene practices. To preserve the natural environment, the project contains solid waste management, vector control, and environmental friendly technologies will be adopted. - In FSL sector The project will provide varied interventions to support livelihoods of vulnerable displaced and host communities that have suffered widespread conflict and violence. The proposed project will achieve this through provision of emergency agricultural inputs and services which include distribution of food crops and vegetable seeds, tools and extension service; provide emergency livelihoods training and start up kits, free animal vaccination as part of emergency livestock inputs and veterinary services that will be provided to the IDPs, formation and support of women farmer associations, and natural resources conservation and management.
- In Education sector, the project intends to increase and sustain access to education in emergencies for IDPs and vulnerable host communities in Damazine, Rosaries, Rosaries, Kurmuk and Geissan localities. IRW will provide access to safe learning spaces for 5,303 boys and girls through construction of 8 semi-permanent classrooms and rehabilitation of 8 classrooms. In Central Darfur State, , the project will
- enhance and sustain access to improved drinking water and basic safe sanitation facilities. WASH services will cover nearly 45000 conflict affected IDPs, returnees and host communities,
- In Education sector, the project will focus on the construction and rehabilitation of both girls and boys government schools, aiming at creating a child and education friendly environment with maximum enrollment and minimum drop outs. Quality of education will be enhanced through teacher trainings and provision of reaching aids e.g. quality furniture for teachers and schools, learning and recreation kits and improved water and sanitation services.

Needs

The last Blue Nile Joint Needs Assessment which conducted February 2018 revealed the education infrastructure in the communities is generally sub-standard. Classrooms for the primary grades are very often makeshift shelters made using poles and thatched roofs. Surveyed schools lack basic WASH facilities. No handwashing facilities. Lack of learning materials is problematic at schools. 35% of schools without hand washing facilities, 78.6% have no adequate seating furniture, 21.4 do not have drinking water containers. 12 schools were either destroyed due to the conflict in the targeted localities. The remaining schools those serving host community and IDPs are overcrowded. Additional classrooms are urgently needed. In addition 57 classrooms need rehabilitation. There is a shortage of qualified and trained school teachers especially female teachers. As result of this situation, enrolment rate is 33% (on average); 55 % male and 45% female. For WASH, there is Limited access to safe water; large number of the populations in Blue Nile State including IDPs doesn’t have access to safe drinking water. Water facilities are frequently braking down due to low capacity in operation and management followed with lack of necessary tool kits. Low community awareness on hygiene and sanitation, In FSL, due to the extremely high cost of production and transportation and persistent macroeconomic challenges. This will likely reduce household purchasing power particularly for poor households in food deficit and conflict affected areas that normally import staple foods from main producing areas, FEWS NET assumes the continued absence of subsidies for essential commodities, including wheat subsidies with high food and non-food prices are likely to persist through September 2019, IRW will focus on extension and training services with local communities and their organization ) including farmer school to improve cultivation practices. Inter-agency mission of humanitarian personnel from Zalingei, together with government officials from line ministries in Khartoum visited Golo in June 2018 to conduct technical assessment of humanitarian and recovery needs in multi- sectors in the area.. In WASH, the Government has rehabilitated existing water sources and installed new water sources thereby increasing access to safe water. A major gap in this sector is the poor sanitation and hygiene practices among communities and poor solid waste management in Golo town. In education, The assessment also indicated high rates of school dropout, a total of 1,200 due to the Inability of parents to pay school fees which led them to send their children out for work, The number of students per classroom ranged from 73-135 (the standard is 50 in Sudan) and the estimated out of school population is 15%. Additional classrooms are urgently needed, in addition to rehabilitation of the existing classrooms, poor learning environment and inadequate education materials were highlighted as main problems.

Activities

In Blue Nile state, Education sector, the project will construct and rehabilitate 8 classrooms; construct 8 school health clubs and teachers’ offices Build the capacity of teachers to improve their skills, train PTA members on school management for quality education. In WASH, The project contributes to sector priorities by saving life and durable solutions to around 47190 affected people. Train WASH committees on water facilities management establish and support sanitation clubs, conduction of sanitation awareness campaigns. Train community members on water quality testing and chlorination techniques. Under FSL, the project will improve agricultural production and productivity including extension and training of community agricultural extensions, support of winter vegetables, crops diversification and related extension and inputs. Sustain and expand FSL services to 23,600 IDPs seriously affected population. In Central Darfur State, WASH sector, the project will drill 4 hand pumps, convert / upgrade hand pumps to mini water yards, rehabilitate existing water facilities, conduct awareness on health & hygiene, waste disposal in the returnee’s villages, train WASH committee on operation & maintenance committees and equip O&M committees with tool kits. Under Education: Project will construct and rehabilitate of girls and boys schools, provides quality teaching, education in emergency supplies and recreational materials for 800 children, Train to PTAs on education and school management in emergency, train teachers by gender on core subject and EIE topics, Form 8 children clubs and conduct 8 enrollment campaigns, the project will provide protection through dealing with all forms of violence against vulnerable people. IRW will consult targeted beneficiaries to assure planned services are accessible to all community groups (especially women and girls) – e.g. avoid their exposure to high risks.

Impact on environment/natural resources

This project is aiming at not to influence negatively on the natural resources in Blue Nile and Central Darfur States. the materials that will be used in the WASH, Food Security and Education sectors are friendly to environment. For WASH, assuring water balance (availability, demand versus supply) IR will only upgrade HP boreholes with adequate water recharge to avoid overuse of existing water resources. Under FSL, the project will support farmers with environment friendly seeds (early maturing seeds). For WASH infrastructures, environment friendly supplies (e.g. solar systems) will be used whenever the context allows.
