Project Summary

Mozambique has been facing complex challenges resulting in serious humanitarian impact. Challenges requiring urgent action are the conflict in the northern Mozambique, Cabo Delgado (CD) province which resulted in more than 539,000 internal displaced people; ongoing cholera outbreak and the COVID-19. A number of hospitals have been destroyed or closed and affected the provision of essential health services both in affected districts and the disyricts currently hosting the fled population. Limited access to essential health is also increasing maternal and child health problems as well as loss to follow-up of treatment for chronic disease such as HIV. All these require measures for disease prevention, strengthening referral systems, establishing services provision though mobile health teams to deliver key essential services. UNICEF will respond the these humanitatian situation in coordination with health cluster partners including government health authorities at province and districts level targeting 334,716 children in Cabi Delgado and Nampula, and funding is urgently required to support these interventions and provide timely assistance to children in need.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

• Strengthen essential health service delivery to IDPs children and host families to prevent and treat main childhood illness by procuring vaccines; malaria medicines, mosquito nets, newborn health equipment and cholera treatment kits; • Implement outreach services to affected children through integrated mobiles health services in districts of Chiure, Ancube, Metuge; Montepuez and Palma • Improve access to quality essential health services through recruitment and deployment of additional health staff; refresher training of health professionals and community health workers on essential helath packages and referral systems in emergency setting to manager common illness and referral systems; • Ensure adequate program implementation through contracting of experienced technical assistance for humanitarian response

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The project aims to assist the IDPs affected by insecurity in Cabo Delgado and Nampula with essential health services to prevent disease outbreaks and death. By doing so, the project will e protecting the affected population to additional suffering. While delivering health service the project will also ensure that vulnerable children and women or victims of violence are identified and refered to appropriate case mananger in coordantion with protection cluster and social action department.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

UNICEF and health cluster partners conduct joint need assessment before the intervention. In the assessment the voice of beneficiaries are heard and will vbe taken in account in terms of the priority intervention s for health.Community will participate in planning and implementation of mobile health brigades and community health workers will be trained to improve social mobilization

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

To improve inclusion of people leaving with disability, the provision of both curative services includided in the project will prioritize and provide adapted facilities based on consultations and identification of needs.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project will include procurement and istribution of life saving intervention such as mosquito nets and vaccines which generate waste. To minimize environmental impact a waste management plan is part of implementation of immunization campaigns, glass and rubber materials are collected for a controlled incienreation on site or in dedicated facilty. For mosquito nets all packaging will be of biodegradable materials. Scocial mobilization and behavior campaign are part of the mosquito net distribution to avoid using them for fishing and other harming utilities.
