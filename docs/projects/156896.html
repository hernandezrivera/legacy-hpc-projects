Project Summary

This project aims to provide life-saving and life-sustaining shelter and NFI support through provision of core and essential NFIs (e.g. mattress/mat, plastic sheet, blankets, kitchen set, including cash/voucher for these items), where we targeting of 3000 HH by distribution of 3000 essential NFI kits as per cluster standard and 3000 HH by distribution of 3000 kitchen set, provision of seasonal and supplementary NFIs and shelter assistance (e.g. winter clothing, fuel, winter-specific shelter upgrades), where we targeting of 6,000 individuals by distributing of 6,000 winter clothing kit, and distribute fuel to 4,000 HH, 100 litre per family.
And to contribute towards the resilience and cohesion of communities and households by improving housing and related community/public infrastructure through support to sustainably repair/rehabilitate housing and related community/public infrastructure and facilities to owners/tenants/host families (can include: materials, cash, voucher, cash-for-work, labour, etc.) Where we target 650 HH by rehabilitate of 650 house in targeted areas.

Needs

4.4 million People require NFI assistance, a 6 percent decline from the 2018 HNO, and the second year in a row that the total number of people in need of NFI assistance has reduced. However, within the situation in some areas, such as the northwest, is, in fact, deteriorating amidst an increase in people in need. Access to NFIs has generally become more stable over the last year, however, needs remain substantial, both in terms of humanitarian life-saving needs, particularly those among the high numbers of newly displaced IDPs requiring core, supplementary and seasonal assistance, as well as longer-term needs, among those communities requiring resilience-oriented support to reduce dependence on external assistance and enhance the ability of families to withstand future shocks and stresses. Relatedly, findings show a decreased need for standard/core NFI items and a significant increase in requests for items specific to the situation faced by certain communities or population groups. Such items include clothing, fuel, and batteries.Economic pressures are key drivers of need, with the affordability of rent and NFIs reported as significant challenges especially in NW area, where a worsening of the situation in areas of the northwest such as Idleb and Western Aleppo. The acute humanitarian situation in the northwest is driven by the high proportion of IDPs, overcrowding and the lack of shelter capacity, high rental prices and unaffordable items, and poor access to markets.For NFIs, at the sub-district level, despite the overall reduction in PiN, 44 per cent of sub-districts (SDs) have actually witnessed an increased in the severity of NFI needs.The number of people in need of NFI assistance in Idleb has increased by over 600,000, Markets are essential in meeting shelter and NFI needs, with 99 per cent of people in Syria using markets to fully or partially meet these needs, and the number of people in need of shelter assistance has increased in Idleb and Aleppo, where the total PIN in Aleppo 1,130,815 and in Idleb 847,579 individuals.Populations in areas which experience cold conditions during winter continue to need seasonal support, with communities surveyed reporting heating-related shelter deficiencies (lack of heating and insulation) as the most widespread issue. The need for clothing, hygiene items, and heating fuel was also among the most reported NFI needs across the country. Communities reported different NFI needs among different groups based on gender and age. For instance boys and girls were reported as being in greater need of clothing and shoes; men as in need of heating fuel, light sources, and water containers; women and girls as in need of sanitary pads; women as needing kitchen-related items and; the elderly as requiring blankets, energy, and light sources. These findings demonstrate the wide range of items that households continue to struggle to access. In Syria, households traditionally listed the male spouse (i.e. not female) in HLP documentation. As a result of the crisis, Syria has an increased number of female heads of household and separated households. Given the above-mentioned protection risks related to HLP and the specific need for HLP documentation as a prerequisite to receive certain shelter assistance and rights, women face an additional burden in accessing their rights and this support. Implementation of activities

• provision of core and essential NFIs (e.g. mattress/mat, plastic sheet, blankets, kitchen set, including cash/voucher for these items) where we targeting of 3000 HH by distribution of 3,000 essential NFI kits as per NFI cluster standard and 18,000 individual, and 3,000 HH by distribution of 3,000 kitchen set, 18,000 individuals:- conducting a rapid need assessment - Preparing beneficiaries list.- Declaring a tender, choosing best prices offered by traders.- Receiving and transporting goods to the warehouses.- Distributing NFI packages to target beneficiaries- Monitoring and evaluation.• Provision of seasonal and supplementary NFIs and shelter assistance (e.g. winter clothing, fuel, winter-specific shelter upgrades; where we targeting of 6,000 individuals by distributing of 6,000 winter clothing kit, and distribute fuel to 4,000 HH, 100 litre per family, 24,000 individuals:- conducting a rapid need assessment - Preparing beneficiaries list.- Declaring a tender, choosing best prices offered by traders.- Receiving and transporting goods to the warehouses.- Distributing NFI kits to target beneficiaries- Monitoring and evaluation.The priority will be to purchase NFIs kits from Syria in order to activate the local market in the targeted areas with the possibility of sending them across the border from Turkey when needed. • Support to sustainably repair/rehabilitate housing and related community/public infrastructure and facilities to owners/tenants/host families (can include: materials, cash, voucher, cash-for-work, labour, etc.), where we target 650 HH by rehabilitate of 650 house in targeted areas (3,900 individuals), and due to the specific need for HLP documentation as a prerequisite to receive certain shelter assistance and rights, the availability of HLP documentation will be the main indicator for conducting this activity:- Selecting areas targeted with restoration works.- Undertaking a field survey to select houses that need urgent restoration.- Selecting houses of those in need for urgent restoration and maintenance. - Importing prices of materials for restoration, maintenance and all.- Purchase of materials for restoration and maintenance according to previous quotations- Start the repair and maintenance of the buildings specified in the previous areas.- Follow up all activities and documentation- Final Report and project closingFollow-up plan:• The progress of the restoration project is monitored and reviewed during the implementation of the project, including all external influences.• Follow-up process the plans are reviewed and adjusted according to any internal and/or external changes that may affect the performance, progress, and project of the renovation project.Assessment and learning plan:Evaluation is done at predetermined intervals, as follows:- Progress evaluation: It is carried out after the completion of each step or stage of the project.- Medium-term evaluation: carried out in the middle of the project implementation process.- Final evaluation or project evaluation: carried out at the end of the project or after completion.- Evaluation can take place a few months or years after the completion of the project to measure the sustainability of the impact of the restoration project in the long term.As for the learning of lessons, the learning process takes place through the project cycle, using the results and changes of the environment and the surrounding circumstances in the organizational learning process in order to improve performance in the remaining part of the renovation project as well as to be applied in future projects.

Protection Risk Analysis

Abuse of Authority by distribution actors (GBV, discrimination, monetary coercion); 1. Ensure that the staff have read and signed the Code of Conduct.2. Develop a range of measures and internal procedures to identify staff who are perpetrators of abuse and exploitation and act accordingly.3. Develop a complaint mechanism in place.Crowded living conditions and lack of lighting and gender segregated sanitation facilities can lead to increase in GBV and sexual exploitation; 1. Advocate for provision of alternative accommodation/expansion of existing sites.2. Provision of gender segregated mass shelter in case of large displacement/lack of space to provide individual family shelter unit.3. Provision of gender segregated sanitation facilities sufficiently close to dwellings.4. Provision of lighting around shelter and WASH facilities. 5. Work closely with protection and other sectors to understand the issues and take necessary actions together to address them. 6. Extending consultations such as on siting of facilities with all groups of population including women, girls and PWD.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

-In case there is a military operation and a resultant escalation of conflict, there may be large displacement of people needing NFIs and clothes assistance, Beyaz Eller will ensure relevant local host authorities are aware of response and commit to ensuring basic safety/access, and Consult community (separately women and girls) regarding location of distribution points, -There is a possibility of fires during fuel distributions (flammable materials), so the white hands will work to be distribution points away from congestion, in addition to the field team equipped with fire extinguishers for use when needed. -Beyaz Eller will conduct a thorough shelter assessment and infrastructural technical assessment of the buildings that are aimed to be rehabilitated, that is to reduce the risk of conducting rehabilitation of building with poor structural integrity.-also working under availability of HLP documentation to reduce disputes arising from property rights during rehabilitation of houses. -There are possibility of beneficiaries selling of some items from NFI kits to secure cash, so Beyaz Eller will closely monitor utilization of supplies and keep a track of what is being sold out in local market. An analysis will be carried out that will advise future procurement and/or selection of beneficiaries.
