Project Summary

The project aims to reduce food insecurity through capacity building, skills acquisition and livelihood support of 22,200 beneficiaries across Borno and Yobe State. The project will focus on empowering vulnerable girls, boys, women and men with various livelihoods options by building their skills on vocational activities, enterprise formation and empowerment, CFW activities, market and financial linkages and will integrate VSLAs across all interventions to promote financial inclusion and ensure that beneficiaries build a sustainable means of livelihoods.

1 - Needs

The impact of several contributing factors such as hazards and vulnerabilities, food availability, food access, food utilization including water and stability was assessed on these outcomes variables. The results indicate that about 10 million (9.8 %) people of the analysed population require urgent assistance in the current period (October to December 2020). During the projected period (June to August 2021), these figures are expected to increase to 13.8 (12.9%) million people unless resilience driven interventions and humanitarian assistance in conflict affected LGAs is sustained. (CH Analysis, October to December 2020). For the current period (October to December 2020), about 10 million people (9.8%) of the analysed population are in the critical phases (phase 3-5) of food and nutrition insecurity. Of these population, 689,128 (6.9%) are resident in completely inaccessible or inaccessible communities of partially accessible LGAs in Borno, Adamawa and Yobe States. During the projected period (June to August 2021), in the absence of intensified resilience-driven livelihood interventions and food aid support from the humanitarian actors; these figures are expected to increase to 13.8 million (12.9%) people across the 15 states and the FCT. Of the vulnerable population during the projected period, about 867,470 (6.25%) persons are located in inaccessible or partially accessible communities of Borno, Adamawa (Madagali) and Yobe (Geidam) States. (CH Analysis, October to December 2020)

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

CARE will ensure that all beneficiaries which include avoidance of further harm, ensuring impartial access to assistance, avoidance of causing psychological or physical harm and assist in the claiming of rights of beneficiaries. These principles will be mainstreamed into all project activities. All project activities will be carried out with a protective lens and from a protection perspective following the principles of protection mainstreaming. Specifically, CARE will take the following approaches to ensure protection mainstreaming throughout the process, conducting protection risk assessments, ensure safety, dignity and Do No Harm Principle is upheld, capacity building of stakeholders on protection mainstreaming principles, advocacy on gender & protection considerations and adapting interventions to COVID-19. Finally, CARE will continue to maintain awareness of levels of transmission in all our operating contexts and act in accordance with public health measures called for in global recommendations and/or national guidance. In all our programming, CARE will follow and continue to prioritize a Do No Harm approach. PSEA: CARE Nigeria adapted the CARE International (zero tolerance) PSEA policy to the COcontext and develop the related accountability measures including a whistleblowing policy, training forstaff, partners and peer organizations, development of contextual gender-sensitive security analysisand implementation etc. CARE's staff at all level and other humanitarian actors will understand andcomply with the PSEA policy and accountability measures will be put in place in order to prevent and ortake appropriate measures should a CARE staff be involved or affected by SEA

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

A functional and contextually appropriate feedback and Accountability mechanism will be put in place in such a way that the beneficiaries can confidentially and safely relay their complaints, dissatisfaction and suggestions of project implementation. Due to the context of where CARE works, suggestion boxes will not be efficient hence the use of complain help desk and a toll-free line by which the beneficiaries may relay their complaints comfortably and confidentiality will be prioritized. This feedback mechanism will ensure to provide the beneficiaries timely and appropriate responses while maintaining the principle of confidentiality.
