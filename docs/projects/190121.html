Project Summary

Islamic Relief Ethiopia(IRE) aims to provide emergency lifesaving Nutrition support to drought-affected vulnerable IDPs and host communities of Bare, Godgod, and Kohile districts in the Afder zone of the Somali Region, with a primary focus on the full components of Community Based Management of Acute Malnutrition(CMAM). The project will target vulnerable children under children, pregnant and breastfeeding women—both displaced and in host communities, addressing immediate nutrition needs by providing nutrition services and promoting optimal feeding practices to meet nutrition requirements as well as the adoption of best practices on IYCF and its integration with CMAM response. In the targeted three woredas, the project will cover all kebeles by enhancing the local health system including OTP and SC sites to deliver quality CMAM and IYCF service to host communities and IDPs. Nutrition services and related activities will be informed by the most recently updated guidelines, considering COVID19 prevention and containment measures. Different services delivery modalities will be considered, from static to mobile/community-based, as a proactive and articulated strategy to overcome the accessibility issues affecting current nutrition care coverage and to reach both hosts and displaced populations.
Further, the project will also strengthen the local health system and build the capacity of health workers and health extension workers to respond to already folded primary health care and nutrition services in all targeted intervention woredas. Besides, IRE will work with district health offices in promoting and strengthening Family MUAC, best practices of IYCF including Mental Health and Psychosocial Support (MHPS) throughout the implementation period of this project. Further, Nutrition technical staff will be deployed in all of the targeted woredas for the provision of technical and logistic support

1. - Projects activities

Islamic Relief Ethiopia(IRE) Emergency nutrition response involves several activities to ensure an intervention that delivers optimal nutritional services for malnourished children and pregnant and lactating women( PLW). The intervention will strengthen Community Management of Acute Malnutrition (CMAM) integrated with Infant and Young Children Feeding (IYCF). It will also complement the ongoing Nutrition support provided by the government health office in the targeted three woredas. Below are the key activities that IRE will undertake collaboratively with woreda health offices: 1.1. Provide community mobilization and outreach services: IRE will utilize existing mechanisms to mobilize community volunteers for identifying malnutrition cases through MUAC for Nutrition program enrollment. Health extension workers will be also supported in tracing defaulters. Additionally, monthly mass screening using MUAC for malnutrition case identification among under 5 children and PLWs will be strengthened and supported.1.2. Provide technical support to ensure quality SAM and MAM treatment services by the National CMAM protocol. 1.3 Supporting services provided at stabilization centers(SC), outpatient therapeutic programs ( OTP), and TSFP sites for malnourished under 5 children and PLWs1.4 Strengthening and supporting monthly nutritional screening for malnutrition case identification of pregnant and lactating women (PLW) and referring to the Targeted Supplementary Feeding Program1.5 Capacity building training for health extension workers and health workers on different topics of Nutrition: IRE will organize and provide capacity-building Training for health workers and Health Extension workers on SAM and MAM management. Similarly, Health Extension Workers and Health Workers will receive training on Infant and Young Children Feeding in Emergencies( IYCF-E) 1.6 Provision of capacity building training on the Family MUAC Approach to health workers and health extension workers 1.7 Orientation workshop on Family MUAC Approach will be provided to Mothers /caregivers including other local communities’ leaders to help mothers to follow their children's nutritional status and referrals to nutrition service provision sites1.8 Nutrition Education and Promotion at CMAM Sites, promotion sessions will be implemented in all health facilities. Awareness of key community persons will be built on major health and hygiene topics as well as information on the availability of Nutrition services.1.9 Strengthen the supply chain of TFP and TSFP supplies through procurement of non-food items and transport of TFP and TSFP supplies to health facilities 1.10 Conduct training on the basic concepts of nutrition in emergencies for different level health care providers and maintain continuous assistance

Islamic Relief Ethiopia(IRE) has a long-standing experience in the Somali region, has been a leading agency implementing multiple Nutrition and Health projects in the Afdher Zone of the Somali Region, and key actor in the sector for years as well as the most active implementing partner of the region in the area. During its stay in the area, it has gained good experience in working relationships with all stakeholders. The major stakeholders that IRE has worked within the project area are the beneficiary communities, different line offices of the government, and humanitarian actors working in the area- from the community, elders, religious and community leaders and IDP focal persons are potential community mobilizers and the main actors in beneficiaries’ selection during the project life. The government line offices are backing the project technically as well as mobilizing the community. Moreover, during implementation, project launching, project review meetings, monitoring by regional partners, final evaluation, training of local community, and handover of project activities will be properly carried out to facilitate project exit from the area and sustain the project outputs. IRE will strongly work in coordination and in complementary with relevant local & national actors and further stretches its coordination with UN agencies, Federal and Regional level Nutrition and Health Clusters, WFP, other humanitarian actors, and government, particularly with humanitarian actors who are currently operational in the region through different types of clusters and task forces technical working groups.

3.6 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members using age and gender appropriate, safe and confidential channels?

Islamic Relief Ethiopia (IRE) already has a strong corporate commitment to accountability and forward accountability to its key constituencies - children and the communities where they reside. Beneficiary accountability is also one of IRE’s quality standards reflected as a cross-cutting issue throughout the project cycle. IRE has engaged the targeted beneficiaries and local actors in all stages of the project. In the targeted woredas, plans for communications and Complaint Response Mechanism (CRM) will be prepared to facilitate inclusive beneficiary feedback and complaint mechanism. During the inception period of the project orientation will be provided at the community level where the project is implemented so that they are aware of their rights to raise complaints should they encounter and have any. After encouraging that they should complain whenever they feel it necessary, they will be assisted to raise complaints by erecting billboards in each of the target kebeles/villages with contact details including telephone, emails, and others of the focal points for contact in case they want to share feedback and/or complaints. The billboards will be erected in a visible place where one can easily see and local language will be used on the billboards so that one can easily understand the instructions and contacts written on the billboard. The project will place suggestion boxes in each village, so people can also put their complaints in those boxes in written form. The keys of the boxes will be kept with the focal points for the same purpose at the field level. If it is sensitive, it has its channel of communication, and the main focal points PSI coordinator and staff assigned for this purpose at the IRW headquarters. Once the PSI coordinator receives the complaint, she immediately will post them on the online system and the necessary investigation will be started.
