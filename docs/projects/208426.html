Project Summary

Enrich-initiative aims to ensure that conflict-affected boys, girls, and adolescents, including those with disabilities, have access to safe and protective skill development opportunities in both formal and non-formal settings in Jere and MMC LGA of Borno state by addressing the challenges of the;
Impact of Conflict in the region which has left a profound impact on the well-being and opportunities available to children and adolescents, necessitating targeted interventions for their empowerment.
Vulnerability of Specific Groups, Children with disabilities face heightened vulnerability, often lacking inclusive access to education and skill-building programs that consider their unique needs.
Limited Protective Skills Opportunities, the scarcity of safe and protective skill development opportunities hampers the holistic development of conflict-affected boys, girls, and adolescents, limiting their potential for a secure and self-sufficient future.
Key Components inclusive Skill Development Programs implementing formal and non-formal skill development initiatives that are inclusive, recognizing and addressing the specific needs of boys, girls, and adolescents, including those with disabilities.
Safe Learning Environments, creating environments that prioritize safety and protection, ensuring that all participants, irrespective of their abilities, can engage in skill-building activities without fear of harm or discrimination.
Capacity Building for Caregivers, providing training and support for caregivers, educators, and community leaders to enhance their capacity in creating and maintaining safe spaces for children and adolescents to develop skills.
Community Engagement and Sensitization, conducting awareness campaigns and community engagement programs to promote the importance of inclusive skill development and protective environments for conflict-affected youth.
Expected Outcome, increased access to safe and protective skill-building opportunities for conflict-affected boys, girls, and adolescents, contributing to their overall resilience and well-being. Enhanced inclusivity in formal and non-formal educational settings, ensuring that children with disabilities are actively involved in skill development programs. Empowered caregivers and communities equipped to sustain safe and protective environments for the holistic development of conflict-affected youth in Borno State.
Through this project, we seek to create a positive impact on the lives of conflict-affected youth by fostering their skills, resilience, and inclusive participation in the rebuilding process of Borno State, North-East Nigeria.

2 - Activities

The project aims to provide adequate response people of concern(POCs)Activity 1. Baseline Assessment;Conduct a comprehensive baseline assessment to understand the current status of conflict-affected boys, girls, and adolescents, including children with disabilities, in terms of their access to education, skills, and protection.2. Inclusive Skill Development Programs:Develop tailored skill development programs that cater to the diverse needs and abilities of the target groups, ensuring inclusivity and relevance to their future aspirations.3. Safe Learning Environments:Establish and maintain safe and protective learning spaces, both formal and non-formal, equipped with necessary infrastructure and measures to ensure the physical and emotional well-being of participants.4. Capacity Building Workshops:Organize workshops and training sessions for caregivers, educators, and community leaders on creating and sustaining safe environments, understanding the specific challenges faced by children with disabilities, and fostering inclusive learning.5. Community Engagement Activities:Implement community engagement activities, including town hall meetings, awareness campaigns, and focus group discussions, to sensitize communities about the importance of providing safe and inclusive opportunities for conflict-affected youth.6. Resource Mobilization:Secure resources, including funding, educational materials, and skill development tools, to support the effective implementation of the project.7. Partnerships and Collaboration:Collaborate with local NGOs, governmental agencies, and community-based organizations to leverage existing resources and expertise, fostering a collaborative approach to addressing the needs of conflict-affected youth.8. Monitoring and Evaluation Framework:Establish a robust monitoring and evaluation framework to track the progress and impact of the project, ensuring that it meets its objectives and adapts to emerging needs.9. Psychosocial Support Services:Integrate psychosocial support services to address the mental health and well-being of conflict-affected boys, girls, and adolescents, recognizing the psychological impact of conflict on their lives.10. Accessibility Measures:Implement measures to enhance the accessibility of skill development programs for children with disabilities, including adaptive technologies, specialized training for instructors, and accessible learning materials.11. Advocacy Initiatives:Launch advocacy initiatives at local and regional levels to promote the rights of conflict-affected youth, especially those with disabilities, and to encourage policy changes that support their inclusion in educational and skill development opportunities.By addressing these field needs, the project aims to create a holistic and sustainable response to the challenges faced by conflict-affected boys, girls, and adolescents in Borno State, North-East Nigeria
