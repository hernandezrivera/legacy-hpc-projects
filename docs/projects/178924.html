Project Summary

Amal’s project will contribute to the Child Protection sector HRP objectives,
so, the goal of the proposed project is to enhance the protection environment and access to CP services for conflict-affected children with a focus on the most vulnerable individuals
(e.g. people with disabilities (PWDs)) through supporting a CFS in Kafr Takharim Idlib, and jendares in Afrin, Syria.
The proposed project is based on supporting a safe environment for children, where they feel physically and psychologically safe
the project provides CP services:
PSS (structured and unstructured) for children
awareness sessions (structured and unstructured) for children, community members, and caregivers
Specialized child protection services (case management) for girls and boys are available in targeted locations and referral services
Training, recreational activities
For children at risk like separated and unaccompanied children, neglected, vulnerable to early marriage, at risk of CAFFAG will employ Amal teams in both safe spaces and schools, and there will be mobile teams.
Emergency response for new IDPs Providing PFA, refer cases at risk focusing PwD to service providers.
total BNFs are 17410 (women 1250 and men 810 BNFs,
child 15350 (Boys 6850, girl's 8500)

4 - Needs

With the approaches of the civil war in Syria its tenth year, nearly half a million Syrians have lost their lives Civilians, especially women and children, are at constant risk of injury or death as a result of airstrikes and the ongoing fighting between armed groups.According to HNO 2021, around 13.4 million people are estimated to require some form of humanitarian and protection assistance, including 6 million in acute need, 25% of them have disabilities and 75% of them are women and children, where children with and without disabilities comprise 44% of the people in need across Syria. As of February 2021, approximately 1.7 million IDPs were living in 1,385 camps or informal sites (including 24,800 people displaced in January and February 2021 alone), in Syria, children have gone through a very difficult situation, a lot of them injured or suffered from psychological trauma, with no effective CP prevention. According to HRP 2020, Children continue to be exposed to multiple protection risks and severe abuses and grave rights violations, Child labor, including in its most hazardous forms such as recruitment and use of children in combat and support roles, violence, abuse, neglect, and exploitation, including GBV, early marriage, early pregnancy, the psychosocial needs of many girls and boys are left unmet.Adverse childhood experiences may lead to negative health and wellbeing outcomes in the long- term. Psychological support for people exposed to intense violence and/or experiencing lasting distress would also be unavailable, reducing people’s ability to cope and recover.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Protection risk 1: survivors drop out of the case plan because they have high expectations that cannot be met.probability: mediumImpact: highMitigation measures: the case management team will have a survivor-centered approach and will remain realistic when discussing the available options with the survivor.Protection risk 2: Beneficiaries will not have access to services if movement restrictions related to COVID-19 increaseprobability: highImpact: mediumMitigation measures: AMAL will put in place a contingency plan to resume activities online.Protection Risk 3: Children with disabilities have no access to the services.Probability: mediumImpact: highMitigation measures: Ensure our centers are accessible to children with disabilities, particularly those with physical disabilities through rehabilitating the WASH facility in the center and the center entrance to be more easily accessible.Protection Risk 4: COVID-19: positive cases are reported to be widely spread among the population of Northwest Syria.Probability: high, Impact: mediumMitigation measure: using hand sanitizers, and the beneficiaries, avoid sharing personal items, wear gloves and masks, Promote the hygiene of the CFS and sessions locations by ensuring and appropriate sterilization and cleaning daily, Emphasis social distance during sessions inside the CFS and during the outreach activities among children and parents, provide awareness raising orientation about COVID-19 risk and ensure the presence of awareness banners within the work area and in a clear place and flexibility to switch to distance sessions whenever needed to prevent health risks among the BNFs.

7.1 - Please list any risks related to sexual exploitation and abuse that have been identified during the project design phase and any risk mitigation and prevention measures that have been adopted and built into the project

Risk: Risk of exploitation or abuse by staff members/service providers, and low knowledge on PSEA and other types of exploitation and where to report. Impact: high protection riskMitigation:Amal has policies, procedures, and other measures in place that relate to preventing sexual exploitation, abuse, and harassment. So, Amal will work on the following: 1- train the staff on code of conduct, PSEA, CSG and follow them periodically2- Amal ensures to have in a place an accessible mechanism for reporting sexual exploitation and abuse in line with Amal safeguarding policy.3- Activating complaints mechanisms to protect PSEA in particular, placing them in easily accessible places, desigsning awareness-raising banners, placing boxes for complaints and suggestions, and activating the hotline number (WhatsApp)4- Designing special complaints mechanisms for children being children through drawing or other tools5 - provide awareness-raising sessions in the targeted community on PSEA and methods for reporting them and gaining the trust of society and spreading humanitarian principles and stick to them.
