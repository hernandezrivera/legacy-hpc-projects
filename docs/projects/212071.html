Project Summary

HEKS/EPER Strategy in Syria focuses on three basic needs: Water, bread and work. HEKS/EPER prioritizes strengthening the work of the pre-existing structures without substituting them. Therefore, HEKS/EPER mainly implements infrastructure support to ensure sustainable impact and restore the initial standards of living.
HEKS/EPER acknowledges the importance of subsidization as a lifeline for millions of vulnerable Syrians and an effective social support system. Therefore, under this project, HEKS/EPER plans to support mainly the subsidized bread value chain as well as some infrastructure related to irrigation and agriculture in five different governorates as follows:
1. Damascus:
• Zahreha neighborhood, bakery rehabilitation, supply & install new production line with generator for Yarmuk bakery, 45,000 POC
2. Rural Damascus:
• Jdidet Artuz, bakery rehabilitation, replace the old production line with generator - 90,000 PoC
• Jaramana, new baking oven, 45,000 POC
3.Aleppo:
• Maskana, bakery rehabilitation, supply & install new production line with generator, 90,000 POC
• Hanano neighborhood, Supply & install new production line with new generator, 45,000 POC
• Ar-Ramouseh Industrial District neighborhood, bakery rehabilitation, supply & install new production line with generator, 90,000 POC
• Qadi Askar neighborhood, rehabilitation, replace the old production line with generator-90,000 POC
• As-Safira, bakery rehabilitation, replace the old production line with generator, 45,000 POC
• Fajdan, bakery rehabilitation, replace the old production line with generator, 45,000 POC
• Abu Jrin Al-Safira, bakery rehabilitation, replace the old production line with generator - 45,000 POC
• Maskana-Onaiza, Rehabilitation of irrigation channel - 20,552 POC
4. Hama:
• Kafr Zeita, bakery rehabilitation, supply & install new production line with generator - 45,000 POC
• Shezer, Rehabilitation of irrigation channel project , 100,000 POC
5. Homs:
Homs, Ar-Rastan, Mechanical, electrical & light rehabilitation works for the Dam, 500,000 POC
The project activities are estimated to benefit 1,295,552 PoCs. The total estimated cost for the above activities is around 2,920,000$ while the total project cost is 4,000,000$. HEKS/EPER secured around 400,000$.
Below is the disaggregation of the PoCs (based on percentages in the HNO 2023):
Male 0-17: 323,888 (25%) - Female 0-17: 272,065 (21%)
Male 18-59: 297,977 (23%) - Female 18-59: 349,799 (27%)
Male 60+: 25,911 (2%) - Female 60+: 25,911 (2%)
PWDs: 220,243 (17%)
The activities will be implemented as follows: Obtaining needed approvals from ministries and security authorities - Conducting the technical assessments - Defining the scope of work - Launch the procurement process - Contract with the selected contractor to launch the execution of the works - Continuous monitoring of the contractor’s work.
Project Assumptions: 1) Stable security situation 2) Consistent humanitarian access 3) No mass movements of populations 4) Availability of material in the local market 5) Acceptance of community.
Project Risks:
1- Protection: Safety incidents during or after the execution of the works – Mitigation: 1- Install caution signs around the work sites 2- Contractors sign HEKS/EPER’s safety measures. 3- Install railings and fire extinguishers in the infrastructure
2- SEA: Due to the nature of the planned activities (Infrastructure works), the interactions with PoCs are almost entirely nonexistent, and thus, the risks related to Sexual Exploitation and Abuse are almost totally avoided.
3- Delays in obtaining needed approvals for assessment/implementation - Mitigation: keep close coordination with the line ministries at different levels to speed up the processes.
4- Theft of rehabilitation/construction materials - Mitigation: coordinate with local ministries to ensure local ownership of materials & facilities to promote cost recovery systems for maintenance and promote community ownership.
