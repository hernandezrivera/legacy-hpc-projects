Project Summary

The project intends to provide integrated nutrition services targeting vulnerable population including children, youths and PLW. The ultimate goal of the project is to contribute for the improvement of nutritional status of the camp and host communities and increase access and coverage of nutrition service . CMAM, IYCF and Micronutrient supplementation are key staragies the project employ to treat and prevent under nutrition. Facility and community based appraoch will be used to reach the community though various interventions including growth monitoring and promotion (GMP), screening , referal and treatment of malnutrition (CMAM), C-MAMI and IYCF counseling services and Micro-nutrient seupplementation for children, youth and PLW. With vast knowledge and experience in emergency and disaster management, the project will have contegency plan to respond to man-made and natural disasters including disease out break, moonson season and cyclone with close collaboration with all stakeholdres.
