Project Summary

The project goal is to improve the protection of Nigerian refugees and other vulnerable populations affected by the conflict in the Far North region of Cameroon and strengthen state and community protection services. For this intervention, IMC will build on its experience on community-based approach implemented in all child protection program activities within the intervention site in close collaboration with MINAS social centers where there exist. In this light IMC will work with MINAS social centers to set up or reinforce existing child protection committees in each locality targeted by the proposed action. IMC UK will lean on the 154 Child protection community based mechanism structures (CPCBMS) put in place during previous interventions (70 in Mayo Sava, 60 Logone and Chari, 24 Mayo Tsanaga) which have been institutionalized. IMC will equally capacitate key community actors such as health service workers, teachers, traditional and religious leaders to strengthen referral pathways and build community ownership especially on birth certificates’ declarations to facilitate their production on one hand and behaviour and perception change towards girls for the fight against early marriage and sexual abuse on the other hand. In fact, it has been present since 2013 in Far North region providing protection assistance (CP and GBV) to refugees and host communities in Minawao camp and host communities from surrounding villages of Gawar, Gadala and Zamai and IDPs and refugees in offsite as well in three the division affected by violence in Mayo Tsanaga, Mayo Sava and Logone & Chari. With operational offices in Mokolo, Kousseri and Mora and strong expertise in protection and good relationship with all stakeholders especially UN Agencies, implementing partners and lines government ministries. IMC UK has built its reputation in reinforcing local protection mechanisms built through existing community and government structures (child protection community based mechanisms structures and community volunteers including club protections). International Medical Corps has been providing protection services to refugees living in Minawao camp for three recent years. Through this project, IMC will seek to consolidate progress achieved so far and enhance collaboration with line ministries (MINPROFF and MINAS) by implementing joint activities such as joint supervisions, training, monitoring of activities. Case management will be provided to GBV survivors and existing referral pathway sustained. Capacity building will be a key aspect of the project, social workers and community mobilizers will be trained on various topics following a training plan that will be developped bases on training gaps identified during a training assessment. Life skills activities will continue to be organised and there will target both survivors and other vulnerable women regardless of their civil status (refugees or cameroonian). Case management will be carried out by the trained social worker using GBV guiding principle and the GBV IMS tools and the IASC guidelines. The GBV Response Officer will ensure the one on coaching with the social worker and enable each social worker to comply with the case file storage which should be kept in the cupboard closed with the key and not easily accessible to those who are not in charge of providing support to the survivors. With the support of the Camp Program Health team the survivor will received medical treatment, and the program team will strengthen the medical treatment in ensuring that the survivors have basis requirement as food, potable drinkable water, confortable restful environment and family support to overcome the effect of the incident. The program team will liaise with UNFPA to ensure that the Health care center is equipped with the PEP Kit and other relevant material to provide support to the survivors.

1 - Besoins / Needs

GBV Prevention and response:Case management will be carried out by the trained social worker using GBV guiding principle and the GBV IMS tools and the IASC guidelines.Clinical management of survivors:With the support of the Camp Program Health team the survivor will received medical treatment, and the program team will strengthen the medical treatment in ensuring that the survivors have basis requirement as food, potable drinkable water, confortable restful environment and family support to overcome the effect of the incident. Capacity building:IMC will recruit gender balanced Community volunteers for camp intervention. These community volunteers will work closely with the community mobilizer and the protection committee made key persons among the religious leader, the women chief of blocks, Gender club, husband school, and the CBO’s leaders to ensure that the refugees community have access to good information and are aware on the consequences of the VBG issues and all the services available in the camp. They will be trained on the GBV core concept, type’s causes and consequences, guiding principles, services available, referral mechanism and the survivor centered approach. They will put in place prevention strategy as per the context and relief the most vulnerable group. Awareness raising and sensitization:The community mobilizers will be responsible of the community outreach sessions in the camp. With the support of the community volunteers they will carry out the information’s sessions, small group discussion and focus group discussion on the protection issues, harmful social norms, the consequences of GBV and the services available. The women leaders and the chief of blocks will carried out their small group awareness session in the community shelter, market, water point, distribution place, the religious leaders will hold their session in churches, mosque, with the ceramics teachers; the gender club will have a monthly planning in both secondary school and primary school. The husband school awareness will be focused towards men and boy and the CBO’s will share information in women associations. The protection officer will take part to the camp sectorial meeting to share GBV information and collect feedback mechanism from each group.Empowerment activities: All the known skills of the survivors will be promoted and those who are willing to share the experience with one other will be organized into group of six for skills building activities. The women and engagement agent will promote skill already perform in the WGSS. Nigerian refugees and other vulnerable children affected by the conflict will receive age and gender sensitive support and services adapted to their needs. Thus, boys and girls will access child-friendly spaces as well as case management or referral services without any form of discrimination based on sex, gender, ethnicity or religion. The design and implementation of activities will also take into account the specificities of more vulnerable children such as disabled, CAAFAG, UASC, girls victims or at risk of early marriage or sexual abuse… The IMC intervention will be built on a strong community-based approach by involving many different local stakeholders including women’s associations. At least 40 % the child protection community based members (RECOPE) will be women. Girls and boys will make up an equal percentage (50%) of adolescent club members. In order to sustain the actions that will be carried out by the project, state social workers will be placed at the heart of the strategy of setting up and supporting community protection mechanisms. Community mobilizers, RECOPE members and adolescents clubs from communities will contribute to the promotion of children's rights and will involve the leaders of their community in the identification and referral of vulnerable children including those in need of birth certificates.
