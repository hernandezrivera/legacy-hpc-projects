Project Summary

Working with our partner organisation, Mukti Cox’s Bazar, COTE suppotrs 150 classrooms in the Kutupalong camp providing education for 7,500 children. Our 75 colourful learning centres encourage creativity and self-expression. Each class includes education lessons, daily opportunities to play, and basic health support for students in safe spaces with trusted adults. The Community representatives are being involved through monthly meetings to mitigate the protection risks. To address social cohesion, project staff and host teachers are being trained that will create an positive mindset towards FDMN community. Lessons on health and protection being provided in the classrooms. We are also conducting DRR activities for the LCs which are at risk of flashflood, landslide and water stagnation.

1 - Identified Needs

We operate in camps 1E, 1W, 2E, 2W, 4, 5, and 17. Each of our 150 classrooms accomodate 50 students in 2 shifts , totaling 7,500 children ages 6-14 -- or about 3% of the total of 239,541 the sector has identified in need of education support in the age 6-14 demographic. Having run education programs with MUKTI in the camps for years, COTE has a clear understanding of the need for Rohingya children to have quality education delivered via a pedagogy they can understand. Since the classrooms are in a shape, we are now more focusing on delivering classroom lessons related to health, protection and gender. Training contents focusing social cohesion will be delivered to Field officers and host Teachers.
