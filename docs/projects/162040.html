Project Summary

The inhabitants of makeshift camp are living with enormous health suffering because limited access to medical center, hygienic sanitation and safe water. To solve the health sufferings, RPN has taken to initiatives the project with a view to aiming provide health service in order to provide medical treatment. Assessment Team of Health Sector was deployed to Cox’s Bazar and findings revealed that the needs and gaps are still exists especially access to critical services. Thereby, in order to fill the gaps RPN with the support of Islamic Help, Global Relief Trust and Humanity Rises will continue the existing Health Post in Camp 8E, Block B-10, Balukhali, Ukhiya and Camp 19, Block C-8, Thainkhali, TajnimarKhola, Ukhiya. Through the helath post serivce to also including host community. Main objectives of this program are to improve access to comprehensive primary health care services of Rohingya communities as well as host communities, with special emphasis on maternal and child health services, adolescent reproductive health.
Under the project, achiving EWARS, DHIS, 4Ws reporting in close collaboration UN and others egencies in camp. Extend & continue the primary health care service which already established in two different camps in order to provide medical treatment, medicine, pathological service, family planning counseling & service and health awareness counseling. Necessary health infrastructure has been built in a suitable place considering short distance traveling of Rohingya women and kids. Pharmacy has been established within the health post compound. The patients take medicine from pharmacy showing their prescription. During medicine delivery the patients gains knowledge about personal hygiene practice through leaflet & booklet. Family Planning Counselor visit door to door for providing counseling service and contraceptive. Midwife provides pregnant women antenatal and prenatal care service that come to clinic. She gives the necessary supervision, care and advice to women during pregnancy, labor and the postpartum period. She helps to decrease maternal mortality rate and pregnancy related sufferings. A mobile medical team served the host community twice in a week. Treatment providing place has being selecting consult with the local community.
Presently RPN has been conducting two health posts; one is under camp 19 and another is under camp 8E. Subject to available of fund RPN has willing to extend the facilities of health service for both community in the same camp.

Objectives

1. Improve equitable access to and utilization of quality lifesaving and comprehensive primary and secondary health services for all crisis-affected populations with special focus on sexual, reproductive, maternal, neonatal, child and adolescent health; mental health and psychosocial support; and non-communicable diseases. 2. Ensure the prevention and timely response to communicable disease risks including diseases with outbreak potential, and prepare for other health emergencies including monsoon and cyclone. 3. Encourage healthy living, improve health seeking behavior and utilization of essential service package among refugees and host populations through community engagement, with special attention to gender and age considerations and vulnerable groups. 4. Strengthen health sector coordination, information management and monitoring towards achieving rational, standardized and accountable health service delivery.

Needs

Bangladesh from Myanmar. This number added to the total of 300,000 members of the Rohingya community who were residing in Bangladesh prior to the new influx. Now Rohingya people are living in Cox’s Bazar district, particularly in Teknaf and Ukhiya upazilas. These refugees are now living in thirty four different camps, and among Bangladeshi host communities. The camps has become the largest and fastest growing refugee camp in the world, where more Rohingya are living extremely close to each other without access to basic health care services. Rohingya influx has been creates an extra pressure for Bangladeshi society. Also it has been creates additional pressure on health system of Cox’s Bazar. Primary health care services remain limited due to lack of quality service provider in the camps. Newborn care is poor, especially at community level and referral linkages need to be improved. Family planning needs remain unaddressed, particularly for the most vulnerable populations and support are required to strengthen neonatal care. Due to shortage of adequate health facility Refugee are in grate risk. There is urgent need of quality health service provider including necessary drugs and diagnose facilities. The proposed initiative will undertake to meet the mentioned problems. It is may be an effective way to reduce the burden of health suffering of Rohingya.On the other hand two years ago, the host community highly welcomed the Rohingya in a view to humanitarian ground but at present it is being to be burden for them. So tension is being increasing between both parties. Day by day the situations are going out of control. So it will be better for every stakeholder (The Rohingya & the Host Community as well as the local administration) if we can engage the host community successfully with the program activities that will help them to reduce health vulnerability and will be able to minimize their extra tension. In addition, The Government of Bangladesh has decided to support host community a certain %of total budget. Health Sector also has given priority to host community engagement into the health program activities.

Activities or outputs

The project usually targets the Rohingya refugee and helps them to gain access to health center. To achieve the target the following activities has been set are: i.Health services will be provided through existing health posts. ii. Provide safe and adequate service to comprehensive primary healthcare iii. Advocate, promote and facilitate of awareness campaign in camps and iv. Establish effective referral system with linkage to appropriate health center for better treatment. v. Provide family planning counseling with necessary supervision, care and advice to women during pregnancy and the postpartum period. vi provide health service to the host community twice a week. vii. establish pathological service. and viii. Arrange necessary logistic support and recruit human resources as sector standard requirement.
