Project Summary

This project will support 600 olive farmers (2 donums per beneficiary), 20% of them at least are women, where they will be provided with agricultural inputs, ploughing the farms, supplementary irrigation, training, and technical support for farmers, where Silk Road agricultural team will supervise and follow up on the crop case during the whole season, supplying the farmer with fertilizer (30 Kg of NPK fertilizer and 30 Kg of urea fertilizer 1 liter of amino acids), 1 liter of pesticides to control ( Dacus oleae or olive fly and Euphyllura olivine or psella).
supplementary irrigation will be supplied for each farmer with three times irrigation during July, August and September, which are the drought months.
On the other hand, agricultural training will be provided at all stages of the project to educate the farmers with correct scientific methods that must be taken into consideration in the process of agriculture, where we will establish the farmers school for the targeted farmers and all technical issues related to olive tree breeding will be taken and the farmers will be able to apply correct practices, also agricultural brochures will be distributed for the farmers to raise awareness and guide them to the correct scientific methods which must be applied.
Thus, the project provides integrated services to olive farmers so that they are guided, directed, and assisted in all stages during the season to achieve integration in the project and ensure the results.
We will target the most vulnerable families like IDPs, women household families, families that have no income generation to work with Olive harvesting during November and December, where 100 daily worker will be hired, to help the farmers families during harvesting.
We will support the farmers with wages of the olive milling. Each farmer will be supported by paying the wages of 500 kg olive milling.

Implementation of activities

600 farmers will be supported with the all of the required items and crop services according to the seasonal calendar, where they will be chosen according to specific criteria like vulnerability, having access to the olive orchard, minimum previous knowledge etc.The farmers will be supported by ploughing their farms two times, one in the Spring and the other one will be in Winter, where we will contract with tractors owners/ vendors during March and December to provide the farmers with this service which is very useful to keep the water in the soil and improve the soil structure. They will be provided also with the insecticides to control two olive insect pests which are ( Dacus oleae or olive fly and Euphyllura olivine or paella) where they will receive vouchers to take their rations of pesticides from selected vendors.We will supply farmers' orchards with supplementary irrigation by distributing water tanks. Each orchard will be irrigated three times during the summer months by contracting with water tankers at fixed same price throughout the contract period, this supplementary irrigation gives a lot of benefits, first of all, it increases the total product and Mitigate the yearly phenomenon where the tree gives a good quantity of crop every year, it also increases the resistance against the pests. The farmers will be provided with comprehensive training throughout the season to cover all technical issues, including farming, pest control, pruning, fertilization, irrigation, and harvesting, where farmers' schools will be established periodically and continuously trained, training and will include field visits where our technical field team will conduct these activities giving the beneficiaries the right technical ways how to deal with any problem they will face during the whole season, training will focus on the theoretical issues at first and the biggest part will contain practical activities to give them a qualitative skills.The farmer will be able to take care of his crop and produce optimal production.The farmers will be supported during the harvest season by hiring daily workers. Farmers will be supported by 10 harvest workers to help them harvest their crop. The workers will be selected from weak families with no income sources or from families are woman house_ held or from the IDPs. Where we will need 100 workers to be hired during harvesting season each of them will work for 12 days to cover the total targeted beneficiaries by cash for work way.Farmers will also be supported by the wages of the olive milling. Each farmer will be supported by paying the wages of 500 kg olive milling, which is expected as 2 donums olive productions. we will contract with modern olive mills to take high-quality oil.
