Project Summary

Needs:
As of December 2023, Syria's WASH crisis impacts 13.6 million people in urgent need. Prolonged conflicts, inadequate maintenance, energy shortages, and poor water management have severely damaged Northern Syria's WASH infrastructure. These challenges disproportionately affect marginalized populations, including people with disabilities. The multifaceted crisis, marked by conflict and climate challenges, particularly damages water and sanitation systems. Diminishing international funding and minimal development support exacerbates the situation, while recurrent disease outbreaks, waterborne diseases, prolonged drought, and a severe water crisis contribute to rising mortality and morbidity rates. Limited access to clean water and inadequate sanitation elevates the risks of diseases like cholera, scabies, lice, and leishmaniasis. Since August 2022, a cholera outbreak has impacted various governorates across Northern Syria, with 211,327 suspected cases, and 26 cholera deaths.
Implementation and intervention activities:
1.The organization will provide water trucking services in a reduced number of 5 sites in NWS where no alternative solutions were found, and communities’ vulnerability was high due in part to their type of shelter
2.The organization will conduct regular water quality monitoring of FRC, PH, and conductivity levels at Source, communal, track and Household level in line with the water supply chain.
3.The organization and its partner will provide water pumping services in 17 sites across Idleb and Aleppo governorates. 49,065 individuals will receive between 30 and 40 liters per person per day.
4.The organization and its partner will support 4 water stations in Aleppo and Idleb governorates with water system rehabilitation and/ or solarization and electrical gird connection.
5.The organization will install a transformer and conduct electrical work to connect the pump to the electrical grid.
6.The organization will rehabilitate and extend the existing water network in Kinnisrin camp to meet the demands.
7.The organization will continue solid waste removal activities in 18 locations across NWS, reaching 41,905 individuals.
8.The organization will continue to operation checks on sanitation infrastructure including latrines, showers and handwashing points in 18 camps in Aleppo and Idleb governorate
9.The organization will provide existing sanitation facilities in 7 sites in Aleppo and Idleb governorates, with a desludging service
10.The organization proposes to rehabilitate and extend the existing network to accommodate the new needs in Al Harameen camp
11.Community volunteers, both male and female members of the communities, will be identified to conduct HP sessions.
12.In Aleppo and Idleb governorates, The organization I will distribute water tanks to facilitate safe storage of water and limit the spread of water borne diseases.
Risk and assumption:
Access/operations: The authorities in areas of intervention do not allow internal access to provide humanitarian assistance; Border closures between Turkey and NW Syria prevent the flow of goods or personnel into Syria; challenges in transportation of goods and equipment into and across NES and NWS due to escalation of the conflict.
Escalation of conflict (including ground fighting, airstrikes / shelling) affects the project activities: Military escalation and opening of new frontlines; Kidnapping, injury or killing of humanitarian workers due to conflicts in operational areas; Presence of unexploded ordnances and mines.
Economy: Price and exchange rate fluctuations; Closure of banks or restrictions from banking system negatively impact the flow of funds inside Syria; Partial or total disruption of the supply chain.
Environment: The project generates significant greenhouse gas emissions coming from energy production and transport of goods, plastic pollution, and groundwater pollution.
