Project Summary

This project is designed to reduce the critical educational gaps and needs for 18 schools in the Northern and Southern parts of kutum Locality in North Darfur state, by tackling the needs of 5,154 people including IDPs, Returnees, and the hosting community. Out of which 4,992 are in school age segregated as (1,924 Boys, and 3,068 Girls) , and 435 adults (230 Males, and 115 Females) encompassing teachers, and parents, in Bairi, biraidik, Maryood azrag, Amray, Ango, Anka, Alhilf alshamali, biraidik, Um teen, Gora, Um sidir, Hahsab, Radma, Hamra albeer, kasa, Naina, Kurbe alhamra, Jadeeda baneen, jadeeda banat, and Um Dilaika schools in Rural Kutum, and other 6 schools in southern Kutum).
FPDO will increase and sustains access to basic education through reaching out the communities residing in the targeted areas by enrollment campaigns in order to promote the importance of children enrollment, and the importance of girls’ education, construction of 18 permanent classrooms and rehabilitation of 38 permanent classrooms, construction of 26 gender sensitive latrines with 6 drop holes, and rehabilitation of 8 permanent latrines. Also FPDO will intend to provide 600 seating units for 2400 students (1200 boy & 1200 girl).
The improvement of educational environment would be through: Distribution of school Uniform for 3000 students (1500 girls and 1500 boys), teaching and learning materials for 3000 students (1500 girls and 1500 boys).
The action will also contribute in improving resilience of local actors through capacity building (PTAs and teachers) in mitigating the bad effects of education emergencies. FPDO will organize 8 teacher trainings for 165 teachers on core school subjects and life skills targeting and train 270 PTA members on leadership, school management, child protection, and school monitoring, gender equal representation and resources mobilization.

Needs

The 2019 HNO has stated that almost 1.6 M all-over Sudan’s states are in need of Education services , and that Darfur alone is a host of 930,585 Persons, 286,175 of them in North Darfur state. Kutum locality houses 221,282 capita, the 2019 HNO confirmed that 49,900 are in need of humanitarian assistance, divided as 25,919 IDPs, almost one thousand returnees, and 22,664 vulnerable residents. The Education sector’s target is about 15,917 persons 99% are of school age.displaced people are registered as living in camps. The majority of children in need of emergency. Due to the mass influx of IDPs into host communities there is a strain on education services in these communities. The situation of people living in protracted IDPs camps has recently been strongly affected by influx of newly displaced people adding more pressure on existing services and by a deteriorating food security situation.The school environment is unfavorable condition, the students are overcrowded at the classrooms which not help students to reach education benefits. That situation wouldn't help the teachers either to instruct and communicate properly with students. The primary education offices stated that: girls are noticed to be neglected of catching schools and being educated mostly to be engaged in home work or send them to work to increase household sources, putting them in a position to face harmful situation and lost their chances in education and protection from exploitation, abuse and harassment. Evidenced by a rapid assessment steered by FPDO in November 2018, reflected that schools of (Hamra albeer, kasa (290, , Naina, Kurbe alhamra, Jadeeda baneen, jadeeda banat, Bairi, biraidik, Maryood azrag, Amray, Ango, Anka, Alhilf alsani, Um teen, Gora, Um sidir, Hahsab, Radma, and Um Dilaika) accommodating 7,325 students (3,036 boys, and 4,289 girls) in Rural Kutum educational section; are in favor for rehabilitation of existing class rooms and latrines, construction of new latrines and classrooms and teachers’ offices, training of teachers and PTAs, fencing, provision of seatings, office furniture, teaching and learning materials providing schools with Water tanks (1000 liter capacity), installment of washing facilities, and soap for hands washing, provision of rubbish trash cans, in addition to a gap in construction and rehabilitation of latrines. It’s worth mentioning that among the 18 targeted schools; only 3 permanent latrines are existing the rest are semi fixed and most of them needs rehabilitated and some are not functioning.
