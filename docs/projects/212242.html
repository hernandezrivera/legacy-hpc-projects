Project Summary

Open a carpentry workshop in the area for the following reasons :
1-The furniture is considered one of the most crafts that the people of Eastern Ghouta relied on to earn their livelihood in the past.2- the great destruction on workshops of artisan in the area.3-the presence of expert manpower and creative craftsmen, with no suitable place for their expertise.4- the inability to work far from their area due to the high cost and scarcity of public transportation and the high cost of the fuel.Most of the region's population lives below the poverty line and in disastrous situation in terms of food security , education and health care.Most of the children suffer from malnutrition due to the high cost of living and the unemployment of the parents.
Implementation of activities :Al Nada will rent a place for the carpentry and buy all the required items and select the personnel the carpenters that lost their jobs during the crisis according to some specific criteria that will jointly developed with organization Also Al Nada will install solar panel to generate the electricity so the carpentry will not suspend the work especially with the lack of fuel. Al Nada will do follow-up visits to check the quality of the furniture and the consistency of the work and ask for periodic inventory of the items and figure out the challenges and difficulties in terms of resources and utilities. Al Nada will also demand a financial report on a periodic basis.
Protection Risk Analysis:-Criteria of selecting carpenters might become controversial since they are only 23% (115out of 500) of the total target who work in this profession, and all of them are returnees in order to provide them with the opportunity to pursue their career. (high)Noting that, the selection criteria will be based on the most vulnerable persons in the area and it might include the persons with disability.-the worker might get injured while using the tools if not restricted to the safety rules. (high) Al Nada will conduct a workshop for the safety of the labors and will provide them with the personal protective equipment (Protective mask, safety glasses, pairs of protective gloves, safety shoes and harnesses) providing the building with first aid supplies Authorities approval to launch the carpentry (middle)
Fire :(high) provide the place with fire extinguishers and Al Nada provide them with a training on how to use the fire extinguisher.Spread of COVID 19:use face masks , ensure physical distancing -Earthquake: (low) providing awareness sessions on how to take safety and security measures-Security situation (low)-Lack of electricity(high):a solar system will be installed-Accompanying children below 18 to the carpentry by their fathers (Child Labor) Risk: High
Mitigation measures: All carpenters should sign a form prohibiting them from accompanying their children ,Al Nada will send a monitoring team on a weekly basis .Exchange rate of US dollar against Syrian pounds continuous monitoring of exchange rate based on the Syrian Bank updated to make the necessary adjustment to the budget.
