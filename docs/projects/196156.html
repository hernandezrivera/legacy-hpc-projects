Start Date

End Date

Project Summary

The urgency of the displacement situation in Syria requires a rapid response. 1,430 IDP sites are listed in the IDP Site Integrated Monitoring Matrix, that host 1,859,651 IDPs or 354,655 families as of 31 / October 2022.
The massive influx reasserted that data collection activities should be prioritized for humanitarian and CCCM partners to respond to the volatile situation in NWS in an efficient and timely manner. The tremendous increase in the number of IDP sites in Northwest Syria has recognizably called more efforts to assess needs and situations in these IDP sites. The CCCM cluster members' capacity to assess these sites on a regular basis got limited due to the increase in the number of IDPs. In the shade of new camp establishment and camp separation, the need to follow up and update the CCCM cluster daily was a gap. The high severity scale of IDP needs in IDP sites, especially in informal settlements and the need to regularly update the cluster members to inform their response and identify gaps were critical to ensuring comprehensive multispectral response.
The proposed project concerns the Internally Displaced Persons in Northwest Syria living in existing and newly established IDP camps and camp-like settings. Based on the IDP Site Integrated Monitoring Matrix ISIMM, 354,655 Families / 1,859,651 Individuals currently reside in 1,430 IDP sites (164 Planned Sites, 1236 Self-Settled Sites, 28 Collective Centers, and 2 Reception Centers).
The concerned population (517,719 girls 27.84%, 515,091 boys 27.7%, 441,933 women 23.76%, 384,908 men 20.7%,75.591 PWGs).
The established access of SMT reaches all needed data and information that CCCM may be concerned about and assign SMT to collect.
This project aims to contribute to the CCCM cluster 60-member organization’s operations, by disseminating timely information on needs and IDP’s situation in their area of operations.
MHD through its existing field network with various key informants of camps management, local councils, community leaders, and other critical KI types and its experience in data collection in and out of camps and reporting will address the planned activities to achieve the project objective.
MHD through the distribution plan of its field staff will cover 9 districts that host 1,430 IDP sites listed (ISIMM October 2022 Update) and other spontaneously established locations that could be identified in the future within the coverage area.
In terms of Staff, MHD will hire 30 experienced enumerators and enumerator assistants that will locate in a plan to cover the targeted geographical areas of Idleb and Aleppo governorates considering proposed activities and high concentration of IDP sites. Hired field staff will have wide experience in data collection, communication, coordination, and computer skills. MHD will distribute them under the supervision of 2 team leaders.
MHD field staff can access physical visits to almost 99% of IDP sites and 100% for remote information data collection in Idleb and Aleppo. Although some locations are not accessible for official visits because of restricted access and interference by local authorities in the Azaz district for NGOs
Below are the activities MHD is planning to implement in the planned, informal, listed, and unlisted IDP sites, throughout this project:
1. (ISIMM).
2. (ISIMM Plus).
3. Track and share IDP movements and analyze displacement trends.
4. Ad-hoc data collection and reporting:
• Distress calls.
• Verification of GPS coordinates and administrative divisions for an IDP site.
• Ad-hoc surveys and assessments.
5. Promote participatory management structures for IDPs living in sites with participatory management committees (including active participation by women).
6. Incident reporting for any events that impact IDPs and IDP Sites.
7. Develop and strengthen the capacity of CCCM Cluster members and camp management actors on camp governance and protection mainstreaming.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

- Coordination of multi-sector responses in IDP sites.Protection Risk: Create a pull factor and expose IDPs to physical safety risks when/if the camps and settlements are located in unsafe areas- Gap in services provision and in targeting the most vulnerable groups.Likelihood: High - Impact: high.Mitigating Measure:1. Maintain the CCCM guidance and position on the camp establishment and camp support ensuring that camps are the last resort.2. Make sure the timely reporting through CCCM cluster relevant tools, and report the unlisted sites in the ISIMM tool through ISIMM plus tool.3. Coordinate a full-service package of support tailored to needs where possible.4. Where applicable and possible, precautionary measures to avoid exposing beneficiaries and the staff to risks, including having a risk management plan, contingency plan, and an Early Warning System in place.- Track IDP movements and analyze displacement trends.Protection Risk: IDPs that have fled to areas where CCCM members are not present are not assessed/tracked and their needs are not identified.Likelihood: Medium - Impact: high.Mitigating Measure:1. contributing and following up on the IDP tracking.2. Coordination with the relevant clusters and humanitarian agencies for better response.3. Highlight the areas that have not been assessed when mapping IDP concentrations during sudden displacements.4. The purposes of the assessments are clarified to beneficiaries without raising expectations.


8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

MUZUN staff have a duty to report any allegations of behavior that might be abusive or exploitative towards beneficiaries or staff. This applies equally to allegations concerning staff from another organization. Any Code of conduct breach related to SEA requires sensitivity and discretion in order to avoid harming those involved any further. 1-Investigating PSEA: whistleblowing, management suspicion, complaints from staff or beneficiaries, notifications from other agencies, police, or from external audits. 2-Confidentiality:Efforts will always go into prioritizing the protection of the Survivor, his/her family members, witnesses, complainant(s), and any other individuals who might be affected by an allegation or complaint.All staff involved will aim to avoid causing any further harm during and following an investigation. 3-Investigation Structure: are carried out by the Turkey office, A committee is formed of 3 people inside Syria to verify the information and submit a report to the Turkey office. The decision is taken by the Executive Director in consultation with the project manager and the HR Director in accordance with PSEA Policy. 4-Reporting:Confidentiality, informed consent, and the wishes of the Survivor of sexual exploitation and abuse will always be given priority. 5-Recording and Reporting SEA Complaints with PSEA Network and appropriate measures must be followed to maintain the confidentiality of all data gathered.


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

1- Intervention by sanctioned:Medium,MUZUN operates in NWS as a humanitarian actor, in which all humanitarian actors are independent in their activities. The field access focal point and HQ access focal point of MHD is responsible to coordinate with the AWG to ensure proper coordination with local authorities. 2-COVID-19 risk for the team or communityMedium,MUZUN has a dedicated procedures document SoP that clarifies how MHD will avoid being a transfer factor of Covid-19 for IDPs in IDP sites through their visits. The SoP will be shared separately. 3-Arial bombarding:Unlikely,Based on the Security plan, all filed MHD staff can shift data collection to remote data collection at any time, thanks to the nexus of key informants. Based on the duty of care policy of MHD, field staff can receive benefits for shelter and food in case they are displaced from their community of origin. 4-Physical access to IDP sites:Unlikely,In Turkey-backed opposition group areas, there could be restrictions to conduct formal visits in planned/formal camps. SMT mitigates this through regular coordination meetings with AFAD and local authorities at the field and HQ levels. 5-Enumerator arresting:Unlikely,All Team leaders and enumerators of SMT clarify the mandate of SMT and the data collection they are doing, questionnaires are shared in inquired by local authorities. MUZUM filed access focal point is responsible to ensure coordination with these local authorities to not interfere.
