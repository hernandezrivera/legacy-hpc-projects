Project Summary

FHI 360’s response to the emergency in Cabo Delgado through integrated approach (WASH, Health and Protection) in Ancuabe and Metuge district. FHI 360’s goal for the proposed program is to improve the wellbeing of conflict-affected and displaced populations through an integrated, multi-sectoral response. Child Protection project comprised with comprehensive child protection case management, child friendly spaces and Safe Healing and Learning Space, community-based child protection mechanism and embedded with MHPSS intervention.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Child Protection project comprised with comprehensive child protection case management, child friendly spaces and Safe Healing and Learning Space, community-based child protection mechanism and embedded with MHPSS intervention in Ancuabe and Metuge district targeted 9,509 (1,515 Male; 1,515 Female; 3,239 Boys; 3,239 Girls) targeted population in IDPs and 501 (80 Male; 80 Female; 171 Boys; 171 Girls) in host communities. Fhi 360 integrated protection, gender and GBV into child protection activities. SRH and MHM will be provided at child friendly space for adolescent.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Setting up system for access to timely and quality child protection case management services for children at risk by using static and mobile service delivery modality in Ancuabe and Metuge district. This is including providing community based alternative care for unaccompanied and separated girls / boys, identify and document unaccompanied and separated children, reunify separated and unaccompanied children with their families or place in long-term community-based care. FHI 360 will consult with community and collaborate with CCCM to identify location to establish two child friendly safe spaces (CFS) which is including Safe Healing and Learning Space (SHLS) to provide SEL class, parenting skills and informal. FHI 360 will setting up and supporting community-based CP mechanisms and structures collaborate with government actors, other CP partners and community: FHI 360 will strengthen or set up Child Protection Committees (CPCs) in each site composed of 15 members (five adult male, five adult females with five adolescent male and female). FHI 360 will conduct community awareness and peer education activities to promote a safe and enabling environment in which children can thrive and build resilience and caregivers and other community members can freely seek CP services.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

FHI 360 conducts situation and context analysis and risk assessment to develop risk mitigation measures. FHI 360 is held accountable for integrating key actions to reduce risks and incorporate 4 essential protection principle elements through out the project cycle management. Prioritize safety, dignity, and avoid causing harm: Protection activities will seek to prevent unintended negative effects of all action such as exposure of the survivors to further forms of harm by maintaining confidentiality. Meaningful Access: As part of protection intervention, FHI 360 will identify affected population with accessibility issues and address issues appropriately to their need and diversity. FHI 360 will arrange for their access to assistance and services. Training will be conducted for staff from all sectors to ensure internal referral is strengthened, which will allow the non-protection sectors to safely refer all program participant including those with accessibility issues to access services. FHI 360 promote program participant’s level of their evolving capacity of participation and empowerment. All actions of our project are based on the result of program participant's feedback. FHI 360 respects the needs and wishes of program participants. We build friendly environment for program participants to share, lead and thrive.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

FHI 360 uphold equity, diversity and inclusiveness. FHI 360 will use inclusive designs to improve access to services for the disabled. FHI 360 will work with the staff at each facility to accommodate physically disabled persons. Each facility will develop simple protocols to physically assist program participants into the facility if necessary and provide care without requiring them to move about the facility. Adequate spaces are provided for people with special needs. IASC Guidelines on the Inclusion of Persons with Disabilities in Humanitarian Action, will be used to assess the specific needs disabled person, so that they receive the necessary support. FHI 360 will collaborated with available district Disabled Persons Organization (DPO) to link with DPO representatives at the field sites who will directly support protection activities by interpreting sign language and assisting the physically challenged and ensuring that their voices are heard, and their needs are integrated into activities.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

FHI 360 engineer will conduct the technical assessment prior to construction of child friendly safe spaces (CFS) and Safe Healing and Learning Space (SHLS), latrine and rehabilitation of water points which is including the environmental impacts analysis to mitigate negative environmental impact to human health and environmental health. And will ensure that the proposed site does not allow for harm to the environment. For the construction/rehabilitation related works, FHI 360 will adopt a contractor driven approach as it requires technical expertise and will be supervised by FHI 360 Engineers to ensure adherence to environmental protection. FHI 360 will ensure that the levelling and backfilling of the ground is done properly after the construction work to prevent any possibility of flooding in the area that could be breeding ground for mosquitoes. For rehabilitation of water points, FHI 360 will also conduct a sanitary survey to check on possible sources of contamination and ensure that any potential source of contamination of water points are prevented before rehabilitation.
