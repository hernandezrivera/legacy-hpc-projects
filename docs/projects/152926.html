Project Summary

This project aims to increase access to emergency essential primary health care services through an integrated service delivery package using both the developmental and emergency service with a focus on the major causes of Morbidity and mortality among the most vulnerable community (Mothers and under-five children). This project will support three mobile clinics, five PHCUs and three PHCCs while one county Hospital will also be supported to enhance integrated service provision in AKobo County. Through this project a total of 70,000 (23990 Men, 23610 Women, 11290 Boys and 11110 Girls) people will be reached with Preventive Promotive and curative health care services, of this 33,600 (48%) are IDPs, 28,000 (40%) are Host community while the rest 8,400 (12%) are Other sections of community. Nile Hope will also provide OPD consultation to 34,618 clients in the course of the project duration, we are also anticipating to reach to a total of 267 (15%) mothers with skilled delivery. To ensure enhanced service on Mental Health and Psycho social support we anticipate to support four PHCCs/ One PHCC in all the four counties Uror, Pigi, Akobo and Maiwut. The PHCCs are to be a center of provision of integrated Health Nutrition and WASH services including MHPSS and CMR service to Rape survivors. Hence a total of 114 under five children with SAM with medical complication will be treated in the targeted PHCCs, A total of 135 beneficiaries will be reached with CMR service. In addition a total of 1,154 beneficiaries with PTSD will received Mental health support and counseling. This funding will also ensure access to Immunization of 14,722 with measles and 16,322 children will be reached with EPI.
A total of 11,66 house holds will be reached with Health education and awareness creating sessions to enhance community level surveillance, Disease prevention and early health care seeking behaviours hence enhancing resilience among crisis affected populations. Nile Hope believes that an enhanced coordination between humanitarian and development approaches is essential. As a result the development support has increasingly been forced to move away from the ambitions of building sustainable health structures and come to focus more on service delivery and resilience oriented activities, In that way, the gap between humanitarian and development interventions has shrunk. Nile Hope will work on enhancing and synergizing the emergency humanitarian response to focus on lifesaving activities while striving at building more durable solutions from the start. With the urgent life-saving needs that exist in South Sudan at present this is a considerable challenge which requires a joint effort of intertwining and synergizing both the development and humanitarian responses together. The laboratory capacity of eight PHCC/Us and One county Hospital will also be strengthened to be able to diagnosis of malaria, TB, HIV/AIDS, Kala-Azar both in Pigi and Akobo/Wechjal PHCC.

Needs

Akobo County had faced repeated conflict that resulted in suspension of mosthumanitarian activities, access to payams within the county had been a challenge as it’s a result of inter-clan conflict and tension. Health infrastructure had been the prime targets Health facilities in Akobo west especially Walgak is vandalized, solar panels and cold chain materials were looted leading to a suspension of services in the area. Akobo west is a land locked location that is severely in lack of trade channels affecting community to access basic items including food supplies. Morbidity have remained high in the greater Akobo and accessibility is also a challenge since some routes have armed elements making it hard to move from one side to the other to provide humanitarian services. Current IPC grading (till end of July-2018) showed that Akobo is Akobo is at a verge of sliding to IPC 4. HMIS facility report showed that Diarhoea and Pneumonia are the leading causes of morbidity and mortality in the county. The WASH infrastructure in in health facilities is a rudiments and none existent. Child health cold chain facility remain to be a challenge, Cold chain infrastructure is in Akobo West remains a challenge hence EPI service need to be rejuvenated. Pigi County is geographically divided in to a government and opposition controlled localities, Nile Hope is supporting one PHCC and Three PHCUs in Kurwai, Pieri, Diel and Kolanyang .The river route had been the front line and all the payams across the river Nile had been abandoned by the local community. Recently following the relative calm there is a high population return from neighbouring Malakal areas to these are areas that were abandoned since the active military conflict in early 2014 Khorflus, Atar and Alam. The county doesn't have safe water source except the river and swamp that are shared with animals. The county is IPC-4/Emergency phase/ with an urgent need for humanitarian support. It’s estimated that 15,000 returnees from Malakal has arrived in Khorflus, Atar and Alam localities areas that are not covered through the unreliable World Bank RRHP funding. Yuai PHCC is supported through RRHP funding, it is the only available referral unit for the whole corridor across Nyirol and Uror Counties and Nile Hope courtesy of the support from the National MoH is able to equip the unit with basic equipment and PHCC level staffing, the facility has a cold chain and is now embarking on providing major PHCC level services provide a primary upgrade which has enabled the team to provide BEmONC care and enable it to deliver a full package of CMR services need a gap filling fund to ensure that the developmental available feeble funding could be toped up to ensure a full package of integrated Health WASH and CMR and BEmONC services in the PHCC.In Uror county, Nile Hope is supporting the IDPs through mobile team, this project ends as end of 2018, which will end by end of 2018, while the identified needs in the county still needs continuation beyond 2018. Latest IPC grading showed that the county is under IPC-4.Like other counties Maiwut is politically under the government and opposition forces and movement within the county had been the major challenge. All the health facilities are in a bad share where there is a need to strengthen the long abandoned health facilities. The community are using water from river and there is no any safe water source in Maiwut County. The county is prone to outbreaks of cholera and other waterborne diseases as it do not have aany kind of WASH infrastructure. The county is under Level 4 According to IPC grading Jan-March-2019.Nile Hope is supporting six health facilities that are under the RRHP project. Following the relative peace there is a high influx of returnees estimated 8500 from Ethiopia crossing over to Maiwut and Jekowv corridor that has not shelter, Health, WASH and food need to have an urgent need of integrated Health WASH, Nutrition and FSL response.

Activities

Provision of outpatient consultation to under five and above five women, men, boys and girls, supplying health facilities with drugs and other medical supplies to provide curative services, Treatment of under-five children with SAM with complications, strengthen basic emergency obstetric and neonatal care including the clinical management of SGBV, Intensify surveillance and support immunization of children against measles, polio and other vaccine preventable diseases and integrated capacity building refreshers (WASH, Health and Nutrition) to prevent, detect and respond to epidemic prone disease outbreaks focusing on cholera/malaria /measles and other diseases of public health concern(TB/HIV AIDS), Providing psycho-social first aid, providing F-ANC and Post Natal Care service check-ups, first aid for mass casualties and putting up ORT- Corner for immediate on site management of Diarrhoeal cases in Leer, Akobo, Pigi, Uror and Maiwut Counties. The community based C4D team will be the arms of social mobilization and awareness creating team that will ensure early case detection and referral of cases to nearest mobile unit. Strengthen active surveillance both at community and facility level, engage Boma Social mobilizers to be part of the HIV/TB case finding and suspected cases will be referred to nearest facility.
