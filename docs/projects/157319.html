Project Summary

This project will assist the IDPs, returnees and host community affected by the conflict in South Kordofan and Blue Nile who are not receiving any other comparable interventions with a special emphasis on the needs of women and children.This project is designed to ensure improved access for vulnerable populations to quality primary health care services. the project is set to provide primary health care services with all its components to the IDPs, returnees and host community in Blue Nile and South Kordofan state. the project will target the rehabilitation of 10 PHCs and the construction of 1 BHU, as well as the provision of life saving emergency drugs to the targeted health clinics, the project also provides reproductive health care services to pregnant women by staffing trained midwifes, providing RH kits and midwifery kits, providing referral services and vaccination for the targeted population. the project will also insure that children under five have access to basic nutrition services and immunization services by providing nutrition supplies to the centers and by training community volunteers to increase the detection of severely malnourished children. the project will also work on increasing the capacity of MoH staff providing consultation and treatment services by enrolling them in basic and refresher trainings.
this project also aims for the best disease control practices by making sure that all the supported centers are part of the national surveillance system, submitting regular reports and monitoring for disease outbreaks. the project will also insure that Pan Care work closely with the SMoH and the health sector in mobilizing resources and repositioning stokes for countering outbreaks and health hazards.

Needs

Blue Nile and South Kordofan states are in the mid of a long armed conflict between the government and the SPLA that erupted years ago leaving large numbers of IDPs scattered. the capacity of the SMoH in Blue Nile and South Kordofan is not able to meet the health needs of the large number of returnees and IDPs that created a great deal of pressure upon the already weak system. this project will address the needs of vulnerable residents, IDPs and returnees that do not have access to essential health care services. the project will take into consideration the high need of women specially pregnant women for reproductive health services.

Activities

increasing access to health services through the rehabilitation of 10 PHCs and the construction of BHU.the provision of secconded medical staff and medical doctors through the collaboration with the SMoH.the provision of life saving drugs including under five medications and nutrition supplies with the needed coordination and collaboration with relevant UN agencies ( WHO, UNICEF, UNFPA).supplying the targeted centers with RH kits and providing referral services for obstetric complicated cases.the provision of immunization services for under five children and PLWs.the training of 55 health staff on drugs management, maternal and child health care and BCC training.training of 100 volunteers on the detection and reporting of disease with out break potential.the training of female community volunteers (50) to increase the coverage and access to ANC & PNC.the training of medical staff on the reporting and handling of GBV cases and the establishment of women sensitive groups.the provision of medical consultations through qualified staff with special emphasis on disabled people and people with special needs. equipping all supported health facilities with incinerators and the training of the working medical staff on waste management and proper disposal of medical waste. the provision of safety boxes, sanitation gloves and autoclaves.the addition of gender sensitive latrines in all the supported health facilities with hand washing facilities.
