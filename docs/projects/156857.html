Project Summary

BINAA will provide life-saving assistance to conflict-affected IDPs in Northern Syria in most needed camps. The camps located in Aleppo governorate in Afrin, Azaz and Jebel Saman. While the camps located in Idleb governorate are those located in Harem and Idleb. The gaps and needs identified by an assessment covered the actual needs in terms of vulnerability and technical sides. The intervention will provide assistance to 6900 IDP Households (34500 individuals) (6522 men, 6788 women, 9960 boys and 11230 girls) in Idleb and Aleppo.
The assessment conducted in 15 IDP sites, 10 sites in Aleppo and 5 ones in Idlep. BINAA will provide tent winterization, gravelling pathways and improving safety and security plans in the all IDP sites.
BINAA has the capacity to provide the assistance in a timely manner. The project will also focus on utilizing safety and security plan including providing fire extinguishers and first aid kits in addition to training on safety and security planning and implementation. The project will consider the accessibility of people with disabilities to the WASH facilities by providing handrails, concrete ramps, and flush WC taking into account the gender separation requirement.
The project will enhance the resilience and livelihood of the camp's residents by implementing an exit strategy targets 300 persons (150 women and 150 men) from all camps located in The camps located in Aleppo governorate in Afrin, Azaz and Jebel Saman and the camps located in Idleb governorate are those located in Harem and Idleb. This strategy depends basically on vocational trainings which will be provided to beneficiaries selected as per of pre-established prioritization and selection criteria. Market place assessment will be conducted to determine the most common practical professions, so the beneficiaries can benefit from.
BINAA will deliver 20 days (60 hours) training in each camp. The training will be conducted in a venue nearby the camp but not inside in order to encourage people to go out of the camps. In addition, the trainees will be targeted with two types of training:
- Technical training: it aims to refresh, enrich and complement the potential good professionals’ skills
• Improving the working skills of workers already skilled.
• Latest updates on the tools, manners and market needs.
Both vocational and practical training will be provided as per the market assessment and trainees needs.
- Business training: it aims to provide the trainees with the needed skills to get the skill to establish their own business and be able to manage it properly. The training will focus on:
• Calculation of the required budget for a small startup so they can prepare an appropriate proposal.
• Skills related to management and leadership side to improve personality traits.
• Preparing proposals for establishing a new business.
Taking into consideration that a gender analysis is critical to understanding the social and gender dynamics that could help or hinder aid effectiveness. We worked on identify gender gaps, such as unequal access to CCCM services for women/girls and men/boys that need to be addressed.
for this intervention, BINAA will ensure that HLP guidance are considered and utilized.

Needs

BINAA conducted a rapid needs assessment to design this project. The assessment and based on the HNO 2019 shared by the CCCM cluster in north-west Syria, the project will cover 15 camps(10 in Aleppo (Afrin, Azaz and Jebel Saman ) and 5 in Idlib (Harem and Idleb), 6900 IDP Households (34500 individuals) (34500 individuals) (6522 men, 6788 women, 9960 boys and 11230 girls) recognized as IDP sites by CCCM cluster in Idlib and Aleppo to develop evidence-based strategy for ensuring an effective intervention including the winterization and weatherproofing needs. The results showed that 92% of tents in assessed camps need insulation and weatherproofing and 45% of them need ground levelling. In regards of safety and security plans and equipment, results showed that 85% of camps do not have any fire extinguishers or first aid kits. Residents of 38% of camps suffer from tripping or barriers hazards and 54% of camps have an unsafe entrance with no gate. The site's infrastructure needs improvement in all camps especially facilitating access of elderly and people with disabilities to the camps facilities. Pathways to WASH and other facilities in most camps are bad or very bad, 70% of camps need to install new latrines and septic tanks to cover the need of the IDPs, 90% of camps need to install tools (handrails, concrete ramps, and flush WC taking) for disabled people to help them when they use the WASH facilities. Some camps have been planned to be targeted because there are new tents settled and still need for winterization and weatherproofing installation or replacement. There is a clear need of the camps residents to be encouraged and motivated to leave the camps and many people responded positively in this term.

Implementation of activities

The project will cover 15 camps(10 in Aleppo (Afrin, Azaz and Jebel Saman ) and 5 in Idlib (Harem and Idleb), 6900 IDP Households (34500 individuals) (34500 individuals) (6522 men, 6788 women, 9960 boys and 11230 girls). IDP Sites improvements including infrastructure upgrade, tents repair, raising the level of the tents (ground insulation/tent levelling), internal Tents insulation, water and weatherproofing and gravelling of the entire site area or camp internal roads and pathways (corridors) ensuring the access to facilities for elderly and people with disabilities. Improving and/or establishing the safety and security measures inside IDP sites like fire points, first aid, fence and night watchers, provision of external lights to critical facilities like toilets.- Establishment of Camp Committees for different purposes and community mobilization.-Establishment and implementation of exit strategy to enhance the resilience of camps residents. The implementation of the activities includes the following:- Attaining the HLP documentation from the landlord to ensure that HLP guidance is considered and utilized in addition to avoid any potential risk of suing in the future. Regarding the all areas and Afrin in particularly, the field team will communicate with the landlords in order to get an official pre-approval to works in the camp located in that land.‐Technical assessment to identify the certain winterization required works for all targeted camps.‐Contract service provider, and conduct and supervise the winterization work (weather insulation, thermal insulation, and tent levelling) to enhance the protection.‐Develop the technical capacity of the camp management team and volunteers from the camp residents on safety and security plans.‐Technical assessment to identify the elderly and people with disabilities required protection works.‐Contract a service provider, conduct and supervise the protection works.‐Marketplace Assessment‐Community mobilization (Conducting meeting with the camps representatives to clarify the project scope of work)‐Selection criteria and application process‐Verification process will be carried out by Binaa team by visiting the applicant to ensure the validity of the provided data.‐Beneficiaries selection after verifying the collected data in compliance with the adopted criteria‐Training It takes 20 days (60 hours) for each training in each camp.‐Trainees evaluation (The trainees will be evaluated at the end of the training)‐Submission of project proposals.‐Implementation phase‐Supervision and monitoring after providing the tools required to get started with the project

Protection Risk Analysis

Risk-01: There is a risk of discrimination or perceptions of discrimination if a service is provided to specific groups or individuals at the exclusion of others. Likelihood: Medium. Impact: High Mitigating measures: 1. Ensure a comprehensive service mapping is undertaken so that risk mitigation and communication with communities can be proactive and informed; 2. Ensure consultation with communities related to location of WASH services to understand relevant community perspectives; 3. Carry out safety audits for beneficiaries’ access to site locations. Risk-02: GBV can be compounded by overcrowding and lack of privacy. In multi-family tents and multi-household dwellings, lack of doors and partitions for sleeping and changing clothes can increase exposure to sexual harassment and assault. Tensions linked to overcrowding may lead to an escalation of intimate partner violence and other forms of domestic violence. Where situational and risk analyses are not systematically conducted, these risks might not be identified and rectified.Likelihood: Medium. Impact: HighMitigating measures: a) advocate for appropriate and adequate assistance to be provided. We will include women, girls and other at-risk groups to determine such things as if systems/criteria are in place to determine how shelters are being allocated; if they are built for safety and privacy; and/or if shelter materials and shelter-related NFIs are being distributed in areas that are safe. B) Consultations with women, girls, boy and men should be organized c) community safety audits. Risk-03: Unintentionally, misapplying Do no-harm principle: the likelihood is low and the impact is high. This risk will be monitored basically by Binaa M&E team. Community mobilization in addition to ensuring proper and adequate need technical and verification process. The verification process should cover both side of the intervention, vulnerability in addition to the technical need assessment. Feedback and complaint mechanism is adopted to contribute in solving the issues. Risk-04: provision the service for not the exact targeted people, elderlies in particular. The likelihood is low and the impact is high. This risk will be monitored by Binaa M&E team. Feedback and complaints mechanism is adopted to contribute in solving the issues.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk-01: Interference from armed groups or the Camp management in the works. The likelihood of occurrence is high and its impact is high as well. this risk will be monitored by Binaa field staff in addition to the report received from the security department. Clear agreement with and consent form signed by the civilian management bodies. However, such kind of risks when happen the activities should be held and the incident will be reported to the relevant stakeholders. Risk-02: Access restrictions to the targeted areas active fighting: the likely hood of occurrence is moderate and its impact is high. The risk will be monitored by Binaa field staff in addition to the report received from the security department. Contingency plan in addition to a reasonable exit strategy is prepared. Once this type of risk has happened all works will be stalled in the low-security locations and being prepared to apply either the contingency or exit plan. Risk-03: Floods especially in the winter season. The likely hood of occurrence is high and the impact is high. The risk will be monitored by Binaa field staff in addition to the report received from the internal security department, cluster reports in addition to the other involved parties. Contingency plan in addition to a reasonable exit strategy is prepared. Once this type of risk has happened the all works will be stalled in the low-security locations and being prepared to apply either the contingency or exit plan.
