Project Summary

South Sudan has been prone to various emergencies related both to the consequences of decades of wars and environmental calamities such as floods and drought. These disasters have caused cyclic movements of communities with the government being unable to respond adequately in provision of necessary infrastructures and basic services to communities affected.
The flooding in both Mayendit and Panyijiar has caused food insecurity (IPC 4 as per the FSNMS) and disrupted access to resources creating internally displaced people. UNIDO’s humanitarian response to these emergencies is focused on the health, Nutrition, WASH, FSL and Education sectors. In the counties of Mayendit and Panyijar many schools lack basic learning spaces with pupils using tree shades. Unidor’s humanitarian response in this sector in conjunction with other education partners targets on the provision of Temporal learning spaces, procurement of hygiene materials for learners and teachers, provision of learning materials, training of teachers and school management committees/ PTAs, rehabilitation of WASH facilities, and procurement and distribution of IEC materials on Covid -19. Unidor will continue with capitation grants for girls to enhance enrollment under GESS project, and school feeding programs in conjunction with WFP to enhance enrollment and retention of learners in schools. In addition to these responses the Covid -19 pandemic has added to an immediate need for response as schools have been closed and are due to reopen in April 2021 after closure in 2020. This will necessitate the need to train teachers and learners on appropriate Covid -19 protocols as provided by UNICEF and the Ministry of Health guidelines. The primary school enrollment and the access to education is strongly unequal for girls and boys (the ratio of girls who attend school is 1 to 3 compared to boys). A similar inequality is seen between the Male and female teacher ratios. Unidor has partnered with Windle trust in implementing the GESS project in Panyijiar, Mayendit, Koch and Leer counties. Unidor has also co-implemented the Education cannot wait project with Mercy corps in Panyijiar County. Unidor did undertook out of school mapping project under Global education partnership with Ministry Education in Mayendit, Leer and Panyijiar Counties. During this mapping exercise it was noted that most out of school children are as a result of; distance - children drop out of school majority of them of ages 3-10 years because schools are far from their residential areas and the areas are full of forests and grass and sometimes flooded with water; hunger - lack of feeding programs were identified as the root causes of schools drop out among children; drinking water - lack of boreholes near the schools in some payams. Unidor has also in the past undertaken Education in Emergency project with UNICEF in Panyijiar with minimum standards INEE adapted to provide temporal learning spaces and learning materials distribution as well as training of school management committees/PTAs.

1 - Needs

Unidor together with Windle Trust have been running the GESS project that targets increase in enrollment and retention of girls in schools through the capitation grants. In most of the schools girls enrollment still lags behind that of boys across all the counties. Most of schools infrastructure including classrooms, offices, latrines, etc. have been damaged by the conflicts and flooding and are currently in bad conditions. Many children learn under trees because there were either no classroom or classrooms are in bad condition in most of the schools even prior to the closure in 2020 due to the Covid -19 pandemic.The education activities are supported by volunteer teachers through encouragement of local education authority and community, due to being untrained these teachers capacity to deliver quality learning is therefore wanting and requires continuous support in terms of in service training. Most of schools have no water source, fences and latrines. The schools suffer lack of school supplies and equipment. Children either seat on ground or chairs brought from home. In the past one year the schools conditions have not been assessed as they were all closed due to the Covid -19 and therefore there’s need to undertake an immediate assessment once the schools reopen in April 2021 to take stock of teachers and students who will have not returned since the crisis of the pandemic started and institute measures of mobilization to increase enrollment and ensure there are adequate available teachers since most volunteers do opt to take other jobs to eke a living. Based on observations and information gathered during the implementation of the GESS project, there’s need to undertake rehabilitation and construction of new TLS and rehabilitation of classrooms, provision of schools supplies and schools equipment, improvement school hygiene and sanitation through construction of gender sensitive latrines, handwashing facilities, availability of water sources for schools, incentive payment of teachers, fencing the schools, and provision of capacity building training for teachers. The status of the School Management committee/PTAs is also wanting with need to re-establish credible school management teams through retraining to be able to achieve better community engagement in the running of learning spaces via provision of community support in construction of fences and other infrastructure.

2 - Project activities

In response to the needs above Unidor will in Mayendit , Pariang and Panyijar Counties in Unity State undertake to provide increased access to safe learning spaces that take care of the Covid – 19 impact when schools reopen in April 2021. Unidor will undertake to establish temporary learning spaces in the following 10 primary schools ( Leah, Dongol, Bangkhor, Wuotliem, Wangkoang, Bargok, Buoth, Pantot, Loanglieth, Rieri, ) in Mayendit County where pupils learn under trees. In Panyijar County the following 5 schools (Kuernor, Maluok , Pathiel , Nyoat Island primary school) have pupils learning under trees and Meer Island primary that has IDPs increased enrolment but require additional TLS. In Pariang County the following 5 schools (Aliilang, Agarak, Labang,Yida and Adiei primary schools) that do require TLS to cater for pupils learning under trees. In total 20 Temporary learning Spaces shall be established.Unidor educational team together with relevant county SMoE staff and Education partners will work closely in undertaking assessment missions during events of the cyclic floods, develop response plans according to the data collected, and initiate rehabilitation and provision of 15 temporary learning spaces and the provision of emergency hand washing facilities and gender sensitive latrines. The response will consist of 22 hand washing facilities and 22 gender segregated latrines (14 being constructed and 8 being rehabilitated). The project will preposition and distribute ECD kits to 35 schools targeting 3,500 learners.Unidor will procure and distribute sanitary and hygiene materials for learners in 35 schools.Unidor will also procure and distribute recreational materials ( Footballs, Volleyball and Nets ) for the schools.Unidor will print and distribute IEC materials for Covid -19 prevention measures based on UNICEF and Ministry of Health guidelines.For the soft component of the project, the response intervention will include the training of teachers on EiE pedagogical skills since most of the teachers and volunteer teachers who are unskilled to offer required child center teaching skills. The project targets to train 150 teachers.The teachers will also be trained on PSS and Covid - 19 protection mechanisms to enable them train the pupils on the same. The same group of teachers will be trained by Unidor protection department on GBV protection referral pathways.The project will also train county education authorities and PTA members on the psycho-social support to children affected by emergencies, disaster risk management and lifesaving Covid – 19 responses as per UNICEF guidance for Covid -19 prevention and control in schools. For this reason, a total of at least 23 County Education officials and 175 PTA membersUnidor will mobilize the education authorities and PTAs in the target schools to undertake back to school campaigns in their respective Payams.Unidor will through multi-sector responses in the WASH, Protection, Nutrition and Health departments endeavor to incorporate School feeding program with WFP to ensure increased enrollment and retention for Boys and Girls in this three counties that are in IPC 4 vulnerability. Unidor is implementing TSFP and Food for Assets programs in the nutrition department for Mayendit county in 2021. In this project UNIDOR intends to incorporate the PTAs to undertake the rehabilitation of key school infrastructure like fences and WASH facilities rehabilitation under the Food for Assets program.

3 - Project indicators

Unidor M&E department shall take lead in utilizing available tools to collect and report on the implementation and achievement of the project indicators. Specifically a team comprising of the County Education authorities, Unidor program staff will be focused on measuring the impact of the interventions, in the provision of temporary learning spaces, training of teachers and PTA members and all other related activities in the project. They will evaluate the number of teachers and PTA members who apply the psycho social principles after the trainings, and the number of children who benefit from the protective temporary learning spaces provided. All monitoring reports shall be shared with the State Ministry of Education and the Education Cluster at Juba. The M & E department will undertake monthly activity review as well as quarterly progress review which shall be shared with partners at both county and state level.The data gathered shall be dis-aggregated by gender with analysis of the data thereof including the % change in numbers achieved from the start and the progression in the project cycle so that remedial actions can be taken to achieve desired outcomes at the end of the project. Data gathered will also evaluate the impact of the project on learners with special needs and specifically on how they get involved in decisions in the project that would affect them. The project will also monitor instances of GBV and PSEA policy implementation and ensure there's adequate awareness done to help curb such incidences and that proper mechanisms built within the community to help stop or proper handling of any incident for the victims.The project will be keen to achieve the following indicators;• # of children & youth (3-17yrs) accessing and attending schools• # of temporary learning spaces established (INEESS-MSEE)• # of children (3-5yrs) benefiting from ECD kits• # of school children accessing gender sensitive WASH facilities ( in line with INEE and sphere standards)• # of school children benefiting from rehabilitated gender sensitive WASH facilities• # of learners receiving sanitation and hygiene materials• # of children and youth (6 -17 yrs ) benefiting from recreational material supplies• # of teachers trained on EIE , basic pedagogy and learner centered methodologies• # of teachers & learners trained and updated on the impact of Covid -19 and its protection mechanisms• # of youth and adolescents (14-17 yrs) benefiting from life skills and basic vocational training.• # of teachers and education authorities trained on referral pathways on protection/GBV, Covid -19, health and Nutrition.• # of PTAs and education authorities trained in school management , Psycho-social support and Disaster risk Reduction in Education

7.4.2. How will your project provide beneficiaries information about Protection against Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA)?

UNIDOR has elaborate PSEA policies which are shared by key staff in project locations appointed by the PSEA focal person in field location who reports to the contact Manager in charge of protection department. All new staff are oriented on the PSEA guidelines and sign adherence to the same. At the field level in this project the Field contact staff will during the various trainings undertake community sensitization of the SEA guidelines and reporting mechanisms established.

7.4.4. How will your project ensure feedback from the most vulnerable members (e.g. individuals living with disabilities, individuals who are not literate, women and girls)?

During implementation the M&E team shall hold focus group discussions with individuals in in each category separately and where language barriers exists, a local staff shall be present to ensure robust collection of feedback is undertaken to facilitate in addressing any challenges during the project cycle.

7.4.1. How will your project provide beneficiaries information about the project, feedback mechanism and/or complaint mechanism?

The County Education authorities and PTAs shall be continuously engaged in the project implementation with Unidor field education officers providing complaint mechanisms and the analysis of feedback from the stakeholders being reviewed by the program manager.

7.4.3. How will your project use the beneficiaries’ feedback to adapt project assistance?

During the project monitoring activities Unidor will use Focus group discussions to collect beneficiary feedback as part of the key stakeholder engagements where views on their needs an concerns in implementation strategies will be used to adjust where deliverables need to be re-adjusted to best suit the beneficiaries targeted. In event resources employed in the project are not adequate Unidor will en-devour to bring onboard other funding to provide additional support to gaps highlighted by beneficiaries where possible.

8.11. Mitigation Measures

Due to the fact that some areas schools are a distant from where children stay, those in ECD would have their parents restrict them from going to school hence this risk of violence due to the issues of distance and at times violence shall be mitigated by ensuring PTAs and Education authorities provide alternate sites for ECD classes so that they do not miss out on attendance.The risks of PSEA shall be mitigated by ensuring the beneficiary community being fully sensitized on the Zero tolerance and understanding the field contact person together with location of complaint boxes where issues relating to SEA are to be reported. Lack of awareness on GBV referral pathways shall be mitigated by incorporating the Unidor protection departments staff conducting sensitization to the PTAs on available mechanisms in the county.The Risks of persons with disabilities being not consulted or being excluded are mitigated by ensuring the county education authorities together with the PTAs being sensitized on the need to mobilize and collect data on children with special needs within their jurisdiction that are out of school enabled to enroll via consultations with the parents involved.
