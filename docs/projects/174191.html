Project Summary

The impact of the on-going insurgency attacks on both lives and properties in the north-eastern states of Nigeria has left many in fear, displaced millions of people, while hundreds of thousands have lost their livelihood and are facing serious starvation. The devastating effect of the conflicts on communities across the Northeast has left at least 77% of IDPs with no access to food, water, and protection and in need of urgent life-saving food, WASH and NFI assistance. Vulnerable such as poorest conflict affected households with little or no assets, households with vulnerable members such as pregnant women and children, households with limited livelihood opportunities and female headed households, considering the finite nature of humanitarian, development and government resources. Non-State Armed Groups (NSAG) have escalated their attacks in the northeast of Nigeria and the military counter insurgency operations have also intensified leading to increased population displacement and restricted livelihood activities in north-east Nigeria, where millions of people remain displaced. Most of the affected population are living in host communities while others are residing in camps and are dependent on humanitarian assistance and community support.
During the current analysis period improvements in livelihood activities were reported across the state except for some LGAs affected by conflicts and insurgency (Katsina, Zamfara, Sokoto, Kaduna, Adamawa, Borno and Yobe States). In the projected period, these LGAs and states particularly Borno are likely to resort to emergency coping strategies leading to a further collapse in livelihoods if the ongoing humanitarian action is not sustained (Cadre Harmonize).
Following recent farmers/herders’ clashes and communal clashes in Lamurde and Guyuk LGAs in Adamawa state, there have been alarming request from individuals, community stakeholders and the government for intervention, therefore, Hope and Rural Aid Foundation (HARAF) embarked on a 1-day Rapid Needs Assessment. Based on severity and urgency, few communities were selected for the assessment among several others in the Lamurde and Guyuk LGA. The assessment was undertaken on the 3rd September 2020 specifically to establish the severity of need, to identify and prioritize humanitarian needs.
The clashes between herders (Fulani tribesmen) and farmers (Bachama Tribesmen and Lunguda Tribesmen) in Lamurde and Guyuk LGAs, and communal clashes between Waja and Lunguda in Guyuk LGA, left houses, animals, assets, livelihood source, food and farms produce looted, damaged and destroyed. Several people have been displaced as a result of the insurgence. Communities highlighted the following priority needs: food and livelihood assistance, shelter and non-food items, medicines, education, WASH and GBV. All the three communities assessed reported a dire decrease in food consumption as a result of loss of food stocks and livelihoods source. Many has resulted to negative coping mechanisms, such as skipping meal (eat ones a day), reducing the quality of meals eaten, borrowing food from a neighboring community, selling productive assets and begging for food.
With the recent inception of Covid-19 in Nigeria which recorded its first case on 27th February 2020 in Lagos, there have been a lot of measures that were put in place by the Federal and some State Governments to mitigate the spread of the virus. Lockdown measures were enforced had a devastating effect on people’s food security and livelihoods.
- The project will identify and target vulnerable populations and Households with no sources of income and livelihoods.
- To contribute in restoring the livelihoods and food security of affected communities living in North-Eastern Nigeria through increased engagement in communal services, production and income mainly generated from crop, livestock and Income Generating activities small businesses.
- Provision of conditional cash transfer to beneficiaries

1 - Needs

The project will identify and target vulnerable populations and Households with no sources of income and livelihoods. To Train, restore, and provide several means of livelihood for the vulnerable population in selected LGAs in the northeast. Most of the HHs in rural communities engage in Agricultural, Skilled, and Unskilled labor as the source of their livelihood. Due to the prolonged crisis in the Northeast, many of these people have lost their livelihood source. Even after returning to their communities, they are finding it very hard to survive without a livelihood source. Hence, many engage in negative coping strategies. The project seeks to intervene in Agricultural and Skilled livelihood sources. This proposed project is justified to increase the livelihoods of HHs and food and nutrition security to those who remain in absolute crisis and in need of regular and adequate food supplies. In line with the current situation, this project seeks to identify the most vulnerable HHs with a focus on women, providing them with sustainable Income Generation Opportunities through training, mentoring, and provision of start-up kits. The capacity building component will focus on building financial literacy and good use and maintenance of livelihood inputs provided to the targeted beneficiaries.
