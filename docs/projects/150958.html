Project Name

Start Date

01/01/2019

End Date

31/12/2019

Project Summary

The proposed project through static and mobile clinics will provide lifesaving essential services to create more access to Nutrition services at community level. The project will provide nutrition services including anthropometric screening for identification of all children under the age of five years and pregnant and lactating women with malnutrition. SAM cases without complication and with good apatite will be enrolled into the OTP program services, while malnourished children and PLW with complications will be referred appropriately. Breast feeding and health seeking behaviour will be promoted. The project will strengthen community referral and diseases surveillance mechanism through community health workers and provide health education and hygiene promotion as well. The project will target women, men, girls and boys from IDPs and vulnerable host communities. In Yaqshiid and Kahda districts.In order to control further disease spread and reduce mortality and morbidity rates in Yaqsheed and Kahda districts, New ways will implement an essential and basic nutrition package (BNSP) including an emergency nutrition intervention and immunisation, targeting <5 yrs children and PLWs in the two districts that are affected by post drought’s sequel. In order to facilitate access to sustainable basic services and improve response to the communities as well as reduce case fatality rates, new ways will provide IDP, returnees as well as vulnerable host communities equitable access to quality integrated nutrition service.

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

One the main obstacles which may hinder IDP and marginalized communities their access to the facilities, is the lack of participation of the IDPs including from marginalized communities in the design, implementation, monitoring and evaluation of the project. New ways organization will facilitate systematic participation of representatives of marginalized IDP communities in consultation meetings with local leaders, conditioning the implementation of the project on equal and direct access with all communities, and establishing direct contact with leaders of IDPs from marginalized communities, outside camps if necessary.

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

NWO is committed to ensure that programs and activities are gender sensitive and take into consideration the gender needs of its target communities and the unique needs and challenges of men, women, boys and girls. By applying humanitarian all humanitarian principles including equality and neutrality, gender imbalance will be avoided. NWO will serve equally men, women, boys and girls during the project life time in Kahda and Yaqshiid districts. NWO adopted a strategy to incorporate the view, opinions and needs of all stakeholders in order to address existing gaps. Through this process NWO identifies priorities and needs for men, women, boys and girls targeted by this project in terms of integrated nutrition services, as well as informing them the existence of these services through CHW mobilization campaign. The project will reach out to elderly people and to those community members with disabilities by establishing mobile teams. Nutrition Workers will ensure that nutrition needs of women, men, girls and boys are taken into consideration. IYCF promotion will support pregnant and lactating women as well husbands and men and those who influence IYCF behavior, such as grandmothers and mothers in low, so that they contribute to improving practices and changing behaviors. Men will be encouraged to play a role in IYCF promotion.

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

NWO will pay particular attention to the risk of violence and sexual abuse related to armed conflict in conflict zones. New ways, with the establishment of a mobile team, will ensure that the hard-to-reach population has access to services and is not trapped in the midst of active conflict or is subjected to violence and abuse on their way to a fixed service site. New ways will make sure that local authorities, elders, and religious leaders to be part of the project planning, all project’s implementation process as well as monitoring and evaluation of the project’s impact, so to create community’s ownership of the project. on the other hand, NW made sure that the selection of project sites was based on security
