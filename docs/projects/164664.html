Project Summary

The project will address community needs in camps in Jarablus, Aleppo, and Al-Dana camps in Idlib. The needs emerging for integrated protection interventions, women protection &empowerment interventions.
This project is designed on a community-based approach by involving beneficiaries in the design phase, involving communities in awareness campaigns for IDPs in camps, empower women to start up their small businesses to serve and address the needs of their communities. The total direct beneficiaries of the project are 1400 women and girls.
Coordination is already established with the camps managements based on MoU already signed between POINT and Turkish and Syrian local authorities in Jarablus. Further coordination will be with NGOs, CBOs working in the camp; Coordination is also already established with the ministry of development and humanitarian affairs for Al-Dana.
The project is drawn according to our 5 years expertise working in camps, empowering women and youth, developing protection service providers and humanitarian workers quality and impact, and enhance women skills to manage women centers and lead activities in
Sarmada camps. POINT has established 40 women committees in the targeted 40 camps in Dana based on community participation and volunteering approach. The committees were led by women.
The project aims are:
1. Providing protection services of PSS, GBV and referral pathways for women and women with disabilities. And the main purpose of these activities is to support them restore their balance psychologically and mentally, and help GBV survivors overcome trauma impacts.
2. Equipping women and women with a disability with vocational/life skills, and starting up small business training and mini-grants to implement those businesses, in order to restore and enhance their dignity by positioning their selves positively in the community as an active member, and decreasing their dependency on their husbands or relatives.
3. Participating in declining child labor by decreasing women's reliance on their children as major or minor income sources.
4. Raising community awareness of women, women with disabilities, and young girls on several topics such as parenting skills principles, early marriages, women's rights, and many more to increase their resilience.
5. making protection monitoring for each camp
All of that will be achieved by:
a. Hire GBV PSS referral pathways specialists for the 2 women centers. and refer to health and nutrition cases to related sector service providers.
b. Conduct awareness sessions led by women, women with disabilities, and young girls on PSS and GBV.
c. Make 10 protection monitoring surveys every month in each location.
d. Provide vocational/skills training for women, women with disabilities, and young girls in the two centers
e. Provide women, women with disabilities, and young girls based with support and information about starting their small business based on a curriculum approved by International Labor Organization ILO
f. Award 100 women with mini-grants and follow up, coaching sessions to implement and sustain their established small businesses.

4 - Needs

Preliminary Assessment Findings for Humanitarian Needs Overview Voices 2019 demonstrates that GBV continues to pervade the lives of women and girls, particularly adolescent girls’ sexual violence and harassment including technology, and reaffirmed domestic violence including family violence against women and girls and early/forced marriage. In Aleppo, GBV results in repression, lack of education, solitude, doing house chores only, depression, poor employment opportunities. 7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Protection risk: There is a risk that the number of women engaged in the projects is less than targeted. Likelihood: Medium. Impact: High Mitigating measures: 1. Ensure our community outreach workers mobilize project communities and explained the effects of planned activities. 2. increase coordination with camp management, local authorities, other CBOs, NGOs working in the same locations to call women to participate.Protection risk: military clashes within AL-Dana preventing the center from operating. Mitigation measures: 1. Suspend activities till peace is recovered. 2. coordinate with POINT programs to resume providing services in nearby safe areas once safe is taking a long time to be restored POINT has experience in successfully delivering various kinds of the project despite the ongoing conflict. Additionally, we have designed security plans for the team and participants according to different scenarios. Our primary priority is the safety and security of the beneficiaries; therefore, we will conduct safety and security assessments prior to implementation and update the safety plans accordingly. And accessible accountability mechanism will be used in the project, known communication lines as well. Do no harm Standards will be applied at all stages of implementation and the project’s life-cycle. In order to do that, POINT will conduct baseline assessments to ensure that none of the project activities will harm the community in any way. Additionally, POINT will conduct security risk assessments and safety and security reports in order to ensure the safety and security of project beneficiaries.
