Project Summary

OCHA promotes and facilitates effective, principled and gender-appropriate humanitarian action in South Sudan in response to widespread and persistent humanitarian needs. OCHA will continue to play a fundamental role in building a common ground and a shared understanding of what is needed to work together to serve the people of South Sudan. As such, and through dedicated support to the HC and HCT, the South Sudan office is focused on responding to the current humanitarian crisis by 1) Strengthening strategic coordination in Juba among the main stakeholders including the Government, donors, UN agencies, non-governmental and international organizations, in order to better respond to the humanitarian crisis; 2) Strengthening operational coordination at the cluster and state levels to optimize the humanitarian response to people in need; 3) Improving humanitarian access and space through monitoring and advocacy efforts informed by access incident data and best practices.

Needs

Since the beginning of the crisis in 2013, the humanitarian situation in South Sudan has steadily deteriorated due to continued conflict, poor economic situation, dire food insecurity, and an alarming health situation. An estimated 7 million people are in need of humanitarian assistance with more than 4 million people displaced. About 300 humanitarian actors are operating in South Sudan including UN agencies, NGOs and government authorities.

Activities

OCHA will continue to support and facilitate key coordination mechanisms, provide evidence-based situational analysis, an effective humanitarian financing system and support meaningful action for affected people. Key activities include:- Chairing the following coordination mechanisms: Humanitarian Coordination Forum, Humanitarian High-level Oversight Committee, Humanitarian Country Team, Inter-Cluster Working Group, Operational Working Group, Information Management Working Group, Civil-Military Coordination Group, Access Working Group, Needs Analysis Working Group and South Sudan Humanitarian Fund Advisory Board. - Production of high quality Humanitarian Needs Overview and an optimally prioritized Humanitarian Response Plan- Synthesize and continue to refine qualitative and quantitative data (through effective management of data bases) to produce a range of information products to support decision makers- Support the acquisition and best utilization of humanitarian funding, including through the CERF and SSHF for crisis situations- Provide strategic support and ongoing advice and support to the HC- Support timely assessment and response in hard-to-reach areas, including on cross-cutting issues such as gender, protection and AAP
