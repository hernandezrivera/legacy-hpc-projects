Project Summary

The aim of this project is to improve the general health status and health awareness of FDMNs (Forcebly Displaced Myanmar Nationals) and bangladeshi host communities, including maternal and child health. Activities will include providing primary health care support through established health post, improving knowledge amount FDMNs in relation to personal hygiene, family planning and nutrition, and to improve nutritional needs of pregnant mothers and malnourished children through advice on nutrition and assistant on nutritional suppliment.

Objectives

1. Enable easy access to primary health care services, and access essential maternity care with ANC/PNC and 24 x7 safe delivery2. Improve knowledge among FDMNs in relation to personal hygiene, family planning and nutrition3. Improve nutritional needs of pregnant mothers and malnourished children

Needs

Both Rohingya refugee communities and Bangladeshi host communities indicates that health is one of their major concern. There shall be adequate medical treatment for all, with improved access to medical facilities. The limited availability of sexual and reprouctive health services and lack of knowledge on sexual and reprouctive health also needs to be addressed. Peace Winds Japan, together with its local partner Dhaka Community Hospital Trust will address those needs by providing primary health care services to the refugee population, as well as to the host communities through its established health post. The project will also address the needs of the mothers, new-born babies and their family members through its assistance on mother and child health care services, and well as by providing health awareness campaigns.

Activities or outputs

1. Provide primary health care support, maternity health care indlucing safe delivery, Antenatal care (ANC) and postnatal care (PNC) through health post in Camp 14 (Health Facility ID no. 501)2. Provide nutritional advice and suppliments for pregnant mothers and malnourished children3. Conduct public health campaign to increase health awareness on personal hygiene, family planning and nutritional education
