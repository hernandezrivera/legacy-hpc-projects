Project Summary

The project is multi sectoral combining the protection and ERL and a comprehensive and inclusive initiative centered on promoting peaceful coexistence, enhancing local programs and institutions, working in parallel with initiatives in three different areas controlled by different de-facto authorities, targeting a diverse range of beneficiaries across various demographic groups, impeding community cohesion, local perspectives of the early recovery and transitional justice in the protection framework to achieve community resilience and making the gender, inclusion and accountability for affected population intersectional cross cutting concepts. It will encompass support for local initiatives in crucial domains, including PSEAH, GBV, the rule of law and transitional justice. The is a holistic and inclusive initiative aimed at fostering peaceful coexistence, strengthening local initiatives, and benefiting a diverse range of beneficiaries across various demographic groups.

The project will prioritize the capacity building and institutionalization of 30 local initiatives operating in the targeted areas of Azaz, Afrin, and Idleb. This process will involve providing training, technical assistance, and resources to these initiatives, strengthening their ability to carry out their respective missions effectively. After a long-term capacity building stage, there will be a transparent and competitive call for applications, where a committee of (local figures, similar organizations, and EE) will assess the proposals and select six local initiatives from the initial 30 and provide them with sub-grants with 7.000 USD to implement the initiatives with the technical support and follow up of EE. This strategic approach ensures that the most capable and impactful initiatives receive additional support, enhancing their reach and impact on social cohesion and peacebuilding. The project seeks to raise the community awareness of peaceful coexistence and decision making of locals and localize the efforts for social cohesion and peacebuilding, recognizing that sustainable peace is best achieved when driven by and tailored to the specific needs and dynamics of the local communities. By enhancing the capacity of local initiatives, sub-granting to the most effective ones, and focusing on protection and inclusivity, this project aspires to create a more peaceful, cohesive, and resilient community in Azaz, Afrin, and Idleb. Moreover, in all the project phases, some main concerns will be addressed which are: religious Minorities (Druze and Christians) in Idleb province, ethnic minorities (Kurds) In Afrin and displaced population across Idleb, Azaz, and Afrin.
