Project Summary

The proposed one year project will be implemented in Ulang and Nasir counities in Upper Nile State and Pochalla County in Jonglie State targeting 25,500 direct beneficiaries and the breakdown by the target population will be IPDS 35% (8,925), Returnees 25% (6375) and host community 40% (10,200). the beneficiaries breakdown by ages will be Men 20% (5100), Women 30% (7650), Boys 20% (5100) and Girls 30% (7650) affected by the crisis due to fighting, abduct children, raid cattle and presently the communities are very poor culminating into problems of unsafe water, poor hygiene and sanitation. UNKEA intends to provide WASH inputs to IDPs, returnees and host community including disabled, women headed households and gender based violence (GBV) survivors and Malnourished in Ulang, Nasir and Pochalla Counties; Upper Nile and Jonglie State and implementing technical guidance training to vulnerable communities.
UNKEA has been implementing WASH activities in Nassir County as well as Ulang County in Riang Payam and this project will be implemented in Ulang Town Yomding, Riang and Makak payams of Ulang County in Upper Nile, Dinkar, Jikmir, Kieckuon, Kuerenge-ke, Roam, Mading of Nasir county in upper Nile State and Adongo, Akiela, Burator, Omiela, Pochalla Town of Pochalla County in Jonglie State.
UNKEA will carry out the following activities; mobilization and sensitization of community through dialogue in the project area to create awareness of what project activities to be implemented, time frame and importance of the project to the community, identification and registration of beneficiaries,
The community where this project will be implemented will be identified through consultation with the local authorities and other stake holders on ground on the most vulnerable groups (IDPS, Women headed Household, child headed household, disabled, gender based violence (GBV) survivors and chronically sick) who will be prioritized in the selection process.

Needs

Ulang County in Upper Nile State hosted numerous IDPs 35,000 (IRNA Report) displaced from Nasir County and other nearby Counties due to the War that broke out in 2013 and recent fighting in 2016 July. This increased the population of the existing 98,935 people from the Host communities (ROSS report). A KAP Survey done by Goal Ireland from 7-14/3/2016 showed that 74.4% of the population in Ulang during the rainy season access unsafe water with only 25.6% of the population accessing safe water. In the same KAPs Survey, 97% of the population does not have access to Latrine with 22.7% of the population with knowledge on critical hand washing and 35.5% reached with Hygiene and sanitation messages in the last three months. The current crises have worsened the already dire humanitarian situation in Ulang County. Since the 2013 crises, WASH service delivery systems remained weak. UNKEA believes that the WASH situation would even worst if no actions are taken. This project will be implemented in Yomding, Riang, Makak and Ulang Town. According to an assessment conducted by UNKEA in May 2018 in Pochalla County of Jonglie State showed the there are no household latrines, while the available public latrines have collapsed therefore people defecate in the open as a mean of excreta disposal although this is a common practice in these communities. In the same assessment findings, there are only 5 functional boreholes one in Pochalla South, one in Pochalla North and three in Pochalla town (Center) while 18 hand pumps are broken down therefore more than 500 people use one hand pump beyond the sphere standard while others drink directly from the river. Although hygiene messages are passed to the community, the hygiene practices like hand washing is not practiced due to lack of hand washing facilities, also many households prefer the use of aqua tab for water treatment to boiling. Accountability to affected population
Does the project provide beneficiaries with information?

Yes

How does the project provide beneficiaries with information?

The program will use community participatory approaches. There will be involvement of beneficiaries in program activities to ensure “ownership” by the community, which will guarantee continuity and accountability. The program will continue to use community agents trained as volunteers for water facilities, hygiene and sanitation. This will help in building local human capital and expertise, which will be able to continue program activities beyond its funding life. This approach has approved a great success in the implementation of the various program of UNKEA. There will be community dialogue through meetings every month with community leaders, this is to provide feedback to the community about the progress of the project and also find out the complains from the community and how it can be addressed.

Are messages context-specific or/and tailored?

Yes

Briefly describe how

The project will promote women and Men participation in the implementation process; it will focus on bringing more skilled women in the staffing and will encourage male involvement in the promotion of health education and promotion of good hygiene practices and use of clean water practices for the school going children in Nasir and Ulang in Upper Nile State and Pochalla in Jonglie. It will ensure that women are given equal say in decision making. Boys and girls will be given equal opportunity in joining the wash campaign. The data will be segregated in to Women and Men, boys and girls respectively. UNKEA will involve women and girls during the hygiene and sanitation promotion activities thus promoting hygiene at House hold level and personal hygiene and sanitation at households. During the project implementation cycle UNKEA will target men and women during the maintenance of the water systems in the respective counties. During mid-project assessment, UNKEA will involve both men and women in the assessment

Feedback /complaint mechanisms?

Yes

Briefly describe the mechanisms

The project has two layers of complain mechanisms:First layer is the project steering committee that directly handles the complaints desk in the regular undertaking of project activities. They are comprised of beneficiary representatives constitution of reprentatives of IDPs, Returnees, host communities, women leaders, religious leaders, and local authority. The second tier is the Payam level oversight committee that does not handle any activities but observe and receive complains on all actors including on project staff, steering committee members, and all actors in project execution. The oversight committee reports directly to Juba based senior manager who engages with the field on quarterly basis

Protection Risk Analysis?

Yes

Briefly describe how

In the proposed project locations, possible tensions of access to limited resources between IDPs and host communities have been factored in the project engagements with key stakeholders whom UNKEA is already currently engaging through other projects in the county. Past experience shows that when prior engaged, local authorities are willing to protect IDP populations to access resources. The evidence of similarities in ethnic backgrounds between IDPs and host communities have also played a vital factor in easing tensions that could otherwise emerge from competition for limited resources. Protection risk Analysis which are specific in the needs of different populations have been factored through conflict analysis and capacity building shall be made to field staff during project implementation.

Addresses inequalities or protection threats?

Yes

Briefly describe how

Numerous threats confront targeted population: the threat of cholera and water borne disease, the threat of GBV from collecting water in distant locations, among others. The project will provide access to clean water within the catchment distance acceptable by SPHERE standards. The project will address sanitation risks by emphasizing on clean environment through appropriate waste disposals and ensuring latrines are available. Girls and women will be protected by ensuring that public facilities like latrines are labeled Female and Male. The beneficiaries will be treated with dignity and impartially. Informed consent will be required in any data collection and all information gathered will be treated with confidentiality. UNKEA will coordinate with other sectors like the protection cluster to obtain guidelines regarding principles of protection in humanitarian context.The community will be sensitized on the protection of the rights of vulnerable groups such as women and children. Children will not be employed for this project during the project implementation.

Does the project monitoring measure access of beneficiaries?

Yes

How does the project monitoring measure access of beneficiaries?

The project will monitor beneficiary access through the following parameters:Distance covered to obtain clean water by beneficiary householdsNumber of persons who use sanitation facilities including latrinesProximity of training venues to participants and spread of water pump mechanics to service broken down boreholes, among others;The WASH Officer and monitoring and evaluation team shall directly monitor project activities to ensure the above parameters are met and fulfilled in all project locations. Tools shall incorporate the above measuremetns for project tracking. The WASH officer who is the direct supervisor of Volunteer and will ensure daily supervision of WASH promotersThe Wash manager will conduct regular supervisory visits to the field; he will check for consistency in the use of reports, ensure data is collected using the standard data collection tools and performs data quality audits. She will ensure that WASH supplies are delivered and proposition, staffing gaps, reporting gaps (Data collection tools and guidelines) and financial gaps and will report accordingly. The Wash Manager will conduct quarterly monitoring visits and will participate in the quarterly programme supervision together with the community leaders, staffing, reports and monitor actions taken by the WASH Officer in regards to the project implementation and results. The executive director will conduct biannual supportive supervision visits to all project sites. He will monitor the performance of all project inputs, activities and outputs. He will provide overall support and feedback to the project management team. Reporting, Data will be collected using the UNKEA collection and reporting tools. This data will be summarized 5WS reporting tools and sent to the WASH ClusterThe Wash Officer is responsible for reporting to UNKEA Juba, he will ensure data is collected and reported in a timely manner (Weekly, monthly). He will share his reports with the WASH manager who will consolidate all reports per reporting site and shares it with the monitoring and evaluation manager. The Monitoring and evaluation Manager will check the reports for consistency, and completeness and will share the reports with the WASH cluster. In addition, feedback from the Cluster will be shared during supervision visits.
