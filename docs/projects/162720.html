Project Summary

This project is designed, to contribute in sustainable, equitable, affordable access to safe drinking water, sanitation facilities and hygiene services for IDPs, returnees and vulnerable host communities in Talodi, Umm Durein, Abu-Khersola and Eleri locality in South Kordofan state. The main project activities include
- Safe Drinking Water: Operation and maintenance of 20 hand pumps, upgrading 2 hand pumps into mini water yards powered with solar system, conduct water quality testing for hand pump in use and new installation, establishment and training of 6 water user committees and re activate water tariff, train 30 mechanics in operation & maintenance of water sources, and provision of 7 repair tool kits, conduct awareness sessions on household water treatment and safe storage, use chlorine or heating for targeted HHs, distribution of water storage facilities and chlorine under WES supervision.
- Safe and friendly Sanitation facilities: We will support in rehabilitation and maintenance of latrines, subsidize CATs, and implement CLTS to reduce effects of open defecation and latrines ownership, and construction of 300 households’ latrines while considering the needs of person with disability, elderly and pregnant women.
- Hygiene practice: This will include training of hygiene Promoters, use of PHAST and CHAST, organize hygiene awareness sessions (including menstrual hygiene awareness targeted women and girl in reproductive age), organize of 50 hand washing session supported with ICE materials, provision of 500 hand washing facilities, and distribution of soap.
ASSIST will set a benchmark to collect, analyse and use sex-disaggregated data, 53% of staff will be women, 60% of staff will complete basic gender training and learning sessions, and 100% of the project reports will have specific gender results and lessons learned.
Secondly, we will ensure that 100% of the project activities have resource allocation to achieve IASC Gender with Age marker code 4(M) by the end of 2020. ASSIST will also have financial tracking mechanism to quantify funds disbursement for promoting gender and women empowerment.
15% of the project grant will be cash based disbursement to project beneficiaries as Unconditional and unrestricted transfers as it gives the beneficiaries chance to chose and prioritize their needs.
Will ensure functioning complaints and feedback mechanisms as an accountability to affected beneficiaries who receive assistance or participate in the project implementation, gender and age dimensions are integrated into 86 percent of Country Strategic Plan, and in 63 percent of ASSIST guidance manuals.
In project designing, risk of gender based violence is considered and incorporated through Separate latrines for men and women, girls and boys, women’s and girls’ latrines in safe location, as agreed with women and girls, locks on inside of toilet doors, water points in safe and accessible locations as agreed with women and girls- as they collect water), introducing strategies to reduce queuing for long duration. In this case most appropriate place for women and children should be considered as the site of water points due to the fact that women have the main role of fetching water. Proactive women participation enables the reduction of the risk for gender based violence (GBV) related to water activities.
project will mitigate of pollution through control disposal in open spaces near water sources, use cement open dumping to burning waste to an average of above 100 meters from HHs and water points, also to avoid ground water contamination, ASSIST will locate latrines downstream of water-wells, at least 30m from groundwater sources and at least 2.5m above the water table and fitting pit latrines.

1 - Needs

Based on inter-agency assessment carried in Talodi, Abu-khersola and El liri locality conducted in June 2019, and Oct 2018 respectively assessed WASH services among IDPs, Returnees and Vulnerable host communities and the findings Only 45% of total WASH needs are covered, most villages in the 3 locality have little access to safe drinking water in summer most of the shallow water sources dry up. Water contamination with cyanide as most of ground water passes through caves systems in this mining area.So, there is need for geological and hydro geological assessment studies, identification of drilling specifications, Water quality monitoring and testing, water testing using WAGTECH to perform in sit analysis of microbiological and physic-chemical water quality tests , waste and groundwater, geophysical survey, Operation and maintenance (O&M), conduct sanitary survey, support to improve sanitation facilities and hygiene practices. In Toladi locality shows that 6 water yards, 1 Mini yard, and 6 Hand pumps are functioning covers 45% of total need of the population with a gap of 55% due to non-functional water facilities that includes 2 yards, 1 Mini and 36 hand pumps that needs operation and Maintenance. In Abu-Kershola 12 out of 14 mini water yards and 57 hand pumps out of 119 are no longer functioning, most water sources are open wells, unprotected, insufficient and is of poor quality with contaminated with waste household latrines have a similar depth, as well as conflict over access to water sources were reported.In El Leri locality there are 12 water yards (WYs) of which three are not functioning, 120 HPs, 70 are not working and only 40 can be rehabilitated. The population mainly depend on 13 Haffirs as the main source of water although its unsafe to drink. no water user committees are available which resulted to misuse of water facilities. During the summer water gap increased due to drying up of most of the water ponds where IDPs, returnees and vulnerable host communities depend on during the rainy season. While in all the 3 localities, Water related diseases such as diarrhea, and UTI due to contaminated water, lack of treatment and chlorination, trained staff and tools for water quality testing even the commissioned 9 boreholes that were drilled by gold mining company are not yet tested and or equipped. In general, the existing water sources have color, turbid and bad odor. Open defecation is widely practiced among the IDPs, returnees and vulnerable host communities, poor solid waste management which have contributed to increased water borne diseases in Toladi, Alliri and Abu-Khersola. The mismanagement of Solid waste collection and disposal with no mechanism established which provided fertile breading ground for flies. It was also noticed that there is no regular community mobilization for hygiene practice. Community needs to be educated on proper sanitation and hygiene promotion.The team also observed poor water storage capacity of water at HH level; water stored in dirty jerry cans which make it unsafe to drink, beside poor personal hygiene especial among the children. The entire target beneficiaries’ awareness on personal hygiene promotion is very limited, communities need hygiene awareness and trainings with supporting IEC materials.There is need to set a benchmark to collect, analyse and use sex-disaggregated data who will benefit/use safe drinking water, sanitation facilities and have improve personal hygiene practices, considering percentage of staff that are women to support in mobilization and reach out, secondly, there need to know number of staff that completed basic gender training, % of Resource allocation; and measuring IASC Gender and Age marker to achieve code 4(M) while ensuring financial tracking mechanism to quantify funds disbursement for promoting gender and women empowerment.
