Project Summary

The project provides direct support to 12,000 out of school or at risk of being out of school children with opportunity to access education in south Kordofan and Central Darfur. The project specifically targets vulnerable populations, including girls, IDPs, and children affected by the conflict and the recent economic crisis.
Labena will directly implement the project in close partnership with Ministry of Education to ensure that the quality and delivery of education services are consistent with MoE policies, protocols and strategies, and coordinate with the MoE for training and monitoring of activities. Partner agencies participate in planning and implementing programmes, contributing more as their experience and knowledge has increased. The implementation approach adopted by Labena is meant to enhance capacity of local actors such as community structures (PTAs), local NGO and government stakeholders on provision and effective maintenance of emergence basic primary education facilities. There is extensive involvement of community members and use of local resources such as labour during construction of semi-permanent classroom, shelters and semi-permanent offices. Local leadership will play a critical role in the identification of PTA members and well as organizing community members who will contribute with labour and other local resources. The PTA community working with local leadership will also identify vulnerable girl children that will benefit from school uniform distributions. Labena will also promote environmentally sustainable activities such as promoting live fencing and tree planting. Labena ensures that it does not run parallel activities to MoH and the project will also promote use of live fencing and tree planting in school.

Activities

The project will ensure that teachers training will be delivered to a reasonable balance of male and female teachers as already agreed up on between Labena and the Ministry of Education authorities. Trainings will be tailored to address the gender equality dimension of emergency education and learning environment for teachers. The project will utilize environmentally friendly local materials for temporary construction of classrooms, while tree planting around schools for live fencing is expected to positively contribute to the local environment. . Conduct gender responsive EiE and core subjects training for 200 teachers (100 female and 100 male).2. Construction 200 temporary learning centers, 100 in each project area. 3. Provision of classroom furniture, and seating chairs, plastic mats, blackboards in all project areas for 200 classrooms targeting 10.000 students. 4. Provision of school textbooks, exercise books, school supplies 1200 students.5. Provision of teaching aids and equipment for teachers. 6. Activation of school child clubs/ societies (environmental and hygiene society, sport, and couture societies) through outdoors activities. 7. Construction of 40 gender sensitive school latrines, 20 in each project area, targeting 12.000 students.8. Planting of trees for school fences in schools in all project areas. 9. Training of 200 PTAs, 100 PTAs in each project area. PTAs will be trained in school management, Education In Emergencies and gender equality in education.
