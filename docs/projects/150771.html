Project Summary

To enhance the resilience of 125 vulnerable families (600 capita); including 167 female, 173 male, 260 children, 298 adult , 42 elderly and 81 refugees through improving the food security of vulnerable farmers through land rehabilitation 6 locations located in Jerusalem governorate on area C . The proposed project addressed directly to the cluster strategic objective of the 2019 humanitarian response plan (part of 2108-2020 HRP). Where “the resilience , food security and the productive capacity of vulnerable households are restored/enhanced livelihoods . The planned interventions are directly response to the need of improving the access for basic services by vulnerable Palestinians, which is in this project is food (rehabilitation of 500 dunums of lands at household level. The project was developed in a participatory approach at community levels (October 2017 and updated assessment April 2018 coordinated by the food security thematic group, and lastly during october 2018 by PARC staff), and coordination and consultation with key stakeholders at institutional level (working NGOs and related govemental ministries). The targeted areas are characterized as a very remote areas with weak services As it is mention on the Strategic objectives, Palestinians are vulnerable to rights violations, especially in relation to their livelihood, with which they struggle due to the prolonged crisis of the OPT. One of the main concerns of the Palestinian population is the impact of protection threats and violations on livelihoods, due to the fact that these communities rely heavily on agriculture and livestock as their main source of income. The inability to access land due to lack of access and resources means that the infrastructure to facilitate the crop is deteriorated or non existent, thus causing many lands to be rehabilitated in order to be re cultivated

Needs

The Palestinian agricultural sector plays an essential role in the economy and food security of Palestine and in the livelihood of its people. One of the biggest impacts in this year 2018 has been the risk of confiscation of agricultural lands by Israel. Farmers need enhancement of the resilience and support to rehabilitate many lands in order to avoid the risk to be confiscated. One of the main factors that differentially affects women is linked to restrictions on access to property, particularly land. Women, by law, have the right to receive 50% of the inheritance that a man receives, this being a law that is already discriminatory in itself. However, in many cases due to social pressure, tradition and custom, women reject this part of their inheritance. This practice is especially problematic if we take into account that households headed by women have higher rates of food insecurity. On the other hand, as far as employment is concerned, women in the West Bank constitute only around 17% of the active population. To this must be added that 5% are dedicated in the informal sector and that 60.2% occupy unsafe, unstable and precarious jobs. The process to identify the specific needs and locations and to coordinate:Firstly, based on the organization experience and information, in addition to the information provided by the Ministry of Agriculture, Jerusalem Governorate has been identified to be targeted in the project proposal for the high need for rehabilitation. Locations that meet the following criteria have been proposed to be targeted in the project: suffer from the surrounding Israeli settlements, separation wall, highly exposed to natural disasters specially the floods that destruct the agricultural roads and lands and locations with small agricultural holdings, lands with a difficult access and lands exposed to destruction by wild pigs and livestock. Then, on the 10th of September 2018, a meeting was held with Jerusalem Ministry of Agriculture where the most needed locations were identified, allowing PARC team to visit these locations identify the specific interventions. During these visits, PARC met with the local councils, farmer’s committees and the cooperatives to update and verify the information, needs about the agricultural areas that can be rehabilitated, during these meetings and visits we have noticed that there is more than of 1000 dunums needs to be rehabilitated, according to the most needed lands we have selected 500 Dunums for this intervention. On the 20th of September 2018, a workshop was conducted to talk about the needs of the farmers, and where the local councils, 15 representatives of Pioneer farmers at the location, Farmers committee and Cooperatives participated. The results previously identify were discussed, being detected the following needs as the main ones: lack of material resources for rehabilitation of agricultural lands, damage to agricultural land due to winter floods, the spread of wild pigs and livestock that destroy agricultural crops, grazing at the agricultural lands, regular harassment by the occupation and settlers and the difficulty of access to agricultural lands. In addition, one of the common needs was the high unemployment rate they face. In the specific needs for women, they mention the particular needs they have in relation with land as mention before. Since the most crucial economic indicators stubbornly high, unemployment rate in Palestine is 26.6% with particular concentration among youth (43%) ,23% among male and 66% among female (PCBS ,2016). Low female participation in the labor market results in great loss of economic potential. The rate of Palestinian working women is the lowest in the region, despite being the most educated. Employment of women is concentrated in services (57%, mostly in public administration, education and health sector) and in agriculture (20.9% often as unpaid family helpers) (PCPS, 2015).

Activities

In relation to the preparation of the project, during the first phase of identification, PARC was involved in the coordination mechanism promoted by the FSS. In addition, PARC organized different workshops with the communities in order to coordinate and to focus on their main needs. Due to that, PARC will be able to achieve the third objective: FSS activities are planned and implemented in a coordinated manner, and partnership with key national ministries is strengthened. Finally, the needed assessments will be done in order to start implementing the different activities. After this analysis, PARC will be able to know in which stage is, for example, the different lands that will be rehabilitated and which one are the specific activities to work on. The selection criteria that will be used will take into consideration different socio economic data, also, the criteria will prioritize women and the particular needs: lands that are close to settlements and the separation wall, level of incurred damage of the infrastructure, level of food insecurity, priority to female -headed households (The percentage of female-headed households was 11.7% in the West Bank), priority to lands owned by women, number of members in the family disaggregated or level of incomes of the families. In order to target the most vulnerable farmers, application forms will be filled up by the farmers including different data such as average income, land location, owned land area, exposed to natural/man made disaster or water prices for irrigation. Also, Tenders for the needed materials will be submitted and timetable and action plans will be prepared, The process of tendering purchasing and contracting is done in accordance with PARC’s protocols and procurements rules and regulations. Prior to any project implementation, PARC's will strictly coordinate the final definition and selection of localities and beneficiaries with related ministries offices, and with any other organization working in the same area and sector of activities.The selection process in general will carried out with transparency and efficiency throughout the project. Clear criteria will be used, while taking into consideration other issues like gender and geographical distribution. Activity 1: Rehabilitation of 500 Dunums of agricultural land: Our proposed Intervention will benefit 125 families. Different sub activities will be implemented for each beneficiary depending on the needs: • Light machinery work to remove the rocks from the target land.• Rehabilitation of the stone retaining walls to protect the soil from drifting and to increase soil capacity to retain moisture. • Cleaning and plowing of the rehabilitated land.• Fencing the land to protect them from wild animals and livestock animals.• Rehabilitation of rainwater harvesting cisterns to irrigate cultivated crops. Seedlings will be provided for farmers as a contribution from PARC, within its annual campaign of seedlings distribution and cultivation. By its nature, land rehabilitation will create jobs for 200 skilled and unskilled workers during 3000 days. Activities will be open to the participation of men and women based on vulnerability criteria, with the option to create only female working groups to facilitate their participation.Once the project is approved, there will be a primary phase where a serious of actions will be done. Firstly, PARC will announce the project to the community and will ask them to create a local committee. In this committee will be representative the village council, the CBOs/cooperatives and the related ministries. Each of them will choose their own representatives with a fair representation of women and men inside the committee. This Committee will be involved in all parts of the project in order to assure: Formalize the participation of representatives of the Local Council and local community organizations; Strengthening relations with the community and the target populatio

The total population will be 600 people from which 152 will be men, 146 women, 260 children and 42 elderly.
