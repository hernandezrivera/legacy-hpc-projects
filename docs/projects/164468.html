Project Summary

PUI’s FOCOS strategy programming continues to be undertaken in a multi-year strategic way,with 2020 representing the fifth year of an integrated area-based approach (IABA). In this regard,PUI considers five layers of synergies:
Synergies for access;between activities for an integrated response;between locations; for transition to longer term development approaches (nexus);and with a multi-year donor actions.
75% of targeted locations (areas within Rural Damascus, Aleppo, Deir Ezzor, Homs and Lattakia) are under 4,5,6 severity scale, with 5% of PWDs targeted. In the aforementioned governorates, PUI will implement the proposed project contributing to the EO1 through the implementation of:Remedial classes&SLP, targeting 9700 school aged children &youth Dropped out/and/or at risk of dropping out of school;rehabilitation of 10 educational facilities,including integrated WASH in school components, targeting slightly&moderately damaged facilities to ensure safe healthy learning environments;Provision of 13300 student kits to ensure children have the tools necessary for educational activities.PUI will contribute to EO2 through the implementation of PSS training; Active Learning training Life Skills training, targeting 450 MoE’s teachers&administrative staff working in the educational facilities,where PUI implements educational activities to ensure comprehensive approach.
Most of the targeted locations are underserved, as they become accessible/ partially accessible to humanitarian aid recently. The majority of the selected locations have witnessed a huge number of IDPs/returnees,which put extra burden on the capacity of the education system.Education needs are huge in those locations due to destruction/ damage of schools,lack of qualified&trained educational staff, fear of children’s safety and security conditions,behavioural challenges within school environments,and financial difficulties leading families’ inability to afford for their children's education.

4 - Needs

Identifying the needs was based on initial data collected from different resources including the HNO, but more specifically through assessment conducted by PUI team in the targeted locations. Daraya,RD:+10,000 PoCs returned with the lack of services.PUI gained initial access in 2019 through the rehabilitation of a public bakery. Duma,RD:PUI entered the city in late 2019 through educational activities and now 44,850 PoCs are residing there. Bait Jan,RD:PUI launched its initial intervention in 2018 through a partial water network rehabilitation. Dayr Hafir,Aleppo: With around 14,500 PoCs residing there,PUI gained initial access in 2019 through the rehabilitation of a public bakery,school&PHC. Tal Refaat,Aleppo:+37,500 PoCs live there.PUI gained initial access in 2019 through the rehabilitation of the public bakery and civil registry. Deir Ezzor:In 2017,PUI responded to the needs of the population in the City through the provision of a WASH contingency stock.Currently, around 193,000 PoCs live in the city and PUI is active in Sheikh Yassin, Jbeilleh, Al Orrdy& Harabesh within the city. Al Qusayr,Homs:PUI has been active in since 2017 through a comprehensive intervention including: sewage rehabilitation, school and bakery rehabilitation, market restoration, OOR and livelihoods activities.Al Qaryatein,Homs: PUI gained initial access in 2019 through the rehabilitation of a public bakery,debris removal, PHC rehabilitation& the provision of HH-level water tanks targeting returnee populations from Rukban. Qastal Maaf,Lattakia:With around 10,000 PoCs in the area, PUI gained initial access to Qastal Maaf in mid-2019 through the OOR intervention. In summary, PUI identified the following observations and needs in the proposed areas: destruction of educational infrastructure; Fear of children’s safety and security conditions; financial difficulties; lack of qualified educational staff, needs for a support dropped-out children, behavioral challenges within school environments.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk:Security situation deteriorates and Learning facilities in some locations could be attacked.Likelihood:Low.Impact:High.Mitigation measures: Assess areas situation before intervention;learning facilities can be de-conflicted using the de-confliction mechanism;Safety protocols are set in the learning facilities;Raise awareness about existing protection and health service providers where victims can seek help.Risk:Increased risk of inter-community tension due to inequality of services provided.Likelihood: Medium.Impact:High. Mitigation measures:Perform conflict analysis before planning intervention;Try to provide equal services in areas where conflict exists;Ensure local community participations to ensure the buy in and build element of success.Risk:Incidents of child labor,youth marginalization&GBV may take place.Likelihood:Medium.Impact:High.Mitigation measures:Raise the awareness on existing service providers where victims can seek help;Enforce policies with/through all partners to prevent such incidents;Establish a confidential complaint mechanism;Train staff on code of conduct; Address any SEA complaints.Risk:Children are exposed to harm by teachers,school personnel,organization staff or related entities. Likelihood: low.Impact: High.Mitigation measures:Raise the awareness on existing service providers where victims can seek help;Enforce policies with/through all partners to prevent children exposure to harm; Establish a confidential complaint mechanism;Train teachers and other involved personnel on positive discipline; Train staff on code of conduct; Address any SEA complaints.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

PUI aims to reduce the likelihood of these risks occurring through: Assessing the areas of intervention prior to the implementation, and ensuring all stakeholders are aware of PUI's planned activities;Ensuring equal access to educational activities for IDPs, refugees, and host communities; Ensuring safe, healthy learning environments for targeted populations.PUI worked with the local communities to identify the capacities, gaps, and needs which informed the design of the proposed project. Local communities including children, education staff (and parents when possible) will be consulted on the contents of the activities and its setting.Also, PUI will conduct DoD visits as part of the BTL campaigns where communities will participate in the identification of the challenges facing children in attending schools. Based on the results of DoD visits&the communities inputs,the BTL campaigns will be designed.In addition, local communities will participate in the monitoring&evaluation of the project through BFM,evaluation forms& involvement in several stages of the project implementation.
