Project Summary

NEEDS:
More than 12 years of ongoing conflict reflected negatively on the protection situation. Protection Monitoring Analysis Working Group conducted in North-West Syria July 2023 focusing on the impacts of security escalation and humanitarian challenges on protection and recovery highlighted Most Significant Protection Concerns for Children During Displacement: 1. Children engaged in hazardous work (65%) . Violence against children (48%) Children showing signs of psychological distress (44%), Concerns Regarding Gender-Based Violence (GBV) during Displacement, The survey indicates that 82% of respondents are concerned about the heightened risk of labor exploitation faced by women and girls, including those with disabilities, potential increase in domestic violence in camps or reception centers is a serious concern raised by 68% of respondents. The risk of early and forced marriage is a pressing concern during displacement, with 64% of respondents expressing worry. Most Significant Impacts of Displacement on legal protection, more than half of the respondents (53%) reported that the loss of personal documentation had the most significant impact on them during displacement. The upheaval caused by displacement often exposes individuals to various human rights abuses, including discrimination, harassment, and exploitation. 14% of respondents identified the lack of access to legal support as a considerable challenge during displacement.Following up the latest hostilities, CCCM Custer mentioned in his last flash update, total figure of 68.100 IDPs (11.000 HHs) from 91 communities in 24 subdistricts (18.000 from Ariha, 14.250 Daret Azza, 8000 Sarmada) between October 5 and 11
56.691 of them have arrived in 162 communities (11.000 Dana, 10.000 Afrin, 7.877 Maaret Tasrim). 21.342 of them in IDPs sites and RC.916 IDPs that have gone back to their homes.
While Reach in the latest RNA stated that escalation has led to significant displacement across Greater Idleb as well as a range of protection concerns, including family separation and psychological trauma, and Protection issues reported across all assessed communities (% of 199 assessed communities). SEMA seeks to continue supporting two community centers in Salqin and Azaz, in addition to supporting 7 legal clinics (Bulbul, Salqin, Ariha, Albab, Sharran, Azaz, Jisr- alshogour) providing preventive and responsive protection services and plan to add interventions related to durable solution The services to be provided through this project is an estimated total of 22130beneficiaries / year by the end 2024 and Expected toe the same for 2025.The intervention will be in high-risk high severity areas such as Al-Bab, Azaz, Jericho, and Jisr Al-Shughour. In areas such as Salqin, located in the back line of sites exposed to escalation of hostilities and receive a large number of IDPs in these cases, .
SEMA will have twoCC in Salqin and Azaz with static team consist of (2PSS workers , 2case mangers , 2Animator , 1 vocational training officer, Legal consultant, Data entry, 1trainer on peace building ,transitional justice …)) and 2 outreach teams in each CC consist of (Team Leader, two PSS Workers, two Case Workers, one Risk Education Worker) , 7 Legal clinics consist of (2consultants,2outreachlegal workers )in the proposed locations how will be responsible for the legal interventions .activites being conducted:•

• Provision of life skills and vocational trainings as tools for mitigation of protection risks and impacts
