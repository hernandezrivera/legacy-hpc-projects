Project Summary

The project intervention area will be in Alroug Plains in Idleb and in AlBab District in Aleppo. HIHFAD has conducted many previous intervention in Alrouge plain started from the rehabilitation of the irrigation system (wells and irrigation channels) in this location, then HIHFAD established an association to manage the water usage in the area in 2018, and during 2019 HIHFAD supported the vegetable farmers and wheat farmers via vouchers to contribute in covering around 75% from their cultivation expenses (seeds, fertilizers, pesticides, irrigation and harvesting).
HIHFAD will target the big HHs, which includes more than 6 members or HHs with disables or
elderly, also women headed households or households in which the women play an important role in income generation.
The project is intending to raise their percentage of income from the crops production.
Also, HIHFAD will target the host communities who have small land size between 5 to 10
dunums, depending on the results of our assessment on land size owner in the targeted
areas.
This project will target 1900 beneficiaries in the target location (750 in Idleb and 750 in Aleppo) by supporting them with vegetable inputs to cultivate 2 dunums and with wheat inputs to cultivate 10 dunums for each. The modality of the response will use the voucher system with 120 USD value for each beneficiary
of vegetable and around 450 USD for each beneficiary of wheat cultivation, the vouchers will contributing to cover the cost of seeds, fertilizer and irrigation costس.

4 - Needs

Nine years into the crisis, the food security situation in Syria has deteriorated compared to last year, with a total of 9.8 million people in need; of which 569,477 people are estimated to be severely food insecure; 7.3 million estimated to be moderately food insecure while 1.9 million people are estimated to be at riskof food insecurity.- This year the estimated number of people in need has increased by 8 percent 1 . The number of food insecure people has also increased from 6.5M (2019) to 7.9M (2020), approximately 20 percent increase from last year, with a decrease of almost 0.7M in people at risk compared to last year – those at risk have likely become people in need this year.- Food insecurity in Syria remains to be defined by continued conflict causing mass and prolonged displacement, loss and lack of livelihood opportunities contributing to high poverty levelsand reduced production capacity.According to a need assessment survey conducted by MEAL team of HIHFAD in Al-Rouge plain, 64% of the interviewed farmers are willing to plant wheat this year if necessary assistance provided. The most important obstacles that prevent them from growing wheat are the lack of sufficient fertilizers, the lack of pesticides and the lack of rainwater, which leads to dependence on irrigation, which is high cost. the respondent farmers said that more than 30% of people working in the lands are IDPs and supporting agriculture projects will have positive impacts on their resilience in their new residential areas. The assessment showed that planting wheat is one of the highest income generating product in Alrouge plain as compared to other products like barely and Cumin.The farmers have long outstanding experiences in wheat production in Al-Rouge and Albab Area, however, simply lacking an initial capital to restart their agricultural activities, once wheat production starts and harvest succeeds, the farmers will be able to save wheat seeds for the next season.

5 - Implementation of activities

This project supports livelihoods opportunities of the war affected families through (agriculture & irrigation) focused interventions, and aims at increasing household resilience in Al-Rouge Plain in Idlib. and Albab Area in Aleppo, based on the experience of HIHFAD in the field and according to the cluster vulnerability criteria, the beneficiaries will be selected according to:1.Access to land2.Land size (in Dunum) 3.Settled in the relevant location for a minimum of 6 months in case of IDP.4.Previous experience in relevant crop farming.5.Access to the irrigation system.6.Agriculture is the main source of income for the household.Priority will be given to:a)Women-Headed Households.b)Households with a large number of children enrolled in schools or who are not being able to enroll in schools due to a family financial situation.c)Households with members with disabilities.d)Households which are hosting IDPs.The project consists of three components:1)Supporting the vegetables cultivation for 900 farmers (Using of vouchers modality), an area of 2 Dunums of vegetables agricultural land will be supported for each farmer.2)supporting 1000 farmers with agriculture inputs (wheat seeds, fertilizer and pesticides) to support the wheat cultivation in the Al-Rouge plain area and Albab sub-district.The amount of fertilizer and Pesticide will be distributed according to the endorsed and recommended amount by the scientific research.3)Providing training on good agriculture practice, storing, marketing and strategic planning for 1900 farmers and staff of the Water User Association in Al-Rouge plain in addition to members from the local entities.GAP sessions will train BNs regarding the appropriate and recommended dosage and amount of pesticide and fertilizer to make sure they have good results and benefits of them and decrease the negative impact on the environment.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

The risk is: sexual abuse against women in the field. Likelihood, medium. The mitigation measures :1. one of the project team members will be female to contact women. 2. All the team members will be trained on the code of conduct and PSEA. 3. Effective complaint mechanism to receive feedback from the ground. 4. Participation of the Local Council in all stage and activities of the project. 5. the team will distribute a flyers of feedback and flyers of PSEA to all of the beneficiaries.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

General overview of Alrouge plain and Albab Area from the Security from the security perspective:In general, Alrouge plain is Stable from the security point comparing to the other parts of Idleb Governorate, as for Albab it's safe since 2018 ,these areas has not seen any major security incidents within the last years, since it is located far from the frontlines with the Syrian Regime.Regarding the personnel Risk Assessment for the field Team and the Management and the Resources for the Risk:1-IEDs and explosive Devices: this Risk is common all around Idleb and Aleppo Governorate, targeting vehicles and military personnel significantly.Mitigation Measures :-Avoid crowded places.-Avoid peak times for movements.-Avoid military points as possible.-Keep 200 meter as a safety distance from any military cars and suspected cars.-Using the main roads only and after check with HIHFAD security team.-Improved internal information management.-Maps of the safe roads (HIHFAD security Team provides them on Daily Bases).-SMP / SOP / CP in place and permanently updated.2-Problems with /among the Beneficiaries: the probability of this Risk is low , but it could happen at any time.Mitigation Measures :-HIHFAD had a good experience in this kind of projects ( HIHFAD had a previous one before in the same area).-HIHFAD explained the project very well to all beneficiaries in this project.-HIHFAD has well-trained Team on the ground.-people in the area are familiar with kind of projects.-Good relationship with the people on the ground.3-Robbery of the Staff : according to the security incidents data in the area , the probability of this Risky is very low.Mitigation Measures :-Activities may be split into several small ones.-Guards to be assigned.-Good communication means.-Avoid moving in the dark.-Thorough check of security situation before departure to the field.-Personal Security training for HIHFAD staff.
