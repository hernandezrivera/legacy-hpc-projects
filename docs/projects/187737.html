Project Summary

As a result of the crisis affecting Syria for over ten years now, the conflicts have left several areas around the country with considerable damages in the infrastructures, housing, and services.. causing a large number of the population to flee their areas of residence & seek refuge in neighboring areas. & after the military actions have ceased, IDPs are eager to return to their houses in their original areas of residence.
The Syrian Society for Social Development is planning to provide basic necessary life saving services, through rehabilitation of Damaged houses for Returnees & Host Communities in various locations in the governorates of Rural Damascus and Idleb (150 houses/750 beneficiaries in 2022 & 100 houses/500 beneficiaries in 2023).
SSSD has previously implemented a similar project in the area of Az-Zabdani / governorate of Rural Damascus, rehabilitating Damaged houses, Schools, and the main sewerage pipe serving the area, in addition to community centers providing services to beneficiaries of the host community & returning population in the area, & official approvals are already obtained for over 180 houses.
SSSD is planning for a similar project in the year 2023 in the area of Kafr Batna / governorate of Rural Damascus where it is providing services in a community center.
SSSD has also started a project of rehabilitation of Damaged houses in the area of Khan Shaykun / governorate of Idleb, in addition to activities providing services in a community center in coordination with a UN mobile team acting in the area.
The planned number of the houses is expected to reach (/150/ houses in Az-Zabadani & Khan Shaykun in 2022 & /100/ houses in Kafr Batna & Khan Shaykun in 2023) expecting to host /1250/ beneficiaries, with around 69% of returnees, selected following the agreed selection criteria, among the most vulnerable of children, women, men, people with special needs and elderly among the affected IDPs.

4 - Needs

Information by SSSD community centers, communications with Municipalities, Mukhtars & neighborhood committees, our staff has conducted a search of Damaged houses & expected number of returnees:in Az-Zabadani/Rural Damascus (listed in the 2022.Geographic locations and PiN, as severity scale 2) over 1500 families are in need of shelter support returning from neighboring Madaya, Boukein, Al-Tall & Damascus, (60.000-80.000 SYP monthly renting fees). SSSD is present in the area with a project of rehabilitation of Damaged houses, & official approvals are obtained for over 180 houses.in Kafr Batna/Rural Damascus (listed as severity scale 3), with around 50,000 inhabitants, over 5000 families are returnees from the affected outskirt farms & neighboring Saqba, Ghouta, Misraba & Jaramana, (50.000-100.000 SYP monthly renting fees). SSSD is present in the area in community centers.in the area of Khan Shaykun/Idleb (listed as severity scale 2) main public utilities have moved from Idleb, & over 1400 families are returnees from Idleb & Hama, (100.000-150.000 SYP monthly renting fees). SSSD is present in the area with a project of rehabilitation of Damaged Houses, & other organizations (TGH, Red Crescent, Danish council..) have worked on the rehabilitation of a school, water well, as well as studies prepared for light rehabilitation of Doors & Windows & providing water cisterns. a technical assessment has been conducted in houses with average damages & no structural destruction.

5 - Implementation of activities

After searching for houses with average, non-structural damages in the selected areas of returning population, The modality adopted by SSSD in implementing the project activities is:- Applying for official approvals for the selected project locations & houses (Kafr Batna & Khan Shaykun, as approvals for Az-Zabadani are already obtained).- Conducting a detailed technical study of assessment in selected houses, & needs of residing beneficiaries.- Announcement of tenders as per the adopted SSSD policy. - Implementation of the project activities through local contractors & encouraging the participation of skilled beneficiaries in the implementation of works, (no capacity building needed for this activity).- Procurement of material from the local market.- Supervision & tracking of the activities by SSSD staff in Rural Damascus & Idleb.- Collecting needed data for periodic reporting as per the agreed templates.- Handing over of the implemented houses to beneficiaries, in presence of the municipality representative.

10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

SSSD, Since 2018, has adopted a PSEA policy, all the humanitarian staff is trained on applying this policy and asked to sign it. As well as training the beneficiaries on established complaints mechanisms in case of offence or abuse, the mechanisms include a hotline in all governorates, as well as through Social Media, in addition to providing activities of protection awareness sessions to beneficiaries. As well as preventing potential GBV, exploitation or harassment, by providing safe, well lit & well equipped spaces in the project area. and providing easy & safe access for elderly & people with special needs to houses, bathrooms & toilets.
