Project Summary

- A majority of Afrin areas are considered of low economic status with little access to work opportunities.
Peace Hands’ food basket distribution will be targeting areas considered the most in need. Many of these
areas are filled with newly arriving IDPs that have not been reached.
- A number of vulnerable host & IDP families in the Jandairis Sub-district heavily rely on the monthly Food
Rations in order to survive the month and the worsening war conditions have only made work
opportunities scarcer. According to HRP 2022, 4 out of 5 Syrians live under the poverty line unable to
sustain themselves.
in Jandairis Sub-district: there are 15,831 IDPs out of 44,447 total population, which in total makes 35.6
% of the population IDPs.
- Peace Hands, through this project will support 12 vulnerable host & IDP families in Jandairis Sub-district
in Afrin district with monthly food baskets during 4 months increasing their food security condition and
support their survival.
1325 Food baskets will be distributed in Jandairis Sub-district for 8Months each month covering the
needs of 1325 most vulnerable IDPs & host families starting from May till Feb 2023.
The target beneficiaries will be the most Vulnerable IDPs & host community members in the sub-district of Jandairis in the district of Afrin, this includes female-led households and children (between 6-23 months.) the distribution of food baskets will focus primarily on reaching Vulnerable IDPs & host community members, who are primarily of low socioeconomic status, arriving from areas that have suffered greatly, such as Deir Ezzor, Raqqa, Rif Damascus, North Rural Homs, Aleppo, Daraa, Al Quneitra, and other locations.
The total number of beneficiaries from the food baskets assistance is 1325 families. This is through covering their food needs each month for 8 months.
Peace Hands will have a complaint-feedback mechanism, which includes a complaint & feedback box at the distribution & beneficiaries Registration sites, also hotline, and an online mechanism. Beneficiaries' complaints will be answered by the Monitoring and Evaluation team, and needed responses will be implemented to better the distributions.
Additionally, the M&E team will perform regular surveys, and door-to-door visits performing PDMs after distributions to measure the project effects and the beneficiary’s satisfaction. This will include 16 % of the project beneficiaries from different categories: IDPs, Host community population, Women, Elderly & youth.
-Procurement of the food baskets in Turkey & Transport the food baskets to Peace Hands warehouse in Jindairis.
At the beginning of each month, 2 months full supply of Food Baskets will be transferred to Peace Hands warehouses in the targeted subdistrict of Jindairis.
- Transporting the food baskets to the food distribution points: Peace Hands will do reception, secure storage, and handling at the mutually agreed delivery points as per Peace Hands quality standards and practices for final distribution to the eligible beneficiaries of the commodities.
- The peace Hands warehouse assistant will make sure about the quality of the food items that will be included in food baskets through regular check of the food basket samples contents. Also, Peace Hands will ensure to procure good food Items quality.
Peace Hands has full access to the Afrin district, including but not limited to the Jindairis sub-district, Peace Hands has work permission from a government that is considered the highest authority there, Peace Hands also has an office there, which consists of full administrative staff to supervise and follow up the project implementation. For Emergencies circumstances, Peace Hands is committed to implementing the project activities in other sub-districts in Afrin District, Where Peace Hands has official permission to implement project activities just not in the targeted areas. In case any risk on the project area will appear that affects the project implementation.

4 - Needs

According to the assessment respondents in Afrin (including Jandairis) stated that food is mainly purchased from market shops (64% from the main shop), even when the majority states that markets are not functioning, while food assistance (42% of respondents) and own food production (42%) were the secondary sources of food. No further details are available Also, as per the findings of Peace Hands assessment in Afrin under the FSL sector conducted by “Peace Hands Data Needs Assessment Unit” in June 2021 in Afrin, there are challenges for the population in Jandairis with regards to access to food and markets, moreover impact of the crisis on livelihoods exist among the most vulnerable categories.The main sources of food in Afrin are currently local markets and food assistance. Also, a sharp reduction in agricultural production and price hikes due to high fuel prices and transport costs continue to impact the availability of and access to food. Populations in Afrin have faced multiple challenges in accessing food markets, attaining sufficient livelihoods, and meeting their basic food needs. 42% of the people have humanitarian assistance as one of the main sources of livelihood for IDPs. And 57% Proportion of assessed areas in which food is a priority need which especially affects children, women & people with disabilities.Through these project activities, Peace Hands will cover the food needs of the most vulnerable families in Jandairis through the most appropriate modality.
