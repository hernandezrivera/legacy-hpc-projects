Project Summary

The mental health inpatient acute unit is a specialized psychiatric interventions, including full assessment, diagnosis with prescription of the appropriate psychotropic medications, will be provided for beneficiaries from both communities IDPs and host, women and men i.e. adults above 18 years old with the most urgent presentations of severe mental health disorders, like schizophrenia (acute psychotic episodes), bipolar disorder (manic episode), severe depression, suicidal risk, severe substance misuse disorder and severe behavioral disorder related to mental health problems, by a professional psychiatrist, which cannot be managed and addressed in the community, in addition to provision of group PSS sessions, by a team of PSWs, including motor activities, motor-sensory activities, concentrating activities and interactive activities since the main objectives of those sessions are to promote and reinforce the patients’ mental capacities and keep them active and also to help in the integration of patients with each other by improving their communication skills to reach an active social network. In addition, those sessions play an important role in improving the patients’ response to the treatment plan, including the psychotropic medications, and are collaborative with the psychiatric nurses, which in turn leads to a stabilization of the patients’ cases and improving their well-being. Also, Families of beneficiaries or caregivers will be provided with psycho–education sessions regarding the symptoms of mental disorders and the appropriate use of medications in line with physicians' recommendations. This is to ensure seamless coordination between the unit and families during follow-up.
