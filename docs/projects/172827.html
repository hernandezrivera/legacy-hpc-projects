Project Summary

The objective of this project is to provide humanitarian assistance for immigrants, IDPs, and host communities.
This project aims to improve sanitation in a number of detention centers, 10 schools in Misrata, and 5 schools in Tawargha.
The number of beneficiaries is 5,000 people, including 1000 irregular migration and 4,000 students.
DETENTION CENTER / estimated women 400 and 600 ( boys &girls )
SCHOOLS / estimeted 2,500 girls and 1,500 boys.

Plan fields

1 - Needs

For decent living in detention centers, sanitation services must be provided (toilets, washing water, drinking water, and personal hygiene items for women and children). In schools, in order for the student to continue the study, the appropriate bathrooms must be kept that protect the child's safety and dignity. Also, there is a need for washing water and drinking water. Target number 5000 person, children 70% from the total, 15% women, and 15% Men, Children Ages 05-14 years boys and girls.

2 - Activities

Activities in this project focus on the maintenance and rehabilitation of the bathrooms, the connection of the washing water network, and the provision of hygiene kits, especially for children. This project seeks to preserve and protect the dignity of beneficiaries without discrimination (not on the basis of sex or color). The project will include a health awareness campaign. Follow-up will be through frequent visits and reports.

5 - Mitigation measures
5.1 - For each risk mentioned under 4 above, please describe the ways you plan to mitigate, prevent and/or address these.

Emdad has already obtained clearance to visit all detention centers and agreed with DCIM on the key areas of interventions. Therefore, we are always keen on periodic visits to DCs and individual interviews with those in need to express their views in order to communicate their complaints. This will give Emdad insight and provide the needed support and intervention for those in need. Also, this will strengthen the identification and referral.Also, to change the detention policies that affecting migrants by conducting awareness campaigns and workshops for those in charge of DCs, for working to improve living conditions at the facility, preserving their dignity and provide a decent living for them. All the facilities to be provided will be gender specific and particular focus will be to address the needs of women and girls. Women specific hygiene materials will be provided to the migrants. All the activities in DCs will be carried out as per the recommendations of principle framework for migrants.
