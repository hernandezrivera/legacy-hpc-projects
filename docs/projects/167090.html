Project Summary

Enlargement of Project # 159991: Since the schools are closed due to Covid-19, the proposed activities to ensure Education continuity include. Provision of hand washing facilities, hand sensitizers, soaps, Establishment of communication system between education authorities, schools and school communities. Establishment of case management and referral system between education authorities, schools and school communities and health and other social. Disinfecting school infrastructure, furniture and teaching and learning supplies and ongoing maintenance/cleaning protocols. (Ensuring safe return to students). Provision of safe and adequate WASH facilities. Train teachers, Head teachers CEC and parents in enhanced child safe guiding WHO guidelines" on how to help children cope with COVID-19 shocks and identify COVD-19 symptoms and referral path ways. Preparation an dissemination of heath messages through the mass media (TV, Radio, websites, sms and mobile ringtones). WASH and Hygiene promotion interventions (including rehabilitation of WASH facilities). Capacity building of MOE&S through provision of online training in DRR and Covid-19 prevention and response mechanism. Provision of psychosocial training to teachers through the online mechanism. Printing and distribution of appropriate IEC print materials (e.g. posters, leaflets, comics) on COVID19 prevention. Development and dissemination of prevention key messages through prerecorded online messaging. Development, printing and distribution of appropriate IEC print materials (e.g. posters, leaflets, comics) on access to alternative learning solutions. Development and dissemination of appropriate messages through pre-recorded radio messaging on access to alternative learning solutions. Development and implementation of alternative education options (Radio and online education programming) incl. the purchase and provision of radios to vulnerable school children. Development and distribution of learning materials for learning outside of school/at home (including the provision of textbooks). Provision of teacher incentives. The activities will be implemented in different regions in Somalia that include: Puntland: the project will be implemented in, Galkio, Galdogob, Garowe, Dangoroyo, Gardho, Eyl, jariban and Lasqaaroy. In South Central Somalia the project will be implemented in Kismayo (Jubaland), Hiraan (Hirshabelle), and Baidoa (Bay region. In Somaliland, the project will target, Buuhoodle, Caynabo, Ceel Afweyn, Laas Caanood, Lughaye, Taleex, Zeylac and Burco Community mobilization and ‘Go-To-School’ Campaign. Procurement and distribution of recreational kits. construction and rehabilitation of TLS including furnishing. Support to Community Education Committees in developing and implementing contingency planning. Training for Hygiene Promotion volunteers/CECs/teachers. Procurement and distribution of trucked potable water to schools. Procurement and distribution of food items for school feeding programme. Training of children clubs. , Total estimated beneficiaries = 164250
