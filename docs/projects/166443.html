Project Summary

Support relevant stakeholders with construction, maintenance and access works specifically related to the COVID-19 response, and cover gaps of critical infrastructure projects which are delayed as a result of ongoing and protracted restriction of movement within and around the camps. This will be undertaken in close collaboration with Army, RRRC, CiC and SMSD and could include material and heavy machinery support for the construction of isolation, quarantine facilities and rehabilitation of facilities for COVID-19 response purposes, relief distribution points, common storage and facilities for example.

Objectives

"Objective 3. To reduce the spread of Covid-19 and decrease morbidity and mortality through upgrading and improving settlement areas and their immediate surroundings through construction, maintenance works, and infrastructure development that enable safe access to humanitarian assistance and Covid-19 treatment (home based and in facilities) before, during, and after weather-related incidents and emergencies.

Needs

Augment engineering and public work support to agencies planning for Covid-19 response, in particular health facilities under construction ( Teknaf ). For instance, access to isolated treatment centres under construction or existing heath facilities is insufficient or inappropriate ( camp 3, camp 5, camp 20 ext) . Some of the key access roads, water crossings or infrastructure has also been delayed due to current lockdown ( camp 1E) . Lack in supplying materials and heavy machinery movement due the lock down and restriction to access to the camp. ( camp 21, Raja Palong ) In general, there is very limited livelihood opportunities in the camp. The number of employed refugees remain very small, and significantly skewed towards men. only 2% of women were engaged in an income-generating activity compared to 27% of men. Female headed households and household without adult males are also less likely to access these activities. Furthermore, emerging data suggests COVID-19 impacts women and girls’ lives differently by exacerbating pre existing gender inequalities. Therefore, this project aims at least 25% women from vulnerable households creating livelihood opportunities.

Activities or outputs

1. Road construction, including water crossing, to give access to the isolated treatment centres and health facilities. ( camp 3, camp 5, camp 20 ext ) 2. Material supply support due the lock down and restriction to access to the camp. ( camp 21 ) 3. Heavy Machinery support to facilitate the movement out and within the camp, due the lock down and restriction to access to the camp. ( Raja Palong ) 4. Support of the key access roads, water crossing or infrastructure has been delayed due the lock-down. ( camp 1E, Tefnak )
