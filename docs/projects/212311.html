Project Summary

In Aleppo and Idleb governorates, food production has faltered, leaving 3.5 million people food insecure in a region that once had rich farmland and a robust agriculture sector.
Global Communities (GC) has designed Lifesaving Interventions For Emergencies (LIFE ) as a continuation of the pervious program with the goal of addressing urgent humanitarian needs for vulnerable, conflict-affected individuals in northwest Syria (NWS) and in Northeast Syria. This goal directly aligns with GC’s mission to save lives, alleviate human suffering, and reduce the physical, social, and economic impact of disasters through integrated multi-sector responses that are harmonized and coordinated with other actors in the field.
To increase food security for an estimated 134911 individuals through food assistance, free bread distribution, flour distribution and agricultural assistance and improvements in the wheat value chain through market strengthening by the end of the 12-month program.
LIFE will achieve its purposes through activities within the following sectors and subsectors:
• Food Assistance:
- Unconditional food assistance (In-kind, CVA, Cash modalities)
- Free bread distribution
- One off cash for RTERs.
- Wheat procurement, milling and flour distribution.
In North East Syria, Global communities (GC) known as Cooperative housing foundation (CHF) will provide 10 rounds of in-kind food baskets (FBs) to four camps reaching a projected 7,574 households (HHs) / 44,911 individuals
1)LIFE will provide monthly unconditional food assistance via $65 value as ceiling value vouchers/Cash for food to 11,750 vulnerable HHs in NWS, in Azaz, Idleb, districts for an eight-month period.. 3) LIFE will procure 1625 MT wheat. GC will hand over the wheat to the mill to be millied and the flour to be distributed to the pre-selected bakeries at the agreed percentage max 50%of its capacity. 3) LIFE will provide 800 MT out of 1625 MT of wheat to be milled into 640 MT of flour. The bread will be distributed by the Partner every other day to the 8,000-food assistance HHs in Azaz camps for free..4) LIFE will use the by-product of milling activities to support 500 HHs vulnerable livestock owners with a one-off distribution of 200 kg of fodder. 5) LIFE will provide emergency assistance of ready to eat ration through Cash modality via 120$ to 750 vulnerable IDP HHs in NWS as one-off distribution 6), LIFE program will provide agriculture support to 500 farmers by distributing certified wheat seeds, fertilizers, duel and cash for harvest.
7) In North East Syria, LIFE will provide monthly unconditional food assistance via 60.28 $ in In-kind modality covering four camps in two governorates (Hasakeh and Ar-Raqqa) for 10 months in the first year covering 65% Kcal / person / day reaching a projected 7,574 households (HHs) / 44,911 individuals from January to June 24

0 - Project Summary (extended)

LIFE program will deliver lifesaving food assistance to around 11750 vulnerable households (58750 individuals) to increase food consumption, expand nutrition diversity and improve HH resilience. We will target HHs who have been recently displaced, defined as those displaced within the last one to two weeks, For the recently displaced, emergency food assistance is a critical need while HHs navigate their new environments and assess their available resources. For HHs which are relatively more settled, access to a reliable source of food not only helps them meet their nutrition needs, but it will also help stabilize the HH economy and empower them to better plan and manage their assets. LIFE program will procure 1625MT of wheat that will be milled (800 MT will support 8000 HHs in Azaz camp with free bread and 825 MT will support the public bakeries with flour for subsidized bread purpose), also 100 MT of the milling by-products will be supported to 500 breeders, also GC will support 500 farmers with agriculture kits (wheat seed, fertilizers cash for fuel and cash for harvest)In North East Syria, Cooperative housing foundation (CHF) will provide 10 rounds of in-kind food baskets (FBs) to four camps reaching a projected 7,574 households (HHs) / 44,911 individuals. Protection Risk AnalysisLIFE is based on the “Do No Harm” principle, with activities designed to be culturally appropriate and tailored to the specific context, while preventing competition over resources. In settings where humanitarian needs are extreme and resources are scarce, GC recognizes that providing material assistance has the potential to cause conflict or to negatively impact local socio-economic conditions.GC will make every effort to mitigate the potential for conflict stemming from the introduction of program resources by maintaining a conflict-sensitive approach to implementation.we will clearly communicate the nature of interventions, beneficiary eligibility, and prioritization criteria to individuals, HHs, community, and other stakeholders.GC will conduct risk assessments and risk mitigation planning during program period to consider the different protection considerations and needs of older persons, PWD,women,children,and at-risk youth. Distributions will be designed to minimize risk, including adapting protection-sensitive implementation and post-distribution monitoring (PDM) guidelines,GC will provide the hotline number (by phone, SMS, or WhatsApp).Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures1) GC staff continue to have consistent access to target locations; 2) Active violence does not directly impact GC's offices in Gaziantep, or Qah for a sustained period of time,3) Camp managers, Local Councils, relief committees, and community stakeholders will collaborate with GC, and facilitate access for delivery of humanitarian assistance.GC has contingency plans and mitigation measures in place should for any environmental disasters like the earthquake
