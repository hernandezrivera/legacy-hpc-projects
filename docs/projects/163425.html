Project Summary

This project aims at providing children, youths and adolescents who have been out of school in hard hit areas of Donga Mantung and Bui Divisions of North West Regions with an alternative community response school resumption with all stakeholders facilitating the right to education as an indisputable strategy. SWOT analysis shall be carried out by REWAC within this hard hit localities where school has been abandoned due to security threats and risk for the children of being killed, kidnapped, raped, abused and suffer trauma effects of not going to school. This project shall focus on improving education for the most vulnerable in communities; children from poor families, girls, orphans and the disabled. By removing the barriers to education and setting up schools and learning centres we are preparing children for a future and skills to build better lives for themselves, their families and their communities. Through the provision of alternative community schools, support for parents and providing educational resources we are giving these children a reliable route out of poverty and a brighter sustainable future. The number of children being kept out of school in Cameroon has spawned educate them now – a homegrown movement urging government forces and separatist groups to respect schools as ‘no-go’ zones. Despite some softening of rhetoric on education, the school system remains a key area of contention and many children are now entering their fourth year out of school. There are indications of recruitment by armed groups, taking advantage of the absence of education as a key structural support to the lives of these children. The purpose of REWAC intervention is to rekindle and reintegrate children into home schools in the quarters by creating group and community schools. Teachers would be identified to be recruited as Volunteers in each area and these teachers should be endowed with teaching multi subjects. Educational provision shall be i packages so as to catch up for the loss and waste of non schools by children. Educational packages shall be delivered to children of First School Leaving Certificate, General Certificate of Ordinary level and Advanced level etc. Also, social reinsertion and trauma cure shall be introduce during this teaching in the educational curricular administered to these children. Children who shall benefit from this classes shall come out being educated, catch up the loss of years wasted or loss not schooling, recover from psycho-social effects and trauma of war. catch up with their age match during school attendance. Prior to this package, an Advocacy Plan meeting shall be carried out with REWAC with the help of partners such as UNESCO, the Administration and non state groups to facilitate the implementation of this project. At the same time, the principles of neutrality, independence, impartiality and humanity shall be the key humanitarian aspects and corner stone for the implementation of this project. A consultant shall be hired to develop the school curricular and a Monitoring and Evaluation consultant shall be hired to ensure that plans achieved the desired results. This project shall essentially target children who are caught in war torn areas who cannot escape, handicaps, orphans, school dropout, non combatants and former combatants who want to pursue education. This project shall advocate and build far reaching bridges for peace and positive relationships as a resilience means of the pious hope of return to normalcy for early recovery in the midst of this crises.
