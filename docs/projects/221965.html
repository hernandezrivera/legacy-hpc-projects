Project Summary

It is estimated that approximately 1.17 million people will be affected by HLP issues in 2025 across the 39 counties and Abyei Administrative Area. The situation is particularly challenging for 86,000 people in need including 16,000 IDPs and 25,000 Returnees and 45,000 host community (SSD HNO 2024). However, due to the influx of returnees and refugees from Sudan Crisis, HLP issues may be on arise.
Notwithstanding the guarantees in the RTCSS of the right of women to land and property, South Sudanese women are denied ownership rights to land, and have weak access rights that are wholly dependent on their relationship with men. The important role of women as the main users of land for agricultural production, and the large number of women headed households makes it imperative that their land rights be strengthened and secured to ensure livelihood security, household food and nutrition security and the productive use of land for economic development. (South Sudan Draft National Land Policy 2023, p.41).
Factors contributing to these higher risk of HLP issues include but not limited to dynamics such as a history of displacement, influx of returnees from Sudan and Ethiopia, conflict, proliferation of unauthorized arms in the hands of criminals, leading to secondary occupation, land grabbing, flooding, lack of widespread knowledge of HLP rights among the communities, forced eviction, loss of documentation and title deeds, disputes over customary land tenure, the increase in population growth, PoC sites transition to IDPs Camps, increasing number and high influx of returnees from the neighboring countries (Uganda, Kenya, Ethiopia and Sudan), and poverty.
Without appropriate interventions taken or immediate assistance provided, the HLP issues will definitely result into further displacement and conflicts which will have adverse effects on the families and children. Therefore, this HLP project will target 13,230 beneficiaries (6,880 Women, 6,350 Men,) HLP awareness-raising and information-sharing activities; Aid Link Organization will conduct awareness and information-sharing activities aimed at identifying and timely referring individuals to appropriate services, as well as informing communities about available HLP services, individualized legal counseling and legal aid such as non-cash and financial support to achieve HLP results through legal aid, establish and/or strengthen disputes resolution mechanisms and train local authorities and relevant institution’s staff in disputes resolution mechanism for them to be enabled to meaningfully participate in disputes resolutions.
In all its implementation phases, Aid Link Organization will strictly adhere to national laws and international norms on HLP, Code of Conduct, Community Complaint and Feedback Mechanism, Community engagement, uphold the Do No Harm Principle, promote centrality of protection principles and enhance accountability to affected populations before, during and after project implementation. Aid Link Organization will implement this project with technical assistance from South Sudan HLP Area of Responsibility (AoR), and in coordination with Protection Partners on the ground together with relevant authorities.
Aid Link Organization already has operational presence in the selected counties where it is implementing humanitarian responses. The key HLP activities are Activity 1: Conduct 10 HLP awareness raising and information sharing activities; Activity 2: Support 11,451 women and men with HLP legal counselling; Activity 3: Support 916 women and men with HLP legal aid – cash; Activity 4: Support 5009 women and men with HLP legal aid - non cash; Activity 5: Establish and strengthen 10 dispute resolution mechanisms:
Activity 6: Train 200 men and women, local authorities and institutions.
To be able to deliver the HLP objective for the selected counties, a total budget of $445,860 that would support 14,862 individuals in need of HLP services IDPs, Returnees and Host Communities.

2. Project activities

Aid Link Organization proposes to support the realization of the Strategic Objectives and Protection Cluster objective for Housing, Land and Property by implementing key activities that contribute to CLPRO/BF4-PRO-HLP - Housing, Land and Property. This project will reach 14,862 individuals (Malakal=4090, Renk=5376, Fangak=2286, Pibor=1919, Uror=1191).Key activities are: Activity 1: Conduct 10 HLP awareness raising and information sharing activities.Aid Link Organization will conduct 10 sessions of HLP awareness and information-sharing activities aimed at identifying and timely referring individuals to appropriate services, as well as informing communities about available services. These people include IDPs, returnees and Resident communities. The awareness and information sharing will center on the issues affecting the community social cohesion and what can be done to avoid conflict in common places of social concerns. Access to land and property is one of the main problems IDPs and returnees face in the area of returns. To facilitate HLP awareness raising activities, Aid Link Organization will establish a HLP desk in the community center at each targeted locations (Fangak, Pibor, Uror, Nasir/Luakpiny, Malakal and Renk) managed by volunteers supervised by aid Link organization HLP staff. They will conduct awareness and information sharing sessions on HLP and distribute the Information, Education and Communication (IEC) materials for HLP issues, as well as identify and respond to cases. Particular focus will be given to women, who often do not have access to justice due to the lack of knowledge of their rights, availability of legal aid and associated payment fees. In addition, very few women are in a possession of land and even fewer who have documents confirming land ownership as highlighted in the National Draft Land Policy 2023. Activity 2: Support 11,451 women and men with HLP legal counselling.Depending on the severity of the concern, the individual may receive more specialized support which might include additional legal advice or referrals to the authorities, whereas individuals who may not require case management will be referred to the HLP Help Desk. Through this component, beneficiaries will be supported to access both formal and informal justice mechanisms (individual cases follow up with law enforcement, courts). Activity 3: Support 916 women and men with HLP legal aid – cash: Individualized legal assistance, pro bono services/case management will be provided for specific HLP cases. Where beneficiaries require referrals to external services, Aid Link Organization will conduct direct payments to these services to ensure the beneficiaries are able to access this critical supportActivity 4: Support 5009 women and men with HLP legal aid - non cash. Individualized legal assistance, pro bono services/case management will be provided for specific HLP cases.Activity 5: Establish and strengthen 10 dispute resolution mechanisms: The 10 HLP ADR mechanisms will be set up in each location with the composition made up of local leaders, representatives of minority groups and people who have knowledge of disputes related to HLP after attending the awareness sessions.Activity 6: Train 200 men and women, local authorities and relevant institution's staff on HLP, dispute resolution mechanisms. This will include Local authorities and relevant institution's staff on HLP, dispute resolution and ADR.
