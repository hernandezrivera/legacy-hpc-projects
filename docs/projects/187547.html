Project Summary

The project will take place in Idlib city- Harim and Al-Dana districts – Idleb governate for two yeas 2022 & 2023. Estimated number of beneficiaries in the first year 2022: 23094 (16719 formal education students – 3750 non-formal education students for low achievers at the risk of drop out of schools - 2000 ECE children – 625 schools’ staff) and in the second year 2023: 19244 (16719 formal education students - 2000 ECE children – 525 schools’ staff) Estimated # of disability beneficiary: 2.5% This project will support the educational activities in 25 schools in Idlib city, Harim, and Al-Dana districts (camps) – Idleb. The project contributes to the education cluster and HRP objectives through: 1- Project will scale up safe and equitable access to formal and non-formal education in Idleb governate age (3- 17) and 2- Enhance the quality of formal and non-formal education for children (aged 3- 17 years) within a protective environment. The project targeted the schools in 2022 through: 1- Rehabilitating 200 classrooms and rehabilitate facilities and sanitation services to access drinking water, and segregate toilets. 2- Providing 25 schools with running cost, supplies, etc. 3- Providing 16719 students (5-17 years, 8125 boys & 8595 girls) with formal education and PSS activities and stationery. 4- Providing 3750 low achievers students (5-17 years, 1688 boys and 2063 girls) with non-formal education (remedial classes) and stationery 5- Providing 2000 children (3-5 years, 1100 girls/900 boys) with ECE- 9 months in same schools. 6- Providing 625 school staff (344 men, 281women) with capacity building and incentives and in the second year 2023 the project targeted the schools through: 1- Providing 25 schools with running cost, supplies, etc. 2- Providing 16719 students (5-17 years, 8125 boys & 8595 girls) with formal education and PSS activities and stationery. 3-Providing 2000 children (3-5 years, 1100 girls/900 boys) with ECE- 5 months in same schools. 6- Providing 525 school staff (264 men, 261 women) with capacity building and incentives. In case of spreading of Covid 19 pandemic and the suspension of work in schools, it will depend on blended education (education via WhatsApp and other electronic channels based on cluster guidance for COVID-19 response, in addition to visiting the school several times by week, considering the rules of social distancing, preventive and personal protection measures. WATAN had Personal Protection Equipment will distribute it to the targeted schools including masks, gloves, etc. as a contribution from the WATAN side in the project. The project will increase the boys’ and girls’ beneficiaries with disabilities by providing schools with the necessary tools and equipment’s to be appropriate with disabilities and training the staff on inclusion education. The affected population will be involved in all the stages of the project planning , designing , implementation , Monitoring , Evaluation where there will be FGDs , PDMs after distributions, satisfaction surveys and distributed items and field monitoring visits , discussions , coordination , consultation and meetings with the parents , teachers , children and local education authorities in the schools on choosing the best ways for implementation of activities and maintain high levels of accountability to affected population during after COVID-19.WATAN (MEAL) Team will be responsible for developing a monitoring methodology that will include the relevant indicators to track project progress and different tools and steps: Collecting information and other relevant data. ii-Conducting observation checklists in the schools. iii-Collecting complaints and feedback. Iv-Conducting evaluation surveys (interview-BNFs) v-Conducting monitoring interviews with the head of schools. The project is not funded yet.

4 - Needs

Based on the draft HNO 2022, there are over 7 M children 3-17 years in need of education support, where 6.3M are children in need of education assistance. According to ACU report “Schools in Syria report 2021” 63% of children aged 6-18 are out of school (drop out). Based on Education directorate statistics there are 328 schools working voluntarily without any support in Idlib (Idlib Education Directorate Report, Dec 2021).Based on rapid need assessment conducted by WATAN in Dec 2021 in 3 sub-districts in Idleb. 25 schools have been selected according to these reasons: These schools are not supported from long time and the students are at risk of drop out, the schools included host community and IDPs students they have been displaced many times. The key findings of assessment were: - There are 16719 children (8125 boys & 8595 girls) enrolled in formal education in 25 schools. - There are 3750 lower achievers’ students (1688 boys and 2062 girls) in 25 schools needed non formal education (remedial classes). - There are 625 teachers and education personnel working voluntarily without incentives in all schools. - The 25 schools do not provide ECE for children and 90 % of caregivers of children don't know the importance of ECE. - 200 classrooms need rehabilitation in the 25 schools including rehabilitation for wash facilities. - All the 25 schools need support with running costs and education material and supply, and all the children need to school bags and stationery kits.

Conduct 25 Back-to-schools campaigns. 3. Provide 16719 children (5-17 years, 8125 boys & 8595 girls - 425 PWD) with formal education. 4. Provide 3750 children (5-17 years, 1688 boys & 2063 girls- 100 PWD) with non-formal education with same time with formal Education. 5. Provide 20469 students with textbooks, school bags, and stationery kits in formal and non-formal education and provide 25 schools with running cost. 6. Support 2000 children (3-5 years, 1100 girls/900 boys) to enroll in ECE in same schools in summer months. 7: Launch the teacher's recruitment process focusing on both Male and females including PWD. 8. Provide all staff 625 (281 female & 344 Male – 37 PWD) desegregation (500 formal, 100 non-formal, 25 ECE) with incentives and training them on Active learning, Classroom management, and Inclusive Education. 9 - Protection Risk Analysis

1. Risk: School staff participates in punishment, bully, and neglect of students. Mitigation: all staff schools sign the code of conduct that ensures enrolling vulnerable girls and disabilities and including training to staff on Protection principles and Childe protection. 2. Risk: Discrimination of PWD or IDPs in the project activities. Mitigation: WATAN will promote an inclusive education approach to include all the beneficiaries in the targeted schools, and wash facilities will be friendly and accessible for CWDs, and they will be provided with rehabilitation for disabilities (ramps, special seats for those children, etc.) 3: Risk: Schools are attacked by parties to the conflict. Mitigation will suspend the activities in the schools to keep children and staff safe and provide the schools with an evacuation plan.4: Risk: The schools don’t protect the children from (flood, rain, etc.) Mitigating Measures: WATAN will do rehabilitation to the classrooms and wash facilities including specific rehabilitation for CWD.


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Risk sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA), including harassment, against women and girls. Risk Management Strategy/Mitigating Measure: Mainstreaming PESA inall the phases of the project When hiring the staff of the project and education personnel in the schools through signing the PSEA policy and code of conduct for all staff and education personnel in the schools, conducting PSEA sessions for all staff and, providing internal reporting mechanism on PSEA, and clear reporting channels that are accessible for all staff and All WATAN staff including field staff that is working directly with beneficiary are regularly trained on PSEA and reporting channels. providing orientation for beneficiaries PSEA reporting Mechanism, through multiple channels (lines, Posters, email, Focal points) and MEAL Dept will: 1- Pay a visit to schools and ensure the safety of children and staff. 2- All beneficiaries will be able to contact WATAN through complaint and suggestion email and phone numbers to follow up on all complaints. 3- All feedback from the community will be discussed in the monthly meeting.


11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

WATAN through conducting this project considers These main risks: 1- Air attacks and indirect shots Likelihood: middle Impact: High Mitigating measures Project location in a relatively safe area because of its closeness to the Turkish border, so the probability of safety threatens is low. For emergency cases, WATAN will provide all schools with safety and security tools to ensure the safety of schools and staff inside the project location. 2- Intervention by local authorities Likelihood: middle Impact: High Mitigating measures the Project will coordinate with two main entities: - Local authorities and other organizations: WATAN will sign an MoU with ED to clarify targeted schools, planned activities, and interventions and take the Authorities’ recommendations into account to achieve project target and community benefits. 3- A lack of skilled teachers Likelihood: middle Impact: High Mitigating measures: Training teachers on formal and non-formal education curricula. 4- Children dropping out of formal education to attend Non formal Education centers Likelihood: medium Impact: High Mitigating measures: Identify specific criteria to all implementing partners for accepting children into the program. 5- Due to the COVID-19 outbreak in the targeted locations/schools the local authorities applied curfew in all governorates, WATAN has a plan B changing most of the activates methods to be blended Education (online & Face to Face).
