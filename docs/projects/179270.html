6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Our main protection risk concerns the safety of those from whom we collect information. We have standing policies for data management, cleaning, and analysis that ensure all personal and otherwise identifying information is protected where it is collected and deleted prior to any use or analysis of any dataset.

7 - Does the project include a PSEA strategy ?

Yes

7.1 - Please list any risks related to sexual exploitation and abuse that have been identified during the project design phase and any risk mitigation and prevention measures that have been adopted and built into the project

ACTED PSEA and Child protection policies are signed by all ACTED/REACH staff members as part of the Code of Conduct. All ACTED/REACH national and international staff receive a two-hour induction training session on PSEA. Furthermore, ACTED has a MENA Regional Protection Advisor which supports ACTED's field missions, including Syria, through the provision of training, fact-sheets, and guidance to uphold protection principles, including gender mainstreaming and PSEA. ACTED recently nominated two PSEA focal points to join the inter-agency PSEA-CBCM network and contribute to the improvement of all humanitarian partners' PSEA policies and measures in the Whole of Syria, particularly ACTED. Under this project, GBV and sexual exploitation, abuse, and harassment complaints would be channelled to ACTED which would lead to the investigations, according to the protocols defined in ACTED PSEA policy.

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

REACH has established relationships with key informants inside Syria, whom they call to collect information on the humanitarian situation, access to services, conditions in IDP settlements, and market functionality. If the security situation in some locations seriously deteriorates or a shift in control occurs, it is possible that REACH will lose contact with these key informants. In this case, a snowballing approach will be used to establish new contacts with traders and/or community representatives – if it would prove low to no-risk to those contacted. REACH may also complement data collected by partners who have maintained access with analyses using GIS and RS.
