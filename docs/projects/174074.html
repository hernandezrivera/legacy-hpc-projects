Project Summary

Insan Dost Social Organization is proposing the project of preventing the Women and Girls of GBV survivors to end violence and discrimination in District Khairpur Mirs, Sindh Pakistan. The main objectives of project are to detent and prevent the survivors of GBV through appropriate services and protection measures; to prevent and support the Survivors through provision of socio-psycho support, counseling, legal aid and conditional support and create awareness among women and girls on PPEs and IPC to reduce the violence and discrimination in rural communities of Khairpur Mirs Sindh, Pakistan. The reliable sources and protection measures are established and survivors support units are established for reducing the vulnerability in Sindh Pakistan. The muliti sectroal stakeholders are mobilized and sensitized to respond the cases of GBV survivors and provide the equitable support to women and girls in Pakistan.
The objectives will be achieved through;
• Project Launching Ceremony at District level and minimum 100 people with 50% women participation will be ensured to share the services and activities of project,
• Arrange orientation workshop with key stakeholders including Police Officials, Lawyers, District Administration, Journalist, Duty Bearers, Youth Groups, Social Welfare, Lady Health Workers and others will be well informed about project deliverable, objectives and strategies. More than 100 people are well informed and recommendation and collaborative efforts will be reported and documented
• Establishing and strengthening the Referral Pathway which consisting on Police Officials, Lawyers, District Administration, Journalist, Duty Bearers, Youth Groups, Social Welfare, Lady Health Workers. The integrated GBV services will be established in health facilities to address issue of S/GBV including CMR and sensitizing the key influencers on social support ,
• Arrange Training workshop to referral pathway members on case management ; referral mechanism role to survivors of GBV, Provision of psycho-social support services to GBV survivors through Toll Free numbers, legal aid, socio –psycho support and counseling services to women and girls survivors,
• Formation Survivors Support Units [SSU] at community level. The member of SSU will be Women complaint cell, CBOs, Lawyers and government representatives. It will be respond the cases and provide the referral services to GBV survivors for ensuring the community based protection mechanism
• Coordination and networking with GBV multi sectoral and referral pathways and case management services to protect and prevent the women and girls of GBV survivors
• Creating awareness among local population on available GBV services; through Mobile messages, Radio Messages, Rickshaw Campaign and others to disseminate the information of services
• Arranging the Training Workshop for Frontline health workers[Lady Health workers, vaccinators, Police officers and auxiliary staff received PPEs and training on IPC
• Monitoring and reporting Visit for gauge the result of project and measure the quality of deliverables
Results
• GBV survivors are responded and dealt their cases through strengthened referral systems
• Increased access of GBV survivors on improved legal aid, socio-psycho support, counseling services and SR services at services centers
• Survivors support Units are established to improved legal aid, socio-psycho support, counseling services and SR services at services centers
• Increased knowledge of services providers and staff to respond the cases of GBV survivors and capable to dealt cases and trained on case management
• Communities are more organized and aware and recognized the GBV survivors cases and provide community managed mechanism to responding the cases of GBV
