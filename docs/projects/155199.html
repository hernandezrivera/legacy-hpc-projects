Needs

The health status of the population in need has improved since the beginning of the influx but remains fragile. Under 5 mortality rates have reduced from 1.22 deaths/ 10,000/ day to 0.86 and CMR from 1.36 to 0.38 deaths/ 10,000/ day between October 2017 and May 2018 (source: SMART survey) but remain close to the SPHERE emergency threshold for South Asia. UNHCR is currently supporting 20 primary health care facilities through its partners, in the first 10 months of 2018 a total of 360,775 consultations were provided and 5,205 patients referred to secondary health care facilities. Key morbidities are upper respiratory tract infections (22%), gastritis (12%), fever of unknown origin (10%), skin diseases (7%), lower respiratory tract infections (7%) and diarrhea (6%) (source: UNHCR HIS). The risk of communicable disease outbreaks remains high with low EPI coverage and poor WASH services. Health awareness remains low overall, additional community based activities are needed to engage communities in disease prevention and addressing major health concerns including non-communicable diseases. A health sector outbreak and response preparedness plan is in place. Additional support is needed to ensure the availability of medicine and supplies in case of emergency to ensure timely outbreak and emergency response. Staff capacity building needs to be maintained and individual facility preparedness plans need to be maintained.

Activities or outputs

UNHCR’s proposed project will complement the submitted project “Enhancing access to comprehensive quality health care services for Rohingya refugees and host communities in Cox's Bazar district”. The project is in support of the Ministry of Health and health sector outbreak and response preparedness plan, all activities proposed will be implemented in close coordination with the relevant coordination bodies. Activities will be implemented in partnership with six health partners. 1. Support for management of major public health risks including outbreak and disaster preparedness and response (Objective 2) • Support emergency response during outbreak response:o Enhance community awareness, early case identification and referral through intensive health messaging by CHWso Strengthen outbreak response at community through additional measures such as ORT cornerso Provide mobile medical teams as appropriate o Enhance service provision at health facility level and provide timely referral of patients to appropriate outbreak response centres • Support emergency response after natural disasters:o Provide first aid including psychological first aid through Community Health Workers during emergency response related to natural disasters o Provide mobile medical teams during emergency and outbreak response in line with health sector agreed priorities and approaches. o Provide camp wide referral through ambulance system to functioning facilitieso Assess impact on existing partner health facilities and ensure rapid resumption of function through tented facilities where needed • Maintain stocks for emergency response (IEHK etc) Note that activities are expected to be implemented through existing health staff of the above mentioned project and are not reflected in the budget here.
