Project Summary

Monastery Saint James the Mutilated Hospital in Al-Hol camp is still one of the most prominent supporters in Al-Hol camp, which provides integrated health care services and supports various types of health care clinics, including clinics: surgery - women - children - laboratory - minor operations - births, whether normal or caesarean section
Monastery Saint James the Mutilated Hospital in Al-Hol camp continues to provide its treatment and preventive programs to patients and visitors in the camp, including adults, children and women, who have lost the most basic health services, and some of them died as a result of the lack of these services and Large numbers of beneficiaries flock to the hospital daily, especially women and children.
As a result of the increased need for additional medical services in Al-Hol camp, this led to an increase in the work pressure on Monastery Saint James the Mutilated Hospital and a significant increase in the reception of cases in the hospital.
Therefore, we are working to expand and support medical services by :
- Expanding the Department of Internal and adding intensive care
- Expansion of the Department of Surgery to include the Department of Burns
- Expansion of the Department of Pediatrics
- Expanding the women's section to include all reproductive health services, including cold and emergency operations.
- Providing care rooms
- Add radiology department
- Add a dermatological clinic
- Add an eye clinic
- Add a Uthaina clinic
- Establishing a special center for the quarantine of patients with infectious diseases within the camp
- A mobile clinic that provides medical services in the Anexx section
