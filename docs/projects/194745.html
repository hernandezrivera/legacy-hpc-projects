Project Summary

The project aims to provide access to education, reduce school drop and increase access of homeless children by creating safe attractive and sustainable education that meets the needs for community of Central Darfur State. Specifically, the project objectives are: (1) change children satisfaction and attitude to reduce school drop and scape through creating safe school environment. (2) creating means of developing learning process. (3) support the social peace in a fragile post conflict environment in central Darfur. SUDO intervention will focus in 3 localities: 1- Zalingi (Azoom, Kongo, Abata, Arokom and Traij), 2- Umdukhun and 3- North/Shamal Jabal Marra-Rokerro. The target will be 2690 children (1451 girls and 1509 boys) in 12 schools. This includes around 30 children with disabilities (1% of the enrolled children in these schools).
The targeted schools and areas are selected based on their high need as below:
Kongo area in Zalingi is comprised of 13 villages and 3 damars (population are 3641, there is 1 school at Amargadeed with 188 children aged 6-8 at school (102 girls and 86 boys ) The existing school is made of local material and covered with plastic sheets to accommodate children from villages and damars including (Arabs and Fur) in a model of peace building process to have shared benefit like school facility to interact with each other on daily base that has already led to decrease of assault and attacks against the villagers from the Arab nomads. the people in area have also developed kind of agreement to maintain the security of the farmers and herders that has created tangible results in form of attraction of the IDPs to return and stay. So the construction of the school will attract more people to return and support their livelihoods by cultivating their land and enroll their children into school. The villagers had fled the conflict and had been staying in IDPs camps in Zalingei, Rongatass and Morni of West Darfur until they finally decided to return to their old villages.
In Zalingi locality, the project will target 8 schools with 1890 children (including 931 girls). The schools targeted are: - Kongo (Amargadeed Primary Co- School), Alshamalia Intermediate School, Omalqora Intermediate school for girls, Abata basic co edu school , Arokom Primary school for girls, Arokom Primary school for boys, Trij primary school for girls and Trij primary school for boys. In Shamal/North Jabal Marah-Rokerro, the project will target two schools; Rokero Primary school for boys and Daralamn primary Co- school (Mixed) with 550 school agaed children (including 520 girls). In Umdukhun; there are 29 schools with 9536 children (2864 girls and 6672 boys). Locality is affected by destruction of education facilities because of conflict. The project will target two schools with 520 school aged children including 270 girls.
The intervention will be proportionate support for meeting the minimum requirement of the students per classroom and it will include rehabilitation of 3 classrooms in 3 schools including primary and intermediate schools with provision of furniture (desks/benches). 2960 school aged children will be supported with learning materials, teachers’/classrooms materials and recreational materials. To improve the learning environment, the project will construct 6 gender-sensitive latrines in 2 school (each with 4 doors) with hand washing stations, provide water sources to one school and provide hygiene/dignity kits. To enhance the learning process of children, the project will organize on-job training of 18 teachers on pedagogy and curriculum and another 18 teachers in EiE, CP, GBV and positive discipline to support protection of children. The project will be implemented in collaboration and coordination with the State Ministry of Education, PTAs, school administration. It will be coordinated with the sector partners to ensure synergy and avoid overlaps.

1 - Needs

In Umdukhon locality: There are 29 schools with 9536 children (2864 girls and 6672 boys). Locality is affected by destruction of education facilities because of war, the support from the humanitarian organizations was very limited, the need is still high in this locality. SUDO assessed the need and found that:a- There are 2 schools (Omdukhun secondary school for boys and girl) merged in one temporary location of Youth and women Center because the school facilities are damaged and there is lack of school materials. The 2 schools accommodate 605 children (235 girls, 370 boys). b- Alikhlas Primary schools for girls (418 girls) no office for teachers.c- Almanar and Alnahdha Primary with 423 children (207 girls, 216 boys), No fence, latrines, seats looted and destroyed during conflict so the education is partially taking place in an open space because of lack of classrooms, latrines, offices, fence, furniture, and teaching materials. North/Shamal Jabal Marra -Rokero: There are 28 schools with 11,188 children (5569 girls, 5619 boys) at primary school aged 6-13. SUDO assessment identified the following:a- Daralaman primary Co- school: there are 550 children (270 girls, 250 boys) no fence, latrines in urgent need for rehabilitation. The school is currently used additionally as intermediate co school because it was decided to accommodate the schools that are not suitable and safe for children. The need is for classrooms, latrines, offices, and fence with equipment provision.b- Rokero high secondary school accommodating 350 boys. There is no fence, latrines need urgent rehabilitation. The classrooms are not covered with roof. c- Rokero high secondary school for girls accommodates 336 children. The need: rehabilitation of classrooms and construction of new classrooms, fence, office for teachers. Girls in grade three have been moved to another intermediate school because there is no classroom for them in the school. 5.2 - Impact on environment/natural resources

SUDO has an Environmental Policy that acknowledges the connection between the climate and other environmental crises and the threat of current and future homelessness, diseases, food and water shortages, poverty and the major damage being caused to our natural eco-system. As a result, SUDO avoids depleting natural resources and compromising future generations' ability to meet their daily needs by employing sustainable resources, avoiding pollution, and mitigating the effects of climate change. Through this project will construct school latrines to reduce the open defecation and raise awareness about hygiene practices. It will train children on this issue and how to preserve the environment. It will provide child friendly desks and benches made from iron to reduce the use of wood materials in order to reduce materials that contribute to the damage of environment.

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

The project adapts the education as a fundamental right for all and the child and it is implementation is involving partners and associated parties that SUDO ensuring a strict adherence to the protection of all the involved and give a particular concern to children by articulating SUDO code of conduct on human rights and protection in addition to SUDO code of conduct and child protection policy.We also using two tracks to mainstreaming the protection a- Advocacy for the protection by highlighting the child rights to all the concerned including teaching staff, implementing parents/ contractors, and the community. Teachers will receive training on CP, GBV and positive discipline. b- Direct intervention and engagement by the reporting lines that gave a clear guideline on prevention of any kind of abuse or violation to take place within the interaction course that involve SUDO in addition to how to report any violation or abuse.on the other hand, our field teams are in close follow up with the Ministry of Education to keep the monitoring mechanisms to the founded rules like prohibiting the corporal punishment within the schools as a form of human rights violation, imposing of school fees that ultimately depriving many people from the education as recognized basic rights and child recruitment not to take place The construction and rehabilitation of the education facilities is direct advocacy to a range of basic rights to education and school meals as discussed during the early MOU developed with MOE that this project will allow the targeted schools meet the criteria of having latrines because the school feeding program of UN WFP cannot provide food to a school that have no latrines.
