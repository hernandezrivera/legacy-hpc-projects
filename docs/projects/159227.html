Project Summary

The project will be implemented in five regions in Somalia namely-Bakool,Bay, Lower Shabelle, Nugaal and Banadir. This will be achieved through the provision of curative and preventive nutrition services at community and health facility level. Treatment of uncomplicated severe acute malnutrition (SAM) and moderate acute malnutrition (MAM) among children 6-59 will be done through Outpatient therapeutic programmes and targeted supplementary feeding programs whereas management of complicated SAM will be provided in four stabilization centers in line with national IMAM guidelines. To achieve this, Action against Hunger will provide the services in eleven health facilities and seven mobile clinics and scale up in the targeted locations if need be. This will be supported by a network of community health workers who will be actively engaged in screening and referral of cases from the community as well as provide health and nutrition education and sensitization sessions to support positive behavior change in child care practices. To improve early detection and referral of acute malnutrition, caregivers of boys and girls 6-59 months will be trained on MUAC screening and provided with color-coded tapes.ACF will tap into the national therapeutic supply pipeline in the treatment of acute malnutrition
The response will prioritize displaced persons and host communities who have been affected by crises and have limited access to these basic services. To ensure a coordinated response is achieved, the programme will link with health, WASH, food security interventions to address risk factors of malnutrition as well as actively engage external stake holders such as local authorities, Ministry of health, sector Clusters and other implementing agencies. In terms of data collection and reporting, Action Against Hunger will ensure that data is disaggregated by sex and age at all levels of project implementation. On the service delivery side, the project will ensure equity in gender representation in the selection of participants for capacity building activities, awareness meetings and in the selection of community volunteers and other community activities.
Action against Hunger will conduct routine monitoring and evaluation of achievement versus the set targets , continuously engage beneficiaries and provide feedback where necessary and adopt lessons learnt from previous projects
.The projects targets 25,227 under five boys and 26,309 U5 girls, 16,064 women and 7270 men in nutrition interventions.

Please provide further explanation

The proposed project will be implemented in close coordination with and active involvement of the district’s local governance structures, ministries, and other humanitarian actors in the region. The project will support the establishment and capacity building of community groups (Community Health Committees, camp management committees, Mother support groups, father support groups) to ensure resilient community systems are in place. The projects seeks to transition health care workers to be managed by ministry of health while still providing training and support to the government to strengthen health systems and ensure that there is good local capacity for continuity of life saving interventions even beyond the project period.Public health will be ensured through proper disposal and management of medical equipment, supplies and waste, including the use of incinerators at the health facility and stabilization centers. Part of the training will include lessons on environmental awareness and waste management.The project is part of an integrated approach where long term resilience projects are being implemented in all targeted locations. This will help mitigate the underlying causes of malnutrition such as food insecurity and poor access to safe and clean water and sanitary facilities. This will be achieved through provision of latrines with handwashing facilities to families of children under five with acute malnutrition, drilling of boreholes and shallow wells in places that are within walking distance and safe for women . To mitigate short time impact of drought, households with acute malnutrition cases as well as other vulnerable groups will be targeted in cash/voucher programmes
