Project Summary

UNFPA’s interventions intend to improve access to essential SRH services and supplies focused to reduce the excess of maternal and neonatal morality, to reduce the transmission of HIV and other STI, to reduce and to treat the consequences of sexual violence and to reduce unintended pregnancies by strengthening capacities for implementation of the Minimum Initial Services Package (MISP) for Reproductive Health in Emergencies.

Project Activities

• Support continued access to integrated essential sexual and reproductive health services in the most affected areas, including clinical management of rape, safe delivery, emergency obstetric care, family planning, and HIV and STI management, among others• Deliver sexual and reproductive health equipment and supplies, including Inter-agency Reproductive Health Kits to support implementation of prioritized MISP components• Strengthen the SRH referral system, including for obstetric care and clinical management of rape• Strengthen community level capacities in lifesaving SRH prevention, promotion and SRH care• Provide rapid training and refreshers to medical practitioners for provision of quality SRH services in line with the MISP, including Clinical Management of Rape (CMR).
