Project Summary

This project intends to provide teaching aids and learning materials to 50 schools in Hawul LGA.
The purpose of the intervention is to support and encourage teachers to perform their duties effectively with the necessary teaching material while the students will also be given the required learning materials. We are targeting thirteen thousand students and about six hundred teachers.

Needs

This project intends to address the problems of acute shortage of school supplies.As we are aware successive government did not pay attention to schools in this part of the state. In addition, the insurgency have worsen the situation where A greater number of children are now out of school.Therefore, we intend to address these issues by making provision of these materials that are not available and fix all necessary items required to make the school environment more conducive for teaching and learning.In fact, our aim is to bring back the considerable number of out of school children back and to actively participate in their studies.

Activities

Provision of teaching aids and learning materials to equip teachers in order to carry out their statutory responsibility effectively.Training of teachers on psychosocial support (PSS ) and pedagogy for quality education. This approach will also encourage teachers to take their work seriously.We shall also train members of the School Base Management Committee SBMC to acquaint them with the necessary skills for effective service delivery
