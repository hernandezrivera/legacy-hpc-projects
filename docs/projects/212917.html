Project Summary

Based on the HNO 2023, HNO summary 2024 and NCA own assessment, the needs identified in the Syrian communities calls for immediate interventions in order to (1) meet the immediate food needs of the most vulnerable households thereby stabilizing the situation and preventing households from resorting to negative coping strategies, (2) restore household productive capacity so that households can gradually move from dependency to self-sufficiency, and to (3) support the most vulnerable households to create assets for sustainable income generation. The proposed intervention seeks to address these 3 priorities through provision of food, supporting income generating activities as well as rehabilitating bakeries to improve access to affordable bread. If the households affected by conflict or earthquake are provided with food assistance and households use the cash/food resources as intended, they will reduce negative coping mechanisms as they will have an increased ability to meet basic needs. The latter will lead to a reduction in protection risks while enabling them to pursue income generating activities to live with safety and dignity

0 - Project Summary (extended)

NCA will directly implement the food security intervention in Aleppo, and Hama governorates, through 1) Food provisioning to vulnerable households below the acceptable food consumption score through cash for food modality to 300 households. 2) Provision of emergency agricultural inputs and training to 300 vulnerable headed households prioritizing women headed households to 300 households. NCA will ensure community participation in setting vulnerability criteria through focused groups discussions and key informants’ interviews. NCA will ensure age-gender sensitive approach, prioritizing the needs, barriers and vulnerability of women headed households and elderlies. NCA will pay households visits to assess the households against the developed vulnerability criteria. Accountability will be ensured through continues consultation and feedback collection from the communities including complaints related to PSEA. Feedback and complaints mechanism will be available in the intervention areas during the project cycle. The cash assistance will be provided through pre-selected financial service provider in line with NCA criteria that ensures suitability of financial service provider capacity to deliver the cash taking into consideration protection and gender related sensitivity. Risks associated with project implementation is mapped and risk mitigation measures are developed to ensure activities are not interrupted nor causing harm to the targeted communities.
