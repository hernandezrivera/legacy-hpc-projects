Project Summary

Biu town is the focal point of Biu Local Government Area (LGA) in southern Borno State of Nigeria. It lies on latitude 10° 36' 39.96" N and longitude12° 11' 42.00" E The town is the administrative center of the LGA and was once the capital of the Biu kingdom, and is now capital of the Biu Emirate. It lies on the Biu Plateau at an average elevation of 626 meters. The region is semi-arid. There has been a decline in the population from 246, 900 to 105,128 in 2021. Over the years, due to the insurgency in the North East, Nigeria, the town have been littered with dirts with no trashcans within the metropolis. The citizens have minimal or no sensitization about proper waste management, which have led to uncontrolled littering.
The sanitation situation in Biu Town poses great treat to the health of humans and livestock. Hence, the urgent need for this project.
The project is subdivided into three (3) phases namely:
1. Cleaning of all the major streets in Biu Town
2. Placement of Trashcans
3. Sensitization of the masses on proper waste management.
The main purpose of the project is to intervene in maintaining a clean and sustainable healthy environment for the citizens of the Biu Town.
The project targets Biu Town as the focal point of execution.

1 - Needs

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:The rapid population decline, and decline in community living standards has been a major contributor to generation rate of municipal solid waste in Biu Town. This assessment examines the waste management activities of Borno State Environmental Protection Agency (BOSEPA) in Biu Town (our project target area). It was found that most waste generatedis from domestic activities with 69% majority of the respondents constituting 45% pointed garbage as the type of waste generated and the results also indicated that majority of the respondents are using plastic containers representing about 43%, while 42% of the respondents disposed waste openly. With respect to the expectation of BOSEPA, 48% admitted that regular collection of refuse is not carried out by BOSEPA as expected (Bala, 2021). The assessment shows that Biu Town is lacking waste collection points, trashcans and the respondents hardly see BOSEPA staff in the few available collection points created by the respondents for refuse collection. IMPACT:Our assessment also showed that the state of the community is having direct impact on the health of the respondents. According to a report by the Borno State Ministry of Health on 23/12/2021, 113 suspected cases of Cholera was identified. Results from WHO's Early Warning, Alert and Response System (EWARS) week 30 report from 223 sites, (including 32 IDP camps) show that malaria was the leading cause of morbidity and mortality accounting for 35% of cases and 46% of reported deaths. In addition, results from the Nigeria Humanitarian Response Strategy (NHRS 2019-2021) indicate 7.1million people are in dire need of healthcare and 6.2million are targeted for immediate attention. The assessment also showed that due to the unhealthy situation of the streets, resulting to illnesses, businesses are also affected. The assessment indicates that the health and business situations of the respondents will greatly improve within the shortest possible time of the project implementation. VITAL NEEDS OF THE COMMUNITY:Our assessment showed that the current situation of the community has more to do with unkempt streets/drainages, inadequate food, inadequate clean water. CRITICAL CONSTRAINTS:Our assessment also indicated that our project in Biu Town will require adequate security of the lives and properties of the workers, transportation and logistics due to the poor road network into the community. RESPONSE PLAN:Currently, there are no visible intervention in the area of placement of trashcans and cleaning of the drainages in the community. The environmental protection board (Biu) is currently working on Tuesdays and Fridays to clear the streets of litters. The response rate is slow according to our assessment. Consequently, we shall carry out: (1) Cleaning of the drainages across the streets: This intervention shall drastically minimize mosquitoes and other infectious insect's breeding across the community. Hence, minimising the rate of Malaria spread/infection and other related illnesses.(2) Sweeping of the streets in order to eradicate litters: This will provide a very neat and appealing environmental sight. Non-biodegradable and biodegradable substances like nylons, plastics, bottles, nails, papers, glasses which poses danger to the soil shall be properly cleaned off the streets.(3) Placement of minimum of 200 trashcans in strategic locations of the streets: This will produce a proper way of bin disposal to the citizens. This will consequently, ease the work of the environmental protection board in waste disposal.(4) Sensitization of the citizens across the wards on proper waste management: This will create environmental awareness. CONCLUSION:With this assessment and response plan, the critical needs of the citizens will be met. In order to also improve the living standards of the citizens, AFCSO shall engage the services of indigenous volunteers. The project shall also absorb the indigenes into the staff team.

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

The project shall ensure marginalised community's/minority groups, including people with disability are included in consultations at all phases. This shall be done through physical interactions, questionnaires issued to the respondents and through focus group discussions. Women and girls are a major part of our consultations and decision-making process. We have equal treatment for all gender. Each time comprises of not less than 50% of women and ladies. We project that inter-community conflict over access to resources will not arise due to the fact that the project does not supply resources to individuals. The resources (trashcans, sweeping equipments, boots, gloves, etc) are for the implementation of the project. Sexual exploitation and abuse shall be properly and strictly addressed using its provision in the Human Resource Manual (HRM). All such acts are a violation of this Act.vii. Recurring acts, which may have a cumulative effect or even a single incident, would be considered enough to establish sexual or gender based harassment. viii. 9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

Accountability to affected people (AAP) is an integrated approach that facilitates the accountability of donors and partner organisations to the affected population. With this approach, improveed efficiency and effectiveness of the humanitarian response is guaranteed by creating greater transparency and a platform to facilitate full engagement of the affected population. This will create a better understanding of problems on the ground and the formulation of humanitarian actions and plans that respond to real needs and build the resilience of affected communities. As part of the organization's core values, AFCSO’s regards accountability as her main quality framework and management operating standards. Consequently, AFCSO shall ensure accountability through the following mechanisms: (1) AFCSO shall hold quarterly town hall meetings with community stakeholders to get feedback on project activities and recommendations. (2) AFCSO shall establish feedback and response mechanisms across the wards through (a)the use of help desks during crucial activities to receive complaints and feedback from beneficiaries and concerned community members (b) the use of AFCSO toll-free hotline (08133450954/07048101806) which will be managed by AFCSO focal persons to receive feedback/complaints/recommendations; provide resolution or referrals as appropriate. This will be made available to community members and posted in each community, to enable the community present complaints and/or feedback. (3) Information from the accountability feedback systems shall be analyzed on a monthly basis and shared with the senior management team for discussion and decision making to adjust program activities in response to the local context, proposal development and knowledge generation.(4) AFCSO will ensure proper sensitization of the need for feedback. Forms/questionnaires, individual and focus group discussions shall shall serve as key media media for collection of feedback.
