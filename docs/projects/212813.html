Project Summary

need: (main findings)
1- The situation of growing insecurity is exacerbated by a combination of factors, including economic crises, conflict, aftermath of earthquakes, and restricted access to services and opportunities. This crisis disproportionately impacts women and girls, with many expressing that in Syria, they no longer feel safe anywhere.
2-The normalization and intensification of gender-based violence (GBV) are apparent both at home and in public areas, including camps and other environments. The community experiences pervasive violence, indicating an alarming trend of escalating and spreading incidents of violence.
3-There is a concerning divergence between the increasing need for humanitarian assistance and GBV services and the decreasing availability of these resources due to funding cuts and program closures.
4-The reduction or absence of GBV services and women and girls' safe spaces (WGSS) exacerbates feelings of insecurity and isolation among those affected, while also diminishing their opportunities to seek necessary support.
5-Limited transportation options have further restricted access to essential services, thereby heightening safety risks for women and girls, and contributing to their increased isolation and lack of voice in addressing their concerns.
Intervention.
- Provide prevention activities organized (recreational and awareness raising sessions).
- provide awareness sessions on GBV topics.
- provide awareness sessions on PSS topics (group sessions and individuals).
- Provide case management services for GBV survivors and at risk.
- Provide RH services and Education group sessions and individuals (MMT).
- Provide life skills and vocational trainings.
implementation:
project services will be provided through:
- two WGSS centers (one in Jaramana and the other one in Qudseia.
- two integrated mobile teames provide GBV and RH services in Jaramana and qudseia.
Protection Risk Analysis
The most important protection risks in the intervention areas:
- Inequalities in access to services provided
Exposure to physical violence and gender-based violence
- Exposure to assault or exploitation
- Hard to reach
Risk mitigation measures:
- Consultation sessions with residents on mechanisms to mitigate potential risks,
- Clear Reporting mechanism published in working sites including Hotline and Complaint box.
- Conducting awareness sessions on protection risks and mitigations
- Training project staff to acquire the proper knowledge of PSEA rules and regulations and its application with regular refreshments.
- Monitoring and Evaluation.
