Project Summary

the project is planned to be implemented in NES- DEZ and Al-hasakeh governorates for two years, to
address the needs for protection services for the most vulnerable communities from IDPs,
Returnees, host communities from women and men, boys and girls, and PWD. there will be
establishing CCs in both Al-hasakeh and DEZ for in and outreach proposed below services; the project includes general protection service for 6000 people from women and men, boys and girls, PWD and elders: by1-improve and raises awareness of the target communities on. 2-establishing referral-supported pathways in DEZ and Al-hasakeh and ensuring they received their referred needs with safe access and quality service. Under GBV: 1-the project includes Case management “CM”services SGBV for 60 cases, IPV received CM training from NES-PWG and has 3 years experience in CM and has the capacity to have opened cases under medium and high risk. 2-vocational training programs for 250 women and girls who are above 17 yrs. provision of structured PSS services for 250 women. Under CP: PSS Sessions 1000 girls, boys under 18 yrs. 400 children will be provided with arts and creative sessions for boys and girls. Parenting programs and mother and toddler program for 100 BNFs. Distribution for dignity kits for women and girls who are participating in the proposed activities for at least 2000BNFs for women and girls and adolescents above 16yrs, the kits are not stand-alone activity but it is complementary for the proposed activities, distribution process will be in CC, by women staff and coding system to avoid any exposure to further harm for BNFs, post-distribution monitoring will be conducted to measure BNFs satisfaction and process. Distribution of PSS kits for children from Boys and Girls who are under 18Yrs for at least 1000 BNfs. And newborn kits for Vulnerable mothers and toddlers for 400BNFs.
the project emphasizes building the capacities of the working staff and actors in the area under protection mainstreaming, GBV core concepts, gender, and disability inclusion, PSEA mandatory training. facilitation skills, PSS, CM, safe identification, and referral training. the project mainstream CP and GP by CSG, PSEA are reflected in mandatory COC for all the working staff. BNfs participation and feedback mechanisms will be addressed through complaint boxes, satisfaction surveys, ME visits.
the project will be implemented with full coordination with NES protection working groups and protection coordination admin offices in NES to avoid duplication in the services provided. the project will take COV-19 outbreak by ensuring awareness-raising, PPE, 8 people per session. and the established center will be cleaned and sterilized on a daily basis, water availability in project centers for safe WASH services. all the project activities will address the inclusion of PWD from women, men, and girls who are under 18. IPV is implementing such protection services three years ago in NES and well know ME tools to ensure proposed quality, like IASC tools for CM and Referral services, all the safety measures, and must have consent before any targeting on BNFs. ISP and data protection is the core of the work. the center will be safe accessed for women and girls by ensure no entrance for men of women shift, the full staff of women.
The project activities will be implemented under full coordination with the working actors
and coordination admin offices in the area to avoid any duplication in the efforts. the
addressed needs are reflected by the diverse needs of women, men, children boys and
girls under 18, PWD and elders. the project is designed and will be implemented under the
DO-NO HARM principle and the humanitarian principles and standards to provide assistance in quality, equity, and gender equality. the proposed activities are reflected by community participation and consultations. 4 - Needs

The project aims to address the protection concerns In NES under GBV-PSEA-CP-GP. According to ,HNO22 and Voices from syria2022: women and girls are particularly vulnerable to gender-based violence because of gender inequality and discrimination in Syria. Women consequences responsibilities do not automaticallytranslate into decision making power. This contributes to increased pressure and stressfor women to provide for their families, often in absence of any other support from familymembers. Consequently, they are exposed to increased risks of violence against themwithin the family and at their workplace. Based on MSNA2022 HH survey; 45% of NESpeople aren’t receiving humanitaria assistance mostly due to: not aware of any, lack ofinformation, and access issues further to being not eligible,etc. % people from children/HH members show signs of anxiety, psychosocial distress, are 39%boys, 42% girls,70%women and 67%men. 76% of the HH reported at least 1 member is suffering frompsychosocial distress among Nes. through IPV experience in DEZ, rate of CP concerns:Physical abuse, child labour, and forced early marriage has been increased. further to badlivelihood situation espcaillly covid-19 impact which increased risks exposure for womenand children and negative coping mechanisms.

5 - Implementation of activities

The project activities are:1- improve and raise awareness of the target communities on the needed protection aspects as shown in the needs assessment and HNO 2021 which is related to improve the community awareness for child protection concerns, PSEA, GBV core concepts and enhance information dissemination for the services provided by the working actors in the areas through updated and live service map documents, 2-establishing referral supported pathways in DEZ and Al-hasakeh and ensuring they received their referred needs with safe access and quality service with transportation service and in-kind assistance which will be determined according to disability level, vulnerability statues”women headed household, child-headed household, SGBV survivals” taking into most consideration ISP to avoid any expose for further risks. Under GBV: CM”services SGBV for 60 cases including all the stages of CM process , ensure coordination with service providers in the targeted areas to have the maximum benefits for the case including in-kind assistance, vocational training programs for 250 women and girls who are above 17 yrs, for literacy and numeracy and other vocations from sewing, knitting, etc. PSS and arts sessions for children, parenting, and distribution kits for women and children. Community consultations and feedback is part of the implementation. working staff will be from local women and men taking into account social norms. the project includes the safety and security of the CC.
