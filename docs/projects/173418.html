So, we have planned to providing skill development traning to ensure skilled targetted beneficiaries and providing agricultaral materials. Project will support to establish Comprehensive Homestead Development and poultry farming and goat rearing to engage the women to reduce their extra load od care work and involvement with economic activities which will increase their income to lead a better life along with nutrition . Economic involvement will make them honoured to their families ans society.Project will provide cash for site development for targetted 4200 beneficiaries of rohingya refugess and cash for work for 3600 beneficiaries of host communities in the lean period.

2 - Activities or outputs

"Output-1: Enhance household ability to fulfil nutritious food diversity and choice through utilizing homestead garden.Project will support establishment of vegetable and fruits gardens, Small scale poultry/livestock in homestead areas. Selected 9,600 beneficiaries will receive 2 days training on comprehensive homestead development (CHD) and also receive agricultural materials to implement different Comprehensive Homestead Development (CHD) interventions.""Output-2: Introduce and establish poor people friendly Income generating activities (IGAs) to increase household level income.Based on the local demand and existing practice project will support the target beneficiary to establish different income generation activities that inspire female engagement."Output-3: Reduce lean period of vulnerable household through Cash for work.Output-4: Providing agricultural materials for poultry farming and goat rearing for host communities as they increasing their current income.Output-5: Targeted community members and service providers are better prepared for, mitigate and respond to disaster and adapt to climate change. Food Security Disaster contingency systems are in place and functioning.
