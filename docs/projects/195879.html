Project Summary

Maram seeks to save lives, uphold rights, and promote access to key institutions and services through providing WASH services in particular. So, MF in Turkey office is remotely managing the delivery of integrated WASH assistance with main-streamed protection and advocacy in NWS. Basic WASH assistance is delivered to the targeted community by taking into account the different needs of men, women, boys, and girls, in addition, taking into consideration the special needs of disabled people in all the project activities, especially during the infrastructure designs.
Due to the huge need of WASH intervention in the NWs and referring to emergency needs in addition to camps management and LCs request in order to provide the main WASH services in Idleb and Aleppo through INGOs and NGOs, Maram Foundation seeks to provide emergency needs as much as possible to help the people in need in different locations at Idleb and Aleppo with coordination and collaboration by WASH cluster, local authorities, and camps management in the area.
MF is applying for HPC to provide WASH response in many locations in Idleb and Aleppo as a result of the large influx of IDPs and limited access to services and to provide the emergency response as part of Cholera prevention and response in NWS.
MF currently has two current WASH projects to provide around 20,000 HHs /100,000 individuals living in 9 camps and 8 communities in Idleb and Aleppo governorate including basic WASH services, MF is applying for HPC to continue the current intervention and provided the emergency WASH services in 2023, in addition, Maram will constantly be targeting new arrivals at all the project's phases.
The provided WASH activities will be drinking water (by operating the water stations in the most locations and using the water trucking in the emergency cases only), SWM, de-sludging latrine septic tanks, construction or rehabilitation latrines, rehabilitation of sanitation networks &water networks, waste water treatment, Hygiene kits distribution (including Cholera kit), and hygiene awareness sessions. The project design will be according to SPHERE standards and WASH guidance in line with current priorities. MF, wherever possible, will provide the proposed WASH activities using the most sustainable solutions such as the establishment of Water pumping stations and water & sewage networks, but nevertheless and when necessary,
Furthermore, the planned intervention will mainstream protection principles especially (Do no harm) throughout the project cycle by considering the different needs of men, women, young children, children, adolescents, young adults, middle-aged adults, and older adults. The provision of sanitation services will be consulted with the targeted camps and communities, and take into account the specific needs of each group. MF also revised the content of hygiene kits by involving the women and girls through Focus Group Discussions (FGDs) during previews implemented projects.
The Project also designed to provide disabilities, the elderly, and another vulnerable group with accessible handicap infrastructure too by taking these groups needs into consideration during the design phase of these infrastructures, with particular focus on vulnerable people such as ‘People with Special Needs’, in addition, to provide access to proper gender-sensitive and disability WASH facilities in camps, schools, child-friendly spaces, and health facilities.
The main impact of the proposed project will be reducing the cholera outbreak and any other diseases among the beneficiaries by providing potable drinking water and the other WASH activities in NWS in addition to providing sustainable solutions for water supply and sanitation which will lead to reducing the operational cost of supplying water.
Maram will take the recommendations of the WASH cluster into consideration throughout the project phases.

3 - Needs

Based on the HRP 2022 report, "WASH needs of affected populations will continue to be high across 2022 with sector partners expected to provide quality emergency WASH services and maintain WASH facilities and infrastructure. WASH needs will largely be influenced by, but not limited to: Water scarcity and drought-like conditions ".In addition to the secondary data provided from CCCM and WASH clusters in addition to HNO, HRP, and REACH reports and as a basic data resource, MF conducted RNA in Oct 2022. The reported percentages in MF RNA reflect the poorly available WASH services in 15 IDP sites and communities in NWs, as the rapid and immediate lifesaving water services, as well as access to basic sanitation during acute displacement, remains a critical priority for many people. One of the priority needs identified by the MF assessment is access to adequate latrines and regular access to safe drinking water.Therefore, as part of MF WASH response in 2023, the proposed project will seek to improve the living condition for 100,000 individuals living in NWs by providing adequate and safe WASH services for the most vulnerable people, affected by displacement and conflicts.The main impact of the project will be reducing the cholera outbreak and any other diseases among the beneficiaries by providing potable drinking water in Idleb in addition to providing sustainable solutions for water supply and sanitation which will lead to reducing the operational cost of supplying water.

4 - Implementation of activities

Implementation plan: All the WASH activities will be implemented according to SPHERE standards and WASH cluster guidelines, through coordination with camps management and local authorities. As well, all the technical studies will be prepared including construction studies, drawings, BOQs .. .according to the needs priorities.- Maram will hold coordination meetings with the targeted camps management and local councils to explain Maram's WASH intervention, update the needs and IDPs numbers.- Regarding the daily activities (water supply, septic tank de-sludging, garbage collection..etc) all studies will be prepared in advance in order to start providing the service at beginning of the project period.- While for the technical studies (rehab sanitation network...etc) all the tenders will be prepared after starting the project according to Maram's policy.- Furthermore, during the project life, monitoring will be conducted at every stage of the project duration by collecting routine data and analysis on a continuous basis, in addition to evaluating the services provided by Maram to evaluate the service and find out how it met the needs of beneficiaries of different ages.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

There are several protection risks as follows: 1- Toilets represent GBV and/or health, safety risk; Access to toilets not available for all; Likelihood: High, Impact: High; MM: Ensure context/SPHERE standards and closely monitor these provisions/aspects during implementation. Ensure women and adolescent girls are consulted during the design, implementation and monitoring phase of projects. Accountability mechanisms are put in place and received feedback from users is followed up. Lessons from previous experience are shared and learned. 2- Potential exposure to explosive hazard (IED/UXO/ERW) during assessment/ rehabilitation of wastewater infrastructure (WWTP, sewer networks). Likelihood: Medium, Impact: High; MM: Work closely with Mine Action actors in the area and conduct assessments/rehabilitation works only in areas declared as cleared; Conduct safety training with staff and contractors. 3- Health risks associated with construction works and exposure to raw sewage and gases. Likelihood: High, Impact: Medium Mitigating Measure: Ensure staff/partners/contractors involved in construction works or working with raw sewage wear appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) and follow established safety regulations; Conduct/repeat safety training for staff/partners/contractors.


8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

MF will Implement training on PSEA to be mandatory for all personnel. Periodically, with the support of Field Managers, Focal Points organize a training session for recruited staff, ideally within three months of recruitment. MF will incorporate an attitudes and beliefs session in the training as many respondents highlighted thisas the key obstacle to reporting. Maram team will ensure that the affected communities are informed on: I. The definition of sexual exploitation and abuse and about Complaints mechanism and information-sharing efforts and scaled up where possible II. The standards of conduct for humanitarian workers. III. Humanitarian workers have a right to humanitarian assistance without being subjected to SEA IV. Where to report Complaints &SEA incidents (all possible options/channels). V. What services are available, including complaint confidentiality assessments, and how to access them. VI. What to expect after making a complaint, including potential referrals, timeframes, and the roles, responsibilities, and any limitations of actors involved. VII. What steps the CBCM (Community-Based Complaints Mechanism) and the concerned agency will take to ensure safety and confidentiality
