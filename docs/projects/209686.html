Project Summary

Sudan is among the top four countries globally with the highest prevalence of global acute malnutrition estimated at 13.6%. Following the conflict in May2023,the acute malnutrition People in Need increased by over13%from 4.0M in January to over 4.5M in May.SAHARI project is intended to deliver sustainable life-saving&preventive interventions to the vulnerable affected population including host communities, internally displaced people,&refugees with prioritizing the youngest&most vulnerable children aged 6–59Ms with the highest mortality risk,&pregnant&lactating women into two states among the greater Darfur region which are North Darfur (Ailliet, El Kuma, & Mellit)&Central Darfur(Zalingei,Wadi Salih &Benedisi). Among the 6 targeted localities 4 were ranked 5&2 localities were ranked 4 for Child WHZ<-2 (GAM)by the nutrition cluster. In Central Darfur state about 77/1,000 number of children who die before their 5th birthday (National: 68),54.6% percentage of girls married before the age of 18 (National 38%),47.5%chronic malnutrition(severe&moderate)prevalence among children U5(National: 38.2%),17.8%GAM(Global Acute Malnutrition)prevalence among children U5(National: 16.3%),108,427 number of children U5 suffering from GAM (National: 3.1 million), 387,029 number of people projected at IPC phase 3&4 (Oct 2022 – Feb 2023) (National: 7.7 million),50.6% percentage of people with access to improved drinking water (National: 68%)(UNOCHA:Central Darfur State Profile-March2023).Generally, in central Darfur prevalence of food insecurity increased, the prevalence of poor food consumption starkly among IDPs(Food Security Monitoring System (FSMS)-2021). As the food availability will be affected especially with high inflation rates are expected to result in high cost of production of agricultural products &high food prices.In addition to the high prevalence of malnutrition in the state, therefore SAHARI project aimed to provide life-saving humanitarian assistance to meet these extensive needs for adequate,safe&nutritious food&support in building resilience&targeting the most vulnerable conflict affected population including host communities, internally displaced people&refugees.North Darfur state’s nutrition indicters are among the worst in Sudan(MICS 2014).Given that Sudan has witnessed a continued decline in under-five mortality rates, it is of great concern that North Darfur is the only state that recorded a sharp increase (of 69 to 90 deaths per1,000 live births which is way above the Sudan average of 68) between 2010&2014.The state also has low performance in water&sanitation coverages (of 50%& 12%against Sudan averages of 68%&33%respectively), which are critical to child survival. In addition, neonatal mortality remained high at around 44 deaths per 1,000 live births, denoting poor maternal&early childhood health care.Although the Government of Sudan, international & national partners, communities &families made a noteworthy effort to address these low indicators, but still many efforts are needed to protect people’s rights particularly the most vulnerable groups, helping them meet their fundamental needs&increasing their chances of achieving their full potential. In the aftermath of the conflict's onset, nutrition services in Sudan are being influenced by factors such as the influx of internally displaced persons, conflict dynamics,&challenges in service accessibility. Between January&July2023, there has been a noticeable decline in admissions to Outpatient Therapeutic Programs &Supplementary Feeding Centers in the greater Darfur, Kordofan&Khartoum regions compared to the average for the same months in 21&22.The objectives of SAHARI project are very much aligned with the 2024HRP cluster strategic objectives, as it will intended to contribute in the reduction of nutritional impact of shocks on vulnerable people through implementation of specific activities under each HRP-SO, which clearly mentioned in the cluster Activities part.

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

The project services will be accessible to avoid exposing the all beneficiaries, particularly women, to any risks. Services will be provided at high quality standards and ensure dignity and rights of beneficiaries to food and nutrition services. The food and nutrition awareness and promotion will consider the culture of the beneficiaries and respect their values and any language barriers. The project will ensure that the provided interventions are acceptable to the community and they take part in it through involvement of community committees and community volunteers who are acceptable to the community. Disseminate GBV key messages through nutrition outreach and services (where GBV services are available), Information on the services provided is posted at nutrition facilities in accessible formats and local languages.SAHARI will apply the gender equality in the project required personal recruitment for both sites and community (Community Nutrition Volunteers) base. Both girls and boys will have equitable access to Acute Malnutrition treatment services and admission data disaggregated to track access by gender, as well will ensure all people (women, men, girls and boys and vulnerable groups) are equally and fairly get the chance of participation and access for other project interventions such as the food assistance and SOCIAL AND BEHAVIORAL CHANGE COMMUNICATION (SBCC) interventions, and pay special attention to individuals and groups who may be particularly vulnerable or have difficulty accessing services.

4.1 - How will the project share information and collect community feedback? Please select as many as relevant.

4.1.1 - How will the project use community feedback to improve and adapt activities? (open-ended/free text, required)4

SAHARI under this project will maintain a complaint and feedback mechanism, The targeted communities feedback data about the services will be collected on regular-based as this data will be part from this project M&E framework. the data will be collected, cleaned triangulated analysis and all analysis recommendations will be translated into corrective actions to improve and adapt activities of this projects to be accepted by the community.

4.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will support Inter-Agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including Sexual exploitation and abuse complaints?

Ensuring SAHARI interventions are designed, implemented and monitored with the equitable and meaningful participation of all people and to achieve the effective participation SAHARI through the community component work will building the trust with leaders and their communities. And ensure to understanding of the barriers that prevent some individuals from having their voices heard, through foster reflection, dialogue and change, particularly with men and boys as they play a critical role in supporting the equitable participation of women and girls in humanitarian response programming. SAHARI through this project will ensure the full involvement and participation of targeted affected communities in all project cycle stages, as well will work on empowerment of those communities with knowledge and boost their ability to take discussion toward the services provided to them and express their views and complain is ensure the success of the response and build their capacities and resilience.
