Project Summary

The 2020 monsoon season in Pakistan triggered severe flash flooding in various regions of Pakistan, most significantly in various districts of Sindh. The recent two shocks i.e the COVID-19 outbreak and the heavy flooding enhanced food insecurity and malnutrition in the province aggravated the already deteriorated situation of the already poverty-stricken population of Badin and Sanghar. Recently, ACTED conducted an early recovery needs assessment in target district Sanghar and found that monthly average expenditures are higher against average monthly income of households, where Sanghar stands at 188% in over expenses. Assessment results indicate that 60% and 59% of total interviewed households are under debt in district Sanghar and Badin, respectively. The assessment also found that more than 50% of HHs reported taking a loan to meet their food needs which was followed by health expenses, agriculture inputs, livestock, education, house repair, and clothing for family members.
For this project, ACTED will intervene in South Sindh, and more specifically in the districts Sanghar and Badin. The UCs and villages of intervention will be selected after a careful needs assessment and in consultation with other humanitarian stakeholders operating in these areas, to avoid overlap and duplication and ensure that all needs are met effectively. Depending on the needs and the vulnerability of population reported through a first village-based assessment.
For the purpose of this project, the activities are based on livelihood restoration support in the form of cash for work activities under selected CFW Schemes on infrastructural rehabilitation. This activity will create short-term employment opportunities for the affected communities that have lost their sources of livelihood owing to the floods. The proposed intervention will also provide multipurpose cash grants to the most vulnerable families and facilitate them in fulfilling their basic needs, including food and non-food items, and ensuring their sustenance and livelihoods, both on farm and off farm.

1- Project Activities

Activity No. 1: Infrastructure rehabilitation and Cash for Work ActivitiesACTED will engage 1190 HHs (8330 individuals) from the target areas in cash for work activity under selected CFW Schemes on infrastructural rehabilitation during the lean period. This activity will create shot-term employment opportunities for the affected communities that have lost their sources of livelihood owing to flood. The proposed intervention will help individuals from vulnerable HHs to earn an income to meet their basic needs, preventing them from adopting negative coping mechanisms as well as to reduce the impact of recent floods. Under this activity ACTED will engage the beneficiaries in agricultural infrastructure work such as distilling of irrigation channels, paths and agricultural plots. The cash for work will be in 3 cycles spanning over 14 days for unskilled labour and 7 days for skilled days for 8000 PKR each. 119 CFW tool kits will be distributed to the beneficiaries - this tool kit will include spades, wheelbarrow, steel bucket, water cooler, manual hand compacter, armada, and steel pan under the proposed intervention. According to a recent food basket rate survey conducted by ACTED, the average monthly household food basket cost is PKR 9659. Hence, through this activity, ACTED will engage the beneficiaries for 14 days and pay PKR 8000 (monthly: PKR 16000), thereby allowing each HHs to meet their food needs. Activity No. 2: Selection of CFW SchemesCash for work schemes will be selected jointly by ACTED and target communities through a participatory process. Village Committees will be invited to conduct consultations and identify priority needs in their communities in terms of agricultural infrastructure. The proposed works will then be reviewed by ACTED technical staff, who will assess the feasibility and potential impact of the schemes identified by communities. On the basis of this assessment, the final decision will be taken jointly by ACTED staff and Village Committees. The rehabilitated works will aim to benefit at least 1290 households (9030 individuals). Activity No. 3: Provision of Multipurpose Cash Grants to Vulnerable CommunitiesTo mitigate increased vulnerability, the proposed activity entails the distribution of multipurpose conditional cash grants (PKR 15,000 per HH) in one go to the 970 worst affected families in Badin and Sanghar. These conditional cash grants will facilitate the vulnerable HHs in fulfilling their basic needs, including food and non-food items, ensuring their sustenance and livelihoods, both on farm and off farm.
