Project Summary

This project will provide lifesaving emergency support to improve shelter conditions of internally displaced persons and returnees in Mocimboa da Praia and Chiure districts in Cabo Delgado Province, who remain in vulnerable situations due to ongoing conflict. The project will include trainings and technical support for local artisans and youth to guarantee the resilience.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

NFI kits and emergency shelter kits will be distributed and shelter training and technical support will be supported in Mocimboa da Praia district where returnees live, ensuring safe shelter construction for returnees. Shelter reconstruction kits for repair and improvement will provided to IDPs who stay in resettlement sites in Chiure district. The project will train local artisans in Chiure and trained artisans will lead the shelter rehabilitation through Cash for Work in Chiure district to improve or upgrade IDPs existing shelters. All activities include a mix of displaced and host communities and be age, gender, and diversity sensitive.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Shelter interventions and distributions of non-food items will focus on saving lives, guaranteeing the rights to safety and physical integrity of affected populations, do not harm principle, safety and dignity, effective access to services and assistance, accountability of affected populations and community participation and empowerment. At the beginning of the project, a community mass meeting will be held to understand their needs, explain selection criteria and the assistance package. During the project implementation period, opinion boxes will be located at each project site where communities can anonymously submit complaints and requests. Also, a free hotline system called “Linha verde (1458)” would be informed to beneficiaries. Protection awareness sessions will be included in all project activities and promoted at all levels. All the complaint will be handled by the focal points at the field level. When necessary PWJ HQ would provide technical expertise on investigation and protection of the beneficiaries. PWJ will thoroughly manage and protect the confidentiality of information collected and take sufficient measures to ensure that victims/survivours or communities are not exposed to danger. All requests or complaints will be taken action to respond within 48 hours.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

At the beginning of the project, every household in the project target area will be visited and assessed to identify and prioritised women headed households, elderly people and people with disabilities. Focused group discussions will be held to understand their specific needs to guarantee access to safe shelters.PWJ will consider gender for activistas selection so that women and vulnerable people can easily express their needs. Also, the project will support women’s leadership in the monitoring of shelter construction/rehabilitation and to strengthen women’s capacity. PWJ will discuss with community leaders and identify the safe places where NFI kits distribution will be taken place. The distribution will be conducted during the day-time and pregnant women and vulnerable people (including elderly people) will be prioritized to receive the kits.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Led by PWJ staffs who have received training on disability inclusion, the project will respond to the needs of people with disabilities throughout its project duration.As mentioned above, people with disabilities will be identified as vulnerable people when women and elderly people are identified, and their specific needs also will be identified.The constructed/rehabilitated shelters will be designed to be easily accessible to people with disabilities. Since it is difficult for people with disabilities to build/repair shelters by themselves, PWJ will work with local young people to construct/rehabilitate shelters for people with disabilities, which contributes to deepen their understanding of people with disabilities and build a cooperative system within the community.For disability inclusion, PWJ will encourage advocacy and capacity building for staffs working in Shelter sector. Monitoring and evaluation will pay special attention to people with disabilities so that appropriate action will be taken to meet the needs of people with disabilities.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

PWJ ensures an environmentally friendly manner during the project implementation;• Data collection, assessment, training and monitoring will be conducted as paperless as possible.• PWJ prioritizes the use of green materials and materials that can be reused or recycled.• After the shelter construction, all waste will be collected and returned to the original environment.• PWJ will ensure that emergency/household latrines are located far enough away from water sources to prevent water contamination.• In constructing the shelters, cutting down trees is avoided wherever possible, and materials that regenerate quickly, such as bamboo, are used instead.
