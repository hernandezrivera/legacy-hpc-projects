Project Summary

The work will focus on improving the lives of orphans, street children women and providing resources for the families affected by the conflict and flood, especially to improve their livelihoods and protect the life of the children.
Yusufari is one of the Local Government that has Child protection concern than any other Local Government in Yobe State, and their land is not fertile enough, because their farms are not effective in farming systems, and also their animals are died as a result of drought.
Our target is to provide support and strengthen the capacity of women, Children, Disabled person's and the most vulnerable families with businesses and train them on how to improve their farming system and to create massive awareness on Child Protection concern and support the victims of protection related concern and provide a mitigation measures that will enable the communities to know how to handle, and refer the victims of protection and the perpetrator.
We believed by supporting the victims and empowering the caregivers we can be able to reduce the rate of child protection concern's within the targeted communities.
Our mission is to render the services that will impact the life of children, women and most vulnerable people, to be self-reliance, and their will be a peaceful coexistent among others within the society.

1 - Needs

The projects will address the issues of Child labour, Child Abuse, illiteracy and joblessness and also support the victims of Flood and IDPs in Yusufari Local Government area Yobe State, assessment revealed that the rate of Child abuse, Child labour, separated families issues and joblessness is always increasing in Yusufari, and we can only overcome the situation through creating mass awareness on child protection and Gender Based Violence GBV, and to improve the living standard of women and the vulnerable people through empowerment opportunity, I believe with this we will be able to change the life of many Children, Women and the most vulnerable people in the society

2 - Activities

Our activities will includes but not limited to:Mass campaign on Child Protection concernCapacity building: strengthening Women, persons with disability Youth and elderly ones on skills acquisition and self reliance Agricultural production: to train farmers, women, Youth, persons with disability and elderly on modern farming systems and how to improve their farming skillsFood processing: To support women on food making and equip them with necessary income generating or start up kits.Handcrafts and commercial activities: to train and support youth, women, and person with disability and support them in other to be self reliance for the benefits of their self and their societiesAccording to current indicator Yusufari Local Government area Yobe State has the high rate of Child protection concern and high rate of poverty.Their land is not fertile for farming system and rearing of animals, which is the causes of death of many children and women as a result of conflict, flood and hunger, many Children Women, Elderly and vulnerable families lost their lives, husband left their wife and Children.This projects is intended to enlighten the community on how to improve their farming systems, protect their Children, women and improve their economic status We believe this projects will go a long way to impacts their lives, and reduce the high level of poverty in the communities.Our organization are currently implementing Child protection, food security and Child Education in Yusufari Local Government area, as self funding, individual donations and collaborations, we believe if our application is approved we will be able to achieved our aims by changing many Children, Women, disable and most vulnerable people life, by supporting them emotionally and economically.
