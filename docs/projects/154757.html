Project Summary

Our organization is offering to intervention in (Anbar) province for the purpose of peace-building and conflict resolution as a result of the crises in these provinces after liberated from ISIS which left conflicts between the community and the families.
With the gradual return of families back to their places of origin, many communities throughout Iraq need to be subject to a comprehensive response going beyond lifesaving assistance and bridging the gap between humanitarian response and socio recovery and resilience because these communities suffer from many problems and social conflicts such as some tribes in Anbar prevented every family from returning to their home if the families with perceived affiliation to extremists and they considered all members of this family must be punished or killed regardless of whether he was a woman, a man or a child.
There are other conflicts based on intellectual contradictions or because he was a member of a specific tribe or he worked in some government departments ...etc.
This project, planned over a period of 12 months (First month for preparation), aims at alleviating conflicts and solving outstanding problems among members of society It also aims to reduce the risk of relapse and going again to a chaos and violent' conflict.
The project focuses on:
• Identify and strengthen sustainable conflict prevention tools to facilitate the resolution of tensions within society
• Promote the empowerment of IDPs, host community and returnees (particularly women) through active participation in peace-building and conﬂict resolution. The project will target 500 youth and community leaders in areas of displacement and of recent returns for the purpose of spreading the culture of dialogue and how to analyze and resolve conﬂicts and conﬂicts by peaceful means and without recourse to violence and force.

Activities

Our organization will regularly assess the security situation in the selected areas to ensure a safe implementation of the activities and ensure activities are implemented according to principles of Do-No-Harm; including avoidance of any behavior contrary to acceptable local norms and practices.Our organization participated in a joint meeting of tribal leaders in the selected areas and the reconciliation committee in the province and everyone expressed his desire for peace and reconciliation among the parties by peaceful means. 1- Individual meetings with the parties to the conflict. 2- Involve the parties to the conflict with awareness campaigns, educational and legal sessions.3- Joint meetings involving all parties to the conflict. 4- Target 500 community leaders and youth (especially women) for capacity building on peace-building and conﬂict resolution. The participants will be composed of IDPs returnees and members of host communities.. 5- Target 5,250 of girls, boys, women and men of both the IDPs and host communities in areas of displacement (camp and non-camp setting) and individuals from recent areas of return to participate in awareness raising activities. Activities targeting IDP and host communities, will bring together members of both communities during each event.6- Our organization will find linkages with governorate-level PWGs to determine which location to target the project activities from the targeted regions. 7- Print and distribute of brochures and posters 8- Field visits for monitoring and evaluation 9- Coordination with local councils in the targeted regions, Al Anbar Command Operations, education departments, universities and council of Anbar province

Protection Risk Analysis

1- Some IDPs are forced to return involuntarily to their regions and at times to conditions which are neither safe nor dignified. 2- There may be some risk to reach people in areas outside Government control especially in rural areas which are far away from the city center.3- Some camps do not allow beneficiaries the freedom of movement according to the need of the program, which puts them at risk in not implementing these instructions. 4- Some societal norms and traditions prevent the beneficiaries from speaking frankly and openness sometimes poses a real danger to them such as sexual assaults and others.

Mitigation Measures

1- As mentioned in our activities, we will work to ensure that people will have a good understanding protection and we will engaging the community to act as actors to advocate for Protection principles and rights. 2- All our activities will be subject to good security standards through coordination with local councils, security forces and tribal chiefs in the regions when implementing the activities3- Our organization will recruit the staff from the targeted regions and in the situation of closing the roads will not be a hindrance in the implementation of activities in the timing of the plans. 4- In all project activities, we will work according to the principle of confidentiality and our organization have professional staffs, they have worked in the Anbar communities and they have good knowledge of the traditions and customs there and what information is dangerous to their owners., so we can ensure that staff will treat this information in a discreet and confidential manner.

How is your project accountable to affected population

Our project accountable to affected population with the principles of (Neutrality, Transparence, Confidentiality and Non-discrimination). The project team will be working to ensure that the beneficiaries will know very well the services which must be provided to them in the project and clarifying the material possibilities of the project Affected people will participants in the result of the assessments, monitoring, and implementation, the project team will review the feedback of the affected people to check if the project activities have a response to their needs. This is done through direct interviews with them and receiving complaints and follow up the results and the project activities performance.Complaint mechanisms:As part of its commitment to accountability, SSORD will put in place a help desk, complaints and feedback mechanism through direct contact with the complainants or their direct review
