Project Summary

Over the last three years, Cameroon has tailored its planning methodology to the shift process from care and maintenance to a sustainable programming approach and from assistance response to protection response and inclusion.
The 2025 year is a logical continuation of the 2024’s initiatives. In this regard, the refugee response will engage in the 3D direction - Dialogue, Data, and Development - to ground the shift towards a sustainable programming approach. This approach prioritizes actions such as highlighting existing gaps to attract strategic partnerships, complementing actions with stakeholders to reach the needy, and fostering their inclusion in the plans and programmes supported by Government and other development actors. UNHCR will therefore emphasize collaboration with the Government and various stakeholders to implement the strategic vision by strengthening protection and coordination, enhancing self-reliance, and pursuing the search for durable solutions for the forcibly displaced persons.
The results framework linked to this refugee response plan is designed in line with the five strategic orientations of the High Commissioner, namely Protect, Assist, Include, Empower and Solve. It is also aligned with Humanitarian Response Plan, the UN Agenda on internal displacement, the United Nations Development Cooperation Framework (UNSDCF) and fits perfectly into the National Development Strategy SND 30.
For the year 2025 the project will target 425 241 refugees including 287 181 CAR, of which 53% are women and girls, and 54% children; and 125 313 Nigerians, of which 54% are women and girls, and 61% children.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

Following the situational analysis in the sub-region and Cameroon, UNHCR envisions to support its implement in the direction of 3D- Dialogue, Data and Development. The operation will continue the shift towards a need gaps-based approach. This approach prioritizes actions such as highlighting the existing gaps for partnerships and complementing actions with stakeholders to reach the needs of vulnerable displaced persons in Cameroon. The operation will prioritize the following:• Reinforce collaboration with the government and establish strategic partnerships by building the capacity of UNHCR’s line ministry – Ministry of External Relations (MINREX) to assume full responsibility of refugee protection in Cameroon. • Advocate for the socio-economic inclusion of forcibly displaced persons and stateless in local and national development plans so that they contribute to the economic development of the country of asylum. • Reinforce UNHCR’s role across the three areas of intervention, namely protection, coordination, and durable solutions.• Apply holistic approaches to address the protection challenges faced by refugees, following UNHCR's four pillars 1) registration and documentation, 2) protection environment, 3) education including professional training, and 4) decent jobs. • Reinforce humanitarian coordination by creating relevant staffing positions, working with other stakeholders and expand our partnerships to fill the gaps of our diminishing resources.• Rationalize the use of the available resources: fleet, purchases, assets, missions with emphasis on planning.• Diversify UNHCR’s funding sources to attract more funders, measure the impact of our actions and communicate for resource mobilization and accountability. • Enhance visibility through proper communication and planning while respecting UNHCR’s mandate. • Ensure the implementation of UNHCR’s impact areas are aligned to the UN Sustainable Development Cooperation Framework (UNSDCF), UN Sustainable Development Goals and Cameroon National Development Strategy (NDS) 2030. • Shift toward humanitarian approach to a transitional and sustainable approach to open doors for more funding. • Mainstream environmental protection in all actions.• Search for durable solutions to be reinforced through the provision of complementary pathways. • Improve the protection environment through advocacy and action for the domestication of the Kampala Convention, ratification of the 1954 and 1961 Conventions on statelessness.• Advocate for the implementation of the MoUs signed in 2024 with the Government ministries (Ministry of Social Affairs, The Ministry of Decentralization and Local Development, and Ministry of Women Empowerment and the Family) and capacitate them for effective inclusion of forcibly displaced persons and stateless.• Continue the process of transferring responsibility for registration, Refugee Status Determination (RSD) and documentation to the Government.• Advocate and support the creation of a national structure dedicated to the management of forcibly displaced persons and those at risk of statelessness. • Work to include forcibly displaced persons and those at risk of statelessness in national programs for GBV, child protection, person with specific needs (PSN), legal assistance, education and health, and promote access to durable solutions (repatriation, resettlement and complementary channels, naturalization).• Coordinate the Protection, the shelter/NFI Clusters and provide protection support to IDPs.• Advocate for the provision of documentation and the extension of the validity of the IDs issued by the government from two to more than five years.• Reduce the timeframe required to produce identification signed by UNHCR, while waiting for the government to issue national refugee ID cards.
