Project Summary

This proposed project is a continuation of an earlier project with modifications according to the needs of the local communities. The proposed project will support the creation of a safe space for community members, where community members can approach to express themselves, communicate and connect with each other. The space will be set up as a community center, mainly attracting children and women and providing them with a safe environment to express and express their needs, and also to conduct unstructured PSS activities and structured PSS activities tailored to the needs. The results will appear in the form of concrete plans that positively affect the community and meet its needs.
The organized psychological support sessions for women consist of five sessions, in each session a topic is presented according to the evidence used from the I deal and she deal . program
As for adolescent girls, there are five sessions as well. As for children, the number of sessions is 11 sessions
As for the awareness workshops, it will be one session for each target group
The comments received by Amal from previous sessions indicate that the current activities must continue without interruption in order to restore societal stability and peaceful coexistence. Attention should also be paid to activating individual sessions, especially for children with special needs, who are considered part of society and have the same rights as others.
It is also preferable to activate the role of MHPSS sessions on a larger scale in the four centers by employing specialized staff for this process.
A legal clinic can also be opened to provide legal awareness, which in turn can contribute to limiting or reducing negative and dangerous phenomena in society, such as underage and forced marriage also provide consultations in matters of issuing identification papers, marriage contracts, or purchase and sale contracts.

4 - Needs

In Syria, the population has gone through very difficult experiences that have caused severe consequences on physical and psychological wellbeing of individuals and the social fabric, more so with no effective child and women protection services available or easy to access. According to HRP 2020, children and women continue to be exposed to multiple protection risks, severe abuses and rights violations, child labour (including recruitment and use of children in combat and support roles), violence and female exploitation - including GBV, early marriage, and early pregnancy. Those difficulties are particularly strong for the returning community, with women and children once again most heavily affected. Stigmatisation and structural exclusion enhances the negative impact on the psycho-social wellbeing of this already disadvantaged group, consequently materialising in exacerbated integration and social cohesion. Amal aimed during the previous project to tackle all of these issues by engaging with the local community to understand their concerns, building a long term partnership with the civil society organization on the ground, ensuring all the voices are being heard, through data collection and analysis, monitoring evaluation, and by sharing outcomes.Contribute to the stability of eastern Deir Ez-Zur region by improving living conditions, raising the level of peaceful coexistence, and contributing to reducing violence in the targeted areas.

5 - Implementation of activities

We will create four community centers in eastern Deir ez-Zor in the areas of Al-Busira, Al-Tayana, Al-Susah and Al-Baghouz. Each community center will be responsible for implementing activities, providing support and receiving beneficiaries according to the planWe will have a cadre consisting of awareness workers, facilitators and psychosocial support workers to give activities and provide support to the beneficiaries within each center, and there will be communication between the four centers and between the organization's main office in the kiosk, which in turn will send reports directly to the headquarters

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

- Content of message is not fully adapted to the social and cultural environment and creates misunderstanding or even tensions and has repercussions on the organisation implementing the campaign - Media chosen is not the most effective in the specific environment/ community-Risk of inter or intra-community tension due to service delivery locations or types of services offered if one community is served and a neighbouring community not served/cannot access or if information offered is not consistent between communities as there may be perceptions of discrimination or favouritism. -The population targeted by the intervention is put at risk as a result of the location of the facility; lack of consideration for the accessibility to services (in terms of freedom of movement, safety and security for men, women, boys, girls, elderly persons with disabilities); lack of consideration of contextual information on the location and the local dynamics. Risk that existing traditional practices and community structures conflict with child protection principles (e.g. child rights messaging is perceive as a threat)- Impact of activity is minimal on children's protection - Risk to physical safety of child protection service providers if perceived to be interfering in family-based concerns- Challenges in measuring the impact of capacity development; compromised quality of trainings due to contextual constraints; high turnover of staff


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

- COVID-19 Related Challenges: Risk of COVID-19 and social distancing affect the project as well. To mitigate these challenges, Amal teams ensure that social distancing is maintained, masks and sanitizers are available at the centers for the staff and for the beneficiaries as well, and WHO and health authorities’ instruction are followed. To ensure a safe social distancing and to avoid crowds at the centers, Amal teams do not encourage the participants of the unstructured activities to visit the center regularly. - Difficulties in obtaining data from local authorities: Amal teams contacted the local authorities (including DoH) to obtain the number of the PWDs in the areas of intervention, although after a long discussion DoH provided a number in Susa and Bagouz, but the number was too general and did not provide a detail about the type and the level of the disability. They just mentioned that there are 450 PWDs in Susa and 425 in Bagouz. Amal will be depending on collecting data from the field by having a registeration form from each student in the school and for each child approach our centers. Amal foundation will work on enhancing the communciaiton channel with the authorities in order to receive solid information on the official numbers. - The Security Situation in the Intervention Areas: The security situation in the intervention areas still unstable (especially in Basira). During the project period incidents were happening between time to time.
