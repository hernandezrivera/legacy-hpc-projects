Project Name

Reduced food and nutrition insecurity amongst Vulnerable Host Communities and Returnees (women, men and youths) by building resilience through engagement in individual and communal assets creation in Income Generating Activities in Dikwa LGA, Borno State and Yusufari LGA, Yobe State.

Start Date

30/12/2019

End Date

29/12/2020

Project Summary

This will compliment the already proposed intervention to ensure that Covid-19 response and preparedness is integrated in all of CARE Internationals activities
Activities for the Covid-19 Integrated Response Include:
1. Training All beneficiaries targeted by the project. A total 7,000 HHs (Cash for Work – 5,000 HHs, VSLA – 200 Groups, IGAs – 2,000 HHs) will be trained on Covid-19 preparedness, prevention, hygiene awareness.
2. Installation of Hand Washing Points at the proposed social infrastructure sites (School and the Community Abattoir).
3. Training of all (30) staff on Coivd-19 Preparedness and Response.
4.Training of All Project Management Committee Members, Community Leaders, Volunteers etc…300
5. Provision of PPE for All 30 Staff
6. Hand wash Stations/Stands with water supply at All Communities – 20 Communities.

7. Distribution of Hand Wash - 10,000 pcs.
7. Distribution of Hand Sanitizers - 10,000 pcs.
8. Training of 200 VSLA Groups (500 Members) on Soap Production, Production of Hand Sanitizers, Hand Wash & Face Masks; supply of inputs for production.
9. Printing of IEC Materials, Posters, Leaflets; Radio Jingles targeting the entire population of the proposed intervention areas.

1 - Needs

Since the emergence of the index case of Covid-19 on 27th February 2020 as announced officially by Nigeria's Minister of Health, there has been increased cases recorded. As at 3rd May 2020 Nigeria has 2388 confirmed cases and 85 deaths recorded with already confirmed positive cases and deaths recorded in the North East. The Northeastern part of Nigeria which is ravaged by Insurgency since 2009 and has been trying to recover is facing double crisis with the emergence of Covid-19. With so many viulnerable HHs that depend on Humanitarian Aid coupled with high illeteracy levels, the North-East is a key area that requires undivided attention.Here, there is need to integrated Covid-19 response and preparedness activities to create awareness and sensitise the people on the virus and to equip them with preventive measures; how to identify suspected cases and what to do when you feel sick.
