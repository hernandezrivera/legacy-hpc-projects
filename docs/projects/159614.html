Project Summary

We are aiming to protect the health of the children by encouraging community women from the middle area in Gaza. We will conduct intervention by visiting homes, conducting awareness sessions, such as nutrition sessions, complementary breastfeeding sessions, or other awareness sessions.
Nutrition sessions are mostly with cooking demonstrations. Also, if we find a severe malnutrition/anemia, rickets case, we will refer the child to AEI clinic and keep follow-up the case until his/her health condition gets improved enough.To maintain its sustainability, we work with 60 women (20 women will be newly selected) from the four project areas and give them training to be the children's health advisors in each area.
The purpose of the activities are 1)to contribute to health improvement of children less than two years old and to save children's life before it's too late, regarding health and nutrition in the middle governorate, where is one of the most venerable areas in the Middle governorate in Gaza strip; Maghazi Camp,Breij Camp, Nuseirat Camp and Deir el Balah City, 2) detect moderate-severe malnutrition.
2) to increase children's care givers' capacities to provide adequate health and nutritional care to their children, and ensure positive nutritional habits for the children at household. Our targets are 2000 children under 2 years old with malnutrition from 1700 families.

1 - Needs

First of all, Health Nutrition Cluster Objective 1 ensures the access to healthcare and nutrition that prevent excessive maternal and newborn morbidity and mortality. Within its activities, 1)health and nutrition interventions for children under 2 years old, 2)women in reproductive age, pregnant and lectating women receiving quality maternal and nutrition services, 3)healthcare workers trained on neonate interventions and SRH topic and management of malnutrition, 4)people directly benefitting from awareness sesions and health education, are mentioned. According to the assessment conducted by Save the Children, Unicef, WFP and AEI, under the current socio-political situations in Gaza Strip, the nutrition risks and vulnerabilities amongst the children under the age of five, pregnant women and mothers/caregivers of children under the age of two, and adolescent girls are very high. 18 percent of pregnant women and 14 percent of lactating mothers are malnourished. More than 6 percent of infants never breastfeed, and more than 55 percent of infants are not exclusively breastfeeding. Breastfeeding continuation at 1 year old was 45 percent, and 2 years was 12.5 percent.According to the result of the assessment analysis conducted by AEI, the middle governorate has the highest percentages of IDA among children under 5 years old. 41% in Maghazi, 37% in Breij, 30% Nuseirat and 38% in Der El Balah. Comparing to lower percentages at other areas. The highest percentages of malnutrition problems were found also at the middle area, Maghazi 18%, Breij 12%, Deir El Balah 10.5%, and Nuseirat 7.6%. Other areas are slightly lower percentage, like Rafah 7.5% and Beit Hanon 7.3%. According to AEI findings of children under five years old at the area of Middle areas have the highest percentage at Anemia (38%), Rickets (6.8%) and malnutrition (18-20%). As for the women, 41% of them are anemic. These findings of AEI tangle with the result of the needs assessment. On top of that, according to AEI experiences, expecially in remote areas in Gaza, like ARA areas, people cannot access to clinics or hospitals. Thus, there are more needs in those areas.

2 - Activities

We try to prevent children in Gaza from being malnutrition, iron deficiency, rickets and anemia and improve the health of the children with those symptoms by conducting activities as follows ;【Adopting Community Health Activators】・The network of 60 Community Health Activators will be established and mobilized towards promotion of young child health and nutrition ・The activators from all over the targeted areas will be a linking point between the beneficiaries and work team・5 times of trainings for 20 Community Health Activators will be conducted ・2 times of review sessions for 60 Community Health Activators will be conducted【Home Visits for the beneficiaries】・1700 home visits with basic child medical screening and referrals will be delivered to 1700 families with young children under age of five by 60 community health activators・2000 Children under age of two will be asessed for nutritional status during home visits and referred to the facility based intervention if needed・Provide treatment and follow up services for detected children with Iron Deficiency, Anemia, Undeweight (ex. When we find a child under two years old with Iron Deficiency, we provide iron drops for follow-up once during the period. Vitamin A and D tablets also can be provided three times for each child during the period. )【Sessions for the communities】・70 cooking demonstrations with nutrition/hygiene sessions will be conducted for vulnerable families by 60 community activators in the target areas・70 awareness raising sessions and consultation on maternal, infant and young child health and nutrition will be conducted in targeted communities・Pre-Post tests for the knowledge and exit intervies for the benefitiaries will be conducted・Educational materials will be distributed to parents/caregivers and community representatives※We include ARA areas for the activities as there are more strong needs.

9 - Protection mainstreaming
9.1 - Do No Harm

Yes. We always ask our beneficiaries if we can take photos for the project and if they say no, we never take the photos. Even out beneficiaries says okay, we reconsider and if it seems not completely safe, we won't put the photos on the public media. Also, during the interview with our beneficiaries, we ask them if we can mention their name or not. Most of the times, we send female staffs to the field so that women can join the activities without any concern. On top of that, we cover our skin with long sleeves to show the feeling of politeness and try not to get too much attention from the local people.

9.2 - Equal and impartial access to aid

We work with 60 Community Health Activators from our project areas and they always have grassroots information and we prioritise vulnerable people in the areas, and top of that, if there's emergency needs based on the infomation from the community, we respond to it.
