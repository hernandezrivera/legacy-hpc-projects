Project Summary

This project will provide lifesaving emergency support to improve water supply and sanitation for internally displaced persons and returnees in Mocimboa da Praia and Chiure districts in Cabo Delgado Province, who remain in vulnerable situations due to ongoing conflict. Beneficiaries will be involved in the maintenance of water supply facilities and promotion of hygiene practices, strengthening their ability to sustain access to safe water and sanitation during this critical period.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Water supply facilities will be constructed or rehabilitated in Mocimboa da Praia and Chiure district where returnees live, ensuring access to safe water for IDPs and returnees. If need is confirmed, emergency water points will be provided to ensure temporary access to safe water in Mocimboa da Praia. A water management committee will be established to maintain the water supply facilities, and training will be provided on how to maintain and repair the facilities, strengthening the maintenance capacity of the committee and preventing breakdowns in the water supply facilities.Through emergency-adapted SANTOLIC approach, temporary community latrines/emergency household latrines and handwashing stands will be built in Mocimboa da Praia and Chiure district. Also, hygiene and dignity kits will be provided and key hygiene message will be delivered to improve hygienic living environments and hygienic behaviors. All activities include a mix of displaced and host communities and be age, gender, and diversity sensitive.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

At the beginning of the project, a community mass meeting will be held to understand their needs. During the project implementation period, opinion boxes will be located at each project site where communities can anonymously submit complaints and requests. Also, a free hotline system called “Linha verde (1458)” would be informed to beneficiaries. Protection awareness sessions will be included in all project activities and promoted at all levels. All the complaint will be handled by the focal points at the field level. When necessary PWJ HQ would provide technical expertise on investigation and protection of the beneficiaries. PWJ will thoroughly manage and protect the confidentiality of information collected and take sufficient measures to ensure that victims/survivours or communities are not exposed to danger. All requests or complaints will be taken action to respond within 48 hours.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

At the beginning of the project, every household in the project target area will be visited to identify women, elderly people and people with disabilities. Focused group discussions can be held to understand their specific needsto guarantee equitable access to WASH services.PWJ will consider gender at activistas selection so that women and vulnerable people can easily express their needs. Also, the project will support women’s leadership in the design and management of community sanitation facilities to strengthen women’s capacity. PWJ will deliver hygiene message reflecting women and vulnerable people's needs and integrating key messages in their culture, religion, ethnic. PWJ will discuss with community leaders and identify the safe places where hygiene and dignity kits distribution will be taken place. The distribution will be conducted during the day-time and pregnant women and vulnerable people (including elderly people) will be prioritized to receive the kits. PWJ will consult girls and women to identify the local menstrual hygiene practices (including the most culturally acceptable supplies), and provide dignified menstrual hygiene management (MHM) and sanitary supplies to women.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Led by PWJ staffs who have received training on disability inclusion, the project will respond to the needs of people with disabilities throughout its project duration.As mentioned above, people with disabilities will be identified as vulnerable people when women and elderly people are identified, and their specific needs also will be identified.The water supply facilities and household latrines constructed/rehabilitated will be designed to be easily accessible to people with disabilities. Since it is difficult for people with disabilities to build sanitation facilities by themselves, PWJ will work with local young people to build sanitation facilities for people with disabilities, which contributes to deepen their understanding of people with disabilities and build a cooperative system within the community.For disability inclusion, PWJ will encourage advocacy and capacity building for staffs working in WASH sector. Monitoring and evaluation will pay special attention to people with disabilities so that appropriate action will be taken to meet the needs of people with disabilities.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

PWJ ensures an environmentally friendly manner during the project implementation;• Data collection, assessment, training and monitoring will be conducted as paperless as possible.• PWJ prioritizes the use of green materials and materials that can be reused or recycled.• The construction of water supply facilities will ensure not to pollute the soil or rivers, and after the construction, all waste will be collected and returned to the original environment.• PWJ will ensure that emergency/household latrines are located far enough away from water sources to prevent water contamination.• During hygiene awareness campaigns, PWJ will encourage each household to set up a garbage pit as part of a sanitation facility and provide guidance on how to manage household waste.
