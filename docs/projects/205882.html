Project Summary

The project aims to improve access to WASH services in six counties of Unity State i.e. Rubkona, Guit, Leer, Mayendit, and Koch. The project will target Returnees, IDPs, and host communities who have insufficient WASH services to meet their needs. This will reduce the risk of WASH-related morbidity and mortality among women, men, boys, and girls. This will be achieved through the repair and rehabilitation of water systems, boreholes, etc; upgradation of existing boreholes to solar water yards; drilling and equipping new boreholes; formation/revitalization of water management committees; construction/rehabilitation of sanitation facilities; health and Hygiene information dissemination; and Environmental Hygiene campaigns.
The project will serve a total of 51,000 individuals (Female 39,617 Male 11,383).
In Rubkona / Bentiu, the project will upgrade 1 existing water point to a solar power system; repair 62 handpumps/boreholes (Koch 14, Bentiu/ Rubkona 18, Mayendit 15, Leer 15); Formation/revitalization of 77 WASH committees; Train 134 Pump Mechanics (Koch 32, Bentiu/ Rubkona 38, Mayendit 30, Leer 34); Re/training of 86 hygiene promoters; distribution of 12 standard tool kits (Koch 3, Bentiu/ Rubkona 3, Mayendit 3, Leer 3); Construction of 5 permanent institutional latrines blocks (each with 4 stances) (Koch 1, Bentiu/ Rubkona 2, Leer 2); Construction of 10 blocks of durable solution family shared-Latrines in IDP Sites A-E; Construction of 150 Household Latrines through subsidy approach, -(Koch 40, Bentiu/ Rubkona 30, Mayendit 40, Leer 40); Form and strengthen 6 school hygiene and sanitation clubs in schools; and facilitate the collection and disposal of solid waste in Sites.
Water supply in different areas will benefit a total of 51,000 individuals. Mercy Corps will engage with the state ministry and target community at the beginning of the project to ensure that the local authority and community take the responsibility of managing the new boreholes. The Water Management Committees (WMCs) will be formed and trained to ensure routine operation and maintenance (O&M) of the hand-operated/ solar water systems. User feel will be introduced in all the water facilities to ensure constant maintenance. Water quality will be constantly monitored.
The sanitation facilities will serve a total of 7,220 individuals in different counties. The family shared latrine blocks where 5 families will share one stance will be constructed and locked. It will be the responsibility of these family members to clean and maintain the latrine block. Institutional latrines will be constructed in schools/ health facilities as per the need. The management of these institutions will also be responsible for cleaning and maintaining the sanitation facilities in their respective institutions. Household latrines will target the most needy families. A selection criterion will be developed for the beneficiaries. It will be the responsibility of the family to dig the pit while Mercy Corps will provide the slab, 4 iron sheets for lining purposes, and technical guidance in the construction of the latrine.
Hygiene promoters will be trained to disseminate health and hygiene messages among the community members. Schools’ hygiene clubs will be formed/ Revitalized and trained on positive behavior changes. It is known that children are very good agents of behavior change and at this point they will be used to influence both at the school level and in their respective homes.
Environmental hygiene will be achieved through public campaigns which will be done through the local leaders. Community members will be mobilized for the clean-up campaigns on a weekly basis. Tools and equipment (Brooms, Rakes, Wheelbarrows, waste collection papers (For IDPs) etc.) will be distributed to the communities to facilitate them in the collection of garbage.

1. Needs

Water Supply - According to an assessment by Mercy Corps in June 2023, overall, the primary water sources include hand pump (41%), tap (30%), and water truck (14%). Within Unity State 56% of households lack secure access to meet their water needs, leading to tensions and competition around water resources among population groups. Among these, 53% are in Rotriak and 24% in Rubkona. Equitable distribution of water has been a challenge. 34% of households indicated that access to water is not equitable across population groups. There is a level of tension and/or competition around resources. Of those indicating there is not equitable access 34% are in Rotriak, 30% in Panyinjiar, 15% in Rubkona Camp, 12% in Bentiu and 8% Rubkona. Women and girls have the responsibility of collecting water (96%) and 39% of households indicated water collection sites are unsafe. The amount of water used in households on average is 10 Lppd which is way below the sphere standards. 39% of the population indicated that it takes them between 15 and 60 minutes to walk to the water source while 4% of respondents must walk for more than an hour. In Rotriak, long queuing observed at existing water points, and women and girls reported extreme challenges in terms of access to safe water and they often return back without getting water due to the congestion at existing limited water sources. The population has significantly increased from 35,921 in 2021 (pre-flood) to current estimated population of over 63,000 people. Sanitation - The sanitation situation in some areas needs urgent intervention. Overall, 58% of households lacked access to latrines with Koch (61%), Ganyiel (96%), Rotriak (84%), Nyal (67%), and Rubkona town (37%). The relatively better situation for Bentiu town (2%), Rubkona camp (21%) and Bentiu IDP camp (22%) where in the camps the facilities are shared. Open defecation at the household level is a health hazard and looming catastrophe. A significant number of women and men have expressed concerns about privacy and indicators for safety at latrines, especially at night. 45% feel there is not enough privacy for women and children while 23% felt locks on doors are not available. This calls for technical teams who are supervising the constructions to ensure that elements of privacy are adhered to strictly. Special people in the community also need attention as 42% of respondents are concerned that people with disabilities have limited access to latrines. Hygiene - Across Unity state, hand washing with soap is not wholly practiced. Over 42% indicated they do not practice regular handwashing with soap. The greatest proportion of households that are not doing regular handwashing with soap are in Rotriak (65%), followed by Nyal (63%) and Koch (63%), Ganyiel (57%) which are all high. The other areas like Bentiu camp (29%), Bentiu town (17%) and Rubkona town (5%). Clearly there is a need for extensive promotional information dissemination on positive hygiene practices like hand washing. Solid Disposal Waste - Disposal of solid waste has been a challenge. The common practices / methods of disposal include burning (52%), use of a dump site (21%), burying (11%), and simply leaving waste (11%). Littered garbage was observed in most of the households as well as public places. There is a need to mitigate environmental and health impacts.The prevalence of waterborne diseases is a serious concern in Unity state. Over half of the households (54%) had diarrhea within the last 3 days of the assessment. Rates were highest among the IDPs (66%) and returnees (53%), and 44% among the hosts communities. Across sites, diarrhea rates were highest in Rotriak (91%), followed by Bentiu (90%), Koch (70%), Rubkona camp (58%), Rubkona town (55%), Nyal (53%), and Ganyliel (50%). The high rates of diarrhea, highlight the urgent need for improving access to safe drinking water, enhanced sanitation facilities and intense positive hygiene information dissemination.

2. Project activities

Water Supply - In clean and safe water supply, emphasis will be put on operation and maintenance of the existing water points. The water systems will be repaired and rehabilitated. This will involve replacement of the broken water supply pipes; repair of solar systems; repairs and maintenance of water reticulation systems; servicing of the generators currently in operation; replacement of the broken and worn out taps etc. Upgradation of existing boreholes to solar water yards will be done. A few selected points will be preserved for new borehole drilling. Water quality monitoring will be done in all the operational water points while random spot check will be done for households. Drilling and equipping New BoreholesUpgradation of existing boreholes Water YardsMajor / minor repair of handpumps/boreholes Water quality testing and monitoring Capacity Building and sustainability model - All the water points will have committees which will be responsible to operate, manage and maintain them. Water management committees will be formed/revitalized hence will address the operation and management of the water points. The user fee model be agreed where the community members will sit and agree households will contribute and after how long. From each water point, two people will be trained as hand pump mechanics. Each village will be equipped with a standard tool kit which the mechanics will freely get at the time of need, repair the borehole and return the tools to the assigned committee. Hygiene promoters (HPs) will be trained on health and hygiene messaging. Schools’ hygiene clubs will be formed/ revitalized and trained on positive behavior changes. Children are very good agents of behavior change and they will be used to influence both in school and in their respective homes. Formation/Revitalization and Refresher/Training of WASH committees on waterTraining Pump MechanicsRe/training Hygiene promoters Standard tool kits to be distributed Sanitation - This will be addressed at community level, institutional level and household level. Permanent institutional latrines will be constructed/rehabilitated in schools, health facilities etc. Within the sites A- E in Rubkona and Guit Counties, the durable solution latrines will be constructed and allocated to families who will be responsible for cleaning and maintaining the latrines. In these sites, repair and maintenance of the existing latrines will be done. Provision of the cleaning material and personal protective gear will be provided to the latrine cleaners. Likewise bathing shelters will also be repaired and maintained. At household level, community members will be encouraged to construct their latrines.
