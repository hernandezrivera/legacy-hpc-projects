Project Summary

Over 767 million poor people in the world today live on less than a $1.9 a day. Cameroon’s economy suffers from factors that often impact underdeveloped countries, such as stagnant per capita income, a relatively inequitable distribution of income, a top-heavy civil service, endemic corruption, continuing inefficiencies of a large parastatal system in key sectors, and a generally unfavourable climate for business enterprise (Cameroon Economy Profile 2018). Such a situation has affected almost everyone in the country especially the youths who make a majority of the population. Most displaced youths remain stranded especially those victims of the crisis and those who returned in their base. Most of the beneficiaries are targeted to be host communities and also IDPs.
The case for the youths of the North West Region has no different. The North-West region (NWR) has an agricultural based economy with very few industries that has limited the number of youths to gain meaningful employment. Access to financial services is limited, particularly for Small and Medium Size Enterprises (SMEs). This situation has been further worsen by the present socio-political crisis in the North West and South West Regions of Cameroon that has left untold suffering within communities. Its present predicament has affected the youths so badly, have caused youths to stop schooling with some pulling out of vocational activities, and are jobless now. There is great hatred for the country and youth empowerment is of the essence.
This project titled Youth Economic Empowerment is conceived on the bases that every youth needs to gain self-employment. Its focus is the provision micro loans for youths to start up independent businesses. The project will select and train 50 selected IDPs and host from two divisions of the NWR (Bui and Donga-Mantung). The selection of these youth will done by an adhoc-committee made up of persons from different backgrounds. This training shall last for a week with theoretical and practical sessions. The theoretical sessions shall focus on principles and practices of budgeting, smart borrowing, saving, product marketing, business growth strategies, peace building, national integrity, and living together. The practical sessions shall focus on the production of small and medium products such bread, cakes, doughnuts, pies, ice cream, chocolate, local cosmetics, washing detergents, agricultural and non agricultural activities and other businesses.
After the training, the participants will be guided to draw a simple business plan indicating what they will produce, how much it will cost, how they will market it and how they will use the proceeds. Upon presentation of the business plan, each participants will be given a 1,000,000 FCFA each to start implementing the business plan. Participants will not be given all the money in cash, a livelihood officer will accompany each of the participants to the market to buy all the major items that will be needed for production and the balance of the money will be given to the participant to run the business. This system will promote entrepreneurship and self-sustainability for youths. With self-employment by these youths, dependency will reduce and the need to long for peace will be primordial among these youths.
The overall objective of this project is to improve quality of life for 50 youths (IDPs and host families) of the NWR. Specifically the project will empower these 50 youths on business concepts, entrepreneurship, peace building, national integrity, and the essence of living together.

1 - Besoins / Needs

Considering the present low economic situation of the NWR that is affecting especially the youths, this project is needed to:Promote self-reliance and independence among youths (IDPs and host families) : This project will instill into the IDPs the potentials to be their own bosses, produce, market basic commodities and earn an independent income for the improvement of their lives. They will gain skills on entrepreneurship and being able to source and secure a job.Promote social cohesion: One of the major reasons why most of the youths in the NWR into this present arm conflict with the Cameroon military is because of lack of jobs which has left to idleness. The project will equip the youths with skills and start up capital (materials/tools) to start business, which will promote workmanship. During the training, they will also gain knowledge on right-base protection, peace building, national integrity and living together. This will promote peaceful coexistence; moral rearmament; hunger for peace, social cohesion; attention on human rights; non-violent communication; need for schooling; and the protection of IDPs and persons living with disabilities (PWDs).Promote peace: The project will empower these youths to have meaningful lives as they go about achieving their visions. This will permit them to be advocates of peace and conflict prevention within their communities.Contribute to Cameroon’s Gross Domestic Product (GDP): According to the Cameroon Radio and Television (CRTV), statistics from the Cameroon Employers Association GICAM, reveals that 30% of government taxes come from Small and Medium Medium Sized Enterprises. Also, Small and Medium Sized Enterprises also provide 40% of Cameroon’s Gross Domestic Product (GDP). The empowerment of these youths of NWR therefore to set up registered and legalized business schemes, the GDP of the country would experience a growth.
