Project Summary

This project aims at solving WASH problems in the three states; Khartoum, West Darfur and Gadaref states. Water and hygiene promotion, solid waste management, hygiene promotion training, conflicts resolutions workshop, capacity building in protection awareness.
Improving familes and communities public health practices through health education of mothers, fathers, other caregivers and members of the community concerned with health issues,
behavior, compliance, care at home and overall health promotion
activities, Community involvement in health services planning and monitoring to purpose Prevention and control of communicable diseases by acting as a strong early warning.

1 - Needs

Major hazards affecting health in Sudan droughts, floods, epidemics, internal conflict .There are frequent disease outbreaks of measles, hepatitis E, Shakinvonia, scabies and acute watery diarrhea due the lack of WASH servsecesAs per Sudan 2022 HNO, 11.07 million population lack handwashing facilities with soap and water. There are also 4.3 million in acute need of basic water services, and 8.4 million require limited/improved sanitation services. As per the 2021 National Multi-Hazard Health Emergency Plan 2021, 3.1 million people are at risk of water-related diseases. More than 11 million people reside in high-risk areas for vector-borne diseases, specifically malaria. In 2022, WASH Sector is planning to target 1.9 m. in need population with basic water, 1.8 m. with adequate sanitation and 4.7 m. with hygiene promotion interventions. Gender inequality is widely existing among the project targeted population, especially with regard to decision making processes, division of labor among family members and gender-based violenceThe prevalence of non-communicable diseases is on the rise with frequent cross border population movement increases the likelihood of the importation of communicable diseases, including wild polio-virus. Shortage and inequitable distribution of WASH, a weak HIS and shortages of drugs and medical supplies exacerbate the overall health situation. • Internally displaced people over 15 years in West Darfur, vulnerable refugees and asylum seekers in Gadaref are in high need of humanitarian assistance, as well as host communities in many areas with lack access to services and support. •
