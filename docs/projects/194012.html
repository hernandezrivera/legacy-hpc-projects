Project Summary

Due to the need of urgent humanitarian and life saving intervention in the uprising civil war in Sudan with the RSF and the Sudanese Military that started in Khartoum mainly ( North Kordofan and the Tribal Conflicts in West Darfur )15 April / 2023 up to date , causing a high number influx of population to near state such as Jazira , River Nile State , Northern State , and White Nile causing high need of protection and education needs .
The main humanitarian needs in Sudan result from different underlying root causes and drivers including instability and multiple conflicts, recurrent natural disasters including droughts and floods as well as disease outbreaks. women, girls, and children continue to suffer disproportionately from protection due to violations of basic rights, insecurity, low economic status, denial of livelihood opportunities, lack of community awareness on women's and child rights as well as due to negative cultural and societal norms. The 2023 HNO indicated that there are 3.1million People in need of GBV , 3.2 million people need General protection service and 2.1 children need child protection integrated intervention services and another services. According to HNO 2023 the target area in the project has high severity ranking (3-4-5) people in need (PiN). GAH will be targetting ( 173,947 ) participants in total in all the mention localities ( 26,092 males, 43,486 females , 60,881 girls and 43,488 boys ) due to most of the effected partivcipant are mostly women and children .

This project is multi-state set of interventions including social welfare and protection services directing different protection Sub-Sector (Gender Based Violence, General Protection and Child Protection this intervention the provision of these protection services for the vulnerable host community and Internally, Displaced People (IDPs) addressing the integrated protection into these States in West Kordofan state localities (Keilak , Wadbanda, Lagawa and Aldibab) and in South Kordofan (Kadougli, Abujebaiha , Altadamon and AbuKarshola) , Abyei , While as the Darfur's states social welfare and protection services directing different protection Sub-Sector (Gender Based Violence, General Protection , Child Protection and Education East Darfur ( Shairia , Abujabrah , Abu Karinka , Assalaya , Bahr Al-Arab ) , South Darfur ( Umdafouq , Al-Salam , Geraida , Ed-Fursan ) , West Darfur ( Kerenik and Baida ). in Addition to the need the news areas North Kordofan ( Sheikan) , White Nile ( Rabak and Kosti ) , Blue Nile ( Kurmuk , Tadamon and BAw) , and Jazira ( Madani) targeeting the IDP camps and settlements.
Baida ).
The project will strengthen care for survivors and individuals vulnerable to violence through comprehensive Gender Based Violence, General Protection, Child Protection and Education service provision, The project aims to establish and build the capacity of community base structure with the relevant knowledge and skills to undertake protection mitigation response and link the community members with the project staff. The project will ensure the participation and inclusion of the project beneficiaries throughout the project life cycle to provide a community-driven response to the protection needs of the beneficiaries, and to foster ownership of such response among the community, thus increasing the sustainability of the intervention after end of the project.
This project will also provide a comprehensive package services of protection sectors are working integrally to ensure the safety of women, girls, and boys, that they get better and improved services, and that long-term effective solutions are attained for sustainable access to protection and basic needs.

1 - Needs

According to HNO 2023 indicated that there are 3.1million People in need of GBV services and another services. According to HNO 2023 the target area in the project has high severity ranking (4-5-3) people in need (PiN). According to the multi-sectoral need assessment of 2023, women and girls, boys and men continue to face the risk of different types of GBV contributing to factors including low economic status with no or little livelihood opportunities, lack of community awareness on women’s rights, and GBV due to traditional and social norms. GBV risks remain significant in Sudan, particularly with women and girls on the move, working in fields, or undertaking livelihoods activities. women and girls are disproportionately affected by the impact of the humanitarian crises in Sudan due to gender inequality, intersecting with other factors of discrimination and disadvantage, including age, ethnicity, and marital status. Girls are at high risk of abuse; The position of women and girls is further diminished due to their lack of access to - and control of – the resources and viable coping strategies they need to survive and recover, as well as the protection of their human rights and their recourse to justice.With a set of a strong foundation for their sustainability for communities’ awareness, engagement, and ultimately adoption of key lessons for better self-reliance will be existing. These activities were addressed due to the severity ranking of most of the chosen localities and the assessment report made by GAH staff and communication with relevant minsters to specify the gaps and needs and the severity ranking in most of the Protection sectors.In Gender-Based Violence, the women and girls especially GBV Survivals require establishing and functioning referral pathways, in addition to Support Women Empowerment Activities and Vocational Training. A special effort should be directed to the identification of the most vulnerable women and Women at Risk. As part of engaging the women and girls as community-based structures, GAH will focus on establishing, training and supporting the functionality of Women Committees, targeting women and girls’ side by side with men and boys in the Community sensitizing session. One of the major and fundamental issues facing women in the targeted localities is the limited or none expectance of female Dignity Kits, other than the lake or limited access for women and girls’ safe spaces. In Child protection, the needs and gaps were identified in different areas such as Case Management, Psychosocial Support Service, Place and support for Separated and Unaccompanied Children, Capacity building of Community- based child protection networks, number and situation of Child-Friendly Spaces, In addition to cases referral Pathways, Capacity Building of service and non-service provider on child right. A major need accrued in the awareness and engagement of the communities especially children, and the need to conduct and provide more recreation kits/activities and support Children at risk. In General Protection the targeted communities have more gaps like a lake of assistive devices for People with Disabilities, also the need for community Watch Group created a huge need to establish and training of Community Based Protection Networks. Also, the assessment showed poor Identification of Elderly at Risk, Persons with disabilities, and People with Special Needs. In the Education, and WASH sectors are working integrally to ensure the safety of women, girls, and boys, that they get better and improved services, and that long-term effective solutions are attained for sustainable access to protection, education, and basic needs.
