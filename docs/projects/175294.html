Project Summary

OCHA contributes to principled and effective humanitarian response in Zimbabwe through coordination, advocacy, policy development, information management, and humanitarian enhancing tools and services.

8 - Accountability to Affected People
8.1 - Describe how your project was designed in consultation with communities? How will you incorporate communities in project implementation and what feedback mechanisms do you have in place?

OCHA will promote and ensure trough coordination that protection is at the center of all activities aimed at ensuring full respect for the rights of the individual in accordance with human rights law, international humanitarian law (which applies in situations of armed conflict) and refugee law. Therefore strengthening situational awareness including through increased efforts to identify barriers, risks and enablers for persons of concerns including person with disabilities and takes concrete steps to strengthen the inclusion of persons with different types of disability. - Continue verification and prioritization of needs and response efforts throughout the year to ensure humanitarian funding is used in the most strategic and equitable way.
