Project Summary

Within the town of Um Elamad in the eastern countryside of Homs, there is a central septic tank with an area of 3,000 square meters. It is located on the northwestern edge of the town, between residential houses, and it is about 500 meters away from the school in the town. It has recently witnessed several floods due to its weak capacity, as polluted water reached neighboring agricultural lands and residents’ homes.
The association aims through this project, to repair and expand the sewage system in this town and desludging Septic tank to reduce the negative effects harmful to the environment and health and limit the spread of diseases and epidemics. The project will be implemented by expanding the village's sewage network and connecting it to the sewage network of the nearby town of Nawa, which has a central treatment station. Accordingly, the sewage network will be expanded by 2.5 km and cleaning and removing the Septic tank which estimated at 2000 cubic metres.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Most of the towns and villages of the eastern countryside of Homs witnessed large displacement operations due to military operations and war during the Syrian crisis, and among these areas is the town of Um Elamad, as its current population is about 15,000 people, knowing that it was approximately 7,000 people before the crisis. For these reasons, this Septic tank has witnessed several floods recently. Due to its weak carrying capacity compared to the increasing population as a result of internal displacement from the rest of the eastern countryside.This has caused the spread of many infectious diseases among the population, including Intestinal Diseases, Hepatitis, Cancer, and Cholera. In addition, insects, rodents, reptiles, and even predatory animals have spread widely due to the intensity of pollution, unpleasant smells, and insects. Approaching that area has become extremely dangerous. In addition, the damage reached agricultural lands and the irrigation system, which became irrigated with polluted water, knowing that the village depends on agricultural activity as a source of income and self-sufficiency. The underground water tanks in the village were also damaged, causing contamination of drinking water and making it unfit for human use.Through this project, more than 3,000 families will be protected from this widespread pollution by providing a safe sewage network, noting that the majority of the population are families of martyrs and the poor class, and there are no health centres or clinics available in the area.The association will commit to providing the necessary engineering studies and plans prepared in advance. During implementation, periodic monitoring reports will be provided in cooperation with the engineering team. On the other hand, the project will take into account all safety and security standards to protect workers from potential risks during the implementation of operations and train them on means of preventing widespread rodents. The project is expected to face several economic and logistical risks during implementation, which will be dealt with according to the previously prepared risk matrix.
