Project Summary

The association owns an empty property in the Babella area and aims to establishing a rehabilitation center (and its mobile team) for people with disabilities that includes the following activities:
• Mental health and psychosocial support
• Linguistic and educational support including speech therapy
• Technical and social support and training for life skills
• Support for those with hearing impairment
• Support for those with physical impairment including physical therapy
• Support for victims of war
• Support for families of people with disabilities including mental support and teaching them how to behave with and treat those in their care
Target beneficiaries will be people with disabilities (regardless of gender and age), however a large focus will be placed on ages 3-15.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

People with disabilities (PWD) make up at least 28% of the Syrian population (aged 2+) according to UNDP. The association currently has a total of 520 registered PWDs (329 males and 191 females), all of whom are also registered as PWDs within the Ministry of Social Affairs and Labor. Their disabilities include mental disabilities (30%), hearing disabilities (35%), visual impairments (2%), loss of limb and/or lack of mobility from war or birth defects (30%), among others (3%). The services will be offered to the PWDs themselves, as well as their families/caretakers, in order to allow them to improve their capacities and skills when caring for their loved ones.Most activities will be in class forms and can be done either individually or in groups, depending on the activity and the unique cases of beneficiaries involved. While the mobile team will attract new cases and provide services at home for difficult cases who are unable to move. Also, Monitoring and evaluation will be done periodically.PWDs and females with disabilities in particular face higher risk of sexual exploitation and abuse, thus the center will offer awareness sessions, as well as trainings to build livelihood capacities. Additionally, codes of conducts will be put in place in order to ensure a safe environment amongst workers, as well as ensuring that if workers are not predominantly female, there is at least an equal ratio of females to male workers.The center will deal with potential protection risks such as lack of permission from families in regards to equal access of services due to gender, this will be mitigated through the mobile teams delivering services at PWD's homes when it is of utmost urgency, as well as reaching out to community leaders and building a trust-based relationship that extends throughout the local community. In addition, humanitarian workers lacking capacities to work with the unique cases that might visit the center, which will be mitigated by hiring highly qualified personnel and ensuring they receive routine trainings from specialists, as well as having regular monitoring and evaluation of the services to ensure the beneficiaries are being well-treated.
