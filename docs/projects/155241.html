Project Summary

There are 9103 Rohingya families setteled in camp 3 and total population is 39198 which significant size of populations demands more health facilities in camp area. UNHCR, Population figures as of 31 July 2018 has shown the specific needs family number is 2659 at Camp 3 includes 332 number families with people with serious medical condition which needs to be medical care. (ref. population figures by specific needs). In order to improve health outcomes of Refugees in Cox's Bazar Dalit will provide Health support through Health post. Through enhancing capacity of health volunteers awareness level of refugees peoples will be increased. Family planning & reproductive health, personal hygiene awareness are indeed required for refugee women, girls, married adolescents girls. The awareness building, referral pathway, door to door visit and counseling will be assist to improve the health status of those targeted women, children.

Activities or outputs

1. Run a Health Post in camp area, 2. Free Medical camp and medicine distribution among patients, 3. Preventive measures through Community awareness building, capacity building of community health volunteers. 4. Family planning counseling and motivation session with targeted women, men. 5. Awareness building on NCD, CD, Health & Hygiene, Personal Health. 6. Door to Door visit and counseling on health issues
