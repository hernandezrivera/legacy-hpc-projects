Project Summary

SOP will ensure that displaced children in camps can join the education system. SOP will establish child friendly space inside the targeted 9 schools and continue previous project, schools in Dana subdistrict (Qah, Atma and Sarmada) communities and consist of 58 classes (tents and rooms) These spaces should be inclusive and physically safe. Furthermore, will do gender-sensitive and disability-sensitive WASH facilities and making the facility more accessible for persons with disabilities PWD like (ramps, side rails for stairs and other needed modification). Moreover, SOP will run learning activities catch up classes, basic literacy and numeracy, and life skills in health, safety and security, which will cover children (5-17 years, 491boys and 510 girls) enrolled in non-formal education. Basic literacy activities require literacy materials in appropriate languages, and can be led by teachers, parents or youth. Moreover, conduct formal education for 3369 (1729 boys and 1640 girls) children (5-17 years) in 105 classrooms , which will be provided with the allocated subjects of curriculum for every stage during the academic year (9 month). SOP will use the modified Syrian curriculum approved by the Interim Government. Which is the same curriculum used by the Syrian regime Ministry of Education, with some key amendments, including the removal of pro-regime slogans, symbols, and propaganda. Conduct Learning assessment pre and post of academic year at 9 targeted schools using ASER tools in order to determine the vulnerability aspects for the assessed children either for catchup or through academic period, (ASER tools consist of basic Reading and Math tools. Teachers will Provide only by SOP with monthly incentives allocated for education project into153 teachers and educational personnel (69 males and 84 females) during the academic year, beside to 3 summer time activities. Furthermore, Provide 3369 students (1729 boys and 1640 girls) with learning materials (stationary, pens, math tools …ETC.) and recreational activities throughout the academic year. Finally, SOP will conduct Training (135) teachers and education personnel (60 men and 75 women) on child-centered and protective pedagogy and on the following subjects: EiE, PSS, INEE and Child-Centered and Protective pedagogy (Effective Teaching & Learning).

Needs

Through study done by ACU 2018 identified some difficulties that prevented children (girls and boys) access to education in the camps that contain a school. Financial and material difficulties were the most important among the assessed schools. The most influential factor regarding student dropout was child labor because extreme poverty pushed 40% children who dropped out of school to work to help their families. Moreover, extreme poverty prevented parents from sending their children to schools in 21% of dropout cases. The lack of stationary accounted for 15% of dropout cases. The reasons also included lack of certification, school unavailability, or the bad conditions within schools if available, in addition to a social reason, early marriage which accounted for 5% of dropout cases especially for girls between 14 and 18 years old. In normal conditions, children (girls and boys) need psychological counseling in school. This need increases exponentially in war time, especially when, “nearly 3 million children (girls and boys) in Syria have never known peace and suffer from psycho-social distress as a result of experiencing the horrors of war.” Consequently, teachers noted the spread of several psychological cases among students including introversion, shame, violence, and depression. According to NA done by SOP 2018 regarding the targeted school in camps (Sarmada, Qah and Atma) which reveals that the schools need some rehabilitation in terms of wash, electricity, paint, gender- sensitive and disabilities modifications), also the teachers (women and men) is still need to be supported financially. Regarding the children aged (5-17) years (girls and boys) are still in need to be included in formal and non-formal education beside to PSS. The number of children aged (5-17) years in 9 schools is 3369 (1729 boys and 1640 girls) distributed on 1 and 2 stage of primary schools, beside to secondary stage. On school has High school stage. The number of educational stuff is 120 (47 men and 73 women), beside to 33 administrator personnel (22 men and 11 women).

Protection Risk Analysis

Changes in the security situation, particularly in project target mentioned areas, affects the safety/mobility/ displacement of beneficiaries/staff and results in being unable to participate in the project. Child safety incidents in school due to bullying and overburdened teachers, or due to the low knowledge of the teachers with child safe-gardening. the possibility of Attacks on schools in the mentioned areas, Risk of children to drop out of formal education to attend non-formal education, due to many reasons related to the children or family concerns. A limited number of beneficiaries in the program, which might cause inter-community tension and pressure on implementing staff, Increased risk of teachers/education personnel leaving formal schools to work in non-formal education. After years of heavy fighting between the Government of Syria (GoS) forces and non-state armed groups (NSAGs) in Idleb governorate including Ariha and Teftnaz for protection project, beside to Aldana district for education project, conflict activity between these two sides decreased significantly as of 10 February. Despite of recent developments the Security and access remain challenging in many areas of Idleb governorate. The project will strengthen the capacities of local staff and protection actors to build situational awareness in the areas of operations. SOP developed safety and security plan that governs operational activities from our base in Gaziantep, Turkey, and Syria Offices, as well as protocols for travel, communications, and operations within Syria to let SOP staff work under above-mentioned conditions. Safety and security of all stuff is a high important issue for SOP and through safety and security plan developed by SOP, which monitoring of security situation regarding of the targeted area will be done on daily basis, in order to ensure the safety of stuff and continuity of project activities as planned. There are safety and security focal points at Ariha, Teftnaz beside to 9 targeted school in Atma, Sarmada and Qah who are responsible to assess and check the security situation in the surrounding areas. They are fully delegated to take the decision of evacuation or temporary suspending the school activities in accordance with the emergent situation (evacuation plan). However, such actions are being reported to SOP HQ office (safety and security) in Turkey immediately. SOP works in high visibility, coordination with local councils while that gives us more legitimacy in the ground and secure our work through the community engagement. SOP is also in close coordination with other NGOs working in targeted areas, and the relief offices of local councils, to share security accidents and recommendations.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risks: 1-Limited number of teacher and education personnel benefiting from the project could cause inter-community tension and pressure on partners and local authorities.2- limit of resources in order to provide the needed materials or un availability of such materials at the targeted area. mitigations: At the onset of the project, SOP will aim to quell expectations by being as transparent as possible by: clarifying the outputs and intended outcomes of the project, the number of people who will benefit from each component of the project, the criteria for selection, and the work methodology (having open discussions with community members and authorities, etc.).-Analysis of markets and availability of the materials beside to good planning financially to provide these needed materials.
