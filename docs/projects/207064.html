Project Summary

Although access to safe water supply, sanitation and hygiene (WASH) is at the heart of sustainable economic and social development of a nation. The project aims to improve living conditions of people in particular the affected by insurgence in Nort of Cabo Delgado living in Ancuabe and Chiúre Districts in Cabo Delago province through improved access to sustainable and quality WASH and contribute to reduced prevalence of water-related diseases, reduced time and efforts for fetching water, and enhanced dignity. The project will also provide opportunities to generate income for women through soap production and sell at local level.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project aims to improve living conditions of people in particular the affected by insurgence in Nort of Cabo Delgado living in resettlement centre and people living in host communities through improved access to sustainable and quality WASHservices. The project will support implementation of WASH infrastructure including the rehabilitation of handpumps and behaviour change related to safe waterconsumption, and handwashing. The project will benefit 20.000 women and men in Ancuabe and Chiúre

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The priority activities are:1. Rehabilitation of water points in resettlement centres or/and host families in Ancuabe and Chiúre2. If there are new entries in resettlement centres, the project will do the construction of emergency latrine in Ancuabe and Chiúre for short term.3. Construction of inclusive latrines for PWD identified and selected in resettlement centres in Ancuabe and Chiúre.4. Promotion of hygiene and sanitation in resettlement centre and host families in Ancuabe and Chiúre,5. Training and monitoring the soap production by IDP women to empowerment of people specifically women living in resettlement centres. Approaches: HELVETAS will implement Community Led Total Sanitation approach to promote the construction of improved local latrines for medium or long term.HELVETAS will conduct trainings and follow-up for water and sanitation committees related to mobilization and management the contributions for operation maintenance of water points.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The integration of protection measures is a fundamental component of the project's design strategy, emphasizing the preservation of safety, dignity, and equitable access. HELVETAS upholds the 'do no harm' principle, with its staff extensively trained to identify and implement solutions in accordance with these principles. Additionally, HELVETAS maintains close coordination with protection actors, actively addressing issues such as gender-based violence (GBV) and other protection needs through effective referrals. This proactive approach underscores HELVETAS's commitment to safeguarding vulnerable populations.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The project has and will encourage participation of both women and men in all aspects, i.e. at least 50 % representation of women in the water and sanitation committees, with a specific support to promote the involvement of women in leadership position. For maintenance of the water points, the project will promote the membership of women in artisan’s associations. For elderly person the project will ensure that their specific needs will be built into the response according to local context.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The project will work jointly with other partners (local and health authorities, health and protection partners/clusters members) to identify people with disabilities. Once identified, the PWD will receive a tailored designed inclusive latrine and other WASH facilities. During the assessment persons with disabilities will be determined in order to ensure that their specific needs will be built into the response.
