Project Summary

Internal conflict in Ethiopia’s Tigray region in November 2020 led to a sudden mass displacement of more than 69,000 Ethiopians to Sudan’s Kassala, Blue Nile and Gedaref States. Thanks to the support of the Sudanese authorities, UNHCR/COR coordination, new permanent settlements have been identified to accommodate the new wave of displacement from Ethiopia.
Gedaref State is hosting the majority of the refugees (62,383) in Um Rakuba (20,572) and Tunaydbah (20,604). additionnlay recent new influx from Qemant tribes are as well in GEdaref State, in Basunda locality, in a very dire situation.
The activities proposed by SI, based on field evidence needs, assessment and secondary data, are: :
- Emergency Water trucking in context without any other alternatives access to water
- rehabilitation and maintenance of wash facilities (latrines and shower places)
- construction of wash facilities in new camps (Babikri, possible new open camps)
- waste management
- hygiene promotion
- Rapid Response team to address the risk of outbreak

1 - Needs

in Gedaref State, the Ethiopian asylum seekers are representing a case load of of approximately 50 000 individuals spread in different camps. Due to the context in Ethiopia, a larger influx may arrived in Sudan. Wash needs remain important in existing camps and will remain important in potential new camps or relocalization between exiting camps. SI is currently working in Tunaydbah settlement, Basunda and Basinga. The needs to be addressed are related to the access to drinkable water, maintenance or rehabilitation of washing facilities, desludging, waste management, Hygiene promotion and rapid response team in case of outbreak. A specific attention will be on Babikri camp, planned to welcome the relocalization of Basinga and Basunda. SI will be fully in charge of all components related to Wash in this camp, planned to host 3000 individuals.

2 - Activities/Outputs

in Gedaref State, the refugee answer will be: Water contingencies re foreseen in order to address Installation, Maintenance and Rehabilitation of water distribution points and to ensure continuous monitoring of water points by SI Camp Supervisor in terms of operation, cleanliness of the tanks/bladders, and in case proceed with repairs to the temporary platforms, gravelling and draining of waste water away from the water points. The activity also covers possible influx of new refugees in the area (expected in the 2 following months). Upon a pre-KAP survey performed in Babikri, emergency activities of Hygiene promotion will cover basic principle of save water handling and water borne diseases prevention and will establish the foundation for an all-encompassing HP programming in 2022.- Sanitation Contingencies are meant to covers installation of Emergency latrine in Babikri, desludging/decommissioning of Basinga Informal site and Basunda camp and all the related items and services to ensure a safe fecal sludge chain.the access to water, according the configuration of each camps and needs, will be organised either through water trucking either through network. - Provision of water through trucking services at the rate of 20L/p/d, using trucks/tractors as per the condition on ground, with each truck/tractor having a Monitoring team to ensure equitable distribution of water across the locations, record each trip/volume delivered and conduct random FRC at the water point and from collection containers. SI water officer/supervisor monitors at water source level the pumping, crosschecking the nr of truck with water distributed at point of use level in order to have the routes planning matching with deliveries, and related demand of water in line with standards. 5% of the volume will be added and delivered to cater for any loss that occurs during trucking thus ensuring the minimum 20L/p/d is achieved. Water trucking material will include consumables and devices, such as pool testers, chlorine HTH 70%, DPD1 and Phenol red reagents, CFU test reagents, turbidity and other chemical consumables, material and items in order to ensure Water quality monitoring. Therefore, the whole water supply line will be monitored at truck (FRC test), water point and also household water collection/storage level with SI team ensuring that water supplied is safe and has FRC >0.3mg/l. The provision of safe water will be ensured in the whole process of relocation of people and assets to the intervention sites . In Gedaref State, Si is already working with a local partner specialized in waste management and hygiene promotion: Green peace association (GPA). this intervention is especially critical as it is mainly organised in host community, having structural but critical needs.
