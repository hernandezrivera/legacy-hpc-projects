Project Summary

This project will help and support vulnerable families in Hawiga and Alabbasy districts. This Project will be cash distribution to 150 families in Al Hawiga and 250 families in Alabbassy so total will be 400 families. district
This will reduce people sufferings that extended for three years,
This project is also commentary work via cash+ Livelihoods via employing local people and buying materials locally
Assessments and identify highly vulnerable families will be conducted
MPCA to the most vulnerable household will be delivered
Based on estimated needs and the poverty marker informing the new CWG scoring tool (using MCNA data), we anticipate the following to be applicable for each caseload of MPCA:
1: 1,449,000 IQD = 23,8 % of our caseload
2: 960,000 IQD = 31,8 % of our caseload
3: 480,000 IQD = 44,4 % of our caseload
In order to effectively monitor the project, EORD will use digital standard methods of PDM the sample is attached form on documents to avoid duplication in addition to normal and traditions methods as visits to the families will be undertaken by the Project Coordinator, and weekly visits by the Project Manager. Progress reports will be received by the distribution team
Project progress will be reporting to the Cluster on a weekly basis in their Salah Alden Monitoring tool and on a monthly basis on ActivityInfo.
Accountability to Affected Populations
An accountability system will ensure community involvement, information sharing and participants from the beneficiaries to improve the quality of the project. As with other projects, EORD will establish a complaint and response mechanism (CRM) to affirm that beneficiaries , children , their parents and communities have the opportunity to register comments and any complaints. This CRM includes suggestion boxes at project sites, a mobile hotline and face to face sessions between staff and beneficiaries.
Complaints and feedback are categorized at different levels from positive feedback to regular and serious complaints. Handling of complaints will be decentralized and field driven, with senior management involvement required only for any serious complaints.
The beneficiaries of the current project will participate in needs assessments, Qualitative and quantitative information will be collected from population in the cohesion area and the most vulnerable families.
During the implementation of the project , there will be listening directly to the participants and families questioning if there is any compliments, comments and notices that might be needed to develop the project or enhance its efficiency. There will be also survey to get feedback from the participants and population.
Protection Mainstreaming
To ensure that protection principles are mainstreamed throughout our programming, we will talk to children about the threats they face on the way to school, shops, streets and the places they may go. These conversations will happen with boys and girls separately and separately with other groups of children that may face additional difficulties (e.g. those with disabilities, minorities, etc.).
Also EORD has adopted standard procedures with staff as code of conduct. Everyone who will work or contract with EORD have to sign code of conduct. Another procedure is EORD will put a compliant box and hot line phone number to receive any concern or feedback from the beneficiaries.
