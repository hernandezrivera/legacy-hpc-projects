Project Summary

Water supply, Sanitation, and Hygiene Promotions are the most important WASH components for reducing water and health hygiene-related diseases in camp settlements, especially for congested and densely populated camp settlements like Bangladesh. There is still a gap in access to safe drinking water, toilet, and bathing facilities. Also, there is limited and proper management of solid waste, menstrual hygiene management for adolescent girls and households, and fecal sludge management in Ukhiya Rohingya camps. Considering these needs IRB has designed the project for 12 months to address the WASH vulnerability in Camp-2W makeshift and the host community living in Cox’s Bazar. Major WASH interventions will be the installation and repair maintenance of the water network at tRohingya refugee camp. As per WASH sector objective, IRB has provision for Pit latrines construction/repair, Sludge desludging from pit latrines, bathing cubical construction & repairing, and hand washing station construction/renovation in the camp. Considering safe drinking water and improved sanitation, there are plans to install Deep tube-well, WASH block construction, and hygiene promotion awareness in the host community areas.
IRB will install/decommission water supply pipe networks, renovate existing pipe networks and provide operational support to ensure a safe water supply for the targeted Rohingya in camp 2W. Furthermore, IRB has a plan for the construction/decommission of 145 cubicles of latrines in camp 2W. IRB will repair & maintain 500 existing latrines, construct 65 bathing facilities in camp 2W. 100 bathing cubicles will be repaired & maintained in camp 2W. Installation of 35 hand-washing devices and 60 hand-washing devices will be repaired & maintained in Camp 2W. Apart from that, IRB will construct 1 Fecal Sludge Treatment Plant (FSTP) under this project for safe excreta disposal and reduce environmental health hazards for the targeted Rohingya and it will be constructed based on the needs assessment. Consequently, considering the fecal sludge management, IRB has a plan for latrine desludging to reduce environmental detrimental as well as controlling AWD in responsible areas and provision 2500 cubicles for latrine sludge collection & management in camp 2W. This proposed project will also implement a large portion of integrated WASH facilities along with a waste management system in the host community. Among the WASH facilities, 17 WASH blocks will be constructed in the institution and market area, and the repair and maintenance of 60 existing toilets and bathing spaces for making easy access to the PWDs and the aged persons. There is also a provision for the installation of 45-deep tube well or water technology in the affected host community especially the vulnerable northern part of cox’s Bazar district. Furthermore, IRB will install solar lights at the WASH areas to ensure effective accessibility and safer uses of latrines and water facilities during the night. In addition, provisions have been kept for drainage cleaning and solid waste management. IRB has the provision of distribution of water storage kits to the Rohingya as well as the host community of cox’s Bazar area. IRB always enhance improved hygiene promotion and hygiene practices and provide necessary session, orientation, and training, to volunteers, staff, community people, user groups, and management committee as an effective stakeholder of the project. IRB will conduct hygiene awareness sessions at the block level as well as provide need-based gender, and age-appropriate hygiene kits will be distributed to the most vulnerable families. IRB will distribute hygiene kits among the most vulnerable Rohingya families through this project. IRB will incorporate camp management, collaboration, and coordination plan to details implementation plan. IRB always keep excellent working relationship and strong coordination among SMS, CiC, UNHCR and other agencies to achieve goals & objectives.

1. Needs
1 - Identified Needs

Islamic Relief Bangladesh (IRB) in consultation with the sector and camp management selected Camp 2W for WASH interventions. IRB will work as WASH focal for camp 2W and responsible for providing WASH-related support. Roughly 75% of FDMN households (HHs) have reported water supply, worth hygiene conditions, and very poor sanitation facilities (J-MSNA, 2021). IRB’s August 2022 assessment illustrated that 81% of HHs in camp-2W couldn’t access safe water, improved sanitation, and hygienic practices. As a result of COVID-19 restrictions, the maintenance of WASH facilities decreased and led to overcrowding in those facilities that were functional. Currently, there are 166 water pipe networks across the camps: among them, there exists only 3 water pipe networks in camp-2W. IRB’s assessment in camps 2W highlighted that the current rate of construction/desludging of latrines is not sufficient as a result significant numbers of latrines remain unusable. According to the need assessment of WASH sector data, 0% of FSTP meeting acceptable COD standards in rohingya camp settlement. Sanitation facilities repair and maintenance is the most urgent need in various camp especialy camp-2W. In camp 2W, 39 people are using every functional latrine. There is a need for functional latrines as well as water points in camp-2W. According to the WASH sectors monitoring report published on 03 October 2022 regarding drinking water, 44% HHs are using tap stand as a primary source of drinking water in camp 2W which is important to increase minimum standard level. IRB will establish 02 DTW with pipe water network facilities in camp 2W. According to MSNA, there are 40% HHs in camp 2W with visible waste in the Provision for drainage cleaning and waste management in the camp along with latrine desludging kept in the project. Besides these, there is a need for safe drinking water sources like DTW in Ukhia Upazila, need of sanitation facilities, and WASH blocks in the host community schools. IRB will spend 25% of its budget on the WASH gap interventions in the host community area.
