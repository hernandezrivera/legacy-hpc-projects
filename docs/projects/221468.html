Project Summary

The purpose of this project is to reduce the vulnerability of adolescent girls, teenage mothers, and PwDs in four localities (Makary, Logone Birni, Kousseri and Blangoua) of the Logone et Chari division victims of natural disasters and conflicts. This will be achieved in the domain of protection, Shelter/NFI and livelihood.
The project will directly target:
- Households that are victims of natural disasters and conflicts.
- Adolescent girls and teenage mothers (under 18 years) who are victims of the natural disasters and conflicts.
- Persons with disabilities (PwDs) victims of the natural disasters and conflicts in the Logone et Chari division.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

Since 2013, the security situation has been particularly volatile due to armed activities; the civilian population has been affected by looting, attacks, killings, kidnappings, and armed clashes.These situations lead to displacement with wider problems such as separation of families, death of caregivers, deprivation of livelihoods, increased risks regarding the protection of women, children, persons with disabilities, and other vulnerable groups. In addition to these, recurrent flooding is contributing to further deteriorate the situation. According to OCHA, the rainy season from July to September 2024 brought heavy rains, resulting in pluvial flooding and rising river waters in the Far North region, particularly in the division of Mayo-Danay and Logone et Chari. Several localities in the Kousseri area have already been affected by river flooding, and a number of preventive measures have been taken, including the creation of informal reception sites. As of October 18, 2024, more than 459,000 people have been affected, more than 56,000 houses have been destroyed, as well as tens of thousands of hectares of crops, which are likely to have a major impact on agricultural production, with a probable increase in the level of food insecurity in the coming months. Despite physical access constraints, 34 humanitarian partners are supporting and providing vital assistance in terms of food, shelter/essential household items, water, hygiene and sanitation, and health. The persistent rains and the continuing rise in water levels raise fears of a deteriorating situation and the risk of epidemics. In regards to persons affected by division, the Logone et Chari is the most impacted with 254,000 people for 31,712 households.The priority needs are in terms of shelters, NFI Kits, food, Water, hygiene and sanitation kits, first aid and psychological support.Following the multi-sectorial need assessment jointly carried out by various NGOs (Red Cross, Internation Rescue Committee, OIM) with the coordination of OCHA, the project will carried out a baseline study to identify the gaps so as to adequately respond to beneficiaries (Households, adolescent girls, teenage mothers and persons with disabilities).Due to accessibility challenges caused by flooding, the Shelter/NFI kits will be distributed based on the purchase and supply of items or cash multi-purpose transfers. Project Activities:- Multi sectorial needs Assessment: Conduct a comprehensive needs assessment to identify the specific vulnerabilities and needs of the target beneficiaries. Afterward, the main beneficiaries will be selected. This will be done under the directives of OCHA in order to avoid duplication of services.- Distribution of Shelter/NFI Kits will be done based on the identify gaps to households of flood victims with regards to persons with disabilities and teenagers’ mothers/girls.- Distribution of Dignity Kits: Provide dignity kits to adolescent girls and teenage mothers, including those with disabilities.- Educate on Hygiene Practices: Conduct educational sessions on good hygiene practices, including hand washing with soap and running water, creating hand washing stations, the dangers of open defecation, personal hygiene, and waste management. Include information on environmental health and the prevention of water-borne diseases like cholera.- Provision of Assistive Devices: Provide adequate assistive devices for PwDs victim of the flood in the concern areas (Logone Birni, Kousseri, Makary, and Blangoua).- Empowering vulnerable women and PwDs through livelihood skills training and capacity building based on needs and demand expressed by the market study that will be conducted.- Provide livelihood support to promote economic self-sufficiency following the skills training and capacity building.- Offering psychosocial support to address trauma and mental health concerns.- Advocating for the rights and inclusion of vulnerable groups.
