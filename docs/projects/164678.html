Project Summary

In its eighth year, the conflict inside Syria shows all the signs of a complex protracted humanitarian crisis. Takaful Al-Sham is aiming to support IDPs and part of vulnerable HCs with SNFI assistance in Aleppo and Idleb governorates according to the following sub-districts:
Afrin 2,500 people (Girls 595, Boys 600, Women 580, Men 725) 500 HHs
Azaz 2,500 people (Girls 595, Boys 600, Women 580, Men 725) 500 HHs
Maaret Tamsrin 5,000 people (Girls 1,190, Boys 1,200, Women 1,160, Men 1,325) 1,000 HHs
Idleb 2,500 people (Girls 595, Boys 600, Women 580, Men 725) 500 HHs
Bennsh 5,000 people (Girls 1,190, Boys 1,200, Women 1,160, Men 1,325) 1,000 HHs
And support in-kind NFI Winter Kits to other households in:
Teftnaz 2,500 people (Girls 595, Boys 600, Women 580, Men 725) 500 HHs
Dana 2,500 people (Girls 595, Boys 600, Women 580, Men 725) 500 HHs
Maaret Tamsrin 2,500 people (Girls 595, Boys 600, Women 580, Men 725) 500 HHs
Idleb 2,500 people (Girls 595, Boys 600, Women 580, Men 725) 500 HHs
Al Bab 5,000 people (Girls 1,190, Boys 1,200, Women 1,160, Men 1,325) 1,000 HHs
The total 6000 HHs will be targeted with various fuel types (Kerosene - Diesel - Firewood and Olive pits) provision, stoves, winter clothing kits and NFI winter kits according to family needs where priority will be given to most vulnerable families which include
women, children headed household, disabled person, sick, PLW and elderly to enable venerable households face severe and harsh winter and meet their basic NFI needs. as verification will take place through visiting their location. Vendors will be selected through
a committee according to specific criteria focus on accessibility, trust in community…etc.
Distribution of vouchers will be conducted in three phases for the same beneficiaries whereas distributions of in-kind winter NFI kits will be one off distribution.
Shelter repair 200 HHs, collective center rehabilitation 600 HHs.
Takaful's shelter interventions will take place as follows:
1-Rehabilitation of Collective Centers: Targeting IDPs residing in collective/ transitional centers that doesn't match minimum standards of emergent shelters and doesn't respond to protection and privacy needs
2-Shelter repair for housing: Targeting IDPs (tenant), and host community members (owners), with focus on returnees to seek sustainability by laying the essential foundations for long-term recovery. This will target the most vulnerable categories
(female-headed HH, widows, people with disabilities, etc.).
3-Infrastructure Rehabilitation: projects that uphold and advocate the social cohesion and the engagement of all community categories in the targeted area with durable and sustainable impact.
In case of sudden displacement during the winter season and during the implementation period, absolute priority will be given to newly arrived IDPs that meet the vulnerability criteria.
At the beginning of the project Takaful will conduct a meeting with local community and local actors to ensure their participation in the project, explain the project goals and take their suggestions and notes to develop the project activities and implementation.
Takaful will coordinate with SNFI cluster and with other organization who works in the same sector and field to reach the right beneficiaries and avoid any duplication
4- Provision of tents & Gravelling inside camps.
