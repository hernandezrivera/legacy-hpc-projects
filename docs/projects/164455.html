Project Summary

In 2018 Amal Organization established a centre in Orm Eljoz which aimed to enhance the protection environment and access to GBV services for conflict affected (W&Gs) with a focus on the most vulnerable individuals, and to continue supporting to it ensure sustainability of delivering the same services.
Through this centre Amal managed to support the (W&Gs) by providing them with safe environment to feel physical and psychological safe. Moreover, this project achieved remarkable reputation among the communities due to the services delivered during the past times and Amal team were welcomed by the community.
The goal of the proposed project is to enhance the protection environment and access to GBV services for conflict-affected (W&Gs) with a focus on the most vulnerable individuals (e.g. children, elderly and single-headed households and (PWDs)) through supporting a WGSS in Orm Aljouz and Kafar-takharem in idleb as well Jendares in ifreen, Syria. The project is based on supporting a safe environment for (W&Gs) where they feel physical and psychological safe (absence of trauma, excessive pressure, violence and fear) and is free to express themselves without fear of discrimination or any kind of harm. The WGSS aims to be in : Orm eljouz and Kafar-tkharem and Jendares, and its neighbouring communities. The activities will include IDPs and the host communities.
The expected outcomes are:
- Providing quality and life-saving GBV response services.
- Providing PSS sessions for women and girls
- Open-day events
- Conducting VT sessions (Computer and communication skills and writing CV, English language, Manual embroidery) for (W&Gs), and GBV prevention activities
- Providing case management services, for (W&Gs)
- reinforce referral pathways
- Providing (PFA)
- Kindergarten
The project contributes to providing of quality services and measures for GBV, and develops appropriate services to prevent and reduce risks related to GBV.
The Centres are already provided with a special treasury to assure confidentiality of information by saving the cases files, and training of case workers and specialists, while maintaining strict confidentiality and privacy measures of the situation and the placement of a special code for each case and its preservation in the treasury.
The organization established a committee of beneficiaries selected by direct election. This committee will contribute to leading the center and selecting appropriate activities according to the wishes and ages of the beneficiaries. The committee will also have a role to lead the center at the end of the project period and has the right to communicate with the interested parties working in the area to sustain the project activities even by transferring the project to another organization or by extending the project activities.
The M&E team will implement the M&E tools for GBV programs in addition to periodic assessments and questionnaires, measuring the impact achieved, drawing on lessons learned and activating complaints and suggestions mechanisms.

4 - Needs

As in previous years, W& AGs (women and adolescent girls),in general, particularly if they are widowed, divorced, displaced, disable and eldery, they are the most affected from risk of GBV. In 2018,W&Gs pointedly noted the inequality that exists between male and females, and identified the role of negative social norms or customs and traditions in hampering the realisation of their rights “Even if women are granted their rights, customs and traditions still exist and we must respect them with regard to freedom". Voice 2020GBV remains a major protection risk, particularly in forms such as domestic violence, forced/early marriage as a coping mechanism and the fear of sexual violence and harassment. The lack of understanding for the need for GBV services in some communities remains high, indicating the need to strengthen GBV awareness, referral pathways, as well as outreach and prevention activities. Disruption of traditional safety nets and livelihood opportunities, in addition to sudden displacement, this put many W&Gs at risk of various types of GBV that affect their lives in different ways, aggravating protection concerns such as psychosocial stress, limitation of movement in some areas of the country, situations and potential for increased recourse to negative coping mechanisms. This context highlights the need for increasing the availability of specialized GBV services. HRB 2020Amal has made a Rapid Assessment of the current situation in “Orm Eljoz and Kafr Takharim in idleb, and Jandairis in Afrin.FGD were held in a number of residential communities’ area, where the questions were related to the needs of women in the current conditions, the problems facing them: Most W&Gs are deprived of basic rights such as education, work opportunities and access to other available services, where men prevent their daughters from getting education under the pretext of fear. This underscores the importance of WGSS to help them facilitate access safe services

5 - Implementation of activities

GBV response services will be provided to W&Gs through the safe space at the Center by providing specialized case management services for survivors, focused PSS for GBV survivors as well as Individual and group counselling /Focused PSS. Awareness sessions such as improving self-confidence, self-awareness raising sessions, IPV, and psychosocial distress will also be offered, and women will be made aware of the need to disclose to the case manager the situations of violence they are exposed to.Curriculum(My Safety is My wellbeing and Girls' Dreams)will be used according to different age groups, providing PFA.W&Gs will also be included in empowerment activities such as life skills and vocational activities. Providing recreational activities and open day events (women's workshops,leisure activities, discussions, competitions and self-confidence activities and promoting leadership. This will be a form of psychological discharge and the inclusion of trauma women in society.The children accompanying their mothers will be taken care of through a special room so that women can benefit from All services provided at the center. Special messages for GBV will be presented through continuous sessions of the GBV Awareness Toolkit, which will target W&Gs within the center, as well as educate W&Gs and men through mobile outreach teams. The org will work to raise the team's efficiency and capabilities periodically through ongoing capacity building by providing training using the GBV SC toolkit:PFA,GBV basic,PSS,SOPs,case management, referral Pathway, life skills as well as undertake the GBV SC online training on PWD inclusion in order to ensure the inclusion of PWD in the services provided at the center, providing referral services through coordination with other providers of GBV services and the use of referral pathways developed in GBV SC where survivors of violence will be referred to services related to GBV, MHPSS specialized, Legal, PSS, medical,non-GBVservices:WASH, NFI, etc.)

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

1. Lack of quality services and referral expose survivors to harm, enhancing risk of stigmatization reprisal and further trauma. Lack of quality services also lead to fewer disclosures and lack of trust in services. Likelihood: High. Impact: High Mitigation Measures: Service mapping including only those that can provide survivor centred services.2. Resistance or backlash from sections of the community and community leaders on GBV messaging 2. Backlash from family or community on women participating in recreational, vocational or empowerment. Likelihood: Medium. Impact: High Mitigation Measures: Community engagement from onset of project, from assessment phase and throughout all project cycle. It includes meetings with religious and community leaders 2. Ensure the support of male family members by engaging them in awareness-raising activities3. Community and family stigmatization for participating in programming, family resistance to girl’s mobility. Likelihood: Medium Impact: High- Mitigating Measure: Targeting adolescent girls with socially accepted activities like life skills as safe entry point

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

1- Change in lines of control: Likelihood: High. Impact: HighMitigation Measures: unfortunately, we can’t mitigate this risk but we will work on decreasing the consequences by having an evacuation plan that we train our team on and also a closure plan that we will use the GBV SC note to develop including a policy to destroy all confidential docume2- Humanitarian actors suspend their funding to those locations: Likelihood: Medium Impact: highMitigation Measure: Train the committee of women to lead and being able to map the resources available in the community to run the basic group activity and donate any equipment available to this committee to support them doing this role3- The sudden closure of the centres due to the change of control over the land. Likelihood: High. Impact: High- Mitigation Measure: The centre’s ethical closure plan will be drawn up and include its activities as a mitigating measure for GBV programs.We will also periodically upload files to the electronic cloud and destroy all papers related to case management, as we will periodically update the closure plan for gender-based violence programs as needed
