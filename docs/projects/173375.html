Project Summary

Prantic Unnayan Society (PRANTIC), Bangladesh in consideration to the COVID-19 pandemic is planning to continue 8 LCs for 840 children (6 to be operated by trained caregivers and 2 under digital learning system) for children (age group 3-14), and will establish 4 LCs (2 to be operated by trained care givers and 2 under digital learning system) for 420 adolescents (age group (15-17), 1 new LC for 105 children in Camp-2E and 1 new LC for 105 children in Camp-1W (both for age group 3-14) as per identified needs by the Education Sector. Thus a total of 1470 students will be from in FDMN Community. In the host community (Rajapalong union under Ukhiya Upazila), PRANTIC will continue existing 2 digital learning centers (age group 4-14) for 100 students and will set up 4 more digital LCs for 200 children to ensure educational access to a total of 300 children. A total of 1,770 children and adolescents will be enrolled through the proposed project. In addition to the above, PRANTIC will attempt to involve 50% women and 50% male in Parents and Learning center Management Committee. In order to follow a very pragmatic strategy and implementation process PRANTIC will jointly work with other acting sectors like GiHA, DRR, youths and WASH to address some cross cutting issues like gender, disaster and WASH. PRANTIC as an education focal lead in Camp-4, intends to utilize its long experience in Rohingya Response Program since September 2018 following the guidance from Education Sector, Cox’s Bazar. The project will cost a total of USD 183,982.00 in a year 2021 (01 January 2021 – 31 December 2021) with a cost of USD 103.94 per student and will require 14.01% as non-operational costs i.e. USD 25,777.00.

1 - Identified Needs

According to the needs assessment done by the Education Sector (Ref: Joint Multi-Sector Needs Assessment (J-MSNA) July –August 2020), there are needs for continuing education for a total of 12,602 children (age group-3-14) in Camp-4. The Gap Analysis Report of Education Sector (Ref: JRP-2021_EduSector_PIN_Target Calculation V11_ (RC & HC) -By Union_18112020) reveals an existing gap of 2,187 children (age 4-14) in Camp-2E, and a gap of 2,142 children (age 4-14) in Camp-1W. PRANTIC is planning to continue education for 840 children of age group (3-14) in Camp-4 (6.7% of needs), ensure access to education for 105 children (age group 3-14) in Camp-2E (4.81% of gap) and access to education for 105 children (age group 3-14) in Camp-1W (4.91% of gap). The same assessment reveals a gap for 5,746 adolescents and youths (age group 15-24) in Camp-4 where PRANTIC will ensure access to education for 420 adolescents (age group 15-17) (7.31 % of the total gap). Considering the prolong existence of COVID-19 pandemic PRANTIC is planning to continue 8 LCs (6 by trained caregivers and 2 by digital learning system) for children (age group 3-14), establish 4 LCs (2 by trained care givers and 2 under digital learning system) for adolescents in camp-4 (age group 15-17), establish 1 new LC in Camp-2E and 1 new LC in Camp-1W (for age group 3-14) to cover the gap. In the host community (Rajapalong union under Ukhiya Upazila), PRANTIC is planning to establish 4 more digital LCs In addition to the existing 2 digital learning centers (age group 4-14). Therefore, PRANTIC is targeting a total of 1,770 children and adolescents in 2021. Out of these 1,770, there would be 1,050 children of age group 3-14, and 420 adolescents (age group 15-17) from FDMN community. Remaining 300 children will be from host community of age group 4-14. PRANTIC will ensure inclusive education facilities with a priority for 5% children (in average and on the basis of availability) with special needs such as disables, handicaps and transgender. From the perspective of gender segregation, PRANTIC is going to ensure educational access to 1,770 children and adolescents in the both host and FDMN community with a total numbers of girls 920 and boys 850 through maintaining 52:48 girls and boys ratio. Out of 1,770 students, there are 1050 children in FDMN community with gender segregation by age (girls 85 and boys 80 of age 3, girls 80 and boys 50 of age group 4-5, girls 350 and boys 405 of age group 6-14). In addition to 1050 students in FDMN community, PRANTIC will ensure education for 420 adolescents of which girls are 245 and boys are 175 of age group 15-17). Besides PRANTIC will ensure education for 300 children (age group (4-14) in host community with gender segregation by age (girls 80 and boys 70 of age 3, girls 80 and boys 70 of age group 4-5). PRANTIC will attempt to involve 50% women and 50% men in Parents and Learning center Management Committee (PLMC). In order to assess the quality of education, PRANTIC will attempt to graduate 100% students from existing level to the next level. Moreover, PRANTIC will conduct FGD to assess quality of education (curriculum plus attitudes and behavior) through ensuring participants from Parents committee, PLMC and Students with at least 50% female participation. In addition to the Education sector curriculum, PRANTIC will conduct COVID-19 awareness program from the perspective of prevention, detection and treatment through conducting in-person campaign, distribution of leaflets and poster, and circulation of audio and video visuals through digital learning system produced by BBC Media Action_ audio content_ Soiyi Hota program at household level
