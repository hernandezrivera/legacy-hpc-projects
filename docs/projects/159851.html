Project Summary

Project aims at strengthening protection of the most vulnerable and marginalized host communities/minority clans including the disabled and displaced women and girls that are exposed to increased risks of GBV at their homes and in IDP camps in Garowe and Qardho, Puntland State, Somalia. Their plight is compounded by Poor living conditions in IDP settlements such as poor shelters constructed with inappropriate materials such as old clothes, plastic sheets and sticks, poor lighting, lack of private spaces for sleeping and over-crowding that exposes them to protection related risks. Limited access to basic social services exacerbates their already precarious conditions. A number of women and girls cover long distance to access water points located far away from their shelters that at times happens in the dark at night. In addition they cover long distance to access food, firewood, markets and health services outside IDP camp thus exposing them to high risks of GBV. The situation is worsened by limited livelihood opportunities especially for female headed households who often cover long distances in search of casual labour opportunities or petty trading to secure their basic needs. There are cases where displaced women and girls are discrimination by host communities that limits them access to basic social amenities with the community. The most common forms of GBV among women and girls are sexual harassment, forced marriages, assault, rape including underage girls, torture and killings. Their plight is worsened by weak and informal protection systems while a lot of GBV cases go unreported due to shame associated with it as well as fear of victimization by the perpetrators while the victims suffer stigma and depression. The situation is worsened by ignorance and lack of awareness hence high prevalence of such cases.
The project will respond to above plight by: providing protection responses to the GBV victims through Survivor-centered multi-sectoral support (medical, legal assistance and safety) and case management of GBV, Training of duty bearers, medical personnel and humanitarian staff on GBV, Awareness raising advocacy and public outreach campaigns on GBV prevention, Provision of material assistance -dignity kits and solar lanterns to GBV survivors, Monitoring and referral (GBV) pathways development. of the project targeted beneficiaries 48% are women and young girls and 52% are men. Therefore the targeted beneficiaries are are a total of 17900 (8592 women and young girls as well as 9308 men)
