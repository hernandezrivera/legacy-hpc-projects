Project Summary

Oxfam is continued working in Somaliland and Puntland implementing essential lifesaving activities for drought affected people in Sool and Sanag, and cyclone affected people in Awdal region of Somaliland and Nugaal and Bari region of Puntland. In 2020, Oxfam will continue working in these areas and deliver lifesaving water supply, by constructing new systems, rehabilitating existing water sources and improving quality of water sources; sanitation support through constructing emergency latrines for high concentration IDP camps, and hygiene awareness campaigns including on menstrual hygiene management. Oxfam will target the most vulnerable communities and IDPs in Oxfam interventions areas and will coordinate all works with the WASH cluster and government of Somaliland/Puntland.

Please provide further explanation

1. Explain if the protection environment is sufficiently safe/stable to enable durable solutions/resilienceIn recent years, it seems that the frequency of droughts is increasing. Instead of once in a few years, it now becoming common to have droughts in either all or part of Somalia every year. Three major factors continued to displace people in Somalia i) droughts, ii) conflict between state/nonstate actors and iii) clan conflicts. In search of a safe environment, people affected by these emergency factors continue to either migrate to another part of the country or establish their own IDP camps looking for support from humanitarian actors. People in the old IDP camps seem not interested to return back to their original location i) they don’t see any change in the environment and ii) they have no resource to re-establish their livelihood. Therefore Oxfam’s WASH strategy is really focusing on local integration in areas of displacement, through either structural support (e.g. Construction of new water supply facilities, extension of piped water networks, or construction of latrines) but also in terms of advocacy and engagement on the needs of IDP’s and their involvement in decision making. This will take a conflict-sensitive approach to ensure that tension between host and IDP communities are mitigated. 2. Is the project linked to government effortsYes, In 2019, Oxfam supported both technically and financially the development of five years water sector development strategy of Somaliland and is in the process of replicating the same support for Puntland. This plan is summarizing the need for the next five years and calling for all actors to support, prioritize, report and monitor the progress of this development strategy. Once finalized, the plan is expected to guide the nexus between humanitarian and development. In 2019, Oxfam rehabilitated over 32 different water supply facilities and solarized over 17 facilities to reduce dependency on fuel. These facilities will continue providing water beyond the project lifetime provided that communities manage and take full ownership. Activities in this project, HRP 2019, will be implemented in selected locations with the same approach. Oxfam’s priority is to provide more sustainable WASH facilities that would have a big impact on the future of the people we are serving and contribute to the development of the sector. 3. Alignment of objectivesOxfam has developed an annual humanitarian strategy, based on the findings of the national assessment, Oxfam's rapid assessment, and the weather forecast. The priorities objective for WASH is aligned with national cluster objectives and Oxfam is committed to support both and contribute to the cluster objectives and the water sector development goals. 4. Sustainability Community engagement during the assessment and other phases of the project implementation is critical for both ownership and sustainability. Oxfam has strong community engagement tools and works with communities to ensure the need comes from the community, they prepare their own action plan highlighting the areas of support for Oxfam and with communities, involvement activities are implemented. After the completion of the work, community selects the management body using their traditional resource sharing mechanisms, set of criteria agreed with Oxfam and we train the selected members and do the handover with the presence of the government representative. So, this project, community engagement will be prioritized, comments will be addressed and facilities will be handed over to the selected members of the community and local government.
