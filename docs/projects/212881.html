Project Summary

This project will be implemented in Al-Hasakah Governorate (Al-Hawl Camp and Tal Aswad Roj Camp) based on a rapid needs assessment in Al-Hawl Camp and Tal Aswad Roj Camp, where information was collected about the environment surrounding the camp and the weather, the efficiency of the installed insulators was evaluated, and interviews were conducted with samples. . Among the camp residents, their needs were identified, the most important of which is isolation
Support will be provided for life-saving and sustainable non-food items to IDPs and refugees in Al-Hawl and Tal Aswad Roj camps, especially during the winter in the years 2024-2025, as it will provide them with protection from rain. And weather factors, especially families who cannot provide basic needs.
The project will distribute plastic insulation to each tent within the camp, as plywood cannot be used in these camps due to its exposure to moisture in the winter or directly to water, in addition to its exposure to heat and the possibility of being exposed to the sun. moisture. It is burned using primitive fuel-powered tools for cooking or heating purposes. Inside the tent) the insulators will be distributed as follows:
Mohaim Al-Hawl:
4270 Plastic insulation for Syrian family tents.
5410 Plastic insulation for Iraqi family tents.
Tal Aswad Roj camp:
(1500) plastic insulator
That is, the total number of plastic sheets that will be distributed in 2024 is 11,180, and the same number in 2025.
The specifications of each plastic insulator will be:
Size: 4 x 6m, Weight: 4800g±5%, Made of high-density polyethylene (HDPE) black fiber fabric laminated on both sides by a layer of low-density polyethylene (LDPE).
Heat-sealed reinforced edges on all sides, aluminum or equivalent vents on all four sides of single panels, Color: Sun-reflective white on both sides, gray stripes. "
Al-Raqi Society for Social Services will implement the project in accordance with quality requirements, quality control for the entire distribution service and non-food specifications to meet the required needs.
The project will apply protection mainstreaming through the principles of do no harm and non-discrimination in project design, implementation and monitoring. The safety and security of the distribution site will be prioritized, as well as meaningful access for people with disabilities, with the distribution team able to go to people who are unable to access the distribution site.
