Project Summary

ADRA will prioritize the access of refugees to basic services in Khartoum State, and enhance cohesion through engagement and awareness of host communities. The project will enhance the protection environment through the establishment of inter dialogue Community Circles. The Circles will include both host community and refugee members, within the circles the members will be trained in various topics, mainly conflict resolution and community protection. As part of its global strategy in Education, ADRA also aims at enhancing education for vulnerable refugee children, through a comprehensive and efficient voucher based response, that ensures that children are able to continue their education in safe environments.
In White Nile ADRA Sudan will continue to support protection for the SSR in 2020 in White Nile. The project will aim at strengthening community based self-protection structures such as Inter Communal Dialogue Groups (ICDGs), Women’s Groups (WGs) and forming Youth Groups by doing so the groups accountability will be strengthened towards decision makers and other actors’ partners. The aim is to institutionalize the groups to be part of the former camp governance system. This action will build the capacity of the SSR and host community (HC) to improve social protection systems and increase the groups’ awareness of and linkage to referral mechanisms.
This action will reduce protection risks associated with negative coping mechanisms by implementing cash for work and distributing charcoal vouchers which will reduce exposure to GBV during firewood collection, supported by production and distribution of Fuel Efficient Stoves (FES). Particular attention will be given to trainings on stove usage and environmental issues and cash for work will include targeted reforestation as well as FES production, thereby strengthening the linkage between activities, aligning interests of SSR and HC and creating space for the SSR and HC to jointly address protection issues.

1 - Needs

ADRA will prioritize the access of refugees to basic services in Khartoum and White Nile States, and enhance cohesion through engagement and awareness of host communities. The project will enhance the protection environment through the establishment of inter dialogue Community Circles. As part of its global strategy in Education, ADRA also aims at enhancing education for vulnerable refugee children, through a comprehensive and efficient voucher based response, that ensures that children are able to continue their education in safe environments. Needs to be focus include; Physical protection needs are very high among refugees living in Khartoum and WN, and many refugees face challenges accessing protection assistance. Host community tensions aggravate physical protection issues for refugees. Many refugee families in Khartoum cannot afford public school fees, and are often charged foreign student fees. Most families cannot afford the cost of school supplies and textbooks. Refugee students living in more remote areas of Khartoum cannot access public schools due to walking distances. Most public schools are over capacity and cannot absorb refugee students. Many of SSR children in WN are in need for education support. The project intervention includes continues coordination among several actors in WN and Khartoum State on to ensure, quality Protection, WASH, energy and environment services
