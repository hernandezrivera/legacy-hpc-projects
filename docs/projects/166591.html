Project Summary

Provision of specialized nutritious foods to children 6-23 months with -1SD height for age. Train health workers, ward nutrition co-ordinators and community health workers on COVID-19, growth monitoring, identification of growth faltering, infant and young child feeding. The project includes training and counselling on infant and young child feeding to caregivers (male and female), pregnant women, caregivers of children 0-23 months. It will also include further investigation and counselling on IYCF and COVID-19 of those children that would be identified as stunted. Investigate reasons why the particular children are stunted, follow up on the feeding practices and offer IYCF counselling to the caregivers. The social behaviour change communication will include messaging on COVID-19, infant and young child feeding using different platforms such as at health facilities, in communities by community health workers, jingles on local radio stations and on social media.

Training of health workers, ward nutrition co-ordinators and community health workers on COVID-19, growth monitoring, identification of growth faltering, infant and young child feeding. The project includes training and counselling on infant and young child feeding. COVID-19 to caregivers (male and female), pregnant women, caregivers of children 0-23 months. It will also include further investigation and counselling (IYCF) of those children that would be identified as stunted.
