Project Summary

The objective of the intervention aligns with the nutrition cluster deliverables and contributes significantly to the nutrition cluster agenda by addressing the critical issue of malnutrition among children under 5 (CU5) and pregnant and lactating women (PLWs) in Northwest Syria (NWS). The project aims to screen, refer, treat, and follow up CU5 (6-59 months) and PLWs suffering from malnutrition, focusing on moderate acute malnutrition (MAM) and severe acute malnutrition (SAM) cases. Early detection and treatment play a crucial role in aligning community strategies to combat malnutrition effectively.
A comprehensive needs assessment conducted in collaboration with local partners and communities identified a high prevalence of malnutrition among CU5 and PLWs in the targeted sub-districts of Ariha, Atareb, Dana, Ehsem, Jisr-Ash-Shugur, Mhambal, and Qourqeena. The assessment highlighted the urgent need for screening, referral, treatment, and follow-up services to prevent life-threatening risks associated with acute malnutrition and improve nutritional outcomes.
The project integrates principles of Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) to ensure the safety, dignity, and rights of beneficiaries, particularly vulnerable groups such as CU5 and PLWs. PSEA measures include staff training, community awareness campaigns, complaint mechanisms, and strict adherence to codes of conduct and ethical guidelines.
Risk analysis identifies potential challenges and uncertainties that may affect the project's success. Assumptions are made based on available information and past experiences. Risks and assumptions include:
Access Constraints: Risks related to security, logistical challenges, and bureaucratic impediments may hinder access to targeted communities and delay service delivery.
Community Resistance: Resistance from community leaders or misconceptions about the intervention may impede participation and acceptance of nutrition services.
Supply Chain Disruptions: Risks associated with supply chain disruptions, including shortages of supplementary nutrients, could affect the availability of essential commodities for treatment and supplementation.
Staff Security: Risks related to staff safety and security in volatile environments may impact the continuity of service delivery and staff morale.
To mitigate identified risks, the project incorporates the following measures:
Community Engagement: Strengthening community engagement and participation through transparent communication, consultation, and involvement in decision-making processes.
Security Protocols: Implementing robust security protocols, including security assessments, staff training, and contingency plans, to ensure the safety of staff and beneficiaries.
Diversification of Suppliers: Establishing alternative supply sources and prepositioning stocks to mitigate supply chain disruptions and ensure uninterrupted access to essential nutrients.
Partnership and Coordination: Strengthening partnerships with local authorities, stakeholders, and humanitarian actors to leverage resources, share information, and coordinate responses effectively.
By integrating these measures into the project design and implementation, the intervention aims to enhance its effectiveness, sustainability, and impact in addressing malnutrition among CU5 and PLWs in NWS.
