Project Summary

This project is part of a health project (HSYR20-HEA-163953-1) that aims to produce, implement prosthesis for amputees and providing a vocational training.
Almost three million people are currently living in Non-State Armed Group-controlled (NSAG) areas of Idleb, western Aleppo, northern Hama and Eastern Latakia, where the security situation remains volatile and unstable. The ongoing conflict has had a negative impact on civilian populations, resulted in mass displacements and the destruction of civilian infrastructure, including medical facilities and settlements hosting displaced populations. Less than half of the previously existing public health facilities remain functional across areas that may soon witness increased violence.
With increasing violence and continued displacement to the north of Syria and many factors, amputations are increasing against the acute shortage of artificial limbs, existing rehabilitation services do not come close to coping with this growing demand. There is a great, unmet need for orthopedic devices (prostheses and orthoses) in low-income people. Only about five percent of those who need prosthetics and orthotics (P&O) services have access to them.
Large numbers of war injuries resulting from hostilities continue are being reported. It is reported that about 30 percent of trauma injuries lead to permanent disability and that the rest of trauma survivors suffer from temporary physical disabilities. This is a matter of concern because the intensive fighting in eastern Syria during the second half of 2019 has increased the number of trauma cases, The continuation of hostilities in the previous months and the resulting shifts in control caused more war injuries that require acute care.
For this reason, Balsam has established a prosthetic and orthotic center in Termanin, Idleb. This center provides amputees with a variety of services, the most important of which are: manufacturing of lower limbs, manufacturing plastic splints, medical shoes for the disabled, limb maintenance, special training exercises after installation, physiotherapy (physical), psychosocial support.
In order to provide these services of the highest quality, Balsam has contracted with professional specialists with expertise in the manufacture, installation and maintenance of prosthetics. In addition, orthopedic specialists with expertise in amputation have been contracted for consultations and referrals.
The loss of livelihoods, jobs and the inability to work have complicated the situation. Education cannot be delayed in times of crisis because it provides hope, stability and protection. In this sense, Balsam is working on building an integrated system for people with movement disabilities and war injuries.
Balsam Vocational Training Center is a part of Balsam’s system for people with movement disabilities. It is considered the second stage following the installation of limbs and physiotherapy for the injured. This stage includes the vocational training and practical rehabilitation of patients in a range of professions that have been selected on the basis of the situation of the injured and their mobility, as well as the community demand for the services resulting from these exercises. This package includes training on (sewing - repairing mobile phones - Computer). This professional training includes professional and scientific knowledge sufficient to enter the labor market and the integration of people with disabilities with the society in a natural way.

4 - Needs

Recent data suggests that the current prevalence rate of persons with disabilities aged 12 and above in Syria is 27 percent, which is nearly double the global estimate (United Nations Humanitarian Needs Assessment Programme HNAP 2019. Disability: Prevalence and Impact . According to a field study conducted by Balsam on a group of the injured in Idleb region, about 63% of the population surveyed are unemployed and about 66% of them do not have a steady source of income and depend on providing their living needs on other members of the family (especially women) and the support of civil society organizations, 95% of them said that the medical and vocational rehabilitation programs were insufficient, and the first need for the injured was to provide job opportunities according to the opinion of 67% of the target group.

5 - Implementation of activities

Registration on the training program:By prior announcement on the Balsam website and its official social media pages, registration is completed by filling out an online form. Selection of beneficiaries:After registration, the project management team will filter the names of applicants according to the selection criteria for vocational training, namely:- An injury to the lower limb exclusively.- At least a primary education certificate.- Family headed by a woman.- Lack of fixed monthly income or fixed income below 15,000 Syrian pounds per month.- Age from 18 to 40 years. Communicate with the beneficiaries and confirm the date and place of the training:Using a WhatsApp group to contact with the accepted beneficiaries and inform them of the date, time and location of training courses. Vocational training:The total number of training courses is 6 courses (2 computer maintenance, 2 mobile maintenance, 2 sewing) each course contains 20 trainees and last for one month during which the beneficiary gets theoretical and practical information on the subject of training. At the end of each training course, a theoretical and practical professional evaluation of the beneficiaries is conducted by the trainers and a graduation ceremony for the training course.
