Project Summary

The project will focus on reducing malnutrition in northeastern Syria by running a stabilization center where malnutrition cases will be treated.
The intervention will address the rising cases of malnutrition in Raqqah, Al-Hasakah Governantes
Where the intervention was fragile compared to the poor nutritional status.
The project will target the sub districts of Tell Abiad(Tell Abiad)- Tell Abiad(Suluk) – Al-Hasakeh(Ras Al Ain)
Were three stabilization centers will be prepared with a capacity of 30 bed each and will have a team consist of 3 specialized women doctors and 6 nurses, and there will be three shifts for the staff as follows:
8:00 – 16:00
16:00 – 00:00
00:00 – 8:00
The stabilization centers will also be prepared with a kitchen and will serve 3 daily meals for the staff and the patients.
6 RRT will be trained and prepared with the necessary equipment, each team will consist of (2CHW-1 nuitrtionst-1team leader and a driver)
The teams will scan the targeted camps for malnutrition cases using MUAC measurement devices and will refer cases with SAM or MAM to the stabilization centers where they will receive treatments based on each case requirements.
The target of the intervention is to reach around 3000 children from the age of 6-59 months.
The cases will then be given treatments in the centers and monitored for improvements.
After the beneficiaries receive their treatments the beneficiaries’ families that are uncovered by any food security program will be given food vouchers after matching and coordinating with other organizations the food vouchers will cover the beneficiaries for 6 months to make sure that the children don’t relapse into stunning again.
As for M&E plan a base line and an end line for the beneficiaries will be conducted via the MEAL team for the effect of the treatments and the food vouchers can be measured, the team will conduct monthly visits for the centers to measure the beneficiary satisfaction, also the M&E team wıll conduct monthy visits to the contracted and non-contracted markets in the project area to compare the prices of food items.
Also, there will be a hotline provided to the beneficiaries to respond to any complaints.

3 - Needs

Syria faces a multitude of challenges that make it one of the world’s most complex humanitarian emergencies. According to (HNO, 2023) 12.1M people are estimated to be food insecure. Over 2M people live in informal settlements and planned camps, a 5% increase since 2021. Moreover, 85% of households are unable to meet basic food needs in 2022.The crisis in Syria has entered its eleventh year, seen further exacerbating the food security and livelihood situation of families across the country. Food security remains a serious challenge due to continued localized hostilities, new and protracted displacements, increased numbers of recent returnees, and the sustained erosion of communities' resilience, in addition to, loss the value of local currency with rising the food prices continuously.ONSUR’s proposed intervention seeks to improve the resilience of vulnerable children who are suffering from malnutrition and the PLWs in both Raqqah, Al-Hasakah Governance. The intervention will contribute to covering the Nutrition sector gaps in Tell Abiad, Suluk, and Ras Al Ain.

4 - Implementation of activities

The Implementation plan of activities will start in 01 JAN 2023, the proposal activities will include the following :1. Renting and preparation of the three stabilization centers in Tell Abiad, Suluk, and Ras Al Ain.2. Recruiting and training RRT to do the scanning for malnutrition.3. Recruiting the staff for the stabilization centers.4. Providing treatment for around 3000 children cases with malnutrition in the centers and follow-ups by the team.5. Providing families with malnutrition cases with Food vouchers if they are not receiving any food aid by other organizations.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

Protection risks will be analyzed throughout the project life-cycle. According to the project activities, the following protection risks and mitigating measures are presented: 1)- Risk of exploitation or abuse by staff members/service providers if access to services Mitigating measures: Communicate regularly and clearly to the community about what services are available and who services are for. Provide regular training to staff and service providers for code of conduct and PSEA. 2)- Risk of doing harm or perpetuating unintended consequences to girls, and boys (and their caregivers) through the provision of specialized child protection services if service providers are not adequately trained/ services are of low-quality. Mitigating measures: 1. Ensure training packages are in line child protection minimum standards and consider remote managed context 2. Services should not be provided by any staff who have not received and demonstrated proficiency in training modules. 3. Service mappings should only include services that have been determined to provide safe and quality services. 4. Establish consultation with communities and ensure feedback mechanisms are in place

9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumptions: ONSUR has good experience in supporting children and women, we have good access in the targeted location in the northeast of Syria and also strong connections with the administrators, furthermore we have good knowledge of the area and the needs of the people and how to interact with the people that we are going to provide support to Risks: 1-the local community not understanding of the project activites, ONSUR will conduct meetings with the local communities leaders to explain the project activities and how it will benefit them 2- Issues regarding the safety of the staff during the changing of shifts ONSUR will provide cars for the teams so they can move safely, 3- Expired materials ONSUR will thoroughly check on all of the materials that will be used. 4- Covid -19 issue ONSUR will strictly adhere to the cluster and WHO recommendations on prevention measures related to COVID-19 (wearing masks, social distancing, increasing the number of distribution points) during the scanning for cases and in the stabilization center.
