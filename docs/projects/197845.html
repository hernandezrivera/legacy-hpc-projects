Project Summary

DEMTOU Humanitarian will implement a 6 months program which will focus on providing a tailored medico-nutritional response to the most pressing needs of the displaced and host communities living in Konye health district, while guaranteeing a protective environment to all.
This intervention will implement activities in two key sectors: nutrition and health. Health and nutrition activities will constitute most of the activities, while specific WASH activities will be directly integrated into health and nutrition sectors in support of those interventions. Protection will be mainstreamed throughout this intervention to ensure a ‘do no harm approach’.
Health Sector
The purpose of the activities carried out in the framework of the health sector is to improve access to community, primary and secondary health care for the populations affected by the anglophone crisis in 8 health areas of the Konye Health District, located in the Meme Division (Southwest Cameroon). The medical assistance delivered in the framework of this intervention will cover the following sub-sectors: health systems support, basic primary care, higher care level, and public health emergencies. For each sub-sector, activities will be tailored to the specific needs of the targeted beneficiaries and carried out at facility and/or community level.
Specific attention will be paid to the medical assistance provided to victims of gender-based violence (GBV) and a victim-centered approach will be adopted. The integration of mental health activities at facility and community level will also be fostered in order to reduce the population’s general state of stress, relieve psychological suffering among traumatized people and to reinforce both individual and community resilience.
Considering the highly volatile security environment and strong mobility restrictions in application in Konye HD, DEMTOU will put a specific emphasis on facilitating referrals to supported health centers and access to free and quality primary, secondary and community health care.
Nutrition Sector
The purpose of the activities carried out in the framework is to improve access to quality nutritional care to children under five who are most affected by the crisis in the area of intervention of this project. The nutritional assistance delivered in the framework of this intervention will cover the following sub-sectors: maternal infant and young child nutrition in emergencies and management of acute malnutrition.
Activities will aim to foster early detection and treatment of acute malnutrition, including its medical complications and psychological needs, among children under five. They will be carried out at facility and community level. Given the extremely volatile security environment in the Meme Division, DEMTOU will put a specific emphasis on the involvement of local communities in the prevention of malnutrition through the training of CHWs, parents (especially, mothers) and caretakers on the MUAC for Parents Approach.
WASH
According to the needs assessment conducted in december 2022 (needs assessment report, DEMTOU Humanitarian), 5 out of 14 health facilities assessed (in Konye, Kumba North, Kumba South, Mamfe and Muyuka health districts) had latrines in a very bad states. On the other hand, untreated water taken directly from unreliable waterpoints is the source of water borne diseases, which form a high percentage of morbidity among the population. Significant household income is spent in treating these diseases, which also lead to a decline in productivity. This intervention will allow DEMTOU to continue to provide much needed support for WASH services (rehabilitation and community-based awareness) in Meme and Manyu divisions, and to implement programs to support vulnerable IDPs.

1 - Besoins / Needs

To better tailor its response to the most pressing needs identified during its rapid needs assessment in December 2022, DEMTOU put forward an integrated emergency response taking into consideration humanitarian needs, the presence of IDPs, access to the health area and beneficiaries, the size of the population and the functionality of health facilities. As a result, the targeted scope of this project encompasses 8 health areas of Konye HD where services will be provided free of charge in 7 health facilities.In the framework of this action, DEMTOU will address nutritional and health needs of the targeted health areas by guaranteeing access to free and quality primary care for vulnerable displaced populations and host communities. Thus, DEMTOU will provide support to health care delivered at primary care level (including nutrition, maternal, reproductive and mental health care) in all facilities. As to the secondary care level, DEMTOU will provide support to inpatient units of Konye District Hospital. DEMTOU will ensure referrals and counter-referrals of pediatric and obstetrical cases with complications to this reference facility. For C-sections, patients will also be referred to konye District Hospital. As to complex adult emergencies, patients will be oriented to the Regional Hospital annex of Kumba. DEMTOU will provide capacity-building to the Konye District Hospital. Capacity-building will include training sessions, focused on the quality of care for certain diseases, such as severe acute malnutrition and specific child diseases (such as malaria, diarrhea and ARI), patient monitoring, management of inputs and medicines as well as the maintenance of medical equipment. DEMTOU will supervise the project’s activities. As there is currently not sufficient and adequate medical staff working at the Konye District Hospital, DEMTOU will give in priority incentivise new MoH medical staff who will be recruited by the HD of Konye to support the Center’s IPD and ITFC activities. If they are not available among the MoH’s pool of staff, these specific profiles will be directly hired under DEMTOU's contracts, namely ITFC/IPD doctors and midwives. In addition, DEMTOU will also have on site an Activity Supervisor, who will be part of DEMTOU’s project’s coordination team. He/She will be based at the District Hospital, in Konye, and will be responsible for supervising the IPD and ITFC activities. Last but not least, DEMTOU will incentivize additional local MoH nurses, nurses aid, hygienists, vaccinators, pharmacy store keepers and laboratory technicians to strengthen the capacities of the health facility.
