Project Summary

Lack of enough WASH services to the population is a main cause of spread of Acute Diarrheal Diseases (ADD) in IDPs in the project target regions, leading to malnutrition in the country and contributing to increasing the existing stunting rate of 25.3% in children below 5 years of age. It is also a cause of relapse (ADD) when treated patients and children returning to their communities where safe water and sanitation coverages are limited, resulting in limited sustainability of health programs.
IRW through this intervention will identify distribution locations through in-depth assessment, and Relief Distribution Committees (RDCs) will be established at each distribution site, to facilitate the identification of the most vulnerable households and their needs, through community participation. This activity will also imply training of the beneficiaries on household water treatment, which will be conducted house-to-house by trained community hygiene promoters. The project activities will also include Focus Group Discussions (FGDs) with women and girls in order to identify collective water points with secure location and access. At the same time, Water Management Committees (WMCs) will be established and trained in each area, which will be responsible for the maintenance of water points identified. vulnerable children, women and men living in the most affected target areas will be supported by this project. From these beneficiaries women constitute 50%, who are the ones most affected by poor water supply and sanitation as the role is traditionally left for them. Selection of beneficiaries will be based on level of vulnerability, access and control over resources; gender (with particular focus on women) and age (children).In this project, IRW will continue its practice of mainstreaming gender in its operations through ensuring that female officers are hired so that the staff can have unfettered access to, and communication with, project beneficiaries and that gender relevant information is collected.
The project activities are designed in a way that minimizes the workloads of women related to water collection and helps women to have more time for other domestic and economic activities otherwise that would have been wasted traveling long distances in
Through this intervention will conduct rehabilitation/improvement of existing water points and construction/ rehabilitation of sanitation facilities, in order to make them secure, protected and gender-appropriate (gender-segregated latrines with provision of locks and doors). This will include other FGDs, 50% with women and 50% with men. These activities will be paired with community sensitization on the use and maintenance of safe water sources and appropriate sanitation facilities, and on the use of correct hygiene and sanitation practices, e.g. the need of washing hands at critical moments, food preservation and proper storage of water safe for drinking, and discourage defecation, especially near open water sources. Community sessions of hygiene promotion will be conducted in common spaces in various locations, accessible by all categories of marginalized persons, by trained hygiene promoters in the course of one year. The project will consult PWD by consulting in the beneficiary identification and community consultation sessions. All the beneficiaries with disability of any sort will be identified and included in the beneficiaries. Based on their demands and circumstances the water facilities will be designed and constructed. In addition, there will be a complaint and response mechanism system established for PWDA at community level where the PWDs can give their feedback and feeling toward the overall project implementation process. All the complaints will be handled in an organized and participatory manner. There will be selected PWDs from community members acting as beneficiary reference groups who can share the feelings and observation of the community toward the project.

Mitigation Measures

The project will involve persons with disability in this project through; • Awareness: Raise awareness among staff and stakeholders (including persons with disabilities themselves) of disability rights and disability inclusion principles and practices;• Participation: Consult persons with disabilities and their families and involve them in all stages of the project cycle (including at the assessment stage to ensure their needs are communicated). Partner and consult with Disabled Person’s Organizations, local authorities, disability-specific organizations and service providers in all stages of the project.The project will Include disability-specific questions in the assessment tools to ask about the situation of persons with disabilities, including barriers they face and their needs and priorities in relation to the project. Then it will organize separate focus group discussions/key informant interviews with persons with disabilities. Preferably, focus group discussions will be separated by gender, with one for women with disabilities and one for men with disabilities. IRW will ensure that planning ways to have persons with disabilities involved not only as project beneficiaries, but also as project staff, in project governance structures, and in the monitoring and evaluation of the project (e.g. persons with disabilities and/ or their representatives should be included in project steering committees). With regards to service provision of the project, all water infrastructure will be availed to accommodate persons with physical impairments. Also, awareness-raising, promotional, teaching and learning materials provided through the project will be made available in alternative formats (e.g. large print, audio, etc.). In addition, beneficiary feedback mechanism will be established. Water committees for water points will also serve as a source of consultation and feedback collection from beneficiaries. This will avert the risk of beneficiaries not using complaint and feedback mechanisms.

Please provide further explanation

The project will address the urgent needs of affected communities to reduce the vulnerability among IDPs and host communities through provision of clean water, construction/rehabilitation of sanitation facilities and hygiene promotion campaigns, the project is linked to the government national development plan and will support the people in need to cope with natural and manmade disasters. The project will ensure target communities and relevant line departments will actively participate from the inception of the project to monitoring/follow up and evaluation stages ensuring they take over the responsibility of sustaining the project up on the exit of Islamic relief, IRW support will be handed over to the communities through line ministries and the community will have the right to utilize the services/utilities provided.
