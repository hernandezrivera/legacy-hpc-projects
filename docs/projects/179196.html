Project Summary

This project seeks to improve the lives of women (female-headed households) by supporting special projects for women within local communities and helping them to establish their own businesses in light of Corona pandemic, where the women will have their home-works which allow them to avoid any physical contact or going to public places seeking for out-door services.
54 women (families led by a woman) will be supported as direct beneficiaries by setting up projects that will secure a good financial income for her family which will highly enhance their resilience in the targeted community, especially in light of the global pandemic (Virus Corona) which had a major role in stopping a large part of business and industries in the region.
The profits that each beneficiary will gain are sufficient to meet their basic needs during the month without the need to seek the help of any other resource

4 - Needs

According to HRP 2021, Erosion of basic and social services, disruption of livelihoods, depletion of productive assets, and limited economic opportunities have all contributed to increasing socio-economic vulnerabilities in many governorates in Syria, The cities and towns of the Idleb countryside are witnessing a rise in the prices of bread, due to the lack of ‎flour in the furnaces and the rise in the prices of fuel which coming from areas controlled by the "Syrian Democratic ‎Forces" (Qassad), and the prices did not rise in a region without others, but also all regions, notably (Harem - kafar takarem - Armanaz - Salken‏‏‎) so that all the region suffers from this problem.‎When we asked about the current status of bread, the answer was as follows:‎The number of furnaces declined due to the inability to provide their needs (fuel, flour, wood, ....)‎Now, The number of furnaces in the area is too low, So that most families in the region depend on Al Tannour ‎bread because it is the main alternative to baking ovens in the areas of Idleb, where most women prepare ‎it manually because of its production costs are simple and its preparation is very easy and fast, it also has a wonderful ‎taste and can be stored for two or three days, This is important in the absence of electricity.‎Bread status between 2011-2021Bread production has started to decline due to bombardments, explosive materials, smoke and gunpowder, the loss ‎of flour as a result of the decline in wheat cultivation in the region, the destruction of many bakeries as a result of ‎shelling and clashes, and the huge increase in the population as a result of internal displacement.‎

5 - Implementation of activities

The support will be provided to women at two levels:Providing specialized medical training remotely (online) to raise their awareness of everything related to the Corona pandemic through a medical specialist who provides training for all beneficiariesEach household will be provided with a twice hygiene basket Supporting 54 women with Women will be divided into 18 groups, each group consisting of three women, taking into account the criteria for prevention of Corona virus, where the group consisting of three women is considered an acceptable a precaution measures in terms of social distance.a)each group of three women will be given an oven, bowls, carpets, bags, baking stuff, flour, yeast, oil, wood, and water, the ovens will be distributed in a way to suit the local market in terms of selling the products. b) women will be only provided with the daily material, providing that they produce 50 bags of bread to be sold at prices that are minimally less than the market price. After that, every three women will produce 50 bundles of bread to be sold at a supported price less than the prices in the markets 400 Syrian pounds with average weight is equivalent to 1.2 kg, then the daily income will be gathered by Amal finance assistant during three months to be buy 3.77 ton of flour for each group after the project, and the flour will be handed over to the management committee, this amount of flour will be enough to work for additional 75.4 days. Amal organization will hire trainers to deliver two kinds of on-line trainings to the women about marketing and how to sustain the business, also training about the good technical practices of bread production by an experienced woman, Establishing of the management committee:
