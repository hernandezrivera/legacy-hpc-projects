Project Summary

the components of the health activities:
- six PHCs & one mobile clinic
As means to ensure access to basic essential Primary Health Care during the COVID-19 pandemic, AFAQ is supporting six Primary Health Care (PHC) centers and one mobile team to cover the locations in Aleppo and Idelb governorates. This will be implemented in close coordination with the Health Cluster and local health directorate. The selected facilities were prioritized in terms of importance, case load, location, availability of services and IDPs concentrations.
- Two physiotherapy centers
The aim of the rehabilitation services for disabilities is to aid them to gain independence at the highest level they can, with the most efficient possible movement.
in order to decrease the pressure on the services and because of the large number of
patients need rehabilitation services , this center will continue to deliver its services.
The Rehabilitation “Physiotherapy & PSS” Services will target the following cases:
• Fracture.
• Spinal Cord Injury.
• Cerebral Palsy.
• Traumatic Brain Injury.
• Club Foot.
• Amputation.
• Burns.
• Stroke.
• Poliomyelitis.
• peripheral nerve injuries PNI
- Comprehensive "Maternal and Child health" center.
• operate an emergency obstetric and neonatal care unit and services (BEmONC & CEmONC) and provide reproductive health care to women in the form of antenatal, prenatal, natal, and postnatal care for women.
• Provide family planning services.
• Provide gynecologic, pediatric and nutrition inpatient and outpatient services for women and children.
• Conduct mobilization activities focusing on reproductive and child health for women by 4 CHWs.
• Provide essential mental health and psychosocial support services.
• Support the provision of essential medicines, medical supplies, and equipment to direct beneﬁciaries; and the provision of reproductive health and safe delivery kits.
- one kidney dialysis center
92 ESRD patients registered at this center , 12 dialysis machines , 24/24 services.
-20 CHWs .
The roles and activities of community health workers (CHWs) are tailored to meet the unique needs of the communities they serve. A CHW's role depends on factors such their education, training, lived experience, and experience working with specific populations. CHWs may perform the following roles:
• Create connections between vulnerable populations and healthcare providers
• Help patients navigate healthcare and social service systems
• Manage care and care transitions for vulnerable populations
• Educate healthcare providers and stakeholders about community health needs
• Provide culturally appropriate health education on topics related to chronic disease prevention, physical activity, and nutrition
• Advocate for underserved individuals or communities to receive services and resources to address health needs
• Collect data and relay information to stakeholders to inform programs and policies

4 - Needs

- Primary health care: (6 PHCs - Mobile clinic and the CHWs)An estimated 4 million people living and being displaced throughout northwest Syria (NWS) continue to endure a severe humanitarian crisis. Their lives continue to be impacted by the compound effects of protracted conflict, ongoing hostilities, and sharp economic decline, all of which impact their well-being. The crisis is characterized by mass displacements, and destruction of critical civilian infrastructure, which results in lack of access to protection and basic services, including access to healthcare services.- Physiotherapy centers :At least 15 per cent of the population of Syria lives with disabilities.”HNO2019” A recent assessment on disability among adults in North western Syria revealed that an average of 45 per cent of surveyed persons injured during the Syrian crisis, and they are expected to sustain a permanent impairment and require specialist support which will far outlast the crisis.- Comprehensive Maternal and Child heathThis project aims to provide life-saving and life-sustaining coordinated, equitable health services for all age and sex groups; boys, girls, women, and men, with attention to people with special needs without any sexual discrimination, among IDPs and host communities in Salqin, Harim, northwest Syria through run and support a specialized hospital that provides comprehensive child and maternal health services, including women's surgery- one kidney dialysis center In north-west Syria, a total of 837 renal failure patients are currently receiving dialysis services through 20 facilities (6,575 sessions on a monthly basis). Although the availability of such services is limited, it is essential for this treatment to continue.
