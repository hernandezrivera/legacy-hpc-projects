Project Summary

The Project aims at supporting education needs of the most vulnerable crisis affected displaced and non – displaced 8177 children (4088 boys and 4089 girls) to access safe, inclusive and continuous learning opportunities in Banadir/Mogadishu, Hiraan, Bay, Middle and Lower Shabelle Regions. The project further aims at enabling safe learning environment of the targeted children/boys and girls that is essential for their physical and emotional development and further keep them away from involvement with armed actors and ensure their protection from exposure to abuse of their rights. The project will put special emphasis on school aged children (boys and girls) living with disabilities to enhance their access and inclusion to education since children (Boys and girls) with disabilities face significant challenges to access an inclusive learning environment as exemplified by recent reports that identified prevalent negative attitudes towards children with disabilities by teachers and parents, and structural challenges that limit them from accessing an inclusive learning environment.
Of particular concern is Ministry of education’s limited capacity to effectively deliver basic education services for IDPs, children living in areas affected by common cases of conflict, insecurity and common shocks against increasing rates of displacements while enrolments rates are at an upward trend. According to UNFPA data, 17% of IDP children access education where primary and secondary has gross enrolments of 30% and 26% respectively. The increase of children (boys and girls) in need of education assistance in 2022 is attributed to the increased population notwithstanding impacts of COVID-19 pandemic, economic deterioration, ongoing environmental shocks and conflict as well as ongoing political tension from delayed elections in the project targeted locations hence need for project to enhance sustained increase of access to education and enable for retention of children in schools in the project locations.

1. - Projects activities

1. Social mobilization to sensitize duty bearers including camp leaders, community leadership and local authorities on their roles and responsibilities to protect and support all children and ensure safe access to and at the school as per the Safe Schools Declaration 2. Establishing community dialogues to raise awareness on mitigating risks and all kinds of child abuse practices to enhance children’s access and retention in education 3. Provision of safe, dignified and gender friendly school facilities including adequate gender segregated latrines at the project targeted locations 4. Provision of emergency school feeding as well as provision of safe drinking water and hygienepromotion in the project targeted schools 5. Provision of sanitary materials to girls at the targeted schools in the project targeted locations 6. Training of MoE Staff and teachers in protective and socio - emotional teaching and learning and psycho social support 7. Provision of emergency teaching and learning materials school to the targeted children and youth (M/F) 8. In addition, adhering to PSEA policy and ensuring girls and boys including those with disabilities are fully aware of the project to mitigate sexual exploitation and abuse during the project. Moreover, ensuring that complaints and feedback mechanisms are in place for easy access by the targeted beneficiaries and other community members
