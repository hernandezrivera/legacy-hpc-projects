Project Summary

ith the approach of the civil war in Syria in its eleventh year, nearly half a million Syrians have lost their lives Civilians, especially women and children, are at constant risk of injury or death as a result of airstrikes and the ongoing fighting between armed groups.
Children are still exposed to multiple protection risks and severe abuses and grave rights violations, Child labor, including in its most hazardous forms such as recruitment and use of children in combat and support roles, violence, abuse, neglect, and exploitation, including GBV, early marriage, early pregnancy, the psychosocial needs of many girls and boys are left unmet.
Those children, according to previous reports, will suffer from a lot of consequences. Mostly, they will show post-trauma symptoms, these symptoms may include one or more of these: irritable, angry, or aggressive behavior, including extreme temper tantrums, hypervigilance, exaggerated startle response, problems with concentration, difficulty falling or staying asleep or restless sleep.
Child survivors would not receive individual support to cope and recover from their experiences.
Adverse childhood experiences may lead to negative health and wellbeing outcomes in the long- term.
Psychological support for people exposed to intense violence and/or experiencing lasting distress would also be unavailable, reducing people’s ability to cope and recover.
Amal is proposing a project to support the children and caregivers through establishing two centers ( CFSs) to provide safe protection services and facilitate their access to them in the targeted area
as, will enhance equitable access to quality CP services.
This project will contribute to the Child Protection sector HRP objectives,
so, the goal of the proposed project is to enhance the protection environment and access to CP services for conflict-affected children with a focus on the most vulnerable individuals
(e.g. people with disabilities (PWDs)) through establishing two of CFSs in Kafr Takharim\Idlib and Afrin\Aleppo.
The proposed project is based on supporting a safe environment for children, where they feel safe physically and psychologically.
the project will provide the following activities:
- PSS (structured and unstructured) for children
- awareness sessions (structured and unstructured) for children, community members, and caregivers.
- Specialized child protection services (case management) for girls and boys are available in targeted locations and referral services,
, recreational activities in addition in- kind assistance for children at risk like separated and unaccompanied children, neglected, vulnerable to early marriage, at risk of CAFFAG to support them and improve their psychological well-being, furthermore, Amal is committed to sharing 4Ws reports per month and reflected the gender perspective in all the activities, guidance Note on Gender-Based Violence Service Provision will be applied to all staff, volunteers, contractors and for sure the beneficiaries.
The new IDPs will receive PFA from Amal field team.

4 - Needs

With the approaches of the civil war in Syria its tenth year, nearly half a million Syrians have lost their lives Civilians, especially women and children, are at constant risk of injury or death as a result of airstrikes and the ongoing fighting between armed groups. According to HNO 2021, around 13.4 million people are estimated to require some form of humanitarian and protection assistance, including 6 million in acute need, 25% of them have disabilities and 75% of them are women and children, where children with and without disabilities comprise 44% of the people in need across Syria. As of February 2021, approximately 1.7 million IDPs were living in 1,385 camps or informal sites (including 24,800 people displaced in January and February 2021 alone), in Syria, children have gone through a very difficult situation, a lot of them injured or suffered from psychological trauma, with no effective CP prevention. According to HRP 2020, Children continue to be exposed to multiple protection risks and severe abuses and grave rights violations, Child labor, including in its most hazardous forms such as recruitment and use of children in combat and support roles, violence, abuse, neglect, and exploitation, including GBV, early marriage, early pregnancy, the psychosocial needs of many girls and boys are left unmet. Adverse childhood experiences may lead to negative health and wellbeing outcomes in the long- term.

The main activities of both centers will be as follow:1. Create an integrated two safe spaces.2. Provide unstructured awareness-raising sessions for children and community members(caregivers, local authorities, Humanitarian workers..) on 4 topics (PSEA,CSG,CR,COVID-19) 3. Provide structured awareness sessions ( ECP methodology) for caregivers.4. Provide structured PSS sessions using different methodologies that are appropriate for each age group- from 6 to 8 years old- from 9 to 11 years old- from 12 to 14 years old- from 15 to 18) years old5. Provide unstructured recreational activities6. Provide specialized CM services to at-risk children.7. Provide referral services for children(linking the beneficiaries with other services that are not available in centers by creating services maps)8. Provide In-kind assistance that will be delivered once and Amal will follow specific vulnerability criteria based on CM SOPs of the CP -cluster in NWS.Amal will integrate PWDs and facilitate their access through rehabilitating its centers (Ramp,toilet,chair,etc.)in addition, Amal will be responsible of transporting the children between centers and them homes by private vehicles, furthermore it will keep closely the protection cluster recommendations regarding COVID-19 considerations.

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

Protection risk 1: survivors drop out of the case plan because they have high expectations that cannot be met. probability: medium Impact: high Mitigation measures: the case management team will have a survivor-centered approach and will remain realistic when discussing the available options with the survivor. Protection risk 2: Beneficiaries will not have access to services if movement restrictions related to COVID-19 increase probability: high Impact: medium Mitigation measures: AMAL will put in place a contingency plan to resume activities online. Protection Risk 3: Children with disabilities have no access to the services. Probability: medium Impact: high Mitigation measures: Ensure our centers are accessible to children with disabilities, particularly those with physical disabilities through rehabilitating the WASH facility in the center and the center entrance to be more easily accessible. Protection Risk 4: COVID-19: positive cases are reported to be widely spread among the population of Northwest Syria. Probability: high, Impact: medium Mitigation measure: using hand sanitizers, and the beneficiaries, avoid sharing personal items, wear gloves and masks, Promote the hygiene of the CFS and sessions locations by ensuring and appropriate sterilization and cleaning daily, Emphasis social distance during sessions inside the CFS and during the outreach activities among children and parents, provide awareness raising orientation about COVID-19 risk and ensure the presence of


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Risk: Risk of exploitation or abuse by staff members/service providers, and low knowledge on PSEA and other types of exploitation and where to report. Impact: high protection risk Mitigation: Amal has policies, procedures, and other measures in place that relate to preventing sexual exploitation, abuse, and harassment. So, Amal will work on the following: 1- train the staff on code of conduct, PSEA, CSG and follow them periodically 2- Amal ensures to have in a place an accessible mechanism for reporting sexual exploitation and abuse in line with Amal safeguarding policy. 3- Activating complaints mechanisms to protect PSEA in particular, placing them in easily accessible places, desigsning awareness-raising banners, placing boxes for complaints and suggestions, and activating the hotline number (WhatsApp) 4- Designing special complaints mechanisms for children being children through drawing or other tools 5 - provide awareness-raising sessions in the targeted community on PSEA and methods for reporting them and gaining the trust of society and spreading humanitarian principles and stick to them.
