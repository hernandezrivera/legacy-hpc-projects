Project Summary

The overall objective of the project is to provide sustainable WASH services and facilities to 3400 IDPs in Abu Jubaiha and 510 returnees in Abu Kashola localities, South Kordofan State. South Kordofan is considered one of the states with the greatest humanitarian assistance needs in Sudan due to effects of protracted conflict. The project will seek to address the target IDPs’ and returnees' immediate WASH needs through rehabilitation of water yards and hand pumps, household latrine rehabilitation, hygiene promotion activities and environmental sanitation services.
Women will be engaged in the project by incorporating them into the established WASH committees and hygiene promotion teams. This will ensure women’s needs (including those related to their safety and protection against SGBV) are identified and addressed in the planning and decision-making processes of the project. Separate latrines will be rehabilitated for females, specially designed latrines will be constructed for older and disabled people, and complaints and feedback mechanisms will be set up to ensure all concerns and feedback of the target population are addressed effectively and efficiently.

Plan fields

Needs

• Limited access to safe and clean water due to broken down water yards and hand pumps.• Limited access to safe and secure latrines due to limited/broken down latrines.• High risks of the IDPs and returnees to water-related morbidity and mortality (especially during rainy seasons) due to widespread open defecation and poor waste management.• Poor personal and household hygiene due to limited knowledge on safe hygiene practices and facilities (such as soap, hand-washing ibriks).• High risk of women to being victims of SGBV due to limited access to water points and latrines (have to travel for long distances to access the facilities)

Activities

• Rehabilitation of 2 water yards (to be fitted with solar pumping system) and 10 hand pumps (considerations for cash for work will be made regarding delivery of rehabilitation of the water yards and hand pumps)• Support the rehabilitation of 80 household latrines ((considerations for cash for work will be made regarding delivery of construction of the 80 household latrines).• Conducting of weekly hygiene promotion activities such as solid waste collection campaigns, household monitoring visits, soap distribution and jerry-can cleaning campaigns, hygiene promotion and environmental awareness raising through use of performance arts.• Capacity building of the WASH Committees through trainings and networking. • Provision of sanitation/hygiene materials to needy families through distribution of 680 potties, 680 ibriks, soap and female hygiene kits (consideration of delivery method via vouchers will be made).

Impact on environment/natural resources

The project will seek to address environmental sanitation through construction of latrines in suitable sites; weekly solid waste campaigns will seek to improve practices on waste (household) disposal and management; proper disposal of defecation. Hygiene promotors will undertake environmental conservation awareness activities with info on effects tree-cutting. Use of bamboo tree (has high regeneration rate) will be promoted in the construction of super-structure parts of latrines. Use of solar pumping system will seek to promote use of safe and sustainable energy.
