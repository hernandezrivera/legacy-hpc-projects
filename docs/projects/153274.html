Project Summary

The WASH response project is planned for a period of 6 months targeting vulnerable communities of IDPs, host communities and returnees in locations Angulumere Boma, Ugwil Boma, Nyadida Boma of Lafon county in Torit with chronic emergency WASH needs. The planned WASH activities will as well support health interventions by creating synergies with other health, nutrition and food security cluster partners such so as to prevent WASH related disease aimed at reducing morbidity and mortality through provision of clean, safe and adequate water, dignified sanitation services as well as hygiene promotion messaging among the affected population in Angulumere Boma, Ugwil Boma, Nyadida Boma of Lafon county.
The project activities and targets will include Rehabilitation and repair of 15 boreholes in Angulumere Boma, Ugwil Boma, Nyadida Boma of Lafon county (1000 men, 2000 women, 300 boys and 450 girls), construction of 10 sanitary facilities, training of 50 hygiene promoters, House hold hygiene promotions, training of water user committee members, distribution of WASH NFIs benefiting 500 men, 1500 women, 1000 boys, and 1000 girls.
The project intends to benefit 10,000 people majority of who are women and children including IDPs, Host communities, Returnees, among others. This project comes at a time when Lafon County has not only experience high displacements due to the conflict but as well as reducing the risks of Cholera/WASH related disease outbreak. The situation has impacted greatly on the already limited basic services in those locations. Therefore this project will address the needs of the most vulnerable women, men, boys and girls affected by the conflict.

Needs

Republic of South Sudan faces considerable humanitarian problems, which have increased substantially since the outbreak of violence across the country in December 2013.The fighting in Eastern Equatoria state have further aggravated the humanitarian situations, producing secondary or third displacement. Among the most affected Counties in Eastern Equatoria is Lafon County having figures way off thresholds in the thematic areas of WASH, health, nutrition, Education and food security. Rising food insecurity is mostly a result of the deepening economic crisis, insecurity, and depleted food stocks from insufficient household production. High levels of acute malnutrition were driven not only by high food insecurity but also sub-optimal child feeding practices and poor water, sanitation and hygiene. People largely depend on humanitarian support due to poor economic situation and broken down social fabrics. The south Sudan Humanitarian Needs Overview (HNO 2019) identified 5,712,041 people in need of WASH services in south sudan .Although food security presents a primary concern for the affected population, however access to safe drinking water and insufficient safe hygiene practices poses a great risk of WASH disease outbreak. In addition, the dire economic hardships limits communities from accessing basic commodity chains, resulting in lack of much needed WASH NFIs such as soap and Aqua tabs, Jerrycan, backet, filter clothes. The affected populations lack appropriate water containers which limit the volumes that can be collected at a given time, thus increasing the number of trips for collection and elevating risk to those collecting the water (generally women and girls) to GBV cases. Lack of containers also results in unsafe water storage, disrupting the safe water chain. The BEDN WASH Team aims to provide WASH services to communities residing in the above locations, in line with the overall strategy of the WASH cluster
