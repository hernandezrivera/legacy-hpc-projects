Project Summary

In line with SO2 and SO4, PUI aims at meeting the basic WASH needs of the conflict affected population in targeted locations by enhancing their access to safe and adequate water supply and to adequate sanitation services, while fostering sustainability through the rehabilitation of community infrastructures. Therefore, PUI’s plan for 2024 includes implementing the following interventions:
- Sewage Network Rehabilitation, targeting the following locations:
o Rural Damascus (Duma): 500-meter length
o Aleppo (jebel Saman): 1000-meter length
o Dar’a (Mzeireb): 600-meter length
o Hama (Maar Deftin): 900-meter length
o Deir ez-Zor (Mayadin, Abu Kamal, City): 2200-meter length
- Water Network Rehabilitation, targeting the following locations:
o Hama (Sabboura): rehabilitation of one borehole
o Damascus (Yarmouk): rehabilitation of one borehole
o Dar’a (Mzeireb): 5000-meter length
o Deir ez-Zor (Mayadin, City): 6000-meter length
- Provision of Healthcare Unit Hygiene Kits, targeting the following locations:
o Dar’a (Mzeireb): 100 Hygiene Kits
o Hama (Maar Deftin): 100 Hygiene Kits
o Deir ez-Zor (Mayadin, City): 200 Hygiene Kits
- Rehabilitation of one water station in Deir ez-Zor (Abu Kamal)

0 - Project Summary (extended)

PUI aims to build a program to meet the needs of girls, boys, women and men living in intervention locations, taking into consideration vulnerabilities, to enhance their access to basic services, foster their self-sufficiency, but also promote social cohesion while ensuring that the program would have no negative side effects and that the risk for girls, boys, women and men to be exposed to any kind of violence when accessing assistance would be mitigated. PUI policies and operational guidelines align with IASC's Commitments to Accountability to Affected Populations. PUI ensures accountability by placing affected populations at the center of all decisions and actions that affect their lives. Thus, targeted populations will be involved throughout all phases of the project to feed into the design of the interventions and guide the identification of both activities and beneficiaries based on PUI’s selection criteria.
