Project Summary

This intervention project intends to provide dry season farming implements to farmers along the Alau dam to encourage and facilitate dry season farming that would boost agricultural production.
In this intervention, we are going to group and register farmers so that they can have access to micro credits.

Needs

It is a known fact that during the lean season which is characterize by shortage of availability of food to considerable number of households. Therefore, this project is aiming at addressing this problem particularly food shortage and to support economic generating activities and empowerment

Activities

1 Registration of beneficiaries that have met selection criteria2. Training of beneficiaries on dry season modern farming techniques.3. Procurement of dry season agricultural inputs4. Distribution of pumping machines, simple farm tools, dry season seedlings and other accessories respectively.5. Grouping of farmers into smaller unit of associations for proper registration with appropriate government agencies for easy access to micro-credit facilities.6. Official closure of the project intervention ceremony.-To incorporate protection mainstreaming component in the activity, our protection unit is there to address matters arising from gender discrimination. Therefore this intervention in escence will not discriminate in whatever form to any person-10% of the total budget would be allocated to protection sector.-The age group to benefit ranged from 20 years - 55 years.-We are obliged to be accountable to the beneficiaries in all aspects in implementation process. By this commitment, every step taken and decisions reach will involve the active participation of beneficiaries.-We are absolutely committed to protection of individuals irrespective of gender or age. The M&E will generate and document all the ongoing activities for monthly presentation at the sector level to update partners in the coordination centre.-This project will also rely on support from other actors as the case maybe. The Food Security Sector coordination team would be consulted at each level for the inputs and suggestion regardless of time.-Evidences of all activities relating to proper accountability would be given prominence.-We shall continue to consult the coordination unit from the beginning of the project to the end until our aim and objectives are met.
