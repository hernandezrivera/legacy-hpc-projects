Project Summary

The object of project is providing streamlined life-saving, humanitarian multi-sectoral assistance and strengthening the basic infrastructure support in IDP sites, through sites renovations and improvements to key infrastructure.
Balad Organization through this proposed project aims to support 5,118 families (4618 Men, 5106 Women, 8253Boys, 9298 girls) of IDPs who are living in seven camps in A'zaz district (Elbil, Mukaveme, Al Resalah, Yazibag-Safiret AlJabal, Abul-dohur 1, Abul-dohur 2, Abul-dohur 3).
The project will improve living conditions of vulnerable households in northern Syria through rehabilitation damaged tents that are affected by weather in winter by repairing or replacing them, in addition to insulating them.

Needs

According to the statistics of (2018 HNO) Since the beginning of the crisis in Syria about 11.3 million of the country population has been forced to leave their homes, 6.1 million of them are IDPs, while the crisis has affected all people living in Syria in varying degrees. Some of the population and locations have been affected more than others. The most severe needs across multiple sectors are concentrated on areas of ongoing conflict or areas with large numbers of IDPs of the estimated 13.1 million people in need across Syria. 5,6 million people are experiencing acute needs.As well as according to the statistics of (2019 HNO), in spite of the use of IDP sites, including camps, remains an option of last resort, however, the lack of other alt contributed to an increase by 16 per cent of newly displaced IDPs seeking shelter in such sites following significant incidences of displacement, also an estimated 34 per cent of the sites’ population, can't receive minimum food assistance on a monthly basis. Approximately 25 and 26 per cent of the population in sites, do not received adequate shelter and NFI assistance, respectively. Challenges also persist in sanitation, waste removal and water (15% of the population). A large proportion of IDP sites in Syria are informal settlements. According to information received from CCCM cluster, in 8 January 2018. 6,079 IDP families living in sites are affected from the flood in 128 IDP sites, a total of 4,983 tents are completely damaged or partially affected. noting that IDPs show concerns of moving to another camp or to the Reception Centres (temporarily or permanently) for different reasons which include; concerns of losing the land where they constructed their shelters, lack of information about moving back from the temporary shelters to their original shelters, IDPs existing connect in the same sub district for income reasons (i.e. agricultural works, casual work opportunities) and the security concerns of moving due to the clashes which happened in parallel with the flood. therefore, it is better to produce solutions and mitigation procedures for the IDPs sites which are vulnerable to the flood.Balad organization in coordination with AFAD have implemented rapid need assessment in many locations in A'zaz district, to assess needs of IDPs and evaluate prices in the markets.According to the result of assessment had conducted by Balad in seven camps in A'zaz district to define their needs, it was noted that these camps are in serious need of assistance, especially Al-Resalah camp, which is considered one of the largest camps in A'zaz district, and it doesn't receive any support from organizations. There are 5305 tents and 487 caravans inside these camps, 1957 tents are damaged and need to repair or replace in addition to insulation. The needs assessment sources:2018 HNO. 2019 HNO.CCCM Cluster's reports (Flood Effect in IDP Sites in NW Syria, as of 08 January 2019).needs assessment by Ballad.

Implementation of activities

The activities that the project will implement is sites renovations and improvements to key infrastructure, through rehabilitation damaged tents that are affected by weather in winter by repairing them or replacing non-maintainable tents, in addition to isolating them with external, internal(thermal), and ground insulators.Project will include the most needy IDPs, 1677 tents will be repaired and 280 non-maintainable tents will be replaced.To do so, the project will:1- Sign an MOU with AFAD which is responsible for managing the whole camps in north-western of Syria (we have signed MOUs with Local Council of A'zaz and governor of Kilis).2- Although all project activities will not cause a change in the land of the targeted sites, but we will take into consideration the HLP issues if necessary by making both the camps’ managers and the landlords sign consent forms which expressly provide the permission to conduct the project activities without any legal consequences.3- Conduct a topographic survey of the targeted sites prior to implementation of repair and installation of tents to ensure that they will be properly placed and will not be affected by flood.4- Recruitment of staff for the project, staff will be males and females, and all of them will sign the code of conduct and will receive training about it.5- Contract with contractors to perform the required maintenance processes and to install new tents and external and internal insulators, in addition to insulating the ground of tents.6- Begin the maintenance processes and installing the new tents with the insulators according to a predefined mechanism that will be announced to the beneficiaries clearly and with gender and protection considerations mainstreamed into plans, noting that the old tent will be disposed to prevent reusing it and selling the new tents.7- Create a child-friendly spaces, and implement recreational activities for kids, and also distribute gifts to children according to their age and gender, Children under 6 years will get toys, while children from six to 12 years will receive stories, children who go to school will receive a special gift to encourage others to go to school. 8- Set up Complaints Response Mechanism (CRM) in the targeted camps.9- Monitor and evaluate all activities from the beginning of the project until to finish.10- Prepare and submit accurate and timely reports.
