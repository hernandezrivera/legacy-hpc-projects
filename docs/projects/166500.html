Project Summary

The proposed project is aimed to strengthening Health care response system of Borno state through Sensitization and education, distribution of COVID 19 Personal protective equipment to reach 200,000 Internally displaced men, women, boys and girls, construction of 500 hand washing points in IDP camps and communities across Kala/Balge, Damboa, MMC and Jere LGAs of Borno state within six months.
GOALPrime shall identify and work with local influencers, community leaders, religious leaders, youth and women leaders, health workers and partners present and community volunteers in the camps and host-communities and local networks (women’s groups, youth groups, traditional healers, etc.) to sensitize 200,000 individuals through house to house sensitization, distribution of COVID 19 response and prevention printed key massages and public mass awareness raising.
The project team of GOALprime Organization Nigeria shall carry consultations with the project stakeholders which includes a representative of the project beneficiary groups to contextualize the project approach and activities to address the need of the targeted groups across the targeted LGAs.

1 - Needs

The Corona Virus Pandemic which has lasted for just six (6) months yet killed 1.17M people over the world has posed a great threat to humanity. One that has not been evident in the past one century. In Nigeria, the modality rate of the virus raise daily since the first case was recorded on the 27 February 2020, now 93 Deaths have been recorded and 2,802 total cases recorded nationwide. In Borno state, 12 deaths have been recorded of the 82 cases discovered and the population is in panic and a difficult lockdown situation. Although COVID-19 outbreak has significant impacts in all contexts, in Nigeria and the Northeast, it poses a stronger threat because the governance and social systems, including health care systems, have seen substantial damage to their infrastructure and services due to continuous attacks by insurgents and recent flash floods. According to the World Health Organization (WHO), “conflict-affected states have the weakest health infrastructure, and displaced populations are especially vulnerable due to the physical environments they live in as a result of armed violence.” Similarly, Dr. Esperanza Martinez, the head of the International Committee of the Red Cross’s (ICRC) health unit, predicts that the virus (COVID-19) could bring down the entire medical systems in certain countries. One of the governments measures to end the pandemic is a total lockdown of the entire state. This policy is directly affecting the economic situation of low-income earners, daily wage earners, small business owners, and generally people working in casual, private and informal sectors. Vulnerable caregivers who are known to shoulder the responsibility of children, boys and girls have lost their businesses and exposed to a lot of vulnerability such as hunger and disease because they certainly must go out daily before they can feed which would further expose them to a higher risk of the corona virus if they are not intentionally targeted for support. This is the huge reality the affected population are facing. More also, Borno state is faced with the realities of congested camps and the people have demonstrated poor hygiene practices over the years in the camps and host communities. If we must reduce the risk of transmission in amongst the internally displaced persons in camps and host communities, it is expedient to support the people of concern with soaps, water and other hygiene kit as well as increasing the number of water points in camps and host communities to reduce their risk of contracting the virus. There is need to scale up hand washing drills amongst the IDPs so as to foster improved hygiene practices. Although GOALPrime is already carrying out RCCE in response to COVID-19 in 5 LGAs Borno, there is a need to scale up activities in underserved locations and hard to reach areas like Kala/Balge and Damboa LGAs with distribution of hygiene kits to make hygiene promotion more effective.

2 - Activities

• • Consultation with the project stakeholders across the project locations.• Development of appropriate awareness message on coronavirus prevention and referral for schools factoring in the language and visual impairments• Printing of developed appropriate awareness message on coronavirus prevention and referral • Carry out community/camp household sensitization and distribution of the printed COVID 19 response and prevention key massages.• Procurement of COVID 19 PPEs for 50,000 individuals• Procurement of 100 hand washing tools (hand washing stand, soap and other hygiene promotion kits), disinfectant, Chlorine for water chlorination• Biweekly water trucking across IDP camps in the Jere and MMC, and Damboa LGAs• Distribution of COVID 19 personal Protective equipment to 50,000 Vulnerable people• Facilitation of the construction and mounting of 100 hand washing stand in Strategic locations across IDP camps and host communities in the targeted LGAs.• Carryout hygiene promotion in IDP camps and communities across targeted LGAs targeting 200,000 Men, Women, Boys and Girls.
