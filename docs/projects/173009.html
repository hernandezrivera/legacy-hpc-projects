Project Summary


Overal project target: Enhance protection and prevention from GBV in Libya and provide response to identified key issues.
This project aims to ensure provision of support to GBV survivors through effective and inclusive protection mechanisms through key partners and local implementing partners that are a lead in GBV protection, prevention and response.

1 - Needs

Seven years of conflict in Libya and widespread violations of International Human Rights Law, International Humanitarian Law and International Refugee Law have resulted in severe protection needs for IDPs, returnees, affected host communities, refugees and migrants. Women and girls have traditionally had limited participation in the public sphere and continue to face discrimination in law and practice. Gender-based violence is reportedly widespread; 40% of respondents in a 2017 survey stated that GBV was common, 87% of women and girls reported that they needed permission from their husband or parents to seek health care. Humanitarian access for assessment, intervention and support to GBV survivors and to ensure access to basic services and referral pathways continues to constitute a major challenge due to the security context in Libya. Women and girls are often afraid to come forward and report cases of GBV or to access the limited available services for various reasons, including stigma, fear of retaliation by the family, lack of services and lack of awareness and information. Libya has long been a transit and destination country for individuals fleeing conflict and persecution, or seeking to escape from extreme poverty, lack of access to economic, social and cultural rights, and other human rights violations.National partners have significant capacity gaps to respond to GBV cases, to ensure proper prevention and response, including a lack of functional referral mechanisms. In addition, communities lack trust and are often unaware of the importance of reporting and requesting support as a result of cultural, contextual and security reasons.

Implementing partners will follow the COVID 19 IPC (infection prevention control) guidelines.Addressing 4.13 Through Implementing partners a safe space for women and girls will be set up to minimize Exposure of women and girls to violence, by providing a safe location where women and young girls can interact, engage, and participate in focus groups and discussions. this will also provide access to key services and information concerning GBV protection and available services.

6.2 - Other accountability mechanisms

Through the project will set up a complaint and feedback mechanism that will ensure that beneficiaries concerns are addressed. the CFM will be systematically communicated to all beneficiaries including children, caregivers, community leaders and members participating in awareness sessions. Complaints will be received via a dedicated phoneline as well as through boxes places where CP activities will take place.Additionally, UNICEF implementing partners working on GBV protection, prevention and response will be providing case management support for identified survivors of GBV, information received will be fed in through GBVIMS+ and PRIMERO as platforms for reporting monitoring and processing identified GBV cases and response.
