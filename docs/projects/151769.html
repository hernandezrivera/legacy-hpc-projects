Project Summary

Poor rains and extended drought compounded with internal conflict over multiple seasons have had a major impact on rural livelihoods and food security in Somalia, pushing the country to the brink of famine, just a few years after the crisis of 2011 that claimed the lives of over quarter of a million people. As the result, most pastoral and agro pastoral lost 70% of the livestock herd which are the sources of income and food for the households. The project addresses the crises facing more than 4.6 million vulnerable households in Somalia.
The Action aims to prevent further depletion of assets, short term support, promotion of income generation activities and livestock related interventions to stem food insecurity and livelihoods to mitigate costlier interventions in the future. Th Action is therefore designed in line with the needs and priorities identified by the food security cluster to prevent further deterioration of food security and livelihoods of vulnerable households and thus directly contributes to the HRP food security cluster objectives which are:
Objective 1: To improve immediate access to food, priority activities include unconditional transfers (e.g. food assistance and cash), conditional transfers (e.g. cash-for-work for small-scale infrastructure repairs).Approximately 1.6 million people facing severe levels of food insecurity (IPC Phases 3, 4 and 5) will be supported under this objective.
Objective 2: To protect and restore livelihoods related to food and income sources. Priority activities include provision of: (i) seasonally appropriate agricultural inputs (e.g. certified quality seed, farm tools, training, land preparation and irrigation support); (ii) emergency livestock assistance (e.g. supportive treatment, vaccination, feed, fodder production); (iii) livelihood diversification (e.g. fishing, backyard poultry and vocational programmes); (v) improved post-harvest practices; and (vi) response to
Objective 3: To support resilience of vulnerable populations against shocks. This objective will contribute to protecting and conserving natural resources on whose lives and livelihoods depend, such as water, soil, land and forest resources.
VSF Germany is proposing an integrated humanitarian response for food security and livelihoods intervention response to address immediate food access for households in IPC3&4 through unconditional cash transfer, protect and restore livelihoods through animal health services, fisheries input and conditional cash transfer(Cfw) as well as livelihood recovery; livestock herd restoring (restocking ) and capacity building through training of CAHWs . The Action targets 1000hh in IPC 3&4(including IDPs) with cash transfer for immediate food access; 1720hh in IPC2 with livelihood support; Restocking(150hh)-for livelihood recovery; Animal health services(1500hh)-for livelihood protection; Fisheries input-50hh and training-for capacity building(20CAHWS) and with income opportunities through Cash for work(Cfw) -1200hh.The Action targets Maroodjeex and ,Awdal reigns(Somaliland) and Gedo and Bay regions( Southern Somalia)

Needs

Poor rains and extended drought compounded with internal conflict over multiple seasons have had a major negative impact on rural livelihoods and food security in Somalia and pushed the country to the brink of famine. Most pastoral and agro pastoral households have lost 70% of the livestock herd which are the sources of income and food for the households. Acute food insecurity remains prevalent among pastoral households who lost most of their livestock and became destitute during the 2016/2017 drought and Cyclone Sagar; riverine populations lost their crops to floods in April 2018; and others experience protracted displacements due to conflicts and natural hazards. These groups face food consumption gaps or engage in negative coping strategies to meet their food needs. In the absence of assistance, food security outcomes are expected to deteriorate to Emergency in Guban Pastoral livelihood zone and to Crisis in Northern Inland Pastoral livelihood zone in Sool and Sanaag and Riverine Pump Irrigation livelihood zone in Hiran and Gedo. More than 1.5 million people will face Crisis or worse through December 2018. An additional 3.1 million people are classified as Stressed which brings the total number of people facing acute food insecurity through December 2018 to 4.6 million. About 403,000 people (in Awdal 121,000, Woqooyi Galbeed 151,000, Gedo 30000 and Bay 261,000) are facing acute food security Crisis and urgently need humanitarian assistance and livelihood support through 2018. About 920,000 people are in IPC2; Awdal 149,000, Woqooyi Galbeed 401,000, Gedo 109000 and Bay 261,000) and require livelihood support. to avoid crisis. About 17000 urban IDPs are experiencing extreme food insecurity and are in catastrophe with acute malnutrition (≥15% GAM) or ≥10.7 percent of children have a MUAC below the 125 mm threshold. They require urgent support for immediate food access and health interventions. Some areas with IDPs include Gedo 34, 414, Bay, 80,444 and Awdal 14,308.

Provision of veterinary kits to 20 CAHWs in Gedo @ 2000 USD. 8. Restocking of 1500 shoats in Awdal @ 60USD for 150 HH (each HH receiving 10 shoats).

How is the project linked to durable solutions/resilience?

Provision of animal health services to 1500 pastoralists hh and covering 30,000 animals (Woqooyi Galbeed; 500HHs, Gedo; 500HHs and Bay; 500HHs) will help improve health of livestock hence improved productivity of livestock which is the main source of livelihood for targeted pastoralists. Restocking will help restore livelihoods of targeted households who lost their livestock during cyclone in Awdal. Cash for work will provide labour opportunity and income for domestic use and possible investment in productive livelihood assets.

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

Community based beneficiary selection criteria as per the community based targeting guidelines (FSC-2018). Beneficiaries targeted for Food Access will be vulnerable households- with members who are chronically ill or have disabled members, Elderly-headed households or child-headed households or female-headed households, IDPs, returnees and host communities and destitute pastoralists’ communities. Restocking shoats will primarily target pastoralists who lost their livestock due to cyclone/flooding in Awdal region in Somaliland. Priority will be given to female-headed households, child-headed households, family with at least a disabled member in the household, chronically ill members or heads of the household. These HHs should be willing to accept shoats herd of 10 and with capacity to keep and are able to take care of the shoats for production. Animal Health Services will target HHs under stressed (IPC 2) situation in Woqooyi Galbeed, Gedo and Bay. Priority will be given to female-headed households, child-headed households, family with at least a disabled member in the household, chronically ill members or heads of the household. Fisheries support will target vulnerable fisher HHs in Gedo region. Conditional Cash Transfer; Cash for Work will target households that have lost their crop or livestock or lost their home but have strong members capable of doing manual work for income; households with no family support/access to remittances, households with members who are chronically ill or have disabled members, elderly-headed households or child-headed households or female-headed households. Households with more than 8 members and only one member with income capacity, IDPs, returnees and host communities, destitute pastoralists communities and those willing to work as per the pre-determined work norms.

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

Elderly-headed households, child-headed households, female-headed households and destitute pastoralists’ communities will be targeted for Food Aid in Gedo and Bay. Restocking activity will mainly target female-headed households, child-headed households in Awdal region in Somaliland while Animal Health Service activities will target female-headed households, child-headed households, family with at least a disabled member in the household, chronically ill members or heads of the household. Conditional Cash Transfer; Cash for Work will target Elderly-headed households or child-headed households or female-headed households among others.

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

Beneficiary selection; Community based selection criteria based targeting guidelines (FSC-2018) to embrace dialogue. DO NO HARM- approach and conflict sensitive lens will be used across all activities will benefit both sides of divided communities and can incentivize peace and trust among stakeholders. It will also work closely with local administration. Humanitarian: Each Activity is risk informed and activities are coordinated to maximize good will and local support to manage risks. Accountability: Mechanism of participation for beneficiaries and stakeholders, information sharing, receiving and responding complaints helps to improve the quality of the project implementation and strengthen the relationship and acceptance of the organization by the community. Furthermore, these mechanisms minimize the risk of fraud and reduce the risk of harm to beneficiaries. Accountability will be integrated throughout project cycle management in accordance with the Humanitarian Accountability Partnership Standard (HAPS). Accordingly, information about the project such as provisions, beneficiary’s right to raise any complaints and the method will be made available to beneficiaries during the start-up of the program and will be continue to be communicated throughout the project period. Complaint and Feedback Response Mechanism (CFRM): As part of VSF Germany’s complaint and feedback handling mechanisms, the system will be established in areas in which information sharing, complaints and response mechanisms and participation of project beneficiaries can be ensured at all times. In this regard, context specific and community friendly accountability systems will be installed and the project will have a detailed accountability plan. Key project staff along with stakeholders will be oriented on the implementation of accountability systems and response mechanisms.
