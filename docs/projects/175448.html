Project Summary

The primary category of vulnerable people groups most in need of education responses in Burundi are children whose education is interrupted due to migration, poor health conditions, disabilities, lack of resources, early or unwanted pregnancies, gender based violence, broken families, natural disasters or conflict. The second category of vulnerable people groups are the parents and teachers of these children who are without facilities/infrastructures, equipments or resources to provide quality education or cover health and nutritional needs for their household members.
The Burundi Bwejo project will support the rehabilitation of 36 school classrooms and 9 WASH facilities through 18 quick impact projects in Bujumbura, Cibitoke, Rumonge, Kirundo, Makamba and Rutana. The rehabilitated classrooms will be equipped to facilitate children's and adolescents' (between the ages of 3-14 years) access to education. Approximately 18,000 students will be supported with back to school kits. 18 parent teacher associations will be created and supported to provide psychosocial support and coaching for students and their parents. Furthermore, they will provide support for school drop outs and students to conduct awareness campaigns on the importance of education, conflict prevention and management techniques through sports and creative arts events.
