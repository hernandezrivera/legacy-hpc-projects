Project Summary

The project will be directly implemented by German NGO Welthungerhilfe (WHH) in A'zaz and Afrin and implemented by the implementing partner in Idleb. The alarming and worsening food insecurity situation is concerning, especially in the backdrop of significantly low wheat production and other agricultural good productions in 2021. FAO preliminary projections indicate that wheat yield dropped to 50 per cent of last year’s output and is only a quarter of pre-crises levels . An additional 1.9 million people are at risk of food insecurity. The main drivers of food insecurity in Syria are related to the deteriorating economic environment, loss of livelihoods, and limited physical and financial access to food. In order to increase agricultural production, this project is suggested to be implemented in NWS. This project will be conducted in those activity locations and shall be complementing the ongoing projects by filling the livelihood needs of camps. Through partner implementation by Shafak in Harim (Idleb), 1000 female and male-headed farmer HHs who own agricultural land will receive agricultural inputs and technical support in order to produce vegetables for in-house consumption. Through self-implementation by WHH, 1000 female and male-headed farmer HHs (500 in A’zaz and 500 in Afrin) will be provided with lease support for agricultural arable lands in addition to agricultural inputs and technical support. These lands will be rented in the neighborhood of camps by WHH. The assistance for agricultural inputs will be provided as an Agriculture Voucher modality worth $120. The assistance based on the support for agriculture inputs (seeds-beans, peas etc., fertilizer, insecticides, fungicides, herbicides etc.) and tools (shovel, hoe, rake, gumboots, one wheel trolley, drip irrigation systems etc.) enough to cultivate 1.2 donum of land. On average 100 USD will be sufficient to cultivate 1 donum of land and according to our previous experiences we supported 1.2 donum of land per farmer. Therefore the assistance is determined as 120 USD. The distribution will be done in three tranches (each worth $40). The beneficiaries will be asked to fulfill their tasks in order to receive their next tranches. The progress in between the tranches will be monitored by a committee of field staff and trainers.
Technical team will conduct quality inspection for the agriculture inputs items prior to contracting and during the redemption to make sure that the items meets the required specifications according to quality, validity.

4 - Needs

The following assessments were considered:Dec 2021-WHH MSNA(RNA);Dec2021-WHH Gender Analysis;Dec 2021-WHH-Rapid Market Assessment; Dec2021-HH Economic Analysis,findings from HNAP &ReachMarket Monitoring Report-Nov2021.In Syria,humanitarian situation keeps deteriorating with prolonged hostilities.The whole region is currently rocked by massive wave of C-19 with higher infection rates(Health Cluster-19/11/2021).The high inflation rates, currency devaluation of TRY (for goods imported from Turkey), the increase in the prices of the agricultural inputs due to devaluation of SYP resulted in shortages of food items & had adverse effects on the purchasing power of the targeted HHs.According to Needs Assessment (11/2021) by WHH, 90% of surveyed HHs in Afrin report either no income or an income below $50, which is in alignment with WFP Data (mVAM, Dec2021), illustrating the 3rd highest food-insecurity level recorded since 2013.The number of food insecure people in NWS is estimated to be around 3.1 m.(OCHA, Nov 2021).The assessment found 45% of HHs in the surveyed locations were female headed, while 3% were child headed which will be addressed by this activity.Lack of food availability &livelihoods has further forced many HHs to adopt coping strategies such as reducing their food intake,forcing children out of school,etc.(WHH MSNA 04/2021).WHH will deliver agriculture input support to sustain their self-sufficient agriculture production.

5 - Implementation of activities

An overall of 2000 male and female-headed farmer Households (HHs) will receive agricultural assistance in order to produce vegetables for in-house consumption via WHH and its partner. 1000 HHs in Idleb will be assisted by Shafak, the Implementing Partner and WHH will be closely monitoring the activities on the field level. The HHs will be provided with agriculture vouchers worth $120 for buying agricultural inputs and with technical training. 500 HHs in A’zaz and 500 HHs in Afrin (the allocation may change based on the needs) will be assisted through self-implementation by WHH. In addition to agriculture vouchers and technical training, the HHs in A’zaz and Afrin will be provided with lease support for agricultural arable lands (for those who do not own agricultural lands), which will be rented in the neighborhood of camps by WHH. In order to track the progress, the voucher assistance will be provided in three tranches and monitored by a committee of field staff and trainers. As a phase out strategy, WHH will further assist the targeted people in the food e-voucher activity

10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (SEA) and Sexual Harassment (SH) are unacceptable breaches offundamental human rights and a deep betrayal of WHH’s core values. Thesexual exploitation and abuseof those who depend on WHH for assistance runs counter to all our personal and organisational values.It is unconscionable, it is intolerable, and it is often criminal. Equally intolerable is the sexual harassmentof our fellow aid workers. Working closely with partners and communities, WHH has significantly stepped up its fi ght against SEA and SH. This work is informed by, andtaking place in tandem with, the work of related actors including the broader UNsystem, donors, and civil society organizations.Strategy covers both SEA and SH,bringing them together in a way that is mutually reinforcing and that creates opportunities to leverage practices between the two. The Strategy also acknowledges the complexity and limitation to WHH’s authority in relation to SEA perpetrated byimplementing partner personnel, including Government personnel. However, it doesinclude measures to address and prevent these violations including supporting partners to fully integrate PSEA into programmes in both development and humanitarian contexts, creating a joined-up accountability culture that abhors,sanctions and prevents SEA and protects victims.


11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumption1:People shall be using agricultural vouchers for vegetables production Risk 1:Vouchers may be exchanged for cash at a lower value Mitigation factor 1:Agricultural support shall be divided into 3 tranches. Those not complying with the planned activities shall not be provided with next tranche. Field team&the trained farmers shall be closely monitoring the activities. A2:People can reach arable land with no difficulty. R2:Rented lands could be very far from camps. MF 2:If the rented land is far from camps, transportation support shall be provided. A3:People can exchange agricultural inputs from the contracted vendors at an agreed prices. R3:Vendors may try to charge more than the agreed list of prices. MF3:Different monitoring mechanisms shall be pursued like ghost buyers.Field team shall be closely monitoring the prices&the redemption process. A4:The goods shall be received by the targeted groups as planned. R4: Delays in delivering the goods from the contracted vendor. MF4:All agreements with the vendors will be implemented in frame of WHH policy and procedures. WHH will take every measure in order to avoid any delays in receiving the items. A5:WHH staff work honestly during agriculture voucher distribution/redemption activity. R5:Staff is corrupted MF5: -We will be open with the community about the project.-Use clearly verifiable beneficiary targeting criteria and check beneficiary list.-Work closely with the camp&sector managers.-Segregation of duties
