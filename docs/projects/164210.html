Project Summary

The project supports IDPs and host communities in Idleb and Rural Aleppo with WASH services both emergency services for IDPs in camps and out of camps. People in camps will receive drinking water through water trucking, water storage tanks, constructing septic tanks, latrine blocks, desludging, garbage bins and garbage collection in addition to conducting hygiene promotion sessions. Hygiene kits and diapers for the elderly and PWD will be distributed three times.
In communities out of camp, both IDPs and host communities will benefit from rehabilitation and operation of water pumping stations, and the rehabilitation of sewage network.

4 - Needs

After nine years of conflict, the WASH infrastructure and service provision in many parts of Syria require significant investments, repair and operational support. WASH systems have suffered from damages owing to years of functioning at high capacity, limited or no maintenance, destructions, displacement of technical staff and poor water resources, HNO 2020.December 2019 witnessed increased attacks and hostilities on the south of Idleb which resulted in the displacement of thousands of people to Idleb and Northwards. These attacks resulted in new wave of displacements towards the north of Idleb and Aleppo. According to CCCM report on 29th of December (HNAP), 283,898 people (56.037 men, 61,156 women, 80, 120 boys, 86,585 girls) were displaced between the period of 1st and 29th of December 2019; 56, 000 were displaced into camps and 13,000 arrived to collective centers. 205,366 were displaced into Idleb governorate. These people have needs of life saving services as they had to flee their homes with only some back bags that include some clothes and essential items. According to the Humanitarian update issued by OCHA on 23rd of December, the latest escalation in violence "exacerbates an already dire humanitarian situation for people, particularly in Idleb Governorate, where in recent weeks, tens of thousands of people have been newly displaced in the hostilities. These newly displaced people add to the over 400,000 that were displaced ... many of them multiple times" HIHFAD also conducted a rapid needs assessment to identify the WASH needs of newly IDPs who reached camps in December 2019. Newly emerging shelling forced thousands of people to flee from the areas of conflict to northern Idleb camps. In the assessed camps, it was found that the number of new arrived households is 1488 HHs in the camps of Mareet Tamsrin subdistrict. Result of analysis showed that 96% of interviewed HHs in the assessed camps get water only from paid water trucking.

5 - Implementation of activities

The intervention will be based on the technical needs assessments by HIHFAD, the targeted camps will receive the following services (water trucking, water storage tanks, Latrine blocks and septic tanks desludging of septic tanks, garbage bins, hygiene promotion and hygiene kits. A sewage line will be installed in Al Faraj and Swaed Al Ikhaa camps. Moreover, garbage bins will be distributed in all targeted camps, and they will be discharged three times per week (for every 10 HHs 1 garbage bin of half a barrel). HIHFAD field team will be responsible of implementing the activities of the projects. Latrines will be purchased from Turkey according to the design which includes three latrines+1 for people with disabilities and are gender segregated. Septic tanks will be constructed through a contractor in Syria according to the specifications prepared by HIHFAD team. On average, these septic tanks will be desludged twice a month in order to avoid the emission of bad odors or sewage over flowing in the roads of the camp. Communal garbage bins will be emptied three times per week in order not to have any garbage accumulation, the landfill will be the formal ones in Kelly and Sarjabala. HIHFAD will conduct hygiene promotion sessions to IDPs in the targeted camps and communities to raise awareness of water borne diseases and personal hygiene. Regarding the pumping stations, HIHFAD is planning to support three water stations in Idleb city with operation costs including fuel, oil, maintenance costs, and incentives for operators. Operators from the local water unit will receive on spot training by HIHFAD engineer and will receive incentives on a monthly basis. These stations will operate for 10 hours a day with capacity of 210 m3 per hour, 90,000 people will benefit from this activity for six months. After preparing the books of terms for sewage network in Kelly and Hazano a contractor will implement the work by supervision of field engineers.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

- Children below 18 years old conduct works on behalf of contractors:HIHFAD inform all contractors and included a paragraph in the contract to forbid any supplier from contracting children in daily labour.- Movements of garbage trucks and water trucks in congested camp:HIHFAD discuss with beneficiaries and camps managers the best routes leading to the water storage tanks, latrines and garbage bins in order to avoid any potential risk.- Health risk associated with garbage removal: HIHFAD ensures that contractors involved in SWM activities wear appropriate Personal Protective Equipment like gloves and face mask during work.- Inequality in distribution; Physical and psychological harm arising from violence and coercion; Timing and process of distribution put women and girls at higher risk of GBV.Mitigation: HIHFAD will promote the establishment of adequate management plan; engagement with communities in distribution planning and feedback from different groups on package for improvement; training of staff and enforcement of code of PSEA code of conduct among partner staff.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

There are several risks that threaten the safety and security, these are the major Risks:1 – IEDs and remnants of war, to be considered one of the biggest and most dangerous threats at all since the region witnessed long-term fighting and clashes which were the main cause of spreading these materials among the villages and the towns.2 - Disputes between the factions of the Syrian Free Army from time to time, these problems often end quickly and on the same day without escalation of the military position.3 - Retaliatory military operations and assassinations by the sleeper cells belonging to the Kurdish militias located in the remote areas.HIHFAD maintains detailed security protocol for its field staff and patients. The security team inside Syria are linked with other NGO teams and community stakeholders for information sharing. Security information is collected as needed throughout the day and staff movement determined after analysis of available information. Teams leaving Turkey to go inside Syria are registered with appropriate authorities in Turkey to ensure administrative protocols are met. Inside Syria, radio contact is maintained among the teams and health facilities to ensure information flow, especially in the case of emergencies. Radio contact will be maintained between the mobile unit and HIHFAD base radio communications in Bab Alhawa. In the event of an emergency, a response can be deployed from Bab Alhawa.HIHFAD has Safety and Security Plan which includes procedures to be followed in case of any emergencies in the field. Also, the organization has a prompt incident reporting system, as well as staff evacuation plan. The organization is able to alert the staff or beneficiaries at risk using the ‘phone tree’ and other communication channels during emergencies,
