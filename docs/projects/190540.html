Project Summary

According to the Somali region DRMB update as of January 2022 and sourcing data from DTM round 27(August-September, 2021) Currently there are 142,671 households comprising 871,176 individual IDPs in 427 sites across the region. Conflict was the main reason for displacement and displaced an estimated 506,089 IDPs {58.1%), a decrease of 3310,111IDPs (40%) since Oct, 2018(after the regional govt change) while drought displaced 266,013 IDPs (30.5%), seasonal floods displaced 65,541 IDPs (7.5%),flash floods displaced 33,196 IDPs {3.8%) and landslides displaced 337 IDPs (0.04%). The border conflict between Somali and Afar caused the displacement of 4923 HHs.These new IDPs are currently in 12 sites in Afdem, Erer, Biki, and Maizo woredas of the Siti zone of the Somali region. they were displaced from Garbaciise, Adeydu, and surrounding rural areas.
ANE aiming at saving lives of conflicted affected people and proposing this specific project. Hence, the objectives of the current project are to ensure access to life-saving shelter & NFIs for 49,664 beneficiaries at Metekel zone for conflict affected IDPs and 5,314 individuals at Somali. ANE also plan to distribute shelter repair kit for 30,979 at Oromia region. During beneficiary targeting the most vulnerable community group including child-headed household, the separated child in need of shelter, Children at risk, female-headed household, elders, and people with underlying chronic illness, a large family with more dependence with no alternative livelihoods will get the priority. The project will follow up COVID-19 specific beneficiary’s targeting, registration, and distribution operation procedure. Post-distribution monitoring assessment will also be conducted for ESNFI using shelter cluster standard tools. However, to minimize the duplication of effort at the woreda level, ANE will coordinate with different stakeholders who are working at the project implementation woredas. Besides, to minimize the risks of protection and GBV for the beneficiaries during targeting, registration, and assistance delivery, ANE will provide PSEA and GBV training to the government’s staff and targeting committees. To undertake the above-mentioned activities, a total of 1,926,024 USD is proposed.
The project has been designed in line with the National Shelter/NFI cluster response plan document and the proposed activities are part of the Shelter/NFI cluster priority. Major activities will include:
- Conduct market assessment and selection and verification of beneficiaries
- Emergency shelter and non-food items ESNFI distribution for IDPs( In kind or cash)
- Covid-19 protection materials purchase for vulnerable and staff at field level
- Conduct post-distribution monitoring
