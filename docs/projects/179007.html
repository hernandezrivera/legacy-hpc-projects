Project Summary

The project aims to distribute winterization heating means, stoves and fuel for 2000 families in NWS which have registered highest number of severity scale, the targeted population are the newly IDPs who are living the camps and in inappropriate shelter (unfinished/bad conditions), in addition to most vulnerable locals (15% of beneficiaries) and the targeted families are who hosting displaced persons (vulnerable groups who should have special concern as described in HNO 2021), the selected winterization items are the life-saving heating means in its minimum, in the areas where (-4) is the lowest registered degree with some coverage of snow in different locations and (4) the average degree in winter, the winterization heating items will be distributed in a timely manner as:
1- Heating stoves: (diesel, coal, firewood, and Perrin) stoves will be distributed with their accessories according to the cluster guidelines and recommendation and by the mid of November 2021 GAN could finalize the distribution of stoves.
2- Heating items: GAN will respond to life-saving winterization needs for the beneficiary: Based on the PDM results and last project and lesson learned, one type of heating fuel did not achieve the expected benefit, families that live in tents prefer to use firewood and/or Perrin for heating others prefer diesel, so GAN will provide heating items according to the preferences of the beneficiaries.
In term of fuel suppliers: according to the assessment result and our ongoing project there are limited fuel suppliers that could support the project, and GAN should be strict in term of fuel quality and beneficiaries dignity (ensure that the distribution points not far away from their homes), so GAN will contract with fuel supplier(s) to supply the needed quantities of high-quality diesel to avoid any harm to beneficiaries health and save their dignity, as a result, the e-voucher modality will be used in distribution points
GAN had a project covered winter 2020-2021 so 16.6% of this project is secured.

4 - Needs

Almost a decade into the crisis, people in northwest Syria are living through some of the worst crisis since the war in Syria began, the humanitarian situation continues to deteriorate at an alarming rate, which has a devastating effect on the 4M people in northwest Syria.The economic situation is increasingly desperate as the Syrian Pound (SYP) continues to sharply lose value against the USD.The MSNA data also shows that female-headed households, youth, elderly and people with functional difficulties have reduced access to basic services including shelter, as well as reduced livelihood opportunities and income that supports access to NFIs, more than three-quarters of those paying rent are unable to afford it.Families report that they are in need of fuel, clothing, and power/heating sources.All of these circumstances drive families to adopt many negative coping methods to meet their daily needs, such as the sale of assets, child labor, and other forms of exploitation.

5 - Implementation of activities

1.1.1 - Beneficiaries identification and verification:GAN will register and verify the beneﬁciaries according to the following criteria: For IDPs: - People living in the camps and in an inappropriate shelter (unﬁnished/bad conditions) that most affected by the weather. For HC: The main criteria: - Families hosting displaced persons and most vulnerable1.1.2 - Contract with e-voucher service provider, then install the project on the portal with all data. -Provide guidance sessions to beneﬁciaries include the following sections:(1- Explain the project and details of the top-ups and redeeming plan.2- Explain the e-voucher system and how it works. 3- Distribute the cards accompanied with brochures that include the instruction manual)1.1.3 - Contract with heating means suppliers (firewood, coal, diesel, and Perrin) to cover all beneﬁciaries (2000 families) during the project period as a contract with stoves supplier with speciﬁc criteria1.1.4 - Distribute 2000 stoves with their accessories through e-vouchers in the distribution point, guidance brochures on best practice of installing and using stoves will be added1.1.5 - Redeem the voucher amounts with the preferred heating item by beneficiaries, the voucher cards will be topped up three times, each one with the value of (50 $)1.1.6 - Post-distribution monitoring to monitor the use and impact of the provided assistance:Three PDM phases will be conducted after each round of redeeming:GAN team will conduct a PDM and a price monitoring to gauge beneficiary feedback in relation to timeliness of the provision of assistance, quality and quantity of assistance provided, the distribution process, behavior of the distribution staff, perceptions on the targeting process.

7.1 - Please list any risks related to sexual exploitation and abuse that have been identified during the project design phase and any risk mitigation and prevention measures that have been adopted and built into the project

GAN is a member of PSEA network, attending a monthly meeting and following the guidelines, so to mainstream the protection from sexual exploitation and abuse in the project, GAN have developed a policy stating standards of conduct, including acts of SEA, and a work plan to implement the policy in place, GAN will ensure all staff members are signed and adhered to a code of conduct policy, PSEA and will support the members to access relevant pieces of training (PSEA, GBV), ensuring that non-humanitarian stakeholders who will be in touch with beneficiaries such as (suppliers) will be informed and adhere to the humanitarian principles and code of conduct, PSEA posters will be available at distribution points. GAN recognizes the rights of all stakeholders, particularly the vulnerable, to access humanitarian programs without the risk of sexual exploitation or abuse. GAN intends to prevent abuse by implementing procedures to protect beneficiaries through best recruitment practices, staff induction, creating an open and aware culture and assigning clear management responsibilities, ensuring that victims are supported, and perpetrators held to account. Information on the referral pathway will be circulated through posters and banners at the distribution points. The distribution points will be inside the camps itself, during daylight hours, and safeguard that there is appropriate segregation for women to create a culturally sensitive and safe operational environment

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumption: - fuel is affordable in markets in the NW of Syria. - the availability of e-voucher service provider countries- COVID context is continuedRisk: 1: increase the price of the fuel by the mid of winter: Mitigation1: GAN will contract with suppliers to provide the needed quantity of fuel items at the beginning of winter so that changing fuel prices will not affect our project, GAN will add a penalty clause for those who will not be committed to the provided price and quality 2: COVID-19 spread in target communities: Mitigation: GAN team takes all necessary measures to avoid the transmission and spread of the disease infection, such as wearing a face mask, gloves, providing hand sanitizers, maintaining social space spacing, and measuring the temperature of the beneficiaries before entering the distribution point, GAN will use the latest version of recommendations in regards of distributing NFI under the cotext of COVID-19 3: Lockdown and curfew in the targeted communities due to the COVID-19: Mitigation: In case that a curfew is imposed, there will be permitted from the local authorities for humanitarian workers to work and movement, and GAN will change the cash assistance with in-kind assistance and will deliver assistance to the beneficiaries' homes (Door to door) 4: Due to the unpredictable situation in NW Syria, there is a risk that markets are not functioning properly due to lack of access, or other unexpected issues. Likelihood: low, Impact: high. Mitigation strategy: GAN confirmed the markets are functioning and the security situation allows the implementation of the project. The situation of the market will be monitored on a regular basis by the program team
