Project Summary

The project shall address a number of issues emerging related to sexual and gender based violence prevention, peace and security. Issues including economic empowerment and livelihoods, sexual and reproductive health rights (SRHTR)
The project will explain methods of how to help the gender based survivors access justice how to improve access to justice where evidence based advocacy is geared towards addressing the gendered crimes and violence against women and girls. The project will mainly assist women to redress for social and political impact of gender based violence. The project will drive women to organize themselves in the Women and girls friendly spaces to pursue economic empowerment program where they will start savings and credit schemes or village savings Associations to allow women to access financial resources and other types of assistance such as education , emergency support and paying school fees for their children Production , distribution and delivery of reusable menstrual hygiene management pads (MHM) to adolescent girls in Schools in the three counties shall be done . Vulnerability of girl child to sexual abuse and teenage pregnancy are factors that also make it difficult for girls participation in education. Early child marriage impact on proportion of male-female enrolment in School .Sexual exploitation and abuse women face are some of the issues the project will seek durable solution for their reduction in the target communities by organizing dialogues on early child marriage talking about the consequences of early and child marriages , identify impact of child marriage to the community , family and individual in Fangak Ayod and Canal .Community feedback and compliant mechanisms shall be set up in all the locations for beneficiaries to report their cases and feelings regarding the project.
The project is designed based on the HRP objectives.

1 - Needs

South Sudan remains one of 10 countries with the lowest life expectancy in the world at 58 years . The under-five mortality rate remains high with 96.2 per 1,000 live births. Such rates are driven by many factors including limited or lack of access to adequate health services . Half of South Sudanese girls are married or promised in marriage before the age of 18. Women and girls hold primary responsibilities for farming, collecting water and firewood, cooking, cleaning, and childcare. Men and boys are traditionally the decision-makers within communities and their families. Conflict has driven changes in some of the traditional gender roles that people play. For example, where men are no longer present, women may be required to support and care for the family and their assets. Women tend to experience greater food insecurity given their cultural and social roles as caregivers of children and older persons, which leads to others being prioritized and served food first. Female-headed households are more commonly found in rural areas. Continued conflict and instability in the country have resulted in large-scale internal and cross-border displacement. Improved security in some locations has prompted some people to spontaneously return to their places of origin, with others moving from one vulnerable location to another. Climate change contributes to a decline in the amount and viability of land for cultivation in Fangak and Ayod . Traditional seasonal migration routes that pastoralists use having their livestock affected, as access to water changes and efforts to seek grazing and water contributes to conflict. As such, women and girls tend to take responsibilities for such activities which place them at the risk of sexual and gender-based violence while they carry out the tasks. Women and girls have a higher risk of domestic violence, in Fangak Ayod and, Pigi / Canal which is viewed as acceptable. The COVID-19 pandemic and climatic conditions exacerbate poverty and general living standards for people in many parts of the Country including Ayod , Fangak and Pigi/Canal Counties. People with disabilities face greater challenges to access services, with greater challenges experienced among women and especially children with disabilities (75 per cent perceive ) having less access to services, food, and education) and among the older persons.The people in Fangak, Ayod and Pigi /Canal continued living in the dire situation due to effects of flooding where most of the people / families were forced to share shelters and accommodation with limited privacy between families, children and parents. Many cases of GBV emerged rampant reported due to overcrowding. Women and girls experienced many cases of GBV when they went out to collect fire wood in Fangak, Ayod and Pigi/Canal Counties. Issues of early marriage increased since parents wanted the dowry paid for their daughter to facilitate them buy food and other essential items for their needs during the lean seasonsandCOVID19 pandemic. Fangak Ayod and Pig /canal counties largely lack efficient and independent judicial institutions committed to upholding the rule of law and safeguarding fundamental human rights. The weak rule of law institutions and economic deterioration have led to increased criminality and targeting of civilians and humanitarian workers. Justice is not observed when dealing with issues affecting women and girls in Fangak, Ayod and Pigi/Canal Customary courts—covering issues like marriage, divorce, childcare and property rights make up to 90 per cent of cases to be heard in these locations. However, the chiefs who preside over these courts are predominantly older men, who tend to have deeply ingrained patriarchal views that are reflected in their decisions in favor of male applicants or perpetrators. The Elimination of the Discrimination Against Women,(CEDAW ) is not strongly observed by the people especially the armed forces on addressing Conflict.

5.1.1. What is the justification for using cash or vouchers instead of in kind?

The project has an element of cash for work where the community members shall participate in contributing their labor effort and in turn they shall receive cash that will enable them buys food and other essential items to improve their lifestyle. Construction of Women and girls friendly space in the three counties shall involve cash expenditure where they shall render their support and in turn receive motivation in cash . The modality of payment shall be determined later since it depends on immediate needs of the community members in the given location. The project will drive women to organize themselves in the Women and girls friendly spaces to pursue economic empowerment program where they will start savings and credit schemes or village savings Associations to allow women to access financial resources and other types of assistance such as education , emergency support and paying school fees for their children , distribution and delivery of reusable menstrual hygiene management pads (MHM) to adolescent girls in Schools in the three counties shall be done by women groups supported . The project will explain methods of how to help the gender based survivors access justice how to improve access to justice where evidence based advocacy is geared towards addressing the gendered crimes and violence against women and girls to get cash for getting litigation services . The project will mainly assist women who are survivors of gender based violence to redress the social and political impact of gender based violence. The program of cash assistance in essence, is something leveraging on investment and demonstrating value for money .This is because the program will aim at advocating for increased investment women and adolescent girls by proving livelihoods to prevent GBV of child marriage and early child bearing . The project shall ensure accountability and value for money for WAO and donor resources that have been allocated on cash programming in the project.

5.1.2. Explain how the project design considered market functionality

The design of this project has factored in the need for enabling the functionality of the markets in the selected locations . Activities which may require materials for construction like WGFs and other food related items can be obtained from the local markets found in Ayod, Fangak and Pig/Canal Counties may require liquid cash to facilitate the trade acting as a medium of exchange . This project leverages the market functionality by injecting in cash in the locations to boost the economy in those counties and at the same time it takes care of inflation and other opposing factors which are difficult to overcome .Just to mention a few, Storage, transportation and designing relevant prices in different locations are challenges that are un avoidable in Fangak, Ayod and Pig /Canal Counties . The project considers empowering women economically by engaging them in lucrative activities which immediately believes in yielding income immediately since they have high demand value in the communities the project will targets .Therefore, the economic empowerment activities planned to be provided to the women and girls really need cash to be injected in the local markets as long as the population is buffed ,to make those markets lively as they focus on the needs which require cash as a medium of exchange to facilitate the enterprising trade activities in the project locations . Sharing of marketing information and exchange of goods and services will absolutely make the local markets sound lively as their functionality is improved and supported by this project.
