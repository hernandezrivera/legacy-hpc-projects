Project Summary

The project intends to do cash transfers for households to enable purchase fresh food such as vegetables and fruits including condiments. Based on experience gained with the continuous engagement with the community in participatory meetings and project implementation, a lot of complaints and requests were raised by project participants on the need to enhance them have access to fresh food, fruits and food condiments which is are provided under the ongoing WFP food assistance programs.
The project will also target project participants who will participate in some agricultural interventions to enable vulnerable households (IDPs, returnees, host community) build resilience and re-establishments of their livelihoods through:
• Micro gardening: CARE will target project participants with small land size within their households including backyard. More than 60% of the beneficiaries targeted are women headed households
• Poultry, sheep and goats: CARE will target the elderly, child headed and female headed households with these interventions.
• Briquette and energy efficient stove production: CARE will target women who have the responsibility of sourcing firewood for food preparation. Due to the ongoing insecurity, women have experienced challenges sourcing fire wood.
• Irrigation farming: CARE will identify 1000 HHs and empower them with farm inputs (Improved seed verities, pesticides, fertilizer etc) and basic farming tools. This will also act as an exit strategy for the cash transfers to households to purchase fresh foods

1 - Needs

During the community based participatory planning (CBPP) conducted by CARE in October 2019, in Dikwa and Yusufari, it was identified that there is scarcity of firewood/fuel energy in the communities. Other rural rapid appraisals that were also conducted in Gwoza show the need and scarcity of fuel wood. HHs indicated that they walk far distances to collect firewood and in the process they are exposed to a lot of risks of being attacked, killed, abducted or injured. It was identified that due to limited knowledge, substantial amount of waste materials is generated but disposed in the community and this is a potential for briquette production. Dikwa, Gwoza and Yusufari have a history of livestock production/management as it is seen as a pride in their tradition to own livestock. At the onset of the insurgency, people lost their livestock as some were killed, others carted away with while others fled the communities leaving behind all their means of livelihoods Through feedback channels, community meetings, FGDs conducted on regular basis by CARE, beneficiaries lamented lack of fresh food, fruits (Vegetables) and condiments which can be integrated in their food basket or provided to compliment other foods.

2 - Activities

Sheep and goats: CARE will identify 7,000 HHs in; Dikwa- 1,500, Gwoza- 3,500 & Yusufari – 2,000 to receive livestock and basic veterinary services and feeds to support their growth for the first three months as part of the package (3 females & 1 male). CARE will work closely with the government ministries, community leadership & beneficiaries to identify suitable livestock species for each community. CARE will engage the ministry of Animal & Fisheries Development to provide specialised trainings on general livestock management. Periodic visits will be conducted to each HH and subsequent refresher trainings will be conducted at intervals. Poultry Production: This will focus mainly on providing to be reared by women. 1,100 HHs (Dikwa - 350; Gwoza – 400 & Yusufari – 350. Each HH will be supported with 10 chickens, veterinary standard drugs and 10 bags of 25kg feeds to support start-up. Beneficiaries will be trained on best practices in poultry production and management. Beneficiaries will be supported with poultry shelter. Energy Briquette & Stove Production: 600 HHs (Dikwa – 200, Gwoza – 200 & Yusufari – 200) will be identified and grouped. start-up kits such as drums and compressing machines will be provided for each group. Beneficiaries will be provided with raw materials for energy stove production. Training's on briquette and stove production will be conducted by CARE staff to the beneficiaries across all locations. Beneficiaries will be grouped into 60 groups which will comprise 10 members per group (female – 5 & male – 5 per group). CARE staff will train the groups on general business management principles and provide them with the knowledge and skills on how to conduct and manage a business effectively putting into consideration the financial management skills and other soft skills. Cash for fresh food and condiments: 12,000 HHs (4,000 HHs), Gwoza (4,000 HHs) and Yusufari (4,000 HHs) targeted to ensure that all have access to fresh foods, fruits and condiments for improved nutrition. 6 rounds of transfers to all the beneficiaries will be conducted at 4,000 NGN/month. To ensure effective utilisation of the funds that will be provided to each HH. Irrigation: 1000 HHs (300 in Dikwa, 550 in Yusufari and 150) will be targeted. Boreholes will be drilled as source of water. Beneficiaries will be supported with inputs and tools. 8 boreholes will be drilled across the 3 locations. Farmers will be clustered in groups to ensure every group benefits from the water supply, fuel will also be provided for 3 months to support smooth running of the farms. 65% of the targeted individuals will be women, 20% Youths and 15% men Wet season farming: CARE will target 700 HHs in the host communities of Dikwa – 200 HHs, Gwoza – 100 HHs and Yusufari – 400 HHs with access to land for wet season farming. Most of the vulnerable households encounter difficulties in accessing improved seed varieties & lack the basic agronomy practices. Beneficiaries will be trained on crop production/ protection and post-harvest management. About 50% of the beneficiaries will be women. Types of seeds include improved varieties of maize, cow-pea, sorghum, groundnut & millet with varieties based on preference and suitability. Training's will be provided on best practices including: land preparation, weeding, planting, fertilizer application, integrated pest management, harvesting and post-harvest management. Micro gardening: 1600 HH (Dikwa - 500 HH, Yusufari - 600 HH and Gwoza - 500 HH) with access to small portion of land at their backyard will be targeted to grow vegetables for improved nutrition and food security. The beneficiaries will be supported with inputs and tools. A minimum of 75% of the targeted individuals will be women, 15% youths and 10% Men. Beneficiaries will receive a combination of improved vegetable seeds and training.
