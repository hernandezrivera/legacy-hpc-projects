Project Summary

Improve the nutrition status of children 0-59months, Pregnant and Lactating Women through the implementation of integrated nutrition activities that include supplementary feeding, appropriate Infant and Young Child Feeding practices, optimal maternal nutrition and systematic identification, referral, and treatment of acutely malnourished in Bursari LGA

2 - Activities

1660 MAM children and 320 Pregnant and Lactating Women (PLW) reached with Ready to Use Supplementary Food and 3000 children 6-23 months reached with MNP supplementation in Busari LGA of Yobe State. Conduct training for 105 community health workers and nutritionist, Community nutrition mobilizers, lead mothers/lead fathers and nutrition field assistants on GBV referral pathway CMAM/IYCF-E, Mother/family MUAC screening, COVID-19 prevention and accountability to affected people and MNP supplementation. This will go to strengthen the capacity of health workers in Government Based OTP and Stabilization Centers as many of such officials indicated the length of training to have been over a year. In total 60 CNMs, 10 Supervisor and 10 Government workers and 15 Lead Mother and 10 Lead Fathers will be trained Ongoing community active case findings in Bursari LGAs of Yobe States: ActionAid Nigeria will implement various community participatory approaches includes driving active case finding, Mother/Family MUAC screening and general screening in the communities in line with Nutrition Sector approved guidelines. Lead Mothers and CNMs will further drive down capacity on MUAC in the MTM and FTF support group sessions. Procurement and distribution of assorted grains (millet, sorghum, soya beans and groundnuts) for production of tom brown for the treatment of MAM children, Pregnant and Lactating Women. Children will be admitted into the programme and discharged after 8 weeks treatment On-going escort services for Referral of SAM cases to OTPs and SCs. ActionAid Nigeria will utilize an Ambulance to ensure the completion of referral. 2000 caregivers are reached through IYCF for the first time and 8000 community members reached through nutrition sensitization approaches. Support the continuous operationalization of 50 Mother to Mother Support Groups and 10 Father to Father support in Bursari of Yobe State. Each group will have a Lead Mother and or a Lead Father. The groups will be supervised and supported by the Community Nutrition Mobilizers who will be identified from the communities and trained by the project. Conduct Mother to Mother and Father to Father Support group meetings in Bursari LGA in Yobe State, conduct support group meeting to discuss various nutrition themes. This will be a platform for sharing of experience but also serves as an avenue for the beneficiaries to receive psychosocial support. Cooking demonstrations will be held on an ongoing basis. This will be done to show hygienic process for cooking locally available nutritious food and a way to improve KAP for them to use locally available resources to achieve maximum nutritional value. Production of SBCC and other materials to include counselling cards, cooking demonstration manuals integrated with COVID-19 sensitization messaging in line of the new wave of infections in the country, poster, fliers, banners, T-shirts, Hijab, Bags, Umbrella, rain boots, bottled water. Refresher training for Community Nutrition Mobilizers. ActionAid will work with UNICEF and the State Nutrition Officer to identify and train CNMS. The Yobe State Nutrition Office is working to harmonize the working of CNMs and maintain a statewide register of CNMs hence the need for collaboration and coordination. Conduct periodic area-based coordination meetings for community nutrition mobilizers in 5 clusters in Bursari LGA. This will be a platform for capacity building, experience sharing and learning. It will also be platform to address key concerns or identify challenges to be escalated to the Nutrition Sector.

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

Action Aid Nigeria pay special attention to mainstreaming protection of beneficiaries into all aspects of the project. AAN values are closely aligned with the core principles of human rights and equity, participation, do no harm, building on available resources and capacities, integrated support systems and multi‐layered support.AAN ensures that the delivery of nutrition intervention is participatory, safe, and in a socially appropriate way. During community engagements where people are expected to gather such as support Group meetings or community dialogues, AAN work with the lead mothers to ensure that water, shades and breastfeeding corners are provided. Identified threats are mitigated Vulnerable members of the beneficiaries such as the aged, Persons with Disability, parents with toddlers, pregnant women participating in such forums are given special attention and care.
