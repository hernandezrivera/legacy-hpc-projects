Project Summary

The proposed project will target conflict affected IDPs and the host communities in affected payams in Aweil East County (Malualbaai, Madhol, Mangok, Baac and Wunlang). The primary focus of the project will be to prevent famine and response to eradicate severe insecurity as per food security and livelihood cluster (FSLC) response plan (RP) and Strategy. It will further support households (HH) affected by 2019 flood. In line with CO1 Provide food assistance to prevent famine and improve food consumption, dietary diversity and coping strategies for vulnerable populations (in IPC 5, 4 & some 3), TARGET proposes immediate response to the humanitarian needs of the conflict affected IDPs, and the host communities in Aweil East through provision of seeds, farming tools, assorted agricultural tools and fishing tools to the affected population in Aweil East. TARGET will offer training to farmer groups on good agronomic practices, seed production and preservation techniques. The training will further cover post harvesting and store management process to increase productivity, availability and nutrition in Aweil East. To improve the local seed varieties, selected farmers will be offered seed farmer training. The extension workers will be trained by TARGET who will offer technical supports to farmer groups and fishing community. TARGET will supply and distribute fishing tools to fishing community. The fishing community will be supported through training-fish preservation processing using local materials/tools and the effective uses of fishing tools. TARGET will select most vulnerable HHs, pregnant/lactating women; female headed families, HHs with most vulnerable members such as children, disabled persons, HIV/AIDs victims, elderly and other vulnerable groups as the primary beneficiaries. In addition, TARGET proposing in-kind food distribution assistance to the affected HHs in Aweil East to reduce malnutrition among children and women and improve their food consumption. Family nutrition remains a challenge to wellbeing of family more special children and lactating mothers. TARGET will offer training to affect HHs to support knowledge of food production, processing and family nutrition. The project will further establish fish smoking and drying oven and rack to the fishing community in Aweil East.

1 - Needs

Aweil East is one of the county of Northern Bahr el Ghazal States. The county is located in eastern part of the state boarders Sudan in North East, South Darfur to the North, Abyei Administrative Region to the North East, Warrap Sate to the East, Aweil South County to the South, Aweil West County to the South West and Aweil North to the North West. The humanitarian situation in Aweil East Remains acute since the 2013 incident followed by 2016 conflict which displaced thousands of populations majorly women and children. The food production and supplies significantly reduced due to the insecurities and the economic distress. The South Sudan: Acute food insecurity and acute malnutrition situation May-July released 11th September 2019 indicates that, more that 6.35 million people (about 54%) of South Sudan Population severely food insecure in August despite large scale humanitarian assistance. Since Aweil is located at the border, the need for returnees from Sudan remains high. The prolonged war since 2013 has eroded livelihoods which continue to compromise majority of HHs capacity to produce and access enough food for the family. The lower harvest, deteriorating road networking reduces market supplies raising the prices higher. Though United Nation Food and Agricultural Organization (FAO) registered improving access to livelihoods, food and market; only 5% reduction in the proportion of food insecure population for the period of July-September 2019 and about 39% of population estimated to be food insecure across the South Sudan. The current heavy rain have affected estimated 52,000 people and swamped crops, vital productive assets, shelters and water sources in many parties of South Sudan including Aweil North and Aweil Center. Since Aweil center serves as the major market center, population in Aweil East become vulnerable due to cut-off. In addition, Aweil Easter humanitarian situation is classified under Phase 4 (acute food insecurity) with high population facing humanitarian food crisis by August 2019 IPC classification. The high food insecure population projected is driven by house hold food shortage due to delayed rainfall, macro-economic crisis, displacement and insecurity in the region. In contrary, the amount of rainfall this year (2019) is too high increasing the risk of incidences of human and diseases such as acute watery diarrhoea, malaria and other diseases that will result into possible lower productivity. South Sudan: severer flooding press release 25th October 2019 estimated that 908,000 people are affected by abnormal heavy seasonal flooding since July 2019. The victims includes IDPs, refugees and host communities, and that there is likely hood to continue for another six weeks meaning more people at risk. It is assumed that considerable damages to crops, livestock, and arable land will occur as well as paralyzing capacity of HHs to support them.
