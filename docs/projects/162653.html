Project Summary

Provide logistics services to support the humanitarian community's response to
emergency needs of people affected by natural disasters and / or by conflict in 2018
and to fill identified gaps in the emergency logistics and transportation capacity of
humanitarian relief items to vulnerable populations, particularly those living in rural
areas with limited access to services. This project is related to the Multi-Year
Humanitarian Strategy (MYHS) Outcome 1: Populations affected by natural or
man-made disaster receive timely assistance during and in the aftermath of a
shock.

1 - Needs

Recurring conflict and the effects of climate change have resulted in multipledisplacements and returns of displaced people throughout the last decade; withoutimmediate and timely assistance, large scale populations are increasingly vulnerable. Theurgent need for timely transportation of humanitarian relief items is crucial, to enable thedelivery of assistance; particularly to rural areas where access to basic services is+−Leaflet | © OpenStreetMapconstrained, and there is a limited presence of humanitarian actors. According to thelatest update provided by OCHA (October 1, 2017), 152,600 persons were displaced inDarfur in 2016. Since January 2017, a reduction in the number of newly displaced peoplehas been observed, but protracted displacement is still an issue across Darfur. In addition,IOM’s Displacement Tracking Matrix (DTM) system in West Kordofan, is currentlyregistering and verifying over 100,000 affected populations, who are currently living indisplacement sites or hosted by local communities. Beyond the conflict relateddisplacements, around 3.5 million people in Sudan are at risk of being affected by naturaldisasters resulting from climate change, which could result in further displacements.Displacement sites across Sudan remain hard to reach (e.g Kutum, Golo, Kebkabiya) andagencies find it difficult to sustain the cost associated with the transportation of reliefitems. Whilst agencies may have contingency or buffer stock, they do not havecontingency planning for the high cost of transportation, resulting in delays to transportlife saving items and a reduction in sectorial response. IOM proposes to mitigatetransportation related delays by having an emergency transportation system that canrespond timely and reliably during critical emergency situations.

2 - Activities / Outputs

Activities or outputs Outcome 1: - Populations affected by natural or man made disasterreceive timely assistance during and in the aftermath of a shock. This project proposes toensure timely and predictable transport services remain available to humanitarianpartners urgently needing to deploy relief items by road or air (through UNHAS) todisplacement sites hosting populations affected by natural disaster and conflicts.Through this project, IOM will mitigate morbidity and related negative consequencescaused by delays in the transportation of relief items. Each partner will submittransportation requests for relief items that urgently need to be transported to target areasin full coordination with the LET sector and OCHA, ensuring a coordinated approachbetween the work of IOM, WFP and OCHA and UNAMID, where relevant. Output 1: -Affected people receive timely life saving humanitarian assistance and protection.Administrate logistic database to track transported items and track fleet management: 1.Receive transportation requests from agencies that include beneficiaries’ gender and agebreakdown, where possible. 2. In close coordination with the LET sector lead and OCHA atthe field level, prioritize requests in accordance type and identified gap of commodities. 3.Select the best and most cost effective means of air or land transportation. 4. Processescorts request depending on the security situation of final destination (UNAMID, Police orboth). 5. Process waybill and associated transportation clearances based on the type oftransportation method (air/land). 6. Loading and offloading of relief items. 7. Report ondelivery of relief items compared to detailed inventory of transported items. 8. Supportcapacity building of HAC counterparts in logistics related training
