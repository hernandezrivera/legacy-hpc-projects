Project Summary

This project aimed to enhance hygiene promotion to effected population including children from IDPs ,returnee and host community in center Darfur (Zalingei, Nertiti ), south Darfur (mushing ,East jabel Marra and beleil localities), East Darfur ( Bu Karunka))with targets estimated about 4500 , the project aimed to, conducted 9 workshop, and conduct hygiene promotion campaign and establish 6 Nursery School in targeting areas, this consider a new initiative linkage between WASH sector and environment activity , the purpose of it is encourage students to participate actively to mitigate the impact of climate change as well as increase awareness of students on environment effect in target areas.

1 - Needs

of courses of two decades Sudan has been considered one as big humanitarian operation receiving humanitarian assistance in Africa and previous in the world ,since conflict started in 2003 between government of Sudan and movement oppositions , WASH services gaps in south Darfur(mushin) central darfur( zalingei and Nertiti) east Darfur(El fardois) has been increased , due to increase the numbers of IDPs particularly areas around Jebel Marra and this made the lack of WASH services as WASH sector reported in states level sector where the project should be carry out, because most of people Displaced and depending on humanitarian services .on 10 may 2018 inter – agency rapid assessment has been conducted in Murshing locality- south Darfur and mainly in IDPs the assessment found that, there gaps in following need to addressed ,Conduct a general cleaning campaign for the entire camp, Conduct training on hygiene promotion for the following segments of the community:Community leaders, Women/ youth groups and Children at the schools (hygiene clubs).Long term (1-3 month)Establishment of one mini water yard (hybrid system),Construction of 351 HH latrines,Formation and training of Sub-Water Committee for the new IDPs. The mission comprised of OCHA, WFP, UNHCR, UNICEF, WHO, IOM, WVI, UNAMID/PoC, UNAMID/ ODO, Jebel Marra charitable organization, Ministry of Health (nutrition and emergency department), Ministry of Social Welfare and, the WASH and hygiene promotion need to enhanced because it will keep children safe from AWD and other disease as well as SSP . between 12-17 November 2016 , WES, HAC, MoE and UPO conducted multi-sectorial needs assessment. On Saturday 15th of August 2020, HAC/UNHCR led an inter-agency assessment mission to El Ferdous town and the Southern Sudanese Refugees settlement and assessment found that, Immediate rehabilitation of 1,234 collapsed latrines in the seven affected areas by the flood , also Decontamination of the areas where the latrines originally collapsed within the host community as well the refugee settlement. In zalingei and Nertiti there are no an updated assessment. . For those needs which pointed , Almasheesh for Peace and Development Organization (APDO) full committed to provide education services for most effected vulnerable population especially children to enable then having access to quality education services . APDO will coordinate with line ministers, local authority and all stockholder to implementing this project with maximum standard . a APDO is active in WASH sector meeting also APDO regularly attending meeting in child protection..

2 - Activities/Outputs

Activities : 1- conduct 9 workshop for community leaders to participate hygiene promotion and peace building. 2- Provide Gender sensitive WASH facilities to 8 schools ad community including hygiene promotion ,gender sensitive hand washing facilities and gender sensitive safe drinking water point . 2- Conduct 30 session on role of children to actively participating in school hygiene campaign, the campaign will be conducted in 8 parimary school , each campaign at least targeting 200 children.3- Establish of 6 Nursery schools to produce 30000 trees in one year( 15000 fruitful tree,15000 shade tree), the nursery schools will provide schools and community with trees to mitigate the negative impact in schools environment will contribute to reduction the impact of climate change and reduce carbon dioxide in the air . as well as provide raise awareness about impact of cut trees . 4- establish 50 Radio episode to send information & messages to community on ( equal education, covid19 messages , hegyine promotion and peace building ) 5- distribute Soap for for women and children target areas .
