Project Summary

The project of the food security and livelihood aim to provide seeds ,training for farmers who effected by the conflict in Central Darfur and south Darfur state , this project aimed to support and secure the food and life condition for most vulnerable population in south Darfur state (Murshing and sharg El jebal marra locality) ,and it provide them with seeds modern training in agriculture techniques as well as animal vaccination for at least 10000 animal head. The project will targeting 750 HHs with seeds and training and vaccination .
The project will not consider political ,social, ethnic and culture background in selection criteria, the FSL Project aim to using methods of training to teaching farmers a new agriculture techniques .

Needs

The humanitarian situation in Darfur between 2014 up 2016 witnessed moderate improvement, but the situation in Darfur deteriorated again particularly around jebel marra due to the conflict. In 2016 almost 157,000 people were displaced and remain displaced according to UN , INGOs other NNGOs partners reports . also HRP indicated, the situation in sudan putting an additional strain on 5.67 million peoples in humanitarian needs and 3.6 million people currently suffering from food insecurity , 2.2 million children suffering from Global Acute Malnutrition(GAM), and returnees estimated around 301,000 already back to their village. According the government of sudan, the numbers has been reduced from 2.3 million to 1.99 million ,based or revised estimated. According to the HNO central Darfur and South Darfur states which will be targeting by the project ,the numbers of population as following figured as following : Nertiti IDPs 69265, Rekero 22289, Um Dukhan 62190. Inter –agency consist from (OCH ,UNCEIF ,UNHCR,WFP and IOM) ,INGOs (CRs ,DRC and WR) the ministries line (SMoE ,SMoSA , SMoE ) WES and HAC they were conducted inter –agency rapid need assessment from 25-29 April 2018, the teams visited Rekero town and Gemiza village purpose to assess the needs and to identify the existing resource and the capacity of IPDs and host community. The assessment which is conducted, resulted that, the agriculture is the main resource of the IDPs and the host community Some of the crops they grow include: Sorghum, Okra, tomatoes, groundnuts, potatoes, onions, garlic, citrus fruits [oranges, lemons, grapes, and Mangoes. Other sources of livelihood include sale of firewood and grass, the assessment report that, no No seeds or other farming inputs have been distributed during the 2018 agricultural season , and that will impacted for their food security in future. In south Darfur inter-agency rapid assessment were conducted in south Darfur the mission consisted from OCHA, HAC, WFP, UNHCR, UNICEF, WHO, IOM, WVI, UNAMID/PoC, UNAMID/ ODO, Jebel Marra charitable organization, Ministry of Health (nutrition and emergency department), Ministry of Social Welfare and WES. The assessment found that , the farming and reaming animals are main livelihood activities in East jebal Marra before displacement ,however , currently they are not engaged with any livelihood activities and the opportunity is very limited and they lack productive , the assessment has reported and identified limited opportunities to household to generate income and produce food with agriculture input activities. also the assessment indicated that the good animal health vaccination were specifically identified as a significant need this was confirmed by ministry of live stock in centeral and south Darfur and rapid assessment addition to that the animal health vey poor. APDO will focus project the improving the life condition among target group as well as animal health
