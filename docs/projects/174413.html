Project Summary

The proposed project prioritizes the pressing needs for flood affected communities in Sindh to rebuild damaged shelters, mitigate worsening food insecurity, observe safe hygiene practices to mitigate risk of COVID-19 and other communicable diseases, as well as safeguard against protection risks arising during the crisis. The project will target approximately 0.7 million people in need with integrated assistance. The assistance will be provided in the four worst-affected districts of Sindh: Umerkot, Mirpurkhas, Tando Muhammad Khan and Tharparkar.
The specific interventions include Shelter Repair Tool Kit and IEC material. A comprehensive toolkit has been designed to ensure that all local shelter designs in the target areas may be repaired through the tools. To ensure the beneficiaries are aware of safe and DRR compliant construction practices, the project engineers will conduct shelter repair trainings for the beneficiaries and build one model shelter per village to demonstrate the construction practices. Furthermore, IEC material will be disseminated to the beneficiaries to allow easier understanding and recall of the construction practices.
Moreover, damage to and losses of crops, food stocks, livestock, houses and other belongings due to the floods have left the affected communities exposed to food and livelihood insecurity. To mitigate this, Cesvi proposes Cash for Work schemes (Rs. 12,000 grants per beneficiary), provision of livestock to women, as well as provision of agricultural inputs, including seeds, fertilizers, beans, pulses, etc.
The proposed interventions also include comprehensive WASH assistance, including repair/rehabilitation of drinking water supply schemes, provision of safe WASH installations at health care facilities treating COVID-19 cases, handwashing stations at health care facilities, schools and public areas, installation/rehabilitation of latrines and awareness raising campaign in intervention areas on preventive measures against COVID-19 pandemic.
The project will also mainstream protection by ensuring it abides by the ‘Do No Harm’ approach and provides the beneficiaries with an effective complaint response mechanism to redress any concerns from the communities throughout project lifecycle. The project will prioritize vulnerable groups, including female-headed households, pregnant and lactating women (PLWs), widows, orphans, chronically ill and persons with disability (PWDs).
Without appropriate shelter, women and children are less secure than men, and more vulnerable to protection-related risks. In the majority cases, due to the prevalent social and cultural customs, women prefer defecation in the night, consequently women and girls are exposed to higher protection risks. The risks are further compounded by power outages resulting from the monsoon rains. Moreover, the local wildlife, such as snakes, can also potentially cause harm in the dark and in the open without shelter. Therefore, the provision of solar light with mobile phone charging outlets has been proposed as part of the Shelter NFI kits.
The project also includes protection specific interventions aimed at raising awareness regarding protection issues among target communities. Cesvi will adopt a two-tier model to implement awareness raising sessions. First, in coordination with district governments, Cesvi will conduct a training of trainers (ToT) on sensitizing communities to protection risks, avoiding stigma regarding protection related issues, utilizing the existing government protection services and mechanisms. The trainers will be responsible to cascade the trainings to communities, facilitated by Cesvi. Further, Cesvi will ensure the coordination and collaboration with provincial protection working group and National Protection Center as well as Social Welfare Department.

1- Project Activities

WASH Activity 1 The issue of availability and access to sufficient and safe water for drinking and other consumption needs will be addressed through rehabilitation of tube-well based water supply schemes that are either non-functional or partially functional. Cesvi’s will establish 200 hand-pump in 4 remote districts of Sindh.Activity 4 Lack of access to proper facilities has forced the communities to practice open defecation and adopt other unhygienic practices, which can expose them to heightened risks of COVID-19 transmission as well as other waterborne diseases. Therefore, the proposed action will provide 2000 emergency portable latrines as a short term support to affected communities to encourage better hygiene and consequently health in 4 rural and remote districts of Sindh. This support will reach 2000 families of target districtsActivity 6 Provision of appropriate WASH installations at schools and healthcare facilities. Our assessment indicates schools and basic health facilities lack sanitation facilities resulting in difficulties for students (especially girls). Provision of latrine in schools & health centers has also been identified as one of the priorities need in our need’s assessment and 4 districts in Sindh.Activity 8 It will be ensured that people have the life-saving information they need to protect themselves and others from the virus and to reduce its impact on their health and social life. Channels will be identified to reach audiences e.g mass media, radio listeners, social media. Cesvi will ensure that all communication is inclusive and sensitive to the needs of people with disabilities. Activity 9 Installation of hand washing stations at key public places and distribution of soap Cesvi will support the government in this sector through the provision of paddle-operated hand washing stations (HWS) and sanitation services. Cesvi will establish 150 HWS in Sindh with a target of public places.Food Security Activity 1 Damage to and losses of crops, food stocks, livestock, houses and other belongings due to the floods have left the affected communities exposed to food and livelihood insecurity. To mitigate this, Cesvi proposes CWS (Rs. 12000 per beneficiary), provision of livestock to women, as well as provision of agricultural inputs, including seeds, fertilizers, beans, pulses, etc (Rs 8525 per household). Total 150 scheme will be designed for target beneficiaries. For men activities will include irrigation channels and communal water ponds, path ways restoration schemes and other public assets at village level, basic toolkit will also be provided.Shelter NFI Activity 1 the proposed intervention will provide comprehensive shelter repair toolkits to the most vulnerable households to help them rebuild their homes. The tools will include steel pans, trowels, cotton ropes, mason hammers, tarpaulins and canvas tarpaulins (water and rot proof), planners, measuring tapes, GI water buckets, hoes, and axes.Activity 2 The affected households will be provided comprehensive shelter repair kits to fulfil their basic needs, stainless steel plates, glasses, jug, jerry can 10 ltr, mosquito net, rectangular medium 130 X 180 X 150cm, solar light.Protection Activity 3 Due to floods and COVID-19 outbreak, women in Sindh are in urgent need of livelihood support. Cesvi will provide 200 women livelihood assets (livestock) and also provide additional 100 soft conditional cash grant to rehabilitate livestock shelter. Activity 10 Provision of Mental Health and Psychosocial Support Services to the most vulnerable children (girls and boys) Cesvi will establish Counselling clubs for student’s psychosocial support at schools, reaching 6000 childrenActivity 12 Cesvi will adopt cascading TOT model and develop module on protection implemented at community level, Cesvi will coordinate with protection working group at province level and national and with protection center to ensure the effective collaborations within the sector.
