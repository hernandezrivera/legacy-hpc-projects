Project Summary

Through the proposed 12-month multi-sectoral intervention, DRC will provide humanitarian and protection assistance for crisis-affected communities in Deir Ez-Zor, Dar’a, Hama, and Idleb governorates of Syria to bridge the existing gap in access to essential services and basic needs and to assist populations to live in safety and dignity. This intervention will support those who continue to suffer as the crisis reaches its 13th year and will address renewed humanitarian needs triggered by the worsening socio-economic crisis and escalations of conflict in multiple locations across the country. Building on DRC’s experience implementing USAID-funded interventions in the region, DRC will support the provision of basic services to individuals experiencing extreme vulnerabilities and/or protection risks by delivering multi-sector assistance across Food Security, Protection, and WASH sectors. DRC will maintain the capacity to agilely address basic needs, while also mitigating the impact of some of the key drivers of the crisis, including water scarcity and severely limited purchasing power, leading households to rely on harmful coping strategies as a means to survive. The proposed intervention aligns with the first strategic objective (SO) of the 2023 HRP ‘Provide lifesaving and life-sustaining humanitarian assistance to the most vulnerable people with an emphasis on those in areas with high severity of needs’ (SO1).
FSA: To meet the immediate food needs of the most vulnerable populations in Ideb (Ma'arrat An Nu'man and Sanjar) and Deir Ez-Zor (Khasham, Deir-ez-Zor), DRC will provide emergency Cash for Food (CfF) for 1,000 households to cover essential food needs and contribute to food security. DRC received FAS endorsement for the proposed locations, caseload size, and modality, and will coordinate with partners to mitigate risks of duplication and ensure appropriate targeting at the sub-district level.
Protection: DRC will deliver a comprehensive package of protection services in Dar’a, Deir-ez-Zor, Hama, and Idleb, to prevent, mitigate, and respond to protection risks through Child Protection Case Management, including cash or in-kind assistance and referrals to other services, as well as sycho-Social Support (PSS) services and protection-centred information sessions.
WASH: DRC will build on previous WASH programs and undertake limited, quick-fix rehabilitation of war-damaged and war-affected water supply infrastructure, light sewage network rehabilitation, and conduct community hygiene promotion.
