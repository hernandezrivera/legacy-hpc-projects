Project Summary

Zimbabwe is currently reeling under many challenges that includes climatic induced droughts and economic instability. The provision of basic services such as water, sanitation and hygiene services has remained critical low. Vulnerable populations in rural and urban areas are lacking access to water of sufficient quality, proper sanitation facilities and information on basic health and hygiene practices. According to the 2020 Humanitarian Response Plan (HRP), 30% of the 55,593 water sources tracked by the rural water information management system, are functional and protected, increasing the risk of WASH-related disease. The HRP of 2020 revealed that over 6.5 million people were affected by the current WASH challenges in Zimbabwe, with over 3.6 million people in need of urgent assistance. The WASH Cluster preliminary results for 2021 HRP 2021 estimated that 2,983,954 people are in severity ranking 3, 4 and 5 are in need of WASH emergence assistance. The continued deficiencies in WASH services are likely to continue due to prolonged impacts of COVID-19 pandemic. Zimbabwe Council of Churches (ZCC) is proposing for a WASH intervention to ensuring access to safe water, sanitation facilities and access to services and information to promote health and hygiene practices to 43,950 vulnerable women, men, girls and boys in targeted districts.

1 - Activities

Inorder to ensure that affected people have access to water of appropriate quantity and quality and awareness messages on health and hygiene practices, the project will adopt WASH interventions that will cover key institutions (schools and health facilities) and communities in targeted districts with activities that include restoration of access to safe water, provision of institutional hygiene kits and dissemination of awareness messages on health and hygiene practices. The project will also strengthen water quality surveillance and monitoring to ensure appropriate action are taken to prevent the outbreak of WASH related diseases such as cholera and diarrhoea. In addition, the project will support health institution with provision of infection, prevention and control (IPC) materials to prevent hospital associated infections. The project will also provide WASH Hygiene kits (NFIs) to the most vulnerable and marginalised individuals in communities. Further, the project will capacitate WASH stakeholders and communities on Community Based Management (CBM) and Participatory Health and Hygiene Education (PHHE) to ensure sustainability.

8 - Accountability to Affected People
8.1 - Describe how your project was designed in consultation with communities? How will you incorporate communities in project implementation and what feedback mechanisms do you have in place?

At the onset of the project, the project will ensure that targeted communities are fully sensitised about the project through inception meetings. The proposed project will take advantage of existing local mechanisms such as the District Water Supply and Sanitation Committee, Village Water and Sanitation Sub Committees, Ward Water and Sanitation Committees to reinforce the engagement with affected people to ensure accountability. Women and men, girls and boys, will be consulted during planning, Iimplementation, monitoring and evaluation of the project through the existing local mechanisms. To ensure full participation of affected people, community representatives will be involved during repair of water points and construction of WASH infrusturure where they will provide local materials and labour. The affected communities will form Water Point Management Committees as well as establish Operation and Maintenance Committees for the water points and WASH facilities. ZCC will ensure that the affected people have ccess to information, and have confidential ways of voicing complaints. The project will adopt already feedback mechanisms that are currer giving their feedback through existing local WASH Committees, suggestion box, and hotline/tollfree line.

9 - Environmental considerations
9.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Mobilising of local resources such as bricks and riversand for restoration of WASH facilities may have negative impacts to the environment such as soil erosion and siltation of reservoirs hence the proposed project will undertake environmental assessment before mobilization of local resources by the community. The project will also mainstream environmental issues at sensitization meetings and trainings.
