Project Summary

The project provides direct support to 7,348 out of school or at risk of being out of school children with opportunity to access education in communities of Kaga LGA of Borno State. The project specifically targets vulnerable populations, including girls, IDPs, and children affected by the conflict and recent economic crisis.
Taimako-CDI will directly implement the project in close coordination with the EiE sector, SUBEB and Ministry of Education to ensure that the quality and delivery of education services are consistent with the Education Ministry policies, protocols and strategies, and coordinate with the same for training and monitoring of activities.
The project will work with critical stakeholders to identify communities with high concentrations of IDPs and host community children that are out of school and will conduct rolling Community Education and Conflict Assessments, engaging both communities and government officials to ascertain the level of safety, number of beneficiaries in the community and the needs for education. Initial assessment results will be used to design an adaptive approach to enroll out-of-school children ages 3-17 into formal schools and non-formal learning centers, as well as provide psycho-social support required to start the emotional healing process.

1 - Needs

Considering the tenth year of the crisis in north-east, Nigeria remains one of the most severe in the world. In recent months, thousands of new arrivals have been recorded, largely coming from hard-to-reach areas for reasons related to insecurity and military operations. This displaced population are highly food insecure and with disruption in commercial activities in the region in general, this increase the rate of out of school children in affected communities. it is diﬃcult for the New Arrivals to have any meaningful means of survival other than assistance from the humanitarian actors.According to 2019 Education Humanitarian Response Plan Dashboard, Kaga LGA is one of the locations with number of out of school children and only 6-10 percent of the target of 2019 HRP reached.Kaga Local government is facing challenges providing quality education services for those affected by the conflict, including the unequal distribution, poor quality and unfriendly learning environment of formal government schools. These educational challenges combined with cultural and religious constraints, the marginalization of vulnerable groups - specifically females and children with physical disabilities - resulted in a high percentage of out-of-school children and youth. Many internally displaced children lacked access to safe and friendly learning environments as the conflict forced some schools to close for several years.Both children and teachers experienced trauma, often in or around schools, making the learningenvironment in the region one of stress and fear. Therefore, traumatized parents and caregivers kept their children out of school, which led to low attendance rates. “72% of primary age children never attended school in Borno state. This compares with less than 3% in most other part of the state.

2 - Activities

Activity 1. establishment of temporary learning centers, Taimako-CDI will establish NFLs and provide tutoring programs in the targeted locations. To ensure age appropriateness and gender inclusiveness in the enrollment of learners, the project will initially establish four types of learning centers: Non-Formal Learning Centers for boys and girls ages 3-12, Adolescent Girls Learning Centers for young girls ages 13-17, Youth Learning Centers for young boys ages 13-17, and Centers for the Physically Challenged for learners living with disabilities ages 3-17Activity: 2 Distribution of scholastic materials, the project will distribute scholastic materials to the learners during enrollment in to non-formal learning centers and while mainstreaming into formal schools.Activity 3. provision of gender segregated WASH facilities and installation of recreational facilities.Activity 4. Training of adolescent girls at AGLC for skill acquisitions.Activity 5. Training of teachers tutors and learning facilitators on improved teaching and learning techniques, as well as psyco-social support skills and positive discipline.Activity 6. Enrollment of learners.Activity 7. Training of SMBC on psycho-social support. Activity 8. Mainstreaming of learner in to formal school.

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

For all projects, Taimako-CDI foresees an interim monitoring system, which includes verification on the achievements and on the status of the activities’ implementation every three months. Keeping these deadlines in mind, the accountability time frame must allow Taimako-CDI staff to collect and analyze information, and to disclose it to beneficiaries respecting the principle of “taking into account”. Another key point for a functional accountability system is the awareness of Taimako-CDI staff of the fact that the monitoring process is a continuum: since the planning phase, through all the implementation process, the gathering of information and feedback has to be continuous and carried out both in structured, specific moments, and in more informal contexts that can be found through the projects’ development. Regarding the opportunities and right times for collecting and sharing feedback, information and changes, Taimako-CDI staff have to be aware that the moments for doing this are not only formal and agreed (meetings, reunions with representatives, interviews, etc.), but can happen during other activities, even those that are not foreseen or implemented by the organization, but of which it has to be made the most.
