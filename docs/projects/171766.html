Project Activities

Under Somalia Protection Cluster/ GBV AOR Strategy GHF undertakes: 1. Conduct survivor-centered multi-sectoral support (medical, legal, PSS, material and safety) and case management. Material support will include dignity kits and T-shirts. 2. To reduce the risks of GBV, the project will distribute solar lanterns to survivors and those at risks and install street lights in selected locations. 3 Social- Economic reintegration and empowerment for GBV survivors through livelihood skills training, legal support and cash-based assistance. 4 Conduct awareness-raising and outreach sessions on prevention and response to GBV, harmful practices, human rights among others. 5. Capacity building and training of non-institutional service providers, this will include training of community based protection structures, duty bearers and humanitarian workers and on GBV Prevention and response. 6 Awareness raising, advocacy and public outreach campaigns on GBV prevention, response and mitigation. GHF will reinforce formal and community institutions for better legal and community-based protection of vulnerable groups from violence, coercion and abuse. Key to achieve this objective, activities will include awareness-raising and outreach among communities on protection issues, capacity building and training for duty bearers, service providers and humanitarian workers on GBV. On advocacy, GHF will strengthen coordination of protection services and enhance advocacy on protection with relevant stakeholders. Key activities will include strengthening of referral pathways, mapping of service providers and Protection Monitoring of rights violations. GHF will further identify and provide protection-oriented support to people with disabilities in the target locations.The traditional legal framework for the land administration institutions remains unclear and insufficient as it is contrary to women's rights to land especially the widows. Many cases go unresolved. Some languish in cells and prisons without fair judicial process.The theory of change is based on strengthening justice and expanding the quality of legal services, creating effective, accountable and gender-responsive justice institutions, initiation and promotion of enabling environment for access to justice and peace among women and men. An enabling environment is supported by international and regional standards and norms, domestic laws, policies and budgets. SDG Target 10.3 clearly articulates the linkages between law, policy and practice and reducing inequality, calling on States to “ensure equal opportunity and reduce inequalities of outcome, including by eliminating discriminatory laws, policies and practices and promoting appropriate legislation, policies and action in this regard”. Increased monitoring on access to and dispensation of justice is critical. Setting up mobile judicial courts to address serious cases touching on land and gender issues. Traditional and Somali national legal practioners are harmonised through training and integration within internationally recognized mechanisms on issues of transitional justice, gender and human rights. We propose Expansion and rehabilitation of courts for good working judicial atmosphere.coordinate with universities and colleges teaching law as well as Government judicial offices in enhancing harmonious realisation of the project objectivesprioritises Law students, internes and practioners in addressing the gender based injustices such as disinheritances and dispossession of properties. The minority farmers and clans shall be top-priority in addressing and redressing their cases. • Setting up verifiable and strong case management register for compilation of cases with special reference to land and sexual and gender based violence and injustices. This is for urgent intervention. We propose Truth and Peace committees at village levels; well trained on human rights and gender, transitional justice

What is the justification for using cash or vouchers instead of in kind?

Effective and efficient , dignified approach allowing right to choose under the humanitarian tenets in using cash intervention. Multi-Purpose Cash Assistance (MPCA) is seen as an effective conduit to aid extremely vulnerable populations, supporting a dignified assistance and value for money allowing the affected people to choose and buy their own food and non-food items from local shops across the country, helping to sustain the local economy in this critical time while paying off their loans.

Explain how the project design considered market functionality ?

In the market risk analysis under the Cash based based assistance, the risk is reduced and transferred to the money transfer-market.We considered the following:• Can we locate a safe environment in the area which can be used for cash distributions without further risking the already vulnerable populations?• Involvement of the local authorities, national and local Cash working groups in enhancing the project efficacy.• Safety and security risks and assumptions along with mitigations in face of the beneficiariesThe more significant challenge was increased displacement of populations, andloss of purchasing power of traders and end-consumers. The vendorsand wholesalers surveyed reported accepting local and international currencies(USD), while mobile money transfers and cash-in-hands are the preferredpayment methods, (99%) and (97%) respectively.

The project implementing teams will develop and implement measures and actions toregularly assess, intervene and manage the risks of SGBV and PSEA. GHF shall setup direct people to people accountability feedback and complaints mechanisms, evenfor them directly reporting to us and the Head of UNOCHA Pool Fund withoutimpediments. GHF will engage a dedicated specialist on risk management andcompliance within UN international Disaster Legal protocols. Each indicator shall beevaluated based on gender, age, disability and minority status-clan inclusivity acrossall project steps.GHF liaises with local and national authorities in ensuring project safety and security, and proper reporting who are useful in sensitisation and community targeting.GHF ensures that beneficiary data is not shared beyond the office and the money -transfer agency in order to protect their data.Persons with Disabilities , women , child-headed households and the elderly shall be prioritised, while not traveling long distances to access aid.The project addresses inter-communal reconciliation, shared justice based on the Xeer Somali traditions and the modern laws.
