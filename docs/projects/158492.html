Project Summary

Activities: 1. Introduce and expand access to the life-saving interventions of comprehensive abortion, PAC, and contraceptive care for women and girls . 2. Conduct rapid facility assessments, implement minor renovations of health facilities, and sensitize facility staff in order to ready facilities to provide high quality, life-saving SRH interventions for women and girls 3. Conduct clinical trainings for public sector health workers in Comprehensive Abortion Care, PAC, and contraceptive services 4. Train nurses from youth corners in the provision of Comprehensive Abortion Care/Post-Abortion Care and postabortion contraceptives, including Long Acting and Reversible Contraceptives (LARC) 5. Provide ongoing clinical mentoring and support activities for Ipas-trained health providers to ensure the safe, consistent, high-quality provision of life-saving safe abortion, PAC, and contraceptive care 6. Conduct quarterly monitoring visits at Ipas-supported health facilities for clinical support and data collection 7. Train pharmacists in supply chain management, harm reduction strategies, and the provision of information related to life-saving abortion and contraceptive commodities 8. Engage youth champions/leaders and youth-based organizations to facilitate outreach and information, education, and communication (IEC) activities that link facilities and community outreach, including information about referrals to health facilities providing life-saving safe abortion, PAC, and contraceptive services 9. Train 60 community mentors from girls’ clubs, girls’ associations, and CBOs to engage in outreach and IEC activities on SRH, including information about the risks associated with unintended pregnancy and unsafe abortion, and accessing SRH services 10. Support the provision of life-saving abortion and contraceptive supplies, including Manual Vacuum Aspiration (MVA) Kits, Medical Abortion (MA) drugs, and back-up supplies of IUCD and implants to Ipas-supported facilities 11. Work with select partner organizations, youth groups, and other stakeholders to develop and pilot rights-based, youth-focused, life-saving interventions (by addressing the risks associated with unintended pregnancy and unsafe abortion) 12. Strengthen local quality assurance mechanisms in facilities and with community outreach workers, including data collection for health outcomes
