Project Summary

Since the Influx of Forcibly Displaced Myanmar Citizen’s (FDMC) in Bangladesh in August 2017, an estimated 9 million Rohingya men, women and children has taken shelter in Teknaf and Ukhiya Upazila of Cox’s Bazar district in Bangladesh. Among them about 4.8 Million have been reported as children of age group 0-24 years. More than 40% of Rohingya children, adolescent and youths of age (3-24) years has been reported as remained excluded from access to quality education under gender equality, safety, protection and DRR preparedness.
Context of the proposed project:
Children of age (3-24) years of FDMC families of Refugee Camps- 4, 8E & 9 under Ukhiya upazila needs quality education with education core materials, safe and protective learning centre as like as other area of Rohingya Camps; UTSA has conducted a comprehensive need assessment on 3-15 years age group for basic needs of Education in Emergency (EiE) and the needs of vocational education for adolescent of age (15-24) years; it is found that Rohingya Girls & Boys of 3-15 years need quality education in Camp-4, 9, & 8E with core materials, nutritional support, recreational support with play and game, etc., and the adolescent and youths needs Vocational education for achieving life skill technical hands for employment & income.
To establish and equip of 8 Learning Centre in our targeted Refugee camps; where Learning Competency Framework and Approach (LCFA) for 700 children ages (3-14) years and vocational education for 200 adolescent of age (15-24) year with inclusion of Gender-age marker, disability, protection, DRR, etc., Establish training and mentor-ship of community-led protection and management committees for the Learning Centre to be done.
A comprehensive Disaster Risk Reduction (DRR) training is to be included and action planning and follow-up for children, adolescents to be arranged at Temporary Learning Centre (TLC) and Adolescent group for protection and learning centre management committees in the project.
A Continuous professional development encompassing conflict-sensitive education, leaner-centre and subject based pedagogy for learning animators, facilitator and teachers to be done; an Integrated safeguard and child protection mechanism including mental health & psychosocial support (PSS) activities to be initiated.
Project activities:
The applicant shall improve & repairing of its existing 7 learning centre (6 in camp- 4 & 1 in camp-8E) and shall construct 3 new centre (1 in camp-4, 1 in camp-8E & 1 in Camp 9) for 700 Rohingya children of age (3-14) years and 200 adolescents of age (15-24) years under the proposed project.
The applicant shall enroll 700 children (girls & boys) of age (3-14) years and adolescent & youths of age (15-24) years to quality education and technical skills, provide qualified & trained teachers/facilitators, ensure community engagement through management committee with inclusion approach of gender-age, disability, safety, protection and DRR preparedness activities under the proposed project.
Target working area:
i. Kutupalong Camp- 4, Balukhali Camp- 8E & Camp- 9
Target group of direct beneficiaries:
i. 700 Girls and Boys of age 3-14 years (50% girls) in the Refugee camp
ii. 200 Adolescent and youths of age 15-24 years (50% female) in Refugee camp.

Needs

Since the Influx of Forcibly Displaced Myanmar Citizen’s (FDMC) in Bangladesh in August 2017, an estimated 9 million Rohingya men, women and children has taken shelter in Teknaf and Ukhiya Upazila of Cox’s Bazar district in Bangladesh. Among them about 4.8 Million have been reported as children of age group 0-24 years. More than 40% of Rohingya children, adolescent and youths of age (3-24) years has been reported as remained excluded from access to quality education under gender equality, safety, protection and DRR preparedness. Despite of the significant contribution of humanitarian support to the Forcibly Displaced Myanmar Citizens (FDMC) in Teknaf and Ukhiya Upazila in Cox’s Bazar since the Rohingya influx in August 2017; still Children of age (3-14) years and adolescent of age (15-24) years has been suffering from lack of standardized quality education and inclusion of gender, disability, protection, MHPSS support and DRR preparedness in the targeted Rohingya camp- 4, 8E & 9 under Rajapalong and Palongkhali unions of Ukhiya Upazila of Cox’s Bazar district in Bangladesh. Lack of safe, protective and inadequate quality learning centre is another drawback of getting quality education in the area; which are the other basic needs of the target group there. Qualified facilitator/teachers, trained on Learning Competency Framework and Approach (LCFA) is seriously needed. At the same time adequate knowledge on gender-age marker principle, emergency disaster preparedness and response planning, protection and child-friendly environment is required. Refurbishment and provision of core materials & equipment consider safety, protection, proper learning environment, age-appropriateness, security is a great concern. An advance/thematic education principle, gender mainstreaming, age, protection and environment-friendly sensitization in learning centre are needed.Gender equity and equality is another basic necessity within the Rohingya community; as girls of above 13 years are usually not permitted to attend in education centre under social & religion barrier. The estimated literacy level of refugees prior to displacement was 50%, there is little correction between those who attended school in Myanmar and those who are now attending at learning centre in refugee camps in Cox’s Bazar. About 80% adolescent and young youths of age group 15-24 years are remain excluded from any learning facility; we see a gender-age marking approach is seriously needed for gender mainstreaming, equality and empowerment.Language barrier and limitation are another area of work for more effective approach in education, learning and application in the camp. All education materials, lesion plan, curriculum shall need to be translated in both English and Myanmar language. Hence teachers / facilitators are needed to be rained on multiple language including English and Myanmar language. Adolescent and youth of age 15-24 years are worried about their future; as they has no technical skill on any trade to generate employment; this is why a comprehensive vocational education facilities are needed for the targeted adolescent and youths in the concern working area.Natural disaster (seasonal storm, cyclone, tornado, flood) are another common threat for lives and resources of this locality; so, on the question of sustainable quality education, a comprehensive natural disaster risk reduction knowledge and preparedness is much needed. In this regard community engagement and ownership will be a mandatory factor and that to be ensured under our project through formation of management committee at every learning centre & vocational centre and that would includes gender equity, equality, disability and shall be trained on inclusion, protection and DRR preparedness.

Activities or outputs

Project Interventions/outputs:01. Enrollment of Rohingya Children, adolescent and youths of age 3-24 years old in the Refugee Camps to ensure the provision of Early Child Development (ECD), Basic Education in Emergency (EiE), Life Skills and Vocational Education with inclusion of gender, disability. safety & protection02. Repairing of the existing and construction of the new learning centre and vocational centre in the targeted camps considering gender and disability friendly WASH facilities, proper drainage & waste management system.03. Ensure standardized core education materials for every children and adolescent enrolled with equitable distribution and follow-up with ASER Plus level competitiveness & assessment.04. Select qualified teachers and facilitators for every learning centre & vocational centre and ensure teacher’s quality with continuous improvement through capacity building training and advance/thematic education principles on gender sensitivity, protection, CwC & DRR preparedness. 05. Formation of Learning Centre Management Committee (LCMC) and Vocational Centre management Committee for establishing ownership and community engagement; provide management training with inclusion of gender, disability, participatory monitoring, protection and DRR preparedness & responsiveness.06. Conduct parenting session on proper management of children in home, gender mainstreaming, child protection, and DRR preparedness.07. Provide Mental Health and Psychosocial Support to children, adolescent and parents for better life expectation and positive responsiveness. 08. Develop a comprehensive contingency plan with DRR preparedness training, demonstration, funding and action plan.
