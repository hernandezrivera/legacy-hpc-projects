Project Summary

The purpose of the project is to ensure the continuity of the humanitarian community activities during COVID-19 despite the supply chain disruptions. To prevent and mitigate the increased restrictions and supply chain disruptions, the Logistics Sector needs to ensure the access to transport and to sufficient stock of the required supplies and commodities across the 47 health districts to answer to the humanitarian needs and support the Government of Burundi in the response to COVID-19. The Government of Burundi needs to ensure that its 1,058 health centres and 93 hospitals are well-equipped with sufficient protection items, medical supplies and equipment to handle the COVID-19 potential cases. The humanitarian community will require facilitation of transport and pre-positioning of their required supplies to be closer to their operations to mitigate any road closure, flooding and logistics constraints due to COVID-19. The assessment and reinforcement of storage capacity in-country will be key. Setting forward logistics hubs close to borders such as Kobero and extend the existing ones in Bujumbura, Ngozi and Gitega will allow to cover the 18 provinces of Burundi through short distances mitigating constraint challenges due to the rainy season and the COVID-19 measures. The transport across country will be facilitated through the logistics hubs that will significantly reduce distances and save time.
