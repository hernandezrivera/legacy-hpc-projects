Project Summary

This year OCHA took a different approach in categorizing IDPs. There are new IDPs that are sub-grouped into IDPs in camps and IDPs out of camps. There are also protracted IDPs. Non displaced populations are classified into host communities, non-displaced conflict-affected populations, non-displaced climate shocks affected populations and IDPs Returnees. In terms of the Severity of Needs, the Eastern, Northern, and Southern part of Ethiopia constitute the main priority of the Cluster. According to the analysis on the severity, it is estimated that 7.85 million people are going to need ES/NFI support out of which 59% (4.62 million) are going to be IDPs and 49% are going to be Returnees (1.99 million) and general non-displaced communities (1.24 million). Based on this, the Cluster has targeted 2.67 million people as IDPs, 0.75 million as Returnees and 1.06 million people as General non-displaced people, bringing the total to 4.48 million people. The responses are going to depend on the settlement types, the profile of the targeted population. The situation in Guji zone is more pronounced due prevailing conflict drought. Thousands of people have been displaced and exposed to different protection risks leaving them vulnerable. The conflict has impacted critical sectors such as, shelter, health, nutrition, education, livelihood –mentioned sectors need prioritization and immediate interventions as confirmed by the OCHA report on IDPs and Host communities in a Negelle Town and Gorodola Woredas of Guji Zone, Oromia Region. Urgent responses are recommended to save lives, alleviate suffering, and uphold human dignity as the IDPs are in dire need of humanitarian assistance. The majority of the conflict induced IDPs are women and children who are extremely vulnerable to the critical situation they are in. To overcome this AID plan to address 5870 Conflict affected individuals by in kind or in cash ESNFI response. Hence, the objectives of the current project are to ensure access to life-saving shelter & NFIs for 5870 beneficiary at Negelle town and Goro Dola Woreda of Guji Zone. The project has been designed in line with the National Shelter/NFI cluster response plan document and the proposed activities are part of the Shelter/NFI cluster priority.
The project will be implemented over a period of 6 months while AID has the full support of the regional, zonal and woreda authorities for the implementation of the project.
Through this project 1,067 Households 5871 IDPs 3,416 IDPs from Gora Dola and 2,455 IDPs from Nagele Town will be reached. AID will undertake the following activities
•Conduct Market Assessment, selection, and verification of beneficiaries- Prior to implementation Market Assessment will be conducted to assess the functionality of the nearby market, the market trend, and the price of items.
•Set-up beneficiaries targeting, prioritizing, and distribution committee in Goro dolo and Nagelle
•Conduct registration of target beneficiaries (both men and women within the household) with government authorities and IDP representatives.
•Conducting Protection mainstreaming awareness for the targeted beneficiaries.
•Procure and distribute in-kind 5871ESNFI (V2) kits to IDP HHs in Goro dola and Nagele - a total of 5871 IDPs will be provided with the ESNFI (V2) kits.
•Provision of Emergency Shelter and NFI Kits for 3416 IDPs in Goro dola woreda through combination of in-kind and cash (40% in- kind and 30 % in cash)
•The most vulnerable 5871individuals in Nagelle town and Goro dola woreda will be targeted and verified for the provision of Emergency shelter and NFI assistance. Hence, the most vulnerable IDPs will be given a priority as the kit aims to ensure the safety and security of IDPs while having the support to construct emergency shelter and basic household utensils.
•Conduct Post Distribution Monitoring (PDM) –
To undertake the above-mentioned activities, a total of 252,886 USD is allocated for AID
