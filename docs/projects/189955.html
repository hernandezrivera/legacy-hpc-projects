Project Summary

The project seeks to accelerate the socio-economic inclusion of IDPs by stimulating growth in local market sectors while addressing the barriers to their inclusion in these market systems. Adopting integrated (targeting both the displaced and host communities) and market-based approaches, will ensure the sustainability of livelihoods transforming the wellbeing of the families over time while also strengthening peaceful co-existence and stimulating local economic growth. Intervention components will include social empowerment, employability, livelihoods creation, market and value chain development and access to financial services.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The program seeks to accelerate the socio-economic inclusion of IDPs (60%) and host communities (40%), particularly women and youth (60%), by stimulating growth in local market sectors while addressing the barriers to their inclusion in these market systems.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Proposed interventions: (1) Social Empowerment: Promotion of mentorship and gender and age sensitive life-skills training focusing on women’s and youth’s economic empowerment. Support the development of formal and informal community-based groups to reinforce social capital and enhance peaceful co-existence. (2) Employability: Provision of vocational and technical training according to the need of the beneficiaries and market labour demand, including in-house learning opportunities through placements in local businesses. (3) Livelihoods Creation: Support to business development and the provision of productive assets and cash-grants/start-up capital to the creation/scale-up of income-generating activities. (4) Market and Value Chain Development: Enhance private sector engagement to promote inclusive market systems and value chain development, ensuring the sustainable participation of the persons of concern as a tool to support local integration. UNHCR will facilitate the interaction with relevant public and private-sector stakeholders to enhance market linkages and increase the opportunities available. (5) Access to Financial Services: Foster economic inclusion of persons of concerns, including access to bank services, microcredit, establishment of informal community groups (e.g., VSLGs), financial literacy and other relevant activities.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

UNHCR foments livelihoods through economic inclusion to address and reduce protection risks and adopt livelihoods approaches targeting groups with specific needs. In order to do so, UNHCR identifies protection risks linked with the lack/or engagement in certain livelihoods activities. Additionally, the agency also anticipates potential risks that might arise from the implementation of the interventions such as domestic violence resulting from changing gender roles. The project interventions will be constantly revised and adapted to identify and prioritize individuals at risk, thereby enabling intervention to mitigate the risk, ensuring that no harm is caused. The proposed interventions will comprise a strong protection referral system to ensure a timely and effective response to the specific needs.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

UNHCR’s work is guided by a rights-based approach and focuses on the meaningful engagement of persons with disabilities in all decisions that affect them; this includes the identification and resolution of barriers - including environmental, attitudinal and communication barriers - preventing persons with disabilities from accessing and participating in programmes and activities on an equal basis with others. To identify and prioritize households with persons with disabilities, the Protection Cluster Targeting Criteria will be utilized.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Most of the activities are limited to institutional capacity building, skills, technical assistance, studies, training, and human and institutional. The project is expected to have a positive environmental impact as it supports the community livelihoods, income, and job creation for youths and security, especially for women and children thereby contributing to sustainable management of natural resources and its environment.
