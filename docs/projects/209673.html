Project Summary

Needs
At Humanitarian Needs Overview 2024 Agriculture in Syria remains a key economic sector and a major source of livelihoods for most crisis-affected families, but it still does not meet the full national need. Syria is seeking to develop comprehensive approaches to food security, as food, in terms of the way it is grown, produced, traded, transported, processed, stored and marketed, is the essential link between people, the surrounding environment, and the path to inclusive economic growth.
The crops deficit and other affected crops production losses is due to various challenges affecting the respective production segment of the various crop value chains. These include the high cost of inputs and unavailability of quality seeds, fertilizers, herbicides, pesticides, fuel and key among these is the erratic rainfall and the increased water scarcity for both rainfed and irrigated crops and climate change. all these points lead to an increase in production costs.
The ultimate goal of the project is to support the livelihoods of small farmers and smallholders, especially by enhancing the quality of agricultural products, which raises the level of income through:
-Reducing production costs.
-Improving the quality of the product and giving it the “clean and renewable” character in their pursuit of producing safety food with achieving some of sustainable development goals.
Project activities have been defined to take into account:
• Intensive practical training for agricultural sector workers (technicians and farmers) on conversion methods to regenerative and clean production.
• Supporting modern regenerative agriculture methods, regenerative cycle planning, co-culture of plants or mixed crops, which leads to ensuring abundant, diverse and early production..
• Supporting local agricultural manufacturing of safe agricultural inputs.
• Supporting farmers through the development of integrated agricultural work ( plants and animals), get more benefits of by-products, and the correct management of recycling agricultural waste.
• Supporting farmers during the project developing with tools and simple equipment's, which needed for applying different techniques of regenerative agriculture.
Implementation of activities
1-Training Program-phase one-: This training aims to build the capacity of agricultural technicians (60 persons-male & female- for three governorates) on regenerative agriculture for establishing farmers groups later. The training includes several topics:
1-1- First: main topics relates to the forming of training of trainers (TOT).
1-2- Second: main topics which relates to the simply techniques of sustainable regenerative agriculture which meet the needs of actual farms in the targeted Syrian rural areas.
1-3- Third stage: related to harvesting, packaging techniques and marketing systems, which come with clean products, to get suitable prices later.
2- Training Program-phase two-: This training aims to build the capacity of targeting farmers (male and female) as a groups: It is conducted by agricultural technicians (trainees in the previous training), who will train farmers practically, and applied on regenerative agriculture techniques, that are applicable on their farms and within the local conditions of each region. by the way, there will be a specific criteria for select the farmers from different gender(M/F), this criteria will be fair and compatible with Local traditions in each region. Then, the new techniques will be initially applied in the experimental field to convince those farmers (in the groups) , and then applied to the rest of the farmers gradually ( 2 groups in each regions, 15-20 farmers in each group). Agricultural technicians will have flexibility regarding the content of the training, and the possibility of making modifications according to the needs, that arise when working on farms.
