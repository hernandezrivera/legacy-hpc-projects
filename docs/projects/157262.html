Project Summary

Mine risk education(MRE) and victim assistance(VA) will be implemented through partner in Northwestern Syria.
MRE sessions will be conducted to IDPs and host community members by trained facilitators by visiting target areas. 13,900 Syrian including IDPs and host community members will receive MRE sessions and the risk of explosive devices will be mitigated.
VA will be provided through partner to persons with disabilities by mobile teams consisting of Physiotherapist, nurse and psychosocial worker. 210 persons with disabilities including survivors of explosive hazard accidents will receive VA services, such as rehabilitation and assistive devices, to improve the quality of their life.

Needs

According to UN findings in 2019, 10.2 million people are at risk of explosive hazards in Syria.(Mine Action Sub Cluster Syria Situation Report, January 2019) Risk Education remains the main priority in the Northwestern Syria due to elevated levels of explosive conflict incidents particularly in populated areas. (Mine Action Sub Cluster, Syria Situation Report, January 2019)Abandoned or unexploded ordnance, including air-dropped munitions and other explosive remnants of war have been left in populated areas, like schools, hospitals, farm lands and main roads in the districts of Al-Ma’ra, Jabal Saman, Idleb and Jisr-Ash-Shugour. Based on our implemented projects in Northwestern Syria, we found out that farming, construction, herding, moving, tampering or playing with items, and rubble removal are the activities most exposing threat. Men and boys are most directly at risk from the immediate threat of explosive hazards, while women and girls are more impacted by indirect threat and long-term consequences of contamination. On the other hands, based on available data, an average of 1.5 people are killed and 2 are injured in each reported explosive accident, with approximately 1 in 3 survivors suffering at least one limb amputation.(Mine Action HRP Inputs). The growing number of casualties from explosive accidents has steadily increased pressure, in particular, on the provision of specialized services and assistance for persons with disabilities, including survivors of explosive hazard accidents. Victim assistance services are limited, as these remain underfunded in comparison with the scale of need in Northwestern Syria.According to our collected data during previous project in Northwestern Syria, more than 90% of persons with disabilities (PWDs) are war injured, 88% are male and 12% are female, including 11% children.

Implementation of activities

Risk Education (RE) sessions will be implemented for the at risk groups through partner’s mobile teams in northwestern Syria, where it is highly contaminated with explosive hazard. The RE sessions will be tailored with age, gender, occupation and localized threats. KAPB(Knowledge, Attitude, Practice and Behaviour) will be conducted before sessions, immediately after and after 3 months.Victim assistance services, such as rehabilitation and providing assistive devices, will be conducted through partner’s mobile teams for people with disabilities including survivors of explosive hazard incidents. Field teams consisting of Physiotherapists, Nurses and Psychosocial workers will conduct need assessment for each case before providing services. PDM (Post Distribution Monitoring) will be conducted to the beneficiaries who receive assistive devices and Satisfaction Survey will be conducted to the beneficiaries who receive sessions. M&E Assistants will be deployed in the field on behalf AAR Japan and monitor the quality of delivered services both RE and VA on weekly basis.

Protection Risk Analysis

Familiarization to explosive devices may cause beneficiaries to become overconfident or complacent to the treat of explosive hazards.One of the mitigation measures is emphasize on "stop, warn and report" message while conducting RE sessions. Also, conduct KAPB survey to monitor changes in risky behaviour, as well as qualitative evaluations to understand the issues underpinning risk behaviour. Local population may become reliant on victim assistance and medical capacity provided by NGO.Mitigation measure is to coordinate with other development organizations to ensure the establishment of local medial capacities.
