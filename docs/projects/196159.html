Project Summary

Bonyan built its educational projects and plans in a way that effectively contributes to reducing the educational gap in Syria and reaching the largest number of children of school age, and in a manner that complies with the objectives of both the HRP and ED Cluster by improving methods and procedures to reach a suitable educational environment for students.
The goal of the Bunyan project will be to return school-age students who have dropped out of education to formal education seats, through various activities that ensure a good quality of education for these students, with a focus on children with special needs who face greater problems than other students, activities will be implemented and project interventions in coordination with local authorities from the Directorates of Education in Aleppo and Idleb, where 18,300 individuals will be targeted for the 2023 education program in which ( 11300 children are in classrooms [ 5865 G / 5435 B, 3-17 years - 550 CwD] and 1,000 teacher and educational personnel )
The project will 28 centers ,2 ECCE centers , 4 Formal schools ,20 NF learning centers, 2 CwDs centers.
** Obj.1: Scale up safe and equitable access to formal and non-formal education for crisis-affected children aged 3-17 years
This Obj will be achieved through a set of integrated activities, including back-to-school campaigns, which will encourage children and motivate them to study, and target parents with the importance and necessity of education for their children. In these campaigns, the project and its activities will also be introduced as providing operational costs for schools and educational centers, protection services, and the distribution of stationery bags. For teachers and students, which represents the biggest problem for parents in the costs of their children’s education, in addition to rehabilitation activities such as repairing doors and windows, painting, and maintaining toilets and water blocks, especially in a way that suits CWD
The project will target a school and a child, divided into ECCE targeting a child (male, female) and primary and preparatory formal education targeting a child (male, female)\
As for non-formal education, children (males and females) will be targeted within temporary education centers in the camps, starting with the ASER pretest, arithmetic activities, therapy, PSS, and referral.
The needs of students and schools will be considered in the winter season, as heaters and diesel fuel will be provided for students within the classrooms, in addition to providing incentives for teachers and administrative staff, for formal and informal education, in addition to awareness campaigns about Covid-9 and Cholera, which there is fear of its spread in the north Syria now,
Obj.2: Enhance the quality of formal and non-formal education for children and youth aged 3-17 years within a protective environment
Within this Obj, Bonyan will work on building the capacities of teachers and the educational and administrative staff, through various and qualitative trainings, such as different teaching styles, classroom management, PSS, PSEA etc.
In addition to securing incentives for teachers and the administrative and educational staff, as well as stationery sets and educational materials necessary to clarify and communicate information to students.
Training will be provided for 1000 teachers and educational personnel (480 males and 520 females). where Bonyan will make use of the extensive and qualitative experience made in the field of training

3 - Needs

The statistics according to HNO show that there are 6.6M people who need assistance in the education sector, including 6.4 million children, At the national level, the data reveals that 18 percent are out of school and half of the children who are not in school have never enrolled, Rates of non-attendance were, Idleb (28%), Aleppo (26%), Over a quarter of households with school-aged children indicated that their children are not in school because there is no school to send their children to. In 1,017 assessed campsites in the northwest, there are only 196 schools. The most dominant form of learning (95 percent) is in-person and formal with less than two percent of children attending non-formal education services that bring children back to learning and address learning loss. In November 2022, Bonyan organization conducted a needs assessment targeting 5 districts in Aleppo and Idlib including 525 schools, indicating the following: 22 of the schools included in the study ) stopped working due to lack of support, educational staff, and bombing As for the remaining 503 schools, they are not enough for the number of targeted children because of the waves of displacement that led to an increase in the population compared to the population in these areas before the waves of displacement.As for the basic necessary needs to support the educational process, it is represented in stationery for students, in addition to incentives for teachers

4 - Implementation of activities

The project will begin with minor rehabilitation of schools and education centers, such as replacing doors, windows, whiteboards, and fixing walls, based on a technical assessment. BTL activities will be conducted to raise awareness about the importance of education and gain local support for schools and education projects. These activities will target 6,000 individuals, including children, parents, and teachers, and will lead to increased enrollment in available education services. ECE will be provided in 2 centers targeting 200 children. The project will link formal and non-formal education by providing support to 4 formal schools and 22 non-formal learning centers, targeting a total of 11,300 children in 2022 and providing incentives for 460 teachers and capacity building for 1,000 educational personnel. The project will also focus on inclusion for children with disabilities by increasing access through rehabilitation, dedicating inclusion officers, and raising awareness about the needs of CWDs. Sterilization campaigns will be carried out to protect against Covid-19 and Cholera, and PTA meetings, caregiver engagement, heating, and stationary will also be provided. All education centers will have access to UN-supported PSEA reporting channels, and a monitoring plan will be developed to follow up the activity, collect and analyze data, conduct field visits, and submit periodic reports.
