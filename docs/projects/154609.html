Needs

Essian camp is home to 16,000 IDPs . A rolling average for the last quarter has AMAR’s PHCC in Essian delivered 3600 services.. This demonstrates the serious need for continued primary medical care in the camp. AMAR continues to see particularly high rates of infectious diseases, including scabies, conjunctivitis, diarrhoea, bronchitis and other respiratory and skin diseases, as well as injuries and chronic conditions.

Activities

AMAR’s PHCC in Essayan provides doctor consultations for disease and injury, maternal health care, child health care, vaccinations (for UNICEF), dental care, laboratory tests and ultrasound scans for diagnosis. AMAR also provides preventative health education through a Women’s Health Volunteer programme. AMAR will continue to support the PHCCs for as long there is an IDP need. As and when IDP return to their homes in and around Mt Sinjar, AMAR will reduce and if necessary close the clinic and return any unused funds, on a monthly pro-rated basis.

Protection Risk Analysis

AMAR uses local or host population medical staff and support staff to deliver medical services.Many (majority/exclusively in Essayan) of the beneficiaries are Yazidi, an especially vulnerable minority, including vulnerable women and girls.

Mitigation Measures

AMAR staff were either from the IDP camp itself or the local area.

How is your project accountable to affected population

There is an IDP Call centre, RPAs, monitoring missions, CwC and RNAs among other mechanisms.
