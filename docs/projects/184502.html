Project Summary

FSNAU and FEWS NET Post-Gu Assessment in September 2021 projected that nearly 3.5 million Somalis would be in Crisis (IPC Phase 3) and Emergency (IPC Phase 4) through at least December 2021, in the absence of humanitarian assistance.That includes 1.1 million internally displaced persons who will face moderate to large food consumption gaps through late 2021.324.Out of the total 3.5 million people in need of urgent food assistance, 640,000 people face significant food consumption gaps.325 In addition, 3.7 million people are classified in IPC phase 2 (Stressed), engaging in negative coping strategies to meet their food and non-food needs, bringing the total number of Somalis experiencing acute food insecurity to 7.2 million by late 2021.
The proposed project seeks to restore and improve immediate food access among drought affected communities in Galgaduud,Lower Shabelle, Bay region in Somalia. The objective of this project is to meet the immediate food and other needs of the most vulnerable households through unconditional cash transfer. The unconditional cash transfers will target households in IPC 4 and 3 areas in the above regions.IRDO will use the Community Based Targeting Guidelines for Somalia in beneficiary targeting and selection. A community engagement process will be undertaken in each district before beneficiary selection is done.
Distribute Food Vouchers to targeted eligible beneficiaries in Lower Juba Region,Bay, Galgaduud and Lower Shabelle. Conditional and Non Conditional food vouchers will be provided on a monthly basis to 16,600 targeted beneficiaries in Lower Juba for a period of 6 months; unconditional vouchers will be supplied to 10%of the vulnerable targeted caseload/HHs. This assistance will address acute food needs by bolstering the purchasing power of targeted households through the projected lean season. IRDO will involve the community benefiting from the VFW programme in the design and, where possible, administration of the project activities. This includes planning the work to be undertaken, selecting participants, determining wage levels (slightly below market wages), keeping records, supervising works and monitoring. This approach helps to ensure that the community feels ownership for the programme itself and for the assets created by it. Rehabilitation of community assets such as water pans shallow wells ,access roads and irrigation canals through communal labour activities. People Living with disabilities will be given priority including those female headed Households
Provide technical support to targeted agro pastoralist through agronomists in Galgaduud,Bay Lower Juba and Lower Shabelle. The project shall target 50 productive groups in Afmadow(each group made of 20-25people) who are able to participate in on-farm related enterprises. Each group shall be trained in modern agronomic practices and supported with farm inputs (garden tools, seeds). During the selection process women headed household will be given more priority, this will also include people living with disabilities. IRDO will establish a complaint and feedback mechanism that will be used by the beneficiaries to report on any issues regarding the project. The identification of beneficiaries will pay particular attention to gender-related vulnerabilities and where possible, female will represent at least 60% of the beneficiaries. Age, gender, and diversity will be mainstreamed in the identification and registration process, to ensure that women, youth and miniority groups are identified and targeted, and that there is evidence that they benefit from this intervention. Accountability to affected populations in this project is anchored around IASC endorsed via global protection cluster, protection mainstreaming four principles are as follows; Prioritize safety and dignity and avoid causing harm, Meaningful access, Accountability, Participation, and empowerment.

2.1.1 - What is the justification for using cash or vouchers instead of in kind?

Flexibility and choice: There is no such thing as a one-size-fits-all solution! The cash or vouchers recognizes that beneficiaries are best positioned to determine what their households require, and allows them to make choices based on their unique needs and preferences. In the context of the operational environment IRDO prefers using the CTP as compared to in kind because of its flexibility.The second aspect to why IRDO will use CTP instead of in kind is because of empowerment and building a sense of responsiveness among many of the vulnerable groups of IDPs and at risk targeted by the projects. IRDO believes that the presence of cash offers households a sense of regaining control over their immediate circumstances. Furthermore, there is evidence that getting income helps empower women in the home, at least briefly. Longer-term, access to financial structures such as bank accounts may empower beneficiaries, particularly women.Subsequently, IRDO is making a deliberate preference of CTP to in kind because Cash-based interventions may benefit more than the immediate users of cash since they stimulate local markets. Households with money to spend may help local traders and service providers, as well as transportation employees, suppliers, and goods transporters especially in the IDP settlements.Last but not least, the adoption of CTP over in kind by IRDO is to facilitate reduction of negative coping strategies among many of the vulnerable IDPS targeted by the initiative. There is also evidence that in today's world, most people rely on currency to some degree. When aid is offered in kind, families are frequently obliged to seek funds in ways that may be detrimental to their long-term lives, such as selling productive assets or entrusting their children to relatives. This case is not different in the operational environment where IRDO is working especially with IDP populations. Cash transfers have been shown to lessen beneficiaries' usage of negative coping techniques.

2.1.2 - Explain how the project design considered market functionality?

The project design took into account the availability of local contractors and supplies on the ground, particularly within and around the IDP settlements where IRDO intends to conduct the project. Similarly, prior to the interventions, a baseline survey (pre) will be conducted, and the market functionality will be monitored during the project's implementation.In addition, IRDO will continue to collaborate with other partners to monitor market functionality (for a variety of commodities) and ensure that the assistance provided to beneficiaries does not endanger the IDPS's already vulnerable and at-risk people. In light of this, unrestricted CTP will be implemented to provide beneficiaries the freedom to choose what and how they wish to spend their cash or vouchers, as well as access anything of value that is legitimately useful to their requirements.

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

IRDO will ensure that all the beneficiaries are involved regardless of clan affiliation, the beneficiaries from the minority clans will be consulted and actively involved with the project activities. IRDO will ensure that women and girls are protected against sexual exploitation a help desk will be put in place whereby beneficiaries will be required to report any cases of sexual exploitation. IRDO will also ensure that the food distribution points are located in secure areas to avoid the risk of violence.
