Project Summary

UNHCR will work through a community-based approach. The project has two components: 1) community empowerment, to ensure that the community structures are able to support their own communities and take part in the decisions that affect their lives; 2) community outreach, through a two-way communication and community complaints and feedback mechanisms

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

In coordination with CCCM cluster, UNHCR plans to contribute to the enhancement of self-governance of displaced population through engagement and participation, and support access to services and provision of information for displaced populations in sites and sites-like settings in Cabo Delgado, Nampula and Niassa Provinces. 2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

1) Community empowermentIn coordination with the CCCM Cluster, UNHCR will support community self-management structures in collective sites in Cabo Delgado, Nampula and Niassa, providing trainings (on protection, human rights, risk awareness, humanitarian assistance criteria, and referral pathways) and material support, so they can support their own communities and organize community activities. UNHCR will engage community self-management structures in discussions on community problems and durable solutions (i.e. local integration, return and relocation). UNHCR will also support communities with community-led initiatives for IDPs and host communities aiming at responding to common protection risks identified and reducing tensions. 2) Community outreachUNHCR will enhance the community outreach by supporting the communities to strengthen a two-way communication system for the exchange of information and will set up a community complaints and feedback mechanism in prioritized communities that could be easily accessible by the most affected people. UNHCR community complaints and feedback mechanism will be in complementarity with other AAP mechanisms already in place such as Linha Verde, CFMs and CBCMs ran in CCCM implementation. UNHCR will consult with the communities about the appropriate communication methods to receive the complaints. UNHCR will also establish protection/information desks to ensure that communities and their members are informed on the different assistance programmes, service mapping, referral pathways and other issues that concern them and have access to timely, transparent, accurate and relevant information, through various accessible, inclusive and culturally appropriate communication channels. UNHCR will consider different communication methods such as direct communication, (individually at the protection desks, home visits or during group information sessions), indirectly (trough community structures who disseminate key messages) and remotely (through hotlines, WhatsApp or text messages).

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

UNHCR staff and its partners are trained on humanitarian principles and the key elements of the centrality of protection: do not harm principle, safety and dignity, effective access to services and assistance, accountability with affected populations and community participation and empowerment. UNHCR has developed a protection monitoring tool for identifying risk profiles and collecting protection incidents, with indicators of age, gender and diversity. In the face of any immediate threat to security that is identified, an emergency protection or assistance service is activated. Protection interventions focus on saving lives and guaranteeing the rights to safety and physical integrity of affected populations. In addition, in order to guarantee the dignity of people, it is planned to provide timely and quality services considering the background of the individuals, the respect for their culture and religion and the survivor-centre approach, respecting their freedom of choice

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

UNHCR considers the affected communities as the center of its operation and UNHCR conducts frequent consultations with members of community structures to involve them in the design, implementation and monitoring of community activities. The main pillar of UNHCR country strategy is the community-based protection which includes a section on community engagement and communication with communities. In 2021, UNHCR will set up community complaints and feedback mechanism to allow people affected by the crisis to have access to information and participate in decision-making on projects developed by UNHCR and its partners. The communication with communities also considers the expansion of the mobile protection desks and hotlines to enhance the community outreach.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

UNHCR undertakes consultations and involvement of persons with disabilities in programming and decision-making on issue that affect their lives. UNHCR also conducts specific consultations with persons with disabilities in order to assess their specific protection issues and provide personalized assistance and material support. UNHCR ensures that persons with disabilities have access to all relevant information by preparing key messages in multiple and accessible formats. For example: for visually impaired, verbal communication or Braille, announcements, writings with large characters and colour contrast; for hearing impaired, written materials, symbols and sign language; and persons with low literacy or learning difficulties, easy-to-understand language and drawings. Community meetings will be located in places which are accessible to persons with disabilities.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

UNHCR take environmental impact into account in all its operations, given that the environmental damage left by displaced populations could be a major concern. As part of its community-based protection strategy, UNHCR ensures that the communities are sensitized and engaged on the protection of the environment, emphasizing their responsibility for the local environment where they settle. As part of the community initiatives that will be supported in 2021, UNHCR will consider joint eco-friendly projects with IDPs and host communities
