Project Summary

A. Needs:
The ongoing conflict in Syria, soon entering its fourteenth year, has caused immense destruction and unimaginable losses to the Syrian people and all aspects of their lives. Protection indicators have tragically declined due to the war, the February 2023 earthquake, and successive crises. Various segments of society face violence, displacement, ignorance, illiteracy, and lack of access to basic human rights.
The region of Sanjar and Abul Dohur and both countryside has been significantly affected by the prolonged conflict, displacement, and the February 2023 earthquake. The area is influenced by customs and traditions that involve discrimination, persecution, and marginalization of certain groups under different labels. In addition to economic crises, there are other social issues such as the absence of safe spaces for women, domestic violence, early and forced marriages, high divorce rates, irresponsible cousin marriages, all negatively impacting rural family life. High rates of disability are recorded among some children and youth due to the lack of care programs for people with disabilities.
B. Intervention, Implementation of Activities (Sub-activities for the Main Activity):
Therefore, we propose a Community impowrement project to protect individuals in Sanjar and Abul Dohur and both countryside. It begins with focused discussion groups to study protection obstacles in area , group and individual consultations, and awareness sessions on protection services. We aim to collaborate with official bodies and the local community to enhance protection activities and psychosocial support with the goal of expanding their reach. Case management will be activated along with group and individual sessions, workshops, designing mechanisms and executive procedures, and mobile teams to monitor protection indicators, identify cases, and refer them. Assistance offices will also be established to enhance the spread of protection services, in addition to training non-protection-related humanitarian actors to generalize protection in a broader sense.
Financial assistance will be provided to 100 nenificiaries with disabilities through transfers delivered to them to support and empower them.
C. Protection Risk Analysis:
The influence of religious heritage on legal matters in marriages, and lineage affairs, along with the control of customs and traditions, has a high impact. To mitigate this, intensifying awareness campaigns and training key individuals to spread legal awareness and facilitate legal procedures through lawyers is essential.
The average impact of the community's weak interest in the center can be mitigated by conducting an intensive awareness campaign about the center before its opening and before each session or training.
The average impact of the difficulty for beneficiaries to reach the center can be mitigated by securing a mobile team capable of reaching families within the countryside.
D. Protection Strategy from Exploitation and Sexual Violation:
Al-Birr and Social Services Association in Idleb adopts a code of conduct and protection policy that all employees and volunteers must adhere to. It strictly prohibits any action or behavior indicating exploitation, harassment, or sexual violation and does not tolerate any behavior that may compromise the dignity or privacy of beneficiaries. The association has assistance and protection offices that receive complaints according to confidential procedures ensuring the safety and security of complainants while providing training on protection policies and procedures for individuals and beneficiaries as needed.
D. Risks, Assumptions, and Mitigation Measures:
Fires or disasters have a medium impact which can be mitigated by having fire extinguishers within the center and appointing multiple emergency exit points.
The presence of accompanied children with their families has a medium impact which can be mitigated by having a room equipped with toys specifically for children

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Emergencies, conflicts and disasters affect the functioning of families and communities, which in turn affects levels of protection in general, especially for children, women and people with disabilities. Protection levels have deteriorated in Abu Al-Duhur and its countryside, so the project sets among its objectives raising protection levels in the targeted areas, mainly through psychosocial support interventions. Periodic awareness sessions will be conducted to view the low levels of protection and discuss ways to enhance it through detailed explanations supported by life activities. Activities outline ways to cope with isolation, uncertainty, fear, anger, loss and grief, and outline appropriate mitigation measures to reduce damage to both mental and physical health. Particular importance will be given to confronting gender-based violence by providing theoretical lectures followed by practical training by following interaction mechanisms, activating individual and group participation, establishing concepts of equality, and promoting respect for human rights without regard to gender, religion, or color. Brochures and awareness leaflets will be distributed to the beneficiary summarizing the previous topics.Within its protection policy, the association guarantees the right to accountability for affected populations. This is done through the help desk existing within the project. The office provides beneficiaries with access to safe and confidential complaint mechanisms when they are exposed to any behavior that harms their dignity, mistreatment, or exploitation in all its forms, including sexual exploitation and abuse. The office holds periodic sessions for beneficiaries to explain to them their right to hold employees and volunteers accountable and to submit complaints and their mechanism, and publishes posters about this in the project’s rooms and halls, including contact numbers and e-mails. The association's administration does not tolerate any case or behavior that indicates misconduct or harassment in any form, and it takes disciplinary measures if necessary.Adopting participatory rural assessment in any target area, the association relies on field questionnaires, focused discussion sessions (targeted individuals), periodic interviews, and a review of previous projects to plan the form and type of intervention that matches the needs of the target area. The association also holds workshops involving government officials and local residents with the aim of integrating various efforts for the benefit of individuals. It seeks to support popular initiatives, prepare the ground for collective action, and enable beneficiaries to play an active role in analyzing their circumstances, problems, and potentials in search of realistic and effective solutions to be applied through project activities. Regarding cash assistance, aid will be distributed to 100 beneficiaries with disabilities with the aim of empowering them, 40$ every 2 months.
