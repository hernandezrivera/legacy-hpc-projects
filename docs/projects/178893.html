Project Summary

This project aims to strengthening IDPs communal coping strategies during the winter season by Equipping and training emergency responders and IDP committees in IDP sites about first aid and fire response.
The training will include live demo in collaboration with white helmets to show participants in the training how they can deal with the fire from the beginning to the end including:
1- Fire extinguishing,
2- First aid to the injuries
3- Evacuate the trapped IDPs inside the tents to safer area to avoid being affected with the fire.
Furthermore, under this project will provide fire extinguishers and first aid kits to each camp in order to establish fire point accessible to all IDPs with presence of trained person nearby to respond immediately to any fire incident.
These activities will be implemented before the winter seasons as proactive preparation for the winter season which caused increase fire incidents due to improper use of the stoves and fuel.
Mercy-USA will focus on Maaret Tamsrin camps based on the trend of fire incident reports provided by CCCM cluster in 2020 which highlighted big need for such intervention to mitigate fire risks.
Also, Mercy-USA has plan for winterization project in the same camps, moreover, we have monthly food assistance project which gives Mercy-USA advantage to follow up the training impact on regular basis and provide advices whenever needed, so this will reflect complementarity and integration approach for strong sustainability.
Mercy-USA will target 3500HH, 17,995 individuals (Men 3,606- Women 3,957- Boys 4,856- Girls 5,576) in 50 camps
Prioritization of activities in case of underfunding will be:
1-Conduct one training and cancel the refresher training
2-Provision of fire extinguishers and first aid kits is top priority
3- Reduce place for the fire points to two instead of three in each site
4-Reduce staff cost
So the prioritizing will be scale down activities rather than remove any activity

4 - Needs

Based on the ISIMM figures as of March 2021, the CCCM database includes 1,402 IDP sites that host 1,683,296 IDPs or 316,484 families. Of these, 80% are women and children, and 23,686 IDPs are reported to be persons with specific needs. In January 19 fire incidents were reported while in February 127 incident and in March 202118 incident. In Maaret Tamsrin sub-district 256 IDPs sites registered in which 43,793 HH (241,427 IDPs) are living which makes this area overcrowded with IDPs and high need for multisectoral humanitarian assistance, so people are looking for food, shelter, WASH and health assistances while they ignore the soft components such as fire response, awareness raising, and management quality which complicated the situation once the emergency hits, so provision of these soft components to IDPs will be critical to be proactive rather than reactive and mitigate many risks might arise due to wrong reactions as response to these risks.

5 - Implementation of activities

Mercy-USA will choose 2-3 persons from each camp to participate in the fire response training, then participants will be divided into groups each group 15 persons to ensure adhering with precaution measures for COVID-19 in terms of social distancing. Each group will attend two days training : one day as theoretical about the safety and security, fire response, first aid..etc and the second day will be practical be using live demo explanation and experiments to show them how they can deal with fire incident properly when it takes place. Also, the same training will be repeated as refresher in the middle of the project duration. These trainings will be provided in collaboration with white helmets inside Syria.Mercy-USA has been providing this training since two years along with winterization projects which had great impact in extinguishing many fire incidents without any damage or injuries. Mercy-USA will provide average of 10 fire extinguishers and 10 first aid kits for each IDPs site and this number may increase based on number of tents and IDPs in each camp. fire points will be distributed in three different places inside each IDP site to ensure full coverage and extinguishing any fire in the right time without impact Mercy-USA will have dedicated field monitor to follow up the proper usage of these equipment once needed, moreover, to report any fire incident in the targeted camps on timely basis to CCCM cluster disaggregated by age-gender and disability to ensure information dissemination and respond to need effectively. Mercy-USA will take cross cutting issue in considerations, so female staff will be hired and ensure females' participants in the fire response training
