Project Summary

Ekklesiyar Yan’Uwa A Nigeria (EYN) is a faith-based organization founded in 2005 due to the rising need to provide intervention to vulnerable people especially people living with HIV/Aids. Due to the crises in the North East Nigeria, EYN expanded its scope to other humanitarian interventions which are food security and livelihood, Nutrition, Education, Protection (Gender based violence/Child protection), conflict resolution and peace building in Borno, Adamawa and Yobe state. EYN Projects has staff strength of 41 officers, 62 supervisors, 204 field assistants and 1,203 community mobilisers.
This funding proposal is intended for Askira/UBA LGA in southern Borno and Magumeri LGA in northern Borno where a combined total of 110,635 women, girls, men and boys are in need of quality lifesaving and enhanced wellbeing through Gender-based violence prevention and response intervention.
Askira/UBA LGA and Magumeri LGA have limited access to GBV services and limited awareness of GBV as a humanitarian assistance based on several assessments conducted which indicated negative coping mechanisms and sexual abuse and exploitations (SEA), including in temporary IDP sites in Mutukum and Lassa of Askira/Uba LGA, four (4) IDP camps in Magumeri.
The GBV Sub-sector have noted the specific targeting of women and girl’s severity as extreme to catastrophic due to the on-going violence they experience under Boko Haram. Men and boys in these locations are also in dire need for specialized services to enhance their coping mechanism and household empowerment.
There has been minimal effort so far to identify and address women and girls’, boys and men’s needs, much less target them as priority beneficiaries for any programming. The services we aim to provide in these LGA’s will address the GBV humanitarian intervention that is urgently needed to address effects of serious human rights violations in particular for individuals who have witnessed murder, subjected to forced abductions, sexual abuse, forced marriages, forced religious conversions and also participation in insurgency activities.
This service will also address the needs of new arrivals in formal and informal IDP camps and host community of Askira and Lassa wards.
The activities we aim to implement in this project includes the establishment of women and girl’s safe spaces (WGSS), men and boys’ spaces, case management for GBV survivors, Mental health psychosocial support, clinical management of rape cases, engagement of boys and men for positive resilience, skills training and referrals to other service providers.
We intend to implement Adolescent girls programming for girls of 10 – 14 years and 15 – 18 Years in the two locations. This we have successfully implemented in Askira, Chibok and MMC. Another component of this project will be the economic and social empowerment (EASE) for women and girls. This will build economic resilience of the women through the village savings and loans (VSLA) scheme and also bring together household financial decision making advocacy through the discussion group series activity for husbands and wives.
Our project plan will also involve community education on GBV to community leaders, religious leaders, youths, women leaders, men and boys in these communities with the aim of establishing a referral framework for GBV cases.
For this project, we are targeting 1,400 women and girls, 400 boys and men in each location. A total of 3,600 women, girls, men and boys will benefit from this project. We aim to educate 400 men, youths and community leaders on our GBV program.
EYN is a faith based organization and our core principle is equality for all without discrimination of sex, gender or religion. Our staff strength consists of well-trained GBV case workers, GBV community engagement officers, GBV officers, Monitoring and Evaluations Officers and GBV community outreach workers.

1 - Needs

Current assessment conducted by the GBV Sub-Sector for the humanitarian response program 2020 for ASKIRA/UBA LGA of Borno state puts its severity score at EXTREME with about 16,366 girls, 11,904 women, 7,593 boys and 2,215 men in need of wellbeing, living standard and coping mechanism humanitarian response.EYN projects has established a safe space for women and girls in Zadawa ward of Askira since September 2017 to date and funding coming to an end December 31st 2019. We have successfully reached out to a total of 1,319 women and girls in this community for improved access to quality lifesaving and GBV response and enhanced wellbeing to survivors and vulnerable individuals through survivor centered service. We have so far strengthened community resilience by engaging 76 men and boys in the year 2019.Our funding request will enable us continue our program in our established safe space in Jadawa ward of Askira and also open a new safe space for women and girls in a community called LASSA upon our conducted assessment with community and religious leaders of Askira/Uba LGA.Lassa is a community located between Askira and Uba. It has been affected six (6) times by insurgency where the community were displaced up until recently in April 2019. The inhabitants of six (6) surrounding villages have moved into the federal technical college Lassa as IDPs. There are currently 3,600 women and girls in the host community, which has a population of about 9,100 people. There are about 510 women and girls who are currently living in the informal IDP camp with a population of about 1,800 IDPs. This community has been ignored because of its location and in need of humanitarian assistance with Gender – based violence reports on the high side. We propose to establish our presence in this community through setting up a women and girls friendly space (WGFS) and provide GBV prevention and response.New location EYN projects is proposing to intervene is MAGUMERI LGA of northern Borno where the assessment conducted by the GBV Sub-sector for humanitarian response program 2020 puts its severity score at catastrophic with about 30,694 girls, 23,921 women, 12,663 boys and 10,098 men in need of wellbeing, living standard and coping mechanism. There is currently minimal service providers offering GBV services in this location and EYN projects is proposing to establish its presence with safe spaces for women, girls, boys and men in the four (4) IDP camps located in this locations.
