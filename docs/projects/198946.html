Project Summary

The aim of the project is to provide a emergency shelter and NFI assistance to the population most vulnerable to the crisis in the Province of Cabo Delgado.
The project will provide assistance in form of emergency shelter construction and shelter repairs for resettled families in Chiure and Mecufi. In the repair component, Caritas will renovate the roof of existing emergency shelters, mainly for the most vulnerable people (elderly people, widows, single women and minor children heads of households). It is expected that this kit will be accompanied by tools, and NFIs including kitchen set, solar lamps, blankets, sleeping mats, mosquito nets With this, Caritas intends to assist 7,500 people, including men, women, children and people with disabilities.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

During the project female headed households, elderly and child headed households will be amongst the prioritized vulnerable individuals. The protection of women and other vulnerable groups will be also ensured through active presence of the Caritas Diocesana field monitors at distribution sites and during post-distribution monitoring, where feasible as well the follow up of project progress by Caritas. It should be noted that in addition to the active presence of protection technicians, other protection mechanisms will be used, such as: mechanism for reporting cases of violence (Green line, complaint box, posters that demonstrate harmful practices, extortion, abuses in the community and all PSEA practices). With the implementation of these income-generating activities, it is expected that a large part of the beneficiaries, mainly families headed by women, will be able to improve their living conditions and reduce the level of dependency to which they are exposed. In addition, sensitization of cooperating partners on gender-based violence and prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse will contribute to triggering prompt action where cases of abuse are reported by the beneficiaries, local leaders and Caritas teams.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

As it is considered an inclusive project, people in need will be the focus of Caritas, including the elderly to identify specific needs for shelter. The infrastructure will be adapted to these needs. One of the selection criteria is the people with disabilities. Distribution points will be serviced, ensuring ease of access for community members, especially people with disabilities and women. If there are any people with disabilities and if they have some leadership character and ability to do some work, such as registering, organizing beneficiaries, writing reports, they can be included in the field team. 7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The focus of the project is the construction and repair of shelters, which aims to deliver tarpaulins, construction material, in short, the entire shelter kit, depending on the situation of vulnerability. Field teams will be trained to explain to target communities how to keep the environment clean, avoiding littering in inappropriate places, as well as promoting the use of latrines, the use of local material such as timber/wood or bamboo and promoting debates to ensure the well-being of society as a whole.
