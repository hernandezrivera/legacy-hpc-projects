Project Summary

In 2021, FHI 360 commenced implementation of the BHA-funded IRAMP project in Ancuabe and Metuge Districts, providing integrated emergency health, protection, and WASH assistance. In 2023, FHI 360 will continue its lifesaving assistance, while also expanding comprehensive support in the target areas by integrating emergency nutrition interventions—an area that has been identified through the Health, WASH and Nutrition Cluster, as well as consultation with other partners, as an area of deep need and significant gaps. FHI 360 will extend its assistance for an additional 11 months (making the total award period 21 months). The modification will build upon FHI 360’s well-established operational presence and platform, its strong existing team on the ground, and positive relationships with local authorities, and community members in its target areas.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

FHI 360’s integrated package of WASH services has three primary objectives: 1) meeting the immediate needs of IDPs in Ancuabe and Metuge, and host communities in the aforementioned geographic areas and 2) restoring conflict-disrupted regular WASH services in the southern districts of Cabo Delgado, and 3) Preventing potential cholera outbreaks and adverse natural disasters such as cyclones in the communities that FHI 360 serves. Number of People Targeted: 32,400 (19,440 female, 12,960 male) (modification)• Number of IDPs Targeted: 18,000 (modification)• Number of Refugees Targeted: NoneBeneficiaries are host community members and IDPs living with host families or resettlement sites in the target geographies.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Districts- Ancuabe and Metuge Priority activities: Hygiene Promotion, FHI 360 will also distribute essential WASH NFIs primarily to IDPs (distribution and consumables replenishment).To support cholera and other outbreak preparedness, the WASH team will develop a list of contingency stocks to be used/handled by trained RRTs, including Ceterza, bathing soap, laundry soap and jerry cans to be distributed (in the event of an outbreak). Environmental Health: FHI 360’s environmental health component has primarily focused on solid waste management and drainage around six IDP resettlement sites within Ancuabe and Metuge DistrictsSanitation: Installation of latrines, bathing facilities, and handwashing points; Provide latrine cleaning tools and other maintenance consumables. Water Supply: water trucking, water system repair, Revitalize, train, and equip water user committees, Implement water safety plan

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

To minimize unintended impacts of proposed WASH interventions, FHI 360 will conduct at the outset of program a Do No Harm assessment in each IDP sheltering sites that will be embedded into the baseline survey. To that end, FGDs will be conducted with different demographic and socio-economic groups. Additionally, further analysis of stakeholders and how they relate to specific activities will be conducted. In terms of ensuring safety, dignity, and privacy, FHI 360 will adopt service standards that will fit the local context. For instance, the inclusion of toilets that will enable women and other vulnerable groups the ability to make use of services safely, with privacy, and without shame.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

FHI 360 will promote empowerment and participation through organizing IDP sheltering site community-based institutions and community-based management. FHI 360 will conduct a series of community consultations on where and how their water delivery points and latrines should be located and oriented

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The proposed interventions are delivered at multiple service delivery platforms, including at the health institution, village, and household levels. Where feasible and appropriate, FHI 360 will use inclusive designs to improve access to services for the disabled. The WASH component will also address other aspects of access, such as designating gender-separated access to sanitation, particularly at the IDP sites. At the community level, communities getting trucked water will be made to have a voice in deciding where the offloading points should be.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

FHI 360 highly considered the environmental impact of the project to ensure all activities possessed either no or very minimal negative environmental impact by prioritizing the sustainability and ability of communities to adapt and build upon the introduced services. As such, FHI 360 incorporated the following environmentally conscious activities: designed activities to mitigate spillover of infection diseases, proper disposal of clinical waste, demarcation of solid waste management pits, ensuring minimum required distances from water sources for toilet installations, and conducting environmental assessments before handing out hygiene kits to ensure IDPs also have safe spaces to use latrines and dispose of solid waste. In the context of Covid-19, all program activities were implemented in compliance with the GoM MoH guidelines and protocols. This was to ensure the safety and well-being of staff, volunteers, participants, and community members. FHI 360 continued to promote evidence-based COVID-19 prevention and control measures that include hand hygiene, the use of face masks, vaccination, and physical distancing, among others. Waste generated from the use of PPEs by program staff was disposed of at designated waste management points and incinerated.
