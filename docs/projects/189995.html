Project Summary

ORDA Ethiopia is a registered Ethiopian Residents Charity organization and it has been operational in the Amhara Region since February 1984. Its head office is located in Bahir Dar, Amhara region, Ethiopia. During the year 2021 ORDA has been implementing various projects in different 10 zones and 86Woredas of Amhara regional state, Ethiopia. It has been getting support (mainly finance) from 21 donors (USAID/CRS, USAID/WV, CBM Italy, CBM Canada, CBM Germany….It is actively engaged in development work aimed to contribute to the overall efforts of ensuring food, livelihood, and environmental security that realize sustainable development and social transformation in the Amhara region by implementing mandated projects.
According to the regional disaster prevention and food security office report still, 11.3 million people in the region need urgent humanitarian support.
Similarly, Returnees in North Shewa, North Wollo, North Gonder, South Wollo, and Waghimera do have not enough Agricultural input (seed, farming tools, livestock, and vegetables) to accommodate the recent agricultural and sustain their life because most of them were damaged during the war. Moreover, Water supply facilities are inadequate for a large number of IDPs which posed health risks, and for this purpose, it is required to build and rehabilitate potable water supply, latrine, and waste disposal as well.
This plan is aimed to support the conflict-affected households through the provision of agricultural input, IGA, Draught power support, animal health, and animal feed support.
ORDA selects to work in North Wollo and Wag Himra Zones and 12 woredas in these zones. The selection criteria are based on the severity of the woredas and ORDA presence in these woredas. The total number of beneficiary people in this woreda is 789,200 people. And the total number of households is 157,840.
ORDA Ethiopia has been integrating the protection issues, in the organizations in humanitarian response and development programs in design, implementation, monitoring, and evaluation.
During the project implementation, we have targeted the most vulnerable groups to enhance safety, and dignity and address causes of discrimination, abuse violence, neglect, and exploitation. As well as Promote and protect the human rights of the beneficiaries and our actions do not contribute to or perpetuate discrimination, abuse, violence, neglect, and exploitation. At the begging of the project implementation, we conduct gender and protection analysis to know the protection situation and the security issue of the area. Therefore the protection analysis has been done on some projects in the organization and the finding report has been shared for donors and Business development and research program in the organization to use an input for project design.
Based on the finding, we implement some common activities at the organization, partner, and community level to mainstream the protection in the program and project. The activities are being done, providing protection training for staff and government partners to establish and strengthen the referral pathway to provide one-stop service for GBV (Gender-Based Violence) victims on the side of the government. In addition to that, community-based protection activities are vital to assure community protection. Therefore establish /strengthen a community protection committee, providing protection training for community members at all levels and Information Education Commination materials (IEC) materials on service mapping, referral pathways, sensitization on protection, including GBV, and gender issues being done in the organization.
Similarly ORDA has its own environmental compliance mechanism , in which all activities will not have a negative impact on the environment, Since the activities in this project do not have any negative impact on the environment.

1. - Projects activities

The major project activities are Agricultural input supply, Provision of Animal Feed, Support Animal health in the selected woredas, and Income generating activities. Agricultural input support Due to the absence of timely plowing, sowing, weeding, protection from pests,s, and harvesting during the Meher season and the missing of main irrigation time as a result of restricted movement during the conflict. Creates production loss this production loss leads to a significant shortage of food and seed for the coming Belg and Meher season production. To alleviate the early recovery of the conflict-affected households will get different types of crop and vegetable seeds as a one-time measure. ORDA will provide essential inputs for agricultural recovery to vulnerable households to help them to produce teff, wheat, barley maize, sorghum, vegetable, and other important crops. The twelve conflict-affected woredas have diversified agroecology (lowland, mid-highland, and Highland) which are convenient for the proposed seed type. And the seed inputs will be verified by the woredas office of agriculture.The seed will be provided for 259044 very poor conflict-affected population who has no seed reserve from their harvest/damaged/taken by the conflict. Seeds for Belg season will be provided as early as February/May 2022, and those for Meher season will be provided in June 2022. Fodder seed provisionLivestock feed in the target Woredas is limited in quantity and of poor quality. Hence the project will complement it with an improved type of fodder seeds. Thus, fodder seeds will be provided for 83327 beneficiaries who have cattle. Each target beneficiary will receive 5kg of fodder seeds. The type of fodder seeds will be identified with respective Woredas livestock offices.Animal healthVaccination and dewormingTo improve the health status and performance of livestock, 374488 population cattle will be vaccinated (for known diseases in the area) and will be dewormed. IGAIncome-generating activities 46857 returnees and youths who have no land and the households who have experience in IGA. Seed money will be provided as initial capital for the IGA activities which they select. Support draught power Most households lost their cattle during the conflict. For this reason, timely plowing is difficult. To alleviate this problem the project arranges rent of oxen for 25484 poor families.
