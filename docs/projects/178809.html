Project Summary

The project targets 25 schools and learning centers in Idleb & Aleppo:
-All targeted sub-districts are considered high severity.
-The Cost per student is about (100 USD).
-The project targets 23,253 beneficiaries about 2 % of them are disabled.
-During 2020, GAN received about (5 M USD) in general, (1.650 K USD for education).
-So far in 2021, GAN has secured about (0.6 M USD). War Child Holland and THF.
-GAN was able to reach a total number of beneficiaries (120 K) in different sectors, about
(20 K) beneficiaries in Education.
This project aims to address the following education needs which are considered as gaps in 18 of the targeted areas:
-Provision of formal and non-formal education for children.
-Rehabilitation / establishment of classrooms / TLCs in camps.
-Provision of stationary and learning materials for teachers, schools and students in
formal and non-formal settings.
-Provision of operational and running costs including fuel for heating.
-Training of teachers in emergencies
-Teaching essential life-saving skills and support provision of information on GBV and
other forms of violence, anti-trafficking, unexploded ordnance, diseases, nutrition, health,
hygiene and psychosocial health.
-Provision of schools’ furniture and students’ desks.
This project contributes to increasing access to education for girls and boys by addressing the needs identified in HNO 2021 and based on needs assessment conducted by GAN (April 2021). The project will provide safe and attractive education and learning environment in targeted areas and schools. It’s worth to be mentioned that GAN will rehabilitate schools and TLCs to be easily accessed by children with disabilities by providing rumps and special toilets and WASH facilities.
If the funding secured is lower than what is requested, GAN will conduct the same proposed activities targeting the same kind of beneficiaries but will decrease the targeted communities and/or schools depending on the most schools in need for rehabilitation and/or schools’ furniture.
ASER test will be conducted to help us better plan and focus on the needed type of educational activities based on ASER results, also to capture the impact of the intervention, also to capture the impact of the intervention, children will be provided with learning materials (bag, stationery, notebooks…etc.) the quantities and colors will be differentiated according to the level, gender, and age.
Following formal education will be followed by the project manager in cooperation with a consultant to provide periodic training TOT to supervisors in schools to train teachers in schools then evaluate the performance and provide additional training as required.
Teachers and education personnel will be provided with teaching and learning material and incentives for 9 months, and schools also will receive the needed tools, on the other hand, the PSS activities will be provided for students (1– 8 grades), one session a week using I-deal, little fellow, and children will be trained on the safety and security plans.
The education system, which has already suffered immensely as a result of the crisis, remains fractured and under-resourced limiting the ability of children to access education services. The curriculum used is SIG and a high level of coordination with ED and other working organization will be ensured to avoid duplications, the whole project activities will be implemented following the EiE, INEE guidelines, and CHPs.(HNO)
To ensure project quality, including beneficiary accountability, GAN MEAL system is such one that stresses regular and systematic consultation with beneficiaries about the project's different activities to ensure their relevance and appropriateness to the needs, so that beneficiary feedback contributes to the project quality monitoring and consequent improvement. Setting up the MEAL plan will help to identify needed monitoring activities and responsible staff for data collection,data analysis, and data reporting.

4 - Needs

According to HNO21: *in 2020, an estimated 2.45 M children were out of school and 1.6 M were at risk of dropping out. Due to the cumulative effect of the conflict, the closure of learning facilities, and/or reduced learning hours due to COVID-19, worsening economic conditions, increased population numbers and displacement, the numbers of children out of school, and children who are at risk of dropping out, is almost certain to increase. COVID-19 and worsening economic conditions have exacerbated the consequences of systemic underinvestment in education service delivery. The areas with high rates of IDPs have a greater disparity between the availability of education services and the number of children in need of services. There is an overall lack of learning facilities and educational interventions in last resort sites and in over-burdened communities*.GAN conducted NA through field visits to schools and FGDs including different age groups and focusing on CwDs, the FGDs included relevant stakeholders in education, schools’ managers, teachers and parents. The big gaps are mainly stationary for students, schools & teachers, teachers’ salaries, schools’ rehab, lack of classrooms and/or students’ desks, clean drinking water, lack of TLCs in camps.Other findings:- It is necessary to provide remedial education for students with low educational attainment, but it is difficult to do this in the evening due to the presence of evening classes in all schools.- Raise the awareness of parents about the necessity of following up with students at home.- Providing awareness to parents about early marriage / child labor / caring for the disabled / complaints.

5 - Implementation of activities

Provide children with formal & non-formal education: GAN is committed to providing the official curriculum issued by SiG in formal settings, in addition to measure the learning outcomes using GAN’s M&E tools. GAN will provide non-formal education (remedial classes in Math, Arabic & English) for those who have poor education score or dropped-out for two months in the summer.Provide PSS and recreational activities: GAN will apply (SRA, I Deal, Little fellow) curricula in targeted schools.Establish and rehabilitate classrooms: GAN will contract with a service provider to conduct the rehab needed for schools according to the deep need assessment that will be conducted, the rehab scope will cover classrooms, windows, doors, walls, and school yard.Rehabilitate, improve or construct gender-sensitive WASH facilities: GAN will conduct a deep need assessment in schools according to the cluster guidelines document, then contract with the rehab service provider and conduct the needed rehab considering finalizing process before the academic year or proceed the rehab after-school to protect students, in addition to take into consideration the disaggregation between boys, girls and adapting the facilities with CwDs.Provide students with learning materials: GAN will distribute bags taking into consideration color and contents regarding gender and age.GAN will apply M&E tools (Student Enrolment, Student Attendance Rate, Gender Ratio for School Attendance, Dropout Rate, Access to Scholastic Materials, Adequate School Infrastructure, Beneficiaries' Satisfaction, Complaint Response Mechanism) during and after activities implementation to measure learning outcomes.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk/Threat: Attacks on schools: airstrikes, bombs/explosions/shelling, fighting/gunfire, arson/fire, looting/theft; kidnapping/arrest of a teacher or students, direct attack on teacher or student, intimidation of school staff / students or military use of the school.Mitigating Measures: Use the deconfliction mechanism administered by UN-OCHA, report attacks on education facility. Providing teachers, students and caregivers with needed training in addition to PSS & PFA services. Risk: Teachers or other education personnel participate in physical punishment, act violently, bully and neglect students.Mitigating Measures: All teachers will sign code of conduct once hired, code of conduct will be explained carefully. Principals/headmasters, teachers and caregivers will be made aware of positive discipline methods. Teachers and school personnel will be trained on prevention and reporting of violence, physical punishment and bullying.Risk: Pressure on selection and hiring processes.Mitigating Measures: GAN will work with local education relevant authorities and community members to develop a positive relationship. Possible education support will be discussed with the local education and other relevant authorities and community members before it starts. Risk: Learning facilities or their surroundings are structurally unsafe.Mitigating Measures: Learning facilities will be improved to ensure that buildings and grounds are safe and protected including stairs, walls, roofs, flooring, electricity, water, etc. All works will be supervised and monitored by GAN technical personnel. GAN will ensure CSG measures are in place before work starts.

7.1 - Please list any risks related to sexual exploitation and abuse that have been identified during the project design phase and any risk mitigation and prevention measures that have been adopted and built into the project

- Aid workers or associated people use their power or influence to sexually exploit or abuse the people they should be serving, particularly children. The risk may be heightened for children with disabilities, those unaccompanied, orphans and younger children: mitigation measure: GAN is a member of the PSEA network, attending a monthly meeting and following the guidelines, so to mainstream the protection from sexual exploitation and abuse in the project, GAN has developed a policy stating standards of conduct, including acts of SEA, and a work plan to implement the policy in place, staff members will sign the policy and received systematized and refreshed training on PSEA. The targeted communities and learning facilities will be trained on GAN’s policies and procedures.

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumptions:1- Access to the target schools’ locations in Idleb & Aleppo.2- Availability of the needed materials (for rehab and others) and equipment.3- Good security situation in all targeted areas. The security situation is somehow stable during the last year. 4- Access to Syria remains possible and safe through the neighboring country (Turkey).5- Schools are opened and physical learning is applied. Risk 1- the risk of shelling by Government of Syrian (GoS) forces and its allies in all or some targeted locations and/or infighting between different armed groups continue causing loss of access to the targeted locations:Mitigation:GAN will assess several options like the temporary suspension of activities until the areas become more secure again, in case of GoS advance in the south of Idleb, GAN will assess new schools in the accessed areas.Risk 2- Lack or unavailability of needed materials in Syria.Mitigation: GAN is able to secure all required materials from Turkey. GAN has all required protocols to ship materials through Turkish – Syrian Boarders and has good relations with Turkish service providers. Risk 3: Schools are still closed, and quarantine enforced:Mitigation:In this case, GAN will apply distance learning methods. All GAN supported schools are equipped with distance learning materials. Risk 4- heavy shelling by the Government of Syrian forces and its allies for a long time (months) or the target locations becomes under its controlMitigation:Shifting program locations to more accessible areas in coordination with the Education cluster, HFU, and other partners and relevel stakeholders. GAN has all required MoUs and protocols to shift locations to Euphrates Shield and Olive Branch areas.
