Project Summary

The intervention targets vulnerable IDPs and their host communities with improving the living conditions and protection of IDPs in sites and ensure equitable access to services and assistance of all persons in need. Through the project, SYPD will strengthen the predictability and effectiveness of multi sectorial interventions at site level and/or areas of concentration of sites by establishing CCCM coordination mechanisms with a clear focus on moving toward attaining durable solutions with full participation of all stakeholders.
Through the project SYPD will also achieve the following cluster objectives;
Strengthen the predictability and effectiveness of multi sectorial interventions at site level and/or areas of concentration of sites; This will be achieved through establishing or reinforcing CCCM coordination mechanisms and participation at the settlement level and other appropriate levels.
The project will ensure that site level information is available and shared with stakeholders and an improved management of sites and settlements. This will be achieved through community engagement, participation, in the transforming the living conditions and safe access to services in selected sites.
Other objectives will include promoting the involvement and participation of affected community to ensure effective response and ownership is increased, ensuring that affected populations living in sites have the information they need to access services, ensuring that settlement sites are safe and hygiene conditions through drainage channels and sanitation facilities, and strengthening community self-management to promote durable solutions for displaced people in sites.

Project Activities

NEEDSThe scale of displacement contributes to high levels of acute humanitarian needs, particularly among IDP populations, due to residual impacts of drought, ongoing displacement, forced evictions, conflict and marginalization. According to the 2019 Humanitarian Needs Overview (HNO), there is an estimated 2.6 million internally displaced persons (IDPs), marginalized communities and civilians in conflict-affected areas who are living in the most vulnerable circumstances, with limited or no access to quality basic services. IDPs are disproportionately at risk of gross abuses of human rights. This is exacerbated for vulnerable groups, such as women, unaccompanied children, large families with young children, the elderly, people with disabilities. Women and children alone constitute 70 to 80% of the IDP population with many IDPs from minority clans suffering pervasive discrimination without the opportunity to enjoy clan protection and connections. Sexual and gender-based violence is also widespread throughout Somalia.Further, displaced and socially marginalized groups require livelihood support to prevent a deterioration of their situation and to help protect their livelihoods, increasing their resilience against climatic shocks. Certainly, the shelter needs of about 2.3 million IDPs remains alarming, calling for urgent action to provide adequate shelter, NFIs, access to housing, property rights and durable solutions. Continued migrations have resulted to overcrowding in settlements putting pressure on existing facilities and coordination capacity for accessing services. PROJECT ACTIVITIES# Establish site camp coordination mechanisms and management committees and reporting mechanisms in IDPs settlements. # Carry out site level assessments in Baidoa, Hiraan, Lower Shabelle and other locations to determine the level of service provision and coordination at the site level. # Coordinate with government institutions to promote durable solutions, housing and land tenure rights for IDPs. #Provide site maintenance support including, street lighting, site layout and management, drainage systems, firebreaks and common areas in IDPs settlements # Establish community level camp management structures including community feedback and complaint mechanisms. # Establish information sharing desks and hotlines in IDPs settlements # Mainstream durable solutions in the project implementation including, returns monitoring, cash transfers and skills training.

Mitigation Measures

PREVENTING RISKS OF EXCLUSION BASED ON DISPLACEMENTSYPD will ensure that beneficiaries safely access basic services without causing harm by promoting meaningful access, accountability and participation of beneficiaries in the service provision. In particular, SYPD will promote the participation of beneficiaries by establishing and strengthening project committees and building their capacity through training and resources support. The beneficiary committees will be involved in identification of vulnerable groups and monitoring the project implementation and feedback/complaint mechanisms. SYPD will safeguard against protection risks by ensuring that all services such as items distributions are carried out during the day, in locations close to IDPs residences and clearly guarded with crowd control mechanisms to avoid theft, intimidation and gender-based violence. Where men and women are all involved in the collection of items, SYPD will keep separate lines for women and men. Care will also be taken to ensure children are not exposed to hard work or exploitation by having to carry heavy items. Each children headed household will be assigned a guardian during the collection of supplies to avoid exclusion and abuse. For example during the construction of shelters SYPD will adopt designs that ensure safety and security and provide dignity for beneficiaries including family unity, respect for cultural norms and the protection for girls and women. Each shelter will be provided with proper lighting to enhance protection and lockable doors to provide protection and privacy. Project Durable Solutions: The building of resilience of vulnerable individuals, households and communities in Somalia to future shocks remains of paramount importance to tackle the key risks and address the underlying causes of vulnerability for IDPs, returnees and vulnerable host communities in terms of sustainable access to safe water, sanitation and hygiene services. With the increasing recognition of governments and local authorities at the target locations, we believe the protection environment is sufficiently safe/stable to enable durable solutions. Our project will coordinate with government institutions and both Federal and State levels as well as local authorities in order to promote durable solutions, housing, and land tenure systems for IDPs. Our initiative is linked to the Somali National Development Plan on Social Protection and Infrastructural Development through the improvement of resilience and improvement in land tenure system, hence making it more sustainable. The facilities built by the project such as streetlights, drainage systems etc, will be subject to warranty to ensure sustainability. Besides, the management of the facilities will be handed over to local authorities and community level camp management structures with proven interests in social wellbeing of the society for a longer-term. The community feedback and complaint mechanisms which will be established at the commencement of the project will have their views incorporated in the refining of the project design; this gives the target community the biggest say in the implementation of the agreed interventions.SYPD will work towards strengthening self-reliance of displaced people, both refugees and IDPs, by ensuring a more active engagement community structures, development actors, government ministry of water, local authorities, private owners and other donors such as the Somalia Stability Fund and the World bank. SYPD will work with the community leaders, relevant ministry at the local and national level, and local government authorities to find durable solutions including development of legally binding agreements giving rights to the target communities to use the facilities constructed by the project, ensuring secure land tenure system, capacity building and income generation activities and hand over mechanisms for community-based projects. During the project implementation, SYPD will mainstream capacity building initiatives for community water infrastructure management systems in order to create a strong management structure and eventual hand over mechanisms.
