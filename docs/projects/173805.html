Project Summary

This project seeks to provide access to Safe, Equitable and Sufficient water for drinking and other domestic uses to about 55,000 vulnerable population of IDPs living in Camps/Host and Returnee Communities. The project will also sensitise these target emergency affected populations on the benefits of practicing good personal and environmental hygiene (including Menstrual Hygiene Management - MHM), through House-to-House and mass session hygiene promotion campaigns. During these campaigns, Information, Education and Communication materials (IEC) will be used.
Similarly, this project will integrate WASH services in the delivery of timely, effective, efficient and quality Health Care system in the target locations, through developing and strengthening the Emergency Response capacity of the Health care workers, including understanding referral pathways for difficult health cases. The IDPs living in camps and Host communities will be supported to have increased access to safe sanitation facilities in some selected Health facilities. The Health Facilities will also be supported with medical/health consumables, which will be affordable and accessible by the target population. This will consequently increase access to timely and quality health care by about 20,000 IDPs living in Camps/Host and Returnee communities.
Furthermore, this project will mainstream Protection (GBV and Child Protection) into the WASH and Health interventions. The project seeks to mainstream protection in the delivery of WASH and Health care services, therefore targeting about 5,000 vulnerable persons living in those locations. The project shall therefore develop and strengthen the capacity of Health Care workers on emergency response. The mainstreaming will be done in the communities and in institutions (including in Schools and Health Centers). The overall objectives of the project is to improve the living standard of the IDPs in Camps/Host and Returnee communities, through the provision of timely, quality and accessible WASH and Health services, without discrimination of any class of the beneficiaries, based on either sex, age nor disability status.

Plan fields

1 - Needs

1. IDPs in camps/host communities and Returnees lack sufficient access to safe and equitable water for drinking and other domestic uses. 2. IDPs in camps/host communities lack adequate knowledge on good Hygiene practice to prevent common hygiene related diseases like Cholera and Malaria. They also lack WASH NFIs to use in promoting good hygienic practice. 3. IDPs in camps and Host communities lack adequate access to safe and disaggregated sanitation facilities. 4. IDPs in camps/host communities and Returnees, particularly women and children, are increasingly susceptible to disease outbreaks, especially Cholera, Malaria including GBV, 5. IDPs in camps/host communities and Returnees face serious challenges in accessing health services due to various reasons, including inadequate Emergency Response Capacity of medical staff, unavailability of drugs and supplies. 6. Challenges in accessing quality health care services, as a result of direct and indirect costs, including the price of quality medicine.

2 - Activities

1. Construction of 40 New Hand Pump Boreholes and 2 New Solar Powered Boreholes to Create access to safe drinking water to 25,000 IDPs/Returnee populations. 2. Rehabilitation of 50 Dysfunctional Hand Pump Boreholes and 2 Dysfunctional Solar Powered Boreholes to Restore access to safe drinking water to 30,000 IDPs/Returnee populations. 3. To train and Equip 12 Local Area Mechanics (LAMs) for the repairs of dysfunctional water facilities, to ensure continuous working and supply of the water facilities. 4. To form and Train 94 Water Sanitation and Hygiene Committees (WASHComs) to ensure the management and maintenance of newly constructed and rehabilitated community water facilities. 5. Sensitizing 55,000 IDPs and Returnees with improved water sources on Practicing good personal and environmental hygiene, to prevent the outbreak of WASH related diseases, through Community-tailored House-to-House and Mass session Hygiene Promotion and Messages. 6. Construction of 20 Blocks of 2-compartment emergency Latrines to 2,000 IDPs living in camps and Host communities. 7. Strengthening Health Care system by Provision of quality and timely life-saving and life-sustaining Health Care services in 16 Health Facilities, including Maternal and Child Health services, Sexual and Reproductive Health Services, services for victims of Gender Based Violence, Psycho Social Support and Strengthening of referral services in the target locations. 8. Making Health Care system accessible and affordable by Procuring and Providing essential drugs to 16 Health Facilities in the target locations. 9. Develop and strengthen the capacities of 32 Health Care workers in 16 Health Facilities on disease surveillance, disease outbreak prevention, preparedness and increase their response capacities for key communicable diseases. 10. Rendering Psycho-Social Support counselling to at least 10 victims of SGBV, including referral to obtain medical services.
