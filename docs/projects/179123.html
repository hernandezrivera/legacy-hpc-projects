Project Summary

The project is to ﬁll the gaps in the FSL sector through Improving the food security status of assessed food insecure people through life-saving and life sustaining food assistance which planned to be achieved by emergency response to crisis affected vulnerable people with short term food assistance through appropriate modalities, where 2,400 food baskets will be distributed to 24,000 families (120,000 individuals), and the provision of the flour or bread directly to households or to bakeries, Where the bakeries will be supported with 1200 tons of wheat flour to be distributed to the beneficiaries (146,400 individuals), also, it aims to support self-reliance of affected households by protecting and building productive assets and restoring or creating income generating opportunities to save and sustain lives in the north of Syria through distribution of agricultural inputs for 1,000 HHs, such as seeds, fertilizer, pesticide and equipment; and related training, and support to asset building and asset protection (small livestock and animal feed distribution) for 200 HHs, including winterization activities; and related training.
Onsur will implement these activities in coordination with FSL cluster, the partners working on the ground and local authorities to prevent any duplication and to ensure that aids reaches the most vulnerable groups.
According to the latest developments regarding the COVID-19, the WHO guidelines, and recommendations of FSL cluster, ONSUR will be to provide the services by reducing the number of beneciaries during the sessions and distribution and increase the outreach activities, taking into account the recommended prevention measures.

4 - Needs

Drawn from a Food Agriculture Sector (FAS) assessment, the situation in Syria has drastically deteriorated with an estimated 14.2 million people in need of some form of food and agriculture assistance, representing 68.6% of the total population, 1.7 million people living in camps are deemed to be 100% food insecure,The evidence highlights an alarming deterioration, caused by a combination of factors including currency depreciation and associated loss of purchasing power, considerable highest in food prices, large population displacements, lack of livelihood and income generating options, losses in agricultural production and assets, all compounded by ongoing conflicts and the Covid-19 pandemicThe agriculture sector, has also been severely impacted, due to high cost and limited availability of agriculture inputs, the damage to the irrigation infrastructure, degradation of livelihoods and income earning opportunities, direct and knock-on effects of Covid-19 on food security and the agriculture sector, the devastating wild fires and other climate-induced shocks, have all contributed to the worsening situation in SyriaThe results of the national assessment showed that 50% of Syrian household reported spending at least 75% of their income on food, while 28% are adopting crisis or emergency livelihood coping strategies. This is extremely high and such families are unable to save and are barely surviving.(2021 HNO FSA_03_Feb)Emergency response at the early onset of any sudden population movement as well as targeted food assistance and lifesaving emergency agricultural assistance to meet the nutritional and kilocalorie deficits of the most vulnerable population as per food security indicators is crucial across Syria

5 - Implementation of activities

Onsur will provide food baskets, flour, bread, agriculture inputs( summer vegetables and its fertilizers, and fertilizers for olive trees), livestock(goats, sheep and cows), and animal feed to vulnerable beneficiaries(people with disabilities, elders, women headed HHs, IDPs, Poor members of hosting communities) -Onsur will coordinate with NGOs to ensure there are no duplications or gaps.Cooperation with LCs, community leaders and the FSL cluster will take place via field meetings, e-mails, Skype. -Onsur will distribute materials (in the appropriate modalities) based on a distribution plan from pre agreed distribution points within communities.Distribution points will be chosen in coordination with the local council and will be walking distance from the homes of beneficiaries, since trucks cannot access houses, and door step delivery to this number of beneficiaries is not feasible.Onsur will provide door step delivery. -Beneficiaries are able to give feedback at FDP, phone, or email.Women and men of different ages will participate in awareness sessions and trainings, Onsur will make sure all beneficiaries will have equal chances to all services.Onsur takes seriously feedback from beneficiaries with regards to access for people with disabilities/ elderly/ pregnant, gender considerations in crowd control, access to drinking water and waiting times.The distribution activity will be fulfilled by Onsur. 1. Identification and registration via Local Councils, Onsur leads verification process. 2. Distributing (Distribution Coupons) to identify the beneficiaries. 3. Identification and planning of distribution. 4. Monthly monitoring 5. The implementation will be supervised on the ground by Onsur field office.

7.1 - Please list any risks related to sexual exploitation and abuse that have been identified during the project design phase and any risk mitigation and prevention measures that have been adopted and built into the project

Onsur will priorities the most vulnerable persons(males and females including IDPs), considering the children situations and difficulties within these HHs (priorities to HHs including children with disabilities or chronic diseases), to receive services during this project. the project is designed to enable equal and impartial access through community consultation and participation across age, gender and diversity in how to reach the most vulnerable groups and not to exclude any groups and provide the identified services with dignity and impartiality.Onsur will focus on using the "Do No Harm" approach to integrate conflict sensitivity wherever.The feedback (complaint) mechanisms will be in place and all beneficiaries must be aware of them (Beneficiaries are able to give feedback at the point of distribution to staff, or via phone, or email). Ensure that vulnerable groups and individuals are able to access services equally and without any discrimination. It will be confirmed that the service prevision process does not generate any Sexual Exploitation and Abuse against girls, boys, women or men.Apply the principle of “different needs equal opportunities.”

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Large numbers of people in need compared to the provided services, to avoid this risk,Mitigations:Onsur will select the beneficiaries depending on a criterion, and this selection criteria will be shared with local community, and the leaders of the community will participate in this process. Changes in prices in marketsMitigations:Onsur will buy the materials under fixed price condition with the suppliers.Increased tension or armed clashes between the parties to the conflict, Airstrike raids or shelling.Mitigations:Program Delays/stoppageReduce exposure to areas that are known to be targeted.Ensure contingency plans are in place for cases of aerial bombardment and indirect fire.Ensure that the distribution point has a safe room equipped as a shelter for the staff in case of conflict-related incidents. As for the transfer of money through the post offices in Turkey to the areas of the Euphrates Shield (PTT), which mitigated a lot the risks resulted from money transfers by informal Hawala agencies. there is the risk of COVID-19, all staff and beneficiaries will always be stressed to take preventive.As part of the Security of Information management, the field team daily basis scans the financial documents and sends them to the main office in Turkey, with all employees signing the information security policies.
