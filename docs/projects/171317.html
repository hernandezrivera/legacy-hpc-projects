Project Summary

This project aim is to focus on Camp Coordination and Camp Management (CCCM) activities undertaken in settlements Daynile, Kahda, Dharkenley districts in Mogadishu and alongside Afgooye corridor specifically at KM13, Siinkadheer IDPs and Elasha IDPs, Afgooye and Merka district. It is designed to mitigate the physical and protection consequences of drought on populations and communities directly affected alongside Afgooye corridor. This overarching objective will be achieved by enhancing the overall protective environment for drought-affected population, increasing access to basic services linked to different sectors, reducing the risk thereof, and availing specialized support assistance to beneficiaries to help them address other interconnected protection concerns. The action is also conceptualized to consolidate the ongoing humanitarian response to the flood, conflict and drought situation in the targeted locations. CCCM,
SOYDA will establish multi-sectoral teams with clearly defined geographic responsibility for specific IDP populations (approx. 8,550 HHs; 51,300 persons in 20 IDP settlements considering 2,565 per IDP). Alongside Afgooye corridor KM13, Siinkadheer IDPs and Elasha IDPs, with necessary services through coordination mechanism set in place with relevant stakeholders and other partners.
This project is designed to meet the needs of 65% IDPs and 35% host communities of the total beneficiaries targeted. Gender will be mainstreamed into the project through the specific consideration of the different needs of women, men, boys and girls during the design of activities. The project will ensure that all participants have equal access to resources and training. Monitoring and evaluation will be based on gender, age and diversity dis- segregated data. Settlement mapping exercises will be conducted to get the information of all targeted settlements for necessary services in place and gap analysis, The settlement mapping exercise data will be shared with CCCM cluster lead, which will be useful in creating a master list of the area. This master list will then be posted online for the ease of access to all humanitarian actors, which will be used by different humanitarian partners for their future planning and response.
SOYDA CCCM activities will also improve the coordination and monitoring of service provision at site level and intra-site to ensure efficiency in service delivery and avoid duplication or gaps in services through capacity building for the existing community mechanisms to ensure access to information and services and improve accountability to affected populations. Throughout the project, the needs of women, girls, men and boys of all ages will be addressed all throughout the planning, implementation and monitoring and evaluation of the activities. Through regular site monitoring, needs and gaps will be reported and shared with partners and all stakeholders. Particular attention regarding needs will be paid to women and girls who also have to content with GBV and protection concerns within camp settings. Through this project, SOYDA will also address the current and newly identified needs by setting up camp coordination structures at the national and, where necessary, regional or district levels, facilitating and supporting the establishment of effective coordination and management in IDP sites, while respecting national context, authorities and existing structures.

Mitigation Measures

The following are the main protection risk related to exclusion of the vulnerable population upon displacement in camp such are, separation of families members, destruction of camp community asset, militarization of the camp through displacement of the vulnerable people in needs, undocumented vulnerable people and poor information sharing, lack of coordination as well as increase sexual exploitation among the girls and women in the camp. In mitigation to the above problem SOYDA will be able to improve community and camp leaders consultation and communication on prior displacement of these vulnerable population hence this will alleviate the grave loss of the people properties as well as have alternative means to move, it shall also ensure that quick camp assessment are done whenever, such eventualities come to any existing camp with its project location. SOYDA will also ensure proper information sharing and timing of session with women and children in the displaced camps to enable capture the vital information for better programming within the target camps. The following are some of the preventing risk associated with exclusion on gender and age such are, lack of consultation among the adolescent girls in the various camps about their sexual reproduction, safety and dignity hence this will create an avenue for oppressed girl child at the age of adolescent, also such exclusion as special group in decision making process in the camp might hinder proper advocacy and awareness among the disable, minority and marginalized population in the camps hence poor diversity within camps. It is also highly luckily that women and girls be excluded from the various employment and education opportunities within the camps hence this increase the disparities within the camp coordination cluster. For more effective and efficient mitigation SOYDA shall propose to have equal men, women, boys and girls in both services centered activities as well as employment within the target/operational camp in Afgooye and Mogadishu IDPs, Also on the issue of decision making SOYDA shall route for proper representation of all interested group within the society such as disable, minority and marginalized as well as elderly and single mothers in these targeted camps. SOYDA will be will to cater the needs of both sex and age and shall strive to ensure all are equally served whether on employment opportunity or service provision with the camp, it will also provide proper coordination mechanism that will enable the camp leaders and project team work in harmoniously for preservation of beneficiary data and without any alteration of their information to avoid any risk of discrimination among the beneficiaries In the IDP camps the following might be some of the main risk facing the target population that may negatively impact the beneficesin the camps these include; poor camp layout, high cases of SGBV, poor beneficiaries data protection, violence and an armed youth within the camp,as well as military and militia cross fire, explosion remnant of war and lack or rather limited information sharing and feedback of compliant mechanism in place to ensure safety and dignity of the vulnerable women,girls,boys and men in the camp. This is coupled with separation and sexual exploitation as well as undocumented needs of the vulnerable group. To mitigate this SOYDA however, intends to carry out properly camp leaders consultation and involvement of all stakeholders in ensuring the camp layout is of one that will ensure easy access to all beneficiary services within the camps, SOYDA will trained community camp managers on simple surveying of the camps as well as management details of these camp hence improve the public relation on the target location. To prevent the rampant and attended sexual exploitation among the vulnerable girls and women in the camps SOYDA will increase social mobilization and awareness aspect within the camps as well as train the community gatekeepers.
