Project Summary

This proposal aims to provide WASH humanitarian life-saving assistance for 27418 newly IDPs (5765 men, 6166 women, 7142 boys, and 8346 girls) including 200 people of special needs in 13 newly established camps or camps that have been recently expanded in Idleb Gove, Harim district Dana and Maaret Tmisreen Sub-districts
Furthermore, this proposal will promote personal and public hygiene and limit diseases spread through insects such as Leishmania, etc. This will be reached through a set of integrated activities that include:
- Establishment of water networks and connecting them to water stations , we take in consideration WASH cluster, SPHERE and AISC committee guidance: maximum distance from individual shelters to water taps and distribution points is 200 m and maximum number of people per water tap is 80 persons.
-support for operation of water stations for 18 months (6 months in 2022 and 12 months in 2023) to provide water for the displaced at a rate of 35 liters per day / person
- six -round distribution of hygiene kit that is designed to fulfill HHs needs of hygiene and in pursuance with their culture( 2 round in 2022, 4 round in 2023 ) , whereas we take in consideration family individual number and increasing soap amount to be 2.5 kg according to WASH cluster recommendation.
- Establishing latrine blocks in sites where gaps are considering needs of elderlies, and people with disabilities, whereas we take in consideration WASH cluster and SPHERE guidance: maximum distance from shelter to toilet/latrine is 50 m, family latrine blocks each one consists of two cabin one for females and another for males, Maximum number of persons per public toilet/latrine is 25 persons, latrine blocks for PWD include Seat toilets with metal handles and ramps for PWD
- Waste management deporting to main dumps that confront to daily health conditions for 18 months . whereas we take in consideration WASH cluster and SPHERE guidance: Number of families per 100-litres garbage container is 5, each person produces 1.1 kg of garbage every day and maximum distance from shelter/tent to garbage container is 100 m
- Establishment and improvement of sewage networks in camps with connecting them to main networks.
The proposed intervention will provide life-saving integrated WASH services through providing safe water, water quality monitoring, construction of gender segregated and safe latrines, solid waste management, desludging, hygiene promotion as well as distribution of hygiene kits in camps and informal settlements , seeking to ensure that the households residing in these settlements continue to survive, and in a way that respects their right to water and safe and dignified sanitation services.
The duration of the project is 21 months, from 1/4/2022 to 31/12/2023, and 27,814 IDPs (4,832 families) will benefit from it.

4 - Needs

According to draft HRP 2020,2023" IDPs living in displacement sites are often fully dependent on humanitarian support, and those in informal sites often experience worse WASH conditions making them particularly vulnerable." SAED has conducted a rapid need assessment in 13 camps in Maart Masrin and DANA sub-districts throw : 1- Technical assessment: This assessment was based on the direct observation of technical staff as a result of a field visit to each of the targeted 13 camps and making interviews with camp managers.2-Conducting FGDs with 7 groups representing populations divided as per age and gender including women, children, elderlies and PWDs. * Key findings: -the total number of beneficiaries was 27419 persons (5765 men – 6166 women – 7142 Boys – 8346 Girls) 5174 HHs. -the total numbers of people with disabilities 200 person - There are gap in water networks and sewage systems in all sites. -148 normal latrines blocks, each one consists of 4 cabins are available in 13 camps which need some minor repairs. The gap estimated with 252 family latrine bocks each one consists of two cabins to cover the needs of the IDPs in the targeted 13 camps. - 13 special latrines to people with disabilities in 13 camps according to WASH cluster, and AISC guidance - most of IDPS - There are no solid waste management services in all camps- All the displaced receive drinking water through tankers intermittently. There are two public wells on the site that can be exploited.

5 - Implementation of activities

Works will start with signing work consent forms the landowners and verifying Camp HLP, with sharing the work-plan with the relevant authorities. Also, prepare tenders for works and contracting with contractors.* during the first 3 months from project.- Saed will equip a water station in the general well , and establish a water network in order to connect the well to the camps and establish water points. -Construction of 13 special latrine blocks for PWDs.- Establishing a sewage network and connecting it to the main sewage pipe in the area.*During 18 months( 6 months in 2022, 12 months in 2023) : - Operating the water station and providing the displaced with water at a rate of 35 liters /person/ day, along with water quality monitoring where SAED’s field staff will monitor the water quality at the household level by taking water samples in each camp and properly test the samples with simple pool testers, and conducting monthly water test for water wells (Biology Test for well). -Monitor the sewage network and repair it if necessary.- procure and distribute hygiene kits to the IDPs in camps six round in each camp.(2 round in 2022, 4 round 2023). - Saed will contract with service providers to implement solid wastes managment activities for 18 months - Implementation of monthly hygiene campaigns, awareness campaigns against COVID in all camps by SAED staff.All interventions will be implemented in accordance with according to the recommendations of the clusterer.
