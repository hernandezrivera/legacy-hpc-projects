Project Summary

Strengthen the cholera response through appropriate risk communication and community engagement strategies as well as providing nutrition, education, protection services to support prevention and response interventions in affected and high-risk locations
Provide integrated Health and WASH lifesaving assistance to the most affected communities, including refugees by reducing cholera morbidity and mortality below emergency thresholds and by preventing further spreading of the outbreak in the priority districts.
