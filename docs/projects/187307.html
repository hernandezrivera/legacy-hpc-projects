Project Summary

This CSP aims to contribute all year-round to: Crisis-affected populations to meet their basic food and nutrition needs during and after the crises; Food insecure school-age children, to access adequate and nutritious food; Vulnerable groups (children, pregnant women and adolescent girls) to have enhanced nutritional status; Food insecure populations and communities to have more resilient livelihoods and sustainable food systems; Capacity strengthening of National institutions; Humanitarian and development partners to access common services until appropriate an sustainable alternative are available.

1 - Besoins

The 2021 national nutrition survey based on SMART methodology indicates that the prevalence of global acute malnutrition (GAM) in Niger was 12.5%, which is slightly above the 10% serious threshold. Analysis show disparities between regions with four regions Diffa (16.1%), Maradi (14.4%), Zinder (14.3%) and Tahoua (13.2%) having rates above the national rate (12.5%). In Tahoua region, GAM rates vary from 9.4% (Illela) to 15% (Tchintabaraden) and all the administrative divisions except Illela (9.4%) have GAM rates above 10%. The rate of severe acute malnutrition (SAM), was estimated at 2.7% above the WHO critical threshold of 2% with five regions (Maradi: 3.8%, Zinder: 3.7%, Agadez: 2.4%, Diffa: 2.2% and Tahoua: 2.1%) having rates above this threshold. Chronic malnutrition remains above the 30 percent "very high" threshold with 43.5% at national level with all the regions except Niamey (19.4%) and Agadez (28.5%) having rates far above this threshold. With the deteriorating securtiy situation in Niger's neighbouring countries (Nigeria, Burkina Faso and Mali), there is a great possibility of split over of these crises in to Niger thus affecting and worsen the actual poor food security and nutrition situations in Diffa, Maradi, Tahoua and Tillabéry regions due to poor access to these areas. The Nutrition Cluster estimated that some 2.564 million people require nutritional assistance but will target 1,777,847 persons including 491,822 children suffering from severe acute malnutrition (SAM), with 122,956 cases with medical complications. Some 885,738 children aged 6 to 23 months (boys and girls) suffering from moderate acute malnutrition, 153,196 pregnant women with an upper arm circumference of less than 210 mm, and 55,829 caregivers (accompanying persons) in the CRENIs and will need appropriate nutritional supplementation. A total of 179,762 children 6 to 23 months (boys and girls) and 11,500 pregnant and lactating women (not malnourished) will need blanket supplementation to stabilise their nutrition status.
