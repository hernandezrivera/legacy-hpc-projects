Project Summary

This project will improve prevention and response to malnutrition by providing malnourished children and pregnant women with multivitamins and feeding supplements.
The project will focus in the most-affected areas of the North-West and South-West, and seek partnerships with local health centers, mobile clinics, protection partners, and local community health workers to reach a wide scope.
Key activities of the project include:
· Mapping Rural Health Facilities with malnutrition knowledge and coverage gaps in Batibo, Ndop, Kumbo (East and West) Fundong, and Ndu in the North-West, and Mamfe, Konye, and Kumba in the South-West. This will go on to inform the project as to the highest need health areas and then these will be targeted for assistance moving forward in the project.
· Signing MOUs with 20 most affected health facilities and with 10 Health and protection partners for effective referrals.
· Training 100 Community Health workers in 4 rounds of trainings, and deploying them in their villages to screen all children 0-5 at risk of malnutrition.
· Supporting pregnant women and adolescent girls with folic acid and iron supplements. Mechanisms for delivery of this will be conducted in a gender sensitive approach, identifying safe locations for gathering of young girls and women.
· Train partner 40 staff of partner health centers on specialized treatment of sam and provide supplies according to gaps identified.
· Providing children with SAM with specialized treatment and refer to protection services for associated needs.
· Accountability mechanisms through suggestion boxes, helpline, and feedback meetings.
· Educate families on Infant and Young Child Feeding through the trained community health workers.
