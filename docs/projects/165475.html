Project Summary

UNDP conducted an organizational capacity assessment of Syrian CSOs between November 2018 and February 2019. To follow-up on UNDP organizational capacity assessment of Syrian CSOs, Consultations were conducted with the following stakeholders: The League of Syrian networks (SNL), the seven networks members of the League, two networks not members of the League, the Humanitarian Coordination Initiative, UN OCHA and UN Women. In addition to that, the draft project document was presented to the donors’ meeting in November 2019 to propose an action plan for the next steps of the programme to identify the intervention priorities. The main objective of UNDP Initiative is to maintain and develop the capacity of the CSOs at the institutional level, that will strengthen their role in representing civil society and improve their execution of projects through enhancing Coordination Platforms.
The Overall Goal of the proposed programme is to enhance the institutional capacity and coordination of Syrian Civil Society organizations and networks, towards an effective and efficient role in serving and advocating the most disadvantaged population in Syrian
The programme intends to achieve the four following Outputs/Results:
 Output 1: Coordination among CSO networks is improved within a gender-responsive common platform
 Output 2: CSOs organizational and institutional capacity is developed, including the capacity to mainstream gender equality and women’s empowerment
 Output 3: Collective actions, joint programming and implementation, and sharing of resources are enhanced in the CSO networks, including actions aimed at promoting gender equality and women empowerment
 Output 4: Improved dialogue and corporation among Whole of Syria CSOs
The programme is about strengthening the capacities of national bodies to manage and
better serve their crises and affected population, therefore, it falls under strong resilience
marker.

4 - Needs

UNDP conducted an organizational capacity assessment of Syrian CSOs. The assessment included:1-Analysis of the provided capacity building, and its impact on the institutional capacities of CSOs.2-Analysis of the current networks and platforms of CSOs and formulation of related recommendations3- Assessment of the organizational capacity of CSOs and identification of the current capacity gaps 4- Formulation of capacity development responses to improve CSOs’ project execution of Humanitarian Response and strengthen their institutional capacitiesThe assessment included quantitative and qualitative tools:1- Online survey covered two-third of the CSOs members of the Syrian CSO platform; and key informant interviews which covered CSO networks, Syrian CSOs, International NGOs, international organizations and donors.The organizational capacity assessment of Syrian CSOs revealed the following gaps in the capacity of CSO networks as well as of individual CSOs.1- Gaps in the capacity of CSO networks and individual CSOs2- Lack of collaboration and cooperation among CSOs 3- Lack of a common vision among members of the CSO networks4- Gaps in the governance structure of CSO networks5- Lack of a strategic framework at the CSO networks level6- Gaps in the current coordination conducted by the League of CSOs 7- Inadequacy of capacity-building programs with CSOs needs.8- Capacity gaps of CSOs in Strategic Planning, Governance, Advocacy strategy and communication, Fundraising, delivery of services and community mobilization.9- Lack of capacity in strategic planning10- Inadequate governance structure11- Lack of capacity in advocacy and communication12- Absence of a funding strategy13- Low quality of the CSOs’ M&E system and sustainability of funding and service provisionPlease refer to the conducted UNDP's "ORGANIZATIONAL AND CAPACITY DEVELOPMENT ASSESSMENT OF SYRIAN CIVIL SOCIETY ORGANIZATIONS" report.

5 - Implementation of activities

Based on the conducted Assessment Report, the program intends to make an implementation phase responding to the conclusions and recommendations, aiming to achieve the four following Outputs/Results: Output 1: Enhance the Coordination among CSO networks within common platforms and working groups1.1 Develop a joint strategic framework among all CSO networks.1.2 Develop a gender-responsive governance structure. 1.3 Initiate an executive management body to coordinate the operations of the platforms with a dedicated effort to ensure gender balance.1.4 Establish working groups of CSOs that will cover various areas of interventions in collaboration with other existing working groups and initiatives.Output 2: Develop and maintain the CSOs organizational and institutional capacity 2.1 Design capacity-building strategic plan of CSOs to be considered in the future as a framework for all capacity development interventions which will be conducted by stakeholders 2.2 Provide specialized ToT training on coordination, governance, HR, gender mainstreaming, community mobilization. 2.3 Creat a working group on capacity-building to support the previous subjects. Output 3: Enhance collective actions, joint programming, and sharing of resources. 3.1 Mapping of CSOs based on a field of expertise towards specialized networking 3.2 Develop a framework/approach for local community mobilization to improve service delivery and ensure local ownership. 3.3 Create a system for sharing collective actions among CSOs in each of the networks. 3.4 Develop a strategic framework (including advocacy and funding strategy) of each of the CSO networks. 3.5 Initiate participatory approach platforms for assessments and prioritization. Output 4: Improv dialogue and corporation among CSOs in Whole of Syria level 4.1 Conduct dialogue bridging workshops 4.2 Creat platform for legal regulations and procedural cooperation. 4.3 Establish platforms for lessons learned and exchange of experiences

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

- The program will be mainstreaming UN cross-cutting principles related to supporting the most vulnerable segments of the targeted societies as an integral part of the ultimate commitment to achieving SDGs. This includes poverty alleviation, empowering women, people with disabilities, youth and gender-related issues. CSOs under the program will be capacitated to disaggregate data and information analysis, and to design projects and programs interventions responding to these principles. In so doing, the program will explore options for joint efforts and/or complementarity with other relevant programmatic interventions.- The program is designed to support the capacity of CSOs, who are on the front lines for dealing with protection issues. This includes the provision of capacity building on protection mainstreaming, human rights, humanitarian principles, negotiation skills, and IHL, which will allow CSOs to design responsive interventions for vulnerable groups. - The provision of technical assistance to establish executive units mainstreaming the protection principles in strategic planning and activity implementation.- Establishment Information management unit that will provide the technical support for all IM officers in the working groups to develop guidance, information tools, and information sharing protocol. Moreover, IMU will supervise and protect the information flow among all the working groups for the benefit of CSOs.- UNDP with the support of the PSEA working group led by IOM will provide technical support for CSOs within the capacity development strategic framework designed by UNDP.- UNDP will encourage the CSOs to participate in implementing the accountability framework to ensure that AAP is effectively integrated within the PCM.- Related to 2.2 output, ToT on community mobilization will be conducted to support the CSOs in the involvement of the affected population to determine and meet their own needs.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

1- Political Factors and Uncertainty: 1.1 Major changes in the political environment that will change the priorities of CSOs1.2 Ongoing uncertainty in the roles of CSOs at the national and local levels. Mitigation: • The programme offers added capacity to CSOs and networks, where it will be perceived as an added value regardless of the political environment. UNDP will continue consultations with stakeholders on political changes with flexibility for adaptation. • The programme offers to create dialogue platforms among all CSOs that legal and policy changes can be discussed and reflected at the national level. 2- Policy Factors: 2.1 Lake of ownership and weak commitment to the changing process by the Syrian Networks League and other stakeholders of suggested governance reforms. Mitigation: • UNDP made comprehensive consultations with stakeholders before the programme design. Agreement with Syria Networks League and other stakeholders will be made at the beginning of the programme to set the main broad lines for the programme before starting which will eliminate future opposing to the change process through workshops and awareness sessions taking into consideration the culture sensitivity (language,..)• Support and emphasize the existing regulations and policies that have been established by CSOs. In addition to that, invest more in the local human resources and opportunities and challenges to be considered.• UNDP conducted advisory group from the CSOs and networks which consist of decision-makers and leaders taking into consideration the gender balance, people with disabilities, youth, expertise and geographical and thematic coverage.
