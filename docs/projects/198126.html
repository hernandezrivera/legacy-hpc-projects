Project Summary

FGI response in 2023 will be realized in the following target communities of the North-West and South-West regions of Cameroon: Menchum-Valley, Wum, Bafut, Bali, Bamenda 2, Bamenda 3, Tubah, Batibo, Mbengwi, Konye and Mbonge and this response will ensure targeted implementation of activities meant to promote infant and young child feeding in emergencies for exclusive breastfeeding support 3000 pregnant women and breastfeeding mothers registered in the baby corners/baby tents/dedicated counselling units! Moreover, FGI's response will ensure targeted implementation of activities to promote infant and young child feeding in emergencies for Complementary Feeding (including breastfeeding involving 3000 breast feeding mothers while also ensuring that 3000 mothers receive IYCF counseling / awareness!
Also, FGI will support Community engagement for behavior and social change by ensuring that 3000 mothers and other caretakers trained on family MUAC activity do effective improve on the nutrition situation of their households.
Finally, the project will also ensure the Integrated management of severe and acute malnutrition (PCIMA-S) involving 3% of children aged 6-59 months admitted for SAM management and discharge as cured with a positive recovery rate meanwhile 1 mobile unit will be established for Child Comprehensive response in these target vulnerable communities.

1 - Besoins / Needs

During the implementation of this project, FGI will be need to mobilize and effectively counsel 3000 pregnant women and breastfeeding mothers registered in the baby corners/baby tents/dedicated counselling units. FGI will also need to identify, register, counsel and disseminate handouts and posters for wider community sensitization and ensure targeted implementation of activities which promote infant and young child feeding in emergencies for exclusive breastfeeding support involving 3000 Children. Further, FGI will have to identify, register and counsel 3000 mothers with infant and young children (M / F) and ensure they receive IYCF counseling / awareness. The project will the support of community engagement for behavior and social change by identifying, registering and training 3000 mothers and caretakers on family MUAC activity. Finally, the project will Integrate the management of severe and acute malnutrition (PCIMA-S) involving children 6-59 months admitted for SAM management are properly cured and discharge with a positive recovery rate of at least 3% while ensuring that mobile unit are established for Child Comprehensive response in these target communities!
