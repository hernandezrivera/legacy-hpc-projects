Project Summary

The deplorable living conditions of the internally displaced, including the hosting community members, many of whom already suffer from illnesses endemic to overcrowded settlements, such as water-borne diseases and respiratory tract infections like pneumonia, which has been identified as a significant threat when coupled with COVID-19 (MSF,2020) will no doubt have a devastating effect on their lifes. COVID-19 has further aggravate the un told hardship facing people in Borno state and highlights the extreme vulnerability of so many who have already endured the horrors of war, diseases, and malnutrition. , The limited available health facilities are already overstretched. This project aims at responding timely to the COVID-19 pandemic outbreaks through rapid response mechanism and coordinated preparedness and prevention actions.
• Strengthen outbreak prevention and response capacities: Strengthen/expand disease surveillance, outbreak prevention, preparedness and response capacities for key communicable diseases.
• Streamline and strengthen the referral system from primary to secondary/tertiary level and to support secondary health care services/systems. The purpose of the intervention is to educate, response and mitigate the spread of COVID-19 in Borno and targeting all health care seekers which are the men, women, girls and boys , including the disable.

1 - Needs

Considering about 2.8 million (IDPs 41%, RETURNEES 19%, HOST COMMUNITY 40%) people in Borno state are in need of health intervention, the health sector assessment of the population in need of access to health information, facilities and protective equipment's and items is 1.74 Million, however, 1.58 Million (IDPs-27%, Rerurnees-25% and Host communities-48%) people have been targeted in Borno state. This project aimed intervene and support to enhance reached health services and safe practice that would reduce and mitigate COVID-19 will be on target. Therefore, the affected population would be supported and healthcare intervention made accessible. however, the insurgency crisis and COVID-19 pandemic has resulted to over stretch of health care workers, health system and facilities, partially or completely disrupted has transformed the access to health services circumventing the efforts made in recent years to improve women, children, and adolescent health. Its affecting people disproportionally with women, children, elderly and disable worst hit. Furthermore, the influx of people to safer communities and camps, couple with lockdown syndrome has subjected many people in the state to be faced with challenges of lack of access to health care and reproductive health, making path to other non communicable diseases to be on the increase too due to lack of money to cared for their health need and inadequate healthcare workers to attend to patients. Also, the stay at home has impacted greatly on Socio-economic activities disrupting means of livelihood for many families, most especially the IDPs. It is in view of this problems that SAHEI wish to target interventions that would address and promote resilience, recovery and tailored needs of each community in order to minimize morbidity and mortality from COVID-19 within the population and would help improve long term response to the pandemic more effectively for the future public health that that would giving informed decision to the affected most vulnerable population like the internally displaced persons in MMC and Jere camps and hosting communities of Bayo.

2 - Activities

1. Research would be encouraged to test community interventions focused on the prevention or slowing of COVID-19 transmission and to evaluate effectiveness and adherence to programs policies intend to mitigate COVID-19 transmission. 2. Intervention aimed at managing stress like health talk and health seeking behaviors that would reduce risk factors. 3. Information sharing through education and awareness rising on COVID-19. 4. Support and enhance capacity of primary health care and local government healthcare workers/educators. 5. Support and train primary health care worker and enhance other essential services. 6. Conduct advocacy and liaise and work with LGAs health care providers. 7. Support to install health facilities system in place. 8. Specific attention and priorities for the most at risk persons. 9. Promote behaviours that prevent spread among the people and prepare for those that may be sick. 10. Train community volunteers that would be conducting house to house visit to promote health education and healthy living behaviours and train women on how to protect themselves and families and empower them on livelihood that would be tailored to soap and face masks making.
