Project Summary

This project will be targeting the most acutely vulnerable children in specific area C locations of Tubas and Qalqilia in addition to Hebron H2 area. Those children along with their parents have little access to basic needs particularly education. The above target children have been imposed to certain violations and restrictions that hindered the primary needs to be directly delivered by the Palestinian National Authority according to Oslo Agreement terms.
In line with the 2019 Education Cluster Specific Objective II, which is focusing on most vulnerable schools to be supported in responding to emergencies through preventive measures, catch-up classes and school-based psycho social services, and also in accordance with the Strategic Response Plan Objective II:" The basic needs of vulnerable Palestinians living under occupation are met through the provision quality basic services and improved access to resources, in accordance with the rights of protected persons under IHL", AFKAR will be closely working with the MoEHE General Directorate of Counseling on delivering its activities by setting up a joint committee consisted of MoEHE, AFKAR and HWC people for effectively and efficiently assuring the best delivery of services in areas targeted. AFKAR will be relying on its strong renewed partnership since 2009 with the MOEHE and also with the HWC's collaboration in executing this project, AFKAR will be leading activities for those schools in providing school-based support to those vulnerable school children and staff on one hand and for most severe cases, HWC will be taking the lead to address who have been exposed to attacks and badly affected these cases at their health centers.
Schools of those areas are in dire need of psycho social support services as the pervasive and coercive environment of violence is an all too common reality there where children are attacked by either the Israeli soldiers or settlers. That type of need is decisive to the quality inclusive education where female and male children will get resilient through such activities.
Thus, this project will be improving the psycho social well being of children through two levels of intervention by the two organizations. The project will be targeting 10 schools of the three areas: 6 schools in Hebron H2 area, 2 schools in Tubas and 2 in Qalqilia province. According to MoEHE classification of schools, those schools are highly classified as the most high tense spots.
Luckily, AFKAR has an access to work in all areas targeted as well as in any location in the West Bank including Jerusalem through a focal point in each directorate, and HWC has its experience and its own branches in Hebron H2, Tubas and Qalqilia. This would give a strong credit to the implementation of the activities.
AFKAR will be working with female and male children from different classes offering them with warm up and breaking ice activities through various activities including facial paintings, dancing and singing. Round table discussions will be executed for older students to be able to diagnose the situation and provide alternatives for their reality. Such activities would improve their psyche once they talk about their situations and providing alternatives. Teachers, counselors and PTA of the schools will be involved in all cycles of the project. This will make the schools a supporting and appealing environment for bettering the situation. Therefore, teachers will be given training, PTA members will also be trained to cope with cases at home in afternoon sessions at HWC centers in the three areas. The idea beyond the first level will be enhanced as AFKAR and HWC will target the most severe cases who face nightmares and negative stress and share regular reports to the MOEHE.

Needs

Due to the protracted and military Israeli occupation, the occupied Palestinian Territories are still fragmented into 3 major areas , over two thirds of which classified as Area C are fully controlled by Israeli military occupation. This situation has created a de facto situation by which the National Authority has little intervention on those areas in addition to Jerusalem and H2 Hebron locations. A profound pervasive and coercive environment of violence has become an all too common reality particularly in the above mentioned locations. According to the UN CAAC Bulletin, 2017, 290 education related violations in the WB affected 47,924 boys and girls which means an increase of 34 incidents in 2016. Most of these attacks were taken place in the three major areas mentioned above. This spate of Israeli attacks whether by settlers or and Israeli soldiers, has had more students and teachers on a heightened pulse. In preparing for this project, gender and age related data is assessed to best result in program impact for boys and girls. The violations in Hebron H2 area are predominantly attributed to the closure obstacles and check points installed since 2015. about 37% of the boys and girls are limited in their access to education and suffer from the lack of a quality conducive learning environment. The recently constructed fence in Hebron H2 now cutting off over 1800 residents from the most basic needs including education and health further increases the psychological stress on children. In Hebron and due to security measures, students have been exposed to attacks and the image of Israeli and settlers presence impose a sense of insecurity and anxiety which will profoundly affect their psyche. Girls have been facing a high risk of drop out because of social variables fearing for their safety during the school commute.Accordingly, this project will be selecting 10 equally female and male schools, which are classified according to the MoEHE standards of highly exposed to double attacks . Six of them will be in Hebron H2 area where the other 4 will be distributed in most hot Area C regions of Tubas and Qalqilia where AFKAR has an access and past experience in carrying out activities in schools. These locations have also health centers for our partner HWC which will be their destination when executing after school activities for PTA and easy to reach targeted schools for further support for health cases

Activities

ActivitiesOutput IStudents, teachers and PTA psychological condition is improvedActivities:Activity I.IProvision of school based psychosocial support to vulnerable school teachers and counselors.Description:Training 40 female and male teachers in addition to counselors on stress relief techniques to deal with their own stress as well as techniques to help their students using chartered manuals. This training will be ensuring the sustainability of the project objectives wither in their schools or others.Activity I.2Provision students with warming up activities. Description:AFKAR will be offering such activity for 900 children to help them get involved and break the ice. these activities will include dancing, facial paintings, singing, etc for targeted 10 schools classes (4 + 5 + 6)= 900 female and male children. An expert from our partner (HWC) in addition to the school counselor if present, will be there to figure out the cases who need further support for the next phase.Activity 1.4Provision of round table open sessions and debate style activities at schools for graders (7+8+9+10) = 1200 students. DescriptionThis will be conducted in the form of debate and round table discussions to give opportunities for them to express themselves freely and critically and thus offer alternatives for better outcome. Issues will be related to human rights, gender role and other cross cutting issues they see as their priorities to discuss over. An expert from our partner (HWC) in addition to the school counselor if present, will be there to figure out the cases who need further support for the next phase.Activity 1.5Carrying out 24 awareness sessions for 120 PTA members. DescriptionAFKAR and HWC will be working together to carry out 24 sessions for 120 PTA members at HWC's centers in the three regions targeted. These sessions will be on PSS and identifying signs of stress and referral mechanism. Parents will help the team to discover certain cases who suffer from traumatic situations and thus refer them to further support.Activity 1.6Providing further health support particularly those with severe cases to HWC health centers.DescriptionAfter having diagnosed of the most needed cases for further psychological health support, all cases would be referred to HWC centers to start mitigating the effects of traumatic effects upon them through a number of follow ups based on cases.Activity 1.7Execution 5 two day summer camps for selected students Description5 two- day summer camps will be carried out in the three major locations targeted. All activities will be on protective and psycho social support. Activities will include interactive drama where children are exposed to 10 minute sketch and then they are encouraged to criticize the sketches and later give alternative solutions via acting the roles.Output II10 schools targeted are equipped with psycho social tools required to PSS activities.Activity 2.110 tool boxes will be distributed to help execute extracurricular activities. these tools and instruments include sports tools and drama tools, speakers, stationary and art equipment.Activity 2.2Equipping 10 schools targeted with LCD to help demonstrate manuals, documents and any other necessary contents to help ease the stress.
