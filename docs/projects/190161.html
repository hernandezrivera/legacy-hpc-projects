Project Summary

This project has been designed to respond rapidly to the global COVID-19 pandemic emergency. It seeks to address the most marginalized vulnerable population in the 5 target African countries: Ethiopia, Kenya, Mali, Senegal and Uganda. As European Union policy makers have recognised, children are the hidden victims, facing threats to their wellbeing and protection as a result of the increased risks of violence and abuse. Infection prevention control measures that national governments have taken to limit the spread of the disease have a direct impact on the lives of children and their increased exposure to violence. In addition, the action recognises that the global pandemic will continue and evolve, both in terms of disease prevalence as well as social and economic impacts. The proposed action seeks to address the immediate protection needs of children and will regularly assess the evolving needs of the target populations and ensure implementation approaches are modified for maximum impact.

3.2 - How will your project work with relevant local & national actors (e.g. CSOs, advocacy groups) in support of effective design, implementation and monitoring of the response, as well as ensure their meaningful participation in supporting this response

In Ethiopia, the design of the action has been informed by a comprehensive community based protection assessment for drought and conflict affected communities in Babile and Gursum Woredas of E.Hararghe Zone, Oromia region, from January 2020, conducted by SOS Children’s Villages with UNFPA. Other relevant studies which were consulted for the development of this project include a child protection assessment in Assosa and a child protection needs assessment report in Gure Shembola and Tsore refugee camps, both conducted by Plan in December 2019, as well as a participatory assessment report in Assosa by UNHCR, and a child protection information management system analysis in Beni Shangul, also from December 2019. the same approach is being followed by each implementing organization to endure meaningful participation in supporting implementation and monitoring. these include community-based child protection committees, child right clubs, foster parent associations, parent-teacher, student associations, local religious faith-based leaders, refugee response committees,e, and other community-based associations.
