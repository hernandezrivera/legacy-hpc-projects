Project Summary

The aim of this project is to enhance the food security of vulnerable families affected by Beirut explosions through the provision of cash-based support and food rations. This project will enhance the access of 2,000 vulnerable households in the neighbourhoods of Borj Hammoud, Marfaa, Medawar, and Rmeil to their basic needs through the provision of multi-purpose unconditional cash, food baskets and hygiene kits.
Another 125 individuals will be supported through cash-for-work, enabling them to increase the food security of themselves and their families, while also developing employable skills.

Plan fields

1 - Needs

As of August 05, Basmeh and Zeitooneh installed its front line response tents in Medawar (facing Electricité du Liban) to respond to the immediate ad-hoc needs of affected households. Basmeh and Zeitooneh’s teams were present at the tents to provide information and support to affected individuals, to support volunteers with ready-to-eat meals, water, and cleaning tools, and to aid with referrals of cases to medical practitioners. Based on rapid assessments conducted by Basmeh and Zeitooneh's field staff that included observation and key informative interviews. For the people residing in the most affected neighborhoods of Borj Hammoud, Marfaa, Medawar, and Rmeil there is a pressing need for emergency income, food, and hygiene, and many other basic safe shelter materials.

2 - Activities

Distribution of cash to the most vulnerable people affected by Beirut explosion allowing them access to basic needs. Each of the 2,000 beneficiary households will receive a cash amount of 600,000 LBP as well as food baskets and hygiene kits. The cash will be distributed based on Basmeh and Zeitooneh's cash distribution Standard Operating Procedures. The composition of the food baskets follows WFP and AUB recommendations and guidance and includes essential food items with necessary nutrient values such as protein, carbohydrates, calcium, and vitamins. These 2000 vulnerable affected households will also receive hygiene kits put together according to the UN-WHO guidelines. Along with the kits, the targeted households will receive an awareness brochure on hygiene measures and necessary precautions related to COVID-19, including PPE, and recipients will be briefed on how hygiene items should be used for effective results. Post distribution monitoring will be conducted to capture beneficiaries' feedback on the appropriateness of the amount distributed and of the packages received. The following vulnerability criteria will be used to target those households that will most struggle to meet immediate needs through their own efforts: 1. Residing in the geographical areas of intervention or have/had a property (on the day of the explosion), 2. Is an elderly-headed household. 3. Is a woman-headed household. 4. Is an unemployed man-headed household 5. Includes a person(s) with a disability. Building on Basmeh & Zeitooneh successful experiences in other regions, 120 unskilled workers will be selected and led by 5 skilled workers providing rehabilitation for damaged infrastructure and HHs. The unskilled workers will be provided with on-the-job training so they can use these new skills after the end of the project allowing them to generate income. Specific pre-set vulnerability and selection criteria will be followed. This will be done following Basmeh and Zeitooneh’s standard operating procedures that adopts World Food Program standards and guidance on CFW projects.
