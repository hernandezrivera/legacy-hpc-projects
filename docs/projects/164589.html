Project Summary

The project has 3 main objectives:
1- To reduce the risk posed by explosive hazards and the likelihood of exposure to them through implementing the Risk Education pillar of Mine Action Sector and raising the awareness and knowledge of civilians and humanitarian workers through implementing tailored RE programming in 4 subdistricts: Afrin, Jandairis, Bulbul, and Sharan-Afrin district.
This objective will be achieved through conducting RE sessions in the mentioned locations, targeting both children and adults in schools, Bahar's community centres, camps, and public spaces; as well as, conducting training for community focal points and humanitarian workers, as an indirect approach to disseminate the knowledge of RE.
2- Enhance equitable access to quality Child Protection services including (1) community-based psychosocial support and AR, and (2) quality specialized CP services, mainstreaming children with disabilities, in Sharan sub-district- Afrin district.
The CP objective entails CFS-based and outreach-based approaches; where CFS-based activities include:
- Structured and sustained PSS program
- Parenting program
- Life skills for adolescents >13 years
- Case management & Referrals for children survivors of violence, exploitation, abuse, neglect, child labour, early marriage, children exposed to severe/extreme psychosocial distress, cases of CAAFAG, UASC.
And transportation for CwDs to access CFS-based services.
While outreach-based activities include implementation of:
- AR for children and adults and IEC campaigns
- Semi-structured PSS
- Referrals of children to the CFS and services in other sectors for a comprehensive response.
- Implementation of emergency response activities and coordinating the protection emergency response amongst Protection organization in Afrin district, given that Bahar is the Protection emergency response focal point for the district.
Note that there will be 2 outreach teams covering the whole sub-district of Sharan and implementing these activities and targeting schools, community centres, IDPs centres, and public spaces.
3- The GBV component aims to provide access for MWBGs survivors to life-saving specialized GBV response services provided at 2 WGSSs, one in each of Afrin and Ma'batli, and increase their access to the specialized CM services through disseminating entry points to these services through outreach and statics-based entry points including:
- GBV Focal Point in each of Bahar's fixed clinics in Afrin and Ma'batli conducting information sharing sessions on GBV, providing non-focused PSS, targeting the clinics in a room dedicated for GBV services and referring survivors and at risk women & girls to the case managers to be provided with CM services/PSS, coordinate closely with medical staff to strengthen mutual referrals and provide them with orientation sessions on referral pathways.
- Outreach teams covering the sub-districts of Afrin and Ma'batli and implementing AR program and information sharing sessions targeting all the community groups, including men and adolescent boys, and referring survivors, to the WGSSs to be provided by CM services, given that Bahar will provide transportation for the referred cases; securing in result, wide coverage of entry points to GBV specialized services.
- Psychosocial support workers based at the WGSSs and implementing GBV AR program utilizing the AR toolkit, and My Safety- My Well-being program for adolescent girls. Note that the WGSSs-based GBV activities will also act as an entry point for women and girls to be aware aware of the importance of seeking help and disclose their cases.
- Quality specialized GBV CM services.
PwDs will be targeted through centre and community AR program, specialized services, and supported by transportation from and to the WGSSs which will be rehabilitated to be inclusive for PwDs.
Bahar has developed an ethical closure plan and shared it with the GBV SC and will ensure regular updating of the closure plan as needed.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

A sudden change in the security situation which could make Bahar unable to implement the activities in the same locations. Mitigation: Bahar is closely observing the situation on the ground and coordinating with the respective parties/actors existed in the target locations.To ensure accountability to affected population while and after the implementation of the project activities staff must be aware of the power dynamics that may exist within the communities.M&E Mechanism: Abuse of power is often at the base of incidents of harassment, exploitation and inappropriate behaviour. The following are mitigation measures:Ensuring teams are gender-balanced at all functions and levels of responsibility, in all locations.Ensuring that no individual staff member can create a situation where he or she alone is perceived to be the sole responsible for allocating benefits.making all beneficiaries aware of their entitlements and informed about beneficiary selection criteria; the quantity and variety of items that they should receive; when and how the distribution is taking place.Bahar has a strict mechanism and tools in place to ensure accountability to affected populations, Bahar team will:• Raise awareness about complain channels• Receiving complaints• Classification and recording• Investigation and Addressing• Transfer to the responsible person• Respond to ComplainantThe channels• Complaint boxes• Hotline• E-mail Address• Direct interviewFor GBV: M&E team will conduct Monthly observation checklist to the GBV sessions taking place by the MEAL field team to observe the convenience of the sessions’ location, performance, sessions’ timing &participation.• Monthly satisfaction survey with staff and female beneficiaries in order to monitor the accessibility to the center, types of the barriers that women face in their daily life, reasons that make girls or women who faced GBV do not request help and the overall satisfaction of the provided services.
