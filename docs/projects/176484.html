Project Summary

Since early July 2020, the heavy rains and unprecedented overflow of river Nile started to ravage the settlement along the riverside and lowland, with the water level rising between 1m to 1.5m high prompting massive population movement to Bor Town, Dhiamdhiam island within Jongolei, Mingkaman, Mangola, and Juba. Within the period, the flood-affected persons displaced from Jonglei to Mingkaman are estimated to be 7,287 households (50,376 individuals), Due to lack of shelter (NFI’s shelter materials) the flood-affected persons are settled in Schools and Churches which are overcrowded and a few available Pit latrines to use. Open defection is being practiced along the road and at the schools surrounding and the new market compound which is a potential risk for diseases outbreak. Most of the IDP community are using surface water and open unprotected water sources for domestic and personal use at household levels. (Bathing and washing cooking items) because of non-functional / breakdown of 07 motorized solar water systems in all the sites of site 1,2,3 and non-rehabilitation of a broken-down hand pump in site 2 sectors 2 and site 3 sectors 1 coupled with long queuing time for water collection from water points that take almost 4 hrs. to collect water for drinking and domestic/personal use .
In Awerial County, nearly 60,000 residents in six Bomas of Gut-Thom, Wunthou, Lim kethie/Ahou, Mingkaman, Duony-Gok, Kalthok, and over 70,000 IDPs from Jonglei State who came to Awerial (Mingkaman and Kalthok ) since 2014 were affected by the floods. From the affected villages, 50% of the population along the river Nile and lowlands have been displaced into higher grounds while 100% of some Bomas such as Wunthou, Gutthom, and Limkethii/Ahou are displaced. This flood-affected population is in dire need of urgent humanitarian assistance .
As of December 2020, the WASH gaps identified in Awerial especially in Mingkaman and the surrounding villages of Padaar, Dounygok, Ahuo, Marik, and Kaltok by the WASH partners are numerous as a result of the influx of IDPs from Jonglei that affected the already strained services delivery in WASH, with no funding by the WASH partners to cover the old caseload of IDPs of 68,000 individuals, and the currently verified new influx of IDPs from Jonglei is 52,015 individuals by the WFP. These numerous WASH gaps identified ranges from the shortage of water for drinking and domestic use that requires drilling of new wells in the up/dryland settlement areas far from the water sources, rehabilitation of the non-functional motorized systems, rehabilitation of broken-down wells, and shock chlorination of wells submerged by the floodwater, construction of shared household latrines, de-commissioning of latrines, distribution of latrine construction materials to family/households willing to construct their pits, hygiene awareness sessions and WASH NFI’s distributions .

1 - Needs

Help – Hilfe zur Selbsthilfe e.V has not conducted new needs assessments since it is already working in these targeted areas and has a very clear overview of the needs: -According to WASH Cluster- Mingkaman situation/assessment report, due to the increased influx of IDPs to Mingkaman, there has been critical water shortage. The shortage of water was contributed more by the breakdown of 11 motorized solar water systems in the sites out of 14 solar motorized water schemes being operated since 2015/2016 in the camp. Help – Hilfe zur Selbsthilfe e.V has rehabilitated 05 out of 11 with remaining 06 motorized solar system needs to be urgently rehabilitated to slightly meet the water demand especially with the onset of dry season.08 new hand pump drilling in the new settlement of the IDPs from Bor due to flooding that had cross to Mingkaman and 07 boreholes in Padaar, Dounygok, ahuo, marik and kaltok. Shock chlorination and minor rehabilitation of 15 hand pumps after the water level has reduced completely in the affected flooding areas.In order to have Improved access to safe water for drinking and domestic use; we shall focus on the following activities,•Drilling of 05 new water points/boreholes to the flood-affected persons settled in highland and without water infrastructures close to them. And the water management committee will be formed and trained for operation and maintenance. •Rehabilitation and shock chlorination of 15 water points/boreholes in the settlement areas. 8.11. Mitigation Measures
