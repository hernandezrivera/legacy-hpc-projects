Project Summary

Health care is considered one of the main priorities in building strong and sustainable societies, as comprehensive health care represents a fundamental pillar for achieving sustainable development and ensuring the well-being of the individual and society. In this context, it is important to strengthen cooperation and partnerships between the World Health Organization And local health associations to enhance health services and improve the level of care provided .
Al -Hasakah Governorate in Syria is witnessing great challenges in the field of health care as a result of the difficult humanitarian and economic conditions. Therefore, it is important to strengthen cooperation between local specialized medical societies The World Health Organization is vital to meet the needs of the population and improve the quality of health services provided .
The World Health Organization works to promote public health and provide technical and financial support to develop health systems around the world. On the other hand, the Syrian Medical Society in Al-Hasakah is considered an active player in the field of local health care, and strives to improve the medical services provided to the population .
This proposal was intended to establish a partnership based on cooperation between the World Health Organization and the Syrian Medical Society in Al-Hasakah, with the aim of strengthening primary and secondary health care, in addition to providing mental health services and raising health awareness to meet the needs of the population in this region. This partnership will include exchanging knowledge and experience, and providing technical and financial support to develop health infrastructure and enhance medical capabilities in the region .
By uniting efforts and resources, we can achieve tangible progress in improving the quality of health care and providing comprehensive health services that meet the aspirations and needs of the population. This partnership reflects our shared commitment to promoting health and well-being, and reflects the opportunity to make a lasting positive impact on the lives of individuals and communities in the Al-Hasakah region .
Let us be positive change makers in the field of health, and work together for a healthy and prosperous future for all .
Despite all national efforts to support the public health system, Syria still suffers from some signs of weakness in its health system , coinciding with public health emergencies and numerous challenges, which not only affect the accessibility, availability and quality of health services throughout the country. , but also on the physical and mental well-being of the population. The increase in the estimate of the population in need of health support for 2023 is attributable to recurring disease outbreaks, a change in methodology to be more proactive rather than reactive and focusing on the availability of services, and the deterioration of the socio-economic situation of communities resulting in an increased rate of displacement and deterioration of determinants of health, Which affects health outcomes. In the health sector, the number of regions classified as health ( disastrous/grade 5 ) increased from 0 in 2022 to 21 regions , while the number of regions classified as health (severe/grade 4 ) increased from 32 in 2022 to 113.
In addition to the Corona pandemic, the Syrian Ministry of Health announced an outbreak of cholera in September 2022. Between August 25, 2022 and September 2, 2023 (epidemiological week 35), 189,374 suspected cases were reported from all fourteen Syrian governorates and 105 from Deaths are associated with a case fatality rate of 0.06%, and the most affected governorates so far are Idlib, Aleppo, Raqqa and Deir ez-Zor.
