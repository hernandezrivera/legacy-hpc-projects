Project Summary

According to the proposed interventions, Aoun will directly contribute to the Shelter Sector’s objective 2 of Syria by supporting the sustainable rehabilitation of housing and related public infrastructures in Homs Governorates.
Proposed locations were identified according to the severity of the needs, their level of accessibility, and the experience of Aoun. The comprehensive, multisector and multiyear
approach was the determining element in the selection of the locations for 2024 and 2025, with the aim of promoting the sustainability of the interventions, community self-reliance and resilience.
In line with the results of consultations with the local community and relevant authorities, and taking into account sector priorities, the proposed Aoun intervention will contribute to
building the resilience of 1,000 individuals in 2024 and 1,000 individuals in 2025 affected by improving housing conditions and related public infrastructure for vulnerable populations through:
(1) Rehabilitating 100 damaged houses in 2024 and other 100 in 2025, (50 Alkhaldiah-50 karm shamsham) areas.
(2) Providing and installing 100 shelter Packages (doors and windows/900 doors and 500 windows ) in 2024 and other 100 in 2025, in Alkhaldiah area.

extensive experience in implementing Shelter projects.
Aoun will minimize protection risk of vulnerable population and will facilitate a safe and dignified return and reintegration of IDPs and returnees along with creating suitable and
safe living conditions for vulnerable communities.

Aoun will implement this project in close coordination with various stakeholders on the ground for an effective response, including the Ministry of Local Administration and Environment, Shelter sector, I-NGOs, contractor, Clusters, local committees,

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Aoun will implement the project based on the following steps 1- Receiving the names of families accepted for the home rehabilitation process from the governorate within the project implementation areas 2 Field needs assessment on the ground for apartments 3 Selection of the most vulnerable cases (Female-headed households; Families with elderly/chronically ill members PwDs; Families with children under 2 and under 5; Families hosting IDPs and Large families more than 7). 4 Upload accepted names for government approvals 5 One window to ensure that the family does not benefit from previous rehabilitation services 6 Announcing the tender to start repair work 7 Renovation work begins 8 Receiving apartments and handing them over to families under the supervision of government agencies and the donor organization AOUN will ensure the safety of families with special needs - Safe working space for all project staff. - Project Facilitator/staff selection and training will be from both genders (male and female) taking into cultural sensitivity. - The safety and dignity of beneficiaries and the Do No Harm principles will be anticipated by the team and the administrators who have a direct association with beneficiaries. AOUN is adopting strict and organized methodology to enhance and develop protection mainstreaming across the project’s targeted areas through the following: 1- Provide expertise to the shelter cluster to address the most prevalent and severe protection risks that intersect with the sector-specific response. Suggest protection mainstreaming activities based on the outcomes of the project’s Protection analysis, including offering to review assessment surveys or questionnaires of other clusters to ensure that protection is mainstreamed. 2- The project manager and assistants will make sure to contribute and provide feedback to the HRP documents and identify areas where protection can be better mainstreamed in these documents. 3- Make sure protection is regularly discussed as a standing agenda item in staff meetings, and staff members are aware of the importance of mainstreaming protection in their programs. moreover, we have to mention that the Project’s principal purpose is to advance gender equality The gender analysis in the needs assessment justifies this project
