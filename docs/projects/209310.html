Project Summary

After 10 months of continuous clashes between SAF and RSF in Khartoum, Darfur and Eljazeera States , Thousand of families fled to River Nile state looking for safety and minimum range of food security , health services and education .
The project is aimed to enrolled 860 children in basic education and 530 girls and boys in the alternative education in River Nile state including children with disability, lack support children and survivors from violence .
Recently the Walli of River Nile state issued a package of decisions calling for regionalism and racism against some groups of citizens who belong to the Darfur, Kordofan and Blue Nile states which destroyed threaten of civil and genocide war. The project aimed to reach 17400 beneficiaries to be provided by live saving integrated services .
Due to OCHA and some NGOs rapid assessment reports (NIDA) in May 2023 the lack of live saving services in the hosting states need more intervention in FSL, Protection Education, and WASH for River Nile state and other states which hosted the IDPs from Khartoum who fled from clashes between SAF and RSF the overall objective of this project is to provide direct funds (CBI),distribution of seeds and livestock for 2200 people with veterinary services, conducting training and start up kids for 600 beneficiaries including PWD , protection services for 6000 beneficiaries including child protection (Conducting awareness sessions, temporary child centers and child friendly spaces , reregistration of sexual abuse ,psychosocial support and distribution of cash for 210 of children in disability ) , BPWVO has integrated and coordinated protection services by laterally with other sectors including health and wash to ensure a holistic approach by enrollment of 2000 children in a child friendly learning spaces in side or near to the main schools including children with disability, provision of emergency feeding program for 2000 child, establishment of 20 temporary handwash stations and 20 safe drinking water in the schools to face the additional numbers , rehabilitation of 20 responsive gender latrines including percentage of disability latrines,
The intervention of this multisectoral project will contribute in reducing physical and psychosocial insecurity of conflict-affected people through protection monitoring, GBV, and child protection responsive actions for 2400 indiveduals. Women and girl’s needed protection will be insured by equipping skilled, education,WASH services, counseling, medical care, and other programs that empower them as well as boys and PWD.
Whereas WASH interventions for 6800 individuals(1200 women of households supported by 2 Jerrycans, 2 buckets with cover and 2 ibrig, of individuals benefited from laundry soap distribution, rehabilitation of existing emergency household latrines rehabilitated/upgraded (including PWD latrines), 12 training for building capacity within 3 campagnas, ) and will help to improve inclusive access to protected water sources, basic sanitation facilities and adopt good hygiene practices through provision of multi-sectoral life-saving assistance, hand wash facilities, gender-sensitive latrines, and hygiene proportion session distribution of hygiene kits and sanitation items for 2,400 W/G .
Pease building capacity for 2200 people to share live saving services through sharing FSL activities (training, distribution of seeds , distribution of livestock and revolving fund activity)

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

Through this intervention, BPWO is committed safeguard Right holders from violence and discrimination and is aimed at obtaining full respect for the rights of the individuals. BPWO will implement the project in impartial and non-discriminatory ways that promote the safety, dignity and integrity of the people receiving assistance during assessment, design of project and delivery of services. The project team will ensure all elements of accountability to affected population to be captured through extensive engagement and consultation with the community. Project is designed to inform all displaced about the availability and location of services. The project ensures safe and equal access to inclusive and non-discriminatory provision of services. The intervention will also carefully apply Do No Harm approach to avoid any negative impact that it might bring to the area or the targeted communities. Manage information in a sensitive manner so that the security of informants or others who may be identifiable is not jeopardized. The assistance and protection measures will not undermine local capacities for self-protection as well as support the efforts of the affected population and local communities to find security and restore dignity. Project intends to respond to the root causes of the protection concerns in the area. To achieve this, BPWO embraces community based protection approach, for building capacity of local communities, service providers and government officials. Their capacity will be enhanced in analyzing & finding solutions to protection problems, inclusive of reducing threats& risks, reducing vulnerability of affected population and increasing their capacity to cope with protection problems following exposure to shock and disaster.
