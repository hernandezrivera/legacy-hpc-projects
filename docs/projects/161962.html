Project Summary

The project will target the Rohygina and the host communities in Cox's Bazar through WASH service provision with more emphasis on community engagement while sustaining the access to basic life saving output but also the expanding and improving the quality of the infrastructures with social inclusiveness. The project will target the beneficiaries and the caseloads that are not covered by the funds provided through UN agencies.
in host communities, the project will support the efforts of social cohesion and attempts to bridge the camp response to host community program.

Needs

As stated in the WASH sector JRP comprehensive document, the access to the basic WASH service in the camp still not at satisfactory level in all the WASH components. BRAC as key partner and resinder will has also identified the gaps within its operation area, Water facilities are still under upgradation from hand pump to water pipe network, though the space limitation and lack of experience vendors to drill and equip the borehole is key issue, water quality at household still showing a significant contamination. the majority Latrines and bathing facilities still remain communal and different feedback from the communities where voice of women loudly demanded more privacy while the functionality rate need to be improved beside rationalization of quality and quantity.hygiene promotion: it is very important to shift the hygiene promotion from message dropping to such kind of interactive approaches with communities thus the recent approaches( RANAS, CCC ...etc.) used to improve and change the behaviors showed good results while the MHM is remained key area to continue target women in reproductive health and adolescent girls.the hygiene kits and Aquatab distribution and monitoring the efficiency of utilization of these items is one the gaps which need to be focused and addressed. the community WASH committee and their effective involvement in WASH program also one of the key area need to be continued with close consultation with government and other stakeholder to adapt the involvement and increase the participation level as well.The integration of WASH program with other lifesaving sectors is crucial and necessary to easily get the outputs(AWD, Safeguarding, WASH in schools and social inclusiveness...etc) that support the humanitarian response, therfire health/nutrition , protection, Education and site management will be the key sector WASH will work with to ensure the joint efforts are combined during the response and at different project cycle phases.

Activities or outputs

BRAC will continue its operation in strengthening the WASH facilities within the targeted areas while the emphasis will put on empowering communities to be more engaged in the project, the consultative approach will be followed in various project cycle stages to determine the needs and demand of the communities structures while gender and inclusiveness will be the guiding principle in this approach.As some areas of the Rohingya settlement and host communities face water scarcity, then BRAC already installed some tube well in an area between the host community and the camp to be used by both the communities and that effort will be continued in 2020 through the drilling of the borehole. Besides, BRAC will take leverage through mobilising its existing community/women-led platforms like Polli Shamaj of Community Empowerment programme to amplify results in a cost-effective way in the host community.In Water supply: the existing water sources and facilities will be operated and maintained through immediate repair and maintenance of any breakdown. This will be through conducting the functionality assessment and field monitoring where the WASH committees and users group will be involved in assessment and implementation with feedback mechanisms. The Project also will work in expanding the access to clean water through construction of water pipe network as per the master plan and with the guidance from WASH sector. the water quality testing and monitoring will be conducted at both households and sources level to monitor the level of the quality while the water treatment using chlorine at tanks , bucket chlorination and Aqua tab will remain options as AWD and other water related disease will be observed and actions will be taken. In Sanitation: BRAC will ensure the infrastructure remain functional, clean and used by the communities, this will be achieved through operation and maintenance, replacement and upgrading of the facilities that constructed at onset emergency. the social inclusiveness factor will be considered in all the facilities therefore; the communities with their different structures will be consulted before any construction takes place. the women , people with disability and elderly will be taken in the consideration in all design while the curren piloted project will be upgraded. Faecal Sludge Management will remain the key activity for 2020 as the focus will be in how to keep the latrines functional and the sludge is managed in a proper way hence BRAC will construct FSM plants with a network that lead to smooth flow of the effluent from the transfer and intermediate stations to the FSM, BRAC also will allocate trained human resources to manage the system with clear follow up and care while expert consultants will be engaged to develop the system and its operation guidelines.Solid waste management will be the sanitation element that BRAC will work for in 2020 and this will include waste mapping , collection, segregation and disposal with appropriate technologies that WASH sector recommends with more emphasis on recycling and involvement of private sector and the communities in the whole process. In Hygiene promotion and behaviou changing: BRAC will work in transmission of messages through awareness campaigns using different approaches which currently been applied such as RANAS, CHAST, PHAST and locally initiated techniques to support the behavior program while the women in reproductive age and adolescent girls will be supported by Menstrual hygiene program where special kits will be provided associated with training session conducted by the female hygiene promotion experts . the people with disabilities, elderly and children will be support with hygiene and dignity kits together with special interactive awareness session and in this regard BRAC will work with the partners to map and find the people with disabilities at the start up of the project for better supporting.
