Project Summary

The shelter infrastructure faces multifaceted challenges from conflict, earthquakes, and climate change. An estimated 6.8 million people will need support in 2024, reflecting a 19% increase from 2023. According to SARD's JMSNA 2023 findings, 24.2% of households reported unstable structures, with 16.1% residing in damaged or inadequately finished residential buildings. Following the February 6th, 2023 earthquake, 71.4% of households reported damages, with 39.1% experiencing minor damages, 49.6% moderate damages, and flooding.
Through this project SARD will assist the most affected people in northwest Syria, providing life-saving and life-sustaining assistance over 12 months, with a focus on improving shelters for vulnerable IDPs.
Implemented with a focus on protection and addressing gender and disability-specific needs, our shelter response aims to provide housing options in emergency and life-sustaining situations. Activities encompass emergency shelter in camp settings, temporary solutions in open fields, shelter upgrades, and house rehabilitation. The intervention modality will be tailored based on urgency, situation, and beneficiary needs. SARD is steadfast in promoting social cohesion through community-based shelter programming.

Our commitment extends to ensuring that newly displaced families access life-saving assistance through emergency basic shelter kits in open fields and quick-fixes at collective shelters/unfinished buildings. 0 - Project Summary (extended)

The latest HNO reports a notable increase in individuals requiring humanitarian assistance in Syria, totaling 16.7 million, up from 15.3 million in 2023. Of these, 5.5 million are displaced, with the majority concentrated in Aleppo (22%) and Idleb (16%). After 12 years of conflict and economic crisis, Syria faced additional hardship with earthquakes in February 2023, leading to significant displacement, nearly 5,900 deaths, and over 12,800 injuries. This project aims to restore the local economy by creating income opportunities for vulnerable IDPs and host communities. It integrates protection and livelihood interventions. Key outcomes include improving access to basic services through short-term work opportunities and rehabilitation, supporting home-based businesses, and enhancing social cohesion through community empowerment. SARD provides shelter assistance, including emergency interventions like shelter kits and house rehabilitation, for newly-displaced families through basic shelter kits and quick-fixing at collective shelters. Vulnerable families receive shelter support through cash grants and upgrades to IDP sites. Activities include assessment, beneficiary identification, distribution, monitoring, and coordination with relevant clusters. Selection criteria consider eligibility and HLP documentation, with BoQs prepared in consultation with beneficiaries. •Protection Risk: Vulnerable groups may face exclusion due to sociocultural barriers. Protection Risk: High project targets may compromise service quality. Likelihood: low, Impact: medium.Mitigation Measures: Prioritize safety and dignity, monitoring assistance quality. Maintain a strong CRM, regularly promoted to local communities. •Risk: Beneficiaries may face SEA by staff or associates. Likelihood: low, Impact: high.Mitigation: SARD ensures a quick response through its accountability mechanism. Staff undergo regular PSEA training. Risk: Socio-cultural context may lead to abuse or harassment. Likelihood: low, Impact: High.Mitigation: Community awareness programs challenge stereotypes and reinforce accountability. •Risk: Interference of local authority in project activities.Mitigation: Before project commencement, field teams will visit local councils to present activities, objectives, and timelines. MoUs will be signed with local authorities to clarify roles and responsibilities. Risk: Financial and Reputation Risks.Mitigation: SARD has developed procedures to ensure due diligence in financial transactions, including a protocol with the Turkish Post Office for fund facilitation and contracts with financial solution companies. Risk: Safety and Security Risks.Mitigation: SARD implements tools to safeguard staff and beneficiaries from conflict-related risks, including a risk management strategy and risk matrix assessment.
