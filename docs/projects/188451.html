Project Summary

the project is planned to be implemented in Idleb and Aleppo Governorates, to address the needs for protection services for the most vulnerable communities from IDPs, Returnees, and host communities from women and men, boys, girls, and PWD. there will be establishing CCs in both Governorates for fixed and outreach activities. proposed below services;
the project includes general protection service for 6000 people from women and men, boys and girls, PWD and elders: by1-improve and raise awareness of the target communities on the needed protection aspects as shown in the needs assessment and HNO 2022 which is related to improving the community awareness include CP/GBV topics: risks of child labor, early marriage, education importance, domestic violence PSEA, GBV core concepts and enhance information dissemination for the services provided by the working actors in the areas through updated and live service map documents. 2-establishing referral supported pathways and ensuring they received their referred needs with safe access and quality service with transportation service and in-kind assistance which will be determined according to disability level, vulnerability statues” women headed household, child-headed household, SGBV survivals” taking into most consideration ISP to avoid any expose for further risks.
Under GBV: the project under go empowerment activities 1-Vocational training programs for 250 women and girls who are above 17 yrs, for literacy and numeracy and other vocations from sewing, knitting, etc. 2-improve wellbeing and psychological situation of the BNFs from women by provision of structured and focused PSS services for 250 women as groups under the curricula of self-esteem. 3-Distribution for dignity kits for women and girls above 16yrs who are participating in the proposed activities in the cc for at least 2000BNFs. The GBV team will be from women, trained on mandatory training under GBV coreconcepts, PFA, selfesteem-PSS program, safe identification and referral, and protection mainstreaming. The GBV team will include facilitators/ trainer for vocational and co-facilitators and one supervisor. All the GBV-BNFs will conduct pre-post activity test. To measure the wellbeing/ resilient improvement, BNFs satisfaction, and improvement in self-confidence and individual skills to measure the quality of the conducted services.
Under CP: PSS Sessions 2200 girls, boys under 18 yrs. 400 children will be provided with arts and creative sessions for boys and girls as it has a positive impact on mental and Psychosocial wellbeing. Parenting programs and mother and toddler program for 100 BNFs. Distribution of PSS kits for children from Boys and Girls who are under 18Yrs for at least 2200 BNfs. And newborn kits for Vulnerable mothers and toddlers for 400 BNFs. The project emphasizes building the capacities of the working staff under protection mainstreaming, GBV core concepts, gender and Disability inclusion, PSEA mandatory training. facilitation skills, PSS program pieces of training, SIR training. the project mainstream CP and GP by CSG, PSEA are reflected in mandatory COC for all the working staff. the project will be implemented with full coordination with protection cluster and its relevant sub-clusters and reporting through the 4Ws to avoid any duplication. the project will take the COV-19 outbreak into most consideration by ensuring the awareness raising regarded, PPE for the working staff and all the BNFs people from the project. the total number of people per session will be no more than 8 people per session. and the established center will be cleaned and sterilized on daily basis in addition to ensuring water availability in project centers for safe WASH services. all the project activities will address the inclusion of PWD from women, men, and girls who are under 18. All of the proposed activities will be delivered in the fixed and mobile centers according to the needs and BNFs access to services.

4 - Needs

The project aims to address the protection concerns which the communities In NWS suffering from under: GBV-PSEA-CP-GP. Based on MSNA2022 HH survey; 45% of NWS people aren’t receiving humainitarin assistance mostly due to: not aware of any, lack of information and access issues further to being not eligible,etc.through RW experience in Idleb, rate of CP concerns: Physical abuse, child labour, and forced early marriage has been increased. further to bad livelihood situation especially covid-19 impact which increased risks exposure for women and children as they are most vulnerable. the protection concerns has negatively affected the communities by negative coping mechanisms as: begging, engage in illegal activities,local community support and rely on humanitarian assistance, especially related to vulnerable women and children from boys and girls, PWD,elders. according to Voices from Syria 2022: GBV is a dominant feature of the Syrian humanitarian crisis, affecting the lives of millions of women and adolescent girls . In 2021, women and girls across all governorates of Syria were subjected to physical violence (hitting, beating, physical injuries, torture,murder, and femicide), psychological and emotional violence (verbal abuse, forced isolation, gaslighting, social stigmatisation and ostracisation), sexual violence (sexual assault, blackmail, exploitation, harassment, and rape), social violence (stigma and discrimination), as well as forced and early marriage.

5 - Implementation of activities

The project activities will be implemented under full coordination with the protection cluster and its relevant sub-clusters to avoid any duplication in the efforts. the addressed needs are reflected by the diverse needs of women, men, children boys and girls under 18, PWD, and elders. the project is designed and will be implemented under the DO-NO HARM principle, humanitarian principles and standards to provide assistance in quality, equity, and gender equality. project activities will address avoiding any exposure for further risks for all BNFs. proposed activities are reflected by community participation and accountable by the feedback mechanisms. the working staff will be from women and men taking into account social norms and community acceptance to be tailored and contextualised further to accepted staff to ensure project expected results. the project staff will be from local communities in the targeted areas to help in improving the livelihood situations in the area. the project activities will be implemented after providing all the capacity building needed to the working staff to have quality and safe services, with ensuring the mainstreaming of PSEA- CSG .All MEAL measures will be in place for conducted activites, by ensuring BNFs's consents to participate/or caregivers for children. pre-post test including many aspects self wise and service wise. distribution list and BNFs satisfaction surveys. Data protection measures and ISP all the BNFs will be coded.

10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

The project includes SEA risks that could be taken place within working staff, or due to some working staff with BNFs from women or children or even within the BNFs level. SEA risks could occur during the staff or BNFs journey to the project centre location by some people from the community. Rahma has set many mitigationstrategies to address PSEA, which include: the PSEA mandatory training and related COC-PSEA and CSG, which the staff must align with and obtained, with the clear clarificacation that the Rahma have zero tolerance with any SEA accidents. Rahma has PSEA focal points in the working area and there will be focal points in the project centre for any reporting needed or support in PSEA mainstreaming.Rahma will do awareness-raising for PSEA which include community level, working staff level and centre level which could include: brochures and banners, videos, awareness raisingmessages and hotlines. Rahma will ensure that all the working staff is well-knownabout the internal reporting and feedback mechanism is taken in place in case of SEA occurrence cases. all the project's service providers or contractures who has directcontact with project benefeciaries from children or women must be trained and signed COC. Rahma will ensure that during any conducted sessions there will be a female member to ensure benefeciaries safety, especially from children and women.
