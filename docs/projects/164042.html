Project Summary

(Planned – ( SO1 )
First: The project plans to secure daily provision of free bread to 4000 HHs IDPs in 18camps of Idleb , and Throughout the project, Minber Al-Sham will ensure the daily provision of 2 KG free bread portions for families in targeted sites.
Minber Al-Sham will carry out this project through a chain of activities starting from purchasing the wheat flour from Turkey, bread baking process through contracting bakeries and distributing the bread through establishing distribution centers in the targeted sites.
Through this chain of activities, Minber Al-Sham will ensure the continuous monitoring of bread quality and daily provision to the people in need.
- Enhance the food security situation of 4000 families (20000 BNFs)
- Reduce negative coping mechanisms.
- Reduce the percentage of child labor by securing some of the family's needs.
- Enhance the economic situation of refugees by creating job opportunities for project workers.
Second: The project plans to secure monthly provision of free 1000 food baskets to 1000 (displaced & residing )families residing in Tell Abiad in north Ar-Raqqa, Throughout life the project(total of 6000 kits), Minber Al-Sham will ensure the monthly provision of free food baskets for internally (IDPs & residing )families residing in Tell Abiad.
Minber Al-Sham will carry out this project through a chain of activities starting from purchasing food baskets from Turkey, the distributing through establishing distribution centers in the targeted sites.
Through this chain of activities, Minber Al-Sham will ensure the continuous monitoring of food baskets quality and monthly provision to the people in need.

4 - Needs

After nine years of crisis, the people of Syria, including those residing in camps, continue to face great challenges, many struggle to feed and educate their children. Food security remains a serious challenge due to continued localized hostilities, new and protracted displacements, increased numbers of recent returnees and the sustained erosion of communities’ resilience after almost nine years of conflict. Around 6.5 million people in Syria are estimated to be food insecure and in need of food and livelihood support. An additional 2.5 million people are at risk of food insecurity and need livelihoods to support to strengthen their resilience (FAO/WFP Crop and Food Security Assessment 2019), referring to HNO 2020,Lack of employment opportunities and increased fuel and commodity prices limited households’ purchasing power, causing more families to adopt negative coping strategies such as consuming less preferred and less expensive food, cutting the number of daily meals or withdrawing children from school in order to work Minber Al-Sham field team conducted need assessment in Tell Abiad during February 2020, during which it was found that there were 180,000 people in Tel Abyad and its countryside, and there is no organization that provides any food aid.
