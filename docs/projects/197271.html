Project Summary

The expected outcome of this project is to ensure that Colombian and Venezuelan communities are better protected from the effects of armed conflict through promotion of IHL and compliance from Colombian armed groups.
This will be reached through 3 different axes:
• First, to Increase armed groups compliance & respect of humanitarian norms through development and dissemination of IHL principles and tools inducing behaviour change of financially motivated OAGs.
Geneva Call aims to work closely with armed actors and groups to strengthen the engagement on IHL/IHRL norms, first on awareness and knowledge, and then towards police and practice. Geneva Call will work on issues beyond awareness and demonstrated intent, to ensure that frameworks for implementation exists, this includes recourse actions when violations are reported. This will be done with as man groups as possible in Colombia.
The main barrier for Geneva Call, as for all humanitarian actors in Colombia, are legal barriers to direct formal dialogues with armed groups. Therefore, Geneva Call is currently ensuring the possibility to obtain a permit from the Presidency to be able to access armed groups (change of law done in 2022).
• Second, targeted communities and targeted stakeholders are better capacitated to discuss and advocate for respect for international humanitarian norms and to develop protection initiatives.
During the previous project, Geneva Call in Colombia developed a large network of partnerships and alliances with local partners to support the project on International Humanitarian Laws focusing on mine action, protection of the medical mission, SGBV and forced recruitment and use of children and teenagers.
During 2023, Geneva Call will develop and further extend those alliances, including the development of our IHL Friends initiatives. Geneva Call will continue organizing capacity-building trainings and workshops with communities affected by the conflict, through direct sessions or training of trainers. Protection initiatives will also be developed with the communities to reinforce their knowledge and impact on their direct protection mechanisms.
Geneva Call will keep its specific attention on engaging local/municipal authorities, community leaders and civil society organizations to generate greater acceptance on international humanitarian norms.
• Third, targeted Indigenous Population communities are better capacitated to advocate for respect of their rights and international humanitarian norms in conflict affected areas:
A specific focus will be further developed on Indigenous Communities to further develop their knowledge in International Humanitarian Laws and on providing visibility to the humanitarian needs they are facing, towards humanitarian actors, donors, governments and international communities.
Indigenous communities are more vulnerable than other communities: their traditional ways of living and cultivating is disturbed by the conflict, the extension of illegal trafficking and illegal mining, the confinements and use of mines are affecting their capacity to cover their basic needs. In addition, for the Indigenous people close to the border, a bi-nationality issue is to be considered to guarantee a proper access to governmental and humanitarian assistance.
Geneva Call plans for 2023 to further develop its relations with the indigenous communities, reinforcing their knowledge and understanding of IHL, as well as their capacities to highlight their humanitarian needs and the level of violence they are exposed to. Dialogue with armed groups will also include a component on indigenous people, and Geneva Call will also keep on bridging the needs identified in the reserves with the international community to ensure better access to emergency and humanitarian services. Specific materials will be developed for Indigenous people, in full respect of their culture, heritage and adapted in indigenous languages.

4 - CENTRALIDAD DE LA PROTECCION
4.1. ¿El proyecto analiza y busca abordar los riesgos de protección, incluyendo los riesgos enfrentados por personas generalmente más vulnerables (género, edad, discapacidad)?

Yes

4.1.1. Provea brevemente información al respecto - máximo 1.000 carácteres

Geneva Call seeks to integrate a gender perspective in all its activities and at all stages of its project cycle, in accordance with International Humanitarian Standards on best practice gender policy, and in collaboration with its partner organizations and donors. Geneva Call is committed to gender-sensitive programming integrating gender analysis; and to consulting, involving and engaging women and men wherever possible in its activities and promoting their full participation. Furthermore, Geneva Call seeks to achieve gender balance in its operational activities as well as its internal structure. Girls and boys are affected both similarly and uniquely by armed conflict. For example, boys may be more susceptible to use in hostilities, and girls may be more susceptible to sexual violence. Programmatic responses consider the special vulnerabilities of children as children as well as the special vulnerabilities of girls and boys.

4.2. ¿El proyecto garantiza la seguridad y la dignidad de las personas beneficiadas y tiene en cuenta el principio “No hacer daño”?

Yes

4.2.1. Provea brevemente información al respecto - máximo 1.000 carácteres

Being conflict sensitive is critical given the highly politicised context of Colombia. The conflict in is moving fast, with new alliances and large level of power fights to control areas. Working transparently and locally in pursuit of Geneva Call’s mandate has allowed Geneva Call to operate so far, and due consideration to conflict sensitivity will be a key aspect of direct engagement and relationship building. Geneva Call will analyse the context and ensure that leaders working with Geneva Call will not be harmed due to their involvement in projects. Some activities may therefore be revised based on the “do not harm” analysis and evolution of the context in some specific areas of intervention.

4.3. ¿El proyecto identifica las barreras y asegura accesibilidad universal e imparcial a la ayuda y los servicios por los diferentes grupos de población?

Yes

4.3.1. Provea brevemente información al respecto - máximo 1.000 carácteres

The Venezuelan crisis and regional migration have led to double vulnerabilities for those migrants who find themselves in conflict settings in Colombia. Geneva Call will specifically address the Venezuelan community both as a community at risk, as well as recognizing the cross-border structure of several armed groups.

The project is part of a larger and longer strategy aiming at reducing the effect of the conflicts and violence on the civilian population in Colombia.
