Project Summary

Intervention Summary:
The intervention aims to address the vulnerability and specific challenges faced by boys and young men in Cameroon, particularly in the Southwest Region, North West Region, and far North Region. The purpose of the intervention is to create safe spaces where boys and young men below 25 years can express themselves, receive psycho-social support, and improve their means of livelihood. By providing targeted assistance and empowerment opportunities, the intervention aims to enhance the well-being of boys and young men, ultimately benefiting their families and communities. The intervention targets vulnerable boys and young men who are affected by the ongoing humanitarian crises, including armed conflict, violence, and displacement, as well as those who face gender-based violence and other forms of discrimination. Through various activities such as awareness campaigns, strengthening community clubs, capacity building for stakeholders, and economic empowerment support, the intervention seeks to promote their rights, provide protection, and offer essential services in areas such as education, health, and psychosocial support.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The global objective of this project is to Empowering Vulnerable Boys and Young Men in Donga and Matung Division and Fako Division of Cameroon. The assessed needs that the project will address through its activities include: Lack of safe spaces and support for boys: Many vulnerable boys in the selected regions of Cameroon lack safe spaces where they can express themselves, receive support, and engage in constructive activities. The project will establish safe spaces specifically designed for boys, providing them with a supportive environment for recreation, life skills training, peer support, and counseling. Protection and support for boys at risk: Boys who are unaccompanied, separated from their families, or at risk of violence, abuse, or exploitation require specialized case management services. The project will provide individualized support and referrals to appropriate services to ensure their safety, well-being, and access to protection. Lack of awareness about boys' rights and needs: There is a need to raise awareness among communities, families, and authorities about the specific rights and needs of boys. The project will conduct awareness campaigns through social media, community engagements, and dialogues to promote understanding and address harmful practices such as child marriage and recruitment by armed groups. Limited access to education: Vulnerable boys often face barriers to education, including financial constraints, lack of documentation, and limited support. The project will support boys in enrolling and staying in school, or accessing alternative learning opportunities. This will include providing educational support, such as school fees, supplies, and scholarships. Inadequate healthcare services: Boys in vulnerable situations may lack access to essential health services, including immunization, nutrition support, HIV testing and treatment, and malaria prevention. The project will provide these essential health services to ensure their well-being and promote good health practices. Lack of platforms for boys' participation and advocacy: Boys' voices and perspectives are often overlooked in decision-making processes and policies. The project will establish BoysEngage groups in schools and communities, empowering boys to actively participate and advocate for their rights. These groups will serve as platforms for expressing their concerns and engaging in discussions related to their well-being. Limited integration of boys' issues in policies and plans: The specific needs and interests of boys are not always adequately addressed in national and local policies and plans. The project will advocate for the inclusion of boys' issues, collaborating with relevant stakeholders to ensure their concerns are considered and integrated into policy frameworks.
