Project Summary

RRI is a German non-governmental organization with two in-country programs (RSRP and RIRP), RIRP has been rehabbed more than 6.300 houses and have built more than 11.500 UNHCR standard L-shape shelter from 2009 on in Iraq. RSRP doesn't have much shelter performance yet due to MOLA approval Which was an issue for us in the past, but with a new framework that has been build by RSRP's professional shelter team (including local staff with previous experience in shelter rehabilitation and expat staff) with STD which will ensure that the approval is obtained with the time frame outlined in HRP 2021. It is worth noting that according to the technical evaluation conducted in 2018, STD, in cooperation with RSRP, implemented the rehabilitation of damaged homes in Deir Ezzor.
The project’s aims to provide basic NFI and shelter to 232280 displaced people, returnees and overburdened host community at risk within the highly neglected sub-districts in the aforementioned areas Located in:
Homs, Foroqles, Taldu, Al-Qusayr, / Homs Governorate
Deir Ezzor, Khasham, Tabni, Abukamal / Deir Ezzor Governorate
Dar'a, Mzeireb, Kherbet Ghazala, Ghabagheb, As-Sanamayn / Dar’a Governorate
Jebel Saman, Nbul, Bulbul, Tall Refaat / Aleppo Governorate.
Duma, Zabadani, Kisweh, Babella / Rural Damascus Governorate.
Sabkha / Ar-raqqa Governorate
Which including also two of the top three communities with limited movement abilities:
Al-Hasakeh / Al-Hasakeh Governorate
And As-Suqaylabiyah, / Hama Governorate
Moreover, that RSRP is currently implementing two projects in Ar-raqqa and Deir Ezzor
The consequences of the conflict and breakout of covid-19 have been particularly severe and have converted many parts of the different governorate into hard-to-reach areas, where people suffer from lack of basic needs, due to the enormous destruction and being cut off from access to markets, loss of livelihood and income, the rise in the cost of basic commodities, and the sharp depreciation of the currency. Displaced people, returnees and the overburdened host communities face tremendous struggles while trying to provide basic products for their families and ensure survival. With this NFI and shelter support, the living conditions of 33183 HHs and 232280people in areas with high rates of displacement large-scale destruction will be improved and the basics of life will be provided through the distribution of essential NFI kits, seasonal kits and emergency shelter kit In addition to conduct training for the community stakeholders.
After assessing the NFI and shelter gap and need and timing of the intervention considering all vulnerability groups and gender including men, woman, boys, and girls. In addition to, female HHs, elderly persons at risk, persons with disabilities, HHs living in in overcrowded conditions, HHs without any income resources. the number of kits, kit components and targeted locations have been determined. Worth to mention that the project is in line with HNO 2021 and the NFI sector strategic objective (SO1 and SO2) and priorities:
Strategic Objective 1: Provide life-saving humanitarian assistance to the most vulnerable people with an emphasis on those in areas with high severity of needs.
Strategic Objective 2: Reinforce an enabling protection environment and community cohesion by improving housing and related community/public infrastructure.

5 - Implementation of activities

RSRP responses is with coodination with the sector response strategy adhering to the needs-based approach, identification of PIN within the framework of HNO21 to sub-district level, and adhering to strategic objectives(SO1&SO2). RSRP proposes the activities by direct implementation only, the main pillars followed to achieve the proposed responses in coordination with the MoLA, STD, sector and other relevant stakeholders. -The hiring processes.-Registration through local community and social media. Identify 232280 beneficiaries by KOBO according to vulnerability selection criteria considering female HHs, elderly persons at risk, persons with disabilities, HHs (living in overcrowded conditions,without income resources).-Identify 600 houses needed rehabilitation by KOBO through assessment according to vulnerability selection criteria considering female HHs, elderly persons at risk, persons with disabilities, HHs (living in overcrowded conditions, without income resources).-Procurement process. -Distribution of 32,571 kits through door-to-door visits with consider, collecting tokens, and signing the receipt form by the beneficiary. After a month of completing, RSRP will be conducting PDM by a questionnaire form/KOBO to assessing beneficiaries’ access, all considering safety measures covid-19.-Rehabilitation of 600 selected damaged houses.-Regular field visits will be conducted to ensure the quality.-Conducting training for 1,000 stakeholders. - The accountability to the affected population through a complaint mechanism and the regular evaluation. The handing over process will be conducted by our field staff in coordination with local authority and the stockholders and according to the UN/RRI/ local authorities’ rules.
