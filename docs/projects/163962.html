Project Summary

Run 7 PHCs in Idleb, Afrin, Euphrates Sheild, and Ras ALein areas, & running 5 mobile clinics nearby PHCs to reach all beneficiaries in surrounding areas due to difficulties in access to PHCs, this 5 mobile clinics will run with additional services to covering NCD needs, in addition to 5 PSS teams.
Running 3 Dialysis centers in areas in need in Idleb, Afrin, and Ras ALein areas.
Running 3 Physiotherapy centers in addition to PSS services in areas in need in Idleb, Afrin, and Ras ALein areas.

4 - Needs

This project will contribute to addressing the needs and objectives of the Health cluster through the following activities: • Support second line facilities to be able to respond to the needs of armed conflict-affected population during displacements by supporting 7 PHCs, 5 MCs, 5 NCD clinics, 5 PSS clinics, 3 Physiotherapy centers, 3 dialysis centers. given that Idleb, Afrin, Euphrates shield, and Ras Alien district as a whole is one of the most likely potential places for IDPs resettlement. • Support 5 healthcare facilities to provide mental health services following the guidelines of MH Gap to increase access to mental health services. Al-Ameen has good knowledge and understanding of the key issues through its active membership and participation in the Health Cluster, partners, local health authorities.Al-Ameen access to Idelb, Rural Aleppo, Afrin and ‘Euphrates Shield’ and Ras Alein areas, established with agreements with local authorities that allow our staff to implement successful interventions. Given that Al-Ameen is currently one of the main providers of health care in the targeted areas.

5 - Implementation of activities

1- Run 7 PHCs level 2 in Ariha, Afrin, Albab, And Ras Alein for 12 months. These PHCs will provide pediatric, GP, and midwifery services for people in need, as well as, cover at least 50% of the drugs through in-kind donations from other partners.and run 3 PT equipped centers in Idleb and Ras Alein for 12 months, will provide physiotherapy and PSS services for disabilities, and provide them by physiotherapy assistive devices, and will respecting TWG criteria. in addition to running 3 dialysis centers in Idleb, Albab, And Ras ALein 5 CHWs per PHC will work to provide public awareness for the targeted communities as recommended in the WHO new module for community management and mobilization2 PSS worker in PT centers will provide beneficiaries by psychosocial support2- support the facilities' staff with salaries and raising their capacities by training 3- Support the facilities with drugs and medical supplies needed. 4- support the referral pathway towards higher management facilities, by collaboration with referral system in the area, as well as, EWARN and data collection.Alameen as an active member in the health cluster and task-forces of Trauma and PT, Dialysis, NCD, SRH. In addition to Alameen part of Afrin, & Tal Abyad and Ras Alein Taskforce. with direct collaboration with the partners in field (NGOs, local authorities, and health cluster members). Alameen already existed in targeted areas and has the facilities with good conditions matching standards conditions of MoH. the rehabilitation process was done in 2019 and started running most of the suggested facilities.Al Ameen as an active member of both the Health Cluster in Gaziantep (as well as several working groups) has been coordinating with a range of other actors to ensure the optimal use of resources and to guarantee that there is no duplication in interventions or utilized funds. The project will be coordinated with health cluster partners, rehab work-group partners, local health authorities.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

During the planning phase, we identified the following risks to our project:a. Quality control of rehab assistive devices and equipment Mitigation procedures: following the Rehab Working Group recommendations about approved vendors regarding technical specifications, service support etc. B. Accidents during outreach activities Mitigation procedures: - First aid procedures and tools are available, and staff will be trained in case accidents. Our field offices are responsible for communicating with the local stakeholders in order to facilitate our teams’ activities. C. Armed conflict in targeted areas: Mitigation procedures: as one of this project’s components is to support second line facility, our staff will be trained on evacuation plans using scenarios developed with the Health Cluster. Our warehouses are located in relatively safe areas and we supply our facilities based on monthly needs rather than full packages.Al-Ameen has a duty of care policy for all our employees in both Turkey and Syria and this extends to all of the above activities, in addition to the broader duty of care it holds for beneficiaries.Alameen durıng project designing and implementation involving beneficiaries and other field stakeholders by putting the real needs and better response and implementation of activities. The beneficiaries' feedback mechanism is respected by either calling on complaints number or by complaints box.
