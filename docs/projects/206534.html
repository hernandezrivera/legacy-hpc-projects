1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

IOM will continue ensuring safe access to emergency and sustainable WASH solutions in the Northern Province of Mozambique. Building upon its multi-sectoral expertise and with an area-based approach, IOM will target underserved areas in coordination with Camp Coordination and Camp Management (CCCM), Health, Shelter, MHPSS-Protection and Displacement Tracking Matrix (DTM) while complementing and supporting critical gaps highlighted by the Wash Cluster (WC). Solutions will align with the cluster and response strategic plans including solar pumps, sustainable water networks, wastewater treatment(latrines and decentralized systems), and faecal sludge management. Hygiene promotion will strive for behavioral change by setting hygiene sessions, hygiene committees in sites and distribution of hygiene kits (including menstrual hygiene management - MHM), while ensuring GBV mitigation across the programming. In line with CCCM site planning operations, IOM WASH will deliver in sites and site-like settings in Ibo, Ciudade de Pemba, Macomia, Metuge, Mocimboa de Praia, Montepuez, Muidumbe, Nangade, Quissanga . IOM will continue promote partnership with local organization to ensure further outreach and capacity building for further access and support to the sector needs. As per IOM needs assessment, new plots are under constant development across Cabo Delgado. Critical areas such as Muidumbe and Macomia will be supported incoordination WC and CCCM through area-based approach.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

WATER: Construction and/or rehabilitation of wells/boreholes with handpump and/or powered by solar energy, and centralized or decentralized water networks, and emergency water treatment and distribution setup: distribution of point-of-use water treatment materials (training and monitoring of use provided); creation and training of gender inclusive water committees for water management.SANITATION: construction of emergency and improved HH latrines, and when appropriate promoting the community lead total sanitation (CLTS) approach; construction of bathing facilities and laundry areas ensuring proper drainage; in collaboration with the shelter unit ensure that the WASH services are resilient, environmentally friendly and making use of local construction methodologies; provision of faecal sludge management solution; provision for environmentally friendly and sustainable solid waste management solutions and vector control activities. HYGIENE: Promotion of hygiene messages though the support of trained “activistas” that will convey messages in sessions, mass campaigns and house to house visits. IOM will make use of mass media and IEC materials to support the dissemination of messages, which will prioritize, among other topics, Cholera prevention and MHM. The hygiene promotion activities will promote the participation of women and adolescent in the IDP governances’ mechanisms. In collaboration with DTM, IOM will perform base line assessments to guide the interventions. Similarly, coordination with IOMs health and protection units we will ensure the development of appropriate hygiene messages. IOM will procure and distribute hygiene kits and other essential WASH NFIs (such as water purification tablets for cholera prevention activities), in adherence with the standards setup by the cluster. The WASH activities will ensure to be sustainable and scalable whenever possible, within the coordination mechanism and ensuring collaboration and synergies with other partners and government.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

In coordination with the Protection-MHPSS and CCCM Programmes, IOM mainstreams protection in all activities. The existing teams have been trained on protection mainstreaming in cooperation with CP and GBV AoRs. The newly recruited community workers will also be provided with relevant training, refresher sessions and IEC materials. IOM will continue conducting community consultation throughout the project to meet protection needs connected to WASH. Also, as part of new activities, OIM will promote training of the committees on protection principles to be applied to WASH services. Furthermore, IOM will consider safety and security issues that can lead to GBV in the sites. To prevent further harm, in consultation with the communities, IOM will deliver WASH services with increased safety for most vulnerable in a culturally appropriate way. In line with that, IOM will consider accessibility issues not only for persons with disabilities but also persons with chronic diseases, elderly, and marginalized (or at risk of marginalization) individuals. In addition, IOM Protection-MHPSS Programme will provide feedback through its field teams and will support WASH programme in referral of persons in need

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The participation of people specifically women and elderly is taking into account in the design phase of projects and also during the participatory design. Furthermore, the programme in coordination with the CCCM ensure the consultations of the different governance's committees specifically the women's committee. Furthermore, in the post-distribution or evaluation surveys this groups are ensured participation, to have a complete representation of the population.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The identification of people with disabilities among the vulnerable will be done in partnership with CCCM, DTM, and Protection-MHPSS. In several sites WASH will hold consultations with the people with dissability committees to identify needs and tailored the solutions accordingly. The solutions will ensure accessibility and equality, some of the solutions proposed is to form not just gender inclusive water committees but also ensure participants with disabilities, hence having an accurate representation of the sites. Furthermore, following the area-based approach implemented by IOM, solutions will when possible be in coordination with Shelter, CCCM Protection and Health Programmes while also including input and feedback from the beneficiaries. Furthermore, IOM will collaborate with already identified partners specialized in WASH for people with dissabilities across the response.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Solutions that will be implemented will ensure environmentally friendly approach and local sourced materials. Furthermore, a reduce, recycle and reuse system will be promoted for WASH activities. Rapid environmental screening and environmental impact assessments will be done in several sites, depending on the WASH activities defined. Also, OIM will closely follow the environmental regulations implemented by the country, while at the same time maintain coordination with relevant authorities.
