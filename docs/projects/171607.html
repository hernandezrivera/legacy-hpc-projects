Project Summary

According to FSNUA, an estimated 2.2 million people are expected to experience Crisis (IPC Phase 3) or Emergency (IPC Phase 4) outcomes through September and are urgently in need of humanitarian food assistance and interventions to prevent high levels of acute malnutrition. The project targeted locations ( Bari, Nugaal, Sool and Sanaag) are among the worst hit areas by recurrent droughts combined by locust impact and limited humanitarian support. Recent flooding in Gardo and surrounding village have also impacted the overall livelihood of Gardo community and generally Puntland people. According to our recent assessment in Sool and Sanaag, there are alarming needs in those areas when it comes food access, few partners notably WFP and some other International partners have so far been recorded supporting few communities in those areas. Dispute/border and remote areas in Sool and Sanaag remain fragile and of high priority. Local communities in Lasqorey and Buuhoodle respectively reported food insecurity, access challenges and less aid. Despite the good rains most of the grazing have now dried up and nomadic started migrating looking better pasture. The he assessment also shown a deterioration in levels of food security, first driven by drought conditions and then by the Locust impact. Covid-19 has also worsened the situation while putting people on risk, particularly those rely on remittance and casual employment. 90% of those rely on remittance from abroad reported great decline while some not received any penny from their relatives since Covid-19 outbark started. Casual workers are also experiencing great challenges and decline of casual work opportunities.
In lieu of the above, OTP is proposing need-based interventions to lessen those crises. While aiming to reduce acute humanitarian needs and excess mortality among the most vulnerable by providing life-saving and sustaining integrated assistance and Increasing resilience capacity of at-risk communities and promote complementary interventions of development partners in social services. Proposed interventions include A. Unconditional transfers and conditional transfers (cash-for-work for small-scale infrastructure repairs) targeting 1500 HHs, B. Provision of seasonally appropriate agricultural inputs (seeds, farm tools, training, land preparation and irrigation support) for 1000 HHs.

What is the justification for using cash or vouchers instead of in kind?

The cash gives the beneficiaries the flexibility to purchase food items of their choice and also improves the local economy since it will be used in the local market

Explain how the project design considered market functionality ?

most of the households income are severely impacted by recurring emergencies especially droughts and cyclones which has had an impact on their purchasing power of the basic commodities. most of the basic food items are available in the local markers however, due to low purchasing power the beneficiaries face a challenge in purchasing them. the cash will provide the affected population with purchasing power needed to buy basic goods and improve the local economy

Mitigation Measures

Despite the food insecurity objective of the project, OTP is aiming to make the project more protection centered while each and every activity of the project will be designed in way that minimizes the protection problems both at induvial level and community level. Rights-based approach will be applied inline with international laws and protection principles. OTP will ensure that activates target the most vulnerable people with less access to human rights and those with special protection needs without exposing them further risks to enhance their safety, dignity and promote their human needs. OTP has set of mechanisms meant to uphold the accountability and protection of effected people. Risk analyses will be carried out to identify potential risks and measure in each of the risks identified. OTP is well prepared to tackle any kind of exclusion that some of the communities or any segments of the people might face during and prior implementation. Exclusion and marginalization is common in our target communities and might happen at different stages, However given our long experience in the target area, we have set clear guidelines to minimize such risks, these are include community entry guidelines, OTP has developed and adapted very clear community entry policy that allows its staff to avoid such risks and encourages communities to participate any OTP intervention to the maximum level. OTP as local organization with grassroots to local community conducts community mapping at onset of every implementation to help them to understand the segments and arrangements of each specific community. Through the mapping, OTP develops mobilization approach, this could be gathering or meeting with specific segments. The other important element that helps communities/committees to avoid any exclusion is the set up of the project relief committees. This is bloodshot area where minorities can be easily marginalized and excluded from the decision-making process. Hence the OTP community mapping protocol/tool helps us to understand different segments and the inclusion required. Women and other people with special needs are also excluded, however OTP ensures that women are fully represented at every level and they are given Hight priority when it comes to committee’s formation and minimum 50% of any committees should be women. Influence from respective authorities is another challenge, however this might not a case to the local organizations as they are already from the community. OTP will also ensure the protection is mainstreamed throughout the project activities. All activities under this proposal will be designed in line with protection principles. Mitigation measures for all kind of protection problems including physical, sexual and gender-based violence will be developed and applied such as referrals, mobilizations and complementing with other projects.
