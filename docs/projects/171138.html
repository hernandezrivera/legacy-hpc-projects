Project Summary

According to JMCNA 2020, Shelter is the first priority need of more than half of the population. The scale of shelter and NFI needs in 2021 is expected to increase in comparison to the needs in 2020 mainly due to the prolonged flooding season that damaged the shelter and caused the loss of NFIs, displacements due to conflict, drought and forced eviction and population unable to repair and construct shelter and to buy basic non-food items due to the poor economic situation that has been further worsened by COVID-19.
In this context, Alight (formally ARC) is proposing a response plan of pre-positioning/distribution of basic Non-Food Items (NFIs) and Emergency shelter kits and construction of transitional shelters for 2000 most vulnerable IDP, returnees and poor host community households (12,000 persons) Jubaland (Lower Juba) and Hirshabelle regions (Hiraan and Middle Shabelle) of South- Central Somalia and Sool region (Las Anood, Talex, Xudun) of Somaliland, where ARC established presence and with critical humanitarian needs driven by climatic shocks, displacements and conflict. The proposed Shelter project will be implemented in close coordination with the relevant authorities in each target region, as well as SHELTER cluster partners and stakeholders in each area focusing on improving the physical protection and dignity of IDPs, refugee returnees and poor host communities across Somalia and Somaliland through provision of the proposed activities in the target areas/regions. In particular, Alight will target 500 HHs (approx. 3000 beneficiaries) for transitional shelters, 1500 (approx. 9000 beneficiaries) for emergency Shelter kits and 1500 HHs (approx. 9000 beneficiaries) for NFI kits distribution across the target locations through in-kind modalities. Necessary Post Distribution monitoring (PDM) and Post construction Monitoring (PCM) will be carried out during implementation of the project activities. In the onset of the project implementation, Alight will undertake shelter needs assessment so to determine households that would need NFI, ESK and Transitional shelters to avoid overlap of service provision. Alight will ensure transparent and fair project beneficiaries’ selection and registration in close coordination with relevant authorities including community groups and District authorities. Before any selection process, a beneficiary selection criterion will be developed in collaboration with project stakeholders that would guide the selection process. Vulnerable groups within the community including female headed HHs, orphans, chronically ill, aged and child headed households will be prioritized. 20% to 30% Poor host community members will be selected while 10% -15% Persons With Disabilities (PWDs) will also be considered to ensure 'DO NO HARM' and other humanitarian principles including humanity, neutrality, impartiality. Alight will use an electronic mobile data collection tool, Kobo toolbox, which provides an accurate and real time data. After completion of the distribution and construction of kits and shelters, a Post Distribution Monitoring (PDM) exercises for NFIs and ESKs and Post Construction Monitoring (PCM) exercises for Transitional Shelters will be conducted to analyze outcomes, the beneficiaries’ level of satisfaction and draw lessons learnt for future programming. On Feedback mechanism, ARC’s unique online platform known as “KUJA KUJA’’ feedback Monitoring is designed to enhance Accountability through community feedback mechanism: ARC builds community trust during the project implementation by committing NOT to ignore concerns of the community, respond their questions and stand against corruption and Use of power or force. As part of our accountability measure, ARC gathers community feedback regularly using KujaKuja Platform. Feedback is often gathered and analyzed on weekly basis.

Project Activities

Cognizant of urgent shelter needs in the proposed target areas, Alight/ARC proposes shelter/NFI emergency response interventions that would provide much needed NFI kits and emergency shelters to the target beneficiaries in the proposed locations/districts. The main activities include: 1. Provision of 1500 Emergency shelter kits to the target beneficiaries in the target locations through in kind modality in collaboration with project stakeholders and District authorities. 2. Distribution of NFI Kits to vulnerable Households in the proposed target locations. Through the proposed intervention, Alight/ARC will provide and distribute NFI kits to 1500 HHs (9,000 persons) in the target locations. 3. Construction of 500 Transitional shelters in the target locations targeting 3,000 vulnerable individuals. Before the construction of shelters, Alight/ARC will ensure security of tenure for a determined period in close coordination with the district authorities, landowners and beneficiary households to mitigate against forced evictions and waste of resources. Settlement planning and/or re-planning will be an integral part of the process, enabling safe and secure access to essential services and facilities.4. Post Distribution Monitoring. Alight/ARC will conduct a post distribution monitoring (PDM) to cover 100% of the households that have received both Emergency shelters and NFI kits to ensure accountability, appropriateness of materials and effectiveness of distribution and use of the distributed kits. Data will be dis aggregated sex and age to evaluate whether specific needs of women, girls, boys and men are met.

Mitigation Measures

1. Risk that beneficiaries are not accessing/using complaint and feedback mechanisms- ARC/Alight will ensure that beneficiaries will be involved in all phases of intervention planning, monitoring, and evaluating using qualitative interviews. ARC will ensure that monitoring and use of field data is done in compliance with ethical values of data including protection of rights of respondents, impartiality and fairness to truth. - On Feedback mechanism, ARC’s unique online platform known as “KUJA KUJA’’ feedback Monitoring is designed to enhance Accountability through community feedback mechanism. As part of our accountability measure, ARC gathers community feedback regularly using KujaKuja Platform. anonymous.- ARC shall Organize awareness raising sessions for the feedback mechanism so that beneficiaries know and understand how it works.2. Risk of interference from external stakeholders in the impartial and needs-based selection of beneficiaries thus negatively affecting social cohesion and sparking intercommunity conflict over access to resources- ARC Involve and consult all categories and layers of the affected population in identifying and responding to shelter/NFI needs. In addition, ARC will closely coordinate with relevant government authorities not limited to IDP stakeholders and committees while undertaking beneficiary selection process. This would reduce risk of IDP gatekeepers taking over the process.- To enhance accountability, ARC will conduct community mobilization and community-based targeting processes in line with ARC’s framework for Community Based Targeting process. This will ensure that community members are aware of project deliverables (caseloads, project components & activities and are in a position to follow up on delivery processes.- ARC Response team will engage the community and committee representative to play an active role in identifying solutions and in the decision-making processes that affect them, so as to promote a sense of ownership, build their self-esteem and improve the relevance and sustainability of the response.3. Risk of land disputes and tenure insecurity in targeted project area- Just like in other regions, evictions also happen in the target areas. As a result of eviction, often IDPs lose their shelters that make them more vulnerable. ARC/Alight will ensure that the land tenure security, certified by relevant local authorities, is provided and guaranteed for a significant amount of time (at least 5 years) prior to any construction activities. - ARC Response team will engage the community and committee representatives to play an active role in identifying issues and disputes and help in providing solutions at the community level.- ARC will also closely work with relevant Government authorities including District Authorities and Land Authorities to develop land disputes resolution mechanisms.4. Risk that marginalized groups, persons with disabilities, women, boys and girls, do not participate in consultations or decision-making processes for the project or are excluded from humanitarian services/assistance/activities- To enhance accountability and information sharing, ARC will conduct community mobilization and project awareness sessions reaching out to all community groups including marginalized and most vulnerable groups – PWD and women with necessary information on project deliverables and activities.- Alight will conduct project launch/inception meeting that would bring together all project stakeholders including such marginalized groups. Person with Disabilities and women will be represented. - During beneficiary selection exercise, 10% -15% Persons With Disabilities (PWDs) will be considered to ensure 'DO NO HARM' and other humanitarian principles including humanity, neutrality, impartiality. Gender will be mainstreamed into the project through the specific consideration of the different needs of women, men, boys and girls during the design of activities.
