Project Summary

HRO aims to continue providing GBV services for IDPs & HC in our WGSSs established in Idleb (Jisr Ash Shugur & Darkosh) & in Aleppo (Afrin), besides planning to establish new WGSS in Idleb (Harim, Salqin, & Kafr Jales) & through outreach visits to the widows’ camps (1 in Janudiyeh & 2 in Darkosh).
The services include GBV prevention activities such as awareness-raising sessions, empowerment activities, training, & campaigns, besides emergency response activities in response to the new IDPs through providing PFA, referral, sharing information, etc. based on GBV toolkit including early marriage and sexual abuse, reproductive health & information session to all age groups & genders, especially the most vulnerable groups in all above-mentioned areas & through outreach visits to the widows’ camps (1 in Badama 2 in Janudiyeh & 2 in Darkosh) and other camps to target the beneficiaries with services such as awareness raising and GBV full component activities (empowerment, prevention, response & mitigation activities), where some of them will receive case fund, Micro Grant, vocational training (in the WGSSs or in widows’ camps). Also, there will be a capacity building activity for non GBV actors (humanitarian actors & WLOs, including camp managers, volunteer teams, women’s committees, teachers, vaccination and nutrition teams, medical staff, food distribution and sanitation staff) on GBV basics and risk mitigation trainings, to reduce the GBV against women, in addition to establishing women committees in these camps and building their capacities, and to ensure that a greater number of people have access to the information related to GBV services in their communities.
HRO will implement capacity-building training for WLOs, CVA through case management, IPA and case fund, referring survivors at risk to other organizations and ensure that GBV risks are mitigated. The planned number of centers is (6) where women and girls can get involved in the activities and interact with their peers and to gain access to lifesaving GBV services, including case management, referrals, PSS for survivors focusing on vulnerable groups such as divorced, widows, PwD and elderly women (over 50) by ensuring their accessibility and inclusion through providing transportation and adapting the WGSS centers to fit their needs especially PwD including installing the needed tools and facilities, besides training the staff on including PwD and elderly in the WGSS activities. It is worth noting that HRO will be using GBVIMS to upload the data of GBV survivors and participate in issuing quarterly reports in coordination with the sub-cluster. The GBVIMS focal point in HRO will provide guidance and supervision for case managers by receiving online training on this system and then its application and filling any gap that is monitored by the system in terms of reports and sessions conducted by case managers. The training will also be applied for the rest of case managers as a second step to include all HRO staff and case managers in this system. Also, we will target male survivors of sexual violence with specialized GBV services. Moreover, adolescent girls will be targeted with Girls dreams and AMAL initiative programs targeting girls (13-19).
Throughout the project period, we will target 35,485 beneficiaries through the project’s activities including 23,904 Females, 11,381 Males (254 Young Children, 11,176 Children, 20,875 Adults, 3,180 Elderly) among them 1,740 PwD, through 9 centers including 36 protection staff (12 outreach teams, 15 case managers, 3 supervisors).
Also, HRO will ensure smooth coordination with the protection NGO partners, local councils, stakeholders, and camp managers throughout the project period.

3 - Needs

According to GBVIMS+ Q3 2022, currently 1.8 million out of 2.8 M IDPs in NWS are living in over 1,400 camps and unplanned, self-settled sites. 84.4% of reported incidents involved IDP survivors, almost all of them have experienced different forms of GBV during displacement, 89% of classified incidents were subjected to intimate partner violence, 17% of communities mentioned that SEA is an issue for women and 14% for girls.According to HPC GBV Analysis (Nov 2022), 58% of HHs mentioned that women show signs of distress in the last 3 months while it is 28% for girls, 84% of communities identify child marriage as an issue for girls, and 72% of them communities identify financial hardships as a key reason behind cases of early marriage. 48%of women and girls live in locations with community movement restrictions for females. According to HNO Draft 2023, 51.2% of surveyed women and 37.8% of girls are in need to GBV assistance, 63% reported that domestic violence has become more common. Also, many women and girls reported an increasing trend of technology-facilitated sexual violence. According to GBV Main findings of HNO 2022, widowed or divorced women and girls reported Forced re-marriage in some cases, higher levels of sexual harassment and exploitation in relation to obtaining shelter, while older women said that they were exposed to neglect by family members, perceived as an economic burden.

4 - Implementation of activities

After preparing the project logistics & recruiting procedures, staff will be trained on GBV basics, GBV Case Management, inclusion of PwD, then we will conduct: GBV prevention & mitigation including empowerment activities, GBV case management, training of humanitarian actors.GBV awareness raising sessions, Life Skills Activities (Girls dreams program & AMAL initiative), Free spaces with focus on vulnerable groups, in addition to providing Vocational Training & Information/sensitization sessions about GBV related services.As for the response activities: Specialized GBV services through GBVIMS & micro grant within the WGSSs, communities, & especially widows’ camps, in addition to implementing safety audits in the widows’ camps relying on the GBV SC toolkits and guidelines. Also, there will be a capacity building activity for humanitarian actors & local authorities & WLOs on GBV basics & risk mitigation trainings.M&E plan will include a target-based performance, ITT, regular site visits by the M&E Field assistants to verify & document project implementation progress including routine meetings with key informants at the local level to generate qualitative data on the effectiveness of project interventions. Depending on beneficiary consent forms, visual evidence that includes photos & videos & MoVs of project activities will be documented. Exit interview questionnaires will be administered to measure the satisfaction of beneficiaries with services being delivered.
