Project Summary

• Needs.
At HNO 2024 FAS PIN is 15.4 million people. Agricultural production still does not meet the full national need, this applies to most crops production, as well as livestock production. The drivers of low agriculture production include a continuous deteriorating economic situation, a decline in both the exchange rate and purchasing power of most people involved in agriculture-based livelihoods, high prices of agricultural production inputs and low quality of available seeds, weather anomalies, water scarcity, destruction of irrigation systems and canals, scarcity of energy supplies, for various farm and off-farm activities, which impacts on supply chains and transportation costs, leading to an increase in production costs and accordingly a decrease in farmers households income. Nutrition is usually neglected in crisis times, it is reported that one of the most common negative copying strategies is reducing number and quality of meals. Focusing on nutrition sensitive agriculture and good agriculture practices are very important to boost local food production, increase income and restore livelihood, diversified nutritious food and food security.
• Intervention, Implementation of activities.
Output 1: Increased 55,600 H.H food production (farming H.H). Activities: 1)Desing the support packages based on local need and nutrition value. 2)selecting beneficiaries. 3)Contracting IP. 4)Procurement and distribution production inputs (assorted seeds/seedlings, micro irrigation inputs, fertilizers, tools, etc.) or poultry production inputs (15 egg laying hens, feed, vitamin and minerals as well as vaccination against the mist common diseases). 5) training for trainers for technicians to facilitate community-level training. 6) training the targeted H.H using the farmer field school approach (FFSA). 7)Create awareness on basic nutrition and health. Output 2: Increased 39,400 H.H income generating through agriculture related livelihood support. Activities: 1)Identify viable agriculture-based livelihood, based on community consultations, market access, location and situation of natural resources, 2)Contracting IP. 3) beneficiaries Selecting. 4)Procurement and distribution of production inputs (mushroom kits, beehives, food processing tools). 5)Train the targeted H.H using (FFSA), focusing on food safety, quality, and marketing.
• Protection Risk Analysis
1) Risk: Beneficiaries being targeted due being selected, community tension arising from beneficiaries selection. Mitigation: Different categories of affected population based on their specific needs and clear selection criteria. 2) Risk: Inclusion and exclusion errors. Mitigation: local beneficiary selection committee; putting in place clear and precise guidance on selection criteria and process; effective communication for guidance. 3) Risk of diversion of assistance/assets. Mitigation: community-based beneficiary selection and monitoring. The project considered protection elements, in line with do-no-harm principles. Where IDPs are selected, representatives from the host communities will be included, to limit possible frictions, and special attention will be for returnees helping them in their re-settlement and resuming their production activities. An accountability system will also be put in place to provide communities with a mechanism to express their grievances.
