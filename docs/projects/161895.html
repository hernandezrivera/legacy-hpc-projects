Project Summary

IRC Nigeria will continue implementing two education projects in Northeast Nigeria in 2020; DFID-funded EiE+ Project and GIZ-funded ASSESS Project. The DFID EiE+ project aims at reaching 45,000 children through non-formal education program (15,000 out of school children); and through tutorial program(30,000 low performing pupils in primary schools for improved academic performances) in Borno and Yobe States until March 2021. The GIZ ASSESS(Adamawa State School-Based Education System Strengthening) Project intends to ensure the 50 primary schools in Adamawa State provide quality education, and are safe, functional and responsive.
