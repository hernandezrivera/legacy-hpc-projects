Project Summary

needs
NFI support during winter is vital for coping with harsh conditions. REPORTS show the varying NFI needs by gender & age. The estimated number of PiN has increased from 5.7 million in 2023 to 6.2 million in 2024. Overall, this is almost 20% increase in PiN from 2023. The HNO 2024 also confirms the importance of energy & winterization considering similar interventions as emergency (HNO 2024). It is expected that during 2023 and especially under the economic downfall Frequent conflicts and displacement, access to shelter & NFI will be more difficult for the people in need. Number of shelter PiN is 6.8 million. 34% of total population in Syria are currently living in damaged or inadequate shelters (HNO 2024 SNFI chapter) . Adequate shelter conditions are vital to maintain physical health, mental well-being, and to promote and sustain learning and educational opportunity (HNO 2024). One of the main challenges in Syria is electricity and relying on alternative energy is a solution to cope with the current power shortage. Targeted areas under this project have severity score 2-4 according to the PiN severity score sheet shared by the sector. ,yet their winterization score is high and TAKATOF SHABABE is intended to do winterization interventions IN Deir ezzor
Implementation of activities
The proposed winter intervention will use the most appropriate modality – in-kind distributions, commodity vouchers, or cash through Financial Service Providers, depending on context. . A total of 5000 HHs are targeted during 2024. Seasonal voucher assistance is for vulnerable families to access core relief items to meet their additional needs during winter months. Vouchers are exchanged for fixed quantity & quality of specified goods from vendors at local markets. In kind distributions of multifuel heaters and fuel will be distributed at the start of the winter.
also we will provide & install seasonal assistant to the most vulnerable HHs in dear ezzor. Monitoring is the responsibility of the country based Manager of Program Quality, supervising MEAL officers the program. The collected data will be sex & age disaggregated. we will select beneficiaries according to set criteria, distribution is through distribution points and if needed door to door. After each distribution does PDM and wherever possible organizes focus group discussions
