Project Summary

Binaa seeks to sustain the existing Protection help desks in 3 camps in Harnboush, Sheikh Bahr, and Bhorta community in Idlib and Aleppo Governorate, in addition to establishing one Protection Help desk in a new camp that will be established in the Bazagha community in 2022 /Aleppo Governorate. the aim is to support vulnerable children, women & girls at-risk groups, and affected population to easily access available services within the targeted areas.
the project is designed to integrated protection services (GP,GBV,CP) in 4 targeted sites for 2 years through providing quality protection services, with a focus on community-based
approaches, including awareness-raising, community initiatives, and psychosocial well-being
through help desks and outreach mechanisms for 1850 BNFs( 964 W,906 M) in 2022 and 2300 BNFs (1175 W,1126 M)in 2023.
The project is designed to improve access to quality and life-saving GBV response
services for GBV survivors and W&G at risk and put measures in place to prevent and mitigate risks of GBV through providing referral services to GBV related, medical, and specialized PSS services according to the referral pathway mechanism provided by GBV SC, plus BINAA's Protection officers will develop the GBV service mapping referral pathways, and coordinate with partners who provide these services on ground. Training of 24 GBV workers, in line with GBV SC (GBV SOPs, PSS, Women empowerment, and CM) and participation in the training organized by the GBV SC when available to ensure alignment with SC GBV standards.
And enhance strategies to empower women and girls, with a particular focus on most at
risk groups (adolescent girls, divorced and widowed W&Gs, W&Gs with disabilities and
older women) to prevent GBV and to change negative attitudes and believes that foster
harmful social norms, and COVID-19 awareness-raising interventions (including on
associated GBV risks and GBV key messages)for 2909 BNFs(826 M,1199W, 224 B,660
G) at 4 sites and 3400 BNFs in 2023.
As well as promote GBV risk mitigation into all aspects of the humanitarian response
through training 45 non-GBV humanitarian workers on GBV risk mitigation according to
(IASC GBV guidelines, GBV SOPs).Development GBV safety audits in the camps and
establishment focal points (IASC GBV guidelines).
the project is designed to increase and more equitable access for 4400 BNFs in 2023 and 4233 BNFs in 2022,2347 adults(1407W,940 M), 1886 children( 855 Boys,1031 girls) to quality child protection
interventions in line with the Child Protection Minimum Standards in Humanitarian Action,
through Provide community-based child protection by engaging 1700 children in non-specialized MHPSS services through structured child protection programs, and 223 (133 W,90 M) in parenting programs, awareness-raising, and community events to prevent and respond to child
protection issues for 2000 Adults, providing specialized child protection services, case
management for 86 (G, B) and Referral of child protection cases to specialized services
(child protection needs) according to the referral pathway mechanism provides by
CP SC, plus BINAA's Protection officers will develop the CP service mapping referral path
ways, and coordinate with partners who provide these services on the ground. Integrating of
PwDs with the community by a specialist for PwDs and integrate them into activities where
the centers are on flat ground, surrounded by a fence, the entrance is paved, and all the
camps are paved, and with special latrines' with hand railing and ramp. Training of 24 CP
workers in line with child protection minimum standards.
The implementing activities will be according to the Operational guidance note for GP, CP, and
GBV Activities in COVID 19.
As BINAA will take into consideration all precautions and procedures related to protection
from COVID-19 epidemic following the clusters guidelines and instructions .As Binaa will reporting to monthly Protection cluster 4Ws.

4 - Needs

In NW Syria, increasingly complex and inter-linked protection needs remain high in camps,collective shelters, and protracted displacement.HNO 2022 estimated 14.114m PiN protection, where older persons and PwD are often at heightened risk of forms of violence, abuse, neglect, or exploitation. Lack or loss of civil documentation was reported by 76 %of communities.HNO 2022 estimated 19% GBV AoR PiN still in acute need in Idleb and 21% in Aleppo.Voices from Syria 2022 reported that women and girls wished for a greater number of WGSS: “Furthermore, they should increase the number of women protection centers within the camp“, over 2021, GBV actors were only able to reach 6.5% (523 out of communities).Binaa FGD findings in the assessed camps:IDPs highlighted the need to provide PSS, support for GBV survivors, WGSS, and empowerment activities. where GBV, particularly sexual harassment, early marriage, and domestic violence continues to be pervasive in the lives of women and girls.HNO 2022 estimated children comprise almost half of the affected population 6.01 m children (55.8%% B,44.2%G) as psychosocial distress among children is reported in 26% of surveyed HHs, Binaa FGD’s key findings in the assessed camps:63%KIs highlighted that there are cases of unaccompanied or separated children and the high rate of children engaging in work that prevents them from enrollment in schools,and caregivers suffering from severe PS problems that may lead to the child’s exposure neglect,violence.

10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

BINAA is a member of the PSEA network and aligns the PSEA intervention with thePSEA network guidance. As (PSEA) is at the core of BINAA responses and all BINAAstaff must comply with. BINAA has a policy of zero tolerance for sexualexploitation. BINAA field team is trained on communication approaches, i.e. how topresent the program transparently in a way that mitigates conflict, anticipatingperceptions of unfairness or other issues that could create conflict, and managing expectations. Risk1-the population, including victims, does not reportallegations of SEA, which results in the BINAA being unable to refer the allegationto investigation, hold personnel to account and assist victims” Likelihood:Medium.Impact: High. mitigate SEA Risks:Sensitise community about PSEA and how toreport misconduct according to PSEA awareness materials according to PSEAnetwork dataset. Risk2-Exploitation or abuse by staff members/service providerswhen accessing assistance and services. Likelihood:Medium. Impact: High.Mitigating measures: -integrating PSEA in BINAA policies and making sure that allpersonnel read and signed code of conduct, and that all contracts withpartners/suppliers/contractors include clauses on PSEA. -Regularly train staff onPSEA policy-. - BINAA will ensure that complaint mechanisms for reporting sexualexploitation and abuse are accessible (Complaints boxes, WhatsApp, email) andthose focal points for receiving complaints understand how to discharge their duties.
