Project Summary

This project has as main purpose to ensure access to Health services (working on availability of drugs) and access to MHPSS in the aftermath of the Beirut explosion. The project focus on an integrated response from awareness to provision of services and referral.
MHPSS interventions will consist in door-to door outreach activities, space in Quarantina to provide the MHPSS, awareness sessions, hotline support.
The project targets 22.425 people, (10.968M,11.457F)

Plan fields

1 - Needs

- Lack of essential medication at PHC level (the damage in the central drug warehouse, along with the expected increase of demand of PHC services, estimated in 25% due to the impoverishment of the population (according to the UN Economic and Social Commission for Western Asia (ESCWA), the headcount poverty rate in Lebanon has almost doubled from 28 per cent in 2019 to 55 per cent in May 2020, and the people living in extreme poverty increased from 8 to 23 per cent (750,000 persons)).- Access to health services affected by the severe damage on 4 PHC, and 19 with partial damage, plus 3 major hospitals heavily affected and substantial damages of 2 hospitals, and for persons in precarious economic situation, the explosion has exacerbated the situation of most vulnerable people in the areas affected and also affecting employment. - Increase needs in Health and Mental Health and Psychosocial Support (MHPSS) due to the the short-term and long-term mental health impact of this collective traumatic event. The people living in the affected areas face multiple stressors as loss of friends and relatives, persons injured, loss of belongings, can trigger trauma and anxiety; on the field affected people expressed reactions mainly feeling anxiety, fear, loss of hope, sadness

2 - Activities

1) Provision of medical supplies and equipment to cover the consequences of damages in the MoPH national warehouses. Medical supplies are destinated to MoPH at PHC level, to be able to continue with the increase demand of public services. 2) Provision of PPE material 3) Organization of outreach activities and awareness in communities affected by the explosion or in areas where people affected has moved, to reinforce information on health (mainly COVID19) and MHPSS services and benefits 4) Establishment of a referral system from the affected areas to the operational health and MHPSS structures under MoPH PHC network, in order to ensure continuum of health services, the referral system will ensure that people can access the services where they are referred. 5) Provision of MHPSS services (psychological first aid and mental health interventions) to people affected by the Beirut Port Explosion through phone support and face to face consultations. All activities are done in close collaboration with MoPH. MDM has been working for the last years along with the National mental health program supporting CMHCs and PHCCs on Lebanon, from the aftermath of the explosion MDM has been in close contact with the PHC department and NMHP who is informed about the activities and plans of MDM
