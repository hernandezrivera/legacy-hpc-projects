Project Summary

Based on the IPC analysis reports released on December 2020 and IPC global support unit on famine review committee report as well as projects that by mid-2021, an estimated 7.24 million people or 60 per cent across the country will face severe acute food insecurity and will need urgent humanitarian assistance including food security assistance since most of the households have exhausted their emergency coping strategies. Whilst estimated 1.4 m children under age 5yrs are expected to be acutely malnourished in 2021. The focused counties are IPC phase 4 and 5 with severity of acute malnutrition-GAM rate of >15% and increased nutritional requirements.
The deterioration of the food security situation in the project focused counties Aweil South, Wau, Jur River and Tonj East is alarming and requires immediate action. Therefore, ASCO will scale up the response by improving on food security resilience and recovery livelihood assistance for emergency affected vulnerable households
The other reports that also justifies the deterioration of the food security situation are FSNMS reports , FSL Cluster monthly updates, FEWSNET, FAO and WFP operational reports as well as Joint Market Monitoring Initiative by Cash Technical Working Group, FSL Cluster Food Security Context and drivers of food insecurity in South Sudan and Humanitarian situation briefing by UNOCHA indicates that, food security situation continues to deteriorate caused by different drivers as well as by widespread sub-national violence and COVID-19 related restrictions.
The focused counties are affected and face multi context crisis: inter communal fighting in the focused counties that resulted in civilian casualties and displacements , wide spread flooding affected most communities and disrupting the lives and livelihoods of hundreds of thousands of people causing massive destruction of households.
Thousands of people were displaced while several household’s livelihoods disrupted and claiming their lives. The affected communities remains grim as the affected population remains displaced in prone areas. This placed extra burden on communities that were already acutely food insecure, disease outbreaks and the collapse of infrastructure, healthcare and social services.
The response will address the critical needs through emergency livelihood response program at households and community level as delivery platform with emphasis on Gender Base Violence prevention by awareness rising and improve on safety audits to mitigate the risks on women and girls.
The response will increasingly recognize the response needs of different beneficiary groups: the IDPs, vulnerable host population, returnees and persons with disabilities will be prioritized with focus on cluster objectives. The project will enhance emergency food production through complementary crop, vegetable and fishing kits.
The project will target 4100 households worthy 24600 targeted beneficiaries that constitute of women 5904, men 5658, girls 6396 and boys 6642 by strengthening, promote Communities capacities households food security livelihoods and access to nutrition by scaling up distribution of main season cropping, vegetable and fishing kits to the most severely affected households and protect their existing and increase resilience.
Project will compliment Nutrition, health and WASH to integrate food security sensitive intervention. Food security and livelihood status is compromised where people are exposed to high level of infection due to unsafe and insufficient water supply, limited access to basic healthcare, poor food security and inadequate sanitation.
Priorities will be given to Women by involving in participation in project design, implementation and monitoring of activities and information sharing to express their views. The project will increase availability of GBV prevention and response services in where many women are suffering from different forms of GBV.

1 - Needs

Its estimated 7.7 million people across the country are expected to experience acute food insecurity and worse IPC 3, 4 and 5 in 2021 according to the latest IPC analysis released in December 2020 as well as FSNMS, FEWSNET briefing to FSL Cluster, the FAO and WFP operational reports and FSL Cluster reports. This includes 310,000 refugees and asylum seekers who need FSL support in 2021.The targeted project counties are found in the states facing emergency IPC Phase 4. Aweil South in Northern Bahr el Ghazal and Tonj East in Warrap states. This is projected at the peak of the 2021 lean season. Whilst Wau and Jur River counties has the highest number of people estimated to be in Crisis IPC Phase. The drivers and underlying factors of food insecurity in targeted counties are based on the needs assessment. The Counties are affected and face multi context crisis: inter communal conflict, flooding, cattle raiding, food insecurity, economic crisis, disease outbreaks and the collapse of infrastructure, healthcare and social services.The focused counties are extremely facing food insecure, whereby food insecurity severity are found in locations where the compounded shocks are exceptionally amplified due to intensified sub-national violence, two consecutive years of widespread flooding, indirect effects of COVID-19, and a protracted macro-economic crisis resulting in record high food price spikes. These multiple shocks have severely impacted people’s livelihoods and access to food from markets, livestock and farming in the targeted counties Aweil South, Tonj East . Wau and Jur River.The households in these focused counties are in IPC Phase 4 and 5 and will need immediate emergency food assistance complemented with emergency livelihood and livestock support across farming, agricultural and pastoral communities, for the medium- and longer-term to build both absorptive and adaptive capacity. There are extreme levels of food insecurity with exhaustion of emergency level coping strategies.Food and nutrition needs faces the most vulnerable groups. Women, Children under 5yrs , people with disability, older persons, PLW and women-headed households are identified as the most vulnerable groups in these project locations. Food security and livelihoods were identified as the most needed forms of assistance.Food security worsened and communities facing catastrophic needs. More children are acutely malnourished. Women and girls continued to face extreme levels of gender-based violence and psycho social distress. People’s coping mechanisms weakened as a consequence of the cumulative shocks, leading families to adopt negative practices. The economy continued to spiral downwards, pushing vulnerable communities to the brink.These counties mostly face natural catastrophic situation seasonal and climatic shocks particularly floods and drought spells increasingly due to large scale displacement resulting in limited food stocks, crop diseases and pests including the Fall Army Worm affecting crops and ongoing lean season of 2021 that is further undermining people’s resilience by depleting household assets. Acute malnutrition has worsened in targeted Counties with extreme critical level of severity Acute Malnutrition GAM rate of >15%. Increases in Acute malnutrition is attributed to severe food insecurity, widespread insecurity, displacement, poor access to services, high morbidity, extremely poor diets and poor sanitation and hygiene.The project will target different beneficiary groups: The IDPs, vulnerable host communities, returnees and shall involve men, women, boys and girls appropriate and equitable participation in program activities to gain relevant skills. 50% of women in the project activities will be consulted including persons with disabilities and will be on the recipient end of the livelihood kits to enhance food production. ASCO aims to scale up the response to meet the severity of needs.
