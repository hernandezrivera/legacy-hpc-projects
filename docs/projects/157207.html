Project Summary

ARC has been working in Karkada and Maramosa providing emergency life-saving interventions since July 2018 and the only international organization implementing Health, nutrition, WASH and distribution of shelter and non- food items (NFIs) to the returnees and refugees. The project will focus on integrated lifesaving health, nutrition, WASH, emergency shelter and distributions of non food items (NFIs) interventions for the new influx of returnees and refugees. The interventions will focus on meeting their basic needs and well-being while increasing their self-resilience. ARC will focus on an inter-sectoral approach that supports integration between the health, nutrition, WASH and ES/NFIs services. Service delivery of all health and nutrition services will be integrated at the fixed site clinics in Karkada and Marmosa. ARC will continue to provide basic primary health care package with key focus to reduce morbidity and mortality among children under five, men, women, women of childbearing age, pregnant and lactation women (PLW), Adolescent boys and girls and elderly men and women including persons with disability. The services includes diagnostic & curative services, provision of essential drugs through support of WHO, reproductive health services (ANC, PNC and FP), including clinical management of rape services, immunization support and outreach health education. ARC will provide outpatient therapeutic and targeted supplementary feeding programs to children under five and pregnant and lactating women respectively. A functional referral system for complicated cases will be established between the Karkada and Maramosa clinics and Tullus rural hospital with support of Commission of Refugees (COR) to provide further diagnostic and inpatient support. The health and nutrition team will also closely collaborate with the WASH team on health education/outreach activities to promote proper hygiene practices and better understanding of the key public health/ nutrition risks associate with WASH. ARC will strength community activities through already established and trained Community Relief and Development (CRD) committee(s) and health, nutrition and WASH volunteer networks from the previous project. They will take a lead role in health, nutrition, and WASH promotion activities, assist with disease surveillance, and encourage community referral processes.
Under WASH interventions in Karakada and Maramosa, ARC will ensure increased access to safe clean water for returnee, refugees and the host community. ARC will support the operation and maintenance of 2 water yards in Maramosa and Karakada through provision of daily fuel and lubricants. ARC plans to drill additional 2 boreholes to compliment the existing ones and upgrade to solar pumping system to increase access to water supply. Water treatment through daily chlorination and quarterly quality monitoring. In addition, extend community distribution water points and construction of new steel water tank to increase water storage capacity. Under this project, ARC will include sanitation facilities through construction of household latrines, communal and institutional latrines. ARC will train masons from the communities to facilitate the household latrines construction. In addition to that the program will extend its support in environmental health by providing cleaning tools to the community and mobilize them to carry out weekly clean-up campaigns including collection, transportation and disposal of garbage to it's final destination.
Community committees and volunteers will be trained to promote hygiene practices such as hand washing with soap at the critical times, use of latrines, safe water and food handling among their communities .

Needs

Karkada and Marmosa being in remote locations; Maramosa 80 Km west of Dimso locality (N 10 23’ 29.39”/ E 24 03’ 51.98”) and Karkada 100 Km SW of Dimso locality (N 10 09 03.46 / E 24 16’ 19.06”A) with ARC as the only implementing agency, there is need to continue providing intergrated life saving interventions focusing on health, nutrtion, WASH and ES/NFIs. According to January 2019 mission report by OCHA, confirmed IOM registration for refugees, returnees and host community in both Karkada and maramosa is 7212. Majority of the population remain women and children. In Karakada, ARC drilled one borehole yeilding 3 cubic meters per hour which is inadaqute to meet the needs of the people (4litres/person/day). This is below the sphere standard of 15 litre/person/day. Because of the poor yeild the refugees, returnees and host community will still depend on unprotected hand dug wells to meet needs. There is no access to sanitation and the population practice open defecation which expose them risk of diseases. The hygiene and sanitation awareness support and materials/ skills coverage is missing for the entire community. Through local partner (United Peace Organization) 1000 emergency shelter constructed which is not adequate to cover people need in both camps with a gap for more than 500. UNHCR distributed 1500 non-food items. There is report from the field, new comers are arriving though not yet verified According ARC health and nutrition data from December 2018 - January 2019, 651(262 Male and 389 female) refugees, returnees and host community accessed the services in both centers. Delay in project implementation and start up due to construction of clinic sites and SMoH allocating frontline staffs to the area. Only 4 (4%) of women were assisted deliveries by skilled birth attendant, 52% of children under year received measles vaccine and 30% receive petavalent 3 dose. 93 cases reported as severe acute malnutrition, 128 with moderate acute malnutrition and 52 pregnant and lactating women admitted in the Target supplementary feeding program (TSFP). With this outcome, there is need for ARC to continue with provision of services in Karkada and Maramosa.

Activities

WASH- Drill additional 2 new boreholes installed with solar system pump in Karkada. This will compliment the exiting borehole to meet the sphere standards.- Operation and maintenance of 2 wateryards in Karkada and Maramosa to increase access to water supply.- Expansion of distribution of water pipeline from the 2 water yards to connect to the four water points for both camps.- Daily water chlorination to ensure safe and clean potable water to the communities.- Elevated water tank to demand good water distribution to the far water points- Construction of 400 household latrines to improve safe access to sanitation facilities.- Soap distribution and installation of handwashing facilities in the community.- Conduct monthly clean-up campaigns and maintain safe disposal for solid waste by transportation to final disposal site.- Training of hygiene promoters on health education sessions and community mobilization to disseminate good hygiene messages among the community.SHELTER- Construction of 500 shelters HEALTH and NUTRITION-ARC will provide basic primary health care package, which includes preventive & curative services. -Provision of essential drugs, medical and non medical supplies to avoid interruption of services. -Support reproductive health services (ANC, PNC and FP), including clinical management of rape services, immunization support and outreach health education. In addition, ARC will procure supplementary buffer stock for essential drugs to compliment the supplies from WHO. - Conduct routine immunization through the Karkada and Maramosa clinic with support of vaccines from SMoH.-Support SMoH in acceleration campaigns and national immunizations days to increase coverage-Training of health worker to increase the skills and knowledge to improve provision of quality of services.-Admit and treat SAM and MAM cases.- Support referral of complicated medical and obstetric cases to secondary level.-MUAC screening.-Provision of RUTFand RUSF
