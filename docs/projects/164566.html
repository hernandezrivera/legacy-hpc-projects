Project Summary

• Our project aiming at establishing a training center within 9-month providing appliances and technical means required. This training center will accommodate (2775) trainees, as it will provide training on various programs related to working skills required in the local market, companies, and organizations. This training will lead to developing personal skills integral to the technical skills the trainees already have. Moreover, we will monitor training results, set guiding sessions for trainees after the training ends.
There are a lot of training programs will be offered to the trainees:
- Effective communication skills
- Writing resume, cover letter, and motivation letter
- Computer skills
- Job interview skills
- Making a good presentation
- Leadership and teamwork skills
- Creativity and problem-solving skills
- Time and priorities management skills
