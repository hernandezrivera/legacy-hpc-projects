Project Summary

The proposed project intends to contribute to prevention, mitigation and response measure to the protection risks specifically Gender Based violence to the civilian population, through provision of immediate lifesaving assistance in addressing the urgent protection needs of the most vulnerable at risk and conflict affected populations in the displaced and hard to reach areas in focus of women, Girls, men, boys, children, elderly, people living with disability including survivors of sexual violence and gender Based violence). With further response of the proposed project factoring mitigating risks and strengthening resilience of vulnerable communities and persons of concerns to recover from consequences of conflicts.
There continues to be inadequate Gender Based violence response and prevention services across some of the parts of Central Equatoria and Eastern Equatoria states of south Sudan, this continues to be seen as a critical gap for women and girls service delivery on lifesaving services which exposes them to further harm and consequences of the violence they have gone through which is a huge protection risk and exposure to their lives. Although there are GBV actor on ground with GBV services in some of the parts of the states mentioned but one still finds out that there are some parts that indicate in adequate service delivery for women and girls.
Based on the reports from the current Primary Health Care project implementation progressive reports by HLSS across Kapoeta County, Juba County and Torit County under Health Pool Fund (HPF) funds as of Oct 2018 there is high need of GBV services parts of the mentioned locations which hosts both IPDs, returnees, Host communities and other affected populations. There has been increase on the number of women and girls who report for health service indicating during consultation that they were either beaten by their Husbands or intimate partners and other showing signs of been psychologically tortured for which there are no GBV services where such survivor can be supported with case management or psychosocial support. Furthermore reports from MHC in the various facilities indicate underage girls attending antenatal services which is an indication of early marriage being common and there are services of information dissemination on consequences of such forms of Gender Based violence.
With this project HLSS intends to cover the service gaps for women and girls which is a huge risk factor for life threatening cases, as the identified and mentioned locations have huge number of returnees, other affected population, IDPS and the host communities where there is total no GBV services were the population is densely populated by women and girl. The proposed project intends to run GBV response services through activities of
1. Establishment of comprehensive gender based violence case management services by setting a safe and confidential space with multi-sectoral services
2. Establishment and supporting referral mechanism through conducting service mapping of existing services and develop/update existing referral pathway in order to disseminate to the community on existing services
3. Establishment of women and girls friendly spaces which shall allow structured activities such as (information session, beading, Bed sheet knitting, crocheting, tea/coffee talk sessions, crocheting) for women and girls to participate in the various structured activities established at the women and girls friendly space.
