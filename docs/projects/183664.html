Project Summary

The project “Combatting De-Palestinization of Education in Occupied East Jerusalem” addresses:
-The need for effective protection of the right of Palestinians in occupied East Jerusalem to preserve and develop their educational system in accordance with their own culture, values and national identity.
- The right of East Jerusalem children to freely learn, express and be educated in respect to their Palestinian identity, culture, language and values. Palestinians, including children, hold these rights as protected persons under IHL, and as individuals and a people entitled to self-determination under IHRL.
This issue is addressed because of: the broad concern about the systematic manner in which Israel has undermined these rights; the failure to obtain redress in the past through repeated but uncoordinated efforts; the international community including governments and UN and international organizations need to acknowledge the serious violation of IHL and human rights; and, the serious consequences for Palestinian society in East Jerusalem, in particular its youth.

1 - Needs

With more than 54 years of occupation of the West Bank, Gaza, and East Jerusalem, Israel continues its process of ‘Israelization’ or ‘de-Palestinization’ of education in occupied East Jerusalem. On the 13th of May 2018 , the Israeli cabinets approve a five year plan to offer financial incentive, were allocated for east Jerusalem schools 705,000,000 Israeli Shekel (242,884,200 US $) that would fully adopt the Israeli curriculum rather than using the Palestinian one. The plan heavily packed by the Israeli minister of education Nafali Bennet as well as well and Jerusalem affairs and Heritage Minister Zeev Elkin. The plan proposed is a five year strategy that aims to gradually increase the number of students using the Israeli curriculum beginning from the first grade with specific emphasis on English and math. The first year would add 15 classes, followed by 18 in the second, 21 in the third, 24 in the fourth, and 27 classes in the fifth. Another goal of the cabinet is to continue to increase the number of East Jerusalem Palestinian students who would earn the Israeli matriculation exam (bagrut).Bennet, the Israeli education minister: was quoted saying, “the time has come for East Jerusalem to also study the Israeli curriculum from first grade. Jerusalem needs to be united in deeds, not in words. The political context of the problem raises additional concerns such as: the failure to obtain redress; the reluctance of foreign aid agencies and governments to engage in what is perceived to be a “political matter”, rather than one IHL and human rights; and, the serious consequences for Palestinian society in East Jerusalem, in particular its children and youth. Part of Israel’s policy of ‘de-Palestinization’ has been aimed at schools in East Jerusalem and the push by the occupying Authorities to introduce the Israeli curriculum which can be seen clearly through the Israeli five years plan foe education in East Jerusalem. This was highlighted in the Israeli newspaper Haaretz: ‘Dr. George Mansour, who examined the history textbooks, found that the books ignore the presence of the Arab-Palestinian people in Israel and the stress is on the Promised Land of the Jewish people: "There is a process of de-Palestinization, instilling of the Zionist narrative and minimizing of Arab culture," reported Dr. Mansour. With the world wide health crisis because of the corona virus which has been effecting many countries including Palestine and the Palestinians people. As a result of the corona, life in Palestine stopped completely, people were looked down, schools and university were closed, commercial life has stopped completely people could not go the work, this has effected the social and economic situation of the Palestinian specially in occupied Jerusalem. Many heads of families could not go to work and they could not have enough food for their children, because thousands of the heads of families they depend on their daily work, and income, if they do not go to work there is no food. This situation increased poverty among Palestinian as a whole and in Jerusalem in particular. As well, the situation has an impact on children whom they were looked down, they can’t go out, which increase the stress among them, therefore with the AFPS intuitive we thought of the idea to provide some support to the poor communities in occupied Jerusalem in an a creative way rather than traditional way therefore we thought of provide children with educational games were they can play, that could be educational and interment for the children and their parents as well, which helped them to improve there thinking skills and to release their tension and stress. Therefore the children were very happy with the games.
