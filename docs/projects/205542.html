Project Summary

Chronic WASH needs continue to emerge in most parts of Upper Nile State specifically the counties of Renk and Melut, which are likely to be exacerbated by unprecedented intercommunal conflicts, flooding, influx of refugees and returnees, food insecurity as well as logistical challenges. Provision of adequate WASH services is key to achieving sustainable solutions to communities in emergency. ARDF, a national NGO currently implementing projects in Renk and Melut Counties envisages a targeted response aimed at reaching internally displaced persons, returnees, refugees and vulnerable host communities with water, sanitation and hygiene services, integrated with nutrition and protection/ GBV programming. Spanning a period of 12 months, the project targets the locations of Renk and Melut with WASH temporary and sustainable services, to address the vital needs, public health and protection/GBV issues.
The response aims at reducing levels of chronic malnutrition especially amongst children, pregnant and lactating women, mitigating WASH related GBV by providing gender appropriate sanitation and hygiene facilities and tackling issues surrounding gender, safety and WASH. Persistence of food insecurity, signaled by high GAM rates, with <5s and PLW mainly affected will be mitigated by the provision of WASH in Nutrition services, through targeted WASH minimum packages at OTP/SC mainly for mother/caretaker – malnourished children. Water related disease outbreaks have been traced to health facilities and community infrastructures/settings without adequate WASH facilities.

1. Needs

Melut and Renk Counties are among locations in South Sudan which continue to face challenges in the thematic areas of WASH, nutrition, health, FSL and protection with an estimated two Million persons in these counties in need of humanitarian aid. According to the 2023 Humanitarian Needs Overview (HNO), the highest level of needs and vulnerabilities are observed in Upper Nile states, due to multiple and recurrent shocks. Counties targeted are all under vulnerability index 4. Inadequate access to clean water, improved sanitation and acceptable hygiene practices within the counties of Melut and Renk Counties (Upper Nile state) contributes greatly to the high levels of malnutrition, disease burdens, food insecurity as well as WASH related protection/GBV issues. Less than 15% of households in these communities have access to an improved water source under 30 minutes; with inadequate water available for domestic use. Deterioration of the economic situation reduced ability of communities to purchase items required such as soap, puri cation tablets, NFIs and hygiene items. Majority of persons resort to unsafe open water sources, that are usually contaminated with fecal material and are co-shared with livestock. Access to sanitation facilities is equally low, WASH severity maps indicate less than 30% of households having access to a latrine, yet majority of the latrines are shared. Open defecation is widely practiced, leading to cycles of water contamination, resulting in acute watery diarrhoea, cholera, eye infections, and skin diseases. Hand washing is equally low, with socio-cultural practices unfavorable to achieving acceptable WASH standards. Women are exposed to a series of protection risks due to distance and time taken at water points, lack of hygiene items and livelihood opportunities, as well as community attitudes towards women. Agro – pastoral communities usually while looking for water for domestic and livelihood purposes tend to migrate to the areas with open water sources yet lacking adequate sanitation facilities. Torrential floods across the targeted locations have undermined efforts by communities to attain better living standards, due to the destruction of infrastructure and livelihoods and massive displacement of communities. There’s a direct correlation between low WASH coverage and high levels of malnutrition, as a result of occurrence of water related diseases amongst U5s, PLW and persons with special needs.

2. Project activities

The following activities are planned through this project in the locations of Renk and Melut counties:• WASH service provision in 19 nutrition facilities (WASH NFI kits and hygiene promotion to 11,111 SAM children) and access to safe water to facilities in IPC 4 locations and provision of sustained water to communities showing high GAM prevalence and poor access to water. • Access to safe water, sanitation to 11,111 people in areas showing high risk of outbreak and hygiene promotion (domestic, water and food hygiene). 211 water points will be rehabilitated with flood-resistant design. 1,055 latrine stances will be constructed with flood-resistant design.• Mitigate Gender-Based Violence through the conduct of 2 safety audits at site level (or use protection mainstreaming tip sheet) to identify GBV concerns and implement mitigation measure. • Building WASH resilience through the provision of permanent access to water and sanitation services for IDPs, returnees, and vulnerable host communities
