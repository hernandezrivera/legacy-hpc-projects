Project Summary

Al-Birr Association is one of the oldest charity and social associations in Syria. It was established in 1956 with a target of giving a hand to all people in need like poor households, people in need of social or health care, in order to achieve a good financial, social, and healthy community, throw those households.
Among the many works and services provided by Al-birr, the most important is Al-Sa’adah (happiness) house in the Northern countryside of Homs (Talbisah) hosting about 200 elderly people who have missed their families and providing them with all services they need such as Health, protection, nutrition, physiotherapy, psychosocial support, Even entertainment.
Due to the crisis in Syria in general and in Homs in particular, many service facilities have been damaged which has made a huge pressure on the needed to have the needed services. One of these services is elderly care for the people who are unable to take care of themselves.
Because of the armed conflict in the northern countryside of Homs, the elderly house has been damaged, which led to a decrease in the carrying capacity of the house and the quality of the services nevertheless Dar Al-Sa’adah has continued its role toward the elderly, although the difficult situations.
During last year, and since the security situation has been improved, there is a huge need to rehabilitate the house and continue providing services through it to those elderly.
This project is to rehabilitate the hospice and operate it during the year to provide various services (medical, psychological, nutrition, and recreational activities) for about 200 elderly hosted in the hospice

3 - Needs

"Older persons and persons with disabilities and chronic illnesses face a range of challenges unique to their individual circumstances, compounded by their displacement where familial/community support networks have broken down or are overstretched, especially in areas directly affected by hostilities or due to multiple displacements. They face stigma, social isolation, are at increased risk of separation from their families, care providers, and dependent on the assistive products which support their independence." (HNO 2022), it's important to find a place where services are provided for elderly people to help them to live good healthy life, reaching their necessary needs. In addition, there are many service facilities have been damaged which has made a huge need to have services. One of these services is elderly care for the people who are unable to take care of themselves. Because of the armed conflict in the northern countryside of Homs, the elderly house has been damaged, which led to a decrease in its capacity and quality of the services. Nevertheless, the work continues within the available resources in spite of the difficulties and the presence of many critical health cases that requires medical supervision and accurate and direct following. About 4% of people in need are elderly, and they need this special care, where Al-Birr is hosting about 200 elderly for free, or for low fees.

4 - Implementation of activities

The project activities mainly depend on carrying out rehabilitation works for the house, such as electricity, air conditioning, and refrigeration network, through contracting with services providers. The project will provide training for workers in the elderly house for ways of dealing with people with specific needs such as older adults and people with disabilities. In addition, it includes delivering services to the elderly such as health services, nutrition, care, physiotherapy, psychosocial support, in addition to entertainment activities (Open days, cinema, theater, etc.) through a staff of the house.

One of the potential risks during the implementation stage of the project is the fluctuation of prices of the needed materials and equipment. Therefore, Al-Birr will sign a frame agreement with the suppliers at the beginning of the project to guarantee that all the needed supplies and materials will be purchased at fixed prices during the whole period of the project.
