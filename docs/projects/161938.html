Project Summary

After influx of Rohingya refugee Nabolok has started providing lifesaving services at camp 26 and 23 for reducing their sufferings focusing on WASH, LPG distribution and Solar street light installation as well as for sufferer host community also. Provision of life-saving measures and strengthening health-promoting behavior through water , sanitation and hygiene ("WASH") activities through the construction and maintenance of Single Chamber Twin-pit Latrine, as well as installation and maintenance of an "ARO" Sea water desalination plant of capacity of 150,000ltr/day for Rohingya Refugee and host community, construction of waste collection-installation/landfills including maintenance and construction of waste bins, training for Environmentally friendly disposal of solid waste and waste suitable for recycling(for Host), set-up construction of a latrine waste sludge care-managements/plant(FSM), construction of Bathing cubical for women and adolescent girls, distribution of hygiene-Kits and conduct hygiene session in schools and mosques, hygiene awareness session for women and adolescent girls, orientation with imams and teachers and to create hygiene awareness including the provision and distribution of IEC materials;

Needs

Sector partners continue to face challenges in improving the water supply and prepare for Teknaf region water challenges in the dry season. According to the Joint Response Plan (JRP), around 56% of Rohingya households have problems with access to water, particularly due to remote drinking water abstraction points and waiting times at significantly higher rates in Teknaf than any other refugee camps. And as far as sewage disposal is concerned, there are still too few adequately equipped sanitary facilities. Here, 53% of households surveyed reported problems with access due to distance and frequent use, the location itself and because the facilities overcrowded on a regular basis. Overall 49% of girls and 40% of women said they felt insecure when using the latrines, 40% and 34%, respectively, when using existing washrooms. The women and girls respond to these conditions by setting up makeshift bathing areas in the shelters and no longer using latrines at night. Also there is a huge gap of solid waste management, hygiene awareness, and hygiene kits here at Teknaf.
