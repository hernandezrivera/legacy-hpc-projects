Project Summary

The project’s proposed interventions seek to improve the humanitarian situation for the war-affected population by rehabilitating the IDPs and the Host communities’ houses or collective shelter that will be able to provide more security and privacy to the inhabitants responding to the increasing demand in the overburdened communities as a result of the continuous arrival of tens of thousands of forcefully displaced people because of warfare. The action will be implemented for the sake of the affected population in Northwest Aleppo and Northern Idleb (AL Bab, Daret Azza, Dana, and Salqin). 40,150 people will benefit from all activities (9,893 men, 12,591 women, 7,773 boys, 9,893 girls) while coordination with local councils, shelter centers’ management, and other actors in the area is the first mandatory step for all output;
• 2000 houses are rehabilitated with medium rehabilitation activity (800.00$ per house), in Al-Bab (400HHs), Daret Azza (550 HHs), Salqin (450HHs) and Dana (600 HHs).
• 300 HHs in 15 collective shelters will be assisted through rehabilitation activity with medium rehabilitation activity (10,000.00$ per center), in both Dana and Daret Azza.
• Distribute of 15,000 MPC assistance to new IDPs in Al-Bab (4000HHs), Daret Azza (3000 HHs), Salqin (2000HHs) and Dana (6000 HHs).
the rehabilitation activity will include:
o Ensuring internal living spaces’ security and privacy by providing closed, sealed and independent environments for the HHS to live-in, by maintaining\providing of doors and windows with different material option (PVC, Iron, Wood or Aluminum) to ensure the most secure and private environment for the inhabitant family against bad weather conditions (Rain, Snow, Cold ...etc.).
o Ensure the most of the beneficiary’s safety against life-threating issues by implementing minor rehabilitation works to the house’s structural elements (walls, ceilings, roofs, floors ...etc.) targeting life-threating issues (cracking walls or structural elements) and other bad situations which thread the beneficiary’s personal health and security.
o Ensuring a healthier environment by promoting hygiene and maintaining the WASH facilities in the house, this activity will include rehabilitation of WCs and bathrooms.
o Providing illumination and a light power source in each room by providing the needed electrical points (lamps, switches, wires...etc.) and connect them to the local power source.
o Ensuring better living conditions for people with special needs by providing special architectural elements (metal grips and handles, metal railings, special needs people suitable toilets...etc.).
IhsanRD will target both IDP and host community’s households in the shelter repair activity, those who live in inadequate shelter, especially where minimum SPHERE standards are not met (3.5m2/individual).
Only houses with a level of damage from light to medium will be targeted, the houses with damaged structural elements will be excluded.
In collective centers, IhsanRD will coordinate with the local authorities and target the most crowded centers if applicable. Two collective centers have been identiﬁed as per of a community-level assessment conducted by IhsanRD.
In order to be accepted by the project criteria, HHs and building owners should agree on the terms of assistance and sign a contract that allows the IDP household to stay in the building for at least one year to ensure that HLP guidance is considered and utilized. To beneﬁt from the project, all beneﬁciaries should present valid HLP documents that verify the ownership of the properties.
Coordination at all levels and with all stakeholders will be continuous throughout the project duration, and the implementation will be monitored and eventually evaluated.

4 - Needs

Shelter and NFI needs in Syria are widespread and acute resulting in signiﬁcant living standard consequences and affecting physical and mental well-being and protection of affected populations. Overall shelter severity has increased across Syria, with a corresponding increase in PiN from 2019 by 20 percent to 5.65 million people.1 Of this, 3.8 million (69 percent) are in areas of severity 4 or higher. Geographically, these areas are concentrated in Idleb and rural of Aleppo, up to one million IDPs reside in last-resort informal and spontaneous settlements. MSNA data demonstrates the cumulative impact of the crisis.12 More than three-quarters of that paying rent are unable to afford it13. Shelter adequacy is critical in 238 out of 272 sub-districts14. This translates into families not having the security of tenancy while others living in an inadequate shelter may be left unprotected from the elements, in insecure situations or overcrowded housing, raising possible risks to personal safety and protection. IDPs are particularly affected by shelter damage and deterioration - there has been a 30 percent reduction in the number of IDPs with access to undamaged shelter15.Beneficiary Selection and Verification Process (including HLP document for shelter activities):- Open beneficiaries selection process will be followed, including announcing the project with selection criteria in the targeted communities and outreaching visits to ensure the participation of all groups then allow applicants' registrationThe selection criteria as follows:1) The newly displaced people displaced less than 3 months.2) The number of times of displacement.3) Households with extremely vulnerable family members (disabled, elderly, mobility-impaired, etc.).4) Women or child-headed households.5) households with no stable income.6) Households who have not received prior shelter/NFI assistance.

5 - Implementation of activities

Activity 1: provide cash support (winter top-up) for 5,000 HHs IDPs and most vulnerable families in Dana sub-district with a value of 130 USD per household, based on the SNFI Cluster and Cash Working Group for Syria recommendations for minimum support in 2019 and it's agreed with all IPs to adopt this amount to cover the need for the current winter response where the project focuses on ensuring the basic winterization needs as well. IRD will implement the project according to the IRD cash SoP.Activity 2: HH Shelter Medium Rehabilitation For this activity IhsanRD is aiming at targeting 2000 HHs who are meet the selection criteria where a greater need for shelter repair has been identiﬁed. Open beneﬁciaries’ selection process will be conducted, including announcing the project with selection criteria in the targeted communities and outreaching visits to ensure the participation of all groups. Qualiﬁed engineers will assess multi-sectoral repair needs to ensure target households are living in buildings capable of withstanding the most common hazards without the risk of collapsing or injuring them. As part of the assessment process, households will be advised if the shelter represents danger and will be connected with the local council or other shelter actors. The engineers will also evaluate if the sheltering space can be rehabilitated to reach Sphere recommendations and cluster standards Once technical assessment completed, detailed BOQs for all targeted houses will be delivered.Activity 3: CC (Collective Center) rehabilitation. Under this activity, IhsanRD aiming to target 300 HHs where a greater need for rehabilitation works has been identiﬁed. The identiﬁcation for the CCs is going to be through LCs and Ihsan referral system and ﬁeld visits. Qualiﬁed engineers will assess multi-sectoral repair needs to ensure target households are living in collective centers capable of withstanding the most common hazards without the risk of collapsing or injuring them.
