Project Summary

The project aims at providing comprehensive protection services to the vulnerable people in Damascus and 11 areas in Rural Damascus, project activities are tailored to support vulnerable groups (men, women, boys, and girls) from different communities (IDPs, returnees, and host community) taking into consideration the inclusion of people with disabilities and variations in needs among all groups.
The project will be implemented in Damascus and Rural Damascus to provide services related to GBV prevention, Child protection, general protection, and risk education.
Activities in Damascus, Duma Harsta, Az-Zabadani will be as an expansion to already existing activities where the need is very huge. Newly established centers in RD(Arbin, Babella, Kafr Batna, AlZabdani, Ein Elfijeh, Madaya, Qudsiya, Maliha, and Darayya)aim at expanding services to new geographic areas and new populations.
Activities in the existing centers will provide an added value and expansion to the services that are being implemented due to the high need and demand for protection services especially with the COVID-19 pandemic and the harsh living conditions, taking into consideration duplication avoidance. AlTamayoz conducted field visits through volunteers who confirmed the needs expressed by people (visits were conducted to 15 locations, out of which 12 were selected where the returnees movement is high and a high percentage of IDPs exists, and where protection issues such as sexual harassment, child marriage and child labour were highly reported.
Activities under this project will interlink with other activities implemented under education especially those of risk education and PSS activities for children in learning centers, students with special needs can be referred for case management and further referrals under protection project when needed.
Activities implementation will consider COVID-19 situation and will maintain social distancing and assure precaution measures
Activities are planned to cover 2022 and 2023 as per the expected needs in Damascus and Rural Damascus.

4 - Needs

according to draft HNO 2022Complex and interconnected protection issues in this protracted crisis are worsening in an overall context of limited access to humanitarian services, rights, justice, and accountability.The deteriorating economy and widespread poverty, lack and loss of livelihoods, destruction and loss of housing and property, protracted and multiple cycles of displacement, substandard living conditions (even for people in areas of relative stability), shortage of natural resources and continuing pandemic, exacerbate protection needs and increase reliance on negative coping mechanisms such as child labour, child recruitment, different forms of exploitation and early/forced marriage.In turn, the devastating impact of the economic downturn, food crisis, scarcity of natural resources, health disasters, and others, exacerbates the existing protection crisis.Adolescent girls continue to be denied education due to early marriage. Explosive ordnance contamination will continue to present a significant risk for populations living in areas having witnessed hostilities, and increasingly for individuals returning to their communities and will remain the top access constraint for safe delivery of humanitarian aid and assistance to the people in need.During AlTmayoz's work in Rural Damascus, it was observed that child marriage reached 55 % of girls 14-17 years of age, 40% of children aged from 12-18 are engaged in child labor about 65% of women are exposed to GBV.

5 - Implementation of activities

Activities in Damascus will be implemented in AlMidan in Southern Damascus where a density of IDPs and overburdened host community exist. This project will support the already exist community center in Almidan in order to expand its activities due to the increased number of beneficiaries seeking for its services.In Rural Damascus,the proposed project aims at establishing community centers (including women and girls safe spaces) in 9 locations (Arbin, Babella, Kafr Batna, AlZabdani, Ein elfijeh, Madaya, Qudsiya, Maliha and Darayya) where severity scale is 3 and 4 and to expand services in 3 existing centers in(Harasta, Duma, and Zabadani). Centers in all locations will act as a space where affected people especially women and girls can express and exchange concerns and experiences and receive appropriate services, and where children can interact with peers and where special needs of children are met. Activities will be implemented in all mentioned areas in coordination with other humanitarian actors in order to provide a complementary service and avoid duplication. The proposed project mainstreams protection through considering the principles of Do No Harm during activities implementation. GBV issues will be taken into consideration through conducting awareness-raising sessions to mainstream GBV aspects and GBV prevention.
