Project Summary

The condition of school sanitation facilities in Lebanon is critical: UNICEF and the Lebanese Ministry of Education and Higher Education (MEHE) have conducted a WASH in schools survey for cholera response and a general WASH in schools assessment in the education sector end of 2022. While the official results of the general assessment are still outstanding, Unicef verbally confirmed the alarming situation in schools in regards to WASH facilities. Most facilities do not conform to any standards and are in dire need of rehabilitation. The schools lack adequate sanitation and water supplies, and constant power outages prevent them from having water and adequately lighting for the classrooms.
The poor hygiene is resulting in a lot of different health problems, which shows the current Cholera outbreak. The already published survey results show that due to poor sanitation, public schools have a high risk of cholera and other waterborne diseases transmission. The risk was estimated based on school WASH facilities and services, such as poorly functioning water systems or insufficient treatment, uncontrolled sewage from a containment facility, student and staff exposure to sewage, unprotected drinking water sources, and lack of functional handwashing facilities, and it showed that especially schools in Akkar district have a high risk.
The proposed project aims to improve the WASH conditions of the most vulnerable Lebanese students in schools through a WASH intervention. arche noVa will improve the WASH facilities in schools through the following results:
Result 1: WASH conditions in schools have been improved and good hygiene practices among students promoted:
arche noVa (AN) will target eight schools with inadequate water and sanitation systems in North Lebanon and Akkar by rehabilitating their sanitation and water systems. Planned and implemented in accordance with the Sphere and national standards, all facilities will be barrier-free and gender-sensitive. The selection of schools will be based on an evaluation and predefined selection criteria, and will be conducted in close coordination with the Ministry of Education and Higher Education (MEHE) and the sector lead and will ensure that students, particularly PwD and female students, participate in the assessment. arche noVa will rely on the WASH in schools assessment conducted by the sector leads for the preselection of schools and will carry out a detailed needs assessment for the final selection taking into consideration the specific needs of the students with disabilities and female students.
Hygiene promotion sessions and hygiene kits will be provided in accordance with UNICEF's "WASH in Schools Guidelines for Lebanon," to improve students' hygiene practices. Additionally, AN plans to install solar systems to power WASH facilities and provide adequate lighting in schools for morning and afternoon shifts.
The intervention will protect the rights and preserve the dignity of children by providing them with access to safe, acceptable and reliable WASH services and conducive learning environments. Children spend a significant amount of time in schools, making them the best place to learn, practice and inculcate good WASH behavior. The benefits go beyond the classroom: school WASH contributes to the health of the future generation, as change agents, children can positively influence the behavior of their family and community members.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

Activity 1.2.1: Rehabilitation/construction of WASH facilities:arche noVa will target the vulnerable schools by constructing or rehabilitating the WASH facilities. The following facilities will be enhanced:- Sanitation system: arche noVa will construct and rehabilitate toilets that are not satisfying Sphere and national standards taking into account the needs of PwD and females’ students and will ensure that the schools have proper sanitation systems and are properly and safely connected to the available sewage system either via sewage network or septic tank.- Water system: arche noVa will ensure that the schools have functioning water systems for the toilets including handwashing facilities and also will make sure the schools have a clean source of drinking water by connecting to the official water source from NLWE or by providing the needed filtration system.- Solar system: arche noVa will install solar systems in the targeted schools to ensure the proper operation of the WASH facilities especially in the schools that have boreholes and pumps and also to use for lighting and safe access to the toilets. Activity 1.2.1: Hygiene Promotion:Improving hygiene practices among the students: arche noVa will carry out hygiene promotion sessions in the target schools and will distribute hygiene kits for the students to enhance the hygiene practices of the students. The kit will be designed taking into consideration the specific needs of the females’ students.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

Target schools and beneficiaries will be selected in coordination with the education sector based on the sector's assessment, and arche noVa will conduct its own participatory needs assessment. The assessment will be conducted in consultation with school personnel and students, taking into account the specific needs of people with disabilities who are often marginalized and culturally excluded from social life, as well as the needs of women who are subject to discrimination and inequality; therefore, inclusive factors are already considered during the project's planning phase. In addition, arche noVa is following its own PSEA policy that also applies for contractors and will be followed up closely by AN staff. Furthermore AN has a MEAL system and complaint and feedback mechanisms in place to monitor the project's implementation, ensure the application of protection and humanitarian principles, and ensure accountability to the affected population.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

The following actions will be taken by arche noVa to ensure accountability to the affected population:- Based on arche noVa´s existing complaint and feedback mechanism procedures, specific project related procedures and referral pathways will be established and closely followed. The MEAL team will ensure that accountability messages are effectively disseminated and introduce the existing mechanisms to the targeted schools.- Participation of beneficiaries: arche noVa will implement the project according to the standards defined by the ministry of education and will also take into account the needs of the students, particularly those with disabilities, women, and other marginalized groups.In addition, post-implementation monitoring, such as satisfaction surveys, will play a key role in gathering input from the affected population and ensuring that program teams are aware of any necessary adjustments or modifications to activities.
