Project Summary

The overall objective of the project is to ensure that vulnerable people in Aweil North, Twic East, and Uror access GBV protection services and have their dignity and well-being restored. The proposed intervention will be through specialised protection services like comprehensive case management to meet the urgent needs of vulnerable women, men, boys, and girls and people with disabilities, strengthening of community based structures, individual support through case management services and mental health and psychosocial support (MHPSS), awareness-raising, capacity building and monitoring and reporting.
The project will address all the cluster three objectives
PC 1-Ensure mobile or static provision of critical protection assistance and services, including specialised response services, tailored to the specific needs, considering age, gender, and disability by;
-provision of GBV case management and case referrals for specialised services like MHPSS,
-Establishment of women and girls friendly spaces
-Provision of psychosocial support services
-Strengthening and updating GBV referral pathways that are functional
-Conduct GBV assessments like safety audits, field monitoring, and gender analysis.
PC 2 - Ensuring that human rights are upheld and promoted for the persons of concern by strengthening coordination, advancing centrality of protection among humanitarian actors to reduce protection risks, and addressing coordination gaps/issues through close dialogue with partners and effective protection monitoring to inform programming, advocacy, and response by;
-Awareness creation through various methods like the media, IEC materials, community dialogues, and celebration of international days.
-Strengthening of community based structures to prevent and respond to SEA
-Supporting ministry of gender, child, and social welfare in coordinating protection activities
-Strengthening the rollout and implementation of primary GBV preventions interventions
PC 3 - Promoting nexus to development and solutions as the transition between the delivery of emergency humanitarian assistance, systems building and the provision of long-term development assistance to meet peoples’ needs, mitigate risks and vulnerabilities, exacerbated by systemic and socially reinforced exclusion of women and girls and persons with disabilities by;
-Provision of need based capacity building trainings to non-GBV humanitarian actors on integration of GBV in their sectoral responses
-Training of project staff on protection mainstreaming
-Sharing of protection analyses and issues to assist in non protection actors program planning and implementation.
The target population is 20,000 ( 4,000 boys, 4000 girls, 7,000 women, 5000 men)

1. Needs

An estimated 6.1 million women, girls, men, and boys will be or are facing protection risks and violations in 2023, with 3.1 million children urgently in need of immediate lifesaving and life sustaining child protection services (FSL and CP AoR report, July 2023)Repeated shocks have weakened community-based structures and services; limited access to services and limitations to accessing services will continue to push families to adopt negative coping mechanisms that expose the vulnerable, particularly women and children to heightened risk of abuse, exploitation, violence, and harm (South Sudan HRP-2022). According to the IRNA report conducted in February 2022, for Twic East, the heavy presence of small arms and light weapons in the hands of local youth and civilians and from the neighbouring Murle bandits and armed criminals presents significant protection risks and concerns among the local community. This led to increased threats of sexual violence perpetuated against women and girls or trafficking of boys and girls, the GBV caseload stands at 21,006 according to the GBVIMS report. Lack of awareness of the health services available, stigma associated with reporting and long distances to the health service centers prevent GBV survivors from accessing timely assistance. Many girls and women of reproductive age face a lot of challenges during menstrual periods including missing classes due to stigma. The need assessment conducted by Tearfund in February 2023 for Aweil, Twic and Uror indicates that women and girls perform most of the productive and reproductive roles in the household, especially collecting firewood and fetching water and are at high risk of GBV. Climatic shocks such as floods have destroyed school, markets and health facilities and economic shocks have resulted to high inflation increasing food prices hence pushing families to adopt negative coping mechanisms and are exposed to violence, exploitation, abuse and child marriage.-There are limited or no women and girls friendly spaces,women adapt to negative coping mechanisms.-There is a high rate of GBV, especially intimate partner violence.-High prevalence of child marriage and harmful traditional practices.-Women and girls of reproductive age lack menstrual hygiene dignity kits -There are no functional GBV referral pathways to access services.-Child labour is on high rise with majority of the children moving on the street to have a living-High rate of mental health due to the economic crisis and high cost of living.-Lack of knowledge on GBV and services-Women and girls move a long distance to fetch firewood exposing them to GBV risks-Prevalence of sexual exploitation and abuse

2. Project activities

Output 1. Women and girl friendly spaces (WGFS) established and strengthened-Construct 08 Women and Girl Friendly Spaces (WGFS)-Train WGFS staff on GBV awareness, risk mitigation and prevention-Facilitate structured and unstructured activities at WGFS-Distribute emergency kits for survivors, vulnerable women and adolescent girls (clothing, bedding, hygiene and dignity kits)-Mobilise and strengthen the capacity of GBV Community volunteers (advocacy) and social workers (women centre case management and case referral) and incentivize their roles.-Equip the women and Girls Friendly space with skill building material (embroidery, beading) material to function sustainably. Output 2. Awareness raising conducted on GBV risk mitigation and prevention-Produce and distribute IEC materials on GBV, PSEA-Conduct awareness raising campaigns, including mass events for international days-Facilitate community dialogue using a curriculum based approach( Engaging men through accountable practice/EMAP). Both men and women will be targeted in this intervention with the aim of reducing harmful behaviours and increasing gender equality. -Conduct quarterly community dialogues with traditional & religious leaders, on GBV risk mitigation and prevention -Conduct quarterly meetings with the Ministry of Gender, Child, and Social Welfare, and local authorities on GBV risk prevention-Conduct quarterly radio talk show on harmful traditional practices like child marriage. Output 3. Protection committees strengthened to engage with communities and refer GBV survivors to available services-Conduct trainings for protection committees on GBV and protection risks, and available GBV response services-Organise quarterly protection committee meetings-Form and train community based protection committee-Train traditional justice actors-Build the capacity of protection committees on Accountability to Affected Population (AAP) mechanisms, principles and practice.Output 4. Protection and GBV mainstreamed across the project-Conduct protection and GBV mainstreaming training for project staff (Health and WASH) to identify risks and develop action plans- Train MOGCSW staff, and community volunteers on GBV risks and protection including PSEA-Integrate protection, and GBV in WASH and Health activities, including distribution of IEC materials and developing cross-sector awareness raising messages-Distribute dignity kits to vulnerable women, adolescent girls, and GBV survivors-Support Ministry of Gender and Social Services in GBV prevention (transport and referrals)Safety audits and perception mapping conducted Output 1.6 GBV case management services provided-Train gender and Protection Officer, facilitators, and Social Workers on GBV case management-Train Gender and protection Officer in GBV Information Management Systems and GBV5W (standard cluster reporting) reporting-Implement case action plans for survivors-Provide unconditional cash transfer to SGBV survivors.-provide Focused and basic psychosocial support
