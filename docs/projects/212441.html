Project Summary

This project aims to continue and increase the accessibility to essential life-saving curative and preventative nutrition services for boys, girls, PLWs&other caregivers with attention to PWDs without any discrimination among IDPs and HCs, where the project will provide comprehensive quality CMAM services, disseminate and support IYCF-E practices and promote health and nutrition and basic hygienic behaviors within the PIN, Besides, the project is going to provide harmonized emergency community-based services for children and women of the PIN by applying integrated health, nutrition, child protection, and WaSH approaches. This project will target 10 sub-districts: Afrin, Raju, Ma’btali, Atareb, Al-Dana, Salqin, Harim ,Tell Abied,Ras Al-Ain and Maaret Tamsrin, targeting :181296 (IDPs 158520, HCs 21876 & 900 returnees) including 4675 PWDs, children U5 years 82968 ,68940 PLWs and 29388 of other caregivers.
The scope of services in 6 fixed CMAM centers, providing (TSFP, IYCF-E promotion and MNs interventions) within the following health facilities:
1. Al-Hakeem PHC in Afrin.
2. Kafr Naseh PHC in Atareb.
3. SDI salqin PHC in Salqin.
4-Harim CMAM center in Harim.
5- Tell Abied center in Tell Abied.
6- Ras Al-Ain center in Ras Al-Ain.
-1 SC within Harim General hospital providing required intervention and treatment for SAM with complication cases referred from SDI centers or other NGOs working there.
-10 outreach teams will be dispatched to the areas of high IDPs and those facing challenges in access to nutrition services located in PHCs. These Teams will provide nutrition, child protection, health, and hygiene-promoting services to children U5 years & PLWs including PWDs and other caregivers.
10 RRTs will screen children 6-59M and PLWs for malnutrition, referral and follow up who are malnourished, provide IYCF-E counseling for caregivers including PLWs, disseminating IYCF-E messages , and distributing MNs for preventing anemia and malnutrition among children and PLWs. in addition to referral for CU5 and PLWs with dangers signs and Medical problems to get required health services Moreover ,they will support hygiene promotion and rising WASH awareness by providing Key messages to prevent the risk of infection transmission.
As well as, SDI will conduct training to build capacity of Staff on IYCF-E and CMAM programs. SDI will coordinate and receive the nutrition supplies from UNICEF through Mearcy and WFP. SDI will ensure from sharing 4W and other reporting with cluster and donor also ,the MEAL team will do their work to ensure from the quality of the services provided including checklist, satisfaction surveys, ,IPTT ,reports and other methods and other specific tools to ensure the quality of the implementation.
SDI will Integrate a CVA component within the services provided by the OTPs and SC to target the identified nutritionally vulnerable groups including PLWS of children U2 alongside with integration of the proper awareness and SBC activities; nutrition education and promotion to encourage them to participate in SBC intervention and attend health service to translate the availability income for food into an improved nutrition outcome.
This intervention will be targeted 1000 BNs according to the nutrition cluster and CVA TWG guidelines.
Risk related to GBV on workers when visiting the homes, also on BNs
Mitigation: each 2CHW will work together, training staff on identification of GBV cases and safe referral pathways to ensure comprehensive management of GBVcases
Risk of discrimination or if a service is provided to specific groups or individuals at the exclusion of others.
Mitigation: the project design encompasses accessibility of services by vulnerable groups to ensure equal access for all BNs including PWDs
Risk of sexual exploitation by humanitarian workers of women and girls
Mitigation: Establish a confidential complaint mechanism, including victim assistance, train staff on the CoC & address any SEA complaints.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Although the security situation has somewhat stabilized, displacement persists, and significant needs persist, especially the situation remains fragile, worsened after Feb-23 earthquake. The catastrophic results have further worsened the humanitarian and nutritional situation, as indicated by the HNO-23 report, assigning a severity score of 4 to the project's targeted areas.On the other hand, the latest surveillance report in Aug-23 summarized that the prevalence of AM is higher than in 2022, with GAM at 6.1%&4.1% in Aleppo& Idlib. Additionally, the prevalence of underweight is 11.8%, and extreme stunting rates were observed.For PLWs the prevalence of AM was 9.8%&10.1% in Aleppo& Idlib. Furthermore, the prevalence of anemia among Women of Reproductive Age is classified as "catastrophic."SDI will comprehensively screen CH and PLWs through OTPs or outreach teams and provision treatmant for SAM and MAM, also refer complicated cases to SDI's SC. SDI will also implement BSFP to to prevent malnutrition During screening; CH and PLWs with external visual signs of corporal punishment, suspected abuse, and violence will be identified and referred to protection centers. As well, The team will also train mothers to use MUAC (FMA).SDI will ensure inclusivity for all, with a particular focus on vulnerable groups like those facing physical limitations or social isolation and PWDs. The staff will promote appropriate breastfeeding practices and other topics according to IYCF including hygiene promotion messages.SDI prioritizes AAP and stakeholder engagement (NC, NGOs, health directorates, local councils, camp managers UNICEF&WFP), with a focus on marginalized groups, is facilitated through a robust CFM accessible via multiple channels to facilitate greater sustainability. Additionally, SDI is committed to integrating protection measures, raising awareness among beneficiaries about potential risks. Gender balance is maintained through the hiring of female field staff for privacy enhancement, and the project ensures non-discrimination, awareness of GBV and PSEA issues, and verifies beneficiary satisfaction. Confidentiality is upheld for beneficiary information, and SDI's documentation policy prioritizes protecting beneficiaries' rights and dignity.The staff will be trained on communication, rights of PWDs, PSEA and GBV.R1: Conflict escalation and deterioration in security.Mitigation: Continuously monitor the situation & develop a comprehensive security plan. Provide staff with comprehensive safety & security training.R2: Traditional habits prevent women from leaving homesMitigation:SDI will bring the services close to communities and camps, to include all people in need.R3: Community ResistanceMitigation: Engage the community through outreach activities & communicate the project's objectives.R4: Closure of Cross-Border between Tukey and Syria.Mitigation: Maintain buffer stocks and a high level of coordination with the NC & supply lines
