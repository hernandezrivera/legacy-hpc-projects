Project Summary

A majority of the IDPs in North Mudug region reside in makeshift settlements characterized with widespread poverty and a lack of most social services. Poor living conditions in the IDP settlements including poor shelter and unfavourable coping mechanisms have exposed IDPs to of sorts of exploitation and abuse including acts of gender based violence. Past experiences have shown that a majority of the GBV survivors are female while men constitute a majority of the perpetrators. This confirms that GBV affects women more than men. Moreover, while all IDPs are prone to acts of violence including sexual violence, those from minority groups are at a much higher risk since they lack the clan protection. Despite GBV, IDP women and girls are also subject to harmful traditional practices including FGM and CEFM. Victims of GBV are in most cases blamed for the violence that they suffer. Most victims hence chose to suffer in silence and are unwilling to report whenever violations occur. While there have been efforts to provide required services, there are existing gaps particularly in far flanged areas such as Galdogob district where most service providers do not cover. While Puntland has passed the Sexual Offenses Act to deal with cases of rape, its implementation remains passive due to limited understanding and resistance from key community stakeholders. The proposed project seeks to respond to the issues raised above through provision of medical, psychosocial and material assistance to survivors; influencing attitudinal change amongst key stakeholders and strengthening the capacity of responsible community stakeholders to respond to and prevent GBV.

Project Activities

The proposed project seeks to enhance the protective environment for IDPs in Galkayo through;- Enhancing access to quality and timely care services for GBV survivors including health, psychosocial and legal services- Strengthening the resilience of the target communities towards responding to and preventing GBV and other protection concerns-Strengthening the capacity of authorities and duty bearers in assuming their responsibilities towards protection of IDPs-Promoting community participation in GBV prevention and response efforts as well as general protection of IDPs-Promoting safe alterrnative livelihoods for women and girls at risk of livelihood related GBV and other protection risks

Mitigation Measures

The following are among the identified risks facing the targeted population:- - The presence of heightened political, economic, and social grievances, - Inter-group competition and conflict - Preparedness and capacity (of at least one party) to use violence and violate human rights - The perceived absence of effective pathways for nonviolent conflict resolution (the lack of alternatives). The appropriate prevention measures include among others the following: Sensitization, Empowerment through capacity building and training to the targeted population and financial assistance.
