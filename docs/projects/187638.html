Project Summary

Coordinate the provision of lifesaving multisectoral assistance and basic infrastructure support in the camps by ensuring affective and accountable camp management, coordination of multisectoral services, capacity-building of CCCM field staff, provision of fire safety measures and prevention, general care and maintenance training and equipment, including developing fire warden systems, and promoting participatory system in the camps.
50000 IDPs will be benefitted from the proposed CCCM intervention in the targeted camps.
the interventions will be covering the following activities:
1- Site management activities
2- Community mobilization and participation
3- Coordination of the multi-sectoral assistance through IPs (food, health services, protection, non-food items, education).
4- Regular care and maintenance of camp infrastructure.
5- fire mitigation

4 - Needs

According to HNO/21, needs remain disproportionally severe among displaced communities. More than 1.5 million of the 2.7 million IDPs in the north-west currently shelter in over 1,200 “last resort” sites, with inadequate access to shelter, safe water, food, health and psycho-social support. Majority of IDP sites listed in the CCCM database are self-settled and designed to act as a temporary option in nature to provide only a minimum level of services, meaning that they often lack proper site planning, infrastructure and camp management systems. Moreover, incidents continue to impact IDP sites, exacerbating existing challenges and vulnerabilities, such as lack of proper infrastructure/site planning, camp management, fire mitigation team and units and maintenance services, especially in self-settled IDP sites where services and space are significantly overstretched due to overcrowding, in particular in North-west Syria.

5 - Implementation of activities

The project will cover the enhancement of the capacity of the camps' representatives in the targeted formal and informal settlements to prevent, mitigate and respond to the fire incidents by conducting FGDs and awareness-raising sessions with the camp management and beneficiaries taking into practice and account the COVID-19 preventive measures during the awareness-raising sessions and FGD's along with developing fire warden systems in the targeted camps. The main activities: - Constructing of fire units in the targeted camps and equipping them with recommended tools and equipment by Fire TF and forming Fire Warden Representatives committees FWRs, ATAA will form the FWRs in coordination with CCCM after conducting ToR. - Provide capacity building and ToT for community leaders and committee members for fire prevention by training IDPs (men & women) for fi re-fighting and first aid.- Awareness-raising campaign for fire prevention for IDPs in the camps.- Establishment of care and maintenance committees in targeted camps and providing them with a set of tools and equipment to be stored in the fire unit blocks. As well as, capacitating these committees with the required trainings to be able to hold the responsibility of assessing the maintenance needs and conduct the necessary action accordingly. - Coordinate the provision of multi-sectoral services to the camp's population through active communication with the actors providing/ planning to provide services within the same targe

10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

ATAA PSEA policy developed in December 2021 and shared with donors, and set a dedicated PSEA committee including (PSEA focal point, HR focal point, MEAL focal point, Grants and partnership focal point).ATAA adopted a core concept in all intervention to make PSEA and CSG as mandatory in all projects and activities, in this regard, all ATAA staff, volunteers and contractors will got orientation sessions about PSEA and CSG policies and sign both PSEA and CSG code of conducts.All project locations will include banners and complaint boxes with delivering messages from accountability teams regarding the complaints and feedback channels in addition to stand alone system regarding reporting and response of any PSEA case starting from receiving the report, to investigation process, and provide aid and support to the survivor till closure of the case after achieving the objectives of investigation.ATAA is PSEA network member participate in all regular network meetings, in addition to submit monthly reports to the network, in addition to conduct training for all staff about PSEA and target humanitarian workers in the intervention location to support the role of PSEA network.Gender balance will be a core point in the project within all teams (programs, media, MEAL) and locations will be accessed by women and girls safely.


11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

1- Overlapping, loss of access. - will perform multilevel coordination (Cluster, Field) -coordinated with all the stakeholder during the design phase2- Risks related to HLP and propriety conflicts - Ensure validation of the documents during the project - communicate clearly with all the stakeholders - Involved the camps management committees, KIs in the process in case of any conflict3- Sudden displacement of HHs. - ATAA will be monitoring all updates on the ground and if we noticed that any of the beneficiaries displaced.4- Increased number of cases infected with COVID - Training ATAA teams about best practices including social distance, sterilization and wearing face masks. - Providing teams with PPE kits (gloves, sterilization and face masks). - Printing awareness posters on food baskets about COVID-19 and best healthy practices to avoid it. - ATAA distribution teams will organize beneficiaries and make social distance between them during commodities distribution.5- Sexual exploitation and abuse of affected community members by anyone associated with the provision of aid. This risk will be mitigated by strict measures and the awareness of the field staff on this topic and by signing codes of conduct of all staff relating to sexual exploitation and abuse, and reporting via established reporting mechanisms of any concerns or suspicions regarding sexual exploitation and abuse, and investigation if necessary (hotline). A female employee will be assigned as a focal point
