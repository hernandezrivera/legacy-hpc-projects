Project Summary

As a result of the failure of the last two consecutive rainy seasons in Borena Zone, critical water shortage occurred and hampered the live and livelihood of the pastoralist communities.
To alleviate this problem, ANE will work to increase access to safe water supply by performing major activities like rehabilitation of non-functional water schemes and provide water through trucking. Construction of communal latrines will be done to increase access to improved sanitation facilities and basic WASH NFIs will be provided for the most vulnerable HHs so that they will improve their HH level hygienic practices and coupling with the intensive hygiene promotion activities that basically planned to improve knowledge and practice of improved behavior.
In general, the proposed set of sectoral WASH activities will ease water, sanitation and hygiene related problems among the targeted drought affected pastoralist communities in Borena Zone; so that the mortality and morbidity rate associated with WASH related diseases and risks will be reduced.
