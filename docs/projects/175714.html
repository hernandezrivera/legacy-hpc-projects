Project Summary

In 2020, the country has experienced one of the worst flooding in recent years and which has displaced a total of 350,000 people in more than 33 counties and affected over 1.04 million people. Intercommunal fighting and revenge killings has further led to displacements and loss of property. This situation compounded by the COVID 19 pandemic which has slowed the economic growth and provision of services to the populations has led to dire needs among the IDPs, Returnees and host communities making them more vulnerable and thus need for live saving interventions.
TADO interventions targets 7 of the most affected counties by flood, inter communal fighting and COVID 19 pandemic in the three states of Jonglei , Upper Nile and Warrap states. Flooding and inter communal conflict has led to displacements, diseases, destruction of farm lands, shelter, and loss household items. This has increased the vulnerability of IDPs, returnees and host community to protection risks. Sudden lock downs and restricted movements due to COVID 19 pandemic has further impacted negatively on the vulnerable populations. All these have led to strained households leading to increased food insecurity, health risks, malnutrition, shelter and NFI needs, protection needs and high inflation rates lowering community resilience to meet their needs.
TADO proposes to carry out a SNFI intervention through provision of in-kind reconstruction/ rehabilitation shelter, transitional shelter and NFIs among the most vulnerable IDPs, returnees and host communities. This will be done with strict adherence to COVID 19 guidelines. The shelter and distribution of in kind NFIs will prevent the communities form health risks, reduce protection risks, improve the nutrition status through food diversity, improve food security situation and improve the community resilience.
The intervention will use the cluster set guidelines in selection and verification of beneficiaries that is inclusive of old people, PLWs, child headed HH, People with disabilities etc.
The cluster AAP guidelines will be strictly followed throughout this intervention. This includes longer stay at project sites to study community dynamics and together set up community tailored AAP mechanisms. This will be through formation and training of community beneficiary accountability committees that will be inclusive to provide input on matters of concern to ensure all voices are heard and no one is left behind during the intervention. The feedback mechanisms will be followed throughout the project cycle.
TADO will mainstream protection in through joint interventions with protection partners on ground during registration and verification of beneficiaries, distribution of S/NFIs and post distribution monitoring exercise. During restructuring/reconstruction of transitional settlements, TADO will follow HLP guidelines to ensure that the interventions do not cause conflict among the communities. This will be through pronged interaction with the community to understand the land dynamics. Together with HLP partners, TADO will train the communities on HLP access rights, referral to mitigate land cases, improve access to legal aid ad set up mechanisms for solving land disputes. This will be done in collaboration with local authorities and other partners.
TADO will employ the static response and will collaborate with state focal persons in all target states to ensure smooth coordination with mobile partners and avoid duplication.

This project will provide access to basic shelter and non-food items to 7 most affected counties of Ulang with 6,000 beneficiaries, Nasir 3,000, Duk 10,000, Twic East 1,460, Uror 4,000, Tonj East 2,500 and Tonj south 4,000 respectively totaling to 30,960 to the vulnerable and affected populations.
Verification and registration reports, distribution reports and post distribution reports will be shared with the cluster for approval. TADO will have a monitoring plan in place to ensure smooth and timely

1 - Needs

South Sudan has massive shelter and Non-food Item needs which can be attributed to the political unrest that hit the country in 2013 and 2016. Internal intercommunal conflict further causes displacements of the communities thus increasing the need for these essential commodities.According to the IRNA conducted in Duk county in July 2020, massive flooding caused displacement of populations with the IRNA report showing that there were 56354 people who had been displaced by the flooding. Some of the immediate needs that were identified were distribution of emergency shelter and nonfood items(Duk IRNA report July 2020) The county is also having high food insecurity thus showing the need to support the vulnerable and affected population with multi sectoral interventions to avert loss of lives.Uror County in Jonglei state is having similar situation as Duk with massive flooding and food insecurity thus straining the existing S/NFI commodities and thus calling for emergency response to alleviate the needs of the vulnerable and affected populations. Ulang and Nasir Counties lie along Sobat river which also due to heavy rainfall burst its banks causing massive flooding and displacing populations in the two counties. A multi sectoral, multi agency IRNA conducted in October 2020 in which TADO participated showed that over 25626 people which is almost 50% of the total population had been displaced by the floods and were in dire need of shelter and non food items. The INRA report showed that shelters had been swept by floods and households had to run to highlands for safety thus straining the shelter and non food item needs in the county.(Ulang IRNA report November 2020) The situation is similar in Nasir county.Tonj East and South were also affected by the flooding and their situation is also made worse by the intercommunal fighting that destroyed shelter and also very severe food insecurity that has left the two counties in IPC 5(South Sudan IPC report Oct, 2020).Twic East has also been highly affected by the flooding and according to IRNA report conducted in July 2020 a total of 42886 people had been displaced by the flooding in the country. The intercommunal fighting also caused displacement and destruction of shelter according to the findings of the report. The recommendation of the IRNA was to have urgent supply of shelter and Non-food items.An IRNA conducted in October 2020 in Tonj East and South showed that massive fighting in the two counties caused displacement of the communities as 150 shops were burned down and looted and 16 villages in Tonj East burned down therefore causing massive need for shelter and non food items for the conflict and flood affected populations.COVID 19 pandemic has caused a major challenge to the needs of the affected populations as lockdown affected service delivery and made it difficult for population to look for livelihood. This has caused high inflation rates and lack of communities in the market further straining Shelter and NFI needs of the affected populations.Its due to the needs identified that TADO will respond to the affected populations by provision of in-kind shelter and non-food items to this vulnerable populations.

2 - Project activities

• Community inception and engagement meeting and formation of beneficiary accountability committees that will be responsible for complaint and feedback on the project implementation.• Needs assessment of the vulnerable and affected populations.• Training of the beneficiary accountability committees and the staffs on AAP principles and HLP guidelines together with protection mainstreaming.• Verification and registration of the beneficiaries as per the vulnerability criteria set by the S/NFI cluster.• Prepositioning of the supplies to the distribution sites• Establishment of CFM desks in the project implementation sites in collaboration with the local leaders and BAC.• Distribution of the in-kind S/NFI items to the registered and verified beneficiaries• Post distribution monitoring • Report writing and submission to the cluster for approval• End project survey to gauge the satisfaction of the services offered.

7.4.2. How will your project provide beneficiaries information about Protection against Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA)?

TADO will carry out mapping exercises to identify in-country service providers, conduct in close coordination with United Nations field missions, agencies, funds and programmes, Community-Based Complaint Networks, protection from sexual exploitation and abuse task forces and non-governmental organizations. TADO will implement its PSEA policy and identify the gaps in the provision of sustainable support and assistance to victims in project locations.

7.4.4. How will your project ensure feedback from the most vulnerable members (e.g. individuals living with disabilities, individuals who are not literate, women and girls)?

Feedback mechanisms provide organizations with data and perceptions from primary stakeholders such as individuals living with disabilities, individuals who are not literate and women and girls about the quality and effectiveness of their efforts. TADO will embrace the use of disability inclusion facilitators who will be able to communicate with people with disabilities, where possible sign language interpreters will be used in order to get feedback and audio recordings for the visually impaired. TADO will employ local community members to be able to get feedback in the local languages from the illiterate and women representatives to get feedback from the women and girls.

7.4.1. How will your project provide beneficiaries information about the project, feedback mechanism and/or complaint mechanism?

TADO will carry out Initial Community Engagement to understand the community and enhance their capacity to participate in the project. Training and orientation sessions on the processes of engagement will be emphasized. Do no harm, protection and gender considerations. TADO will Strengthen and train the community on the formation of Beneficiary Accountability Committees and other feedback and complaints mechanisms during project implementation (especially during assessment /registration and distribution of S/NFIs). A feedback desk will be setup during distribution. Committees will take into account gender and age, ensuring that committee members are representative of women, youth and elderly as well as people with special needs. Rapid Monitoring will be carried out at the distribution stage to hear grievances from the complainants and will try to resolve the issues as they arise. The CFM desk team will be trained and composed of individuals who can effectively address needs of the disabilities, illiterate individuals and women and girls separately and discreetly.

7.4.3. How will your project use the beneficiaries’ feedback to adapt project assistance?

TADO will proactively engage stakeholders and beneficiaries as well as partner organization about the project activities for timely access to relevant information on issues related to: the proposed project (objectives, approaches, budget, staffing and contact details) and how to lodge a complaint. Positive feedback will be used to improve the project implementation. Negative feedback will be used as a measure to correct mistakes and improve programming as well to the satisfaction of the beneficiaries.

8.11. Mitigation Measures

To mitigate the risk of violence against women and girls, the proposed interventions will be brought closer as possible to the beneficiaries and distributions will be done on time to ensure that the beneficiaries get to their respective homes in time to avoid exposing them to protection risks during distribution, TADO will also ensure women and girls are given priority during distributions.To mitigate the risk of lack of awareness on protection services, such as GBV and others, TADO will train the beneficiaries during registration, distribution and PDM, TADO will work closely with the protection partners t create linkages and referral pathways for survivors of GBV.To mitigate the risk of exclusion of marginalized groups in project activities and decision making, TADO will engage all the stake holders during project design project implementation and closure and conduct focused group discussions that will give an insight of the needs of the marginalized groups. The beneficiary accountability committee will also comprise of representatives from the marginalized groups thus ensuring inclusivity.The risk of land disputes will be tackled through following the HLP guidelines. TADO will ensure that they have maximum time with the communities to ensure that the project team understands the land dynamics of the host communities. TADO will also train the communities on the land rights and access to justice especially for the returnees. TADO will work in collaboration with the community leaders and relevant government authorities to mitigate all cases that might arise from settlements and this ensuring that the project activities to not bring or escalate conflict.

12 - Contribution to Collective Outcomes
12.1. Does your project contribute to any of the two Collective Outcomes, i.e. Food insecurity and Gender-Based Violence?

Yes

12.1.4. Quality, timely and inclusive protection services (including medical care, legal support, safety and mental health psychosocial services to children, women at risk and GBV survivors

During the project cycle, TADO will engage with protection actors to create linkages and referral pathways for vulnerable populations facing protection risks. This will include having protection specialist during verification of beneficiaries, distribution and PDM to mainstream protection and train the beneficiary accountability committees on protection issues.

12.1.2. Decreased proportion of populations facing IPC 3,4 and 5

Shelter NFI activities will contribute to food insecurity in a way that the provision of the items will enable the affected beneficiaries to save resources to cater for food and improved nutrition. Besides, Shelter NFI activities will save families who used to share house hold items which triggers Gender Based Violence to curb the practice and also avoids protection issues and saves dignity. The availability of non food items and shelter will give the venerable and affected populations time to concentrate on livelihood activities thus improving the food security in the target counties.

12.1.3. Equitable access to cross-sectoral basic services to vulnerable populations (including women, children, the elderly, persons with disability and older persons)

The provision of Shelter NFIs will enable the beneficiaries benefit from awareness on issues of COVID -19, GBV, Protection and will ensure that they are aware of their rights. It will also empower the different categories of beneficiaries to fully participate in decision making due to involvements and engagements for project sustainability. TADO will ensure that the most vulnerable in the communities are reached with services without discrimination based on the selection of beneficiary criteria.

12.1.1. Decreased prevalence of global acute malnutrition among children under age 5 in severity levels 3 and 4

By distribution of shelter and NFIs TADO will ensure that the children under five are not exposed to health risks that are an underlying cause of malnutrition. The distribution of kitchen sets will enable mothers to have food diversity thus improving the nutrition status of the vulnerable and affected populations and especially children under five years.TADO by distribution of these essential commodities will give the vulnerable and affected populations enough time to concentrate on livelihood activities thus improving the food security situation in the households and therefore reduce the food insecurity index in the counties.
