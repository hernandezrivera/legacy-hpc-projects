Project Summary

Spontaneous return of IDPs and refugees has already taken place in Darfur locations where improvements have occurred. Since the beginning of 2018, more than 280,000 displaced persons have returned voluntarily and permanently to their places of origin across Darfur . However, return communities still suffer from access to basic services, shortage of economically and environmentally friendly shelters, as well as limited access to land and environmental assets.
This project will provide environmentally friendly transitional shelters using Soil Stabilized Block (SSB) technology and train returnees and host communities on construction of shelters using the above SSB technology as alternative to Fired Bricks which used to cut trees for firing the bricks.
Environmental studies in Sudan revealed that a Fired Brick Factory of 100,000 bricks needs to cut 27 trees in order to fired the bricks,this Brick Factory is only constructed three houses, this reflect how many trees were cutting for processing of these Fired Bricks Factories.
Sustainable Action Group is a front line responder on the geographical area of North Darfur since 2005, expert good coordination and relationship with all humanitarian actors on the area.
In Addition to provission and training on SSB technology, in addition the project will select most vulnerable 1,000 IDP's households (5,000 individual; 1000 men,2500 women, 1200 boys and 1300 girls) from six IDP's camps include Rwanda, Argo and Dally IDP's camps in Tawila locality, Zamzam IDP's in El Fasher locality, El Abbasi IDP's camps in Mellit locality and Kassab IDP's camps in Kutum locality.These households will provided with restricted cash based (conditional cash transfer) to buy shelter materials locally produced.
The project will cover four localities in North Darfur:
1 - Kutum locality according to HAC there were verified returnees return from Kassab and Fatta Barnu IDP's camps to their original villages and also from Republic of Chad, these returnees will be targeted by the project in addition to most vulnerable IDPs in Kassab IDP's camp.
2 - Rural area of Dar El Salam locality show evidence of return from Zamzam, Shaddad and other IDP's camps
3 - Tawila locality endorsed Ruwanda, Argo and Daly IDP's camps from which thousands of IDPs recently returned to their villages in rural area of Tawila locality, these returnees will be targeted in addition to most vulnerable households from the three IDPs mentioned
4 - Zamzam IDP's camp in El Fasher locality targeted to select most vulnerable households to be provided with conditional cash tranfer to by shelter materials locally produced.
The aim of the project intervention is to support the communities (both returnees and host communities) by providing Soil Stabilized Machines (SSB) and train the community on how to operate and sustain the production of SSB and train them on how to use these SSB on construction of their house.
The project will follow the design of UN Habitat the professional UN on housing and settlement,that constitute of providing of SSB machines,construction of 25 houses for most vulnerable affected families as samples and a package of training of youth (male and female).
in addition the project will select most vulnerable 1,000 IDP's households (5,000 individual; 1000 men,2500 women, 1200 boys and 1300 girls) from six IDP's camps include Rwanda, Argo and Dally IDP's camps in Tawila locality, Zamzam IDP's in El Fasher locality, El Abbasi IDP's camps in Mellit locality and Kassab IDP's camps in Kutum locality.These households will provided with restricted cash based (conditional cash transfer) to buy shelter materials locally produced.

1 - Needs

According to Humanitarian Needs Overview (HNO) 2020, Sudan still host large number of vulnerable populations including those affected by natural disasters, armed conflict and drought, some two millions are still displaced of which 75% are women and children. Spontaneous return of IDPs has already taken place in Darfur locations where improvements have occurred. Since the beginning of 2017, more than 280,000 displaced persons have returned voluntarily and permanently to their places of origin across Darfur . However, return communities still suffer from a lack of access to basic services, shortage of economically and environmentally viable shelters, as well as limited access to land and environmental assets and livelihoods. . The propject prioritization based on the HNO/HRP 2020 mentioned below: 1- Severity scale (areas of severity 3-5). 2- Targeting by vulnerable groups residing in high severity areas ranked in HNO – based on displacement trends, hazard prone areas, health risks. 3- Targeting groups of highest vulnerability based on protection vulnerability criteria. Based on Rapid Needs Assessment conducted by SAG and Plan Sudan on November 2018 aim to identify gaps and needs on three sectors: Food Security and Livelihoods, WASH and NFIs &ES as well as recent Need Assessment conducted jointly by UNHCR - North Darfur, UNAMID, HAC and other national NGOs on first week of December 2019. SAG will intervene through NFIs&ES to provide Soil Stabilized Block technology (SSB) as environmentally friendly shelter materials to assist returnees and their host communities to change their behavior of cutting trees for housing to new environmentally friendly SSB. Based on criteria of selection the project will select most vulnerable 25 returnees households from the three localities and provide them SSB constructed houses as samples to train beneficiaries on construction. SAG will select youth (male and female) age between 19 to 40 years and men and women age 40 to 55 years, the parentage will be 30 % female and 70 % male (culturally the construction is a business for men). They will train on SSB Production and usage of SSB on construction of shelters. Based on Humanitarian Response Plan HRP) 2020, the project will select 200 participants (30% female and 70% male) from El Fasher locality,200 participants (30% female and 70% male) from Kutum locality, 200 participants (30% female and 70% male) from Tawila locality and 200 participants (30% female and 70% male) from Dar El Salam locality. In addition the project will select most vulnerable 1,000 IDP's households (5,000 individual; 1000 men,2500 women, 1200 boys and 1300 girls) from six IDP's camps include Rwanda, Argo and Dally IDP's camps in Tawila locality, Zamzam IDP's in El Fasher locality, El Abbasi IDP's camps in Mellit locality and Kassab IDP's camps in Kutum locality.These households will provided with restricted cash based (conditional cash transfer) to buy shelter materials locally produced.

2 - Activities/Outputs

Output 1: Returnees and host communities in four localities - North Darfur received training on environmentally friendly shelter techniques Soil Stabilized Block (SSB). Activity 1.1: Conduct coordination meetings with all stakeholders to inform and define the project for them. Activity 1.2: Contract with supplier to provide 20 Soil Stabilized Machines. Activity 1.3: Contract with engineer to select suitable soil for mixing with cement to prepare the material of SSB before start operation of the machines. Activity 1.4: Select 850 participants (youth male and female) from the target localities for training on both production of SSB and construction of shelters using SSB. Output 2: Most vulnerable 25 returnees households in rural areas of target localities and re- integrated IDPs in Abu Shouk and El Salam IDP's camps - North Darfur provided with constructed shelters made of SSB using as samples for training. Activity 2.1: Select the most vulnerable households mentioned above using selection criteria. Activity 2.2: Transport and distribute the machines, cement, water and suitable soil verified by the engineer to 25 sites of selected houses. Activity 2.3: Distribute the 850 participants on the 25 households selected to construct their houses Activity 2.4: Start training of participants on production of SSB and construction of shelters using SSB simultaneously. Output 3: Most vulnerable protracted IDPs in six IDP's camps - North Darfur benefited of conditional cash based intervention to buy shelter materials locally produced. Activity 3.1: Coordinate and collaborate with IDP's community leaders (Sheikh) in six IDP's camps mentioned above to select the most vulnerable 1,000 households using selection criteria. Activity 3.2: Contract with suppliers of locally producing shelter materials agreeing to receive vouchers from beneficiaries and provide them with shelter materials equivalent to their vouchers. Activity 3.3: SAG project staff to collect the vouchers from the suppliers and pay for them amount equivalent to their vouchers received from the beneficiaries. Activity 3.4: Conduct Post Distribution Monitoring (PDM) to ensure all beneficiaries received their preferable local shelter materials.

5.2 - Impact on environment/natural resources

The communities in Darfur use fired bricks to construct their shelters,the fired bricks factories use wood which come from cutting of trees. Study results show that a fired brick of 100,000 bricks which construct only three houses will need to cut completely27 big trees to be fired. The Stabilized Soil Block (SSB) technology is an environmentally alternative technology for the above negatively environmental impact, so the project will reduce cutting of trees and contribute positively on the climate change
