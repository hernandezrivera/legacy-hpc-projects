Project Summary

The project aims to increase access to life-saving and life-sustaining coordinated, equitable health services through:
1-Sustaining and expanding essential health services for those most vulnerable in need in northwest Syria by supporting pre-exist two PHC facilities and one mobile clinic for 2022&2023. in
(Idleb / Ma'arrat Tammasrin / Kelly) Kelly PHC G0700008
(Idleb / Ma'arrat Tammasrin ) G0702103- Mobile clinic 1
(Idleb / Aldana ) G0703011

out of them (45,000IDPs21,600 Male 23,400Female) - 7000 Pregnant and lactating women, -15,000 Boys and Girls, -3,065 disabled populations,1,596 Male/1,469 Females.))
through:
a- Provision of essential health care services by supporting two comprehensive health care centers and one mobile clinic.
b- Provision of medical supplies (these supplies are intended to support delivering essential PHC services, RH services, essential NCD services, CD, mental health, and child health.
Mercy-USA seeks to ensure these health facilities' continued operation through sustained staffing, provision of medical supplies, data management, and reporting, finance, governance, training, monitoring and oversight of program quality, and infrastructure maintenance support.
These facilities will provide obstetric and gynecological services, treatment for diarrhea, respiratory tract infections, skin conditions for boys and girls under five, and chronic diseases, including hypertension, asthma, cardiac ailments, and psychosocial support (PSS).
2- Mercy-USA will establish a cholera community treatment unit and 6 ORPs in NWS and operate according to agreed TORs. RCCE activities will be in place and coordinated with the RCCE task force to ensure harmonized cholera interventions in NWS.
Therefore, the project aims to continue increasing access to appropriate and impactful health services to address these urgent needs.

3 - Needs

The second quarter 2022 report of the HeRAMS shows that there were 546 health facilities in the country.The number of functional health facilities during the second quarter of 2022 was 372. There were 67 percent of hospitals, 168 percent of which are fixed PHCs, 44 percent of which are mobile clinics, 38 percent of which are specialized care centers, and 55 percent of which are other health facilities. Out of the total of 372 facilities, 159 were non-functioning, and 5 were functioning. There were over 200 health facilities in North of Syria that were functioning during the second quarter of 2022. These facilities are concentrated in Idleb and Aleppo governorates. Out of 546 assessed health facilities, 58% (316) were reported as fully functioning and 10% (56) as partially functioning.In September 2022, the Ministry of Health (MOH) of Syria reported an outbreak of Cholera.Between 25 August and 19 November, 46,409 suspected cases have been reported, including 97 attributed deaths to date at case fatality rate of 0.2%. Of the 3,571 samples tested with rapid diagnostic tests, 1,714 have tested positive. These additional crises contribute to the country's vulnerable population's increasing morbidities and mortality rates.Kelly PHC is one of the crucial health facilities that existed before the crisis providing comprehensive primary health care). 8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Protection /Risk/Threat: Gender-Based Violence GBV Likelihood: Low Impact: High Mitigating Measure: - Minimum Initial Service Package (MISP) as part of the essential health package. - Trained specialized health staff on providing first-line support, clinical management of sexual assault and rape, identification of GBV cases, and safe referrals. - Safe referral pathways to ensure comprehensive management of GBV cases connecting health services and clinical management with social services, mental health and psychosocial support (MHPSS), in a manner guarantees safety, confidentiality, and privacy of the survivor. - Recruit female staff in health facilities will increase access of women and girls to health services. Monitoring: Case Management / Reports Resources Required: Train the specialized health staff Protection /Risk/Threat: Sexual Exploitation and Abuse PSEA: Likelihood: Low Impact: High Mitigating Measure: -Mercy-USA maintains a ZERO TOLERANCE policy towards SEA in accordance with internationally-established protocols and standards for SEA protection.
