Needs

The crisis has left many adolescents and youth struggling to identify ways to positively engage in their communities and wider surroundings. Unemployment rates among youth are estimated to be close to 75 per cent, and are significantly higher among women. With limited opportunities, and uncertain about what the future has in store, adolescents and youth have grown increasingly frustrated and disempowered. Adolescent boys are more likely to be killed and injured, detained and recruited by parties to the conflict, or to be involved in child labour. Adolescent girls are particularly at risk of early marriage and other forms of gender-based violence, including sexual violence.Several recent studies with adolescents and youth inside Syria reveal that adolescents and youth have been drastically affected by the conflict. Security and peace inside Syria is the strongest and the most heartfelt aspiration by adolescents and youth. They have expressed fear and uncertainty of the future, limited access to quality education and opportunities for safe, dignified livelihoods, issues related to documentation and dangerous onward movement as some of their key concerns.The studies also highlight that young people are growing extremely frustrated due to continued lack of action on their key concerns and feel disempowered by their lack of voice in contributing to their communities, and opportunities for positive engagement in decision-making processes and as a result, many of them are adopting risky coping strategies.The needs of adolescents and youth require particular consideration both in the short-term and with a longer term view towards rebuilding the social fabric of Syria. Adolescents and youth (10-24 years) constitute 28.4 per cent of the population and are often amongst the most economically and socially marginalized groups in Syria. Young people not only have very few opportunities to participate in economic activities but also lack opportunities to participate in decision-making processes that can contribute towards the realization of their rights. They lack formal channels to effectively participate in social, civic and economic life. Ultimately, young people feel disenfranchised and have little faith in the existing participation platforms to have a positive influence on their futures.

Implementation of activities

- Selecting the geographical locations for centers.- employing trained and qualified staff.- Advertising the services that these centers intent to provide.- Registration stage based on deep assessment with engagement of key persons and community committees.- procurement of assets and required furniture and equipment,s.- providing the services to beneficiaries that are summarized as following:1- Life skills based education (LSBE).2- Youth Entrepreneurship Skills training.3- Vocational training sessions.4- Community-based-initiatives (CBI).5- providing grants to start own small business.- M&E visits and reporting.- Mid-term narrative reporting.- Final narrative reporting.

Protection Risk Analysis

Potential Risk 1: Sexual exploitation and abuse risk to which girls or women may are exposed to through either registration stage or during implementing project activities.Mitigation Measures: - employing well trained staff on (code of conduct).- assigning equatable number of male and female in staff.- awareness sessions for female of beneficiaries about the protection risks.- adopting specific complaint mechanism and assigning persons to track complaints.Potential Risk 2: limited opportunities for disabled to access the provided servicesMitigation measures: - Conducting of domestic visits during registration process to specify disabled persons in targeted areas in engage the key persons.Potential Risk 3: Tension may be generated between population in same community or between neighbored communities due to implementing services.Mitigation measures:- Give a priority to most in -need people and most vulnerable groups like female- headed households and divorced.- selecting beneficiaries equally among the different regions.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk1- Delayed getting required approvals from local authorities.Mitigation measure: close coordination with governmental and local authorities to speed up the procedures.Risk 2: Lack of essential services (electricity - water - sanitary) may delay executing the project.Mitigation measure: - Procurement appropriate generator and secure huge tank for water and selecting fit geographical location.
