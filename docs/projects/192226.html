Project Summary

This campaign is CCCL’s major campaign for the year 2022. Through this campaign, we are asking donors and supporters from all over the globe, to help the center’s mission in this critical time where all medications are scarce with less governmental subsidies and support in addition to the devaluation of the Lebanese Lira; and this is through donating via this campaign to help the center save kids’ lives
it is a part of an on-going project to fund raise for the treatment of children with cancer for the next 6 months. this evidential shortages of cancer medicines in the country and sky-rocketing prices which both mean a much needed intervention by CCCL is even more critical than ever and the support of international and national partners and supporters can go long way in fulfilling the goal of the project.
Today, we need to insure the cost of medication so that we can import them from abroad.
These medications are critical to every child with cancer.
At CCCL we have a survival rate of 85% but can reach in some types of cancer the 92%, It is unacceptable to lower this survivorship percentage because medications are not secured. It is unacceptable because this percentage is not a number. This percentage is kids with names, parents, siblings and friends at school. It is unacceptable because your support on medications will not stop.
the exporters, pharmacists, government, hospitals and providers of services such as the CCCL continued to struggle in ensuring the most needed supply of cancer medication. this shortages is surely published and mentioned by all kind of medias, be it audio and visual, written and social network. Reasons are many but it can be summarized by the lack of foreign currency and cash in the country, movement of capitals internationally and nationally to pay the international pharma and importers of these medication and many more such as the political and economic collapse.
the CCCL have identified a list of most crutial and urgent needs for the next 6 months and is aiming on building international affiliations and partnerships to ensure the needed fund to safe guard the patients lives. around 175 - 200 children with cancer could benefit from this project and hence retain the survivor rate and the quality treatments for our children suffering from all sort of difficulties which will includes treatments such as chemo therapy and beyond such as psychosocial support. this project will ensure this continuum and the quality of care to 50% of children with cancer in Lebanon.
