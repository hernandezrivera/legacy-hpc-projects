Project Summary

With the current policy set by the Borno state government on returning IDPs back to their localities, and the needs of them to rely less on the non-governmental organizations in life saving assistance program, there is a need to establish an equitable access to quality and adequate vocational educational empowerment program to the people building back their communities. As the conflict subside and people are returning back to their localities the need to provide a sustainable life building empowerment program to especially vulnerable people, youths and girls is needed. According to the 2023 Global humanitarian needs overview North-East Nigeria remains one of the world’s largest humanitarian crises, with at least 8.3 million people in need of assistance in 2023. The scale of suffering borne every day by women, men and children across Borno, Adamawa and Yobe (BAY) States remains undiminished. Yerwa Empowerment foundation wants to work diligently to provide targeted local community members with basic and vocational education programs aims at improving their livelihoods and that of their families, reduced the number of out of school girls, children subjected to child labor, and vulnerable community members and articulate their needs and facilitate their contribution to their own economic and social development towards promoting child protection and safety, and enhance positive child rearing, YEF aims to mobilize vulnerable members of the communities and IDPs for the inclusive, equitable and accessible school basic and education program. The program will work in two areas: intervention through basic and vocational educational programs, that will promote the sustainable development goals of equitable access to quality basic education program and protection of vulnerable people through a comprehensive humanitarian services that protect children’s rights and promote a culture of sound parenting and child safety. The program’s overall objective is to promote and improve inclusive and equitable basic and vocational educational program that will not only give them knowledge but also empower and trained them on demand skills within their localities. The project is targeting to reach 12,000 vulnerable children and girls both returnees and IDPs whom were out of school or are into child labor, disabled and cannot have a means to quality and inclusive education. About 6,000 number of out of school girls will be provided with adequate and equitable education and be empowered, 3000 number of vulnerable people will be provided with adequate and inclusive education and be trained on demanding skills, 3000 number of children who are into child labor within the age of (4-10) will be targeted and sensitized on basic education. The empowerment program will be based on demand skills needed within the targeted community. Child protection, climate change and hygiene promotion program will be mainstreamed into the program.

1 - Needs

It’s a well-established fact that most children especially vulnerable people within the targeted LGAs were abandoned and neglected by caregivers and community, inability of the parent or caregiver to provide quality education for this children and no emergence response have been taking to solved the issue or meet to their need while most girl child still faced difficulties in having access to an equitable and inclusive education with most either engaged into child labor, forced marriage, or roaming around the town hawking or begging with the number of turnouts in school especially during raining season becoming low due to children going to farm looking for a way to sustain their life. Bearing in mind the increasing number or nature of the out of school children and will still be increasing if emergence response of the communities were not met. To meets up with the HRP and Sector objectives, Yerwa Empowerment foundation will focus on providing timely educational emergence response services to Ngala, Mafa and Jere local government area of Borno state which are at high risk of increasing number of out of school children in other to restore peaceful co-existing and promote sustainable development in target location. It’s an established fact that lack of basic and vocational education and empowerment is inimical to the economy and would not promote their welfare but actually create stress and frustration among them which will encourage them to join street gang or accept an invitation to engage in violence.

2 - Activities

1. Establishing 10 temporary basic and vocational learning centers within 10 wards of the selected local governments were basic education and empowerment programs will be carried out to the selected targeted beneficiaries. 2. Selection of target population in the selected ward. Youths and CRGs leaders would be ambassadors that will ensure continuity of the program. The selection of the LGAs is because they are the LGAs mostly affected and are currently returning. YEF will initiate this up this project to other LGAs within Borno State as the security situation for running formal education improves. 3. Rolling out of package of services under the basic and vocational education services: This service will include; Supplies of ECD kits, School Hygiene Promotion Activities, climate change program, and vocational education and empowerment training. 4 initiation of the empowerment education Program which will include selection of CRGs leaders (2 Male and 2 Female in each) who will serve as Empowerment Ambassadors and capacity building of and as Empowerment Ambassadors and also developing a comprehensive curriculum on the empowerment program. 5. Community Participation, which includes parent and community involvement (which consists of involving a wide range of community stakeholders—parents, students, educators, social service personnel, and business and political leaders—to develop and form the structure of the school empowerment program tailored to meet each local community’s needs, resources, perspectives, and standards). 6. YEF intends to pioneered this project in the targeted communities as there is little or no such program in schools in Borno State. We at YEF are convinced that should this project be implemented, it will reduce the number of out of school vulnerable children and promote gender inclusive education and in addition, the project will increase school enrolment as number of vulnerable people and children subjected to child labor will be reduced and also parents will be eager to send their kids to school since and empowerment program has been put in place and from the testimonies most beneficiaries gives of the program in the communities. The empowerment incentives given to the children benefiting from this program will attract other children to attend and enroll in the school to also benefit.
