Project Summary

This proposed food security and livelihoods program is aimed to save lives through support to food access to meet basic food and nutritional requirements and prevent the deterioration of living standards through provision of emergency agriculture support targeting 20,000 vulnerable food insecure women, men, boys and girls in targeted districts.

1 - Activities

The project will undertake an intervention mix in all the targeted geographical locations of Buhera, Mwenezi, Mberengwa and Matobo districts. Firstly the project will provide monthly food baskets through in-kind assistance in which each of the 20,000 beneficiaries will receive a monthly food ration of 7.5 kg cereal, 1.5 kg pulses and 0.825 kg cooking oil for 3 months from January to March 2021 to scale up emergency food assistance. Secondly the project will provide horticultural seeds and small livestock breed, support livestock vaccination, disease surveillance and management to minimise the risk of further food insecurity deterioration. About 4,000 households tarteged to receive food assistance will receive horticultural seed packs of 20g tomatoes, 20g carrots, 20g leaf vegetable rape and cabbage, 100g butternuts, 20 g cucumber, 20g onions and 500g beans or peas per household as well as small livestock package of either 6 hens and a cock indigenous bushveld chickens and 50 kg poultry feed or 2 females goats and 1 buck in community at a ratio of 1buck per 13 females. Furthermore the project will provide training, extension and advisory services to extension officers and farmers focusing on climate smart agriculture, crop pest management, post harvest handling, storage and processing to increase yields and benefits.

8 - Accountability to Affected People
8.1 - Describe how your project was designed in consultation with communities? How will you incorporate communities in project implementation and what feedback mechanisms do you have in place?

Participatory approaches will be adopted by the project to incorporate community participation during implementation, monitoring and evaluation of the project. Consultations, interviews, focus group discussions and transect walks will be conducted by community representative as a way of incorporating them. ZCC will establish a set of clear and transparent procedures appropriate to the local context that will provide communities with access to information, and confidential means of voicing complaints on issues that may be within the control of the organisation. The project will adopt already feedback mechanisms that are currently used by ZCC in different programs. These feedback mechanisms are the Help Desk (complaints desk), suggestion box, hotline/tollfree line and Community Accountability Focal Persons
