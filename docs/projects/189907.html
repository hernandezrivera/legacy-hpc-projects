1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

To prevent an increase in acute malnutrition, and associated mortality, for the most vulnerable, specifically children under five and pregnant and lactating women, including those with HIV a multi-pronged approach will be implemented that focuses on both prevention and treatment of malnutrition. Support to PRN will focus on treatment of moderate acute malnutrition in children 6 – 59 months and acute malnutrition in PLW, including those with HIV. Blanket supplementary feeding will focus on prevention of acute malnutrition and micronutrient deficiencies in children 6 – 59 months through provision of a specialized nutritious food through the General Food Assistance. In addition, integrated nutrition & infant and young child feeding in emergency services are maintained and scaled up through integrated mobile brigades, static health teams and community health workers that support with demand generation and social mobilization. Community engagement for behaviour and social change will be implemented to support the adoption of key life-saving practices in health, optimal nutrition, infant and young child feeding practices, signs and symptoms of acute malnutrition, WASH, child protection and prevention of sexual abuse. In addition SBCC will focus on raising awareness and reducing stigma related to HIV and providing key information on where to access HIV/TB services.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Output 1: - Prepositioning of essential nutrition supplies at health facilities Output 2: - Implementation of Blanket Supplementary Feeding programme (BSFP) for children aged 6 to 59 months Output 3: - Procure and distribute (to the end user) lifesaving nutrition treatment supplies - Capacity strengthening of health staff at the facilities and accommodation centres - Support provision of quality care to prevent and treat child wasting Output 4: - Mobile brigades interventionsCommunity engagement for behaviour and social change to support the adoption of key life-saving practices in health, optimal nutrition, infant and young child feeding practices, signs and symptoms of acute malnutrition, WASH, child protection and PSEA- Demand Generation to increase the uptake of nutrition services and improve the adoption of IYCF practices through radio, and NGO partnerships Output 5: - Conduct SMART assessments to monitor the nutrition situation and inform program implementation- Monitor PRN implementation at health facility for program performance, stock management to include end user monitoring, and demand generation

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Working closely with the Protection Cluster and PSEA taskforce, WFP will coordinate in dissemination of awareness raising on gender-based violence and child protection. Furthermore, training sessions on protection, accountability to affected populations and protection against sexual exploitation and abuse (PSEA) aim to strengthen awareness of field-based teams to ensure the centrality of protection throughout the assistance.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

Complaints and Feedback Mechanism (CFM). During all stages of the project life cycle, to ensure beneficiaries can refer their feedback, complaints, suggestions or seek clarifications on any aspect of the assistance, WFP ensures awareness of the affected population on three key CFMs: a) suggestion boxes made available on distribution days; b) helpdesks/ clearly indicated focal points to directly address issues on distribution days c) tollfree inter-agency hotline, Linha Verde da Resposta a Emergencia (1458). Furthermore, regular protection monitoring through focus group discussions and individual interviews by protection staff also present opportunities for the population to present their concerns and provide accountability mechanisms.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

There is overlap between beneficiaries that are disabled and those that are acutely malnourished and the same quality of services will be provided regardless of disability status. Additionally, the BSFP is blanket for all children 6-59 months, including those children with disabilities.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

There are not strong environmental considerations related to this project nor any environmental assessments planned.
