Project Summary

To prepare for and respond to an outbreak of COVID-19 in North-East Nigeria, IOM will scale up the ongoing WASH intervention (HNGA20-WSH-161691-1) through: (a) Provision of safe, equitable and adequate water, including repair and upgrading of water systems, water trucking, chlorination/disinfection of water points, and support health facilities with the provision of water; (b) Provision of safe and digniﬁed sanitation facilities, including care and maintenance of sanitation facilities, regular desludging of latrines, and installation of handwashing stations; (c) Public health awareness/hygiene promotion and disease containment through risk communication and community engagement, including risk communication through the door-to-door campaign, installation of a public-addressing system, production of Information, Education, and Communication (IEC) materials and provision of personal protective equipment (PPE), sanitation, and hygiene materials and tools to community volunteers and WASH committees; and (d) Provision of basic WASH/hygiene items, including procurement and distribution of basic Hygiene Kits at household level and cleaning supplies for latrines, home and health facility disinfection.

1 - Needs

The possibility of a spread of COVID-19 in North-East Nigeria is very high. The already dire situation in North-East Nigeria is likely to be further exacerbated by the COVID-19 pandemic, putting additional strains on WASH facilities in camps and surrounding communities.Adequate and functional WASH services are required to avoid a spread of the COVID-19 pandemic. The need for strong epidemic preparedness and response activities in WASH is essential to avoid the spread of COVID-19 throughout the BAY (Borno, Yobe, Adamawa) states. IOM will therefore strengthen COVID-19 preparedness and response efforts in the area of WASH in camps and camp-like settings, and surrounding host communities. This will be achieved by scaling up existing WASH service provision, including hygiene promotion, sensitization and community engagement, including on sound prevention including barrier measures, provision of clean and safe water, distribution of essential hygiene items and kits, and construction and maintenance of sanitation facilities, including handwashing stations, as part of activities towards the enhancement of enhancement of Infection Prevention and Control (IPC) activities. The intervention will be embedded in a coordinated multi-sectoral COVID-19 response led by WHO and the Ministry of Health, engaging the Borno State Ministry of Water Resources (MoWR)/State Government, the WASH, Health, and Camp Coordination and Camp Management (CCCM) Sectors, and the communities, thereby directly contributing also to the public health capacity for preparedness and response to other risks. All IOM activities will be in line with the WASH Sector strategy

capacity building of water system maintenance committees and WASH committees. - Provision of safe and digniﬁed access to improved sanitation facilities through construction of new sanitation facilities (latrines and showers); Rehabilitation and upgrading of existing sanitation facilities; Operation and maintenance of sanitation facilities; desludging of latrines, provision of hand washing stations (inclusive of soap and management systems for refilling and maintenance.) at public sanitation facilities and institutions as well as other key places e.g. entrances to camps, distribution sites, community meeting centres, water collection points and isolation centers if established. provision of solar lamps at blocks of latrines and showers; provision of waste bins and waste disposal pits; and transportation of garbage to disposal sites. - Affected communities beneﬁt from community-tailored gender and age-sensitive hygiene messages aiming to promote hygienic behavior and practices, and sound prevention including barrier measures, through sensitization sessions with community members and stakeholders through appropriate communication channels that adhere to COVID-19 prevention measures, in particular avoiding generating crowds and respecting social distancing guidelines; establishment and /or continuation of WASH Committees and provision of WASH facility maintenance tools - WASH Committees and cleaning staff will be trained on appropriate cleaning and disinfection procedures, and equipped with the appropriate PPE.; establishment of School Hygiene Clubs; training of Hygiene Promoters and conducting hygiene promotion activities; cholera preparedness and response activities; distribution of hygiene, cholera and menstrual Hygiene Management (MHM) kits and COVI-19-kits, production and dissemination of contextualized and agreed Information, Education and agreed Communication (IEC) materials. - Conducting Safety audits on WASH facilities and consultations with key informants. - Provision of safe and digniﬁed access to improved sanitation facilities through construction of new sanitation facilities (latrines and showers). - Provision of safe and digniﬁed access to improved sanitation facilities through desludging and cleaning and desinfection of latrines as per sector’s standard. -Provision of safe and digniﬁed access to environment through provision of sustained environmental sanitation services (solid waste management). - Provision of safe and digniﬁed access to improved sanitation facilities through construction of sanitation facilities in institutions and relevant public places provided with adequate gender segregated sanitation facilities including those or people with disabilities (latrines, showers, handwashing stations) and services as per sector’s standard and in consultation with the community. - Provision of safe and digniﬁed access to improved sanitation facilities through establishment and/or continuation of WASH committee- composed of 50% women. And training on Operation and Maintenance (O&M) activities, including appropriate disinfection methods -Distribution of hygiene, cholera and MHM kits complemented by cash modalities where feasible. Hygiene kits will be paired with contextualized IEC material on the correct use of items (e.g. for the safe usage of water purification tablets and chlorine). -Affected communities beneﬁt from community tailored hygiene messages Including on COVID-19 prevention measures aiming to improve hygiene behaviors and practices through hygiene promotion trainings and campaigns. - Conducting Safety audits on WASH facilities and consultations with key informants. - Strengthen the resilience of conﬂict-affected people and promote early recovery, durable solutions and social cohesion.

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

IOM will ensure accountability to the affected population and most vulnerable community throughout all stages of the project life cycle. During the design of the project and throughout the planning stages, IOM will promote active participation and feedback from the targeted population in assessing their needs and specific vulnerabilities, including those of women, youth, elderly, and persons with disabilities, among others. This will ensure, for example, that the physical abilities of the elderly and people with disabilities are factored into any forward planning in terms of accessibility to services provided. IOM will also seek regular feedback from the targeted population during the implementation of the project, most notably through the community-based WASH committees. Affected populations may also provide feedback or submit complaints through the suggestion boxes or other elements of the community-based complaint and feedback mechanism. This mechanism is managed by the IOM Camp Coordination and Camp Management Unit. After the project has been completed, IOM will conduct post-intervention surveys to ensure further accountability to affected populations and promote learning.
