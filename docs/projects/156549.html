Project Summary

The Project will provide two on-wheel Mobile Medical Units fully equipped made in Syria with Primary and Secundary Healthcare mobile equipments for diagnosis (X Rays, Ultra Sounds, ECG, Lab blood and urine tests, primary and secundary surgery, obstetrics, paediatrics), with a full team of doctors and nurses, to serve the most deprived population in hard to reach areas in Deraa Governorate (Laja and environment)

Needs

Close to eight years into the crisis, the scale, severity, and complexity of needs across Syria remain deep and far-reaching. The conflict has claimed countless lives, over half of the country’s population have fled their homes, and 13 million people remain in need of humanitarian assistance, with 5.2 million of them facing acute humanitarian needs. Millions of people have fallen into poverty, and many were forced to resort to harmful coping while facing increasing protection risks.Despite some reduction in the level of hostilities in certain parts of the country and an ease in access restrictions, prolonged hostilities have caused extensive displacement, infrastructural damage and a disruption of service provisions. As the crisis endures, people’s resourcefulness alone remains inadequate to counter deprivation that – for millions– is only mitigated by humanitarian assistance. MSJM is running in hard to reach areas in Syria an impressive fleet of seven PHC and Hospitainers and 12 ambulances fully equippped with specialized medical teams. We have assessed regions where rapid deployed mobile devices are required as an alternative to the dramatic lack of medical center and active hospitals. So far we did not find better that mobile devices. The needs are immenses.The location chosen by our Project is rural Deraa The needs ass assessed by our medical staffs are generally in this Province as follow: The majority suffers from war after effect and general health negligence that has empowered the proliferation of infections, chronical diseases and allergic symptoms. Cardiovascular problems, hypo and hyper tension as well diuretic problems and diabetes are more generalized due do the lack of prevention strategy. The local population has to move kilometers to find a medical center and, much more, for a hospital. Especially the pregnant women face many dangers for the lack of proper obstetric centers and healthcare. Prenatal and postnatal care are non-existent and this puts at risk the new born as well as the mother.Some dermatological diseases and infections are also noted (like rosacea, fungus, leishmaniosis etc...). We have assessed cases of dermatitis, infections (including sexual) due to the lack of hygiene. Gastritis, hypertension and diabetes proliferate. We have cases of hernia that could be easily treated but the lack of treatment could develop into occlusion. Many adults and children are affected by malnutrition and are in dire need of vitamins. A high rate of children remains not vaccinated and thus should be oriented to the government vaccination mechanism.Many persons carry untreated wounds that are infected. Others would need a bone surgery for lack of proper post fracture treatment. Many recent fractures needs prosthesis and, of course, many persons are awaiting for an artificial limb to recover. If a Mobile Hospital will not be able to answer to all the expectations, at least can we accompany those people in distress and be their intermediate with the government authorities and facilities or with private donors. It is important to relocate these needs in the actual geographical context: After the shift of control of Deraa Province, there have been freedom of displacement from and to this governorate. This has helped the relocation of people. In the countryside of this governorate medical facilities are not coping with the number of returnees and their needs. People must cross between 25 to 50 kms to reach an active hospital. The region still lacks of internal public transportation and the patients need to hire taxis that they cannot afford. The existent medical facilities lacks of permanent medical staff (we have been told by the local health directors).

Protection Risk Analysis

This project is tailored to be an experimental protection implementation tool with the 80000 patients that are beneficiares: The medical services will serve all vulnerable people without any consideration of ethnic, gender, religion or political background.During our contacts with the patient, we should be aware to notice the following : What steps are taken to reduce the risk of sexual violence and exploitation in public and private settings? Are men and women, girls and boys asked how safe they feel in and around the hospital? Are staff familiar with the referral path for GBV incidents?What barriers exist for specific groups? Are some members of polygamous families disadvantaged? What efforts are made to ensure people with mobility issues can access healthcare? Do some groups resort to harmful strategies to access or prevent healthcare for themselves or for others? Do our health and sanitary solutions respond to the different needs and priorities identified in the analysis? What is the feeling of the patients toward the way of acting of our staffs ? Do they feel protected or aggressed ? To better implement the No harm/minimize the harm's principle we will be in direct contact with the beneficiaries, especially the most vulnerable. Our mobile hospitals are opened to all beneficiaries that seek medical services without ethnical, religious, gender and/or political discrimination. MSJM, following the WHO and ICRC, will provide a gender base analysis (GAM/GEM) in the timeline of the implementation of this Project : How does the crisis affect the medical needs women, men, girls and boys in different age groups? In the need analysis we have taken into account the following :What are the relative rates of male- and female-led families accessing health treatment? What is the best way to put women and children at ease in the auscultation and diagnostic process as well during first aid or surgery intervention?How to resolve delicate questions of GBV, sexual infections, physical trauma due to physical agression ?How to propose an inclusive treatment for women whose cultural or religious tights refuse to be auscultated by a man ?Is there any gender controversial position taken by a patient ? How to help him/her feel secure ? The Medical Supervisor and the M&E manager will focus on the performances following the NO HARM principle applied to the medical field as to take the maximum rate of precaution in the treatment of the patients : diagnosis, prophylactic treatment, small surgery, second care surgery. Our stall will partner with our patients for the best output of the Project. Interviews with the patients beneficiating from the Project are an important task of the M&E scrutiny. The Medical Supervisor and the M&E manager will accompany the Project management in order to always create an up to date adjustment of the Project outcomes in order to ensure its total success in providing the services that are aimed to be provided in the desired timing. A complain box will be visible in the mobile hospital and will be opened by both the M&E manager and aht Medical Supervisor.MSJM will ensure regular monitoring of all activities through its staff, and namely the Project Manager and the M&E manager, who will ensure a first level of control and the achievement of the project's objectives and results through constant monitoring of activities and related indicators. Every medical staff will have its own registry duly dated with the name and status and affection of each patient. The clinic field and the surgery fields will have their own records with the explanation of each case and the treatment/surgery that was administrated as well as the outcomes.
