Project Summary

Silk Road seeks through this project to meet the needs of people through the provision of housing rehabilitation services and seasonal supplementary NFIs and shelter for the affected beneficiaries (Host, returnees & IDPs) whether they were tenants owners depending on the actual needs of the targeted people which will be identified and verified by our field team where Silk Road will provide information and data of existing vulnerabilities related to Shelter and damage level, negative coping strategies and NFIs needs.
The side of the project focusing on Shelter will support to sustainably repair/rehabilitate housing to owners/tenants/returnees/host families while the other side of the project focusing on NFIs will respond to the housing and most pressing material needs of populations affected by conflict, especially IDPs are protected from seasonally and adverse weather and their core and the essential non-food item needs to be met.
The first sid of the project is targeting 560 shelters units through the provision of housing rehabilitation services to meet the needs of people through the provision of housing rehabilitation services for returnees & IDPs people for the affected beneficiaries whether they were tenants owners depending on the actual needs of the targeted people which will be identified and verified by our field team after targeting communities in real needs for shelter assistance, where Silk Road will provide information and data of existing vulnerabilities related to Shelter, negative coping strategies and damage level as well.
This Side of the project will target at least 15 to 20 % female/child-headed households (female-headed HHs mean the absence of a male supporter/child-headed HH refers to the loss of supporting adults; classifying both cases as vulnerable). Furthermore, the provision of shelter rehabilitation services meets one of the basic survivals needs of women, girls, boys and men and can strengthen protection in a physical sense and improve the shelter and living conditions for the people who will be targeted.
The second side of the project aims to address the seasonal NFIs needs and improve the living conditions of vulnerable IDPs and hosts families living in harsh conditions difficult during the winter season.
Silk Road approach in the implementation of this part depends on accuracy and efficiency where we will conduct the vulnerability assessment for all registered /potential beneficiaries (Lists come from secondary data we have, local councils, other like active actors on the ground) to identify the most vulnerable in need of seasonal NFIs support as follows
1- Provision of winter NFIs and winter clothing for 1620 families. Silk Road will allocate $20 per person for purchasing winter non-food items that include, but are not limited to:
Mattress / Sleeping mats, Blanket, Carpet, Plastic Sheet, Boots, Gloves, Hat, Jacket, Jumper / Wool Sweater, Socks, Trousers, Scarf and Thermal Underwear. This means that not all families will receive the same amount (Silk Road will consider the cluster and Cash working group guidelines).
The vouchers will be loaded with 50% of the total amount allocated to the family at the end of November while the rest amount will be loaded after 20-25 days as redemption will be opened for targeted beneficiaries for 20 days after the loading each time. The purpose of this system is to enable the monitoring team to verify that the amounts are being utilized according to what is planned for purchasing winter items.
The proposed activities will ensure that they do not create additional risks for the affected population but will provide the best solutions to the suffering population in the targeted areas, also Silk Road will ensure that there is no health impact in this S-NFI approach on the targeted population beside we will adhere to internationally recognized standards and best practices, ensuring that services are provided in line with the Do No Harm framework, and humanitarian

4 - Needs

According to "IDP Shelter Situation - HNAP 2021", 12% of IDP households live in damaged shelters, while 43% of IDP households have been unable to afford shelter repairs and 42% of IDP households reported cold damp conditions in their shelter as the primary issue. Silk Road will provide shelter rehabilitation in 5 subdistricts which are Dana, Atareb, Jisr-Ash-Shugur, Badama and Ra's Al Ain where the severity scale is 4 (Shelter cluster) while it is 3 in all target locations in terms of NFI severity. According to the need assessment conducted by Silk Road, the Shelter situation continues to worsen, in terms of the available shelter capacity, the adequacy of shelter conditions and in particular the affordability of rent. There is a lack of financial resources, high prices, and limited purchasing power, which places additional burdens on the population, especially with the onset of winter and the high price of heating and cooking materials such as gas and fuel. Regarding the most common adaption strategy used to deal with the lack of non-food items, most of the interviewed said the sale of household assets, borrow money, the sale of productive assets and some of them reduce food intake. Regarding how families face winter heating and the strategy for coping with the cold, most of them are depending on burning firewood when available with no or lower cost, burning plastic and other waste such as paper including cardboard and coal, or they may use blankets if available.

10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Silk Road has zero-tolerance for some special cases such as any acts related to terrorist or radical groups, PSEA related issues including rape, assault or sexual exploitation, blackmail, possessing a weapon, threatening or assaulting another person or corruption. These cases necessitate the most severe types of disciplinary actions besides the termination of employment with the possibility of legal prosecution in the critical cases that require this. This applies to employees, beneficiaries, workers, suppliers, partners and any party that may be related to Silk Road work (All these groups are requested to sign and understand the code of conduct of Silk Road before proceeding in any type of work with Silk Road). Bullying, harassment (including sexual, gender and racial harassment) and discrimination are not tolerated, along with any unwelcome comment or behaviour that is offensive, demeaning, humiliating, derogatory, or any other inappropriate behaviour that fails to respect the dignity of an individual.Silk Road requires all team members and partners to immediately report suspicions of sexual exploitation or abuse. Silk Road encourages all beneficiaries and community members to report suspicions of possible sexual exploitation or abuse of or by any team member, partner or visitor. Any staff member who has concerns about the behaviour of another staff member should raise these immediately with the line manager, HR or another senior manager, or the CEO.
