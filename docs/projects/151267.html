Project Summary

The Project interventions is targeting 14806(2990 Women,2956 Men 4060 Boys &4800 Girls) in 4 districts in Lower Shabelle region Merka, Qoryoley, Kurtunwarey &Wenlaweyn districts, The populations being targeted are IDP's &Vulnerable hosted communities especially families with malnourished children .
Through this project health activities will be carried out in the sense of sectoral response of the bellow activities in the program Implementation
-Supporting 11 health facilities in the 4districts, functional with all the necessary requirements enterms of Human recourse Health Equipment, Laboratory, Pharmacy, but they are working on Voluntary bases &their agreement was expired , AYUUB will strength &proposing for this project for supporting them staff incentives &Supplies,, Provide basic PHC services including EPI Nutrition &consultations of communicable &non-communicable to 2990 Women, 4060 Boys &4800 Girls including 4,500 <1 year of age in IDPs/Host Communities, Access quality integrated health, Nutrition &Wash service by providing safe routine &supplementary immunization to 4,500 children under the age of five years &to 2600 Women of child bearing age including <1 years through outreach Integrated Emergency response service delivery &include the Service AWD Cholera prevention, holding per month 36 Awareness campaigns &Social Mobilizations on AWD Cholera Prevention, childhood illnesses including malaria, pneumonia, diarrhoea &measles, carry out Referral Services support at the delivery cases complicated &children Under five to the Merka hospital &to the referral service targeting 603 , 2% of the PLW's &of the children U5 &providing Training on emergency preparedness &response
AYUUB will coordinate closely with regional sub clusters of Nutrition through monthly sub cluster meeting in Lower Shabelle to enable it share its program progress, lesson learnt and challenges as well as the existing gaps in line with proposed intervention. AYUUB will also coordinate with Federal ministry of health and WHO for technical guidelines and supportive supervision.
AYUUB will demonstrate their commitment to accountability to affected populations by ensuring feedback and accountability mechanisms are integrated into organizations’ strategies, programme proposals, monitoring and evaluations, recruitment, staff inductions, trainings and performance management, partnership agreements, and this will be highlighted in reporting. AYUUB will promote accountability and provide information to the MOH and donors about the progress of the programme in terms of project activities and financial.
AYUUB will collect gender disaggregated data by sex and age of relevant data related to Nutrition activities. AYUUB will ensure that all segments of the affected population including women have equal access to nutrition assistance and that targeted support to advance gender equality is based on a gender analysis.
AYUUB shall apply gender segregated community consultation meetings enabling women and girls to address their specific concerns, prioritize their needs and appropriately inform the development of proposed activities.

Needs

According to the Humanitarian needs over view and health Cluster reports, The number of people-in-need of health care and support is estimated at over 4 million in Somalia 49% % male and 51% % female . Excess mortality is driven by malnutrition, disease outbreaks and lack of healthcare for the most vulnerable and frail. IDPs, especially those displaced by conflict and those from minority groups have lower access to healthcare than others (JMCNA 2018) and face barriers to social and health services. Significant numbers of people in hard-to-reach areas and are exposed to violence due to conflict.Measles affected over 6,600 children up to August in 2018 and the recent cases of vaccine-derived (cVDPV) polio underlines the continued risk posed by overall low routine vaccination coverage (average of less than 65%) in Somalia. The high incidence of communicable disease is concerning within the food insecure population, and leads to worse health outcomes in malnourished children and adults. Furthermore In 2018, by end-August, over 6,200 cases of AWD/Cholera were reported, along with over 9,200 cases of malaria. In 2017 Somalia experience and alarming 79,172 cases of AWD/Cholera.Lower Shabelle region alone accounted for 36 % of all malaria cases, 99% of all cholera cases and 12% of all measles cases reported in Central South Regions of Somalia. The lack of adequate antenatal care puts women at elevated and recurrent risk of death due to obstetric complications.In September 2018, Some communities in Marka district experienced displacement and eviction after FG aligned with AMISOM Kicked off AS , affecting about 29 villages in these districts.The need of the IDPs is to access services of life saving primary health services to enhance prevention and treatment to communicable and non-communicable diseases, access to maternal health care services to reduce maternal and child morbidities, providing immunization to children and treatment of common child hood disease.

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

The Project is delivering health care services to all affected population, by giving priority gander and age equality, regardless their affiliation or background, which can significantly contribute in the improvement of beneficiaries’ protection. Beside this, the areas where the project is delivering the services is safe and full control of SNA forces, secondly, AYUUB had a good planning in the project design stage, related with the selection of facilities sites, away from military base of forces checkpoint, stations or governmental premise, this makes the likelihood of protection risks occurring very rare. In terms of general security in the area, there can be armed clashes between AS and SNA forces, usually results from AS hit and run attacks to the SNA points, AYUUB by applying its Risk Measurement and Mitigation policy, will mitigate the risks of protection for the beneficiaries and staff through awareness of the area security situation during the Health Education and Hygiene promotion sessions conducted in the facilities.

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

The Project is targeting all different ages and gender of the affected population in the project areas, with health care services, the services will equally reach to all population who access the facilities and need for treatment, there will not be risks of exclusion based in displacement expected in the project services, as AYUUB had a clear policy in the project design and planning phases, by capturing a better estimated population with all respective ages and ganders, in consultation with the community and local authority in the needs assessment.among the additional mechanisms to prevent such risks is the monitoring and evaluation system that will go with the implementation process to disclose all alteration from the planned activities of the project for correction and timely improvement.Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence *Please identify the main risks facing the targeted population, as related to the lens proposed above and the appropriate prevention/mitigation measures in your project.Basically, the areas where the project is planned to serve is fully controlled by SNA forces, as part of AYUUB policy which was revised after the AS banned NGOs in their held territories, the organization doesn’t deliver visible services like health and Nutrition in the AS controlled areas to avoid looting and staff detention, therefore, impacts of armed conflicts are rarely likelihood in the services areas, except small things like that one of AS hit and run launch against SNA bases.To mitigate such risks, AYUUB had a good plan in the site selection of the facilities, by having MCHs where the beneficiary’s access in away from SNA bases or governmental facilities, to avoid impacts of armed conflict on the beneficiaries, staff and facilities as well

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

AYUUB has a risk and Mitigation Policy in place that will be applied during Project Implementation. The area where the project is planned to deliver the services is full control of SFG, there is no other actors can create armed clash, there can be small intra- clashes between SNA forces, but this will not be target to the beneficiaries of the project and AYUUB in applying its Risk assessment and mitigation policy will do mobilization to both beneficiaries and project staff in the respective area when need calls.
