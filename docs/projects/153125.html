Project Summary

Jonglei and parts of Eastern Equatoria are currently on IPC 4 category. ACROSS is proposing a project that will swiftly deliver urgently needed but targeted humanitarian assistance while simultaneously rolling out a livelihood recovery for 10,000 households, in Jonglei and Eastern Equatoria states. The purpose of this project is to improve and sustain essential crop production and productivity through direct seed and farm tools and protect and rehabilitate the livelihoods of the most vulnerable groups in the community through essential crops, livestock, and vegetable production as per the livelihood zone capacity in the targeted counties. ACROSS will fully engage community leaders, women’s groups, the youth and others within the community throughout the life of the project. The project is designed and will be implemented with knowledge of the seasonality context and the livelihood calendar. The communities in these regions have limited productive assets, and require immediate assistance to tackle the food insecurity which is high on the cluster agenda. This project will be integrated with SSJR4 Dutch Relief Alliance (DRA) funded and Swedish PMU funded projects that ACROSS is currently implementing in Pibor and Kapoeta counties. The project will seek to diversify the household diet and address malnutrition, which is mostly a problem for children, pregnant and lactating mothers. The provision of improved inputs like seed and agricultural tools in a timely manner will significantly contribute to the restoration of agricultural inputs in remote locations enabling communities to ensure that planting is on time. The project will ensure that vulnerable people have direct access to more food through the provision of vegetable and crop seeds and tools, fishing equipment, animal health services as well as cash for work project planned. The project will integrate and augment responses to immediate needs, restoration of livelihoods, and capacity building to pave the path for long-term recovery in order to enhance communities. ACROSS proposes actions that will connect with the humanitarian response and resilience programs that will target 10,000 households (60,000 beneficiaries) in Jonglei and the Eastern Equatoria States, comprised of 29,400 males (14,406 above and 14994 below 18 years) 30,600 females (14994 above 18 years and 15606 below 18years).

Needs

As per IPC outlook for September 2018, the food security situation in the Country is expected to worsen dramatically and there is an urgent need for emergency livelihood interventions providing agricultural inputs (crops and vegetables) and fishing gear to avoid hunger, malnutrition, and destitution over the next months. ACROSS has extensive, on¬the¬ground experience in Jonglei, Pibor County and Eastern Equatoria, Kapoeta county where it is currently active in education, peacebuilding and livelihoods interventions. ACROSS has conducted rapid needs assessments in several cross¬sectoral issues including food security and livelihoods. The total number of target beneficiaries is reached based on consensus that this project will focus severely affected payams based on the depth and severity of problem. In this project, the State Ministry of Agricultural played role in indicating the more affected sites which again endorsed by the state officials. Based on the current September IPC analysis, Jonglei and Kapoeta are variously on IPC 4 category, where 1.7 million falls under. Deteriorating food insecurity conditions have been worsened by conflict and forcible displacements. And the situation has been exacerbated by soaring inflation, deteriorating incomes, and high food and commodity prices; perennial livestock looting has equally eroded key livelihood assets. Other factors include a shortage of seeds and tools; erratic rainfall patterns and reduced market functionality. In all ACROSS operational areas, limited education and employment opportunities, increasing failure of pastoral livelihoods, and weakening traditional governance structures all contribute to environments where positive influences and outlets for young people are diminishing. It is, therefore, ACROSS planned to engage the young generation into various livelihood activities whereby the livelihood activities are designed in a way entertaining dialogue and peace-building activities. These actions actively promote a multi sector integrated response with food, livelihood, nutrition, protection, WASH, & health actors; and analysis based on supporting communities to cope with hazards and shocks. Resilience building will be highly encouraged through restoring market functionality, protecting and building community assets, increasing community engagement. ACROSS being a faith based organization will closely work with beneficiaries, including religious leaders to ensure ownership and mitigate potential risks via collaboration with counterpart agencies on the ground. Peacebuilding messages will also be disseminated in all our relief and resilience actions as we aim to change to more development programs and foster peaceful coexistence among communities even beyond the project lifespan. ACROSS is currently on the ground both in Jonglei State and Eastern Equatoria with competent and experienced staff, facilities and logistics that will be rapidly deployed to respond to the needs The organization currently has ongoing emergency food security and livelihoods project funded by Dutch Relief Alliance (DRA) under South Sudan Joint Response 5 (SSRJ5) in Pibor County and PMU a Swedish Faith base Agency is Support ACROSS FSL in Eastern Equatoria and leverage experience and lessons learned from PADD, a Germany Protestant Agency FSL project in Lakes State. ACROSS has built very good rapport and excellent community goodwill with local state ministries linkages, state religious leaders, strong local networks and coordination with the FSL Cluster, FAO, and other agencies on the ground which will complement effective and rapid implementation.
