Project Summary

People of District Badin are badly affected in recent flood. Majority of the population depends upon agriculture, labor, fishing and cattle rearing. But stagnant crop area has been totally eroded by rain water and livestock have been perished in the target districts. The affected people are bound to live either in the open sky or in the camps. Most of the affected population is living on roadsides and on the embankments of canals. There is less camp management for the displaced population where less number of peoples has been provided shelter. The affected and displaced people have become devoid of almost all their belongings and sources of their livelihood, hence have no more capacity to make expenses on WASH facilities at their areas. Therefore, there is a dire need to provide Water facility, Hygiene Kits until they are able to rehabilitate themselves. In this way, due to usage of contaminated water, we believe that the further loss of lives can be avoided and the suffering people can be saved from water borne-disease. So based of need the proposed interventions are planned for increasing access to safe drinking water so as to minimize the water borne infections occurring due to intake of unsafe drinking water. These infections lead to diarrhea and digestive tract infections resulting in malnutrition among children less than 5 years of age. Secondly to sensitize the communities, awareness sessions related to safe drinking water and methods of water storage and treatment at household level will be conducted in the form of small groups along with household visits as follow ups. Water testing of the drinking water sources will be done to educate the community members about the quality of drinking water.
