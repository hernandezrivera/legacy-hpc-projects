Project Summary

his project aims to support the overall humanitarian response by enabling and enhancing access to beneficiaries and project implementation sites through a provision of air service.
Its main objectives are:
• To provide NGOs, UN agencies, donor organizations and diplomatic missions in Cameroon with safe, effective and efficient air service;
• To transport light cargo, such as medical supplies, information and communication technology (ICT) equipment;
• To provide adequate capacity for medical and security evacuations of humanitarian staff.
UNHAS was established in Cameroon in November 2015 to provide air transport services to the entire humanitarian community and their implementing partners principally NGOs, UN agencies, red cross/crescent movement, donor organizations and diplomatic missions. Ever since, UNHAS has offered safe humanitarian flights on a regular weekly basis to Adamaoua (N’gaoundere), the North (Garoua) and the Far North (Maroua), as well as to Tchad (N’Djamena). As the conflict worsened in the Northwest and Southwest regions, and following a request from the Steering Committee, UNHAS extended its operations in September 2019 to serve the locations of Bamenda, Mamfe and Tiko.
As the continued insecurity, vast distances and poor road conditions remain the main constraints to the provision of assistance by aid organizations, but also the prevailing Covid-19 pandemic worldwide and in Cameroon in particular, the continuation of UNHAS in 2021 will be vital in enabling humanitarian organizations to access the populations in need and project implementation sites

1 - Besoins / Needs

1. UNHAS offers safe, reliable, and efficient service to 72 registered user organizations on a regular biweekly basis to the Northwest (Bamenda) and the Far North (Maroua), as well as to Chad (N’Djamena), enabling indirect access to Kousseri. 2. As the conflict worsened in the Northwest and Southwest regions, and following a request from the Steering Committee, UNHAS extended its operations in September 2019 to serve the locations of Bamenda, Mamfe and Tiko. Mamfe and Tiko were suspended due to security concerns raised by the CCAA. Discussions on restarting Tiko are ongoing, based on the high demand for access depicted in the need assessment exercise conducted in 2021. 3. A commercial airline operates in country; however, humanitarian actors have reported poor reliability and regular cancellations as factors affecting their ability to reach programme implementation locations on time. Therefore, UNHAS services will remain essential to ensure the only reliable link in-country between Cameroon’s capital and locations in the Far North, North, Adamawa, Northwest and Southwest regions. 4. UNHAS operates a 12 seater Cessna Caravan to Bamenda, and a 50-seater regional jet aircraft (EMB-145) which serves operations in both Cameroon and Chad, thereby representing a cost-effective approach and offering a regional connection. The jet also enables charter flights across the region. In 2021, 90% of UNHAS Charter flights were regional charters. UNHAS serves four destinations on a regular basis (Bamenda, Maroua, N’djamena and Yaounde) and two destinations on demand basis (Garoua and N’gaoundere). The destinations of Tiko and Mamfe were suspended in January 2020 after the Cameroon Civil Aviation Authority raised safety and security concerns in both locations due to their inability to provide for personnel and equipment. WFP initiated a series of discussion with the Government to elaborate an MOU that will provide for an enhanced collaborative framework for UNHAS flights in those locations but also for all the other locations served by UNHAS in Cameroon. The MOU process has been delayed and put on hold following the outbreak of the COVID-19 pandemic.
