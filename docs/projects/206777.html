Project Summary

The project is aimed at enhancing capacity of engagement with the CAAC agenda in Cabo Delgado, Mozambique. The main objectives are to: (i) raise awareness on the CAAC agenda, pursuant Security Council Resolution 1612, and increase support to monitoring and reporting on the six grave violations against children affected by armed conflict; (ii) support capacity-building of justice actors, law enforcement agents and security forces on children’s rights framework and on the six grave violations against children, with the aim of strengthening the justice system response to such issues in Cabo Delgado; (iii) support advocacy for the adoption of a handover protocol by the Government of Mozambique and other preventive and protective measures as appropriate.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

- Strengthen UN regular monitoring and reporting, including quarterly Global Horizontal Notes and annual reporting, on grave violations against children, pursuant to Security Council Resolution 1612. - Support awareness raising awareness and improved responses on protection of children affected by armed conflict and prevention of grave violations against children, through capacity building and technical assistance to administration of justice actors and security forces in Cabo Delgado. - Enhance capacity for human rights analysis to strengthen early warning and prevention of grave violations in areas of concern as well as the identification and implementation of measures to mitigate risks of violations against children in Cabo Delgado. - Support advocacy for the adoption and implementation of a handover protocol and other preventive and protective measures as appropriate, recognizing children associated with armed groups or armed forces in Cabo Delgado primarily as victims. - Support and engage with UN coordination structures at provincial and national level related to children and armed conflict and child protection, as appropriate.
