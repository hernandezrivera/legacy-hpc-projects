Project Summary

Two years ago, the world witnessed one of the largest forced displacements of recent times. In a few weeks, close to a million Rohingya women, girls, boys and men were forced from their homes in Rakhine State of Myanmar to seek safety in Bangladesh. They have been bound to live inadequate space without any humanitarian facilities in makeshift camp at Ukhiya and Teknaf in Cox’s Bazar. The inhabitants of makeshift camp are living with enormous health suffering because limited access to hygienic sanitation and safe water. To solve the water problem, RPN had been taken to initiatives the project titled “WASH for Rohingya & Host Community “with a view to aiming establishing pure water supply network. Under the last year WASH project RPN had been established 3 deep tube well and purchased 3 food grade water tankers and established 30 distribution points in different place of the Rohinigya camp. Through using this tools a distribution network had been established. Every day the inhabitants of the adjacent distribution's point came for collecting water. It was very effective tools to minimize the water scarcity of Rohingya community. Considering the last year experience RPN, has plan to expand the project activities. Under the project, will expand the water supply network through using of existing water sources and water vehicles. In addition 20 distribution point will increase which will added with the total of 30 prior distribution points. In the same way every day the water tankers will collect fresh water from existing and newly install deep tube well and deliver it in specific distribution point. The Rohingya community will collect water from the points. During water collection they will be motivated to improved hygienic practice through leaflet. Our WASH volunteer will disseminate the information among the community.
RPN has willing to extend the WASH facilities among the FDMNs as well as host community. In addition further construction and operation and maintenance of existing latrine and bathing space according to sector priority. Out of camp's activities 200 hand pump will be installed for the of host community in order to minimize the scarcity of pure water and reduce the water borne diseases from the community. Installation place will select consult with the local community and Department of Public Health Engineering.

Objectives

To ensure effective Implementation of WASH services for the Forcefully Displaced Myanmar Nationals (FDMNs) at camp 7, 9, 10 & Nayapara RC and Cawkhali as well as host community at Palongkhali union under Ukhiya upazila of Cox’ Bazar District. The specific objectives which are suit in mission & vision of the project are described in below; 1. To mitigates the scarcity of fresh water for the Rohingya community as well as host community of proposed areas. 2.Operation and maintenance the existing and new tubes well around Rohingya community To provide fresh and safe water to the both community in order to reduce water borne diseases. 3. To increase access of fresh water to the targeted people. 4. To return back the targeted people from traditional unprotected water sources which affect their health, economy and environment. 5. To create awareness about hygienic practice.

Needs

Refugees continue to face challenges in collecting safe water.Water quality surveillance continues to record that 52% of hand pumps are showing signs of contamination from e coli.Community engagement to development of sustainable operation Maintenance frame work be a priority. Access to sanitation - Despite 99% of households reporting using pit latrines in the camps , functionality of the latrines remains low at 78%. 32% of refugee households are facing problems accessing or using latrines due to low coverage (too many people using latrines), while 14% face a problem of distance between the latrines and their shelters. There is still a significant need for further construction of sanitation and hygiene infrastructure to bring coverage up to minimum Sector standards. So operation of maintenance of existing latrines cubicles is required to make if functional to ensure safety privacy & address specific gender disability age and other vulnerabilities. Overall 37% HHs use communal /public facilities for bathing (of which 68% are functional) within the numbers of refugees specially (especially female) using makeshift bathing spaces in side shelters.

Activities or outputs

The project target the Rohingya refugee and host community intent to help them to gain access to clean and improved water sources. To achieve the target the following activities has been set; 1. Operation and maintenance the existing and new tubes well around Rohingya community. 2. To establish water tank at different location in the camp according to populations demand.3.Operation and maintenance of existing and new Latrine & Bathing Spaces.4.Ensuring Hygiene promotion maintain facility safe & healthy menstrual hygiene management practices.accessing hygiene supplies through by volunteer voucher systems.
