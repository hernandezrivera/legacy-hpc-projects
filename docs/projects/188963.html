Project Summary

The project aims at increasing basic accessible, equitable, protective, quality education to young children in Awerial, Rumbek East and Chueibet Counties. This program will build on what Charity and Empowerment Foundation and Save The Children international have been doing in most schools in these areas and build the capacity of education stakeholders in responding to emergency. CEF will respond to Education gaps brought by influx in displaced and host populations and floods while integrating the intervention with WASH in schools, nutrition knowledge through establishment of gender segregated latrines and peace building in schools.
in this project, CEF will respond to the need which are identify by several assessment reports which were conducted in Awerial, Rumbek East and Chueibet Counties 2021 which include lack of Classrooms, lack of adequate qualify teachers, lack of Scholastic materials.
Education indicators remain worse and thus a huge gap in Awerial, Rumbek East and Chueibet Counties of lake State and this is due to communal conflicts that always affects learning institutions and Children.
CEF will establish 32 TLS and 32 Segregated latrines, distribute scholastic materials, Train 150 teachers on Child-centered pedagogy.

1 - Needs

Lake state is one of the most affected state by communal clashes and flood in South Sudan in 2021.The findings from the 2021 ENA and HNO highlight that most of the education challenges that have been highlighted in previous assessments persist and have been impaired by COVID-19 and the subsequent Closer of Schools. Many of the challenges noted by County education officials, headteachers, learners, and community members revolve around lack of finances for both the supply and demand side of education such as families not being able to afford school fees for their children to attend schools, or teachers not receiving regular and sufficient salaries. Erratic weather patterns have also immensely contributed to drop outs, due to lack of learning structures, and issues surrounding access and safety especially of adolescent girls. This situation is compounded by the increase in the demand for education by IDP children and host communities. Adolescent girls are at risk of early and forced marriage, as well as sexual abuse and exploitation due to the low value placed on the girl child. Education indicators remain amongst the worst in the Country, Far fewer girls make it to the last grade of primary education. For every 100 boys completing primary school, only 63 girls completed the cycle (UIS, 2018). Cultural and social factors play a role in keeping girls from transitioning from primary to secondary education. South Sudan is ranked among the top twenty countries in the world with the highest prevalence rates for child marriage, and, according to the 2010 NBS survey, 45% of women, ages 20-49, were married before they were 18-years-old and 7% of women, ages 15-49, were married before turning 15-years-old. Of women ages 20-24, over half (52%) were married before they were 18-years-old. Of these women, 20% of women with at least a secondary education were married, compared to 59% of those with no education (NBS, 2010). Globally, girls who have completed secondary education are six times less likely to become child brides and have better health and economic outcomes throughout their lives; under-five mortality rates also tend to be higher in child marriages (UNFPA, 2018). The challenge of unqualified teachers within the Counties has continually been detrimental factor to realizing education goals as many are not conversant with appropriate child -centered pedagogy. The Approaches/methodology.This project will see an outcome of children and adolescents aged 3-18, particularly girls and other vulnerable groups, having increased and more equitable access to quality basic education learning in Lake State by the end of 2022.The above outcome will be achieved through input of-school children, IDP and host community children, adolescents/youth, aged 3-18 years have equitable access to safe and protective learning opportunities, Improved quality of education through enhanced literacy and numeracy skills and life-skills and building resilience of conflict affected children, adolescents/youth, aged 3-18. Funding will enable maintenance of learning spaces through establish of TLS to enable continuity of Education programming, enabling new enrollments and retention of learners. lack of intervention will erode previous gains achieved,CEF will support ECD and primary schools in Lake State.The project will further ensure the provision of Education in emergency learning materials, capacity building of Primary teachers and PTA on Pedagogy, Psycho-social support, life skills and lifesaving message, school management, School monitoring and record keeping and training of ECD caregivers on the use of improve ECD kits and establish safe, gender segregated and gender sensitive latrines and hand washing facilities. Conduct education awareness raising activities (BTL) and girl education day,through puppetry shows and campaigns, for boys and girls from the targeted communities on lifesaving messages including WASH, child protection, girls child education.
