Project Summary

Pakistan is prone to multiple natural hazards including floods, droughts, earthquakes, landslides, snowfall, avalanche and cyclones. The Province of Sindh experiences flood and drought, often together. The recent flooding of August 2020 in Sindh resulted in declaration of emergency in 20 of the 29 districts. Dadu in Sindh is the worst hit area in recent monsoon floods and is also COVID-19 affected. In respond to this situation Save the Children (SC) Pakistan proposed integrated WASH response for flood and COVID-19 affected communities of district Dadu. SC Pakistan is already implementing Hygiene Behaviour Change Coalition (HBCC) Project in district Dadu and maintains a good coordination with health, education, PHED, and other relevant government departments and stakeholders. SC Pakistan has also signed a five year strategic MOU with the NDMA. Under this MOU, NDMA considers SC as a core partner to response to all emergencies. The proposed project is in line with the WASH cluster strategic objectives and geographical location. The integrated objective of the project is provision of essential WASH services including for the population groups most exposed and vulnerable to the flood and pandemic. In order to contribute to our impact communities need to adopt improved hygiene practices to reflect recommended COVD-19 prevention and control measures. This will be achieved by improving access to and provision of safe drinking water, appropriate sanitation, and promotion on safe hygiene practices access, with a strong focus on social and behavior change communication through the project activities. The communities will be capacitate to adopt improved hygiene practices to reflect recommended COVD-19 prevention and control measures.
The project aims to ensure most vulnerable flood and COVID 19 affected population, schools and HCFs have safe, sufficient, equitable, and dignified access to safe water, sanitation, and hygiene services that meets national and global minimum standards and to contain the spread of diseases through provision of these essential WASH services. Specific activities will be new and/or rehabilitation of damaged water supplies mainly hand pumps, construction and/or maintenance of sanitation facilities, hygiene promotion and distribution of essential WASH Kits. Culturally women and girls are responsible for household activities including water collection, hygiene and sanitation, and children and elderly care. These crisis are putting them at further vulnerability as they bear most of the burden of carrying out these household tasks. The project will prioritize the most vulnerable groups of the affected population, pregnant women, lactating mothers, women headed households, elders, people with disabilities and people living with COVD-19 positive. The selection criteria will be set in coordination with the community traditional leaders. In the selection and prioritization process women will be heavily involved and will have a decision making role.
SC will implement the project activities through IP who is already present in Dadu and will work with male/female Community Resource Persons (CRPs) by providing basic training on hygiene promotion, how to utilize point of use water treatment, safe storage of water and COVID-19. Female CRPs will help address the particular hygiene needs of flood affected women and girls, as females in disaster affected communities often feel more comfortable approaching female CRP to discuss sensitive health and hygiene issues. By selecting CRPs from within the community, there will be an enhanced sense of community ownership for the programme’s interventions and increased self-reliance, further restoring dignity. In implementation all groups of the population will be involved. Women will constitute at least fifty percent of the positions of CRPs, water management committees (WMCs), Water Sanitation and Hygiene (WASH) Non Food Items (NFIs) distribution committee and in other activities.

1- Project Activities

Installation of 20 new gender and age inclusive communal water points (hand pumps)• Rehabilitation of community water sources (50 hand pumps and 10 wells)• Provision of 200 food graded water storage tanks with tap• Distribution of 7000 low cost, innovative and user friendly emergency water treatment kits (with alum, chlorine drops and residual chlorine check drops) for the removal of turbidity and treating microbial contaminated drinking water• Distribution of 7000 secure contextually appropriate Hygiene Kits for HHs and Schools (including waste disposal of MHM material) in line with government of Pakistan’s standards. School WASH kits will include essential items needed for implementing a massive cleaning and disinfection of schools reopening after COVID-19 interruption• Installation of 200 gender and age inclusive HH hand washing basins• 150 Hygiene orientation sessions with male and female effected beneficiaries• Repairing of 100 existing latrines• Installation of 100 new gender and age inclusive emergency latrines • Clean and disinfect school and HCFs facilities with emphasis on surfaces that are touched by many people. • Repair and maintenance of WASH services in 5 Health Care Facilities and installation of new hand washing stations along with support in management of Infectious/hazardous waste to support Infection Prevention and Control.• Reconstruction and rehabilitation of WASH facilities (hand washing stations and sanitation facilities) in 25 schools and distribution of hygiene kits among children and their families. • Support Public Health Engineering Department (PHED) in rehabilitation of 5 Drinking Water Supply Schemes in compliance with Water Quality Standards.Hygiene promotion and awareness raising activities: • In support and coordination with MoCC, engaging youth and community activists as Clean Green Champions in district Dadu to promote hygiene practices, particularly around COVID-19 prevention among peers, children and their families. • Awareness raising material will be designed in emergency humanitarian context in alignment with ministry of climate change behavior change campaign and promotion of the clean green initiative. The advocacy and communication campaign will be designed and contextualized in the WASH humanitarian context aligned with COVID-19 response of the MoCC. • Mass media awareness in the context of WASH and COVID-19 with focused hygiene and behavior change campaign for children and their families.
