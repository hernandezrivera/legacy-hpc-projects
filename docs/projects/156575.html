Project Summary

Considering the difficulties and challenges of Syrian-Turkish border crossing; The Project aims to provide accessible training center for Syrian humanitarian filed staff workers and civil society members to conduct trainings and workshops at zero point “ Cut-off point between Syrian-Turkish border” also will give opportunity for international expat trainers and trainees to meet in secure and accessible place in order to increase their capacities, sharing knowledge and gain skills, and all humanitarians in Syria will benefit from the training and the services providing, the main goal for this project to conduct a professional training to humanitarians they can't travel to Turkey and establish a safe center in a safe zone in Syria to provide a safe environment to the international trainers from they come from Turkey.

Needs

Seven years and no foreseeable end to the conflict, the plight of the Syrian people continues. If there is any hope to stabilize the country, restore basic services and engage in peacebuilding and reconciliation, it largely relies on Syrian civil society organizations. One hundred thirty-six active Syrian organizations and sixty-four international organizations have been registered in OCHA in 2018, across opposition-controlled areas with estimated total population 3 million, in order to provide people with basic services within the absence of the governmental bodies . Today, that glimpse of hope is threatened by the reluctance to support civil organizations at a time when their capacities are reduced as a result of the migration of the expertise, and the growing role of armed groups, which required new techniques and skills for the field workers to avoid any break of the humanitarian principles, and donor regulations. Currently, the training offered to the field workers either through trained trainers in Turkey and sent back to Syria for training, this is facing great difficulties related to governmental approvals, as well as loss related to information or being carried out through online applications. This is a less effective way of being with the trainer in the same place. Or through securing security approvals to cross the field staff from Syria to Turkey to train them at high costs and long procedures.Which led to the need for a border training center to provide training where foreign experts who cannot cross into Syria are able to meet field workers to exchange information, expertise and capacity-building exercises, also can be hub for coordination meetings

Implementation of activities

Project activities : 1- finalize with local authorities and Turkish government to obtain the needed approvals to establish the center in Bab Al Salama zero point and handover from the landlord 2- Establishment of the training centre and area with 3 training halls, restaurant area , WC, administrative rooms, warehouse and services area and the accommodation centre that host 48 person separated for male and female in addition to all other needed infrastructure and power supply3- Establishing first fully equipped humanitarian innovation lap in Syria response linked to it’s board of directors who is come from different backgrounds “technical, legal, and managerial fields” who’s will put the centre strategy and operational plan and the relation with similar international bodies who can provide support through partnership 4- Recruit the centre's operation staff 5- Conduct a meetings and surveys with all partners, SNGOs, INGOs, UN agencies, stakeholders, and donors to put the centre annual service plan according to the priority and need6- Agreed operational manual with partner and exit strategy and final ownership for the centre 7- Setup training contracts 8- Launch the centre to be working according to plan
