Project Summary

The main objective of the project is to improve protection, safety and security and enhance access to justice and peaceful coexistence between refugees and host communities (HC) in Cox's Bazar. The project will give particular attention to vulnerable groups such as women, elderly and people with specific needs. The project will provide a forum through which refugees and host communities will have improved access to protection services and conflict resolution mechanisms, as well as access to legal assistance and victim support services. The expected results will reinforce the capacities of actors that are currently working on community safety and security, justice and protection in both refugee and host communities, focusing on addressing crime prevention, access to relevant safety and legal services, promoting women and youth empowerment, and responding to any violation of rights. The interventions proposed are based on the findings of a community safety pilot project carried out in camps and HC in 2020 and 2021, which emphasized the need for increased police presence in the camps and capacity building of actors on refugee law, GBV, counter trafficking, protection principles and community outreach and conflict resolution. The project will focus on coordinating with law enforcement agencies and the judiciary to ensure refugees and host communities have access to justice and they are aware about reporting mechanism. The project will contribute towards improving governance of the relevant institutions. In implementing the project, the agencies will ensure that persons with different age-gender and other background can benefit equitably from the activities. The project will be jointly implemented by UNDP, UNHCR and IOM in close collaboration with the RRRC and Camp in Charges, APBn, District Police, local government institutions and Judiciary in Cox’s Bazar. The propsed proposal will have synergy with many projects implemented by the UN agency, INGOs and NGOs to contribute the peaceful-coexistence between and among the host and camp communities.

1. Needs
1 - Identified Needs

"The camp's safety and security situation has been deteriorating for a long time, and peaceful coexistence between the refugees and the host community is challenged due to limited resources and competition for livelihood opportunities. There is a need to enhance access to Justice for refugee and host communities and create a platform for the refugee communities to resolve minor conflicts at the camp level with support from the legal assistance partners. Peaceful coexistence between and among the host and refugee communities is critical to ensure social cohesion. The security and justice actors responsible for providing security and safety and access to Justice for refugees and the host communities surrounding the camp areas need to be supported on refugee law, access to Justice, GBV, counter-trafficking, protection principles, community outreach, and conflict resolution. Community safety and security are vital for social cohesion and peaceful coexistence in Ukhia and Teknaf Upazila of Cox's Bazar. According to the UNDP's Cox's Bazar Analysis and Research Unit ( CARU) report, in last 10 months, a total of 25 camp communities were killed by the arms group, including the Majhi and Volunteers. The conflict within the camps increased to control over the camps, and the gang groups have been working inside to enlarge their numbers targeting the young men and women. There is also fighting among the drug traders and traffickers inside, affecting the camp community's safety. Due to the lack of capacity of the APBn forces, they are not in a position to support the victims of GBV and protect the women who are victims of family disputes. This myriad of problems, not only in the camps but also in the host communities need to be addressed through the proposed intervention. The project will activate and implement the existing mechanism of Community Policing, a support system for the host communities. And It will also activate the Community Safety Forum (CSF), a support system for the camp communities to identify safety issues and prevent crimes. The both mechanisms will be strengthened through the engagement and building capacity of the District Police, Armed Police Battalion and the respective forum members. They will play a critical role in mitigating social tension. The UNDP, UNHCR, and IOM will jointly work on these initiatives. UNHCR, IOM, and the UNDP have implemented a joint pilot programme on ""Community Safety"" The pilot project has conducted a series of FGDs, KIIs, and dialogue to identify the potential group of people, including the vulnerable communities who are at risk in the societies. Apart from the pilot programme results, the camp communities experienced huge crises, such as massive family disputes, theft, kidnapping, polygamy, rape, torture, and extortion. Drug and human trafficking are common in camp communities. In this context, the project has found the need to design and implement the community safety project in collaboration with the UNHCR and IOM to ensure the camp and host communities are safe and lead a peaceful coexistence. The project will develop a coordination plan to keep close inter-sectoral linkage and develop a plan to actively participate and coordinate with the respective sectors to comply with the "

2. Rationalization
2 - How is your project aligned to the Rationalization Principles (in particular reference # 3 - Adopt an integrated approach, # 4 - Ensure predictability and continuity)?

The project will be implemented with close involvement of refugees, host-community members, local government authorities, police, civil administration and judiciary. The partner agencies will implement the project for three years ending in 2025 with funding from PRM. The safety issues are critical as the conflict between and among the host and Rohingya communities increases significantly, especially in the camps. More than 17 killings in the last two months and other incidents created panic about safety and security. And also, the escalation of tension and fear of insecurity increased among the communities. The project will map to identify the potential communities who would participate at the higher level of the programme, and other communities will participate directly through the project activities. The various capacity-building initiatives will enhance all levels of communities and other relevant stakeholders' capacity to attain the project objectives. The project will ensure the quality and higher level of participation of the stakeholders and the communities from its inception across the entire implementation period.

3. Cross-cutting Themes
3.1 - Protection

"The 3 agencies will ensure mainstreaming of protection throughout the implementation of the activities. ""Do no harm"" is the guiding principle for implementing the activities for the vulnerable people. The privacy and confidentiality of the beneficiaries will be considered while designing and implementing the project. Beneficiaries will also receive awareness-raising sessions to protect them from any unwanted incidents. The agencies will deliver services in ways that preserve the physical integrity of individuals and communities, that are culturally appropriate, and that avoid any unintended negative consequences. The project will provide assistance and services according to needs and not based on age, sex, gender, nationality, race, or ethnic allegiance. The project will provide training on PSEA to the project staff members and partners to ensure PSEA aspects. "

3.2 - Gender

"The activities will be targeted for all gender in the targeted camps and host-community areas irrespective to their gender. The needs assessments will identify the specific needs of different gender groups and provide tailored support to them. The project will institutionalize and integrate gender into the entire spectrum of activities -projects, programmes, and staff policies. For gender issues to be fully integrated into the UN values, all staff need to understand and implement the strategy in their work. It also includes initiatives for men when needed, provided they promote overall gender equality. The project will target both men and women. Since women are left behind in many societies, it will especially focus on increasing women's empowerment and leadership throughout the project. The capacity building programme for the youth under 18 will include and benefit them. "

3.3 - Age including child safeguarding

The project aims to include youth and adult community members. under the direct beneficiary of the project activities. However, the activities will indirectly benefit all the age groups of the benefited household. The interventions will be designed considering the safeguard of the children.

3.4 - Diversity

All the activities planned will consider people from different age, gender and other diversity group. The activities will try to benefit all the beneficiaries in need. The activities will be tailored to their specific needs based on the outcome of the needs assessment.

3.5 - Accountability to Affected Populations (AAP)

Accountability remains a vital and integral component of this proposed project to comply with the AAP. During the needs assessment, the affected populations in all the targeted locations will be consulted in designing, planning, and implementing activities in camps and host communities. The affected people will take the lead in deciding their roles and local resources mobilization considering safety, security, and gender dynamics. Affected women and girls will be consulted, in addition to and separately from men and boys. The project will practice the following tools: a. PSEA- Accelerating the PSEA plan at the community level. b. Conduct Need Assessment- c. Ensure Disability Inclusion: d. Ensure Gender Age Marker- e. Feed Back Mechanism- f. Effective coordination

3.6 - Does your project have any DRR/DRM elements and/or environmental issues and/or climate change mitigation measures? If yes, please explain.

The project will analyse the community disaster risks and vulnerabilities and will address the associated protection and safety concerns in coordination with key stakeholders. The project has scope of contingency funding (5%), if any disasters happened, the project would respond to ensure the protection and safety of the affected communities. The project will also address the gender-based violence that raising from disasters and climate impacts. The project ensues that the community protection and safety activities are risk informed and contribute long-term development and reduce the humanitarian burdens. The project will also build capacity of local government, police, security stakeholders and communities on the protection and safety concerns raised by and during disasters.
