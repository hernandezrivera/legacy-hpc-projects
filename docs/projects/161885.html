Project Summary

Child protection intervention will continue to improve capacities of the community based mechanisms, and processes, alternative care in emergencies, strengthening government capacity, prevention of, and response to GBV, and provision of PSS & specialized services eg (MHPSS) to children in emergencies.
Mainstream CP across all sectors and all phases of project implementation in save the children to maximize the benefits of save the children intervention and reduce possible risks against boys and girls.
After the implementation of the above mentioned intervention, about 80,000 people will be reached children (27,000 girls; 35,000 boys) and (900 men; 9000 women) will be reached by 2021.
As part of the implementation, SC common approaches such as parenting without violence (PWV) and Steps to Protect children in conflict.

Plan fields

1 - Needs

Improve impartial access to services for children to their basic rights and strengthen the overall protection environment. These includes child-headed-households, unaccompanied and separated children, and adolescent boys and girls are particularly at risk of SGBV, child recruitment, and human trafficking.Thousands of children are displaced and in need of protection intervention such as psychosocial support through establishment of child friendly spaces, alternative care arrangement/placement or provision of foster parents, establishment/strengthened community based protection structures, as well as prevention of and response to GBV and further provision of more advance mental health and psychosocial support (MHPSS).

2 - Activities

- Establishment of child friendly space with age appropriate and culturally sensitive orientated activities for boys and girls- Training for project staffs, animators, facilitators and volunteers on HEART(Healing and Education through the Arts) (SCI approach). - Conduct HEART (Healing and Education through the Arts) activities for boys and girls at the Child friendly spaces. - Establishment of community based protection mechanism (child protection committees)- Formation of child rights clubs (CRCs)- Capacity building for government and other community based structures including community stakeholders- Identification and provision of case management service to UASC and other children at risk including child headed household, unaccompanied and separated children, survivors of SGBV, children associated with arm force and arm groups (CAAFAG) etc.- Identification and training of standby foster parents- Sensitization and awareness rising session to populates on child protection key messages includes gender base violence. - Protection mainstreaming in other components such as WASH, Nutrition among others.- Train adolescent boys and girls, CFS animators and facilitators on youth resilience program.- Conduct Youth resilience program sessions with boys, girls and caregivers at the child friendly spaces.

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

Save the Children has a complaints response mechanism system in all our project locations, Save the Children will engage with community members, boys and girls on preferred local systems of providing feedbacks. Community engagement in monitoring and evaluation will be encouraged through participatory methods such as KIIs and FGDs that are organised for various groups of people segregated by gender and age. Group discussions with alternative caregivers and children in alternative care will be routinely organised as a means of ensuring children's voices are heard. The participation of children will be embedded in the development and operation of the Save the Children accountability systems and reporting mechanism.
