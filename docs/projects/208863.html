Project Summary

The project seeks to deliver quality life saving and life sustaining, inclusive and gender responsive interventions to 1110,000 IDPs Returnees and populations whom affected by ongoing Conflict , across Khartoum and Darfur Region East South and West Darfur States. This will be achieved through the following thematic areas;
• Provide crisis-affected and vulnerable people access to basic water services.
• Provide and enable crisis-affected and vulnerable people access to basic/limited sanitation services
• Provide and enable crisis-affected and vulnerable people access to hygiene services
• Provide Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM) affected population access to minimum WASH for Nutrition package
• Provide outbreaks affected and ‘at-risk’ population access to minimum WASH for Health package
The response targets five localities in East Darfur , Central Darfur ,Khartoum, South Darfur and West Darfur ),
For the INF Emergency Response
The conflict between SAF and RSF has been one of the most violent conflicts in Sudan’s modern history. The conflict had led to one of the highest global displacements with over 6.1 million people forced to leave their homes, out of which 4.85 million have suffered internal displacement. In areas such as Darfur, especially West Darfur South Darfur Central Darfur and East Darfur is the biggest State hosted IDPs in Darfur Region which has witnessed some of the most violent clashes, especially intertribal clashes, ARCO’s assessment has indicated a high need for NFI and Shelter as many households had to flee for their lives without the ability to carry of their essential items. According to ARCOs assessment (Rapid Protection Assessment in East Darfur State , south and West Darfur Al Genina had indicated that the majority of households being displaced internally are female led households, and without access to adequate Shelter items they facing increased risks of SGBV. Furthermore; many of the households report looting reroute to the three assessed localities, making those households unable to purchase the most basic of supplies.
The current crisis has mandated a more flexible and tailored responses towards the growing needs. ARCO in 2023 had piloted several response modalities and through this has explored what would be suitable for the different beneficiary groups. In 2024 ARCO aims at continuing with emergency responses to serve a total of 22,000HH (110,000 individuals) in West Darfur, Khartoum, East Darfur ,Central and South Darfur States. Assistance packages will include NFI and Emergency Shelter Kits. Such as Jyrcans ,Blankets ,Plastic sheets ,Kitchen sets ,pockets , water pots plastic and plastic mats

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

Protection will be mainstreamed into planned activities. The project will ensure that each activity will not expose vulnerable groups including women and children to protection risks. Community consultation will be conducted throughout all the project cycle. Community feedback and complaint mechanism will be set up. The feedback mechanism will ensure anonymity and confidentiality to protect the reporting person. In cases of GBV or child abuse cases, support including referral to other service providers will be provided and the survivor will be linked to protection agencies (Government entities or other organizations dealing with protection issues) to prevent exposure from further harm. To possible extent, community discussions will be held separately between women and men to create safe space for everybody to participate equally. Discussions for children will always be held separately from the ones for adults. The project will ensure inclusion of most vulnerable groups (like minority groups, PLWD, among others) within the target communities in close coordination and collaboration with key stakeholders. Selection of villages will be done in consultation with line ministries, HAC and other relevant agencies such as UNFPA, department of welfare in the Ministry of social welfare to ensure needs of people with special needs are taken care of. The safety of beneficiaries, other project stakeholders and ARCO staffs is not negotiable and project will ensure implementation activities will not put beneficiaries, ARCO staf or project stakeholders on security risks. Activities for children will be done during the day with the present of caregivers or other responsible adults from the communities. While the project will prioritize female and child-headed families to participate in project activities or benefit from the assistance, it will also ensure such inclusion does not create another type of exclusion. Discussion will be held to inform communities on beneficiaries’ selection criteria. The discussion will involve different groups within the communities including women and children. To adhere to Humanitarian Accountability Standard, feedback mechanism will be set up using suggestion boxes or other means preferred by the communities. Program staff will use current safeguarding and accountability standards and policies. Orientation to new staff will include Code of Conduct and safeguarding principle to prevent exploitation of the beneficiaries. Sanitation facilities in schools will ensure gender segregation, be located in well lit safely accessible spaces especially for girls and be designed and constructed to offer safe and private dignified access to boys and girls. Care givers will be educated to ensure that children only carry age appropriate jerrycans when they have to help in fetching water.Protection is not only mainstreamed into all activities but it's also takes a focus in the project.
