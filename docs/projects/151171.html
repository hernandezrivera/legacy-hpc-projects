Needs

Malnutrition (undernutrition) remains the major problem persisted over time in Somalia with high levels of Global Acute Malnutrition (GAM) well above emergency threshold of 15%. During 2016 and 2017, the humanitarian situation in Somalia continued to deteriorate because of the poor rains in Gu and Deyr seasons of 2016 and 2017, causing widespread food insecurity, deepening health, nutritional and water related issues and exacerbated by large-scale movement of people. In response, nutrition cluster alongside other humanitarian clusters developed a pre-famine response plan which addressed operational systems, staffing and supplies management in a holistic manner;As a result, nearly 954,007 children are expected to be acutely malnourished, including 173,594 who are expected to be severely malnourished during the next one year period (Sep 2018-Sep 2019). The surveys indicate Critical levels of GAM (15-29.9%) in 12 out of 33 populations surveyed (7 IDP and 5 Rural) while Critical levels of SAM prevalence (≥4-5.6) were only seen among IDPs in Mogadishu and Guban pastoral. Similarly, the Crude Death Rate (CDR) rates are Critical (1 to <2) in 3/33 population groups surveyed while CDR rates are Serious (0.5 to <1) in 11/32 and U5DR rates (1 to 1.9) in 6/32 population groups surveyed, respectively. Furthermore, Morbidity rates are high (>20 %) in many parts of the country but highest among population groups surveyed in northeast and central parts of the country.In response to the above stated problems and needs SOVA plans to provide integrated nutrition intervention in16 Villages in Bardera district which is identified gap areas and during its project implemention in Bardera district SOVA will attend nutrition cluster regional cluster in Gedo Region and quarter review meeting at nationa l level Nairobi/Mogadishu. Activity 1.4 Number of community discussions & surveys implemented for behavioral change 2 community surveys and discussions 1 at the start up of the project and 1 at the end of the project.Activity 1.5: 16 Counselling of mothers on the appropriate time introduces complementary feeding promotion the continuation of Breast feeding each month for the 16 nutrition sites Activity 2.1: Screen, admit and refer moderate/severe acute malnourished children and pregnant lactating women to either OTP or from TSFP treatment sites 2000 PLWs and 6600 CU5 (3300 boys and 3300 girls at the 16 TSFP/OTP /BSFP sites.Activity 2.2: Train 32 community nutrition workers (16 male and 16 female) on basic nutrition services using a harmonized package probably UNICEF or WFP guides or Somalia IMAM Guides.Activity 2.3: 32 Community nutrition workers given awareness in the communities to pregnant and lactating mothers on the importance of attending ante natal and post natal clinics and importance of adequate nutrition during pregnancy and lactation each nutrition sites 2 CNWs for the 16 sites.Activity 2.4 Number of pregnant and lactating mothers admitted at nutrition facilities and will receive breastfeeding counseling information: 2000.Activity 2.5: Train 48 nutrition staff, for the Somali IMAM guide 1 at the start up of the project and the other after six months of project proceedings..

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

Poor dietary risk,sharing of individual food entitlement,poor community mobilization,insecurity in some location which are hard to reach areas,bias,discrimination,and exclusion among the eligible people to be registered in nutrition sites.All prevention measures will be taken to prevent happening of above mentioned risks,all staff,will be trained in order to prevent risks,rules and regulations SOVA code of conducted and all suitable program policies will be adhered.

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

All beneficiaries will utilize the nutrition program equally including boys,girls,PLWs and men and there is potential risk of excluding among the beneficiaries and each every beneficiary will receive his or her entitlement as per the IMAM guideline and UNICEF nutrition field guide.

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

The main potential risk for the targeted program population violence and extremism mostly carry out by the Somali insurgent,looting of assets such as food and nutrition measurement equipment may happen but to avoid this SOVA organization will strengthen coordination and cooperation with local community before travel of health and nutrition to the program sites and before nutrition supply dispatch security of the area will be assured in order to prevent unbush or attack to the sites to safe lives of vulnerable already affected population.
