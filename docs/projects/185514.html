Project Summary

It is the aim of the project to ensure for all refugees living in camps-24 block E and camp-27 block A & C along with adjacent host communities:
- regular, sufficient, equitable and dignified access to safe water for drinking and domestic needs.
- adequate, appropriate, and acceptable sanitation facilities (including household waste).
- change of potentially dangerous behaviors through innovative hygiene promotion (including hygiene items).

1 - Identified Needs

The Joint Response Plan 2021 estimates that 1.4 million people (8,80,000 RRs and 4,72,000 HCs) affected by the crisis are in urgent need of WASH support. The needs range from access to safe drinking water and water for domestic needs, adequate sanitation facilities including solid waste management and access to hygiene services need to improve. The proposed project focuses on Block A & C in Camp 27 and Block E in Camp 24, where many HHs have high needs in all these four areas simultaneously. Overall, 65% (JRP 2021) RRs and 67% HCs still have access on water services for drink and domestic use purpose according to some basic source like tube-wells, water networks and some surface water, but sometimes there found AWD cases when they used tube-wells and surface water in Teknaf Upazila. This project will be implemented in the said location to ensure sufficient, adequate water for drinking and domestic purpose to establish new water networks, maintain & operational existing system and simple water purified (Aqua tab, WQT test and cleanness) process. The project BGD1031AA of ANANDO/WHH will cover maximum households of Block A&C under Camp-27 and Block E under Camp-24 to improve access to safe drinking water and domestic use.The JRP 2021 found that till many RRs and adjacent HCs including women and girls did not get sanitation facilities near their shelters. Altogether only 78% (JRP 2021) of RRs and 81% HCs have been getting sanitation facilities like Latrines and Bathing cubicles which need to provide regular operation and maintenance for the prospect of functionality. Moreover, elderly and people with disabilities; girls and women are being exposed to gender-based violence, especially at night. As a result, HHs level solar light to be ensured. In addition, due to the poor quality of WASH facilities and lack of quantities, unexpected incidents occur. The project ensured those supports in some part of the locations but due to relocation and adding new block there are still some challenges that continued with existing facilities by overcoming the challenges with and small-scale construction during the proposed project. Hygiene messages, events and demonstrations etc will continue to make behavioral change and practice specially in the newly added block and relocation areas.
