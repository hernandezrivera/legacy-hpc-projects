Project Summary

In 2024 over 14.9 million people in Syria are estimated to be in need of life-saving primary and secondary health care assistance. In 2024, the Health Severity scale of Al-Hasakeh is 5 while in Hole it is 4. In Raqqa, the severity scale is 4, as well as for the Sabka area too, while Maadan is 3. Based on Health sector reports for 2024, the PIN in selected locations are as follows: In Al-Hasakeh (1,060,491), in Ar-Raqqa (615,384).
Intervention, Implementation of activities: This project aims to increase access to lifesaving and life-sustaining coordinated, equitable and quality humanitarian health services across all levels of care in Al-Hasakeh and Ar-Raqqa:
Primary health care: Implementation of primary health care services includes contracting according to GOPA-DERD policies with laboratories to conduct laboratory tests, X-Rays, and echo graph services.
As for medical consultation services, awareness sessions, and investigation of malnutrition cases, they will be provided by the specialized team that will be employed, and finally with regard to medications, the medicines will be purchased according to the available budget based on the GOPA-DERD policy in purchasing.
Secondary health care: The referral process will be started by the mobile medical team specialized in assessing the need and assessing the medical condition, and after obtaining the papers proving the medical condition by the beneficiary, the papers will be checked by the referral officer and make sure that all the criteria match in terms of medical condition and economic condition. Finally, the beneficiary will be contacted and handed over the stamped referral paper.
Assistive devices: After identifying needs and selecting groups with higher protection risks, in order to arranging the suitable procurement method by either a tender, direct purchase or both.
Mental health: Mental health services will be provided by the specialized team within the mobile team.
Protection risks: Attacks on health facilities and personnel and lack access to essential medicines&supplies: providing protective Equipements to health workers and facilities to prevent or respond to attack and enhancing access of essential medicines& supplies especially for chronic diseases
SEA of beneficiaries by external contractors or humanitarian personnel: There are unreported cases of victims: GOPA-DERD is committed to protecting all staff, beneficiaries, partners and other individuals it collaborates with from sexual exploitation and abuse at all times especially vulnerable young adults, women and children who are particularly at risk of sexual exploitation and abuse. All of staff sign for the policy of PSEA. GOPA-DERD takes seriously all the complaints and concerns about SEA with providing immediate and confidential mechanisms for reporting potential or actual SEA through: Staff members of all levels who have reason to believe that a SEA incident may occur within the organization or through another cooperating local or international employee must report either through a specific e-mail used solely for this purpose, or, by calling the SEA hotline to report violations, they will receive the follow-up by PSEA FP.
Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures: Breach of data security, sensitive beneficiary information is lost: Likelihood: Unlikely, Impact: Substantial, Risk: Moderate
Mitigation procedures: Anonymize beneficiary information and record only essential information that does not identify any individual. Use encryption of files containing sensitive data, Carry out data protection audit for GOPA/DERD. Impact after mitigation: low risk.
Financial Risks: Corruption: Likelihood: Unlikely, Impact: Moderate, Risk: Moderate
Mitigation procedures: Train staff on anti-corruption policies (lower likelihood), Regular monitoring of applied procurements and accounting policies (lowers likelihood), Strict administrative and financial procedures (lowers likelihood and impact).
Impact after mitigation: low risk
