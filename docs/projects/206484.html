Project Summary

UNHCR will work on an area-based approach in site and settlement support, targeting 60,000 displaced and host communities (54% women and 46% men) living in site-like settings, rural and urban locations. The project has three main components: (i) Enhancing community self-management that facilitates resilience and promotes solutions, (ii) Enhancing the protection environment- with a priority on addressing protection gaps through infrastructure maintenance and development interventions, and (iii) Enhancing coordination - facilitating equitable and dignified access to cross-sectoral humanitarian service through service monitoring and coordination

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

UNHCR will build on the gains made towards advancing self-management of IDP sites and localization through partnership with the relevant government departments. UNHCR will target sites and settlements that the local administration considers villages (also referred to Aldaires) and are part of the local municipality plans, providing an opportunity for a local integration solution. Across the targeted areas, the project will adopt the CCCM Cluster agreed implementation model for 2024, the ABC model: (i) A- Adjusting Approach by investing in population capacity and resilience. This phase involves phasing management techniques - from static to mobile and mobile to remote, introducing new tools and indicators, building government capacities, and adopting site integration with the host community. (ii) B- Building bridges between displaced persons and host population using a whole-community target model; building on local integration and social cohesion initiatives, including using cash-for-work and linkages with livelihood programming by the development partners and (iii) C- Creating cohesive community by providing a more dignified and improved standard of living; investing in community resource centers, AAP, accessible CFM, investing in settlement planning in line with the local municipality plans and promoting disaster resilient standards at settlement level.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The project activities will focus on three objectives in Cabo Delgado with particular focus on Mueda, Montepuez and Nangade districts. The project will aim to build on the gains made in 2023 in enhancing community-self management, safeguarding the protection environment, and facilitating effective coordination at the district and site-level to ensure the most in need are reached with the required assistance. The project will also aim to foster solutions including local integration opportunities where available. Specifically, the project will build on the gains made in the sites of i) Nicupa, ii) Ntele and iii) Massingiri in the Montepuez district and iv) Lianda, and v) Eduardo Mondlane in Mueda to enhance local integration approaches in line with the local district administration priorities. These among other activities listed below will include working with the local administration and communities in the restructuring of the site governance structures to ensure inclusive participation with Age, Gender and Diversity (AGD) representation, build the capacities of the community leadership on site governance, transition static CCCM presence to a mobile outreach to include host villages in service delivery, establishing community resource centres that serve both the displaced and the host communities and promotes accountability to the affected population (AAP). The project aims to partner with the local administration departments such as SDPI and the District Administration (DA) office to enhance ownership and facilitate solutions delivery.Activities:1. Enhancing community self-management that facilitate resilience and promotes solution: Facilitate integration of site-governance mechanism into the hosting village in close collaboration with the community leadership and local authorities. Establish site and settlement committees where required to facilitate community participation and engagement at all levels. Map and build the capacities of community structures through tailored site management and protection training modules.  Establish and or enhance community complaints and feedback mechanisms that are accessible to all. Establish community resource centers in strategic locations to act as information hubs serving the communities and service providers.  Provide capacity-building on camp management and protection principles to facilitate protection-sensitive self-management and self-governance structures. 2. Enhancing the protection environment- with a priority on addressing protection gaps through infrastructure maintenance and development interventions: Site and settlement infrastructure improvement and maintenance- through community-led cash-for-work initiatives. These include repairs required and the decommissioning of some structures that are out of use. Support quick impact projects in urban or rural settings: small to medium-scale community-led initiatives /projects that directly impact the displaced population and host community and promote peaceful coexistence. 3. Enhancing coordination - facilitating equitable and dignified access to cross-sectoral humanitarian service through service monitoring and coordination: Conduct quarterly surveys or feedback sessions with the displaced population.  Implement a robust data collection/headcount system that records demographic information (sex, age, and disability status) at the site level. Develop and share with service providers quarterly site profiles. Facilitate monthly district-level coordination meetings.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

UNHCR maintains a strict zero-tolerance towards sexual exploitation and abuse. Through the code of conduct, project staff are continuously sensitized on the Prevention from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) while ensuring that any sexual exploitation and abuse complaints are channelled through the appropriate internal mechanisms.UNHCR staff and its partners are trained on humanitarian principles and the key elements of the centrality of protection: do no harm principle, safety and dignity, effective access to services and assistance, and accountability with the affected populations. UNHCR has developed a protection monitoring tool for identifying risk profiles and collecting protection incidents, with indicators of age, gender, and diversity. UNHCR will ensure CCCM programmes mainstream protection delivery throughout its project cycle. UNHCR will also ensure CCCM interventions undertake due diligence, including issues around Housing Land and Property (HLP) associated disputes and risks, are mitigated and in close coordination with the Protection Cluster and HLP AoR. In addition, to guarantee the dignity of people, UNHCR’s CCCM will ensure timely and quality services are delivered while considering the specific needs of the affected populations, the respect for their culture and religion and respecting their freedom of choice.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

In 2023, UNHCR will aim for a more robust AAP mechanism that puts the affected communities at the center of programming. UNHCR will reinforce the existing community complaint and feedback mechanisms by deploying dedicated mobile teams. UNHCR will continue deploying static (information desks) and mobile units to support outreach activities, including key messaging on services available in the settlements. UNHCR will establish community resource centres in selected locations, providing a one-stop information hub for IDPs and host communities in the settlement area. UNHCR will ensure that persons with disabilities have access to all relevant information by preparing gender-responsive key messages in multiple and accessible formats. UNHCR will conduct household surveys to identify service gaps at the household level using a contextualized CCCM Cluster tool. Perception surveys will also be conducted to measure the service satisfaction levels and provide feedback to the community. Where required, UNHCR, as the co-chair of the CEAAP WG, will work with partners to review the functionality of the CFM mechanism and deploy a harmonized approach across sectors.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

UNHCR will build on the gains made in 2023, including the partnership with Humanity Inclusion in ensuring tailored assistance to persons with disabilities is provided. UNHCR CCCM, in close coordination with the protection team and specialized partners, will regularly register and identify persons with disabilities to ensure assistance is provided timely and, where required, referred for specialized assistance. UNHCR will ensure that persons with disabilities can access all relevant information by preparing key messages in multiple and accessible formats. UNHCR CCCM will also ensure site and settlement infrastructures are accessible to persons with disabilities and provide community centers that are easily accessible (disability friendly).

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

As part of its CCCM strategy, UNHCR ensures that the communities are sensitized and engaged in protecting the environment, emphasizing their responsibility for the local environment where they settle. UNHCR will support community-led initiatives in mitigating environmental impacts, including flood risk mitigation measures such as constructing drainages and tree planting interventions. Community engagement in eco-friendly activities will also enhance peaceful coexistence between the IDPs and the host communities, with key messaging provided throughout project implementation.
