Project Summary

In Somalia, the primary school enrollment ratio is among the world’s lowest at 70% or 3 million children of 4.4 million school-age children are out of school. Only 17% of children living in rural areas or IDPs settlements are enrolled in primary schools. According to the Somalia Education cluster, education gaps and needs are largely from a lack of adequate learning facilities, teachers, basic emergency teaching and learning materials, insecurity, lack of food/water, and limited WASH facilities to keep children in schools.The most affected children are the children living with disabilities as most of them are kept out of school. The available schools do not have the facilities to accommodate children living with disability and especially those from the rural areas.
Limited learning spaces, limited teachers support, limited learning kits, and CEC structures were identified as the education needs in the Southwest State of Somalia. As a result of displacement of IDPs into Baidoa and Berdale Districts has meant that the number of schools have reported increased enrollment, placing a further strain on already limited resources used by the host and IDP communities. In response to this, WRRS is proposing and intervention that aims at improving access to education by supporting four schools in Baidoa and Berdale rural through provision of school learning materials,Teacher incentives, WASH activities that includes hygiene promotion, construction of classrooms and provision of school meals to ensure that children are retained in schools. Priority will be given to children living with disabilities,disability friendly classrooms will be constructed as well when designing hygiene promotion messages will incorporate the needs of children with disability. The project will target 7951 boys and girls of school going children in Baidoa, Berdale, Afmadow and Kismayo Districts

1. - Projects activities

The project will identify the schools that the project will be implemented this will be done in consultation with the Ministry of Education, the local authorities, the IDPs leadership and other education stakeholders.1.Distribution of school learning materials to 7951 boys and girls,10% of the learning materials distributed will go the children living with disability.2.Construction of child friendly classrooms, a total of 15 classrooms will be constructed in the target 4 districts. WRRS will ensure that the classrooms constructed are disability friendly.3.Provision of teacher incentives to at least 50 teachers both male and female in the target schools of the 4 selected districts will be provided with incentives.4.Training of school education committees, at least 60 CEC will be trained 30 female and 30 male in the target 4 districts out of the 60 trained CEC,5% will be those living with disability to ensure that the needs of the disabled are incorporated in school management.5.Hygiene promotion campaigns will be conducted in the target schools of the 4 selected districts this will be mainly to reduce the risk of spread of AWD/ and cholera in schools. Messages will incorporate disability friendly messages.6.Provision of water in schools through water trucking in the selected schools in the four districts7.School meals will be provided to the target school of which 7951 boys and girls will benefit this will be done mainly to ensure that children are retained in schools8.The project will also train boys and girls on child protection and offer counseling services for the children who may have experienced trauma, psycho social support will also be offered in schools

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

WRRS will ensure that all the children and especially those from the minority clans are enrolled in schools, this will be done with the help of the camp leaders and the local authorities, this will be mainly to avoid the risk of exclusion for these children. The organization will also ensure that there is no risk of sexual exploitation for women and girls by establishing a help desk and a hot line whereby all cases of sexual exploitation will be reported and action taken. The project will also ensure adequate security in schools and especially ensure safety of girls from violence.The organization will work with the local authorities to offer adequate security in schools.
