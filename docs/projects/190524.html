Project Summary

FFor the past six years, the Amaro Special Woreda of the Southern Nations, Nationalities, and People's Region (SNNPR) has been known for recurrent conflict that has cost the lives of hundreds of people. Furthermore, the area is home to gambling dens where different groups spread conflicting messages among various ethnic groups who have lived in peace for centuries, including the Koore, Burji, Ale, Oromo, and Konso ethnic groups.
44,202 persons were displaced as a result of conflict that started by unidentified armed groups on January 10, 2022. Many individuals were forced to flee their homes, leaving behind their properties as a result of the conflict, and residents from 18 kebeles were split in half, fleeing to different direction. The IDPs lead precarious living conditions, as newly displaced people are added to the already worse situation.
Therefore, this project aims to provide ESNFI assistance to needy IDPs in Amaro special woreda. Dorcas did provide similar support in the last year. But the problem persists as new displacements are taking place following fresh clashes among the different ethnic groups in the area. A total funding of USD 454,554 is required to provide 1584 IDP households (estimated 8711 people) with badly needed ESNFI assistance over a period of 3 months (May 1, 2022 to July 31, 2022).
