Project Summary

The Project will mainly seek to strengthening COVID-19 response through-out the country. The priority areas will be determined by the COVID-19 case load in the affected provinces as well the needs that would have been identified. Primarily the project will seek to strengthen the surveillance, laboratory, case management, Infection prevention and control and Coordination pillar of the National COVID-19 preparedness and response plan.
For furtherance of Surveillance, RRTs, contact tracing and case investigation the initiatives carried out under this pillar capacity building of Rapid Response Team at all levels. The activities will also seek to strengthen contact tracing with the aim of increasing coverage from 75% to 100%. Follow-up on returning residents and strengthening information management will also be pursued including improving the information management.
Under strengthening laboratory capacity the project will reinforce the new government strategy to increase testing for COVID-19 through decentralization of testing centres to all provinces to a minimum of 1,300 PCR tests per day.
To strengthen case management pillar WHO and partners will carry out activities such as capacity of training health workers on case management. Establishment of isolation centres at all provinces and districts levels shall also be done.
The component on strengthening the pillar on Points of Entry (PoE) will be done jointly with IOM. This will entail strengthening screening, provision of isolation centres and providing initial management to 100% of suspected cases of COVID-19.
Strengthen capacity for Infection Prevention and Control will be done through provision of screening, triaging, source control in every health facility, local production of PPEs, implement empirical precautions for health workers as well as protecting the community, eg washing of hands, social distancing.
For enhanced coordination, planning and monitoring, WHO and other health partners will support the activated Public Health Emergency Operations Centre ( PHEOC )to be fully functional including conducting regular coordination meetings, drafting and sharing of sitreps and other relevant information.
