Project Summary

This project aims to restore and enhance access to quality, inclusive education for children and youth affected by the ongoing socio-political crisis in the Northwest and Southwest (NWSW) regions of Cameroon. The intervention targets internally displaced persons (IDPs), returnees, and host community children who have faced disruptions in their learning due to violence, displacement, and limited access to educational resources.
The project will focus on the rehabilitation of damaged schools, establishment of temporary learning spaces, provision of teaching and learning materials, and psychosocial support for learners and educators. To ensure continuity and inclusivity, capacity-building sessions for teachers on conflict-sensitive education and the integration of children with disabilities will be conducted.
Additionally, the initiative will engage parents, caregivers, and community leaders to promote safe school environments and reduce barriers to education, particularly for girls and vulnerable groups. By addressing immediate educational needs and fostering resilience, the project will contribute to a sustainable recovery of the education sector in the NWSW regions.
Targeting approximately 40000 children and 800 teachers across 220 schools, this intervention seeks to lay the foundation for long-term stability and development in these crisis-affected areas.

1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

1.1 Needs Assessment and Project Description The ongoing socio-political crisis in the Northwest and Southwest regions of Cameroon has disrupted access to education for thousands of children and youth. Schools have been closed, damaged, or destroyed, forcing many children out of the classroom. Internally displaced persons (IDPs), returnees, and host community children face significant barriers to education, including a lack of safe learning spaces, inadequate educational materials, and limited psychosocial support to cope with the trauma of conflict. Teachers have also been displaced, leading to a shortage of qualified educators. Identified Needs: Safe Learning Environments: Many schools are unsafe or non-operational due to conflict, necessitating the rehabilitation of damaged schools and the establishment of temporary learning spaces.Educational Resources: Students and teachers lack essential teaching and learning materials, including textbooks, writing tools, and didactic resources.Teacher Training: Teachers require capacity-building in conflict-sensitive pedagogy and psychosocial support to address the specific needs of learners in a crisis context.Psychosocial Support: Both learners and educators are experiencing trauma, requiring psychosocial support to improve their well-being and ability to engage in education.Community Engagement: Parents, caregivers, and community leaders need to be involved to promote education and ensure children’s safe return to schools.Activities and Implementation Plan: School Rehabilitation and Temporary Learning Spaces: Rehabilitate damaged schools and set up temporary learning spaces equipped with essential furniture and safety features.Provision of Learning Materials: Distribute textbooks, school supplies, and teacher kits to support effective teaching and learning.Teacher Training: Organize workshops to train teachers in conflict-sensitive teaching methods, classroom management, and inclusive education practices.Psychosocial Support Services: Provide psychosocial support sessions for students and educators, including recreational activities and counseling services.Community Sensitization: Conduct awareness campaigns to encourage school enrollment, particularly for girls and marginalized children, and to promote the importance of education in crisis recovery.Expected Results: -Improved Access to Education: Approximately 40000 children and youth will gain access to safe, inclusive learning spaces.-Enhanced Learning Outcomes: Learners will have access to quality educational resources and skilled teachers, improving their academic performance and resilience.-Strengthened Teacher Capacity: Teachers will be better equipped to deliver education in a conflict-affected context, ensuring continuity of learning.-Increased Community Engagement: Community members will actively support education initiatives, fostering a protective environment for children.This project directly addresses the urgent educational needs of conflict-affected populations, laying a foundation for long-term recovery and development in the Northwest and Southwest regions of Cameroon.
