Project Summary

In the Deir ez-Zor countryside, which aims to control the Syrian Democratic Forces (SDF), residents face a range of challenges and risks in various aspects of their daily lives. Among these challenges:
1. Security:
The population may be exposed to threats as a result of continuing disputes and disputes in the region.
2. Health:
There can be significant challenges in healthcare, as they are likely to affect neurons in part, due to their expression.
3. Protection of women:
Women may face additional challenges in terms of personal safety and protection from any instances of sexual, sexual or social violence.
4. Protection of children:
Children can have many children, but their limited access to education puts them at risk of recruitment under difficult circumstances.
5. Appearance protection:
You will find more additional problems regarding health care and knowledge of the services required.
These experiences highlight the lack of comprehensive and relief support unique to the region, including the provision of medical care, personal insurance, educational opportunities and social protection.
Among the many organizations that are designed to support the protection of the general environment for women and girls in rural Deir ez-Zor, a project can be implemented to activate service centers. These Centers may include a variety of diverse services that provide support to partial and at-risk groups. :
Psychosocial support:
Providing psychological counseling and psychosocial support services to deal with the psychological and social challenges they may face as a result of difficult circumstances.
Awareness and education:
Providing awareness and education programs on public health, humanitarian, human rights and gender equality issues.
Protection of rural women:
Providing tailored protection services for women experiencing violence, exploitation and discrimination, including establishing reporting and factual history in suspected cases.
Special service for the elderly:
Social support for the elderly to improve their quality of life and meet their special needs.
Implementing such a project requires close cooperation between the local government, non-governmental organizations, and international humanitarian agencies and providing the support required for sustainability and effectiveness in meeting the needs of the target population.
In rural Deir ez-Zor: Systematic facilitation groups on post-traumatic stress and self-change for children and adolescents will be launched. Facilitation sessions will be structured to enhance women's capacity to recognize, cope and report cases of sexual violence at the social level. It will facilitate structured sessions for the elderly to deal with the special needs of this age group. Age groups will also be available at the community center for civil status administration services.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

SALAM.org is committed to developing a clear and elaborate approach for MEAL based on the logical framework of the project and the indicators described in it, in addition to constantly responding to the observations and suggestions of the beneficiaries and key stakeholders, which will reach us through several channels such as monthly meetings, complaint box, WhatsApp, accountability committee, and e-mail. assigned to the project.Before starting the project, a detailed and accurate plan will be prepared to follow up the project activities and clarify the details of the indicators and the method of measuring them. The team will share the weekly follow-up reports with the project manager and the administrative team so that they can take appropriate action when necessary.The Monitoring and Evaluation Section will be responsible for the follow-up operations through the periodic review of attendance records. It will also compare the indicators that show the performance of the beneficiaries before starting the systematic activities with the final evaluation of them after each activity.Evaluation processes: The results that show the development of the beneficiary will be analyzed on a phased basis, as the follow-up and evaluation team will conduct interviews with the beneficiaries and review the pre- and post-evaluation forms, and their other comments will be analyzed, including the results of the focus groups, and accordingly we will issue our reports per month and we share it with the partner.Accountability: We use the Community Accountability Reporting Mechanisms (CARM) system which is located on the SALAM.org websites, we receive comments and complaints through WhatsApp, email, and compliant boxes that are located on different project sites. The boxes can only be accessed by the MEAL coordinator. This will ensure that beneficiaries provide comments and express their concerns and suggestions, their comments will be reviewed and addressed on at least a weekly basis and those comments will be included in our periodic reports to improve the implementation of activities and services.Our organization is committed to the methodology of documenting and learning from the results of our activities. This process will result in analyzes and useful observations summarized in periodic reports on the results and effects of participation in project activities on beneficiaries when participating in any activity in the center.Lessons learned and important observations will also be discussed in the monthly meetings of the project management team and transferred through the appropriate communication channels to the internal departments in SALAM.org or the main stakeholders whose work is related to these observations.
