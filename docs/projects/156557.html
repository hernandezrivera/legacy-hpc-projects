Needs

According to HNO 2019 draft version: An estimated 6.5 million Syrians are food insecure and an additional 2.5 million people are at risk of food insecurity, from the assessed households, an estimated 40 percent of Syrians spend more than 65 percent of their expenditure on food, 57.2 percent of food insecure households reported to have outstanding debt and 65 percent of food insecure people are adopting crisis or emergency negative coping strategies. The worst drought in 30 years affected cereal production in 2017/2018 winter season in Syria. This has come as an additional burden on the prolonged crisis with the lowest wheat production at 1.2 million metric tonnes since 1989. There is a shortfall of wheat to meet domestic requirements (food use) of 3.4 million metric tonnes.There is an urgent need for multiple actions to provide food and livelihoods assistance as per the most feasible modality to the People in Need and at risk as established by the sector.Syria’s dependence on food assistance is expected to continue throughout the coming year and access to a diversified diet will remain a key need to ensure adequate nutritional status of the acutely food insecure, there are 6.5 million acute people in need, and 4 million risk people in need. The two targeted areas became accessable in recent months. IDPs and marginalized households have been deprived access to humanitarian support. The project is therefore needed to respond to the needs in the two areas due to the following :* Dilapidated/damaged bread supply infrastructure,* Extreme poverty,* Starvation and malnutrition, * Limited number of active local bakeries in Jarablus and Menbij, * High price of flour and bread especially for the families headed by women who lack a stable source of income.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

1. Low Capacity coupled with challenges in recruitment and retention of appropriately skilled staff at all levelsMitigation measure: - Bridging the staff gap- implementing a capacity building and training program for staff at various tiers and implementation responsibilities2. Lack of coordination and synergies with representatives of target communitiesMitigation measure: involve focal persons from the local council to coordinate with the beneficiary elders 3. Identification of beneficiaries and appreciation of project achievements will be weak because they do not hear, read or see enough of project activities and positive impactsMitigation measure: - Encourage field visits by community elders with appointed focal persons to see the results and hear from beneficiaries directly- Develop a communications strategy to guide constituency building through internal and external communications with Project stakeholders- Consider Media communication and mobile applications 4. Grievances and complaints of expected direct beneficiaries are ignored by the implementing partner with all the negative consequences this will haveMitigation measure:- Establish a comprehensive Grievances Redress Mechanism (GRM) with well-defined guidelines for complaint acknowledgement and redressal. The GRM to be publicized externally as part of each project communication's strategy- staff are provided with the necessary training to report unaddressed complaints 5. Insufficient supervision and poor-quality control will cause faults and deficiencies in project deliverablesMitigation measure: periodically carry out spot checks, certify quality and report back to QRC 6. Continuing risk of insecurity and potential escalation in insurgency related violence with direct impacts on project activitiesMitigation measure:- train field staff on MOSS compliance (Minimum Operational Safety Standards)- Use of alternative measures for project supervision including Remote Asset Monitoring to carry out spot checks in the target areas 7. Non-transparent procedures of recruitment and procurement processes may lead to project getting a bad reputationMitigation measure: Establish a wider and transparent dissemination of project procurement and recruitment advertisements 8. Sustainable functioning of distribution or project implementation not well ensuredMitigation measure:- Contribution of identified and planned capacity building by trainings - Encourage communities to provide their feedback on the distribution process or project deliverables- Ensure M&E efficacy- Qualified staff hired
