Project Summary

This proposed project is designed to improve the livelihoods of poor farmers those have no access for agriculture inputs to sustain their agricultural production. The project will primarily target the most vulnerable farmers those in need for such assistance and extremely affected by the recent droughts in Gedo region. The project will exercise and focus on improving agricultural production through provision of assorted seeds, pesticides and fertilizers, farming tools, land preparation support, and training on Good Agricultural Practices by targeting 1,500 small-scale farmers in Luuq and Bardere districts of Gedo Region, Somalia. The target families for this project includes: extremely poor households, female headed households, child headed households and families with disabled people. Thousands of hectors of fertile lands in these areas are currently remaining unused due to lack of proper investment. Despite many of people in Gedo region have land or can access to lands, however, due to lack of capital investments they are not capable to utilize their lands properly.
According to the most recent data by UNDP, Somalia has ta 73% poverty rate. Somalia is among the world’s poorest nations, according to its national GDP and other poverty indicators. The government’s instability contributes greatly to this problem. In addition, incessant threats to food and water security maintain poor standards of living.
Somali women and children have been living in severe conditions for over decades. Recurrent droughts and armed conflicts have extremely resulted in repeated crop failure, reduction of livestock increase due to poverty and vulnerability. Inflation, deteriorating purchasing power and lack of coping mechanisms further intensify their poverty and vulnerability and make them the most poor and vulnerable.
Currently, agriculture is the second most important economic activity in the project area. It is the main source of food security and livelihood option for a large number of small-scale farmers. Agriculture is currently an underdeveloped sector; as such, it has a considerable potential, especially for both cereal and vegetables and also for the creation of employment opportunities, mainly in the riverine communities in proposed project areas of Luuq and Bardere districts.

Project Activities

1. Community mobilization and sensitization through meetings with stakeholders (HA staff, Local Authorities, MoA representatives in the project locations). 2. Identiﬁcation, selection and registration of 750 of small-scale farmers in Luuq and 750 small-scale farmers in Bardere district. extremely poor households, female headed households, child headed households and families with disabled people, households with elderly people.4. Procurement and distribution of assorted seeds, farming tools, pesticides and fertilizers. The seeds will contain: Maize, Sorghum, Cowpeas and Vegetables (Tomato, Onion, Sweet Pepper, Watermelon, Carrot, Okra, Cucumber and Hot Pepper). 5. Extensive land preparation support for the most vulnerable 750 small-scale farmers, each farmer will get 3hours of land preparation support.6. Training of 200 farmers as ToT trainers on good agricultural practices, disease control and proper storage for the fodder after harvest. Each farmer will train other 10 farmers.

Mitigation Measures

Human Appeal UK has been operating in the districts in Gedo Region since 2017 and is well aware of the local context, needs and potential risks. Human Appeal UK has sound risk management practices in place, complies with country and Global level standards and has comprehensive context specific contingency plans in place. Our country office has devoted safety and security focal point who provides guidance to the implementing staff in these areas. Since 2017, Human Appeal UK had maintained close working relations with relevant local authorities in these districts and receive regular updates from them on safety and security concerns. Risk 1a – Mitigation Plan: Similar to many parts in Somalia, the safety and protection of people in most locations within the districts of Gedo Region remain volatile. There are potential risks and a threat of exposure in most parts of Gedo Region. To mitigate this, the project has selected Luuq and Bardere as target locations for intervention, as these areas are more easily accessible for support and are less exposed to these threats. The chances of beneficiary exposure to this risk is low and will not have significant impact on project implementation. To mitigate this risk, HA UK will consult and cooperate with UNMAS to verify IED clearance missions to the proposed locations before the start of the project activities. Risk 2a – Mitigation Plan: Minority groups prone to being ignored for most interventions in these areas, in most cases members of marginalized community’s/ minority groups are not included in consultations and/or are excluded from humanitarian services/assistance. The chances of Risk are low. To mitigate this risk, HA UK will ensure the inclusion of these groups in consultations and humanitarian services as well. HA UK will also establish selection criteria for the project beneficiaries that will make sure people’s access to assistance without discrimination. Risk 3a – Mitigation Plan: External stakeholder interference in beneficiary selection: Protection concerns stemming from displacement. In some case the local authorities may intervene and influence on selection project beneficiaries. The chances of risk are medium. To mitigate this risk, HA UK will cooperate with relevant line ministries at Federal and State level to avoid any influence from the local administration and will also ensure registration of beneficiaries and distribution of assistance will have to be done in safe and protected areas in coordination with local authority being duty bearer.
