Project Summary

The project aims to contribute to improving the living conditions of families displaced to the districts of Mecufi, Ancuabe, Chiure, Balama and Namuno, through the distribution of NFIs to repair and finish their shelters, which they are building with local material, however, in the midst of several difficulties, mainly regarding coverage.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Since 2017, Cabo Delgado has been experiencing attacks by non-state armed groups that have gradually displaced communities who are now seeking resettlement in other provinces, such as Nampula and Niassa. These attacks have led to the loss of lives and have several damaged infrastructures that were already severely affected by cyclone Kenneth in 2019. In order to attend the vulnerable families that are now Displaced in south districts of province, the project targets to distribute NFIs to an estimated number of 8000 affected households (Mecufi – 1000HH, Ancuabe – 3000HH, Chiure – 2000HH, Balama – 1000HH and Namuno – 1000HH) particularly female headed households, elderly headed households, orphans and vulnerable children, child headed households, widows headed households and families caring for orphans.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The priority activities to be implemented in the areas selected for the execution of this project, specifically Chiure, Ancuabe, Mecufi, Balama and Namuno will be the following: The first activity in making this project feasible is to contact the government and local authorities in order to conduct a community consultant to explain the modality of targeting for the most vulnerable beneficiaries and it will end with selection of beneficiaries. The NFIs to be distributed by Caritas are: tarpaulins, stakes, sticks, bamboo, ropes, nails, hammer, handsaw, wire, machete, for 8000 households. Caritas will explain the modality such as dissemination of information on how the distribution will take place in accordance with “do not harm “ration size for beneficiaries, complaints mechanisms. Distribution points will be conveniently located ensuring ease of access by community members particularly women and places where there are easy transports. The last activity will be monitoring the distribution process, accountability which will consist of compiling partners reports and reporting to the Shelter Cluster, and the local government authorities as well as to the donor. Note: the activities will be implemented simultaneously, as Caritas has a large team capable of covering the implementation areas.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The identification of risks will be done through involvement and dialogue with local leaders, it is known that the target group belongs to several ethnic groups. To seek to know, together with the leaders, what are the main priorities and immediate needs for the beneficiaries. All target groups will be included to avoid possible confusion between them.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

Caritas as the humanitarian arm of the church, serves all the needy without any discrimination. If there are people qualified to support the distribution, Caritas involves them in their work and in return, they gain experience in the work of assisting the needy, which they can use in the future.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

People with disabilities will be one of the priorities during the distribution, reason why within the community consultation, the field officers will be trained to understand the modality of targeting for the most vulnerable beneficiaries such as people with disabilities with leadership character and ability to do some sort of work, they will be included in the team. Distribution points will be conveniently located ensuring ease of access by community members particularly people with disabilities and women.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

During the distribution of shelter kits, the field officers will be trained to explain the targeted local communities on how to keep the environment clean by avoiding to throw garbage in inappropriate places, will be respected the environment consideration by avoiding opening big holes, duping rubbish at any place and promoting hygiene debates.Some sessions of individual and collective hygiene will be carried out by the activists and good use of latrines, water sources.
