Project Summary

Over 1.6 million children are in dire need of educational assistance in South Sudan due to multifaceted crises caused by conflict, flood and external displacement from the neighboring Sudan war (Education Cluster Need overview). In northern Bahr EL Ghazal only, more than 300,000 children are affected by flood emergency and the number keep increasing in Aweil East, North, South and West counties. about 50 percent of the displaced population are school going children who need continued access to education at resettlement highland areas.
The displaced people including teachers and learners are completely disorganized, learning process is majorly disrupted and children have lost sense of social security and protection provided by school. The highland areas where displaced population sought refuge are just empty open spaces which do not have classroom and education materials like books, blackboard and chalks to start under tree school whilst families do not have financial capacity to purchase new books and chalks/backboard although the teachers are willing to volunteer and provide continued access to learning for displaced children.
By January 2025, the number of the affected population might increase to more than double because the Uganda Government keep releasing water from Lake Victoria into River Nile which trigger subsequent flooding. The overflow of the Nile result to flood of lowland areas and hence displacement of uninformed population including damaging of school infrastructure and scholastics materials.
In consultation with education cluster and MOGEI, Nurture South propose to response to education needs of the most affected children in Aweil east, North South and west counties through enhancing community structures to mitigate risk pose by flood on children and education, establish at least 10 learning spaces in highland to enable continuity of learning at relocation site, construct 10 latrines with 4 stances to withstand gender sensitive sanitation need in school. Provide services for mental health and psychosocial support to children and school management committee, establish 10 coordination and communication mechanism to strengthen delivery of humanitarian assistance and improve community capacity to protect children from any form of abuse and delivery of education supply to affected children in Aweil East and Aweil West counties.

1. Needs

Twenty-three (23) schools submerged under water and education process for about 12000 learners (65% females) have been disrupted by flood displacement in Aweil East, North, South and Aweil West. 25% of the displaced learners are semi-candidates. The displaced candidates have higher chances of missing 2025 final examination for primary leaving certificate and the hope is deeming for them to to continue with studying if no plan is initiated within 2025. Almost 80 teachers, 25 volunteers are redundant at the IDPs site. Lack of semi-permanent Temporary Learning Structures continue to delay resumption of education in displacement site as well as absence of education materials like books, chalks, blackboard to motivate volunteer teachers to commit to learning to vulnerable children. The other factor affecting resumption of emergency education is the random movement of teachers in search for life sustaining jobs in town like Aweil and Wau which is exacerbated by delay of teacher salary and in-activeness of motivation initiative programme that would provide teacher with incentive to encourage teachers in emergency and hard-to-reach areas to commit to provision of education where deem necessitate.

2. Project activities

a) The action will undertake mass campaign to encourage parents to enroll children into established semi-permanent TLS at the camp will be conducted with active involvement and meaningful participation of community members in particular camp leaders and concern local authority as back to learning mobilization drives enable children resume learning at displaced person’s camp.b) Nurture South Sudan propose to construct 10 conducive semi-permanent temporary learning spaces (TLS) as an alternative education facility to enable continued access to education for flood displaced children in Aweil East, North, South and Aweil West. High consideration will be put on use of available local materials which are suitable and durable and will be obtained within the community and use for construction of TLS. However, few modern materials shall be included when deem necessary.c) In addition to, supply of scholastic materials is essential to expedite resumption of learning. d) Nurture South Sudan proposes to facilitate local authority to screen teachers, identify and recruit volunteer teachers to meet teacher classroom ratio and provide refresher training on school management and psycho-social support to teachers. e) Provision of dignity items to adolescent girls will encourage and safeguard girls continued access to education in emergency. f) Nurture South Sudan will facilitate teachers and school administrators will help to identified children that have improper performance, assess their need and provide necessary catch-up lesson through designing special session for them either after school or follow up to home classes. g) Provision of incentive to teachers including volunteer teachers is necessary in ensuring continuity of education for displaced children. h) Conduction community training on risk management to enable community safe education facility against future flood occurrence including creation of dyke around the school. i) In coordination with Mental health and Psycho-social Support Sub cluster/Working Group (MHPSS) and Education cluster, Nurture South Sudan will facilitate provision of tailored services of MHPSS to children traumatized by flood related crisis.j) Community will be mobilized to involves in creating protective measures around the schools like dyke and fence to protect school from damage by water and animals. Young people will mostly be engage in providing such services to promote continuity of education in their camp for longer period.
