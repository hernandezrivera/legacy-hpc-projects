Project Summary

This project seeks to ensure a better GBV response through GBV prevention and effective community response approaches which creates significant impact in target vulnerable communities. The project will be targeting a total number of 32,175 conflict affected populations in vulnerable target communities affected by the ongoing crisis!
These target communities involved in the implementation of this project in Mezam, Momo, Boyo, Bui, Menchum and Meme divisions of the North-West and South-West Regions include the following: Bafut, Bali, Bamenda, Bamenda 3, Batibo Benakuma, Fundong, Kumbo East, Kumbo West, Mbengwi, Kumba_North and Kumba_South localities.
In implementing this project, COHESODEC is committed to ensuring the following response activities:
> Ensure capacity building for 4 women led organizations-(WLO) on GBV response, prevention and coordination in the humanitarian context while ensuring that 20 women are trained from these organizations and making sure that 100% of these WLO, received resources to implement GBV activities.
> Provide basic vocational training, economic support and dedicated livelihood activities to 15 women and girls participating in training and involved in income generating activities while also making sure that women and girls are supported with startup kits or CASH for income generating activities.
>Reinforced GBV prevention through men engagement through 30 men and boys platforms established in all our localities of implementation.
> Strengthen GBV prevention activities at community-level including sensitization of 60 conflict-affected communities, local authorities, traditional and religious leaders making sure that at least 30,000 people are reached-out to during awareness raising and sensitization activities at the community level.
> Conduct 8 safety audits and risk and vulnerability mapping for GBV
> Strengthen through training the capacities of 50 GBV service providers in case management including MHPSS
>Provide holistic support to 350 survivors of gender-based violence to enable them regularly access mental health or psychosocial support services, receive medical support, 40 Post Rape Kits, 80 GBV survivors receive legal assistance and 350 GBV survivors are referred to other service providers for service provision.

Plan fields

1 - Besoins / Needs

This project will need the services of experts from within and without COHESODEC in the provision of capacity building to women led organizations (WLO) involving GBV response, prevention and coordination in this humanitarian context.The project will also require financial resources to provide basic vocational training, economic support and dedicated livelihood activities to women and girls involved in income generating activities who shall be supported with startup kits or CASH for income generating activities. The project will also need financial resources to reinforced GBV prevention through 30 established platforms of men and boys. Moreover, this project will require financial resources to produce sensitization materials and pay for radio airtime with which to strengthen GBV prevention activities at community-level including conflict-affected communities, local authorities, traditional and religious leaders through outreach sessions conducted at community and during awareness raising and sensitization activities at the community level.Furthermore, the project shall require financial resources to conduct 8 safety audits and risk and vulnerability mapping for GBV while also strengthening the capacity of 50 GBV service providers in case management including MHPSS.Finally, COHESODEC in the implementation of this project will require financial resources to provide holistic support to survivors of GBV, ensuring at least 350 GBV survivors access mental health or psychosocial support services, receive medical support services, supported with 40 Post Rape Kits and 80 GBV survivors receive legal assistance while over 350 GBV survivors get referred to other service providers for service provision.
