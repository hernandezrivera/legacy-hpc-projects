1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Mozambique has been facing complex challenges resulting in serious humanitarian impact. Challenges requiring urgent action are the conflict in the northern Mozambique, Cabo Delgado (CD) province which resulted in about 1,093,256 internal displaced people of which 81% are in Cabo Delgado and the remain in Nampula and Niassa; ongoing cholera outbreak in Niassa province and the COVID-19 pose additional health risk. A number of hospitals have been destroyed or closed and affected the provision of essential health services both in affected districts and the districts currently hosting the fled population. Limited access to essential health is also increasing maternal and child health problems as well as loss to follow-up of treatment for chronic disease such as HIV. All these require measures for disease prevention, strengthening referral systems, establishing services provision though mobile health teams to deliver key essential services, and functioning community health workers. UNICEF will respond these humanitatian situation in collaboration with health cluster partners including government health authorities at province and districts level targeting about 215,000 children in Cabo Delgado, Nampula and Niassa, and funding is urgently required to support these interventions and provide timely assistance to children in need.

• Implement outreach services to affected children through integrated mobiles health services to deliver essential child health services (immunization, newborn care, IMNCI, PMTCT defalter tracing) in IDPS and host families in the districts afected by conflict.• Improve access to quality essential health services through recruitment and deployment of additional health staff; refresher training of health professionals and community health workers on essential health packages in emergency setting to manage common illness and referral systems; • Prevent measles and malaria outbreaks in IDPs children and host families by supporting vaccination campaigns and mosquito nets distribution, • Ensure adequate program implementation through contracting of experienced technical assistance for humanitarian response Geographic areas: Ancuabe, Balama, Chiure, Pemba, Ibo, Macomia, Mecufi, Meluco, Metuge, Mocimboa da Praia, Montepuez, Mueda, Muidumbe, Namuno, Nangade, Palma, Quissanga, Angoche, Cidade de Nampula, Erati, Ilha de Mocambique, Lalaua, Larde, Liupo, Malema, Meconta, Meconta, Mecuburi, Memba, Mogincual, Mogovolas, Moma, Monapo, Mossuril, Muecate, Murrupula, Nacala, Nacala-Velha, Nacaroa, Rapale, Chimbonila, Lichinga, Cuamba, Lago, Majune, Mandimba, Marrupa and Ngauma

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The project aims to assist the IDPs affected by insecurity in Cabo Delgado, Nampula and Niassa, with essential health services to prevent disease outbreaks and death. By doing so, the project will be protecting the affected population against additional suffering. While delivering health service the project will also ensure that vulnerable children and women or victims of violence are identified and referred to the appropriate case managers in coordination with protection cluster and social action department..

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

UNICEF and health cluster partners conduct joint need assessment before the intervention. In the assessment the voice of beneficiaries are heard and will be taken in account in terms of the priority interventions for health. Communities will participate in planning and implementation of mobile health brigades and community health workers will be trained to improve social mobilization

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

To improve inclusion of people leaving with disability, the provision of both curative services included in the project will prioritize and provide adapted facilities based on consultations and identification of needs.Will also support the community level through APEs, to be able to identify and refer children with disability.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project will include procurement and distribution of life saving interventions such as mosquito nets and vaccines which generate waste. To minimize the environmental impact a waste management plan is part of implementation of immunization campaigns, glass and rubber materials are collected for a controlled incineration on site or in dedicated facilties. For mosquito nets all packaging will be of biodegradable materials. Social mobilization and behavior campaign are part of the mosquito net distribution to avoid using them for fishing and other harmful aplications.
