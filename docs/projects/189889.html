1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Taking into consideration existing needs of IDPs popoliations, the project aims to reach 9,000 displaced individuals (1,800 Households) living in the districts of Montepuez, Ibo and Balama thought provision of 1,800 shelters, non-food items life-saving assistance and access to livelihoods for most vulnerable crisis affected population. The project activities wil be implemented with community participatory approaches in line with AVSI AAP policy. The selection criteria will be developed in consultation with the communities prioritizing vulnerable household.AVSI, in 2021 provided CCCM and Shelters and NFI interventions in Montepuez, benefitting about 32.100 IDPS.The construction of the shelters would be based on the experience gained in the actual interventions in Mararange, Mirate and Massingere where, when possible the procurement of shelter material would be done through the affected community in order to provide in the same time a source of livelihood, and when not possible in the local market. For new resettlement, the shelter construction would be accompained by distribution of NFI kits in coordination with partners and site management.Building up on AVSI solid and multisectorial presence (CCCM, WASH, Protection, FSL, Nutrition, Education, Shelter/NFI, Energy) in the area, which would allow an area based and multi-purpose response.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

1. Evaluation, Identification and Selection of the HHs utilizing vulnerability criteria in case of need of prioritization.2. Shelter assessment (safety audit -if not done recently by site planner), shelter checklist); 3. Community focus group to share and define construction standars as well as main need and possible solutions more fit to the camp/environment4. Capacity building and on site support to local artisans – done mostly with technical and continuous support on ground.5. Procurements of Material for shelter construction or NFI.6. Selection of IDPs engaged in constrction as local artisans, promoting inclusion and involvement of women and people with disability/members of HHs who host a person living with a disability.7. Shelter construction – will be done involving as much as possible the affected population and preferably with local material or locally procured material. 8. NFI kits distribution, which includes eventually items received in-kind. 9. Monitoring – shelter construction, satisfaction, promotion of best practices10. Complaint meccanisms setting in line with AVSI AAP policy and possibly with harmonized cluster/partners tools.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The project will ensure protection mainstreaming process in order to esure DO NO HARM approach, promoting meaningful access, safety and dignigy in all actions. In order to guarantee this principles all the staff will be trained on the principles and procedures in place and will sign the reference documents such as the Internal Code of Conduct, the Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse Policy (PSEA) and the AVSI Children Safeguarding Policy documents as well as the AVSI lead protection mainstreaming checklists. In order to avoid conflicts between IDPs (new or extended) and host communities, targeting for individual assistence will be done in consultation with the communities, defining together the criteria of vulnerability and ensuring that all the different groups are represented in the consultations. This will also ensure the use of the principles of impartiality and neutrality: no distinction of nationality, race, religion, social condition and political affiliation will be made and no sides will be taken in any dispute. Protection monitoring will be garuatee through routinely monitoring access to services by women, girls, boys and men through spot checks and discussions with communities, and promptly address the obstacles to equal access

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

Gender equality and women’s empowerment are preconditions for effective and sustainable development and the enjoyment of universal human rights. The project will ensure IASC GAM within the different phases of he project:- Need Analysis set, in order that all and the right people are understood and counted - - Tailoring and adapting the interventions according to gender and age- Ensuring a gender and aged balanced participation on the feed-back processThe project shall make ensure gender equaility and inclusion criteria are used. AVSI promotes and encourages active participation of women. As such, the project has a strong commitment to ensure the full participation of women in decision-making and implementation of its programmes including in beneficiary selection, implementation, and monitoring to the greatest extent possible within culturally appropriate contexts. Gender will be mainstreamed throughout the entire project. For all constructions, community members, and women in particular will be consulted on the proposed models, as well as on the structure’s orientation and placement; women with previous knowledge on construction practices will be prioritized in the selection of artisans. All artisans and daily workers will receive a basic PSEA training before initiating construction, provided by trained AVSI protection personnel.The project shall collect and report program monitoring data which includes sex- and age-disaggregated data of beneficiaries and will ensure that such informations are used purposely to improve all groups and age inclusion, participation and satisfaction.This project will follow AVSI Foundation SOP described in the AVSI FOUNDATION ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY on ‘feedback and accountability mechanism’. Newly recruited and old staff are always oriented and receive refresher trainings to stress the importance of the opinions and views of communities concerning program implementation. Beneficiaries and the population at large is also made aware of the forms available to provide suggestions and complaints. This project will adopt a community-based and participatory approach. The selection criteria will be developed in consultation with the communities and will aim to upgrade living-conditions within a settlement and when possible through the construction of new shelters. AVSI will conduct the assessment in KOBO according to the agreed criteria, which will be reviewed and verified by the local authorities and the representatives of the communities. In AVSI’s experience, this has proven time and again to be particularly productive, as not only people’ concerns and priorities are taken into account.Example of good practices:https://assessments.hpc.tools/assessment/c891c32a-7c60-4aa9-bf6d-a8b48a84730aActivities of MEAL and surveys will be utilized not only for informing program strategies, but also to devise possible and feasible way forward with local communities as per cases described below: https://assessments.hpc.tools/assessment/cded105d-be61-4480-b95c-117ea9fb4013

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

In the present project proposal screening, surveillance and participation of people with disabilities have been included as key activities and indicators. During their implementation, the detection of people with disabilities will be reported (disaggregated by gender and age) and given special attention. This translates into prioritizing the abovementioned target population as to the assistance for Shelter, NFI and Protection, especially for those cases having motor disabilities to which AVSI Foundation will provide logistics support.AVSI Foundation will, when possible, organize specific studies on the inclusion and conditions of persons living with a disability and will utilize such studies to devise inclusive strategies on round table with PwD as well as local communities. Examples of such work in similar context can be found at: https://assessments.hpc.tools/assessment/e3f17126-d1f5-4a78-80a4-99dea7c46e69AVSI Foundation will ensure the implementation of sensitization sessions throughout its activities to ensure that these people are supported within the community and given special attention when coordinating and planning activities with local SDSMAS and partners
