Project Summary

The proposed project will contribute to support Lebanese families with the needed hygiene material complemented with elderly and baby kits as needed. The hygiene kits will be distributed to 644 vulnerable Lebanese households (3220 individuals) meeting one or more of the following profiles:
4+ members
HH with children (≤5 years)
HH with 1+ members living with disability
female- or elderly-headed HH
HH with pregnant/lactating women
HH with 1+ elderly members
Identification of HH will be through referrals from the WFP, National Poverty Targeting Program (NPTP), Anera’s own outreach, and local organizations and national coordination mechanisms. Selection of beneficiaries will be based on the sector-approved vulnerability assessment of HHs.
Monthly Assistance - 644 HH will receive 2 rounds of hygiene kits complemented with elderly and baby kits as needed. Distribution will be through: 1) distribution points or 2) door-to-door, based on the geographic distribution, density of selected households, and mobility capacity.
Anera will follow necessary health precautions and safety measures in both distribution modalities (including distancing, use of PPEs and crowd management).
Verified delivery notes, stock control, and distribution receipts will be the basis of monthly distribution monitoring and reporting. Within 30 days of the distribution, Anera's team will conduct a post-distribution evaluation with a sample of beneficiaries to measure the immediate impact of the assistance.
The project will be implemented in partnership with 4 local organizations which will primarily handle distribution. Anera will also mobilize a team of trained youth (through UNICEF-funded cash for work activities) to help in HH identification, baseline assessment, and post-distribution assessment.
Anera's management and operations team in Beirut will lead the development of tools, competitive procurement, finance and compliance management, MEAL, and reporting.
Anera will leverage its solid relationships with the local NGOs with which Anera has had successful previous experiences and which have the infrastructure that facilitates orderly distributions (outreach, space, logistic support, etc.). For safe and proper implementation of the project, Anera will implement a capacity building plan targeting all partner organizations prioritizing PSEA, COVID-19, accountability and reporting, and anti-fraud/anti-corruption policies and practices.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

644 HH (3220 individuals) will receive 2 rounds of hygiene kits; each round includes the distribution of 1 hygiene kit. Out the of 644 HH, 64 HH identified with elderly in need for elderly kits will receive 2 elderly kits distributed with the hygiene kits, and 32 HH identified with babies will receive a total of 2 baby kits with the distributed hygiene kits.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

Anera has well-established policies regarding protection of vulnerable populations including PSEA, child protection, and data protection policies. During the distributions and the follow up visit, if GBV is detected or beneficiaries request assistance in this matter, they will be referred through pre-existing referral pathways, to the Anera Program Team (which will investigate further), and to other specialized inter-agency partners. All project staff members will also be required to sign a Code of Conduct for protection measures, especially on Child Protection and Safeguarding and on of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA). Protection risks from travelling will be reduced by allowing goods to be delivered directly to female-headed households where needed.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

This project relies mostly on vulnerability assessments and beneficiary inputs to create the primary recipient list and determine suitable kit and distribution requirements. Anera also drew from its previous experience in the distribution of hygiene kits in the areas to beneficiaries of similar profiles to inform the kits’ contents, and the best procurement and distribution policies to enact, in order to most suit the needs of the recipients in a timely manner. The involvement of local youth from Anera’s UNICEF-lead project in the kit assembling and distribution will be another way of involving stakeholders from the local community in the project.
