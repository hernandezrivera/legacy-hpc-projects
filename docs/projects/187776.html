Project Summary

Maram has designed this proposed to provide Early Recovery and Livelihoods assistance for the most affected people in NW Syria to strengthen the community resilience and reduce of socio-economic
vulnerabilities. The project will target Darkosh community and 6 camps in Darkosh sub-district
This will be achieved by the following outcomes :
1.Access to short term livelihoods opportunities involving CFW approach among others provided
Maram Foundation will support 300 short-term work opportunity creation through selection and verification of CFW beneficiaries within the targeted community and camps . those CFW un-skilled labours will support the road rehabilitation , market rehabilitation and bakery rehabilitation activities
2.Physical access to and from markets rehabilitated for movement of people and goods
Maram Foundation will rehabilitate 20 KM of roads that connecting the main market to facilitate the goods ,and people (IDPs and host communities )
3.Providing vocational training and skills development for 600 individuals in Babysitting and parenting, electronic literacy, knitting
wool, manufacturing homemade detergents, sewing, and hairdressing administering a startup business grant for 200 of them program in 6 camps
4.Local small businesses and entrepreneurs are supported are restored
5. Essential community and public services are revived and restored
Maram Foundation will design of solid waste and rubble removal plan , Provide of waste management requirements and personal protective tools and Supervisie and monitoring of rubble removal
This outcome will be achieved through CFW labours as well
6- MARAM will establish CNs that contains the society members to participate in the project planning , all the project activities will ensure the social engagement and monitoring via CN members that were selected by the community members.
Mainly , Maram Foundation will target 20000 beneficiaries in this project as direct beneficiaries of the market rehabilitation, bakery rehabilitation, road and other services / 5200 HHs and those beneficiaries are IDPs and host communities (3680 men , 4720 women, 5020 boys, and 6580 girls ) from different age groups (Young children, Children,
Adolescents, Young, Middle-aged adults, and elderly). This includes about 780 Persons with Disabilities
MF will consider Do No Harm approach by consulting community/local stakeholders in the area of implementation on project design to avoid harming local producers.
As part of MF intervention and during the implementation, Maram will coordinate and harmonize with the Clusters and the NGOs working at the targeted areas to ensure that there is no overlap with other agencies of the provided assistance, especially for CFW activities.

4 - Needs

based on the most recent (HSOS) NWS -Reach Feb 21, the most reported overall priority needs were Livelihoods, Winterization, Food, and Healthcare. During early 2021, heavy floods occurred in NWS. More than 100 IDP sites suffered unusual floods and bad weather. According to CARE International - Need Assessment report for the camps affected by the flood - 19 February 2021” The access to the camps in NWS creates a big challenge facing the humanitarian organizations who are planning to respond to the IDPs who are living in these sites.” And the report also added” The respondents have been asked about the challenges in accessing the food items which face the IDPs who are living in the 242 assessed camps. 491 respondents (44%) mentioned that the high price of food creates challenges facing people in accessing food, 482 respondents 44% mentioned the lack of money to buy food, 58 respondents (5%) mentioned that the available food is not enough, 58 respondents (5%) said that there are no cooking materials, and the rest 15, 1% varied between there is no food available or no functioning market.” In addition to the urgent need of livelihood and cash-based assistance reported by monitoring humanitarian institutes, these floods have come to add additional level of vulnerability to these affected HHs. According to last reported risky and flooded sites by CCCM/ERL clusters, there are 98 flooded sites in Maaret Tamsrin, Idleb and Qourqeena. In these sites, 11330 HHs were affected by floods.
