Project Summary

The project will address community needs in Al-Jabal Camp in Jarablsu, Aleppo, and Daret Azzeh SD in Aleppo. The needs emerging for women protection and empowerment interventions
This project is designed on community-based approach by involving beneficiaries in design phase, involving community in awareness campaigns, empower women to start up their small business to serve and address needs of their communities. The total direct beneficiaries of the project is 1400 women and girls.
A coordination is already established with Al-Jabl based on MoU already signed between POINT and Turkish and Syrian local authorities in Jarablus. Further coordination will be with NGOs, CBOs working in Al-Jabl camp; Coordination is also already established with local authorities Daret Azzeh.
The project is drawn according to our 5 years expertise working in camps, empowering women and youth, developing protection service providers and humanitarian workers quality and impact, and enhance women skills to manage women centers and lead activities in ٍSarmada camps. POINT have established 5 women empowerment centers based on community participation and volunteering approach. The centers were led by women.
The project aims at:
1. Providing protection services of PSS, GBV and referral pathways for women and women with disabilities. This is to support them restore their balance psychologically and mentally, and help GBV survivors overcome trauma impacts.
2. Equipping women and women with disability with vocational/life skills, and starting up small business training and mini-grants to implement those businesses. This is to restore and enhance their dignity by positioning their selves positively in community as active members, and decreasing their dependency on husbands or relief.
3. Participating in declining child labor by decreasing women reliance on their children as major or minor income source.
4. Raising community awareness of parenting skills principles by training women and women with disabilities on parenting skills principles.
All of that will be achieved by
a. Hire GBV PSS referral pathways specialists for the 2 women centers. and refer health and nuitriton cases to related sector service providers.
b. Conduct awareness sessions led by women and women with disabilities on PSS and GBV
c. Provide vocational/skills training for women and women with disabilities in the two centers
d. Provide how to start your small business training to women and women with disabilities based on a curriculum approved by International Labor Organization ILO
e. Award 100 women with mini-grants and follow up, coaching sessions to implement and sustain their established small businesses

Implementation of activities

The project activities will be implemented as the following in Al-Jabl Camp in Jarablus and in Daret Azzah: First component, “Providing GBV specialized services to including case management, PSS support and legal services”In this component, POINT through women empowerment centre will provide PSS, GBV, and referral pathways services to 500 women in Al-Jabl camp and Daret Azzah. To provide the services GBV case management workers will be hired, and referral will be made to other sectors of health and nutrition. This will ensure community and especially women mobilization to play significant role from the beginning of the project implementation. Second Component, “Ensure safe spaces and community centres for women and girls”In this component, POINT trained and GBV, PSS specialized staff, and the established women and girls centre will attract women and girls to receive services and exchange ideas. The centre management will work together with women and girls to develop the centre safety and services. Through the vocational training and mini-grants, success stories of women and girls will be shared with other women and girls in the targeted areas, once allowed by the story’s owners. Third component, “Provide livelihood opportunities to empower women and girls and strengthen their resilience”In this component, POINT will participate in providing inclusive strategies to empower women by creating skilled women capable to design their own businesses to women that faced discrimination, financial abuse and physical violence. POINT to this result will call women to participate in defining vocational/skills needed to restore their role and fight norms, target is 300 women. POINT will depend also on its expertise in empowering women economically to found livelihood opportunities and restore dignity. POINT will provide 100 mini-grants to 100 women Fourth component, “Provide activities empowering women and girls and GBV prevention activities”In this component, POINT will involve women by conducting 10 campaigns on GBV awareness. Those campaigns will be led by women to join and participate in centre activities and reach target 1000 women. The campaigns will draw women and girls’ attention and raise their awareness on the impact of early/forced marriage on children and communities. The campaigns will target the boys and men as well to eliminate GBV in their own lives and speak out against it with peers. That will also increase understanding of the impact of GBV on children, i.e., that those who witness GBV are likely to become more aggressive or violent themselves and the cycle is thus perpetuated. To increase family cohesion and reduce risks on children, POINT will conduct 10 parenting skills sessions to women. To ensure progress of implementing of the mentioned activities and reach targeted project objectives, POINT MEAL department will work on matching project log-frame indicators, monitor and evaluate project through:• Monthly progress reports• Number and quality of Cases and referrals reports• Number and quality of vocational/life skills training report for vocational training • Number and quality Campaigns and reached population report• Mini-grants success stories reports• Number and quality of services provided monthly report

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Protection risk: There is a risk that number of women engaged in the projects is less that targeted.Likelihood: Medium. Impact: HighMitigating measures: 1. Ensure our community outreach workers mobilize project communities and explained effects of planned activities. 2. increase coordination with camp management, local authorities, other CBOs, NGOs working in the same locations to call women to participate Protection risk: military clashes within Daret Azzah SD preventing the center from operating.Mitigation measures: 1. Suspend activities till peace is recovered. 2. coordinate with POINT programs to resume providing services in nearby safe areas once safe is taking long time to be restoredPOINT has experience in successfully delivering various kinds of project despite ongoing conflict. Additionally, we have designed security plans for the team and participants according to different scenarios. Our primary priority is the safety and security of the beneficiaries; therefore, we will conduct safety and security assessments prior to implementation and update the safety plans accordingly. And accessible accountability mechanism will be used in the project, known communication lines as well. Do no harm Standards will be applied at all stages of implementation and the project life cycle. In order to do that, POINT will conduct baseline assessments to ensure that none of the project activities will harm the community in any way. Additionally, POINT will conduct security risk assessments and safety and security reports in order to ensure the safety and security of project beneficiaries.
