Project Summary

Above average Deyr 2019 and Gu 2020 rains that impacted to very serious flooding in most parts of South Central Somalia coupled by locust invasion, intermittent conflict and insecurity as well as socio – economic impacts of Covid -19 containment measures has significantly impacted onto food insecurity status of the most vulnerable IDPs, returnees and marginalized host communities in Kismayo Lower Juba Region, Somalia. (FSNAU and FEWS NET report of May, 2020). Their plight is exacerbated by completely eroded purchasing powers coupled by very weak and negative coping strategies to meet their food and non-food needs. Of particular concern the fact the situation is likely to deteriorate further to unprecedented level that will weaken the already fragile food insecurity situation and worsen livelihoods from IPC 3 to IPC 4 owing to the fact that analysis of household food security survey data collected in the July/August 2020 post-Gu assessment revealed reduced food estimates through December 2020. Furthermore, anticipated La Niña conditions are associated with and high likelihood of consecutive below-average rainfall seasons, while there is projected high risk that drought conditions will develop by early 2021. The above plight calls for timely life-saving Food security intervention to enable the worst crisis men, women boys and girls in the project targeted meet their immediate food security needs through conditional and non conditional safety nets as well as earn income in form of cash for work to sustain their family food needs in order to mitigate already precarious food insecurity conditions and further restore and protect their livelihoods and strengthen their resilience with suitable food security support programmes to cope with the current and future shocks. The project targets 15500 persons (6448 women 5952 men, 1550 boys and 1550 girls)

Project Activities

1.Provision of food vouchers, 2500 HHs of most vulnerable and displaced women and children headed households and those with chronic illnesses and the disabled in Kismayo Lower Juba Region2.Rehabilitation of 30 water catchments, farming lands/rangelands, canals and 15 feeder roads on CFW scheme to benefit 2500 vulnerable host communities and IDPs/returnees in Kismayo Lower Juba Region, Somalia3.Provision of hay/fodder to the most vulnerable and drought, flooding, conflict and Covid-19 containment measures affected 1500 pastoral families in Kismayo Lower Juba Region, Somalia4. Distribution of farm inputs to the 4500 most vulnerable and displaced farming households in Lower Juba region, Somalia 5.Provision of tractor hours to 800 farmers in Kismayo Lower region, Somalia
