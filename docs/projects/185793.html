Project Summary

The aim of this project is to provide primary health care services to Rohingya refugees and surrounding host communities with especial concentration on the vulnerable groups including pregnant and post-natal women, newborns, the under-fives, including those with malnutrition and persons with disabilities. To achieve these objectives, the project will focus on delivering maternal, newborn & child health interventions, prevention and treatment of communicable and non-communicable diseases, mental health and psychosocial services and lastly emergency referral service. Since 2018, our health post in Camp 17, is providing primary health services 5 days/week. To address maternal and newborn health, we are providing ANC, PNC, Stabilization and referral of newborn and obstetric emergencies. We are also conducting routine immunization (0-2 years) and growth monitoring of children to ensure overall growth and development. In regard to communicable diseases, we have services like identification (especially TB, Malaria), isolation, HIV prevention and referral system. For non-communicable diseases like cardiovascular diseases, hypertension, high blood sugar, COPD and asthma, we are providing screening services and free medicines for management purposes through our outpatient service as per national protocol. Beside these, we have family planning services including counselling and oral pills. We have also started our laboratory services providing all the testing facilities except hemoglobin testing. Moreover, our infrastructure is gender friendly as we have separate latrines for males and females. We also have color-coded waste segregation system. We follow patient feedback mechanism and EWARS reporting & maternal and perinatal mortality surveillance system for information management and accountability purpose. To provide quality and equitable services, our health post currently has one medical doctor, one midwife, one medical assistant and security and support staffs. The project will be implemented in coordination with WHO, UNHCR, Health Sector, CIC, RRRC and other assigned local administrations.

1 - Identified Needs

Since our inception we have provided about 20000 medical consultations including 2589 ANC & PNC services. We have also provided counselling and awareness sessions to 3152 rohingya refugees adressing family planning, menstrual hygiene and nutrition & infants feeding and immunization. If we cease to provide our continuous services to these unfortunate rohingya refugees, the targeted community will be deprived of these essential services which will increase maternal and child mortality. So it has become imperative to continue the services that we are providing through our health post facility.

2 - Activities or outputs

Activity 1: Continue the operation of gender & disabled friendly health post with stable electricity supply, cold chain facility, access to water, emergency site, consultation room with confidentiality, and smooth entrance of medical personnel. Activity 2: Provide ANC, PNC, Stabilization & referral of newborn, IFA supplementation for pregnant women and adolescent, Td vaccination, IMNCI, routine immunization (0-2 years) and growth monitoring of newborn and child. Activity 3: Provide different methods of contraception, counselling and referral for family planning. Activity 4: Clinical management of intimate partner violence and rape survivors’ according to minimum package of essential health services. Activity 5: Free outpatient curative care and referral for communicable and non-communicable diseases. Activity 6: Psychological first aid and identification & referral of persons with mental health needs. Activity 7: Running the pharmacy in accordance with minimum essential health service package and provide medicines at free of cost. Activity 8: Pathological services including usage of RDTs (Malaria, HBsAg, RPR, urine pregnancy, HIV/AIDS testing), Urine dipsticks, Hemoglobin and Blood glucose tests. Activity 9: Health care waste management and IPC as per minimum essential health service package. Activity 10: Monitoring and Supervision. Activity 11: Sustain a community health and MHPSS program. Activity 12: Clinical diagnosis and management of malnutrition of children 6 to 59 months and adolescents according to nutritional status and treatment guidelines and referral to nutrition center. Activity 13: Information management and Accountability.
