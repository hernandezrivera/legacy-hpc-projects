Project Summary

This project is designed to address the problem of inadequate education services for refugees and host communities in Kassala State, where live about 108, 466 refugees in Khashm Ghirba (Kilo 26 and Girba camps), Wad El Helew (Shagarab I, II and III camps) and Kassala (Wad Sherifey camp). According to RRP 2020, the most vulnerable refugees with severity rate 4 in Khashm Ghirba and Wad El Helew localities (4 camps with 102,154 refugees). The educational environment where the resident's refugees and the new arrivals are cited is very poor and not attractive for the families to enrol their children and as well as for the teachers to communicate the students properly, 258 teachers distributed in the project targeted schools must be trained in by MoE. 17 Parents & Teachers Associations needs to be formed and building their capacity to enable them to increase the self-sufficiency of schools. The total number of schools in camps are 17 schools, it needs rehabilitation to ensure capacity to accommodate the refugees' children, additionally, essential retention initiatives are needed, such as; provision of school supplies, textbooks, uniforms, with some targeting of host community children to support peaceful coexistence. Vulnerable individuals including girls, their drop-out rate is higher than boys. Some of the reasons behind this are the early marriages, livelihood activities, domestic support at home, among others.
The main interventions are:
1/ Enhancing the school environment through rehabilitation.
2/Improving the capacities for teachers and PTAs.
3/ Provision of school essentials retention for the most vulnerable students.
4/ Awareness raising sessions/campaign in school and community level.
The target beneficiaries of this project are the refugee students living in camps, with an extension of support will be provided to the most vulnerable host communities student accessing education services alongside refugees.

1 - Needs

In refugees camps and the host communities in Kassala the common needs related to inadquate servisecs of eductaion as follow:1. Most of the existing permanent classrooms/ schools need rehabilitation and to be complemented with seating sets (bench/desk)2. The teaching, learning and recreational materials for student and teacher are not sufficient.3. The teachers and PTAs considered as the main governors of schools are not capacitated.4. School retention support for the poor and extremely poor families, to enhance the schools' enrollments. 5. the awareness on child rights, education importance for students and communities especially on girls education

2 - Activities / Outputs

This project is designed to be implemented in close participation and collaboration of the project stakeholders (Beneficiaries, PTAs, Teachers, MoE and CoR & HAC). The bellow activities will be delivered:1. Rehabilitate the existing permanent classrooms and complement the seating sets (bench/desk) in the targeted 17 schools.2. Provide the teaching, learning and recreational materials for student and teacher in 17 schools.3. Formulate and train 17 PTAs members in 17 schools.4. Teachers training for 258 teachers.5. Deliver school retention support for the poor and extremely poor students, about 8604 students.6. Conduct 68 awareness sessions/campaign for students and community members with special emphasis on girls education (4 sessions per school in the camp/HC per a year). The project outputs:1. Increased enrolment rate in basic schools 2. Improved the educational environment
