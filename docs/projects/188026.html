Project Summary

Through this project, UOSSM aims to provide life-saving integrated nutrition services by supporting 10 Rapid response teams (RRTs) in Idleb (Dana, Ariha) sub-district and Aleppo (Atareb, Jandirs, Suran) sub-district, the targeted areas include a total of 152973 population (79288IDPs, 73685HC) RRTs will target PIN Children under five (CU5) and caregivers including pregnant and lactating women (PLWs) and people with disabilities. The proposed activities will be for12 months during 2022 and 12 months during 2023 including one-month preparation. The 10 RRTs are distributed between the targeted subdistricts as follows: (2RRTs in Dana,2 RRTs in Ariha, 2 RRTs Atareb, 2RRTs Suran,2RRTs Jandirs) those RRTs will be linked to UOSSM’s health facilities in Dana and Ariha, and partner’s health facilities in other subdistricts,
According to the nutrition cluster % of CU5 is 25% and PLWs is 8% so direct beneficiaries will be 33% of 152973 so a total number of beneficiaries 50482of them 38243 children under five (male, female) and 12238 PLWs during 2022 and we expected to deliver services to a same number of beneficiaries during 2023 including new beneficiaries and following up beneficiaries. each team will visit each beneficiary multiple times based on the need within the project period. RRTs’ main activities will include: • Strengthening early case finding, treatment for MAM and SAM cases without complications in case supplies is available from UNICEF. If the supplies are unavailable, SAM and MAM cases will be referred to UOSSM’s health facility in the same community. • Providing screening, treatment, and follow-up, and referring the complicated malnutrition cases to the nearest hospitals to ensure the completion of the CMAM program cycle. • Referring beneficiaries with dangerous signs to the health facilities for treatment. • Providing emergency IYCF counseling for mothers with moderately malnourished children, and micronutrient distribution for preventing anemia and malnutrition among children and PLWs. • RRTs will support integrated nutrition services with hygiene promotion. • Screening and referring CU5 and PLWs with signs of abuse/violence to GBV case management and women and girls’ safe space (WGSS) centers to receive services according to their needs. According to UNICEF and Nutrition cluster, protocols, to enable RRTs to deliver integrated nutrition services with high quality UOSSM aims
to conduct IYCF & CMAM training during the first quarter. conduct GBV training for all members of RRTs staff. On the other hand, UOSSM’s M&E team developed the monitoring plan where the M&E team monitors implementation of project activities in the field through field visits, conducting beneficiaries satisfaction, controlling services quality, following up the progress of activities, and achieving indicators monthly and quarterly. Nutrition programmatic adjustments will be implemented in the context of COVID-19 to mitigate the risk of infection while ensuring continuity of life-saving nutrition services. Furthermore, RRTs teams will be provided with PPE during delivering services.
