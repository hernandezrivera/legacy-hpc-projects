Project Summary

As a result of insurgency in the Northeast Nigeria, So-many House-hold have food consumption gaps, therefore, the project target those that are marginally able to meet their minimum food need but only by depleting essential livelihood assets or through crises coping strategy.

Plan fields

1 - Needs

The project aims to reach the food needs of 1005 HHs in three LGAs of Adamawa (Michika and Madagali) and Borno (Gwoza).

2 - Activities

Due to insurgency that ravage the communities of Northeast Nigeria, this left thousandsof HH with minimal inadequate food for consumption score. The project target 1005 HHs 70% women and 30% men in three LGAs of Adamawa and Borno State. The food assistant modality will be through E-voucher while the technology that will beemploy will be Red-Rose platform
