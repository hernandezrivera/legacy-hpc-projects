Project Summary

SAWAB (Social Agency for Welfare Advancement in Bangladesh) is planning to initiate livelihood and development program in FDMN camps no. 7 and 18 located in Ukhiya Upazila. We will set up two training centers each having 160 women on sewing and handicraft. There will be 4 batches in a year consisting of 40 members in each batch and of three months duration. Training will be conducted five days in week and two sessions in a day while each session will be conducted for three hours with 20 participants in the morning and 20 participants in the afternoon. Each trainee will get altogether 180 hours training in three months. The project will span from January 01 to December 31, 2023. The two major parts are training and creating employment opportunity. Under training part two components are worthy to mention e.g., sewing and handicraft. About activities of the project, we should set up two centers at a convenient location of the camp and after that we will select the beneficiaries based on need and vulnerability having discussion with CiC, SMSD, Sector Focal Point and Camp Majhi. About intended results, at least 320 women will receive training on sewing and handicraft on each. After having completed training they will be provided with sewing machine and handicraft materials based on their respective training and will be followed up until they become able to create their employment opportunity. Staff will be trained properly both on the job as well in class room. The project also emphasize on social cohesion between host community and FDMNs by by adapting and sharing learning.

1. Needs
1 - Identified Needs

It is known to all that FDMN people are poor and vulnerable. So livelihood and development is a must to do for them. Because, mere relief and rehabilitation won't help them much. Moreover, Rohingya women are more vulnerable than men. They are victim of domestic violence, polygamy, child marriage and patriarchy. In addition, women are often deserted by their husband leaving with her children. So they need economic empowerment. To ensure this economic emancipation of women livelihood and skills development program is a much needed. So to choose trainees we will give first priority to the widows as many women lost their husbands while they became displaced from Rakhain state of Myanmar. Secondly those women who were abandoned by their husband or divorced. Finally, those who are vulnerable though they may have husband. More importantly, these selection process will be conducted through the Majhis of each block. It is expected, that after having training they will be able to work for their community people in the camp initially and finally when they will be repatriated to their own land Myanmar.

3.3 - Age including child safeguarding
