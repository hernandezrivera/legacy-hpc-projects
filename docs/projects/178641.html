Project Summary

The project aims to invest in available local resources, and focus on dealing with the major challenges facing the olive sector in the target communities, specifically in relation to costs at all stages of production. In addition, the project aims to overcome the gaps of knowledge about the best agricultural practices for the olive sector through Integrated Olive Management. In addition, supporting the local community including the olive farmers to access production assets in Afrin district in 12 villages
Through the following steps:
- increase the food security through supporting the farmers.
- Distributing agricultural input vouchers in addition to fertilizer as following:
(100 kg compound NPK fertilizer (high phosphorus),50 kg of Urea 46 % fertilizer , 1.5 liters pesticides Dimethoate ,3 envelopes (800 gr) fungicide, 3 liters of Foliar organic fertilizer, 1 Scissor, and 1 Hand Saw
- Distributing vouchers to cover the cost of oil extraction and harvesting as following:
Distribution of olive harvesting kit to enable the olive farmers and women involved in CFW activities to start olive harvesting at the optimal date for olive harvesting season till extraction in Olive presses.
•Olive harvesting support: Each beneficiary is to receive 3 Linoleum sheets (Dimensions 6*4, total area 24 m2 for gathering of olive fruits 15 pieces of harvesting plastic tools, 15 Burlap sacks, ( capacity 60 kg) and 12 tins (capacity of 16 liters) for oil packaging, and olive harvesting for 3 dunum to be conducted by CFW . (The expected production of 3 dunum olive orchard is about 800 KG of olive fruits (which equal to 200 liters of olive oil as it is expected that olive fresh fruits will give about 25 % olive oil)
- Capacity building for farmers.
Deliver theoretical training program:
Stage 1. Harvesting, the best techniques for obtaining high-quality olive oil, packaging, and storage of olive oil
Stage 2. Tillage, pruning, fertilization, pest control, and supplementary irrigation techniques
Stage 3. Integrated Pest Management (IPM),
- Contracting and provision of spring tillage.
- supervision on the farms of olive farmers
- Contracting with 200 seasonal workers - cash for work.
- Mapping the presses that need rehabilitation - 2 presses.
- Supporting the marketing of olive products as following:
(Marketing trainings: for all farmers (Men and Women who will benefit from the services BRD provided for farmers)
- Management training
- Marketing from harvesting to selling
- Modern Marketing
- Social media for marketing
- How to sell your product).
- Conducting awareness sessions for the important of saving the olive, harvesting and COVID-19 measures.
Total number of HHs will benefit from olive value chain services provided by BRD are 600 HHs.
Total number of individual will benefit from olive value chain services and other services (training - CfW ..etc) provided by BRD are 4500 HHs.

4 - Needs

6.7 million Syrians are food insecure and an additional 2.7 million people are at risk of food insecurity, loss and lack of livelihoods and reduced production capacity which in turn results in limited physical and financial access to food (HNO, 2020) The population is 28000 including 33% IDPs. Currently, the targeted location is under Turkish government control and remains stable and with acceptable levels of security allowing for high numbers of IDPs to find refuge 95% of the population in the targeted location was dependent on agricultural activities (especially olives). According to needs assessment analysis, the main problems are as following: •The high cost of compost, pesticides, pruning, and milling services, leading to an increase in production costs. •The high cost of fuel for supplementary irrigation, that led to the olive farmers depending on only rain to water the trees as a negative adaptation •The spread of pests (insect diseases, and fungi diseases) .. •Marketing difficulties. The needs assessment also showed that the target areas are villages lacking agricultural support, in addition, these areas are difficult to access for most organizations, due to the need to request formal approval from the Turkish government. need assessment results showed that people mainly depend on olive production as a source of income. In addition, there is the availability of irrigation sources The high prices of agricultural inputs (fertilizers, fuel, pesticides, have led to the reluctance of farmers to take care of their olive trees, which has led to the spread of many agricultural diseases. FGDs revealed women within Afrin district are shouldering much of the economic burden and striving to ensure livelihoods for themselves

5 - Implementation of activities

the Project is designed to increase the food security and enhance the livelihoods of IDPs and HCs -Distributing agricultural inputs and fertilizer (2 types of pesticide vouchers (insecticide, and fungicide), accompanied by related training. - Distributing vouchers to cover the cost of oil extraction. - Capacity building targeting male and female farmers and local community members. - supervision on the farms of olive farmers during the production cycle of olives and provide technical advice and periodic follow-up necessary for them. -Contracting with 200 - 200 seasonal workers (200women - 200 men) of IDPs to harvest 1,800 dunums of olives for 600 HHs, accompanied by related training. Mapping the presses that need rehabilitation to contract two of them regarding the selection criteria. BRD will cover the cost of rehabilitation of 2 presses. After rehabilitation of these presses, BRD will recoup the costs of presses rehabilitation from the total costs of vouchers of oil extract (BRD will also contract again presses owners to extract the oil of olive farmers beneficiaries) - Supporting the marketing of olive products by providing training about the marketing of olive products. In addition to establishing an exhibition to support the olive farmers for the marketing - Conducting awareness sessions with the vendors and beneficiaries to explain the vouchers' redeeming mechanisms BRD will identify, verify from the beneficiaries through community leaders, and local councils where a long list will be prepared depending on eligibility criteria, then a shortlist will be identified depending on the vulnerability criteria for olive farmers and, seasonal workers (cash for work) beneficiaries
