Project Summary

In order to address the identified needs, NW plans an intervention aiming at providing assistance to medical institutions to improve capacity and sustainability of healthcare services providers at different levels.
It is planning to implement the next activities:
• roofs, walls, windows, doors repair and internal plastering;
• thermal modernization and building insulation;
• creating safe access to adequate water, through newly water sources creation, internal and external water supply systems rehabilitation;
• creating new or rehabilitating existing sewage systems in medical institutions such as ambulatories;
• rehabilitation of toilets in medical institutions (ambulatories and hospitals);
• procurement and distribution of equipment to health facilities.

1 - Needs

While eastern Ukraine remains the epicenter of critical humanitarian needs due to the armed conflict, 2020 has brought yet another threat to the region. The rapid spread of COVID-19 around the country has reached two oblasts in eastern Ukraine, and the number of cases is climbing daily. By estimates of the Ukraine COVID-19 Humanitarian Response Plan, some 3.4 million people in eastern Ukraine will need humanitarian assistance during 2021. This crisis has significantly disrupted two critical services in the region: healthcare and water supply. 29% of the healthcare facilities reported problems with the facilities main source of water for cleaning. Healthcare infrastructure is significantly disrupted through damage or disrepair due to lack of maintenance, especially in rural areas. According to HNO 2020, some 38 per cent of the households within 20 km of the LoC and 57 per cent within 5km of the LoC (GCA) reported problems with access to healthcare and require life-saving and essential health services. A general limited access to medical service (primary, secondary and tertiary level) due to disrupted healthcare system, incl. poor infrastructure, lack of essential equipment, understaffing, COVID-19 restrictions, has concerning impact on vulnerable population already facing a range of barriers due to poor economic condition worsened by the conflict.
