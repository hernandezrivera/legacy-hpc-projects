Project Summary

The recent Sudan confrontation betweem the Sudan armed forces (SAF /RSF) occrred in April 15th 2023 and still on led to severe protection issues including killing of civilians, huge displacement from the capital cities including Nyala in South Darfur to safe localities: Edel Fursan, Kass, Tulus and Elsalam, rape of women and girls, increased street children, women violation and hard labor situation to teen ages.
The project will assess and conduct awareness on GBV, Child protection and women empowerment, as well as provide specialized PSS services for communities through referral mechanisms, establishment of mental health groups and capacity building of communities and service providers. The project also aims to increase awareness amongst communities of protection issues and its effects on various groups.

1 - Needs

In April 15th, 2023, a widespread political clash explodes between two the Government armed sides Sudan Armed Forces and Rapid Support Forces in Khartoum and other States of Sudan including Nyala, the Capital of South Darfur State. The project is designed to include the principles of vulnerable lifesaving approaches with regard to provision of protection assistance through transparency, with no discrimination and equality and all category of people in need. Access to target population in camps and rural areas is commonly planed by the members of the Sector using the agreed assessment standard in the Core Standards. Assessment reports are shared to all members of the groups. Identification of beneficiaries and targeted population is decided by the sector group. Beneficiaries and locations: Comprehensive reports of the recent humanitarian actors' joint rapid assessment mission conducted by UN and NGOs in last August and October 2022 reported people in need (PiN) of 153,065 IDPs spread in Nyala Janoub 45,000 (HH 9,000), Kass 36,000 (HH 7,200) and Nyala Shimal 37,000 (HH 7,400), Edel Fursan 10,500 (HH 2,100), Bileil 13,000 (HH 2,600), Mersheng 4,515 (HH 903) Elsalam locality 4,050 (HH 810), lacking the minimum standard of protection services due to continuous fighting, relocation of humanitarian actors from all Darfur that reduces the means of life for vulnerable IDPs, No access to banks transfer and then shortage of money at hand and also displacement situation. This project is designed to immediately respond to the protection needs. Access to the areas where IDPs are located when the two sides agree on ceasefire. GFO will used its communal relationship to implement this project. The project will contribute to reducing the impact including morbidity and mortality of conflict and disasters on vulnerable boys, girls, women and men in Nyala city and surrounding localities through timely and effective assessments, appropriate responses and enhanced coordination among humanitarian actors with regards to protection needs. It is anticipated that 2023 will witness continued high levels of humanitarian needs, increased protection risks.

2 - Activities/Outputs

This intervention seeks to address some of the most urgent protection needs of the most vulnerable individuals and families selected beneficiaries through a community-based approach including direct lifesaving assistance and psychosocial support. The action will contribute to support beneficiaries to attain safety and dignity by improving their protective environment taking into account both external threats to the target population as well as the coping strategies adopted to counteract the vulnerabilities will use its already existing programs to approach and strengthen coordination to leverage referral pathways to ensure the needs of the survivors are addressed and considered with respect and confidentiality. This approach includes prevention through awareness raising and community-based education and information campaigns, capacity development for partners, monitoring and reporting, and provision of services for the survivors by, conduct protection assessments for communities, targeting Persons with Specific Needs (PSNs), CBCPMs are strengthened and incorporated to CPCs CAPs through the development of risk reduction action and monitoring plans by CP focal points has a strong partnership and linkages with local communities and authorities that facilitate access and operations in the targeted areas. The project paves the way for resilience through supporting the capacity of the community structures and local authorities in response to child protection, general social needs and GBV concerns.The community will be closely involved in the project activities. The local authorities, including the State Ministry of Social Welfare, State Ministry of Education, and the National CCW will be also consulted and engaged in the project design and implementation. Smooth exit strategy will be elaborated to ensure the sustainability of the activities. The project has thoroughly investigated and focused on the “DO NO Harm” principle during the consultations with the community to avoid any harm that might be brought about by the intervention. In addition, and during the selection of beneficiaries, the project will ensure participation of all groups, including women, children, people with disabilities, minorities, and others. The project will use the vulnerability criteria applied by the protection sector, providing child protection services to contribute towards GBV elimination for refugees, IDPs, returnees and vulnerable host communities.
