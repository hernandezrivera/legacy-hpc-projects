Project Summary

Islamic Relief is planning to introduce inclusive basic formal and informal education in three Localities (El Sunut, Kailak and Lagawa) in WK, targeting 12,283 emergency, conflict and disaster affected children and adolescents in thirty two communities, through improving their access to quality education. The project is planning to target 32 schools, (Ten in Lagawa, ten in Kailak and twelve in El Sunut) by delivering education activities to contribute in increasing access to inclusive quality basic education.
The proposed intervention is contributing to Education sector objectives and strategy that aims to ensure school age children affected by emergencies (including conflict, natural hazards), particularly children with disabilities, continue to get access to safe and quality formal and non-formal education. This project will implement series of education activities to achieve those objectives guided by three sector objectives.
Under objective (1) Provide conflict and disaster affected children and adolescents with inclusive, basic formal and informal education:
The project will contribute in increasing the learning space in target Localities through construction of 9 new classrooms and rehabilitation of nine ruined/damaged classrooms or semi-permanent classrooms in nine schools, identified and divided equally between the three target Localities. Three hundred sets of student seating and six sets of teacher’s furniture will be provided to furnish the target schools. Six new gender sensitive latrines will be constructed in most needy schools, connect with water sources from nearest water points and equipped with hand washing facilities. 2,560 pieces of soap will be distributed to each target school on quarterly basis to encourage students practice personal hygiene promotion during using toilets and before/after eating.
Under objective (2) Improve the quality of education to ensure continuity of relevant education services for children and adolescents in emergencies:
IRW planning to avail and distribute three hundred sets of teaching and learning materials to targeted schools which include text books, school bags, pencils and erasers. 32 target schools will be supported with school grants through PTA associations and child rights clubs to cover school running costs and activities contributing to child protection & GBV prevention. The students will be supported with 32 sets of recreational kits to encourage student’s participation in schools activities. The recreation materials consist of football, volley ball nets, sport uniform and shoes. The students will be mobilized and encouraged to organize sport completion games between schools to promote competition spirit and leadership. The project will organize and conduct six training workshops to train 120 teachers and parents to enhance and increase their knowledge for quality education.
Under objective (3) Ensure schools and learning environments are protective and responsive to the needs of conflict affected children and adolescents:
The project will form child rights clubs, train them on life skills activities, hygiene promotion and support with cleaning tools and garbage disposal facilities to contribute in improving schools environment and to strengthen the student’s social skills that lead to promoting their meaningful participation in enjoying their rights. Sixty school teachers and parents will be trained on protection, human rights, sexual and gender-based violence and non-discrimination approach during accessing humanitarian aid. 32 awareness raising campaigns will be conducted for students and teachers including target communities on school violence, attacks on education and temporary school closure to avoid schooling interruptions and ensure continuity of learning during school year. 32 awareness sessions and campaigns on girl’s education will be organized to mobilize communities in target Localities to increase girl’s enrollment in schools.

1 - Needs

West Kordofan State is one of the vulnerable States of Sudan in regard to availability of basic services and infrastructure. The State is bordering South Sudan country from South and bordering South Kordofan from the east and Darfur from the west directions. Total population of the State is 1,945,520 individuals. During the past decade, the State was affected by the war and conflict that rose in South Kordofan resulting in huge displacement of war affected populations especially towards adjacent Localities of Kailak, Lagawa and El Sunut, which are the target Localities through this intervention. The tribal conflict in Darfur during the years 2017 -2018 has also resulted in displacement of many populations to WK.The effect of displacement was revealed to adding pressure to existing vulnerable communities and affect basic services such as health, WASH and education facilities. The effect was observed in education facilities in particular which cannot accommodate new students due to limited space and resources.A significant number of displaced children and resident children age 7 to 15 years and youth both male and female, remain out of school due to economic factors and due to non-availability of learning space. The existing schools environment is not attracting out of school children for enrollment as well as the local culture of communities regarding education, especially girl’s education. The schools in target locations are overcrowding which lead to hampers provision of quality education and appropriate learning environment. Inadequate sanitation facilities, lack of safe play spaces, shortage of teachers and learning materials, all are adding negative factor. In regard to schools structures, the classrooms are below the recommended standards. Due to these factors, there is a high risk that young boys and girls are not returning to formal education, which lead to increasing their vulnerability to protection risks as well.
