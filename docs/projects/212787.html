Project Summary

This project is designed to provide integrated health, nutrition, food security, and hygiene promotion services in NWS in Idlib and Aleppo for 2024.
The proposed project will reach 175,000 people (75.000 PLWS out of them 2000 PWD) and 100,000 CU5 (52,000 G, 48,000 B) out of them 5000 CWD) through supporting (5 OTPs) health facilities, 6 Nutrition centers, and 23 nutrition outreach teams each team is linked to one health facility providing preventive and curative nutrition servises in addition to establishing 20 mother-baby areas. The nutrition centers and outreach team will provide IYCF and CMAM services through different modalities and will conduct the following center-based/outreach activities:
IYCF; Breastfeeding and lactation counseling, complementary feeding counseling will be provided for mothers and caregivers of children under two years.
CMAM; Screening and treating malnutrition cases (SAM and MAM) for PLWs and children under 5 that do not show signs of any medical complications, those with medical conditions are referred for support to the appropriate field partners, also distribute micronutrients for PLWS and children to address the micronutrient deficiencies among the PLWs and children. Distribute fortified blended foods to prevent stunting among the children in the targeted areas.
Mercy-USA will support Two stabilization centers to provide treatment for children with severe acute malnutrition with medical complications that would be referred from our outreach, HFs, and other nutrition partners.
Moreover,20 mother-feeding spaces will be established in 20 different camps in Maaret Tamsrin and Harim where the lowest proportion of Exclusive Breastfeeding is found that provide a safe space for lactating mothers to breastfeed their children. Each space will have 1 nutritionist and 2 CHWs who will be responsible for providing IYCF sessions and MUAC screening.
Also, 5,000 PLW will be provided with a $55 soft-conditional CVA for 8 months; this intervention will be linked to food security, health, and nutrition services to increase access to nutritious food, PNC, ANC, SAM treatment, and micronutrient tablets. Changing behavior activities will be conducted alongside cash distribution, one-to-one counseling, mother-to-mother support groups, and MIYCN messages.
Mercy-USA will coordinate with food security partners to advocate for supporting HH and transitioning the targeted PLW to fall under food security programs. Once they are included in these programs, $35 assistance will be provided according to the CVA operational guidelines. This support will depend on the availability of food security actors and available funds.
Additionally, all children under the age of 2 in the targeted HH that are not covered by the BSFP will be eligible for CVA as follows:
HH with one child under 2 will receive monthly assistance of $15.
HH with two children under 2 will receive monthly assistance of $20.
HH with three or more children under 2 will receive monthly assistance of $30.
This project will empower mothers and HH members through training to screen their children for malnutrition by teaching them how to use color-coded Mid-Upper Arm Circumference (MUAC) tapes and check for Odema.
Nutrition supplies:
Mercy-USA will undertake nutrition supply distribution in NWS for the cluster partners; this activity will be coordinated closely with the UNICEF and cluster team.

M&E will include surveys, focus group discussions, and routine data collection through staff reporting.
