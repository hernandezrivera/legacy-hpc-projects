Project Summary

The proposed project aims to address the significant needs of children and their caregivers/parents through deploying five Protection Mobile Teams consisting of 10 CP workers. These teams will provide specialized CP services that adhere to CP Area of Responsibility guidelines and protocols, including case management. This includes initial/intake assessments for cases involving boys and girls with protection concerns, such as early marriage, sexual violence, child labour, and domestic violence. The CP case management will also include an Emergency Case Fund, which will cater to the most vulnerable children and their caregivers/parents, providing items such as hygiene kits, dignity kits, food kits, shelter (renting houses or purchasing tents), mattresses, assistive devices (medical eyeglasses, hearing aids, crutches, wheelchairs), medications, and other necessary assistance based on individual assessments. The project will also offer PFA for children and caregivers/parents, implementing evidence-based programs like I DEAL, My Joyful Friends, and I and My Feelings as part of PSS activities. We will introduce a Parenting Skills Program to help build strong relationships between children and their caregivers/parents. The mobile teams will conduct group awareness-raising sessions on child protection concerns, children's rights, the importance of alternative care, and child protection risks pr in the targeted communities mentioned. Our protection teams will receive training in CP case management and minimum standards in child protection before launching these activities.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Specialized child protection interventions including CP case management in line with the CP AoR SOPS and guidelines will be provided for boys and girls, by CP case workers, with CP concerns such as unaccompanied or separated, neglected, sexually/ /physically/emotionally abused, who live or work on the streets or who participates in dangerous work, experiencing emotional distress, mental health problems, involved with armed groups or armed forces, been detained or recently released, with follow-up referrals to the other needed services. The CP case management will also include the provision of in-kind Emergency case Funds to the most affected and at heightened protection risks individuals and families, based on the NWS Cash and Voucher Assistance for Child Protection SoPs and Case Management SoPs. The interventions will also include Psychological First Aid (PFA) to help children feel safe, connected to others, calm and hopeful, and have access to social, physical, and emotional support. In addition, the interventions will include non-structured PSS activities for children, based on a tailored “I deal” program which is a play-based program to help children deal with complicated feelings and stress and interact with each other. The CP facilitators will also provide recreational activities for children which will include painting & drawing, and sensory-motor activities which help children build communication skills and team building, and enhance peer relationships and interpersonal skills. Because parenting skills can be acquired and passed on from one generation to another, “Parenting Skills” Program, will be implemented with parents/caregivers to help build strong relationships between children and their parents/caregivers to enable them to resolve problems together. The CP outreach teams will also conduct awareness raising sessions for the targeted community members on child protection concerns including child’s rights and child protection risk prevention.
