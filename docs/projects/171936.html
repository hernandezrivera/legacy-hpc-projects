Project Summary

Save the Children will continue to support the continuation of the education of boys and girls who are affected by food insecurity, heavy rains with flooding and the COVID-19 pandemic or possible second wave through creating safe and inclusive learning environments, improving the quality of the teaching and learning processes, school feeding and providing alternative learning opportunities that complement and enhance the formal education system. Save the Children will refurbish old classrooms, especially those affected by Cyclone Idai, the storms in October 2020 and any other storms or Tropical Cyclones that may emerge this seasons due to Climate Change. This will ensure that boys and girls have a safe environment for learning. To ensure access to safe water and prevention of waterborne diseases and COVID-19, measures like provision of hand washing stations will be installed in schools, hygiene supplies will be provided among other measures Accelerated learning and catch up programs will be introduced to support children who have lost school due to the long Covid 19 lock down and teachers’ strike. Our interventions will be hinged on the efforts to help all boys and girls who may face economic or social challenges in their attempt to return back to school. Save the Children strengthen childsafeguarding and also ensure that it is safe for all children to return back to school and enjoy their right to quality and inclusive education.
Save the Children will also continue to provide coordination and technical support to the Education Cluster especially capacity building of MoPSE and other education partners on Education in Emergencies (EiE).

8 - Accountability to Affected People
8.1 - Describe how your project was designed in consultation with communities? How will you incorporate communities in project implementation and what feedback mechanisms do you have in place?

Accountability to beneficiaries will be supported through gender-responsive, community-preferred, inclusive and child-friendly feedback mechanisms (including specific attention around the development of mechanisms for child participation) which will be established at the beginning of every action and maintained throughout, and will be located across target locations, while taking into consideration the risks and mitigation measures engendered by humanitarian risks. SC’s accountability mechanism and guidelines will be applied throughout project implementation in accordance with the CHS (Core Humanitarian Standard). The project will be accountable to children, communities and partners to strengthen partnership and to enhance transparency and working together. Project specific accountability mechanism will be established such as information sharing mechanism, establishing context based accountability mechanism/beneficiaries complaint and feedback collection and participation of children/adults/vulnerable groups. Beneficiaries complaint and feedback will be collected through one-to-one discussion keeping two meters physical distancing, using hotline, small group discussion with less than five participants on monthly basis. The collected data will be analysed using CFRM database and will be shared to all concerned for performance improvement and managerial decision making. Additional information is included in a Annex – Accountability to the Affected Population.
