Project Summary

Qansaxdheere is served by at least 3 small scale community managed borehole water supply and Water catchments as sources of water.located in the different town sections. With The supply fluctuate seasonally with the amount of rain. The availability of water is limited by its relatively high cost at around US$ 5 per 200 liter barrel, due to the relatively high cost of water system operation and maintenance. Seasonal outbreaks of diarrheal diseases are therefore linked to both the quantity of water, which for the average person is below the recommended 15 liters per day, and the quality of water, which ranges from moderately to highly risky throughout all the four seasons especially during the wet season when most household water directly from water pan.
Most institution and public facilities generally have neither WASH structures nor permanent water connections. Schools and health and nutrition facilities provide important social services that are made less effective by the lack of complementary WASH services. The majority of education, health and nutrition services are provided with the support of humanitarian assistance, but budgets for their establishment do not often include the provision of WASH services, which are left to be contributed by the catchment communities. However, these communities, which fall into the very low income category, cannot afford the initial capital investments required for establishing the required WASH services.
Open defecation is a common practice in the proposed locations with various reasons. Outlet of sanitation product is very limited, and the prevalence of latrine usage is currently less than 4 in 10 households. Hand washing practice at critical times with soap/ash and water are insignificant. Moreover, lack of awareness exists amongst the communities regarding the links between poor sanitation/hygiene and WASH related diseases. Furthermore, hygiene practices are also quite limited at institutions (School and Health centers) due to lack of functional WASH facilities.
Gaps in hygiene and sanitation are primary drivers of the increased incidence of diarrheal diseases, which often turn into outbreaks during dry spells and flooding times. Poor health, in turn, contributes to increased malnutrition among the most vulnerable households within the beneficiary communities. Currently, malnutrition is manifesting itself in Qansahdheere town and its surroundings, with the latest trends indicating global acute malnutrition (GAM) levels nearing 13%. The increased rates are partially attributed to poor access to basic services in water and sanitation compounded by poor hygiene awareness and habits.
The project will increase access to sustainable safe water to reduce the risk of AWD/cholera through the establishment of 4 communal water points and 1 school supplying water through piping system. ADRO will construct 30 new latrines and 2 gender sensitive twin school latrines in Qansaxdheere town. Comprehensive hygiene promotion awareness campaigns including distribution of 1500 HP kits will be distributed and conducted by 20 trained CHPs to reach communities in Qansaxdheere district.

Project Activities

Establishment of 4 communal water points and 1 in school using existing boreholes through piping system. Women and girls will be consulted the location and design of the water points to preserve their safety and avoid GBV during the water fetching as both women and girls fell the responsibility of water collecting to the households.Chlorinate the 3 boreholes and conduct monitoring of the free chlorine residual testing (FRC) by providing the well operators training and equipping with appropriate amount of chlorine needed to disinfect the wells on daily basis.Chlorination of wells is vital to avoid AWD/cholera diseases outbreak in the town caused by unsafe water. To keep the sustainability of the chlorination of the wells, ADRO will stocks chlorine in store and will provide on weekly basis the required amount of chlorine/borehole/week, Secondly, the trained operators are from the community, who will be stationed in the wells and they will be trained to chlorinate the wells on daily basis. 9000 persons(approximately 1500 Households) will access safe water.In consultation with the community members, identify, select and conduct continuous capacity building of community institutions in operation and maintenance of developed water infrastructure throughout the project implementation period, which will ensure that there is a local technical skill to ensure sustainability of developed water facilities beyond the project. The training will be given: 15 WASH committees, each committee will compose 7 members (4men, 3women) will be trained on better water resource management, HHWT use, and maintenance of water facilities 3 days.6 well operators from the 3 boreholes will be trained on O&M; the training will also include the chlorination of the wells to keep the sustainability of the chlorination for 2 days.(3) As a cross cutting, 150 community members (75 men and 75 women) shared equally by men and women, will also be given one day training on disaster and risk reduction response (DRR) and conflict resolution mechanisms (water resources conflict mostly occurs among the communities due to its scarcity during the dry seasons).ADRO will select 30 poor household who are ready to contribute by digging abit latrine in thier homes while ADRO team will provide a slap after confirming that a latrin has digged, ADRO will construct the walls and the doors for the latrines after supplying the slab.ADRO will contruct 2 latrines for the main school in Qanssxdheere town which gender sentive in design.ADRO will distribute 1500 hygiene kits to 1500 poor household in qanssxdheere town and 4 farming sorrounding villages in qansax dheere district. Conduct hygien promotion compaigns.
