Project Summary

This project aims to help addressing the gaps in WASH services for the target communities in Kahda and Daynile districts; outskirt of Banadir region. The projects is proposed to support 12,300 individuals (2,050 HHs) of the targeted communities that lack the safe access to WASH services and are also among the most vulnerable individuals within their communities due to lacking the livelihoods required for the convenient living including IDPs and income- vulnerable individuals of the host communities.
The project will support people with disability who have no access to fetch water from remote places due to their vulnerability and lack of income. It also fills existing water gaps around the IDPs camps and host communities whose income sources cannot cover the cost of the needy water in their daily life activities.
The project activities and objectives will consistently be in line with cluster objectives SO1 and SO2.
The areas where the project is planned to be implemented are well known of hosting large numbers of IDPs that fled their homes over the past 10 years because of conflicts, drought and floods as well as large numbers of marginalized host community members. The majority of the IDPs within the targeted communities are those who recently evacuated their homes in Beletweine, Bulaburte, Jalalaqsi, Jawhar, Madahay, and Balad in Hirshabele State due to the recurring riverine floods for the last three years and those affected by both the flashes and floods in Lower Shabelle; South West State, where all of whom have shifted to Mogadishu- Banadir region seeking more secure and stable living conditions
The proposed project entails various interventions that aim to improve the dignified and safe access to WASH assistance for the targeted communities, and those are:
• Installation of 30 taps within/ around the targeted camps.
• Hygiene promotion.
• Construction of 200 ventilated improved pit (VIP) latrines.
• Establishment of 5 waste management sites.
• Distribution of WASH/ NFI kits and sanitation facilities.
• Provision of training and workshops for 30 individuals of the local community in regard to water- borne diseases and COVID-19.
The project is anticipated to help achieving dignified and safe access to drinkable water needed as part of the life basic needs. Also, it will remarkably result in reduction of the water-borne diseases and COVID-19 spread through improving the sanitation services for the targeted communities and providing them with adequate qualities of water safe for drinking, cooking, and maintaining personal hygiene. In the same contest, this project will help reducing the diarrhea–related morbidity and mortality rates among the targeted population through the provision of clean water that can help minimizing the exposure to water- borne diseases and managing the cases of dehydration resulting from the diarrhea itself. Furthermore, the proposed intervention will potentially result in a substantial reduction of the GBV incidents particularly among the girls and young women within the targeted communities as they will have improved access to more nearby sources of drinkable water and also will have the VIP latrines set in closer locations to their residences.
It’s worth mentioning that the project is in line with the 6th goal of the Sustainable Development Goals of the WHO/UNICEF Joint Monitoring Programme JMP (to ensure availability and sustainable management of water and sanitation for all). Additionally, Human Appeal is planning to implement this project following accordance with the global WASH SPHERE standards and the standards operating procedures applicable to the Somalian context.

Project Activities

Project site assessment:HA Field office will send the project team to assess the area in which the project is planned to be executed. The team will collaborate with the local authorities and camp leaders to assess the vulnerable individuals who are most in need with special focus of prioritization on women, children and people with disability.Meetings with local authorities and stakeholders:In order to appropriate project implementation HA field office will have several meetings and consultations with the local authorities over the course of the project implementation. Participation and collective responsibility are inevitable when it comes to such critical activities mass targeting disaster-affected vulnerable population including aged women and men and disabled people.MOU signing with local authorities:In order to preserve the sustainability throughout the project lifecycle, HA Field staff will sign memorandums of understanding MOU with the local authorities relevant to the project implementation. It’s imperative to mention that the local authorities and camp leaders are anticipated to facilitate finding a secure and sustained site for the project particularly given that the land disputes widely exist due to fragile of government institutions.Installation of 30 water taps:HA will install 30 water taps within the camps to improve the access to safe drinkable water for the targeted population, and eventually help improving the hygiene measures and reducing the risk of acquiring water-borne diseases. Additionally, the implementation of this activity will potentially help minimizing the risk of GBV against young women and girls as they will fetch the water from more closer points to their residences. HA will ensure the provision of equitable access to adequate quantity of water for all of the individuals within the targeted communities that are in need.Construction of 200 VIP latrines:To further improve the hygiene, reduce the risk of diseases that might be transmitted because of the exposure to inappropriately disposed human excreta, and provide more dignified measured of waste disposal, HA will construct 200 VIP latrines to be used by the targeted communities..Establishing of 5 waste management sites:The beneficiaries of the project will be supported with the establishment of 5 waste management sites to help them dispose their domestic wastes conveniently and effectively and help maintain the cleanness of the surrounding environment and reduce the risk of diseases outbreaks. Distribution of WASH NFI kits:The project intervention will entail distribution of WASH NFI kits the include kitchen utilities, hygiene and chlorination facilities, sanitation and other portable kits used to fetching and storing water. This activity is meant to help reducing water-borne and COVID-19 diseases spread.Training of 30 local trainees:During the project implementation, 30 local trainees comprising youth individuals of both sexes will have training in regard to sanitation and hygiene promotion as well as the awareness on the COVID-19 infection.Monitoring and evaluation:During the project lifecycle, the HA project team will conduct continuous monitoring and supervision to ensure that the project is on the right track against the adopted work plans. At the project completion stage, the HA field office will carry out post-distribution monitoring PDM and evaluation sessions to evaluate the project overall intervention and the appropriateness of implementing similar approaches in future.

Mitigation Measures

The measures Human Appeal will undertake to help mitigate the anticipated risks include:Human Appeal will utilize its strict feedback system to provide the beneficiaries with convenient feedback mechanisms they can use to report about the services provided including a hotline number that they can call directly any time to direct their complaint.Make tent to tent visit when registration and distribution takes place. Take collective responsibility and collaboration among the stakeholders to secure the stability of registration and distribution sites • Strong collaboration among the stakeholders including the organization, local authorities, camp leaders and gate keepers to secure and maintain the safety of the beneficiaries from threats of GBV and other dangers they might encountered.Human Appeal was keen to design the proposed intervention in a way that ensures that the individuals of such groups will have equitable access to the provided WASH assistance, and that includes: • The provision of access to drinkable water and distribution of the WASH/ NFI kits for all individuals in need and the conduction of hygiene promotion sessions for the individuals within the targeted communities regardless of their gender grouping. • The provision of VIP latrines that are compatible with people with disability, • The inclusion of local individuals of both sexes in the training sessions and workshops. Mitigation measures • Coordination with the government to facilitate the provision of the land needed for the implementation of the project activities. • Official agreement/s and contract/s with landlord/s by the local authorities
