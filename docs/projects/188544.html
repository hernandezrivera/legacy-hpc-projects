Project Summary

Hope Revival Organization (HRO) aims to continue providing GBV services in the WGSSs established in Idleb (Kherbet Eljoz & Foz-Zuf) in our already existed community centers in Idleb (Jisr Ash Shugur, Darkosh, Ariha, Mhambal, & Kafr Jales) & in Aleppo (Afrin), & through outreach visits.
The services include GBV prevention activities such as awareness-raising sessions, empowerment activities, training, & campaigns, besides emergency response activities in response to the new IDPs through providing PFA, referral, sharing information, etc. based on GBV toolkit including early/forced marriage and sexual abuse, reproductive health and information session to all age groups and genders, especially the most vulnerable groups in all above-mentioned areas and through outreach visits to the widows’ camps (2 in Janudiyeh and 2 in Darkosh) and other camps to target the beneficiaries with services such as awareness raising and GBV full component activities (empowerment, prevention, response & mitigation activities), where some of them will receive case fund, Micro Grant, vocational training (in the WGSSs or in widows’ camps). Also, there will be a capacity building activity for non GBV actors (humanitarian actors and WLOs, including camp managers, volunteer teams, women’s committees, teachers, vaccination and nutrition teams, medical staff, food distribution and sanitation staff) on GBV basics and risk mitigation trainings, to reduce the GBV against women, in addition to establishing women committees in these camps and building their capacities, and to ensure that a greater number of people have access to the information related to GBV services in their communities.
HRO, will target WLOs with capacity building trainings on two levels technically and administratively to ensure their continuity. As a response to the GBV cases, we will provide CVA through case management, IPA and case fund within our projects in accordance with the vulnerability criteria, in addition to signing MoUs with other organizations to refer survivors/at risk cases to them and ensure that GBV risks are mitigated. The planned number of centers is (6) where women and girls can get involved in the activities and interact with their peers and to gain access to lifesaving GBV services, including case management, referrals, PSS for survivors focusing on vulnerable groups such as divorced, widows, PwD and elderly women (over 50) by ensuring their accessibility and inclusion through providing transportation and adapting the WGSS centers to fit their needs especially PwD including installing the needed tools and facilities, besides training the staff on including PwD and elderly in the WGSS activities. Also, we will target male survivors of sexual violence with specialized GBV services. Moreover, adolescent girls will be targeted with Girls dreams and AMAL initiative programs targeting girls (13-19 Yrs).
Throughout the project period, we will target 20,732 beneficiaries through the project’s activities including 13,590 Females, 7,142 Males (150 Young Children, 6,075 Children, 10,932 Adults, 3,575 Elderly) among them 775 PwD, through 6 centres including 29 protection staff (10 outreach teams, 10 case managers, 2 supervisors).
To ensure gender equality and quality of activities, M&E plan will include a target-based performance, ITT, regular site visits by the M&E Field assistants to verify & document project implementation progress including routine meetings with key informants at the local level to generate qualitative data on the effectiveness of project interventions. Depending on beneficiary consent forms, visual evidence that includes photos & videos & MoVs of project activities will be documented. Exit interview questionnaires will be administered to measure the satisfaction of beneficiaries with services being delivered.
Also, HRO will ensure smooth coordination with the protection NGO partners, local councils, stakeholders, and camp mangers throughout the project period.

4 - Needs

According to MSNA, as of October 2021, approximately 2.8 million IDPs were living in 1,389 camps or informal sites, the Cluster tracked over 273,000 individual displacements, which include multiple displacements), Women and children represent 80% of this caseload.According to GBV Main findings for HNO 2022, widowed or divorced women and girls reported Forced re-marriage and forced abandonment of children in some cases, higher levels of sexual harassment and exploitation in relation to obtaining shelter/housing, while the older women said that they were exposed to neglect by family members, perceived as an economic burden.According to MSNA Findings- HNO-2022, 62% of HHs mentioned that women show signs of distress in the last 3 months, compared to 31% of HHs mentioned the same for girls, 71% of communities/locations (compared to 62% last year) identify child marriage as an issue for girls aged 12 to 17. financial hardships were a key reason behind cases of early marriage in 72% of communities who took part in data collection.17% of communities/locations mentioned that PSEA is an issue for women in the last three months and 14% of them mentioned the same for girls, while according to HRO Protection need assessment conducted in October 2021 in Jisr Ash Shugur, Janudiyeh and Badama, 97% reported that gender-based violence is common in these areas, 65% reported that domestic violence has become more common.

5 - Implementation of activities

After preparing the project logistics & recruiting procedures, staff will be trained on GBV basics, GBV Case Management, inclusion of PwD, then we will conduct: GBV prevention & mitigation including empowerment activities, GBV case management, training of humanitarian actors.GBV awareness raising sessions, Life Skills Activities (Girls dreams program & AMAL initiative), Free spaces with focus on vulnerable groups, in addition to providing Vocational Training & Information/sensitization sessions about GBV related services.As for the response activities: Specialized GBV services & micro grant within the WGSSs, communities, & especially widows’ camps, in addition to implementing safety audits in the widows’ camps. Also, there will be a capacity building activity for humanitarian actors & local authorities & WLOs on GBV basics & risk mitigation trainings.M&E plan will include a target-based performance, ITT, regular site visits by the M&E Field assistants to verify & document project implementation progress including routine meetings with key informants at the local level to generate qualitative data on the effectiveness of project interventions. Depending on beneficiary consent forms, visual evidence that includes photos & videos & MoVs of project activities will be documented. Exit interview questionnaires will be administered to measure the satisfaction of beneficiaries with services being delivered.
