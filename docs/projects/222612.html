Project Summary

The program addresses the complex needs of vulnerable populations in northern Mozambique, in the context of the ongoing conflict in Cabo Delgado. It integrates Child Protection (CP), Gender-Based Violence (GBV), General Protection, and Housing, Land, and Property (HLP) rights to provide a comprehensive response that safeguards human rights and restores dignity.
Key Objectives:
Child Protection (CP): Protect children from abuse, exploitation, and violence by establishing child-friendly spaces, providing psychosocial support, and facilitating family reunifications where separations have occurred. Special attention is given to unaccompanied and separated children. And a significant access to services for recent displaced families.
Gender-Based Violence (GBV): Prevent and respond to GBV through survivor-centered approaches, case management, access to health and psychosocial services, and community-based prevention initiatives to challenge harmful gender norms. Risk mitigation of GBV is also a fundamental outcome through women economic empowerment activities.
Protection: Ensure the safety and rights of displaced and conflict-affected populations through legal assistance, documentation services, and community-based protection mechanisms. Special attention is given to strenghethen community based protection system and local structure ensuring a faster and more sustainable response respective international standards.
Housing, Land, and Property (HLP): Support displaced families to secure tenure rights, resolve disputes, and regain access to land and housing. This includes advocacy for land rights and community sensitization on HLP rights.
The implementation strategy is an integrated approach: each thematic area is addressed holistically to ensure synergy and avoid duplication of efforts. For example, CP and GBV interventions are closely aligned to address intersecting vulnerabilities. Community engagement remain one of Helpcode’s priorities structuring effective AAP two ways systems and ensuring meaningful participation especially of women and girls in decision making processes. Capacity Building is also key, both in collaboration with the government and the local structures but also ensuring participation of national partners. Due to the volatile context, mobile teams provide outreach services in remote and underserved areas, delivering case management, legal assistance, and referral pathways for medical and psychosocial support.
Expected Outcomes:
Enhanced safety and resilience of vulnerable children, including access to education and psychosocial care.
Improved response to GBV survivors with reduced stigma and strengthened prevention mechanisms.
Increased access to documentation, legal aid, and protection services for IDPs.
Strengthened tenure security for displaced families, enabling recovery and self-reliance.
This program adopts an inclusive and rights-based approach to address the intertwined protection needs of conflict-affected populations in northern Mozambique, contributing to durable solutions and community resilience

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

This program addresses the critical needs of conflict-affected populations in Cabo Delgado, integrating Child Protection (CP), Gender-Based Violence (GBV), General Protection, and Housing, Land, and Property (HLP) rights. The focus of the programme is the most affected northern districts in Cabo Delgado: Mueda, Palma, Nangade, Mocimboa da Praia and Muidumbe. Key activities include: CP: Establishing child-friendly spaces, providing psychosocial support, and facilitating family reunification for unaccompanied children.GBV: Offering survivor-centered case management, access to health services, and community-based GBV prevention.General Protection: Providing legal aid, facilitating access to documentation, and supporting community-based protection mechanisms.HLP: Securing tenure rights for displaced families, resolving disputes, and promoting restitution or compensation.The program’s holistic approach ensures synergy across sectors, with mobile teams delivering services to underserved areas and training local authorities to sustain interventions. Close collaboration with government entities, humanitarian actors, and community structures ensures relevance and impact. By strengthening safety, restoring rights, and fostering recovery, the program contributes to durable solutions for internally displaced persons (IDPs) and host communities, promoting dignity and resilience amidst ongoing conflict.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

• Child Protection (CP): The program establishes child-friendly spaces to offer a safe environment for children to play, learn, and MHPSS. It delivers psychosocial support to address emotional distress and facilitates family reunification for unaccompanied and separated children. Awareness campaigns promote child rights and strengthen community-based child protection mechanisms.• Gender-Based Violence (GBV): Survivor-centered support includes case management, access to health and psychosocial services, and fundamental referral for personal safety and legal assistance. Prevention efforts focus on engaging communities in challenging harmful gender norms and addressing root causes of GBV. Capacity-building sessions equip local service providers with tools to respond effectively to survivors.• General Protection: The program facilitates access to civil documentation for internally displaced persons (IDPs), ensure MHPSS response and strengthening community based protection structures. • Housing, Land, and Property (HLP): Community sensitization on HLP rights mitigates tensions and prevents secondary displacement in collaboration with legal response institution such as IPAJ. The program leverages mobile teams, community structures, and local authorities to deliver services efficiently; ensuring vulnerable groups can access protection, rebuild their lives, and restore dignity.Prioritized districts are Mocimboa da Praia, Mueda, Nangade, Muidumbe, Palma, Chiure. Other activities such has strenghtening local capacities, and community based protection structures, or protection monitoring, are targeting Cabo Delgado as a whole.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Assumptions and Risks Identified with Mitigation Measures Lack of Commitment and Support from Sub-national Institutions:Sub-national institutions involved in the action may not remain committed throughout the project's duration or fail to support critical activities such as surveys and data collection. This could result in restricted access to populations and delays in obtaining permits to enter target areas, thereby hindering the initiation of protection activities.Mitigation: Memorandums of Understanding (MoUs) will be signed with relevant District Services, clearly defining the project scope and their roles. District technicians will be actively involved, assigned responsibilities, and provided with appropriate incentives to ensure their sustained engagement. Inadequate Protection Services at the Sub-national Level:Local institutions may lack the capacity to provide effective protection services, leading to failures in referral systems and inadequate assistance to affected populations.Mitigation: All activities will be implemented in close collaboration and coordination with District Services, focusing on enhancing their capacity to deliver protection services and advocating for gaps coverage in the response in the specific districts. This will include building preparedness for continued service provision after the intervention concludes. Security Challenges in the Intervention Area:Attacks or conflict in the intervention area may restrict access, preventing the implementation of planned activities.Mitigation: A comprehensive security plan will be developed and regularly updated by the project core team and the appointed security officer. This plan will leverage information from District Authorities, local intelligence, and cluster actors. In the event of an attack, beneficiaries will be reassessed, and activities adapted accordingly. Community Resistance to Participation:The target population may be unwilling to share information or engage as protection focal points, jeopardizing the establishment of a community-based protection system.Mitigation: The project will emphasize clear and culturally sensitive communication with village and community leaders, as well as key stakeholders. Efforts will focus on explaining project objectives transparently to align expectations and foster collaboration, reducing the risk of conflict or disengagement.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

- Gender-Focused Planning: Activities are designed to address women’s needs, including those of pregnant and nursing mothers. For example: GBV support includes counseling and safe spaces and HLP programs ensure women’s access to awarness raising about land and housing rights specifically for women.- Economic Opportunities: Women will receive training, funding, and support to start businesses, reducing their dependence on others and increasing their independence.- Psico-social Support: The project will provide counseling and support to help women recover from the trauma of displacement and violence, helping them rebuild confidence and strength.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Helpcode’s project on the inclusion of people with disabilities adopts a comprehensive and multi-faceted approach to ensure meaningful impact. Coordination with local district organizations working in the disability sector will play a central role, with joint training efforts integrated into capacity-building processes to strengthen institutional understanding and improve service delivery. Alongside this, targeted awareness-raising initiatives will disseminate key messages and tailored materials to foster inclusion, reduce stigma, and encourage the active participation of people with disabilities in community life. The project will also ensure the effective use of referral pathways, addressing barriers and enhancing accessibility to guarantee equitable and timely access to essential services. By integrating these elements, the initiative aims to build institutional capacity, foster community awareness, and ensure that people with disabilities can fully access the opportunities and support affected population deserves.
