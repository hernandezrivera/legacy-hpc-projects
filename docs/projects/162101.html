Project Summary

This project will address the shelter and Solar light need for Rohingya Refugees in Camps 13. The aim of the proposed project is to improving the living conditions of the Rohingya refugees in camp 13 through the provision of safe, adequate and appropriate shelter and solar light assistance. To achieve this, ISDE take shelter support initiative for Rohingya Refugees in Camp -13. Capacity development training on disaster risk reduction (DRR) issues and shelter upgradation/ construction will also be arranged for the Rohingya community members under this project.

Needs

Recent Shelter/NFI sector Gap analysis report published in October 24, 2019 shows that need of Transitional shelters and Mid-term shelters about 90% Rohingya families in Camp 13. Only 1182 shelters have been constructed out of 9800 shelter. Refugees are residing in makeshift shelters that are severely below basic humanitarian standards, overcrowded, and will not withstand seasonal climatic conditions. The low quality of the shelters and poor terrain as well as the lack of privacy within these shelters has a serious impact on the physical and psychological well-being of the refugees - especially for women, children and older refugees and refugees with disabilities. Upgrades to shelters and site improvements are urgently required prior to the next monsoon and cyclone season. Also, the lack of adequate lighting in shelters and public spaces was reported as a top safety concern in the camp areas. Considering the volume of shelter needs in the targeted areas of implementation, this proposal aims to support persons of concern with transitional shelter solutions and NFI items (HH Solar light) in targeted areas.

Activities or outputs

Need Assesment: To find out the different needs related to gender and age, ISDE will conduct a simple need assesment survey with specific questions related to shelter, households items and distributions. The findings of this survey will support select the beneficiaries for shelter programming. After household survey ISDE will prepare a primary beneficiary list. After approval of CiC, it will be finalized. Transitional shelter assistance will not replacing all materials in the existing shelter, but replacing the damaged materials, allowing for an incremental process towards more durable shelters. The TSA will also require future top-up assistance in the future aimed at replacing some of the material while providing additional material that will increase the durability of the shelter. With the provision of additional materials, training and technical assistance are needed to emphasize key messaging related to strength, durability and maintenance of the shelter.Training and Technical Assistance: It is essential that all households receiving transitional shelter assistance also receive training. It is advisable to provide training prior to the provision of materials. Training will include revised Key Messages particularly raising bamboo using footings. Special attention will be given to the DRR features such as retention walls, drainage, bracing, tie down and fire protection (especially cooking areas). Upkeep and maintenance of shelter will be part of training as it goes a long way in ensuring durability of shelter. Special trainings will be provided to women. Diversity determinants (i.e. include older and disable persons) need to be considered while providing training, as all members of the community can play essential role in shelter maintenance.Technical assistance for EVIs will be provided so will be the technical assistance to other beneficiaries. ISDE will request Shelter/NFI Sector to support with the necessary training and support to our staff. Distribution of transitional shelter kits [300HHs]: All material will be distributed directly to the selected beneficiaries. Treated Bamboo will distribute to the beneficiaries for long lasting of Shelter. ISDE will purchase treated bamboo from BRAC/DRC/IOM. ISDE will engaged local skilled and unskilled labour force for all shelter construction. Trained Community Volunteer also developed for shelter assistance. Community Volunteer will support the community members in implementing transatioanl shelter assistance. HH level Solar light distribution [300HHs]: ISDE will distribute Solar light to the selected beneficiaries. Regular Monitoring and Reporting: ISDE project staff regularly monitor the project activities and report the Shelter sector regularly. ISDE closely monitor the activities through field level staff and volunteers. Direct feedback mechanism from beneficiaries to Executive Director via mobile and inspection from HQ will established.
