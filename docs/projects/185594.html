Project Summary

The project proposed will be implemented in two departments of the Extreme North
Region for a period of 12-months, in favor of internally displaced populations (IDPs). The
implementation strategy is based on Cash Based Assistance (CBT) to the IDPs in two
subdivisions in Mayo-Tsanaga (Mokolo and Koza) for 6 months, through a, local mobile operator, the
assistance targets 4000 PDIs: 1460 boys, 1360 girls, 440 men and 740 women; while in
Logone et Chari, the In-Kind assistance (distribution of food in kind) will target 10,000
IDPs in 4 subdivisions (Logone Birni, Fotokol, Makary and Blangoua), i.e. 3,558 boys, 3,397
girls, 1,117 men and 1,928 women for a food ration of 540g/person/d. In collaboration with the WFP at the regional level, the planned distribution to 10,000 beneficiaries will require the provision of 1944 tons of food (cereals, pulses, vegetable oil, super cereal, and iodized salt) during 12 months.
General objective: The main objective will be to reduce household vulnerability to food
insecurity most especially the Internally Displaced Populations (IDPs), who are the most
affected by the crisis in the Lake Chad Basin. For agricultural activities 4533 beneficiaries (2100 men and 2433 women) will be targeted
Specific Objectives will be:
- To provide input support to household farmers in order to increase their agricultural
productivity
- Supporting and accompanying households farmer in the implementation of agricultural
production activities.
The main results will therefore be to provide in kind assistance through the distribution of
almost 1950 tons (270g/d/person) of food within a period of 3months (which is the planting season) in
order to strengthen the nutritional status of these populations and to strengthen the local
economy through resilience activities, which including the CBT modality (10254Fcfa/Person/month).
