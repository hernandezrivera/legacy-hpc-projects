Project Summary

O projeto REEME: Resposta de Emergência em Educação no distrito de Nampula, tem como população alvo a comunidade escolar, ou seja, professores, alunos e pessoal administrativo das escolas dos distritos de Memba, Ilha de Moçambique, Mongicual, Liupo, Meconta e Erati, totalizando 23 540 crianças que em situação de emergência beneficiarão de melhores condições de ensino e aprendizagem.
O projeto assentará na perspetiva sistémica, com a criança no centro da intervenção. Com base nos direitos das crianças que salvaguardam o seu bem-estar, iremos trabalhar com formadores, professores, pais, encarregados de educação e comunidade em geral. Em particular, queremos contribuir para a prestação de assistência educacional multissetorial às crianças, professores e comunidades em situação de emergência, bem como para desenvolver a capacidade local de preparação, resposta e recuperação de emergências, contribuindo para reduzir o risco e a vulnerabilidade nos distritos alvo da província de Nampula.
O projeto busca capacitar as comunidades alvo, para que possam garantir maior proteção às crianças em situação de emergência e promover o acesso à educação. Para isso, são essenciais a abordagem de temas transversais, além dos conteúdos escolares, como GHM, AVC, GBV e Educação Inclusiva.
A ação está organizada em torno de 9 atividades-resultados esperados complementares que, em conjunto, contribuem para a consecução dos objetivos específicos do projeto.
Considerando a "Estratégia de Preparação, resposta e recuperação do Sector de Educação em Situação de Emergência (2020-2029)" do Ministério da Educação de Moçambique, a nossa intervenção situar-se-ia entre as fases I e II (Primeira Resposta e Recuperação Inicial, respetivamente), que envolve acções como a reabilitação de latrinas, fornecimento e instalação de TLS, escolas reabilitadas, fornecimento de material pedagógico/aprendizagem, campanha “Regresso às aulas”, ministrado “Programa de Aprendizagem Acelerada” por parte das crianças, Formação de professores, cuidadores e sobre GHM, material fornecido e formação em questões de GBV e formação em BESP para professores, diretores e “círculos de interesse”.
Esta proposta reforça ainda a educação como uma das componentes mais importantes no processo de planeamento e implementação da resposta humanitária, que se pretende um processo contínuo, envolvendo a prevenção e mitigação dos efeitos das catástrofes e a resposta de emergência. Assim, a educação de emergência destina-se a fornecer proteção física e psicossocial imediata, bem como conhecimentos e habilidades que salvam vidas.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

This action will target the school community, that is, teachers, students and administrative staff of schools that will benefit from better teaching and learning conditions. The community in general will also benefit from the set of actions to be carried out: Students (girls and boys) from the Nampula province in the districts Cidade de Nampula are 10 168 girls and boys, Mogincual 3821 and Mossuril 4806. In addition to the classrooms and materials that will contribute to the improvement of the teaching and learning process, they will also benefit from sessions to stimulate life skills, prevent violence against children and, particularly for girls, the distribution of education dignity kits that are a central element for girls to remain in school, avoiding dropout and related problems, such as premature marriages. Teachers (women and men): about 136 teachers from the 55 schools that will be covered by this action: 73 teachers from Nampula city, 28 from Mogincual and 35 from Mossuril. They will benefit from improving their working conditions, but also by improving their educational skills through a set of trainings that aim to enable them to better respond to situations of calamity and emergency. Community in general: the entire community surrounding the schools will benefit from improved teaching and learning conditions in schools. In Nampula city 241 members of the community will be involved, 28 members in Mogincual and 114 in Mossuril. On the other hand, by being directly involved in the rehabilitation process and the sensitization campaigns. In total, this action will cover 19,376 beneficiaries, 10,482 in Nampula, 3939 Mogincual e 4955 Mossuril.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The project will be based on the systemic perspective, with the child at the centre of the intervention. On the basis of children's rights that safeguard their well-being, we will work with trainers, teachers, parents, caregivers and the general community. In particular, we want to contribute to deliver multi-sectoral education assistance to emergency situations that affected children, teachers and communities, as well as to build local capacity to prepare for, respond to and recover from emergencies, contributing to reducing risk and vulnerability in the districts of Moussuril and Ilha de Moçambique in Nampula Province.The project seeks to empower the target communities, so that they can guarantee greater protection for children in emergency situations and promote access to education. 5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Low Risk - Political instability caused by changes in the sociopolitical context lead to political changes and discontinuity in implementation of reconstruction/DRR policies.The project will be implemented transparently and through a participatory approach aimed at ongoing dialogue and involvement of all actors (which happens from the initial phase of formulating the concept note). This will allow for a good understanding of the project by the local actors involved, promote ownership of the project by local entities and communities and facilitate the adaptation of strategies in case of changes. will be Collaboration agreements established at the beginning of the project with a view to engaging local political authorities in addition to the people (individual contacts). Low Risk - Reduction of political interest by the local government for participation in activities and support for producers.The project will promote the training of technicians from the sectors government at the local level via participation in all consultations and training, awareness and information activities, which will lead to ownership of the project and consequent interest in the activities and their inclusion in the district plans after the project. Medium Risk - Access to the intervention zone is affected by weather shocks/weather conditions.Activity planning will take into account the constraints of the station and the project team will monitor the situation. If one worst case scenario arrives, the project can be prepared to propose appropriate aid alternatives. Medium/High Risk - Potential conflicts/attacks in the intervention zone make it difficult to access and implement activities.Oikos has a security plan for the regions, which will be adapted and used within the scope of this project. The implementation of the plan considers a training component to project team.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The project's priority is to promote girls' access to education in an emergency situation, also guaranteeing them a safe environment free of violence or sexual abuse, through training and raising awareness among the community and teachers.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The project will have as an indicator, the disaggregated quantification of beneficiaries by sex and by being a person with a disability or not. This will help to give visibility to people with disabilities but also to promote the right to education.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Environmental issues and adaptation to climate change will be transversally integrated into the different components of the project, since the underlying action is intended to increase knowledge about these issues and increase the resilience of populations in the face of the impacts of climate change/natural disasters, in order to reduce their impact on human lives, property and livelihoods. It is intended inin particular increase knowledge about climate change and risk management/necessary adaptation measures among agricultural producers. This will be achieved through actions to strengthen risk management mechanisms, such as the creation/training of Disaster Risk Management Committees, and specific training sessions incorporated in the training plan, which includes training packages on climate change and conservation agriculture that include information on protective measures against climate change: drought, irregular rainfall, high rainfall and very large discharges in a short time, erosion, strong winds, etc. Awareness-raising actions will also be carried out, using participatory tools, such as social theatre, and awareness-raising through radio messages, for the expansion of information. On the other hand, the project includes approaches, means and knowledge that aim to increase the resilience of producers and communities in the face of potential natural disasters. This perspective of resilience, inserted in the objective of the project, is a guiding aspect of the entire intervention: The project is oriented not only to restore the food security of the affected families, but mainly to provide them with resilience in the face of similar future situations. This resilience comes from a reduction in vulnerability, either through the provision of adequate means, diversification of productive and economic alternatives, increased skills and knowledge, but also from greater information, preparation and structuring of the community itself and the support structures on the part of the local authorities.Additionally, the introduction of technologies or materials with a negative environmental impact is not foreseen. On the other hand, appropriate practices and technologies with a positive impact on biodiversity, such as conservation agriculture, will be promoted.
