Project Summary

The violence in Benishangul Gumuz, Oromia, and Southern Nations Nationalities and Peoples’ (SNNP) regions have further caused high numbers of displacement and damage to infrastructure and basic services and exposed the population to major protection risks. UNHCR will respond to reduce the various crises affecting the population that have serious implications on their lives and livelihoods with increased protection concerns through harmonizing its IDP response with the Cluster system, the Humanitarian Response Plan (HRP), the Humanitarian Needs Overview (HNO), the Sustainable Development Goals, the Guiding Principles on Internal Displacement (1999) and the Kampala Convention (2009).Recognizing that contexts across the region is both changeable and highly specific, with unique needs per location, UNHCR Shelter and Non- food item response is planned to address the needs of people in all phases of displacement, with a focus on the emergency phase, care, and maintenance of shelter for people in protracted displacements and supporting sustainable returns and recovery where the context allows. The planned interventions include consideration of shelter preparedness, response, and shelter recovery support to account for both returns and secondary displacement and will be conducted in close coordination with multi-sectoral stakeholders to ensure that approaches are integrated, and that broader needs are addressed. Holding the protection of affected populations at its core: protection principles will be mainstreamed throughout our responses as per the ESNFI cluster strategy. Through a bread of responses options, in-kind assistance and cash when feasible, UNHCR planes to reach 65,444 HH (327,220 Individuals) across the six region through the below responses.
Emergency Shelters (Communal, family shelters or Emergency Shelter kits) provision Non Food Items and ESNFI provision. For the displaced who have returned to their original homes, support such as Shelter Repair kits either through Cash or In-kind with technical guidance on building better and addressing Housing Land and Property (HLP) will be the focus. Shelter Repair is also planned for displaced populations who are unable to return to their place of origin due to protracted displacement, where government allow to settle in a secondary location. Capacity building of staff, authorities, affected populations and advocacy for those with shelter and household needs is also considered. The IDP hosting communities will also benefit from the responses. As per the cluster guideline 10-15% of the vulnerable communities hosting IDPs will be targeted. Acknowledging any responses that exclude groups and individuals, increase violence and SGBV risks and increase risk of exploitation, UNHCR will undertake essential actions to reduce risks and mitigate Sexual and Gender-Based Violence (SGBV) associated with shelter and NFI responses. Awareness-raising sessions will be delivered to ensure that the communities are made aware of the protection risks in shelter and NFI response and how best to mitigate them. UNHCR will continue to use IASC GBV guidelines & related tools for SEA risk assessment and mitigation, including its newly developed GBV policy guidelines, National action plan and Victim centered approach for addressing sexual misconduct to ensure accessibility to all women, girls and other at risk group. Most often persons with disabilities are disproportionately affected by disasters and conflict and they face multi-layered barriers to accessing humanitarian services. As an effort to enhance inclusive humanitarian action principles, UNHCR will provide awareness-raising and training of those at the forefront of service delivery and from the overall target reserving 5-10% of kits (tailor made for disables) to be allocated to households with family members with disabilities.

1. - Projects activities

Under the Shelter and NFI cluster, UNHCR will respond to basic shelter and NFI need of IDPs, returnees and non- displaced population through the already agreed set of responses as per the ESNFI cluster strategy. Based on the priority set out by the cluster and the type of displacement UNHCR will respond through the below activities.1. Provision of emergency shelter through cash and/or in-Kind 2. In kind and cash shelter repair kits provision for Returnees 3. Non-Food Items distribution 4. Emergency shelter and Non-food items distribution 5. Durable shelter solutions for returnees and relocated. The activities that are going to be undertaken under each response type will be carried out in close coordination and collaboration with all stakeholders including the affected population. In general, the main activities of the project include: Targeting of beneficiaries, Establishing the response approach, communication of the response, Organizing the distribution/construction site as per cluster guideline. Accordingly, the targeting of beneficiaries will be done based on the cluster endorsed vulnerability criteria of Female headed households, Child headed households, Households with a high dependency, Households caring for one or more members with chronic diseases, Elderly (60+) without family support, People living with disabilities, Households with pregnant or lactating women, Households with children under the age of 2 years and Households living in sub-standard shelter conditions (like in collective centre, partial or fully destroyed shelter) and the stage of the shelter in the area of return In consideration of safety of all involved, physical access for the most vulnerable community members, well-being of the community, access to facilities and additional factors response approach will be established. Before and during intervention (distribution and/or construction) UNHCR will ensure effective communication with affected communities on date, time, place, type of items and quantities to be expected, what documentation beneficiaries should bring etc based on the response type through available means of communication like, banners, mass communication and etc. With lesson learned from previous intervention, best practices and as per the ESNFI endorsed guideline each response will be carried out through the implementing partners with close coordination with DRM and the affected population. In addition UNHCR through its implementing partner will provide the required technical guidance and support to beneficiaries. To gather feedback from the target population on the impact, relevance of responses meeting their needs UNHCR will undertake review of distribution through Post distribution/construction monitoring Survey.
