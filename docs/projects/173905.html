Project Summary

This project targets affected populations (children) of the ongoing Anglophone crisis in Kupe Muanenguba Division living in all the 03 Sub Divisions namely: Tombel, Bangem and Nguti. The Division counts approximately 8,500 Internally Displaced Persons with Nguti Sub- Division having the majority, seconded by Tombel Sub Division and lastly Bangem Sub Division. Since the inception of the crisis right to this moment there have an increasing need for services to vulnerable children. Therefore, this project is inclusive, targeting children in need, and putting attention equally to children with disabilities in order to give hope. AFRINET has benefitted lots of capacity building workshops organized by various UN sub-clusters and all these have strengthened the technical and human resource abilities to intervene with maximum adherence to do-no-ham and other humanitarian principles.
Consequently, this project will address several child challenges to ensure maximum child protection. This will include but not exhaustive intensive advocacy for children’s rights as contained in the Convention on the rights of the child. This affects the quality of life of all violated children and also, many changes in society are a disproportionate or negatively effect children.
This project is eqaully intending to establish birth certificates to most vulnerable children affected by the crisis in Kupe-Muanenguba. This actually needed beacause the school (private schools especially) environment in this division is quiet encouraging now. And with the economic hardship of most farmilies currently, it will be a great help to support these category of children with this eduactaional neccessity especially for those in examination classes.
Psychological and psychosocial support to Orphans and Vulnerable Children (OVCs) is also a major target for this project. This is a key needed area for child intervention in order to remove that psychological trauma with children and give them that level of exposure to succeed in life. This will bring the children back to a positive mind set. Also, the project will consider children who lost their parent(s) or whose parents were kidnapped together with the children during the course of the crisis in Kupe-Muanenguba Division.
This project is also aimed at Identifying and implement measures to prevent family separations that will be at the detriment of the life of children. Thus contineous talks, presentations, discussions and focus groups discussions, advocacy sessions with target population (families) in different platforms will be carried out. This will build togetherness, love, mutual living together, respect for one another within families and increase family togtherness that favourable for the welbeing of children. In addition, this project is intended to provide relief to children already engaged in child labour, including worst forms other forms of child labour and expliotation, with other child protection services; multisectoral services (such as psychosocial support earlier mentioned ); and pathways to decent work for children of legal working age to earn a living. This intervention is highly needed beacause such exposure of children during risky hours in certain environment is detrimental to the life of the child especially the girl child. Therefore, families will be sensitised on regular bases on issues related to child labour to minimise dangers that children encounter in streets. However, better opportunities will be provided to families of such children as a way towards ensuring livelihood to sustain the home. Also, families will be trained on how to counsel their children for positive behaviours in society.
