Project Summary

The aim of this proposed project is to provide multidisciplinary; medical, rehabilitation and psycho-social support services to people with new disabilities (amputations and paralysis) after Mass Demonstrations, newly injured people and also people with disabilities who live in access restricted areas and do not have proper access to adequate medical and rehabilitation services which may lead to complications that may threaten their lives and put it in danger, moreover, those people with disabilities in ARAs were indirectly affected by GMR events because of the concentration of humanitarian response and donors priorities to the needs of people affected by GMR.
The service is provided by 4 well-trained outreach home care teams that will cover in all Gaza Strip governates; North Gaza, Gaza, Middle area and south Gaza; where each team includes; A Physician, A physiotherapist, A nurse, An occupational therapist and a psychologist. Teams also will provide in-house training for the families of patients and PWDs on home care basics, dressing and proper nutrition. The project also includes providing assistive devices, medical kits, hygiene kits, Food parcels/Vouchers and also food supplements to target group. The project also contains a referral system to assist patients in need for further medical, psycho-social and mental health services.
Moreover, the project focus on improving the psychological status of both target groups and also their families who are in a bad need for psycho-social support after the trauma they faced through psycho-social support activities like group psychological sessions and outdoor activities.
In addition to that the project aims at increasing the capacity and knowledge of newly disabled people towards their rights trough peer o peer sessions that also raise their awareness towards international laws for PWDs.

1 - Needs

Needs for this project were identifies based on the problem & causes analyses in addition to NCCR field teams experience in serve injured & PWDs in Gaza Strip in a project funded by OPT HF (2018). NCCR teams highlighted the very bad needs among injured people & people with new disabilities for continuous medical, rehabilitation & also psycho-social support services clarified by a fact sheet of the project.The increase in needs takes place against a backdrop of an overall precarious humanitarian situation in the Gaza, expressed through a health system on the verge of collapse, widespread despair & hope for political solutions diminish.Since 30 March 2018, Gaza Strip has witnessed a significant increase in casualties in the context of mass demonstrations along Gaza boarders. According to the updated statistics of Health Cluster in their latest situational report (August, 2019); a total of 31338 people injured, 18549 of those casualties were stabilized & transferred to MoH & NGO hospitals emergency departments 7,545 of those cases were live ammunition gunshot injuries; from them 87% are limb gunshot injuries. Injured people with limb causalities will need limb reconstruction & will require up to 7 surgeries & extensive rehabilitation & treatment for up to 2 years. Moreover, Patients with bone fracture that do not have enough medical & rehabilitation treatment may suffer from pin tract infections, nonunion, malunion, delayed union & hardware failure. Patients are often discharged early from hospitals because of the acute shortage in medical resources. Most of these patients need continuous medical care to avoid any advanced effects that may led to amputations. Based on health cluster's situational report; updated total number of amputations is 149, including 30 children, out of this total, 122 were lower limb amputations & 27 upper limb amputations, the total number of patients with paralysis due to spinal cord injury is 24. Those types of disabilities need professional intervention by experienced rehabilitation teams and also psycho-social support services. PWDs in Gaza already suffer from acute shortage on rehabilitation & care services, this caused them serious health complications such as loss of muscle strength & endurance, contractures, osteoporosis etc., and may also lead to death because of bed sores. All of this made them isolated & unable to live a normal life. Moreover, recently the funding priorities are very concentrated on needs resulted from the continuing Mass Demonstrations, which even made this people more marginalized in services. specially those who are living on border areas who are more exposed to be in danger, besides, their families are very poor, have not enough awareness & knowledge on how to deal with their children &cannot afford buying assistive devices & paying transportation nor medical cost to them, so that their access to health services is very restricted. The families of PWDs & injured are very vulnerable, the majority of them are living a very bad economic & social circumstances, as a result patients & PWDs in these families do not have an acceptable level of proper nutrition, medical resources & hygiene. This has been noticed by NCCR teams to cause a long delay in their healing process. So, those families need to be trained about the basics of home care; to be able to provide medical care needed to maintain their children psychological & health stability at the end of this project. Besides medical treatment, patients also need food and food supplements are very important factor that can affect how quickly or slowly a broken bone healing.Moreover, these target group and their families are in a bad need for psycho-social support and also recreational act .

2 - Activities

- Implement Home Care evaluation visits for 600 vulnerable injured people & persons with new disabilities (amputations&paralysis) after GMR & PWDs living in ARAs. Then choose 400 cases, of them(220)PWDs ,67 in South,56 in Gaza,48 in North and 49 in Middle of Gaza.Opening initial files for cases baseline assessment & setting up multidisciplinary approach case management plans for injured& PWD,fill their needs from assistive devices&other supplies.Teams meet regularly to discuss the care plan&activities given to patients&their families, this meeting is registered in MDO file where members write down their input for follow up.-Provide Medical,rehabilitation&occupational therapy services for 400 Injured&PWD from all ages&both genders.Those multidisciplinary services will be implemented by 4 highly experienced home care teams in different governates of Gaza strip,every team consists of; physician, physiotherapist, psychologist, occupational therapist&nurse .This activity will contribute to the needs of target group by improving their access to health care&rehabilitation services. Also,will solve the problem of accessibility&lower the economic burden on their families.-Provide in house training & psychological support services for 800 care givers from the target group families about basics of home care services, as part of preparedness for emergencies & also to sustain the services for the PWD who needs intensive care; such as Basic physiotherapy home exercises & dressing of injuries&bedsore as well as nutrition consultations,those training will be carried out by home care teams as part of home care visits. This activity will contribute to the needs of target group by providing their families with training on basics of home care&dressings that those families are very vulnerable and poor so those training will raise their awareness &speed healing process of their children-Provide Assistive Devices e.g.crutches, walkers,Air Mattresses for almost Injured & PWD from the targeted group.This will contribute to the needs of target group by supporting the rehabilitation process and promote the opportunities for independent living, achievement of individual life goals, and inclusion in the community.- Provide 200 Medical kits, 300 hygiene kits and 300 food parcels/Vouchers and food supplements for injured and PWDs .This contributes to the needs by solving the problem of acute shortage in medical disposables&lower the economic burden of buying it on families of patients and PWDs. Also, providing hygiene kits & food vouchers in addition to food supplements supplies help speeding the healing process &recovery of injuries as the nutrition for those people getting better.Food supplements will be given based on pre-lab tests covered also by the project for selected target group- Psycho-social support activities for target group & their families .This will contribute to the needs of target group by releasing pressure & discharge negative feelings and making them respond better to physical treatment.- Self-care activities for staff .This activity will contribute to the needs of both staff & target group, staff by letting them recharge their energy & discharge their work pressure during past two years working in trauma pathway caused by GMR, so that they can deliver more quality services to target groups-Refer cases in need for further medical interventions,psycho-social &mental health services, the project will cover the cost for transportation to the cases; and this assure their affordability & accessibility to health & psycho-social support services.Proposed activities are aligned with; SO2 of the HRP by meeting the basic needs of vulnerable people through quality services&better access & SO1 of Health sector by ensuring the availability,accessibility,acceptability and quality of services to vulnerable communities.Project Ensures access to information via radio spots,leaflets,evaluation visits,brochures,social media news&office cards

9 - Protection mainstreaming
9.1 - Do No Harm

Teams consists of 5 members each, and visits are conducted by no less than 2 members (male and female) to avoid any harmful acts, and also project staff and NCCR staff sign code of conduct policies before starting work. In addition to that the psychologist in the teams help to facilitate teams working with cases and their families.The proposed project is totally a service-based project that has no side effects on surrounding environment.The project also prioritizing safety & dignity of targeted communities through the provision of their needs of medical, rehabilitation and psycho social support service and providing them with medical, hygine and food kits, and these services will be based on confidentiality and trust.

9.2 - Equal and impartial access to aid

This project enables equal access to services including targeting vulnerable groups and people with specific needs . The project provides the services to vulnerable injured people and people with disabilities, all target groups will access project’s services equally, and will be encouraged to claim their rights and to have self-protection mechanisms. The project target group consists of 200 out 500 who are people with disabilities. The target group are the most vulnerable including; Women and children in these areas are suffering from in-equal access to services comparing to men.
