Project Summary

The proposed emergency nutrition intervention will provide integrated life saving for the treatment of acute malnutrition in the targeted areas of the South West Region of Cameroon, through the community based management of acute malnutrition and targeting children under the age of five. The project aims to increase coverage and access to the management of severe acute malnutrition (SAM) in the face of Covid-19, de-worming and as well as promotion of optimal infant and young child feeding (IYCF) practices. To ensure quality of services, formal training and field support visits will take place through out the project cycle. Community participation and mobilization will be key in ensuring early detection and improved coverage in the nutrition services in the South West Region. Where mobilization fails, a door to door campaign will be implemented which is a better strategy in the face of Covid-19.
In addition, the beneficiaries will receive IYCF interventions through IYCF training, mother to mother group sessions and hygiene education to help prevent diseases and acute malnutrition among children under the age of five.The project will help children who are malnourished from deteriorating further and also prevent the others from acute malnutrition especially those who are malnourished as a result of inadequate dietary intake.
The community outreach component which entails the recruitment of community workers will be used in a large scale in order to identify children with acute malnutrition on time and refer them to the health centers for those who have complications. The project will constantly train field workers on how to identify malnutrition with the help of MUAC tapes, edema and referral forms.
Following Covid-19 and the NW/SW crisis in Cameroon with the displacement of the population, transportation and preposition of supplies will be a key objective in order to reach maximum number of beneficiaries.
The targeted health districts are; Limbe, Ekondo Titi and Mbonge. In Limbe Health district we plan on targeting Bojongo, Moliwe, Sea Port and Zone 2 health areas while in Ekondo Titi health district our targets health areas are Ekondo Titi, Bekora, Lobe and Bamusso. Finally we plan on reaching out to the people of Mbonge district who are in Boa Balondo, Kotto Barombi, Mbonge and Kombone health areas.
This project seeks to protect children below five years of age of both sexes with now gender discrimination. It does not aim to focus on one gender but for all children below five without an exception. Also, the affected persons will be well informed and necessary measures taken as well as community workers of every health area will be involved in the campaign to enable a better follow up since they are more vast with the communities.

1 - Besoins / Needs

Since the outbreak of the crisis in 2016 and the Covid-19 pandemic, a lot of people especially in the North West and South of Cameroon who have been greatly affected by the crisis. though the situation in the country seems stable, conflict has persisted in these regions. As a result of the continued violence, the number of people displaced continues to grow. Prior to the crisis as reported by UNICEF, SW region presented vulnerabilities to malnutrition with high rate of stunting 28% . Also there are poor indicators on infant and young child feeding practices and were reported at 58.3%.The nutrition condition in the SW region remains unclear due to absence of comprehensive nutrition anthropometric survey using SMART methodology. It is in this light that TeksGlobal Foundation aims therefore to provide data measuring the prevalence of malnourished children in Limbe, Mbonge and Ekondo Titi health Districts with our diverse plan, give them an appropriate treatment when ever needed, empowering each parent particularly mothers on what we call a balance diet.Moreover, it is anticipated that living conditions in the SW region may deteriorate significantly, with growth in urban food insecurity, if the macroeconomic context continues to decline. Concern will integrate nutrition interventions at the community level with a special focus on child health & nutrition, Infant & Young Child Feeding (IYCF­), and community screening and referral, for better program outcomes. In addition to the high prevalence of malnutrition, capacity in the prevention and treatment of malnutrition is significantly lacking in SW region as a result of conflict, which have undermined the training and education of health professionals and prevented the establishment of an effective, wide ­reaching health system. Consequentially, there exists an urgent need to train and build the capacity staff among conflict ­affected populations in order to carryout screening as well as well referral of malnourished children. Our experiences from the field shows due to Covid-19 the mobilization who people in the community is not the best measure to be adopted but a door to door campaign would give the bet results. Again, most of the parents are afraid to bring their children to the screening center but prefer to be met at home. Coupled with the fact that those areas are not very closed to medical facilities, a lot of these children suffer because of this. All these factors and more contributed to the birth of this project by TeksGlobal Foundation.
