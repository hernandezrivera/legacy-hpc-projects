Project Summary

The planned project will increase the self-resilience in the targeted communities by supporting most vulnerable households with sustainable livelihood through providing under-conflict doable income generation sources,
The project will assist internal displaced and host communities households with 200 milk cows for 200 HHs to be as an income generator, supported with practical vocational shorth-term training about cow breeding conducted by senior vet trainers, trained new bread winners will be supported throughout 6 months with a close veterinarian backup by providing vet care and treatments field by expert veterinaries, moreover the project will strengthen the created livelihood by supporting cows with the required fodder for 6 months.
In addition to the provided livestock the project will support the wheat value chain in the communities with high severity by providing 200 most vulnerable farmers with wheat cultivating inputs for 1 hectare, the following inputs will be provided: 250 Kg/he seeds and irrigation vouchers for 50 mm irrigation depth, 20% of another vulnerable farmers yield will be procured to supply a complement QRCS project with flour.
QRCS also will support mid-scale agricultural practices by supporting 400 most vulnerable farmers who planting 0.5 hectare with legumes by providing seeds, fertilizing materials, and irrigation vouchers for 200 mm of irrigation water, and supporting 200 most vulnerable farmers who have olives trees by providing services, fertilizing materials, and irrigation vouchers for 200 mm of irrigation water.

4 - Needs

According to HNO 2020 draft version: The number of people estimated to be food insecure is 7.9 million or 39 percent of the total population of Syria. Of this at least 569,477 (6%) are estimated to be severely food insecure. The majority of those are located in Aleppo, Idleb, Rural Damascus and Ar-Raqqa governates. the increase in the number of people in need particularly those who are moderately food insecure is largely due to households depleting their savings and assets as well as struggling to find livelihood opportunities to meet their food needs, as the crisis continues into its ninth year. The report pointed that some 69 percent of Syrians are believed to be living on less than USD 1.90 a day, more than double since 2011 . moreover, Unemployment rates are believed to be at 50 percent, with only 16 percent of women working in the labor force, About 216,000 hectares of wheat and other crops such as barley, fruit trees ,Vegetables and grazing areas (17 percent of national areas planted) were affected by a number of shocks including floods, fires, frost, hailstorms, high temperatures and plant diseases and pests specifically yellow rust..FSS stresses the need to combine medium term approaches through enhancing primary production capacity and incomes, especially for livelihoods based agriculture, and providing inputs to improve the agricultural services sector in order to improve local availability of food (supply) with short term measures through food assistance and imports which remain key to addressing food insecurity in the country in a holistic manner and hence physical and mental wellbeing as well as the living standards of crisis affected people.QRCS conducted a multi sectoral assessment (Attached) in North and North-West of Syria shows that IDPs form 32% of population in NW of Syria, assessment reported a high percentage in unemployment about 50-90% in 77% of the surveyed communities, which mean more than half of HHs has not a real income source.

5 - Implementation of activities

The project intervention will be throughout:- Providing 200 milk cows in addition to fodder and vet materials for 6 months.- Providing in-kind 50 MT of wheat seeds for 200 farmers, with fuel vouchers.- Providing in-kind legumes seeds and fertilizing materials for another 200 farmers, with fuel vouchers.- Providing agricultural services, and fertilizing materials for 200 olives farmers, with fuel vouchers- Purchasing 160 MT of wheat yield from 200 wheat farmers. Project implementation cycle will start from coordination with the communities representatives and local councils, performing livestock market assessments to evaluate cows, fodder, and seeds availability.Beneficiaries selection will be built according to their vulnerability and need prioritizing displaced families affected by last escalation with the focus on female/children headed HHs and disabled persons, the project has been designed to mitigate the conflict consequences on adolescents as well by included them in the targeted beneficiaries. Qatar Red crescent society will implement the project by Syrian expertise through an infield team with wide knowledge of the Syrian context and belong to the targeted communities, the field team will comprise: vet experts, field, logistics officers and workers, all members have high awareness and long experience in the humanitarian service providing and had been trained on GBV, PSEA, doing no harm, protection and security aspects.Beneficiaries selection will be as a result of an interactive process will start with introducing the project plan, beneficiaries criteria and targets to the targeted communities in order to mainstream them in the project.Trained M&E team will be assigned/hired locally on this project who will becharged with collecting information regarding beneficiaries and data analysis and will alsoclosely monitor and verify the services and renovations rendered to beneficiaries toensure correctness, timeliness and efficiency

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

1. Low Capacity coupled with challenges in recruitment and retention of appropriately skilled staff at all levels Mitigation measure: - Bridging the staff gap - implementing a capacity building and training program for staff at various tiers and implementation responsibilities 2. Lack of coordination and synergies with representatives of targetcommunities Mitigation measure: involve focal persons from the local council tocoordinate with the beneficiary elders 3. Identification of beneficiaries and appreciation of project chievements will be weak because they do not hear, read or see enough ofproject activities and positive impacts Mitigation measure: - Encourage field visits bycommunity elders with appointed focal persons to see the results and hear frombeneficiaries directly - Develop a communications strategy to guide constituency buildingthrough internal and external communications with Project stakeholders - ConsiderMedia communication and mobile applications 4. Continuing risk of insecurity and potential escalation in insurgency related violence with direct impacts on project activities Mitigation measure: - train field staff on MOSS compliance (Minimum Operational Safety Standards) - Use of alternative measures for project supervision including Remote Asset Monitoring to carry out spot checks in the target areas 5. Cows, fodder and vet materials unavailability in the local market or an increase in the item’s costs. Mitigation measure: QRC has the flexibility of supplying items from the local markets or through the boarders from Turkey.As a further measure, QRC team will observe markets and conducting surveys to determine the prices and available resources.QRC has a list of reliable capable suppliers that will be invited through vendors process to mitigate the risk of supplying through boarders.6. Fuel misuse by the beneficiariesMitigation measure: fuel will provided via vouchers throughout multi stages depends upon irrigation dates
