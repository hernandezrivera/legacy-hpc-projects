Project Summary

The project seeks to provide 1,021 transitional shelter units approximates (5,105 people) who were affected by Tropical Cyclone Idai in 10 wards of Buhera and 8 wards in Mutare Rural district. The project will provide construction material and pay locally hired and trained builders for the works. The community will mobilize local material which include aggregates and bricks.

1 - Activities

The project will do proper targeting which is informed by secondary data of host IDPs affected by Tropical Cyclone Idai. Registrations and thorough verification shall be done in consultation with all stakeholders.Local builders will be mobilized and trained on proper construction practices. Project participants will be encouraged to mobilize local material like bricks, sand and other aggregates. Procurement and delivery of construction material based on bill of quantities will be done. Project staff and stakeholders to monitor all construction work and certify completed structures before payment of builders.

8 - Accountability to Affected People
8.1 - Describe how your project was designed in consultation with communities? How will you incorporate communities in project implementation and what feedback mechanisms do you have in place?

District and community stakeholders will be involved at every stage of the project cycle (Inclusive) to ensure ownership and joint monitoring of construction work. Accountability at registration , regular project monitoring visits and end line will incorporate both remote based and physical interaction that includes Whats App, suggestion boxes and use of toll free numbers. Physical accountability mechanisms will be guided by COVID 19 WHO and host country guidelines in terms of social distancing, hand washing, use of face masks and recommended numbers per meeting.

9 - Environmental considerations
9.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Local stakeholders will be consulted on suitable sites for shelter construction ie. Sites not located in waterways. Protection of trees as carbon sinks will be empahasized at project inception to avoid deforestation and soil erosion.
