Project Summary

the project aims to 1) Increase access to lifesaving basic service to the
most vulnerable South Sudanese refugees in Darfur 2) Enhance the community capacity to
prevent risks, neglect, violence, trafficking and ensure effective protection for the at risk
and most vulnerable refugees/returnees populations. 3). Provision of livelihood coping
mechanisms to the most vulnerable CAR refugees to enable them produce to meet their
food demand and increase self reliance.4) provision of WASH ,Protection, health and education services to
the refugees in Eastern Sudan.

1 - Needs

The influx of Ethiopian refugee into Eastern Sudan due to the ongoing conflict in Tigrayregion, with over over 50,000 new arrivals in In Gadaref have created newhumanitarian situation that require urgent supports in all sectors. South Sudanese andCOR refugees continue to be in urgent needs for assistance in diverse sectors. althoughlarge response is ongoing, there is a significant gap in WASH, Education protection,livelihood assistance . In conflict contexts, women and girls bear thebrunt of the violence and brutality as rape, sexual abuse, sexual exploitation and othergender-based violence are common. The forms of violence and abuse encountered byminors including child marriage, child labor, child recruitment by armed groups,unaccompanied and separated children and child trafficking. Women and youth are alsothe most dynamic in seeking day to day opportunities for income to cover partial need oftheir demand which has not been satisfactorily met throughout this period; this exposesthem to risks, violence, and neglect. Provision of livelihood coping mechanisms is urgentlyneeded to help the women, girls, boys to meet their demands and contribute to self-reliance. It was observed that, most of the family’s male and females are sharing houseswith their extended relatives and accommodated in shelters built partially from localmaterials (bamboo, sticks and grass) but lacking preventive materials that protect themfrom rain. Insides the shelters, they managed to collect very few items that can assistthem for cooking, fetching and keeping water, therefore there is an urgent need for ES andNFIs. Refugees living in Umrakuba, Anteneitba, and Village 8 are in desperate situation and lack ofurgent supports not limited to water , food , education and health. Increased caseloads ofCAR refugees in Umdafug ,Alradoum areas, with the majority settled in temporary sites areposing a health risk in each of these areas, . UPO will integrate GBV risk reductionstrategies into the implementation of the activities through the consultation of women inlocating resources and services, as well as including them as members of the decision-making committees that govern these resources and services

2 - Activities/Outputs

1. Conducting training for 200 members of government institutions and local authorities (100 females and 100 males)on protection and refugee and human rights. 3. Establish and train 12 community-basedprotection committees, composed of male and female members to enable them identifyand monitor protection incidents (risks, violence, kidnapping, SGBV) 4. Provision ofAgricultural inputs and seeds to 3000 most vulnerable refugees a (1100 women and 900men) to enable them produce to meet their food demands and increase self-reliance. 5.Conducting vocational training for 500 most vulnerable women and young girls 3.Provision of IGAs to 1,000 vulnerable people (550 women and 450 men).6. provision ofWASH services to the refugees in Darfur and Eastern Sudan.7. provision of educationservices including construction of classroom, provision of learning materials, teachertraining and capacity building , incentive for the teachers etc. provision of health services to the Ethiopian refugees in Gadaref in Anteneitba refugee camps.
