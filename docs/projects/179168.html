Project Summary

The project’s main objective is to provide access to WASH basic services for 985,000 displaced people, returnees and overburdened host community at risk within the highly neglected sub-districts in the aforementioned areas Located in:
Deir Ezzor, Khasham, Tabni, Abukamal, Jalaa / Deir Ezzor Governorate
As-Sanamayn, Ash-Shajara, Da'el, Mzeireb, Tasil, Jasim, Izra’ / Dar’a Governorate
Jebel Saman, ,Zarbah, As-safira / Aleppo Governorate .
Duma, Kisweh, Nashabiyeh / Rural Damascus Governorate.
Masyaf, Muhradah/ Hama Governorate
Salkhad, Shahba, As-Sweida, Qarayya / As-Sweida Governorate
Qaryatein , Foroqles, Taldu, Ein Elniser, Tadmor / Homs Governorate
Based on HNO 2021/WASH in Homs, those sub-dsitricts Qaryatein (4) , Foroqles (3), Taldu (3), Ein Elniser (3), Tadmor (3) with severity scale (3-4). And after coordination with local authorities and municipalities, which indicate that there is a huge need to WASH services especially since the targeted sub-directorates are still witnessing a steady number of returnees, but they face great difficulties in resettlement in their communities, due to the enormous extent of destruction.
The consequences of the conflict and the outbreak of Covid-19 have been particularly severe, as many parts of different governorates have been transformed into hard-to-reach areas, where people suffer from a lack of basic water, sanitation and hygiene needs to survive, due to the massive destruction of the Wash and sanitation services. Loss of livelihoods and income, high cost of basic commodities, and a sharp devaluation of the currency. Displaced people, returnees and overburdened host communities face massive conflicts as they try to provide basic water, sanitation and supplies for their families.
By supporting WASH, water, sanitation and hygiene conditions will be improved for 985,000 people in areas with (06% to 37% high rates of displacement with severity scale (–3-5), the hygiene awareness will be improved through distribution of hygiene kits, water tanks and Garbage containers, And Rehabilitation of water and sewage networks. In addition to carrying out garbage collection campaigns and awareness campaigns.
After assessing the WASH gap, need and timing of the intervention by RSRP team with consider all vulnerability and gender groups including men, women, boys and girls, female HHS, the elderly at risk, people with disabilities, and HHs with limited access to hygiene supplies and safe drinking water, and with consider the sectors results, the number of kits, Kit components, water tanks, garbage containers, damaged water network and sewage networks, and target locations have been identified. Adding that the available budget is still not confirmed from the donor.
It should be noted that the project is in line with HNO 2021 and the Sector Strategic Objective for the Water, Sanitation and Hygiene (SO1 and SO3) and the priorities:
Strategic Objective 1: Provide life-saving humanitarian assistance to the most vulnerable people with an emphasis on those in areas with high severity of needs.
Strategic Objective 3: Increase the resilience of affected communities by improving access to livelihood opportunities and basic services, especially among the most vulnerable households and communities.

4 - Needs

The conflict has created around 12.9million people who need WASH assistance. 36%of the population is relying on unsafe water sources and alternative to piped water to meet daily water supply needs. Estimated 70%of sewage being discharged untreated and at least half of the sewerage systems are not functional, and with 12% of garbage inappropriately disposed, HNO2021.According to the conducted field assessment and in coordination with the municipalities, the current needs are 30.000 hygiene kits(4000 in Homs, 3500 in Deir Ezzor, 5000 in Dar’a, 9000 in Aleppo, 3000 in Rural Damascus, 1500 in Hama and 4000 in As-Sweida). Installation of 7.500 water tanks(1000 in Homs, 1000 in Deir Ezzor, 1500 in Dar’a, 1500 in Aleppo,1000 in Rural Damascus,1000 in Hama and 1000 in As-Sweida) Rehabilitation of 3 water networks to ensure access to safe water sources in Homs, Deir Ezzor and Aleppo. Rehabilitation of 3 sewage networks to ensure its safe disposal and prevent waterborne diseases in Homs, Deir Ezzor and Aleppo.750 garbage containers to be provided and installed in the assessed neighborhoods (150 in Homs, 75 in Deir Ezzor, 100 in Dar’a, 200 in Aleppo, 100 in Rural Damascus, 75 in Hama and 50 in As-Sweida)The COVID-19pandemic is anticipated to be continued in 2021, especially because of the low awareness of the population in targeted areas.Therefore, mainstreaming COVID-19response is crucial to mitigate risks by conducting awareness campaigns and hygiene promotions. Garbage collection campaigns in Dar’a, Aleppo and rural Damascus will be implemented where no access to public garbage disposal services and in addition to this, 2 water treatment plant stations will be rehabilitated in Aleppo and Deir Ezzor.

5 - Implementation of activities

RSRP responses to increase access WASH services for affected households in targeted areas, In line with the WASH sector’s, identification of PIN/framework of 2021 HNO to sub-district level and adhering to HRP 2021strategic objectives (SO1andSO3), RSRP planning the activities as mentioned in the cost project section, the main pillars followed to achieve the proposed responses in coordination with the MoWR, STD, WASH sector and other relevant stakeholders:-Obtaining approval in coordination with STD.-Hiring process.-Announcing about registration for hygiene kits and water tanks through local community and social media.-Identify beneficiaries by KOBO according to vulnerability selection criteria considering female HHs, elderly persons at risk, persons with disabilities, HHs living in overcrowded conditions, HHs without any income resources-Procurement process.-Distribution 30.000 WASH hygiene kits, 750 garbage containers and 7500 water tanks will be accomplished by door-to-door visits and in accordance. After a month of completing, conducting PDM through questionnaire form on KOBO to assessing beneficiaries’ satisfaction. All with considering safety measures regarding covid-19.-Conducting awareness campaign and hygiene promotion activities including distribution brochures and SMS, focus group discussion. -Rehabilitation of 4000m sewage networks and 4500m water networks after conducting a geometrical analysis and technical study.-Removal 75000m3 of garbage through garbage collecting campaigns. -Rehabilitation of 2 water treatment plants.-Regular field visits to ensure the quality of implementation.-Accountability will be guaranteed by the establishment of a complaint mechanism and regular evaluation feedback.
