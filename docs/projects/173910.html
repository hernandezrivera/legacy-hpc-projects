Project Summary

As of January 2019, Food security status of general population in the North West and South West regions, 1.5 million people are food insecure, thereof 906,461 (60%) in North West and 600,952 (40%) in South West. 312,154 people are severely food insecure (corresponding to 8% of the combined population of both regions), thereof 227,781 (72%) in the North West. Meanwhile, Food security status of IDPs is seen to be 50% of IDPs in the North West are food insecure (52,080 people) with 13% being severely food insecure (13,650 people); and 41% of IDPs in the South West are food insecure (99,876 people) with 8% being severely food insecure (19,926 people). While local communities are better off than IDPs in terms of food security, food insecure non-IDPs are significantly more in absolute terms.
More than a quarter of IDP households in South West have a poor food consumption score (FCS) compared to 16% in North West. However, the share of IDP households with an inadequate food intake (which includes both poor and borderline food consumption score) is higher in North West (around 60%) than in South West (around50%). Across regions, IDPs face high stress due to food shortage. IDP households adopt food-based and livelihood-based coping strategies that are more severe and/or with more frequency than non-IDP households. In North West, almost 40% of IDP households adopt potentially irreversible emergency coping strategies, compromising their productivity and future ability to cope with shocks.
Due to the pandemic, food insecurity has increased, due to increase price of goods. Reducing number of meals is currently the highest method of coping while others chose to borrow and also consuming stock of seeds meant for next season due to financial constraint. Restricting food for adults so that children can eat has also not solved the problem of food insecurity (result from rapid need assessment on Covid-19 pandemic by Miracle Charity Foundation). Miracle Charity Foundation intends to engage in unconditional food distribution to IDPs and local vulnerable population and cash transfer.
We intend to physically distribute in kind food items to vulnerable population with little or no access to market. Meanwhile cash transfer to those that are near to market with much security level. All aged group, sex and disability are involved. We shall be covering both south west and north west region. Divisions involve are Ndian, Kupe Muanenguba, Lebialem, Fako and Boyo. Target of 2000 for all divisions mentioned with 58% for female and rest of 42% for male population. Basic food items that will be distributed are grains and vegetable oils meanwhile some with access to market shall receive cash voucher assistance. 50% cash voucher assistance shall be considered. We shall equally extend assistance on livelihood to other persons in need.
To attained this said target, the foundation shall put down distribution mechanisms and other apportioned tasks to staffs. It will last for a period of 9 months with a total budget of 159,971USD.
