Project Summary

The Project aims to improve access to food and contribute to improving social cohesion and building resilience of vulnerable and food insecure communities affected by the Boko Haram crisis and violence in the Far North region. This project will address immediate food needs of 50,000 vulnerable people including returnees and local populations that are severely affected by Boko Haram attacks disrupting economic, agricultural, and pastoral activities in the Far North region. The implementation of livelihood activities, Community-based Participatory Planning (CBPP) sessions are carried out together with local community members (both women and men) to identify actual needs and priorities of the local communities that will support the affected populations to come up with their own response strategies and build resilience. This people-centered approach ensures the provision of effective solutions together with beneficiaries and reduce the dependency on relief.
Assistance through for FFA, will be implemented using cash modality and in-kind Food for three months. Therefore, the cash transfer value is aligned with the in-kind food ration. It considers beneficiaries' food preferences for the commodities and prices on the local market. The transfer value is calculated based on the identified food gap and is designed to assist beneficiaries to reach the recommended minimum calories intake of 2,100 Kcal per person per day. The use of cash-based transfers is expected to improve the cost-effectiveness of WFP's operations, while seizing the economic opportunities of local markets, with particular consideration for the beneficiaries’ choice and dignity.
WFP will collaborate closely with Government technical ministries, cooperating partners and communities to ensure project implementation and sustainability. Nutrition, Gender and protection issues are taken into consideration at all the stages of the project elaboration, implementation, monitoring and evaluation. Gender and protection risk analysis will be carried out and the most vulnerable will be targeted, including female-headed households, pregnant and lactating women, persons with disabilities.
Project sites will be in secure areas and at convenient distance from the homes of the beneficiaries.
To support smooth project implementation and ensure sustainability, capacity strengthening sessions will be organized for all stakeholders on gender and protection policy, project management, monitoring and nutrition.

Besoins / Needs

WFP Cameroon under the framework of the Country Strategic Plan (CSP) 2018 - 2020 is implementing a series of activities with the support of partners, to deliver food assistance to refugees and internally displaced persons g(IDPs) as well as to improve the resilience of vulnerable communities. Early recovery and resilience activities are geared towards the implementation of strategic objective (SO) 2 of the CSP which ultimately aims to achieve the Sustainable Development Goal 2 of ‘Zero Hunger’.The goal of these activities is to address immediate food needs of vulnerable individuals and households including refugees, returnees and local communities, while helping them to reinforce their livelihoods, to reduce the risk to natural disaster and to build resilience to present and future shocks. All these activities include the provision of food assistance to vulnerable persons where general food distributions have considerably reduced or stopped.
