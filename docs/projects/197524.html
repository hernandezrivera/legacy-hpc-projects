Project Summary

The conflict in the North and South West Regions of Cameroon has continued to orphan many children as young parents die or get disabled as a result of the war. As a result of the loss of a major bread winner, these war orphans face daunting challenges to pursue studies and grow effectively. Similarly, an increasing number of girls continue to be sexually exploited by armed men. Cases of NSAG occasionally approaching families and taking their daughters as labour and sex slaves within the camps have been reported. Similarly, the sexual exploitation of young girls by the military in who have been positioned in make shift barracks in rural communities where military did not have a presence are reported.
In respond most rural families in localities have displaced their daughters some below 15 years of age to the city of Bamenda, where they live as child IDPs either with friend and relative without adequate support to enrol and stay in school. These child IDPs have limited ability to manage city life especially with repeated attacks on schools and high rate of kidnappings. Our project therefore seeks to support these war orphans and other sexually abused child IDPs with academic and vocational training. The initiative shall empower them through basis safety education so they can recognize danger and take adequate safety measures. The project shall also advocate for the protection of children and schools.

1 - Besoins / Needs

The project is a respond to the educational needs of unaccompanied child IDPs especially girls who are victims of GBV in Bamenda. It is an initiative which is in its pilot phase and shall support 150 children with school kits, psychosocial support while engaging them in community safety education. Academic activities are timidly gaining grounds in Bamenda though sporadic attacks on schools can still be noticed. The intended results of the project include:- Enhance the academic development of 150 children affected by the crisis in Bamenda through the provision of school kits. The kits shall compose of tuition fee, school book. Bags and sandals for school. - Sensitized and empower the beneficiary in basic personal-security measures especially within school environment in five partner schools in Bamenda. - The project hopes to reduce vulnerability of the girls to sexual exploitation, child prostitution and sex trafficking in the project area.- Through referral and outreach campaigns, the project shall promote local cooperation and skills sharing within the humanitarian community as the initiative shall liaise with other actors in the Bamenda. - Strengthen the capacity of survivors-in-training to identify and support their peers vulnerable to trafficking. Also support them to raise awareness in their communities.
