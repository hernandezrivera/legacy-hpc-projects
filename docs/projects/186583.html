Project Summary

The International Rescue Committee (IRC) is seeking funding opportunities to implement a one-year women empowerment project in Mubi North and Michika LGAs of Adamawa State, Northeast Nigeria. The project design recognizes that majority of crisis-affected people especially women and girls have experienced violence, repeated displacement, disruption of livelihoods, and lack access to farmland due to fear of being attacked, raped, abducted, and even being killed while going to their farms or collecting firewood particularly as they travel further from their communities. With the security challenges in accessing farmland, most vulnerable IDPs and host community women are engaged in off-farm activities including groundnut processing for household consumption and for income generation.
The project will build on IRC’s previous and ongoing Women’s Protection and Empowerment (WPE) programming in the two LGAs. The IRC will identify with relevant private sector to provide locally, quality, and affordable improved technologies for groundnut processing (groundnut oil extractor and grinding machine). The technologies will add value to the groundnut and help to maximize profit as against the traditional way of processing which takes longer production processes, reduced quality, and deprived the women from carrying out other household tasks. The IRC will work with these women in groups of 15 to 25 members, provide training on how to produce high quality groundnut products such as groundnut oil, groundnut butter, groundnut bread, groundnut snacks, groundnut cake (Kuli-Kuli) etc. to meet the nutritional food need of their households and generate income. To ensure the success and sustainability of their microenterprises, the women will also be trained on business development and financial management skills. The project will target 2000 direct beneficiaries (women and adolescent girls), in 80 – 100 VSLA groups.
The group formation will be done using the VSLA (Village Savings and Loans Association) model. This enables the women to access small loans without collateral and to obtain emergency insurance through their social fund and increase their savings by engaging or expanding their businesses which will then enable them to generate income and to acquire skills on financial management. Business Development skills training will be delivered using the CEFE (Competent Economies through the Formation of Enterprises) methodology. CEFE is a comprehensive set of training instruments using an action-oriented approach and experiential learning methods to develop and enhance the business management and personal competencies of a wide range of target groups, mostly in the context of income and employment generation and economic development. The methodology’s ease of breaking down concepts makes it very suitable for training low literate populations.
Women protection will also be mainstreamed in the project, through Discussion Group Series (DGS). The Discussion group series is usually lasts for 8 – 10 weeks. These are sessions where women who are being empowered socially and economically through the project, and their male spouses sit together to discuss on how to improve women’s participation in household financial decision making and reduce intimate partner violence. Through series of activities, the DGS groups will be able to identify and explore manifestations of male privilege and women’s oppression, learn and practice non-violent conflict resolution strategies, and brainstorm ways to work towards gender equality in their families and communities.

1 - Needs

The major need the proposed project seeks to address is that of inequality in income generation capacity between men and women and also in household decision making. It will also help to mitigate the incidence of gender-based violence among intimate partners. IRC recognizes that majority of crisis-affected people especially women and girls have experienced violence, repeated displacement, disruption of livelihoods, and lack access to farmland due to fear of being attacked, raped, abducted, and even being killed while going to their farms or collecting firewood particularly as they travel further from their communities. 2 - Activities

PROJECT ACTIVITIES1.0 Village Savings and Loans Association (VSLA) Support1.1 Community Entry, Stakeholder Mapping and Community Sensitization on VSLA and other project activities: The IRC project team will visit the communities to identify relevant stakeholders and partners to ensure community participatory approach during the project implementation. Project team will ensure community participatory approach by engaging with project stakeholders on project orientation, beneficiaries’ selection, activities/items delivery and trainings.1.2 Formation of VSLAs: The IRC project teams will support targeted beneficiaries to form Village Savings and Loans Associations (VSLA) with membership of 15 to 25 persons per association or group. Through support with VSLA kits and knowledge transfer on group cohesion, the groups will serve to increase access to highly responsive and safe financial services.1.3 Training of VSLA members and continuous support: The IRC project team will provide real time training and support to the VSLAs, within the first three months of the formation of the groups. Within this period project team members and/or IRC incentive workers will be available to support the groups in their weekly meetings. Thereafter, the visits can reduce to bi-weekly. 2.0 Income Generation Activities (IGA)2.1 Conduct market assessment for groundnut value chain: IRC will carry out a market and context analysis of groundnut value chain in the intervention communities. 2.2 Training of IRC project staff and community-based incentive workers on groundnut oil extraction technology: IRC will engage the services of an expert consultant to train project staff and community-based incentive workers on groundnut oil extraction technology, as well as on the preparation of different groundnut recipes. 2.3 Step-down training of groundnut value chain approach technology to beneficiaries through demonstration: Trained community-based incentive workers, under the supervision of project staff will step down the trainings to the women in their various groups. 3.0 GBV Survivor Support and Discussion Group Series3.1 Gender-based violence (GBV) survivor care and support: GBV survivors will receive appropriate care and support that meet their immediate needs during and after the referral.3.2 Discussion Group Series (DGs) for VSLA members (women) and their spouses: The IRC project team will engage the spouses of the VSLA members in discussion group series to ensure resources are jointly used and women are engaged in decision making at the household level4.0 Business Development Skills training4.1 Training of IRC project staff and community-based incentive workers on business development skills using the CEFE (Competent Economies through Formation of Enterprises) methodology: IRC will engage the services of a certified CEFE master trainer to train project staff and community-based incentive workers on the methodology4.2 Step-down training on business development skills using CEFE methodology5.0 In-kind Support for Start-up5.1 Distribution of groundnut value addition business start-up input to beneficiaries: In-kind distribution of start-up inputs including grinding machine and locally fabricated groundnut oil extraction machine to each of the groups. At individual beneficiary level, 20kg of groundnuts, oil collection buckets & jerrycans, frying pans, and personnel protective wears for food handling – face masks, transparent hand gloves, apron vests, and headgears. 6.0 Market Linkages6.1 Meeting with external wholesalers, retail traders and farmers in the groundnut value chain in locations of project implementation: The project team will organize individual and joint meetings with key actors in groundnut value chain within the project locations, to have a deeper understanding of the market system. 6.2 Linkage of women supported with VSLA/Groundnut value chain to external whole retail traders and farmers
