Project Summary

The World Vision Zimbabwe COVID 19 project seeks to raise awareness and improve infection prevention and control of the corona virus pandemic in five districts of Zimbabwe through distribution of hygiene promotion messages, distribution of hygiene kits and improve availability of hand washing facilities and other WASH services at health care facilities. These activities will benefit 141 548 people in Beitbridge, Marondera, Mutoko, Murehwa and Hwedza districts.

Plan fields

1 - Activities

1. Distribution of hygiene kits that include soap and disinfectants to households. This will also include disinfectants for markets and public places in the districts.2. Using platforms such as social media, radio, television and public address systems such as loud hailers to give hygiene promotion messages.3. Provision of hand washing stations for use at quarantine centres, isolation centres, schools, health care facilities, markets and other public places.4. To improve water supply systems and provide for improved waste management at health care facilities.

The success of the project will be measured through tracking of achievement of the following indicators.1. Number of hygiene kits distributed.2. Number of vulnerable people reached with hygiene kits dis aggregated to men and women.3. Number of people reached with hygiene messages dis aggregated to numbers of girls, boys, women and men and also by age group.4. Number of hand washing facilities distributed.5. Number of health care facilities supported with water systems and waste management.
