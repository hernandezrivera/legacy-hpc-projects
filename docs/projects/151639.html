Project Summary

Populations exposed to the floods or any form of a disaster also face increased protection concerns, especially in relation to housing, land and property rights, Gender Based Violence and family separation compounded by the challenges in accessing populations directly in flood affected areas. To response the above situation, WARDI will scale up GBV prevention & response through three interrelated sets of activities: prevention, response (survivor assistance) and coordination among partners. 1) The prevention activities include; training Community Safety & Awareness Mobilizers in approaches and methods to proper survivor centered care, raising awareness in communities about available confidential services, referral networks and dignity kit distributions. 2) The response activities of the project include; provision of medical services quality to women and girls who are survivors of GBV through three GBV one stop centers in (Belet Weyn Hospital, Wanla Weyn and Mogadishu). WARDI will also apply the following principles "privacy, confidentiality, safety of survivors and family” while providing medical assistance to GBV survivors.3) Since WARDI is focal point of Hiran GBV sub cluster, the organization will strengthen GBV coordination at national and Hiran sub cluster WG to improve coordination among partners, reporting and ensure that mainstreaming of protection across cluster and partners in the Hiran region. Furthermore, the project will also improve the capacity of national staff (8 female ,7male) on CMR as well improve the capacity Internal displaced people (IDP) and poor host community on GBV prevention methods. The project will target a total of 30000 people with lifesaving GBV services including medical support, psychosocial support, awareness on GBV prevention and capacity building for case workers and community members on GBV prevention and response, the project is complementary with WASH, health and Nutrition interventions that WARDI is implementing in the targeted locations. The target will include; 21,000 IDPs, 9,000 Host community, 21,000 Female, 9,000 male, 20,600 children, 9,400 Adult and 900 Elderly in Hiran (Belet Weyne), Lower Shabelle (Wanla Weyn) and Banadir (Mogadishu).

Needs

According to to HNO 2018, At least 3.6 million people are in need of protection support services, gender-based violence (GBV), such as rape and sexual assault, due to conflict and protracted displacement as well as natural hazards. Settlements are often ungoverned or governed by gatekeepers; overcrowded and displaced people have limited access to protective shelter, safe water and sanitation facilities as well as other basic needs. The majority of those affected are women and children. Protection risks are increasing and the need to centralize protection throughout response activities to the most vulnerable is vital. The lack of rule of law, pervasive protection violations, including forced evictions, gender-based violence and exploitation, remain widespread and continue to affect the most vulnerable groups, especially women and girls, people with disabilities, the elderly and minority groups. Provide skills development (beauty salon) for 100 female headed households in Banadir region. Activity 7. Organize community awareness session on FGM practices targeting 200 elders and religious and TBA.

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

Insecurity and conflict due to insurgency and clan conflict is now one of the main causes for displacement. Drought, floods and cyclone are also the root cause of the displacement in Somalia. Somalia faces a dire humanitarian situation, having endured drought and famine as well as continued fighting and heavy rains, all of which have aggravated the conditions of IDPs. People denied the rights of the IDPs as human being and they are facing evictions, rape and looting due to the fragility of the central government who was under their responsibility to protect them. Most of the IDPs left behind their livelihood assets and displaced to the big towns with empty hands. Thus, the IDPs have the right to receive necessary service, such as protection. Unfortunately, conflict sometimes is preventing humanitarian agencies from delivering assistance to the IDPs. In this project, WARDI will try to reduce the burden of IDPs (women and children suffer most) and bring the services their WASH requirement to their settlements, in order to reduce the risk of GBV against women/girls for searching needed services. WARDI will also include the IDPs (giving women IDPs their share) to the established committees for the project implementation to get their views and to be heard their complains if arise. The IDP rights will be availed in the course of the project implementation.

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

The overall goal of the proposed project is to promote protection concerns of women, men, girls and boys including reducing GBV against women and girls. Safety and security measures will be taken into account by close consultation with women and people with disabilities during the project design like latrines to be constructed close to their settlements and distribution of water inside their settlements. In this project women, men, girls and children will give equal opportunity to take part the project design before its implementation and also to be equally heard their voices. Women, men and marginalized communities will equally share the members of the Project committees and also the project staff. All men, women, boys, girls and disables will equal have access to services offered in this project.

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

Security in Somalia is dynamic and the districts security focal point will analyze security situation and advice the frontline staff the situation every morning. It is anticipated that the security will remain calm and allow smooth implementation of activities within the project time frame. WARDI will identify the risk of conflict in the project area and immediately avert this risk, as water and pasture are important commodity to the drought affected community and conflict between neighboring clans starts the management of those resources, WARDI will closely engage the local leaders, government officials and the community to sort out any possible threat and the community prominent members as cross-cutting will be trained on Disaster Risk Reduction and conflict resolution mechanism measures. WARDI is currently implementing WASH, food security, Education, Nutrition and Health projects in Hiran, Banadir and L/Shabelle regions with out any security problem, although sometimes there is security issues in L/Shabelle region. Safety and security for the project staff and beneficiaries are our top priority, there will be security briefing every day during staff meeting to minimize risk. The project will be implemented in accessible villages/camps where the community settles to reduce risk of walking long distance to women and girls. Rapid needs assessment and technical assessments were done in the target locations by WARDI staff without security challenges.
