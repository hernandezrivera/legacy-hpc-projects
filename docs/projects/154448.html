Project Summary

SSORD is aiming to implement the three Child Protection community centers to improved child protection services in conflict affected areas in Anbar (Ana, Rawa & Haditha).
Our organization has conducted rapid assessments carried out in Anbar Governorate included focusing on Child Protection, we observed that large numbers of children under 18 years living under bad psychosocial, physical and health situation.
The locations have selected for this project to implement child protection programming is chosen based on coordination with the CP sub-cluster after identifying the urgent needs and gaps in those areas.

Needs

The humanitarian situation in Iraq is one of the most severe and rapidly deteriorating crises in the world which left 1,283,292 Returnee & 69,960 IDPs living in various places in Anbar. The children and their families suffer a lot at this stage because some of them are still displaced and others were returned from the crisis of displacement to the areas of return which are needing much to be rehabilitated, therefore they need an interest in the psychosocial support and provision of basic services. Child protection, in particular, sexual violence, is widespread and one of the largest crises emanating from the current conflict. Current CP service provision is insufficient to meet the needs of survivors with substantive gaps in the availability of CP case management and psychosocial support (PSS) services across Anbar, especially care for child survivors. Children meeting the following vulnerability criteria will be targeted: children showing signs of severe trauma and distress (selective autism, persistent anger or withdrawal, bed-wetting etc.); children who have survived grave violations (sexual violence, child recruitment, abduction or maiming); children with disabilities; children with abusive, neglectful or exploitative parents/caregivers; child survivors of GBV (physical, sexual and psychosocial ); and children who have been tortured. Assessment Sources. 1- DTM of IOM (Report October 30, 2018) 2- Our organization assessment (Since 2016 our organization has worked in the field of a child protection continuously as a partner with UNICEF in Al Anbar governorate and its districts, and we have a good understanding of the reality of children situation on the ground and what are the children need there).

Activities

1- Fundamental CP Interventions to reach the children and youth with structured PSS programs (Resilience & DEALs or Child/ Youth Resilience, or equivalent evidence-based PSS modules). 2- Case management service provision for vulnerable children including legal support, to include legal advices and obtaining legal documentation for children. 3- Engaging the community to act as actors to advocate for Child Protection principles and child rights. 4- Establishing referral pathway in all targeted locations and strengthening the already existed ones in coordination with CP- Sup cluster. 5- Raising awareness on CP issues and Child right. 5- Target the community based approach (child protection committee or assisting child club/ youth clubs which aims to reduce and respond to child protection risks). 6- Target the parents in parenting module by training on Child Resilience module or Adolescent Toolkit.

Priority / Category

High

Protection Risk Analysis

1- Some camps do not allow beneficiaries the freedom of movement according to the need of the program, which puts them at risk in not implementing these instructions. 2- Some societal norms and traditions prevent the beneficiaries from speaking frankly and openness sometimes poses a real danger to them such as sexual assaults and others.

Mitigation Measures

1- Our organization will recruit the staff from the targeted regions and in the situation of closing the roads will not be a hindrance in the implementation of activities in the timing of the plans. 3- As mentioned in our activities, we will work to ensure that people will have a good understanding of child right and protection and we will engaging the community to act as actors to advocate for Child Protection principles and child rights.2- In all project activities with children and their families, we will work according to the principle of confidentiality especially those working on case management. Our organization have professional staffs in case management, so we can ensure that staff will treat this information in a discreet and confidential manner.

How is your project accountable to affected population

Our project accountable to affected population with the principles of (Neutrality, Transparence, Confidentiality and Non-discrimination). The project team will be working to ensure that the beneficiaries will know very well the services which must be provided to them in the project and clarifying the material possibilities of the project Affected people will participants in the result of the assessments, monitoring, and implementation, the project team will review the feedback of the affected people to check if the project activities have a response to their needs. This is done through direct interviews with them and receiving complaints and follow up the results and the project activities performance.
