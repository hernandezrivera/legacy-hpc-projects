Project Summary

In the context of Covid-19 border closures have meant families are often stuck and inneed of humanitarian assistance and children often face stigmatization, social exclusion,violence and extreme poverty, especially returnees. In Guatemala, hurricanes ETA & IOTAleft unprecedented damages, with nearly 500 schools affected and a growing number ofstudents without access to formal or non-formal education, delaying school reopening in2021. Through this action UNICEF will create opportunities for children exposed to highlevels of violence in communities that include returnees or displaced families, through theprovision of multiple pathways to education, access to quality learning materials and
psychosocial support that facilitate staying in school. UNICEF will include the provision ofWASH in schools to ensure a safe “back to school”. UNICEF will work to reintegratechildren that have been out of school in 2020 and prevent their school desertion onceschools open. For children, C-19 has essentially meant the loss of one year of learning.Urgent action is paramount to ensure access to education for the most vulnerable childrencurrently at risk of losing a second year of education. This would have a devastatingimpact on their right to learn and thrive, and children will face increased exposure toviolence both if their families stay or embark on a dangerous migration route. Thedepartments of Alta Verapaz, Izabal and Quiché are among the most impacted by Eta andIota. With schools reopening school´s repairs are urgent, especially with regards to safeWASH facilities which are mandatory for reopening. Through the proposed action UNICEF´s support to the Ministry of Education will increase the number of students that benefi tfrom a restored school infrastructure and WASH facilities an ensure opportunities foreducation continuity.

1- Project Activities

Provide opportunities for safe reinsertion and quality continuity of Education for out-of-school, returned migrant and other at-risk children and adolescents, through the access tosafe and protective emergency services by:1) Ensure the access to Education inEmergency services to children at risk and on the move with fl exible modalities of formaland non-formal education for continuous distance learning and support for socialintegration through the development of alternative educational opportunities.2) Provideessential teaching and learning materials to ensure quality and continuity strategies forinclusive education in emergency services. Minimum packages of learning as well asteaching aids/materials will be provided to students including textbooks, teaching guidesand stationery according to the different education and grades modalities.3) Ensureessential WaSH in Schools (WiS) that will contribute to the elimination of physical andadministrative barriers to the reopening of schools in Central America through low-costcritical WASH facilities rehabilitation such as for hand washing points, toilets, watersystems storage and/or purifi cation, among others.4) Support non-specialized ChildProtection services delivery to students and education personnel, through the provision oftrainings, guidelines and tools for the prevention, opportune detection, delivery of PSS andreferral to local and specialized services to address the suffering due to traumas, stigmaand discrimination.5) implement of multi-level communication for development (C4D)model, which would combineadvocacy and community actions in support of what areconsidered the most suitable fl exible and alternative learning and teaching modalities forthe targeted population groups.
