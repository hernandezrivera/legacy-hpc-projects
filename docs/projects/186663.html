Project Summary

Tdh plans to implement a 9 months project which focuses on the provision of life-saving WASH support to vulnerable populations most in need in Gwoza and Kala Balge’ local governorates. In this frame, Tdh intends to support a total of 54,662 individuals. This project will build upon Tdh’s experience providing WASH services in the targeted areas and contribute to the overall objective of the Nigeria humanitarian response strategy plan to save lives by providing timely and integrated multi-sector assistance and protection interventions to the most vulnerable, IDPs, returnees, and host communities. The project will strive to increase and improve access to safe and equitable water, sanitation, and hygiene thereby contributing to saving lives, improving the health, safety, and dignity of the affected population. The action will also contribute to prevent and respond cholera outbreak in the targeted areas.
In water quality and quantity, water facilities will be both constructed and rehabilitated to increase safe water access. Both bucket and batch chlorination of water will be done at specific water points to ensure safe water. In sanitation both institutional and household latrines will be constructed and rehabilitated. Also showers at both institutional and household will be constructed and rehabilitated. The household facilities will first target the most vulnerable community members. Hygiene promotion dissemination will be done to increase positive behavior change among the community members. Hand washing facilities will be fabricated and installed. Soaps will be procured and distributed to ensure hand washing is effectively done. Environmental sanitation kits, latrine cleaning kits, menstrual hygiene management kits, hygiene kits and cholera kits will be distributed to communities. Preposition of cholera kits will be done so that they will be used for emergency interventions during the rainy season.

1 - Needs

Rann (Kala-Balge Local Government Area), composed of 37,842 individuals, is a landlocked location suffering from massive humanitarian needs since 2016. In Nigeria (ranked at 8.5 in the Children’s Climate Risk Index), Rann is one of the most isolated locations prone to flooding resulting from the rainy season, punctual water release from Cameroonian dam which results in cutting the road access and regular harassment by insurgents (latest attack on October 28th, 2021). The attack led by a Non-State Armed Group took control of the entire city for a few hours on August 30th, 2021 (resulted in displacement of 1,769 individuals to Cameroon). All these factors make of Rann one of the most challenging areas operationally in humanitarian contexts in Borno State. Heightened access constrains and annual flooding that destroys hectares of cultivated fields, hinders road access from Ngala to Rann during the rainy season (road access only from December to June in best case scenario and from February to June at worst) and put the population at very high-risk WASH-related diseases prevalence. The round 37 of the IOM DTM, indicates that the humanitarian situation in most of Nigeria’s north-eastern states (Borno included) is currently approaching famine levels, characterized by high food insecurity, malnutrition, and exposure to disease outbreaks. As for WASH particularly, boreholes are the main safe water sources in Rann where over 63% are hand pump operated while 37% are solar powered. Among all the water sources, 16% are not functional. As such, access to safe water is still a challenge in Rann, with an access to safe water which stands at 22.5l/P/D (Gap Matrix July 2021). Following the end of the Access to sanitation facilities especially latrines is challenging. In Klagaru host community, the gap is standing at 147 units of latrines. There has been partial vandalism of sanitation facilities, including theft of doors and iron sheets were stolen, while other sanitation facilities were blown by wind during the rainy season. There is need to increase hygiene promotion through organising environmental campaigns and cleaning sessions. Klagaru village has the highest needs for hygiene promotion as it has 15,260 people in need of hygiene promotion and hygiene material.In addition, Pulka in Gwoza LGA of Borno state is not also left out of the negative impact of armed conflict suffered due to insurgency activities. This has seen humanitarian actors having less or no activities in certain areas. WASH sector gap analysis of October 2021 shows that pulka town especially host communities have high needs in access to sanitation facilities especially in the communities of Bulabulin, Kiva and Balangele. Kiva and Balangele are the worse served having a total population of 16,820 individuals (DTM31). In both the areas, the percentages access to sanitation facilities is at 0% while the percentage access to hygiene kits in 90 days stands at 0%. Thus, in both the locations, the latrine gap stands at 490 and 351 while the people in need (latrine, hygiene promotion and hygiene kits) are at 9796 and 7024 respectively. The massive needs are a clear indicator that most of the humanitarian actors are mainly focusing on the camps population. It is important to keep on emphasizing on hygiene promotion as Pulka is just recovering from a major cholera outbreak which struck the area on 13th September 2021 as per the Borno state Ministry of Health. Hence this calls for more hands and efforts in terms hygiene messages dissemination, water quality monitoring and safe disposal of human waste.

2 - Activities

Through the proposed project, Tdh will carry out water quality monitoring of all water points in Rann thereby serving all the population of the town (37,842 individuals). Tdh will ensure all that is required to undertake daily chlorination of the 50 water points (such as supplies and materials) ensuring free chlorine level tested attains the WHO standard of (0-2-0.5) ppm, part per minutes at all time. Routine operation and maintenance of all the water points will be conducted. To do so, quick-moving parts of handpumps will be procured and prepositioned to be utilized in fixing all the failed sections of water points. Prepositioning will ensure maintenance at all times even when access to Rann by road is hindered during the rainy season due to flooding. To enhance increased access to sanitation, Tdh will procure materials for rehabilitation and construction of sanitation facilities (latrines and showers), selects, and engage community artisans that will carry out the construction and rehabilitation of the sanitation facilities under the supervision and guidance of Tdh WASH technical engineers. In addition, for Tdh to ensure proper and effective utilization of the services and infrastructures provided, Tdh will conduct intensive hygiene promotion messaging and hygiene kits distribution. For an effective and appropriate response to the affected population, Tdh team is composed of a WASH coordinator, a WASH program manager, a WASH deputy project manager and two WASH supervisors (segregated into hygiene promotion supervisor and WASH technical supervisor) who are further assisted by hygiene promotion assistant and WATSAN assistant which work directly at field level with the team of hygiene promotion volunteers (65) and water point chlorinators (105). 8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

Tdh being a protection orientated organization, it will be ensured that all WASH infrastructure and services implemented in this program will specifically consider relevant gender issues, especially gender-based violence. In addition, all WASH infrastructures will be accessible for Persons With Disabilities (PWD) and elderly people using the sector technical guidelines for designs and implementation. In addition, the community leaders, men, women boys, and girls will be informed about the activities through community entry meetings. Special attention will be paid to gender equality in all the program teams, both at the international and national levels. At the community level, specific attention will be dedicated to ensuring that WASH committees and other relevant community-based structures respect gender ratios and include all members of the community, including elders and youth. In addition, Tdh will sensitize the community leaders and the community members on the dangers associated with PSEA, and indicate the procedure of reporting. All Tdh staff, volunteers including vendors will sign the code of conduct and child safeguarding policy to protect children from violence, abuse, exploitation, and violence. Furthermore, the address concern@tdh.ch will be diffused among affected population and communities to receive complaints on staff misconduct, any discriminatory practices, corruption or abuse of power cases, lack of quality of an activity or product delivered, lack of communication and information, and any potential negative effect or harm caused by the project.
