Project Summary

This project aims to provide integrated FSL services. The project will support 2000 beneficiaries in 10 communities in Afrin and Ariha districts through the following activities:
1- Supporting barley value chain of 400 farmers who have access to 5 planted donum by distributing:
- Fertilizer (75 kg per farmer).
- Cash irrigation voucher with value 50$.
- Cash harvesting voucher with value 50$.
And purchasing about 70 % of the final product of the total farmers, which is equivalent to 360 tons of barley grains and 180 tons of straw. While 30 % to be used for next season.
That fodder will be distributed to 1200 livestock breeders, enough for three months of feeding:
- 360 tons of barley grains will be distributed to 800 sheep breeders. Every breeder will receive 450 kg for one time (enough for feeding 5 sheep for 3 months).
- 180 tons of barley straw will be distributed to 400 cow breeders. Every breeder will receive 450 kg for one time (enough as filler fodder for 1 cow for 3 months).
The selection criteria of farmers are (have access to at least 5 donum of land planted with barley crop, have no access to fixed monthly income, are not benefiting from similar support, pledge to sell 70 % of the final products to the IHR). high priority will be given to smallholders.
The selection criteria of animals breeders are (sheep breeder has from 5 to 50 sheep, cow breeder has from 1 to 3 cow, have no access to fixed monthly income, are not benefiting from similar support). high priority will be given to breeders who own fewer animals.
3- Empowering 400 women by training on food processing, to be by two parts:
i) Supporting 200 women by:
- Holding 12 training sessions including theoretical and practical parts (for 4 months).
- Distributing 200 food processing tools-vouchers with value 150 $ per woman. Those assets can be used sustainably in future small projects by women
- Distributing 200 raw materials-vouchers with value 200 $ per woman divided into 4 rounds/months. These vouchers will be used to produce food that comes from vegetables, fruits, milk (high priority for our livestock BNFs), or other required food items. That small grant aims to encourage women to start their small business projects.
- Establishing 5 women groups and building their capacity to be able to work as a team by producing processed foods and cooking meals in joint food processing workshops and selling them to markets. To participate in supporting women' groups, IHR will hand over the training tools to women's groups’ members to be assets of their sustainable projects
ii) Supporting other 200 women by:
Holding 12 training sessions including theoretical and practical parts (for 4 months) by using the same assets for an extra round of training. Every woman trainee receives 5 $ per attendance day as cash for training (transportation and refreshment).
Training will be done by the first groups' members under IHR supervision. Which is an opportunity to consolidate the gained knowledge.
The selection criteria of those women are (women with age 18 to 40 years, have no access to fixed monthly income, are not participating in similar training projects, have the willingness and the ability to participate in the training sessions, pledge to come to the training canter to attend all training sessions). High priority will be given to female-headed households.
The required fund under this project is planned for the years 2022 and 2023. Since it is not secured and IHR planning to secure the fund through advocacy with possible donors.

10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Base on IHR PSEA policy and before starting project interventions, Ihsan RD will conduct a SEA risk assessment to identify all potential SEA risks such as :1- Risk ofSEA act by non-PSEA trained staff while providing the support to BNFs, 2-BNFs do not report SEA cases for IHR staff, which results in the IHR being unable to investigate these cases and provide the needed assistance to them. Mitigation: After an in-depth assessment for these risks, IHR through collaboration with PSEA focal point will be designed the mitigation plan for all these risks as follows Mitigation measures: 1- IHR will ensure that all its staff had signed a code of conduct to prevent acts of SEA by its staff and all recruited staff should attend PSEA training before any contact with BNFs.2- Beneficiary awareness-raising (Beneficiaries must be aware of: what SEA is, what their rights are in regard to SEA, what they can do if SEA occurs 3- Establish safe and accessible community reporting channels to report SEA complaints tool will be also available (e.g. complaints box/telephone hotline to deal with these cases).
