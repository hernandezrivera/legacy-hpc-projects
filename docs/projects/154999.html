Project Summary

'An average of 5.6 million people in Niger are unable to fully meet their food and nutrition needs because of insufficient food availability associated with inadequate production, security constraints, demographic growth and other factors. Of these, about 2.65 million are affected year-on-year. The global acute malnutrition rate of 15.3 percent and the stunting rate of 45.2 percent exceed the World Health Organization’s threshold for a “critical” nutrition situation. In addition.
Conflict in neighbouring countries is also resulting in increasing threats at the borders with Mali, Libya, and Nigeria, and negatively impacts Niger’s security, economy, migration, humanitarian needs and long-term development.
The country ranks 187th out of 188 in the 2017 Human Development Index, with a poverty rate of 44.1 percent. Widespread poverty, demographic pressure, economic shocks combined with persistent gender inequalities, limited national budgets for food security, and constraints on national capacities significantly exacerbate food insecurity. All these factors impede development and adequate government response.
