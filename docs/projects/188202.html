Project Summary

An estimated 6.9 million people (97% children) need humanitarian education assistance
based on HNO summary 2022. The sector is not able to provide a 2021 estimate of out of
school children, however, in 2020 an estimated 2.45 million children were out of school
and 1.6 million children were at risk of dropping out3. Due to the cumulative effect of the
conflict, the closure of learning facilities, and/or reduced learning hours due to COVID-19,
worsening economic conditions, increased population numbers and displacement, the
numbers of children out of school, and children who are at risk of dropping out, almost
certain to increase.
HF works to strengthen the capacity of the education facilities in Northern Syrian and
enhance the possibility of delivering better spaces for learning in schools to formal and
non-formal education for crisis-affected children aged 5-17 years This designed project is
to enhance the overall status of schools through repairing to return to their schools or
enhancing the current condition of schools.
HF will rehabilitate 40 damaged schools in Afrin and Dana.
400 class room and 20000 beneficiaries will benefit from this.
The rehabilitation activities will include repairing damaged classrooms and WASH
facilities; sanitation systems, bathrooms, and adequate water for personal hygiene and to
clean toilets rely on INEE standards. After rehabilitation of the schools, the project will
provide furniture; such as desks, chairs, blackboards.
HF has extensive experience in rehab works for damaged building and schools, more than
6000 Houses was rehabilitated through teams of HF funded by many donors, where SCHF
is one of them. in addition, HF rehabilitated about 9 schools in the Syrian crises were
damaged in Southern Syria.
HF will implement the rehabilitation work for those 40 schools in Afrin and Idleb in order
to provide a better place for the students to have proper, safer, and more adequate
education. Those schools are meant to be run by Syrian NGOs that are providing
education services while they can not provide rehab work. In this context, HF will provide
needed services as rehab work starting from construction works and WASH facilities, not
to forget providing proper furniture for those schools.
special attention will be given to children with disabilities through providing needed
facilities for children with disabilities regarding ramps that facilitate their entrance to the

5 - Implementation of activities

HF will rehabilitate 40 damaged schools. we will target the schools in (Aleppo/Afrindistrict- Idlib Dana) where its severity scale 5 (based on HNO2021).Schools will berepaired to targeted areas that promote equity and the physical safety of learners,teachers, and other education personnel. Schools will be designed giving careful thoughtto who uses the learning space. Spaces will be appropriate to sex, age, physical ability,and cultural considerations of all users. Entrances and exits will be repaired to allowstudents, teachers, and other education personnel to exit safely in an emergency.rehabilitation activities will include repairing damaged classrooms, roofs, doors, andWASH facilities; such as sanitation systems, bathrooms (Girls, boys, and CWD), solidwaste disposals facilities, drainage facilities, and providing adequate water for personalhygiene and cleaning toilets rely on INEE standards. Sanitation facilities will be accessiblefor PWD and will maintain privacy, dignity, and safety. Toilet doors will lock from theinside.To prevent sexual harassment and abuse, separate toilets for boys/men andgirls/women will be located in safe, convenient, and easily accessible places. Afterrehabilitation of the schools, the project will provide furniture; such as desks, chairs,blackboards, and laboratory equipment.
