Project Summary

This project is going to improve the safe and equal access to quality inclusive education services in a protective and clean environment in66 schools to 41192 where 39566(179016boys & 216656girls) of them are school-aged children (6-17 years) including 980CWD in Salqin, Darkosh, Dana - Maaret Tamsrin, ِAriha ,Ehsim, and Idleb sub-districts In Idlib governorate, in addition to Afrin, and Janadaris sub-district in Aleppo governorate, where targeted locations are considered as high severity (4, 5) as mention in HNO2023 draft. That will be done through conducting light repair to 342 classrooms, and198 wash facilities in 66schools, in addition to constructing 66 gender-sensitive and disability-sensitive WASH facilities, supplying 66 schools with new furniture, doing maintenance to the old furniture, providing 66 schools with safety equipment (fire extinguishers and safety equipment), provide 66 schools with operational cost (drinkable water - stationery for school personnel cleaning tools & materials, fuel, heaters). Providing formal education services to 36092 children (16235 boys &19857 girls) enrolled in formal education in 66 schools using SIG curriculum,and non-formal education services to3474 children (450 OOSC children in catch-up classeswho will be transferring to formal education using the curriculum(B) &3024remedial classes to children are at risk of dropping out and behind in their education using the adopted Syrian curriculum physically with preventive measures of Covid-19, Provide 66 schools with cleaning and disinfection materials and conduct weekly and monthly cleaning as a preventive measure for Covid-19.
Contracting with teachers and supporting staff (66 head of schools, 1463formal, 163non-formal teachers, 66 cleaners and66 security guards, and 17 supervisors), and pay their incentives, building their capacities in different areas including (Distance Learning & COVID-19 preventive measures - Child Protection Principles - Modern Teaching Techniques - Integration of children with disabilities in the education response - Child safeguarding).
Printing and distributing copies of 3474(450 copies of curriculum(B)to the children in catch-up classes and 3024copies of the adopted Syrian curriculum to the children in remedial classes).
Procuring and distributing 41192 stationery kits (school bags & stationery) to 41192 children in 66 schools, and 66sets of teaching materials to 66 schools.
Conducting Back to learning BTL campaigns and awareness campaigns for children and their caregivers, parents, teachers, and community key members, conducting training on safety and evacuation for 1626(teachers, supporting staff), and 41192children in the schools, conducting structured PSS and life skills for all students in formal and non-formal education. conduct monthly PTA/SMC meetings with the parents in 66 schools.
contracting with 66 PSS workers to provide the PSS sessions and activities in the targeted locations,
provide awareness -sessions and preventive measures on Cholera,and Covid-19 and hygiene protocols to the children and caregivers, support the mid and end-year exams in the 66 schools, conduct ASER and provide awareness sessions to the staff and PTA members on PSEA during the academic year.
As well, sadad will focus on mainstreaming the principles of child protection, and GBV by conducting awareness-raising sessions by sadad's protection team.

8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Risk: organization’s Staff, volunteers, or associated people use their power or influence to sexually exploit or abuse the people they should be serving. Mitigation and Prevention measures: 1. all staff, volunteers, and associated personnel sign the SHO's code of conduct which includes PSEA issues. 2. all staff, volunteers, and associates receive induction and (where relevant) annual refresher training on PSEA, and staff members are aware of their obligation to report SEA/misconduct. 3. HR and relevant hiring bodies check employment history during interview and questions related to PSEA asked during the interview.4. criminal records check is conducted where possible and new employees sign contracts which include PSEA and the organization code of conduct.5. complaints mechanisms developed to receive and respond to reports of SEA with train all SHO's staff, employees, volunteers, and contractors on these mechanisms and tools to make sure they are aware of how and when to use it. 6. The awareness-raising sessions on PSEA will be conducted for children by classroom teachers or schools' management. In case any violates the CoC/CSG; SHO will follow the investigation procedures in order to take the appropriate disciplinary actions based on OB’s CSG - PSEA policy.


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

- Risks, Assumptions, and Mitigating measuresTargeted schools locations could be attacked by bombing or airstrike, Mitigation:SHO safety and security field team will conduct training, awareness-raising, and drills appropriate for the age of the involved students in addition to the staff. Topics include self-care, first aid, mine risk, and unexploded ordinance awareness, fire drills, evacuation drills, and stay-and-shelter drills. Cholera ,COVID-19 pandemic: Mitigation: SHO has developed policies to be followed due to COVID-19 , Cholera including prevention measurements, conducting hygiene awareness, providing Schools with cleaning materials, dividing students into shifts in case the school is not able to apply the social distance between the students, in addition to following the recommendation of WoS re-opening schools. Re-Closure of learning facilities: Mitigation: Transform the physical learning to distance learning throughout SHO learning platform, train the education staff on the distance learning modalities methods, and approaches.
