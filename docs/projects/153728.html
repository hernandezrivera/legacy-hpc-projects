Project Summary

In recent years, especially since the passing of the NGO Act 2016 and the proliferation of State and National-level operational requirements, NGOs have become increasingly the target of requests for fees, authorisations or taxes in relation to their operations, staff and equipment. These processes/ activities by authorities can sometimes provide assistance and facilitation to operations, however there are many more requests which detract from humanitarian activities and can lead to difficulties aid workers when compliance is not forthcoming. The NGO Forum is a membership body of over NGOs provides a unique platform to collaborate and share information among humanitarian actors working in South Sudan. The NGO Forum also serves the purpose of reinforcing capacity of National NGOs for improved and effective delivery of humanitarian services, sharing information and best practices, internal networking of NGOs for collective voices and joint representation to create an enabling environment for the INGOs and NNGOs in South Sudan. The NGO Forum project aims to effectively support the NGO community in South Sudan through information sharing on the security situation, trends and risks, representing member organisations at various fora, take the lead on advocacy and to create an enabling environment, and to coordinate with other actors such as the Government, UNOCHA and UNMISS. Therefore, this project will provide support to 214 national and 116 international NGOs who are the members of NGO Forum that supports its members to effectively respond to the humanitarian and development needs in South Sudan. All member organisations provide assistance to the South Sudanese people regardless of ethnic background, political affiliation, or religious belief.

Needs

In recent years, especially since the passing of the NGO Act 2016 and the proliferation of State and national-level operational requirements, NGOs have become increasingly the target of requests for fees, authorisations or taxes in relation to their operations, staff and equipment. These processes/ activities by authorities can sometimes provide assistance and facilitation to operations, however there are many more requests which detract from humanitarian activities and can lead to lack of understanding and confusion for aid workers when compliance is not forthcoming. In some cases this has led to organisations being delayed in some areas of the country, or their staff recruitment being delayed. In addition of these impediments, where direct intervention from the NGO Forum, OCHA or national-authorities would help resolve the issue, there have also been many instances where there is contradictions in advice, requesting information, confiscating assets or various groups detaining staff. In these case there have been instances whereby the national authorities in Juba cannot influence their local counterparts. It would benefit NGOs to have a resource for mobilising the relevant NGO Forum, UN, or government authorities to address State-level issues impacting NGO operations in a timely manner. This could prevent unlawful detentions of aid workers, the confiscation of project-assets and threats or intimidation against service-providers and better coordination and acceptance with government. Coordination is often the key element missing which leads to misunderstandings between NGOs, UN agencies and government, and, inevitably, a reduction in the available services for beneficiaries. National NGOs often lack the networks to engage with relevant coordination mechanisms, obtain information and advocate for their beneficiaries. The ability to close that gap, and improve communications channels at the very local level around South Sudan can be a multiplying factor for the NGO Forum members and improve the humanitarian operating environment for all actors.

Activities

Information Sharing: This will focus on compilation and disseminate relevant information pertaining to humanitarian action, recovery and development activities and policies. This includes relevant government publications, announcements, and circulars following up with the appropriate line ministries to ensure that they are understandable to the NGO audience. The NGO Forum will Maintain and disseminate a contact directory of different stakeholders (i.e. donors, CDs) for NGOs. The NGO Forum will prepare and publish briefings on issues of common interest to the NGO Forum (to be defined by the Steering Committee). National NGOs will also be supported in diagnosing institutional gaps and strengths as well as generate quantitative information to inform NNGO capacity status discussions.Coordination: The NGO Forum will continue to maintain links to other coordination mechanisms in South Sudan and ensure that NGOs have accurate and updated information on these mechanisms. In addition, the NGO Forum will facilitate and enhance a peer learning spirit by investing in creating a conducive environment and tools for peer learning and support. This includes coordination with government, UN agencies and donors.Security information sharing: The security information sharing and coordination function will be a key function of the NGO Forum. The NGO Forum will continue to play a critical role in ensuring NGOs have access to relevant information to make critical, real time decisions on security. The NGO Forum will also assist members in developing risk mitigation strategies through regular dissemination of analytical products, development of relevant statistics and maps as well as provision of regular briefings. Advocacy: The NGO Forum will continue its engagement with government on issues around regulation and humanitarian access. To protect individual NGOs from negative consequences of engagement on more sensitive issues, some sensitive issues will be addressed through group fora such as the Humanitarian Country Team, the Humanitarian Coordination Forum and the High Level Oversight Committee proposed by the President of the Republic of South Sudan. The RRC has an interest in some joint working groups and have engagement around capacity building between the RRC and the NGO Forum. Collaboration will allow for NGO Forum and RRC to work more effectively and efficiently on humanitarian and development issues. The project will focus on the joint field visit together with government officials (RRC and Ministry of Humanitarian affairs) OCHA to the areas with the affected population. The NGO Forum will also facilitate relevant meetings on bureaucratic impediments at the national and state levels. Better practices intiatives: The NGO Forum will collaborate with members on best practices and principled humanitarian delivery by sharing briefing notes, reports, and guidance documents aimed at increasing the collective knowledge of practice within the humanitarian and development community. The NGO Forum will continue to engage with pooled and other funding mechanisms, particularly the SSHF it remains an important source of funding for many NGOs and NNGOs operating in South Sudan. Capacity building of national NGOs: The NGO Forum will support NNGOs strengthening of capacity through the NNGO Focal Point, NNGO Information Officer, short-term NNGO consultants that will be recruited periodically, and State Coordination Trainer to support stronger networking of national NGOs and other civil society groups. This will ensure that NNGOs are able to meet on a regular basis to share information and develop common approaches on humanitarian and development issues. Support will also be provided to the National NGOs during the incidence such as evacuation of the national staff from the conflict-affected areas.

Does the project provide beneficiaries with information?

Yes

How does the project provide beneficiaries with information?

The NGO Forum plans to continue to support NGO access to critical information for programmatic, operational and strategic decision-making through data collection and analysis. This includes enhancement of Knowledge Network. This an information platform where research on South Sudan is stored. The NGO Secretariat will continue to collect and upload relevant research publications on the South Sudan Knowledge Network online database for research conducted on South Sudan that is available for public access. The Secretariat also plans to improve the NGO Forum’s website, based on users’ feedback. The message board continues to be one of the most interactive features of the website that provides the NGO members and the public information such as current job and tender advertisements. Relevant circulars and other documents are accessible from the document repository providing NGOs with information on the regulatory environment.

Are messages context-specific or/and tailored?

Yes

Briefly describe how

The focus will be on providing information relevant to INGOs and NNGOs needs for up to date and time relevant information on areas such as security, donors, government regulations and coordination mechanisms. Information will also be designed by use of government staff, UN agencies and donors to advocate NGOs' roles in meeting humanitarian and development needs of the conflict-affected and vulnerable populations in South Sudan while adhering to humanitarian principles. This will encourage greater acceptance and thus security for members.

Protection from sexual exploitation and abuse

Yes

Briefly describe how

Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse is very important part of the NGO Forum’s programming and where key is to advocate for gender mainstreaming and other relevant activities. The NGO Forum has started the training on gender mainstreaming and has a focal point for it in the organisation. As the NGO Forum is hosted by Concern Worldwide, all staff and consultants are required to sign the Code of Conduct, Programme Participant Protection Policy, Child Safeguarding Policy and Anti Trafficking in Persons Policies. Included is mandatory reporting of abuse and exploitation.

Nature of the consultations

Yes

Briefly describe the nature of the consultations

Annually, the NGO Forum Secretariat surveys the Forum’s membership to provide feedback on the performance of the Secretariat, the Steering Committee and the overall functions and services of the NGO Forum. To better meet the needs of both the International and National NGOs, two separate survey are conducted while maintaining a core group of common questions. The survey includes satisfaction levels of member’s and usefulness of the NGO Forum’s services.

Protection Risk Analysis?

Briefly describe how

South Sudan NGO Forum has capacity in the providing the providing more timely warning on potential protection risks to NGO operations in South Sudan Provide response to emergent security issues This includes the provision of flash and daily incident updates, weekly and monthly security analysis reports and maintain an incident database. The NGO Forum provides weekly security briefings and will coordinate NGO security-based relocations. In addition, the NGO Forum will conduct outreach activities to support field-based NGOs and manage and coordinate information sharing platforms.

Barriers to Access?

Yes

Briefly describe how

Humanitarian needs throughout the country continued due to the impacts of conflict and erratic climate. With the government having limited capacity or resources, NGOs provide 80-90% of humanitarian interventions in South Sudan. However, in providing relief and protection, NGOs have encountered a number of challenges including: difficulties in staff recruitment, harassment of aid workers, diversion of aid, extortions and intimidation, blockages and illegal taxation, looting of humanitarian assets/materials, permits restrictions and restrictions of access to areas with populations in need of assistance.

Addresses inequalities or protection threats?

Yes

Briefly describe how

The project will aim to support NGOs in their engagement with local authorities, addressing impediments to NGO operations at the local level and engaging directly with authorities when relevant to ensuring acceptance or any attempts to divert humanitarian funding away from populations in need. In addition to supporting NGOs and the humanitarian operating environment, the interventions of this project will also facilitate the protection work of NGOs, by working with them to address access constraints, to increase funding for protection-related activities and to support NGO staff who are in need of reinforcing capacities in mainstreaming protection and humanitarian principles into their work.

Project activities address inequalities or protection threats?

Yes

Briefly describe how

The project will provide up to date security information to members for them to make informed planning decisions to reduce protection risks for humanitarian responses. The NGO Forum will continue to engage with government such as the RRC and Ministry of Humanitarian Affairs including joint-field visits to improve acceptance as a security strategy and improve access and thus protection risks. More sensitive issues will be dealt with through relevant fora in the NGO Forum.

The NGO Forum will conduct surveys with members to understand improvements or challenges in access to populations in need of assistance and use the information collected for advocacy with the relevant government departments.
