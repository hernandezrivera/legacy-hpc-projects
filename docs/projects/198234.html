Project Summary

Proyecto Global ONU Mujeres – Colombia con otros 8 países piloto
Temática: coordinación sobre género en acción humanitaria
Duración: hasta Diciembre 2023
Departamento priorizados: Cauca, Chocó, Nariño
Resultados: 1. En los 3 Departamentos de Cauca, Chocó y Nariño, 50 mujeres locales fortalecidas en sus conocimientos sobre Género en acción humanitaria, capacidades en incidencia colectiva sobre alertas y necesidades humanitarias con enfoques de género e interseccionales, y articulación con los mecanismos de arquitectura humanitaria local y nacional.
2. 20 puntos focales de Género en acción humanitaria designados y formados en las estructuras de coordinación a nivel nacional y local.
3. Enfoque de género como elemento salva-vida posicionado de manera más visible y articulada en las estrategias del Equipo Humanitario País, a través de la construcción y difusión documentos anuales conjuntos entre OLM y mecanismos de coordinación humanitaria.
In implementing this pilot project, UN Women will be leveraging and applying its coordination mandate to ensure the integration of gender into humanitarian response planning and action in both the field and global levels. UN Women provision of sustained and official, mandated capacity will complement GenCap’s short-term and/or other ad-hoc support placements and ensure that momentum towards systematic integration of gender into the processes of humanitarian coordination is not lost once the short-term deployments end their support to the Humanitarian Country Teams (HCTs). This is particularly critical in ensuring accountability for humanitarian leadership with regards to their policy prescribed obligations to gender in humanitarian action (GiHA), including maintaining the GiHA systems and processes established, once the short-term gender capacity ended. As the mandated entity to ensure the integration of gender and the empowerment of women and girls across the UN’s output – including in humanitarian settings - UN Women is uniquely placed to provide this longer-term capacity in humanitarian crises. This is buttressed by the findings of the recently conducted IASC’s internal evaluation on Gender Equality and the Empowerment of Women and Girls which concludes that the humanitarian system’s lack of gender capacity – both globally and at the field level - is directly correlated to the lack of prioritization of the gendered needs of women and girls beyond GBV and Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA).

4.1.1. Provea brevemente información al respecto - máximo 1.000 carácteres

• Development of specific evidence, data and gender analysis to inform the development of OCHA led Humanitarian Response Plans to ensure they adequately identify and address the specific needs of affected women and girls.• Creation/enhancement of inter-agency and inter-cluster coordination Gender in Humanitarian Action Working Groups to support the mainstreaming of gender, and complimenting GBV sub-cluster work across all clusters and the HCT.• Capacity building for humanitarian leadership, stakeholders, including cluster leads and government officials on the integration of gender into humanitarian action.• Development of localized accountability frameworks in partnership with local/women’s organizations to hold humanitarian actors accountable to existing policy commitments, standards and designated roles and responsibilities relating to the integration of gender into humanitarian response.

4.2.1. Provea brevemente información al respecto - máximo 1.000 carácteres

Whilst the development of guidance resources and tools to support the development of Humanitarian Response Plans (HRPs) are crucial, they are not an end in themselves. The challenge remains to translate all of these policies, commitments, standards and defined responsibilities into humanitarian responses that address real needs, that do no harm and offer protection for the most affected and vulnerable.

4.3. ¿El proyecto identifica las barreras y asegura accesibilidad universal e imparcial a la ayuda y los servicios por los diferentes grupos de población?

Yes

4.3.1. Provea brevemente información al respecto - máximo 1.000 carácteres

In keeping with the IASC’s recent evaluation on gender in humanitarian action and its management response to improve on delivery of the 2018 IASC Gender Policy commitments, this proposed programme is intended to ensure the requisite capacity and monitoring/accountability is available to field level humanitarian coordination mechanisms to ensure minimum standards for gender in humanitarian action are adhered to. This will lead to better prioritization of the needs of the most affected populations based on data and gender analysis, improved access for women and girls to the lifesaving and protection services they need, as well as enhanced participation of women and girls in the decision making and planning processes. It will also strengthen the implementation of Germany’s Federal Foreign Office’s Strategy on Humanitarian Assistance, looking at the humanitarian principles of prioritization of needs and doing no harm.
