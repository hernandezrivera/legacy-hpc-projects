1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The socio-political crisis that has unfolded in Cameroon for the past six years in the north-west and south-west regions of Cameroon, led to the displacement of over two million people. In 2023, 4.7 million people (including 1,5 million children), need humanitarian assistance, out of which 2.7 million are targeted by the humanitarian response plan (HRP) with projects aiming to support internally displaced persons (IDPs), returnees, refugees, host communities and those left behind . In the North-West and South-West regions the ongoing crisis led to attack on education and an increase in poverty levels, as well as damaged livelihoods and resulted in gross human rights violations, including grave violations of children’s rights and Gender-Based Violence. Children and women are affected differently. This situation has further led to an increase in the number of Unaccompanied and Separated Children (UASC), with over 19,000 separated children reported in 2022 in the NWSW, making family separation a severe threat (HRP, 2023). In the North-West and South-West regions, child protection concerns primarily revolve around psychosocial distress, mental disorders, and family separation. Many children have been exposed to or have fallen victim to extreme violence, sometimes, witnessing the tragic loss of parents, siblings, or other close relatives. Boys, in particular, are susceptible to recruitment and exploitation by armed groups, posing a grave threat to their own safety and also exposing other children to the risk of arbitrary arrest, based on suspicion of affiliation with or potential recruitment by these armed groups. The impact on education is still very glaring, with 54% of schools in the NWSW non-functional or closed. Out of school children are therefore continuously facing a range of child protection risks such as sexual exploitation and abuse, physical abuse, association to armed groups, increase in child labour, teenage pregnancies and lack of civil documents, which most government officials and local organizations do not have the capacity to handle. Lack of capacity and effective systems in place continue to hamper the provision of quality child protection services, leading to an increase in the vulnerability of affected children. Children now experience significantly more physical and sexual violence in these communities. We are expecting this project to respond to worsening child protection needs by providing a comprehensive action in the north-west and south-west regions that focuses on capacity and system strengthening as well as on prevention and response to violence against children.
