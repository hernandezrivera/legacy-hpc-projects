Project Summary

The Tackling of Acute Malnutrition in Emergency (TACME) project is conceived by GREENCODE to contribute to tackling the child malnutrition among new arrivals, IDPs and host communities in Yobe and Bornostate using the community mobilisation in IYCF-E approach as the rate of malnutrition is increasing among children of under 5 years. The increasing rise in severe acute malnutrition is a as result of increasing insurgent attacks that has destroyed the health care facilities and make food insecure in the past 3 and 4 months.
The recent SMART survey of conducted by Save the Children International (SCI) in Kaga, Konduga and Magumeri in July, 2019 and validated by the Nutrition Sector in November, 2019 indicates high prevalence of global acute malnutrition as 21.1% (17.6-25.2, 95% C.I), with a severe acute malnutrition prevalence of 5.5% (3.6-8.2, 95% C.I). The GAM prevalence was well above the emergency threshold of 15% and increased significantly (p<0.05) compared to 15.7% (12.2-20.0, 95% C.I) in 2018. Further, more the report disaggregated analysis by LGA which acute malnutrition was highest, which may be classified as “hot spots”. were Magumeri, followed by Konduga, then Kaga.
GREENCODE with the funding support from donor will implement a 12 month project window will support the capacity strengthening of community structures to support acute malnutrition treatment by organizing community level training for community nutrition mobilizers, mass MUAC screening, conduct counselling and referral to other INGO’s OTPs/stabilization centers, set-up mother-to-mother support groups, father to father support group, training of community nutrition mobilisers, Hygiene/Sanitation, training of lead mothers, cooking demonstration, training of religious leaders, training of religious leaders on the involvement of IYCF/CMAM. Under this project GREENCODE will be reaching and improving nutrition for 3,000.

2 - Activities

GREENCODE will under take the following activities 1). Community social mobilisation in the 2 states2). Identify, Select and Train Community Nutrition Mobilisers (CNMs)3).Hold community and house to house session to pass on IYCF messages, 4). Trainings and mother to mother group sessions and hygiene education to help prevent diseases and acute malnutrition among children 0 to 23 months. 5). Cash support to caregiver and parent of under 5 and PLW that are malnourished for food and enhanced food supply in order to prevent SAM and MAM children and PLW.The community outreach component, which entails the recruitment of Community Nutrition Mobilisers (CNMs), will be implemented on a large scale within the communities in order to identify children with acute malnutrition on time and refer them to the nutrition OTP and stabilization centres. 6). Conduct cooking demonstration using local recipes will be conducted and popularize in the project location as well as implementation of cash for caregivers in critical conditions to empower them and sustain tackling the malnutrition in project location.7). Mass MUAC Screening and referrals of SAM and MAM children and PLW to OTP/SC in the LGAs
