Project Summary

Needs

BTSC is an active national NGO leading and coordinating response efforts to the Health activities and AWD outbreak surveillance in Buurhakaba in close coordination with health cluster & partners. BTSC will compliment WASH services and provide WASH related response activities especially in the areas where AWD outbreak is raised. Due to the vastness of Buurhakaba district and based BTSC experience and previous observations access to adequate health services, particularly for women, children, is extremely restricted by cultural and social barriers, poor infrastructure, poor service quality. According to the Somali Health Cluster Bulleting #04, July 2018 The last month Somalia did not experience any major shocks, but the effects of the floods and cyclone Sagar still exists. In addition, insecurity continues to hamper partners access to the rural parts of the country. In 2018, the partners through several sources of funding reached 2,246,742 people. Of these, 1,739,910 individuals were reached by humanitarian response plan funded projects. While providing medical consultations to these individuals, health cluster partners noted prevalence of common diseases including other acute diarrheal diseases, influenza like illness, severe acute respiratory illness, measles etc. Since April, however partners continue to report increases in cases of malaria in mainly the flood affected areas. There are still major gaps in health services delivery in Buurhakaba. Routine immunization coverage remains very low. BTSC will also sensitize the drought affected communities on prevention of communicable diseases through enhanced health education and awareness. The under five mortality rate is 137 per 1,000 live births while approximately 732 women per 100,000 live births die from pregnancy or childbirth related complications

Activities

The proposed interventions are in line with Humanitarian Response Strategic and health cluster objectives: • Deliver minimum basic package of primary health care services including maternal and child health) and support referral services to secondary health facilities• Supply the health facilities with medical supplies, equipment and medicens including provision of essential and live saving medicines for communicable and no communicable diseases.• Expand and maintain the health surveillance system with early warning mechanisms.• Support Reproductive Health services including Emergency Obstetric, new born care.• Support the implementation of expand program of immunization: routine and outreach immunization activities • Support the referral system for selected emergency cases especially obstetrical complication.• Rapid response to diseases outbreaks in affected areas with close coordination with heath sector. • Building the capacity of health facility staff through regular training and close supervision. • Involve the communities and raise their awareness through conducting several health campaigns about certain diseases control and prevention.
