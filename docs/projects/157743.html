Project Name

Children affected by Cyclone Idai can access timely and quality prevention and response interventions to child protection concerns

Start Date

01/07/2019

End Date

31/05/2020

Project Summary

1: Children and adolescents’ psychosocial wellbeing is improved through resilience programming and children aged 3-18 have access to structured psychosocial activities.
2: Children and families can access timely and quality case management support for child protection concerns appropriate to the best interests of the child that meet the Minimum Standards for Child Protection in Humanitarian Action, including SGBV, alternative care, and FTR services.
3: Facilitate and support the activities of community-based child protection mechanisms to prevent and respond to children being exposed to abuse, exploitation, violence, and neglect especially during natural disasters (CCPCs, awareness-raising, IEC to prevent separation and what to do in the event of an emergency, capacity building of CCPCs to follow up on high risk cases, etc).
4: Research on post-resettlement coping strategies (200 participants)
5: Cash grants
