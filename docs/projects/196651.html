Project Summary

The Project is planned to contribute to the Sector Objective 2: "Increase access to Livelihood and job opportunities".
This program is going to increasing the resilience and livelihood opportunities of most vulnerable BNFs in NW Syria With focus on the vulnerable segments of the community such as women headed HHs and PWDs. Due to the bad economic situation and COVID-19 pandemic effects many families are not able to sustain income which makes their situation more critical. Under this Project SOP will support women and men with focusing on youth and most vulnerable groups. Through the most needed activities in the areas of Idlib and Darkoush, (soft skills for man and women, vocational training for women, and business entrepreneurship initiatives), part of the women will be the HHs, and all of them are from people who have no-income and not enrolled with similar projects and increase their ability to Strat-up / Scale-up their business (Priority will be given to Start-up ideas) and give them opportunity through providing small grants (400$) for each.
Activity 1: Provide vocational and skills training opportunities: Sessions are provided in the WGSSs, targeting women and adolescent girls. the main targeted segment in this activity is the vulnerable women who want to develop their skills and need vocational skills to face their social and economic obstacles.
Target: Women 400.
Activity 2: Support business entrepreneurship initiatives through training: Supporting the vulnerable Women to start a business entrepreneurship initiative.
Phase 1: Announcing the start of receiving applications, selection of candidates by scores applied to conformity with eligibility and admission priority criteria
Phase 2: Participants will be enrolled in entrepreneurship training to improve their knowledge of planning, designing, managing, and marketing for their own businesses.
Target: Women 400.
Activity 3: Support business entrepreneurship initiatives through small grants:
The full grant amount is $400 to be given in two installments (200$ for each one) through the purchase of the project's needs by the logistic officer.
Target: Women 400.
Activity 4: Training on soft skills:
SOP will give soft skills training on many topics that will enhance the skills of the segment of women and men over the age of 18 years old, which will help them improve their chances of accessing professional opportunities and increase their efficiency in work teams.
Target: Women: 150, Men:50.
Activity 5: Monitoring and Evaluation.
*In order to avoid any possible tension caused by this intervention and to ensure proper application of "do no harm" principle; SOP will apply IASC's principles and ensures active participation of the targeted BNFs throughout the whole project life cycle including the development of the selection criteria.
Priority will be placed in the criteria for selecting beneficiaries, so that the vulnerable groups are at the forefront of the people who are selected if they have the required qualifications, especially women and people with disabilities, regardless of the type of disability that exists, but so that it does not prevent the beneficiary from being able to effectively participate in the activities. The SOP center is equipped with access for people with disabilities, and their needs will be studied to provide the center with any additional needs that facilitate access. In addition, women, the elderly, people with disabilities, and other vulnerable groups are an essential part of the needs assessment study.

3 - Needs

According to ERL HRP chapter 22-23 more than 12M are in need for ERL activities 25% of them are from PWDs.The continued deterioration of the socio-economic situation in Syria increases the number of People in need for income-generating activities the major factors driving the steep rise in humanitarian needs were related to socio-economic dynamics, in particular a loss of purchasing power among working Syrians, who despite being economically active, could not make ends meet (the “working poor”)As a result of the ongoing crisis several segments within the population are in particular need of income generating and livelihood support due to increased vulnerability, particularly female-headed households and heads of households living with a disability who reported the lowest average income across all population groups- HNO 2022Thus, the intervention intends to respond to the above-mentioned need by contributing to building resilience throughout supporting SMEs and sustainable income-generating opportunities.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

Protection risks that may be occurred during implementation of this program: Protection risk (1): Child labor (involving children in the established businesses). Mitigating measures: SOP will be sure that the staff working in this project are trained and fully aware of child safeguarding and child protection risk (2): Lack/difficulty of accessibility for PWDs for the proposed activities. Mitigating measures: Availability of wheelchair for PWD at the reception point, trained staff to deal with PWDs, priority for the PWDs, included in the criteria, house to house distributions for PWDs, Protection Risk (3): Sometimes, crowding during distribution of support, may not provide the services at the desire quality, which might affect the BNFs dignity, especially women. Mitigating measures: Set up separate waiting and entry lines at registrations, Establishment of multiple distribution sites to avoid creating crowds, ensure that female staff are assigned to work during distributions and other activities that will need supervision training and field visits. Risk: GBV survivors will have limited access due to social restricts Mitigation: include GBV survivors in the selection criteria and collaborate with SOP's protection department to sensitize the community on GBV and basic related issues when needed.


8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Risk of exploitation or abuse by staff members/service providers if access to services or information are linked to a fee or limited to/excluded for specific persons/groups. SOP will provide regular training to staff and service providers for code of conduct and PSEA Policy, and will conduct induction sessions for new staff to ensure that they fully understand the content of CoC and PSEA policy. Strict disciplinary measures will be applied on any breach of the CoC. Affected population will be consulted on the convenient reporting channels to ensure effective and timely response for any compliance. SOP is a member of PSEA network (Adopting network's CoC and Policy) and regularly coordinating on the relevant issues and disseminating the central hotline numbers during the provided activities along with the organizational own reporting channels and hotlines. SOP periodically assessing the targeted communities on the suitable reporting channels for them and feed the information into threatening the existing CRM and deploying new channels when needed.


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

SOP identified several Risks and Mitigations approaches as follows: 1- authorities interference in the exclusion or inclusion of beneficiaries regardless of the selection criteria. Likelihood: Low - Impact: High Mitigation: Early ensuring that the local authorities clearly informed through signing MoUs with local councils that will clarify that SOP team will be fully independent in BNF selection according to the criteria developed2- Delay in funds which will have an adverse effect on the start of activities according to project work plan. Likelihood: Low - Impact: High Mitigation: SOP will coordinate and respond to the donor requirements rapidly to ensure that the agreements are signed on time 3- Corvid -19 Outbreak Likelihood: Low - Impact: High Mitigation: Ensuring that all protective measures had been carefully considered and applied to avoid COVID-19 infections project interventions. 4- The beneficiaries can disburse the amount of the small grants for a target other than its intended purpose.Mitigation: the materials required for each initiative or project will be purchased in coordination with the logistics team.5-intra-community tension due to perceived discrimination Mitigation: SOP will consult the BNFs and share information about the progress and selection criteria with the targeted communities in a transparent manner.
