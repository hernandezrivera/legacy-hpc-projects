Objectives

1) Ensure regular, sufficient, equitable and dignified access for women, girls, men and boy refugees living in camps and affected host communities to safe water for drinking, domestic needs and medical purposes. 2) Ensure women, girls, men and boy refugees living in camps, host communities or affected by Covid-19 have adequate, appropriate and acceptable sanitation facilities to allow rapid, safe and secure access at all times. 3) Ensure the change of potentially dangerous behaviors through hygiene promotion and distribution of hygiene items with strong focus on highly contagious diseases, for all refugees, affected or vulnerable communities of the district.

Needs

WASH was identified as a top 3 priority sector with increasing needs to respond to Covid-19 crisis. Moreover, most of the activities as included in the JRP were identified as “critical activities”.Regarding access to water, 54% of the refugees have access to chlorinated water supply through small water networks completed by an extra 10.000 boreholes (variable water quality). Chlorine in the water destroys Covid-19 virus so it’s essential to finish building water networks or operate them daily to increase safe water coverage and availability of water to support handwashing as the main barrier to covid-19. Access to adequate sanitation remain a challenge in the camps mostly due to the lack of space, maintenance and improvements of sanitation facilities including latrines (20 persons per latrines on average but only 78% functioning), bathing facilities (40 persons per shower), fecal sludge management units and solid waste management remain critical activities as Covid-19 survive in sludge and wastewater requiring extra-care for treatment.Hygiene promotion focusing on covid-19 need to be strengthened to increase awareness of the refugees. Distribution of hygiene kits, soaps and aquatabs will be increased to make sure of full and regular coverage in the camps. WASH sector is also significantly increasing the number of hand-washing points in strategic places over the camps and organizing disinfection through spraying of disinfectant on WASH infrastructures and communal spaces.Finally, WASH needs to response to increasing demand for new facilities and increased water quantity in the health centers and isolation facilities upon request by the Government or Health sector. Specific hygiene kits for persons in isolation wards are provided by WASH partners. In Host communities, only 63% of people report having sufficient water to meet household needs and 86% use household latrines. WASH needs to focus on Operation & Maintenance of water and sanitation facilities in host’s communities (no new construction during covid-19 restrictions). During the dry season, water need will increase, and WASH will cover any emergency needs identified.As part of Covid-19 response in host’s communities, hygiene promotion including distribution of soap for handwashing will be strengthened. Additional hygiene items will be distributed according to specific needs and to the impact and diffusion of the disease. As in camps, WASH is organizing disinfection of WASH facilities and public buildings to limit the spread of the disease.

Activities or outputs

Provide emergency WASH supplies including soap and PPE for workers. Organise disinfection of WASH facilities and common spaces. Provide adequate WASH facilities in health care facilities and isolation centres. Increase coverage and operation of handwashing stations in camps and host communities. Strengthen hygiene promotion focusing on Covid 19
