Project Summary

The purpose of this project is to provide lifesaving WASH services to conflict affected vulnerable populations, including IDPs, Returnees and Host Community in Aljazeera, South Kordofan, Blue Nile, Khartoum, and Central & West Darfur States in Sudan. The project targets 586,300 crisis-affected people (239,211 women, 229,829men, 59,802 girls, 57,458 boys), including 196,800 IDPS, 163500 Host Community, 226,000 Non displaced community in above-mentioned states in Sudan.
The Sudan 2024 HRNP depicted critically deteriorating WASH coverage that even existed before the ongoing conflict. The conflict led to further destruction of water infrastructures and disruptions in the WASH supply chain, and collapse of the revenue system to the extent that employees of water and sanitation facilities have not been paid since the crisis began. Without government subsidies, critical supply shortages, inflation, and decreasing community incomes, the population increasingly resorts to unsafe water and sanitation practices and adopts unhygienic behaviors. The increasing concentration of newly displaced populations strains the already scarce WASH resources. Due to limited financial means and capacity, neither communities nor the government can sustain existing WASH services or establish new ones to address the growing needs. The ongoing outbreak of cholera has claimed 131 lives, with 4,114 confirmed. The Interagency rapid needs assessment conducted in Central and West Darfur in September 2023 showed that in security in all the states of CD&WD plus some clashes between SAF&RSF is still remains the main cause of displacement for more than 300,000 people and death of more than 2000 innocent civilians in Geneina, Sirba and Kreneik and localities in West Darfur and displacement of many women and children from Nertiti, Zalingei, Mukjar and Azoum areas in Central Darfur state. The displaced persons have lost all their valuables including residences, livestock and crops. The ethnic conflict in North and Western Ethiopia contributed to migration of around 15,500 refugees to Blue Nile. All the vulnerable people have poor access to clean water supply, sanitation and hygiene facilities. The inflation affected the purchasing power of poor families, accelerating the food insecurity and malnutrition levels increase the venerability of the affected people of the society - IDPs, refugees and hosting.
In response to the first strategic objective (SO1) to provide safe, timely, principled and gender-responsive life-saving humanitarian assistance to the most vulnerable groups to reduce mortality and morbidity in areas with high severity of need or at risk of a further deterioration of critical humanitarian needs, this project aims to ensuring access to basic water, sanitation and hygiene services for crisis-affected populations. Firstly, interventions ensure drinking water from an improved source with a collection time of not more than 30 minutes roundtrip, including queuing to affected people. People targeted with basic water services will benefit from new installations and maintenance, operations, and rehabilitation of existing handpumps, water yards, protected wells, check-dams, and haffirs where feasible. Secondly, project aims to ensure improved access to adequate, protected, and gender sensitive sanitation services for vulnerable populations. Interventions will ensure the installation, rehabilitation, and use of ventilated improved pit latrines (VIP), pit latrines with slabs. Household latrines (basic), latrines shared between two households (limited), and communal latrines to reduce open deification, and to have their household latrine for family use. Thirdly, the project will establish information dissemination system at the community level guided by trained community hygiene Promotors, 1 promoter per 50 HHs to ensure effective awareness razing for behavior change. Women and girls who are part of the displaced population will receive personal dignity kits.

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

While providing WASH services and facilities, this project will consider girls' andwomen's security and safety. Their engagement will be critical while identifying waterpoints and new water sources to save them travel too far to unsecured places to fetchwater. Furthermore, this project will ensure that the communal and institutionallatrines are gender-segregated, safe with latches on the doors, and easy to access insecure places. The project will link women and girls to income-generating women'sgroups to receive training on making sanitary pads and soap. Additionally, WRS willinstall and construct suitable facilities to suit the aged and disable population. WRSwill uphold the five minimum commitments, including consulting or engaging theaffected population in assessments, designs, implementation, monitoring, andinclusion of women and girls. Efforts will be made to strengthen feedbackmechanisms and post distribution surveys.
