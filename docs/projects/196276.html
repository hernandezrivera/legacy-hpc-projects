Project Summary

MWL's intervention will target 24120 families (120600) as: : (Male: 25326, Female: 27738, Boys:32562, Girls: 34974/ PWD: 2112) for 12 months through six interventions as follows:
1. MWL will target 14 camps in Dana, ldleb, and Maaret Tamasrin sub-districts through providing adequate drinking water by trucking water. (tanks) (35 L/Per/day) with constant water quality assurance through chlorination treatment and testing, this will target 2900 HHs (14500 persons). MWL will ensure connect the families with water stations before 2 months of leaving the camps.
This can be with cooperation with other worked NGOs in areas, or conducting advocacy campaigns to operating the water station by MWL's donors.
2. Providing Hygiene kits for 2000 families (10000 persons for nine months) while the aim of distributing hygiene kits to cover the needs of infected families of Cholera. All the prevention measures (hand sanitizer, wearing masks, keeping social distance, and wearing gloves. The team will deliver the families’ kits without any touching or entering the BNFs homes.
3. Awareness-raising sessions on personal hygiene and protection measures adopted in the camps (based on WHO recommendations) will be conducted in targeted camps and communities. The sessions will be provided physically by MWL team. They will be conducted in a hall or tent with a maximum number of participants 15 persons. The hall will be equipped with all protection tools such as masks, stabilizers in addition to ensure the social distance between people. In each targeted camp MWL will conduct two sessions, 13 camps in the targeted communities, 26 sessions at the beginning of the project with total 3000 families.
4. MWL will target 14 camps with conduct desludging septic tanks for nine months targeting 4500 HHs (22500 persons).
5. MWL will carry out solid waste management works in 14 camps and remove the waste to landfills. This activity will target 6537 HHs (32685 persons).
6. MWL will conduct Latrines care and maintenance for 14 camps targeting 25915 persons.

3 - Needs

In late 2022 , MWL participated with ACU in collecting data about the WASH services in schools where the study found that water for drinking and other usage is available in 91% (3,267 schools) of the operating schools; it is not available in 9% (331 schools). Students bring drinking water with them, and there is no water available for use within these schools (water for bathrooms or personal hygiene). Water for toilets was not available in 6% (183 schools) of operating schools, available in small quantities in 50% (1,642 schools), and available in appropriate amounts in 44% (1,442 schools) of operating schools.For camps, MWL's MEAL field team conducted 28 focused-group discussions on late 2022 in 28 different camps that MWL served within four sub-districts as following: Dana, Sarmda, Maarrat Tamsrin and Atareb. Out of findings, 60% of camps have public bathrooms which poses girls and women at risk of protection. 90% of those informal camps were not supported by any organization, where people buying their water needs at high prices through water tanks periodically. The average water consumption per person on a daily basis according to the data collected at: 9.38 liters per day. Skin diseases such as leishmania, eczema, etc., are common due to difficulty of bathing and water scarcity. The assessment also showed 70% of camps have nearby water stations ; however these stations are not supported since 9 months ago.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

Movements of water trucks in congested, camp-like settings L:H, I: L Mitigation: Consult community (separately women and girls) regarding location of distribution points, delivery route and its schedule; Avoid location of water points in narrow streets, congested areas; Establish clear routes and schedule for water trucking that use wider access routes, • Long distance to water collection points to be travelled not safe (exposure to GBV risk, UXO/ERW hazard). L:L, I: H Mitigation: Ensure agreed context/SPHERE standards(200-500m) and closely monitor these aspects during implementation; Consult separately girl, boys, women and men, including older people and those with disabilities in the assessment


8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

This project is built taking into account: Do No Harm, Age, Gender, and commitment to safe and equal access to assistance. The project team will set the necessary participatory mechanisms for different groups in the targeted locations, including men, women, boys, and girls, in addition to the PWDs, elderly people who are the affected people in the COVID -19 and decide which stakeholders need to be involved in the different implementation stages. MWL ensure preventing and responding to SEA acts by integrating PSEA in MWL policies and making sure that all personnel read and sign the code of conduct.
