Project Summary

The ETS will provide reliable data connectivity services (Internet access) in camps and operational areas (treatment centres in camps and a common meeting area in Cox’s Bazar where many staff also reside). The ETS through coordination, information sharing and technical assistance activities will facilitate the lifesaving work of humanitarian responders.

Needs

"Due to the lack of the health facilities that are responding to the Covid-19 the humanitarian community iis setting up treatment of patients/refugees centres. There is a requirement from the ISCG, to set up a communication channel across these facilities so referral can take place. Considering the limited presence of the commercial ISP solution, the ETS therefore was approached as the provider of last resort to implement timely and reliable communication solutions.Through the existing ETS working group mechanism constraints are identified and resolved through a collaborative effort, and to avoid duplication. The Coordination effort will continue to ensure an effective Covid 19 IT response.With the current lock down in place, the ability for humanitarians to carry out their duties remotely becomes challenging due to the unstable internet connectivity."

Activities or outputs

"Most ETS COVID-19 activities are in line with the existing services provided to responders. However the ETS will expand connectivity services to partner organisations and/or new sites based on the evolving needs on the ground. As the provider of last resort, the ETS is coordinating closely with the ISCG and the EPRWG on providing data connectivity services for patient/refugee treatment centres (Severe Acute Respiratory Infections Treatment [SARI] centres).All partners involved in the health response will need access to a communication channel to enable staff at each of the facilities to communicate with each other so patients may be referred and the pandemic can be tracked and monitored. At the humanitarian congregation point in Cox’s Bazar (Windy Terrace Hotel), the Emergency Telecommunications Sector (ETS) will set up stable and reliable data connectivity services to facilitate the lifesaving work of humanitarian responders from UN agencies(WFP, UNICEF, IOM, UNHCR and WHO) and NGOs."
