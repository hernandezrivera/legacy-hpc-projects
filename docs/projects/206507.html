Project Summary

MAG in Chad is proposing to work in Massaguet, a town close to Ndjamena, to clear explosive ordnance from a former battlefield situated in an area frequently crossed by people following a nomadic lifestyle, where there have been numerous accidents. MAG will carry out risk education sessions for communities living in Massaguet, as well as in the Lake Chad province, and other provinces affected by the threat of explosive ordnance. MAG will also deploy explosive ordnance disposal teams who will be able to respond to reports of dangerous items. These interventions will improve the security of Chadian civilians.
