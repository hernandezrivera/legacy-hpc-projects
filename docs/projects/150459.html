Project Summary

The old city of Hebron is considered one of the most struggling areas in the West Bank due to the settler’s arrogance and crimes, arrogance of the Israeli occupation, and the large number of military checkpoints and repetitive closures. Therefore, HWC decided to establish Hebron Emergency Center based on its mission for providing health and development services to marginalized people in order to enhance its steadfastness. Hebron Emergency Center became one of the main health facilities that provides Women Health Program’s services, Chronic Disease Program’s services among other primary health care services in Hebron H2. The current project is an outcome of the continuous assessment of the general situation and particular health needs of the communities in the old city of Hebron (H2) through HWC’s Hebron Emergency Center with special focus on the health needs of the vulnerable communities. This is in addition to analyzing secondary data review published by others. The assessment revealed the presence of two pressing problems faced by the communities in the old city of Hebron (H2), which are the escalation of political violence imposed by the Israeli occupation and its settlers upon communities in the old city of Hebron (H2) and so these communities have been considered one of the most vulnerable communities in the West Bank. As the presence of the Israeli occupation and its systematic violence practices have been imposing threat on the livelihood of these communities. Moreover, the Israeli occupation has been imposing restriction movements upon these communities, which hinders the ability of these communities to reach out for health services outside the old city of Hebron (H2). Thus, the project “Access to Essential Health Services and Emergency Preparedness for Marginalized Communities in Hebron H2” has the overall objective to contribute to the promotion of equitable access to essential and quality health services to communities in the old city of Hebron (H2) and have proper emergency preparedness. The overall objective will be accomplished through two specific objectives; (1) To increase the capacity of Hebron Emergency Center of Health Work Committees to provide essential health services to communities in the old city of Hebron (2) To increase the access to women’s health services, chronic disease services and access to medical supplies and lifesaving drugs in the targeted communities. This will be accomplished through two expected results (1) The capacity of the Hebron Emergency Center of HWC to provide essential health services is increased, this correspond to the first specific objective and (2) The communities in the old city of Hebron (H2) have received women’s health services, chronic disease services, emergency services and preparedness and medical supplies and lifesaving drugs are accessed through Hebron Emergency Center of HWC. The first expected result will be accomplished through the implementation of the following activity; providing the Hebron Emergency Center with basic medications and medical disposables. While the second expected result will be accomplished through the following activities; the Hebron Emergency Center provides healthcare services including primary health services, through the Women Health Program, the center provides services that focus primarily on early detection of diseases, especially breast and cervical cancer and on health education. In addition to the organization of open health days in which topics related to women and their health in all aspects of life, legal and human rights, are discussed. Moreover, GBV cases are to be detected and the women will receive legal, social and psychological services and referred when needed. Through the Chronic Disease Program, the center provides services which is essential for the early detection of complications of diabetes and therefore early treatment. It also provides educational sessions in areas of proper nutrition personal hygiene, and how to deal and cope with diabetes. The project will also involve emergency preparedness of 11 schools in H2 and the schools are provided with essential first aid kits and fire extinguishers. 2 teachers from each school will be trained for emergency preparedness and the students will receive training on evacuation.

Needs

The proposed project aims at addressing the problem of limited accessibility to primary healthcare services in the Old City of Hebron with special focus on women’s health and chronic disease. In 2018, the total population of Palestine has reached around 4.9 million Palestinian, 49.1% being female. With total fertility rates reaching 4.0 which has decreased from 6.0 in 1997. Women’s health services are of vital importance thus several indicators for SRH services have been reported at the national level; for antenatal care services, 95.5% of women aged 15-49 years received health care during pregnancy at least 4 times by any health care provider in 2014. And for postnatal care services, 89.7% of women 15-49 years in the West Bank received health care after birth, both during their stay in the health institutions or at home after birth or during a visit to post-natal care within two days of birth. This implies that there is high coverage for the antenatal care and postnatal care services. While in terms of family planning, 57.2% of married women aged 15-49 years in Palestine used a family planning method. 44.1% of currently married women aged 15-49 years used modern methods, and 13.1% used traditional methods. Despite the fact that there is high coverage for the family planning services, there is still unmet needs especially in H2 area. It is important to ensure the access to a full range of modern contraceptives, including emergency contraception, which should also be included in the essential medicines’ list to increase availability. In terms of chronic diseases, Palestine is facing challenges and constrains by diabetes mellitus on the Palestinian health system; there has been an increase on the prevalence of diabetes mellitus between 2000 and 2010; it was estimated that the prevalence of diabetes mellitus in 2000 was 9% for people aged 30 years and above. While in 2010, the prevalence of diabetes mellitus was estimated to be 15.3%, and expected to reach 20.8% by 2020. Moreover, it has been ranked as the 5th leading cause of death accounting for 6.4% of Palestinian deaths. Prevalence of chronic disease is increasing within women due to the increase in the elderly population of women. An increase in overweight and obese women has also been observed. This is related to the assigned reproductive role of women in most communities in Palestine. This limits the women’s physical activity to only house chores and taking care of the children, which even though consumes energy, is not considered a physical exercise to improve health. In many areas, it is not socially acceptable for women to exercise in public or even take walks. Moreover, constant increase in stress levels from the unhealthy lifestyle is proven to correlate with unstable blood sugar levels. All these factors, with the relatively high cost of gym subscriptions, prevents the women from leading a healthy and active lifestyle and eventually leads to increases in chronic disease.Females in the Palestinian society are still being exposed to GBV and the risks it carries. One third of women exposed to some sort of GBV specifically from a spouse would rather stay silent about it, and that a huge percentage of 37% of Palestinian women previously married have been exposed to one or more forms of GBV. Of those, 58.6% have been exposed to emotional violence at least once, 55.1% to financial violence, 54.8% to social violence, 23.5% to physical violence and 11.8% were exposed to sexual violence from their spouses.HWC conducts needs assessments for areas with and within the community throughout our work in Hebron. This is done by meetings with community leaders, focus groups from women and men, feedback and assessments after services provided. The following documents supports our justificationsNational Strategy to promote gender equality, Violence Survey in the Palestinian Society, Country Assessment of the SRH and Right Situation in Palestine, PCBS.

Activities

-Hebron Emergency Center is provided with basic medications and medical disposables.In order to ensure the quality, availability and accessibility to chronic and women’s health services, essential medications and medical supplies and disposables should be available. Thus purchasing of the listed required medications and supplies will be performed through this project. -Hebron Emergency Center provides primary health care and emergency services to the population in H2 area. Essential services for chronic diseases including the early detection and treatment of diabetes are provided.Hebron Emergency Center will be providing prevention and treatment of the most common diseases and emergency injuries. -Through the Women’s Health Program, SRH services including post and anti-natal, family planning and gynecological services are provided.Women’s Health Program’s services have been provided through Hebron Emergency Center in which Anti-natal & Post-natal services, family planning services, early detection of breast, cervical and gynecological cancer, clinical and gynecological services -Identify women exposed to GBV and provide them with legal, psychological and social group and individual counselling and refer them to specialized organizations and institutions when necessary. -Organize and conduct open medical days to provide women’s health and chronic disease services to marginalized communities of the H2 area in HebronThe open days are planned to be conducted in enclosed areas where accessibility as well as availability is minimal, and residents in these areas have very difficult times trying to access health services. These areas include neighborhoods like Haret Jaber, Tel Irmeideh and the areas surrounding the Ibrahimi Mosque. -Conduct awareness sessions on topics of GBV, SRH and Chronic disease.This is supported by the community education program in which health awareness lectures are provided to raise awareness about healthy practices and healthy lifestyles. Moreover, the sessions will include group and individual sessions and counselling tackling GBV issues. -First Aid training and dealing with Emergency situations for 11 schools (6 boys and 5 girls schools) in H2.The schools are located in dangerous and exposed areas of H2 with high occurrences of emergency situations due to the increased Israeli military presence in the area. 2 teachers per school will be trained on specific emergency training and evacuation training will be conducted for the students for the entirety of the schools. -Provide the targeted schools with first aid kits and fire extinguishers.The schools are located in marginalized H2 and area C locations so do not receive basic first aid kits and fire extinguishers. 11 first aid kits and 11 fire extinguishers are to be provided for the schools.

Indicators

-Needed essential medications, lab supplies and medical disposables are purchased for Hebron Emergency Center. -255 pregnant and lactating women receiving SRH healthcare services. -7000 patients (2165 males, 2870 females, 1117 boys and 840 girls) have received primary healthcare services. -4 open medical days (2 chronic diseases and 2 women’s health) are conducted in marginalized low accessibility areas. -600 individuals (150 men, 300 women, 90 boys and 60 girls) benefit from the services provided by the open medical days in the marginalized areas. -60 women exposed to GBV cases are provided with the appropriate legal, social and psychosocial services by HWC specialized staff. -15 GBV cases are referred to other specialized organizations. -1000 community members (including 200 males and 800 females) benefit from the psychological and awareness sessions conducted. -11 first aid kits and 11 fire extinguishers are provided for the 11 schools in H2. -22 teachers from the local schools (10 women and 12 men) have received emergency preparedness training. -3008 students (1973 boys and 1035 girls) have received evacuation training in their schools.
