Project Summary

Evictions continue to represent a constant risk for vulnerable communities, including displaced populations living in collective settlements and other urban poor in densely populated areas. The project is focused on addressing forced eviction and housing, Land and property rights violations in three regions in Somalia with prevalence of HLP rights violations. More than 90,000 individuals have been evicted across Somalia in 2021 alone (HLP Sub cluster). Many IDPs end up as squatters on public or private land and are at heightened risk of forced evictions. Most evictions are done without due process, including without prior notice. Authorities have failed to provide adequate notification and compensation to the communities facing eviction, nor have they provided viable relocation or local integration options required by international law, exposing the affected population and survivors to protection risks such as SGBV, among others. Increase in land values and inadequate access and competition over the control of land and land resources have often led to conflicts.
The protection of Housing, Land and Property (HLP) rights is a foundational component within the broader solution framework to facilitate recovery and durable solutions for displaced communities in Somalia. In essence, the project will contribute to progress made by members of the Housing, Land and Property (HLP) sub cluster. Key activities include; i) Access to information services and awareness raising targeting persons displaced, landlords and site leadership ii) Expand outreach and contribute to eviction monitoring systems iii) Eviction prevention and mitigation initiatives with the aim to identify HLP needs through community outreach, referrals and legal counselling iii) improve referrals and screening through the vulnerability assessment to identify those individuals and families at high risk of HLP violations, specifically those at risk of forced evictions iv) facilitating mediation and negotiation for lease agreement to improve tenure documentation v) capacity development /Training of community leaders, camp leaders, local authorities and paralegal interns in Law schools on Housing, Land, and Property (HLP) issues such as security of land tenure, forced evictions, displacement and durable solutions

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

The affected communities will participate in the eviction risk assessment which will inform the analyses, i.e. needs, gender, etc. contained in this project. Specific initiatives designed to increase accountability will be mainstreamed into the project to ensure continuous engagement with the affected communities. Given that local communities in Somalia practice a representative form of community management, meetings and consultations will be held with the legitimate representatives of the affected communities, including those from known marginalized groups, persons with disability and female headed households to over come conflict resolution challenges.
