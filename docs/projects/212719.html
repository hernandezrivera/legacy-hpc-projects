Project Summary

This project delivers a critical, and significant set of interventions to increase the resilience of affected communities by improving access to livelihood opportunities, especially among the most vulnerable households and communities in several communities, in Aleppo Governorate - Aghtrin and Atareb sub-districts to IDPs, returnees and other conflict-affected people at risk of eviction after the EQ, residing in areas directly impacted by the EQ and ongoing hostilities.
The project contributes to the main FSL sector cluster objectives and Priorities by mitigating food insecurity through the Recovery of food production capacities and the creation of rural income-generating opportunities.
By providing income Generation and Livestock activities such as supporting market-driven IGAs related to food processing, including IVT for 100 beneficiaries and Start-up business grants for 80 BNFs.
POINT has conducted a field rapid needs assessment, focusing on the most affected areas, KII, and POINT concentrated on the most important interventions, where immediate response is needed. Therefore, this proposal was built based on evidence-based needs assessment.
The targeted locations were visited physically by the field team, with the attendance of related stakeholders and authorities.
POINT has secured access to the proposed locations through its field offices, also POINT has active programming in the sub-districts delivering similar services and has an active protocol with Turkish authorities. Thus, the proposed activities will be expanded to the already ongoing operations.
The project is planned to be completed in 6 months and includes the following:
Firstly, light rehabilitation of 2 bakeries in the targeted locations.
POINT will ensure the IVT provided matches the market demand and needs of targeted beneficiaries. environment and skills needed for crisis-affected beneficiaries to secure income-generating opportunities that can positively.
Conduct SME training for the 100 BNFs after completing the IVT for 2 months, The trainees who show commitment to attend more than 70% of the IVT will be invited to attend the business management training for 5 days which is provided to enable beneficiaries to prepare business plans that include all aspects of the establishment of small businesses, then POINT will Evaluate and select best business plans and select the accepted 80 and support them with the food processing start-up grant.
POINT will form community committees within the targeted sites to enhance community engagement, participation, and social cohesion.
The project may face challenges such as low participation, stigmatization, and harm to target groups, especially women and PWDs. Mitigation strategies include engaging community leaders, adapting messages, and employing only female staff in specific roles. Unclear beneficiary selection criteria and risks like sexual exploitation require working with local teams and ensuring coordination to avoid duplication. Mitigation involves staff training, code of conduct adherence, and complaint mechanisms. Assumptions include workshop owner commitment and stable markets. Trainee dropout and PWD exclusion are addressed with concise training, transportation stipends, clear contracts, and inclusive facilities. Violations of protection policies are mitigated through training, awareness, and complaint mechanisms. Tensions between community groups are managed through effective engagement. Exclusion due to local norms is countered with gender balance, suitable professions, and proven inclusive practices. Specific measures target PWD inclusion, including prioritization, monitoring, referrals, and staff capacity building. Finally, adherence to protection principles is ensured through policies, training, and awareness initiatives.
