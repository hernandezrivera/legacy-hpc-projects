Project Summary

The project will be utilizing the private sector service providers and rely on community-based structure to provide life-saving WASH immediate term interventions in Al Quoz, Kadugli localities, South Kordofan as well as Geisan, Ed Damazine localities, Blue Nile states respectively. This will include infrastructure repair and maintenance in areas where supply is disrupted including health facilities and the establishment of distribution points, including in gathering and transit points. Essential WASH supplies and NFI emergency sanitation and handwashing/bathing items will be provided in the hotspot locations. Hygiene promotion and household water treatment practices will be promoted as an immediate alternative, especially for those reliant on surface/river water. WASH support, emergency latrines, showers for arrivals; hygiene items, hygiene promotion interventions, waste management, as well as hygiene supplies; and menstrual hygiene management for adolescent girls will further be provided.

1 - Needs

The Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene situation (WASH) as well as the protection status of women and girls has deteriorated due to the clashes between the Sudanese Armed Forces and the Rapid Support Forces that broke on April 15, 2023 with its negative impacts on the already existing situation in terms of political instability, economic crisis, non-functional or aging WASH infrastructure, increased concerns regarding the prevention of gender-based violence in the two targeted states. The targeted localities in the two states (South Kordofan: Al Quoz and Kadugli 466K people in need; Blue Nile "at risk areas": Geisan and Ed Damazine 360K people in need) ,is also grappling with severe water shortages caused by blackouts, fuel scarcity and lack of supplies. The populations in the targeted areas suffer from food insecurity coupled with the already prevailing extreme poverty has increased and they cannot afford WASH services and practices. South Kordofan and Blue Nile is one of the prone-to-floods states of Sudan. Even acute respiratory infections – another major cause of children’s mortality- are closely linked to WASH gaps. Sanitation and handwashing represent crucial barriers to faecal-oral contamination, and OD elimination can reduce diarrhoeal morbidity by 36%. The influx of displaced persons from different neighboring states has further highlighted WASH gaps, and exacerbated the need for improved access to clean water and WASH services as communities need to ensure regular sanitary practices such as hand washing to limit the spread of disease transmission. Vulnerable people with specific needs, including unaccompanied and separated children, survivors of GBV, women-headed households, elderly persons, and persons with disabilities with health concerns, are in need of WASH services. This population group often has lesser coping mechanisms to mitigate the multiple risks they face and are often confronted with additional barriers to meet their basic needs and access their rights.

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

FPDO has a good experience in the targeted ‎areas and totally familiar with the context of ‎‎refugee communities residing there. This project is using the (Do No Harm) approach and is designed to minimize harm by supporting local capacities through trainings and awareness sessions to build peace. protection of the targeted community is mainstreamed as a cross cutting theme‎.‎ The project team will share regular information with targeted community to ensure their knowledge about their basic rights and the available protection services in the area and how to access them. FPDO will strive to avoid tensions between host communities and SSRs through its holistic approach to projects whereby both old and new SSRs caseload are targeted based on the prioritization of needs. FPDO will make sure that over the project lifetime beneficiaries will not be exposed to any kind of physical or psychological risks. FPDO, will consider and apply neutrality and nondiscrimination based on age, gender, or group especially in the selection of promoters, construction labors, sessions, and trainings’ target individuals. And ensure all beneficiaries has equal and free access to services. The project activities are designed in a way that promotes protection and safety e.g. the construction of latrines will take into consideration protection aspects, dignity, and privacy Furthermore, FPDO will work closely with community and local authorities to avail awareness and improve their knowledge to GBV issues through sessions. Open defecation also causes loss of dignity, especially for women and girls, loss of privacy and brings with it protection concerns when women and girls go out to defecate in the open. The project efforts shall be directed towards eliminating open defecation and ensuring daily cleaning of latrines. proper use and cleaning of latrines, will also help women and girls to safely manage their menstrual periods, safe their dignity, privacy, and mitigate the protection concerns women may face as they constitute about 57% of the overall population.
