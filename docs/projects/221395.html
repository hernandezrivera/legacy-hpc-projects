1. Needs

South Sudan Humanitarian Needs Overview estimates that 9.3 million people require humanitarian assistance in 2025. The increase of population movements in 2024 due to continue conflict in Sudan, South Sudan continuous to receive returns, and and refuges from Sudan, the flooding and food insecurity continued to drive humanitarian needs, a bout 4.2 million South Sudanese are currently internally displaced across the country and will need shelter non food items, many of them have been forced to be displaced multiple times, in search of better living conditions. The recent floods in South Sudan about 1.4 million people remain affected by flooding across 43 counties including Abyei Administrative Area. More than 379,000 people are flood-displaced across 22 counties, in Jonglei, Upper Nile, Warrap, Northern Bahr el Ghazal, Unity, and Eastern Equatoria. The impact of rampant flooding in South Sudan have also worsened the soil capillarity resulting rise of water level above natural surface level at some points, creating ponds and swaps at many places. Flood recurrence in 2024 and higher water level in most of the areas in Unity and Jonglei state has not allowed communities to return to their areas of origin or villages hence relying solemnly on emergency support for non-food items services due to living in higher ground or congested sites, some communities settling with host communities thereby putting additional burden on the limited available shelter services in less flooded area. The Continues of heavy rains since May and increase level of River Nile combined with controlled water releases from Lake Victoria, have significantly raised level of Nile affecting about 1.4 million people across South Sudan, Currently the flood water level is increasing in Warrap areas the state which is currently hosting IDPs the case load expected to increase in case any further flooding happens in the area. In some areas the situation may get even worse if the flooding magnitude came with higher magnitude than expected and if the dykes are breached when the river water level continue to increase.The flood emergency expected in some other locations based on the map shows areas where floodwater has been detected in multiple Months between May and November 2024. Unity, Warrap, Jonglei and Upper Nile are among the worst affected states. Wetlands are indicated on the map as these areas are characterized by seasonal flooding, whilst flood extent beyond these areas represents more exceptional flooding. A recurrence of flooding combined with soils low capillarity, higher ground water table and filled aquafer led flood on vast areas and expected to push communities in many states in the country to move to higher grounds for longer period of times.Norwegian Church Aid (NCA) demonstrates unwavering commitment and active engagement in providing vital emergency support, particularly focusing on life saving WASH programming within South Sudan's Northern Bahr Ghazal, Upper Nile and WarrapSouth Sudan. NCA response strategy is founded on our extensive experience in emergency interventions and our ongoing efforts in NFI, Multipurpose Cash, and Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene (WASH) programs. With this project, NCA targeted goal is to assist approximately 25,000 individuals, specifically aiming to reach 12,000 males and 13,000 females within these target areas. Our strategic project focuses on cash voucher assistance, the distribution of shelter materials, and household non-food items, integrating these interventions within our broader emergency response initiatives. These efforts are part of NCA's holistic approach, intertwining Gender-Based Violence prevention and WASH programs within South Sudan. By implementing these strategic interventions, our project seeks not only to provide immediate relief but also to instill resilience and contribute to the long-term recovery of communities grappling with these crises.
