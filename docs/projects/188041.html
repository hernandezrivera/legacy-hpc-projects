Project Summary

Since March 2011, Syria has faced protracted conflict causing loss of human lives, significant displacement, and migration, weakened social services, destruction of basic productive infrastructure, disruption of livelihoods, and deterioration of rule of law. Combined, this has undermined social cohesion and stability and contributed to an overall decrease in the ability to meet basic needs and access services and opportunities within Syria. In Idleb, nearly half of the population are IDPs (44%) and one of the main drivers of food insecurity in Idleb is a high concentration of IDPs and their increasing vulnerability.
SKT is seeking support to improve the food security of 2,380 households as 11,900 individuals within Idleb region living in camps through food basket distribution. SKT will distribute 2,380 food baskets to the beneficiaries in the following camps Omahat Al Moumenien, Al Azraq, Al Aman, Al Madenh Monarh, Dalama, Jabal Harim, Abna`a Al Ghab, Tajmae Aleiza, Kafr Hum, Alsafsafh, Al Taakhi, Alzeraah, and Ayidoun in Idleb governorate as one food basket will be distributed to each family over a period of 4 months to ensure a response to time-critical requirements of the vulnerable families, including women headed households. Therefore, this project will contribute to other ongoing and planned food security and livelihood interventions within Idleb to person, 10-12% of which come from protein and 17% from fat accurate and sustain gains in line with cluster sector objectives and plans. SKT food basket will provide 2,100 kcal according to WFP/cluster guideline (1791.4 Kcal provided by the food basket, complemented by 200-400 Kcal by the bread bags). In addition, SKT has an ongoing bread distribution project within the same selected geographical location which will supplement the food kits distribution. Hence, families' nutritional status will be increased and therefore will lessen the burden of buying bread as each HHs will get a free bread bag on a daily basis. The food basket takes into account beneficiary preference in commodity type and quantity and will be delivered on a daily basis within a period of 4 months.
SKT will coordinate its activities with other organizations providing food security support in the same local areas to ensure the broadest coverage of persons of concern not otherwise receiving food kits and to avoid duplication and enhance complementarity where feasible. This cooperation will be through field meetings, e-mails, Skype, including essential participants, such as camp managers, local councils, and community leaders, in addition to active and regular coordination with the FSL cluster. This practice is to avoid any potential overlaps and improve knowledge of good practices and lessons learned. SKT has good communication with Turkish Red crescent may be used to mitigate any risk that may face exporting Flour and Sugar to Syria. SKT has active humanitarian registration and maintains good relations with Turkish border authorities.
At the end of the project, the targeted beneficiaries will have their nutritional state improved by 60% based on the assessment led by SKT and the comparison of the initial and final HHs dietary diversity situation. The different monitoring tools implemented by SKT in order to measure the improvement of the nutritional state of targeted beneficiaries will be selected in accordance with FSL Cluster indicators.

4 - Needs

SKT conducted a rapid need assessment (RNA) in 25 camps in Harim and Salqin locations. The methodology of data collecting included key informant interviews (KIIs) with camps managers, members of the administrative committees, sector experts, and representatives of local authorities (LA). The selection of key informants was based on the nature of each section in the assessment and took into consideration that the local authorities, community leaders and camps managers are commonly trusted resources for this information.As per the findings: 1- Almost all people in surveyed camps faced difficulties accessing enough food due to:- No resources/income to purchase food.- High food prices.2- A majority of people purchased on credit or borrowed to obtain food and bread.3- Looking for income opportunities, sharing items, borrowing items, and taking credit / going into debt were the most common things people do when they could not meet their basic food needs.4- When asked about their needs and priorities, the majority have defined food baskets as their priority need.Based on the above, SKT will provide life-sustaining food assistance (GFA) to 2,382 HHs in need of critical and sustained food assistance in 13 camps through GFA. The GFA will be supplemented with bread bag distribution for each family for a period of three months. Regarding the targeted beneficiaries, SKT is putting particular attention to targeting both newly arrived IDPs as well as previously displaced people set

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

SKT during implementing the activities of the project may face these risks according to the project's activities. Protection .1- Risk : The presence of people infected with Covid-19 may come to receive the baskets .Likelihood: mediumImpact: highMitigation: Coordination will be made with camp managers and nearby health centers in order to avoid these issues.Risk 2: Crowding of women during the distribution of food baskets.Likelihood: Low.Mitigation: SKT will hire females for the distribution process.Risk 3: The displacement and arrival of new families to the targeted camps.Likelihood: HighImpact: highMitigation: Coordination with the SCHF team in the event of a surplus in the budget or through coordination with the organizations working in the field, under the umbrella and recommendations of the FSL cluster.Risk 4: Risk of harassment of women and girls by the humanitarian workers.Likelihood: medium.Impact: high. Mitigation: establishing a confidential complaint mechanism and enhancing accountability mechanisms besides training the staff to deal with beneficiaries in addition to providing workers with training on humanitarian standards and signing a code of conduct. Risk 5: in the distribution area, we may face a risk of harassment for female beneficiaries by male beneficiaries.Likelihood: High. Impact: High. Mitigation: we will conduct separate distribution timed for males and females.


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

The staff of the project will have awareness sessions on GBV and PSEA to ensure that all of them are aware of dealing with these cases and able to refer them to the respective focal point in the area based on Protection Cluster shared the list of focal points. Female field staff will be hired to increase the protection of women and girls, and accountability boxes, as well as 2 complaint numbers, will be available.


11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Additional numbers in form of IDPs huge waves reach project implementation areas. 2- Closure of Bab Al Hawa crossing border resulting in zero or limited access for staff or goods inside Idleb 3- Lack of access to targeted areas for the team inside Idleb due to Security situation 4- Limited capacity for staff to transfer funds to Idleb due to Turkish regulations 5- Turnover of staff 6- Overlapping of project activities and double agreement by local councils as a result of a lack of coordination. 7- Syrian regime offensive onto Idleb governorate (airstrikes, a ground offensive, chemical attacks…) 8- Violent armed conflict between opposition armed groups in Idleb. 9- BNFs are using the voucher for the unplanned activities, or selling it to the local suppliers Mitigation: 1-Keep close coordination with the local councils and active organizations in the targeted area in order to harmonize the intervention and advocate through FSL cluster to the donors to address the gap and big demand for the food. 2-SKT will follow the Hawa system to mitigate any risk related to border closing 3- SKT has good coordination with camp management allows reaching project location. in severe cases, remote management with the field staff accommodating and working in the target areas will be implemented after informing HPF and cluster. 4- SKT will shift project activities to a new location in case of shifting the control lines, or displacing the BNFs to new areas after getting the needed approval.
