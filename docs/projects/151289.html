Project Summary

The overall objective of the project is to provide immediate access to food and livelihood support to the vulnerable women headed household and food insecure populace of Dhusamareeb,Adado,Abdwak and Galkacayo in Galgadud as well as Cadale in middle Shabelle region in Somalia that were most affected by the recurrent conflict,flooding and movement restriction. The project entails a cash for work component for pastoral livelihood locations which involves the rehabilitation/construction of 12 communal water catchments in the aforementioned districts in order to reduce the vulnerability and enhance resilience of the target beneficiary households composed of 60 skilled laborers and 100 unskilled laborers who will be engaged for 4 months and an unconditional cash grant aspect for 600 labor inactive rural households in rural in the target region. Through this project PDA will ensure to distribute emergency agricultural inputs packages containing land preparation support through provision of seeds (sorghum, maize and cowpeas seeds) to 2000 (1400 women and 600 male ) agro-pastoral HHs in Cadale district of middle Shabelle andDhusamareeb,Abdwak,Adado and Galkacayo in Galgadud region. The project will also capacity build 600 Lead Farmers of (agropastoral livelihoods in middle Shabelle and Galgadud regions, of which (300 are female, while 300 are male) on good Agricultural Practice (GAP) and Integrated Pest Practices Management (IPPM) through Farmer Field School (FFS).
PDA will closely coordinates with food security sub cluster and other humanitarian actors to ensure coordinated response to the affected women, men, boys and girls in the target region.It will also ensure communities are involved in all decision of project inception, implementation and close up to ensure community ownership and sustainability of the project.

Needs

The recently released FSNAU and FEWS-NET post-Gu assessment results show continued food security improvements in Somalia. However, acute food insecurity, malnutrition and livelihood needs remain. These needs require sustained humanitarian assistance in 2019 to protect progress and combat the acute food and livelihoods insecurity that prevails among the wider population in Somalia. Humanitarian needs overview (Food security cluster) indicate 3.4 m people are currently in needs of food security response of which 49% are Male and 51% Female. This includes a 50 percent decrease in the number of people in IPC phase 3 or worse. When compared to the preceding Deyr season, the overall number of people in need decreased from 5.4 million to 4.6 million,the total number of acutely food insecure people has decreased by 25% between GU 2018 and Gu 2017. The population in IPC 4 and 3 also show a significant reduction (50%) over the last two years. The People in Need (PIN) are populations who lost most of their animals, particularly poor households across the country and became destitute during the 2016-2017 severe drought; as well as these affected by flooding in April and May 2018, particularly in the riverine and low-lying areas and cyclones in North West and East, Rural, IDPs and urban poor in severe acute insecurity phases (crisis and worse) because of a combination of factors, such key driving factors include climate change, residual impact of drought and cyclone sagar in the north east, GU rain insufficiency in some areas,flooding,displacement and fall armyworm. However, PDA current operation areas in Cadale district, Dhusamareeb, Adado and Abdwak have had less rainfall and were severely affected by 2017 drought will therefore, intend to provide coordinate response to the target areas.

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

The food security risk related to exclusion of the vulnerable population upon displacement in IDPs settlement are, poor information sharing, discrimination in the distribution of seeds and other agricultural input, poor criteria in the selection of vulnerable and most needs boys, girls, women and disadvantage group for conditional an unconditional cash as well as poor representation of women in village relief committee. To mitigate this PDA will ensure to create proper coordination and information collection and analysis platform for the target men, women, boys and girls this will minimize the exclusion of such special group as disable and marginalized groups, PDA will ensure target community are selected based on their vulnerability giving that women household headed with severely malnourished children boys, girls are most vulnerable hence this will reduce the chances of enlisting such beneficiaries. To mitigate the issue of poor representation PDA will give upper hand to the women to enable them highly participate in these avenues, however, through closer coordination all the necessary information on beneficiaries will be gathered and considered.

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

Prevention of risk associated with exclusion on gender and age are, lack of consultation among the adolescent girls, exclusion of special group in decision making process in the IDPs might hinder proper advocacy and awareness among the disable, minority and marginalized population in the Idps and host communities hence poor diversity within the communities. It is also highly luckily that women and girls will be excluded from the various employment and education opportunities within the camps hence this increase the disparities within the protection coordination cluster.On mitigation PDA shall propose to have equal men, women, boys and girls in both services centered activities as well as employment within the target/operational areas, Also on the issue of decision making PDA shall route for proper representation of all interested group within the society such as disable, minority and marginalized as well as elderly and single mothers in these targeted camps.it will also provide proper coordination mechanism that will enable the IDPs leaders and project team work in harmoniously for preservation of beneficiary data and without any alteration of their information to avoid any risk of discrimination among the beneficiaries.

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

In the refugee camps the following might be some of the main risk facing the target population that may negatively impact the benefices in the camps these include; poor IDPs layout, high cases of SGBV, poor beneficiaries data protection, violence and an armed youth within the camp,as well as military and militia cross fire, explosion remnant of war and lack or rather limited information sharing and feedback of compliant mechanism in place to ensure safety and dignity of the vulnerable women,girls,boys and men in the violence areas. This is coupled with separation and sexual exploitation as well as undocumented needs of the vulnerable group.To mitigate this PDA however, intends to carry out properly IDPs leaders consultation and involvement of all stakeholders in ensuring the IDPs layout is of one that will ensure easy access to all beneficiary services within the camps, PDA will trained community IDPs managers on simple surveying of the camps as well as management details of these IDPs hence improve the public relation on the target location. To prevent the rampant and attended sexual exploitation among the vulnerable girls and women in the camps PDA will increase social mobilization and awareness aspect within the camps as well as train the community gatekeepers on the proper reporting of these verse hence improve coordination between the community leaders, staff and the local authority for prosecution and cultural solution for the such cases within the camps. However, in the IDPs set up there are extremist violence youth who terrorize vulnerable people hence create community tension as well as causing disharmony among the affected population this will however, be handle through youth awareness creation on their safety and dignity to shun away from engaging from such violence and as well focus their future PDA will accelerate the community active engagement with youth in the IDPs and as well create a better avenue for them to get empowered.
