Project Summary

the program will ensure that women and girls are at risk / survivors of violence access quality GBV services through mapping of local actors: community based organizations, civil society organizations ( CSO) to address violence perpetrated against girls and women through community outreach activities: awareness raising on GBV prevention and mitigation aiming at changing social norms, beliefs and practices. in addition, capacity strengthening on GBV response targeting non GBV actors, humanitarian workers as well as local structures heads ( traditional and religious leaders). furthermore, protection core materials shall be distributed to women/ girls at risk / survivor as well as a livelihood package for women/ girls survivors of GBV.

1 - Needs

The Nigerian Bureau of Statistics (NBS) in its Q4 2016 report confirmed a consistent pattern of worsening unemployment in the country, rising from 9.9 % in 2015 to 33.3 % in 2022, with a corresponding 42.5% youth unemployment rate. The North-eastern Nigeria (the region most affected by the Boko Haram insurgency) has experienced chronic under-development and higher unemployment rates when compared to other states. Due to the insurgency, poverty indicators continue to worsen – despite overall poverty decrease in every other region in the country. Adamawa, Yobe, and the Borno States are the most affected by the conflict, related military counter operations, and mass internal displacement outside and within each state. Borno and Adamawa States, dominated by agriculture and farming activities, were hampered within the last 10-12 years. In addition, all commercial activities were completely paralyzed. The growing number of unemployed youths contributed negatively to the insurgency and the governments of the two states have developed strategies to train vulnerable youth groups by adopting Technical and Vocational training packages for the displaced youths. Almost all the investigations carried out on the root cause of the Boko Haram insurgency by the GON and other non-governmental bodies within and outside the country have identified un-employment as one of the root causes of the problem. Therefore, the affected state governments are paying more attention to vocational and skill development in its poverty alleviation and youth economic empowerment programmes. To secure a conducive life for Internally Displaced Persons (IDPs), returnees and host community members it is proper to design a post settlement plan of sustainable livelihood for youths and women in the affected communities to effectively improve their standard of living and weaned them off vulnerability that exposes them to radicalization and other negative coping strategies

2 - Activities

all beneficiaries: women and girls/ boys with protection concerned shall be identified, registered and provided with protection core materials assistance. in addition, persons of concerns with others needs shall be provided with information on other service availability and accessibility; referred to other services providers and follow up for service provision. Furthermore, community outreach services ( awareness on GBV prevention , mitigation; GBV response services, PSS capacity strengthening to non GBV specialized workers, humanitarian workers) shall be delivered to women / girls at risk/ survivors. moreover, a livelihood package shall be provided to women and girls survivors of GBV.

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

the program in itself is a protection program. to enforce protection throughout the project cycle, humanitarian workers including local actors ( traditional and religious leaders) capacities shall be built on PSEA. in addition, all the partners involved shall be briefed on the organization's code of conduct and sign the commitment to respect the PSEA policy. however the program actors shall have a respectful relationship with local actors to maintain the principle of " DO NO HARM".
