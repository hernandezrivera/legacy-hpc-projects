Project Summary

Strengthen prevention and management of severe acute malnutrition among most vulnerable population (children 6 59 months and pregnant and lactating women) in in East, Adamawa and North regions of Cameroon.
29,000 children (13,920 boys and 15,080 girls U5 SAM)
70,300 children U2 admitted in the Homebased Food fortification programme
90,000 people (M/W) sensitized including 35,000 women trained for SAM detection (mother MUAC)
Activities or outputs
*Assurer la mise en œuvre ciblée des activités de promotion de l'alimentation du nourrisson et du jeune enfant en situation d'urgence
*Formation des personnels de santé sur les interventions nutritionnelles (PCIMAS, ANJE…)
* Mise en œuvre des formations des mamans PB
* Mise en œuvre du programme de fortification alimentaire à domicile
* Prise en charge intégrée des cas de malnutrition aiguë sévère (PCIMA-S)
* Mise en œuvre d’un paquet d’activités intégrées à la PCIMAS (Wash in Nut, VIH/Nut…)
Indicators and targets
Number of children under five admitted in the PCIMAS program: 29,000
Program performance: Cured rate (> 75%), Dropout rate (< 15%), death rate (<10%)
Number of health staff trained on PCIMAS and IYCF: 428
Number of PB mere trained: 35,000
Number of children who received MNPs: 70,360
Number of people sensitized on IYCF: 90,000

Besoins / Needs

The results of the latest nutrition survey (with the SMART method) conducted in November 2019 by UNICEF and the government of Cameroon showed GAM rate of 6.2% in the North, 3.8% in Adamaoua and 5.5% in the East with 1.3%, 0.3% and 0.7% of SAM respectively for the same regions. For chronic malnutrition, the regions were in an alert situation with rates of 37.3% in East and Adamawa and 34.9% in North.Projection of NeedsIt is estimated 93,000 would be in need of treatment for acute malnutrition in 2020 amongst whom 30,000 boys and girls who will suffer from severe acute malnutrition.
