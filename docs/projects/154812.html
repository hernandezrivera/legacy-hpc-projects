Project Summary

According to the Ministry of Migration and Displacement, about 2,000 returnees are arriving in Mosul every day, mainly in the south and east of the city. Darya aid efforts are focusing on providing food assistance for 2,000 families through Voucher distribution. Darya will target returnee, and host community families in Mosul focusing on out of camps areas. The number of targeted households will be 2000 HHs. The intended vouchers (equivalent food basket ) to be distributed has been designed in consultation with the local beneficiaries as per their actual food intake calories and beneficiaries desire. It has been calculated that each person will get an estimated 1800 calories per day for approximately 26-30 days for a family of 5-6 members based on the family size. The project will target returnees, and host communities in the targeted areas and select the beneficiaries among the most vulnerable conflict affected people based on specific selection criteria such as female headed household, size of family, people with special needs, households with children under 5, pregnant and lactating women, and elderly people. Overall objective of the project is "The most food insecure returnees and host community in Mosul have access to life-saving and nutritious food throughout the year". The specific objectives are: to improve the diversity and consumption household and food security; to ensure continued and regular access to food for the vulnerable population; to allow the households to purchase diverse food commodities; to improve self-reliance and coping capacities of people in need by protecting, restoring and promoting their livelihoods through vouchers transfer; to protect and rehabilitate livelihoods for the vulnerable population at risk of hunger and malnutrition; 2000 insecure returnees and host community beneficiaries in Mosul , are able to meet their basic food and non-food needs through the receipt of immediate, monthly voucher transfers. The main activities for this project are; 1. Coordination with relevant cluster, working I/NGOs, and Local authorities; 2. Conduct a quick field visit to identify beneficiaries’ lists; 3. Develop, design and printing the vouchers; 4. Provision and distribution of the vouchers to the targeted beneficiaries .The vouchers will be printed and monthly voucher distribution to beneficiaries, with the voucher valid for a month for purchasing food items Each voucher equivalent to 22,000 IRQ or 20 USD per month. The household can transfer voucher to the food at any shop already been contract.5. Monitoring and Reporting. The project duration is 12 months , the intended food basket as voucher will be distributed monthly for each householder (2000 HHs) of family (5-6 members) Every HHs will receive 20$ monthly through the vouchers, which will allow them to improve their food security through the year , as the voucher will be used to buy family food rations. The total budget estimate for thisproject is 763,600 $.

Protection Risk Analysis

In this project, the process for making a risk analysis will be done by Darya Teams. Step 1: Establishing focus group for conducting the risk analysis. Led by the programme team the group should include Vulnerability Analysis Mapping (VAM) Officer, finance, procurement/administration, security, logistics, human resources and possibly partners. Step 2: Based on the context and nature of the this project, agree on a broad list of relevant risks (internal, external, macro and micro). The Macro and Micro risks meanings are as follows: Macro risks are country-level, agro-ecological or socio-economic zone risks (e.g. inflation, supply, quality and safety, capacity of financial institutions, security, corruption, fraud etc.). Micro risks are specific to the implementation of vouchers in the context of specific project city, town or village. This project looks at issues such as shop capacity, shop availability, localized price fixing, safety of delivery arrangement, additional costs to beneficiaries in terms of transport etc. Step 3: Divide and evaluate the risks: • Manageable risks – risks that can be mitigated by precautions or contingencies. • Non-manageable risks – risks that cannot be mitigated. Identify the extent and severity of these risks. Darya takes special care where vouchers could negatively affect Darya reputation, corporate strategic objectives, accountability requirements, financial safeguards or the security of staff or beneficiaries. Step 4: If the risks are manageable or the non-manageable risks are not so severe as to undermine the project: • establishing the limit at which a manageable risk becomes non-manageable, and if this will change, the alternative transfer modality to be used. • Establishing contingency measures for dealing with each manageable risk. • Assess and plan for contingency practicalities, especially if this entails reverting to food distribution, as this has considerable. Darya will consider in risk analysis these issues •• Corruption and fraud (internal and external) •• Gender (disadvantages for women and children) •• Design and implementation capacities (partners) •• Monitoring (cost, availability and accessibility) •• Targeting and reliability of systems for beneficiary identification •• Political interference in beneficiary selection •• Size of market compared to beneficiary numbers (demand exceeding supply) •• Potential negative implications on non–beneficiaries (risk of doing harm) •• Financial institutions delivering cash making payments (ability, reliability, reach and potential for fraud) •• Shops’ non-compliance with food and price standards/quantities •• Counterfeiting of vouchers (internal and external risks) •• Authorities inability or unwillingness to enforce quality standards for food chain between suppliers and shops (upstream quality assurance) •• Security of Darya staff, partners and beneficiaries •• Effect on food-based programme, especially if implemented nearby •• Implications for Darya, partners and beneficiaries if abandoning vouchers modality becomes necessary •• Long–term implications for the government in terms of sustainability •• Potential for creating dependency and sense of entitlement among beneficiaries. Mitigation Measures

Counter measures for this project risks by Darya team are; •• The cards are protected by a PIN number. When lost or stolen, the beneficiary will inform the implementing partner, who will contact Darya. Darya will then contact the technical partner so the card is stopped and a replacement issued. •• Agree on fixed price for fixed period with selected shops. •• Adjust /voucher value based on market prices. •• Set a maximum limit for acceptable price increases. •• Design system favoring free competition. •• Monitor, identify and disqualify offending shops. •• Sensitize shops and beneficiaries to voucher regulations. •• Monitor to identify occasional or systematic abuse. •• Disqualify or replace if systematic abuse is found. •• If for short period: allow carry-over of vouchers. •• If for long period: plan for food distribution. •• Spread value over time and in smaller amounts. •• Consider including conditional such as work. •• Strengthen targeting arrangements. •• Consider physical verification of beneficiary list •• Darya will liaise with the technical provider to provide alternative solutions in case of network failure. In the event of a network failure, the beneficiary will inform the implementing partner, who will inform Darya. Darya will then contact the technical provider for action.

How is your project accountable to affected population

Gender is mainstreamed in all Darya interventions. Darya staff will enlist the participation of women, children (boys and girls) and men in project activities as much as possible and as appropriate. Darya recognizes that the rights of children and women are mutually reinforcing. This project is targeting total of 2000 household, equivalent to 10,000.This target group is based on their vulnerability in the community with focus of women and girls needs.This project is targeting 50% girls and women to ensure that women and children nutritional status is improved and have better food intake. To be accountable to the targeted communities and donors, project activity information will be shared by Darya team with beneficiary communities. Distribution standards will be made accessible to the community. A help desk will set up at the distribution point, which will be able to provide more background and contact information. Discussions with beneficiaries will be done during project monitoring to make sure that their voices and feedback are heard. Feedback/Complaint: Darya is displaying complaint phone number and an email in the banner that is put up in every distribution where beneficiaries can call/send email placing their complaint, Based on the complaint seriousness the complain is registered and recorded in the complaint system and then forwarded by the M&E Coordinator to the relevant department and Darya management. When implementing voucher programme, Darya program staff must consider the most appropriate and convenient methods for beneficiaries to provide positive and negative feedback. This project will have a minimum of one feedback mechanism, although two are ideal. Feedback mechanisms will be vary depending upon local capabilities. Here Darya Team will include a well-publicized phone number for reporting fraud or abuse, SMS, email, a locked box for paper feedback, individual surveys and/or interviews. When Darya choosing between different mechanisms, at least one will provide beneficiaries with a way to contact Darya directly. Feedback and complaints will be processed by a designated team member who has been trained in confidentiality and processing feedback. Ideally, this person is not directly involved in implementing the program. Training both a female and male staff member in processing complaints will be helpful, particularly given the type of complaint. If one complaint mechanism involves a program implementer – as is typical – then the secondary mechanism will bypass team members directly involved in program implementation like M&E team member.
