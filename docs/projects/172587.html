Project Summary

The project considers the vulnerabilities of rural and urban populations as a result of multiple hazards, mainly drought, ﬂoods and COVID-19 worsened by the on-going economic crisis. The primary attention is given to children under ﬁve years at risk of dying due to acute malnutrition, providing life-saving nutrition treatment to 47,955 children, and protecting the nutritional status of 596,157 children aged 6-23 months and 274,281 pregnant and lactating women from deteriorating. The response will also target school age children and adolescents with advocacy and support to access community- and school-based package of interventions. Under this project, UNICEF will therefore target 38 districts with a high intensity nutrition emergency intervention package while the remaining districts will be supported through systems strengthening and intensive monitoring. The main outputs for the response will be:
 Nutrition commodities pre-positioned and supply chain management strengthened.
 Emergency related capacity building activities conducted for facility and community-based health workers.
 Care givers and mothers of children below two years capacitated on optimal maternal, infant and young child nutrition and care in emergencies.
 Nutrition information management strengthened at all levels.
 Nutrition cluster coordination strengthened.

1 - Activities

Early detection of acute malnutrition through active screening and early referral for treatment. Provision of live saving services through management of acute malnutrition through in and outpatient care.Provision of standard IYCF-e package through capacity building for community volunteers and health workers. Supporting the trained cadres to offer IYCF-e counseling for caregivers of children 6-23 months and encourage the use support groups and food demonstrations using fuel efficient stoves. Encourage optimum complementary feeding through the use of cooking demonstrations in support groups using fuel efficient stoves.Support implementation of the micronutrient program by ensuring vitamin A supplementation and micronutrient powders for all children 6-59months as well as iron and folic acid supplementation for pregnant and lactating women. Provision of cash transfers or vouchers and supplementary feeding to about 2000 young children and pregnant and lactating women in Harare and Gutu districts .Procure and distribute essential nutrition commodities for treatment and prevention of acute malnutrition in all rural and urban locations in the country.The project will also aim to conduct Nutrition SMART assessments and nutrition surveillance in the 8 Urban and 30 Rural areas observing high levels of acute malnutrition to inform programming. Accountability, monitoring and evaluation capacities will be established as well as supporting data quality assurance activities for community and health level workers. Support coordination at national and sub-national levels for coordinated response in the priority districtsVarious mechanisms will be employed to consult communities on intervention issues from planning to implementation stages of the project.

8 - Accountability to Affected People
8.1 - Describe how your project was designed in consultation with communities? How will you incorporate communities in project implementation and what feedback mechanisms do you have in place?

Overall the project is committed to being accountable to the people it serves, through listening and responding to their need’s perspectives and priorities.Communities will be involved from the planning stages through engagement of local leadership such as the members of the HCC and local community leaders.Lessons learnt and feedback from previous implementation of similar projects will also inform our programming.Feedback from the community will be gathered through the use of toll free lines, community dialogues, exit interviews, suggestion boxes at health facilities and also suggestion boxes with mobile teams doing outreach activities.

9 - Environmental considerations
9.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project will promote energy saving measures, such as use of fuel-efficient stoves and cooking techniques, fast cooking foods and consider using cleaner energy sources. Promotion of use of fuel-efficient cook stoves helps prevent deforestation and they also minimise depletion of Ozone layer through reduction of carbon emission.Safe disposal of empty packaging materials obtained from products used in treatment of acute malnutrition and micronutrient supplementation will be established as mother and caregivers will receive adequate education in that regard.
