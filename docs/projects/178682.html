Project Summary

Hajin is a sub-district that located in Abu Kamal district in Deir Ez Zor Governorate.
Area is currently controlled by The Syrian Democratic Forces SDF since 2017 after kicking out the Islamic State IS from area after intensive military conflicts. even before start crisis, in 2011, was neglected by the government of Syria, and public health and service indicators were relatively low comparing to other provinces in Syria.
Crisis aggravated the humanitarian situation and deteriorated infrastructure and service delivery.
Intensification of hostilities in some areas of country has had a negative impact on thousands of children who were not able to access education opportunities, especially in Deir Ez Zor governorate. With Islamic State recently leaving the areas, the affected population’s needs remain acute in north-east.
In addition, winter will bring upon a new set of hurdles for children accessing schools. With the harsh winters in the north east, the education sector will be impacted greatly, and students will deter from going to school where no heaters are available.
Based on HNO 2021 1,350 attacks on education and medical facilities during the crisis and related personnel as Syria Monitoring and Reporting Mechanism (MRM) verified 61 attacks on schools and education personnel in 2020.
To respond to the humanitarian crisis over there is remarkable needs in multiple areas.
WHOSD has recently conducted a needs assessment report to better understand the current response and existing gaps and needs.
4 communities in Hajin sub-district; Hajin, Bahra, Abu Hasan, Abu Alkhatir communities will be targeted.
Intervention will aim to deliver Education services using shared financial and human resources to achieve optimistic results in communities mentioned. Importance of this project lies behind its impact to increase the school attendance rate. It is a fact that the education system in target area is completely collapsed and needs a further support to respond to the emergency needs to strengthen the community resilience and to start preparing for the development phase after end of crisis in Syria.
Schools and TECs: Delivered education services to children in 17 schools and 4 temporary learning centers.
WHOSD will contract with building contractor/s to repair classrooms, corridors, stairs, yards, and the perimeter fences. support education facilities with heaters and fuel for classrooms in winter weather (March, and April months). In addition, will provide students and the schools’ staff with education kits to facilitate education in schools and encourage the community to send their children to schools. To better enhance the education services in the target areas, WHOSD will rent 4 centers in the 4 target communities (1 center per community) to be as TECs after equipping them with necessary stuff in order to deliver non-formal education. Along with the mandate of WHOSD to improve access of vulnerable people to humanitarian services, WHOSD will dedicate six vehicles (14 seats of each) to transport children with disabilities, motor difficulties, and who are based in far places to the education premises (2 in Hajin, 2 in Bahra, 1 in Abu Hasan, and 1 in Abu Alkhatir).
community: four outreach teams will be raising awareness to encourage parents and caregivers to send their children to schools and TECs after informing them about the available services and the changes to the education facilities that occurred due to this project. i.e., the schools became safer and better (Information, Education, and Communication IEC campaigns) to encourage the community and households to send their children to schools as part of the Back to Learning BTL aim of this project.
WHOSD will train the target staff on CP, basic numeracy and literacy (Arabic, mathematics, and English).
