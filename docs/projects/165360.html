Project Summary

We aim through this project to improve access to agricultural facilities in the project areas through rehabilitation of infrastructure within a comprehensive intervention through the targeted areas, these services will be provided to farmers who have difficulty accessing their fields, especially while providing crop services in the rainy months where it is not possible to access to their agricultural lands during these months due to the fragile muddy land structure that absorbs rainwater as the soil in the project areas is of clay type, so these roads will be rehabilitated to be usable even in rainy seasons which will reflect positively on agricultural operations. This will be done through conducting road paving, supplying quarry residue material, these works will be implemented by contracting with a qualified supplier under the supervision of our technical team on the ground, as the main roads leading to the fields will be paved with the using of the oil residue (fuel) in order to increase the quality of the pavement so that layer of soil will be is isolated from rain water, and daily workers will be hired to by cash for work modality, here job opportunities will be secured for the affected people in the project areas where they will be chosen from families that suffer unemployment among their members, large families with low income, members of newly displaced families Where they will have the opportunity have the income assist them in securing the requirements of their life, including health, education, Food ...
On the other side, lighting for these ways will be provided to improve visibility, especially in the early morning and sunset hours, by installing lighting poles with energy-saving lamps. Electricity for these columns will be supplied by solar energy systems that will be installed upon the poles. In this case, a sustainable service will be provided for years to come and a clean, environmentally friendly energy source. This service will be secured by contracting with a contractor specializing in the required field

5 - Implementation of activities

- Paving of the roads in the targeted communities: The most vital roads in the targeted areas and the semi-main roads that farmers use to reach their fields to be rehabilitated. 30 km of roads will be rehabilitated, as the roads consisting of poor soils for using as roads were chosen to be rehabilitated, needs for that assessed by conducting field visits from the engineering team on the Silk Road, interviews and meetings were held with local councils in the targeted areas with specialized committees in the targeted communities. Field interviews were also conducted with farmers who owned agricultural lands in those areas in order to meet the needs. Discussing them in this regard, on basis of that, an action plan was established with quantitative tables shown on the basis of the need in terms of the total areas to be constructed and the needed materials are calculated for that with the estimated costs, preparing for the launch of tenders needed for that.- Cash for work activities: daily workers will be hired to involve them in the operations of roads contraction securing job opportunities for the people living in these communities specific vulnerability criteria will be applied to choose those workers like families without employment members, large families with low income, members of newly displaced families Where they will have the opportunity have the income assist them in securing the requirements of their life.- Roads lightening: field visits with surveys through the areas, in conjunction with an assessment of the initial needs for maintenance and paving of roads, have been conducted. The specialized committees in the local councils were interviewed with interviews made with the potential beneficiaries. Accordingly, based on the technical standards, the number of necessary lighting points was defined, and BOQs were set up in order to contract with a qualified contractor working in this field to start works immediately after the project starting.
