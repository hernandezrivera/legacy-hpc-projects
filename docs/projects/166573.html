Project Summary

The first imported COVID-19 case was reported on 21 March 2020 and local transmission started on 24 March. As of 20 May 2020, Zimbabwe has recorded 46 positive cases, with 18 confirmed recoveries and 4 deaths. Although the transmission rate is still low the Ministry of Health and Child Care (MOHCC)
remains on high alert and a number of measures remain in place to support a cautious return for
industry and commerce following a five-week lockdown, including mandatory testing of employees,
wearing of masks/face coverings in public places, and all businesses required to implement and
facilitate physical distancing. According to the Shelter, NFI & CCCM Contingency and Response Plan April 2020 there has been an increase in the People in Need to 128270.

1 - Activities

"• Promote hygiene in high density areas with non-food items for hand washing as well as encouraging decongestion at household level and discouraging unnecessary movement by staying at Conduct multi-sector scenario planning and simulations for the deployment of even more stringent measures to interrupt transmission chains as needed e.g the suspension of large-scale gatherings.• Fully educate the public on the seriousness of COVID-19 and their role in preventing its spread.• Develop behavioral change communication towards community members for improved hygiene practices.• Integrate COVID-19 prevention messages in existing Shelter/NFI and CCCM activities,• Promote the adoption of key hygiene practices, namely washing hands with soap and clean water.• Encourage people with fever and cough to avoid unprotected close contact with people and encourage communities to seek early treatment if they have a fever, cough and difficulty breathing.• Encourage the people to follow the social distancing message by staying at home to avoid contracting the disease as well as to spread the virus."
