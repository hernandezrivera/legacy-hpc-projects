Project Summary

The economic consequences of the war in Syria are still ongoing, and people are still suffering from devastating economic and social effects especially after the earthquake that struck the country in February last year and the recent flood that occurred in Tartous.
Despite the improvement in the security situation in many parts of the country, the living situation remains difficult, and even deteriorating in several locations due to the exhaustion of people savings and level of resilience. Therefore, a large number of the population are in need for assistance in various forms.
The Council has conducted several consultations in the targeted villages to identify their needs and find ways to support food insecure and vulnerable households. The consultation concluded that people need activities that can be implemented at household level, need low level of labor, and their technical natural assets are available in their households, i.e. land, water, space for poultry raising, and/or pound for fish.
The aim of this proposal is to ensure the continuity of realizing the project objectives (restore beneficiaries and IDPs livelihoods and build their resilience for future shocks) and expandability to include new areas which are in most need of assistance. The intervention will help returnees and affected families in providing technical inputs, technical training, and food security assistance (in-kind or vouchers).
Furthermore, the new project will also contribute to building and promoting agricultural awareness through the training programs offered to the local farmers in cooperation with the expertise of the trainers.
The overall objective of this project is to strengthen resilience and food security of vulnerable Households in the targeted communities through restoration of natural assets as well as creation of physical assets (kitchen gardens, food cooperative processing units, Fish breeding farms) , skills development of targeted households, strengthen the relation with available technical expertise within local communities, and improve access to nutritious food and food consumption during and after the project.
This objective will be achieved through two key outcomes:
Outcome One: Improved access to physical livelihood assets . Outcome one will be delivered through the following outputs:
 Creation of assets in fields of kitchen garden, cooperative processing units, poultry distribution and fish breeding.
Outcome two; increased knowledge of targeted HHs on management of the assets created. Outcome two will be delivered through the following outputs:
 Targeted HHs are trained on creation, best use and maintenance of Assets.
Outcome three; improve access to nutritious food and food consumption during and after the project.
Outcome three will be delivered through the following output;
 Improved access to nutritious food and food consumption for the HHs targeted with FFA Programme.
Support for small holders has been identified as a key opportunity for Syria to achieve the following SDGs: 1: No Poverty; 2: Zero Hunger; 6: Ensure Availability and Sustainable Management of Water and Sanitation for All; 12: Responsible Production and Consumption; and 17: Partnership for the Goals.
The project also includes expanding the food processing unit’s component, which has proved successful in helping beneficiaries generate additional income by learning new ways to preserve food and produce food items that fits the area they are working in and fulfilling the local market demands.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The project is composed of four main components: kitchen garden, group-based food processing units, poultry, and family fish farms. Each component has three main activities: providing technical inputs, providing technical training, and providing conditional food security assistance. SYC will notify OCHA offices in the targeted governorates on actual timeline of distribution and training, to allow and facilitate OCHA supervision and follow up accordingly. Note: Since it is too late for most of the proposed projects, we can suggest creating field schools for beneficiaries after they are selected and included in the four components of the project. Beneficiaries will be trained on basic skills and good agricultural practices regarding each component of the project with the aim of developing their capabilities and increasing their knowledge.We can also propose a special agricultural input component in Tartous Governorate after the recent flood such as a hive, several bees or bee nuclei, protected agriculture supplies such as seeds, nylon roll, fertilizers, and a drip irrigation network.5-1 The first main component- establishing kitchen gardens:5.2 The Second main component: Food cooperative processing units:5.3 The third main component (Poultry); 5.4 Fourth main Component: Fish farming:
