Project Summary

(Rohingya Refugee Camp)
This project will be implement in Teknaf Rohingya Refugee Camps to enhance self-reliance through training of agricultural techniques, this project aims to Rohingya Refugee learn agricultural techniques suitable for the camp environment and harvest vegetables to build resilience to shocks, and also improvement their nutrition and health through practice of homestead gardening and vegetable consume properly. Agriculture advisors will support their skill development through field guidance based on their needs.
*Project will be given to priority following; households headed by women or children, elderly, single parents, PWD (Persons With Disabilities) who need care, pregnant women, and lactating women.
(Host community)
This project aims to improve livelihood for women of vulnerable households through skill development of agriculture as to ensure people are able to sell their agriculture products at Hnilla public market. Provide training on vegetable and fruits cultivation and poultry rearing with group management. Also to construct Hnilla Public Market corporate with Hnilla union to make market linkage and form advisory board to support management of Hnilla public market.
* Project will be given to priority following; (1) Female head of household or children, head of household with many children, or female family member living together who is 15 years old or older, living alone, single parent, PWD (Persons With Disabilities) who needs care, 2) Household income less than 5,500 Taka/month.

1. Needs
1 - Identified Needs

The spread of COVID-19 infection and inflation has reduced the number of programs related to livelihood and nutritional improvement. On the other hand, the challenge for the future is to shift to support from a medium- to long-term perspective that can strengthen the ability of the Rohingya Refugee to recover on their own and contribute to the realization of sustainable and independent livelihoods. The average monthly income is significantly low for the FDMN community which is worth BDT 2,436.This is due to their limited opportunities for livelihood in the camp area contribute to their low income (RIMA 2020). To provide technical improvement will be more effective for home stead gardening intervention for Rohingya Refugee Camp and support their capacity building. On the other hand, the average monthly income of the households from the host community is BDT 14,694.The major share of host community household income is coming from the agricultural sector which is around 49 percent (RIMA2020). Women in vulnerable households will improve their skills in vegetable cultivation and poultry farming. Improved income from the sale of agricultural products and poultry farming in Host community.

3. Cross-cutting Themes
3.1 - Protection

Livelihood program activities are responsible for ensuring the beneficiaries safety access to their services without causing harm, to ensure dignity reducing negligence, and encouraging inclusiveness without paying to perpetuating discrimination, abuse, violence and exploitation. The project will support in strengthening participation and empowerment of beneficiaries and communities so that they can realize their rights. Mukti has followed PSEA guideline and provide regular training to its staffs on PSEA and GBV and Mukti has strong CFRM system functioning in place.

3.2 - Gender

Gender mainstreaming: Project will be given priority to households headed by women or children, elderly, single parents, sick or PWDs (persons with disabilities) people who need care, pregnant women, and lactating women. More than 90% of beneficiaries (Refugee and Host) will be female and more than 1% will be PWDs (persons with disability). They will be engaged in agricultural practices which is linked with Hnila market place especially women friendly made by the project. The project will ensure the more involvement of women participation in project activities. Project will also follow the household approach and contribute to food and nutrition security to the household.

3.3 - Age including child safeguarding

As Mukti is a partner of different UN organization like UNHCR, UNICEF, UNFPA for quite a long time, it's policy related to protection and child safeguarding has evolved highly in recent years. Each and every recruited employees needs to sign different code of conducts prior to the joining. It is for ensuring that everyone is aware and will abide by the policies. Mukti has its own PSEA and child safeguarding focals in the head office who is responsible to deal with such issues if occurs. The whole staffs including teachers, and other field staffs are regularly being refreshed about the child safeguarding issues in monthly meetings and workshops. Mukti has its own reporting mechanism in order to help and support the victim at the earliest.

3.4 - Diversity

Project will promote social cohesion and peaceful existence: Support for livelihood improvement leads to the creation of an economic foundation, which in turn leads to social stability and cohesion between camp and host community. Mukti mainstreams age, gender, and diversity (AGD) approach throughout the planning, design, and implementation, processes of the project. The project will engage a variety of different groups, especially women, PWDs, faith leaders, caregivers, youth and adolescent from both Rohingya and Host Community.

3.5 - Accountability to Affected Populations (AAP)

Mukti will provide accurate, timely and accessible information to affected people about its assistance. Information provided has to be clearly understandable by everyone, irrespective of their age, gender or other characteristics. The project will mainstream the accountability to affected populations principle in the process. Through community consultation, monitoring/evaluation/analysis and complaints/feedback process, affected people have a right to be actively involved in the decisions and in program decisions will make assistance to effective improvement.

3.6 - Does your project have any DRR/DRM elements and/or environmental issues and/or climate change mitigation measures? If yes, please explain.

Mukti will promote climate change adaptation agriculture inside all agriculture project to mitigate the risk of cyclone, drought or any kind of disaster. Also we develop heat and disease tolerant varieties of vegetable(tomato, melon, etc.) to increase vegetable production in rainy season.
