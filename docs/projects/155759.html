Project Summary

FATA for the past decade has seen a lot of unrest. The residents around 5 million have been forced to leave their homes and communities a number of times from time to time due to volatile security condition. The Government of Pakistan formally announced to the complete return of the TDPs back into their areas of origin after de-notification and facilitate their rehabilitation in a sustainable way.
As per Vulnerability Assessment Report Some 47 per cent of the population in former FATA’s districts uses contaminated water for consumption. About 80 per cent of people use different means to treat water, however cases of waterborne diseases have been reported with diarrhea (33 per cent) being most prevalent with minor cases of typhoid also noted. About two thirds of the population do not have water facilities close to their homes, thus significant time is spent fetching water. Women and girls are mostly responsible for this and often need to walk more than 30 minutes through difficult terrain. Women, being primarily responsible for childcare in FATA, often leave their children unattended when fetching water and conducting other household chores. This can limit adequate attention and negatively impact hygienic and nutritional care for children. Similarly, a lack of nearby water has increased the domestic burden for pregnant and lactating women that have subsequently suffered health problems. School enrolment rates for girls has also dropped, as they play a crucial role in supporting their families with increased domestic work.

Women also complained about the lack of appropriate water storage containers to fetch and store drinking water. Due to cultural and social beliefs, hand washing is common at critical required times but soap is not used in general. Overall the hygienic condition and awareness among people in both agencies is quite poor which will have a negative impact upon the health of the returnee population especially women, children and people with disability

Activities

Activity 1.1.1: Identification and rehabilitation 15 public and community-owned Drinking Water Supply Schemes (DWSS) for rehabilitation in target areas benefiting 5299 locals including 52 % of women.Activity 1.2.1: Provision of sanitation kits for the construction of semi-permanent Latrines/toilets at the community level in consultation and participation of the local community. (3974 beneficiaries) Activity 2.1.1: Community mobilization for the promotion of hygiene in target areas.Activity 2.1.2: Holding of 250 Hygiene promotion sessions with 50% session for the female in vulnerable communities benefiting 6624 local beneficiaries) Activity 2.2.1: Needs Assessment / Identification of beneficiaries through community mobilization Activity 2.2.2: Distribution of gender-sensitive 1000 hygiene kits in deserving beneficiaries women headed HHs in target areas.Activity 3.1.1: Provision of WASH facilities to one government school benefiting 100 school children.
