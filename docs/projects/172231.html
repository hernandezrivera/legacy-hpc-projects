Project Summary

The project intend to ensure that children of school going age have access to equitable and inclusive education even during emergencies. World Vision Zimbabwe (WVZ) will focus on (1)supporting most vulnerable children to enroll and attend school (2) support school feeding to encourage school attendance and improve the health status of pupils and (3)implement complimentary WASH activities for schools to have safe drinking water .The project will target 3 districts with an overall severity of 5 in Matebeleland South (Gwanda, Insiza, and Mangwe). (WVZ has presence in ALL these districts implementing other projects hence this project will be complimentary. This project will focus on (1) supporting 101,540 (50.262 boys & 51.278 girls) most vulnerable children ( 3-18years) to ensure that they enrol and attend school,(2) support school feeding to boost school attendance as well as improve the health status of 101 540 pupils and (3) implement WASH in schools activities for improving schools access to safe drinking water for 16,467. The project will also incorporate cross cutting issues like Gender, Pyscho- social services (PSS), Disaster Risk Reduction ( DRR), Child Safeguarding and Protection in Emergencies, Children feedback meetings, Teacher training on emergency sensitive teaching and learning activities will also be incorporated.

1 - Activities

Objective 1: The following key activities will be carried out in 3 districts of Gwanda, Insiza and Mangwe targeting a cummulative total of 101,540 people in need: aged 3-18 years • Community awareness on the importance of education for boys and girls including those with diasabilities. • Mobilisation of children particularly girls, orphans and those with disability to return to schools• Providing Block Grants for most vulnerable children particularly the disabled, • Procurement and supply of teaching and learning materials and kits for vulnerable children including those with disabilities• Procurement and supply of dignity kits (sanitary pads,soap, buckets and towels to targeted adolescent girls in schools.• Carry out School catch up activities like printing of story books, use of technology to enhance learning• Capacity building of teachers on classroom pedagogical delivery methods for Education in Emergencies, psychosocial counseling techniques for one -on- one sessions and mass counselling and specicifically managing learners with special needs. All the 3 districts highlighted above have an average of 81.9% children out of school with Mangwe having as high as 95% chidren out of school. Objective 2: The main focus of this objective is to link up with the WVZ WASH department to facilitate drilling or rehabilitation and installation of mechanized boreholes in 5 schools in Insiza,The mechanized boreholes will also be used for schools’ nutrition gardens to compliment Objective 3 below. The % of schools without access to improved drinking water source in Insiza district is 72%, The interventions will improve access to clean drinking water to 16,467 people in need from the one(1) targeted district in need of clean water. Other activites will be • Creation /Reconstitution and training/ retraining of School Water point committees. • Provision of water storage facilities such as water tanks where drilling or rehabilitation of boreholes would be not feasible.• Provision of at Point of Use water purifiers for water that may not be unsafe for human consumption Objective 3: The following key activities will be implemented in 3 districts:• Procurement and supply of pulses and vegetable oil for school feeding to compliment the starch from Government..• Supporting most vulnerable schools with school feeding utensils like pots plates and cups and construction of kitchens/cooking areas.• Capacity building of School Development Committees (SDC), teachers and caregivers) to effectively manage school feeding programme. • Support monitoring and supervision of the school feeding programme and growth monitoring by Ministry of Primary and Secondary Education (MoPSE) and Ministry of Health and Child Care (MoHCC). • Support the establishment of school nutrition gardens to complement the school feeding activities. The nutrion gardens which have a permanent water source will be used as a sustainability approach to phase out school feeding supported by this project to home grown school feeding where schools would be able to support themselves through nutrition gardens.The average percentage of children not receiving school feeding is 81% with the most affected being Mangwe (95%), followed by Gwanda (80%). The target for this objective is 101,540 boys and girls including those with disabilities

8 - Accountability to Affected People
8.1 - Describe how your project was designed in consultation with communities? How will you incorporate communities in project implementation and what feedback mechanisms do you have in place?

While communities were not directly consulted in the development of this project WVZ conducted needs assessment in other emergencies, including Cyclone Idai, floods, and the ongoing COVID-19 epidemic.in which stakeholders such as school authorities, subject matter specialists, MoPSE and MoHCC were involved. These stakeholder identified the challenges in relation to school dropouts and prioritised them. Community groups will be formed to monitor and track school attendance borrowing from mothers groups concept. Regular community meetings will be held with community groups to discuss issues coming out of the project as well as a platform for community feedback. WVZ has a number of feedback mechanisms which include use of hotline through a toll free number, suggestion boxes and community meetings and beneficiaries will be trained on how to use these mechanisms.

9 - Environmental considerations
9.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Where drilling and mechanisation of boreholes will be undertaken, Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) will be carried out.to ensure that any adverse environmental impacts will be mitigated during project implementation.WVZ will partner with the Environmental Management Agency (EMA) and the Forestry Commission in conducting training of school authories and children on sustainable environmental management practices such as promoting localised Farmer Managed Natural Regeneration (FMNR) activities, Watershed management, Improved soil and water management as well as the rehabilitation of degraded forests through reforestation.WVZ will promote/support more efficient energy saving alternatives e.g. tsotso cooking stoves during preparation of school feeding meals.WVZ will promote the establishment of orchards/windbreaks at schools, institutions and in the community in line with environmental management4. WVZ will conduct training of project beneficiaries on climate change adaptation borrowing from its sister projects like ZIMJR project in Hwange World Vision program (2017) and in (2019) ENSURE program done in Chipinge, Chimanimani and Buhera districts of Manicaland, WVZ will deliberately mainstream the training of school-based Disaster Risk and Reduction (DRR) focal point persons and Environmental Sub Committees (ESC) members on Natural resources management in addition to the concept of Farmer Managed Natural Regeneration (FMNR) during training of school authorities under this project.
