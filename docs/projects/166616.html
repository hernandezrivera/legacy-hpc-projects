Project Summary

Integrating HIV Services into Humanitarian Preparedness and Response Plans and providing lifesaving and protective nutrition interventions to the most vulnerable children under 5 years in 7 drought affected districts in the context of COVID19. 1)To Provide PPE for CHWs to safely track children, adolescents and young people and pregnant and breastfeeding women and maintain in HIV care and treatment in response to drought and COVID19; 2) To assist carers with PPE to provide life-saving nutrition treatment to all children affected by acute malnutrition and screen, test and appropriately treat for HIV and TB and mentor carers in IYCF in response to the drought and COVID19; 3) To Provide OPHID staff with PPE to mentor HCWs improve decision making and effective nutrition response through strengthened data collection, reporting and information systems at all levels from community, health facility, district, provincial and national level during time of drought and COVID19 pandemic

Plan fields

1 - Activities

• Mentoring 1020 HCWs on ensuring strict COVID19 hygiene promotion and safety standards and reinforcing training on use of PPE • Provide PPE to 1020 HCWs in all clinics in 7 districts on Outpatient Therapeutic Management of Acute Malnutrition and inpatient facility based management of Severe Acute malnutrition and screen, test and treat children with acute malnutrition for HIV and TB in children under 5 • Support HCWs to mitigate COVID19 transmission in management of child and carer by supporting HCWs in providing carers at risk of transmission of COVID19 with information on hygiene and safety standards at all 276 health facilities • Ensure Decongestion of clinics, ensuring strict adherence to recommended hygiene and safety standards under COVID19. • Support with PPE 4,005 VHWs, EHTs and WNCs working in context of COVID19, to mentor community members on hygiene and safety standards, use of mother MUAC active malnutrition screening and referral for treatment for acute malnutrition and HIV and TB screening, testing care and treatment and IYCF hygiene and safety standards during COVID 19 pandemic• Strengthen effective nutrition documentation, reporting and information systems by providing data and airtime to enable HCWs in remote facilities to be mentored remotely to accurately complete registers and reporting forms in all 276 health facilities in 7 districts and submit data remotely.
