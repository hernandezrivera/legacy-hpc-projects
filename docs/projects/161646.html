Project Summary

This project is aimed at providing an all inclusive education for conflict affected children in Monguno LGA in Borno State using a safe school common approach.
This methodology aimed at addressing the holistic wellbeing of the child with “the child” at the center stage, the school, community (including family, peer groups,LGA education stakeholders, IDPs and Host community leaders/Bulamas stakeholder), and the greater society. We believe that by integrating child protection,Mental Health and Psychosocial Support Services(MHPSS), Capacity Building and socioeconomic support in mobilizing community structures to effectively support and sensitize parents and caregivers to enroll children to schools and monitors enrollment,retention and performance will have sustainable results to the greater society.
This approach aimed at enrolling 5000 vulnerable children In IDPs and host communities in Monguno LGA who are out of school, and children who are in school but struggling academically and are at risk of dropping out by constructing 5 Temporal learning spaces with gender sensitive hand washing facilities and water points,train 300 teachers(recruit 50 volunteers) and learning facilitators in pedagogy and classroom management,Teaching methodologies,child protection, psychosocial support and psychological first aid,life skills for adolescents,and referral pathways.Provide hygiene/dignity kits for 1,500 adolescent girls, provision of essential learning, teaching and recreational materials,seating arrangement, blackboards to targeted schools within the IPDs camps and host communities in Monguno, Construction of 10 inclusive (boys, girls, persons with disability) WASH facilities/latrines, empower communities to strengthen school-community Integration through School Management Committee (SBMC),introduce a 100 beneficiary targeted in-kind/cash transfer programme to encourage girls, poor boys and disadvantaged groups to enrol in junior secondary school,increase public awareness campaigns in IDPs and Host communities.

1 - Needs

The decade long conflict in Northeast Nigeria weakens the already weak education system as the crisis pushed more children out of school via the systematic destruction of the education system. More children been exposed to horrific forms of violence,abuse and exploitation which has negative effects on their psychosocial wellbeing. Unicef’s 2018 estimation of Nigerian children and youth (aged 3 17) that are in need of educational assistance due to the attack on the education system by Boko Haram since 2011 is over 5.3 million of which 3 million need immediate education in emergency support in Northeast Nigeria (Adamawa, Borno and Yobe states). IOM estimated children to represent 56% of the total IDP population.Several assessment conducted in 2018 by agencies such as Plan-International, IRC, Unicef etc, in Adamawa and Borno state found that there is an inadequate number of teachers in schools, classrooms are dilapidated and in need of repair, there is a high student to classroom ratio. The North-East Nigeria Education Sector Humanitarian Response Plan Dashboard report in July 2019 estimated 2.2mil conflict-affected boys, girls and adolescents with no access to basic quality education or vocational training skills identified as People in Need in North-East Nigeria. 1.5mil of this number was targeted in 2019 - 2021 Humanitarian Response Strategy. Over nine months down the year, only 0.319mil conflict-affected boys, girls reached, including teachers/volunteers trained. 0.773mil internally displaced conflict-affected boys, girls and adolescents (ages 3-17). 0.338mil Out-of-School internally displaced conflict-affected boys, girls and adolescents (ages 3-17). 6,000 Teachers targeted to be trained in improved learning approaches and Psychosocial Support. 3,000 Classrooms, Temporary Learning Spaces targeted to be constructed, rehabilitated or re-opened. 316,940 (154,949 girls and 161,991boys) conflict-affected boys, girls and adolescents reached with learning materials, learning space, trained teachers or WaSH facilities and 2,895( 1,373 Female 1,522 Male ) teachers trained in pedagogy and PSS.Achieving this goal by 2021 requires an all inclusive, comprehensive community-based integrated approach which this Intervention is aimed at addressing. This approach targets vulnerable children In IDPs in Monguno LGA who are out of school, and children who are in school but struggling academically and are at risk of drop out. The strategy is focusing on increasing access to education/enrollment via the provision of learning spaces(new construction and rehabilitation, Temporal learning spaces) with gender sensitive hand washing facilities and water,recruitment of volunteer teachers, capacity building on Teaching methodologies,psychosocial support,etc, provision of essential learning, teaching and recreational materials,seating arrangement, blackboards, empower communities to strengthen school-community Integration through School Management Committee (SBMC),introduce a targeted in-kind/cash transfer programme to encourage girls, poor boys and disadvantaged groups to enrol in junior secondary school,increase public awareness campaigns and provide economic livelihoods for IDPs households to enhance children’s attendance in school.
