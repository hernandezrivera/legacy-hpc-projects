Project Summary

Since the beginning of its presence in the Country, in 2006, Armadilla has worked in the field of health assistance, with a specific focus on children and youngsters with disabilities. The Syrian crisis in March 2011, has dramatically worsened the situation, especially for the displaced people that are leaving behind the little they owned. Armadilla has been supporting the work of both the local partner Zahret Al Madan (ZAM) in implementing rehabilitation activities for children with disabilities (both physical and cognitive), through the provision of specific training to the local health workers with the aim of increasing their professional skills and capacity of attendance. The Zahret Al Madan Centre, originally located in Hajar al Aswaad, indeed was born with the specific task of assisting vulnerable families, in which there is a presence of minors with disability both physical and/or cognitive. The main goal of Armadilla and Zahret Al Madan is the implementation of rehabilitation paths with the aim of developing the skills of minors with disabilities and favouring their social inclusion through a wide range of activities. Due to the conflict, the further lack of resources of the families of coming from Hajar Al Aswad and Midan (host community) does not allow them in access quality services for their relatives. The reinforcement of the technical skills of volunteers and workers of the ZAM association, as well as of the other associations newly involved in the activities, is the first answer to the increasing needs of the population of Damascus and Rural Damascus. The project foresees the strengthening of capacity of ZAM volunteers and workers in the field of assistance to minors with disabilities, as well as the training of other 4 associations of operating in Damascus and Rural Damascus in the field of assistance and care of children and youngsters with disability. Armadilla and Zahret Al Madan has been working since last October in identifying the other 4 associations, among a range of the ones working with minors with disabilities; the NGOs have been identified, but process of MoU drafting is still ongoing.Beneficiaries of the activities will be both volunteers and workers of the associations, as well as children and youngsters with disabilities, through the development and implementation of individual assistance plans.
Moreover, a component of psychosocial assistance addressing relatives and family of minors with disability, will be realized in order to support the wellbeing of the families, as well as the development of community activities with the aim of strengthening the awareness around the people with disability theme.
The project will be integrated with activities implemented by Zahret Al Madan and Armadilla in the Southern Suburbs of Damascus (Midan Kawakibi) and proposed under the relevant sector (e.g. Food Security), which purpose is the support of the vulnerable families of the area through the distribution of monthly food parcels.

Needs

In areas such as the ones proposed in the present project, despite the reduction of hostilities civilians are suffering the effects of over seven years of conflict, starting from the disintegration of community structures, safety nets and high levels of trauma and psychological distress. All the beneficiaries living in the Area of Midan, in Damascus, where the Zahret Al Madan (ZAM) centre is located, should be considered extremely vulnerable. The initial conditions of poverty, low income, scarce access to basic services forthese families have been further aggravated by the negative impact of the conflict, especially as a consequence of displacement from their home places (more than 90% of targeted beneficiaries come from the areas of Hajar Al Aswad, Yarmouk camp, Sbeneh, Set Zeinab, Babila, Yalda). All the families subject to the intervention see the presence of one or more minors with disability, and their lack of resources, aggravate the physical and cognitive status of the minors, as well as the emotional and psychological burden of the families, especially for the women whotraditionally are the ones in charge of the wellness of children and youngsters, as well as of elderly. It worth noting that as per the survey conducted by Armadilla and ZAM, 79% of the families interested by this proposal are led by breadwinner women, due to the absence of husbands as a consequence of the conflict. The proposed intervention is part of the multi-year project realized by Armadilla and ZAM, where children and youngsters with disability are assisted, as well as their families, through an intensive therapeutic programme delivered by the ZAM workers and volunteers. According to the continuous analysis realized by the I-NGO with the local partner and the Ministry of Social Affairs, there is a huge need to increase the capacity of the workers, as well as the quantity of volunteers and workers in order to match the increased quantity of beneficiaries which see the ZAM Centre as the most viable solution to assist their special minors. Moreover, it worth mentioning the huge lack of capacity in providing diagnosis for Autism Spectrum Disorders (ASD). About this specific aspect, it should be highlighted that Armadilla will collaborate in partnership with experts belonging to the Public Health Service of Italy (AUSL Reggio Emilia). They already performed a feasibility mission in Damascus (because the relevance of the problem was highlighted during a previous project) and they designed the specific training course (12 months length) addressing volunteers and workers of local associations..The necessity of receiving specific training on the topic, has been assessed because as per ZAM, data, in fact, ASD affect at least 30% of the beneficiaries, but without the correct tools and skills it is not possible to validate it. Moreover, ZAM has been requested by other local associations of Damascus and the Rural Damascus with similar needs in supporting the development of analogue activities with children with disabilities (physical therapy, individual plans, etc.) in their communities. Specific demands regarding the possibility to train their workers and volunteers have been discussed, in order to improve their capacity to attend and assist beneficiaries.

Implementation of activities

All the project activities will be implemented in the Zahret Al Madan (ZAM) Centre, located in Midan. Since more than 10 years, the Centre work in the field of assistance to minors with disability, in strict collaboration with Armadilla and the CPR of the Hospital Ibn-Al Nafis of Damascus. Project activities will be implemented by Armadilla and ZAM staff. ZAM volunteers will be coordinated by specialized health workers (physical therapists, pediatrics, physiatrists) of the CPR. Recreational days involving both minors with disabilities and peers without disabilities will be coordinated by volunteers of ZAM and the other NGOs involved in the project. Training on specific subjects (Co-construction of social services, network of community services, Autism Spectrum Disorders) will be delivered by International Experts through both onsite and remote sessions. The training will be given on a 12 months basis length. The following structure is foresees1 start-up meeting on site, with a tentative duration of 4 days, in which the volunteers and workers will work together with experts, which will illustrate methodology and techniques;2. 10 remote sessions, tentatively one day each month, which serve also by follow-up about the progress of the trainees. The remote training sessions, in order to maximize the impact of the activity will be delivered through a specific platform expressly realized for the purpose (see also below). In this way, there will be the possibility for trainees to contact in every moment the experts, which will be able to respond to direct inquires that will be useful for all the users of the platform. 3. One final training session on site, with the purpose to evaluate the final results obtained and stress potential further support for the associations. Remote sessions, particularly, will be delivered through a specific platform created by International experts, in which all the trainees will find the material presented in the sessions, an interactive space where questions can be asked to the trainers (a chat space and a forum with relevant topics will be opened), video demonstrations, electronic books, etc. The platform will be customized in order to be not only a space of training, but also the tool to perform a continuous follow-up thanks to the permanent exchange between trainers and trainees on concrete topics.Moreover, monthly follow-up of specific cases will be done by Armadilla staff in the Country, trainers and managers of the Associations involved in the project, with the remote support of the International Experts, which will assist to équipe meetings on cases, and supporting in this way the local staff in increasing their capacity.Psychosocial activities for parents and relatives of minors with disabilities will be given, according the experience gained in the past 4 years, by the psychosocial expert of the ZAM centre.List of activities:Therapeutic (cognitive and physical) paths for children with disabilities, including assessment of their conditions held in ZAM Centre;PSS sessions addressing relatives and families of minors with disabilities;Training for volunteers and workers of ZAM and other 3 associations (located in Al Tal, Qudsiya, Kafr Sousa), held in ZAM Centre by Hospital Ibn Al-Nafis professionals.Recreational days for children with disabilities and children (peers activities).Supply of equipment (assistive aids and educational toys for children with disabilities) to the associations.Realization of an interactive platform for the training.Training on specific subjects: Co-construction of social services, network of community services, Autism Spectrum Disorders diagnosis, using TTAP, ADOS, ADOS 2 kits, given by International professionals (Psychologists, Sociologists and Medical Doctors).

Ensure a comprehensive service mapping is undertaken so that risk mitigation and communication with communities can be proactive and informed. 2. Capacity enhancement for community members to share information and refer protection concerns to services providers. Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Access to the Area of the activities The access to the area of activities, both in Damascus and Rural Damascus, does not seem problematic nowadays. However, the Organisation will be in continuous communication with the relevant security actors in order to organize activities and movements only after consultation with them. Monitoring activities will be realized previously informing the Ministry of Social Affairs (MoSA), who has specifically approved the activities of the present project.Lack of permission to enter in Syria for expatriate staff: while there are no problems at all in obtaining visa for professionals blem, there is the possibility that some Armadilla’s management staff may be face difficulties in obtaining the visas to come in the Country (e.g. for internal monitoring work). Visas issue will be tackled by advocating with the MoSA and MoH in supporting the Organisation. This method worked just fine in recent years. However, training sessions may be delivered by remote methodology, and activities on the field will be guaranteed by the Local partner and local staff of the Organisation. Availability of goods (assistive aids and toys for children disabilities): Goods will be purchased on the local market, looking for the best vendor in terms of economy and capacity of guarantee the provision of the service. A back-up list of potential suppliers to contact will be drafted, in order to avoid any delay. In case there will be the need to purchase items abroad, the MoSA will facilitate the import of goods.
