Project Summary

According to OCHA's HNO 2023 Statistics indicate increased exposure to ongoing hostilities, particularly in the Northwest. limited access to the Services; Displacement, which often separates children from family members, including primary caregivers, is disrupting the education of 2.45 million children aged 5-17 years out of school, increasing protection risks reported by parties to the conflict. Children and primary caregivers also suffer Vulnerabilities associated with direct risks of the worst forms of child labour, disability, physical/psychological injury, family separation (including anticipated separation due to COVID19), child recruitment, and child marriage. The project aims to reduce the above-mentioned protection risks for displaced children and the host community in northwestern Syria in Jisr al-Shughur in the al-Janudiyah area within the CP Center of the AHF, in line with the minimum standards of CP, as the center will provide multiple services in the field of CP from through it:
1. Awareness campaigns through awareness teams targeting women, men, and children for CP issues
2- Implement CM services that address the vulnerabilities of children and primary caregivers associated with direct risks of worst forms of child labour, children with disabilities, physical and psychological injury, severe neglect, family separation (including expected separation due to COVID19), child recruitment, and child marriage. These services will be provided through referrals from the community protection committees and through the monitoring team, according to joint procedures between the authorities.
3. Provide (CVA- ECF) to cover protection gaps for children if the service is not available within the communities
4. Capacity building of CP workers.
5. Implementation of mental health and psychosocial support activities within the AHF Center for war-traumatized children.
6. Provide parenting sessions for primary care providers
The center will serve the most vulnerable children and Sexual differences between children will be taken into account by providing male and female case workers to provide protection services in a manner that takes into account gender differences. Protection services will also be directed at 50% for male children and 50% for female children. All activities are aligned with the 2022 Humanitarian Response Plan as well as the Strategic Objectives of the Protection Cluster. Services will be provided for one year.
.The project will focus on mitigating protection risks for children with disabilities by providing them with protection services, integrating them into society, and educating the community about the mechanism of dealing with this group., and all activities will be implemented according to Recommendations of the Protection Cluster and the WHOto deal with Covid-19 and the necessary preventive measures to protect beneficiaries and humanitarian workers.
The project will ensure the accountability of the affected population and stakeholders through coordination, participation, transparent accountability, complaints mechanism, feedback mechanism, and worker competency monitoring.
Reports will be prepared and coordinated with the Child Protection Sub-Cluster (4Ws, Case Management Reporting Tool) to report on project activities
The monitoring plan will ensure that:
1. Monitor the quality of the implemented activities and compare the action plan using case management monitoring and evaluation tools and report any issue Case management monitoring (recording - evaluation - intervention plan - closing the case and ensuring access to the service) while maintaining confidentiality and privacy of the case and that the names are encrypted
2. Track and collect project indicators to measure project progress at the output level
The project is designed to reduce protection PSEA,
The project is designed to strengthen GQ to include women and girls as an integral part of the project's activities at all stages during its tenure.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk of tension within or within the community over locations of service delivery or types of services provided if one community is provided and a neighboring community is not served. 1- Ensure comprehensive planning of services to mitigate risks and communicate with communities in a proactive and informed manner. 2. Ensure that neighboring communities are consulted regarding site services and remediation. Including access barriers for those with injuries/disabilities and communication mechanisms for people with disabilities. 3 . Forming community committees to strengthen community protection mechanisms. Girls, boys, women and men are at risk as a result of violence associated with the civilian population and a lack of consideration for the safety and security of accessing/receiving services; Not taking into account contextual information. 1. Consider patterns of hostilities and contextual analysis as part of decisions to establish a fixed site or provide a mobile service. 2. Ensure that a safety audit is conducted to ensure safe access (road and location) for people with mobility impairmentsRisk of tension in society, due to the perception of inequality, discrimination, nepotism, and gender insensitivity1- Developing appropriate mechanisms for selection criteria based on weaknesses.2. Consult with the actors on the ground to set targeting criteria that are compatible with protection criteria3. Avoid exclusion from helping people on the basis of social or nepotism


8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Low level of obligations related to sexual exploitation and abuse, disproportionate exposure of women to misconduct by humanitarian workers at risk of sexual exploitation and abuse, and low level of safe reporting of complaints of sexual exploitation and abuse. Develop a strategy to mitigate protection from PSEA through the following:1- Recruitment of project staff who have no prior records or allegations of SEA cases2- Conducting induction sessions to introduce the risks of PSEA and to explain and sign the codes of conduct3- Provide mandatory PSEA training for all project personnel and obtain the PSEA Network accredited certificate4- Educate all project beneficiaries about protection risks from PSEA by employing a PSEA officer within the project5- Disseminate awareness materials related to PSEA within the center and places where humanitarian services are implemented6- Establish secure reporting mechanisms by allocating a special PSEA number and email for PSEA-related complaints and disseminating it among beneficiaries and local communities.7- Attend periodic meetings related to the PSEA network for the purpose of coordination and exchange of information8- Promote gender perspective within the project to mitigate SEA risks9- Explain the PSEA policy approved within the AHF to all project staff and frontline workers including management, field staff, day laborers, volunteers, contractors, and vendors


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk1: COVID-19 pandemic and its impact on awareness activities, CM, and training probability: high Mitigation: - Maintaining social distance between beneficiaries and the project staff - Use methods of protection, including masks, gloves, and sterilizers -Avoiding meetings that contain many beneficiaries.Risk 2: CP activities are suspended due to the conflict. probability: medium. Mitigation: in the event of attacks or invasion of the region, the service center will be moved to the JisrAl-Shughour area in coordination with the local authorities to ensure the safety of project personnel and beneficiaries and after acquiring the required approval Risk 3:Council local intervention and impeding the implementation of the project interference by armed groups to prevent the implementation of child protection activities that are related to early marriage and CP issues situation probability: Medium Mitigation: signing a memorandum of understanding with the local council to avoid obstacles, interference or external dictated by armed groups Establishing community protection networks that are made up of community leaders and influencers to support unhindered child protection activitiesThe danger of providing protection services to males without females without taking into account the gender perspective1- Developing appropriate mechanisms for selection criteria based on weaknesses, including PWDs.3- Making the proportion of males 50% and females 50% within the project
