Project Summary

The project will aim to ensure access of all children and women to the comprehensive
quality CMAM services, disseminate and support C-IYCF practices and promote health
and nutrition seeking and basic hygienic behaviors within the population in need.
throughout the project, the following facilities will be supported:
- Orient OTP center in Idleb-Idleb-Idleb.
- 10 RRT teams in Idleb- Idleb- Idleb.
The project will improve equitable access to high quality, life-saving, curative nutrition services through early identification, referral, and treatment of acutely malnourished boys and girls under five years and pregnant and lactation women (PLW). Provision of management of acute malnutrition will be provided at the health facility and community levels and in integration with infant and young child feeding services and primary health care services.

4 - Needs

Chronic malnutrition is evident in most sub-districts throughout Syria – notably in the Northwest of Syria (Aleppo, Idlib) . Chronic malnutrition is also on the rise with almost half a million children stunted (low height for age) in 2020 and face losing their future due to hindered learning and physical developments, as well as early death. While at least one out of eight children in Syria is chronically malnourished this ratio is worse in areas affected by displacements, returns, and over-burdened communities such as Idlib (1 out of 5 children). Acute malnutrition remains relatively high among internally displaced children and mothers as well as population affected by conflict. Throughout Syria, about 240,000 children under the age of five years and over 50,000 pregnant and lactating mothers are in need for urgent lifesaving curative nutrition services . At least 36,000 boys and 35,000 girls are acutely malnourished and are at increased risk of health complications, including death.

5 - Implementation of activities

The project will aim to support 2 components:- Improve equitable access to high quality, lifesaving, curative nutrition services through early identification, referral, and treatment of acutely malnourished boys and girls under five years and pregnant and lactation women (PLW). Provision of management of acute malnutrition will be provided at the health facility, and in integration with infant and young child feeding services and primary health care services, throughout the support of Orient OTP center.- Sustain and scale up the provision of respective lifesaving essential integrated health, nutrition and child protection field services throughout the RRT modality, provided services will include: screening for malnutrition and referral, one-to-one IYCF-E counselling, IYCF-E messages, screening for external visual signs of corporal punishment and suspected abuse and violence cases and referral, screening for danger health signs and referral to healthcare centers, in addition to WaSH messages on: hand washing practices, food processing and toilet and sanitation.- Orient will start the implementation by communicating with the staff, local health authorities and other partners to inform them about the project and the changes that will take place on the targeted facilities.- Orient will renew the contracts with the health staff of the ongoing facilities and start the recruitment process to hire the staff of the new ones.- The logistics department of Orient will conduct the procurement process for the items mentioned in the budget according to the Standards Operating Procedures SOPs of the logistics and Finance departments.- Orient will conduct an Information, Education, and Communication IEC campaign in the target communities to inform them about the health services provided through the targeted facilities- Orient will communicate with UNICEF to allocate nutrition supplies according to the number of beneficiaries and type of services.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

-Violence against Health Facilities and Healthcare Providers: Orient will report any attack on health facility, and support any efforts to highlight protections challenges facing health workers across Syria. -Gender Based Violence GBV: Hiring of female staff to increase access to health services for women and girls, training gynecologists, midwifes and nurses on the clinical management of sexual assault and rape, identification of GBV cases, and safe referrals, and link the health facilities with the social services to ensure safe referral pathways between both of them.-Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse PSEA: Orient will conduct orientation session on PSEA on all health facilities to ensure all the staff are aware about PSEA, all the recruitment process will include PSEA in the criteria of selection, and all the staff should sign Orient code of conduct, and PSEA policy.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

-Cooperation and coordination with authorities: Orient have good coordination with the local health authorities in Idleb, in addition to good relation and coordination with the local councils and all the active partners on the targeted areas.-Access to implementation areas: Orient has good experience in implementing projects in the targeted areas, and to decrease the risk of the lack of accessibility, all the project materials will be procured as a on shot.-Changing front lines, changes in the parties controlling the implementation areas and significant population movements as they flee shifting conflict areas: Orient has an evacuation plan and can ensure the security and the evacuation of the staff in case of emergency.-Available, qualified staff: Orient has a good experience in implementing health projects, and have many high qualified staff, also, we will build the capacity of the staff to increase the quality of services.
