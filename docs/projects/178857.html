Project Summary

Mercy-USA for Aid and Development will support internally displaced persons (IDPs) and host community in Maarrat Tmasrin, Idleb, Dana, Jandairis and Afrin Sub-districts with winterization support with mixed modalities in-kind and E-vouchers (based on markets functionality), to provide eco-friendly biofuel, and stove kits (in line with SNFI activity 1.2 Provision of seasonal and supplementary NFIs and shelter assistance (e.g. winter clothing, fuel, winter-specific shelter upgrades; or summer/flood assistance including cash/voucher for these items)
From January to March 2021, Mercy-USA targeted 8,076HH, 41,525individuals (Men 8,321- Women 9,131- Boys 11,206- Girls 12,861) with winterization by using in-kind modality.
From October till December 2021, Mercy-USA will target 3500HH, 17,995 individuals (Men 3,606- Women 3,957- Boys 4,856- Girls 5,576) by using with in-kind and E-vouchers methodologies for implementation.
Total target 11,576HH, 59,521 individuals (Men 11,927- Women 13,088- Boys 16,062- Girls 18,443)
Mercy-USA will target each household with in-kind or value E-voucher with 900 kg of olive pit fuel and stoves for one time throughout the winter months (November, December, and January) A distribution/redemption of 300 kg per month along with stoves or the most vulnerable households.
On the other hand, Mercy-USA will target 200 HH (Men 200, Women 220, Boys 270, and Girls 310) in Idleb Sub-district with shelter rehabilitation and repair for slightly damaged houses due to fighting on this area, in order to enhance safe access to shelter such as repair windows and wall holes without extreme damage taking in our considerations HLP issue prior to any house selection can be made by using contractor with cost no more than 1000 USD per HH.

4 - Needs

According to HNO 2021 Figures, roughly 5.88 million are People in need for shelter and 4.70 million are people in need for NFI assistance. In the north-west, there are critical levels of overcrowding putting significant pressure on overburdened communities, with few if any remaining shelter options available.In NW Syria, 2.2 million are PIN for shelter while 1.6 million are PIN for NFI.In the targeted sub-districts, total population 861K from them 414k are PIN for shelter and 334K are PIN for NFI with severity scores 3, 4 and 5. NFIs continue to be essential and life-saving particularly for those newly displaced of forced to relocate. While market access appears to be gradually improving, many life-sustaining, basic items continue to be unaffordable for people. NFIs become increasingly important during the cold weather months to ensure families can live in safety and dignity. Families report that they are in need of fuel, clothing, and power/heating sources. Over 60 per cent of people state a preference for receiving assistance in the form of cash or vouchers rather than in-kind as they can select specific items needed for their families.

7.1 - Please list any risks related to sexual exploitation and abuse that have been identified during the project design phase and any risk mitigation and prevention measures that have been adopted and built into the project

SEA Risks: • Overcrowded during the Fuel distributions/ redemption which may cause SEA• Mixed distribution lines for males and females • All distribution staff are males • The distribution is not accessible to all gender groups • No shared clear information about timing and content of distribution • No clear procedure regarding loss of card/voucher• Taxation on the assistance • No complaints and feedback mechanism (CFM) in place Risk mitigation:• Ensure extend distribution days to avoid overcrowding • Separate lines for males and females in all distribution/ redemption process • Ensure gender balance in the hiring process, ideally to be 50% of the team are female• The distribution sites will be chosen to be accessible to all gender groups especially female HH and disables• Clear information about timing, contents, and procedures about loss of cards/vouchers of the distribution will be shared with all beneficiaries beforehand and will be existed in the banners in the distribution sites • Sensitization about the scope of the project to all communities with highlighting “This assistance is Free” and this statement will be printed on the beneficiary cards.• Different CFM channels will be in place such as whataspp, hotline, email, phone as well as physically by MEAL team. • PSEA awareness materials (such as posters and pamphlets) and reporting hotline will be visible to beneficiaries at the distribution site and/or are included in distribution kits.
