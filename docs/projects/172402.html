Project Summary

This project aims to prevent morbidity and mortality among women and children affected by the multi-hazard situation in priority districts. The primary focus will be on Child Health, Maternal Health and emergency programs. UNICEF will support procurement and prepositioning of essential medicines, capacity building and other activities detailed below.

1 - Activities

Activities that will address the needs identified in the 69 priority districts include, 1. Procurement of essential medicines commodities and supplies to enable people to have access to health services ensuring there are no stock outs; 2. In order to reduce the under five mortality rate, UNICEF will conduct capacity building for Health Care Workers and Community Health Workers on integrated case management, IPC, surveillance and data management of epidemics and childhood illnesses as well as referral from community level; 3. In addition, UNICEF will support measles vaccination catch up campaigns in these districts to reduce the chances of any measles outbreaks; 4. To reduce the proportion of children born without assistance of a skilled health personnel, UNICEF will conduct capacity building on BEmONC CHW’s on proper referrals of pregnant women to health facilities for safe delivery, and Emergency Triage and Treatment (ETAT) for health workers caring for sick children in health facilities.. 5. In addition, it is important to provide incentives to skilled health workers in order to retain them at health facilities through Result Based Financing (RBF). 6. UNICEF and MOHCC will conduct monitoring visits on the response activities in these affected districts.

8 - Accountability to Affected People
8.1 - Describe how your project was designed in consultation with communities? How will you incorporate communities in project implementation and what feedback mechanisms do you have in place?

To ensure accountability to affected people UNICEF will work with the community structures at all levels. All stakeholders including the community will be consulted for the health intervention, delivery modality, and quality of service they receive. Community engagement platforms such as community meetings and Health Centre committees will be utilized to ensure participation and inclusion of affected communities, and health partners will ensure representation of men and women, girls and boys in the platforms. Joint field monitoring visits and regular meetings will be held with partners to foster knowledge development, share good practices and ensure coordinated health interventions.

9 - Environmental considerations
9.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project does not have much impact on the environment. However, the known environmental hazards will be managed by the specific technical people. Disposal of biohazardous and chemical waste will be done with the guidance of experts. Health care workers will be trained on proper disposal of waste to protect the environment. All dangerous objects such as sharps will be disposed as guided by Ministry of Health and Child Care protocols. The community will be made aware of standard medical waste disposal methods at household level such as burning and disposal in septic tanks or blair toilets. Health Care Workers will be encouraged to work with the Environmental Management Authority (EMA) for technical guidance.
