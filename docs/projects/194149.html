Project Summary

Landmine (LM) and Explosive Remnants of War (ERW) pose a significant threat to the safety and security of IDPs, returnees, at risk populations and humanitarian actors in South Kordofan and Blue Nile states. Women and children are particularly vulnerable. SK and BN states have the highest number of land mine casualties in the entire Sudan, According to the International Organization for Migration (IOM) integrated locations assessment ILA (IOM web side , there is 396,509 IDPs in South Kordofan (245,353) and Blue Nile (151,156), most of whom have moved from SPLM-N- controlled areas to the government-controlled area in search of food, medicine and other basic services. Despite the series of ceasefires in the Two Areas in the last three years, the dire humanitarian situation in the opposition-controlled areas, compounded by a lack of access for humanitarian assistance, has pushed people to move for survival. Mine Action activities in South Kordofan has been very limited since 2011 until UNMAS was granted access to the government-controlled areas in 2019 , while areas under the control of SPLA faction led by Abdul Aziz Al-Hilu have not been accessible to date. The exact level of contamination is not known as during past conflicts, records of planted landmines were rarely kept and a comprehensive survey has not been possible to date. The project aim to reduce the risk posed by EO. JASMAR will implement two Manual Clearance teams , tow Multi Task Teams (MTTs) to clear and release 300,000 of lands contaminated by mines and Explosive Remnants of War (ERW) in BN 160,000 sqm & 140,000 in SK that posing direct threats to IDPs, host communities and prevent the access to provide humanitarian interventions through land release. The EO contaminated lands in the target localities ( Habila ,Talwdi in SK and Bow , Elkurmuk in BN) will be cleared from the EO threat , closed and released. The released land will be handed over to 76,614 (16220 men, 18340 women, 20487 girls and 21567 boys) IDPs, returnees, at risk population and humanitarian workers in SK and BN. Furthermore JASMAR will implement tow teams through EO Risk Education to enable 326000 IDPs, returnees & people in host communities to identify and adopt safety measures that reduce the death and injuries posed by landmines/ ERW in in the four target localities in BN 163,000 individual ( 44,000 men 37,500 women, 48,820 boys, 32,680 girls) and SK 163,000 individuals including (43,080 men, 38,420 women, 46,880 boys, 34,620 girls). Also JASMAR will improve the life condition of explosive accidents survivors and promote the socioeconomic reintegration for them into their societies. AMSAR will conduct EO Victim assistance that include the provision of support to 15 ( 4 men, 4 women, 3 boy and 4 girls) EO accident survivors lives in SKS Habila & Talowdi , and to 15 ( 4 men, 4 women, 4 boy and 3 girls) EO accident survivors lives in Kurmuk , Baw, localities in BNR.
Land clearance from EO Priorities and needs of women, girls, boys, and men, their mobility patterns, different roles and responsibilities will be considred during project implementation in the target localities. Defient group of Community will be involve in different stages of the project management of operations including planning, prioritization, implementation, release and delivery of mine action services . JASMAR will gathering information from both women and men in the mine affected communities, and setting up LR & EORE teams made up of both females and males. Also Gender equality will be observed both in victim assistance staffing positions (project manager, Filed officer…etc) as well as project beneficiaries’ selection.

1 - Needs

According to the Information Management System Mine Action (IMSMA) September 2022 report in South Kordofan state , 752 EO victims since start of the war conflict from this number 200 killed segregated (60 x men, 10 x women, 26 x boys ,22 x girls and 92 not specified). Additional to that 552 Furthermore and according to the same report 63 EO hazard areas still open in Blue Nile while 243 EO hazard areas still opened in South Kordofan states the landmine /ERW contamination impeded the socio economic development of the area as inhabitants are unable to utilize this land for agriculture, grazing & blocking accessibility of roads. Women and children are particularly vulnerable either as direct victims or as part of families who become even further marginalized economically, socially & psychologically. Although women and girls comprise a smaller percentage of the total recorded casualties, they are particularly vulnerable either as direct casualties during daily livelihood activities, cultivating land, attending animals, collecting water and firewood or as part of families who become even further marginalized economically, socially & psychologically. Women and girls face additional discrimination, including abandonment, limited access to medical services, Injured (handicapped) a female’s chance of marrying is likely to be affected, the responsibility of caring for the ill or disabled of children and husband adds heavily to the workload of women. This indicates the necessity for landmine clearance/NTS/TS/ and EORE interventions.

5.2 - Impact on environment/natural resources

Destruction of EO (mines and UXO) founds during LR operation will have impact of the environment including cutting trees , soil movement and disturb watercourse The LR teams will adhere to environmental conservation during LR operations by, the location of Central Demolition Side (CDS) to consider the environmental aspects. Items found during the clearance will be destroyed with minimum impact on environment, avoid burning/cutting trees & vegetation as much as possible ; if necessary mitigate the damage by adopting the control measures according to JASMAR SOPs, SNMAS & IMAS 10.04 & at the end of the operations all items removed for camping purposes will be returned to the original situation before intervention.
