Project Summary

The project proposes to improve access to safe water, sanitation facilities and promote good hygiene practices for 3,825 female and 3,675 male beneficiaries in underserved and hard-to-reach areas. The target districts of intervention include Belet Hawa and Luuq districts in Gedo. A gender analysis conducted in September 2019 in Gedo by Trocaire has informed the different needs of women, girls, men and boys, noting a lack of access to clean water as the biggest problem in Gedo. The location of WASH facilities such as toilets in camp- like setting presents unique protection concerns thereby increasing the vulnerability of women. Another finding is that women and girls are rarely involved in the design, or in determining the location of these facilities. This project will aim to address these gaps by ensuring inclusion of women and girls in the design of the project, determining locations of WASH assets, and through measurement of the satisfaction of all social groups with the project deliverables. The project aims to contribute to the achievement of two specific objectives of the WASH cluster: delivering lifesaving WASH assistance to reduce acute needs, and providing/ restoring sustainable access to safe water services in target locations. This will be done through construction of one hybrid water system with piping to communities, construction of 25 permanent gender-sensitive latrines with handwashing stations, hygiene promotion sessions with distribution of hygiene kits, and environmental cleanup campaigns. In addition, Trocaire will conduct water testing and treatment of water facilities in target locations, and conduct trainings and refresher trainings for representative Water Management Committees while equipping them with tools necessary for effective management of water points. Lack of access to safe and adequate water due to under performance of successive rainy seasons and the resultant drying up of Dawa and Juba Rivers, low sanitation coverage coupled with poor hygiene knowledge and practices puts these communities at risk of waterborne diseases and loss of life. On the flip side, the flooding of these two rivers presents a high risk of waterborne diseases especially during Gu and Deyr seasons. Trocaire will collaborate with Somali Humanitarian Relief Action (SHRA), a national NGO with strong experience in implementing WASH projects in Gedo, in delivery of this project in the two target districts.

Project Activities

1. Construction of one hybrid water system - This will target IDP communities with durable solutions that enable them access safe water continuously during and after the emergency period. This will also include operation and maintenance support for 10 months of the year.2. Conduct training to one representative Water Management Committee on management of water resources. 3 women and 2 men will then be equipped with tools for effective management of water points. They will also receive one refresher training after 6 months of the first training. 3. Training of 15 hygiene promoters on WASH, and equipment of these community volunteers to conduct promotion sessions in rural and urban villages.4. Water quality testing/Monitoring - to ensure that drinking water is safe and in accordance with the WHO standards. 100 water points will be targeted. Water treatment will be conducted at the water source, though households will also be provided with chlorine tabs in the hygiene kit for water treatment at the household level. 5. Construction of 25 gender-sensitive permanent latrines with handwashing stations.6. Procurement & distribution of 900 emergency hygiene kits (20 litre Jerican, a bucket, bar soaps, and 200 Aqua tabs) for affected host households and IDP families. 7. Distribution of sanitary material to 900 adolescent girls.8. Conduct behaviour change hygiene promotion activities (door to door and group sessions) to benefit about 7,500 individuals9. 6 Environmental clean-up campaigns held in the 2 districts.

Mitigation Measures

2a. Exclusion and marginalization:- Trocaire will ensure that the location, distance and access routes to service delivery points are accessible to all members of the community, including the most vulnerable groups.- Trocaire will identify and consult women, men, boys, girls, persons with disabilities, older persons and marginalized persons to collect accurate information about their specific WASH requirements and preferences and prioritize needs based on this consultation.- Trocaire will maintain a standard targeting criteria and proper documentation to prevent exclusion of vulnerable groups.- The project staff will regularly engage women and children directly and adapt program activities to meet their needs.- Community representatives will be involved in identifying solutions and the decision-making processes that affect them, so as to promote ownership, and improve the relevance and sustainability of the response2b. Exclusion and marginalization- Hold meetings with the community and conduct outreach activities to include more vulnerable individuals;- Implement mobile outreach programmes through the community health volunteers to include more coverage;- Provide for ramps near WASH facilities to increase access to people with mobility difficulties;- Ensure that community outreach teams are composed of both male and female members in reasonable proportion;- Ensure representation of women and youth in water management committees.3b. External interference by stakeholders in beneficiary selection- Have a pre-determined beneficiary selection criteria developed from the needs assessment sessions with beneficiaries;- fine-tune the beneficiary selection criteria with representative village selection committees;- Work closely with women and girls to determine the most vulnerable among-est them and consult those selected on the project design;- Keep lists of beneficiaries and personal data confidential and safe from unintended use;- Work closely in coordination with protection actors to put in place measures to prevent and respond to physical/sexual violence arising from use of or lack of WASH facilities.
