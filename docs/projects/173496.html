Project Summary

The project seeks to support the coordination and COVID-19 preparedness and response to the estimated 6,000 front-line NGO aid workers in light of the COVID-19 pandemic. Support mechanisms will ensure inclusion of distance consultations and detection and treatment referral pathways and improvement of health facilities. The facilities that will be made available will allow for testing of the virus and be equipped to address the needs of NGO aid workers should they test positive for COVID-19 and need urgent assistance.

1 - Identified Needs

Rohingya Refugees currently residing in camps in Cox’s Bazar are highly vulnerable to COVID-19 due to overcrowded living conditions, poor WASH infrastructure. Rohingya refugee camps are among the most densely populated place on earth with more than 42,000 people/ km2 and physical distancing measures are expected to have a limited impact. Poor food consumption scores and underlying malnutrition contribute to weakened immune status of individuals. Elderly persons above the age of 60 and those with underlying health conditions are at higher risk of developing serious disease and will be affected disproportionally. Even though the number of confirmed cases in the camps to date remains low, there is an urgent need to have systems in place to rapidly detect, diagnose, and prevent the spread of the virus to minimize human-to-human transmission and be able to provide dignified care for those who contract the virus. To this effect, there are around 6,000 NGO staff members currently providing critical services to the refugees and host communities. Despite the risks incurred at the frontlines, there is currently no special system in place for the detection and treatment of NGO aid workers. Should there a be a sudden uptake of the spread of infection in Cox's Bazar district, these frontline workers will be extremely susceptible. Consequently, detection and treatment facilities need to be established for the aid workers, including medical staff. In the likely event that the virus spreads to the camps, all efforts must be made to minimize morbidity and mortality of the front-line aid workers of the various NGOs working in the camps.

2 - Activities or outputs

>Provision of technical leadership, coordination, collaboration and information management support to all aid workers responding to the COVID-19 pandemic in the camps. Coordination with Agencies and actors as well as government counterparts to construct, equip or improve health facilities to accommodate host community and aid workers.>Procurement of PPE and medical supplies for the detection and treatment facility>Establish hand washing stations and train health staff on IPC measures, strengthen waste management protocols >Train health care workers on COVID-19 case definitions, treatment protocols etc>Train health care workers and establish homebased care for mild and moderate cases >Establish distance consultation service>Establish coordination mechanism for the referral and treatment of NGO aid workers
