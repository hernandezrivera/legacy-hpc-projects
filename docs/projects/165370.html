Project Summary

The main goal of this project is to help the people of the NE Syria to overcome the
burdens of life by training them to practice one of the specified vocations so that they can
be able to start a new career and get involve in the labor/business market.
Such intervention would help decreasing the dependency of people and filling the gap in
labor market caused by the loss of skilled people as a result of displacement and
conflict.
Participants will also receive business start-up courses to decrease the risk of failure and
guarantee the sustainability of their businesses/careers.
The project will target 150 beneficiaries who will be distributed on 4 vocational areas:
Automotive Mechanical Systems, Automotive Electrical Systems, Turning and Welding.
Tool kits will be provided to 70% of the most successful ones, this will be decided based
on post-evaluation test, attendance rate and commitment to learning.
By applying these four industrial vocations, the program will contribute in replenishing the
labor market in the NE of Syria.
Choosing the NE of Syria is a result of that this area is the biggest agriculture area in
Syria, which imposes the use of heavy duty machines that need continuous maintenance
and spare parts. That's why the program focuses of such industrial occupations.

4 - Needs

The North-East region is the essential source of wheat production in Syria. Since thebeginning of the Syrian crisis in March 2011, the agriculture sector was heavily affectedby losing many resources (machinery, workshops, lands and skilled people). This lossimposes the need of focusing on filling the gap between the Wheat production and themarket needs, by replenishing all related industries (Mechanical, Electrical, Turning andWelding). 5 - Implementation of activities

The project will target the most vulnerable people in NE Syria and will aim to train themon one of the four mentioned professions. The training will be conducted by serviceproviders who have the required experience to guarantee optimal implementation.Dealing with service providers will be through tendering procedures that are compatiblewith GOPA-DERD policies. The training will take place in vocational training institutes ifavailable, otherwise it will take place in private workshop specialized with thecorresponding professions. During the implementation, GOPA-DERD will conductcontinuous monitoring to ensure that the quality of training, curriculum and usedequipment complies with the quality mentioned in the tender books. After completing thevocational training, beneficiaries will receive business start-up courses provided byexperienced service providers.
