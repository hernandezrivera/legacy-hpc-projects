Project Summary

The project will aim to give effect to the commitments made in the World Humanitarian Summit and through the Grand Bargain of fostering new and strengthened partnerships and collaboration between international and national actors, including government institutions, place an emphasis on enhancing local capacities and expanding access to funding channels and mechanisms for local actors. INTERSOS, will build this intervention on an existing agreement with UNICEF, which is foreseeing technical capacity building on child protection with 7 local partners. In order to contribute to Objective 3 of the Protection sector, INTERSOS will support partners in conducting a capacity self-assessment related to institutional capacity building to come up with plans and then help the NNGOs prioritise what they want to strengthen, moving form one-off training programmes to be replaced with coaching and mentoring approaches which are more sustained, intensive and sustainable.

Needs

Access to vulnerable groups in Libya is one of the major constraints hampering the delivery of humanitarian assistance. Applying the Principle of Partnership and the Grand Bargain commitments, INTERSOS is planning to support 7 national partners in developing their institutional and technical capacity based on the assumption that locally led and locally delivered response based on humanitarian principles offers the best opportunity to maximize reach, quality and sustainability.

Activities

Through providing dedicated coaching and mentoring to staff of pre-selected local NGO involved in programmes.Methodology: Delivering of a tailored mentoring programme for local NGOs, including preliminary assessment, dedicated ongoing coaching and effectiveness assessment.Objectives: 1. Establish and/or strengthening internal systems in the areas of finance, HR, admin and logistics. Priorities can be decided based on previous capacity assessments/audits; 2. INTERSOS will provide the selected NGOs with the needed technical coaching to achieve qualified programmatic aspects, this will be through supporting each organization to develop their own child protection policy and PSEA policy;The training shall include :- protection mainstreaming, - what is CPP and PSEA, - how to develop CPP and PSEA- Accountability to effected populationTrainings are subject to change according to the organization assessment. Time frame: The period will be for +6 months. Measurement of success: National NGOs/Govt receive UN due diligence approval and/or UN risk rating reduced by one level and/or targeted audit recommendations closed Satisfaction ratings + other measures to be determined.
