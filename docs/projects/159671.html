Project Summary

Since 2016 Somalia faced extended drought affected vulnerable communities to be exact displaced people, leaving 2.6 million people displaced and are highly in need for humanitarian assistance. Currently the CCCM is estimating approximately 2.4 Million people are in need ,where 1.4 million will be targeted in the coming HRP 2020. And there is an urgent need to improve coordination and service delivery at the site level, of which Ocean Training and Promotion (OTP) will implement cccm activities in Sanaag, sool and Bari regions to cater the identified needs while the objective of these interventions, will be improved coordination and information management. OTP will re-enforce or establish Camp Management committees who will be fully trained in CCCM and protection principles. During the course of the project, OTP will conduct 4 detailed site assessment in targeted locations. OTP will also construct community spaces to improve communication of communities or simply communication channels, this project will also erect or install solar lights in the targeted IDPs to minimize GBV risks at night . An estimated 10,000 H’Hs will benefit from this project activities in other words, 60,000 Individuals will benefit from this intervention. These numbers might vary due to external factors such as the drought, which might attract more IDPs, excessive flooding or conflict with the landowners or the authorities, which may cause IDPs to flee to other areas.

Project Activities

OTP will create coordination structures and a code of conducts for both gatekeepers and partners working at the site level in order to ensure high standards in terms of respecting the rights of IDPs and improving coordination at site level. This will be done in the framework of the CCCM intervention but will also aim at mainstreaming protection at site level through improved coordination. The content of the code of conduct will be elaborated in consultation with gatekeepers. OTP will map existing community infrastructures within target sites. And will also map and gather information on the social or observation of community dynamics, gatekeepers, community interaction,host-IDP relationship etc. and encouraging participation to strengthen existing systems. Information management products will be produced per site such as partner mapping (or 3W), in targeted sites. OTP will also establish camp management committees and will be trained. CMCs are pivotal to the success of CCCM to ensure efficient communication between coordination structures (including OTP) and beneficiaries. In order to be efficient, CMCs will strive to be as inclusive and representative of the site’s population by involving women, IDP, host communities all relevant stakeholders including gatekeepers.The primary aim of OTP’s intervention is to make the sites a safer place for all IDPs. This is done through different approaches: firstthrough a coordination intervention to improve the delivery of services and the dissemination of information and second through a protection approach which aims to prevent protection risks and improve the partner’s capacity to respond to SGBV.Based on data collected through partners, coordination meetings, and clusters, OTP will map existing community infrastructures within target sites, and gather information on social profile of the community, community dynamics, the involvement of gatekeepers, host-IDP interactions etc. Support in site verification exercises will be considered to ensure changes in the IDP environment

Reaching long term solution for the displaced people in Somalia always takes longer than expected as it requires joint efforts for all partners, governments and other stakeholders, therefore, OTP will work towards securing a long lasting solution for the displaced people in Somalia through being active in the important meetings or fora where durable solutions is discussed so that both IDPs and refugees will must likely get a permanent solution in the near future. OTP will closely work with local authorities by advocating the rights of the people so that jointly all the needs or longer terms solutions for the displaced people are addressed collectively through durable solution working groups or related forums so that partners and other stakeholders will be able to secure lands which are public so that these lands can be given to the displaced people and permanent shelters are constructed as well as ensuring rights of these vulnerable people are respected and they will have chance to live a dignified life.
