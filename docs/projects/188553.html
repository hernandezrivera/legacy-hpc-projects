Project Summary

The project will provide integrated training programs for young people aged 18-25 who are not enrolled in schools and universities to invest their skills and engage them in society.
Section I: includes 3 training programs:
1. Vocational training 3 months (ICDL-designing - mobile maintenance-hairdressing)
2. Management training 2 months: (Project Management- MEAL - English – Secretarial - Writing Reports - Principles of Humanitarian work)
3. Community awareness and democracy workshops 2 months in parallel with Management training
Section2:
- First is to provide specialized kits in the ¦eld of training for 50 % of participants to open a workshop in which he could work and we will follow up for 3 months to ensure the success of the work and provide consultancy.
- Secondly: Provide opportunities to volunteer for 50 % of trainees in the management training in organizations and pay incentives for 3 months, ensuring that they have a good income and practical experience that will enable them to continue.

4 - Needs

Poverty and unemployment aren't the appropriate environments for participation in democratic life, especially for people who will be searching for a living instead of thinking about their rights and taking part in the electoral process, this can make them a tool in hands of those who buy votes and put them on the wrong side The contribution of the project to securing job opportunities is not sufficient enough so we will conduct awareness seminars to raise the awareness of participating in democratic life. The problem we are trying to solve is: A whole generation is vulnerable to loss and delinquency, especially among young (males and females) the reasons are: 1- The lack of secondary schools. 2- The difficulty of returning to school due to the long dropout of education. 3- The lack of voluntary Courses that are concerned with young (males and females). The project will provide integrated training programs for young (males and females) aged 18 - 25 who are not enrolled in schools and universities to invest their skills and engage them in society. The needs assessment sources: Local Council of in Alkesrha sub-district in Deir Ez-Zor City . Reports by TOTOL for Relief and Development Field MEAL personnel.
