Project Summary

This project is aimed at reducing child malnutrition and ensuring that pregnant and lactating women have access to nutritious food for improved child development. The project will target vulnerable host communities where 1,500 IDPs, returnees and vulnerable host community women will be organized into social groups for group cooking and social support.
The project will also identify 12 boreholes in target locations for rehabilitation and upgrade to solar to ensure that women have access to safe water. The WASH component of the project will also focus on the hygiene and sanitation improvement of target communities through Community Led Total Sanitation and distribution of NFIs and Hygiene Kits

1 - Needs

The ongoing conflict, which is now in its eleventh year, and its associated displacement ofpeople has continued to put pressure on communities in the north east. Borno state which is the most affected with a large number of people still displaced and ongoing conflicts further displacing civilians has high malnutrition rates, child protection gaps and the need for enhanced water and sanitation support.

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

SOS Children's Villages consults widely with all community members during projectdesign and provides a platform for all beneficiaries to give feedback freely. The projectwill target school children and utilize child representative platforms to seek their viewson all decisions that concern them. SOS Children's Villages also implements projects inconsultation with caregivers of children children, who are part of the Key ImplementingPartners (KIPs) and are fully involved in decision-making. To ensure that everyone'svoice is heard, SOS CVN utilizes community volunteers and zonal leaders who areselected from the beneficiaries and serve as the first point of call for beneficiaries to beable to give feedback. In implementing this project, SOS Children's Village will publicizea toll-free line for all beneficiaries to be able to give feedback of the intervention andraise any concerns they may have. this toll-free line will be printed and displayed at all women group meeting sites and communicated during meetings

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

The project will collect feedback in three ways: 1. Toll-Free Line- to be publicized in allproject locations, including Women Safe Spaces and during meetings.There will be arrangement for Complaint Response Mechanism officers to be able tospeak the available local dialects in the area of intervention. All beneficiary feedbackand complaints will be logged in and responded to by SOS CVN senior managementusing our safeguarding guidelines. 2. Group Leaders will be selected from PLWs inthe community and are accessible to the community members for easy access andassistance. 3. Periodic feedback sessions with different categories of the communitiesand camps will be carried out during quarterly surveys. Attention will be given tofeedback from all categories, including boys, girls, men, women, elderly and personswith special needs. The monitoring and evaluation team of SOS CVN will carry outquarterly post distribution monitoring of the food and ensure that beneficiaries receivetheir entitlement and give feedback on the support received.
