Project Summary

This project plans to meet objective 2 and 3 aiming to improve camp management quality in 20 informal IDP settlements in Northwest Syria, particularly in northern-west Idleb in Harim district. It targets the IDP sites that reported to be impacted by recent natural disaster incidents during winter 2020-2021 in which still suffering from these incidents. Targeted IDP sites will be selected based on specific criteria that ensure
1- which informal settlements are recommended for basic infrastructure maintenance.
2- IDP sites has lack in camp management or weak management structure observed.
The interventions will ensure resilience, participation and safety of IDPs by enhancing camp management capacity. MHD will establish 3 camp committees in each of the targeted sites depend on the need and gap, these 3 camp committees together with the camp management office will have direct support to ensure ideal camp management structure and regular maintenance carried out by the committees themselves.
Following technical CCCM guidance of Camp Management Toolkit and other guidance by CCCM cluster, 1 IDP representative committee and 2 sub-committees of 1-Fire Safety and Security and 2- Infrastructure maintenance will be established and elected. These committees will have women, youth and disability direct participation across the 3 committee to ensure community engagement.
The proposed activities that will take place by MHD during 2021 will contribute to the winterization plan of 2021-2022. In which proposed activities will increase the precaution measures taken against man-made and natural disaster incidents.
The basic infrastructure maintenance will include latrine, bath, drainage system, sewage system, lighting, gravelling and other items could be selected by the maintenance committee within the scope of CCCM interventions.

4 - Needs

IDPs sites remain highly exposed to weather conditions. More than 200 IDPs sites were directly affected by foods that hit several areas in northern Syria in 2020. More than thousand tents were destroyed partially or completely requiring replacement and further compounding needs of IDPs in these sites. MHD has been assessing IDP sites affected by various incidents during winter 2020-2021, 140 flood incidents occurred in IDP sites in northwest Syria in spontaneously established IDP sites in Idleb and Aleppo governorates. Most of the incidents resulted in completely and partially damage of tents, however, the common reason of these good incidents was the absence of basic infrastructure and/or maintenance and the spontaneous establishment by IDPs or unaccountable actor.The lack of accountable management and weak management structure have increased IDPs suffering and increased the need severity as camps are expanding.The increase of spontaneously established IDP sites in northwest Syria in 2020 is showed an increase in IDP severity in many areas. Based on the IDP Site Integrated Monitoring Matrix Plus, currently, 1.6 million IDPs are residing in 1,396 IDP sites and (55%) of them has no IDP committee within the management structure at all. Vulnerability of women, girls, boys and men and PWD in the last resort IDPs sites have become protracted over time without accountable management and lack of IDPs engagement in decisions.

5 - Implementation of activities

The interventions will be carried out in 20 IDP sites that will be selected based on high vulnerability reported by CCCM cluster through criteria mentioned. Each IDP site will have elected 1 IDP representation committee, and 2 sub-committees for fire mitigation and camp maintenance.Each committee will be elected through transparent elections considering gender balance, youth and people with disabilities participation. Each committee will have a developed Terms of Reference by CCCM consultants and in coordination with CCCM cluster.The maintenance committee will receive dedicated training to be able to implement basic maintenance activities by themselves with the support and supervision of MHD engineers. the committee will be able to identify the gaps, needed interventions and the necessary equipment to conduct these maintenance.The fire safety and security sub-committee will mitigate fire risk through the provision of fire safety measures, training on fire precaution measures, and equip them with necessary materials.MHD will establish fire warden system across these 20 IDP sites to maintain communication, rapid response and equipment sustainability. MHD will include part of the maintenance budget for this committee to ensure materials and equipment are always available to use.The IDP representatives committee will lead both sub-committees and will be responsible to follow up, report, support and engage with other committees, camp management, and CCCM cluster directly.

7.1 - Please list any risks related to sexual exploitation and abuse that have been identified during the project design phase and any risk mitigation and prevention measures that have been adopted and built into the project

The risk of sexual exploitation and abuse during the intervention: All MHD staff are receiving regular training on the code of conduct and PSEA policy and they signed on these policies, The contractors have the obligation to sign an ethical declaration with a specific clause on PSEA with an emphasis on mitigation measures and sanctions in case of abuse by an employee, Beneficiaries will receive information on the CRM and the expected behavior of MHD staff and its contractors with an emphasis on how to report any SEA incidents. A woman staff is present during all visits to women and child led-households. MHD has already existing SEA system in which MHD protection specialists at the HQ and field offices will apply the measures and keep monitoring the implementation accordingly.
