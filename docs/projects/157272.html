Project Summary

The project overall goal is to contribute to reduction in nutrition related mortality and morbidity among girls and boys aged under five years, women and men mainly through community based nutrition services .
This will be achieved through improved access and coverage of quality Management of Acute Malnutrition as well as strengthening local capacity of communities .
The project will be directly implemented by PCO in the targeted areas in Blue Nile,South Kordufan and Kassala state.

Needs

Sudan has high level of acute and chronic malnutrition indicators. Acute malnutrition measured as global acute malnutrition, GAM is 16.4%, which is above the international ‘emergency’ thresholds of 15%. Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM) rates are also worryingly high at 5.3% which translates into half a million children suffering from SAM. Put another way, at any point in time, 1 in 20 Sudanese children are severely malnourished, with a greatly increased risk of death (SHHS, 2010). About 35% of children under fives were moderately or severely stunted (too short for their age). The proportion of children who are stunted is highest in Kassala state (55%). Almost one of three under five children (32.2%) in Sudan was found to be moderately or severely underweight. In Sudan, the immediate causes of malnutrition are the inadequate dietary intake and disease. There are many indicators showing the high prevalence of these aggravating factors for malnutrition for instance, approximately 75% of children age 12 23 months received BCG vaccination by 12 months of age, while the coverage for measles vaccine was at 62%. Overall, the percentage of children who had all the recommended vaccinations by their first birthday was as low as 39%. Pneumonia is the leading cause of death in children. The SHHS 2010 indicates that about 19% of children less than five years were reported had symptoms of pneumonia during the two weeks preceding the survey. The SHHS 2010 indicated that overall, 27% of under five children had diarrhoea in the two weeks preceding the surveyThe economic situation in Blue Nile, Kassala and South Kordofan States compounded by the conflict , low access to healthcare and diseases has direct impact on the nutritional status of large number of the population. According to the result of S3M , 550,000 children are expected to suffer from severe acute malnutrition in coming year, where 59 localities out of the 184 localities in Sudan have a prevalence of Global Acute Malnutrition (GAM) above emergency threshold levels of 15 per cent,. Displacement and other factors are contributing to the deterioration of the situation including feeding habits, child care, sanitation and access to health services. Pan Care will support 20 OTP in 6 localities 5 in Dallam( Kadbar, Kukaya, GardoodAngya, Umheatan and Dalami villages ),5 in Abogibiha( Alrahmania,Alsisaban,Aldibibat,Malam Alkour,) 4 in Algoz locality (AmDibibat,Alhamadi,Nabag,Dibikar),2 in Altadamun locality (Raiba,Amrakouba) 2 in Aldiling (Aldiling hospital,Alsafa))2 in Habilla (Habila hospital ,Kartala) in south Kordufan state and 8 OTP (Misfa,Gabal Alnoor in Bau ,Fazugli,Daim Said in Gessan locality,Shafur,Hamda,Abuzure in AlRosirs locality and Griwa in Altadamun locality in Blue Nile and 3 OTP in Aroma (Alsidira,Alsinari,Koga)in Kassala state.

Activities

-Establishment of 31 OTPs in South Kourdufan ,Blue Nile and kassala state.-Conduct MUAC screening for children under 5 and PLWs will be conducted every 2 months until the end of the project. -Provision of referral services for malnourished cases during MUAC screening campaigns, malnourished cases will be identified and referred to OTP and SC.-Conduct basic and refresher training on community management of acute malnutrition (CMAM) to 93 staff.-Recruitment and training of 310 volunteers (10 from each one of the targeted villages) selected from 31 OTP targeted villages to be trained on CMAM .-Establishment of 10 support groups in the targeted villages 10 mother-to-mother support groups will be established in 10 of the targeted villages so as to conduct IYCF practice and knowledge sharing activities. Each group will be composed of 10 women.-Dissemination of IEC/BCC materials:This action will support printing and dissemination of IEC/BCC materials related to IYCF, malnutrition case identification and management in the targeted localities through organizing mass awareness creation sessions among the communities and within the health and nutrition facilities. -Conduct training of 5000 mothers on ‘MUAC-by-mother’:This community-level training will be carried out by community nutrition volunteers. Training of the mothers will be at the household level with practical demonstrations.-A celebration of breastfeeding week.- trained mother on counsellings of IYCF .-Conducting monthly community awareness campaigns.-
