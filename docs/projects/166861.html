Project Summary

Enlargement of Project # 159924: Proposed activties remain the same as original submission. However, certain activities will modify in implementation: trainings will include COVID-19 specific elements, health workers and health centres will follow social distancing guidelines. Mobile Medical Units will provide further coverage and outreach with COVID-19 prevention messaging and health assistance, and procurement will include essential PPE items and continuation of ambulance services to allow health staff to remain on the frontline addressing morbidity of COVID-19 and other persistent health issues., Total estimated beneficiaries = 100400
