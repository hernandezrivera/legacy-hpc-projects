Project Summary

IOM aims to provide humanitarian Shelter/NFI Assistance to 165,000 newly displaced and protracted IDPs across Somalia.
The 2020 strategy covers three distinct areas:
1) Contingency Stock with in-kind NFI kits for 12,500 households (for 75,000 individuals). These kits are available for distribution nationwide by NGO partners where need arises and as endorsed by Shelter cluster.
2) Emergency Shelter & NFI support to newly displaced IDPs in Gedo and Bay Region targeting 13,000 households (78,000 individuals). In total, 8,000HH across the regions will receive NFI and 5,000HH will receive emergency shelter provision. Where assessed appropriate, beneficiaries may receive both emergency shelter and NFI provision. Interventions will be conducted by the IOM SNFI response teams. Geographical priority will be Gedo and Bay regions given the prevalence of high numbers of newly displaced IDPs affected by conflict, drought and forced eviction in these areas. IOM has active CCCM teams within these regions which enables optimal targeting of beneficiaries, response tracking & accountability. Additional focus will be given to regions such as Lower Juba and Benadir through use of mobile interventions. The target is to have 70% of the beneficiaries stated in the proposal reached out through cash-based interventions, whenever this is feasible and appropriate. The quality programming of this response will revolve around a solid approach to accountability to affected population by promoting community participation in programme implementation and accountability to inform analysis and future response.
3) Transitional shelter support to 3,000 households (18,000 individuals) in Bay region. Improving the durability of IDP shelters to those who already having received basic emergency shelter provision. Shelter responses will be subject to needs assessment, however, will target 3,000HH (18,000 individuals) in Baidoa.

Project Activities

1) To contribute towards cluster Objective 1, IOM will provide Shelter/NFI assistance to newly displaced populations, with a specific focus on IDPs/ host communities who have been affected by conflict and natural disasters. This objective will incorporate two activities. 1) SNFI Interventions: 8,000 households will receive life-sustaining NFI and 5,000 households Emergency Shelter Kits through static interventions in Bay, Gedo, Kismayo and Benadir Regions. These activities will be implemented through cash-based interventions whenever it is feasible and appropriate but could also include in kind. Firstly however a needs assessment will be conducted to identify the most appropriate modality of response and where it is safe to do so & operational markets are in place, cash will be prioritised subject to a full market assessment. Where assessed that beneficiaries require both shelter and NFI, there will be an overlap of provision made. Based upon previous responses we estimate 20% of the 5,000 recipients of emergency shelter will require NFI (1,000 beneficiary HH). 2) IOM Contingency Stock: where funding is possible the preposition of contingency stock in Mogadishu will be strengthened to provide an additional 12,500 NFI kits. NGO cluster partners are able to request this stock upon assessment & authorisation by the Shelter Cluster Coordinator to those affected by conflict and natural disaster who are in immediate need of assistance and as a last resort when other modalities are not feasible. 2) To contribute towards cluster Objective 2, where the resilience and living conditions of affected populations is improved through housing and related community infrastructure IOM will continue to focus activities on vulnerable IDPs who are under high threat of eviction in Bay region. Whilst providing life-sustaining Shelter & NFI assistance through cash based interventions (whenever appropriate) to 13,000 households in Bay, Gedo, Kismayo and Benedir Regions, a further 3,000 households will receive life-sustaining transitional shelter assistance where possible in conjunction with CCCM to ensure adequate site planning in Bay. As part of the shelter assistance, to build resilience, a sample of the community will be trained in construction methods and those most vulnerable provided with construction support. 3) To contribute towards cluster Objective 3 to improve the quality of shelter and NFI assistance and ensure accountability through effective complaint and feedback mechanisms IOM will conduct Post Distribution Monitoring (PDM’s) after each distribution and ensure that after sharing with the SNFI cluster that this forms the basis of learnings for future responses. There will also be an active complaints feedback mechanism operational at each SNFI response allowing beneficiaries to raise issues and receive further assistance if required from the IOM teams.

Mitigation Measures

3a1) Collect and use data disaggregated by sex, age and diversity to effectively inform programming. 2) Ensure that assistance and services are reaching the most vulnerable - Identify and prioritise the most vulnerable groups in the community and prevent discrimination or exclusion of marginalised groups. 3) Note which group may have greater difficulty finding/renting accommodation or more prone to eviction. 4) Apply a systematic outreach process in registering beneficiaries for distributions. 5) Ensure lack of documentation does not exclude individuals from accessing services 3c 1) Ensure shelter/NFI staff, implementing partners and volunteers working with affected populations understand, sign and adhere to a Code of Conduct stating their commitment to respect and foster humanitarian standards and the rights of beneficiaries. 2) Ensure all staff, implementing partners and volunteers involved in distributions and in direct contact with beneficiaries have received training in prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse (PSEA) 3) Rotate distribution teams so no one team constantly visits the same settlements. 4) Provide clear information to distribution actors and affected population explaining that beneficiaries do not have to pay or provide services/favours to anyone in exchange for receiving shelter or NFI assistance. Ensure that this information is communicated to women and girls. 5) In cooperation with protection actors, enhance the capacity of community shelter/NFI workers to monitor, report and refer protection concerns (such as abuse and exploitation) in accordance with standard operating procedures. 4a1) Take into account the land tenure arrangements of IDPs, including statutory/legislative and customary access rights to land and inheritance rights. Advocate with land and property owners and local authorities to avoid eviction or disproportional rising in rent. 2) Obtain permission before using or building on any land or property, in writing, where possible.3) Avoid community tensions, by providing the local/host community with access to camp services and assistance in situations where they are experiencing similar shortages of non-food items. 4) Consult both displaced and host communities about shelter and NFI needs and assess whether assistance could cause tension, harassment or conflict between the two communities. 5) Keep lists of beneficiaries and personal data confidential and safe from unintended use - apply effective data protection measures.

The IOM shelter & NFI team will work closely with our Camp Coordination and Camp Management & Recovery and Durable Solution teams where possible to ensure that there are community led solutions which address longer term durable solutions in our shelter provision. This will address areas such as site drainage, solar street lighting, road & site access to shelters, addressing longer term protection risks & access to facilities such as schools, health clinics, police stations and community gathering areas.
