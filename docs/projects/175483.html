1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project aims to improve the life conditions of families displaced by the conflict and host communities. It foresees an initial assessment on wash needs for the most needy displaced families and families hosting IDPs fleeing from conflict areas. Higiene & sanitation training and sensitizationwill be privileged for IDPs in the newly established accommodation sites created in close partnership with local authorities and reference clusters.The beneficiaries lists created by the government must be verified by AVSI shelter staff in order to proceed with higiene kits distributions and therefore to avoid duplications and overlapping during the distributios. The distributions will be followed up and assessed against monitoring assessment created with Kobotool.AVSI will target 35.000 IDPs among the districts of Metuge, Pemba, Montepuez and Ibo.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

• Distribution of point-of-use water treatment materials / products, including training and monitoring on proper use at household level; • installation and management of temporary sanitation and bathing facilities in accomodation centres/transit camps/ or resettlement sites (first phase); • promotion activities for self-construction of houselhold latrines and garbages pits in resettlement sites and hosting communities (including the provision of sanitation supplies for most vulnerable households); • dissemination of WASH related messages through mass communication media; procurement and distribution of WASH related Non-Fodd items, with focus on most vulnerable families/households

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Potential harms and risks may arise in the general access to re-allocation procedures, access to services provision, with special concerns towards vulnerable categories, such as un-accompanied minors, female headed households, elderly, people with disabilities, victims of abuse. AVSI will ensure participation of all groups within the decision making process and governance. Also, through constant monitoring – both through the said multiple CFM and routinary HHs assessments – and sensitization campaigning on protection standards, PSEAH, GBV prevention the community will be capacitated and well informed on rights and prerogatives, as well as on referral and complaints systems. To avoid the major protection risks of resistance, exploitment and/or conflict between IDP and host community consultation meeting will carried out in order to create a community engagement comprehensive mechanism.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

Consultations will be done with affected populations (not only community leaders) to define needs and priorities. Updates on the project will similarly be shared with the affected populations through vulnerability and accountability mechanism. Affected populations will be informed in advance of selection criteria, date/time, and process. All requests for responses to the Linha Verde mechanism will be responed to within 48 hours of receipt from the WASH Cluster.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Consultations will be done individuals with individuals living with disabilities and the elderly to identify specific adaptation needs for infrastructure. Infrastructure will be adapted for these needs.In the present project proposal, the participation of people with disabilities have been included. During their implementation, the detection of people with disabilities will be reported (disaggregated by gender and age) and given special attention. Moreover, it is worthy to note that very often people with disabilities are discriminated within their own communities. This can be a compounding factor when people with disabilities arrived in a host community, as in the case of IDPs. AVSI Foundation will ensure the implementation of sensitization sessions throughout its activities to ensure that these people are supported within the community and given special attention when coordinating and planning activities with local authorities and partners.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

AVSI aims to promote a sustainable and healthy environment in order to improve the wellbeing of the community. In addition to that, the use of local materials will be prioritized in order to adopt an environment-friendly approach, by trying to increase awareness in the affected communities. AVSI can rely to an internal Policy on environment on May 11th 2020.
