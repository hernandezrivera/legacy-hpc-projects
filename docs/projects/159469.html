Project Summary

To improve access to life saving (equally accessible) quality Primary Health Care for the crisis affected most vulnerable male and female populations (61,000 persons including: 36,600 female and 24,400 male) through supporting 5 health facilities which are currently operational in 5 target locations of most priority areas in Wajid District, Bakool region in order to address prevailing gaps, prevent and reduce morbidity and mortality relating to common communicable diseases, emergency obstetric cases and other emergency health conditions

Project Activities

Activities or outputs Output 1. .Improved availability, affordability and accessibility of PHC services in Humanitarian setting. Activity 1.1 Provision of Primary health services to 120000 affected people (including 30000 women ,42000 girls ,30000 boys and 18000 men ) through health facilities with equal access to boys, girls, men and women. Activity 1.2 Provision of medical supplies and equipment in the target 11 facilities Activity 1.3 Conduct skilled deliveries to 3000 pregnant women at target location Output 2 Improved community awareness and on knowledge on SRH related issues including FP and Capacity of staff in humanitarian setting Activity 2.1 Organize 10 community awareness and education sessions on available SRH services including FP targeting 200 adolescent girls, 100 women and 50 men at target location .this will be conducted by mobile clinic Activity 2.2 Provide training for 30 staff(15 male , 15 female) on safe motherhood and IMCI guidelines.
