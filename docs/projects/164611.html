Project Summary

Since the beginning of its presence in the Country in 2005, Armadilla worked on health topics, with a specific focus on children and persons with disabilities. The Syrian crisis in March 2011, has dramatically worsened the situation, and Armadilla has been working since then in the Damascus Area in strict cooperation with local actors and in agreement with the Syrian Ministry of Health. At least 15 per cent of the population of Syria lives with disabilities. A recent assessment on disability among adults in western Aleppo, Idleb and Ar-Raqqa governorates revealed that an average of 45 per cent of surveyed persons injured during the Syria crisis are expected to sustain a permanent impairment and require specialist support which will far outlast the crisis.
Armadilla will continue to focus its work on the support of local actors (specialised services, associations, health centres) in delivering special services for people with disabilities and will seek to wide its range of activities also thanks to the framework partnership realized at HQ level with other Italian organisation specialised in health, including PSA and emergency health. The main goal of the project is to fill the void of human resources capacity in order to sustain and expand essential, quality health services to address health needs of affected populations, and support the health structures involved in the activities through the provision of medical equipment and health material.
The proposed intervention aims to reinforce the capacity of the Ibn Al-Nafis Hospital, which is located in Damascus, but serves the entire population of Syria as specialised centre for prosthesis and rehabilitation of PwD, through supply of technical equipment and specialised training cycles on rehabilitation and prosthesis making. The main objective is increase the number of physical rehabilitation sessions thanks to the specific training for health staff on related topics. Moreover, the project will support the work of the University Al-Manara of Latakia, through the provision of teaching given by International professors of the University of Modena and Reggio-Emilia (Italy) aiming to train students and professionals on Occupational Therapy topics.
Armadilla will also collaborate with 2 hospitals of the rural Damascus, through an intensive training performed by medical teams coming from Italy in order to perform specific interventions during their missions in Syria. All the operations will be realized involving Syrian health professionals with the aim of implementing a learning on the job path which will establish a permanent link among health facilities of the Area with Italian expertise and resources.
The project is integrated with activities related to Child Protection sector, implemented by Zahret Al Madan and Armadilla in the Southern Suburbs of Damascus, Yarmouk Camp and communities of the rural Damascus which volunteers and workers collaborate on permanent basis with the staff of the CPR for the care and therapy plans for children and youngsters with disability.

4 - Needs

In the whole of Syria health care systems for trauma, suffers for lack of equipment and, above all, health workers are insufficiently trained. The Prosthetic and Rehabilitation Center (CPR) of the Ibn Al Nafis public hospital is the only public hospital in Syria where prosthetics and orthosis are built and physiotherapy sessions for people with born or acquired (amputations) disabilities are extensively implemented, which bring uncountable of patients in requesting assistance and health cares. The CPR receives a huge number of requests for diagnosis and treatment, which can be processed only minimally. Treatment for minors is particularly complex, due to the low number of technicians specialized in working with children and youngsters, both in prosthetics and rehabilitation paths. Moreover, the CPR is rehabilitating with public funds a new building, in order to guarantee the access to more users and the possibility to hosts people coming from other areas for the needed complete treatment cycle. Indeed, it worth to be mentioned that one of the most challenging point, is the incapacity of the Centre to offer the possibility of hospitalization (inpatient care), which does not allow patients to follow the entire cycle of therapy, due to the high costs of staying in Damascus. Regarding the Occupational Therapy degree, has been observed that the practice is totally absent on the Syrian territory, while professionals trained on the subject will be able to immediately give the possibility to People with Disability to perform daily personal and work tasks, allow them to quickly recover abilities which support their social inclusion. Armadilla, is working since the end of 2017 in establishing the first Occupational Therapy (OT) degree course.Needs assessment are continuously performed by the I-NGO, through bi-.weekly meetings with the Responsible of the CPR, in order to determine needs and possible activities to be performed in the Centre. 5 - Implementation of activities

Project activities will be implemented in strict collaboration with local partners (CPR of Damascus, Ministry of Health and University Manara of Latakia). At this aim, Armadilla already established general Memorandum of Understanding with the partners, which are willing to cooperate to the project. Armadilla since the year 2006 has signed a protocol of cooperation with the Ministry of Education of Syria, through the collaboration with the CPR, which it is just extended to the Autumn 2022.Activities will be monitored by Armadilla’s staff in Syria and abroad, which also will take care of the logistics details needed for the realization of the project.Main activities to be performed are:Training for CPR health workers (doctors, therapists and prosthesis/orthosis makers), which will be performed by Italian and European professionals. Training course (topics and modalities) will be drafted in conjunction with the responsible of the CPR. Training for students and professionals experts in Occupational Therapy topics will be performed by International professors of European Universities, which will deliver the teaching according to the approved Teaching Plan of the OT degree course in Manara University.Surgical interventions in rural Damascus hospitals will be performed by a roster of Italian health professionals (surgeons, nurses, doctors)Purchase of goods (equipment for the CPR and hospitals, as well as medicines) will be realized in strict coordination with the partners representatives, according to the relevant needs observed.Armadilla participates in the Health and Protection clusters, as well as sub-working group GBV, child and disability. Coordination with other relevant actors is insured through this participation.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Access to the Area of the activitiesLocations where the project will be implemented, Centre for Prosthesis and Rehabilitation (CPR) of Damascus, University Al Manara of Latakia and rural Damascus hospitals, are areas so far considered totally secure and accessible. However, the Organisation will be in continuous communication with the relevant security actors in order to organize activities and movements only after consultation with them. Availability of goodsGoods will be purchased on the local market, looking for the best vendor in terms of economy and capacity of guarantee the provision of service. A back-up list of potential suppliers guarantees the possibility of finding the goods in case of problems will be drafted. Lack of permission to enter in Syria for expatriate staff Armadilla is not experienced problems in obtaining by authorities visas for expatriate staff/experts, however to avoid any delay and obstacle in implementing the activities, monitoring and logistics activities will be realized through Syrian staff of the Organisation, supported by remote by international experts. Experts and professors, in case of temporary problems in coming to Syria will support the training activities through remote sessions.
