Project Summary

This project seeks to enhance community-based protection in North Darfur . The project will be identified through community consultation to support returnees and host community in ND, also will support flood affected people to avail humanitarian assistance for sustainable life-saving. The project will increased access to basic services to sustain socio-economic (re)integration and local economic development for returnee, IDPs, refugees and hosting communities in North Darfur.
The project will assist people through provision of legal assistance, psychosocial support, emergency cash assistance to mitigate protection risks, and referral to GBV and Child Protection. In addition, community-based protection will continue to be a key element of the project’s response in the target state - North Darfur. This will include establishment of and support to community-based protection networks (CBPNs), community-based dispute resolution mechanisms, youth networks and women’s protection networks, in IDP camps/sites and return areas. Community support projects driven, small to medium size infrastructure initiatives – will serve to foster social cohesion between IDPs, returnee and host communities
The project will support deliver high-quality protection assistance, particularly in the areas of protection monitoring, case management, psycho-social support, legal assistance, community-based dispute resolution, and advocacy. Protection Information Management (PIM) systems will be reinforced to implement protection strategy.

1 - Needs

Many at risk population from Returnees, IDPs and host communities face harassment while undertaking farming activities which can contribute to food insecurity. GBV, including sexual violence and domestic violence against women, children, and young people remains a concern amongst all vulnerable groups, but particularly amongst IDPs and refugees. Refugees and asylum seekers continue to face specific protection risks. Although improvements have been made there is a continued need to increase registration, documentation as well as increased access to basic services and formal work opportunities. While there is a plan to mainstream protection activities across all the strategic objectives, interventions under this objective will focus on efforts directed at mitigating protection risks across vulnerable groups.The Protection Sector will introduce updated systems and tools for protection monitoring- including household-level protection monitoring- to enable identification of persons with specific needs and referral to specialized service providers. This is complimented by community-level protection monitoring tools - key informant interviews and focus group discussion that will enable evidence-based reporting on protection needs, threats and risks, and analysis of trends over time, protection monitoring and reporting will be scaled up and geographically concentrated on protection hotspots.NPO implemented protection project in the same localities and according the evaluation report done there is a gap in the protection , women , children and female with disability are most groups at risk especially during COVID-19.

2 - Activities/Outputs

Output 1. Supporting community self-management to prevent and mitigate protection risks for durable solutions planning, to address protection needs where feasible. Community-based support structures in crisis-affected areas of North Darfur have often escalated into violence. The proposed activities to address the output are : 1. Community consultation training to design community-based locality action plans that will enable the sustainability of durable solutions activities in the respective areas.2. Capacitate and train the local government and community leaders on the know-how of developing action plans.3. Establish Community based Protection Networks. Include participation of Youth and women in the networks.4. Strengthening the existing and established women’s centers in the localities – by provision of capacity training to women and girls on prevention and response of Sexual Gender Based Violence (SGBV) and small scale training on hand-crafts, tailoring for women and/ girls .5. Provision of startup kit for women after identifying the Income Generating Activities in the communities6. Conduct vocational training for women/girls and Establish of community social workers to provide information regarding protection issues in weekly basis 10 social worker per locality7. Conduct training for women on SGBV prevention and referrals.Output 2. Support for persons with special needs and highly vulnerable households.1. Conduct PSN capacity building trainings .2. Establish Youth networks that will foster dialogues in the community.3. Consult with the Youth in the communities to come up with targeted youth programs that will generate income.4. Train community networks to identify PSNs in the community and referral mechanisms as well as linking them to services such as health, police and judiciary.5. Train community on briquette making. Briquette as an alternative to firewood collection, will curtail SGBV related incidents in the communities and serve as an income generating activity for the vulnerable persons6. Train and equip outreach volunteers from each of the localities on SGBV, Community-Based Protection mainstreaming as well as identifying disabled PSNs and linking them to services. NPO will budget for 10 wheel chairs, 10 walking sticks, to aid the identified disabled youth, children, and women in their daily work and attendance of community meetings and school.7. Provision of legal aid and link individuals in the community to civil documentation. Train of native administration members on the basic legal principles to help them conduct their services in regards to land issues arising within the community. Train existing community leaders on alternative dispute resolutions within the communities, so as to foster peaceful co-existence.8. Provision of 100 small projects for PSN activities for Micro-projects implementation can have a value of up to 2000 USD and must be identified, designed, partially implemented and monitored by the communities. Conduct awareness session campaigns on child protection, gender equality and prevention of child recruitment.Output 3. Protection and returnee monitoringThis activities will focus on protection services provision to vulnerable crisis-affected people, particularly those with special needs - such as women, youth, elderly, persons with disabilities and chronic illnesses - suffer disproportionately from various protection risks and face a range of challenges in accessing protection services as : Regular protection for vulnerable at risk monitoring visits to the communities/ camps of return in the target localities. , protection visits in a monthly basis and collect data on number of returns in the communities, and an analysis on the peaceful co-existence in the communities.
