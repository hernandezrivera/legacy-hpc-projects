Project Summary

Maintain and strengthen the system of data collection in the North West and South West regions of
Cameroon through IOM’s Displacement Tracking Matrix (DTM) to support the coordination of humanitarian activities and enhance communication on DTM activities. The DTM aims to provide reliable and up-to-date information on the numbers of displaced populations, displacement trends, reasons for displacement and multi-sectorial need assessment to better assist and inform the humanitarian response. The assessed groups consist of internally displaced persons and returnees. The data will be disaggregated by age and sex. The DTM has the potential to be expanded and/or modified to take into account changes in the regional security context.

Besoins / Needs

The humanitarian community is still facing challenges as to assess the magnitude of the displacements and related needs. IOM has conducted two comprehensive DTM rounds in the North West and South West regions as well as in neighbouring localities of the West and Littoral regions, which are also impacted by the crisis. This internal crisis has proven to trigger momentous dynamic displacements of populations. It is therefore crucial to systematically update the information on displacements of the affected populations and their needs. This would allow a better understanding of the displacement trends and the follow-up on the support displaced populations have received or not. The DTM is a tool that provides vital information in order to bring efficient and effective provision of humanitarian assistant to conflict-affected populations. It will also allow to understand the magnitude and dynamics of the displacements occuring in these areas. The newly launched Emergency Tracking Tool (ETT) will also enable to collect up-to-date timely information on striking incidents provoking displacements in the region, out of assessments periods.
