Project Summary

WFP’s programme focuses on preventing and Managing malnutrition amongst the most vulnerable populations through strengthening supplementary feeding with focus on the fi rst1000days. The project also targets People living with HIV:AIDS for prevention and treatment of acute malnutrition; The programme activities include: Screening for malnutrition and referral of severe cases for treatment, Social and behavioral change communication in favor of appropriate infant and young child feeding practices and other essential Family practices, distribution of specialized nutritious food to children aged 6-23months and PLHIV, promotion of the use of the blanket feeding platform for provision of complementary services including immunization, micro-nutrient supplementation, deworming, malaria and other childhood illnesses prevention, family planning, etc. WFP in 2025 intends to target about 73527 beneficiaries across the North, East, and Adamawa regions

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The results of the latest nutrition survey conducted in December 2022 by UNICEF, WFP, UNHCR and the Government of Cameroon revealed varying and worrying under 5 GAM and SAM prevalence across the 4 target regions of the Adamawa (6.6% GAM,1.3% SAM) East (3.9%GAM,1.1% SAM) North (6.9% GAM/2.6%SAM) and Far North (8% GAM, 1.7% SAM). Chronic malnutrition remains very worrying in the 4 regions. Further analysis of the prevalence of acute malnutrition in children under 5 years old among the off-site and on-site refugee populations in the Adamawa, East, and North regions of Cameroon reveals alarming rates. These rates vary between 10% in off-sites settings in the North and 17.4% in the Adamawa and East sites, exceeding the intervention threshold of 10% for off-site refugees from the Adamawa and East regions and the emergency threshold of 15% for onsite refugees in these two regions. All refugee populations, in the East, Adamawa and North regions, have rates above the emergency threshold for severe acute malnutrition. With regards to chronic malnutrition, all strata have rates above the emergency threshold of 40%. The nutritional situation of CAR refugees is therefore extremely preocupying. Further desegregation of Data from SMART survey reveals worrying wasting situation in children under 5 in the 2 municipalities of Mandjou and Garoua Bulai with GAM rates (9.0% and 8.8%) and SAM (1.9% and 3.2%) respectively. These rates are 2 times above regional average (3.9% / 1.1%), an indication that Mandjou and Garoua Boulai would constitute pockets of malnutrition in the East region. WFP intends to assist about 73527 beneficiaries with nutrition prevention and treatment services across the regions affected by the CAR crisis in 2025.
