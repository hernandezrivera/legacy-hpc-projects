Project Summary

SOP intending to continue its provision of integrated community-based protection interventions and provision of life-saving protection activities to conflict and earthquake-affected populations across Northwest Syria.
The Project aims to enhance the prevention and mitigation of protection risks, and respond to protection needs by providing GBV, and CP services to the most vulnerable PiN with a focus on Women, Girls, Boys, and youth(14-24) (including PwDs), prioritizing critical gaps in life-saving protection services, and designed to be people-centered project supports self-sustainable protective environment for the most vulnerable IDPs, in targeted Community and camps special widow camps and neighboring communities through dedicated outreach teams
SOP will Establish WGSS and CFS (in the same community center) centers will be in Dana, Ariha, Daret Azza, and Darkosh Subdistrict (accessible for PwDs)
SOP aims to provide the following activities
GBV (Structures and non-structured)
Case Management for the most vulnerable Women and Girls, case fund will be allocated on the budget to support the needs of the most critical cases as per Protection cluster and SCs relevant SOP and guidance
PSS activities structure and non-structured
Awareness-raising sessions structure and non-structured (cumulative interventions) other than legal awareness
Life Skills Sessions for Women and Girls
Life Skills Training for Women and Girls
Periodical Safety Audits in the camps by the outreach team
Establishment and support of community Women's network
CP
Case Management for Children, case fund will be allocated to the budget to support the needs of the most critical cases as per Protection cluster and SCs relevant SOP and guidance (Inter-Agency Guidelines for Case Management & Child Protection will be applied).
Structured PSS sessions for children and care cavers in camps and the CFS
Awareness-raising activities for children, parents, humanitarian workers, community members, and camps management on key child protection concerns, focusing on the following topics (Child Labour, FTR, Basic CP, Child Rights) Using UNICEF IEC materials
Center-Based and outreach parenting skills sessions, using IRC Parenting Skills Manual
PSS recreational activities for children
Gender sensitivity will be considered during the implementation of all activities, as the most significant percentage of girls and women, will be targeted, and ensure CWD accessibility in all CP-specific activities
PM
Protection monitoring surveys in coordination with PMTF
Each center will be acting as the primary portal to Share information and enroll/refer beneficiaries to the available services
Information/Data will be collected under each activity as per the SoP’s internal reporting mechanism while taking into consideration proper data gender age disaggregation and MoVs, Monthly figures will be shared with Clusters and SCs through 4Ws, Case Management tools, and other reporting channels
The project will also focus on educating the community about protection risks and PSEAH (Preventing Sexual Exploitation, Abuse, and Harassment) to promote positive behavioral changes within communities
