Project Summary

Gender-based violence is a major public health concern and a violation of human rights in Puntland regions particularly within IDPs, returnees and refugees’ settlements, and rural villages where education and sensitization on GBV is limited.
Despite many efforts to minimize gender Based violence, the cases go unabated. Increasingly it is becoming clear that more focus needs to be placed on prevention as a sustainable way of dealing with gender violence issues. We aim to increase the volume of voices for change from within the communities where we work in engaging women, girls, community leaders, men, and boys in the effort to support positive social change. The health consequences of GBV can be particularly serious. GBV Survivors may suffer sexually transmitted infections, reproductive injuries, fistula, and a wide range of other physical injuries - even death. Psychological, emotional and social consequences can be equally severe. Survivors may experience post-traumatic stress, depression, fear, or anxiety. And they often face social stigma and rejection by husbands and families.
For this reasons, PSA is proposing to implement a GBV prevention and response project in Bari and Nugal regions dubbed ‘’Enhancing and improving access to quality and comprehensive care for survivors of GBV among vulnerable displaced communities’’.
The proposed project is designed to support Internally Displaced Persons (IDPs) and host communities) in Bari and Nugaal through Provision of appropriate medical care to GBV survivors , Age appropriate psychosocial support to GBV survivors , Safe spaces that meet the minimum standards, recreational and livelihood skills, GBV/RH education sessions, Provide age appropriate and gender responsive legal aid support/Education and information, provide dignity kits (menstrual hygiene kits and solar lanterns), training of national actors (government officials, security personnel, NGOs ) and cluster field coordinators on GBV prevention and response including PSEA, strengthening existing referral pathways on protection , community Engagement and Advocacy, strengthened the existing complaint and feedback mechanism (toll free hotline and desk) and counseling desk, facilitate commemoration events (women’s day on 8th March and the annual 16-days of activism), community Dialogue Sessions , social media campaigns and developing and Printing of IEC Materials
