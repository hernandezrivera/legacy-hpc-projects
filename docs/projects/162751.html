Project Summary

The need to establish a strong community based system that monitor, refer and report abuses and violence against children is critically important. SHF funding will be used for core life-saving CP activities as per SHF definition and in line with SORR and State ministry of social welfare, CBOs and the community since there are the baseline of every succees project implementation strategy.
In particular Family Tracing and Reunification (FTR) activities will aim at strengthening existing State Focal Points and create linkages between those and the central system .At least new Child Protection Networks (CPNs) will be established since wadi salh locality previously was found out with the interagency assessment report that found out there is highly need for protection intervention in the locality , the children will will be supported through child rights, needs assessments, protection monitoring and referrals training will be conducted. Monitoring of grave violations of child rights will be implemented in wadi saleh since there was a crisis on September 2019 that in the affected area. This entails capacity building for field monitors, as well as MRM Working Groups specifically in the targeted locality . Psycho-social support through the creation/maintenance of Child Friendly Spaces (CFSs) in wadi- salih affected areas will also be foreseen. Within SHF fund, we will be able to deliver thinking and provide protective environment for women and children as most vulnerable, as well addressing GVB, and children rights to be protected from neglect abuse and exploitation and havening access to social services such, justice for children. The project design is focused on achieving purpose of the Fund in supporting activities and processes that enable diverse communities to come together through common goals and improving peaceful co-existence, building trust and confidence towards resilience.
An initial assessment on tensions and needs was undertaken in June 2019 and this was used as the basis for preliminary conflict sensitivity analysis for all these areas

6.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

Protection is a major component of this project. Sorr will ensure protection and GBV is well mainstreamed in each sector since the project itself is targeting protection and SORR having implemented Protection activities and still under going some community level activities related to protection is done. In addition, all activities implemented improve the wellbeing of women and children and contribute towards improving women's access to and control over assets created by the project or resources provided by each sector. In Sudan, women and girls are overburdened in domestic labor including preparation of food, fetching water, collecting fire wood, agricultural labor during planting and harvesting, marketing domestic supplies, looking after livestock, preparing flour mills and serving as the sole responsible for child care in the household. Also, women have almost no right to make decisions on the utilization and transfer of the major assets. This project has analyzed all the constraints that affect women and girls and developed strategies that ensure their active participation and benefits. Women will constitute 50% of the number of committees, and task forces and they will be empowered to have active voice in the decision making process during the planning, targeting, and implementation and monitoring of this project. The organization recognizes the importance of the human rights of host communities, returnees, IDPs and especially the most vulnerable groups such women, girls,abandoned children and minority groups. While implementing this project, SORR will make conscious and intentional efforts to avoid all situations that expose beneficiaries to risk, threat, violence and abuse of their rights, reaching out to populations who are unable to access the static facilities, thus ensuring that individuals afraid or unable to move to the static facilities are not denied assistance or services. This is particularly important for elderly and disabled beneficiaries who may be unable to walk the required distances to project sites. Besides the strategic choice of service locations, strategic choice of staff can also strengthen protection of beneficiaries. SORR will also be sure to promote peace sensitivity by ensuring that all community groups including IDPs, host community members, returnees, pastoralists, and varying tribal and ethnic groups have equal access to project services by selecting project sites in areas accessible to the different groups and hiring diverse staff. This minimizes the risk of conflict due to competition over social services. The possibility of discrimination by locally hired staff against beneficiaries who do not hold the same tribal or political affiliations mitigated by ensuring that program teams that consist of combinations of locally hired national staff representing all ethnic groups.

7.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will inform and interact with inter-agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including SEA and abuse complaints?

- SORR have been implementing lifesaving and resilience building actions in Protection sectors in west and central Darfur state, leveraging resources from donors including SHF, UNDP, UNAMID, , and addressing a coverage area of around 50,000 households. Synergies are identified at three levels. There is synergy with other projects in the proposed sectors as well as the different projects currently being implemented by agency members.Overall, the proposed actions will fill significant gaps in services existing in the target communities. Maximum precautions are taken to avoid duplication of efforts. Protection activities will be implemented within the same villages as protection activities funded elsewhere to enhance the impact of all actions. There are other INGOs and NNGO is operating in the same localities, but operate agreed to work in different villages since they work in different sectors but still there will be close collaboration for any needs or avoid duplication and enhance overall humanitarian service coverage. SORR has been actively involved in the sector coordination led by the respective UN agencies. They will continue its coordination with the line ministries and the clusters to ensure that NGOs are not duplicating resources, effort and geographic areas and to avoid competition over resources. The members participate in the inter-agency joint assessments, monitoring, and evaluation of projects and programs in Central Darfur.. SORR has a ‘one program’ approach which aims to consolidate the efforts of all sectors to bring synergy in achieving a broader goal. Additionally, SORR will effectively coordinate multi-sector programs with Humanitarian Aid Commission (HAC) at state and federal level to facilitate signing project agreements..
