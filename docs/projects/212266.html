Project Summary

The planned activities are intended to enable vulnerable IDPs and host communities in Northern Syria to survive in dignity and contribute to self- determination. Life-saving assistance will be provided through distribution of bread and food support (parcels and e-voucher). Importantly support by water trucking provides lifesaving drinking water to affected communities of water shortages and distribution of hygiene kits and vouchers will prevent outbreak of diseases. In NES, the water supply conditions of households affected by poor water quality and network services are improved by installation of water filters and piloting the upgrade of water station power supply by installation of solar panels.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Inclusion and safeguarding of vulnerable groups are essential during the before and throughout the project implementation. Both aspects are ensured through various measures: - Planning: Men, women, girls, boys, elderly, and PWD are involved in planning, and their feedback is collected to inform project evaluation. Their feedback is considered to take corrective action in a timely manner and ensure equal access. In the planning phase, especially focus group discussions, previous (recent) beneficiary feedback and baseline/ endline information are the most common tools to include beneficiary feedback. - Feedback mechanisms: The project utilizes feedback mechanisms throughout the project implementation (WhatsApp, E-Mail, complaint boxes), which are used by the beneficiaries to provide suggestions, complaints, and feedback. The target group is informed about these feedback mechanisms through information events and via info boards near distribution centers. The needs of girls/women and boys/men of different age groups with and without disabilities are considered via different measures:-Food distribution: vulnerable beneficiaries (elderly, PWD, women, women with children) are given priority. Food distribution may be done in multiple locations to make it easier for vulnerable beneficiaries to access.-Distribution of hygiene kits: The special needs of women, young girls, elderly people and people with disabilities are taken into account in the contents of the hygiene kits. Vulnerable beneficiaries (elderly, PWD, women, women with children) are given priority.-The construction of latrines will take into account the needs of vulnerable groups as much as possible, e.g., some latrines will be designed to be more spacious and with special fixtures for disabled and elderly people so that safe access and use is possible for all.-Paths to latrines will be paved, latrines will be equipped with handrails, lighting and ramps.-Distribution points and selected shops to be evaluated and small measures applied to account for age and inclusion needs to be safe and accessible, where possible.
