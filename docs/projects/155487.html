Needs

To date, and mainly due to the major access difficulties, the nutritional situation of displaced persons and host communities is poorly known, surveys to define the prevalence of malnutrition have not yet been implemented. However, these areas, and particularly the North West, are regularly affected by chronic malnutrition. The latest surveys showed rates of 28.1% (close to the alert threshold) of stunting for the South West and 36.1% (near the emergency threshold) for the Northwest. Pre-existing chronic malnutrition is an evidence of a context of multiple deprivation for girls and boys under 5 in these areas both in terms of access to health services, quality of water, and diversified nutritio. In the context of massive population displacement in the bush, these deprivations could increase and lead to increased acute malnutrition rates among the most vulnerable populations (children under 5 and pregnant or breastfeeding women). At present, more than 135,000 people are considered to be in need in both regions (one in four people in need in Cameroon), including more than 111,000 girls and boys under 5 and 24,000 pregnant or lactating women vulnerable.
