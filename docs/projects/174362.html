Project Summary

The proposed project aims to respond to the emergency floods through multipurpose cash assistance which can be utilized for food, NFIs and water needs of the communties. The multipurpose cash assistance will give affected community members the decision to prioritise and purchase what they need. The existing presence and experience of multi purpose cash will be an added advantage in reaching out to the communities for identification of participants, selection and distribution of cash in short span of time. Mercy Corps implemented cash distribution project to flood affectees in Dadu district, through START funding in partnership, as lead organization in consortium with HANDS and CWSA. The preferred means of distributing cash among affected communities will be online or mobile transfer. However, where mobile transfer is not possible, physical cash transfer through cheques for households with bank accounts will take place. COVID-19 preventive measures will be followed and awareness around COVID-19 and infection prevention will be part of the program. The activities will be implemented while following the COVID-19 preventive measures. Similarly, strong coordination will be maintained with local administration and organizations for identification of communities in need and to avoid duplication of activities.
