Project Summary

To reach the goal of satisfactory conditions of water, sanitation and hygiene for Rohingya refugees and host community, Green Hill (GH) will improve WASH facilities and interventions in camps 1W, 4 and 17 as well as in selected host communities under Cox’s Bazar District. The activities will address key issues by enhancing community engagement resulting in increased access to WASH services for sustainability of system through proper operations and maintenance.
According to the WASH Sector’s JRP strategy, GH will focus on rationalization, cost-efficiency, AGD mainstreaming and sustainable interventions through strengthening participatory approaches and capacity building for Rohingya refugees and host community people aiming to improve behavioral change. Gender responsive community mobilization will be reinforced to ensure participation, especially of women and girls, in decision-making related to design, feedback mechanisms, implementation and monitoring of essential WASH services. GH will prioritize meeting community needs for critical hygiene supplies, soap, water containers, and menstrual hygiene management material. In addition, GH will integrate clean energy solutions by installing and regular repair and maintenance of solar lights in and surrounding of the WASH facilities. These solar lights will also provide further protection at night to all beneficiaries accessing the WASH facilities including pregnant women, children, older people and persons with disability etc. All activities will be implemented with a focus on PSEA, accountability to affected populations, protection, age, gender and diversity, disaster risk management and environmental ecosystem with the concept of ‘do no harm’.
Since early 2020, GH has been implementing essential WASH projects in camp 1W, 17 and host communities. Building off this experience, this project seeks to ensure approximately 5,525 households, covering approximately 26,195 individuals in 4 blocks of camp 1W, 4, 17 and 2 unions in the Host community level with quality water supply, improved sanitation facilities and hygiene practices, improved waste & fecal sludge management in respect with Sphere WASH Standards and WASH Sector guidelines. GH prioritizes quality implementation, durable solutions and building resilience through community engagement. Moreover, emergency support for AWD, scabies, COVID-19, fire, flood, monsoon and cyclone will be carried out as per the need during the implementation of the project as planned.

1. Needs
1 - Identified Needs

Given the large number of refugees, it is critical for WASH actors to continue strengthening efforts to ensure quality, sufficient, equitable and dignified access to WASH services for all women, men, girls and boys, inclusive of all age groups and persons with disabilities. Water: According to WASH Sector assessment, 12% of the Rohingya population in FDMN camps reported not having enough water for their family needs. Besides this, as per the WASH sector monitoring report, 85% water sources are reported functional, moreover only 65% households have biological risk-free drinking water. Day by day, the underground water table is moving downwards which results in many tube-wells becoming non-functional especially during the dry season. Hand pumps are still a major source for drinking water, bathing and domestic use. With consideration of these factors, this project will prioritize quality water at the household level as the critical stages of the safe water chain. Sanitation: As per the WASH Sector strategy 2023, sanitation facilities functionality is satisfactory however to keep sanitation facilities functional through regular repair and maintenance is necessary. One bathing station is utilized by at least 51 FDMN, the shortage of bathing house is visible. Solid waste management is another mentionable challenge as only 51% of the households are disposing waste in designated areas. 27% households reported disposing waste near to their shelter. Moreover, proper waste and fecal sludge management is another concerning issue in this area; especially during the monsoon season. Hygiene Promotion and Community Engagement: According to WASH Sector Monitoring Report, Sept. 2022, 93% of the community have access to soap for hand washing. As per WASH Sector Strategy 2023, 66% of women and girl accessing menstrual hygiene materials, However, the scenario in the case of practice level is not satisfactory. Access to MHM facilities remain a barrier for many females due to stigma, taboo and conservative practices. In some instances, we have identified that IEC material regarding MHM is not contextualized. Communicable disease like as AWD and Dengue cases are also increasing at the camp level. Considering all of these important factors and gaps that remain to be addressed, GH will continue its current WASH program in three camps and host communities within camp boundaries with technical and financial support from Community Partners International (CPI). GH will contribute to operations and maintenance systems necessary to expand and ensure sustainability of WASH infrastructure. This will be done by ensuring community participation and engagement, leveraging the existing projects using a multi-level approach that seeks to identify and address problems at the individual, household, community, and environmental levels in a complementary way.

2. Rationalization
2 - How is your project aligned to the Rationalization Principles (in particular reference # 3 - Adopt an integrated approach, # 4 - Ensure predictability and continuity)?

The main goal of this WASH project is to improve public health, disease prevention and protection through improved water, sanitation and hygiene support to FDMN and host community population. Adoptive strategy and integrated approaches will be reflected in the project design and implementation. GH has been maintaining close coordination with the WASH, health, protection, education, site management and food security sectors for effective and inclusive WASH interventions. This project will continue the same camp level coordination mechanism like coordination with health sector regarding epidemiological disease control, coordination with shelter and SMS for effective collaboration and construction site selection, WASH interventions in schools will be ensure through coordination with education sector. This project is designed for both camp and host community population. Participation of the government stakeholders will be ensured from the planning phase of this project. CiC office’s will be engaged through the need assessment, approval, site selection for new WASH facilities, beneficiaries’ selection, quality monitoring, community feedback and project completion. This WASH project started in 2020 and we are planning to continue in upcoming years as well with keeping in consideration of continuity and stability. GH's major feature of this project is to ensure sustainable WASH intervention through strengthening community capacity and community engagement. Periodical meeting with different community groups will take place to ensure community accountability. Strong liaison with government stakeholders, WASH Focal, SMS and other WASH actors will be ensured for addressing service gap and to avoid service overlapping. To improve relations with government and other partners we ensure timely report submission, following the government rules and regulations and implementing any new instructions and guidelines provided by the government.

3. Cross-cutting Themes
3.1 - Protection

All four principles of protection will be maintained in this project. This project will cause no harm to the affected community. Community people’s safety and dignity will be the first consideration for any kind of WASH facility construction. Impartiality and equity will be addressed in any services delivery. All the community people will have equal access to the WASH services without discrimination. Non of the population will be left behind in water, sanitation and hygiene services and access. Special consideration will be given to older people. Periodical meeting with different community groups will take place in phases of the project management cycle. Strengthen community people’s capacity through capacity building training will be ensured to empower themselves. Moreover, All staffs and community WASH volunteers will have training and orientation on PSEA and GVB.

3.2 - Gender

The understanding of these differences, as well as inequalities in women and men roles and workloads, access to and control over resources, decision-making power and opportunities for skills development is achieved through gender analysis by this project. Green Hill aims at maintaining proportionality and impartiality in which attention must be paid to achieving fairness between women and men and ensuring equality of outcome. 3.3 - Age including child safeguarding

This project will ensure all the children are protected from any harm and will have equal access to WASH services. All age groups are been considered for developing this project. A larger portion of the community people are children, their effective participation in every step of the project cycle will be ensured. Children’s expectation and perception regarding WASH facility design will be reflected in this project. This project will ensure WASH facilities are designed with the safety of children in mind, include size of the toilet and by building walls and fences around open sources of water, tanks or wells. Older people are strongly considered in this project as well. Older people friendly WASH facilities will include access from the road to facility with railings, handy solar light, and WASH facility site selection will be done in close proximity to older people.

3.4 - Diversity

This inclusive WASH project is designed in consideration for all group of people (Women, girls, men, boys, older people and persons with disabilities). This project will ensure equitable and nondiscrimination WASH services to all gender, age and persons with disabilities. Community consultation with diversified groups is being ensured during planning and designing. Participation of the diversified group of people in implementation and monitoring will be ensured. This project plans is consider diversified WASH facility design to ensure access to persons with disability. Separate latrine will be constructed for women, men and older people. Hygiene session will be carried out with Women, men, children, adolescent groups. Bathing house with MHM chamber and MHM kits will be ensured for women and adolescent girl. Moreover, some of the WASH facilities will be upgraded as per diversified need.

3.5 - Accountability to Affected Populations (AAP)

This project is fully accountable to the affected people. This project will ensure all affected people have quality and equitable access to WASH facilities. Affected people’s participation and perception will be included in every step. Discussions with the community in identifying needs, perspectives, and priorities will take place. GH has established a complaint feedback mechanism to ensure community voices are heard. Moreover, community-based monitoring mechanism will be ensured for effective monitoring. Open discussion platform with affected people like majhi meeting, community consultation meeting will be conducted for better learning and effective intervention.

3.6 - Does your project have any DRR/DRM elements and/or environmental issues and/or climate change mitigation measures? If yes, please explain.

Yes, this project has DRR/environmental elements as disaster preparedness course of action. This project has emergency preparedness activities to reduce the WASH facility vulnerabilities. Site protection for the WASH facility, include disaster resilience feature, disinfection of the water point, tie down etc. Moreover, Green Hill is equipped with contingency stocks (jerry cans, aqua tabs, desludging machine, bamboo, bleaching powder etc.) to provide immediate support during and after the disaster. This project will not make any negative impact on the environment rather proper safety system for the toilet pits, a positive impact will be created. Green Hill will consider average flood level during the construction WASH facilities to mitigate the upcoming disaster risks.
