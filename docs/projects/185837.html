Project Summary

AMAN (Association for Mass Advancement Network) has developed the project with objectives to Percentage of refugee households reached with timely food assistance in case of a disaster;
The project location is Camp 10, 14, 15, 16, 17 & HC-Palong Khali with total 10 Community Promoter, Trainers and Teachers will be assigned with the project as its cross-cutting activities along with Health, WASH, Shelter, FSS and others project activities will be covered during 2022. Total budget for the project is US$ 455,012 to reach 48,723 individuals for one year of the project period. The proposed project will be implementing following sector sub sectors policy and guidance.
Major activities will be: Refugee households and Host community reached with timely food assistance in case of a disaster, Households receiving capacity building support through skills development and self-reliance activities, Refugees participating in food for assets/cash-for-work activities, Households implementing climate smart homestead gardening practices and climate sensitive self-reliance interventions.
AMAN has skilled manpower to run the assigned project. AMAN has necessary policy and guidelines those will be followed for implementing the project.

1 - Identified Needs

Protection principles into aid delivery, humanitarian actors in Cox’s Bazar ensure that activities target the most vulnerable individuals, enhance safety, dignity, and promote and protect the rights of refugees, encouraging inclusiveness without contributing to or perpetuating discrimination, abuse, violence, neglect, and exploitation. It is not a one-time activity but a continuous exercise, while intervention are designed, implemented and evaluated.JMSNA 2021 key finding that, 18% of households reporting that members in their community wanted to report a safety or security incident, or access protection services for other reasons, in the 12 months prior to data collection; 14% households having reported that members in their community wanted to access protection services, reported that these were not able to access the service they needed; 65% of households reporting unavailability of service/staff due to COVID-19 as the reason for not having been able to access protection services, among households having reported community; 56% of households reported needing protection services or support.To mainstream protection, actors need to understand who is at risk, from what or whom, and why, and the consequences their actions or inaction may have on the threats people experience and their vulnerability and capacity vis-à-vis these threats. When mainstreaming Protection into the program cycle, the following must be consideredin all humanitarian activities: Prioritize Safety and Dignity and Avoid Causing Harm, Meaningful Access, Accountability, Participation and Empowerment and Support the development of communities’ and individual capacities and assist people to claim their rights, including – not exclusively – the rights to shelter, food, water and sanitation, health, and education.The proposed project will contribute to conduct and compile comprehensive Protection Analysis that details the main protection threats, vulnerabilities, and coping strategies of the refugee population to inform decision and programming. Provide the technical lead and support in ensuring Protection mainstreaming is both regularly discussed at the ISCG meetings and put into practice. Efforts will be scaled up to integrate protection principles into the delivery of assistance and services so that Sectors and service providers can ensure response activities are inclusive, targeting the most vulnerable, enhance safety, dignity, and promote and protect the rights of the beneficiaries in line with the “DO NO HARM” principle and without inadvertently contributing to, or perpetuating discrimination, abuse, violence, neglect and exploitation.
