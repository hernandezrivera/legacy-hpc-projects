Project Summary

The project will take place in Idlib city-Salqin - Harim and Al-Dana districts – Idleb governate. Afrin and Azaz in Aleppo governate. Estimated number of beneficiaries: 21,005 (11747 formal education – 6093 non-formal education - 2676 ECE children - 489 schools’ staff). Estimated # of disability beneficiary: 2.5%
This project will support the educational activities in 14 schools in Idlib city, Salqin, Harim, and Al-Dana districts (camps) – Idleb governate and Afrin and Azaz in Aleppo governate by establishing 3 temporary Education Centers (TECs) and supporting 11 schools considering the access of children with special needs besides the cultural appropriateness of the context in order to ensure that girls have safe and equitable access providing children with the chance to continue their studies and providing access to quality formal and non-formal education is ensured by providing incentives and capacity building for teachers and education personnel. The project contributes to the Education Sector’s HRB objectives through:
1- Project will scale up safe and equitable access to formal and non-formal education in Idleb and Aleppo governate age (3- 17) by establishing and operating three temporary Education Centers and supporting another 11 schools and Conducting Back-to-schools campaigns in 5 communities and providing children with formal non-formal education, ECE and stationery.
2- Enhance the quality of formal and non-formal education for children and youth (aged 3- 17 years) within a protective environment by providing Stipends/incentives for teachers and education personnel, PSS activities and raising the capacity of teachers and education personnel. The project targeted the schools and centers through the following activities:
1- Establishing three temporary Education Centers (TECs) and rehabilitating 84 classrooms and rehabilitate facilities and sanitation services to access drinking water, and segregate toilets.
2- Providing 14 schools and centers with running cost, supplies, etc.
3- Providing 11747 students (5-17 years, 5175 boys and 6572 girls) with formal education and PSS activities and stationery – 9 months.
4- Providing 6093 students (5-17 years, 2866 boys and 3227 girls) with non-formal education (remedial classes) and PSS activities and stationery- 3 months.
5- Providing 2676 children (3-5 years, 1338 girls/1338 boys) with ECE- 3 months.
6- Providing 489 school staff (226 men, 263 women) with capacity building and incentives.
In case of spreading of Covid 19 pandemic and the suspension of work in schools, it will depend on blended education (education via WhatsApp and other electronic channels based on cluster guidance for COVID-19 response, in addition to visiting the school several times by week, considering the rules of social distancing, preventive and personal protection measures. WATAN had Personal Protection Equipment will distribute it on the targeted schools including masks, gloves etc. as a contribution from WATAN side in the project.
The project will increase the boys’ and girls’ beneficiaries by providing the activities of formal and non-formal education and both will access these activities for children and youth (3 – 17) The project will increase the boys’ and girls’ beneficiaries with disabilities by providing the centers and schools with the necessary tools and equipment’s to be appropriate with disabilities and training the staff on inclusion education.
WATAN (MEAL) Team will be responsible for developing a monitoring methodology will include the relevant quantitative and qualitative indicators to track project progress and different tools and steps: Collecting information and other relevant data. ii-Conducting observation checklists in the schools. iii-Collecting complaints and feedback. iv-Conducting evaluation surveys (interview-BNFs) v-Conducting monitoring interviews with the head of schools.
The project is not funded yet, and 20% of the budget there is potential to be funded.

4 - Needs

According to UNICEF,more than 2 million children were deprived of education in Syria within 10 years of conflict,2.4 million children are out of school, 40% of them are girls. Basing on the HNO 2021, an estimated 6.9 million people(97% children) need humanitarian education assistance, 94% of school-aged children are living in areas with sever, extreme or catastrophic education conditions mainly concentrated in Idleb.More than 5,300 teachers working voluntarily mainly in Idlib and rural Aleppo and 375 schools are non-supported schools (Edu Cluster).The COVID-19 pandemic has exacerbated the consequences and risks of systemic underinvestment in education service delivery and the consequences of this will continue to culminate in 2021.The delivery of education services faced many obstacles and the education system in Syria continued to face formidable challenges.The project is based on the needs of the targeted community through need assessments conducting by WATAN Team in 14 schools to increase access to education by the provision of formal education for 11747 children(5-17 years, 5175 boys&6572 girls)out of them 273 PWD and non-formal education for 6093 children(5-17 years, 2866 boys&3227 girls)out of them 142 PWD and enrollment 2676 children(3-5 years, 1338 girls/1338 boys)in ECE and conducting minor rehabilitating of classrooms, facilities, and sanitation in schools, and Improving the quality of education by the provision the schools with supplies, teaching, and learning materials; and incentives for 489 individual (220 female&269 Male) who are currently working on a voluntary basis; initial orientation sessions and training delivered to school staff about COVID 19, PSEA and PSS and referral mechanisms.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

1. Risk: School staff participates in punishment, bully, and neglect of students. Mitigation: all staff schools sign the code of conduct that ensures enrolling vulnerable girls and disabilities and including training to staff on Protection principles and Childe protection.2. Discrimination of PWD or IDPs in the project activities. Mitigation: WATAN will promote an inclusive education approach to include all the beneficiaries in the targeted schools, and wash facilities will be friendly and accessible for CWDs, and they will be provided with rehabilitation for disabilities (ramps, special seats for those children, etc.)3. Risk of sexual exploitation by humanitarian workers (risk of harassment) of women and girls • Mitigation: all staff school sign PSEA policies including training to staff and MEAL Dept will: 1- Pay a visit to schools and ensure the safety of children and staff. 2- All beneficiaries will be able to contact WATAN through complaint and suggestion email and phone number to follow up on all complaints. 3- All feedbacks from the community will be discussed in the monthly meeting.4. Risk: Delays in implementation due to local authority intervention in the project implementation. Mitigation: community engagement measures; and signed protocols with authorities on rights of children.5. Risk Schools’ attacks and occupation • Mitigation: all the targeted schools far away from operations military and will be provided with the evacuation plan including training all staff and students on it.

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

WATAN through conducting this project considers These main risks: 1- Air attacks and indirect shots Likelihood: middle Impact: High Mitigating measures Project location in a relatively safe area because of its closeness to the Turkish border, so the probability of safety threatens is low. For emergency cases, WATAN will provide all schools with safety and security tools to ensure the safety of schools and staff inside the project location. 2- Intervention by local authorities Likelihood: middle Impact: High Mitigating measures the Project will coordinate with two main entities: - Local authorities and other organizations: WATAN will sign an MoU with ED to clarify targeted schools, planned activities, and interventions and take the Authorities’ recommendations into account to achieve project target and community benefits.3- A lack of skilled teachers Likelihood: middle Impact: High Mitigating measures: Training teachers on formal and non-formal education curricula.4- Children dropping out of formal education to attend Non formal Education centers Likelihood: medium Impact: High Mitigating measures: Identify specific criteria to all implementing partners for accepting children into the program.5- Due to the COVID-19 outbreak in the targeted locations/schools the local authorities applied curfew in all governorates, WATAN has a plan B changing most of the activates methods to be online instead of the physical presence.
