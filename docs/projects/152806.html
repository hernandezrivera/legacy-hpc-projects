Project Summary

The purpose of this project is to provide displaced and affected vulnerable host communities with essential materials to ensure their protection and life with dignity through distribution of household and shelter materials. The project will target vulnerable displaced, returnees and affected vulnerable host communities. The Project will combine some Cash component of NFI and in Kind donation from NCA Contingency Stocks in Warrap and Wau funded from MFA -Norway funds. Parts of the funds from this Project will be used to support distribution costs for NCA Contingency items. Cash vouchers are also included as a conditional cash distributions where assessments carried out by NCA have shown strong markets, and access to markets to provide right holders a more flexible access to much needed NFI and shelter materials . NFI commodities will be distributed where markets are non functional such as in Baggari Corridor.

Needs

Needs and market assessments were carried out in Jur River and Baggari Corridors between June - October 2018. Markets assessments were carried out to determine the functioning of markets for shelter and essential household material and to determine capacity of markets to meet the supply needs for the projected demand for shelter materials as well as to understand and compare prices from different supply chain intervals. The household needs assessments were carried out by undertaking household surveys, interviewing key informants, carrying out general and disaggregated focused group discussions and was undertaken with Mobile data collection systems. In Baggari corridor only general focused group discussions were carried out as household interviews proved difficult to undertaken due to access constraints and since most people are scattered in the thick dense settlements far from main roads due to protection concerns. Baggari corridor has significant access constraints as there are limitations on where humanitarian organizations can go and often access is not granted due to bureaucratic impediments related to access approval and negotiations which often do not get granted. In Jur River, we carried out Key informant interviews, Focused group discussions and household interviews and market assessments. The fighting in Jur River caught many by surprise and families had to run for their lives with little or nothing to salvage from their house and fled with little or no belongings.Children are sleeping in the cold without anything to cover them and they are susceptible to URTI due to cold weather at night. The lack of cooking materials is affecting children as mothers have to take turns to cook food which takes long and often doesn’t allow food to be cooked fully. Lack of mosquito nets is exposing children and adults sleeping in the open to mosquitos and malaria attacks. The lack of basic household materials is rendering IDPs susceptible to diseases and at risk of harm. Additionally, many women have reported increased cases of SGBV due to sleeping out in the open. Being the wet season, grass which is the main material for the construction of roofs for traditional shelters has been unavailable and families are forced to live in structures without roofs or with relatives and well-wishers while others use classrooms disrupting studies in schools but also exposing young girls and women to SGBV. A number of agencies have carried out assessments in both Kuajieno and RochRoch Dong and eventually distributed a limited number of shelter and household materials in both Locations. For instance, in Barwol IDP camp and Nikijo in RocRoch Dong. ICRC and WFP have both distributed a limited number of household and shelter materials. These materials were provided to only a small number of pregnant and lactating mothers although there are many other equally vulnerable people who desperately needs assistance with Shelter and household materials and who did not receive. It is estimated that some 600 additional individuals need household and shelter materials in Barwol and another 370 in Nikijo in Jur River County. In Baggari, it is estimated that up to 2500 households are in need of shelter and basic household materials in 4 locations in Baggar, Ngodakala , farajalla and Besilia. Due to recent clashes and displacements, this figure may be higher. Additionally, extremely vulnerable individuals within the host communities such as elderly and child headed homes are also in dire need as do household hosting IDPs. Markets assessment was not carried out in Baggari as the markets are not functional. Information gathered from market traders in the markets in Jur River showed limited market functions and most materials are not available. The results of the market assessment indicated that the markets in both locations are not functional and therefore NFI distributions will need to be commodity based. Shelter and NFI needs and critical and urgent.

Activities

1. Community Consultations:- NCA and its partners will carry out a number of activities to achieve the outlined objectives, these includes Community consultations to identify the most vulnerable and needy individuals and households. During community consultations , we will also engage communities in developing accountability to affected populations , providing them with information in order to ensure their full particiapation in the planned activities.2. Registrations and verifications :- We will carry out registration and verification exercises to identify the most needy and vulnerable households and individuals. This verification will be undertaken in all locations in Warrap, Jur River and Baggari Corridor working closely with local leaders.3. Coordination:- NCA will continue to play an active role as SFP in Warrap , supporting agencies working in Warrap to coordinate their SNFI activities within Warrap. NCA will play an active role in leading assessments, facilitating cluster meetings and reporting in Warrap state SNFI activities to the national Cluster. We will maintain the NCA Warrap Focal Point to continue to coordinate the cluster acitvities in Warrap but also linking up with the Wau based teams. We will be active players with other partners in Wau and Jur River. 4. Contingency Stock: NCA keeps NFI contingency stocks help to supplement cluster supplies when the pipeline breaks or where pipeline supplies are not enough. NCA being a partnership organization has a unique approach to responding to emergencies by working with ecumenical actors. This means we are able to reach populations in hard to reach areas.5. Complaints handling :- Establishing communication and feedback mechanism with communities to explain to them the proposed programme as well as the targeted individuals and the rationale for the identification of the people to benefit from the project. NCA and partners will discuss with beneficiaries the manner in which the shelter and NFI distributions will be distributed Systems put in place with the input of the communities, the leaders and other stakeholders for community feedback and complaints systems. NCA will identify the most appropriate means for providing feedback and complaints.6. Distributions of NFIs :- NCA and our partners will carry out distribution of emergency shelter materials which will help displaced and vulnerable host communities to be protected from harsh weather elements and ensure that their health, physical wellbeing is protected. The planning of the distributions will take into account considerations such as safety, protection and GBV concerns of special interest groups such as of Women, girls, elderly , disabled, lactating and pregnant mothers and put in place systems to ensure that the special needs and circumstances of all people are taken into account when distributing the materials. Distributions will be planned in such a way that beneficiaries do not needs to travel long distances and that those who need help are assisted to carry the materials back to their homes safely.Additionally, through the distribution of essential household kits such as jerry cans, mosquito nets, blankets and kitchen sets, access to other basic services such as Food, nutrition and WASH and health support will be enhanced. The provision of Shelter NFI materials will also enhance protection of individuals and help improve their hygiene status and protection and dignity.7. Post Distribution monitoring :- After a minimum of 1 month after the distribution of the materials, NCA will visit the locations where the materials were distributed to carry out post distribution monitoring to interview beneficiaries on how they felt the materials distributed has helped to change their situation, perspective in quality and any feedback that they may have . Information received will be analyzed and used to inform other distributions in the area.

Feedback /complaint mechanisms?

Yes

Briefly describe the mechanisms

Since NCA works with and through partners, right holders will either file a complaint with partner or directly to NCA. Partners shall be required to inform NCA country or field offices immediately a complaint is received from a rights holder. All complaints shall be received by CRM focal point in NCA Country Office in Juba, NCA field offices in Torit and Kuajok. Non-Sensitive complaints shall be filed and handled in Juba, Torit and Kuajok field offices while sensitive complaints shall be registered, filed and forwarded to the NCA Head Office in Juba. Sensitive complaints shall be handled by Country Director and/or an Investigation Team constituted by the CRM Committee. Suggestion boxes and or registers shall be placed at strategic points for use by rights holders and stakeholders in lodging complaints. E-mail addresses and telephone numbers shall be included in partners’ contracts and shall be clearly visible in community and within partner office premises for easy access by rights holders and stakeholders. Other methods identified or outlined by communities can also be established in cases where email and telephone communication systems are not available. Once complaints are received, they will be registered and handled in a timely and appropriate manner that protects dignity and safety of the complainant. NCA shall acknowledge receipt of the complaint within 24 hours. CRM focal point shall convene a CRM Committee meeting in each office to review the complaint and take appropriate action including recommendation to NCA Country office in Juba to carry out investigations. In dealing with sensitive complaints such as allegations of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse and fraud and corruption, NCA CRM Committee shall refer to relevant sections of our Code of Conduct and institute an investigation in the matter. Findings of the investigations shall be forwarded to the CRM Committee who will then forward recommendations to Senior Management Team for further action and decision

Does the project monitoring measure access of beneficiaries?

Yes

How does the project monitoring measure access of beneficiaries?

When we develop our programme implementation plans we consider the time it takes to complete the most critical aspects of the project assuming that we will have reasonable access to sites and communities without hindrance. Sometimes , our visits need travel authorizations to allow us to visit those locations . We have developed benchmarks for turnaround times for obtaining Approval's from the various Government authorities. Any time an approval is not granted , this is recorded as an incident within our security management system. When our teams travel in the field and are refused to pass roadblocks to access our intended project areas, we also record this as an incident on the Simpson website . These tools help us to monitor access for beneficiaries.
