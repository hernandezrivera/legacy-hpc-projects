Project Summary

The project is secured .
This project support resilience at medium- and long-term at large scale for strategic wheat chain. Leading to the increase of the economical returns from this investment, leading to using the savings later by families (especially for children and women) for health, education, and their food supplies, to 500 vulnerable and small size farmers. Those 500 vulnerable he/she farmers from local communities or IDP’s including families headed by women and/or disabled, whom planted their land this season totaling 250 hectares in 2021/2022 season, will be selected to receive an agricultural basket which contain:75 kg for each farmer of N-fertilizer (applied at two phases, mid-February and March as recommended by FSL Cluster), cash voucher (50 $ /farmer) to harvest , will be distributed at the beginning of May; and irrigation voucher(100 $/farmer) (selected farmers must have source of water) will be distributed at the beginning of February to be applied whenever there is rainfall shortage to be applied at tillering, stem elongation, flowering or milky stage upon the recommendation of Agriculture Engineers Experts. The agricultural baskets will be distributed at 8 affected villages at Al Rouj Valley and surroundings in Idleb(SY070000), Armanaz(SY070305), Ma’aret Tmasreen(SY070005) and Mhambel(SY070502) sub-district (Google map attached with yellow pins for the villages) using the following criteria to construct the beneficiaries’ list:
-Households (HH) who relies on agriculture as a source of income and show interest in this project and
its instruction, with access to 5 du whom planted wheat seeds in 2021 with Sham 7, or 1, Bohuth 9 or 5 for durum and Sham 4, 8, or 10, Bohuth 6, 8,or 10 for soft wheat varieties under irrigated conditions according to WFP/SPEG/GOSM, and will harvest the crop in May/June 2022.
- The selected farmer must have source of water to receive irrigation voucher.
- HH must not receive any agriculture support from other NGOs for this year.
In addition he/she should achieve one or more of the following criteria:
-HH headed by females with access to land, (up to 20% of the total beneficiaries) will be selected.
-HH having a person or more with special need.
-Large household with more than 5 individuals in the family.
-Vulnerable local resident and IDP’s households whom were affected by this conflict and crisis.
• 400 Tons of wheat seeds will be purchased (after testing seed samples using Standard Operating Procedures before purchases) from local farmers whom were selected to receive this agriculture basket and will harvest their crop in June 2022 to support their wheat production and their income, hence support their resilience.
• 400 Tons of wheat seeds (75%soft and 25%durum) will be stored at ICO warehouses under controlled conditions, and later will be sent to mills to produce 300 tons of flour that will be baked to distribute 675,000 bread bundles (2250 bundle/ton) to 3850 affected and vulnerable IDP families in 20 camps in rural Ma'rat Tmasreen up to 4 months including families headed by women and/or disabled. Bread distribution criteria will be: 1 to 3 individuals will receive one bread bundle (0.5 kg), 4 to 6 individuals will receive two bundles, 7 to 9 will receive three bundles and more than 10 will receive four bundles.

4 - Needs

In October, 2021, (ICO) conducted rapid assessment with local council and KII at 8 villages at A Rouj valley .Main agricultural constraints for crop production was : high dependency on food aid, limited access to agriculture inputs, following some poor farming practices, adverse climatic conditions, high production costs and transportation costs as well as lack of quality inputs resulting in less cultivation of land. Neglecting using sterilized seed, cannot cover their operational cost due to the lack of resources; the high cost for seeds, pesticides; cultivation irrigation and harvesting cost, led to the reduction of the net return income to cover the next planting and cultivation cost. Due to the deteriorating of economic activities, COVID19 epidemic, and labor scarcities, led to most people in the areas of study to depend on agriculture as a main source of income. However, the high cost of production, caused the reduction in the economic return for, to pay off their loan, led to the reduction of the net return income to cover the next planting and cultivation cost. In addition to that most of farmers relies on borrowing to purchase agriculture supplies and wait until harvesting(HNO2021). On the other hand, on April 2021, ICO has conducted need assessment in 20 camps in three communities in Maaret Tamsrin 90% of breadwinner are males while10% are female from whom 88% are widowed.• 94 % of the IDPS stated that the most important needs for them is bread while 5% food basket.

5 - Implementation of activities

Advertise for the project , meet with local councils, and coordinate with stakeholders.prepare the beneficiaries list by surveying families in 8 villages, using the criteria to select beneficiaries, Advertise for tenders and receive quotations .Signing MOU with beneficiaries explaining clearly rules and regulations for the project. Prepare and print guidelines and brochures on the how to apply the project activities .Coordinate with local council, stakeholders and beneficiaries the time for each activity for each village .Receive, and distribute fertilizers to beneficiaries for each village according to beneficiaries list. Distribute irrigation voucher to all selected farmers .Organize frequent field trips to random farmers, giving advice, and documenting progress, at least once a week for each village .Project monitoring and evaluation across the target areas. Coordinate with farmers to purchase 400 tons of wheat seeds .Distribute harvesting vouchers to each selected farmer .Conduct survey for tracking IDP’s in the camps and update the database (baseline survey).Construct the final beneficiaries list . Contracting a mill to grind 400 tons of wheat seeds to make flour and transport it to the contracted bakery. Contract bakery to bake 3.4 tons of flour daily to bake good quality. Distribute 7670 bread bundles daily according to work plan (22 days / month) up to 4 months free of charge. Total 675000 bread bundles to be distributed. Project monitoring and evaluation . PDM

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk : some beneficiaries can't go to distribution center to receive their allocations . mitigation measure :INSAN team will hand over bread to the tents of disabled , sick and elderly people .Risk : conflict between beneficiaries and non beneficiaries of agricultural inputs . Mitigation measure :INSAN will prepare beneficiaries list based on vulnerability selecting criteria and will announce the criteria for the selection of beneficiaries to avoid any conflict may arise.Risk : women and children walk a long way to reach to distribution centers . Mitigation : Distribution centers will be near the camps to make getting to distribution centers easier , in addition to that distribution will be done during the day to avoid any harm may happen .Risk : Spread of COVID-19 pandemic among staff members Mitigation: INSAN team will use precautions measures to prevent exposure to COVID-19 and in case any confirmed cases among the team , INSAN will shift the activities to other staff until recovery of project staff .Risk : Arguments between employees and beneficiaries during distribution . Mitigation : INSAN has clear complaint mechanism . In addition to that , INSAN will monitor the behavior of the workers on site and make sure they are abiding with humanitarian principles and code of conducts .Risk of GBV :Distribution centers of bread will be well equipped to avoid any possibility of GBV , where there will be two queues during distribution one for men and one for women.


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Risk of SEA:mitigation measures:*Complaints mechanism in place for all people by WhatsApp and email which is directly connected to M&E manger in Turkey office, complaints box in the distribution center, and face to face complaints by M&E officers in the field *Distribution centers of bread will be well equipped to avoid any possibility of SEA. *SEA complaints will be dealt with urgency and confidential manner with referral cases to the focal point assigned by PSEA network in the targeted area, and the beneficiaries information will not be shared with any external party. *INSAN is a member in PSEA network and PSEA focal point will be responsible for monitoring the behaviors of workers in work sites, in addition to that posters and roll-ups include PSEA network email and phone numbers will be hung in project sites . *All banners and coupons will be in Arabic.*Capacity building of all staff in relation to protection and PSEA , by training them on PSEA . Bread *Distribution will take place during daytime and the distribution point will be located inside every camp to facilitate access of beneficiaries . *INSAN team will hand over bread to the tents of Disabled , sick and elderly people .*Female staff including ME team , distributors , agricultural engineers .....will be hired to prevent any exploitation to female beneficiaries .*INSAN has clear PSEA policy which will be explained to all staff .


11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

- Beneficiaries protesting for not receiving agriculture input assistant. Mitigation measures: ICO team will explain clearly the mechanism for selecting the beneficiary list, also we will rely on local authorities, civil society leaders to explain the criterion for selection and resolve any dispute. - Beneficiaries selling agriculture inputs to benefit from cash . Mitigation measures: ICO team and local authorities will explain goals for the project, and before distribution beneficiaries will sign an agreement not to sell.In addition, the distribution of agriculture inputs will be done over a period of time and not lump sum, and regular field visit by M & E team will reduce any irregular selling activity.- Crops planted by beneficiaries getting exposed to diseases and insects Mitigation measures: ICO team will visit planted fields periodically- The area gets exposed to security or military crisis Mitigation: The area that we will work the project at, is considered secure and no major security issues were encountered. area is under armed groups control since a long time and clashes have medium frequency to occur Mitigation: A coordination between the various groups is already in place.- Lack of local experienced staff Mitigation: Look for skilled expertise from outside the area of implementation, and give incentive such as housing or transportation fare- Lack of quality agriculture input and in the market to purchase Mitigation: Import agriculture input from Turkey.
