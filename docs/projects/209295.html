Project Summary

The general food security situation in Sinnar state has been affected in the last months [the ongoing armed war that recently broke out in Sudan. According to updated population table for the projection period: October 2023 – February 2024 that conducted by the IPC team, Sinnar State indicated that 411,277 (17%) people out of 2,403,581 are food insecure (phase 3 and above)
 Part of this large number of displaced people settled in gathering points in the state, and part of them with their families from the local community due to the lack of camps for the displaced in the state, as there are only gathering points distributed at the state’s local level (there are 21 gathering points) according to reports from the Sudanese Red Crescent Society.
 The ongoing armed clashes in Khartoum State have negatively affected the current situation of food security in the state, especially access to food.
The project aims to enhance food security and livelihood of (19,858) farmers and agropastoralists (IDPs, Host community and non-displaced) through the availability of agricultural inputs and animal health services, thereby improving the agricultural production and productivity and minimizing livestock losses resulting from diseases and ensuring food and nutrition security for vulnerable pastoral communities. The initiative will primarily focus on four localities of Sinnar State namely Eldindir, Abu hujar, Sinja and, Elsuki localities. The purpose is to restore and improve the food and nutrition security of 3972 vulnerable pastoral household (19,858) individual) through provision of agricultural inputs, veterinary medicines and vaccines and providing the appropriate capacity building. Special attention shall be given for women-headed households and people with disabilities.

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

The project staff will do their best to ensure that their activities target the most vulnerable, enhance safety, dignity, and promote and protect the human rights of the beneficiaries without contributing to or perpetuating discrimination, abuse, violence, neglect and exploit. Criteria will be determined to ensure that the beneficiaries selected without any bias. The protection could be mainstreamed by including protection principles in the general project management and as core principles in delivering humanitarian aid. Moreover, it could be mainstreamed by incorporating protection principles into project cycle management through protection mainstreaming indicators for the sector. The Organization as a humanitarian actor shares an ethical responsibility for mainstreaming protection across the humanitarian response. The Project staff would be responsible for ensuring that activities within their respective sector and to be carried out with a “protection lens”, and in particular for ensuring that their activities integrate protection principles. The organization will work closely with the Protection's Sector Lead to ensure that the staff has supported with the suitable protection tools and packages.
