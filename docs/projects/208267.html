Project Summary

The persistence of the ongoing conflict in the North West and South west regions of Cameroon over the years, has continuously deteriorated the security context and protection environment in these regions. The most recurrent protection incidents are property destruction, killings, physical assaults, unlawful arrests and detention, abductions, forced recruitment, and Gender-based Violence (GBV). This breakdown in the socio-economic tissue has created gaps in the mitigation/prevention of GBV cases, families have fled their habitual residents leading to an increase in displaced persons and separated families. An increase in the number of persons without civil documentation either through lose/destruction or inaccessibility to institutions responsible for the establishment of these documents, especially young children (boys and girls). This has led to an increase in unlawful arrest and detention. Due to the destruction of property/livelihood and the persistent lockdowns, families have lost their sources of income and they are unable to afford their basic necessities especially for young children and survivors of GBV (Hygiene and MHM kits).
This Project is aimed at improving and strengthening the protective environment and support services for children and adolescents (girls and boys) in crisis-affected communities of the North-West (NW) Region of Cameroon. Children and adolescents especially girls will have increased knowledge, skills, wellbeing, positive coping strategies, and psychosocial support through the establishment of Child Friendly Spaces (CFS), strengthening of community-based systems and provision of specialized services to at-risk children and adolescents- including survivors of Sexual Gender-Base. The project will target 25 communities- including hard to reach areas, targeting a total of 2,582 people (1,571 females, 1011 males) direct beneficiaries.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The NW region of Cameroon has experienced increased violence and insecurity since the beginning of the crisis in 2016. The situation remains highly volatile, and as fighting continues between SSF and NSAGS, the protection environment in the NW continues to deteriorate. About 2 million people have been affected and about 1.7 million are in need of urgent humanitarian assistance within the NWSW regions (OCHA Sit Rep, Aug 2023). This includes over 638,421IDPs and 442,580returnees within NWSW. Protection is a priority concern in the NWSW region as civilians continue to be arrested, assaulted, abducted and denied access to basic services. According to the MSNA-R9 statistics of the protection cluster, about 6162 protection cases have been reported in 2023 alone. Amongst the protection incidents, we have an increase in Human rights violations, perpetrated against civilians (physical assault and harm), an increase in GBV (1121 GBV incidents reported from January to June 2023 across 3 regions). The humanitarian situation currently indicates that people in these areas have limited access to health care and there is a collapse of essential services and infrastructure. Children and adolescents continue to be one of the most affected groups by the crisis and schools have become a direct target, with attacks on education facilities and personnel, kidnapping of students, burning of schools and military occupation of school facilities. Consequently, children and adolescents are at high risk of violence and have significant psychosocial needs. Amongst some of the major risks to children is the recruitment and use by armed groups, sexual violence, harassment, child early and forced marriage, as well as child labour and sexual exploitation (OCHA HRP 2022). Within NWSW region in the last year, 264 unaccompanied children and 1,203 separated children were reported, though it is assumed that the numbers are much higher as family separation is a major threat in this crisis. Separation is a direct consequence of attacks on villages forcing family members to scatter, but also as families seek economic opportunities in other areas/ regions, or because of children being sent to urban centres to continue their education (OCHA HNO 2022). The results of the RNA of Plan Cameroon, showed a prevalence of UASC, higher in the NW region (96% compared to 85% in the SW), more girls than boys have been separated from their caregivers in the NW (44%) compared to the SW (23%), and that children aged 5-14 were most exposed to risk and violence in the community with girls being more exposed. This project will address the identified needs through the implantation of the following activities.1. General protection;- Risk and service mapping in targeted communities - Sensitization campaigns and dissemination of tools with SGBV referral pathways- Identification of protection needs for SGBV survivors. - Case management and referrals- Medical assistance to SGBV survivors.- Provide PFA and PSS to at risk/survivors of SGBV.2. Child Protection:- Provision of Medical and nutritional assistance to identified cases- Provide support of food items to identified cases. - Distribute NFI’s (Blankets, bedsheets, cups, matrasses etc) to identified UASC- Distribute hygiene and MHM kits.- Establish birth certificates to 600 children- Creation and rehabilitation of 10 child friendly spaces- Provide linkage of UASC to foster families- Creation of community base child protection mechanisms (CBCPM)- Distribute starter kits to identified UASC.- Distribute reunification kit to unified families. - Community sensitization on family re-unification
