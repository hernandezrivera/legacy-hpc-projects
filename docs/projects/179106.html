Project Summary

Silk Road proposes 9 months direct ERL response to continue and expand its humanitarian services to the vulnerable population in different communities in NWS.
This project will provide and improve the vocational training and supporting women and youth-led community projects, also to empower the capacities and abilities of the youth ages 18-30 years for both gender with a deep focus on IDPs in Aleppo, Idlep and Ar-Raaqa Governorates.
ERL packages will work to help vulnerable people to overcome the negative economic and social results left by the spread of the Covid 19 besides the Syrian crisis through the provision of short-term labour opportunities to skilled and unskilled workers.
Silk Road will strive to target all affected people applying aligned and reliable selection criteria to benefit from the ERL activities in addition to the priorities that will be given for IDPs, vulnerable groups such as the elderly, people with disabilities, and women taking into consideration the women and children exposed to violence based on Gender (Cash for work to protect them from the Exploitation).
Silk Road will promote the active participation of women, girls, and other at-risk groups in all ERL assessment processes (Especially assessments focusing on the satisfaction and accountability to the beneficiaries besides the regular post monitoring distribution for all activities). We aim to support the beneficiaries of this project to build, plan, and manage their future in a realistic environment that helps them to be more involved in the community by providing them with a self-employment package of services and raising their self-confidence.
Supporting men, women and youth-led community projects activity will take place after each vocational training course as a course requirement to have applied what they have learned during the Life Skills course. Following each life skills course, Silk Road will request from beneficiaries to integrate themselves in small volunteer separate groups which will implement a community initiative in the targeted locations. Furthermore, ERL activities will not be limited to only CFW activities, vocational training or life skills courses, but we have plans to extend the ERL activities to include:
1- Rehabilitation or repair of basic, local economic infrastructure
2- Removal of debris and waste
3- Rehabilitation of education facilities
4- Rehabilitation of health facilities
5- Rehabilitation of access to basic utilities (electricity, gas, water, sewage)
6- Basic housing repair support
7- Community initiatives support
8- Producing reusable masks to protecting people from COVID19.
Silk Road will train and equip men, women and youth volunteer or through the Cash-for-Work activity ( Slight support) to provide temporary employment for public projects to the most vulnerable segments of a population targeted through this project. Furthermore, the training will include best practice for each speci􀂦c training subject, and explain what safety risks they may face in their work and how to mitigate them (This will be explained during the vocational courses and before implementing community projects activity). One of the ultimate goals of this project is to provide the targeted beneficiaries with the tools and resources to work towards self-empowerment and sustainability by providing each trainee with, Knowledge, expertise and full equipment kit to enable him/her strongly enter the labour market.
Silk Road expect that the local community will facilitate project activities and from our side, we will motivate them to implement similar projects and activities, attract their attention to the importance of vocational training and involving them in counselling as well as we strive to support trainees and providing favourable conditions for them and encourage them to ensure the success of the project depending on the extent of their interaction, commitment to the program, and seriousness and commitment of qualified trainers.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk 1-Security issues related to cash transfer. Mitigations measures: 1- The frequency of payments will be weekly or monthly (This will be agreed with women and men).2- Payment mechanism: The delivery instrument that will be used to make the payment is a direct cash distribution (We will encourage all women and men before signing the agreement to open a PTT account where they can receive all payments on their accounts put if it is too hard or inapplicable then we will deliver it in direct cash in the workshop.3- Payments must be made in the presence of the workshop supervisor and one of the monitoring and evaluation team.4- The safety of the beneficiaries must be taken into account when delivering the payments, and it is not permissible to deliver any payment if the safety is not ensured. Ensure that the instructions mentioned in this regard are applied. If any women or man want to change the way of delivering the payment or the place, it should be through a written letter to the management of the organization.5- The hotline will be activated with the direct administration in Turkey in order to receive any complaint, feedback or note. Risk 2 - PSEA / Child labor / HLP / Aid diversion risks. Silk Road will ensure that her activities will target the most vulnerable, enhance safety, dignity, and promote and protect the human rights of the beneficiaries without contributing to or perpetuate discrimination, abuse, violence, neglect, and exploitation. All staff have signed up to and been trained in a code of conduct. Silk Will inform child protection actors immediately about observe any protection concerns for children or PSEA / Child labour etc.

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk 1-Increased armed conflict due to increased tensions between different groups. This could delay, pause, or suspend programming. Mitigations measures: 1.Consider conflict patterns and contextual analysis as part of decisions for establishing a fixed site or mobile service provision. 2. Engage with diverse members of the community on safety and security in the community 3. If this reaches Silk Road project towns, they will pause activities and wait until the situation on the ground becomes safer. Risk 2-inflation,especially the cost of fuel and construction materials, may result in higher than planned prices. If prices spike too high, then SRO may not have the budget to complete the project.Mitigations measures: 1. Conduct a strong and constant market monitoring before starting any local procurement 2. Keep the prices of the market monitoring with short and periodic frequency 3. Explore alternative supply lines through local procurement 4. Provide logistics capacity 5. All contracts will be in USD Risk 3-Market System Disruption (due to changes in con􀂧ict dynamics, border closures interrupting supply chains, etc.). Mitigations measures: 1. Include the ability to ensure procurement as a criteria in the contractor's bid analysis. 2. Conduct a strong and constant market monitoring before starting any local procurement to have early warning of potential lack of supplies, and also to map out alternative supply. Risk 4-Exploitation or abuse by staff members/service providers when accessing assistance and services. Mitigations measures: 1. Communicate regularly and clearly to the community about what services are available and for whom they are intended 2. Reinforce the message that humanitarian services are FREE OF CHARGE
