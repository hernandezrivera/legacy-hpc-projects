Project Summary

Protracted Political conflict in South Sudan has rendered many populations in dire need of humanitarian assistance. Since the Break out of violence in 2013, mass displacements of populations has been witnessed internally raising IDP camps across the country. Many other people have crossed borders to the neighboring countries.
The conflict has seen mass destruction of the basic infrastructure and WASH facilities have not been left out.
There has been lack of essential wash facilities like latrines and water points leading to people resorting to taking untreated and contaminated water from the rivers and earth dams. Open defecation due to lack of latrines has seen the existing water points being contaminated with human waste. This has seen the rise of waterborne disease outbreaks with the latest outbreak of cholera being reported in April 2017 according to WHO situational analysis of April 2017.
Gender based violence and cases of sexual abuse have been reported in Budi County and can be attributed to lack of latrine facilities. Women and Girls tend to relieve themselves in the bushes therefore exposing them to danger of gender based violence and sexual abuse.
Health Facilities and nutrition sites in Budi County lack clean water for drinking and for use. Mothers of the malnourished children use unclean containers to draw unclean water for use. This has increased the disease burden to the already vulnerable populations.
This Project will ensure that the latrines are rehabilitated and others are constructed to ensure that the water points are not contaminated and therefore reduce the cases of water borne disease outbreaks.
The project will also ensure that latrines are segregated and that they have locks to ensure that women and school going children are safe as they use the facilities.
Women and school going children will be supported with menstrual hygiene kits to ensure that their dignity is well preserved. School going children who miss school and fail to participate in recreational activities during the menstrual days will be able to interact freely with other people without fear and will also be able to attend school without fear.
The project will also ensure that there is provision of clean drinking water for use to the required standards of 15 liters per day per person by ensuring that new boreholes are dug, and others are rehabilitated to meet the needs of the community.
Water management committees will be formed to ensure that they take ownership of the water points and ensure that the water points are secure and are not contaminated.
Community engagement will be done to ensure that the beneficiaries identify the needs and that they are involved in the implementation of the project.

Needs

Budi County experienced a cholera outbreak which according to WHO was one of the longest outbreaks in the county in 2017. This was due to the fact that open defecation is practiced by over 90% of the population according to multi agency assessment done on 2016. This therefore calls for urgent rehabilitation and construction of latrines.Knowledge on good WASH practices is still very low in the communities of Budi County. This project will create awareness through mass campaigns which will improve knowledge on good hygiene and sanitation practices.There are cases of GBV and sexual abuse to women and girls. This is linked to the fact that women and girls relieve themselves in the buses becoming vulnerable to GBV and sexual abuse. The project will ensure that the latrines constructed and rehabilitated have locks and are safe to be used by women and girls. Health Facilities and nutrition in Budi county lack clean water for drinking and for use by the patients and children admitted with malnutrition. This has increased the disease burden to the already vulnerable populations. This project will ensure that the health facilities and the nutrition centers have access to lean and safe water for drinking by rehabilitation and construction of water points within the health facilities and nutrition centers.Women and school going children lack sanitary pads and are forced to use unhygienic method to manage menses. Girls miss classes during this period and are unable to participate in recreational activities due to this problem, this project will ensure that menstrual hygiene kits are distributed to the girls and mothers to enable them reclaim their dignity and freedom.

Activities

1. Distribution of hygiene KitsHygiene kits will be distributed to women to enable them store water well and in hygienic conditions. It will also help the beneficiaries treat their water well and reduce cases of water borne infections and disease outbreaks2. Construction and rehabilitation of water pointsThe project will construct new water points to help alleviate water shortage and meet the required standards of 15 liters per day per person. existing water points that are broken down will be rehabilitated to also help meet the needs of the vulnerable populations.3. Construction and Rehabilitation of latrines.In order to reduce cases of open defecation which leads to water contamination hence waterborne diseases, there will be construction of latrines in schools and in the community. health facilities too will benefit fro this initiative as those without latrines will have them constructed and those with broken down latrines will have them rehabilitated. This will greatly help reduce disease outbreaks and also reduce cases of gender based violence and sexual abuse.4. Distribution of menstrual hygiene kitsThis will help reduce cases of absenteeism among school going girls and also allow them participate in co curriculum activities without fear. It will also help restore dignity of the mothers and the girls.5. Training health workers on minimum WASH package activitiesHealth workers will be trained on the WASH activities in order to pass the key WASH messages to the patients they are seeing and reduce cases of WASH related illnesses.
