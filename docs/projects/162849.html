Project Summary

Kassala is one of the most vulnerable states in Sudan to extreme weather phenomena, droughts and floods in particular. Long periods of drought cause loss of crop and livestock, reduce the amount of groundwater, wear down forests and increase the risk of wild fires.
The consequences on the livelihood of the local population are disastrous and add to the already precarious living conditions.
Kassala region is one of the poorest and most marginalized in the country despite the good potential for agriculture and livestock and is location nearby important commercial hubs.
Kassala bases its economy on agriculture and pastoralism.
The recurrent and prolonged periods of drought, accompanied by sporadic torrential rains that cause flooding and destruction of the vegetation cover, increasingly deteriorate the subsoil, reducing its capacity to absorb water.
In addition, the quality of the soil has been ruined by the mesquite, a plant introduced in the '80s for its fertilizing properties.
The local diet is very poor (consisting mainly of sorghum and vegetables) and the parcels of land are too small to meet the needs of families. Communities therefore depend on the purchase of food at the market, where prices are extremely volatile and rise dramatically as a result of droughts or floods (which cause the loss of crops and livestock with a consequent reduction in the supply of products on the market). This is aggravated further by the devaluation of the local currency.

1 - Needs

Following field visits and meetings with the institutional representatives of the Ministry of Health and Social Welfare, Ministry of Production and Economic Resources, SDWC and Ministry of Animal Resources and Fisheries (Ministry of Animal Resources and Fisheries), HACs and local communities (displaced and resident), the following needs of the population of the target locations of the proposal have been identified: 1. Promote the adoption of environmentally friendly practices through awareness campaigns on the sustainable management of natural resources, with a view to increasing the resilience of local populations and reducing risks and disasters. 2. Need to improve and diversify agricultural production through: - Distributions of seeds and agricultural tools; - Livestock feed distributions; - Training on new sustainable agricultural techniques. 3. Need to improve breeding through: - Livestock supply; - Improvement of veterinary services

2 - Activities / Outputs

Result 1: FSL and resilience A1.1 Implementation of actions to strengthen agricultural activity: supply of agricultural tools, cereal seeds, vegetables and fruit trees A1.2 Implementation of interventions to strengthen pastoral activity: large-scale vaccination of livestock (goats and sheep) A1.3 Implementation of reforestation interventions, through the creation of nurseries and fodder banks, environmental education and mitigation of the effects of climate change

5.1 - Impact on environment/natural resources

The project livelihood component specifically the livestock health services related activities (vaccination & treatment) have a potential negative environmental impact unless proper waste disposal measures are taken. Accordingly, the proposed project recognize the risk and put in place proper handling of wastes during and after the planned vaccination and treatment campaign.

6 - Protection mainstreaming

3=Yes, room for improvement

6.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

COOPI will work with the local authorities and the beneficiary representatives to ensure that distribution sites are safe for beneficiaries; adequate facilities such as toilets and shade will be in place to protect people’s well-being and dignity. Activities will be organized to minimize waiting time and taking into account the distances beneficiaries need to travel.COOPI will work with community leaders to put in place relevant measures to prevent and protect against sexual exploitation and abuse by staff or community members. This will be ensured through sensitization, close monitoring and enforcement of staff rules and regulation.

7 - Accountability to Affected People and Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse
7.1 - How will the project share information and collect community feedback and use it to improve your activities? Please select as many as relevant.

Face to face with community leaders with aid workers

7.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will inform and interact with inter-agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including SEA and abuse complaints?

COOPI and partners will create awareness to the affected population on their rights and potential risks of safeguarding concerns. In addition, all gender and age groups within the affected population will be sensitized on the need and channels for reporting safeguarding concerns. The project partners will position themselves as willing and ready to welcome concerns from beneficiaries through regular interaction and soliciting for feedback. Key project staff involved in the project and in contact with the affected populations will be sensitized on their obligations in ensuring safeguarding of the beneficiaries.
