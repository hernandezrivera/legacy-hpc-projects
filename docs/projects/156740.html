Project Summary

The proposed project’s WASH interventions seek to improve the humanitarian situation for the war affected population by restoring water and sanitation systems that will be able to respond to the increasing demand in the overburdened communities as a result of the continuous arrival of tens of thousands of forcefully displaced people because of warfare. The action will be combined with emergency response WASH activities to the affected population in Idleb and Aleppo. Directly 197,146 people will benefit, indirectly. coordination with local councils, camp management, and other actors is the first step for all outputs. Through the proposed project IhsanRD will produce the following outputs:
• Rehabilitation four water systems with operation and maintenance for three months with gradually activation for cost recovery system: the rehabilitation includes provision and installation of submersible and horizontal pump set pump sets, generator sets, chlorine dosing pumps, with all valves and other accessories. Also, the rehabilitation includes Construction of water station building that included (generator room, pumping room, worker room, and groundwater tank and repairing the water network. 152,146 BNFs will be served directly.
•Rehabilitation two sewage systems: the rehabilitation includes excavation, extension concrete pipes, reinforced concrete for manholes, and cleaning 35 manholes. 21450 BNFs will be served by this activity.
• Water trucking and solid waste collections for IDPs in 45 informal settlements in Idleb and Aleppo governorates.
1125 m3 of safe drinking and chlorinated water to 45000 BNFs for 6 months in the average of 25l/day/BNF will provide to most vulnerable IDPs by trucks, including daily water quality control in the water source, trucks, and household level.
- Daily removing 25 trips of 4m3 of solid waste for 6 months to serve 45000 BNFs.
• Hygiene kits to most vulnerable IDPs. 9,000 hygiene kits will be distributed to most vulnerable IDPs in Idleb and Aleppo governorates. the selection criteria include women or child-headed households, disables, more than 5 individuals per family, who did not receive hygiene kit recently. verification will be done, and people who meet the criteria will be listed in the final distribution list. Within the distribution, two separate queues for men and women in distribution points and there will be chairs for elderly people.
PDM report will be conducted after distribution to measure the level of satisfaction.

Needs

Access to the WASH service is a major barrier, among many others, for newly displaced and most vulnerable people. Moreover, the lack of clean water, storage containers, and hygiene items potentially increase risks of possible outbreaks of water-borne diseases. An initial desk study conducted by IhsanRD of available resources found the proposed areas of intervention to be in critical need of WASH interventions. Currently, water systems are inoperable due to improper operation, absent of maintenance, looting of equipment and facilities, and lack of management systems (water utility) to ensure the sustainable operation of the system. Deterioration of the infrastructure and equipment in addition to the overall effects of the crisis and sanctions also feeds into the deterioration of the infrastructure. According to IhsanRD’s recent infrastructure study, (April 2017), Al Mar’a District requires 53% of its infrastructure to be restored. Based on HNO 2019, Based on WASH assessments, there are an estimated 15.5 million people who require WASH assistance people who are at elevated risk of consumption of unsafe water, including people vulnerable due to displacement, 6.2 million of whom are considered in acute need (displaced people in camps, informal settlements, and host communities, also the findings also indicate that the waterchlorination level remains insufficient in many areas and that many people are still forced to complement water received from the network or to solely rely on alternative sources. Unregulated water operators that are still meeting part of the water demand, often with poor quality water, seriously increase the risk of contracting a waterborne disease.Furthermore, all of the targeted communities are ranking 4 to 6 out of 6 in terms of the water-need severity scale. This data is further bolstered by direct requests from the local communities and their local councils in Dana, Ariha, Basqala, Armanaz, Ehsem, Saraqab, Albab and Az’az which have highlighted that the proposed water and sanitation systems are in fact in dire need of rehabilitation.The suggested communities for these interventions are currently depending on water trucking, with no sustainable source of water in place. Ensuring access to safe and clean water is key, as several reports of water-borne diseases have been surfacing throughout different areas in Syria.Additionally, sanitation systems in the proposed communities are gradually deteriorating due to damage and lack of maintenance, although the bulk of the water is being discharged into drainage networks. However, these networks suffer from partial destruction as a result of exposure to the bombing which in consequence made sewage water leaking through cracks underground, which affects the potable groundwater resources. The percentage of damages in the sewage network for AlMara and Azaz is 35%. Especially children suffer from the war physically and mentally. The project is designed to respond to the needs the hosed people and IDPs especially recently IDPs which came from Idleb, Aleppo, HOMS, and rural Damascus who stayed on target areas by delivering humanitarian WASH supplies and services to most vulnerable people. men, women, boys, and girls will be served from the activities which aim to increase access to sustainable water, sanitation, and environmental hygiene solutions, and the WASH gap component and made a deep needs Assessment the findings from the need assessment showed that the WASH service gaps will be:1- Drinkable water services. 2- Solid waste management.3- distribution hygiene Kits.

Implementation of activities

This Project has been developed in coordination with Cluster leads/ WASH and THF and is in line with the allocation priorities of a focus on reducing WBD by improving access to water in Basqala,Albab and Az’az through Restoring water stations and distribution networks (Repair/replace/extend water distribution network and ensure wide connection);Provision of supplies and materials (fuels, water disinfectant (chlorine), oil and spare parts) necessary for systems operation; and Capacity development of water utility unit through staff training on operation and maintenance and the establishment of cost recovery system to ensure self-operating. High prevalence of water-borne diseases (WBD) such as acute diarrhea, acute bloody diarrhea, and Typhoid fever has been observed (3,500 cases reported in 2018). ALMara,Azaz and Al-Bab sub-districts have been within the top four sub-districts of waterborne disease monthly hotspot list for the last year and a half (EWARN WBD epi reports). * Sewage system rehabilitation:- Confirm the BOQs and drawings through field visit to achieve last technical assessment for sewage system before - Starting the procurement and contracting phase for excavation, provision, and installations the pipelines, and backfilling the pipes.- Installation phase for signed contracts.* Water trucking and solid waste collections for IDPs.- contracting phase with water tankers and solid waste collection trucks.- providing water for IDPs in the daily average of 25l/day/BNF, in the total of 1125 m3 to 45000 BNFs for 6 months- Daily removing of 25 trips of solid waste for 6 months to serve 45,000 BNFs. * Hygiene kits to most vulnerable IDPs.- Beneficiary selection and verification.- Procurement phase.- Distribution phase. Monitoring and closure- monthly monitoring report will be conducted through all activities.- water system and sewage system will be handed over to LCs.

Protection Risk Analysis

IhsanRD is committed to mainstreaming protection into all of the projects and does so through the use of several mechanisms. These mechanisms include the creation of feedback and complaint mechanisms for projects, capacity building of all staff in relation to protection and mainstreaming protection throughout all phases of implementation, and creating a work environment that encourages the consultation and active participation of women and children throughout programming. Collection of beneficiary information is kept internal and only shared at the request of beneficiaries and in the presence of MOUs with partner organizations. Furthermore, IhsanRD documentation policies, including photography and videography, strongly consider protecting the rights of the beneficiaries and maintaining their dignity. Participants and beneficiaries will be protected by hired staff. the principle of “Do No Harm” is of utmost importance in the implementation of the project, and staff will take care not to put others in any unnecessary risk, taking into account the existing security situation at a given time and adapting to changing contexts as needed to further ensure safety as a priority. The concept will be added when providing safe and clean water to the household, in this way, women and children will no longer be forced to face the dangers that they may encounter waiting in long lines in order to receive water from water trucks. Through this project, IRD will be able to provide better access to basic necessities for all including women and children. Also, populations, including women and children, will be less vulnerable to communicable diseases that are spread through unsanitary water. Furthermore, by providing water services to locations that IDPs are fleeing to, the IDPs will be welcomed into a more protective, well-serviced environment thereby decreasing the possible difficulties that families may face during this transition. It's worth mentioning that the project has designed to response under two different sectors, but with an integrated services "WASH".

Consult separately girls, boys, women, and men, including older people and those with disabilities in the assessment, design and implementation phases; Protection Risk for Support of the water supply systemsUnequal distribution of servicesinadvertently empower or strengthen the position of armed groups or other actors.Risk of theft of WASH rehabilitation/construction materials such as generators and construction materials (during delivery/storage/ after installation) by armed groups and used in conflict activities.Health risk associated with construction works and exposure to chemicals.Mitigating MeasureEstablish strong links with authorities and operators to promote equity and “do no harm principle” all the time;
