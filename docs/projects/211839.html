Project Summary

Several factors have adversely impacted access to WASH services in Syria, including the February 2023 earthquake, conflict hostilities, economic decline, power shortages, and the impact of climate change. As a result of the conflict and earthquake, damage to WASH systems has restricted the population's access to water and sanitation services in affected communities. Additionally, economic decline has impeded service providers' technical and financial capacities to operate and maintain water and sanitation systems adequately. This economic downturn has also affected households, limiting their ability to adopt coping mechanisms to maintain basic access to WASH services.
The proposed interventions depended on the findings of primary data collection by SECD staff during October 2023 who collected the data by using two methodologies: KII and rapid need assessment with IDPs and hosted people and deep technical assessment, The results showed the importance of supporting the targeted communities who are suffering from the lack of the resources to ensure access to the basic life-saving WASH needs, Basic needs versus provided assistance and gaps in the assessed location. will enable its access to clean water while the others need to connect it to the water network.
The project is designed to respond to the WASH needs of 94551 people (Men: 18910, Women: 19800, Girls: 28421, Boys: 27420),( 37820 IDPs, 56731hosted people, and returnees 1001) with 1319 people of them are people with disabilities during 2024-2025 protracted needs by ensuring good access to sustainable WASH service, The project is designed to respond to the needs of IDPs, Returnees, and hosted people, stayed in the north of Syria in the communities and the IDPs camps .to cover their needs by delivering
humanitarian WASH supplies and services to most vulnerable people: men, women, boys, and girls will benefit from the activities which aim to increase access to sustainable water, sanitation, and environmental hygiene solutions, the project will be transferred the water station from working by fuel to working by a hygiene system most of it solar energy. Through this project in 2024-2025, SECD is planning to support the targeted communities with the following activities:
-Installation of the solar power system to turn the operating system of the water stations: 16 water stations. and conducting a water safety plan and establishing a cost recovery system to ensure the suitability of the project.
-Distribution of 5000 consumable hygiene kits (second round) for the most vulnerable people the component of the HKs.
-Conducting 36 hygiene promotion campaigns and Risk Reduction and Community Engagement (RCCE) provision.
-Provision and installation of 8150 garbage bins 0.1m3 size for the small camps.
Rehabilitation of the sewer system which includes but is not limited to Rehabilitation and
