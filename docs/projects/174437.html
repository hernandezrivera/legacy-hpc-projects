Project Summary

The purpose of the Gender Based Violence Early Warning System is to prevent women and children and other vulnerable communities through a speedy and effective response of law enforcement agencies and other line departments regarding gender based violence issues in the Mirpurkhas Region (Tharparkar, Mirpurkhas and Umerkot). This will be supported by establishing a 24/h Toll Free number at already established Women Protection Cell at the office of Deputy Inspector General.(DIG) Police office Mirpurkhas. The Toll Free number will have the facility to record all the calls and proper log of the cases. The registered cases shall be carry-forwarded to the concerned stakeholders on daily basis. The Regional Women Protection cell shall be linked with the district based women protection cells which are also proposed to be strengthened at the Umerkot, Tharparkar and Mirpurkhas districts.
Women and Children Protection Committee (WCPC) at Tharparkar, Mirpurkhas and Umerkot shall be formed with the participation of the key stakeholders including the lawyers, police, health official, Media and other civil society. It shall act like an advocacy group which will develop the GPV action plans.
The CSSP swill identify the community level women activists (Females) as the potential women paralegals from the selected 30 Union Councils (10 UCs/ district) of the concerned districts and these para legal after selection shall participate in a 5-days training for the women paralegals will be organized to orient them about their role and develop basic understanding regarding pro-women laws and the referral mechanism. These paralegals shall also be oriented on the structure of project, Toll Free number and meetings with the Regional Women protection cell and the linkage with the District level Women protection cells
Information about the toll free number and paralegals shall be disseminated to all the general public for the reporting of GBV cases. The paralegal shall participate in the Quarterly Review Meetings to review the progress as well as the follow-ups of the GBV cases if reported.
GBV cases will be reported through the Toll Free number, Women Protection Cell or by the paralegals to address on immediate basis. Once the case is reported at the Cell / toll free number, it will be categorized and referred to the concerned line departments and the district women and children protection cell for further process and rescue them if the matter is of emergency nature.
Women protection cell at the regional level as well as at the district level will also work closely with the women help desk already established under the Ministry of Women Development Department at the divisional level. MoU shall be signed with the Ministry of Human Rights for the formation of Human Rights Coordination desk at the provincial level. The CSSP shall establish the GBV awareness campaign focusing on different women and child protection laws including Sindh Child Marriage Restraint Act 2013, the Domestic Violence, prevention and protection Act 2013, The Protection Against Harassment of women work place act 2010.
For the awareness of general public about the Toll Free number and para legal support, different IEC activities will also be organized including the 45, street theaters, 5000 posters, 500 pamphlets and FM Radio program shall be on aired so that maximum people can be reached to know about the proposed early warning system.
Two Provincial conferences shall be organized with the participation of key stakeholders to discuss the gaps in the implementation of women pro laws and on the GBV courts in Karachi as per Supreme Court ruling on the GBV cases.
45 Community based Kacharies will also be organized with the participation of police, education, health and district administration for increasing the confidence of communities

1- Project Activities

1) Installation of Toll free number along with PBX system for the 24/7 recording of complaints: A toll free number shall be installed at the office of the Women Protection cell at the office of the DIG Police Office Mirpurkhas.2) Strengthening Women & Children Protection cell at the DIG office Mirpurkhas: CSSP will organize training for the already deployed police official to work as a call operator at the WPC for the 24/7 service. The staff will receive the complaints and forward to the concerned authorities as per SOP developed for the center. 3) Developing Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for the WPC operation: The CSSP will facilitate the police department in developing SOP for WPC operation and making an effective referral system. (4)Formation of WPC Coordination Committee: A coordination committee comprising police, social welfare department, women development department, district administration, women leadership forum and members of citizen support group will be constituted to ensure effective referral mechanism. (5) Establishment of Women and Children Protection cells at the Human Rights cells of the SSP offices of Mirpurkhas, Tharparkar and Umerkot: Three SSP offices shall be approached to allocate one room at each of the offices of SSP Umerkot, Tharparkar and Mirpurkhas. SSP shall identify/notify dedicated staff to be available for the 24/7 to attend the referrals forwarded by the Regional Women Protection cells. These cells shall act under the direct control of the SSP office and in case of any referrals reported from the Regional WPC6) Formation of district women and children protection committee: The committee will be formed to act as the pressure group to raise GB issues and make followups. It will also develop GBV Action Plan and review the plan on quarterly basis. (7)Identification of Paralegals (from 60 UCs, 20 UCs per district): Total 60 women paralegals (age 20- 50) years old shall be identified from all the three districts (20 from each district). They shall be at least Middle/Matric(8)Training of Paralegals: Three training on “Role of Women Paralegals to address the GBV related issues” shall be delivered to these paralegals (Manuals already developed).9) Establishment of Paralegal camps./ Legal Clinics Each paralegal shall establish at least 2 camps at their areas to aware the community on the different GBV laws and her access to them for the front line information on FIR and GBV issues.(10) IEC: In order to disseminate the information to the general public about this early warning system following activities are proposed.- 60 Street theaters on GBV shall be organized (20 at each district)- FM radio messages for 5 months- 5000 Pamphlets and 5000 posters shall be printed on GBV and institutions available like Omdusrman, WPC11) Strengthening the inter departmental linkagesCSSP shall establish linkage with the SAFE House Mirpurkhas, Women police Mirpurkhas , Bar Councils at the district level and the Human Rights Ministry for the effective implementation of the project.(12) Capacity building of Police on Women Pro Laws12 one day GBV laws awareness workshops shall be organized at the districts level (4 at each district). These sessions shall be participated by the constables and Assistant Sub Inspectors/ Sub Inspectors for improving their knowledge on handling GBV cases and GBV laws.(13) 2 Provincial conferences on gaps in Women laws and GBV courtsCSSP shall organize two provincial conferences on gaps in Women laws and GBV courts as per Supreme court directions. Key stakeholders including Ministry of Law, Ministry of Human Rights , Women development department, Social Welfare departments and others shall attend.(14) Community Kachehries with the departments: 45 community based Kachaeries shall be organized with the participation of the police, media15) 16-Days Activism: CSSP shall celebrate the upcoming 16 days of Activism on GBV in December 2021.
