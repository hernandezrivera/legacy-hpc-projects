Project Summary

This project aims to prevent new HIV/TB infections, facilitate access to treatment for the dislocated and ensure the holistic wellbeing of people living with HIV/TB (PLHIV/TB) in Montepuez and Mueda. This will involve both -specific and -sensitive programming, to improve the food security of vulnerable groups; strengthen access to health services; reduce stigma; and increase knowledge and health-seeking behaviour.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

In its HIV/TB programming, WFP Mozambique will combine interventions to prevent HIV/TB infection, as well as to reach PLHIV/TB with treatment and involve both -specific and -sensitive programming, to improve the food security of vulnerable groups; strengthen access to health services; reduce stigma; and increase knowledge and health-seeking behaviour. As part of the food security and nutrition programming, WFP will support access to HIV/TB services at the community level through counselling and testing, and referral of HIV and TB positive cases to health facilities among IDPs and host communities. In addition to this it will support follow-up of beneficiaries who have received HIV/TB treatment at health facilities to support treatment adherence and retention in the program. To support the District Service for Health, Women and Social Action (SDSMAS) and the Provincial Department of Health (DPS), have been programed discussions and trainings that should be conducted with health technicians, community and religious leaders on HIV/TB prevention, treatment and stigma, as well as on the importance of nutrition in HIV-positive people, lactating women and children under 5 years of age and adults (Woman and men).

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Through awareness-raising campaigns on situations of stigma and discrimination for PLHIV, activities in the area of protection have been strengthened in order to guarantee the restitution of human rights, participation and access to services for women, children and men regardless of their HIV status.In addition to that WFP working closely with the Protection Cluster and coordinate in dissemination of awareness raising on gender-based violence.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

In coordination with the Provincial Health Directored and based on statistics and information from health units thought on cases of abandonment and the needs of HIV/TB patients, several challenges were identified, a common factor being the difficulty of access to food and the impossibility of sustaining treatment and enjoying an integral state of well-being. Therefore, in order to combat cases of malnutrition in HIV patients and to promote adherence to treatment and ensure access to nutritional rehabilitation programs and through protection programs, a mechanism has been created through which the group of lactating women, pregnant women and children under 59 months with HIV are beneficiaries of the nutritional rehabilitation plan and adults are beneficiaries of food assistance programs.In addition to this the culinary demonstrations have been planned with the aim of having an active participation of the community through the use of local food.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

For the inclusion of people with special needs, and through IEC material, spots will be created to be broadcasted on radios, allowing people with special hearing and vision needs to have access to information on HIV/TB prevention and treatment.In addition to this, home visits will be made to raise awareness and access to services for people with motor difficulties who do not have access to health units.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

There are not strong environmental considerations related to this project nor any environmental assessments planned.
