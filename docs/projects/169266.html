Project Summary

In order to support children affected by August 4th explosion and provide them access to education opportunities and learning, the present project aims at supporting the rehabilitation of 2 affected Educational Institutions, increasing access to quality formal and nonformal learning opportunities for 150 affected children and at providing psychosocial support to 40 teachers and 150 students.

1 - Needs

On the 4th of August huge explosions hit Beirut city, causing more than 180 deaths and at least 6,500 people injured. Damages have been assessed and reported up to 20 km from the area of explosion, including buildings, shops, health facilities and schools. MEHE reported 159 schools and more that 85,000 have been affected by the explosion. Out of 159 schools, 92 are public and 32, out of them, were used as second shift schools. 7,600 are also the teachers affected by this crisis, and 3,000 among them worked in public schools. The total number of affected children in the 92 public schools is near to 43,000, including 16,000 who are not Lebanese ones. Damages to school infrastructures, furniture and materials prevent children from accessing safe and adequate learning facilities. Moreover, families and individuals, including children, their caregivers and school personals have been affected by multiple traumas related to the explosion and particularly the ones living in the areas affected by explosion.

2 - Activities

Upon project's commencement, an assessment for two schools will be conducted by COOPI engineer in collaboration with education team provided by the schools. Assessment report will be used to agree on rehabilitation works and equipment and supplies to be procured. At the same time, a procurement for the purchase of IT devices for home learning and remote support will be launched and finalized. Devices will be provided to the schools and in turned borrowed to the children in need. At the same time, a consultant will be contracted to delivery PSS capacity building to the schools' Education cadre (we are considering around 20 front-line staff per each school). Given the current uncertainty due to the Covid-19 public health situation and the movement restrictions due to the lock-down in place, the capacity building is programmed to be be delivered through online platforms over two-month duration (expected 5 topics, of the duration of around 7 hours each), and will include topics such as: how to deliver effective individual counseling remotely, providing students and families resilience; how to support the reaffirming of sense of safety and security. Taking into consideration the challenges being faced in Lebanon in terms of electricity power cut-off and therefore the troubles with internet disconnections, the 40 schools' Education staff will be provided with a recharge-card to support them with internet bundles to ensure their attendance to the courses (being the course delivered over a two-month period, recharge cards will be provided for two months, for each of the 40 staff). Moreover, individual counseling will be provided to Education cadre to support them in coping with the compounded pressures they may experience due to anxiety and stress caused by the remote teaching and the current crisis. PSS recreational kits will be provide to targeted school children to nurture their emotional situation. Kits will include various educational games and activities to be utilized at home on their own or with the siblings. Activities will be continuously monitored by COOPI PSS officer and staff. The proposed intervention is based on previous experience and expertise of both COOPI and MADA in providing EiE support and PSS activities to host communities and refugees in Lebanon. COOPI and MADA have been partnering not only in Akkar within a EU funded project but also are currently partnering in Beirut to provide food support to households affected by explosion and to front liners and volunteers.COOPI will be responsible of the procurement and rehabilitation works, overall project and administration management and M&E. PSS activities, establishment of link with School, school staff and local institution will be carried out by MADA in close coordination with COOPI.
