Project Summary

Takaful has designed this program aims to support the food security of the most vulnerable IDPs and part of the host community in Idleb and Aleppo governorates,
Main interventions:
First: providing most vulnerable IDPs and HCs standard food baskets on monthly basis, targeting 9000 HHs 45000 individuals (9640 men -10776 women – 11260 boys -12524 girls ) in Idleb and Aleppo governorate, The distribution will be conducted at specified food distribution points (FDPs), safe FDPs will be chosen, away from the military centres with enough and safe distance and beneficiaries have easy access to them. For beneficiaries who have difficulties to reach to distribution points such as families headed by child or Disabled persons, the baskets will be transferred to their location using light vehicle. The HHS who live in camps will receive baked bread in. the HHS which will receive the bread will be 1000, 5000individuals (men 1080 - women 1222 - boys 1320 - girls 1378).
Second: providing newly IDPs with Ready to Eat rations (RTE) which enable them to provide their families with required food for the first week of the displacement. This intervention targets 5000 HHs 25000 individuals (5480 men - 6332 women - 6420 boys 7068 girls ) The identification of these IDPs is the responsibility of the Takaful MEAL team, who track the movements of the IDPs, collect their data, and verify these data.
Third: Cooked Meals: In this intervention, Takaful Al-Sham will provide 5,000 cooked meals to 5,000 individuals (1296 men, 1466 women and 1653 girls 1584 boys), The new IDPs from the areas that have been displaced by the recent military operations will be targeted. The meal will contain the calories agreed to in the standards set by the Cluster. Takaful will coordinate with the food security cluster and with other organization who works in the same sector and field to reach the right beneficiaries and avoid any duplication. The project will ensure equal access for both genders with the priority for women, children headed household, disabled person, sick, PLW and elderly in addition the project staff will include female and male members trained on the protection principles and gender equality.

4 - Needs

According to HNO 2020 report, 9.8 million people in Syria are in need of food and agricultural assistance. According to Food Security and Agricultural Sector, 7.6 million people suffer from food insecurity, and 1.9 million people are at risk of food insecurity (HRP, Jan 2020). Agriculture is considered one of the essential sources of income. 47% of communities reported barriers to accessing income from agriculture to meet basic needs, (REACH, Dec. 2020).The HNO report that was published in Mar 2021, confirmed that the number of people in need was increased to reach 13.4 million, a 21 per cent increase compared to 2020.Takaful Alsham conducted a Rapid Needs Assessment in March 2021, the first need for farmers in the assessed subdistrict was found to be Agricultural inputs as 35% mentioned, harvesting 28.5%, marketing the crops 10%, trainings 12%, agriculture guidance services 17.5%, other 17%. According to 80% and 70% of KIs at the subdistrict of Afrin and Azaz, respectively, Sunn pest and weeds are widely spread in crops and it poses a threat to the quality of crops. 5 - Implementation of activities

1. Provide emergency response to vulnerable people with short- term food assistance through providing newly IDPs with (RTE), for the first week of the displacement. It targets 5000 HHs 25000 individuals (5480 men - 6332 women - 6420 boys 7068 girls) 2. 5,000 cooked meals will be provided to 5,000 individuals (1296 men, 1466 women and 1653 girls 1584 boys) the newly IDPs from the areas that witnessed the recent military operations will be targeted 3.Monthly food assistance for the assessed food insecure people: Takaful will implement this through providing most vulnerable IDPs and HCs standard food baskets on monthly basis to meet their basic food needs, targeting 9000 HHs 45000 individuals(9640 men -10776 women – 11260 boys -12524 girls) where Takaful will either receive baskets from the donor, or purchase them from the markets according to Takaful policies. The distribution will be conducted in specified food distribution points (FDPs), safe FDPs will be chosen, away from the military centers with enough and safe distance and beneficiaries have easy access to them. For beneficiaries Who have difficulties to reach distribution points such as families headed by a child or Disabled persons, the baskets will be transferred to their location. 4. Supply flour or bread directly to households or to bakeries: the activity will be as follow: Takaful Al-Sham will agree with the bakeries through tenders in order to provide them with flour to bake it and deliver it to the beneficiaries as a bread bags, each 1 ton of flour gives 1000 bundles of bread, and the organization will monitor the entire process from the time the flour enters the bakeries until it is delivered to the beneficiaries in the form of bread.

7.1 - Please list any risks related to sexual exploitation and abuse that have been identified during the project design phase and any risk mitigation and prevention measures that have been adopted and built into the project

In case the complaint was related to SEA classification we share it with our trained field protection focal point who is in charge of reporting such a case to the General Management immediately in order to investigate of a case and take the required action with the employee according to SEA Code of conduct and refer the case to GBV sub-cluster to follow up with the survivor as well as we report the case to PSEA Network. We classify under the sensitive complaints about sexual exploitation or abuse (SEA), Takaful has zero-tolerance for sexual harassment, exploitation, or abuse by any of its staff members, any report or allegation of verbal or physical sexual exploitation or sexual abuse by organization staff or providers against child or women or disable groups of beneficiaries or people in needs to get humanitarian services, such cases should be reported, where the beneficiaries of Takaful services have the right to report SEA, and TAS staff are required to report SEA by another staff member. - We applied some steps to avoid SEA cases as the following: Most of Takaful management and field staff (males and females) had attended workshops to increase their awareness of PSEA cases.- TAKAFUL has trained a field protection focal point who is in charge of reporting such a case to the General Management immediately in order to follow up on the reported case and take the required measures.- Ensuring that all volunteers who work with TAS signed PSEA CoC

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures Assumption 1: The Information that the project built on it is true and neutrality. Risk: there is a risk of giving incorrect data to our team. Probability: Low Impact: High Mitigation strategies (MS): Our team will take in consideration IDPs tracking tables from CCCM and after that will crosscheck the data from local relief offices in the targeted location in addition to site visits to samples of supposed beneficiaries. Assumption 2: The access of all beneficiaries especially women and elders are secured. Risk: There is a risk that some selected beneficiaries has no access to distribution points. Probability: low Impact: Medium Mitigation strategies (MS): We will establish multiple distribution points to facilities the receiving of rations and reducing risks. Assumption 3: The activities are done according to planed timeline. Risk: Because of weather in winter may causes a delay in some activities. Probability: Medium Impact: Medium Mitigation strategies (MS): when designing work plan and the timeline we will consider the weather and its effect. Assumption 4: All roads are open and there is a full safe access to targeted location\s Risk: Road closures and lack of access to targeted locations. Probability: low Impact: High Mitigation strategies (MS): Delay the distribution for several days pending the calm situation - develop an alternative plan to deliver assistance to the beneficiaries in the new situation – ask suppliers to secure the material long enough.
