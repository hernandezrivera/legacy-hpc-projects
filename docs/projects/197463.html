Project Summary

his project aims to support the overall humanitarian response by enabling and enhancing access to beneficiaries and project implementation sites through a provision of air service.
Its main objectives are:
• To provide NGOs, UN agencies, donor organizations and diplomatic missions in Cameroon with safe, effective and efficient air service;
• To transport light cargo, such as medical supplies, information and communication technology (ICT) equipment;
• To provide adequate capacity for medical and security evacuations of humanitarian staff.
UNHAS was established in Cameroon in November 2015 to provide air transport services to the entire humanitarian community and their implementing partners principally NGOs, UN agencies, red cross/crescent movement, donor organizations and diplomatic missions. Ever since, UNHAS has offered safe humanitarian flights on a regular weekly basis to Adamaoua (N’gaoundere), the North (Garoua) and the Far North (Maroua), as well as to Tchad (N’Djamena). As the conflict worsened in the Northwest and Southwest regions, and following a request from the Steering Committee, UNHAS extended its operations in September 2019 to serve the locations of Bamenda, Mamfe and Tiko.
As the continued insecurity, vast distances and poor road conditions remain the main constraints to the provision of assistance by aid organizations, but also the prevailing Covid-19 pandemic worldwide and in Cameroon in particular, the continuation of UNHAS in 2021 will be vital in enabling humanitarian organizations to access the populations in need and project implementation sites
