Project Summary

The proposed project intends to contribute to the reduction of excess morbidity and mortality caused by common communicable and water related diseases for IDPs and floods affected population of Baliet County by ensuring access to quality and Integrated Essential Lifesaving mobile emergency health care services to women, men, girls and boys, Baliet health facilities e.g (Dhol-Achol , BangLai and Abong PHCU) and the needy population of 2 villages i.e. Panthany and Banglei.
Consortium Medical Care (CMC) found that the primary health facilities in the proposed County have been reaching only the nearby population of IDPs from Ulang and Nasir county due to communal conflicts, just like at Dong PHCC, Baliet PHCC, leaving other population without essential lifesaving primary health care services, unprotected against bad practices, sexual exploitation and abuses and more vulnerable and exposed to increased morbidity and mortality. This has been augmented by vulnerability of the communities due to the common infections, frequent snake’s bites due to flooding and in accessibility of health services to hard-to-reached population of Baliet county with some trekking bare foots and cross River Sobat for several days to access medical services in Baliet and Adong PHCC. These Health Facilities are supported by IMC under the World bank project but with challenges reaching out far villages in the mentioned areas.
CMC plans to open 1 Mobile PHCU at Dhol-Achol Payam to save IDPs and host community in Baliet County and establish 2 mobile clinics; 1 in Panthany and 1 in Rengnhom Payam to reach returnees and host communities Baliet areas.
To save lives by providing timely and integrated health services to reduce acute needs among the most vulnerable women, men, girls and boys in those places, and for emergency lifesaving health care services. The 3 mobile clinics will do integrated services of curative consultations, ANC, Vaccination of children under 5 years of age screening and treatment of MAM and transfer of SAM cases to the nearest PHCC, disease surveillance and health promotion and education.
CMC will also train the key project workers in both at the mobile Clinic/PHCUs and at the 2 mobile clinics each in the proposed counties in preparedness and response to epidemic prone disease outbreaks with the main focus on cholera, Measles, malaria, Ebola, Hepatitis E & Rift Valley Fever as well as meningitis.
Based on the health cluster objective 1 of Save lives by providing timely and integrated multi-sector assistance and services to reduce acute needs among the most vulnerable women, men, girls and boys, CMC intends to ensure adequate provision of essential and emergency healthcare services including addressing the major causes of Morbidity and mortality among children under 5 I.e. Malaria, acute watery diarrhea and Pneumonia among under 5 children, basic emergency obstetric and Neonatal care, (BEMONC) services, SGBV services, Clinical Management of Rape Cases (CMR) services to women and girls in Baliet County of Upper State, South Sudan. This will be achieved through the continuity of curative consultations care services under 3 mobile clinics at Baliet county.
Other activities will include not limited to conducting clean, safe and hygienic deliveries by skilled birth attendants, minor surgeries, BEMONC including Health education messages, ANC to pregnant mothers including provision early exclusive breast feeding education, IPT, LLITNs and micro nutrient supplementation, growth monitoring and nutrition assessment to under 5 Males and Females, provision of screening and supporting treatment for MAM/SAM. This project is design to directly beneﬁt a total of 4,508 IPDs and the host communities over the entire period and segregated by age and gender.

1 - Needs

Baliet County is located in eastern part of Malakal town Upper Nile state capital; bordering Ulang and Nasir county in the Republic of South Sudan. It is one of the most conflict affected area with very few functional static health facilities due to inaccessibility of roads to some far villages I.e, Dhol-Achol, Kuel and Rengnhom Payam of Bailiet County of Upper Nile State in South Sudan. These areas were affected by internal conﬂicts between 2014 and 2016 with sporadic frequency fighting from local neighboring communities in Nasir and Ulang County; other conﬂicts in the areas in Upper Nile State. For these reasons, there are more than 4,508 individuals mostly at Dhol-Achol, Rengnhom, and Kuel Payam in addition to estimated 16,850 IDPs due to conflicts in neighboring communities of Ulang and Nasir County with the returnees from Ethiopia borders. These areas have nonfunctional numbers of primary healthcare units (PHCUs) which are currently not support by neither government nor health partner in Upper Nile state. It is reported that only the Primary Healthcare Centers at Adong and Baliet center and are currently supported by International Medical Corps IMC international organization ) as a leading partner now on ground. Therefore, CMC has a plan to take Integrated Lifesaving health care services closure to these affected populations. In consultation with leading partner on ground, county health department, community stake holders and SMOH, we identify serious health gaps that needs humanitarian emergency intervention. In May 2018 report by REACH indicates the leading causes of death and morbidity as malaria (65% prevalence among children less than 5 years), pneumonia and acute watery diarrhea and other water borne diseases. Prevalence of malnutrition is also indicated by 6% SAM, 5.9% MAM and 12.9% acute malnutrition cases among the assessed children aged 6 to 59 months while 50.6% of pregnant and lactating mothers were found to be acutely malnourished during the assessment.

2 - Project activities

The project will contribute to reduction of excess morbidity and mortality caused by common communicable diseases in affected IDPs and host populations in Baliet County. Ensuring access to quality and Integrated Essential Lifesaving mobile emergency health care services to women, men, girls and boys with considerable attention to persons with specific needs (PSNs). The project will support 3 PHCUs and 2 mobile clinics focusing on all the activities aligned to clinical package as per HRP health cluster objectives for this allocation. CMC will carry out; screening of U5 children and PLW for malnutrition with MUAC, rapid testing for malaria in U5 children and pregnant women, Tetanus vaccination of pregnancy, screening for vaccination needs to all under 5, screening, referral of PTB cases, management of uncomplicated MAM in areas with severe food insecurity through OPT and SFP, outpatient consultation for common diseases, MCH Clinic (antenatal care, uncomplicated/normal delivery), routine EPI and Mass vaccination, OPD consultations, application of the Minimum Initial response to snake bites, basic dressing for wounds, provision of psycho-social support and basic Management of SGBV survivors (PEP, treatment STI and wounds). The project will also include basic preventive services like Folic Acid distributions to pregnant women, Vit A supplementation for U5children every 6 months, Rapid Diagnostic Test (RDT) for Malaria with prophylaxis to pregnant mothers, de-worming and treatment for under 5 children every 6 months, pregnant mothers training of Nurses, and Community Health Workers (CHWs ) on epidemic prone diseases like Cholera , Hepatitis E and Meningitis with minimum Initial response for Cholera, routine immunization against all under 5 killers diseases and partnering with the leading partner on ground for proper cold chain monitoring and vaccination, Antenatal care and Family planning services in each facility level.CMC will conduct community mobilization and sensitization to increase the services utilization of Antenatal Care (ANC), Delivery and Post-natal services, conduct training on IMCI/ICCM in order to manage and control common morbidity including: Acute Water Diarrhea diseases (AWDs). We will also conduct rapid screening for Malaria, TT in pregnancy & vaccination of under 5 children against national targeted killers’ diseases with clinical referral of suspected Tuberculosis cases to Primary health care Centres for further investigations and clinical management. The project management team will also request and transport supplies from PSI to the areas of project implementation.Provision of health integrated services (MHPSSS), referral of complicated Malaria cases, Severe Acute Malnutrition cases, Vit A supplementation and awareness raising on Sexual Gender Based Violence (SGBV) services availability at Baliet and Adong PHCCs and provisions of monthly incentives for facility –based health workers in three (3) mobile clinic in Dhol-Achol, Rengnhom, and Kuel PHCUs.CMC will conduct and improve emergency and routine measles immunization throughout hard-reach the target villages. Routine health education messages in three (3) mobile clinics and also at the community level and also train health providers on disease surveillance and outbreak response in selected three locations of Baliet. Conduct weekly, monthly and quarterly reports with provision of weekly data on IDRS /EWARS and strengthen active surveillance to out breaks alerts in addition to monthly reports prepared by M&E field officer. This project will target 4,508 in Baliet County (Dhol-Achol ,Kuel and Rengnhom Payams) and strengthen the capacities of 3 PHCUs and 2 mobile clinics to reach more than 4, 508 targeting beneficiaries including IDPs in Baliet and neighbouring counties at the cost of 207,932 USD.
