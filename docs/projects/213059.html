Project Summary

In response to the pressing needs of displaced populations in northwest Syria, Elaf RD is implementing a comprehensive Shelter and Non-Food Items (SNFI) project aimed at addressing critical gaps in shelter, non-food items (NFIs), and winterization support. With a focus on vulnerable IDPs in areas heavily affected by displacement, our interventions are designed to provide immediate relief and promote dignified living conditions while aligning closely with the recommendations of the SNFI Cluster.
Non-Food Items (NFIs):
One of our primary interventions under the SNFI project is the distribution of Non-Food Item (NFI) kits to vulnerable IDPs. We aim to distribute 1000 NFI kits, each designed to meet the essential needs of displaced families. These kits will benefit 5000 individuals residing in Jaindairis and Salqin, two areas with significant IDP populations. Each NFI kit, costing approximately 230 USD, includes essential items such as blankets, mattresses, hygiene kits, and other household necessities. By providing these kits, we aim to alleviate the immediate suffering of vulnerable families and improve their living conditions during challenging times.
Winterization Support:
In addition to NFI kits, we recognize the importance of winterization support to help families endure harsh winter conditions. As part of our SNFI project, we will distribute cash vouchers for winterization to 500 IDP families. Each voucher, valued at 180 USD, will enable families to purchase essential winter items such as heaters, fuel, warm clothing, and blankets. This assistance will be targeted at IDP families in Harim and Raju, where winter conditions can be particularly harsh, ensuring that vulnerable families are adequately prepared to face the cold weather and its associated challenges.
Shelter:
Another crucial component of our SNFI project is the provision of shelter assistance to displaced families. We plan to construct 300 dignified shelters, each designed to provide safe and secure housing for IDP families. These shelters will be equipped with essential amenities to ensure the well-being and comfort of residents. With an average cost of 2900 USD per shelter, our construction efforts will focus on Sarmada and Azaz, areas with significant displaced populations. Additionally, we will undertake infrastructure rehabilitation projects in three IDP camps, investing an average of 8000 USD per camp. These infrastructure improvements will enhance living conditions for approximately 1500 IDPs in Harim, ensuring that camp facilities meet basic standards of safety and functionality.
Coordination and Alignment:
Throughout the implementation of our SNFI project, Elaf RD is committed to close coordination with the SNFI Cluster and other relevant stakeholders. By aligning our interventions with cluster recommendations and sector standards, we ensure that our assistance meets the most pressing needs of displaced populations and contributes to a coordinated humanitarian response. Our approach prioritizes the dignity, safety, and well-being of IDPs, with a focus on providing holistic support that addresses both immediate needs and long-term recovery.
In conclusion, Elaf RD's SNFI project represents a comprehensive response to the shelter and non-food item needs of displaced populations in northwest Syria. Through the distribution of NFI kits, winterization support, and shelter construction and rehabilitation efforts, we aim to provide vulnerable families with the essential assistance they need to withstand challenging living conditions and rebuild their lives with dignity. Our commitment to coordination, alignment with sector standards, and responsiveness to community needs ensures that our interventions make a meaningful impact and contribute to the overall well-being of displaced populations.
