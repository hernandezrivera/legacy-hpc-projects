Project Summary

In November 2017, tensions in the North West and South West regions turned into violence with dramatic humanitarian consequences. The nutrition situation in the Northwest and Southwest (NWSW) remains unclear due to the lack of a comprehensive anthropometric survey using SMART approach and inaccessibility. Over 40 000 people were denied food assistance due to insecurity and roadblocks in the NWSW regions. This might have a significant impact on children’s nutritional status (OCHA situation report, 12-Oct-2021).
The proposed action aims i) to guarantee free access to nutrition services through a fixed strategy and a mobile strategy via the deployment of 'a mobile clinic, and ii) to provide support to the national health system in sensitization, training and counselling.

1 - Besoins / Needs

In the North West (NW) and South West (SW) regions, the situation remains volatile. The "Non-State Armed Groups" (NSAG) are increasing the attacks against the government and its symbols and against the populations and suspected sympathizers. The central government's response is the deployment of a repressive military apparatus. A deployment which has led to an intensification clash with consequences for civilians at the start of 2021, the crisis went beyond the limits of the two English-speaking regions with an attack in the West region, the second of its kind.The persistence of insecurity threatens local populations, exposes them to risks affecting their physical integrity and leads to population movements, disruptions and losses of their goods and livelihoods, thus reducing their access to health care and their ability to meet their health costs.The nutrition situation in the Northwest and Southwest (NWSW) remains unclear due to the lack of a comprehensive anthropometric survey using SMART approach and inaccessibility. Over 40 000 people were denied food assistance due to insecurity and roadblocks in the NWSW regions. This might have a significant impact on children’s nutritional status (OCHA situation report, 12-Oct-2021).Activities:DEMTOU Humanitarian will support the CMA of Wabane and 20 integrated health centers (IHCs) in;1. The Management of severe acute malnutrition (SAM); 2. The provision of support to the national health system in sensitization, training and counselling.Approach: Nutritional careIn the NWSW, DEMTOU Humanitarian will support Wabane's CMA and 20 IHCs. In connection with the health district, support will be provided in terms of human and material resources. DEMTOU will also take care of the referral and counter-referral of complicated cases from the IHCs to the CMA of Wabane. It will also support the establishment of a ITFC to ensure management activities for SAM with complications.In the NWSW, DEMTOU will guarantee the application of national protocol through close supervision and technical training. DEMTOU will provide the missing and essential equipment for a quality management and will ensure the availability of inputs. Orders will be made in an integrated manner with the staff of the health structures so that they retain their autonomy in ordering and storage. In the supported structures and in the community, accompanying persons will receive training in early detection of “Family-Muac-approach” malnutrition, as well as awareness sessions on essential family practices and the prevention of malnutrition.Deployment of mobile clinicsIn the NW, a mobile clinic will be set up to reach and provide nutritional care to the population who no longer have access to it due to insecurity. It will offer preventive and curative outpatient care. The mobile clinics will also ensure the referral of complicated cases to the district health structures.Results:1. The provision of free and quality nutritional care for children under 5 years of age with uncomplicated and complicated SAM2. Strengthening access to healthcare for populations affected by conflict
