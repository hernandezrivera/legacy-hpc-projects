1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

UNHCR will work through a community-based and right based approach in site management targeting 113,151 IDPs in site and out of camps settings. The project has four components: I) Strengthening community participation, II) Strengthening site level coordination, III) Capacity-building and preparedness, and IV) Supporting site planning and site development.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The project targets the provinces of Cabo Delgado and Nampula. The district of focus includ; Ancuabe, Chiure, Metuge, Mueda, Montepuez, Mecufi, Ibo, Pemba in Cabo Delgado and Nampula and Meconta districts in Nampula province. Priority activities under this project include;i. Community Participation Strengthened- UNHCR will support community self-management structures in the IDP settlements in Cabo Delgado, and Nampula by establishing and or reinforcing Community Complaint and Feedback Mechanisms (CfMs), support existing (facilitate where absent) governance structures; ensuring structures are inclusive with an AGD mainstreamed. Regular community consultations will be conducted throughout the programme cycle including undertaking intention surveys. - UNHCR will engage community self-management structures in the IDP settlements in discussions on community problems and durable solutions (i.e., local integration, return and relocation).i. Site-level coordination strengthened- UNHCR will support government efforts to register IDPs and ensure reliable information management in the IDP settlements including ensuring breakdown of registered data by (age-sex segregated data), population trends monitoring; through site service mapping, intention surveys, and undertaking gap analysis. Regular engagements and or participatory meetings with the community, local authorities and service providers will be conducted. ii. Capacity-building and preparedness -UNHCR will provide site management and protection mainstreaming trainings targeting the IDPs, service providers and local administration. Through this training, UNHCR will ensure CCCM concepts and best practices are implemented in the sites and strive to harmonize the CCCM mechanisms across partners.iii. Site planning and site development- UNHCR will support community led initiatives and site planning and site development interventions that mitigates protection risks ensuring safe and dignified living conditions while fostering community ownership in the process through regular consultations and engagement of the IDPs in the implementation of activities.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

UNHCR staff and its partners are trained on humanitarian principles and the key elements of the centrality of protection: do no harm principle, safety and dignity, effective access to services and assistance, accountability with affected populations and community participation and empowerment. UNHCR has developed a protection monitoring tool for identifying risk profiles and collecting protection incidents, with indicators of age, gender and diversity. Through this project, UNHCR will mainstream protection delivery across all sectors including in CCCM and shelter assistance, its core sectoral lead in situation of conflict induced internal displacements. Protection interventions focus on saving lives and guaranteeing the rights to safety and physical integrity of affected populations, UNHCR will ensure CCCM programmes mainstream protection delivery throughout the project cycle. UNHCR will also ensure site management interventions undertake due diligence including on issues around Housing Land and Property (HLP) associated disputes and risks are mitigated and in close coordination with Protection Cluster and HLP AoR. In addition, to guarantee the dignity of people, UNHCR’s site management and support services will ensure timely and quality services considering the background of the individuals, the respect for their culture and religion and, respecting their freedom of choice

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

UNHCR considers the affected communities as the center of its operation and UNHCR conducts frequent consultations with members of community structures to involve them in the design, implementation and monitoring of community activities in site management and response. UNHCR CCCM’s strategy emphasizes on reinforcing community participation as central to protection and assistance delivery. In 2022, UNHCR will set up community complaints and feedback mechanism in IDP settlements where there is no functional CFM mechanism to allow people affected by the crisis to have access to information and participate in decision-making on projects developed by UNHCR and its partners. UNHCR has also developed a Communication with Communities in Site Management, a guidance note to ensuring information reaches out to all population in the area of response deploying both static and mobile mechanisms including the use of the multipurpose centers (protection desks) as safe spaces for women and girls to seek information. Through this project, UNHCR will seek to expand its CCCM static and mobile outreach to reach wider audience with key protection messeging and site management services

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

UNHCR undertakes consultations and involvement of persons with disabilities in programming and decision-making on issues that affect their lives. UNHCR also conducts specific consultations with persons with disabilities in order to assess their specific protection issues and provide personalized assistance and material support. UNHCR CCCM will ensure, community structures in sites are inclusive with AGD representation. UNHCR ensures that persons with disabilities have access to all relevant information by preparing key messages in multiple and accessible formats. Site Management community meetings will be located in places which are accessible to persons with disabilities

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

UNHCR takes environmental impact into account in all its operations, given that the environmental damage left by displaced populations could be a major concern. As part of its CCCM strategy, UNHCR ensures that the communities are sensitized and engaged on the protection of the place of residence and settlement facilities, emphasizing their responsibility for the local environment where they settle and build ownership. Through community-led initiatives, UNHCR will consider joint eco-friendly projects with IDPs and host communities, that also enhances peaceful co-existence between the different IDP groups and the host community in the Northern Mozambique
