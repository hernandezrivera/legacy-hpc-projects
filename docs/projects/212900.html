Project Summary

This intervention is in line with Sector Objective 2: Most vulnerable HHs near markets receive repeated MPC and are able to prioritize a variety of basic needs, reducing reliance on negative coping strategies over time. The goal is to empower these households to address various essential needs, thereby reducing their reliance on negative coping mechanisms . The project centers on distributing regular cash assistance to identified most vulnerable households, with key focusing on the percentage of households assisted and the percentage reporting improved living conditions due to this assistance.
Leveraging TDH's prior experience in providing multipurpose cash assistance in these areas, the intervention will be targeting 6,000 HH across Latakia, Aleppo, and Hama (with 2,000 HH in each area) through 8 cycles each cycle is aligned with the transfer value by CWG (1,430,000 SP as per the latest update) for each HH, the project aims to swiftly and effectively assist those facing hardships. This multipurpose cash aid allows households the flexibility to prioritize urgent needs such as food, shelter, healthcare, and other vital services. TDH is regularly attending CWG meetings to update on the values and prioritizations' of the MPCA.
Implementation strategy revolves around a thorough process to identify and reach the most vulnerable households, ensuring that assistance reaches those who need it most, and are situated near vital marketplaces. This careful selection process guarantees impactful and efficient aid distribution.
Furthermore, the project adheres to CWG guidelines, meticulously assessing local market functionality and accessibility in the target areas. This assessment evaluates market infrastructure, supply chains, price stability, and product diversity to ensure that beneficiaries can utilize their cash assistance effectively.
Protection Risk Analysis:
PR1: Beneficiaries and project staff may face physical safety risks due to conflict, violence, or insecurity in the targeted areas.
M2: TDH have a Security officer who regularly conduct security assessments and implement measures to ensure the safety of beneficiaries and staff, establishing secure distribution points, and coordinating closely with local authorities and security actors. Provide training on personal safety and security protocols./PR2: Women and girls may be at heightened risk of GBV, including domestic violence, sexual exploitation, and harassment, especially in contexts of displacement and instability/ M2: TDH integrate prevention and response measures into the program activities, such as providing gender-sensitive information sessions, establishing safe spaces for women and girls, and training staff on GBV identification and referral protocols./ PR3: Collecting and storing beneficiary data may pose risks to privacy and confidentiality, including unauthorized access, misuse, or data breaches. M3: TDH always obtain informed consent from beneficiaries before collecting personal data and ensure compliance with relevant data protection laws and regulations, also, TDH ensure data privacy by collecting minimum beneficiaries information during assessments.
