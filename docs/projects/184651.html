Project Summary

The project intends to provide access to an integrated package of emergency lifesaving services including nutrition, WASH and education to 16,536 IDPs and host community in Luuq district. Nutrition access is low for families resulting quite high prevalence of acute malnutrition. The project intends to respond to the apparent lack of access to nutrition ration by providing nutrition services and IYCF conselling to 5500 women and care takers.
For the target population, access to basic nutrition services is limited, increasing morbidity and mortality from treatable malnutrition conditions. Nutritoin challenges/needs are mostly profound among the most vulnerable groups, pregnant and lactating women and children less than five years. Global acute malnutrition among this population is over 15% whereas severe acute malnutrition prevalence stands at 1.9%. Additionally, children below five years of ages’ death rates are at 0.17% with morbidity as high as 26.4% recorded. In response to this, EDRO will sustain and scale up nutrition service delivery in its sites located in Luuq and with its mobile outreach. The facilities will provide integrated maternal, neonatal and child nutrition services. Key services to be availed will include; immunization, IYCF sessions and management of childhood malnutrition, detection and management/referral of malnutrition cases. Critical component to the intervention will be aggressive community education and sensitization on critically important issues of infant young child feeding, prevention and treatment if malnutrition cases. In total 3,336 treatments and 5500 (IYFCF) children and mothers will access nutrition services.
WASH: The project will increase access to sustainable safe water to reduce the risk of AWD/cholera and GBV against women and girls through the establishment of 6 communal water points to 6 IDP camps and 3 school supplying water through piping system, EDRO will sign agreement with the boreholes management to continue supplying the water to the communities by supporting maintenance and operation cost. EDRO will dislodge 20 filled IDP latrines; construct 30 new IDP latrines and three gender sensitive twin IDP school latrines to the IDP school in boyle/balanbale. General cleanup will be conducted twice/month to 6 IDP camps. Comprehensive hygiene promotion awareness campaigns including distribution of 2,000 HP kits will be conducted by 10 trained CHPs to reach more than 75,00 IDPs and host communities in Luuq district.
The WASH project will improve access to basic education to children through establishment of piping water for 1 school in Boyle/balannale area. In the WASH component, the programme will construct three gender sensitive twin latrines and 1 water kiosk through piping system, as well as hygiene promotion awareness campaigns to the children.
The proposal is inline with the intentions and purpose the integrated package is aimed to reduce morbidity through nutrition and WASH interventions for IDPs and host communities.

1. - Projects activities

Increase and scale the nutrition services for newly displaced families who lost livestock in centers. Provide adequate nutrition supplies to the target facilities across the larger Luuq district centers)and mobile nutrition units. Conduct community mobilization/awareness raising sessions(one session per week per facility, several fixed and mobile units)to create awareness on IYCF with emphasis on malnutrition prevention through facility staff and community health workers.Wash - Establishment of 6 communal water points (6 in IDP camps and 1 in school) using existing boreholes through piping system. Women and girls will be consulted the location and design of the water points to preserve their safety and avoid SGBV during the water fetching as both women and girls fell the responsibility of water collecting to the households.Support fuel, operation and maintenance to 2 boreholes in a period of 3 months to supply chlorinated safe water to 2 communal water points through piping system. Chlorinate the 2 boreholes and conduct daily monitor the free chlorine residual testing (FRC) by providing the well operators training and equipping with appropriate amount of chlorine needed to disinfect the wells on daily basis. Chlorination of wells is vital to avoid the recurrent AWD/cholera diseases outbreak in the IDPs caused by unsafe water. Construct 20-flood proof desludging latrines (10 for male and 10 for female) with lockable doors and hand-washing facilities to the newly arrived IDPs. Women and girls will participate the selection of the latrines' location to keep their safety and to avoid SGBV against them. The aim of deluging latrines (recommended by WASH cluster) is when filled, it can be easily emptied and no need to construct new latrines (space limitation and land ownership issue), For the sustainability of the desludging, EDRO I will mobilize the community to promote appropriate hygiene practices related to the design, use and maintenance of the facilities.Distribute 2000 HP kits to IDPs from households with malnourished children, households at risk of AWD/cholera diseases.Establishment of WASH facilities with gender friendly WASH facilities in Boyle area in Luuq, to provide education to (boys and girls) school going IDP establishment of gender friendly WASH facilities including establishment of water kiosk through piping system.
