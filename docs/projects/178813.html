Project Summary

4 - Needs

Over the conflict years in Syria, an extremely adverse impact affected the health, social, service and education of the whole society’s aspects. According to the HNO 2021 estimated that there are over 7 million people in need of education support, and 2.45 million children (three to 17 years old) were out of school3 and 1.6 million were at risk of dropping out. Due to the cumulative effect of the conflict, the closure of learning facilities, Displaced communities remain significantly underserved, with most IDP camps having insufficient or non-existent education services.Lack of educational resources is the main reason behind students not getting taught so as an expected result, children are dropping out of the school.Since the academic year started recently and to attain the noble goal of providing children in Syria with equal opportunity to children around the world for their right to get education, our assistance is must. The intervention model aims to enhance and ensure the access to formal education in 5 schools in camps and 5 schools in host communities and improve access to education in 7 camps. During the design of this project, BINAA conducted rapid needs assessment in the communities that the new established camps located, and as per the assessment 2 neighbor camps (Omran, Yasin) are not supported with education intervention and 5 schools in the 2 host communities are not supported for the 2020-2021 academic year.As per assessment results,there are 3636 HHs (18,180 Individuals),8900 students(4840 boys,4060girl) there is a huge gap in support for education sector such as: (no education facilities in the camps, lake of improvement of WASH facilities to be more gender sensitive and more prepared against COVID 19.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk-01:Insufficiently safe learning facilities, as learning facilities unable to put into place and maintain Covid-19 safety measures including positive behavioral changes.the likelihood is low and the impact is high. Mitigating Measure: BINAA will provide the learning facilities with Covid-19 safety protocols in place. Protocols will include identification, prevention measures, awareness raising and a referral system between the learning facility and the relevant health center. These protocols will adequately resourced and regularly practiced. Risk-02: Teachers or other education personnel participate in physical punishment, act violently, bully and neglect students.the likelihood is medium and the impact is high, Mitigating Measure:BINAA will ensure an enforced code of conduct, and train the teachers and school personnel on prevention and reporting of violence, physical punishment and bullying. sensitize the students and their caregivers on the Code of Conduct and related prevention, identification, reporting systems. Risk-03: Access restrictions to the targeted areas active ¦ghting: the likelihood is medium and the impact is high. Mitigating Measure:the risk will be monitored by Binaa field staff in addition to the report received from the security department. all works will stalled in the low-security locations and being prepared to apply either the contingency or exit plan.Risk-04: GBV, sexual abuse or facilities are not disability-sensitive in the rehabilitated schools’ facilities. the likelihood is low and the impact is high. This risk will be monitored basically by Binaa M&E team. Referral path system and Feedback and complaint mechanism is adopted to contribute in solving the issues.
