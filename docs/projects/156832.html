Project Summary

The Syrian Networks League was founded to coordinate between the multiple CSOs and Networks present on the Syria and Turkey. It intends to undertake a leadership role in the defense of the aspirations of the Syrian civil society by rebuilding and developing its capacities. The League aims to become a balanced and harmonized representation of the Syrian humanitarian workers and civil society and support it through crowdfunding and advocacy for its main issues. Moreover, the League like an umbrella between 6 Syrian Networks and seeks to establish a network of Syrian humanitarian workers and organizations dealing with Syrian affairs in order to support nascent initiatives and organizations by backing and developing their projects, pairing them with international partners, and exchanging expertise between operators. The League aims to provide a reliable environment for renovation, exchange of ideas and best practices to facilitate the coordination of an integrated approach for fulfilling the needs of the Syrian civil society. Moreover, the League intends to perform the role of the advocate of the Syrian civil society exigencies in front of international actors to promote partnerships between Syrian CSOs and global organizations. The project aims to secure a transparent coordination mechanism based on multilateral participation of trustworthy actors through the activity of the League for the purpose of leading the aspirations of Syrian CSOs and civil society and rebuild its capacities, through training sessions, coordination meetings, Workshops, and Conferences with all Syrian networks and Syrian NGOs in Syria and Turkey, All the benefices will be from Syrian CSOs and we planning to reach 200 CSOs, the league will manage the transportations for humanitarian Syrian Representatives .

Needs

The needs are weak in the internal work policies (SOPs) of the Syrian Network League and the need for a strong coordination body to support the Syrian networks and Syrian Organizations and the need for greater representation in the international conferences to convey the voice of the Syrian institutions to the UN, Geneva and Brussels, and the weakness in the capacities of Syrian networks and institutions in terms of governance and decision making.

Implementation of activities

- Establishment of an updated monthly database for all member organizations of the Syrian Network, including their respective operational areas and place of work in Syria - Establishment of a legal office for the University specialized in conflict resolution and consultative support - Establishment of monthly coordination meetings with Syrian NGOs and their introduction into humanitarian programs - Organizing 5-day training in the media - Organizing 5-day training in governance - Organizing 5-day training in news editing - Organizing 6-day training in advocacy - Identifying 4 sectors of focus (health, education, human rights, community Civil) for performance analysis The development of the sector and the areas of improvement; each sector will be examined by groups of 15 people to resolve policies and working methods - 4 public meetings will be organized: First meeting: establishment of database, advocacy group, legal office and identification of 4 business sectors o Second meeting: Third: to discuss the reports of the advocacy group and follow up the fourth meeting: to analyze the final reports and responses and follow-up and the preparation of the new campaign .establish a data base for Humanitarian Representatives to manage their transportations

Protection Risk Analysis

- Loss or inconclusive Supporting Documents, monitoring and Materials: This includes the ability to take signatures, photographs, GPS locations, assessment data, receipts and other similar - Finding the appropriate trainers in terms of technical knowledge and communication skills

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

- always back up the data - going back to documents owners to have them again - generalizing over the staff to "keep all documents related to work whether you consider it important or not" - Taking this fact into consideration before selecting the type of training, Finding a center next to the community, providing a transportation allowance
