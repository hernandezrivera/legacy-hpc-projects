Project Summary

LPG assistance will be provide for both Rohingya and host community at camp-25 and Baharchhara Union respectively in Teknaf upazilla from Jan. to Dec' 22 considering to ensure safe and dignified living conditions and contribute to environmental sustainability to decrease dependency of forest wood for cooking purpose. Actually, we have already working with these services through implemented some projects from 2019 to until Oct' 21 except Feb-Mar 21 in camp-23. But, from 1st Sep'21 we had to shift at camp-25 for LPG refill distribution with camp-23 in order to the government taken decision to camp closer so that the beneficiaries have been shifting other nearer camps and Vasanchor as well. As its continuity, we will plan to distribute LPG refill services and will cover the whole camp among 1705 Rohingya Families throughout upcoming year. Apart from this, we also plan to continue our LPG refill services among 750 HHs for host community in Baharchora Union, Teknaf Upazila. In this connection, the total beneficiary under LPG refill service will be 2455 for the next JRP year'22 and Nabolok will distribute LPG refill services among the targeted beneficiaries the both communities as per their schedule on basis of family size as well as project plan. Beside these, to reduce the probability of fire incidents through expired LPG stove, Nabolok also plan to replace all the existing stoves which are already expired and provide the new one for each of the targeted 2455 beneficiaries through this project early of the 2022. And, we will include an training session in this project on fire safety especially on LPG safety so that they can gather some sort of knowledge on fire safety issue and they can apply those knowledge gathered from training and cook themselves more quickly and safely which also help to mitigate the chances to create fire incidents by LPG in our working areas. In addition, addressing any hazards in our working areas at both camp and host level, we will allocate some contingency funding in this project proposal which is 2% on the total proposed budget so that we can provide LPG support among approximately 224 the affected beneficiaries by providing new LPG cylinder and stove just after the incidents. Like the previous projects, this project also we will have plan to provide our LPG service for women, persons with special needs and elderly peoples as priority basis due to reduce their vulnerability as well as we will provision wide queue place with shed minimize their waiting anguish and we have also the separate queue for women and PWDs beside of male beneficiaries so that women beneficiaries will comfort to maintain queue and they won’t face any sexual and physical harassment by the male beneficiaries. In this connection, we will have plan to hire Rohingya beneficiaries especially women, older people and person with disabilities and other forms of gender diverse population as volunteers they will perform some small scale works just like crowd controlling & management, maintain COVID protocol like maintain social distancing during queue, ensuring sanitized hands to the beneficiaries before enter the distribution center and so on so that they can have income generating opportunity by this activity and contribute their family expenses and their personal cost as well. So that, 288 Rohingya beneficiaries especially women, older people and PWDs and other forms of gender diverse population will take working opportunity from our LPG activity as volunteer. Beside this, we will also plan to provide porter assistance for most vulnerable beneficiaries just like elderly & injured person and women pregnancy period if this family have no alternative person to collect LPG refill from distribution center by reached their LPG refill at their home by porter volunteer which hired from Rohingya as well as we will change the porter volunteers after certain period so that more people will give income generating opportunity.
