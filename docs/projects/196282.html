Project Summary

SARD focuses under this Project on more sustainable solutions at the community level, in which 10 water networks will be rehabilitated/upgraded to assist up to 3.000 families (15,000 individuals approx). In addition to rehabilitation/upgrading of 10 sanitation and sewage systems, targeting 3,000 families (15.000 individuals approx). Water and sanitation facilities also fall under this action, where 12 facilities are planned to be rehabilitated (6 schools and 6 health facilities), benefiting up to 9.000 individuals (direct and indirect). The rehabilitation of public infrastructure facilities (water/sewer networks, schools, and healthcare facilities) is anticipated to involve 50 percent of the local population and 50 percent of IDPs.
As an aspect of emergency response, essential WASH NFIs are also part of this project since hygiene kits are a household-level WASH intervention recommended for use during emergencies, Covid-19 and cholera outbreak responses, and other crisis contexts; 6.000 families (30.000 individuals approx) dispersed in open fields and informal settlements are anticipated to benefit from this service.
All SARD WASH activities are carried out with a focus on protection and a well-structured complaints mechanism.

3 - Needs

SARD Yearly Needs Assessment in NW Syria for WASH indicated that the reliability and efficiency of water systems have sharply decreased for the first time since 2016, with nearly 2 million fewer people using water networks as the only household water supply modality, this means up to 47% of the population is relying on often unsafe alternatives to piped water, to meet or complement their water needs. Investment in sanitation infrastructure is critical, as at least 70% of the sewage is discharged untreated and at least half of the sewerage systems are not functional. Throughout this study’s analysis of the status of the essential community services infrastructure, it was determined that the water infrastructure, specifically the water network and sewage system, is marginally damaged and semi-functional in all assessed locations. These inadequate WASH conditions promote the spread of disease, particularly acute diarrheal infections that disproportionately affect young children, thus requiring substantial investment. In addition, KIs frequently reported damage to other service infrastructure, especially in Suran, Azaz, and Maaret Tamsrin. According to them, the most affected areas are the main streets, roundabouts, parks, healthcare and schools, which have sustained moderate to severe damage and require immediate intervention to restore their functionality.

4 - Implementation of activities

Under its First Line Emergency Response (Result 1) SARD will target the most vulnerable IDPs families scattered in the open fields in informal settlements and under trees with rapid emergency WASH assistance including:- Hygiene kits for each family for three months. Compositions of the hygiene kit will be designed based on cluster recommendations, discussed, and agreed with people of concern (beneficiaries). Under result 2, SARD focuses on more sustainable solutions, looking at community level rather than a family level. Activities under result 2 include: - Rehabilitation and extension of existing water and sewage networks.- Rehabilitation of water and sanitation facilities in schools and health facilities. Prior to site works, SARD technical team conducts site visits and studies to prepare BoQs. Site works are implemented thereafter through contractors under close supervision by SARD site inspectors. SARD WASH activities are implemented with a protection lens, with a well-structured complaints mechanism.

9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

One of the risks that the project might face is the steeling of NFIs, SARD Has a dedicated staff to look after the warehouses and a well-observed storage system. Distribution of expired unhealthy items, SARD has a technical committee to review any purchased items right after the tender and selection process, SARD chooses according to specific criteria that suit the activity intervention and time of implementation; one of these criteria is the expiry date and the storage conditions, SARD team monitors the status of the items periodically to ensure its validity. Unequal distribution of services inadvertently empowers or strengthens the position of armed groups or other actors, SARD Establishes solid relations with authorities and operators to promote equity and “do no harm principle” all the time; Organise joint planning culture and agrees on the minimum standard; Include community and authorities in targeting; Ensure sensitive information is handled with care. Health risks associated with construction works and exposure to raw sewage and gases, SARD team and contractors are always under observation considering the usage of PPE, SARD has a safety rehabilitation working SoP and ensures the application of it at all SARD operations areas, SARD provides safety training to its engineers and makes sure that the contractors are aware of such safety measures.
