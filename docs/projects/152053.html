Project Summary

Under the proposed intervention the AAH in collaboration with the SMoH and other humanitarian organizations (UNICEF/UNFPA) planned to continue providing life-saving primary health care services targeting 60 facilities and mobile clinics in 9 LGAS through an integrated approach. The proposed health care services will include support to improve Health system and clinical support, identification, assessment and management of communicable Disease; Sexual Reproductive Health care services; referral for Non-communicable disease to specialized secondary and tertiary care centers, supporting community health through supporting community based detection and referral of illnesses, promotion of appropriate health care seeking behavior, provision of pharmaceutical and medical commodities. strengthening of the health system will be an integral part of the proposed health interventions.

Needs

The ongoing conflict has also affected the capacity of the SmoH to respond to the population need health system with only 30 per cent of health facilities remaining functional in Borno State, where malaria is endemic and cholera and other diseases affect the population regularly and are often life-threatening. Recent assessment conducted by the humanitarian community and most specifically the joint assessment conducted by REACH during the month of August 2018 highlighted key health and gaps that the AAH proposed health interventions plan to address. Limited access to quality health care services by the conflict affected communities, inadequate capacity of the health providers, limited human resources to deliver health services, shortage of medical supplies and commodities, inadequacy of health services offered to women victims of rape as well as difficulty in delivering humanitarian services in hard to reach areas due to the current dire insecurity situation are the needs that that Action Against Hunger planned to continue addressing through the proposed health interventions. Under the proposed intervention the AAH in collaboration with the SMoH and other humanitarian organization (UNICEF/UNFPA) planned to support the provision of primary health care services targeting 60 facilities and mobile clinics in 9 LGAS through an integrated approach. The proposed health care services will include support to improve Health system and clinical support, identification, assessment and management of communicable Disease; Reproductive health care services; referral for Non-communicable disease to specialized secondary and tertiary care centers, supporting community health through supporting community based detection and referral of illnesses, promotion of appropriate health care seeking behavior, provision of pharmaceutical and medical commodities.
