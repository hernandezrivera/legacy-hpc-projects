Project Summary

Across the two affected Regions, the situation continues to deteriorate with 1.3 million people now in need of assistance. The conflict has left over 536,000 internally displaced, 237,349 returnees and 39,602 Cameroonian refugees in Nigeria. The outlook as at now is one of emergency response as displacement continues due to the ongoing conflict and vulnerability is compounded as services deteriorate and resilience is eroded. The situation is characterized by human rights and protection violations that have engendered an upsurge in gender based violence.
Engaging young girls and women in the response to their needs of reproductive health services, is the main purpose of this proposal.
Empowerment Corner Cameroon (ECOCAM) is a non-governmental, apolitical, charitable, and non-for-profit organization, created for the purpose of promoting the rights of women, children and youths in Cameroon. Through participatory approaches, the organization works to identify especially the need of the urban and rural youths, the underprivileged and the weaker section of the society.
Our mission is to facilitate the uplifting of the basic living conditions of children, youths and women and to promote human values through advocacy and community outreach programs.
ECOCAM will focus on the provision of reproductive health services, focusing on selected communities in the Meme and Fako Divisions
Project objectives
The main objective of this project proposal is specifically to prevent increasing maternal and neonatal mortality, and respond to gender-based violence among the most vulnerable populations affected by the humanitarian crisis in SWR with focus on women and young girls.
The project aims to respond to needs in the targeted conflict-affected communities through two main components – women health and women protection
Women health component
It focuses on strengthening the health system in order to reduce morbidity and mortality rates among women at childbearing age.
Key activities:
• Provide emergency RH kits and life-saving emergency obstetric care medicines and equipment to health facilities providing basic and comprehensive emergency obstetric and neonatal care services
• Improve capacities of health providers in MISP for RH
• Operating mobile medical teams and clinics to provide reproductive health services in remote conflict-affected areas
• Support referral between fixed health units and mobile clinics and hospitals offering comprehensive obstetric and neonatal care services
• Provide contraceptives to health facilities and mobile clinics
Communicating with Communities

• Media campaign to improve people’s understanding and access to reproductive health and gender-based violence services
• Engaging key information gatekeepers within communities
• Developing a systematic two-way communication channel among beneficiaries

For this purpose, ECOCAM will use its contracted mother care and maternity supplies company and their warehouses for storage while implementing these activities. Monitoring, audit and reporting Due to the difficult security situation in these vulnerable communities, ECOCAM will rely on a key partner (Pan African Institute for Development – West Africa) to monitor the implementation of the above mentioned interventions. In addition, ECOCAM is open to get monitored by any other organization if imposed by the funders of this project. So ECOCAM is open to remote and third party monitoring.ECOCAM Project and Operations Support ECOCAM will have capacity to provide project and operations support at community level as well as at the divisional levels, within the vulnerable communities and the Meme and Fako Divisions of the SW Region of Cameroon. Information sharing and lessons learned will be effectively communication for possible replication of the project implementation and successes for other CSOs to implement in other communities within the SW and NW Regions of Cameroon. Project management and implementation is further supported by freelance project managers passionate and experience in RH, GBV and humanitarian response. A Communication Specialist will provide support to communication, advocacy and donor relations. Other ECOCAM staff will provide part-time support to the project. Due to the situation in these areas there will be staff-related security costs. Safety and Security Due to current security situation in the region, ECOCAM project will focus on life-saving activities based Safety and security mainstreamed in all project processes in line with the risk acceptance modality of UN Security Management System. To ensure safety and good storage conditions of the commodities procured by ECOCAM, its partner supplier will ensure storage and logistics on the specific days of delivery to the communities’ sites. Project visibility ECOCAM will produce materials that will be used for project visibility such as posters and video documentary. A Communications Specialist is available to provide support. Donor funding arrangements ECOCAM is open to donor funding arrangements and will streamline operationality based on donor specifications and budget limits.
