Project Summary

• Community based approach for rehabilitation of 18 cyclone damaged classrooms with mixed materials
• Community based approach for setting up of 8 TLS’s using locally available materials
• Learning materials for 10,000 students including paper based instruction for remote learning if prolonged closure of schools.
• Teaching materials for 200 teachers
• Teacher training for 200 teachers on PSS, CP, GBV, DRR, Hygiene and COVID-19. If schools remain closed for a prolonged period this will include training on Street Child's model for distance learning, Last Mile Learning.
• Provision of reusable masks and soap for 10,200 students and teachers.
• Provision of handwashing stations and soap for 10 schools
• Training for school council members on PSS, CP, GBV, DRR, COVID-19 and development of basic school emergency plan.
• PSS activities for 10,000 students delivered in school or remotely
• Local partner capacity building on integrated approaches to CPiE and EiE.
• Remote learning for students if schools continue to be closed, using Street Child’s Last Mile Learning model. All material is developed and sensitised for inclusion, gender and IDP’s.
(Mecufi - School rehabilitation (for 9 classrooms), delivery of 8 TLS, teacher (50) and school council training for 15 members in PSS, DRR, CP and remote teaching methodology and on COVID 19, material and supply distribution of masks and soaps for 2500 students and 50 teachers. Handwashing stations for 3 schools.
Metuge - School rehabilitation (for 9 classrooms), teacher (150) and school council training for 35 members in PSS, DRR, CP and remote teaching methodology and on COVID 19, materials and supplies distribution of masks and soaps for 7500 students and 150 teachers and handwashing facilities for 12 schools. Street Child have already concluded rehabilitation of 3 classrooms in Nacarramo community in Metuge alongside teacher training on PSS, CP and DRR. In addition to this we have conducted COVID-19 prevention messaging and hygiene material distribution in the communities of Nacarramo, Impire, Mieze, Nanila and Muepane. We are about to commence further COVID-19 prevention activites and materials distribution in 5 IDP reception centres in Metuges).
