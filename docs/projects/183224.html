Project Summary

Borderless is a Lebanese non-profit-organization with a mission to create life changing impact on the lives of vulnerable families. The NGO emerged out of existing needs within the local community with a central aim to serve the people. We strive to address the structural barriers to overcoming hardships in disadvantaged and at-risk communities, such as patterns of discrimination and exclusion; lack of access to essential public services; conflict and social disorder; and major public health threats.
THE VALUE OF A COMMUNITY KITCHEN
Malnutrition especially high among marginalized communities, Borderless operates in two of the most crucial areas when it comes to beneficiaries in need, serving the Lebanese Communities.
Food insecurity is the predominant issue among underprivileged families and by offering healthy meals we want to reduce the risk of malnutrition, a problem that needs to be addressed immediately. Malnutrition is not only a great threat to health, it also causes a aftermath of struggles and trauma.
We noticed that due to early childhood malnutrition a large percentage of children do not have the cognitive capacity to keep up with classes. Nutrition is a crucial supplement to enhance our child-/ protection and educational programs in place.
Mother’s without sufficient nutrition do not have the capacity to breastfeed their children, one of the healthiest approaches to grant a healthy upbringing.
With our yearlong experience and our strong bonds we are confident to reach over 2,000 individuals daily.
Both our centers cater to around 1500 families in both communities, our community kitchen will ensure that each family will get at least 1 hot & healthy meal a day for a year delivered to their doorstep.
By creating a community kitchen, we will help these family directly and economically help our communities indirectly through the below steps that we will take;
1- Benefiting small businesses and purchase all the produce through SMEs within these communities
2- Creating jobs through hiring staff from the communities we wish to serve.
3- Community Kitchen works on a lower budget rather than providing cash assistance to families to purchase / cook their own meals. Discounted rates through bulk purchases allow us to cater to a larger number of families
4- Increase Community Involvement
5- Direct Health Impact in improving the children & parents through healthy meals
6- By Providing healthy hot meals to children & youth, we will help with their cognitive learning and help them preparing for the academic years & activities
7- Lowering crime, theft rates if the basics are provided to their families, which lowers the burden on the parents to provide such needs.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

Securing 2,000 meals a day through a community kitchen that serves the Ouzai and Karantina Areas.These areas are amongst the most marginalized areas in Lebanon.Ouzai has the highest density rate of Immigrants in Lebanon, whereas, Karantina has a high rate of mixed nationalities experiencing extreme poverty, especially, after the 4 August 2020 Beirut Blast. Our activities will involve cooking hot meals to 1000-1500 families (2,000 meals/day) & distribute them door to door over a period of 1 year. Targeting the Food Security issue, is positively impacting our other activities within these communities, such as educational & child- / protection activities. We will be working with a nutritionist to establish a well-grounded healthy menu each month consisting of proteins (which is the most expensive product in the market currently), Vegetables, Whole Grains, Fruits & Beverages Inc Water. One of the main emphasis will be the calcium factor . Currently at Borderless, we provide the children that attend our center with just a breakfast portion for each child such as (mankouche, croissant & water), however, we know that the capabilities of a child can be fully reached when nutrition plays a big part in his/her life. So we would like to change our portions from a regular breakfast item that we can barely afford as they are paid directly by us, to switch it to a healthier approach by providing them a healthy breakfast and a hot meal for lunch, fresh juices and water.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

All employees and staff recruited for this project, must abide by Borderless Code of Conduct, HR Policies and other correspondent policies. Reporting mechanisms are in place to ensure all incidents must/should be reported to Borderless Top Management and immediate action will be taken. Budget to be monitored closely by the Project Manager, Operation Director, Top Management and external audit company to ensure accountability.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

Due to the our close connections with both communities at our existing Borderless Centers throughout the year cooperation, we have the strongest commitment to serve them in their very best interest. Our accountability will be demonstrated by close monitoring on daily basis by transparency & the willingness to provide information to donors at any given time. Feedback and accountability mechanisms are integrated into our trainings, evaluations of our ongoing staff and partnership agreements. Suppliers also must abide by such measurements.
