Project Summary

Areesha sub-district is under the control of the SDF without any presence of ISIS cells, so the area is considered safe, stable, and accessible to provide humanitarian services. The area is underserved according to HNO 2020 and under a high severity scale. The area hosts IDPs, and returnees, in addition to the host community of boys, girls, women, men, and PWD. Most of the issues faced by the community due to the crisis is a lack of accessible multi-sectoral services like health, FSL, ERL, Education, and protection services. lack of income resources especially women-headed households, and PWD. Due to the crisis, the protection needs of most vulnerable people women, girls, PWD, and children are highly increased and critical to be delivered, adding more lack of income resources for those vulnerable people, especially under the COVID-19 epidemic.
Women and young girls are the most vulnerable groups in NES. Their access to services is minimal, as they always need to be with a trusted male to have access to any service like education, training, community center, work, etc. Girls and women are less considered to reach education, vocational training, etc., as most of the services provided don’t take into consideration the difficulties of those groups to reach it. but at the same time the communities in NES believe that if safe access to services is provided, they are very supportive of having those vulnerable groups reach their needs. The People with disabilities are fully marginalized to reach their needs for protection, livelihood, education, and health, in addition to women-headed households which need have.33% of HHs mention that women and girls feel unsafe in their community,84% of children facing child marriage issues for girls aged 15 to 17. 48% of women and girls face spousal/family/community restrictions as causes of movement restrictions for women. Members of the Areisha and Shaddadi communities face numerous protection risks, such as child labour, child marriage influenced by cultural traditions and financial difficulties, children leaving education prematurely, increased bullying targeting children with disabilities, intimate partner violence, and abandonment of women and girls to take charge. Responsibility for raising children and providing income. Other issues of concern include polygamy, frequent pregnancies at short intervals, and low literacy rates.
The proposed project aims to address the humanitarian emergency in NES and provide VAWG assistance to the affected population (especially the most vulnerable couples) Areesha subdistrict and Al-Hassakeh district for host communities thorugh WGSS
Project interventions are aimed to benefit the most vulnerable section of the host community population. Specifically; couples. The project will also benefit men and women through community-level intervention sessions. The design of activities will take into account household roles and the time availability of women to facilitate their participation.
In the Areesha sub-district: the project will target couples from the community for the couples curriculum activities, and men and women in group sessions. For the community-level intervention, the beneficiaries will be from different categories (women, men, boys, and girls)

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Protection Risk Analysis: 1. GBV Risk Assessment: A thorough assessment of the specific GBV risks and vulnerabilities in the targeted Areesha Subdistrict , and Hassakah (Shadada) will be conducted. This assessment will consider factors such as social norms, power dynamics, displacement, and access to basic services. It will provide critical insights into the prevalence and forms of GBV in the project areas. 4. Risk Mitigation Strategies: Based on the identified protection risks and vulnerabilities, appropriate risk mitigation strategies will be developed and integrated into project activities. These strategies may include community engagement and mobilization, awareness-raising campaigns, capacity-building initiatives, and the establishment of referral mechanisms to ensure that women and girls can access timely and appropriate suppor. Before starting the project, a detailed and accurate plan will be prepared to follow up the project activities and clarify the details of the indicators and the method of measuring them. The team will share the weekly follow-up reports with the project manager and the administrative team so that they can take appropriate action when necessary.The Monitoring and Evaluation Section will be responsible for the follow-up operations through the periodic review of attendance records. It will also compare the indicators that show the performance of the beneficiaries before starting the systematic activities with the final evaluation of them after each activity.Evaluation processes: The results that show the development of the beneficiary will be analyzed on a phased basis, as the follow-up and evaluation team will conduct interviews with the beneficiaries and review the pre- and post-evaluation forms, and their other comments will be analyzed, including the results of the focus groups, and accordingly we will issue our reports per month and we share it with the partner.Accountability: We use the Community Accountability Reporting Mechanisms (CARM) system which is located on the SALAM.org websites, we receive comments and complaints through WhatsApp, email, and compliant boxes that are located on different project sites. The boxes can only be accessed by the MEAL coordinator. This will ensure that beneficiaries provide comments and express their concerns and suggestions, their comments will be reviewed and addressed on at least a weekly basis and those comments will be included in our periodic reports to improve the implementation of activities and services.Our organization is committed to the methodology of documenting and learning from the results of our activities. This process will result in analyzes and useful observations summarized in periodic reports on the results and effects of participation in project activities on beneficiaries when participating in any activity in the center.
