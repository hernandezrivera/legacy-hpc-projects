Project Summary

The project 'CSCEP' has been designed for different stakeholders directly or indirectly. Children, Adolescents and Youths (4-24) are the main target group of this project. Beside this the parents, teachers, LCMC members and the community leaders are also the beneficiaries of this project. Through this project we will have to cover 6,000 learners in 75 Learning centres, 12 Camps and 36,000 beneficiaries directly and indirectly in the Rohingya Camps for awareness raising, capacity build up for any emergency preparedness, continuing education and disinfectant measures. We also trained up the teachers, parents, caregivers, LCMC members, community leaders, project officers to run the project smoothly as well as community mobilization and continuing education. Not only this but also we will build up the capacity of the different stakeholders and the project personnel for emergency preparedness. To run the project smoothly different interventions will be taken place.
Interventions:
Using technology:
Now a days the most powerful bat of disseminating messages for education and awareness raising as well as capacity building is to use technology. To raise awareness and capacity building for the COVID-19 in this project will prepare or made some audio visuals on different topics such as hand washing, social distance, personal cleanliness, and nutritional foods to increase immunity system to prevent against the virus etc. On the other hand some educational audio or video or audio video clips (stories, rhymes, awareness and emergency preparedness issues etc.) for the learners according to their Workbooks and learners easily use it on their houses through audio and/or video players to continue their learning.
Set up awareness board/Digital board:
Some awareness board will be set up in the Camps prime location regarding COVID- 19.Rohingya and English language will be given priority for the messages. Messages will be in written form with relevant pictures.
Posters:
Posters on different awareness raising issues, emergency preparedness will be prepared and distribute to the community.
Social mobilizer:
Appoint social mobilizer from the Rohingya community to deliver the awareness messages and the habit of personal cleanliness to follow the rules of hygiene.
Providing health and hygiene materials:
Health and hygiene materials such as soap, masks, gloves, antiseptics etc. will be provided for the learners, teachers, supervisors, project personnel and community people especially for the families of the learners to ensure safety.
Disinfectant process for LCs:
After reopening the LCs and MPCs disinfectant process will be taken place for every LCs and MPCs to ensure the safe and hygienic place for the learners, teachers and associated with all.
Prepare demo class:
Demo classes will be prepared for the learners by the BLI and Host Teachers on each subject topic for Level I-IV. This demo classes will be in audio format. Learners will listen through an audio speaker and do the tasks accordingly.
Para teacher/ Mentor:
Appoint Para teacher / Mentor to follow up the students learning during the closure of LCs. He/ She will follow up at least 10 learners nearby him/her by maintaining social distance.
Parenting education:
Parenting education is a must to develop learners at home. Through this project parents will receive different orientation and training on COVID-19 and how to engage their children in learning. If the parents are well aware about their children future only then they will involve themselves in learning. So this intervention will help the learner to continue their education from home.

Objectives

The main objective of the project is to continuous support in the education of the learners (Level I-IV), create awareness on COVID-19 through social mobilizing and parenting education (Objective 1,2 &3). The project is also aiming at disinfectant issues on Learning Centres, communities as well as providing materials for the learners, teachers and project officers (Objective 2 & 3). Not only this but also taking preparedness on emergency issues after this situation through different orientation and training (Objective 4).
