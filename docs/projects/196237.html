Project Summary

The main goal of the project is to deliver flexible humanitarian assistance (regular Multi-purpose cash MPC) to the most vulnerable IDP people in the subdistricts Azaz and Afrin in Aleppo and Maarat Tamsrin in Idleb. It is planned to assist nearly 1300 HH (6500 Men, Women, Boys, Girls) through multi-purpose cash assistance to cover their minimum survival needs. The value of the cash is planned to be $100 per Household (HH) per month for 3 rounds.
The beneficiaries will be selected according to the need and vulnerability in coordination with civil authorities to reach the most vulnerable HHs, and 100% verified by the MEAL team in the targeted areas based on the vulnerability and eligibility criteria including displacement duration criteria since the newly IDPs are the most vulnerable groups to the shocks. Also, the project will prioritize the women-headed households as well as the households with children, disabilities, and pregnant, and lactating women.
The financial service providers in the targeted area will be assessed and contracted for the redemption process. Market assessments will be conducted in the target areas. Considering the price of basic food commodities remains volatile and is subject to speculation, and disruptive events, specifically displacement, COVID-19 and Cholera have harmful and immediate consequences on the households’ economy and may result in reduced consumption and exclusion, requiring the delivery of unconditional/unrestricted cash assistance that gives households the agency to prioritize their most immediate needs. Therefore, the activity will help them to increase their food security and their most urgent NFI and WASH needs while reducing negative coping strategies.
Prior to the distribution, a rapid market assessment in terms of availability of the food items, prices, and accessibility will be conducted to make sure the local markets are functioning and able to replenish stocks without causing a shortage in supply and to ensure all beneficiaries have good and equal accessibility to the different goods and services.
Accountability to beneficiaries’ is ensured through three main channels: (i) redress and grievance mechanisms; (ii) Post Distribution Monitoring Surveys (PDMS) and focused group discussions (FGDs) as needed; (iii) on-site monitoring activities.

3 - Needs

The main goal of the project is to deliver immediate humanitarian assistance to the most vulnerable IDP people in/off camps and members of host communities (HC) in Azaz and Afrin in Aleppo and Maarat Tamsrin in Idleb, who has been suffering from the deterioration in their ability to meet basic needs as the food and basic need prices increased by 19% since last years according to Joint Market Monitoring Initiative (JMMI) Oct 2022, especially under the lack of or insufficient income or job opportunities leading to negative coping mechanisms including dept and selling important assets according to RNA conducted by SDI in Nov 2022.It is planned to assist nearly 1300 families through multi-purpose cash assistance to cover their needs. The value of the cash is planned to be $100 per Household (HH) per month for 3 rounds. The vendors in the targeted area will be assessed and contracted for cash distributions. Considering the price of basic food commodities remains volatile and is subject to speculation, and disruptive events, specifically displacement and COVID-19 have harmful and immediate consequences on the households’ economy and may result in reduced consumption and exclusion, requiring the adoption of a framework of priorities and actions that are focused on livelihoods. Therefore, the activity will help them to increase their food security and meet their most urgent NFI and WASH needs while reducing negative coping strategies.

4 - Implementation of activities

The proposed intervention is designed to cover the basic food needs and important WASH and NFI needs of IDPs and vulnerable HC members through the distribution of multi-purpose cash assistance worth $100. Following the necessary approvals obtained from local authorities in A’zaz and Afrin, coordination and consultation with the affected community and relevant stakeholders such as vendors and Local Councils will be made to ensure information sharing and accountability. Moreover, market assessment and vendor assessment will be made to ensure no harm, feasibility of cash, availability of stock and replenishment capacity. Beneficiaries will be selected according to preset criteria according to need and vulnerability and inclusion of all gender and age groups including PWDs. Following to the assessment and vetting of financial service providers, contracts will be done with them to conduct the cash distributions. Each family will receive their cash on a monthly basis for 3 rounds. Throughout the project, Covid-19 measurements will be applied by all stakeholders. Beneficiaries will be able to submit complaints and suggestions through the complaints boxes and hotline (WhatsApp), and through the M&E field teams.
