Project Summary

The proposed project will provide durable solutions to chronic and recurrent water problems by providing adequate safe water, sanitation facilities and hygiene education targeting 66,000 beneficiaries (32,340 male and 33,660 female) - without double counting. 60,000 beneficiaries will be reached through water supply services. Specifically, 24,000 beneficiaries will benefit through water provision (18,000 in Mogadishu and 6,000 in Lower Shabelle), 21,000 will benefit through construction of mega rain water catchment pan in Bardhere, 13,800 will be reached through construction of water treatment unit in Bardhere, and 1,200 will be reached through construction of 3 berkerds in Somaliland.
On sanitation, 21,000 beneficiaries will be reached through constriction of 700 latrines (250 Bardhere, 150 Mogadishu, 200 Baidoa and 100 Lower Shabelle). 15,000 beneficiaries will be discounted from the total since the same beneficiaries will benefit from water facilities. On hygiene promotion, 7000 AWD kits (2,500 in Bardhere, 1,500 in Mogadishu, 2000 in Baidoa and 1000 kits in Lower Shabelle) will be distributed to reach 42,000 beneficiaries. Each kit will comprise 200 sachets of aqua tabs, 2 jerry cans and 12 pieces of soap each weighing 200g to give 2400g. All hygiene promotion beneficiaries will be discounted from the total to avoid double counting.
In Gedo region, Bardhere, the water treatment unit is aimed at improving the water quantity and quality will comprise the intake unit, flocculation chamber, sedimentation chamber, chlorination unit, elevated tower and distribution system. The intake unit will comprise a suction pump fitted with a flexible pipe with a floater extending to the river. Water will be delivered to the flocculation unit where aluminium sulphate is added and mixed .Water will then flow to the sedimentation unit which allows settling of large flocs. Water then flows to the chlorination unit where chlorination occurs before it is pumped to a 50 cubic elevated tower through a 2km pipe before distribution occurs through four the water kiosks constructed within the IDP camp. Water will also be pumped to existing elevated RC tank 12meters high with a capacity of 130 cubic and distributed through and existing water distribution system to serve host communities. ; On sanitation concern plans to construct 250 latrines in the IDP camps. The latrines will be fitted with hand washing facilities aimed at promoting handwashing after use of the latrines and at critical times for AWD/Cholera prevention. In Belet Xawa, Concern will construct a mega water pan with an improved sand filtration system. The water pan will be lined with a geotextile material to prevent seepage. The Mega Water pan will have a holding capacity of 26,965,000 liters capacity and expected to have water in all the seasons. The water pan will be fenced and provided with a drawing point and fitted with water pump. Animal troughs will also be provided for use. On hygiene, the project propose hygiene education through trained Community Health Promoters. The project will recruit and train hygiene promoters to disseminate hygiene messages during house to house visit; mass hygiene promotion awareness using radio messaging, hygiene messages on bill-boards located in strategic locations; distribution of hygiene kits will be made to the most vulnerable members of the community; promotion of house hold water treatment through distribution of water purification tablets for rural communities
In Banadir and Bay (lower Shabelle) region, concern will provide water to 24000 IDPs through pipelines. Water will be supplied through existing pipelines and where possible extension of pipelines will be made to ensure beneficiaries get water within a minimum distance of 500 meters. Private water companies owning boreholes near the IDP locations will provide water. All the water points will be metered and payment will be made based on the actual amount of water consumed by the beneficiaries.
In Somaliland, 3 berkerds will be constructed to reach 1200 beneficiaries. The berkerds will be constructed in strategic locations to ensure maximum volume is captured during the rains. The water harvested will be utilized during the dry season.
In order to ensure sustainability of water facilities, Concern will work with the community and local authorities to identify and train a private company managing water in Bardhere. The Private Company in Bardhere and management committee in Belet Xawa & Somaliland will be trained on management, operation and maintenance of the water supply system. The trained teams will then be issued with operation and maintenance tools for water treatment unit in Bardhere and water pan in Belet Xawa and equipment. The committee and private company will be then continue the operations after the end of the project. This will be done in close collaboration with the local authorities and leaders, the ministry of water and other stakeholders.
To ensure environmental sustainability for the water infrastructure, the operators will be trained on sludge management. Water treatment unit will be designed to have score pipes that will flush accumulated sludge from the system to a soak away pit to ensure there is no environmental pollution. Further, water point locations will be identified based on sanitary survey and absence of source contamination within 30 meters radius.
In order to ensure the infrastructure will continue to deliver water in cases of flooding, the water treatment unit and water points will be sited on a higher ground free from flooding. Beneficiaries will access water at water points delivered through pipeline to avoid any cases of contamination. The Mega water pan will have a raised draw off well, fitted with a hand pump and linked to the Mega water pan through pipe. This will ensure beneficiaries access water even in times of flooding and the source is not contaminated by flooding waters.
Concern recently did a pilot of a water treatment unit in Bardhere which was successful.. Bardhere town is served by private water companies providing water at a fee to residents through pipeline, The water treatment unit proposed is an upscale of the existing system and is aimed at providing a treatment unit to major water supply companies in the town with the aim of reducing cases of AWD and water related diseases in Bardhere.

Needs

The main water source in Bardhere district is the River Juba. Five private companies in Bardheere town have piped water directly from the river into elevated tanks from which they supply the town through water kiosks and individual connections. This water does not undergo any form of treatment and is extremely contaminated due to the poor sanitary condition. As a result of water scarcity and use of unsafe water from the river, AWD/Cholera cases have been on the rise since January 2017. A total of 403 cases and 5 deaths were reported from Garbaharey, Bardhere and Luuq districts.Belet Hawa District is a pastoralist livelihood zone, where communities have lost their livelihood assets to the drought and some have been rendered destitute with no access to income nor food. Gedo Region hosts 750,000 inhabitants, half of which are concentrated in its main city, Beled Xaawo. Rural riverine populations collect water directly from the river while distant pastoralist villages rely primarily on open catchments and underground water tanks that harvest rainwater. However, the few surface water sources available normally dry up very soon after the rains thus the community remains without water during the frequent drought seasons. AWD risk is very high in the region due to severe water shortage, poor sanitation and hygiene practices, especially in IDP settlements. All these factors have contributed to characterize the region as ‘extremely vulnerable’ according to the WASH cluster analysis in May 2017. Mogadishu and Lower Shabelle receive high number of IDPs as a result of conflict. High numbers of displaced people often leads to high public health risk. Concern seeks to address this by provision of water, sanitation and hygiene services. Pastrolist families in Somaliland are affected by extreme water shortages caused by drought. Concern seeks to assist affected families through construction of Bekerds.

Activities

In Bardhere, One water treatment systems will be constructed by Concern at a strategic sites within Bardeere town and adjacent to Juba river. Water will be pumped to the flocculation chamber and flow in the rest of the system by gravity and chlorinated in a 150M3 ground tank. Water from the ground tank shall be pumped to an elevated tank 50M3 and 130M3 respectively then distributed by gravity to the communal water point located at strategic part of the town. In Belet Xawa, one mega rain water catchment water pan will be constructed at a strategic location. The mega water pan will have a capacity holding capacity of 26, 965,000 liters and expected to have water in all the seasons. In in Mogadishu and Lower Shabelle, water provision will be made through pipeline to the affected IDPs. 3000HH and 1000HH will be targeted in Mogadishu and Lower Shabelle respectively including 5 institutions in Mogadishu. Three berkerds will also be constructed in Somaliland to serve 1200 people. Distribution of Household Water treatment to 7000 households: This activity aimed at promoting household water treatment preventing AWD/Cholera cases in cholera hotspot areas. Distribution of water treatment tablets will target communities at risk of contracting AWD/Cholera due to poor hygiene conditions.Construction of 700 new Latrine units: This activity aims at providing new latrines to 21,000 individuals. The latrines will be raised at least 0.5m above ground level to prevent flooding, will be lined, have lockable doors and the superstructure made of iron sheets. The latrines will be equipped with hand washing facilities to promote handwashing aimed at improving health. Distribution of 7000 Hygiene Kits: As part of the AWD/Cholera preparedness and response and as a way of promoting hygiene among the target population, A total of 7000 Kits will be procured and distributed. Community Hygiene Promotion: Training of 120 Hygiene promoters is aimed to improve the knowledge and skills of CHPs.

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

The program will prioritize safety and dignity and avoid causing harm to beneficiaries by construction water points in agreed locations with the beneficiaries. Further, while distributing hygiene kits the program will identifying central locations to serve as distribution points. These locations will be agreed together with the community representatives this will be aimed to ensure beneficiaries do not traverse long distances or pass through conflict zones to access distribution points. Distributions of hygiene kits will take place in daylight hours giving beneficiaries sufficient time to travel home before nightfall. Design and construction of WASH facilities will preserve the safety and dignity of its users. Women, girls, boys and girls will be consulted in determining appropriate locations suitable for construction of water points and latrines in order not to expose them to harm. The water facilities will be fenced to provide safety. This will also prevent contamination of the source. Latrines will be fitted with locks on the inside doors to ensure privacy of the users preventing any protection related issues. Water points will be sited within 500m of the dwelling places to ensure beneficiaries do not walk long distances which may expose them to protection issues. The program will use hygiene promotion materials that are culturally and religiously acceptable to the beneficiaries.

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

The project will ensure the gender balance of the distribution teams and WASH committees in order to have women as contact points for other women in the community. As such, at least 30% of the committee will be made up of women. Concern will encourage the use of its hotline to receive any confidential information and of the feedback mechanisms to receive suggestions and complaints in order to improve programming, understand community perceptions, promote beneficiary empowerment and assist in detecting misconduct.

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

The main risk facing the target population is the outbreak of AWD/Cholera cases among the population. This may be caused due to the lack of sanitation facilities leading to open defecation and poor hygienic conditions. Concern will respond rapidly to emerging cases of displacements within the project area providing basic services to the affected. Water provision, Sanitation facilities and AWD kits will be provided during the response. Water quality and quantity will also be monitored to ensure program quality. The program will work closely with the community leaders in both the host community and IDP camps to ensure appropriate allocation of resources. Beneficiaries will be selected based on the vulnerability.
