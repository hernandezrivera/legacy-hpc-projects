Project Summary

This influxes created an invisible crisis due to enormous number of Rohingya entrance which is not yet stopped as started since 25th August (2017), last year. Deprived & packed living situation in camps refugees make a further vulnerability. All have the right to be set up a shelter. Maximum population passed over their night beneath the blue sky where personal & family safety & security are in zero ! Children from all socioeconomic backgrounds, across all ages, religions and cultures suffer violence, exploitation and abuse every day.
AGRAJATTRA will work towards enabling refugees to establish the protection of shelters & infrastructure as site development for better living condition with DRR resilient of Rohingya refugee camp in Bangladesh.
Objective : 1) To establish the provision (New arrivals, Emergency & PWD) of ESK & NFI contingency stocks support to upgrading shelters for better living condition with dignity and safety.
Objective : 2) To Establish and Enhance the existing shelters to improve towards a Transitional or Mid Term Shelters and site improvement of the camps for better living condition with DRR resilient through contributing to reduced suffering and enhanced protection, dignity, and safety.
Objective : 3) To Enhance & improve the social cohesion with DRR resilience in the camps through Capacity & awareness building on DRR, EWS, formation of Rescue team and protection.
Objective : 4) To Promote use of sustainable solutions to reduce impact on the environment through improvement of camps better living condition; Street Solar Lighting, (Biological) protection the slope of hills, Tree Plantation etc.
The following intervention will be selected to achieve the project goal & objectives.
1. Provision of contingency stocks of ESK for new arrivals or any Emergency arises.
2. Provision of Input NFI Kits support (Aluminium Safe Box - 01, PE FOAM - 02, Bed Sheets - 02, Solar light - 01, Umbrella - 02 Pcs) and Support Kits for PWD & elderly HHS.
3. Establish and enhance the existing USK shelters to improve towards Construction of Transitional Shelter Assistance (TSA),
4. Establish and enhance the existing USK shelters to improve towards Construction of Midterm shelter assistance (MSA).
5. Formation of CFW labours groups and awareness session at Camp level on Site improvement work & DRR (Plantation, CFW, protection) etc.
6. A no. of Labour groups of Rohingya people will involve for 25 days with Cash support for Site improvement through Cash for Work/asset. And no. of site improvement work like Earth Work, Infrastructure, like footpaths, drainage channels, flood attenuation areas, community safe spaces, and stabilized steps at camp level.
7. Sensitization of UzDMC, UDMC, WDMC, Camp Management and Organize Training for Capacity & awareness building on DRR, EWS, formation of Rescue team, site development and protection.
8. Formation of Disaster Rescue team and emergency search & Rescue kits for any emergency (Preparedness & rescue) in the camps.
9. Installation of Solar Street Light in the camps.
10. Establish and enhance the Site development in the camps through (Biological) protection slope of hills (Jute mate/bags, Bamboo Woven fencing, Bamboos & Vertiver plantation) etc.
11. Involvement of mass Rohingya people for Plantation of Trees at Camp level etc.
Consequently it will be formed different groups, recruit Volunteers & coordinated with Diverse Service Providers for most outcomes of the project. Rohingya people will get safety and secured life in the camp mainly women, Girls & people in need getting privacy, protection and dignity in their daily life. Most vulnerable refugee people will get preparedness for natural disaster as well as different stakeholders will involved for future Disaster risk. It’ll promote use of sustainable solutions to reduce impact on the environment through improvement of camps for better living condition.

Needs

Since 25th August 2017 Violence in Rakhine State had been driven and Rohingya people crossed over the border (Myanmar - Bangladesh) through pushing which made a large scale and escalating humanitarian crisis. Many NGOs were responding to reach more people in need of life-saving assistance. Hereafter, they are settled in different camps under Ukiah & Tehnaf sub districts under Cox’sBazar, Bangladesh. The speed and scale of the influx had resulted in a critical humanitarian emergency. The people who had arrived in Bangladesh came with very few possessions. They have used the majority of their savings on transportation and constructing a shelter, often out of no more than bamboo and thin plastic. The Rohingya population in Cox’sBazar is highly vulnerable, having fled conflict and experienced severe trauma and now living in extremely difficult conditions. There are 34 Nos. of Makeshift camps which all areas are hills and reserve forest. Rohingya refugee people living slope of hills which are 500 – 600 Feets high as well as the areas are most vulnerable for Bay of Bengal’s TS cyclone, Tsunami, tidal surge, also Flash flood, Land slide, Mud slide and another big threat for Asian elephants. Most of camps are settle down at the several crossing points of the Asian elephants. This influxes created an invisible crisis due to enormous number of Rohingya entrance which is not yet stopped as started since 25th August (2017), last year. Deprived & packed living situation in camps refugees make a further vulnerability. All have the right to be set up a shelter. Maximum population passed over their night beneath the blue sky where personal & family safety & security are in zero ! Children from all socio-economic backgrounds, across all ages, religions and cultures suffer violence, exploitation and abuse every day. Moreover, different aged people especially women & old ages with breast feeding children. The maximum people were starving due to food insecurity & lead vulnerable life having no money to buy other essential things like Health-hygiene kits and dignity kits etc. The Rohingya Refugees have no source to gather Upgrading Shelter Kits (USK), Durable shelters (Transitional or Midterm Shelters) those are the most essential to lead life. So, it is vital to arrange a vast scale disbursement with different things among listed refugees. In another way to cash distribution to identified vulnerable families as an intervention for short range area. Shelters provide secure accommodation for women and girls who are at risk of or have been subjected to violence, although they contribute far more than just a safe place to stay. Shelters provide essential aspects of protection, services and resources which enable women who have experienced abuse and their children to recover from the violence, to rebuild self-esteem, and to take steps to regain a self-determined and independent life. With proper resources, shelters have the capacity to provide the range of protection and support services necessary to help survivors and those at risk of violence to avoid future abuse. Shelters can also contribute to awareness-raising and social change as part of broader efforts to prevent violence against women and girls altogether. The presence of and supports offered by shelters can address some of these barriers by encouraging women and girls to seek assistance. Shelters promote women’s equality and often have a role in speaking out on Systemic issues of discrimination, drawing links between individual women’s experiences and the conditions of women within society that give rise to violence against them. The influence on system responses and advocacy by shelters to promote access to various services is important in assisting women to cope with the extensive range of challenges (from physical and emotional consequences, to financial, legal and social impacts) resulting from the violence committed against them.
