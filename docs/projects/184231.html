Project Summary

The situation in the Banadir and Lower shabelle region remains volatile and uncertain due to limited development coupled with recurring climatic shocks, such as drought and conflict give rise to high levels of need among affected populations. Daynile and Kahda districts IDP sites are hosting a Total of 61,727 Households or 263,783 Individuals, and 50,146HHs or 190,308 individuals respectively were assessed and mapped as per Detailed Site Assessments (DSA) conducted in Banadir in march 2021 by the REACH initiative. Among the IDPs targeted are vulnerable new Displaced populations of around 7,686 and 4,995 individuals in Daynile and Kahda whom they arrived in the past three (3) months, which is equivalent to 12,681 are displaced between December 2020 - March 2021 due to drought and conflict. In this, almost 60 -70 percent of the total population in kahda and Daynile are severe and extreme conditions for WASH intervention as per the DSA report in April 2021.
The Proposed project will contribute to increased investment to address the water infrastructure development gaps through rehabilitation of existing water sources and expanding the supply networks to improve access by reducing access in needs to safe and clean water and improved sanitation of most vulnerable and underserved communities living in floods affected areas in Daynile, Kahda districts in Mogadishu and Merka, Wanlaweyn and Afgooye district of Lower Shebelle region. A total of 18,700 flood affected men, women and children including the 15% disabilities, marginalized communities and IDPs will bene t safe drinking water and will have access equally throughout the project period. SOYDA in collaboration with the local authority, partnership with main water vendors in rural villages in target districts, will provide emergency water voucher to the 7,440 (1808 men, 1,536 women, 2,048 boys and 2,048 girls) targeted beneficiaries including people with disabilities and marginalized communities in the targeted IDP camps, which they can redeem for water at the specified water vendors (water trucks). The action also includes to hire water truck of a capacity of 6,000 liters of safe water to the targeted locations. Each voucher can be used to redeem specified quantity of safe water of 7.5 liters per person per day for a period of 30 days. The project will build the capacity of 10 WASH Committees (composing 4 men and 3 women per Committee) shared equally men and women on operation and maintenance of developed water infrastructures shall be throughout the project implementation period, to ensure the sustainability of the developed water facilities after the project is faced out. SOYDA will make sure that men, women, girls and boys including people with disabilities and marginalized community have equal access to the rehabilitated/constructed WASH services. SOYDA will upgrade/rehabilitate two boreholes equipped with hybrid to improve its water quality and reduce its running cost, in order to receive the community their water needs at an affordable cost. SOYDA will replace the engine powered boreholes in to hybrid system and construct 8 water kiosks nearer to the IDP settlements supplied water through piping network to avoid the risk of rape against women and girls in target districts. The project will provide reliable and sustainable access to environmental sanitation through construction of 200 IDP latrines in target districts. The latrines will be gender separated and flood proof with lockable doors and hand washing basins. construction of latrines will be implemented through market-based programming.
The project will ensure the participation of people with disability in all decision-making processes and ensure WASH- related information is in formats that people with different types of disabilities can understand and as well will consider disability groups in the design, monitoring and evaluation of development and emergency WASH interventions through disability mainstreaming
