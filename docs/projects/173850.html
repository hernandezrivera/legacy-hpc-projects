1 - Needs

Ngala L.G.A has a large number of female out of school children both in IDPs and returnees. In general, the number of out of school children is alarming in the Northern L.G.As of Borno State, Nigeria. Especially the girl child who is always seen as inferior and so her education is of little or no importance. The need to access quality education is very paramount to them. Teachers lack of skills and teaching method is another need that can be addressed through training by education experts.Sensitization, psychosocial support is also key to boost the moral and bring them out of the trauma they went through.

2 - Activities

The choice of the activities are largely based on the strategic planning for the out of school children, particularly females in the insurgency affected L.G.As. The line activities are aimed in addressing some of the challenges faced by the large number of out school children in the L.G.A. we also believe that the activities will enhance enrolment and retention in our schools. The targets are chosen because we are familiar with the culture, tradition and mindset of the target population. Some of our members hail from these communities. We also have IT experts that have put in place a way of assessing and delivering a quality well execute project
