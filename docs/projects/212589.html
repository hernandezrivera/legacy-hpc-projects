Project Summary

In response to the pressing challenges faced by vulnerable communities in North-East Syria (NES), a comprehensive project is proposed to enhance resilience and livelihood opportunities amidst conflict and climate change. Over a targeted a year long period, the project aims to address key needs identified in Al-Hasaka's sub-districts and several sub-districts of Ar-Raqqa governorate through integrated interventions.
Livestock Support and Agricultural Innovation:
The project will provide vital support to 1200 livestock breeders, including medical kits to prevent diseases and increase productivity. Additionally, innovative approaches such as Azolla hydroponic systems will be piloted to offer low-cost fodder for livestock, reducing the economic burden on breeders. Mobile clinics and vaccination campaigns will ensure the health and productivity of more than 50,000 cows, sheep, and goats, while community animal health workers will be trained to respond to potential disease outbreaks.
Empowering Small Businesses and Strengthening Value Chains:
To bolster economic resilience, 500 vulnerable female-headed households will receive vocational training and equipment for dairy processing, enabling them to establish sustainable businesses. Business management training will further enhance dairy production and entrepreneurial skills, while tailored support will be provided to micro home-based businesses. Market linkages between livestock producers and processors will be facilitated to strengthen the dairy products value chain and improve income for smallholder breeders.
Agricultural Rehabilitation and Sustainable Development:
In NES, the project will introduce modern irrigation methods like drip irrigation to 250 HHs and support greenhouse vegetable production to mitigate the effects of climate change and water scarcity to 250 FHHs. Rehabilitation of irrigation infrastructure and the establishment of Water User Associations will ensure community ownership and sustainability.
Fruit Trees Associations will be supported to promote collective action and economic recovery targeting 200 fruit growers, while revitalizing the beekeeping sector will provide stable income opportunities for 100 rural households.
Cash assistance may increase risks of GBV through potential exploitation by service providers or other parties in the community, or increasing domestic violence against women and girls.
Mitigation:
Implement PSEA policy for employees, clarifying procedures in case of SEA.
Conduct staff orientation on PSEA and distribute brochures and posters at redemption points.
Investigate cases of SEA and ensure appropriate actions are taken.
Carry out strict beneficiaries' verification and interviews.
Provide specialized response services by well-trained referral facilitators.
In the proposed project for vulnerable communities in North-East Syria, inclusivity is a fundamental aspect, including the inclusion and participation of Persons with Disabilities (PwD). Here's how the project will specifically address risks and barriers to PwD inclusion and participation
All aspects of livestock support, including the provision of medical kits and piloting hydroponic systems, will be made accessible to PwD. This may involve providing training materials and equipment that are tailored to different disabilities, ensuring that facilities are physically accessible, and employing inclusive practices in training and support sessions.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The proposed project in North-East Syria aims to enhance resilience and livelihood opportunities amidst conflict and climate change. Key components include livestock support and agricultural innovation, empowering small businesses, agricultural rehabilitation, and disaster preparedness. Livestock support involves providing medical kits and piloting innovative hydroponic systems for fodder. Empowering small businesses includes vocational training for female-headed households in dairy processing, with a focus on market linkages. Agricultural rehabilitation efforts will introduce modern irrigation methods and support greenhouse vegetable production. Disaster preparedness initiatives include establishing groups for training on topics like firefighting and emergency response, as well as distributing disaster management kits. Mitigation strategies are in place to address risks such as gender-based violence, including implementing PSEA policies, staff orientation, beneficiaries' verification, and providing specialized response services.
