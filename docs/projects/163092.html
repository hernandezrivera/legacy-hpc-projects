Project Summary

The project will be implemented in Chimanimani and Chipinge Districts where the International Rescue Committee (IRC) has provided water supply, sanitation and hygiene promotion to communities affected by Cyclone Idai and the current drought. The proposed project will target 40 000 being 15 000 people in Chimanimani district recovering from the effects of cyclone Idai and another 25 000 affected by the drought in Chipinge District. The target population in the target districts require support with water supply systems and hygiene promotion. The IRC will seek to provide WASH services to affected communities with a focus on vulnerable groups including women and girls, people living with disabilities, child headed and women headed households.

1 - Activities

According to the Zimbabwe 2020 Humanitarian Needs Overview, 71 076 people in the target districts do not have access to safe water. In addition, 12% of the population in the rural areas of the two districts practice open defecation and 47% of the population have no access to safe water. The situation has been exacerbated by cyclone Idai that affected the two districts in March 2019 and has further deteriorated due to the effects of the poor 2018/2019 agricultural season. A further drought forecasted for Southern Africa during the 2019/2020 season will further accelerate the dire situation as access to water deteriorates in both rural and urban areas. The IRC will seek to provide WASH services to affected communities with a focus on vulnerable groups including women and girls, people living with disabilities, child headed and women headed households. The IRC will carry out the following activities: (1) Rehabilitation of water supply systems with a focus on motorisation (2) Hygiene promotion and (3) Distribution of WASH related NFIs.In the absence of an urgent humanitarian intervention, WASH related diseases are likely to increase and these include cholera and typhoid which are already a high risk in Zimbabwe given they have been recurrent outbreaks. There is need for rehabilitation of water points with particular consideration for solarisation and protection of natural springs, construction of sanitation facilities to eliminate open defecation, accelerated hygiene messaging and complimentary distribution of WASH NFIs to enable proper hygiene behaviors.

2 - Indicators

The project success will be measured through the number of people reached with safe water, the number of households reached with key health and hygiene messages and the number of people reached with WASH hygiene kits. Through its Monitoring Evaluation, Accountability and Learning (MEAL) team the IRC will gather data through direct observation, post distribution surveys, field monitoring and endline survey.
