Project Summary

The civilian population of Donetska NGCA, whose homes were destroyed or partially damaged during the conflict, continue to live in poor sanitation and limited amenities. Most of them have their own households and apartments requiring urgent restoration of the heating circuit and medium repairs, which they cannot afford due to financial difficulties. In this situation, people of vulnerable categories suffer the most.The minimum pension for vulnerable people is UAH 1,839 ($ 65), of which utility bills are in the order of UAH 500 ($ 17.5) from the benefit received. For the purchase of foodstuffs, 800 UAH ($ 28) remains, for the purchase of medicines 450 UAH ($ 15.7), and for disabled persons of the 1st category, diapers are needed, the cost of which is 450 UAH. ($ 15.7) Also, many of the residents of Donetsk NGCA cannot receive a pension on the territory of Ukraine. Based on these calculations, vulnerable people have no money left for the restoration of the heating circuit (replacement of damaged windows, doors) and for medium repairs. People do not want to leave their only home with the hope of rebuilding it (at least partially), with the help of humanitarian organizations and local administrations. Pensioners continue to live in extremely difficult conditions, with windows clogged with plywood, in houses and apartments with low temperatures that do not meet standards during the cold period. All these factors, especially in a pandemic, have the most negative impact on the health of people with disabilities and older people with pre-existing chronic diseases. Moreover, these factors significantly increase the risks to life, given that the immunity of people in these vulnerable categories is too weakened due to malnutrition and the inability to obtain the necessary drugs to sufficiently maintain health. CO "ICF "MIRA" plans to restore the heating circuit in 150 households and provide each household owner with a hygiene kit (150 pcs) for sanitary and hygienic treatment of premises in order to prevent COVID-19. These measures will not only improve the quality of life of the vulnerable population, but also significantly reduce the risks of diseases.
Thus, assistance will be provided to 375 beneficiaries (people 60+ and disabled people). Of these, the share of female heads of households will be 63%, and men - 37%.
The implementation of measures to restore the heating circuit is extremely urgent, since these humanitarian response measures will not only reduce the risks of disease, but also save many lives of people in vulnerable categories during and after the pandemic. Over the course of 4 years, CO "ICF "MIRA" has successfully implemented a number of projects to restore the heating circuit and provided assistance to 1285 beneficiaries in the 0-5 km zone. But some of the beneficiaries of vulnerable categories living in the 0-20 km zone are still forced to live in damaged apartments and houses, since humanitarian response measures to restore the heating circuit of households affected by the conflict in this geographic zone are only partially implemented.
CO "ICF "MIRA"has extensive experience in the successful implementation of such humanitarian projects in the NGCA, each of which is obligatorily accredited, as well as highly qualified personnel who know and adhere to the principles of working with international organizations, local administrations and beneficiaries.
The organization has clear standards and internal policies for humanitarian activities on the territory of the NGCA, developed in accordance with the requirements of international partners and the recommendations of the clusters, all employees are familiar with them and strictly adhere to them.

1 - Needs

Over the past 4 years, CO "ICF "MIRA" HAS successfully implemented a number of projects to restore the heat circuit and provided assistance to 1,285 beneficiaries in the 0-5 km zone. However, some of the beneficiaries of vulnerable categories living in the 0-20 km zone are still forced to live in apartments and houses damaged as a result of the fighting . Humanitarian response measures to restore the heating circuit of households affected by the conflict in this geographical area have only been partially implemented. Urgent intervention is required to ensure the necessary living conditions, through the restoration of the heat circuit.Moreover, there is a need to provide people of vulnerable categories with hygiene kits to prevent problems caused by the coronavirus pandemic, in order to reduce the risk of disease among the population living in the NGCA territory.To prevent the spread of COVID-19 and support the forced self-isolation of people of vulnerable categories, CO "ICF "MIRA" plans to provide assistance to 150 vulnerable households in the form of a hygiene kit, which includes: toothbrushes, toothpaste, toilet soap, shampoo, washing powder, whiteness, dishwashing gel, toilet paper and rubber gloves for cleaning rooms. When forming the hygiene kit, the recommendations of the Wash cluster were used: covid-19 small Family Kit, designed for the needs of elderley-Eastern Ukraine (last updated on April 8, 2020).
