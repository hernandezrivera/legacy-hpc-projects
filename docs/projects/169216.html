Project Summary

Following Beirut’s blast, the Ministry of Agriculture has reported a complete destruction of the plant and animal quarantine offices in the Port of Beirut, including all the necessary equipment needed for inspection. These two offices provide essential inspection and control functions to ensure that all plant and animal materials imported through the port satisfy Sanitary and Phyto-Sanitary (SPS) requirements before such materials are authorized to enter the country. The proper functioning of these offices will ensure that plant and animal diseases and pests are not brought into the country in order to protect the Lebanese agriculture sector form the spread of such diseases and pests. The current proposal intends to rehabilitate the premises through setting up new prefabricated containers within the Port of Beirut with the provision of equipment. The urgent re-operationalization of these offices will be critical component of rebuilding trade logistics to ensure the uninterrupted flow of safe and healthy food to the country as well as to the flow of food exports.
In addition, The Ministry of Economy and Trade has reported the complete destruction of the wheat laboratory in the Port of Beirut. This laboratory serves a critical function to inspect the quality of all imported wheat as well as ensuring its proper storage conditions. The replacement and operationalization of this laboratory (including equipment and prefab offices) is an urgent requirement to ensure that all wheat imports are properly inspected before being allowed to enter the country.

Plan fields

1 - Needs

1- Provision of prefabricated containers for plant and animal health for plant and animal quarantine inspection at port of Beirut.2- Provision of inspection equipment and tools necessary for the inspection of plant and animal quarantine premises at the Port of Beirut3- Provision of prefabricated containers for the Wheat laboratory at the Port of Beirut with related basic laboratory equipment for initial screening of the wheat and grains received at the port to maintain a good quality control.

2 - Activities

For the plant and animal quarantine premises: the existing 150 square meters office will be replaced with new prefabricated offices with the provision of all related equipment (IT equipment, office and furniture). In addition, the project will procure and install all necessary inspection tools and equipment required to restore the full operationalization of these facilities necessary for maintaining inspection of all raw food items (plant and animal) entering the country. For the sake of conducting inspection of imported wheat, a sampling unit structure will be purchased to allow accurate and representative sampling is done and then the lot delivered to the inspection office for analysis. In addition, the project will replace the two refrigerated vans, destroyed by the blast, which are essential for transporting samples to be tested at the main laboratories of the Ministry of Agriculture and of the Lebanese Agricultural Research Institute. For the Wheat Laboratory: New prefabricated containers will be procured and installed in order to set up the laboratory and to accommodate the qualified staff of the Ministry of Economy and Trade responsible of operating it. In addition, and in order to maintain the good quality control of stored wheat, the basic laboratory equipment used for the initial screening of the grains received at the Port of Beirut will be procured. These tools include among others: (i) sampling and testing tools (infrared thermometric gun, probe thermometer, manual sampling probe), (ii) humidity and moisture testing tools (grains laboratory mills), and (iii) specific weight testing tools (precision balance, Nilema hectolitre, calibration weights), (iv) weevil infestation and continuous monitoring tools (shaker, temperature monitoring hygrometer, fridge for keeping samples and aluminium phosphide leak detectors).
