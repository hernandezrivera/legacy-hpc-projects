Project Summary

WASH Hygiene Promotion: Support to girls and women, PSN, IDP, and hosting communities in hygiene NFI in the resettlement and or hosting areas by (i) distribution of women’s dignity and hygiene kits, and hygiene promotion as preventive measures against diarrheal diseases and COVID -19, (ii) access to water in quantity and quality for families, Access to appropriate sanitation and hygiene facilities, including management of solid waste and (iii) safe programming.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project activities will focus on the WASH component, on the distribution of water in trucks in accommodation centers, hygiene promotion campaigns with different focus groups, distribution of dignity and hygiene kits, home visits to raise awareness among families to promote good practices of hygiene through center-level activists. Construction of public emergency latrines through the number of beneficiaries in the centers. On regular basis, safe programming risk assessments will be conducted to address the population’s needs and safety. Key targeted groups are Men, Women, Boys, Girls, PSN, IDP and host communities.For phase 1, we are planning to respond in one district (Metuge), but we acknowledge that due to unstable circumstances, we might extend the response to other districts, like Ancuabe, Montepuez and Chiúre. 36.000 (12.000 IDP beneficiaries and 24.000 host communities)

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Metuge:• Safe programming risk assessments • Mapping numbers and registration of beneficiaries disaggregated by sex, age and type of vulnerability.• Hygiene promotion sessions• Volunteers and Community Leaders training• Distribution of NFI• Identification of trucking water sources and delivery points• Construction of public emergency sanitation and bathing facilities.• Construction of 3 mini-photovoltaic water system Water Committee training

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

• The project will ensure that all personnel will undergo induction on PSEA, CP and Code of Conduct prior to engage in activities.• The project will have a PSEA and CP focal person.• During the meetings communities will be informed about expected staff behavior and how to report SEA and CP concerns.• Regular Protection and PSEA risk assessments will be conducted for all activities.• Sensitive data will be handled with the utmost confidentiality and forwarded to competent entities.• Communities and beneficiaries will always be informed that the aid is free and any case or tentative of fraud needs to be reported via available mechanisms.• To prevent staff or community illness, the necessary equipment will be provided to mitigate covid-19 and other disease risks during the project. Special attention will be given to children's well-being for early referral.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

• Community leaders and members of the beneficiary groups will be involved in the identification and registration activities and project design events • Community complaint mechanisms will be put in place • Community safety or well being will be regularly assessed • Community members involved in the project will be encouraged to speak about their perceptions of safety and consulted for their views about what aspects of the project make them feel safe/unsafe Community members will be assessed whether they feel safe to file a sensitive complaint (gender and vulnerability)

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Consultations will be done individuals with individuals living with disabilities and the elderly to identify specific adaptation needs for infrastructure. Infrastructure will be adapted for these needs.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Sanitation and waste disposals management: the construction of public emergency sanitation, promotion of hygiene practices while dealing with waste disposals will help, not only the health of people but also to protect the environment around the accommodation centers. We will promote the creation of waste disposal collection points within the resettlement area.
