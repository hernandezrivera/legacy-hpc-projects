Project Summary

The project aims to do education and child protection activities in towns and camps, the camps will be visited through 6 school buses which will be equipped and ready to receive children from the age 5 to 12 each bus can accommodate 20 child per session. While another 5 centres will be established in NW Syria.
The curriculum to be used in both is the SLP and recreational PSS activities aiming to do formal + nonformal + ALP + remedial education.
Each school will have:
8 teachers + 3 PSS facilitators in addition to the administrative staff,
while in the bus there will be:
1 driver + 1 education specialist + 1 PSS facilitator + 1 admin staff
The project will extend over 17 month starting by Alp, remedial and engagement activities for drop outs then start to do formal + nonformal education along with PSS
The locations might be as follows:

Each school will accommodate about 250 students,
while each bus will target 20 students per session targeting 4 groups, that means the total beneficiaries of each bus will be 80 students.
Thus, the total beneficiaries of education in addition to CP might be 2000.

4 - Needs

As there is a heavy waves of IDPs and lots of the locals in NW Syria are moving from a place to another due to the heavy bombardments, So olive branch decided to do an assessment in regards the education in the area. From our assessments we could find a lot of people who arrived at the already existing camps and some others who arrived to unformal camps or even trying to establish a new one, in addition to those who arrived in the more safe towns.That all led to more pressure on the educational services if they exist but apparently in almost all the towns the educational service providers needs more support to absorb the heavy IDP waves.We did an assessment in the areas below:-Kafr Takharim-Sarmada-Aldana-Batbou-Harbanoush-Kafr Derian-Beert armanaz-Kafr Ouroq-Ezmarin-Quorqonia-Kafr YahmoulOut from the assessment we got that for the meantime 1500 child aren’t attending any educational activities, so it was clearly required to intervene in education to support the children whom aren’t accommodated in any school or even receiving any educational service leaving the areas threatened to have schools drop outs increasing.So we decided to do ALP + remedial + SLP+ PSS

5 - Implementation of activities

The project will extend over 16 months starting by Alp + remedial and engagement activities for drop outs this activity will start on 1 June 2020 extending over 3 month per cycle each cycle will have 300 child who didn’t attend any educational activity for 3 months at least so they can attend with their peers next semester this activity will be done in the centres and buses the second cycle will be in 1 October 2020 expecting less beneficiaries / 150 at least. while will start to do formal + nonformal education along with PSS will start from 1 January 2021 to 30 September 2020 Monitoring and Evaluation:will develop an implementation plan, performance indicators, and M&E matrices, including appropriate data collection tools to support data flow and analysis. 6 - Protection Risk Analysis

All the direct beneficiaries of the activities are in a serious need for child protection activities to reduce the trauma and war effects which will be dealt with in the PSS activities in addition to the parenting sessions which will raise the awareness about the CP aspects and GBV also. While for the staff the protection, OB is concerned with protection issues that can be faced by our project staff resulting from acts such as violence, mental torture, exposure to conflict trauma, exposure to victims of conflict, and false allegations. We, therefore, have put in place some risk management practices that enable them to effectively conduct their work with those in need. OB also has an organisation wide duty of care obligations towards its staff

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

*Security situation, Bombardments, stopping the work, security changes of which will be mitigated through:-Evacuation plan/ ready-Daily security update-Good location of the center as consulted to best avoiding possible risks-safety and security officer dedicated to follow up with the buses and assure their daily trips are safe *Financial risks: Materials, services prices increase, transfer fees increase, we have a well-established financial control, procurement and anti-fraud policy to ensure the duties are sufficiently segregated for the benefit of transparency, control and avoidance of any frauds. In terms of disruption to our operation due to shortage of required items, we estimate the risk to be low in that the required food items and kitchen tools are available in the market. In addition, the risk is minimized by having more than one supplier to work with OB. *Case referrals system to be not well active in the area-already communicated the protection cluster to make sure of finding the service providers
