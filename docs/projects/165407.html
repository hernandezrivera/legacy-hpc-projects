Project Summary

This project seeks to improve access to agricultural facilities in the project areas through rehabilitation of infrastructure within a comprehensive intervention through the targeted areas, these services will be provided to farmers who have difficulty accessing their farms, especially while providing crop services in the rainy months where it is not possible to access to their farms during these months due to the fragile muddy land structure that absorbs rainwater as the soil in the project areas is of clay type, so these roads will be rehabilitated to be usable even in rainy seasons which will reflect positively on agricultural operations. This will be done through conducting road paving, supplying quarry residue material, these works will be implemented by contracting with a qualified supplier under the supervision of our technical team on the ground, as the main roads leading to the fields will be paved with the using of the oil residue (fuel) in order to increase the quality of the pavement so that layer of soil will be is isolated from rain water, and daily workers will be hired to by cash for work modality, here job opportunities will be secured for the affected people in the project areas where they will be chosen from families that suffer unemployment among their members, large families with low income, members of newly displaced families Where they will have the opportunity have
the income assist them in securing the requirements of their life, including health,
education, Food ...
On the other side, lighting for these ways will be provided to improve visibility, especially
in the early morning and sunset hours, by installing lighting poles with energy-saving
lamps.
Electricity for these columns will be supplied by solar energy systems that will be
installed upon the poles. In this case, a sustainable service will be provided for years to
come and a clean, environmentally friendly energy source. This service will be secured by
contracting with a contractor specializing in the required field

4 - Needs

On January 2020, SECD conducted a Humanitarian Need Assessment in Daret Azza in Aleppo Government and in Jisr-Ash-Shugur in Idleb Government. The assessment applied quantitative and qualitative methods of data collection from the communities through household and KII interviews. Totally, 562 individuals were interviewed while 91% of individuals were male and 9 % were female. 68.63% of total interviewed HHs were Residents .After collecting and analyzing the data , it should be shown that 84 % of respondents reported that they have difficulties accessing to their farms due to the poor condition of the roads leading to fields in general and the inability to use these fields at some times of the year, especially during rain, because it become muddy,and not usable .On other hand, 73 % of respondents reported that they could not go to their fields early or return late at night because of the fear of darkness and the lack of clarity of the lack of lighting.

5 - Implementation of activities

Rehabilition of the roads in the targeted communities: The most vital roads in the targeted areas and the semi-main roads that farmers use to reach their fields were identified to be rehabilitated, as the roads consisting of poor soils for using as roads were chosen to be rehabilitated, needs for that assessed by conducting field visits from the engineering team on the SECD, interviews and meetings were held with local councils in the targeted areas with specialized committees in the targeted communities. Field interviews were also conducted with farmers who owned agricultural lands in those areas in order tomeet the needs. Discussing them in this regard, on basis of that, an action plan was established with quantitative tables shown on the basis of the need in terms of the total areas to be constructed and the needed materials are calculated for that with the estimated costs.Cash for work activities: daily workers will be hired to involve them in the operations of roads contraction securing job opportunities for the people living in these communities a specific vulnerability criteria will be applied to choose those workers like families without employment members, large families with low income, members of newly displaced families Where they will have the opportunity have the income assist them in securing the requirements of their life. Roads lightening: field visits to the sites with surveys through the areas, in conjunction with an assessment of the initial needs for maintenance andpaving of roads, have been conducted.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

The project ensures extensive communication with the community (including leaders,women, youth, and children) to determine the needs that can best be supported by SECD in the implementation period. Additionally, issues such as the hiring of staff and the contracting with vendors will be looked at in a manner that no discriminating against any specific populations will be done (host community populations are intentionally targeted) and by being aware of the existing social dynamics. SECD takes all aspects -gender, and age - into account in its project cycle management: • Assessment: SECDcarries out consulting meetings with women and girls on ERL needs to ensure program strategies that mitigate exposure to risk. Implementation: (1) Women and girls are consulted to ensure they feel and are safe, especially when using ERL services.(2)Adequate numbers of women are involved in the formation of focus group discussions.(3) The female staff is recruited for ERL interventions to mobilize women and girls fromdiverse groups and to facilitate feedback from these groups. Protection Risk Assessments: Risks and mitigation (Mitigating Measure) : Risk (1): A. Increased the risk of intercommunity tension due to varied assistance packages across members. Likelihood: medium, Impact: medium. Mitigating Measure: Use the Cluster guidance on minimum standards and recommended content, Use recommended minimum standards in quality and specification of items, If different to recommended standard, identify and exclude from variance any items particularly prone to create tensions.Risk(2): Risk of sexual exploitation by humanitarian workers (risk of harassment) of women and girls. Likelihood: Low, Impact: high. Mitigating Measures : -SECD team follows SECD Sexual Harassment policy. SECD will establish a confidential complaint mechanism also Code of conduct in all staff contracts Address any SEA complaints.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

SECD conducts a security assessment for its projects by receiving information on security situations from its network of local councils and civil society activists, as well as by remaining continuously apprised of security situations through bulletins released by the international community. For emergency relief projects, when a security situation arises that threatens the safety of staff, the staff is redirected to a nearby, safer location until the area is safe enough to conduct the aid or distribution project. For non-relief projects, an assessment of the security situation is conducted in order to determine the location ofthe project. Risk (1) of overcrowding living conditions and the absence of adequate services might expose to risk vulnerable beneficiaries. Likelihood: medium, Impact: medium. Mitigating Measure: Ensure that adequate space is provided by family, after the completion of the rehabilitation, Raise awareness among the beneficiaries of the dangers of overcrowding, Ensure that a management system is in place for big collective shelters rehabilitation, Consider sphere standards when planning the response, and ensure that management is coordinating services with other actors to cover needs and gaps. Risk (2): Outbreak of conflict prevents access to project locations. Likelihood: low, Impact: high. Mitigating Measure: Lesson learned from 2013 operations found that while conflict occurred, access could be restored within a few days. A cautious approach that plans for these types of delays is expressed in the project work plan. Risk (3): Airplane shelling . Likelihood: medium. Impact: Very High .Mitigating Measure: The risk of Security situation (only heavy shelling) and The risk of the local council buildings being targeted by airstrikes are a very low probability according to The historical information for 9 years ago.Risk (4): child laborlikelihood: mediumImpact: highMitigating Measure: The age limit for admission of employees must be over 18
