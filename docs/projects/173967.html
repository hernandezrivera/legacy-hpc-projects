Project Summary

Strengthening Participatory Organization (SPO) is a rights-based service delivery organization striving to improve the well-being of communities ensuring that citizens have access to & are aware of their rights. SPO has been working consistently in the field of education since 2015, towards achieving SDG-4 & 5 through multiple education projects in Pakistan. SPO has taken initiatives across Pakistan through focused efforts to increase the enrolment of school children, particularly girls, introducing modern interactive education models & mobilized communities to become members of school management committees. SPO has also ensured increased recruitment of women Teachers & enrollment of girls across all education projects to ensure equal number of women & girl beneficiaries. All goals developed herein inscribe how SPO strives to enable access to education in areas where education for girls has not been a priority for parents, advocate for the rights of girl child to education, to create an interactive yet secure environment for girls & women teachers to participate & to increase access for all girls & boys
In the aftermath of the seasonal floods that had devastated various parts of rural Sindh, SPO conducted a rapid damage assessment in the education infrastructure. SPO was shocked to discover that hundreds of schools have been destroyed or damaged & many others are being used as shelters for displaced families. The Data were gathered from a survey through telephonic key informant interviews (KII) with education officials, school heads teachers and other stakeholders. The assessment was conducted in 142 schools & the damage due to flood were also gathered.
In keeping with the organization’s commitment to SDG-4 & 5, the ‘Education Sector Response Project’ seeks to achieve the following objectives;
I. To establish safe and friendly 90 Temporary Learning Centers (TLCs) to ensure access to primary education for 3150 girls and boys children affected by floods in 05 Districts; Hyderabad, Badin, Sanghar, Tando Mohamed Khan and Umerkot, of Sindh and ensure provision of teaching and learning materials for 250 women and men teachers and 3150 girls and boy students and support home-based learning and alternate/distance learning in the case of school closure.
II. To mobilize communities and revitalize 875 members of 35 Parent Teacher School Management Committees (PTSMCs) in 35 schools in five districts of Sindh for new enrolment of 2000 out of school children and improve functioning of respective schools
III. To organize training of 250 teachers on Child Friendly Schools (CFS), life skills-based education, psychosocial support and teaching methodologies including teaching through different modalities.
IV. To implement Safe School Operations Protocols (SSOP) including Infection Prevention and Control-IPC) through development and distribution of risk-related communication material in local language, implementing safe school guidelines; equipping 35 schools with hygiene packages and IPC kits in 5 Districts of Sindh
SPO has a longstanding commitment to ensure gender equality in access to education in Pakistan. It has been working specifically in the education sector since the last decade. To ensure equal participation of girls & women teachers in education as part of its humanitarian response in the sector, SPO espouses to implement the following strategy in its activities; a. Sensitization & mobilization from door to door to convince parents to send their kids especially girls to school. b. Recruitment of women teachers from adjacent areas & ensuring their safe passage to schools in locations where no qualified women teachers were available. c. Making gender sensitization & inclusion as an important aspect in all capacity building & training activities with Teachers, PTSMCs and communities. d. Ensuring access to safety & protection by providing separate WASH infrastructure & facilities to women and girl teachers and students in schools.

1- Project Activities

Objective 1. To establish safe & friendly 90 Temporary Learning Centers (TLCs) to ensure access to primary education for 3150 girls & boys children affected by floods in 05 Districts; Hyderabad, Badin, Sanghar, Tando Mohamed Khan & Umerkot, of Sindh. 1.1. A technical research consultant will be hired for 40 days to conduct RNA in the identified primary schools selected in the 5 Districts of Sindh to assess the teachers & communities’ capacity, knowledge & access to WASH, water, COVID-19 safety protocols, content knowledge, pedagogical skills & technological aptitude & vulnerabilities (women, elderly, PWDs including households. 1.2. 90 TLCs will be established in the 35 selected fully/partially damaged schools. Each TLC will consist of 30 to 35 students, an estimated number of 3150 students including girls & boys will be directly enrolled. 1.3. School-in-a-Box kits for students & teachers for developing a continued learning system in 90 TLCs will be complied and distributed. 1.4. Standardized teaching kits with stationary items for 250 primary school teachers in 90 TLCs & stationary kits to 3150 students will be awarded. 1.5. Homebased Learning kits will be provided to selected TLCs comprising (about) 2000 students. The material will be provided on monthly basis for select 20 home based schools where TLCs cannot function. 1.6. To partner with relevant stakeholders & training of sector partners to ensure smooth running of schools/TLCs, safe & secure mobility of teachers to schools/TLCs, sustainability & ownership as exit strategy.Objective 2. To mobilize communities & revitalize 875 members of 35 Parent Teacher School Management Committees (PTSMCs) in 35 schools in Sindh. 2.1. Social mobilizers will be hired to mobilize communities within the 5-kilometer vicinity of 35 schools in 13 Unions Councils of the 5 Districts of Sindh. They will revitalize 35 PTSMCs in partnership with the District/Provincial Education Department. 2.2. 10 Monthly meetings will be held of PTSMC to develop engagement plan to ensure the enrolment of children, oversee the school improvement plans & observation of school safety protocols. 2.3. 08 cluster based two days each training of selected 200 members of PTMCs will be conducted on Safe School Operations, hygiene maintenance of schools, usage of IPC kits & home-based learning, budget management of PTSMCs & advocacy tools to influence district education departments. 2.4. SPO will conduct an enrollment drive with 35 PTSMCs in each District to enroll 2000 boys & girls for the newly built schools. PTSMCs will be mobilized to encourage parents of out-of-school children especially girls to send them to the newly rehabilitated schools.Objective 3. To organize training of 250 teachers on Child Friendly Schools, life skills-based education, psychosocial support & teaching methodologies including teaching through different modalities. 3.1. Two cycle of capacity building trainings will be conducted for 250 teachers of 35 schools. 3.1.1. Ten two-days capacity building workshops will be organized with on CFS, teaching methodologies, use of IPC & Hygiene Kits tele schooling & digital platforms/resources to be used during COVID-19 pandemic & Home-based Teaching. 3.1.2. Ten two-day capacity building workshops will be conducted with Teachers on Psychosocial Support, Life Skills-based Education & running resilient schools. Objective 4. To implement Safe School Operations Protocols (SSOP) including Infection Prevention & Control-IPC). Distribution of risk communication material in local language, implementing safe school guidelines; equipping 35 schools with hygiene packages & IPC kits. 4.1. SPO will develop SSOPs in coordination with District/Provincial Education Departments to be implemented in schools 4.2. Develop & distribute IEC material translated in local language. 4.3. Distribute IPC kits in 35 schools along with medicines & will continue to provide them till three months for 5500 students, teachers & staff.
