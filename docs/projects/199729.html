Project Summary

The intervention will focus on activities that produce a direct and immediate impact on the restoration and protection of livelihoods to ensure and increase the availability of food in the most vulnerable households, paying special attention to households headed by women, single mothers, and indigenous women. Specifically, it will focus on providing knowledge and experiences, inputs and materials for the protection and reactivation of food production and diversification and the management of productive assets such as water and soil. The foregoing will be implemented by applying suitable climate change adaptation techniques in the dry corridor, in the east of the country. The project will be carried out during the rainy season 2023 and will last until the dry season 2024, for which water is key for the irrigation of vegetables so that 2 or 3 production cycles can be produced in the 12 months of project period.

Emergency Response1.1 Cash-based Transfers.Cash-based transfers through vouchers, cards or cash delivery will allow people to make decisions about the goods and services they need and want to buy in local markets. LIVELIHOODS – EARLY RECOVERY2.1 Produce food locally to improve food security.Include vegetable production (community gardens and individual parcels), chicken meat and egg production, and improving livestock production.2.2 Improve the use of water for irrigation purposes.In order to produce even in the dry season, gravity-driven drip irrigation systems will be installed.2.3 Use renewable energies for water pumping.The design and configuration of the photovoltaic systems will depend on the site conditions in the community gardens or plots where it is feasible to install them.2.4 Increase the capacities of male and female producers.Project participants will receive a series of training sessions to improve their knowledge.
