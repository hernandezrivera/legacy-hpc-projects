Project Summary

In North-western Syria, draft HNO 2024reports high numbers of PIN in the two governorates, Aleppo and Idleb.
Draft HNO 2024 captures the PIN for Idleb as 2.6M and segregated PIN for Aleppo as 1.1M.
Idleb has 23 sub-districts with severity 4 and 5 while Aleppo has 17 at the same severity level.
This year’s MSNA conducted in northern Syria and the 2022 MSNA show that the top unmet needs for the resident populations in Syria were food, livelihood support, electricity services and health services.
The MSNA 2022 and 2023 show that residents persistently depend on negative coping strategies such as borrowing, selling assets, and remittances.
The humanitarian catastrophe worsened only further by multiple earthquakes in February 2023 Within this context, malnutrition is rising with every year in the Northwest of Syria.
The prevalence of Global Acute Malnutrition (GAM), among children under the age of 5 ranges from 4.9% to 6.5% in 2023 according to HNO 2024 data, at the “serious” threshold as identified by WHO, a clear indication of the weakened nutrition fabric in those locations. Stunting rates have consistently risen across Syria, increasing from 12.6% in 2019 to 16.1% in 2023.
Alarmingly, 5 out of 14 Governorates reported unacceptably high stunting rates exceeding 20%, including Northern Aleppo and Idleb in the NWS.
Maternal malnutrition, on the other hand, remains a prevailing concern, with prevalence of acute malnutrition reaching 10% in most areas across the Northwest of Syria excluding very few areas in Idleb where the maternal malnutrition rate is 5.5%.
The project aims to improve the financial situation for the most vulnerable families in northwest Syria by providing live-saving humanitarian assistance for 1000 vulnerable families (6000 individuals: 1287 men, 1353 women, 1649 Boys, 1711 girls) That will be achieved through:
- Distribution of MPC for 1000 vulnerable families (6000 individuals: 1287 men, 1353 women, 1649 Boys, 1711 girls) in Idleb governorate while each family will be supported with monthly e-vouchers (100 USD balance) to address their needs for continuous 8 months.
The e-voucher (NFC) modality provides beneficiaries with the freedom to receive the cash from several cash distribution points.
AHF will prepare a vendor map and provide the selected vendors with devices and training on how to use these devices. in addition to conducting orientation sessions on using e-voucher cards for the beneficiaries.
We are targeting both males and females in this project including all ages. We will also take the elderly and people with disabilities.

They will utilize appropriate tools such as PIM and PDM to enhance and improve the services provided within the project and related initiatives. Design of the Electronic Distribution System:1. The system provides complete management and monitoring of the distribution process with easy and secure access without the need for the internet at the distribution points.2. It displays the amount received by each beneficiary and the total quantity delivered, allowing the project manager and procurement team to track the balance. This enables daily checks and distribution monitoring while synchronizing data daily, avoiding any low-quality duplication (scanning and packaging).3. It offers a live web portal based on roles and permissions for multiple users to provide project information, flexible reports, including financial reports. It provides administrative capabilities to add, edit, and remove data, including beneficiaries, items, and vendors, as well as adding credit for beneficiaries.4. The system serves as an alternative solution to traditional distribution using paper transactions.5. During the distribution process, the system instantly displays the quantities distributed daily for each category and each beneficiary without any duplication, based on distribution reports showing the beneficiary's date (signed, audited, automated, and accurate) according to the uploaded data.6. NFC distribution cards are password-protected, ensuring that only the beneficiary can use them.
