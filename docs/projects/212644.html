Project Summary

This project is designed to enhance the living conditions and resilience of vulnerable populations, including host communities, returnees, and internally displaced persons (IDPs), in both camp and non-camp settings. It aims to mitigate risks associated with floods and adverse weather by restoring basic infrastructure, replacing outdated tents with alternative transitional shelters, and rehabilitating damaged houses. Additionally, it seeks to address the winter needs through fuel support through CVA modalities and provision of Non-Food Item (NFI) kits based on established targeting criteria, thus improving shelter conditions and avoiding the loss of shelters due to seasonal weather fluctuations.
MWL key activities include:
- Rehabilitating moderate and light-damaged houses or unfinished housing to restore community resilience.
- Provision of core and seasonal NFIs to address immediate and seasonal needs, in addition to providing winterization assistance (180 USD on 3 rounds) through CVA modalities.
- Site-level infrastructure enhancements, installation or replacement of tents, and construction of temporary transitional shelters to provide dignified living spaces.
- Household-level improvements such as tent graveling and solar energy systems to ensure sustainability and comfort.
MWL plans to target 4,600 households (22,957 individuals), including specific interventions in:
Idleb: Idleb, Harim, Dana and Maaret Tamsrin sub-districts.
Aleppo: Al Bab, A'zaz, Afrin, and Jandairis sub-districts.
Focusing on moderate and light rehabilitation of houses, installation of RHUs, and delivery of NFI assistance, including winter clothing, stoves, and fuel through CVA modalities. Priority will be given to households led by females, children, the disabled, or the elderly, and those with severely ill or disabled members, children below 5, the elderly, pregnant or lactating women, especially those recently displaced or living in overcrowded conditions.
The distribution will cover 20 camps and 11 sub-districts across several regions, ensuring a wide-reaching impact and prioritizing the needs of the most vulnerable populations to improve their life conditions and resilience against weather conditions through Shelter and NFI activities.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

MWL will strategically select project locations to address the needs in specified regions, focusing on Non-Food Items (NFI) and Shelter activities across various sub-districts within Aleppo and Idleb. The targeted areas for NFI activities include Aleppo's Al Bab sub-district, along with multiple sub-districts in Idleb: Idleb, Maaret Tamsrin, and Harim. Shelter interventions are planned for similar areas in Aleppo, such as Al Bab, Afrin (Afrin and Jandairis sub-districts), A'zaz sub-district, and several sub-districts in Idleb, including Idleb, Maaret Tamsrin, and Dana. To ensure the effectiveness of these efforts, MWL commits to HLP considerations for all SNFI-related activities. 1. Signing Memoranda of Understanding (MOUs) with local communities to clarify roles and responsibilities.2. Coordinating with Shelter/NFI and CCCM clusters to prevent activity overlap in targeted camps.3. Organizing focused group discussions with older individuals and women to raise awareness about project objectives and to streamline the beneficiary selection process for fairness and to prioritize the most vulnerable.4. Implementing house rehabilitation for 1,200 households (HHs), beginning with a meticulous selection and verification process. Qualified engineers will evaluate the housing conditions.5. Installing 300 RHUs with comprehensive interior amenities for the most vulnerable IDPs living in substandard shelters or tents, validated through field assessments. These units will be fully equipped with essential infrastructure and facilities.6. Replacing or installing 500 tents for the most vulnerable IDPs residing in inadequate tents, with verification conducted through field visits before installation.7. Enhancing site conditions for 300 HHs with emergency shelter kits/materials and site-level improvements.8. Distributing seasonal and supplementary NFIs to 1,500 HHs via CVA, including provisions for heating fuel. Beneficiary lists will be meticulously reviewed, with field visits conducted to sample beneficiaries, ensuring alignment with the project's vulnerability criteria. Throughout these initiatives, MWL's MEAL team will assess the satisfaction and overall impact on beneficiaries and targeted communities, ensuring that interventions are effective and responsive to the needs of those most in need.
