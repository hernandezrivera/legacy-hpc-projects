Project Summary

he project is designed to respond to the WASH needs of 160244 people (Men: 33564, Women: 36480, Girls: 44199, Boys: 46001),( 72109 IDPs, 88135 hosted people, and returnees 11583 ) with 1315 people of them are people with disabilities, to respond to their protracted needs by ensuring the good access to sustainable WASH service, The project is designed to respond to the needs of IDPs, Returnees, and hosted people, stayed in the north of Syria in the communities and the IDPs camps .to cover their needs by delivering humanitarian WASH supplies and services to most vulnerable people: men, women, boys, and girls will benefit from the activities which aim to increase access to sustainable water, sanitation, and environmental hygiene solutions, the project will be transferred the water station from working by fuel to work by a hygiene system most of it solar energy.
One of the causes of the spreading of Leishmania is the bad solid waste management in place and this is another factor that can increase the threat on the vulnerable people
SR is aiming to solve this long-known problem by undertaking by supporting the local council to activate good solid waste management which is reducing and eliminating the Leishmania causes.
Through this project, SR is planning to support the targeted communities with the following activities:
-Installation of the solar power system to turn the operating system of the water stations: 4 water stations. and conducting a water safety plan, and establishing a cost recovery system to ensure the suitability of the project. The solar system will be used for both wells operation and/or for pumping stations
-Conducting 39 hygiene promotion campaigns, SR will adhere to the recommendation of the WASH cluster in the implementation of the hygiene promotion and will focus on using the recommended methods, also during these campaigns SR will distribute PSEA for beneficiaries to increase the knowledge and access to PSEA reporting hotline.
Supporting sanitation works which include but not limited to :
-Provision and installation of garbage bins 0.1m3 size for the camps and the communities.
--Rehabilitation and or /providing of the solid waste truck.
-Support SWM unit with salaries and fuel for 6 months, and uniforms for the workers, masks, gloves to protect the workers in this activity SR with Conducting solid waste campaigns aim to encourage people to participate in this humanitarian effort.
-Rehabilitation of the sewer system which includes but is not limited to Rehabilitation and extending of Sewer system network ( (HDPE 30cm diameter ):3800m,- (HDPE100 cm diameter ):9600m.
- Emergency WASH support to the camps in need with considering the connection of those camps to the water and sewage network where available.
SR will be in a close position with the local council, the beneficiaries, the IDPs, and the host community and will involve them in all the project stages from the planning till the closure of the project to adhere to the community engagement approach by inviting them to regular FGDs and open all the channels to receive their feedback, complaints, and opinions.

3 - Needs

Comprehensive WASH assessments were conducted in mid-2022 to inform the HNO 2021 These assessments included a representative, individual-level household survey that focused specifically on IDP camps and informal settlements in northwest and northeast Syria . The results indicate a slight increase in the overall water share received from the water distribution networks in 2022 compared to previous years. However, the results also indicate that the water chlorination level remains largely insufficient, and that many people are still forced to complement the water they receive from the network with other sources or to solely rely on these alternative sources. , According to HNAP assessment of PIN’s WASH activities in the area in Dec 2022, the PIN rate in Afrin 100% of total population, In A'zaz is 100 % also , While in Idleb is 90 %of total population ,in DEC 23 ,SR conducted full WASH need assessment in Idleb and the key findings are :52% of respondents said that they need 20% to 35% of their monthly water expenditure ,through 35% that they need less than 15% of their monthly water expenditure.-63% of respondents do not have the ability to purchase all cleaning supplies, while 31% are unable to purchase cleaning fluids for the home.-48% of respondents declared that the most common way they disposed of garbage in the last 30 days was private garbage collection paid, while public garbage collection free was 52 %, - 25 % of the elderly in the camps do not have access to a toilet

4 - Implementation of activities

Planned proposed activities will improve public health and mitigate against the risk of WBDs, COVID and cholera through ensuring adequate access to improved WASH services. Sustainability of WASH service provision is built into program design through the provision of solar system with the necessary technical support to establish cost recovery mechanism During the implementation, Syria Relief will rehabilitate and provided support cost for four water stations , ensure access to safe water through water chlorination and training of water station staff on water treatment. When required, essential rehabilitation of water networks will be completed to ensure all households are connected to the main water network. More sustainable solution will be on the ground through install Hybrid system in water stations and through connect the water stations with electricity grid from Turkish side , That will help to reduce the cost of water pumping and reduce the Malfunctions With the electrical items in water stations , all of that will lead to have good chance for a successful cost recovery system. Recent technical infrastructure assessment have also identified the need for rehabilitation of sewage systems in many of the communities targeted, which will be implemented during 2023. SR will also support the camps in need for WASH basic services with comprehensive WASH response with considering the connection of those camps to the public water and sewage networks where applicable.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

The 2022 Protection Risk Analysis for WASH highlights areas of risk for which we have already established effective mitigation measures. In line with the WASH sector, SR will ensure ‘Do No Harm’ principles are applied to identify and mitigate against potential protection risks that may arise from the implementation of the WASH sector strategy and related activities. We will ensure that SPHERE standards are strictly followed and adapted to the context during each part of project implementation, especially during emergency sanitation infrastructure implementation phase when the risk of GBV is higher. Measures will also be taken to minimize exposure of the most vulnerable groups (women, adolescent girls, children, people with disabilities, female headed households) to greater protection risks, and particularly to GBV. WASH staff will be trained on key GBV concepts and referral pathways to properly support survivors. Women, adolescent girls and people with disabilities will be consulted during the design, implementation and monitoring phase of each project, with a need to put the necessary mechanisms in place to facilitate feedback from users. We design our projects with an emphasis on meaningful access, safety, dignity and non discrimination so that all potential beneficiaries will benefit from inclusive service provision to the greatest extent possible. We also ensure that all staff sign code of conduct, which includes the obligation to report abuse by staff or beneficiaries,


8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

• All staff will be trained, acknowledge, and sign a code of conduct for sexual exploitation and abuse, moreover, SR has zero-tolerance against SEA. • PSEA messaging and awareness-raising will be done with beneficiaries through one or more of the following (PSEA posters will be distributed to beneficiaries, PSEA banners will be placed at the location of the project, PSEA messaging/videos and hotline information will be shared with beneficiaries through SMS/WhatsApp). • All project staff will be trained on GBV prevention and response in emergencies to ensure full adherence to the Do No Harm principle and to stress that PSEA and GBV issues will be dealt with firmly and expeditiously. • Ensuring that services and supplies are affordable, free, accessible to all of beneficiaries without any discrimination or exploitation. • Establishing internal SEA reporting and investigation mechanism, with ensuring that all protection cases, including GBV and PSEA will be referred to the respective focal point in the respective locations and sensitive data will be handled and kept confidentially with the PSEA field FP.


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

SR will create a risk register, in which it will identify relevant risks, conduct their analysis and their evaluation by identifying mitigation measures. This will allow to have understanding of the risks and agree on the internal controls to be applied and monitored. Overview of the risks and internal controls: The risk of fraud and misappropriation of assets and supplies due to inadequate financial controls, the lack of segregation of duties and personal integrity. This will be mitigated by having robust internal procedures with the adoption of checks and balances allowing the prevention and detection of fraud., monitoring teams from SR is present in the field to report irregularities by monitoring and documenting the delivery of activities and conduct post-distribution monitoring. A thorough stock management process is established with a field capacity to spot-check warehouses and conduct stock counts. A whistleblower hotline is available to all project stakeholders and they are aware of it early in the project to report any suspected act of fraud or corruption. An emphasis will be put on the risk of mishandling Personally Identifiable Information (PII) related to project beneficiaries, staff, contractors, vendors, etc. SR will ensure to adopt data protection and privacy measures and ensure compliance throughout the implementation. A particular attention will be given to data protection in case management due to the sensitive nature of its related activities
