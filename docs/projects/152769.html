Project Summary

This response plan aims to increase access of emergency lifesaving health care through mobile clinic package to vulnerable populations of Panyikang in Upper Nile and Wonduruba in Central Equatoria state. Approximately, 10,000 vulnerable hosts, IDPs and Returnees are targeted to be reached in the mentioned states. Specifically vulnerable population in Panyikang, Upper Nile state is 2100 men and 1900 women while in Wonduruba, Lainya in Central Equatorial state is 3200 men and 2800 women. Emergency mobile services will be extended in Wondruba (Lainya County) where only 4 out 10 facilities i.e. Lainya, Wonduruba PHCC, Katigiri PHCC, Moje PHCC and Mengelle PHCC are functional with minimal health services provided, the rest closed after destruction during conflict

Needs

The long conflict in South Sudan has exposed more damages not only health facilities but also the services that are provided by such institutions. Its effect is conspicuous as existence and functionality of health facilities which provide lifesaving healthcare to vulnerable IDPs/hostCommunities have been destroyed, looted and killings of health workers witnessed (SSA 2017/18). According to recent IPC report, 26,000 people in Panyikang (former Upper Nile state), counties may go into catastrophe (IPC 5) if there are no measures to improve post-harvestin October and December. Also, a critical GAM rate (15%-29.9%) level of acute malnutritionin Upper, Jonglei, and Lakes States while Central Equatoria state at alert rate. All is attributed to poor access to health care, nutrition and WASH services. It was recommended that a multi-sectoral humanitarian intervention be scaled up to meet the pressing needs and improved access to basic services such as health (IPC Report, September 2018). • Lack of support for sexual reproductive health and other cross-cutting issues like HIV/AIDS, TB, Mental health, disabilities An increased risk of Ebola Virus Disease (EVD) and malaria endemicity in Central Equatorial and upper Nile requires stringent health and WASH securities in the hotspots or high risks of transmission areas. (WHO EVD report at week 43, October, 2018). Low level of immunization coverage in Wondruba and Panyikang and increased malaria prevalence severe acute malnutrition CIDO proposes in this humanitarian response plans for the year 2019 to forge mobile clinic services in Panyikang and Wonduruba in Central Equatorial state CIDO seeks your approval to enable the get funds to enhance access, of emergency health services to provide emergency lifesaving mobile health care services to the IDPs/host communities in Central state (Wonduruba) and Upper Nile (Panyikang)” for Humanitarian Response Plan (HRP) for Health Cluster in the coming Year 2019. CIDO will implement emergency lifesaving mobile health care services in Panyikang and Wonduruba where health facilities are dysfunctional by the crisis. This response will reach 10000 vulnerable populations i.e. IDPs, Host and Returnees through mobile health care services The community will be consulted and provided with information regarding the project implementation. This will include engaging existing CHD, Community elders, Women, Youth groups, religious leaders and representatives of beneficiaries. Wider consultations with communities representative was initiated prior to this plan, reaching to the grassroots community leaders such as women representatives, youth representatives, local chiefs, and to the medium level to consulted with Payam administrators, and county commissioners. Lastly, highest level consultation was initiated with CHDs, and SMOH,

Protection from sexual exploitation and abuse

Yes

Briefly describe how

CIDO has a functional policy on PSEA. The policy has been signed by all signed by all staff in respect to the 6 core principles of PSEA. The respective project staff leading this project will be guided by the standard operating Procedures (SOP) of PSEA and where applicable will use the community based complaint Mechanism to address cases of SEA. CIDO will ensure that PSEA IEC materials are used and are visible in all designated areas of service delivery. In collaboration with other partners and I recognition that CIDO is an active protection partner, efforts will be consolidated to conduct capacity building of front line workers on PSEA and extend applicability of SOP to other NGOs at grassroots level

Centrality of Protection in humanitarian action
Protection Risk Analysis?

Yes

Briefly describe how

CIDO being an active Protection Cluster partner has conducted a protection Mapping and risk analysis exercise of the targeted beneficiaries. Both exercises provided essential information on groups and individuals who are at risk, the reasons why they are at risk and how, while taking into account the specific vulnerabilities that underlie these risks. The project therefore take into consideration specificities and unique needs for men, women, girls and boys, and groups such as internally displaced persons, older persons, persons with disabilities, and persons belonging to sexual and other minorities and most importantly how to mitigate the identified risks.

Barriers to Access?

Yes

Briefly describe how

In reference to the risk analysis exercise, it is noted that some beneficiaries could be falling under the elderly, Physically handicapped or under Persons Special needs (PSNs) categories. Under such circumstances, the project activities are designed to give priority to safety and dignity of all men, women boys and girls and prevent negative coping strategies and mitigate risks including issues of discrimination in regards to accessing services. Through regular coordination meetings, partners on ground will be encouraged to train the frontline workers on disability mainstreaming and also report and share protection concerns for persons with specific needs.
