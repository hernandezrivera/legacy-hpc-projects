Project Summary

Extensive use of explosive weapons has been reported since the outbreak of the ongoing conﬂict between Sudan Armed Force (SAF) & Rapid Support Force (RSF) from the middle of April 2023 to present.
The exact level of EO contamination in the conflict affected areas is not known as during the conflicts, survey has not been possible to date. The project aims to reduce the risk posed by EO As displacement continues, people face heightened risks and exposure to EOs due to their increased movement. JASMAR will response to the MA needs in 2024 through comprehensive MA interventions that includes land release operations , EORE activities implementation and VA data gathering
JASMAR will implement tow Multi Task Teams (MTTs) to clear and release 70,000 of lands contaminated by mines and Explosive Remnants of War (ERW) that posing direct threats to IDPs, host communities and prevent the access to provide humanitarian interventions through land release. The EO contaminated lands in the target Blue Nile Region in Gissan, Bow , Elkurmuk localities , will be cleared from the EO threat , closed and released. The released land will be handed over to 50,000 individual including (13000 men, 12500 women, 13000 girls and 11500 boys) IDPs, returnees, at risk population and humanitarian workers in Blue Nile . Furthermore JASMAR will implement two teams through EO Risk Education to enable 574,487 IDPs, returnees & people in host communities to identify and adopt safety measures that reduce the death and injuries posed by landmines/ EO in the target localities in Khartoum state 248,487 individuals (62197 men,58400 women , 67320 boys, 60570 girls). In BN 163,000 individual ( 44,000 men 37,500 women, 48,820 boys, 32,680 girls) and North and SK 163,000 individuals including (43,080 men, 38,420 women, 46,880 boys, 34,620 girls). EORE and LR teams will collect victim assistance data in the appropriate IMSMA format in the three target states specially in Khartoum .
Land clearance from EO Priorities and needs of women, girls, boys, and men, their mobility patterns, different roles and responsibilities will be considered during project implementation in the target localities. Different group of Community will be involve in different stages of the project management of operations including planning, prioritization, implementation, release and delivery of mine action services .
