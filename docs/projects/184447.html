Project Summary

This Response will target among the vulnerable 2.9 million people that internally displaced and host communities that requires assistance in the Somalia/land. DRC will target over 3000 HHs(18,000 beneficiaries) in both livelihoods with conditional and unconditional cash transfers. The injection of cash is meant to increase the purchasing power for target households with limited access to food commodities that are available in the local markets. The provision of cash will be a dignified means of supporting beneficiaries as they allow them to make their own choices in terms of the types and quantities of food to purchase. Under cash for work activities component, community members will be engaged to rehabilitate productive community assets to restore them to usable condition. The community members will identify and select assets that are not in proper working condition and those that have been damaged by human activities or natural calamities for rehabilitation/restoration. Possible cash for work activities will be bush clearing, erosion control, digging latrine pits, road work to improve access to markets, basic services and other work to preserve community assets and recover from effects of drought. Women and men will be equally involved in determining the final gender appropriate cash for work activities. Care will be taken to select activities that allow a high percentage of women to participate. Work norms will be established to ensure a reasonable quantity of work but allow women time to carry out their family responsibilities. Accountability to affected population (AAP) will be applied Additionally, unconditional cash transfers covering at least 75% of MEB will be provided to HHs that do not meet the cash for work criteria and do not have an available adult laborer within the family to be involved. Examples include female-headed households where all members of the family are children except for the caregiver

1. - Projects activities

(1) Provision of farming inputs (spade, wheelbarrow, panga, shovels, seeds, fertilizers) to support irrigation and increase agricultural production for small scale subsistence farmers from asset poor HHs and minority people who lack the necessary collateral to access credit. This activity will target 40% female headed households and People with Disabilities to reduce negative survival mechanisms including livelihoods that increase their risk of violence and exploitation. (2) Cash for Work: Under this activity, DRC will target 1,500 of which 60% will be female participants and People with Disabilities. Gender roles and cultural context will be a key consideration, so that women can undertake activities that are consistent with Somali cultural tradition. (3) Provision of unconditional cash transfers: This will be targeted towards vulnerable households and those at particular risk of exclusion from accessing livelihoods to increase their access to food, and strengthen purchasing power for essential household items and services. This activity will target at least 50% female beneficiaries and People with Disabilities. DRC will target activities to generate sufficient income for households in Emergency and Crisis to access at least 75% of the cost of the MEB while maintaining and rehabilitating communal assets. Provision of unconditional food vouchers to vulnerable households in urban areas in emergency and crisis in order to increase access to essential food and non-food items through supply of selected quantities of dry food items from selected vendors on a monthly basis. The project will target the most vulnerable persons including boys, girls, men and women and People with Disabilities . At least 50% of the targeted households will be female headed and will be given at least 75% of MEB. Specific food that is culturally and religiously appropriate to the beneficiaries may be provided on a monthly basis DRC will integrate COVID19 messaging in all the above activities in order to protect the elderly and persons with underlying issues

2.1.1 - What is the justification for using cash or vouchers instead of in kind?

Distribution of cash will allow households purchase food items and other essential commodities. Cash is also a more secure modality compared to in-kind contributions and is also the most preferred among beneficiaries. The injection of cash is also seen as a means of stimulating the local economy as beneficiaries are able to purchase commodities thereby increasing the circulation of currency in the market. . In addition to cash, the project will also apply voucher for food where the market is not functioning.

2.1.2 - Explain how the project design considered market functionality?

DRC will ensure that the cash distributed does not negatively impact market forces such as inflations and thus will regular conduct market assessments working alongside, and adhering, to the cash working group guidelines for cash transfers
