1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

UNFPA is committed to continuing its leadership in emergency GBV responses during the next, 2025 humanitarian project cycle, in line with its mandate under the IASC. Therefore, in collaboration with its implementing partners and in coordination with the GBV AoR, UNFPA will continue to provide and expand quality, life-saving GBV prevention, risk mitigation, and response services in Mozambique, working closely with the respective provincial governments and institutions in the targeted provinces. The proposed interventions aim to increase the availability and accessibility of quality, life-saving GBV prevention and response services in areas affected by climatic shocks where women and girls have limited access to survivor-centered, multisectoral GBV services.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The project aims to increase the availability and access to quality, life-saving GBV response and prevention services across the central and northern provinces of Mozambique. UNFPA will expand its interventions, ensuring a survivor-centered approach within an integrated SRH-GBV service model, and will continue to provide life-saving specialized GBV services, including comprehensive case management, mental health services, psychosocial support, and the establishment and reinforcement of referral pathways throughout the implementation areas. While targeting the northern and central provinces of Mozambique prone to natural disasters, the intervention will primarily focus on Zambezia, Nampula, and Sofala, with the following activities. 1) Individual GBV Case Management including support to existing Centros de Atendimentos Integrados (CAIs) or One-Stop Centers.2) Procurement and distribution of dignity kits with GBV and PSEA awareness-raising initiatives.3) Training caseworkers on the safe and ethical management of GBV-related data. 4) Individual justice, legal aid, Clinical Management of Rape, MHPSS to GBV survivors including specialized care for conflict-related sexual violence (CRSV) including GBV CM and MHPSS capacity-building. 5) Construction, maintenance, and repair of women and girls’ friendly spaces/community centers and psychosocial support services through WGFS 6) Develop and roll out economic empowerment initiatives to support the resilience of women and adolescent girls. 7) Safety and risk mitigation activities through GBV Safety Audits, Referral pathway mapping and strengthening activities, GBV risk mitigation training and assessments. 8) GBV awareness-raising including public communication campaign sessions organized during mass awareness-raising events such as International Women's Day (IWD), 16 days of activism against GBV, and others.9) Provide training and awareness to humanitarian actors on victim assistance services and how to make referrals for SEA survivors. Furthermore, UNFPA will continue to lead the GBV AoR coordination mechanisms and improve the quality of GBV response by enhancing the capacity of member organizations and ensuring coordination among partners. This will involve updating referral pathways, training packages, advocacy messages, and SOPs.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The proposed project identifies potential risks and takes proactive measures to avoid causing harm through thorough safety audits and risk assessment processes. To avoid causing harm, the project ensures that all interventions are designed with a survivor-centered approach, maintaining confidentiality and safety as priorities. Services are provided in a manner that minimizes the risk of re-traumatization, and clear referral pathways are established to ensure safe access to services. Training and capacity building of staff, implementing partners, and local actors are crucial elements of risk management. This includes strengthening their understanding of the guiding principles of GBV response, particularly confidentiality, informed consent, and non-discrimination, to ensure the interventions are safe.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The project is primarily focused on promoting gender equality, including across age groups. UNFPA integrates tailored approaches that acknowledge the diverse needs in different contexts. The intervention will therefore ensure equitable access to services and opportunities for women and girls affected by the crisis.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The project promotes meaningful access to assistance by employing inclusive approaches that address the diverse needs of different groups. Together with its Implementing Partners, UNFPA prioritizes community engagement and facilitates decision-making processes that are inclusive and transparent. As the GBV AoR lead, UNFPA will organize and facilitate training for a variety of humanitarian actors to ensure that GBV prevention and risk mitigation actions are integrated into their responses. Additionally, UNFPA ensures that all services remain confidential by building the capacity of its partners on the guiding principles of GBV. To remove barriers to assistance, UNFPA implements targeted measures such as making facilities (WGSS) and services (e.g., Mobile Brigades) physically accessible and ensuring staff training on inclusive practices. In addition to involving people with disabilities and disability advocacy groups in various consultation processes, UNFPA expands its collaboration with national non-governmental organizations specializing in disability support. This partnership aims to strengthen our efforts in addressing the needs of individuals with disabilities.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project remains committed to minimizing its environmental footprint and implementing measures to mitigate any potential environmental impact. UNFPA Mozambique and its implementing partners will be guided by the UNFPA Social and Environmental Standards (SES) as outlined in its Environmental Efficiency Strategy (EES) 2021-2025. To mitigate environmental impact, UNFPA will implement various measures, such as waste management protocols, utilizing eco-friendly materials where feasible, and promoting energy efficiency in operations.
