Project Summary

Recognizing the importance of education and ensuring equal learning opportunities for all children in the country, especially those who have been deprived due to various factors, Caritas Lebanon (CL)'s project aims to directly support the community by providing vulnerable children in the Mount Lebanon (ML) and Bekaa governorates with access to education, enabling them to continue learning despite the complexity of the crises facing the nation.
To achieve this goal, under ERP’s first outcome (ensuring equitable and inclusive access to, participation in, and completion of safe and protective education for all learners in Lebanon, with a focus on the most vulnerable children and their caregivers), the CL team will work to prevent dropouts and re-engage children in education through awareness sessions (1,1), cash for education (1.2), and provision of assistive devices for children with disabilities (1.5). The Education Trust Fund is one of Caritas Lebanon's strategic plans to respond to the crisis. The CL team, in collaboration with Oeuvre d'Orient, has developed a database of approximately 300 schools, which includes critical school-level data covering students, teachers, gender statistics, refugee status, and the types and levels of payments provided by students. Additionally, F2F interviews with school administrators provide clear evidence of the deteriorating situation in schools over time and its impact on teachers and students.
Given CL’s positive relationship with several school principals, the team is a valuable partner in executing and completing many of the activities of the Education Sector ERP's first strategic objective. Using their relationships, contact information, and communication skills with a variety of schools, they will be able to reach out and identify students who have dropped out of private schools due to financial reasons, allowing them to follow up and ensure that the most vulnerable Lebanese and migrant children, including children with disabilities, are reached. They report having extensive interaction with at least 84 schools in their target areas: 24 in Bekaa and 60 in ML. The CL team, with the help of education awareness assistants, will continue to raise awareness among 5,000 students and caregivers, as well as refer Out of Education Children (OOEC) to essential services to help them return to school.
Cash for education will be a critical instrument to employ and supply to 950 of the most vulnerable households to help them stay in school while still receiving other forms of social cash assistance.
On the other hand, it is well known that special needs students in Lebanon continue to face social, financial, and physical barriers to education, particularly now that the country is in an emergency situation and neither parents nor schools can afford the high costs of materials, equipment, and assistive technologies for disabled children. Changing this is critical because investing in special education has beneficial spillover effects, such as job creation and allowing children with special needs to realize their full potential as adults. CL believes that we must strive for an inclusive education system that enables all children, regardless of their ability, background, or faith, to realize their ambitions, providing learners with greater opportunities to learn from and relate to their peers. Therefore, under the same objective, CL will support 50 students with physical disabilities learning in CL's special education center (Patriarch Sfeir, Zahle – Vocational school owned by Caritas, supported by the MoSA) and children with hearing impairments with assistive technologies to enable their access to and retention in education.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

Under Obj 1.1 - CL will train Education Awareness Assistant (EAAs) to properly identify Out of Education Children (OOEC) and provide awareness – CL will recruit and train 6 EAAs. Under Obj 1.6 - Outreach, awareness raising & referrals to bring them back to education: This activity consists of following up with school administrators to identify dropouts and students who left the school. EAAs will coordinate with parents and caregivers about students ‘enrolment for the scholastic year 23-24. They will be also responsible with the education team leader of the respective area to plan for awareness campaign through different modalities and methodologies of sessions. Under Obj. 1.5 - Provision of assistive technologies for children with disabilities: This activity consists of reaching out 50 children with hearing impairment and providing them with assistive technologies. The tools will be procured based on accurate medical assessments of an audiologist covered by this project, who will perform a test and measure hearing loss to assess the type and degree of loss.Under Obj. 1.2 - Cash for Education targeting vulnerable Lebanese and migrant families to enable retention in education: This activity consists of providing 950 households with cash for education. 2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

Caritas Lebanon prioritizes preventing sexual exploitation and abuse (PSEA), promoting protection, gender, and social inclusion in all its projects. A comprehensive PSEA policy outlines its zero-tolerance approach, and staff and volunteers undergo background checks and regular PSEA training. Reporting mechanisms are safe, confidential, and accessible to all staff and beneficiaries, with investigations conducted promptly and impartially. Moreover, CL strictly follows the Do-No-Harm methodology and acknowledges that project beneficiaries may be among the most vulnerable groups, requiring special consideration in project design and monitoring, and therefore designs projects in a way that prioritizes their needs. Additionally, CL has a set of policies and procedures to mitigate operational risks and maintain ethical behaviour, including a Code of Conduct, Child Protection Policy, Safeguarding Statement of Commitment, Anti-Harassment Policy, and Anti-fraud and Corruption Policy.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

Caritas Lebanon will actively engage communities, local authorities, and the MEHE within the frame of the project. Through this engagement, CL is ensuring the project is designed and implemented based on the priorities. Caritas Lebanon has operationalized the organizational PSEA and safeguarding mechanism as well as a Feedback and Complaint Handling Mechanism which provides staff, communities and others, with multiple channels to raise concerns and provide feedback including through the complaint boxes and hotlines, and through the MEAL process which include accountability questions and satisfaction surveys. Caritas Lebanon will fully incorporate Accountability to Affected Populations (AAP) modalities into all stages of this project while strengthening the approaches to reflect the Core Humanitarian Standard (CHS) and protection mainstreaming.
