Project Summary

The project aims to provide access to clean water and improved sanitation facilities in rural areas of Syria, specifically in Al Nashabiyeh around 33104 People , Darayya around 91760 People , and Maliha around 42828 people Districts. Through the improving of Water net and main water tanks and sewage systems, the Hifz AL Nema Association seeks to address the urgent need for reliable water supply and improving proper sanitation infrastructure in these underserved communities.
The project will involve the improving of water networks and main water tanks to ensure a consistent supply of clean water to households and public facilities. Additionally, the improvement of sewage systems will help prevent waterborne diseases and improve overall hygiene standards in the targeted districts.
By improving access to clean water and sanitation facilities, the project aims to enhance the quality of life for residents in these rural areas and contribute to their long-term health and well-being. The Hifz AL Nema Association is committed to ensuring the successful implementation of this project and creating a positive impact on the lives of the communities it serves.
