Project Summary

The proposed project is a multi-tiered support mechanism that creates an enabling environment for vulnerable children and families aims to protect and promote children’s wellbeing with the full participation of families, communities, and government child protection service providers.
Community-based mental health and psychosocial support mechanisms assure child wellbeing is safeguarded in terms of Personal wellbeing, Interpersonal wellbeing, and Skills & knowledge. The proposed project will also promote MHPSS's critical role in creating and supporting conditions for children’s optimal development and their family wellbeing at the community level in collaboration with MHPSS and child protection services providers. Thus strengthening the community-based referral mechanism of Child abuse, violence, exploitation to relevant entities.
Vulnerable children, their families, communities, and key stakeholders capacities will be developed on child protection issues to mitigate the risk of child abuse and Exploitation, etc. simultaneously families, communities, CSOs, Government Departments such as police, social welfare, and Child protection bureau are engaged and participated through dialogues to ensure enabling environments for child development and will secure their protection and wellbeing.
Mental health and psychosocial support mechanism/ Help-lines will be developed in collaboration with key stakeholders through consultative workshops to conceptualized and strengthen MHPSS helpline at the community level dually supported by the case management system and robust referral mechanism powered by Tele-services established at government premises.
Our proposed project experiences and Learnings will be shared through Social media platforms such as Face book, LinkedIn and MHPSS Youtube channel for e-education of Social Media Users, SMS Messages on child protection issues and online reporting mechanism for timely registration of incidents with relevant helplines for psychosocial support first aid for vulnerable children.
Community-led monitoring committees will be established at the community level to ensure robust MHPSS Mechanisms are in place and all key stakeholders including government children protection providers and relevant government department bonding and linkages are strengthened for a sustainable and foreseeable time period.
