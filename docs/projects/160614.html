Project Summary

The purpose of this project is to provide displaced and affected vulnerable host communities with essential materials to ensure their protection and life with dignity through distribution of household and shelter materials. The project will target vulnerable displaced, returnees and affected vulnerable host communities. The Project will combine some Cash component of NFI and in-Kind donation from NCA Contingency Stocks in Warrap and Wau funded from MFA -Norway funds. Parts of the funds from this Project will be used to support distribution costs for NCA Contingency items as well as from the core pipeline. Cash vouchers are also included as a conditional cash distribution where assessments carried out by NCA have shown strong markets, and access to markets to provide right holders a more flexible access to much needed NFI and shelter materials. NFI GIK commodities will be distributed where markets are non-functional such as in Baggari Corridor.

1 - Needs

Needs and market assessments were carried out in Jur River and Baggari Corridors between July - October 2019 to determine the functioning of markets for shelter and essential household material and to determine capacity of markets to meet the supply needs for the projected demand for shelter materials as well as to understand and compare prices from different supply chain intervals. We carried out household surveys, interviewing key informants, carrying out general and disaggregated focused group discussions. In Baggari corridor only general focused group discussions were carried out as household interviews proved difficult to be undertaken due to access constraints and since most people are scattered in the thick dense settlements far from main roads due to protection concerns. Baggari corridor has significant access constraints as there are limitations on where humanitarian organizations are not granted access due to bureaucratic impediments related to access approval and negotiations which often do not get granted. In Jur River, we carried out Key informant interviews, Focused group discussions and household interviews and market assessments. Findings show that the fighting in Jur River resulted in families deserting their homes with little or nothing to salvage from their houses. Children are sleeping in the cold without anything to cover them and they are susceptible to URTI due to cold weather at night while women and Girls are susceptible to SGBV risks due to lack of shelters at night. The lack of cooking materials is affecting children as mothers have to take turns to cook food which takes long and often doesn’t allow food to be cooked fully resulting increased incidences of diarrhoea and infections from poorly cooked food. Lack of mosquito nets is exposing children and adults sleeping in the open to mosquitos and malaria attacks. The lack of basic household materials is rendering IDPs susceptible to diseases and at risk of harm. Additionally, many women have reported increased cases of SGBV due to sleeping out in the open. Being the wet season, grass which is the main material for the construction of roofs for traditional shelters has been unavailable and families are forced to live in structures without roofs or with relatives and well-wishers while others use classrooms disrupting studies in schools but also exposing young girls and women to SGBV. A number of agencies have carried out assessments in both Kuajieno and RochRoch Dong and eventually distributed a limited number of shelter and household materials in both Locations. For instance, in Barwol IDP camp and Nikijo in RocRoch Dong. ICRC have both distributed a limited number of household and shelter materials. These materials were provided to only a small number of pregnant and lactating mothers although there are many other equally vulnerable people who desperately needs assistance with Shelter and household materials and who did not receive. In Baggari, it is estimated that up to 2500 households are in need of shelter and basic household materials in 4 locations in Baggar, Ngodakala , farajalla and Besilia. Markets assessment was not carried out in Baggari as the markets are not functional. Information gathered from market traders in the markets in Jur River showed limited market functions and most materials are not available. The results of the market assessment indicated that the markets in both locations are not functional and therefore In kind NFI distributions will be undertaken. Our assessment information in all three locations corroborate findings from multiple sources on the need for support with support for families and communities affected by floods and violence. REACH and ACAPS assessments reports also shows high levels of needs in Pibor , Baggari and Jur River. UNOCHA Latest bulletin also captures on Access limitation in Bagari The results of the market assessment indicated that the markets in both locations are not functional and therefore GIKs

2 - Activities

i) Community Consultations: - NCA and its partners will carry out a number of activities to achieve the outlined objectives, these includes Community consultations to identify the most vulnerable and needy individuals and households. We will carry out registrations and verifications to identify the most needy and vulnerable households and individuals. This verification will be undertaken in all locations working closely with local leaders. We will mobile communities and involve them in all stages of our work, consulting them to identify vulnerable populations, in managing distributions of SNFIs, in verifying and registering right holders.we ii) Rolling out Accountability to affected populations: - During community consultations, we will develop accountability to affected populations systems (AAP) providing them with information in order to ensure their full participation in the planned activities. in collecting feedback and complaints and in assessing vulnerable populations but also ensure that the system are safe for use and effective. Iii) Coordination within and between clusters: - We will also ensure that we play an active role as SFP in Warrap, supporting agencies to coordinate their SNFI activities within Warrap and with other clusters, we will ensure that we share information on the integration of SNFI with GBV, WASH and Health clusters. We will lead assessments, facilitate cluster meetings, at state level and also at the national Cluster. We will maintain the NCA Warrap Focal Point role to continue to coordinate the cluster activities in all locations. We will be active players with other partners in in ensuring effective coordination and information sharing among partners in SNFI and interclusters. Iv) Management of Contingency Stocks: - NCA keeps NFI contingency stocks help to supplement the SNFI Pipeline supplies. When the SNFI pipeline breaks or where pipeline supplies are not enough, we will mobilise our SNFI supplies to support the pipeline. Our NORAD/ MFA funds will support distribution and administrative support for the NCA and Pipeline SNFI supplies. We will ensure proper management of warehouse and provide support to the pipeline supplies including storage, including distribution and assessment support for the cluster partners in all the three locations. v) CASH and Vouchers distributions: - . There will be a small part of our project that will involve conditional cash and voucher distributions in areas with good market access. NCA and our partners will carry out distribution of emergency shelter materials which will help displaced and vulnerable host communities. The planning of the distributions will take into account considerations such as safety, protection and GBV concerns of special interest groups such as of Women, girls, elderly, disabled, lactating and pregnant mothers and put in place systems to ensure that the special needs and circumstances of all people are taken into account. We will print cash vouchers, systems and platforms for transfer of mobile money transfers though mobile phones registration and distribution of cash vouchers and monitoring of the same.vi)Protection and Gender audits :- Distributions will be planned in such a way that beneficiaries do not needs to travel long distances and that those who need help are assisted to carry the materials back to their homes safely. Additionally, through the distribution of essential household kits. The provision of SNFI materials will also enhance protection of individuals and help improve their hygiene status and protection and dignity some of the key activities in those areas will include market surveys, protection, SGBV and risk assessments, safety audits vii)Post Distribution monitoring: - NCA will visit the locations where the materials were distributed to carry out PDMto understand on quality and imapct of materials distributed .Training :- Train community on collection of information on EWS. Ensure reslience and sustainability of action
