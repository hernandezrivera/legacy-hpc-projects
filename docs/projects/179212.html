Project Summary

NGO X will conduct a comprehensive business needs assessment of the bakeries it supports under its food security programme. This assessment will aim to determine the key challenges and opportunities that the bakeries experience. NGO X will also undertake a conflict sensitivity assessment of the proposed support to determine the intended and unintended effects which NGO X’s intervention could have.
Based on the conclusions of the assessment, the area selection will be mapped to determine bakery eligibility for business support. NGO X will subsequently provide a package of system support to up to eight bakeries; four functional and four un-functional. The selection criteria of the bakeries will be based on the evaluation of the received applications. The scheme will include technical business support that covers compliance with minimum bread and nutritional requirements and the related testing, equipment maintenance, and compliance with environmental health and food safety regulations, as well as business and financial management training and mentorship. Some rehabilitation or maintenance of equipment may be covered under the scheme. The package of support for the bakeries will help to improve the functionality of bakeries in the area, keep bakeries open, and improve the bread quality, and thus reduce the burden on affected households.
Through the rehabilitation of the bakeries, we raise the production capacity of the bakery to enable it to deliver bread to more villages in the same area, and we rely on the mechanism of distributing bread through accredited people at points close to the gathering places, which ensures that no financial and physical burden is added to the beneficiaries, especially the PWD to travel large distances to get their daily bread
NGO X will also focus on flood prone areas providing preventive measures to alleviate the effects of flooding and prepare for winter, road rehabilitation will be carried out to ensure the continuity of access for the whole community, including PWD to the basic and lifesaving assistance during rainy days and mitigate against the risk of serious degradation on road access caused by flooding. Contractors will be engaged to carry out the works and will be closely monitored by NGOX Engineers at all stages to achieve the optimum standards related to the road alignment, width, gradient, soil type, and drainage sufficiency. Geotechnical and materials tests will be conducted through NGOX to ensure the quality of the used materials (gravels) when they are provided to the site and conforming to the specifications of the roads during and after the roads gravelling.
Approvals from local authorities to conduct the activities in the selected locations will be sought, and roads construction and surfacing will be coordinated with any agency that has infrastructure maintenance, construction, or extension activities to ensure the logical sequence of activities is coordinated and that the work will be done in a complementary manner and with no delays.
Procurement process will aim to identify contractors to conduct the road and rainwater drainage construction and rehabilitation, the contractors and suppliers are selected through competitive bidding process, the selection is made based on the technical evaluation criteria set by NGOX Engineering team, and the quoted price. The technical evaluation for the roads, gravelling and rainwater drainage systems works will be done to assess the contractor capacity to carry out the work (having previous experience, sufficient expertise within their teams, having access to the required tools and heavy machinery), in addition to evaluating the compatibility of the offered materials against the specification set by NGOX Engineering team in the tender documents.
Both components benefit the whole community as they blanket target, as opposed to directly targeting individuals, leading to a high beneficiary number for the project.

4 - Needs

Bakeries: The GAP from FSL cluster indicates a current gap of 114,000 IDPs in a number of areas. The transfer of wheat and flour from Idleb to Aleppo and the need for additional rehabilitation activities for bakeries and flour mills were reported as a major gap that needs to be addressed. Farmers are highly constrained by the lack of agricultural inputs, increased prices, and limited access to high quality seeds - especially high yield wheat varieties. NGO X has assessed functioning and non-functioning bakeries to inform the the level of need to be addressed in order to scale up bread production. More specifically, the assessment collected information on the type and the status of functioning bakeries, as well as the number of beneficiaries; the bread production capacity and gaps; sources of flour and support; and the prices of bread. Roads: NGO X conducted needs assessment in camps in Maaret Tmisrin subdistrict, the assessment found that poor road conditions had a negative impact on access to essential services. Camps rely solely on water trucking, either to households or to communal tanks. When asked about water this past winter, 35% of respondents reported an interruption of water trucking due to road conditions for an average of 5-days. 58% reported hindered access to key facilities on rainy days due to muddy ground and water ponding; facilities included private and communal latrines, communal water tanks, schools, and medical points. When asked about available camp infrastructure, 58% said that available roads leading to camp needed repairs; 79% reported that roads in the camp need repairs, including resurfacing, extension, increasing the width to accommodate daily traffic and reduction of the road gradient

5 - Implementation of activities

NGOX will provide a package of system support up to eight bakeries; 4 functional and 2 non-functional bakeries. The application will be open for all bakeries from non-supported bakeries in NGO X area of operation based on the assessment sheet weighting which already conducted by NGO X. Once the training completed, bakeries owner will submit their proposal for bakeries rehabilitation and upon the receiving the application will be weighted through scoring sheet NGOX will sign a contract with the 6 bakeries and start providing a training for bakeries management and staff .The scheme will include technical business support that covers compliance with minimum bread and nutritional requirements and the related testing, equipment maintenance, and compliance with environmental health and food safety regulations, as well as business and financial management training and mentorship. It will be structured to cover the costs of equipment (paid directly to equipment supplier).NGO X will start evaluating the cost for conducting bakeries rehabilitation based on the received business plan, issuing a tender to contract with a vendor to carry out the small-scale rehabilitation and/or maintenance of the bakeries For the functional bakeries, support is likely to include maintenance of diesel pump machine, compressor motor, iron chain series etc. For the un-functional bakeries maintenance or small-scale rehabilitation will be provided for the firehouse, cylinder, generator.NGO X will conduct rehabilitation works including resurfacing for 2 main roads in Kelly village, these roads are providing access to 21,355 IDPs living in camps, in addition to the residents of Kelly village.
