Project Summary

This project aims to address the current gaps on shelter needs identified by UNCHR Protection Monitoring teams, by providing emergency shelter and wash assistance to returnees from the United Republic of Tanzania through the voluntary repatriation; and reinforce the recovery mechanism in-country through a Disaster Risk Reduction approach, by:
Four (4) types of Shelter/WASH assistance will be provided:
(1) Emergency shelter repair kits (material and tool kits), including Construction and Rehabilitation of VIP latrines
(2) Grants for rental/host assistance
(3) Hygiene sensitizations
(4) Support refugee returnees to obtain documentation on the land and/or house property titles
The assistance will be determined by an evaluation of houses by IOM’s shelter team. The response will be based on the results of the evaluation and prioritize, by vulnerability, the households that do not have shelter or that are creating a significant burden on host families.
And two(2) types as part of the recovery response to be provided:
(1) Building a recovery response to natural disaster through the distribution of livelihood assistance
(2) Implementing Quick Impact Projects (QIPs) through cash-based intervention and social cohesion activities
Targeted areas: The locations of the shelter response for returnees are Rutana, Kirundo, Cankuzo, Ruyigi, Muyinga, and Makamba.
