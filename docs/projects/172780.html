Project Summary

Child Protection Sector estimates that 1,335,509 girls, boys, adolescents will be in need of protection services as a result of multi- hazards including COVID-19 and expected climate related hazard from projected above normal rain season. The sector’s target is 164,584 which is 30% of the PiN. This project will be implemented in partnership with 15 CSO and government departments in all the 10 provinces to prevent exposure to increased child protection risks and respond to child protection violations. UNICEF Child Protection is targeting 54% of the sector which is 90,000 boys and girls and their caregivers and adolescents in 13 priority districts. UNICEF will partner with several Civil Society Organisations and require $2,700,000.00.

8 - Accountability to Affected People
8.1 - Describe how your project was designed in consultation with communities? How will you incorporate communities in project implementation and what feedback mechanisms do you have in place?

UNICEF has led a process for engagement with partners and CPWG membership on community engagement and participation. Most of the partners engage with project participants at subnational level and work with community cadres. There are several layers that act as platforms to receive and document community feedback such as Child Protection Committees, Child Care Workers, Community Adolescent Treatment Supporters, Champions of Child Protection, disability ambassadors that are being supported through developmental programmes . UNICEF CP is also lobbying for more involvement of grassroot organisations to widen choices for providing feedback by communities. UNICEF will also ensure child protection actors receive adequate training in CPiE minimum standards so that safe and dignified services are provided to affected communities. Communities will also be engaged to provide feedback with a view of adapting the traditional mode of service delivery when there are travel restrictions as well as community support in observing set guidelines and rules for preventing the spread of disease outbreaks such as COVID-19

9 - Environmental considerations
9.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Establishment of Child Friendly Spaces – will focus on existing strucutres such as schools, community halls and recreational facilities. These structures which are already approved by authories for use by chidren will limit exposure to climate related hazards. Activities will be planned for few hours in each day to avoid the need to provide meals and limit use of energy and pollution of the environement. Partners will also be encouraged to share resources such as office space, transport and other limit traffic to affected population
