Project Summary

REACH’s mandate is to support the information needs of the humanitarian community through coordination mechanisms such as OCHA, the clusters and technical working groups. REACH will provide support to the Health Cluster through the consolidation and dissemination of complementary health factsheets such as from the Area of Knowledge (AoK) light monitoring system, or from analysis of health data through the Integrated Needs Tracking (INT) system. Additionally, REACH can provide ad hoc technical support as requested such as through trainings on ODK/mobile data collection, or inclusion of health indicators in rapid needs assessments.

1 - Needs

Health information is essential in order to identify areas with greatest need, and prioritize resources for scale up and response. The health cluster has several regular data sources they utilize including the Health Resources Availability Monitoring System (HeRAMS), which informs on health systems functioning, and Early Warning, Alert and Response System (EWARS) which reports on epidemiological data. However, given the poor infrastructure in South Sudan, there are often health information gaps due to poor access to populations for assessment or lack of functioning health facilities. REACH has been collecting multi-sectoral information through our Area of Knowledge (AoK) light monitoring system since 2013, assessing the needs of hard-to-reach settlements and summarizing indicative needs through monthly sectoral factsheets. In order to address health information gaps, REACH has been collecting a set of health questions mostly focusing on perceived morbidity and mortality, as well as access to health facilities. For 2020, REACH would collaborate with the Health Cluster and partners to customize health indicators for an AoK Health Factsheet specific to the needs of the cluster and health partners. With agreement from the health cluster, these factsheets can also consolidate other complementary health data sources that may not currently be captured in the Monthly Health Bulletin publications. In addition to Health AoK, REACH currently co-chairs the Needs Analysis Working Group (NAWG) with OCHA and maintains the Integrated Needs Tracking (INT) system. The INT system consolidates available data across the life-saving sectors (food security, WASH, health, nutrition) in order to identify priority areas with high likelihoods of emergency needs. REACH will continue to coordinate with WHO on incorporating monthly EWARS data into this system for analysis within the NAWG. In addition, REACH can provide ad hoc technical support to the Health Cluster and partners through the inclusion of standardized health indicators on rapid assessments, participation in Ebola response technical working groups, as well as ad hoc trainings on ODK or other capacity building initiatives.

2 - Activities

• Production of monthly AoK Health factsheets customized to the needs of the health cluster and partners. • Analysis of EWARS data through our INT system to assist the NAWG and humanitarian partners in prioritizing areas with the highest life-saving needs. • Continued participation in Ebola Technical Working Group for ad hoc assessments to inform preparation and response. • Other ad hoc trainings at request of the health cluster such as ODK or other capacity building initiatives.

8.18 - Please indicate how the risks selected above will be mitigated.

Efforts will be made to select research participants in a representative, transparent and equitable manner, as well as ensuring the views of vulnerable populations are included. Wherever possible gender will be considered in data collection and results disaggregated by sex. Children under 18 years of age cannot consent for interview according to REACH policy, and therefore are not included as participants without the consent from parents or caregivers. REACH will ensure all information provided in assessments is confidential, and that participants are explicitly asked for consent prior to interview for any potential data sharing of personally identifying information in line with our global data protection standards
