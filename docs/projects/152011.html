Project Summary

This project is designed to address some key drivers of violence such as grievance arising from unemployment, limited or lack of access to quality education for internally displaced persons (IDPs), and out of schools children in the host communities, poverty, economic deprivation and inequitable access to formal education in the targeted Communities.

Needs

According to 2019 Education Target of Nigeria dated September 2018, Maiduguri, Jere, Konduga, Damboa, Dikwa and Bama have the largest number of IDPs with education need. According to the Reach MSNA education preliminary findings dated August 2018, In Borno state, other than MMC, Monguno and Jere have the highest number of IDPs with 25,919 and 23,388 respectively. In Yobe state, Gujba has the highest number of IDPs and Host communities in need. This project will address some of the issues stated.

Activities

Conduct sensitization to parents and stakeholders on the importance of education.Establish Non-Formal Learning Centres.Engage, Train and Re-train Learning Facilitators, Instructors and Tutors.Procure and distribute Learning Materials. Conduct Regular Monitoring Visits to the Project Areas.Establish Formal Learning Centres.Engage Communities gatekeepers in the programs and activities for buy-in.Conduct compound meetings with parent/caregivers on girl child Education. Mainstream Learners from Non Formal Learning Centres to Formal Learning Centres
