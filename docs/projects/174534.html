Project Summary

"Under the proposed nutrition-specific project UNICEF will support the government to contribute to the reduction of COVID-19 related morbidity and mortality by protecting the nutritional status of the most vulnerable individuals and families across most affected areas through enhanced access to safe promotional, preventive and curative lifesaving nutrition services for people affected by and people at risk of coronavirus infection, while implementing appropriate infection prevention and control measures with below specific objectives.
1- Ensure all severe acute malnourished girls and boys less than five years of age have access to life-saving therapeutic treatment services.
2- Ensure that Mothers and caretakers in targeted communities’ access skilled support for appropriate maternal, infant and young child nutrition (MIYCN).
3- Ensure all girls and boys less than five years of age and pregnant and lactating women access multimicronutrient supplements for the prevention of micronutrient deficiencies."
