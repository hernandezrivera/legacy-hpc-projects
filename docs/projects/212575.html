Project Summary

Needs in response to the Edu Sector SO1: the proj is designed to meet returnees dire need based on Edu sector HNO 2024, DEZ city PiN 117,374, RDam- Maliha PiN 18,229.
We conducted several assessment rounds in the areas, info & data collected in collaboration with beneficiaries, local committees, municipality &Mukhtar.
We defined the actual needs for improving Edu conditions to the most vulnerable people in close coordination with Edu national & sub national sectors.
Activities: Coordination with MoE, DoEs & Governorates. Technical assessment, Procurement procedure, contracts, Site supervision & monitoring on materials & works, M&E, Field visits, beneficiaries’ feedback survey on assistance provided. Final receipt with stakeholders. Final reports to Donor.
Intervention in 2 governorates (schools 1 in Deir Elasafir-RDam & 1 in Hatlah-DEZ) chosen per severity scale for subdistricts & covering the following activities:
Activity 2.2: Repair or rehabilitate classrooms (temporary or permanent). Including Segregated WASH, with PWDs units.
Activity 2.3: Provide schools with school furniture, include desk, chairs & blackboards.
Activity 2.5: Provide solar power system in schools covering the electricity bills.
Activity 2.8: Provide children school supplies in formal settings.
Activity 2.13: Provide children with winter clothes. distribution will be conducted to registered students.
Female students would lose their right to education in high density classrooms; therefore, this project will enhance female students’ resilience via securing education potential. We have DHR project beneficiaries’ resilience will also be enhanced with having schools in the area. Safety & dignity are paramount throughout the project, with a focus on "Do No Harm" principles based on conflict sensitivity analysis. Efforts will ensure equal access for all, including people with chronic diseases or disabilities. GBV considerations are mainstreamed in the project. Establish a call center & CRM system to safeguard against GBV & PSEA. Induction to staff & all personnel at site to ensure to implement GBV prevention measures throughout project implementation CoC signed by all staff, partners, & contractors. Establish a beneficiary CRM system to address concerns & complaints related to SEA & GBV & receive complaint, suggestions, requirements,
Potential risks, including explosive hazards & lack of access for persons with disabilities, will be mitigated through careful planning & collaboration with relevant stakeholders.
Operational risks such as approval delays, miscommunication, currency devaluation, security issues, fuel shortages, are acknowledged & addressed through clear communication channels, contingency planning, adherence to safety protocols & strong coordination with partners. The project will complement ongoing initiatives, particularly in areas of high need & aims to provide a comprehensive response to the multifaceted challenges faced by vulnerable populations in the targeted areas.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Rehabilitation of the school in Deir Elasafir con-insides with Rescate ongoing damaged houses rehabilitation project. The consideration of Inter-Sectoral Collaboration in targeted areas is highly recommended so the rehabilitation of the damaged school is very essential specially for vulnerable returnees’ children. In fact, there is high density in the functional schools in the area. In case of securing fund, the project is essential to support comprehensive intervention in this area which is low funded. In addition, Rescate plans to intervene the same area in multisectoral needs (providing solar system for the existing borehole with rehabilitation of elevated water tank-WASH), (providing street solar lighting-ERL). Operational Risks R1 miscommunication with different stakeholders’ groups. with major consequences M1 ensure clear communication channels & stakeholders’ collaboration in project from design phase to closing R2 The currency devaluation due to the inflation may cause differences between prices M2 Contingency is already accounted for in the budget, Contractor 25% down payment to purchase materials under our observation for quality, quantity & availability validation. If Exch increased, we will reflect the gains on the project target R3 The security issues that may occur & stop access during works & M&E phases, M3 our staff will commit to the safe access SOPs by UNDSS & other concerned partners R4 Fuel shortage & procurement supply delays M4 it is the Contractors’ responsibility under our supervision R5 Approval delays foreseen in the proj duration & works schedule M5 Rescate has strong coordination with ST, stakeholders & communities to facilitate focused response with strong need-based approach. In worst case scenario, apply for NCE. Rescate did not provide 4Ws because we did not have ongoing activities, we are committed to provide 4Ws, financial & narrative reports per progress schedule & at the project closure phase.
