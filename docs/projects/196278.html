Project Summary

As part of Takaful Al Sham emergency response, TAS seeks to provide emergency WASH intervention to help the people in need and cover the WASH gaps in different locations in Idleb and Aleppo including the IDP Sites and communities) with full coordination and collaboration by the WASH cluster, local authorities, and camps management in the targets area.
TAS has designed the proposed project to provide WASH response for the most vulnerable people as a result of the limited access to WASH services and to provide the emergency response as part of Cholera prevention and response in NWS.
This project will target 90,000 individuals (18,000 HHs ) living in many of the IDP Sites and communities in the both Idleb and Aleppo governorates with basic WASH services including; potable water (by operating the water stations in most locations and using the water trucking in emergency cases only), solid waste management (including garbage collection, garbage bins distribution), de-sludging latrine septic tanks, water tanks, construction of latrines (including the latrines for the PwDs), rehabilitation or extending water and sanitation networks and systems, and Hygiene activities (including hygiene /cholera kits distribution and hygiene promotion). In addition, ensure the functionality of water supply systems and operationalize of sanitation/sewage and solid waste management systems.
TAS will seek to provide the proposed WASH activities using the most sustainable solutions such as the establishment of Water pumping stations, renewable energy....etc..
The planned intervention will mainstream protection principles especially (Do no harm) throughout the project cycle by considering the different needs of men, women, young children, children, adolescents, young adults, middle-aged adults, and older adults. The provision of sanitation services will be consulted with the targeted camps and communities and take into account the specific needs of each group. The Project also was designed to provide disabilities, elderly and another vulnerable group with accessible handicap infrastructure too by taking these groups’ needs into consideration during the design phase of the infrastructures, with particular focus on vulnerable people such as ‘People with Special Needs’, in addition, to provide access to proper gender-sensitive and disability WASH facilities
All the activities will be implemented according to SPHERE standards and WASH cluster guidelines, through coordination with camp management and local authorities. Furthermore, all the technical studies (construction latrines, solar system....etc.) will be prepared as per the Cluster recommendations.
The main objective of TAS's project is to reduce the cholera outbreak and any other relevant diseases among the beneficiaries by providing potable drinking water and a clean environment by implementing the full WASH intervention in NWS in addition to providing sustainable solutions for water supply and sanitation which will lead to reducing the operational cost of supplying water.
Finally, TAS is in close contact with the WASH Cluster and will apply all the recommendations of the WASH cluster during all the project phases.

3 - Needs

As part of TAS preparation to apply for the HRP 2023, in Nov 2022, TAS launched RNA including the WASH component to assess the current situation and needs identification. The conducted assessment was in line with the gap listed by the WASH Cluster and targeted the sub-districts of Dana, Maaret Tamsrin, Jisr-Ash-Shugur, Ariha, Atareb, Albab, Agtrin, and Azaz. Assessment finding:- It was confirmed that access to drinking water is a huge need in most of the assessed locations as many IDPs Sites are suffering from water shortage for more than one year. The IDPs pay to purchase water, and there is no clear mechanism for water sterilization.- Sanitation activities are also one of the most important needs, especially during the cholera outbreak. There is a need to rehabilitate many of the sanitation networks, construct latrines blocks, and desludging the septic tanks in a proper way and follow the Cluster guidelines. - Hygiene promotion and awareness also was confirmed as a major need, as there is a need to provide hygiene intervention including control of water borne diseases (AWD/Cholera) through chlorination, provision of soap for hand washing and Risk Communication and Community Engagement (RCCE).- In addition to the needs to provide the SWM intervention in many of the IDPs Sites and some communities. AS a result, TAS prepared this project and seeks to improve the living condition for 90,000 individuals living in NWs by providing adequate and safe WASH services.

4 - Implementation of activities

1. Hygiene Kits/cholera kits will be distributed to the affected people in the IDP sites.2. hygiene awareness-raising sessions including the relevant IEC materials. 3. Water testing through water disinfectants (Chlorine will be added to the water following the Cluster updated measures "Standard range: FRC between 0.2 – 0.5 mg/L- Range during cholera outbreak: 0.5 – 1 mg/L").4. Improving access to water through providing both emergency or sustainable solutions based on the available resources. TAS plans to establish water networks and connect them to the nearest water station and borehole, In addition to equipping the water stations with all necessary equipment (renewal energy, chorine pump, generators...etc.). TAS will use water trucking only in emergencies (no more than 3 months until the water systems are completed).The provided water quantities will be based on the Cluster recommendation as follows: Trucking threshold: 25-35 lpcd (during cholera outbreak: 35-40 lpcd)Pumping threshold: 40-45 lpcd (during cholera outbreak: 40-50 lpcd)" 5. Improving access to sanitation through rehabilitaion and construction of sanitation networks and installation of latrines tacking into acount the PwDs needs. TAS will use the desludging septic tanks when needed.6.Providing solid waste management through garbage collection activity and garbage bins distribution.
