Project Summary

WRRS will work toward Improved access to food. This will be in line with the cluster target of reaching 2.1 million people both IDPs and non IDPs facing severe acute food insecurity. This will as well work towards improving Livelihood and related food sources to ensure that they are protected for 2 million people farmers, agro-pastoralists, pastoralists and IDPs across Somalia through emergency agriculture, livestock and other livelihood support in line with their livelihood and seasonality. WRRS through this response will work towards Improving access to food for beneficiaries within the targeted district both IDPs and non IDPs facing severe acute food insecurity. Through the outlined activities WRRS will work towards restoring livelihood and related food sources are protected for farmers, agro-pastoralists, pastoralists and IDPs across our targeted districts through emergency agriculture, livestock and other livelihood support in line with their livelihood and seasonality.
The current areas that we will be targeting in this response have diverse livelihoods and based on this the interventions will be tailored towards improving the current state to a better one accordingly. WRRS will seek to offer response to the needs that has affected both the host community and the IDPs who have sought to take refuge in these proposed areas. This will be attained through improvement of HH access to food by provision of unconditional and conditional cash transfers to the beneficiaries of the targeted district of Lascaanood and lower Juba. Besides this those who are pastorals will receive restock of animals offered with the vaccination, and poultry support with major focus of the female headed HH which will be accompanied by capacity building on best farming practices; veterinary care and pest control. The need to offer sustainable access to livelihood assets in hard to reach areas of Lower Juba and Sool will remain crucial and need urgent humanitarian response especially after the flood that has caused a lot of damage to the previously restored livelihood. The project interventions will give priorities to the less fortunate as well the marginalized including women, female headed households as well as people living with various disabilities.

Project Activities

Provision of emergency unconditional cash transfer and safety nets to 800 (300 IDPs and 500 HC) vulnerable IDP and host community households considering 60% of the beneficiaries registered as vulnerable women household in Bay and Lower Juba region south central Somalia. Each household will be received 70 USD per months including 7 USD for transport to the money vendor for 3 months period. Provision of unconditional cash transfers to vulnerable households and those at particular risk of exclusion from accessing livelihoods to increase their access to food, and strengthen purchasing power for essential household items and services. Provision of unconditional food vouchers to vulnerable households in IDPs urban areas in emergency and crisis in order to increase access to essential food and non-food items through supply of selected quantities of dry food items this is with an aim of improving access to food access to the targeted beneficiaries. The project will target the most vulnerable persons including boys, girls, men and women. At least 50% of the targeted households will be female headed and will be given at least 100% of MEB. Specific food that is culturally and religiously appropriate to the beneficiaries may be provided on a monthly basis Provision of farming inputs (spade, wheelbarrow, panga, shovels, seeds, fertilizers) to support irrigation and increase agricultural production for small scale subsistence farmers from asset poor HHs and minority people who lack the necessary collateral to access credit targeting 40% female-headed households to reduce negative survival mechanisms including livelihoods that increase their risk of violence and exploitation. •Provide cash for work activities to 1500 men and women household heads and enable create short term job opportunities and create income to the vulnerable drought displaced and AWD affected IDPs and host communities to enable improve the community livelihood structure hence provide alternative livelihood. The provision of Cash for Work activities to generate sufficient income for households in Emergency and Crisis to access at least 75% of the cost of the MEB while maintaining and rehabilitating communal assets. WRRS will target 60% female participants.•Sensitize vulnerable household benefiting from unconditional cash transfer on nutrition, health and hygiene promotion for food preparation to enable reduce malnutrition and disease hence improve community living standard. To 2000 HH restock livestock and animals depending on the livelihoods zone, some will receive farm inputs ranging from seeds and tools.•Conduct training to capacity building farmers (48 men and 62 women household headed) on proper preparing, harvesting as well as management of farm inputs storage hence enhancing their knowledge and thus inculcate skills for their livelihood in futures. Gender roles and cultural context will be a key consideration, so that women can undertake activities that are consistent with Somali cultural tradition.

Mitigation Measures

Gender disparities are on the rise in the operational communities, where women usually take a back seat in issues of community development. WRRS through the project, through community mobilization and awareness, will ensure women are empowered and represented to influence decisions and outcomes. A Do No Harm approach will be adopted in all our interventions. Contextual analysis is important in the implementation of this project considering the various potential sources of conflicts in the target locations both at leadership and community level. Regular assessments and community engagements will be done to learn if the project activities, approaches and methodologies are sensitive to the local context. Vulnerable community members generally refer to those disadvantaged with compromised access to resources to meet their daily basic needs. The project staff will ensure that during community mobilization and sensitization, all community members are given equal opportunity to participate.WRRS has been working in areas where security and conflict has been a major factor affecting the beneficiaries. Accountability for violations is limited, with traditional or formal mediation and justice mechanisms having been disrupted, or having become disrespected or inaccessible. Building community structures including local temporary learning space that will ensure tension is minimized. Staff will be trained on human rights based approach where all people should be treated equally, especially those who ran from conflict and violence. Communities fleeing violence will be immediately supported through access to immediate food security and livelihood services without any discrimination. Where IDPs are involved, sites that support them will be built in consultation with them and the host communities. Marginalized groups will be prioritized so that they don’t feel excluded, which can otherwise aggravate their situation if they have run from violence.Accountability to affected population is a key aspect in the trans-formative agenda. The project intends to involve the community in all stages of the program cycle, so that they understand the aim and the outcome of the entire project and embrace its as it rolls, they will also understand the guiding principle behind the decisions made.The project intends to use already existing community governance structures and use them as community committees. These will be involved in recruitment of staff, community mobilization and monitoring of the project.Their presence will represent the interest and role of the community in successful completion of the project. The project will also have a feedback mechanism , from which complaints and comments from the community will be collected, and fed back to the same. This will be done in a highly confidential manner with very few staff in the management allowed to interact with this process. WRRS will address the complains as they come and embrace the comments for the sustainability and acceptance of the services within the community since these are live saving interventions that will help the households have a sustainable livelihood income.

Please provide further explanation

WRRS intents to link the interventions to MOA (Ministry of Agriculture) so as to ensure sustainability of the interventions and towards handing over the responsibilities with the in line ministries. This project is designed to be implemented over a 12 month time frame with benefits gained from involvement extending beyond the duration of this intervention especially in terms of improved livelihood service delivery and child protection. By working with civil society leaders, district committees and other key members of local and regional administration and linking them together for maintenance and monitoring of the works will help reduce tensions between the groups, improve cohesion and aid with coordinated maintenance and responsibility of the works in the long term to ensure the legacy of this work. The Project will contribute to durable/ resilience of vulnerable populations against shocks as part of FSC objectives. This will include protecting and conserving natural resources on which lives, and livelihoods depend, such as water, soil, land and forest resources. The activities will be directed at repairing and effectively managing small-scale productive infrastructure to mitigate shocks (e.g. river de-silting and embankment repair to prevent seasonal floods) using a combination of machinery and human works as appropriate. As well, life skills training activities will be prioritized to increase household income for the vulnerable and food insecure households. Community-based preparedness, surveillance and early warning systems/networks will contribute to prevent damage or losses to crops and livestock.
