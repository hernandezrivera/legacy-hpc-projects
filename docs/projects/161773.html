Project Summary

Northeast has suffered greatly due to the Boko Haram insurgency. The activities of Boko Haram insurgents has created chaos and devastation in the NE region with colossal loss of lives and properties as well as severely affecting socio-economic, physical and emotional wellbeing of the people especially women and children. Since 2014, the war waged by the extremist group has affected the entire community, but among those hit the hardest were women, girls and children who now comprise a great percentage of IDPs in camps and host communities. Yobe State like any other State in the Northeast continues to face significant threats from the militant group known as Boko Haram, with growing potential for spillover effects throughout the region. Given the protracted nature of the crisis coupled with limited access to basic services, human rights violation happens to be on the increase as most women in affected communities endures domestic violence, including physical and sexual assault as identified by the United Nation Interagency Humanitarian needs assessment 2018/Gander Based Information Management System Mid-year review 2018 as a major challenge in humanitarian response in the Northeast.
As a serious human rights problem further intensified by the insurgency, it’s imperative to note that GBV requires all sectors to work in concert to strengthen prevention and response initiatives. Hence the need for adopting comprehensive approaches that allows women and girls to fully enjoy their rights to safety, dignity, and nondiscrimination.
In an effort to strengthen GBV protection and response mechanism at the grass roots level, African Youth for Peace, Development and Empowerment Foundation (AFRYDEV) seek create awareness among women groups on GBV prevention and response as well as build capacity of local actors such as the civil community based organisations, traditional and religious leaders, Women and Youth leaders in Damaturu, Geidam and Gujba L.G.As on how to prevent and respond to GBV related issues through documentation and reporting as well as referral, linkages for effective utilization of available services.
Specifically, AFRYDEV will hold 2 days in two series of sensitization on prevention and response to GBV targeting 200 women from most affected communities of Damaturu, Geidam and Gujba L.G.As. Other component of this activity will provide training for 300 stakeholders includes; traditional and religious leaders, women and youth leaders, L.G.A and Police Gender/Human rights desk Officers, out of which 50 will be selected to form the GVB response group in each of the L.G.As follow by it establishment and inauguration of members at the L.G.A level to facilitates documentation, reporting and referrals for quick response and access to comprehensive services for survivors.
These activities aim to build capacities of local actors on prevention, documentation and effective response to GBV related issues. The activities will directly benefit 1500 participants from three L.G.As which include 750 women and 150 men and more indirectly 1500 families and the communities. By implementing this activity we empower women and other stakeholders to be better equipped with skills and knowledge of GBV, hence reduce prevalent in insurgency affected communities.
Goal: The overarching goal of this activity is to strengthen prevention and response to GBV through awareness creation, reporting and effective linkages for access to comprehensive services.
