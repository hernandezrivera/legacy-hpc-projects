Project Summary

The project is designed to respond to the needs of the vulnerable targeted returnees dis-aggregated by age group and gender: women and adolescent girls (+ 12 years old) who are more prone for GBV and Psychosocial suffering.
Cordaid since 2017 works in different locations of Ninewa; Hamdaniyah, Bartella, Baghdaida, Wardak, Namrud, Sayed Hamad, Baashiqa and Sinjar, providing primary health services, GBV, PSS and MHPSS. Cordaid has a good experience in integrating GBV and MHPSS in PHCCs that Cordaid is supporting, especially after the success of such integration in the village of Seje in the governate of Duhok.
During this long period of working on Ninewa plain and Seje, many cases of GBV were identified. Cordaid discovered that many girls dropped out of schools due to long displacement, lack of schools and economic difficulties. Poor economic conditions have negative impact on families, particularly women and girls who are suffering from psychological problems. Women and girls also lack access to legal assistance with birth registration, new marriage and renewing displacement documents and IDs. Traditional practices such as forced marriage, marriage out of court, illegal immigration of husbands to third countries thus leaving wife and children under high pressure from family and community, all have the effect of increasing women and girls vulnerability and risk of GBV. Domestic violence, including physical and psychological assault by family members against women and girls is also very common.
Through this project, women center in the west side of Mousel will be established to provide case management and GBV psychosocial services such as dealing with depression, anxiety and stress from prolonged displacement, domestic and spousal stresses, physical illnesses due to the lack of basic needs, food, and employment. PSS will be provided for the returnees specially women and adolescent girls to minimize the consequences of GBV.
Supplies, equipment, and materials required for initial project start-up, implementation and activities will be procured and delivered, ie visibility materials, training materials, furniture, materials for VTC courses. The project site will be supplied with visibility banners and stickers to advertise the projects donors, partners and services offered at the center.
Qualified staff will be solicited from the area targeted, selected applicants will be interviewed, background checked, hired and contracts to be signed. Internal training for all staff (GBV service providers and non GBV service providers) on GBV prevention and response. The recruited staff also will be trained on referral and case forms, data collection, and internal policies including confidentiality, code of conduct, do no-harm guidelines, safety and security.
In necessary cases Cordaid utilizes the referral pathways to refer cases to the appropriate legal and medical service providers for additional support where appropriate.
Mobile teams throughout Mousel introduce Cordaid services available at the center through the dissemination of verbal and flyer information and gathering basic needs information in a friendly and professional manner. The teams emphasize that all services in the center are free of charge and that confidentiality is absolute. The mobilizers also assist the trained community members and center staff in mobilizing for Safety Audits.
Cordaid also provides women and adolescent girls with economic empowerment through vocational training program in sewing and hairdressing. The participants receive information on opportunities for market and income generations and how they can apply their newly acquired skills to gainful economic employment. Graduates of the courses receive start up kits to maximize empowerment potential. How is your project accountable to affected population

The project mainly is to support the returnees a increase the possibility of the resilience, but also the host community is participating in the activities, providing GBV, PSS and VCT to the returnees and host community will help in reducing the risk of GBV, also it will help them to overcome the crisis they have passed through and how to deal with such situation in the future. It helps them to feel like survivors rather than victims. Qualified staff will be solicited from the area targeted, selected applicants will be interviewed, background checked, hired and contracts to be signed. Internal training for all staff (GBV service providers and non GBV service providers) on GBV prevention and response. The recruited staff also will be trained on referral and case forms, data collection, and internal policies including confidentiality, code of conduct, do no-harm guidelines, safety and security. In necessary cases Cordaid utilizes the referral pathways to refer cases to the appropriate legal and medical service providers for additional support where appropriate. Mobile teams throughout Mousel introduce Cordaid services available at the center through the dissemination of verbal and flyer information and gathering basic needs information in a friendly and professional manner. The teams emphasize that all services in the center are free of charge and that confidentiality is absolute. The mobilizers also assist the trained community members and center staff in mobilizing for Safety Audits. Cordaid also provides women and adolescent girls with economic empowerment through vocational training program in sewing and hairdressing. The participants receive information on opportunities for market and income generations and how they can apply their newly acquired skills to gainful economic employment. Graduates of the courses receive start up kits to maximize empowerment potential. As part of prevention and awareness raising on GBV, Cordaid will conduct group meetings with women and girls, and organize awareness sessions. Community members will be trained on GBV prevention and response to increase the capacity of returnees in the targeted locations to enable them to identify and respond to GBV risks within their communities. TORs and Training Agenda Plans for the community members will be done including training on Safety Audit. Even though the project is mainly for women and adolescent girls, also men and boys will participate in some of the awareness sessions, focus group discussions, awareness campaign and other activities. Coordination will take place amongst the GBV WG and GBV SC. A feedback mechanism will deal with and follow up on complaints. Feedback will be received through complaint boxes, hot lines, Safety Audit and satisfaction surveys. Post activity monitoring will take place and the results to be shared with the cluster and relevant partners.
