Objectives

Ensure minimal disruptions in support of the food production system, aligning with the government of Bangladesh department of agriculture extension policies and directives in order to ensure continuity of crop production and harvest throughout critical agricultural seasons.

Needs

1. Access to food security and agriculture inputs 2. Access to entrepreneurship or small business(LoCOS analyze the community needs through observation, site visit, and consultation with the beneficiary as well as different level of stakeholders. Along with that, the primary need analysis of LoCOS crossed checked and verified with different secondary data and information from relevant sector and concern organizations).

Activities or outputs

1. To provide seasonal seeds and fertilizer to grow crops and vegetables for the host community households 2. To provide group-wise training on agriculture-based entrepreneurship building for the host community women Based on LoCOS primary data and field observation, due to outbreak of COVID 19, a long days lockdown is going on throughout the country that put negative impact on demand and supply side, people cannot go outside to purchase daily needs, cannot earn having no work, production decreased, farmers and produces cannot buy agricultural inputs. LoCOS crossed checked the data and information with the secondary sources. Therefore, it is high time to provide training on agriculture based entrepreneurship building and inputs for the vulnerable host community women & youth that beneficiaries can produce their own crops, vegetables, vermin compost to cover up the pandemic. However, no cash support will be provided among the beneficiaries. All activities will be accomplished according to the COVID 19 implementation procedure such as, social distancing will be maintained, necessary protection will be ensured such as gloves, mask, hand washing, personal cleanliness and so on

Key project/performance indicators

1. No. of Host community household get agricultural inputs2. No. of host community women get agricultural entrepreneurship building training
