Project Summary

AHF will maintain the provision of nutrition services through the stabilization center in Sarmada children’s hospital which is considered the pediatric referral hospital in Idleb governorate, 4 OTP centers in Sarmada, Salama, Atareb, and Afrin (these areas have high incidence of malnutrition according to the last SMART Survey conducted in July 2022), 15 RRTs covering the camps in communities around Sarmada (280,000) in Dana subdistrict, 3 covering Salama IDPs camps (40,000), and 2 RRT covers Atareb area (70,000), (IDPs 296,000, Host communities 100,000 and 4,000 returnees) including 16,000 PwDs, the number of targeted children under 5 years is 191,600, 66,600 PLWs and 2,950 of other caregivers including men.
The staff of the centers and the RRTs are qualified and well-trained in many topics in the nutrition cluster.
The nutrition services include:
- Outpatients therapeutic Program Centre (OTP), 4 centers in Sarmada, Salama, Atareb, and Afrin run by 1 pediatrician, 2 Nutritionists, and 1 data Entry cover OPD services on a daily, providing assessment and management of outpatient pediatrics with malnutrition including SAM, MAM patient follow up, routine assessment and treatment for MAM and SAM without complication, receive referrals from RRTs to OTP Centre.
- Stabilization Centers in Sarmada children’s hospital and planned Afrin hospital: this is run by 1 pediatrician, 1 Nutritionist, 3 nurses (1 nurse every 8 hours, 3 shifts), and 1 data Entry, 3 cleaner cover inpatient services daily 24/7, providing daily assessment and management of inpatient pediatrics with SAM with complication.
- RRTs: 20 RRTs (15 in Dana subdistrict and Idleb, 3 in A'zaz subdistrict, and 2 in Atareb) each team includes: 2 Nutritionists, 2CHW, and 1 data Entry, covers outreach services:
Routine assessment and treatment for MAM and SAM without complication, screening, referral to OTP center and follow-up children 6-59, pregnant and lactating women who are malnourished, Providing emergency IYCF counseling for mothers with moderately malnourished children
Distribute micronutrients for preventing anemia and malnutrition among children and pregnant, lactating women, IYCF-E recommended feeding practices, monitor distribution and use of breast milk substitutes, Support health/nutrition education and promotion, and Nutrition Supplements distribution, These RRTs will be linked to AHF's health facilities in the target communities to receive the referred cases.
The RRTs will provide FMA related to recommendation and outbreak as (Cholera and COVID19).
During the project implementation, the M&E team will regularly collect the required data to help make enlightened decisions. Monitoring and evaluation of the quality of the service provision through analyzing the collected data based on the Log-Frame, the project will be monitored and evaluated through three levels:
1) The operational, for which the project manager is responsible.
2) The technical, for which the technical team is responsible.
3) The objectives, for which the MEAL team is responsible.
Monitoring tools:
• Performance Indicator Tracking Tables (PITTs), issued on a monthly basis.
• Satisfaction surveys among beneficiaries and stakeholders will be analyzed and reported to stakeholders.
• Project Evaluation Report, by the end of the project term.

3 - Needs

The nutrition situation in NWS is deteriorating, CU5 & PLWs are the most susceptible to malnutrition since their nutritional requirements are relatively high, based on Syria-HNO 2022, the prevalence of malnutrition especially chronic type is a significant public health challenge in Syria. The main groups affected by malnutrition are infants, CU5 and PLWs. According to the last SMART Survey conducted (July 2022), The overall Global Acute Malnutrition (GAM) rate by Weight for Height (WHZ) was 3.3% (2.0 – 5.4%.C.I.) and Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM) was 1.7% (0.6 – 4.4%.C.I.). The GAM rate by Middle Upper Arm Circumference (MUAC) was 5% (3.3 - 7.5%.C.I.). The prevalence of wasting among pregnant and lactating women (PLWs) was 12.9% (8.8 – 18.6%.C.I.).In addition, according to the SMART survey conducted by PAC in July 2022, the prevalence of chronic malnutrition among children aged 6-59 months the stunting rate did not show a significant improvement compared to 2021 results, no significant difference. While the GAM rate by MUAC showed approximately the same rate in comparison with 2021 SMART survey, the GAM rate decreased from 4.7% (2.7 – 8.1 95% C.I) to 4.3% (2.6 – 6.9 95%.C.I.) otherwise, the SAM rate by MUAC increased from 0.8% (0.2 – 2.7 95% C.I.) to 1.3% (0.5 – 3.4 95% C.I.).According to HNO 2022, the project areas had the following number of PiN: Dana subdistrict has 0.37 million, A'zaz has 0.1 M, Mareet Tamisrin has 0.26 M, Atareb 0.06 M, 5 level at the severity scale.

4 - Implementation of activities

Through this project, AHF will work on recruitment, provide the salaries to the staff and support the operational costs, and provide capacity building program to staff members covering many topics.Through 2 SCs, 4 OTPs, and 20 RRTs AHF will provide the following:- Screening, referral and follow-up children 6-59, pregnant and lactating women who are malnourished. - Provide emergency IYCF counseling for mothers with moderately malnourished children.- Disseminating IYCF-E messages among mothers of children under five (individual counseling and group sessions)- Registering all screened cases and reporting to the nutritionists and data entry team members. - Distribute micronutrients for preventing anemia and malnutrition among children and pregnant, lactating women Disseminate messages (IEC/BCC) on IYCF-E recommended feeding practices.- Nutrition Supplements distribution.- Disseminate messages (IEC/BCC) on IYCF-E recommended feeding practices.- Referral CU5 with dangers signs and Medical problems, referral PLWs with Medical problems. - Referral of medical cases to nearby health facilities.- Treatment MAM in CU5 by OTP and the RRTs.- Treatment SAM uncomplicated in CU5 by the OTPs.- Treatment SAM with complications in CU5 by the stabilization centers.- Identifying the abused cases among boys and girls 6-59 months, pregnant and lactating women to be referred to child protection partners to take an action.-Monthly 4Ws reporting to the cluster (M&E mentioned in Summary)

8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

AHF is an active member of the PSEA network, AHF has updated PSEA and child safeguarding policies and procedures in early 2021, including all levels from prevention, reporting, investigation, and survivor support. AHF has male & female PSEA focal points in Syria and in Turkey offices, they support the implementation of AHF PSEA strategy, and they carry out the following tasks:- Ensure that employees are aware of their obligations related to protection from sexual exploitation and abuse. - Equip employees with the knowledge and skills to prevent Sexual Exploitation and Abuse and Response to Both.- Create mechanisms for reporting protection from sexual exploitation and abuse at the community level, including establishing contact points, according to the location and settings for reporting and posting.- Engage with communities and key stakeholders in strengthening community complaints mechanisms, including determining the provision of victim assistance services in all targeted locations.- Conduct regular awareness-raising activities on protection from sexual exploitation and abuse, policies, standards, and codes of conduct related to protection from sexual exploitation.- Provide specific awareness sessions to target groups such as women, girls, boys, and men in reception centers, camps, sites for displaced people, host communities, and society.- Ensure all project personnel sign the Code of Conduct for PSEA.
