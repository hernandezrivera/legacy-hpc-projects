Project Summary

UNICEF will contribute in 2023 to the interagency humanitarian response and It will also complement UNICEF development work under its Country Programme 2023-2026. Will focus on strengthening delivery and enhancing access to basic services to address the multiple deprivations in health faced by children and children on the move, while building local resilience and capacities. UNICEF response incorporates a child-centred approach and is articulated around community establishments, including healthcare facilities and protection centres, learning spaces, and surrounding communities. Priority will be given to identifying children that reflect the multiple deprivations and urgent needs that require assistance. Opportunities to access for health services will be delivered, while ensuring safe and adequate WASH services, together with child protection and interventions in schools to enhance the overall mental health and wellbeing of children and caregivers. Moreover, an important aim of the proposed action is also to build the resilience of the targeted children, their families, and communities through the implementation of health, nutrition, child protection and WASH interventions at the community and healthcare facility levels. These action will be implemented in selected urban, peri-urban and rural remote areas of 8 prioritized states at nationwide and will reach 683,649 people, including 553,649 children and 150,000 pregnant women.

1 - INTERSECTORIALIDAD

UNICEF's response incorporates a child-centered approach and is articulated around community facilities, including health facilities and shelters, learning spaces and surrounding communities. Priority will be given to identifying children who reflect multiple deprivations and urgent needs requiring assistance. Opportunities to access health services will be provided, while ensuring safe and appropriate WASH services, along with child protection and interventions in schools to improve the general mental health and well-being of children and their caregivers. UNICEF will support the provision of maternal and child services, especially care for safe delivery, prenatal consultations, adolescent pregnancy, promotion of mental health in adolescents and learning spaces, pediatric emergencies, immunization, provision of ART for children under 15 years of age and delivery of neonatal services.UNICEF, as the main provider of ARV treatment for children in Venezuela, maintains close coordination with UNAIDS and PAHO, to avoid ARV shortages in the most remote places. A joint distribution has been agreed with UN agencies to ensure the deployment of ARVs for children and adults at the same time, with UNICEF being the agency that leads the distribution of all ARVs in 13 states.UNICEF also in coordination with the Ministry of Health and PAHO has completed the evaluation of the cold chain and is the main agency for the strengthening of the cold chain, also leading the introduction of ecological equipment with renewable energy.UNICEF has established strong coordination with UNFPA to improve maternal and sexual reproductive health. Joint interventions established for a training program focused on improving differentiated consultation for adolescents.

2 - ESTRATEGIA BIANUAL

The strategic interventions in Humanitarian Action Project for Children are aligned with CPD approach and will complement UNICEF development work under its Country Programme 2023-2026. The strategy to reach the results will focus on strengthening delivery and enhancing access to basic services to address the multiple deprivations faced by children and improve thier rights.
