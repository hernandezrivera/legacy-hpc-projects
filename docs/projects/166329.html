Project Summary

This project aims at containing the spread of COVID_ 19 Epidemic and safe provision of essential Child protection services to enhanced the wellbeing of conflict affected children, targeting 8060 Boys, Girls, Caregivers, Community Members and Stakeholders in Mobar, Jere, Rann and Damboa

1 - Needs

• Life-saving and essential child protection case management services for existing and new caseloads are likely to be disrupted due to control measures including movement restrictions, physical distancing and limited access to protective equipment for CP case workers. • Separation of children from their parents/caregivers/families as a result of movement restrictions, isolation measures by health authorities, hospitalization and death of caregivers and other family members as a result of the pandemic. Children without parental care, either living on the streets or in other institutions may also be at heightened risk. • Mental health and psychosocial well-being (MHPSS) of children is likely to be negatively impacted due to disruption of education and social activities, reduced access to existing MHPSS services, distress arising from lack of information or misinformation about COVID 19 and reduced or disrupted access for children and families to basic goods and services such as food and health. • Negative coping mechanisms as a result of new and exacerbation of existing stressors may lead to increased abuse of and violence against children such as child labour, child marriage and other forms of gender-based violence. • Monitoring of grave child rights violations and provision of interim and community-based rehabilitation and reintegration services will be affected, and this will in turn affect the verification and reporting for evidence-based programming and advocacy. • CP workers may be at heightened risk of exposure and infection due to the nature primarily of child protection case management and psychosocial support services. CP workers require orientation on COVID 19 and preventative measures as well as access to protective gear. Some children may reject child protection services when they are provided by CP workers wearing protective gear due to stigma or fearGuidance Note: Child Protection Service Provision and Caring for Children in the Context of the COVID 19 Pandemic

2 - Activities

Awareness campaigns on specific Child protection key messaging targeting children, parents/care givers, pregnant women and their families, people with disability : focusing on actions to prevent Covid-19 infection and stop disease transmission, Social distancing practice, hand washing with soap after sneezing/coughing, before collecting water, attention to multi touch surfaces (doors, chairs, tables, phones etc) Provision of essential mental health and psychosocial support services including psychological first aid for children and caregivers to alleviate stress and anxiety resulting from the pandemic and related control measures Child protection case management including referral to specialized services for existing caseloads and newly identified vulnerable children

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

An alternative reporting measure have been explore by YIPDI M & E Team using technology in this Covid 19 context, 10 individual including adolescents are going to be selected as two Monitoring and reporting team under the CBCP working group and will be trained on reporting using their cell phones Via( calls, Whatsapp, voices mails, and text messages), and would be trained to snap pictures for evidence base reporting pattern in case of total lockdown. The essence of the Child Protection monitoring and reporting Team (CPMRT) is to promote a valuable opportunity for the CBCP working group and the community to reflect on progress to date and to alter plans for effectiveness and efficiency. The shared reports will inform partner, Donor and Community stakeholders to ensure accountability, enhance transparency and could be used for Advocacy, decision making, resources used as well as progress toward outcomes and goals.
