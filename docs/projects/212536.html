Project Summary

The proposed project is aimed to ensure education continuity for these affected children, which is considered vital for children who require further support tailored to their levels through non-formal education program after they had either displaced or dropped out due to the hard circumstances of earthquake they witnessed, and since still many children in northwest Syria have learning gaps that affected their level which can be assessed through ASER and other adapted tools to determine the most appropriate non-formal education intervention for them.
The project will consider children with disabilities to offer them access to schools and learning opportunities helping to include and merge them in the mainstreaming classes to develop and promote their academic and life skills while giving attention to their individual impairments and learning difficulties.
The project targets both in and out-of-school children, supporting 6 new schools in 3 subdistricts in Idleb: 2 in Harim, 2 in Kafr Takharim and 2 in Qourqeena to include children at both primary key stage 1 & 2 (ages 6-14) and children with disabilities (ages 6-12). Specific activities will include non-formal education for 1,500 children (800 boys-700 girls), 500 children in each subdistrict, establishment of 6 special education centers inside the 6 schools to reach a number of 24 children with disabilities (15 boys- 9 girls) at these centers, where each center will welcome and work with 4 children who have moderate to severe cases of disabilities in preparation to include them in the mainstreaming classrooms with their peers, and each center will be equipped with an assigned special education teacher and the needed materials to assess each child’s need and implement the activities of each child’s individual plan aiming to develop the motor, mental, cognitive and academic skills of these children throughout the academic year.
Hurras’ team will ensure quality of education is offered through a set of planned activities with teachers and students, including the follow up on students’ progress on the pre-posttest, and tailor the NFE program to the specific gaps extracted from these results, additionally our team will provide reflections and feedback to the teachers through the coaching and technical support approach whenever our team will attend samples of the sessions offered.
The project will include
There are still other activities as part of the design, including the use of established WhatsApp groups with children and caregivers to share exercises to do at home aiming to increasing the self-learning hours and promote the caregivers’ role in their children’ education and progress, as well as holding monthly sessions of the school’s parents- teachers association “ PTA” to discuss issues related to the education and protection of the enrolled students.
The project will establish child friendly spaces inside the 6 schools with the necessary recreational materials in order to maintain a routine of schools-based PSS program with the enrolled children, the sessions will follow a recreational and inclusive approach for all enrolled children at all grades.
Hurras will also support the schools with the monthly running materials including stationery for teachers, cleaning materials, as well as filling the schools’ water tanks on a monthly basis with an amount to suffice for the needs of children and staff.
Another major part of the project is the rehabilitation of schools’ buildings that were affected by the earthquake, since they are located in areas significantly affected by the devastating earthquake, and utilizing this to address aspects of gender and disability inclusion of the WASH facilities within the schools.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Education in northwest Syria is not equally stable in all areas, the security situation in addition to natural disasters are usually the key factors in creating this imbalance and protracting the situation. There is a number of over 900,000 Children and people in need of education in different areas in Idleb governorate, with the highest severity in districts of Ariha-Harim-Idleb and Jisr-Ash-Shugur, children in hard to reach areas including camps still experience high rates of education disruption, leading to academic loss and academic delays, therefore the planned intervention is to target and support schools within these areas. in 2023, the MSNA in northern Syria shows that the percentage of children with disabilities not enrolled in school stands at 72 per cent, compared to 40 per cent for children without disabilities. The data also shows that enrolment rates for children with disabilities drop by about 10 percentage points after the age of 14, reaching 81 per cent for children aged 15-17. The project’s activities will be implemented by experienced education staff who will be supervising the teachers and other staff while delivering the education activities, in order to ensure the below: -Proper registration of children and quality of delivery of the non-formal education sessions by teachers, where the program will be defined according to the assessment results. -Proper identification and assessment of children with disabilities and the provision of individual sessions as per the developed plan to each child in the school by the special education teacher. -Establishment of child friendly room and provision of PSS sessions to children. -Rehabilitation of the supported schools which will be decided according to assessment of buildings and may include the WASH facilities, classrooms and play yard. -Conducting the monthly PTA meetings. -Providing the schools’ personnel stipends.
