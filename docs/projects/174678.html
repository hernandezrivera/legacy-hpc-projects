Project Summary

The objective/outcome of this project is to support vulnerable people among out-of-camp refugees and host communities affected by the Boko Haram crisis in the Far North Region to be more resilient to environmental, social and economic shocks through the Humanitarian-Development-Peace Nexus.
Output 1: Improve economic capacity of vulnerable persons and households
Activity 1.1 Strengthen economic capacity of vulnerable people like youth and women among IDPs, returnees and host communities through labour-intensive activities like cash for work for community recovery/development
Activity 1.2 Rehabilitate/construct basic economic infrastructure (e.g. boreholes and markets)
Output 2: Promote sustainable empowerment of vulnerable people through multi-sectoral and inclusive economic recovery activities with the environmental respect
Activity 2.1 Provide training on economic integration such as technical expertise, business management, transversal issues of life and environment protection/respect
Activity 2.2 Provide inputs including kits and capital for sustainable economic activities
Activity 2.3 Establish/strengthen community savings systems
Activity 2.4 Strengthen the capacity of socio-economic institutions such as training centres and market management structures
Output 3: Strengthen institutional, community and individual capacity to improve resilience and to Promote risk prevention and management
Activity 3.1 Strengthen the capacity of key stakeholders such as local authorities, community and religious leaders and associations of youth, women, farmers, herders and out-of-camp refugees in risk prevention and crisis management
Activity 3.2 Strengthen the capacity of individuals in communities for risk prevention and crisis management through training, awareness raising events, local initiatives, debates and forums
Main target: vulnerable people like youth (18-34 years old) and women among out-of-camp refugees and host communities, and authorities such as government officials and religious/community leaders

1 - Besoins / Needs

The crisis led by armed groups, esp. Boko Haram has negatively affected people and communities in the Far North region as limited economic opportunities, down-sized local economy and limited access to basic social services and natural resources. The majority of displaced persons could bring minimum goods/assets when they fled and they have lost means for their livelihoods. Moreover, many of them have also faced difficulty to change livelihoods due to available inputs in displaced places. The capacity of existing mechanisms has also been limited to prevent and manage conflicts which could be increased due to the massive displacement of people, in particular refugees, IDPs and returnees. There is risk to deteriorate risk and conflicts among people in communities. Therefore, displaced persons including Nigerian refugees, IDPs and returnees, and vulnerable host communities need to be economically empowered while communities need to strengthen the capacity of risk prevention and crisis management.
