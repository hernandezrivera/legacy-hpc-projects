Project Summary

The HALO Trust will continue to deliver an integrated Mine Action approach in northwest Syria involving minefield clearance, explosive ordnance risk education (EORE), non-technical survey (NTS), explosive ordnance disposal (EOD) and victim assistance (VA).
Using armoured heavy machinery, HALO will complete clearance of an anti-personnel minefield in agricultural ground in Ariha, and aims to commence clearance of another minefield by the end of the year. This will enable farmers to use land productively without fear of injury, death and loss of livestock and equipment. HALO is the only organisation in NWS with capability for sub-surface clearance of explosive hazards.
HALO will continue to deploy two mixed-gender explosive ordnance disposal teams to locate and safely dispose of explosive hazards found on the surface of the ground. Using a range of EOD techniques and supervised remotely from Amman by technical specialists, each team disposes of an average of one item per day, ranging from grenades and mortars to rockets and various improvised devices. They are also focusing on agricultural areas where the removal of explosive hazards not only contributes to casualty reduction but also boosts economic activity.
HALO will provide EORE to men, women, boys and girls, with a focus on community members most at risk of accidents (including children, teenagers, IDPs, agricultural workers and rubble removal workers).
Following the completion in 2020 of HALO's contamination impact survey work in all accessible communities in NW Syria, capturing the scope of explosive ordnance (EO) contamination in the region, in 2022 HALO started conducting detailed non-technical survey (NTS) to define precisely the types of hazards and technical requirements for disposal. NTS will continue in 2024 and provide local authorities, humanitarian actors, the Gaziantep cluster network, and the donor community with a greater understanding of EO threats, including specific locations.
The support to survivors of EO accidents will continue through a Victim Assistance (VA) component. HALO will provide victim assistance case management services, providing referrals and transportation to specialised services for survivors of EO accidents.
HALO acknowledges the requirement to avoid activity duplication within the Mine Action Working Group as well as addressing gaps in providing EORE (to children, adults, CFPs, and NGO Focal Points), NTS and EOD. This will be achieved through ongoing and regular coordination with other members of the MAWG, of which HALO is a leading member, as well as engagement with local authorities, communities, and other NGOs throughout the implementation of the project.
