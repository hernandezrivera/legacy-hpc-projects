Project Summary

The people in need of WASH services across the country is 5.9 million people in need for WASH assistance for 2021 across the country. Living standard and wellbeing of an estimated 5.6 million people will be negatively impacted by lack of or inadequate access to clean water and improved sanitation and hygiene practices including the project targeted counties Wau, Aweil South and Tonj North.
WASH project will focus on 2021 WASH objectives and consequently the lifesaving needs to the affected population. The differential needs, concerns and priorities of women and girls, men and boys of different ages and disparities will be addressed basing on the three main WASH cluster objectives.
Based on WASH Cluster weekly updates, Cluster interactive dashboards, Cluster response and monitoring framework and IOM displacement tracking matrix. The main drivers of WASH needs include limited physical infrastructure, sub-national violence and recurring flooding and COVID -19 pandemic in project focused counties. These shocks have led to the continued displacement of people and restricted their access to already limited and functional WASH infrastructure. Increased displacement also places an extra burden on existing WASH infrastructure in displacement sites..
The project response 2021 response will scale up by improving access to primary health care services by constructing latrines, training of hygiene promoters male and female, creating awareness and educate the communities on the importance of good hygiene practices, promoting better health and reducing the vulnerability linked to diseases caused by poor hygiene activities, provide WASH messages in the OTPs sites and environmental hygiene, awareness on dangers of COVID 19, cholera and provision of clean drinking water.
The project will target 33300 beneficiaries constitute of Women 7992, Girls 8658 Men 7659 and Boys 8991 to undertake different roles and activities of the project. Community approach and vulnerability criteria will be used in beneficiary selection whereby men, female boys and girls shall be given equal opportunities in project participation.
The focused counties, the communities rely on surface water and lack of sanitation facilities leading to diseases outbreaks as well as conflicts that led to inadequate delivery of basic services that include better health and provision of clean drinking water. The project will ensure WASH facilities are safe, gender appropriate and maintained in secure locations as discussed with affected groups most focus on women, girls and persons with disabilities.
The IDPs, GAM rates and displacement puts a strain on existing water sources and increases WASH needs such as water and vector borne diseases. High malnutrition rates linked to poor WASH conditions, as exemplified in the diarrhea malnutrition cycle prevalent in vulnerable populations.
The project aim at scaling up provision of clean water, sanitation and health services to the communities with focus on 2021 WASH Cluster objectives and consequently the lifesaving needs to the affected population where WASH priorities remains to address the acute vulnerabilities.
Since conflict, sub-national violence, COVID-19 restrictions and flooding have exacerbated the already extreme levels of GBV. Women and girls who are responsible for water collection face increased risk of harassment, assault and sexual violence when collecting water far from their homes.
The project will mitigate WASH related GBV with high risk of protections issues by enhancing safety audits whereby women shall be given equal opportunities in project implementation, monitoring and evaluation. The project will as well respond to the protection crisis GBV by providing services to survivors of GBV and dedicate services for women and girls with protection concerns.
