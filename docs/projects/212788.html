Project Summary

In line with the health sector's objective to increase access to life-saving and life-sustaining coordinated, equitable humanitarian health services for those most vulnerable and in need, this project aims to enhance access and utilization of essential, quality health and nutrition services through community and facility-integrated platforms through:
Provision of essential primary and secondary healthcare services in 5 PHCs, 2 stabilization centers, 15 CHWs teams, provision of health facilities with essential medicines and medical supplies, including personal protective equipment (PPE), and provision of vaccines to vaccination teams in NWS.
1- Based on Mercy-USA's assessment and in coordination with the Idlib Health Directorate and the health cluster, five Primary Health Care (PHC) centers have been identified as gaps in the following health facilities:
Idleb – Maaret Tamsrin – Kelly (Kelly PHC), Code: G0700008 (health severity 4 – Nutrition severity 4)
Idleb – Ariha – Mhambal (Alij PHC), Code: G0705047 (health severity need 4)
Idleb – Darkosh (Aldaher PHC), Code: GO70404042 (health severity need 5)
Idleb – Salqin (Azmarin PHC), Code: G0703062 (health severity need 4)
Idleb – Jisr-Ash-Shugur (MechemchanPHC), Code: G0704014 (health severity need 4)
Under this activity, Mercy-USA will employ a strategy targeting unserved areas classified as severity 3 and 4. This strategy is designed to deliver: A facility-based approach with CHWs. Sustaining and expanding essential health services for those most vulnerable in need in northwest Syria by supporting pre-exist five PHC facilities in 2024.
These 5 PHCs will adhere to the EHSP for northern Syria, developed in 2016 and revised in 2019. Community-level services will align with the CHW curriculum established by WHO for NWS
PHC Service package:
the supported health facilities under this PD will provided the below services:
Maternal and Reproductive Health: Maternal and reproductive care will adhere to the Minimum Initial Service Package (MISP) and SRH protocols established by UNFPA and SRH TWG for NWS. This includes but is not limited to Antenatal care, Postnatal care, and family planning services, syndromic management of STIs, Post-abortion care, and referral services.
Child Health: Immunization, Neonatal and child health care: Prevention and case management of Communicable and Non-Communicable diseases. This includes Essential Newborn Care (ENC)- thorough care for newborns, focusing on thermal protection, clean cord care, initiation of breastfeeding, and infection prevention- Immunization, Management of Acute Malnutrition, Integrated Management of Childhood Illness (IMCI)-Holistic care for common childhood illnesses, emphasizing early diagnosis, appropriate treatment, and health education- Safe Motherhood Practices, Psychosocial Support, and Child Protection(Implementing measures to safeguard children from exploitation, abuse, and violence during emergencies).
Prevention and case management of Communicable and Non-Communicable diseases.
Health and nutrition awareness raising and hygiene promotion.
The PHCs also will be part of the referral network so Mercy-USA in coordination with the referral's partners will identify a referral pathway or cases in need of a higher level of healthcare.
2- Two stabilization centers (SCs) will be operated to provide in-patient services for children between 6- 59 months diagnosed with SAM with medical complications:
Idleb, Harim, Kafr Takharim, Facility code G0703020
Idleb, Jisr-Ash-Shugur, Badama, Ein El-Bayda community, facility code G0704002
3- Support vaccination teams in NWS with vaccines in partnership with UNICEF. Mercy USA will be responsible for receiving vaccines from UNICEF at zero points and managing the cold chain and storage of vaccines in addition to the distribution of vaccines to vaccination teams in NWS in close coordination with the Syrian Immunization Group (SIG).

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The five PHCs and two SCs will serve a total population of 70,000 individuals will be targeted, including 1,400 persons with disabilities (PWD). with the following breakdown: 25,000 women and adolescents, 15,000 men, and 30,000 children.In close coordination with the health cluster CHW TWG, the 15 CHW teams will be affiliated with the 5 PHCs and provide community-based services using the WHO CHW curriculum developed for NWS.In addition, the management of moderate and severe acute malnutrition without medical complications will be provided in the 5 PHCs as an integrated service with the PHC service package. The project will aim to enhance and optimize the Immunization Supply Chain System in NWS. It aims to enhance ANC/PNC nutrition counseling for PLW/newborns through a participatory, iterative human-centered design process. It ensures stakeholder engagement and attention to inclusivity and power dynamics in co-design sessions.
