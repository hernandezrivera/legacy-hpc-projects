1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The escalating humanitarian crisis in Cabo Delgado, exacerbated by conflict and climate change-induced drought, has a particularly devastating impact on girls and boys with great intensity in the central-northen districts (Quissanga, Macomia, Meluco e Muidumba, Nangade and Mocimboa da Praia) part of the province. The situation escalates sporadically to the southern districts of Chiure and Ancuabe. The project aims to ensure equal access to education for all children and physical and safety barriers to inclusive quality education at the school level by enhancing the safety of the learning environment, considering gender and disability-specific needs in consultation with children and other key stakeholders, and by mainstreaming gender approach in project implementation (e.g. implementing gender sensitive activities gender responsive PSS- SEL, addressing girls’ increased risk to school dropout and SGBV in collaboration with School Councils and referral groups and through parental training), as well as integrating gender specific actions to the activities such as Menstrual Health Management for students aged 11 to 17, supporting adolescent mothers to be enrolled and retained in school, training on gender transformative education tackling harmful gender norms, and conducting vulnerability analysis for beneficiary selection considering gender, disability and population type (IDP, returnee, HC). The project will reinforce the already existing systems in place, particularly in terms of safeguarding, PSEA and Accountability.To identify and respond to the potential environmental impact of the action, Plan International uses the Global PI Environmental Impact Assessment Tool. An initial screening pointed potential negative impact associated with classroom rehabilitation and latrine construction and outlined preliminary measures to ensure environmental sustainability following local best practices and WASH cluster guidelines.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Protection consists of protecting children and affected people from violence, exploitation, abuse and neglect and keeping them safe from any danger. The school environment, where a child must acquire cognitive knowledge, needs a more protective environment. Wherever possible, the School Councils should work in close colaboration with the Community Child Protection Committees and the Child Protection Reference Group.Teachers and School Council members should be trained in Protection and Safeguard. IEC materials on protection should be placed in public places to ensure proper dissemination of information.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

Plan ensures a range of accessible and rapid communication channels with affected people, and uses them to inform affected people about the procedures, structures and processes that affect them, so that they can make informed decisions and choices through sessions with beneficiaries. From time to time, Plan facilitates friendly discussion sessions with school councils, pupils' classes and other project participants to record their concerns and satisfaction.Arrangements are put in place to enable meaningful participation at all stages of the planning and implementation phases of the operation. The provisions are accessible to all groups in a community and communicated to all those affected. 6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The project will provide humanitarian support to approximately 1,500 of the most vulnerable out-of-school children (i.e. victims of SGBV, UASC, CwDs, ex-CAAFAG) to cover compulsory school enrolment. Plan International will provide SDEJTs with support and capacity building on disability inclusion disability mapping, using the Washington Group question sets and implementing improved tools piloted by the other projects since 2022 in other districts, with the aim of identifying PWDs as well as their barriers and facilitators to education, including learning support technologies. The project will then facilitate referral to specialised services and work, in coordination with the working group on disability work in coordination with local government institutions to defend the right of people with disabilities to education and provision of assistive technologies on the basis of the assessments carried out.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Plan International, through the Basic School Emergency Plan PEBE (UNIHABITAT) and the Preparedness, Response and Recovery strategy of the Education sector in Emergency Situations (MINEDH), are guiding instruments for education, training and production of a document for prevention and response to all catastrophe situations that affect the environment and the school community. These activities will include:- Collection of data on areas, students, teachers and infrastructure populations in need;- Train school councils on maintaining infrastructure and providing clean teaching-learning materials;- Preparation of an immediate response plan including resources;- Repair partially affected and destroyed classrooms, temporary spaces, latrines;- Establish mechanisms to ensure that learning is resumed/continued as quickly as possible for all students and at all levels of education;- Strengthen EeE coordination groups at district and school level;- Integrate education on health and healthy nutrition into the local curriculum of all schools and in the continuing training of teachers;- Ensure that each school has an intern school disaster risk management committee and an appropriately updated basic school emergency plan
