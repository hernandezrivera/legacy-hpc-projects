Project Summary

The project “ Multisectorial services for vulnerable women and girls in COVID-19 response” mainly aims at raising voices of women and adolescent girls in COVID-19 and increased their access to quality GBV multi-sectoral and gender responsive services. The project has two main outcomes; (1)Increased access and use of multi-sectorial andgender responsive services by vulnerable women and adolescents’ girls’ (right holders) survivors of GBV (2) Community increased awareness and effectively engaged in GBV response during COVID-19 . Outcomes will be achieved within the project duration “12 months” with total budget 200.000 $ Project’s scope of interventions is directly linked to OCHA emergency plan through outbreak of Covid-19 and its impact on Gender justice in the community . The project will target vulnerable women, adolescents’ girls’ survivors of GBV and their families in the Two governates of the Gaza Strip (Gaza, North Gaza). CWLRCP will apply “Theory of Change” in designing the response impact, outcome and outputs with emphasis on change at individual level (vulnerable women & girls: suspended women, divorced, wives of mental health patients, addicts, WWDs and women with cancer, at risk/early married girls) and their families through provision of remote multi-sectoral comprehensive services.Change at community level (men from families of women and local leaders) through remote awareness raising and discussion forums, The project considered the outbreak of COVID-19 and restriction on movement besides the continuous deteriorated humanitarian situation in Gaza Strip through using ICT tools, and remote counseling system in addition to flexibility in the design and implementation plan

9 - Protection mainstreaming
9.1 - Do No Harm

CWLRCP follows the international standard for protection and the Hayat SOPs for GBV in Gaza with high coordination the protection cluster,-CWLRCP adopted “do no harm ”principles & will ensure confidentiality and safety of target groups so no disclosure of cases information will be done during mental health and psychological support .CWLRCP will follow the PA and WHO official precaution procedures related to the spread of corona virus/COVID-19 CWLRCP will take safety and protection measures in implementing the project activities such as distributing Hygiene kits for all men CWLRCP has security policy, complaint system ,sexual exploitation and abuse policy ,anti-corruption policy ,code of conduct ,gender policy, child protection policy, internal control policies, project management manual ,MEAL plan

9.2 - Equal and impartial access to aid

CWLRCP will implement the project activities in Gaza Strip particularly Gaza City and arrange for people’s access to assistance and services – in proportion to need and without any barriers (e.g. discrimination). Pay special attention to individuals and groups who may be particularly vulnerable or have difficulty accessing assistance and services in addition to that , CWLRCP follow the guidelines in its "Risk management plan including risk analysis and risk mitigation" and "Security Manual" to protect the lives of their staff during humanitarian work in Gaza StripCWLRCP follow also shifting approach via shifting power within, power with and power to vulnerable women and girls’ survivors of GBV.- The project will be adequately announced in the targeted communities using various outreach tools to give equal opportunities for women, girls, men and boys to benefit from the project.- The targeted groups will be selected based on transparent selection criteria that ensure humanitarian assistance is delivered without bias, and with a view to alleviating existing inequalities and vulnerabilities.- The selection of beneficiaries should fulfill the global prioritization criteria for the project to be lifesaving, reducing suffering, maintaining human dignity, time critical or critically enabling as well as the oPt context-specific criteria, such as focus on communities at imminent risk of displacement, or particularly vulnerable demographic groups.- CWLRCP apply Gender-Based Analysis to this project to capture the gender differences including the different situations, priorities and needs of women, men, girls and boys at all stages of the planning and implementation process As precaution measures,CWLRCP has taken safety and protection measures since the beginning of the crisis including the development of emergency response planned work continuity plan ,online counselling,phone counselling,legal representation at courts till active, in addition sterilizing the office building and distributing hygiene kits for all staff and beneficiariesThe internal environment:The potential impacts of the pandemic have been identified as part of the general analysis of the risks that the plan will deal with in terms of:a. Determine the legal and regulatory requirements for the functions and operations of the center.B. Determine the potential impact of the pandemic on the core business and activities of the center.T. Establishing backup arrangements to mitigate any potential risks. 2.2: Possible Scenarios: The preparation of the contingency plan will be carried out according to the perception of three possibilities (scenarios) so that the center continues its role in providing multi-purpose protection and empowerment services to combat gender-based violence.1- The first possibility (scenario): In light of full closure of emergency status where women and girls are unable to access services due to the imposition of a curfew by the Ministry of Interior.2- The second possibility: when the state of emergency is moderate, where women and girls have limited access to services due to facilitating limited movement of individuals.3- The third possibility: the stage of exit from the state of emergency or the so-called phase of coexistence with the epidemic and allowing individuals to move freely according to the determinants of prevention and individual safety.
