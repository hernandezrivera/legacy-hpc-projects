Project Summary

This project has been designed to responds to the needs for WASH services in Atareb, Daret Azza, Afrin, Kafr Zeita, Khan Shaykun, Kafr Nobol, and Dana sub districts,
The locations are prioritized as per the HNO 2019 data "draft" and according to prioritization of BINAA to the location as per the field teams communication with the local councils and their field visits to some villages.
The project will target the IDP’s and Host Community in the targeted locations, (IDP’s 611,577 ; Host Community 485,228; Women 250855 ; Men 238618 ; Girls 317,323 ; Boys 305,643) in order to improve the safe, adequate and sustainable access for these service along with the improvement for the public health and environment to avoid any outbreaks caused by contamination of the drinking water and lack in the SWM which might cause an outbreak in leishmaniasis either cholera. In this project the rehabilitation for water system, sanitation system and restoration for the SWM system. Moreover, rehabilitation for water system will target increasing the efficiency for the water station and network by conduct the needed maintenance, replacement and extension for the existing infrastructure and water station as well along with establishment for water flow meters in order to re-activate the tariff system. Rehabilitation for the sanitation system will target maintenance for the sewage network to repair the leakage points to avoid the mixture between drinking water and waste water along with conduct needed extension for the network in order to ensure that all shelters and settlements are connected to the sewerage network to ensure the best practice for waste water disposal along with install for rain drainage in the targeted communities and collective centers to avoid floods which caused by heavy rain . The Solid Waste Management restoration will cover distribution for the containers in the targeted areas along with providing the needed equipment’s, trucks and tools to the local council/ authorities to collect and transfer the solid waste to the related landfill which will also be rehabilitated. Distribution for Hygiene kits will be considered to the newly come IDP’s in Camps and communities for two times in the year (30,000 beneficiary) will receive a hygiene kit based on the need along with consideration for age and gender in each family as per the assessment.

Needs

Based on the HNO 2019 for Syria and the WASH assessments there are an estimated 15.6 million people who have a lack in the WASH services access and require a WASH assistance while they are facing a difficulty in accessing the safe drinking water, lack in transfer the solid waste while 10% of the garbage is left in the public areas, and mule functioning sewerage system.Based on Binaa Field Surveys, it is clear that all peoples in Syria are facing difficulties on accessing the WASH different services due to lack in these services along with their economic situation for the peoples which are living in these areas. Moreover, the huge movement of the IDP’s inside Syria especially to from the areas which are nearby the tension boundaries towards more secure and safe areas caused much more pressure on the existing systems with taking in consideration that all infrastructures, facilities and equipment facing a lack in maintenance which put these systems out of service fully or partially. The lack of access to water system for example pushed the people in these area to receive the water using water trucking which are filled from unknown and unsafe water sources along with high cost for the water which effect negatively on the public health and caused many of WBD cases according to EWARN and health facilities monthly medical reports especially for women and infants which caused many death cases. Also lack in the proper SWM caused extreme increase in leishmaniosis cases in the targeted areas which might cause huge outbreak in these areas. Contamination and flooding for waste and rain water which are caused by lack in the Sanitation system also a founded case in many communities and also in the informal settlements as well which are also caused a humanitarian crisis and caused more displacement movements. Mainly the increase of population along with lack of the maintenance for these systems and infrastructures contribute in increasing the need for restore these systems to be operated an increase the safe, adequate and sustainable access for these services in communities, informal settlements, health and education facilities with taking in consideration protection for women and children as well.The beneficiaries in the targeted location also reported to Binaa Field Staff during the field visits to conduct survey that they used to pay high cost to receive the water along with lack for the protection and privacy for females once they use the public WASH facilities in camps, informal settlements, schools, health care points especially latrines and the water collection points.

Implementation of activities

The project will be implemented with taking consideration of participating for the women, youth and children’s in order to ensure the best impact and practices for the beneficiaries by conducting several FGD with the management of protection centres, health facilities, schools and local authorities in order to understand their needs and respond to them as well. Using the WIS tool which are developed by UNICEF and the technical tools for sanitation system in order to define the technical needs to restore these systems and put them in duty. During restoration process and conducting the needed works another FGD will be conducted in order to discuss and design the sustainability mechanism through the cost recovery and build the capacity for the Services Unit which will be established from the available human resources into the community/ Collective Centre by conducting several technical trainings on cost recovery system along with Operation and Maintenance for the recovered systems. In the beginning of the project and after the FGD in the design stage, base line need assessment on HH level will be conducted and the results will be used also in order to define the gaps and needs for different groups of beneficiaries to be considered in the implementation phase. Registration for vulnerable beneficiaries of Hygiene Kits distribution will be conducted with taking in consideration that the priority for the families which are headed by women, contains handicapped family members, children’s and displaced people.

Protection Risk Analysis

- Risk: Unequal distribution of services inadvertently empower or strengthen the position of armed groups or other actors. The likelihood of this risk is medium and the impact is also high. The mitigation action is Establish strong links with authorities and operators to promote equity and “do no harm principle” all the time; Organize joint planning culture and agree on minimum standard; Include community and authorities in targeting; Ensure sensitive information is handled with care.- Risk: Children below 18 years old conduct works on behalf of humanitarian organisation, its partners or contractors. The likelihood of this risk is high and the impact is high too. The mitigation action will be Ensure proper registration system for staff/partners/contractors’ laborers; add zero tolerance disclosure on child labor in the contracts; check and register civil documentation.- Risk: Potential exposure to explosive hazard (IED/UXO/ERW) during assessment/ rehabilitation of wastewater infrastructure (WWTP, sewer networks). The likelihood of this risk is medium and the impact is high. The mitigation action will be Work closely with Mine Action actors in the area and conduct assessments/rehabilitation works only in areas declared as cleared; Conduct safety training with stuff and contractors; Add explosive hazard awareness material to your programming.- Risk: Movements of garbage trucks in congested, camp like settings; Reliability of services. e likelihood of this risk is high and the impact is low. the mitigation risk will be Consult community (separately women and girls) regarding SWM services modality/ frequency, truck routes and schedule; Provide household level solid waste containers/plastic bags to ease disposal of waste from H/H to the public SW collection points. Establish clear routes and schedule for garbage trucks that use wider access routes, avoiding school areas during the time children travel to/from school.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

- Risk: Access restrictions to geographical area due to active fighting and/or shifting of front lines would prevent the humanitarian actors to implement the intervention to the affected population. The likelihood of this risk is high and the impact is high too. The mitigation action will be considering and monitor the security situation contagiously, with the active members and local authorities and prepare the backup plan if suitable.- Risk: Lack in the fuel in the targeted areas. The likelihood is medium and the impact is high. The appropriate action will be taken in accordance with the progress of the situation on the ground, and shall report on any possible changes as they occur.Binaa will recognizes the risk of assistance dependency, especially in the emergencycontext; as a mitigation strategy Binaa will consider the capacity building in the community to ensure sustainable provision of WASH services which are rehabilitated and provided in this project.
