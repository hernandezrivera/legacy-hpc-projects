Project Summary

CRS’ proposed Early Recovery (ER) actions for Matobo, Nkayi and Beitbridge Rural and Urban districts will include interventions that aim to restore the capacity of vulnerable communities and local institutions to recover from impacts of drought, prevent further deterioration and shorten the need for humanitarian assistance. CRS will target 7,851 beneficiaries in Matobo, 22,252 in Nkayi and 7,182 in rural and urban Beitbridge.
