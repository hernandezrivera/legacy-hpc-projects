Project Summary

In response to the growing humanitarian crisis in Abyei Administrative Area and Raja County, in Western Bahr el Ghazal State, ACEA is proposing the interventions to expand lifesaving GBV services to the returnees and flood affected populations in Abyei and Raja. The complexity of the operating environment in Abyei Administrative area and Raja County in Western Bahr el Ghazal State that involve no access to services due to distance, movement restrictions or other reasons requires multifaceted approaches that are tailored to each location including: establishment of GBV Mobile Service Delivery to meet the needs of vulnerable women and girls and protection emergency response services. The proposed twelve month intervention is designed to increase knowledge and skills of vulnerable women and girls on GBV, PSEA and range of available response services including GBV case management support and psychosocial support services, improve GBV risk mitigation through the provision of fuel-efficient stoves, income generating activities and enhanced availability of GBV response services, specifically provided in Women and Girls Friendly Spaces and integrate protection monitoring and mainstreaming into the GBV intervention to improve the overall women and girls protection from GBV and PSEA. ACEA will strengthen community-based protection mechanism and facilitating access to information and appropriate services through existing community protection committees in Abyei and Raja. Provide a platform to establish community-based protection specifically for women and girls collects firewood, making charcoal and other livelihood activities to facilitate the identification of and response to protection concerns. ACEA will support the development of community-based mitigation plans, prevention messaging.
The purpose of this project is to increase availability of life-saving and specialized quality GBV services, including clinical management of rape, case management and psychosocial support and reinforce referral pathways (with a focus on women and girls and GBV survivors) and promote GBV risk mitigation into all aspects of the humanitarian response in Abyei Administrative Area and Raja County in Western Bahr el Ghazal State.
The main beneficiaries of this project are the returnees and flood affected population in Abyei Administrative Area and Raja County in Western Bahr el Ghazal State. Key populations who have influence to address some of barrier for survivors’ access to services will be identified and involved. The community outreach will target all community members. Structured community engagement will be made with influential members of communities. ACEA will conduct community consultation to identify key community members who can play critical roles to prevent GBV and involve them into more structured community engagement. General community will be also benefited from general messaging. Whilst a majority of survivors who come forward for services have been women and girls, partners will be supported with the knowledge and skills to respond to men and boys as well.
The project will benefit a total number of 14,143 direct beneficiaries both Abyei and Raja, of which 8,982 will be flood affected populations and returnees of Sudan Crisis in Abyei. Meanwhile 5,161 beneficiaries will be returnees and IDPs in Raja County, Western Bahr el Ghazal State. The direct beneficiaries including Women and girls, elderly without family support persons with disabilities, minorities and other vulnerable groups.

Implementing Partners

This project will establish synergies and comprehensive coordinated approach that acknowledged the roles and responsibilities of all the stakeholders to increase access to safe, equitable and adequate GBV services in Abyei Administrative Area and Raja County, Western Bahr el Ghazal State. This project will undertake and improve coordination mechanisms for GBV prevention, risk mitigation and GBV response services for the returnees, flood affect populations and host communities in Abyei Administrative Area.
To avoid duplication of services, ACEA will ensure that there is collaboration, coordination, inter-organizational and cross sectoral exchange with Protection Cluster, GBV AoR, Child Protection AoR and Ministry of Gender, Child and Social Welfare in Abyei Administrative Area and Western Bahr el Ghazal State. ACEA will work hand in hand with Relief and Rehabilitation Commission (RRC), local government and community structures in respective locations to support project coordination, planning, implementation, monitoring and evaluation. During the sequence of project implementation, ACEA will involve Protection actors and other interagency clusters, and other humanitarian actors as listed below:
Protection Cluster- through this project, ACEA will coordinate and conduct a joint response with protection cluster members including activities update and report to protection cluster and GBV AoR through 5Ws. As part of project implementation, ACEA will support monthly GBV AoR meeting, conducting a joint safety audit assessment, GBV service mapping, GBV SoP development and GBV referral pathway workshop and training for FSL, WASH, Nutrition, Health actors on the IASC Thematic Area Guidelines on integrating GBV actions and Protection, GBV minimum standards and GBV risk mitigation, and Disability Inclusion, and on GBV disclosure and referrals.
Health Cluster-ACEA will continue to work closely with health partners and provide clinical management of rape training to increase their presence for the health and medical support for the GBV survivors and improve referral system across the locations of the project.
FSL cluster members-ACEA will coordinate GBV prevention and response activities with FSL partners on cross-cutting issues related to the FSL cluster to increase support for the affected population and, for the GBV survivors to ensure confidential, well monitored feedback mechanisms on food distribution and tailored to the needs of women and girls to ensure their effective utilization. Ensure clear risk mitigation strategies and follow up actions for minimizing threats of GBV to Women and girls.
WASH Cluster-ACEA will collaborate with WASH partners to ensure GBV risk mitigation activities integrated in WASH activities and particularly in the schools and other facilities.
NFIs/Shelter Cluster- ACEA will collaborate with NFIs/shelter partners to ensure GBV risk mitigation activities integrated in their respective activities and particularly in the construction of the shelters.
During the project implementation, ACEA will share the report regularly to keep stakeholders informed and ensure smooth progress. Initially, ACEA will provide weekly and monthly reports on the implementation status of GBV interventions in Abyei and Raja on 5W’s and resources mobilized by ACEA on Financial Tracking Services. Is ACEA commitment to ensure timely updates without delay is provided to GBV AoR and Protection Cluster.

1. Needs

Since civil conflict broke out in Sudan in April 2023, hundreds of thousands of individuals, primarily South Sudanese returnees, as well as refugees and third country nationals, have fled to South Sudan and Abyei Administrative Area. Many of these individuals have experienced rights violations and various forms of Gender-Based Violence (GBV) in Sudan, including during their journey to South Sudan in areas of reception, transit and destinations in South Sudan, such as Renk, Aweil, Rotriak, and Abyei. Women and girls have seen an increased risk of violence due to the increased presence of armed groups resulting to rapes, sexual assaults, abduction, sexual exploitation and intimate partner violence are widespread and GBV services are limited. The escalating food insecurity increases risks of GBV as women and girls have to walk longer distances in search of food or are forced into exploitative situation to survive. The availability of trained medical personnel to provide clinic management of rape and basis PSS continue to be insufficient. In Abyei and Raja, few have access to lifesaving GBV services and in some locations where health agencies are working, health facilities are unable to provide post rape care due to a lack of trained staff, limited supplies and treatment. Many IDPs and returnees have suffered from repeated displacement due to shifting conflict lines from Southern and Northern part of Abyei Box and the search for basic commodities. Services in Abyei and are almost nonexistence and many walks for hours to access available services. Without assistance to safely access services, individuals are at risk of relying on negative coping mechanism. Children are often at highest risk for being directly affected by the negative coping mechanisms with few protective strategies available to them and increase exposure to violence can have a lasting and detrimental effect on their development. During IRNA need assessment on 23rd -27th September 2024 in Abyei Administrative Area reported vulnerable groups, especially women and children are at increased risk of exploitation, abuse, and violence in the displacement setting. It is a fear that there will be increased incidents of gender-based violence, particularly to the overcrowded households living in temporary shelters along the main roads; however, child protection services are needed to prevent child labor. Lack of Gender-Based Violence services as well as lack of awareness on GBV and PSEA has pervasive and critical threat in Abyei Administrative Area and Raja County in Western Bahr el Ghazal State affecting primarily women and girls who experience greater risk of sexual violence, including rape, sexual harassment, conflict-related sexual violence and domestic violence. According to the Area-based Protection Risk Assessment conducted in August by the Protection Cluster sub-national coordination mechanisms, Gender-Based Violence continue to rank at the highest of the protection risk, as indicated by the communities. This reads that a staggering 79% of the country suffers significant exposure to the risk of GBV with the breakdown of the findings having it considered a very high in five counties (5%), a high risk in 47 counties (59%) and Abyei Area, while a medium risk in 12 (15%) counties of South Sudan.

2. Project activities

Activity 1.1: Project launching and enhancing visibility of the project in Abyei/Raja (6 signposts, 16 puller up banners and 100 T-shirts with logos) Activity 1.2. Support 1,018 survivors of GBV with specialized GBV response services (GBV case management, referrals, legal aid & WGFS programming) Abyei/Raja. Activity 1.3: Provide individual and group psychosocial support and recreational activities to 6,931 vulnerable women and girls in 6 Women and Girls Friendly Spaces in Abyei/Raja Activity 1.4: Support local production and utilization of 461 fuel-efficient stoves to reduce the risk of gender-based violence (GBV) that occurs during the process of collecting firewood in Abyei/Raja Activity 1.5: Support 2,600 Women and Girls to enhance their skills on income generating activities such as sewing, craft-making etc in the WGFS to mitigate the risk of GBV-Abyei/Raja. Activity 1.6: Support 223 women and girls with cash and voucher assistance in Abyei and Raja. Activity 1.7: Establish two semi-permanent structures of 9mX6m WGFS in Boro Madina and Mayonga in Raja and equip with essential resources, while continuing to support the operationalization of the four WGFS already established in Abyei under ACEA. Activity 1.8: Provide information and awareness raising of 105 sessions in the WGFS and surrounding communities targeting women and girls on range of topics such as GBV, Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse, available response services and timely reporting- Abyei/Raja Activity 1.9: Reach out to 1, 484 through GBV prevention initiatives (e.g. SASA, EMAP, community patrolling groups). Activity 1.10: GBV case management training for 24 staffs including WGFS manager, WGFS caseworkers, WGFS facilitators, protection and data clerk, Social Workers and community mobilizers on GBV minimum standards are including case management, PSS, GBV guiding principles, safe referrals and PSEA. Activity 1: 11. Support County 2 levels functional referral pathway, service mapping and GBV standard operating procedures development workshop for 100 participants from GBV service providers in Abyei/ Raja. Activity 1.12: Support trainings of 25 from FSL, WASH, Nutrition, Health actors on the IASC Thematic Area Guidelines on integrating GBV actions and Protection, GBV minimum standards and GBV risk mitigation, and Disability Inclusion, and on GBV disclosure and referrals in Abyei/Raja. Activity 1.13: Provide capacity building training on clinical management of rape survivors for 30 frontline health service providers. Activity 1.14. Distributes 2,147 Dignity Kits of GBV AoR for women and adolescent girls in Abyei/Raja. Activity 1.15. Project staff capacity development Activity 1.16: Field monitoring visit
