Project Summary

The presence of over 1.4 million registered Afghan refugees, and approximately 1.24 million Undocumented (840,000 Afghan Citizenship Card holders and 400,000 Undocumented Afghans), Pakistan hosts one of the largest protracted refugee situations globally. IOM supports the Government of Pakistan in the implementation of cross-border programming, with voluntary repatriation as one of the key elements, together with integration support, which aims at preserving space for Undocumented Afghans and promoting peaceful co-existence with host communities.
More specifically, IOM activities have focused on (i) support to the Government during the Documentation Exercise; (ii) advocacy for inclusion of ACC holders and Undocumented Afghans to inform and plan aid assistance; (iii) support for ACC and Undocumented Afghans through livelihood and skills trainings; (iv) facilitation of voluntary and safe returns; (v) access to healthcare; and (vi) protection and assistance to the most vulnerable.
To respond more effectively to the joint vision between Afghanistan and Pakistan for return of Afghan migrants, including anticipated multifaceted challenges posed by the complexity of large-scale cross border flows in 2021, IOM Pakistan aims to facilitate safe, dignified and voluntary returns of Undocumented Afghans from Pakistan to Afghanistan through a cohesive cross-border programming approach. In addition to creating an environment conducive for voluntary repatriation, IOM will also introduce them to skills development opportunities during their stay in Pakistan, to ensure access to livelihood opportunities in host communities as well as for sustainable reintegration in Afghanistan.
IOM requires $40 million to address the immediate needs of 1,240,000 Undocumented Afghans in 2021 and beyond. Of this amount, $29 million included in this appeal is urgently needed for the provision of cash grants to returnees to ensure repatriation is made more sustainable.

1- Project Activities

This project is intended to facilitate voluntary return and repatriation of Afghan Citizen Card (ACC) holders and Undocumented Afghans from Pakistan to Afghanistan. IOM Pakistan will support voluntary repatriation of the return caseload by promoting cross-border programme responses in areas of social protection, focused on the following interventions: o Continue Flow Monitoring activities at two formal border crossings with Afghanistan to quantify outflows, demographics, intentions, origins, routes and destinations of returning ACC holders and Undocumented Afghans. o Set up transit centers to provide pre-departure assistance including repatriation grant for the most vulnerable families (indicative cash grant at a rate of USD 100 per family), and cash for transportation based on final destination and to cover custom clearances newly introduced by the Government of Pakistan (maximum USD 50 per family).o Conduct undocumented status determination survey to verify numbers of Undocumented and ACC holders in Pakistan, under its mandate and on behalf of the Government of Pakistan. o Conduct information campaigns in areas of high concentration of Afghan population in order to assist vulnerable migrants make an informed decision on return. o Provide basic and primary healthcare services, medical screenings and provision of essential medicines including vaccinations for polio and measles (for children under 10 years of age), Tuberculosis, Coronavirus, and other infectious diseases in collaboration with health partners. o Implement integrated, community-wide skills development training programmes for sustainable integration of ACC holders and Undocumented Afghans to ensure income generating opportunities. Potential courses include: embroidery, mobile-repair, electric works and plumbing. o Special consideration will be given to the needs and requirements of vulnerable population groups including women, girls, boys, men, diverse gender groups (LGBTI), elderly, and persons with special needs (disabled etc.).
