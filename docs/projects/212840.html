Project Summary

For this HRP cycle, our NGO aims to address urgent and lifesaving nutrition needs for pregnant and lactating women, adolescents, and youth (10-24 years) are included. and children under 5 years, the most vulnerable group, in Northwest Syria. Nutrition activities throughout 2024 will continue scaling up lifesaving curative and preventive nutrition interventions to the mothers and children living in Al-Bab sub-district, Al Bab sub-district (Bazagha town) and Afrin sub-district (Afrin city) in Aleppo governorate, and Dana sub-district , Maaret Tamasrin sub-district, and Idleb sub-district in Idleb governorate. Nutrition activities will continue prioritizing the following responses:
-Quality improvement in the treatment of acute malnutrition
-Prevention of micronutrient deficiencies using different modalities: supplementation through distribution of micronutrient powder and tablets targeting children aged 6 to 59months and pregnant and lactating women (PLW) in collaboration with the health sector,diet diversification awareness, and fortification.
-Protect, promote, and support recommended maternal, infant and young child nutrition practice including breastfeeding/ complementary feeding and Social Behavior Change SBC via counseling or education. In tandem.
-Prevention of chronic malnutrition and maternal malnutrition through adequate focus on the first 1,000 days of the child’s life including the pregnancy period.
-Capacity building of Staff on key nutrition interventions (community management of acute malnutrition, promotion of appropriate maternal, infant and young child nutrition and caring practices and age-appropriate micronutrient supplementation).
-Strengthening collaboration between Nutrition, Health, and Protection activities on implementing an integrated response that addresses the immediate and underlying causes of acute and chronic malnutrition.
- Conducting Family MUAC approach among the mothers and caregivers to include them more in preventive nutrition activities and early detection of malnutrition cases.
Cash Voucher Assistance for Pregnant and Lactating Women (PLWs) and caretakers of children under 2 is a targeted support activity designed to meet these vulnerable groups' specific nutritional and health needs. This assistance comes in cash vouchers, which can be redeemed for food or other essentials to improve the well-being of pregnant and lactating women and young children and their caregivers.
PSEA Risk: Violate PSEA or CoC principles.
Mitigating measures: Signing PSEA and child safeguarding MWL’s policy for all staff.
- getting training on PSEA for all staff.
- PSEA awareness through brochures and videos.
- Assigning complaint mechanisms for SEA issues.
- Code of Conduct, PSEA, and child safeguarding policies are mandatory for all staff and vendors working with MWL.
The accountability Officer in the field will ensure all beneficiaries are aware of MWL complaints and the feedback mechanism in place. The mechanism will include fixed and mobile complaint boxes, a WhatsApp number, and a hotline that the Accountability Officer will handle.
The officer will compile all complaints/suggestions or feedback and share them with the related Project Manager to be addressed.
MWL will coordinate closely with the cluster to ensure no overlapping with other partners in the same area through regular reporting, attending the cluster meetings, and emails
from other hand, in case of supplies procurement, the organization will receive the nutrition supplies (for preventive and treatment) on quarterly basis from UNICEF through Mercy USA organization as a donation.
