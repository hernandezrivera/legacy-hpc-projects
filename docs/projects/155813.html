Project Summary

Coinciding with HF’s vision of livelihoods support through food security activities for crisis-affected area. This program aims to increase the resilience and livelihoods of the most vulnerable populations in Aleppo and Idleb, where IHR currently have access to. The first main objective is improving the food security of 400 vulnerable households’ women and men from host communities and IDPs by providing them agriculture inputs of summer vegetable crops, fertilizer, pesticide, and cash for irrigation. While the second objective is ensuring the source of income for 100 female-headed households through support each HH with four sheep and vouchers of fodder and veterinary service.
In accordance with the latest FSL report on priorities, responding to the need assessment that the IHR team conducted in east Idleb and north Aleppo. This project aims to reduce the financial burden of vulnerable households and have access to one done through providing
• Agriculture voucher with cost 120 $ per HH. Each HH has to select seeds enough for cultivating one donum at minimum 40$ of the total amount will be for summary seeds. So Depending on results of need assessment which conducted by IHR ON 2019 the priority will be for the flowing (okra, cowpea, jut mallow, cucumber, tomato, zucchini, yellow corn, and Bean, ) which are classified as crops less need for water, less affected of environmental conditions and highly productive. In addition getting on fertilizer, pesticide or agriculture tools based on the value of selected seeds. Which means, selecting the seeds is mandatory for all, while they remain the value for 120 $ is optional to select from fertilizer, pesticides or tools.
• Cash voucher for irrigation with cost 50 $ per HH, which is enough for ten irrigation times. since the Targeted area is under the second and third stability area, which faced challenges related to big depletion of water resources. So that value of money will enable the farmers to reach for optimal irrigations times, which is one of the responses plan to mitigate the impact of drought on farmers.
• Furthermore, IHR technician team will follow up on the progress of agriculture steps through daily visits to beneficiaries’ land to provide the required technical information, which can enhance plants grow. in addition to providing awareness sessions for all 200 beneficiaries, which aims to enhance their knowledge about good agriculture practices, the best irrigation techniques and food processing of final products.
Moreover, the availability of vegetable products which are the basic daily needs of HHs in the targeted area will reactivate the local market and improve access, especially for women. Therefore, that mitigate the burden and risk of movement to farthest areas where the markets exist.
The second output of this project is increasing the resilience of 100 vulnerable female-headed households of host communities and IDPs with high priority to women and GBV survivor. Each beneficiary will receive
• Four sheep
• Fodder voucher enable each HH to receive 960 Kg of fodder (4 sheep * 30 Kg per sheep * 8 months =960KG* 0.35 $ cost per kg = 336 $ cost of fodder per beneficiary during eight months). Whereas, the fodder distribution will be for three batches to feed the sheep along eight months.
• For eight months, the distributed sheep will be under control by IHR’s veterinarians who will visits the beneficiaries in their places to provide the required treatment and vaccines.
• All targeted beneficiaries will get on training sessions on field about dairy processing and marketing. Which aims to enhance the knowledge of female-headed households and improve their access to market.
Finally, the project aims to produce beneficiaries with strong knowledge background, especially which relate with good manufacturing practices to ensure optimum utilization of agriculture products through dryers producing or dairy industry, which can secure source of income of double value

Needs

Depending on HNO 2019, entering the eighth year of the crisis, the scale, severity, and complexity of needs across Syria remain overwhelming. Some 5.89 million people are in acute need due to a convergence of vulnerabilities resulting from displacement, exposure to hostilities, and limited access to basic goods and services. Conflict continues to be the principal driver of humanitarian needs, with the civilian population in many parts of the country exposed to significant protection risks which threaten life, dignity, and wellbeing on a daily basis. At the end of 2018, the conflict renewed in the north of Syria, which led to an increase in displacement, therefore, the number of the insecure population increasing in those areas. As one of the organizations operating in the North of Syria, IHR launched a needs assessment at the beginning of 2019 in the north of Syria (Azaz, Aleppo and Dana, Idleb). Which aimed to the more accurate determination of people’s needs and design the response plan according to it. The IHR M&E field staff have met key informants of targeted communities (female and male) which represents the largest categories of society to stand by communities needs and the response it through proposing suitable projects support food security. The assessment designed by taking into consideration the women situation and their needs for meeting it.The most in-need people were working in agriculture which is the basic source of their income. Whereas more than eight years of the crisis made dependence on agriculture declining due to some challenges related with the access to good quality inputs and availability of the resource to irrigate. Supporting the food security and responding for those needs in Dana Subdistrict, Idleb governorate and Azaz Subdistrict, Aleppo governorate, the project has been designed to reach for most vulnerable 400 people by providing required agriculture inputs (summer vegetable seeds, pesticides, fertilizers, tools and cash for irrigation), which aims to enable them achieving sustainable source of income. Additional that The second intervention of this project is increasing the resilience of 100 vulnerable female-headed households of host communities and IDPs with high priority to women and GBV survivor. Each beneficiary will receiveHowever, the most common source of income of affected women in the assessments’ communities were the agriculture and livestock works. While the expertise in livestock breeding and dairy production has gradually been reduced as older women who held this knowledge have either died or been forcefully displaced. Due to the need for income and food products to supplement family consumption, some women have become breeders without any real experience, especially when it comes to food production. Vulnerable women do not have the same access to disposable income needed to purchase fodder, vitamins and minerals, and veterinary drugs needed particularly during winter. Without these, they cannot care for their sheep to ensure viable production and are in danger of losing valuable assets. Distributing kits (sheep and fodder for eight months) to these breeders and training them on dairy products to meet the local market requirement will generate life-saving income.Finally, this project aims to promote the strengthening resilience in the region. Through create generating income opportunities and decrease the price of basic goods like vegetables and dairy product in local markets and reducing gender-based violence by giving high priority for GBV survivor,

Implementation of activities

The first activity will be the recruitment of IHR staff by taking in consideration of that 20% of the employees will be from women. IHR by collaboration with LCs will sign the MOUs at targeted communities. Based on fair criteria of vulnerability and needs the beneficiaries will be selected, which will clearly be introduced to all stakeholders during the community engagement stage. According to IHR policy and procurement procedures, the vendors will be selected through transparency and competitive tenders. Then the procurement team will finalize the contracting issue after confirmation from the program team about the quality of submitted items. Before launching the distribution plan, an introducing meeting with selected beneficiaries will be conducted to explain the voucher redeem process and raise their awareness about project objectives, focusing on the importance of selecting the Drought resistant varieties. The main interventions of this project will be leaded directly by IHR team, and managed by FSL team in cooperation with M&E team who will monitor the progress of implementation and confirm commitment of program team toward selection criteria and required percentage of female of total targeted beneficiaries.in all activities , its worth to mentioned that IHR will Give high priority for survivor GBV . The programmatic and technical team (most specifically on the field) will be composed of 20% of women as well in order to ensure the participation of women in the program. Support’s departments will play supportive roles throughout the implementation stages of the project life cycle. As the coordination with all stakeholders is a key component of IHR program management methodology to ensure successful projects. Accordingly, IHR will engage in the targeted communities to introduce the project objectives, responsibilities of both parties through introducing meeting with representatives of each community to build their capacities of humanitarian response programs.The first output is the distribution of agriculture inputs’ vouchers, which enable each beneficiary of getting on agriculture inputs (seeds, fertilizer, pesticides or tools) with value 120 $ (one kind of seeds with quantity enough for one donum will be selected) 40 $ as a minimum will be for seeds. additional that the beneficiary will receive a cash voucher for irrigation with cost 50 $ per one, enough for 10 irrigation times, which cover the 80 % of water’s needs of most selected crops. (All vouchers details mentioned in BOQs). However, the knowledge of all selected beneficiaries will be improved through theoretical and practical training sessions on the field. Which aims to empower them all, especially women and GBV survivor about best practices and marketing. The contacting with the female of beneficiaries of all stages will be through female staff, to ensure the traditional conditions is respected. Here is good to say that IHR will participate in referral pathway in the target area. For the second activity, each female-headed household will receive 4 sheep and fodder voucher (4*1*240*0.35=336$) enough to feed those received sheep for eight months. Along the project period, the sheep will receive veterinary services like; treatments and vaccination by IHR veterinarians.To ensure the sustainability of project and keeping received assets by beneficiaries. IHR will sign an agreement with all targeted beneficiaries at the end of the project. The agreement shows the non-eligibility of the beneficiary to sell the received assets like; sheep and drip irrigations network for no less than one year. The agreement implementation will be under supervising of the local authority as third-party monitoring

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumptions: We assume that none of the selected beneficiaries will leave their lands and all of them will use the provided assets in land service. Risk1:- This amount of cash for irrigation may be used for another purpose. Mitigation1: - Distribute the cash irrigation vouchers after agriculture vouchers, which means verifying form beneficiaries commitment toward received items. Risk2:- Selling the voucher or replacing it with money from the supplier.Mitigation2:- IHR will add penalty terms to contract if supplier re-procured or paid money to beneficiaries- Field team will present at redeem centers to monitor the process.- We will apply the secret beneficiary’s mechanism to test the vendor’s commitment.Assumptions: We assume that none of the selected beneficiaries will leave their places and all of them will use the received fodder to feed the received sheep. Risk1:- Selling all received sheep or some of them to the supplier or other persons. Mitigation1: -select the in need beneficiaries and raise their awareness about the importance of keeping sheep and take care with them. -None distributing the fodder for those beneficiaries who sold sheep.-Retrieve the remaining quantity of sheep from those who sold some of them and re-distribute to new beneficiaries. Risk2:- Selling the voucher or fodder or replacing it with money from the supplier.Mitigation2:- IHR will add penalty terms to contract if supplier re-procured or paid money to beneficiaries- Field team will present at redeem centers to monitor the process.- We will apply the secret beneficiary’s mechanism to test the vendor’s commitment- The beneficiaries who will sell the first batch of fodder, will not receive on the other batches, while the remain quantities will be divided equally to beneficiaries who did not sell.
