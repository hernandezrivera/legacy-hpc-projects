Project Summary

According to JMCNA, approximately 8.9 million people are affected by the prevalent poor WASH conditions in Somalia. The 10% put under the catastrophic severity is mainly due to lack of access to water both in terms of quality and WASH Cluster Guidance Note HPC 2021 quantity accompanied by a lack of access to functional sanitation. In comparison to the last year’s revision of HNO after the Covid-19 situation, the overall WASH needs have not reduced. due to the availability of limited resources to the needs exacerbated by the Pandemic and the ongoing complex humanitarian crises in the country. Following this year’s trend, it is anticipated that over 1 million people could be in acute life-saving needs along the Shabelle and Juba river basins in case of above-normal rainfall performance during the Gu’ and Deyr 2021. This intervention will address the dire WASH humanitarian needs for flood-affected communities in Mahaday town of Jowhar district of the Middle Shabelle region through access to safe water, emergency hygiene, and sanitation. The project will address WASH gaps and risks of AWD/cholera outbreaks, morbidity, and mortality among emergency flood-affected communities and vulnerable populations. This project will target 12850 people elder 128, Men 2056, Women 4240, Displaced 5270, including disadvantaged families and elderly persons, Disabilities in emergencies to be provided adequate safe water, sanitation facilities, and hygiene promotion services.
WASH services of good hygiene and sanitation practices will be promoted in the affected communities by providing adequate WASH facilities such as the construction of 300 emergency latrines ( 200 in Jowhar, 100 Mahaday) with handwashing facilities, with lockable doors, with disability inclusion with handwashing facilities only in areas where latrine rehabilitation is not possible or where there is a complete gap of sanitation facilities especially in IDP/host communities, and rehabilitation of 150 ( 90 in Jowhar, 60 in Mahaday) flood-damaged latrines through CFW modality through selected skilled persons among the flood-affected communities. provision of water to vulnerable affected communities through the construction of 15 shallow wells with handpump ( 5 in Mahaday and 10 in Jowhar), and rehabilitation with disability inclusion of 30 shallow wells ( 20 in Jowhar, 10 in Mahaday), provision of emergency safe drinking water through CVA (target 1000 families ( 650 in Jowhar, 350 in Mahaday) with a duration of 3 months) families will receive safe drinking water to flood-affected communities through Cash Voucher Assistance (CVA) while water points will be rehabilitated to recover the damaged essential WASH infrastructures and as well as improve the livelihoods of flood-affected communities through CFW modality. The CFW will be used to strengthen WASH market-based programming and this fully in line with the Somalia WASH cluster strategy to promote the integration of MBP for all WASH. The project will focus on those severely affected by floods. The shallow wells are rehabilitated and upgraded to have protection, including repair, disinfection, and protection against further flooding with hand pumps installed. SCC will establish and train hygiene promoters 40 (25 male, 15 female) select from Jowhar and Mahaday. Community Hygiene Promoters will be trained 5-days to promote change in the behavior of the beneficiaries through PHAST & CHAST training components, in close collaboration with the local authorities, community leaders, and the ministry of water and energy and ministry of health and social services of Hirshabelle state of Somalia. This project will establish and train 40 (25 Male, 15 Female) water committees that will be selected 2 persons from each water source. A total of 500 (150 male, 350 female) will be distributed to hygiene kits (soap, water treatment tablets, and 20L jerrycans. a total of 30 ( 20 in Jowhar, 10 in Mahaday) wells will be disinfected and chlorinated.

Mitigation Measures

As mitigation measures of risk analysis, SCC will ensure WASH community engagement of all groups as a key requirement in the response mobilization phase and ensuring that marginalized groups are by default among the prioritized groups. WASH cluster proposed also to include clear strategies for reaching marginalized communities in all the program partnership documents with implementing partners. Other WASH partners reiterated inclusive consultation with different groups of households, mentioned reinforced complaint and feedback mechanisms to identify any situation of exclusion, and to conditionimplementation on equal and direct access to all communities. This is particularly relevant to IDP setups where informal settlement managers often control access to communities and influence the beneficiary selection process.SCC will communicate with community elders, local authority, and regional state to discuss any risks that may arise during beneficiary registration to have clarified the requirements, and to get the right team, communicating and listening to marginalized groups and other target groups of the project.
