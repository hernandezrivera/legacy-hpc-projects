Project Summary

Through the proposed project Oxfam plans to enable the most vulnerable families in the targeted subdistricts through two-year programming / project duration to have access to the essential food items. Depending on local market functionality, people preferences, and availability of appropriate FSPs, distribution modality will be through cash, noting that Oxfam had performed market assessment in several communities in the proposed subdistrict and found that cash modality is preferable and feasible. Oxfam will provide up-to 6 rounds of cash transfers to 14,000 HHs, at least 30% of which will be headed by females. This will benefit a total of 70,000 individuals in acute need through the project period. The monthly distribution value will be equal to 65% of the MEB covering food needs for 5 family members “58.4” USD using the Central Bank preferential rate at the time of distribution. The transfer value might be changed based on the discussions within the CWG. Oxfam will coordinate with other humanitarian actors to avoid overlapping in and ensure complementarity in geographical targeting, their coordination will be done through FSA Sector, CWG and even through bilateral meetings. Oxfam will make sure to engage the communities in the selection of the most vulnerable families. Therefore, Oxfam will form a community committee where at least 50% of participants will be women. The committees will be responsible of identifying the most vulnerable families. Oxfam will follow the internal procedures of verification process for the beneficiary list in coordination with the local community committee. The aid delivery process, impact and market prices will be monitored regularly.
Assessments conducted by Oxfam staff show that cash-based assistance would be feasible for cash grants in the targeted subdistricts. Cash modality is aligned with the targeted household preference and the majority of households already reported using markets as their main source for purchasing food and non-food items.

4 - Needs

The protracted crisis, now heading into its 11th year, is exacerbating food security and livelihood needs for many families across the country, further eroding the resilience and recovery prospects. With an estimated 14.6 M people in need of some form of food and agriculture assistance, representing 69% of the total population. Based on the FSS MYR 2021 alternative methodology analysis and technical consultations, at least 12.8 M people are estimated to be food insecure, of which 1.3 M people are considered severely food insecure.According to needs assessments performed by Oxfam in the targeted sub-district,Oxfam found that the food system has been affected in rural &urban communities,due to severe depreciation in the Syrian lire, the significant unprecedented increase in the prices of food and basic NFIs & social services, the disruption of food production value chains, fuel crisis, very limited income sources, & livelihood opportunities. In addition, the international sanctions have hindered the government’s capacity to subsidize the essential food items and agricultural inputs the matter which affected agricultural production level as well.Therefore,the food availability in the local markets has been affected to a certain limit,but peoples’ accessibility to food has been affected especially with the fact that suppliers’ capacity to pay in depths very limited.All the mentioned factors led to the need to support people in the targeted locations to meet their basic needs.

5 - Implementation of activities

As Oxfam has competent staff in the hubs and based on their experience and needs assessment findings, Oxfam will do rabid need assessment before starting the activities implementation and coordinating with other working agencies to avoid overlapping.The activities will be as followed:Community mobilization, sensitization, and committee’s formation. The project will be implemented in a manner that deepen connecting factors and minimizing the dividing factors in the targeted communities.Identification, registration, and verification of most vulnerable food insecure HHs IDPs and Host Communities for cash-for-food transfer Assistance. as per the defined selection criteria.Up to 6 tranches of Cash for food transfers will be provided to 14,000 HHs through the 2-year project duration, with at least 30% female headed households, through licensed and vetted FSPs.Monthly Price Monitoring and analysis to measure the impact of cash injection on the prices of essential commodities and services in the local markets.Process monitoring to measure the beneficiaries’ satisfaction with the cash delivery process, Oxfam MEAL team will perform daily DDMs during the whole distribution period. Also, Oxfam will measure the impact of the projects through PDMs where MEAL team will calculate the essential indictors like FCS (Food Consumption Score) and CSI (Coping Strategy Index).
