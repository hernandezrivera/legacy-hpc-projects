Project Summary

R1. Adapt school structures to population pressure and the pandemic of COVID-19: A1.1. Build community support rooms in the 3 schools (8 classrooms; 4 at EPC Impire, 1 at EPC Nanlia, 3 at EPC Nanacaramo); A1.2. Improve sanitation structures in schools (8 latrines per sex and separated by teacher / student; 3 in EPC Impire, 3 in EPC Nanlia, 2 in EPC Nancaramo); A1.3. Improve access to water in schools; A1.4. Rehabilitate classrooms / construction equipment.
R2. Train 80 teachers and educators: A2.1. To train teachers on hygiene issues using distance training in order to prevent COVID-19; A2.2. Train teachers in psychosocial support topics using distance training model; A2.3. Train teachers in risk reduction themes using distance training model. R3. Ensure access to school supplies for children and hygiene for the entire school community: A3.1. Distribute hygiene kits with individual protection masks to teachers and students; A3.2. Equip schools with signage and hygiene kit COVID-19; A3.3. Distribute cell phones (with radio) to 100 secondary school students
