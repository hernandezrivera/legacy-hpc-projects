Project Summary

Maram Foundation (MF) aims through this project to provide adequate and full spectrum of humanitarian lifesaving multisectoral assistance with full CCCM core activities to 11,029 IDPs - 2,115 families/ HHs in total, including (2897 G, 2870 B, 2289 W, 1991 M, 689 PWD), in both Idleb and Aleppo governorates.
Where the project's areas of intervention include three location ( 3 planned camps/ RHUs sites); in Idleb (Kafr Jales and Shiekh Bahr PC), and Aleppo governorates, in the Euphrates Shield area - (Ihtemlat PC), in northwest Syria (NW Syria).
- In Ehtemlat planned Camp, the number of beneficiaries/ IDPs reached to 1029 HHs/ 5252 IDPs.
- In Sheikh Bahir planned Camp, the number of beneficiaries/ IDPs reached to 372 HHs/ 2175 IDPs.
- And in Kafar Jales planned Camp, the number of beneficiaries/ IDPs reached to 714 HHs/ 3602 IDPs.
Thus, Maram will continue the following up and serve as the camp management agency, and set in place proper staffing/management personnel to conduct CCCM activities, including care and maintenance to basic infrastructure, registration/data collection, vulnerability mapping, verification, establishment of gender-balanced camp governance/ participation management structures, and accountability mechanisms, routine site renovations, and strengthening fire mitigation and response in the identified Planned Camps.
In addition to enhance community participation and form committees, strengthen the capacity of staff and committees through providing relevant trainings for them, build accountability mechanism towards IDPs by established mechanism for coordinated multisectoral services provision, established mechanism for community engagement and accountability to IDPs.
This project also works on strengthening household and communal coping mechanisms in camps and developing spontaneous exit strategies through providing tailored livelihood activities for better solutions (Vocational trainings and In-kind grants).
It will consider the different priorities and needs of IDPs, and the planned intervention will mainstream protection principles especially (Do no harm) throughout the project cycle by considering the different needs of men, women, young children, children, adolescents, young adults, middle-aged adults, and older adults.
MF’s extensive CCCM experience is demonstrated by establishment and the ongoing successful management of Planned Camps in NW Syria.

4 - Needs

Referring to the HRP 2021, around 13.4 million people are estimated to require some form of humanitarian assistance, including 6 million in acute need, due to a convergence of factors arising from a sharp reduction in purchasing power, the loss of essential livelihoods, and income, mounting food insecurity, limited access to basic services, as well as the immediate and longer-term effects of the COVID-19 pandemic. More than 2.7 million people in NWS vulnerable in need of essential, multi-sectoral assistance in addition to mitigation measures for fires and floods. As of December 2021, the CCCM database includes 1,398 IDP sites that host 1,694,712 IDPs or 321,085 families. Of these, 80% are women and children, and 45,997 IDPs are reported to be persons with specific needs. Only during December 2021, CCCM Cluster reported incidents in 446 IDP sites(Fire, flood, wind..)Thus, and In line with the CCCM Cluster objectives, MF will support camp management system in the targeted sites through providing care and maintenance to basic infrastructure, strengthening fire mitigation and response, enhancing community participation and strengthen the capacity of staff and committees . Although there are camp management teams that have been established, the need to continue their roles is still a necessity to empower them more for care and maintenance of the targeted sites and achieve better responses.MF M&E unit will continue assessing the needs and the gaps - if any, to be addressed.

5 - Implementation of activities

MF through this project aims to provide 11,029 IDPs/2,115 families with core CCCM services.The project activities aligns with CCCM cluster priorities, by provide lifesaving services through services provision coordination mechanism, and enhancing the safety provisions by continue strengthening fire mitigation and response, care, functional and maintenance to basic infrastructure in the identified planned camps. and by enhanced accountability to IDPs through establishment and implementation of CCCM mechanisms.Also by strengthening HH and communal coping mechanisms in camps through providing tailored livelihood activities (450 IDPs with Vocational trainings and In-kind grants).Hence, the activities will be as below;-Providing streamlined humanitarian lifesaving assistance, multi-sectoral assistance to the affected IDPs and strengthen the basic infrastructure support in identified sites.-Accurate registration process.-Regular maintenance of site infrastructures.-Enhanced accountability to IDPs through establishment of CCCM mechanisms.-Set up accountable complaint and feedback mechanism (AAP).-Establish community committees, making sure participating of women.-Capacity Buildings for the camp staff and Committees.- Fire Mitigation Prevention& Response, in line with CCCM Cluster and Fire mitigation TF.All activates will consider the different priorities and needs of IDPs from different ages including men, women, boys, girls, elderly people and people with special needs.
