Project Summary

Over 3.9 million people in Somalia need urgent action to maintain their right to health and wellbeing, and to assure their equitable and dignified access to healthcare. The health sector is overburdened, remaining fragmented, under-resourced and ill-equipped to provide life-saving and preventive services. in order to address the above problems this project aims to Improve equitable access to quality emergency and essential lifesaving health services for the crisis affected population as well as improve on the coverage, reproductive health including BEmONC as well as referrals for CEmONC. This project will make sure that immunization services are adequately offered to children under 5 years as well as pregnant mother for tetanus toxoid. also ensuring readiness to prevent and respond to outbreaks such as malaria, measles or Acute Watery Diarrhea AWD/Cholera and promote health update through health education and beneficiary sensitization as well as linking this with the nutrition program in order to enable provide a holistic services within the organization target sites. Wellbeing of the communities affected by conflict and displacement coupled with effects of COVID 19 will be offered psychosocial supports as well as referral for the mostly affected beneficiaries to ensure access to basic primary and life-saving health care services, integrated with mental health and counseling to survivors of GBV to at least 60,000 Vulnerable IDPs and Host Communities including 16,800 Girls, 15,900 Boys, 18,000 Women and 7000 Men.
JF has the capacity to access the proposed districts with our presence being represented in the targeted districts. where we previously supported communities and participated in the analytical assessments conducted by different clusters JF has been actively participating in both regional and national coordination meetings with MOH and other stakeholders within the targeted districts. Participation in the coordination meetings within the regions will still be carried out progressively.
While this project is aiming to improve basic health services for target affected communities in the selected districts though fixed health facilities, outreach teams and awareness mobilization, it will also safeguard and protect the wellbeing of both staff and beneficiaries by ensuring all sites to have COVID 19 precautions including social distancing, face masking and encouragement to take vaccines .
The project will adhere to cluster priorities, strategies and government policies
