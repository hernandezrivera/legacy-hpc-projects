1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project, to be implemented in the three provinces of Cabo Delgado, Niassa, and Nampula, aims to provide displaced, and vulnerable girls and women of reproductive age in the host communities, with life-saving and life-sustaining Sexual Reproductive, maternal and newborn health services. The project, to be implemented through Government and CSO implementing partners, aims to provide immediate life-saving access to SRMNH services guided by IASC Minimum Initial Service Package for Reproductive Health principles. The project will also equip health facilities in the 3 provinces with essential reproductive health commodities and supplies in order to establish sufficient capacity for provision of Emergency Obstetric Care to prevent avoidable Maternal and Newborn deaths. The project will ensure integrated mobile brigade services are delivered in the underserved, remote, and hard--to-reach communities and IDP settlements. Lastly, this project will establish capacity for and support skilled birth attendance through skills development and orientation of community and Health Facility-based SRH service providers.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Conduct 2 integrated Mobile Health Brigades per week in each of the target Districts hosting IDPs in the 3 provinces.Equip Health Facilities providing SRH services with essential reproductive health medical supplies and equipmentSupport deployment of Maternal and Newborn Health (MNH) Nurses to Health Facilities providing SRH services in the 3 provinces.Provide support to strengthen referral system for obstetric complications in the remote areas with limited accessConduct information an sensitisation sessions on SRH to increase awareness and access to SRH information and servicesConduct active screening of malaria, STI, HIV/AIDS and TB among other communicable diseases, provide treatment and prevention in maternal and newborn health services.Conduct capacity building on the MISP and other important SRH topics such as EmONC, Contraceptive Technique Update (CTU) etcSet up temporary health facilities for implementation of emergency SRH and other integrated health services.Support sensitisation and availability of SRH services for adolescents and youth including safe sex, contraceptives access, safe post abortion care and GBV/CMR services.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

This project will promote meaningfully safe, secure, and dignified access to SRH services for women and girls. It will ensure access for displaced women and girls who stand a higher risk of exclusion from access to services. Young adolescent girls (10-14years) in particular, stand a higher risk of exclusion from SRH services and are a particular target for this project, with awareness messaging for delayed sexual debut and access to youth-friendly services. The project will also ensure equitable and dignified access to services for women and girls living with disabilities. The project will support availability of free services without charging user-fees.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

To facilitate these, the project will support:Collection of data on vulnerable groups including pregnant women, breastfeeding women, women with disability, people living with HIV/AIDS and develop protection mechanismsGender mainstreaming and to advocate for gender responsive interventions across different clusters to prevent and respond to Gender-Based ViolenceEngagement of target population in every phase of project implementation and to use the feedback mechanism as part of communication with the affected population

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The project will ensure dignified access to SRH services for women and girls living with disabilities. In particular the project will: Collect sex and age disaggregated data (SADD) including data on people with disability (and their specific location at the IDPs camps)Support customisation of spaces and facilities at Health Facilities to enable access and use by women and girls living with disabilities.Reach people with disability through mobile brigade services, home visits (visit the tents) for the people with difficulties to come to the health facilitiesModify and adapt the IEC materials and information session’s methodology and to reach the target based on the type of disability (voice messages for people with visual impairment, using sign language, etc)

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Unsafe disposal of medical waste is one of the biggest environmental hazards today. This project will support adoption of safe medical waste disposal mechanisms and systems for all SRH facilities and service points that includes waste segregation equipment. The project will also support infection prevention and control measures and protocols in all service points.
