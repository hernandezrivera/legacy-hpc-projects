Project Summary

The proposed WASH project is aiming to improve the minimum reception situations and conditions of the persons of concerns at detention center and disembarkation points. The Conditions in those locations are generally inhumane and severely overcrowded, without adequate access to toilets or washing facilities, or clean water.
In coordination with IOM we will choose 6 identified disembarkation points and 6 detention centers to cover while IOM covers other places to avoid any duplication.
UNHCR’s WASH response in those locations will be done through a permanent presence for UNHCR’s and partner’s teams, ensuring a protection monitoring and delivery of assistance are conducted in an educate .

Needs

In close coordination with the protection unit, the field team will continue to identify and prioritize the detention centers and disembarkation points in need for urgent WASH interventions. For 2019 UNHCR estimates the number of detention centers and disembarkation points in need for WASH rehabilitation (some are major others are small work) are 6 detention centers and 6 disembarkation points.

Activities

Under this project the following activities will be implemented:- Enhancing WASH intervention activities in targeted DCs, through the provision of fans, heaters and support with waste management. Prevention measures against waterborne diseases through a comprehensive intervention, which includes sanitation, fumigation, hygiene kits, and regular cleaning of facilities will be put in place. This will help ensuring conditions improve in these most crowded centers. - Maintenance and rehabilitation of WASH facilities such as toilets and waiting areas in 6 disembarkation points such as (but not limited to those areas as this will be coordinated with IOM) Az Zawiya, two locations in Tripoli, Tajurah (Al Hamididyah), Al-Khums and in Tripoli Commercial Port.
