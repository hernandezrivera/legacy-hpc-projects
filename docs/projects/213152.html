Project Summary

Humanitarian needs in Syria continue to rise, and in 2024, 16.7M people are assessed to need assistance. Worsening economic conditions, inflation, currency depreciation, and increases in commodity prices, coupled with the lack of access to fuel and basic services and commodities have determined an overall increase of NFI PiN, estimated to reach the 6.2M in 2024. Lack of access to livelihood opportunities and HHs ability to meet basic needs, along with severe winter conditions, have a major impact on the most vulnerable, and about 10M people lack sufficient and adequate winter clothing and heating devices to cope with low temperatures, making winterization responses imperative as life-saving measures. The population at risk includes youth, PwD, female headed HHs, and a high number of out-of-school children with their parents and caregivers. The goal of the Action is to ensure targeted HHs, including those in underserved communities, meet their essential non-food item and winterization needs to maintain their health and dignity. The Action entails the distribution of core NFIs and minimum winterization kits to 2000 HHs with vulnerable and underserved adults and children (12000 individuals) in GoS areas of rural Aleppo, Deir Ez Zor and accessible areas of Ar-Raqqa. CoreNFI Kits will include: 1 Kitchen kit, 6 mattresses, 6 blankets, 1 jerry can, 1 solar lamp, 1 plastic sheet. Core winter items will include: 1 Winter Clothing Kit, 2 Winter Jackets, 6 winter boots.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The main goal of this Action is to ensure targeted crisis-affected households meet their essential non-food item and winterization needs before the winter of 2024-2025. The action will ensure immediate access to non-food items and winterization items to the population in need, including those in underserved communities, to maintain their health, privacy, and dignity. The Action entails the distribution of core NFIs and minimum winterization package (excluding fuel) to 2,000 households with vulnerable and underserved adults and children (12,000 individuals) in GoS areas of rural Aleppo (Subdistricts of Haritan, Jebel Saman, Maskana, Al-Khafsa, Menbij, and As-Safira), Deir Ez Zor (Al Mayadin, Ashara, Tabni, Khasham), and in accessible areas of Ar-Raqqa (Mansura: in Debsi Afnan area). The kits will be distributed to HHs in rural areas with high severity and unsuitable shelter conditions, and upon identification of needs through WWGVC's other programs, in particular education. Distribution of the items will be in accordance with the specifications of the NFI cluster. Beneficiaries will be selected based on vulnerability criteria established in close consultation with the target population. Various stakeholders, the target population, community leaders, and SARC, will be consulted in establishing the criteria to ensure transparency, acceptance, and accountability. Selected households will include households headed by women, households with elderly and/or persons with disabilities and/or infants, households with out-of-school children, and households with low or no income. GVC will coordinate with SARC, MoE, NFI and WASH Sectors as well as other humanitarian actors (including UN Agencies, i.e. UNICEF and UNHCR) to ensure information sharing, and avoid duplication and overlapping during implementation. Project activities will include: 1) Needs assessment, to study the different needs of the targeted population in the prioritized areas of Aleppo, Deir Ez Zor, and Ar-Raqqa. 2) Market study to assess the availability of core NFI and winterization kit sin the local markets. 3) Procurement and storage of kits in SARC warehouses in eachgovernorate. 4) Identification of community distribution points taking into consideration precautionary measures following the guidelines of the S/NFI cluster. In-kind distribution of core NFI kits and winterization kits to 2,000 HHs. Kits constitution will be in line with the NFI cluster's guidelines and customized depending on the needs assessment. 5) Monitoring, evaluation, and reporting using standard monitoring tools to collect qualitative and quantitative data from the field, distribution reports, KII and observation checklists. PDM will also be conducted following the unified template by the NFI sector.
