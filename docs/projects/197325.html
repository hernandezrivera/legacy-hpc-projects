Project Summary

This project is a continuation of existing Health and GVB activities by NDYD organization, as well as scaling up Health and GVB activities in two centres and two locations. the project will contribute to HRP/ASO 1. Helping to create a safer and more secure environment for people with disabilities, injured and elderly people. This priority will focus on the most vulnerable IDPs especially PwDs and elderly people in and out of the camps who will be the main beneficiaries of the two centres by providing an integrated set of services that targets the gaps and needs of the most vulnerable groups while ensuring the centrality of protection. The project will support two (2) centres with a total of 21000 medical therapy sessions for beneficiaries, including men, women, and children. 65 % are women and girls, the project also will provide sessions for people who have disabilities or special needs and relatives to enable those people to be included again in the community. 41% of the beneficiaries are adults, parents, and the community.
The main goal of the project is to integrate GVB into Health to provide a safer environment for PwDs and elderly people. NDYD organization regularly integrates Protection into Health and other sectors, and the integration for these programs is specifically designed by guidance from the GVB. The project contributes to the quality and integrated Health and MH/PSS as GVB services, as well as towards maintaining and enhancing equitable access to services for IDPs. GVB (MH/PSS) is critical for health. A lack of access to health has direct negative impacts on wellbeing and development. PwDs and injured people out of the centre face greater GVB risks and also may have some mental disorders due to the neglection and the stress that lies on their shoulders, and the project will provide case management and referral services.
NDYD organization conducted a needs assessment that identified needs & possible significant impact. The target centres include:
1) 2 NDYD organization centres that were able to be funded during the 2022 by UK ( GRT): Khotwa physical therapy centre in Atmeh and Taiba physical therapy centre in Kafr Lousin These are performing well and those centres need funding after 31/12/2022.
This project seeks to scale up safe and equitable access to health facilities for PwDs and injured people, especially the people who had physical handicaps due to the explosion and direct attack, in a safe environment, by providing them with movement assistant equipment. The project ensures safe women and child-friendly and accessible for people with physical therapy needs and with disabilities.
the recruitment for the centres was done o trained and experience staff through face-to-face interviews and the certificates were verified with the health directorate.
NDYD organization will increase understanding of the value of Health and GVB principles among, medical staff, beneficiaries, caregivers, and the community. 20 Health staff will be trained on protection and health activities (PFA, PSEA, Gender sensitivity ) and the 6 GVB(MH/PSS) workers will also be trained on PTSD, PM+, Gender sensitivity, GVB(MH/PSS) case management and referral). NDYD organization will also provide the families of the injured people, parents and the community on GVB concerns and the importance of PwDs inclusion in the meetings at the centre or the outreach visits.
This project will cover the incentives for physical therapy and GVB staff at the two centres. The project will distribute movement assistant equipment for PwDs, with all needed stationery and GVB & MH/PSS tools, especially needed materials for children-friendly spaces.
Psychosocial support (PSS) will be provided for the beneficiaries, their caregivers and their families, run by case management with referrals to other relevant services.

3 - Needs

NDYD conducted extensive needs analysis, including primary and secondary sources. The first stage of analysis included a review of secondary sources, including assessments and reports from the Health Cluster and Humanitarian Needs Overview 2022 (HNO). According to the HNO (February 2022), 14.4 Million people are in need of health services.And since the beginning of 2022, NDYD field staff conducted a technical needs assessment, which identified the target two centres. The assessment was conducted as follows:- Assessment methodology:NDYD health needs assessment adopted a purposive sampling method and consulted selected community leaders/representatives, centre principals, as well as project staff to select representative locations for conducting interviews and surveys. Focus group discussions with medical therapy heads, physical therapy staff, Centre Management Committees, parents/community members, and women’s groups. NDYD has been working for over one year in (Khotwa centre in Atmeh village, Taiba centre in Kafr Lousin Village). NDYD has been providing Health and GVB & MH/PSS activities in the centres , supporting IDPs and consistently assessing needs. For these centres, NDYD conducted a baseline assessment as follows of Health and GVB & MH/PSS:+Focus group discussions (FGDs) with male and female participants from the camps + NDYD field staff conducted key informant interviews+Direct Observation Facility Assessments +HH Interviews

4 - Implementation of activities

This project incorporates both health (80%) and protection (20%) sectors and also includes inclusive psychosocial (PSS).This project focuses on the health and GVB&MH/PSS of PwDs and handicapped people in and out of the centre, for IDPs in western Idleb province, Syria. This project targets 21000 physical therapy sessions for people who will directly benefit from health activities and regarding the value of health and will also benefit from Protection concern activities. A critical part of ensuring PwDs, female and elderly people attendance in the centre is also targeting awareness for their surroundings ( parents, relatives and community); therefore, this project will focus on inclusion priority by targeting 10500 beneficiaries of surroundings IDPs and community with PSS services. -Continues physical therapy for beneficiaries in the two centres.- Provide services to the handicapped and injured IDPs in the random camps due to hostilities and the repeated armed attack on the IDPs camps in North Idlib.- Will increase the quality of life of PwDs and elderly people by providing them with movement aid equipment to make sure that they will be self-dependent and enhance their chances to be included again in the community.- Will scale up the availability of quality inclusive health through training centre staff on Protection concerns, inclusive practices, GVB & MH/PSS.- Provided the patient who needs a medical consultant with the needed consult by using referral management.

8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

NDYD is working to be a member of PSEA network so when it comes to sensitive complaints they are referred to PSEA focal point persons, which are the HR manager and M&E manager, therefore they are treated in a very high confidentiality. When it comes to the duration of the solving, the normal complaints take 1 to 3 days but the sensitive and serious ones take 1 to 8 days to collect evidince and dicover the perpetrator. Besides NDYD will Implement training on PSEA as for all personnel. Periodically with support of Field Managers, Focal Points organize a training session recruited staff ideally within three months of recruitment. NDYD will incorporate "Attitudes and Beliefs" session in the training as many respondents highlighted this as the key obstacle to reporting. Regular meetings with local councils will be conducted to discuss the challenges and difficulties that the beneficiaries confront including feedback regarding implemented activities.The following are standards for Child Protection Interventions of NDYD: The staff should treat all children and parents equally and with respect and dignity. There should be no discrimination on the grounds of age, sex, cultural group or any other reason. Staff should not be physically or verbally abusive to children or other staff members.


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

NDYD will take all precautionary measures to avoid exposing our teams and project beneficiaries to danger. Key roles involved in this include the Safety and Security Management (SSM) which is responsible for approval of the security plan, leads and participates in the risk management process, and takes ownership of risk mitigation/contingency planning and execution. Project team members participate in the risk identification process and discuss risk monitoring and mitigation activities at team meetings. Project stakeholders (centre principals) assist in monitoring risk action effectiveness and participate in risk management, as necessary.Risks that are assessed and evaluated include operational, security, financial, management, and protection for safety of children. Risk monitoring and control: Planned risk responses will be executed as required over the life cycle of the project. During risk monitoring and control the following tasks are performed:a. Identify, analyze, and plan for new risksb. Keep track of identified risks and monitor trigger conditionsc. Review project performance information (such as progress/status reports, issues, and corrective actions)d. Re-analyze existing risks to see if the probability, impact, or proper response plan has changede. Review the execution of risk responses and analyze their effectivenessf. Ensure proper risk management policies and procedures are being utilized.All the staff will trained on S&S precedures.
