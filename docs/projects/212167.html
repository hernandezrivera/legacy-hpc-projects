0 - Project Summary (extended)

SKT plans to support to improve the food security of 4,520 households as 22665 individuals within Idleb region live in camps through food basket distribution. SKT will distribute 4520 food baskets to the beneficiaries in the following camps Omahat Al Moumenien, Al Azraq, Al Aman, Al Madenh Monarh, Dalama, Jabal Harim, Abna`a Al Ghab, Tajmae Aleiza, Kafr Hum, Alsafsafh, Al Taakhi, Alzeraah, and Ayidoun in Idleb governorate as one food basket will be distributed to each family over 4 months to ensure a response to time-critical requirements of the vulnerable families, including women-headed households. In addition, SKT has an ongoing bread distribution project within some of the planned camps selected in a geographical location which will supplement the food kits distribution. Hence, families' nutritional status will be increased and therefore will lessen the burden of buying bread as each HHs will get a free bread bag daily. The food basket takes into account beneficiary preference in commodity type and quantity and will be delivered daily within 4 months. SKT will coordinate its activities with other organizations providing food security support in the same local areas to ensure the broadest coverage of persons of concern not otherwise receiving food kits and to avoid duplication and enhance complementarity where feasible. This cooperation will be through field meetings, e-mails, and Skype, including essential participants, such as camp managers, local councils, and community leaders, in addition to active and regular coordination with the FSL cluster. This practice avoids potential overlaps and improves knowledge of good practices and lessons learned. SKT has good communication with the Turkish Red Crescent may be used to mitigate any risk of exporting Flour and Sugar to Syria. SKT has active humanitarian registration and maintains good relations with Turkish border authorities.
