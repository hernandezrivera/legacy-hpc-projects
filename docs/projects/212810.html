Project Summary

The 2023 NW Syria SMART Survey revealed alarming trends. There has been a concerning increase in wasting among children, with a combined Global Acute Malnutrition (GAM) prevalence by weight-for-height z-scores (WHZ) increased from 3.3% in 2022 to 4.4% in 2023. Stunting among children under five years remained a significant concern, with a prevalence rate ranging from Critical to Extreme at 22%. Additionally, exclusive breastfeeding rates witnessed a sharp decline, dropping from 54.6% to 35% in Aleppo and 37.8% in Idleb. All of which are compounded by poor access to adequate and safe water, sanitation, and hygiene services.
Anaemia levels continue to pose a critical health issue, affecting both children under five years and women of reproductive age. The prevalence of anaemia was reported at 37% for children under 5 and a staggering 51% for women. Even more alarming was the prevalence of anaemia among adolescent girls, reaching 59% in Aleppo and 41% in Idleb.
Only 5.5% of children aged 6-23 months consume the minimum acceptable diet (MAD), and 10.2% consume diet from 5 or more food groups (MDD), indicating critical food insecurity (12 million are facing acute food insecurity in Syria, according to HNO 2022).
Insan Charity Organization (ICO) through this project aims to target Pregnant lactating women (PLW), women of reproductive age, children under 5, and caregivers of children under 2 through comprehensive nutrition interventions.
9900 Beneficiaries will be targeted (4,220 Girls 4,034 Boys, and 1,654 PLWs)
Persons with Disabilities (40 PLW 31 Girls 9 Boys)
Idleb - Harim-Harim/ Dana sub-district: Five Rapid Response Teams (RRTs) in locations with no access to the health facilities.
Locations at the sub-district level for the time being as per Nutrition Cluster recommendations, so the exact camps will be identified after have the project got approved.
This project consists of 2 components that aim to address the needs of PLWs and children in accessible camps:
1. Rapid Response Teams (RRTs):
RRT will use the agreed RRTs SOPs which include:
5 RRT will be established and dispatched to affected areas where RRTs will provide lifesaving essential integrated health, nutrition, and child protection field services at scale in locations with no access to the health facilities..
Each RRT will work in specific locations.
The following services will be provided:
· Screening, referral, and treatment of SAM without medical complications
· Skilled IYCF Counselling to PLW and primary caretakers of children under two years and providing messages on food preparation and feeding practices to mothers or caretakers.
· Screen PLWs and refer those with any danger signs or medical problems to the nearest facilities.
· Distribute multiple micronutrient supplements to children and PLW through a community-based approach.
Moreover, if the team notices or identifies any sign of Gender Based Violence (GBV) or abuse among children under five and/or their mothers, they will be referred to a pre-identified center for relevant support.
2. CVA for Nutrition Objective:
Distribute CVA to the PLW and children 6-23 months (CU2) through their caregivers who were identified by RRT staff:
ICO will distribute the CVA with one transfer value as per NW Syria Nutrition CVA operational guidelines as follows:
Provision of CVA plus SBC for individual feeding assistance within a household receiving General Food Assistance (GFA): 300 PLW: 35 USD per PLW monthly for 8 months
Provision of CVA and SBC to facilitate access to treatment for acute malnutrition with no medical complication: 15USD per child for 200 CU5 for 8 months
The cash distribution will be done by Financial Service Provider (FSP) in the presence of ICO team.
