Project Summary

COHESODEC seeks through this project to ensure a more effective GBV prevention and response approach which creates significant impact in target vulnerable communities. The project will be targeting a total number of 10,770 vulnerable communities affected by the ongoing conflict! These target communities in Mezam, Momo and Mezam divisions in the North-West and South-West Regions include the following: Bafut, Bali, Bamenda 2, Bamenda 3, Tubah, Batibo, Mbengwi, Konye, Kumba 2, Kumba 3 and Mbonge.
In implementing this project, COHESODEC is committed to ensuring the following preventive and response activities:
>Provision of holistic support to survivors of gender-based violence involving 700 GBV survivors (women, men, girls, boys) who will be receiving at least one form of assistance in the response. The project will also be strengthening the capacities of staff of 40 operational organizations and relevant authorities on GBV standards and policies.
>COHESODEC will also be conducting GBV prevention activities involving 10,000 beneficiaries at community-level including community sensitization and sensitization of traditional leaders and religious leaders while also providing vocational training and economic support with the involvement of 30 women and girls (including livelihood activities for women and girls, victims of sexual exploitation).
Our response in this activity will significantly address/mitigate GBV related issues as far as prevention and response in vulnerable communities is concern!
