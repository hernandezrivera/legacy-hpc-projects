Project Summary

The association relies on providing financial and community support to orphans and widows registered with the association from the neediest and poorest families. Financial support consists of sums of money. At the beginning of each month, the situation of the most needy family is assessed by filling out a questionnaire approved by the association’s social assistance office, which includes a number of specific points that determine the family’s situation: (widow, divorced, number of children, children in school, health status of the mother and children, presence A specific disease, a disabled child, the condition of the house (owned or rented, the widow’s work) and then the number of points is collected to determine the most vulnerable family and determine the amount of assistance required, and it is re-evaluated through field and periodic visits to families to follow up and monitor the extent to which the social and economic situation improves after the assistance to determine the continuation of the assistance. Help. Provide or stop assistance and transfer it to another family. Providing financial support helps women secure their basic needs. It protects her from exposure to sexual exploitation and harassment to provide the necessary needs. It helps them secure opportunities for a small manual labor project in which women can secure an income. This material protection protects them from exploitation, harassment, or even exposure to gender-based violence. Providing a financial return for them protects them from all types of exploitation. In addition to support, the association relies in its work on providing the various needs of families, especially in the field of supporting the educational level of orphans through follow-up. Promoting free lessons in school for orphans, and the association pays teachers’ salaries. It also provides empowerment courses for widows and women who have lost their breadwinners through vocational courses at the association’s headquarters for free, and the association covers the wages of the trainers. The association also works, through activities and training, to increase and raise community awareness among women and families.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The association provides financial and community support to orphans and widows registered with the association from the most vulnerable families after studying the questionnaire and determining aid. Financial support is through the distribution of monthly cash amounts. Providing financial support helps women secure their basic needs. It protects them from exposure to sexual exploitation and harassment to provide for their family’s needs. The amount of money helps them secure opportunities for a small manual labor project or any profession in which women can secure an income. This material protection protects them from exploitation, harassment, or even exposure to gender-based violence, because they are a vulnerable group that can be exploited. Achieving a financial return for her protects her from all types of exploitation and makes her strong, able to rely on herself, protect her children, and protect herself from any type of exploitation or sexual harassment in exchange for financial benefit or any need. The association works to follow up and monitor the development of the family situation, and makes many visits to follow up and evaluate the situation, while community support is provided through educational activities and dialogue sessions to raise community awareness among children and women, which aims to fill the gaps. Free educational activities for children, developing their skills, and psychological support activities. The association bears all costs. Which contributes to their integration into society, raises the level of community awareness, and enhances self-confidence. Through targeted activities for children entitled “Our Children...The Future of Our Country.” To enhance the role of women and their families in society through the “Be Strong” activity, vocational training courses, dialogue sessions, and community awareness sessions are provided to widows and women who have lost their breadwinners at the association’s headquarters for free. The association covers the trainers’ wages. Securing financial income for families is important and necessary, especially in light of the economic deterioration that Syria is witnessing and the sharp rise in the prices of basic and other materials. It helps provide the necessary needs, and ensures the protection of women and children from exploitation and harassment in order to obtain services and needs. We also ensure that children continue their education and are protected from child protection risks such as working to support their families. Reducing the risks of societal gender-based violence between males and females by providing equal opportunities for both nationalities to obtain their rights.
