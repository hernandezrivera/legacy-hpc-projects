Project Summary

In response to the increase in protection risks, distress, and impact of the multiple emergencies in-country, Plan International Lebanon with himaya, SHIELD and IMC will implement an integrated child protection-GBV intervention with a number of Child Protection and GBV activities that are designed to be child centred-, adolescent/youth- friendly, gender responsive ad inclusive and following the socio-ecological model to respond to the needs. The intervention is aligned based on the Emergency Response Plans1 and targets the most vulnerable Lebanese, Palestinians and migrant children and women. Interventions under Child Protection includes case management, psychosocial support, ensuring access to specialized services, as well as enabling girls and boys to improve their protection through strengthened community and family support.
While the intervention under GBV includes awareness sessions targeting young women and men and parents, community-based PSS, Focused PSS, emotional support groups, safety audits, community action groups and hygiene kits distribution.

Plan Lebanon will target adolescent girls and boys, young women and men and their families, including: adolescents and youth affected by the economic situation and considered at high-risk or having increased vulnerability and negative coping mechanisms; adolescents and youth at-risk of deterioration of medical and MHPSS conditions; adolescents living in poor conditions with limited financial resources and lacking basic needs and access to income; Out of school adolescents and those at-risk of dropping out; Adolescent headed households; Adolescents with special needs; Adolescents at-high risk or survivors of violence, abuse and exploitation including Child Labour, WFCL and child marriage; Adolescents with high level of psychosocial distress.

The project will be rolled out in Akkar, south and SDCs in BML

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

CPObjective 1: Provision of Case Management support to vulnerable adolescent girls and boys, along with referrals to specialized services based on needs detected throughout the CM intervention. Medium-High risk adolescents will also be provided with Focused PSS Support, and their caregivers will receive Parenting Skills to help build their positive coping mechanisms. Objective 2: Vulnerable adolescents (including medium-low risk) will also be supported through Community Based PSS Support, and some will be involved in planning for a CP initiative to actively mobilize communities to take actions to prevent violence, abuse and exploitation and enable them to better respond when this does take place. GBV: Objective 1: Adolescent Girls, boys, and youth survivors and at risk of GBV have access to awareness raising sessions, community based PSS, emotional support group and will benefit from hygiene kits distribution. Safety audits will be conducted and community action groups will be established to prevent, mitigate, and respond to GBVObjective 2: FPSS for adolescents will be provided to promote the well-being of adolescents and youths, as well as raising awareness on SRHR and GBV and providing a safe entry point for referrals to specialized SRHR and GBV services. Parents and caregivers will be engaged in Parenting Skills sessions with the aim of improving their psychosocial competencies and resilience and strengthen their knowledge and skills related to SRHR and SGBV.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

Plan International integrates PSEA and child safeguarding in programs through policies which provide staff and partners mandatory requirements, awareness and trainings for the protection of children, staff and program participants from violence, abuse and sexual exploitation. Plan also adopts a gender transformative approach to ensure gender equality and inclusion and aims to reshape unequal gender and power relations to realize girls’ rights and equality across children, youth and adults. The intervention approach includes gender and inclusion analysis, using Gender Transformative Marker to identify areas to be strengthened tackling gender inequality & exclusion. Project activities ensure PwD accessibility, sensitization of staff, partners, and community committees on Plan’s Disability Inclusion training package including identification and mitigation of barriers to physical and psychological inclusion. Data disaggregation include disability, gender, age, and diversity considerations.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

To ensure Accountability to Affected Population, Plan prepared an age- and gender- appropriate feedback and complaint mechanism (FCM) toolbox. All Plan International partners are trained on Plan's FCM. At the beginning of project implementation, FCM is adapted, through consultations with partners, children and project participants to select appropriate channels, to fit the purpose and context of the project (geographic location, COVID-19 situation), and to be aligned with existing mechanisms within the partner organization. In case of remote implementation (following covid-19 restrictions), the main channels used include hotline, WhatsApp and surveys. Following consultations, a process will be set to ensure tracking feedback and complaints, referring to relevant focal persons and closing the feedback loop in a timely manner. Additionally, Plan will conduct a FCM survey with all the project beneficiaries to assess their satisfaction with the services and assistance provided.
