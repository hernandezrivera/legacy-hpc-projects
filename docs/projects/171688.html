Project Summary

The proposed project comes in the second term of the implementation of the Puntland sexual offences act that aims at reducing the risk of SGBV through prevention, response and quality service to survivors. This doesn’t exclude Galmudug regions in which the project will operate as the principles enshrined in the act are still relevant and applicable.
Similarly, the project will focus on reducing the risk of SGBV through comprehensive interventions that build community resilience most particularly Persons of Concern (PoC) and IDP’s.
The Proposed project will foster on mechanism of how Persons of Concern could access medical service, a robust responsive and from the community through grass root members engagement including women advocates, religious leaders and youth groups will pave way for a sustained and enhanced mechanism.
TIDES will employ a community based participatory approach that empowers local structures response mechanism through peer engagement, learning and coping mechanism.
The project will foster an integrated approach that harness the needs of the host community and persons of concern including refugees, asylum seekers and IDP; s. The magnitude of their problem differs however, providing an enabling environment and mutual trust and relations thus the project will achieve the expected outcome.

Project Activities

TIDES will apply a community based participatory approach to deliver the interventions aimed at achieved the project objectives and outcomes. TIDES will deploy a professional project manager with profound experience in gender and human rights issues as well as deeply conversant with Persons of concern needs. TIDES will coordinate the work of the safe shelter and space in Galkayo to support the survivors of sexual and gender-based violence. TIDES will establish a community-based structures to support community outreach and referral pathways.TIDES will engage with local medical providers to provide medical services including post-exposure prophylaxis (PEP) treatment. TIDES is uniquely placed as it had facilitated provision of medical service to People living with HIV AIDS. The contractors will be selected jointly by the representatives from the community leaders from the PoC groups. The selection will be administered in a free, transparent and fair manner, competitive bidder with quality services and accompanying financials will be selected from the bidders. TIDES will procure an average amount of sanitary materials and materials to assist the survivors of violation, the purchased materials will be kept in safe stores managed by the organization. The survivor to receive the assistance will go through series of verifications on the eligibility, this is to prevent from people canvasing on the service been offered. TIDES will train case workers and collaborate with other case workers from different programs to Strengthen referral system allowing children with specific needs to access timely protection services. TIDES already is familiar with the Ministry of Justice Juvenile justice program that meets UNHCR child protection under the key strategic objectives. TIDES will collaborate with GBV/IMS that is used by the protection cluster at national level. TIDES will populate the information management system and share the report on monthly basis.TIDES will train community leaders on how to respond and prevention SGBV through community dialogues, community awareness raising program and facilitated discussion on the role of the community. TIDES will facilitate access to legal service by providing connections between the formal/informal justice institutions and persons of concerns. TIDES have profound experience in mindset programs and will employ classified approaches to engage the public on their role in SGBV prevention and response at the community level. This includes reporting and understanding the justice chain that will help members gain the confidence. TIDES through the leadership of the executive director and specific responsibility of the project manager will be responsible for the monitoring of the progress. The project manager will submit a comprehensive monthly report that specified the indicators and targets. The information submitted shall disaggregated by sex, age, locations and nature of services offered. The project manager will conduct a monitoring mission in all the location on quarterly basis. Corrective measures will be recommended accordingly and issues that need the intervention of UNHCR will be share in timely manner. The Monitoring missions will use diverse mechanism/methodologies to access information from client records, client contact, among others. The mission will keep the information from the clients anonymous so that not affect the service of the client.
