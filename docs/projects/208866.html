Project Summary

Since fighting between the Sudanese Armed Forces (SAF) and the Rapid Support Forces (RSF) erupted on 15 April 2023 in the capital Khartoum, This resulted in high contamination of Explosive Ordnances (EO) and different types of remnants of war Khartoum and Darfur. Explosive Ordnance Risk Education ( EORE) is aiming to address short term emergency needs as well as to contribute to long term sustainable response in line with the thematic areas of the national plan for protection of civilians . NADA used to deliver EORE projects since its establishment in 2014 .NADA strategy aims to provide EORE services that meet short-and long-term needs with special focus in the remote areas, IDPs camps, new settlements, rural villages and among nomads, people on move . NADA plans the EORE program following the International Mine Action Standards (IMAS) and SNMAS. In the short term, emergency EORE will be provided to at-risk population such as IDPs, returnees, nomads and local communities in the form of direct presentation, Community based EORE training, safety orientation sessions , public information campaigns (PICs), and radio program broadcasting. Affected communities including EO victims and Persons with Disabilities (PWDs) will be given awareness raising on the rights and issues of victims and PWDs. The long-term strategy will focus on integrating EORE into the existing national and community services and structures through provision of Community based CB EORE targeting of teachers, health /social workers, community leaders, members of the community based protection networks (CBPNs) and community volunteers including children. This will contribute to better access of communities to information through the presence of trained community members This EORE project will consider raising ERW victims’ awareness in terms of prevention of incidence; victim-related rights and available information on services providers. The project will be executed through 6 EORE professional teams 2 teams in each state, the EORE team will be composed of team leader and three EORE officers .The project is designed taking to consideration the current conflict and security instability. During the implementation ; NADA will consider two aspects a) age and gender – appropriate methods of communication will be used. Messages for drama, songs, and public campaigns were developed for different age group with full consideration of culture. NMAC and UNMAS will be consulted prior to printing /production and/or distribution of messages. b) new innovation NADA will pilot new innovation such as silent theater methodology. Also the project will consider delivery of EORE to school children during the school year, while focusing on the other children out of school . During the limitation of EORE teams ‘movement, the project team will focus on CB EORE, radio, PIC and media channels to ensure wider coverage in case of road locks due to insecurity or conflicts and clashes.

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

NADA will recruit project staff from targeted communities, the staff who recruited will be involved and fully dedicated to implement and follow up on project activities. NADA is one of the leading agencies in GBV and thus The staff will be trained on PSEA, GBV, protection monitoring and reporting , principle of humanitarian (accountability and do no harm ect..), and code of conduct to avoid potential risks of violence, exploitation and abuse of beneficiaries. NADA organization used to promote meaningful access, safety and dignity, and to prevent and minimize as much as possible any unintended negative effects of intervention which can increase people’s vulnerability to both physical and psycho-social risks, through recruitment the project staff from targeted communities. The staff will be trained on PSEA, GBV, protection monitoring and, principle of humanitarian aid (accountability and do no harm etc.), code of conduct to avoid potential risks of violence, exploitation and abuse of beneficiaries. Also NADA set-up appropriate mechanisms to address concerns and complaints. The project will adopt community based approach which will applied through community based EORE interventions that promotes active engagement and participation of all community segments in design and implementation
