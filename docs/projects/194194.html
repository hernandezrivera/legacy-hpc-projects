Project Summary

The project interventions will empower child protection sector, Elgossa Charity Organization for Rural Development (ECORD) proposing to improve the provision and access to safe environment for children, thus increasing accessibility to child services and rights of children to live with dignity. This in turn will ensure a smooth and continuous flow, while keeping children away from the danger caused by idleness and expose. the project proposed interventions will empower Elgossa Charity Organization for Rural Development (ECORD) to attain the key objective of strengthening the accessibility to important services through integrated interventions targeting vulnerable communities in Nyala Shimal and Nyala Janoub Localities. Under child protection harm. the interventions, with increased cases of conflict, ECORD believes that tackling child protection issues through strengthening the capacity development of existing structures while increasing awareness among communities, and enhancing monitoring and linkages and integration to service provision at individual, household and community levels.
Through this proposed sector interventions, ECORD is hoping that by using a combined approach to tackle the challenges faced by communities in Nyala Shamal, Nyala Janoub in South Darfur State, ECORD will provide much needed lifesaving services, and set a stable basis for their sustainability through community outreach, appointment and finally adoption of key lessons for better self-reliance. The project aim is to enhance the child protection of population affected by the crisis through community-based and individually targeted protection interventions; The project aims at strengthen the delivery of child protection services and interventions for girls and boys with a focus on those most at risk of violence, exploitation and abuse in conflict affected locations, Increasing access to safe community spaces to empower women and children; Mental health and psycho-social service provision for Conflict-Affected children; improving Psycho social resilience through Social Capital for IDP's and host communities; The project aim to improve psycho-social well-being of children most affected by the conflict in South Darfur State . And increasing access to child protection and reintegration services for conflict-affected children and adults in South Darfur state through implementing comprehensive Community based approach and full package of C PIE Activities. ECORD will contribute to the child protection main Objectives as below:
Objective 1: Acutely vulnerable children and their caregivers are outreached, and their needs addressed through specialized child protection interventions including multi-sect oral case management services.
'Objective 2: Child protection risk factors are addressed and protective factors that bolster the well-being of children, families, and communities are promoted using primary and secondary prevention approaches .
'Objective 3: Improved well-being, resilience, and protective environment of girls and boys through provision of psycho social support, life skills, parenting skills, and advocacy in addition to mobilization of communities and duty bearers across the sociology-ecological mode.

1 - Needs

Children throughout Sudan continue to face violence and a range of protection risks. Grave child rights violations remain a significant concern with children at risk of killing and injury, recruitment and use in hostilities, torture, detention, abduction, sexual violence, attacks on schools and hospitals and denial of humanitarian access by parties to conflict. From January 2021 to June 2022, the UN verified 349 grave violations against 326 children (228 boys, 93 girls, 5 sex unknown) in Sudan. The most prevalent violations, killing and maiming, continue to remain concerning, and children represent 31.8 per cent of total casualties from landmines or ERW. Access to conflict-affected children in Jabal Marrah, Jebel Moon, and SPLM-N controlled areas in Blue Nile, South Kordofan and Abyei administrative locations remains a challenge. The compound effects of conflict, continuous displacement, economic situation and extended dry spells has deepened poverty, Family separation continues to be a persistent child protection issue. A total of 4,761 children unaccompanied or separated (UASC) were reunified with families/caregivers. Separation from one’s primary caregivers exposes children to greater risks of exploitation, abuse, neglect, and psycho-social distress The Child Protection Cluster's 2023 PiN is 3.8 million. Up to 60 per cent of the people in need of protection services in Sudan are children who continue to be exposed to protection threats of recruitment, family separation, abuse, neglect, abduction, exploitation, and violence. About 3.5 million children (51 per cent female, 49 per cent male), including 15 per cent of children with disabilities, need immediate child protection services. In 2023, 361,000 children are in extreme need (10 per cent), about 2,061,920 in severe need (58 per cent), and about 939,650 are in stress need (27 per cent) and require humanitarian child protection assistance. Children with disabilities are among the most vulnerable. South Darfur state precisely in Nyala Shimal, Nyala Janoub Localities there are four target IDPs Camp, and host community within Nyala town are targeted in this proposed project. On 1 September 2022 an inter-agency assessment mission was led by OCHA, for Nyala Shamal IDPs camp (OTASH, Dereige), and the assessment identified needs related to child protect which will be addressed by this project proposal. another assessment conducted on 4 of September 2022 for Belil locality IDPs Camp (Al sereif, Alsalam IDPs camp), and there are needs identified for child protection, as the locations mentioned above. These assessments identified the CP needs for the above mentioned areas which will be addressed by the activities of this project.

7.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will inform and interact with inter-agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including SEA and abuse complaints?

Accountability to the affected population is core in the ECORD response and will be strengthened through continuous consultation with the affected population on the response modalities as well as their satisfaction with services provided. In addition, ECORD is an active member of the national PSEA technical and coordination team and leads the survivor support task team and together is working with the team members to ensure that GBV referral mechanisms are accessible to survivors of SEA. ECORD will equip implementing partners and targeted communities with information regarding the SEA, and how to report and access services including health, legal and psychosocial support services. ECORD ’s partners and beneficiaries play an active role in the decision-making process in regards to the planning, implementing, and evaluating of activities.This is done by conducting focus group discussions with women and girls in women centers. The project will support the development of self-protection capacities and aim at improving beneficiaries’ daily lives through improved formal and informal protection mechanisms. This is considered the most effective way of tackling gender inequality as the root cause of child protection issues .
