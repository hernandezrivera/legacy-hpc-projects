Project Summary

The project aims to provide protection, child protection and gender-based violence prevention and response services to the most vulnerable men, women, boys and girls through community-based interventions and protection emergency response mobile teams in Northern Syria (North Aleppo) and Northwest Syria (West Aleppo, Idlib and North Hama)
The project will cover 10 WGSS, 3 CFS, 3 Youth and Adolescents centers and 31 Mobile teams (16 of those teams are emergency response protection mobile teams to response for the new displacement movement and to conduct the assessment needed to respond for those IDPs) covering most of Idlib governance, west and north of Aleppo.
The main objective of the project is to enhance the prevention and mitigation of protection risks, and respond to protection needs through supporting the protective environment in Syria, by promoting international law, IHL, HRL and through quality principled assistance. And to contribute to expanding the availability and quality of Protection, CP and GBV services in targeted locations, In line with the strategic priorities of the 2019 GP, CP and GB to ensure the protection of population affected by the crisis is improved through community-based and individually targeted protection interventions and through advocacy with duty bearers, Increase and more equitable access for boys and girls to quality child protection interventions in targeted locations in line with the Child Protection Minimum Standards in Humanitarian Action and Survivors have access to quality specialized GBV services and measures are in place to prevent and mitigate risks of GBV .

Needs

IhsanRD has been implementing Protection activities in different subdistricts and according to the HRP 2019 Protection issues and needs in Syria remain prevalent and continue to affect all population groups. The protracted nature of the crisis has led to the emergence of increasingly complex and interconnected protection issues and needs, in a continuously evolving environment. In some areas of Syria, in particular the North West and North East, the civilian population is still exposed to hostilities, leading to civilian casualties and forced displacements in seek of safety.The most reported reason for lack/loss of civil documentation is described as concerns about approaching authority This is a possible consequence of cases of families reporting challenges in seeking official documentation while residing in non-government-controlled areas, the result of shifting lines of control, policies of confiscation of personal documentation in the IDP sites in North East Syria. Additional main causes identified were the loss of personal documentation and instances when documents have been left behind when fleeing.Lack loss of civil documentation continues to be described as a protection issue affecting all population groups, with a higher prevalence among female Movement restrictions are described as particularly affecting people without civil documentation, as well as men and boys, a situation likely deriving from the overall security situation in different areas of the country. Qualitative sources also indicate how restricting movements is a prevailing coping mechanism in a volatile security environment, especially for women and girls. Community-based protection (CBP) empowers communities to obtain their rights safely and with dignity. Using CBP, crisis-affected communities and the humanitarian actors who assist them can identify a community's most serious protection risks, explore their causes and effects, and jointly decide how to prevent and respond to them. It achieves better protection impact and improves the lives of people of concern. So, Ihsan is working on providing protection services in the community center which consists of CP-GBV-Legal Activities in addition to specialized services. Communities are spread throughout the country and correlate to areas that have seen most explosive incidents over the past 12 months. Assessments which indicated a gender-based violence needs in Syria, Ihsan is implementing GBV programs by WGSS. The length of the crisis and deep-rooted social norms, in conjunction with the mounting lawlessness in some areas, are normalizing this violence, resulting in the detrimental psychosocial wellbeing and continued erosion of women’s and girls’ rights. The shame and stigma surrounding sexual violence means that survivors often do not talk about violence when it happens. Families arrange marriages for girls, believing it will protect them and ease the financial burden on the family. Also regarding to the Child Protection program, Ihsan has been implementing CP program since 2015 in different Sub-Districts.Children throughout Syria continue to face pervasive violence and a range of protection risks. Grave child rights violations remain a significant concern. The conflict and its impact on children and families have fueled negative coping mechanisms such as child labor, early marriage and child recruitment, putting adolescent boys and girls at particular risk, while straining the capacities of families and communities to protect children.Child labor interferes with children’s schooling by depriving them of the opportunity to attend school.Child marriage mainly affecting adolescent girls, though adolescent boys are also at risk. Girls and boys are married early as a household negative coping strategy.Recruitment of children, particularly adolescent boys, by all parties to the conflict continues to be reported in 12 of 14 governorates of Syria

Implementation of activities

IhsanRD has been working in the protection sector in Syria since 2015. And we will continue responding to protection concerns in 2019, Protection Program staff will oversee the direct implementation of all proposed activities. The program will deliver GP, CP, and GBV services through WGSS, CFS, and mobile protection services. The Child Protection services engage children in activities designed to support positive psycho-social wellbeing and coping strategies and our CFS is aimed to provide children with a protected environment in which they can participate in organized activities to play, socialize, learn, and express themselves as they rebuild their lives. Our CP activity included: structured PSS, Recreational activities, Awareness Raising sessions about CP issues for women, men, girls and boys by MT,these sessions are being provided in the community by coordinating with Stakeholders. Sessions is being provided to be suitable and harmonized with the community needs and Protection cons. Usually a huge number of beneficiaries can be targeted in this kind of activities as this session are being conducted by ER and CP teams and targeting all the community with different ages and genders. Parenting skills courses are also crucial to improve overall household wellbeing by helping parents to deal with their own stress and anecdotally we have seen significant improvement in child well-being as a result. Our YEPCentres in Syria providing youth and adolescents with services concentrating on building their capacity by improving their skills in different domains including Life Skills and VT which can help them in using these skills as a positive coping mechanism instead of negative one. A great commitment in attendance among targeted groups in addition to the community acceptance for this kind of programs. An additional WGSSs and linked MTs provide GBV prevention and response services to women and girls, providing empowerment activities for women and adolescent girls and through VT, life skills programs, and awareness raising sessions about GBV issues, response services for survivors which includes case management, direct support, and referrals. case management team has extensive experience and are a trusted source of support for women, girls, and children in the area. These teams include experienced case management staff that provide high-quality case management as well as counselling and structured PSS in CP and GBV. These teams are also available to receive referrals of cases from other sectors. We also coordinate to distributions dignity kits to IDPs women and girls during the ER Also, IhsanRD in coordination with the protection cluster aims to alleviate suffering, enhance protection and build the resilience of individuals and communities. and respond to the high number of IDPs displaced to NW Syria, this will be done through the mobile teams providing protection, CP, and GBV and MA services; and improved coordination of the emergency response in Jebel Samaan District. This will be done through Provision of integrated emergency protection services through MTs including specialized services. One of the most important components in the Emergency services is providing Direct support for the most vulnerable cases among IDPs according to the CP concerns and Risk level. Regarding to legal services Ihsan started applying this kind of activities by applying protection monitoring assessments in addition to providing awareness raising sessions about Protection issues related to Documentations and other concerns in the community. MEAL team will establish a final monitoring framework based on the finalized programmatic work plan. This includes the frequency of field visits. Different monitoring tools include surveys and FGDs with beneficiaries and none beneficiaries, on-site observation reports, meetings with the community.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

1- Resistance or backlash from sections of the community and community leaders on GBV messaging. Ihsan will Engagement Community throughout all project cycle. It includes meetings with religious and community leaders and Adaptation of GBV messaging to ensure sensitivity to the context and culture.2- And other risks associated with the project are difficulties in GBV mainstreaming with other humanitarian sectors projects, Therefore, Ihsan will train the other sectors on the principles of gender-based violence and Identifying the risks of gender-based violence in each sector and the referral pathway.3- Services duplication as many CP organizations that also working on providing Child protection activities. Ihsan will do high coordination with CP Sub cluster depending on the services map to make sure that there is a CP need with no sufficient responders in the targeted areas.4- Difficulties in coordination with other sectors regarding to the Referral path way during case management services, as each organization has its criteria in selecting beneficiaries especially Livelihood Programs which can be a restriction of doing an effective referral path way. Ihsan will work on more coordination with other sectors by setting service mapping for all services that exist in addition to be aware of the other sectors criteria trying to do more advocacy and mainstreaming for Child Protection concerns and needs. 5- Lack of qualification in Protection domain especially in Northern Aleppo. So, Ihsan will work on a comprehensive capacity building plan in all protection sectors regarding to GBV Concept, CP and General protection.
