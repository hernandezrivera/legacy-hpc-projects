Project Summary

preventive and promotive services such as Infant Young Child Feeding (IYCF) promotion and counseling as well as awareness creation and nutrition messaging within the community will be done to further create awareness and prevent relapses as well as improve treatment outcomes during the treatment process for those diagnosed and enrolled in the program. Treatment of severely malnourished children under 5 years without medical complication will be done through the out patient therapeutic program targeting to treat 14170 under fives while pregnant and lactating women will be targeted and benefit from infant young child feeding promotion with a target of 2258 pregnant lactating women and care takers of children. Pregnant women will be screened and relevantly referred to other programs as needed although they will also benefit from supplementation of micro-nutrients to prevent any cases of maternal malnutrition and intrauterine growth retardation. Nutrition and health promotion sessions will also be conducted at the nutrition sites as well as within the community through community health workers assigned house to house. Nutrition sessions will include sessions on Infant and Young Child Feeding, hygiene and health promotion as well as food and nutrients education. Mothers of children under five who are visiting the nutrition treatment centers will be targeted for nutrition session and IYCF counselling at the nutrition center while community health workers will continue with nutrition messaging at the community level targeting men and women in the catchment areas. Capacity building of key technical teams who will provide treatment as well as community members will be done to improve quality of service provided and gain acceptance and support from community with improved understanding of the program.

Implementing Partners

ADA will independently implement the project in all the specific target locations. To strengthen coordination at the field level ADA will also work closely with other relief and development organizations that are present in the specific project locations such as UNICEF, WFP, IOM, Ministry of Health (Federal Government), Regional Health authorities as well as the local community members. Given the context, ADA will take an integrated but flexible approach to this program, responding to opportunities as they arise, while maintaining the overall objectives of improved quality, coverage and sustainable management of the health facilities and the outreach sites ADA is a member and a partner of the Health and nutrition clusters. Monthly participation at the national and field level in the health and nutrition co-ordination meetings will be maintained in order to represent the project needs and achievements throughout the implementation period, while sharing ideas and information with other agencies.

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

Due to the looming food insecurity and possible flooding in the event of more than average rains, the targeted population face among other risks starvation, displacement and upsurge of diseases.

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

ADA has gender policy that ensure that when hiring staff that women are given 50% consideration in different positions. In addition, the project target populations are segregated into men, women, boys and girls hence giving emphasis to all gender groups in the community. During the screening and admission of children into nutrition programs, boys and girls will be given equal chances. Gender considerations will be taken into account throughout the assessment, planning and design of the interventions and significant consideration will be given to pregnant women, mothers, and women of child-bearing age. In the provision of health and nutrition promotion campaigns, messaging will be targeted for specific gender and age groups and as appropriate the events will be organized separately. Equitable and gender-sensitive participation and decision-making approaches will be adopted throughout the project, including women’s participation in the community nutrition committees and access to gender-specific health and nutrition information. Community consultations through focus-group discussions will take place during the project implementation, and stakeholders will be involved in decision-making processes to tailor the project to the needs of different gender and age groups. Furthermore, data will be dis-aggregated by age and gender in all recording and reporting.

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

Currently, the situation in Gedo is stable with no major attacks in the town and surrounding villages however ADA will undertake necessary assessment of the security situation and work with relevant authorities.
