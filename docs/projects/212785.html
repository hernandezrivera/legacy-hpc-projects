Project Summary

According to the SNFI HNO 2024, 2,122,956 PiN for Shelter and 1,166,770 PiN for NFI in NWS. Per the MSNA 2023 findings, in NWS, the level of safety, security, and privacy provided by the shelter is 26% unsafe, 2% dangerous, and 9% no privacy. 50% of HHs have partial access to internal basic services and 68% have partial access and 8% have no access to external common services and infrastructure. 65% of the shelters are damaged and 75% have adequacy issues.
WH proposes a 12-month action using a flexible integrated programming approach that includes NFI distribution, using pre-positioned in-kind and cash/voucher assistance modalities, construction of dignified shelters with integrated infrastructure improvements, and basic shelter repairs.
WH will contribute to the two objectives of the SNFI sector, which are:
SO1) Provide life-saving and life-sustaining shelter and NFI support.
SO2) Reinforce an enabling protection environment and community cohesion by improving housing and related community/public infrastructure.
The project formulation has been based on needs identified by the SNFI cluster, supported with NA conducted by WH in Dec 2023 and firsthand consultations with camps management and local authorities/stakeholders.
The proposed project aims to address emergency and more sustainable shelter and NFI needs of vulnerable IDPs, returnees, and affected host communities with acute needs living in Atareb, Daret Azza, and Mare' sub-districts (Aleppo) in addition to, Bennsh, Idleb, Ariha, Mhambal, Armanaz, Dana and Harim subdistricts (Idleb) in NWS (Severity 3&4).
WH’s shelter & NFI program recognizes the different needs and vulnerabilities of boys, girls, men, and women of different ages and strives to address the specific needs of each group across the range of shelter and NFI interventions.
WH’s NFI response aims to assist 1,000 HHs/5,000 INDIVs with a one-off distribution of emergency NFI kits, including cash/voucher for NFI, and 7,000 HHs/35,000 INDIVs with the provision of seasonal and supplementary NFIs including heating fuel including cash for winter, to heat their shelter during the winter season 2024.
WH’s dignified shelter solutions aim to:
(1) Provide essential infrastructure rehabilitation in prioritized IDP camps that may include site upgrades, graveling main and sub-roads, rain drainage system & channels, paving paths between tents, and lighting on main roads and pathways as needed.
(2) Provide dignified shelter solutions by piloting an innovative shelter approach to accommodate 600 HHs/3,000 INDIVs with all accessible features for HHs with PWDs to ensure accessibility, privacy, and safety for all vulnerable groups.
These components will be guided by Dignified Shelters Techincal Guidance V3 published by the SNFI cluster in January 2024 as well as TWiG on Flood Risk Reduction and ensure that HLP documentation is well carried out in parallel with field coordination with the relevant local councils (guided by HLP TWG Due Diligence Guidance Nov 2023).
Equally, the basic shelter repairs component will enhance sustainable, recovery-oriented shelter solutions through support to IDPs returning and returnees to their original homes and contributing to rehabilitating buildings in newly accessible areas. This will include assisting 500 HHs/2,500 INDIVs with moderate rehabilitation of damaged houses. This component will be guided by the shelter rehabilitation guidance published by the SNFI cluster in 2018 and ensure that HLP documentation is well carried out and guided by HLP TWG Due Diligence Guidance Nov 2023.
The program will seek feedback from both male & female BNFs and fully interact with both genders to ensure equitable access & equal opportunities.
The overall target number of beneficiaries is 45,500 INDIVs for 2024. 40,000 INDIVs are provided with NFIs, 3,000 INDIVs will be supported through dignified shelter solutions, and 2,500 INDIVs will be assisted by basic shelter repairs.
