Project Summary

The project aims at providing access to protective learning routines for 701,400 conflict and extremism affected children and adolescents through support to operational formal schools (pre-primary, primary and secondary) in North-West, South-West, Littoral, West, Centre, East, Adamaoua and Far North regions. The project intends through the blend of interventions proposed (distribution of school inputs, teachers training, studies) to prevent attacks on education and ensure education in emergencies delivery in conflict and high insecurity context.

1 - Besoins / Needs

Eight regions affected by the crises are targeted by this project for a total of 701,400 children and adolescents. Also, a total of 2,000 teachers and other education personnel will benefit the capacity building activities to contribute to the improvement of the quality of teaching and learning.For the Far North Region, the target population consists of pupils and teachers from three divisions focusing in zones hosting the highest numbers of enrolled children affected by the Lac Chad Basin crisis. The target population for East and Adamawa, will consist the CAR crisis affected divisions in six subdivsions. The intervention zones are the subdivisions hosting the highest numbers of enrolled refugees children Adamaoua and East regions.As for the NWSW crisis, the target population consists of pupils, teachers, other education personnel, and community members from five subdivisions in the 5 regions. The intervention zones are the subdivisions hosting the highest numbers of enrolled children from North-West and South-West regions, affected by the Anglophone crisis.
