Project Summary

Needs
According to the HNO narrative, there are 2,300 built IDPs sites that host approximately 1,723,187 IDPs. These IDP sites are mostly located on private land where IDPs are subjected to displacements such as evictions by landlords and floods. The sites are also overcrowded, inaccessible, and lack proper planning to allow for efficient emergency services.
The most vulnerable to the challenges faced by IDPs have been identified as women who head 60% of IDP households, households headed by girls, minority clans, and person’s living with disabilities or old people who have no source of income. Factors contributing to their vulnerabilities have been identifies as inadequate shelter conditions, lack of long-term security of tenure, and limited employment opportunities. These factors affect their ability to secure durable, safe, accessible, and decent shelter and settlements which exposes them to gender-based violence, increased spread of diseases especially covid-19, and flooding due to poor drainage. Additionally, a person’s living with disabilities and old people are hindered in their access to access to information and physical access to humanitarian assistance including shelter and NFI assistance.
The project will target 4,340 vulnerable IDPs households, assuming 6 people per household this is approximately 26,040 individuals (13,541 male and 12,499 female), prioritizing women and persons living with disabilities for shelter and NFI projects in the cluster locations of Banadir, Baydhaba, Belet Weyne, Afgooye, Luuq and Balet hawo. Beneficiaries are further disaggregated by their marginalization status, for instance, minority clan, elderly, or people with disabilities, in addition to gender and age disaggregation. These cluster locations were selected based on high population densities of IDP sites and SYPD's operational areas.
The project activities include:
1: Provision of shelter for 1,340HHs benefiting a total of 8,040 individuals (4,181 male; 3,859 women).
2: Provision of NFIs for 3000HHs benefiting a total of 18,000 individuals. (9,360 male, 8,640 women).
3 : Conduct Post Distribution monitoring for shelter and NFIs interventions.
4: Conduct PDM as part of accountability and to learn from the activity.
5: Develop emergency resettlement guidelines for easy resettlement in case of displacement events like floods
The project will target population age; infants (0-5 years), children (5-17years), adults (18-60 years) and elderly (60+ years).
The project will target a special population namely; disability and chronic ill persons.
The project will adopt a flexible voucher to encourage aid recipients to have a level of choice on NFIs and shelter materials. This helpfully provides a sense of more dignified assistance to populations that better meets the unique needs of every household. The project will conduct PDM every two weeks after distribution. The PDM will involve the relevance the assistance and satisfaction of the beneficiaries.
The project will conduct PCM through mobilization meetings with all the stakeholders to ensure the inclusivity of the groups and avoid exclusion of marginalized groups. SYPD will set up a feedback mechanism through setting hotline numbers for channel any complaint and or query.
the project outcome is to increase the proportion of Somalis, particularly the most vulnerable and marginalized with equitable access to and benefiting from government-led and regulated inclusive, affordable, and quality and basic social service at the different state levels by 2025.

What is the justification for using cash or vouchers instead of in kind?

The project will involve a mix of in-kind through flexible vouchers to encourage aid recipients to have a level of choice on NFI and Shelter materials. This hopefully provides a sense of more dignified assistance to populations that better meets the unique needs of targeted households. The project interventions will aim to rebuild household economy as well inject cash into local economy.

Explain how the project design considered market functionality ?

The project will conduct market assessment to ascertain the availability of supplies and its market prices. Based on the market assessment, the materials that are not available locally, SYPD will work with local vendors to procure the items.

Mitigation Measures

Risk that beneficiaries are exposed to violence during registration/distribution as a result of the distance to or location of humanitarian assistance and services. - Distributions will be conducted at site. - Security assessments will be conducted in close coordination with community leadership structures.- The projects will integrate and benefit a percentage of host community to avoid related conflicts.- Use of flexible vouchers instead of cash minimizes the risks associated with distribution of money such as theft. Risk that marginalized groups, persons with disabilities, women, boys and girls, do not participate in consultations or decision-making processes for the project or are excluded from humanitarian services/assistance/activities - The project specifically aims to include the marginalized, specifically persons with disabilities, women, boys and girls. The project deliberately will aim to involve these groups and the communities in which they live in the entire project cycle. - The beneficiary selection criteria of the project prioritizes marginalized groups. - Project staff will work closely with the community to ensure the right beneficiaries are selected for the project, including a verification process with the community. - A complaint and feedback mechanism will be set up for the project. It is essential that the mechanism is all inclusive and gives voice to the marginalized. however, it the event that the beneficiaries have difficulties in sharing feedback to SYPD. the project will conduct routine meetings with the village relief committees to gather concerns and issues related to the programming, address amicably. Risk of land disputes and tenure insecurity in targeted project area - The shelter cluster will not specifically focus on permanent shelter in the coming year. However, SYPD will follow relevant protocols and due diligence to ensure land on which IDPs are settled have minimal risks of eviction.- Stakeholder involvement including with camp managers, host community representatives, relevant government institution to ensure reduced risks related to land tenure.

Please provide further explanation

The project will collaborate with local authorities to facilitate secure land tenure to minimize eviction and movement of displaced population losing the resource investments made to the IDP. The project will also work closely with local administration and other actors to facilitate local integration. the project will assert responsibility to local administration to enhance government legitimacy in taking a lead role in supporting IDPs. District level officials will be empowered to facilitate engagement between humanitarian partners and IDPs so the latter are not exploited by the gate keepers.SYPD will advocate for support to the vulnerable population in their original location to avoid displacement to the major cities and town in the region.
