Project Summary

Excess mortality in Somalia is driven primary by malnutrition, disease outbreaks (e.g., COVID-19, AWD/cholera, measles, malaria) and violence; as well as non-communicable disease including complications of pregnancy and chronic disease. These are compounded by displacement due to conflict and natural disasters and lack of preventative and curative healthcare. Health services for host communities are scarcer in rural areas than in urban settlements. Young children, girls and women of reproductive age and persons with disabilities are most at risk of dying because of a lack of access to health services.
Relative security in Dollow, Ceel Waaq and Baardheere towns provides an opportunity to improve health services in these districts significantly. This proposal focuses explicitly on the urgent health care needs existing in Dollow, Baardheere and Ceel Waaq districts to be implemented over one year.
The project targets 61,437 (31,335 female, 30,102 male, 4,300 boys and 4,300 girls, and 1,229 elderly) beneficiaries from both host and IDP communities with healthcare services, out of which 9,215 beneficiaries will be persons with disabilities. The project will work through local committees and leadership structures, ensuring participation of persons with disabilities. Regular meetings, and information sessions will be held throughout the project period to ensure genuine participation from individuals, with specific focus on people with disabilities and consequently a high quality and inclusive response, guided by the IASC guidelines for inclusion of persons with disabilities in humanitarian action. The project will directly contribute to the Health cluster objectives of reduce excess morbidity and mortality due to disease outbreak driven by climatic shocks and conflict, contribute to improvement in the physical and mental well-being of the population affected by conflict and displacement, improve case management and referral services for survivors of sexual or gender-based violence and improve equitable access to quality emergency and essential health services.
Trocaire will work jointly with the Ministry of Health and Community Health Committees to improve staff and health facilities (static and outreach) to provide essential healthcare. This will include building the capacity of MoH team and Community Health Committees to provide these services autonomously in the near future, widening the reach by breaking geographical, infrastructural, cultural and economic barriers to access to health, investing in prevention measures through vaccination exercises and community awareness-raising, and prepositioning of supplies in anticipation of crises. The programme's intended impact is to: Contribute towards improved health, particularly for women of childbearing age and children under-five, by providing health care services at the targeted facilities in Baardheere (2 health facilities), Ceel Waaq (2 health facilities and Dollow Districts (2 health facilities). The programme builds on Trócaire's 28 years of experience operating in Gedo. The project will work closely with the Ministry of Health and the community through community structures- District Health Boards (DHBs), to provide quality healthcare services that meet MoH and Health cluster standards. The DHBs are community-based governance structures that have proven to be an effective and sustainable model in delivering healthcare services in the region. This programme complements Trócaire's existing programme providing the EPHS in Five districts (Belet Xaawo, Dollow, Garbaharey, Luuq and Burdhuubo) of Gedo.

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

Trocaire will ensure that the marginalized communities are targeted in the areas of operations by having in place an effective two-way Feedback and Response mechanism, involving communities in the targeting process especially for outreach services. Trocaire will also have in place community representatives in the project oversight and all groups will be included through the CHCs. Trocaire will provide its beneficiaries with IEC materials that will guide them on how to give feedback. Trocaire will provide a toll-free line for the communities to provide feedback.
