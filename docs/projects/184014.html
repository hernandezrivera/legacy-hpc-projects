1 - Needs

Access to birth registration is limited in most locations, placing refugee children born in Sudan at heightened risk of statelessness, also GBV risk is aggravated by inadequate lighting in camps/settlements, limited access to energy, and water supply gaps that require women and girls to travel long distances to collect water and firewood. Young men and women, boys and girls are often physically and sexually assaulted by traffickers/smugglers. Refugee survivors of GBV are treated within the traditional justice system as they have very limited access to formal justice and legal aid.

2 - Activities/Outputs

Activities of this project will include: raising awareness on general protection topics and GBV, Capacity building for service providers on general protection topics, Issuance of ID cards and birth certificates, establishing of Community-Based Structures, Do advocacy interventions for refugees. These activities will be implemented in the targeted states through Mutawinat offices that exist in the states, a work plan will be developed to follow up implementation.
