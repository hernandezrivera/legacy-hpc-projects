Project Summary


In the face of Syria's protracted crisis, now entering its 13th year and recognized as one of the gravest humanitarian disasters globally, WATAN Foundation is spearheading a critical initiative. The conflict has inflicted catastrophic damage, claiming hundreds of thousands of lives and devastating civilian and agricultural infrastructure, including homes, schools, health facilities, as well as water supply and irrigation systems. This has led to a dire situation where approximately 13.4 million people require humanitarian aid, emphasizing the urgency for intervention.
WATAN Foundation's project is meticulously designed to offer a comprehensive emergency Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene (WASH) response to both host communities and internally displaced persons (IDPs) within camps and informal settlements throughout northern Syria. This initiative not only addresses the immediate challenges associated with water supply in northwest Syria but also aims to fortify crucial life-saving interventions. By prioritizing the enhancement of long-term WASH infrastructure alongside emergency services, the Foundation is committed to significantly improving the living conditions for those affected by the crisis.
Given the enduring nature of the crisis and the dense population concentration in confined areas, the need for expanded WASH services, including waste treatment, has become increasingly apparent. Moreover, environmental contamination has emerged as a critical concern, necessitating dedicated efforts to mitigate its impact. The project targets host communities and IDPs in 30 camps and 3 communities, serving approximately 120,000 individuals.
To effectively address general and personal hygiene, as well as to ensure the functionality of water and sanitation infrastructure, WATAN Foundation has formulated a strategic plan aligned with the WASH cluster's priorities, now incorporating measures to combat environmental contamination. This multifaceted approach includes:
Enhancing access to potable water and improving living conditions through the rehabilitation and construction of infrastructure, encompassing:
Construction and rehabilitation of water systems.
Provision of water disinfectants.
Operational and maintenance support for water systems.
Establishment of water safety plans.
Support for sanitation systems, including sewage networks and wastewater treatment plants, to prevent environmental contamination.
Integration of solar solutions with constructed and rehabilitated water stations.
Wastewater treatment through the construction of wastewater treatment plants, crucial for preventing environmental contamination.
Medical and industrial waste treatment, including the installation of incinerators, coupled with advocacy and training in waste management practices to minimize environmental impact.
Promotion of hygiene through various modalities, such as vouchers or direct cash transfers, to foster environmental consciousness.
Advocacy for industrial waste treatment to address and mitigate environmental contamination.
Through these comprehensive interventions, WATAN Foundation seeks to alleviate the devastating impact of the Syrian crisis on vulnerable populations, offering sustainable solutions to the acute WASH challenges and environmental contamination issues faced by communities in northern Syria.
