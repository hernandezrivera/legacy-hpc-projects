Project Summary

Kibaruna association took responsibility towards the elderly and their families, since 2011. According to DOH in latakia city, an estimated number ( 75368 ) elderly persons are living in latakia rural and city at 2024 out of 1345855 population, which means 5.6% of habitants are above 60 years old.
The project ( Kibaruna Project for Elderly and their Caregivers) aims to provide :
1- primary health care services for elderly and their care givers.
2- psychological support services in addition to recreational. activite is for elderly and their care givers.
3- vocational training for elderly or their care givers.
4- Raising awareness for elderly and their caregivers.
targeted categories:
A- elderly with no resource of income
B- Elderly whom are providing care for children left by their seperated parents.
C- Elderlies in shelters.
D- Elderlies whom are suffering from any kind of abuse .
project location : Latakia city, (Alshameeyeh).
Target : 6600 beneficiary
Risks Analysis:
While planning it was obvious that considering potential vulnerabilities and risks is essential, first of all the project stakeholders are divided into three groups: Beneficiaries, staff And partners. Assessing their roles and responsibilities was the second step.
The third step was to identify potential risks of sexual exploitation and abuse, these risks may include:
1.Exploitation by project staff or other community members.
2.Lack of awareness or reporting mechanisms.
3. Abuse of power dynamics ( caregivers exploiting elderlies)
Mitigation Strategies:
We already have strategies and we have developed some to mitigate identified risks:
training project staff, beneficiaries and caregivers on PSEA principles, reporting mechanism.
Signing the association code of conduct by project participants.
Activating the complaint mechanism which consists of : Hotline, complaint box.
Involve community members in project design and decision-making.
Monitor project activities to prevent and address risks.
