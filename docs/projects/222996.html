Project Summary

“Unified Growth: Skills Development and Livelihoods for Cox’s Bazar Humanitarian Response” is a comprehensive one-year project aimed at enhancing the livelihoods and skills of both the host communities and Rohingya refugees in Cox’s Bazar. This project will be implemented in accordance with the Livelihood and Skills Development (LSDS) guidelines and the National Skills Framework of Bangladesh, ensuring that all activities are aligned with national standards and best practices. This project is also a part of the long-term Rohingya Response Strategic document of Oxfam in Bangladesh. The project focuses on providing non-formal technical training and skills development for women and youth, equipping them with valuable skills that can lead to sustainable income generation and improved quality of life.
Key activities include training in green skills, such as sustainable agricultural practices and eco-friendly technologies, as well as handicraft production, solar light repair and maintenance, soap making, and the creation of jute bags and other climate-friendly handicrafts. These skills not only provide immediate income opportunities but also promote environmental sustainability. To further support economic resilience, the project incorporates market linkage interventions, including e-marketing, to connect beneficiaries with broader markets and enhance their ability to sell their products. Additionally, adult literacy and learning programs will be integrated to improve overall educational outcomes and support lifelong learning, ensuring that participants have the foundational skills needed to succeed in various economic activities.
The project will also conduct a thorough analysis of demand and supply-based off-farming opportunities, focusing on the specific needs and potentials of both the Rohingya refugees and host communities in Cox’s Bazar. This analysis will help tailor the training programs to market demands, ensuring that the skills acquired are relevant and in demand. A comprehensive skills database will be developed to track employability and facilitate communication with government and non-governmental service providers, enhancing the alignment of training programs with employment opportunities. By fostering an inclusive approach that addresses the unique needs of vulnerable populations, “Unified Growth” aims to build economic resilience, promote sustainable livelihoods, and enhance the overall well-being of the communities involved.
Through these efforts, the project seeks to create a foundation for long-term growth and stability, empowering individuals with the skills and opportunities needed to thrive in a challenging environment. By integrating technical training, market linkages, and educational support, “Unified Growth” not only addresses immediate livelihood needs but also contributes to the broader goal of sustainable development in Cox’s Bazar. The project’s holistic approach ensures that both host communities and Rohingya refugees can benefit from improved economic opportunities, greater self-reliance, and enhanced social cohesion, ultimately leading to a more resilient and prosperous future for all.

In addition to skills development and capacity building, the project ensures a minimum tenure of three months of employment as skilled labor to promote the utilization of skills such as household-level soap production, handicrafts, and sanitary napkin production.By fostering an inclusive environment, the project aims to empower all community members, recognizing the valuable contributions of every individual regardless of age or ability, and promoting equal opportunities for all. 2.6 - Does your project have any DRR/DRM elements and/or environmental issues and/or climate change mitigation measures? If yes, please explain.

The “Unified Growth: Skills Development and Livelihoods for Cox’s Bazar Humanitarian Response” project integrates robust climate change action and mitigation strategies to enhance the resilience of both the host communities and Rohingya refugees. Recognizing the significant impact of climate change on food security and livelihoods, the project promotes sustainable and marketable green skills that include solar repair and maintenance, climate-friendly handicraft production and marketing, soap making and marketing with WASH sector actors within camps to promote hygiene management. This proposed project will also support host community youths to lead green skills and employment based on ILO and LSDS sector’s market assessment report analysis. By integrating these adaptation and mitigation measures, the “Unified Growth” project aims to build a sustainable and climate-resilient future for the communities in Cox’s Bazar, ensuring that they are better equipped to cope with the challenges posed by climate change. Regular training and capacity-building sessions will be conducted to raise awareness about climate change and its impacts, empowering community members specifically women and youths to adopt and advocate for sustainable practices. Through these comprehensive efforts, the project not only addresses immediate livelihood needs but also contributes to the long-term environmental sustainability and resilience of the region.

4. Development linkages
4.1 Does your project promote self-reliance and resilience for refugees? If yes, briefly describe.

The project aims to establish robust development linkages within the Rohingya camps by integrating livelihood and skills development activities that connect beneficiaries to broader economic opportunities. This includes creating market linkages for products like handicrafts, mushrooms, and poultry, ensuring these goods reach camp-level outlets and local markets per government guidelines. Partnerships with local businesses, NGOs, and government agencies will provide training and support for sustainable IGA. E-marketing strategies will help beneficiaries use digital platforms to sell their products, expanding their market reach. Adult literacy programs will ensure participants have the skills needed for economic activities. A skills database will track employability and align training with market demands. By addressing immediate and long-term needs, “Unified Growth” aims to create a sustainable economic environment, capacitated refugees to build self-reliance.
