1 - Needs

The needs addressed under this project concern the safety of humanitarian workers, which is put under additional pressure through the COVID-19 outbreak.The mitigation measures put in place against COVID-19 require costs (budgeted at USD 42,000 per year). These were not part of the original appeal but if we have to ensure that the hubs remain operational and safe, we need to put these mitigation measures in place. Furthermore, many partners reduced their footprints in the northeast. This dramatically reduced the occupancy of the hubs (from 71% to 60% for Red Roof and 51% to 37% across the field hubs). This affected the cost-recovery income of the hubs which is projected to supports significant part of the hubs’ operating costs. However, the hubs need to continue operating to support those who are left to respond to the needs of the IDPs and host communities.

2 - Activities

To mitigate the impact of COVID-19 to the safety of the humanitarian workers using the services of the hubs, all the hubs implemented mitigation measures which include regular monitoring of the body temperature of the humanitarian workers accessing the hub, installation of handwashing/sanitizing stations, adjusting the operating modalities of the hubs to ensure the observance of proper social distance and ensuring that hubs staff are wearing face masks and gloves. The mitigation measures we put in place to ensure that the hubs remain operational and safe cost money. These were not part of our operating plans and budget before COVD-19. Furthermore, COVID-19 caused partners to reduce their footprints which affected our occupancy and consequently, our income. However, since there are still partners responding to the needs of the IDPs who are using the services of the hubs, the hubs need to continue operating. Funds are required to offset the cost-recovery income we lost to ensure that we can continue to operate.
