Project Summary

Low level of access to clean water continues to be a major problem in South Sudan with more than 40% to 70% of the population in Mvolo Counties respectively drinking unsafe water. The lack of safe drinking water in conflict affected areas causes health hazard especially diarrhea for IDPS and those who are returning to their homes. Over 65% of the population has no access to sanitary means of excreta disposal. Approximately 30% of death of children under -five years is attributed to diarrhea caused by poor hygiene and unsafe drinking water.
The project aims to contribute to the progressive realization of the right of children and Women in conflict affected areas of Mambe Payam Nagyili Payam and Kozi payam, and Maridi payams, then in Mvolo Bah- el -grindi payam, Bogori, Kokori, Yeri payams to have access to safe drinking water and improved sanitation services,thereby reducing waterborne diseases and improving quality of life.
The project will focus on most needy areas and the most vulnerable groups in conflict affected areas Inhibiting by IDPs and returnees.
Strategies
This project will provide material necessary for appropriate and low cost technology water supply systems. Rehabilitation of existing water schemes will be given priority over instillation of new facilities. Priority will be given to areas where the competition over water resources may lead to conflict among water users.
To promote community management and sustainability of water supply and sanitation facilities, the project will facilitate the formation of community organizations such as Community Development or village health Committees with emphasis on gender equality and participation of youth and children.
The project will build the capacity of national counterparts and local community partners through training in planning management and participatory approaches.
The project will work closely with Ministry of water resources and ion of programme for eradication and other partners with special focus on water and sanitation.
The environmental sanitation will focus on demonstration of low cost sanitary facilities at village level and promote school sanitation and hygiene.
