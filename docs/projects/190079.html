Project Summary

According to the Multi Agency Rapid Humanitarian Access & Needs Assessment Report KOBO IDP Sites, Eastern Amhara is among the severely affected areas due to the spread of Tigrayan conflict. From July to December 2021, the entire South & North Wollo, Wag Hemra zones fall under the control of Tigrayan Forces. As a result, thousands of people were displaced, and public and private properties vandalized and/or damaged.
Livelihood in North Wollo Zone is characterized by diversity. Its economic activity is based upon agriculture and livestock. The Livelihood in the North Wollo Zone has been largely affected by the recurrent Tigray conflict expand to the neighboring Amhara & Afar region.
Since the expansion of the war to the zone more than 400,000 people have left their homes without securing their means of livelihood. The impact of the war is evident in the deterioration of means of livelihood of the rural people, infrastructure and services, lack of government resources, and consequent instability and insecurity.
Over five months war in North Wollo Zone has resulted in drastic changes in quality of life and modes of livelihood. Currently the income level of the communities in the project area is almost zero. The majority of them failed to engage in the farming system for the upcoming cropping season. This is because most households in the farm communities lost their draft animals, seed and have no money to purchase farm tools.
The agricultural sectors of the area emerging from conflict often have to contend with a multitude of challenges: destroyed crops and food stores, weakened farmers’ seed production, damaged equipment, and degraded plant and animal resources.
The agriculture sector has been severely affected by the conflict, leaving many people with limited sources of food and livelihood opportunities.
The loss of animals, seeds and money are the clear indicator of the negative effect of the war on livelihood.
The economic situation of the targeted Woreda such as Habru is strongly deteriorated due to the effects of the recurrent war and give the priority by the Food Security (Agricultural) cluster in Ethiopia.
Moreover, as the common, traditional, subsistence sources of income declined as result of war, and new sources for generating income were limited, hence most of the young people left the area in search of other opportunities to generate income. These and other problems in the targeted Woreda require the livelihood for both the displaced, non-displaced and returned migrants.
In order to enhance the resilience capacity of the community , external support to create an access for seed and farm tools is a must. The community in the intervention woreda has a land but they lack seeds and farm tools. The project so as to provide emergency sorghum & maize seeds that will be cover 7,500 hectare of land (10,000 HHs * 0.75 hectare/HHS) and 20 USD will be allocated for each HHs to purchase and distribute the farming tools.
The Purpose of the project is to contribute to improving the livelihood condition of 10,000 HHs from the IDPs & Non – Displaced in Habru, through creating an access for seeds and farm tools.
Since it is integrated farming system, the project can improve the micro climate by reducing local temperature and precipitation. This shows that the project activities are not harmful to climate and environment.
The Program will be implemented as a integrated multi-sector approaches involving the agricultural offices at all level as well as the private sector.
Within one year project intervention period, the project will target to support 10,000 House Holds. In this intervention different crops seed and farm tools will be provided.
In order to achieve the broad goal of the project Light Ethiopia needs to have 650,000 USD.

1. - Projects activities

The overall goal of the project would be to contribute towards enhancing food security status of the IDPs & Non-displaced in Habru Woreda of North Wollo Zone. This would be achieved through enhancing crop production through providing emergency seeds of different crop type and farm tools.The project will implement the following specific activities: 1. Project Launching Workshop: The Zonal level workshop will be organized to familiarize the project for all regional and local level stakeholders. The participants of the project launching workshop will be invited from agriculture, livestock, and DRM offices from the targeted Woredas, zones and regional level and from the donor agency. 2. Establishing Woreda & Kebele level targeting committees: The committees will establish the targeting committees at target Kebeles of the Woreda. Light Ethiopia technical staff will provide and facilitate an awareness creation workshop for one day in each Kebele. The established committees will have the mandate to select beneficiary HHs at each Kebele.3. Purchase and distribution of 1,125 qt of improved emergency maize seed to 5,000 vulnerable HHs. The 5000 HH are expected to cultivate 0.75 ha of land each and will be provided 22.5 kg of improved emergency maize seed.4. Purchase and distribution of 3,750 qt of improved emergency Sorghum seed to 5,000 vulnerable HHs. The 4000 HH are expected to cultivate 0.75 ha of land each and will be provided 75 kg of improved emergency wheat seed.5. Purchase and distribution of farm tool for all 10,000 vulnerable HHS. All the HHs will receive the farm tools with the cost of 20 USD for each. 6. Capacity Building training for 100 Light Ethiopia front line staffs, Woredas and zonal level government stakeholders and agricultural extension agents will be organized. During the implementation of each above mentioned activities Children, women, elderly and persons with disabilities are the most vulnerable segment of a society at times of crisis and disaster. There are critical protection issues among IDPs, non-displaced and returned migrants including child protection issues with several unaccompanied and separated children present among the IDP community. Girls and Boys of school ages reported being traumatized by physical violence and death of parents and friends during the conflict. In addition, there is an increasing number of people with specific needs including elders, women headed households. Light Ethiopia will work with the existing government and community based structures to reach the real needs of the vulnerable groups. Community participation will be crucial part of the project and women participation in all aspect of the project will be encouraged.

The project will work together with the local and national stakeholders. The main stakeholder of the project is the beneficiaries and other communities itself. They will have a role in planning, implementing, monitoring and evaluation of the program. In this level the beneficiaries at all level and the beneficiary selection committees will be actively involved through a regular joint meeting with the project implementer (Light Ethiopia). The other major stakeholders are the concerned line departments’ at regional, zonal and Woreda level. At All level, agricultural offices, Finance and Economic Development Office, the agricultural extension agent and DRM offices will be will be involved in actual implementation and monitoring of the project. The regional bureaus will closely collaborate with the head and program offices to follow up and support the implementation of the project, join in the periodical evaluation and exchange of experiences. Joint supervision with national level sector ministries such as MOA and donor agencies will be organized in a regular bases that helps to check the healthy implementation of the project and to provide the timely corrective action for any deviation from the normal. Agricultural Extension workers have a good potential of being involved in the implementation of the program through its grass root structure.
