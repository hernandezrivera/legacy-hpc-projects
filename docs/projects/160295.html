Project Summary

In response to worse food insecurity in Duk, Twic East and Bor South counties, the project is planned to address lifesaving food assistance and complementary livelihoods support to empower communities to build resiliencies. The food assistance will be provide 10 1500 HHs through cash based modalities and. 1600 HHS will support with local crop seeds through seed fair and relevant farming training and seed preservation, 1400 female headed households support with vegetable seeds and training on vegetable growing practices, 750 households will be organized in to 30 farm field schools (25 each groups) and will be support by seeds and season long training. The project will ensure durable solution and resiliency of communities to manmade and natural hazards/disasters through building capacities of customary institutions, local administration, women and youth groups. The project will ensure the sustainability of its outcome through working together with customary institution, line departments, women groups, youth groups, and local administration from beginning of the project. At the end proper handover of the project will happen. This project will contribute to objective 1 and 3 of food security and livelihoods cluster of South Sudan

1 - Needs

According to IPC Alert April –August 2019 in South Sudan 6.35 million people was under IPC 3 (Crisis) i.e. worse acute food insecurity, 1.7 million was under IPC 4 (emergency), and 10,000 people are under IPC 5 (catastrophe). It was expected that the situation will improve from September- December 2019 due to harvesting season and new harvest will be available. However the in the post-harvest period of coming year from Jan-April 2020, it is expected and project that 5.5 million people will be under crisis (IPC 3). In the August it was projected that Jonglei state will have highest number of people under crisis (IPC 3), the estimate was 1.25 million. The same report projected that from September –December 2019 in Duk County of the Jonglei state emergency (IPC4) will persist among other counties in the country. According to Counties RRC offices, in Jonglei state in Duk, Bor and Twic East counties are hosting about 29,000 IDPs and about 12,000. This related to conflict displacement and returning from refugees and IDPs. In Twic East, Bor South and Duk, the IDPs and returnees in dare need of assistance for lifesaving food. The case load becomes highly increasing due to flood case in the Duk, Bor south and Twic East counties. For example the flood has displaced communities in Payuel, Ageer and Panyang of Duk County, Jalle of areas of county, and highly affected Pakeer, Ajoung, Lith, Nyuak and Konor counties of Twic East county. There was multiagency assessment of flood disaster in Twic East that come up flood affect people 8722, however recent record is in October the people affected raised to 29,259 and multiagency assessment this October 2019 in Duk confirmed that 1870 HHs were affected by flood. The flood has caused damage of early crop growth in June, then it has affected remaining matured crops in September, it caused damage/collapse on Tukuls (grass hatched houses), damage household item, pit latrines collapse, and even loss of human life. As the major crop planted (sorghum) has been damaged at early growing stage and the resisted and survived crops damaged at harvesting point, there is an alert for food crisis as it come up with IPC alert in Duk, Twic East and part of Bor South Counties.

Contamination of targeted project areas with explosive hazards Medium.
