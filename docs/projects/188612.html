Project Summary

During the project, olive farmers in the region will be supported by helping them with production costs (pickup workers, providing the farmers with needed machines to the olive production and training courses for the farmers to raise awareness about farming methods, proper care, and necessary agricultural guidance, and providing vouchers for the farmers in the costs of the times and olive conservation requirements), thereby supporting olive production and the local market and securing employment for a large number of families through the project.

4 - Needs

The ongoing conflict led to lack of inputs and food resources, damages the manufacturing machines, increasing prices of basic supplies, and collapse the economic situation has led to depreciation of the olive price in addition to increasing the production costs (olive harvest, labor costs, trees service operations......), while the olive cultivation was the basic resource for the residents. This caused many farmers, especially smallholders, to move away from cultivation and production olive.

5 - Implementation of activities

During the project, olive farmers in the region will be supported by helping them with production costs (pickup workers, providing the farmers with needed machines to the olive production and training courses for the farmers to raise awareness about farming methods, proper care, and necessary agricultural guidance, and providing vouchers for the farmers in the costs of the times and olive conservation requirements), thereby supporting olive production and the local market and securing employment for a large number of families through the project.
