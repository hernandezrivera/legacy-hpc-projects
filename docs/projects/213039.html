Project Summary

The project aims to provide a multi-sectoral support dignified solution to 443 HH (4.647 individuals) the most vulnerable IDPs in Idleb sub-district in the following camps.
1. Hawaa Al- Janouby = 32 HH (CP CODE: CP000920)
2. Al Zeyara (Eman Billah) =288 HH (CP CODE: CP000694)
3. AlRabiee-Almaqlaa =123 HH (CP CODE: CO002158)
The areas targeted by TRC to ensure risk mitigation within the framework of the do-no-harm approach and to prevent protracted displacement will be relocated to a new centre where the shelter problem has been solved within the framework of the project. Before this process, TRC held the necessary discussions with local people in need, authorities and community leaders and obtained the necessary approvals. Additionally, HLP Due Diligence processes finalised. The Al Maqla (Idleb-Sarmada-Kefr Daryan) region, which is the project application area, does not have an HLP problem.
TRC's response plan showed innovative environmentally friendly and dignify solutions. The provision of physical safety and security for the displaced communities, while maintaining the accessibility and inclusion of IDPs and vulnerable groups, would improve measures and light humanitarian infrastructure at the IDP site. The TRC team will be led by pertinent cluster priorities and safeguarding and protection principles, including the Do No Harm approach, across all phases of the project cycle as it aspires to continue providing these services and all activities to 443 HH and 4.647 people in 3 camps. The IFRC/ICRC, SPHERE standards, and humanitarian ideals are fully embraced by TRC.
On this project TRC aims to enhance the prevention and mitigation of protection risks and respond to the protection needs by supporting a protective environment in Syria, by promoting adherence to the rule of law, International Humanitarian Law and International Human Rights Law, and through principled assistance. Additionally, TRC target to ıncrease the resilience of affected communities by improving access to livelihood opportunities and basic services, especially among the most vulnerable households and communities. By monitoring the provision of streamlined lifesaving, multi-sectoral assistance in IDP sites, TRC aim at improving the camp management quality and accountability in IDP sites. By implementing CCCM activities, TRC aspire to strengthening household and communal coping strategies in IDP sites and developing exit strategies and disseminating operational information on IDP figures and movements on a timely basis
CCCM activities focus on informing the humanitarian community on the needs of IDP Sites, tracking IDP movements and coordinating the provision of multisectoral assistance in IDP Sites. By multisectoral CCCM assistance,
It is aimed to reduce the vulnerability of 443 households, including PwDs, through the provision of safe humanitarian infrastructure. In conjunction with the CCCM governance mechanism, improve the humanitarian status of vulnerable groups while ensuring physical safety and security for displaced communities. Under CCCM activities, it is planned
to set up confidential complaints/feedback/suggestion boxes for re-located area. Additionally, 4 fire points will be installed in the camping area. Fire response training will be organized to strengthen the community and increase resilience. This project has adopted the exit strategy of supporting the individuals staying in the camp to become able to earn their own living. In this context, vocational training courses will be given to those living in the camp. Material support will be provided to selected individuals who complete vocational training courses so that they can open their own businesses. Wheelchairs and crutches will be provided to disabled individuals to ensure their access to social life and the labor market. GESI, PSEA and Safe Referral Training will be given to all personnel who will work in the project.
