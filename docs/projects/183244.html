Project Summary

This project aims to enhance protection assistance for migrant survivors of Gender-Based Violence and for vulnerable migrant and Lebanese children in need of protection, including victims of child labour, child marriage, and human trafficking. The project will aim to expand outreach to both groups by adapting a ground-up approach to recruit and build a pool of appropriately placed community volunteers, who will help to improve identification of target beneficiaries, and enable safe and sensitive referral to available services. The project will also aim to boost the capacity of local service providers, well-positioned to provide the protection services needed. These include psychosocial support, safe shelters for survivors of GBV, and legal assistance, both in cases of GBV and Child Protection (CP). CP activities will aim to meet the needs of unaccompanied and/or undocumented children, in line with their best interests. Onward referral to multisector assistance such as food, education or health support will be mainstreamed across the project.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

1: Survivors and individuals at risk access quality specialized GBV services and measures are in place to prevent and reduce GBV. Engage communities to develop outreach, context appropriate messages, and disseminate information about services. Link survivors to specialized services.Provide services via implementing partners to survivors of GBV, including case management, shelter, psychosocial support, multipurpose cash assistance, clinical management of rape, legal aid, and voluntary humanitarian return.2: Child Protection services are available and accessible to children and caregivers. Support safe identification and referral for vulnerable migrant children, including unaccompanied migrant children, undocumented children, victims of violence, exploitation, and abuse, etc.Provide case management via implementing partners to vulnerable children both migrant and Lebanese, including best interest assessment for migrant children in need of protection, alternative care services, family tracing/assessment and reunification, legal aid, PSS counselling, emergency cash assistance, liaison with health, education, and voluntary humanitarian return, as well as cross border case management and cooperation as needed. 3: Beneficiaries improve their protection through strengthened community and family support. Engaging caregivers, boys and girls in community-based protection activities. Facilitate migrant led protection initiatives in 6 different locations.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

As part of its implementation strategy, IOM will focus on promoting resilience and sustainability as well as ensuring IOM’s programming are safe, dignified, and accessible to all. This will be achieved by ensuring women’s and girls’ voices and migrant-led initiatives are promoted during all phases of the response; enhance community-led initiatives by engagement of outreach volunteers to expand awareness of, and referrals to available services; train partners and outreach volunteers on protection, GBV core concepts, PFA, humanitarian principles, risk identification and mitigation measures, data protection, PSEA, AAP and code of conduct; support evidence-based response and learning between partners using data collected through direct assistance.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

IOM’s approach toward meeting AAP standards, will be guided by a range of frameworks and tools developed by IOM HQ, and designed to see key principals put into working practice. These frameworks cover a range of themes and issues, from operating in crisis contexts, gender equality, mainstreaming protection, and humanitarian principles. IOM’s AAP approach, will see a critical attention paid to upholding several core operational principles, including do no harm, non-discrimination, zero tolerance for sexual abuse and exploitation, data protection, principled humanitarian action, and protection.
