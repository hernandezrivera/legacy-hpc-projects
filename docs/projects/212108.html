Project Summary

Muzun (MHD) presents this proposal to Support the self-reliance of affected households by providing urgent and time-sensitive emergency agriculture inputs for protecting and building productive assets and restoring or creating income-generating opportunities to save and sustain lives, and to Scaling up and sustaining minimum food and diversified consumption needs through lifesaving nutritious life-sustaining food assistance. This includes supporting the wheat value chain from production to market access, distributing free bread and cash for food to displaced families and host communities, providing cash support for lactating and pregnant women, and supporting the olive value chain.
This will be achieved by implementing FSL activities into NWS in total 4224 HH (5945 women, 5421 men, 6785 girls, and 7312 boys) taking into account the varied needs of vulnerable groups. The project activities fall under sector objectives 1 and 2 as follows: 1. Distribute monthly cash for food(CFF) distribution to 1200 households in Aleppo (300 HHs in Azaz, 300 HHs in Afrin, and 200 HHs in Atareb sub-districts) and Idleb (200 HHs in Marat Misrin, and 200 HHs in Harim sub-districts). 2. Provide a one-month top-up cash assistance to 1000 pregnant and lactating women facing extreme food insecurity through the e-voucher where each beneficiary will receive 35 $. This will help them purchase fresh food items like dairy products, vegetables, fruits, and meat from the market. 3. Purchase of 800 tons of wheat grains directly from at least 300 wheat farmers: A. 570 wheat grains will be milled to produce 456 Ton of flour (570*80/100), and 114 ton of bran (570*20/100). The products of this quantity will be divided into two sub-activities: a) 114 Ton of bran will be distributed for 570 herders (200 KG for each herder). b) 456 of flour will be baked to produce 524.4 Tons of bread (extract rate is 1.15), this quantity will be distributed to 1200 households from IDPs and HC in/out camps during 300 distribution days.
For the flour, packaged with bags capacity 50 kg. it must store in warehouse that is isolated from the production hall to avoid exposure to moisture, insects and rodents, Stacking the bags of flour to a maximum of five bags high, Keeping the floor and corners of the warehouse clean, Leaving corridors between the stacks of flour bags to allow ventilation, Putting wooden pallets with plastic insulation under the bags. For the bread: All the bread produced should be put into plastic bags made from low-density polyethylene (LDPE), the diameter of loaf 26-28 cm, each bag 12 loaves(1 kg).The bakery should be provided with tables and metal racks to carry the bags of bread in good condition. The bread packages should be put into plastic baskets (with capacity of no more than ten bread bags). for delivery to distribution points. the plasticbread baskets should be stacked at a maximum of five baskets highB. The remaining part of the purchased wheat seeds, 230 tons, will be stored, sifted, andsterilized. After the screening, 226 tons (4% impurities) of pure wheat will be distributedto 904 new wheat farmers (250KG for each Farmer), along with 150 kg of fertilizers,irrigation fuel vouchers (150 $ for each Farmer) and three technical training on GAP.4. Support 200 olive farmers )200 dunum (with an agriculture start-up kit that includes plowing cost, pest control and harvesting.
Coordination: MUZUN will coordinate the activities with FSL members and with all other FSL working groups partners (ATWG, BBCG and ACGs) through Skype groups (FSL coordination group, BBTWG and IDLEB online group) and inform them about the project intervention plans for better coordination with the other I/NGOs that are carrying out similar interventions to avoid any duplication/overlap. At the field level, Muzun through it field team will visit the target areas, and sign MOUs with local authorities, and then conduct community mobilization sessions to explain the project interventions.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Project activities: 1. Distribute monthly cash for food distribution to 1200 households:The proposed modality is an e-voucher, where each HH beneficiary will get 65 USD per round for 8 rounds. The selection criteria will be as follows:The targeted families should be living in a specific area, community, or camp.Families that have a large number of children and either have PWDs or elderly members.Families with no regular income or limited income will be preferred.Targeted families should not be from contracted vendors for vouchers.2. Distribution top-up cash assistance to 1000 pregnant and lactating women from the same selected families though contracted with the suppliers.3. Bread distribution for 1200 families where each family will receive free bread for 300 consecutive days.The bread distribution will be based on minimum survival needs (250 grams/person/day) assuming that the family size is four. Accordingly, Muzun identified the following criteria to distribute the bread to the affected families: The bread distribution will be based on several modalities: 1/2 Bundle for families or individuals consisting of 1-2 members, 1 bundle for families consisting of 3-4 members, 2 bundles for families consisting of 5-7 members, 3 bundles for families consisting of 8-10 members, and 4 bundles for families consisting of 10 members or above.4. Support 200 olive farmers with an agriculture start-up kit that includes plowing cost, pest control and harvesting. Activity: A breakdown of this will be implemented as follows:Collaborate with local agricultural authorities or community leaders to identify 200 olive farmers who are in need of support.Procure and distribute agriculture start-up kits to each farmer. Distribute Cashe vouchers worth $50 to each farmer after the harvesting season to cover plowing costs. Provide guidance and support to farmers on pest control methods and harvesting techniques to ensure optimal yields.Provision of the pest control component will include 1/2 liter of insecticide and 1/2 liter of fungicide for each dunum of land. This will help farmers protect their olive crops from pests and diseases, ensuring better yields and overall crop health.To support harvesting, Muzun will implement a Cash-for-Work (CFW) activity targeting olive farmers during the harvest period. Throughout out: Recruit 150 CFW workers from the host community and IDPs based on specific criteria, with a focus on female workers, to promote gender equality and empowerment.The CFW workers will receive $7 per day for 40 days of work. (One donum needs 30 employees per day.) This compensation will help provide income for the workers and support their livelihoods.
