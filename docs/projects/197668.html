Project Summary

Through this project, GOALPrime will improve the life of 100,000 conflict-affected individuals (30% female, 20% male, 30% girls & 20% boys) through intensification of the WASH response to sustained safe and quality water, clean latrines, and sanitation facilities across 14 LGAs in BAY States. 50,000 individuals (60% women/girls & 40% men/boys), including 30,000 household heads will benefit from hygiene promotion via both door-to-door and 100,000 individuals will benefit from the mass motorized speaker campaign for hygiene promotion sensitization approach. 20,000 vulnerable women in camps and adolescent girls in schools and camps will be sensitized on menstrual hygiene management, promotion, and education after which they will receive reusable sanitary pads (RSP) and laundry soap for daily maintenance of the RSP. Key messages will include prevention and control of COVID-19, Cholera, Measles, Lassa fevers and messages on PSEA, gender, and disabled inclusion. GOALPrime will also distribute 20,000 hygiene kits and PPEs from the WASH common pipeline to 30,000 household heads. 5% of the total target will be people living with disabilities.
Handwashing has been established as a component to achieve proper hand hygiene and a range of health and development Objectives. Through this project, GOALPrime will established 200 handwashing facilities which will be positioned across all the latrines provide and in some other strategic locations across the camps and host community, and would be managed maintain by the WASHCOM team. Constant water supply to the handwashing stations with a mild chlorine content at 0.5% chlorine to ensure decontamination of jams and other hands transmitted diseases .
Also, through this project, GOALPrime will rehabilitate 100 boreholes to provide safe and clean water to 150,000 individuals and continue to maintain rehabilitated boreholes via the services of community local artisans which would be trained by the GOALPrime boreholes engineering team who will continue overseeing the O&M activities of the community based local artisan and WASHCOM. This approach is to ensure that there is no disruption of water supply across the target LGAs.
This project will support the Water Point Chlorination (WPC) and free Residual Chlorine (FRC) in line with best practices and standards as recommended by the Water Technical Group (WTG) through an engaged community water point chlorinator. An online chlorination method will be majorly used by using an automated Dosatron on the rehabilitated boreholes, 100 dosatrons would be installed and calibrated according to the sector standard.
Through this project, GOALPrime Water Testing Lab will continue the water quality testing and monitoring of the chemical parameter and bacteriological contamination of water samples from the target LGAs and will also support partners from other LGAs referred by the WASH sector to assure continuous quality and safety of the water accessed by beneficiaries across LGAs of implementation.

1 - Needs

According to the WASH Sector gap analysis, the well-being and living standards of an estimated 3.03 million people are adversely affected by shortages of clean water, and poor sanitation and hygiene practices in 2022. The WASH Sector’s latest PiN and HRP 2022, shown that 828,056 people are in need of WASH services in the 14 selected LGAs by this project with 52,518 in fufore, 47,273 in Madagali, 36,214 in Yola North, 73,092 in Yola South (Adamawa State), 18,619 in Kala Balge, 11,8970 in Konduga, 64,804 in Mafa, 248,617 in Maiduguri (Borno State) and 28,528 in Bade, 51,453 in Geidam, 57,427 in Gujba, 30,541 in Jakusko (Yobe State). 1,736,819 people have limited access to a latrine, as per sector standard and 2,074,319 persons are in need of hygiene kits and over 3 million people are in need of continued Hygiene promotion across the BAY state, This has continued to make a significant contribution to the increase of the conflict's psychological and physical impact on the population. The conflict in north-eastern Nigeria, now approaching its 13th years and has damaged the economy, wrecked houses and other infrastructure, spread insecurity, caused millions of people to lose their livelihoods, peace and dignity and restricted their freedom of movement. it is 8.4 million people currently require urgent humanitarian aid as a result of the deteriorating humanitarian situation that worsened. Among them, 6.1 million live in host communities and IDP camps. [HNO 2022]. There have been cases of AWD resulting from a lack of adequate safe water supply and poor hygiene practices in more than 30 LGAs in the BAY states which includes fufore, Madagali, Numan, Yola south, yola north (Adamawa State), Kala Balge, Konduga, Mafa, Maiduguri (Borno State), and Bade, Geidam, Gujba, Gulani, Jakusko (Yobe State), On August 29, the Adamawa State Government (ASG) formally declared an emergency due to an increase in probable cholera cases in the state. The Ministry of Health then turned on the state's incident management systems and requested assistance from partners. Yola South, Madagali, Fofure, and Numan LGAs are the most severely affected by previous cholera epidemics, these LGAs are still in danger of an outbreak. [ASG Press Statement]

2 - Activities

Activity 1. Map out borehole gaps in target LGAs in coordination with community stakeholders and other complementary service providers.Through this activity, GOALPrime will determine which borehole if rehabilitate would serve people around and know the damage level of each of the boreholes planned to repair. Activity 2. Rehabilitate and upgrade selected non-functional boreholes in target locationsThrough this activity, GOALPrime will Rehabilitate non-functional borehole to provide clean and safe water to the affected population per sector standard. Water stress is a serious issue in many parts of the LGAs selected for this project, Pressure on water resources is increasing in several of the camps and communities. Water scarcity generates sanitation problems by forcing people to drink unsafe water and lead to cholera diseases. Activity 3. Carry out water point chlorination and Free Residual Chlorination (FRC) Monitoring.Through this activity, GOALPrime will carry out chlorination of the boreholes rehabilitated by this project including government own boreholes and monitor the FRC level in the water systems to ensure beneficiaries use safe and clean water across all the borehole. Automated chlorine pumps will also be mount for an online chlorination where the technology will be automatically injecting the chlorine mother stock solution into a large body of raw water and allow to observe contact time then open for beneficiaries to fetch. Activity 4. Identify, coach, and engage community-based borehole operators (CBBO), Water points chlorinator (WPC), Community Hygiene Promoters, and Local Area Mechanics (LAM) for the continuous operation of the boreholes, chlorination, and hygiene promotion sensitizations activities across all the project targets.Through this activity, these are community based staff (CBS) which will be responsible for carrying out Door to Door Hygiene Promotion (D2DHP), Borehole operation for daily opening and closure of the water facilities and systems troubleshooting to report to the Local Area Mechanics (LAM) that have been trained by the GOALPrime Borehole Engineers, which will be carrying out minor Operation and Maintenance (O&M) of the water system and GOALPrime will provide them with Borehole Operation and Maintenance repairing tools and borehole spare parts to ensure uninterrupted water supply across all the targeted locations. Activity 5. Construction of reticulation lines in the camps, schools, health facilities and communitiesThrough this activity, GOALPrime will construct water reticulation networking from the rehabilitated borehole a good yield to other locations where members of the community cover longer distance before accessing for water. GOALPrime would ensure sector standard on waiting time and distance to covering before accessing water. Schools with with few water fetching point will be consider to during this project implementation and schools with no borehole or damage will be construct or rehabilitate to make the learns and student more comfort to learning and be free from sanitation challenges. Activity 6. Rehabilitation, Dislodgement and upgrade of non-functional and filled-up latrines in the locations targeted by this project in the camps, schools, health facilities and child friendly spaces.Through this activity, GOALPrime will provide sanitation facilities according to sectors standard to ensure the people in need live with dignity and to ensure reduction of Open Defecation. Through the CLTs triggering under this activity GOALPrime will coordinate with other existing partners in the location to End Open Defecation (ODF) across all the locations. Activity 7. Distribute Hygiene kits to the affected people who will be received Door to Door Hygiene Promotion Sensitization. 8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

By integrating every strategy with the protection principles, GOALPrime will mainstream protection (GBV & PSEA) throughout the execution of this project and advance meaningful access, safety, and dignity of both beneficiaries and of employers and volunteers. Safety, decency, and avoiding any kind of harm to both beneficiaries and employees are priorities for GOALPrime. Additionally, GOALPrime will also make sure that the target population has equitable access to the service, free from impediments and prejudice, in accordance with their needs. GOALPrime will pay attention to people and groups who could be especially vulnerable or have a hard time obtaining help and resources, including those with disabilities and GBV survivors who have been identified. GOALPrime will integrate GBV Risk Mitigation efforts to reduce or decrease GBV risk to beneficiaries. GOALPrime will work consciously in compliance with the Sphere Humanitarian Core Standards and Imperatives. All approaches will align with the principles of protection as we remain committed to ensuring that this project does not expose beneficiaries to more risk in compliance with the DO NO HARM Protection Principle. Also, GOALPrime will be intentional about maintaining an environment where there is zero Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (SEA). All staff, volunteers, contractors, and vendors engaged directly or indirectly to implement this project will be given PSEA orientation. They will be familiarized with GOALPrime's policy on PSEA, and they MUST express their unwavering commitment by signing GOALPrime's Zero Tolerance to SEA Code of Conduct. Also, GOALPrime will also lay down reporting protocols for the reporting of PSEA cases if unfortunately, they occur. GOALPrime will also ensure that the identity of the reporter(s) is kept very confidential. When convenient, GOALPrime will encourage that reports are made on anonymous grounds. Also, being guided by the Inter-Agency mechanism for reporting SEA, GOALPrime will ensure a systematized engagement with affected populations including confidential complaints and investigations procedures at both the system and individual agency levels. As recommended by IASC Task Force on PSEA which identified inter-agency cooperation in the creation and maintenance of community-based complaints mechanisms (CBCMs) as a key component in the prevention and response to SEA. GOALPrime will continue to raise awareness to the target population that humanitarian assistance is free and never conditioned on sexual favors as well as to be informed on how to access the appropriate complaints mechanisms, and support to set up in the community if SEA occurs. Furthermore, GOALPrime will ensure that established CBCMs in target locations are effective and that there is inter-agency coordination to ensure consistent messaging and that access to the mechanism is as broad and straightforward as possible for potential complainants. GOALPrime will also be intentional about the environment and safety of the communities where we implement by ensuring that our approaches and tools does not contribute to the degradation of the environment. In addition, GOALPrime will also integrate climate change and provide routine sensitization of the project beneficiaries on climate change prevention.

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

During this project, monitoring visits to project locations will be conducted on monthly basis to supervise project progress, quarterly FGDs will be conducted in intervention communities to get feedback, and the groups involved in the FGDs will include male, female, young, old and people with disabilities. GOALPrime will collect feedback from these processes will be analyzed and used for learning. Routine internal spot checks will be conducted to ensure the project is on track and reviews will also be organized during the project duration. Post Distribution Monitoring would also be conducted for each distribution carried out in this project. Vulnerable communities feedback will be used to understand how to serve them better on every months. GOALPrime will create beneficiaries communication and feedback mechanisms which will be opened 24/7 for queries and complaints across all the locations.
