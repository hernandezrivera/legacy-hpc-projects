Project Summary

Despite the situation described above, the Drought Relief Committee Report (2019) indicates that there is no organisation supporting Lean Season Assistance (LSA) in the identified districts, Government only covers 300 households per ward in terms of food security initiatives only covery a third of the communities in question according to the Famine Early Warning Systems Network. More specifically this is that is a total of 9,922 HHs out of a possible 20,000 HHs in Lupane and 8,512 HHs in Insiza against 23, 333 HHs. Therefore, World Vision proposes an 8-months relief and recovery program (this includes 1 month of start-up, 6 months of activities implementation and 1 month of close-out,) for 12,920 HHs (64,598 individuals) to respond to the food insecurity of households in IPC 3 and 4 in three emergency declared provinces of Matebeleland North and South. The project will particularly focus on three districts with Lupane in Matebeleland North and Insiza and Umzingwane in Matebeleland South. The beneficiary targets will specifically be Lupane 26 581, Insiza 20 335, and Umzingwane 18 716 beneficiaries. The districts are in agro-ecological region IV which receives low and erratic rainfall between 450-600 mm where 70% of the population is food insecure and the figure is increasing. These IPC 3 & 4 districts will be targeted from February 2019 to September 2020. The project aimed at covering the most severe food and nutrition needs during this lean season when hunger is at the peak. The project will also allow early recovery during the critical rainy period when farmers should engage in agricultural activities. The project will prioritize the hard to reach and most vulnerable rural areas, with poor harvest forecasts and vulnerability to drought shocks. The proposed activities include: Providing Unconditional Title II In-Kind Food Transfer, Seed Distributions, Climate smart agriculture, Pre- and Post-Harvest Management Training, Farmer Managed Natural Resources (FMNR). The project will ensure gender and disability issues are considered through trainings on Child Safe Guarding issues and Gender awareness trainings.

1 - Activities

Activity 1 - Provide Unconditional Title II In-Kind Food Transfer to 16,150 Households: WV will provide unconditional food transfer to 100% of selected vulnerable HHs in 3 districts. Food transfers will be provided on a monthly basis for a period of 6 months starting from March to August 2020 (6 total transfers). The proposed start date is aiming to cover the most severe food and nutrition needs during the lean season when hunger will be highest and allow early recovery during the critical rainy and planting period. Activity 2 - Seed Distributions: World Vision proposes to provide a package of horticultural and main field cropping seeds (5 kg millet, 5 kg, sorghum, 5 kg cowpeas, 20g butternut, 20g pumpkin, 20 rape, 20g tomato, 20g onion) to the targeted 12,920 HHs for crop production and vegetable gardening. Households will receive seeds for drought tolerant and nutritious small grains and legumes in addition to seeds for nutritious horticultural crops, which will mainly be used as relish for consumption together with the small grain cereals. Distributed seeds will ensure that men, women and youth in every household targeted by the project have access to adequate seeds for planting during the 2019/20 farming season. The targeted districts are in agro-ecological region IV where most HHs have access to land. Harvests from planted crops will contribute towards increased food security and nutrition for targeted households.Activity 3 - Climate smart agriculture, Pre- and Post-Harvest Management Training:All households will be trained on climate smart agricultural practices with a focus on crop rotation, mulching, minimum tillage, Integrated Pest Management, crop rotation and the selection of drought tolerant seed cultivars. In an effort to minimize losses during harvest, households will also receive training in improved post-harvest practices and technologies (processing, handling and storage). Activity 4 - Farmer Managed Natural Resources (FMNR)All targeted households will be trained on better methods of natural resources management. Agro forest management with support from forest commission and Agricultural extension workers. Trainings will also focus on soil and water conservations techniques, watershed management, and disaster risk reduction among others. All 12,920 HHs will be targeted for the trainings and the training modules will be covered in a 30-hour course. All trainings will be coordinated by the Agriculture and Livelihoods Advisor and facilitated by the field-based Government extension workers to enable the continuous monitoring of farm practices and the sustainability of the project intervention. Provide monthly food baskets through either in-kind or cash-based transfers to acutely food insecure population in rural areas.Provide vegetable seed packs and tools for household, community and school nutrition gardens.- Provide drought-tolerant seeds and fertilisers.- Provide training, extension and advisory services to extension officers and farmers focusing on good agriculture practices, climate-smart agriculture. crop pest management, post-harvest handling, storage and processing.Beneficiary Targeting. Beneficiaries will be identified through a community participatory process that allows them to rank and select those who are the most food insecure using food security indicators refined by the community, overlaid with the following broad categories and thereafter ranked by order of vulnerability:• Chronically food insecure households with priority to female headed households• Food insecure households with pregnant and lactating women• Poor households worst affected by poor crop harvests with low or no cash income • Poor households worst affected by livestock losses with low or no cash income• Poor households where head and/or spouse’s major livelihood activity is classified as negative coping mechanism WV will use participatory beneficiary selection from household demographic data using LMMS databas.
