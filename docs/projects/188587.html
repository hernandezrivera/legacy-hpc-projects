Project Summary

Access to clean water and adequate sanitation facilities is among the most urgent needs, especially for children in schools. Psychological scars resulting from the war, and the lack of educational materials and qualified schools. These factors put children at an increased risk of dropping out. The massive influx of displaced children into schools in these areas combined with inadequate water and sanitation with suboptimal hygiene practices puts children’s health at risk.
Without adequate safe water for drinking and personal hygiene, it is difficult to maintain good health and fight disease. Without proper sanitation, water supplies can be contaminated and diseases can spread rapidly. This prevents parents from sending their children to schools, thus preventing them from continuing the educational process.
The project aims to contribute to securing a healthy educational environment for children in schools by meeting the needs of establishing and rehabilitating water, sanitation, and hygiene gaps, taking into account priority interventions within the framework of allocating toilets for males and females, taking into account the category of people with special needs and previous rehabilitation, including the required numbers and category age. The project also provides clean toilets for boys and girls secured with doors and windows, in addition to securing safe drinking water by establishing a sufficient number of fountains in proportion to the numbers in each school, which reduces the spread of infectious diseases and raises the level of hygiene awareness to promote good practices.
Each school will be provided with water tanks usable water throughout the semester. In addition to finding long-term sustainable solutions by drilling a well inside each school, in addition to the proposed project's focus on modernizing existing water systems through the establishment of solar-powered water systems.
Children also get bars of soap, lice shampoo, and a small sanitary bag, which ensures that parents are encouraged to enroll their children in school as well as protects the children’s welfare, all of which provides an environment conducive to further education, an increase in their education rate and improved school attendance, thus ensuring a healthier and better-educated generation.
In addition to the above, the Derna team will place awareness posters against COVID-19 in schools, bathrooms, and children's gathering places, in addition to making some murals to raise awareness of hygiene and health next to the water bars and bathrooms in each targeted school.

4 - Needs

After nine years of a continuous military attack on civilians and infrastructure, and despite the stability of the security situation in the eastern region for nearly 4 years, the situation in the eastern countryside of Deir Ezzor is still unsafe due to repeated attacks on an area of ​​ISIS remnants, especially with the recent escalation For the military operation during 2022, not to mention the movement of internally displaced persons issued by OCHA, which is estimated at more than 79% of whom are women and children. Our monastery conducted a baseline assessment of WASH needs in January 2022 in more than 20 schools in the region that were selected in consultation with the WASH Cluster, and the main results of this assessment showed that the per capita share of water is less than 3 liters/day during a number of hours studying. Without observing global Sphere standards, access to clean, sanitary, and gender-separated toilets is also very limited, with most students refraining from entering them due to unsanitary conditions and overcrowding, causing most of them to drop out of school. Many of them are forced to defecate in the open due to their lack of hygiene, not to mention the risk of sewage accumulation, creating an unhealthy environment. The situation has worsened further as the spread of COVID19 continues

5 - Implementation of activities

Deirna aims to phase out all of the emergency activities related to destroyed water supply and sanitation in schools in addition to restoring and renovating bathrooms and providing them with the necessary technical equipment, installing doors, windows, toilet stone, and ceramic floors, taking into account privacy for both sexes and the vulnerable group of people with special needs. While maintaining the water supply for the targeted schools through the method of trucking water during transitional periods to sustainable solutions(boreholes), installing the necessary tanks for each school in proportion to the number of students, and conducting an inspection of the area that enables us to boreholes to supply the school with the necessary water.In addition to providing them with solar panels, constructing drinking water points and fountains in proportion to the number of students, conducting hygiene awareness campaigns to promote physical hygiene and awareness against COVID-19, distributing physical health care kits, drawing murals, and hanging awareness posters. Each school is provided with a set of hygiene kits (cart, shovel, broom, etc.) and garbage containers of different sizes.14 schools are targeted in the eastern countryside of Deir EzzorIn each of the villages of Jdeed Akidat, Jdeed Baggara, Dahleh, and Sobha
