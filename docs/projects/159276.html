Project Summary

This project will give attention to dire humanitarian needs in living conditions of crisis-affected communities in services in Jowhar and Buurhakaba district of Middle Shabelle and Bay Regions through sustainable access to water, hygiene, and sanitation. The activities will improve current WASH gaps to reduce incidents of AWD cholera spread, morbidity and mortality among emergency affected communities and vulnerable populations with a high burden of active Agro-pastoral/Cholera outbreaks. The proposed project will target 18000 families including elder men, Diasbalities, children and women in emergencies to be provided adequate safe water, sanitation facilities, and hygiene promotion services. WASH services of good hygiene and sanitation practices will be promoted in the affected communities by providing adequate WASH facilities such as construction of 350 new communal latrines (200 in Jowhar and 150 in Burhakaba) with handwashing facilities, provision of WASH packages to vulnerable affected communities, provision of clean drinking water and establishing water system through rehabilitation of 25 Shallow wells (15 in Jowhar and 10 in Burhaba) the shallow well will have with hand pumps installed. SCC will rehabilitate 5 water catchments in Burhakaba in hot spot villages where there has been water scarcity for the last 3 years. SCC will Train and recruit 25 (10 Community Hygiene Promoters in Burhakaba and 15 in Jowhar) to promote hygiene and change the behavior of the beneficiaries through hygiene education. This will be done in close collaboration with the local authorities, community leaders and the Ministry of Water and Energy of Southwest and Hirshabelle States of Somalia. The project beneficiaries will receive hygiene kits distribution including soap, water treatment tablets, and 20L jerrycans. The project aims to reach 18000 people comprising 5580 Male, 5940 Females, 3060 Boys and 3420 Girls.

Project Activities

Construct 350 ( 200 in Jowhar, 150 in Burhakaba ) new latrines for the vulnerable communities in the district benefiting 18,000 people. Rehabilitate 5 water catchments and 10 shallow wells in Burhakaba district. SCC will also rehabilitate 15 shallow wells with hand pumps in Jowhar which project proposes hygiene education through trained community hygiene promoters. Recruit and train 10 Community Hygiene Promoters of Burhakaba and 15 in Jowhar to promote hygiene and change the behavior of the beneficiaries through hygiene education. 175 Water Management Committees members to be selected 5 members each of 25 wells. Project beneficiaries will also receive Hygiene and sanitation kits including soap, chlorination of wells, 20 litter jerrycans and water treatment tablets. The project ensures that the activities are implemented in line with the project's strategic objectives and HNO 2020. The project will target 18000 beneficiaries ( 10544 Jowhar, 7456 Burhakaba) districts of middle shabelle and Bay regions respectively.

Mitigation Measures

2a. Exclusion and marginalization: The project will ensure that no one is excluded who is eligible for the project intervention based on disability, marginalization and stigmatization. It guarantees this through the coordination of the relevant stakeholders and identifying those who happen to socially excluded and marginalized. The project engages and consults with the local authorities and relevant line ministries in the identification and selection of the beneficiaries so that all stakeholders are aware of the beneficiary selection process and will identify the vulnerable groups including the disabled and marginalized groups. Project staff is also made aware of this issue so that they know how to include all vulnerable groups in the selection process. 3a. External stakeholder interference in beneficiary selection: 4b) The project will ensure external stakeholders do not influence the beneficiary selection process and are not involved in the targeting and selection of the beneficiaries. The project sensitizes all stakeholders of the project about the project objectives and implementation plan and the transparency of the beneficiary selection. The local authorities are also engaged in the sensitization process since authorities often interfere in beneficiary selection.
