Project Summary

This project is seeking to increase access to critical dignity and menstrual hygiene services of needs of adolescent girls and women in MMC, Konduga and Bama LGAs, northeast Nigeria in Borno state. The project will work to evolve and manage a unique community facilitated strategy to the distribution of dignity kits for vulnerable women and girls. Specifically, the project will include a local structure for sourcing and distribution of the GBV sector approved standard dignity kits that meet the needs of adolescent girls and women in the project communities. Life at Best Development Initiative (LABI) will identify and train women and girls in production of some key items and will also be involved in the assembly process. Items that cannot be produced will be sourced locally to further contribute to the local economy.
The core focus of the project will be to meet the immediate, lifesaving dignity needs of women and girls of reproductive age by providing a standardized kit/package. By using a localized production and sourcing approach, the project will ensure that to the greatest extent possible local procurement will be prioritised LABI will periodically update this assessment to ensure quality control of locally sourced materials. Also, by prioritizing local production and procurement, the project will serve as income-generating activity for women and girls affected by conflict. Women and girls will be trained in production of some key items and will also be involved in the assembly process.
A core component of the project will be utilisation of GBV sector established referral pathway for the distribution of the dignity kits. This will go a long way in bridging the gap of non-availability of essential commodities at women and girl safe spaces thereby improving service delivery and quality of life of vulnerable women and girl accessing the safe spaces in the project locations.
The project will also implement a robust community led sensitisation and education component that will seek to address information gaps and influence behaviour change with respect to gender-based violence in communities. Any cases of GBV requiring case management of further service provision will be referred to the existing partner safe spaces in the project location hence the implementation of a two way referral for the project.
LABI will work closely with the GBV sub sector and other GBV actors in the project locations to ensure a well-coordinated approach in the sourcing, assembly, distribution and post distribution monitoring, to avoid duplication of services, ensure the most vulnerable are prioritised, set and uphold quality standards. Also to support the localization of humanitarian response agenda, LABI will employ 1 skilled GBV coordinator who will be support the GBV sub sector for in conducting location specific GBV service mapping, periodic gap analysis, development and strengthen referral pathways and directories, organize field coordination meetings, capacity building for field actors, periodic monitoring on behalf of the GBV SS, coordinate activities key technical working groups among others.

1 - Needs

Following continuous acts of violence by Boko Haram and the new counter insurgency operations of the Nigeria Military in form of the 'super camps' which has seen the Nigerian security forces close their smaller bases to for bigger camps, there has been an increase in displacement of persons from troubled areas into areas of relative safety with military presence. LABI and other actors such as IOM tracking of these revealed that women and children constitute 87% of the new displacements. The critical needs for dignity, including in menstrual hygiene management remain largely unmet for women and girls of reproductive age.In preparing this Response, LABI conducted a rapid assessment to further validate some of the secondary information that it had gathered from engagement with other actors and this further point to the overwhelming need. The rapid assessment sought among other things to also try to establish the availability and accessibility of basic hygiene materials to women and girls. Key findings from the assessment further validated the fact that there was huge gap in the supply and accessibility of certain dignity materials. For menstrual hygiene management, many of the women mentioned that they use pieces from torn clothes for their menstruation, others use pads. Key concerns identified ranged from the lack of dignity, menstrual and famine hygiene items available to women and girls of reproductive age. Other concerns were also related to privacy and disposal.

2 - Activities

In the course of project implementation, the following activities will be carried out: • Establish a production/demonstration centre for making quality re-usable pads and dignity kit packaging bags• Engage women and girls of reproductive age to work at the dignity kit production centre• Procure/source for other items/contents of the standardized dignity kits with priority sourced from women groups that are already organised in production of items like soap, before going to the open market• Establish dignity kit assembly process that involves hence employing and empowering vulnerable women and adolescent girls• Utilise existing GBV referral pathway in the distribution of dignity kits intervention• Identify and recruit 1 consultant to support on quality control, training on kit production and provide technical support for roll out• Conduct periodic post distribution Monitoring• Conduct Monthly mass community sensitization activities in project communities• Conduct on-going house to house behaviour change messaging in the project communities• Conduct training on core GBV concept for project volunteers and key opinion leaders• Provide access to quality lifesaving and well-coordinated GBV response services for survivors and individuals at risk• Enhanced wellbeing among survivors/vulnerable individuals through survivor centered provision and GBV risk mitigation efforts into humanitarian response efforts• Strengthened community resilience and system/institutions that prevent and respond to gender based violence harmful practices
