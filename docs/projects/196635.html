Project Summary

the project is emergency response .The planned activities should enable internally displaced persons in informal camps in the Idlib region to survive in dignity and live saving . By providing 13800 drinking water trucking for 5500 IDPs in 8 informal camps , 3300 desludging truck for 5500 IDPs in 8 informal camps , 600 solid waste trailer will removal from 8 informal camps for 5500 IDPs , hygiene promotion and 5500 hygiene kits will be distributed for 1100 families in 5 rounds . The construction of 40 gender-sensitive and safe latrines should ensure better living conditions. Solar-powered lighting to latrines road should offer more protection to the target group, especially women, the elderly, people with disabilities and children.

3 - Needs

Access to WASH services is compromised by prolonged decay of the existing systems, . This is exacerbated by the cascade effect of climate change, economic downturn putting 13.55 million people in need of WASH assistance, a 2.6 per cent increase over 2022. An already vulnerable population is now facing further downturn, with almost half a million more people in acute need. with The 2022 outbreak of cholera in Syria.Access to safe water, adequate sanitation, solid waste management and/or hygiene supplies will remain a challenge for more than 2 million people seeking shelter in IDP Sites.They are highly dependent on continued humanitarian assistance, with 95 per cent of interviewed residents of IDP sites having received some kind of WASH humanitarian assistance. Water trucking services are the only source of water for 73 per cent IDP sites residents . 43 per cent of IDPs in sites could not access one or multiple hygiene items, and 32 per cent faced barriers to effective handwashing.WASH needs will continue to be high across 2023, with a large proportion of people in acute need due to situation deterioration owing to, but not limited to: Public health crisis, with high prevalence of waterborne and vector borne diseases Increased pressure on the existing WASH systems Worsening economic conditions , drought impact on livelihood, and economic effect of Ukraine crisis, Programming implications for possible non-renewal of UN Cross-Border Resolution after January 2023
