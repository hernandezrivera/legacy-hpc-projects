Project Summary

The protracted crisis in the North East of Nigeria has caused unprecedented consequences on men, women, boys and girls across sectors. The continued insurgency has disrupted livelihoods, broken-down basic infrastructures and services. In addition, sporadic attacks and military operations have forcefully displaced population from their homes to safer places in search of food assistance, nutrition, protection and WASH services; of this number, women and girls are the most vulnerable.
According to the DTM, in Borno, 745,397 displaced individuals are residing in 237 camp and camp-like settings (camps, collective settings and transitional centers) and 1,345,817 are residing in 701 sites in host communities. 56 percent of the IDP population is children (under 18 years of age). However, an estimated 930,000 people within this category are in areas that are hard to reach by humanitarian organizations and have therefore not yet been reached with aid. Congested populations in displacement sites, notably in 15 LGAs in Borno State, water-borne diseases notable acute watery diarrhea and cholera outbreaks have been reported over the years. In 2018, there were 12, 276 Cholera cases with 197 associated deaths in 35 LGAs in 3 states. A total of 1.5 million people were considered to be at risk and required cholera prevention interventions measures. In 2018, flooding, fierce winds and sand storms caused substantial damage to WASH infrastructures which affected at least 20 per cent of IDPs living in camps in Borno state and 28 per cent of IDPs living in camps in Adamawa states respectively. New arrivals from hard-to-reach areas, especially women and children, are also in urgent need of WASH support. They include at least 100,000 children under five.
It is to be recalled that, the insurgency has destroyed schools and water services, thus leading to increasing number of drop-out from schools of adolescent girls and boys. The congestion in existing school facilities in targeted LGAs is a driver of opened defecation, water-borne diseases and poor hygiene practices.
This program shall therefore deliver sustained access to WASH services to vulnerable returnees and host communities in targeted LGAs in Borno state.

2 - Activities

Act1: Sustaining access to 15 L of safe drinking water per person per day for 7000 returnees and host populations as per standard through water trucking in Adamawa and Borno Act 2 sustaining access to long-term water facilities to 30,000 returnees and host communities as per standard through new construction /rehabilitation in targeted LGAs of Adamawa and Borno. Act3 provision of safe drinking water to 20,000 persons through operation and maintenance .Act4 providing sustained access to sex-segregated sanitation(100 latrines for 5,000 persons as per 1:50),50 showers,50 hand-washing points) with pictorial images, light, locks inside and messages written in local languages to reduce cases of G.B.V reported at sanitation side and enable safe access to vulnerable IDPs with intellectual impairment ) for 15,000 returnees and host population in Adamawa and Borno State through new construction/ rehabilitation. Act5 providing safe access to sanitation (latrines) to 5,000 returnees in camps through dislodging as per standard. Act6 supporting 2,000 crisis-affected persons in host communities with access to household-type latrines through provision of materials ( planks, nails, zinc). Act7 Sustaining access to waste management services for 5,000 returnees as per standards through distribution of waste bins, dust-bins in selected LGAs of Adamawa and Borno State. Act8 capacity building of WASHCOM on operation and maintenance of water and sanitation facilities through distribution of water and sanitation kits ( aqua-tabs, head tanks, boreholes, brooms, detergents, rakes..). Act9 disseminating community-informed hygiene key messages to 15,000 returnees and host communities promoting change of hygiene attitude, behaviors in order to prevent cholera outbreak and water related diseases. Act 10 Distribution of menstrual hygiene materials and MHM education to 6,000 women and girls. Act11 distribution of NFI (hygiene kits) including top-ups to 15,000 vulnerable crisis affected persons in selected LGAs in Adamawa and Borno state. Act12 conducting water testing and PDM on water quantity per person as per standard through installation of meters on water facilities.
