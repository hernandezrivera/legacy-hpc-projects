Project Summary

"This project is intended to support HHs with Shelter and NFI support to capicate them by ownerdriven approach, tranparency, complain and feedback mechnism, participation ( AAP) including training of volunteers who will be trained with safety PSEA and Medair code of conduct, utilize resources from the community as already worked previously in the same camp. Shelter support consists of providing emergency shelter items to affected HHs for any hazard such as fire, strong winds, rain, landslide etc. Activities will include: assessing, distributing tokens, providing labor support and construction support (for HHS in home care and HHs with elderly members). Normal HHs will be provided with a token (equivalent to a voucher) that will grant them access to the distribution; HH with elderly people and home care will be provided with shelter items delivered to their doorstepnd also contruction support will be provided , but also with labor support. The emergency items will be prepositioned in an accessible place to disburse in need.
NFI will be delivered to the vulnerable HH with elderly people and HHs in Home care, while isolation packs will be provided to people who need to be isolated due to Covid-19."

Needs

"For the upcoming monsoon and Covid 19 restriction it is difficult for the people to mitigate their risk and fill their needs. The need of Shelter emergency support is increasing and also the potential need of NFI package is emerging gradually. For repair, maintenance of existing structures people cannot be out to collect it from distribution centre because of contamination risk. So, the assistance needs to reach their doorsteps which is porter support can be extended to construction support.Also, for the ones in home care and HHs in home care require NFI support. So, one-time NFI package also need to reach to their doorstep with porter. The NFI package (consist with blankets, floor mats, mosquito nets, solar lights etc.) will assure their dignity and separation to the people with symptoms to other family member.For NFI and Shelter immidiate support an emergency stockpile of materials need to be prepositioned. "
