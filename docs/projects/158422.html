Project Summary

Cyclone Idai has damaged / severely damaged 84 between classrooms and administrative units in 19 primary schools of Gorongosa district. Currently, 12,399 students and 174 professors have limited access to proper school infrastructure and educational materials. The district, in the last few years, has been severely affected by the conflict as well as by moderate to severe climate-related events – a situation that has been negatively influencing children’ school attendance (parents do not see the advantage of sending children to schools that are not proper, whereas a renovated school is considered the “community pride”).
This situation is also expected to have an impact on the number of early marriages in the District – both as a coping strategy for vulnerable families that might receive some food for their daughters’ “lobolo” and because education, in this condition, is perceived has providing no opportunities for the future of the children.
The project aims at ensuring school access and availability of adequate educational materials for 12.573 boys, girls and teachers (Girls: 6,073; Boys: 6,326) in Gorongosa district. As results 84 classrooms and administrative units will be rehabilitated and refurbished and, at the same time, 19 schools, 12,399 students and 174 professors will be provided with adequate educational material.
