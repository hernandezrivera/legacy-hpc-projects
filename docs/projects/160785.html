Project Summary

This project is twelve months emergency lean season food security and livelihood support intervention with promotion of appropriate small scale resilience activities amongst 15000000HH high food insecure households in Yirol West Yirol East and awiel West of Lake state and NBgs the immediate objectives is to support vulnerable households with vegetable seeds, fishing kits and tools so as to enable them to have access to food through their own production .the long term objective is to protect vulnerable households from shocks that will affect their ability to access food. The objectives are in line with HRP strategic objectives 2, (SO2) and are in support of FSL cluster objective 2.
To deliver the require results, the key program strategy is to through direct distribution of vegetable seeds and fishing kits. In term of seasonality this is in appropriate activities within these two areas programmatically. To ensure accountability to affected population, a committee would be formed to address issues that will address that will not affect the beneficiaries as a result of implementation of the project. The project will target mainly vulnerable, IDPS, Returnees, Host communities that missed the main cropping season. a total of 20,000 households will be supported from Yirol West, Yirol east and Awiel West Counties . The main drivers to the disaster are drought, community conflict. The project will promote inter cluster strategy with WFP food Asset beneficiaries and farmers affected by the fall army worm. SSGID is a very strong and active partner of the food security and livelihood cluster at both national level and state level and have participated in several assessment missions in Awiel, Yirol west and warrap EState. It has also implemented food security and livelihood projects in Warrap , Yirol West and Awiel West
SSGID has historical experience working with FAO ELRP activities in greater Warrap, Awiel west and yirol west , they have been with FAO since 2013
SSGID is currently implementing food security and livelihood project support by in Yirol West
SSGID has a solid experience in term of context and active presence in its operational areas and relevance at implementing FAO Similar projects.

1 - Needs

Food security situation continues to deteriorate due to conflict-driven displacement, low crop production, economic crisis, climatic shocks and humanitarian access challenges. In 2019, the Humanitarian crises continued in South Sudan despite the signing of the revitalize peace agreement in august, and it continues to be defined by widespread food security concern and devastating major food gaps in the targeted areas is limited access to food and people limited capacity to produce food which is the result of the prolong conflict or due to economic hardships that is hitting the country. The most recent IPC report (may 2019 clearly indicates that more than half of the population is facing hunger and starvation with an estimated 6.9 million people959%) of the total population faced crises (IPC phase 3 or worse acute food insecurity 3 at the peak of the lean season (July- Aug) of whom 47000 were in catastrophe (IPC 5) and 1.7 million were in emergency (IPC4) the severity of the food insecurity is mainly driven by the on-going conflict which have affected production , led to loss of livelihood , hyperinflation and soaring market prices, disruption of market and trade routes, destroyed infrastructures, there is also some disasters that have affected peoples access to food such as flooding , the incident of fall arm worm During Rural Women for Development base line survey conducted in Wau which is attached the specific needs identified includes, hand tools like hoes , assorted vegetable and crops seeds, fishing gears, training of fishing groups in fish Preservation and in improved production, Post-harvest management, preparation and Utilization of Vegetables are the immediate needs identified to re-build the livelihoods of IDP’s returnees and host communities. However, the affected community has existing strange such as access to farm land, availability of labour and water for irrigation for vegetable production, access to market that they can local buy some of food items , existence of local skills like black smith to provide the community with local produced tools like meloda. According baseline assessment, the leading challenges faced in farming are; shortage of farm tools, shortage of seeds, pest/disease, prolonged drought, lack of skills on vegetable production, veterinary services and lack of pest sides among others.

9.14 - How will you project ensure feedback from the most vulnerable community members?

The selected beneficiaries were identified through a participatory process through community needs assessment. The need assessment was done during implementation of the other projects in the area. During the need assessment the community need to be supported with vegetable seeds and fishing kits were established. In same assessment the needs of boys, girls were considered hence the inclusion of them as direct targets. Accountability to affected committee will be formed at boma levels in each payam to ensure timely flow of information. The AAP Committee will be the main body in ensuring that the beneficiaries are fairly treated in a transparent manner and will ensure proper accountability to the affected people that are suffering. as a result this it is expected for the AAP Committee members to be actively involved in beneficiary selection as that is in line with the project document and falls within the project context. The AAP committee will further ensure that beneficiaries will be at risk in receiving the humanitarian assistant, therefore, the committee will advise SSGID of any potential risk that will affect the beneficiaries as a result of the implementation. To ensure that people need are address and protection issues such as disabled , vulnerable households disable, the AAP committee will ensure that these people are identified and given priority to receive the service.On distribution days, there will be several issues arising such as certain beneficiaries not able to come because of illness, old age, some might even misplace their cards. The AAP committee will ensure that these problems are addressed during the distribution days. In order to achieve these functions SSGID will identify and established AAP committee, these Committee members will be trained on their roles and responsibilities
