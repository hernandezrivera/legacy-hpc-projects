1 - Needs

Increased access to quality, innovative, inclusive, holistic education for crisis-affected girls and boys (including adolescents). The Outputs are 1. Increased access to alternative learning for children affected by conflict 2. Increased, sustainable access to formal education for children affected by conflict The implementation is to enroll children who are not currently enrolled in school through activities such as:Supporting educator stipends in selected alternative education facilities, with a focus on supporting high proportions of female instructors.Supporting vocational education and training for adolescents, with a focus on ensuring additional support and inclusion of girls.Supporting the scale-up of innovative, localised approaches to early childhood education in target areas. Developing accountability and incentivisation mechanisms, as well addressing teacher motivation issues, in collaboration with relevant government authorities (L.G.E.A)Undertaking a variety of awareness raising, promoting child protection and GBV, advocacy, and back-to-school campaigns in targeted communities, seeking to improve rates of enrolment and attendance for all

2 - Activities

Existing learning environment are child-friendly, gender-responsive, equitable, disability-friendly and inclusive. The Outputs 1. Supported facilities are inclusive, child friendly, disability, friendly and gender-sensitive 2. Key teaching, education management, and partner staff have improved capacities to deliver child friendly, equitable, and inclusive education programming 3. Proportion of female teachers in alternative and formal education is increased. The outcome focusses on improving the inclusivity, equitability and protectiveness of supported learning environments and can be implemented through: Creating and implementing teacher training modules and materials, focussing on inclusion, disability, gender, equity, conflict-sensitive education age-appropriateness and other relevant issues. Training of local authorities and education leaders on the same set of issues. Supporting the creation of effective monitoring systems and deployed with a focus on ensuring the establishment and operation of disability, gender, and child-friendly learning environments. Creating a teacher mentorship and peer support programme, focussing on teachers’ own MHPSS and wellbeing needs, as well as peer support toward creating more disability, gender, and child-friendly learning environments.Creating a teacher recruitment and retention strategy, as well as deploying a national recruitment campaign, in collaboration with local authorities, focussing on attracting and retaining strong teachers to formal school, with a special focus on improving the representation of women and teachers with disabilities.
