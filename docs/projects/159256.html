Project Summary


The project objective is to “enhance protection and respect of rights of most vulnerable women>18-59 and >59 years and girls 14-17 years including GBV survivors with disabilities, elderly women and women with cancer (WWC) and increased their access to digital coordinated multisectoral specialized responses based on IHL and IHRL. The main intervention related to outcome 1 as fellow ( provide 500 psychological consultations and 6-8 sessions at least to women based on the manual prepared and credited from GBV SC members in a participatory approach and their children based on the cases severity via hotline of wefaq hotline besides remote modalities if its Zoom ,WebEx or what up applications according to beneficiaries choice of communication which keep privacy and safety for them or in wefaq HQ if the case needed that ; legal consultations to 500 women and girls, and court representation to 160 women and married girls especially those survivors of GBV represented in courts,: Distribution of 200 dignity kits according to GBV SC standards and based on vulnerability selection criteria , Organize 2 videos as a community meeting with community to inaugurate the project to take in account the preventive measures of COVID-19 then share it on CBOs websites and social media ,Refer (30) traumatized women and girls (3 WWDs)>18-59 and >59 years and 18 girls (1 GWD)<18 years to a psychiatrist and coordination with MHPSS department in Ministry of Health for sever cases if they need home visits , provide psychosocial support session for 300 affected children (150 girls /150 boys) of victims and survivors of GBV women who participated in the psychological sessions, Provide emergency safe motherhood health care services for (500) women (24 WWDs, 3 WWBC)>18 years and (200) girls (12 GWDs) <18 years wefaq refer to UWHC health center in Rafah and khanyonis governorate in the south of the Strip ,WEFAQ provide a medical referral for 300 needed children to have medical services to UHWC ; Refer 6 cases of GBV survivors in need of sheltering to hayat center , Provide 40 child visitation services monthly for (divorced couples) with coordination with high counsil of sharia courts which entails biweekly visits to the WEFAQ credited family center; distribution of50 $ for 100 unconditional cash assistance to most vulnerable cases and share it with Ministry of social development and cash cluster working group to avoid duplication; case management services to 100 women from all Gaza strip. Outcome 2 activities: periodically meeting with the PC GBV SC, CPC, organize 4 Focus group to measure the quality of services and engage the community in the project; training on PSEA for project staff; Elaboration of a fact sheet documenting learned lesson to treat GBV survivors in coordination with CC,4 Discussion meetings about received complaints even from emails, hard template or via hotline, 4 initiatives prepared by WEFAQ`s women led protection and responsive trained committee . WEFAQ ensure complementary of the project with previous and ongoing projects, advances participatory monitoring and reporting plan and MEAL plan, adopts accountability to Affected Populations, realizes 2b gender marker and neutral environmental marker, ensures protection mainstreaming, guarantees safety and security of staff, and asserts safe access to the project to cover all the strip. The project will cover the southern areas of the Gaza strip.( al manarah ,al salam areas in khanyonis .( kherba adas ,seyamat ,oraiba in RAFAH Governorate

1 - Needs

* HNO 2020 -HRP2020-2021 -Response plan of covid-19 HNO (2020 ) stated that the factors and causes of humanitarian needs in Gaza included blockade, hostilities, violations of IHL and IHRL relating to COVID-19, internal Palestinian divide 18 years and 295,913children <18 years at the end of 2017. Women and girls from families affected by casualties due to COVID-19 were at high risk of GBV particularly in the south of GAZA STRIP in Rafah and Khan- Younis. wefaq conducted Rapid need assessment which findings indicate that COVID-19 increase the percentage of violence in the south of GAZA STRIP.worsened the status of GBV and that the level of GBV increased noticeably throughoutCOVID-19 pandemic.Remote-Modalities Tools in GBV in the lockdown was effective and staff of MHPSS services available Whenever required, hotline reports and case managers inform us that GBV survivors have duplicated kinds of violence and they refer them to complementary services offered by hospitals, shelters or the police, as well as referrals according to specialist organizations for further help. Whenever possible, GBV service partners in line coordination with WEFAQ COVID-19 EMERGENCY PLAN which managed successfully during this pandemic. To illustrate wefaq increase the team of remote modalities as a result of increasing load. Recently, in 2017, UN Women published a document regarding GBV services in Gaza Gender alert.Their investigation showed clearly that the hardship and poverty exacerbate the GBVin Gaza Strip. Their study also highlights the contributing factors of GBV in Gaza Stripincluding perpetrators rarely facing legal penalties; violence against women beingsocially acceptable within marriage and a prevailing culture of silence to preservemarriages at the expense of victim's rights. Their examination recognized the need forformal training in GBV sensitization, capacity building and the support of incomegeneration support for GBV survivors [R3]. In 2017, oPt Humanitarian Fund (HF)funded projects tackling GBV in Gaza Strip. These projects supported at least 12,800people, 77 % of whom were women, mainly those heading households, disabled,internally displaced and other survivors of GBV.The reality of disability in Palestine during the Corona pandemicAccording to the 2017 statistics from the Palestinian Central Bureau of Statistics (PCBS)1released in July2020, the number of persons with disabilities was 255,228, constitute 5.8% of the total population inPalestine; 127,266 in the West Bank 5.1% and 127,962 in the Gaza Strip 6.8%.The disability rate among males was 6.3% and 5.4% among females, and mobility difficulties are the mosta common type of difficulties, which reached 32.2% with a prevalence of 2.9% among the population.In this context, persons with disabilities in Palestine, particularly in the Gaza Strip, face a number ofchallenges in acquiring basic human rights, obtaining basic services such as health, safe access to servicesand information, and poor access for persons with disabilities in the public and the governmental servicescentres.The most demanding issue is that RAFAH IS THE ONLY GOVERNORATE WITHOUT QUALIFIED HOSPITAL and few numbers of GBV actors .

9 - Protection mainstreaming
9.1 - Do No Harm

we focus on prevention and minimize as much as possible any unintended negative effects of our intervention which can increase people’s vulnerability to both physical and psychosocial risks. To illustrate, continuous coordination with beneficiaries in delivering any kind of services. we design our regard to COVID-19 related risk mitigation measures refer to measures in place to mitigate risks to women and children while accessing services for example (dignity kits will distribute and deliver to homes, medical treatment daily coordination with heath centre (UHWC) to keep organized and avoid crowded in delivering services, 10 participants in any training in the projects, individual psychological session ) key persons to hygiene all face to face participants who need any clearance about any activity, a coding system with beneficiaries to keep privacy and security when we contact them .only trained staff on the manual of remote modalities which updated by APS and GBV SC.

9.2 - Equal and impartial access to aid

NO ONE LEAVING BEHIND is one of the principles which take into consideration. firstly, we cross-check the nominated targets of in-kind assistance with MOSD.
