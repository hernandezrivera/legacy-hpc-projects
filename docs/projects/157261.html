Project Summary

The project will support emergency food security and strengthen livelihoods of extremely vulnerable HHs targeting newly IDPs and severely conflict affected communities in Talkok, Kassala and Geissan, Blue Nile States.
The proposed activities will support 4000 HHs, approximately (9360 men, 9840 women, 2640 girls, and 2160 boys) through different interventions that aim to meet the immediate food security needs, diversification and improvement of livelihood opportunities, and collaborate with local actors to systemically respond to humanitarian needs. Key interventions include:
i) provision of agricultural inputs, accompanied by related agricultural extension activities;
ii) provision of veterinary services, including construction of water structures for animals;
iii) livelihoods diversification and income generation through goat restocking, off-farm income generating activities, Village Saving and Loans (VSLAs) and business training.
iv) Community capacity building interventions.

Needs

By October 2018, Sudan devaluated its currency to 47.5 SDG/USD, though by early December it had depreciated to 70 SGD/USD on the parallel market. The continued shortage of hard currency and the rapid depreciation of the SDG have significantly reduced the private sector and government’s ability to import essential raw materials, including fuel. Consequently, the ongoing harvesting period is also being negatively impacted by low fuel supply and high prices, particularly in central and eastern semi-mechanized areas. Additionally, the high price of agricultural labor and the fact that farmers have prioritized sesame and groundnuts production is expected to result in delayed harvesting and some crop losses of sorghum and millet.According to SUDAN Food Security Outlook (December 2018 to May 2019), Food security improvements during the November to February harvesting period will be less significant than in typical years as many poor households obtain some or most of their food needs from market purchases even during the harvesting period. Between March and May 2019, food security will deteriorate as poor households begin to deplete household food stocks and become even more dependent on markets. Many areas will be Stressed (IPC Phase 2), while much of North Darfur, Jebel Marra, Red Sea, and parts of West Kordofan, North Kordofan, South Kordofan, Blue Nile, Kassala, will be in Crisis (IPC Phase 3).IPC (valid from April to July 2018), reported that, almost of 108778 persons in Telkok locality, 30% are classified in phase 3 and of 43411 persons in Geissan locality, 33% are classified in phase 3 and in need for support to cope the acute food insecurity.Telkok locality is considered with a high percentage of asset poor households, with 38 percent of the households in the poorest quintile and 42 percent in the second quintile. Approximately one fourth of households in Geissan were unable to sustain acceptable levels of food consumption, More than 30 percent of households in Geissan were unable to afford the local food basket.

Activities

1. Provide seeds & tools (on a recovery bases) and extension services in both targeted localities, benefiting approximately 9000 individuals. 2. Provide 50 family drip irrigation tool kits to 100 households women (450 women, 90 Girls, and 60 Boys). Drip irrigation is an effective tool to enhance water consumption. It is ideal for the camp conditions & small-scale agricultural production in rural areas with water shortage or limited supply. The system will maximize the water utilization efficiency through the drip distribution.3. Providing livestock vaccination and treatment through vaccination campaigns and drug revolving fund to serve 20000 livestock belong to 2000 IDPs (50% will be women owned livestock). 4. Working with 25 Community animal health workers (CHAWs) on prevention and control of animal diseases outbreaks and improved animal health and production.5. Support diversified livelihood through extensive Business Development Services (BDS) to 2900 VSL members and through IGAs for 100 female headed households internally displaced. All women will benefit from the BDS that will be provided in the village saving and loans groups.6. Establish 100 saving and loans association (VSLAs) to 3000 women. 7. Construct 2 water points in the animal migratory routes. The activity will benefit 12000 livestock belong to 2000 households.
