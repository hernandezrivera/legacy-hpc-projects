Project Summary

The Cyclone Idai caused massive loss of life, casualties, destruction of houses and property. The flood waters swept across the communities causing more destruction. The communities are at risk of communicable diseases which dramatically increased due to stagnant flood water as well as over-crowding in the collective centres where displaced people are now staying. There are serious concerns regarding the risk of malaria and cholera outbreaks in the days, weeks and months ahead. Children, lactating and pregnant women are at high risks of communicable diseases. Therefore, the affected communities need access to health care services within their localities.
Objective: Increased access and quality of health & nutrition services, disease prevention and psychosocial support to the most vulnerable children and women
Activities
• Community sensitization via health campaigns
• Make referral of those that need specialize care from community health facilities to higher levels of care
• Staff Capacity building on
o Infant and young child feeding in emergency (IYCF-E) including MUAC screening.
o Mental Health and Psychosocial Support including PFA
o Management of acute malnutrition at the community level
•Establish WAYCS with breastfeeding corner and appropriate WASH facilities
• Set up women peer support groups and psychosocial support activities
• Health and WASH educational activities & training in WAYCS
• Sensitization on child development for caretakers
• Referral for MHPSS, antenatal care and malnutrition services/interventions
• Distribution of baby kits & dignity kits
• Community mobilization via health educational sessions for family members Health Household visit by midwives & community volunteers
•Screening of young children on severe and acute malnutrition.
•Sensitization on infant and young child feeding, immunization, child care practices, cooking demonstration etc
• Use of LLIN and Malaria prevention, cholera prevention and family planning
