Project Summary

This project has been designed to meet the most critical need of the host community, IDP’s, refugees and returnees in Afmadhow district lower juba, Somalia. The proposed intervention will increase access to safe, quality water, sanitation and hygiene services.

Project Activities

Activity 1.1. Rehabilitation of two strategic boreholes which will improve access to safe water supply (targeting a total of 10,000 beneficiaries, specifically 5,000 beneficiaries for each boreholeActivity 1.2. Rehabilitation of five (5) shallow wells with solar water pumping system (each serving at least 1,000 beneficiaries)Activity 1.3. Purchase and installation of water tanks Activity 1.4. Installation of water reticulation systems at nine public primary schools Activity 1.5. Establishment and training of water management committees for 2 boreholes and 5 shallow wells with 40% female representationActivity 2.1. Construction of 4 twin latrines hand washing facilities in 4 public primary schoolsActivity 2.3. Rehabilitation of existing latrines in 5 other primary schools in Afmadow districtActivity 2.4. Establish child-to-child (C-to-C) clubs and community Education Committees (CEC) in 4 public primary schools and build their capacity on operation and maintenance of the WASH facilities Activity 2.5. Rehabilitation of 5 institutional latrines (3 in Dhobley general hospital and 2 in Afmadow general hospital) installed with hand washing facilitiesActivity 2.6. Install water reticulation system to provide water to the 4 latrines, 2 delivery rooms and 4 hand washing facilities.Activity 2.7. Construction of 50 family shared latrines with hand washing facilities in Dhobley (20 latrines) and Afmadow IDPs (30 latrines)Activity 2.8. Rehabilitation of 20 existing shared communal latrines fitted with hand washing facilities were affected by flash flood in 2018 Activity 3.1. Support 960 vulnerable households from IDP camps and communities in Afmadow and Dhobley satellite villages with temporally access to safe water through water trucking or vouchers for 2 months Activity 3.2. Production of water trucking vouchers Activity 3.3. Support 850 vulnerable households from IDP camps and communities with hygiene kits including menstrual hygiene kits (targeting 500 with hygiene kits and 300 women including 200 school girls supported with MHM kits)Activity 3.4. Conduct hygiene promotion training in 4 schools, and IDP camps Activity 3.5. Procurement of IEC materials and banners Activity 3.6. Purchase of NFI kits for 200 householdsActivity 3.7. Support chlorination/disinfection of 7 water sources for 5 months (2 boreholes in Dhobley and 5 shallow wells in Afmadow)
