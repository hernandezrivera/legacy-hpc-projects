Project Summary

Economic livelihoods across Syria have been severely impacted by the crisis. This impact is particularly apparent in the agriculture and livestock sector. Increases in input and production costs and disruptions in value chains, coupled with reduced availability of power and water resources are all factors leading to decreases in production levels, poor availability of locally produced foods, rising sector-related unemployment, and placing added strain on already weak social structures and communities in general.
Taking into consideration the above trends, the Communities to Market (C2M) project will contribute to strengthened livelihoods of crisis-affected populations and improved social cohesion among local communities through the promotion/adoption of socially inclusive business models. The project will aim to create 680 durable, sustainable, and green direct and indirect jobs in the dairy value chain through the creation of two pilot community production clusters in Hama and Rural Damascus as well as two pilot co-operative and business governance structures in the same locations. Using environmentally-friendly technologies which are designed to work off-grid, the community production clusters will integrate all economic activities related to livestock development, dairy productions, cheese production, high protein value bars, and other economic activities within a closed-loop model. Direct job creation will be managed and governed through the co-operatives responsible for ensuring that employees benefit from quality income opportunities, social safety nets such as health insurance, and skills enhancement. Vocational training will also be provided to direct beneficiaries in the areas of system operation, quality management, product development, and life skills training. Indirect jobs will be supported across the dairy value chain including the areas of food retailers, transportation, logistics, etc. The project will prioritize job creation for women and persons with disabilities in rural areas given the high levels of vulnerability this segment of the community faces in accessing resources and income-generating opportunities.

5 - Implementation of activities

To increase access to direct and indirect jobs for rural-based HHs, focusing on women and PwDs, 2 pilot community dairy production clusters will be established. The clusters will integrate economic activities in livestock development, dairy and cheese production, high protein value bars within a closed-loop integrated model. Outputs of the cluster will include cream cheese, protein bars and high-quality meat products for domestic and international markets. Cream cheese will be packaged and sold to local retail markets and adapted based on local tastes. High nutrition protein bars will be directed to ongoing nutrition programs. 70% of net profits will be used to cover the direct and indirect costs of the clusters. 30% will be distributed across health insurance, training/capacity development and community investment fund used to upgrade community assets. A community-level business entity with a cooperative structure and governance framework will be established in target areas to ensure decent jobs are secured and health insurance is available. Vocational training will be given to direct beneficiaries in areas of system operation, quality management, product development and life skills. Process and outcome monitoring activities include baseline and end-line assessments of the target groups, case studies, ongoing feedback with project beneficiaries, post-project monitoring, household surveys and Focus Group Discussions with project stakeholders.

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

Breakdown of social cohesion within the district due to increased sectarianism. Likelihood: Low. Impact: Medium. Through CBOs, AKF will focus on the inclusion of stakeholders, and promote project benefits in local communities. •Female workers may be subject to discrimination due to negative cultural perceptions of work types. Likelihood: Medium. Impact: Low. CBOs will raise awareness of work types to identify vulnerable individuals willing to take such work. Confidential monitoring and compliant reporting system will identify and investigate exploitation and take actions against them. •Gender-related norms may limit the ability of women to participate in some types of work. Likelihood: Medium. Impact: Medium. Women workers will be asked preference of specific work/task to ensure their comfort. AKF-Syr will monitor women working environment, activate complaint reporting system, and facilitate gender equality awareness-raising to promote positive interaction with female members. •Community representatives/Cooperatives’ Board of Trustees accused of bias in selecting beneficiaries, Likelihood: High, Impact: Medium. AKF will manage the selection process and ensure a precis implementation based on the selection criteria. •GBV and job associated risk. Likelihood: Medium, Impact: Medium. Workers will be given protective equipment. AKF will maintain close monitoring of the working environment and safe and easy access minimizing GBV risk.


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Risk 1: There is a risk that the project activity may lead to safeguarding issues and might be exposed to sexual abuse or social stigma. Risk 2: The project activities time and locations might be unsafe for the vulnerable groups especially for women. In line with AKF safeguarding policy, manual and standards, the project PSEA strategy will ensure the following mitigation measures are in place: • Train all project staff on safeguarding policies. • Ensure all involved partners adhere to AKF's Safeguarding Policy and Code of Conduct • Develop gender-responsive referral pathways and ensure project beneficiaries and vulnerable groups are aware of safeguarding policies and can access reporting mechanisms. • All data collection will consciously abide by robust ethical standards, guided by AKF’s Codes of Conduct and Safeguarding Policies. • Conduct an assessment of safety aspects in spaces used for activities implementation as well as the surrounding environment. The project will adopt measures and mechanisms to protect beneficiaries and hold superiors and subordinates to account alike. AKF follows specific processes and procedures to integrate safeguarding in the Project Cycle which are: • Determine expected safeguarding risk using (Risk Register tool) • Determine safeguarding measures to reduce the risks through (mitigation plan and indicators). • Evaluating safeguarding at the project Cycle before, during and after implementing the project, using Project Cycle Checklist.


11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

-Reduced productivity of cow’s milk and meat due to the spread of pathogens. Mitigation: Cows will be fed organic feedstock produced by the organic feedstock production unit. Healthy conditions will be ensured and well-trained veterinaries will be contracted.-Lack of adherence on the part of MOSAL to deliver the required permits can result in late or protracted approval processes for the legalization of community co-operatives. Mitigation: AKF contacted MOSAL and got official approval to establish a cooperative.-The transfer of funds to implementing unit may take too long due to the Syrian sanctions and change in banking procedures. Mitigation: AKF and AKF’s head office in Geneva monitor the time transfers take and bridge financial resources to ensure minimal disruption.-Exchange rate fluctuations may affect the level of funds available at the field level. Mitigation: AKF monitors the exchange rate to consider the impact on the project.-Limited availability of quality goods, services, equipment and difficulties procuring items and delivery to Salamieh. Mitigation: AKDN is experienced in rural livelihoods programming and will seek to source from local suppliers and expand procurement tendering offers to Governorates when needed.-Deterioration of the security situation limiting movement of staff and beneficiaries. Mitigation: AKF monitors regional and local security situations and will delay the activities implementation if the situation is not safe.
