Project Summary

ADRA proposes a direct food distribution project providing a complementary basket on a monthly basis for 6 months to 1680 individuals or 300 households (average family size 5.6). This is an increase of 300 households to the current project CEV2. The idea is to reach more people to move them out of critical food insecurity phase. The modality of this project is direct food distribution in San Felix, a small city located in the North-East side of the priority state of Bolivar, the location of Food for Hope (CEV 2). The food will be distributed in the communities of 25 de Marzo (11 de Abril parish), Vista al Sol (Vista al Sol parish), Francisca Duarte (Chirica Vieja parish), and Dalla Costa (Dalla Costa parish) in the priority state of Bolívar.
ADRA proposes to provide a food basket of 2100 Kcal per person (WFP foodbasket) which cost about US$250 per people per month (detail prices). This food basket is out of reach of our beneficaries.

4.2. ¿El proyecto establece estructuras / mecanismos accesibles, confiables y confidenciales para recibir comentarios y quejas de la comunidad durante la implementación, y utilizar esa información para adaptar el proyecto?

4.2.1. Provea brevemente información al respecto - máximo 1.000 carácteres

At the moment, ADRA has a phone number where beneficiaries can call, and always has a feedback box in a visible place at distribution events. In both cases, the mechanisms guarantee anonymity but requires a certain level of literacy. In order to ensure all participants, regardless of literacy level is fully able to participate in reporting, ADRA Venezuela utilizes field officers, and volunteers as points of contact for complaints in weekly visits. Volunteers engaged in phone contact and home visits, as well as ADRA staff are all trained to report all feedback to ADRA program coordinators within 48 hours. Distributions are also important points of contact where project participants can choose (male or female) staff they would like to engage. All these options and mechanisms are first discussed and put into place prior to project start-up by the community advisory group and then communicated at the beginning and throughout the project life (as reminders) to project participants.

If the complaint is not made in-person, the staff member have to report immediately to the Security Director without revealing the identity of the complainant.
