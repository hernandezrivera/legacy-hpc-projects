Project Summary

The project will take place in two different geographical areas in term of the political situation; North West Syria NWS, where the access to this area is mainly from Turkey as it is controlled by Turkish-supported armed groups and HTS, and North East Syria NES, where the access is from Iraq - Kurdistan, and is controleed by Syrian Democratic Forces SDF. Bahar has an office in Erbil and Erbil-based staff can officially cross the border from Kurdistan to NES.
The model of the intervention will be the same across the two different areas, and will be consisted of CMAM and IYCF activities. Bahar has health and stabilization centers situated in the both areas, and will be utilized to complement the nutrition intervention.
NWS) Afrin: 3 PHCs in Afrin, Ma'abatli and Al Basuta, communities,1 SC in Afrin city center.
NES) Ar Raqqa: 4 PHCs, in Ar Raqqa city center, Khneiz(SC), Abbarah, and Tal Alsamen communities.
Some of the nutrition services will be delivered through the facilities where the main services will be IYCF and IMCI.
Target population:
- Afrin: U5: 49000, PLW: 5000
- Ar Raqqa: U5: 22910, PLW: 10507

Needs

4.7 Million girls and boys under the age of five years and pregnant and lactating women are at risk of undernutrition and in need of comprehensive curative and preventive nutrition services to continue during 2019. Around 92,000 children under the age of five years are expected to suffer from acute malnutrition during 2019, out of which 19,000 girls and boys are at risk of death due to severe malnutrition if not treated appropriately. Without appropriate care, acute malnourished children are eleven times more likely to die than well-nourished children. 865,295 girls and boys under the age of five years suffer from micronutrient deficiencies. It is estimated that 1.6 Million mothers will require maternal nutrition services and skilled support for optimal infant feeding and caring practices. Global Acute Malnutrition among boys and girls under the age of 5 years remains within acceptable international benchmarks in most of the assessed areas during 2018. However, there are some pockets where SMART surveys, rapid assessments, and nutrition surveillance information show an increase in chronic malnutrition rates such as Eastern Ghouta (36%) and Tel Abyed (32%) which exceed the very high stunting rate classification according to WHO 2018 thresholds. Chronic malnutrition was a problem in Syria even before the crisis and increased rates have been observed recently. Women and children living in hard-to-reach areas, overburdened communities, and those ‘one the move’ deprived of basic services are in urgent need of nutrition interventions to ensure that their nutritional status remains stable and improves. Considering aggravating factors such as ongoing displacement, anticipated returns, change in control, challenging access to nutrition/health services and food, high food prices, diminishing employment opportunities and low service coverage of nutrition partners, are forecasting an increase in undernutrition cases especially in hard-to-reach areas, where the severity of the situation is considered 'critical'.

Implementation of activities

Nutrition activities including, CMAM, IYCF, micronutrients distribution, SAM and MAM management, and capacity building. The activities will be implemented in an integration manner with WaSH, protection, and health.Bahar will implement integrated CMAM program included all pillars (community mobilization, TSFP, OTP, Inpatient care (SC)and IYCF program for people in need providing outreach services in both NWS (Afrin), and NES (Ar-Raqqa).Nutritionists and CHWs will provide services at the fixed health facilities and at the community level, visit homes and camps and will provide the following activities :1. MUAC screening for acute malnutrition of all children (6-59 months of age) and PLW.2. Referring identified malnourished cases to receive the treatment.3. Providing appropriate IYCF counseling. IYCF counselling will take place either at the target house or at the facility.4. Distribution of micronutrient supplementation.5. Following up the SAM and MAM cases.6.Provide outpatient care services at Bahar's PHCs to manage SAM without complications cases.7.Provide inpatient care services at Bahar's Hospital- Afrin and Khneiz SC, Ar-Raqq to manage SAM cases with medical complications.8.provide capacity building and training to the community health workers, doctors, nurses, midwives, nutritionists, and volunteers to detect and manage malnourished cases properly.Bahar will rely on nutrition cluster criteria and forms to register the beneficiaries.To address the gender issues in the target communities, the main providers of the services will be female CHWs to consider the existed culture and habits in the area as the counseling will target PLW, so ensuring that the services will be efficient and that there will be interaction relationship between the services provider and beneficiary.

Protection Risk Analysis

Violence against Health Facilities and Healthcare Providers: Bahar will deconflict all the targeted health facilities, report any attack on them, and support any efforts to highlight protections challenges facing health workers across Syria. - Gender Based Violence GBV: Hiring of female staff to increase access to health services for women and girls, training gynecologists, midwifes and nurses on the clinical management of sexual assault and rape, identification of GBV cases, and safe referrals, and link the health facilities with the social services to ensure safe referral pathways between both of them.- Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse PSEA: Bahar will conduct orientation session on PSEA on all health facilities to ensure all the staff is aware of PSEA, all the recruitment process will include PSEA in the criteria of selection, and all the staff should sign Bahar code of conduct, and PSEA policy.
