Project Summary

This project aims to implement Housing, Land and Property Rights’ project in
Yambio County in Western Equatoria state and Rumbeki County in Lakes state
This project covers the following protection cluster strategic objectives
The 3 S.O. for the Protection Cluster are:
1. S.O.1: Provide immediate lifesaving assistance to address priority protection needs for the most vulnerable populations in hard-to-reach areas
2. S.O.2: Prevent, mitigate and respond to protection risks through enhanced preparedness and resilience
3. S.O.3: Enable durable solutions for IDPs and other affected populations
The project proposal has five activities:-
1.1. Provision of age and sex appropriate legal awareness, assistance and counseling to 560 beneficiaries on housing land and property rights. This activity intends to hire services of legal experts to create awareness, provide legal assistance and legal counseling to men, women , boys and girls whose rights are under threat.
1.2. 1.2. Provision of quality community awareness on fundamental rights relating to housing, land and property targeting 9,520 beneficiaries
1.3. Formation, training and support of 15 housing, land and property Right protection committees to link communities with survey departments, land commissions, local government authorities and community land committee to advocate for respect of land rights, inclusion of women in land management and guarantee community rights to compensation when government discuss contract. The committees will consist of 14 members (at least 6 female and male members) will be formed in following places in Yambio county:- Li Rangu, Bangasu, Saura, Gangura, Hai Masia, Remenze, Timbiro, Hai Aworo, Hai Sujunu and Mumbai all in Yambio and Amongping, Jiir, Maleki, Matangai and Mayom in Rumbeki. . This activity will benefit 3,640 individuals.
1.4. Conduct 12 Protection assessments and continuous monitoring in Yambio and Rumbeki. The activity is meant collect and analyze data related housing, land and property rights.
1.5. Provision of individual Protection Assistance( NFI) to individuals and or families whose land and property rights have been abused . This activity targets 280 beneficiaries.
This project plan targets in total fourteen thousands beneficiaries (men 3,500=25%, women 4,760= 34%, boys 2,520= 18% and girls 3,220 =23%). The project is developed following grassroots consultations in different payams of Yambio. The total Project cost will be USD 400,000. The project has established mechanisms aimed at enhancing accountability to affected populations through provision of information about assistance, protection from sexual exploitation and abuses, continuous consultation with affected population beyond community leaders, Feedbacks and complain mechanisms and community engagement monitoring and evaluation are put in place. To ensure the centrality of protection in humanitarian actions, this project is based on protection risk analysis that considered specific needs of different groups. It also seeks to Identify and address barrier to access that different population could face. These project activities are made in a way that promotes gender equality and addresses protection threats such as violence, coercion, exploitation etc. The project tools are tailored to specifically measure beneficiaries’: accesses, safety and dignity.

Needs

Since the eruption of the violent conflict in South Sudan in 2013 and renewed fighting inJuba in 2016; South Sudan has been undergoing catastrophic humanitarian crisis.Millions of the South Sudanese population got displaced as internal displacedpersons and refugees in neighboring Countries.There have been total breakdown of rule of law. While Yambio county got engulfed into armed conflicts following emergency of various armed groups in the area all militarily opposing the government, Rumbek Centre got entangled into vicious cycles of revenge killings and cattle raiding. This led to huge displacement to as refugees and from villages to towns as internal displaced persons camping in church, scools, health centres ad other community centres. Displaced persons have lost great deal of land, houses and property to armed groups and other people who took advantage of situation. Some of those who abuse the housing, land and property of others are very powerful people with connections to the government or armed groups. Women and children are disproportional badly affected. This project seeks to provide legal awareness, assistance and counseling services to address the situation. The local cultures do not allow women, boys and girls to own housing, land and property. Most women who to gather with their husband work hard to acquire housing, land and property may end up with nothing in the event of the death of husband. The property inheritance practice unjustly allows relatives of the deceased husband to share up all the housing, land and the property of that family. The poor and innocent people continue to lose their property to the powerful. This practice has left many widows and children very vulnerable. Provision of quality community awareness on fundamental rights relating to housing, land and property is the activity meant to address the issue. There is coalition between individuals within the land survey department and those wishing to take advantage of vulnerable and poor to deprive them of their rights. Areas in Yambio like Hai Kuba, hai Kuza, Hai Masia Amongping, Jiir, Maleki, Matangai and Mayom in have experienced abuses of rights to land. To address this issue this project intends form, train and support 10 housing, land and property Right protection committees to link communities with survey departments, land commissions, local government authorities and community land committee to advocate for respect of land rights, inclusion of women in land management. Many people in Yambio and Rumbki counties have been victim of government contract negotiation with investors. For example when state government under Governor Bakasoro try to build an international airport in Munbai village in Li-Rangu Payam, the land owners were just pushed out of the land with out compensation in any form. Similar situation also happened when state government negotiated for establishment Equatoria Tick plantation. This project will form, train and support 15 housing, land and property Right protection committees to advocate for fair government contract negotiation with investors so as to take into account rights of the indigenous people. Following launch of Partnership for Resilience and Recovery in attempt is being made to expand the private sector which may mean more displacement. When states were divided into state, many people from areas outside Yambio and Rumbek Centre counties had to go back to their new states which puts housing, land and property of these people at risk. Many civil servants opted to leave their women and children behind and some of the women and children have been forcefully evicted from their house. This is why this project plans to conduct 12 Protection assessments and continuous monitoring. This project through activity “ Provision of individual Protection Assistance( NFI)” intends to support individuals and or families whose land and property rights have been abused .

Accountability to affected population
Does the project provide beneficiaries with information?

Yes

How does the project provide beneficiaries with information?

The project intends to continuously and timely provide beneficiaries with information regarding assistance provided by the project and others that require referral. There will be monthly meetings to inform the beneficiaries. Information can also be made available through beneficiary groups like women groups. In places where there are local radios, there will be radio talk show pass information to the beneficiaries. Use of announcements in social functions such as funeral prayers to inform will be prioritized. Equal announcements will be done in schools and health centres to reach many beneficiaries. Referral pathway will be developed or updated and disseminated to wider beneficiaries. Visibility materials such as T shirts, sign posts, brochures and where possible billboards will be developed to provide beneficiaries with information about assistance. Prevention messages with emphasis on referrals to services will be developed in context specific ways. Volunteers will visit homes to inform beneficiaries about assistance provided. Persons that have benefited from the assistance will be made ambassadors to go and speak to others. Focus group discussions will be organized for different groups to help pass information conveniently to different groups. Reports will be published and made public to beneficiaries.

Are messages context-specific or/and tailored?

Yes

Briefly describe how

Messages to specific population groups will be tailored for example women and girls’ pictures will be used to advertise existence services. Literate group of population will need posters or brochures while illiterate group will need verbal or symbolic communication. Efforts will be made to translate messages to Arabic , Zande Balanda, Jur and Dinkalanguages . Sensitivities to local cultures, religion or gender will strictly be taken into consideration. Staffing priority in specific location locations will be given to local population to help in contextualizing messages to specific areas. Local artists such as local band groups will be used to pass specifically contextualized messages.

Protection from sexual exploitation and abuse

Yes

Briefly describe how

GREDA exercises zero tolerance policy toward sexual exploitation and abuse whether in development, humanitarian and peace building. All staff sign commitment to that guarantees that they will not engage in sexual exploitation and abuses. GREDA has a robust policy on prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse. No staff, partner or volunteer is allowed to engage in any sexual relations with beneficiary. Any staff or volunteer who commits or fails to report cases of sexual exploitation has committed gross misconduct and faces a disciplinary commit and if proven guilty will be dismissed. GREDA prohibits any sexual activity with a person under the age 18 and considers any such relation as non consensual and therefore punishable by the organization laws. All Managers working for GREDA are obliged to create and maintain environment which prevents sexual exploitation and abuse.

Nature of the consultations

Yes

Briefly describe the nature of the consultations

Over the last few months GREDA has engaged extensively in consultation with local communities in both counties. Different approaches were used to consult with different sex and age groups. Separate focus group discussions for women were organized. Key Informant Interviews were conducted to get specialized view on issue of protection. Rural Participatory approach was used with different groups to identify needs. Once needs were identified participants got involved in prioritizing the needs. The needs prioritized through community consultations are the once used to design this proposal. Feedbacks from different groups have been incorporated in the needs analysis and activity development for this project proposal. In Yambio GREDA team visited and consulted with affected population from following places Saura, Li Rangu, Remenze in Yambio and and Amongping, Jiir, Maleki, Matangai and Mayom in Rumbeki. . This plan is informed in large part by contributions from the affected population. The following numbers were consulted Men 207, Women 354, Boys 173, and Girls 260.

Feedback /complaint mechanisms?

Yes

Briefly describe the mechanisms

GREDA considers feedback from beneficiaries as important part of performance monitoring and result evaluation. All Beneficiaries form important arty of monitoring and free and encouraged by GREDA to report to any concerns they have regarding the project activities/team through a suggestion box at the site concealing their identity. A group of beneficiaries may select representatives to have face to face meeting with the management. All managers are accessible who have a feedback or complain. Contact of head office especially Program Coordinator, Monitoring and Evaluation Coordinator will be made available to beneficiaries to launch complain against any GREDA team member. Regular surveys will be conducted to get feedback on satisfaction of beneficiaries. All feedback will be documented. Feed-backs constitute lessons learnt and will be used to improve performance towards achievement of the results.

Community engagement in this project is crucially important and guaranteed under the project management policy of GREDA. Centrality of Protection in humanitarian action
Protection Risk Analysis?

Yes

Briefly describe how

This project is based on protection risk analysis taking into account specific needs of different population groups. It puts focus on identification of specific risks for a specific population group for example sexual exploitation and abuse of women and girls rights by project staff, volunteers, other humanitarian actors and partners. This explains why GREDA has a strict code of conduct relating to any sexual relation. It also has put emphasis on setting mechanism that reduces the risks to the population group. This project also identified sexual violence against beneficiaries as a possible risk that seeks to mitigate that by providing services to all gender equally. The project also in its analysis identified some possible risks to staff including ambushes and burglary against project supplies. Safety policies are put in place by GREDA to mitigate possible.

Barriers to Access?

Yes

Briefly describe how

This project has identified number of barriers to access for example women and girls may face threat from land dealers. Other barriers to accessing services may include bad roads in parties of Lokiri due to heavy rains. Areas like Li Rangu challenges of in security caused by presence of armed groups in the areas may cause accessibility problem. Cultural norms may discourage women from acquiring land and reporting threats. This project has put strong mechanisms in place to address all access changes

Addresses inequalities or protection threats?

Yes

Briefly describe how

This project tries to address inequalities that exist between women, men, girls and boys, between rural and urban communities, different age groups by including all groups to access services. It seeks to establish services more close to rural populations in hard to reach project areas. The project intends address inequalities caused by lack of skills among girls and women. This project is designed in a way that addresses protection threats such as physical violence, coercion etc by educating communities on dangers of committing violence.

Project activities address inequalities or protection threats?

Yes

Briefly describe how

. Project activities such as awareness raising are meant to discourage violence, promote equality among different sex, ages and areas. Legal assistance spaces will reduce protection threats such as sexual violence that may be planned against women and girls when they report threats. Protection assessment and monitoring is meant to identify issues that affect protection of beneficiaries and share the reports with humanitarian actors to inform planning.

Does the project monitoring measure safety of beneficiaries?

Yes

How does the project monitoring measure safety of beneficiaries?

The overall project is to achieve safety for all beneficiaries of the assistance. In line with the project monitoring is tailored to capture safety status of the different.
