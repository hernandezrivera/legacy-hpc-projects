Project Summary

The intensifying crisis in the North West and South West Regions has continued to inflict untold suffering on people . 1.3 million people are in need, 530,000 displaced, with children recording the highest number of those in need The continuous displacement and killings has led to an increase in the number of orphans, unaccompanied and children separated from the families . This displacement has exposed children to significant risk of diverse forms all leading to extended distress. There is an Increase in number unaccompanied children, orphans and separated children . Children are exposed to violence, abuse, child labor and early marriages etc. Adolescents are victims of arbitrary arrests, forced recruitment and extrajudicial executions and are at greater risk of injury or death directly related to the conflict. As a result, some families limit their sons' movements or send them to cities to eliminate the risk of their children being killed or forcibly recruited. Finally, as a result of displacement and destruction of homes, many boys and girls no longer have birth certificates or have never had one, which has a direct impact on administrative procedures such as national examinations and makes them more vulnerable to exploitation or trafficking and arbitrary arrest and detention, particularly for boys aged 14 and over.
This project seeks to improve on the protection and respect of fundamental rights of crisis affected children exposed to risks of violence, abuse, sexual exploitation.
The project is targeting 500 children separated from their and unaccompanied children in Mezam, Bui and Donga Mantung Divisions.
The project intends to carry out an assessment on child protection needs in Mezam, Bui and Donga Mantung Divisions, identify at least 500 ,children separated from their families and unaccompanied children, identify triggers of separation, carryout case management and reunification of at least 200 children with their families, providing direct psychosocial support to the 500children and at least 200 care givers , sensitize parents /care givers on positive parenting, Provide basic needs for the most vulnerable children separated from their families and those reunited with their families that are extremely vulnerable. (disable children, children of disable parents and care givers, female headed households with no source of livelihood and elderly parents or care givers with no source of livelihood.) The project will equally support the establishment of civil documents (birth Certificates) for 200 Children.
The project will benefit 60% female and 40 % male . Disable unaccompanied and separated children and disable care givers/ parents will be prioritized.
The project will last for 12 months from January to December 2021.
SHUMAS will actively participate in all cluster coordination meetings to share and learn from other organization .
