Project Summary

The project aims providing critical life-saving and life-sustaining WASH services and further strengthen resilience of the most vulnerable 7500 persons (3120 women, 2880 men,750 boys and 750 girls) in South Galkayo and Hobyo Districts, Mudug Region, Somalia by supporting their urgent as well as long term WASH needs for women, men, boys and girls from villages that are yet to recover from impacts cumulative effects of below average rains experienced across most parts of Somalia during the 2018 Deyr (October-December), followed by harsh weather conditions during the dry 2019 Jilaal (January–March) season and the delayed and poor performance of the 2019 Gu (April-June). These consecutive seasonal poor performances have been exacerbated by limited recovery from the extended impacts of devastating 2016/2017.

Training of 50 water committee members ( 35men and 15women) 4. Water Distribution by Voucher for 5 weeks in 15 drought affected remote villages in Hobyo and South Galkayo District targeting 1000 Most vulnerable HHs5 Construction of 50 gender sensitive(for men and women) new latrines fitted with hand washing facilities and lockable doors and are lighted at night in IDP camps in South Galkayo. 6. Conducting CLTS in remote villages South Galkayo and Hobyo Districts with very limited sanitation facilities 7. Distribution of 1000 Hygiene kits in South Galkayo and Hobyo Districts to villages with cases of AWD/Cholera and other water borne diseases 8. Chlorination of Water sources (shallow wells and boreholes) in the project locations in South Mudug

Mitigation Measures

1b - Making sure that access routes to distribution points of WASH services are safe and selecting centralized locations for distribution to limit beneficiaries from covering long distances to access distribution points of the WASH services and NFIs. In addition distributions should take place in daylight to enable beneficiaries travel back to their homes before cover of the dark at night.2a. Ensuring that WASH services are reaching the most vulnerable populations by identifying and prioritizing them and further ensuring prevention of discrimination or exclusion of marginalised groups such as IDPs, minority clans members, as well as those with disabilities.3b. Making sure that the mode and frequency of distribution minimises safety risk to the targeted beneficiaries by ensuring that beneficiaries are aware that they may face theft, intimidation, sexual assault and extortion upon receipt of their entitlements
