Project Summary

The project is in line with the Education Sector HRP Objectives number 1 Availably & Access ( Increased safe and equitable availability and access to in-person and home-based formal and non-formal education for crisis-affected children and youth1response framework in Syria 2021 following) and 2 Quality (Enhanced quality of formal and non-formal education for children and youth, All the targeted sub-districts fall under severity scores 4-5 based on HNO 2022 Education Sector PiN severity which are : Jabel Samaan in Aleppo , Dhameer , Harasta and Az-Zabdani in Rural Damascus, Khan Shaykun in Idleb and Dar'a
In Daraa,
the targeted areas selected based on the sector priorities of communities selection criteria as following: Communities with little or no education services, Communities with high numbers of out of school children (OOSC) , Communities where students are learning in unsafe or unconducive learning environments. Also, this project will be complemented by Multi-sectoral projects, the complementarities will be from different sectoral projects as TdH followed Area Approach by implementing multi-sectoral projects to provide the targeted communities with comprehensive services
The General Objective of the proposed project is to contribute to increasing education safe and appropriate spaces along with increasing the quality of the education process , this objective will be achieved through two categories of activities, Conduct light maintenance for the schools who need it to be functional and provide them with Pre-fabricated schools to be as extra classes in addition to increasing the quality of Education through conducted focused ToT trainings titles ( Active learning approach, New teaching method..) and Cascading the aforementioned training to the rest of teachers and educational personnel by ToT beneficiaries, TdH focused during planning the education project on Education sector HRP Needs two main pillars: Access and Quality
ACCESS includes:
•Investments include repairing, expanding, establishing and operationalizing permanent and temporary learning spaces and classrooms, and establishing/enhancing WASH facilities.
•Distribution of teaching and learning materials
Quality includes:
•Teacher training and continuous professional development.
•Integration of child safeguarding and MH/PSS for students and education personnel,
•Provision of appropriate learning materials,
The Project activities focused on two years approach hence TdH will focus on the first objective of the Education HRP plan during the first year in order to set a strong ground for the second-year interventions which will focus on increasing the quality and improving the school's teachers and educational personnel to improving sustainable and equitable access to basic education services in
communities affected by the crisis.
Project Main activities will be as the following :
1- Light maintenance for 10 schools ( 2 schools in each location ) and transform it to be inclusion school
2- Supply and distribute School kits for the students in the targeted schools
3- Supply and distribute Teacher kits for the teachers and admin staff in the targeted schools
4- Distribute Cash for Education based on specific selection criteria
5- Supply and Install 10 pre-fabricated rooms as resources rooms and WASH facilities
6- Distribute PPE to the targeted schools
7- BTL campaign include printing materials (Posters, Flyers ..) targeted the locations communities

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

PR1:Learning facilities could be attacked. Attacks may include: airstrikes, bombs/explosions/shelling, Risk of explosive as landmines, looting/theft; kidnapping/arrest of a teacher or students, direct attack on teacher or student, intimidation of school staff / students or military use of the school. M1: 1-Provide students, their caregivers and school personnel information on safer routes/times to travel.2-Provide students, their caregivers and school personnel information on what to do and what not to do if they face different types of threats-3-Engage community leaders/members and change-makers to support awareness raising interventions to highlight the importance of safety and related solutionsPR2:Teachers or other education personnel participate in physical punishment, act violently, bully and neglect students. Students participate in bullying or act violently towards other students and/or to teachers. M2Learning facilities should have an enforced code of conduct against all forms of violence and unacceptable conduct, including bullying, corporal punishment, and physical, verbal and sexual abuse.Teachers and school personnel should be trained on prevention and reporting of, and issues related to, violence, physical punishment and bullying and the use of positive discipline The application of these skills by all school personnel should be monitored as part of ongoing professional development and support.
