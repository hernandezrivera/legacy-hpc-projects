Project Summary

GOALPrime Organization Nigeria (GPON) via this 12-month nutrition intervention will reach a total target of 6400 (40% children and 60% adults) beneficiaries in Dikwa, Dambao and Gubio LGAs of Borno state. The target children are aged 6-23 months while the adults are Pregnant & Lactating Women (PLW) as well as fathers who will constitute the father-father support groups. This intervention will focus on improving access to quality lifesaving nutrition services aimed at reducing acute malnutrition for children through the provision of micronutrients to Moderate Acutely Malnourished (MAM) children 6-23 months, and Pregnant & Lactating Women (PLWs) through the provision of Ready To Use Supplementary Food (RUSF)
400 children (60% girls & 40% boys) will be reached with micronutrient supplementary powders (MNPs) 200 children with moderate acute malnutrition admitted in the targeted supplementary feeding Programme. GPON will also be intentional about targeting children living with disabilities.
GOALPrime will ensure quality nutrition service delivery by strengthening the capacity of the existing health and community structures for sustainability. Systematic identification, referral, and treatment of acutely malnourished children and pregnant and lactating women will be carried out. MAM cases will be treated by using fortified locally produced supplementary food (Action Meal). GOALPrime will source for and provide Vitamin A, micronutrient powder supplementation, and deworming to school-going children and all eligible children under five years with support from the nutrition sector and UNICEF. These children will also be identified through the mother family MUAC approach which guarantees the prospects of sustainability.
The anthropometrics of children under this program will be monitored on a weekly basis. On the other hand, children with severe acute malnutrition will be referred to outpatient programs and monitored weekly. All these interventions will be integrated with preventive nutrition interventions such as IYCF counseling and social behavioral change communication to 3000 adults out of which 2400 will be Pregnant and Lactating Women (PLWs) and 600 will be fathers. This will be situated within a comprehensive Social Behavior Change Communication that will include an Infant and Young Child Feeding (IYCF) Programme in Emergency, formation, and operationalization of Mother to Mother and Father to Father Support Groups where WASH, Health and GBV key messages will be integrated.
GOALPrime will adapt the manual developed by the sector and other partners for this component of the project. GOALPrime will manage moderate acute malnutrition via the Targeted Supplementary Feeding Programme established for MAM children aged between 6-23 months & MAM Pregnant and Lactating Women (PLW) using Action Meal. GOALPrime will also produce Tom-brown and conduct porridge mom simulations for targeted women. GOALPrime will also conduct a monthly cooking demonstrations of 30 support groups (20 mother-to-mother and 10 father-to-father) to be established in Dikwa , Dambao and Gubio LGA. GOAL Prime’s implementation approach will be preventive and curative of Acute malnutrition without medical compications. GOALPrime will be strengthening an inclusive and appropriate community-based feedback and complaint mechanism.
GOALPrime will be sensitive to the protection of the environment and ensure that our approaches and tools do no harm to the environment and the beneficiaries.

1 - Needs

The HRP Food insecurity and Malnutrition Plan (2024) reveals critical Nutrition gaps in BAY states.(HRP, 2024). Urgent action is vital, with approximately 2 M people facing hunger and over 1.5M children under five experiencing acute malnutrition during. Severe malnutrition affects 511,807K children and 1M Children with MAM due to food insecurity.suboptimal feeding practices, disease outbreaks, conflict, limited access to safe water, sanitation, and climate change.To address these challenges, the project integrates preventive and treatment services, in OTPs and scales up local solutions for feeding, and promotes appropriate nutrition practices. season surge actions.The 2023 IPC Acute Malnutrition Analysis projects a worsening nutrition situation across the BAY states indicating that the severity of acute malnutrition and number of children and pregnant or breastfeeding women and girls (PBWG) in need of nutrition assistance is expected to remain high through 2024. This is aggravated by increasingly prolonged lean seasons, worsening acute food insecurity, economic crisis, high prevalence of measles and acute watery diarrhea (AWD), outbreak of diphtheria and flooding. During the lean season, the impact of all the deteriorated drivers of acute malnutrition will be at their peak and the nutrition situation in most of the LGAs will likely be worse than same period of 2023.A nutrition situation categorized as Serious or worse is anticipated in 14 LGAs including 10 in Borno (Damboa, Gubio, Jere, Kaga, Konduga, Mafa, Magumeri, Maiduguri, Marte, and Monguno) and four in Yobe (Machina,The project integrates efforts to address nutrition and gaps, collaborating with other sectors and prepositioning resources. 8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

By integrating every strategy with the protection principles, protection (GBV & PSEA) is mainstreamed the project design and will throughout the execution of this project and advances meaningful access, safety, and dignity of both beneficiaries and of employers and volunteers. Safety, decency, and avoiding any kind of harm to both beneficiaries and employees are priorities for GOALPrime. Additionally, GOALPrime will also make sure that the target population has equitable access to the service, free fromimpediments and prejudice, in accordance with their needs. GOALPrime will payattention to people and groups who could be especially vulnerable or have a hard timeobtaining help and resources, including those with disabilities and GBV survivors whohave been identified. GOALPrime will integrate GBV Risk Mitigation efforts to reduce ordecrease GBV risk to beneficiaries. GOALPrime will work consciously in compliancewith the Sphere Humanitarian Core Standards and Imperatives. All approaches willalign with the principles of protection as we remain committed to ensuring that thisproject does not expose beneficiaries to more risk in compliance with the DO NOHARM Protection Principle. Also, GOALPrime will be intentional about maintaining anenvironment where there is zero Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (SEA). All staff,volunteers, contractors, and vendors engaged directly or indirectly to implement thisproject will be given PSEA orientation. They will be familiarized with GOALPrime'spolicy on PSEA, and they MUST express their unwavering commitment by signingGOALPrime's Zero Tolerance to SEA Code of Conduct. Also, GOALPrime will also laydown reporting protocols for the reporting of PSEA cases if unfortunately, they occur.GOALPrime will also ensure that the identity of the reporter(s) is kept very confidential.When convenient, GOALPrime will encourage reports to be made on anonymousgrounds. Also, being guided by the Inter-Agency mechanism for reporting SEA,GOALPrime will ensure a systematized engagement with affected populationsincluding confidential complaints and investigations procedures at both the systemand individual agency levels. As recommended by IASC Task Force on PSEA whichidentified inter-agency cooperation in the creation and maintenance of communitybased complaints mechanisms (CBCMs) as a key component in the prevention andresponse to SEA. GOALPrime will continue to raise awareness to the target populationthat humanitarian assistance is free and never conditioned on sexual favors as well asto be informed on how to access the appropriate complaints mechanisms, andsupport to set up in the community if SEA occurs. Furthermore, GOALPrime will ensurethat established CBCMs in target locations are effective and that there is inter-agencycoordination to ensure consistent messaging and that access to the mechanism is asbroad and straightforward as possible for potential complainants. GOALPrime willalso be intentional about the environment and safety of the communities where weimplement by ensuring that our approaches and tools do not contribute to thedegradation of the environment. In addition, GOALPrime will also integrate climatechange and provide routine sensitization of the project beneficiaries on climate changeprevention. GOALPrime shall mainstream CP, GBV & SEA into all training contents as well as approaches

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

In target LGAs, GOALPrime will create or improve new or existing procedures for complaints and feedback. In order to understand the project's impact on adolescents. GOALPrime will organize biweekly feedback focus group talks with girls and boys who are beneficiaries of the project as well as those beneficiaries andcaregivers. Discussions in the Focus Group shall, however, adhere to the COVID-19rules regarding keeping social distance and the use of face masks. Furthermore, GOALPrime will continue to file a complaint, and If beneficiaries and their parents feel more comfortable makinganonymous complaints, they can place them in suggestion boxes placed in strategic locations throughout the project area. They can also leave suggestions for how theproject might better serve them. As soon as complaints are received and reviewed,GOALPrime will inform the beneficiaries, parents, and other pertinent communitystakeholders. The program approach will be reviewed, and ideas will also be taken intoaccount. Concerns raised through our established feedback channel will be addressedin a timely and effective manner. This will be addressed by GOALPrime as soon as complaints arereceived and reviewed, GOALPrime will inform the beneficiaries, parents, and otherpertinent community stakeholders.
