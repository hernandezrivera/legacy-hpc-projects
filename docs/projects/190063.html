Project Summary

Plan International Ethiopia (PlE) is implementing a five year (2020-2025) project entitled “PlayMatters (PM)” an education initiative funded by LEGO Foundation. The program reimagines childhood for 800,000+ refugee and host-community children across Ethiopia, Tanzania, and Uganda, especially children whose education and social development has been affected by displacement and trauma. Building on a growing evidence base that children demonstrate resilience when supported by Learning through Play (LtP) methodologies, PM cultivates holistic learning for children ages 3-12+. PM led by the International Rescue Committee and includes Plan International, War Child Holland, Innovations for Poverty Action, the Behavioral Insights Team in partnership with the LEGO Foundation. As part of the consortium member PIE is implementing the project in Afar, Benishangul-Gumuz and Gambella region of Ethiopia.

1. - Projects activities

Meetings/workshops focused on building relationships, understanding LtP, and reaching agreements/approvals (as needed),Advocacy materials and key messages about LtP, informed by evidence, LtP workshops for school directors, SMCs, inspectors, and local officials to support LtP implementation and monitoring, LtP professional development program for educators (workshop series, mentoring) and ongoing peer support guidance to encourage use of LtP in the classroom and to provide educators with techniques for stress reduction and wellbeing for themselves and their students, Resources to facilitate play (i.e. guidance for creating materials, activity guides, Media programmes (radio/video),Workshops with PlayAmbassadors and PlayChamptions (identified by PlayAmbassadors) on the importance of LtP, how and why to promote play to caregivers and in the community.
