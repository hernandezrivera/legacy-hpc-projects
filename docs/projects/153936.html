Project Summary

Providing PSS & resilience courses for primary & secondary schools students in Ramadi aged 10-15 years in the most conflict areas of Ramadi to strengthen children's resistance to difficult conditions. Project including 5 districts (7 Nissan , Al-Hawz, Al-Malaab , Al-Moalmeen , and the 5 Kilo area) targeting 8 schools /dist. 20 facilitators will be trained to provide training. 1000 students will be targeted. Each course will train 25 students. Teachers (male&female) will trained on CP integration , building capacities of target children parents on the best CP methods and direct response to children needs , establish CP groups (10) from community structures
