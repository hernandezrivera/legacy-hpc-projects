Project Summary

Maram Foundation (MF) will apply for HPC to carry out a project in 3 locations inside Syria, serving the populations of three IDPs camps (Al Harameen , Ahl Al Sham and Tal Jubeen camp) , as a result of large influx of IDPs and limited access to services, there was a significant lack to the basic WASH services.
Maram Foundation will provide 3027 households (HHs) living in Northern camps in Aleppo governorate with basic WASH services, latrine construction , sanitation system construction and hygiene kits distribution ,the project design was done according to SPHERE standards
The planned intervention will mainstream protection principles specially (Do no harm) throughout the project cycle by considering the different needs of men, women, boys and girls. The provision of sanitation services (latrines, washing facilities etc), will be consulted with the targeted community, and take into account the specific needs of each group. Maram Foundation also revised the content of hygiene kits by involving the women and girls through Focus Group Discussions (FGDs) during previews implemented projects
To cover the gaps of elderly and disabled beneficiaries, Maram Foundation will include accessibility for the latrines design in the targeted location when there are needs, also Maram Foundation under hygiene promotion will conducted segregated sessions for men, women and children to increase the personal and environmental hygiene awareness.

Needs

Maram Foundation targeted the camps in northern Aleppo governorate depending on secondary data resource represented through periodic reports of WASH Cluster, Assistance and Coordination Unit (ACU), EWARN biweekly reports for the period week December 2017 comparing to December 2018. The EWARN report shows that there is increase in the percentage of the WBD especially (Typhoid fever (STF))In addition, the Curved water-related diseases show increase especially in summer Furthermore, as a basic data resource Maram field staff upon the launch of this allocation conducted technical assessment (depending on WASH Cluster assessment form) in the camps in Azaz district, in addition to the direct observation and key informants’ interviews (KII) with the camps managementAccording to assessment analysing and as Maram Foundation in view of its ongoing projects in northern Aleppo camps noted the gaps in the mentioned camps where the main problems in the camps are as following , the latrines and showers in the mentioned camps are not usable and has no privacy due to lack of maintenance and the floods of septic tanks especially in winter in addition to depending on random sewage pits which are constantly suffering from the flood that leads to swamp formation

Implementation of activities

MF will implement WASH activities directly through dividing the project to 4 phases: Phase 1: contracting with suppliers and contractor in the first month for services provision Phase 2: implementation of project activities In (Al Harameen , Ahl Al Sham and Tal Jubeen camp) camp :-latrine construction in Tal Jubeen camp -Latrines cleaning in Tal Jubeen camp-Sanitation System Construction through sanitation network construction in Al Harameen camp -Sanitation System Construction through sanitation network construction in Ahl Al Sham camp -Hygiene kits distribution and Hygiene promotionIn Tal Jubeen CampPhase 3: monitoring the project activitiesMF staff will monitor project operating directly and provide reports for THF and 4Ws to WASH and CCCM ClustersPhase 4 : Exit Strategy :Maram team will conduct sessions with BNFs living in the camps to try to encourage them thinking and focus on their plans to leave the camps and try to build their life outside the camp, during the implementation of this project Maram will also try to advocate for livelihood projects to be implemented there.
