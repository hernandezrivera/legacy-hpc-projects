Project Summary

Under MPCA, UNHCR and partners will support the most vulnerable refugees, asylum seekers and Libyans (IDPs, returnees and non-displaced) to meet their urgent basic needs through the provision of multi-purpose cash assistance. In line with the MPCA Section of the HRP, emergency one-off and multi-month cash assistance will be provided to 7,200 HHs (4,500 Libyan and 2,700 non-Libyan). Multi-month cash assistance will be provided to Libyans for up to 3 months and non-Libyans for up to 6 months, depending on their level of vulnerability. Vulnerability criteria and beneficiary selection for Libyans will be aligned with other cash actors, and for refugees UNHCR Protection will ensure selection criteria are in line with needs. Refugees will be reassessed for eligibility of assistance after six months, to see if their socioeconomic situation has changed. UNHCR plans to continue its support to the leadership of the Cash Working Group (CWG), as it has done in 2018.

Needs

The deteriorating economic situation, inflation rate, devaluation of the Libyan dinar in the black market and shortage in liquidity in the banking system have all led to the reduced purchasing power of the Libyan population, especially those affected by the conflict, which further deepens the vulnerabilities and increases exposure to various protection risks. Given the volatile security situation in Libya, safety and security of civilians is severely affected, and shelter, cash and NFI needs are constantly increasing. MPCA will continue to be provided to the most vulnerable refugees in Tripoli, as part of UNHCR’s ongoing refugee cash programs, with possible expansion to Misrata. Tripoli, Benghazi, Misrata, Sebha and Sirt have been prioritized for MPCA based on the inter-sectoral severity ranking in the HNO.

Activities

Conduct vulnerability assessments and identify highly vulnerable households who need support to meet their basic needs- Provide emergency one-off cash assistance to highly vulnerable households in need of urgent cash assistance- Provide three months of cash assistance to highly vulnerable Libyan households and up to six months of assistance to highly vulnerable refugee and asylum seeker households to meet their basic needs Protection colleagues will be strongly involved throughout the project, including in refining of targeting criteria, protection risk assessment, communication with communities and beneficiaries, and post distribution monitoring. Multi-purpose cash assistance will be coordinated under the Cash Working Group, led by UNHCR and co-led by Mercy Corps. Key sectors (including Protection, Shelter/NFI, and Food Security) will be consulted by UNHCR and other CWG members in the composition of the MEB, targeting, joint assessments and other critical processes. Accountability to affected populations will be achieved by including beneficiaries throughout the project cycle. This includes ensuring the population of concern participate in identifying needs and priorities for the response, effective two-way communication with beneficiaries and communities (information provision and complaints mechanisms), and ensuring PDM findings are included to improve programming over time.
