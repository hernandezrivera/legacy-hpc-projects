Project Summary

Through this project, Mehad (Ex-UOSSM France) intends to provide lifesaving and life-sustaining nutrition services to affected people in NWS through integrating two Outpatient Therapeutic Program OTP centers and three Rapid Response Teams RRTs with the primary healthcare services provided in two PHC centers in two locations in two governorates (Idleb anI d Aleppo) in NWS. The project was designed in alignment with people’s needs, the severity scale data published by the nutrition cluster, and the HRP objectives endorsed by the cluster. The project is designed to provide the targeted population with equitable life-saving and preventive nutrition services, enhance the well-being and welfare of people and strengthen the capacity of local health professionals. The project goal is to contribute to decreasing the nutrition-related morbidity and mortality of the people living in NWS through:
- Enhancing the availability, accessibility and affordability of the nutrition services through equipping the OTP facilities, hiring the project staff, and providing free-of-charge services.
-Providing the project targeted groups with community-based house-to-house screening services to early detect malnutrition cases and providing timely and coordinated referrals for those with malnutrition to the proper and nearest nutrition facility, i.e., OTPs or stabilization centers SCs
-Enhancing the quality of the provided services through adhering to the UNICEF technical guidelines and improving the capacity of the health providers.
-Enhancing preventive health approach through raising public awareness particularly care-givers of children under 5, elderlies and adolescents on best nutrition practices.
The services delivered by Mehad through this project will be as per the technical standards of UNICEF, CMAM and IYCF nutrition cluster technical working groups TWGs, and in accordance with the Mehad operational policies and SOPs. To achieve this output, Mehad will actively attend the nutrition cluster meetings and the CMAM and IYCF TWGs and will capture every possible opportunity through its network with other partners and health actors to maximize the technical capacity of the project staff regarding nutrition programs such CMAM and IYCF. Moreover, project activities will be reported to the nutrition cluster reporting platforms i.e., 4Ws.
The project was also designed to ensure the inclusion of people with disability, the adherence to the PSEA principles, and the provision of gender and age-sensitive services. In addition, Mehad will put efforts to collaborate with other partners in terms coordinating the referrals and achieve a better integration with the other sectors, for instance, The RRTs may support in responding to a specific public health issue, i.e., Cholera outbreak, through delivering key health and hygiene messages in accordance with the relevant case management guidelines in WHO.
The OTPs services will be provided in two primary healthcare facility supported by Mehad :
1- Barisha PHC which is located in Barisha community / Qourqeena sub-district /Harim district
2- Bab Al Hajar PHC which is located in Ar-Ra'ee sub district / Al Bab district.
As for the three proposed RRTs, one RRT will be operating in Qourqeena sub-district and linked to Barisha PHC while2 RRTs will provide their services in Ar-Ra'ee sub-district where the coverage is lower and the need is even greater. The MEAL Team monitors the project indicators and activities by analyzing beneficiary data, conducting post-service interviews, taking field notes, filling out technical checklists, and publishing feedback tools.
The catchment population is 128,269 for both locations.

3 - Needs

Based on the 2022 SMART Survey report conducted for Northwest Syria, the chronic malnutrition (stunting) rate was found at 22.3%. The prevalence of the combined GAM rate showed recorded at 5.0% (3.3 - 7.5 95% C.I) with a combined SAM rate of 1.7% (0.6 – 4.4 95% C.I). Based on these facts, approximately 50.000 children may suffer from severe wasting in 2023. If not timely identified and treated, those children would be at risk for death, disability, or low cognitive development. Maternal Wasting is of serious public health concern among Pregnant and Lactating Women. The survey found that 1 out of 10 (11%) PLW (140,000) suffers from acute maAccording to the funding gap analysis report published by the health cluster for third quarter of the year 2022, only 61% of the nutrition needs are met in NWS and nearly 0.3 million people are affected by this gap. The gaps identified and reported by the health cluster in primary and secondary healthcare has been increasing. The September health cluster bulletin showed that only 58% of 328 primary healthcare units are functional in NWS while 18 specialized hospitals went out of service in many sub district. All these gaps put more stain on the already exhausted health system and would impact the economic, health and nutrition status of the affected populations. Moreover, the Cholera outbreak was declared in Syria in September 2022. Till 27th of November 2022, the total number of reported suspected Cholera cases reached up to 16389 cases.

4 - Implementation of activities

Mehad proposes to address malnutrition among PLWs and U5 children in Barisha and Ar-Raee communities located in AR-Raee and Qourqeena sub-districts. The proposed OTPs and RRTs will work to serve a population of 128,269 (64,293 males and 63,976 females) including 71239 IDPs. The activities will be as follows:CMAM program: - Screening of all Children 6–59 months and PLWs in the targeted location- Treatment and follow-up of SAM cases according to UNICEF guidelines by using the RUTF supplies provided by UNICEF- Treating and following the MAM cases among CU5 and PLWs. - Referring the SAM cases with complication to the inpatient treatment program (SC). b) Infant and Young Child Feeding in Emergency (IYCF-E) Program: -Screen PLW on infant feeding difficulties and provide facility and community-based skilled counselling on WHO recommended Infant and Young Child Feeding in Emergencies (IYCF-E) and maternal nutrition practices- Providing the caregivers including pregnant and lactating women (PLWs) councelling/ awareness sessions on appropriate infant and young child feeding (IYCF). The counselling focus will be placed on optimal maternal nutrition and care during pregnancy, timely initiation of breastfeeding and exclusive breastfeeding for the first six months.Mehad will also enhance early identification of malnutrition cases by scaling up the family MUAC approach and strengthening of referral pathways for outpatient management of moderate, severe wasting among CU5 & PLWs.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk 1: A sexual abuse against the children, the PLWs, or the children care givers occur. Mehad will have zero tolerance policy regarding sexual abuse or exploitation and all the staff will be well oriented on this topic and on how to provide dignified services to the women and children. Likelihood: moderate, Impact: severe, Risk level: very high Risk 2: The women in the targeted community have low access to nutrition services due to different reasons including their low social power. Mehad will raise the awareness of women to seek health and will reach those women and their children with the outreach activities done by RRTs proposed in the project. Likelihood: Unlikely, Impact: moderate Risk level: moderate. Risk 3: The nutrition services provided have low acceptability among the women, i.e., providing activities that do not respect the community norms and culture. Mehad will coordinate and meet with representatives of the local council, women and the community leaders to learn more on the cultural aspects of the served population so it provides more acceptable services. Likelihood: Moderate, Impact: significant, Risk level: Medium Risk 4: Cholera outbreakMehad has a contingency plan in place for such emergencies and outbreaks which is updated regularly. Moreover, Mehad will commit to the safety recommendations by the coordination platforms, WHO, Nutrition cluster and local health authorities.


8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Mehad is an active member of the PSEA network and it is committed to protecting beneficiaries from sexual exploitation and thus it is very serious to adhere to the PSEA standards and to make it inclusive to accountability measures.To achieve that, all staff of the proposed projects will be required to sign a PSEA code of conduct at the contacting stage after being fully oriented on PSEA and the available complaints mechanisms. On the other hand, posters and banners about PSEA will be adopted and visible for beneficiaries in all project facilities and the PSEA videos published by PSEA network will be shared on Mehad social media accounts i.e: Facebook.Mehad will raise the awareness of the beneficiaries at the levels of facility and community on how to recognize SEA cases and how they have the full right to report any of these cases through the PSEA network complaint hotline/ email which Mehad make them visible for all beneficiaries in the health facilities. Also, the PSEA focal point will conduct frequent training to the project staff on PSEA standards and the best practices to achieve high engagement between service providers and beneficiaries. Mehad is aware of the importance of the presence of female employees to conduct and follow PSEA activities, particularly when approaching female benefices. PSEA focal point will ensure the mainstreaming of PSEA standards and policy in all project activities.


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumptions: -The border between turkey and Syria remains open for humanitarian assistance and staff.-The security situation is stable to provide the services and there are no major movement or displacement occur or a change in the controlling map between the military forces. -The local health authorities keep supporting the provision of services through the project. -The staff of the project are competent to provide assured quality services.Risks:Risk 1: Cholera outbreak spreads widely in NWS. Likelihood: Moderate, Impact: Significant, Risk level: Moderate. Mitigation: Mehad will keep attending the Cholera task force in addition to the surveillance TWG to keep updated on the epidemiological status of Cholera and will ensure that its facilities are well covered by IPC and WASH measures to protect the staff, patients and visitors. Also, Mehad will intensify its efforts to distribute nutrition supplies to eligible recipients to enhance their ability to fight the outbreak.Risk 2: Overlapping in the provision of the services, Likelihood: Unlikely, Impact: significant, Risk level: moderate. Mitigation: Mehad will make sure to maintain high coordination levels with the health cluster and local health authorities, i,e, HDs, and will also make sure to attend the health and nutrition clusters meetings in addition to the relevant technical working groups TWGs. Through these coordination channels, Mehad will exchange information and ensure all parties are updated.
