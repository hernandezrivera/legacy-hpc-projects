Start Date

End Date

Project Summary

The project aims at strengthening the WASH conditions for IDPs, returnees and hosting communities in Palma, Metuge and Mecufi by: contributing to increase the number of people accessing a sufficient quantity of safe water for drinking and domestic needs, by establishing, rehabilitating or constructing water points at accomodation centres, community's resettlement areas, local returnees communities or public institutions (schools, health centres, etc), maintenance of water points; by contributing to increase the number of people accessing appropriate sanitation facilities in accomodation centres, community's resettlement areas, local returnees communities or public institutions through construction, decommissioning and maintenance of temporary, household or standard latrines (the latter in public institutions); by ensuring an operational solid waste management system in IDPs camps; and by disseminating appropriate awareness messages on WASH.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project aims at strengthening the WASH conditions for IDPs, returnees and hosting communities in Palma, Metuge and Mecufi by: contributing to increase the number of people accessing a sufficient quantity of safe water for drinking and domestic needs, by establishing, rehabilitating or constructing water points at accomodation centres, community's resettlement areas, local returnees communities or public institutions (schools, health centres, etc), maintenance of water points; by contributing to increase the number of people accessing appropriate sanitation facilities in accomodation centres, community's resettlement areas, local returnees communities or public institutions through construction, decommissioning and maintenance of temporary, household or standard latrines (the latter in public institutions); by ensuring an operational solid waste management system in IDPs camps; and by disseminating appropriate awareness messages on WASH.The key beneficiaries' group of the interventions are households that have been displaced as a result of theseveral attacks that have affected Cabo Delgado, and that are living in accommodation centres and resettlement areas (Metuge and Mecufi) as well as hosting communities (Metuge and Mecufi) and returnees (Palma). Following an initial assessment to determine specific areas where activities under this project will be implemented, the interventions include the outputs and activities described in the following section more in detail.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Output 1: Access to safe water of appropriate quantity and quality: Activities are concentrated in resettlement/accommodation centres as well as in returness areas, which still have urgent WASH needs and include increased access to water through (1) Improving access to water through new water systems or water points rehabilitation, and operation and maintenance of existing infrastructures; (2) support to FIPAG water treatment plant and network and (3) establishment/refreshment of water committees to ensure mechanisms for operation and maintenance of water systems are in place.Output 2: Access to appropriate sanitation and hygiene facilities, including management of solid wasteHELPCODE will be in charge of: - the installation, maintenance and decommissioning of emergency latrines/toilets, washing /bathingfacilities and handwashing points in the target areas to reduce the risk of disease transmission, while ensuring dignity, privacy and protection. - promoting activities for self-construction of household latrines and garbage pits in long-term resettlementand returning areas (including the provision of sanitation supplies for most vulnerable households), including through training of local artisans in quality slab production. Helpcode identifies professionals to construct quality slabs as well as local artisans to be trained by the identified professionals participate in the construction; - improved drainage and the collection, removal and disposal of solid waste; - Conducting a gender-based-violence safety audit to identify risks to women, girls, boys, and men related to WASH infrastructure in each site; - Vocational training of local artisans (including women) for production of slabs, soap, and maintenanceof water points and other WASH related skills.Community consultations will be held to inform about the location and segregation of facilities, as well as the identification of specific needs, such as adapted facilities for people living with disabilities, and the provision of adapted facilities to cover these needs.Output 3: Dissemination of information on safe water, sanitation and hygiene to prevent the spread of wash related diseases.Programme beneficiaries will benefit from hygiene promotion activities such as community engagement sessions and others that are required and possible. Participatory Health and Hygiene Education (PHHE) will be an integral component of the WASH activities.Community engagement sessions will be spearheaded by hygiene volunteers, who will be sensitised on safe water chain, good water, sanitation and hygiene practices, as well as prevention and control of communicable diseases such as cholera. Hygiene volunteers engaged will receive token incentives, standardised and agreed by the WASH Cluster.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Assumptions/risks identified: - Sub-national institutions involved in the action are not committed across its whole duration and do not support the initiative. Access to population and permits to enter areas of interest are not released and so the data are not collected to start working on WASH activities. Expected solution: MoUs are signed with the relevant District Services clearly explaining the project and their role. District technicians are involved in the interventions and given responsibilities and incentives. - Attacks occur in the intervention area so access is not possible and activities cannot be implemented. Expected solution: A security plan is drafted and updated regularly by project core team, including based on the information received from District Authorities / local intelligence and cluster actors. Beneficiaries are reassessed in the case of attacks.- Population is not inclined to provide information and be involved as focal point for the WASH related activities. Expected solution: specific attention will be devoted to communicating properly with village/community leaders and key community stakeholders with regards to the project specific objectives, and their collaboration will be requested to minimize risks of conflict linked to expectations.Overall is to be reported that Helpcode is a recognized protection actor in the emergency response in Cabo Delgado, and has an estaablished protection mainstreaming system in place.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

Helpcode AAP’s strategy abides to IASC 5 commitments, according to the following: Leadership - Promote a culture among staff of acceptance of failure and negative feedback from affected people. - Ensure that relevant staff and managers collect feedback from affected populations in appropriate age groups (sensitive to gender & age); respond to this feedback; and report back. - Work with local and national partners, so that there is a coordinated plan and capacity which is sensitive to gender, age, disability and ethnicity. Transparency - Provide accessible and timely information on organizational procedures, structures and processes that may impact communities in order to support informed decisions, and engage communities in a dialogue as part of information provision Participation - Work with relevant local and national partners and actors to support and influence effective design, assessment, implementation and monitoring of the response. - Engage all parts of a community, including those who are vulnerable or at risk to draft robust, contextual vulnerability and capacity analyses. Inclusivity is ensured through a thorough analysis of local social dynamics, mobility capacity of beneficiaries, differences in the use of time by women and men, identification of “role models” among community stakeholders that can promote social change. Feedback and complaints - Provide information to all in affected communities about the organisation, the principles it adheres to, how it expects its staff to behave (including commitments made on PSEA), the programmes it is implementing and what they intend to deliver. - Communicate in languages, formats and media that are easily understood, respectful and culturally appropriate for different members of the community, especially vulnerable and marginalised groups. - Establish and maintain effective feedback systems, using a variety of communication channels that are accessible to all persons of concern and that are appropriate for both sensitive and non-sensitive feedback. The feedback is disaggregated by age and sex at minimum. Design, Monitoring and evaluation - Assess the implementation of the Core Humanitarian Standard (CHS) and other collective standards including the IASC AAP commitments through assessments of organizational performance on accountability to measure and improve accountability to all persons of concern. - Include persons of concern as partners throughout the operation's management cycle and report the results of assessments and follow up actions to them

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The project will work with the District Services of infrastructure and based on Sphere Humanitarian Standards in order to go beyond the minimum standards for disability inclusion in humanitarian action Community consultations will be held to inform about the location and segregation of facilities, as well as the identification of specific needs, such as adapted facilities for people living with disabilities, and the provision of adapted facilities to cover these needs.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Among its internal procedure, Helpcode has developed an environmental policy aiming to avoid, minimize and mitigate the environmental impact of its activities in the field, in line with Sphere Humanitarian Standards and global environmental standard, guidelines and tools. The construction of water and sanitation works will be carried out in coordination with local institutional stakeholders such as SDPI and FIPAG, that are responsible for controlling and monitoring the execution of the works in accordance with environmental standards. In construction activities that involve the use of local materials, priority will be given to material supplied by certified traders, avoiding, when conditions permit, inadequate use of informal material available directly in the intervention sites. The construction and disposal of sanitation facilities will be carried out by in compliance with standard hygiene and humanitarian intervention procedures.
