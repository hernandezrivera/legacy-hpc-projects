Project Summary

Presently NGO Forum for Public Health implements a project titled “Provision of Life Saving WASH Service to Vulnerable Communities at Cox's Bazar’’ in support of Norwegian Church Aid (NCA) for a duration of May 2020- December 2022 to serve the vulnerable community induced by the proximity of climate change affect (salinity intrusion, repeated cyclone hits and seasonal storm), influx of Rohingya Refugee to Bangladesh (displacement from Myanmar in 2017) sheltered in Teknaf and surrounding area; consequently the critical and complex situation are more worsen and badly impacted on overall livelihood challenges, increased potentiality of human trafficking and drug abuse by the host community as well as refugee in the camp context.
Under the above circumstances, the project since its journey in May 2020 has covered 26392 populations (of 5250 HHs) with the objective to reduce this vulnerability of the refugee (Camp-25, Alikhali – Teknaf, Cox’s Bazar) and host community (Ward-2, 3,5,6 of Hnila Union, Teknaf Sub-district of Cox’s Bazar) through facilitating the provision of lifesaving WASH service. The approach of the project is an integrated WASH services that includes Safe Water Supply, Sanitation facilities (Toilet, Bathing, Solid Waste and Fecal Sludge Management), Hygiene promotion, Covid respond and distribution of hygiene materials, NFIs and practiced strengthening coordination and cooperation mechanism with the key stakeholders includes District and Sub-District Administration, Department of Public Health Engineering, Refugee Relief and Repatriation Commissioner (RRRC), WASH Sector at local and Cox’s Bazar.
In regards of the provision of Water supply, project has facilitated construction of Rain Water Harvesting, deep tube wells, boreholes to meet the community demand of safe water through a number of tap-stands and pipe networks covering 8000 people. For sanitation facilities, project has supported construction of substantial numbers of twin pit latrines, bathing cubicles, WAH Blocks, drains that directly benefited 19000 beneficiaries. To comply the environment, project has also accommodated the provision of fecal sludge treatment plant and treated 792 cubic metre sludge; for solid waste management activities, processed 30,000 kg of organic waste in a recycle unit that generated 2,700 kg composting fertilizer as by-products so far. For community education and promote hygiene awareness, rigorous monitoring through regular door to door visits and hygiene sessions, , soap/ NFI distribution related activities remains ongoing that resulted a significant change in the livelihood of 26392 beneficiaries in the project locations. The project has been finalized its operational plan and implementation modality based on need assessment, sectoral recommendation and approval of the concern authority.
However, since the project has processed the services at a desired standard level over the period, is at stage of momentum for further gear-up and continuation of the Life Savings WASH services as a priority, sensitive and urgent need of the vulnerable community. NGO Forum, realizing the importance, demand and impact of the inclusive WASH services, wish to continue the services (with a further expansion on livelihood option/ linkage- to address the vulnerability of the host community, since NGO Forum experienced that a substantial number of ethnic community and extreme poor in the host community at Teknaf still remains uncovered by the livelihood opportunity). But unfortunately NGO Forum faces lack of funding (after December2022) to continue the momentum of activities, thereby approaching your support for the purpose.

2. Rationalization
2 - How is your project aligned to the Rationalization Principles (in particular reference # 3 - Adopt an integrated approach, # 4 - Ensure predictability and continuity)?

The goal of the project is to ensure lifesaving WASH service to the vulnerable community of Ward-2,3,5,6 and Camp-25 of Teknaf, under Cox’s Bazar District. The approach of the project is integrated WASH services includes Safe Water, Sanitation facilities (Toilet, Bathing, Solid Waste and Fecal Sludge Management), Hygiene promotion, Covid respond and distribution of NFIs dedicatedly serving both in Camp-25 and Host Community of Hnila Union of Teknaf Upazila. As nature of the programme implementation process, the project ensured coordination and cooperation with the key stakeholders includes DC, UNO, DPHE, CiC, RRRC along with the due consultation of WASH Sector Cox’s Bazar. To educate and promote Hygiene Awareness, regular door to door visits and hygiene sessions, , soap distribution and NFI Kit distribution related activities remains ongoing and has brought a significant change in the livelihood of 26392 beneficiaries in the project areas. To respond coordination mechanism, the project regularly follow-up and incorporates the sectoral recommendations, tracks result to ensure water quality as well as supporting WASH sector in conducting AWD JART response and celebration of different national and international days. All the services delivered were based on the need assessment and sectoral recommendation from host and camp. After attaining proper concern and approval from CIC, RRRC, UNO, DPHE Focals of Teknaf and Cox’s Bazar and the project was finalized its operational plan. During the period there was substantial numbers of internal and external visitors to visit the project activities includes the Govt. officials and donor agency with a highly satisfactory remarks.

3. Cross-cutting Themes
3.1 - Protection

) Separate toilet arrangement for men and women. b) Ensuring safe sanitation facilities especially for women and girls through solar system lights. Addition of MHM Waste chamber in bathroom for women.

3.2 - Gender

a) Ensuring the participation of each gender representation in each forum (stakeholder meeting, user group, WASH committee). b) Assisting disadvantaged groups in achieving gender equity. For example, distribution of reusable sanitary pads for women and bringing men to menstrual related sessions.

3.3 - Age including child safeguarding

Conducting sessions in the presence of parents to protect children's hygiene. Welcoming community feedback by placing girls and boys on WASH committees.

3.4 - Diversity

a) Inclusion in AWD JART Response to contribute more to public health rather than just focusing on WASH activities. b) Select representatives from different levels of the community to take their views and provide feedback.

3.5 - Accountability to Affected Populations (AAP)

Dissemination of all information related to the project to the community, receiving feedback and ensuring accountability by implementing it properly.

3.6 - Does your project have any DRR/DRM elements and/or environmental issues and/or climate change mitigation measures? If yes, please explain.

Yes. The project already contributed to the massive flood that occurred in the last year through implementing an emergency wash relief for the flood affected people at different parts of Teknaf Upazila. Moreover, keeping in mind the climate change and environmental degradation, it always adopts the Sector guidelines in planning its future operational plan as well as its existing plan.
