Project Summary

This intervention aims at providing vulnerable women of households of low incomes in host communities with life-skills acquisition, capacity building on small savings and transfer of resources to start up a POS business to build their resilience, reduce overcrowding at banks and ATM and spread of covid-19 in host communities. In addition, the response will also strengthen access to schools in Madagali and Michika by rehabilitating 15 schools, supply of schools materials.Furthermore, the response will raise awareness campaigns on covid-19 prevention, distribution of PPE.

1 - Needs

The multi-sectoral Assessment conducted by REACH in October-November 2020 in the BAY states through focus groups discussions, key informant interviews and direct observation in Madagali, and Michika, revealed that 40% of wards of Local government Areas of Michika and Madagali experienced a relative security, reason although 67% of IDPs in host communities lack access to livelihoods as results of insurgency and covid-19 out break, vulnerable IDPS carry out incomes generation activities. in madagali, and Michilka, 300 schools lack doors, benches, and some wall fell down as a result of insurgents attacked and military operations, thus 100 schools needs rehabilitation. in addition, it was reported that 45 % of crisis affected IDPs women in host communities lack livelihoods opportunities as a result of displacement and disruption by Covid-19. The lock down of businesses , and periodic opening of banking institutions( Monday and Friday in Adamawa State, created overcrowding at ATM machines and banks with spread of Covid-19; there is need to improve women access to livelihoods opportunities and curb the spread of corona virus by extending banking transactions, through start up of small business of POS machines via transfer of resources to women, life -skills acquisition, awareness creation on covid-19 and distribution of PPE.

2 - Activities

Activity1-1 rehabilitation of 15 dilapidated schools (repair of doors, benches, boards, walls) in Madagali (1-4 months) Activity1-2 supply of school materials (desks, benches, office chairs...) in Madagali, Michika. . After repairs of doors, benches and fallen walls, AHA shall supply schools materials to schools .This shall take place from 5- 8 month.Activity 2-1 Capacity building of women on POS machine( money transfer)Activity 2-2 Distribution of business start up kits.Activity 3-2 transfer of resources ( small grants) as per woman to start up POS business. Activity 3-3 capacity building on savings and scale up techniquesActivity4-1 awareness creation on covid-19 and distribution of PEE

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

To ensure protection of beneficiaries especially girls and youth from sexual exploitation and abuse, AHA shall provide tools and build capacities of her staff on PSEA. in addition, AHA shall provide details ( Name, phone number, email address ) of her protection officer to report any incident of sexual exploitation and abuse. in addition, AHA shall provide a toll free line for reporting any incident of gender-based violence, while suggestion boxes shall also be placed in a secured place in schools for reporting of SEA. However, AHA shall ensure minority groups, youth and women across age and sex inclusion into the response through separated focus groups discussions of 5 to 8 persons each as per sex and gender and age, key informant interviews.
