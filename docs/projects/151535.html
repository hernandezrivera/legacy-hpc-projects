Project Summary

The proposed interventions aims at contributing to the reduction of morbidity and mortality among children under 5; through enhanced access to lifesaving essential health care services and WASH support for women, men, boys and girls vulnerable and marginalized communities from IDPs and rural communities, children and their families in Guri-El and EL-Bur . The health interventions will be directly provided by KAAH in partnership with Gamudug MOH through 4 mobile clinics .
In Guri-El and El-bur, OTP services will be integrated into the mobile clinics to tackle cases of Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM) and mitigate fatality rate among children under the age of 5 due to malnutrition. Furthermore, the proposed interventions aims at scaling up service provision in Guri-El Hospital through support with skilled human resources, provision of essential medical supplies and equipment, and staff refresher training to enable the IDP and rural community access both tertiary and secondary level health care services at the district hospital- which is the main referral facility in the region. The hospital will provide outpatient medical consultation and treatment of common ailments, in patient admission of patient who require admission, Expanded Program on Immunization (EPI) services, Basic and comprehensive obstetric care services, SAM case management and referral of SAM cases that require in-patient care at the stabilization unit run..
In GuriEl and El Bur the proposed intervention will address the basic health and WASH needs of IDP and rural based communities that are hard-to reach, through mobile clinic services that will provide both health, nutrition, and vaccination services to beneficiaries. The project will carry out, construction of latrines, conduct hygiene and sanitation promotion, including distribution of hygiene and dignity kits, support with waste management and disposal both at household level and at the schools, chlorination of water sources.

Needs

A survey undertaken by FSNAU in July 2018 shows; Acute malnutrition >15 percent accompanied by crude death rate >1 percent suggests humanitarian emergency exists among Mogadishu IDPs. Gu 2018 assessment has recorded a GAM rate of 16.7 percent and SAM rate of 4.1 percent indicating Critical nutrition situation, which reflect sustained critical since Deyr (November) 2016. Of major concern is increasing Crude Death Rate (CDR) and Under Five Death Rate (U5DR) because of dwindling interventions targeting the IDPs and increased morbidity particularly AWD/Cholera that partly explain the significant deterioration in this population.According to FNAU assessment results; The Gu 2018 season nutrition assessment amongInternally Displaced Persons (IDPs) in the main settlements and two urban areas shows Critical GAM prevalence in 7 out of 14 population groups surveyed in June: Qardho IDPs (20.7%), Galkacyo IDPs (16.6%), Garowe (17.6%), Mogadishu IDPs (16.7%) and Baidoa IDPs (17.7%). These results solicit not only to continue but also to be scaled up in order to avert the actual poor conditions to deteriorate further .

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

One the main obstacles which may hinder IDP and marginalized communities their access to the facilities, is the lack of participation of the IDPs including from marginalized communities in the design, implementation, monitoring and evaluation of the project. KAAH organization will facilitate systematic participation of representatives of marginalized IDP communities in consultation meetings with local leaders, conditioning the implementation of the project on equal and direct access with all communities, and establishing direct contact with leaders of IDPs from marginalized communities.

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

KAAH is committed to ensure that programs and activities are gender sensitive and take into consideration the gender needs of its target communities and the unique needs and challenges of men, women, boys and girls. By applying humanitarian all humanitarian principles including equality and neutrality, gender imbalance will be avoided. KAAH will serve equally men, women, boys and girls during the project life time in GuriEl and ElBur districts. KAAH adopted a strategy to incorporate the view, opinions and needs of all stakeholders in order to address existing gaps. Through this process KAAH identifies priorities and needs for men, women, boys and girls targeted by this project in terms of integrated nutrition services, as well as informing them the existence of these services through CHW mobilization campaign. The project will reach out to elderly people and to those community members with disabilities by establishing mobile teams. Nutrition Workers will ensure that nutrition needs of women, men, girls and boys are taken into consideration. IYCF promotion will support pregnant and lactating women as well husbands and men and those who influence IYCF behavior, such as grandmothers and mothers in low, so that they contribute to improving practices and changing behaviors. Men will be encouraged to play a role in IYCF promotion.

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

KAAH will pay particular attention to the risk of violence and sexual abuse related to armed conflict in conflict zones. KAAH, with the establishment of a mobile team, will ensure that the hard-to-reach population has access to services selecting areas with relatively safe record. Latrine building in IDP camp will consider separate latrine for male and female, the latrine sites will be illuminated at night time. KAAH will make sure that women, marginalized communities, and other minorities to be part of WASH committees as well any other committees engaged in decision making.
