Project Summary

This project will focus on improving WASH services in Schools and Health Care Facilities (HCFs), to address and adapt to the new Covid-19 normal and build resilience by strengthening local WASH systems and sustaining behaviour change to ensure sustainability.
The hygiene behaviour change component aims to increase knowledge and awareness of hygiene behaviours for prevention of Covid-19 and other water-borne diseases.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

This project is aimed at the scaling-up of WASH services in schools and HCFs in the target areas (Rural and Urban) ensuring sustainability of infrastructure and hygiene behaviours. This will include strengthening local WASH systems and sustained behaviour change to ensure sustainability and service levels. In 2020, WA has conducted a rapid assessment of 15 HCFs in Niassa province, to identify the WASH needs that will inform this project. WA has worked in the targeted districts over the past 3 to 5 years, which provides us this good contextual understanding. However, there are many schools and HCFs that are in dire need of improved WASH services. Where necessary further rapid needs assessments of will be carried out to assess the immediate WASH needs and plan services for upgrade or new construction. A strong hygiene behaviour change component will be implemented to ensure that all beneficiaries practicing Covid-19 related WASH behaviours to protect themselves and their communities. This will also include sensitization of communities to continue construction of sanitation facilities and hand washing facilities at household level. This project will aim to sustain many of the project activities implemented in 2020, including continued support to community-based hygiene promoters and water user committees to promote good hygiene behaviours and water safety.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Scaling up WASH servicesEnsure sustainable continuity by planning for high priority construction and rehabilitation of WASH services of selected schools and HCFs in target areas. Activities include:Provide comprehensive training on O&M of WASH facilities.Construction of inclusive sanitary blocks in schools and HCFs that include ramps, handrails, toilets for people with disability and space for menstrual hygiene management. Create or revitalise chool hygiene clubs on hygiene and sanitation issues. Installation of water supply and water drinking points to ensure safe water availability.Hygiene Behaviour Change:Design and disseminate IEC materials and awareness messages that are inclusive and promote equity, of key hygiene behaviours to prevent the spread of Covid-19. WA aims to work with targeted communtities to increase knowledge and awareness of hygiene behaviours for prevention and precautions against Covid-19 for the most vulnerable and marginalized communities. These activities will include a mass media campaign, radio spots using community radio stations, touch points, door to door outreach visits, use of mobile (Eg. moto valas) campaigning. Distribuiton of menstrual hygiene kits. Provision of hygiene kits (soap and hand sanitisers) to improve hand hygiene in schools and HCFs. Provision of cleaning products to maintain sanitation facilities. Training of hygiene promoters, water user commitees and all partners on hygiene behaviour change.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

WaterAid has policies that are critically important to ensure our high standards in relation to child protection, PSEAH (preventing sexual, exploitation, abuse and harassment) and safeguarding are maintained, and that we are adequately ensuring our procedures are being implemented and adapted where necessary to mitigate increased risk. As with any emergency the Covid-19 crisis does not create new safeguarding and PSEAH responsibilities but instead requires the maintenance, adaptation and strengthening of existing measures.The project will ensure that staff, contractors and implementing partners will sign our Code of Conduct and are trained on safeguarding issues and how to report any misconduct. Beneficiary communities will be made aware of:• WaterAid’s safeguarding commitments • What conduct they can expect from staff and partners• Acceptable and unacceptable behaviours for WaterAid partners and staff members• Available safe and confidential complaints raising mechanisms and those which are child-friendly for children and young people (where relevant)

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

Consultations will be done with affected populations, including specific discussions with women and girls. Promote meaningful female involvement in decisions about the response All hygiene distributions will include items for menstrual hygiene management as per the WASH Cluster hygiene kit standards. Siting of WASH infrastructure will include consultations with women and girls to ensure that these groups feel safe in using these facilities.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

All activities will be under-pinned by the principles of equity and inclusion. All WASH services will be inclusive and accessible to all. Training of HWS operators, community leaders and mobilisers will include aspects of equity and inclusion to ensure that there is a reduced risk of discrimination and stigma. Consultations will be done individuals with individuals living with disabilities and the elderly to identify specific adaptation needs for infrastructure. Infrastructure will be adapted for these needs.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

WaterAid embeds disaster risk reduction in programs and aims to address climate change in our planning and in the differing contexts in which we work. This not only affects the types of programs we implement, but also the types of sustainability plans and measures put into place. This project will consider any environmental impacts and ensure that mitigation measures are applied. For example:- Assessments will be conducted to ensure that water points are located in appropriate sites and do no harm to the environment;- Water quality testing will be conducted to ensure water is safe and appropriate mechanisms in place to maintain water quality; - Quality infrastructure will be deisgned and implemented, incorporating resilience where feasible;- Technology selection will ensure few emissions.
