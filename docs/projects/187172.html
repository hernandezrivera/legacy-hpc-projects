1 - Needs

In the beginning of 2014, there was an increased violence, caused by Boko Haram Insurgent which triggered a massive wave of displacement in the North East. Boko Haram violence is said to have affected over 15 Million and displaced closed to 2 Million people. The level of destruction undermining physical security and livelihoods has instilled deep fears in the affected population, where many have left their homes having loss their loved ones and properties to seek refuge in communities that are less affected and this made it difficult for them to meet their basic daily needs. (Oxfam, 2014 and IRC, 2014). Therefore this project is to improve most vulnerable crisis affected people's access to timely and appropriate food assistance, including fuel and energy related support, to meet their immediate food needs. Demsa, Gombi and Maiha among the Local Government Areas (LGAs) of Adamawa State that host large population of IDPs, which cause the need of this project. In Demsa, Gombi and Maiha the project intends to provide boys, girls, women and Men Returnees and Host communities total number 4335 Persons. (Demsa1515, Gombi 1060 and Maiha1760).
