Project Summary

There is an estimated 2.6 Million displaced people in Somalia. The displaced population coping mechanism have been eroded by droughts flooding, conflict and the outbreak of Covid 19 since March leaving them vulnerable to more shocks. Marginalized clans and vulnerable groups and especially those living with disability are reported to be particularly affected and are at risk of exclusion. The number of individuals suffering internal displacements continue to rise daily and especially in Baidoa. The vast majority of the displaced people in Bay and Lower Juba are facing protracted displacement crisis. The IDPs are currently residing in urban and peri-urban informal crowded settlements, with insufficient access to basic services and poor shelter conditions that raise protection and health concerns. They remain in need of Shelter and NFIs support for protection from harsh climatic conditions, overall improvements to their living conditions and improvement to their chances to access livelihoods and durable solutions. Most of these IDP sites are heavily congested and are in need of de-congestion.it is with this in Mind that WRRS is proposing to intervene and support an estimated 45,600 beneficiaries (12,000 men and women with ESKs),(30,000 men and women with NFIs) and (3600 women and men with Transitional shelters). Beneficiaries receiving NFIs may not receive ESKs.The project will target IDP women, Men, boys and girls who are exposed to risks and other vulnerabilities. Women and girls from the IDPs will be given first priority as they are considered at risk and have no protection mechanism. WRRS will also include establishment of beneficiary feedback and complaint mechanism that will be accessible to all the beneficiaries including those living with disability. Stakeholder meetings will be held and especially on the issue of land tenure.
WRRS will ensure that assistance provided is appropriate and acceptable to the target people which includes the host community and the IDPs in Lower Juba particularly in Kismayo, Afmadow districts by engaging on community consultations before project implementation and beneficiary accountability through participation and feedback
People living with disability will not be left out as in many instances they have been excluded in service delivery. The Berdale districts in Bay region have seen an increased number of new displacements. WRRS will be targeting the newly displaced and settled IDPs that are in urgent need of transitional shelter ESKs and NFIs. WRRS will ensure that coordination is up to date with the key stakeholders being involved in resolving this long term crisis of shelter within the targeted regions. WRRS will consider cash/voucher modality, this will be based on market survey that will be done to ascertain availability of commodities in the market.

What is the justification for using cash or vouchers instead of in kind?

Using vouchers is cheaper than using cash, there are multiple risks associated with cash, and especially bulk cash. Sometime transportation of in kind is cumbersome due to poor roads and also restriction of movements and especially during these times of covid.

Explain how the project design considered market functionality ?

WRRS conducted a market and cash feasibility assessment in the project target area, the assessment was to determine that the traders and their potential to supply the required goods to the beneficiaries. it was determined that the market is able to supply and also that the cash voucher is cheaper than cash as cash posses the risk of beneficiaries to thugs and other forms of violence. Therefore before any distribution market functionality will be given done to determine the modality.

Please provide further explanation

WRRS will facilitate access to durable solutions for IDPs who are willing to locally integrate or return to their place of origin. WRRS will achieve this by providing safe, habitable transitional shelter, site planning, secure land tenure and disaster resilient mainstreaming. As a general approach, WRRS will promote linkages with camp coordination and camp management, and relief and development by promoting local building culture and building back safer, supporting vocational training and ensuring linkages with the private sector. Where markets are functional, cash transfers and voucher delivery modalities will be explored in the shelter and NFIs provision. WRRS will ensure that assistance provided is appropriate and acceptable to the target people by engaging on community consultations before project implementation and beneficiary accountability through participation and feedback. Accountability to affected people will be supported through formation of complaint mechanism and dispute resolution.
