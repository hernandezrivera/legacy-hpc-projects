Project Summary

The project aims at identifying and support vulnerable population in emergency areas in
Puntland exposed to Gender Based Violence (GBV) including children survivors of
violence.
The project tackles both prevention and response realms through a combination of
activities which rely on the consolidated experience in the sector together with long-term
presence of GRT in the area of intervention (identification and case management GBV; referral for mental health care and PSS support ; alternative care; training to workers and community enhancement)

Mitigation Measures

3a. External stakeholder interference in beneficiary selection: Protection concerns stemming from displacement - direct presence and analysis of context in the areas of displacement;- presence of trained focal persons for timely reporting of abuses/concerns;- transparent and periodic discussion with local power holders on protection concerns; - personal trained and supervised;- taking ito account gender-specific needs in the areas of oepration 3d. External stakeholder interference in beneficiary selection: Delivery of humanitarian assistance affects peaceful coexistence and spark intercommunity conflict over access to resources - sensitization of local power holders prior to implementation; - clear identification of vulnerability criteria related to material assistance;- service provision in safe and secure space;- training to staff on principle of impartiality, neutrality;- implementation of GRT code of conduct and protection policy; 4d. Protection mainstreaming: Barriers for beneficiaries to report concerns through complaint and feedback mechanisms - adoption of multiple channels of communication beneficiary- agency ;- safe and anonymous reporting guaranteed;- presence in sensitive and risks areas (i.e. IDP camps) guaranted by trained focal persons;- managing of expectations from early stage;- periodic monitoring of feedback/complaint reporting
