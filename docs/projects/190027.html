Project Summary

World Vision is planning a humanitarian response to COVID-19 and conflict displacement and will carry out implementation of an integrated preparedness and response project in three districts - Meconta, Memba and Monapo - of Nampula Province, that will include provision of water, sanitation and hygiene facilities, dissemination of COVID-19 prevention measures for the most vulnerable people. The project will benefit about 18,317 people including internally displaced people in the target districts.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

World Vision is planning a humanitarian response to COVID-19 and conflict displacement and will carry out implementation of an integrated preparedness and response project in three districts - Meconta, Memba and Monapo - of Nampula Province, covering about 18,317 people. WV proposes to, in partnership with UNICEF, improve access to appropriate WASH facilities (as per WASH cluster standards), disseminate information on safe water, sanitation, hygiene and COVID-19 prevention measures as well as distribute non-food items for the most vulnerable. With these, World Vision expects to prevent the spread of acute diarrhea, cholera and COVID-19 and mitigate its impacts on the most vulnerable people, including the Internally Displaced Persons living in resettlement sites through improved sanitation and hygiene practices.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The following are priority activities to be conducted: • Improve access to sufficient quantity of safe water for drinking through provision of new water points;• Install handwashing stations in public spaces and vulnerable and highly impacted communities/ barrios, including provision of soap;• Facilitate solid waste management (including construction of communal pits as well as collection, removal, disposal and drainage);• Promote self-construction of household latrines and garbage pits (including the provision of sanitation supplies for most vulnerable households);• Promote good sanitation and hygiene practices;• Procure and distribute WASH related Non-Food items, with focus in most vulnerable families / households;• Disseminate WASH related and COVID-19 prevention messages through mass communication media.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

For the sake of Protection/ safeguarding, children at Risk of Vulnerability will be taken priority specially those who live in a: elderly headed households, child headed household, single parent headed household. These groups will be the top priority of this project and to ensure protection/Safeguarding issues are also taken into account whenever anyone comes across them, formal and informal protection structures and stakeholders are going to be consulted on the way vulnerable people and Protection issues are handled in the community. Reporting and referral mechanisms and systems will be also accessed and strengthened to ensure people know how to identify, report and follow-up on Protection/Safeguarding insures properly. A component of Protection /safeguarding will be conducted to all WASH committees to ensure they have all necessary to act as a safeguarding/ protection structure and ensure vulnerable people are safe to participate a make part of the project activities. WVMoz is aligned to the holistic integration of PSEA policy and practice. The protection of sexual exploitation and abuse of children and adults the Organization serves is fundamental to all activities, programs and streams of World Vision (Aid, Development and Advocacy). WVMoz will apply strong accountability mechanisms, distribute PSEA information internally and externally, speaks openly about its integration at office leadership meetings.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

Consulting with communitiesCommunities, through their representatives will be consulted in all response interventions, to contextualize the interventions so to respond to community needs. Consultation activities will include: o Working with community leaders on the most appropriate means and forums for providing information and setting up feedback mechanisms;o Working with community leaders, and WASH committees / partners to identify the most vulnerable households. Promoting ParticipationThese interventions will not be implemented by WV in isolation, communities will be mobilized to support the implementation of the response as follows: o Household members will be used as agents of change in their communities in terms of behavior and attitudes about how to rebuild their lives through training and awareness messages.o Support for each other – households will be encouraged to support each other in the efforts to resettle and rebuild, including supporting most vulnerable households with essentials, ensuring children are protected and cared for.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

World Vision uses the rights-based social model of disability inclusion which recognizes and partners with women and girls, men and boys, elderly persons and persons with disabilities and addresses barriers. Building on World Vision’s Guidelines for Inclusion of Persons with a disability (PWD), and individuals from minority groups all face distinct risks in crisis settings and have unique needs. WV response teams will proactively seek to understand the specific experiences of these groups, and will ensure that the assistance and services are accessible to the most vulnerable. This includes ensuring a gender perspective, disability awareness, and proactive identification and support for individuals with specific needs.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

World Vision is aware of environmental considerations and will act appropriately to implement activities that are not harmful to the environment. The project does not foresee a rapid environmental impact assessment, however, promotion of self-construction of household latrines and facilitation of solid waste management (including construction of communal pits as well as collection, removal, disposal and drainage) will include appropriately sitting of latrines and garbage pits and in an adequate distance from any surface or groundwater source to avoid contamination.
