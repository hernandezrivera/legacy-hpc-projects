Project Summary

The project aims to reduce the mortality and morbidity rate caused by high incidences of water borne diseases outbreak specifically cholera in the Prone and vulnerable communities in Awerial County and Ikotos County through provision of lifesaving safe water supply (new boreholes drilling, repairs and rehabilitations), increased household rural water treatment using Pur/Aquatabs, hygiene promotion sensitization at household and institutional levels, improved safe sanitation practices awareness, and breaking the cholera transmission routes between victims and caretakers/community members by 'contact tracing', disinfection, sensitization, WASH NFIs support and monitoring the gradual progress in hygiene and safe sanitation practices.

Needs

To reduce the mortality and morbidity rate caused by high incidences of water borne diseases outbreak specifically cholera and diarrhea in the identified prone and vulnerable communities.To improve hygiene status, general health and reduce the risk of waterborne diseases among the vulnerable population through hygiene promotion, scaling up improved drinking water source use and consumption and reduce level of open defecation at household levels through safe human waste disposal methods. To ensure the distribution of water treatment supplements and training of undeserved households with safe water sources on safe/proper water chain management to reduce the risk of water borne disease outbreak. Recruitment of local volunteers, training and creation of hygiene awareness messages and sessions will ensure increased practices of appropriate hygiene behaviors.

Activities

The project shall ensure the drilling of 10 hand pump boreholes and repairs and rehabilitation of 30 broken hand pumps/boreholes to serve 10,000 people (Internally Displaced Persons and returnees in Cholera prone areas) with safe clean water, and establish and operationalize their Water Management Committees to ensure the sustainable use and management of water infrastructure. Scale up hygiene promotion activities through the training of 50 voluntary hygiene promoters from within the communities to sensitize on proper hygiene awareness and practices, and ensure the sharing of improved hygiene knowledge and information to ensure households adopt good practices and have improved health.

Protection Risk Analysis?

Yes

Briefly describe how

The WASH specific activities will be designed to target GBV mitigation in collaboration with the protection cluster and through gendered risk assessments. Staff will be trained on Protection and GBV guidelines and communities consulted (dis aggregated by sex/age) before designing a program/project. The WASH Cluster will continue to advocate for high protection standards from partners and will work to appropriately target specific vulnerable groups in order to ensure high protection standards when designing and implementing WASH projects, and during maintenance. WASH infrastructure serves the basic needs of the beneficiaries, however, the use and management of these facilities like water points and latrines shall ensure that rights of human beings/beneficiaries are not deprived or neglected, and the use shall not be limited to anyone regardless of age, vulnerability, disability, gender, etc. However, education on gender and vulnerable groups shall be communicated through the voluntary hygiene promoters and followup done together with community and county leadership.Children rights will also be put into consideration in the access, use and management of the WASH facilities.
