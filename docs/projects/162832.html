Project Summary

The protracted crisis in South Darfur, North Darfur, East Darfur, South Kordofan, West Kordofan and White Nile states and Abyei PCA Area; and Khartoum 3rd June crackdown in addition to 2018 & 2019 accidents, had major social and economic on the region, making a significant number of peoples displaced, causing significant loses, destruction of livelihoods and infrastructure and negative access to public services which affect negatively on girls, boys, women and men. This project propose to enhance the local capacities of conflict affected communities in order to encourage cooperation between community member and resilience, and reinforce community based structures and social services. 8 states and 13 locations will be targeted including Jabal Awliya in Khartoum, Abujebeha, Leri, in South Kordofan. Gerida, Belil, Dimsu in South Darfur, Yassin, Abukarinka in East Darfur, Elliat in North Darfur, Elnehood and Elmeriam in West Kordofan, Eljabalien in White Nile and Abyie PCA like Alshmam ,Alaskar ,Alfaroug ,Umkanashl.
The project intends to have robust community based and non-community based activities that not only target the support to the target beneficiaries but also aims at building community capacity to identify respond to and refer protection issues and to create self-confidence and assertiveness for them to enhance their protection against violence including Gender Based Violence (GBV) and service will be provided in a timely and appropriate manner. The designed interventions in these locations will aim at building capacity ,providing capital ,creating awareness among communities and increase their educational capacity in technical educational vocational training (TEVT). Its tackle general protection plus a very sensitive neglected area of protection that of GBV including SGBV and would like to try innovations and practices to reach out to communities to achieve its goals. enhancing the protection status of women, young mother, EVI, WAR, including PWD and single house headed mothers by reactivating 15 women/ community centers to provide access to quality services which will support the frameworks created and ensure longer term impacts, safety and security for women and girls through advocacy to tackle issues like FGM ,early marriage ,girl child education ,women voices and rights recognition and participation of women in communities. As protection oriented organization, GAH has the sufficient capacity and competences to undertake the proposed activities under this HRP. GAH has well established offices in all mentioned locations and has been implementing protection related intervention since 2012., GAH has solid M&E system, financial system and procurement manual which recognized by UN and international donors. During 2019 GAH has supported more than 150 PLWD with assistive devices, moreover more than 760 children, 450 fostering families received cash support (cash grants, vouchers). This project will work with communities to increase level of service seeking behavior for victims and integrating them in the communities ,empowering women through IGA, crafting entrepreneurship training, literacy classes and filling the gender gap ,and integrating people with disabilities in the community, most importantly to impact behavior change that has reinforced the violation that have continued over time, as the situation with the economic crisis in Sudan has had a great impact on families and has further pushed more people to be vulnerable and this implies protection issues will increase with families(women) sometimes seeking negative coping mechanisms. As situations worsen PSN can no longer be supported with the existing community systems as resources are scarce and thus become destitute (PLWD, Elderly, Vulnerable female headed HH). The target beneficiary is 28,700 (14,700 Female 9,000 Male) Reached through activities 15 WC, 13 locality level CBPN and 180 community awareness sessions.

1 - Needs

According to Sudan Humanitarian Needs Overview (HNO) 2018, the total number of people estimated to be in need of humanitarian assistance in 2018 was 5.5 million. The economic crisis, which is affecting Sudan since the beginning of 2018 and during conflict and protracted displacement situations, women and children take the biggest burnt, in South Kordofan, West Kordofan, South Darfur, East Darfur, North Darfur, White Nile, Abyie PCA and 2019 in Khartoum following the 3rd of June crackdown.Given the general lack and limited protection data in general, most of the information herein is inferred, the data is even more scarce for GBV and information here is based on interactions with the community and other actors , assessments by other sectors, some protection assessments, program reports and mission reports;Inter agency rapid assessment for South Kordofan (Alabassiya, Abujebeha and Alliri) February 2018. inter agency rapid assessment in South Dafur in Marshang in May 2018, ARC Dimso 2018 and in Nyala North January 2019 and 3,000 HH visits in Abyie, GAH running operations in SD,ND, ED, WK & WN, and according to the protection HNO, the protection situation in these areas is dire and immediate action is needed. According to assessments, in SK 60% of the IDPs are women and children where for female headed house hold is above than 40% also, cases of minors and child labor ,SGBV (rape cases) had been listed, with huge harmful practices from early marriage, girls school drop out of girls. moreover, domestic violence which resulted in suicidal of 3 cases of women had been reported by our operational team on the field. Also, in keilak locality and found that there is a noticeable gaps in all sectors, as there is a significant lack in the basic services in all villages. As for protection, 60% of the assessed population were women and children. The lack of protection structures (women centers, CFS, community centers), trained PSS staff and huge gaps in responding to protection matters. huge number of unaccompanied and Separated Children were reported in the assessment and consultations sessions. In addition, children are subjected to recruitment into militias, Community children are prone to/ and facing an increased risk of physical, sexual, and emotional abuse, neglect, exploitation and child labor. Girls are also facing the risks of FGM and early marriage. Vulnerability to exploitation is further increased due to the current economic situation that is increasing the risks and reducing economic independence with fewer families able to cope. In SD, there were new arrivals of returnees and CAR refugees from Mbororo due to internal communal fighting. Indications and feedback from the arrivals confirmed that their movements started in October 2017 and with more arrivals trickling in since then (majority of them being women and children). These locations surveyed lacked access to basic services with limited access to markets, which resulted in limited goods and services supply, compounded by rise of commodities prices and fuel shortage. Women’s financial status force them to work in unfavorable and insecure environments that expose them to violence and protection risks (working in gold mines, collecting wood and water and caring for the family). In all target local there is lack or very limited care given to PLWD , elderly or other PSN. gender discrimination also contributes to the denial of opportunities to basic services such as education that can be a cause or consequence of violence and exploitation against vulnerable women. Social acceptance of the general lack of education for girls in these areas has a huge bearing on low literacy rates.Vulnerability to exploitation is further increased due to the current economic situation that is increasing vulnerability and reducing economic Independence.

5.1 - Impact on environment/natural resources

This proposed project responds to the situation by promoting long term solutions to the needs of IDPs, HCs and SSR. This project intends to achieve this by contributing to the stability and resilience of communities to promote enhancement of community based protection interventions as well as promoting social and peaceful coexisting among the targeted communities. 6 - Protection mainstreaming

4=Fully in place

6.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

Through protection mainstreaming, GAH interventions aim to ensure ease of access by the targeted population to safe space. This approach will be implemented throughout the project in a fair and just manner devoid of discrimination and will take into account challenges of mobility and access by intended target beneficiaries. Additionally, the enhancement of safe and reliable referral mechanisms and more specifically for persons with special needs will be given top priority. In the same breath, to tackle the challenging social cultural barriers affecting GBV survivors from accessing services and assistance, GAH intends to adopt intense and inclusive awareness raising sessions with the involvement of all stakeholders so as to bring to fore and sustain much needed advocacy necessary to addressing GBV related concerns within the communities. through involvement of local partners.

7.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will inform and interact with inter-agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including SEA and abuse complaints?

As an organization that places protection at its forefront to wards program interventions, GAH ensures that all its implemented interventions across all projects applying the principles of offering services with dignity and conflict sensitivity. Through the use of technical skills, and experience to support this project, a complaint and feedback mechanisms will be established to ensure the satisfaction level of the beneficiaries. GAH MEAL team will work alongside program staff through conducting focus group discussions and community meetings aimed at sharing project information with direct and indirect target groups and collecting their views. All feedback and complaints will be logged into a Complaints and Response system, which will then be analyzed and utilized for project management and planning. In addition, GAH continues to apply the PSEA and child protection policy across all of its interventions.This is aimed at ensuring the needs and concerns for affected persons are addressed in a dignified and accountable manner.
