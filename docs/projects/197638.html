Project Summary

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
OVERVIEW :
The on-going “Protracted Armed Conflict” in the North West/South West (NWSW) Regions of Cameroon since 2016/incorporate with recent Cholera outbreak/diarrhoea has resulted in increased displacements of people/lead to lack of micronutrient supplementation programme to children aged 6-23 months, pregnant/lactate women. There is highest death rates of children aged 6-23 months, pregnant/lactate women. As a result 1.4M among the 2.0M people in need of humanitarian assistance in Nutrition BSFP supplementary ration and programmes on neonatal, pregnant/lactate women to reduce malnutrition/deaths will be targeted including to 628K IDP/420K Returnees.

DEMOGRAPHIC INFORMATION : Plan intervention in 06 Subdivisions of 12 villages in rural, urban/semi-urban in Nkambe, Batibo, Mbengwi, Bamenda, Bafut, Wum in 4 Divisions (Donga Mantung, Momo, Mezam, Menchum) in the NW Region of Cameroon. Target population: 10,000 (dissag. by gender, age, disability of (1,712 Boys/1,737 Girls) <18 yrs, (1,702 Men/1,724 Women) > 18 yrs, (900 Men/1,000 Women) +60yrs, PWD (600 Men/625 Women)).
AIM: To improved healthcare feeding to children aged 6-23 months, pregnant/lactate women, with a BSFP supplementary ration and programmes to neonatal, pregnant/lactate women to reduce deaths rate by the end of 30th June 2023.
INDICATORS :
-# of pregnant women and breastfeeding mothers registered in the baby corners/baby tents.
-# of mothers and other caretakers trained on family MUAC activity.
-# of children aged 6-23 months receiving a BSFP supplementary ration.
-# of Pregnant and lactating women receiving a BSFP supplementary ration.
-% of children 6-59 months admitted for SAM management and discharge as cured (Recovery rate)
ACTIVITIES:
-Ensure targeted implementation of activities to promote infant and young child feeding in emergencies for
-Support Community engagement for behavior and social change
-Implementation of micronutrient supplementation programmes for Children under five years
-Implementation of micronutrient supplementation programme for pregnant women and breastfeeding mothers
-Integrated management of severe acute malnutrition (PCIMA-S)
STRATEGIC OBJECTIVES:
-Improved healthcare feeding to children aged 6-23 months, pregnant/lactate women with a BSFP supplementary ration to reduce malnutrition/stunted growth.
-Improved healthcare programmes of neonatal, pregnant/lactate women to reduce deaths rate
-Reinforce Capacities/coordination of local actor to improve family MUAC activity/resilience of affected populations.
BENEFICIARIES:
Affected population (IDPs, returnee’s/host communities) of Infants, Boys, Girls, Men, Women, Elderly people/PWDs of Gender, Age, Disability/Diversity mainstreaming i.e. 50% IDPs, 10% PLWDs, 30% host communities/10% Returnees.
METHODOLOGY: Sensitisations.
STRATEGY OF IMPLEMENTATION:
Through community based approach, awareness raising campaign (door-to-door sensitization)/radio talks, standard visibility/IEC materials, collaboration with stakeholders, community’s mobilisation/engagement. Open space distribution/homes delivery, organized workshops, M&E. IDPs/the communities are consulted/participate in the conception of the entire project cycle as decision-makers/local management/ownership. Appropriateness, relevance/feasible. Rapid HH Survey, FGDs, KIIs, field visit/report from communities, AAP/PSEA. Engage MINSANTE.
SUSTAINABILITY:
Trained (CPCs for the continuos sensitisaton on micronutrient supplementation programmes; family on MUAC activity & Nurses/CHWs on nutrition programmes)
JUSTIFICATION : Lack of BSFP supplementary for is too expensive which will reduce SAM, malnutrition to neonatal/deaths rate. With these BSFP supplementary rarion, it addresses physical/mental well-being, living standards to children aged 6-23 months, pregnant/lactate women. Reduces maternal/neonatal deaths/malnutrition.
