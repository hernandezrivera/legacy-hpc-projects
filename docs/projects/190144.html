Project Summary

Escalated war in several parts of the region has worsened the living condition of displaced communities and creating secondary displacement. According to DTM’s report, an estimated 1.1M IDPs were displaced from active conflict areas to nearby locations in the region While most displaced people are seeking shelter with relatives and friends, forced thousands of IDPs to live in overcrowded collective centres and substandard shelters. Regional authorities also reported that, about 45,000 IDPs were displaced recently from north Shewa and South Wello to Basso woreda, Mekene Selam, Mota and Deber Birhane town which it makes the total IDPs in Region 2.1M and most displaced people are seeking shelter with relatives and friends, forced thousands of IDPs to live in overcrowded collective centres and substandard shelters. Meanwhile, authorities also reported that displaced persons are in urgent need of emergency shelter and basic household items, including kitchen utensils, and further they highlighted a dire situation for newly displaced people. Many displaced people are taking shelter in schools, and host communities still many are forced to stay in the open air, exposing especially women and girls to gender-based violence (GBV), in addition to other health and protection-related concerns. This situation is especially concerning given the current very cold climate in highland areas which threaten to aggravate the suffering of displaced people living without proper bedding or winterization kits. The target beneficiaries for emergency shelter and NFIs are IDPs who are devoid of assets and income. Supply of emergency shelter and NFIs is critical in an emergency response intervention as people move empty handed off their location due to the unprecedented disruption of their normal life due to war. YNSD in collaboration with IOM/RRF will provide emergency shelter and non-food item kits in Ambase Woreda for 1748 HHs (7867 Individuals) affected by war. COVID-19 awareness will be into consider during the distribution of ES/NFI Kits. YNSD will give priority to persons with special needs, such as persons with disabilities or elderly people.
The proposed Emergency relief support will alleviate the suffering of targeted 1748 HHs (7867 Individuals/IDPs) through the provision of emergency shelter and NFIs in Ambasel woreda of the South Wello Zone in Amhara Region region. This will restore their dignity. Families with severe damage, most vulnerable like children, persons with disabilities, girls/ women and the elderly, and those live in pocket areas for intervention will be prioritized during selection process of the beneficiaries. This project is designed to benefit IDPs who lives in communal sites and overcrowded shelters with highest risk of infection, transmission of communicable diseases including COVID-19 and different forms of violence and abuse. YNSD’s ES/NFI kit distribution will be in harmonizing with other aid providers, as coordinated by the cluster. As one of the critical approaches of emergency response mechanisms, YNSD will maintain COVID-19 prevention strategies both for its own staff and for those whom we are intending to serve throughout the course of the emergency response.
Henceforth, the reason for this potential partnership between YNSD and IOM is aiming at the provision of key supplies of non-food item kits and Emergency shelters in Ambasel Woreda of South Wello Zone of Amhara region. YNSD’s ES/NFI kit distribution will be complementary with other organizations providing shelter support, as coordinated by the cluster.
