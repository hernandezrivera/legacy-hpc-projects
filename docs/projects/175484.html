1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

This project is aimed to implement and improve the case management system in order to identify and manage cases of vulnerability among children and women in Pemba, Ibo, Metuge and Montepuez districts. The main objective is to strengthen the case identification system and the coordination between the response services, involving all the competent local authorities and creating a close link with the case workers. They will be also able to provide protection and psychosocial support services for children, adolescent, women, caregivers and communities.AVSI will rely with its long experience on urban planning on support the IDPs on the HLP assistance based on indication of the local authority. AVSI is already working closely with DPGCAS and SDMAS, using the same identification and reference forms, involving staff in training and holding monthly meetings to coordinate the referral system. This approach is aimed at sharing the work with the local authorities, in order to support and allow them to carry on after the end of the project. At the end of the project the trained activists will be made available for community and might be supported by the district departments of Acçao Social and Education. The Action we look to target 40.000 beneficiaries: 10.000 of GP, 15.000 of CP, 10.000 of GBV and 5.000 of HLP

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

AVSI will be engaged in creating a protected and safe environment, in particular in an emergency context, focusing on the improvement of child well-being and family support through case management and provide access to basic services. Animators/facilitators groups are aimed at creating a protected family environment: providing psychosocial support in emergency and case management are two key actions to be planned in all the interventions. Moreover, this project will be implemented jointly with the AVSI-UNICEF child protection programme. Case Management activities allow the identification and management of the most vulnerable cases. Through a mapping of the districts, AVSI is able to have a clear picture of households and their residences.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

AVSI will provide information to women, girls, boys and men in affected communities about the organisation, the principles it adheres to, how it expects its staff to behave, the programmes it is implementing and what they intend to deliver so that communities and people affected by crisis are fully aware of the expected behaviour of humanitarian staff, including organisational commitments made on the prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse. (CHS 4.1 and 5.6). AVSI’s staff will collect feedback in appropriate ways (e.g. groups are segregated by sex and age, confidential where necessary) and make sure the analysis of the feedback is disaggregated by at least age and sex at minimum so that there can be comparisons of the issues according to roles and norms. Complaints will be managed in a timely, fair and appropriate manner that prioritises the safety of the complainant and those affected at all stages and refer complaints that do not fall within the scope of the organisation to a relevant party in a manner consistent with good practice (CHS 5.7). AVSI will, at all time, assess the implementation of the Core Humanitarian Standard (CHS) and other collective standards including the IASC AAP commitments, Gender Equality Programming, Minimum Operating Standards on PSEA; and the Best Practice Guide to establish Inter-Agency Community-Based Complaint Mechanisms (CBCM) and its accompanying Standard Operating Procedures through self-assessments, peer review, third-party verification or certification.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Inclusive activities are mainstreamed in all AVSI projects. Animators and Case workers are trained on safe identification and referral and according to the needs, specific topics are tackled concerning physical or mental disability. In some cases, special educators are hired to provide continuous support and mentoring to teachers on the inclusion of children, from needs assessment to progress monitoring, as well as coordinate the work of specialized service providers. Activities can be implemented in different context (non-formal, formal, or informal). In different projects we engaged also specialized services – speech therapy, psychology, psychomotor therapy, mobility assistant for the visually impaired children, etc both for the assessment of child’s needs and for the provision of support services. Referral pathways are always mapped and shared. The engagement of parents for these activities, in the parental meetings to discuss the child’s progress and problems is fundamental.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The proposed activities will adopt an environment-friendly approach by trying to increase awareness in the affected communities. With a strong community engagement and participation; on rehabilitation activities, specific attention regarding the site selection and sourcing of materials, favouring the use of local resources, like the locally sourced building materials, sun-cured soil bricks produced in affected communities and the limiting of the use of timber wherever feasible, for all rehabilitation and construction. WASH activities will be managed with a special care for the selecting sites and constructing latrines to ensure that there is no overflow or infiltration into ground water, a safe access to potable and clean water, and proper location of sanitation facilities, that will ensure that environmental harm is reduced or eliminated and environmental benefits are maximized and increased at the same time proctetion issues. Finally, health and hygiene education will have a component of key messages regarding the environment.
