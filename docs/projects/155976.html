Project Summary

Through the assessment of needs, and the emergence of many psychological problems, social and behavioral for children in schools and the poor professional efficiency of teachers and poor parenting with their children were prepared these activities:
– Training the project team on psychosocial support mechanisms.
– Provide psychosocial support activities for children through the moving and psychosocial support team.
– Provide with child management services.
Distribution of awareness brochures for psychological support for children.
To achieve the following objectives:
Raising the level of health and psychological prevention of children in the area (Western Aleppo countryside Darat Izza) By achieving the following objectives (goals and final impacts):
• Reducing mental stress among students and raising their level of prevention through the mobile psychosocial support team and through the centre itself.
• Raising the level of awareness among parents, teachers and the community that is surrounding children and their psychological problems.
• Reduce the effects of psychological stress on the low of education level and educational problems for students.
• Overcoming children trauma disorders and their effects by providing child management services at the school.
