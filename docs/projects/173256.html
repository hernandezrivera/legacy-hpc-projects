Project Summary

The Intervention aimed to reduce gradually the displacement-linked needs and vulnerabilities of displacement-affected communities Sharg Eljabel (Kadgir, Beli Elsreaif and Dog Omda villages), Merching (Amaar Jadeed, Mawashi and Tegay villages) and Belail (Belail, Isma and Nangi villages) localities. The Intervention aimed to strengthen their capacities, skills, and increasing their resilience, so that displaced persons can increasingly enjoy their human rights without discrimination based on their displacement. The total target is (37, 000).
The intervention is focus on support of the target groups with Shelter and NFIs assistance in addition to protection assistance. The intervention is to respond to the needs of the targeted vulnerable groups. PODR will continue to conduct needs assessment in coordination with other partners to have thoughtful understanding on the gaps and change and to prioritize their needs and to assess the level of damages in houses affected. As of PODR’s physical presence and the assessment conducted, the target communities are in desperate need of shelter and NFIs to improve the way of life and to build their resilience. In order to have effective and efficient response to the needs. The intervention will work to mitigate the negative impact of hazards, and ensure preparedness to the target groups through trainings of the community members on shelter construction techniques, prepositioning of life-saving NFIs namely Jerry cans, blankets, Cooking sets and plastic sheets. PODR will work closely with the Shelter and NFIs Sector to enhance Shelter-NFI response through effective coordination, timely information sharing and reporting

1 - Needs

The needs are (1,100) shelter (800 out of them is transitional while the 300 shelter is emergency shelter) to provide adequate security, privacy, and maintains possessions to (5,500) people targeting (14) locations in (3) localities (Sharg Eljabal, Belail and Merching) in South Darfur State. Moreover, (800) NFIs kits (Jerry cans, blankets, Cooking sets, plastic sheeting and plastic mats) other IDPs' basic needs. The overall number of people in need of Shelter and NFIs and protection are (9,500) who are potentially affected by Risk of hazards including armed conflict, tribal clashes and flood. Generally, Shelter and NFI assistance is required for Internally Displaced People (IDPs) and returnees. Pregnant and lactating women, chronically ill people, elderly, and physically disabled are particularly vulnerable amongst all affected populations who, in addition to their NFI needs, are highly vulnerable without shelter to protect them from the elements and remain highly exposed to further health risks. Moreover, training in Shelter building and construction techniques is part of the need to act as IGAs for some of the target groups.In Darfur, camps have been established as early as 2003, and de facto became suburbs of the towns and villages they adjoin. Furthermore, those who chose to remain face complex issues regarding land rights that often prevents them from constructing more durable shelter, among other protection risks identified by the Protection Sector. With the reduction in armed conflict, there has been some increase in returns, but this remains limited. UHCR and IOM’s SNFI team, in line with Cluster policy, responds solely based on need. Hence, the intervention meant to be implemented with very close contact with them. Awareness on of Covid-19 preventive measures will be applied to reduce the risk the target can face. The protection will be mainstreamed through the implementation of the project

5.2 - Impact on environment/natural resources

The emergency shelter will be established based on the standards and quality proposed by the agency (IOM and UNHCR) to avoid any harms that accompanied the intervention. PODR committed to integrate environmental considerations into programs wherever possible and to be compliance with applicable international requirements. Building on lessons learned from partners engaged in the provision of transitional shelter, the strategy will promote adherence to agreed standards and best practice by all sector partners and ensure effective links with other sectors including WASH, and protection. This will include integration of agreed on cross-sectorial priorities such as protection, gender, and environment. The assessment, planning and implementation of shelter and NFI activities will consider the impact of activities on the environment and will aim to mitigate the impact through ensuring synergy with afforestation activities. The materials and construction methodology will be defined, selected and adopted to prevent over-exploitation, pollution and degradation of environmental conditions. Moreover, PODR will conduct training on risks reduction and emergency management

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

Protection concerns and other needs of the most vulnerable will be taken into consideration while determining the operational modalities of each response, and the specific items provided. The needs of women, men, girls and boys, in addition to Persons with Special Needs (PSN) and other vulnerable groups in line with UNHCR protection criteria are prioritized in all shelter and NFI interventions. Shelter/NFI and protection responses are closely intertwined, notably the principles of Do No Harm. protection activities will mainstream across all affected populations, highlighting the needs of EVI, elderly and people with disabilities. selection of beneficiaries among protracted IDPs and returnees with the involvement of community; liaising with local authorities to obtain their support and agree on selected locations and beneficiaries; procure and transport locally sourced shelter materials to distribution locations; distribute shelter materials to identified beneficiary households and train community members in the construction of appropriate shelters.The project will be implemented with in impartial and non-discriminatory ways that promote the safety, dignity and integrity of the people receiving assistance. The activities will be undertaken to safeguard people from violence, coercion, deprivation, and discrimination and aimed at obtaining full respect for the rights of the individual. The protection mainstreaming principles of dignity and respect for the population and the different protection elements including avoiding causing harm and prioritize safety and dignity, ensuring meaningful access, practicing accountability and promoting participation and empowerment will be incorporated during the assessment, implementation and monitoring phases.The following issues has to be considered during the assessment and impelimantation:Demographics of the target group collected in a disaggregated form, focus group discussions have been held to discuss any shelter and NFI needs or concerns, including privacy and design features to allow for dignity and comfort, separate focus group discussion spaces have been provided for specific groups (gender, age, disability) as necessary, consultative meetings separately held with women and girls, men and boys. Security-related issues have been identified (e.g., threats against certain groups preventing the construction of shelters or NFI distribution) and physical hazards that could pose dangers in the construction of shelters or in the NFI distribution locations have been identified and mitigation of these risks has been considered
