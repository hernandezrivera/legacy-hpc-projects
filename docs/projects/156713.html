Project Summary

The project’s role is to distribute cattle on most vulnerable families aiming at building animal assets and establish income operating opportunities in north of Syria as per the following:
• 100 cows distributed to 100 families to produce milk.
as normally the family has 5 people as average then the direct beneficiaries will 500.

Needs

As per draft HNO 2019:Animal resources in Syria suffered a lot in 2010. By the decrease of cattle’s numbers (from 30 to 40% for cattle, sheep, goats and cows). By the year 2017, the number continued to decrease as cows reached 18%, sheep 23% and goats 20% in addition to illegal export of producing women cows. Moreover, vaccines and routine medications to cure animal disease cross border is about to finish in Syrian Veterinary Services. This lead to lack of required medications in markets. Cattle owners are obliged to use medications of low quality, which has unknown side effects.1- Nearly 9 million citizen who lives inside Syria need humanitarian aids including food, water and medical care.2- Support Syrian people to be independent and their ability to endure via creating and supporting make a living opportunity. Moreover, small projects must be supported, ability of production must be improved and linking them to value chain via supporting community services markets and economic infrastructure. Also, supporting production assets and veterinary care at once in conjunction with providing food assistance.3- Many families were obliged to abandon framing or shepherding or decreasing production due to unavailability of inputs and prices increase like seeds and fertilizations. Also, lack of fuel, land and water is one of the most important obstacles.And as per the same report, one from every 3 person in Syria suffer from lack of food security. Also as per the same report, lack livelihoods sustainability or filling the gap between emergency aid and long-term aid will result in increasing of unemployment rate, economic dangers, and depend on aids, especially most vulnerable men and women.In addition, as per humanitarian needs overview, food security and livelihoods assessment indicates that families participated in crop production or cattle growing whether it is a principle resource or a secondary one, earned more money and achieved high levels of food security. Despite beneficial effects to continue agricultural and animal production. Many families were obliged to abandon framing or shepherding or decreasing production due to unavailability of inputs and prices increase.This project is a part of second strategic target of food security sector within humanitarian response plan.

Protection Risk Analysis

Risk of protection: There is a risk of discrimination if services are provided to specific groups or individuals and others are excluded. Probability: Average Impact: High Mitigation measures: 1. Ensure that there is a map showing the locations of the beneficiaries who are in real need of assistance so that communication with beneficiaries is proactive and based on knowledge. 2. Communicate with local councils to understand the views of the community 3. Educate the beneficiary community so that the person who does not receive the service knows that it is not needed and that there are beneficiaries who are more in need of it. Risk of protection: extortion Probability: Average Impact: High The employee responsible for the distribution of aid extorted women or children even men for personal needs before handing over aid or providing service to them and exploitation of the situation of disability and need. 1. Inform staff that this is contrary to regulations and laws and educate women and children in need to tell officials directly about any cases of extortion or harassment. 2. Use M & E and questionnaires mobilized by beneficiaries to control distribution and non-exploitation of those in need.
