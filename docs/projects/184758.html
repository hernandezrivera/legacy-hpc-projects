Project Summary

The Impact of collapse of state, decades long civil war, break down of law and order, accompanied by droughts, floods and displacement have created unending humanitarian catastrophe in Somalia. Currently, drought is in the making which could result in humanitarian crises and famine if enough rains are not received. Weak law and order and lack of physical protections for displaced population in the country allows widespread violations and abuses.
This Project will support IDPs, returnees and host communities affected by conflict, floods and drought through Cash and Voucher Assistance.The project will target HLP rights and access awareness trainings for women, youth, elders, religious leaders selected from IDPs, returnees, Host communities, minorities as well as NGOs and local authorities and disabled members of the communities. The project will support the different communities to set up a dialogue mechanism to facilitate coexistence between the communities and address issues that negatively affect the HLP of IDP's ,returnees and host communities. Women, youth and PWD's will be included in the forum that will provide a link between the communities, local authorities.
Households from IDPs, returnees, minorities, affected host communities, the disable will receive cash assistance to meet their daily needs, pay rent, rehabilitate flood damaged houses/shelters and help integrate into the host communities by establishing livelihoods. Beledweyne in particular and Hiran in general host large IDP population. Protection risk such as GBV, CP, HLP, exist among IDP populations in Beledweyne. Women and girls experience GBV due to their vulnerability in the IDP settlements. Cash and Voucher Assistance will target these groups and help improve their living conditions. IDPs are marginalized and seen as unwanted encroaches of land. Being from minority clans and living in poverty, they are twice discriminated and left behind in forums where their issues are discussed. Their poverty and being from minority clans denies them a voice and strips them of their dignity, the little left after the indignity of displacement.
The cash transfer will support them in bringing some dignity to them by empowering them make independent decisions to purchase commodities that will meet the needs in their homes or even do minor improvements.
In Addition, Lack of adequate humanitarian assistance in Hiran has been existing for a long time. IDPs, returnees, minorities, and vulnerable communities (specifically, children, women, elderly, disable) have limited access to humanitarian assistance because of inadequacy and limited interventions. They lack adequate housing and live on land they have no ownership, this exposes them to risks of violence including GBV and destruction of their property. Since they lack recognition and a forum to pursue legal channels for recognition and abitration in cases conflict, their marginalization, insecurity and indignity intensifies. Their occupation of land where they have built shelter has no legal framework in some cases. To bring dignity to their HLP and protect them, this project will develop a forum to negotiate with local and national government for a framework of recognition of their HLP rights hence secure their HLP and improve dignity and security especially to the marginalized groups within the IDPs and returnees. This project will give special consideration to marginalized communities. This project will also target all excluded and disadvantaged communities.

2.1.1 - What is the justification for using cash or vouchers instead of in kind?

The project will contribute to nutrition and food security helping in resilience of the vulnerable people affected by natural disasters in this case flooding and conflict. Reducing life cycle risks through CTP and vouchers the project will contribute to the protection of beneficiaries. The cash and vouchers will be used in Beletweyne this supporting the economy of the host community and enhancing the livelihoods and resilience of the community especially the business community and their families.

2.1.2 - Explain how the project design considered market functionality?

Beletweyne is a bustling economic hub of Hiraan and the food and NFI market is stable. Agrocare is headquartered in Beletweyne and has previously implemented NFI distribution and other projects that source items from the local market. We also did a survey of the market and relating it to our experience and confirmed the stability of the market and prices.

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

2Violence and displacement in Hiraan as in Somalia in general usually takes a clan-based paradigm that also defines the violence and vulnerability in case of flooding. Through government officers and local elders, the project will prioritize IDP’s from minority clans who lack local social buffers. From the identified minority clans the gender, age, and disability populations identified above will be identified to participate in the project. Women-headed households, households with teenage girls and babies will be included to mitigate the risks of GBV.
