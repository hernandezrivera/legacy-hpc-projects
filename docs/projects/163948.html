Project Summary

This project is a hope to improve the lives of more than 55872 IDPs (Men: 9312, Women:
9915, Boys: 17946, Girls: 18699) stayed camp cluster in northern Idlib in the last six
months, which poses an additional burden on the area.
The project targeted existing camps to improve the infrastructure and expanding or establishing new camp, The targeted 55 self-settled camps are either an extension for pre-established camps or new
camps established by the IDPs in haste to find a place to stay and start a new life, the
camps were added in ISIMM database, and the project will establish an effective camp
management system to support the humanitarian efforts and organize the work by an
effectively.
The camps are the last resort of the people left their homes and assets searching for a
safer place away from the danger to the lives of their children, women and the elderly.
The living conditions in camps are riddled with a lot of difficulties resulted from the lack
of safe places and the poor experience of those people to organize their new living
conditions including finding education sources and alternatives for their children.
The rapid establishment of these camps led to its lack of the simplest elements of the
adaptation to the rocky and mountainous surrounding environment and the absence of
the protection factors from life and dignity threatening the IDPs such as the complete
darkness at the nights, unpaved roads and the high possibility of the flood of rainwater in
the winter.
SECD is aiming by the support of the donors to put an end to the threats to 55872 IDP’s
lives and dignity by implementing complementary activities to improve the management system of targeted camps and as part of the contingency and emergency plan SECD will do infrastructure support, ensure provide protection for the women, children and elderly with
focusing on the inclusion mainstreaming for the people with disabilities through the
following key activities:
- Land leveling and graveling for approximately 52,495 M of the main and sub roads
inside the camps to improve road conditions and access to the WASH, and education and
other facilities
-Improving tent insulations for 4000 tents for current residents who suffer the risk of
floods and soil erosion. By using an environmentally friendly insulation method.
-Establish ramps for the elderly and people with disabilities to facilitate the entrance of
insulated tents.
-Installation of 670 solar light poles to light the key access roads and facilities.
-Establishing camp management and training them on the necessary knowledge to
-Establish a 6750 M rainwater drainage system to improve the living and hygiene
conditions of the IDPs.
Establishment Camp Management system
- Forming in camp committees
-CCCM training, including on the job
-Contingency planning (fire fighting system, evacuation plan, etc)
-Prepare a fire response plan in the targeted camps with establishing of 181 fire
-Extinguishing points and training for a fire safety plan.

4 - Needs

A further intensifying of the crisis in the north-west and north-east Syria could result in significant levels of displacement to already fragile areas, with little capacity by humanitarian partners to respond appropriately, due to funding, situational and access constraints resulting in significant numbers of IDPs without shelter or basic means of survival. At the same time, both IDP and refugee returns are expected to continue increasing in 2020. 75 percent of IDPs have suffered multiple displacements and about one in three have been displaced four or more times. These high levels of multiple displacements are typical of IDPs in north-west Syria, who have likely been moving from onethe place to another in response to extremely high levels of conflict.In November 2019 and January /2020 SECD team conducted a field visit by its field engineers who discussed with key stakeholders at the targeted camps the activities which will focus on the improvement of the sites infrastructures and provide the protection for the most vulnerable people including the people with disabilities, women and elderly in an inclusive approach and to consider their concerns regarding the current situation and the coming winters with the high odds of repeating the suffering of the last winter which caused the flooding of the rainwater and the damage of tents, soil erosion and mudslides, highlighting the need for tent for site improvement activities including tents insulation such as graveling.The Concerns of the women and the girls were the access to WASH facilities in the evening and since the camps are not lit, on the other hand. During the visits and the preparation of the technical studies, the poor management skills of the representatives of the camps were evident and as per the CCCM cluster directions, the rational management of the camps is a fundamental factor to facilitate the humanitarian interventions and to identify the needs of IDPs and deliver it to the concerned humanitarian a

5 - Implementation of activities

All the activities will be conducted by the technical staff of SECD, including thevolunteers. Regarding the procurement process, SECD will commit to the procurementpolicy to select the vendors who will only supply the commodities from the local marketsif available. The distribution and installing activities and the workshops will be performedby the technical staff of SECD. SECD approach in the implementation is conductingconsultation with beneficiaries through meetings with their representatives in localcouncils and public meetings will be done regularly before the start of implementation tofinish. - Since the inception SECD, the organization has used a two-pronged approach forproject implementation. As a major part of the SECD strategy is to support theempowerment and institutionalization of the camp management and other coordinatingbodies, SECD has implemented projects through these bodies on the ground. The secondapproach for project implementation has been the use of direct implementation, workingthrough the SECD team inside, but in close coordination the aforementioned actors. Forthose reasons, SECD has a proven track record of successful project implementationemploying both approaches. Implementation for this project will be completed throughSECD. 7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

ECD conducts a security assessment for its projects by receiving information onsecurity situations from its network of local councils and civil society activists, as well asby remaining continuously apprised of security situations through bulletins released bythe international community. For emergency relief projects, when a security situationarises that threatens the safety of staff, the staff is redirected to a nearby, safer locationuntil the area is safe enough to conduct the aid or distribution project. For non-reliefprojects, an assessment of the security situation is conducted in order to determine thelocation of the project.
