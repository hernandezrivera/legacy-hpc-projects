Project Summary

the project aims to support the outreach activities and raising awareness the communities regarding proper IYCF practices covering the exclusive breastfeeding, complementary feeding, continuing of breastfeeding up to two years in addition to support the OTP services to treat the MAM and SAM without complications in two health facilities. SIMRO will coordinate with the UNICEF to receive the required supplies to manage the cases. After the CHWs will screen the cases at the community level and detect the cases they will refer the SAM and MAM cases to the PHC to recive the supplies and then the CHWs will follow the cases at the community level
These CHWs will conduct HH visits targeting PLWs and children under five years to screen them on acute malnutrition and refer the cases who are in need to treatment and educate them regarding proper nutrition practices. the CHWs will conduct orientation sessions at the community level to support the good communities knowledge and correct the misconception between the IDPs and affected population.
the project will support the OTP services in Idleb city and Dadikh community by supporting the two GP and 4 nurses.
the CHWs and the project staff will distribute the IEC materials for targeted beneficiaries.

Needs

According to the HNO 2019 Number of children under five: 34266 (Boys: 18000Girls: 16266)Number of PLW: 15715 The results from of a Knowledge, Attitudes and Practice (KAP) survey on IYCF from March 2017 conducted by cross-border Nutrition partners in parts of Aleppo, Idleb and Hama governorates indicated prevalence rates of exclusive breastfeeding were at 30.9 per cent (25.3 per cent - 36.8 per cent, 95 per cent CI), prevalence rates for early initiation of breastfeeding were 37.8 per cent (34.6 per cent - 41.0 percent, 95 per cent CI) and the minimum acceptable diet was achieved for 57.3 per cent (53.4 per cent - 61.1 per cent, 95 per cent CI) of those surveyed. SIMRO conducted rapid need assessment in Idleb governorate, Saraqb sub district and Idleb city in 25-J-2019. This assessment included interviews with lactating women randomly covering 50 lactating women with child less than 6 months.And 50 women who have children more than 6 months. The assessment aims to assess the exclusive breastfeeding and continuing of breastfeeding up to two years.The results were as follow:In Dadikh 20 lactating women with child under five years 25 % exclusive breastfeedingAnd other have not exclusively breastfeedingIn Idleb governorate 30 lactating women 30 % of them have exclusive breastfeeding and other have not exclusively breastfeedingRegarding the contentious breastfeeding up to two years The results were as follow:In Dadikh 20 women with child less than two years 20 % of them have continued breastfeeding up to two years. The rest are notIn Idleb city 30 women with child less than two years 30 % of them have continued to breastfeed up to two years and the rest did not have this practiceAdditional interviews have been conducted with key informants in Idleb city and Dadikh communityThose Key informants insisted of the importance of raising awareness the communities regarding proper CIYCF practices and to support OTP services in Dadikh PHC and Idleb city.All these facts showed the importance to support outreach activities in targeted areas by supporting the mobile team to raising awareness the communities regarding proper CIYCF practices and to establish the OTP centers

Implementation of activities

conduct HH visists to screen the children under five years and PLWs regarding acute malnutritionconduct raising awareness sessions at the community level and distribute the IEC materialsSAM and MAM services will be strengthened at two health facilities conduct the c-IYCF training Lead mother(s) is (are) enhanced capacity in counselling of community members and disseminating of C-IYCF recommended practices through mother-to-mother support groups.All agreed nutrition programme supplies are dispatched to the targeted health/nutrition facilities.

Protection Risk Analysis

In effect, SIMRO will do all its best to prevent and minimize as much as possible any unintended negative effects of this grant by Preventing Sexual and Exploitation Abuse through explain the humanitarian principles for the project staff and encouraged the health partner to oversee the beneficiaries satisfaction and do like separate interviews with the key informants and medical staff and beneficiaries to ensure that there are no abuse or violation within the provision of nutrition services.all targeted beneficiaries will have equality access to nutrition services regardless the ethnic, religious or believes. SIMRO will arrange to train the CHWs on PFA to mitigate any physical and psychosocial risks may occurred due to the pressure of work and complexity of Syrian context.SIMRO central team will put a complains box in two health facilities which will subject to the management of MEAL team who will open this box and send the content to the MEAL team Gaziantep, the MEAL will discuss with program manager to try solve any problem may occur. Any suggestion produced by PLWs will take into account and subject to discussion with program manger and health facility management. Awareness session with the local communities leaders will conduct by SIMRO field team to explain the scope of work and kind of nutrition services provided to avoid any misunderstanding between the local communities and health facility staff.
