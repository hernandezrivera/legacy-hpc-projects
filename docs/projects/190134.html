Project Summary

This emergency ESNFI response project will be carried out by OWDA in Adadle woreda, Somali region. The project will last nine months and will address the critical needs of vulnerable groups in the planned woreda, with a focus on drought IDPs and some vulnerable host communities. With a total budget of $300,000, the project will support 12,599 IDPs and 1,816 (14% of the target population) hosting communities in Emergency ESNFI interventions. ESNFI in kind receives 58% percent of the budget and in cash will be 19%, the project will address PWD ,gender mainstreaming ,PSEA and AAP and allocate 10% of the budget. The primary goal of the project is to provide access to ESFI. Conduct Market assessment, protection mainstreaming will be carried out for the beneficiary communities in order to support and ensure the IDPs have the necessary ESNFI. The project will provide emergency shelter kits to 2,403 IDPs, conduct hygiene awareness campaigns, and provide ESNFI kits to the most vulnerable IDPs and host communities.
