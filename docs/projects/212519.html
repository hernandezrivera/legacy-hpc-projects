Project Summary

The project aims to provide Syrians with the opportunity to improve their quality of life. The destruction left by the war besides EQ, additionally to the lack of safe homes contributed to displacing children and families to different areas. Therefore, the existence of a safe home became the main goal. All these indicators led us to highlight the urge of rehabilitating building, apartments & commercial shops in Aleppo & Rural Damascus based on ST previous experiences within the mentioned areas noting that the majority of community residents are returnees.
Aleppo was considered as one of the EQ most affected areas, while Rural Damascus was classified as one of the most destroyed areas due to war.
Bostan Al Basha neighborhood, situated in the northern part of Sulaymaniyah- Aleppo governorate, is a socially & economically active area, covering 0.78 km² with an estimated population of 16,623 individuals. There are 3,272 families, 22% of them are hosting community. An assessment conducted at the end of 2023 on the economic situation revealed the presence of 3,236 economic units in Bostan Al Basha, among them 528 operational units (149 commercial shops, 379 artisanal workshops) while 2,708 units have been closed, mainly due to the business owners’ absence & the challenging economic conditions caused by inflation & lack of infrastructure.
Al-Khafsa district is located in the north-east of Aleppo, it belongs to Manbaj area and contains an important water station pumps that supplies water to all Aleppo areas. Its re-operation has encouraged many families to return.
Harasta is one of the biggest districts in Douma’s area, Rural Damascus governorate, containing a huge space of agriculture lands.
The project contributes to achieving the Cluster’s Shelter strategic objectives, by improving housing conditions for the most vulnerable people within the targeted areas in both Aleppo and Rural Damascus:
Aleppo: Repair of structural damage of 50 buildings and Rehabilitation of damaged 400 apartments in Bostan Al Basha, Implementation of minor repairs to damaged 60 houses at Al Khafseh.
Rural Damascus: Rehabilitation of damaged 75 apartments in Harasta.
During the rehabilitation of apartments, we will secure a safe space for beneficiaries, whether children, women and vulnerable groups, and by providing lighting for the surrounding buildings, we will provide safety for the local community as a whole by rehabilitating apartments and taking into account the groups that have special needs, as they will also contribute to their support in general. This will reflect on community and spread public awareness and perhaps urge the local community to take initiatives and encourage individuals to return to those areas and contribute will expand to raise awareness within the community and achieve the optimal image of social solidarity in supporting causes.
The main objectives include providing safe shelter for nearly 535 families, ensuring a sustainable income source by rehabilitating 50 shops, & aiding survivors in recovering lost professions through operational grants. The project also seeks to provide safe residence for EQ & war survivors, alleviate financial burdens caused by rentals, and create job opportunities.
Key performance indicators & outputs include the number of returnees, rehabilitated & activated shops, accepted beneficiaries opening their projects, & reinforced buildings.
The project's evaluation & monitoring involve assessing progress, adhering to timelines, utilizing performance indicators, & submitting periodic reports. These reports will detail project progress, challenges faced, & encountered obstacles on weekly & monthly basis.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Occurrence of tension between host communities, IDPs, and returnees. Syria Trust consulted community members and key stakeholders when designing the project to define the selection criteria, which will be clarified, and disseminated by trained staff along with the project objective, activities, and any needed documents. Lack of accepted documentation may unfairly exclude certain beneficiaries. STD will ensure that project information, selection criteria, and documents required are accessible to all without feeling discriminated against or excluded through information sharing and complaint mechanisms during the project cycle. Trust has very good coordination whether with MOLAE to discuss accepted documentation and alternative to ensure no exclusion occurs in collaboration with our LR Program.Distribution staff commit any acts of discrimination, harm/violence, GBV or exploitation...etc. The Trust will ensure that the project-recruited staff is well-trained on PSEA & GBV concepts with good communication skills. To avoid SEA and harassment risks that might arising during the registration and selecting phase by elaborating awareness sessions facilitated by our CCs aim to spread protection concepts, particularly related to PSEA & GBV. Reporting channels for local community customers and residents will be established (email, hotline, complaint box). Women Committees will be formed to address protection needs for all age groups. The project also focuses on aligning commercial shop & building entrances for people with disabilities, adhering to the AGD concept. The comprehensive implementation plan involving several key steps will be adapted:1- Selection of buildings in collaboration with Local Authorities. This initial phase is crucial for ensuring the legal & structural aspects are aligned.2- Preparing the related structural study and obtaining the Engineers Syndicate’s approval.3- Debris removal in coordination with local community.4- Sewage network maintenance & restoration 5- Conducting interviews with shop occupants in coordination with the Market Committee.6- Operational grants will then be distributed to beneficiaries, providing financial support to those affected by EQ. The handover of apartments & shops to building owners & occupants will follow, facilitated by coordination efforts with Aleppo Governorate, Aleppo City Council, and the Market Committee.7- Monitoring & evaluation mechanisms will be put in place to assess the activation and success of the commercial shops within the reinforced buildings, ensuring the sustained impact of the project.
