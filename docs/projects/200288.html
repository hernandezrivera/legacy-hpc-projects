Project Summary

This project aims to enhance protection for Lebanese and migrants' survivors of Gender-Based Violence in need of protection. The project will expand outreach to survivors of Gender-Based Violence by adapting a ground-up approach to recruitment and build a pool of appropriately placed community volunteers, who will help to improve identification of target beneficiaries, and enable safe and sensitive referral to available services. During this project, IOM will work with one or multiple partners to boost the capacity of local service providers, well positioned to provide the protection services needed. To identify suitable and interested partners, a call for proposal will be launched and partners will be selected through a competitive and transparent process. These include case management, medical care, psychosocial support, safe shelters for survivors of GBV, financial and legal support. Onward referral to multisector assistance will be mainstreamed across the project.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

SO1 This includes sensitization and information sessions on several topics including GBV, Sexual and Reproductive Health, women’s rights, etc. The project will provide GBV risk mitigation and prevention services (distribution of dignity kits, information, and awareness on services - link beneficiaries to specialized services). The risk mitigation and prevention component may include information sessions with communities on GBV and community focal points capacitated to identify and refer individuals at risk or survivors of GBV and development of communication materials.SO2 Provide services directly or via implementing partners to survivors of GBV, including case management support to GBV survivors and individuals at risk, shelter, psychosocial support, financial support through multipurpose cash assistance to address different needs, clinical management of rape, legal aid for GBV survivors, and voluntary humanitarian return. To note that case management for migrant survivors of GBV also needs specific linkages with voluntary return program and activities and cross border interventions related to the continuum of care principle.S03 Provide emergency cash assistance to respond to the immediate and mid-term needs of GBV survivors and individuals at risk to mitigate the risk of further exposure to GBV/harm. This specific intervention cannot be completely replaced by multipurpose cash assistance program because of the emergency nature of protection emergency cash.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

Migrant exploitation include forced/ unpaid labor, restricted movement passport confiscation, psychological abuse, physical and sexual harassment. IOM aims to strengthen prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse (PSEA) and respond to incidents, support interagency response, inform migrants of their rights, protect against sexual exploitation, train frontline staff on appropriate principles and standards. IOM is an active member and in close coordination with interagency PSEA network; contribute to provision of capacity building support to implementing partners and local authorities, conduct community-level PSEA and support interagency PSEA network efforts in accordance with Action Plan of PSEA network 2023.IOM mitigation plan: - Awareness raising and capacity building of IOM Lebanon’s staff. - PSEA awareness to affected population.- PSEA visibility across IOM offices.- Participation in PSEA network - Securing a referral mechanism for SEA survivors whenever needed

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

IOM’s Accountability to Affected Populations (AAP) framework provides overview of IOM’s commitments to and actions on AAP, as well as definitions and explanations about the rationale and internal and external policies shaping the Organization’s approach. IOM Lebanon is in the process of refurbishing its complaint/feedback & response mechanism (CFRM) in order to create an effective, and efficient one. The mechanism is dedicated to receiving submissions related to complaints/feedback/suggestions from external stakeholders (including the affected population) and ensure appropriate and timely feedback to the complaints. The creation of the CFRM serves the overall objective of enhancing and reinforcing accountability towards the affected populations at IOM Lebanon. IOM Lebanon’s current CFRM channels are face to face, comment box, helpdesk (walk-ins), consultations with beneficiaries/migrant-community leaders, dedicated email address and the “We Are All In Platform”.
