Project Summary

Linha Verde 1458’ is an inter-agency complaints and feedback mechanism (CFM) composed of three primary platforms: (7) days a week, from 6am to 9pm cross-network toll-free hotline; outgoing sms service for targeted awareness raising and a dedicated email address. A two-way communication channel, it is designed to prevent abuses in the humanitarian assistance, promote informed decision making by affected communities and improve programming quality.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

To promote accountability to affected populations (AAP), Linha Verde is designed to contribute to the IASC commitments to AAP and protection from sexual exploitation and abuse (PSEA) , key elements of which are: information provision; complaints, feedback and response mechanisms; and, community participation. Needs and priorities captured by Linha Verde 1458 are circulated to humanitarian response partners through agreed feedback referral pathways and monthly data and narrative reports. As AAP is a key connector in the emergency, early recovery and development nexus, Linha Verde 1458 can support evidence-based decision making in the humanitarian assistance. Linha Verde 1458 is designed to complement existing and planned accountability mechanisms. To ensure the effective two-way flow of accurate and up-to-date information between affected communities and response providers and the resolution of feedbackLinha Verde 1458 is the agreed inter-agency SEA reporting mechanism in country, with 70% of the allegations received since the establishment of the PSEA network coming through the hotline. SEA cases are reported to the PSEA network within 24hrs capturing key information required to identify the responsible actor or organisation and facilitate an investigation, in accordance with the PSEA network SOPs and the Secretary General’s bulletin for special measures for PSEA .Linha Verde 1458 is operated through a Maputo-based call-centre contracted and managed by WFP on behalf of the Humanitarian Country Team (HCT), with the operation cost-shared by HCT members. For the second year of operation it was agreed that Linha Verde 1458 report to an inter-agency steering committee consisting of UNICEF, CARE, UNHCR and WFP.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Linha Verde 1458 collaborates with all humanitarian partners to raise awareness among the affected population of beneficiary rights, of the different complaints and feedback channels available to them and to define and strengthen complaint/ feedback referral pathways. Humanitarian partners are jointly commited to ensuring the timely closure of complaints and negative feedback loops. In addition to fielding tollfree calls from the affected population, in Portuguese and local languages, Linha Verde 1458 has the capacity to send out mass sms messages on key issues in the humanitarian context or providing mass clarification on common issues raised through the hotline service.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

There is growing global recognition that systematic and collective engagement with communities can improve programme quality as well as build safer and more resilient communities. Linha Verde 1458 functions as a multi-platform, interactive information, complaints and feedback mechanism which any Mozambican can access for free seven (7) days a week, from 6am to 9pm. Linha Verde is operated by a team of multi-lingual national operators, with at least 50% of the call centre operators being female. Through its different communication channels, the platform will systematically share and capture data whilst taking account of the different needs within communities from an age, gender, and diversity perspective.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

Linha Verde 1458, being an inter-agency mechanism is linked to the humanitarian protection structure and defined referral patheways. Through regular reporting and designation of sector focal points, humanitarian actors are able to analyse community feedback and trends to identify protection risks. This facilitates timely preventive and mitigation measures as well as opportunity to refer the affected population to vital services in coordination with the Protection cluster.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Linha verde 1458, presents one means through which the affected population can report on exclusion errors and access barrier to disabled people for rapid handling by responsible actors, whether directly as part of humanitarian interventions or through the disability working group.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

As the project is based on telephony focusing on remote assistance provided to affected population across the country and as such does not have a significant environmental footprint on the ground.
