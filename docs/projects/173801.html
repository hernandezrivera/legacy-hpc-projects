Project Summary

Child abuse and neglect increases during a pandemic, the Covid-19 pandemic have caused mental health problems in children and will hit hard on children prone to abuse. During the lockdown, children are home 24 hours or, in the circumstances of a parent contracting an illness, may be sent to relatives and acquaintances, making them even more vulnerable to sexual abuse. Particularly at risk are children who have undergone previous trauma, have mental or physical health problems, and have parents who have mental health problems or are at high-risk of emotional disturbance because of current medical, financial, and social conditions.
This pandemic is going to significantly affect children’s safety unless we prepare for it and spread awareness. The proposed interventions would Establish and support the operations of a case management and referral mechanism to ensure access of vulnerable children (girls and boys) to basic services, the intervention will further strengthen the knowledge on child rights at the community level and will empower the children in identifying child protection issues and using referral pathways set up in the community for protection concerns. In order to lessen the effects of the pandemic, Mental Health and Psychosocial Support (MHPSS) services to the most vulnerable children (girls and boys) will be provided; the project will also disseminate information and promote dialogue among families and communities on stigma and Violence Against Children (VAC) and GBV prevention. The purpose is to build a system at the community level that would sustain on its own and is used for dealing with child protection issues in the community. The ultimate goal is to protect children from violence, abuse, exploitation, and neglect.
