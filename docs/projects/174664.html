Project Summary

Conflicts adversely impact food security in many different ways. They cause mass displacements, deep economic recessions, drive up inflation, disrupt employment and erode finances for social protection and health, and make basic necessities, including food, less available and inaccessible to the population especially children, the old sick and PLWD. The burden is always on the household heads who have to figure out how to continue to feed the family and in most cases it is the women who suffer most. Where people’s livelihoods rely significantly on agriculture, like in Cameroon and in the NW and SW regions in particular conflict undermines agricultural supply chains and marketing channels from production to harvesting, and transportation. Conflict undermines resilience and often forces individuals and households to engage in increasingly destructive and irreversible coping strategies that threaten their future livelihoods, food security, nutrition and dignity.
When household can produce at least 50% of their own food supplies, it is a good sign and a guarantee of good nutrition and food security. The crisis that has rocked the NW and SW regions for the past 4 years plus has caused mass displacements of populations both urban and rural whose lively hoods hitherto depended on Livestock and agricultural activities. The food needs of households especially for children, the old, sick and women are threatened especially in the conflict affected peri-urban and urban areas in the NW /SW regions.
According to IOM/OCHA data, as of September 2020 some 409,173 people were displaced within North West and South West Regions and 360,547 are considered as returnees. Female population count for 48% of the total displaced population, children 44% (51% girls and 49% boys), and older people for 12%. The crisis which has resulted in many people being vulnerable especially women, girls and children. While humanitarian organizations are providing food rations to the victims of the crisis, there is an urgent need to build resilience and sustainable food security approaches in this context to complement the direct food rations the households receive. This can happen if peri urban and urban dwellers especially Host families and IDPs have alternative sources for food especially vegetable and animal intake.
This project is designed with the purpose of supporting / re-enkindle livelihoods for IDPs and vulnerable host communities and complement humanitarian food aid supplies. The project will equally enhance participation and build resilience and sustainability in food security.
Through this project beneficiaries will be supported with Agricultural, livestock and fishery inputs, materials as well as technical assistance to enable them engage in production in order to meet essential food needs, sell surpluses to improve the quality of life thus improve dietary needs of the entire household (children boys and girls, old and sick persons and PLWD). This project will target 1000 persons through 200 households (500 IDP and 500 vulnerable host community persons) with 50% being women headed households in peri urban and urban localities.
The project localities are: Mezam ( Bafut, Tubah and Bamenda II ) with 500 beneficiaries and Momo (Batibo, ) with 500 beneficiaries as well. Beneficiary capacities will be built and they will equally benefit technical support and follow to construct / set up their production units, and this will be followed with the provision of supplies for start -up in the domain they chose as well as Mentoring along the process to guarantee success of the venture. Accountability to beneficiaries will be ensured through education and sensitization throughout the project, involvement in scheduling of activities as well as through beneficiary complaints and feedback mechanisms that will be put in place. Accountability to the partner will be through regular sharing of reports.

1 - Besoins / Needs

The conflict in the NW/SW regions has significantly destroyed livelihoods especially for IDPs and then left a vast majority of community member in a precarious livelihood situation.Needs the project will address: Food SecurityThe project seeks to address food security through livelihoods activities in the area of agricultural, livestock and fishery production. The project will support the beneficiary households to produce small ruminant, local poultry, table birds/layers, pigs, fish and crops.The project will thus contribute to: Improve the % of households with acceptable Food Consumption Scores (FCS) out of the total targeted severely food insecure households from IDP the local population Improve the % of targeted households with Reduced Coping Strategies Index (CSI) (5 universal coping strategies) Improve the % of targeted households that meet minimum requirements for Household Dietary Diversity Scores (HDDS) Activity and strategyThe core project activity is to provide support to targeted people (IDPs and vulnerable households (female and male)) through agricultural, livestock and fishery assistance in the production (small ruminant, local poultry, table birds, piggery, fishery and agriculture) Strategy The profiling for this emergency assistance will give priority to the most vulnerable IDPs and host community members and Pregnant and Lactating Women and Girls (PLWG) with minimum space to set up a production unit. Targeting will give priority to those households with a presence of special needs persons but also to households, which are more exposed to protection risks such as abuse and sexual exploitation, survival sex, women and children household. 1000 beneficiaries will be targeted from Mezam ( Bafut, Tubah and Bali, and Bamenda II ) with and Momo (Batibo, Mbengwi )The project will provide •Technical capacities and follow up for the beneficiary households to ensure success in the livelihood initiative,The project will ensure that protection principles, including non-discrimination, participation, meaningful access, risk reduction, and safety and dignity are included throughout the project implementation process.The project strategy envisages the following sub activities :-Profile peri urban and urban displaced persons and vulnerable host families (300) to benefit small ruminant, local poultry, table birds, piggery, fishery and agriculture production support. -Select 250 beneficiary households, build their capacity on a chosen production option (small ruminant, local poultry, table birds, piggery, fishery or agriculture)-Provide material and financial support to set up / establish production units. -Accompany beneficiaries through mentoring and follow up visits, demonstrations of good practical methods to support and empower them to succeed in the activity. Expected results•1000 persons (250 households) receive capacity building and small ruminant, local poultry, table birds, layers piggery, fishery and agriculture support as start up.•1000 persons in the of beneficiary households have an improvement in animal and vegetable intake especially children, PLW as well as old and sick persons within the households.•Increase number of households with acceptable Food Consumption Scores in project localities •Increase number of targeted households with Coping Strategies •Increase number of targeted households that meet minimum requirements for Household Dietary Diversity
