Project Summary

The project aims to provide life-saving assistance and improving living conditions of IDPs inWASH sector. support vulnerable families with low/no income, poor housing conditions, severe disability or other special needs, or essential items in informal settlements.
The WASH components aim to support newly displaced families, women, and children in need in informal settlements through water-trucking activities where (30-35 liter is distributed daily per individual), latrine construction for 8,000 IDPs, solid waste removal 1-3 times a week, and desludging services. These activities intend to reduce the humanitarian needs among the most vulnerable IDPs by providing target groups with access to safe water and basic sanitation facilities.

4 - Needs

The north west Syria has the highest number of internally displaced persons in Syria, IDPs living in last resort sites are fully dependent on humanitarian assistance, and those in informal sites often experience worse WASH conditions making them particularly vulnerable. Also, newly displaced populations face specific WASH needs and require emergency WASH response with new displacement, mostly taking place in North-west Syria. Meanwhile, of displacement sites populations, women and girls, people with disabilities and the elderly, together with female headed households face more constrains and various protectionrisks in accessing WASH services. All population groups in the community could be affectedby limitations in functionality and efficiency of WASH infrastructure and services, in particular those living in overburdened communities, areas of high return or areas with access constraints (parts of Aleppo, Deir-Ez-Zor and Idleb governorates,) and areas under-served by public services due to limited access to electricity, high dependency on informal water sources, severe issues with sewage disposal or lack of garbage removal services. With deepening economic crisis, the most financially vulnerable households would face challengesinequitable accessing WASH services. as to March report from WASH cluster there are about 2,975,000 person need at least one of WASH service.8.3% of this gap is water trucking ,15.75% SWM , 8.95% latrines and shower, and 18.03% HP .

5 - Implementation of activities

Activity 1.: Daily distribution of drinking water by water truck to 8.000 IDPs in 9 informal settlements. Contracts with water truck owners will be done for (on average) 90 days. Our monitoring team will check the water quality and quantity. Activity 2.: Two times per week at least the solid waste of 8.000 IDPs will be collected in 9 informal settlements. The contracts with the sub-contractors will be set up for 1 – 3 months. The sub-contractors collect the waste from the informal settlements and bring it to the next landfill. Activity 3.: Emptying waste water in 9 informal settlements for 8.000 IDPs. Arche noVa will cooperate with sub-contractors for the desludging of septic tanks and taking the waste water to the next sewage system. It is planned that contracts will be issued for 1 – 3 months. Activity 4.: Construction of 100 latrines for 2.000 IDPs in 7 informal settlements. The construction of toilets will be done by local construction companies. Activity 5.: Purchase and distribution of 200 water tanks to 2.000 IDPs in 9 informal settlements. Purchasing/contracting of water tanks is done in Syria.
