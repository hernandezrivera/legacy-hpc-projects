Project Summary

The project aims to restore functionality or keep water infrastructure at the minimum level
of operation; and to carry out life-saving/ emergency WASH interventions including, water
system rehabilitation and extending water network, as well as improvement of hygiene
promotion, and raise awareness of COVID-19 pandemic precaution measures, targeting
IDPs, host communities and returnees. also, include soap and detergent distribution. RF
takes a new approach in this intervention to increase the access and reach more
vulnerable people in need for water services, RF will be working with Vision for A Better
Future organization (Vision) as a Sub-Implementer referred as SI in this proposal, which
RF is going to distribute work load with Vision as 60 % RF, while 40% will be implemented
through Vision, the same divided percentage will be used for the project components.
Vision is going to operate the activities for Yazibagh, Qastal Jendal, where Vision will be
responsible for the activities and resources in these 2 locations, Yazibagh C1565 Qastal
Jendal C1521 while RF will take the responsibility of for running all activities in these 3
locations.
Azaz C1564
Maarin C1574
Kaljibrin C1575
The project consists of three main components targeting 257,573.
*Connecting power lines electrical grid which come from Turkey with the water pumping stations in (Al Qastal, Maarin, and Yazi Bagh, and Diversion water station in Sharan) the power line has already connected to Kaljibrin. The boreholes are already connected with the water network in the four villages and Azaz city. Improve access to safe water, through the water network to minimize the financial burden on already impoverished families targeting 257,573 individuals in NW Syria are overburden due to high IDP numbers resulting from continued displacement in addition to limited functionality and efficiency of water infrastructure and services in addition to schools, health facilities, nutrition centers, and protection centers. Therefore, RFs” will provide sustainable power supply to the 5 drink water stations through an electric company.
Based on this project fund RFs” will provide operational support for 90 days to supply water to the most vulnerable people, meanwhile, will develop tariff-based system management in coordination with relevant stakeholders (mainly the Local Council in the project area).
Chlorine pumps will be connected to each water station
*For the total target population of this project, the RFs” will distribute soap and masks to the elderly, and people with disabilities at a percentage of 4.40%. from the total population. it is the most a segment of the population susceptible to infection with the COVID -19
*In a crisis, people tend to follow survival instinct which means prioritize avoiding tangible risks with less care to intangible risk such as the dangerous impact of incorrect hygiene practices. RFs” will conduct online Hygiene promotion campaigns (via social media, Radio of Azaz) hygienic behavior, knowledge, attitude, and practices among the most vulnerable people. Besides, signboard and consultant for training volunteer campaigns about hygiene promotion and COVID-19 raise awareness online sessions by the application.
- Professional consultant for training volunteer campaigns about hygiene promotion and COVID-19 raise awareness online sessions by electronic communication programs, campaigns consist of (15 males and 15 females). house to house targeting just KI family person which will receive soap and masks sharing their experience in hygiene promotion and COVID-19 raise awareness by sharing videos and photos by WhatsApp or orally, besides to campaigns
The MEAL field team will be provided with data collection tools such as Kobo Toolbox to get the data in real-time.
- A verification survey will be conducted on 50% of beneficiaries
- baseline survey for figure out the daily water intake calculator (DWIC)

4 - Needs

People in the targeted communities live now on Azaz district water trucking or closed wells to provide their unsafe water needs, many families, have to face difficulties in reaching the needed water where there is one private source with a high price and not available at all times. This situation seems difficult for people like widows, divorcees, andpeople with special needs because all people depend on this source (water trucking). Azaz district is suffering from a massive lack of drinking water supply, currently, it receives water in a limited time and during specific days of the week as this kind of services is not adequate to all families need and even it doesn’t reach faraway houses who had to buy water from tanks owners with a 1000-1500 SYP for each barrel 220 liters.
