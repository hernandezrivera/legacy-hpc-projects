Project Summary

The project aims at improving sanitary situation in the selected Areas of the suburb of Shweika in Tulkarm Governorate where 11417 people live. The improvement of the sanitation situation in the target areas is essential for a stable life with basic services that will help the residents to suffer less when Suburb is served with sanitation facilities.
The proposed project serves the western area of Shweika, north of Tulkarm city, which is located along the separation wall (about 5 km2 located in area C and 2 km located in A and B areas) with a total estimated population 4.500 (Turkarem Municipality 2021). Based on a recent survey conducted by House of Water and Environment (HWE), about 2700 (1326 female and 1339 male) people will benefit from this project. These areas suffer badly from a lack of wastewater collection system as 90 % of their residents rely on septic tanks and cesspits, which cause unsanitary conditions and pollution of soil and groundwater resources in addition to affecting negatively on the public health and safety of the women, men, children elderly and disabilities. Therefore, there is an urgent need to mitigate the bad sanitary conditions in these areas through installing wastewater collection system to serve the targeted population in Shwaikeh area and to promote good access to environmentally sound municipal wastewater services. It is worth to mention that the proposed HWE interventions in the targeted area will contribute to HRP WASH Cluster Objective 2 (Ensure equitable, affordable and sustainable safely-managed access to sufficient WASH services for the most vulnerable Palestinians).Public awareness campaigns will be conducted to address hygiene practice issues at the household level and the High schools to improve the capacity of the families and students in the targeted areas to effectively respond to emergencies and reduce tensions across the communities. It worth to mention that, transmission of COVID-19 and other water borne diseases will be lowered. The proposed interventions ensure protection mainstreaming incorporating protecting principles and promoting meaningful access, safety and dignity; accountability as well as participation and empowerment through conducting regular meeting and focus groups. HWE will implement the project with the partnership with Tulkarm municipality and highly coordination with PWA to achieve the project outcomes and outputs in addition to ensure the sustainability of the project.

1 - Needs

Over 60 % of the West Bank is considered Area C. Under Oslo II Accord, Area C is under total Israeli civil and military control and is subject to extensive restrictions seen to hamper Palestinian development. Recently, due to increasing Palestinian population in the West Bank, and consequently the expansion of residential areas, these is a crucial need to build a live area C to become vital and significant for human living conditions sustainability. In addition, Palestinians move to live in Area C, due to land low price comparing to the rest of the lands in areas A&B. In addition, the construction of the separated wall by Israel impedes access to water, food and sanitation services. Palestinians in area C, lack of basic services and needs, because of Israeli obstacles and inability of Palestinian local government councils and municipalities in offering such services in Area C. In Addition to, the Local Councils and municipalities face huge challenges to deliver wash services due to lack of Finance and technical capacities within their boundaries control.Inadequate WASH facilities expose women and girls to threats and burdens associated with meeting their personal hygiene needs, undertaking basic domestic chores, managing household water needs, and securing the needs of children disabilities, the elderly and the chronically ill. In addition to secure the locality from spreading COVID-19 and in decreasing the number COVID-19 cases. cases As result of this inadequate wash situation, an awareness campaign will be conducted to disseminate the importance of the project. HWE has conducted need survey for all targeted communities in Area A, B and C , this survey was driven by coordination between Tulkarem Municipality and HWE. In Addition to the need assessment conducted by Wash Cluster team with full coordination with them, one potential location was selected and screened in Tulkarem (Shweika). Shweikeh, is a suburb zone located within Tulkarem municipality borders, where 11417 people live (PCBS 2017). Shweikeh is an expansion area of the normal growth of the city The urban area of Shweikeh is restricted by Israeli separation wall in the west where construction of new infrastructures prohibited. Some lands can’t be served by municipality because their location in Area C and the lack of finance to fund the construction of sewer systems. Shweikeh, is a suburb zone located within Tulkarem municipality borders, where 11417 people live (PCBS 2017). Shweikeh is an expansion area of the normal growth of the city. The urban area of Shweikeh is restricted by Israeli separation wall in the west where construction of new infrastructures prohibited. Some lands can’t be served by municipality because their location in Area C. About 2700 capita: 1326 women and 1339 men (Tulkarem Municipality and HWE, 2021) suffer from a lack of wastewater collection networks. 1410 households and 1090 live in apartments in the targeted area are not connected to sewerage networks. They rely on septic tanks and cesspits, which cause unsanitary conditions and pollution of soil and ground water reservoir that is used to be close to the ground level. Lack of access to sewer system, affects women and girls’ (18-65) ability to engage in income generating activities as most of them work in agriculture. Target area approximation to separate wall, leads to limit community ability to access their farms. Thus, there is a need to mitigate bad sanitary conditions. In addition, using cesspits in these target areas forms heavy economic burdens on men, due to high cost of emptying cesspits. Children (0-18 years) and elderly people (+65 years) from mixed-sex group are sensitive to unhealthy conditions caused by improper behavior in wastewater disposal and expose the community to be affected by COVID-19 Virus. Violent activities and social disputes are common among communities, especially young people (19-55 years) from both sex groups. To solve this non-healthy condition, it is required to build/install wastewater collection network to serve the targeted population.

2 - Activities

Needs assessment has been conducted by HWE with the coordination with Tulkarem Municipality for the targeted areas, this includes identifying the targeted areas and the proposed activities. A high coordination with PWA was done to identify the planned interventions. It is worth to mention that the coordination with Tulkarem Municipality and PWA will be continued during the project implementation phase. In addition to the data obtained from WASH cluster teams. Proposed interventions and activities can be categorized as follows: Activity 1: Installation of sewer connection network and HH connections. This activity consists of the followings: 1.1 Construction of sewer lines in Shweikeh Suburb: The proposed networks to be installed are detailed as:Length: 2450 m Number of House connections: 300Number of Beneficiaries: 2500 (1240 Males and 1228 Females) Proposed activities include: design of sewer line, excavation works, UPVC pipes installation, manholes installation, filling and compact excavations with sand and base coarse layers. Activity 2: Hygiene promotion Awareness campaign: 2.1 Conduct Public awareness targeting the targeted community and 4 high schools to promote household connection to municipal networks and improve hygiene behavior. Consultive meeting with Tulkarem municipality, the representatives of the communities and schools’ principals and teachers will be conducted to design the awareness campaign. HWE will develop communication plan identifying the key messages, the most popular social media to publish the messages in the targeted area. The key messages will focus on the positive impacts of the connection to sewer networks on conserving the environment, public health and the economy. Sanitizers will be distributed to the beneficiaries. All proposed activities will ensure protection principles on safety and dignity, meaningful access, accountability, participation, empowerment and claiming of rights.

10. – Do No Harm
10.1 - Do No Harm

Installation of wastewater network will ensure a safe and hygienic disposal of wastewater. Therefore, cesspits flood, odour, groundwater pollution, waterborne diseases, accidents risks esp. for children and disabled people and conflicts between neighbours will be prevented. HWE team will develop a safety plan integrating all the preventive measures to avoid any harm to the targeted community. Further, all preventive measures to minimize the risk of the COVID 19 pandemic will be taken into consideration. Hygiene kits and equipment will be provided and distributed to work team, contractors and community as needed.HWE will follow a participatory approach to ensure the equal participation of the vulnerable people taking into consideration the needs of women, children and disable people to strength their roles and equality. These groups will be enhanced to participate in decision process of proposed interventions and the monitoring phase of the project. HWE will conduct a variety of communication tools to protect the community esp. vulnerable groups, ensure gender equality and their participation. Mainly the public awareness session, workshops and focus group will addressed the do no harm principles to avoid the negative impact of potential conflicts. In addition, key massages on WASH project will be disseminated through social media and posters. This Safety plan generally will consist of the following protection activities in line with all humanitarian principles and needs: 1) Responsive activities: immediate and urgent violation and effects are to be predicted, immediate actions to treat with such effects are to be defined. Example 1: Immediate effect: sudden collapse in a trenchImmediate action: Evacuation of people around the collapse, consolidation of collapse edges to prevent expansion and first aid for any injury, if any. Example 2:Immediate effect: Cut in water pipe inside trench. Immediate action: Fix it as soon as possible.Example 3: Immediate effect: Violation between two neighbours on their cross border. Immediate action: Cooperate with representatives of community to solve such problems, and try to avoid creating violence between neighbours. 2) Remedial activities: long term effects are to be indicated; remedial plan is to be set.This activity will also include forming a committee from community with equitable gender representation (5-7 people) to facilitate project implementation and solve any upcoming problem with community.Example: Remedial effect: House owner explained about crossing sewer line hishouse sidewalk because during time of implementation he will has social activity such as wedding party. Remedial action: negotiation plan and awareness activity. 3) Environmental activities: Define activities to create safe work environment for workers and community.Example: Installation of warning signs to warn from any expected harm, e.g. warning from falling in a trench.HWE will set a complaint and feedback mechanism to treat confidentially with any complaints esp. complaints related to sexual abuse and exploitation. This mechanism will be stated by HWE code of conduct.
