3 - Needs

Involuntarily displaced populations, including those living in refugee and IDP settlements, wholly solely depend on others for their health and well-being and often have heightened vulnerability. Furthermore, their unclear political and social status can worsen existing social sentiments that cause displaced persons to be undervalued and neglected. These conditions combined with other factors can lead to inadequate Hygiene services and behaviors exacerbate health of the inhabitants and also become the source of increase in risk of outbreak in water, food and air-borne diseases. Due to Syrian crises, Northeastern Syria is currently facing increased risk of outbreak in water, food and air-borne diseases and lack of safe drinking water, food, and proper sanitary resources in its growing formal and informal IDP settlements. These conditions lead to enhanced concern for women and children's health and safety. SRP’s project strives to contribute to this problem by providing 500 hygiene kits and safety workshops for women living within these camps. The expected benefit of this will be increasing access to essential hygiene resources for IDPs, prevention of common communicable diseases and increased safety for women within the community.Hence it is crucial to initiate a short term project support to increase awareness and provide water, hygiene and sanitation services to IDPs, to avoid human rights violation and ensure women and child protection to the inhabitants of IDP camps.
