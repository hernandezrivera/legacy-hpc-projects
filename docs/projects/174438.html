Project Summary

“The Awakening” is a right based organization working for the protection of rights of the vulnerable people (women, girls, religious minorities and trans communities) in Malakand Division, KP.
The COVID-19 virus has affected almost all the countries of the world and Pakistan’s Swat Valley is one of the area in which the COVID-19 infected cases are increasing day by day. The disruptive impact of COVID-19 on existing legal, social and policing structures means that security, justice and social services are likely to be severely curtailed, leaving limited avenues to services, safety and justice for survivors of gender-based violence. In addition, with many health services abandoned or severely disrupted, survivor’s ability to access essential treatment will be further restricted.
“The Awakening” works on Rule of Law and we have an established facility with the name of DASTAGEER Legal Aid Center (DLAC) in District Swat, in which we provided Free Legal Aid, Legal Counselling, Psychosocial Counselling to the survivors of violence, Tele Counselling, Awareness on Legal rights and Referrals to the relevant partners. During the COVID-19 we still are providing Counselling and referral services and this project will further strengthen our efforts buy responding to GBV issues in the COVID-19 situation. As per our research and data report 6 women and girls were murdered in the beginning month of COVID-19 Pandemic in District Swat. We received more than 85 calls reporting domestic violence and abuse of women by their husbands and family members. Last week a woman was killed by her husband as he was not able to feed his wife and children (there were continuous fights between them over this issue) due to financial problems faced due to COVID-19. “The Awakening” collects data about the incidences of violence against women in District Swat and publish it annually as “The Status of Violence Against Women in District Swat” as per our collected data from different sources (newspaper, police, walk ins to DLAC, direct reporting through telephone, network members) in 2016 53 women were killed in the name of honor, 2017 – 50, 2018 – 34, 2019 – 32, 2020 – 32 women and girls were killed in the name of honor. These are alarming figures and this needs to be addressed properly.
That is why there is a dire need to address the issue of GBV and Domestic violence during the pandemics.

1- Project Activities

We will adopt a two pronged strategy to address the issues of violence against women, One we will focus on the delivery side and provide services to the vulnerable population during the COVID response from our DLAC (Dastageer Legal Aid Center) and Secondly by supporting the Reproductive Health services in BHUs, Provision of PPEs to the frontline health workers and sharing our online Comic Books and its Audio Visual Previews online and through Radio for Menstrual Hygiene Awareness. We have the Comic Books and the Audio Visual will be finalized and it could be utilized for the greater good of the vulnerable communities. Activity 1: “The Awakening” will conduct services mapping and assessments and identify the gaps in GBV survivor-service provision, prepare to provide essential stop-gap measures where feasible in the quarantined and/or locked down areas and share it with all the relevant stakeholdersActivity 2: “The Awakening” DLAC (Dastageer Legal Aid Center)will utilize its current helplines as we have already set SoPs and capacity to address issues of GBV and will provide the following direct services to the vulnerable population (Women and Girls, Ethnic and Religious Minorities, Trans Communities and Persons with disabilities;i) Walk-in (following proper SoPs)ii) Psychosocial Counselling (in-person and tele services)iii) Mental Health Counselling (in-person and tele services)iv) Legal Advice v) Referral Services (we have a referral directory and will update it once more)vi) General Advice on legal rights vii) Referrals to Government Cash services (Ihsaas Programme) Activity 3: COVID mental health support helpline: This will include training a team of 05 counsellors to respond to COVID related mental health problems and establishing a helpline that those requiring individual support can call. This will be done by strengthening the existing referral mechanism of “The Awakening”-Dastageer Legal Aid Center to ensure survivor response services are maintained as life-saving interventions through the emergency telephone helpline Activity 4: Organize (02) online training workshops through Zoom: 4.1)Training No.1; Mental Health wellbeing during crisis : This training will include development of a “Coping with Corona” curriculum, GBV service provision standards including guiding principle applying COVID-19 and that will be delivered to groups of up to 30 frontline health workers, patients, caregivers and women leaders 4.2) Training No.2; This training will include all the relevant stakeholders i.e; WHRDs, frontline health workers and police and their capacity building on protocols of (PSEA) Partner Sexual Exploitation and Abuse, code of conduct for responders and complaint mechanisms and referral services for survivors Activity 5: Digital and Radio Awareness Campaigns for community: This will include developing educational clips and distributing them through, social media and broadcasting through radio about complaint mechanisms and referral services for survivors. (This will be done in consultation with the cluster leads)Activity 6: “The Awakening” will develop a coordination mechanism with 20 health facilities /BHUs from all the seven tehsils of District Swat for the provision of counselling services of the survivors of violence visiting these health facilities and proper referral to the relevant partners. Proper PPEs will be distributed among the staff members of the health facilities/BHUsActivity 7: Keeping the RH in consideration “The Awakening” had already developed Comic books on Adolescent Girls about Menstrual Hygiene Management. We have a website on Menstrual Education and have also prepared an Audio Visual preview of the Comic book which will be disseminated through social media and other means to be used for mass awareness about Menstrual Hygiene Management during COVID-19 response. As majority of the adolescents girls do not know how to manage their periods and face complications and difficulty.
