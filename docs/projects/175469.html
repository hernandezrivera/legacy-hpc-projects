Project Summary

The project aims at tackling the needs of the IDP population within sites/site-like settings and host community with a comprehensive CCCM approach. The intervention will respond to three layers of needs: basic life-safing assistance in the priorities areas identified by the Cluster; community engagement and protection mainstreaming approach; infrastructural and urban improvement and planning of the site/area.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project aims at targeting the displaced population within Montepuez, Metuge, Ibo and Pemba districts with a multi-purpose response including three main areas of intervention: i) Protection, community participation and engagement; ii) first relief services provision; iii) site structural organization, planning and development. First and foremost, the intervention will ensure the participation of all groups of the population in the decision-making and the site/camp governance structure so that to uphold the basic rights of the displaced population. Such objective will be achieved through establishing diversified, transparent and community based CFM and with the creation of a camp committee respecting the diversity of groups residing in the area and ensuring inclusion of the most vulnerable groups. The estimated number of the people in need targeted will be 48.000 individuals amongs the targeted district.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Potential harms and risks may arise in the general access to re-allocation procedures, access to services provision, with special concerns towards vulnerable categories, such as un-accompanied minors, female headed households, elderly, people with disabilities, victims of abuse. AVSI will ensure participation of all groups within the decision making process and governance designing of the re-allocation/re-settelement area. Also, through constant monitoring – both through the said multiple CFM and routinary HHs assessments – and sensitizstion campaigning on protection standards, PSEAH, GBV prevention the community will be capacitated and well informed on rights and prerogatives, as well as on referral and complaints systems. To avoid the major protection risks of resistance, exploitment and/or conflict between IDP and host community consultation meeting will carried out in order to create a community engagement comprehensive mechanism.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

The project itself is based on the strong participation of the beneficiaries for the activities implementation. Before the beginning of the project, focus group activities will be planned to understand which are the beneficiaries expectations concerning project impact. These meetings will be organized and held in close coordination with local leaders and the existing government structure (provincial, district and administrative levels) and at community level. Firstly, community’s opinion on how to adapt the general aspects of activities implementation and coordination according to local culture, customs, beliefs and capacity, will be taken into account and incorporated into the work methodology. Secondly, it will be created a specific hotline for feedback and complaints related to the project and it will be promoted the Linha Verde. It is worthy to note that AVSI has adopted an internal policy on accountability to Affected People on the March 23th, 2020 based on the IASC Commitments on Accountability to Affected Populations (CAAP).

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

In the present project proposal screening, surveillance and participation of people with disabilities have been included as key activities and indicators. During their implementation, the detection of people with disabilities will be reported (disaggregated by gender and age) and given special attention. This translates into prioritizing the abovementioned target population as to the assistance for Shelter, NFI and Protection, especially for those cases having motor disabilities to which AVSI Foundation will provide logistics support. Moreover, it is worthy to note that very often people with disabilities are discriminated within their own communities. This can be a compounding factor when people with disabilities arrived in a host community, as in the case of IDPs. AVSI Foundation will ensure the implementation of sensitization sessions throughout its activities to ensure that these people are supported within the community and given special attention when coordinating and planning activities with local SDSMAS and partners.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

AVSI aims to promote a sustainable and healthy environment in order to improve the wellbeing of the community. Given the experience of AVSI Mozambique in energy efficiency-related projects, method to save charcoal and wood during cooking demonstration will be also promoted so that to sensitize the target population on preventing deforestation that in turn impact positively on family finances by saving wood and charcoal. In addition of that, it will be prioritazed the use of local materials in order to adopt an environment-friendly approach by trying to increase awareness in the affected communities. Moreover, with a strong community engagement and participation; on rehabilitation activities, specific attention regarding the site selection and sourcing of materials, favouring the use of local resources. Finally AVSI can rely to an internal Policy on environment on May 11th 2020.
