Project Summary

The project proposal is aimed at promoting the recovery of agricoltural activity after the cyclone IDAI through a process of improvement of farmers’ technical agricultural skills combined with the seed distribution.
The project will be implemented in the Nhamatanda district - Province of Sofala, through a support action of the bean, corn and vegetable value chain. Nhamatanda is a 200,000 inhabitant city, located about 90 Km west of Beira and its economy is based on both subsistence and industrial agriculture.
The Nhamatanda discrict was strongly affected by cyclone IDAI that caused the almost complete loss of the production of the principal agricoltural season contributing to the increase of the food insicurity.
The production recovery will be supported through the seed and hydraulic pump distribution and training sessions aimed to the strengthened of the farmers’ agronomic techniques knoledge and the developing of strategy to rapid response to climate change. 1,200 farmers, coming from 40 farmer associacions, will be involved on this project and each association will make available 1ha in order to create the experimental field where, through the FFS methodology, agronomic techniques will be taught to preserve soil fertility and improve productivity (rotations, intercropping, conservative agriculture, etc.). The selection of local crop varieties, well adapted to the climate of the Nhamatanda, will support the biodiversity. The intercropping coltivation will contribute to reduce the use of pesticides and agrochemical contributing to decrease the production cost and the enviromental impact of the agricolture.
Training sessions will be organized in order to improve farmers' agronomic skills on soil fertility use of crop rotations between maize and bean, water resources management and improvement crop yield and will be organized on the basis of 3 sessions, using the Farmer Field School methodology.
Providing hydraulic pump to the farmers will help them to reduce their dipendence from the availability of rainwater, increasing the possibility to produce during the dry season and so, contributing to the resilience of the population involved on this project.
To facilitate the know-how transfer, the project will make use of the instruments already tested by DPASA on agricultural training and will refer to the manuals for the implementation of good practices drawn up by CEFA in other projects aimed at setting up production chains (Mozambique, Kenya, Somalia).
