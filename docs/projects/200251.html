Project Summary

This project aims at working with MEHE to address learning loss and provide additional learning support in low performing public schools, focusing on children in grades 3-4. The project includes three phases. First, design a pilot catch-up approach program for children at risk of early drop out to ensure learning continuity, based on the mapping of existing/ongoing interventions from within Lebanon and following international good practices. Phase 2 includes the pilot of catch-up approach program in public schools. The third phase valuate the catch-up approach, through measuring the quality and efficacy of the catch-up classes, including recommendations for quality improvements on a large scale.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

To ensure that children catch up on lost learning and develop Foundational Literacy and Numeracy skills, MEHE and partners will be organising summer catch-up classes for children. Recognising that this may be insufficient for all children to be able to achieve grade level or appropriate foundational skills and to aid in the consolidation of skills acquired during Summer classes, specialised catch up learning support is needed throughout the next school year, with a focus on children in grades 3-4 who will be at greatest risk of dropping out and failing to succeed if they do not have the foundational skills necessary to continue in primary school and beyond.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

The Prevention against Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) and Child Safeguarding Policy (CSG) are the main policies that SCI relies on to ensure the implementation of safe education. These policies state that SCI has zero tolerance toward any form of abuse that children or their caregivers may face. To ensure safe implementation, SCI's Child Protection team will train public school teachers on identifying and reporting abuse. They will also provide induction on PSEA and CSG policies and require teachers to sign these policies to ensure they are respected during the implementation phasae. In addition, SCI has put in place a safe reporting mechanism through an online system, and a focal point will always be available to provide assistance. Moreover, SCI requires police records, criminal history and adopts safe recruitment practices to ensure that the recruited team has no abuse history, and the team will take all safeguarding inductions.
