Project Summary

The project will contribute to the reduction of morbidity and mortality due to multi hazards in the priority districts in the country. This will be done through the procurement and distribution of essential medicines for primary health care facilities. The primary health care facility shall also receive capacity building on case management for conditions associated with drought , COVID-19 and other hazards. To improve early detection of priority conditions, the project will also seek to strengthen laboratory capacity to detect priority disease conditions including COVID-19. The project will also seek to strengthen disease surveillance in the priority districts through capacity building on Intergarted disease surveillance and response.
To respond to child health issues, the project will also seek to reduce vaccine preventable diseases through supporting vaccination campaigns especially in districts will low vaccination coverages.

8 - Accountability to Affected People
8.1 - Describe how your project was designed in consultation with communities? How will you incorporate communities in project implementation and what feedback mechanisms do you have in place?

To ensure accountability to affected people the project shall work closely with community structures starting from primary health care level with the thrust of attaining Universal Health Coverage. The partners will ensure that the communities have a voice on the type of health intervention, delivery mode as well as the quality of service rendered. Platforms such as community meetings as well as the Health Centre committees shall be utilized to ensure that there is participation and inclusion of affected communities. The project shall ensure representation of men and women, girls and boys in the aforementioned platforms. During programme implementation, WHO and health partners will also ensure that the community leadership structures are consulted in priority setting and participation in development structures. To foster knowledge development, brokering and networking health partners will also hold regular meetings to share good practices on AAP with the thrust of developing integrated approaches on AAP. These platforms will also seek to strengthen utilization of input from situational analysis and needs assessments carried out by other health partners.

9 - Environmental considerations
9.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

During the implementation of the project WHO, MOHCC and other health partners will be working closely with the health facilities. To ensure compliance with environmental standards, the project will identify all the risks associated with health care waste management and develop mitigatory measures. Further to that the project will promote proper disposal of waste material emanating from the laboratory
