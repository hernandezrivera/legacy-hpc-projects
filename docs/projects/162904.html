Project Summary

The proposed project aims to develop evidence based, innovative and sustainable approaches to address the challenges faced by refugees and their host communities in a more structured manner with practical insight and foresight in partnership with the local government and other stakeholders. It is expected that by improving protection, enhancing self-reliance opportunities and integrated service delivery and developing capacity of the local authorities for delivery of such services, the refugees, and the host communities will benefit from a safer and more favourable environment, increasing sustainably their livelihood opportunities. An integrated approach focusing on vocational skills and business development will provide beneficiaries with the skills needed to engage in income-generating activities and ultimately play a positive role in their communities and local economy. The training in vocational skills and business development to the refugees and host communities will be provided through the vocational trainings and entrepreneurship centres (VTECs) in Khartoum State.
The proposed project will contribute to enhance employment opportunities and stimulate entrepreneurship among the unemployed youths including refugees and the host communities in Khartoum States by increasing access to educational and vocational training services for at least 500 direct beneficiaries in marketable vocational skills combined with institutional entrepreneurship and career guidance for productive livelihoods, and ultimately contributing to the creation of sustainable sources of livelihoods and development, poverty reduction and demand-driven vocational technical training and professional skills relevant to the needs of the Sudanese industrial sector in general and to the achievement of SDGs #1, #2, #4, #5, #8, #9 and #16 in close partnership with the private sector and other stakeholders.

1 - Needs

Nearly one third of the refugees living in Khartoum state are living under extreme poverty with very limited livelihood opportunities. Therefore, scaling up of livelihood programs are important to improve resilience and reduce dependence on external assistance and aid. At present, refugees are employed mainly in the informal sector with highly uncertain working conditions and very low wages due to low technical and business management skills. Despite government directives on eligibility to work, refugees continuously face challenges of work permit and discrimination and harassment by the employer and or local authorities. Moreover, majority of the refugees and other stakeholders are not aware of the privileges granted to the refugees through the Asylum Act 2014. Pressing needs of refugees include but not limited to limited financial resources aligned to their needs, particularly solutions for sustaining income and access to business services including integration of refugees in the private sector and local economy. For the same reasons, vocational skills training and income generation activities have to be conducted on a smaller scale than needed and this leading to failure to accommodate the pressing needs of refugees. Nonetheless, Sudan is embarking on a new course of action under the new regime to foster sustainable development. This new era is also fertile for such initiatives that consider “Humanitarian-Development-Peace Nexus.” The starting point which this proposal adopts is investing in building the capacity of the most in need communities of refugees and the host communities, respond to their skills gap and deficits for better inclusion within the local community. The inclusion will stem from the skills enhancement, and beyond to accommodate aspects of access to microfinance, business counselling, and protection life skills. These are important needs of refugees and host communities living in urban areas, in such settings, been a refugee means deprivation of most of the livelihoods assets and capital. Restoring at least some of the livelihoods assets and capabilities for urban refugees is an important need, a need of today, cannot wait for tomorrow.Therefore, project will support refugees and host community with industry relevant marketable vocational skills (including language training) and post training support such as facilitate obtaining work permit, linking them to private sector to gain employment and assist in set up of business to promote self-employment.

2 - Activities / Outputs

Following activities will be implemented in cooperation and partnership with the Supreme Council for Human Development and Labour (SCHDL), Khartoum state, UNHCR, IOM and other partners identified during the implementation process:• Conduct institutional and technical capacity development for the management staffs, instructors and supporting staffs of the vocational training and entrepreneurship centres (VTECs) in Khartoum, to deliver improved services and vocational training to the target beneficiaries.• Develop capacity of SCVTA and SCHDL staffs to plan and implement demand driven vocational training and education in Khartoum State through training and workshops.• Support development and implementation of sound human resource development policies for registered refugees in Khartoum State.• Facilitate partnership between VTECs and private institutions to plan and implement dual training system in Khartoum State• Identify project beneficiaries in compliance with the national legislation and legal provisions in close cooperation with related national bodies (such as Commission of Refugees/Refugee Counselling Service) and agencies such as UNHCR, IOM, etc. using agreed upon selection criteria.• Plan and conduct training of unemployed vulnerable youths on demand-driven marketable vocational skills.• Improve financial literacy among beneficiaries through training on enterprise development, business management and entrepreneurship.• Facilitate development of business plan and access to the financial services.• Organize a job fair in Khartoum to match the trained job seekers and private companies.• Conduct regular monitoring, and a terminal project evaluation to measure the impacts and draw lessons of the project.
