Project Summary

This project is multi-sector approach will contribute to the Sudan humanitarian response plan focusing on addressing of life-saving multi sector assistance and protection services in line with HRP Strategic Objective 1 and 2. through timely provision of life saving and resilience multi sector intervention Protection , Gender Based Violence, Child Protection , WASH and Education among Conflict-affected IDPs in White Nile state .
The project is in line with sectors Strategic Objectives for the revised HRP 2023 and will focus on lifesaving and protection activities , and aimed at providing the affected vulnerable Internal Displace Persons and hosts communities with sustainable basic services to ensure well-being and self-reliance , improved enrolment and absorption of school age children and improve school environment by provision of hand wash facilities and provision of potable water in schools , provide WASH and protection services for Internally Displaced Persons and host communities to strengthen emergency preparedness, and Support the preparation of resilience solutions to address their need .
This project targeted most vulnerable Internal Displace Persons and hosts communities including displaced children at school age in White Nile state mainly in Kosti and Rabak , Aljabalain and Edwaim localities , the project total beneficiaries is 18,720 Internal Displace Persons (3,310 Men - 6,560 Women – 4,325 Boys – 4,525 Girls ) how are affected by the current wave of fighting between Sudanese Armed Forces (SAF) and the Rapid Support Forces (RSF) which has caused tremendous human suffering and exacerbated significant pre-existing humanitarian needs amongst the civilian population in Sudan
The proposed integrated intervention as below:
for the General Protection response : Targeted 3,590 IDPs (2,145 Men- 1,245 Women - 80 Boys – 120 Girls ) and will deliver the basic Protection services (formation of Community based structures ,awareness session and Capacity building for service providers on protection issues and Provide Multipurpose Cash Assistance for most vulnerable HHs )
Child Protection response : Targeted 2,120 IDPs (210 Men - 610 women – 550 Boys -750 Girls) children and will deliver the basic Child Protection services ( Case Management and referral services ,awareness session ,Capacity building for series providers on children protection issues and provide assistant for PWD ) and JASMAR will ensure the community’s active participation to prevent and respond to child protection issues.
Gender Based Violence response : Targeted 2,520 IDPs (485 men -1,485 women – 125 boys - 425 Girls _ and will deliver the basic GBV services (GBV Case Management and referral services ,GBV awareness session and Capacity building )
Education response : targeted 5,800 internal displaced children at school age ( 150 men -150 women- 3050 boys – 2,450 women ) and from total targeted children 1% children with disabilities) , the intervention will be through emergency education and absorption in schools ,provide scholastic martials for teachers and students , Provision of Temporary Learning Spaces, provision of hygiene kits for adolescent girls , provision of recreational kits and psychological and social support activities .
The project includes WASH activities in school (Establish Temporary hand washing stations, and availability to potable water by water tanks )
WASH Response : Targeted 4,620 IDPs and host communities, will deliver the basic WASH service , and will including schools .
JASMAR have operation ,presence and good knowledge of targeted communities in White Nile state , and already coordinate the project activities with other humanitarian actors and forums (sectors Education , WASH ,CP and GBV) and respective government departments in the project sites .
JASMAR implementation strategy will be direct implementation which include community orientation ,mobilization and engagement.

1 - Needs

Nearly one month after the start of the conflict on April 15, clashes between the Rapid Support Forces (RSF)—a paramilitary force—and the Sudanese Armed Forces (SAF) continue to result in civilian casualties and generate humanitarian needs, particularly in and around Sudan’s capital city of Khartoum and in Darfur and Kordofan regions. The ongoing clashes has caused significant displacement and humanitarian needs for IDPs , who reportedly were displaced to different regions across Sudan, including the White Nile state. according to last update of Displacement Tracking Matrix /DTM Sudan /IOM All IDPs within White Nile have reportedly arrived from Khartoum state. Since the beginning of conflict on 15 April there is about 212,265 IDPs in White Nile. Displaced households are sheltering with their relatives or in gathering sites/IDP camps in different locations across Rabak, Kosti, Al Getaina, Tendalti, Ad Diwaim, Um Rimta, As Salam / Al Rawat, Aj Jabalain, and Guli localities. 39.60% (84,065 IDPs) of the displaced caseload in White Nile are reportedly non-Sudanese nationals the majority is south Sudanese refuges ,. An about 42,453 HHs were displaced in the first month of clashes . New arrivals have scattered in areas with relatives seeking protection and humanitarian assistance. Upon arrival, most IDPs were assisted with essential food assistance, including ready meals. currently the humanitarian actors seek to provide access to essential services, shelter, protection services, healthcare, education, etc., within local communities, ensuring freedom of movement and protection.moreover, The current conflict has negatively impacted the education of affected girls, boys and adolescents, including children with disabilities who face challenges in accessing inclusive quality education in a safe and protective learning environment. an estimated 285,000 school aged children have been displaced. This includes 208,000 school going children, 106,000 girls and an estimated 15 per cent of children with disabilities according to revised HRP and education sector response priorities there is need to support the most vulnerable and marginalized girls and boys at risk of exclusion from education is a priority for the displaced children and vulnerable host communities in White Nile and There is limited data on children living with disabilities to adequately analyse their education barriers and promote an enabling environment for increased access to education.The last interagency assessment conducted on 9th to 15th May 2023 in White Nile state , the targeted Internal Displaced Persons identified their priorities and needs and the key finding and recommendation to provide essential service include WASH intervention ,Education intervention and Protection service . This Proposed project will targeted most vulnerable Internal Displace Persons and host communities ( sudanese ) in Aljabalain , Rabak Kosti , and Edwaim localities in White Nile state
