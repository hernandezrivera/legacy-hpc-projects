Project Summary

Medair’s proposed intervention will provide life-saving and sustainable NFI interventions at household-levels to 16,885 target beneficiaries (excluding overlap) by providing core NFI and seasonal items. The intervention is building on the experience, expertise and relationships from Medair’s previous project cycles to reach the most vulnerable and in-need, with protection mainstreaming and sustainability at the core of its programme.

Needs

As a result of the ongoing crisis in Syria since 2011, there remains an urgent need for core and seasonal NFI assistance for those displaced from their homes and/or seeking to return to their places of origin. This is especially the case for those recently displaced or for extremely vulnerable individuals regardless of their status (IDP, returnees, host community) who do not have access to sufficient financial means to purchase basic NFI supplies in areas with high severity levels (4 and over). This has been evidenced in Medair’s assessment in late 2018 in Ash Shajara district in Dar’a. Most households were found to be in need of assistance for basic needs such as mattresses and blankets (78.5%), as well as heating solutions (84.2%) to meet their basic needs and to increase their way to cope with the weather conditions during the winter months.

Implementation of activities

Medair will distribute 3,070 core NFIs and 3,070 winterisation kits and with these kits will target those subject to displacement within the previous three months as well as vulnerable, overburdened households in urgent need for basic assistance and/or those in recently accessible areas. There is an assumed 90% overlap between NFIs and winterization kits as most HHs will receive both. Targeted assessments will be conducted to customise the assistance in the form of NFI and winterisation materials according to household size and specific need. 3,395 Female Hygiene Kits (FHK) will be distributed addressing sanitation and protection needs of women of reproductive age are included as part of the intervention. There will be 100% overlap between the beneficiaries that receive FHK and the ones that receive NFI/winterisation kits so these have not been included in the total beneficiary count. Vulnerable households eligible for distributions will be identified as having at least 3 of the following: Female headed households Family member with a disability Family member >65 years old Family member with a chronic illness More than 3 children under 12 years old At least 1 child < 5 years old At least 1 pregnant or lactating woman Economic poverty as indicated by having minimal basic possessions (≤4 of the following: stove, refrigerator, TV, sofa, water tank, washing machine, microwave, beds, wardrobe, carpets, curtains, heating device) Medair will directly implement distribution activities in coordination with SARC. Medair will have a team of trained enumerators (both male and female) conducting house-to-house assessments and distributions with oversight from the Programme Manager. A PDM will be conduct within a month after each distribution to monitor acceptability of the items provided and beneficiary satisfaction with the distribution process.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

ASSUMPTIONS:- There is no significant change to the political and security situation inside Syria and the GoS continue to hold control over recently-retaken governorates- The security situation remains stable enough to allow project and support functions to be set up and to continue- Market systems remain relatively stable- There is no excessive fluctuations affecting the value of the Syrian Pound and any inflation/deflation will be able to be offset against hard currency (USD)- Medair is able to recruit adequate numbers of appropriately qualified staff and/or contractors- Acceptance and participation of authorities, local leaders and communities during interventions- Continued cooperation and coordination among relevant authorities and partners. - No major deterioration in logistical conditions and the supply chain for essential supplies remains reliable and necessary goods are approved for transportRISKS:- Medair cannot secure approvals from ministries within the timeline outlined in the work plan, delaying project delivery- Delays to the visa approval process which can cause delays to project implementation.- Desperation of beneficiaries with level of support or feeling of being missed out resulting in disagreement or physical protest- Significant injury to team members within Syria either due to targeting or "wrong place, wrong time"- Central Bank imposes regulations or restrictions which stop or markedly constrain Medair’s ability to maintain adequate cash flow to pay programme expenses- Medair staff or contractors submit fraudulent expenses or do not comply sufficiently with procurement guidelines leading to a loss of financial resource.MITIGATING MEASURES:- Conduct clear, robust, regular context analysis as part of security management- Continue with keeping security planning up to date and training a crisis management team for each location - Daily, careful monitoring of security situation and trends (INSO, UNDSS, National Staff etc.) including close liaison with local stakeholders on security issues and a clear briefing to all staff and volunteers that safety comes first- Remain flexible in project locations if access to a particular community or programme site is temporarily unavailable access is refused to certain locations - Continue to use the Medair Regional Office as a base for staff to work remotely from whilst awaiting visa approval.- Maintain good contact with SARC, Ministries, local authorities, communities, UN agencies and other stakeholders- Balance activities for IDPs, returnees and host population to avoid unnecessary tensions, noting that lead factor for beneficiary selection is vulnerability not status- Complete training for all new staff on procurement guidelines, conduct systematic internal and external audits to assess adherence to financial and logistic guidelines and spot check for any fraudulent activity
