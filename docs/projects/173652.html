Project Summary

As a result of the heavy rains, many parts of the country were left flooded causing losses of life and damages to infrastructure and shelters. As per NDMA, a total of 78,736 shelters were fully damaged and 139,113 shelters were partially damaged across the country due to floods and heavy rains. Sindh province was the worst affected during this year’s monsoon as 77,343 houses were left fully damaged and 137,178 as partially damaged. As per the RNA conducted by NDC partners in early September, 100% of the assessed households had reported loss of or damages to their shelters. Findings of the ERNA show that shelter needs still remain high in the affected areas – 54% of assessed households in Umerkot, 39% in Mirpurkhas and 32% in Sanghar have reported living in partially damaged households. Similarly, 51% households in Sanghar and 29% households in Mirpurkhas are reportedly still living in open air / chappars. The OCHA RNA indicates that out of 6,142 kacha shelters / houses in Dadu and 4,133 kacha shelters / houses in Tando Muhammad Khan that existed before the floods, 694 in Dadu and 877 in Tando Muhammad Khan has been completely damaged, whereas 2,194 in Dadu and 1,072 has been partially damaged in Tando Muhammad Khan. Similarly, out of pre-disaster 601 pakka shelters / houses in Dadu and 3,616 in Tando Muhammad Khan, 105 shelters in Dadu and 307 shelters in Tando Muhammad Khan have been partially damaged.
Considering upcoming winter and fast plummeting temperature, the affected populations urgently need shelter assistance to support them in repairing or rebuilding their damaged shelters. Moreover, with the risk of second wave of COVID-19 spreading in the country, living in communal spaces or in the open is highly detrimental to the health and life of the flood affected and vulnerable communities. Therefore, as per findings of the ERNA and OCHA-RNA, there is an urgent need of repair and rehabilitation of shelters supported through distribution of essential shelter material, kits and tools to the affected families.

1- Project Activities

Without adequate shelter, the affected population are unable to restore their lives/livelihoods as they remain vulnerable to protection and health issues, and their dependence on external support to meet their most basic needs is prolonged. NDC has devised a coordinated and customised shelter support package, taking into consideration the level of damages and actual needs of the affected population. These customised packages will include essential tools and equipment and cash grant (PKR 10,000 for fully damaged and PKR 5,000 for partially damaged shelters) for repair or rehabilitation of damaged shelters. In this regard, all three NDC partners ACTED, CONCERN and HANDS will distribute shelter packages among selected vulnerable beneficiaries to enable them to repair or rehabilitate their damaged shelters. The package will constitute shelter construction material / tools, including 16 ft. long steel girder, 10 ft. long bamboo for roof, chicks, lime, polythene sheet, 5-6 mm cotton rope, nails, iron door and iron window. The shelter repair kits for fully damaged households will include iron door and window whereas, kits for partially damaged households will not include doors and windows. The package will further include cash grant worth PKR 10,000 for fully damaged and PKR 5,000 for partially damaged households for labour work. The already provided bamboo and tarpaulin sheets under the Flood Emergency Response will complement the use and design of this package. The selected beneficiary households will also receive training and shelter IEC material with the aim to help them construct safer and resilient shelters using the provided/locally available material (bamboos, rope, lime and tarpaulin sheets). The IEC material will include information and messages on skill development for improved construction and maintenance of the shelter units, following weather and hazard resilient construction techniques. The sessions will be delivered by the project technical staff to orient the participants on building back safer shelter techniques, maintenance and safe use of lime, mainly for plastering and its slacking process. The sessions will be delivered using pictorial messages and guidelines. Most vulnerable House hold beneficiaries without house hold items will receive NFI (Non Food Items) Kits. Through this proposed shelter NFI intervention, partial and fully shelter repair kits and NFI Kits will be provided by NDC partners HANDS, ACTED and CONCERN Worldwide to vulnerable communities in Sindh province. NDC partner ACTED will implement in district Mirpurkhaas and Umerkot , HANDS in Sangar and Sajawal (HANDS), and CONCERN in Dadu and Tando Mohammad Khan (CONCERN). Through the proposed intervention 2000 beneficiary households (2800 individuals) with fully damaged houses and 2500 households (5600 individuals) with partially damaged homes will be provided with NFI kit and one shelter repair kit, containing the necessary materials and tools needed for the rehabilitation of a shelter. By providing in-kind material, vulnerable members of the community – particularly those belonging to female-headed households, widows, victims of gender-based violence (GBV), pregnant and lactating women (PLW), persons with disabilities (PWD) as well as child and elderly-headed households – will be able to repair their houses and live in a dignified and sustainable manner, without having to travel long distance to markets and transporting the materials back home.
