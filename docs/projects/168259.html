Project Summary

1. Mapping and strengthening of school protection mechanisms in 15 schools with identification of community CP and PSS focal points. Training for focal points on identification and referral of children to CP and MPHSS services.
2. Identify of child protection needs particularly for unaccompanied children and child-headed households, responding to needs with provision of case management services across 15 communities targeting 2,000 children.
4. Provision of MHPSS services for 7,000 people ensuring accessibility for marginalised groups with particular focus on children, women and child-headed households and people with disabilities.
5. Set up of CP and GBV community groups, including school focal points, to strengthen reporting mechanisms in 15 communities, including complaint and feedback systems. Development of advocacy plans for wider community engagement on CP and GBV and sharing of information about available services.
6. Establish women and child friendly spaces within 15 communities.
7. Provide training to local partners on GBV risk mitigation and integration of GBV in wider humanitarian response programming across clusters.
8. Conduct assessments on protection, child protection, gender and GBV, (including PSEA) and advocate for an integrated approach on these issues of assessments conducted by other sectors
9. Strengthen capacity of government to deliver quality services, by provision of capacity building workshops for social workers and support in collection and reporting of data on protection issues and services.
