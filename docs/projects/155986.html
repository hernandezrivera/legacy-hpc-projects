Project Summary

Since the beginning of its presence in the Country, Armadilla has worked in the field of health assistance, with a specific focus on children and persons with disabilities. The Syrian crisis in March 2011, has dramatically worsened the situation, especially for the displaced people that leaving behind the little they owned. Armadilla has been supporting the work of both the local partner Zahret Al Madan (ZAM) and of the Centre for Prosthesis and Rehabilitation (CPR) of Ibn Al-Nafis Hospital, in implementing rehabilitation activities for people with disabilities (both physical and cognitive), through the provision of specific training to the local health workers with the aim of increasing their professional skills. The Ibn Al-Nafis Hospital, has a huge gap in human resources capacity, as well as technical equipment, in the health sector in general and particularly in the field of rehabilitation services (diagnosis, prosthesis, physical therapy), as many experienced health professionals were displaced inside and outside Syria. The main objective of the project is the increase of number of physical rehabilitation sessions thanks to the specific trained/re-trained for health staff on related topics. Specifically, the purpose of the project is to strengthen the capacity of technical workers of the Centre for Prosthesis and Rehabilitation (CPR) Ibn Al-Nafis Hospital of Damascus (medical doctors, physical therapists, occupational therapists) through an intensive cycle of training about physical therapy, and prosthesis making, including specific modules on children and youngsters.
The project is integrated with activities related to child protection, implemented by Zahret Al Madan and Armadilla in the Southern Suburbs of Damascus (Midan Kawakibi), which volunteers and workers collaborate on permanent basis with the staff of the CPR for the care and therapy plans for children and youngsters with disability.
Moreover, the project aims to support the establishment of the Occupational Degree in the University Al-Manara of Latakia, through the provision of teaching given by International professors of the University of Modena and Reggio-Emilia (Italy).

Needs

In the whole of Syria health care systems for trauma, suffers for lack of equipment and, above all, health workers are insufficiently trained. The Prosthetic and Rehabilitation Center (CPR) of the Ibn Al Nafis public hospital is the only public hospital in Syria where prosthetics and orthosis are built and physiotherapy sessions for people with born or acquired (amputations) disabilities are extensively implemented, which bring uncountable of patients in requesting assistance and health cares. The CPR receives a huge number of requests for diagnosis and treatment, which can be processed only minimally (about the 25% of the requests may be accepted due to understaff and lack of space). Treatment for minors is particularly complex, due to the low number of technicians specialized in working with children and youngsters, both in prosthetics and rehabilitation paths. Moreover, the CPR is rehabilitating with public funds a new building, in order to guarantee the access to more users and the possibility to hosts people coming from other areas for the needed complete treatment cycle. Indeed, it worth to be mentioned that one of the most challenging point, is the incapacity of the Centre to offer the possibility of hospitalization (inpatient care), which does not allow patients to follow the entire cycle of therapy, due to the high costs of staying in Damascus.Regarding the Occupational Therapy degree, has been observed that the practice is totally absent on the Syrian territory, while professionals trained on the subject will be able to immediately give the possibility to People with Disability to perform daily personal and work tasks, allow them to quickly recover abilities which support their social inclusion. Armadilla, is working since the end of 2017 in establishing the first Occupational Therapy (OT) degree course. Needs assessment are continuously performed by the I-NGO, through bi-.weekly meetings with the Responsible of the CPR, in order to determine needs and possible activities to be performed in the Centre. Concerning the OT related activities, the need to establish the course has been widely discussed with relevant experts of the sector, which have stressed the importance to introduce this speciality, under a legal framework, in the Country, due to the high number of PwD in needs.

Implementation of activities

Project activities will be implemented in strict collaboration with local partners (CPR of Damascus and University Manara of Latakia). At this aim, Armadilla already established general Memorandum of Understanding with the two entities, which are willing to cooperate to the project. Moreover, Armadilla since the year 2006 has signed a protocol of cooperation with the Ministry of Education of Syria, through the collaboration with the CPR.Activities will be monitored by Armadilla’s staff in Syria and abroad, which also will take care of the logistics details needed for the realization of the project.Training for CPR health workers will be performed by Lebanese professionals (which can guarantee their constant presence in the Country). Training course and sessions (topics and modalities) will be drafted in conjunction with the responsible of the CPR. Also purchase of goods (equipment for the CPR) will be realized in strict coordination with the CPR Responsible, according to the relevant needs observed.Training for students in OT will be performed by International professors of European Universities, which will deliver the teaching according to the approved General Teaching Plan for OT degree course.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Access to the Area of the activitiesThe two places where the project will be implemented, Centre for Prosthesis and Rehabilitation (CPR) of Damascus and University Al Manara of Latakia, are area so far considered totally. However, the Organisation will be in continuous communication with the relevant security actors in order to organize activities and movements only after consultation with them. Availability of goodsGoods will be purchased on the local market, looking for the best vendor in terms of economy and capacity of guarantee the provision of service. A back-up list of potential suppliers guarantees the possibility of finding the goods in case of problems will be drafted. Lack of permission to enter in Syria for expatriate staff Training activities for CPR health workers will be developed using Lebanese professionals, which do not have problems in entering the territory. Monitoring and logistics activities will be realized through Syrian staff of the Organisation, supported by remote by international experts. In case of impossibility for International professors to give on site teaching to students, remote training will be implemented.
