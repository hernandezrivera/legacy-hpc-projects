This project is proposed in response to Shelter Obj 2: Reinforce an enabling protection environment and community cohesion by improving housing and related community/public infrastructure. 2.1.1.1: Rehabilitation of damaged or unfinished housing & 2.1.1.2: Supply and installation of HH level solar energy systems.
NFI Obj1: Provide life-saving & life-sustaining NFI support 1.1.1.1: Provision of core NFIs
Rescate proposes this project to meet the dire SNFI need of host communities and returnees’ population to Aleppo, Deir Ez Zour (DEZ), and Rural Damascus (RDAM). In consideration of the improving security situation in Syria, and the refugees return, coincided with security deterioration & IDPs influx from South & North. The increasing numbers of return compounded by the deteriorating economic situation makes SNFI a much-needed sector, especially in areas of dire vulnerability due to high returns rate & lack of basic services compounded with very limited livelihood opportunities which curbs HHs ability to live in normalcy.

In line with the SNFI objectives & guidance, Rescate selected the most vulnerable communities, we will respond to the urgent population needs in rehabilitation of their houses in DEZ, Jabal Saman, & RDAM which have been assessed & are considered severity 2&3 where services resources & assistance are limited. As a result, immediate response is needed from humanitarian organizations & stakeholders to minimize the suffering of those who have been living in hardship circumstances & to encourage people to return to their areas of origin.
In DEZ, Rescate implemented WASH project in 2021 in Al Omal & Air Port neighborhoods, due to limited fund, the pipes were laid in the main axis only, yet, the buildings links were not implemented. Under this proposal, Rescate will include the common WASH infrastructure to facilitate clean water at homes & ensure sanitation network is also connected to the main line.
In RDAM, Rescate completed a project for safe access to schools, during the community mobilization, the city councils urged Rescate to submit DHRs &NFIs to meet the dire IDPs & returnees needs. Rescate received 250 approved applications from HHs. The project will complement the endeavor to enhance social cohesion with the football play-ground provided from Rescate contribution to RDAM in 2022 on the fringe of the project.
Socio-economic assessment in the targeted areas showed that poverty rates exceed 90%, therefore; the project will include an activity to distribute one off CVA at the core NFI rate $200 to improve community & HHs level resilience. This will ensure the appropriate protection against seasonal conditions given the shortage of power & limited purchase capacity. Blanket distribution to all 575 HHs to avoid tension or cause harm.
Rescate in DEZ will focus on residents in the Old Airport area where returnees since 2018 are unable to rehabilitate their houses. Rescate proposes to rehabilitate 75 houses in Al Omal &100 in the Old Airport. Similar cases in RDAM & Aleppo where we targeted most vulnerable returnees, Dahret Awad fully inhabited by returnees under lack of public services, & electricity. Target of this intervention to support vulnerable communities & HHs with core NFI CVA & streetlights as a step coupled with reinforce an enabling protection environment & community by improving housing.

3 - Needs

DEZ project was not funded, Aleppo project is funded,150 DHRs under implementation. Rescate conducted needs assessment in Aleppo, DEZ, and RDAM on the fringe of its projects in the same targeted areas. Assessment BOQs in compliance with Shelter SOPs were prepared, in parallel; info also obtained during community mobilization & FGDs. Rescate coordinated with the sector, community leaders & using data from UN OCHA and sectors. To identify the gaps with prioritization on need-based methodology, especially that needs are increasing due to the economic situation deterioration & lack of basic services. Lack of access to basic services resulted in a total of 5.2m persons in need, including all segments of society. Over 83% of POP are living under the poverty line (HNAP) eventually unaffordable with insufficient daily labour. HNO 2023 shelter Revised PiN 2,827,609 & NFI Revised PiN 4,424,803 show critical need for support. Rescate provide HH initial services, per SWG SOPs & WASH/PWDs & elderly care. Supply & installation HH solar energy to privacy & protection, study & mostly normalcy. Rescate propose to distribute CVA to cover core NFIs in addition to provide streetlights & Common WASH to enhance safety & protection. Proposal is designed to meet the operating plan as developed by inter-sectoral coordination priorities & complementarity with other projects under area-based intervention.

4 - Implementation of activities

Rescate designed this project and will work through both needs and area-based approaches where relevant.In adherence to SWG guidance RDHs, the project will contribute to the strategic cluster objectives by supporting vulnerable returnees to live in dignity in their homes. The solutions are rather durable to assist inhabitants to live in decent life sustaining standard. The project has direct contribution to the S/NFI sector by improving the housing conditions to the most vulnerable people in the selected and approved areas in DEZ, Aleppo & RDAM. This will be done through RDHs & upgrading quality standards by including WASH component for each building, streetlights if needed, & one round of CVA to support 575 HHs for core NFIs. Activities are: Coordination with MOLAE & Governorate approvals.Data collection & beneficiaries’ registration in collaboration with MayorsCall for proposals for DHR, HHs solar system, common areas WASH & street lights work. Contractors awarded to implement works per the standard procurement and evaluation policies. Site supervision & monitoring on materials & works, M&E, Field visits, beneficiaries’ feedback on the assistance. Distribution of CVA Final works’ receipt at each house concluded with the governorate committee Post works’ evaluation, beneficiary satisfaction surveys conducted upon receipt of works after warranty period 4Ws, PDM & closing reports financial & narrative submitted to Donor & sectors.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

SNFI project is unique as NFIs will be shared via the center, in mitigation to ensure blanket benefits, supervision & staff training to ensure all centers’ residents are equally benefitting. Risk of excluding any beneficiary is likely & is clearly communicated within community & amongst different groups. Monitoring & analysis effectively disseminated for the use of all actors. Safety protection & dignity will be transversal during the project activities. The principles of Do No Harm enforced as Rescate will have a conflict sensitivity analysis, we will act upon understanding interactions, context & identifying the relations between different targeted groups to avoid negative impacts. We will ensure children, elderly & PWDs benefit & their needs are foreseen. NFI direct outreach by volunteers & shelter install additional gear. The project enables equal and impartial access since assistance is provided on equal terms to all vulnerable BENs. Rescate will avoid discrimination based on gender, age, and community under RDHs and NFIs. To support self-protection, Rescate will adopt protection guidelines and SNFI & SOPs in the intervention. GBV is strongly mainstreamed in this project via enhanced privacy in discussion with beneficiaries on needs (doors, lights, windows). Safeguarding accountability by direct discussion with community/centers residents. Claim & reporting mechanisms are in place & supervised by PM and HoM. Coordination with health sector if need arises.


8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Risk of sexual exploitation: Gender-based violence and sexual exploitation and abuse Likelihood: Low – Impact: High Mitigation measure: All Rescate staff, contractors, & partners receive induction awareness sessions & are required to sign the Code of Conduct which directly refers to the (PSEA) and to (GBV). As part of ongoing efforts to improve accountability, Rescate hangs the contact information at each site in commitment to implement a beneficiary Complaints and Response Mechanisms (CRM) in all of its project to allow beneficiaries to raise concerns, make complaints and give feedback on Rescate work, as well as the contractors’ work, & to relay any arising issue, including potential PSEA or GBV abuse. Collaborate in seeking case management services to connect those at risk/survivors of protection abuses with relevant services. Rescate attends trainings to enhance Rescate team capacities, noting that two female engineers are in the team for easier communicates with females in the project. All PSEA and/or GBV issues will be addressed first by the Project Manager, who will share the concern with the HoM based in Damascus, in order to establish a solution or way to proceed to enhance the protection of the PSEA/GBV survivor. When needed, the HoM will scale the issue to ONG Rescate HQ in Spain, where the Director, in consultation with the gender specialists’ team, will look for potential solutions and/or an appropriate way to proceed.


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Operational, one of the possible risks is miscommunication with different stakeholders’ groups. This event could have major consequences. Resources & response capacities being limited & far below known needs, there will always be perception of biased selection of response activities, thereby creating or exacerbating tension. Risk of NFIs increased prices due to heavy inflation, exchange rate. M already accounted for the increased inflation in the budget per core NFI costs, Contractor shall have 30% down payment to purchase materials under Rescate observation to quality, quantity & market availability Fuel shortage & procurement supply delays are the Contractors’ responsibility & mitigated to ensure immediate storage of the purchased materials. Approval delays & Covid-19 restrictions are calculated in the project duration; hence schedule works in a manner to avoid mixing workers. Rescate will be working in strong coordination with ST, stakeholders & communities to facilitate focused response with strong need-based approach. Rescate had reviewed reports by OCHA, & SNFI sectors on conflict analysis before planning the response, ensured that; 1 comprehensive needs analysis is conducted with stakeholders & is documented & made transparent, 2 during implementation, ensure those in similar situation of need, at least within the same vulnerability, are reached equally, 3 keeping stakeholders & communities regularly informed of the project & its implementation progress.
