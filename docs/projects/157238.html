Project Summary

This project would be implemented to accelerate the provision of Emergency life-saving integrated primary and reproductive health services for Elmeiram locality, West Kordofan State.
Doaa Al-Afiya Medical Organization (DAMO) will work closely with the health sector to Support health center in Elmeiram locality to provide primary health services through training of health care workers; providing operational costs (stationery, water supply, soap, cleaning materials and medical waste management tools, casual workers for cleaning, and consumables); providing essential lifesaving drugs, consumables and lab reagents; and reporting format, Support joint supervisory visits to the targeted health facilities by SMOH, locality health teams and ,Collect disease surveillance and Early Warning data from sentinel sites and share it with MOH and WHO on a weekly basis ,Train health committees on community mobilization treatment and make awareness against communicable disease especially child hood diseases, Provide services through SMoH Health Centre including ANC and PNC, vaccination , birth spacing messaging will be offered as part of the mother counselling during the PNC visits, distribution of clean delivery kits, midwives delivery kits, and financial support for referral for obstetric emergencies (With reference to SMoH guidelines and decision taken by the medical practitioner in light of the guidelines, Conduct service training for village midwives on guidelines for the routine care of women during pregnancy.

Needs

In line with the MYHRP outcomes, This project will Provide access sustain f basic life-saving health services to South Sudan Refugees (SSRs) and host communities in El Meriam, West Kordofan, and build their capacity to be self reliant and able to respond and overcome Health related emergencies by the end of the project in 2020 In August 2005 West Kordufan State was abolished and its territory was divided between North and South Kordufan States, in implementation of the Protocol between the Government of Sudan and the Sudan People’s Liberation Movement (SPLM) on the resolution of conflict in Southern Kordufan/Nuba Mountains and Blue Nile States signed at Naivasha, Kenya, 26 May 2004. The new state has been formed in a poor infra structure as it didn’t got enough care to establish a well-defined health system. The instability of the state and the continuous conflicts and tribal clashes also has a negative impact on the provision of basic services. The persistent armed conflicts in South Sudan between the official army and the rebelled faction led to substantial influx of South Sudan refugees to the state since 2013 till date settled in identified areas but predominantly sharing the available resources with the host communities increasing the burden to the state. . The influx of the refugees continued till after the mid-2017 and it is expected to receive more refugees due to the unceasing fighting mainly in the neighbouring counties and the prolonged instability which might lead to food shortage in South Sudan.According to HAC, an estimated 5,462 refugees have arrived in El Meriam and Kharasana since 1 February 2017. This brings the total new arrivals since January to 7,148 individuals excluding the old caseload of 4,361 and 13,195 refugees in El Meriam and Kharasana respectively. Those refugees are arriving from a country with disrupted health system, experienced acute communicable disease outbreaks . According to HNO 2018 Poor access to water, sanitation and hygiene(WASH) services has contributed to the occurrence and spread of communicable diseases such as Acute Watery Diarrhea (AWD) across all 18 States in the country. As a result, acute respiratory diseases, diarrhoea, measles, and malaria are the main under five killers. There have been outbreaks of measles, AWD and recently Chikungunya in the country (FMoH/WHO ).

Activities

1.Provision of primary health care services with particular focus on women, children, and vulnerable population SSRs by supporting PHC with staff. 2.Rehabilitation of 1 PHC (with incinerator for Medical Waste treatment), latrines, and a water supply system, and Maintain one central store for medicines supplies MoH and RH commodities (UNFPA) 3.Purchase and provision of essential medicines. Provision of UNICEF PHC kits, WHO RRK kits and Reproductive Health supplies from UNFPA at Health facilities. 4.Training sessions for the health staff (CHW, Nurses, etc.) in public health threads and infectious diseases (topics such as ARI, Measles, Diarrhea, Malaria, Eyes Infections and Meningitis, EWARS). (3 days sessions, 2 separate training for health staff and PE) 5.Strengthen the coordination and health information system on outbreak and emergency preparedness and response in collaboration with the WHO, and the SMoH. 2 days training sessions for the health staff and the PEs on report writing and EP&R report formats . 6.Strengthened the preparedness and control mechanisms for emergencies and infectious diseases outbreaks in coordination with the SMoH, the local community committees and local authorities. and support FMoH vaccination campaigns. 7.Provide incentives for state Ministry of Health staff including :Medical Doctor 1Medical Assistants, 1 Pharmacist Assistance, 2 Nurses, 4Midwives, Nutrition, and community health promotion CHW. 8.Creation of 2 Health Committee Group and training of 20 SSRs and 20 host community leaders Health Engaged with local authorities on topics including Supervision & monitoring,and facility management
