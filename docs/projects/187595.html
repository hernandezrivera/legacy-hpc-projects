Project Summary

The project aims to support the resilience of communities through food security and agriculture Response in NW Syria to address the identified needs of the most vulnerable population of IDPs and Host Communities in NW Syria Aleppo (Mainly Afrin, and Atareb). The intervention will be designed in line with FSL Cluster guidelines, and the strategic objectives of the sector in HRP 2022 with considering the lessons learnt from similar projects that have been implemented by Bonyan. The project aims to provide humanitarian support to improve food security and increase access to livelihood opportunities in northwest Syria. Targeting 91,800 individuals (22,032 Men, 25,704Women, 20,196 Girls, 23,868Boys – 5% Persons with disabilities - approximately 4500 households). through the project, Bonyan will provide:
1- distribution of 1500 bread bundles on a daily basis for 900HHs per cycle. The activity will be implemented for 9 months, 3 cycles the total HHs 2700, 16200 individuals.
Each cycle 3-month long (78 days) 26 days per month.
2- Provision of Food Vouchers to 2000 HHs 12000 individuals of the most vulnerable population in Afrin district, on monthly basis for a total period of 8 months.
3- Supporting the Households to sustain food consumption through vouchers for backyard gardening for 1500 HHs 9000 individuals.
4- Creating income-generating activities for 600 HHs 3600 individuals including Building capacities of youth and women through Vocational training and enabling them to start their own businesses.
5- Infrastructure rehabilitation (Bakeries, flour mills and silos) which will assist 3500 HHs, 21000 individuals.
6-Rehabitation of economic infrastructure targeting 5000 HHs 30000 individuals.
Bonyan will establish community committees in the target locations to involve the community in the selection and verification process of the beneficiaries including HLP due diligence and selection criteria. The committee will consist of locals from (IDPs and host community), members of agricultural associations, and members of local councils. The committees will facilitate communication with the beneficiaries as well as the follow-up process.
The fund of this project is secured for all of it’s activates with 50% capacity only, regarding the secured percentage Bonyan has already started the implementation of its activates.

4 - Needs

According to the Humanitarian Need Assessment (HNA) report conducted in mid of 2021, an estimated 6.5 million Syrians are food insecure and an additional 2.5 million people are at risk of food insecurity. An estimated 40% of Syrians spend over 65% of their expenditures on food, 57.2% of food-insecure households reported to have outstanding debt, and 65% of food-insecure people are adopting crisis or emergency harmful coping strategies. The main drivers to food insecurity are prolonged displacements, loss and lack of livelihoods, and reduced production capacity which in turn resulted in limited physical and financial access to food, high prices, and inflation contributing to reduced purchasing power and continuous livelihood depletion of the most vulnerable populations. Bonyan has conducted a needs assessment covering all the project areas overstretched capacities, unemployment, or displacement. Limited livelihood opportunities lead to food insecurity due to limited income, especially for women. Thus, vocational training can support women who need sustaining their families such as opportunities/ knowledge/ skills to create income. Moreover, distributing food baskets and bread will help these families to find better jobs, and it will reduce the protection risks, especially women and children as well as reduce the increase of negative coping mechanisms of the communities such as crime, child labour or early marriage. Further, PWDs will have access to the opportunities

5 - Implementation of activities

In the initial stage, Bonyan will conduct an outreach campaign to announce the project in the target area including selection and vulnerability criteria, as well as feedback mechanisms. Coordination efforts will be made with the relevant stakeholders.Market assessment will be conducted to be a baseline for all items prices (foods, backyard gardening inputs, seeds, and VT). during this Bonyan will define the BNFs after data collection and analysis.Bonyan will identify the bakeries that will be contracted, where the bakery should have accessible and safe distribution points.identifying the E-voucher system provider, and announcing the other activities tenders.Following that, the distribution of E-vouchers will be conducted in a distribution center by Bonyan team. the E-vouchers provider will provide training on how to use the system for both Bonyan team and the suppliers. Regular monitoring of the prices of food items that are included in the assistance will be carried out monthly.Bonyan will design a VT plan with the preferred subjects by the BNFs and based on the market need.where the distribution of the seeds will be also based on the beneficiaries’ preference. Bonyan will establish community committees in the target locations to involve the community in the selection and verification process of the beneficiaries including HLP due diligence and selection criteria. 9 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk: Abuse of Authority by implementing contractor or construction workers (GBV, Sexual exploitation, discrimination, monetary coercion). Likelihood: low. Impact: High. Mitigation Measures: Discuss with community members about the best mitigation measures for female headed HHs or people with special needs (i.e., conduct the tent construction works in presence of members of community representatives or in presence of a male relative of the beneficiary) 2. Ensure that staff have read and signed their Code of Conduct.Risk: Children below 18 years old conduct works on behalf of humanitarian organization, its contractors. Likelihood: low. Impact: High. Add zero-tolerance disclosure on child labor in the contracts.Risk: Crowding at bread distribution points may expose women to the risk of sexual harassment. And Distance between beneficiary residents and distribution points may increase the risk for female to sexual harassment. Likelihood: low. Impact: High. Mitigation Measures: Contract with bakeries that have several distribution points Ensure that daily distribution period in all points does not result crowding
