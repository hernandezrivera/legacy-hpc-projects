Project Summary

The World Food Programme (WFP) is United Nations frontline agency in the fight against hunger and working closely with the Government of Ethiopia (GoE) and humanitarian partners to save lives as well as strengthen and support the resilience of the most vulnerable population.
Ethiopia CO (WFP) continues to provide emergency and lifesaving food assistance in conflict, post-conflict or disaster situations based on government request. WFP has been supporting the Government of Ethiopia in its effort to tackle the country’s food insecurity problems through providing humanitarian food assistance and addressing underlying causes. Hence, WFP supports the Humanitarian Response Plan (HRP), providing General Food Distribution (GFD) to close to 6 million food insecure beneficiaries in Somali, Tigray, Amhara and Afra regions. The majority of GFD is provided through in-kind (food assistance) with an increasing portion in cash-based transfers (CBT).

1. - Projects activities

WFP will work with the regional Disaster Risk Management Commission (DRMC) at federal and regional level to coordinate food regions in Tigray, Somali and Amhara regions. Based on information from various food security assessments and monitoring surveys, WFP will support DRMCs and partners in conducting beneficiary targeting during the inception of the project, and this will include following the national targeting guidelines that provides key indicators to support in identifying households to be assisted. After the targeting exercise, WFP will work on the prioritization exercise, based on available evidence to ensure that assistance is provided to the most food insecure people in the regions. The prioritization will follow the guidelines that are agreed by other food partners in the country. Food dispatches will be dispatched from main logistics hubs that are strategically located in the targeted regions, and commodities will be delivered to various food distribution points that are located close to where beneficiaries are located. To follow-up on response achievements and challenges, WFP will conduct onsite and post distribution monitoring during the food response period. This will be conducted through field visits to various food distribution points, where field monitors will conduct household interviews with both beneficiaries and non-beneficiaries. Cross cutting issues will be incorporated in the monitoring tools, including gender, protection and PSEA, to ensure that these are followed during the project cycle. A call center will also continue to be managed by WFP to ensure that all complaints are recorded, and feedback is provided to beneficiaries. This is expected to improve project implementation and ensure that voices of beneficiaries are heard. Continuous evaluation exercise will be conducted, through both external engagement of experts and internal evaluation missions. These oversight missions will help to identity areas that require strengthening by WFP and partners. WFP has warehouses and sub offices that are strategically located in the target locations. During the implementation process, WFP will make a reference to assessment that will be conducted by partners including the government.

3.2 - How will your project work with relevant local & national actors (e.g. CSOs, advocacy groups) in support of effective design, implementation and monitoring of the response, as well as ensure their meaningful participation in supporting this response

The community are put at the center of the project cycle. FGD, physical meetings and sensitization sessions, food distribution committees, community representatives will be used to bring community participation. Community feedback and response mechanism is functional in the response locations. Active helpline is established centrally with various language where target community could share their query, concern and seek advice. The help desks are also established in distribution points. The various community structures include representatives from the target population, host community and government officials. WFP closely works with the various government sectors at Woreda level including women and children affairs, education sector, health, labor and social affairs and disaster risk management commission, youth groups, and women groups.
