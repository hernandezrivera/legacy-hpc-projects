Project Summary

The humanitarian response and resources are shrinking day by day hence limiting the response to be targeted for the most vulnerable individuals only. The returnees specifically women will face difficulties in accessing humanitarian assistance and other government sponsored services due to the unavailability of identity cards and other legal documents. So there will be an urgent need of ensuring that they get their legal documents and to be aware about their rights and entitlements. The vulnerable individuals including women, children, disabled and elderly will be further at risk because they are mostly neglected and are unable to access services due to their vulnerabilities. The proposed project will be targeting specifically the most vulnerable individuals to access services equitably, without any discrimination and in a dignified way.
