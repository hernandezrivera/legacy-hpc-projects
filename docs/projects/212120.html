Project Summary

The association owns a building consisted of two-floors structure with an area of 275 square meters for the one floor in Sidi Mekdad area which is located in the countryside of Damascus (Western Ghouta).
The association aims to complete the work of rehabilitate, finish and equip the medical clinics with the necessary medical equipment according to the standard specifications approved for medical centers.
The project is based on creating a medical center that includes 12 specialized clinics operating on a daily basis, in addition to a medical analysis laboratory, a radiology department, and a 24/24 ambulance unit.
Finally, the project aims to provide integrated medical services to the beneficiary families of the poor, with nominal fees and free of charge for the severe need families. In addition to reducing congestion in nearby medical centers.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

During the Syrian Crisis, a lot of infrastructure destroyed in whole the areas which witnessed a struggle (the houses-services properties-factories,..... etc.) and also the health aspect was affected by this destruction while many of medical centers or hospitals were affected On the other hand, many properties stopped to continue their constructions or equipment’s. Therefore, the idea of the project came from this framework (Rehabilitating and equipping clinics in the medical center owned to the association in Sidi Mekdad).the clinics targeted are distributed between 4 clinics( Gynecology clinic, Pediatric clinic, Physical therapy clinic, General check-up clinic).Sidi Miqdad is located on the Western Ghouta of the Capital Damascus from the southeastern side. The area was subjected to large-scale military operations, which led to partial destruction of most of the residential and service buildings and witnessed cases of forced displacement. At the end of 2017, the return movements began. On the other hand, the basic services to the region have been delayed to the present time (no clinics or medical centers) According to the local authority’s estimation, about 7,000 families ,and the area hosts 2,000 families of the displaced people. The population is divided into 40% children, 30% women and 30% men. all the mentioned families suffer from high levels of poverty. In addition, the nearest medical point is about 5 kilometers away from the targeted area, taking in consideration the lack of ways and the difficulties in transportation.It must be in consideration during the implementation process to conduct an engineering inspection of the specified medical center, estimating the quantities of materials needed for the required rehabilitating and medical equipment’s, and contracting with the contractor while adhering to the necessary medical standards.Conducting periodic monitoring reports and field visits throughout the implementation period until the end of the project, preparing the final report by the competent supervisor of the project and closing the project. Obligation of occupational safety standards is necessary to make the workers and workspace safe (risks during Construction-Theft-Fire occurring) also avoiding Child labor by Obliging the contractor to provide a list of workers and their ages.The project also aims to provide integrated protection for the returning families, including protection from Sexual Exploitation and AbuseThe project is subject many kinds of risks such high prices of rehabilitating materials and medical equipment and the lack and scarcity of them so they must allocate a contingency margin in the budget and diversifying sources in addition to the risks of lack of fuel allocated for rehabilitating operations, so it needs to estimate the fuel quantities or buying fuel from the black market when necessary.
