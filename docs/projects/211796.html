Project Summary

SHO conducted a needs assessment in NWS in the (Raju, Bulbul and Mabtali) Of over 77529 residents, 65% are IDPs. Most of the population depends on daily work and agriculture related activities.
Over 1454 F-HH were found in the three SDs.
Support access to food/and food markets.
Improve agriculture-related livelihoods and support farmers and small businesses.
Enhance the female-headed household’s access to IG activities and source of income.
Support the CBI through CFW
Provision of small grants to already available households’ livelihoods
"In 2024, 16.7 million people are assessed to need humanitarian assistance across Syria, up from 15.3 million in 2023. Of the 16.7 million people in need, 5.5 million are displaced, including over 2 million who live in last resort sites."
AND As indicated in the Syria HNO 2023, the top three unmet priorities are:58% Food/Nutrition assistance, 53% Non-food items assistance,
48% Livelihood assistance
This project will contribute significantly to supporting the self-reliance of affected families by building productive assets and restoring or creating income-generating opportunities to save and sustain lives.
The project includes several interventions
Supporting the olive sector and vulnerable olive farmers in the Idlib and Afrin regions by providing agricultural services and inputs in addition to cash for work related to income generation activities by supporting farmers and families. For 1000 families, 5 dunums/HH (500 hectares)
Olive harvest service through CFW activities
Controlling agricultural pests
Ploughing the lands in the fall and spring seasons
Providing agricultural inputs to 200 families with the aim of stimulating summer crops, which provides a source of income for the targeted families through the distribution of agri-inputs covering 500 square meters of summer home crops. Each voucher is equivalent to 400 US dollars, along with providing training on modern agriculture compatible with Climate changes.
3-Distributing bread for free in the camps at a rate of 10,000 bundles per day for 6 months in Afrin and Idleb( 1100 MT flour )
4-Providing 200 grants to 200 female heads of household to establish income-generating projects related to agricultural projects and food processing ($1,000/per family)
5-Distribution of 8 rounds of CFF vouchers to the most vulnerable families. The value of the voucher is 65 $, divided into categories according to the number of family members. This activity targets 2,000 families in the villages of Afrin
The M&E team will provide follow-up services and supervise field observers, develop the M&E plan and tools to track and monitor project indicators,Daily monitoring of bread distribution(weights and quality)
The project will be implemented in coordination with local authorities and organizations working in the region, and beneficiaries will be selected according to criteria of eligibility and vulnerability to participation by the Food Security Cluster.
After the process of selecting beneficiaries and conducting the necessary contracts, agricultural activities and training for summer crop farmers will begin, and plowing and harvesting services will be provided to olive farmers.
Electronic vouchers will be approved in the summer crop support program to reduce logistical expenses.
Protection risk analysis:
