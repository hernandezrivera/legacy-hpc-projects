Project Summary


In response to the growing needs among PLW and infants, the project main goal is to protect vulnerable women and infants from malnutrition and disease through the implementation of an Infant and Young Child Feeding intervention, including promotion and support for optimal feeding practices through sessions and counselling. According to HNO 2019 draft, "Infant and young child feeding practices remained poor in Syria, the exclusive breastfeeding rates below precises levels at 24 per cent in 11 governorates. Findings from an IYCF assessment conducted in Dara’a shows exclusive breastfeeding as low as 3 per cent in some communities. Barriers to appropriate feeding of young
children include breastfeeding difficulties, illness of the mother, affordability, and lack of support from family.
Coverage of IYCF services are largely awareness raising messages rather than the needed specialized
services such as one-to-one counselling to better support pregnant women and caregivers on maternal
nutrition and to safely feed their young children during the critical window of 0-24 months of age. Capacity of health and nutrition practitioners in specialized IYCF skills needs to be strengthened on a national scale."
The previous paragraph, from HNO 2019 draft, shows that the need for such serveries is enormous, and it reflect that the need is delivering the right messages and habits to PLWs in order to solve the issue.The project will target total of 15000 pregnant and lactating women (PLW) in Homs, Tartous, Dar'a, Aleppo and Rural Damascus.

Needs

Livelihoods depletion, limited financial and physical access to food, the limited access to quality water as well as significantly reduced purchasing power; all these factors negatively influence the nutrition situation, especially in the context of worsening health-care and service coverage that characterizes the situation faced by many people in Syria. According to HNO, total of about 4.7 million people are in need for nutrition assistance including an estimation of 84200 children, under five years old, are considered undernourished. This reflects the spread of various wrong habits in the community regarding the nutrition of infants and young child. Overburdened communities, areas deprived of basic social services and displaced people are in urgent need of nutrition interventions and should be prioritized, to ensure that their nutritional status remains stable and improves. The limited access of boys and girls under five and pregnant and lactating women to lifesaving preventive and curative nutrition services related to Infant and Young Child Feeding (IYCF) in emergencies, micro nutrient supplementation, and treatment for acute malnutrition added to their vulnerability, especially those living under harsh conditions, or in areas with inadequate community and institutional capacity have a significant impact leading to this huge needs. This project will contribute to raise the awareness of pregnant and lactating women in terms of good habits concerning IYCF.

Implementation of activities

The implementation will start by selecting the targeted beneficiaries through several ways:  Word of mouth. GOPA centers Community leaders National and international NGOs. Posters. Awareness sessions will take place over the course of a month, each course will consist of four consecutive sessions with different topics (one session a week). In each course, total of 50 beneficiaries will be targeted (200 attendees). It is planned to target total of 15,000 beneficiaries.The awareness sessions will cover the following topics:* The importance of optimal feeding including exclusive breastfeeding.* Basics of breastfeeding.* Various preventative measures to help mitigate infant malnutrition and enhance good hygiene practices.* The significance of and approaches to family planning.Community awareness sessions will be held in GOPA centers, community halls, schools, shelters or any other facility that can comfortably hold around 50 persons. Through the community awareness sessions, GOPA aims to improve infant and young child feeding practices (IYCF) in the project catchment areas through the promotion of optimal feeding practices. The awareness sessions will target pregnant women, young mothers and other care-givers in the family. Women will be asked to invite family members - including husbands, sons or fathers -- to the awareness sessions. Appropriate halls in the community will be rented to conduct the session in it, contracted midwives and pharmacists will conduct sessions with women. In addition, after the sessions, if women faced any trouble breastfeeding their infant they can call the specialists to get appropriate answers, or help if needed. Conducting awareness sessions will defiantly increase the knowledge in the community in general and especially for pregnant and lactating women, elder women, in addition, will be able to distribute the project messages in their medium, so the benefits will not stop on attendees level, yet there will be indirect beneficiaries from such project.GOPA will distribute a post-session survey to evaluate the effectiveness of the sessions, measure the level of improvement and behavioral changes on the topics addressed and note any suggestions, challenges or concerns to integrate into future sessions. Those who participate will be tested before and after the sessions through a survey to determine improvements in their understanding and knowledge of the issues presented.Each woman will be provided with one dignity and one infant kit after the four awareness sessions are completed (One course).
