Project Summary


The proposed program aims to increase access to PSS assistance to conflict affected populations in East and West Mosul

3 The second specific objective of this program is to strengthen the capacity to respond to child protection
As the staff has already been trained on PSS, PSS Advanced and First Aid,
This project module is gained through the previous experience over one year EADE had delivered in Mosul through its partnership with Mission East in implementing Child/youth safe place in Mosul.

How is your project accountable to affected population

The principle of Do No Harm will be fundamental to ensuring equitable access to services, recruitment, potential legitimization effects, and communication. A detailed MEAL Framework and plan will be developed for the project. Monitoring visits by EADE will be conducted at least monthly to all project sites. The outcomes of these monitoring visits will be discussed with the project team to address quality shortcomings. The system involves sharing project information with beneficiaries, creating opportunities for beneficiaries to participate in implementing the project and a mechanism for receiving and handling feedback and complaints (including a way to be able to provide anonymous feedback). This is performed either through a telephone hotline and/or a suggestion box. The accountability system will encourage information-sharing among children and their families in the targeted IDP camp areas, empowering them to identify areas in which EADE can improve the quality of its programs. During program set-up, EADE will hold consultations with children and adults in targeted areas to seek input on what success would look like for the program and identify the most appropriate method for the CRM. Beneficiaries will also be engaged through active participation in identifying the most pressing needs and developing strategies to address these needs, mapping risks and ensuring feedback is mainstreamed throughout the project.
