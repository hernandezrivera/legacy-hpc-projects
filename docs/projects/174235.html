Project Summary

Climatic shocks have become a recurring phenomenon in Pakistan, exposing the population to food and nutrition insecurity. Pakistan ranks 8th on long-term climate risk index of 2019, and is prone to climate-induced disasters (floods, snowfall, drought, and locust). Vulnerable communities have witnessed multiple climate-induced disasters in previous few decades with the increased frequency and intensity. In a country, where 25 percent of the country' population lives below the poverty line, multiple shocks including recent COVID-19 outbreak have further deteriorated the socio-economic indicators.
With this backdrop, Islamic Relief aims to provide support the most vulnerable communities in targeted districts of Chagai, Noshki, Pishin and Washuk (Baluchistan), Thatta/Sujjawal, and Badin (Sindh), North Waziristan, South Waziristan, Orkazai, and Khyber districts (Khyber Pkhtunkhwa). Targeted districts fall under HRP with prevailing multiple socio-economic vulnerabilities.
The immediate humanitarian assistance will include livelihood assistance through conditional cash disbursement, promoting resilient livelihood initiatives, restoration of affected on and off-farm livelihood, and capacitating youth with entrepreneurship and technical skills.
Proposed action will be implemented in close coordination with the relevant district and provincial authorities/mandated institutes ensuring ownership, linkage development and long-term uptake of the interventions.
Islamic Relief will adapt participatory approach while ensuring inclusion of persons with disabilities, women (prioritizing women headed households and PWDs), elderly, young boys and girls, and children under the age of five.
The proposed interventions will contribute to the overall humanitarian response in Pakistan, with an effective mainstreaming of DRR and protection. Proposed action is in alignment with the Government’s Strategic Preparedness and Response Plan and Global Humanitarian Response Plan (GHRP) to address the direct and indirect immediate humanitarian consequences of the multiple disasters across Pakistan.
Beneficiary targeting strategy of the proposed action is in synergy with the Pakistan 2021, Humanitarian Response Plan's targeting strategy. Multiple vulnerability criteria will be employed in the disaster affected communities to target the most vulnerable segments of society/individuals.
