Project Summary

In 2019, LUKMEF in collaboration with UNFPA, CARE intentional and UNWomen , reached out to over 63,059 women and girls with holistic GBV services including comprehensive services to 431 GBV survivors in the South west and North west regions
LUKMEF in 2020 will continue providing comprehensive GBV services to women and girls affected by the current crisis in the North west and south west regions of Cameroon. Our 2020 intervention will target 110,000 women and girls with life-saving interventions as well as some 20,000 boys and men. This is just a very small number from the over 2.3 million persons who have been affected by the deteriorating humanitarian crisis and are in need of different services. There are 4.3 million persons affected by the crisis (UNOCHA HRP 2019).
Beneficiaries targeted by our project are female IDPs and host communities who are survivors of GBV or are high risk of GBV in the South West and North West regions of Cameroon.
In our intervention throughout 2020, focus will be on:
• mainstreaming GBV prevention and response across the different humanitarian clusters in the North west and south west regions
• Increased capacity of GBV services providers including those in hard to reach areas to provide safe and adequate GBV services directly and through a well established and responsive referral system
• Increased access to critical life saving medical services as well as other GBV services such as PFA, Legal and rapid income generating activities for economic empowerment for survivors of GBV
• Reduce mortality and morbidity among women, girls, adolescents and vulnerable groups through provision of the Minimum Services Package for Reproductive Health (MISP)
• continue to provide integrated temporal shelter to women and girls in needs
• collect, analyze and share GBV data including via the GBVIMS
• distribute 3000 dignity kits to women and girls including those living with disabilities as a key entry point to other GBV service uptake

Besoins / Needs

in 2019, the number of GBV cases continued to rise in the two regions requiing conprehensive services. Moe than 2136 GBV cases were reported in this region alone. In the South West, North West with over 700,000 IDPs, and host populations in need of relief assistance, these populations will indirectly benefit from awareness/information campaigns, an expansion of GBV and SRH services and the increased capacity of health and other service providers to give quality GBV services (including case management, GBV prevention and mitigation and psychosocial first aid (PFA), psychosocial support (PSS) and clinical management of rape survivor services). They will also have increased access to safe delivery, family planning and other SRHR information and services.In the North West, the GBV and SRH program for women and girls living with disabilities will also benefit men, boys, the elderly and caretakers by providing the community with information/awareness on GBV and SRHR issues and information/referrals to available services for people living with disabilities. given the vulnerability levels of most women and girls in these affected regions, this project seeks to address the livelihood needs of women through rapid income generating activitiesas well
