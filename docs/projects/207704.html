Project Summary

The project aims at providing access to protective learning routines for 90.000 conflict and extremism affected children and adolescents through support to operational formal schools (primary and secondary) in Far-north region. The project intends through the blend of interventions proposed (distribution of school inputs, teachers training, studies) to prevent attacks on education and ensure education in emergencies delivery in conflict and high insecurity context.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The target population consists of pupils and teachers from seven subdivisions in the Far-north region. The intervention zones are the subdivisions hosting the highest numbers of enrolled children affected by the Lac Chad Basin crisis, which are Logone and Chari Division (Fotokol, Makary, Blangoua), Mayo Sava Division (Kolofata) and Mayo Tsanaga Division (Mokolo, Koza and Mayo Moskota)The project targets 90.000 children and adolescents in school and vocational training. It will build the capacities of 1.900 teachers from primary and secondary schools on PSS and C/DRR, for them to use adequate pedagogical and in-class practises.In order to generate foster school-based risk informed humanitarian action and evidence generation, the project will also conduct a Protective Learning Environment study.
