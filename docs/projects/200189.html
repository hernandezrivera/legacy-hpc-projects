Project Summary

The crises that Lebanon has been assailed by have deepened the vulnerabilities and poverty levels among the community members. The economic crisis has led to the loss of livelihoods for many households and the shrinkage of the purchasing power of families due to currency devaluation. The COVID19 pandemic has also led to the layoff of various employees from their jobs. This highly affects the Palestinian refugee populations who are already restricted to working in specific sectors. The decreased purchasing power affects the families’ ability to purchase hygiene materials and disinfectants which have highly increased in price. Additionally, the poor housing conditions, interrupted access to clean water, and poor waste management, intensify the burden of communicable and infectious diseases. Thus, Anera plans to respond through following a holistic approach which includes:
1- Supporting UNRWA with the provision of 25 chlorinators for communal water wells in targeted camps based on needs to ensure the access of around 40,000 community members to safe drinking water and lowering the risk waterborne diseases.
2- Distribution of hygiene kits including necessary hygiene, sanitation, and disinfection items to 15,000 community members.
3- Installation of solar panel systems at 2 water establishments in coordination with municipalities.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

In response to the exponential increase in the prices of hygiene and disinfecting items, Anera plans to support 15,000 households with the distribution of hygiene kits including necessary hygiene and disinfection items. Every hygiene kit will include an awareness leaflet on management of waterborne diseases. The families will be selected from different Palestinian camps based on set vulnerability criteria . A total of 25 youth will be provided with livelihood opportunities through the cash for work modality to support with the distribution of hygiene kits over a 120 days duration. The youth will be selected based on the following criteria: Age: Between 18 – 24 years of age● Employment status: Unemployed● Resident of camp where project will be implemented● Able to read and write2- Chlorination of communal water wells: Anera will support UNRWA with the distribution of 25 chlorinators, 119 PVC tanks, and distribution of sodium hypochlorite solution for the water wells. 3- Installation of Solar Panel Systems for Water wells: As the electricity crises is limiting the access of communities to water, Anera in coordination with municipalities and water establishments plans to install solar panel systems at 2 community water wells which will support the access of 100,000 beneficiaries to potable water.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

Anera will include protection-related elements in its response. The selected partners and the youth in the camps will receive training on detecting potential protection cases, and referring such cases for available services. Anera will prioritize PSEA and child protection as mandatory training themes for selected partners. Anera uses a Referral Information Management System (RIMS) for protection referrals and notifies inter-agency referral focal points immediately of any protection cases as a matter of priority. Anera has proven PSEA and child protection policies that are compliant with UNHCR and UNICEF requirements and will require that all partners in this program, including clinics, sign its Code of Conduct and adhere to anti-harassment policies.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

Anera ensures involving the community members from the planning phase to ensure that the provided hygiene kits are compatible with the population needs. Anera also assesses the needs per camp to provide support to the camps of high need. Anera also has effective monitoring to ensure the beneficiaries receive quality services. These include: Monitoring visits, satisfaction surveys, and water quality and access of community members monitoring. Anera also allows the community members to provide feedback through various channels including: Hotline, Social media platforms, and comment boxes.
