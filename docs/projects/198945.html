1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

This activity aims to improve the living standards of people affected by displacement in Cabo Delgado. Emergency Shelter support will be provided to newly displaced families and/or extremely vulnerable households living in host communities. This support will include an emergency shelter kit, tools and technical advice to facilitate access to covered living spaces that protect the safety, health and well-being of displacement-affected populations. Shelter improvements and repair assistance will be delivered to most vulnerable households to initiate a recovery process in the place of return, origin or displacement. RC will provide technical support for (i.e.) stronger roofing anchoring, orientation for better ventilation, and locks to improve safety and security. The implementation will preferably be delivered through cash/vouchers and whenever not possible through in-Kind. Household items (NFIs) are essential to protect the health and well-being of the targeted population and are fundamental contributors to the process of starting a longer-term recovery. Regarding the disaggregation by sex and gender, this is not pre-defined in our project but it is part of the selection criteria that are normally used in Mozambique to select the most vulnerable groups.Children head of household, women head of household, women lactating, etc should be prioritized.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The first key objective for the Shelter and NFI response will be to save and sustain lives through the provision of timely, targeted and appropriate shelter assistance and relief items. This will include the provision, distribution of core relief items and contribution for emergency shelter kits or materials complemented with technical trainings to displaced people in temporary and relocation sites. It will also include the rehabilitation, repair or upgrade of existing shelters that are below minimum standards in potential return locations such as, Mocimba da Praia and Muedumbe.In-kind assistance will be prioritized in the forms of family NFI packages during emergency responses and also for long-term displaced populations in need of support. Seasonal items (such as warm clothing and thermal blankets in winter, and summer clothing), will also be distributed to sustain lives and reduce the impact of exposure to extreme conditions and will be one of the main focuses of the NFI response.A combination of in-kind and cash or multipurpose voucher-based assistance will be promoted for people that have been assessed as socio-economically vulnerable through a close linkage with actors providing multi purpose cash to ensure that scarce resources are directed to those most in need.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Communication with communities will be done through focus group discussions (FGDs) and directly with NRC staff engagement with communities. Participants for FGDs and other assessments will include a broad range of representatives within the community, including marginalized groups with potentially higher protection risks and access. In the selection of FGD participants, NRC will identify and include female representatives with specific protection risk exposure in each context. FGDs will include the participation of diverse community representation with women, girls, boys, and men of different single-headedaded households, lactating and pregnant women, individuals with disabilities, people living with HIV and/or other health conditions, and other marginalized individuals or groups with higher levels of exposure or lower levels of access.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

NRC integrated protection-specific questions in each assessment and selection criteria to ensure prioritization of safety, and dignity, and to avoid doing harm throughout shelter activities. Programming was designed with specific attention to individuals with particular vulnerabilities who may have barriers to access. Adaptations will be assessed in an ongoing manner to identify any barriers to access or exposure to protection risks and program adjustments will be made. To ensure accountability to the affected people, activities will be monitored by project teams to proactively ensure meaningful access and through post-intervention monitoring to assess potential gaps or community feedback to inform implementation. The design of shelters and distributions of NFIs will be informed through community engagement to identify accessible locations and times for distributions that accommodate all individuals in a safe and dignified approach at the same time, CFM channels will be created for feedback and complaint mechanism.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

In the PwD, the selection of the most vulnerable affected people will prioritize people with disabilities of different ages and gender. Thru FgDs and assessments, NRC will identify and include the PwD in the decision-making and ensure their protection and inclusion. In addition of priority and preference during any kind of distributions, People with Special Needs PwD’s committees will be included or ad-hoc focus groups will be consulted for the revision of shelters design, adapting sectorial shelter designs to their specific needs. This may include, among others, shelter access adaptation, privacy screens, kitchen units dimensioning, wheel-chair drainage friendly design, lightening, ramps, handrails, etc... to be determined based on PwD’s community consultation. To reach out to a representative sample of PwD unable to attend consultation sessions in the community, NRC team will take care to include households visits at their dwellings during assessment, planning, implementation, and M&E activities.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Due to the nature of the proposed activities under this sub-sector, the localized environmental impact is relatively low. Some of the items (plastic sheets/tarpaulins and non-food items) will likely be procured in Cabo Delgado. As such, suppliers will be required to achieve minimum environmental standards for their products. Shelter materials such as bamboo and wood will be procured locally through suppliers in close coordination with local authorities, who have been interested and insisting on the use of sustainable and controlled sources, mitigating the environmental impact. Other mitigation measures will be been taken by local authorities and humanitarian partners, such as the limited/reduced number of families per site or the use of efficient cooking stoves, in order to reduce the environmental impact in the surroundings of each relocation site.
