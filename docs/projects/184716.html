Project Summary

ARD in the proposed area through the support of the health facility will ensure that the provision of health care services is delivered to the vulnerable community through the provision of equitable access to health care services. This project targets the most affected community hit by drought and aftermath of prolonged conflict, displacement, influx of IDPs as well as unstable climatic conditions ranging from floods as well as drought. There is limited access to health care services more so in the rural outskirts of the area this has been highlighted by the gaps in accessing maternal, new born and child health, mobile outreach and MHPSS services to the affected populace among them the IDPs, Host communities.
This project will therefore provide support through the allocation on full utilization of the mobile services, fixed facilities have been established to reach the most vulnerable households in the district. ARD will have 2 fixed facilities as well as 4 mobile services operating across the district.
The approach to this intervention will be through:
Provision of Emergency and Essential Health Care Services through PHC level/Mobile medical services to IDPs and hard-to-reach host communities, ARD will scale up the provision basic consultation services where a clinician will have to attend to 40 consultations, we will utilize the use of referral services for the complicated cases for comprehensive services.
ARD will as well provide the Emergency Reproductive Health Services for underserved and hard to reach areas; this will be in cooperated with the maternity services, both deliveries, antenatal care (ANC) and PNC, with family planning component. Complicated delivery services will be referred for CEmONC within the nearest referral hospital in Dollow. In this case in collaboration with the regional ministry of health we shall provide Immunization programs/VPD (BCG, penta, IPV and Measles) to women of child bearing age, Boys and girls under 5 years.
ARD within the district will establish the provision of GBV services, including clinical management of rape (CMR), Psychosocial Support Services (PSS), and community awareness. Referral services will be provided for the comprehensive services that our facility will not be able to handle. An Integration of Mental Health/Psycho-social Support (MHPSS) services, community engagement, and health education to community members to enhance identification and referral.
ARD will Scale-up early warning and response (EWAR) and case management of epidemic prone diseases through surveillance and data use. Weekly reporting shall be submitted to the MOH for swift response in cases of outbreaks. ARD will carry out the training of the health care service providers to getting adequate knowledge on EWAR.
We would like to note that ARD is working closely with partners to monitor and respond to the ongoing COVID 19 pandemic and will plan to modify and adapt programme activities according to the emerging situation if necessary. A Conflict-Sensitive Approach will be implemented throughout the project cycle. Resilience operations are assured through ARD’s risk reporting protocols and minimum operating security standards.
ARD will work in liaison with various stakeholders in the implementation of the project among them FMOH, WHO Somalia and the community. The project will further support local health facilities to increase utilization of the facility and ensure sustainability of the project. Furthermore, the project will work closely with other organization working in the area to complement each other’s services. People living with disabilities will be included in the project design, implementation and monitoring. ARD will ensure that people living with disabilities be highly considered in the project design and implementation.
ARD has previously implemented health and WASH programs in the targeted districts and are currently are present with health centres working in Garbaharey and El-work.

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

Gender based risks: ARD sets targets for female inclusion in all activities and project staff ensures these are met; communities including people living with disabilities are sensitized to this requirement prior to implementation.- Acceptance: ARD is already mitigating this risk by implementing programmes in the target area and having a good relationship with local authorities and communities. ARD also employs extensive community consultation and feedback mechanisms to mitigate this risk.- Insecurity: The established government structures responsible for security issues in the respective district is functioning. In case of deteriorating the security of the target location (in the worst scenario).ARD has a longstanding presence in the target locations where it implemented different projects. During its presence, ARD established direct relations with the community leaders/elders and local authorities to promote humanitarian principles, gain access, enhance program awareness, monitoring on issues including complaints/feedback about operation within the framework of the new complaint/feedback system. ARD also maintains proper balancing between different clans/sub-clans, including service provision, contracting for services or goods or in staff recruitment. These measures reinforce local acceptance and protection of the staff by the communities, local authorities and other interest groups.- Conflict: ARD will identify the risk of conflict in the project area and immediately avert this risk. ARD will closely engage the local leaders, government officials and the community to sort out any possible threat. At all times, the Do no harm principle will applied to prevent harm to the staff, clients and community.
