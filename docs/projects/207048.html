1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Fundação Ariel Glaser contra o SIDA Pediátrico (F. Ariel) has worked closely with MoH and CDC/PEPFAR to strengthen the national health system and to roll out national strategies to advance HIV epidemic control since 2011 providing a comprehensive package of interventions, including Gender Base Violence care, HIV and TB prevention, care and treatment, health systems strengthening, community involvement, monitoring and evaluation, and quality improvement in Cabo Delgado. The CDC-funded Victoria project and the Karibu project support comprehensive HIV, TB services, MCH and GBV services in 34 Health facilities in CD and in the surrounding communities through Mobile Brigades and community outreach approaches. F. Ariel is proposing to continue in FY24 the activities of the Victoria and Karibu project, continuing focusing on the prevention of GBV and improving care to post-GBV victims, aiming to build on the integrated effort to strengthen the conventional health system complemented by an equally robust community sub-system through a wide range of health facility- and community-based integrated activities that include technical assistance, health system strengthening.F. Ariel will strengthen the continuum of response between GBV prevention and clinical post-violence response services and integrate age-appropriate GBV case identification, first-line support, and clinical and non-clinical GBV care into HIV services and literacy interventions in the HF supported by the Victoria and Karibu project and their surrounding communities.In the past year approximately 3200 gender-based victims were identified and received post-GBV care at the supported Health facilities. All population in the districts covered by the projects will benefit from the planned interventions, according to their respective relevance: special target groups will be children, adolescents and young women that are those more vulnerable to GBV; however, men and the population in general will be covered by community and literacy interventions.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

F. Ariel will continue to support the implementation of the GBV program at 34 supported HF in CD in 11 districts (Pemba City, Chiure, Ancuabe, Balama, Montepuez, Namuno, Mueda, Metuge, Mecufi, Nangade and Mueda) including IDP camps and resettlement area through the following activities: 1) Implementation of GBV routine screening at key Health Facility entry points (MCH, SAAJ, HTC and C&T sectors), including first line LIVES support and referral of GBV victims to trained staff for post-violence care; 2) on job training, of health providers, community providers including MM, Adolescent and Youth Mentor, case managers and other actors of the multi-sectorial mechanism in GBV and first LIVE support; 3) on-the-job training of health providers (60) on GBV package and Intimate Partner Violence (IPV) screening; 4) provide in-service training on post-GBV care at all entry points, including friendly care for KP victims of GBV; 5) provide technical assistance to implement the mental health care package for victims of GBV and provider self-care (vicarious trauma); 6) implement GBV prevention activities, focusing on girls aged 10-14 years and adolescents during Mobile Brigades visits in the community; 7) conduct on-the-job training on a monthly basis, using TeleSaude platform; 8) implement the package of quality of care assessment (e-IMD) to assess internal and external GBV program performance at 11 health facilities in CD (Metoro, Balama, Ocua, HD Chiúre, H.R.Montepuez, H.R.Mueda, Ed.Mondlane, Natite, HPP and Namuno) and expand to 4 new HFs in Metuge and Chiúre; 9) train of 60 staff on the GBV QA MOH tool; 10) Implement the provider gender transformative standards package through provincial/district replicas; 11) Strengthen SISMA and GBV data collection, analysis and M&A system at HF level through regular triangulation of collected and reported data; 12) conduct annual DQA sessions; 13) conduct monthly virtual sessions to discuss data and program progress including provincial and central level stakeholders; 14) implement the ‘emergency GBV package’ to address the specific challenges of IDPs; 15) integrate the dissemination of the updated National health sector GBV response plan into all GBV trainings activities; 16) assist the SPS/DPS in organizing and leading quarterly meetings of the provincial GBV TWG; 17) collaborate with the OVC partners in both provinces to integrate GBV prevention into their activities and strengthen referral to HF for post-violence care of victims identified in the community; 18) complete the mapping of post-GBV services in all districts; 19) implement the post-GBV caregiver mentoring package through supervision; 20) implement communication and literacy activities to sensitize communities on GBV prevention and notification; 21) Implement the new GBV M&E tools and ensure that data are included in SIS-MA by training NED officers and ensure the training of GBV providers (60) in the use of PEP registration tools; 22) Print M&E tools, job aids, GBV SOP, IEC material; 23) conduct semiannual joint supervision with DPS/MoH; 24) organize quarterly discussions within the Community health committees on GBV cases notification and referral to the HF; 26) support activities of the celebration of the 16 days of activism in coordination with MGCAS; 27) continue to co-lead the Provincial GBV cluster and coordinate the interventions of stakeholder in CD with UNFPA.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

All the interventions and activities that will be implemented in the Victoria project within the GBV programmatic area, will follow MOH approved guidelines and will use MOH approved tools to identify people at risk of GBV as well as to provide care to pos-GBV victims.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

All the interventions and activities that will be implemented in the Victoria project within the GBV programmatic area, will follow MOH approved guidelines and will use MOH approved tools to identify people at risk of GBV as well as to provide care to pos-GBV victims.Through community interventions and literacy activities targeting GBV topics we will encourage participation and empowerment of women and young girls

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The project will implement activities that will benefit people with disabilities and will encourage their inclusion in the literacy program and in the pos-GBV care at all level of interventions (community and Health Facilities).
