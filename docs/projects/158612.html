Project Summary

"Overall Objective
To contribute towards improved health status and access to potable water to the selected communities and schools of Matobo District.
Specific Objectives
• To improve access to potable water for the El Nino affected selected communities and schools in wards 7,8,14 & 17 in Matobo district by April 2020.
• To reduce open defecation in selected communities through increasing the sanitation facilities in selected schools and communities of 7,8,14 & 17
• To increase positive health behavior adoption through the establishment of community and school health clubs as vehicles of improving health status of communities and schools in the project area.
