Project Summary

Project summary
In the context of the current multi-faceted emergencies (increased risks of ﬂooding, protracted conﬂict, droughts, Covid 19, Cholera/AWD Outbreaks) and the projected phenomena expected in Somalia, HDCpropose this emergency WASH project to target IDPs and most vulnerable host communities, from Gedo ( Doolow, Buurdhuubo, Celwaq Baardhere and Hard to reach population and IDPs in Baardhere, Doolow, Ceelwaq and Buurdhubo of Garbaharey districts of Gedo region with aim of saving lives and reducing morbidities associated with Water and Sanitation. HDC used to work with IDPs and people with disabilities and understands that internal displacements are the key driver of humanitarian needs in Gedo region Many IDPs and Poor vulnerable Host communities are currently residing in Urban and pre-urban informal crowded areas with insuﬃcient access to water and Sanitation. According to JMMCA, 1.1.6million IDPs are in need of adequate WASH services in 2021. JMMCA also report that, 53% of the IDPs face challenges in terms of access to WASH services in Somalia
This proposal aims at addressing the needs of people affected by crisis, COVID-19, conflict and droughts in four district of Gedo includes (Buurdhuubo under Garbaharey district Ceelwaq, Damasa, Doolow IDP’s and Baardhere Districts of the Gedo region, in the Jubaland state of Somalia through improving access to Water, hygiene and snatiation services with targeted 65000 vulnerable persons including Hard to reach Populations. The needs for this intervention has been identified in through HDC presence similar intervention in Health and nutrition project location with community action plans developed by Disaster Risk Reduction and RRT in the target locations through a recently conducted WASH/health need assessments HDC.
Our appeal activities fall in line with the WASH cluster identified objectives in the WASH cluster 2021 Primary Response Plan. Under Cluster Objective 1, 2 and 3 on delivering life-saving WASH assistance to reduce acute needs among most vulnerable settlements and communities (including men, women, boys and girls with respecting disabled people) in minimizing shocks and stabilizing.
For Cluster Objective 3 HDC proposes the construction of 200 safe and accessible gender segregated latrines in the four districts . This will be in line with maintaining safety as well as equity and accessibility of the vulnerable groups that include women, girls, boys, the elderly as well as people with disabilities when accessing WASH services.
Under Cluster Objective 2 that informs of the continued restoration of access to safe and equitable basic WASH services in target location that include non-IDP men, women, boys and girls in a dignified manner, HDC proposes three key intervention objectives that include one, the rehabilitation of 6 water catchments , 3 Badhere and District 3 in Elwak districts . The water catchment will be 14000m3 and will be rehabilitated through CFW using Marke based programing –MPP. 2, is the rehabilitation of 6 existing shallow in Ceelwaq districts The third interventions includes the rehabilitation of the two water intake in Baardhere town. 3, Chlorination of water. The final key intervention found under Cluster 2 is the establishment as well as the training of 6 water committees to ensure the proper maintenance as well as the sustainability of water services in Ceelwaq and Baardhere.

Project Activities

Activity 1. Construction of 200 safe and accessible gender-segregated latrines in the four districts to reach 6000( 2500 women, men, 1500men, Girls 1040, 960 boys): This will be in line with maintaining safety as well as equity and accessibility of the vulnerable groups that include women, girls, boys, the elderly as well as people with disabilities when accessing WASH services. Activity 2. ) Rehabilitation and / extension of water infrastructures to provide reliable and sustained access to safe water-based on identiﬁed strategic water points. Rehabilitation of 4 water catchments, 2 Baardhere, and District 1 in Elwak districts 1 in Burdhubo (The water catchment will be 12000m3 and will be rehabilitated through CFW using Market-based programming –MPP. rehabilitation of the two water intake in Baardhere and Buurdhuubo town. Activity 4. Chlorination of water. Community water point chlorination and that has a required level of FRC (0.2-0.5mg/l)as per the WHO standard reaching 4400 people including. Activity 5) Establishment of Gender balanced water management committees for the targeted water points to ensure continuity of services, appropriate operation, and maintenance as well as strengthening women participation in the WASH project. Activity. 6 HDC will also conduct community mobilization on environmental sanitation and provide environmental Sanitation tools to support active sanitation groups in the camps and target villages; HDC will provide environmental solid waste management services targeting 6000 people (2500 women, men, 1500men, Girls 1040, 960 boys) living Buurdhuubo, Ceelwaq, Baardhere, and Doolow districts. The cash for work (CfW) approach will used to undertake this activity. In consultation with communities, 80 vulnerable women/mothers will be selected targeted settlements.Activity 8. HDC also proposes hygiene promotion campaigns including outreach levels. The hygiene awareness-raising campaigns target locations Garbaharey-Buurdhuubo, Ceelwaq, and Baardhere and Doolow through 60 community-based hygiene promotion volunteers who will be trained on hygiene promotion topics (60% of those volunteers being women). The volunteer-based hygiene promotion activities will be regularly supervised and supported by dedicated hygiene promoters to ensure that project reaches 11000 people (men 3320 Women 5480 Boys 1120 Girls 1080)

Mitigation Measures

HDC will provide refresher training to all its staff on PSEA involved in project implementation on preventive measures against sexual exploitation and abuse/ protection mainstreaming and ensure they sign the code of conduct on physical sexual exploitation and abuse as a commitment to respect and foster humanitarian standards and the rights of beneficiaries, including the confidentiality of patients. HDC will ensure the selection/targeting criteria is shared with local communities/IDP management committees while staffs will ensure that the selection criteria’s adhered al time. The committees must be selected based on AGD criteria to ensure all affected groups are involved in the interventionHDC will involve all the stakeholders and target beneﬁciaries at all stages of the project so that there are sense of ownership and inclusivity right from the beginning. HDC will involve local leaders, women, men, youth and community elders during beneﬁciary identiﬁcation, selection and registration. This will allow the community to do vetting and identify the most needy community members. It will also reduce risk external interference as well as exclusion of marginalized community members. Community will shared with complains and grievance feedback mechanism as well as opportunity to engage the organization to identify most suitable and friendly way of raising their complains
