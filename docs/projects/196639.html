Project Summary

This project will greatly contribute to supporting the self-reliance of affected households by building productive assets and restoring or creating income-generating opportunities to save and sustain lives
This project aims to support the olive sector and vulnerable olive farmers in Idlib and Afrin districts to support workers' group resilience through the provision of agricultural services and inputs in addition to the cash for work related to income generation activities through supporting farmers and households.
And the provision of agricultural inputs for 200 women who head a family with the aim of revitalizing summer crops, which provides a source of income for the targeted families by distributing restricted vouchers to purchase seeds, agricultural medicines, and fertilizers that cover 500 square meters of summer home crops, each voucher is equivalent to 350USD
The project’s interventions will target IDPs and host residents regardless of their ethnic, cultural, or social background, focusing on female-headed households by increasing their knowledge on how to bene t and use the olive chain value through food production and marketing awareness sessions provided. Moreover, the elderly and persons with special needs to be targeted through the project activities with appropriate modalities Sadad well-trained and experienced technical team will focus on improving food security and supporting livelihood by providing
Support for best agriculture services to increase the productivity of 1000 hectares of olive trees (2000 HHs, 10% of them females headed by support 5 dunums per household) through providing the following:
• Autumn and spring land plowing;
• Harvest service;
• Two rounds of leaf-nutrition high K and high N;
• Pest controls ( olive moth, stem digger, and peacock eye disease);
• Technical pruning service;
• Providing the targeted 2000 olive farmers' HHs with olive-oil extraction vouchers per household (each voucher will be used to extract olive oil from 1000 kg of olives)
• Conducting the olive oil extraction process in the selected and contracted mills;
• Conducting awareness-raising seminars for 700 farmers on integrated olive-cultivation management, pest control, olive trees' services calendar, and avoiding negative methods related to olive cultivation, food production, and marketing of products for local usage. The project targets 2535 workers in the CFW system to support 50 thousand olive trees by providing support for 1000 A farmer who has olive trees as follows:
2690 CFW worker will provide the following services:
- 1875 workers will work in the olive harvest for 1,000 hectares, at a rate of 22 working days for each worker
- 450 workers will work in olive pruning, at a rate of 36 working days for each worker
- 365 workers will work in combating olive diseases at a rate of 30 working days for each worker
Equipping a sewage treatment plant in the Jandires district, and pumping the treated water into irrigation channels to use it in watering crops This provides 560 cubic meters per day ( 16 hours) within the irrigation canals It is produced by the treatment plant and mitigates the effects of environmental pollution affecting the Afrin River and irrigation canals
The daily treated quantity is enough to irrigate 1120 dunums
Anaerobic fermentation is used in the sewage treatment plant, which does not require operational costs, as it depends on the water flow. Additionally, there is no need for parts maintenance, but two workers occupied by the local council can perform cleaning and removal tasks.

3 - Needs

The agriculture sector, which was once a cornerstone of the Syrian economy, has also been severely impacted. High cost and limited availability of agriculture inputs, degradation of livelihoods and income-earning opportunities, devastating wildfires, and other climate-induced shocks such as erratic rainfall, have all contributed to the worsening situation in Syria. According to the needs assessment analysis conducted by Sadad shows • There is a high cost of agriculture services • The high cost of fuel for supplementary irrigation, led to the olive farmers depending on only rain to water the trees as a negative adaptation; • The spread of pests and the high costs of the control process • Marketing difficulties and limited access to local markets. • The high costs of daily workers led to low farmer incomes and high service costs. Also, the needs assessment shows that 90% of residents of the surveyed areas depended on olive tree production or the service provided to it as a source of income, especially in light of the Covid-19 epidemic and the devaluation of the Turkish lira. Sadad makes sure to target the most vulnerable groups and high Districts (Afrin and Idleb)In addition to the pollution of the Afrin River with sewage and the decrease in the level of rain, all of this led to a decrease in the flow of water in the irrigation channels.,

8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Sadad has a Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse PSEA policy that usually is attached to each employee’s job contract clarifying all the related procedures. Sadad is an active member of the PSEA Network and will collaborate with the PSEA network to support quality implementation in the project. Risk: Risk of exploitation or abuse by staff members/service providers. Mitigation: Sadad ensures to have in a place an accessible mechanism for reporting sexual exploitation and abuse in line with Sadad safeguarding policy. Communicate regularly and clearly to the community about what services are available and who services are for. Provide regular training to staff and service providers for code of conduct and PSEA. Risk: Lack of understanding of employees about PSEA issues. Sadad understands that, in order for the PSEA/Child Safeguarding policy to be well understood and effectively implemented, it is essential that there are high levels of awareness regarding the policy and that staff and others are clear, con dent and competent in putting the policy into practice. Disseminate and promote the policy widely. Copies of the policy will be made available and accessible to all stakeholders, both in hard copy and electronically in order to ensure that the policy commitments, principles, and practices are publicized and that everyone is clear about Sadad's position in relation to PSEA and safeguarding children.
