Project Summary

This project seek to increase access for displaced-affected populations living in the Far North areas bordering Nigeria to shelter; WASH; livelihoods and food opportunities; and legal identity (access to civil documentation) and Housing, Land, and Property (HLP) rights. This project will target IDPs, returnees and vulnerable individuals among host populations. Beneficiary selection will take into account gender-specific needs, with the aim of identifying at least 55% female beneficiaries at the identification stage. This is an integrated project and NRC will target the same vulnerable communities in the aforementioned locations across the different sectors.

Needs

NFI: The subdivisions of Fotokol (Logone et Chari), Kolofata (Mayo Sava), Mokolo (Mayo Tsanaga) are the areas where NFI needs are huge. Some severely affected villages in these areas have not yet benefited from NFI assistance. Shelter: Houses in many villages, especially those along the Cameroonian/Nigerian border areas have been destroyed and belongings have been confiscated. WASH: The functional water points are under pressure and the waiting time is longer. In the rainy season, affected populations fetch water from untreated sources (rivers), exposing them to Cholera especially in the villages bordering Nigeria where annual Cholera outbreaks are endemic. Livelihood and Food Security: Having lost or abandoned their crops and livestock during displacement, households are unable to feed their families and meet their basic needs. 37.4% of the total population in the Far North is considered below the acceptable food consumption score and 33.6% is currently food insecure (CARI approach).Information, Counselling and Legal Assistance (ICLA): In the region, issues related to accessing civil documentation, and housing, land and property (HLP) are limiting access to basic rights and services; increasing the risks of statelessness; limiting access to education and livelihoods; and creating social cohesion concerns.
