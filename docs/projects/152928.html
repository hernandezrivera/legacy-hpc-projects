Project Summary

The project aims to support most vulnerable populations comprised of men, women, girls and boys (7,000 households) in Jonglei and Central Equatoria States. These will be supported through provision of agricultural livelihood kits which will be pre-positioned through the core pipeline - crop, vegetables and tools and fishing kits for both main season and dry season response for vegetable and fishing kits. Stop Poverty Communal Initiative (SPOCI) will as well carry out livestock treatment, vaccinations, trainings and capacity building including equipping of Community Animal Health Workers.
SPOCI aims to respond and by establishing a nexus between Humanitarian response and Resilience programming and responf to the FSL Cluster Objective 2:food production for the most vulnerable households: Increase vulnerable household food production to protect the erosion of livelihoods through agricultural seeds and fishing inputs; with activities tailored to deliver an integrated WASH, Nutrition, Health, Protection package in liaison and coordination with other agencies on ground .
The project will target 7000 HHs - 15000 girls, 15000 boys, 6000 men and 6000 women drawn from recently arrived internally displaced persons, host community members, returnees among others. These will include elderly persons, pregnant and lactating women, people with disabilities drawn from the most vulnerable households. These different categories will be consulted and involved in implementation of the project including monitoring and evaluation; while taking into consideration Accountability of Affected Populations, Gender Equity, Protection concerns including GBV and sound environmentally friendly approaches.

Needs

The latest IPC September 2018 projections indicated an estimated 5.2 million people in the country will be facing unprecedented levels of food insecurity and malnutrition in the first quarter 2019. Recent SMART surveys conducted in May in Jonglei indicate 20.7 per cent GAM rates, way above the WHO emergency threshold of 15 per cent. Recent and renewed fighting in Central Equatoria, compounded by ambushes and attacks on humanitarian convoys continue to impede access to beneficiaries with aid. Cattle related fighting incidents in Pibor, Lainya and Kajokeji. These have subsequent led to multiple displacements in nearby towns of Yei, Kajokeji, others scattered in remote villages and make shift settlements and in the bush along the border with Uganda. An estimated 37,510 people live near these settlements including as refugees in Uganda. In In Jonglei, inter-communal violence has been rampant with recent deadly attacks being witnessed in Pibor and Laukangole (South Sudan Humanitarian Bulletins, 2018). These vulnerable populations cited insecurity as a primary concern, with persistent disruptions in livelihood activities consequently leading to unprecedented levels of food insecurity. This is projected to deteriorate further with a projected early onset of the lean season in 2019 attributed to protracted conflict, poor access to inputs, low productivity, pests such Fall Army Worm invasion, livestock diseases, raiding and subsequent high mortalities, reduced cross border trade and subsequent high malnutrition rates(FEWSNET Food Security Outlook, April 2018). This has had significant impacts more so on children, who are at risk of malnutrition; and under 5 years; whose accessibility to affected populations has been constrained by recent cyclical waves of insecurity and poor infrastructure conditions in Pibor County (July 2018 Boma State Inter agencies Rapid Assessment; IPC September, 2018). Most vulnerable and displaced populations needs are further worsened by protection concerns including gender based violence and sexual abuse, predominantly among girls and women. Rampant child and women abduction practices by the Boma state communities and endemic age set fighting further, with recent fighting among Lango and Kurenen age groups in Pibor (Protection Trends 2018). Emergency food & livelihood support: fishing kits/ rapid response kits (fishing kits/ cow pea & three variety of fast maturing vegetable seed)3. Reducing pest and crop disease e.g. FAW surveillance, detection, prevention & eradication4. Livestock vaccination of 30,000 heads in Central Equatoria and Jonglei 5.
