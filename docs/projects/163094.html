Project Summary

Eight months after Cyclone Idai, the humanitarian crisis is still unresolved, and it has been exacerbated due to the economic context and the limited resources to cope with the situation. IOM has recognized the necessity to continue the humanitarian response.
The proposed response will include a component of Camp Coordination and Camp Management (CCCM) activities to continue managing the three IDPs camps in a proper and dignify manner as a commitment to Accountability to Affected Persons (AAP). IOM, as cluster coordinator, will lead the implementation of a standardized approach to enhance accountability, will upgrade the living conditions in the camps, and work in close coordination with the Government to ensure and appropriate development of an exit strategy and a relocation plan.
