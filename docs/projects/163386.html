Project Summary

The above mentioned project has as two specific objectives:
Specific Objective 1: To reinforce the capacities of local actors to improve WASH services and resilience to affected populations;
Specific Objective 2: To reduce the risk of poor hygiene-related morbidity and mortality of affected populations in the current crises affecting the NWR and SWR of Cameroon. Specifically, the project targets vulnerable persons within the Meme and Fako Divisions of SWR. Activities, Outputs and Outcomes will be centered around each specific objective.
Activities for Specific Objective 1:
Training of 28 volunteers and some community members to help facilitate, train and supervise the reinforcement of capacities of local about 1050 community members especially children, girls and women, through workshop training sessions.
Activities for Specific Objective 2: About 420 vulnerable children will be identified and given WASH kits after participating in the WASH training session.
About 630 girls and women identified, will be given Hygiene Kits after going through WASH training and Health Screening Tool by Empowerment Corner Cameroon (ECOCAM) staff.
Outputs for Specific Objective 1:
Output 1: 28 volunteers and community-based facilitators are trained to train and facilitate training on WASH practices.
Output 2: 28 volunteers have been trained in Cholera prevention to be able to transmit this knowledge to their vulnerable communities.
Output 3: About 1050 children, girls and women have been trained to develop WASH knowledge, attitudes and skills to protect themselves within their communities against emergency related issues.
Output 4: About 1050 community members have been trained to develop knowledge and skills, and are able to adopt attitudes that will help prevent the occurrence of cholera in their communities.
Output 5: About 630 vulnerable women and girls have been given practical knowledge in safe hygiene and sanitation practices.
Output for Specific Objective 2: About 420 malnourished children will receive WASH Kits and
About 630 women and girls hygiene kits.
The Outcomes for Specific Objective 1:
Outcome 1: 28 volunteers and community-based facilitators who got trained can now train and facilitate training on WASH practices.
Outcome 2: 28 volunteers trained in Cholera prevention are able to transmit this knowledge to their vulnerable communities.
Outcome 3: About 1050 children, girls and women trained have developed WASH knowledge, attitudes and skills to protect themselves within their communities against emergency related issues.
Outcome 4: About 1050 community members who have received training, have developed knowledge and skills, and are able to adopt attitudes that will help prevent the occurrence of cholera in their communities.
Outcome 5: About 630 vulnerable women and girls that have been given, get practical knowledge in safe hygiene and sanitation practices.
Outcome for Specific Objective 2: About 420 malnourished children received WASH Kits and
About 630 women and girls received hygiene kits.

Besoins / Needs

The humanitarian crisis in Cameroon has expanded from four regions in 2017 to eight in 2019 (OCHA, 2019); and the situation of conflict-affected children in the South-West, North-West and Far North regions has worsened. Villages, schools and health facilities are under attack. In the South-West/North-West, 9 in 10 children have been out of school for three years (UNICEF, Education Sector Report 2019). Cameroon Ministry of Health in its 'Cholera Report No. 49', September 2019 declared that between 23 and 65 per cent of hospitals are not functioning in these regions, and in some areas, only 6 per cent of women are giving birth in health centers. Insecurity has disrupted vaccination activities and primary health care services, placing children at higher risk. Humanitarian access is limited by parties to the conflict, the remoteness of populations in hiding in rural locations and frequent lock-downs across towns and cities by non-state armed groups. Internally displaced people are facing high poverty levels with few prospects for short-term return. Rising insecurity threatens communities, children and humanitarian workers. The needs of internally displaced persons are overburdening health services, schools and water systems in host communities. Projections for voluntary return are low and demographic pressures are jeopardizing social cohesion. . ECOCAM intends to Strengthen WASH initiatives in vulnerable communities in Meme and Fako Divisions of the SWR through capacity building and promotion of WASH activities in the vulnerable communities of the South West Region, specifically in villages and communities within Meme and Fako divisions. Following series of trainings received under the various clusters and having had clear understanding of the needs of the affected communities, ECOCAM presents this proposal to assist in the realization of various humanitarian initiatives in these particular areas.
