Project Summary

Kassala State is located in the northeast part of Sudan bordering Eritrea& Ethiopia countries, and adjacent to Ethiopian borders. The state is divided into 11 localities. Kassala hosts considerable amounts of refugees and IDPs, Both influxes have put additional pressure on the fragile resources, particularly on agriculture and pastoral land. Kassala state economy is built on agriculture and agro-pastoralist activities, which believed to constitute around 70% of employment and source of income. The state enjoys huge animal resources estimated to exceed 3.5 million heads, including camels, cows, goats and sheep. Both agriculture and animal resources are poorly contributing to improving economy due to major factors related to poor production, lack of appropriate conducive policies including removal of subsidies following the implementation of liberalized economic policies and effects of conflict and drought. The gender roles and relations were highly distorted by the effects of local culture, values and inherited traditions works together to widen gender gaps, reduce women access to resources and participation in public and political domains.
This project is proposed to be implemented in two localities (Reifi Kassala & Reifi Telkok). Hosted about 655177 people). In Sudan, an estimated 9.8 million people face crisis or worse levels of food
insecurity (IPC Phase 3 and above) and need urgent action to save lives. This represents a 38 percent increase from 7.1M compared to the same period last year (IPC analysis 2020). Around 2.6 million of the 9.8 million food-insecure people are facing emergency levels of acute food insecurity (IPC Phase 4) and around 7 million people are facing crisis levels of acute food insecurity (IPC Phase 3). An additional 17 million people are estimated to be under stress (IPC Phase 2), and any additional shocks like floods, inflation, and poverty can push them to severe levels of hunger.
This situation has created a disproportionate effect on the lives and livelihoods of the host communities in general. In the two targeted localities according to the IPC analysis, there are 65518 person on phase 4, 98277 on phase 3 and the average (phase 3 and above) are 163,794 who facing food insecurity as crises level.
The targeted beneficiaries will be supported to improve their FSL status, 3600 HH (women &men) will receive agricultural support for 2000 men, livestock support for 300 individuals beside provision of CAHWs training and Kits for 100 individuals. Vocational training with suitable startup capital will be delivered for 1200 women on food processing to improve the nutrition status of their families, Soap making and tailoring training. For the general population, community-based awareness sessions on COVID19 will be delivered twice a month for 4 months, awareness sessions on nutrition and personal hygiene for 10 months will be conducted. For all project beneficiaries, 10 VSLA Associations will be established, 5 per each locality, in addition to entrepreneurship training for all women who have vocational training.
The targeting mechanism to select the project beneficiaries to target the most vulnerable population, who are living in a real fragile situation. As preparation phase, a participatory rapid assessment will be done to select and list the real target, representatives from communities and relevant stack holders and local authorities in the selected localities.
The project will impact positively on the beneficiaries living status and livelihood condition by increasing the self resilience through enhanced livelihood opportunities for the most vulnerable people including social cohesion support among the 2 localities in Kassala State.
Specific objective:
Improve the self-employment of and entrepreneurship skills of host community members through access to applicable vocational, technical, and skills training.
The duration of this project is 12 months.
