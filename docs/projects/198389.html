1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project, to be implemented in the three provinces of Cabo Delgado, Niassa, and Nampula aims to provide to the conflict affected population with life-saving and life-sustaining Sexual and Reproductive health services, including newborn health services. The project interventions will be implemented through Government and CSOs guided by the Minimum Initial Service Package (MISP) for Reproductive Health principles. Through this project, UNFPA will enhance the availability and accessibility of life- saving SRH care to conflict affected populations in affected areas through the implementation of the MISP for SRH which includes: Clinical Management of Rape, Basic and Comprehensive emergency obstetric and newborn care (EmONC), HIV prevention and STIs prevention and syndromic treatment, family planning and other SRH interventions such as safe abortion care and antenatal care. The implementation of the MISP for SRH aims at: reducing the health, physical and emotional consequences associated related to GBV and limited access to SRH/GBV services such as exposure to sexually transmitted diseases including HIV, unwanted pregnancies, trauma; maternal and neonatal mortality and morbidity. The project will target displaced and vulnerable women and girls of selected districts of Cabo Delgado, Niassa and Nampula provinces.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

This project aims to provide life-saving Sexual, Reproductive, Maternal and Newborn Health services to displaced and vulnerable women and girls in Cabo Delgado, Niassa and Nampula provinces with the following activities: a) Equip Health Facilities providing SRH services with essential reproductive health medical supplies and equipment, namely Inter-Agency Reproductive Health kits, truck-mounted mobile clinics and other necessary equipment; b) Support deployment of Maternal and Newborn (MNH) nurses in remote areas of the three provinces; c) Conduct integrated mobile health brigades to IDP and host community areas; d) Strengthen referral system to ensure timely referral of complication cases in the remote areas of the three provinces; e)Conduct information sessions on SRH to increase awareness and access to SRH information and services; f)Capacity building of service providers on the MISP and other important SRH topics.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The conflict against the actions perpetrated by Non-State Armed Groups (NSAGs) in Cabo Delgado Province, now in its 5th year, puts women and girls in a continues risk of human rights violations, including forced recruitment, kidnapping, and conflict-related sexual violence.The continued insecurity and the recent attacks in areas that until recently were deemed to be safe resulted in additional constraints for the response. As displaced, returnees and host communities continue to experience severe needs in all sectors, the situation in some of the inaccessible areas in the north of the province is of highest concern. Recent protection analysis focused on Cabo Delgado Province, although the conflict has already resulted in the arrival of displaced families in the neighboring provinces of Niassa and Nampula. By working with provincial authorities we aim in reducing the impact of the conflict on women and girls.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

Throughout the implementation of this project, UNFPA will ensure that beneficiaries are consulted through regular assessment and informative discussion discussions to ensure that their concerns, needs and suggestions are taken into consideration. Regular consultations will be held at the Women and Girls Safe Spaces, health facilities and through the mobile health units to facilitate access among the most marginalized.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The project will take into consideration the needs of people with special needs by:a)collecting disaggregated data by age, sex special condition and specific location at the IDPs camps;b)involving peer groups of people with disability in activity to reach the targets because the peer group usually know the locations and people with disability will feel more comfortable to talk with their peers;c)it will reach people with disability through mobile brigade, home visits, community outreach activities for people with difficulties to come to the health facilities;d)modify and to adapt the IEC materials and information session’s methodology and to reach the target based on the type of disability (voice messages for people with visual impairment, using sign language, etc).

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project will take into consideration Environmental Protection and Permitting Program (EP3) as unsafe health-care settings contribute to a significant proportion of some diseases. It is well-established that humanitarian context has high risk associated with health-care facilities, with an average proportion of health-care associated infections close to 10%. Sharps waste, although produced in small quantities, is highly infectious. Needles and if poorly managed, they expose health-care workers, waste handlers and the community to infections. Therefore, all identified environmental risks will be mitigated by remediation measures, cost effective, locally and innovative solutions.
