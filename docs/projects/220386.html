Project Summary

1. Procure, distribute and undertake control and vaccination activities to combat opportunistic infections, parasites, diseases, and health stress induced impact. 2. Distribution of drought tolerant fodder crops to the most vulnerable households in the affected areas to promote livestock nutrition and safeguard deterioration of animal body conditions and eventually promote or sustain good health. 3. Procure and undertake livestock restocking programmes by distributing disease-free breeding stock to households that lost their livestock (i.e. poultry, goats, and pigs) under pass-on programmes to maintain or increase livestock numbers and eventually achieve food and income security.
