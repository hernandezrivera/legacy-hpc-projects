Project Summary

Critically, this project builds on significant knowledge of SOS Sahel Sudan on the ground and the trust with targeted communities that have established a strong foundation.
In the three targeted States (White Nile, North Kordofan and Kassala) at least water sources are in need for urgent rehabilitation because of its low performance or not functioning at all (Source of information: The government technical staff).
The percentage of people have access to safe water in the three states is low but still the situation in some parts of the states is worse where people depend completely on water trucking during the dry season. Water quality is generally a big concern as most people especially in rural areas prefer to use unsafe surface water due to shortage of safe water or high water fees in some areas. Limit of sustainable supplies of water quality materials is one of the constraints in this regards. NGOs stated that more than 80% of HH members consumed less than 15 liters of water per person per day (Oxfam needs assessment report 2018 in Kassala). but still sphere standard considered consumption of one person per day is 20 liters.
One of the main challenges currently affecting people in the three states (North Kordofan, Kassala and White Nile) including IDPs, Host and Nomads is inequitable access to adequate humanitarian aid especially WASH and livelihood services due to the general economic situation in Sudan and COVID19 epidemics and flood which lead to a limited allocated humanitarian services. Through this proposal SOS will work to reduce the vulnerability of the target communities and improve their live conditions by the provision of water, sanitation and hygiene promotion (WASH), distribution of and improve FSL activities through provision of agricultural inputs.
The project main objective is to increase household access to adequate humanitarian aid such as WASH and livelihood services to contribute in improvement of income and FSL for poor and marginalized women, men and children in the three localities targeting 7500 families.
The overall purpose is to address community FSL and WASH needs in Shiekan, Guli/Kosti and Telkok localities of North Kordofan, White Nile and Kasala respectively through provision of water, sanitation and hygiene promotion (WASH), distribution of NFIs and emergency food security package to the target communities. Approximately 45,000 beneficiaries will be reached by this project in term of ReifiTelkok: 14,550, Sheikan: 21,150 and Guli/Kosti: 8,550. . The main objective will be achieved through three outcomes by December 2021:
1. Improved agricultural production for smallholder farmers.
2. Improved economic empowerment for targeted households.
3. Improved access to safe drinking water and improved sanitation practices in targeted locations

The implementation of the project will be done by SOS in collaboration with the government technical body like (WES MoA and MoH at the state and locality levels) and involvement of the target community will be the key issue to ensure the sustainability and smooth implementation of the activities In delivering promoting health related activities to the community in our target localities, SOS will commit to the national and international specifications and standards (Sphere, Sudanese standards, WHO standards etc) to provide outstanding WASH and FSL services.

1 - Needs

In the three target localities There is a general limitation and shortage of basic infrastructure and public services, which is related to the distribution of resources in the country and the historic marginalization of rural areas. Mostly traditional farming methods are used and as a result of increased pressure on land, soils become depleted, affecting crop yields. Availability of water is a major challenge for families and school children in the targeted project locations. Though, rain fall over the last two seasons are relatively good, the crop yield was poor due to limit of technology and knowledge and skills transfer, the number of people who are food insecure is significant. Economic issues such as fuel shortages have resulted in substantial increases in the price of goods. In November 2020, prices of sorghum, millet and wheat grain were at near record levels in most monitored markets and nearly double their previous year values. There are continued constraints on the availability of and accessibility to agricultural inputs such as machinery, seeds and fertilizer as a result of the high and increasing inflation. The targeted localities are expected to remain in IPC (Integrated phase classification) Phase 3 (Crisis) from May until September 2021, the peak of the lean season. According to FAO-WFP regional report on “Early Warning Analysis of Acute food security Hotspots 2020) about 9.6 Million population will be acute food insecure in 17 state in Sudan, Likewise FAO Led join assessment “ Food impact rapid assessment 2020) showed food insecure were 527968 person in rainfall while in irrigated sector the numbers were 49200 out of these 42% are female heading HHs, the most states affected by flood are White Nile and Central Darfur each 9 localities then come North Kordofan 7 localities. Moreover based on the need assessment done by SOS in the three target localities number of food inscure persons is estimated in Rifi Telkok 450 (2300 male, 2205 female, 720 chidren), in Sheikan (4000 male, 5000 female , 1440 children) and in Guli (665 male, 835 femle, 240 children) in State of Kassal, NK and White Nile respectively Activities recommended by the community to be introduced in this project include support of demonstration farms, distribution of certified seeds & tools and the construction of HPs and mini water yards using solar energy and rehabilitation of water points, and animal vaccination, training of community animal health workers in the selected village was also recommended.In the targeted communities, it is safe to say that all community members are vulnerable, particularly as the effects of the prolonged drought and skyrocketing commodity prices continue to affect our targeted populations. Further, the limit of access to water to communities presents a challenge. However, the most vulnerable groups are generally: women headed households with large family sizes, people lacking income sources, households hosting people with disabilities, farmers with lack of access to inputs and improved farming techniques and large families, families with children attending schools that lack sufficient water and families that their house or farms affected by flooding. The needs of these groups are varied, but generally include: a need for improved access to improved inputs (seeds, tools, techniques) for agricultural production and small businesses (starting capital and the relevant equipment).Due to COVID-19 movement restrictions, it has been challenging for SOS to conduct a full baseline. However, other baseline will be conducted during the inception phase of the project. Also SOS adopt to perform post assessment to touch the initial improvement and identify the gap, and this assessment usually conduct by its staff in the 4th quarter, so the proposed activities in this proposal made based on the findings that resulted from the recent assessments done by SOS in the three targeted localities in three states.

2 - Activities/Outputs

SOS Sahel has presence in the three targeted states and from the beginning of the project implementation process, SOS Sahel will involve all people in the project areas and related government departments in the process of the project implementation. SOS Sahel in consultation with community representation will develop selection criteria for the most vulnerable people. Following this, SOS Sahel will use the information provided by the FSL and WASH sectors to cross checked and verify the target. Likewise the project will focus on Interventions that foster the improvement of hygienic practices associated with using hygiene facilities and the health use of water and food. The proposed WASH interventions aimed to enhance health situation in the target localities through construction of different types of community assets such as, (HH/,schools latrines and communal latrines) and disseminate proper hygiene and sanitation messages (personal hygiene, usage of latrine and general cleanness) among targeted, school children and community members through drama and theater. The activities will be accompanied with awareness sessions accompanied throughout the implementation stages. To reach zero open defecation and improving environmental sanitation CLTS approach and solid waste management program will be planned to be implemented in the target community. Health committees at community level (whom know the context and culture of the targeted communities) ,will be established and trained to equip them with the required capacities and knowledge to promote community health. so big behavior and behavior change will be expected. The proposed activities:Water:- Rehabilitation of water sources using solar system- Establishing/ Training of water management committees. Sanitation:- Construction of new communal latrines - Support community learning total sanitation (CLTS) in communities - Support sanitation in basic school (Hand washing facilities) Hygiene Promotion:- Provision of health education materials (IEC materials)- Support environmental cleaning campaigns (Provision of garbage collection hand tools & home to home visits)- Conduct hygiene sessions and health awareness- Formation of health school clubs- Formation /Training of Community Hygiene Promoters (CHP) Food security and livelihood:- Provision of improved agricultural seeds and tools for small farmers- Training of extension agents- Training Farmers on Smart climate agriculture extension packages- Training and support women on income generation activities (IGA)- Provide lactating Goats for most vulnerable families with children expected outputs:- Broken water yards were fully rehabilitated using solar system, and the associated water committee trained - New Communal latrines built - CLTS sessions conducted for for target community - Hand washing facilities constructed in the target schools - Hygiene sessions and health awareness conducted- Health school clubs were formed and trained - CHPs were identified and trained - Environmental cleaning campaigns were organized - Improved agricultural seeds and tools provided to small farmers- Agriculture knowledge and skills improved for small Farmers- Income generated activities for target women provided - Children diets improved

5.2 - Impact on environment/natural resources

The project integrates protective environmental measures as the improved agricultural techniques result in greater soil porosity and lesser soil erosion. Further, by providing alternative livelihood pathways (agri-business, goat raising and improved agricultural production), ideally the need for environmentally harmful methods like collecting firewood will be minimised. Additionally, by increasing and diversifying livelihood pathways, this ideally reduces the need to cut trees for firewood. Further, the WASH intervention includes a hydrological survey activity that ensures no unnecessary drilling will occur and water harvesting activities will be included.SOS Sahel is a natural resource management and environmental focused organization. The project partners will consider SOS Sahel methods for analysing environmental risks that will take part at different stages of the project cycle. The community participatory planning approach gives the community stakeholders the opportunity to discuss the environmental impact of community selected, interventions and ways of mitigating negative ones. For example, this may be the environmental impacts that a Hand Pumps could have an immediate environment impact if it is located next to a migratory route. Community led Monitoring & Evaluation processes will be used to monitor and assess the real impacts of certain inputs and interventions on the environment during the delivery services in particularly in case of rehabilitation of water sources. SOS Sahel will engage high quality competent staffs who are committed to ensuring adequate service and product delivery to the targeted, the conflict over natural resource and protection of water sources, grazing land farming land and forest will also be part of the project through training workshop to enable community improve environment for their better livelihood and life
