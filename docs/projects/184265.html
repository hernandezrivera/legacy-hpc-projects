Project Summary

In response to the growing humanitarian crisis in southern Somalia, SOS Children villages is proposing the following interventions to expand lifesaving child protection services to vulnerable populations in Middle Shabelle, Galgadud , Bay and Bakool regions. The complexity of the operating environment requires multifaceted approaches that are tailored to each location including; Strengthen and capacitate community-based child protection mechanisms (CBCPM) to prevent and mitigate CP risks through multi-layered, community based psychosocial support interventions to reduce vulnerabilities and risks to children by building a protective environment at family and community levels in the targeted districts. The project support for the effected individuals include direct support of identified cases through conducting case management including referral, mental health and psychosocial and counselling support. In addition to this, unaccompanied and separated children in the intervention targeted districts will be reunified with their caregivers/guardians and those children who do not have caregivers/guardians will be supported through community based foster care placements. In addition, the proposed action will ensure inclusion of person with disabilities, mainstreamed in every action of the project, and as well carry out awareness creation on the rights of children and person with disabilities. Child protection frontline staff, Child safeguarding and Child protection focal points, community volunteers as well as staff from local government authority will be capacitated on child safeguarding, PSEA and code of conduct to ensure that all interventions are safe for children.
