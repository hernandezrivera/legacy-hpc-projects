Project Summary

Save the Children, through the proposed response, will provide integrated lifesaving nutrition services to underfive children, adolescents, pregnant and breastfeeding women in Akobo county and Abyei administrative areas. The proposed action seeks to improve access to quality preventive and curative nutrition services through CMAM and MIYCN approaches with the aim of reduce excess morbidity and mortality related to malnutrition among vulnerable children and women. SCI emergency nutrition programs will include; i) Community mobilization to increase access and coverage of CMAM services; ii)treatment of under-five children and PBWs with acute malnutrition through in-patient and outpatient therapeutic and supplementary programs; iii) Institutional feeding program; iv) Maternal and adolescent dietary supplementation; v) Promotion of optimal IYCF through mother-to-mother support groups; vi) Improving dietary diversity through household kitchen gardens and chicken rearing in selected locations; vii) Building household resilience through provision of emergency cash assistance to households with SAM cured children at high risk of relapse; viii) Strengthen nutrition information systems and operations research to ensure an evidence based response; x) Building capacity of frontline nutrition workers and institutions at all levels. In all nutrition sites, the SMOH will deploy nutrition promoters who will be incentivized by SCI as well as integrate nutrition programs with health and WASH in championing institutionalization of nutrition services within health sector and transition agenda to SMOH. SCI will work through communities to promote, protect and support recommended IYCF practices through one-on-one and group counselling sessions. In addition to the CMAM and behavior change interventions, SCI will support 35 static OTP/TSFPs, 7 mobile OTP/TSFPs and 2 stabilization centers integrated within health facilities. the services will be delivered through static and outreach/mobile facilities for remote and hard-to-reach areas in the targeted locations. SCI will work with MOH, UNICEF and WFP to secure timely provision of nutrition supplies necessary for nutrition service delivery. SCI targets 84,667 U/5 children and PBWs with SAM, MAM and MYICN services. SCI will make deliberate efforts to enhance coordination and integration with WASH, FSL, Health, Protection and Education clusters to address the drivers of malnutrition through a multi-sectoral approach that aims to reduce the vulnerabilities, prevent malnutrition and build resilience among the vulnerable people. Specific multi-sectoral activities in the proposed action include increasing access to safe water and sanitation services, promotion of critical WASH messaging, integration of early childhood development strategies in nutrition sites, kitchen gardening. chicken rearing pilot, screening and treatment of children with malaria, diarrhea and cough in nutrition sites, child protection and SGBV risk mitigation interventions within nutrition sites. Hygiene interventions will be promoted among household and nutrition facilities to reduce risk of hygiene-related morbidities and improve nutrition outcomes. SCI will monitor the nutrition situation and the needs of affected people through SMART surveys, needs assessments, monthly program data collection and analysis and joint monitoring visits. Spot checks, supportive supervision and monitoring visits will enhance service delivery and engage with community members and other stakeholders to gauge evolving needs. Mother-to-mother support groups will be supported to undertake household visits, provide peer counselling to caregivers/ parents as well as PBWs on dietary diversity, food preparation, handling, storage and preservation in promoting optimal nutrition within the households. Community awareness, complaints and feedback mechanisms, FDGs will be utilized in enhancing accountability to affected people.

1. Needs

South Sudan (SSD) continues to face overlapping crises including floods, conflict, and food insecurity – all contributing to persisting high levels of malnutrition. Approximately 4.3 million children under five, as well as pregnant and breastfeeding women, will need life-saving treatment and preventive care. This includes 0.65 million children under five at risk of severe acute malnutrition, 1.4 million at risk of moderate acute malnutrition, and 1.1 million mothers requiring urgent treatment (IPC-AMN 2024). an estimated 2.1 million children aged 6-59 months are expected to suffer from elevated levels of acute malnutrition of which 650,000 have been projected to be severely malnourished (IPC 2024). The South Sudan Nutrition cluster county priority map rates Abyei as priority 1 while Akobo as priority 2. Akobo county and Abyei administration areas are among the 49 counties facing critical acute food insecurity and among the 67 percent of the locations facing acute malnutrition burden for the post-harvest and lean seasons. inadequate child feeding practices, high disease burden, with malaria, diarrhea and cough reported as most common illness treated among under-five children, low coverage of health and nutrition services, food insecurity and limited access to sanitation facilities continue to pose children and PBWs at risk of acute malnutrition. The SMART survey conducted by SC (April 2024) in Akobo County indicated a dire situation regarding the nutrition status of children. The survey indicated GAM rates greater than the WHO 15% threshold in the county. The high GAM prevalence is associated with morbidities among under-fives coupled with poor feeding practices. Low coverage of immunization i.e. VAS was at 41.3%, measles vaccination was at 54.8% and deworming at 49.8% all of which were below the recommended 80%. MIYCN indicators surveyed revealed exclusive breastfeeding rate of 61.4%, minimum dietary diversity among 6-23 months children of 8.4% and minimum acceptable diet of 5.6%. The progressive improvement in exclusive breastfeeding rate from June (SMART survey- SCI 2024) to Dec (SCI's Multisector needs assessment - MSNA) could be attributed to intensified MIYCN interventions which needs to be further strengthened so that gains made are not lost. In addition, the food consumption score, household dietary diversity score, and coping strategy index are not impressive with 51%, 72%, and 68.9% respectively. The critical Malnutrition situation in Akobo East has further been exacerbated by influx of approx. 43,000 returnees from Ethiopia (Dec.2023 Akobo County RRC coordinator report) majority of which arrived while visibly malnourished. The situation is similiar in Abyei administration area as 77% of the catchment population have access to treatment services in nutrition sites (SCI's MSNA 2024). Abyei is among the prioritized counties considering high rates of female headed households within IDP communities, high GAM burden, low deworming coverage (53.7%) and poor MIYCN, childcare and WASH practices. just 76% of infants were reported as exclusively breastfed in the first 5months and minimum acceptable diet for children 6-23 months was 11%. The malnutrition situation in Akobo and Abyei is expected to worsen due to the multifactorial etiology of malnutrition in complex and protracted emergencies. it is therefore paramount to promote and support preventive and therapeutic nutrition programs as well as optimal maternal, infant and young child feeding and care practices focusing on the most vulnerable populations including under-five children, adolescents and PBWs both at nutrition sites and in the communities.

2. Project activities

SCI will implement nutrition sensitive and nutrition specific interventions with special focus on strengthening nutrition systems and service delivery environment in line with the cluster objectives. Objective 1: To increase equitable access and utilization of quality preventive and curative services for management of wasting in emergencies to children underfive, adolescents and PBWs. SCI will conduct the following activities - i) provide therapeutic and routine treatment in nutrition facilities for 6-59 months and PBWs with SAM and MAM ii) screening using MUAC and referral of children and PBWs from community to nutrition facilities iii) conduct refresher trainings of CNVs, caregivers, M2MSGs, health and nutrition workers on CMAM, MAMI, MIYCN, ECD, child protection, safe recognition and referral of SGBV survivors to appropriate services iv) Provide micronutrient supplements to underfive children, adolescent girls and women through routine and mass campaigns v) Capacity strengthening of health care workers at all levels on CMAM protocols, identification and management of acute malnutrition and monitoring of CMAM activities vi) equip volunteers with screening and referral materials vii) provide on-going support to facility and community health and nutrition workforce through supportive supervision, joint monitoring and review meetings ix) Establish community complaints and feedback mechanisms in all nutrition facilities by setting up private complaints desk Objective 2: To increase access to integrated and quality preventive nutrition services and adoption of positive nutrition practices in emergencies. SC will conduct the following activities - i) provision of individual and group counselling on MIYCN to pregnant women, mothers and caregivers of children less than 23months ii) support M2MSGs including fathers to deliver key MIYCN messages, key WASH messages and GBV protection, prevention and mitigation within nutrition sites iii) Provide training to volunteers on counselling skills and social behavior change (SBC) approaches iv)Establishment of kitchen gardens, chicken rearing at nutrition facilities and community to improve dietary diversity and positive feeding habits v) Provide ECD recreation kits in OTP/TSFP sites and stabilization centers vi) train M2MSG on IYCF promotion mother led MUAC screening and cooking demonstrations vii) conduct gender and inclusion audits for all nutrition sites to identify potential barriers to services and ensure accessible, safe and inclusive services to women and girls viii) conduct weekly education sessions on MIYCN in communities through M2MSGs. Objective 3: To enhance needs analysis of nutrition situation and robust monitoring including operational research for nutrition services. SCI will conduct the following activities i) conduct anthropometric and mortality SMART surveys (lean season and post-harvest season) and multi-sectoral needs assessments (MSNA) in targeted locations ii) conduct operational research on effectiveness of cash plus interventions in nutrition programs iii) Support in data collection of Food security and Nutrition monitoring system (FSNMS)
