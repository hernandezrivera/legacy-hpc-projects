Project Summary

Food Basket: The overall goal of the project is to increase access to monthly emergency food provisions for recently displaced households, thereby contributing to improving the food security status of food insecure people in Idleb Governorate
Specifically, the project aims to distribute emergency monthly food baskets (FBs) for 1000 for displaced households and vulnerable host community members, and Ready-To-Eat Rations (RTERs) for 500 newly displaced households (including women, children, people with disabilities, and elderly people), through conducting rapid distribution of Ready-To-Eat Rations (RTERs) after registration and verification of beneficiaries, in order to provide a rapid response to newly displaced households; that will happen in an emergency context. as well as MWL field team will conduct door-to-door monthly distribution of Food Baskets to the selected families over a period of eight months.
Agriculture intervention: The project aims to take advantage of what is currently available in the form of local resources and services, and focus on dealing with the major challenges facing the olive sector in the target communities, specifically in relation to costs at all stages of production. In addition, the project aimed to overcome the gaps of knowledge about the best agricultural practices for the olive sector through Integrated Olive Management.
The project is designed to contribute to enhancing stabilization and rebuilding olive value chain through support 700 HHs from IDPs and Host Communities from mid-scale olive farmers who own 4-10 dunums of olive orchards in Aleppo Governorate (Afrin district) and support seasonal workers by select 1050 workers from (HHS IDPs and Host Communities) for manual harvesting, and pruning by cash for work activities.
Livestock intervention: MWL aims to improve the food security and livelihood situation of vulnerable conflict-affected people and enhance their resilience in Idleb and Aleppo, five district will be targeted (Afrin, Ariha, Harim, Idleb and Jabal samaan), through the fodder distribution for the vulnerable herders in the locations, targeted the livestock by the animals treatment for the 600 HH, and to raise the capacity and skills of veterinary service providers, MWL proposed project aim to t raise the capacity and skills of veterinary service providers and the herders on the Livestock topics for both of males and females by conducting the training which will be hold in two locations: Afrin and Dana. in addition, MWL will support 100 vulnerable family headed by women and have children under five years old with 2.5 L milk daily at no cost for six months which will improve child nutrition and enhancing Social cohesion in the targeted families

4 - Needs

Due to the severity displacement’s movement happened in the last three months where the number of IDPs reached to 100,000 based on the CCCM Cluster and with a huge need to the basic aids.Based on the need assessment conducted by MWL, the main problems are as following:•The high cost of compost, pesticides, pruning, plowing, and milling services, leading to an increase in production costs.•The spread of insects (olive fruit fly, olive moth, and olive psyllid), diseases (olive wilt, and Olive leaf spot (peacock eye spot)).•Lack of skilled workers for conducting services of pruning and pest management.•Marketing difficulties.All of these factos have caused a deterioration in the olive sector, olive oil unavailability, olive oil low quality, this has led to low farmer incomes, low market movement, and food insecurity accompanied by weakened livelihoods.The needs assessment also showed that the target areas are villages lacking agricultural support, in addition, these areas are difficult to access for most organizations, due to the need to request formal approval from the Turkish government. MWL team targeted (Afrin, Daret Azza, Ariha, Dana, Maaret Tamsrin) with assessment.MWL has made sure that project is targeting most vulnerable people MWl select the sub-districts that witnessed the displacement movement and have a big number of IDPs and PIN,The assessment analysis results showed a huge need for the interventions. 54% of respondents mentioned that one of the challenges on the livestock field is no availability of fodder and pastures, and 47 % of them see that supporting the herders by fodder will mitigate the challenges where 46 % mentioned that securing the medicine will contribute to face the challenges. 100% of respondents reported that the price of livestock feed has raised highly. 77% of respondents mentioned that the diseases are spread between the livestock. All the respondents mentioned that there isn’t training targeted veterinary service providers

5 - Implementation of activities

Food Basket: MWL will conduct direct distribution 6,000 emergency Food Baskets regularly (1000 per month for 8 months) to a total 1000 displaced families living in Idleb governorate by delivering eight rounds of food kits to targeted HHs based on set criteria, as MWL continues to operate in a context prone to new waves of emergencies, the distribution of RTERs for 500 newly displaced households.MWL will register the BNFs corroborating by LC in the work areas, the data entry will enter the BNFs information on the specified file. MWL will put the initial list of eligible beneficiaries progressively during the first month, then MWL will check all the beneficiaries’ identification cards to ensure they are the eligible by MEAL field team, after that MWL will purchase and distribution of FB and RTERs.Agriculture: MWL will distribute vouchers of the agricultural inputs (manure, 2 types of pesticide vouchers (insecticide, and fungicides), in addition to fuel vouchers for irrigation, vouchers to cover the cost of oil extracting, and vouchers of agricultural services for olive farmers (Manual harvesting, and pruning) -MWL Will contract with 700 seasonal workers (women) of IDPs and Host Communities, to harvest 2,800 dunums of olives for 700 HHs, within one month.-MWL Will contract 350 seasonal workers (men) from IDPs and Host Communities, to pruning 2,800 dunums of olives for 700 HHs, within one month.- Capacity building to supported olive farmers and increase the technical capacity and knowledge on the best agricultural practices of olive trees.Livestock: MWL will target 600 HH (400 for sheep and 200 for cow herders ) that met with identified criteria by 8 fodder rounds distribution with needed veterinary services, in addition supporting 100 vulnerable families headed by women and have children under five years old with 2.5 L milk daily at no cost for six months. which will improve enhancing Social cohesion in targeted families.
