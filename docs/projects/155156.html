Project Summary

The proposed project will aim to target 15000 beneficiaries in selected camps and host communities in provisioning safe and durable WASH services. While the main aim is to construct and rehabilitate water systems including latrines or bating cubicles, hygiene promotion will also be promoted in these communities through effective community outreach methods. It is envisaged that targeted communities will then take on the responsibility of maintaining their hygiene including the upkeep of their WASH facilities with a sense of collective ownership.

Needs

WASH infrastructure operations and maintenance, fecal sludge management in under-served areas, and human resources for hygiene promotion activities remain critical in target locations WV is proposing. There is a significant need to clean the latrines, both existing and new, through the FSM systems and also through solid waste management processes. Given the sheer proximity of WASH facilities to shelters, more efforts need to be put toward constructing water points which can provide safe water, particularly for consumption through more sustainable water supply systems. Protection meassures need to be considered when constructing WASH facilities including using designs that can serve PwDs. Finally, the need for hygiene promotion remain dire given the already dreadful conditions in the congested camps. Need for communities to take the ownership is necessery, so WASH committees need to be formed and robust hygiene awareness campaigns need to be carried out throughout the target camps.
