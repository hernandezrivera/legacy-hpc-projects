Project Summary

Medair aims to provide multi-sectoral response to crisis affected communities in Blue Nile State, including health, nutrition, and WASH services. The Shelter/NFI response plan includes the distribution of shelter materials for upgrades/repairs, NFI for affected populations, cash for work, trainings, and site maintenance (flood mitigation) works. The targeted population is the host communities by natural disasters, conflict or other peoples displacement.

1 - Needs

Targeted population has been affected by the influx of South Sudan and Ethiopian populations settling in camps and settlement. More than 50,000 new arrivals have been introduced into small and remote communities, and these people have received most of the humanitarian assistance. According to recent flood reports, the states of Kassala, Gedaref, Sennar, White Nile and Blue Nile saw 5,251 houses destroyed and 30,730 houses damaged by the most recent flood season. From the 184,058 people in these 5 states, only 34,850 people have received aid Shelter/NFI assistance. The gap is over 80% of the affected population without receiving assistance. The lack of assistance to the host communities or over emphasis on helping displaced peoples in camps has caused disparities in aid to the local communities. In addition, existent infrastructure and existent markets have been affected by the refugee response. Furthermore, social cohesion and community participation is lacking to solve existent issues that have been excavated by the current situation. In general terms, host communities are under represented and under covered by humanitarian actors responding to a refugee crisis. Exemplary dispariteis have been recorded in the Tunaydbah and Um Rakuba camps, where Medair has had presence since mid this current year.

2 - Activities/Outputs

The project plans to implement: - Construction and construction assistance of Tukuls (mud-brick houses). The construction will be community lead as much as possible. For extreme vulnerable households, Medair will provide construction assistance.- Distribution of Emergency Shelter kits (repair material and tools). These kits will be provided in the instance of an emergency but can be provided as a rehabilitation project in a targeted community. - Distribution of NFI kits. These kits will include general items and wash items as recognized in needs assessments. - DRR trainings (drainage for flood mitigation, promotion of community participation and disaster preparedness). This will include the creation of community lead activities to promote community participation and cohesion.

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

Medair seeks to ensure safe and equitable access to Shelter/NFI assistance free of harassment and partiality. Particular attention is given to women and children, as well as vulnerable groups such as the elderly and disabled, in the design and siting of infrastructure and in ensuring that they have equal opportunity to provide feedback and participate in the management of community facilities. Specific examples include: during distribution of NFI items, single head of households and elderly people whom the community cannot assist, Medair will facilitate safe and prompt assistance; community-wide engagement on labor and participatory selection to allow for input of women and other vulnerable groups such as the elderly or physically disabled; encouraging the involvement of women in trainings and meetings through Participatory Approach of Shelter and Safety committees. All staff members are trained on gender sensitive service delivery and share information on reporting mechanisms for beneficiary feedback. Efforts are made to ensure beneficiary feedback is available in gender and culturally appropriate methods, particularly to encourage women in the communities to feel comfortable providing their input.

7.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will inform and interact with inter-agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including SEA and abuse complaints?

Medair will work in coordination with inter-agency structures including accountability and feedback measures. This will include reporting cases of, or suspected cases of, SEA using the established reporting mechanism. Medair will further participate in and ensure training of all staff members in PSEA, implementing appropriate policies and reporting mechanisms and provide clear information to beneficiaries relating to the definition of SEA and reporting options available to them both internal and external. All Medair staff receive training in Protection against Sexual Exploitation and Abuse.relocatable staff are trained in protection-sensitive programming as part of Medair’s standard briefing procedures. Medair liaises with existing protection partners in allocations to identify and refer cases requiring specialized protection support.
