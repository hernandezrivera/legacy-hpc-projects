Project Summary

At national level, there has been steady progress in advancing gender equality and empowerment of women and girls on the elimination of all forms of discrimination against women resulting from the political commitments and efforts the federal government show cased in the past 27 or so years. This progress has been specifically geared towards areas that include eliminating gender disparity in school attendance, access to sexual and reproductive health care services, an increase in the number of women regional parliaments and an increase in women’s labor market participation.
It goes without saying that achieving gender equality and the empowerment of women is essential for effective action on all aspects of sustainable development. Urgent action is needed to empower women and girls ensuring that they have equal opportunities to benefit from development and remove the barriers that prevent them from being full participants in all spheres of society.
many girls, during the conflict, have experienced situations where they watched a family member, a friend or someone getting shot in front of their eyes at home or around and a number of dead bodies lying across the street swamped in blood. This could also render them to become mentally ill that could trigger fear, anxiety, depression, inability to sleep and take care of themselves. Be it in a normal situation and crisis, women and girls have special responsibility and carry the burden of taking care of the whole family, including cooking, fetching water, searching for means for food, taking care of sick person in the house, and taking care of children threefold of men and boys. That’s why, female students are so tired that they don’t feel good at school, esp. during lesson-they would rather not go to school.
Empowering girls, improving education facilities at public schools and creating community awareness, supporting educational materials and enhancing access and quality of education would be an ideal approach to minimize the multi-dimensional problems the rural women and girls, and schools in the four woredas such Ofla, Endamehoni, Atsbiwonberta and Enderta are now facing. To this end, TDA initiates this project concept note to empower girls through education in collaboration with your organization.
The main target groups are the mostly affected sections of people by the crisis and poverty in the project area. Initially, the project is intended to target a total of 3,834 with a possibility of expansion increasing in the future once the structures are fully in place and additional funds obtained. Emphasizing on supporting the girl children, poor girl students are to be the main target. The other specific target people are mothers who will be involved in income generating activities
• Direct Target :3834 female students
• Indirect Target: 14,338 community members in 4 woreda

3.2 - How will your project work with relevant local & national actors (e.g. CSOs, advocacy groups) in support of effective design, implementation and monitoring of the response, as well as ensure their meaningful participation in supporting this response

To achieve this project TDA working with different partners such that Tigray education bureau, health bureau, women and youth. The project will be implemented by TDA alongside community-based organizations and local government authorities as coordinating and collaborating partners in four woredas. Skill development training activities be arranged to build and strengthen the capacities of these organizations and agencies and thus contribute to sustainability of the project. Prior to implementation, orientation and planning workshop/meeting, which involves diverse stakeholders, will be organized in the project area. The purpose of these workshops is to introduce the nature and objectives of project to the stakeholders and prepare joint plan to be used for future implementation. TDA will make use of its own organizational structure to carry out different activities. It is in charge of implementing, monitoring, supervising and evaluating the project. Monitoring will be carried out on regular basis through project officer and coordinator to be employed. Education and training directorate has the responsibility of providing technical support, monitoring and evaluation. The community will participate in planning and handing over the project besides selecting of poor students, project monitoring and evaluation. Also, the funding agency shall monitor, evaluate and receive the financial and physical accomplishment report. A baseline study will be carried out at the community level under the supervision of TDA to document the performance of the project with regard to school enrolment, dropouts, facilities at schools, etc. Besides, understanding and awareness of the community on the significance of education at individual woreda and regional level, the problems facing the communities in educating their children and themselves and alignment of the factors associated with quality and inclusive education with the government policy and programs will also be assessed. Regular reports, during implementation, will be managed on a quarterly and annual basis or depending on the interest of the supporting organization. A completion report of the project will be prepared and submitted to the donors. TDA will manage the necessary resources (staff, logistics, planning and monitoring tools) to ensure the smooth and timely implementation of the proposed activities.
