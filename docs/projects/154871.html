Project Summary

Recognising that humanitarian response in Iraq is shifting focus from emergency frontline assistance to addressing recovery, resilience and development-peace nexus activities, the Emergency Telecoms Cluster (ETC) has planned to phase out of Iraq by 30 March 2019. Therefore, during Q1 of 2019, the ETC will close internet connectivity services in 18 camps in As Salamiyah, Haj Ali and Hassan Sham as there is availability of services from local providers that humanitarian actors can contract directly. The ETC will also complete outstanding projects for which commitments have been made to the humanitarian community and handover support and maintenance of the security communications network to either United Nations Department of Safety and Security (UNDSS) or lead agency to be agreed by Iraq ICT Working Group.

Needs

Results from ICT assessments have identified the need to continue operating robust security communication services to guarantee the safety of humanitarian responders operating in the Kurdistan Region of Iraq and Ninewa. Demobilisation of the internet connectivity infrastructure across 18 camp locations, responsible shutdown and handover of services including accountability for assets to ensure proper transfer and/or disposal are key phase-out activities that the ETC will undertake.

Activities

1. Demobilise internet connectivity, IT and power supply equipment installed in 5 camps in As Salamiyah, 9 camps in Haj Ali and 4 camps in Hassan Sham.2. Provide technical advice to humanitarian organisations to contract the right internet connectivity from local providers to replace ETC internet services.3. ETC will work closely with the humanitarian community to ensure a smooth transition of the ​security telecommunication network to either UNDSS or lead agency designated by the ICT Working Group including preparing and ensuring endorsement of the inter-agency cost-sharing plan for sustainable support of security communication services.4. Account for all ETC assets and ensure handover and/or disposal and return of equipment loaned to ETC in Iraq by global partners such as Ericsson Response.5. Provide coordination, training and information-sharing services through ETC regular Local and Global ETC meetings, including dissemination of IM products.

Mitigation Measures

How is your project accountable to affected population

ETC supports the whole humanitarian community by providing common services to humanitarian responders, to enable relief and life-saving interventions. All UN agencies, National and International NGOs benefit from these services to reach the beneficiaries of their assistance and are directly accountable to the affected population.
