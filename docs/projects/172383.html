Project Summary

Welthungerhilfe will deliver assistance to the most vulnerable rural communities in Gokwe North and South through lifesaving cash assistance and short-term livelihood, food and nutrition support. WHH’s dual approach of linking humanitarian efforts to development processes will ensure immediate needs are met, while laying the foundation for long-term sustainability.

1 - Activities

1.1 Provide monthly food baskets through cash-based transfers to acutely food insecure population in rural areas: Activity will support 8000 individuals, translating to 1600hh (800 GKN; 800GKS) in the two districts with unconditional cash transfers of $12.29 per person per month at the start of the lean season between October and December 2021 and at the peak of the lean season, between January and April 2021 to help offset loss of income due to shocks and stresses, including the impact of COVID-19 Objective 2 Activities. Activities under this objective will be ongoing from January 2021 to December 20212.1 Train 25 000 households on sustainable agricultureThe activity will target 125 000 individuals, translating to 25 000hh (3 000 hh Gokwe South, 22 000hh Gokwe North).Farmers will receive 2 day trainings on topics including Good Agronomic Practices, climate smart agriculture, water conservation. Emphasis will be placed with emphasis on resilient small livestock production. The target for Gokwe The trainings will be done between January and March 20212.2 Support small scale vegetable production for 25 000 households in Gokwe North and Gokwe South: The activity will target 125 000 individuals, translating to 25 000hh (3 000 hh Gokwe South, 22 000hh Gokwe North).In the first half of the project, beneficiary households will receive standard seed packs comprising peas, rape, tomatoes, onion seed. The activity will be done between February and June 2021. 2.3 Support 5 000 food insecure households with sorghum seed: 5 000 households (2 000 Gokwe South; 3 000 Gokwe North) will each be supported with 5kgs of seed, 50kg compound D and 50 kg Ammonium Nitrate, sufficient for 0.4 ha production. Selection of farmers will be based on availability of labour and adequate farming land. The activity will be done between Sept and Dec 20212.4Faciliate rehabilitation of community infrastructure: Activity will support rehabilitation of 40 community dip tanks,. This will ensure continuous water supply for the livestock and contribute towards reducing poverty deaths . The acticitry will be on going from January to September 2021

8 - Accountability to Affected People
8.1 - Describe how your project was designed in consultation with communities? How will you incorporate communities in project implementation and what feedback mechanisms do you have in place?

WHH promotes a participatory approach to project cycle management In this way, women, girls, men and boys across the age spectrum were involved in the assessment and design of the action. In this regard, Welthungerhilfe undertook a Multi-Sectoral Needs Assessment in Gokwe South in March 2020 to fully understand the needs and gaps of the communities in food and nutrition security and WASH. The assessment was done in the context of a failing macro-economic environment, increase in prices of basic goods and a poor 2019/20 agricultural season. Data were collected through household surveys and focus group discussions with women of reproductive age, youth male and youth female. Key informant interviews were also conducted with rrelevant stakeholders at district and national level. Findings from the assessment were also used in the design of this project.Communities and stakeholders will be able to share their feedback and complaints about the quality and implementation of the project outputs and activities through a variety of methods including phone or SMS on +263 772414076, email to complaints.zimbabwe@welthungerhilfe.de and also directly to field staff and community representatives in the field. Feedback will be communicated with the participants, including the staff, and to all key stakeholders.
