Project Summary
Takaful has designed this program aims to support the food security of the most vulnerable IDPs and part of the host community in Idleb and Aleppo governorates,
Main interventions:
First: providing most vulnerable IDPs and HCs standard food baskets on monthly basis, targeting 23,200 HHs 116,000 individuals (14268 men 27492 women – 35960 boys -38280 girls) in Idleb and Aleppo governorate, The distribution will be conducted at specified food distribution points (FDPs), safe FDPs will be chosen, away from the military centers with enough and safe distance and beneficiaries have easy access to them. For beneficiaries who have difficulties to reach to distribution points such as families headed by child or Disabled persons, the baskets will be transferred to their location using light vehicle. For the e-voucher beneficiaries, TAS will contract with shops and e-voucher system. The targeted location will be selected according to the FSLA and gap analysis issued by FSL cluster. TAS will coordinate with the food security cluster and with other organizations who works in the same sector and field to reach the right beneficiaries and avoid any duplication. The project will ensure equal access for both genders with the priority for women, children headed household, disabled person, sick, PLW and elderly in addition the project staff will include female and male members trained on the protection principles and gender equality.
Supporting wheat value chain by providing the assistance to 500 farmers (males and females),500 hectares in A’zaz and Afrin, 500 farmers will be supported in 2024- 2025, where the main activities are:, providing farmers with theoretical and practical extension training sessions, supporting them with cash for land preparation, seeds, and fertilizers the harvesting process, marketing their crops, and establishing units for extinguishing the fires that may occur prior to the wheat harvest, additionally, TAS will support the market power by purchasing part of the wheat crops of the supported farmers to allow them to encourage them to cultivate wheat in the next season. Project’s locations were identified based on clusters reports, HNAP reports, HRP, and detailed need assessments conducted by the TAS team.
Supporting livestock sector by providing assistance to 500 livestock (sheep and goats) keepers (males and females), about 5,000 sheep and goats in Afrin, A’zaz and Al bab districts. The main activities are providing feedstuffs, mobile clinic, vitamins and mineral blocks, and deworming support. In addition to extension services and trainings. This intervention is proposed according to LEGS recommendation in recovery phase.
We already secured 3 M $ though BHA fund.
Implementation of activities:
In-kinf food assistance for the assessed food insecure people: Takaful will either receive baskets from the donor, or purchase them from the markets according to Takaful policies. The distribution will be conducted in specified food distribution points (FDPs), safe FDPs will be chosen, away from the military centers with enough and safe distance and beneficiaries have easy access to them. For beneficiaries Who have difficulties to reach distribution points such as families headed by a child or Disabled persons, the baskets will be transferred to their location.
Food voucher modality: The value of each month $65 enables the beneficiaries to obtain approx. 60 KG of food assistance which covers the around 1700 Kcal per person per day for the family as the items selected are in accordance with the cluster guidelines. The vouchers will be distributed to households after verifying their IDs. female members will be present and will distribute vouchers to women to ensure access and protection. The household must sign beside their names on the distribution list. The beneficiaries will receive also an electronic brochure including vendors' addresses, numbers, WhatsApp number for complaints and small descriptions about how to redeem the e-voucher. More importantly, the beneficiaries will be provided with the specific date and time for voucher redemption. After the completion of the previous activities, and once the beneficiaries’ balances are loaded with the approved amount, the beneficiary will have the capability to redeem the available balance with the approved items from the contracted vendors (sell points), where the beneficiary will choose the items he/she needs from the identified list of items, where maximum amount of each item the beneficiary can buy, will be defined. During the redemption process, the project management team manages and monitors the activities of the beneficiaries and the vendors in real-time including beneficiaries’ balances, vendor balances, product activities and much more. Redeemed vouchers will be paid to vendors within 15 days of each phase, this quick process enables vendors to manage their cash and restock for coming vouchers. Supporting Wheat Value Chain by providing assistance to 500 farmers, 500 hectares in A’zaz and Afrin, where the main activities are: • Cash for land preparation. • Cash for seeds. • Cash for fertilizer. • Theoretical and Practical Extension Services.• Spot Check Visit and Providing Consultancy Services.• Removing Undesired Weeds and Dealing with Diseases.• Disaster Risk Reduction (DRR)• Harvesting and Crops Marketing.• Market Power Support: by purchasing part of farmers' wheat crops (500 kg from each farmer), totaling of 250 ton which will be filtered and processed through a contracted mill, in order to produce 182 tons of wheat flour (which will be baked and distributed as free bread) in addition to the production of 68 tons of bran (kind of fodder for livestock) (which will be distributed for 366 livestock breeder).Supporting Livestock Value Chain by providing assistance to 500 farmers, 5000 sheep or goat in A’zaz and Afrin, where the main activities are:• Identifying and Verifying Beneficiaries• Selecting and Contracting with Vendors.• Providing Theoretical and Practical Extension Services• Feedstuff and mineral blocks distribution:• Mobile clinic services and consultancy visits:
