Project Summary

The goal of the project is to encourage students in schools to learn by providing them with a daily meal (this meal provides something simple and negligible for the student, but it can be considered as alleviating the burden on the families by providing food for the students and continuing their education). The group that will be targeted in the project is primary students only from 6 years old to 12 years old
Students in the city of Al-Tall, which number 9,159 students in 14 schools, will be targeted Needs: The circumstances that the country went through did not only affect the economic, health, nutritional, etc. levels But it also affected education, because the cost of living has become difficult for families, many of whom are no longer able to support their children for their education (education is one of the rights of children), and therefore aid must be provided to families with children, and here comes the role of school meals, which will ease the burden on families. The burden of feeding their children to nourish them during the work period. The child needs nutrition in general to live a healthy life, and he needs to eat during work in particular, because the student needs energy that he derives from eating his food.

Project implementation
A workshop will be held in a place designated by the association through which sandwiches will be made for a specific number of students on a daily basis and sent to schools by loading them into a car, and then the distribution staff will distribute them to the students during the break.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The project will be implemented in a ready-made location, which is owned by the association and has its own warehouse School meals will be provided to students in Al Tal City schools during the two breaks Work starts from the morning at 7 until 12 noon. Every day, about 2,600 sandwiches will be made for 2,600 students. During the day there will be two shifts, the first of which /1300/ will be made, after which the sandwiches will be placed in baskets and then placed in a car and transported to specific schools according to a weekly distribution plan. Upon arriving at the school, one supervisor remains to distribute the sandwiches to the students in coordination with the school principals. After completing the distribution of allocations for the first shift, the car driver returns to pick up the distribution supervisors for the second shift During the first distribution operations during the students’ break time, the sandwich making team prepares the second meals (the second shift) to be transported and distributed during the students’ second break in schools.After completing both shifts, the team cleans up to prepare for the next day. During the process of delivering the baskets containing the sandwich, a worker with the driver delivers those baskets to the supervisors (this worker is responsible for receiving the baskets from the workshop and delivering them to the supervisors, while remaining with the baskets as a guarding operation for them). The process will be repeated daily over a period of a month. Every 4 schools will be targeted in a month. We have 14 schools over a period of 7 months. The work in the workshop will be monitored during the making of the sandwich and carried out by the project manager and logistical supervisor.From the beginning of the preparation process until the completion of the two shifts and packing it into the transport vehicle (with follow-up assistance by two workers during the sandwich making process).Then comes the process of monitoring the delivery of the sandwiches placed in the boxes by a supervisor who is with the driver and who delivers those sandwiches to the distribution employees, while recording the quantities of the number of sandwiches and boxes, delivery dates and hours in a special note for the supervisor (monitoring the delivery of sandwiches to schools).Distribution staff will distribute the sandwiches in schools, with the possibility of a logistics officer being with them during distribution operations. The project manager may also be present at times. And write the quantities distributed by them in their own memorandum.Operations are generally supervised by a project manager and logistic, and each operation has its own supervisor.The report preparer will communicate and coordinate with all departments in order to take the required data at each stage and coordinate with the purchasing and logistical staff, recording the data and information in the reports of receiving and entering mater etc
