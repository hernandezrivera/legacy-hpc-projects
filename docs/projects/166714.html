Project Summary

Train activists in the use of mobile databases to register and track beneficiaries, Identify and register the beneficiaries, Train community activists and leaders on prevention against the spread of COVID-19, Promote multi-sector coordination meetings with the key sector and community leadership to share information and create energies in the effective care of victims, Support the key sectors of protection and community leadership in publicizing the protection policies of the group at risk from the pandemic of COVID-19, Promote awareness actions in the community about the preventive measures of COVID-19, Promote awareness and awareness actions on GBV, Faced with the COVID-19 pandemic, spaces at the level of women in resettlement centers, Sensitize groups of the rich in the community about the practice of equalization in the face of the COVID-19 pandemic, Provide psychosocial assistance to the at-risk group so as not to suffer stigma in the community, Train women heads of families in the production of homemade masks, Distribute life-saving items (homemade masks, soap, plastic buckets to the at-risk group and dignity kits for girls with disabilities), Promote radio debates in local language to disseminate training on preventing the spread of COVID-19, Produce IEC material for visibility to raise awareness and awareness of the community in terms of protection against COVID-19, Refer to the risk group for basic protection services.
