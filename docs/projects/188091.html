Project Summary

The project aims to respond to 16,500 HH with a total of 84,500 individuals disaggregated roughly as(18,590 men, 20,280 women, 22,815 boys, 22,815 girls) of the most vulnerable conflict-affected IDPs, and host communities in Idleb and Aleppo governorates, and prioritizing households headed by female, child, disabled, or elderly, then households with severely ill or disabled members, children below 5, elderly, pregnant or lactating women; with special attention to those recently displaced or living overcrowded.
The project contributes to providing life-saving and life sustaining shelter & NFI assistance through the following activities:
1- provide 8,500 HH with seasonal and supplementary NFIs and assistance including winter clothing, stoves, and fuel in Al-Bab, Afrin, and Azaz subdistricts, and camps. Each household will receive cash assistance with value of 150$ and prioritize its needs of these items accordingly.
2-Provision of emergency shelter through instalment of 1,000 tents in (Azaz, suran) in Aleppo governorate, (Dana, Maaret Tamsrin) in Idleb governorate.
3-Construction of temporary transitional units for 500 HH in (Azaz,Al-Bab) in Aleppo governorate,(Dana, Maaret Tamsrin) in Idleb governorate.
4-Conducting sites improvement works (Infrastructure in IDP sites)for 2500HH in (Dana, Maaret Tamsrin) in Idleb governorate.
This project will also contribute towards reinforce an enabling protection environment and community cohesion by improving housing and related communities through
-Rehabilitation of 700 of damaged or unfinished housing with an average cost of 750$ per house in (Harim, Salqin, Iskat, Kafr Takharim, Dana) in Idleb governorate, (Azaz, Al-Bab, Suran, Daret Azza) in Aleppo governorate
Needs assessment was conducted early this year to identify the project locations.
In all activities, SDI considers the protection of the beneficiaries, so the distribution centers and the shops provide the assistance will be prepared with functional firefighting equipment, brochures of safety and security practice will be distributed to beneficiaries, Moreover, female staff will be recruited and trained to ensure more protection from exploitation and sexual abuse for girls and women and to deal with any GBV cases may encounter during the project.
Most importantly SDI pays high attention to people with disabilities, elderlies, and people with special needs and for those assistance will be delivered to home.
Housing, Land and Property (HLP) rights of shelter sites, shelter beneficiaries, and house owners will be considered throughout all stages of shelter repair interventions, and an agreement will be signed to guarantee the rights of the beneficiaries. Coordination with SNFI cluster and other active members is essential to ensure geographical separation and avoid overlapping/duplication.
All covid 19 precautions measures were adopted during the project, such as (respecting social distance, wearing masks, Sterilizers). Also, banners were fixed at meeting points to raise beneficiaries against covid19, complaints mechanism, PEAS…etc.
M&E staff will monitor the activities implementation and continually give their feedback and comments to the project manager, the surveys will include questions on the teams’ performance, beneficiary satisfaction, and complaints

4 - Needs

Based on OCHA reports, Syria remains one of the world’s most complex humanitarian emergencies characterized by ongoing hostilities which have killed hundreds of thousands of people, triggered one of the worst displacement crises of our time, and led to the widespread destruction of civilian and agricultural infrastructure, including homes, schools, health facilities, water supply and irrigation systems. Today, 13.4 million people in Syria are in need of humanitarian assistance - a 21 per cent increase compared to 2020 - with needs increasingly being exacerbated by economic decline.The decade-long crisis has inflicted immense suffering on the civilian population who have experienced massive and systematic violations of international humanitarian and human rights law, including more than 1,350 attacks on education and medical facilities and related personnel, bombardment which has caused over 12 million people to flee their homes, and arbitrary detention, abduction, torture as well as other serious abuses. Almost 12,000 children have been killed or injured since 2011, and 47 per cent of young people have had a member of their immediate family or close friend die. With around half of Syria’s children having known nothing but a lifetime of crisis - 2.45 million of whom were estimated to be out of school in 2020 alone - an entire generation is at risk of being lost. Long-standing and deep-rooted trauma, much of which remains unaddressed, means a mental health crisis looms large.

5 - Implementation of activities

SDI will engage with local communities through holding meetings and/or focus groups with prominent figures in order to inform them of the project goals and increase awareness to the project activities.In rehabilitation of damaged or unfinished housing activity which targets 600 shelter unit, SDI announces the project and open a registration office to receive applications directly from house owners and beneficiaries and check the HLP documentations, MEAL staff will conduct community HLP verification to 100% of houses. The technical staff, will conduct a technical field inspection of all shelter units to verify that they are safe and repairable, prepare BOQs and scope of work needed in each unit, and closely supervise the works of contractors to ensure quality rehabilitation works as well as media staff will document the works in each house.Beneficiaries will be hosted in delivered houses after signing house owner- beneficiary free tenancy agreement. In activities 1.1.1.2Provision of seasonal and supplementary NFIs including heating fuel which targets 8500 HH, SDI will receive the beneficiary lists and pay visits to a random sample of beneficiaries to verify they meet the project vulnerable criteria. The modality of delivering the assistance is cash.In the Implementation site improvements works activity which targets 2500 HH, SDI engineering team will monitor and follow up the implementation stage on daily basis to ensure that all works are in line with the specifications.

10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Beneficiaries are at risk of being exposed to sexual misconduct and they might not report the incident due to: 1. lack of knowledge of reporting mechanisms, especially for PwDs and women living in faraway areas. 2. feeling afraid of retaliation. 3. fear of stigma. 4. fear of deprivation of assistance. 5. being unable to understand written banners for being illiterate and/or for having a visual disability.6. for children, language might be complicated. To mitigate these risks, SDI will : 1.aim at clearly announcing the hotline number and email of PSEA of SDI and UN through many means, like, banners, social media, and messages to beneficiaries’ phones through telegram channel to ensure reaching to the biggest number of beneficiaries including women living in faraway areas and people with disabilities. 2. Conduct PSEA training for all staff members and contractors/suppliers dealing with beneficiaries and make them read and sign CoC that state Zero Tolerance towards SEA violations. 3. Conduct community awareness raising sessions on PSEA reporting any violations and stress on the confidentiality and on eliminating any other fears. 4. Conduct awareness raising sessions for children in an appropriate language. 5. complaint boxes 6. Using PSEA field and organizational checklists tools. 7. Approach PwDs with appropriate means to be informed of PSEA complaint mechanisms. 8. Conduct consultations and FGDs with community members and leaders on PSEA mitigation measures.
