Project Summary

The project is designed to address the Protracted IDPs in camps needs based identified by Inter Agency Need Assessment led Sector lead including OCHA, IOM and UNHCR - ESNFI sector, the key element of data collection is also HNO, HRP and Sector Objective Plan.
In Nyala locality joint inter -sectors' need based assessment had been conducted in March and September 2019. Accordingly the project is designed to effectively intervene.
Standard NFIs will be distributed 10,450 to selected protracted IDP in gathering locations in Nyala and Marshang. 62373 returnees 60% women headed households, vulnerable man 20%, disabled 15% and 5% single old aged are supported by Shelter kits, out of the 500 most vulnerable such as people with disability, older aged over 70 years sigle are given environment constructed houses in Marshang 30%, Kass 25% and Nyala 45%.
- 600 youth of both gender trained in SSB fabric 75% male and 25% female.
- PDM and survey are periodically reported to stakeholder.

1 - Needs

The huge displacement due to the conflict of Darfur during the 16 years ago resulted to many livelihood aspects, including long living in camps witch created dependency in external donation and very high expectation from humanitarian actors.In the last 3 years, many displaced people though about leaving the stressed life in camps to their villages of origin when security is expected. But still many others can not leave camps because of the uncertainty on what going on outside camps but resist in this undignified stay due to increased of family size and the growth of the children.Returnees in Marshang, Kass, and Nyala localities witness shortage of shelter materials and non food items.According to the need-based assessments in early March 2019 and September conducted by Inter Agency Mission in South Nyala surrounding frigile locations, Kass locality and Marshang. Finding of these assessments and the Sector Indicator allow GFO to address the needs of identified locality target. 73,373 individuals PiN are found in need of NFI kits and environment shelters IDPs,returnees and vulnerable host communities in with 8,500 are provided with NFIs in Nyala and Kass. 500 Most vulnerable Households including single headed female with more than 5 children, people with disability and older aged need shelters environmentally constructed in the three target areas.For more sustainability 600 youth are trained to fabric SSB and construction technique and get certificate of construction. Vocation mason training to 500 physically capacity to work to undertake the construction of new houses. To maintain effectively the project results, training of implementing will be organized for monitoring and reporting, also reorganization of participation of women in the leaders' committees will strengthen accountability of the project.

2 - Activities / Outputs

Activities undertaken by this project are cited as foloow: - Distribution of standard NFI kits to 63,000 in Kass, Marshang, and Nyala localities.- 8,500 returnees and host communities are provided by shelter kits in the following locations: - 18% in Kass locality (Singita,Tobofito, and Muray Jangue) - 82% in Marshang locality- Provision of 100 environmental completely build shelters to 500 most vulnerable returnees, single female headed household with more the 4 children 80%, 15% people with disability returnees and 5% of single old aged above 70 years returnees.- Training of 600 youth returnees 75% male and 25% female on Sustain Soil Block (SSB) for income generating activities to increase opportunity of sustainability in Nyala and Kass localities.- Vocational training for 100 masons (500 individuals), 30% female and 70% male in Kass and Marshang for construction cheaper and resisting house replacing the grass cave.- Reformation of 25 community groups with participation 40% of women to improve governance in the project intervention. - Reporting timely to the Stakeholders including the Sector lead through GMS and direct report.- Conduct Post Distribution Monitoring report after any distribution - Prepare and share progress reports.
