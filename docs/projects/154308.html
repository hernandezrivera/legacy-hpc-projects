Project Summary

The projects aims at strengthening the national Emergency preparedness and response capacities in line with the Inter agency efforts with a focus on the reinforcement of the government capacities and the contingency stocks. It includes training and technical support to government and other actors as well as implementing innovative information management tools.
The project will allow the government and humanitarian community sufficient capacity and resources to provide timely humanitarian assistance to 150,000 affected people (22,000 households) in the event of a crisis

Needs

As it is often the case in the countries of Sahel region, Chad is prone to natural disasters. In recent years flooding and drought have been recurring with high frequency, which is linked with high risks of cholera outbreaks. Moreover, recent the security situation in neighbouring countries continues generated movements of people to Chad, especially south from the Central African Republic (RCA), as well as internal displacement in the Lac area causing the displacement of over 120,000 people, demonstrates potential risks of such a large-scale displacement and hence a need for a timely and effective humanitarian response mechanism to provide life-saving assistance to those who are affected. To better prepare for and respond to emergencies, UNICEF has been working with the government to strengthen the national capacity of disaster management. In 2018, in partnership with OCHA and NGOs, UNICEF supported the General Directorate for Civil Protection who is the first responder to crises in developing contingency plans for the regions of Salamat and Logone occidental. UNICEF also supported the government with essential emergency stocks, including non-food items (NFIs) and water, sanitation, hygiene (WASH) kits that were pre-positioned in regions where high potential disaster risks were identified. While such efforts turned out to be effective in 2018, particularly during the flood response in, critical capacity gaps were recognized in the existing national preparedness and response mechanism. Additional efforts are, therefore, required to further strengthen the capacity of the government and national NGOs in both emergency preparedness and response. To this end, UNICEF, through its partnership with NGOs, can play a major role in accompanying national actors to leverage their technical expertise. Furthermore, to ensure the timely response, key administrative and logistical pre-arrangements need to be put in place. This includes scaling-up of contingency supplies that are strategically pre-positioned in disaster-prone regions and pre-agreements with the government and NGOs. Finally, an early-warning system needs to be reinforced by improving the communication chain across communities to central levels. To optimize the impact of collective responses by various actors, a better information management system should be developed to further improve the national coordination mechanism.

Activities or outputs
