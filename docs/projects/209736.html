Project Summary

• Needs:
After 12 years of hostilities and an ongoing economic crisis, people in Syria saw their situation worsen when a series of earthquakes struck northern Syria and Turkey starting on 6 February 2023. In 2024, 16.7 million people (8.4 million female, 8.3 million male) were assessed in need to humanitarian assistance throughout Syria. The multifaceted crisis in Syria creates ongoing cycles of vulnerability, disproportionately affecting those most in need, including individuals with disabilities. The situation is characterized by an increasing number of people resorting to unavailable basic services, making them more vulnerable and putting pressure on social cohesion.
• Intervention and implementation of activities:
To meet part of these needs, especially in the governorates of Aleppo, Hama-Salamiyah and Deir ez-Zor, work will be done on:
Health Sector: By providing primary health services such as medical consultations, tests, x-rays, and providing appropriate medications, as well as providing physical and speech therapy sessions, individual education, psychological support services, appropriate movement aids, lower prosthetic limbs, hearing aids and diapers for affected and disabled peoples.
Protection sector: through raising awareness about the dangers of early marriage and providing health services to pregnant women
• For the protection risk analysis:
- YBY has data protection policy dissemination and adherence to data protection principles.
- YBY has set a system for beneficiaries to provide complaints and feedback through note boxes and phone numbers.
- Gender analysis depending on previous projects data.
- Clear implementation strategy.
- YBY has a sufficient number of human resources trained to provide services with high quality to beneficiaries from all ages and both gender in a dignified manner and with zero discrimination of any kind.
• In PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) Strategy:
YBY staff have been trained in issues related to sexual exploitation and abuse, particularly the permanent physical, educational, and psychological and speech therapists. Thus, in the case of identifying any case in this field, the therapist follows the instructions that he was trained to deal with the case and inform the management.
YBY has implemented PSEA protection policies, circulated them to all employees and included them in employment contracts.
PSEA awareness sessions are conducted periodically. Moreover, PSEA posters were printed and published through YBY’s centers. YBY has set a policy states that all the newcomers have to do PSEA training before they sign their contract.
• Risks, assumptions and mitigation measures:
Competency of staff: YBY is able to establish a network of competent specialist and therapists to implement project activities.
Access to Der Ez Zor and Salamiya: YBY has equipped headquarters in both Der Ez zor and Salamiya in addition to its headquarter in Aleppo (Souq Al-Intaj and Hanano Centers)

0 - Project Summary (extended)

As shown in the humanitarian response plan, summary humanitarian needs overview and WHO’s Health Emergency appeal 2024 of the Syrian Arab Republic published in December 2023.Based on the databases, on disabled and affected persons, available at the Yadan BiYad (YBY) centers in its main headquarters in Aleppo and in its unrecognized branches in Salmiya and Deir ez-Zor, and after reviewing the guidance to appealing agencies how to submit a project to the 2024-2025 Syria HRP.We propose to submit a project to enhance our capabilities in the health and protection sectors for disabled and affected persons in the sites, which we are licensed to work to implement the following activities: 1. Providing medical consultations in the specialties (orthopedic, neurological, internal, endocrine, children) through Static Medical P Points (SMP) and Mobile Medical Teams (MMTs) in Aleppo, Salamiyah and Deir EzZor (the 3 sites).2. Providing the appropriate medicines in the above-mentioned specialties through SMPs and MMTs as well.3. Providing physical therapy, Speech therapy and intellectual development sessions through our centers equipped with all the necessary equipment, tools and materials in the 3 sites, which It has been equipped with previous WHO and SHF fundings since 2021.4. Providing suitable mobility aids for disabled persons in the 3 sites, through the manufacture of these aids in the factory that the association has equipped with previous funding from SHF since 2018.5. Providing diapers and personal hygiene supplements to disabled and immobile persons in the 3 sites.6. Providing lower prosthetic limbs to some beneficiaries in the 3 sites, where the beneficiaries from outside Aleppo will be transferred to Aleppo and hosted in the accommodation center that the association equipped with previous funding from SHF in 2021, which is located next to the prosthetic limbs center of the association in Aleppo, which It has been equipped with previous UNDP funding since 2020.7. Providing psychological support services to the disabled and affected persons and their families and providing some social activities to them.8. Providing social protection for pregnant women from the most vulnerable communities and providing health-physiological services.9. Providing social protection for girls under 18 and empowering little women who are at risk of child marriage or the ones who already got married.Awareness and prevention of the Covid-19 virus will be carried out by following preventive measures and adhering to the rules for preventing the transmission of the virus between workers and beneficiaries.Thus, the project will enhance the capabilities of YBY Association to provide its services in Aleppo, Salamiyah, Deir ez-Zor and its countryside, with a total number of beneficiaries estimated at / 27048 / within a period of 8 months.
