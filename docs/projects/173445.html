Project Summary

The main objectives and activities of the project:
1. Ensure that all victims of gender-based violence have access to legal services by providing legal aid.
2- Raising the community’s awareness through messages and awareness programs presented through local and state radio stations and adopting different awareness methods.
3- Improving the quality of services by training service providers for survivors of gender-based violence.
4- Training of legal assistants
Target areas:
The project targets six states as follows:
1) South Kordofan (Abu Jubaiha).
2) West Kordofan (El Geneina).
3) Khartoum (east of the Nile).
4) North Darfur (El Fasher).
5) South Darfur (Nyala South).
6) The Red Sea (Tokar-Sankat-Dordeb).
Total number of target groups:
43,450 people
It also works as a cooperating organization in the field of providing legal aid services to immigrants, refugees, women victims of violence and children, among a team of distinguished lawyers and human rights defenders. It also works on community legal awareness on the previous issues.
She has years of experience in the legal field of gender-based violence.
It is also a part of the monthly GBV Sub-Sector meetings and has a thorough knowledge of GBV.

1 - Needs

The six states of Southern Kordofan, West, North and South Darfur, Khartoum and the Red Sea in the specified areas witness weakness in services due to the nature of the regions, so we find that women in Darfur are exposed to many forms of violence. Medical support may often be available to them, but it is difficult for them to access legal aid services. The customs of these regions also prevail on this, as we find that most women are unable to obtain legal aid due to their families and fear of stigma.As for the regions of South Kordofan, specifically Abu Jubeiha .. Through our work in that region, we find that due to the nature of the region and its specificity, legal aid services are usually not available to everyone, not only because of their scarcity, but also because of the lack of knowledge of the women of those areas of their rights guaranteed to them Law.As for Khartoum and the challenge of the eastern Nile region, we find that there are many cases of violence, but there is a weakness in providing legal aid services, despite the women’s awareness of this, but the number of lawyers who provide this service is few to meet the region’s needs.Most of the needs of these areas comply as a limitation with what was previously mentioned in:1) Poor legal aid services2) A misunderstanding of the nature of females and the harmful customs and traditions practiced by society (female circumcision, early marriage, or child marriage).3) Address misunderstanding and awareness of rights and raise community awareness.4) Address the lack of good communication by service providers to ensure that victims receive integrated services.

2 - Activities/Outputs

1) Conduct public community awareness raising campaigns on GBV at locality level.Community awareness campaigns include the following types:1) Transmitting awareness messages via radio, rings and television.2) The theater.3) Seminars.It also includes topics that will be educated about:1) Raising awareness of the national legal framework for societies in a simple language that the target communities can understand.2) Awareness about the use of technology, social media, and the misuse relationship with the Information Crimes Law.3) Awareness of gender-based violence, its forms and types.The outputs of this activity are also represented in targeting 40,000 people of all kinds of awareness campaigns in the designated areas (South Kordofan, West and North and South Darfur, the Red Sea and Khartoum), provided that the nature of the target groups is taken into account when implementing these campaigns to ensure that all the targeted are understood and absorbed. 2) Provide different services to vulnerable women & girls including GBV survivors.Through this activity, Mutawinat aim to provide legal aid services to women and girls who are survivors of gender-based violence, provided that the main way out of this activity is to provide legal services to 1,200 survivors in the targeted areas (Abu Jubaiha, El Geneina, El Fasher, Nyala South, Dordeb, Sankat, Tokar and East Nile) through the branches of the cooperating office in these areas and the network of lawyers present there as well.This is to ensure that all survivors obtain their rights to access justice, provided that legal aid services are to provide legal advice and representation to all courts of all kinds, as well as work with regional and international mechanisms to establish women's rights g Ensure that laws are properly applied and implemented and work To change them through issues if needed.Then the cases are referred to receive other services they need through other service providers with whom they work.3) Conduct trainings on GBV for frontline GBV service providers.The outputs of this activity is to train 250 front-line service providers to address gender-based violence in the targeted areas by the end of the project period.This training targets lawyers, doctors, midwives, police investigative officers and prison officers as service providers who are in the front ranks and deal with cases and women directly.The training contains legal materials that interfere with doctors in their work, such as Form 8 and expert testimony, as well as the midwives' enlightenment about new texts related to the criminalization of female circumcision and the punishment related to this crime, as well as the officers on how to investigate with women survivors to ensure that they narrate all the facts of the accident without Fear and specifications of the places in which the investigation should take place in relation to prisons. Training is carried out on prison regulations related to the treatment of inmates, ensuring that they do not cause harm and protect them from violence.4) Conduct trainings for community based staff members, paralegals.The outputs of this activity are training of 2000 community employees and paralegals in all targeted areas by the end of the project period.Given the importance of the role of assistants and their ability to establish close contact between them and service providers, this activist seeks specifically for these reasons to train legal assistants on what gender-based violence is, how to treat it, and what are the legal frameworks that govern this issue.

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

This project aims, from its design and until its implementation, to ensure the generalization of protection and the attempt to implement and establish the principles of protection from gender-based violence specifically related to avoiding causing harm through activities related to raising the awareness of the target communities to ensure avoiding causing harm to women, such as refuting the practice of female circumcision and other Harmful practices.The project also aims to ensure access to legal aid and referral to other service providers with whom Mutawinat work and provide services for free, all of this to ensure that survivors receive the services they need.The project, through its design and even its implementation, aims to protect people from all forms of violence. Therefore, we find programs that take the awareness part by raising the awareness of communities through direct awareness sessions or messages that are broadcast on radio and television, as well as by providing legal aid services that are related to If a large number of people benefit from it, it guarantees legal protection from violence in many cases and varies from case to case, but basically it establishes the principle of legal protection from violence.Raising community awareness, providing legal services to them, training them, and engaging them in work, such as legal assistants, all of this guarantees universal protection and the rejection of violence in all its forms in the target communities.
