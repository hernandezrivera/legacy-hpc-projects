Project Summary

Sudan at large has been in war since 15 April 2024 to date. many parts have witnessed fierce city fighting as has been observed world-wide. The State of South Darfur and its capital city of Nyala was among the hard-hit locations in the country, probably second to Khartoum. This has resulted in severe humanitarian sufferings including loss of human souls, loss of private property, destruction of livelihood assets, and horrific damage to institutions and facilities responsible for provision of basic services.
Total insecurity forced international and LNGOs to flee Nyala and the State as well, augmenting the sufferings of the vulnerable groups including IDPs to face survival challenges. On the other hand a handful number of LNGOs staff opted to stay behind and use the limited opportunities for positive interventions. Great Family Organization (GFO) was among those who decided to stay and take initiatives including information sharing on the humanitarian plight of Nyala citizens and the area around.
GFO had managed to orchestrate meetings while fighting was going on to assess the needs of the people. The remaining staff of some LNGO had participated in the process. In some points this activity was culminated in a Humanitarian Call sent to some relevant international agencies. One o the challenges faced by this process, in addition to insecurity, was the poor capacities of those involved, and the difficulty to have an effective coordination amid lack of communication networks, and absence of accredited coordination mechanism.
After more than nine months of fighting the aforemention situation still prevails, though tranquility in Nyala has been gained. With the current strong interventions and mediation efforts exerted by the regional and international parties, hope for peace in Sudan has cropped up in the minds of the people., while sufferings are building up on daily bases due to absence of state functions and international humanitarian support.
GFO is putting in this proposal to fill in the direly needed coordination gap for the expected role of LNGO in the near future. This application is justified by the following factors:
1. GFO has been working in the humanitarian field in South Darfur for over 15 years continuously. As such it has gained vast amount of practical experience and recognition of many international donners. It has an experienced staff as well as committed consultants. Its office is in an accessible location with required working environment (though furniture was plundered by banditry acts).
2. GFO is targeting the most active 30 LNGOs using a rigorous criterion to upgrade their capacities while engaged for the expected humanitarian intervention.
3. GFO will act as the main hub for these LNGOs, through availing office space internet connection, a meeting hall, and provision of training. the coordinative role shall feed into the expected wider coordination to be assumed by the international community in the upcoming months.
4. Training to be provided by GFO will focus on the following aspects:
a. ability to write proposals for funding.
b. Humanitarian law, SGBV, Child Rights, and Human Rights, since most of these LNGOs are newly established while the experienced ones had run away with staff scattered all over the country and abroad.
c. Plan, design and implementation of surveys and field assessments to capture the humanitarian needs of the affected people.
d. Report writing.
5. Formation of geographical and thematic partnerships among LNGOs to facilitate good performance and proper delivery of services.
GFO has committed itself to undertake this responsibility to fill in this direly needed gap. However, this would require funding support amounting to $ 500000 (Five hundred thousand Dollars) within the year 2024. Details of this amount is shown in the budget slot of this format.
