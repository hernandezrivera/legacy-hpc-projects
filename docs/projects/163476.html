Project Summary

This projet aims at support vulnerable IDPs and host communities, Far North to have access to food through direct provision of cash/voucher and enhance emergency food production through complementary vegetable and crop seeds and livestock support.
The main target are men and women, form IDP and host communities households.

Besoins / Needs

In the Far North region 1,800,000 people are need , of which an estimate of 1,300,000 Cameroonians are food insecure, 128,000 of whom are severely food insecure . Food insecurity affects most IDPs, refugees, and vulnerable host communities in rural areas and female-headed households are among the most prone to it, as they are unable to access land because of insecurity, lack of finance and local customs. The precarious security situation continues to negatively affect people. This has contributed to IDPs and poor households in urban areas remaining in Crisis (IPC Phase 3). A majority of households reported not having adequate resources to feed their families. Equally, households expressed difficulty in meeting water needs and accessing healthcare and education. The largest barrier to accessing services is cost or lack of financial resources. A large proportion of displaced and returnee households are paying rent, despite having limited access to livelihoods, regular work or other sources of income, putting further stress on their already limited resources. Markets are functioning and vendors are able to keep their shops open and stocked, suggesting market-based responses should be prioritized.
