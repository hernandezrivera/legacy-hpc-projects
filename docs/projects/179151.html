Project Summary

-Many displaced camps are exposed every winter to the risk of floods, which leads to the destruction of tents and the loss of the property of the displaced, in addition to many deaths, especially since these tents do not protect the displaced from the summer heat or the cold of winter, and the long-term stay of the displaced in them.
-The proposed project will secure decent and safe housing for displaced families whose camps are exposed to floods every winter and it will be a temporary alternative to the tent and will secure their privacy as well as provide them with protection from winter cold and summer heat with its own insulation properties, as the walls are 15 cm thick in addition to the thickness of the internal and external plaster, and the roof is made of wood. Which is considered a good insulator and there is an insulating material of bitumen on the wood to protect the alum from the influence of weather factors such as humidity, in addition to that this housing provides the displaced with a better health status in terms of hygiene and protection from diseases, and the provision of appropriate education for children. The idea of the project started from the need to find temporary and low-cost housing as an alternative to the tent, which has become an insufficient solution for the example of the Syrian case, which is what we see from the periodic reports from the shelter sector about the floods and torrents that many camps are exposed to every winter, which can be found in the reports of the sector of the shelter.
-Engineering expertise seeks to solve this problem through a proposal that aims to replace flood-affected tents with suitable temporary housing
-We aim from this project to implement a model that may be an important and economical alternative to tents as we plan to construct 100 typical housing units with attached service facilities.
-Project elements: After conducting the necessary hydrological survey and studies, an infrastructure will be established consisting of the following:
- Settlement work for the work site.
-Establishing drinking water networks
-Establishing sanitation networks
-Main roads
-Rainwater drainage channels: a well with a high reservoir. A fence for each project
-Housing units:
-The residential unit consists of two separate rooms (a men's bedroom -and a women's bedroom),a living room, in addition to a kitchen and a bathroom, with an average area of42m2.
-Building specifications for the residential unit:
-Foundations: Concrete with stone:40%stone and 60% concrete.
-Walls: concrete block measuring15 x 20 x 40cm
-The roof: simple main and secondary bridges made of iron or woods which can be dismantled and installed, and they are topped by wooden panels covered with an insulating layer.
-One-sided internal and external plaster
-The floor:200kg/m3concrete
-Bathroom floor:ceramic
-Bathroom walls:1.5meters high ceramic
- Installing a wash basin
-Iron doors and windows.
-Water tank: from zinc, 1.25 mm thick,(1 m3 )for each housing unit with the required connections.
AHF organization seeks to provide support and to response to 1500 conflict affected IDP HHs in both in AL Bab camps Al-Sharqiya camp / Hilal market camp / Al-Bata camp / Baylasan camp / Al-Azraq camp throughout providing stoves, and sufficient heating fuel for 90 day as per 4 liters for the day stove and 3000 clothes .
Each household will receive stove and diesel for 3 months based on 4 liter per day, Total 360 liter and Two clothes baskets
The distribution will be in kind at the main distribution points and the quality of the diesel will be checked before distribution, however, the fuel kits will be delivered to the elderly , people with special needs, and the people who are unable to reach the distribution point for any reason directly to their house (door to door) that will decrease the risk of COVID 19 on the elderly people.
