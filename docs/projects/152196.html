Project Summary

This project is aimed at improving and developing the capacity of social workers to mitigate gender base violence GBV in communities.
The purpose is to promote the resilience of social workers to contributing to reducing frequency of GBV in the society.
The target consist of mainly social workers, community as well as community leaders in Jere LGA.

Needs

It is a common fact that the society is witnessing the occurrences of gender base violence on daily basis. The consequences are enormous and the need to address this problem is key to the wellbeing of the family in particular and the society in general.Therefore, the project seeks to develop the capacity of social workers to be on the driving seat by empowering them with the necessary tools and skills.The role of the social worker is key element to this response because they are part of the community and knows the community better.

Activities

We shall first and foremost engage in a familiarization visit to all stake holders to get acquaintances with relevant stake holders.The targeted social workers will be reorganized for proper training on a higher scale.It is expected that case of all GBV matters are recorded accordingly. Issues that are unresolved would be forwarded to the appropriate channels to adopt other measures.A weekly summery of all activities are to be submitted to our secretariat for onward delivery to be followed by monthly presentation at forum level.
