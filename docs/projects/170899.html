Project Summary

According to the somalia_2020_post_gu_briefing_for_fsc_partners_-_7_oct_2020, the 2020 Gu season cereal production in southern Somalia is estimated at 74000tons, including 11500 tons of off-season harvest expected late Sep/Oct2020.The 2020 Gu harvest in southern Somalia is 40 percent lower than the long-term average for 1995-2019 due to locust invasion, drought successive-severe flooding, erratic rainfall and a prolonged dry spell and insecurity/conflict. There is low milk and meat access.
Floods during the 2020 Gu season have destroyed farm land, planted crops, and population displacement, leading to a significant decline in crop harvest, loss of agricultural employment opportunities. Covid 19 Pandemic has worsened the socio-economic food and livelihood security in Beledweyne and Matabaan Districts among minority clans and Persons with Disabilities.
The response will cover Beltweeyne (5,004 HHs) 834 IDP and Host Community, 834 Minorities, 834 Persons with Disabilities, 834 poorest families and elderly persons, 834 Women and Girls, 834 men and Boys in Hiraan :Beledweyne and Mataban for conditional cash in both Districts equally sharing the number.
GHF calls for conditional and unconditional monetary approach for food and livelihood resilience enhancement. Unconditional cash transfer is meant for the elderly, sick, persons with Disabilities and some women such as Pregnant and Lactating mothers. We include Community Disaster preparedness Committee composing of women and men (20 Females and 15 men) including Persons with Disabilities.
The project targets the persons severely affected by droughts and conflicts across the populations groups of IDPS, Rural Pastoralist, Agro-pastoralist and fishing in the target Districts. The specific activities will largely include cash based interventions that will target emergency crisis affected households of above IPC 3 level. The planned cash transfers which will include both conditional and unconditional transfers aimed to increase households’ purchasing power and improve access to food and basic need based on the calculated Minimum Expenditure Basket: 40 USDs.
The project is also designed to main stream protection principles and to promote access, safety and dignity for all beneficiaries and in particular women and girls from IDP camps and others who have faced increased risk of SGBV since the outbreak of COVID-19 pandemic.
GHF employs Community Based Targeting (CBT) and sensitisation selection criteria including women headed households, persons from minority clans, households with elderly and disable individuals and large size poor families and IDP households, rural and fishing households with none or minimal sources of income.
Project consists of participatory programming to increase acceptance of cash based assistance by local communities and authorities; while insisting on harmonized programmatic
quality approaches, incorporating the Guidelines for Cash Programming. All entail sensitization and community targeting ensuring involvement of the elderly, persons with disabilities, women, non-dominant clans….
The project entails both conditional and unconditional cash interventions. GHF takes 2000 HHs for Conditional cash based intervention on repairing livelihood assets such as roads, markets and water systems such as boreholes and wells.

Explain how the project design considered market functionality ?

In the market risk analysis under the Cash based based assistance, the risk is reduced and transferred to the money transfer-market.We considered the following:• Can we locate a safe environment in the area which can be used for cash distributions without further risking the already vulnerable populations?• Involvement of the local authorities, national and local Cash working groups in enhancing the project efficacy. The market analysis confirms loss of purchasing power of traders and end-consumers.The target Districts have efficient markets for commodities and also cash transfer systems that are supportive of market functionality. The markets have the available basic commodities including food from imported as well as local sources.
