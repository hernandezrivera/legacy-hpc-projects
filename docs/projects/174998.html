1 - Needs

Saving Lives Together (SLT) will act as an enabler to promote early action and response provide timely humanitarian assistance and access to people in need and enhance response to time critical requirements. Actors must work together to reduce risks outside of times of crisis, including by strengthening capacity and capability at local levels, creating higher degrees of interoperability between diverse actors. In 2021, in a changing context including the UNAMID drawdown, it is essential to invest in this capacity in Sudan. Not enough funding goes to risk management activities, and an additional effort is required in 2021 to ensure there is a shared assessment of crisis risk and conflict.

2 - Activities/Outputs

Saving Lives Together (SLT) aims at enhancing UN and NGO security collaboration in the field. Under the SLT framework, the humanitarian community contributes to the collection, analysis and dissemination of critical security and safety information. Information and analysis are made available to humanitarian security managers in the interest of our mutual safety and all decisions made, on the basis of, or with consideration to, such information remains the responsibility of their respective organisations. The STL framework was approved by the Steering Group on Security (SGS) stablished by the IASC in December 2008. The main objectives are: Reporting and liaison• Convening broad-based forums for field security collaboration and information sharing, including NGO/IO engagement with the UN Security Management Team;• Establishing and maintaining regular contacts with Security officials of UN Agencies, Funds from Programmes and Security Specialists for NGOs, if present in area, to share security information pertaining to humanitarian staff safety and security;• Establishing and maintain contacts with national or local security entities to obtain the best security information for safety and security of UN staff, assets and operations. Security Briefings• Ensuring that all operational humanitarian partners obtain regular security briefings related to the latest security situation in the country and area of their deployment;• Seeking Adherence to Common Humanitarian Ground Rules (CHGR);• Collaborating and consulting on the development and delivery of contextually based security trainings;• Ensuring that security specialists working for humanitarian NGOs are fully briefed on the latest development related to security situation and information is regularly shared as per adopted policy “Saving Life Together”;• On request conduct ad-hoc security briefing for incoming humanitarian staff on temporary missions or short assignment to the field;• Identifying minimum security standards based on agency or organisation specific Security Risk Assessment (SRA) Model; Coordination tasks and information sharing• Facilitating Inter-Agency Emergency Telecommunications;• Immediately report all security related incidents involving UN staff and eligible dependants to the Area Security Coordinator (ASC);• Assist UN OCHA in conducting missions and/or the Inter-Agency Response Missions to the field after the successful Security Risk Assessments • Attend Humanitarian Country Team (HCT) meetings, if required.• Provide support to humanitarian organisation under “Saving Lives Together” policy.
