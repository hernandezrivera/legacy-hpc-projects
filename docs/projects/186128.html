Project Summary

The crisis affecting the Northwest and Southwest regions of Cameroon has markedly disrupted the health system in these regions. This has impacted the health status of the affected populations by making it difficult to access essential health services and weakened the epidemiological surveillance system which has increased overall mortality and the likelihood of epidemics in these areas.
The purpose of this project will be to ensure access to and delivery of quality primary health care services through restoration and expansion of life-saving health care services to affected population, with particular focus on the most vulnerable group. This will be achieved through the free delivery of health care services with the provision of medical care via a voucher mechanism and a mobile strategy through the deployment of 'a mobile clinic where health facilities are non-functional. Also, the project envisages to provide support to the national health system in sensitization,IPC, training and protection of staff; and in case management, of cholera and Covid-19.

1 - Besoins / Needs

Cameroon is experiencing several security crises. In the North West regions (NW) and South West (SW), the situation remains volatile. The "Non-State Armed Groups" (NSAG) are increasing attacks against the government and its symbols and against the populations and suspected sympathizers. The central government's response is the deployment of a repressive military apparatus. A deployment which has led to an intensification of clashes with consequences for civilians at the start of 2021, the crisis went beyond the limits of the two English-speaking regions with an attack in the West region, the second of its kind.The persistence of insecurity threatens local populations, exposes them to risks affecting their physical integrity and leads to population movements, disruptions and losses of their goods and livelihoods, thus reducing their access to health care and their ability to meet their health costs. Proposed activities:1. Health Vouchers to offer health care to affected populations including paediatric and EPI services2. Deployment of mobile clinics; a mobile clinic will be set up to reach and provide primary health care to the populations who no longer have access to it due to insecurity. It will offer preventive and curative outpatient care, as well as the SRH package as well as psychosocial support. The MBCs will also ensure the referral of complicated cases to the district health structures3. Support for the response to the Cholera and COVID-19 epidemic; DEMTOU will support the health system in the response to Cholera and COVID-19. This support will focus on community awareness and education activities on Cholera and COVID-19; on IPC through the implementation of barrier measures, staff training and the provision of PPE. All community and awareness-raising activities will take place in compliance with barrier and social distancing measures. In the mobile clinic, teams will be equipped with PPE, hand washing and disinfection points will be set up and social distancing measures and awareness sessions will also be observed4. Mental Health Support; The integration of the psychosocial dimension into medical activities is necessary to ensure that the mental health needs of populations are taken care of, especially those affected by conflicts. A psychologist positioned on the project will train the different teams at different operational levels for better integration of mental health, take charge of specific situations and organize the referral circuit for severe cases. Results:1. The population benefit from a timely access to essential health care 2. The provision of primary health care to the populations with difficulties of access to care structures, through the establishment of a mobile clinic 3. Supporting the response to the Cholera and COVID-19 epidemic through infection prevention and control (IPC), staff training
