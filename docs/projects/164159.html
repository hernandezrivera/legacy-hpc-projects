Project Summary

The project aims at providing the necessary WASH services to the internally displaced people residing in camps. These services include providing safe drinking water, provision of sanitation items, and improve the sanitation services including rehabilitation of existing facilities and construction of additional facilities according to need. Additionally, solid waste management services will be provided in the targeted camps.

4 - Needs

Due to recent hostilities in North-West Syria, tens of thousands of civilians have fled from in southern Idleb governorate, moving further north due to renewed intensive aerial bombardment. Displacement happening in winter months is further exacerbating the vulnerability of people in need. Many of those who fled are in urgent need of humanitarian support. In light of the high population density and the dependence of civilians on alternative sources such as water trucking, which are absolutely unsafe due to the unreliability of water from wells transported by tanks, new concerns arise from the spread of diseases due to the lack of water, especially among children.The most urgent need currently is the safe drinking water, in addition to personal hygiene items, moreover, an emerging need for sanitation facilities (latrines) due to the escalating numbers of IDPs.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk1: The extreme location of the camps in rugged areas impedes the arrival of trucks and materials to carry out activities. low.Mitigating Measure: Local suppliers of trucks and contractors have the appropriate vehicles to access and execute the required works and activities.Risk 2: Ownership of the land which the camps are based and allowed to carry out activities. low.Mitigation measure: All papers and permissions on matters related to the camp's land are available at the camp administration in the areaRisk 3: Movements of water trucks in the congested, camp-like settings, low.Mitigating Measure: Consulting community regarding the location of distribution points, delivery routes and its schedule; Avoiding location of water points in narrow streets, congested areas; Establishing clear routes and schedule for water trucking that use wider access routes and avoiding school areas during the time children travel to/from school.Risk 4: Movements of desludging trucks in the congested, camp-like settings; Reliability of services.Mitigating Measure: Consulting community regarding desludging services, truck routes and schedule; Establishing clear routes and schedule for desludging trucks that use wider access routes and avoiding school areas during the time children travel to/from school.Risk 5: Health risk associated with garbage removal.Mitigating Measure: Ensuring staff, partners and contractors involved in SWM activities wear appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) and follow established safety regulations.
