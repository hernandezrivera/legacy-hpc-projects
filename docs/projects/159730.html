Project Summary

The project is aimed at increasing sustainable access to WASH services and further strengthen resilience of the most vulnerable 10000 persons (4160 women, 3840 men, 1000 boys and 1000 girls) in Abudwak District, Galgadud Region, Somalia by meeting their urgent as well as long term WASH needs in the project targeted villages that are yet to recover from impacts of past successive seasons of below normal rains as well as impacts of cumulative seasons of below average rains experienced across most parts of Somalia during the 2018 Deyr (October-December), that was preceded by very harsh weather conditions during the dry 2019 Jilaal (January–March) season and the poor performance of the 2019 Gu (April-June)that started very late, worsened by devastating 2016/17 drought season.

Distribution of 1500 hygiene kits to 1500 HH in Abdudwak4.Triggering and follow up on CLTs for 1000 families/6000 persons (women 2496, men 2304, boys 600 and girls 600 in Abudwak5. Construction of 70 gender sensitive(for men and women) new latrines fitted with hand washing facilities and lockable doors and are lighted at night in IDP camps in Abudwak6.Conducting comprehensive hygiene promotions in schools, nutrition and health centers as well as the in villages and IDP camps in Abudwak through house house visits, demonstration, media as well using IEC materials translated in Somali Language for the targeted beneficiaries
