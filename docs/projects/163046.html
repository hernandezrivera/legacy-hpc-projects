Project Summary

Zimbabwe is a country of origin, transit and destination for Victims of Trafficking (VoTs). In a context of general vulnerability that Zimbabwe is currently in, there are often factors that leave families with no viable alternative for survival other than situations that could be defined as exploitation and trafficking in national and international law. In addition, experience shows that in the aftermath of natural disasters such as Cyclone Idai, rates of irregular migration and trafficking rise. In this context, the project seeks to address knowledge gaps on Trafficking in Persons (TiPs) through awareness raising and community sensitisation sessions targeting crisis affected populations. The project also seeks to build the capacity of key protection actors to monitor trafficking trends, identify victims of trafficking, and respond to the needs of victims including referring them to appropriate service providers. The capacity building component will include specific modules on how to care for victims of human trafficking with disabilities, identifying their unique type of need and strengthening the assistance that should be put in place by care givers. The project will be implemented in the provinces of Harare (Epworth, Harare Rural, Harare Urban), Manicaland (Chimanimani, Chipinge Rural, Mutare Urban, Mutare Rural), Mashonaland Central (Mbire), Mashonaland East (Mudzi, Rwua), Mashonaland West (Kadoma), Matebeleland South (Beitbridge Urban, Beitbridge Rural) and Midlands (Chirumhanzu, Gokwe North, Gokwe South, Gokwe Town, Gweru Rural, Kwekwe, Kwekwe Rural, Mberengwa, Redcliff, Shurugwi Rural).
