Project Summary

Provision of education services (including back-to-school campaigns, TLS [temporary learning spaces], ECCD [Early Childhood Care and Development] centers, home-based learning, distribution of materials, NFE [non-formal education] programming (remedial, catch-up, ALP [accelerated learning program], BLN [basic literacy and numeracy], ELM [Early Literacy and Numeracy]); as well as running costs and winterization support; teacher stipends, capacity building, and PTA establishment; School safety and adaptations (incl. safety plans, rehabilitation, safety assessments, distribution of key, life-saving messages); PSS [psychosocial support] and SEL [socio emotional learning], preventing violence in schools and communities, awareness raising to community and local actors; Support for teachers including professional development, integration of CP and SEL, safe identifications and referrals of cases of violence, teacher learning circles, coaching and mentoring, positive discipline and referral; wellbeing support; awareness raising for children on how to identify and respond to violence; in-service training on gender responsiveness and SNAP [student needs action pack for inclusive education], CPiE [child protection in emergencies], PFA [psychological first aid]; Training for PTAs to support school improvement/safety, gender equality, GBV/MHPSS awareness raising sessions, CPiE, PFA etc.; provision of parenting programmes and peer-to-peer support; Training for children and teachers on disaster risk and emergency response with a focus on services required by different groups, including update of school safety plans, school drills/evacuation, service mapping for referrals; Contingency planning at school, community and system levels with a focus on needs of different groups including students with disabilities; and strengthening of data management systems including evidence building; Disaster-resistant school design (i.e., Awareness raising on climate change).
