Project Summary

Medair will continue multi sectoral intervention in its proposed catchment areas of Leer, Pibor and multistate under the Emergency Response Team (ERT). The total targets for 2024 based on Medair’s mapped catchment populations and at least 60% coverage in all areas will be 3952SAM admissions(Pibor 484, Leer 1380 ,ERT 2088) ,and 6225U5 MAM(ERT 2369, Leer 3856) and 4434PLWs(ERT 2339 Leer 2095) 26,679 under-fives screened(Pibor 1600,Leer 11,079,ERT 14,000) ,10,673PLWs screened(Leer4193, ERT 6840)) , and 21,126 caregivers reached with MIYCN messaging(Pibor 1600, Leer 11079,ERT 14000)) .Medair will continue to integrate MIYCN and IYCF at both community and health and nutrition site levels and will support VAS in Medair’s catchment areas when no other actors are conducting. The proposed intervention in the plan aligns with the nutrition cluster strategic and sector objectives 2024 and contribute to increase equitable access and utilization of quality preventative, lifesaving, early detection and treatment of malnutrition and program monitoring, analysis and utilization of nutrition information. According to the HNO 2023 the GAM rates in 31 states including Jonglei, Unity where Medair operates, have a GAM prevalence above the emergency threshold of 15 per cent while IPC 2023 estimated 72 percent of the acute malnutrition burden concentrated in five states including Jonglei and Unity State. The FSNMS round 28 estimates GAM rate in Leer County as 19.2% (SAM 6.1%) .Medair is the nutrition main nutrition actor in Leer County covering a catchment population of 77,663 with 10 sites and 1 stabilisation centre in Leer town. Medair has a plan to expand its nutrition program to cover the whole county by increasing its sites to 19 from January 2024. Medair is continuing multisectoral programming,community health, nutritionCMAM and MIYCN via Care Groups (CG) WASH, and MHPSS to address some of the underlying factors.In the recent 2023 IPC classification (HNO 2023) Pibor was classified as IPC 4 with pockets of IPC level 5, “famine likely” with extremely critical levels of acute malnutrition. The GAM rates in Jonglei state remain very high with a prevalence above the emergency threshold of 15 percent. In 2024 nutrition cluster prioritized Pibor as one of the neediest areas with no significant improvement from last year's situation. Medair continues to the operate the Stabilization Centre in Pibor town working closely with the main nutrition partner For Afrika operating the OTP and TFSP nutrition sites in Pibor. For Afrika is the main health and nutrition partner in GPAA and plans to continue OTP and TSFP services. To avoid duplication, Medair continue to partner and coordinate activities with For Afrika to manage all the coexisting illness referrals. As part of Medair’s health and WASH programming Medair will continue to support and expand the community CG network which will cover MIYCN and other public health topics as well as ensure Mother’s MUAC is established and well linked to For Afrika services through referrals. Health facilities will also conduct nutritional screening and referral of U5 and PLWs as part of BPHNS. As no actor is addressing at risk infants <6 mo, Medair will continue integrating MAMI within the health services at the 2 PHCUs( Gumuruk and Lekuangole est population of 55,126(Feb 2021 OCV Campaign)) , Pibor Hospital and also at the SC in Pibor town with the CHDs approval.Medair will continue supporting minimum WASH packages and child friendly spaces and early child development activities will also be promoted at the nutrition sites. ERT continues to respond to emergency levels of acute malnutrition by establishing CMAM services to address the most lifesaving needs, in areas with high GAM and low program coverage. The RRTF in collaboration with the NC gives input on priority areas and intervention modalities in scenarios characterized by agreed triggers where other actors are absent or unable to fully intervene.

1. Needs

All Medair’s projected intervention sites are located in states classified as high priority areas by nutrition cluster with emergency levels of GAM. In Leer, Medair is the main nutrition partner and currently runs nutrition services in 11 nutrition sites. From January 2024, Medair plans to run a total of 19 sites after handover of 8 sites from Nile Hope. Medair was selected by UNICEF and WFP as the only nutrition partner to implement OTP/TFSP in Leer County with the estimated population of 77,663. The Leer population has experienced recurrent displacement and disruption of services due to flooding and repeated waves of conflict. In the recent FSNMS+ findings reported a GAM rate of 19.2% (SAM 6.1%) among children 6-59 months in Leer County. The FSNMS round 28 estimates GAM rate in Leer County as 19.2% (SAM 6.1%). According to the latest IPC report 2023, the nutrition situation in Leer Couty is in “critical” situation (IPC Acute Malnutrition 4) and “crisis” (IPC Acute Food Insecurity 3) respectively. It is expected to remain in IPC 4 throughout the lean season, April to July 2024. Medair continues to provide nutrition, WASH, community health, and mental health and psychosocial support (MHPSS). MIYCN is integrated at nutrition sites and via the Care Group network. Medair led household surveys as in other locations show a need for improved complementary feeding and dietary diversity in IYCF. Medair will continue providing lifesaving services in nutrition, health, WASH and MHPSS in up to 19 sites. Medair will support a SMART survey to measure GAM rates this year. Pibor: Pibor has experienced multiple emergency humanitarian situations due to floods and intercommunal violence for the past two years with significant impact on health and nutrition services. Throughout Jonglei State, the insufficient number of health facilities and staff is further aggravated by limited road access during rainy season, recurrent flooding, drought and outbreaks, and continued violence. The HNO 2023 report classified Greater Pibor Administrative Area as IPC 4 with pockets of IPC 5, “famine likely”. There are high levels of morbidity and mortality in children U5, low vaccination coverage, poor IYCF and health seeking behaviors. As noted above For Afrika is the main health and nutrition partner and providing nutrition services in the area. Medair is providing multi sectoral services--health, WASH and integrated nutrition in the communities around the supported Gumuruk and Lekuangole PHCUs while addressing the needs of at risk <6 months children linked to RH services. Medair is also running the SC in Pibor town to treat SAM cases with medical complications and support a referral network with For Afrika. Medair has also set up a MAMI corner at the Hospital in Pibor Town . The Emergency Response Team (ERT) will usually be deployed for a period of up to 3 months to respond to nutritional emergencies through helping integrate nutrition services in health facilities, building nutrition capacity of local partners or scaling up mobile responses in remote areas with nutrition service coverage gaps. Covering identified needs after rapid assessments. With the South Sudan crisis and influx of returnees and refugees being experienced in most of the counties Medair intends to deploy the ERT teams to provide health, WASH and nutrition services in these affected areas working alongside the main partner for 3 months before handover.

2. Project activities

Infant Young Child Feeding and Behaviour ChangeIn Leer, and Pibor, Medair will use the National MIYCN Guidelines to govern the dissemination of IYCF messaging. This will be integrated into each level of contact beneficiaries have with staff throughout the CMAM programmes as well as Care Groups to provide sufficient opportunities for knowledge acquisition and to promote behaviour change. Child friendly spaces will be used as an opportunity to support mothers, in particular in the violence-affected areas of Leer, and Pibor. Where MAMI is supported at health facilities rapid IYCF assessments will be conducted on mother-infant pairs and more vulnerable dyads will have more intensive IYCF assessment and multisectoral support as needed from MAMI breastfeeding counsellors. Management of Acute MalnutritionMedair proposes to continue treating children under 5 years for SAM and MAM, through the provision of the CMAM programme (static, mobile and outreach sites) in up to 45 locations including up to 19 in Leer to including 1 SC, 1 SC in Pibor, and 25 in ERT. Medair will use the 2016 National Ministry of Health approved CMAM Guidelines to govern implementation of the Nutrition Programme. As per standard protocol, all children with complicated SAM will be referred to an SC run by Medair (i.e. Leer and Pibor) or another implementing agency. OTP and TSFP will either be established alongside health activities being implemented by other actors (as is the case across some sites in Leer) or will consist of screening in supported health facilities and referring U5 children and PLWs to the nutritional actors in the area (Lekuangole and Gumuruk) whilst receiving malnourished children that require coexisting illness treatment at the PHCUs. The community mobilisation activities will rely on the development of networks of Community Nutrition Volunteers/Workers (CNVs and CNWs) in Leer and ERT as well as Care Groups in Pibor, and Leer. Preventative measures to mitigate the risk of developing acute malnutrition will be put in place and will include activities of behaviour change communication targeting maternal, infant and young child feeding practices (noted above), participation in the National Immunization Days (NIDs), as well as periodic campaigns of mass distribution of Vitamin A (children aged 6-59 months) and deworming with albendazole (children aged 12-59 months) when other actors are not conducting.IntegrationAs explained above, the minimal WASH package, health package (RDT and measles vaccination; provision of amoxicillin, and deworming according to the CMAM protocol, management of co-existing illnesses; referral to the stabilisation centre); FSL linkage and ECD activities will be implemented in the patient’s best interest and according to the directives of the South Sudan Nutrition Cluster.The Emergency Response Team (ERT) will aim to provide the full CMAM package in all emergency interventions as needed. The actual response will depend on the needs and gaps identified in the Needs Assessment. Emergency nutrition interventions will be rolled out in the following cluster rapid response phased model depending on security and accessibility of target areas:•Phase 1: High insecurity location with limited accessibility, day trips: MUAC screening at a central location and BP5 distribution •Phase 2: High GAM/immediate need to respond, limited access and absence /lack of capacity of nutrition actor: MUAC screening, provision of 2-week ration for MAM and SAM with deworming, key MIYCN/HHP messaging and follow-up visits as security permits•Phase 3: Secure location with access and absence /lack of capacity of nutrition actor: establish full CMAM programme, identify or capacity building of long-term partners for handover
