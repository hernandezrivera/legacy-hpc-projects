Project Summary

Title: Improving the protection of women and girls affected by the conflict in the North-West Regions of Cameroon As a result of the conflict in the NW and SW regions of Cameroon, more than 800,000 people have been displaced from their homes amongst them women and girls. The out break of the COVID-19 pandemic in the region, is a crisis within a crisis, because it has only exacerbated the sufferings of the vulnerable especially women and girls. The COVID-19 measures which include confinement, has also aggravated existing GBV risks for most women and girls who are obliged to stay indoors with their abusers or potential abusers without access to their usual community support. Further, given that most women and girls are the main bread winners for their families since a majority of men and youths have left the communities they are unable to respect confinements because they will still go out and take the risk to fend for their families. Women are also primary health care providers for their family members thus if they don’t prioritize safety measures they will be putting themselves at risk if they have ro care for family members infected by COVID-19. All these, expose women to COVID-19 more that men. As an effect of the challenges they might go through, as a result of the GBV trauma, COVID-19 confinement measures and the general fear of instability due to the ongoing conflict, most of them will be exposed to mental health and psychosocial issues which has a direct effect on their overall health and well being. Thus the intervention has as objective to respond to the needs of women and girls amidst COVID-19 in the conflict zone of the NWR of Cameroon through economic support, awareness raising on GBV, COVID-19 and raising awareness on how and where to access basic services including clinical management of rape, MHPSS, safe shelter to receive support and care among others. The project will take into consideration measures to prevent the spread of COVID-19 by ensuring social distancing, engaging community based protection members to carry out services/interventions in large scale, providing mask to community members and also putting up hand washing stations and reenforcing hand washing measures in the community.
