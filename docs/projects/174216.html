Project Summary

The proposed project for the duration of 7 months intends to provide support to vulnerable communities of 20 villages in 02 UCs (1. Chalghezi 2. Saddar of district Chaghi Balochistan. As per WFP Vulnerability Assessment & Mapping (VAM) study in Dist. Chaghi these both Union Councils were prioritized due to higher vulnerability to food insecurity. Muslim hands also conducted a need assessment exercise in area and assessment findings indicate that these areas are much neglected and need immediate support in terms of food security. The total funding solicited for the project is USD 481275.90 while the contribution of Muslim Hands for the project from its core funding is USD 47,200. Total beneficiaries’ families will be 800 through different interventions and 20300 individuals. The proposed project aligns directly with VAM findings and Food Security and Livestock cluster/ working Groups districts enabling them to meet their emergency needs and hence protecting them from related risks of food security. The need assessment conducted by Muslim Hands revealed alarming situation of food security in Chaghi due to poor situation of livelihood and agriculture like inappropriate cropping patterns and lack of raining and secondly locust attack during March to July 2020. The socioeconomic impacts for these circumstances are vivid on incomes, nutrition and livelihood of the population especially small land holder farmers of the district. The proposed interventions are in response of recent pandemic COVID-19 in Balochistan. Most of the affected communities are suffering due to unavailability of basic needs of food and livelihoods. Beneficiary families are small land holder farmers, women headed households, poor and needy who lost their income and livelihoods sources.
The proposed project will address the food security issue among the affected communities within three major interventions including unconditional food/cash grants, conditional cash grant distribution and in-kind support for crop production and provision of livestock packages. These activities will ultimately compliment the cluster priorities in this regard. The proposed interventions will contribute to address the basic food needs of target community and it will be implemented through integrated approach. To achieve sustainability of the intervention and linking relief, rehabilitation and development, the actions will also support in building the capacity of the affected communities in operations and maintenance and improving their knowledge base for exercising best practices at communal and household level. Muslim Hands would invest in the development systems that are local community and government owned as relevant to address long term sustainability.
The proposed project would be executed with close coordination of Food Security and Livelihood (FSL) working group nominated by PDMA, local administration, agriculture, livestock and health department at district level as well as provincial level, which would complement their efforts and services. Assuming health & wellbeing benefits certain behaviors; hand washing practices with soap at critical times, utilization of good practices regarding livestock and farming will be measured for reporting. Additionally, M&E focus on; Improve/install/repair/rehab of existing food security systems to create more durable sources and options at community & institutional level, Improve/increase water storage at community, HH & institutional level, M&E focus on set indicators in Log Frame, participatory monitoring & evaluation through stakeholders i.e., PHED, Local Administration & village committees from concern community i.e., pre-KAP to post-KAP.

1- Project Activities

Activity 1: Households will receive cash or food transfers in exchange for participating in livelihoods-enhancing activities, such as building/ rehabilitation of productive assets or participation in trainings (in particular for women). These cash/ food transfers will help households meet their immediate food needs, while the livelihoods activities will support them to recover and build resilience to future shocks on the medium/ long term.150 household will be facilitated through unconditional cash grant These cash transfers will help households meet their immediate food needs on immediate basis. Activity 2: Provision of cash+ packages (a combination of cash and in-kind crop support packages) to extremely vulnerable households who lack financial resources to cultivate their land. These packages will include cash transfers, seed and fertilizer packages and climate smart agriculture FFS. 300 Household will receive Conditional Grant (CFW) in 02 targeted UCs of District Chagai.Activity 3: Households will be provided with certified high yielding vegetable seeds, basic gardening tools and Farmer Field School (FFS) support over a 12-week period to assistance households in increasing the production of a diversified range of vegetable products. 50 Women will be trained on Kitchen gardening, Certified High yield vegetable seed will be provided.Activity 4: Fodder seed and fertilizer will be distributed to vulnerable small holder farmers, particularly farmers who have lost crops early in the season to locust damage or other natural shocks. Good Quality Fodder seed and Fertilizers will be provided to 300 small farmers families.Activity 5: Vulnerable households with limited or no access to dairy products will be provided with a set of dairy goats and FFS training and support to increase household access to dairy products, food safety, increase dietary diversity and increased household earnings. Female targeted activity. Livelihood package will be provided to 300 identified families.Activity 6: Vaccinations for small ruminants against contagious diseases. Deworming to protect livestock health and improve productivity.20 VETs workers Received Training on livestock management and vaccination skill and 1200 Animals vaccinatedActivity 7: Households will be provided with a set of poultry birds and FFS support regarding poultry management, production, breeding and poultry disease control to increase household protein production. In Total 150 Vulnerable beneficiaries Received training on Poultry management and started Backyard Poultry through 150 Sets of Poultry package.
