Project Summary

The project consists of three main components,1) maternity and pediatric hospital (Alamal Hospital in Azzaz G0700127), 2 vaccination centers and the ambulance network. The hospital will provide gynecology and obstetrics health care services, as well as pediatric services through inpatient and outpatient clinics serving women, new-born and children in Idleb governorate. Total number of people expected to visit the hospital will be around 22501 disagregated to 11053 women above 18 and 1560 women under 18 in the gynecological section while in the pediatric section the hospital will serve 4652 girl and 5157 boys. also Violet will support 4 ambulance networks through Idelb and Aleppo governorate were 9 ambulances works for transferring patients among health facilities and from field to health centers. Estimated number of beneficiary will benefit from the ambulance network is 1455 female above 18, 1541 male above 18, 516 girls below 18 and 724 boys below 18.
in addition , in line with Syrian immunization group SIG plans for North West Syria, Violet organization; as a member of the SIG; proposed to continue operating two vaccination centers (Mhambel and Al Shiekh Bahr (Kafr Omiem previously) EPI centers B0204097 and G0705059). Total number of children expected to benefit from the vaccinations centers will be 1800 disaggregated to 900 girls and 900 boys
Violet, in addition to attending the health cluster biweekly meeting to elevate the level of coordination with other stake holders coordinate on field level with all health facilities operating the area of intervention and was part of Ariha referal netwrok when the hospital was located in Ariha, now the hospital is reallocated to Azzaz, Violet has informed the SRH working group and will make the hospital as a central hospital for the other BEMOC centers in the area as well as for primary health care clinic that need to refer women and children who needs specialized care. Same think the ambulance network coordinate its activities with the main Ambulance network operating in Idleb that belong to the IHD and violet is part of the Referral working group. The vaccination centers location id defined in coordination with the SIG. Also, the SIG is monitor the work at the centers and provide it with vaccines on monthly basis and based on need.

4 - Needs

Nine years of crisis have heavily disrupted the health system in Syria, including 85 attacks on health care center reported between January and 31 December 2019. Up to 70% of health workers have left Syria and many others have been injured or killed. Access to reproductive health services still remain one of key health needs and priorities. NWS currently affected by the population movements, an estimated 650,000 women and girls of childbearing age are affected by the disruption of sexual and reproductive health services, either because they have been displaced, or because they are part of the host community (so the services they use are overwhelmed by the increase of clients). Of these women, almost 78,000 are pregnant. Around 8,600 will give birth each month, requiring access to safe delivery conditions. An estimated 15% of these pregnancies, and 9% to 15% of newborns will require a life-saving specialized intervention in referral hospitals. Efforts to reach remote populations and to ensure quality reproductive specialized health care services and early referral, such as through outreach teams and recipient with existing community health workforce, are critical in saving the lives of mothers and babies. (Needs of Women and Girls further threatened in North-western Syria-Gaziantep, SRH Working Group - January 16th 2018). The deliberate damage of the health care facilities and the lack of medical care staff in Idlib Governorate, reinforce the situation. In addition, there is a serious lack of medical care services for pregnant women as, after the latest displacement waves, there is increased demand for health services in northern Idleb and a pressing need to reach every person with much needed healthcare. The data shows that individuals are arriving at sub-districts such as Salqin, Harim and Qourqeena, Afrin and Azzaz close to the Turkish border, where the few primary heath care. Currently, there is only one Maternity and pediatric and gynecology health services.
