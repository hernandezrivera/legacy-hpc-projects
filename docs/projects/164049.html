Project Summary

based on comprehensive needs assessment carried out during 2019 for beekeepers in North West Syria (NWS), Seeds of Hope (SOH) aims to protect and develop the livelihood of 100 Households working in beekeeping (in Idlib/Harim and Aleppo/Al Bab & Azaz) by providing them with Vocational Training that enhance productivity and marketability of their honey, in order to create feasible income that saves their dignities and sustains their businesses and protect them from negative coping strategies under the stress of conﬂict. The project include the study of Honey value chain in the local context and ﬁnding solutions for main barriers hindering this social/economic activity from reaching its maximum limits. The project will provide the following services for the beekeepers targeted: 1- Vocational/Professional Training
This project is integrated with FSL cluster

4 - Needs

The project will address the following assessed needs: 1- Productive Assets owned by beekeeper (quantity, quality, and suﬃciency for supporting livelihood of HH) 2- Practices followed by beekeepers in handling profession and business wise (maturity level) 3General constrains aborting beekeeping activity (Security, Checkpoints, Agricultural practices ...etc.) 4- Availability of inputs necessary for production and affordability to beekeepers. Seeds of Hope carried out primary needs assessment for beekeepers starting 8/4/2019 and the MEAL activities are continuous and ongoing.

5 - Implementation of activities

The project activities will be implemented by professional agricultural engineers specialized in beekeeping, and utilizing reliable connection and relationship in the local communities targeted by the project, and according to FSL Cluster framework to standardize the intervention. The project Intervention (activities) will focus on the following:Expert beekeeper (Consultant) will evaluate the beekeepers businesses/livelihoods from two aspects:1- Professional practices of beekeepers in utilizing existing beekeeping assets in production (to be followed by Vocational Training whenever necessary). 2- Supporting small beekeepers to increase their production and generate suitable income, (to be achieved by distribution of production assets such as "beehives", "queens"...etc.) This project is integrated with FSL Cluster

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Protection Risk/Threat 1: Training will not be utilized by beekeepers to enhance livelihood activities, Likelihood: medium, Impact: medium, Mitigating Measure: Consult community throughout program cycle to determine the reasoning behind poor practice of training and solicit their feedback with regards to solutions, Monitoring: Enhance data collection: (i) align PDM tool with global standards; where possible, (ii) collect qualitative data through FGDs with different segments of the community; and (iii) consistently review and address contents of feedback mechanisms. Resources: Staff time to: (i) enhance tools; (ii) conduct FGDs; and (iii) regularly monitor feedback mechanism.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

The project took in consideration the critical situation in Idlib based on the escalation reported in January 2020, therefore, provisional measures were taken to control all activities from redundant oﬃce in Al Bab city in Northern Aleppo in case the situation worsen.
