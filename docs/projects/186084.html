Project Summary

The ongoing crisis in the North and South West Regions which continues to inflict pain and harm to the inhabitants of these Regions and has affected 2.2 million people with more than 712,800 persons displaced with some of them seeking refuge in the West and Littoral regions, 333,900 are returnees in the NWSW Regions and 67500 are residing in Nigeria as refugees (OCHA situation report 31st July 2021). An assessment conducted by AMEF in May 2021 in manyu reveals that rape and early marriage is a common practice with young girls between the ages 10-16 is a common practice perpetrated by warring parties. There is also the presence of intimate partner violence and many single mothers. Many women are experiencing wife battery from their partners with no food money but forced to stay in the marriages because they have no livelihood. This is coupled with the violation of the inheritance rights of widows’ leaving them with nothing to cater for their children. All of these have led an increase in the number of widows, single mothers and teenage mothers.
AMEF has four main departments through which she carries out her activities. AMEF has a department called Gender Human Rights Protection through which AMEF carries out her protection activities. This department is equipped with gender experts and qualified staffs who are well advanced in knowledge and experienced. The organization is headed by a social anthropologists and gender expert with more than 30 years working experience, followed by the general manager with more than 10 years experience in gender and peace building. AMEF has a gender club with the Apostolic Secondary School Mamfe with about 200 adolescent girls and boys where they are taught on GBV thematic. and also owns a cooperative where she builds the capacity of women through agriculture and other livelihood activities.. AMEF has impacted the lives of many vulnerable persons through her partners such as WFP, NRC, UNICEF, UNESCO, Street Child and IRC. In addition, AMEF’s depth of experience working in the target communities means they have the knowledge, relationship and access to be able to build a community based project which is accepted and engaged by community members and stakeholders.
Overall goal
 Build the capacity of GBV survivors, persons at risks, persons with disability through film making and other vocational skills.
Specific Objectives
- to train beneficiaries on film making and experience sharing
- to carry out awareness sessions and outreach activities on GBV
- to conduct GBV case Management, psychosocial support and referral of GBV cases
- to conduct training on vocational skills
Methodology
This project is on making a documentary film reporting GBV situation in Manyu Division South West Region. It is a call for a research that actively listens to the way voices of women who have suffered abuse can be heard and seen as an attempt to transmit their messages further and break the circle of isolation of victims of GBV experience. The location of the project is manyu division South West Region Cameroon.
- The project targets 1000 persons comprising of women between (19-49 years old), girls (8-18 years old), boys (8-18 years old) and persons living with disability as direct beneficiaries and 5000 as indirect beneficiaries. The aim is to reduce the risks and occurrence of GBV. It is a documentary on experience sharing of GBV survivors, persons at risks of GBV, with their consent. Also, some of them will be trained on filmmaking which comprises of script writing, management/usage of cameras, managerial skills etc. Others will also be placed in different vocational fields of their choice with a master trainer for 6 months per session. Throughout the whole project whether at vocational learning or filmmaking, the women, girls, boys and persons with disabilities will meet weekly in club settings for psychosocial support. These weekly sessions will be semi-structured following a curriculum of key life skills.

1 - Besoins / Needs

The crisis in the North and South West Regions which continues to inflict pain and harm to the inhabitants of these Regions and has affected 2.2 million people with more than 712,800 persons displaced with some of them seeking refuge in the West and Littoral regions, 333,900 are returnees in the NWSW Regions and 67500 are residing in Nigeria as refugees (OCHA situation report 31st July 2021). An assessment conducted by AMEF in May 2021 in manyu reveals that; • Rape and early marriage is a common practice and young girls between the ages 10-16 are most affected. Rape is mostly perpetrated by the warring parties and family relations because young girls are exposed. • There is also the presence of intimate partner violence and many single mothers. Many women are experiencing wife battery from their partners with no food money but forced to stay in the marriages because they have no livelihood. In Nkembong for example there are so many young widows who lost their husbands as a result of this crisis with no source of livelihood. There are also many single mothers who are victims of denial of resources.• There is also violation of the inheritance rights of widows’ for example there are many cases of widows from Eyumodjock village in manyu whose husband’s properties ceased from them and some have been exiled from the villages leaving them with nothing to cater for their children. More of the IDPs are women and children who are at risk of sexual violence, sexual slavery, trafficking, forced marriage and an increased prostitution for survival and security. Findings of various assessments indicated that most of the humanitarian needs have a direct impact on women and girls’ protection from GBV. The crisis have brought about changes in gender roles whereby women have become the household heads carrying the burden of the family because most men have been killed and some are in hiding. There is also an increase in widowhood and single female household heads. Young girls between the ages 10-19 years old are exposed to rape and exploitation. Decision making in most communities are in the hands of the non-state actors due to the breakdown of protective community structures and impunity. Many persons have lost their sources of livelihood especially women and young girls have adopted dangerous coping mechanism to meet their basic needs. The outbreak of the COVID 19 pandemic makes the already difficult conditions caused by the ongoing crisis worse. COVID 19 pandemic was registered in Cameroon on 5th March 2020, the country became among the top ranking countries in Africa with confirmed COVID 19 cases. Globally women and girls are believed to be more affected by the pandemic. This includes the impact of the pandemic on delivery and access of health care and particularly reproductive health services; increased core burden on women and girls as traditional care takers of the family and heightened risk for all forms of gender based violence especially sexual violence; sexual exploitation; forced marriages and denial of resources. GBV is a life threatening protection, health, human rights issues that can devastating impact especially on women and children as well as families, communities and persons living with disabilities. Despite awareness efforts, this evil still persist.
