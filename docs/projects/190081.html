Project Summary

To enhance the capacity of teachers in Ancuabe, Metuge, Mecufi, and Pemba, in issues of Child Protection and PSS support services during crises, to address children’s needs within the education system. IsraAID will train educators in PSS and self-care tools, and support their implementation through regular follow-up and refresher training.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

This project will enhance the capacity of teachers in Ancuabe, Metuge, Mecufi, and Pemba regarding issues of Child Protection and PSS support services, within the school system. Using PSS support tools and knowledge, based on the Social and Emotional Learning (SEL) framework, the project will enhance the existing capacity of educators to support children’s emotional, social and PSS needs in times of crisis. It will cultivate skills, and an educational environment, that enhances PSS learning and development, as well as practically address children’s PSS needs, and promoting teachers and students’ well-being. Topics will include: identifying Child Protection (CP) risks; existing referral pathways; ethics/guidelines of CP intervention; social life skills such as relationship skills, self/social awareness; and basic PSS tools. The program will provide teachers with practical self-care and coping strategies, to promote well-being and prevent burnout. It will incorporate practical tools at an individual and school level, to strengthen personal and organizational emotional resilience, stress relief and emotional regulation, and tools to promote safeguarding of children’s rights. The program will target 375 female and 520 male teachers (more male teachers, on average, per school). The selection criteria will prioritize internally dislocated, teachers with disabilities, and a gender balance.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Activities are standard for all geographic areas. Activity 1: 3-4 day teachers training, for groups of 25-30 teachers, in SEL and PSS tools, including self-care modules. Self-care will be an imperative component of the training, all follow-up meetings and refresher training. Activity 2: Monthly follow-up meetings to monitor the implementation of the content learned and provide a space for mutual learning and collaborative care study discussion. Activity 3: Refresher training is provided on a quarterly basis; it will include strengthening knowledge and skills related to SEL and PSS in education.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Protection mainstreaming is an important aspect of IsraAID’s interventions globally. We minimize the risk of causing harm by using risk assessments and working according to Protection and Protection mainstreaming ethics and guidelines provided by the GPC and the IASC. Our approach to MHPSs content and teacher´s training is adapted to context and culture. Awareness and access to referral and Protection services will be promoted through the dissemination of referral pathways, outreach awareness efforts.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

Individual protection assistance and services (PSS) are one of IsraAID’s main areas of work and will be a major component in the project’s teachers’ training, using SEL methodologies. Addressing the PSS needs of children, providing basic PSS assistance and tools, and identifying children who need further assistance, will be the core activities of the program. All IsraAID Mozambique staff, partners, and service providers are trained on advanced Protection principles, with additional emphasis on the needs of women and children in emergencies. IsraAID will use a multi-sectoral approach to teachers training on ethics and guidelines of intervention, CP risks, referral pathways and PSS. Our training content is updated on an ongoing basis, following two years’ experience in Mozambique, running a similar MHPSS training program (funded by GIZ), with teachers, educational staff and students. Plus, it will be regularly updated following the quarterly refresher training. IsraAID also conducted a pilot training with schools in Mahate (Pemba), to assess the province-specific teachers' emotional and psychological needs, as well as gather their feedback on their challenges in the classroom vis-a-vis MH and PSS. Training content development and project design are informed by the results of the pilot.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The selection criteria for the teacher´s training will be inclusive for teachers with disabilities. The training facilities will be selected, to provide access for all teachers with disabilities who are participating in the training, so that no barriers will affect the individual.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

In all locations, the project’s CO2 footprint will be minimized by refraining from car usage as much as possible, and awareness will be raised amongst IsraAID staff members of the importance of water conservation. To reduce plastic waste during training and workshops, water dispensers and non-plastic reusable individual cups will be given to each staff member for reuse. A dedicated waste management team will ensure the safe disposal of waste and prevent access to hazardous substances.
