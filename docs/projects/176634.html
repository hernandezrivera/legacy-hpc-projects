Project Summary

This project aims to address the needs of vulnerable returnees, IDPs, and host communities in Burundi, focusing in priority in the provinces neighboring the United Republic of Tanzania, where most returns are expected by UNHCR for the year 2021: Kirundo, Muyinga, Cankuzo Ruyigi, Makamba and Rutana.
These areas host the largest numbers of returnees, with important needs of shelter/NFI as well as livelihoods. Reintegration assistance through shelter will provide a comprehensive approach to assisting the most vulnerable beneficiaries. The primary stakeholders are identified as not only those people returning through the VolRep program, but also spontaneous returnees, IDPs and host communities to facilitate a holistic approach, as well as to enhance the social cohesion in these communities.
Partnerships with NGOs, local leaders, civil society, and the broader UN family will be upheld and strengthened through the implementation of this project. Given the existing gap in overall funding from development partners in Burundi that focus on returns and reintegration through shelter construction, the aim is to provide services that build upon previous projects in line with donor priorities.
