Project Summary

The project will strengthen the protection system, inclusive child protection, MHPSS and general protection through technical capacity building of communities, partners and the government to prevent and respond to violence against girls, boys and their families, with improved access to quality services, coordination, and advocacy on child rights monitoring mechanisms and meaningful children's participation.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The humanitarian situation in Cabo Delgado Province has significantly deteriorated over 2020-2021. IOM Displacement Tracking Matrix September 2021, indicates a total of 744,949 IDPs have been displaced due to insecurity, 52% being children, women represent 27% and men 21%. Increased fear of attacks in Nangade and Muidumbe have caused new displacements and migrations, into neighboring districts with increased movement with some IDPS seeking asylum in Tanzania through border point of Negomano in Mueda district. Save the Children will coordinate with other humanitarian actors and government to provide access to safe, quality, gender responsive, and inclusive child protection services. SC will provide case management and referrals for the most vulnerable girls and boys and caregivers, affected by ongoing violence and displacement in Cabo Delgado inclusive family tracing and reunification (FTR) and protective interim/ foster care arrangements for separated and unaccompanied children. SC will provide structured mental health and psychosocial support services to UASC, their families and communities through signature approaches like HEART, TEAMUP and Child and Youth Resilience Programme in safe child friendly spaces, mobile and home-based psychosocial support. Through Caring for Child Survivors of sexual abuse approach, and aligned to IASC standards for GBV case management, SCI will ensure survivors of sexual gender-based violence (SGBV) access to quality care. SC will strengthen community-based child protection mechanisms in cooperation with local structures and members of communities in collaboration with Department of Social Welfare. The project will enhance advocacy to reduce violence against girls and boys affected by adversity in Cabo Delgado including monitoring and documentation of the protection and human rights violations against children and communities affected by conflict.Target Population.The project aims to reach approximately 61,945 IDPS and host community people (girls 57,53019,420, boys 12,95055,367, women16,757 27,953, men 25,731 12,812 and 4923 1,858 persons with Disability) with integrated protection and survival services for child survivors, focusing on, UASC, CAAFAG and children with disabilities from IDP, asylum seekers and host community in Cabo Delgado

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The project will ensure that the most vulnerable boys and girls, women and men will be identified and reached. Guided by Save the Cildren Gender Equality policy and leveraging on the global IASC standards for gender equality, the project will conduct gender assessments in the communities using the gender equality marker. Gender and safety audits, will enable identification of deep rooted gender inequalities including harmful traditional practices to girls boy, women and men, and using participatory community appraisals and risk mapping exercise, SC will empower the community to recognize and do away with harmful traditional practices like child marriage, sex preference among others, that are a barrier to gender equality. The project will work with relevant agencies including the UN, PSEA network and coordination office, and the government to build capacity of girls and women through leadership and equality trainings, mentorship and empower programme, and boys and men champions to create awareness on gender equality and its positive impact to families and the community. The project will develop and utilize standardized protection and PSEA tools, e,g code of conduct, SOPs, checklists, case referral pathways including information education and communication materials (IECs ) that will enable all sectors and programmes to identify and mitigate risks to women and children in the camp settings where we operate. Specifically, it will enable staff, partners and community-based protection structured to safeguard women and children from the risk of Gender-based Violence (GBV), including the risk of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (SEA), and other forms of abuse and harm, such as physical abuse, neglect and emotional harm. The protections tools will support safe programming and a safe environment for women and children, including those who have disabilities to speak out.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

The project will ensure accountability to children and other affected persons through regular feedback and accountability mechanisms. Children and host communities will be engaged for regular feedback and accountability meetings, informing implementation. IDPs and host communities will be involved from project design participating in assessment to roll out through monitoring surveys, evaluations and other response and feedback mechanism including PSEA helpline, Linhna Verde, child helpline and other mechanisms. Through the Accountability for Affected persons cluster, SC will ensure issues raised by IDPs, children and affected persons are raised at various levels including the HCT, INGO forums and other Humanitarian leadership teams, and feedback and accountability provided to the affected persons. Capacity of IDPs will be built leveraging to access to services adhering to the IASC principles of none discrimination, Do no harm, confidentiality and respect of human rights, Sphere and Core Humaniterain Standards.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The project will ensure access to services is guided and based on a vulnerability assessment that is inclusive, gender sensitive considering, age and ability. Assessments, registration and inclusion criteria will be guided by the Washington Group Questions ensuring persons with disability are consulted and included in the project. SCI will collaborate with DPOs including FAMOD,AIFO and UN family for strategic planning, advocacy and and implementation of activities. SC will work with DPOs to ensure capacity strengthening of partners, communities and government on disability and inclusion standards, tools and approacesand general child protection. A mentorship and coaching approach will be employed for quality assurance, reaching higher numbers of children with disability (at least 5%of total target) improving accessibility and responding to needs.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Save the Children prioritises the Centrality of Protection and the proposed intervention considers context specific vulnerabilities, including those based on gender dynamics, and a need for conflict sensitivity approaches throughout the entire project lifecycle. To ensure environmental considerations, SC conducts conflict sensitive and security assessments, with the aim to understand the context in which we operate, understand the interaction between our interventions and the context, and act upon this understanding to minimize negative impacts and maximise positive impacts of our organization on conflict factors. SC ensures risk mitigations and management at all levels from senior management to field level, ensuring operations and interventions are safe and adhere to both national and international environmental management standards and practices.
