Project Summary

Improved access to emergency obstetric care services for 1289 women living in Diinsoor and Qansah.Dhere Districts ,Bay Region to Provide antenatal and emergency obstetric care services to women in Diinsoor and Qansah.Dhere Districts ,Recruit and train 20 health care workers (of which 50% are females and 50% males) in basic emergency obstetric care (BemOC), focused antenatal care (FANC) and Essential newborn care (ENC).
