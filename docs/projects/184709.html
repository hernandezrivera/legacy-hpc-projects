Project Summary

The continuation of protracted crises in Somalia, now compounded by the effects of Covid-19, Floods and droughts has resulted in massive challenges pertaining to Water, Sanitation and Hygiene.
An estimated 6.4 million people, 40 per cent of the Somali population need emergency life-saving WASH assistance of which 3.5 million will need safe drinking water, 5.1 million sanitation services, and 6.4 million hygiene supplies. 16 per cent of these are females, children for 64 per cent and the elderly for 4 per cent. This presents an increase of 39 per cent from 2021, in which 4.6 million were estimated to be in need of WASH services across Somalia. This increase is due to adjusted population figures of Somalia, in combination with continuous failed rainy seasons, which are expected to further strain existing limited WASH services, especially in rural areas424. The population in need of WASH services in 2022 includes approximately 1,619,415 IDPs and 4,818,944 vulnerable host communities. Estimated that 6,058,603 people (38 per cent) in the Severe category” lack access to safe water and proper sanitation services and are highly vulnerable to AWD and cholera and around 379,757 people (2 percent) are in “extreme condition as they access water directly from rivers and surface bonds, practice open defecation and lack access to soap and other essential hygiene materials and are susceptible to communicable diseases.
The proposed project is designed to boost the ongoing lifesaving assistance and will target the most vulnerable displaced, floods, and drought-affected communities in Banadir, Galgadud, and Middle Shabelle regions of Somalia. At the moment, IDPs and urban poor and rural communities are among the most vulnerable groups in the society. Since the capacity of the local community to address their needs is very weak it is clear that the increasing number of IDPs and urban poor in crisis requires an urgent comprehensive action to support the target populations. The project responds to urgent humanitarian needs including (1) Deliver life-saving WASH assistance to reduce acute needs among the most vulnerable settlements and communities (2) Reduce the risk of violence against women, children, and vulnerable groups when accessing WASH services, and (3) Provide or restore sustainable access to safe water and adequate sanitation services in targeted communities and institutions.
Further, the target population will be involved in activities planning, implementation, and monitoring. Special attention will be given to women, children, and the elderly, and people with disabilities during the designing and construction WASH activities (i.e. 15% of all latrines will have disability-friendly features and will be constructed close to their shelters to ease access). Moreover, women will be part of WASH committees and will be represented in all community structures to empower them. Minority groups and femaleheaded households likely to face exclusion will be part of community-based committees to represent the voice of the minorities. Distribution sites will be pre-selected by target population and hygiene kits contents shared with beneficiaries allowing them to understand their rights and entitlements. Different distribution modalities will be used where protection concerns are mitigated and reported if they occur. The household-level distribution will be employed if people with disabilities and the elderly cannot access distribution sites.

1. - Projects activities

Activity 1: Water trucking/water Vouchers PAH will implement emergency water trucking activities for 2 months reaching a total of 24000 people(Middle Shabelle and Galgadud) and water provision through boreholes to 21 people(Banadir region).Activity 2: Water point construction or rehabilitation As part of the exit strategy for outcome-1 above, PAH will rehabilitate 3 shallow wells installed with a solar system to have a hybrid system (generator set and solar system), submersible pump and construction of water pipeline connected to elevated water storage tanks, and provision of operation and maintenance tool. Activity 3: Water point construction or rehabilitationTo provide reliable and sustained access to sufficient safe water to vulnerable communities affected by the floods, PAH will construct 15 water kiosks (connected in strategic shallow rehabilitated in activity 2.1.1) equipped with pipeline extension. To mitigate the risk of violence against women, children, and vulnerable groups when accessing WASH services, a few measures have been foreseen. First, water points will be located close to shelters – a maximum of 500 meters from a household where every beneficiary can access. It will not only allow to increase their functionally but also to mitigate the Gender-Based Violence and protection risks associated with women and girls walking a long distance to fetch water. Activity 4: Capacity building (water committees and WASH training) PAH will provide capacity building and training to community members in order to ensure the sustainability of community water management systems. PAH will select and train 42 water management and Operational and Maintenance personnel (O&M) (30% female), 30% male and 30% people with disability, and 10% from minority groups. Activity 5: Chlorination (standalone separate to O&M) To guarantee the safe consumption of rehabilitated water sources, PAH will ensure that all water sources are free from microbiological (pathogens) and chemical contamination. Testing will take place at the water source, water collection points but also from randomly selected households.Activity 6: Latrine construction or rehabilitation. To reduce the risk of AWD/Cholera outbreak triggered by open defecation and spillage of waste from the damaged latrines, PAH will construct 900 communal emergency latrines (420 Banadir, 240 middle and 240 Galgadud) to reach 45,000 beneficiaries based on WASH cluster sphere standards (50HH/latrine). Activity 7: Community Hygiene promotion As part of the complementarity of the water trucking, water source construction/rehabilitation sanitation, and hygiene kits distribution activities, PAH will implement Hygiene promotion activity in 30 villages (10 in each region) in Banadir, middle and Galgadud region. Community Hygiene promoters will be selected from all the targeted settlements under the project.Activity 8: Hygiene kit distribution (complete kits of hygiene items) Hygiene kits will be distributed among 2500 HHs. Those displaced and conflict-affected will be prioritized. The hygiene kits adhere to the WASH cluster standard of agreed packages including Soaps, Jerry can (Noncollapsible), Aqua tabs, Buckets, and sanitary towels). The hygiene kits have an important role in preventing disease outbreak and helping those affected by the floods and conflict to carry out their essential daily activities such as collecting and storing water and maintaining personal hygiene. Activity 9: Hand washing facilities construction PAH will install 210 handwashing stations near the water sources constructed (Activity 2.1.1). PAH hygiene promoters and water management committees will monitor the hygiene aspect of the water facilities with beneficiaries required to WASH their hands when collecting water

2.1.1 - What is the justification for using cash or vouchers instead of in kind?

The project will also support the local economy and provide income-generating activities to the community through market based programming. First, by sourcing construction materials from local vendors. Through PAH market survey and analysis, it was already confirmed vendors capacity to provide needed WASH items. In addition, constructing emergency latrines will be done through cash for work approach (selecting skilled individuals from the target population) except for disability latrines. This has been already successfully used by PAH in the current SHF funding project in Banadir, and Middle Shebelle region of Somalia.

2.1.2 - Explain how the project design considered market functionality?

PAH confirmed the market in middle shabelle, Banadir and Galmudug are functional and able to provide WASH Supplies such as hygiene kits, latrine construction materials, sanitation and WASH committee tools.

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

Minority clans/ethnic groups are not included in consultations and/or are excluded from humanitarian services/assistance.The project will ensure the inclusion of minority group in decision-making process and part of the beneficiaries of the project. The selection criteria will target the minority people where the will benefit the basic Wash services. The identification and selection of beneficiaries will be carried out in close coordination with local leaders and volunteers including local authority, religious leaders, and women to identify the most vulnerable among the affected and displaced population including minority/ethnic groups. Exposure of women and girls to sexual violence, including sexual exploitation and abuse in relation to access to humanitarian assistance.The sitting of WASH facility will be identified together with women beneficiaries to avoid protection related risks. To mitigate protection risks, hygiene kit distribution will take into consideration that size to ensure that it is appropriate for the beneficiaries to be able to carry home with no additional physical or financial burden and without being dependent on others to carry it for them. PAH will ensure that beneficiaries know about the distribution process well in advance. A safe and accessible distribution venue will be identified. The distribution will be done in central locations and during daylight to ensure that beneficiaries should not have to traverse long distances or pass through conflict zones to access distribution points, especially after dark.
