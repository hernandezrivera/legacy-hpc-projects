Project Summary

This project seeks to enhance community-based protection and peace building in North Darfur, North Kordfan, Khartoum , targeting IDPs ,returnees and host communities, in Darfur we are focusing in three localities ( Tawilla, Kutum and El Fasher rural )

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

The project designed to promote protection for affected population IDPs ,returnees and host communities, the planned activities NPO will delivered according to needs and prioritize safety and dignity and avoid causing harm( do no harm) and will ensuring full meaningful access, accountability and empowerment and participation across all of project implementation .NPO will implement the project through jointly with the people of concern, and other stake holders. And the activities addressed in the proposal are come from the community through assessment and consultation in which all community categories will participate (men – women- youth and elders). NPO will give attention to gender mainstreaming and gender quality and pay attention to the vulnerable women, that the majority of project participants will be women. NPO will involve all ethnic groups living in same area in the project implementation, and designing, also the project will consider ages - gender and diversity ethnic groups.

4 - AAP & PSEA
4.1 - How will the project share information and collect community feedback? Please select as many as relevant.

Face to face with individuals (in homes) through aid workers ,Face to face with individuals (in other venues/locations) through aid workers,Face to face with group consultations with aid workers,Face to face with community leaders with aid workers,Phone calls

4.1.1 - How will the project use community feedback to improve and adapt activities? (open-ended/free text, required)4

NPO have complain box in each office and mobile hot spot to facilitate communication between beneficiaries and decision maker, HAC and line ministry.

4.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will support Inter-Agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including Sexual exploitation and abuse complaints?

NPO will ensure the affected population are engaged throughout the project cycle. Views of women, men, boys, girls and other people of diverse background will inform the design, implementation, adaption of activities. NPO will closely coordinate and collaborate with UN agency ,Cluster, Community Base Protection Network CBPNs, Community Reconciliation Committee CRCs, youth networks and other community members to provide timely, and relevant information in a consistently manner for informed decision making and participation of affected population. Through community consultation, NPO will establish safe, accessible systems for collection, documentation and analysis of feedback with appropriate responses.
