Project Summary

In the midst of increasing IDP caseload in Somalia, Juba Foundation is submitting this proposal with the aim of enhancing the living conditions of the Internally Displaced People (IDPs) through enhanced coordination, access to information and multi-sectoral services at site level, site maintenance, and durable solutions. Proposed interventions will covers IDPs in need in Bakol, Bay, Lower Shabelle, Lower Juba, and Gedo . Combined, these regions host 1,337,000 IDPs, or 51% of the entire IDP population in Somalia (OCHA HNO 2020). This project aim is to focus on Camp Coordination and Camp Management (CCCM) activities undertaken in selected districts of Bay, Bakool, Gedo, Lower Juba and Lower Shabelle by enhancing the overall protective environment for flood affected population, increasing access to basic services linked to different sectors, reducing the risk thereof, and availing specialized support assistance to beneficiaries to help them address other interconnected protection concerns.
To achieve cluster objectives, Juba Foundation will undertake a number of interrelated and complementary activities including setting up and strengthening of camp governance structures where necessary to enhance information-sharing and foster system-wide coordination among service providers. Besides, we will support community-led site maintenance activities through cash for work schemes to ensure upkeep of sites, enhance hygiene and sanitation levels and contribute to overall reduction in communicable diseases. Where necessary, JF will implement emergency site improvement projects to minimize protection risks and ensure safety. Key program activities will also include service mapping and site monitoring processes that will be carried out in close coordination with the CCCM cluster and other partners to effectively address existing gaps and maximize eﬃciency of the humanitarian response. Compiling of a master list of sites and mapping of both accessible infrastructure and partners operating in sites will be prioritized to enhance service delivery and coordination. In the selection of beneficiaries to participate in program activities. We will adapt a systematic approach with a clear criterion to prioritize most vulnerable households including the marginalized, single headed-households, large families and/or families with children under the age of 5, women at risk as identified by protection oﬃcers and those with special needs. Data collected to inform beneficiary selection will be segregated by gender to ensure that no group is left-out. The effectiveness of the aid package will be evaluated through monitoring of activities thus ensuring that beneficiaries access their services safely and in a dignified manner. Protection will be mainstreamed throughout project duration by closely coordinating with other partners operating Sexual and Gender Based Violence (SGBV) crisis centers and facilitating principled referrals where necessary. An elaborate feedback and complain mechanism will be setup to ensure accountability to the affected population.
JF with the presence in the targeted regions/ districts will ensure that there is proper coordination to ensure quality service delivery to the beneficiaries. JF has focal points in each of the district of which they have often participated in the assessments within the settlement schemes where IDPs have settled. therefore JF has proper access to the targeted areas proposed.
