Project Summary

According to the JMCNA 2021, shelter is one of the top three priority need of more than half of the entire population and of 58 percent of IDPs. An estimated 3.7 million people need shelter and NFI assistance mainly due to poor shelter conditions, overcrowding and lack of space, poverty, and lack of security of tenure. Those in need of shelter and NFI assistance include displaced and non-displaced populations. Conflict, drought, flooding, evictions and cyclones are the key drivers of displacement, which in turn exacerbates humanitarian shelter and NFI needs. Natural disasters are one of the main causes of displacement and therefore accelerate migration from rural to urban and peri-urban centers that started decades ago. Urbanization is rarely organized through urban planning and multiyear plans. Women and girls living in sub-standard and overcrowded shelters are particularly vulnerable. They tend not to feel safe in makeshift shelters which do not offer privacy and sufficient protection from weather elements and increase the risk of gender based violence. Juba Foundation conducted a quick assessment that aimed to ascertain the needs of over 50,000 beneficiaries in the targeted regions, it was noted from the assessment that women and girls are at risk of disaeses as most of them live in makeshift shelter and face the risk of eviction, there is no land tenure security. It is with this in mind that Juba foundation proposes to intervene and provide emergency shelter kits to 3000 HHs (18000 individuals both women, men, boys and girls) 4000 HHs with Non Food Items (24,000 women, men, boys and girls) and also construction of 400 transitional shelter) in Lower Shabelle, Lower Juba, Galgaduud, Hiraan, Bay and Bakool Regions. Juba foundation will conduct a market assessment in the target areas to establish the functionality of the markets and the availability of the commodities, this will be done to ascertain the modality that will be used for delivery of the Non Food items and ESK. Where feasible vouchers will be used.

2.1.1 - What is the justification for using cash or vouchers instead of in kind?

Use of cash is more risky and especially when dealing with bulky cash, use of redeemable vouchers is more likely and especially where markets are functional, the vouchers are redeemable for specific item of use and also in specific trader stores hence reducing the risk of fraud. In kind may be inefficient and especially where items are to be transported by road, they may encounter road blocks or bad weather and roads may be impassible.
