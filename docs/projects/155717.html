Project Summary

The proposed project is based on the needs identified by coordination meetings with Kurram tribal district education department carried out by the Takal Welfare Organization (TWO) last year. The project is mix of activities going to be implemented with participatory approach. Through the proposed project the communities will be provided improved education infrastructure and access to quality education through rehabilitation of schools at 20 locations where the existing primary schools are completely damaged. WASH facilities will be provided along with pre-fab structures. Additionally through the project 1,384 PTC/SMC teachers will be supported through capacity building of the teaching staff on child friendly learning, MRE, DRR and teaching techniques. Community participation will be ensured through the PTC/SMCs while all the teaching material and the learning support material will be provided through the project to all the students of the schools under the scope of the project.

Needs

The situation of basic education in Pakistan stands at a critical point which is clearly reflected in the UNESCO 2013 Global Monitoring Report and ranks Pakistan 113 out of 120 countries on the Education for All Development Index. Khyber Pakhtunkhwa in Pakistan has been severely challenged by extremism, terrorism, food insecurity, and widespread poverty. Women and children in particular have been profoundly affected and are one of the most vulnerable segments of society. According to statistics, Central Kurram, an area of 250 square kilometers with 250,000 inhabitants, is one of the tribal area where female literacy rate is at its lowest. There are 143 educational institutes for boys in Central Kurram, including 108 primary schools, 15 middle schools, six high schools, four community schools where as there are 51 girls’ schools 34 primary, nine middle and seven community schools. The reasons for poor state of education in central kurram are; lacking of basic facilities in schools, cultural and religious taboos in female education, and teacher absenteeism in remote rural areas. Other reasons also include as in majority locations, teacher students’ ratio is 1:50 which is difficult for the teachers to manage, while in many other locations one single school has three to four teachers with 20-25 enrolled students which is one of the management issue. The low literacy and poor state of education among the children and especially marginalized girls in the targeted areas has a profound impact on their socio-economic and political life which further multiplies in shape of widespread poverty and deprivation. The low level of education further aggravates cultural, religious and social barriers and restrain open discussion on issues affecting girls. The action is critically required as “Pakistan in general is lagging on meeting the MDG targets, and is unlikely to achieve the development levels it had aspired towards a decade ago. The province of KPK, which has been in the frontline of the security problems that have rent the nation, is not on track to achieve the ambitious MDG agenda”.In line with the cluster response plan 2019, TWO proposed project is meant to rehabilitate the damaged school, reduce the drop-out cases of schoolchildren, increase in school enrolment especially the female students, delivery of quality education through teachers training, strengthening of PTCs for ownership & sustainability and provision of drinking water, latrine in military operation affected schools of Central Kurram.The proposed project addresses gender disparities in education in the targeted areas through right to education and rights through education. The project will make concrete efforts to mobilize communities to ensure access and participation of women and girls in the overall interventions. Gender sensitive environment in schools will be ensured to counter social, cultural, and religious taboos against girls’ education at the villages’ level. The project will build the capacities of Parents Teachers Association in school management and monitoring and evaluating the activities. Transfer of schools to communities is expected to promote sense of responsibility and accountability in school management which will result in improving learning and quality of education in the targeted schools. Physical and instructional transformation in schools is expected to enhance the credibility of schools among the community members which will reduce drop out and increase school enrollment.

Activities

The project entails software and hardware components including the Base line survey, Community Mobilization and advocacy for girls & boys enrollment in the school, the formation of parent teacher councils (PTC/SMCs) their reactivation, strengthening and training of on their roles and responsibilities, Training of teachers on psycho social support, MRE, peace education and disaster risk management, provision of missing facilities like drinking water and latrines. The key activities to be undertaken to achieve the project objectives and ensure active involvement and ownership of relevant partner/stakeholders and project sustainability are as follows:Targets: • Pre-interventions assessment of all 194 target schools including 142 primary, 24 middle schools, I high school and 11 community schools of central Kurram• Pre and Post Base line survey, Community Mobilization and advocacy for girls & boys enrolment in 194 schools in central kurram, 25 community mobilization events will be organized, 02 at least 02 at union council level. • Formation of 194 parent teacher councils (PTC/SMCs) their reactivation, Capacity Building, strengthening and training of their roles and responsibilities • 56, 1 day Training sessions for PTC/SMCs and 56, one day refreshers for the PTC/SMCs• Training of 694 teachers (M+F) on school development plan, psycho social support, MRE, peace education and disaster risk management. • Provision of missing facilities under school development plan in 75 schools primary schools 60 & middle 15 schools will be developed based on the need assessment. • Post-interventions assessment of 194 targeted schools after the completion of project interventions to evaluate the success of the project. 2.1 Pre-interventions Assessment A base line data (technical & social) will be collected from all the target schools in selected area on the prescribed format as agreed with UNICEF, this excise will be accomplished with in one & half month after start of project. All the intervention under school development plan will be made based on actual data collected during this assessment. This will also help to develop monitoring indicators and impact evaluation the project at end. Data collection team will be fully trained to collected data and the collected data will be maintained in hard and soft form for further use. 2.2 Community Mobilization and Advocacy (Awareness Campaign)The quality education is of great importance particularly at school level. Therefore, mobilization, advocacy and awareness of community for the new enrolment & control the drop out of children in schools and their participation in schools development need to be done. TWO’s team will adopt different mobilization & advocacy techniques to achieve the project objectives. This will include; • Individual meetings, person to person communication• Community meetings• Two mobilization events will be organized in each of the target union council, a total of 25 community mobilization events i.e. awareness walk, seminar/workshop, street theaters, putli tamasha, celebrating enrolment day, child right day, mother/father day etc. will be organized during the project period along with education department 2.3 Formation & Training of PTC/SMCsFor the sustainability and ownership of community & teacher toward quality education formation & strengthen of parent teachers associations (PTCs) need to be established. Empowerment is the process by which an organization PTC are fostered and nurtured so that it will ultimately be in full control of its decision-making capacity.2.4 Reconstruction/Rehabilitation of 10 partially damaged schoolsAs Kurram tribal district was continuously hit by militancy and ethnic related issues. The educational institutions were damaged very badly both partially and fully. So a total of 10 schools will be reconstructed/ Rehabilitated under the project plan to provide the space for Teaching/learning to the kids and teachers.
