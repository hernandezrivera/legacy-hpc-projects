Project Summary

The proposed project aims to address the urgent psychosocial and economic needs of women and girls affected by the ongoing Anglophone crisis in the South West region of Cameroon, directly contributing to peace-building efforts in the region. The crisis has led to significant social and economic disruptions, with over 490,000 women and girls among the internally displaced population facing heightened risks of gender-based violence, trauma, and loss of livelihoods.
Purpose
The primary purpose of this project is to enhance the psychosocial well-being and economic resilience of these vulnerable populations, fostering community cohesion and promoting sustainable peace. Addressing the psychological trauma and social disintegration caused by the crisis, the intervention promotes trust and solidarity among community members. This is vital for rebuilding social networks that are essential for peace. Also, empowering women and girls play a crucial role in peace-building processes. By equipping them with coping strategies and economic skills. the purpose here is to empowers them to be active participants in community reconciliation efforts, thereby fostering a culture of peace. By providing all the above mentioned targeted support through comprehensive psychosocial workshops, vocational training, peer support groups, and community awareness campaigns, the intervention aims to create an environment conducive to healing and reconciliation.
Target Population
The project specifically targets 120 women and girls directly affected by the crisis, focusing on those in Buea, Tiko, and Limbe. Additionally, it seeks to engage the broader community to foster inclusive dialogue and collective healing, which are essential for long-term peace-building.
In summary, this intervention not only aims to provide immediate relief and support to women and girls but also strategically contributes to the broader peace-building efforts in the region by fostering resilience, promoting healing, and strengthening community ties.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The assessed needs include: 1. Psychosocial Support:Many women and girls have experienced severe trauma due to violence, displacement, and loss of loved ones. There is a significant need for psychosocial support to help them cope with these experiences and rebuild their mental health. 2. Economic Empowerment:The Anglophone crisis has disrupted traditional livelihoods, leaving many women and girls without a means to support themselves and their families. There is a pressing need for economic empowerment through vocational training and access to small grants for income-generating activities. Hence, Many affected women and girls lack the skills necessary to engage in viable economic activities. Training in vocational skills and business management. 3. Community Awareness and Education:There is limited understanding of mental health issues within the community, leading to stigma and discrimination against those seeking help. Educational campaigns are needed to raise awareness and promote understanding of the importance of psychosocial support. Again, the ongoing tensions have created a need for training in peacebuilding and conflict resolution. Equipping community members with these skills will help promote healing and reduce future conflicts. 4. Supportive Networks: Establishing peer support groups will address the need for communal support systems where women and girls can share their experiences in a safe environment. This will enhance resilience and provide emotional support during the healing process. 5. Monitoring and EvaluationThere is a need for a wholesome monitoring and evaluation framework to assess the effectiveness of interventions. This will help ensure that the support provided is meeting the specific needs of beneficiaries and can be adjusted as necessary. By addressing these assessed needs, the project will contribute to restoring dignity, enhancing resilience, and fostering sustainable community cohesion among women and girls affected by the Anglophone crisis.
