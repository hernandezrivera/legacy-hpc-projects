Project Summary

Strengthened self-reliance of 14345 CAR refugees and 3605 host community households
in East region of Cameroon
This project will target new bene¦ciaries in new zones and it introduces activities such as
connecting smallholder farmers to markets, strengthening household and community
assets, improving post-harvest handling, supporting women farmers and pastoralists,
strengthening government ownership and building resilience amongst refugees and
vulnerable host population for long-term development.
In line with PRM and UNHCR livelihood strategies, the project takes a multi-year,
incremental approach to build sustainable livelihoods for refugees and host communities.
It facilitates improved livelihoods skills and opportunities for increasing income through
agro-pastoral production.

vulnerable national communities in the East region of Cameroon through agro-pastoral
production
