Project Summary

UNHCR will continue to ensure the centrality and mainstreaming of protection across the shelter and NFI response, supporting the prompt delivery of immediate lifesaving shelter and NFI assistance to newly displaced people, alongside carefully targeted support for improved shelter solutions for protracted populations residing within temporary, and relocation sites or returning to places of origin.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

UNHCR's response will focus on enabling vulnerable IDPs (Internally Displaced People), host community and returnees to have access to essential Non-Food Items (NFIs), in building emergency and transitional shelters using local construction materials and practices, thereby promoting dignified living conditions, enabling privacy, security, and protection from the elements. The project aims to address needs induced by: 1) new displacements requiring emergency shelter/NFI assistance. 2) protracted displacements requiring transitional shelters. 3) returns to place of origin or integration within host community requiring early recovery shelter solutions. The project aims to support vulnerable households with adequate shelter materials that will allow a household to build a house and contribute in part or in whole to a more resilient shelter. Similar to other UNHCR programmes, the project will adopt a community-based participatory approach, integrating the centrality of protection, age, gender and diversity approaches. The project focuses on assisting IDPs and communities in constructing safe shelters that meet established technical requirements, with the PoC's (Populations of Concern) as informed decision-makers. The project is defined by four fundamental principles: promoting access to durable solutions (empowerment of the local community through capacity building and reinforcement of government institutions); building safer environments; sharing safe construction techniques; participatory decision-making. The intervention will consider potential challenges on access to local materials, their effects on the environment and safeguard support from local authorities. Together with shelter support, vulnerable households will receive the standard NFI kits. Districts: Chiure, Macomia, Mocímboa da Praia (MdP), Mueda and Muidumbe districts.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

In coordination with the Shelter Cluster, UNHCR will undertake the following activities: A) Provision of emergency shelter and NFI assistance to 38,805 vulnerable IDP and host community households. The project aims to support 1) newly displaced households in need of immediate emergency /NFI assistance and 2) protracted households who have yet to receive assistance or whose items are considered inadequate or obsolete. The NFI component is designed according to cluster guidelines and will be provided from UNHCR in country stockpile. Vulnerable households will receive the standard NFI kit according to the family size. Blanket distributions will be considered in the event of large influxes or to IDPs in informal settlements where basic living conditions have not been established. All distributions will be guided by UNHCR’s Standard Operation Procedure (SOP). Key activities will include needs assessments, pre-screening/verify cation of assessed needs, selection and prioritisation of most vulnerable households, community engagement and sensitization, transportation & logistics, safe distribution of CRIs and PDM (Post distribution monitoring). B) Provision of transitional and upgraded shelter assistance to 2,000vulnerable IDPs and host community households. This will include supporting vulnerable households with adequate shelter materials that will allow a household to build a house and contribute to a more durable shelter solution. The transitional and durable shelter phase aims to achieve 5 key features: - Improved walling and roofing materials similar to host communities providing improved water proofing. - Upgraded and additional living space accommodating the increase in household size. - Shelters with improved ventilation and daylighting. - Shelters provided with locks, hinges, and tower bolts for enhancing security. - Tailored shelters that meet the needs of the IDP households. Similar to other UNHCR programmes, the shelter program will adopt a community-based and participatory approach. The selection criteria will be developed in consultation with the communities prioritizing vulnerable households unable to support in construction of their own shelter. Partner technical staff will closely monitor and advise the beneficiaries on basic construction techniques, the quality of materials needed to ensure successful construction. Key activities will include needs assessments, pre-screening/verification of assessed needs, constitution of a beneficiary selection committee, selection and prioritisation of most vulnerable households, community engagement and sensitization, procurement, and safe distribution of shelter materials, training, technical support and oversight, regular monitoring, certification, and handover and PDM (Post distribution monitoring).

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

- UNHCR staff and its partners are trained on humanitarian principles and the key elements of the centrality of protection and these principles are integrated in its programmes. In line with the centrality of protection approach, UNHCR shelter interventions and distributions of non-food items will focus on saving lives, guaranteeing the rights to safety and physical integrity of affected populations, do no harm principle, safety and dignity, effective access to services and assistance, accountability of affected populations and community participation and empowerment. In order to guarantee the dignity of people, it is planned to provide timely and quality services considering the background of the individuals, the respect for their culture and religion and the survivor-centre approach, respecting their freedom of choice.- The shelter programme will prioritize localized shelter solutions with respect to space arrangements, design, materials, access for PSN (person with specific needs), provision of partition and locks, in view of privacy and cultural norms.- In addition, UNHCR has adopted the Protection cluster vulnerability targeting criteria based on the specific needs of individuals. The shelter interventions and distributions of non-food items will consider the vulnerability criteria for targeting/prioritizing its beneficiaries.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The shelter program will prioritize localized shelter solutions with respect to space arrangements, design, materials, accessor PSN, provision of partition & locks, in view of privacy and cultural norms. In addition, UNHCR has adopted the Protection cluster vulnerability targeting criteria based on the specific needs of individuals. The shelter interventions and distributions of non-food items will consider the vulnerability criteria for targeting/prioritizing its beneficiaries.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

- UNHCR shelter interventions and distributions of non-food items will consider the targeting vulnerability criteria adopted in the Protection cluster, in which the disability is one of the criteria.- UNHCR also conducts specific consultations with persons with disabilities in order to assess their specific protection issues and provide personalized assistance and material support, including shelter support and distributions of non-food items. - In shelter interventions and distributions of non-food items, UNHCR will ensure accessibility for persons with disabilities including safe, equitable access to distribution points, adequacy of mode, size, and frequency of distributions, provision of inclusive NFI packages among others.- During distributions of non-food items, UNHCR will consider separate/fast track queues for persons with disabilities. When feasible, UNHCR will organize door-to-door distribution for persons with disabilities, persons with mobility problems (in particular older persons) and injured persons.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

- As part of its community-based protection strategy, UNHCR ensures that the communities are sensitized and engaged on the protection of the environment, emphasizing their responsibility for the local environment where they settle. - With the environmental impact of the selected materials used for construction, as far as possible, UNHCR and partners will seek to ensure that wood and timber products are from sustainable local sources. As part of the community initiatives that will be supported in 2025, UNHCR will consider joint eco-friendly afforestation projects with IDPs and host communities.
