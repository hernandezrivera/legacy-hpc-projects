Project Summary

After 12 years of hostilities and persistent economic crisis, at the beginning of 2024, 16.7 million people are assessed to need humanitarian assistance across Syria, Economic indicators continued to worsen, with high inflation, currency depreciation and an increase in the prices of commodities, all of which remain the biggest drivers of needs. They drive more people toward poverty.
Intervention, Implementation of activities: The proposed project will contribute to support early recovery and livelihood in areas of Daraa, Quneitra and Homs. The objective of the project is to improve resilience to the economic conditions through early recovery and livelihood, beside to food security and agriculture activities in some areas for individuals and communities. GOPA-DERD will implement the project in Daraa (Sanamain - Ghabagheb), in Qunaitera (Khan Arnabeh), in Homs (Qusair).
The proposed activities are informed by the findings of a need assessment carried by GOPA-DERD R&D unit during 2023. The main findings of the needs assessment confirm that among the main vulnerabilities people are facing there are inability to provide food for their family and lack of employment opportunities.
Sector Objective 2: Increase access to Livelihood and job opportunities: PIN receive VT and life skills opportunities that contribute to their recovery and resilience. GOPA-DERD will Carry out a market assessment on skills demand/ job opportunities. GOPA-DERD will ensure VT service during 2024. GOPA-DERD will ensure the selected VT closely align with the market demand and is committed to overcome gender stereotypes. All the participants will receive tool kit to support home based business in concern to the trained profession. Procurement of tool kits will conduct in parallel with vocational training courses and the distribution will be at the end of the training.
We will ensure business training for individuals and identify participants based on set vulnerabilities and selection criteria
Sector Objective 1: Support the Maintenance&improve equitable access to basic services: Based on all the received reports from ERL sector and internally from GOPA-DERD areas coordinators, about lack of electricity access and high cost in all the targeted areas, and the real need to alternative energy for safe life and in otherwise to be able to start their small project at home or in facilities. GOPA-DERD will ensure solar system to support the families that receive vocational training and in need to energy support, also GOPA will ensure solar system to support the beneficiaries of MSME.
Protection risk: Physical and mental health of staff and partners affect pace of program implementation: Healthy staff to be hired
Breach of beneficiaries confidentially in small communities: Training on sensitive data protection.
SEA of beneficiaries by external contractors or humanitarian personnel: There are unreported cases of victims: GOPA-DERD is committed to protecting all staff, beneficiaries, partners and other individuals it collaborates with from sexual exploitation and abuse at all times especially vulnerable young adults, women and children who are particularly at risk of sexual exploitation and abuse.
