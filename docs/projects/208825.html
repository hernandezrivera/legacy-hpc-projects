Project Summary

The situation in Sudan has significantly worsened since the last update on humanitarian needs was released in May June 2023The conflict in Sudan, which began on 15 April 2023.The humanitarian crisis in Sudan has peaked intensively since the armed conflict took place between the Rapid Support Forces (RSF) and the Sudanese Armed Forces (SAF) on April 15, 2023, The armed conflict has affected 9. Million people since the conflict began, DTM Sudan estimates that approximately 2,152,936 individuals (431,031 households) have been displaced internally as a result of the conflict Several other factors contribute to the dire need for humanitarian assistance in West Darfur and the other states that received the fled IDPs. The current conflict only adds to an already deteriorating situation context, especially in the Darfur and Kordofan Regions, The critical gaps are assessed as Health, WASH, Protection, Food Assistance, and Shelter life-saving interventions for the IDPs and the entire population in West Darfur
The program targets to implement multi sectors (health, nutrition, protection) at the above-mentioned localities to ensure synergies. Health, nutrition, sector activities focus on the provision of life-saving services aimed at preventing disease outbreaks and reducing morbidity and mortality among IDPs, and host communities, and retune and non-displaced, The life-saving interventions include primary health care sexual and reproductive health, child and maternal health, treatment of severe & moderate acute malnutrition, clinical consultation, mental health, and psychosocial support services.CDF will be implemented through primary health care (static & mobile) facilities, with associated community health volunteers, to provide curative (OPD, OTP for SAM, TSFP for MAM CU5 & PLW) and preventative (EPI, ANC, PNC, MUAC screening, IYCF) essential integrated health and nutrition services according to the SMOH standard package. The activities will serve the vulnerable groups, IDPs and host communities, retunes, and non-displaced who are disproportionally affected by the current conflict in the targeted areas. The protection sector focuses on the prevention and case management of risks connected with abuse and violence to children, GBV, protection monitoring, and strengthening the local capacity to establish and manage community-based protection structures and networks.
Nutrition cluster/sector objectives SO1:LIFESAVING: Provide timely multi-sectoral life-saving assistance to crisis-affected people to reduce mortality and morbidity through strengthening community malnutrition management including case finding, reporting, referral, and follow-up, providing treatment to needy children, providing supplementary feeding and close follow up at facility level, providing support in referral of children with medical complications and improve community awareness including men inappropriate feeding for infants and young children.
Health this project will contribute to the cluster/sector objective. Strengthening of service delivery for all supported HFs through providing a free-of-charge consultation and treatment for the conflict-affected vulnerable people including children under five years of age and pregnant and lactating women,2.CDF will improve community health workers' capacity by working closely with Community Health Promoters (CHPs) to enhance community awareness of treatment-seeking behavior by implementing community health promotion activities both at health facilities and in communities. health education/promotion activities will be generated from the weekly disease epidemiological trends of health facilities3. Maternal, Neonatal, and Child Health services will contribute to the reduction of maternal and neonatal mortality rates among the people in the targeted areas.

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

The program project will provide integrated and specialized general protection, child protection, GBV, Education, Health, and Nutrition of life-saving services. The project activities will contribute to the safety dignity and life-saving in health and nutrition services of beneficiaries at different levels. The project will train and mobilize CBPNs and social workers as front liners to monitor and report on the protection situation as well as contribute to mitigating community risks. CDF determines gender equality in the whole project implementation and addresses the specific needs and interests of women and men, girls and boys enjoy the same opportunities, rights, and obligations in all program phases and applied to the methodological approach, as well as the targets, objectives, and activities of the projects. This includes formulating gender-responsive results and indicators and establishing a gender-responsive monitoring and human resource policy. CDF follows a three-pronged approach; Gender mainstreaming means to incorporate the category of gender in all our strategies, measures and all sectors as a cross-cutting issue; empowerment means to implement measures against gender-based discrimination and disadvantages, as well as specific activities to strengthen women’s rights. Often small adjustments to our activities are enough to enhance gender equality, e.g. the identification and participation of change agents for gender equality, the integration of gender focal points in our partner institutions, or discussions in sex-disaggregated groups. During high-level bilateral and multilateral development policy dialogues, in-sector policy dialogues, and in policy advice the strengthening of women’s rights and gender equality is established. Gender: Men, women, Girls, Boys, and young and old people in the community experience different conditions and have different needs, and capabilities that have to be addressed and taken care of; Not harm: Community mobilization has to consider the conflict dimension and seek not to increase tensions within and across communities. The community has to play a role in reducing friction within and across communities. CDF and partners deep understanding of local dynamics, gender and power relations, and local entry points for ensuring a cross-cutting theme for all program activities. In terms of sustainability project benefits will be preserved through the capabilities that will be built through project activities that will enable beneficiaries to work towards preventing, protecting, mitigating, and reducing violence in their community, peer groups, and local communities.

4.1.1 - How will the project use community feedback to improve and adapt activities? (open-ended/free text, required)4

Accountability to the population of concern will always remain a core principle for CDF, This principle has been reflected in all project design phases and will continue throughout the project implementation with a strong commitment to delivering principled responses to the people of concern needs. CDF will conduct continuous consultations with Community members and authorities will be involved in all stages of the projects to increase beneficiaries’ ownership of the project activities and sustainability. In addition, other project stakeholders will be informed of a role in this project cycle, CDF will conduct sensitization and orientation through meetings and sessions to publicize and disseminate project information to people of concern explaining in a very simple language.In direct beneficiary selection, the CDF team includes the participation of all groups of vulnerable people, including minorities, disabled, and other marginalized groups through the approach of Age, Gender, and diversity mainstreaming (AGDM).The project team will formulate a feedback mechanism to receive feedback and opinions of communities on the delivery of services to ensure the optimum implementation by providing a mobile number (the person calls and informs then the designated person calls the back person to allow reporting his/her complaint, feedbacks and suggestions for any issues related to the project activities). This way will prove to be more effective than the feedback and complaints box because the majority of affected communities especially returnees and PSNs do not read or write. A community-based network that will be established and trained on reporting cases such as; child protection, GBV, SGBVs, and referral will play a role in monitoring closely the project's planned activities and will ensure that CDF practices are in line with the community norms

4.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will support Inter-Agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including Sexual exploitation and abuse complaints?

Accountability to the population of concern will always remain a core principle for CDF. This principle has been reflected in all project design phases and will continue throughout the project implementation with a strong commitment to delivering principled responses to the people of concern needs. CDF conducted several consultations with community members during the design of this intervention. Dedicated sessions and FGD with women and boys were done to ensure their voices and opinions were incorporated within the response. The project aims to engage the local communities and authorities in all stages of the project to increase beneficiaries’ ownership of the project activities and sustainability. CDF has standards of safeguarding and PSEA which are implemented during the design and implementation of the project. The project management will focus on building the capacity of the staff and strengthening their understanding of GBV and PSEA will assign focal points for PSEA reporting. Segregated data will be collected and analyzed to address gender disparities.
