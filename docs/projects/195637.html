Project Summary

The project aims to reach 20,000 children (9,000 male and 11,000 female) ,teachers, PTAs and education
officials with essential education services. The influx of newly displaced children due to the recent armed conflict between SAF and RSF since the the 15th of April has
exerted pressure on the already inadequate education services in several states that is currently receiving IDPs and return areas which is lacking the infrastructure of education services. According to the
revised HRP and the education sector response framework , its estimated that over 8.6 million school age children need assistance to attend schools. according to Sudan Humanitarian key massages , Feb. 2023 over 6.9 Million children ages 6 - 18 are out of school which is 35% of the total school aged population. and due to the ongoing war and the daily displacement to the states the existing schools there had to accommodate an additional newly displaced thousands of student’s boys and girls which in turn affected the quality of education and effective classroom management,
leading to an increased risk of drop out in schools due to lack of conducive learning environment for children especially for girls and children with special needs.
inflation and economic challenges impacted Sudanese people’s lives and their purchasing
power , in addition to leaving behind all their livelihoods and belongings, many families cannot afford schooling costs. As a result, children are in
risk of dropping out of school, becoming exposed to additional risks and violence and
child labor and many protection risks such as being recruited or used and kidnaped due to the rising crime rates in all of Sudan.
The overall objective of the project is to Ensure vulnerable or marginalized children (ages 6-18) have access to safe and protective learning environments in the most deprived and overcrowded locations, camps, gatherings and return areas.
1) Provision of protective and safe temporary learning spaces by using semi-permanent
material linked with basic separated sanitation facilities to children, youth and teachers
affected by acute emergencies 2) Capacity building and enhancement of Teachers, PTAs
and Gos officials on EiE, child centered disaster risk reduction, early childhood development
and on psycho social support and lifesaving messages

1 - Needs

The ongoing WAR between SAF and RSF and the increased Hostilities continue to be reported across Khartoum, Darfur, Kordofan and Blue Nile States and prospects for the newly displaced remain continuing. according to the revised HRP more than 24 million ppl are in need among them 8.6 million school aged children, with the majority being hosted in the Eastern states of Sudan, Darfur and South Kordofan the vast majority of whom are women and children , elderly and people with special needs . the Education basic infrastructure is significantly ruined. The influx of newly displaced children has exerted pressure on the already inadequate education services in several states and return areas which is suffering from lack of education infrastructure services. According to the education response framework only 4.3 million school age children will be targeted in this round , and it was also reported that many existing schools had to try and accommodate an additional newly displaced thousands of student’s boys and girls which in turn affected the quality of education and effective classroom management, leading to an increased risk of drop out in the schools due to the lack of conducive learning environment for children especially for girls and children with special needs. Based on the feedback from the departments of education( state ministries ) in North, South, Central Darfur and South Kordofan that there is urgent needs to support establishment of TLs and provision of teachers in undeserved localities and camps . several reports show that the education service coverage in these areas is below the minimum standard for EiE . One classroom is used by 180 pupils(boys and girls on average in IDPs camp in South Darfur 130 pupils and 96 in South Kordofan. In all the locations, over 100 pupils, both boys and girls use a single latrine and the existing structures are not gender segregated or sensitive which increases the protection concerns. Although community volunteers have stepped in to assist, the teacher to pupil ratio is also far from the INEE standard of one teacher to 60 learners and many of these volunteers are urgently lack of capacity building, further affecting the quality of education. Overall, more IDPs make school absorption capacity even less and necessitates provision of semi-per classrooms and WASH facilities that meet immediate children's boys' and girls' needs. The education sector in East Darfur also faces tremendous challenges due to a huge IDP caseloads and ongoing tribal conflicts, Based on the assessment conducted by UPO in August 2022 in close coordination with MoE east, South and West Darfur States, gap has been reported and schools are identified to be in need lifesaving education. WASH in schools is a keycomponent of safe learning environment and has been identified as an urgent need. Itreported by the community worst indicators in terms of percentage of children in TLspaces without access to water and latrines. Teachers, PTAs also lack of capacitybuilding. inflation and economic challenges impacted Sudanese people’s lives and theirpurchasing power decreases, many families cannot afford schooling costs. As a result,children are in risk of dropping out of school, becoming exposed to additional risks andviolence and child laborUPO has a WASH project in the HRP and plans to coordinate its project locations under the different sectors to harmonize and better serve the targeted population in need.
