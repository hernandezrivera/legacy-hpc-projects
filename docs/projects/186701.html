Project Summary

The proposed project seeks to Reduce Hunger and Enhance Agricultural Resilience and Innovation in Borno, Adamawa and Yobe states(REHARI), furthermore the proposed project will support vulnerable populations to engage in basic farming activities that will improve food security, increase agricultural income and improve resilience among smallholder farmers, IDPs in host communities, returnees and their families in Borno, Adamawa and Yobe States. The activities involve creation of a coalition of partners; multiple partnerships with the private sector and government actors. This is to facilitate improved agro- inputs production and supply, farming and food production, high-value market access, mechanization and access to financial institutions and financial/business advisory.
REHARI aims to strengthen the IDP's in host communities/returnees who are farmers, MSMEs, the institutions that form the market system as well as their networks; who have been disenfranchised by conflict. The intervention will give deliberate priority to Women and Youth. Additionally, REHARI will function on multi stakeholder innovation Platforms (IPs) that network diverse stakeholders along and between community value chains to disseminate modern production and processing techniques. The proposed project will be operated on a market driven approach crafted to reach the full spectrum of the beneficiaries.
It has been noted that majority of the population in the three (3) states depend on agriculture as their main source of livelihood therefore, the project will support the beneficiaries with advisory services on Good Agronomic Practices (GAP) and establish link to Agro inputs dealers for improved seeds and fertilizers.

1 - Needs

Borno, Adamawa and Yobe states are particularly vulnerable to socioeconomic decline as a result of Insecurity and Covid 19- its effects on highly informal agriculture and food systems that rely on micro and small enterprises as well as smallholder farmers. Steepest increases in poverty due to lost income over the short-term were experienced in urban areas where restrictions were more stringent. Pre-existing crises (conflict, displacement, poor medical care) are compounding the effects of COVID-19 and its attendant social distancing measures. Borno, Adamawa and Yobe states dependence on seasonal agriculture may have variable impacts with upcoming lean seasons and concerns about last mile market access to inputs, information, extension and advisory services and reduced access to credit and other financial services for IDP's and returnees, smallholder farmers and MSMEs. All of this has compromised livelihoods, increased the costs of food and basic services, disrupted logistics and operations within agricultural food supply chains and consumer markets.Based on the foregoing, the proposed project is designed to address assessed needs though its activities: the project will support IDP's in host communities and returnees farmers to engage in basic farming activities. these groups represent the most vulnerable in these areas, lost their livelihoods as a result of the crisis. Furthermore the proposed project will catalyse new investments in viable economic opportunities around market systems, agribusiness, and employment, while stimulating market development and thus reducing the economic, social, and environmental drivers of conflict through strategic partnerships with private and public sector stakeholders.To return life to “normal”, there is the need to address the challenges of unemployment and poverty in the target communities. Therefore, to address this negative development, the proposed project in the context of strengthening the resilience of crisis affected people aims to re establish, improve and diversify key agriculture livelihood in the target communities. Furthermore the proposed project will empower IDP 's in host communities, returnees and people living with disabilities (youth, and women) which is key to the project.

2 - Activities

1. Community Participation and Empowerment: This activity focus is on targeted advisory services, Good Agricultural Practices (GAP) and innovations in Agricultural practices. Beneficiaries in Borno, Adamawa and Yobe states will receive advisory services on methods of advancing from subsistence to commercial farming and farm business planning for income generation and job creation, they will be supported to own or rent plots or pieces of land as genuine farmers ready to go into production. These participants will be linked to Agro inputs dealers where they can get varieties of Agro inputs for their farming Activities. 2. Capacity Building: this includes innovative approaches to agricultural financing, and Introduction to Innovative Approaches for Agricultural Practices, business management skills, access to improved agro-inputs, and high-value market access and value addition to agricultural produce. Additional capacity building will involve how to identify constraints and solution for improving value chains in agriculture, facilitation of improved access to high-yield agro-inputs, high-value market access as well as value addition to agricultural produce. Specific training will include agro-income generation activities, basic financial literacy and bookkeeping.3. Innovative Agribusiness Interventions: The proposed project will set up a multi-stakeholder implementing tech platform which will drive innovative Agricultural business strategies by improving market linkages between farmers, buyers and consumers. Furthermore the platform will promote financial inclusion, access to information and affordable credit facilities which will increase their income levels and improve their entrepreneurial skills. The most vulnerable community members (Returnees/IDP's in host communities, PLWD) will be targeted and their active participation will be key
