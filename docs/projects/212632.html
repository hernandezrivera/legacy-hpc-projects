Project Summary

According to HNO24, NWS already grappling with the protracted crisis, faces exacerbated challenges in its education sector following the February earthquakes. The situation is dire, 4% increase in the # of children Out of School (OoSC) compared to last year. Over 2.45 million OoSC, comprising 46% girls and 54% boys. This situation exposes children, particularly girls, to risks such as child marriage. Moreover, the education infrastructure is severely strained, with 3,700 schools requiring rehabilitation. Qualified teachers shortage also hinders access to education.
The project aims to provide integrated interventions in education and protection to crisis-affected children and youth in Idleb and Aleppo. Focusing on increasing safe and equitable access to formal and non-formal education throughout 24-25 academic year. BINAA in total planning to target 12,738 project participants, 12,100 of whom are children and youth (6,470 girls, 5,630 boys, including 748 Persons with Disabilities) across 18 schools located in both Aleppo and Idleb. The project will address identified needs through a variety of activities, including school rehabilitation, construction of new classrooms, and support for formal and non-formal education.
For formal education, BINAA will support 6,600 children, including vocational education for 1,100 children, and for NFE BINAA seeks to support 5,500 children. 16 BTL campaigns will be conducted. BINAA will support the most vulnerable students with student kits and 598 teachers in both FE and NFE will be provided with teaching kits.
The project includes the establishment and rehabilitation of 44 classrooms, providing school furniture to 8 schools for both formal and NFE education, and improving and constructing gender-sensitive and disability-sensitive WASH facilities for 18 schools. Safety and security equipment and systems will be provided to enhance the learning environment, along with maintenance and running costs for all 18 schools. Teaching materials will be provided to 598 teachers in formal and NFE, and transportation support will be given to 1,000 children 500 of them will be under NFE, particularly those in nearby camps and children with disabilities. Moreover, 638 teachers and education personnel will be supported with incentives in both FE and NFE.
Furthermore, the project will offer psychosocial support to 12,100 children and life skills programs to 1,100 children, aiming to enhance the quality of education in addition to enhancing the professional development of 598 teaching staff and education personnel. This development includes training in various areas such as psychosocial support, pedagogical skills, classroom management, life skills, positive discipline, technical and vocational training, EiE, and others.
For NFE BINAA will support 5,500 students, this will include remedial classes, ALP, catching-up classes, literacy and numeracy classes. Conduct 16 BTL campaigns in targeted communities. by providing the necessary support and resources to ensure their access to high-quality education. Students will be equipped with the necessary education and skills required for successful integration into formal education.
The project also aims to support the most vulnerable children by providing them with winter clothing, ensuring a conducive environment for them to exercise their right to education. Moreover, BINAA will implement health awareness campaigns, including mine awareness
BINAA will fully contribute on monitoring the project and reporting through 5W tools, and OCHA Financial Tracking Service.
Binaa is committed to following education guidelines and instructions, taking into account all education cluster's recommendations, and ensuring that teachers and education personnel receive incentives and necessary teaching resources to facilitate effective learning outcomes.
BINAA will take into consideration all the needed procedures to harmonize the participation of the CWD in all the project's activities.
