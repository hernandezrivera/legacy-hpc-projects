Project Summary

The proposed project is intended to provide a critical capacity for the humanitarian community to stay and deliver in insecure and hazardous conditions in Cabo Delgado, including through appropriate security risk analysis, information-sharing, and support. Security officers travel to locations and open humanitarian space through the conduct of ad-hoc risk assessments of locations desired to be visited and suggest appropriate preventative and mitigation measures to enable UNAFPs and humanitarians to carry out their mandate. This links to the Saving Lives Together and is a strategy used for protecting humanitarians.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The project is intended for Cabo Delgado only. Priority activities will include: • Security Risk Assessments.• Evacuations/relocations or medical evacuations conducted against requests.• Incidents responded to against the total reported.• Humanitarian personnel transported for urgent humanitarian missions in response to the situation.• Provision of security training to humanitarian personnel.

5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

One of the priority activities of the project is to provide security risk assessments to humanitarian activities in Cabo Delgado. The risk assessments are intended to identify risks the personnel can be exposed to and to within the context of programme criticality provide mitigation measures that will support programme delivery.
