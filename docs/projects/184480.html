Project Name

Saving and sustaining the lives of the most severely vulnerable persons including children, women, persons with disability and minority groups by decreasing the prevalence of abuse, violence and child protection risks and ensuring safe, equitable and dignified access to protection assistance in Jubaland State.

Start Date

01/01/2022

End Date

31/12/2022

Project Summary

The project will identify critical activities and immediate requirements to respond to child protection issues and Gender Based Violence in Gedo and Lower Juba region. This will be achieved through 12 month implementation plans focusing on livesaving/first line response.

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

1- Risk: Minority clans/ethnic groups are not included in consultations and/or are excluded from humanitarian services/assistanceMitigation: The project will place minority groups at the heart of project implementation. Project committees, training participants and any other beneficiary categories will also get representation from minority groups. In every beneficiary category, minority groups will receive 15%. The project design will also involve inputs by minority groups.Risk #2. Exposure of women and girls to sexual violence, including sexual exploitation and abuse in relation to access to humanitarian assistance.Mitigation: SEDHURO staff, suppliers and service providers subscribe to a strong PSEA policy that protects beneficiaries and communities from sexual exploitation. The project will further establish feedback and complaint mechanism to enable reporting on sexual exploitation. Risk #3: Risk of exposure of violence as a result of distance to and location of humanitarian assistance/services or indiscriminate attacks from armed actors. The project will take services close to affected communities. Women and girls safe spaces, child friendly facillities and One-stop centers will all be established close to IDPs or places safety audits will establish that there is less likelihood of indiscriminate attacks by armed conflicts. Risk #4 Risk of dispute on land ownership/land tenure and/or contamination by explosive hazards in targeted project area. Mitigation: The project will coordinate with Jubaland administration to ensure disputes stemming from land ownership is solved amicably considering the vulnerability of tenants
