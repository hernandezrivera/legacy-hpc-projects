Project Summary

To address the identified critical and sensitive needs of the host community facing a lack of gender-sensitive hygiene knowledge; the twelve-month NRC Wash Programme will provide gender-sensitive hygiene promotion to 1000 HHs of host community and contingency emergency support to vulnerable crisis-affected 600 HHs in the host communities of Ukhiay Upazilla, Cox's Bazar District.
In the host community, NRC will promote Hygiene as part of the Wash Sector’s Hygiene promotion strategy and distribute energy wash kits that will target the most vulnerable host communities during any natural disaster considering social stigma, taboo and restriction. Throughout the process, the wash beneficiaries will be engaged through hygiene promotion sessions by trained volunteers. Different communities will be formed consisting 15 person per groups segregation of males and females near the HHs. They will be introduced themselves as to how properly they could take their safety measures from water-born, vector-born and water-washed borne diseases. People will be sensitised with the water safety measure, personal and family hygiene, practices on proper hand washing, and hygienic latrines. They will also be aware of food and environment hygiene, AWD, Covid-19 prevention. WASH sector developed IEC materials will be distributed among the BNF. The composition of WASH kits will be distributed periodically to encourage them for changing their practices. Minimum requirement of ISCG WASH Sector standards, NRC will conduct this intervention following the latest Covid-19 guidelines.

1 - Identified Needs

The needs of the host communities have been identified as a result of the Aug 2021 Joint Multi-Sector Needs Assessments, which identified wash as one of the critical priority needs in the host community. Community still using latrine without slab (13%) or even open hole (4%), don’t have enough water for drinking, cooking, hygiene practice as well access to hygiene remains a barrier for many females through stigma, taboo, restriction and behaviour change remains an issue that requires further assistance on hygiene promotion. Furthermore, climate-related disasters (Flood, cyclones, landslides) and fire are leading to increased vulnerability. Therefore, in an event with humanitarian consequences, the project will also address the immediate needs of affected people who are in most acute need, providing wash.
