Project Summary

The objective of this project is to provide assistance and protection for the displaced population in White Nile State in the gathering sites and IDP hosting communities essential site management activities in line with the site management objectives as well as WASH and protection services.
This project will respond to caseload IDPs sheltering within White Nile State in Al-Daiwaim, Rabak and Al-Jabalin localities.
The proposed project will directly contribute towards the following sector's objectives: A- Site management SO2 & S03 of improving living conditions through site care and site maintenance.
protection SO 2: promote risk GBV mitigation in all aspects of humanitarian response. The activities proposed under the WASH component will directly contribute to the sector objective 3: 4.8 million crisis-affected people have access to gender-sensitive hygiene and the most vulnerable people will be reached with environmental health intervention
The proposed intervention aims to respond to the critical and urgent humanitarian needs of displaced people in White Nie State through delivering site management services to children and women, GBV awareness raising, life-saving hygiene supplies and community hygiene promotion and awareness sessions to displaced people.
JASMAR will implement site management activities to improve living conditions and enhance self-resilience in the three targeted gathering sites. To realize this the project will empower 1500 individuals (30% semi-skilled and 70% skilled) from households which contain vulnerable people or are in most urgent need of livelihood through cash for work (CFW) and enable them to conduct site maintenance with 45 tool kits and material for maintenance, safety and hazard prevention that JASMAR will hand over to them in the three targeted gathering sites.
A WASH activity aimed at improving community hygiene awareness will contribute to a community's first line of defense against illness. Cholera is a growing concern and community awareness needs to be improved as well as prevention and control of the disease.
Through WASH activities, 2,900 individuals ( 856 women, 790 men, 672 girls and 582 boys) will receive hygiene promotion messages thereby improving overall hygiene behaviors and practices in the targeted sites. This activity focuses on hygiene promotion and cholera prevention. It also raises awareness about the importance of clean water, proper sanitation and personal hygiene.
Furthermore, to provide comprehensive WASH assistance to internally displaced people in White Nile State, JASMAR will construct critical hygiene facilities. As part of JASMAR's project to improve hygiene and prevent cholera, 15 temporary handwashing facilities will be constructed at critical locations, 2900 soaps will be provided, three trainings for WASH committees will be delivered, 30 cleaning tools will be distributed for solid waste management, and 6 cleaning campaigns will be held at targeted gathering sites.
Each handwashing facility is expected to benefit up to 50 individuals, with 750 individuals including; 450 females and 300 males benefiting from the 15 handwashing facilities.
The project will adopt an area-based approach utilizing community committees to ensure tailored responses to address protection needs. two site management committees will be established at each of the three targeted sites by JASMAR and these committees ( There will be 15 members on each committee, 50% of whom will be women)) will consist of pre-existing community leaders ensuring that women, vulnerable groups and persons with disabilities are included within the level governance structure. The vulnerability of women and girls to GBV will be reduced through improved knowledge of community participation in GBV response and prevention activities. 6 committees will be supported, 3 campaigns will be conducted, 600 dignity kits will be distributed, and community-based awareness raising will benefit 2900 individuals.

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

JASMAR will take into account vulnerabilities such as age, gender, origin or social status. It will also ensure vulnerable individuals have access to assistance and services equal to those offered to other community members. The project places a high priority on providing meaningful access, safety and dignity for internally displaced people. This commitment requires JASMAR to operate in such a way as to not create or increase risk or harm to the people we serve but instead promote accountability, empower people, and ensure their participation following DO NO Harm principles.The project will ensure that all activities comply with human rights principles including; non-discrimination, participation, meaningful access, risk reduction, safety and dignity.All beneficiaries will be aware and have access to CRM including; direct contact with JASMAR staff or committees so that any protection concerns can be reported directly via accessible, safe and transparent channels.

4.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will support Inter-Agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including Sexual exploitation and abuse complaints?

All the project activities will be meaningful, relevant, and community-driven with the involvement of affected displaced people through the design, implementation and monitoring phases.Through the establishment of committees that ensure quality and consistency in communication with internally displaced people, JASMAR will involve beneficiaries at each step of the intervention.The committees will assist with project implementation through sensitization and sharing of correct project information with displaced people. The committees also play a key role in facilitating consultations with internally displaced people for choosing beneficiaries based on agreed-upon vulnerability criteria.JASMAR will involve internally displaced people in the design and implementation of the project which means that beneficiary perceptions and voices will be taken into account as well as through feedback provided on the activity conducted to integrate such feedback into future projects.A complaints and response mechanism (CRM) will be used by JASMAR to allow beneficiaries to provide feedback and raise complaints regarding the project activities and to track how action is being taken to resolve the issue ensuring beneficiaries receive relevant and accountable responses.JASMAR will provide internally displaced people with an orientation on CRM channels at the beginning of the project including; direct contact with JASMAR staff or committees.
