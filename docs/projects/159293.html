Project Summary

The proposed Water Sanitation and Hygiene (WASH) intervention seeks to contribute to the reduction of mortality and morbidity, related to the poor access to drought condition among IDPs and Host population in Daynile, Kahda districts in Mogadishu and Afgooye corridor of Lower Shebelle region. This will be achieved through increased access to adequate, safe and sustainable water and supporting and promoting a culture of good hygiene practice in order to reduce the related protection and diseases risks especially among women and girls in target locations.
The project will ensure and provide emergency and sustainable WASH services (latrines with hand washing facilities and safe drinking water through water trucking. For sustainable safe water services, SOYDA will upgrade one borehole equipped with hybrid to improve its water quality and reduce its running cost, in order to receive water in affordable cost. SOYDA will replace hybrid borehole and construct 5 water kiosks nearer to the IDP settlements supplied water through piping network to avoid the risk of rape against women and girls. The project will also promote adaption of good hygiene and sanitation practices through carrying out comprehensive hygiene promotion awareness & hygiene kit distribution and construction of 100 gender-sensitive and flood proof IDP latrines with lockable doors and hand washing facilities, rehabilitation of 30 IDP latrines and establishment of faecal sludge management (FSM) sites and systems to the Daynile and Kahda districts ; the team is composed 6 persons shared equally by men and women. Systems will be composed of trained FSM teams equipped with personal protection equipment, safe excreta transportation system (trash pump and donkey cart with tank) and a final disposal site designed so as to avoid contamination of water points. The FSM team also will serve as mobile sanitation team in case of AWD/Cholera outbreak in the camps and will be trained on AWD/Cholera preparedness.
Due to recurring floods SOYDA will make use of early warning systems to provide early warning messages in case of flood risk and to also create effective response. SOYDA proposed WASH project would develop synergies with other ongoing health, protection and nutrition projects targeting the same areas in order to maximize the impacts. SOYDA will attend all coordination WASH meetings at all levels as well as have close coordination and collaboration to line ministries of Federal government and state authorities.

Project Activities

1. In consultation with the community, identify and recruit 26 community Hygiene promoters from the community and 4 public health workers from the local authority shared equally men and women to conduct 3 days training on best hygiene and sanitation practices, hand washing with soap, water storage, environmental sanitation using WASH cluster adopted training materials including hygiene promotion in emergency global tools, AWD/Cholera preparedness and response guidelines, and HP training material. 2. Distribute 2000 Hygiene Promotion kits (HP kits) to 2000 to HH (UNICEF will supply the HP kits from Regional supply hub), SOYDA to complement hygiene promotion by CHPs and inspire behavior change, hygiene items will be distributed to the most vulnerable HHs affected or at risk of AWD (female/child headed households, pregnant and lactating mothers, households with malnutrition child or with cases of AWD. 3. Conduct comprehensive Hygiene awareness and Promotion among the IDPs settlement of 18,000 (3100 men, 4340 women, 6080 girls and 4480 boys) drought/AWD affected IDPs and host communities receive key hygiene messages through dissemination of hygiene messages in mass campaign, Household visits , talking walls, jerry cans cleaning campaign sessions and distribution of soaps during mass campaign/hand washing events, provision of Hygiene kits to the target populations and recruit Community hygiene promoters who are based at community level to improve safe hygiene practice at critical times leading to disease control, targeting needs of women, girls, boys and men. 4. Upgrade one diesel-powered borehole in Daynile IDPs through hybrid solar powered pump system, which will reduce the running cost. The solar powered system will be used during the daytime while the diesel-powered system will be used on nighttime (if required) or during the cloudy seasons. The solar-powered system will save fuel, service and repair kits and ensure cheaper and reliable access to safe water. Solar powered submersible pump system will save fuel, service and repair kits. Mitigation Measures

Domestic violence within the IDPs camps families that led many children unsupported. This is a rampant within the displaced person in Mogadishu and Afgooye area where, SOYDA is currently presence with active program, however, in this proposed program, SOYDA will ensure these unaccompanied or separated children were provided with adequate dignity kits as well as material assistance that will ensure to deter any form of abuse. We will ensure to provide hotline/call centers within the community WASH committees who will in turn report to the project team for quick feedback and attention these vulnerable children and mothers. SOYDA will ensure awareness creation to reduce the occurrence of domestic violence by conducting joint community and other stakeholders meeting within the project area Conflict a raising from water point, SOYDA will mitigate this menace through building the capacity of WASH committee as well as improving proper coordination with government line ministry for sustainable water services. The WASH committee will be selected among the communities with the project location as well as provide them with infrastructural equipment that will enable them to perform the minor breakdown within the water sources. Resettlement of the IDP – This is mostly evident in Mogadishu IDPs where after the construction of community water points and training of the WASH committee the IDPs are relocated to far areas that will risk vulnerable girls and mothers to walk to distance and sometime susceptible for rape, however, we will ensure on timely community information sharing and dignified relocation of the IDPs as well as proper handling of rape cases by providing psychosocial services integrated within the WASH activities hence this will further reduce the protection risk related to the poor vulnerable girl and women in the target location. Lack of information and coordination- project staff will ensure every step is being coordinated with the community as well as FGS. SOYDA will ensure more responsive response to the needs of the target population through the following to avoid the risk of gender disparities that is: Equal employment and services provision opportunity – The minority and disable people as well as these elder people might be left out for either employment and services provision such the long queues for the elderly or disable as well as pregnant and lactating women, to avoid mishandling of such group SOYDA will ensure to provide mobile registration during the project initiation and documents people with such needs hence given priority during the implementation period, SOYDA will also ensure to employ both sex as well as have no discrimination policy on age, race and religion in its services provision Project WASH committee representative- Lack of women, disable and minority group might create conflict hence exclude one of the special group mention, to ensure inclusivity, SOYDA will provide equal opportunity for both men, women, boys, girls, disable and minority group when constituting its WASH committee as well as its staff or community hygiene promoters hence this will drastically reduce the exclusion of either group. Lack of beneficiary information might also hinder the inclusion of girls child needs however, SOYDA will ensure include sanitary pads to cater for girls better hygiene and promote conducive learning environment as well as reduce the risk of discrimination against girl child in social gathering. SOYDA organization intends to implement the above proposed WASH activities in Afgooye corridor and Mogadishu IDPs thus due to the nature of the areas inhabitant by IDPs it might bring the following risk however, SOYDA will mitigate such risk as indicated below. Conflict zone - in lower Shabelle areas where there high number of displace person and sometimes fighting between government force and the militia group might escalate to the nearby settlement.

The project will complements the already ongoing reliance activities that have been implemented by SOYDA organization and will ensure timely and visible access to clean water services to the beneficiaries, in doing so it will ensure the wash facilities are within a less distance with properly lightening system, however, SOYDA will ensure to coordinates with NFS sectors by ensuring these vulnerable women, girls and boys are provided with alternative lightening material such as lamps to enable their security during the night time.
