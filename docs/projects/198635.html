2 - Activities

The attendance of children at school has dropped in Borno states since attacks on civilians, kidnappings and lootings have been perpetrated by groups associated with Boko Haram. Enrolment levels for the two LGAs are average. After dropping from primary school due to the violence, the children are often significantly older when they enrol back into Junior classes and a significant proportion have difficulties coping with the curriculum. Subsequently higher rates of dropping out over time are observed. The government does not have enough resources (Human and Financial) to run double-shift, therefore non-formal education has been overlooked with a priority given to formal education in primary and junior secondary schools and mainly in Maiduguri Municipal Council (MMC) before investing time and money to re-open schools that have been shut down in remote areas of Borno. Another factor discouraging the government to invest further in remote areas is the loss of training provided to teachers when those ones do not stay due to the lack of safety. The very volatile security environment of Borno’s LGAs, encouraged the state to prioritise the education system in MMC which translates in a high level of both primary and junior secondary schools in the capital while the ones located in remote areas remain are shut down or are left to the responsibility of LGA’s authorities.Currently, most of these children are idle making them susceptible to being recruited into insurgent groups, subjected to victimisation, rape cases (GBV issues) and other social related vices. The teacher-pupil distribution in the Municipal, city capital, is more than those across the hard-to-reach rural areas since teachers are ill-motivated to go to the rural areas particularly due to the prevalence of insurgent activities in those areas. Through this activity, ActionAid will select teachers across 2 local government of implementation for 2 5-day training in Maiduguri. This training will be conducted in collaboration with the Borno State Agency for Mass literacy This training will better equip the selected teachers with good knowledge of non-formal curriculum and necessary psychosocial support skills needed to manage the student needs and to support children’s access to the formal education systems through supporting non-formal education which gives leeway for teaching additional subjects and missed classes (due to the spate of insurgency) before children are re-integrated into the formal education classes and curriculum.This will also contribute to the Strategic Objective 2 of the Social Justice to End Poverty of ActionAid Nigeria Country Strategy Paper 2018 – 2023: Work to protect women and girls from violence, create opportunities for economic independence and decision. making
