Project Summary

This project aims at providing vocational education services to cameroonian refugees ( adolescent girls and boys) and crisis-affected host communities of Mubi South, michika and yola south The project will strengthen access to vocational skills through enrollment of adolescent out of schools boys and girls into government established vocational training hubs, supplies of vocational learning materials ( kits) and provision of small grants after graduation of six(06) months trainings to set up small businesses. However, the project will also ensure that learners access quality vocational training through capacity strengthening of vocational training teachers, support of education stakeholders capacities to carry out labour market job assessment to develop vocational training programs contents and curriculum driven by the labour market job demand.

1 - Needs

Access to education for girls and boys of school-age (3-17)has been obstructed by a range of complex challenges, including poor and unsafe school infrastructure, lack of proper water and sanitation facilities, insecurity, attacks on educational facilities, shortage of teachers, lack of premises adapted for children with disabilities and other protection concerns. These challenges have varying effects on different population subsets, for instance, girls are likely face the risk of early marriage, while boys may force to recruitment by armed groups. The crisis has exacerbated the situation for children with disabilities, who constitute 15% of the population (8% of whom are girls), depriving them of essential, needs-based educational services. In fact, 56 percent of all public primary classrooms are deemed inadequate and nearly 1,400 schools have been destroyed since the onset of the crisis in the North-east. The 2022 and 2023 flooding and climate change affected 132 schools depriving 50,490 girls, 48,510 boys and 9,000 children (4,860 girls) with disabilities of access to educational facilities. Climate change related crises will continue as a significant threat to children and causing disruptions in educational services.However, the consultation via mixed methodologies with all relavant beneficiaries, refugees reported: parents/ caregivers of out of schools adolescent girls and boys lack resources to enroll into formal schools; adolescent girls are at risk of Gender-based violence, vocational education programs content and curriculum are not driven by current labour market job demand. vocational teachers reported limited capacities. African humanitarian aid international shall work based on its mandate, education strategy, state development plan, UNDP developed vocational training guides to deliver quality vocational trainings in government approved vocational training hubs.

2 - Activities

To meet the over-aching needs of learners, the project will carry out labour market job assessments to assess the current livelihoods jobs, market accessibility as well as employability opportunities, that will enable to shape labour market job driven vocational training programs content and curriculum. learners shall be enrolled into the government established vocational training hubs for six (06)months training; learners will be provided with learning materials including digital as well as skills and certification relevant from the labour job market for employability. However, the refugees shall be provided with small grants to set up small businesses. However, vocational teachers capacities shall be strengthened on delivery of current labour job market skills, education stakeholders shall be supported for the development of labour market based vocational education programs content and or curriculum.

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

The project will consult via mixed methodologies (qualitative and quantitative ) with beneficiaries separately as per age, sex, vulnerability disability across diversity to mainstream protection throughout the project cycle. As referring to Sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA) quarterly capacity building plans of staff will be developed. staff will go through a refresher training on PSEA. in addition, all contractors and vocational training teachers and education stakeholders shall undergo a capacity building on PSEA. moreover, suggestion boxes, toll free lines shall be placed in secured places to collect views from beneficiaries, however the organization protection focal points details including names, titles, position, organization shall be displayed on a banner to confidentially report any allegation of SEA. The organization will ensure that all staff including contractors sign off PSEA form prior commencement of work. in addition, a special clause shall be included in contractors' contracts. However, the organization has an internal confidential communication channel to investigate any allegation of SEA. 2% of the organization's core funding is allocated for investigation.
