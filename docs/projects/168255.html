Project Summary

• Supporting advocacy and promoting multisectoral cooperation, with a view to creating synergies between government and civil society actors in response to the promotion and protection of human rights, in particular women.
• Offer practical strategies on how to deal with barriers to gender inequalities, adopting support mechanisms to promote gender equity, ensure accessible and inclusive communication and provide adequate social protection.
• Identification and training of community leaders, focal points, school boards, teachers in the management of GBV cases.
• Awareness and dissemination of information through lectures and awareness raising for groups of women and girls, on protection policies and mechanisms for reporting cases of PSEA against women in resettlements and in the community.
• Creation of a space for empowerment of women and girls, and training of women and girls in cutting and sewing.
• Identifying vulnerable women with specific needs that require extra support and case management and providing psychosocial support to women victims of GBV.
• Reporting of sexual exploitation cases and abuse against women and girls and referral to competent protection bodies.
• Production of IEC and visibility material and production of videos and documentaries of testimonies of sexual exploitation and abuse .
