Project Summary

This project aims to improve the food security status of the most vulnerable IDP households living in camps in the Ma’aret Tmasreen sub-district within the Idleb governorate through lifesaving and life-sustaining food assistance by providing free bread which was confirmed by the RNA done recently, where bread is the first priority for 91%. 750 tons of flour will be purchased from Turkey or the local market in (Syria) with the following specifications: the wheat seeds milled from fully mature wheat grains; free of filth and rodents’ excreta; clean and free from any foreign material and substances hazardous to health; free from rancidity and unacceptable odor or taste; homogenous in color and free from agglomeration; and suitable for Syrian Bread making number 650. Sent to the bakery to be baked to distribute 1,687.500 bread bundles (500 g bundle weight) to 3850 HH (23,100 individuals) in 20 camps in rural Ma’aret Tmasreen with (2250 bundles/ton) 26 days monthly up to 10 months, from the beginning of February 2024 to the end of November 2024 by well-trained ICO distribution team with great and proven track record dealing with BNFs using a well-controlled mechanism to ensure accountability and transparency.
The distribution criteria will be:
HH with 1-3 individuals will receive one bread bundle.
HH with 4-6 individuals will receive two bread bundles.
HH with 7-9 individuals will receive three bread bundles.
HH with 10 individuals or more will receive four bread bundles.
Each ton of flour can produce 2250 bread bundles, the weight of each bread bundle is 500 g.
The targeted beneficiaries will be IDPs in 20 camps within the rural Ma’aret Tmasreen sub-district.
The bread bundle will be distributed for free.
The baking cost will include baking, storing the flour, and transporting the bread bundles to distribution centers in the camps.
Bakery Responsibility:
1- Securing a warehouse for flour that will be delivered by the flour supplier.
2- Securing workers to load the flour into the bakery.
3- Providing all the necessary materials for bread, such as quality yeast, salt, and plastic bags printed with the human and workers logos, and so on.
4- Delivery of 2250 bundles weighing 500 grams per ton wrapped in a printed bag with the INSAN Logo.
5- Transporting the bread from the bakery to the distribution centers in Ma’aret Tmasreen camps.
INSAN Organization Responsibility: Purchasing flour and delivering it to the bakery warehouse.
The procurement process will be in line with INSAN procurement policy by tendering process publicly. Technical specifications of the flour will be appropriate to produce Syrian bread.
Production of the bread will be contracted with one bakery and the cost will cover the storing of the flour and the transportation of bread from the bakeries to the distribution points.
The project will be the only project distributing free bread (project duration end of February 2024 to the end of November 2024) and provide 1,687.500 free bread bundles to 20 camps for up to 10 months using the distribution blanket modality.
All activities will be explained to stakeholders and get feedback along with internal monitoring from the MEAL department. The implementation of all activities will be gender-sensitive, with feedback from BNFs through different mechanisms suited for illiterates. INSAN team will hand over bread to the tents if the HH is headed by the disabled,
