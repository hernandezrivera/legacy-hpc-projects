Project Summary

This project will provide smart LPG cylinder, Refill and stove among the Rohingya refugees to cover 6597 household in camp 8W.
LPG is the cheapest and cleanest alternative fuel option and provides a rapidly scalable
solution to meet the needs of the entire camp. LPG is already being used by families in all areas of the camps & is widely available in the host community. HTN will ensure 100% of HHs in Camp 8W receive LPG refills in a regular basis.
The risk of fire is high with any open flame, particularly with the materials used for shelter in the refugee camps in Cox’s Bazar region. Sparks from firewood are more likely to cause fire than the well-controlled flame from an LPG stove which produces no sparks.
Distributions of cooking sets, training and refills will be provided among the refugees aiming to meeting the following goals:
- Provide all households with safe access to sufficient clean fuel to cook their food,
without risk to health or well-being
- To improve food security and nutrition by enabling households to cook their meals
without adopting negative coping strategies
- Eliminate the need to cut firewood to prevent further deforestation and to allow
agencies to implement projects to restore and sustain forests and biodiversity
- Improve air pollution

1 - Identified Needs

Hundreds of millions of people use LPG safely across the globe. LPG is already being used by families in all areas of the Rohingya camps and they have become familiar in using LPG. Partners are distributing LPG Cylinders, refueling and cooking set among the Rohingya refugees. The area is paying heavily in lost of forest resources which provide this large population with the fuel they need in order to cook. Wildlife habitat has already been severely impacted with risk of increased danger from elephants whose routes & water sources are now heavily crowded with refugee shelters. There is also increasing social tension with the host community which needs assistance to protect their natural resources and livelihoods. To address the environmental, wildlife habitat, the LPG is the major elements to distribute among the people in need. The LPG program has been welcomed by RRRC, all Development organizations, UN bodies, Rohingya refugees and Local host community. Women and girls are mostly involved with cooking work in the Rohingya community. Collecting of firewood is seriously protection risk to them like- abuse, sexual harassment and domestic violence. Fire smoke is a serious health risk while cooking. LPG provides the women and girls relax & dignified life, leisure and protect them from protection risks. LPG is a highly flammable fuel, and, without adequate safety regulations and precautions, could present a serious fire risk. Fires due to LPG installations are the result of gas leaks and faulty appliances, so that equipment should be carefully handled and regularly checked. Gas leaks from poor connections, faulty gas regulators and damaged hoses are the most common causes of fires. There are international regulations regarding the storage of LPG cylinders to prevent explosions from causing damage to people or property, and also to remove the possibility of cylinder explosions causing other nearby cylinders to explode. According to the LPG regulations of Bangladesh, cooking cylinders should be separated by a distance of 600mm from the cylinder to other structures and up to 400kg of storage, a separation distance of 1m between the cylinder and other property, walls, other cylinders etc., is recommended. Every household must be trained to on the safe and efficient use of LPG equipment, before receiving their cooking stove. Help The Needy is providing those training to all the beneficiaries. Cooking methods for saving fuel and nutritional education is also included in the training session.The project will provide information about any additional programmatic components and information shared by the sector which could enhance the LPG project towards achieving the main objectives. These components can include activities to protect and restore the environment, or, to develop livelihoods and increase household income.Monitoring and Evaluation- The organization will ensure monitoring and evaluation of all the activities and reporting to align with the overall M&E plan. Feedback and complaint mechanism: HTN have a feedback mechanism with different entry points for feedback collection- electronic devices, Hotline, and complaint boxes. These feedbacks are processed, and regularly analyzed by the Safeguarding and M&E team and shared with higher authority to action & program team to re-design the programming. Inter-agency and inter- sectoral coordination: HTN participate in various inter-agency and inter-sectoral coordination meetings . HTN attends Shelter/NFI coordination meetings and CiC Coordination meeting in the field. At CXB, HTN will continue participation in the Sector meeting. HTN will continue reporting in the 4W and contributing to the SitReps. HTN will follow sector guidance on safe distribution in light of COVID-19 and ensure LPG delivery to the HHs on quarantine. As per J-MSNA, 2020, 88% households are using LPG in camps. Based on Help The Needy data 100% HHs in the Camp 8w receive LPG in a regular basis.
