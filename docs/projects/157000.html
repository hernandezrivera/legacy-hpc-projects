Project Summary

The project will provide improved WASH services in 1 Govt. Schools through rehabilitation of water sources, construction of latrines and provision of improved hygienic hand washing places.
In addition to provision of clean drinking water and sanitation facilities in a school, the project is aiming to organize trainings for teacher and health facilities staff on hygiene promotion. To improve the personal hygiene practices of school children the project plans to target 950 school children both girls and boys through school specific hygiene campaigns at schools’ level by using CHAST approach. Sessions will focus on children representing a cluster of households, different social background, economic groups and age.
Also PRDS plans to provide humanitarian assistant on war steps basis to 5299 return beneficiaries (1091 Men, 1143 Women, 1507 Boys and 1559 Girls) of South Waziristan through rehabilitation of communal water schemes. In addition to the convenient access of return families to safe and clean drinking water sources, 3974 returnees (818 Men, 857 Women, 1130 Boys and 1169 Girls) will be provided sanitation kits for basic latrines along with cash for work support to make sure the returnees with construction of their latrines. The provision of sanitation kit will minimize the health risks associated with open defecation and will enable returnees to construct their own household latrine on a self-help basis that is sustainable and contributes to longer-term development goals.
Clean water alone will not prevent water and sanitation related diseases. In the same target zones as water and sanitation interventions, 6624 beneficiaries (1364 Men, 1429 Women, 1883 Boys and 1948 Girls) will be reached through mass awareness on hygiene education using PHAST techniques. Apart of awareness sessions/campaign on hygiene education, family hygiene kits will be distributed to ensure the return families with improved hygiene at an individual and household level.)

Needs

Since 2004, In result of four major horrible operations against the militants and extremism triggered by the Pakistan Army in South Waziristan Agency, 71124 families made constraint to leave their places for the sake of successful operations as well as complete eradication of militancy forever from the area. After the conclusion of the aforementioned military operation, the authorities announced de-notification and the return process initiated. As of December 19, 2018 FDMA Daily Situation Report (DSR), a total number of 71124 families (100% displaced families) have returned to their places of origin in South Waziristan. Militancy and the ensuing military operations in South Waziristan have caused colossal damage not only to infrastructure and the economy, but also to the human dimension, both socially and psychologically. The FATA Sustainable Return and Rehabilitation Strategy, initiated in 2015, planned for the return of displaced families to their areas of origin following the completion of security operations in five agencies. Militancy and the ensuing military operations in South Waziristan have caused colossal damage not only to infrastructure and the economy, but also to the human dimension, both socially and psychologically. During the military operation against the militant in South Waziristan the unexpected terrorist attacks, shelling through tanks and aircraft bombings have destroyed the houses in the area leaving the residents with no other choice than to migrate. Most Government facilities in the areas including schools and health facilities are damaged and non-functional due to lack of trained staff (including female staff) and unavailability of basic equipment’s. Returnees do not have access to suitable housing, WASH facilities, basic health services; agriculture input and livelihood activities; since markets are barely functional and no other basic amenities are available. The IDPs returned to areas where most drinking water infrastructure have been damaged or destroyed and open defecation is common. The return IDPs families face a particularly challenging situation accessing safe drinking water and improved latrines. Women are responsible to fetch water for the domestic use. Within this devastating situation the availability of the clean drinking water is become a major concern. This deteriorating situation has increased the women vulnerability in finding clean drinking water for domestic use. The affecting factors of vulnerabilities are Remoteness, Women health, Sexual health, security threats, difficult terrain, and harsh weather and sexual harassment threats. After returning back to their native areas currently they are dependent on the available (fully damaged and partially damaged) water sources i.e. Fountains / springs, streams, open dug wells and few operational hand pumps. Having limited available water sources have increased the labor and time of fetching water. Schools and Health Centers in return areas of targeted tehsils often lack drinking water and latrines, contributing to the spread of disease especially among children. Latrine coverage in return areas is very low and open defecation is common heightening the risk of water-borne disease. The poor WASH situation increases the risk of disease outbreaks including polio and diarrheal diseases, placing the lives of return populations at risk. To cope the above mentioned situation, PRDS proposes to support the conflict induced returns families of SWA by providing the lifesaving WASH support to ensure a smooth transition to longer term recovery and development. The proposed assistance is actually demanded from the returnees for the basic survival and this assistance need to be delivered as early as possible to decrease their vulnerability especially of children and women.
