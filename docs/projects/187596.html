Project Summary

Through this project, Bonyan aims to create a better response to maintain the infrastructure of the camps and prevent\minimize the number of fires that are escalating especially in winter based on the CCCM cluster reports. Bonyan will apply some of the camps minimum standers in the targeted IDP camps to provide better living conditions with dignity, at least 12000 beneficiaries will be benefited (women 2750– men 3250– girls 3000– boys 3000) in 18 sites in Idleb, Harim, Salqin, Dana, Afrin, Maaret Tarsrim, and Al-Bab sub-districts in Idleb. The project will come with a comprehensive package of integrated interventions in CCCM sector by supporting the forming and establishment of active, accountable, and competent management structures to promote the skills of IDPs in camps to conduct maintenance activities for infrastructure and fire mitigation and response within these camps.

in the interest of enhancing the IDPs capacities to prevent and respond to fires, Bonyan will work on establishing fire points that will be equipped with all the needed tools (based on Fire Working Group recommendations), conducting training for 800 displaced men and women on the use of fire extinguishers and first aid by certified trainers.
Establishing personal cooking areas for each HH to be used as a kitchen, to prevent one of the essential fire causes. Bonyan organization was managing five camps for one year and a half, during this period around 10 fire accidents took place in the camps, the common denominator between these incidents was the cooking inside the tents using stove or any source of fire, therefore Bonyan team came up with the idea of establishing a separated area for each tent to be used for cooking this will prevents the usage of fire inside tents, in addition, the cooking area will provide extra space inside the tents that can be used by the families because all cooking equipment will be stored in the cooking area
During the project, Bonyan will ensure the redness of all fire prevention distributed equipment. Throughout the project, BO will select the target IDP community in coordination with CCCM Cluster and will ensure the engagement of the selected community, do no harm principles, target the most vulnerable people and apply AAP mechanisms.
Moreover, Bonyan will work on polishing\building the IDPs capacities to conduct check-ups and maintenance activities for the infrastructure of the camp, in coordination with the camps community committees, Bonyan will form maintenance committees, each committee consisting of 12 individuals from different ages, the committees will be trained on maintenance work and light repairs based on each camp infrastructure and kind of facilities. All committees will be provided with an essential maintenance tools kit, the committees will be monitored by Bonyan team, in addition, Bonyan will assess them with technical and limited financial support.

Bonyan will report all fire incidents to the CCCM cluster, share gaps in the target camps, and submit 4W reports monthly.
Bonyan is planning to repeat the implementation of this project in 2023 to cover more camps with its activities.

4 - Needs

Based on CCCM cluster the number of IDPs residing in IDP sites is 1.7 million as of Dec2021. the households in IDP sites are entirely dependent on humanitarian assistance while services and infrastructure in these sites are overstretched and in need of repair. only in Dec 2021, the CCCM Cluster reported incidents in 446 IDP sites. Of these, 269 incidents were flooding, 110 incidents were wind and 67 incidents were fires, according to UNOCHA. Given the increase in the number of people seeking shelter in IDP sites in NW Syria, severe overcrowding was reported as a growing concern. Based on Bonyan experience in CCCM sector it and the data collected during the previous CCCM projects, Bonyan team conducted FGDs in some of IDPs informal camps to get a better understanding of the situation and solution methods, the results showed that the main reasons for fire can be summarized in:1- IDPs neglect and lack of knowledge.2- The camp structure.3- Lack of fire points near the camp.Moreover, the IDPs reported the need of finding a rapid response mechanism to follow up on the camps infrastructures as not maintaining it adds more difficulties for the life in camps, especially when IDPs do not have the ability to carry out repairs and maintenance without external financial and technical support.

5 - Implementation of activities

To achieve the project activities Bonyan will work on establishing fire units (points) in the targeted camps, based on the CCCM cluster recommendations each 250 tents\HHs will have one fire unit, these units will be provided with the essential equipment for firefighting. the fire unites will be implemented by contractor based on the provided design by CCCM cluster.BO will conduct wide consultation with IDPs living in the targeted camps to establish both, the camps firefighter teams and care and maintenance committees, Bonyan is planning to encourage women to be an essential part of these teams.The firefighting team will be provided with training on Firefighting, First Aid, and Rescue Principles in case of emergency. where the care and maintenance committee will be trained on how to conduct light maintenance activities for their camps infrastructure.The firefighter team ( men and women) responsibilities are:1- Checking the preparedness and safety of all materials.2-Acting quickly in case of fire.where Bonyan will provide the team with regular follow up to check their readiness and inform them regarding any fire incidents reported in other camps.As for the maintenance committee, Bonyan will provide them with a maintenance tool kit, financial support for maintenance and light repairs, regular follow up on the committee work.regarding the cooking areas it will be implemented by contractor based on Bonyan design which was perfectly effective in previous project.

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

The main protection risks can be listed as follows: risk: The danger of the current global pandemic. spreading of COVID-19 between the camp population and BO team. Possibility: High Mitigation Measures: - PPE kits will be distributed to all staff. -following WHO\CCCM guidance in case of emergency. 2- risk: not applying the fire prevention measures. Likelihood: high - impact: high. Mitigation measures: all targeted camps should be registered in the database of ISIMM tool, maintain and support the CCCM guidance and position on camp establishment (camps are the last resort). 3-risk: disagreement by the camp population on constructing any kind of infrastructure on the camp land. likelihood: high. impact: medium. mitigation measures: consulting camp population before the construction of any infrastructure and being made aware of the risks that the current project was attempting to mitigate.Risk: in 90% of the fire incidents Women\kids was the first to identify fire, where women\men and they react most of the time in an arbitrary way which might make the damage caused by fire more as they had no access to information/training on fire mitigation and response. Likelihood: high - impact: High Mitigation measures: 1-Maintain the CCCM and SNFI Cluster technical guidance. 2-Fire awareness campaigns and training. 3- Promote participation of women and youth in training and awareness-raising activities.


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Risk: Abuse, exploitation or discrimination against beneficiaries Likelihood: high -impact: high Mitigation measures. 1-All project staff will attend PSEA specialized training. 2-Sufficient number of PSEA-related posters will be distributed in work locations, these posters will contain a hot telephone number for reporting, more care will be taken on raising the awareness of beneficiaries especially women about violence and exploitation issues, moreover, all the information related to PSEA will be shared on WhatsApp group in each camp with media material prepared by PSEA network. 3-Ensure that confidential reporting mechanisms are in place to address any potential abuse or conflict during project implementation. 4-All GBV and CP cases will be referred to specialized care service providers in the operational areas following the formal referral protocols
