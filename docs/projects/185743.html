Project Summary

The main objective of this project is to improve maternal, infant and young child feeding practices in the target camps and host communities. The project is targeting mainly households with pregnant and lactating women and children under 2 years, older people (male and female) over 60 years, people with disabilities and female headed households. The main activities under this this project include supporting households with home gardening and provision of small livestock. Children under two years and PLW are targeted because these are vulnerable to malnutrition. The project has a strong focus on social behavior change communication aimed at improving practices were community sessions are provided for pregnant and lactating women, husbands and mother-in-laws of Mother Care Group members to assist them for IYCF activities and practices. Through community volunteers all people with disability will be identified and assisted in line with the project objectives. SARPV will identified boys and children under five years with nutrition related disabilities such as rickets to ensure they are treated and managed accordingly.

1 - Identified Needs

Maternal, Infant and young child feeding practice are sub optimal in the camps and the host communities. Most recent reports indicates that 26.4% of children 6-23 months in the target areas are receiving minimum acceptable diet. Based on this Concern proposes activities to promote optimal IYCF practices a both facility and community level. Dietary diversity is very poor among children 6 -23 months and this can be due to limited availability of diverse foods, hence the plan to support production of nutritious vegetables at household level. The lack of awareness and knowledge on optimal IYCF practices is also high among some of the influential people at community level mainly fathers and mother in laws. to address this gap through community volunteers Concern will actively reach them with key messages on essential nutrition actions. Most recent reports indicate that rickets prevalence is estimated at 1.2% in the target areas and therefore the need for this support.

2 - Activities or outputs

1. Facilitate Mother Care Group sessions to support households with pregnant and lactating women and children under two years for sustained infant and young child feeding to maintain the minimum acceptable diet and strengthen breastfeeding practices, acute malnutrition screening and referral, and COVID-19 prevention messaging. 2. Home gardens, have been an integral component of the project across the phases, implemented through the Mother Care Groups, contributing to both minimum acceptable diet for IYCF and increase household dietary diversity at host and Rohingya community. In this project phase, innovative home garden types will be explored, and will include techniques to sustainability. 3. Capacity building of volunteers to support innovative home gardens, IYCF practices support, COVID-19 prevention resilience for households and continued acute malnutrition screening, referral and awareness session conducting by mothers at household level. 4. Rickets identification and treatment 5. Support small livestock 6. Sensitization sessions with mother in laws for caregivers of children under five7. Provide support to national partner
