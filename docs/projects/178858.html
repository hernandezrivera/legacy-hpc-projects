Project Summary

Misk is aiming to promote
integrated livelihood modality through two interventions under this project, Misk will improve resilience and livelihoods of vulnerable people through strengthening agriculture in Jarablus,
Ghandorah sub-district, this geographical area was selected based on its high number of farmlands and the lack of resources for farmers to plant their lands, both organizations will depend on their technical
staff capacity to successfully implement the proposed intervention.
- Misk currently is implementing a WASH project , in addition to irrigation support projects in different areas of northern Syria, so it will use its experience to rehabilitate seven irrigation canals (3 restorations + 4 maintenance) through cash for work for 25 individuals in addition to contractual services, these canals link between several villages, and its irrigated more than7000 dunum, and serve about 500 farmers. Misk will also maintain and provides some equipment drip irrigation system for 200 dunum /50 farmers (2-5 dunum per farmer) who our field team visited a few days ago and
checked/verified their drip irrigation system needs.
The canals and the drip irrigation system will continue to be monitored and maintained during the life of the project and technical training of the beneficiaries on the modern irrigation system and related topics. A field technical assessment was conducted in the targeted area to identify the above-mentioned needs.
Under the improved food security situation for newly IDPs in hos community, Misk will add an activity
a cash food security support in the form of three rounds of 120 USD per vulnerable household for 200 HH, for a total of 72000 USD
Under vulnerability criterion:
-Recent displacement since Dec.2019
-Female-headed HH
- Unacceptable living conditions – living in unofficial comp concentration
- # of children (more than 4)
- Disability
According to the assessment it is estimated that a 10 dunum of wheat cultivation will cost 600 USD, the
project is planning to support the farmers with 80% of this with agricultural inputs and irrigation support,
thus reflecting an overall positive impact on the livelihood situation of the local community and
enhancing the chances of wheat cultivation success in profitability as a strategic crop
- While Misk implemented agricultural projects similar in other areas and have an expert agricultural
team, Misk will be supporting ( vegetables) to 200 farmers through the distribution of agriculture inputs 200 farmers through small-medium farming activities in the autumn season (2 dunum per farmer).
The project will contribute to assisting 90% of the target community's farmers, strengthening agriculture
to recover the local economy and improve the living conditions of the displaced and host families.
The project will priorities the do-no-harm principle throughout the activities. The M&E team will verify
the commitment of the project team towered the prevention of the spreading of COVID-19

4 - Needs

Meanwhile, according to the Humanitarian Needs Assessment Programme (HNAP), 242,468 individuals have returned to their place of origin in northwest Syria in 2020. Between January and February 2021, close to 7,400 people are estimated to have returned to southern Idleb and western Aleppo governorates despite difficult conditions for return, including contamination of explosive hazards, and limited access to basic service and livelihood opportunities.Ghandorah sub-district is one of the areas that have many changes in the dominant forces during the last nine years and is now marked as stable and secure. It has been a safe haven for several displacement waves from many governorates such as; Ar-Raqqa, Deir Ez-Zoor, Homs, Damascus, rural Hama.Misk field team conducted need assessment on Apr 2020, in Ghandorah to identify the number of affected people and the various needs, including, rehabilitation needs, and availability of commodities, functional markets, and available job opportunities.-key findings;*The populations of Ghandorah sub-district was 25,300 people (5000families) 51% of the population are males, 49 % females, Elderly is about 4 % of the population. 6 - Protection Risk Analysis

The project promotes equal opportunities for both males and females, and focuses on women's participation in project activities. It works on empowering and developing women's capacity and offers them the opportunity of being active and effective in the community. The project also enhances the participation and activation of different age groups (male and female) in addition to persons with disabilities through the diversity of activities aimed at strengthening the society economically and socially. Consideration will be given to targeting the youth (male & female, and persons with disabilities) in different activities and providing them with a safe and creative environment free from any kind of abuse, sexual harassment, or discrimination of any kind. Feedback mechanisms will be established in place to file any complaints regarding misconduct by any member of the partners' team.Care was taken to ensure the participation of women, youth, and persons with disabilities in the project through:• Designing project objectives to meet the needs of women and enhance their roles in the community. As well as, activating youth- energy.• Designing indicators of achievement for project goals where equal consideration was given to both sexes and youth and persons with disabilities as a measure of achieving the project's impact and results.• Providing a positive and safe environment to encourage females to participate by providing female employees.• Ensure that a female employee is in place for all activities that target women beneficiaries. The principle of gender and protection mainstreaming will be promoted directly and indirectly, by integrating it into the various activities that the project offers
