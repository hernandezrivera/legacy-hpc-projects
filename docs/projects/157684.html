Project Summary

In collaboration with the Korean Federation for the Protection of the Disabled, HI is supporting 11 health facilities and outreach mobile camps to improve access to functional rehabilitation services for persons with disabilities. Interventions focus on improving working conditions of health facilities, strengthening physiotherapy and rehabilitation departments with essential rehabilitation and therapeutic equipment, early screening of childhood disability and provision of early intervention for the new-born and children at risk of developing a disability, production of orthopaedic appliances, provision of assistive devices, and enhancing technical skills of health and rehabilitation care professionals through in country and international clinical training.
