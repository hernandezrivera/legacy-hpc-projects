Project Summary

The project targets 22,050 age 4-6 children in IDP/returnee and host community. Out of which 7350 children attend ASR program in four woreds; 2888 in kercha woreda of west Guji , 2887 and 2888 in Legahida and Raytu woredas of East Bale respectively, and 2887 children in Medewelabu woreda of Bale Zone. Age 4-6 children will be direct benefited from the project within the first inception of the project. Hence, 3.16 (50% girls) million children in Oromia region impacted through regional ECE system strengthen. On the other hand, 518 administrators at REB, ZEOs and WEOs, school principals, and ECE teachers with capacity building and ECE teachers with capacity building. Moreover, 22,050 parents/ care givers in community engagements activities to promote play based early learning.
Imagine1day designed a project with the objective of Promoting play based early learning through a system strengthening Approach for IDP and returnees/ host children affected by conflict in Oromia region. Mainly to support ECE curriculum, IOD will make both financial and technical contributions to the project. Imagine1day has ample experience of implementing successful pre-primary education modalities both in formal and informal settings. Hence, Imagine1day will contribute technical expertise in training key stakeholders to ensure effective project implementation, management, and monitoring and evaluation.
Imagine1day will employ strategies to create systemic lasting results, by focusing on building the leadership and technical capacity of key stakeholders, who will influence the long-term impact of this project. In so doing, some key points will be given attention including strengthening the management and leadership of the community particularly the PTAs, CTEs, TEVET, REB, ZEO, WEO and enhancing the teacher's capabilities to deliver a quality teaching learning process through continuous capacity building and technical support.
This project deals with the Education decision makers prioritize pre-primary education in legislation and strategy to promote effective and sustainable expansion of pre-primary education with quality, Programme review meetings and workshops held to review implementation progress and to share experience as part of learning and capacity building for partners, Regional ECE sub-sector develops and implements new gender sensitive ECE curriculum that is free from gender bias and promote equality in gender relations with a strengthened play-based approach to early learning and in line with primary curriculum, Teachers apply their training on ECE standards including improved, inclusive, and gender responsive teaching-learning environment and materials to benefit 7,350 children (50% girls, 50% CWDs).
The project inputs include financial, human and material resources. The total cost of the project is estimated at USD 1,843,797 (one million eight hundred forty-three thousand seven hundred ninety seven) and out of this 1,618,806 (87%) allocated for Operational cost and 224,991 (12%) allocated for administration cost.
Major project outputs include; Renovate 64 ECE damaged schools, build 10 new ECE class in the operational districts, furniture provision for 64 innovative schools, provision of 10 playground for newly constructed classes, reach out directly 7,350 age 4-6 children in targeted woredas and schools, distribution of new curriculum frame work. Moreover, to support the program to revise CTE curriculum on play-based approach train and support 4 TVET for ECE kit production and capacitating as resource center. There is a quarterly and annual participatory project monitoring and evaluation with stake holders and project phase-out strategy begins from project inception through final phase-out, with an underlying objective that the beneficiary Community and districts own the project and are committed to the long-term sustainability of its outcomes

3.6 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members using age and gender appropriate, safe and confidential channels?

Imagine1day believes that beneficiary’s involvement in project planning, implementation, monitoring and evaluation is indispensable. As an approach, our project team conducts discussion with beneficiaries at every phase of the intervention and they will discuss on problems with the concerned sectors or community leaders to solve the problems. Access of suggestion box is facilitated to create a free environment for beneficiaries to comment whatever they need. The suggestion box will be placed in safe and protected location identified by the CRC within the IDP/returnee sites. Our project team will also work as close as the family member of the beneficiaries and can easily gather beneficiary problems and works towards solution. The measures taken could be discussed with beneficiaries as required on the sensitivity of the issue and confidence at the side of beneficiaries could be built. Imagine 1 Day will take a responsibility to hire female employees as a mandatory action that helps to handle feedbacks on the side of female beneficiaries. Accordingly, depending up on the feedback forwarded by the beneficiaries, the case will be solved within the project or to be referred to other sectors as per the referral mapping designed by Inter-Cluster Coordination Group with a cloth follow up.
