Project Summary

Access to adequate health services, particularly for women and children, is significantly restricted by social barriers, poor infrastructure, low service quality, high levels of insecurity, and a limited number of health facilities and trained health workers. Relative security in Burdhubo and Luuq towns provides an opportunity to improve health services in both districts significantly. This proposal focuses explicitly on the urgent health care needs existing in Burdhubo and Luuq districts (Northern side), to be implemented over one year.
The project targets 37,048 (18,820 female, 18,288 male, 2,595 boys and 2595 girls, and 739 elderly) beneficiaries from both host and IDP communities with healthcare services, out of which 1,852 beneficiaries are people with disabilities. This project will directly contribute to the Health cluster objectives of reducing excess morbidity and mortality due to disease outbreak driven by climatic shocks and conflict, contribute to improvement in the physical and mental well-being of the population affected by conflict and displacement, improve case management and referral services for survivors of sexual or gender-based violence and improve equitable access to quality emergency and essential health services. The proposal is also aligned to the Ministry of Health (MoH) strategic plan Objective 1 and 4, which focuses on improving the Somali population's health, focusing on maternal and child health and the cluster objectives.
Towards this end, Trocaire will work jointly with the Ministry of Health and Community Health Committees to improve staff and health facilities (static and outreach) to provide essential healthcare. This will include building the capacity of MoH team and Community Health Committees to provide these services fully in the near future, equipment of health facilities, widening the reach by breaking geographical, infrastructural, cultural and economic barriers to access to health, investing in prevention measures through vaccination exercises and community awareness-raising, and prepositioning of supplies in anticipation for a crisis.
The programme's intended impact is to: Contribute towards improved health, particularly for women of childbearing age and children under-five, by providing health care services at the targeted facilities in Burdhubo (2 health facilities) and Luuq Districts (2 health facilities). The programme builds on Trócaire's 28 years of experience operating in Gedo. It will work closely with the Ministry of Health and the community through community structures- District Health Boards (DHBs)-, to provide quality healthcare services that meet MoH and Health cluster standards. The DHBs are community-based governance structures that have proven to be an effective and sustainable model in delivering healthcare services in the region. This programme complements Trócaire's existing programme providing the EPHS in Four districts (Belet Xaawo, Dollow, Garbaharey, and Luuq) of Gedo.

Project Activities

Reduce excess morbidity and mortality due to disease outbreak driven by climatic shocks and conflict among 26,493 IDPs and 10,555 nondisplacedActivities:•Support to early detection, investigation, and response to disease (e.g., outbreak) through Rapid response teams (RRTs), HCW training, monitoring, and supplies.•Health care workers trained in trauma and case management.•Community Health workers trained in early cases detection and referral within five days. The training will be conducted in Burdhubo for five days, and the Emergency Response Guideline will be used to train the Community Health workers.•Weekly health Education sessions on behaviour change and communications will be conducted at the Burdhubo Health Centre and Qoney static health facility daily and bi-monthly at the outreach sites. The CHWs from the selected villages will continue with home visits, deliver key health messages, and do referrals and defaulter tracing.•Provision of Immunization services at ten (10) outreach sites in rural villages of Burdhubo and Luuq and two (2) health centers located within the Burdhubo town and Qoney. The vaccines which include Measles, BCG, Pentavalent, Oral polio vaccines. The villages will be served weekly. The team will work with the CHWs drawn from the ten (10) villages.•Conduct training for 8 Health workers on Immunization services for five (5) days. The training package will be as per the Approved MOH guideline on the immunization services. The staff will be drawn from the 2 Health Centres and 2 Outreach team. Contribute to improvement in the physical and mental wellbeing of the population affected by conflict and displacement among 26,493 IDPs and 10,555 nondisplaced, including 1851 people with disabilities Activities•Training of Health Workers and Community Health workers on Mental Health and Psychosocial support.•Minor rehabilitation of the health facility at Qoney Health Centre. The health facility requires minor rehab to be up to the standard to offer the service as per the EPHS standard. The rehabilitation will also minimize occupational health safety and make it a disable friendly facility.•Health awareness, social mobilization, education, and advocacy activities on MHPSS, disabilities. Improve equitable access to quality essential lifesaving and emergency health services for the crisis-affected population to 26,493 IDPs and 10,555 million nondisplaced host populations.Activities:•Provision of lifesaving primary care health care services through fixed and mobile outreach services to 37,048 (18,820 females, 18,288 male beneficiaries. The service will be offered through 2 mobile Outreach clinic that will serve 20 rural villages and 2 Static Health Centre located at Burdhubo and Qoney Village in Luuq.•Health care workers training on Somali Standard Treatment Guidelines. The training will be conducted in Luuq town for five days. The trainers will use STG guidelines developed by WHO and MoH Somalia. Trainees will receive a copy of the standard treatment guide.•Procurement of essential medical supplies and equipment for the two (2) outreach teams and two (2) health centers.•Conduct training for 8 Health workers on Emergency Obstetric Care for 5 days, The training package will be as per the Approved MOH guideline on the EmOC (Emergency Obstetric Care). The staff will be drawn from the Health Centres and Outreach teams. •Conduct training for 6 Health workers on Neonatal resuscitation on the job training. The training package will be as per the Approved MOH guideline on the New-born care. The staff will be drawn from the Health Centre and Outreach team. Improved case management and referral services for 100 survivors of sexual or gender-based violence.Activities•Provision of sexual and reproductive health (SRH) services (including for SGBV) to the vulnerable population in Burdhubo and Luuq. This service will be in line with the MoH guidelines.•Conduct CMR training for 5 Health workers.
