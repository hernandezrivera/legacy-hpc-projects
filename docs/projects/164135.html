Project Summary

The project aims to invest in available local resources, and focus on dealing with the major challenges facing the olive sector in the target communities, specifically in relation to costs at all stages of production. In addition, the project aims to overcome the gaps of knowledge about the best agricultural practices for the olive sector through Integrated Olive Management. In addition, supporting the local community including the olive farmers to access production assets in Afrin district in 12 villages
Through the following steps:
- increase the food security
- Distributing the manure of animals
- Distributing agricultural input vouchers
- Distributing vouchers to cover the cost of oil extraction
- Capacity building
- supervision on the farms of olive farmers
- Contracting with 200 seasonal workers
- Mapping the presses that need rehabilitation
- Supporting the marketing of olive products
- Conducting awareness sessions

4 - Needs

6.7 million Syrians are food insecure and an additional 2.7 million people are at risk of food insecurity, loss and lack of livelihoods and reduced production capacity which in turn results in limited physical and financial access to food (HNO, 2020)The population is 28000 including 33% IDPs. Currently, the targeted location is under Turkish government control and remains stable and with acceptable levels of security allowing for high numbers of IDPs to find refuge95% of the population in the targeted location was dependent on agricultural activities (especially olives).According to needs assessment analysis, the main problems are as following:•The high cost of compost, pesticides, pruning, and milling services, leading to an increase in production costs. •The high cost of fuel for supplementary irrigation, that led to the olive farmers depending on only rain to water the trees as a negative adaptation•The spread of pests (insect diseases, and fungi diseases) ..•Marketing difficulties.The needs assessment also showed that the target areas are villages lacking agricultural support, in addition, these areas are difficult to access for most organizations, due to the need to request formal approval from the Turkish government. need assessment results showed that people mainly depend on olive production as a source of income. In addition, there is the availability of irrigation sourcesThe high prices of agricultural inputs (fertilizers, fuel, pesticides, have led to the reluctance of farmers to take care of their olive trees, which has led to the spread of many agricultural diseases.FGDs revealed women within Afrin district are shouldering much of the economic burden and striving to ensure livelihoods for themselves and their families. The survey conducted by the M&E staff in these regions, in the local community which includes the olive farmer, found the project areas include large holdings of olive trees for farmers who lack agricultural support

5 - Implementation of activities

the Project is designed to increase the food security and enhance the livelihoods of IDPs and host communities-Distributing the manure of animals to produce organic fertilizer - Distributing agricultural input vouchers (2 types of pesticide vouchers (insecticide, and fungicide), in addition to 2 fuel vouchers for supplementary irrigation), accompanied by related training. - Distributing vouchers to cover the cost of oil extraction. - Capacity building targeting male and female farmers and local community members. - supervision on the farms of olive farmers during the production cycle of olives and provide technical advice and periodic follow-up necessary for them.-Contracting with 200 seasonal workers (women) of IDPs to harvest 1,800 dunums of olives for 600 HHs, accompanied by related training. Also, Basma Organization will contract 50 seasonal workers (men) from IDPs, to prune 1,800 donums of olives for 600 HHs, accompanied by related training-Mapping the presses that need rehabilitation to contract two of them regarding the selection criteria.BRD will cover the cost of rehabilitation of their presses. After rehabilitation of these presses, BRD will recoup the costs of presses rehabilitation from the total costs of vouchers of oil extract (BRD will also contract again presses owners to extract the oil of olive farmers beneficiaries)- Supporting the marketing of olive products by providing training about the marketing of olive products. In addition to establishing an exhibition to support the olive farmers for the marketing- Conducting awareness sessions with the vendors and beneficiaries to explain the vouchers' redeeming mechanismsBRD will identify, verify from the beneficiaries through community leaders, and local councils where a long list will be prepared depending on eligibility criteria, then a shortlist will be identified depending on the vulnerability criteria for olive farmers and, seasonal workers (cash for work) beneficiaries

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

The proposed location of intervention is Aleppo governorate - Afrin district-Raju sub district in12 villages (Raju, Beit Adin, Haj Khalil, Atman, Meidan Ekbis, Big Siwan, Maamel – Oshagi, Sheikh Mohammed, Tholathiyeh, Birein - Jtal Qoyo, Dawdaw and, Janjaleh). BRD has a specialized Safety and Security department to ensure safety of staff, beneficiaries and volunteers, and mitigate damage when not avoidable. Risk management in BRD is based on a scientific basis through the development of a risk matrix during project design that includes all possible risks as well as risk mitigation mechanisms. Also, weekly reports to be prepared for the management of the organization and partners, in addition of course to security updates and incident reports, if any. The S&S department follows any developments in the project area, conducts a daily risk assessment of the area and informs the administration of any possible scenarios and how to deal with them, including evacuation and contingency plans and training the field staff on all expected scenarios. The S&S department is committed to all humanitarian principles, especially neutrality and impartiality, and it strictly follows the JOPs principles, and worked to prevent any kind of intervention by the controlling armed groups or any other de facto authority. The S&S department is careful to choose the centers that achieve the highest levels of safety.
