Project Summary

Basma for relief and development (BRD) will support a planned established camps for the 5,400 newly displaced populations affected by the ongoing conflict (2 camps yearly in same sub district - community), including forced evacuations, and provide CCCM services for the IDPs living in those 2 camps in addition to management of the data and information of the IDPs in those 2 camps as well as collect and management of the data of other non CCCM services provided to the IDPs including temporary shelter, non-food items, WASH, Food, Protection, Health and other in Idleb Governorate.
BRD will start providing CCCM activities and services for the IDPs as following:
Set in place proper staffing/management personnel to conduct CCCM activities, including registration/data collection, vulnerability mapping, verification, establishment of gender-balanced camp governance/ participation management structures, and accountability mechanisms, routine site renovations Procedure of Reception of IDP’s in the camp Once IDP families arrive at the camps.
Availability of the tents in those camps, some tents to reception the new family until providing them with needed items and tents while we taking thier information.
All those activities in addition to BRD will establish fire response committees -2 committees.
2- Establishment of contingency planning with firefighting system and evacuation plan
BRD will organize on the ground on effective fire prevention, mitigation and response training/workshops for camp management staff 2- Organize ‎trainings through develop a fire warden system at IDP sites, including identifying and training wardens and back up wardens on roles and ‎responsibilities‎
In addition BRD will be responsible to report monthly about those 2 camps yearly, as well as in case of any risk will face those camps BRD will report directly.
BRD will target the BNs in two camps yearly in Maaret Tamsrin sib district, Harbanush which the process of establishing going, which will be follow the methodology managing the camps and targeting the IDPs who are considering as most vulnerable people.
BRD will target the BNs who displaced many times, newly IDPs, IDPs who have no shelter, the camps established recently and targeted the same criteria of BRD selection criteria, Families headed by women, PwDs, ..ETC.(selection criteria).
Discrimination:Ensure there is a map showing locations of BNs who are in real need of assistance so that communication with BNs is proactive and based on knowledge We will ensure to put in-place mechanisms for any concern and feedback of the targeted community, and other stakeholders as well, including; dedicated email Address, WA number, anonymous online forms, complaints box (when appropriate), and interviews. MEAL officer, will be responsible for complaints. All evaluation and selection processes of BNs, will be announced and circulated to target communities. LAs will also be consulted on selection criteria to ensure appropriate and inclusive access to services. The project prioritized safety and dignity of BNs targeted and aim to mainstream protection and GBV from early stages of project design -Training centers are accessible, safe, and secured location for M/W. Encourage participation of all community members (IDPs, HCs) in project activities. Participation of M/F and PWDs in measuring intervention impacts.
BRD planning for those 2 camps yearly to deliver the services for the most vulnerable IDPs regarding the situation of IDPs which they facing.
Importance of those activities to deliver the services for the most vulnerable IDPs, new sites and BNs need to be provided with those CCCM services, to achieve the goal of saving lives (through information management, fire extinguishers, fire committees, ..and others.) and protect the IDPs through providing them with needed services.

4 - Needs

According to CCCM Cluster tracked displacement of 615K-116K to end of21, Idleb especially has experienced a significant IDP influx This inflow of IDPs is tied to North Idleb, which led to movement of up to 615K individuals. Thus, residents in such camp-like settings are amongst most vulnerable, continuous influx of evacuees from South of Idleb has raised demand for shelter, food, and NFIs. Additionally, the influx has led to a shortage of drinking water, basic food and NFI, in particular for the evacuees from South of Idleb.According to HNO22, child recruitment is a prominent concern in 42 % of 262 sub-districts and children are often the main breadwinners.As hostilities persist, more IDPs will likely exhaust their household resources and take refuge in camps, informal tented settlements IDP sites have reached capacity or already have exceeded optimal population density conditions. Many camps damaged during winter, many tents burned, many camps getting double services, Irregular services. Those aspects directing BRD to deliver CCCM services in those camps about to be finished. Set in place proper staffing/management personnel to conduct CCCM activities, 2- Establishment contingency planning with firefighting system and others, (as those camps considered 0 point of reaching the newly IDPs which BRD has to register them and recording their data as well as provide them with needed items, Targeting methodology for newly displaced people and women who head families first)

5 - Implementation of activities

BRD will serve as CM agency, and set in place proper staffing/management personnel to conduct CCCM activities, including registration/data collection, vulnerability mapping, verification, establishment of gender-balanced camp governance/ participation management structures, and accountability mechanisms, routine site renovations Procedure of Reception of IDP’s in camp Once IDPs arrive at camp, the following procedures will be followed:The families arriving to camp will pass through registration reception tent to be registered, and undertake a profiling questionnaire with focus on identifying vulnerable and special cases. Registered families will also be given a registration card Vulnerable cases (disabilities, elderly) will be given priority during the process They and their family will be allocated a family tent near to access points. BRD will conduct workshop to start project. BRD will organize monthly planning and progress meetings and monitor activities through periodic field visits by MEAL team. The methods used for data collection and observing changes will be beneficiary interviews PDM after MEAL team will ensure community participation during the project implementation through activated feedback mechanism, where many channels will be available includes but mot limited, WhatsApp, email, and field team. Feedback mechanism will support the accountability within the project staff and targeted camps.BRD will implement activities in 22 in 2 camps and 23 in other 2 camps.

BRD will attain high level of accountability through engagement of BNs in decision-making and information sharing mechanism.
