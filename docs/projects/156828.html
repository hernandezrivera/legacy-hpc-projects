Project Summary

The main objective of this project is to ensure the safety of children in the winter and cold / hot weather and mitigate the effects of the harsh atmosphere through the distribution of vouchers through which the affected families will be able to secure the clothing of their children for free and non-refundable way through the distribution of clothing with the involvement of parents and their children visiting the distribution point and choosing the color and measurement and shape appropriate for them.
This project will serve the most needy and weak groups that are the children of males and females and people with special needs and homeless.
The total number of targetted families are 8000 meaning 28000 children including 2900 children with disability

Needs

Following to MSJM assessments :The deterioration of living conditions and the lack of purchasing power of most families affected by the crisis as a result of the deterioration of their economic situation and the absence or lack of sources of income in thousands of them, negatively affect the living situation, life and health of its members, which leads during winter periods to the inability of parents to ensure the needs of their children, especially the young one, of winter clothing to protects them from the harsh cold, causing permanent increase in cases of illness or even deaths depending on the situation and the place where families live. around one million people were living in what is considered shelters of last resort, that is collective shelters, camps or makeshift settlement.

Implementation of activities

1- Identify and register beneficiaries according to UNICEF standards, following a severity scale. The families that will be selected are among the most vulnerable (women household, children with disability, orphans, homeless, without any ressources)2. Distribution of vouchers to beneficiaries through a comprehensive registration process that requires verification of national identity numbers. 3-Procurement and Purchasing of the clothes and transporting them safely to location. 4- Distribution in-kind of the clothes vs the vouchers of the beneficiaries5- M&E Process. 6- Mid-term narrative report. 7- Final narrative Report.

Protection Risk Analysis

1- Explosive devicesMitigation: Prior to a distribution, make sure that distribution sites and routes to/from distribution sites are free from explosive hazards by consulting security forces, local authorities and mine action organizations2- Tension between the beneficiaries during the distributionMitigation Communicate before and during distribution with local officials/leaders and the community about ourrole, procedures, criteria and shortages to avoid causing conflict and tension 3- Crowd and chaos during the registration or the distributionMitigationEstablish multiple distribution sites to avoid creating crowds and beneficiaries crossing conflict lines ortravelling long distances; choose locations with shading, sex-segregated toilets, drinking water andfunctional health facilities nearbyEstablish multiple storage sites to enable quick distributionsConsider house-to-house or tent-to-tent distributions with female staff present or transportation supportfor people who are unable to leave their homes or have movement limitations (e.g., PWD, elderly,pregnant women, female- and child-headed households)

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Delay in the delivery of the goodsMitigating measures : we will underline a timeline in the MOU with the vendor that won the tender Difficulty of the beneficiaries to arrive to the distribution center : Mitigating measures :MSJM will open more than a distribution center and will provide if necessary a door to door deliveryThe convoy of goods may be facing security issues -no permission - halted in nowhere - Mitigating measures: MSJM will study the path of the convoy and will secure all the permissions before the distribution
