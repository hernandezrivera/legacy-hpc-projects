Project Summary

According to the Assessment report by IRC’s Addis Ababa Emergency Response Technical Team in collaboration with the East Bale Zone Drought Impact Task Force in Dawe Serer, Legahidha and Sewena Woreda, in January,2022, a total of 114,788 people and 720,200 livestock are in extreme need of emergency assistance. The zone has seven woredas and one town with a total population of 717,929 people of which five of the Seven woredas are pastoralist woredas and known for their food insecurity and WASH challenges. With support from Sida under HFA V Year 1 RRM funding, IRC Ethiopia’s Adama Field Office has implemented an integrated lifesaving WASH and economic recovery intervention reaching 40,000 affected clients through water trucking, water scheme rehabilitation, hygiene promotion, water line connections with health and school facilities, and provision of unconditional multipurpose cash response for the critically affected people by drought in Legahidha and Sewena of East Bale and Shalla and Siraro of West Arsi. The project was designed based on the rainy season bringing rain. This intervention has phased out to end on March 9th, 2022, but with the continuation of the drought, the humanitarian needs are still acute and are getting worse, putting a greater number of people in need of assistance.
IRC proposed a project with the continuation of the recently ended SIDA project aiming that People affected by climate shocks are protected from Water, Sanitation and Hygiene (WASH) related diseases. And meet their basic needs and avoid negative coping strategies, The IRC will achieve this aim by increase access to safe drinking water to a high number of people through water trucking, minor maintenance of two water schemes (hand pumps), Expansion of two pipeline, intensive hygiene promotion activities and the provision of essential hygiene kits to 800 households (HHs) including child-headed households, women-headed households and other vulnerable households like people living with disabilities, elderly and those living with HIV/AIDS. Simultaneously, the project will provide unconditional multipurpose cash assistance either through bank account or the cash in envelope modality (administered by the Financial Service Providers (FDPs)) for the most affected 1980 HHs, to support them to access their basic needs which includes but not is limited to food, health care services, scholastic materials for their children, milling costs among others. The modality will be decided based upon FSPs’ availability to deliver cash and the preference of the beneficiaries.by doing the proposed activity a total of 6,071 HH or 30,355 individuals without double counting will be Benefited with in the coming Six Months( 15th March to 15th of Septemper,2022)
