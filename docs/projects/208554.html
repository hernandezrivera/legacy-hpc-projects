Project Summary

The project is designed to assess the environment and living condition of pregnant and lactating low-income women, provide protection and medication against malaria. The project also covers series of counselling and quality health education in a local language settings. Women are selected from any age group, provided they are pregnant or lactating. The intervention in targeted at women only. At the end of this project, we expect that women are malaria-free, healthy and equipped with knowledge to train other women through gestation periods. We are building a sustainable community resilience against malaria and similar epidemics.

1 - Needs

A view of the health issues facing the population lead to agreed priorities and resource allocation to improve the resilience of pregnant and lactating women. The target population is medical known to be susceptible to diseases due to lowered immunity within the gestation period. A statistics of 7 out of 10 women were diagnosed to have undergone significant malaria disease. The prevalence of malaria was also traceable to environmental factors such as untreated waterlogs, unprotected settlements, lack of mosquito nets, lack of insecticides lack of protective wears. The need for the intervention becomes a priority that demands urgent solicitations.

2 - Activities

The project response is viewed as critical and borders around the survival of the beneficiaries. Women and foetus or infants are at mortal risk due to preventable and curable diseases. The World Health Statistics shows an estimated 619,000 malaria deaths globally in 2021, which show a slight decline as compared to 2020 which stands at 625,000. Responses around the world such as this one would keep the global figure declining until the globe is malaria free. MODALITIES: The project will run independently, providing a survey interview and identity documents at initial stage to onboard beneficiaries. The beneficiaries are placed on health support throughout the period of the project. The health status of all beneficiary would be verified and recorded on a monthly report bases while other infrastructures and supplies will be provided and utilized accordingly. Our overall aim is to ensure a healthy pregnancy and lactating period in low-income women which ultimately declines the global statistics of malaria death records.

9.1a - Specify (and give URL link if possible to) the relevant needs assessment document.

A simple malaria map-shrinking feasibility assessment was conducted in a friendly method with local malaria program workers in pursuing elimination, with past and present elimination practices. Data was collected from previously and presently pregnant women. The assessment shows the significant prevalence and high mortality index of malaria parasite among pregnant and lactating women as a result of common low immunity during pregnancy. An environmental and shelter assessment also shows a deplorable condition of vulnerability to malaria. Cases of untreated water logs and uncovered road side drainages. The response to use of mosquito nets and insecticide was significantly poor in ration to the whole population.
