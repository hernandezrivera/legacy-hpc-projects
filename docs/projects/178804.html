Project Summary

As the food security situation in Syria continues to worsen, with a further deterioration of key food security indicators seen over the first two months of 2021, Syrian households rely on increasingly severe negative coping strategies to cover their essential food needs. The Project aims to improve the food security & livelihood status of most vulnerable people through life-sustaining food assistance & income-generating opportunities with special focus on female-headed households, people with disabilities, elderly people, &daily workers who lost their jobs during COVID-19 outbreak, through the distribution of food vouchers targeted 5125PIN (1025HHs) in the highest severity scales locations in Aleppo, Rural Damascus, & Hama.
In purpose of providing supplementary food assistance to Persons with Specific Needs (PSN) through appropriate modalities & based on the conducted assessment in the targeted locations, TdH will distribute specialized Food voucher (Malnutrition voucher) for cases of malnutrition (identified by other TDH project in the area of implementation) in order to support the malnutrition treatment, especially when they are fully dependent on food aid. The size & composition of the food voucher is tailored to local preferences, demographic profile, activity levels, climatic conditions, local coping capacity, existing levels of malnutrition. It is designed to meet the nutritional requirements of a population rather than individuals (who can be targeted through tailored nutrition programs). When people are fully dependent on food assistance, the standard is to provide 2,100 calories per person,10-12 percent of which come from protein, 17percent from fat (according to WHO/FAO guidelines), micronutrients such as vitamin A, iron, iodine & zinc. As for beneficiaries who are partially dependent on the food aid, the program only aims to “top up” food that is available, based on an understanding of current access to food. A supplementary ration is mostly given to a vulnerable group such as malnutrition children under 5 years of age and/or PLWs.
The amount of each voucher will be 200,000 SYP according to WFP Minimum Expenditure Basket for 8 Cycles/Months according to Food Security Sector minimum standard & CWG.TdH will Conduct a market assessment in the targeted locations and contracting with the appropriate once.
In the purpose of providing income-generating opportunities & based on the conducted assessment in the targeted location, Tdh will conduct specialized vocational training for 420persons (60 from each location) mainly for PwDs & Female-Headed households & daily workers for three months in a training center in the same location taking into consideration COVID-19 Prevention measures. Training subjects will be chosen based on the outputs of the conducted needs assessment in the targeted locations & beneficiaries list from the food vouchers distributions will be nominated by the community committees & verified through household visits by the local partner volunteers in order to avoid any duplication with any humanitarian actors working in the same neighborhoods.
Tdh will coordinate with other FSA actors to ensure there is no duplication in receiving the services. TdH regularly participating to the cash working group, & we will share 5Ws report reflecting the achievements with food sector for more efficient & effectiveness in response & coordination. All the activities will be supervised by TdH staff and will be carried out in coordination with the local partner to guarantee a continuous collaboration, also M&E staff will conduct field visits regularly, also, M&E Officer will make active calls or personal interviews to a random sample of beneficiaries (20% of the targeted beneficiaries) to collect information, feedback from beneficiaries on how they feel about the project. Moreover, determine whether the local partner adhered to the agreed targeting criteria and the level of beneficiary participation in the targeting process

4 - Needs

Based on HNO 2021, the situation in Syria has drastically deteriorated as the estimated sector PiN for 2021 – of food insecure plus those at risk of food insecurity – has increased from 9.8 million to 14.2 million.The protracted crisis in Syria has intensified needs over the past year over a complex and deepening socio-economic crisis, lack of livelihood and income generating options, knock-on effects of COVID-19 as well as the magnitude of the food security situation affecting dietary diversity as well as maternal health and nutrition status.The ongoing conflict in the North of the country and major losses in the food production sector. Also, the economic crisis has seen a particularly devastating impact on the South and Central governorates. In addition to an increase in the vulnerability of households in Northeast Syria. The results of the national assessment showed that 50% of Syrian household reported spending at least 75% of their income on food, while 28% are adopting crisis or emergency livelihood coping strategies.Food insecurity is likely to increase in urban areas as well, where the majority of people rely on markets, which are increasingly operating under difficult economic conditions with high food prices and the majority of food commodities such as rice, wheat flour, vegetable cooking oil and sugar, being imported. The high food prices, coupled with lack of income-generating opportunities, will worsen the situation. Accordingly, TdH is planning to intervene in the distribution of food vouchers in the following locations taking the severity sore into consideration: Jebel Saman, Hama, As-Suqaylabiyah, Qudsiya, Haran Al'awameed, Az-Zabdani, Madaya.

5 - Implementation of activities

The proposed intervention is aligned with the SO1: To Scale-up and sustain minimum food consumption needs through life-saving and life-sustaining food assistance, and SO2: Support self-reliance of affected households by protecting and building productive assets and restoring or creating income generating opportunities to save and sustain lives.through systematic and sustained humanitarian response with ensuring immediate as well as consistent access to food to the most food-insecure people, thus the project activities will be in line with Food sector and the CWG priorities and recommendations as follows:Provision of food voucher assistance to food-insecure households in underserved locations with the highest severity levels of food insecurity for 8 Cycles/Months.Specialized food voucher will be provided to PLWs with Moderate Acute Malnutrition MAM, Caregivers of children with SAM &/or MAM, & female-headed households for 8 Cycles.Vocational training will be conducted for most vulnerable people according to the results of the assessment which will target 420 PWD & female-headed households out of the Job seekers with no profession backgrounds in consulting with community committees in each location to provide them with specific Vocational Training where livelihood sector was reported as a priority needs in the mentioned communities.Meanwhile, the targeted locations considered as a priority Food sector: (4 Severity scale as HNO2021).TdH will follow up closely the prices and variance in the market through regular and spot-check market assessment through the program staff and M&E officer in order to keep aligned with the power of purchasing of the voucher and will coordinate with the donor and local partner accordingly.
