Project Summary

Since March 2020, the already challenged healthcare system in the oPt is experiencing added strain
from the COVID-19 outbreak. The reallocation of already scarce resources to respond to the pandemic,
along with the contagion of healthcare workers, has undermined people’s access to health. Direct
mortality from the virus and indirect mortality from preventable diseases is of major concern. Overall,
the pandemic has rendered an estimated 115,000 people in need of health humanitarian assistance.
In Gaza, the rapid spread of the virus within the community since late August has posed a heavy burden
on a healthcare system, already over-stretched due to 13 years of Israeli blockade, internal Palestinian
divide, recurrent hostilities and violence. The care of non-communicable diseases has been postponed, affecting more than 64,000 people. About 3,500 elective surgeries per month are being postponed. In tandem, there are on average approximately 2,000 elective surgeries added per month to the waiting list, which has reached 11,400 surgeries.
To respond to this need, Al Ahli Hospital (AAH) in Gaza has proven capacities as a surgical hospital with a high reputation in safe surgical management. It has operated as a surgical hospital for 140 years and has been an implementing partner for three previous Country Based Pooled Fund (CBPF) projects either in elective surgeries, trauma management and limb reconstruction.
Therefore, AAH with the support of Augusta Victoria Hospital (AVH) operated by the Lutheran World Federation (LWF), found it crucial to support vulnerable women, men, boys and girls who are now on the waiting list of the Ministry of Health (MoH). Coordination was done with MoH to conduct 1,700 surgeries. 1,000 will be ENT. The rest are advanced Minimally Invasive Surgeries (MIS), general surgery, orthopedic, gynecology, and urology. Orthopedic surgeries will include anterior cruciate ligament (ACL) which is a major, advanced, and expensive surgery. Moreover urology surgeries are complicated surgeries and cost lot of money.
AVH will further develop AAH’s capacity in Infection Prevention Control (IPC) and rational uses of antibiotics, especially with Palestine's current COVID 19 emergency situation.
AVH will train 30 staff from AAH either through coaching, mentoring and onsite demonstrations. AVH is aiming to visit AAH 3 times, the 1st for follow up visit/training and another 2 visits for follow up.
LWF has strong financial, monitoring and accountability systems in place and adheres to policies ensuring sustainable quality programming, such as the Code of Conduct, Child Protection, Core Humanitarian Standards, Gender Justice and Complaints Response Mechanism (CRM) all to be shared with AAH.
Furthermore, AAH will work with Handicap International to develop a checklist to determine the People With Disabilities (PWD), to track and report on them. GBV cases will be referred to specialized centers to address this issue.
Scenario risk analysis was done based on previous experiences and similar projects and based on the new proposed COVID 19 possible scenarios as mitigations were planned such as remote management with AVH.
Coordination with MoH, the Health Cluster (HC), Women’s Centers, the Union of Health Work Committees (UHWC) for complementarity and referrals are well defined in the project.
Regarding finance and procurement, AAH has implemented successfully USAID projects, CBPF projects and followed either USAID or OCHA guidelines and has a strong PMER (planning, monitoring, evaluating, reporting) system in place.
This project links to Health cluster Obj. 1 Ensure the availability of acceptable and quality essential healthcare services to vulnerable communities in oPt. and to strategic obj.2: , meeting
health needs and providing basic health services.

1 - Needs

In Gaza, the rapid spread of the virus within the community since late August has posed a heavy burdenon a healthcare system, already over-stretched due to 13 years of Israeli blockade, internal Palestiniandivide, recurrent hostilities and violence. In the first half of 2020, 43 per cent of essential medicines atGaza’s Central Drugs Store were completely out of stock. Due to the chronic electricity deficit, Gaza’shealthcare facilities continue to rely on fuel donations to run backup generators during black-outs.Efforts to contain the outbreak have undermined the delivery of other essential healthcare services. InGaza, only 19 out of 51 primary healthcare centers are functioning. Healthcare centers have beenconverted into COVID-19 quarantine and isolation centers, while some 400 doctors, nurses andadministrative staff were mobilized to support their operation. The care of non-communicable diseasesand palliative care has been postponed, affecting more than 64,000 people. About 3,500 elective surgeries per month are being postponed. In tandem, there are on average approximately 2,000 electivesurgeries added per month to the waiting list, which has reached 11,400 surgeries. To respond to this need, AAH is committed to conduct 1000 ENT surgeries: 830 moderate/100 major/ 70 supra major. Also, AAH has further capacity to conduct Gynecology and general surgeries: 200; Orthopedic including anterior cruciate ligament (ACL):300, Minimally Invasive Surgeries (MIS): 100, and urology;100. AAH is committed to conduct 1,700 surgeries in total. AAH has also been equipped recently with a state-of-the-art MIS unit for general surgery/gynecology/orthopedic/ENT. As such, AAH has achieved major and supra major ENT surgeries that may reach $2-3000 USD which are unaffordable for most Gazans. The soonest appointment for such cases is also the end of 2021. Moreover, children in need for moderate and major surgeries who can’t access services in a reasonable time may have complications in heart or kidneys later in life leading to lifetime disabilities. The AVH/AAH partnership adds value in developing AAH’s capacities of Infection Prevention Control (IPC), a needed project segment now during the COVID 19 pandemic. Finally, AVH will work with AAH on the rational use of antibiotics, which is essential to mention that previous successes were recorded for AAH and AVH in IPC as both managed Osteomyelitis cases successfully and prevented proposed amputations, a project referenced in the trauma report of WHO and the HC. As such, AVH as a leading hospital in infection prevention and AAH as an implementing hospital want to continue such cooperation in doing safe surgeries for Gaza's needy citizens.

2 - Activities

The activities related directly to performing the elective surgeries are: 1- Conduct Polymerase chain reaction (PCR) tests for suspected patients ( COVID-19 tests) prior to their admission to the hospital. ( around 500 tests)2- Conduct 1700 elective surgeries including women, men, boys and girls1) E.N.T - surgeries (830 Moderate, 100 Major, and 70 supra major)2) Orthopedic including ACL- 3003) General and Gynecology Surgery - 2004) Minimal Invasive Surgeries - 1005) Urology - 100 2- Provide postoperative care in the departments as inpatients and follow up through outpatient clinics (OPDs) including dressing & nursing care, medical follow up until Physical therapy stage and full recovery. 3-AVH team to conduct project, procurement and financial monitoring visits (if possible). AVH will undertake procurement and procurement support to AAH relative to the three main tenders for medicine and medical equipment. AVH will also undertake financial transfers, management support and reporting the progress of the project. If onsite visits are not possible by AVH's staff, AVH/AAH staff will undertake remote monitoring and cooperative work using online software like ZOOM or SKYPE. AVH staff will all time register according to the AVH project management system on the administrative budget line segment (PCS).AAH will undertake accountability monitoring which will be reported on according to the cooperative agreement with CBPF in the mid term and final report. 4- daily like activities checklist to be distributed to all patients upon their follow up visits.The survey will measure the ability of patients to perform daily life activities after surgeries. Activities related to building the capacity of 30 of AAH technical team through Augusta Victoria Hospital in Infection prevention and control ( IPC ) and rational use of antibiotics:1- conducting three (3) visits throughout the project cycle and provide assistance to AAH in IPC and other needs due to the needs on the ground. In the event that onsite visits in Gaza are not possible, due to COVID-19 or closures, the training will be undertaken using remote learning tools utilizing online systems like ZOOM and SKYPE. 2-Observation sheets related to hand hygiene to be filled by Infection prevention officers at AAH on a weekly basis.3- surgical site infection checklist to be be filled by Infection prevention officers at AAH on a weekly basis.

3 - Indicators

AAH is a known surgical hospital that provides quality of health care as it has qualified surgeons consultants in general surgery, urology, gynecology, E.N.T and orthopedic as well as AAH has been recently equipped with MIS towers and instrument through the USAID MIS project that was called Gaza2020:health matters. MIS minimize patient suffering, lenghth of bed stay, less pain, and patient can resume quicker personal activities • As opposed to large incisions, you will only need some small cuts with MIS. • There is less trauma to your nerves, tissues, and muscles. • You will experience less bleeding. • After recovery, you will have less scarring. • Your organs will undergo less trauma Moreover, AAH has partnership with LWF/AVH team that includes Infection Prevention Control to ensure high quality provision of services. Also, AAH has been considered recently a trauma hospital for its capacity and role performing and managing sever infected inured wounds and saving them from amputations which was published at WHO trauma report that covered GMR from end of March 2018 till March 2019. The success of the project will be measured through several indicators.Indicators related to performing the elective surgeries will focus mainly on two things. The number of patients who received elective surgeries disaggregated by age and gender, and the percentage of patients who were able tp resume their daily like activities after the surgeries. Those two indicators can be verified through patients' files, tracking sheets, daily life activities checklist, and during follow up visits. The indicators related to building capacity of AAH staff in IPC and other related issues will focus on three things:The number of staff who attended the training disaggregated by gender. The number can be verified by attendance sheet. In addition we will have an indictor that measure AAH compliance with hand hygiene, and another indicator that measure compliance with surgical site infection tool. Both indicators can be verified by checking the tools( sheets) and compare real data with data on the excel sheet.

9 - Protection mainstreaming
9.1 - Do No Harm

Based on International Humanitarian Law (IHL) that includes of Right to Health and Right to Life and equal and impartial access to aid, the Health Cluster which indicated in its Humanitarian Needs Overview (HNO) for 2021 that 11,400 patients who are in need for elective surgeries should be prioritized by health providers with focus on E.N.T.. AVH and AAH want to support Gaza’s vulnerable population by conducting 1,700 electives surgeries to needy patients. These patients have the right of access to health care which is not fully met as the MOH is not able to do these surgeries on time related due to many factors; the13 years blockade on Gaza, 2014 war, the internal Palestinian division, other recent humanitarian crises, COVID-19 pandemic which are depleting the MOH resources. These factors has made a domino effect and shifted the elective scheduled cases which are in need for surgeries to months and even years especially E.N.T cases.

9.2 - Equal and impartial access to aid

Health Cluster has indicated in its HNO for 2021 that 11,400 patients who are in need for elective surgeries should be prioritized by health providers with focus on E.N.T. Therefore, such group is being vulnerable and cases will be referred by MOH based on coordination through referral system.
