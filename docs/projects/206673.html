Project Summary

The project aims to improve the life conditions of families displaced, host communities, returnees families and affected by the conflicts, in Cabo Delgado Province.

The intervention foresees an initial assessment on wash needs for the most needy displaced families, families hosting IDPs fleeing from conflict areas include the new events of IDPs returned to areas of origin. Hygiene & sanitation training and sensitization will be privileged for IDPs accommodated in different sites based on needs identification done in partnership with local authorities and reference clusters.

The beneficiaries identification activities such as registration for kits distribution, will be conducted in coordination with the government and other partners and verified by AVSI WASH and Protection staff in order to guarantee transparency and objectivity in the selection process, avoid duplications and overlapping.

Targeting will be made according to vulnerability criteria e not by status, in order to guarantee the do no harm principle and do no create conflicts among targeted communities.

In all interventions, AVSI will coordinate with the WASH Cluster at national and sub national level, as well as with distrital and municipality structure for all districts proposed. Government bodies involvement, such as Ministério de Obras Públicas, Habitação e Recursos Hidricos and District Services of Planeamento e InfraEstrutura and Servicos Provinciais de Infra-estruturas, Direccao Provincial das Obras Públicas will be done to ensure smooth coordination, facilitate prioritization and promoting ownership of local authorities. Where possible, in order to promote localization and in line with AVSI approach, interventions will be done with (with, not through) national partner as CSOs possibly reinforcing existing established.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project aims to improve life conditions of families displaced, host communities, returnees families and affected by the conflict, epidemics or natural disasters in Cabo Delgado Province. Each intervention will be based on results of initial assessment (performed either directly by AVSI or by partners) on wash needs for the affected communities in targeted areas. Diversified and integrated WASH services will be provided to the affected population to ensure that are delivered and received in a dignified and accessible manner, taking into account their beliefs and culture. Higiene & sanitation training and sensitization will be privileged for IDPs in accommodation sites where needs are identified, in close partnership with local authorities and reference clusters. For individual action, such as distribution of kits, the beneficiaries lists will be elaborated in coordination with the government and verified by AVSI WASH and Protection staff in order to guarantee transparency and objectivity in the selection process, avoid duplications and overlapping during the distributions. When possible, Cash Transfer approach will be used in the project to stimulate the local economy, reduces implementation/delivery costs and the same time will bring more dignified distributions process of WASH supplies, as affected populations will have the freedom to spend according to their own priorities. AVSI will use an integrated approach with other sectors and leverage on other complementary intervention particularly in the sectors of protection and education. Pupils will be trained together with the teachers in school health and hygiene in order to implement a school WASH program and in these schools near the centres AVSI will guarantee the existence of minimum WASH conditions to accommodate children. AVSI will target 121.919 people, IDPs, host community and returned families among the districts of Balama (4.000), Cidade de Pemba (25.000), Metuge (40,000), Ancuabe (4.500), Ibo (9.000), Quissanga (8.000), Namuno (2419), Meluco (6.000), Mocmboa da Praia (11.000 and Palma 12.000). In all interventions, AVSI will coordinate with the WASH Cluster at national and sub national level, as well as with distrital and municipality for all distritic proposed, the Ministério de Obras Públicas, Habitação e Recursos Hidricos and District Services of Planeamento e InfraEstrutura and Servicos Provinciais de Infra-estruturas, Direccao Provincial das Obras Públicas to ensure smooth coordination, facilitate prioritization and avoid overlapping of activities. AVSI will implement the activities under this project following the Mozambican WASH Cluster response strategy and standards for emergency interventions and will regularly report results to the WASH Cluster.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

For each of the proposed districts, an integrated WASH intervention will be carried out in all its components, focusing on activities such as Provision emergency access to water through Water trucking, rehabilitation of small water systems, Distribution of WASH NFI kits, Construction or rehabilitation of water systems, Hygiene Promotion activities, Construction of new borehole with handpump, Rehabilitation of handpump, training of WASH comities and creating jobs through Cash For Work approach.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The project will ensure protection mainstreaming process in order to esure DO NO HARM approach, promoting meaningful access, safety and dignity in all actions. In order to guarantee this principles all the staff and service providers will be trained on the principles and procedures in place and will sign the reference documents such as the Internal Code of Conduct, the Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse Policy (PSEA) and the AVSI Children Safeguarding Policy documents as well as the AVSI lead protection mainstreaming checklists. In order to avoid conflicts between IDPs (new or extended) and host communities, targeting for individual assistance will be done in consultation with the communities, defining together the criteria of vulnerability and ensuring that all the different groups are represented in the consultations. This will also ensure the use of the principles of impartiality and neutrality: no distinction of nationality, race, religion, social condition and political affiliation will be made and no sides will be taken in any dispute. Protection monitoring will be guaranteed through routinely monitoring access to services by women, girls, boys and men through spot checks and discussions with communities, and promptly address the obstacles to equal access

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

This project will follow AVSI Foundation SOP described in the AVSI FOUNDATION ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY on ‘feedback and accountability mechanism’. Newly recruited and old staff are always oriented and receive refresher trainings to stress the importance of the opinions and views of communities concerning program implementation. Beneficiaries and the population at large is also made aware of the forms available to provide suggestions and complaints. This project will adopt a community-based and participatory approach. The selection criteria will be developed in consultation with the communities and will aim to upgrade living-conditions within a settlement and when possible through the construction of new shelters. AVSI will conduct the assessment (possibly using electronic format) according to the agreed criteria, which will be reviewed and verified by the local authorities and the representatives of the communities. In AVSI’s experience, this has proven time and again to be particularlyproductive, as not only people’ concerns and priorities are taken into account. 6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

In the present project proposal screening, surveillance and participation of people with disabilities have been included as key activities and indicators. During their implementation, the detection of people with disabilities will be reported (disaggregated by gender and age) and given special attention. In WASH intervention AVSI prioritise disability inclusion as essential to: - achieve the basic human right to water and sanitation; - reach the most vulnerable - set the tone for inclusion in other community-based projects - achieve Sustainable Development Goal. In all project interventions, the inclusion of people with disabilities will be ensured, it will be a priority to have at least one person with a disability on this and will be required the disability friendly add-ons for PLWDs thus addressing training on inclusion for all staff and other stakeholders. Access to water and sanitation for all. AVSI focus on assuring disability inclusion in the proposed WASH intervention form the identification and selection process up to the adaption of the WASH facilities for persons with disabilities (latrines, handwashing points).; up to sensitization messages who will always focused on inclusivity.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

No project is exempt from damaging the environment, but AVSi will ensure that this damage is minimised. AVSI aims to promote a sustainable and healthy environment in order to improve the wellbeing of the community. The water caption systems will be done in a sustainable way, respecting the integrity of natural sources and ensuring that the process of disposing of waste wate is also safe by instalation of communitie process of water treatment. Local procurement of materials will be a positive contribution to local economies. Moreover, with a strong community engagement and participation; on rehabilitation activities, specific attention regarding the site selection and sourcing of materials, favoring the use of local resources. In addition of that, the use of local materials for construction of Wash facilities will be prioritized in order to adopt an environment-friendly approach by trying to increase awareness in the affected communities.The process of latrines construction will always take into account the protection of aquifers, whether through architectural design or the physical location of the latrines, while also ensuring that they are appropriately decommissioned after use. Will be promoted the use of reusable sanitary pads for menstrual hygiene management, not only for economic and sustainability reasons but also to reduce the production and proliferation of waste in communities. WASH NFI Kits will be distributed in no plastic bag and in general use of plastic will be limited in the implementation of the activities as well as in the operation. For this, AVSI can rely to an internal Policy on environment on May 11th 2020.
