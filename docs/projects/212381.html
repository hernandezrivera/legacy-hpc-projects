Project Summary

In alignment with the sector objectives, this project aims to support vulnerable conflict-affected populations in Syria to achieve self-sufficiency through implementing the following activities:
1- Supporting income-generating activities by providing agricultural grants to farmers to start-restart their agricultural business. This activity will target about 980 beneficiaries in Aleppo, Dar’a, Deir ez-Zor, Hama, Idleb, Lattakia, and Rural Damascus.
2- Providing agricultural inputs for family farming activities, targeting 290 beneficiaries in Aleppo, Dar’a, and Deir ez-Zor governorates.
3- Providing training on the best agricultural practices for 770 beneficiaries in Hama, Lattakia, Deir ez-Zor, and Rural Damascus.
4- Rehabilitating the Olive seedling nursery in Dar’a, Ashajara, benefiting about 15,000 individuals.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

PUI aims to build a program to meet the needs of girls, boys, women and men living in intervention locations, taking into consideration vulnerabilities, to enhance their access to basic services, foster their self-sufficiency, but also promote social cohesion while ensuring that the program would have no negative side effects and that the risk for girls, boys, women and men to be exposed to any kind of violence when accessing assistance would be mitigated. PUI policies and operational guidelines align with IASC's Commitments to Accountability to Affected Populations. PUI ensures accountability by placing affected populations at the center of all decisions and actions that affect their lives. Thus, targeted populations will be involved throughout all phases of the project to feed into the design of the interventions and guide the identification of both activities and beneficiaries based on PUI’s selection criteria.
