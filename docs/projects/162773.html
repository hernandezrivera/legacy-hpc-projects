Project Summary

The 2018/2019 poor agricultural season resulting from drought and mid-season dry spells and the prevailing macro-economic environment characterized by food shortages, high food prices, fuel shortages and inflation has adversely impacted on the Zimbabwe education sector. Food insecurity and economic hardships have contributed in pupils missing classes and parents failing to meet basic education requirements for their children. Gutu and Mwenezi (Masvingo province) and Mberengwa districts (Midlands province) are among the worst affected.
CARE seeks to contribute in ECD and primary school education outcomes through school feeding, child safeguarding support and promotion and advocacy interventions in Gutu, Mwenezi and Mberengwa. The project running from January to December 2020 will reach 61 538 boys and girls, female and male health coordinators and parents representatives. Deliberate attempt will be made to ensure inclusion and meaningful participation of pupils living with disabilities. The project theory of change (TOC) is “If school feeding and child safe-guarding is enhanced then school attendance, concentration and retention increases resulting in improved education outcomes”. The specific activities for school feeding objective will include capacity building relevant school stakeholders, supporting 20 satellite schools in construction of cooking and serving shelters and cooking utensils, provision of nutritious food (cereal, vegetable cooking oil & pulses) to primary school learners orphans and vulnerable children, Establishment of school nutrition gardens. In order to enhance safe guarding the project will train teachers on child safeguarding and protection in emergencies, provide TOT training for teachers and school health coordinators on PSS and DRR, establish (or re-establish), train, and support children’s health clubs in all project schools and To provide 153 schools with non-food items (dignity kits, soap, buckets,) that are complementary in the school feeding program.
The third and last project objective on promoting and advocating for increased school enrolment will be realised through provision of learning materials. The proposed activities will require total budget support of USD1 143 280
