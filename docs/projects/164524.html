Project Summary

The project main goal is to increase access to safe and sustainable water and bolster positive hygienic practices through the provision of water infrastructure, hygiene promotion awareness sessions and distribution of WASH NFI kits.
The project will target newly accessible areas in NES as people are suffering the lack of pure water access that kept them out of access to sufficient water resources which are basic human need. The project targets are the most vulnerable IDPs and host communities, with an emphasis on those in most need: female-headed households, families with pregnant or lactating women, children under five years of age, and/or disabled/elderly members, households with many family members, and others with qualitatively similar burdens (families with no income and dependent on relief).
The project includes conducting repairing to damaged water stations in NES, in addition to implement hygiene promotion activities for targeted communities to ensure providing safe access to clean water and raise the people hygienic conditions by targeting them with hygiene promotion activities due to the fact that these affected and returnees people are extremely vulnerable to water-borne infections and other diseases related to personal hygiene. The areas of implementation (Admin 4) will be selected upon receiving fund in coordination with WASH sector, MoWR and Ministry of Agriculture in order to implement the project in the most vulnerable areas.

4 - Needs

The water crisis in most of the targeted areas was as a result of the lack of water sources due to several points, as the increasing in population as a result of displacement from hot areas to safe ones, which requires searching for new water sources as there are insufficient existing sources.Some water sources became out of service – mainly wells - either because the equipment is old in addition to continuous operation interruptions, the dryness of these wells, or reduction of well water flow.According to HNO 2020, the WASH infrastructure and service provision in many parts of Syria require significant investments, repair and operational support. WASH systems have suffered from damages owing to years of functioning at high capacity, limited or no maintenance, destructions, displacement of technical staff and effects of climate change and poor water resources management. Countrywide, 42 per cent of the population is relying on alternative and often unsafe water sources to meet or complement their water needs, and in extreme case of Idleb up to 85 per cent . Communities not served with public water networks are in general at higher risk of unsafe water consumption, use less water than people well served via free/low cost water networks and spend significantly more of their incomes on purchasing water.

5 - Implementation of activities

GOPA has access to most of NES areas, program staff will oversee direct implementation of all proposed activities. A comprehensive package of assistance will be conducted by GOPA including essential rehabilitation to ensure stations can operate at full capacity in addition to implement hygiene promotion sessions and WASH NFIs distribution in the targeted communities.Detailed BoQs will be prepared for each location of the water station in addition to the preparation of the technical specification book and then starting the tendering process. The tender for the provision and installation of the required water stations’ equipment will be announced in public.The winning contractor will be responsible for providing, transporting, installing, and testing the equipment for all the water stations.During the life of this project,GOPA will coordinate with the community leaders, other humanitarian actors providing assistance in these provinces, WASH sector, to ensure covering the needs of huge numbers of families in need and to avoid duplication and overlapping of intervention
