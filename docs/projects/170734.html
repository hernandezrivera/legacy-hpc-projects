Project Name

Start Date

31/03/2020

End Date

Project Summary

Enhance UNRWA PHC services to provide essential early detection and referral in West Bank. Establish triage procedures in 43 Health Centers and Qalqilia Hospital. Introduce a rotation system for medical staff, telephone support and a health advice line
