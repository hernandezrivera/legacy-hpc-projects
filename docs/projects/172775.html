Project Summary

This project aims at providing immediate life saving protection and GBV assistance to vulnerable 68363 Women, Men, Boys and girls mostly IDPs, Returnees and host communities in four states of South and East Darfur, South Kordofan and Blue Nile. The Project intervention will target the most vulnerable population through provision of IGA, Construction of Women and Girls safe spaces/ Community multipurose centers, capacity building and awareness campaigns on SGBV, Child protection activities such as FTR to respond to unaccompanied and Separated children. Case management will be done at community centers. Community based child protection mechanisms will be established and their capacity enhanced to respond to SGBV and child protection issues while improving the household income through IGAs and lifeskills development.

1 - Needs

As the protracted conflict continue to devastate the Darfurs, Kordofans and Blue Nile states, women and children continue to bear the brunt of abuse coupled with age old cultural practices. Women and children lack safe spaces to play and nurture their youthful age while women do not have the opportunity to engage as women and discuss issues affecting them as women. In communal facilities, there are no gender appropriate sanitation facilities that can guarantee their safety and dignity during critical times. Women and children lack recreational facilities and materials that can help them debrief the acquired trauma brought about by the protracted conflict as well as lack of psycho-social services. Communities lack knowledge and skills on how to protect children and women from SGBV while the community does not have a mechanism to protect themselves. Women and especially SGBV survivors lack basic livelihood to improve their household income due to lack of requisite life skills that can help them improve their independence and reduce over reliance from the abusers.

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

World Vision follows the domains of Child protection framework when designing its projects. This project has the main objective as protecting Children from abuse and enhancing community mechanisms to protect children through the use of Community Based Protection mechanisms. Focus will also be on protecting women from Gender Based Violence. The children will also be involved in protecting their interests through awareness on their rights, protection and safety concerns through children clubs. During the implementation, the beneficiaries will be made aware of their entitlements through community based feedback mechanism that ensures protection and safeguarding of children and beneficiaries.

7.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will inform and interact with inter-agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including SEA and abuse complaints?

WV will roll out the four components of its Program Approach and Feedback (PAF) mechanism in order to align to international humanitarian standards and requirements and to provide beneficiaries and other key stakeholders with relevant project information and feedback. Staff capacity building as well as community training and sensitization will be undertaken in order to roll out PAF effectively for this project. World Vision will strengthen the application of PAF in order to align to the different international humanitarian standards requirements. PAF will be rolled out to provide beneficiaries and other key stakeholders with relevant project information, to ensure that the targeted communities and stakeholders are consulted, promote meaningful participation of beneficiaries and giving them a platform to give feedback on the implementation of the project and other grievances they might have. World Vision Sudan will ensure timely responses to address beneficiaries’ feedback.The main focus of the accountability system is to ensure that transparent community feedback mechanism is in place and is operational. World Vision Sudan will categorize beneficiary feedback in three ways:• Feedback that requires immediate attention. • Feedback that can be resolved locally. • Feedback that needs to be addressed by senior management in Nyala level or Senior Leadership at Khartoum. Other accountability mechanisms used by WVI include providing implementation reports during public gathering and peer accountability during cluster meeting with SMOA, SMARRF, FAO and other stakeholders.The information generated through the system is used for follow-up and to track the status of the response to questions, suggestions, issues and concerns. This is part of internal monitoring and oversight of the functioning of the accountability mechanism. World Vision will also provide responses to questions, suggestions, issues and concerns raised by the project beneficiaries once appropriate decisions are made in response to the feedback.
