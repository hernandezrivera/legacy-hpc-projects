Project Summary

Global surgery encompasses anaesthesia, all surgical specialties including trauma surgery, general surgery, Obstetric & Gynaecology, peri-operative medicines, critical emergency medicine, pain management, palliative care, and rehabilitation.
The main goal of this project is to strengthen the surgical, obstetric, and anesthesia (SOA) cares of ten hospitals in five geographical areas. The project will equip the selected secondary facilities with the critical emergency surgical specialties, perioperative medicines, surgical supplies, anaesthesia medicines, and advanced training of health professionals involved in care of the surgical patient on life support, patient safety, pain management, and palliative care. Furthermore, the project will support expansion of trauma care and life support through establishment of efficient referral system from primary health care facilities in the five geographical areas to the targeted hospitals to ensure maximum coverage of vulnerable people including migrants, displaced families, returnees and people with special needs.
During 2020, COVID-19 added more strain on health services and many people lacked sustained access to secondary health care services. In addition, hospital staff is used inefficiently for non-emergency consultations as the first point of contact, rather than the PHC system. Six essential services are offered in only 6.4% of 78 public hospitals allover Libya. Comprehensive Emergency Obstetric and Newborn Care (CEmONC) is available in 49%, emergency in 63%, and major surgery in 52% of the 78 assessed hospitals in the different four regions of the country. The targeted hospitals were selected based on the shortages of qualified health professionals in the different surgical fields.
WHO member states declared surgical, obstetric, and anesthesia (SOA) care as both ‘‘critical’’ and ‘‘integral’’ components to achieve global health goals and emergency care (World Health Assembly Resolution 68.15). Therefore ten hospitals in five different geographical areas will be targeted to provide sustainable and quality global surgery and referral services across Libya.
Activities
Activities:
1. Establish two Emergency Medical Team (EMT) according to EMT standards of WHO with the needed specialties
2. Sub-contracting the Libyan Red Crescent to strengthen referral services including emergency
3. Provide technical and financial support to the first line trauma care respondents
4 Provision of trauma kits, emergency surgical (TESK) kits , anaesthesia medicines kits, and Reproductive Health kits to ten hospitals
5. Provide maintenance services to laboratories in ten hospitals
6. Increasing capacity of ICU beds in ten hospitals
7. Conduct five training workshops for health workers on health service quality, patient safety, and infection prevention and control
8. Conduct five training workshops for health workers on Basic and Advanced Life support courses (BLS & ALS), neonatal resuscitation and new borne care at birth and dissemination of different related guidelines
9. Develop surgical indicators to identify the most pressing needs, plan appropriate service delivery, and evaluate ongoing interventions.
10. Integrate SOA data with the National DHIS-2 platform to create SOA data systems to measure impact at national level
11. Support SOA planning processes by engaging and informing key national stakeholders while leveraging external support for developing National Surgical, Obstetric, and Anesthesia Plans (NSOAPs) and national emergency plan
