Project Summary

This project seeks to improve the livelihood situation for the olive farmers in Idleb countryside three sub-districts of Kafr-Takharim, Armanaz, and Jisr Ash-shughur through conducting the integrated olive management . where the general character of the targeted area is characterized by olive cultivation as a main source of income.
The project will target 750 olive farmers within one season all of them are from small ownership between 5 -8 donum.
The project will provide the targeted beneficiaries (750 olive farmers) with the inputs, intercropping kit, and trainings. with estimated budget 413,500 USD$.
However, the project concentrates on four main activities:
- Providing the agricultural inputs (fertilizers, harvesting tools, pruning tools).
- Providing legume seeds for the intercropping between olive trees.
- Providing the agricultural services of the olive trees (tillage, harvesting service, pruning service).
- Providing the agricultural training beside the agricultural guidance for the olive farmers.
the total direct beneficiaries of the project are /1875HHs/ distributing as following; 750HHs of olive farmer, 750HHs of unskilled worker in harvesting with total workdays 15,000, and 375 HHs of skilled worker in olive tree pruning with total workdays 7,500. Amal still seeks to secure fund to implement this project as an urgent need to the targeted communities and olive farmers.

3 - Needs

The food security situation in Syria has drastically deteriorated since last year. In 2023, an estimated 15 million people will need some form of food and agriculture assistance, representing 68 per cent of the total population. At least 12.1 million people are estimated to be food insecure (UNOCHA-HNO, 2023). The WFP remote food security monitoring and recent food security and livelihood assessment in Syria (FSA, FSLA) estimates that seven in ten Syrian families is food insecure with the highest concentration in Idleb (74 per cent),There is need to support the olive value chain starting with olive farmers, who need the inputs and services, which cost more in compere with the production and small properties.There is need to provide Machines (Tractor, plow, grinder) for olive farmers to overcome the plowing cost (which usually conducted with two cross tillage’s 4 time in year), and to grind the pruning residues.Provide agricultural extension and training especially in the field of managing pruning residues, integrated olive pest management, building olive farmers associations for information exchange, support collective marketing, and conduct services collectively to decrease the cost of production.

4 - Implementation of activities

Amal Org. and in collaboration with the local councils will announce for the project and its activities in the targeted areas and select the beneficiaries from the olive farmers according to the project eligibility criteria with priority for the most vulnerable HHs. - The project will provide the agricultural inputs for the targeted beneficiaries from (fertilizers, harvesting tools, pruning tools) in the aim of improvement the olive tree situation and productivity.- The project will insert additional component /the intercropping/ between the olive trees in the aim of improving the soil fertility and rise the income of olive farmer, resulted from the intercropping, where the legume seeds will be distributed to the olive farmer beneficiaries to grow.- The project will provide the agricultural services of the olive trees (tillage, harvesting service, pruning service) and to do that will recruit the unskilled and skilled worker to do these activities where 750 unskilled worker and 375 skilled workers will be recruited to do these services.- To ensure sustainability, the project will provide agricultural training besides the agricultural guidance for the targeted olive farmer beneficiaries, which in turn will consolidate the knowledge and contribute to exchange it among the farmers.Total beneficiaries are 1875 HHs, they are distributed as follows: 750 olive farmers, 750 harvesting workers, 375 pruning workers.All project interventions are in line with the do no harm approach.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

The project will engage the target beneficiaries in the design process, planning and activities implementation. So, the project targeted both host community and IDPs in the all project stages.the beneficiaries are from both women and men with priority for the following families: orphan, female headed household, family with disability person, elderly, huge number of children under five. the project will consider gender issues during the training session by recruiting a female for training, large hall, training time, ..etc.same thing during input distribution to allow women receive the inputs and service in appropriate way. the project will conduct complaint mechanism to receive and solve any issue in manner time.the percent of the targeted women in the project consider the activities nature so the unskilled workers will be mostly from female.


8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy
