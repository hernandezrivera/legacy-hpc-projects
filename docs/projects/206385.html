1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Under this project, AeA aims at contributing towards the access of protection information and services to the most affected population of the district in Meluco, which is one of the most forgotten districts in Cabo Delgado province. By doing so, AeA pretends to provide early response in protection and supporting both communities and local authorities, allowing them to be familiarized on their rights and the different available channels and mechanisms to improve their emergency protection situation and basic assistance based on international standards. (M=400, F=450, B=200, G=250)

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

AeA ensures that, during the design phase of its projects, information is gathered on the specific needs of each gender and various vulnerable groups. To achieve this, AeA conducts a socio-spatial analysis to identify the requirements of each demographic (considering age, ethnicity, gender, disability, etc.). This involves exploratory walks, interviews with key informants, focus groups, and direct observation to pinpoint the challenges and risks, including access to public spaces and services, associated with each specific group.AeA then analyzes this data to target groups where it can have the greatest impact, actively coordinating with relevant stakeholders to avoid duplication of efforts. Subsequently, the activities are customized to address the actual needs observed in the field. AeA also monitors these activities, placing a strong emphasis on using a gender-aware lens throughout the process. AeA prioritizes preventing sexual exploitation and abuse (PSEA), promoting protection, gender, and social inclusion in all its projects. A comprehensive PSEA policy outlines its zero-tolerance approach, and staff and volunteers undergo background checks and regular PSEA training. Reporting mechanisms are safe, confidential, and accessible to all staff and beneficiaries, with investigations conducted promptly and impartially. Moreover, AeA strictly follows the Do-No-Harm methodology and acknowledges that project beneficiaries may be among the most vulnerable groups, requiring special consideration in project design and monitoring, and therefore designs projects in a way that prioritizes their needs. Additionally, AeA has a set of policies and procedures to mitigate operational risks and maintain ethical behaviour, including a Code of Conduct, Child Protection Policy, Safeguarding Statement of Commitment, Anti-Harassment Policy, and Anti-fraud and Corruption Policy.
