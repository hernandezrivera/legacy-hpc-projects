Project Summary

This project is ongoing and its fund is secured. Strengthen the access of 120 most vulnerable households in Afrin to Basic Needs through 2 rounds (@ 3 months each) of short-term income generation via CfW rubble removal/irrigation canal cleaning. (Payment is attendance/daily based) it is directly linked with FSL priorities. Farmers were identified as one of the main affected groups by the crisis, as lack of proper governance structure in place resulted in losing the access of farmers to subsidize inputs and the damage of agricultural infrastructure increased the burden on farmers where the production of their crops recorded a dramatic increase. The output of this result, 1.2.1, will help farmers in overcoming the irrigation challenge they face and will help them reduce the operational cost, relatively, on farmers ‘shoulders. The main source of water for these canals is Medanki dam which stores water from Medanki lake that is located Sharan sub-district in Afrin district.

3 - Needs

Strengthen the access of 120 most vulnerable households in Afrin to Basic Needs through 2 rounds (@ 3 months each) of short-term income generation via CfW rubble removal/irrigation canal cleaning. (Payment is attendance/daily based). Farmers were identified as one of the main affected groups by the crisis, as lack of proper governance structure in place resulted in losing the access of farmers to subsidize inputs and the damage of agricultural infrastructure increased the burden on farmers where the production of their crops recorded a dramatic increase. The output of this result will help farmers in overcoming the irrigation challenge they face and will help them reduce the operational cost, relatively, on farmers ‘shoulders. The main source of water for these canals is Medanki dam which store water from Medanki lake that is located Sharan sub-district in Afrin district. Furthermore, a detailed need assessment will be conducted in coordination with Agricultural working group. A close coordinator with INGOs on ground is conducted by field IMDAT team to avoid duplication

4 - Implementation of activities

120 households will be selected based on an application to work in 3 rounds each round three months (6 months rubble removal/irrigation canal cleaning and 3 months rubble removal/city cleaning). Each beneficiary will receive 100 USD on a monthly basis based on the last agreed SMEB value by the Food Security Cluster. The number of allocated beneficiaries under this result was developed in close coordination with agriculture office in Afrin. It is expected to help 200 farmers from this result, and clean around 20 KM of secondary canal. IMDAT will (1.1) Coordinate with the Agricultural Office to select irrigation channels that need cleaning. The application process will start by publishing an (1.2) Online COVID sensitive announcement followed by Verification (COVID sensitive) of application requests based on vulnerability and eligibility criteria (100% of selected applications). The program and field team will (1.3) Set work-plan and form the supervisor and beneficiaries’ teams. the CFW team leader will work on (1.4) Providing training to the CfW participants and distributing CfW tools. Then the selected teams will (1.5) Start the Working Cycle (3 months each). After each month, the payment will be issued for all the BNFs by (1.6) Verification of attendance and distribute payment tokens for the beneficiaries, (1.7) Cash disbursement. Finally, the M&E team will (1.9) Conduct PDM and end line assessment.

9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

- IMDAT has developed a security guideline and plan that covers the evacuation of the sites in case of emergency. - iMDAT to provide training for the boths staff and beneficiaries before starting the activity, including evacuation training in case of danger, and first aid training.· iMDAT to continuously monitor the security situation and coordinate with the local authorities.· iMDAT to suspend all field work in case the security situation deteriorates in a way that may pose a danger to the employees or beneficiaries of the project.· iMDAT to coordinate intensively with partners in case the security situation changes.· In case of security risk cases, participants will be asked to stay at home and he/she will be eligible for the wages during the stopped period. In accordance with the mitigation and escalation plan that has been developed throughout the previous project, the red light alert will be activated once the first scenario came true. In this case the following emergency activities will be kicked. Below are the main designed activities. 1. Move from Green to Orange zone according to our emergency response2. Halt all field activities during the fight period 3. Provide an advance salary, covering three months, to all field staff.
