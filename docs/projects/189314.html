Project Summary

In South Sudan, sub-national and localized violence is ever-present; manifesting in clashes between State and non-State armed groups, community-based armed militias, and groups of displaced civilians (HNO 2022). Without political stability and peace, communities will continue to be affected by violence leading to subsequent displacements. Continuous violence without recourse to change in attitudes and building mechanisms for reconciliation, peacebuilding initiatives and advocacy for peaceful coexistence will lead to increased localized and sub-national violence, resulting in repeated and increased civilian displacements, thus impacting on people’s access to services and the disruption of humanitarian operations. HACO, therefore, proposes to engage in protection monitoring, engaging communities to reconcile and build inter-communal synergies for peaceful resolution of conflict. Identification and assistance of women, men, girls, and boys with protection concerns during static and IPMT, protection monitoring and reporting, general protection awareness-raising, giving protective cash-based assistance in connection with safe, dignified, and voluntary return by people of concern to the PC, Conducting training on conflict resolution, mitigation, mediation, and peacebuilding, carrying out outreaches to people of concern through peacebuilding awareness-raising activities, individual, group, and specialized PSS. Humane Aid for Community Organization (HACO) intends to reach 19,807 individuals in Unity State.

7.4. Community Engagement Strategy

Yes

7.4.3. How will your project use the beneficiaries’ feedback to adapt project assistance?

There will be continuous engagement/involvement with project beneficiaries. The complaints from the project beneficiaries will help the project team to adjust their project implementation modalities/processes when appropriate.

7.4.4. How will your project ensure feedback from the most vulnerable members (e.g. individuals living with disabilities, individuals who are not literate, women and girls)?

Since the AAP Committee will comprise persons from affected groups, they will be able to reach out to their own group members such as youth at-risk groups representatives, women groups representative, elders, and persons with disabilities.

7.4.2. How will your project provide beneficiaries information about Protection against Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA)?

HACO Project staff will sign the Organization code of Conduct and PSEA policy as annexes to their contracts. The Project staff will be trained on PSEA policy and the project beneficiaries will be told of HACO's zero tolerance for SEA. they will also be given contacts for referral in case there are cases of SEA to report.

7.4.1. How will your project provide beneficiaries information about the project, feedback mechanism and/or complaint mechanism?

All project beneficiaries will be informed of ongoing and planned Project activities by the project management team. They will also be informed on the outset of project scope, duration and limitations. The AAP committees that will Mann the beneficiaries' complaints and feedback desk will be pivotal in recording complaints from beneficiaries and responding to those complaints
