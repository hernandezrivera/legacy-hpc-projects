Project Summary

Between May and October 2019, more than 17,000 security incidents including shelling, airstrikes, and explosive hazard accidents, were reported in northwest Syria including Afrin and Idleb district. Explosives, as a result, are often left in the public places like agricultural lands, access roads, private property, schools, and other public buildings, exposing civilians' lives to risk based on our implemented project in north western Syria. As a result, there is still a huge gap to be filled in. Risk Education (RE) will be implemented through our partner to protect IDPs and Host communities from the risk of explosive incidents. MRE sessions will be conducted to IDPs and host community members through trained facilitators by visiting targeted areas. 13,300 Syrian including IDPs, host community members and returnees will receive MRE sessions and the risk of explosive devices will be mitigated.

4 - Needs

According to Protection Chapter of HNO Draft 2020,11.5 million men, women, boys and girls live in areas affected by explosive hazards such as mines, Unexploded Ordnance (UXO), and Improvised Explosive Devices (IED). However, only 25% of the target received risk education in Syria, despite of the critical needs because of the high level of contamination (2019 Humanitarian Response Plan Monitoring Report, October 2019).In addition, between May and October 2019, more than 17,000 security incidents including shelling, airstrikes, and explosive hazard accidents, were reported in northwest Syria (North West of Syria Mine Action Sector Dashboard, November 2019). Explosives, as a result, are often left in the public places like agricultural lands, access roads, private property, schools, and other public buildings, exposing civilians' lives to risk based on our implemented project in north western Syria. As a result, there is still a huge gap to be filled in. One of the enhanced contamination impact survey conducted by NGOs last year in Idleb and Aleppo Governorates shows that more than half of the targeted communities in Afrin, Azaz and Al Bab Districts are contaminated with mines, UXOs and IEDs. Also, Harim and Idleb Districts include multiple high contaminated communities. Men are more likely to be directly exposed to the explosive hazards’ incidents due to their jobs such as farming, herding, construction and rubble removal. Boys and girls are also particularly vulnerable to the risk of explosive hazards because of curiosity and unawareness of those danger. While women are more impacted indirectly, for instance through the deprivation of livelihood in case male breadwinner got injured by explosive incidents. RE is a high priority in northwest Syria, as a preventive approach of the risks of explosive hazards accidents, as they cause the death of 1.5 people and injury of two on average (Humanitarian Needs Overview 2019, March 2019).

5 - Implementation of activities

RE sessions will be implemented for at risk groups through partner’s mobile teams in north west Syria, which is highly contaminated with explosive devices. The RE sessions will be tailored by age, gender, occupation and localized threats. One team consists of three facilitators will conduct around 40 RE sessions per month for six months. We will select 40 persons among beneficiaries and train them as community focal points. This will allow us to spread RE messages to community members who cannot join RE sessions provided by our teams. Post-test will be conducted after each session to check the level of understanding of beneficiaries. In addition, KAPB (Knowledge, Attitude, Practice and Belied) survey will be conducted before sessions, immediately after sessions and after three months. Samples of the survey will be selected considering age, gender and location. Appealing organization will deploy directly recruited M&E staff in the field and monitor the quality of delivered services on weekly basis.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Familiarization to explosive devices may cause beneficiaries to become overconfident or complacent to the treat of explosive hazards. One of the mitigation measures is emphasize on “stop, warn and report” message while conducting RE sessions. Secondly, conducting KAPB survey before sessions, immediately after sessions and three months after to monitor changes in risky behaviour.Sexual exploitation and abuse by staff member are also one of the protection risks. We will mitigate the risk by recruiting female staff members, establishing feedback mechanism, appointing PSEA focal points and provide PSEA training for staff.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk is familiarization to explosive devices may cause beneficiaries to become overconfident or complacent to the treat of explosive hazards. Assumption is stable security situation in targeted and surrounding areas to conduct RE sessions. One of the mitigation measures is to emphasize on "stop, warn and report" message while conducting RE sessions. In addition, KAPB survey will be conducted to monitor changes in risky behavior, as well as qualitative evaluations to understand the issues underpinning risk behavior.From the security perspective, the situation might deteriorate. As a mitigation measure, suspension will be considered for staff and beneficiaries' safety by monitoring security situation on daily basis, in addition to changing some locations if access is denied.
