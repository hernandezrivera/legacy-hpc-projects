Project Summary

The Project aims to focus on WASH services in particular along with comprehensive awareness raising and hygiene promotion using IEC/BCC materials and also by installing hand washing facilities as a part of COVID -19 response both at the refugees and selected host communities in Teknaf.

Needs

The Rohingya refugees are already under the threats of natural disasters in the camp areas, and it has a severe negative impact in the life of Bangladeshi host communities. The COVID-19 has recently become a major threat in the camp area, considering the risk of density and number of populations. Access to adequate healthcare is already limited and a pandemic is likely to create an even larger gap in medical supplies, including masks, soaps and hand sanitizers, and in terms of infrastructure hand washing stations. Therefore, a critical WASH measures like more handwashing stations and more awareness building are needed to mitigate the impacts of Covid-19 as much as possible.

Activities or outputs

Considering the NGO Forum's project activities, expertise and needs, the main project activities include the following: Increase coverage and operationalisation of hand-washing stations both in camps and host's communities; Strengthening awareness raising and hygiene promotion focusing on Covid-19; and Organise disinfection of WASH facilities and common spaces.
