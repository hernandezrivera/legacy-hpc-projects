1 - Needs

Since 2020 , HDPO works with communities in various areas of Darfur in supporting the people in need and the village dvelopment committes however still the basics WASH services remain high in North Darfur localities ( Ambaro, Kornoi, Al Tina and Kabkabyia) and water supply scarcity incukding the open defectaion issues are the common in Eastern Sudan. in Darfur the pockets of conflict, the waves of rertun from neighbering countries toward the villages of orgins has made the high demand to the WASH services. in Eastern Sudan the host communities use to share their limited water reosuces with the refugees in many areas such as Gadaref localities and Kassala ( both North Delta and other hosting communities.The economic crisis and severe rise in the food commodities and the over all living cost have contributed in problems of accessing the water and sanitation facilities including the spare parts and inputs of the O&M, all these have contributed in worsening the water supply and sanitation facilities across various location and level of the communities contribution has decreased accordingly. HDPO assessed the water situation in these localities and found that about most of the people depend on haffirs as drinking water while few available water sources are not functioning well, HDPO has connection with many village development committees from these localities with sort of community mobilization to contribute in any rehabilitation work starting in almost all the areas however these initiatives need real humanitarian support. The involvement of women's in WASH committees remains low while the CLTS approach has to be introduced in rural areas. The issue of hygiene kits for people with special needs and MHM kits for the adolescents and women in reproductive ages also curial and there is serious gap in these areas spec ally the rural ones. improvement of WASH in schools and other institutions remains needed and that was clear from the MoUs that HDPO has signed with ministry of health, WES,HDPO will do the same with the WASH sector in both Kassala, The red Sea and Gadaref state to improve the access to water, hygiene and sanitation

2 - Activities/Outputs

Water supply: • Provision of spare parts, technical knowledge to conduct the quality operation and maintenance 23 water sources in Tina, Kapkapiya, Amboru and Kornoi localities with focus on community contribution and participation where women will be involved in WASH committee and decision-making process following the water safety process . Improve the drainage system to ensure the wells are protected enough from the any external contamination; this will be done thorough repair and maintenance of the platforms and soak way pits where possible. • Drilling of 25 new bore holes to cover the needs in areas with no access to water following the WASH sector guidelines • Water quality monitoring and testing as per Sundanese water quality standards Sanitation and Waste Management• Provision of materials and mobilize the communities in repair & maintenance including new 800 units of latrines and bathing facilities where communities will be trained to maintain the gender use of the facilities associated with hand washing practices and soap . • Waste segregation will be ensured from the source, i.e. from household, communal place and the marketplace through the hygiene promotion team and the required community engagement. the solid waste management aims to collect the waste, segregate and provide household bins where communities will be engaged in operation and maintenance of these bins through assigning users’ groups connected to WASH committee, volunteers and WASH field team. Hygiene promotion with focus on AWD and COVID19 • Form WASH management committee develop a community engagement mechanism including participatory monitoring and feedback system ensuring sufficient representation of different population sub-groups including at least 50% of participants as females. Organize regular meeting and consultation with the committee members ensuring the users are empowered to report to HDPO staff members and volunteers about any issue of concern related to WASH facilities and services which will be timely and appropriately addressed by the BRAC field team. • Conduct focus group discussion session at camp, village or block level , in markets and open spaces, this will target the whole population over the project period • Bi- weekly clean up campaigns session in each block followed by the cleaning campaigns where total number of the participant will be clearly recorded. • Weekly Jerry can and water containers cleaning campaigns and conduct water dialogue during the campaigns. • Conduct MHM session through trained hygiene promotion officers/volunteers, the activity will include mapping the adult’s girls and women in reproductive age, they will be trained in MHM and specific kits will be distributed while the MHM session group will be facilitated at villages/camp and block levels. • Household visit using the BRAC household visit checklist and modules to measure the indicators with clear documentation of the records and messages delivered to the household members. • Post distribution survey will be conducted and reported while the finding will be shared with the communities for lesson learning and adapting the distribution.
