Project Summary

The United Nations Resident/Humanitarian Coordinator (RC/HC) has requested WFP as the lead agency of the Logistics Cluster to activate the sector in Lebanon to start identifying options to facilitate a reliable access to fuel through the set-up of relevant logistics and supply chain networks to prevent the discontinuation of critical lifesaving activities within the Health, WASH and other relevant sectors and through the provision of fuel to humanitarian actors.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

a) Ensure sufficient fuel required for basic and life-saving health services – this includes key hospitals (public and private) and primary health care clinics (PHCCs) and dispensaries throughout the country.b) Ensure fuel to keep water stations active and operational across the country and thereby ensuring the provision of public water supplies to more than 2/3 of the population.c) Ensure sufficient fuel is made available to maintain business continuity through functional office facilities and movement of staff for the larger humanitarian community (UN agencies and key NGOs).
