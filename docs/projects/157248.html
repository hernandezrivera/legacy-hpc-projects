Project Summary

This project aims to increase access to quality education for school aged children and increase the enrollments and Retention rates for school age children in Ombeitein villages in Kalimendo locality and returnees in Saraf Omra Locality in North Darfur State. Saraf Omra and Kalimendo Locality are one of the areas that have never witnessed any educational intervention although it is one of the most affected areas by Darfur conflict. The approximate population is over (3000 household) 15000 Individuals and the assessed 9,400 returnee’s house hold. The areas has completely been neglected from Humanitarian services. According to DPI , UNDP , IOM and UNHCR assessment findings the area has two basic schools, the project will involve construction and rehabilitation of schools, in the two localities , standardized latrines , WASH facilities ,It was witnessed that there is no access or education and child protection services in targeted localities , Kaimendo locality have three schools with local materials and the ministries have no staffs in the locations, children studying on mats , luck of scholastic materials , teaching material and tools for teachers .

Needs

This project aims to increase access to quality education for school aged children and increase the enrollments and Retention rates for school age children in Ombeitein villages in Kalimendo locality and in 2 returnee villages in Saraf Omra locality in North Darfur State. Ombeitein village is one of the areas that have never witnessed any educational intervention although it is one of the most affected areas by Darfur conflict. The overall project targets to reach 800 children (Boys and girls) in two villages of Saraf Omra locality and 8 classrooms in Ombeitein village of Kalimendo locality will reach 800 boys and girls . The areas have completely been neglected from Humanitarian services for many years due to lack of access . According to DPI assessment findings in Kalimendo there are 1,200 children and 100 PTAs and education and child protection committees and volunteers in each village (300 committees ) will be directly reached with the propose project including returnees , and most vulnerable residents (host communities) house hold’s host community children have no access to education , returnees were verified in the inter agency mission conducted by RRR sector the area Saraf Omra returnee villages children have limited access to education due the recent return and financial disabilities . Most of them sit on grounds while few others sit on plastic sheets and plastic backs. In addition, there no latrines in both schools children must go to their homes for defecation. As a part of Saraf Omra locality of returnees , the area has witnessed a complex and multidimensional conflict dynamic. It has experienced violence and tribal tensions in the recent years, and there haven’t been any significant interventions that could contribute to stabilization of the situation in the past few years. In addition, returnee villages is one of areas which was blocked by Musa Hillal armed faction group for several years and therefore no any education or other humanitarian interventions provided to the community, There will be a high need of conducting training on Mine risk education , Disaster and Risk Reduction.

Activities

DPI’S intervention will be focusing on implementation of full packaged education and child protection sector through implementation of bellow activities.1. Construction of 3 schools (2 in saraf omra and 1 in Kalimendo) with 8 classrooms each.2. Construction of 2 offices in each school.3. Construction of 1 kitchen in each school.4. Construction of 6 units of latrines in each school.5. Provision of furniture including classes and offices.6. Provision of full packages of students scholastic materials.7. Provision of teacher’s kits.8. Formulation of PTA.9. Conduct training to PTAS on child protection related issues.10. Conduct training for the PTAs and community leaders on different topics such as Mine education, Disaster and Risk reduction, health and sanitation , ToT.11. Provision of WASH facilities such as solid waste management.12. Provision of water Tank.13. Establishment of hand washing facilities.14. Establishment of 1 CFS in each school.15. Conduct child Sophocles social activities.
