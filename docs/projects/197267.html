1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Crisis (IPC Phase 3) outcomes will continue through January 2023 in the conflict-affectedareas of Cabo Delgado, drought-affected areas of southern Mozambique, and flood- andtropical storm-impacted areas of Nampula due to low food reserves and limited access toincome. In these areas and communities, poor households have started experiencing foodshortages due to the lean season. In most parts of central Mozambique, Stressed (IPCPhase 2) outcomes continue as poor households recover from multiple hazards stemmingfrom cyclones, floods, irregular rainfall (droughts), and the COVID-19 pandemic.Vulnerable and poor households will be supported with food aid or food assistancevouchers to ensure that these have a little surplus and/or may be able to extend theirreserves for January to April 2023. World Vision will aim to reach 16,000 affected peoplein Meconta, Memba and Monapo Districts, in Nampula. The intervention will address theimmediate food needs of the vulnerable communities and address sustainable livelihoodsneeds. The selection criteria will target female-headed households, elderly persons (>65years), child headed households, disabled headed households and the chronically ill. Thegoal of the project is to reduce vulnerability to shocks by providing food assistance andsupporting rebuilding their livelihoods in Nampula Province. The goal will be realizedthrough utilization of low-tech-low-risk assets building approach knitted withintraditional knowledge and practice.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Risk: Children vulnerable and exposed to abuse/neglect Mitigation: Use ofcommunity radio, and “Linha Fala Crianca” to report abuse cases. 2. Risk: Increase inviolence against women and girls in resettlement camps Mitigation: Train social workers to address issues affecting women and girl child. Develop quick reporting and follow-up mechanisms in partnership with local government and partners. 3. Risk: Fraud/Theft during the distribution of food parcels / cash vouchers Mitigation: The project will set up the “Linha Verde” (Green Line) so that beneficiaries can report anycases or attempt of corruption, fraud or inappropriate behavior 4. Risk: Discrimination of afected people by host communities Mitigation: Carry out community mobilizations and involve local/host communities in planned activities and extend the scope of assistance to vulnerable host household

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

World Vision has an accountability mechanism hinged on 4 Program AccountabilityFramework pillars, including community consultation, participation, providinginformation, and receiving and responding to complaints. WV will conduct communityconsultations that will include the most vulnerable groups to establish feedbackmechanisms. Community consultations and learning from previous projects will helpto design accountability mechanisms for 1) information provision like banners, use ofmegaphones, ration boards, visibility materials; 2) complaints and feedback through suggestion boxes, linha verde, help desk. Awareness on the existence of mechanismswill be reinforced in pre-activity meetings (registrations, verifications, distributions,asset creation). Pre-distribution and mass awareness meetings with beneficiaries,community and local leaders will explain beneficiaries’ rights, PSEA, responsibilities tocurb any gender-based related violence and abuse and how to access reportingchannels in case of abuse and infringement of rights. Affected populations will haveopportunity to provide project related feedback to mitigate risks of fraud, corruptionor sexual exploitation and abuse. A committee of about 6 to 8 key members with 60%women. The committee will be composed of different socioeconomic group membersi.e. representative of girls, boys, men, women, chiefs, youth, elderly, religious leaders,secretary, chiefs of blocks, extensionists and will be engaged in asset identification;Beneficiary identification/registration; food distribution, redemption of vouchers,monitoring assets and trainings and marking attendance registers. Project willconsider distribution time for women protection.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

World Vision uses the rights-based social model of disability inclusion whichrecognizes and partners with women and girls, men and boys, elderly persons andpersons with disabilities and addresses barriers. Building on World Vision’s Guidelinesfor Inclusion of Persons with a disability (PWD), and individuals from minority groupsall face distinct risks in crisis settings and have unique needs. WV response teams willproactively seek to understand the specific experiences of these groups, and willensure that the assistance and services are accessible to the most vulnerable. Thisincludes ensuring a gender perspective, disability awareness, and proactiveidentification and support for individuals with specific needs. World Vision will partnerwith PWD organisations and specialized organizations, including Humanity & andInclusion (formerly known as Handicap International) in Mozambique and otherinclusion specialist agencies to ensure disability is streamlined across programmingand to ensure accessible to people with disabilities. in-depth understanding andmainstreaming of disability awareness and inclusion.
