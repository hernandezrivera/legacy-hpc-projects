Project Summary

The proposed health interventions has objective to respond effectively to the humanitarian needs of conflict-affected populations in Cabo Delgado (CD) province through emergency response and early recovery interventions aiming at reducing the morbidity and mortality among Internal displaced peoples (IDPs), returnees and Host Communities (HC), with a focus on pregnant and lactating women (PLW) and children under 5 and by strengthening Health services.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

In order to contribute to the overall cluster objectives of saving lives and ensuring continuity of essential services for IDP and host communities, ACF will respond to the urgent health and nutrition needs of the affected population by supporting the existing and well-established MOH care modality, Mobile Brigade, ACF mobile clinics will be complementary. ACF will use this intervention modality to ensure that the target population has safe access to essential healthcare services and security risks occurring in the search for care and treatment are reduced. The mobile clinic will respond to the needs of the target area beneficiaries by providing primary healthcare packages with emphasis on interventions such as timely treatment of the most common illnesses, antenatal/postnatal care, vaccination, nutrition screening, malnutrition treatments for children under five and pregnant and lactating women, and health and nutrition sensitization including childcare practices. The focus of the intervention will be children under five (CU5) and pregnant and lactating women (PLW), being them the most vulnerable population with the highest risk of health and nutrition problems, among internally displaced persons (IDPs), returnees, and host community members.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The humanitarian crisis is widespread in all the districts of the province of Cabo Delgado, including the neighboring provinces of Niassa and Nampula, where a large proportion of the internally displaced people have emigrated to. however, on one hand, based on the combination of the following indicators: health facility functionality, vaccination coverage, Acute watery diarrhea cases, IPC (Integrated, Food Security Phase Classification), nutritional risk levels and natural disaster risk level, to calculate Clusters People in Need and Severity, on the other hand, taking into account the history of ACF's presence in Cabo Delgado province since 2021, the target districts would be: Macomia, Quissanga, Mueda, Meluco and Muidumbe.Apart from providing support to 5 MOH Mobille brigade, one in each district, ACF will ensure linkage by adopting the essential package of health care provision for mobile brigades as per the MoH guidelines in Mobile clinic, Services will include outpatient consultations; life-saving emergency referrals as available based on IECHC Mozambique; ANC; PNC; supplemental immunization activities, screening for malnutrition and treatment of SAM and MAM with referrals to SC if required; and support to MoH during disease outbreak. MoH technicians will join the mobile clinic team for Expanded Program on Immunization (EPI) services.The mobile clinics will follow routine health information reporting systems, reporting the outreach data to the catchment facilities to ensure that the data is captured. ACF will obtain data from MoH for further submission to the health and nutrition clusters. Existing MoH reporting tools will be adopted.In addition, ACF will support local health facilities through the provision of operational and technical assistance. Support will include, but will not be limited to, capacity-building training for existing staff on health and nutrition topics; supportive supervision in collaboration with the health authorities both at district and state levels

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Providing Humanitarian assistance in general, primary health cares in particular to IDPs, Host communities and or any other targeted groups in emergency context comes with potential risks, these risks can arise from the complex and volatile nature of emergencies and the vulnerabilities of the beneficiaries. Some of potential risks include: 1. Physical and security risks: to beneficiaries- they are exposed to risks of violence, exploitation..etc., 2. Inequitable Services Delivery- Marginalized groups (e.g., Women, Children, disabled individuals may face barriers in accessing services, 3. Dependency- Over –reliance on humanitarian aid can undermine local Health systems or discourage long- terms sustainable solutions, 4 Data Privacy and Protection- Collecting sensitive health information without proper safeguards can expose IDPs to risks of exploitation, stigmatization or discrimination.As a way to avoid causing Harm, there are a lot of actions that will be taken, depending on the sort of risks, some of them are the following:1. Uphold Humanitarian Principles: The team will be Engaged to respect and apply humanitarian principles such as Humanity, Impartiality and neutrality.2. Conduct Rigorous Need Assessments: we will use inclusive and participatory methods to identify the specific health and nutrition needs of the beneficiaries, the team will also be encourage to pay more attention and prioritize the most marginalized groups such as women, Children, the elderly and people with disabilities3. Ensure Ethical and Quality Care: Staff will be trained on respecting beneficiary rights, Informed consent and Confidentiality there will be regular monitoring and evaluation services delivery to ensure high standards of the services and guarantee respect for human rights.4. Establish Accountability Mechanisms: complaint Mechanisms through the set up accessible , confidential channel for beneficiaries to report grievances or concerns and will be ensured all staff are bounded by strict guidelines prohibiting exploitation, abuse, or discrimination.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

To encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women, girls, children under 5 years of age, and people living with HIV or TB, the project will adopt the following strategies: 1. Provide affordable and Comprehensive Care -The project will offer free services for health and nutrition, including screenings, vaccinations and therapeutic feeding programs for children under 5, Ensure comprehensive care that integrate nutrition, maternal health, and disease management services to meet diverse needs.2. Encourage participation: Community Engagement: The project will engage local leaders, Women`s groups and youth representative in planning and implementing the activities and will establish feedback systems (e.g. suggestion boxes, focus groups) to ensure services are responsive to the needs of beneficiaries, especially those who have special need of attention.3. Involvement in Decision Making - women, people living with HIV/TB and caregivers of under 5 years children will be involved in decision-making process and their opinion will be taken in account in the provision of the services.4. Empowering Vulnerable groups- health education sessions on maternal health, nutrition, disease prevention and child care to empower women and girls to take control of their health5.Confidential and Stigma Free Services- the project will ensure clinics maintain confidentiality for sensitive services like HIV testing, TB treatment, and sexual and reproductive health6. Prioritizing Women, Girls, and Children Under 5 - The Project will prioritize the provision of ANC, PNC, immunizations, and growth monitoring for children

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

ACF has experienced staff with knowledge and skills to work with people with unique needs such as children, girls, women, PWDs, and older people. To ensure dignity, the staff will be sensitized to promote full and equal respect for the rights of all persons - irrespective of ethnicity, religion, language, education, socio-economic status, gender identity, age and disability, sexual orientation, and political views, recognizing that individuals have specific needs and face inequalities and risks, including facilitating access to all interventions. The project directly includes the disability component through activities addressing the specific needs of people with disabilities through identification, assessment, provision of services, and referral to specialized services if needed,the project will identify PWDS through: 1. Community Engagement and outreach- the project will conduct community awareness sessions to ensure people understand the importance of identifying and assisting individual with disabilities2. in Collaboration with a Partner, Train health staff, community actors and members to recognize signs of physical, sensory, intellectual, and psychosocial disabilities.3. Engage the community to identify people who might be excluded or isolated due to their disabilityTo remove barriers, the project will incorporate universal design principles in the provision of mobile clinic and Mobile brigade, such as removing the barriers in the deployment area. Accessibility training will be provided to staff to ensure sensitive and effective service delivery, while information will be disseminated in accessible formats (e.g., visual aids depending on needs). Regular feedback loops, such as community meetings and surveys, will allow persons with disabilities to voice any challenges they face, enabling the project to make adaptive changes to maintain inclusive and equitable access to all health services

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project will have no significant bearing upon any climate change and environmental sustainability issues. However, the effective management of the primary health care components will improve water and waste management at the level of health facilities with a positive spillover effect on the environment. Besides, any minor rehabilitation of health facilities will be conducted with a sustainable and environmental-sensitive approach
