Project Summary

GOALPrime intends to target a total of 10,000 (60% women and girls and 40% men and boys) are children including adolescent girls and boys and those living with disabilities in schools, camps, and host communities. A target of 5% of the overall individuals to be reached will be persons living with disability with Multi-sectoral case management services, Life skill education, Recreational support services, Positive parenting, alternative care placement of unaccompanied and separated boys and girls whose FTR support is unsuccessful, Counseling and awareness on child protection preventive measures across Maiduguri, Bama, Ngala LGA of Borno state and , Geidam,LGA, in Yobe state and Madagali, Michka LGA of Adamawa state.
The entire 5600 children to be reached will have access to continuous community-based and school-based recreational activities. Also, out of the total children target 400 adolescent girls and boys will be reached with life skills education, 200 girls and boys will be provided with multi-sectoral case management support services.
Working with the school-based and community-based child protection committees (SBCPCs/CBCPCs) and the School Based Management Committees (SBMCs), GOALPrime will continue to strengthen the
child protection mechanisms in both schools and the communities in the target LGAs. GOALPrime will continue to work with all stakeholders in the camps, host communities, and schools to enhance awareness on safeguarding and Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (SEA) and also with the CP and GBV sub-sectors and Partners in Borno; as well as the case management task force to develop or update and strengthen the referral pathways in the target LGAs.
The overall 10,000 target beneficiaries (including caregivers and people living with disability) will be reached with awareness raising on GBV prevention, PSEA, positive parenting, and child protection concern risk communication using tools developed by consultants in the GPON Humanity Studio via door-to-door, megaphones, motorized campaigns, and edutainment approaches. Using the community-based PSS as an entry point, GOALPrime will work with the CBCPCs to identify and profile Out of
School Children (OOSCs) who in collaboration with Education partners will be enrolled back to school. GOALPrime will be sensitive to the protection of the environment and
ensure that our approaches and tools do no harm to the environment and the beneficiaries.
GOALPrime, through her NHF-supported Humanity Studio, will also support CP AoR with the designs of the sub-sector's referral pathways, newsletters, advocacy notes, and other relevant resource materials.This project will support the continuity of CP service provision
to the children as well as CP AoR coordination as part of our mandate as Northeast CP AoR co-host National Organization.

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

By integrating every strategy with the protection principles, GOALPrime will mainstream protection (GBV & PSEA) throughout the execution of this project and advance meaningful access, safety, and dignity of both beneficiaries and of employers and volunteers. Safety, decency, and avoiding any kind of harm to both beneficiaries and employees are priorities for GOALPrime. Additionally, GOALPrime will also make sure that the target population has equitable access to the service, free from impediments and prejudice, in accordance with their needs. GOALPrime will pay attention to people and groups who could be especially vulnerable or have a hard time obtaining help and resources, including those with disabilities and GBV survivors who have been identified. GOALPrime will integrate GBV Risk Mitigation efforts to reduce or decrease GBV risk to beneficiaries. GOALPrime will work consciously in compliance with the Sphere Humanitarian Core Standards and Imperatives. All approaches will align with the principles of protection as we remain committed to ensuring that this project does not expose beneficiaries to more risk in compliance with the DO NO HARM Protection Principle. Also, GOALPrime will be intentional about maintaining an environment where there is zero Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (SEA). All staff, volunteers, contractors, and vendors engaged directly or indirectly to implement this project will be given PSEA orientation. They will be familiarized with GOALPrime's policy on PSEA, and they MUST express their unwavering commitment by signing GOALPrime's Zero Tolerance to SEA Code of Conduct. Also, GOALPrime will also lay down reporting protocols for the reporting of PSEA cases if unfortunately, they occur. GOALPrime will also ensure that the identity of the reporter(s) is kept very confidential. When convenient, GOALPrime will encourage that reports are made on anonymous grounds. Also, being guided by the Inter-Agency mechanism for reporting SEA, GOALPrime will ensure a systematized engagement with affected populations including confidential complaints and investigations procedures at both the system and individual agency levels. As recommended by IASC Task Force on PSEA which identified inter-agency cooperation in the creation and maintenance of community-based complaints mechanisms (CBCMs) as a key component in the prevention and response to SEA. GOALPrime will continue to raise awareness to the target population that humanitarian assistance is free and never conditioned on sexual favors as well as to be informed on how to access the appropriate complaints mechanisms, and support to set up in the community if SEA occurs. Furthermore, GOALPrime will ensure that established CBCMs in target locations are effective and that there is inter-agency coordination to ensure consistent messaging and that access to the mechanism is as broad and straightforward as possible for potential complainants. GOALPrime will also be intentional about the environment and safety of the communities where we implement by ensuring that our approaches and tools do not contribute to the degradation of the environment. In addition, GOALPrime will also integrate climate change and provide routine sensitization of the project beneficiaries on climate change prevention.

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

GOALPrime will be consolidating its established community-based project accountability mechanism through this project to improve community stakeholders and project beneficiary participation both in the project decision-making and implementation. This project was designed in consultation with the community stakeholders and children based on lessons learned from our previous implementation in the target LGAs. GPON uses a people-centered feedback mechanism to gather opinions, and complaints of the community members and beneficiaries on its project to inform decision-making for quality project implementation. However, lessons learned from the approach includes, forming an LGA-level project committee that will include all community groups' representative and stakeholders to improve participation of the affected community in the project implementation process. GPON has also made provision for anonymous feedbacks complaint/feedback boxes, SMSs, and direct calls to the Compliance and Accountability team. GPON project team will conduct an LGA inception workshop with the established community-based project committee to discuss the project deliverables and the community-level needs and how this project will contribute to resolving the needs. Also, the LGA project committee will work with the project team to define project beneficiaries’ vulnerability based on the context of the LGA, agree on vulnerability criteria, and provide a recommended approach on how the project can be implemented to better reach and serve the project targeted groups. Also, the project team will conduct quarterly review meetings with the community-based project committee at the LGA level to inform how each quarter can consolidate with the previous quarter's achievement to improve the quality of services rendered.
