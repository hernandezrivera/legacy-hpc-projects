Project Summary

On behalf of the Humanitarian Country Team (HCT), WFP will take the lead in coordinating and managing the Inter Agency Common Feedback Mechanism (CFM) in connection with delivery and impact of humanitarian assistance programs in Palestine and ensure 2-way communication channel with affected communities.
The CFM will be in the form of a toll-free, country-wide number that affected populations can call to obtain information on humanitarian assistance programs; submit their feedback and get referrals to the humanitarian organizations that are best-suited to handle their issues.

Implementing Partners

IA CFM membership is open to all UN agencies, INGOs, and NGOs and Cluster Coordinators in the State of Palestine. In order to become a formal member of the IA CFM, entities must endorse the SOPs, assign a permanent Focal Point, involve senior management as part of the IA CFM Steering Committee.
Donors and governmental institutions are not formally part of the IA CFM. However, engagement with the authorities and donors is essential to guarantee coordinated access to services, mobilize required resources and support visibility, outreach and advocacy efforts.
The IA CFM is operated by WFP on behalf of the Humanitarian Country Team.

1 - Needs

A functional common feedback mechanism (CFM) is a cornerstone of Accountability to Affected Population (AAP). Part of the CFM is having a functional Hotline in the form of a toll-free, country-wide number that affected populations can call to obtain information on humanitarian assistance programs; submit their feedback; and, get referrals to the humanitarian organizations that are best-suited to handle their issues. According to the Multisectoral Need Assessment that took place in July 2022, the findings found that 27% households in the Gaza Strip reported not receiving any assistance in the past 6 months. As well, 58% of the surveyed households in the Gaza Strip and 83% of the surveyed households in the West Bank reported their satisfaction of the received aid. In addition, 11% of surveyed households in the West Bank and Gaza Strip reported that women and girls avoid areas because they feel unsafe in specific areas.The above findings demonstrate the need for CFM to reach the people in general and those who are in need to understand their needs, concerns, follow up on their inquiries, complaints and address them.The CFM will enable the broad humanitarian community to collect feedback directly from the affected populations; better understand the needs on the ground; and, take better informed decisions. The CFM will systematically receive calls, queries, complaints, and requests from the affected population with the goal to address such calls taking account of the different needs within communities from an age, gender, and diversity perspective. CFM will refer cases to a partner or sector coordinator when additional information or follow-up is required. This may cover information needs, complaint/negative feedback, and case referral (as per agreed upon guidelines).Referrals are intended to be timely, accurate safe and secure method to ensure relevant pathways for information sharing are available in the right modality to the appropriate partner for resolution of issues.In emergencies precedent has shown that many humanitarian organizations’ in-house feedback mechanisms become compromised and/or inactive, best practice recommends using a single phone number as an entry point for queries from affected populations. Hence, a coordinated mechanism built on international humanitarian architecture allows members of the affected population easier access to answers, speeds up the resolution of issues and allows greater access for humanitarian decision-makers, including Government, thereby improving aid delivery efficiency.The CFM will be accessible for all Palestinians in Gaza Strip and West Bank, including East Jerusalem.

2 - Activities

For the CFM will enhance and support the HRP objective #1 through the following activities:- Establish a network connection with international and national organizations and clusters members to be part of the CFM in order to receive referrals for AAP queries received through the toll-free hotline. - Monthly meetings will take place with CFM members’ or focal points to provide key information and feedback to CFM- Sensitization activities about the common feedback , mainly referral, mechanism through different communications channels including distribution of leaflets/posters, SMS messages, and face to face sessions.- Referring the inquiries/requests/complaints to the specialized agencies for their follow up.- Conduct Needs and Trends analysis to be provided to the Inter-Sector Coordination groupWFP will engage with humanitarian stakeholders through the clusters and individual agencies and organization to understand the humanitarian needs, projects, specificity of the affected populations and based on that modify and update the CFM. The referral procedures will be prepared and discussed in consultation with the humanitarian stakeholders and will be linked to other functional CFM. This CFM will complement the CFM related to GBV and PSEA (handling sensitive cases) to ensure proper referral and follow up. A specific SoP has been developed depicting the links between the two CFMs, the referral of the cases, the follow up, the case management and the close of such sensitive cases.Affected populations can reach the IA CFM through the toll-free telephone number 1800-124-126. The inbound calls will be answered by the IA CFM Operators who will ask for consent to start registering the call (for quality assurance purposes), opening the case in the CRM system and start filling out the in-take form with the required information (see Annex I – In-take form).All IA CFM feedback and complaints shall be disaggregated by age and sex group, disability, type of complaint (based on standard categorization), geographic location, and other relevant factors to enable timely and effective call handling process, analysis and reporting. However, no identifying information will be collected by IA CFM users, registered in the system and shared with other actors (referrals) unless the information is necessary to further process the case and informed consent is previously obtained by the IA CFM usersThe IA CFM aims at ensuring all received calls are satisfactorily addressed as soon as possible, either through immediate information provided by the IA CFM operators to the IA CFM users and closure of the case on the spot (First Case Resolution – FCR) or through timely and relevant referral procedures and systematic closure of the loop with the IA CFM users within pre-defined timeframes. These calls are unique and cannot be addressed with a key message and require to be referred to the appropriate IA CFM Member (organization/agency/Cluster) upon agreement established with it. These calls are further categorized based on their priority level: High priority; These calls may require life-saving interventions or otherwise time-sensitive responses. adopted ticketing system. The Focal Point’s supervisor and relevant Cluster Coordination Team’s Focal Point (if different from the primary Focal Point) will be also notified to ensure close follow up and facilitate escalation, if needed. Calls are referred immediately to the relevant IA CFM Members’ Focal Points. Focal Points’ supervisors, relevant Cluster Coordination Teams’ Focal points and the IA CFM Manager will also be immediately notified and will have access to non-identifying information. For the referral of High priority cases, the classification will be indicated in the ticket’s name for easy reference, so that the receiving Focal Points can easily recognize and follow-up with particular attention.Regular Priority; These calls may be important but the caller will not be impacted excessively if the case is referred within 1 week. These cases are referred to the appropriate IA CFM Member’s Focal Point on a weekly. Calls received during the week are registered, stored and referred on a weekly basis in bulk to IA CFM Members’ Focal Points and/or relevant Cluster Coordination team’s Focal Point. These referrals should cover the previous week (including the previous weekend. Once a case or cases are received, the receiving IA CFM Focal Points should provide written confirmation through the IA CFM ticketing system acknowledging that they have received the case. In this message, the following information should be provided. Once the case has been managed, the receiving IA CFM Member’s Focal Point and/or relevant Cluster Coordination Team’s Focal Point should inform the IA CFM using the established ticketing system. Once the ticketing system has been updated with the taken action, the IA CFM operator who opened the ticket will receive a notification and will call the IA CFM user back and confirm with him/her about the action taken by the concerned actor or provide him/her with the feedback provided by the concerned actor. Whenever a case potentially related to SEA is received through the IA CFM, the IA CFM operators shall use the Significant Incident Report Form designed for this purpose by the PSEA Network. It is the operator’s responsibility to gather the relevant information from the complainant, enter it into the IA CFM’s ticketing system, and ensure that the allegation is referred immediately to the PSEA Network’s Coordinator, as per the process outlined in the PSEA Network’s SOPs. The allegation is immediately referred also to the PSEA FP of the organization to which the allegation pertains, if identified and if the organization is an IA CFM partner. Non-identifying details will be immediately shared also with the IA CFM Manager to ensure close follow up.

5.2 - Please briefly explain why the specific modality/ies for this response were chosen.

The Complaint Feedback Mechanism (CFM) supports the gathering, production and sharing of information required for ensuring feedback from beneficiaries and non-beneficiaries are received, heard and actioned. The CFM activities support partners to address key protection needs of vulnerable people in Palestine, and by promoting protection mainstreaming in programming. The CFM does not provide any cash or in-kind tangible assistance, however it is a service provision that is aiming at strengthening the humanitarian community’s accountability to affected population. Service provision by the CFM is delivered through meetings, workshops, and analysis, in cooperation with partners.

11. - Equal and impartial access to aid
11.1 - Equal and impartial access to aid

The CFM facilitates collective accountability to affected populations and consolidates limited resources that are currently only available to selected humanitarian organizations individually while putting the affected populations at the centre of humanitarian and development response. The affected population will have free access to common feedback mechanism (Hotline) as it is toll-free line. The hotline is functional 7 days per week. Specific consideration will be taken into account for hard-to-reach population and people with special needs (such as people with hearing and speech disability) to be able to access the common feedback mechanism and place inquires/requests and complaints
