1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

- Cap Anamur will support the MoH with the technical rehabilitation and reopening of destroyed health facilities in Muidumbe district. Furthermore, Cap Anamur will support with medical assistance, logistic support and gap filling with medicaments and equipment, to ensure basic medical services for the returned host population.- As in 2023, Cap Anamur will in 2024 continue support the Hospital Provincial Pemba with medical assistance, logistic support and gap filling with medicaments and equipment to improve the capacity as reference hospital for the Province of Cabo Delgado.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The project prioritises the rehabilitation and reopening of 5 von 7 currently non-functional health facilities in Muidumbe district and the Hospital Provincial in Pemba. Two remaining health facilities in Muidumbe are currently not accessible due to the security situation.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The current unsafe security situation in the eastern and southern part of Muidumbe limit the access to some district areas. A close monitoring and analysing of the dynamic and fast-changing security situation in the northern districts of the Cabo Delgado Province is essential for the implementation of the project. A Threat Analysis and security plan, including a realistic hibernation- and relocation plan has to be made and regularly updated. Due to the current insecurity, some areas of the district are still not accessible for health and worker.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

One of the main focus during the rehabilitation is to ensure that each health facility has a functional maternity ward.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

During the technical rehabilitation of health facilities the accessibility for disabled people will be considered and barriers will be removed.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project includes the setup of a waste management in each health facility, including training of health staff in using correctly incinerator.
