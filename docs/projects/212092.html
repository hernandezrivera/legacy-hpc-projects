Project Summary

A. The ongoing conflict in Syria, now entering its fourteenth year, has caused immense destruction and unimaginable losses to the people of Syria and all aspects of their lives. Protection indicators have tragically declined due to the war, the February 2023 earthquake, and successive crises. Various segments of society face violence, displacement, ignorance, illiteracy, and limited access to basic human rights. Hundreds of thousands have lost their lives, others have suffered varying injuries, and disability rates have alarmingly increased. Low levels of protection are accompanied by extremely negative socioeconomic impacts.
Sanjar and its countryside are witnessing a promising return of residents after stability, but they face significant challenges, including high unemployment rates, a lack of professional and artisanal skills in the area, and a shortage of equipment for tradespeople due to the war and the February 2023 earthquake. These events led to widespread destruction of infrastructure, resulting in significant economic, social, and livelihood burdens. In addition to economic crises, there are other social issues such as the absence of safe spaces for women, domestic violence, early and forced marriages, high divorce rates, irresponsible cousin marriages, all negatively impacting rural family life. High rates of disability are recorded among some children and youth due to the lack of care programs for people with disabilities. All these factors hinder early recovery efforts in the region.
B. We propose a socio-economic support project for families in Sanjar and its countryside that includes providing a safe space for women where they undergo vocational training workshops to empower women in society. There will also be vocational training for men, social workshops as part of an organized psychological support program, and life skills training workshops. The project will support non-formal education opportunities with the aim of eliminating illiteracy and ignorance in the area. Supportive recreational activities for life skills and disabled individuals will be provided, including training and referral to specialists to improve their situation.
C. customs and traditions and gender norms of men/high impact/mitigated by establishing a safe space dedicated to women and promoting correct concepts regarding women's rights and the need to combat violence against them.
Absence of women in the labor market/high impact/mitigation involves engaging women in awareness campaigns, workshops, and educational programs in schools and communities.
The inclination of locals to work in agriculture and animal husbandry while neglecting trades and artisanal professions/moderate impact/mitigation involves ensuring that training workshops do not conflict with women's and men's work in agriculture and animal husbandry.
The high impact risk posed by men or women not attending awareness sessions, supportive campaigns, or discussions can be mitigated by conducting a pre-training awareness campaign to encourage attendance.
D. Al-Birr and Social Services Association in Idleb adopts a code of conduct and protection policy that all employees and volunteers must adhere to. It prohibits any behavior indicating exploitation, harassment, or sexual abuse and does not tolerate any behavior that compromises the dignity or privacy of beneficiaries. The association has assistance and protection offices that receive complaints following confidential procedures ensuring the safety and security of complainants. They also provide training on protection policies and procedures for individuals and beneficiaries if necessary.
E. Theft/moderate impact/mitigation includes guards, alarm systems, and choosing a proper location.
Fires/moderate impact/mitigation involves fire extinguishers in the center and multiple emergency exit points.
Accompanying children with their families/moderate impact/mitigation involves rooms equipped with toys for children during sessions.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

We will start by engaging beneficiaries through key individuals and supporters within the areas. We will have focused sessions at the community center that include comprehensive awareness on the topics at hand conducted by specialized individuals such as lawyers and advisors to educate beneficiaries about their rights and duties, with an average of two sessions per week at the community center or during visits where the team identifies cases that require legal, civil, or initial psychological assistance. Workshops will also be conducted involving both genders on general protection topics with the aim of prevention and mitigation.
