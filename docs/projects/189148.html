Project Summary

The United Nations Humanitarian Air Service (UNHAS), managed by the World Food Programme (WFP), offers safe, reliable, cost-efficient and effective passenger and light cargo transport for the wider humanitarian community to and from areas of crisis and intervention. It is the only humanitarian air service that gives access to all humanitarian entities. UNHAS responds to the need for access to the South Sudan’s most remote and challenging locations, often under precarious security conditions, where no safe surface transport or viable commercial aviation options are available.
In 2022, UNHAS is targeting to provide air transport services to 65,000 passengers and 2000 metric tons (MT) light humanitarian cargo from 313 registered humanitarian organizations to approximately 54 regularly scheduled locations in South Sudan.
The main objective of UNHAS is to continue supporting Humanitarian community with a safe, effective, cost-efficient and reliable air services. In addition to serving destinations on the fixed regular schedule, UNHAS will support 100 percent of non-scheduled location booking requests for emergencies response (security relocations, medical evacuations and Inter-Cluster Working Group Rapid Response Missions).
UNHAS requires $51,400,000 to sustain its operations across the year and continue supporting the entire humanitarian community.

1 - Needs

The physical infrastructure of South Sudan is one of the major challenges of humanitarian operations throughout the country, causing delays and adding substantial costs. The road network, primary mode of transportation, is among the most underdeveloped in the world. Air transport remains a necessity to enable humanitarian operations in the country. Movement of humanitarian personnel and cargo from the major hubs to areas of operation is challenging, if not impossible, without a viable air service. Especially during the rainy season, when roads and ports significantly deteriorate, the reliance on costly air transport exponentially increases. Only four airstrips are equipped with tarmac (Juba, Malakal, Paloich and Wau). Another 234 landing sites (including 119 suitable for Fixed-Wing aircrafts and 115 helipads) are functioning in the country, enabling humanitarian actors to serve populations in hard-to-reach locations. However, due to low maintenance and very limited infrastructure, a large majority of these airstrips becomes accessible to helicopters only during the rainy season, while others become non-accessible at all for any type of air assets. There are no reliable commercial options in the country serving deep field locations and UNHAS remains the only common air service for the entire humanitarian community. While other humanitarian air services exist, these are not available to the wider humanitarian community. UNHAS continue to work in close collaboration with other air services, including UNMISS, ICRC, MAF, and Samaritan’s Purse, sharing information on airstrip conditions, safety, etc. UNHAS anticipates that the 2021 high-level of humanitarian air transport need will continue throughout 2022 due to the consequences of floods, COVID-19 global pandemic, armed clashes, inter-communal fighting, etc.

2 - Project activities

Under strategic outcome 4 of its Interim Country Strategic Plan (ICSP), WFP works to ensure that the humanitarian community has access to reliable common services until satisfactory alternatives are available. The main objectives of this proposal are to: Provide non-governmental organizations (NGOs), United Nations (UN) agencies, donor organizations and diplomatic missions with reliable, safe, efficient, and effective air service to access remote and isolated programme implementation locations. Transport light humanitarian cargo, e.g. medical supplies, specialized food commodities, high-value equipment.1. Provide medical evacuation and security relocation capacity for the humanitarian community.2. Facilitate access to common logistics services, including cargo transport by air, road and river and common storage services3. Provide logistics coordination, information management, and preparedness services to the humanitarian community
