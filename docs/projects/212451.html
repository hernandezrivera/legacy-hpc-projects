Project Summary

The project aims to provide comprehensive support to WASH activities, offering a lifesaving response to enhance access to inclusive, accessible, conflict-sensitive, and gender-sensitive WASH services. Targeting 571,443 crisis-affected individuals in Syria aligns with the key objectives of the WASH cluster. The project strives to ensure regular water, sanitation, and waste management services (Objective 1), deliver humanitarian WASH supplies and services while promoting hygienic behaviors (Objective 2), enhance WASH coordination structures (Objective 3), and improve facilities/services in camps to minimize sub-standard conditions (Objective 4).
Needs:
The ongoing Syrian crisis has strained socioeconomic foundations, leading to a dire need for comprehensive WASH support. Cholera outbreaks reflect the deteriorating water situation, necessitating substantial investments in WASH infrastructure. Communities face challenges related to inadequate sewage, waste management, and hygiene practices, contributing to waterborne diseases.
Intervention and Implementation of Activities:
1- Solar Power Systems: Implementing stations in Afrin, Idleb, Ariha, and Harim for free water provision to 86,360 individuals through 6 pumping stations, reducing costs and ensuring safe water.
2- Repair and Rehabilitation of Water Systems: Addressing challenges in accessing safe water, benefiting 60,758 individuals in Harim, Afrin, and Azaz.
3- Solid Waste Management: Providing essential equipment for garbage containment and preventing environmental degradation, benefiting 190,624 individuals in Afrin.
4- Sanitation/Sewage Repair and Rehabilitation: Mitigating health risks by rehabilitating damaged sewage networks in Azaz, Afrin, and Harim, benefiting 196,753 individuals.
5-WASH Facilities for Schools: Maintaining and constructing WASH facilities in Azaz and Afrin districts to benefit 30,000 pupils, ensuring hygiene and reducing interruptions.
6-Hygiene Kits for Elderly & PwDs: Providing essential hygiene materials for 580 people with disabilities in Azaz camps and 1,000 PWDs in Al-Bab, ensuring their special needs are met for 6 months.
7-Complete WASH Support for Camps: Offering daily clean water, solid waste management, septic tank desludging, latrine cleaning, and provision of cleaning materials for vulnerable communities in Al-Bil camps for 5,368 individuals, Soran, Azaz, and Aleppo.
Protection Risk Analysis:
Addressing diverse vulnerabilities related to gender, age, and vulnerable groups, IRW conducts FGDs, ensuring appropriate facility locations and community consultations. The implementation of facilities and adherence to policies uphold accountability to crisis-affected communities.
PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) Strategy:
IR's CFM employs multiple channels for complaints and feedback, prioritizing high-risk complaints. Confidentiality, data protection, and safe access options for marginalized groups are ensured through various communication means.
Risks, Assumptions, and Mitigating Measures:
1-Escalation of Hostilities: High likelihood - High effect. Adaptation of action plans in consultation with donors to ensure emergency WASH activities for new arrivals.
2-Challenges in Money Transfer: Very low likelihood - High effect. Financial transfers through PTT bank branches minimize risks.
3-Child Labor: Low likelihood - High effect. IR ensures proper registration, age verification, and a zero-tolerance stance in contracts.
4-GBV/Safety around Latrines: Assuring appropriate safety measures and awareness campaigns to mitigate risks related to gender-based violence.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Amid the Syrian crisis, the WASH project, targeting 571,443 individuals, strives to mitigate the profound challenges faced by communities. The pressing need for water, sanitation, and hygiene services is evident, underscored by the outbreak of cholera. Through strategic interventions aligned with WASH cluster objectives, the project envisions comprehensive support for affected populations. The multi-faceted activities include solar power systems, and addressing water challenges in Afrin, Idleb, Ariha, and Harim. This initiative ensures free water provision to 86,360 individuals through renewable energy sources, reducing costs and promoting sustainability. Repair and rehabilitation of water systems respond to earthquake-induced damages, benefitting 60,758 individuals in Harim, Afrin, and Azaz. Solid waste management initiatives, providing essential equipment in Afrin, contribute to a clean environment, reducing the spread of diseases. The repair, rehabilitation, and operational support to sanitation/sewage networks in Azaz, Afrin, and Harim aim to mitigate health risks, benefiting 196,753 individuals. Schools in Azaz and Afrin districts will witness the construction and maintenance of WASH facilities, ensuring a conducive environment for 30,000 pupils. Hygiene kits tailored for the elderly and persons with disabilities (PwDs) prioritize the unique needs of 580 individuals in Azaz camps and 1,000 PwDs in Al-Bab. This six-month initiative aligns with prior distributions and emphasizes the importance of maintaining dignity through accessible hygiene materials. Complete WASH support for camps in Al-Bil, Soran, Azaz, and Aleppo is a pivotal aspect of the project. The provision of clean water, solid waste management, septic tank desludging, latrine cleaning, and essential cleaning materials addresses critical needs, preventing disease transmission. The Protection Risk Analysis is integrated into the project's design, ensuring facilities' appropriate locations and adherence to policies.
