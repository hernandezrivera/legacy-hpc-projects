Project Summary

The project seeks to contribute to the protection and improving the living conditions of newly displaced people and the protracted internally displaced through the provision of NFI kits and Emergency kits.
The projects will target (32,376 PIN) and respond immediate needs of IDPs, those displaced more recently, and returnees that are affected by natural and man-made disasters; through the distribution of Non-Food Item (NFI) kits to 4054 HH from affected population and an Emergency Shelter Kits to 1342 HH from the affected population. The project seeks to maintain the emergency response capacity to distribute NFI minimum packages. Mercy Corps will ensure a timely response to sudden-onset disasters and will take into account the specific needs of women and, where appropriate, women’s dignity kits will be distributed.
To improve accountability; Post-Distribution Monitoring (PDM) will be done and feedback mechanisms will be done as part of the implementation. Mercy Corps will use its existing track record to successfully implement the proposed shelter program in Kismayo, Afmadow, and Biodata.

Mitigation Measures

1a. Mercy Corps has updated security and safety plans in place to prevent and respond to threats. Mercy Corps works closely with UN agencies, local partner organizations, and stakeholders to share security information, coordination, and joint security emergency response including evacuation and relocation. Furthermore, MC has strong community networks at the locality level and the level of acceptance is generally good. 4b.Mercy Corps team members have a responsibility to ensure that our beneficiaries and community members are treated with dignity and respect and that certain minimum standards of behavior are observed. Mercy Corps committed to training all members on PSEA which is mandatory for team members to gain greater awareness of the organization's awareness.Do no harm practice is encouraged. It’s critical for team members to practice do no harm. 4c.To mitigate this risk, Mercy Corps staff are oriented to and required to follow a code of conduct that reflects the values of Mercy Corps. All staff must sign the fraud, bribery and corruption policy and whistleblowing policy. Employees know and have provided written consent to our policy that any breach may lead to disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment. Non-employee team members understand that any breach may lead to early termination of the assignment. In addition, Mercy Corps program and operation systems have clear disaggregation with clear roles and responsibilities assigned in accordance with an approved “Approval Authority Matrix” with the aim to reduce collision or abuse or theft.
