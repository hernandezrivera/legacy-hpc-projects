Project Summary

Protection Gabs in kass locality and South Jeble marra
• There are no protection services in entire area of Gorlangbang cluster
• Communities indicated that they are facing challenges when they go out for farming, fetching water or collected firewood especially in the government controlled areas.
• Due to insecurity, they couldn’t manage to access their farming lands since 2018, especially in the areas of Sabon Elfagor, Gobo and Kawar village.
• There is lack of awareness raising among women, girls and boys on GBV and other risks related to protection. This led to occurance of GBV and put them at protection risks.
• There are no community safe spaces such as (community centers, women centers or child friendly spaces) in the all 27 villages including Gorlangbang.
• In all area, there is no registration or birth certificates system which is means that, all children are being without birth certificates and national ID.
• Early marriage is observed among girls and women.
• Lack of knowledge on Covid-19 and social distancing in Gorlangbang.
• Along the road from Gubo to Gorlanbang, the team observed presence of ERW due to shelling from SAF.
• Women are subjecting to rape and killing by militia while on their way to collect firewood, farming or go to markets points located nearby areas control by government.
• Many girls are dropped out of schools due to economic reasons and the limitation of the education facilities.
• There No women or community centers and No any GBV activities implemented/provided to the community.
• Poverty, insecurity, lack of access, and extremely poor road conditions are the root causes of GBV.
• Role of law is completely absent in the entire area of Gorlangbang and its surrounding villages
• Awareness on human rights, women rights, and child rights has never been provided to the community.
• Many women and young girls are illiterate, and the community highlighted the need for literacy classes.
• women are performing heavy duties to support their families even while they are pregnant. The protection concern from these heavy duties may lead to many complications during pregnancy and may lead maternal death.
• Lack of trained midwives in the area, community reported that there are only 2 trained midwives support pregnant women in the 27 villages including Gorlangbang.
Activities :
- Establishment of Community- based protection networks
- Establishing child friendly spaces. - Provision of recreational kits for school dropped children.
- Provide tangible protection assistances to at risk individuals and most vulnerable people including PSNs and PWD.
- Awareness raising sessions needed to be conducted for women, girls and atrisk individuals to mitigate protection risks among the community. - Community awareness on child rights and protection - Training for community volunteers on PSS
- Community awareness on UXOs.
- Advocate for birth registration and issuance of national identities.
- Vocational training for youth.
- Economic empowerment/life skills of women and girls through IGAs, Provide training for women and girls on (food production, handcraft, and management of small projects) and provision of seed money for small projects.
- Increasing awareness raising on Covid-19.
– There is need to send Risk Education (RE) teams to conduct Explosive Remnant of War ERW and conduct awareness sessions for people of Gorlanbang.
- Recommend sending clearance teams to conduct EOD and conduct training for focal points.
- Opening the blockage on the road to allow UNMAS team to conduct road verification and make sure that all roads to Gorlanbang are safe.

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

The following elements must be takeninto account in all humanitarian activities:1 –Prioritize safety & dignity, and avoid causing harm: prevent and minimize as much as possible anyunintended negative effects of your intervention which can increase people's vulnerability to bothphysical and psychosocial risks2 –Meaningful Access: arrange for people’s access to assistance and services - in proportion to needand without any barriers (e.g. discrimination). Pay special attention to individuals and groups whomay be particularly vulnerable or have difficulty accessing assistance and services.3 – Accountability: set-up appropriate mechanisms through which affected populations can measurethe adequacy of interventions, and address concerns and complaints4 – Participation and empowerment: support the development of self-protection, capacities andassist people to claim their rights, including - not exclusively - the rights to shelter, food, water andsanitation, health, and education. incorporation of protection principles into aid delivery, humanitarian actors can ensurethat their activities target the most vulnerable, enhance safety, dignity, and promote and protect thehuman rights of the beneficiaries without contributing to or perpetuating discrimination, abuse,violence, neglect and exploitation.Protection can be mainstreamed:• By including the above elements in the general project management as central to deliveringhumanitarian aid;• By incorporating the above elements into project cycle management through protectionmainstreaming indicators for each sector.
