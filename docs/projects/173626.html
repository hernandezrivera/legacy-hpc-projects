Project Summary

Civic-19 has affected formal educational system severely and continuity of learning environment in both public and private schools in urban and rural setting upset at high level. School closure disrupts the delivery of in-person instruction which has negative impacts at the individual and societal levels and carries an immense potential to increase the existing disparities between different socio-economic strata of the society. In the country the educational activities are standstill since March 2020, only the schools were partially opened from mid of September to end of November 2020. Now the schools are again closed due to second layer of COVID -19 pandemic. Information about COVID-19 in children is still somewhat limited but the children with COVID-19 can spread infection to others due to close contact with their peers at the schools.
Apart from the 19.1 million children already out of school and ongoing efforts to provide educational access for them, the closure of education institutions due to COVID-19 has directly impacted 40 Million school going learners from pre-primary and primary to higher secondary levels. The situation will magnify the risks and vulnerabilities of an already weak education system with threats emanating from both endogenous and exogenous factors. The continued closure of education institutions means the loss of learning for children that will further widen the gap between the expected years of schooling and learning adjusted years of schooling and will potentially increase dropout rates and therefore the number of out of school children.
The crisis has the potential to further increase educational disparities as a result of the spillover effects of an economic recession, due to COVID-19, which will magnify the existing rural-urban, gender, and socio-economic divide with the potential to increase the prevalence of child labor both in the short and long run. There is a great risk of children being pulled out of education due to the increased need for child labor or to assist their parents in work and at homes. In crisis situations, girls are twice as likely to be out of school and face greater barriers to continuing their education.
In future educational institutions can play an important role in slowing the spread of diseases, protecting vulnerable students/staff and to ensure a safe and healthy learning environment. By considering the facts in the crises situation, it has been proposed that HIN will provide the support to 100 Public schools for their safe and secure re-opening and increase the enrollment of children in SWAT and SHNAGALA district of Khyber Pakhtoonkhwa. Through this project interventions HIN will ensure that Schools have regular environmental cleaning, disinfection and handwashing services available by provision of hygiene packages including IPC material. HIN will also ensure training of teachers to perform environmental cleaning at school level. Children will also be oriented to ensure personal safety and care at the schools and home. Targeted schools will also be provided with the Risk communication materials which will be displayed/distributed among the children and teachers. Teacher capacity will also build on life Skill based education, Psycho social support and modern teaching methodology including teaching through different modules particularly distance learning. Teaching and learning material both for teachers and children will be provided in the form of supplementary material and study guide for the teachers. Training and capacity building workshop of school management comities will also be done to ensure effective school management and children access to education. The damaged schools repair and rehabilitation will also be done in the flood affected areas of propose districts. During the project implementation Community participation, Gender with Age Marker (GAM) and safeguard to Prevent Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) will be ensure in each stage.

1- Project Activities

• Identification and Selection of Public Schools affected areas At the initial stage the school identification and selection will be carried out. A comprehensive assessment of education needs and resources for different level and type of education is undertaken with the participation of key stakeholders. Disaggregated data collection process will be undertaken for education response and assess the most urgent needs. • Repair and rehabilitation of damaged schools in rain /flood affected areasRepair and rehabilitation of damage schools on need based will reinstate educational facilities. All those facilities will provide children’s a perfect protection, gender sensitive and conduced environment. The no of school buildings will be repair and rehabilitated for making better access and learning environment. • Mobilize communities and School Management Committees An active community participation in planning and prioritizing are important to ensure safe, effective and equitable delivery of education in the schools. Community involvement and ownership in the schools enhance the accountability, strengthen to local resources mobilization and support the maintenance of educational facilities in the longer time. School management committee members will be oriented and trained to plays a vital role to ensure effective school management and children access to education. • Teachers training on Child Friendly schools, life skills-based education, psychosocial support and teaching methodologies including teaching through different modalitiesAccess to education is only meaningful, if the educational interventions contribute quality teaching and learning support for the vulnerable children. Teaching and learning practices in the area remain mired in old age methodologies. Popular teaching methodologies are rote memorization and lecture method. Out dated teaching and learning practices lead to poor educational outcomes. To overcome the deficiency teacher training workshop will be arranged for all teachers. • Implementation of safe school operation protocols (Infection Prevention and Control-IPC). Safe and secure environments provide protection from all threats in the schools. To ensure safe School Operation Protocol-IPC, regular environmental cleaning, disinfection and handwashing services at school will done through provision Hygiene Packages including IPC material, Orientation of teachers of safe school operation protocols (SOPs) along dissemination and distribution of risk communication material among the children with key messages. • Provide teaching and learning material for teachers and children Teaching and learning reading material related to curriculum, professional development and tools which help in the pandemic situation will be distributed. Children will be provided the learning material in various forms and teachers will also be provided the study guides for continuing teaching through assignment basis. • Support of home-based learning, remedial and catch up classes and alternate The effects of the COVID-19 crisis on learning are particularly worrisome and its especially true at the primary level as basic competencies are the building blocks for all education. The project will support home based learning, remedial and catch up classes during the school closure
