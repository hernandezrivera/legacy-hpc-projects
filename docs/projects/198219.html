Project Summary

In line with the strategic recommendations of the UN InterAgency SEA Working Group in Burkina Faso, Viamo Burkina will set up a channel for feedback and complaints related to SEA via the dedicated Affected Communities Chapter on the 3-2-1 service – an interactive voice response hotline containing essential information in French and local languages on Health, Livelihoods, Education, Weather etc.
The 3-2-1 service is available 24h/day and can be reached for free (4 free calls per month, 25 FCFA per call after the free calls) by Orange-Burkina subscribers (11 M) by calling the shortcode 321 via a (simple) mobile phone. Once on the service, the caller can navigate between theme and topic menus by using his phone keypad, listen to messages and/or respond to questions. The service has existed in Burkina since May 2017 and receives (November 2022) around 650.000 calls per month.
The Affected Communities Chapter on 3-2-1 is oriented towards refugees, internally displaced persons and host communities in Burkina Faso and contains messages on GBV, Child Protection, Documentation, Security, Social Cohesion and Livelihood Opportunities which have been developed and validated by the Community Engagement and Accountability Working Group (CEAWG), financed by UNHCR and published on the 3-2-1 service in April 2022.
Together with the Burkina Faso SEA inter-agency network working group, CEAWG and the CBCM/CFM Inter-agency Steering Committee led by UNHCR, Acted, WFP and Tin Tua , a set of messages on SEA in 6 languages will be developed and added to the existing messages for the Affected Communities on the 3-2-1 Service. Listeners to messages on the Affected Communities Chapter and/or specifically on SEA will be asked if they have encountered situations of SEA. If so, the listener will be registered as a potential victim or witness of SEA and assigned a ticket. A dedicated Feedback Mechanism Manager will verify the registrations/tickets and refer them to the appropriate respondents who will further handle each ticket. An SMS alert per ticket will be sent to the respondents, who will indicate on the platform if and when they have accepted to start working on the ticket.

The feedback mechanism will be available 24h/day and will be promoted via regular broadcasts of the SEA messages by local radios in the high insecurity zones of Burkina Faso, via flyers in the refugee camps and the host communities, and via demonstration sessions for women and girls in the refugee camps.
Statistical and demographic platform data on listenership and SEA tickets (number of listeners, number of tickets, number of listened messages; gender, age and geographic distribution etc.) will be used for analysis and improvement of the CFM, and quarterly mobile surveys will be executed in order to complement the user data with user satisfaction-related data.

2 - BESOINS, ACTIVITÉS ET RÉSULTATS ATTENDUS
2.1. Décrivez les besoins, activités et résultats attendus

By providing a 24h/24 channel for information on AAP/SEA and a direct entry point for questions, complaints and/or testimonials of SEA victims and witnesses, the SEA-CFM via mobile phone responds to an urgent need of information on SEA and on how to signal cases of SEA in Burkina Faso. Lack of access to information was one of the major limiting factors cited by displaced persons and host community members in Burkina Faso who were surveyed about their living conditions by resp. Ground Truth Solutions, Fondation Hirondelle and REACH during 2021. Preferred channels of information cited by the interviewed persons were respectively radio, telephone, IDP leaders and community leaders. The Internet is practically inaccessible outside urban areas. While local radio can theoretically satisfy large numbers, its reach and impact can be affected by its coverage radius, by the number of people owning radio receivers, by the fact that its content is volatile, and by the fact that it is one-directional. The multitude of poorly managed accountability mechanisms offered by the humanitarian organisations too has contributed to a sentiment of non-responsiveness among the target groups, probably limiting the number of SEA cases being reported. And while IDP and community leaders were seen as important and trusted channels of information, many interviewees also indicated that their (im)partiality was a source of concern. Simple mobile telephones are widely available, and network coverage is relatively good in Burkina Faso (though some areas may be affected by terrorist attacks against network infrastructure). Stored vocal information on hotlines has the advantage of being available at any time, of being accessible for poor and for non-literate populations, of enabling real-time collection of user and usage data, and of allowing the user to interact with the offered content.The SEA-CFM via mobile phone should thus contribute to an accessible and solid SEA case reporting system, complementing other existing channels on SEA. The overall aim of the project is to provide an easy-to-access and anonymous channel for feedback and reports on SEA to the population in high-risk areas in Burkina Faso (+10 million people), and to set up a mechanism that is cost-effective and sustainable over a longer period of time. We expect the project to reach more than 500.000 persons via the 3-2-1 service, the listener groups and the radio broadcasts. Viamo’s platform will generate exact figures of listeners to the SEA messages and users of the feedback channel. The expected direct impact of this project is that populations at risk and victims of SEA are better informed on SEA and use this (and other) feedback mechanism(s) to report on cases of SEA. The activities can be divided into the following main parts:- Online training and certification on PSEA for project staff (2 p)- Content production, validation and pretesting of the SEA messages for the 3-2-1 service (Viamo, HCR-Burkina , SEA interagency Network and CEAWG)- Close monitoring of 3-2-1 statistics (monthly reports) (Viamo)- Survey design, implementation and reporting (Viamo, the CBCM/CFM Inter-agency Steering Committee Burkina and SEA coordinator)- Design and setup of feedback mechanism on SEA (Viamo and SEA coordinator) - Training of the persons who will be designed as respondents to the SEA-related tickets (Viamo and SEA coordinator)- Training of the members of the 5 regional CBCM hubs on the use of the 3-2-1 service (Viamo and SEA coordinator)- Purchase and distribution of 300 phone+solar kits to the 5 regional hubs (Viamo and SEA coordinator)- Training of 300 community committees on the use of the 3-2-1 service (CBCM hubs)- Women and girls listener group sessions (Community Committees)- Daily monitoring of tickets, referral to respondents (Viamo)- Promotion of the 3-2-1 service via Orange-Burkina SMS campaigns and local radio broadcasts (Viamo)- On-site listener sessions (90 sites)
