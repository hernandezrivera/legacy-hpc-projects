Project Summary

The project seeks to support the coordination and COVID-19 preparedness and response to front-line NGO aid workers in light of the COVID-19 pandemic. Support mechanisms will ensure inclusion of distance consultations and detection and treatment referral pathways and improvement of health facilities. The facilities that will be made available will allow for testing of the virus and be equipped to address the needs of NGO aid workers should they test positive for COVID-19 and need urgent assistance.

1 - Identified Needs

"The more than 862,000 Rohingya Refugees currently residing in 34 camps in Cox’s Bazar are highly vulnerable to COVID-19 due to overcrowded living conditions, poor WASH infrastructure. Rohingya refugee camps are among the most densely populated place on earth with more than 42,000 people/ km2 and physical distancing measures are expected to have a limited impact. Poor food consumption scores and underlying malnutrition contribute to weakened immune status of individuals. Elderly persons above the age of 60 and those with underlying health conditions are at higher risk of developing serious disease and will be affected disproportionally. As of 29 November 2020, Bangladesh has 462,407 confirmed cases, including 5,536 in Cox’s Bazar, 356 which are in the refugee camps. Starting in September 2020, level of activities requiring direct staff engagement in camps has increased as a result of the relaxation of the restrictions set ion March 2020 as means to reduce the potential for the spread of COVID-19 among the beneficiary population. Larger presence of humanitarian staff on the ground unfortunately does increase the potential for infection, with a number of infected cases requiring specialized medical assistance. As the capacity in then operational area to provide the necessary health support continues to be limited, and in order to be able to dully exercise the Duty of Care for humanitarian personnel, it is essential to maintain a response capacity that would include quick and quality health service."

2 - Activities or outputs

Support coordination, collaboration and information management to humanitarian staff responding to the COVID-19 pandemic in the camps. Establish a COVID-19 Medical Treatment Facility (MTF) to provide medical care that is compliant with WHO guidelines on infection prevention and control and manage severe acute respiratory infections and other complications resulting from COVID-19 infections. Procurement of PPE and medical supplies for the detection and treatment facility.
