Project Summary

This project will target the areas (Hazano, Afrin and Jandiris) that have witnessed a significant increase in water-related
diseases which have been confirmed by medical reports.
The project is aiming at eliminating the causes of the widespread water-related diseases in Hazano, Afrin and Jandiris through the following actions:
- Rehabilitation of water and sewage networks to avoid mixture of sewage with water, which is the main cause for waterborne disease;
- Provision of clean drinking water using mobile water treatment plant to treat water and supply it to the network
- Provision and installation of prefabricated latrines in camps in Der Hassan camps

Needs

According to HNO 2018 - Access to safe water remains limited for much of the population,with increased water quality assurances necessary to ensure the population hascontinuous access to safe water. - Alternate water supply services and WASH supplies areavailable but they are relatively expensive, pushing vulnerable families to adopt unsafecoping strategies. - Water and sewage networks require increased support, includingpower supply, to continue to provide a minimum level of services - People in newlyaccessible (like Afrin) lack sufficient access to critical WASHservices and critical WASH supplies. The project will contribute significantly toreducing water-related diseases. According to Hand in Hand for Aid and Development teamobservations, the main reason for the spread of these diseases in the targeted areas (Afrin, Hazano) is the erosion of theinfrastructure due to negligence and inability to carry out the maintenance and the lack ofsupport to the village infrastructures. As for the sewage lines, it has not undergone any maintenance workfor more than 20 years. The erosion of sewage lines leads to the leakage of polluted waterinto the water network and groundwater reserves in the area, which poses a significantdirect danger to the beneficiaries. In addition, the lack of expansion of the sewage networkled the beneficiaries to drill random septic tanks in different locations of thevillage. The conditions of personal safety and the environment are not taken into accountwhen digging these random septic tanks, in addition to the contamination of drinkingwater, there have been cases of falls in some of the septic tanks, because they have nocovers or protection for individuals, especially children. Since the women are dealing with household duties, which includes washing, cooking, cleaning and childcare, they need easy access to water the most. Having access to safe and clean water at their houses will greatly contribute to the safety of women, adolescent girls and children. Not only mitigating water-related health risks, their risks of encountering harassment, GBV, decreased assess to other humanitarian services (such as health, education) will be reduced significantly instead of spending time in queuing or carrying water to their homes. Women and girls with disabilities are two times at higher risk of neglect and exclusion from the families and the service providers, to compare with their peers without disabilities. By providing disability sensitive toilette facilities will contribute to maintaining their self-esteem and dignity. In December 2019, Hand in Hand for Aid and Development held meetings with local councils and health officials in these areas. These meetings revealed the following needs which lead to the spread of diseasesThe diseases ate mainly due to the following technical problems:- Overloading of the pumping stations in the targeted areas - the stations cannot supply enough water to people- Low or no maintenance of drinking water facilities (water pumping stations, water tanks and water networks) in Hazano, Afrin and Jandiris Due to lack of technical capacity of the local council in the area to maintain and operate the pumping water in addition to the poor economic situation of people to pay for the fuel to run the stations - no repairs of significant leakage in the water network pipes, leading to mixing with sewerage water and the spread of water-related diseases.The water pumping station in Hazano is the only station in the area supplying water, it was intended to pump clean water to serve 7000 people while the current population is 23,000 living in the village and has not undergone any maintenance or expansion of for a long time. inefficient and old sewage networks in Hazano and Jandiris. HIHFAD team technical teams visited the locations and confirmed the needs and estimated to project cost and prepare the BoQs and all needed technical studies

Implementation of activities

All administrative work will be managed by respective departments of finance, logistics,and human resources in Gaziantep Office while coordination and transactions in the fieldwill be done by the respective field teams. There will be regular visits to the sites by the WASH manager and technical team from Turkey to ensure that the implementation of activities is according to the required quality. Activities will be distributed in the followingorder: 1- Maintain and rehabilitate existing water pumpingstations in the area and add the necessary accessories to provide clean water to thebeneficiaries 2-Maintenance and rehabilitation of water networks and water tanks toensure proper water access 3-Ensure the safety and maintenance of the sewage networkand drinking water to reach new homes in the targeted areas 4-Providing support for thecontinued pumping of water at the pumping stations, in particular, the provision of fuel forthese stations and the technical and technical support of the workers in these stations 5 -The hygiene promotion campaigns to advise the beneficiaries and involve the communitygroups in the project and explain the importance of the project and the importance of thecontribution of the local community in various phases of the project 6 - provision and installation of prefabricated latrines in camps in Deir Hassan campscluster
