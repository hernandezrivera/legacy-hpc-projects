Project Summary

WVZ in collaboration with the Early Recovery Cluster and as part of of the Government of Zimbabwe's overarching Drought mitigation Strategy will seek to facilitate early recovery of 37, 755 beneficiaries in the affected communities of Nkayi , Umzingwane, Mbire , Kariba, Insiza, Harare, Mberengwa & Insiza and at the same time build their resilience to withstand the impact of future disasters through disaster risk reduction activities. This action will therefore aim to strengthen the sustainability of community-based activities and rebuild livelihood support mechanisms that have been adversely affected by the 90% likely Elnino, focusing on activities that promote sustainable livelihoods and enhance long-term coping strategies to the drought. In Zimbabwe drought is a perennial problem that has adversely affected the livelihoods many communities therefore To achieve poverty eradication, special focus is needed on strengthening the resilience of poor rural households and their livelihoods to shocks. This is also critical for ensuring Household food security and nutrition and sustainable natural resource management.
This requires both enhancing the capacity of poor rural people to manage the risks they
face and lowering the level of exposure and vulnerability to them. Therefore WVZ will focus on Disaster Risk Reduction , Natural Resources Management, Climate Smart Agriculture as well as Economic development as guided by the Minimum Economic Recovery Standards (MERS). WVZ will as guided by the Ultra Poor Graduation Model focus on the following activities
1. Rehabilitation of community productive assets
2. Cash for Assets/ Work
3. Savings for Transformation (S4T)
4. Income Generating Activities
5. Disaster Risk Reduction in communities & Schools through the CBDRM Model
6. Natural Resource Management ( Cash for Work
7. Women & Youth Skills Development
