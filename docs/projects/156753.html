Project Summary

the project aims to strengthen the education services and increasing drop-out levels in
Syria and sustain the educational activities in the targets areas, by the following:
Encouraging the students to back their learning via implementing BTL campaigns, from
girls and boys in the age of 7 to the age of 17,
Induce people to send thier children to the kindergarten by provision ECCD sessions; from
boys and girls with the age of 3 to 5 years
providing assistance to formal primary school building, ensure the sustainability of the
teacher's stipends from women and men especially via Non-formal education by provision a monthly incentives for both unpaid teachers and Teachers with irregular salaries.
Further more the project intends to provide a technical support for the teacher in order to make them familiar with the different types of education in emergencies.
As the local authorities is committed to pay the salaries for the teachers in public schools and pay for schools running cost, IPV intervention in the public schools would be via providing the school building with the required rehabilitation, providing the school furniture with essential supplies to ensure that the schools itself is suitable and
could be comfortable for students attendance further to equip the schools with the PWD
equipment's from girls , boys, women and men , in addition, the running cost for the
schools is a very important component in the project for the schools and teachers by
providing school kits, teachers kits,textbook, stationary and hygiene kits, and the running
cost for the formal schools itself by providing drinkable water , electricity, and fuel for
heating purposes.
Developing the mentality for students will be strongly considered in the project by
provision the life skills sessions and equipping the learning centers by book banks, this
activity will be for the youth and adolescents in the addition to the primary school children
from boys and girls.
The project also aims to provide assistance for non-formal education for out of school
children who are dropped out of school due to many reasons like displacement and
security issues especially girls, to reach for those children IPV intends to conduct a detailed needs assessment in order to catch a respect data base to show the drop-out reasons and levels for drop-outed students, the percentage of children at school but at risk of drop-out, the main point of the proposed assessment, to identify the a appropriate intervention, where the project is committed to provide the neede education program for the targeted students,
(ALP and SLP) for the drop-out children
Catch-Up classes for the children at risk of drop-out.
Provision a PSS sessions and SRA sessions for the ALP students from boys and girls. all the running cost for this non-formal program will be covered by the project, including the ALP teachers stipends, PSS
facilitators stipends, the required materials for the PSS and textbooks for ALP program,
which is approved by UNICEF. furthermore, according to the needs to provide the capacity
building for the teachers there will be training for the education personnel from teachers
(women and men) in addition to add new component related to the classroom control and
develop the education process with the classroom level, this will be by providing training
to coaching the classrooms in the formal education program and for the non-formal
program, the coaching program will take a very important role in developing the education
process with the classrooms levels in the formal education and the non-formal education,
this will be supported with teachers learning circles " TLC" and PTA, and all the required
and related training will be provided by the project.
The project also intent to measure the developing of the student level by assessing their
academic achievement by conducting ASER tool for the targeted student aged from 7-14
years from boys and girls.
