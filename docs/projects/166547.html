Project Summary

This COVID addendum is in support of the GOAL Nutrition Emergency Response proposal. The proposed action includes additional activities in response to COVID 19 including awareness raising, IPC, and procurement of key PPE for community volunteers.
The activities will be based in 6 districts, 3 in Manicaland Province namely Buhera, Chipinge rural and urban and Mutare rural districts and 3 in Masvingo Province namely Bikita, Gutu and Masvingo rural.

1 - Activities

The proposed activities will contribute to the nutrition cluster specific objectives as follows: - Awareness raising: This will be cross cutting all the 7 specific cluster objectives. With regards to support for cluster coordination, this will be a platform where awareness on new guidelines, standards and protocols is done for further dissemination to health workers, VHWs and communities With regards to specific objective 2, awareness on the Family Led MUAC approach to active screening as an IPC measure as well as adaptation of the wasting management protocol to reduce frequency of collection of rations for OTP cases needs to be intensified. Specific objective 3 awareness raising is critical in dissemination of IYCF-e in the context of COVID, in case a mother or caregiver is a suspected or confirmed COVID-19 case or the child is a suspected or confirmed COVID case. On specific objective 4, awareness raising is critical in directing caregivers for micronutrient supplementation to health facilities as the decentralisation to VHWs might increase cross infection of corona virus as directed by the adapted protocols. Awareness raising on where to report the identified malnutrition cases following detection via the Family MUAC approach needs to be strengthened under specific objective 5. Awareness on the minimum buffer stock of Nutrition Commodities should be strengthened to prevent stockouts under specific objective 6. On specific objective 7, overall, communities need awareness on the new adaptations to COVID context for the nutrition emergency response.Activities for awareness raising:-Support radio broadcasting sessions to disseminate the information-Support development and reproduction IEC leaflets to disseminate the information. - IPC: At community level, there is a gap of MUAC tapes for caregivers who are screening their children using family MUAC approach. Activities for IPC;- Procurement of MUAC tapes PPE for community volunteers: PPE in form of hydro-alcoholic sanitisers and masks are being proposed in the context of COVID to support VHWsActivities for PPE for community volunteers:- Procurement and distribution of PPE in form of hydroalcoholic sanitisers and face masks.
