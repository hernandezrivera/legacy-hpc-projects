Project Summary

The need to assist children and women has increased with the last displacement influx that started at the 1 of December 2019 till the 31 of January 2020, as 80% of IDPs are women and children.
This project have two separate components: children and women.
Through this project SDI is aiming at protecting and empowering children under the age of 18. Interventions will include conducting need assessment, communal awareness and dissemination of information on available child protection services, training of social workers, opening safe spaces for children where recreational and PSS services will be provided, enhance the educational status of children, especially children with learning difficulties, support adolescents, especially girls to safely access secondary education, support referring cases in need for mental or psychosocial support to the appropriate service providers, optimizing children protection through engagement with other sectors, like education and health, provide training to teachers on how to engage with survivors of GBV and provide referrals in an ethical, safe, and confidential manner, make school facilities more safe for girls to enhance their protection, and provide sensitization and training for teachers, students, parents, and community on health and GBV issues.
Moreover, SDI will support women and adolescent girls empowerment and protection through, distribution on dignity kits, conducting recreational activities.
Beneficiaries of this project will be 70% of IDPs and 30% of host communities.
M&E will be part and partial of all steps of the project.

5 - Implementation of activities

SDI has offices in Syria and Turkey. Both offices coordinate together to get the best result in implementation. All activities on the ground will be led by our protection team in Syria under the supervision of the protection manager in Turkey office. All procurement and recruitment operations will be done according to the procedures and policies of SDI. Beneficiaries will be selected away from any kind of discrimination and from the most vulnerable categories. All staff members dealing directly with beneficiaries will receive sensitization training. SDI will ensure having female staff members as part of its working team on the ground. M&E will be present at all implementation stages; continuous monitoring of the project activities and outputs will be conducted through the local M&E field officer.M&E team will conduct regular field visits to the project locations. Moreover, beneficiaries’ satisfactions surveys and focus group discussions will be conducted to ensure project compliance with the plan and expected outputs. The M&E team will identify best practices and lessons learned to feed into SDI’s future programming. The project manager in Turkey office, will ensure the smooth implementation of the project in accordance with the approved plan, and will conduct periodic meetings with the team to maintain the progress as planned.Reporting will be a key compo¬nent throughout the implementation of the project. Implementation activities will be done in coordination with local authority and/or community leaders. Accountability will be placed enabling beneficiary feedback mechanisms and complaints-receiving mechanism will be set and clearly announced to beneficiaries. All employees of SDI are read and sign the code of conduct and will receive orientation towards no harm principles and protecting the rights and dignity of beneficiaries

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

1. Risk of having harmful impact on children in friendly spaces if the children were mistreated. Level: low. Impact: High.Mitigation: conduct sensitization training for all staff dealing with children, and having regular visits by M&E team and making a clear well-announced complaint receiving mechanisms.2. Risk of children receiving/referred to specialized PSS/psychological assistance feeling shame and stigmatized. Risk: low. Impact: high. Mitigation: Staff trained to how to refer children in need for assistance in a manner that protect their privacy and security. 3. Risk of adolescent girls getting harassed or exploited or feel ashamed during distribution of dignity kits. Level: Medium Impact: high Mitigation: choosing a place that is safe for women to be the distribution point, putting complaint box at the distribution point, employing women staff for distribution, M&E officer shall be present at the distribution point.

Risk of lacking required permission that impede opening friendly spaces for children or safe spaces for women. Level: low Impact: highMitigation: working in location were our organization has good relation and strong contacts to acquire permission from local authorities and/or community leaders. 3.
