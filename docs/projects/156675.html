Project Summary

The project aims to provide vulnerable groups of population women and children from both the host community and IDPs (Internally Displaced Populations) with access to reproductive health and pediatric-related services.
The project is an emergency response to the dire increasing need of beneficiaries to such services as Jisr Alshoghour MCH is the only one available that provides CEmONC services in Jisr Alshoghour city, in addition to the dynamic security situation in light of the recent multiple largescale military operations in Idlib, and the subsequent displacement movements within the area.
Based on health cluster and health directorate of Idlib, Jisr Alshoghour hospital is still considered as a priority and sustaining the introduction of its services continues to be essential. Furthermore, the region was classified as high severity in the map distributed by OCHA.
The project will be providing life-saving and life-sustaining services as the following:
• Reproductive health services CEmONC including IPD, OPD, ANC, PNC, Normal and C-section deliveries.
• Gynecological services from the clinical services to surgical procedures.
• Pediatric services including inpatient and outpatient care services.
SEMA has a technical team consists of two medical advisors and one SRH field supervisor to do the clinical direct supervision through over the period of the project.
The project is currently funded by WHO and it will finish at the end of March 2019.

Needs

SEMA in Nov 2017 conducted a Health Need Assessment in order to identify the needs on the ground. The data was collected through surveys, key informant interviews, clinic assessments and focus group discussions in Idlib governorates and Homs governorate and northern of Aleppo, the results show a huge need for continuing delivering RH and pediatric services, especially in the western area of Idlib governorate, that shows also that Jisr Alshoghour city which is the second biggest city in Idlib governorate was out of SRH services since 8.2015 till 2.2018, and all the cases were being referred to the closest hospital which is 24KM far away from Jisr Alshoghour city, on the other hand, the population of Jisr Alshoghour and the surrounding area exceed 100k, which means there should be a CEmoNc facility to serve women in the area according to international criteria In 2018, and in line with the November need assessment and the annual assessment of SEMA health facilities and programs, SEMA considers developing the RH program by adopting the levels of care in the health facilities supported by SEMA to meet the new needs on the ground.Based on health cluster and health directorate of Idlib, Jisr Alshoghour hospital is still considered as a priority and sustaining the introduction of its services continues to be essential. Furthermore, the region was classified as high severity in the map distributed by OCHA.In addition, the hospital is located in the area that is considered demilitarization zone according to the recent agreement between the Russian and Turkish authorities, in which will mitigate the risk of targeting the hospital and expecting an increasing number of returnees to the area, which will increase in turn the number of beneficiaries.

Protection Risk Analysis

SEMA has its own written feedback and explanation mechanism "SAFE" (System for Accountability feedback and explanation) which provides multiple channels for submitted complaints and suggestions from the clients, staff as well, the mechanism has the standard procedures of complaint priority and importance categorizing, as well, a written SOP of investigation committee in place for the critical and high priority complaints to follow up and reporting minutes; critical complaints considered the highest risk which also could be related to PSEA, the SAFE system has tracking mechanism for received complaints and feedback whether through complaint Box, hotline (Whatsapp), Email, and electronic form as well. Additionally, the system has a referral strategy based on the complaints priory and importance to follow up till closing the complaint. The primary risk associated with this project relates to continued access and security contexts in the proposed areas of operation. SEMA has proved flexibility in the past in finding new locations beyond IS or GoS controlled regions to ensure maximum safety of its staff and beneficiaries. SEMA works closely with the local councils to ensure close contextual analysis on the dynamic security situation and liaises closely with SEMA’s Safety and Security Unit to assess and mitigate risks. SEMA has a dedicated Safety & Security officer in place who is responsible for keeping abreast of developments in our programming areas, analyzing information coming from multiple sources and supporting partners with operational decision making based on thorough risk assessments. If it is deemed unsafe to operate in a certain area, the facility is moved to an alternative location. The majority of equipment in the health facility is therefore of a portable nature to enable timely relocations.
