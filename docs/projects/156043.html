Project Summary

We will support and enhance the quality of educational process in the most school-needed areas for both boys and girls between (5-17 years) in Aleppo and Idlib targeting 2900 existing students (1300 boys, 1600 girls)in the selected schools in coordination with ED including formal, awareness campaigns, in addition to capacity building for (200) existing teachers and educational actors (120 women / 80 men) on : PSS, Punishment Alternatives, Teaching Methods, Public Safety Principles, Education Patterns, Active Learning, INEE Standards, school management, administrative skills and more in (2) training centers located in Aleppo and Idlib. The trainees will be selected in coordination with ED to enhance overall educational process quality providing better education for students and the ED will provide them verified certificates after the training the educational specialists will be Supporting teachers with appropriate supervision inside the classrooms, and then we will do a small scale rehabilitation for targeted schools covering protection, lighting, heating and cooling and facilities for children with disabilities and WASH facilities . the project is employing (200) teachers and school staff (120 women / 80 men) Finally, 10 summer schools will be set and summer activities will be conducted for 1000 dropped-out boys and girls 100 in every school, providing them with equitable information allowing them to return to formal education. This component implemented during summer break (3 months), and will include, numeracy, entertaining physics, Arabic, English courses. Furthermore, there will be open day activities every week including sports competitions, cooking for girls, drawing and painting
- Monitoring and evaluation activities will be implemented during the project.

Needs

According to Education HNO Narrative end of 2018 2.1 million children in Syria are out of school and 1.3 million are at risk of dropping out. More than one in three schools are damaged or destroyed 5.85 million school-aged children and about 245,000 education personnel are in need of education assistance inside Syria, 61 per cent of whom are in acute and immediate need2.63 million children have been displaced within Syria The total number of enrolled children in formal and non-formal education is decreasing for many reasons, most dangerously dropout boys who are forced to recruitment, training and indoctrination into armed groups. This situation is widespread among armed groups, and occurs at increasingly younger ages. On the other hand, early marriage for girls in cases of poverty, lack livelihood, families’ worsening economic situations have become main reasons for girls dropping-out of schools. Back to Aleppo ED need assessment report early 2018, around 60% of children who are in need to education between (5-17) years are girls and the rest are boys.up to the ED in Kilis-Turkey Jan-2019, 100 schools in Azaz district are in need for summer school activities, and as they mentioned there is a big need for the teachers' training courses and vocational training for youth(both males and females).Poor quality of education is one of the reasons driving children to drop-out of schools. according to our need assessment Jan 2018 for the need of training courses inside syria shows that most of teachers are in need for courses like PSS, classroom management and others. For that, we have designed training programs covering most of capacities needed.To summarize, we are focusing on supporting school education, conducting capacity building for educational actors to improve education quality and finally with summer school activities to reduce the number of drooping-out children.
