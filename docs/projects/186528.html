Project Summary

The ongoing conflict in northeast Nigeria in the three BAY states for over a decade now have been a big threat to education and poses many physical and psychosocial threats to children and their caregivers. The 2021 Humanitarian need overview shows that 8.7 Million people are in need with 58% percent projected to be children, these population of children required urgent assistance because of the criticality of the protection and education needs of children that are Displaced, separated and unaccompanied children that are at heightened risk of abuse, exploitation and neglect.
children were increasingly used as tools for various unholy acts due to the facts that they are either out of school or neglected to just go about their life with no supervision of adults, children are used as explosive device bearers by non-state armed groups, with number of children recruited and used for attack and various terrorist act increasing actively, children recruited and used by armed groups, and face rejection, inter-communal tensions and other stresses when reintegrating.
FRAD Foundation is focused on implementing the integrated programming in Borno state Between its Child Protection and Education departments. FRAD Foundation will have the integrated Protection and education programming in a holistic approach to ensure children with various protection concerns are addressed and they have access to school and build their resilience to feel safer in their homes and communities.

1 - Needs

According to the 2021 HRP, 1.7m people are in need of child protection services, while 1.1M in need of education, Thousands of children are out of school and subjected to risk of discrimination, violence exploitation and abuse, it is still not yet clear how efficient the reintegration of children recently released and free by the AOGs will be, how these children will be reintegrated and re united successfully to their families without any form of stigmatization or discrimination either in their communities, schools or in other social gatherings. it is certain and clear that this children require protection from violence, abuse, exploitation, and violations of rights. The ongoing conflict and continued displacements and resettlement that is currently ongoing will further increase out of school children while they move to their new settlement, it could also further lead to increase in children separation, which means they are in need of immediate, integrated case management. The risk of recruitment and kidnapping for adolescent boys and girls remains high with the breakdown of family and community protective mechanisms. The majority of the conflict-affected children and their caregivers have experienced brutal violence, loss of family members, friends and property, accumulated and protracted stress, deterioration in living conditions, increased military presence, social disruption in communities and lack of access to social services, such as schools and healthcare. These experiences have a significant impact on the development of children and the psychosocial wellbeing of adolescents and caregivers. other impact of these is that children may not be willing to go to school due to reasons from their end or from the end of their caregiver. These Project will support vulnerable children and other children living in conflict affected locations and new areas of return to attend critical education services, caregiver through the intervention will receive support to enable them manage stress and use good coping mechanism and render positive parenting to ensure children go to school and also remain in school.

2 - Activities

Case Management:FRAD will provide case management services for vulnerable children and adolescents, including UASC, children at risk, children that have experienced abuse, neglect or exploitation. and CAAFAG, FRAD case worker will have to hold a minimum of NCEs, diplomas, and university degrees in social work, sociology, education, health, or psychology. SAFE/SHLSFRAD Foundation will be conducted safe and healing learning space for children i n community of return, the SHLS will be managed by community facilitators in the community. Supporting adolescents and their families in emergencies session will target adolescents girls and boys and also their caregivers for caregivers session to equip them with positive copping strategies to deal with stress.Both SHLS and SAFE facilitators will receive training on how to provide support through structured, evidence-based social and emotional learning activities and the SAFE Curriculum. Adolescent Children Support:FRAD Foundation will support adolescent children after graduation them from the SAFE Curriculum and life skills curriculum, the support will be in form of small grants that will support adolescents boys and girls to continue school while they make small income to be able to remain in school. SAFE Caregivers CAFAAG Support:FRAD will take caregivers of adolescents girls participating in the SAFE session in caregivers session, caregiver of CAAFAG will also be taken through the Growing stronger curriculum which is a curriculum specifically targeting caregivers of CAAFAG and other vulnerable communities. Back to School Educational Support to ChildrenFRAD Will support out of school children from its Case management case load to go back to school and address all future challenges that will make them stop school, focus will be paid specifically on children formally associate with armed groups. Train Teachers on Digital SkillsFRAD Foundation will target school teachers to train them on Digital skills, this training will equip teachers with modern skills to access resources and the use of modern technologies to support and facilitate learning. The training will include practical simulation of EdTech software's that will enable learning in emergencies and learning for persons with disability. Scholastic Materials Support:FRAD will support enrolled children back to school with all necessary school materials that will support them in learning and also developing interest to continue in school and also remain in school. Community Learning Support:FRAD will develop a model that will support children to learn from their communities, Community Volunteers will be train to facilitate learning of small group of both out of school and in school children, the learning support will be an avenue where out of school children will develop interest in school and they will be enrolled. Advocacy Visits:FRAD will consistently conduct advocacy visits to prominent traditional leaders and head of ministries, and religious leaders to advocate for education enrollment of children back to school.

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

FRAD Foundation Protection/ PSEA focal person will support to train staff and partners in order to ensure the sustainable incorporation of protection principles in all the project FRAD will also work with local stakeholders in Borno State to inform the assistance we provide and help ensure that protection considerations are integrated into the full package of services for children, caregivers and communities. FRAD will deploy Protection Mainstreaming Monitoring and Evaluation System that will guide the project’s protection mainstreaming interventions. The project will integrate four key elements of protection mainstreaming which aremeaningful access to assistance and servicesprioritizing safety, dignity and doing no harmaccountability and participationempowerment of beneficiaries. FRAD will ensure these elements will be promoted through updating the current protection mainstreaming strategy at project inception. The update will be informed by findings from beneficiary assessments, staff capacity assessments and findings from various project activities. Foundational capacity building activities for project staff will be conducted to strengthen and promote a shared understanding of protection mainstreaming, including child safe guarding principles, and prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse. FRAD will sensitize the community on our accountability mechanism for quality services and on the ways they can provide confidential feedback.
