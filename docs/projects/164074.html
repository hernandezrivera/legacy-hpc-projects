Project Summary

This project is going to close some of the gap the PiN. It's FSL project that will help support 7000 direct host and IDPs with agriculture, cash for work related to agriculture, as well More than 100,000 Indirect beneficiaries of the rehabilitation of bakeries warehouse

-distribution of agricultural inputs (The wheat seeds)
-Cost sharing of fuel for irrigation

-The value chain includes training activities like TOT of key technical personnel from the Local Council & Ministry of Agriculture for the extension units, which will provide long term sustainability after project closure.

The agricultural sector in Syria is considered one of the most important economic
sectors on which many of the region's residents depend .
Agricultural production during the past nine years of the Syrian crisis witnessed a
significant deterioration and the cultivated areas shrank as a result of the crisis,
desertification and displacement, the lack of sufficient agricultural inputs and the inability
of the farms to secure them as agricultural production decreased by more than 30-50%
and all of this led to : 1 - The reduction in cultivated areas 2 - the spread of unemployment
3. - sale of land and migration 4. agricultural production and lack of unavailability of
appropriate quantities and prices .
The area of North Idlib and Aleppo is an agricultural region with distinction, as it is the
first and second settlement areas for rainfalls and also fertile soil suitable for cultivation (
red rich in humus and mineral elements ).
Also, the majority of the population in the region is more than 80 % farmers, not to
mention the displaced people in all of Syria's governorates .
The cultivation of vegetables during the summer period is one of the most important
agricultural activities during this period, as it works to alleviate the shortage in food and
works to increase the farmer's adherence to the land and cultivate it, and provide
opportunities for a decent life for his family .
The project is providing agricultural inputs to1000 Farms ( summer agricultural basket )
worth 350 dollars for each farmer containing ( seed + fertilizer + pesticide + equipment
agricultural ) Tkd m for farmers through vouchers provided for farmers + provide semi -
drip irrigation for each beneficiary .
The project is also based on opening agricultural extension centers in each of the
implementation areas that work to follow up agricultural operations and transfer modern
agricultural practices to farmers,
