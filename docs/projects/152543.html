Project Summary

This project aims at strengthening resilience of 9,960 most vulnerable IDP`S and host community / conflict affected population in 2 payams of Mundri East by the end of December 2019 through: conducting protection assessment and monitoring in Mundri East County, provision of individual / group psycho-social support (PSS) to 200 vulnerable persons, provision of safe referrals to 300 most vulnerable and their family members and disseminate information on available services, carry out community awareness raising sessions targeting 100 people per week on protection, and sensitization on fundamental human rights, establishing two protection monitoring committees in Mundri East County, provide 90 women, 90 men with life skills and livelihood / income generating activities and link them to VSLA groups, provide training to 60 community leaders on peace building and conflict resolution.
Our approach for this project is based on the humanitarian principles of humanity, neutrality, and impartiality where we shall provide services without discrimination.
In line with the cluster response priorities of provision of immediate lifesaving assistance to vulnerable populations, this project will meet the needs of the conflict affected communities by ensuring access to protective services. Sudan Evangelical Mission (SEM) will focus on internally displaced persons (IDP`S), host communities and other conflict affected populations to whom we shall address their urgent and unmet humanitarian needs.
SEM intends to use local capacity and resources to address distress and shock amongst the local communities whom we shall directly involve in the project in order to strengthen their resilience. We shall promote their resilience of the IDP`S, host communities and the conflict affected population through a comprehensive strategy that includes prevention, response and sustainability dimensions in full account.
We shall work to ensure that community members especially IDP`S who have found themselves in a new environment are aware of existing services through conducting awareness raising. Messages aim at upholding human rights, legal awareness will be passed to the community.
The awareness raising will help foster social cohesion and smooth integration of the IDP`S in the community. Through these approaches, SEM will identify and strengthen existing coping mechanisms and support vulnerable community members with PSS and referral to existing services. By training community leaders in conflict resolution and peace building, they will help to improve the security and harmonious co-existence by resolving and diffusing tension that arises within their community. Providing such an avenue, the community becomes empowered to prevent and respond to incidents of protection.
SEM consultation in July 2018 with the community of Lakamadi area on protection issues, we established that persons with disabilities are at risk due to immobility, and improving their mobility through devices like wheelchairs goes a long way to improve their response to distress as they can be able to move independently when the necessity arises.
SEM will create sustainability by working with and empowering and working with volunteers from the community where we shall implement this project.

Needs

The specific need that this project seeks to address is the response to urgent protection needs of the most vulnerable in Mundri East County, South Sudan that include women, children, elderly, disabled and survivors of sexual violence in conflict. The prolong conflict in South Sudan has devastated the lives of millions of South Sudanese; exacerbated and created new security risks especially for the most vulnerable groups including disruption of community and family structures, presence of arms and vigilantes, prevalence of trauma, increased alcohol consumption, weak security institutions and poor law and order and displaced more than 1.6 million internally in South Sudan and about 1 million as refugees in neighboring countries (UNOCHA, 2016). Grave violations of children rights continue to occur; in 2016, the UN verified 182 incidents affecting 2,005 children reported through the Monitoring and Reporting Mechanism (MRM) 46 per cent, were documented in the Greater Equatoria region. South Sudan Gender Analysis by Oxfam International, March 2017 revealed that both men and women are suffering from severe and often gendered psychosocial distress due to the conflict; the Oxfam analysis also established that out of boredom and frustration, many men and boys are resorting to alcohol and/or substance abuse, which leads to antisocial behavior, often creating additional protection risks for women and girls. It also called for specific protective and supportive activities for women, children and persons with specific needs including persons with disabilities and older persons.Due to lack of life saving services, women and girls of reproductive age are unable to access dignity kits which hinder their participation in community activities like gardening, fetching water, and firewood. Conducting protection monitoring, assessment, information sharing will help a lot in identifying the emerging needs of the vulnerable conflict affected population/IDP`S. Sharing information with other actors will provide a platform for coordinating the efforts aimed at preventing and addressing the protection needs of the community. Community consultations done by SEM in Lakamadi areas during assistive devices distributions in the area identified persons with disabilities as part of the vulnerable groups as family members told of the challenges they face during times of conflicts as they have to carry their family members with disabilities using wheelbarrows or on their backs. Therefore, improving their mobility through issuing them with wheelchairs and other devices will ease the burden from their family members and empower the persons with disabilities to respond with ease to protection risks. To improve access to services amongst the conflict affected community of Mundri East, SEM will implement activities that will make them more resilient, and enhance their protection.SEM will work in close partnership with government departments in Mundri East and other stakeholders so to develop and implement clear coordination mechanisms to ensure coordinated response and referral for the vulnerable community members.This project will built upon the gains made and the lessons learned from the implementation of similar projects by SEM

Activities

SO 1: Provide immediate lifesaving assistance to address priority protection needs for the most vulnerable populations in Mundri East County. Activity 1 Conduct protection assessment and monitoring in Mundri East County:1.1 Conduct quarterly protection specific assessments in Lui and Lakamadi Payams in Mundri East County.1.2 Conduct focused group discussions composed of 30 members of the community on safety per month.1.3 Participate in any planned Inter –Agency needs assessments that are to be carried out in Mundri East County.1.4 Conduct monthly meetings in Mundri with stakeholders on protection.1.5 Document and disseminate assessment reports to protection cluster and other partners working in Mundri East County. Activity 2 Provision of individual / group psycho-social support (PSS) to 200 vulnerable persons:2.1 Conducting individual counseling to 200 vulnerable persons at their homes through social workers.2.2 Conducting weekly group psycho-social sessions composed of 10 members in homes and churches. Activity 3 Provision of safe referrals to 300 most vulnerable and their family members and disseminate information on available services:3.1 Social workers and community based protection focal points identify 300 vulnerable community members for referral services in the community and outside Mundri East. They will be referred for the most relevant service e.g medical care, legal services or other required services. Activity 4 Carry out community awareness raising sessions on protection, and sensitization on fundamental human rights:4.1 Conduct weekly outreach activities (through traditional dances, sports, public rallies) to disseminate information on rights and protection risks, and available services targeting 100 people per session. SO 2: Prevent, mitigate and respond to protection risks through enhanced preparedness and resilience.Activity 1 Establish two protection monitoring committees in Mundri East County:1.1 Mobilize community members to identify protection focal points who will be members of the protection monitoring committees. 1.2 Conduct a five days training for the 60 protection monitoring committee members. 1.3 Conduct bi weekly protection monitoring committee meetings to advocate for community based protection to ensure cases of vulnerable persons at risk are addressed and record minutes which shall be shared with the Mundri County stakeholder working group. SO 3: Enable durable solutions for IDP`S and other affected populations. Activity 1 Provide 90 women, 90 men with life skills and livelihood / income generating activities and link them to VSLA groups:1.1 Mobilize 90 men and 90women to form a VSLA group.1.2 Conduct six trainings lasting 5 days to 180 men and women in income generation, saving, and accessing loans.1.3 Provide 6 VSLA kits (one kit has: cash box passbooks, calculator, pens, padlocks, bowls, counter book and other items) that will enable them start saving money and borrow loans through the group. Activity 2 Empower community leaders to promote peaceful coexistence and resolve conflicts and reduce their exposure to protection risks in the community:2.1 Conduct training to 60 community leaders on how to analyze current situation, agree on priorities and design responses that work for the communities of Lui and Lakamadi payams.2.2 Trained leaders engage community members in monthly meetings that aim at empowering community members to address protection issues that they face within their community.

Does the project provide beneficiaries with information?

Yes

How does the project provide beneficiaries with information?

This project will continuously share information with the beneficiaries inorder to keep them updated on the progress of the interventions and eventually empower them to identify other persons in the community who are facing protection risks and are not in the scope of identification by the field project staff.

Are messages context-specific or/and tailored?

Yes

Briefly describe how

All messages will be context specific so as to ensure that the various target groups get the information on the way it has been intended to be. Therefore the same message can be packed differently depending on the targeted group and location.

Protection from sexual exploitation and abuse

Yes

Briefly describe how

The project has put safeguards to ensure that all staff do not exploit or abuse co workers and beneficiaries during the course of implementing this project. This will deter degrading, humiliating or exploitative behavior towards the beneficiaries.

Nature of the consultations

Yes

Briefly describe the nature of the consultations

The consultations went beyond the community leaders (church leaders and payam executive officers, and chiefs) to include youths, persons with disabilities and community members who attended the consultation meeting. This was done after our routine visits in Lakamadi area of Mundri East County. They were able to express what problems they faced and what they thought was the best way to meet their concerns.

Feedback /complaint mechanisms?

Yes

Briefly describe the mechanisms

We have put in place several mechanisms to collect complaints and other feedback from beneficiaries. We already have a protection desk in SEM with a focal person assigned. This will ensure that complaints can be collected while maintaining confidentiality of the beneficiaries involved. As part of our practice, we shall provide telephone numbers of key staff to respond to complaints where beneficiaries have queries that they need to be addressed. In addition to this, we shall have a monthly community reflection session at the payams where we shall implement this activity to give the community members an opportunity to give their feedback on the activities being implemented.

Community Engagement?

Yes

Briefly describe how

This project will gather responses from the community routinely and will involve them to respond on the project implementation progress and they shall give their responses on what they feel needs to be improved on as well as the areas of strength they have identified during implementation.

Protection Risk Analysis?

Yes

Briefly describe how

Yes, this project is based on protection needs assessments done in the planned project area. Interagency fact finding assessment report for Mundri counties in March 2018 indicated that many persons with special needs and women reported that their husbands were killed during the conflicts while others do not know their whereabouts and no communication has been established as most of the locations have no telephone network.This explains the protection needs of the community of Mundri East and Mvolo and why it is important to carry out the interventions planned by this project.
