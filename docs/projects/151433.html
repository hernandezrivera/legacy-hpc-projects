Needs

in August 2018 There was heavy militia fighting in the southern suburbs of Tripoli, families from the Ain Zara, Salah al-Deen and Khalat al-Furjan, families displaced from thire area, and accomdated in some schools in Tripoli. they have been identified in need of humanitarian WASH assistance. also there are detention centres in/around Tripoli , for refugees and migrants need to improve water, sanitation, garbage collection and hygiene situation, especially those hosting persons with special needs, children and women. Moreover, in July 2018, some families from the east, south and west of Libya attempted to return to Tawergha following the signing of a decree on 28 June 2018 by the Tawergha Council (PC) which indicated the start date for the return process. families returned to Tawergha city lack adequate access to safe drinking water, hygiene and sanitation services, and hygiene facilities, also rehabilitation of health facilities.

Activities

The project aims to provision of emergency WASH assistance to different vulnerable people groups in particular vulnerable children and their families in Libya, a 10000 individual such as conflict affected IDPs, Returnees, and Non-displaced population, also to migrants in the detention centres. also provide life-saving WASH assistances at health care Facilities (clinic), that can safe primary health care to number of people that returnees, and non-displaced in Tawergha, also maintenance schools, and public water network there. The project activities include: a) provision of safe water supplies through water trucking, minor rehabilitation of municipal water networks, and installation of water storage tanks. b) provision of safe sanitation facilities through installation of emergency latrines, emptying septic tanks, fumigation of potential areas of contamination, Cleanliness campaign, plantation campaign, awareness campaign c) Light rehabilitation of WASH facilities in schools and health facilities, detention centres . Propositioning and distribution of supplies and hygiene items. UNICEF and its implementing partners will apply all the humanitarian principles including do no harm and accountability to the affected population.d) minor rehabilitation of water networks,e) provision of safe sanitation facilities through maintenance of emergency latrines,. f) rehabilitate of the clinic building.
