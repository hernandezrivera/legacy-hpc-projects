1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

WFP aims to provide food parcels to 100,000 most vulnerable Lebanese households for a period of 7 months. This intervention will be complemented by a top up cash assistance of 400,000 LBP per household to support in covering the cost of their basic needs.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

WFP strives to ensure that all food assistance activities are made to be safe, inclusive, and dignified, with emphasis on gender-based violence, protection from sexual exploitation and abuse and conflict-sensitivity.” The CO is strengthening context and protection analysis at the community level and enhancing our partners’ capacity to create practical and appropriate gender budgets, while risk, prevention and mitigation measures are reflected in project plans. Distribution site standard operating procedures indicate and guide on best practices for security and emphasize how to safeguard boys, girls, men and women (including persons with disabilities and older persons) from various forms of violence, abuse, and exploitation.A call-center for grievances, suggestions and questions is fully operational for all projects

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

WFP implements a robust corporate protection policy which signals commitment to prevent whenever possible, to respond to protection risks and craft successful protection outcomes for people of concern. WFP through its operational footprint is often closest to affected populations and is accordingly well placed to support effective protection outcomes. This policy is consistent with UN systems and one-UN protection approach. WFP Lb has developed a context specific strategy focusing on improved data collection and analysis, partnerships and stakeholder engagement and strengthened integration in risk management, supported by sufficient human and financial resources, strong leadership and a clear policy framework. Building on this, implementation will be supported by detailed guidance, indicators in data collection and analysis tools, and select activities. Through the WFP Lebanon AAP & Gender workplan, all WFP staff and cooperating partners will be trained on relevant topics
