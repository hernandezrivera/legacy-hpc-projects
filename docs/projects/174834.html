1 - Besoins / Needs

This project aims to improve water, sanitation and hygiene situation in communities hosting CAR refugees in Cameroon. Last report produced by national Institute of statistic reveal that about respectively 32% and 39.4% of population does not have access to safe drinking water in East and Adamaoua region which host the majority of refugees. 46% does not have access to improved basic sanitation and up to 6.5 % of population practice open defecation in the East. There are 2.5% in the Adamaoua and up to 16.3% in the North . Communities member collect water in open water sources (well, river and stream). The access to basic sanitation is low and remains a challenge. There is very few knowledge's among population and respect of good water, sanitation and hygiene practices. In refugees’ sites, the situation is similar with 5/6 refugee site which does not have access to water and sanitation up to normal standard. This project will then target refugees living in host communities. The project will target about 50 000 people. The main interventions are provision of water supply through construction of borehole powered with solar panned and improving access to hygiene and basic sanitation through integrated (WASH, health, nutrition, education, protection) community approaches to total sanitation including support to household for latrines construction and assessment Interventions relative to management of water point and wash services will also be included to adress gender and disabilities issues Main expected output include- Number of affected people with a sustainable access to safe water for drinking, cooking and personnal hygiene 30,000 Number of affected people with an access to adequate basic sanitation to meet their vulnerabilities 12,000 Number of people reached with critical WASH supplies (including hygien items) and services 40,000 Number of functionnal sector group : 2Number of organizations trained : 20Number of women who received a MHM Kit : 10 000
