Needs

According to the recent assessment conducted by DAMA organizations in many different locations in Mosul , Alqayarah and Hamdaniya , the main needs had been found as below: 1- It must be pointed out that the health profile of the Iraqi population (mainly the old aged women and men group) has been characterized with increased prevalence of chronic conditions (Hypertension , diabetes mellitus , and Ischemic heart disease ) and thus, increased demand over chronic medications. With reference to sphere standards, prioritization of None – Communicable Disease (NCD’s) management comes from the importance of ensuring that individuals with NCDs whom were receiving treatment before the displacement are still being granted the health access to do so and in order to avoid sudden discontinuation of treatment since patients with chronic medical conditions are particularly vulnerable to exacerbation of their condition or to complications such as secondary infections and are at risk when treatment is interrupted. furthermore securing comprehensive medications for management of communicable diseases which is mainly upper respiratory infection in winter season , and acute watery diarrhea in summer season (targeting all age and gender groups). 2- Indeed, the recent joint inter-agency/government emergency rapid assessment is needed to assess the situation and detect SAM cases. To monitor the impact, children under 5 years highlighted an urgent need for immediate action with regards to malnutrition programs. Meanwhile, malnutrition case management remains insufficiently strong with an expected fatality rate in the referral hospital . In line with the little administrative data available from regular health centers.4- Nonetheless, 80% among Women do not practice exclusive breastfeeding, and 38% of them brutally wean their children, generally after the third or fourth month. This favors the sharp increase of malnutrition. The main nutritional deficiencies is deficit in energy, protein and anaemia due to iron deficiency and deficiency in Vitamin A. 5- The lack of education of mothers and their subsequent lack of knowledge regarding nutrition issues clearly aggravates the already fragile nutritional status of their children. Worse yet, this bad nutritional status is also widely spread in the pregnant and postpartum women, making them weaker and less able to take adequate care of their children. 6- The IDPs in the camp including children below 5 years of age have been living in the regions that are controlled by ISIS in the last 3 years, the immunization program to control highly communicable diseases has not been implemented according to the ministry of health and WHO standards. Recent assessment that has been undertaken in the camps shows that not all the children had received vaccination according to Iraqi MoH Vaccination programs.7- The provision of basic services in the host community including primary health care activities will be needed to encourage the process of returning of IDPs to their original regions. Overall, humanitarian aid is currently unable to fully meet the burden of needs. Insecurity, poor infrastructure, and hindrance of aid continue to undermine relief efforts.there will be need to cover the health services in Hasan sham U3 camp in Hamdaniya , Jedaa 6 , Jedaa 3 , camps in Alqayarah .

Activities

Activities : 1- GP consultation , Chronic and Acute medication prescription, Management of Communicable and Non -Communicable Diseases (NCD’s), in Hasan sham U3 camp and Qaza Fakhra PHCC in Hamdaniya , and Jedaa 6 camp in Alqayarah 2- Laboratory services in the PHCCs. in Jedaa 6 camp in Alqayarah and Qaza Fakhra PHCC in Al-Hamdaniya 3- Health Education and Health Promotion. in Hasan sham U3 camp in Hamdaniya , and Jedaa 6 camp in Alqayarah .4- Coordination with DoH to transport and refer patients to receive secondary and tertiary medical services in MoH hospitals as per needed. in Jedaa 6 camp in Alqayarah and Qaza Fakhra PHCC in Al-Hamdaniya 5- Nutrition health care activities in Jedaa 6 , and Jedaa 3 camps (Growth monitoring of children under 5, Treatment of malnutrition cases , social mobilization)6- Procurement of essential medical supplies to support the PHCCs in Nineveh region. 7- Building the capacity of the national health staffs through conducting the training programs in the different health sectors according to the need of MoH which will be helpful during phase out stage of the project. Note: By the end of 2019, DAMA will negotiate with DoH Nineveh about their capacity to run the PHCC without the support from DAMA organization, all the medical and non-medical supplies will be handed over to DoH according to MoH and donor regulations, after the exit from the PHC DAMA will make sure that the beneficiaries including (the IDPs in Jedda 6 camp within Qayyarah and the host communities in Qaza fakhra within Hamdanyia) will not be affected.

Protection Risk Analysis

- Risk of infections transmission to the health workers in the project. - Risk of Project Staff facing Road traffic accident during transportation from Erbil or Mosul to the project sites- Risk of environment pollution from the medical waste that will effect the beneficiaries , project workers, and humanitarians. - Deterioration of Security situation in the areas of project implementation

Mitigation Measures

- Project health workers to follow the international standards of infection control- Vehicles speed limit, instruct the drivers to follow Road Safety protocols , application of driving safety procedures. - Segregation and management of medical waste.- Registering in IMMAP and INSO to get Security information, recruiting Security and access officer to analyse the security notifications and alert from IMMAP , INSO and local security authorities.

How is your project accountable to affected population

DAMA is making sure to remain accountable to affected population through followings : 1-Information sharing : DAMA will adopt information sharing mechanism about the activities through banners and flayers to make sure all beneficiaries aware about the service availability and why it is important to get access to the services. 2-Participation of beneficiaries : This will be done by active participation of beneficiaries in the design of the program and allow them to give suggestions to improve the modalities. 3- Feedback Mechanism : DAMA will put a suggestions and complains box as well as we will let the beneficiaries know the hot line call number for both DAMA and UNOPS to receive beneficiaries suggestions and complains, Those suggestions or complain will be recorded in log book and will be discussed in the DAMA HQ in Erbil , and Accordingly action will be taken. This will be evidence of the community complain or suggestions and how it will be improved. Monitoring visit will be conducted periodically in the projects sites by DAMA M.E.A.L Department those visits will include Community focus group discussion and individual interview with randomly selected beneficiaries to discuss the Quality of services provided in the project site, Rapid Need Assessment will conduct in the area of project implementation periodically to update the need of the community there, in some are area we have community health worker who will play good roles in Communication with the community and social mobilization.
