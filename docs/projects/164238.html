Project Summary

The project main goal is to protect vulnerable women and infants from malnutrition and disease through the implementation of an Infant and Young Child Feeding intervention, including promotion and support for optimal feeding practices through sessions and counselling. IYCF service provision will focus on on improving feeding and care practices during the first 1,000 days of life (from pregnancy to 24 months), early childhood survival and development, and maternal nutrition support during pregnancy and lactation.
IYCF counselling including breastfeeding support and promotion of appropriate complementary feeding will be provided. Activities include screening for PLW and provision of appropriate micronutrients to PLWs.
According to HNO 2020 draft, "4.6 million people are in need for nutritional services".
The project will target total of 55,000 pregnant and lactating women (PLW) in South area, Rural Damascus, Homs, Hama and NES.

4 - Needs

According to HNO, total of about 4.6 million people are in need for nutrition assistance, throughout Syria, about 240,000 children under the age of five years and over 50,000 pregnant and lactating mothers are in need for urgent lifesaving curative nutrition services. At least 36,000 boys and 35,000 girls are acutely malnourished and are at increased risk of health complications, including death. This reflects the spread of various wrong habits in the community regarding the nutrition of infants and young child. Overburdened communities, areas deprived of basic social services and displaced people are in urgent need of nutrition interventions and should be prioritized, to ensure that their nutritional status remains stable and improves. The limited access of boys and girls under five and pregnant and lactating women to lifesaving preventive and curative nutrition services related to Infant and Young Child Feeding (IYCF) in emergencies, micro nutrient supplementation, and treatment for acute malnutrition added to their vulnerability, especially those living under harsh conditions, or in areas with inadequate community and institutional capacity have a significant impact leading to this huge needs. This project will contribute to raise the awareness of pregnant and lactating women in terms of good habits concerning IYCF.

5 - Implementation of activities

The implementation will start by selecting the targeted beneficiaries through several ways:  Word of mouth.  GOPA centers  Community leaders  National and international NGOs.  Posters. Awareness sessions will take place in rented halls, GOPA community centers, GOPA Child Freindly Spaces, each course will consist of four consecutive sessions with different topics. It is planned to target total of 25,000 beneficiaries. The awareness sessions will cover the following topics: * The importance of optimal feeding including exclusive breastfeeding. * Basics of breastfeeding. * Various preventative measures to help mitigate infant malnutrition and enhance good hygiene practices. * The significance of and approaches to family planning. Through the community awareness sessions, GOPA aims to improve infant and young child feeding practices (IYCF) in the project catchment areas through the promotion of optimal feeding practices. The awareness sessions will target pregnant women, young mothers and other care-givers in the family. Women will be asked to invite family members - including husbands, sons or fathers -- to the awareness sessions. Appropriate halls in the community will be rented to conduct the session in it, contracted midwives and pharmacists will conduct sessions with women. In addition, after the sessions, if women faced any trouble breastfeeding their infant they can call the specialists to get appropriate answers, or help if needed. Activities include screening for PLW, IYCF counselling of PLWs and provision of appropriate micro nutrients to PLWs. The team will be trained on IYCF and CMAM before starting the implementation. For each activity implemented, staff will submit a standardized activity report. Monitoring staff are present during all implementation activities to ensure that the job is carried out according to schedule. Different tools will be applied in monitoring the project , A post monitoring will be conducted through with 10% of beneficiaries
