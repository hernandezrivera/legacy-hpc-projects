Project Summary

Takaful Al-Sham aims to support IDPs and part of vulnerable HCs with SNFI assistance in Aleppo and Idleb governorates in the following sub-districts: Afrin, Azaz, Maaret Tamsrin, Idleb, al-Bab, Teftnaz, Saraqab and Bennsh where we are planning to target 2500 HHs (6500 females-6000 males) with NFI winter kits, 3500 HHs (9100 females-8400 males) with heating items and winter cloths.
The priority of distribution will be given to most vulnerable HHs which include women, children headed HH, disabled person, sick, PLW and elderly.
For shelter interventions Takaful aims to target 600 HHs with rehabilitation of 20 collective centers, 200 HHs (520 females-480 males) with Shelter repair for housing, 2000 HHs (5200 females-4800 males) with rehabilitation of Infrastructure. We will target in the above mentioned shelter interventions 200 individuals with Cash for work schema.
At the beginning of the project Takaful will conduct a meeting with local community to
ensure their participation, explain the project goals and take their suggestions and notes
to develop the project activities.
Takaful will coordinate with SNFI cluster and with other organization who works in the same field avoid any duplication.

Needs

An estimated 4.4 million people require Non-Food Items (NFI) assistance, representing a 6 per cent decline from 2018, This reflects improvements in market functionality in some areas, However, the need for life-saving and life-sustaining core and seasonal/supplementary NFI assistance remains critical, especially for those experiencing sudden-onset displacement, those without access to markets and those facing exposure during winter and summer due to inadequate shelter. Idleb now face total saturation, with no more shelter capacity available for further displacements. An estimated 4.7 million people remain in need of shelter support in Syria, representing an increase of 14 per cent from 2018. This includes those in need of emergency assistance due to a lack of any basic shelter, millions currently in temporary hosting arrangements, and people living in damaged, crowded, unfinished or otherwise inadequate shelters who urgently need more durable shelter solutions. The increase has been driven primarily by an increase of severity of need in high-density urban population centres. The need for rebuilding and reconstruction of shelter and infrastructure in Syria, across the country. In Syria, households traditionally listed the male spouse (i.e. not female) in HLP documentation. As a result of the crisis, Syria has an increased number of female heads of household and separated households. Given the mentioned protection risks related to HLP and the specific need for HLP documentation as a prerequisite to receive certain shelter assistance and rights, women face an additional burden in accessing their rights and this support. Takaful response will be to save and sustain lives in area which classify at high severity scale of needs for SNFI through the provision of timely, targeted and appropriate shelter assistance and NFI relief items. Takaful will increase its focus on Seasonal items.
