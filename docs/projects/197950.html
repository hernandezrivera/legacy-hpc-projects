Project Summary

This project seeks to provide life-saving WASH interventions to displaced persons in dire need, living in IDPs camps and host communities of Adamawa State. The intervention will also target to reach out to persons who have returned to their homes but having little or no access to WASH services. The unavailability or absence of WASH services and facilities has contributed to the outbreak and spread of diseases like cholera, diarrhea and typhoid. Lack of knowledge on WASH good practices and common ancestral practices has attributed to the spread of these diseases.
This project is therefore aimed at providing access to safe water for drinking and domestic purposes through the construction of new and rehabilitation of dysfunctional water sources, so as to mitigate the spread of water borne diseases. This project will also sensitise the population on behavioral change towards good hygienic and sanitation practices, through hygiene promotional messages and campaigns using IEC materials and media houses. Vulnerable Households will be given WASH NFIs to support their WASH needs for personal hygiene and to mitigate diseases.
Similarly, this project will also reach out to girls and women with Menstrual Hygiene Messages, to educate them on the benefit of practicing good menstrual hygiene. This will help to improve their personal hygiene and wellbeing.

Plan fields

1 - Needs

This proposed project will seek to address insufficient access to safe water for drinking and domestic purposes, through the construction of new water sources and rehabiliation of dysfunctional water sources. The project will also sensitise the target population on good personal and environmental hygiene, through the use of IEC materials and House-to-House/Mass session campaigns. Households will also be reached with the distribution of WASH NFIs (WASH & Dignity kits and MHM kit) to promote their well being.

2 - Activities

This project will seek to provide the following WASH services to address access to lifesaving WASH services.1) Creating access to safe and equitable water for drinking and domestic purposes, through the construction new Hand Pump fitted Boreholes and new Solar Powered Boreholes.2) Restoring access to safe and equitable water for drinking and domestic purposes through the rehabilitation of dysfunctional water sources.3) Conduct of community-informed hygiene messages promoting hygienic attitudes, behaviours and practices, including, household water treatment, proper handwashing with soap at critical times and Menstrual Hygiene Management.4) Provision and distribution of WASH NFIs to the people in need (hygiene kits, replenishment hygiene kits, WASH cholera kits, WASH Nutrition kits, sanitation kits and MHM kits).
