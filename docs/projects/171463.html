Project Summary

Despite advance work to minimize the impact, children have become more vulnerable in the wake of cyclone Amphan to violence including GBV, abuse, exploitation and psychosocial distress. In the cyclone affected areas the most susceptible vulnerabilities among women and children, are those in various institutions, children that are unaccompanied and living on the streets, women and children working in hazardous labour. The CP Sector requires urgent support to respond with critical life-saving supplies, expertise and services & longer-term support to reduce the vulnerabilities and suffering of children living in cyclone-affected zones. The cluster priorities include UASC – Family Tracing and Reunification, Case Management and PSS incl NFIs, Child Protection Awareness Raising /Referral and PSS messaging, Strengthening and Establishing Community Based CP Mechanisms.
