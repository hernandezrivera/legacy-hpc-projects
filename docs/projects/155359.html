Project Summary

Activities will be based on the identified interventions for the emergency phase of the Cholera Elimination Plan for the Haiti. The integration of activities in a longer-term action plan will ensure the sustainability of interventions of continuity and mechanisms put in place toward the achievement of the overall goal of eradicating cholera from the Haiti.
The project will be implemented At the institutional level, the response strategy is focusing on the reinforcement of the capacity and quality of health care delivery at health structures managed by the MSPP (especially in CTDA the DSC-MSPP). The proposed interventions under this project aim to ensure the continuous availability of essential medical and WASH supplies for cholera treatment and management, including drugs, medical items and hygiene products to cover the new expected cases for the next 10 months; support health care delivery capacity through supplementary health personnel and hygienists during peaks (surge capacity); facilitate on-site refresher trainings of health personnel to proper application of cholera care protocols; and support epidemiological surveillance activities.
Global surge capacity (pool of nurses/auxiliaries and hygienists that are deployed to CTDA, hospitals and health centers when the health facility’s capacity is overwhelmed and does not have adequate personnel) of all actors health involved in the action is estimated at 40 staff and 4 CTDA in the Center Deparment. Around 4 health structures(Saut D´Eau, Baptiste, Savanette and Maissade) are receiving suspected cholera cases, depending on the intensity and localization of the alerts and possible outbreaks. Each of these structures will potentially be supported by this project.
Especially in CTDA the Maissade, Savanette, Saut D´eua, Belladere and Baptiste. As temporary WASH interventions are still needed in the short term in order to rapidly address localized outbreaks and ensure adequate treatment in proper sanitary conditions, MdM Argentine and PAHO/WHO will provide WASH supplies to health institutions with cholera services MSPP to respond to outbreaks and reinforce the MSPP's care delivery capacity.
