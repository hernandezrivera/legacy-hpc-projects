Project Summary

The Project objectives are Provision of necessary material to GBV, capacity building of duty bearers and coordination of general protection. Protection activities are fully secured with the following activities: Training of duty bearers, medical personnel, Humanitarian staff (GBV) and Provision of material assistance (dignity kits; solar lanterns) (GBV). Similarly, Advocacy on protection by targeting population of 4500 (90%) female. The project will be based in Banadir Region, and South/west state of Somalia.

Project Activities

Output 1. Activity 1.1. Training of 50 case workers (70%) female duty bearers, medical personnel, and humanitarian staff (GBV)-the training entails prevention and response of GBV and case management, identification and documentation of GBV cases, attitude and perception, communication and introduction of GBV survivors. 1.2 Training of 120 (50%) female duty bearers, medical personnel, and humanitarian staff (GBV)the training is all about Overview of domestic violence.2. Cause and consequences of domestic violence.3. Understanding the Domestic Violence Act and the duty bearers (the role of police, courts of law, healthcareworkers and local council courts) to enforce it.4. Guiding principles of handling cases of Gender Based Violence.5. Interviewing cases of Gender Based Violence.1.3 Provision of material assistance (solar lanterns) (GBV)-3000 (100%) female HHs at risk to GBV survivor and those at risk dignity kit1.4 Provision of material assistance (dignity kits) (GBV)-2500(100%)female HHs who are at risk for GBV1.5 Advocacy on protection-political participation, gender equality, cultural dynamics and policy development (juvenile justice and sexual offence bill (SOB).1.6 Protection monitoring of displacement and protection violations (GP)- research and monitoring, eviction causes, returnees settlement status , IDPs status monitoring and thematic days like girls child education and world refugee day.

Project Indicators

# of case workers trained -to work within the community to handle cases survivor centre approach and with high confidentiality # of duty bearers trained- to handle cases of GBV at most professional way by following guiding principles of GBV.# of dignity kits distributed-given to GBV survivors to leverage their severing and join the community easily. # of solar lanterns distributed-to the HHs who are at risk to protection and prevention on GBV cases. # of protection advocacy initiatives (notes, communication with stakeholders, and review of law/policy/action plans) build a steering committee within the community to address and solve the disputes. Advocacy on eviction policy, draft bill of land disputes and improving legal aid framework. # of protection studies conducted and published (exclusive of protection/displacement/eviction monitoring) e.g. IDP profiling, intention surveys, thematic studies, case studies, protection assessment-publishing of GBV Somali/English books , social medias.
