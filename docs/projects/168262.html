Basic Info

Project Name

Ensure life-saving GBV prevention and response services are available and accessible to all, including women and girls in greater situations of vulnerability due to violence and displacement in the Provinces of Cabo Delgado and Nampula

Start Date

31/05/2020

End Date

30/12/2020

Project Summary

1. 1. Conduct rapid assessments of GBV trends and risks, safety audits and impact assessment of GBV interventions in targeted districts of Cabo Delgado and Nampula;
2. Establishment of 10 new women and girl-friendly spaces in districts hosting internally displaced women and girls and roll out of life-skills and economic empowerment activities;
3. Ensure communication with communities through distribution of mobile phones/credit to community volunteers and frontline workers in hard-to-reach areas;
4. Increase access to life-saving GBV services, including clinical management of rape, PEP Kits, case management, medical and psychological first aid, to respond to the needs of women and adolescent girls survivors of GBV and other types of violence in hard to reach areas through support to functioning of a boat ambulance in coastal areas, mobile services and deployment of social workers to targeted districts;
5. Promote dissemination of messages and information related to GBV services and conduct awareness sessions to prevent GBV, SRH and PSEA and available services, in host communities and resettlement sites;
6. Provide training on GBV prevention and risk mitigation and advocate for improved prioritization of GBV multisectoral mechanism and strategies to reduce the risks of GBV in the humanitarian response, targeting frontline responders for other clusters (e.g. Health, WASH, FSL and Shelter etc), activists and members of the multisectoral mechanism;
7. Procurement of Dignity Kits to be distributed to women and girls in targeted districts of Cabo Delgado and Nampula Provinces.
