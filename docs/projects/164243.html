Project Summary

This project will serve as urgent humanitarian nutrition intervention by filing crucial nutrition gap in areas populated with IDPs and vulnerable group in Harem, Qurqaneea, Dana,Idleb and Jisr Alshougour sub- districts.
This project will support seven RRTs in targeted area as follow:
one in Qurqaneea sub-district
tow in Harem sub-district
One in Dana sub-district
Tow in Idleb city
One In Jisr Alshogour sub-district
These seven RRTs will serve PLW 36000, 22,000 boys, girls 20000
Therefore, the total benefeciaries will be 78000
Those RRTs will Screen of children, pregnant and lactating women for malnutrition signs and PSS cases. Also, they will Refer boys, girls, pregnant and lactating women to the fixed primary healthcare centers if there is a malnutrition case and PSS center if needed. Along with disseminating IYCF-E messages among mothers of children under five (group sessions). And finally they will Facilitate IYCF-E campaigns integrated to the Child Health Days, EPI campaigns.

4 - Needs

As a matter of fact, since the escalation in north of Syria has worsened the living conditions, the nutrition humanitarian basic needs are increasingly growing. According to the several nutrition assessments that had been done in north of Syria under supervision of cluster. Several gaps arose manifesting the dire needs of nutrition humanitarian intervention. Based on SMART survey, issued in Gaziantep, April 2019 pointed out to the Inappropriate IYCF-E practices remain an issue of concern, whereby feeding infants with formula milk in an emergency carries high risks of malnutrition, morbidity and mortality. By the internationally accepted standards and guidelines, any procurement and use of Breast milk substitute (BMS) should be strictly controlled to prevent its negative impact on the population in need. In Addition to that, the results from IYCF findings report mentioned the following: Exclusive breastfeeding is 38, 57 % divided into 26 % boys and 12, 57 % girls. Continued breastfeeding up to 2 years 12.5 % divided into 6.25 boys and 6.25% girls. Also mentioned the minimum dietary diversity is 38.25 % divided into 21.8% boys and 16.45% girls. Minimum meal frequency is 30.48%. According to OCHA report, Violence in northwest Syria continues unabated since November 2019 in southern Idleb and western Aleppo governorates impacting civilians and impeding the delivery of assistance in many areas. As the fighting continues, the humanitarian situation further deteriorates reaching unprecedented levels of needs;The escalation of hostilities from 1 December 2019 to 31 January 2020 resulted in the displacement of nearly 520, 000 people mainly from southern Idleb and western Aleppo governorates, 80% of displaced are women and children. Many of the people displaced have been displaced several times, intensifying existing vulnerabilities. More people have moved in the interim period.SIMRO has conducted rapid needs assessment in Jisr Al Shogour sub-district, Western Marj Akhd

These activities will be for 12 months in Harem, Qurqaneea, Dana, Idleb and Jisr Alshogour. These activities will target PLW and children under five years including IDPS and host communities. 6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Practically speaking, giving our experience in dealing with similar context, SIMRO has taken into consideration all the appropriate protection analysis. As such, all the RRTs team will be female to ensure full engagement with Syrian culture. On the other hand, SIMRO as usual will be conducting field monitor visits to ensure full satisfaction for the beneficiaries.finally, SIMRO has always been fully aware of all activities and procedures in regard to PSEA and GBV making sure to raise the awareness for their staff

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

the soaring prices of fuel: we will procure it from the begining and make sure it will be located in safe and secure places targeting: we will suspend the services until the targeting has been stopped .The terminating the cross border resolution: we will close the contracts funded by OCHA and we will try to secure the fund from other donors
