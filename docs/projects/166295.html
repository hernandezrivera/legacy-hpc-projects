Project Summary

Healthcare and WASH facilities in the BAY States have suffered destruction from the protracted conflict in the Northeast of Nigeria. One of the most significant attributes of the insurgency which has ravaged Nigeria’s North East region since 2009, is the widespread destruction of housing, infrastructure and public services, with some entire communities completely destroyed. Furthermore, the outbreak of the Covid-19 pandemic has made the situation more complex. In Nigeria, as of April 25 2020, 1,182 cases have been registered with fatalities across the country. Of this number, Maiduguri has 30 positive cases with 1 death, Adamawa one case while no case has been recorded in Yobe state. According to the Strategic Framework on COVID-19, 4,000,000 people are in need of health care services while 771,428 persons are in need of WASH services in Northeast of Nigeria. More than 40% of health facilities across the BAY state are partially or non-functional. The global preventive measures of hand washing and social distancing become a challenge as capacity of primary health care facilities are weak and Water and Sanitation facilities have been destroyed by the insurgents. Vulnerable population already affected by the insurgency are faced with the challenge of accessing health services that are readily available to the general public. The restrictions on movements to curb the spread of COVID-19, disrupts Households’ livelihoods and this will increase the vulnerability of the population especially those that live on a day’s earnings and female headed households. Also, patients suffering from other diseases with weaker health conditions will be affected due to Restrictions and the divergence of funding to curb the spread of COVID-19. Apart from weak healthcare systems, there is low coverage in terms of water supply and near impossibility of social distancing in homes with large number of person and congested IDP camps. Moreover, while others are still living in denial of the existence of the virus, there are others with misconceptions about the prevention and mitigation of COVID-19 such as traditional beliefs in the intake of some particular food will be effective in the treatment and contentment of the Virus.
AHA will be engaged in the rehabilitation and equipping of existing/collapsed primary healthcare facilities in the Borno and Adamawa States to strengthen the response to COVID-19 and Water and Sanitation facilities to ensure the effectiveness of COVID-19 mitigation. In addition, AHA will empower vulnerable IDPs Households with low incomes in Borno and Adamawa States with new means of livelihoods.

1 - Needs

The protracted crisis in the North East has impacted negatively entirely communities and individuals with massive displacements of women, men. This has drastically affected the livelihoods of men and women.the recurrent attacks of insurgents and military operations, storm and fire outbreak in the BAY States have partially or totally brought down basic infrastructure and social services. The declaration of corona virus pandemic by the director of WHO has also increased the burden on IDPs and communities who formally suffered the lost of their means of livelihoods. As a matter of fact, the lock down carried out by various governments in the North East, has been affecting seriously IDPs , specially households with low incomes. In addition, the low capacity and dilapidated state of both health and WASH facilities render difficult and effective response to curb Covid-19 in the north East.It therefore urgent, to rehabilitate the primary healthcare at the level of communities and urban centers to effectively respond to covid-19. In addition, provision of water, sanitation and hand washing points are of capital importance to implement regular hand washing. Furthermore, equipping quarantine centers and supplies are also necessary measures to take to spell out corona virus out of the north East. In addition an awareness raising on what corona virus is , means of transmission , signs of infection, symptoms are preventive measures of of great importance.

2 - Activities

- Dissemination of age, gender and context specific messages among IDPs on preventive measures of corona virus, means of transmission, persons at risk, signs of infection and symptoms.- Renovate and expand hospital facilities to cater for quarantine, isolation and in-patients admissions.- Cash/ voucher to support local manufacturing/ procurement of hospital equipment (beds, ventilators…) and PPE (face masks, glove, alcoholic-based disinfectants…)- Decongesting super-crowded camps by carrying out an explosive assessment and constructing walls for protection.- Decongestion of water points to curb the spread of Covid-19 and effective appliance of preventive measures by providing safe running water through new construction /rehabilitation. -Decongestion of sanitation (latrines hand washing points) to limit the spread of the virus through construction/rehabilitation of latrines and hand washing points - Preventing the spread of Covid-19 and effective appliance of good hygiene practices through provision of WASH NFI (buckets, soap, hand sanitizers, disinfectants, face masks…) - Dissemination of age, gender and context specific messages on Covid-19 prevention in local languages through context specific media (radio, pre-recording messages, posters, leaflets...)- Rehabilitation and supplies in isolation/ quarantine centers (nutrition centers, schools) for adequate management of covid-19 cases.- Distribution of PPE for nurses and health workers.- Identifying and equipping public places (markets, schools) with other Covid-19 prevention materials and supplies (hand washing points, disinfectants, hand sanitizers, face masks…)- Capacity building of health staff on Covid-19 to extend prevention and treatment to host population, IDPs in local communities.-Training of WASHCOM on maintenance/repair of water and sanitation facilities through remote approaches.-Distribution of water and sanitation materials (over head tanks, boreholes, locks, doors, aqua tabs, gloves, face masks, brooms, detergents) for repairs and maintenance of WASH facilities to curb the spread of COVID-19.-propositioning of Covid-19 preventive materials (thermometers, hand washing points, disinfectant, hand sanitizers) at the entrances of health, nutrition facilities, schools.
