Project Summary

Community initiative for Development organization (CIDO) is planning to provide Gender based violence (GBV) services to Returnees, conflict affected host community and IDPS living in Nyirol , Akobo &Pibor counties of Jonglei State who are at high risk of various shocks aggravated as a result of violence like, conflict, floods, diseases, death and injury. The primary purpose of the project is to address compounding effects of converging factors i.e. displacement , severe food insecurity /loss of livelihoods, economic decline, protection threats and lack of services which contributes and aggravates the prevalence of GBV. In line with the GBV sub cluster and 2025 HNRP objectives, CIDO will adopt an integrated approach and coordinate with WASH, Health, MHPSS, FSL, Education and Nutrition partners to prevent protection risks and to provide timely GBV response services to men, women, boys and girls affected by conflict. As such CIDO will use its leadership role to prioritize joint safety audits, capacity building sessions and GBV mainstreaming activities with multi sector partners CIDO will also ensure that the project staff adhere to the AAP principles as well as PSEA principles while delivering the activities at the project sites. The target population is 10,000 individuals broken down as 1,000 boys and 3000 girls, 4000 women 1000 men. The project has a special focus on adolescent girls and boys, female headed households and people living with disability. CIDO will implement this project directly in the proposed locations in collaboration with the target population, the administration in counties and partners on ground to promote durable solutions, sustainability and achieve maximum project results. The project activities will build on current GBV interventions provided in Pibor supported by USAID and Gender & Learning systems (GALS) delivered by CIDO &Oxfam in kobo and Nyirol counties. The planned activities focus on mitigating GBV risks, preventing and responding to GBV cases through; WGFS programming, Case management / Individual PSS, community Outreach and dialogues, EMAP and safe referrals. It is expected that by the end of the project period, lives of vulnerable women and girls will be saved, they will have increased access to multi sector services, life skills and positive coping mechanisms entrenched in them. It is anticipated that through EMAP boys and men will be better informed on GBV prevention mechanisms in emergencies and will make meaningful sustainable contribution to the prevention of GBV and overall population needs for safer dignified lives.
CIDO will regularly report implementation of GBV interventions on 5W's and resources mobilized on Financial Tracking Services.

1. Needs

A convergence of factors including severe food insecurity exacerbated by drought and floods , displacements , economic decline, armed conflict, returnee influx and lack of services has contributed to increased GBV cases. IRNA report of October 2024 indicates that there 28,000 returnees from Sudan living in Akobo County . According to IRNA assessment conducted in Nyirol county there are 5000 returnees from Sudan and 2, 836HH were displaced by floods between July-october ( IRNA report 19th October 2024) . Many of the returnee- men boys , girls and women suffer emotional distress of coping with life amidst existing social factors like loss of land and property and often have to depend on host community for casual jobs for survival. There is need to enhance coordination with local authority and Multi sectoral partners to strengthen GBV risk mitigation strategies , prevention and response activities for both adult and child survivors.South Sudan’s localized famine and continued scope of severe food insecurity demonstrate the compounding impact of multiple needs, including food security, nutrition, and health and WASH which affect men, boys, women and girls differently. Women and girls are traditionally required to provide food for their families yet men dominate and make decisions in regards to their farm income. Drought/food insecurity increases the risks of SEA and transactional sex among men and women. Revenge and targeted killings coupled with cattle raiding continue to be reported in Akobo and Pibor(IRNA reports of October 2024 ). It is common to hear reports of abduction of children and sexual violence among women and girls during cattle raids . Cattle raids is known to trigger early /forced child marriages as cattle is used to pay bride price. The prevailing inflation that has engulfed the country has affected men and women differently. There is reported increase in stress levels and Intimate partner violence affecting bread winners who are mainly women and girls attributed to the economic decline. There is need for a coordinated and integrated approach with Nutrition ,FSL partners and Mental health partners to provide specialized PSS services and access to livelihood options for women and girls so as to strengthen resilience and positive coping mechanisms. There is need to coordinate with WASH partners to ensure that women and girls are not exposed to GBV risks and have access to safe and clean water. CIDO is currently delivering an integrated GBV and Child protection project in Pibor ,Nyirol & Akobo with funding from BHA/USAID and Oxfam and will coordinate with other protection partners to share good practices in GBV programming. This will include liaising with partners to ensure girls, women boys and men access timely and quality GBV prevention messaging, CMR and psychosocial services within the GBV minimum package per IASC guidelines.

Conduct comprehensive case management and safe referrals targeting women, men, boys and girls in collaboration and coordination with child protection partners. 6. Mobilize community based protection committees and target women, men, girls and boys with individual, group, PSS services and referrals for specialized PSS 7. Strengthen/Equip 4 WGFS in Nyirol ,Pibor & Akobo . 8. Conduct referrals of women, men, girls and boys supported to legal aid and legal counselling services in close coordination with general protection partners including law society 9. Conduct awareness campaigns targeting women, men, girls and boys on GBV prevention and risk mitigation strategies including PSEA in coordination with FSL and WASH partners 10. Conduct awareness of boys , male youth and adult men on EMAP through FGDs. 11. Distribute dignity kits and hygiene kits targeting women of reproductive age and adolescent girls in collaboration with menstrual health and hygiene partners and WASH partners. 12. Conduct training of community mobilisers and sectoral staff on GBV risk mitigation, preparedness and early warning ( WASH, FSl, Health, Education and Nutrition) 13. Conduct training of women and girls on how to make Fuel efficient stoves and use of alternative fuel in order to mitigate GBV risks associated with firewood collection. 14. Conduct assessment on available livelihood options and needs of women and girls and provide training on various IGA activities at WGFS. CIDO will coordinate the activity with FSL partners and conduct appropriate referrals to livelihood opportunities like village savings and loans and seed/farm input distributions
