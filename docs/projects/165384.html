Project Summary

Through the research carried out by our team for studies and evaluation in Syria, we have found that there are few drinking water sources ... and the camps in the Harem region have a significant shortage of drinking water.
The evaluation team visited a large number of these camps and conducted a number of studies on the quality of the water that was provided and on the severe water shortage in many camps, so a solution to this water shortage had to be found.
We searched in the Harem region for drinking water and found two wells pumping each well 20 meters per hour. We studied pumping water within 10 hours per well. We arrived at the result of 200 meters per well during the 10 hours ...
The pumping is done through 4 inch submersibles. In the experiment, there was no decrease in this pump within the 10 hours.
The water engineers took samples of the water during separate times and examined it in Sarmada and Kafr Takharim laboratories. It was found after re-examination more than once that the water is pure and usable without desalination or purification plant and by matching the two testers ...
The project aims to establish a water pumping center to serve a large number of displaced families in the camps in northern Syria, where there is a large water shortage, and through an evaluation conducted on the number of 35 camps in the Harem region it was found that most of the camps have a shortage of water.
And that the wells that will be inserted can serve 16,000 people daily in the camps ... based on the evaluation conducted by the Al-Diaa team in Syria ....
The initial cost of pumps and extensions on wells will be one of the main costs for the project.
The water will be distributed through tanks that reach all the communities where the 16,000 inhabitants live.
All operational costs are included in the overall project operating plan.

4 - Needs

The project will address the urgent and urgent need of the displaced, as water is the continuation of life for them and their availability means the availability of health and the availability of psychological and physical security. Based on an extensive study and assessment of the need for the state of water shortages in the camps, the team came out with a number of important points.1- The project is based on providing safe drinking water and general use to prevent diseases.2- The project reduces gender-based violence and reduces violence against children by delivering water to points very close to shelters.3- The project maintains a safe environment for families without a breadwinner and for children without a breadwinner, bringing water sources closer to their homes, and mitigating risks.

5 - Implementation of activities

We have a number of wells in the Harem region that have been placed at the disposal of the association to secure clean and drinking water for the displaced families in the north of Syria. After examining and analyzing the water we have found that the percentage of purity in the well water on which the project will be implemented is 95% and the percentage of impurities is very weak not exceeding 5% Therefore, a simple desalination plant will be placed on well tanks, and then the water will be chlorinated and delivered to the tanks, which will in turn distribute it to the camps.
