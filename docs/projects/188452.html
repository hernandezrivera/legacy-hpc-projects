Project Summary

POINT has a prolonged professional technical CCCM experience and had successful interventions in 185 IDP sites through a strong strategic partnership with CCCM Cluster, SCHF, IOM, ACTED as POINT has implemented more than 10 CCCM projects in Northwest Syria 5 of them in partnership with IOM. POINT plans to conduct this project and continue supporting IDP sites to provide CCCM professional activities and support the IDPs in 46 sites based on the extreme and essential needs of the displaced people inside these sites and mitigate the negative coping mechanisms at the community level.
POINT will start the project by ensuring that all project human resources are in place, and this includes all the direct and support staff and completing all related processes like hiring or renewing the contract of the project staff. POINT CCCM team will start the interventions by conducting advocacy activities and the targeted 46 sites to acquaint the community and sites residents about the project's activities and objectives and gain all needed support and endorsement and ensure a high level of participation in the project's activities for all the population segments in the sites. POINT CCCM team will conduct a detailed gap assessment in the targeted sites to specify the contextual status and generate needs analyses regarding all sectors including CCCM, WASH, shelter, education, health, and more, especially the CCCM sector, to design an appropriate intervention plan to ensure the project achievement and its objectives will be met at the end of our intervention and the end results to be delivered as planned. The planned project core activities will be in place and are being carried out in the middle of the first month of the project as per the project work plan by starting the establishment of the site management committees (SMCs) and it will be followed by providing the online CCCM training for the committees' members and they will sign an official ToR after the CCCM training to start their roles and activities in their sites accordingly, and the CCCM online training will be followed by providing COVID-19 online training for the established site management committees (SMCs) as planned.The 1st objective: Building camps management and camps coordination capacity (CCCM capacity) for 46 IDP sites. Conducting advocacy and community sensitization through public sessions, and disseminating advocacy messages such as leaflets, and posters to introduce the project activities to the targeted communities. Coordination with local authorities, sites managers, and relevant focal points to ensure their endorsement and facilitation. Conducting a detailed assessment of management capacity in the targeted (46) IDP sites. Conducting CCCM and SPHERE online training for the site management committees Conducting physical fire safety practical training (maneuver) for the site management committees. Forming sub-committees in each IDP site. Mentoring and coaching for committees and sites management. The 2nd objective: Improving living conditions and fire safety in 46 IDP sites Community care and maintenance component. Establishment of 322 fire points in the targeted and maintaining 400 old fire extinguishers in In previously targeted sites. The 3rd objective: Raising communities' health awareness to mitigate the risk of COVID-19 pandemic in 46 sites. POINT has a health expert person to deliver COVID-19 online training to site management committees (SMCs) which will be delivered for two days and will be similar to the CCCM online training in terms of methodology. This training aims to mainstream the COVID-19 protection measures, raising awareness on how to follow the best protection procedures based on WHO guidelines. The 4th objective: Increasing the well-being of camps residents through conducting recreational activities and social events that will be initiated by the established SMCs and the sub-committees in each IDP site

4 - Needs

The assessment aims to determine the situation in 46 sites in case of the availability of the site management committees who are responsible for managing the camps affair and take the lead in the coordination between camps residents and the local authorities to deliver people needs and voices to the management representatives, also to define the most in-demand needs within the camps and what are the most rehabilitation and restoration activities which can raise the living conditions inside the camps and give more suitability atmosphere within the camps.Most of the sites in Idlib and Aleppo governates need support with first aid tools and fire extinguishers, also establishing trained teams on first aid and fire suppression, in most of the camps about 92 % of the total number of camps there are no fire extinguishing or first aid tools even there are some people about 33% of the total number of targeted camps who attended training and have experience on fire extinguishing as many of them are working with the civil defense organization, but they need to receive technical training about fire extinguishing and first aid and need to be formed as an official committee inside the camps with clear terms of reference and supported with the needed tools to respond when a fire happens in the site or any emergency.It is being recommended to establish fire points inside the camps supported with fire extinguishers and first aid kits under the supervision of the fire safety committee members

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

All POINT staff and contractors sign or will sign on POINT gender and code of conduct to follow our policies related to gender and protection.POINT organization is committed to gender balance within its team and always seeks to have a balance between the number of female and male team members and during any activity implemented the team should include a member from both males and females in order to ensure the best way of response for beneficiaries that do not cause any embarrassment atmosphere especially for women beneficiaries, furthermore, POINT team is trained on the protection principles and have a good experience dealing with people from special needs as well. Gender is mainstreamed in the design of the proposed project, ensuring that the needs of women are considered and properly addressed. Duringbeneficiaries’ selection phase, specific emphasis will be put on women and women-headed households to ensure that women have equal access to the provided services.POINT will closely monitor protection risks throughout the implementation period and will ensure coordination with Protection Cluster partners.POINT has developed mitigation measures set in place to ensure that protection is mainstreamed in all project activities and GBV risk is minimized to minimum level Individual residents may over-rely on any civil response/DRR committee for responding to a natural disaster and may shirk individual responsibility.IDPs that have fled to areas where CCCM members are ignored


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

POINT is strongly committed to protecting children and women from abuse, a commitment that is reflected in their Child Protection and PSEA Policies and is a member of PSEA network and follows the guidelines. Gender is mainstreamed in the design of proposed project, ensuring that the needs of women are considered and properly addressed. During the beneficiaries’ selection phase, specific emphasis will be put on women-headed households and their specific vulnerability to protection concerns and reduced access to employment opportunities due to their gender, ensuring women have equal access to the provided services.POINT will closely monitor protection risks throughout the implementation period and will ensure coordination with Protection Cluster partners. POINT has developed mitigation measures set in place to ensure that protection is mainstreamed in all project activities and GBV risk is minimized to the minimum level: Ensuring that protection principles are incorporated throughout all project activities, including the promotion of meaningful access, and ensuring the safety and dignity of beneficiaries. POINT trains its field staff to safely identify and refer protection concerns to the correct unit(s) and external actors to reduce risks and support safe and dignified access for all affected populations. Ensuring that staff members are trained on following do not harm principles in interventions and signed a code of conduct, receive PSEA training and have a reporting system.
