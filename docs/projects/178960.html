Project Summary

The project study was prepared by a specialized engineering team after many field visits for several days with technicians who were overseeing this system and the study concluded that it can solve the previous problems by the following:
1. Expansion of the sewerage network to suitable with the huge urban in the town
2. Clean the main moth of the outlet sewerage to all town and complete it in order to move away from residential areas and reduce the damage that will be followed by the farmlands.
In general, there is a functional main water network in Tell Abiad, but recently, as a result of the power outage and the lack of fuel supplies, the main water network in some parts of the city of Tell Abiad did not function properly. Currently, local authorities, in coordination with local stakeholders on the ground, are working on resolving this issue.
The effective running of the main water network again will allow IMAAR to implement the proposed intervention on the sewage network.
There is main sewerage in the town which uncovers one. This sewerage was blocked because of the increase of the bin, that led to spread the diseases such as leishmaniosis which multiple during the summer, in addition to that a lot of houses have built around it, as well as the farmers have damage from it because it takes a large space of the lands along its course.
Implementation of the project will lead to the following:
1. Cancellation of septic tanks, which was formed earlier, unsafe in two sides environmental and civilians, and get rid of the swamps.
2. Raising the level of health of the town residents and reduce or eliminate the diseases that may be caused by the sewage system or swamps.
3. This project will interact with the rest of the activities that take place in the city by several local and international organizations to rehabilitate the infrastructure of the city and support civil society with development projects, agricultural and animal to recover and start again.

4 - Needs

The number of displaced people in Tell Abiad has increased because of its location and distance away from the places of danger. This led to a very large urban expansion, where urbanization horizontal and vertical is increased and the sewerage system connects to each other, that led to the establishment of many random septic tanks which is unsafe in two sides environmental and civilians.total population is about 25000, out of them, there are 7000 IDPs. Turkish Government announced that the cross-border gate between Akcakale city in the south of Turkey and Tell Abiad in northern Syria was opened for humanitarian organizations based in Turkey and for Syrian refugees willing to return to their communities in Tell Abiad.People in the targeted communities live now on unsafe water trucking or closed wells to provide their water needs which are unsafe, unclean, and are not regulated as well. This situation seems difficult for people like widows, divorcees, and people with special needs because all people depend on this source (water trucking).The waste management sector in Tell Abiad needs support in order to reduce the prevalence of epidemics and diseases due to the suspension of the garbage removal besides the limited number of waste metal bins and workers available.
