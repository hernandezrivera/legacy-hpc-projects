Project Summary

The project purpose is to strengthen community based child protection systems promoting child sensitive lifesaving assistance in the vulnerable communities of Mazowe and Chipinge Districts. The project intends to reduce vulnerability and enhance the protection of 10,500 children in need mitigating identified protection risks in the selected wards which are regarded as hot spots (Farm worker communities and wards around business centers and growth points) of the two Districts in Zimbabwe. The project complements TDH Italy's Education assistance programme strengthening the community based child protection structures and building the capacity of ward level Disaster Risk Reduction committees and district level child protection committees to prevent and respond to abuse, neglect, exploitation, and violence against children in the aftermath of a disaster.
The project strengthens children’s resilience using the solutions focused approach in psycho-social support to ensure children's needs are appropriately identified incorporating age and gender diversity with their active participation.
The expected outcomes from these interventions include;
- Improved preparedness and management of emergency response without exposing children to further harm (2 District and 10 local level Child Protection Committees) in Chipinge and Mazowe
- Community based child protection systems in Chipinge and Mazowe districts are able to identify and respond to child protection risks and violations in real time
- Increased participation of women and girls in emergency response, early recovery, resilience building and their needs are reflected in Community Disaster Risk Reduction Plans.
It is expected that the transformational leadership training will have a positive influence on the 80 Religious and Traditional leaders to promote child safeguarding in their respective communities during and after emergencies.

Needs

Drought magnifies life threatening human rights violation making girls more vulnerable to school dropout, sexual exploitation and abuse leading to increased child pregnancies and early marriages. Both girls and boys may become victims of child labor and neglect due to family separation as a result of economic migration leaving behind child headed households. Child safe guarding and provision of PSS to both children and care givers will build resilience in coping with distress associated with drought. There is need for targeted PSS to children, families and community to promote community involvement and voluntarism in identifying their needs on their own, acting for solution and building self-help skills ensuring that the services currently provided to the community respond to their needs.

Activities

• Capacity building of community based child protection structures in surveillance, response and prevention of child rights violation during emergencies • Provision of age and gender appropriate PSS using the Solutions Focused Approach in building resilience targeting boys, men women and girls• Engagement with men and boys to become more mindful of women and girls rights in emergencies and gender equality in rebuilding their livelihoods after the drought
