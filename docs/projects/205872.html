Project Summary

Among the various activities to be carried out in order to achieve the project's objective in the desired quality and quantity, we will carry out screening for malnutrition in children under 5 years of age, pregnant women, lactating women and women of childbearing age, not only that, we will promote community awareness by disseminating information on exclusive and complementary breastfeeding to pregnant women, lactating women, women of childbearing age, matrons, traditional midwives and carers in order to improve the nutritional status of the children mentioned above, lactating women, women of childbearing age, matrons, traditional midwives and carers of children under 5 with the aim of improving the nutritional status of the above-mentioned children, but also of pregnant women for good foetal development, women's health and well-being, as well as preventing the onset of diseases such as gestational diabetes, hypertension and excessive weight gain. Last but not least, we intend to improve the intervention capacities of the District Services for Women's Health and Social Action, especially the Polyvalent Health Agents (APS) with a view to improving the quality of the services provided and coverage, as well as contributing to the distribution of supplements. Cooking demonstrations will be held in communities and health centres, and key therapeutic nutritional messages will be disseminated, as well as door-to-door talks on basic sanitation. We will also implement activities on early childhood development (ECD) and sensitise the community to develop nutrition awareness, as well as taking cross-cutting issues such as protection, inclusion and the environment with greater concern and seriousness. In short, the effective implementation of the project will contribute to bringing health services closer to the population, with a view to constantly improving early childhood development.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project will disseminate information on exclusive and complementary breastfeeding to pregnant women, lactating women, women of childbearing age, traditional midwives and carers of children under 5, with the aim of improving the nutritional status of the children mentioned above and of pregnant women, lactating women and women of childbearing age. It will also bring health services closer to the population, especially for early childhood development. To this end, various activities will be carried out, including screening for malnutrition in the entire target group, especially (pregnant women, breastfeeding women, women of childbearing age and children under 5), providing advice on healthy eating and referring serious cases to health units. We will also raise awareness about healthy eating, as well as carrying out cooking demonstrations, promoting hygiene and food care, as well as raising awareness about the practice of nutrition-sensitive agriculture and activities inherent to early childhood development. To this end, it will be necessary to strengthen the intervention capacities of the health officials in the district (APS) in particular, through training that fulfils the purpose of the project, since they are extremely important players in the project in terms of collaboration in carrying out the activities. As a community outreach strategy, with the collaboration of SDSMAS, APS and local leaders, activities will be carried out in the communities, organising the target public into nutrition groups, which will be made up of up to 25 members per group (quotas of pregnant women, nursing mothers, women of childbearing age, child carers, local leaders, matrons, traditional midwives, fathers/men). We will also carry out door-to-door awareness-raising, especially on hygiene issues. We will also carry out awareness-raising and cooking demonstrations in health centres, especially on days when there is a large influx of users, especially on the days of antenatal appointments and postnatal appointments (child weighing days).

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The project will cover the geographical areas belonging to the districts of Mocimboa da Praia. Amongst various activities, we will have the following:1. Training PHCs in exclusive subjects on nutritional intervention packages, especially exclusive breastfeeding and complementary feeding; screening for cases of malnutrition, cooking demonstrations. This activity aims to increasingly strengthen the intervention capacities of PHCs, improving the quality of the services provided and their scope. Training for this group will continue throughout the implementation of the project, and we will complement this with in-service training during our supervisory visits.2. Carrying out cookery demonstrations related to complementary feeding. In this activity, we intend to use and encourage the massive use and utilisation of local foods, improving the ways in which they are prepared in order to obtain the maximum nutrients that these foods provide. The same activity will be carried out in health centres, especially on days when there is a greater influx of users, especially antenatal appointments, postnatal appointments and other days when there is a greater influx of users. We will also do this in the communities, with groups of mothers, child carers, pregnant women, breastfeeding women and women of childbearing age, fathers, local leaders, matrons, etc. The activities will be very interactive and the participants will be prepared beforehand to demonstrate them to others. This way, we think it will be easier to promote behaviour change in a short period of time, and the food will be local and easily accessible.3. Malnutrition screening for the entire target group. This activity will take place at the same time as the awareness-raising activities and cooking demonstrations. It will result in counselling on healthy eating/good nutritional practices, and referral of cases to health units if the situation detected requires this. Carrying out this activity at the same time as the others will allow families to be occupied not only with project activities.4. Reproduction of IEC material. We will endeavour to provide the APS with the necessary teaching resources relevant to their activities so that they can carry out their activities without any major problems. 5. Community training on exclusive breastfeeding and complementary feeding. The training will be carried out in nutrition groups and health centres. The nutrition groups will be made up of child carers, pregnant women, breastfeeding women, women of childbearing age, local leaders, matrons and, not least, we will try to involve men in these groups, since they are often left out, even though they play an important role in the family in terms of managing the family's financial resources and making decisions about the family's diet. In this way, we believe that if they are aware of healthy eating, they will be able to buy healthy food for their families.6. Conducting the baseline and end of the project. We intend to carry out an assessment at the start of the project in order to identify the most critical communities in terms of nutrition in the district. But also, to ascertain security phenomena so that the project can be implemented smoothly.7. Hold clarification meetings with the district SDSMAS and other relevant actors in the communities. This activity is seen as an act of accountability.8. Encouraging the practice of nutrition-sensitive agriculture. As this is a nutrition project, we believe that its implementation is extremely important so that families can at least consider which food products to produce that can benefit them nutritionally.9. Support SDSMAS in distributing supplements and organising health fairs in the communities. Based on the challenges faced by the SDSMAS in serving the entire population, especially in the most remote health regions, we will support the distribution of supplements.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

As with PSEA, we also have a protection strategy that carefully monitors any suspicion of abuse or harassment at different levels, as well as the reporting and protection of whistleblowers. However, all staff will be trained in these matters and reporting mechanisms will be shared in order to guarantee the protection of those involved.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The programmes will be developed mostly in the community. Local families will be trained to eat healthily, taking into account the local diet. We will be relying on local actors to do this. We recognise that the massive involvement of the population is important for the success of the project. That's why the population will be involved in all the project's implementation cycles, including planning. We will also significantly involve the local authorities throughout the project implementation process, especially in community mobilization, bearing in mind that this is a district with security concerns. It is also important to note that among the various criteria we will use to select the target group, we will highlight carers of children, pregnant women, women of childbearing age, fathers, etc. regardless of age or gender.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Based on the baseline, as well as research work with local state agencies, we think it is possible to reach people with disabilities of different age groups, who will be treated as a priority in all our intervention.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Bearing in mind that any human intervention in nature has its impact, whether significant or not. In this sense, it is important to state that this project will take into account all aspects that are harmful to the environment, prioritising the principles of prevention, precaution, conservation and other aspects that do not put the environment at risk, in other words, that protect the environment. In this context, various activities will be carried out, mainly in the area of cooking demonstrations, where participants will be taught how to build homemade fireplaces with low wood consumption, and will also be encouraged to carry out conservation agriculture, among other environmentally beneficial materials. Not least, the team will greatly reduce the excessive use of paper.
