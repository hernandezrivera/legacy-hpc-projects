1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

OCHA contributes to principled and effective humanitarian response in Mozambique through coordination, advocacy, policy development, information management, and humanitarian enhancing tools and services. OCHA Mozambique also operates one sub-office located in Pemba.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Maintain effective humanitarian coordination while reinforcing the collaboration and relationship with the government. 2) Enhance coordination and advocacy for effective humanitarian operation. 3) Improve safe, timely and unimpeded access to people in need in hard-to-reach areas through access and civil-military coordination approaches. 4) Ensure that the implementation of the Centrality of Protection (CoP) Strategy is at the core of humanitarian action in Mozambique.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

OCHA will promote and ensure trough coordination that protection is at the center of all activities aimed at ensuring full respect for the rights of the individual in accordance with human rights law, international humanitarian law (which applies in situations of armed conflict) and refugee law. Therefore strengthening situational awareness and security analysis, including through OCHA’s field teams and access and Civil military coordination unit.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

OCHA will promotes AAP in humanitarian action fostering accountability to affected people, by facilitating coordination approaches that integrate gender, age, disability and other vulnerability considerations into all aspects of humanitarian response. OCHA recognises and supports: the right to life with dignity; the right to receive humanitarian assistance; the right to protection and security; the transfer of resources to minimise harm and promote positive effects; meeting needs; ensuring humanitarian action promotes a postive message; improves programme relevance, effectiveness, efficiency, sustainability and impact; improves quality of services by ensuring that the response meets what the affected people feel are their needs, not what others may feel are their needs

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Increase efforts to identify barriers, risks and enablers for persons of concerns including person with disabilities and takes concrete steps to strengthen the inclusion of persons with different types of disability.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

OCHA will promote environmental responsibility with implementing partners to aims to reduce environmental damage.
