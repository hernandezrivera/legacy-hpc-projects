Project Summary

According to Humanitarian response plan 2020, acute malnutrition remains relatively high among internally displaced children and mothers and the population affected by conflict. Throughout Syria, about 240,000 children under the age of five years and over 50,000 pregnant and lactating mothers need urgent lifesaving curative nutrition services. Al least 36,000 boys and 35,000 girls are acutely malnourished and are at increased risk of health complications, including death. Furthermore, it is the youngest children that are the most vulnerable and affected, with higher numbers of acute malnutrition amongst infants under six months.
The project aims to deliver facility and outreach-based nutrition services (CMAM and IYCF) and raising awareness services within 22 nutrition centers, 4 child-mother-friendly spaces, and 4 rapid response teams in eight districts of Aleppo and Idleb governorates; Afrin, Al Bab, Harim, Azaz, Jarablus, Idleb, and Jabal Saman targeting reproductive-aged women, PLWs, and U5 children of the host and IDP communities.
The target facilities are IDA PHCs in Kafr Karmin, Azaz, Soran, Bab Iman, Bab Noor, Ekdeh, Shamarekh, Al Bab, Jarablus, Zoghra, Al Salam, Halab Labieh, Hezri, Kelli, Mariamin, Dana, Afrin, Bulbul, Medanki, in addition to 2 hospitals (M. Wassim Maaz and Hope) and 1 MC in Azaz, 4 child-mother spaces in Azaz camps and 4 rapid response teams in Azaz, Jarablus, Al Bab and Afrin districts.

4 - Needs

According to HRP 2021, 4.9 million girls and boys under the age of five years and pregnant and lactating women (PLW) are at risk of undernutrition and in need of comprehensive curative and preventive nutrition services to continue during 2021. Around 141,540 children under the age of five years are expected to suffer from acute malnutrition during 2021, out of which 37,863 girls and boys are at risk of death due to severe malnutrition if not treated appropriately.According to the Surveillance Report/March-2021, it's done by Nutrition cluster partners; in Idled and Aleppo showed the increase of GAM rate as:2019 GAM : 2.3 %2020 GAM : 2.06%2021 GAM : 2.09 %Where Idleb the GAM among IDPs was (2.72 %), and Aleppo the GAM among IDPs was (0.6%)And the danger is the noticeable increase of MAM-PLWs, that Idleb showed the MAM-PLW among IDPs (19.6 %) and Aleppo showed the MAM-PLW among IDPs (4.56 %)However, due to the IDPs waves, and the absence of integrated programs that meet the health and nutrition needs in the area, the need to keep serving the IDPs and host community members with a focus on CMAM, IYCF, and community out-reach is recommended by cluster in the project region. The proposed nutrition activities will make sure to.- provide emergency IYCF counseling for mothers with moderately malnourished children- Disseminate messages (IEC/BCC) on IYCF-E recommended feeding practices.- Support health/nutrition inclusive education and promotion- Nutrition Supplements distribution

5 - Implementation of activities

Screen the target communities and IDP camps through CHWs to identify MAM cases (using MUAC screening) and refer them to the nearby facilities. Monitor cases and refer SAM cases to higher-level management facilities run by IDA.Take corrective actions to improve service provision.Distributing nutrition supplies to the MAM children and PLWs (e.g., multi-vitamins, iron syrups, and antibiotics), to support the nutritional status and prevent severe malnutrition and complications. Develop multi-sectoral awareness key messages including protection, health, WaSH and IYCF advice and distribute them through sessions and IEC materials. This will help reduce under-nutrition and break the intergenerational cycle of hunger by raising awareness of malnutrition's main causes, which will help reduce bad dietary habits. Establish a referral pathway towards medical, nutritional and protection higher management facilities.The MEAL team will conduct frequent field visits to collect data and monitor the activities implementation using checklists, observation and interviews with beneficiaries and staff and share notes with project management for corrective action and follow-up.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk 1:• The ever-changing security situation may badly affect the response(likelihood: weak, impact: high)Mitigation:• Safety and security measures and emergency plans will be set and considered before and during the implementation period. Project activities will be designed flexibly to be modified appropriately with the changeable security situation. Implementation sites could be located to safer places as needed in coordination with the Nutrition Cluster and other stakeholders.Risk 2:• The local authorities decide to prevent IDA from implementing the project or part of it.(likelihood: weak, impact: mid)Mitigation:• Intensive advocacy, negotiation, and coordination with local councils, stakeholders.Risk 3:• a GBV violation takes place.(likelihood: weak, impact: high)Mitigation:• Raising awareness through messages about GBV topics in addition to having a complaint mechanism in place and sensitizing beneficiaries on how to use it. Also, staff will be trained to refer suspected GBV cases to specialized facilities.
