Project Summary

194,473 Returnees, IDPs and host communities in Um Dukhun, Bindisi, Wadi Sali, Rokero and Golo Localities, Central Darfur State and Agig, Dordeb, Haya, Sinkat, and Tokar in Red Sea State have increased access to safe water, improved sanitation facilities, and hygiene services.

Needs

Central Darfur: With an improving security situation and reduced conflicts in the targeted locations, returnees are returning to the target area and other IDPs are arriving, stressing WASH services and putting vulnerable groups such as women, children, elders, and people with disabilities and special needs. The project will give high consideration to gender issue through addressing the following needs: '1' Construction of new, and rehabilitation of existing, water sources at the targeted under served locations;'2' Upgrading of existing water sources, hand pumps and hand dug wells, to increase the efficiency of the sources and improvement of water quality;'3' Capacity building for the communities through establishment of Water User Committees, Sanitation and Hygiene Committees composed of equal percentages of male and females '4' Construction of public sanitation facilities; public latrines and solid waste management; '5' Awareness raising on sanitation and hygiene and community contribution to WASH services; Output 1.1Description:1. Increases access to new constructed and rehabilitated safe water sources in Rokero, Um Dukhun and Golo localities of Central Darfur State;2. Increased access to upgraded existing water facilities, hand pumps, and hand dug wells for the communities in Rokero, Um Dukhun and Golo localities of Central Darfur State;3. Increased access to capacity building and participation of women and men in committees;4. Increased access to improved sanitation facilities; Red Sea State: In RSS, CRS has been providing WASH in Haya, Dordeb, and Toker and plans to add Agig and Sinkat. CRS works with women, girls, men, boys, to create accessible and equitable WASH systems through activities including: 1)Household visits, hygiene session/s focusing on women's participation2)Water quality monitoring & testing ( FRC, Bacteriological…etc.)3) Rehabilitation of water scheme/s (Hand pump, Mini water yard, water yards)4) Water Chlorination (chlorination at source level and HH level)5) Construction of new Household or shared latrine/s CRS also plans to implement and scale intensive CLTS throughout all CRS localities (above) in Red Sea State and this is the bulk of the gap we would propose SHF to help us fill.

Activities

'- Drilling 3 new hand pumps;'- Repairing and rehabilitation of 12 of the existing hand pumps;'- Rehabilitation and upgrading of existing hand dug wells;'- Upgrading of the existing potential hand pump to mini-water yard;'- Establish and train Water User Committees on water resource operation, maintenance, management, water quality monitoring and treatment;'- Establishment of community based sanitation and hygiene promoters committees and training on community led total sanitation (CLTS)'- Conduct periodic community mobilization and awareness sessions on best best hygiene practices;'- Establish solid waste management system among the community and support them with necessary tools;

Impact on environment/natural resources

CRS will promote the sustainable use of water sources and promote soil management techniques to prevent erosion. CRS will also collect secondary information on aquifers and water tables to determine the impact of any new or improved water sources on the water table. CRS will work with communities to promote best practices for latrine construction in regard to distance from water sources. CLTS practices will reduce open defecation which will have a net benefit to the environment and areas that are associated with open defecation.
