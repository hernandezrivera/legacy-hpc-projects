Project Summary

the project will be implemented in 5 districts in lower shabelle ( Afgoye, Barawe, Kutuwarey, Marka and Walanweyn), 1 district in Banadir region (Daynile-Garasbaley), 2 districts in Galmudug ( Dhusamarreb and Galkacyo), 2 districts in Puntland (Bossaso and Garowe), 1 district in Jubaland ( Kismayo) and 1 district in Somaliland (Gebiley) and it will cover the health needs of the of internally displaced people and host community targeting women, men, boys and girls.
the project will use an integrated approach to offer free curative and preventive primary health care services through facility and community based approaches with the aim of contributing towards the reduction of maternal and and child morbidity and mortality.
this will include screening and registering at the point of entry or triaging, outpatient consultations for under 5 children, antenatal and post natal care for PLW, Immunization against vaccine preventable disease for both U5 and PLW.
this project will use an approach of establishing strategically located mother and child health clinics and mobile teams .
integrated community case management approach will be used to treat U5 boys and girls at household level.
a total of 442,026 ( 45% of vulnerable people in target districts) persons including 51,839 (51% of WCBA in the target populations) women of child bearing age, 66,304 ( 15% of the total target population) U5, 120 community health workers and 185 staff will be targeted in 10 districts for a period of 12 months.

Project Activities

1. Integrated management of childhood illness through decentralizedMCH and Mobile teams for 66,304 under five children ( 36,467 Boys and 29,837 girls) .2. provision of antenatal and post natal care for 5189 pregnant and lactating women, including micronutrient supplementation and provision of safe delivery kits through decentralized MCH and mobile teams3. provision of immunization services as per IPI calendar for U5 children and PLW attending MCH and mobile outreach sites and through periodic immunization campaigns as per district micro-plans. 4. establishment of district health management committees to oversight, feel ownership and provide support to the project.5. EWARN system is supported at 12 MCH facilities and weekly surveillance data will be submitted to MoH.6. provide 12 mobile health teams in hard to reach and under served areas in Lowe shabelle, Bari and Nugaal regions as well as in Galmudug, Somaliland, Puntland and Jubaland states.7. provide community case management of (ICCM) of common endemic diseases like Malaria, Pneumonia, and Diarrhea to remote and hard to reach in lower shabelle.8. support health strengthing system in all federal states by supporting HMIS team and district health officers.9. provide all tools for demand creation at both community and facility levels through behavior change communications at community networks to sensitize and mobilize individuals, families and communities to adopt targeted health seeking behavior which in turn will impact the health outcome for women and U5 girls and boys.

Mitigation Measures

1. Exclusion and marginalization: this project mainly targets children under five of age and women actively in the reproductive stage regardless of their age. communities in hard to reach areas due to insecurity and poor infrastructure suffer from lack of access to health services. young, mostly first time mothers are likely to lack pre and pst natal services which puts them at risk of birth related complications. immunization services are also likely to be difficult to access for these communities which is a risk to PLW and under 5 children to communicable diseases specially VPDs.the mobile teams will ensure that remote but accessible villages are reached with essential health services. this is included engagement of CHWs who represent various clan profiles, who will increase to reach more target communities, moreover, the project will engage with all major groups to ensure that they are aware about availability of the services and their entitlements. all male care givers have specific considerations in this project in order not to miss the services.data collection and analysis designed in a way that allows PAC to better understand age and gender needs to insure inclusivity.2. External stakeholder interference in beneficiary selection: the amain target population for this project are IDPs and surrounding host communities. displaced populations, youth, women, and the elderly are the most notably in caps who lack of health services. in addition, poor access and long distance to these services expose them, especially women and young girls to SGBV.. access to basic services and opportunities is further compounded by the pervasive marginalization and exclusion of IDPs and certain sections of the host community families from weaker clan or minorities face extra vulnerabilities and protection threats including economic exploitation, trafficking and discrimination. to mitigate against risks identified, PAC will ensure protection sensitive data collection and analysis to identify the specific protection challenges of target population at implementation stage of the project cycle. selection of the project locations and beneficiaries will be guided by the need with specific attention to house holds that are marginalized because of family or clan related factors. at engagement level, all community groups will be represented at target locations. all PAC staff all already trained on PSEA and have signed core of conduct. this project will employ approaches that reduces distances to the service delivery which includes use of mobile teams that will visit villages where the most vulnerability is existed. at inception phase of the project, proper consultation will be done with all key stakeholders in the target districts to ensure that those who benefit from these interventions are not put at risk following the principle of do not harm.3. Armed conflict and violence: PAC works in areas where there is active conflict between state and not-state armed actors or between clans. the key risks include service delivery being appropriated by different sides to the conflict against the others, aid services being diverted to the most power full groups, attack on beneficiaries on their way to service delivery facilities. PAC local team and well trained and experienced in handling sensitive community issues. PAC will implement a neutral as part of humanitarian principles. security SoPs will be implemented for logistical movement of both staff and supplies at field level.
