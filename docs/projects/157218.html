Project Summary

Following urgent estimated in the Sudan HNO 2019(Draft) UPO will focus primary to support 35,000 families and ensuring that the most vulnerable
Women, men, boys and girls are the center of UPO ESNFI response. ESNFI Needs assessment will continuously be conducted with support from other partners to understanding the gaps and social issues affecting vulnerable communities and triangulate into programmes addressing these needs. Based on the inter-agency assessment mission in Central Darfur, conducted in April 2018, assessment in North Darfur conducted in July 2018 and East Darfur conducted in Sept 2019 and UPO needs assessment conducted in South Darfur in Nov 2018, the Communities in the target areas are in desperate need of NFI and shelter materials as a step of support improving their lives to cope in managing recurrent shock and build resilience. In order to have effective and efficient response to the needs, UPO will continue with its ESNFI response in Darfur and South Kordofan. UPO will priorities North Darfur, El Fasher, Tawila in South Darfur, Kass, Nyala, will be prioritiesed. In East Darfur, UPO will target all the localities Ed Daein, Bahr El Arab, Assalaya, Yassin, Abu Jabra, Adila, and El Ferrous. In Central Darfur, UPO target (Nertiti Rokoro Umm Dukhun. and in South Kordofan, UPO will target three localities with it ESNFI intervention (Abu Jubaiha, Rashad, El Abassiya ), localities that scored 4/5 in the HNOs 2019 will be prioritized to reach many people that are mostly in need of NFI and emergency shelter. UPO will ensure close coordination with all partners throughout its interventions. UPO activities in target locations will include coordination of needs assessments, IES distribution, training, awareness raising on fire prevention, gender and environment protection, SSB shelter and NFIs distribution, mobilization of resources, sensitization of communities and coordination of responding to the immediate needs of the people.

Needs

An estimated 250,000 people have been displaced by the conflict so far since 2013,Including some 120,000 people within South Darfur and 40,000 people who have fled toCentral Darfur with trends indicating continued displacement as armed violence Continues. Hundreds of thousands have lost their livelihoods and access to basicServices. The Jebel Marra massive, straddling Sudan’s North Darfur, Central Darfur andSouth Darfur states, has since mid-January seen significant civilian displacement as aresult of increased fighting between the SAF and the rebel groups. ongoing conflict in SKhas resulted in an increased influx in 2018. The majority of people, in particular displaced, women headed households and those with no or limited productive assets, are living in a state of extreme poverty. For the past years since the conflict of 2013 and July 2016, humanitarian agencies made efforts to rescue people from appalling humanitarians’ conditions however, due to limited resources and immense needs across the country; it is far from reaching milestone of improving lives of all affected people. Amongst other basic human needs, one of the most immediate needs remains getting access to NFI and emergency shelter for vulnerable people in particular children, lactating and pregnant women, women headed household, newly displaced people, elderly and people with special vulnerabilities. based on the results of multi-sectorial needs assessment conducted by UPO in Sept 2018, South , Central , North Darfur State, UPO aims to support 35,000 individuals (14, women, 8000 men,8 girls and 5 boys) . Needs-based assessment will continuously be conducted in coordination with other partners to understanding the gaps and social issues affecting vulnerable communities and triangulate into programmes addressing these needs. Activities

1. UPO will continue coordinating with partners to conduct participatory need assessments to determine NFI and Shelter needs and identifying the most vulnerable persons using sex and age disaggregated data 2/ provision of lifesavings NFI and emergency shelter materials to new IDPs, Returnees and host communities 2. . Conduct community consultation meetings on risks and social cohesion related to NFI and emergency shelter response that will enhance coping mechanism 3. Conduct community awareness session on coping mechanism and social cohesion of vulnerable people in the communities4. Conducting NFIs/ES needs assessment, verification and distribution of NFIs for 8,200 HHs of newly displaced, protracted IDPs and other most vulnerable populations in timely manner. 5. 2 Provision of improved emergency shelter materials for 1000 (600 female headed, 400 men) of newly displaced populations.6. Conduct training and awareness sessions for 1800 (600women, 400men, 400boys400 and girls) on environmentally friendly shelter construction techniques, SSB productions as alternative to fired bricks (red brick) and environment conservations.7. Training 1000 community leaders (400 female & 200 male) on risks reduction and emergency management e.g. proper shelter construction, fire incident prevention etc... . 8. Training 500 most vulnerable women on fuel sufficient stove (mud) production skills to enable them to possess new income opportunity. 9. Construction of 600 transitional environmentally friendly shelter (350 female-headed HHs, 250 male headed HHs) most vulnerable returnees and protracted IDPs in rural areas.
