1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

For over four years, the province of Cabo Delgado in Mozambique has experienced brutal attacks by armed groups, while neighboring provinces of Nampula, Niassa, Zambezia and Sofala are seeing a spillover of displacement. As a result of the insecurity, 735,334 people have been displaced in Northern Mozambique as of November 2021 and the humanitarian situation continues to deteriorate in both hosting and displaced communities, and for those left behind. In total, an estimated 1.5 million people are in need of urgent humanitarian assistance and protection in 2022 (Humanitarian Response Plan 2021). To respond to the needs, this project will focus on the following interventions:• Intervention I: Camp Management and Camp Coordination (CCCM) • Intervention II: Emergency shelter and upgrades, Shelter Cluster coordination and Shelter NFI Pipeline • Intervention III: Mental Health and Psychosocial Support (MHPSS) / Protection • Intervention IV: Water, Sanitation and Hygiene (WASH) • Intervention V: Displacement Tracking Matrix (DTM) • Intervention VI: Civil/Birth Registration • Intervention VII: Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA)

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Training on data collection techniques, methodologies, introducing IOM’s data protection, general protection, disability, and inclusion as well as PSEA standards in data collection efforts for local authorities, partners and IOM staff.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

Direct data collection and data management that is gender responsive and is inclusive of sex and age disaggregated data, population needs vulnerabilities

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Disaggregated data collection that includes identification of persons with disabilities in process of monitoring conditions and needs of affected populations
