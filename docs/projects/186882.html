Project Summary

The Covid-19 pandemic has negatively impacted the social and economic life of populations already living in difficult conditions, requiring a rapid humanitarian response from humanitarian actors and partners present in the country. Thus, Catholic Relief Services (CRS) and Caritas partners Organisation Catholique pour le Développement Et la Solidarité (OCADES) Kaya, OCADES Dedougou, and Association of Private Catholic Health Structures (ASPC) are proposing a multisectoral response to improve the living conditions of the population by strengthening the capacities of health centers in the Boucle du Mouhoun and Centre Nord regions.
With OCADES and ASPC as implementing partners, this project’s interventions expect to reach at least 450,000 people and 12 health centers through the following activities:
• Sensitization on COVID-19
• Rehabilitation and construction of latrines and showers.
• Water connection.
• Provision of PPE equipment
In order to ensure sustainability, the project will also train health workers and community-based health workers, as well as support the strengthening of hygiene committees for the management of WASH facilities and COVID prevention actions.

2 - BESOINS, ACTIVITÉS ET RÉSULTATS ATTENDUS
2.1. Décrivez les besoins, activités et résultats attendus

COVID-19 was first identified in Burkina Faso in March 2019. As of December 7, 2021, all regions of the country are affected, with a cumulative number of 16,334 cases, resulting in 290 deaths. Although significant progress has been made in preparing and equipping local health facilities to manage COVID-19 cases, access to health services equipped for the early diagnosis of suspected cases of COVID 19 remains limited outside Ouagadougou and Bobo-Dioulasso, especially in areas of insecurity. CRS, OCADES, the Tougan District Health Centre authorities, the Catholic Church of Burkina Faso aim through this project to support critical health care needs for underserved communities in the context of COVID-19 with a focus on health centers in the Centre Nord and Boucle du Mouhoun regions, while strengthening the ability of health center workers to plan and act quickly to respond to the health emergency. This intervention will aim to prevent the spread of infection. Activities will include COVID-19 sensitization through radio ads, prevention, infection control, WASH messages in health care facilities, community case management, procurement of essential supplies such as PPE kits, surveillance, risk communication and community engagement (RCCE). Community-based health workers will receive capacity strengthening support and will help notify community cases with symptoms but who are reluctant to seek care at health facilities. The project will promote collaboration between targeted health facilities and the district health team in order to facilitate monitoring and referral of complex cases. As a result of the intervention, participants will know the first signs of coronavirus infection, such as fever and a cold, or cough. Participants will also know how to prevent the spread of coronavirus infection, such as the use of barrier measures, hand-washing and social distancing. Finally, supported health centers will have the equipment they need to fight covid-19, such as laboratories, isolation rooms, and or incinerators.
