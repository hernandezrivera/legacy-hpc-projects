Project Summary

FSNAU and partners survey conducted September 2020 indicate the persistence of widespread acute malnutrition in Somalia due to a combination of factors, including food insecurity, high morbidity, low immunization and vitamin-A supplementation, and poor care practices. At the national level, the median prevalence of Global Acute Malnutrition (GAM) prevalence has remained Serious (10–14.9%) for the past four consecutive seasons (11.8 in Gu 2020, 13.1% in 2019/20 Deyr, 13.8% in 2019 Gu and 12.6 % in 2018/19 Deyr). High levels of acute malnutrition continue to persist driven by several factors, including high morbidity, below sphere immunization and Vitamin-A supplementation, poor childcare practices, inadequate access to clean/safe drinking water and acute food insecurity. Consequently, the country is facing a Triple Threat of Desert Locust, flooding’s and the global pandemic COVID-19 contributing towards the deterioration of production outputs, social economics increasing vulnerabilities of household. A total of 2.4 M under five children and PLW in need, while 1.14 M need treatment services and 1.16 M needs prevention services, despite the nutrition cluster effort in reaching 66% of the target in HRP 2020 still the needs on the ground is higher and needed urgent focus on the both treatment and prevention services to the vulnerable populations. However, Crude Death Rate and Under-Five Death Rate were relatively low in most areas. Urgent treatment and nutrition support is required for an estimated 1960,300 children under the age of five years (total acute malnutrition burden), who will likely face acute malnutrition through June 2021, including 341,679 who are likely to be severely malnourished. Integrated interventions should be provided to support recovery and prevent deterioration in the nutrition situation. The proposed activities will improve equitable access to quality lifesaving curative nutrition services through systemic identification, referral, and treatment of acute malnourished case boys, girls and pregnant and lactating women. It will also provide holistic life-saving and life sustaining nutrition intervention. Through this intervention ARD will ensure to provide age and gender appropriate while improving the protection risk of these vulnerable under 5 children boys,girls ,pregnant and lactating women including women in the target location
ARD will ensure to coordinates with both nutrition cluster at regional and federal level as well as the other actors in the target location

Mitigation Measures

Limited information and communication might hinder the understanding between ARD teams in the field and community leaders, however, to mitigate any such eventuality ARD will create feedback mechanism and reasonable compliance officer within its office as well as hotline mobile services to ensure all beneficiary complains are addressed timely. ARD intends to overcome this by having a need based assessment and disaggregated data so that the special needs of the vulnerable children boys, girls and pregnant and lactating women as well as disable people and those with special gender needs are taken care of. Location, distance and access routes to the OTP sites and services might be a challenge of preventing factors limiting the access of the OTP sites ARD will enable its beneficiaries have accessible to all of the boys and girls, including disable and minority group as well as the most vulnerable IDPs children. To support the inclusion of women ARD will support training of female members through employment and capacity enhancement.
