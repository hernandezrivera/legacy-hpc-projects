Project Summary

Enhancing the resilience capacity of 60most vulnerable HH in Afrin by providing them with vocational, business and life skills training, (2 rounds each round 8 months targeting 30 HHs) consumption support and coaching to enhance their capacity to have access to income generation opportunities/employment.
will took place at the livelihood center in Afrin. Selected beneficiaries will have access to on-job vocational training and income generation opportunities for vulnerable households over eight months, in addition to sustainable support to strengthen their capacity and access to income generation/employment opportunities. The period of eight months was designed, where beneficiaries will be supported by monthly wages, equal to Survival Minimum Expenditure Basket (SMEB), over the eight months. This support will be conditional as beneficiaries will be asked to commit to 6 months of vocational training and 2 months of life and business skills training. Over the course of this intervention, two rounds will be conducted under result 1.1 (each round is 8 months). The main vocational training scheme will be the textile scheme. However, during the project, IMDAT is planning to conduct labor market assessment to study the feasibility of right sizing the textile vocational training and/or adding different type of vocational training based on the context, demand, and supply, of the local market at targeted areas.

3 - Needs

If vulnerable people at targeted areas are provided with income generation opportunities, technical and market knowledge, skills training and supported to achieve improved access to markets and sustained employment; then the capacity of these vulnerable people to have more sustainable access to income generation will be improved and will be able to purchase more food, leading to increased food security, meet their basic needs, and a decrease in the use of negative coping strategies, while becoming less dependent on humanitarian aid.The main goal of this intervention (cash for training) is to provide paid sewing training opportunities for vulnerable groups of men and women, and to prepare them to enter the labor market after completing the training.So iMDAT will provide all training requirements, monthly financial assistance in exchange for commitment to training for 6 months + 2 months of mentoring. Also, beneficiaries will be provided with assets (sewing machines and tools) after graduation, to be used at home in the case of women who prefer to work from home or to facilitate obtaining work in sewing workshops.Priority will be given to families headed by women, and those include PWD and elderly. Also, families whose breadwinner does not have a profession. And the categories of IDPs and residents will be taken into consideration.

4 - Implementation of activities

The 8 months will include 6 months center-based training and 2 months coaching, in addition to that this will be followed by two months monitoring and following up. Further a sustainable component will be distributed after they finish the vocational training (in kind toolbox, value up to 500USD per beneficiary).(1.1.1) Production cycle by skilled instructors will take place. The application process will start by publishing an (1.1.2) Online advertisement and application for both rounds (self-targeting approach). Then, (1.1.3) Verification of application requests for 100% followed by a (1.1.4) base-line assessment for both rounds of VT trainees and (1.1.5) Selecting unskilled beneficiaries, based on eligibility and vulnerability criteria, attending the vocational training over 8 months for 2 rounds (30 trainees round 1; 30 trainees round 2). (1.1.6) After the selection is finished the (1.1.7) Tailoring-Skills Training (hard-skills) for both rounds of VT trainees take place. After finishing the VT training cycle, during the two months coaching the (1.1.9) Business and life skills Training (soft skills) for both rounds of VT trainees. At the same time of VT training, the (1.1.10) Cash disbursement (under iMDAT supervision) for both rounds of VT trainees on monthly basis. After the 6 months VT is finished, the livelihood expert will (1.1.11) Conduct skills-graduation exam for both rounds of VT trainees at the end of each round.
