Project Summary

Nigeria reports 2,950 confirmed COVID-19 cases with 106 cases in Borno State. The purpose of this project is to promote awareness and prevention of COVID-19 and support constant handwashing. This will be done through awareness messaging through creative means that promote less contact such as pre-recorded messages, IEC materials, use of public address system and hygiene promotion done by trained volunteers from the community. Handwashing stations will be established and handwashing kits (soap, buckets) will be distributed. The target areas are Monguno and Jere where ZOA currently imlements WASH and FSL projects with 14,890 individuals ( 2,481 HH).
Monguno and Jere have congested areas and have the first and second highest percentage of all IDP population in Borno State, 18.1% and 14.6% respectively, according to IOM . IASC guidelines on Scaling up COVID 19 response refer to Camps and Camp like settings as having specific challenges and vulnerabilities . In these two areas, congestion and sanitation are perennial challenges. As such, these two locations already have annual recurrence of cholera outbreak.
The project will also equip staff and volunteers with necessary personal protective equipment (PPE) and will implement SEMA mandated protocols of social distancing in all activities including no gathering of more than 20 persons and spread its distributions accordingly

1 - Needs

ZOA currently works in the areas of Monguno and Jere with FSL and WASH projects. In Monguno, ZOA works with food insecure populations selected for vulnerability including poor food consumption scores. However, the current cash transfer program does not cover the costs incurred for much needed hygiene promotion and handwashing kits. In Jere, ZOA’s WASH initiatives target populations that have less access to hygiene messaging, poor access to WASH facilities ( water source, latrines, bathing shelters, soap). This intervention will need additional awareness and additional handwashing stations brought about by COVID 19 as it necessitates that even public places such as business and markets be the site of handwashing stations and awareness The reality of the COVID-19 pandemic has brought to fore other needs . Both areas have challenges of congestion and sanitation that requires specific attention and intervention. There is a need to increase awareness of CODVID-19 as well as support preventive measures such as hand washing.

2 - Activities

The activities include :1.) Conduct training for staff and volunteers carrying out messaging on COVID-19 key messages, as well as on how to protect themselves as recommended by the WASH Sector in its COVID-19 – Hygiene Promotion Protocols. 2.) WASH: Hygiene: Conduct hygiene promotion with focus on COVID-19 prevention messages without physical contact with beneficiaries. Use Governmental and sector approved messages, have pre-recorded messages in various widely spoken tribe languages and have same messages disseminated using megaphones or public address systems by locally recruited field staff. 3.) Distribute handwashing materials (soap, buckets, etc.) to targeted beneficiaries as recommended by the WASH Sector. ZOA would also include business owners and work with health actors to share information and training facilitation4.) Establish access to handwashing stations in strategic areas.

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

ZOA has an established complaints and response mechanism embedded in its community enagement strategy. Upon community entry and formation of community commiittees , ZOA introduces its policies on welcoming complaints and response in accordance with core humanitarian standards. This is followed by an orientation on the complaints and response mechanism and PSEAH. Concrete mechanisms are established on ground such as the establishment of help desks to receive face to face communication , suggestion boxes and a hotline for complaints. ZOA uses a kobo based system on phones and tablets to ensure fast and accurate documentation and reporting of complaints leading to resolution.
