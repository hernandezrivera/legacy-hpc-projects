Project Summary

Project summary
This Project is designed to provide life-saving nutrition services in poor neighborhoods with high density of Lebanese and migrant in vulnerable situation through both community and facility-based interventions including:
1.Infant and young child feeding (IYCF) practices optimization, micronutrient supplementation distribution, and reporting of blanket distribution of Infant formula
2. Screening, referral, and treatment of malnutrition (SAM without complications and MAM) among children under 5 and PLW
The project will be implemented by Mercy-USA in the North and Akkar Governorates. The project will target 23,963 people (Host communities, PRS/PRL and migrants) disaggregated as follows:
- 8,954 Pregnant and Lactating Women (PLWs)
- 15,008 Children under 5
35 localities will be targeted through the support which will be provided to 6 Non-supported Primary Healthcare Centers(PHCs) in high populated areas in the targeted Cadasters (List of pre-selected PHCs is attached to the proposal).The targeted PHCs were identified according to:
1-The list of non-supported PHCs shared in the allocation paper
2-The need assessments conducted by Mercy-USA
Note: The selected PHCs could be changed based on MoPH guidance, and based on the evolving situation needs in terms of humanitarian assistance.
2 outreach staff (1 IYCF counselors and 1 Community Health Workers) will be based out of each PHCs to provide visual screening, referrals and IYCF services for PLW and children under 5. These outreach teams will work in parallel and direct coordination with the selected health facilities in the targeted areas.
Staff will assess the nutritional status of children under 5 and distribute nutritional supplies to them as needed. Additionally, IYCF services will be provided through:
- IYCF sessions
- Counseling PLWs and caregivers of children under 24 months of age and providing messages on IYCF recommended practices to mothers who have malnourished children
- Promoting timely initiation of breastfeeding, exclusive breastfeeding and introduction of safe, age appropriate and nutrition diversified complementary food (including related hygiene messaging)
- Provision of Skilled Infant and Young Child Feeding (IYCF) counselling and support to for breastfed and non-breastfed children under 6 months of age
- Distributing micronutrients and nutrition supplies for the PLWs and children
- Reporting breast milk substitute distribution to the cluster
The Micronutrient survey will be conducted at national level across all 8 Lebanese Governorates + the camps. The overall objective of the survey is to conduct a nutrition assessment representative at the national and governorate level to determine the nutrition status of children under 5 years of age, maternal nutrition status for pregnant and lactating women as well as critical IYCF practices. The survey results will be used to derive recommendations needed to inform the nutrition sector response in the country.
Hence this project will contribute to sector specific objectives through:
- The provision of life-saving nutrition services in high needs and remote locations through both community and facility-based including screening, referral, treatment, micronutrient supplementation and infant and young child feeding (IYCF);
- An assessment of the nutritional status of children aged 6-59 months in order to generate information for an improved response by conducting a Micronutrient survey.
This response will adopt the safety recommendations in the COVID-19 context.
This project will empower mothers and HH members through training them to screen their own children for malnutrition by teaching them how to use color-coded Mid-Upper Arm Circumference (MUAC) tapes.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

This project directly contributes to the Nutrition cluster-specific objectives, HRP objectives, and the allocation's common objectives through the provision of life-saving nutrition services in high needs and remote locations through both community and facility-based including screening, referral, treatment, micronutrient supplementation, and infant and young child feeding (IYCF). The distribution of nutrition supplies for children will increase the micronutrient intake and hence lead to an improvement of micronutrient status, which will, in turn, improve the child’s health, including reduced morbidity and mortality, improved growth, cognition, appetite, and other functional outcomes, and prevent stunting and malnutrition. Moreover, the provision of micronutrients for pregnant women to avoid micronutrient deficiency during pregnancy could lead to anemia, hypertension, obstetric complications, and even maternal death, and in fetus, potentially causing a failure in growth and development.Pregnant and lactating women (PLWs) that haven't received any micronutrients during their pregnancy or after will receive micronutrients enough for 90 days.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

Mercy-USA conducted an assessment during the design phase of the project to engage all affected populations and reflect their needs in the project and how it will affect them, so they are able to make informed decisions and choices. Mercy-USA will carry out consultations with community members, including and not limited to women, disabled, elderly, and PLW, throughout the varying project stages through FGD to ensure they are at the center of the response and contribute to the decision-making process. The project activities are designed to be conducted at facility and community levels (HH) by identifying and referring mothers and children (including PWDs) who display breastfeeding difficulties or have malnutrition or health issues to the pre-identified health facilities. For any PWDs in need of a referral, transfer to the health facility will be facilitated by Mercy-USA.
