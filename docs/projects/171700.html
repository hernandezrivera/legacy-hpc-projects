Project Summary

As identified by Child Protection AoR, 1.87 million children including 10% of children with disabilities are in need of immediate child protection services. Boys and girls of all ages in Somalia continue to endure multiple protection risks and violations of their rights. COVID has had negative impacts on children and their families including exacerbating risks of domestic violence, conscription, child trafficking, child marriage, child labor, children needs are unmet, family separation among others in Somalia. A child protection assessment conducted by Relief International in schools revealed myriad of safety and protection issues that affect children in schools. Including bullying, corporal punishment, girls being harassed and attacked on their way to school/home, boys being harassed by teachers among others. There is lack of harmonized data on child protection needs and support provided in Somalia which hampers data based planning and programming. While case workers and government social workers have inadequate skills and coordination capacity for enhanced service delivery. RI recognizes, children with disabilities are in most times invisible and excluded from basic services in the society. Most of girls with disabilities consist of high proportion of sexual violence survivors. RI will work with communities, schools and child protection system to address the above mentioned concerns. We shall integrate child protection and safety into education projects. This will include identification and training of a teacher or CEC member as child protection focal point. Updating/mapping of child protection referral pathways and linkages with school protection focal point for referrals. Training of teachers on PSS, psychological First Aid, Emotional Learning, positive discipline and child safety and protection. Children/learners will be trained on child rights, safety and protection and will reach out these messages to other learners through a peer outreach model. Training of school management on learners’ safety and protection and supporting them to develop protection and safety action plans for their schools and implement them. Supporting schools with recreational materials and training focal points on identification of children who may have psychosocial challenges for support (PFA) and referrals to specialized MHPSS service providers.
RI will continue to work in the target communities to establishment/strengthening of community based child protection mechanisms and support these structures to contribute towards prevention and response to child protection concerns in their community. Community protection committees will be established where they are non-existence while those that exist will be strengthened and linked to formal child protection system for referrals of cases reported through this structure. The members will be trained on promoting safe reporting by upholding confidentiality, safety and protection of survivors as well as upholding their dignity. The members of the child protection committees will be supported to facilitate community led child protection and safety outreaches in their communities, schools and through radio outreach. Religious leaders will be engaged in advocating against harmful practices that exposes children to risks such as FGM, early/forced marriage and domestic violence. Parents will be targeted for positive parenting and PSS skills development through face to face and through radio messaging to ensure the immediate layer of protection around the child is strengthened. RI will provide child and gender friendly case management and support state level and regional CP AoR on capacity building of social workers and CP AoR members on age and gender sensitive child protection services. Updating/mapping of CP and MHPSS service providers in the target areas and supporting CP AoR at regional and state level to strengthen referral pathways amongst the identified service providers.

Project Activities

RI will implement the following activities: - Train and support community based protection mechanisms members to conduct child safety and protection community outreaches, receiving cases of abuse and link these cases to CP service providers, the community based protection will be sensitized to target vulnerable households including those of children living with disabilities. Radio and face to face community outreach on child protection and safety and psychosocial support and positive parenting. Caregivers/parents of children living with disabilities will be sensitized on needs of children living with disabilities and ensuring appropriate physical and psychosocial support care. Provision of child friendly space services to children including children living with disabilities. Provision of child and gender sensitive case management to children survivors of abuse or at risk including children with disabilities. Conduct Identification, Documentation, Tracing and Reunification for Separated and unaccompanied minors to their immediate families. Families receiving re-unified children will be supported to receive these children through training and basic material support that will include support for children living with disabilities. Teacher training on social-emotional learning, inclusive education for children with disabilities, protection and Psychosocial Support /Psychological First Aid. Provision of water and soap as well as hygiene education including COVID prevention and early detection awareness raising targeting teachers and learners including use of various modes of communication to ensure information is accessible to children and teachers with disabilities. CEC training on school management, resource mobilization, child protection and safety of all learners including how to put in place appropriate COVID protocols in school and how to make the school facilities and infrastructure disability friendly. Identification and training of child protection focal points. Establishment/fostering of referral linkages with CP, MHPSS, services for children living with disabilities and health facilities (for COVID) service providers. Provide age, disability and gender responsive recreational and play materials. Support teachers to engage all learners in play and organize play times for learners of different ages;- Coach and work with school protection focal points to identify signs of distress in children and for referrals to MHPSS specialized service providers. Child rights and protection outreach to all school children using peer to peer approach. Training of case workers on child and gender sensitive case management and the use of CPMIS+. Advocacy workshops with religious leaders. Mapping/updating of CP and MHPSS service providers in the target areas and supporting CP AoR at regional and state level to strengthen referral pathways amongst the identified service providers. Project personnel, community based protection committees will be sensitized against discrimination of children living with disabilities and to address access barriers and ensure equal participation. RI has elaborate safeguarding policies and procedures that includes child safeguarding and Protection from Sexual Exploitation, Abuse and Harassment (PSEAH)mechanisms. RI will ensure all engaged personnel are aware of safeguarding commitments and are engaged after through background checks to ensure they are fit to work with children and vulnerable adults. All personnel will commit to uphold safeguarding commitment through signing up of safeguarding behavior protocols. Children and communities will be alerted of expected code of behaviors from personnel and on available channels to providing feedback to RI. RI already has properly trained and supported safeguarding focal points whose contacts and names are shared with parents and children for any feedback. Through this project, RI will facilitate children and communities to conduct safety audit of 10 IDP settlements.

Mitigation Measures

-Mitigation measures on lack of access/awareness of protection assistance or referral services for survivors of protection incidents; RI will work with community protection mechanisms to create awareness at community level on where to safely report safety and protection concerns affecting children including reporting to designated community protection committee members, report to government child protection units and hot lines and through RI protection focal points/staff. Through the radio outreach, RI will also disseminate to communities various platforms that they could use to report protection concerns. At school level, children, teachers, CEC and parents will be alerted of the school protection focal point so that all concerns of protection can be reported to the focal point. The school protection focal points will be linked with specialized CP and MHPSS for support. The mapped/updated CP and MHPSS referral pathway service providers’ directories will be disseminated among community based protection mechanisms, school protection focal points and service providers.- Mitigation measures for barriers faced by beneficiaries to access complaints and feedback mechanisms; RI will hold consultations with different members of community (boys, girls, women, men, IDP, minority clans, host community, PLWDs and elderly) to gather their preferred mode of providing feedback, identify issues of access, safety and community confidence in the feedback mechanism. These inputs will be factored in the design and operationalization of the feedback mechanisms. RI will ensure there are diverse age, gender, and diversity friendly ways of providing feedback. RI will ensure that, once the mechanisms is set-up, it is highly socialized among beneficiaries using diverse user friendly and appropriate communication modes. Communities will be made aware of the expected support and code of conduct for personnel involved in the project and encouraged to report any concerns and unacceptable behaviors. RI will uphold confidentiality, safety and ensure prompt response is provided to issues raised through the mechanism. - Mitigation measures under risk of unsafe/unethical data management; RI will train all personnel involved in the project including community protection committee members and case workers on data protection. Caregivers’/parents’ informed consents and children’s assent will be sought before intervention and sharing with other service providers. Personnel will be required to sign up safeguarding protocols that have data protection protocol as a way of committing to protect the beneficiaries' data. RI has elaborate data management and protection policy and procedures which guides all our data management processes, this includes ensuring safety of hard copies case files by keeping files with personal data locked and only accessible to staff on 'need to know' basis. Soft copies are safely safeguarded with passwords that are renewed after staff leave or take up other responsibilities. Official information including survivors and patient information is only saved on few selected office laptops and sent/received through official emails only (with passwords). Case files are coded and coding information kept under lock and key in a separate cabinet.
