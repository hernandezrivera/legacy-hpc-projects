Project Summary

Qualified technical support to Shelter Cluster and its Members in urban areas, with a special focus on informal settlements, to harmonize and implement adequate, affordable and resilient interventions for the COVID-19 pandemic response and recovery in Maputo City and Metropolitan area:
SHORT-TERM INTERVENTIONS
OUTPUT 1: Rapid Participatory Mapping and Assessment of available spaces and vacant buildings to set up spaces for separation and quarantine for infected persons and suspected cases, NFI distribution centres, potential places for overcrowding and contamination in informal settlements (market, worship places, water collection points):

- Activity 1.1: Elaboration of Rapid Mapping supported by georeferenced information and technical local and global standards of potential agglomeration points in informal settlements and 48 available spaces and/or vacant buildings in peri-urban areas to accommodate short-term interventions to the COVID-19 response and mid-term interventions in the recovery phase-100,000
- Activity 1.2: Site assessment and identification, based on close interaction with local authorities and cluster partners to accommodate 25 emergency quarantine shelters and 13 COVID-19 treatment centres in peri-urban areas-50,000

OUTPUT 2. COVID-19 specific messages advocating for mitigation measures at-household level to be broadcasted in potential agglomeration points within and nearby informal settlements and during emergency shelter and NFI distributions in peri-urban areas:

- Activity 2.1: Elaboration of communication strategy based on WHO recommendations and tailored for informal settlements and local contexts-5,000
- Activity 2.2: Preparation of tailored and ludic communication and advocacy materials-50,000
- Activity 2.3: Distribution and dissemination of COVID-19 specific messages in partnerships with local authorities, community leaders, religious leaders and other relevant local stakeholders. 100,000

OUTPUT 3. Additional shelter targeting vulnerable groups who need self-isolation, suspected and infected cases of families living in overcrowded conditions -- including families in urban informal settlements, families hosting IDPs and large families living in one-room shelters- to facilitate expansion, upgrading and partitioning of shelters and homes to reduce overcrowding and improve ventilation:

- Activity 3.1. Based on identified vacant buildings and sites, in line with available shelter solutions, design of prototypes to rapid implementation of 18 emergency quarantine shelters in Maputo (including cities, districts and municipalities in the metropolitan area-200,000
-Activity 3.2. Considering minimum interventions and prioritizing existing vacant building, organization of spatial layout and subdivision of spaces to provide appropriate and adequate solutions for vulnerable groups in need of self-isolation and isolated areas for suspected cases and infected cases that do not need hospitalization- 400,000
-Activity 3.3. Coordination with the WASH Cluster to ensure beneficiaries have access to toilets and handwashing facilities-200,000
