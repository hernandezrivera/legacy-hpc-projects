Project Summary

NGO X proposes to provide lifesaving safe water to approximately 518,999 vulnerable individuals in Idleb Governorate and North Aleppo, Northwest Syria. This will be implemented via the provision of essential support to 37 Water Stations (WSs) that are currently functioning and 4 Water Units (WUs).
NGO X has extensive experience delivering water supply programming in Idleb since 2014. In 2022 NGO X provided clean safe water to the homes of over 800,000 people in Idleb via 51 WS and 12,190 people in North Aleppo via 2 water stations.
The proposed project builds upon the experience of having successfully delivered several similar projects funded by OCHA, FCDO, ECHO and BHA. The aim of the action is to ensure access to reliable, safe, and clean drinking water to vulnerable conflict-affected communities and IDP sites including host and displaced populations in high priority northern sub-districts of Idleb Governorate and North Aleppo.
Core activities under water supply sector will include procurement of electricity and/or fuel, minor infrastructure rehabilitation, training the water station operators, coaching of WUs and water quality monitoring. The proposed action will enable people living in the targeted areas to get enough clean and safe drinking water. Improving the access to safe water will reduce the time spent on collecting water, reduce protection risks and prevent the spread of waterborne diseases, often affecting children. Following WASH cluster standards, between 30 - 40 liters/person/day (LPD) will be piped directly to households (HH) to ensure equitable access for all community members. It should be noted that due to funding constraints and limited water network capacity, it may not be possible to go over 35 LPD.
Under sanitation sub sector: the activities will include the rehabilitation of sewage infrastructure in 4 locations including IDP sites, through converting the open sewer system to closed sewer system in order to reduce the impact of open sewage system and to ensure the sustainability in the sanitation services.
In addition to that NGO X proposes to implement infrastructure rehabilitation or improvement in targeted sites including roads / shelter rehabilitation to ensure complementarity, sustainability, and access to WASH facilities.
In target areas, to complement WASH activities, HHs—including school children, WU staff and vulnerable individuals benefiting from NGO X food or cash distributions will be reached with essential hygiene promotion sessions adapted to COVID-19 and cholera best working practices as outlined by the WASH cluster. Themes covered will include water safety, water conservation, COVID-19, cholera prevention and safe hygiene practices.

3 - Needs

In Nov 2022, NGOX conducted HH and KII assessments in 10 communities, 22 camps in 3 subdistricts in Idleb and North Aleppo, which figured that the main source of drinking water for the affected people varied between water trucking to houses with 49%-65 HHs and water trucking to collection points with 47%-63 HHs and the main challenges of accessing safe and clean water were high prices of water, lack of HHs water storage capacity, distance, and water is not appropriately treated. In Idleb, 63% use some form of water trucking spending around 4 USD/month, 54% of key informants stating that high water prices are a significant barrier to accessing water- REACH report 2021, while 49% of assessed communities cited the high cost of water trucking as a common challenge- REACH Sep 2022. Through the NGOX 2022 intervention, free water provided to more than 800,000 BNFs, and helped reduce the financial burden on BNFs. In addition, NGOX was able to install a solar system for 3 WSs, and connect 39 WSs with local electricity grid, which reduced the monthly costs of water pumping and supported establishing a sustainable environmentally friendly energy source. The 2022 WASH action helped to reduce water borne disease from 299,126 to 249,727 in Azaz and Afrin. As for the IDP sites, till September 2022, 1,420 IDP sites, of which 1,234 sites are ITSs, with a total population of around 1,84 M IDPs (CCCM Cluster report, Sep 2022).

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk of unequal distribution of services to inadvertently empower or strengthen the position of armed groups or other actors. Mitigation: Ensure services are based on needs and minimum standards for WASH; Establish close coordination and joint planning with local authorities to promote equity and “do no harm principle” all the time; Inform targeted communities of available services and reporting mechanisms. Monitor unintended consequences and inadvertent benefits to opposed groups. Risk of improper storage of hazardous supplies (chlorine and other materials) can result in explosions, human casualties, and asset destruction. Mitigation: Ensure proper storage of supplies with clear provision of care and safety consideration; Train staff and safe storage, usage and distribution of hazardous supplies; Make sure that clear instructions for use are available for chlorine products primarily for household use. Regularly monitor that storage, instructions and handling comply with specifications for the different supplies. Risk of theft of WASH rehabilitation/construction materials such as generators and construction materials (during delivery/storage/after installation) by armed groups and used in conflict activities. Mitigation: Promote close collaboration and MoUs with local authorities to promote local ownership; Ensure NGO X’s have SOPs to mitigate risk of theft and that the investigation department is equipped to investigate cases of theft. Monitoring includes regular inventor


8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Risk: Risk of sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA) by NGO X staff or other stakeholders involved in the project, disproportionately affecting women and girls. Mitigation: 1) Ensure Safeguarding policies, Code of Conduct and PSEA protocols are shared and signed by staff and partners2) Conduct PSEA trainings with staff and inform of potential sanctions if PSEA policies are violated 3) Consult women, men, girls and boys and persons with disabilities and older people about their needs, access to services and the design of activities to understand risks and to identify mitigation measures 4) Conduct awareness raising activities with beneficiaries on entitlements and rights, and NGOX’s feedback mechanism 5) Ensure NGO X’s dedicated safeguarding focal point are trained in handling sensitive issues arising from actual or perceived breaches of policiesMonitoring: Regular monitoring of activities and consultations with targeted communities. NGO X will ensure women are included in consultations, activity monitoring and PDMs.


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumptions: - Continued access to targeted areas and vulnerable populations, while security conditions remain stable; - Sufficient and timely access to required inputs through procurement, available transportation remains; - Strong coordination and collaboration with local authorities and humanitarian actors in the AoO prevails; - NGOX's strategies and mechanisms to prevent armed and sanctioned actors from interference in programmes Risks:- risk of international and Syrian staff losing legal status; - sudden border closures disrupting operations; - fraud including banking, cash management, procurement, transport, and programming; - Tools/repair supplies not available: - Fuel theft or misuse at water stations; Aid diversion. - NGO X loses access to some areas in Idleb or Aleppo governorate as a result of shifting political and military control on the ground. Mitigation: - Safety and Security Evacuation Plans and Standard Operating Procedures in place, and an experienced security team on both sides of the border; - Ongoing communication with actors operational across the operational area, and continued adherence to Acceptance Strategies built on community and stakeholder understanding of NGO X’s programming; - NGO X has a strong reconciliation system. Any suspected cases are reported by the WASH team directly to area management team and to SMT to open an investigation.
