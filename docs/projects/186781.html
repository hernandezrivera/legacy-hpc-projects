Project Summary

In the Afar region, the humanitarian situation deteriorated by multifaceted natural and man-made hazards such as erratic rainfall patterns and dry spells, ethnic conflict as well as Tigray war which spills over to Afar , degradation of rangeland (due to invasive species of Prosopis juliflora, outbreaks of human (including COVID-19 pandemic) and livestock diseases, invasion by various pests (Fall Armyworm and Desert Locusts) which all contributed towards to poor crop harvests and loss of plant and pastures, which then lead to low crop and livestock production as well as livelihood losses. VSF G collected and analyzed at ground level information with beneficiary communities and then the prioritization of these locations also informed by the currently IPC 3 & 4 priority locations based on the January IPC projections, IDP numbers, experienced droughts and areas with emergency needs as the lean season deepens. To contribute to the achievements of agriculture cluster objective one “protect and sustain core-agricultural livelihoods of climate and conflict affected households in Ethiopia through humanitarian interventions in 2022” and to enhance vulnerable households’ ability to withstand recurrent and future shocks in Ethiopia through early recovery and resilience interventions”, VSF Germany designed this project with key activities which includes: provision of veterinary services (voucher based treatment and vaccination); provision of supplementary animal feed to milking goats and provision of emergency seed pack (cereals, legumes, vegetables) and fodder seeds including farm tools. Moreover, this project has proposed restocking of assets for those lost their asset due to drought and multipurpose cash assistance to improve household income through unconditional cash transfer. Different Capacity building training to communities and government experts including awareness creation on COVID-19 control and prevention measures and mainstreaming of accountability to affected population and GBV. The project will give emphasis to beneficiaries such as female-headed households, people with disabilities, child-headed households, as well as IDPs. As indicated above the identified and proposed activities by the cluster and planned by VSF G are in line with the draft 2022 HRP which aims to address the needs of the most at risk and vulnerable populations by focusing on priority needs. The proposed action will ensure synergies/integration with ongoing project or resources being implemented internally and with other partners’ projects in the target woredas. In order to augment the impact of the projects, VSF-Germany links the proposed activities with nutrition, WASH and other livelihood interventions specifically during the targeting of beneficiary HHs. The integration/synergies will have positive impact on the quick recovery of HHs from the shock specifically HHs with cases of malnutrition and other related shocks. The project will contribute to protection of the environmental by training VSF G staffs, CAHWs, government and community where proper collection and disposal of all packing materials of drugs such as bottles, feeds and seeds after use. This action will be implemented for a period of nine months starting in Mid-April to May in the five woredas (Aba’ala, Erebeti, Berhale, Dalol and Kuneba of zone 2 (Kilbati rasu) of Afar region.

1. - Projects activities

VSF Germany designed this project with key activities which includes: provision of veterinary services (voucher based treatment and vaccination); provision of supplementary animal feed to milking goats and provision of emergency seed pack (cereals, legumes, vegetables) and fodder seeds including farm tools. Moreover, this project has proposed the replacement/restocking of assets for the HHs lost their asset due to drought; capacity building training to communities and government experts; awareness creation on COVID-19 control and prevention measures to all stakeholders impacted by project and mainstreaming of accountability to affected population/GBV during the course of the project implementation. Details of activities by categories are highlighted below: Output1: protection service provided to 20,000 vulnerable households-Conduct Voucher based trainings; design print and distribute veterinary voucher; refresher training to CAHWs and equip them with kits; provide logistics and technical support to vaccination campaign and monitoring of activities Output2: Livestock feeding to livestock owned by 5095HH through supplementary feeding and fodder production.-beneficiary selection; procure, transport and distribute animal feed and feeding materials; awareness training on animal feeding: procure and distribute fodder seeds and tools; conduct post distribution monitoring. Output 3:Restocking of core breeding animals (goats) to 1000 drought affected HHs to improve milk for children-facilitate beneficiary selection; procure and distribute breeding goats; provision of awareness training on feeding and husbandry practices; post distribution monitoring. Output4: Multipurpose cash assistance through unconditional cash transfer to 920HHs-facilitate beneficiary selection; conduct cash transfer using financial institution; conduct post distribution monitoring;Output 5:Agricultural input shortage mitigated to 290 Agro-pastoralists through supplying seeds and tools-facilitate beneficiary selection; procure , transport and distribute seeds and tools for agro-pastoralists; monitor, provide on job technical support during from planation until harvesting; implement COVID 19 awareness, prevention measures and control during project implementation; conduct final evaluation of the overall performance of the project
