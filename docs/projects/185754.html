Project Summary

"Reaching People in Need Bangladesh (RPN)’s education in emergencies (EiE) program started in 2018 and, based on the learning’s into the response, will continue in 2022 to support across the 4 objectives of the Cox's Bazar (CXB) Edu Sector's JRP, corroborating equitable access to inclusive, quality learning opportunities and community engagement for girls, boys and youth in camp At Ukhiya. In 2022, RPN will continue to support equitable access to culturally and age appropriate care, learning and development in a safe, protective and inclusive environment, spanning early childhood care and development (ECCD) to functional literacy, numeracy and life skills for older children, adolescents and youth. In camps, RPN will Improve to establish a community-based learning facilities (CBLF) with modality RPN has successfully engaged over 200 G & B as part of the 2020 JRP in different-sized facilities across different age groups, while ambitiously transitioning a portion of the learners into 03 LCs and piloting 2 two-storey LCs, conscious of the considerations of community acceptance and GoB approvals. Meanwhile, RPN will continue to support education in on its long experience with GoB across the camp, and collaboration in CXB over 2017, particularly in ECCD.
Structured trainings for learning facilitators in camps and teachers in HC will aim to strengthen the quality of teaching and learning process for G&B across the age spectrum targeted by CXB Edu Sector, with regular monitoring of quantifiable and qualitative progress to feed into regular mentoring and on-the-job coaching. PIB adopts a pairing modality of learning facilitators from camps with HC Mentors, bridging the distance between both communities through capacity building initiatives. HC Mentors and education officials will be trained to provide better mentoring and supervisory support to learning facilitators and teachers for inclusive, quality education.
RPN in purposefully aims to incorporate gender and inclusion considerations for inclusive quality education as an organizational priority. Across all interventions, PIB’s programming will continue to link with GoB relevant stakeholders and Edu Sector guidance.
