Project Summary

This project aims to provide health services directly and indirectly to the affected People, IDPs and host communities,
Considering the area fragmentation in Syria due to the conflict, and the scattered health facilities; the support of an ambulatory system will allow a maximization of the remaining functional health resources to continue providing life-saving services to the patients, including:
1. Increasing the coverage scope of medical evacuation using the ambulatory system.
2. Enhancing the efficient utilization of the referral system through applying TRIAGE to hospitals to emergency cases only.
3. Enhancing the response speed by linking the ambulances’ systems with a communication network that would contribute to a better use of existing resources and increased coordination.
4. empowering the health directorate in managing the referral system By sharing one communication network and the same operating room to avoid all types of duplication and overlapping
The project aims to support running of existing ambulatory referral system with 20 ambulances and 40 paramedic & 40 drivers.

Needs

According to HNO draft Report 2019 13.2 million people in need. 3.7 million male and 9.5 million female.The conflict in Syria resulted in extreme trauma and increased number of disabilities. the largely hampered health system was unable to deal with this drastic effect of the conflict not only due to destroyed infrastructure but also due to an acute reduction in health professionals either due to direct targeting or departure for fear of being targeted. This has led to a lack of knowledge and skills required for dealing with complicated cases by the remaining health providers; and an acute reduction in the scope covered by remaining ambulatory services and life-saving services and system. This lack of knowledge and skills in ambulatory services has been one of the leading cause of high mortality and morbidity rate among people, especially among women, children and the elderly. the intensive conflict inside Syria and increased numbers of injuries among civilians, especially complicated cases, requires high levels of coordination between ambulatory system and health facilities. according to the 2017 reports , the number of beneficiaries 12339 2430 trauma cases 28 % 5172 other medical 59 % 242 deliveries cases 3 % 946 new born services 11% The needs assessment is done with Engagement of local health directories in Idleb governorates, the project is highly needed and serving marginalized community with limited access.to health especially after the last influx of IDPs to Idleb governorate According to the information of health directorates in the North of Syria, still there are a gap in covering the needs of the referral system in the governorates of Idleb and Hama so this project will serve the non covered areas with the ambulatory services based on the a working plan from Idleb health directorate The importance of our project, which will provide professional service in the ambulances through a highly trained staff and through cars which are equipped with specialized medical devices to be able to address the critical and traumatic cases.
