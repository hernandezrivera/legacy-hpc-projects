Needs

Continued displacement, destruction of homes and properties has led to compelling shelter needs across Borno, Adamawa and Yobe state (BAY States). Sectoral need assessments and vulnerabilities affirms that nearly 3.5 M individuals will require shelter and settlement related assistance annually with 1.8 M number targeted in 2019 through 2022. This comprises of 1.3 M IDPs’, 916,000 people living in host communities, 822,927 hard-to-reach people and 407,000 returnees in need of both Shelter and NFI support. Since beginning of the crises, the impression on shelter needs has tremendously evolved complexed further by the various trends of displacement, rate of influx from hard-to reach areas and populations returning to their original places of displacement. While the ongoing military operation in hard-to-reach areas continues to lead to new displacement, majority of the displaced population across the BAY states fled their ancestral homes before 2016. The transitional shelter needs of returnee population is quite significant as vast majority of these population dwell in partially damaged houses and ill-suited condition. Factors such as paucity of capital and means of sustainable livelihood to procure building materials frustrates opportunities for stimulating early recovery in return areas. Essential household and kitchen items such as NFI remains the second most reported need for both IDPs’ in camps or in camp-like settings and host community across north eastern Nigeria. The high NFI need is mainly as a result of the absence of systematic NFI targeting of new arrivals due to lack of propositioned and stockpiled items to cater for sudden emergencies in key receiving areas. Assessments indicates that the main NFI items requested by the affected population includes mosquito nets, plastic mat and kitchen sets.
