Project Summary

According to OCHA Sudan office, River Nile state hosting the greatest number of IDPs affected by the fighting broke out in Sudan between RSF and SAF in mid of April 2023. Overall Sudan has been affected the people flee their homes seeking safety as displace people and as refugees to neighboring states. The IDPs settled in 27 gathering places such as schools health clinics universities campus or clubs. In August 2023 UNICEF and RN state government related authorities conducted assessment trips to identify the humanitarian gap at those gathering sites and the trip came up with a response plan at that time.
Limit humanitarian assistance had been provided to the IDPs by local actors and initiatives and therefore, an inter-sector assessment mission to Shendi was suggested to further assessment of the situation and update.
On 26 -27 October 2023, An inter-sector assessment mission to Shendi locality took place. The mission composed of the following Actors: OCHA, UNICEF, UNDP, NPO, HAC and government local authorities. Again in Dec 2024 the conflict expanded to Gazira state which led to people displacement to River Nile state according to HAC River Nile the number of IDPs was doubled.
This project aim to improve access to lifesaving WASH services for IDPs and host community through upgrading and/or operation of WASH existing facilities, capacity building of humanitarian and government actors and empowerment of targeted communities.

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

During implementing this WASH project NPO will take all kinds of design consideration especially in the construction of sanitation facilities, latrines so as not to cause any kind of risk to women, girls, boys and people with special needs. NPO will construct more number of sanitation facilities keeping reasonable distance from the IDP families, this way will minimize the risk that women and girls potentially face during night time while walking to sanitation facilities, and also will enable children to access latrines at night in close vicinity of the house. In addition awareness raising sessions on gender based violence will be conducted for women, men, boys and girls. While implementing the hygiene promotion sessions, the community (especially women) will be given awareness on HIV/AIDS to increase their on the risks that might result from SGBV if no timely treatment was sought.
