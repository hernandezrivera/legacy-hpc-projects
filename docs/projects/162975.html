Project Summary

Considering the economic challenges nowadays in Sudan ,especially in SD and SK and based on the need assessment done by Gayat and El Ruhama as consortium field staff in the targeted areas mention above the assessment reveal women and girls experienced gender based violence (GBV), The term GBV is used to underscore how systemic gender inequality acts as a foundational characteristic of most forms of violence perpetrated against women and girls in the targeted areas. The term is a reflection of unequal power relations and that violence is often used to maintain and reinforce gender inequalities including rape .sexual assault, intimate partner violence, sexual harassment, trafficking, and sexual exploitation and abuse of girls, early married, FGM this specific considering violence is socially ascribed intended to control female body and live them in subordinate position , moreover, women and girls are economically and socially vulnerable and have a higher incidence of poverty, they lack job-based skills and means of production which increases their vulnerability to GBV; it more recognizable that returnees, and Host communities in South Darfur state mainly at Bielel and El Radoom localities and in South Kordofan mainly at Dilling and Kadogoli still have challenges in accessing services of SGBV survivors, Survivors of S/GBV are not support properly . There is a need to offer more support to these vulnerable targeted communities and the social workers and services provider need more training including Psychological First Aid (PFA), there is also demand for formation of SGBV protection mechanism . this project aims to ensure that the survivors have access to quality specialized GBV services and measures are in place to prevent and mitigate risks of GBV. Elruhama and Gayat will implement this project in close collaboration and partnership with key stakeholders like local community CBOs, HAC, locality leaders in particular the administrative leaders , popular committees and other effective key persons and line miniseries. This project will use a community based approach to reach the protection needs of the most vulnerable population especially women, and girls ,its planned activities will targeted 5200 vulnerable in South Darfur and South Kordofan states a total of 2600 per each state , the intervention will be done with comprehensive need assessment to a void overlapping and duplication .The activity foster to enhance protection services to most vulnerable women in the above targeted areas through establishment/ rehabilitate of women centers with local materials to serve multi- functional purposes such as strength protection committees , provision of in –kind supports for women , strengthening the capacity of the services provider , social worker and local CBOs ,provision of protection services and motivate the community protection networks through community engagement and capacity building along with related line ministry to insure the quality of the service delivery to vulnerable / survivor women and for project sustainability..

6.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

the intervention is completely focus on gender equality through which advocating in all forms of gender based violence is mandatory to the all project activities The protection mainstreaming as the process of incorporating protection principles and promoting meaningful access, safety and dignity for survivors ,to ensure that a protection is incorporated into operations”. Practically, “it is a way of designing and implementing all programmed so that protection risks and potential violations are taken into consideration. It is a need to understand who is at risk, from what or whom as well as why, and the consequences their actions or inaction may have on the threats people experience and their vulnerability and capacity in relation to these threats. This includes knowing how and where to refer vulnerable in need for specialist support to prevent or recover from violence and exploitation, as well as understanding when, how, and to whom to refer specialized protection issues.
