Needs

Kutum is witnessing protracted displacement since 2011, where WASH gab are highly critical, widespread & urgent, especially due to ‎ security deterioration in month of February 2016 serious conflict erupted in Jabal Mara. In Wash the highest priority of the new IDPs is that the WASH is poor & immediate water supply & sanitation are required. In Kutum water consumption is 5.8l/p/d, (Umlayona, Hasahba . The ‎situation of sanitation services is severely deteriorated. Dignity & gender sensitivity in emergency community latrines are rarely ‎considered. According to recent data obtained from WES (Feb 2019) there is a dire WASH needs including lack of water resources, need for water filtration,and sanitation facilities, where there is no latrines so it is an area of need. It was stated that there is a need to repair one water yard in hashaba village, and rehabilitate 6 hand pumps in um layona. On the other hand, BN is a host for IDPs, and vulnerable residents where water consumption is 3.8l/p/d. Ongoing conflict in BNS since 2011, accelerated in the first quarter of 2015. The displacement is primarily from parts of Bau, Ge Kurmuk localities due to conflict between SAF and SPLM-N. Newly displaced IDPs have moved to more secure locations, predominately Damazine, Rosaries, Giessen, and Bau localities. According to State Water Corporation (SWC)/(WES)/Nov/2018 El Kurmuk locality have a total of 632,400 people not accessing adequate sanitation services, and having a significant gaps in pit latrines coverage. There are huge WASH gaps among vulnerable IDPs and host communities showing that 80% of the targeted population practice open defecation. There are no active plans to reduce open defecation, there is lack of monitoring and follow-up plan on CATS/CLTS, no qualified trained TOT hygiene promoters, limited access to safe drinking water and no trained mason on design, plan and construction of latrines slabs. Founded on a sanitation unit capacity analysis done by UNICEF in 2018; there are 4 established sanitation and hygiene units in BN and 2 in ND none of them has the minimum number of required staff (7 staff). Consequently open defecation is a major factor to a multiplicity of water sanitation related diseases such as diarrhea, cholera and typhoid, Not only is open defecation & lack of improved sanitation facilities detrimental to human health but also to economic and social development. . And regarding access to sanitation facilities only 33% are using improved latrines. in BN there is a total of 1,676 hand pumps, 157 water yards, 5 treatment plants, and 1966 BASIC water facilities, out of which 41%, 22%, 67%, 40%, and 45% are respectively functioning (WASH 2018-2021 Strategic Plan, Nov 2018). Both localities will be needing a CLTS approach to be injected within the intervention.

Activities

-rehabilitation of 6 hand pumps, in Kutum/Um layoona , and construction of 3 new HPs in BN/Alkurmuk, Jorot west, and bulang villages, rehabilitate 2 water yards one in hahsba/ND, and the other is in BN, besides upgrading of 2 hafirs in Bulang as recommended by WES. the sanitation component will mainly target schools, and health centers, by constructing a 3 drop hole latrines in the centers, and a 4 drop holes gender sensitive latrines in abubakr alsideeg, and khadija bit khuwalid schools, with an attached hand washing devices. the project will promote the hygiene practices through formation of new school hygiene clubs, and strengthening the already existing ones, cleaning campaigns for schools and the communities,distribution of soap, and sanitary kits for girls at schools, and women at reproductive age. FPDO will adapt CLTS approach in both localities of alkurmuk and kutum targeting 6 villages /communities. 60 hygiene promoters will be trained to undertake the house visits, and the hygiene promotion sessions (40 in ND, and 20 in BN). as an exit strategy and to grantee the sustainability of the provided services or construction/rehabilitation hand pups, and water yards FPDO will train 10 persons from each community on managing the water yards, and hand pumps (O & M).FPDO will implemented all the above mentioned activities is close coordination with WES, MOH, and the targeted communities, as it will also consider the gender, and environmental factors during all project's phases.
