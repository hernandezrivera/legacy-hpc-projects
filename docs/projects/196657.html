Project Summary

The interventions include awareness sessions on the most important topics related to protection and psychosocial support, which aim to raise the level of awareness, which is reflected in self-esteem, understanding of the environment, and modifying behaviors that help in greater adaptation and thus higher productivity. Individual sessions for individuals who need help at the individual level and who have psychological disorders.
Psychosocial support sessions (PSS) for children and adolescents in the area.
Establishing committees of young people to form strengths in the community that this group can refer to even after the end of the project.
Community initiatives projects, which are based on providing community services that stem from the needs of individuals and are implemented by themselves (which ensures continuity of service), with logistical support from the organization.
A cash assistance service that helps the most vulnerable families released, according to specific criteria, to secure their urgent needs, which involve reducing a specific risk.
Services are provided to all segments of society (children - males and females - men and women who are caregivers and members of society in general).
AVSI is already operating in the area of East Ghouta, and Rural Damascus and access is provided through the partner SARC.

3 - Needs

According to the HNO-2022 https://reliefweb.int/report/syrian-arab-republic/2022-humanitarian-needs-overview-syrian-arab-republic-february-2022The severity scale in Daraya, the area in which it is planned to intervene, is 4, PIN 42,828 IDPs 2,84, returnees 3,885. The project is planned in a way that addresses the strengths of the community (individuals' core resources, groups, individual skills, general awareness, experiences, etc.), which will be known through focus groups and community committees.Then the collective needs are addressed (which are related to the needs of the community as a whole and its results are also reflected in the community as a whole on the individuals) and this is represented in the axis of community initiatives that are based on meeting the societal needs expressed by the members of the community itself and which are implemented in cooperation with the members of the community themselves.Also, individual needs will be addressed through the focus of individual psychological counseling sessions. Psychological needs will also be met, and protection risks will be addressed through psychosocial support activities.

4 - Implementation of activities

Focused discussion sessions will be held with groups that form samples from the local community to identify needs and risks, and then form community committees that will be the link between the organization and the community.The project will be implemented within 4 axes: the first is group sessions for women caregivers on the most important topics they deem important to discuss,The second: group sessions for male and female caregivers to formulate community initiatives, which are based on raising one of the most dangerous problems in society, for example, the presence of a dark road in the area where girls are exposed to harassment or children are exposed to kidnapping. The organization provides the community committee with the logistics they need. And they play the role of the labor force according to their skills, so that the road is illuminated, for example, with solar panels, which limits this danger, and since they themselves are the implementers of the initiative, this will ensure the continuity of the assistance provided, as well as the effective participation of members of the local community.The third is psychosocial support activities for children, boys and girls. The fourth is individual psychological counseling sessions for individuals from the age of 4 to 50

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

One of the main risks is the presence of unsafe places for children and community members, and to mitigate these risks, one of the first goals will be to implement a community initiative that meets the needs of community members, including children, and that reduces the risks involved in those needs.Violence and bullying can also be a threat to the correct development of FE and NFE being promoted which is why specific training sessions (PSS/CP, Inclusion) are planned for volunteers on the partner side, psychological support is given to children and constant monitoring and feedback will be put in place. A mechanism for submitting complaints.In order to ensure a safe space for children and adolescents, psychosocial support sessions will be provided for children and adolescents, in addition to psychosocial support sessions and protection based on social violence for adolescent girls and women.In order to avoid the risk of acts that carry gender discrimination, such as depriving girls of education, it was planned to confront this risk by holding awareness-raising sessions within the community to reduce this risk. Avoid discriminatory abuse that is associated with specific gender and disability risks in terms of participation or harassment. In fact, participation can be hidden due to parents' lack of consent, following regular schools, or not adapting activities to their needs. 8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Child protection policies and PSEA policies will be put in place. In particular, at the start of the project, a specific child protection risk assessment form will be developed to mitigate all risks related to adults dealing with children and to avoid unnecessary direct contacts between adults and children.AVSI ensures that all staff and volunteers involved in this program are aware of the Secretary General's Special Procedures for Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) Bulletin, child safeguarding policies, and internal reporting mechanisms for CP and SEA cases, through mandatory annual training sessions and signing of the Code of Conduct and Bulletin Secretary General.AVSI has worked to strengthen internal Minimum Standards for Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse by participating in trainings provided by UNICEF in 2020. For this project, AVSI will enhance field monitoring, outreach activities, presence and work of case managers (including reporting and referrals), and awareness among staff and beneficiaries and local populations in relation to protection from sexual exploitation and abuse, and risk limits.


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

AVSI promotes cooperation with the local population through the formation of focal committees, thus ensuring that it is available locally even in the event of curfew. Furthermore, excellent partner cooperation and coordination and relationships with local authorities allow AVSI to further reduce these risks, with the possibility of requesting special access permits and/or monitoring mission even in the event of curfew.The risks of contracting Covid19 virus for both staff and beneficiaries, which could prevent the implementation of activities, are mitigated by adopting AVSI measures related to the COVID pandemic including the use of PEE, social distancing, early warning system, limited number of children in activities, etc.There are other risks related to the arrival of money to Syria, high inflation and exchange rate volatility. AVSI has already put in place mitigation measures such as changing the bank at the main country level to send money to Syria, expedited procurement schemes, additional oversight of the procurement process by the Regional Operations Manager and expedited payment procedures to avoid any delays in procurement.Moreover, it has increased domestic emergency funds in both Syrian pounds and US dollars to balance out any fluctuations. The risk of fraud or corruption is mitigated through internal procedures (DIGE 8/2020) that set high standards of accountability, transparency and regulatory compliance.
