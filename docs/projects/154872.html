Needs

According to the Round 2 SMART survey, Global Acute Malnutrition(GAM) stands at 12% in the makeshift camps. Though slightly less than the 15% global threshold for critical emergency levels, aggravating factors such as poor IYCF practices, overcrowding, poor health and sanitation environments as well as a poorly diversified diet indicate that nutrition needs remain high and therefore efforts should be emphasized to ensure that positive gains observed into the 2018 JRP are maintained or improved
