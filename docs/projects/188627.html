Project Summary

In line with the UN Education Cluster objectives, 2022 Humanitarian Response Plan (HRP) strategic objectives (SO) and AKDN’s Global and in-country frameworks, the proposed project addresses basic humanitarian needs at educational facilities to ensure a safe, secure, and enabling environment for the students and teachers at school levels. The objectives of the proposed project are a) Improved physical facilities (WASH) at schools to enhance access to schools b) Prepare schools for any emergency response by providing them training and school safety kits c) contribute to improved eco-friendly environment at schools to foster learning and school attendance. The project will be implemented in 30 selected schools of Salamiyeh District in Syria. The proposed response project encompasses number of activities contribute to improved physical environments at schools with provision of basic humanitarian needs contributing to enabling learning environment for students and teachers. These includes provision of access to improved WASH facilities, improving eco-environment and promoting school safety at school levels. The proposed activities considering the short and longer-term needs of the vulnerable schools in Salamiyeh District in Syria. Following are the outcomes:
Outcome 1: Improved physical environment at schools to promote learning and access to schools by improving WASH facilities: Under this outcome, schools will receive access to solarized power to ensure water available at schools for domestic uses. Also, WASH facilities in the targeted schools will be rehabilitated and maintained. Access of different abled students and gender considerations will be also ensured.
Outcome 2: Improved natural environment through plantation around the schools and cleaning drives. Under this outcome, students and teachers will be engaged in different awareness sessions focused on environment and climate change. Also, plantation and cleanliness drive at school levels will be encouraged by engaging students in different activities at school levels.
Outcome 3: Enhanced disaster and crisis response capacities at schools through school safety sessions and incentive kits. Under this outcome school safety sessions including session on first aid, fire safety and disaster management will be arranged for teachers and students. Also, school safety plan will be developed and school safety kits including fire and first kits will be provided to each school.
The interventions will be implemented in selected 30 schools of Salamiyeh benefiting over 6000 (3100 M, 2900F) students and teachers. Aga Khan Agency for Habitat (AKAH) in Syria has carried number of similar activities in different schools in Syria and gained very positive results including increase in school attendance and improved learning environment.

4 - Needs

In line with the UN Education Cluster objectives, 2022 Humanitarian Response Plan (HRP) strategic objectives (SO) and AKDN’s Global and in-country frameworks, it is important to ensure a safe, secure, and enabling environment at school levels. Major issue, highlighted in the acritical in the post conflict situation was the poor physical conditions including access to WASH at school levels. Likewise, as per Education Sector Findings 2022 Humanitarian Needs Overview 88% children going to school were dissatisfied with the WASH in their schools. The ratio for Hama governate is about 75%. As per the assessment by AKAH, in Salmyieh District, there are over 200 schools and are connected to the public water network, and it is under the rationing system when schools are receiving public water for 12 hours every 20 days. Local water resources are insufficient to meet daily demand due to severe fuel shortage and the recurrent electricity disruptions. As a result, the schools are depending mainly on purchasing water from private water trucks. Similarly, schools are also lacking appropriate hygienic practices and is badly impacting the safe physical environment at school levels. Additionally, as per the school principals, there is challenges in operating computers, printers etc.) without electricity. A large part of eastern Salamiyeh countryside was exposed to many explosive munitions.Many schools are missing the fence when the playgrounds are connecting directly to contaminated lands.

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

In Salamiyeh district, the risk of ERWs is considered high. The involved WASH staff and the local-communities committees will be trained on the mitigation measures along with the implementation of AKAH-supported MRE program in the area for raising the awareness among the beneficiaries. 11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

A. Instability of the market prices and the sharp changes within short time, affect the implementation of the activities. The donor will be constantly consulted and informed on the situation and potential solutions. B. Lack of specialized workers to do the installation in the targeted sites. AKAH currently – in cooperation with AKF- is implementing R2R project targeting agriculture areas with solar system to improve the irrigation systems, the works are managed by technical local teams trained. Same modality can be utilized for this project. C. schools (students and principals) are not fully cooperative with the committees. AKAH will provide a capacity building to the committees’ members on behavior change related communication at school level and will rely on the existing structures to raise the communities’ awareness on the ownership and the participation aspects. D. The installed systems exposed to damage due to human or natural reasons. AKAH is considered in the design of the systems, to provide protection metal fence.
