Project Summary

The intensification of the crisis in the Northwest and Southwest Regions of Cameroon has led to the displacement of thousands of people. Majority of this people are found in the bushes and some of them have been moved to semi urban and urban towns. Kumba stands out as the highest host community in the two Regions with more than 60,000 IDPs. Majority of these displaced persons have lost their means of subsistence and have limited access to livelihood. Community Household hosting these IDPs are now facing difficulties in providing the necessary minimum requirements for their families, particularly that both the IDPs and host community Households have extensively relied on their diminishing savings since agricultural activities which is the backbone of the SWR and Meme in particular have drastically reduced due to the socio-political crisis.
As IDP women, youths and people with disabilities are using all forms of coping mechanisms for survival, Majority of them have adopted negative ways such as theft, prostitution, eating less, selling goods, removing children out of school as a means of survival.
This project therefore seeks to enhance the socio-economic development of IDP women, youths, and people with disabilities, while promoting gender mainstreaming as well as building capacity of vulnerable small holder farmers who have been displaced due to the crisis. The project aims to be implemented in Kumba I Subdivision, Meme Division in the South West Region of Cameroon.
The global objective of the project is to encourage livelihood alternatives, empower displaced female and youth headed households, People with Disabilities and other vulnerable groups through capacity building on Climate Smart Urban Agriculture (CSUA), food transformation and small businesses with limited capital. To achieve this objective the following specific objectives will be taken into consideration:
i) Carry out a rapid assessment on the alternative livelihoods activities IDPs in Kumba have adopted as coping mechanisms.
ii) Create awareness and experience sharing amongst targeted groups on Climate Change and Climate Smart Urban Agriculture
iii) Build capacity for income generating activities of 450 participants, comprising of 350 IDPs identified during the rapid assessment on CSUA, production, transformation, and commercialization of diverse food products.
iv) Carry out livelihoods support for 250 households, 80% being IDPs through the distribution of Cash Assistance per household as a start-up capital
For over 7 years, AMEF has stick to its vision of ensuring that the rights of children and other vulnerable groups are protected and the socio-economic wellbeing of women, youths and People living with Disabilities in local communities improved through a participatory approach. The Women/Youth Economic Empowerment program of AMEF has been actively running activities such as building capacities of women and youths on livelihood activities, transformation of agricultural produce, life-skills acquisition, and production of organic fertilizer, urban agriculture and poultry farming and recently the establishment of AMEF Cooperative. AMEF has trained agricultural personnel’s in the domain of integrating climate smart agricultural practices to boost yields as well as preserve the environment. Over the years over 300 individuals have benefited from this arm of AMEF.
AMEF works with a team of dedicated staff with special expertise in economic recovery, finance, protection, and management. AMEF has impacted the lives of many vulnerable women, youths and disables through their partnership with International NGOs such as WFP, NRC, DRC, UNICEF, UNESCO, and IRC.
This project will be an opportunity for AMEF to address sustainable goal number 1, 2, and 13 in Kumba 1 subdivision community. It targets 450 Persons (350 Female, 100 Male) including 350 IDPs and 50 persons with disabilities that will benefit directly and 2700 persons (1900 Female, 800 Male) benefit indirectly.
