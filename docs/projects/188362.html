Project Summary

This project targets IDPs, community groups and enterprises founded in Idleb and Aleppo governorate as Al-Bab, Azaz, Afrin sub-districts in Aleppo and Idleb, MaraatTamasrin, and Dana sub-districts in Idleb . The project’s main objective is to strengthening the resilience of 4000 people (M:2200, W: 1800) including 100 PWDs against exacerbating living conditions related to both conflicts and floods in where economic deterioration and its effects such as income loss, reduced purchasing power are severely experienced. Through the provision of livelihood opportunities consisting of integrated interventions, the project aims to support ERL efforts and community revitalization; It will contribute to rehabilitation and restoration of disrupted livelihoods, providing skills, income opportunities through three main interventions.
By pursuing the main objective referred above, the project aims to achieve the following outcomes:
1. Local small and home-based enterprises and related activities are built up with financial, in-kind supports and restorations
2. Access to given term livelihoods opportunities involving CFW both in restoration works
3. Support livelihood& empowerment embedded Local small businesses and entrepreneurs
4. Community initiatives with focusing on elderly.
MWL engage with target communities throughout the project design, implementation, and monitoring, to ensure that all interventions are meaningful, relevant, and community-driven. Beneficiaries (both IDPs and host communities) are engaged at each step in the intervention and information is shared freely and openly through direct consultations with a variety of actors, including men, women, youth, local producers and traders through both qualitative interviews.
Both IDPs and host communities members in particular HHs headed by a child, a female member and/or PWD, will be prioritized as beneficiaries for CFW. Enterprises of which facilities-activities are affected by floods in medium and high level will be prioritized for financial and in-kind supports. Both to ensure all potential beneficiaries have equal and non-discrimination access to the interventions of the project. MWL will follow Do No Harm approach through coordination meetings with related stakeholders such local councils, Local market owners, enterprises before submitting the proposal, identifying the locations with access issues that are being targeted under this proposal. In addition to, cash working group to follows the Cash for Work (CfW) Minimum Standard guidance (final draft version) to ensure the harmonize and do-no harm within implementing the CFW activity.
Furthermore, a regular channel of communication with ERL, SCHF will be established for information sharing on developments and possible emerging issues to market stability or market distortion / markets volatility. Also, an articulated coordination with Shelter, and WASH clusters will ensure the promotion of access to livelihoods activities that would contribute to the supply of health, shelter and WASH related material/services including through start-up/support to businesses and related employment opportunities.

4 - Needs

In NWS, people living in IDPs sites reached 1.5 million . In 2021, heavy rainfall and flooding further increased the needs of communities where CCCM Cluster reported, more than152,700 IDPs were affected, with 27,700 tents destroyed or damaged.The cluster estimates that 65% of HHs reporting insufficient income in Aleppo and 80% in Idleb. According to HNAP in January 2021; %98 of individuals is in extreme poverty, %83of HHs reported their income is insufficient , %66 of HH suffer lack of employment opportunities. MWL conducted RNA in Dana sub-district using 3 main methodologies:Personal Interviews with Key informants (KII), & FGDs on voluntary basis. MWL targeted23 camps and their surrounding communities (16). Based on RNA, 76% of adults are unemployed and75% of the families earn less 100,000 SYR monthly. 80% of KI said people can only afford about less than 25% of their basic needs. Recent floods and pandemic affected the local manufacturing where 39% suffered from reduction in profits,26% experience difficulties in securing raw materials, 23% are unable to sell products, &21% had to shut down their businesses. In addition, 81% of participants have ideas for micro, small, and home-based enterprises. Based on RNA, report show facilities that need rehabilitations such centeral markets, mills, slaughterhouses, local shops. In terms of entrepreneurial , 93% of enterprises owners said that will receive trainees and in need support in 41% logistics.93% said training not available.

5 - Implementation of activities

The project will address the needs through an interconnected and integrated set of activities, as below.-CfW activities for local population and IDPs, which will contribute to the rehabilitation of essential socioeconomic infrastructures and services, and thereby also promote social cohesion between IDP and local populations as a step towards recovery. - Rehabilitate community and public infrastructures, destroyed markets, mills, slaughterhouses as a means to help recover the local economy/businesses which also paves way for sustainable supply of livelihood opportunities for local communities. - Provide for technical/entrepreneurial capacity-building and small grants to businesses, both startups and existing ones, to help them initiate and restore their livelihoods. The lack of livelihoods is one of the biggest challenges in northwest Syria.The proposal aims at strengthening the purchasing power of individuals by tailor-made set of activities defined by the needs and specific cities of each population group, focusing on vulnerable IDPs and host communities to enhance both affordability &access to NFI/health/water related goods &services.The project will focus on supporting small businesses and beneficiaries’ economic empowerment, that have been affected by the conflict, COVID-19 crisis &recent floods.- Conduct comprehensive labor market assessment to identify the needs, demands, and gaps of careers, and vocations to avoid the increased demand and potential impact on market
