Project Summary

District Sujawal is one the locust infestation and rain flood affected district of Sindh. COVID-19 pandemic and lockdown further increased the vulnerability of the locals. GAM ratio is also very high and children/PLWs facing malnutrition. Lack of water, sanitation and hygiene facilities increase the vulnerability of targeted communities. Lack of WASH services at targeted health facilities is the major challenge for the district administration to cope with the COVID-19 and rain flood emergency situation and this is also compromising the primary health care facilities. CIP believes in the inclusive and evidence-based approach. For this, CIP has collected primary and secondary data from affected communities, district administration and also utilized the data from Rapid need assessment report (RNA 1) collected by UN agencies during the rain flood. CIP designed all the activities based on the finding of the RNA report and secondary data from district admiration. Drought/locust and COVID-19 affected population, targeted population have lack of water & sanitation facilities and awareness about the hygienic practices during the COVID-19 pandemic. Targeted community is exposed to the CIVID-19 threat due to a lack of awareness and WASH facilities at targeted health facility/targeted areas. Moreover, targeted district is the desert locust affected for last couple of years. Due to week WASH infrastructure community is vulnerable and needs humanitarian assistance. Recent rains in the month of Aug 2020 also badly affected the already vulnerable communities.
The proposed interventions will includes the rehabilitation / installation of hand pumps in communities, rehabilitation of toilets at health facilities and schools, installation of handwashing stations at health facilities, schools and public places, formation and capacity building of village WASH committees, hygiene sessions in communities, formation of WASH clubs at schools, hygiene sessions at schools with teachers and students, Capacity building of key front line workers on measures related to COVID-19 and C-19 IPC via radio and face-to-face sessions and distribute IEC materials with government of Pakistan and WHO approved messages. CARE will finalize beneficiary identification criteria and registration through community mobilization and engagement. In total 50,000 affected individuals will be reached.
The proposed project intervention are directly aligned with the sectoral guideline and need of the targeted communities. CARE will ensure compliance of Gender Marker and SPHERE standers. CIP will also follow the guideline/SoP set by the Government of Pakistan and WHO in implementation of all the activities.

1- Project Activities

Activity 1.1.1: Formation of 30 Village WASH Committees and training on O&M at the village or community level. Village WASH Committees (VWC) will be structured by the CIP program implementing staff with community consensus in each village containing 11-15 members with equal participation of women to the carry-out different development program, i.e. water, sanitation and health etc. The VWC will provide implementation support by improving participation to field implementation teams.Activity 1.2.1: Rehabilitation of 50 hand pumps at community level: After the baseline study, CIP project team will identify the non-functional hand pumps in all targeted UCs. Selected nonfunctional hand pumps will be rehabilitated in the proposed project. Hand pumps with minor repair or major repair work will be rehabilitated. CIP team will compliance with the SPHERE standards, Protection laws and WASH standards and technical guidelines, and comply with DRR aspects of rehabilitation.Activity 1.3.1: 60 Sessions on water treatment at the household level, water quality, and waterborne disease will be conducted to the male and female community of targeted villages. Total 30 session will be conducted at village level. 15 participants per session is designed due to COVID-19. CIP team will implement all the set standard by WHO and SoPs by Government to prevent and break the chain of COVID-19. PPEs will also be provided to all project staff and face masks to participants.Activity 1.4.1: 60 Hygiene Sessions at village level: Health and hygiene session will be conducted to the male and female community of targeted villages. Female sessions also cover the MHM topic for better MHM understanding of adolescent girls and women. Total 20 participants per session is designed due to COVID-19. Following will be the important topics of the proposed activity • General mobilization on rights & responsibilities as a community member • Three Pile Sorting • Stories about germs • F-diagram • Importance of hand washing and how and when to do this practice • General cleanness • Personal hygiene • Water Bourne diseases • Proper use of latrines • Waste management • ORS/SSS demonstration • Hand washing demonstration • Disadvantages of open defecation • Ways of treating water • COVID-19 mitigation measures. • MHM for adolescent and women.Activity 1.5.1: Distribution of 2500 Hygiene & MHM items and jerry cans: CIP field team will set the selection criteria for the distribution of Hygiene kit with MHM item and jerry cans to adolescent girls and women.Activity 1.6.1: Promotion of critical WASH-related information using mass media, leaflets, posters and radio broadcast.Activity 2.1.1: WASH Club Formation: The project team will establish 15 School WASH Clubs involving children to learn basic themes and skills to maintain health and hygiene at school and home.Activity 2.2.1: 30 Hygiene sessions at schools with teachers and students on C-19, WASH and DRR.Activity 2.3.1: Rehabilitation of Latrines in 10 Schools: CIP project team will identify the non-functional latrines in targeted schools. Selected nonfunctional latrines will be rehabilitated in the proposed project. This includes the repair of WC, doors, plumbing work, electric work, improvement of the septic tank and painting work.Activity 2.4.1: Installation of handwashing stations at 10 schools.Activity 2.5.1: Installation / Rehabilitation of latrines and handwashing stations at 3 public health facilities.Activity 3.1.1: 3 Trainings for frontline and sanitary workers on measures related to COVID-19. Activity 3.2.1: Installation of 30 handwashing stations at public places i.e. NADRA office, DC Office, bus stands etc.Activity 3.3.1: Ensuring access to preventive measures against COVID-19 through community outreach and mass communication aligned with WHO’s global guidance and GHRP.
