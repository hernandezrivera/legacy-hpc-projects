Project Summary

Tell Abiad, is located to north of Ar Raqqa city and was recently controlled by opposition armed groups supported by Turkish army. Town was militarily controlled by multiple parties, including Islamic State IS, Syrian Democratic Forces SDF, and Syrian Opposition Groups SOGs.
Due to military operation in the area, many of the resident population fled their communities. Nevertheless, after the end of the conflicts in the regions, the local people started to return to their communities. Recent reports declared that more than 300 households came back to Tell Abiad from Turkey through Turkish cross-border gate. Latest feedback refers to a total of 25,000 population; 7000 of them are IDPs coming from surrounded areas.
The responsible committee of the Water Hygiene and Sanitation – Tell Abiad local council started to conduct check up on the existing water stations to assess their technical situation. Two water stations provide the city with water. The first one is the old water station, which consists of three pumps; each is linked to one well. The capacity of the three wells is 100 m3/h, 75 m3/h, and 75 m3/h. This station provides the city with 80% of the total need for water. The new station is the new one that consists of 1 pump related to a well of 100 m3/h capacity. The new station provides the city with 20% of its total need for water. Nevertheless, the two stations are currently damaged and in need of immediate rehabilitation for the submersible pump, electric transforming panel, generator, and the control panels. Additionally, water pipelines must be maintained. Furthermore, the challenge of obtaining the financial resources to operate the stations still exists. The quality of water is compromised due to the lack of quality assurance procedures. In general, the two stations are not 100% functional, which leads to a gap regarding the smooth and safe access to water in Tell Abiad city. In other words, the core problem that must be addressed is the miserable situation of the two water stations in Tell Abiad city.
Assessment of the 2 water stations has shown the follows:
Station : Old station, No of wells: 3 well, Wells capacity: 100m3/h, 75m3/h and 75 m3/h, Number of high tanks: 2, High tanks capacity:, 200 m3 and 100 m3, Operating through generator, fuel consumption /h: 150 L/h, Pumping across network: Flowing.
Station: New station, No of wells: 1 well, capacity: 100 m3/h, No of high tanks: 1, capacity: 300 m3, Operating through generator, fuel consumption: 70 L/h, Pumping across network: Flowing.
The project aims to improve safe access of the target population to drinkable water. This aim will be reached by rehabilitating the water stations in Tell Abiad city to address the core problem that was presented in the previous paragraph. The project will last for 10 months and will start on 01/03/2020.
The primary approach of this project is delivering development services to the affected population in Tell Abiad city through the rehabilitation of 2 water stations along with raising-awareness sessions.
The project consists of 2 phases; phase 1 will last for 4 months and throughout which the maintenance and rehabilitation activities will be fulfilled. phase 2 will last for 6 months, and it will include the provision of operation resources to function the two water stations.
The project will serve more than 25000 population and will prioritize the most vulnerable groups in the target communities. The cross-cutting issues like gender, accountability to affected people, and exit strategy will be taken into account in this project. WHOSD will coordinate with other humanitarian actors and stakeholders in the area in order to integrate the proposed WASH services with other humanitarian services.
WHOSD has guaranteed access to the target area through its team in Tell Abiad. WHOSD was part of last meeting with the Turkish governor of Sanliurfa city in which the Turkish authorities guaranteed access to Tell Abiad for humanitarian purposes.

4 - Needs

TThe total population is about 25000, out of them, there are 7000 IDPs. The town was controlled recently by the Syria Opposition armed Groups (The National Syrian Army) supported by the Turkish military forces after SDF controlled it in 2017. Nowadays, the city is considered to be relatively stable and secure compared to other areas in the north of Syria. The Turkish Government announced that the cross-border gate between Akcakale city in the south of Turkey and Tell Abiad in northern Syria was opened for humanitarian organizations based in Turkey and for Syrian refugees willing to return to their communities in Tell Abiad. From a humanitarian perspective, the situation is deteriorated in terms of available services, especially the prioritized ones. Acoording to a recent needs assessment carried out by WHOSD in Tell Abiad; the prioritized services nowadays are related to access to health services, availability of food, and availability of drinkable water which in turn led to undesired behaviors like using clean water for cooking and reduce the number of baths weekly. The key informants prioritized issues related to the low level of hygiene in the community during the needs assessment interviews. In addition, most of the prevalent diseases are related to the existed poor public health within the city, especially, diarrhea, fever, and gastroenteritis. Some people pointed out some concerns related to accommodation. The demographic figures in Tell Abiad are not so different from other areas in northern Syria. The females represent 55% of the total population. In fact, 55% of the community population is under 18 years old which might refer to some households led by youth or non-adults. The needs assessment report shows that there are 185 females and 295 male with a disability.The deteriorated socio-economic status in the city is challenging the access of the target population to the available services in the area.

5 - Implementation of activities

Phase one activities:1-1- Rehabilitate the two water stations in Tell Abiad city.1-2- Repair the water pipelines linked to the target two water stations in Tell Abiad.1-3- Operate the two water stations in Tell Abiad for three months under the support of WHOSD in cooperation with the local water authorities. Phase two activities:2-1- Install chlorine injection pump for every target water station.2-2- Carry out chlorine tests FRC every week for the drinking water from the target water stations. The FRC tests will be carried out on three levels; the first one is in the container, the second one is after the chlorination, and the third one is in the households. The rehabilitation description:The project aims to increase the daily per capita share and sustain the water supply service to the city by:1- Rehabilitation of (Tell Abiad old water station):• Purchasing and insulting submersible pump 70 hp• Purchasing and insulting generator 460 KVA• Providing and insulting control panels for two wells• Maintenance of electricity transformer• Maintenance of generator• Purchasing and insulting chlorine Injection Pump2- Rehabilitation of (Tell Abiad new water station):• Purchasing and insulting battery 200 mAh.• Purchasing and insulting Chlorine Injection Pump• Purchasing and insulting generator 225 KVA3- Maintenance of the water network within the city, including the maintenance of pipes, valves and manholes.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

1- Do not harm: The beneficiaries will have improved access to WaSH services at their places and houses. In other words, people will not head to specific centres or facilities to get the planned services. Therefore, the harm of accessing the planned services will be at its minimum. However, WHOSD will assess the potential harm due to this program to develop a plan to mitigate it.2- Meaningful access: Attention to vulnerable people was paid during the design of the project, and will be paid throughout the implementation phase and the M&E activities. The most vulnerable groups will be identified, and the M&E teams will collect data about access from these groups to be separately analysed in order to amend the intervention according to the findings. The access will be measured on the level of the individual rather than the level of the household to ensure that the results represent the entire target community. The barriers to access the WaSH services will be identified through the MEAL reports in order to change the plan of intervention and share the recommendation with the stakeholders and UNICEF.3- Accountability: WHOSD is developing its policies and guidelines related to the accountability to the affected population. The current feedback mechanism is through the complaint boxes, direct interviews, and WhatsApp numbers shared with the target population.4- Participation and Empowerment: WHOSD will support the empowerment of the target communities through the capacity building component for the local water authority so they will be capable of operating and raise funds for the operational costs of the water stations.
