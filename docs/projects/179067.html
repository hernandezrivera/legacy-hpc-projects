Project Summary

This proposed project duration is 1 year (mid of 2021 till mid 2022), the beneficiaries and budget here reflects only the activities in 2021.
POINT aims to support the camp managements in (25) informal settlements in NWS through providing multidimensional intervention that aims to improve the sites’ management quality and accountability among IDPs, increase fire mitigations measures, raise awareness about COVID19, and provide care and maintenance activities.
POINT has an experienced team whose members are trainers, supporters and facilitators are trained on the CCCM toolkit, Sphere standards, Disaster risk reduction, community mobilization, First Aid, fire safety and COVID19 awareness.
To support the camps’ management, POINT will coordinate with all stakeholders. In the field level and in order to mitigate the risk of local authorities opposing the project, POINT will invest its previous engagement with these authorities and will engage with them and advocate for the project and its activities to guarantee their collaboration. On Turkey level, POINT will coordinate and advocate with all NGOs and clusters to avoid any overlapping and will encourage them to engage the committees in their programing.
POINT team will start the activity by visiting each camp to advocate for establishing the community committee and will work closely with the existed camps managers, community leaders and respectful persons in each camp. then POINT team and specialized trainers will deliver CCCM, Sphere, COVID19, Disaster risk reduction First Aid and fire safety for these committees. the training will be online to mitigate the risk of COVID19 infections, Point will secure internet access for the participants.
After completing the planned training for the established committees, Point in collaboration with the formed committees will establish fire-safety points in each IDP site, those points will be equipped with a fire extinguisher and first aid kits, and other tools that are efficient to fight fires and respond for floods. The approach establishing these fire points will be a community-based approach where the fire point contents will be delivered to the IDPs directly to be put in their tents (for the committees' members as a first priority and the other IDPs according to their locations in the camp). This fire points equipment will be used in case of emergency and adopting this community-based approach will guarantee a safer place from being misused and better access to this equipment. It’s worth mentioning that this equipment is owned by the camps, not by the recipients and should be used for the benefit of the camp's residents.
Moreover, POINT will provide community-based care and maintenance activities in the same targeted camps in collaboration with the formed committees where FGDs will take place in each camp with the committees who will be consulted and be the main source of data for gaps and needs in the camps, and for proposing an in intervention to fill these gaps.
Each camp will be supported with around 8000$ grant to implement some minor infrastructures and rehabilitation activities. POINT team will be the one responsible for managing and following up with the agreed-upon work and will be monitoring other minor infrastructures and technical maintenance activities according to its policies and procedures in collaboration with the committees.
it’s worth mentioning that POINT has been implementing similar activities in collaboration with IOM in the last two years and aiming to expand this intervention by targeting new sites in different locations that have not been targeted before. POINT will make sure that there will be no overlapping with any other similar intervention by any organization through coordinating in the field and in the clusters level and through an assessment that is planned to be conducted before starting this intervention to confirm if there is any established community committees or fire points in the proposed sites or not

4 - Needs

A large proportion of IDP sites in Syria are informal settlements requiring phased upgrading including firebreaks and firefighting tools, surface water drainage, and infrastructures.Point during its implementation to several projects in camps in NWS conducted community based gaps assessment where IDPs reported that they often face challenges in accessing basic services and they identified some gaps they believe filling it will improve the living conditions in their camps like: lighting network in the camp, repairing the sewage network and tent insulation, provide fuel for heating, improve shelter conditions, rehabilitation of roads and water trenches… etc beside identifying some other general needs like education and livelihoods.Moreover, the absence of accountable camp management and maintenance of facilities in IDP sites are deeply concerning and contribute to alarming reports of uncovered needs in these sites. These are key concerns that have important consequences on the protection environment, particularly for women and girls.Regarding COVID19 and according to the EWARN report on 8 February 2021, there were 21,041 cases in NWS where the highest number of confirmed cases were observed in Idleb, Dana, and Afrin which highlights the needs for more awareness among IDPs to minimize the infection probabilities.These needs express the importance of this kind of intervention, failing to fill these gaps will lead to more IDPs jeopardized to fire and floods incidents with no tools to deal with them, poor living conditions, increased protection and GBV related issues, IDPs including women and PWD not being able to participate and express their need efficiently, increased COVID19 risk of infection among IDPs.

5 - Implementation of activities

• IDP sites assessment: POINT will conduct an assessment in the proposed 25 sites in NWS to identify the situation of the camp management and the fire safety measures.• Improving IDP Site management, Quality, and accountability by community participation for the (25) sites by: Coordination and advocacy with all stakeholders, Announcement and selection of camp management committees, Conduct the planned training for camp management committees: CCCM training and Sphere standards, Risk reduction and community mobilization, COVID19, Fire safety and first aid. And there will be field practical training as well in each camp, Mentoring and coaching and site management support visits.• Improve fire mitigation measures by establishing fire points in the targeted camps according to each camp density and number of IDPs.• Provide Care and maintenance activities in collaboration with the committee in each of the 25 IDPs Sites to implement minor infrastructure rehabilitation and maintenance activities that are being identified by the community committees through FGDs sessions which aims to cover the gaps in the targeted sites.Point will review the suggested project ideas and proposals and suggest needed modifications and approve the last version of the project ideas. Later on, POINT in collaboration with the committees will implement the proposed project activities, Point MEAL team will monitor these activities.

Sharing the selection criteria with the local community before starting with the implementation.Assumptions: collaboration from camps management to support this community initiative and community participation.Risk: Lack of responsiveness from IDPs in camps to interact with the newly established initiatives. Mitigation: to announce, introduce, and simplify the idea of these initiatives to IDPs and promote community participation.Risk: little number of female applicants due to social norms.Mitigation: Advocate and coordinate in several ways for the project idea and its importance with all stakeholders especially women to guarantee their participation.

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumption: to avoid COVID19 risk, the training will be delivered online.Risk 1: COVID-19 spread in target communities during some direct activities.Mitigation: POINT will take all necessary measures to avoid the transmission and spread of the disease infection, such as wearing facemasks, providing hand sanitizers.Risk 2: Trainees are not aware of the online training and its applications and tools (for example, ZOOM).Mitigation:1. Support the online training by creating WhatsApp coordination groups to provide communication and share videos and links in these groups.2. Sharing learning videos with all trainees. Test the online training and the access of trainees to the internet. Conducting a test session before starting the actual online training.3. Assigning one or more technical persons to communicate with the selected trainees and provide all related instruction to receive training remotely by the trainees and support them at the field level in case if they need to ensure receiving the training on time.4. Support the online training by distributing flash memory containing all training materials and videos for all trainees at the end of the training. Risk 3: Restriction from the local authorities for the care and maintenance work.Mitigation: coordination with them before the activity, contracting with outsourcing contractor who will be responsible for securing the final approvals.
