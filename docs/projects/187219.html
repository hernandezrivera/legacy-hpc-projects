Project Summary

Mercy Corps will continue to provide unconditional food assistance through electronic vouchers to vulnerable households in Damboa and Bama LGAs in Borno state. All food-assistance will be provided through unconditional, restricted value vouchers, using an e-voucher modality through the Nagis platform. The proposed modality was selected due its ability to provide beneficiaries with a dignified means of food assistance where they can select freely from pre-approved commodities and vendors, which allows Mercy Corps to monitor and ensure the quality of the items provided to beneficiaries. 41,460 people from the most food-insecure, conflict affected IDP, returnee and host households will have access to sufficient, nutritionally adequate and diverse foods.

ADAPT II will respond to humanitarian needs precipitated by the protracted crisis that continues, across Borno state, focusing on the Food Assistance, Nutrition, Shelter, WASH, MPCA, Protection, Agriculture, and Humanitarian Analysis sectors. Wherever possible, the program will explore opportunities to support recovery of affected populations, and also pivot towards meeting critical, life saving needs should existing patterns of coerced returns to unsafe areas continue and/or on account of new displacement among affected populations.

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

Mercy Corps ensures that all households remain empowered to allow for women to redeem the vouchers in exchange for food – and will make sure that both male and female heads are targeted in terms of the consultation on scope and mechanics of the food-assistance process. Furthermore, Mercy Corps specifically targets vulnerable female-headed households for assistance, as these find themselves in a uniquely vulnerable situation, because of a shortage of alternative income generation opportunities – with the few available typically being occupied by men. This leaves women with fewer, safe potential livelihoods and, therefore, in greater need of food assistance. Finally, Mercy Corps trains all project staff, vendors and volunteers on Do No Harm (DNH) principles and Protection from Sexual Explotation and Abuse (PSEA).
