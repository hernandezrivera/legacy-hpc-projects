Project Summary

Global Aid Hand has been implementing an integrated Protection and Education project in 2018 and is seeking support to continue to enhance and strengthen the work already done in 2018 into 2019 and beyond. In this regard GAH has developed this proposal to increase and expand coverage, access new areas and strengthen systems that were created/ formed in 2018. The 2018 project was based on needs and gaps identiﬁed by GAH,UNHCR, UNICEF and other partners and this proposal puts those assessments plus information gathered in 2018 into perspective, in addition to the information from the Inter-agency AGDM Participatory Assessment, conducted by UNHCR and partners that indicate more than half SSR in Sudan are children, high number of whom are UASC. This project is designed in line with Global Aid Hand GP CP , GBV and Education programming in the areas of operation aiming to continue providing and supporting an integrated lifesaving education and protection response for persons affected by the South Sudan emergency. The affected include but are not limited to children at risk (CAR) including Separated and unaccompanied children (SUAC)Women at Risk (WAR) and other vulnerable women and children, Persons with Speciﬁc Needs (PSN) Extremely Vulnerable Individuals (EVI) People Living with Disability (PLWD), vulnerable refugees and their host communities. Target localities are; El Fardous, Elnimir and Kario in East Darfur, Beliel & Alradom in South Darfur, Al Kharasana and Elmariam, in West Kordofan and AbuJubeiha, Aliri and Sirajia in South Kordofan and Alait in North Darfur State. The project aims to provide a community based approach to programming in Protection i.e. General Protection (GP),Child Protection (CP), Gender Based Violence (GBV), in Education i.e. Parents Teachers Associations (PTA) other School Committees, and the locality and state level mechanisms. GAH proposes to use an integrated interlinked multi sectoral methodology that harnesses all possible outcomes to achieve desired goals working in coordination and collaboration with other sectors related directly or indirectly to Protection and Education, Justice Systems, Water Sanitation and Hygiene (WASH) Nutrition, Health and Livelihoods. GAH would also like to apply the new approaches to Child Protection by integrating CP with Education and using community based systems and methodologies

Needs

This project is designed to Restore access to education and provide lifesaving protection (GP , CP and GBV) services for emergency affected South Sudanese refugees in target localities in the 4 states. Women and children carry the biggest burden of suffering in any conﬂict and the South Sudan Emergency is no exception. The situation is further worsened by the fact that the target locations already carry a burden of internal conﬂict with protracted displacements; the areas are also rural remote and have poor infrastructural development as a result of years of continued conﬂict. The project aims to provide a community based approach to programming in Protection i.e. General Protection (GP),Child Protection (CP), Gender Based Violence (GBV), in Education i.e. Parents Teachers Associations (PTA) other School Committees, and the locality and state level mechanisms. GAH proposes to use an integrated interlinked multi sectoral methodology that harnesses all possible outcomes to achieve desired goals working in coordination and collaboration with other sectors related directly or indirectly to Protection and Education, Justice Systems, Water Sanitation and Hygiene (WASH) Nutrition, Health and Livelihoods. GAH would also like to apply the new approaches to Child Protection by integrating CP with Education and using community based systems and methodologies. The target locality have been ranked poorly in the IAAS conducted and the UNHCR AGDPA held in November in WK and SK. Darfur continues to carry the huge burden of refugees and the gap continues to widen with the current economic situation moving HH further into vulnerability. Most of the refugees targeted under this project live within the host communities and thus further puts pressure on the little resources in the area. Aliri has the highest number of chidren out of school further putting protection risks on these cases. Most of the infrastructure in the aras are underdeveloped and secondary education even for host community children is missing.

Activities

GAH will conduct Child Protection rapid assessments through at Focus Group Discussions and mapping of available services to inform referral pathways, GAH will work with Nada Alsar to support PSN WAR and EVI. The interventions will aim at protection against abuse and violence to women children and other vulnerable groups, The project intends to have robust community based CP activities that not only target the support to children but also aims at building community capacity to identify respond to and referCAR including Separated and Unaccompanied Children (SUAC) and their foster families, The project aims to provide a community based approach to programming in Protection i.e. General Protection (GP),Child Protection (CP), Gender Based Violence (GBV), in Education i.e. Parents Teachers Associations (PTA) other School Committees, and the locality and state levels. The project aims to increase access and retention of children in school by making the schools child friendly, construction, rehabilitation and facelifts of schools, community centers and other infrastructure will be doneThis project would like to work with secondary education in order to motivate enrollment for primary education and increase employ-ability and livelihoods for the graduates. Girls access to education will be enhance through gender sensitive activities and working around protection issues that bar girls from accessing school. Child protection including protection from SGBV and PSEA will also be considered and capacity building of duty bearers to ensure continuity and sustainability will be enhanced.
