Project Summary

MICHAFOUND aims at reducing the problem of food within some IDPs in the SWR of Cameroon. 2000 families shall receive food assistance
These IDPs are as a result from the current Anglophone crisis ongoing in Cameroon for the past three years.
IDPs and non IDPs related to food as a problem based on data collected in 2019 shall be called to secured venues for distribution(raw foodstuffs).
Project execution will be done in Kumbe, Buea, Mutengene, Limbe and Tiko.
Feedback will be collected in-house (proposed venue) through short paper surveys for general reporting. This shall reduce hunger, rebuild livelihood and encourage development.
Total budget is at 164,850USD beginning 01/01/2020 – 31/12/2020

Besoins / Needs

The findings from emergency food security assessment that WFP and partners conducted showed a proxy GAM of 4.4% and 5.6% for the NW and SW respectively. However, access constraints hindered the survey and there is possibility of higher nutritional vulnerability in hard-to-reach areas. Compounded with the aggravating factors including food insecurity, very limited access to health services and poor water and sanitation, the nutrition situation is expected to deteriorate if immediate nutrition response is not provided. Based on above reports, we shall be giving out humanitarian assistance in areas of food insecurity both internally and non-internally displaced persons. In an attempt to reduce the rate of food insecurity, we aim at giving out few fmcg (fast moving consumer goods) to 500 families to meet up part of the humanitarian target.
