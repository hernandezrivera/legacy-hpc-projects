Project Summary

This intervention links to HRP Need Overview in Sudan 2017-2019 through outcome 2 and 3 by supporting displaced populations, returnees and host communities to meet their basic needs and/or access to essential basic services while increasing their self-reliance (O2), and focusing on vulnerable residents to improve their nutrition status and increased resilience (O3) in West Darfur, West Kordofan and South Kordofan States. This intervention also contributes to FSL sector outputs and activities to achieve the FSL sector strategic objectives by increase equal access to quality basic food, and income option (output 2.1., 2.2.), and improved capacity to prevent malnutrition through community based livelihood interventions.
The four interrelated pillars of the proposed project:
1) enhanced food production through access to farm input (seeds, tools, irrigation inputs) and extension services (Farmers Filed School approach) with capacity building in a timely manner
2) increased the access to essential livelihood services mostly for females through income generating activities and safety net initiatives linking to capacity building and locality/state level existing market systems. Establish community village saving groups/associations, community gardening, skills/vocation training will be implemented that serves the most vulnerable in establishing save income resource. The intervention will ensure that the learnt skills, the established resources get to the local market, through taping on locally available market systems (local markets groups, committees and value chain systems). This linkage will be strengthened during the implementation.
3) improved nutrition and food diversification for vulnerable households through home gardening, vegetable production, food processing and related skills development. This project component will be linked to the first pillar and will provide an improved variety and quality of seeds and seedlings for beneficiaries with a climate smart gardening techniques (e.g. minimum tillage, organic composting, organic pesticide) so the quality of vegetable production will improve, and it will remain cost effective. This project component also will teach beneficiaries on food processing and food conservation that will contribute to the positive coping mechanisms and reduce the huger gap period. As a result, it will also improve the nutrition and health status of the most vulnerable by providing quality healthy food, during lean season (May, July). During the implementation fuel efficient technologies will be applied to reduce the environment degradation and the negative ecological footprints.
4) developed skills in forest/tree planting and management to increase regeneration of trees linking to environment protection. This component will strengthen resilience against climate related shocks and stresses for the entire community through tree planning and tree management in and outside of the community.
The project aims at implementing in West Darfur, West Kordofan and South Kordofan States with integrating in other cluster interventions where possible and feasible.
Gender and environment mainstreaming, and cross-cutting linkage will be established and emphasized during the intervention through priority focus on female participants in the project activities, and while implementing agriculture interventions environment risk management will be considered.

Needs

According to the latest IPC results, 5.6 million people in Sudan are classified as food insecure. There are an approx. 349,000 people in need of food security assistance in West Kordofan, South Kordofan and West Darfur, according to the FSL cluster geographic priorities.In West Darfur, West Kordofan and South Kordofan the food insecurity, especially in hard-reach areas where challenging access reduce the availability of food and livelihood opportunities, limits the households to generate food stocks especially during the lean season. Vulnerable people, particularly women and children suffer from limited dietary diversity causes malnutrition, vitamin deficiency, and related health problems. Due to the marginalized condition of the rural population, the majority of the HHs don’t have the resources, the skills, and the knowledge to improve their coping mechanisms against climate driven shocks e.g. flooding in West Kordofan, or man-made stresses e.g. conflict over grazing/land in Jabel Moon, Sirba in West Darfur. The situation has been deteriorating with the devaluation of the currency, lack of basic food, and the cash crisis. Particularly in West Darfur, the latest insecurity in Jabel Moon, resulted forced migration that will certainly increase the number of food insecure people, particularly youth, women and children, as a result of lack of land cultivation and husbandry, income. In West Kordofan, climate extreme weather (heavy rain) has hit El Nuhud and Gybesh localities last year forced nearly 30,000 people out of their homes and their lands. Additional the state has been witnessing mass movement of SS refugees (55,000 UNHCR 2018), that is an additional burden on the host communities (sharing cultivating land, water sources). There is a need for an integrated emergency response in locations like Jabel Moon in West Kordofan, and some locations in West Kordofan by providing basic agriculture inputs (seeds, and tools) and capacity building of the beneficiaries to improve and sustain the given services. The recovery and resilience-building with focusing on climate smart solutions in West and South Kordofan is a critical need to protect and restore the livelihoods opportunities for the most vulnerable including children, persons with special needs, female-headed households, protracted displaced and newly displaced people. There is also a need for a longer term food stock stabilization, income generation for the most at risk groups (women, children, young adult, elderly) with constant access to local market chains (local market groups). Capacity building on disaster risk reduction practices (DRR) is needed not only for the direct beneficiaries but for stakeholders such as agriculture authorities, other humanitarian organizations, research institutions to reduce the risk, to be prepared for disasters (e.g. flooding, conflict eruption), and able to respond where needed.

Impact on environment/natural resources

The proposed project significantly considers environment protection and climate change adaptation through three pillars:1) all agriculture inputs (seeds, seedlings, tools, pesticide etc.) will follow the minimum standards for environment protection. Improved seeds and seedlings will be procured through certified suppliers that are recognized by the Ministry of Agriculture in country, and the products are safe for human consumption and are not harmful for the environment. Other inputs such as pesticides will be procured based on MoA standards though the project will introduce only organic pesticides to minimize the risk of harm for the nature. 2) Capacity building training, skills training, IGA training will have components to address environment management within the given context of the training. The availability of local resources will be also highlighted and participants on the training will learn on how to use these resources in a sustainable way (e.g. land use with minimal tillage, reducing waste around water sources, using natural repellents, tinctures and mixtures for crop management, instead of chemicals). These training will also advocate for forest protection and the project also has one activity that addresses tree planting within/outside of the communities. 3) The intervention has a strong linkage to resilience and climate change adaptation practices through improved seeds and seedlings distribution that has already been tested and succeeded. The establishment of Farmers Field School will ensure that the improved seeds and related farming methods will be disseminated and adopted by the selected farmers. The project will also address environment protection through community campaigns, marking international days e.g. Int. Tree Days, Earth Day etc.
