Project Summary

The project will support host community of Ibo, Mecufi Districts and Cidade de Pemba and IDPs to have protected and reliable access to safe water, sanitation and hygiene facilities and a safe and healthy environment, contributing to reduce the risk of transmission of WASH related diseases

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project have the following objectivies: Promote protection of populations and prevent conflict and gender-based violence through provision of inclusive and gender sensitive water; sanitation; and hygiene services, items, and facilities for vulnerable populations, including hosting communities Provide inclusive and gender sensitive life-saving water; sanitation; and hygiene services, items, and facilities for vulnerable populations affected by conflict, including displaced populations, returnees, and affected individuals in difficult to access areas.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

PEMBA- Construction of new water sources for host communities and resettlement areas- Expansion of piped networks to affected communities, resettlement sites, and hosting communities- Dissemination of WASH related messages MECUFI- Rehabilitation and disinfection of existing water points in host communities, including support for water committees; - Construction of new water sources for host communities; - Dissemination of WASH related messages IBO- Rehabilitation of wells and construction of school RWHS and improvement of 2.500 RWHSs for IDPs at HH level- Improvement of 12 traditional manually dug water point and testing and promotion of water filtration with candle filter and solar water disinfection - Monitoring and support of water and sanitation committees- installation of community latrines - Creation of drying beds and organization of desludging for IDPs and host community - Promotion activities for feces controlled disposal - Hygiene Campaign - Installation of latrines and handwashing facilities for people with disabilities

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The project will create spaces for interaction, such as public consultations, focus groups and community data collection, to identify constraints and ways to better production techniques. It will make the results more reliable, fostering ownership and capacities. All the activities will include specific sessions to discuss the issues and new habits promoted to enhance the full comprehension and adopting. The Districtal service for infrastructures SDPI in the selected district for implementation of the project activities will work with assistance from the WASH cluster and active partners in Cabo Delgado and utilize existing feedback mechanisms such as Linha Verde and Linha Fala Criança for which specific pre-formulated questions and replies already do exist. All requests for responses to the Linha Verde mechanism will be responed to within 48 hours of receipt from the WASH Cluster. Therefore, the DPOPHRH, SDPI and partners will be aware and can follow up about any comment on the distribution or availability and quality of the services and products provided, and of specific requests for information.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

This action is designed according to the expressed need of target groups assessed during periodical focus groups, surveys and other participatory methodologies during years of presence of Oikos in the area. Furthermore, participation of target groups is mainstreamed during the whole project implementation. The action focuses on working in close collaboration with the community utilizing their internal resources and valorizing traditional and cultural knowledge. This will facilitate the acceptance of the intervention by the community and will guarantee its participation.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Local Institutional stakeholders will participate in the beneficiaries’ selection to ensure to include the most vulnerable households, including people with disabilities. Further, consultations will be done individuals with individuals living with disabilities and the elderly to identify specific adaptation needs for infrastructure. The support offered to the vulnerable families/people with disabilities will take into account the special need, adapting infrastructure to the specific needs.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project will take into account some environmental considerations that can come up during the NFI distribution (such as the production of waste) or linked to the construction material provenience. The project identified the following solutions: ● During distributions activities, the population and other beneficiaries will be taught the correct way to dispose the waste produced (such as plastic bags and packaging) in a way that would not be prejudicial to the environment. ● All the construction material will be provided assuring its sustainable provenience and avoiding to encourage the illegal traffic of wood, in order to prevent the exploitation of the resources of the QNP (Quirimbas National Park) ● The project will avoid to promote material coming from non-renewable resources and it will ensure a minimal use of non-re-used material to reduce mass waste production in refugee accommodation sites and host communities.
