Project Summary

COER is a National NGO, strong Food security and livelihood cluster partner with over four years of implementation capacity, and a very strong presence in Western Bahr El Ghazal. COER is proposing in 2022 Humanitarian appeal an Emergency livelihood support interventions in Western Bahr- El Ghazal . The proposed project runs from January 2022 to December 2022 and plans to support 20000 Households ( 120,000 Beneficiaries) The key objective of the project is to reduce vulnerability and increase resilience amongst Vulnerable household access through support to increased food production and to reduce the level of vulnerability amongst malnourished children through linkage of nutrition related activities to Food security activities.. The project is line with HRP strategic objective 2 ( SO2) and is in support of FSL cluster objective 2. The programmatic delivery approach will be direct distribution targeting mainly IDPs, returnees, and vulnerable host population in these areas in a way that prevents the spread & transmission of COVID 19.A total of120,000 people will be targeted Livelihood input distribution, resilience training in improved vegetable production with vaccination, and treatment of livestock are the key activities to be undertaken. The project will target women, girls, men & boys, elderly, disabled, PLW, children U5, HIV & chronic sick including boys children, girls, and men that are at high protection risks throughout the implementation of the project . The project will focus high number of returnees in Wau County and internally displaced population in and around Tambura County. Additionally the project has major consideration for the accountability to the affected population, as it will work through Accountability to affected community AAP committees. Feedback mechanisms will be established in the community so that beneficiaries are well informed about the project's actions The project will integrate or work closely with both the protection cluster and the nutrition cluster. With the nutrition project, the project will set up demonstration sites at OTP centers and train households with malnourished children. These households will also be supported with high nutritious vegetables which they will produce to address the nutritional needs of their malnourished children.

1 - Needs

With 8.9 million people in need which include 8.54 million South Sudanese women, men, girls and boys and 328,000 refugees and asylum seekers. Two million people are internally displaced; of these 1.4 million require urgent support, and 1.8 million are returnees, of these 1.2 million have urgent needs (UNOCHA 2022), the Humanitarian need in South Sudan is overtly higher than the preceding years. High Food insecurity needs has exacerbated to this high threshold of the Humanitarian situation.The high food insecurity are covariate climatic , conflict and economic shocks ( such as floods and dry spells, inflation and rising food prices an the continued disruption of livelihoods due to violence or fear of violence and household level idiosyncratic stressors ( such as death of bread winner, gender -based violence, morbidity and mortality due to poor hygiene and lack of access to essential services) which are greatly amplified by the continuous effect of shocks . According to Integrated Food Security phase report, food consumption gaps are extreme and people have largely exhausted Emergency coping strategies. In Tambura Country, the high food insecurity was exacerbated by the tribal conflict which left more than 200,000 people displaced. This specific displaced number of people missed all the period of cultivation in 2021 and had to rely on desperate Food assistance. In Wau , the high number of returnees to the area of origin have put much more stress on the community that have led to exhaustion of the coping strategies. The most affected are children, people with disabilities, older persons and Women headed households that are identified as the the most vulnerable to increase in Food insecurity. According to WFP, 19 percent of the 3.1 Million caseloads receiving Emergency food assistance from January to October, 2021 are internally displaced people (IDP’s); 47 percent are male and 53 percent are female, 61 percent are children , 35 percent are adults.According to the intersectoral analysis, the main factors affecting food security are food availability, access, utilization, stability and seasonality. These are influenced by other sectors such as nutrition , water and sanitation and protection. The quest of this proposal therefore aim at enhancing and sustaining emergency food production through complementary vegetable and crop livelihood inputs, fishing, and livestock support in a way that prevents the spread & transmission of COVID 19..

2 - Project activities

The key activities for this project include: Formation and training of Accountability to affected Population committee, distribution of main agricultural seeds ,tools and fishing; distribution of lean season dry season vegetable seeds, tools, fishing kits Distribution of rapid response kits, promotion of resilience light vegetables and fishing kits training;, vaccination, treatment of livestock and Training of Community Animal Health Workers, provision of timely food security information and provision of key messages that will help reduce the impact of COVID-19

The below are specific project indicators that will be monitored. • Number of HHs (female, child & male-headed) receiving agricultural inputs/ vegetable & fishing kits • Quantity & variety of seeds distributed • Quantity & variety of crops produced (outcome) • % women receiving livelihood kits (crop/ veg/ fish); % of women receiving livelihood support; number of national and State level food security and livelihood coordination meetings • Number of SAD returnees receiving livelihood support • Number of animals vaccinated & treated The success of the project will be collected through regular field visits to monitor the progress of the intervention, beneficiary list, field visit monitoring data, mid and final reports, field monitoring reports will all be the major source of data for measuring the success of the project.
