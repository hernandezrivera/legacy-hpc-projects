Project Summary

The proposed intervention is implemented jointly by Norwegian Church Aid (NCA) and its local partners-Ekklesiya Ya’uwa Nigeria (EYN), Federation of Muslim Women Association (FOMWAN) and Jireh Doo Foundation (JDF) in 7 conflict affected Local Government Areas (LGAs) of Borno state, Nigeria.
The proposed intervention focuses on provision of life-saving GBV assistance to the most vulnerable populations (women, men, girls and boys) in conflict affected areas, as well as to strengthen the resilience of the affected population.
The proposed intervention feeds into the Humanitarian Response Plan 2020-2021 (HRP) and is coordinated through respective GBV sub sector cluster with lead agencies as well as other partners in the target areas. All partners are particularly focused on actions designed to enhance physical and mental wellbeing, improve living standards and coping mechanisms of the affected population.
The affected populations (women, men, girls and boys) are in the center of the intervention and participatory methods are used throughout the project cycle management to ensure ownership and sustainability of project results. On top of that, NCA GBV intervention focuses in strengthening of the GBV response through capacity building and development of guidelines and trainings especially in specialized GBV programming that will allow all GBV partners in Borno state to provide better services.

1 - Needs

Accordingly, to the GBV sub sector (October 2019), there are 1,325, 776 people in need, among these 490,579 are IDPS, 349,756 are returnees, 485,442 are host communities. The GBV HRP for 20202 targets a total of 1,015,865 individuals in Borno, 153,229 in Adamawa and 156,681 in Yobe states-Northeast Nigeria. Out of these, 446,686 are women and 597,184 are girls. 122,669 men are targeted and 159,237 boys Majority (101586) of the target population lives in Borno state Currently 67 % of the GBV targets for 2019 have been reached (GBV sub sector situational update) The GBV sector identified the following key gaps: (I) Socio economic assistance for women and girls formerly associated with armed forces (7% coverage). (ii) provision of specialized response services for GBV (44% coverage) (iii) empowerment and skills building (74% coverage). (iv) Community engagement and sensitization (52% coverage) The GBV sub sectors states that A system wide response is required to reduce the risks of survival sex among affected communities which appears to be on the rise. Furthermore, partners are encouraged to Strengthen and scale up efforts that promote safe livelihoods opportunities. This is key to reducing GBV risks for women and girls. In addition to the above, the situation facing women and girls is particularly dire. Violence against women and girls, including sexual violence, and exposure to trafficking and abduction is widespread but under reported. Some 99 per cent of reported GBV incidents in the first half of 2018 were made by women and girls. Among the women and girls who were forced and abducted from their homes by NSAGs, many are raped, forced into marriage and labor, abused physically, sexually and/or emotionally, exposed to sexually transmitted diseases, and often impregnated by their captors. Anecdotal reports indicate that in addition to the everyday struggles faced by women and girls, they are often forced into survival sex in exchange for food, movement and items to meet their basic needs (OCHA, 2019).

2 - Activities

-Safe shelters: NCA will identify and support government owned institutions and provide safe shelters for women and girls- NCA will provide capacity building for staff of governmnet institutions providing care to GBV survivors- Case management services will be provided for GBV survivors using the inter-agency guidelines and tools including the GBVIMS+/primero. - PSS. NCA and its partners will provide daily PSS activities at individual and group level at the safe spaces and other well-being activities will be provided. These activities are to be identified by the women and girls. Skills building activities will be carried out at the spaces to engage women and girls in productive activities and to support their psycho social wellbeing. – CMR, SRH NCA will provide capacity building to health care workers on CMR, SRH, nutrition and family planning. In addition, NCA will provide Onsight technical support to health facilities in close collaboration with ministry of health and UNFPA. - GBV mobile response. NCA will work close and build the capacity of its partners to enable both NCA and partners carryout weekly mobile services including case management in hard to reach areas or where services are not available/limited. -NCA will provide mobile clinics in areas with high influx of new arrivals and where services are limited. - NCA and its partners will provide context and age appropriate NFIs/material support to vulnerable women and girls to promote their dignity and self-esteem. - Field coordination. NCA will ensure leadership at field level and ensure coordination of all GBV activities at both communities, camp and reception centers levels and will coordinate closely with sector. monthly meetings will be held and reporting to cluster. - Referral pathways will be continuously updated in areas where NCA is a lead agency at field level through an effective service, mapping of all services. safety audit will be done and partners informed of the findings as the need arises. NCA and its partners will ensure the capacity building of local leaders and communities and will support in establishing local mechanisms for GBV prevention such as community action groups. - NCA and its partners will provide various empowerment training and livelihood supports to women and girls who visit the safe spaces. Women and girls formerly associated with armed groups will be targeted to benefit from livelihood material supports based on the skills learnt. - Numerous community outreach, sensitizations and dialogue sessions will be carried out and engagement of communities and various stakeholders in GBV prevention. - capacity building of front-line workers on GBV integration, referral mechanisms and basics of GBV. field level workers will be provided with various capacity to enable them appropriately to respond to beneficiaries needs and provide standardized packages at field level and ensure implementation. - NCA and its partners will also ensure appropriate feedback mechanism through CRM and also conduct regular focused group discussions. - NCA will implement the GBVIMS+ and provide support in the roll-out and implementation. capacity building initiatives will be done for both NCA and other DGO partners. - NCA will conduct by-monthly safety audits and the monitoring of GBV risk and measures implemented.
