Project Summary

In 2021, Help a Child in collaboration with the IOM, the Red Cross, OCHA, the national platform for disaster responses and the Ministry of solidarity, gender and social affairs supported IDP households affected by floods along Lake Tanganyika with 524 NFI kits.
In 2022, Help a Child would like to build on its previous responses with the launch of Hembura Project III. A Project that aims to provide 1000 IDP households affected affected by natural disasters with NFI kits and 3 months’ rent fees to facilitate their reintegration in permanent shelters.
