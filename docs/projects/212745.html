Project Summary

a. Needs:
The ongoing conflict in Syria, now entering its fourteenth year, has caused immense destruction and unimaginable losses to the people of Syria and all aspects of their lives. Protection indicators have tragically declined due to the war and its consequences. Children specifically face violence, displacement, and limited access to basic rights. Thousands of children have lost their lives, while others have suffered injuries of varying degrees. The battles and the February 2023 earthquake have led to temporary or permanent disabilities. Psychological wounds in children are as serious as physical injuries, hindering the lives of those who survived the war. People with disabilities bear a double burden, facing increased risks of violence, hunger, abuse, and lack of education.
The suffering of children in Ma'arrat An Nu'man and Abul Thohur regions is exacerbated by the prolonged conflict, displacement of residents, and widespread destruction in all sectors. Nearly all children in these regions were born during years of conflict, knowing nothing but war, conflict, and tragedies. Fighting and displacement have interrupted their education, increasing illiteracy and ignorance among them. Poverty, deteriorating living conditions, and severe shortages of basic goods and services worsen the situation, leading to child labor, early and forced marriages. Even after residents returned to these regions, children still live in constant fear of violence, landmines, explosives, and other war remnants. The children in these regions have paid a high price for the war and are now in urgent need of enhanced protection levels, increased education and cultural awareness, and the provision of safe spaces away from violence and harm.
b. Intervention, Activities Implementation:
The project will train the team on responding to child protection from war remnants, raising awareness about dangerous areas and immediate reporting mechanisms. It will conduct educational sessions and provide psychosocial support services for affected and unaffected individuals from explosives. Additionally, it will distribute educational materials on the risks of explosive devices and provide physical rehabilitation by supplying prosthetics for the affected. Financial assistance will be provided to children with disabilities through transfers delivered to their families to support and empower them where as The cash support program provides $40 per child to families of children with disabilities every two months in ma'arrat An Nu'man and its contrysite.
c. Analysis of Protection Risks:
One of the main risks children may face is the lack of reporting mechanisms and disclosure of danger threatening their lives or losing a limb in some uncleared areas. This significantly affects the child's psychological safety by not having a safe environment and losing trust in their surroundings.
d. Strategy for Protection from Exploitation and Sexual Abuse:
Al-Birr and Social Services Association in Idleb adopts a code of conduct and protection policy that all employees and volunteers must adhere to, prohibiting any behavior indicating exploitation, harassment, or sexual abuse. The charity does not tolerate any behavior that may compromise the dignity or privacy of beneficiaries. The charity has assistance and protection offices that receive complaints following confidential procedures ensuring the safety and security of complainants, providing training on protection policies and procedures for individuals and beneficiaries if necessary.
e. Risks, Assumptions, and Mitigation Measures:
- Difficulty accessing mental health care: A community center providing psychosocial support services and initial psychological first aid.
- Absence from schools: Educational awareness activities and recreational materials.
- Lethal foreign bodies: Training on identifying contamination sites and reporting mechanisms.
- Lack of awareness: Educational sessions on war remnants risks.
