Project Summary

Identify and map areas of major concentration of people displaced from their origin areas because of the conflicts
Conduct a rapid needs assessment (RNA) of displaced people from there origin areas particular to women and girls, to identify the vulnerability level of violence "sexual abuse, sexual exploration and other gender-based violence".
Identify and select community gender equality promoters and their integration in the communities with the higher concentration of displaced people from there origin areas
Train the community gender equality promoters, Community Leaders and Police Authorities in specific issues such as " i) Psychosocial Support, ii) Women and Girls Protection against all kinds of violence, iii) Women's Rights, iv) Sexual and Reproductive Health through the (Minimum Initial Service Packege - MISP)
Through the community gender equality promoters, conduct community sessions to disseminate the women and girls rights and dignity, to prevent and fight all types of gender-based violence against displaced women and girls
Through the community gender equality promoters, conduct community sessions to disseminate the women and girls rights and dignity, to prevent and fight all types of gender-based violence against displaced women and girls
Establish and train local gender Committees for women and girls protection against all kinds of gender based violence at targeted communities
Organize and conduct focus groups in areas of concentration of displaced people, to discuss key aspects such as i) women and girls protection, ii) gender and violence, iii) access to basic services and resources, iv) sexual and reproductive health, including family planning (MISP)
Establish in the target communities, specific mechanisms for complaints of violence of cases against women and girls, as well as suggestions and feedback through complaint boxes, telephone line and among other mechanisms
Identify specific cases of abuse and violence against Women and Girls and their referral to police and judicial authorities for legal assistance
