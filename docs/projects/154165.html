Project Summary

- The project will organize training for community members including faith and community leaders, CBO and CSO members on child protection through the Channels of Hope methodology
- The project will set up community based structures such as Community Hope Action Teams (CHAT), Child Protection Clubs and Service provider committees. The CHAT members trained by the project will develop action plans to address child protection issues within their communities. They will organize regular meetings to provide updates on child protection, monitor the progress of action plans and organize awareness campaigns on child protection issues within the community. Orphan guardians will participate in awareness sessions and induction on CP which will be documented through the signature of CP induction forms to be archived in orphan files.
- At the start of the intervention, existing service providers will be identified through mapping and partnerships developed with them for the management of cases. A committee of service providers will be established within localities which will address cases of abuse in line with their field of competency. Child protection clubs will be established which will allow children to voice their concerns regarding the violation of their rights or any cases of abuse. These will also contribute to their development process. Communication for behavioral change sessions will be organized by the Project team towards orphan guardians. In addition capacitated faith leaders will organize awareness sessions in order to empower the community members on channel of hope for child protection. The main channel they will use is the Qutba or preaches. Once aware, the community will be able to adopt positive behavior in line with child protection.

Needs

After the crisis that stroke our country in 2012, the problems of protection related to children and women have gained ground. So, the rights of children and women are violated days and nights making them the most vulnerable strata of the population. So, given such a critical situation experienced by children and women in our country, we have deemed necessary to design this project channels of hope for child protection. By channels of hope we would like to be the hope, the light for these women and children who are suffering continuously from abuses, tortures and gender-based violence. So, the project channels of hope will be an approach to mobilize all the community entities and mainly the most respected and influential. These are the faith-leaders, the community leaders and all the protection stakeholders who will be involved in sensitization, trainings, preaching and advocating protection within communities, houses, schools and services. Channels of hope will enable to set up protection committees and clubs to ensure women and chilldren have the rights to voice their abuse and are protected as well.

Activities

Activity 1.1.: Training religious leaders, schools teachers, the youth association leaders, women and child leaders on child protection, complaints mechanism and reporting of child abuse Activity 2.1: Setting up CHATs and community protection entities in the project intervention area.Activity 2..5: Set up children’s sharing clubs to enable children to have a space to disclose abuse, their problems either though drama, comedy, sketches, poetry or drawings.Activity 3.1.: Mapping the available referral services for child protection issue managementActivity 3.2: Organization of meetings with the referral services Activity 3.3: Setting up a communication mechanism for children’s support and referral Activity 3.4: Broadcasting CP messages on local radios in order to reach the widest number of the communities for entailing behavior about child protectionActivity 2..6: Organization of meetings with the children’s clubs to follow closely what children are facing in their daily life and how to pave the way for their wellbeing based on their own selected activities.Activity 2.8: Organization of meetings with community protection committees
