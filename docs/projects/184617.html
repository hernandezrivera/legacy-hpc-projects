Project Summary

The Humanitarian Response Plan (HRP) for Aamin 2022 targets Galkayo, Balcad, and Benadir. We target 66,810 beneficiaries as we plan to expand our humanitarian services to areas needed most. Aamin Organization will provide emergency free ambulance services, maternal, newborn and child health, fixed clinic services, mobile outreach and MHPSS services to the affected populace among them the IDPs, Host communities and refugees under 2022 the HRP project.
Under this HRP plan, Aamin undertake to bridge the gap of accessibility of health services both in drought and flood-stricken areas under these target locations whilst increasing availability of essential medicine with advocacy for pull system of drug acquisition. Aamin Ambulance will provide a twenty-four-hour, unparalleled free ambulance service in Mogadishu, Somalia. The service aim to Improve equitable access to quality emergency and essential lifesaving health services for the crisis affected population. Aamin ambulance will thus bridge the gap of unreliable health and emergency response system through this free ambulance and first aid system to the people of Mogadishu. We take cognizance of the needs of the vulnerable in the community particularly women and children. Our project design therefore ensures the needs of the vulnerable are entrenched in all project activities.
Aamin will further utilize the use of mobile clinic services that will see health services accessed by vulnerable population living in hard-to-reach areas. Under this HRP, Aamin will see both utilization of 5 fixed health facilities and 13 mobile clinics to meet its target of providing OPD consultation of 120 consultation a day. This will contribute immensely to improved access to health services in Somalia.
Aamin ambulance will integrate Mental Health/Psycho-social Support (PFA) services, community engagement, and health education to enhance identification and referral of persons of concern in need of MHPSS while also conducting a focused MHPSS intervention through trained social workers. The project activities will include conduct of psycho-education sessions for persons in need, including raising awareness on key protection risks faced by PSNs i.e discrimination, stigma.
Aamin will remain true to the centrality of protection in its implementation of projects through protection mainstreaming, accountability to affected populations and monitoring of the protection environment.
Aamin will further provide the following services:
1. Provision of GBV services, including clinical management of rape (CMR) and Psychosocial Support Services (PSS) and community awareness.
2. Scale-up early warning and response (EWAR) and case management of epidemic prone diseases through surveillance and data use. The project will build and support the national HMIS and reporting of notifiable diseases of national concern for appropriate action. This will involve building the capacity of the health care service providers to getting adequate knowledge on EWAR.
3. Integration of Mental Health/Psycho-social Support (MHPSS) services, community engagement, and health education to community members to enhance identification and referral of persons of concern in need of MHPSS while also conducting a focused MHPSS intervention through trained social workers.

Increase in consultation services to an average of 45 consultations per day to offer treatment for men, women, boys and girls, which is inclusive of people living with disability. We will provide treatment of common communicable diseases, STD (sexually transmitted Diseases) and appropriate follow ups to the affected population. We will establish a referral pathway for complicated cases that the mobile clinic may not be able to handle to the nearest referral hospital facilities. 3. Provision of BEmONC, Antenatal and postnatal clinic services through the established Clinics. 4.6 - Mitigation Measures

1: Protection of civilians: Aamin Shall ensure the following:• The access routes to the health facilities and services are safe by identifying routes for our ambulances to operate and avoid areas near military installations or threats such as attacks, stray bullets, crossfire or violence. • The project employ staff with skills and experiences working with children’s patients • Consult both displaced and host communities about health needs and assess whether Aamin ambulance interventions could cause tension, harassment or conflict between the two communities. Risk 2: Exclusion and marginalization • We shall ensure that beneficiaries know of available health services and assistance where/how to obtain it. • We shall communicate information through various means to ensure messages reaches the broader community • We shall involve and consult all categories and layers of the affected population in identifying and responding to health needs. • Consult women, men, boys, girls, persons with disabilities, chronically ill, older persons, pregnant and lactating women and marginalized persons to collect accurate information about their specific needs and preferences for emergency health services. • Engage the community and committee representative to play an active role in identifying solutions and in the decision-making processes that affect them, so as to promote a sense of ownership and improve the relevance and sustainability of the response Risk 3: Protection mainstreaming:• In cooperation with protection actors, establish reporting mechanisms to report security incidents concerning hospitals to advocate against attacks on hospitals and military use of hospitals.• Ensure the location and access routes to health facilities and services are safe.• We are aware of how power dynamics between our staff and the patient can affect the patient’s response and attitude towards the staff/organization and service provided • Ensure all our staff, implementing partners and volunteers working with the beneficiaries understand, sign and adhere to Aamin Code of Conduct • In cooperation with protection actors, we shall enhance the capacity of community health workers to monitor, report and refer protection cases (such as abuse and exploitation) in accordance with standard operating procedures.
