Project Summary

The project focuses activities on GBV prevention and awareness raising related workshops & messages, and as well as case management. While it reduces risk of GBV through prevention and mitigation strategies and promotes resilience by strengthening community-based systems that prevent and mitigate GBV, and enabling survivors and those at risk to access supports they need for recovery physically and mentally. Also it supports local capacity to create lasting solutions to the problem of GBV through community care and support.
People with disabilities particularly women and girls either physically or mentally disabled have no the opportunity to practice their rights as well as normal women/girls and are at risk of neglect, exploitation and abuse. They face exclusion from social services and as well humanitarian responses due to social marginalization and isolation. PWLD are most vulnerable and women from this group further marginalized. During economic crises or emergencies their access to humanitarian assistance is limited, in which they become despair and distress. So the project preserves people with disabilities to participate the same as free citizens.
Baidoa hosts large IDPs and as well diverse ethnic minorities reside different villages of the town also Barawe hosts IDPs and early settlers of the town are Somali ethnic minority known Barawani. Women and girls face violations against their rights either in the family or in the community. They face intimate partner violence, denial of dowry, denial of inheritance, rape, intimidation, verbal abuse, and physical assault; more over women from IDPs, minority are more vulnerable to be victim of human rights violation due to the physical environment they reside and humanitarian situation in the ground. Therefore the project intervention includes reaching most vulnerable individuals such as IDPs, minority, through community structures and/or local communities to collect and use information about minority and promote participation of minority women in order to improve their quality of life.
From the 2022 HNO; it is recorded the women and girls living in IDP Camps are more prone to sexual violence and abuse than those living in host communities with a better housing infrastructure and service sites and markets. Nevertheless project intervention creates safe environment for all men and women and respect women's rights.

1. - Projects activities

Project activities include: 1. Capacitating for relevant actors (government officials, security personnel, NGOs) and cluster field coordinators with knowledge on implementing GBV mitigation, coordination, response strategies and PSEA for vulnerable women and girls (including Persons Living With Disabilities and minority clans); and conduct 16 days activism. 2. Conduct sessions to provide women, girls, boys and men (including those with disabilities and from minority clans) information on service availability, prevention and mitigation of GBV/FGM. 3. Identification and Referral of survivors to age appropriate and quality services of clinical management of rape cases.4. Facilitate psycho-social support for FGM/GBV survivors in line with minimum standards and MHPSS guidelines5. Conduct GBV/RH education sessions on Women and Girls 6. Facilitate access to age appropriate and gender responsive legal aid support/education and information. 7. Provide dignity kits, menstrual hygiene kits and solar lanterns to vulnerable women and adolescent girls (including those living with disabilities and from minority clans)
