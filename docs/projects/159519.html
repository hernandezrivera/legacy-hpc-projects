Project Summary

The purpose of the project is to enhance the coordination and management of IDP camps in Mogadishu/Afgoye corridor. This project will target 67,200 beneficiaries all the beneficiaries will be drawn from the camps in Mogadishu and the Afgoye corridor Most of the IDP camps are manned by gate keepers. The relationship to and with the IDP community is similarly diverse but in the long absence of stable Government providing IDPs with assistance and protection, informal managers have often become the primary go to person and provider of support and, to a certain extent, protection, in particular since communities of IDPs are often marginalized and discriminated against because they belong to minorities or are separated from the protection of their clans. Aid diversion has been allegedly high in informal settlements, but the extent of it remains unclear. SSWC intends to establish new community structures and strengthen the existing structures in collaboration with the local authorities in Mogadishu/Afgoye . SSWC will focus on creating coordination structures and mechanisms that help the beneficiaries’ access different services in their settlements. Communication channels will be enhanced to foster information sharing in the camps with other service providers in the targeted areas. The project will develop service mapping mechanisms to address the population movements, trends of new arrivals, exiting individuals and returnees in the IDP camps. This will be enhanced by engaging focal points in the camps, the focal points will conduct service mapping and record all the essential needs/gaps in the camps.
SSWC will conduct safety audits in the camps and share with the cluster on quarterly basis, Moreso there will be strong measures in place to collect feedback from the community within the project areas so as to know the what are the complaints and how to address them amicably.
The project will take into account all the beneficiaries irrespective of their colour, tribe and the ability. This is to enhance inclusivity of all the communities in the project area.

Project Activities

The project will have varied activities in the camps. These activities will be site management, capacity building of the camp leaders, conducting community forums, service mapping in the targeted IDP camps and providing effective structure for practical collaboration and coordination of humanitarian interventions. SSWC will conduct site improvements including road improvement, drainages, site improvement kit distributions, public light improvements benefitting IDPs living in IDP sites. SSWC will strengthen the existing coordination mechanisms and in sites where there no existing structures SSWC will establish active structures which will be governed by Standard Operating Procedures(SOPs). The structures will be identified through camp leaders and existing projects staffs.This will help in the identifying needs and duplication of services. SSWC will ensure at-least 20 community structures exist in the vast IDP settelements in Mogadishu /Afgoye corridor with specific sector task assigned to each structure. 20 groups comprising of 8 members will be trained and strengthened as part of the camp structures establishment. Establish/reinforce community mechanisms for communication channels (centres of information/centres of communication/centres of coordination) SSWC will develop communication centres in all the targeted IDP camps for essential information recording and sharing. The centres will allow the existing and new IDP arrivals to access information of the various services offered at the camps.The centres will also provide a linkage between the SSWC and the IDP residents whereby SSWC will further coordinate with other services providers in the IDPs. Its expected 5 communication centres be established in different IDP settlements in Mogadishu/Afgoye. Site information on availability of services-Creation of IM product providing information on population movement and numbers (new arrivals, exits or returns, population by site, identification of gaps, monitoring of services, mapping of or establishment of focal points) after Inventories existing tools and identify gaps in information SSWC will conduct to service mapping and monitoring and safety audits on quarterly bases. On the the hand the will be stakeholder monthly meetings that will be chaired by SSWC, deliberations will be made on where there are challenges, gaps and make decisions on the enhancement of implementation of interventions for all actors in Mogadishu/Afgoye.

SSWCalso employs extensive community consultation and feedback mechanisms to mitigate this risk.Exclusion and Marrginalization- SSWC will consider women, girls, men and boys in the project. The will be part of decision making during the process of project design, implementation and monitoring the project activities.- SSWC will seek all the beneficiaries voices by setting up feedback and complaints desks this will help in collecting feedback irrespective feedback irrespective of age and gender.-SSWC will inculcate the attitude of engaging all the gender during the sensitization campaigns so as to avoid exclusion and marginalizationProtection Mainstreaming:-SSWC will collect data that is disaggregated by sex and gender and different groups in order to ensure protection for all particularly the less disadvantaged.-SSWC Make sure that a comprehensive registration system is in place, enabling women to be registered in their own name and provisions are made for child-headed households. Please provide further explanation

The project is linked to durable solutions since it has the basis of closely working with the community in the IDP camps. The leaders together with the local authorities will be trained on the management and coordination of proper structures for their settlements. This will in turn help the community form committee members that will continue working in the camp even after the end of the intervention. More so the local authorities will be engaged through the community forums and they will be part of the participants during the training and will be invited during decision making processes.
