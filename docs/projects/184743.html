Project Summary

The project is related to conducting different activities involved on awareness raising for different stakeholders like IDPs Community Committees, Host community and government. The main activities include the following:
Awareness Campaign and Capacity building
- Provisions of trainings to build their capacities such as Camp Committee Capacity building
- Awareness campaigns for human rights and GBV
- Awareness campaigns for human rights and GBV
- Awareness campaigns for local community through media
- Consultation workshops with different stakeholders
- Behavior changes trainings for IDPs youth and Host communities
- Community mobilization and organization for leaving harmful traditional practices
- Conduct awareness sessions for IDP adolescents on human rights and conflict resolution
- Empowering and capacity building for IDPs' established organizations
- Income generating activities through workshops and trainings
