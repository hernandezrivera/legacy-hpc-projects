Project Summary

Chabash Development and Health Initiative will will address critical gaps and protection concerns within WASH sector to meet the needs of vulnerable women, men, girl and boys living in 1 out of the 11 priority humanitarian affected LGAs of Borno and Adamawa State through all components of Hygiene promotion. This intervention aims to increase access to lifesaving WaSH services for IDPs and host communities with the highest needs within one of the most underfunded sectors.
Reduction of health risks and increased dignity of affected women, men, girls and boys through provision of adequate and safe drinking water via water trucking (at least 10,000 beneficiaries) in Selected LGAs of both Borno and Adamawa state, distribution and replenishment of hygiene kits to support safe and appropriate household hygiene with focus on cholera prevention (minimum 8,000 individuals), reinforcement of community capacity on Operations and Maintenance (O&M) of WaSH facilities for their sustainability, through inclusive capacity building.

1 - Needs

There is regular shortage of latrines and showers cleaning products such as detergent and disinfectant hence it is difficult to maintain their cleanness. Furthermore, approximately 35% of households are not covered with hygiene promotion and more than 80% of IDPs families have not benefited from basic hygiene items within the last three months. In Borno and Adamawa States, despite the efforts made by humanitarian actors, the daily amount of water is still less than 4 liters per person in most congested IDPs camps while a proportion of the host communities use untreated water from unprotected sources such as open well as some of their improved water points are broken down due to lack of proper maintenance. A bacteriological analysis of a water sample from one these wells, done in September 2019, revealed a high level of fecal contamination (more than 100 UFC). Additionally, while the sanitation services coverage is significantly below the sphere standards in all the camps due to land issues, around 78% of latrines and showers existing in some of the camps and 90% of garbage pits are non-functional as they have been destroyed by windstorms and floods or filled up.

2 - Activities

The proposed activities are in line with CDHI’s humanitarian Organizational strategy and CDHI’s engagement in WASH, as well country-level strategy for 2022 focusing on an interventions in WASH . CHabash Development and Health Initiative - CDHI disposes of adequate capacities for such intervention in terms of human resources (employing its best pool of team that are result-oriented when it comes to project implementation) and in terms of in-depth experience in the proposed areas of intervention as well as quality of programing delivered. The requested grant will therefore allow CDHI to maintain its humanitarian presence in the above mentioned Locations (Borno and Adamawa states) as one of the first line National NGO responders hence contributing to provision of timely, integrated, sustainable and flexible assistance to most vulnerable as stipulated in PIN & HRP of 2022 objective 1.
