Project Summary

According to the humanitarian needs overview 2019, Over 80% of internally displaced persons in Borno state, the epicentre of the crisis, and over 60% are living in host communities, making it harder to access them with assistance and putting additional pressure on the already stretched resources of these communities necessitating provision of Integrated services including WASH, protection, FSL and MPC in these communities. The fluid security situation in Konduga local government in the last four weeks have left many within host communities in Konduga especially in of Tungushe, Amarua and Ngomari with little or nothing to hold on to, and always on the run for safety, this have also made women/girls and children more vulnerable to GBV, traumatized due to the constant need to run for safety, while the lack of livelihood options for women and men have made survival more difficult, both parties (Women and Men) have adapted wrong coping mechanisms. Men have overtime even become less accommodating while responding to challenges at home due to trauma and inability to provide basic needs. JDF seek to implement protection activities targeting vulnerable children through psychosocial support, child protection in emergencies and mainstreaming of child support and safeguarding through capacity development and mobilization at their currently existing child and women -friendly spaces within host communities in Konduga local government area with funding from Nigerian Joint response and Nigerian Humanitarian Fund with the view of meeting the protection needs of women, girls, Boys and men within this community and it's environs, through the spaces women/men/ children will receive/acess MHPSS and services, case management services and economic strengthening activities through this activities JDF will reach women and girls also with proper protection messaging that will ensure Gender based violence in all its forms is curbed, while working closely with men through community sensitization, engagements with community and religious leaders, while designing training sessions within safes spaces on Sexual reproductive health, menstrual hygiene and skill acquisition. JDF will therefore implement the following activities
1. Procurement of dignity kits for beneficiaries and survivors
2. Community cluster and forum engagements (engagement with stakeholders, religious and community leaders)
3. Sensitization
4. design and printing of IEC's to be used for sensitization and advocacy
5. Case management, PSS and PFA support for identified persons
6. Referrals to other agencies
7. Capacity strengthening for community stakeholders, religious and community leaders.
8. Skill Acquisition to provide livelihood options for women and men
JDF will implement these planned activities in coordination with other agencies while reporting on the monthly 5w's to the GBV Sector group. considering the peculiar nature of Konduga local government area, there is need to continue to address concerns around GBV in a multiple faced approach, hence the need to work with men, children women and girls. while strengthening their capacities to be positioned to respond to these challenges themselves.

1 - Needs

According to the humanitarian needs overview 2019, Over 80% of internally displaced persons in Borno state, the epicentre of the crisis, and over 60% are living in host communities, making it harder to access them with assistance and putting additional pressure on the already stretched resources of these communities necessitating provision of Integrated services including WASH, protection, FSL and MPC in these communities. JDF while working within these local government area have discovered that men who are majorly the custodian of power and mostly perpetrators of Gender based violence, be engaged actively to further buttress their importance and need to support in bringing gender based violence to an end. with the increasing displacement due to the actions of SF against Armed opposition groups many women, girls and children are exposed to forms of GBV. it has also been established that for actions against Gender based violence to be effective and sustained, religious, community leaders and Men generally must be engaged with their capacities strengthened such that they are able to better respond to these challenges within their communities, the lack/ absence of livelihood for both women/girls and men have also worsen the situation creating more tension within communities and homes. while putting women at more risk

2 - Activities

Jireh Doo Foundation (JDF)as part of efforts to ensure Gender Based violence within host communities is brought to an end will engage community structures with the aim of strengthening their response toward cultural practices and actions that encourage Gender Based Violence, focusing on actions that will bring Perpetrators to justice. JDF will work in close coordination with other actors and the Sub sector group to ensure a coordinated and a beneficiary centred approach is maintained throughout the implementation of the project, JDF will also report these activities using the 5w's to the sub sector. activities will be implemented majorly by community members and supported by JDF supervisory team. The capacity of Religious and traditional leaders will be strengthened as custodians and influencers of the culture and norms to gradually change the narrative within these locations. this will be implemented through established women friendly spaces from other funding (Nigeria Joint response and Nigeria humanitarian fund). the security situation in Konduga remains very fluid as there have been reported attacks in Tungushe, Ngomari, Amarua communities of konduga, this have made already recovering and established members of these communities to be displaced/distabilized, mostly always on the run for safety, the trauma of these events have made many women, girls, boys and men live in fear and distrust, exposing them to various forms of GBV in the quest for survival, many have adopted wrong coping mechanisms. Men have become more aggressive majorly because of the lack of livelihood options. the activities are as summarized below 1. Provision of case management , PSS and PFA support to identified survivors (women, girls, Boys and men)2. Mass community sensitization on GBV 2. Procurement and distribution of dignity kits to most vulnerable beneficiaries 3. Skill Acquisition as a means to strengthen livelihood options 4. Monthly community cluster and forum engagements 5. Capacity Strengthening for stakeholders, religious and community leaders as influencers 6. Referrals of identified cases using referral pathways to other agencies through these activities JDF will cover existing gaps and will offer outreach services to Mashamari and Boarding Camps in Konduga where no GBV actors are currently providing services.
