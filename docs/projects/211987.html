Project Summary

In 2024, Dorcas will continue its successful IYCF project in Hanano and Hellok communities, aiming to improve the nutritional status, growth, development, health, and survival of infants and young children.
The project, titled " Improving the well-being, empowering, and developing the life skills of women and young girls accessing Infant and Young Child Feeding (IYCF) services," will last for 12 months, from January 2024 to December 2024, and will focus on raising awareness of the importance of breastfeeding and recommended IYCF practices among 2000 young girls and women in Hanano/Hellok areas and surroundings. This will be achieved through awareness-raising sessions and IYCF counseling, as well as supplementary micronutrient supplementation to pregnant and lactating women to accelerate anemia reduction.
The project's main goal is to further improve the nutritional status, growth, development, health, and survival of infants and young children in Hanano and Hellok communities. To achieve this, Dorcas will continue to reinforce the recommended IYCF practices based on global guidelines (UNICEF/WHO), such as:
- The importance of maintaining good nutrition during pregnancy, Weight gain during pregnancy
Effects of malnutrition during pregnancy
- Initiate breastfeeding within 1 hour of birth
- Exclusively breastfeed for the first 6 months
- Introduce age-appropriate, adequate, safe, and properly fed complementary foods starting from 6 months up to 2 years of age, quality, and frequency of meals,
- Feeding during illness
- Continue breastfeeding for up to 2 years of age.
Dorcas will also continue to support beneficiaries with psychosocial and 500 women (from 2450) will be supported by counseling individual sessions, as well as provide pregnant women with regular medical check-ups and/or referrals. The project will take place in Hanano and Hellok community centers and will target young girls and women who are pregnant or planning to get pregnant in Hanano/Hellok areas and the surroundings.
In 2024, Dorcas aims to prevent malnutrition by sustaining high awareness levels on nutritional matters among women, providing awareness-raising sessions and IYCF counseling, and providing supplementary micronutrient supplementation to pregnant and lactating women to accelerate anemia reduction.
Dorcas will Distribute food vouchers to pregnant women, mothers with infants and people with disabilities Pregnant women, mothers with infants: 450 families People with disabilities: 450 families Total 900 families
Dorcas hopes that the project will contribute to the improvement of nutritional status, growth, development, health, and survival of infants and young children in the region.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Based on HNO 2023, In Syria, there is a worsening nutrition crisis with approximately 5.9 million people in need of nutritional assistance, an increase from 5.5 million in 2022. This includes 3.75 million children and 2.1 million women. Malnutrition is established in multiple forms, including chronic malnutrition (high levels of stunting), acute malnutrition, and anaemia among children and women. Aggravating factors include food insecurity, high food prices, water shortage, disease, and loss of household income. Stunting affects 609,979 children under five years old, with long-term consequences on individuals and societies. Acute malnutrition affects 363,556 children under five years old, with varying prevalence rates across different regions. Maternal malnutrition is also a concern, affecting 544,752 pregnant and lactating women. The situation is further complicated by emerging evidence of a double-fold increase in severe and moderate wasting among children.
