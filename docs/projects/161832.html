Project Summary

With the inadequate levels of access to basic services and public infrastructure relative to the size of the population, limited income, livelihood opportunities, and increased resource demands in Borno, Adamawa and Yobe state, GOALPrime Organization Nigeria aimed to improve and strengthen livelihood capacity and self-reliance of internally displaced persons living in host community, IDPs in camps, and other vulnerable persons in the host communities of Maiduguri, Monguno, Jere and Magumeri LGA of Borno state, through creation of employment opportunities to engage 2400 conflict affected women, men, girls and boys on cash-for-work activities prioritizing women and girls headed households and livelihood skill training.
This project is designed to contribute to sector strategic objective one (SO1) that focuses on Supporting Livelihood stabilization of conflict affected people through providing cash-for-work to engage 200 young women and girls headed households and special needs persons, vulnerable men and boys on environmental sanitation and waste disposal activities, Emergency employment opportunities for the rehabilitation of community infrastructures, such as community marketplaces, community drainage and water supply systems, and small earth dams, cleaning of blocked drainages, to ensure that a reasonable level of environmental cleanliness is achieved, improve the living standards of vulnerable women, men, girls and boys and promote GBV risk mitigation and prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse of the vulnerable women and girls as well as promoting economic activities of the communities in Jere, Maiduguri, Mongono and Magumeri LGAs.
Most of the displaced persons are without livelihood skills. GOALPrime Organization Nigeria shall identify 200 young women, girls, men and Boys and people with special need who were not captured in the cash for work activities and provide local context livelihood skill training that will enable them to produce marketable products and services within their communities to promote economic activities of the community. GOALPrime shall conduct local context mapping of the marketable product and services in each of the LGAs to adapt the livelihood skill training to the local and cultural context of each LGAs in terms of what is the most marketable product and services in each of the LGAs, and also provide small grants to women, and men that benefited from the livelihood skills training.
GOALPrime Organization Nigeria shall constitute project committee that involved community and camp stakeholders and other minority groups represented to provide a platform for feedback and concerns on the project implementation process. GOALPrime also shall Provide toll-free phone to targets communities and IDP camps to facilitate beneficiaries’ feedback on the project. GOALPrime Organization Nigeria shall coordinate with the early Recover and Livelihood sector and partners to provide solutions to concerns identified that is beyond the capacity of GOALPrime.

1 - Needs

At present, across Borno, Adamawa and Yobe states, assessments indicate inadequate levels of access to basic services and public infrastructure relative to the size of the population, limited income, and livelihoodopportunities, and increased resource demands. This situation has led to societal tensions between IDPs, returnees and host communities and resulted in increased hardship for 5.6 million people (comprising1.8 million girls; 1.5 million boys; 1.2 million women and 1.1 million men) across the three states. Prior to the crisis, poverty, development and socio-economic conditions in north-east Nigeria were very low with relative poverty recorded at 76 per cent in 2010. The conflict has further aggravated the situation. Onein four conflict-affected households report having no economic opportunity and 63 percent of vulnerable households within the most conflict-affected areas in the northeast are headed by women and girls. Over650 public buildings have been completely destroyed or dysfunctional. The conflict also resulted in more than 400,000 damaged and destroyed housing units, 95 per cent of which are located in Borno State. The cost of damage attributable to the conflict has been significant across Borno ($6.9 billion), Yobe ($1.2 billion) and Adamawa ($829 million) states.GOALPrime Organization Nigeria through this project aimed to provide cash for work related activities to engage vulnerable women, men and girls living in IDP camps to improve their living standards and promote economic activities in the communities of Jere, Maiduguri, Monguno and magumeri.

2 - Activities

1. Community and camp mapping to identify community and camp environmental sanitation situation across the camps in Jere, Maiduguri Monguno and Magumeri, 2. Identification and training/orientation of 120 young women and adolescents headed household, special need persons, 80 vulnerable men and boys living the camp and community on environmental sanitation across the four LGAs, 3. Consultation with community stakeholders of each of the LGAs to understand the existing sanitation structure, routing and involvement of the community members and to initiate the concept of the project and seek harmonization and corporation to strengthen the existing sanitation structure in the camp, 4. Environmental sanitation and waste disposal management in IDP camps and host community using the trained 120 young women and girls headed household, and vulnerable men and boys across the Maiduguri, Jere, Monguno and Magumeri LGA, 5. Local vocational skill mapping in each of the four LGAs,6. Identification and profiling of 200 young women and girls headed households, vulnerable men and boys for vocational training, financial empowerment and Mentoring, 7. Consultation with Bank of Industry on how to work out soft loan for 100 Identified young women and girls headed household, vulnerable boys and girls in Jere, Maiduguri, Monguno and Magumeri LGAs, 8. Training of identified and profiled young women and girls headed households, vulnerable men and women in vocational training thriving in the local context, 9. Continuous hygiene promotion in the camps or community within the period of the project with the camp members to promote good hygiene practice in the camps across Jere, Maiduguri, Monguno and Magumeri LGAs.

The identity of complainants shall remain confidential unless otherwise requested by the complainant or feedback provider.
