Project Summary

According to HNO the crisis enters its seventh year, there are now 5.8 million children and youth from pre-school to secondary-age (including more than 100,000 Palestine refugee children) and over 300,000 education personnel in need of education assistance inside Syria. Therefore the aim of this project is to support the educational activities in Idlib, Aleppo by providing 13738 students (5795 boys & 7943 girls) (10738 formal students and 3000 nonformal students) with the chance to continue their studies and Improve quality and relevance of formal education within a protective environment in this respect, the project will support 18 schools13738 students (5795 boys & 7943 girls) (10738 formal students and 3000 nonformal students) and 526 staff (312 Male & 214 female) in 18 schools by organizing the following activities:
- Conduct back to learning campaigns and communities mobilization in support of education.
- provide formal education for 10738 students in 18 schools.
- provide formal and non-formal education for 3000 students in 18 schools.
- provide Psycho-social support (PSS) to10738 formal students in 18 schools
- provide educational materials for schools, teachers, and students.
- Covering the salaries costs of the staff and administrative expenditure of school running.
- provide training for the staff to increase their technical capacity.
- Organizing educational activities within and outside the school curriculum.

Needs

According to HNO the crisis enters its seventh year, there are now 5.8 million children and youth from pre-school to secondary-age (including more than 100,000 Palestine refugee children) and over 300,000 education personnel in need of education assistance inside Syria. While new EMIS132 data for the school year 2016/17 is currently under review, an estimated 1.75 million children, or almost one-third of school-age children (aged 5-17 years) from the 2015/16 school-year are out of school and a further 1.35 million are at risk of dropping out. Around 180,000 education personnel, including teachers, left the education system, which has negatively affected the quality of education for all children. Hostilities have seen over one in three schools damaged, destroyed, no longer accessible or occupied for shelter and other purposes. Schools, accounting for an estimated 33 percent of collective centers, were reported as the most common building type used for IDP sites in Syria.133 Increased internal displacement has put the current infrastructure under enormous pressure and highlighted the need for school rehabilitation.We have received information and secondary data from the education complexes in northern (Idlib and Aleppo) who are the local education authorities, they mentioned there are 300 schools are not supported.According to the analyzing of the need assessment conducted by WATAN team, we selected 18 schools to be targeted by our interventions (11 schools in Idlib and 7 schools in Aleppo) and the number of students 13738 (5795 boys & 7943 girls) * 11 schools in Idlib as follows:1 - Implementation of activities

WATAN will implement the project directly through its field team, the project period is 12 months, The implementation plan will be divided in several phases:1- Establish field coordination office:WATAN will establish field coordination office, and recruit a specialized team (field coordinator - field M&E - field logistic - psycho-social specialist supervisor), the field team will supervise all project operations, and they will supervise and follow the daily implementation works, reports, and communicate with the administrative team in the head office in Gaziantep.2- Maintenance and rehabilitate the schools.The rehabilitate will include (Sanitation, Water tanks, wall paint, Repairing doors and windows, and electricity, and the needed construction works)WATAN will work on ensure a good quality and quantity of safe drinking water by set-up water tanks, prepare pipe network, Washbasins, and keep it clean and healthy, in addition to maintenance the sewage to ensure safe access to WASH facilities such as: latrines, sewage disposal in a healthy way for beneficiaries and environment.3- prepare and equip the schools:We will work to prepare and equip the centers with:- Provide schools with (furniture – laptops – projectors - printers – educational materials,…)- Set the school manual for security, safety, bylaw.- Conducting job interviews, recruitment the operational project team, hire the needed staff of teachers and administrators according to the policies, procedures, expectations, and vision for the education directorate depending on the context.4- Operation:- Conduct back to learning campaigns and communities mobilization in support of education.- Conduct placement test (ACER) for children to determine their levels and their educational needs.- Register the students and distribute them into groups according to their educational abilities.- Prepare the curriculum books for the students and distribute it.- provide formal and non-formal education for students.- Conduct structured PSS, extracurricular, and recreational activities (sport, art, Agricultural, and cultural activities) through the enrolling period.- Conduct mid-exams and final exams.- Covering the salaries costs of the staff and administrative expenditure of school running.5- Staff Training:The staff will be provided by 2 training, the trainings will focus on pedagogical and psych-social issues, all teachers and psychologists will be trained. 1- Design Thinking.2- Learning Through Play.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

The Assumptions and Mitigating for the risks in the project are:- Changes in the security situation, particularly in project target areas, affects the safety/mobility/ displacement of beneficiaries/staff and results in being unable to participate in project. This includes the risk of targeted attacks and bombing of schools/ communities and the risks of mines, airstrikes and cross fire and the solution will be to mitigate it is Closely monitor the security situation in coordination with partners and relevant stakeholders and conduct a security assessments and also Communication with donors to accept flexibility and support relocation of activities to safer spaces whenever needed.- Risk of students Dropping out of schools Ways to overcome are Motivate students through psychosocial support and recreational activities and providing awareness for students about education and forming Parent Teacher Associations (PTA) in each school- Risk of long distance and young children walking alone. Children may get lost, be attacked or lose interest in learning and the solution will be to mitigate it. is providing transportation to students and safety training for organization, students and school staff.- Resistance to gender mainstreaming from male leadership structures the solution will be to mitigate it is projects will benefit all members of the community and will always seek prior agreement and active involvement of the traditional leadership structure.- Closure of borders risking delay in transporting goods and supplies and the solution will be to mitigate it is INGOs work with partners that are known to and authorized by governments relevant departments to operate cross border. Partners will establish strong relations with relevant government actors and need to strengthen coordination with other INGOS on the same issue.- Insufficient safety and health standards at schools the solution will be to mitigate it is Security/safety training for organization, students and school staff and Do-no-harm training for organization staff. - Limited number of beneficiaries in the program, which might cause inter-community tension and pressure on implementing staff and the solution will be to mitigate it is Set a clear-cut selection criterion and inform the community of limited resources and the selection criteria.-The schools are overburden because of the Hight no of displacement that is recently happened: Watan has developed an emergency plan in case of increased no of children who want to join the school and trying to fund more schools to absorb the no of newly displaced children. - Risks of sexual exploitation and abuse & Gender-based violence1- WATAN has a strong feedback system where children and adults can raise a complaint, WATAN responds to complain in 48 hours. 2- All staff signed on the code of conduct and child safeguarding policy.3- All WATAN staff received protection training.
