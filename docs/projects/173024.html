Project Summary

The project in line with the nutrition sector strategic objectives that aiming to provide equitable life saving intervention in the geographical priority areas. The project targeting vulnerable communities, IDPs, returnees and host communities in total (475,940 )under localities identified by the nutrition sector with severity ranking 4 (extreme need) and 5 (catastrophic need) . These localities have a GAM and SAM rate ranking of either very high or severe. Nutrition intervention will cover treatment of Acute Malnutrition and preventive services of IYCF, provision of nutrition supplies, training of volunteers, case identification and follow up, strengthen the community awareness of causes, danger signs and treatment of acute malnutrition, promoting positive health and nutrition behavior change. SC integrated services delivery based on need and is inclusive and non-discriminatory. Nutrition staff will reflect the gender, ethnic, and linguistic balance of the beneficiary population. Enforced policies prevent and respond to any breach of SC's Child Safeguarding Policy. The total budget proposed for this project is 3,231,688 USD .

1 - Needs

After a year of political change, humanitarian needs continue to rise. More than 23 per cent of the population – will need humanitarian assistance in 2021. The transitional government is prioritizing peace and ending the economic crisis, issues closely intertwined with the drivers of humanitarian need in the country. Nutrition needs were exacerbated by political instability and the economic downturn including high inflation rates, devaluation of the local currency and fuel shortages .Based on data available (S3M II 2018) 36.6 per cent of children less than five years of age are stunted .The national prevalence rate of Global Acute Malnutrition (GAM) was reported at 14.1 per cent . Severe wasting measured by weight-for-height Z-score was 2.72 per cent nationally ; Among localities, 56 of the 185 localities had a severe acute malnutrition (SAM) rate, defined as WHZ wasting prevalence of over 3 per cent. The national S3M survey 2018, revealed low awareness of appropriate child feeding practices in all states. The national exclusive breast-feeding prevalence was 62.31 per cent, with 73.29 per cent age-appropriate continued breast-feeding up to 2 years of age or beyond, 63.24 per cent age-appropriate meal frequency and 25.40 per cent age-appropriate dietary diversity. the factor negatively impacting the malnutrition rates are gaps in community-based management of acute malnutrition (CMAM) services in the state. Low food production and unaffordability of food in the markets also increases communities’ risks of malnutrition. The Darfur State Emergency Profile also cites lack of nutrition awareness in communities as another main issue leading to malnutrition. according to sector projection for calculation of target. the nutrition sector estimated the target that over 570,871 children require treatment for severe acute malnutrition (SAM) and the operational costs of reaching these children are rising. An additional 2.4 million children need moderate acute malnutrition treatment.

2 - Activities/Outputs

In line with the SC country Office strategy and nutrition sector strategy SC will inspire the breakthrough that no child dies from preventable causes before their fifth birthday by delivering high impact child survival interventions; i.e. quality management of acute malnutrition and infant and young child feeding in emergencies. SCI will respond to the basic needs of IDPs, returnees and host communities while increasing their self-reliance and improving their nutrition status and resilience. Provision of lifesaving nutrition service for acute malnourished children (boys and girls 6-59 months of age) and PLWs .Key activities under nutrition will be active monthly screening for acute malnutrition, establishment of referral mechanisms between the community and the Community Management of Acute Malnutrition ( CMAM) sites, Outpatient Therapeutic Program (OTP), Stabilization/Inpatient Care (S/IC), SC will support management of moderate acute manultrion through TSFP services . implementation of infant and young child feeding in emergency ,behavior change activities, provision of technical support and mentorship to Ministry of Health and health workers to ensure quality of care in CMAM services as well as capacity building of staff and community health workers in their respective roles in the CMAM program. on-the-job training and mentorship on effective management of acute malnutrition will be adopted according to the CMAM guidelines. Will address the gender and age speciﬁc concerns as a crosscutting approach throughout the design, implementation and capacity building and resilience mechanism, The environmental issues will be considered through monitoring of water sources, waste management and sanitation as the main environmental issues affecting health and nutrition status of target communities.
