Project Summary

The Cameroon INGO Forum (CHINGO) was created in June 2019 with the aim of strengthening collaboration and coordination between INGOs and external stakeholders (line ministries, UN agencies, donors, civil society organizations) involved in the humanitarian response to populations affected by the three ongoing crises in Cameroon.
The INGO Forum aims at strengthening information sharing between INGOs, in particular to support the coordination of operations and build common positions, improve INGO representation and joint advocacy, and contribute to the overall humanitarian coordination architecture. The project comprises various services to INGOs, including training for INGO and partner staff, sharing of experiences and lessons learned, and facilitation and support on issues of common interest. CHINGO also engages with external stakeholders and provides a collective voice for INGOs as well as an entry point for requests to NGOs. Advocacy and communication activities aim to foster a better understanding of the assistance provided by INGOs in Cameroon, identify and propose solutions to the challenges faced by INGOs in humanitarian operations, and increase the visibility of crises in Cameroon with the overall objective of better meeting the humanitarian needs of populations affected by displacement. Finally, the INGO Platform is strengthening the active participation of its members on the issues of Localization (as part of the Localization Working Group), NEXUS (as a member of the NEXUS Task Force) and Accountability (as a member of the Accountability to Affected Population (AAP) Working Group).

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

INGO’s action in Cameroon is severely limited by unique access problems resulting from a combination of insecurity caused by various armed groups and physical access constraints, administrative obstacles at central and field level, limited visibility of Cameroonian crises and insufficient funding, difficult coordination with three different coordination architectures in the country, and limited human resources available within INGOs to allocate sufficient time to coordination issues.The INGO forum collectively addresses these challenges. CHINGO's support is essential for INGOs as it supports a coordinated and effective INGO response to humanitarian needs, and enables coherent representation and common messages to be shared across the global coordination architecture. CHINGO also contributes to improving the capacities of INGOs and their partners, to ensure that humanitarian principles are respected in the delivery of assistance and that humanitarian space is preserved in this difficult context.Legally and administratively hosted by the Norwegian Refugee Council (NRC), CHINGO provides certain basic services to its 23 members and 3 observers, in terms of information sharing (efficient and transparent sharing of information relevant to its members, coordination between INGOs (sharing of lessons learned, best practices, identification of common interests and priorities) and with external stakeholders, representation of its members' interests in external coordination mechanisms with key stakeholders, including donors, the United Nations, government, Cameroonian civil society organizations, to ensure INGO participation in critical decision-making platforms, joint advocacy and evidence-based partnerships and capacity building.This project is expected to contribute to the achievement of the following results:1. CHINGO members collectively advocate on key issues of common concern, engage more effectively in response coordination and leadership, and help ensure that humanitarian operating space is maintained to meet the needs of those most affected by the various crises in Cameroon.2. CHINGO gains the acceptance of donors, authorities, civil society and the Cameroonian population, in order to improve their access to humanitarian aid, program continuity and unhindered service delivery.To achieve these results, the project will implement the following activities:1. Organize internal CHINGO coordination meetings: monthly plenary meetings of country directors to define common positions on key issues, quarterly steering committee meetings to provide strategic direction for CHINGO, monthly working group meetings (support issues & quality programming), coordination of INGOs at regional/field level (North-West, South-West, East and Far-North regions).2. Actively participate in meetings of the Humanitarian Country Team, the Intersectoral Working Group, the Localization, AAP Working Group, the Nexus Task Force, and any other external meetings relevant to CHINGO's mandate.3. Systematize the dissemination of all information gathered, including all meeting minutes and documents from all stakeholders.4. Hold regular meetings with representatives of national authorities to ensure mutual understanding of issues relating to humanitarian aid.5. Organize training sessions on humanitarian principles, HEIST, humanitarian access negotiations and any other relevant training for CHINGO members and their partners (including national CSOs).6. Produce fact sheets and position papers on the humanitarian context in Cameroon and the situation/perspective of INGOs.7. Organize advocacy initiatives (roundtables, press releases, position papers and short documentaries) and develop communication tools on the actions of CHINGO members.8. Organize roundtables with target stakeholders (ministries, donors, diplomatic community).
