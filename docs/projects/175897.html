Project Summary

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project’s “protection by presence” approach is strongly community based and integrates strengthening and expanding existing actions and supporting government-led protection and MHPSS services, incl. through the deployment of integrated teams, site and community-based community activists, referring and providing direct assistance to the most vulnerable IDPs, ensuring that the government and all CCCM actors have the capacity to comply with all minimum protection standards at accommodation, transit, relocation and resettlement sites. IOM’s direct interventions under this project cover the first three levels of the MHPSS pyramid, with some referrals to specialised services (the strengthening of which is included in IOM’s Health Cluster proposal). By reinforcing the protection-related indicators of the DTM, the humanitarian community will gain additional insight to protection/MHPSS risks and needs which will eventually improve evidence-based protection/MHPSS programming. Based on the results of IOM’s safety audits, the DTM and protection monitoring, as well as the HNO, IOM will target host family and site-based IDPs, and particularly support the most vulnerable, such as elderly, women and child headed household, victims of violence and GBV, human trafficking and people with disabilities. Host families and communities, whose coping capacities and resilience are affected by the presence of IDPs, may also be targeted. Recipients of direct assistance, incl. victims of human trafficking, will be selected jointly with the social services based on established vulnerabililty criteria and their individual assistance plan developed with their and the beneficiaries’ active participation.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

1. Deployment of integrated Protection/MHPSS teams with strong community based support, which raise awareness and facilitate access to protection/MHPSS information, services and referrals for specializes care and assistance by IOM, government and NGOs. The teams’ coverage will be expanded to districts with high influx of new IDPs, incl. in Nampula. 2. Provision of direct assistance and counselling to the most vulnerable IDPs, incl. people with disabilities, victims of GBV or trafficking 3. Mainstreaming protection into CCCM and strengthening protection/MHPSS services at site level through safety audits; capacity building, incl. on protection in CCCM, PSEA and psychological first aid (PFA); support to protection desks, deployment of site-based community activists able to provide information, PFA and referrals, support to government service provision 4. Data collection: support to multi-sectoral protection assessments and safety audits, strengthening DTM based protection data collection and analysis 5. Support to government protection/MHPSS institutions with infrastructural, material, equipment, technical or logistical assistance to re-establish operations and access hard to reach communities 6. Building the capacity of government and non-governmental protection actors on human trafficking in emergencies; protection in camp management, community based MHPSS and PSEA

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

IOM employs an integrated protection/MHPSS approach which ensures intersectional considerations of protection and MHPSS needs as well linkages with health programming in a whole-of-community approach. IOM identifies individual and structural-level protection risks thorugh outreach to IDPs thorugh its activists and by conducting regular protection/safety audits in the districts. In the provision referrals and assistance.IOM’s community-based activists are trained to identify vulnerable groups, incl. among children and adolescents and the elderly, refer them to available services, advocate and assist them to gain equal access to available humanitarian aid and protection services. IOM follows strict data protection rules and assistance standards in order to ensure meaningful access, not to cause further harm and empower inidviduals and communities assisted.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

IOM conducts community based rapid assessments in targeted communities and settlements, to map and assess populations main needs, vulnerabilities, resources, expectations and capacities. This approach ensures that the project activities are tailored and respond to the needs of the communities and that community members can participate in project design and implementation.As part of these assessments, IOM also conducts mapping of available services for MHPSS, Protection and Health essential services, their respective capacities and gaps, to ensure that the project strengthens overall capacity of services providers to ensure continuity and sustainability of services. All IOM staff at all levels receives PSEA trainings soon after entry and the Linha Verde mechanism is promoted at most community-based awareness interventions. IOM’s protection team works closely with the CCCM Cluster to ensure that affected population’s feedback is timely addressed and leads to practical and visible improvements of protection and safety related issues at site level. IOM always strived to ensure that children’s opinions are heard and taken into account.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

IOM Mobile Teams and community-based protection activists identify and refer people with special needs including individuals with physical and mental disabilities to adequate services. IOM’s trained MHPSS professional are able to provide counseling and mitigate some mental health impact of displacement. IOM pays special attention to ensuring that persons with disability, incl. albinism, are protected from human trafficking, abuse and exploitation. under the integrated health-protection-MHPSS activities.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Proposed project does not include any construction work or other activities with significant environmental impact. In IOM’s community-based MHPSS activities strive to use environmentally friendly, recycled or reusable materials, and respect for the environment is included in some awareness raising sessions with communities.
