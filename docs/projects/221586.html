Project Summary

PRONANAC EDUCACIONAL is committed to providing emergency safe and sustainable WASH solutions in some districts of Cabo Delgado province, northern Mozambique, focusing on disadvantaged areas such as Muidumbe, Mocimboa da Praia, Palma, Nangade, Quissanga and Metuge, in collaboration with various sectors. The initiative, aligned with the WASH Cluster and response plans, includes manual water points, hygiene and sanitation promotion, and local production of concrete slabs. Operations span multiple locations, reaching IDPs, host communities and returnees with solutions tailored to their specific needs, emphasizing partnerships with artisans and local people for localized capacity building. The effort encompasses rehabilitation and construction of water points, community engagement in the construction of family latrines with menstrua hygiene bath units, and direct construction of emergency latrines, distribution of concrete slabs , promotion of emergency-adapted CLTS and hygiene training, targeting crisis-affected communities, returnees and internally displaced people in these project target districts.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

PRONANAC EDUCACIONAL aims to deliver a minimum emergency WASH package to internally displaced people (IDPs) and returnees in Cabo Delgado. Using local knowledge and multi-sectoral expertise, PRONANAC EDUCACIONAL adopts an approach that encompasses ensuring safe access to water, ife-saving sanitation and hygiene promotion. This involves several emergency response activities, such as rapid rehabilitating or improving water points in affected communities, installing emergency latrines and support communities in the construction of family latrines through the distribution of latrine slabs. The initiative also includes critical hygiene promotion efforts in affected communities, the distribution of hygiene kits and the implementation of the emergency-adapted Community-Led Total Sanitation (CLTS) process, adapted in the humanitarian context. The overall goal for these initiatives is to reach 41,020 individuals in the six (6) districts targeted by this project.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

PRONANAC EDUCACIONAL, in coordination with the Protection cluster, ensures the integration of protection aspects into all activities, emphasizing staff training and community worker education. The organization prioritizes safety and protection to prevent GBV, providing culturally sensitive WASH services to vulnerable populations. Accessibility concerns are addressed for individuals with disabilities, chronic illnesses, the elderly, and marginalized individuals. PRONANAC EDUCACIONAL’s design strategy emphasizes the integration of protection, safety, and dignity, conducting a multisectoral risk analysis, promoting equitable access, participation, and transparency. PRONANAC EDUCACIONAL is committed to training employees in protection, humanitarian norms, and principles to prevent harm and maintain ethical and safety standards in the execution of activities.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

PRONANAC EDUCACIONAL guarantees significant participation by involving women and the elderly from the initial stages, through focus group discussions. They will be involved in designing and coordinating the WASH programme in the community, and actions such as committing to a minimum 50% representation on water and sanitation committees and hygiene promotion teams will empower women and elders in the community.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Throughout the project cycle, PRONANAC EDUCACIONAL ensures active participation and soliciting of ideas and opinions from people with disabilities. The team actively involves people with disabilities in the design of WASH infrastructure, incorporating suggestions for improving access, such as handrails, seating or ramps. PRONANAC EDUCACIONAL disseminates disability-related information to the community, improving understanding of needs, and shares progress with the WASH group for continued input on WASH inclusion. To remove barriers, a community survey will determine the number of people with disabilities.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

PRONANAC EDUCACIONAL ensures environmentally friendly solutions with locally sourced materials for WASH activities, promoting a system of reduction, recycling and reuse. Rapid environmental screening and impact assessments will be carried out in multiple locations, aligning with country regulations and maintaining coordination with relevant authorities.
