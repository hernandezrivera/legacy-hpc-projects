Project Summary

This project intends to strengthen the capacity of vulnerable boys and girls to prevent abuse against children in their communities. The project will target the following areas: Seleea, Tamor, Goz Laban, Aarafa in the locality of Jebel Moon; And Fwfw, Bit Mora, Habila, Keny in the locality of Habila. SAO will mobilise 4000 boys and girls and train them as peer educators to lead peer education activities on child rights; help seeking behaviour and safe access to services that address consequences of child related abuses. SAO will work with children and other humanitarian actors to develop behavior change messages and well as Information , education and communication messages on child rights, child protection, as well as gender equality. SAO will target schools and Khalwas (An informal education which is widely spread in Sudan) . SAO will raise awareness on sexual abuse/exploitation identification and prevention through workshops, lectures and theatrical sketches. SAO will also select a number of individuals and form them into groups to serve as advocates, and participate in activities and facilitate current and future interventions.

Needs

The two target localities of Habila and Jebel Moon together has a total sector PiN of 13,882 And both localities has vulerbility indicator of ‘4’. Child sexual exploitation is widely spread in post-conflict areas and very rarely reported to concerned Government Department due to feel of shame or ignorance of legal procedure or due to threat from abusers. Many children in the selected localities go to Khalwa rather than schools (Literally Kalwa means a place of privacy. But in Sudanese culture it refers to a place of learning Holly Qura'an and its Islamic Studies. It is an informal education which is widely spread in Sudan but very intensively in Darfur. Historically Darfur inhabitants prefer to send their children to Kawla rather than to schools as they believes that Kawala is a religious place where child will grow as strict Muslim). In some Khalwas, children live in poor primitive hostels, where all age groups are living together. From our surveys we found a severe sexual abuse from elder students and sometimes from teachers and in most cases parents are not aware of that. In some cases children at Khalwa were forced to perform hard work for their master's interest. Children often are unable to defend themselves or speak about the issue due to lack of awareness and because of the shame and stigma that accompanies sexual acts in their communities.

Activities

The program will contain the following activities :- 1/ Workshops on ‘how children can defend themselves’ against sexual abuse/exploitation. 2/Workshops for parents to encourage them to report any type of sexual harassment and highlight the laws that criminalize it, and illustrate the legal procedures. 3/ Theatrical short sketches, to illustrate in a child-friendly way, types of harassment methods in society, and how children can protect themselves from it.4/ Distribution of gifts with printed material that contains child-friendly stories and illustrations to raise overall awareness on the issue. 5/ Training workshops to educate teachers, social workers, parents and nurses to be able to early identify sexually abused/exploited children and provide immediate help. 6/ Selection of local advocates (boys, girls and adults). to be able to continuously raise awareness on the issue. 7/Selected advocates will be formed in groups. And will receive intensive training to become peer educators and to participate in implementing activites. Selected groups will be trained to become references to facilitate future interventions, and to help with reporting after the project period. -- Before the implementation of the main activities, some procedures will be conducted in order to ensure accountability and dignity for beneficiaries :- a/Consultation of benficaries and stake holders in preliminary visits. this will include explanation of the activities in detail. b/ Involvement of community leaders like Hakamas and tribe's Sheiks.
