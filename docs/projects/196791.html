Project Summary

3 - Needs

The Multi-Sectoral Needs Assessment Report highlights that the most significant threats and risks for children in the region include child labor (61% - 85%B, 15%G), school dropout (40% 56%B, 44%G). Additional risks such as violence, physical hazards, war remnants/UXOs, and early marriage also pose a concern (10%). According to the North west Syria CP Situation Monitoring Dashboard 2022, a significant portion of key informants (KIs) reported that children in the area require psycho-social support services (78.49%). Additionally, many KIs noted that children are prevented from attending school due to their engagement in work (86.29%). A significant percentage of KIs also reported that children are facing separation from their usual caregivers (36.17%). The Voices from Syria 2020 Report and WoS GBV sub-cluster also indicated that psychological/emotional abuse (34%) and physical assault (29%) are prevalent forms of gender-based violence (GBV) in the region. Denial of resources and opportunities (24%), rape and sexual assault (10%), and early/forced marriages (4%) are also reported. A recent needs assessment conducted by IRD identified a high need for protection services, particularly in Widows' Camps. IRD aims to address these needs through the provision of child protection, GBV prevention and response, and GP response services. (The resources are from HNO 2023, VOICES 2022 and IhsanRD need assessment.)

4 - Implementation of activities

IhsanRD will provide CP, GBV, GP, MA services. These include Provision of CP services, such as PSS, CM, and recreational kits distribution to in need children regardless of their gender, through establishing CFSs to engage children in activities that support positive psycho-social well-being and coping strategies. Mobile teams will also provide outreach activities and target communities with CP services. Provision of GBV services, such as life skills, vocational training, PSS sessions, and income-generating, targeting both boys and girls through the WGSSs and outreach teams. Establishing mobile teams to provide protection emergency response in emergency situations, including information sharing, referrals, surveys and assessments in coordination with the protection cluster. All protection services will be accessible to persons with disabilities. IhsanRD is the protection emergency response focal point in Jebel Seman district. Monthly meetings will be held at both Syria and Turkey level to share information and community-based component is an important part of the project. An M&E plan for CP activities is in place to include: regular monitoring of child-friendly spaces, surveys to assess child participation and well-being and to ensure appropriate support is provided to children. Evaluation of the program's impact on the community and children's well-being through the use of quantitative and qualitative data.For MA activities: (EORE sessions, RE sessions, Capacity building)

8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

IhsanRD adopts a zero-tolerance approach towards sexual exploitation and abuse.And it is committed to the prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse both within IhsanRD and within the framework of IhsanRD programs and their beneficiary.To this aim, IhsanRD’s Policy Against Sexual Exploitation and Abuse: • Encourages prevention; • Promotes detection; • Sets out a straightforward investigation procedure; • Sets out a mechanism of sanctions IhsanRD fully upholds principles of freedom of speech and protection of victims of sexual abuse and exploitation. As such, IhsanRD has developed mechanisms that can contribute to preventing sexual exploitation and abuse such as • Signing of the Code of Conduct by the staff in order to clarify unacceptable behavior and in particular sexual exploitation and abuse; • Information to the beneficiaries, staff, and all stakeholders about the existing Complaints and Response Mechanisms - CRM which can allow them to send their concerns and complaints in a confidently way without fear • Implementation of training and awareness sessions to train and empower the teams, particularly for staff exposed to working with minors or the most vulnerable population; • Identify risk factors on an ongoing basis and in particular throughout the project cycle; • Put in place evaluation procedures for IhsanRD staff, partners, and stakeholders by carrying out due diligence, particularly with regard to their ethical commitments and past behaviors.
