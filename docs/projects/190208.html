Project Summary

Within the initial two weeks of displacement in April, the Oromia and SNNP regional authorities agreed to facilitate the return of the I DPs to their respective areas of origin and conduct relief and rehabilitation efforts in areas of origin. Despite peace and reconciliation!' efforts led by traditional elders, there were continued reports of secondary displacements of those IDPs who were earlier returned. The security situation has been challenging despite the deployment of the Ethiopian Defense Forces in the area, with reports of continued destruction of houses, other assets and service infrastructure. The Federal Government has set up a commission to look at the challenges arising from the current regional boundaries and explore better ways of addressing them and has committed to work with Regional Government s to work promote reconciliation efforts. Source OCHA this project is aimed to enhance access to services for internal displaced people, returnees and host community in Gedeo zone and Konso and Alie Woredas of SNNPR and Guji Zone of Oromia regional states. The Ethiopian government is working to bring about peace and security. This process and the support for early return of displaced people are priorities for all. And all families have returned home to Gedeo and West Guji and Konso and Alie Woredas; but many houses have been destroy that is why this additional shelter is needed
Following the ethnic conflict between the Guji and Gedeo people in April and June 2018, an estimated 800,000 people were displaced in Gedeo and Guji zones. In June of this year, UNHCR received a request by the Resident Coordinator and OCHA to assume partial responsibility for the delivery of NFls, to those displaced as a result of the above-mentioned conflict and to provide advice and support to the government on site management. Based on the Inter Agency Standing Committee/ IASC/ responsibility in terms of protection as well as the endorsement of UNHCR's engagement by the Humanitarian Coordinator, UNHCR deployed Emergency Response Team in Dilla in SNNPR and Buie Hora in Oromia to provide protection support to the IDP operation and play a more robust role, particularly in the area of protection. In addition to that Konso and Alie were two of the woredas under the former Segen Peoples' Area zones. However, since the last two years Konso was granted zona l administration status and Alie remained as special woreda. They are located to the south of SNNPR. The livelihood of people living in Konso and Alie are largely dependent on crop production and livestock rearing is also widely practiced.
The security situation in Konso and neighboring woredas is not stable since Konso was granted zona l administration and the cause of the conflict is related with competition for resource in the border areas and the question of self-administration. The recent conflict was erupted in the third week of July and further worsened in the 3rd week of November 2020 which actually has claimed death of lives and resulted in displacement of many people from kebeles of Konso, Alie and Derashe.
The cause of the conflict between Konso and Alie was competition for the Kukubasal forest and grazing land. Both are claiming that area as their own land and expansion for farm lands co ntinued from both sides and will remain as a potential cause of conflict unless the government intervenes and resolve the situation by demarcating the boundaries.There was a question of self-administration by some kebeles in Segen area; which is not accepted by the regional government and it has become the source of conflict between kebeles of Segen area with the neighboring kebeles of Konso, Amaro and Burji and resulted in displacement of many people and damage of property.
The other conflict around Konso was the conflict between two kebeles of Gato which are under the Derashe special l woreda that extended to Fuchucha Kebeles of Konso and resulted in damage of many houses and properties and
