Project Summary

The project aims to support formal and non-formal education for 79000 (38710 B, 40290 G) dropped-out children (boys – girls)or who are at risk of dropping out of school through supporting 203 schools which their fund will be ended. The project also works on expanding schools' capacity so that they are able to receive more displaced children and to reduce classroom crowding as a response to the Covid-19 pandemic.
-During 20-21 academic year, formal and non-formal education will be provided in targeted 203 schools as follow:
Provision 203 schools (one class each school) of two-cycle non-formal education through catch-up classes for 5000 out of schools’ children (boys – girls) students using curriculum B, cycle's duration is 4 months. Students who pass the ASER test will be given a certificate of completion so that they can continue their learning process as formal students
-Supporting formal education in 203 schools for 74000 children by providing operational costs, learning and teaching materials, schools’ supplies, and heating materials, in addition to providing weekly PSS sessions to overcome the harsh experience of war conditions and to motivate them to continue their studies at schools.
-In order to increase access to children to the targeted schools, the project will work on increasing schools' absorption capacities by conducting light rehabilitation for damaged/unused classrooms based on accurate need assessment, in addition to providing TLSs to decrease overcrowding and facilitate social distancing and to receive new students.
-To ensure safe and easy access for CWD (boys and girls), Takaful will provide schools with tools and equipment for CWD such as ramps, Iron stair handrails, special toilet, not connected desks and chairs with height adjustable function in addition to providing transportation for CWD.
Takaful will work to conduct awareness sessions to minimize the spread of the Corona Virus for all staffs and students, together with sterilizing all schools before students get in.
-The project will provide 3100 (1519 M,1581 F) teachers and education personnel with incentives -To ensure the best community engagement, meetings will be held with local actors (males and females) of the targeted locations in order to describe the project's goal and hear their recommendation and collect detailed information about the specific locations of dropping out children and the reasons for their dropping out and their needs.
Plan B:
* In case of continued schools' suspension due to the Corona pandemic, Takaful will go to distance learning methods (as it's currently used) by setting up WhatsApp groups that include all classroom's students together with teachers and followed up by educational personnel in the school, where lessons are given through audio and video clips which are published on groups. Students' questions are received through those groups and they are answered at the same time.
* if we weren't able to reach some schools or they received support from another NGO, by selecting schools with the same criteria in the target districts or other sub-districts, where we have full access.
- All the project activities will be monitored and evaluated and the findings will be shared with the project management on time to implement the right action.

5 - Implementation of activities

Takaful will support formal and non-formal education in 203 schools where those schools include 74,000 students (boys and girls) and their fund has ended or will end soon.Teachers will be provided with incentives, as they don't receive any financial support from any other NGOs, monthly operational costs, and heating materials to face the cold of winter. PSS weekly sessions will also help children, who spent a long time away from school, ridding of the harsh experience of war conditions and motivate them to continue their study at school. The 203 targeted schools will be equipped with safety and security tools, also, the project will involve 400 children with disability, who drop out of school, and overcome the difficulties that they may face by providing them with transportation service in addition to providing schools with tools for children with disability. Learning materials will be distributed to students, boys and girls. distributing teaching materials to teachers and teaching resources for the school. Moreover, teachers will receive light training sessions on non-formal education, curriculum B, child protection, and PSS.Takaful will support the educational process through providing 5000 Out of School Children (OOSC), boys and girls, IDPs, and HCs with non-formal education through catch-up classes using curriculum B.

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Safety and security:locations are far from front-lines so change control is not expected.Risk: Changes in the security situation or authorities/groups in control of project areas may make the projects activities less appropriate to the new situation.Likelihood: highImpact: HighMitigation:-In coordination with the Access Working Group, the security officer will help mitigate the risks through collecting information on security updates from field staff, the network of local councils and civil society activists.-Ensure that staff members are trained in safety and security.-All critical documents will be saved as soft copies, so that they will not get lost due to an attack.-If an emergency arises that may threaten the safety of staff and/or beneficiaries, staff is provided with directives to move to a safer location until the area is safe enough to continue with project activities or to ask for location change.- Preparing plan B, where if one of the targeted locations affected by the military operations, it will be replaced with another locations.Programmatic:Risk: Some of targeted locations may be supported from other donor, which lead in duplication of services.Likelihood: LowImpact: MediumMS: -At the beginning of project, we will re-check with related clusters and other networks to make sure that there isn’t any organization implementing similar programs in the targeted location.-If it appears that any organization was responded in one or more of the targeted locations, we will coordinate with cluster to change the location.
