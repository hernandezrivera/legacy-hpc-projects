Project Summary

This program is aiming to contribute in improving the resilience and livelihood opportunities of most vulnerable women headed HHs and PWDs in some communities related to Azaz, Afrin and Mhambal sub districts. In order to address the many gaps in livelihoods opportunities and income generation that have arisen in the target locations due to the ongoing crisis in Syria, Ihsan RD is proposing to support 500 household (of them 400 women head HHs and 100 PWDs) with small business grants (1000 $ per BNF), in addition to provide them with entrepreneurship and innovative training programs on small and medium businesses . The planned interventions will help the targeted beneficiaries to start their own income generation businesses.

4 - Needs

The planned interventions had been designed based on key messages of HNO report, 2021, which mentioned that the ongoing crisis had caused high deteriorating economy and widespread poverty, lack and loss of livelihoods. Also, destruction and loss of housing and property, protracted and multiple cycles of displacement, substandard living conditions (even for people in areas of relative stability), family separation and the breakdown of family or community support structures have depleted the coping abilities of individuals and communities and generated high levels of psychological distress. As a result, people resort to harmful coping mechanisms (including child labour, child recruitment, irreversible impacts, particularly on women, children andolder people. COVID-19 restrictions further and heavily impacted employment opportunities across the country, with unemployment estimated closer to 50 per cent of the working-age population at the end of 2020, pushing up prices and further increasing dependence on negative coping mechanisms. Several factors, including restrictions on movement, lack of or reduced access to transportation, fear of catching the virus, and uncertainty regarding the future, have driven the COVID-19 pandemic’s impact on social cohesion. Being locked down also caused tensions among family members and even violence, including GBV. Furthermore, the impact of COVID-19 on social cohesion notes COVID-19’s worsening of existing and sometimes deeply rooted political, economic, social, and security challenges, highlighting its resulting impact on the country’s diverse social fabric, which can lead to tensions among different social groups, enhance the likelihood of conflict, and undermine trust in the country.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Protection risk (1): Child labor in the established small businesses Mitigating measures: Ihsan RD will be sure that the staff working in this project are trained and fully aware of child safeguarding, Child protection and GBV principles. Protection risk (2): Lack/difficulty of accessibility for PWDs in the proposed activities. Mitigating measures: 1- Availability of wheelchair for PWD at the reception point (IRD contribution), a trained staff to deal with PWDs Protection Risk (3): Gender-based violence and sexual exploitation Mitigating measures: Choosing appropriate time for distributions small business grants (cash vouchers), voucher exchanging in Hawala agent will taking place during daylight hours to allow women to return home safely, ensuring regular security and incidence reporting, conducting awareness sessions about GBV , ensuring that the filed staff include female members to coordinate with female BNFs.Protection risk (4) : Low access to accountability mechanism, self-protection and for beneficiaries to claim their rights especially (SEA) Mitigation Measures: By starting of the project Ihsan R D will manage complaints mechanism and ensure accountability to beneficiaries by assigning one MEAL staff to manage any received feedback/complains throughout the implementation stage of the project, with high attention to PSEA cases after advanced coordination with Ihsan RD protection centers and PSEA focal points in the targeted location, conduct a SEA risk assessment, Conduct sensitization on SEA for the BNFs and field staff, establish (safe and accessible) community reporting channels to report SEA complaints tool will be also available e.g. complaints box / telephone hotline to deal with these cases.

7.1 - Please list any risks related to sexual exploitation and abuse that have been identified during the project design phase and any risk mitigation and prevention measures that have been adopted and built into the project

Base on Ihsan RD PSEA policy and before starting of project interventions, Ihsan RD will conduct SEA risk assessment of to identify all potential SEA risks such as :1- Risk of SEA act by non-PSEA trained staff while provide the support to BNFs, 2- BNFs do not report SEA cases for Ihsan RD staff, which results in the Ihsan RD being unable to investigate these cases and provide the needed assistance to them. Mitigation: After in-depth assessment for these risks, Ihsan RD through collaboration with PSEA focal point will designed the mitigation plan all these risks as follows Mitigation measures: 1- Ihsan RD will be ensure that all its staff had signed code of conduct to prevent acts of SEA by its staff and all recruited staff should attend PSEA training before any contact with BNFs. 2- Beneficiary awareness-raising (Beneficiaries must be aware on: what SEA is, what their rights are in regard to SEA, what they can do if SEA occur3- Establish safe and accessible community reporting channels to report SEA complaints tool will be also available (e.g. complaints box / telephone hotline to deal with these cases)

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumptions:1- All protective tools materials needed to avoid COVID-19 infections are available during project cycle2- Timely disbursement of funds 3- Stable security situation4- Smooth distribution of cash support to the BNFs Risks and mitigation: 1- Corvid -19 Outbreak in the target location Mitigation: Ensuring that all protective measures had been carefully considered and applied to avoid COVID-19 infections all stage of project such as, social distancing at least two meters, providing of masks and gloves for all persons involved (field staff, vendors, drivers and ensuring the availability of sterilization materials at distribution points). Applying all updated preventive measures for COVID-19 shared by WHO, ERL and FSL cluster. 2- Delay in fundsMitigation:Senior level of coordination with donor team and responding to the donor requirements to push them to release the requested payments at the optimal dates3- Changing the security situation Mitigation: Monitoring and analysis security situation, temporary suspension of field works. When it is impossible to implement the project due to the bad security condition, IRD will develop a plan B for project implementation to change the project location to safer areas 4-Rsks related to provision of cash interventions. Mitigation: Anti-Terrorist Sanctions Checks will be conducted by Ihsan RD for each contracted Financial Service Provider (FSP), contracting with FSPs who have a record of previous working with other aid agencies and being approved by donors, ensure continuous receiving of feedback of the BNFs, distribution of cash should be conducted during daylight, beneficiaries will be well informed that cash support are providing to them without taxes or fees
