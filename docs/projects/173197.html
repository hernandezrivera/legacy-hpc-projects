Project Summary

Lifesaving services are being addressing since June, 2018 to till date and planning to continue for the coming year of 2021 at camp-23, 24, 26 and host communities in Teknaf, Cox’s Bazar. Considering the camp context, Field experience and assessing the needs of beneficiaries regarding WASH services, we found that maximum facilities has already been installed or constructed. Now need to ensure the proper use, operation & maintenance of those facilities as well as proper access of every beneficiary and need to address the new challenge or demand. Bearing in mind the above issues we are planning to address the currents need of targeted beneficiaries Just as: Supplying sufficient safe water to targeted Rohingya and Host Beneficiaries from Advance Reverse Osmosis (The first ever Sea water treatment plant for Rohingya and Host Beneficiaries), sanitation and hygiene activities through regular cleaning, repairing & maintenance of every single WASH facilities Just as: Twin-pit Latrine, Bathing Cubical, Double chamber Dustbin for organic and non-organic waste, Drain cleaning, Latrine disludging, maintaining of Fecal Sludge Management (FSM), giving the high preference for Solid waste management as there is a huge gap of this, aware beneficiaries regarding hygiene knowledge through hygiene promotion and distribution of ICE materials etc. Disinfection activities and Key message forwarding have included in our plan considering COVID-19 pandemic. All of above mentioned activities are included in our plan to ensure that 100% of targeted beneficiaries have proper water, sanitation and hygiene facilities throughout the project duration.

Plan fields

1 - Identified Needs

1. Improve water service: As per wash sector analysis 45% of Refugee and 26% of Host communities in Teknaf area don’t have the access of safe water and water scarcity is very high here in Teknaf region as it’s a saline area and underground water is also contaminated with saline. Peoples are depend on rain water of this area as a consequence during dry season water scarcity become intense here. So ensuring safe water for 100% beneficiaries is a challenge and need to fulfil this gap as well as who has the access of safe water, need to maintain the quality, quantity and supply of water regularly. 2. Access to Sanitation: 99 % of Rohingya refugee have enough hygienic latrine in contrast host communities has 63% and many qualitative challenges remain with 22% of sanitation infrastructures requiring O&M and 10% being built in flooding areas as per ISCG. As well as 68% of households reported having a private space inside or attached to their shelter for bathing but those bathing places sometimes associated with laundry (56%) and dishwashing places (52%) are informal and often unhygienic. One of the major concerns regarding sanitation is the quality of the fecal sludge treatment as facilities require upgrades and improvements. Solid waste management is still a huge gap in our targeted area. 3. Hygiene Promotion and Community Engagement: Agencies are trying to change beneficiary’s hygiene behavior but it’s remain low as it’s a behavioral change that’s why it’s totally depends on beneficiary’s behavior. Only 60% of the refugees can mention three critical time to wash their hands. In respect of Menstrual Hygiene Management (MHM), 1.3 MHM kits were provided to women and girls on average between January to September instead of one every trimester. Host community’s scenario regarding this little better than Refugee though they have been less supported by hygiene items than refugee.

2 - Activities or outputs

1. Supply of sufficient safe water daily among 5000 Rohingya refugee and 2000 Host beneficiaries from sea water treatment plant (ARO- Advance Reverse Osmosis Plant).2. Regular cleaning, repairing and maintaining of every single wash facilities Just as: Twin-pit latrine, Bathing cubical, Double chamber Dustbin, Dumping station and Drain cleaning.3. Operation and maintenance of existing three (03) nos of Fecal Sludge Management (FSM) Plant by desludging every single latrine of targeted area through sludge networking system. 4. Solid Waste Management activity at targeted area by regular cleaning of every dustbin, segregating, composting at barrel compost as well transfer the non-organic item to nearer recycle plant.5. Disinfection of every single wash facilities including public space at targeted area and key message forwarding on COVID-19 through household visit and Media messaging.6. Hygiene Promotion activity at targeted area through hygiene session with communities, door to door visit and hygiene promotion during COVID-19 and distribution of IEC materials.
