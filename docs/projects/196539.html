Project Summary

This project will provide pre-primary level education to the children of 3 to 5 years old in an innovative way. Education materials will be playing instruments, such as toys, puzzles, pocket board, blackboard etc. children will learn pre- school level education (kindergarten level education) through games. 2 spaces or centers will be started under this project. In Areeshah center there will be 600 students and in Washokani center there will be 400 students. Total 1000 children will be selected from 2 camps (Areeshah and Washokani) in Al-Hassakah. Specific Objectives:
• Provide quality non-formal pre-primary education to 800 children in 20 schools through non- formal and formal pre-primary education in high quality learning centers.

3 - Needs

63% of children in Areeshah and Washokani Camp don't take ECCD or any Pre-primey School education.Children have no socially and emotionally healthy, confident, and friendly; has no good peer relationships; Don't tackles challenging tasks and persists with them; has No good language skills and Don't communicates well; goal of Early Child Development Education (ECCD) programs is to improve young children’s capacity to develop and learn. ECCD programs change the development trajectory of children by the time they enter school. A child who is ready for school has less chances of repeating a grade, being placed in special education, or being a school drop-out. ECCD interventions include educating and supporting parents, delivering services to children, developing capacities of caregivers and teachers, and using mass communications to enhance parents and caregiver's knowledge and practices. Here ECCD Programs for children will be center based, non-formal, and include parent education.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risks and Obstacles inside the Camp:- The spread of narcotic pills among the youth group in the camp, and they must be sensitized.- The appearance of rabid dogs in the camp, and the number of cases that were bitten by a dog reached 5.- Sewers and rain are exposed, and they impede the movement of children and the elderly, and there have been several accidents when they fell into them, and it is especially present in Stage 3 and Stage 4.- There is a large number of child labor in the camp.- An increase in child marriages in the camp, due to customs and traditions, as well as the difficult economic situation inside the camp, but so far there is no specific percentage.- There is an illiteracy rate inside the camp, especially the age group between 18 years and above.- Existence of school dropouts, but in a small percentage.- There are not enough schools and child-friendly spaces in the camp.- Lack of medicine in the camp.- The absence of a medical point in Stage 2, after the end of the official working hours there is a difficulty in transferring cases to medical centers in other sectors.
