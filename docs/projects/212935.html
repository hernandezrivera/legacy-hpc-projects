Project Summary

According to CCCM Cluster, as of January 2024, the CCCM database includes 1,556 IDP sites that host 2,006,977 IDPs or 381,310 families. Of these, 80% are women and children, and 91,187 IDPs are reported to be persons with disabilities. HNO 2024 also sites "People in sites with no CCCM services lack access to equal, predictable assistance, living in risk prone, undignified, hazardous and congested environments experiencing limited freedom of movement, exclusion and harassment including mass evictions. In north-west Syria, 87 per cent of IDP sites do not have a site management system in place." As most of the camps are self settled there are several incidents monthly in camps that requires attention, "According to Camp Coordination and Camp Management (CCCM) Cluster, more than 50 fire incidents and 78 flood incidents occurred in IDP sites during January 2024. These incidents caused 3 deaths and about 2,000 tents were damaged".
The project consists of training on camp management, legal awareness, and mobilizing committees for community-led initiatives empower residents to tackle issues collaboratively. Additional measures like constructing fire points and conducting awareness campaigns further enhance safety and resilience, collectively aiding vulnerable populations in Northwest Syria.
the main activities in the project are:
Establishing camp committee: the committee will compose of camp manager and representatives of beneficiaries. The committee will be from at least 4 members, ensuring that all sectors in the camp are represented, HIHFAD will establish two separate committees, one for men and one for women, so women feel safe and more willing to participate.
Establish and mobilize camp committee to support community level activities. The committee will be urged to identify problems they are facing and how they can solve them by community led activities such as maintenance of a road, cleaning campaign in the camp, etc.,
Conduct training on camp management: Main topics will be importance coordination, registration, care and maintenance and participation.
Construction of a fire point in order to mitigate the risk of fire. one fire point to a maximum of 200 families will be constructed and equipped with the necessary equipment in the targeted camps. The point will be equipped with essential tools and equipment in addition to a first aid kit. The location will be accessible to all households, and it will be determined in consultation with the committees.
Fire awareness campaign: this campaign will be conducted through direct sessions in addition to displaying banners in public places in the camp.
Conducting community-led integration and skills-building activities including women and youth-led initiatives and start-up grants to 50 IDPs in the camps.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

HIHFAD aims to target three subdistricts in Aleppo and Idleb based on severity levels in the selected locations of Dana, Maarert Tamsrin, and Jandaris subdistricts. The project seeks to empower residents through training on camp management, legal awareness, and mobilizing committees for community-led initiatives, benefiting 1150 families, including (1475 women, 1450 girls, 1416 men, 1409 boys), , encompassing 5,750 IDPs. These efforts aim to increase resilience and access to essential services while additional measures such as constructing fire points and conducting awareness campaigns are implemented to further enhance safety and resilience, collectively aiding vulnerable populations in Northwest Syria.
