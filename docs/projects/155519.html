Needs

The crisis in the North-west and South-west regions begun in the last quarter of 2016. Since then about 80% of schools have been declared closed denying almost one million children right to education. Over 80 schools have been attacked and destroyed in the two regions. Teachers and other education allied workers have attacked, killed, maimed and continue tro be under threat. Most of the teachers and education workers have vacated the two regions due to the insecurity and threat on their lives. These have mostly relocated to the neighbouring regions of Littoral and West. Some have come as far as to the Centre region, Yaounde.Parents who can afford the cost and space have moved their children to Littora, West and Centre regions to continue with their education. This of course is at a very high cost both directly and indirectly. Those parents who are not able have resorted to keeping their children at home. Large numbers of families have also been taking refuge in their farm house where it is very difficult for humanitarian actors to access to conduct needs assessment and to provide the needed education services for the children. Thus, in summary, more than 800,000 children are in need of education assistance in the two regions. The project will seek to respond in the areas of improving infrastructure, providing learning materials and utilizing modern technology to reach children with appropriate education services.
