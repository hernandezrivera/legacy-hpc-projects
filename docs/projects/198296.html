Project Summary

The purpose is the reduction of preventable maternal deaths by increasing the proportion of births attended by skilled health personnel in the targeted Fako and to contribute to the reduction of unmet need for family planning; and gender-based violence and harmful practices. the main activities of our project consist of identifying and organize a capacity building training for emergency response team;
identify and provide antenatal and post-natal assistance to lactating women and babies; Organize counseling for vulnerable household; Identification and Sensitized vulnerable household on family planing; Provide pregnant test to vulnerable women/girls; Provide HIV test to vulnerable people; Sensitization of girls/women on the use of reusable sanitary pads.

1 - Besoins / Needs

About 295 000 women died during and following pregnancy and childbirth in 2017. It reports that vast majority of these deaths (94%) occurred in low-resource settings, and most could have been prevented.In Cameroon, it has been reported that 529 women died per 100,000 live births in 2017 (Knoema, 2022). Maternal death is therefore an important societal problem that continues to plague our communities. Maternal death is preventable if the root causes are addressed properly. Research has shown that low resource settings, poor access to health facilities and harmful practices are the main causes of maternal deaths (WHO, 2019). In the context of the socio-political crisis in the North West and South West, and most crises, women are more susceptible to harm because of their biological predispositions. For most of the time, women face aggravated GBV issues such as rape, loss of children and deprivation of resources. Unhealthy menstrual hygiene and other harmful practices are adopted as coping mechanisms during crisis. These unhealthy practices lead to severe consequences, such as unwanted pregnancies, lack of ante-natal care and venereal diseases. In the short run, this causes an increase in maternal deaths due to the poor conditions that arise. The immediate consequence of increasing maternal deaths is the likelihood of infant mortality due to improper care, increase in orphan and vulnerable children, child labor, early marriages in the long run and so on. To keep this in check and prevent the aforementioned consequences, it is high time, women are educated on healthy reproductive practices and sensitized against GBV and other harmful practices that are normalized in the communities. Also, is imperative to adopt data driven decisions as most statistics, including those of the WHO date back to 2017. Insufficient data, impedes the tracking of reproductive practices around the communities and the world at large which in return makes is difficult to handle and discourage poor practices. To remedy this, CARITAS is planing to:Sensitize 500 household yearly on family planning,Conduct Pregnancy testing and counseling of 500 household yearly,Provide antenatal and post-natal assistance to 400 pregnant/lactating women and babies yearly,Sensitize 1000 household yearly on GBV and harmful practices.
