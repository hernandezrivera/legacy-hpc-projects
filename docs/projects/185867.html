Project Summary

The North West and South West regions have been experiencing serious socio political crisis that have stirred up humanitarian needs. The crisis has had serious negative effects on water, hygiene and sanitation with mostly women and girls highly affected, as they are the ones who access WASH facilities often. The breakdown of the WASH systems in the crisis affected regions plays negatively on their access to portable drinking water, better hygiene and sanitation and affects their dignity and safety. This breakdown in the WASH system reduces the regional and global contribution to achieving Sustainable development Goal 6, which aims to achieve universal access to water and hygiene for all by 2030. According to OCHA SITREP as of November 5, 2021,insecurity in the North West and South West regions displaced over 4,724 people in the NWSW to the West and Littoral regions, and 573,900IDPs within or displaced from NWSW. These displaced persons in the West and Littoral regions lack meaningful and inclusive access to WASH facilities and services. This is because lots of humanitarian support target mostly in the North West and South West, while a greater population of the displaced persons in the Littoral and West are not reached with WASH support and humanitarian WASH response services. The humanitarian WASH needs is further compounded with the health crisis of COVID 19 and cholera outbreak which have hit the already crisis stricken and affected communities in the North West and South West regions, and rumors and potential cholera outbreak in the Littoral. The SITREP on cholera in November highlights that total cases were 79, 6 deaths (CFR 7.6%), a cholera attack rate at 8.52 per 1,000 people. The barrier measures to cholera and Covid 19 require washing of hands regularly with running water, and keeping clean our environment. It is therefore imperative for a good WASH system to be developed in the communities with poor WASH system, which are hosting displaced persons or are affected by the socio political crisis, so as to reduce the spread of covid 19, cholera and provide equitable and inclusive access to water, hygiene and sanitation. The WASH needs have increased and are more urgent as these regions are in dire need of WASH supports. The WASH needs include access to portable drinking water, hygiene dignity and WASH kits. The project in its response will prioritize the safety and dignity of the targeted women and girls, and will reduce the risk and exposure to gender based violence and other violence related to accessing WASH facilities. The project will be implemented in the South West (Buea, Limbe 1,Tiko, Kumba 1 subdivisions), North West (Tubah, Santa, and Bamenda 2 subdivisions), and Littoral(Douala 4, Mbanga, Loum and Djombe/Penja subdivisions). These are communities hosting displaced persons and have traces of covid 19 and cholera.
