Project Summary

The project is targeting IDPs and host populations affected by conflict in Montepuez and Meluco districts through enhanced access to safe drinking water, sanitation and hygiene promotion services. The project activities will include drilling boreholes, rehabilitation of water points, water committee trainings, latrines construction and hygiene promotion.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

This project is targeting IDPs and host populations affected by conflict in Montepuez and Meluco districts. The project seeks to enhance access to safe drinking water, sanitation and hygiene promotion services to these vulnerable populations.JAM will target IDPs living in the resettlement sites, accommodation centres and host communities affected by the conflict in Cabo Delgado with the proposed services. JAM intends to drill boreholes and rehabilitate water points in locations with high populations and lacking essential services, including schools. Water committees will also be established and trained for the maintenance and repair of the rehabilitated boreholes. Their role will promote the sustainability of the WASH facilities beyond the project period.Households will be provided with concrete slabs to construct latrines. The project will construct latrines for people living with disabilities (PLWD) and female-headed households. JAM will further construct emergency communal latrines in transit centres and new resettlement sites.Hygiene promotion activities will be carried out to help the affected population avoid and limit hygiene risks associated with poor sanitation. The hygiene promotion will also help reduce the spread of diseases.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The following priority activities will be implemented in Montepuez and Meluco districts targeting vulnerable IDP's, host communities and returnees: a) Drilling of boreholes : Establishment of water points through boreholes with handpumps and solar powered systems will be prioritized to ensure that vulnerable populations have access to sufficient quantity and quality of water.b) Borehole/water point maintenance/rehabilitation: Rehabilitation of boreholes will also be undertaken; vulnerable families will also be supported with point of use water treatment and purification materials/products.c) Water committee establishment/training/re-training: Water committees will also be established for new water points and trained for the maintenance and repair of the rehabilitated boreholes. Existing water committees will also be targeted based on assessed training needs. d) Sanitation: Access to appropriate, safe, clean and reliable sanitation facilities especially for conflict affected populations will be prioritized. This will achieved mainly through households will being provided with concrete slabs to construct latrines. People living with disabilities (PLWD) and female-headed households will also be assisted to construct latrines. Emergency communal latrines in transit centres and new resettlement sites.e) Hygiene promotion: Hygiene promotion supporting information dissemination, behavior change and actions to reduce the risk of preventable disease will be undertaken. The targeted population will be reached with sanitation messages which will be delivered by staff on one to one basis through use of megaphones and community leaders. Hygiene kits will also be distributed to households – with women and girls prioritized with products that support their dignity and well being.WASH in public spaces and institutions will also be carried out.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

JAM will ensure apply and promote protection mainstreaming principles in the design and implementation of this programme. Given that this programme is targeting vulnerable populations affected by conflict – special care will be taken to ensure that vulnerability of girls, boys, and women are not exacerbated in the provision of Emergency WASH services. As this project will ensure that it: ● Prioritize safety for all beneficiaries and avoid causing further harm to the IDPs and host communities especially in provision of WASH services e.g. gender segregation, access, lighting and privacy for in sanitation facilities.● Encourage meaningful access to interventions to all IDPs in proportion to their need and without discrimination. JAM will provide special attention to more vulnerable groups such as people with disabilities, elders or debilitated who usually have difficulty accessing services.● Enhances accountability by setting up an appropriate mechanism for the IDPs and host communities to measure the adequacy of intervention and address the concerns and complaints.● It promotes the IDPs and host communities' participation and empowerment by developing self-protection mechanisms and self-awareness within the beneficiaries to claim their rights.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

JAM understands accountability to affected population (AAP) as the process where the organization uses power responsibly and in trust while providing timely lifesaving actions to populations affected by conflict. The organization also sees accountability to the beneficiaries and stakeholders as a key component of the process. JAM is currently implementing Emergency WASH and other complementary services in Cabo Delgado through which it has consulted different groups in the design of this programme. The groups were mainly consulted through community consultations, face to face meetings, formally and informally and through their community leaders. For this project, JAM will take the following actions as part of AAP: a) Information sharing and consultations: Affected populations will be provided with the right information on the program and services to meaningfully participate in and play an active part in decisions related to this programme. b) Handling complaints and feedback: JAM will also use these platforms such as help desks, complaint boxes, telephone, e-mails, staff and trusted intermediaries to receive feedback, complaints, addressing them as well as reporting. The feedback will be recorded, investigated, and reported back to the complainant. All complaints will be treated confidentially.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

JAM will be guided by the IASC Guidelines on the inclusion of Person with Disability in Humanitarian Action (2019) and UNICEF’s “WASH Disability Inclusion Practices” which encourages organizations to promote meaningful participation, remove barriers, empower persons with disabilities, and disaggregate data for monitoring inclusion. Specifically, JAM will:i. Implement organizational policies that promote non- discrimination and rights of persons with disabilities to access and inclusion.ii Ensure feedback and complaints mechanisms are designed to ensure accessibility for persons with disabilities.iii. Train staff on the inclusion of persons with disabilities. JAM has so far identified 150 people living with disabilities (PLWD) in various resettlement sites in Montepuez district. JAM will involve them in implementation and ensure that they are targeted with WASH services. iv. Co-ordinate with other organizations to advocate and share experiences on disability inclusion.v. Ensure that all data collected is disaggregated at a minimum by gender, age, and disability.vi. Ensure that persons with a range of disabilities participate in key informant interviews, FGD, in needs assessment and implementation.vii. JAM will understand the needs, capacities, and difficulties of persons with disabilities and their caregivers.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Environmental considerations will also be taken into consideration during the planning and implementation of the project. Specific activities will include sensitization of community leaders and households on proper waste disposal and management; proper rehabilitation and decommissioning of wells, conducting hydrological surveys and assessments before driiling, advocacy on proper utilization of water resource management to avoid pollution and over extraction of water resources, proper disposal of damaged WASH infrastructure that can cause environmental pollution and promotion of water conservation. JAM will also engage with beneficiaries regarding the practice of digging unplanned hand dugs in the resettlement sites to ensure that the process is well managed.
