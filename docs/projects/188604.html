Project Summary

NGO will deliver Integrated Protection services through Child Protection and Women& Girls Protection interventions reaching 8284 vulnerable conflict-affected people in Raqqa governorate.
The integrated approach will allow the NGO to provide more comprehensive support to meet more protection needs of more community members including children, adults, and people with specific vulnerabilities such as PwD. NGO through its response will give the people who experienced or exposed to violence or abuse the opportunity to recover, heal and draw their empowerment path by connecting them to the various specialized and non-specialized services and activities NGO will deliver. To ensure mitigating protection risks and contributing to safety outcomes for the harmed individuals (at individual, home, and community levels), NGO will ensure connecting all vulnerable individuals to needed services. NGO will do monitoring on the trending child protection concerns and GBV risks through analyzing the case management and referrals data to inform the program design to meet the needs. Besides of regular assessments, NGO will ensure inclusion of people with disability through adaptation of PSS programs, changing modalities where applicable, rehabilitating the safe spaces to maximize the accessibility of PwD , besides having Disability and GBV sensitive WASH facilities inside the Safe Space .NGO will deliver their services and activities through different modalities, center-based activities, mobile activities, and community-based activities. NGO has a strong MEAL system that built on regularly collecting, analyzing, and reporting of data, considering the accountability and data control. NGO’s established trackers will be used for tracking the data of outcomes of the integrated protection program delivery to the clients by using registration records, attendance records, wellbeing improvement measurement tools, Child Protection Information Management System (CPIMS+)_which facilitates effective case management for individual vulnerable children as it promotes best practice and accountability, and to assist child protection programs in delivering quality care_ In addition to the Quality Participation and Client Satisfaction surveys at the end of all cycles of different PSS activities. NGO will inform their clients and the community about the NGO Clients Responsiveness Mechanisms to allow them to communicate their feedback on the delivery of the program, needs and concerns.

4 - Needs

Armed conflict, displacement, COVID-19 outbreak, social practices, and deepening poverty continues to fuel harmful coping mechanisms and strain the capacities of families and communities to protect children, particularly adolescent girls and boys: child labor, including its worst forms is reported in all governorates. specifically in Raqqa where 62% reported the child labor as very common. in addition to child marriage which was reported as common by 23% in Raqqa due to social practices and financial hardship per MSNA 2021 in NES. Child labor was the main pushing factor fueling the recruitment and using the children by the military forces and being exposed to additional abuse (physical abuse, exploitation), and associated with distress. above factors have a gendered and disproportionate impact on girls and women in NES and specifically in Raqqa, which manifests itself through different forms of gender-based violence (GBV), especially early/forced marriage, domestic violence, sexual harassment and/or violence, denial ofresources, and psychological and/or physical abuse, in all walks of life.

5 - Implementation of activities

Through this project, NGO will connect vulnerable individuals services via referrals conducted using updated service maps. NGO will deliver specialized child protection case management, support CAAFAG intervention (case management, distribution in the detention centers, adolescent livelihoods center). NGO will set-up safe spaces, deliver structured and non-structed PSS to caregivers and children, distribute PSS kits for children and conduct awareness sessions on child protection and GBV topics, NGO will deliver trainings to the community actors on SIR, PSEA, PFA, PSS trainings. NGO will do monitoring on the trending child protection concerns and GBV risks through analyzing the case management and referrals data to inform the program design to meet the needs. NGO has a strong MEAL system that built on regularly collecting, analyzing, and reporting of data, considering the accountability and data control. NGO’s established trackers will be used for tracking the data of outcomes of the integrated protection program delivery to the clients by using registration records, attendance records, wellbeing improvement measurement tools, Child Protection Information Management System (CPIMS+).In addition to the Quality Participation and Client Satisfaction surveys at the end of all PSS activities' cycles. NGO will inform their clients and the community about the NGO Clients Responsiveness Mechanisms to allow them to communicate their feedback on the program delivery, needs and concerns.

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk: Barriers to client’s access to the specialized services due to the physical impediments services in addition to the normative restriction to access services for women and girls, Likelihood; medium impact: High mitigation: use the community outreach modality to support the vulnerable individual with referrals and ensure integration with other, with considering the rehabilitation of the Safe space to be disability friendly and where PwD and different genders can access disability and GVB sensitive WASH facility in the Safe Space. Risk: stigmatization that individual vulnerable children and GBV survivors may face as a result of receiving specialized service as case management or to be referred to a specialized GBV case management. Likelihood: high impact: High Mitigation: invest in building rapport with the community through distribution of PSS kits or delivering awareness sessions as non-specialized service targets all in the community. Ensure the staff who do referrals are trained on safe identification and referral of the cases. Clear messages on the services to be in place and used by the staff who deliver the services and activities. In addition to work toward more integration internally.


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

The Major risk: Risk of sexual exploitation and abuse and sexual harassment of women and girls clients by staff and field facilitators. Likelihood: Medium/Low Impact: Very High Mitigation: information materials to the clients demonstrating how the reporting channels are functioning within the NGO regarding PSEA and ASH. In addition NGO had a strong internal code of conduct in place that clearly related to prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse (PSEA) and safeguarding the clients, specifically the children. and firmly shows the Zero Tolerance policy for any sexual misconduct. furthermore the NGO and through its reporting mechanism allow for anonymous reporting any misconduct to ensure all possible violations of the policy are reported by staff and clients.


11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk: Change of context and limited access to the supported sites. Likelihood: Medium Impact: High Mitigation: activate the links established throughout the program lifetime with the stakeholders and start negotiation through the NGO Access team. All NGO staff are from the targeted community to ensure the continuity of the program during the access limitation. Risk: Security tensions and COVID-19 lockdowns that will restrict the access of the clients to the activities conducted in the Safe Spaces or lock the movement of Field staff. Likelihood: Medium Impact: High Mitigation: NGO will depend on revising the modalities where possible benefiting from the successful experience of remote delivery of the program. With Access team continue the assessment of the situations and inform the program delivery.
