Project Summary

Based on Save the Children learnings and successful models replicated through common approaches, Child Protection Minimum Standards and the IASC guidance on MHPSS in emergencies, the proposed project will focus to strengthen the Mental Health and Resilience of 600 vulnerable children (10-14) and 600 youth (15-17) boys and girls including disabled and transgender of targeted districts of Sindh and ICT. Following due protocols around personal distancing, the support will be particularly provided at Level 3 of the MHPSS intervention pyramid (focused, yet non-specialized support) in the form of Save the Children’s “Children and Youth Resiliency Program”. The Resilience program will build skills linked to behavior and social interaction which will help children and youth in building and sustaining resilience, protection and wellbeing. The interventions will also develop confidence and communication and overall MHPSS and resiliency. Learning on innovations from UNHCR 2017, storytelling will be used as key aspect for psychological first aid, healing and resiliency during the program. Negative coping mechanisms of girls and families, such as child marriage, domestic labour, Gender Based Violence and other risky behaviours will be addressed through this program. To capture the complex interaction of individual and social factors that facilitates resilience, the interventions will be framed around the concept of “I AM”, “I CAN” and “I HAVE.”
Similarly, building on Save the Children’s, existing work in the targeted districts, child protection systems approaches will be implemented to protect and prevent children from abuse, neglect, exploitation and violence through case management support. Based on the case management guidelines, data protection and referral mechanism Save the Children will provide case management support to 10,000 (2,500 per district) vulnerable children including transgender and disabled through referral mechanism. Save the Children has already established and strengthen the Child Protection Units at Social Welfare Department and Child Protection Desks at department of education, Bait ul Mal, health, law and judiciary and labour in the targeted districts of Sindh. So during the proposed project, Save the Children will continue to identify, register, refer and follow up on the children cases with relevant Govt lines departments for the provision of required protection support. Save the Children will also works with existing community based informal structures (Child Protection Committees and children clubs) and will also form additional 200 (50 per districts) structures and will employ social mobilization strategy to ensure that community themselves have identified the prevalent child protection issues; developed action plans and challenged societal norms that perpetuate violence in the community. Communities including girls, boys and transgender including disabled will also be empowered and organized to monitor and refer the cases of protection.
Similarly, to compound the impact on community sensitization, on the stigma and Violence Against Children (VAC) and Gender Based Violence (GBV), Save the Children will reach out to Approx. 25,000 people at the targeted districts of Sindh and ICT. Different dialogues and sessions will be organized at the community / village level through the active engagement of families, community members including men, women, girls, boys and community influential. For the purpose, child sensitive IEC materials will also be developed and disseminated through various means e.g. during group community mobilization sessions and dialogues (following due protocols around personal distancing), mass media such as local FM radio and cable TV channels to reach out to Approx. 1.5 million population. VAC and GBV will be of the outmost importance therefore informal community structures will also be reoriented to prevent and respond to such issues themselves within their community.

1- Project Activities

Save the Children, Pakistan is implementing a 5 years Child Protection program and already has presence in targeted districts (ICT and 4 district of Sindh; Dadu, Larkana, Shikarpur and Jacobabad). The project will be implemented through existing partner to ensure it is locally driven, further build trust and will allow to gain access to hard to reach communities. Save the Children will deploy its beneficiary Feedback Mechanisms for the communities to hold us accountable for our actions. Similarly, Save the Children PSEA policy and measure will also be fully implemented to ensure safeguarding.1) Provision of Mental Health and Psychosocial Support (MHPSS) Services to the most vulnerable children (girls and boys).A holistic MHPSS / resiliency program will be delivered to children and youth in shape of Children and Youth Resilience Program through series of workshops (Keeping in view personal distancing). This dynamic process will be influenced by combinations of different types of skills and knowledge. These structured workshops will support the processes between three dimensions, by strengthening life skills linked to cognitive, emotional and social functioning. Workshops sessions will further be contextualized through incorporation of children and youth inputs. The workshop will use participatory techniques with fun, safe and inclusive environment. The workshops will be complemented by a series of meetings with the parents, caregivers and TOT of partner, children and youth to trickle the work beyond project life for sustainability and broader impact. Children and youth requiring specialized MHPSS support will also be identified and referred for specialized services.2) Establish and support the operations of a case management and referral mechanism to ensure access of vulnerable children to basic services.The interventions will build on our existing child protection system strengthening work in the targeted districts. The interventions under this activity will be supported by the work with provincial and districts governments of Sindh to further strengthen the CP system approach to case management. Vulnerable children including disabled and transgender requiring protection services will be identified and their case will be registered and referred to Child Protection Units (CPU) and different Govt lines departments for the required support through case management protocols. These Govt lines departments are already in partnership with us through the CPU inter-agency protocols. This will help Save the Children to facilitate the children protection issues through referral mechanism. Similarly, Save the Children will also work with its existing informal community based structures (Child Protection Committees and Children Clubs) to further strengthen Community Based Case Management System. Technical capacities of these CPUs and informal structures will be further strengthened to carryout case management and prevention and response work beyond project life. 3. Disseminate information and promote dialogue among families and communities on stigma and Violence against Children (VAC) and Gender Based Violence (VAC) prevention. Save the Children will design and implement campaign on Stigma, VAC and GBV to create momentum at district and community level to change the widely existing perception on these issues. Campaign will use the “edutainment” strategy (radio and cable tv programs, social media, community theaters) to bring the attention of masses to the issues. Similarly, at the communities and home level, dialogues on Stigma, GBV and VAC will be organized for sensitization and mobilization. These issues will also be addressed through informal structures through creating awareness and working with the parents, families and communities and by including these issues in their respective action plans. Information Educational Communicational material will also be developed and disseminated on stigma and VAC and GBV.
