Project Summary

This project aims to provide conditional and unconditional food assistance to the returned TDPs in South Waziristan district. This project will cover 2,000 vulnerable returnees households to receive support in the form of food assistance for participation in critical livelihood assets restoration/recovery and capacity building training. The assets to be restored will include lining and rehabilitation of irrigation channels, construction of water ponds, rehabilitation of retaining walls, culverts etc while the training component will include training sessions on Pattu weaving, embroidery, health and hygiene, IYCF, water purification etc.
In order to implement the food aid activities, a detailed assessment will be carried out to identify the most appropriate interventions in consultation with concerned communities followed by feasibility surveys by engineering team of HUJRA. Work norms will be identified for each soft and hard intervention in consultation with FSC and expert engineers.
One participant form one household will be selected for participation in the food assistance activities. The beneficiary selection and registration will be done in consultation with the concerned livelihood committees after a detailed door to door survey is conducted by the social mobilisation teams. Beneficiaries list will be developed and shared with cluster for verification.
Each participant will be engaged for 15 days a month to carry out the activities for a maximum period of three months. The project will provide food assistance to each participant in lieu for his/ her participation in the food assistance activities. The assistance will be provided after physical work on livelihood asset is completed or participation in training is ensured.
Financial Service Provider(s) will be involved in cash disbursement to the beneficiaries depending on the donor preference. The final lists of attended beneficiaries for each cycle will be shared after verification from the field teams with concerned bank for creating disbursement cycle on its system. Feasible point will be identified for disbursement and proper security arrangements will be ensured.
By end of the project a study will be conducted to identify the proportionate use of assistance by communities in different heads like food, health, clothing, education, debt repayment etc.

Needs

According to the HUJRA’s rapid assessment recently conducted in Shib Khel, Nanu Khel and Manzai of South Waziristan district, using the FGD and key informant interviews 95% of the female respondents and 100% of male respondents requested for immediate cash support to meet basic livelihood needs. They showed their willingness and commitment to actively participate in the cash for work activities. The FGDs and key informant interviews inform that complex emergency has resulted in a drastic shift from dependency on agriculture to reliance on day (unskilled) labour activities. Similarly, dependency on shops and small businesses has been reduced due to destruction of markets. A large proportion of the population is entirely dependent on humanitarian assistance. Under the conditional cash transfer initiatives 95% of male preferred lining and rehabilitation of irrigation channels, construction of water ponds, animal shelters, provision of water troughs for watering animals, kitchen garden while 100% percent of the female preferred trainings on Pattu weaving, embroidery, health and hygiene, IYCF, water purification etc. In terms of agriculture 75% of the male respondent’ males preferred trainings on improved sowing techniques, grafting and pruning, livestock management, nursery raising etc. During the discussions they showed keen interest in Farmer Field Schools and establishment of training demonstration plots.
