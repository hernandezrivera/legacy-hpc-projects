Project Summary

The project seeks to respond to part of the immediate needs of persons affected by the Sociopolitical crisis in our zone of intervention (Fako Division), which is a general call for concern among the various humanitarian actors and sectors intervening in the area. The project will assist 2000 individuals around 04 communities in the area. The project activities include:
-Provision of safe drinking water in most efficient way under the given circumstances (02 borehole drilling) based on the multi-barrier approach.
-Hygiene promotion activities based on the situational needs especially in health structures, schools and camps/settlements (Refugees, IDPs and Host Community). Focus is on most critical hygiene behaviors that could have adverse impact on health.
This includes two hygiene promotion campaigns in the two communities the boreholes are being drilled as well as two additional ones in other communities (2 trainings in each community). As part of the WASH kits, hygiene items such as soap, bucket, toilet tissue and dental supplies will be distributed in two distributions to 300 individuals. The representation according to categories are as follows:
Women 70% (Men 30%)
IDP hosts 30% (IDPs 70%)
OVCs 10%
Elderly 5%
Persons with Disability 10%
-Provision of dignified and culturally appropriate Menstrual Hygiene Management (MHM) items and sensitization of the target group.

1 - Besoins / Needs

The Diocese of Buea which covers the Fako Division, has been seriously affected by the worsening socio-political crisis in the North West and South West Regions of Cameroon; especially after it degenerated into an armed conflict in 2017. Since then, the situation in the division has been deteriorating rapidly. Since 2018, the Diocese has continued to witness recurrent violence, human rights violations and the erosion of human dignity, collapse of the rule of law and growing culture of impunity, untold human suffering and reckless abandonment. The Covid-19 pandemic that hit the region in February 2020 has also been a cause of fear among populations and also limited activities in the communities. Basically, the following needs will be addressed through the project implementation: WASH: through the provision of safe drinking and accessible drinking water in two communities and provision of hygiene and dignity kits to four communities around Fako (Tole, Bokwaongo, Miselele and Tiko-Douala road). Also, the hygiene kits, and hygiene promotion campaigns, will go a long way to promote safety measures against covid-19, and raise awareness on the cholera epidemic which was currently recorded in the SW region.
