Project Summary

The on-going “Crisis” in the NW/SW Regions of Cameroon which breaks out since 2016 /incorporated with Cholera outbreak in 2022/flood disaster in 2023/landslide in 2023 in the NWSW regions due to climate change, has resulted in increased displacements of people, school children, high risk of hygiene-related morbidity/mortality rates leading to high death rates of school children. 1.033,000M among the 1.5M PiN of humanitarian assistance in Education/WASH including 638,421 IDPs/442,580 Returnees/education target 940K including 225,026 children IDPs & PIN-TARGET WASH HNO-HRP2024 of 20 Nov. 2023 is 787,8K will be targeted
DEMOGRAPHIC INFORMATION: Plan intervention in 12 Subdivisions in 15 communities/40 schools in rural, urban/semi-urban in 7 Divisions (Donga Mantung, Mezam, Boyo, Menchum, Momo, Fako/Manyu) in the NWSW Regions of Cameroon. Target: 15,000 (dissag. by gender, age, disability of (1,890 Boys/1,908 Girls) <5yrs; (1,890 Boys/1,908 Girls)<18yrs; (1368 Men/2,100 Women) 18-59yrs; Elderly (990 Men/1,008 Women) +60yrs, disability (621 Men/630 Women)).
AIM:
To enhance learning in schools with supplies of didactic materials and improved learning environment with access to sustainable latrines, hygiene services to vulnerable children by 31st December 2024.
INDICATORS :
-# of latrines built
-# of girls, boys & adolescents affected by crisis who received learning materials
-# of people reached along awareness raising campaigns or hygiene promotion of good wash practices
-# of affected populations who know how to provide feedback and complaints to humanitarian actors
-# of community mobilisers (community health workers, volunteers, animators..) trained on WASH issues
ACTIVITIES:
-Construction of school and training infrastructure (classrooms, latrines, water points, temporary learning spaces).
-Purchase & distribution of learning materials (bags, books, pens, pencils, etc.)
-Promotion of safe hygiene and sanitation practices amongst vulnerable people
-Capacity-building of WASH actors in emergency WASH response/relative issues

OBJECTIVE:
-Schools/education services serve as a platform for accessing basic Water, Sanitation, hygiene/health promotion for crisis affected children/communities.
-Vulnerable children/teachers in schools/communities access basic teaching/learning materials for improved quality EIE by 31st December 2024.
-Reduce the risk of poor hygiene-related morbidity/mortality of affected population in crisis affected areas.
-Reinforce Capacities/coordination of local actor to improve WASH services/resilience of affected populations
BENEFICIARIES:
Affected population (IDPs, returnee’s/host communities) of Infants, Boys, Girls, Men, Women, Elderly people/PLWDs of Gender, Age, Disability/Diversity mainstreaming i.e. 50% IDPs, 10% PWDs, 30% host communities/10% Returnees.
METHODOLOGY: Sensitisation.
APPROACH:
Community based approach, awareness raising campaign (school-to-school/door-to-door sensitization)/radio talks, standard visibility/IEC materials/PSEA, collaboration with stakeholders, community’s mobilisation/engagement/inclusively school children. Open space distribution/construction, organized workshops. IDPs/the communities are consulted/participate in the conception of the entire project cycle as decision-makers/local management/ownership. Appropriateness, relevance/feasible. Rapid HH Survey, FGDs/KIIs, field visit/report from communities, AAP/PSEA. Engage MINEDU, MINSANT/Municipal Council/advocacy actions.
SUSTAINABILITY:
Trained (school health clubs (SHCs)/CPCs for the continuity of sensitisaton on HP; schools administration on how to treat/reuse squatting slabs once the pits are full). Installation of HWFs/Improved sustainable latrines & ensuring education continuity with learning materials.
JUSTIFICATION:
Vulnerable children are assisted with learning materials. Improve latrines/good wash practices reduces cholera/diarrhea, death rate of children <5years/GBV cases that are latrines related.
