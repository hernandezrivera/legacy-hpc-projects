Project Summary

The sector animal Production in Syria is considered one of the most important economic sectors that many citizens depend on, of all kinds (sheep, cows, goats, buffalo, and poultry)..
The project serves 1250 Livestock keepers and goats in the project work area and the expected number of sheep heads 25 thousand sheep
The project is working to provide the necessary vaccine for sheep
During this period of the life of the project vaccines are (Foot-and-mouth disease -Sheep Pox-fmean enterotoxaemia)
Thus, livestock in the project work areas are surrounded and preserved .
The project also works to train veterinary technicians in all areas of the project who provide technical and technical support to livestock breeders and provide the necessary advice . And provide training courses for breeders and sensory detection of sheep heads They have.
Among the activities of the project also is the work to establish a mobile veterinary clinic that includes most of the veterinary medicines and equipment to serve the people of the region and includes veterinarians and technicians and will be the rst of its kind in the region.
The project will also provide concentrated feed ( with a source of carbohydrates - protein and support vitamins) to breeders in three batches, and this is preceded by a census of breeders and the preparation of sheep heads for each batch of 100 kg total of 37.5 tons .
Extension Units:
The project works to re-activate the Extension Units
necessary technical support shall be provided through competent staff and feld tours through which:
Carrying out veterinary visits to livestock and poultry breeders, beekeeping, counting thenal numbers, organizing livestock numbers, follow-up of veterinary activities according
to schedule and provision of technical and logistical support available from vaccines. Analysis of the data collected and organized in the tables on the Excel program at the level of one crop and the preparation of a report of all those data for inclusion in the statistical group and drawing of agricultural plans based on it.

pigeons, sheries and beehives. Developing a preliminary estimate of production of meat, milk, eggs and honey.

4 - Needs

Based on the HNO Report/2021 •Key Trends in Needs:• The situation continues to deteriorate drastically with an estimated 14.2 million people in need of some form of food and agriculture assistance, compared to 9.8 million in the previous year. The food insecurity PiN has risen by 45 per cent in the last year. Livestock witnessed a serious decline, which led to a signi cant decline in their numbers during the nine years of the war, According to the statistics of the Ministry of Agriculture and Agrarian Reform in the government of the Syrian regime, the number of sheep declined from 18 million heads in 2012 to 15 million heads in 2018. The number of cows decreased from 1 million heads to 300 thousand heads during the same period, while the number of goats decreased from 2. million to 1,500 million heads during the same period. As for the numbers of poultry, they decreased from 25 million birds to 17 million birds. Thus, meat production decreased from 400 thousand tons to 300 thousand tons, milk production decreased from 200 million tons to two million tons, and egg production increased from three billion eggs to one billion eggs during the same period . meaning that the number of current breeders is approximately 30% of their number before 2011 . Unfortunately, as a result of the massive displacement during the previous period, the number of breeders increased in a narrow area (northern Syria) and in the camps, without an increase in pasture. . This resulted in a decrease in the number of sheep heads And he pushed the breeders to reduce their livestock numbers because there was no space for grazing and the inability to buy feed
