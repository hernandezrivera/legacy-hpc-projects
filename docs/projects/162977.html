Project Summary

This program seeks to respond to the drought emergency in Zimbabwe by implementing various programs in education assistance, school feeding, resilience building and livelihoods strengthening in Mutoko and Makonde districts with particular attention to children between the ages of 3 and 12 years in 2020. Through community based interventions and partnerships, FOST will implement twelve main activities which include establishment of low input nutrition gardens in and out of schools, school feeding committees and training of committee members in order to complement the schools’ efforts in the school feeding scheme in face of the drought. With children as the targeted beneficiaries, the drought response program will take a gender sensitive approach in implementing its activities, consulting members of marginalized groups, women, children, persons with disability and other stakeholders to ensure impartiality of the humanitarian assistance.

1 - Activities

Objective 1: To improve learner well-being for 69935 children by strengthening the school feeding program in 100 schools within Mashonaland West (Makonde) and in Mashonaland East (Mutoko) by December 2020. Activities:  School nutrition gardens for 100 schoolsEach targeted school will have at least one nutritional garden with drought resistant vegetables for relish in the school feeding of learners  School Feeding Committees and Training for 100 schoolsEach school to have a functional school feeding committee comprising of male and female school staff, school development committee members, health workers and village head representatives.  Food handling training Each school feeding committee to be trained on proper hygiene, food handling, preparation and storage for the school feeding program Clean water sources for schools advocacyEach school to have at least one clean source of water for the school feeding program and for learners' drinking  Clean kitchen areas for schools advocacyEach targeted school to have a safe kitchen area for storing and preparing food for school feeding. Schools will be encouraged to use the block grant to buy materials such as cement that cannot be made available locally Objective 2: To reduce by at least 70% the risk of school drop-outs for boys and girls between the ages of 3 and 12 years in Mashonaland West (Makonde) and Mashonaland East (Mutoko) through strategic education support and awareness raising by December 2020 Activities  Education Awareness Campaigns Communities in the two district to be sensitized on the importance of education as a right of children, particularly girls  Block grants School fees assistance to at least 50% of boys and girls who are in need of education assistance through block granting for schools covering 3 school terms in 2020  PSS in emergencies trainings for teachers Each school will have 1 Male and 1 female teacher trained on PSS and emergency sensitive education delivery to learners  Sanitary packs for girls in 50 schools Girls between 11 and 14 years to receive reusable sanitary wear in schools at the beginning of the year. Objective 3: To strengthen livelihoods and improve food security for 20000 households and Orphans/Vulnerable Children from the targeted 69935 people in need in Mashonaland West (Makonde) and in Mashonaland East (Mutoko) by December 2020 Activities :  Pfumvudza ‘feed the hungry’ plots Very vulnerable and child headed families to be trained on and given inputs for small scale intensive farming  Low input nutrition gardens200 Child headed families, granny headed and women headed households to receive implements for low input nutrition gardens to ensure that children do not go to school with empty stomachs yet they walk long distances to school Food packs distribution 100 Boys and Girls in child headed families to receive food packs with basic commodities like grain and high protein cereals
