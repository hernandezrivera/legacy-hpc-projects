Project Summary

(Planned – ( SO1 )
First: The project plans to secure daily provision of free bread to 4000 HHs IDPs in 18camps of Idleb , and Throughout the project, Minber Al-Sham will ensure the daily provision of 2 KG free bread portions for families in targeted sites.
Minber Al-Sham will carry out this project through a chain of activities starting from purchasing the wheat flour from Turkey, bread baking process through contracting bakeries and distributing the bread through establishing distribution centers in the targeted sites.
Through this chain of activities, Minber Al-Sham will ensure the continuous monitoring of bread quality and daily provision to the people in need.
- Enhance the food security situation of 4000 families (20000 BNFs)
- Reduce negative coping mechanisms.
- Reduce the percentage of child labor by securing some of the family's needs.
- Enhance the economic situation of refugees by creating job opportunities for project workers.

4 - Needs

Around 6.5 million people in Syria are estimated to be food insecure and in need of food and livelihood support. An additional 2.5 million people are at risk of food insecurity and need livelihoods to support to strengthen their resilience (FAO/WFP Crop and Food Security Assessment 2019), referring to HNO 2021, • Between 2020 and 2021, the number of people in need of humanitarian assistance in northwest Syria has increased from 2.8 to 3.4 million people. The overall humanitarian situation remains dire for 4.2 million people living in Idleb and Afrin as well as A’zaz to Jarablus areas in northwest Syria. Humanitarian needs in northwest Syria continue to be driven by the impacts of over a decade of hostilities exacerbated by economic deterioration and multiple displacements.• Meanwhile, according to the Humanitarian Needs Assessment Programme (HNAP), 242,468 individuals have returned to their place of origin in northwest Syria in 2020. Between January and February 2021, close to 7,400 people are estimated to have returned to southern Idleb and western Aleppo governorates despite difficult conditions for return, including contamination of explosive hazards, and limited access to basic service and livelihood opportunities.

Assumptions & Risks Risk 1: Possible new emergency displacements Status: high Mitigation Measures: 1-Minber Al-Sham (MSO) MEAL team will assess the needs and share them with the cluster. Risk 3: risks related to storing large amounts of food such as damage and security risks. Status: low Mitigation Measures: 1- MSO will procure the dry foodstuff in batches congenial with the capacity of the stores in each site 3- MSO MEAL team will check the status of dry foodstuff before distribution
