Project Summary

The project will focus on the light to medium rehabilitation of apartments and buildings categorized under Level 2 (Moderate damage) according to the classification of damages done by the multisectoral assessments carried out by Wash and Shelter sectors. AAH will work in close collaboration with the Wash Sector to rehabilitate the plumbing system inside the apartments. The intervention will focus mainly on rehabilitating and fixing glasses, doors, walls, ceilings and light rehabilitations inside kitchen and bathrooms if needed. Also, AAH will support on the rehabilitation of the communal areas damaged in the blast, such as stairs, windows frames and glasses, entrance door (glass and lock). AAH estimations considers than only around 50% of the HH in the area of intervention will need support, either because the appartments have been already repaired or because the damage in some appartments didn't reach level 2. This assumption has been taken into consideration to calculate the number of HH and beneficiariaies benefitting from the intervention: 522HH for a total 1.566 individuals (disaggregated: 0-2 years: 35 females; 35 males/ 3-17 years: 156 females: 156 males/ 18-64 years: 661 females; 348 males/ 65+ years: 105 females; 70 males)
As it has been discussed in different coordination forums, this intervention needs to be a combined between Shelter and WASH sectors in order to reestablish a minimum standard of living conditions and AAH will do so. AAH will also apply to the Wash Flash Appeal, so the combined intervention inside the building (WASH + Shelter) will be fully undertaking by AAH, facilitating the process for the victims and the coordination with the different actors implicated in the Beirut Blast Response.

Plan fields

1 - Needs

Both WASH and Shelter Sectors have carried out multisectorial assessments in the different neighborhoods affected by the blast to identify the level of damage of each building and categorize the damaged areas into 3 levels: 1) Light Damage, where the WASH infrastructures are in good shape and only a Shelter rehabilitation will be needed. 2) Moderate damage: basic services to the houses are affected, including access to water, wastewater and electricity services. A combined intervention between Shelter and WASH is needed, in order to reestablish a minimum standard of living conditions. 3) Severe damage: Buildings too close to the blast have been severely damaged, including structural damages, and a light or medium rehabilitation from the WASH/Shelter partners is not applicable.This project will address rehabilitation Works of level 2 buildings and apartments in the selected area.

2 - Activities

1.1 Technical visit and elaboration of the BOQ’s: Based on the assessments, AAH Shelter and WASH teams will visit the apartments to confirm the needs of the first assessment and establish a detail BOQ for the rehabilitation of the apartment and the plumbing connections.1.2 Rehabilitation Works at 522 apartments. This will be undertaken by external contractors in order to accelerate the process. This way, AAH can control the quality of the works and supervise that the contracted company follows the BOQ’s established by the team.1.3 Rehabilitation works inside 87 buildings. In case there is any need to rehabilitate any water or wastewater connection to the distribution and collection network, AAH will take in charge this rehabilitation too.
