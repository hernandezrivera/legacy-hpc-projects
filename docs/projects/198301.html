Project Summary

GOALPrime Organization Nigeria (GPON) targets to reach a total number of 47,000 people; out of which 10,000 (80% girls & 20% boys) who are survivors of sexual assault with Comprehensive GBV case management and specialized services including Clinical Management of Rap (CMR), and psychosocial support services. 21950 (60% women/girls and 40% men/boys) people will be reached through house-to-house sensitization, on GBV prevention and principles that address harmful social norms, 10,000 women and girls will be reached with the distribution of Menstrual Hygiene management kits and dignity kits while 5000 (60% women/girls and 40% men/boys) with peer support, mentoring/coaching, skills building and empowerment programs in camps and communities of Konduga, Ngala, Damboa and Mafa LGAs. 20,000 Information Education and communication-IEC Materials will be printed through GPON Humanity Press and distributed to beneficiaries who will be reached with GBV sensitization as well as posted in all locations of engagement strategically. The IEC materials will be developed in Hausa, Kanuri, and English languages in collaboration with the GBV subsector, the Ministry of Women Affairs and Social Development (MWSAD), and Translators without Boarder. 10% of the total number of beneficiaries to be reached is targeted at people living with disabilities.
GOALPrime in collaboration with the Ministry of Women Affairs and social development (MWSAD) will develop prevention and response jingles/messages on gender-based violence which will be aired consistently on the Radio Station with coverage reaching Konduga, Ngala, Damboa, and Mafa LGAs. GOALPrime will also set up GBV child-friendly reporting mechanisms and zero GBV clubs in schools and camps of its location of implementation, as well as provide phone booth services for case management support for survivors, especially in cases of emergencies.
GOALPrime will carry out capacity building quarterly for 50 women and girls in each location of its engagement throughout the period of the project implementation, empowering them as result to be the first respondents in their communities and camps, also GPON will work with the women leaders in these locations of engagements to create a “No GBV Women’s Network” in each location of engagement. GPON will also conduct capacity building and strengthening for GBV workers on proper case management at their field level.
Through Coordination with partners in the project locations, GOALPrime will identify livelihood actors for linkage of Survival of Sexual and Gender-Based Violence (SGBV) for livelihood support. Through this project, GOALPrime targets to identify 400 girls (SGBVs)with livelihood challenges and provide linkage to access livelihood support from livelihood partners across the project location.
GOALPrime seeks to strongly strengthen GBV referral pathways in each location. Through its ongoing GBV project in some of these locations, GPON has seen the need to strengthen actors' coordination and seeks to support quarterly coordination amongst actors in each of the sites. GOALPrime will be fully operational in all LGA throughout the implementation of this project. And continues assessment of camp and community response to the cases of GBV to inform subsequent project design and planning.

1 - Needs

An estimated 3 million people across Borno, Adamawa, and Yobe states need interventions for the prevention of and response to gender-based violence (GBV). The underlying drivers of the conflict and conditions of displacement continue to exacerbate the occurrence of GBV incidents. Half of this population in need consists of children (below 18 years) with the majority being adolescent girls. Sexual violence - including rape, sexual exploitation, forced and early marriage, survival sex and domestic violence, physical assault, denial of resources, and psychological and emotional abuse - are among the most frequently reported violations, recurrently perpetrated by intimate or former partners. Minors, particularly adolescent girls, are also at risk of GBV, which occurs mainly in the context of child sexual abuse and early marriage. Also, an estimated 2.7 million children and adolescents (1.5 million girls and 1.2 million boys) in northeast Nigeria need protection and cross-sectoral services. Threats and risks to the well-being of girls and boys include family separation, gender-based violence including child marriage and conflict-related sexual violence, recruitment, and use of children, abduction, and other grave child rights violations. At least 6,000 children (more than 50 percent girls) are estimated to have been recruited, abducted, killed, or victims of violent attacks by armed groups since the beginning of the conflict. Of those, approximately 50 percent are survivors of SGBV. The crisis also has caused severe psychological distress among children. The conflict has not only disrupted the social fabric but has also negatively affected the ability of parents and communities to appropriately care for their children. The Humanitarian Response Plan for 2021 shows the critical Gender-based violence (GBV) needs in Kala-Balge, Ngala, Damboa and Mafa LGAs, with a PiN estimate of30,198, 39,479, 156,665, and 113,939 respectively. These critical gender-based violence needs have also been confirmed by GOALPrime Organization Nigeria (GPON) through her activities of engagements in these LGAs and feedbacks from the community’s members, communities’ stakeholders. Hence, the need to build the resilience of survivors of Gender-Based Violence by strengthening the prevention and response services through community sensitization, capacity building, PSS, and referrals for specialized services including provision of clinical management of rap in Kala/Balge, Ngala, Damboa and Mafa LGAs of Borno state.

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

By integrating every strategy with the protection principles, GOALPrime will mainstream protection (GBV & PSEA) throughout the execution of this project and advance meaningful access, safety, and dignity of both beneficiaries and of employers and volunteers. Safety, decency, and avoiding any kind of harm to both beneficiaries and employees are priorities for GOALPrime. Additionally, GOALPrime will also makesure that the target population has equitable access to the service, free from impediments and prejudice, in accordance with their needs.GOALPrime will pay attention to people and groups who could be especially vulnerable or have a hard time obtaining help and resources, including those with disabilities and GBV survivors who have been identified. GOALPrime will integrate GBV Risk Mitigation efforts to reduce or decrease GBV risk to beneficiaries. GOALPrime will work consciously in compliance with the Sphere Humanitarian Core Standards and Imperatives. All approaches will align with the principles of protection as we remain committed to ensuring that this project does not expose beneficiaries to more risk in compliance with the DO NO HARM Protection Principle. Also, GOALPrime will be intentional about maintaining an environment where there is zero Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (SEA). All staff, volunteers, contractors, and vendors engaged directly or indirectly to implement this project will be given PSEA orientation. They will be familiarized with GOALPrime's policy on PSEA, and they MUST express their unwavering commitment by signing GOALPrime's Zero Tolerance to SEA Code of Conduct. Also, GOALPrime will also lay down reporting protocols for the reporting of PSEA cases if unfortunately, they occur. GOALPrime will also ensure that the identity of the reporter(s) is kept very confidential. When convenient, GOALPrime willencourage that reports are made on anonymous grounds. Also, being guided by the Inter-Agency mechanism for reporting SEA, GOALPrime will ensure a systematized engagement with affectedpopulations including confidential complaints and investigations procedures at both the system and individual agency levels. As recommended by IASC Task Force on PSEA which identified inter-agency cooperation in the creation and maintenance of community-based complaints mechanisms (CBCMs) as a key component in the prevention and response to SEA. GOALPrime will continue to raise awareness to the target population that humanitarian assistance is free and never conditioned on sexual favors as well as to be informed on how to access the appropriate complaints mechanisms, and support to set up in the community if SEA occurs. Furthermore, GOALPrime will ensure that established CBCMs in target locations are effective and that there is inter-agency coordination to ensure consistent messaging and that access to the mechanism is as broad and straightforward as possible for potential complainants. GOALPrime will also be intentional about the environment and safety of the communities where we implement by ensuring that our approaches and tools does not contribute to the degradation of the environment. In addition, GOALPrime will also integrate climate change and provide routine sensitization of the project beneficiaries on climate change prevention. GOALPrime shall mainstream CP, GBV & SEAinto all training contents as well as approaches.

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

GOALPrime will be consolidating its established community-based project accountability mechanism through this project to improve community stakeholders and project beneficiary participation both in the project decision-making and implementation. This project was designed in consultation with the community stakeholders and children based on lessons learned from our previous implementation in thetarget LGAs.GPON uses a people-centered feedback mechanism to gather opinions, and complaints of the community members and beneficiaries on its project to inform decision-making for quality project implementation. However, lessons learned from the approach includes, forming an LGA-level project committee that will include all community groups' representative and stakeholders to improve the participation of the affectedcommunity in the project implementation process. GPON has also made provision for anonymous feedbacks complaint/feedback boxes, SMSs, and direct calls to the Compliance and Accountability team.GPON project team will conduct an LGA inception workshop with the established community-based project committee to discuss the project deliverables and the community-level needs and how this project will contribute to resolving the needs. Also, the LGA project committee will work with the project team to define project beneficiaries’ vulnerability based on the context of the LGA, agree on vulnerability criteria, and provide a recommended approach on how the project can be implemented to better reach and serve the project targeted groups.Also, the project team will conduct quarterly review meetings with the community-based project committee at the LGA level to inform how each quarter can consolidate with the previous quarter's achievement to improve the quality of services rendered.
