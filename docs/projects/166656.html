Project Summary

1. Train and Support the work of Protection Community Focal Points (PFPs) who play a key role in identifying protection risks and solutions at community level. PFPs are rained on protection, COVID-19 prevention and mitigation measures, serve as focal points in IDP sites and other camp-like settings on protection matters and have mobile phones so community outreach and case management can be facilitated remotely. New PFPs will be identified, trained and equipped with mobile phones in priority locations.
2. Use innovation and technology to reach vulnerable communities: map out community structures in hard-to-reach areas and provide focal points with mobile phones and air time which enables quick dissemination of life-saving informaiton and remote case management where possible.
3. Undertake collection of critical life-saving protection data (protection monitoring) and analysis based and produce bi-weekly protection dashboards and reports. Collection of protection data will better enable the promote the inclusion of everyone, irrespective of status, in the national response plans including IDPs, refugees, asylum-seekers, migrants and stateless persons. This will also enable the identification of groups that may be more vulnerable to COVID-19, allowing necessary interventions.
4. Distribute life-saving items, such as soap and water tanks, to vulnerable groups such as persons with disabilities and older persons, especially to IDPs and refugees in camp or camp like settings as well as urban settings in which these groups are also located.
5. Set up and strenghten a urban protection respoonse in highly vulnerable urban communities, providing life-saving information to vulnerable groups such as street children and older persons in urban settlements, which may also have a positive impact on refugees and displaced communities.
