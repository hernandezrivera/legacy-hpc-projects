Project Summary

The integrated Somalia nutrition response aims to save lives and alleviate further suffering through accelerated nutrition recovery and sustained rehabilitation of 211,412 beneficiaries affected by malnutrition. The interventions will accelerate active case finding for timely diagnosis, prompt malnutrition treatment, Health, nutrition and hygiene promotion and linkage with livelihood and health interventions aimed at sustaining the rehabilitation. Active case finding will be done through nutrition screening of most at risk groups, promote and strengthen family participation in nutrition surveillance through Family-led MUAC screening for timely detection and referral of malnutrition cases. Through prompt identification and referral of cases, diagnosed children, pregnant and lactating women will be referred for nutrition rehabilitation through outpatient treatment of moderately malnourished beneficiaries. Severely malnourished children will be provided with therapeutic nutrition treatment at outpatient therapeutic feeding program sites. Severely malnourished children with medical complications will be admitted for stabilization care support at 3 stabilization care centers that will be managed by this project and integrated with the health interventions .Case management of moderate acute malnutrition will be integrated and linked to targeted supplementary feeding program facilities with support from WFP and partners in the respective locations .These services will be provided at 38 fixed and 7 outreach sites located in 15 districts across 6 regions in Puntland, Somaliland and Southern Somalia. The interventions will also provide high impact health and nutrition interventions aimed at mitigating malnutrition and its consequences among vulnerable groups, actions will include deworming ,micronutrient supplementation ( vitamin A ,Iron and folate supplementation) and home fortification of foods including distribution of micronutrient powders) and systematic treatment as outlined in the CMAM protocol .
Through Family-led MUAC , Household members will be trained and supported to carry out MUAC screening at the household level and facilitate self-referrals for treatment .Households with children on malnutrition treatment will be linked to the food security and livelihood interventions aimed at improving household access to basic food. IYCF knowledge, support and practice will be promoted through campaigns, mother to mother and father to father support groups. These nutrition interventions will further be promoted through timed and targeted counselling (ttC) that focuses on 10 key messages during scheduled CHW home visits with the emphasis message informed by the beneficiary physiological stage

Mitigation Measures

WV will put robust measures to address barriers that hinder access to health and nutrition services or prevent health-seeking behaviors especially among youth, disabled, illiterate and poor. WV will coordinate its activities and identify disadvantaged and vulnerable groups (IDPs, minority clans, other marginalized) that may face unequal access to nutrition services, preventing unintended harm working in close collaboration with other community resource persons. Their feedback will influence program to continuously adapt and cater to nutrition needs of all groups .In consultation with community members and with MOH, WV will be responsible for identification of beneficiaries. WV will promote active and representative participation of different groups in communities to influence decisions that affect their lives. To ensure transparency, communities will be sensitized on available services, nutrition program admission, treatment process and beneficiary entitlement and discharge criteria, their rights and what is expected of community members and each beneficiary. WV will further work together with MOH to strengthen coordination mechanisms, which will ensure effective service provision and prevent duplication of efforts while reallocating and prioritizing resources to underserved areas. WV working with MOH will provide timely information to target population regarding project objective, interventions and timeline to make informed decisions and provide relevant comments. Inception meetings will be held both at regional and community level to share information on all aspects of interventions including support for SGBV, protection and evictions, Accountability and Feedback System: The project will take into consideration Inter-Agency Standing Committee commitment to affected populations. Functional feedback system will be established for continuous collection of feedback from community. The project will leverage on existing WV Community Response Map platform that facilitates online recording, tracking, compilation and visual mapping and feedback received from community members daily. A 3 digit Toll free number already in use in the target locations will be strengthened and integrated with Interactive Voice Response system, suggestion boxes and community help desk teams to facilitate feedback.WV will engage the CNW to deliberately do home visits for Households with children,pregnant and lactating women living with dissabilities whilst sensitizing community beneficiaries on issues affecting PLWDs ,in all project engagements, WVI will strive to have 15% particiapnt representation by PLWDs and engage dissability association groups and other advocacy groups to mitigate stigma against PLWDs
