Project Summary

Under this project, The Rescue Mission will conduct life-saving WASH activities which will contribute to the reduction in morbidity and mortality resulting from waterborne diseases, by increasing access to safe water, sanitation and better hygiene practices among displaced populations, vulnerable communities, and host communities in Morobo, Pibor, Kapoeta South, and Ikotoes Counties.
TERM’s activities in this project will include safe water provision through water point rehabilitation (hand pumps), Water yards rehabilitation water quality monitoring and treatment of contaminated water source, provision of household water filters, depending on the needs); provision or rehabilitation of sanitation infrastructure (latrines, hand washing facilities and solid waste management); hygiene promotion (hand washing campaigns in communities and institutions, safe water chain and jerrycan cleaning campaigns); capacity-building of community institutions (pump mechanics, water user committees, community hygiene promoters, school's hygiene clubs), and distribution of WASH NFIs (soap, hygiene kits, menstrual hygiene kit, water containers, water purification filters, PUR or others).
Delivery of this lifesaving intervention will help in increasing knowledge and capacity to prevent outbreak of WASH related diseases including cholera and diarrhea that undermines and threatens lives of children such as under 5, people living with disabilities, pregnant and lactating women in areas affected by severe acute malnutrition. The project will also reduce the risk of GBV associated by women and girls walking long distances in search of water, the repair of water sources will reduce chances of young girls dropping from school to collect water while children in school will have opportunity to WASH services that seek to improve their health. The intervention is designed to build community resilience
The proposed intervention will reach a target of 30,000 vulnerable people (7500 men, 8500 women, 7000 boys and 7000 girls) among internally displaced persons, returnees, and host communities including people with disabilities in the targeted locations
Throughout the response life cycle, TERM team will coordinate with the authorities, community and the key partners on ground who are implementing WASH, nutrition, health, and protection for integration of activities to ensure maximum utilization of resources.
The intervention will include detailed needs assessment to identify the needs, affected gender, age and diversity groups, the vulnerabilities of the targeted community and coping strategies employed by the affected population. Based on the assessment report, interventions will be designed accordingly with TERM capacity, response with other partners will be planned.
