Project Summary

Recent earthquakes and floods have caused significant damage and displacement in Lattakia and Tartous, Syria. This proposal aims to address the urgent need for winter supplies by providing Non-Food Items (NFI) kits to affected households.
Target Population:
The target population includes families residing in:
Emergency shelters: Individuals and families displaced from their homes due to the disaster.
Partially damaged structures: Households living in buildings deemed structurally safe after minor damage.
Proposed Intervention:
The proposal seeks to distribute winter NFI kits containing essential items to help families stay warm and protected during the harsh winter season. These kits may include:
Winter clothing: Jackets, sweaters, thermal wear, hats, gloves, and scarves for adults and children.
Bedding: Blankets, sleeping bags, and mattresses.
Shelter accessories: Plastic sheeting for temporary repairs, ropes, and tools for minor shelter improvements.
Essential household items: Kitchen sets, jerry cans for water storage, and solar lamps for lighting.
While the information I provided outlines the NFI proposal for winter kits, it unfortunately doesn't include the exact number of affected families in Lattakia and Tartous. The proposal focuses on targeting specific groups like those in emergency shelters or partially damaged structures, but the total number isn't explicitly stated.
