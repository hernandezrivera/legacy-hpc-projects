Project Summary

In 2020, Mali is still facing multiple security tensions, natural disasters and inter-communal conflicts which primarily affects Segou and Tombouctou regions. These factors have caused numerous population displacements and put a strain on resources (raw materials, energy, shelter, etc.) and livelihoods. These recurring shocks affect the vulnerability of these populations who are not able to access basic needs and suffer from a critical lack of access to basic goods and services, in particular WASH services. Insufficient access to safe water, both in terms of quality and quantity, especially worsen their exposition to waterborne diseases. In parallel, health facilities are not able to provide quality care due to a lack of means and the absence of operational WASH services. In the given settings, Mali is particularly vulnerable to the COVID-19 epidemic that appeared in China at the end of 2019 and out broke in the country since March 11, 2020. Indeed, the Malian health and sanitary system is insufficient to hamper the propagation of the virus and to ensure an adequate response to the crisis. The lack of access to water of most vulnerable populations restricts basic hygiene practices (especially frequent hand washing with soap) that could limit their exposition to the virus.
This project aims at improving the living conditions of populations particularly vulnerable to the various shocks (conflicts, climatic variations, sudden populations movements) and to enhance the prevention against malnutrition through a WASH in Nut integrated approach. This project comprises three key goals:
1/ The first goal is to contribute to meeting water needs and to prevent health risks related to population displacements. An emergency plan is maintained preventively in order to allow the rapid deployment of emergency activities related to safe water access, distribution of water purification products and WASH NFI kits, and to carry out the necessary repairs on water points that are out of use or overexploited.
2/ The second goal is to cater for water needs of breeders and agro-breeders and to reduce sanitation risks and diseases to which these communities are exposed. To achieve this goal, SI plans to rehabilitate water points by transforming them into improved village hydraulic systems (IVHS) or Improved Pastoral Hydraulic System (IPHS) for agro pastoralism. A specific focus will be made on water services’ sustainability in coordination with local stakeholder and by supporting beneficiaries’ ownership and good local governance.
3/ The third goal is to develop a set of WASH in Nut response by providing a complete WASH package within the health facilities and through the detection of Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM). This axis will be implemented in partnership with Health/Nutrition actors of the area as well as the community health workers.
These three program’ axes will be combined with crosscutting hygiene promotion activities in order to influence behavioral changes and to promote best hygiene practices to reduce water borne-diseases and to limit the spread of the COVID-19 virus. These crosscutting activities will be supported by WASH NFI kits distributions that are tailored to households’ specific needs.
