Project Summary

SEMA plans to build on the previous projects in order to proceed in providing preventive
and responsive CP services in 8 CFSs in Azaz, Bulbul, Sharan, Albab, Ariha, Janoudyeih, Harim and Idleb
sub-districts including:
1-Structured PSS to the children(B&G) and youth (M&F) through using "Child Resilience"
and “Youth Resilience” curriculums for children and youth to develop their self-resilience
and social skills such as cultural and recreation skills.
2-Document and provide services for child survivals and at-risk cases including children
with disabilities, where a supervision mechanism will be in place to support caseworkers
to provide safe, ethical and competent case management services.
3-Providing emergency CvA to enable immediate response for cases when it is needed.
4-Provision cumulative awareness-raising sessions that will cover CP issues (Child labor, Family
separation, dropping out of schools ,Child marriage, dealing with CWDs, and Trafficking) through outreach teams.
5-Supporting 5 Community Child Protection Networks (CPNs) in order to respond to
violations affecting children. CPNs will be composed of youth and adults (M&F) including
people with disabilities volunteering to protect children in their communities.
6-Providing rehabilitation program for children with disabilities in 3 locations in addition
to parenting skills for CwDs parents.
7-Providing children under 8 years old with Early Child hood Developing program using
IRC curricula “Ahlan Simsim" and Reach Up and learn“ to enhance their social and
emotional skills.
Moreover, SEMA will mobilize its Health, Nutrition, Physiotherapy, Specialized Mental
Health services to build an internal referral pathway in order to contribute to a family
strengthening approach.
The project activities will be implemented through 6 CFS and outreach teams (M&F) in
Albab, Azaz, Idleb,, Bulbul Sharran, and Aljanoudeyh. PSS animators will provide structured PSS,
ECD services, and parenting skills. The 6 CFS will be static, the outreach team will take
each facility as a starting point for their activities in the surrounding communities. In each
location, there will be 1 CP case management supervisor, 3 case specialists, and 2
outreach teams each one consisting of 4 outreach workers who will identify and refer the
cases to the CFS and provide semi-structured PSS and awareness-raising sessions to (B, G,
M, F) on CP issues a. One SNR specialist will provide Rehabilitation Program for CwDs in 3 locations .
SEMA will ensure good access of adolescents girls to the services through provision transportation.
Supervision will be conducted by CM Supervisors to ensure the quality of Case management service, and ensure that all
CvA documents are in line with CP Sub-Cluster guidelines. SEMA is committed to submitting the 4Ws on monthly basis. SEMA MEAL
team will conduct different activities to monitor the work progress in each CFS including the following tools:
Monitoring visits- Bimonthly, satisfaction surveys, FGDs, and pre-post test of the training
and PSS sessions.
