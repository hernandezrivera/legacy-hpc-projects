Project Summary

" To facilitate our collective planning and resource mobilization for the COVID-19 response, RPN had been taken to initiatives the project titled “WASH for Rohingya & Host Community “with a view to aiming establishing pure water supply network. Under the last year WASH project RPN had been established 3 deep tube well and purchased 3 food grade water tankers and established 30 distribution points in different place of the Rohinigya camp. Through using this tools a distribution network had been established. Every day the inhabitants of the adjacent distribution's point came for collecting water. It was very effective tools to minimize the water scarcity of Rohingya community. Considering the last year experience RPN, has plan to expand the project activities. Under the project, will expand the water supply network through using of existing water sources and water vehicles. In addition 20 distribution point will increase which will added with the total of 30 prior distribution points. In the same way every day the water tankers will collect fresh water from existing and newly install deep tube well and deliver it in specific distribution point. The Rohingya community will collect water from the points. During water collection they will be motivated to improved hygienic practice through leaflet. Our WASH volunteer will disseminate the information among the community.
RPN has willing to extend the WASH facilities among the FDMNs as well as host community. In addition further construction and operation and maintenance of existing latrine and bathing space according to sector priority. Out of camp's activities 200 hand pump will be installed for the of host community in order to minimize the scarcity of pure water and reduce the water borne diseases from the community. Installation place will select consult with the local community and Department of Public Health Engineering."

Needs

"The project target the Rohingya refugee and host community intent to help them to gain access to clean and improved water sources. To achieve the target the following activities has been set; 1. Operation and maintenance the existing and new tubes well around Rohingya community. 2. To establish water tank at different location in the camp according to populations demand.3.Operation and maintenance of existing and new Latrine & Bathing Spaces.4.Ensuring Hygiene promotion maintain facility safe & healthy menstrual hygiene management practices.accessing hygiene supplies through by volunteer voucher systems.

Activities or outputs

"1) Provide soap, hygiene kits, aqua tabs both in camps and Host’s communities2) Organised disinfection of WASH facilities and common spaces3) Provide adequate WASH facilities in health care facilities and isolation centers4) Increase coverage and operationalisation of hand-washing stations both in camps and host’s communities. 5) Strengthen hygiene promotion focusing on Covid-19 "
