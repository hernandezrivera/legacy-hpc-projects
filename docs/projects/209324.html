Project Summary

Kassala Teaching Hospital is the main hospital in Kassala State. The emergency/ surgery room is the only one, currently, in kassala state which provides the services for the whole of the state's residents in addition to the refugees and the current IDPs. This emergency room is currently working with only 30% of its intended capacity. The emergency room has a limited refurbishment done by the ministry of health resources was 5 years ago. As the only healthcare unit of this type in the state, it is important to help raise its capacity to it full potential especially in the current situation of huge displaced people moving in to Kassala state.
