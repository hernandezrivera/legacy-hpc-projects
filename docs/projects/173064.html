Project Summary

GFO Great Family Organization is a NNGO founded in 2006 in South Darfur. GFO used to be the Co-coordinator with UN OCHA and HAC of El Salam IDPs camp from 2007 to 2011. GFO was managing all humanitarian activities in the camp including WASH.
GFO is member of the WASH working Group in South Darfur, attending all meeting and reporting as an implementing partner with WVI in OFDA project since 2014 until todayc covering 4 localities in South Darfur.
The result of the last joint rapid assessment' final report issued this December 2020 indicated following findings: The withdrawal of the Sudan Government from the entire Jabal Marrah and the continued confrontation of the two sides and among the oppositions' fractions, this resulted to un-sustainability among the population of the areas, more than 100,000 people fled from their home villages in East Jabal Marrah to several areas within Marra Jabal, particularly: Dirbat 47,000, Soni 2,214 and Jawa 29,101. The recent report of the humanitarian Agencies' joint assessment mission conducted in Nov 15th, 2020 indicated the high need of humanitarian services including water supply and sanitation to more than 78,000 persons in very poor locations around their destroyed villages.
The project is designed to rehabilitate water supply facilities to provide clean water to the beneficiaries, construct new emergency latrines and sustain latrines to those who return back to their homes. Formation and training of 50 water user committees (WUCs), provision of maintenance tools for sustain water supply. Conduct training of Wash emergency preparedness and risk reduction techniques.
For Covid 19 program, the project will provide 100 hand-washing facilities, distribution of 78,000 face-masks for every individual, the project will disseminate awareness on Covid 19 through workshops, trainings, information sharing and coordination between the community leaders, the Humanitarian actors, Mothers, Fathers, Boys and Girls. Refer chronic status to health services, staff training on protection of Covid 19 risk.
