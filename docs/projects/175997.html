Project Summary

2 - Project activities

UNIDOR will carryout activities to realize the identified needs in the communities in Torit, Yei, Koch, Rubkona and Panyijiar as follows: Mobile and Static protection monitoring and assessment, the project will provide mix of mobile and static interventions to provide general protection services for equitable access to basic services for vulnerable population while ensuring protection monitoring to reduce risks and threats of people affected by crisis. 1. Conduct Mobile outreaches through mobile team of staff in Yei ant Torit within local structures like schools, churches and public facilities, pitching tents to conduct awareness, identification including people with specific needs, MHPSS assessment and counseling, distribution of NFIs and protection desk for information sharing and incident reporting, with reports analyzed and presented to cluster as necessary. 2. Conduct static response in Rubkona, Koch, panyijiar where UNIDOR will use its facilities and partner with community structures where to avail static protection desk centers.3. The project will engage the communities in Protection awareness raising and prevention messaging, including use of media in raising awareness, this will inform the communities of the context risks and threats and empower them to make decisions to protect themselves and their loved ones. 3. Provision of Core relief non-food items (NFIs) to those affected by life threatening shocks of displacement due to violent conflict and effects of climate change observed in floods affected counties of catastrophic and crisis levels. Provisions of such NFIs will improve their living conditions to ensure some level of dignity, while saving life of the affected communities.Activity 4. The project will conduct Mobile and static capacity building training on Community based preparedness and risk mitigation to leaders of women led groups, youth leaders, leaders of organization for persons living with disabilities(OPLWD@7% representation level), community leaders and local government authority to further engage the community to map communities perceived risks and threats and to best prepare to prevent and mitigate to identified trends and spontaneous crisis that may be life threatening to the population. 5 : The project will organize peaceful co-existance activities and conduct community awareness on COVID-19 community based risks awareness and response , to prevent spread of Corona Virus and awareness on updated referral pathway for COVID-19 treatment. During the community awareness sessions, distribution of COVID -19 preventive protective equipment will be distributed to general public and staff and health workers, including handkerchiefs to reduce open spitting in public.6. Involve experts to conduct training to frontline workers from health facilities including project staff on COVID-19 Risk communication & Community engagement to better engage with the community and cadre of experts to ensure safety in service deliver during the COVID-19 pandemic .7. Provide mental health and psychosocial support(MHPSS), including PSS assessment to conflict and crisis affected women, girls, boys men and people with disabilities in the target areas to o reduce individual distress, improve their wellbeing and living conditions to adequately respond to shocks of distress and crisis. 8. Conduct safety audit FGDs to understand the benefits and gaps in General protection needs during project implementation. Document lessons learned for knowledge sharing, adaptation.9. Training community leaders including women and youth leaders on peace building and conflict management, reconciliation and form community conflict management committee better manage conflict within the communities and create peaceful co-existence for durable solutions. baseline survey and individual assessment will be conducted to determine activities specific

7.4.2. How will your project provide beneficiaries information about Protection against Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA)?

UNIDOR recognize PSEA cases as Zero tolerance The project will be guided by Humanitarian and UNIDOR PSEA policies to ensure beneficiaries are informed, protected against sexual exploitation and abuse-PSEA. staffs including field staff and community volunteers will be trained on PSEA to further create awareness to the beneficiaries on -SEA. during such training the information on reporting and communication pathway will be outlined and made accessible by the community through displaying the name and contacts of PSEA focal point. UNIDOR organization through project provide information of PSEA focal point person and hotline( mobile contacts, for reporting allegations and incidences of SEA towards beneficiaries.All partners in contact with beneficiary must be informed of PSEA and child safeguarding policies. people with organizations contracts must also be informed and sign PSEA and child safeguarding policies to protect beneficiaries. The community will also be informed of Timeline for PSEA intervention and response.

Project staffs and particularly those managing -AAP information/protection desk will be trained on disability inclusion to best facilitate inclusions of persons with disabilities.There will be awareness raised to the general public on disability inclusion and how to facilitate communication and feedback barriers of reporting incidences and cases affecting all gender and ages.
