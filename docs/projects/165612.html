Project Summary

Red een Kind, (in English: Help a Child and hereafter named ‘Help a Child’) proposes a response to support returnee and IDP households affected by natural disasters in Bujumbura Mairie, Bujumbura Rural, Cibitoke and Rumonge. The project’s objective is to support these families with NFI kits, emergency shelter, food vouchers for women and girl heads of households, cash for work activities and capacity building support for savings and loans groups. The following interventions will consist of:
• Communication on COVID-19 prevention;
• Food vouchers for 140 women and girl heads of households;
• Reunification of 250 street children with their families or family friends;
• Distribution of emergency rent fees through mobile cash transfers for 3 months;
• Support for 1,500 IDPs and returnee households with NFI kits;
• Environment friendly cash for work activities for IDPs and returnees;
• Capacity building support for women savings and loans groups;
This intervention will assist primarily returnee and IDP families most affected by natural disasters. Help a Child will select its beneficiaries from registered IDPs and returnees by IOM, UNHCR or the Ministry of human rights, social affairs and gender in Burundi.
Help a Child will facilitate the installation of these families in collaboration with local authorities into the selected neighborhood homes. All support will be in line with validated sectoral standards and guidelines. Help a Child works in line with the Core Humanitarian Standards and has expertise in providing emergency support for IDP households.
