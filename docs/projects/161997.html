Project Summary

BRAc will support 8,236 HHs in camp 1E and 3,756 HHs in camp 17 with materials, training and technical support to ensure those HHs meet all minimum performance standards, construction of 300 midterm shelters in camps of 7, 26 & 27 and 100 host community shelter repair and maintenances. All shelters supported will also include household level drains, househokd access stairs and retaining walls for individual households.

Needs

Under the leadership of RRRC, the Shelter/NFI Sector has been performed and developed a needs analysis and strategy for Refugee, also in coordination with other Government counterparts at various levels to ensure that shelter approaches are informed and supported. Refugee are residing in same makeshift shelters that are severely below basic humanitarian standards, overcrowded, and will not withstand seasonal climatic conditions. The low quality of the shelters and poor terrain as well as the lack of privacy within these shelters has a serious impact on the physical and psychological well-being of the refugees, and especially for women, children and older persons. Shelters improvements and site improvements are urgently required prior to the next monsoon and cyclone season. Lacking adequate land, Rahingya refugee have built shelters on hills which are at risk of landslides and in areas prone to flooding. Assessment revealed that essential household needs vary considerably according to distributions to date and geographical location of HHs. Rohingya refugee are requesting support with replenishment of items which have been distributed before as well as some specific NFIs. Clothes and lighting sets are among those commonly requested items.

Activities or outputs

"1. Other Shelter Support: Any type of shelter intervention focus on those who are living in existing shelter. This support will be provided to 8,236 HHs in camp 1E and 3,756 HHs in camp 17. Shelter will be improved by providing shelter materials like RCC footing, Treated bamboo, plinth raising etc. as per sector guideline. This support will also aims to ensure that DRR elements as included such as drainage, land protection, bracing, fire safe kitchen areas and others. Special attention will be given to the HHs with EVI members including person will disability. BRAC will also treat bamboo that will be required to these HHs and by doing this increases durability of shelter and minimize negative impact on environment. 2. Midterm Shelter Assistance (MTS): Construction of MTS by treated Bamboo with following the Sector guidelines and also drawing, design approved by RRRC and UNHCR. Total 300 HHs for Camps will get MTS support in camps 7, 26 and 27 where space is available and BDT 1,60,000 per shelter. Funding can be converted to other type of shelter assistance if land is not available or there are any Government instructions. 3. Site Improvements interventions: Construction/Re-construction/Repair of small Stairs, Drains and Retaining walls etc. at HH level 4. 100 HHs in the host community will be supported with shelter re-construction or needed repairs at BDT 1,60,0005. BRAC will cover two catchment areas with catchment focal points. One in catchments in Teknaf (C21, 26, 27, Nyapara RC) and one in catchment area in Ukhia (C 1E, C1W, C7). Catchment focal point will support shelter emergency response with coordination of damage verification and provision of assistance as well as shelter support in case of cyclone. Collect and analyses sex, age and disability dis-aggregated data and conduct a participatory gender analysis to understand different shelter needs, capacities, barriers and aspirations and identify populations with special shelter support needs.Ensure a gender balance in the shelter assessment and analysis teams to enhance effective, safe and inclusive consultations with women, girls, men and boys. "
