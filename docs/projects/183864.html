1 - Needs

In Sudan, the humanitarian situation remains complex and critical needs remain. The 2019 HNO identified 5.7 million people in need, 1,640,419 million IDPs in Darfur. shortages of fuel and cash increase in prices of key commodities and medicines and subsequently reduced purchasing power. External shocks (conflict, natural disasters, and economic crisis), leading to a critical situation and poor wash, health and education outcomes among the most vulnerable. Nearly three million school-aged children are not in school mostly in the conflict-affected areas of Darfur region (HNO 2019. the Gross Enrolment Rate (GER) for Sudan is 71.4 percent. GRR in Darfur varies 22 per cent gap to 50 percent.1.8 million people, with women and children estimated 75 per cent rely on life-saving humanitarian assistance.2.9 million people in 11 states need humanitarian WASH assistance, of water, sanitation, and hand washing facilities.84 per cent of the people in need live in conflict‐ affected areas in the Darfur states. IPC 2018 projected over 5.7 million people in Sudan faces Crisis or Emergency levels of food security in early 2019, including 17% of North Darfur state residents. Of North Darfur Kabkabiya , Elserief , Saraf Omrra,, Allait and Tawilla Localities remains fragile with, chronic poverty, weak structures that lack capacity to provide services. Auttash organization in Partnership with UNICEF has been working tackle the humanitarian crisis soince 2006 across communities and maintained regular access to the areas . through this project Auttash organization is planning to provide WASH services to improve the health status, quality of education, Child protection provision of clean WASH of conflict-affected populations in five localities, reaching a total beneficiary population of 36000, person (direct and 27000 indirect beneficiaries ). Auttash has Operating in the area as UNICEF partner of WASH, Education and Child protection since 2008 in Kabkabiya , Elserief , Saraf Omrra,, Allait and Tawilla Localities. While the security situation has improved, the conditions underlying the conflict – ethnic and tribal tension, linked with deep mistrust of the government, presence of pro-government. The 18-months project will support In North Darfur State only 50% of the population has access to improved water supply. 12% of the population has access to improved sanitation, with 41% practicing open defecation and the incidence rate of diarrhoea is 38%. (Source: Sudan MICS 2014) In the IDP settlements of Daba Naira11% and 19% of households are still practicing open defecation respectively while 85% and 70% of households in Rwanada , Arko and Tawilla Town respectively do not have handwashing facilities, while amongst those who do the majority are not using soap. The Environmental Sanitation and Hygiene Master Plan (ESHMP) is an actionable roadmap towards achieving sustainable access to basic sanitation and hygiene for all citizens of El Fasher town by 2027. UNICEF under its DFID funded Urban WASH for Darfur (UW4D) project is assisting the state government in implementing the ESHMP plans. The interventions proposed under this project proposal will further contribute to the implementation of the ESHMP activities. And Provide free eduThe number of out of school children in Alliet is estimated to be around 10,000 children. During the inter-agency missions, a key finding was that the locality did not provide ALP education to the school-aged out of school population. Only two ALP centers are registered in the entire locality with an enrolment of 87 students in the academic year 2015/16 (EMIS data). A UNICEF-led assessment in March 2018 revealed that Alliet localit has 59 pre-primary Education Centers largely located in Alliet town and the admin unit main villages. Not a single child from the South Sudanes refugee community is enrolled in any of those centers which are signified by a poor learning environment and low capacity of teaching and caring staff.
