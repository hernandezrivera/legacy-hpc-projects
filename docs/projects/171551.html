Project Summary

The project aims at providing critical lifesaving assistance and strengthening resilience of drought, flood and conflict affected IDPs, returnees and most vulnerable host communities in Galgadud, Hiraan, Bay, Middle Shabelle and Lower Shabelle Regions, Somalia through safety net interventions as well as restoring and protecting their livelihood conditions. The project targets 55,000 persons (22880 women, 21120 men, 5500 boys and 5500 girls) whose plight is exacerbated by eroded purchasing power, very weak and negative coping strategies, fragile food insecurity situation compounded by impacts of Gu 2020 seasonal flooding, desert locust invasion that negatively impacted to their crop production, conflict and insecurity in Lower and Middle Shabelle that disrupted trade and socio-economic impacts of COVID-19 containment measures that weakened their already fragile food security status. Analysis of household food security survey data collected in the July/August 2020 post-Gu assessment showed reduced food estimates through December 2020, whereas anticipated La Niña conditions are associated with and high likelihood of consecutive below-average rainfall seasons and projected high risk of drought conditions by early 2021. (FSNAU and FEWS NET May 2020 report).
The project will be implemented through the following activities: Provision of food vouchers for most vulnerable and displaced women and children headed households and those with chronic illnesses and the disabled, rehabilitation of water catchments, farming lands/rangelands, canals and feeder roads on CFW scheme, Provision of hay/fodder to the most vulnerable and drought affected, pastoral families, distribution of farm inputs (seed package) to farming households as well as provision of tractor hours to the targeted farming households.
The project will implementation while ensuring adherence to Federal Government States (FGS) Galmudug, Hirshabelle and South West States/ MoH and WHO protocols on protection and prevention of Covid-19 through regularly hand Washing and hand sanitization, wearing safety masks and keeping safe distance of 1.5m by project targeted beneficiaries at the project targeted locations during the project time.

Mitigation Measures

1. Risk that beneficiaries are exposed to violence during registration/distribution as a result of the distance to or location of humanitarian assistance and service: Mitigation Measure : Enhancing security at the project sites by contracting security officers/guards as well as ensuring there are many distribution points for assistance to limit time take to access them. This will enable women and girls ample time to get back to their homes before cover of darkness that is associated with a lot GBV cases. in addition, ensuring centrality of distibution points of assistance to limit distance covered to access them thus reducing risk of GBV2. Risk of women, girls and boys exposed to sexual violence, including sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA), when accessing humanitarian assistance. Mitigation Measure: Ensuring beneficiary feedback and complains mechanism in place in addition to all staff signing PSEA code of conduct3. Risk of no access to or awareness of protection assistance or referral services for survivors of protection incidents (i.e. GBV, child protection, evictions, explosive hazards, etc.)Mitigation measure: Conducting awareness on protection assistance for survivors of protection incidences4. Risk that marginalized groups, persons with disabilities, women, boys and girls, do not participate in consultations or decision-making processes for the project or are excluded from humanitarian services/assistance/activitiesMitigation measure: Conducting comprehensive awareness for persons with disabilities, women, boys and girls on importance of their inclusion and participation in decision making processes.5. Risk that beneficiaries are not accessing/using complaint and feedback mechanismsMitigation measure: Establishing beneficiaries complain and feedback mechanisms and conducting awareness targeting the beneficiaries for utilization of the same
