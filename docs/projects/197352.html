3 - Needs

The area took heavy bombardment over 12 years, and guns have a wide spread among communities. Injuries from unexploded bombs and mines have increased significantly in the past five years (20% increase in Edlib)Due to a lack of awareness about protection from unexploded bombs Most families in the countryside of Idlib make an income by collecting unexploded bombs and selling them. Raising awareness about unexploded bombs has become a must in Idlib, especially among children.According to the Idlib data center; raising awareness sessions about unexploded materials is conducted in three camps (Abu-AlZohour, Saraqeb, Idlib) between 2015 and 2022, 4 NGOs are working on the subject targeting 11800 beneficiaries according to the dashboard.There is a huge gap in awareness needs and reporting for victims of unexploded materials in the war zone, which led us to assemble awareness sessions in Idlib and Ihsim

4 - Implementation of activities

- Training our staff (10 women) on the materials for raising awareness about unexploded materials of war within a month in our office in Idlib- Raising awareness sessions about unexploded materials from the war in 11 schools and 5 non-formal education centers by November in Edlib and Ihsim- Providing awareness materials in the mentioned schools at the end of the sessions in November

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

- Edlib in general is considered a war zone and always includes the risk of bombardment, in all of our activities we ensure our staff does not operate in times of ongoing strikes or battles.- Diseases including CP, Covid-19, and Cholera are general risks we make sure our staff wear necessary protection while working and maintain social distancing among themselves and all beneficiaries. In addition, our staff has a process of cleaning working spaces (halls, offices) in external work sites.

9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures
