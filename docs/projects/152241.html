Project Summary

Njobolio and Njoboli are two neighboring village areas located within Yola District of Yola South LGA of Adamawa State. The two communities have shared history spanning through a period of over 270 years. Over the years while on the one hand Njobolio communities essentially derived their livelihood from farming and fishing activities, on the other hand, the Njoboli communities are agro-pastoralists who erk their living from livestock rearing and farming. Also, in terms of geographical location, Njoboli occupies a plain land and the neighboring Njobolio is located around riverine area with lush grasses. Thus, a combination of history and geography has provided the two communities with a basis for beneficial mutual communal co-existence. For all through the ages, the people of Njoboli do enjoy unhindered access to grazing areas, watering points as well as dry season corn stalk residue. And, whenever there is a conflict, the issues are resolved amicably without resort to violence.
However, increased population expansion and agricultural activities led to increased competition over resource use. Thus, conflict between herders and farmers and land disputes generally escalated.
Furthermore, expansion of western education has created a pool of unemployed youths who also lacks opportunities thereby resulting to bitter frustrations. A challenge which is worsened by lack of forum or channel where they can voice their interest and concerns.
Project Goal
The goal of this proposal is aimed at increased awareness and support for peaceful and harmonious communal co-existence between Njoboliyo and Njoboli communities of Yola-South LGA, through dialogue and shared economic opportunities.
Objectives
The objective of the project is focused on:-
i. By the end of the year 1 project, strengthen local leadership and support their capacity to initiate, plan and implement local responses and ownership of peace negotiations prevent conflict and promote peace building between the two communities of Njoboli and Njobolio.
ii. By the end of the year 1 project, facilitate the evolution of 20 cooperative groups with membership of 300 youths of to serve as platform for shared economic opportunities and empowerment on improved livestock management techniques and alternative income generating skills in Njoboli and Njobolio.

Activities

Advocacy To secure the support, cooperation, buy-in and acceptance of local authorities and participants, the platform will conduct advocacy visits to community leaders to solicit for their support and cooperation at the community level. Thus an advocacy visit to each community will be conducted targeting community leaders both pastoralists and farming community leaders to inform them on the commencement of the project and solicit for their cooperation in its implementation.Mobilization and Sensitization MeetingsTo engage the communities, the platform will conduct community sensitization meetings as it start-up activities with target communities to discuss and identify best strategies for efficient project implementation. Thus, sensitization meetings in each of the two focal communities will be organized by the project, each focusing on specific objectives.Conflict Assessment To address the issue of conflict, a thorough conflict assessment will be conducted. This is intended to analyze its background, participants, issues, dynamics and possible solutions.Establishment of Peace Committees:The platform will facilitate the setting up of a democratically elected/selected committee to serve as a permanent peace committee which will provide forum for dialogue, early warning system and rapid response mechanism for conflict resolution. The constituted committee will have gender and age representation. And, it will include members of all the ethnic and religious groups.Consultative meetings with peace committee:Following the selection of the members of the peace committee, a formal orientation workshop for the members of the committee will be held in Yola. The meeting will dwell on the objectives and strategies adopted by the project.Capacity building trainingsPreliminary assessment of conditions in the two affected settlements indicates that, there are some challenges in respect to food security, intense competition over resource use and low income levels. In this regard, capacity building training will not only enhance level of productivity but also increase efficiency of organizational and operational capacity of the cooperative groups and hence raise the income levels of the community members.Procurement and provision of material supportFollowing skills gap assessment and capacity building training, necessary material support will be procured by the project. The procurement process will be based PARE procurement policy guidelines. Thereafter, each cooperative group will be provided with its own required materials for the operation of the selected business enterprise. The project coordinator and project officer will be responsible for this activity which will be conducted within the second month of the commencement of the project.Community led resource mappingParticipatory resource mapping is an important activity which is expected to be conducted at the commencement of the project. The mapping will be conducted across ethnic and religious divide, to determine the available resources within the community. The mapping exercise will provide the communities with a frame work to analyze and determine the linkage between the resources and conflict and identify shared needs and opportunities.Capacity building training on interest based negotiation. (IBN)To serve lasting peace and reconciliation, it is proposed that, the capacity building training on interest based negotiation for the local leaders be conducted. The training will equip the local leaders with capacity and strategy to address the seemingly intractable conflicts between their communities. A negotiation strategy which if applied will provide them with opportunity to collaboratively find a “win-win “solution to their disputes. This activity will precede the setting up of peace committee. It will be conducted by a resource person within the first month of the commencement of the project.
