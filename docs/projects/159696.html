Project Summary

In the midst of increasing displacement caused by conflict and natural disasters in Somalia, AVORD is submitting this proposal with the aim of enhancing the living conditions of affected communities through provision of NFIs, emergency and transitional shelters and Community Infrastructure. Program activities will target 6,700 households (40,200 persons) from affected communities in need in Banadir, Bay, Bakool, Lower Shabelle, Middle Shabelle, Hiiraan, and Galgaduud. Combined, these regions host 1,085,000 IDPs, or 42% of the entire IDP population in Somalia (OCHA HNO 2019). Where market conditions allow, AVORD will use a cash/voucher modality for implementation. Otherwise, in-kind modality will be used during which AVORD will distribute NFIs and emergency shelter kits directly to beneficiaries.

Project Activities

Following need based and transparent selection of beneficiaries, AVORD will provide emergency, transitional shelters and Non-Food Items to 40,200 vulnerable persons (6,700 households) from the IDPs, refugee returnees and host communities in specific regions of Somalia where these groups are concentrated. Of the target group, 30,000 persons in humanitarian crisis (5,000 households) will receive Non-Food Items including core items such as blankets and plastic sheets that will provide protection from inclement weather conditions while providing basic levels of privacy. The content of each NFI kit will be based on most recent Shelter Cluster Standards and will be gender-sensitized to ensure various items cover the specific needs of women, girls, men and boys. Where market conditions, AVORD will use a cash/voucher modality for the provision of NFIs and emergency shelter kits. Otherwise, in-kind modality will be used during which AVORD will distribute relief items directly to beneficiaries. Additional 10,200 persons (1,700 households) will be assisted with emergency shelters (1,000HHs) and transitional shelters (700 HHs). Site planning and/or re-planning will be an integral part of shelter construction, enabling safe and secure access to essential services and facilities while ensuring appropriate privacy and separation between individual household shelters and that pathways exist to provide all-weather access to individual dwellings and facilities. In addition to shelters, community-level infrastructure will be identified and rehabilitated to enhance affected communities' access to basic services. The design of activities will always be informed by context-specific need and protection analysis and will be completed in close coordination with target communities to ensure their priority needs are met. Where market conditions allow, AVORD will employ a cash-based modality for implementation. AVORD will also adapt a systematic approach to beneficiary selection with a clear criterion to prioritize the most vulnerable in target communities including unaccompanied children, older Persons, persons with disability, single women, pregnant and lactating mothers, single headed-households, persons with chronic illness or psychological condition, large families and/or families with babies, toddlers or children attending school and persons at risk as identified by protection officers. Data collected to inform beneficiary selection will be segregated by gender to ensure that no group is left out. The effectiveness of the aid package will always be evaluated through Post Distribution and Post Construction Monitoring exercises, field visits, and other evaluation methods thus ensuring that beneficiaries access their services safely and in a dignified manner. Elaborate feedback and complain mechanisms will be setup to ensure accountability to the affected population.

Mitigation Measures

Mitigation measures to exclusion and marginalization based on status (2a): 1)Undertake direct identification and registration of beneficiaries without use of middlemen or gate keepers. 2) Develop and enforce an elaborate selection criteria withpriority accorded to most vulnerable households including those from marginalizedand minority groups. 3) Adapt the highest levels of transparency in beneficiaryselection and provide target communities with with timely information on the selectioncriteria, organisational procedures and processes that affect them. 4) Establish Jointproject implementation committees involving different fabrics of target communitiesthus creating a platform where marginalized communities can share their grievances,if any. 5) Operationalize a project-level hotline where target beneficiaries can shareviews and/or concerns. Mitigation measures for exclusion and marginalization basedon gender and age: 1) Collect and use data dis-aggregated by sex, age and diversity toinform beneficiary selection and maintain gender parity. 2) Ensure that activities aredesigned in a manner that address the unique needs of women, men, boys, girls andthose with special needs. 3) Establish Joint project implementation committeesinvolving youth and women groups from target communities thus creating a platformwhere youth and women can share their grievances, if any. 4) Undertake directidentification and registration of beneficiaries without use of middle men or gatekeepers to ensure inclusivity regardless of age and gender. 5) Develop and enforce anelaborate selection criteria with priority accorded to most vulnerable groups such aswomen, boys and girls. Mitigation measures for exposure of beneficiaries to violence:1) Continuously assess local dynamics and make plans accordingly. 2) Ensure thataccess routes to distribution points and services are safe with enough space fororderly and safe distribution. 3) Undertake program activities during daylight hours,ensuring ample time for beneficiaries to go back home before nightfall. 4) Adapt andobserve the Do-No-Harm principles at all times to prevent unintended negativeconsequences. 5) Provide maximum protection to beneficiary data and householdinformation to prevent misuse and exposure of target persons to risk.

Please provide further explanation

This proposal is humanitarian in nature focusing on the provision of life-saving shelter assistance to people in humanitarian crisis. However, it also contributes to building the resilience of targeted communities in a number of ways. On the one hand, provision of shelter and NFIs in an environment of humanitarian crisis protects beneficiaries from harsh climatic conditions, health hazards and suffering which leads to improvement in their living conditions and chances to access livelihoods. Complementary multi-sectoral interventions such as food aid, WASH and protection are often more effective when combined with shelter, leaving IDPs more resilient to future shocks and able to cope with the grim reality of displacement. On the other hand, construction of shelters will take into account land tenure arrangements to avoid eviction and will be facilitated in close coordination with local authorities and other key stakeholders such as land owners. To maximize impact and sustain project outcome, AVORD will seek integration with other clusters providing response in areas whereproposed activities will be implemented. Details pertaining to project activities will be shared through relevant clusters and 4W reporting matrix to avoid duplication of effort. Local communities will play a key role in different phases of the project to create sense of ownership which will contribute to sustaining of project outcomes. AVORD will strive to build local capacities thus ensuring that various community leaders and committees are able to sustainably maintain resources following project completion and handover.
