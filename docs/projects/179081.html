Project Summary

SR will target 1400 households in Maaret Tamsrin camps with life-saving and life-sustaining food assistance.
The project consists of two components:
1-Regular food assistance through value-based E-Vouchers modality, each HH will receive value 60 USD monthly for 8 months along with the FSL and CWG recommendations to cover the Kcal needs for these households to be aligned with the food component in the SMEB.
The E-Vouchers value can be redeemed with a list of food items from contracted markets/ shops in the same targeted areas.
The beneficiaries in this component will be selected from IDPs who received RTER as response to the flood response in the last winter during January and February (including disabled, female-headed households, women and young girls, PLW, and the elderly )
The targeted beneficiaries are 1000HH (6000 individuals) (Men 1000, Women 1000, Boys 1800, Girls 2200) (from them 600 disabled)
Each voucher will be loaded with extra value to purchase 700 gram of soap monthly to be aligned with FSL and WASH cluster for prevention from COVID-19
2- Emergency unrestricted Cash 100 USD as one-off (Multipurpose Cash) for households who affected by any emergencies such as displacement, flood, wind, fire.etc
400HH (2000 individuals) (Men 400, Women 450, Boys 1000, Girls 1150) (from them 200 disabled)
This intervention is fully aligned with the allocation strategy and FSL& CWG recommendations by:
-Life-saving intervention to Scale-up efforts to sustain minimum food consumption needs of the most vulnerable IDPs
-The modality is Vouchers and Cash
-The value of the voucher is 60 USD for regular (8 months) and 100 USD for emergency (one-off)
-Maaret Tasmrin Sub-district is high priority ranking
SR is proposing to contract service providers for the E-vouchers in Turkey to be sub-contracted with markets in the targeted locations, these e-vouchers to be distributed one time for the beneficiaries and to be redeemed regularly on monthly basis.
Accountability to affected population will be given high priority by transparent beneficiary selection criteria, as well ad Complaints and Feedback mechanism which will be in place and accessible to all people through different channels.
SR will have MEAL plan in place in order to monitor the whole process starting from the vulnerability assessment, redemption/ distribution monitoring and post distribution monitoring with corrective measure to be taken on timely manner to ensure continuous improvement of the project implementation in order to reach the planned objectives
SR considers the coordination is key aspect to apply complimentary and integration approach by coordination with other NGOs in Maaret Tamsrin Camps in other services as well as complement with other SR projects.

4 - Needs

HNO 2021 states that 14.2 million people in need of food assistance, at least 12.4 M people are estimated to be food insecure, of which 1.3 M are considered severely food insecure. Furthermore, 1.7 M living in camps are deemed to be 100 % food insecure, and 1.8 M are estimated to be at risk of food insecurity. The Mobility and Needs Monitoring factsheet March 2021(HNAP), total Population in NW Syria 4,429,062 from them 2,727,326 IDPs (62%) out of them 1,751,080 IDPs in Idleb governorate. In Maaret Tamsrin Sub-district, food is the priority need for 61% of IDPs in March, 40.7% of all Newly IDPs and host HHs are food insecure and in need of assistance (NWS Newly Displaced IDPs Assessment, Dec 2021). Priority needs for HHs with members with disabilities: food 68% (HNAP 2021). According to FSLA 2021, in Maaret Tamsrin, a total population of 286,899 from them 234,310 IDPs in camps and all of them food insecure with only 23% coverage which highlights big concern about the food security situation in this area. According to REACH ENT 2021, multi-purpose cash grants are first priority need for 64% of the assessed communities while 20% for food rations, in 84 communities (62%), KIs reported that the preferred modality of assistance for newly-arrived IDPs was cash/voucher assistance. SR conducted RNA in Maaret Tamsrin using FGDs and the main findings indicated a need for food assistance in any modality and a preference by beneficiaries to receive vouchers or cash as otherwise they are using negative coping strategies including and not limited to offering less and smaller meals, where all or half of the households “based on the location” have relied on less preferred coping strategies before the beginning of the food assistance.

5 - Implementation of activities

8 rounds of distribution,5 in 2021 and the rest in 2022. This project will be implemented by the SR field team under the direct supervision of SR manag. SR committed to attend all FSL Cluster meetings, submit 5W's, along with regular information and updates about the project. SR considers coordination as a key aspect to apply complimentarily and integration approach by coordination with other NGOs in Maaret Tamsrin Camps in other services as well as a complement with other SR projects. The regular food assistance component will be provided via monthly value-based vouchers for food with contracted markets in the targeted locations and beneficiaries will be informed of the contracted markets and redeemed food list. SR staff will ensure protection measures are taken to ensure equitable access particularly for women and young girls. To follow up the market’s functionality, accessibility, prices, replenishment, price Monitoring will be conducted before and after each redemption, where the market assessment will be conducted quarterly. Accountability to the affected population will be given high priority by transparent beneficiary selection criteria, as well as Complaints and Feedback mechanisms which will be in place and accessible to all people through different channels. SR will have a MEAL plan in place in order to monitor the whole process starting from the vulnerability assessment, redemption/ distribution monitoring, and PDM with a corrective measure to be taken in a timely manner to ensure continuous improvement of the project implementation in order to reach the planned objectives. The program coordinator will oversee the project, compile and submit reports, and coordinate with the donor to ensure full compliance.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

SR will do many actions to mainstream protection and gender across all project stages as following:SR conducted a rapid needs assessment by using Focus Group Discussions (FGD) with males and females separately as well as the most vulnerable groups such as disables and PLW to ensure the program design meets the exact need for beneficiaries to avoid doing harm in the implementation stages.Female staff will be hired for distribution/redemption and MEAL to ensure equal access for all beneficiary categories including females headed of household, girls and widowed.Beneficiaries will be clearly informed that all distributed value-based vouchers are free and that cash distributed is provided with no obligations on their end.Options for home delivery of the needed food commodities for vouchers which will be redeemed on behalf of vulnerable persons (e.g., PWD who cannot access the markets, elderly, etc.)confidential complaints and protection reporting mechanism system will be designed and beneficiaries will be clearly informed where to refer or report incidents or violations.The day, time, and location of the distribution of the vouchers will be discussed with different community members and leaders to determine the safest most accessible location to allow all individuals including women, children, and the elderly to access the service with dignity and without exposure to further risk of harm; moreover, the list of contracted markets, allowed food commodities, and the redemption days for each location will be shared with all BNF.Vulnerable individuals who may need assistance during distribution/ redemption such as persons with disabilities will be identified and prioritized by placing them first on distribution lists.
