Project Summary

In the rural areas of the backward districts of Jere and Konduga local governments there are large sections of poor and deprived people including socially excluded communities whose children has either not been to schools or are school drop-outs. These children could be seen engaged in one or the other child labour with the purpose of supporting and supplementing family livelihood.
An analysis would reveal that if we are able to address the problems of earning livelihood of the people then the parents will feel free to send their children to schools. With proper empowerment and motivation the mind-set of the parents can be changed and the children can be enrolled in schools and given proper education.
The proposed project to be implemented by Hallmark Leadership Initiative for a period of one year, envisages attaining sustainable development for the poor and deprived sections of people including socially excluded communities in the rural areas of the Jere and Konduga LGAs, in Borno State, NIGERIA.
This project aims to address:
• Promotion of education for girls/boys children who have never been to school or are school drop-outs and are exposed to child labor.
• Providing awareness and support (educational) to the parent whose children would be enabled to join schools.

Needs

In the rural areas of the backward districts of Jere and Konduga local governments there are large sections of poor and deprived people including socially excluded communities whose children has either not been to schools or are school drop-outs. These children could be seen engaged in one or the other child labor with the purpose of supporting and supplementing family livelihood. An analysis would reveal that if we are able to address the problems of earning livelihood of the people then the parents will feel free to send their children to schools. With proper empowerment and motivation the mind-set of the parents can be changed and the children can be enrolled in schools and given proper education. The proposed project to be implemented by Hallmark Leadership Initiative for a period of one year, envisages attaining sustainable development for the poor and deprived sections of people including socially excluded communities in the rural areas of the Jere and Konduga LGAs, in Borno State, NIGERIA. This project aims to address: • Promotion of education for girls/boys children who have never been to school or are school drop-outs and are exposed to child labor. • Providing awareness and support (educational) to the parent whose children would be enabled to join schools. Literacy and Education are the foundation on which socio-economic development either of an individual or a society or nation is possible. The high level of education indicates the advancement of the society. In the Nigerian context there is high level of literacy and education in urban areas whereas the same is in a much dismal state in the rural areas and the governments both at the Federal and the States have been conscious of this continuously expanding gap. The gigantic budgetary provisions have not been able to bring out the desired achievements on account of the ever growing population, perpetual poverty, lopsided priorities of the parents, dilution of objectives while implementing educational oriented programs at the grass root level. The conditions prevailing in Northeast states are well known and in these states at the grass root level in rural areas the people are still devoid of basic infrastructure facilities like roads, sanitation, safe drinking water, electricity, schools and colleges, vocational education facilities, hospitals, transport etc. The status in the country side of Borno is very worse and in case of the proposed Konduga and Jere under central senatorial district of Borno the rural people are still yearning for any worthwhile development in these sectors. On a closer scrutiny of the problems of the rural areas it will be apparent that the pre occupation of the people especially those belonging to rural Community is to somehow earn livelihood and remaining aspects are given secondary importance. Such category people being poor can be found struggling from dawn to dusk to somehow earn for two square meals a day and in this endeavour all the members of the family irrespective of age and gender can be seen engaged. Even urban children of tender ages can be seen supporting the labor activities to generate resources for the livelihood of the family. This takes away the valuable opportunity which they are supposed to spend at schools. Thus the problems of livelihood earning and the associated struggles foreclose the scope of education of out-of-school children and their progress in the socioeconomic ladder.The organization under the project envisages enrolling all non-school going and school dropout IDP/host community children of the villages in Jere & Konduga in schools being run by government and NGOs. The project will focus on children of IDPs/host community with particular emphasis on economically poor and persons with disabilities. As far as children is concerned the project will extend benefit to an estimated number of at least 1200 children (non-school going and school drop-out category) and 800 children (formal school).

Activities

a. Orientation Training for Project Staff The selected personnel will be given orientation training on various aspects of the project including objectives, mode of implementation, assignment of duties and responsibilities, monitoring of progress, anticipated results, reporting and documentation etc. The training will also feed the participants about the various statistical indicators of the district and the activities that are proposed to be undertaken for improvement in these indicators in the light of the objectives of the project. b. Field Study and Survey for identification and selection of beneficiaries: A field study and survey would be conducted throughout the project villages under the Jere and Konduga for which the required number of project volunteers will be deputed. From the data so collected an analysis will be made for planning the most deserving village under the project can be implemented for maximizing the benefit to the people. It is proposed that the project will benefit an estimated number of at least 2000 children of the non-school going and school dropout category during a period of one year. c. Counselling and Motivation Program Counselling and motivation of the parents will also highlight the noble cause in the interest of sustainable development in the countryside and the activities that will be carried out focusing on promotion of education to children and simultaneously offering livelihood enhancement and empowerment activities to the mothers/women dependents. d. Coordination Meetings with School Authorities Having already prepared area wise list of deserving children the next step would be to approach concerned schools where these children could be admitted. Meetings will be held with school authorities where in the aims of the project will be explained and their cooperation will be sought for solving this humanitarian problem of education. Their cooperation will also be sought for a holistic approach to the children so that no discrimination on account of social exclusions based on caste, class etc. is allowed to occur and the children feel a conducive environment for continuing their studies. e. Procurement and Distribution of Educational Kits Since the children would be belonging to poor IDP/host community families, the parents may not be able afford the initial expenses for sending their children to schools due to many reasons. Therefore, as a start-up initiative, it is proposed to provide the children support in the form of educational material which would include uniform, stationeries, school bag, books etc. A community level program will be organized in which parents and children will be invited and the aforesaid school kit will be handed over. Those who missed to collect the same will be offered the facility to receive the kit at respective schools where admission would be granted. f. Orientation Workshop for Teachers The teachers will be coming of different backgrounds and disciplines and their mind-set may also require to be moulded for creating the right environment in schools. A discriminative approach may lead to discouraging the children from attending schools. Therefore it is necessary to provide proper guidelines and seek the teacher’s cooperation so as to provide a receptive approach to the children. It is hence proposed to conduct an orientation workshop which could be organized at LGA level where representatives of teachers from adopted schools would be invited. g. Monitoring and Interventional Activities Retention in schools of the newly admitted children under the project throughout the year of admission and subsequent years will be an indicator of the success of the program. In order to ensure that the schools are following the guidelines of the project periodical monitoring will be required. This will take stock of attendance of children, teachers approach and help in finding solutions that may cause hurdles if any.
