Project Summary

The intervention will contribute to the sustainable and equitable development of communities in Syria and to the full enjoyment of all human rights by all.
Strengthen the capacity of local CSOs (Youth of Change Network) to engage in gender equality and youth inclusion by empowering youth and women to access livelihood opportunities to support their families that were impacted by the earthquake and prevent them from joining violent activities.
Youth and women have decent work opportunities and a sustainable income for themselves and their families.
Youth and women have active participation in social initiatives and activities in their local community.
The intervention will target the Youth of Change Network (YCN) which is going to be the main target group (strategic partner) during the implementation:
* Self-sufficiency team, women and men influence circle of YCN.
* Business Owners who are going to host the apprentices
* Youth and women who are searching for decent work opportunities.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The project focuses on promoting inclusive growth and socio-economic development in NWS through enhancing the access to decent work opportunities among women and youth. This project will contribute to providing vocational on-site training to effectively empower youth and women to access livelihood opportunities to support their families that were impacted by the earthquake and prevent them from joining violent activities. This project will contribute to the sustainable and equitable development of communities in Syria and to the full enjoyment of all human rights by all by integrating economic empowerment and community development for youth and women in one intervention. This project will contribute to strengthen the capacity of local CSOs (Youth of Change Network) to engage in gender equality and youth inclusion by achieving the following specific goals: Obj.1. Youth and women have decent work opportunities and sustainable income for themselves and their families and Obj.2. Youth and women have active participation in social initiatives and activities in their local community.Through participatory market study Act. 1.1.2. youth and women will be aware of the needs in the local market that match their preferences Opt. 1.1. Later, Youth and women will have the vocational and soft skills that enable them to join the labor market Opt.1.2. through conducting several activities, such as Act. 1.2.1. Conduct training workshops on programming. Later, Act.1.2.2. Conduct training workshops for young males and females on soft skills related to entrepreneurship, later, Act.1.2.3. Connect young males and females to the business owner so they can have a paid apprenticeship opportunity with them for three months. After that, Youth and women will have access to financial support to startup their own business Opt. 1.3. by fund qualified youth and women with microfinance to start their own businesses Act.1.3.1. by doing that, Youth and women will have decent work opportunities and sustainable income for themselves and their families Otc.1. For youth and women to have active participation in social initiatives and activities in their local community Otc.2. during the project we will Invite youth to participate in workshops on community development and engagement. Act.2.1.2 and Invite youth to participate in community initiative by the LDCs Act. 2.2.1. so Youth and women will be well-equipped with community development skills Opt.2.1. and will be connected to LDCs in their areas Opt.2.2. To enhance the capabilities of our strategic partner and ensure the continued impact beyond the conclusion of the project, we have devised two key activities. These efforts are aimed at empowering the self-sufficiency team in Activity 1.1.1 and providing the necessary skills to the women and youth circles in Activity 2.1.1. Through these endeavours, we intend to identify proficient individuals who will play a leading role in all forthcoming related activities.
