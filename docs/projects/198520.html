Project Summary

Borno State is highly impacted by conflict and violence and has experienced significant structural and socio‐economic damages as a result of the violence. In addition, the near absence of civil authorities in communities negatively impacts services and has led to critical development gaps, therefore resulting in community tensions. Since Nigeria’s return to democracy in May 1999, armed non-state groups have significantly undermined the country’s internal security environment, largely using young men as foot soldiers.
The proposed action is targeted at strengthening the resilience of vulnerable youth in Southern Borno to contribute against joining violent extremism. It also seeks to reduce the vulnerability of the target communities to infiltration by Violent Extremist Organisations (VEOs) as a result of distraction during electoral processes.
Through the action, vulnerable youths and youth groups will be empowered to promote peace, civic participation, and social cohesion and mitigate conflict by engaging and working with LGA electoral bodies, LGA officials, traditional and religious leaders, youth, and women’s groups. Geographic locations targeted in Borno State include Biu,Jere, Konduga, Monguno, and Gwoza. The youth in these locations currently feel a sense of marginalization and feel powerless in the decisions affecting their lives, leading to a sense of hopelessness about their futures hence the urgent need for action which proffers a sustainable approach to engage vulnerable youth and youth groups in peacebuilding and civic activities thereby promoting positive roles for the youth and as a result, contribute to reducing their feelings of marginalization and susceptibility to conflict and VEO risks.

2 - Activities

Activity 1.1: Mapping of existing youth groups and capacity needs assessment on conflict prevention and advocacy.Activity 1.2: Three days of ward-level conflict management and violence prevention training to enhance the capacity of youths to counter violent extremism and strengthen community resilience against infiltration by VEOs. Activity 1.3: Conduct three ward-level civic engagement training for youth groups to develop strategies for engaging civic spaces to promote accountability and good governance.Activity 1.4: Youth group consultative meetings with community stakeholders to understand and consolidate issues, needs, and priorities action plans in target communities.Activity 2.1: Organize three LGA youth-led advocacy round tables and multi-stakeholder dialogues with stakeholders featuring the presentation of the charter of demand. Activity 2.2: Advocacy visits to secure the buy-in of the LGA traditional council in engaging stakeholders and political leaders through the action plan developed. Activity 2.3: Design and produce 5,000 information, education, and communication materials to create awareness in three local languages (Hausa, Kanuri, Babur) with key messages on civic engagement, conflict prevention, and CVE .CATAI will design and produce 5,000 information, education, and communication material with key messages on civic engagement, conflict prevention and countering violent extremism comprising 1000, 1000 stickers, 100 placards, 900 branded notepads/pen, 1,000 branded T-shirts and 1000 branded face caps. The objective of this activity is to create visibility and increase awareness on civic engagement, conflict prevention and countering violent extremism across the targeted communities. Activity 2.4: Organize three Ward-level celebrations of International Youth Day to raise awareness on youth action plans and sensitize youth groups on civic engagement, conflict prevention, and CVE.Globally international youth day is commemorated on 12 August. The day is dedicated to promoting awareness on issues, needs and priorities of youth. CATAI will utilize the significance of Year 2022 International Youth Day to convene ward-level awareness creation campaigns and sensitization on civic engagement, conflict prevention and CVE across the target project communities. CATAI Activity 2.5: Community-led initiative for peace and sensitization awareness (cash for work).CATAI will pay 200 youths across communities to lead the implementation of community initiatives that advance the need for peaceful coexistence and involvement of young persons in civic spaces. Each youth will collaboratively engage other youths in the target communities. Illustrative examples of initiatives to be implemented by these youths include joint sanitation activities, door-to-door campaigns, and sensitizations. CATAI will provide stipends as logistics for the targeted youths that will implement the initiatives. Through these activities, CATAI will strengthen the capacity of local youths to serve as champions in sensitizing other youths on the importance of community development, peaceful coexistence, and activity engaging in the civic space.
