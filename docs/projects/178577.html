Project Summary

Sadad proposed project seeks to provide protective education environment opportunities to vulnerable children (boys and girls) in Jandairis, and Harim sub-district for 20 schools to ensure expanding ongoing education services by availing inclusive protective learning spaces to ensure that no children absent or drop out of schools due to lack of learning spaces, learning and teaching materials, the economic capacity of the parents to cover all the schools & student’s needs. The targeted locations are considered and classified as high severity (4.30-4) as mention in population composition HNO2021 draft, the quality of the education environment has dropped out due to COVIDE-19 pandemic coupled with displacement, especially with the increased percentage of IDPs more than 60% of the total population in Jandairis SD, the number of children who out of school is still increased, and children are at risk of dropping out, and child protection risks due to the cumulative effect of the conflict.
Sadad proposed project aims to contribute to supporting 5665 of crisis-affected-students to get access to formal and non-formal education and raising the quality of education in line with EiE standards and inclusion 137of Children with Disability through light rehabilitation, equip schools, education services,COVIDE-19 awareness-raising, and provision PSS, in addition to protection issues through mainstreaming child protection and child safeguarding measures across education.
All the following activities will be achieved in both Harim and Jandarias sub-district with the total number of beneficiaries in both of them ;
1) Conducting light maintenance in schools to 122 classes. although we have targeted the same schools in Jandiaris last year, these schools were a place of residence for IDPs when the schools' closure for Covide -19, so we need to light repair for it.
2) Providing operational costs for 20 schools, safety and security equipments, and recreational materials.
3) Provide 525 (272 boys,253 girls) students with learning materials in non-formal education, Students who are late for their classmates are identified by teachers with remedial classes during the summer-time period through physical or distance learning modality accordıng to the situation.
4) Providing 5665 children (aged 5 - 17) with learning materials and school bags informal education.
5) provide students, and teachers in formal and non-formal education with internet vouchers to facilitate their access to online education services, because the education process is limited to be physically just for first until fourth class, and the rest of classes online modality, and we have a plan B online modality if the school' course due to covide -19.
6) Training the 194 teacher and educational personnel on PSS and Safety, child-centered and protective pedagogy, and CP principles.
7) Provide 22PSS workers on the PSS and Life skills to provide the PSS sessions and activities in the targeted locations.
8) Provide 95of ( 53 males and females teachers +20administrative teachers + 22 Janitors ) who are responsible to provide the non-formal education with monthly incentives during summer time
9) Establishing20 CFSs in the schools. In line with INEEE & CPMS, that will provide structured PSS
10) Establishing 20 PTAs to follow up with student’s education improvement and issues.
11) Conducting structured PSS and life skills for all students in formal and non-formal education
12) Identify and referral the children who need specialized services.
13) Conducting Back to learning BTL campaigns and awareness campaigns for children and their caregivers, and parents, and teachers, and community key members
During the COVID-19 context, Sadad will adopt the online modalities in provision the formal and non-formal education as per the situation on ground by adapting using the Telegram platform (preparing and set-up the platform, uploading the textbooks, and follow up with lessons.

4 - Needs

According to HNO 2021: 6.9M are PiN for humanitarian education assistance in which 97% ~6.7M are children and as the 2021 draft HNO; in 2020 an estimated 2.45 million children were out of school and 1.6 million children were at risk of dropping out. Based on the findings of the rapid need assessment conducted by Sadad teams, the key reasons children were not going to school include, 80% of KII reported that schools not being available, 70% schooling not being affordable and children working to support the household. 137 children with disabilities were identified and they were less likely to access learning due to Insufficient investment in learning facilities, specialized teachers, and materials. Needs assessment findings;5665 school-aged boys and girls (5-17), 60% of them were IDPs (N=3399), of the total students 2719 were females (48%); of them, 137 were considered CWDs;As indicated in the reported KII: 100% Running costs, 75% stationery, 40% hygiene promotion sterilization). Student’s needs as reported by the KII: (100% Bags, stationery, and protective materials, 50% of them expressed their needs of sterilization, while the non-formal education is more likely to be conducted in Harim District as 80% of KII reported, In Afrin only 56% of KII reported of the importance of non-formal education. The needs fo internet vouchers were among the high needs after the spread of COVID19 where 100% of KII reported on it and PSS sessions were also of the most important components of this need assessment as 78% of KII reported. Teacher’s needs were reported as 90% of KII on the need for capacity building and training, while internet vouchers were at 100% and 100% of the KII reported on educational materials and stationery needed.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

According to the Education Protection Risk Analysis (PRA) Matrix at the education guidance package, Sadad will mitigate the risks of violence and abuse and ensure child protection through mitigation procedures. All workers in schools (teaching, managerial, and service staff) will sign in: 1- Code of Conduct, that ensures enrolling vulnerable girls and disabilities. 2- Childe protection documents that ensure all rules of dealing with children and clarify all aspects of abuse.A- Operating in a warzone exposes staff to high safety and security risksLikelihood: L - Impact: HMitigation: continues evaluation of roads, development of a communication network, capacity building for staff on how to deal with checkpoints, good and effective communication with parties and forces, preparing plan-B and relocation of project activities to more safe areas, temporary suspension of project activities, monitoring of security situation, direct reporting to donor.E- Violate PSEA or CSG.- Signing PSEA and child safe- capacity building for staff on reporting violations- Lack of accessibility for the targeted groups (special people with disabilities), likelihood: Medium because we ensured that most of the schools are accessible for the CWD. Mitigation Measures are: - The school design must be friendly to people with different types of disabilities.
