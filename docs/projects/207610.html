Project Summary

Support Education (SUED) and Mani Queen Consultancy and Couseling Services (ManiQueen) focus on SDG4 ‘’Quality Education’’ and mainstream other SDGs: SDG 3 ‘’Good Health and Well-being’’, SDG 5 ‘’Gender Equality’’, SDG 16 ‘’Promote just, peaceful and inclusive societies’’, SDG 17, ‘’Partnerships for the Goals’’, etc. Key areas of work include any form of education of internally displaced and underprivileged persons through sourcing and financing scholarships, workshop practicum, essential books, psychosocial support, tools, and training types that enhance teaching, learning, and good practice.
There is ignorance and implicit injustice on a vital aspect of health in education such as psychosocial support. The violent armed conflict in the two Anglophone regions of Cameroon since 2016, where people encounter abrupt gun battles between the state and non-state armed groups (NSAGs), attacks on education, horror killings, etc., is becoming normalcy. This is disturbing because most communities and establishments sideline, or are ignorant of the negative impact of the horror killings and attacks on education on human mental and emotional health.
Statistics show that in most elementary schools in our conflict-stricken zone, most head teachers and teachers are women, that is, they are those at the frontline, they are those who receive on-campus live threats from the NSAGs, but they are those who understand and know how to use the language of peace and humility. They are the mothers and wives of the fighters and victims of the conflict. In all of these, they remain traumatised, haunted by daily threats, the kidnapping or killing of colleagues and learners, burning of schools. This multi-role group needs psychosocial support more than ever before so that they learn to heal and subsequently, heal others. The boys and men in this crisis are the highest victims of death, those who go to the war front. These men need psychosocial support to heal from the scars of loss, pain, and humiliation, and restore a forgiving heart and a discerning mind.
Considering the Cameroon Education Cluster (CEC) response plan, Objective 2: Learner-centred pedagody, this project purpose is to train 150 teachers on psychosocial support (PSS) and conflict risk reduction strategies (CRRS) as aspects of health in education in Cameroon, and advocate for inclusion and good practice. The target population comprises the traumatised in the armed conflict since 2016, and the sample population includes learners, and teachers, the latter who double as parents at home in the communities.
The project activities include 01 CRRS and 08 PSS training activities for teachers to incorporate into their teaching-learning, for mothers to incorporate in child-care, and for parents in family growth for the psychosocial wellness of the community. At the end of the 06-month project (September 2024-February 2025), 12 schools, 150 teachers, 6,000 learners direct beneficiaries, healing psychologically, emotionally, socially, and morally. 02 public health policy units in Mezam Division and Tubah Subdivisions will benefit from the advocacy.
