Project Summary

According to the Somali region DRMB update as of January 2022 and sourcing data from DTM round 27(August-September, 2021) Currently there are 142,671 households comprising 871,176 individual IDPs in 427 sites across the region. Conflict was the main reason for displacement and displaced an estimated 506,089 IDPs {58.1%), a decrease of 3310,111IDPs (40%) since Oct, 2018(after the regional govt change) while drought displaced 266,013 IDPs (30.5%), seasonal floods displaced 65,541 IDPs (7.5%),flash floods displaced 33,196 IDPs {3.8%) and landslides displaced 337 IDPs (0.04%). The border conflict between Somali and Afar caused the displacement of 4923 HHs.These new IDPs are currently in 12 sites in Afdem, Erer, Biki, and Maizo woredas of the Siti zone of the Somali region. they were displaced from Garbaciise, Adeydu, and surrounding rural areas.

1. - Projects activities

ANE will implement the shelter projects with two strategic approaches, In the 1st approach ANE will provide a full shelter Kit, and also shelter construction technical support will be given by ANE technical team while the IDP returnees or community full engage in the contribution of their labor for the shelter construction selection of Priority IDPs returnees Woredas/Kebele for Shelter support, selection of Beneficiary Households, Procurement of Construction Materials, Distribution of Shelter kits, Construction of transitional and emergency Shelters, Handover of the Constructed transitional and Emergency Shelters, the 2 nd approach is Selection of Beneficiaries, Distribution of EE/NFI

ANE will work in consultation and collaboration with all relevant actors and stakeholders at both national and sub-national levels in the design and implementation of this program. ANE attends coordination at the national and district level and provides updates on its activities and responses, specifically with the shelter and NFI at the Regional coordination cluster and Federal government level. This ensures that programming follows the government’s standards and guidelines and ANE activities are also reported in the 5Ws/Report Hub reporting tools. Target areas are discussed with the cluster at all levels to avoid any duplication amongst agencies as well as referring potential gaps.

3.6 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members using age and gender appropriate, safe and confidential channels?

ANE currently uses various methods to receive complaints, including the establishment of complaints hearing committees, comprised of beneficiaries (2 women and 2 men). Suggestion boxes and phone numbers of ANE staff are all ways that beneficiaries can give feedback or share any concerns or complaints and in exchange receive feedback or response from ANE. This goes to strengthen ANE’s humanitarian accountability and the overall quality of our humanitarian programming. This will also enhance the participation of the communities with whom we work.
