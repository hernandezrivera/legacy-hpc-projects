Project Summary

The implementation will be on two-phases: • The ﬁrst phase will focus on series of technical trainings for the volunteers to enhance their capacity on Case Management, MHPSS, MRE, PSS, CP, Case management, and disability inclusion during the ﬁrst 2 months of the project to enable them to conduct the activities with high quality, and including PWDs in activity, with range of different disabilities. In addition to creating Child and Woman Safe spaces in each location which will be selected based on the location severity scale, TdH access and plans, Local partner access and plan • The second phase (implantation phase): This phase will be done through the trained Local Partner volunteers, and TDH will provide them with technical support, supervision, and will monitor the implementation on a daily bases by TDH field staff and monthly bases by TDH National staff (PM, M&E, Protection officer). The work structure in each location will include: a mobile team (6 Local partner volunteer+ 1 TDH technical supervisor), and a stationed team inside safe space accessible to PWDs via soft-rehabilitation (8 Local partner volunteers+ Local Partner Case Manager + TDH technical supervisor, TDH field Coordinator). • Activities will include: 1-awareness- raising sessions on General protection including CP, GBV, topics will be (protection from violence, diversity acceptance, PWDs acceptance, child labor, early marriage, and Mine risk education…). all materials used will be from approved sources such as UNICEF, and UNMASS. 2- Recreational and Structured PSS activities for children, life skills activities for youths & women, these activities will aim to increase social skills and emotional wellbeing, improve coping mechanisms, will be designed and supervised by TDH PSS technical expert 3- Case Management, this will be done via Local Partner Case Manager and under the supervision of TDH technical supervisor. 4- In addition to focused assistance for victims affected by explosive hazards via MHPSS support.
In addition, TdH will provide The CASH assistance for protection (cash specific sector / cash for protection) in alignment with the CASH Working Group And will be integrated with the Psychosocial support for beneficiaries, to be complementary assistance to improve the coping mechanism of the most vulnerable groups such as refugees, elderly, people with chronic diseases, women head households. The CASH will be delivered to each beneficiary for 8 cycles and will be transferred through a financial service provider who has branches in each location, in alignment with the CASH working group standers.

4 - Needs

According to the HNO 2021, Critical protection needs persist and have been aggravated by the COVID-19 pandemic, including gender-based violence (GBV), with women and girls across the countryreporting that it has become a feature of everyday life. One in two people in Syria is estimated to be at risk of explosive ordnance; needs for humanitarian mine action interventions. Women and girls are particularly vulnerable to GBV because of gender inequality and discrimination in Syria. In the households, women and girls have taken on additional roles traditionally associated with men, including as heads of households and principal breadwinners and although they have more responsibilities, this does not automatically translate into decision making power. This contributes to increased pressure and stress for women to provide for their families. furthermore Children of all ages face violence and protection concerns in many life situations. Adolescence brings exposure to new forms of violence due to harmful coping mechanisms within the family. TDH is planning to intervene in the following locations through admin3 taking the severity score into consideration: Damascus, Rural Damascus: (Qudsiya, At Tall, Az-Zabdani, Harasta), Ziyara, Homs Dar'a and Izra' Al-Hasakeh Quamishli Areesheh Lattakia Deir-ez-Zor.
