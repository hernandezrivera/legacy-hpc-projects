Project Summary

By the proposed Program Al-Fayhaa association (AFA) is willing to apply an Education retention and remedial support program targeting Lebanese, migrants, and PRL in Akkar, Tripoli, El Koura, El-Minnieh-Dinnieh, Zgharta.
At the beginning of the program, AFA will hold an outreach activity to target the most vulnerable children who will participate in the program, on top of that AFA through different community engagement initiatives and awareness sessions will make sure to fill in the vulnerability criteria checklist to be able to refer Out of School Children to Formal education, non-formal education, protection or any other needed services.
Aiming to retain in school children from dropping out, AFA will implement retention and remedial support program targeting 2400 child.
In this program AFA aims to cover Akkar, Tripoli, El Koura, El-Minnieh-Dinnieh, Zgharta Areas, in school children from grade 1 to 6 who are at risk of dropping out or due to their poor academic performance and achievement results will get interactive learning support for 6 months in all major subjects of the Lebanese curriculum (Arabic, Foreign language, Math, Science, Social and emotional learning sessions).
The proposed educational program will also support 5% of the target children with disabilities, and 50% of total children with disabilities will receive 16 therapy sessions each. In addition, AFA will procure assistive devices such as hearing aids, wheel chairs, walkers to cases in need in an aim to facilitate their access and retention in education.
The program focuses on girls, boys and women, who reside in marginalized areas in North and Akkar governates.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

Many of the identified children at risk will be girls’ survivors of gender-based violence (GBV) or at risk of GBV, those vulnerable groups alongside with caregiver/parents that are GBV survivors will be provided with sensitive and critical services to offer the best support. These principles include, but are not limited to: ensuring survivor-centered approaches and do no harm.Facilitators, staff and volunteers will be trained on the do no harm approach as well as on Al Fayhaa Association PSEA and child protection policy and code of conduct. Inclusive trainings will be cascaded to ensure delivery of our programs respecting the do no Harm to PWSN. On the other hand, parents will be trained on positive-parenting approaches where topics to increase their self-esteem will be implemented. A Gender Focal point will support all the beneficiaries and affected population claiming their rights and reporting correctly the incidences.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

During implementation of projects, AFA uses assessments for feedback from affected populations (AP) about needs, thus design projects catering for requirements & ensure accountability in implementation. Also, centers are administrated by center supervisors responsible for communication with parents of AP. This will act as effective channels for communication to enhance project sustainability. Education specialist, inclusion & social workers executing regular classroom observation to monitor implementation, provide feedback and refer individuals as needed. In addition, a complaint box is kept at centers, to provide feedback confidentially that is key to sustainable outcomes & greater impact. Thus, feedback is usually taken at various stages of project using diverse means such as surveys, evaluation forms… In order to follow up on all complaints in line with AAP policy, AFA has an Accountability Focal point to ensure proper delivery & Do No Harm approach.
