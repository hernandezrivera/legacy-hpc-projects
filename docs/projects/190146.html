Project Summary

Through this project, MEPT intends to carry out monitoring
and evidence-gathering campaigns, for the purpose of
advocating for the Government to finance and provide
quality educational services for all in emergency situations
without any discrimination.
With the Citizens for Education in Emergency project,
MEPT will be able to involve different actors from
parliamentarians, journalists, government, and civil society
in the monitoring of humanitarian and emergency response
actions implemented by the Government, Partners, national
and international NGOs, including the different members
that make up the network. Considering that MEPT is a network that acts in the area of
​​advocacy and policy influence in the Education Sector, the project
will involve different member organizations of the network at the
national level, with a special focus on MEPT, Nampula Cabo
Delgado, Niassa. The executive secretariat and MEPT members will
also benefit from the project, as they will be endowed with technical
skills in order to advocate for quality and inclusive Basic Education
in times of emergencies.
MINEDH will also be a target group of the project, since the actions
in question aim to influence the improvement of the implementation
of policies aimed at promoting access to education in emergency
situations.
Parliamentarians as well as the media are also allies since they will
be used in the different actions of the project to achieve the
recommended results.
The final beneficiaries are all Mozambican children, youth and
adults of both sexes affected by the emergency situation who need
primary and secondary schooling.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

MEPT is an advocacy network made up of Civil Society organizations and individuals working in the field of Education, so its interventions always take into account the actions carried out by members at community level.For this particular proposal, sendencias from studies carried out by the company were used, as well as information resulting from training carried out to members of the network and communities.In this sense, we can say that this proposal reflects the needs of network members and communities.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The studies to be carried out will have a specific analytical approach to people with disabilities and how they are affected by the crisis.The data to be collected will also consider elements such as gender and age, the data to be collected will serve to strengthen the work that MEPT has been doing with MINEDH so that policies are more inclusive and take people with disabilities into account and be created specific intervention strategies for people with disabilities in situations of any type of emergency;

7 - Environmental considerations
