Project Summary

Under the STRATEGIC OBJECTIVE 1: Provide life-saving &life-sustaining humanitarian assistance to the most vulnerable people with an emphasis on those in areas with high severity of needs not covered with any other assistance. Coupled with the Sector Objective 1, activity 1.1.3 Community-based protection services as well as CBI/CVA.
Rescate proposes distribution of CVA to older men & women & abandoned children with or without disabilities at two charity Care Centers in East Aleppo, Dar al-Safa (Al-Mashatia) & Al-Mbara al-Islamiya. The aim is to support the charity Centers because their work is negatively affected by the spiking commodities' prices, consequently are of the least affordable types of items which clearly urged families to abandon their siblings (parents &children especially PWDs) due to dire poverty. Poverty resulted with inability to cover PWDs needs for medicines, nutrition, mobility equipment & special care. The 2 centers are under philanthropies from endowment & community that cover management, service providers & caregivers' salaries, food/nutrition, water, electricity, health care and medicines, cleanliness and hygiene for residents & maintenance. Noted that the private contributions reduced since the crisis in Aleppo, hence; the needs of the vulnerable residents are no longer achieved. One of which is electricity repairs, the centers need batteries & inverters for lights & TV. Rescate will settle this cost with separate CVA. It is worth to note that the Centers premises & facilities are clean & equipped for PWDs. The residents are divided in sections for men, women & children. Service providers & caregivers attend on the residents 24/7.
The deteriorating economic situation coupled with increasing prices resulted in the unaffordability of key needs such as clothing, bed sheets, blankets, fuel for heating, electricity alternative for TV, & a very important commodity (diapers) for the targeted vulnerable older men & women, & children especially those with disabilities. This project is designed under protection AoRs which continue to focus on the most at-risk men, women & girls, including adolescents’ girls with disabilities, as well as supporting widows, divorces, separated & single men & women & girls. CP AoR prioritizes the delivery of services to neglected, conflict affected children, unaccompanied & separated children. Under this context, Rescate had access to 2 charity centers accommodating 225 residents from these targeted categories with CVAs to cover urgent needs (blanket distribution to avoid tension) on 6 rounds of transfers during the year. And one-off CVA to support lighting and TV with batteries. Rescate will seek to introduce the Sector SoPs & modalities in arrangement with the sectors for Capacity building at community structures, social workforce, & caregivers at the centers to enhance the residents care and wellbeing. Rescate will have dedicated GBV & CP staff to monitor & ensure the progress. The project will provide capacity building to labour force, caregivers, supervisors & management to raise awareness, address their own attitudes & build skills to design & deliver age-oriented & child care programs, identify & manage chronic & non-communicable diseases among children & older people & to provide accessible information on healthy children & ageing & self-management of chronic diseases, paying attention to the different health needs of older women & men with & without disabilities. The proposed project to provide aid to the centers with CVA 1+ 6 rounds in 2023 to meet most important needs as linked to support hygiene & health impact will enhance their resilience, dignity &positive relation with themselves &community.
Rescate will benefit from its presence in Aleppo for easy access to ensure timely provision of assistance & monitoring results.
Rescate can secure timely approval from MOSAL based on previous cooperation in four rounds of CVA distributions in Qunietra and coastal area.

3 - Needs

This project is accepted; not funded. Prot. PiN 14.74M, targeted affected PiN 11,1M in 2023, Jabal Saman severity 3, criticality due to decreased funding in 2023. Rescate conducted assessments at 2 charity centers with access to financial records that showcase decrease of support vs needs’ status. Rescate witnessed large percentage of residents’ families’ poverty due to unemployment, diminished income opportunities & lack of access to PWDs basic services forcing abanding their siblings’ parents & children.Rescate conducted evidence-based needs analysis by talking to the elderlies, they expressed needs, PJs, blankets, electricity for TV &mostly diapers. Rescate intervention & response activities consider the health & protection implications on elderlies & children. Underfunding means reduction in the protection activities & leaving these peoples’ needs unaddressed & curtailing the impact of protection interventions. The current spiking prices of diapers, IPCs & detergents, clothes, bed sheets, food & medicines making it difficult to maintain intact residents’ health, nutrition, cleanliness & wellbeing.

8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

The proposed intervention aims are providing vital assistance to affected population to reduce negative coping mechanism among the most vulnerable elderly, children &PWDs. To ensure provision of assistance in a safe, dignified, & accountable manner, Rescate mainstreams protection into all its interventions. Risk of sexual exploitation: GBV and PSEA, likelihood: Low – Impact: High Mitigation measure: All Rescate staff, partners, & service providers, sign the Code of Conduct which directly refers to the Protection standards from PSEA & GBV & CP. To improve accountability, Rescate is committed to implement a beneficiary Complaints and Response Mechanisms (CRM) in all its projects to allow beneficiaries (currently are the elderlies, caregivers & service providers) to raise concerns, make complaints and give feedback on Rescate work, as well as the concerned service providers’ work. Any arising issue will be conveyed, including potential PSEA, GBV, or CP issues. All PSEA, GBV &/or CP issues will be addressed first by the PM and GBV and CP officers. They will address the case and will seek professional support Health or PSS if needed via our network. Then share the concern with the Head of Mission based in Damascus, to establish concrete solution & way to proceed to enhance the survivor protection. If needed, HoM will scale the issue to Rescate HQ the Director, in consultation with the specialists’ team, will construct potential durable solutions and/or an appropriate way forward.


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

CVA for protection: Risk that aid provided will be diverted or cause unintended harmful consequences. Mitigation: Targeted risk assessments and ongoing monitoring and adjustment of program accordingly shall be conducted. Modalities are selected based on a thorough assessment of risks and benefits. Joint work between cash and protection colleagues will be fostered to strengthen technical aspects throughout the programme life cycle.Coordination with other sectors, particularly health, remains critical to overcome challenges amongst direct beneficiaries, caregivers, and staff.During the implementation of the project unattended people may feel discriminated and tensions arise. Low that this could happened because blanket mechanism is adopted, hence no tension created. Assumption that pandemics will not disrupt the project. It is difficult to assess beforehand the probability for this to happen and its consequences. Rescate will take prevention measures since the project launch to avoid contagion: PPE will be distributed for the caregivers & service providers. Assumption is that there will be stability of prices for the project implementation, there is a high risk that this will not be the case, and could have grave consequences. Mitigation by support to adjusted budget vs needs & commodities to encourage purchase stock for the project. In terms of support & approvals, the assumption is that stakeholders, Sector, & local authorities maintain a strong collaboration with Rescate
