Project Summary

This project is aim to end Open defecation, train and equip community representative on good hygiene practices, on hand washing techniques, door to door sensitization of the community,This programme will target boys, girls, women and men across all genders.
This programme goal is to end open defecation and improve the health and social economic well-being of communities by reducing the diarrhea-related aliments through providing sustainable proper sanitation and good hygiene practices including capacity building.

1 - Needs

Water and Sanitation related diseases cause significant deaths and sickness in the north East Nigeria. Even without the disruption of an emergency, diarrhoea kills over 30,000 children per week worldwide. During conflict in particular, simple diarrhoea diseases can often kill more people than the conflict itself.the main focus of this programme is to help people to understand and develop good hygiene practices, so as to prevent disease and promote positive attitudes towards cleanliness. And also for the reduction of diarrhoea, and other water related dieases.

2 - Activities

SANITATIONS1. Construction of accessible and gender friendly latrines2. Refuse pit constructions3. Solid waste management trainingHygiene Promotion and Capacity BuildingPHAST (Participatory Hygiene and Sanitation Transformation) will be used. The methodology will focuses on participatory learning and will aim to empower communities to manage their water supply and to control sanitation related diseases by promoting health awareness and understanding. The approach includes basic steps that helps the community identify their issues themselves and find solutions by themselves using local knowledge and wisdom. 1. Sensitization on hand hand washing benefits 2. sensitization on the dangers, health implications and environmental consequences of Open defecation3. Implementation of Community-Led Total Sanitation training for community representative and leaders.4. Selection and Training WASH committee members water system management5. Organizing a Training the Trainers (ToT) on the community hygiene promoters, hygiene promotion sessions with a focus on the prevention, transmission and treatment of diarrhea, especially focusing on children under five.6. Training of artisan on how to construct latrine and other WASH structures.7. Provision of Hygiene kits
