Project Summary

With insurgent attacks worsening in the Cabo Delgado province, the population of IDPs contiues to increase. This situation in exerting pressure on the existing resources such as WASH infrastructure. Affected population are in need of WASH service and the intervention is focused on reducing the suffering of these families. The intervention will provide WASH services for IDP and host community in Montepuez and Palma districts. The intervention will implement the following activities:
a. Rehabilitation and disinfection of existing water points in host communities, accommodation centers, and resettlement areas, including support for water committees;
b. Construction of new water sources for host communities and resettlement areas-
c. Installation and management of temporary sanitation and bathing facilities in accommodation centres / transit camps and / or resettlement sites (first phase);
d. Promotion activities for self-construction of household latrines and garbage pits in resettlement sites and hosting communities (including the provision of slabs);
e. Dissemination of WASH related messages;
f. Procurement and/or distribution of WASH related Non-Food items, including menstrual hygiene health materials;
g. Gender based violence prevention safety audit for WASH planning

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

With the ongoing attacks by insurgent groups, hundreds of thousands of families have fled from their homes, in search of safety in other districts in the southern region of Cabo Delgado. The families have been accommodated in resettlement centers established in Montepuez and returnees in Palma. The projects seeks to alleviate the suffering of these affected populations. The project will provide WASH services to IDPs and returnees in Montepuez and Palma. The project targets 12000 people; 10000 in Montepuez and 2000 in Palma

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Rehabilitation and disinfection of existing water points in host communities, accomodation centers, and resettlement areas, including support for water committees; Construction of new water sources for host communities and resettlement areas- Installation and management of temporary sanitation and bathing facilities in accommodation centres / transit camps and / or resettlement sites (first phase); Promotion activities for self-construction of household latrines and garbage pits in resettlement sites and hosting communities (including the provision of slabs); Dissemination of WASH related messages; Procurement and/or distribution of WASH related Non-Food items, including menstrual hygiene health materials; Gender based violence prevention safety audit for WASH planning

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The project will conduct security risk assessments to identify potential risks. Social and Environmental Responsibility assessment will be contacted to assess any possible negative impact of the project. Through community consultations, possible sources of conflict and risk will be identified.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

Beneficiary identification and selection will be done in consultation with community leaders and target community to identify the most vulnerable persons. This will be done in order to make sure that no one is left out. The project will design a vulnerability assessment tool. The project will consider different vulnerabilities in targeting and selection of beneficiaries.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Persons with disabilities will be identified through consultation with affected population and community leaders. These will be prioritized to getting assistance. Distribution plans will be design to cater for the needs of these, making sure that they easily get access to their entitlements

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project will design a social and environmental sustainability tool to conduct this assessment. Through this assessment, necessary mitigation actions will be identified. The assessment will analyze the different risks which can arise due the implementation of these activities. The risks are related environmental degradations, economic effects on the market, security and conflict between host and IDP communities
