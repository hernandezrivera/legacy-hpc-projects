Project Summary

MARDO urgently calls for aid to safe and dignify lives and livelihoods among the extremely vulnerable households in the IPC 3 and 4 drought-hit, poverty ravaged, under-served and under-funded but formerly hard to reach and access in Bakool region. A rapid scale up is needed given the surge in the number of people in IPC 3 & 4 which most likely increase through December 2019. Without humanitarian assistance, the people facing crisis and emergency food insecurity (IPC 3 and 4) worsen into huge food consumption gap and engage in irreversible coping strategies
Contrary to UNFPA 2014 population survey which says less than 17% region’s population live in the urban, for some time now, the migration from rural areas to urban increased due to the drought, the humanitarian sanction imposed by Insurgents and the returnees from Dadab refugee camps in Huddur, Elbarde and Qansaxdhere. Thus, there are very many IDPs, poor urban and rural dropout vulnerable HHs of whom their situation is very grave as the assessed in August 2019 by MARDO. Adhering to humanitarian vulnerability categorization/ranking and specifically targeting; The Unaccompanied children, Older Persons, Persons with disability, Single Women, Single Headed-households, Persons with chronic illness or psychological condition, Large family and/or family with babies, toddlers or children attending school, Persons at risk (to be identified by protection officers); MARDO identified over 1,000 vulnerable HHs in Huddur, Elbarde and Qansaxdhere towns. Based on this, MARDO proposes six months unconditional cash support for 800 most vulnerable households among these.
According to FSNAU-FEWS-NET- Post-Gu-Technical-Report released - 2 Sept 2019, the number of people facing acute food insecurity in Bakool region are 168,000 persons of which 53,000 are in crisis/emergency. This is an increase of 116% when compared the total reported in February this year by FSNAU, where 78,000 persons only were food insecure in the entire region. in this report, the humanitarian situation in Bakool is not expected to improve in the near future and the number of people currently facing emergency will most likely increase from 8,000 (Sept 2019) to 26,000 till December this year.
Malnutrition remains the major problem persisted over time in Somalia with high levels of Global Acute Malnutrition (GAM) well above emergency threshold of 15% (NCC - Detailed Input for HNOHRP 2019_Revised) and the humanitarian situation continued to deteriorate because of the poor rains in Gu, causing widespread food insecurity exacerbated by large-scale movement of people in Bay and Bakool regions.
MARDO, in agreement with the national/humanitarian response plan and drought intervention strategy on Food Security Cluster, adopts the unconditional transfers (food assistance and cash) and conditional transfers (cash-for-work for small-scale infrastructure (75 KM feeder-road). Cash-based intervention addresses the low-purchasing power per person and per household in Qansaxdhere (Bay region), Elbarde and Huddur (Bakool region) accessing functional money. Cash dignifies the beneficiaries; used for buying food, drugs, vegetables, fruits and milk, re-stocking livestock and acquiring veterinary related drugs and equipment, paying off debts, paying school-fees and increases the credit-worthiness among the economically vulnerable persons.
The proposed intervention (conditional and unconditional cash transfer) will increase the target beneficiaries’ access to food through self-purchase nutritionally rich-food: The program will also improve the local market through injection of considerable amount of money into the local economy as the unconditional cash beneficiaries will purchase their food requirements from the local markets – MARDO identified the intervention will have various impacts in the region. Along with this, the project will improve the future transport system of the target through feeder roads in most affected areas in region.

Project Activities

For the past seven months the number of persons facing humanitarian crisis only in Bakool region increased 116% - i.e. from 78,000 persons to 168,000 persons (FSNAU-FEWS-Reports – February and Sept 2019) and will most likely continue increase till December 2019. In addition, leaving in war-torn country where all the basic social services have been frozen for so long and covering the 100% needs of these people have always been impossible, MARDO put more effort to systematically select the most vulnerable drought affected households from the returnees, IDPs and the drought affected population in Qansaxdher, Elbarde and Huddur districts of Bay and Bakool region – SWS of Somalia. Reaching the people who are most in need of assistance is central to the humanitarian mandate and is reflected in the humanitarian principles of humanity and impartiality by ensuring both access to protection and assistance in safety and dignity. In a humanitarian emergency, people with disabilities, IDPs, women-headed households, unaccompanied minors are often among those most in need of assistance as they are at heightened risk of violence, exploitation or abuse. These groups and among others also face discrimination due to significant barriers in accessing needed humanitarian assistance and these are the concerns that the proposed program aims to intervene through improving household access to food and other basic needs which are the factors determine the vulnerability and nutrition level.The program entirely aims at improving households’ purchasing power and access food varieties hence elevate the malnutrition level. In addition, MARDO has identified in recent assessment that the unconditional cash beneficiaries can purchase the food varieties they need from the local markets and, as a result, the program will also revitalize the local market by injecting a substantial amount of money into the local economyMoreover, the following include the program specific target beneficiaries; the Unaccompanied children, Older Persons, Persons with disability, Single Women, Women/Single Headed-households, Persons with chronic illness or psychological condition, Large family and/or family with babies, toddlers or children attending school, Persons at risk (to be identified by protection officers);- Community mobilization and re-sensitization on the program activities and formation of project community committees comprising of member representing all community components i.e. Community leaders, elders, religious leaders, women/youth groups leaders and representatives from the local authority The project specific activities include;• Community mobilization and the formation of the community project committees where all categories of the community will be represented including Women and Youth Groups and Disables• With the full support and the involvement of the project community committees, re-selection and registration of 700 beneficiaries for the unconditional cash transfer component of the project output – these 700 beneficiaries will be selected from the most vulnerable households from Returnees, IDPs and the drought affected and the vulnerable from the host community targeting; the Unaccompanied minors, Older Persons, Persons with disability, Single Women, Single Headed-households, Persons with chronic illness or psychological condition, Large family and/or family with babies, toddlers or children attending school, Persons at risk (to be identified by protection officers); the poor households with PLW and/or disable/bedridden persons) in Huddur and Ceelberde districts – Bakool region and Qansaxdhere of Bay region. • Selection and registration of 220 casual workers for the conditional cash transfer program and the identification of project activity site (the feeder road)• Payments for the 700 unconditional cash transfer beneficiaries for 6 (six) months – USD 100 per month per household• Reporting, monitoring and evaluation of the program

Project Indicators

Project Outcome Indicators700 most vulnerable returnees from refugee camps in Kenya, IDPs and drought affected households provided with emergency relief cash support to save their lives and livelihoods situations. 220 more HHs will also targeted as the direct beneficiaries of the proposed cash for work program. Taking an average of 7 members per households, the project will reduce the percentage of people in severe acute food insecurity phases (crisis and worse) from the total population in Somali from post Gu 2019, particularly, the most vulnerable drought affected people (IDPs, Returnees and the host community) in Bay and Bakool regions by 6,440 persons. The program will also revitalize the local market by injecting a substantial amount of money into the local economy – lastly, as the program entirely aims at improving households’ purchasing power and access food varieties hence elevate the malnutrition level; Food consumption score, household dietary diversity and reduced coping strategy index among the target community include the general project outcome indicators. Categorically, the following are the outcome indicators of the proposed intervention;• Prevalence of severe food insecurity in the total population reduced • Percentage of population accessing basic food varieties at households level (i.e. Frequency as well as Food groups)• All categories of the community segments including women / youth groups and disables represented in the community project committees• 700 unconditional cash transfer beneficiaries selected/registered and paid for six months with USD 100 per month• 220 casual labour selected and registered for the rehabilitation of 75 KM feeder road in Huddur district• 75 KM feeder road rehabilitated• Prevalence of anemia among the most vulnerable PLW, elderly, unaccompanied minors and disables reduced• Program success and challenges as well as evaluations reported based on the donor required intervals.

Mitigation Measures

MARDO always considers gender equity from the planning to implementation through the monitoring and the evaluation of projects. A gender dimensions based on understanding of women, girls, boys and men's different needs, roles, responsibilities, capacities and risks are also put into consideration throughout the project period. Generally, with the aim of promoting community participation and engagement on the project activities, MARDO forms a group of committee members from the community named Project Community Committees (PCC). During the formation of the PCC, the project considers gender proportion by ensuring all categories of the community are represented in the project committees. In this regard, the members from traditional leaders, elders, women and youth groups, religious leaders and disable groups (including physical handicapped and hearing/visually impaired people) and the local authority will all be represented in the project community committees. Therefore, the intervention will ensure equity through equal selection representative from all categories of the community in the consultation, decision making and capacity development. MARDO will collect gender-disaggregated data by sex and age of relevant data related to Nutrition activities. MARDO will ensure that all segments of the drought affected population including women have equal access to humanitarian assistance and that targeted support to advance gender equality is based on a gender analysis. The program will meet the distinct needs of women, girls, boys and men to generate positive and sustainable outcomes.MARDO shall apply gender segregated community consultation meetings enabling women and girls as well as the disables to address their specific concerns, prioritize their needs and appropriately inform the development of proposed activities. MARDO shall Endeavour to provide equal chance, inclusiveness in participation of minority and majority community members as well as disabled groups in the district and be able to transparent enough in terms of employment, capacity building, empowerment and resource sharing hence anticipated to foster comprehensive community engagement in the target locations. As part of inception activities, MARDO will collect information on areas of concern in regard to both vertical and horizontal equality approach. MARDO will be protection conscious, particularly, during the selection and payment of the program beneficiaries.
