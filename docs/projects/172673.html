Project Summary

The Sudan IPC Acute Food Insecurity Analysis (JUNE - DECEMBER 2020) estimated that 6 million people (21% of the population analyzed) are experiencing humanitarian Crisis or worse levels of food insecurity (IPC Phase 3 or above). This is the highest figure ever recorded in the history of IPC in Sudan compared to previous years. Around 2.2 million people are facing an Emergency acute food insecurity situation (IPC Phase 4) and around 7.4 million people are facing Crisis acute food insecurity (IPC Phase 3), while nearly 15.9 million people are estimated to be in Stress (IPC Phase 2). This is also a high figure compared to previous analyses, Overall localities from 18 states.
White Nile state one of state that affected directly by the flash floods and economic crisis since the separation of South Sudan. This left behind a host of economic challenges in White Nile state especially after beginning of conflict in the new born country led to large numbers of south Sudanese asylum seekers inside state and as all other Sudan states after appear of COVID-19 White Nile state take strict preventive measures and adopted, including a nightly curfew, banning gatherings, and closure of markets, schools, and universities, have impacted the livelihoods of many urban poor who depend on daily work in the informal sector.
This project aims at building the resilience of vulnerable communities in White Nile state. It targets 6 communities and 8,700 vulnerable people in targeted areas with below breakdown:
Food security and livelihood response: Targeted 6,600 (63% female and 37% male) and through provide different livelihood alternatives (Vocational training, livestock support, Agriculture support through agriculture tools and inputs for vulnerable HHs)
Education response: targeted 2,000 ( (49% girls and %51 boys and from total targeted children 1% children with disabilities) through improve quality education and other services by construction and rehabilitation of classroom and provision of furniture(desk and bench set) , direct support through cash grant for HHs and incentive for school teachers and response to COVID-19 pandemic through deliver of awareness sessions ,hard copies and training on COVID-19 school protocols and distribution Non medical mask for children ,teachers and school staff.
The project will provide sustainable assistance through multisectoral interventions that promote resilience among t affected populations and strengthen human security in targeted locations

1 - Needs

According to FSL HNO/HRP and IPC 2020-2021, there is high food insecurity levels driven by high inflation, a deteriorating economy and high food price exacerbated by the impacts of the COVID-19 pandemic. An estimated 7.1 million people face crisis or worse levels of food insecurity (IPC Phase 3 and above) and need urgent action to save lives. IPC Acute Food Insecurity Analysis (JUNE - DECEMBER 2020) in White Nile state estimated that About 479,350 from 2,612,410 of the population analyzed are experiencing humanitarian Crisis or worse levels of food insecurity (IPC Phase 3 or above) in Aljabalain (about 87,269) and Alsalam locality (about 83,105) and about 57,609 of population analyzed in Kosti locality. In addition to this multiple crisis situation (Inflation, acute food insecurity, floods and COVID-19 pandemic) that targeted localities is facing, this effect is even worse for other marginalized groups such as persons with disabilities and those in extreme poverty. Projections indicate that the informal sector will be particularly impacted by the COVID-19 containment measures. As estimates show that women make up 50 percent of this workforce, the limiting of work opportunities will likely affect household income and have more severe and longer-term impact on women and as families increasingly resort to negative coping mechanisms, pre-existing, child protection problems become more acute and widespread. While the effects of food insecurity are felt across this targeted communities. JASMAR in targeted localities responses to the identified needs are in line with the FSL sector response strategy to improve their access to livelihood alternatives and life-sustaining basic services to enhance their resilience.The project will targets 2000 children’s from host community - in White Nile state in Kosti locality and targeted AL wadi primary school for girls hosting about 1030 and AL wadi primary school for boys about 910 childern and all of them between (7 to 13 years old) .Abosharif community one of communities affected directly by COVID-19 pandemic and first areas in White Nile state reported by COVID-19 cases and in this community 2 primary school serving more than 2000 children from 5 areas in Kosti locality near to Abosharif community which lead to crowding in some school and other activities .

2 - Activities/Outputs

For food security and livelihood sector :The project will targets 6000 host community - in White Nile state in six areas which is Abosharif B3 ,Abosharif B4 and Alaideen in Kosti locality.Alagaya and JODA in Aljabalain localities and Alzelate Asola in Alsalam localtity .The project will support more than 6000 HH in Kosti ,Asalam and Aljableen locality.This project aims to enhance the self-reliance of vulnerable host communities by promoting activities targeting households and individuals through different livelihood alternatives for people in need.Activity 1 : conduct a rapid needs and market assessment: The assessment will be comprised of three-tiers and will explore the access to supporting functions, including their access to information, training and financial services. The next aspect will be a target group assessment to gain a profile of the host community members and their skills, , education and work experience. Finally, a market analysis to gain an overview of the relevant sectors and subsectors and potential for sustainable growth and employment creation will be done. This assessment will inform the future design and development of a needs-led and market-driven livelihood program for refugees. Activity 2 :Beneficiary Selection: The project team will carry out several meetings with different stakeholders including community leaders, relevant local officials, and host community leaders. These meetings will elaborate and clarify the roles and responsibilities of partners. will focus on improving the employability, self-employment and entrepreneurship skills of beneficiaries through access to applicable training's, learning, and skills building. Activity 3: If confirmed by the needs/market assessments conducted at the start of the program, syllabi could include the following: food processing, traditional perfume making and soap making, cell phone maintenance, fishing, bread making, vegetable trading, agriculture and livestock support. If livelihoods are identified which differ from the established syllabi, vocational trainers will be found to develop new curricula; Activity 4 : Business Start-Up After completing vocational training, each participant will be equipped with a full essential toolkit to enable her/him to commence livelihood activities based on the knowledge acquired in training. If determined feasible through the early assessments, participants may be provided with either a cash transfer or voucher to purchase kits in local markets, the exact modality will be determined pending the outcome of the assessment. If not feasible, business start-up kits will be purchased in Khartoum and provided through in-kind distributions to participants. If feasible (political and community acceptance exists) a cash grant will be provided to each person successfully graduating from the vocational training courses to cover business startup costs. The exact amount of the startup grant will vary depending on the training area and the location of the beneficiaries.Planned activities for education sector :The project will targets 2000 children’s from host community - in White Nile state in Kosti locality and the planed activities is: Primary school 4 classrooms constructes, 4 Primary school classrooms Rehabilitation, Provision of 200 Primary school furniture, provide incentive and cash grand for HHs and teachers targeted 500 HHs economically affected by COVID-19 pandemic. response for COVID-19 prevention measures and protocols will be through all School staff / PTA members trained on COVID19 school protocols and targeted schools received Hard Copy Of COVID-19 School Protocols, Teachers Trained On COVID-19 Teacher Preparedness Package or Similarned and provision of 5000 Non-Medical Masks for children’s, teachers and other school staff
