Project Summary

This project is in response to the critical humanitarian needs in Dar El Salam and Saraf Omra locality's North Darfur State, and Elsalam Locality targeting refugees and host communities under sectorial outcome , The project mainly targets Returnees who have return to regional village due to the Stability of security. It also targets the most vulnerable community categories in the locality. The overall goal of the project is to reduce morbidity and mortality rate among Returnees beneficiaries and most vulnerable people. According to recent assessment conducted in Dar El Salam,and Saraf Omra the returnee in Dar El Salam and Saraf Omar locality's were found not considered by humanitarian assistance. The focus has mostly been in other Returnees areas. Such influx of returnees to the area has put extra pressure to an already existing country host issues as lack of health care, drugs and devaluation of the local currency. Total project target is 4528 individuals, divided to 13862 Returnees and 18422 vulnerable residents. The strategy is to provide primary health care and nutrition services through opening of primary health clinics. DPI has conducted assessment and found out the Returnees s suffer a lot to access health facilities. DPI had consulted the MoH and agreed to establish the basic Primary Health clinics. The project aims to establishment of primary health center and running of the centers in the targeted localities, in line with WHO , and MoH DPI will provide supplies such as drugs , nutrition supplies in collaboration with UNICEF as Nutrition sector lead, The strategy is to provide primary health care and nutrition services through opening of primary health clinic. The project also is designed to contribute towards environmental protection. the project will also contribute in provision of Nutrition services such as MUAC screening , MAM and CIMAM, SAM targeting pregnant lactated women and under five children from one to 56 months.

Needs

The humanitarian situation in the Darfur states remains critical as thousands of IDPs are remaining in camps as no grantee for safety and protection and security in original village of returns. Adding to the complexity, South Sudanese have continued to flee Sudan refugee arrivals are triggered by conflict, hunger and the lack of (or limited) humanitarian support from within South Sudan sudanese refugees In South Darfur and vulnerable Returnees and host communities of Saraf Omra and Dar elsalam localities DPI assessment identified emergency health and nutrition concerns services in Saraf Omra and Dar elsalam Al Salam Localities. The assessment recommendations include the provision of primary and reproductive health services and moderate and acute malnutrition treatments

Activities

In response to these needs, the proposed activities will include health, nutrition interventions. DPI will establish primary health care clinic to deliver primary healthcare service packages such as referral service for selected cases, medical services and environmental protection. In nutrition will target under 5 children and pregnant lactated women , DPI will facilitate change in behavior and practice not only through mothers who are key in the process, but also other household members such as fathers, boys and girls who play a critical role for change to take place. Some action will also involve community engagement as norms and practices are deep rooted and need community wide interventions. Adolescents and the youth will be reached through community level awareness campaigns and The project will develop gender sensitive and environmental sensitization , the project will involve formulation of health and nutrition committees / volunteers and train them on message sending and awareness among their communities , 15 females and 5 male will be formulated in each targeted areas trained on different topics such as maternal , nutrition , primary health approaches , MUAC screening and referral , forbidden practices within communities provision of Mobil clinics for far distant patients , and for referral provision of nutrition packages will be through line ministries secondary staffs, formulation of health and nutrition volunteers for awareness practices and household reach DPI will as well formulate and train number of women in each targeted location maternal volunteers.
