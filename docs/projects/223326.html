Project Summary

Through this project, YOPCODI seeks to improve the resilience of Children includingchildren living with disabilities affected by emergencies through providing continuousequitable access to child protection services in Yunusari, Geidam, Bade, Gujba, Yusufari,Fune and Nangere. YOPCODI will implement the project through 3 result areas.
Result area 1:
Children at risk (boys and girls) including adolescents, children with disabilities accesspreventive and comprehensive quality response services in a timely manner, this includesprovision of both access to quality to effective case management of MHPSS for childrenaffected by emergencies, including children with disabilities through structured age-specifi c, creative and recreational manner including life skill education interventions foradolescents.
Result area 2:
Parents, caregivers, children, and communities promote positive social norms that protectchildren and women from violence, abuse, neglect, exploitation, and harmful practicesthrough awareness creation, caregiver’s economic empowerment and positive parentingsession to caregivers including caregivers of children living with disabilities.
Result area 3:
To improve sustainability, YOPCODI will enhance the capacities of child protectionpartners and social welfare workforce to deliver quality prevention and response servicesthrough a strengthened child protection system at community, LGA level, and state level.
More so, YOPCODI will engage government and community stakeholders to have an all-inclusive monitoring of project activities and also workshops will be organized to identifyand discuss solutions to problems identifi ed and develop action plans for effi cientimplementation.

1 - Needs

Approximately, 2 million individuals require child protection services from abuse, discrimination, exploitation, neglect, and violence in the north-east Nigeria due to the absence of adequate child protection systems and community-based safety nets. The CP AoR aims to reach 1, 044, 526 million individuals from the People in Need (PiN). The Child Protection Area of Responsibility estimates the number of target beneficiaries based on LGAs with severity ratings of 2, 3, and 4 applied across the population of IDPs, host communities, and returnees in alignment with Humanitarian Need Overview. This has increased needs due to the following factors: secondary displacement of IDPs due to the government-enforced “returns”/relocations into unsafe areas not allowing for self-sufficiency (movements, livelihoods, services etc.). Thirteen (14) years of war in North-East Nigeria has deprived children of a normal childhood, exposing them to violence, abuse, neglect, exploitation, and conscription into NSAGs. The most vulnerable include children in IDP camps, secondary displaced children from closed camps, returnee children, Children associated with Armed Groups, unaccompanied/separated children, and children with disabilities. Girls are exposed to grave violations of children’s rights such as limited access to education, child marriage, sexual gender-based violence, emotional/ physical violence, and trafficking. Boys face the risk of forceful recruitment/abduction/re-recruitment byNSAGs and trafficking. Parents and caretakers have been exposed to stress /violence and need child protection services. Based on the Child Protection Area of Responsibility Strategy 2022-26, which is flexible and caters to both humanitarian and development given the different levels of the humanitarian situation across BAY states will enable this project to bridge the humanitarian and development nexus while setting up a robust child protection system and fostering the localization agenda as part of the grand bargain where the Child Protection Area of Responsibility in Northeast Nigeria is quite a head. The concept seeks to build on this strategic direction to ensure comprehensive prevention and response services linked to the humanitarian country team centrality of protection strategy, the humanitarian response plan 2025, and the Child Protection Area of Responsibility strategic priorities in the following Local Government areas of Bade, Damaturu, Fune, Geidam, Gulani, and Tarmua LGAs in Yobe State that have been identified with the highest severity levels of needs.

2 - Activities

This proposed project is designed to respond to the most critical child protection needs and gaps identified in Bade, Damaturu, Fune, Geidam, Gulani, and Tarmua LGAs. This is proposed to be delivered through the provision of psychosocial support through structured and mobile recreational facilities in child-friendly spaces, the provision of psychosocial support services, counseling, positive parenting to caregivers, and the reinforcement of community-based child protection mechanisms. 8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

Understanding that women, men, boys, and girls are affected differently by disasters and displacement, the project will prioritize gender equity and the promotion of women's rights in all our humanitarian responses. YOPCODI policies recognize that SGBV is a violation of human rights and seek to mainstream work to reduce SGBV and protection in all programs. YOPCODI has an internal gender policy, and an SGBV policy, and has minimum standards for gender in emergencies as a tool to measure consistency in promoting gender equality through interventions. The organization trains its program personnel in gender mainstreaming and has GBV and PSEA focal points that work closely together to mainstream gender into programs and projects. All activities will incorporate considerations for gender and the different needs of women, men, boys, and girls as well as people with disabilities

9 - Accountability to Affected Populations
9.1 - Check here if the project is based on a specific needs assessment(s)

Yes

9.1.a - Specify (and give URL link if possible to) the relevant needs assessment document.

YOPCODI will work within the framework of accountability designed by the AAP/CE working group that enforces the provision of information, involvement of the target community/persons in decision-making process related to the humanitarian response, and setting up of complaint mechanism and feedback loop during the implementation of the project. Therefore, complaints and feedback generated through such channels will be used to make necessary adjustments to program delivery. At the inception of the project, a meeting with state and LGA-level stakeholders will be held in the community for easy attendance by the beneficiaries including special interest groups such as persons with disability, women, and girls to explain to the beneficiary groups and government partners the project deliverables, their roles and expected results of the project, and the change the project aims to support. To ensure that the principal beneficiaries’ (Children) views are captured, YOPCODI will promote child participation in the appropriate layers of the project, to give them a sense of belonging and encouragement. This will create continuous communication with communities to inform them about the program (at inception), gather and respond to feedback on monthly basis, and disseminate assessment findings, lessons, and data (baseline). The dissemination will be followed with an explanation of what the results meant and brainstorming sessions on what the representatives felt could be done to improve the situation in their communities. YOPCODI will adopt community participatory approaches in planning, implementation and monitoring of the project. Examples of approaches that will be used in ensuring accountability to the affected populations include age, gender and culturally appropriate consultative forums with stakeholders at the state and community levels on key aspects of the project, community mobilization and sensitization including feedback from and to stakeholders in the affected communitie.
