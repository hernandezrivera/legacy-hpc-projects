Project Summary

This humanitarian initiative will provide emergency water through trucking and the rapid rehabilitation of damaged water points, along with the establishment of emergency sanitation facilities to improve access sanitation for IDPs, returnees, and non-displaced populations. Hygiene education campaigns will focus on handwashing, safe water storage, and sanitation practices to reduce waterborne diseases. Essential hygiene kits (including dignity items) and waste management supplies will be distributed to vulnerable households while local committees and health workers will be trained to manage water and sanitation infrastructure. In the conflict-affected areas of Quissanga, Metuge, and Mocimboa da Praia, emergency WASH assistance will be provided to internally displaced people, returnees, and vulnerable non-displaced populations. This immediate humanitarian response aims to deliver urgent relief to these vulnerable groups while also strengthening the resilience of local government, civil society, and WASH infrastructure.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The project will prioritize urgent humanitarian activities in the Quissanga, Metuge, and Mocimboa da Praia districts of Cabo Delgado to deliver lifesaving WASH assistance to internally displaced persons (IDPs), returnees, and vulnerable host communities. Each district’s interventions are tailored to address specific vulnerabilities and the unique humanitarian needs of the affected populations. Key activities include:Emergency safe water provision: • Rapid rehabilitation and repair to damaged small scale water supply systems (boreholes, small networks, wells), water trucking, household water treatmentEmergency sanitation• Construction of emergency sanitation facilities for affected communities in accommodation centers. Support to communities to ensure HH sanitation access. Critical hygiene promotion:• Implement lifesaving hygiene education campaigns focused on handwashing, safe water storage, and sanitation practices to reduce waterborne diseases.• Distribute essential hygiene kits (including menstrual hygiene management supplies, waste management tools/supplies Capacity building: • Train local committees and health workers to manage water and sanitation infrastructure and deliver health and hygiene education.Cross-District and Support Activities• Implement a comprehensive M&E system to track progress and adapt activities based on feedback and changing needs.• Collaborate closely with WASH Cluster, local authorities and civil society organizations to ensure that the project aligns with regional policies and sustainability goals. The project outcomes are:1. At project end, at least 75% of WASH target groups (of which Ø ≥40% women, ≥8% elderly (ÄM), ≥5% persons with disabilities (PWD)) regularly use the improved/new WASH facilities at community level.2. By project end, at least 80% of WASH target groups have access to adequate clean water (≥15 liters/person/day with 0 fecal coliform bacteria)3. By the of the project, at least 80% of the target population report knowing at least 3 of 5 critical times for handwashing 4. By the end of the project, at least 75% of the users of the rehabilitated/constructed sanitary facilities of the local health facilities report that they are clean and usable.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The proposed project utilizes a thorough risk assessment and safeguarding strategy to proactively identify and mitigate potential risks, ensuring no harm to beneficiaries, especially those most vulnerable.• The project initiates with a detailed assessment of security, environmental, cultural, and logistical factors. Regular updates allow the team to anticipate and respond to shifting conditions, especially those impacting displaced or conflict-affected populations.• Community members, particularly women and leaders, are actively involved in risk identification. Feedback mechanisms enable beneficiaries to report concerns, raise issues, and provide recommendations, ensuring risks are swiftly addressed and interventions remain safe and effective.• Adopting a “do-no-harm” approach, the project emphasizes gender sensitivity and the protection of vulnerable groups. Activities respect cultural contexts while encouraging gender equality and health improvements.• Regular monitoring and adaptive planning allow adjustments to strategies in response to emerging risks, supporting safe delivery of WASH, health, and nutritional services.This approach maintains a safe, respectful, and adaptable environment, ensuring positive, risk-sensitive impact across target communities.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The proposed project fosters meaningful access to assistance and actively empowers women and elderly persons through targeted, inclusive strategies:• From the outset, women, elderly persons, and local leaders participate in decision-making processes to ensure that interventions reflect their specific needs and perspectives. Inviting women’s groups and elder representatives into planning sessions promotes interventions sensitive to their unique challenges.• This project will deliver a gender-sensitive humanitarian response by training women as community health and hygiene promoters, empowering them to lead local WASH and health initiatives. Through skills-based training tailored for women and elderly community members, the project will support active, inclusive engagement in sustainable solutions. This training will equip women with knowledge in emergency health and hygiene practices, fostering their capacity to champion WASH practices and contribute to long-term community resilience in humanitarian settings.• WASH and health services are designed to accommodate elderly individuals, with mobility-friendly facilities. Women receive hygiene kits that include menstrual health items, and maternal health sessions address women’s specific health priorities.• Regular feedback sessions with women and elderly groups encourage community ownership. These sessions allow participants to voice concerns, share challenges, and guide adjustments to project activities, ensuring responsiveness and accountability. This approach enhances immediate access to essential services, emphasizing the roles of women and elderly persons in community well-being and leadership.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The project will take an inclusive and proactive approach to identify individuals with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance:• The project will collaborate with community leaders, and health workers to identify individuals with disabilities within target areas. This will involve direct outreach, household assessments, and consultations to ensure these individuals are included in all project activities.• A thorough needs assessment will be conducted to identify the specific challenges faced by people with disabilities in accessing WASH, and health services. This will focus on physical, informational, and social barriers, addressing both environmental and attitudinal challenges.• Project facilities like water points, latrines, will be designed or adapted to be accessible, with features such as ramps and handrails. • Project staff and volunteers will undergo training on disability inclusion and rights, ensuring they are equipped to meet the needs of individuals with disabilities. Community members will also be sensitized to reduce stigma and foster an inclusive environment.These measures aim to ensure full participation and equitable access for people with disabilities, empowering them as active participants in emergency WASH activities. Regular monitoring and feedback will help address any emerging barriers.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The proposed project in Cabo Delgado, Mozambique, is committed to minimizing environmental impacts while implementing emergency lifesaving WASH interventions. Several measures will be taken to ensure the integration of environmental considerations:• An initial EIA will assess potential environmental risks associated with emergency infrastructure works, including water points, and emergency latrines, this will identify risks such as water resource depletion and waste management challenges, informing appropriate mitigation strategies.• Water points and latrines will be designed with minimal ecological disruption. Waste management systems will be implemented to ensure proper treatment and prevent pollution, particularly from latrine waste.• The project will prioritize the use of locally sourced materials for construction to reduce carbon emissions. • Environmental education will be provided to promote sustainable WASH practices, such as efficient water use, proper hygiene, and responsible waste disposal. This will help reduce environmental degradation, including deforestation and soil erosion, common issues in the region.• Regular monitoring will assess the effectiveness of environmental mitigation measures, ensuring that project activities are adjusted as needed to reduce risk of harmful effects.
