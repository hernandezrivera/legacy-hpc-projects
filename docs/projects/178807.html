Project Summary

The intervention aims to respond to shelter and winterization needs for the most vulnerable population in NWS, where we plan to provide shelter rehabilitation assistance to 1,000 HHs, we also plan to implement temporary dignified shelter units for sheltering 600 HHs from the newly displaced IDPs, besides implementing disaster risk reduction activity targeting all Atmeh camp population, through providing the essential infrastructure rehabilitation for 15 km of roads and drainage, as well as more than 10 culverts rehabilitation in Atmeh camp. NFI assistance will be provided to approximately 4,000 vulnerable HHs in Atmeh Camp, and overall Idleb, while the winterization activity will be implemented in Atmeh Camp where we expect the population to reach to 40,000 HHs of IDPs.
The plan will require $ 9,360,000 for supporting 208,000 people with the winterization, non-food items and shelter support.

4 - Needs

Ten years of conflict in Syria have made the population’s needs extremely large, issues related to shelter adequacy, winterization need, and non-food items lack remain significant and keep reported throughout all needs assessment reports and surveys; recent cross-data (REACH, HNAP, CCCM, and SNFI Cluster) indicate that among NWS, Dana subdistrict comes in the high level of severity and the highest number of PIN between NWS subdistricts. According to Whole of Syria Shelter/NFI Sector-March 2021, Dana SD contains 801,481 PIN for shelter in catastrophic severity level.Due to the continuous waves of displacement that the area has received, it is severely overcrowded. the rapid need assessment conducted by Global Communities during Feb 2021, confirmed that many IDP live in unfinished buildings, Collective Centers, and damaged shelters (in different levels of damages), in general, the majority of newly arrived IDPs were preferring to seek to shelter in Collective Centres, Unﬁnished Buildings, self-settled settlements, but these accommodations are temporary and intentions are to move elsewhere if they have the chance which create lack in shelter space availability in that area. As Shelter Conditions in NWS report stated (HNAP & Shelter/NFI Cluster), "Damages are most reported in unfinished houses/apartments and collective shelters. Overall, 41% living in unfinished houses or apartments and 38% living in collective shelters report damages. One in ten IDP households living in tents also report damages" besides "Lack of space inside the shelter is the most important issues as reported by households in regard to their current shelter (top three subdistricts reported in Dana, Atareb, Maaret Tamsrin)"

5 - Implementation of activities

For shelter rehab GC will work closely with the most in-need communities and those which hosted a large number of IDP to prioritize the intervention locations, after conducting the technical need assessment, GC will start registering and verifying the beneficiaries in the targeted locations and conduct the required HLP due diligence at HH level, before having the tri-parties agreements and start the implementation, which will be carried on through local contractors. For temporary dignified shelter units, using the same methodology to identify the safe and proper locations for the activity implementation and examining the recommended guidelines in site selection and preparation standards for creating the planned camps, and considering HLP due diligence for the site land, topographic levels and flood considerations, enough farness from the front lines, nearness to exist settlements, main roads, and vital locations, and availability of the essential services in and around. We will identify the beneﬁciaries either from those on move and don't ﬁnd any shelter, or from the overcrowded collective centers and camps in unsafe locations. GC will rehabilitate critical infrastructure in Atmeh camp to facilitate the delivery of life-saving essential WASH services and improve the overall shelter status of Atmeh Camp residents. GC will follow the HLP guidance outlined in the SNFI Cluster’s due diligence guidelines, where we will implement the proposed repairs on the land with no HLP issues of problems.GC will target the most affected households by harsh winter’s rainstorms in Idlib, and particularly Atmeh Camp, through distributing essential non-food items to support people in need with household lifesaving items to sustain family.

7.1 - Please list any risks related to sexual exploitation and abuse that have been identified during the project design phase and any risk mitigation and prevention measures that have been adopted and built into the project

Women are at particular risk when it comes to being subject to protection concerns such as harassment and exploitation when they navigate markets and aid assistance. Prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse (PSEA) considerations are overriding components of all activities. Under this plan, we will take several steps (training staff, feedback mechanism, male and female staff members, and clauses in contracts) to work towards mitigating any susceptibility to such risks. All staff will receive training on the importance of placing beneficiary feedback into the design and assessment of the program, and the importance of PSEA of beneficiaries [this helps ensure that the project does not contribute to any abuse between beneficiaries and implementing organizations]. GC staff are required to sign a code of conduct including protocols for maintaining confidentiality of program and beneficiary data to ensure that sensitive beneficiary information is kept confidential. GC engages male and female staff to ensure access to females and women-headed HHs

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

The following risks and assumptions are expected while implementing the activities; 1.Fake beneﬁciaries / Duplication / Non-adherence to selection criteria. 2.Selecting service provider with low capacity (technical/ﬁnancial/project management) 3.Implementing of unneeded rehabilitation works or over reporting. 4.Procuring with high price, Over budget, A contractor with low capacity/quality 5.Safety and Security risk during the implementation 6.Market failure - Limited supply and large demand The existing and planned mitigating measures; -Review assessments by senior staff and return to ﬁeld team with correction action -Feedback mechanism -Beneﬁciary registration and veriﬁcation process -Post Distribution Monitoring -Proper inventory management - Reviewing the data/ purchasing requests by senior technical team member, send correction notes to the ﬁeld. -Precise market assessments -Negotiation process -Safety procedures.This plan is designed based on our nuanced understanding of the current safety and security situation, including risks posed by loss of cross-border access, and health risks such as COVID-19. Through our ongoing programs, GC has contingency plans and mitigation measures in place should the COVID-19 situation in Syria shift during 2021. GC will regularly review and update plans according to the extent of COVID-19 pandemic in Syria, we will consider all the SNFI cluster guidance in this regard.
