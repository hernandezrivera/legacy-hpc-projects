Project Summary

With the passage of 11 years since the Syrian crisis, which brought with it many crises that were mainly reflected on the poor families, who were hardly securing their daily food, as this family suffered in particular from consequences, whether on the economic, social, moral or psychological level, as it caused a decrease in awareness Al-Kafi has, especially with regard to the social and psychological aspect of these families, as these detachments were reflected on the members of their families, young men and women, as the bulk of their lack of education took them to work in difficult circumstances and to great ignorance in many of the dangers of this life.
Among these areas that we are talking about is the As-Suwayda Governorate, which is located in the south of Syria and economically depends on agriculture and the assistance of its expatriate sons, but in light of the current crisis the agricultural sector has suffered from weakness in support in addition to the fact that many wells are out of service, which has caused financial difficulties for these families In addition to what the Corona pandemic has imposed on the countries of expatriation and continuous stone, which weakened the capabilities of the sons of these families to support their families, the number of the governorate’s population, according to population statistics for 2019, is approximately 425,000 people and approximately 125 thousand families, and the percentage of youth in it is approximately 40% of the number of people in the governorate.
Our project aims to launch awareness campaigns specifically for young people to raise awareness about the many dangers faced by the youth group, identifying (early marriage), where the youth group suffers from a wide spread of these two phenomena in particular, as the phenomenon of early marriage for young men and women under the age of 16 has spread in light of this crisis. In the villages of As-Suwayda Governorate, this is due to several reasons, the first of which is the lack of awareness among the parents, and the second is the economic weakness of these families, where dozens of marriage cases are recorded every month. In 2019, nearly 800 cases of divorce were recorded, which is a very high rate if we know that the marriage rate in the same year amounted to 2759 marriages. Accordingly, the divorce index is close to 40% in the governorate as a result of early marriage.
The project aims to launch a series of dialogue sessions first with community figures in these villages to discuss this phenomenon and spread the thought of the danger of this issue to society. We will hold 100 dialogue sessions in 100 villages over the years 2022 and 2023, at a rate of 50 sessions per year for the community figures, and from these sessions The dialogue we proceed to the second stage, which is to launch awareness sessions for a group of young men and women whose names are suggested by community figures to attend these sessions at a rate of 10 young men and women from each village, taking into account the gender ratio of 50%. We will conduct 100 dialogue sessions during 2022/2023 at a rate of 50 One session each year and then proceed to the third stage, which is subjecting a group of young men and women who participated in these workshops, at a rate of young men and women from each dialogue session, to be selected by their colleagues in the dialogue sessions to undergo a set of trainings over a period of two weeks, during which they are able to hold training sessions within their villages to raise awareness about this danger and other dangers that target young people, while we follow up on them during implementation and provide all possible facilities for the success of their sessions. Finally, the young men and women participants in a session Youth dialogue is involved in conducting social surveys to ensure the extent of society’s satisfaction with such sessions and their impact on them, and to take feedback on our future projects.

In 2019, about 810 divorce cases out of 2,850 marriages were recorded, which is a very high number, close to 40% of marriage cases. Nearly 70% of divorce cases were monitored for girls under the age of 16.The project seeks to hold 50 dialogue sessions with collective leaders during the year 2020, at a rate of 10 people in each session, and then to conduct 50 dialogue sessions with young men during 2022, at a rate of 10 young men and women in each session, within the remote villages, and then the establishment of training courses for a group of 100 men and women From the targeted villages to be able to run such sessions in their villages and raise awareness of the various dangers, including early marriage, and then conduct surveys by the participating youth within their villages to find out the benefits, impact and feedback.
