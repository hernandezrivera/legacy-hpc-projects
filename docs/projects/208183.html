Project Summary

The project seeks to provide a holistic protection response to IDPs, Returnees and Host Communities in northern Mozambique in response to the insurgency in the region. The project will also provide integrated protection services (including GBV and Child Protection ). The project seeks to provide GBV case management as welll as provision of mental health and psychosocial support services to GBV Survivors, including awareness raising on GBV, PSEA as well as promotion of accountability to affected persons. Capacity building on GBV, referral pathways (local level as well as the hotline), community mobilisation, positive masculinity, as well as in use of various modalities will be used. Women and Girls' Safe Spaces will be established and strengthened to ensure an integrated interactive intervention contributing to social cohesion among internally displaced people, returnees and host communities. The WGSS will provide a comfortable environment for women and girls to express themselves without fear of judgment or harm. Protection Focal Points will be identified and trained to provide first aid MHPSS to GBV Survivors as well as in case identification and referrals. The project will create a comprehensive model for GBV responses, incorporating rapid assessments, simplified referral pathways for hard-to-reach areas, community mapping, and orientation for GBV community volunteers (activists) as well as Protection Focal Points.
In terms of Child Protection, the project seeks to provide child protection services to unaccompanied and separated children, including identification and provision of foster care for the children, while at the same time providing mental health and psychosocial support services to traumatised children due to natural disasters as well as due to the insurgency in northern mozambique. Family tracing and reconciliation will also be prioritised as most children get lost and/or lose contact with their parents and/or guardians as they flee from man-made as well as natural disasters. Child Friendly Spaces will be set up, including recreational parks for the provision of MHPSS as well as for case identification. Integrated case management including filing and documentation will be prioritised.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

AAMoz has a dedicated team responsible for security and monitoring the security situation to minimize security risk. The team is guided by the organisation’s Safety and Security Plan. AAMoz also trains and works with general Protection Focal Points who also act as watchdogs in the communities where we work and give intelligence on any security risks that may arise. Being part of the cluster system, AAMoz also relies on regular updates shared by OCHA. AAMoz also follows the Do No Harm Principles in all its interventions

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The project aims at raising the capacity of women, men, girls and boys as well as the elderly to understand the tenets of GBV, Child Protection and General Protection. By bringing together IDPs, Returnees and Host Communities, the project contributes significantly to social cohesion, tolerance and peacebuilding and the activities in the WGSS including cookery, tailoring and others which will be communally agreed on will contribute to revenue generation which will contribute to the sustainability of the interventions

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The project is sensitive to Persona with Special Needs and will endeavour to include them in all responses, from design, implementation to monitoring.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project will continue to conserve the environment, avoiding pollution and making use of interventions and strategies that guarantee safe use and maintenance of damage free activities including but not limited to reduction in sue of paper, not using plastic cups and bags, and ensuring use of biodegradable material whenever necessary.
