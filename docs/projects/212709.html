Project Summary

According to HNO 2024, The multifaceted crisis in Syria creates continuous cycles of vulnerability, disproportionately impacting those most in need, including individuals with disabilities. People are deprived of basic services, which makes them more vulnerable and puts strain on social cohesion. Without urgent and sustained funding, essential services like water, sanitation, health care, electricity, and education will continue to deteriorate , pushing more people into desperate need, and increasing the risk to resort to negative coping mechanisms. Investing in essential basic services now is essential to pave the way for a more durable response and prevent an even larger crisis from unfolding.
Based on the priorities identified by the Education and protection clusters and the findings of the needs assessments conducted during the last period, Sened seeks to provide education and life-saving protection services in 4 locations as follows: 2 in Shamarekh camp & 1 in Yazi Bagh, and 1 in Al Iman Annex Camp while CP will be provided in 1 location in A'zaz city as per the following components:
1- Sened will support 4 schools with formal & non-formal education, student kits, schools rehabilitation, pre-fab classes, PSS, and incentives for education personnel providing non-formal education. Sened will also support 4 schools with fuel for heating and stoves as follows: Abdullah Al-Saleh school in Al Iman annex Camp, Al Shuhadaa School in Shamarekh Camp, Al Sanabel School in Yazi Bagh Camp, and Al-Qadisiya School in Shamarekh Camp. The intervention aims at increasing the access of children with and without disabilities to formal education and minimizing the risks of dropping out. Sened has already signed a protocol with the Education Directorate of Kilis and A’zaz to support schools in A’zaz District.
2- CB for Education personnel: Sened takes into account building the capacities of educational personnel who can play a vital part in providing qualitative lessons to students attending formal education at schools. The training entitled “Class Room Management & Active Learning”. 3 more pieces of training will be provided to Edu staff as well including: Safeguarding, Gender Equality, and How to deal with persons with disabilities. BTL Campaigns will take place in 4 locations to reach drop-out children.
3- Rehabilitation of 4 schools: Light repair in classrooms, common spaces, and WASH facilities in line with Sphere Minimum Standards will take place as per a needs assessment and the necessity of continuing formal education.
4- Sened will provide case management, referral pathways, and emergency case fund to children with and without disabilities in 1 pre-existed CFS in A’zaz due to the lack of such support there and Sened would like to provide additional interventions including SNR and parenting sessions to persons with and without disabilities.
5- Sened will also provide WaSH support to 4 schools in camps including water trucking, maintenance, and special toilets for persons with disabilities.
Sened will assign 2 focal persons in the field to identify and report violations related to PSEA & Safeguarding to Sened's focal person based in Turkey. Moreover, Sened will provide Edu personnel with pieces of training to enhance their knowledge about identifying protection risks including abuse, exploitation...etc.
The project activities will be implemented by Sened's field team under the supervision of Sened's staff based in Turkey. For each cluster, there will be an officer to supervise the implementation and report to project coordinator in Turkey.

The intervention aims at increasing the access of 6168 children with and without disabilities to formal education and minimizing the risks of dropping out. 10 % of persons with disabilities will be reached by this project. Moreover, Sened will provide Edu personnel with pieces of training to enhance their knowledge about identifying protection risks including abuse, exploitation...etc. The overall target for Education activities will be as follows: 6371 (2937 boys, 3231 girls, 127 men, and 76 women) while 8200 individuals (2500 boys, 2500 girls, 1500 men, and 1700 women) will receive protection services. Protection risks: Risk1: risk of sexual exploitation by humanitarian workers (risk of harassment) of women and girls. Likelihood: High - Impact: HighM: Sened will establish a confidential complaint mechanism and train staff on code of conduct.Risk 2: increase risks of GBV if latrines are not separated or there are no locks on the doors. Likelihood: High - Impact: HighM: Sened's Case Manager (female staff) will consult with women and girls on location of latrines and ensure that locks are places on the doors. PSEA Strategy: Sened gives high priority to the prevention of any kind of abuse and exploitation through providing PSEA awareness to staff and project participants, disseminating posters, and providing confidential complaint channel for PSEA and safeguarding incidents to be addressed within 24 to 48 hours.
