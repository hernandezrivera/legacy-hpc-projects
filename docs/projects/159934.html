Project Summary

Access to adequate health services, particularly for women and children, is extremely restricted by social barriers, poor infrastructure, poor service quality, high levels of insecurity, and a limited number of health facilities and trained health workers. Relative security in Luuq and Garbaharey towns, provides an opportunity to significantly improve health services in both districts. This proposal specifically focuses on the urgent health care needs existing in Garbaharey and Luuq districts (Northern side), to be implemented over one year. The project targets 32,048 (16,280 female, 15,768 male) beneficiaries from both host and IDP communities with healthcare services out of which 1,602 beneficiaries are people with disabilities. This project will directly contribute to the Health cluster objectives of reducing excess morbidity due to diseases outbreak, contributing to improvement in physical and mental wellbeing of the affected population, and improving case management and referral services for GBV survivors. It is also aligned to the Ministry of Health (MoH) strategic plan Objective 1 and 4 which focuses on improving the health of the Somali population with a special focus on maternal and child health and the cluster objectives. Towards this end, Trocaire will work jointly with DHBs to improve the capacity of staff and health facilities, including mobile clinics to provide essential healthcare. This will include building the capacity of DHBs and MoH to provide these services fully in the near future, equipment of health facilities, widening the reach by breaking geographical, infrastructural, cultural and economic barriers to access to health, investing in prevention measures through vaccination exercises and community awareness raising, and prepositioning of supplies in anticipation for crisis.
The intended impact of the programme is to: contribute towards the improved health status of the Somali population, particularly women of childbearing age and children under-five, through the provision of health care services at the targeted facilities in Garbaharey and Luuq Districts.
The programme builds on Trócaire’s 27 years of experience operating in Gedo and will work closely with the Ministry of Health and the community, through community structures- District Health Boards (DHBs)-, to provide quality healthcare services that meet MoH and Health cluster standards. The DHBs are community-based governance structures that have proven to be an effective and sustainable model in the provision of healthcare services in the region. This programme complements Trócaire’s existing programme delivering the EPHS in three districts (Belet Xaawo, Luuq, Garbaharey and Dollow) of Gedo.

Project Activities

 Train the 12 Health Workers (Midwives) on Emergency Obstetric care by July 2020. Facilitate the referrals from BEMOC to CEMOC on need based. Equip 4 Antenatal and Maternity units. Recruit Health workers for the 4 Facilities (4 Health Centres-2 Garbaharey and 2 Luuq). Procure Equipment and Furniture for the 4 Facilities. Minor rehabilitation of 4 outpatient departments in the supported health facilities Distribute vaccines to the 4 supported health facilities on quarterly basis Support the Measles and Polio campaign. Train 12 Health workers on EPI planning, forecast and tools for immunization. Procure IEC materials for the 4 supported health facilities. Train the 12 Health workers and DHMTs on sentinel reporting and response mechanisms. Procure AWD response kits for the 4 facilities. Develop Emergency Preparedness plan for the 2 Districts.

Project Indicators

 Number of trained Health Workers (Midwives) on Emergency Obstetric care.  At least 60% of the facilities fully equipped to offer BEMOC services. Increased number of deliveries by Skilled Birth attendants from 36% to 48%. Number of health care facilities strengthened to provide health care service from baseline of 0 to 4 Number of consultations per person per year increased by 20%.  Percentage of Luuq and Garbaharey IDPs who have access to health care services from baseline of 45 to a target of 90%,  Percentage of Penta 3 coverage for children of under 1 year from baseline 42%-80% Number of children from 9-15 months immunized against measles in targeted areas increased by 40% Number of Health workers trained on EPI planning, forecast and tools for immunization increased from 3 to 8. Number of Health facilities with an updated IEC materials on immunization increased to 4. AWD case fatalities reduced from 2% to 1%. Number of health workers, DHB and DHMT trained on sentinel report and response increased from 3 to 12. Number of emergency preparedness planned developed 0 to 2
