Project Summary

Life and Hope Initiatives (LHI) Will intervene in providing an access to quality and alternative Education to the vulnerable and out of school children, which they have been deny access to Education as a result of the activities of insurgents. LHI after conducting needs assessment in Gwoza in respect to Education, there is need to bring back children that have lost their access to universal and quality Education. Needs identified include: 1. Lack of infrastructure ( the basic needs That were destroyed by insurgents) 2. Lack of teaching and learning materials was a major factor contributing to poor attendance and retention of learners in schools. (scholastic materials which embedded teaching and learning materials) 3. skills acquisitions 4. school furniture and WASH facilities was also recorded as a critical need. among others. LHI will be targeting 5000 vulnerable and out of school children.

Needs

An estimated 1.8 million people are displaced in Borno, Adamawa and Yobe and around 40 percent (0.8 million) of that population is comprised of school aged children between three and seventeen years displaced across the three states. While the number of displaced persons and returnees increased compared to the previous year, the total number of children and education personnel in need decreased from 2.9 million for 2018 to 2.2 million for 2019 based on education sector estimates (EiEWG HNO, 2019).Recent data1 on the key education indices in the NE still indicate a dire situation in education with Borno is reported in the same survey (MICS 2016-17) as having the highest number of girls out of school in Nigeria at 71%. Education sector faces an upward task of infrastructural rehabilitation and expansion, education management stretching and the need to address emerging education needs such as skills acquisition and protection, there is also needs to introduce the children(youths age 12-17) to 1. Entrepreneurial concept, 2. there is need of social and emotional learning to cope with the negative effect of conflict. 3. there is need of involving religious and community leaders to help in peace building. 4. needs of skills acquisition, 5. there is need for scholastic materials, 6. there is need to support care givers which as a result of poverty they could not carter for the school of there children. 6. there is need for dignity kits

Activities

LHI with the help of community coalitions will get access to community as they have used them during needs assessment conducted earlier. project will address the issue of infrastructure by constructing temporary learning center and rehabilitation of existing structure destroyed by the insurgents. will also address the needs of entrepreneurial concept by teaching the youths among the children age range from 12-17 years by using a market II concept of USAID to help them fit into the society through that they will rebuilding themselves in addressing the need of advocacy to the community and religious leaders in inculcating the norms of good character and behavior to the populace (youth 12-17 years) we will create a good society free of violence if the fear of God is preach and teach to them. introduction of skills acquisition will address the need of dependency of our girls and boys on their parents, they will be teach on different skills acquisition, which it encompass so many things to learn. to address the issue of scholastic materials,
