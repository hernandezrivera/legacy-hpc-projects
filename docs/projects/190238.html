Project Summary

Inter-communal conflict between Gumuz and Oromo in 2018 displaced many from their place of residence in Kamashi and along boarder areas of East and West Wellega zones. After Government launched IDP return process in 2019, multiple displacements has been reported due to fear and protection issues in the place of return. In 2021, a renewed conflict in Kamashi of Benshanul Gumuz and within Wellega zones resulted in new crisis displacing thousands from their place of origin.
As of today, the IDPSsituation remains dramatic and the security condition remains highly volatile due to expanding movements of UAGs within Oromia and BGR, resulting in continuous displacements and difficulties of implementation to partner's projects in the area. According to the data received from these zones, more than 200,000people in Wellegas and 100,000 people in Kamashi zone are displaced, as of November 202
In East wollega there are more than 179,299 IDP's, In West wollega Zone there are more than 66,734 IDP's and in Kamashi Zone BGR there are 102,254 IDP's are displaced from their home. People displaced by the violence are seeking immediate shelter, food and non-food assistance. Humanitarian partners are monitoring the situation, supporting and working with the Government in assessing the needs and providing assistance. Regarding the shelter response, the majority of the newly displaced IDP populations in East Wollega are currently living in collective centers. They are currently living in tent or temporary shelter.

In West Wollega and Kamashi zone most of the IDP's are currently living with host communities while sharing a single house with multiple families in overcrowded situations. Some IDPs are living in rental houses in urban areas. To address the emergency needs of the vulnerable displaced people, this project will provide shelter assistance through the distribution of ESCRI for targeted very vulnerable IDP households and construction of Durable shelter. ANE willalso support woreda disaster and risk management office (DRMO) by conducting capacity building Trainings, West and East wellega zones of Oromia region, and Kamashi Zone of BGR. In addition, ANE will improve health service for IDPs and hosting community by rehabilitating heath posts which are mostly used by IDP communities
