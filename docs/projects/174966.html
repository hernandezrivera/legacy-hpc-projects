Project Summary

The COVID -19 Pandemic has rattled the Global village like never before with bringing disruptions in all walks of life ranging from Health systems to economic development of the world. It is for the first time that a pandemic like COVID-19 has really slowed the prospects of human development specially economically which brought the people on the brink due to lost work as the world resorted to lockdowns in order to contain this Pandemic. Weak and Vulnerable segments of the society were exposed to all sorts of dangers and many problems, Among the vulnerable group Women and Children are the most effected especially in the under-developed countries which were already struggling to cope with economy problems. Pakistan is also no exception to this problem and particularly Balochistan Province which is the least developed of all having the lowest literacy rate has lot of room for more work to be done on all sectors exclusively in the women empowerment and protection.
Taraqee Foundation (TF) is a Balochistan based National NGO working for the poverty alleviation and social development for the past twenty-six years now. TF’s vision is “Prosperous, enlightened and just society”. Its mission is “To develop and empower our deprived and vulnerable people by providing services addressing basic needs of life, responding to calamities and by promoting social justice”. TF is registered under companies’ ordinance of 1984 and has got its MoU with EAD1renewed under Governments new NGO policy.
TF has worked in every nook and corner of Balochistan and has also worked in some districts in Sindh, KPK and Azad Jammu Kashmir as well. TF has its deep roots in communities in Balochistan where it has its local staff, who speak the same language and are a part of the same culture but well equipped with social development knowledge, skills and motivation for change.
With a backdrop of high multi-dimensional poverty (71%) low HDI (0.477) Balochistan paints a bleak picture of Gender equality situation. Gender based violence is widely prevalent and highly under reported in the province mainly because it is socially tolerated and is even accepted to a large extent especially in rural communities. Women’s access to formal justice system remains very low and stigmatized. Lack of awareness on what constitutes Gender Based Violence (GBV) as well as knowledge about available formal redressal mechanisms is a part of the problem. Identification of GBV prevalence is still a challenge in remote rural communities . Law enforcement is also bifurcated into police and levies areas, which further complicates the referral pathways. The proposed project is designed to address immediate GBV issues and empower women to take a stand against violence and rebuild their lives in through support of livelihood opportunities in the targeted districts

1- Project Activities

The project is designed with an integrated approach covering multidimensional intervention; providing livelihood opportunities through skills development trainings, marketing and linkages development, educating and raising awareness among men and women towards SGBV prevention and response, psycho-social counselling services, basic literacy and numeracy, and health and hygiene.. It is planned that TF will target 600 females (200 from each district) by imparting skills related training on Finishing & Design, Group Management and Business Marketing.Main Activities:Formation of District GBV Advocacy Groups:TF will form 01 DGBVAGs (Half male and Half Female) each having 75 members in each of the targeted districts of Balochistan, who will comprise of local community members, These members will also be provided training on GBV and CRC so that they are also equipped with the knowledge identify protection issues and respond to them effectivelyReferral Partners Meetings /Referral Mechanism:TF will engage with all stakeholders (Doctors, Police and Legal support) of the targeted areas whereby strengthening the referral mechanism to which the relevant cases will be referred ensuring the confidentiality of survivors for speedy support. along with holding Quarterly meetings at district level by supporting GBV survivors for their access to timely live saving support and servicesPsycho Social Support:TF will hire psycho-social counselors for the support of GBV and SGBV survivors by establishing a helpline and also on site counselling services.Skills Training as Per TTB criteria: TF will identify and train the 600 vulnerable women in the targeted districts who will be enrolled for 06 months training covering the modules of 1)Finishing and Designing 2) Group Management 3) Business & Marketing. 02 groups of 300 women (A batch of 200 from each district) will be trained on 06 monthly course with pass percentage of 70% and after completion of course, awards ceremony will take where the beneficiaries will be provided tool kits and Certificates issued by Trade Testing Board Govt of BalochistanTraining GBV and Convention of the Child Rights (CRC):TF will provided One Day training to 225 Community members, 225 Doctors (Male& Female) and 225 police officials (Male & Female) from the targeted districts on the Topics of Gender Based Violence and Child Rights Health & Hygiene Session:Beneficiaries will be provided H&H sessions along with Basic Numeracy/Literacy classes will be given on daily basis keeping in view COVID-19 context its very essential and literacy classes will empower women on business related activities for better understandingGBV Sessions for Beneficiaries:GBV sessions will be given to beneficiaries in order to raise there awareness to better respond to GBV Focused Group Discussions: FGDs will be conducted with Male Counter Parts of beneficiaries in order to sensitize them on GBV and indirectly provide support to vulnerable women and Children also highlighting importance of this interventionChild Care Centers and Nutritional Support: Child CCs will be established at each center for the children of mothers with learning material and provision of Nutritional eatables on daily basis to 600 children.Exhibitions: Exhibitions will be planned to showcase beneficiary products for maximum coverage and encouragement, also beneficiaries will be encouraged to take part in exhibitions being organized for womenDistrict GBV Advocacy Group Press Conferences:These press conferences will arranged with participation of important figures whereby Women issues will be highlighted and masses will be sensitized, this activity will further boost and help advocate the cause of women empowerment during the current Pandemic.Legal Aid For Beneficiaries/Survivors:Project beneficiaries along with GBV Survivors will be provided with Legal Aid by a dedicated lawyer who will be the member TF project team

2 - Project Indicators

The project aims to address the issue with a three prong approach: 1) it will adopt a community-based approach for protection against GBV where community groups comprising of notable community members, community human rights defenders and community leaders will be formed. Women leaders of the community groups will not only facilitate identification and reporting of GBV cases but also advocate in their communities against domestic violence, sexual harassment, child marriage and cultural forms of GBV;2) the project will ensure that lifesaving livelihood support is extended to the GBV survivors keeping gender equality and social/disability inclusion in leaving no one behind (LNOB) at the heart of it; 3)GBV survivors will be facilitated through well managed referral mechanism to service providers including Doctors, Police and Legal support.The project envisages to address immediate lifesaving needs of GBV survivors in populations in COVID 19-affected areas and provide livelihood support to help them rebuild their lives through identification, referral and redressal mechanisms using community-based approaches.Project Goals:Communities are mobilized to address Gender base violence and empower GBV survivors, also Self-reliance and improved livelihood opportunities.
