Project Summary

Displacement, insecurity and the economic downturn have compounded the impact of historically low coverage of water infrastructure. The economic downturn has exacerbated the disruption of water services, limiting resources available for the maintenance of wells and boreholes with accompany with limited knowledge on health promotions messages. In areas hosting displaced people, above-capacity demand results in water points being unable to supply enough drinking water to the people, with the situation worsening when additional people are displaced due to flooding or insecurity. Recurring multi-faceted socio-economic impact on people, including severe economic contractions, soaring in prices of basic commodities, loss of livelihoods particularly in rural areas, increased protection risks, and disrupted access to basic services.
The IAWASH project will respond to the WASH Cluster Sectoral Objective 2: Contribute to reducing WASH related Morbidity and mortality, and WASH Sectoral Objective 3: Provide access to basic WASH services in underserved locations affected by disasters. This will be done through drilling of new boreholes, rehabilitations of nonfunctional boreholes, health promotions, use of CLTS approach to mobilized communities for household latrines constriction. The targeted area is Tonj North County, Warrap State – an area prone to flooding water scarcity due to extended dryness, disease outbreak, and displacement of people due to communal conflict. The targeted County falls under WASH Severity Classification 3 and Priority 3 Area for the HRP intersectoral prioritization and may jump from Priority 3 to Priority 2 due to the unstable context of the County with a high number of people in need of WASH services. According to the IRNA conducted in September 2023 in which ZOA-Dorcas South Sudan took part, it was furthermore found that Awul Payam in Tonj North County which this project received a number of returnee Households.
According to latest IRNA conducted in August 2022 in Tonj North County , the following recommendations were made for WASH specific.
• Repair the damaged hand pumps in the accessible area, when the water recedes.
• Distribute aqua tabs and water PUR
• Chlorination of hand pumps and water yards.
• Distribute Jerry cans to the displaced people.
• Start awareness raising and hygiene promotion messaging.
• Distribute sanitary kits to girls in schools and the displaced women.
• Construction of emergency pit latrines at the displacement sites.
• Distribute plastic slabs for communal latrines in public places like markets, health facilities and schools
• Distribution of hygiene kits/sanitary pads for adolescent girls and women of childbearing age
• Training women and girls on menstrual hygiene management.
• General soap distribution to flood affected population.
• Hygiene promotion campaigns in public places (schools, health centers and markets) and door to door hygiene awareness.
The proposed project will target 20,000 people through WASH interventions in Awul & Manlor Payam’s of Tonj North County, supporting with host communities and IDPs affected by internal communal conflicts within the county.
Under WASH Cluster Sectoral Objective 2, the following activities will be conducted: Organized public hygiene and sanitation Promotions campaigns, CLTS triggering, training of Community village sanitation committee ,Formation and training of Community hygiene volunteers (CHVs).
Under WASH cluster Sectoral Objective 3, The following activities will be conducted, drilling of new hand pumps, repairs and rehabilitations of nonfunctional hand pumps, water quality testing, formations and training of water management committee (WMCs).

1. Needs

According to the latest Interagency Rapid Need Assessment (IRNA) conducted in September 2022 in which ZOA-Dorcas took part, an estimated 15,700 households (or 94,200) people have been affected by the flooding and conflicts during 2022. The IPC projection of 2023 indicates that half of Tonj North population will face IPC3 (crises), with some pockets of IPC4 (emergence) during the lean season of 2024, between April and July 2024. With continued flooding and intercommunal fighting, the needs are high. Prior to the IRNA, On 7th August 2023, the South Sudan joint Response (SSJR) Partners for Warrap and Western Bahr El Ghazal States including ZOA Dorcas South Sudan, Mary Help Association (MHA) and Help a Child conducted internal need assessment with local authorities at Payam’s and County level of Tonj North. It was found that about 469 households were displaced by both communal conflicts and flooding at the Toch area of Manlor (Eastern part) boarding Pagol Payam in Parisika which is one of the boma’s in Manlor Payam. Partners identified high needs of food and access to safe drinking water for human consumption for both hosts and IDPs displaced by communal conflict and flooding. Women and girls travel long distances to fetch water in swampy areas where they are exposed to dangers including snake bite sand risk of harm including sexual and gender-based violence.Furthermore, it was found that WASH infrastructures (notably hand pumps) were destroyed by flooding and communal conflicts leaving a shocking total of 380 hand pumps unfunctional in two Targeted Payam’s (Manlor and Awul) in Tonj North County according to County WASH department and IRNA Conducted in September 2022. Further, there’s a poor sanitation and hygiene including protection awareness in the communities, notably sensitive to special needs of girls and women men. Persons with disabilities and elderly face more challenges due to lack water resources and inability to move long distance distances. Therefore, the project will focus on rehabilitation and maintenance of broken boreholes and drilling of new boreholes, form and train management committees to manage water resources, conduct water quality testing and conduct community hygiene awareness campaigns and use of CLTS approach to mobilize the communities for household latrines construction at the grassroot level in the villages to reduced WASH related diseases.

2. Project activities

In the project areas targeted, ZOA Dorcas will conduct internal needs assessments for the sites that need new boreholes drilling in all the Bomas of Awul and Manlor Payams. Final decisions will be taken to prioritize the villages in needs according to population size, and community willingness to maintain structures and lack of access to other optional water sources in the communities. The team will also conduct technical boreholes assessment to determine the villages for repairs and rehabilitations in the communities with high needs. The following activities will be carryout by ZD South Sudan to achieved Objectives of the project: 1. New drilling of boreholes (15 hand pumps in 2 Payams) 2. Rehabilitation and repairs of boreholes (25 boreholes) 3. Hygiene and sanitation promotion campaigns (6 campaigns) 4. Training of water management committee (WMCs) 400 members 5. Training of Community hygiene promoters (CHVs) 240 members 6. Training of hand pump mechanics (30 members) 7. Supply of bicycle for hand pump mechanics (15 bicycles) 8. Provision of repairs tool kits for boreholes (6 kits) 9. Provision of dignity kits for adolescent girls in schools (500 kits) 10. Conduct water testing for chemical and bacteriological tests (40 boreholes) 11. Setting up of handwashing stations in public markets (7 stations) 12. Organize experience exchange visit among WASH Cos 13. Conducted Safety audits for WASH facilities under this project in two Payams of Tonj North County. 14 Rehabilitation of Institutional Latrines at the Nutrition sites and schools
