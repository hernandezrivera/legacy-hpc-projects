Project Summary

Pak Women will implement the proposed project in selected areas of Tribal district South Waziristan after consultation with and approval of education department at South Waziristan level and also approval of education cluster. Pak Women will work directly with selected and approved government girls primary schools and government girls’ secondary schools.
The main goal of the intervention is to “Provide gender sensitive education services and support cluster coordination activities in emergencies to ensure that children, particularly girls have access to quality education opportunities”.
The strategic objectives of the project are;
1. 20 temporary learning centers (TLCs) for girls have been established in areas of return in tribal district of South Waziristan.
2. 5000 girls has been enrolled and retained in government schools and TLCs through cash stipends, accelerated learning programs and recreational activities in areas of return in tribal district South Waziristan.
3. School management committees are reactivated and their capacities are built.
4. DRR and mine risk education programs successfully completed at community and schools level.

Main activities will include;
1. Coordination meetings with education department.
2. Broad based community meetings.
3. Mapping of out of schools girls of age bracket 03 to 18 years.
4. Counseling sessions with families of out of schools girls.
5. Establishment of safe, child and adolescent girls’ friendly temporary learning centers (TLCs)
6. Enrolment and retention of 5000 girls of age bracket during year 2019.
7. Provision of recreation materials for psychosocial support to raise children’s self-esteem, reinforce resilience and provide an opportunity for self-expression, including early child development materials;
8. Cash stipend/incentive to encourage enrolment and retention of girl children at primary and secondary level.
9. Coordination with government for provision of textbooks.
10. Provision of school supplies (school-in-a-box), recreation kits, school bags and stationery.
11. Hiring of teachers on contract basis to reopen targeted number of closed schools.
12. Formation of school management committees for improved school-community linkages.
13. Capacity building trainings for school management committees.
14. Accelerated Learning Programme for over-age dropped out children.
15. DRR program for improved school safety at school level.
16. Mine-risk education sessions at schools and community level.
Pak Women will keep strong coordination with tribal district South Waziristan administration and education department and will regularly attend district level monthly meetings of development and government partners, will share progress and issues if any and will seek solutions.
Pak Women will also attend regularly education cluster monthly meetings and will share progress and issues if any and solution applied at tribal district level.
Pak Women is working in partnership with Takra Qabaili Khwendy (TQK/ Brave Tribal Sisters), with support of TQK, Pak Women will form community based “Women Organizations (WOs)”, with proper structure and defined roles and responsibilities, WOs will be responsible for mapping of out of schools girls in their respective villages, will coordinate and make follow up with parents for enrolment of girls in primary schools, secondary schools or temporary learning centers, which the case may be. WOs will also be responsible for making follow up with schools management committees and head teachers for retention of girls. Pak Women in partnership with TQK will also build capacities of WOs in importance of education, relevant international instruments and national and provincial laws etc. Also will build their capacities in motivational and communication skills. One literate young woman will appoint as secretary of the WO to keep record and submit reports.

Needs

Between 2017 and 2018, some 60,121 families have returned to their areas of origin in five tribal districts of KP (UNICEF Fact Sheet, October 2018). This translates into a population of 162,326 children in age-bracket of 3-18. The erstwhile FATA EMIS report for 2016-17 shows that more than one million (1,038,933) children in the age group of 04-14 are out of school in the tribal areas. The overall drop-out rate in Government Primary Schools over a cycle of 6 years is 76% (74% Boys, 80% Girls). Per the FATA Vulnerability Assessment (August-September 2017) report around half of the respondents expressed dissatisfaction with the quality of education due to lack of basic facilities, lack of teachers, teaching materials and damage to school infrastructure. In terms of damaged infrastructure, the RRU data shows that 943 educational institutions were partially and fully damaged in five tribal districts. Beside providing a safe and secured environment by rehabilitating damaged institutions, there is a need to initiate conditional cash transfer schemes to enroll and retain children, particularly girl students in schools. The experiences shared from field underlined the need for ensuring provision of water, and sanitation facilities in schools. It was noted that there is a need for initiating a fast track or second-chance condensed education programme to support and mainstream over-age children, particularly girls, complete their primary/secondary education. The reported landmines/UXOs in and around schools in some areas prompt the need for a specific mine-risk education programme to protect children and communities from any harm. Also there is grave need for DRR education and exercises in schools. The proposed project will fill the gap of lack of education institution due to damages through establishment of temporary learning centers for girls, Pak Women will enroll and retain out of schools girls in age group of 03 to 18 years, the project will also provide missing facilities, recreational activities, sports kits and stationary to new enrolled girls and schools. The project will organized mine and DRR education programs at schools and community level.

Activities

1. Coordination meetings with education department: Series of coordination meetings will be organized with district education department to gain their trust and confidence over the intervention, as well to avoid duplication of activities. Besides getting of NOC from FDMA and district administration, will also seek NOC from education department to avoid delays in implementation.2. Broad based community meetings: Pak Women will organize broad based community meetings both with men and women to get their support and cooperation during the project implementation.3. Formation of Women Organizations (WOs): Pak Women with support of TQK will form village level WOs, will build capacities of WOs in motivational and communication skills and report writing. 4. Mapping of out of schools girls of age bracket 03 to 18 years: WOs will be responsible for mapping of out of school girls of age bracket 03-18 in their respective villages, total 5000 to 7000 girls will be mapped out.5. Counseling sessions with families of out of schools girls: WOs will also be responsible for counseling with families, enrolment and retention of out of school girls in government primary and secondary schools as well as in TLCs. 6. Establishment of safe, child and adolescent girls’ friendly temporary learning centers (TLCs): 20 TLCs will be established, local teachers will be hired and basic supplies and facilities will be provided.7. Enrolment and retention of 5000 girls of age bracket 03-28 during year 2019: WOs will be responsible for enrolment and retention of 5000 girls, Pak Women and TQK will conduct supervision and monitoring during the process.8. Cash stipend/incentive to encourage enrolment and retention of girl children at primary and secondary level: Pak Women will provide cash stipend to all enrolled 5000 girls to ensure their retention as per rate decided by funding agency. Cash transfer methodology of the funding agency will be followed.9. Coordination with government for provision of textbooks and examination of girls students in TLCs: Pak Women will closely coordinate with district education department for provision of textbooks to girls enrolled in government schools as well as to girls in TLCs, Pak Women will also coordinate with education department for examination of girls students at TLCs.10. Provision of school supplies (school-in-a-box), recreation kits, school bags and stationery: Pak Women will provide supplies, recreation kits, school bags and stationery to 100 schools as well as 20 TLCs.11. Hiring of teachers on contract basis to reopen targeted number of closed schools: Pak Women in coordination with education department will identify girls primary and secondary schools and will hire teachers for the schools on contract basis to reopen targeted number of schools.12. Formation/ Reactivation of school management committees for improved school-community linkages: Pak Women will form/reactivate school management committees for improved school-community linkages, enrolment and retention of out of schools girls etc., total 100 schools will be targeted for this intervention.13. Capacity building trainings for school management committees: Pak Women will organize capacity building trainings for school management committees on international instruments and national/ provincial laws on free and compulsory education, Article 25 A Right to Education of constitution of Pakistan and importance of girls education.14. Accelerated Learning Programme for over-age dropped out children: Special module will be developed and approved from education department and will ensure primary level examination of 200 girls under one year program.15. DRR program for improved school safety at school level: Education sessions on DRR for 5000 girls students will be organized16. Mine-risk education sessions at schools and community level: 100 Mine risk education sessions will be organized for 1000 girls students, 500 women and 500 men from community.
