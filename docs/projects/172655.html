Project Summary

The general objective of this initiative is to improve the health of the most vulnerable populations in Venezuela - women of reproductive age, children and adolescents, including indigenous populations and returned migrants - through improved access to essential health services and beneficial interventions throughout the life course, with an emphasis on sexual and reproductive health and nutrition, and strengthening primary health care - PHC - in 5 border states and prioritized communities. The specific objectives of the initiative include:
• Promote access to beneficial interventions throughout the life course with an emphasis on sexual and reproductive health, and nutrition in the communities of the prioritized sites in each state.
• Improve the capacities and competencies of the basic health teams expanded at the first level of care, for the implementation of beneficial interventions throughout the life course with emphasis on sexual and reproductive health, and on nutrition in the communities of the prioritized sites in each state.
• Strengthen information systems on women's health, perinatal, and nutrition that allow the analysis of the health situation, monitoring and evaluation of the implemented interventions. The central strategy is the "Comprehensive community health approach" in women of reproductive age, children and adolescents in vulnerable conditions, with emphasis on sexual health, reproductive health and nutrition, from a life course perspective and strengthening the PHC, through the improvement of the institutional response at the first level of care and community participation.
To do so, the project comtemplates four main components:
• Strengthening the management and implementation of key interventions in the different stages of the life course, including: o Improvement of the coordination of actions between the community levels (which includes the first level of care) and the institutional health level (levels 2 and 3).
o Provision of supplies, medicines, equipment, among other essential or key items for the implementation of beneficial interventions throughout the life course.

• Improving the skills and knowledge of basic health teams expanded at the first level of care through the continuous training and health human resources. Training is a necessary condition for the implementation of these life-saving interventions, especially in the current context of massive migration of qualified health professionals.
• Improving and strengthening of information systems in women's, maternal, perinatal, childhood and adolescent health through the implementation of the Perinatal Information System (SIP) PLUS developed by CLAP-SMR / PAHO-WHO; and in nutrition through the Nutrition Information System (SIN). Articulating both systems with the Single Integrated Health System (SUIS) of the MPPS.
• Increasing the knowledge, representation and participation of the most vulnerable women of reproductive age, children and adolescents for decision-making based on rights, gender, cultural diversity and other inequities that impact access and coverage of SRH services, health maternal and nutrition; including the strengthening of coordination between relevant actors and different sectors at the national, regional and local levels
