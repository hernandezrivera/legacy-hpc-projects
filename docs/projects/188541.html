Project Summary

Preparing the community to be more efficient in terms of mental and psychological state to face the negative effects of conflicts in the region, and facing the effects/sequences of quarantine and the spread of Covied 19 on the individuals then community level
Expected results (Outputs)
PSS-1 # of community members over 15 years old received psychological and physical reinforcement and appropriate empowerment, such as sessions and entertainment activities online

4 - Needs

project will be implemented in Kafrtkharim and Dana where olive branch started working since 2016 and has ready equipped centers, personnel, stores and all the required equipments, about the war in north-western Syria, in addition to the prevalence of Covid-19, the lack of adequate intervention by humanitarian workers, the absence of support, and the weakness of services of the authorities in the region, all of these reasons were behind thousands of people of different groups suffer from many social, financial, educational, psychological struggles and that requires intervention to treat and not exacerbate them, we mention some for example: autism, shyness, fear, anxiety ... etc. The foregoing requires us to intervene to address those needs and develop what we are able to do, to ensure that the people there can continue moving forward as everywhere else and a healthy generation is established in the region to be able to manage it and direct future efforts in the right direction.of course a need assessment built on the protection clusters, HNO and OCHA reports is ready when required , but unfortunately we cant paste it here due to lack of space

5 - Implementation of activities

all the activities will be online over 4 cycles , each cycle will be as clarified below: • Activity 1: Community activities:starting from the community of Kafr Takharim to target all NWS to do activities below:1.1 Recreational activities (PSS sessions, Life skills, Art sessions, sports sessions…) (3 months-4 cycles)1.2 Sports & arts (3 months-4 cycles)Beneficiaries were taught a set of movements :1- Weight loss, a live interactive broadcast, from which the coach personally monitors the beneficiaries2- Flexibility and elongation3- Online competitions • Activity 2: Children activities:2.1A Recreational activities (PSS sessions, Life skills, Art sessions, sports sessions…) (3 months-4 cycles) 2.1B Awareness sessions (Children rights, how to report a violation, ….) (3 months-4 cycles)2.1C Caregiver's sessions: Parenting and awareness sessions, discussions, planning, training / 2 sessions monthly (3 months-4 cycles)2.2 Referral system (refer the hard to deal with cases to specialized experts) • Activity 3: GBV activities:3.1 Awareness raising of GBV / 2 sessions monthly (3 months-4 cycles)3.2 meetings to discuss/share responses, strategies, with the local community / 2 sessions monthly (3 months-4 cycles)• Activity 4: Mine Action activities4.1 Awareness sessions / 2 sessions monthly (3 months-4 cycles)4.2 Trainings (3 months-4 cycles)4.3 compose a mine action comity from the locals / 1 coordination meeting monthly (3 months-4 cycles)
