Project Summary

The aim of this project is to equip IDPs, returnees and the host communities with technical skills to improve income opportunities to in Rural Damascus, by building technical skills linked with life skills and PSS sessions to increase their well- being. This will enable people to live with dignity and create future for themselves and their families in Syria. Further the capacity of the staff will be enhanced and the schools will be light rehabilitated and equipped with needed items.

4 - Needs

The growing unemployment rate, increasing loss of livelihood opportunities and lack of regular income have led many households to resort to harmful coping mechanisms to reduce their expenses and meet their needs. Children often need to work to support their families and through movement people lost their jobs and need to seek work in the new place with often less paid opportunities. Vocational training programs and activities will support the development of vulnerable individuals, households and community member’s capacities to generate an income in order to meet their basic needs, resulting in an increased level of self-reliance, reduced dependency on assistance and exposure to protection risks. The self-reliance will be strengthened through technical and advisory support which will be provided by the team and the institute to the beneficiaries. In general according to the Comprehensive Report on Mapping of Availability, Variety and Contents of Vocational and Skills Training in Syria (UNDP 2018), the main gaps in TVET sector are: 1- Lack of clearly-defined development strategies that invest in developing national production capacities, and lack of investment in human resources; 2- Lack of coordination between recruiting and employment policies and education and investment policies. 3- Low number of labor market studies and lack of reliable statistics resulting in the absence of strategic plans for the needs of labor force and required training and skills. 4- Lack of specialized institutions that provide reliable information and data analysis with regards to the current and potential changes in supply and demand for all types of jobs, professions, and vocations. 5- Weak response of TVET to labor markets in accordance with economic and social challenges. 6- Lack of reliable and accurate statistics on labor market and types of skill sets required.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risks 1: Way to the TVET school not fully safe or secure Mitigation: Provide transportation or organize groups going together to the TVET school Risk 2: students struggle with past experience which affects their emotional wellbeing Mitigation: train teacher and facilitators in protection and PSS themes to be able to identify cases and refer them.
