Project Name

Start Date

01/04/2019

End Date

31/05/2020

Project Summary

*Permanent monitoring of service provision in Transitional Centers to make sure that IDPs have equal access to basic services irrespective of their social, physical and finananial conditions by creating local local committees;
*Coordinate the service provision with all the partners involved in humanitarian response to inform the level of needs satisfaction and gaps to better plan mitigation measures;
*Provide capacity building to government officials on CCCM (protection and assistence) of people in camps based on international minimum standards;
*Provide direct assistance and support in site planning, relocation (transportation) of families from risk areas, opening of access roads, drainages, access to basic products and services and putting up of identification signs as a critical protection measure in the resettlement sites.
