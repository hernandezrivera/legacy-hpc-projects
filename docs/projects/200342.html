Project Summary

1. To ensure that children affected by cholera outbreaks in Malawi receive adequate protection and support.
2. To prevent and mitigate the negative impact of cholera outbreaks on children's health, well-being, and development.
3. To raise awareness among communities and relevant stakeholders on the importance of child protection during cholera outbreaks.
