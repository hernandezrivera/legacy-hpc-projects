The Sudan unstable context has ruined much to the common standard persons especially the protracted and newly return population due to the unstable situation that has been occurring since the overthrowing of the 2 government at a rapid way, not forgetting the insecurity that was occurring in the year 2023 in the villages which hindered the situation of farmers to settle for cultivation , in addition to that , the un constant economical and inflation of the country is maximizing the vulnerability of the common people who are depending especially on daily sources of income and grazing , in the year 2023 a labour worker was earning 3000 SDG and spends at least 5000 SDG per day , the household used to send the whole house hold including women and children to work for a daily income which caused risk of women to violence , insecurity , children living schools in order to cop up with the standard of living , many households experienced malnutrition cases due to having single meals per day, furthermore many of the households never had fruits or vegetable for over 3-4 months which means no nutrition affordability. In the year 2023 the price of 1 bread is 100 SDGs which is not enough / satisfactory for even a child, a [Malwa] of Sorghum is 3000 SDGs, a bottle of cooking oil is 1400 SDG, a packet of salt is 1000 SDG, dry Okra the list is 2800, SDG, dry paste the list is 2000, SDGs, however the situation has and is still becoming more chronic especially with the law standard living people.
However , the much of effects has also affected the livestock practices who lost their economical basic activities such as their livestock assets during the protracted conflicts in Darfur especially, furthermore women headed families still depending on the humanitarian relief which is not a guarantee and not sustainable.
The project will provide life-saving interventions for the vulnerable IDP, returnee, and resident households in South Darfur state to prevent further losses of their productive assets and enhance their capacity to access, through crop and livestock-based livelihood interventions, sufficient nutritious food and to diversify their food basket and improve their income. Overall, the project will target 20,600 food insecure 10,400 HH in Bull bull,Katella , 10,200HH locality IDP , returnees and host community. The project aims to restore and improve the productive capacity of the targeted farmers to reduce the severity of the hunger gap and enable them to become self-reliant down into the harvest period. This will eventually support in halting the deterioration of the food and nutrition situation and preventing relapse of Malnutrition. to children and pregnant and lactating women,. AOSCD intended beneficiaries of the project.
