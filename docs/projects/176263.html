Project Summary

The action will be for a period of 12 months and will address the prevailing gaps and needs in service provision taking into account the prevailing risks such as insecurity and COVID-19. The main purpose is to contribute to the reduction of morbidity and mortality due to preventable diseases in the vulnerable populations in 3 Counties of Greater Warrap State namely Gogrial East,Gogrial West and Twic Counties through provision of integrated essential Primary and secondary Health care services in accordance with the Basic Package of Health and Nutrition services. The intervention will target the existing vulnerable populations and person's in need estimated to be about 163,445. The project will supplement and compliment service provision in 59 static Health facilities which are currently being supported by the HPF project. The project will ensure PHCUs, PHCCs and secondary health facilities deliver promotive, curative, and preventive outpatient and inpatient health services to all community members seeking services in these facilities free of charge. The main objectives of the project are:
1. To reduce excess morbidity and mortality of epidemic-prone diseases and health insecurities
2. To mprove access and utilization of integrated life-saving quality health care services to crisis-affected and vulnerable populations through static, mobile and community-based health service delivery systems
3. To increase access to services for survivors of SGBV, disabled, mental health disorders and the elderly.

1 - Needs

GOAL is currently implementing a HPF funded health and nutrition project in Lot 18 consisting of Twic, Gogrial West and Gogrial East supporting service provision 59 health facilities distributed across three the counties. Of these facilities two are referral hospitals. Influenced by environmental conditions, high levels of morbidity and a limited access to services is a major concern. World Vision International SMART survey conducted between June and August 2017 Indicated mortality rates for three counties. Under five mortality rates in Twic 1.44/10,000/day, Gogrial West 0.24/10,000/day and Gogrial East 0.26/10,000/day. In the same survey, GAM rates in Twic, Gogrial West and Gorgial East were estimated: 35.9, 26 and 18 respectively. Malnutrition underlies a large proportion of the unacceptably high levels of child morbidity and mortality in counties.The available budget and resources provided by Health Pooled Fund are hardly enough to adequately cater for and meet the immense health needs of the population. This is further compounded by the fact that these counties often face natural disasters and calamities such as flooding. In addition, the current ongoing covid-19 pandemic has also put immense pressure on the available resources and also increased both the population needs and the resource envelope required to provide adequate and quality health care services.Its against this background therefore that more resources are being sought to supplement and compliment the resources envelope currently being provided through the health pooled funding.

2 - Project activities

The project will carry out the following activities in line with the sectoral objectives: Sectoral Objective 1: Reduce excess morbidity and mortality of epidemic-prone diseases and health insecurities. Activities: Epidemic disease surveillance, prevention and control including outbreak detection, investigation and response Sub activities will include the following: • Training of CHD and health facility staff on IDSR/EWARS /HMIS • Timely Preparation and submission of IDSR /EWARS/HMIS reports • Preparation of emergency preparedness and response plans Strategic prepositioning of emergency supplies • Conducting outbreak/epidemic investigations of detected disease • Conducting routine EPI at static and outreach sites • Support mass immunization campaigns including child days and covid-19 vaccination initiative Conduct training on Immunization in Practice (IIP),cold chain management to health facility and CHD staff • Conducting community awareness and education on epidemic prone/immunizable diseases.Objective 2: Improve access and utilization of integrated life-saving quality health care services to crisis-affected and vulnerable populations through static, mobile and community-based health service delivery systems. Activities • Recruitment of Skilled Birth attendants in PHCCs and hospitals • Payment of incentives to SBAs and other essential staff • Procurement and distribution of BEMONC equipment and supplies • Training of health workers on MISP,BEMONC Diagnosis and treatment of common illnesses at PHCC/PHCUs/hospitals including non communicable diseases, clinical management of rape, metal health and psychosocial support • Conductng integrated outreaches in hard to reach areas • Training of health workers on the diagnosis and treatment of common illnesses • Procurement of essential medical supplies and equipment for health facilities • Conducting regular support supervision to health facilities• Provide treatment and support to SGBV survivors • Conducting regular IPC assessments and continuous IPC improvement in health facilities• Conducting quarterly review meetings of the project with all stakeholders including the community members • Conducting client satisfaction surveys on a quarterly basis. • Implementation of a community complaint and feedback mechanism (CFRM) at community level Conduct regular health awareness and education to the communities.• Integration TB/HIV services in the PHCU/PHCC and hospital• Strengthening of the referral systems through support to communication, transportation, referral protocols and training of health facility staff .Sectoral Objective 3: Increase access to services for survivors of SGBV, disabled, mental health disorders and the elderly. Activities here will include:• Training of health workers on MHSSP/CMR • Provision of support to SGBV survivors within 72 hours • Training of CHD on GESI • Linking SGBV survivors and MHPSS clients to appropriate service providers (Referral network)

7.4.2. How will your project provide beneficiaries information about Protection against Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA)?

GOAL has a robust policy on PSES and will use its communication mechanism to provide information through the community volunteers, project staff. This will be done during community meetings and health awareness sessions at static and outreach sites. GOAL will also use IEC materials to create awareness on PSEA

7.4.4. How will your project ensure feedback from the most vulnerable members (e.g. individuals living with disabilities, individuals who are not literate, women and girls)?

GOAL will ensure that these persons are well represented during any community engagement meetings/dialogue sessions/focus group discussions and they will be given equal chance t participate and voice out their concerns on the project.GOAL will also ensure that these group are represented and participate in project review meetings at all levels

7.4.1. How will your project provide beneficiaries information about the project, feedback mechanism and/or complaint mechanism?

GOAL has a community complain and feedback/response mechanism (CFRM) where the beneficiaries will be required to provide and file their complaints on a regular basis through their leaders, suggestion boxes at the health facilities and through community meetings/dialogue sessions. The GOAL team will collect, analyze and response to the complaints in a timely manne.

7.4.3. How will your project use the beneficiaries’ feedback to adapt project assistance?

GOAL will analyse the beneficiaries feedback and will use this to adapt assistance accordingly
