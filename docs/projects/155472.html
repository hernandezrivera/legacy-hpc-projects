Project Summary

Early recovery actions will be spearheaded in line with 2019 Flash Appeal Strategic Objective 3; which focuses on strengthening the resilience of drought-affected communities to absorb and recover from climatic and economic shocks. The vulnerable communities in Zimbabwe are most likely to succumb to hazards, shocks and stressors due to the probable upcoming El Niño. In addition, the communities’ recovery capacity is currently compromised by dual outbreak of cholera & typhoid; and the economic instability coupled with high rates of inflation depreciating household (HH) financial savings. In order to rebuild livelihood support mechanisms that might be affected by erratic climatic conditions, the project will address early recovery through Disaster Risk Reduction (DRR), creation of employment and commercialization of agricultural and Non-Timber Forest Products (NTFP) as source of alternative livelihoods and support re-establishent of markets for vulnerable households. Further the project will also strengthen the capacity of key government departments to fulfil their mandates in the provision of early recovery services. In Mbire District the project will support 3 cattle and goat pen fattening projects, support 4 community nutrition gardens and establish baobab, marula and Masawu fruit enterprises. The enterprises will be supported with machinery for value addition and these will in turn support bio-fortification school feeding programs for child nutrition. Coincidentally the project is being implemented during the harvest period for these indigenous fruits. In Victoria Falls the project seeks to harness and support the craft enterprises to yield income for the beneficiaries and also establish a recycling plant that will process waste into bales and packaging material for sale (e.g to honey, peanut butter and oil producers). In addition separated biodegradable waste will be used for worm farming by vulnerable youths supplying the lucrative Zambezi River fishing industry. In Rusape urban the project will establish 5 market gardening groups who will focus on mushroom production and water saving backyard gardening. This will include growing and value addition of the mushrooms. All the groups established will capacitated in processing, business management and marketing. Proper machinery for production will be availed and the groups will also be assisted with registration as legal enterprises so that they can venture into contracts with lucrative markets. The project will target a total of 4164 beneficiaries i.e 3457 beneficiaries in Mbire, 230 in Rusape and 477 in Victoria Falls. All established groups of enterprises will be composed of 20 people each except the nutrition gardens which will be 40. The majority of direct beneficiaries in Mbire district will be primary school children on the bio-fortified nutrition meals provided at the 5 target schools. The rest of the community members in all districts will be indirect beneficiaries who will be selling raw materials to these enterprises for processing thereby generating income at household level. This will support the sustainability of these enterprises during and after the project life.in addition, all the districts will be trained in DRR and plans will be develop to guide implementation of early recovery initiatives.

Needs

The action will support the needs of the targeted communities in the three areas to respond to the elnino effects. In Mbire district the main community needs are livelihood restoration necessitated by loss of livestock, erosion of agriculture related incomes and food stocks due to reduced agricultural production. The communities’ will need to switch to alternative sources of livelihoods. Basic needs include technical capacity, material and technological needs to pursue immediately available options from non-timber forest products (NTFPs) harvesting of baobab, marula and masau. Constraints in water supply will need to be addressed by restoring waterpoints and weir construction through community works. Material support to promote nutrition and food needs through promotion of gardens include fencing material and seed inputs where small weirs and water points are successfully re-established. The third key community need is livelihood restocking. Mbire district is faces livestock losses due to poor pastures and lack of water in drought period and hence there is need for small livestock re-stocking. In Victoria Falls urban community needs start-up capital grants/loans to support the lucrative tourism attractive crafts enterprises. The groups need to be organised and registered so that they produce quality products and provide consistent supplies to the market. The municipality lack of capacity to manage waste in the town has given rise to recycling that will bring additional income to the households. In order to create such green jobs, the communities require technical, technological (balling equipment) plant that will process waste into bales and packaging material for sale (e.g to honey, peanut butter and oil producers). In addition, biodegradable waste will require processing equipment and technical skills to produce worms for the lucrative Zambezi River fishing industry and the compost providing manure for organic gardening. In Rusape Urban the vulnerable population faced by unemployment and harsh economic conditions will need support to engage in quick return and employment creating livelihoods and start-up funding. Early recovery support through gardening and mushroom farming are potential options.

Activities

• Conducting inception/sensitization and baseline meetings in the 3 districts at district and ward levels• Beneficiary selection, economic activity establishment and project rollout • Short term vocational Trainings in processing, business management, business plans and marketing • NTFPs value chains development and support with processing machinery • Support Enterprise re/establishment through Start-up grants/loans to re/capitalize small enterprises and link to them to markets • Environmental awareness and clean-up campaigns to curb cholera and typhoid • Facilitation of access to short term and tailor-made grants support for viable NTFP small economic activities• Facilitate registration of all enterprises as business entities to allow them to venture into trading contracts with lucrative markets• Promoting ISALs (Internal Savings and Lendings schemes) as well as (Village Lendings and Savings) VSLs for all joint economic activities or CBOs• Create employment in all interventions• Establishing feeder financing schemes for livestock survival feeding and watering; and farmer price discovery in meat markets through use of android mobile apps (LIMEVA)• Facilitate the development of entrepreneur led alternative risk management structures through micro insurance – peril, index, trade credit insurance – targeting value chains. Delivered via relevant ICT platforms• Re/establish strengthening fora and platforms at district level for supporting timely, rapid, early response to El Niño by NGO and government partners who are already operating in the areas.
