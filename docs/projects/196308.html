Project Summary

Prior to intervention, Concern will conduct a need assessment in order to have a clear picture of the situation closely, the need assessment will target the vulnerable groups as well including women, elderly and people with disability, the need assessment will use three approaches including baseline survey, FGDs and KIIs. Based on this assessment, Concern will distribute 2,000 NFIs kits to 2,000 most affected IDPs families in Aleppo and Idlib governorates, the context of each kit will be based on the cluster recommendation. In addition, Concern will rehabilitate 250 damaged and/or unfinished houses in the target area, the rehabilitation will include but not limited to installation of doors, windows, and WASH facilities. The dignity and privacy of each rehabilitated houses will be ensured during the rehabilitation.

3 - Needs

After over a decade of violent conflict, humanitarian needs across Syria remain severe with the continued need for emergency humanitarian assistance to meet the needs of over 5.5 million in need. In response to these needs, Concern will distribute 2000 NFIs kits targeting the IDPs with most severity in Aleppo and Idlib governorates, and will rehabilitate 250 damaged and/or unfinished houses. Concern built this request based on the secondary data and assessments, mainly based on the cluster recommendation, severity report, and coordination with other partners on the ground. Based on that, the suggested NFIs will be implemented in Maaret Tamsrin and Dana as the severity reported there for 3, with a total population of 322.116 and 96.635 PIN in Maaret Tamsrin, and 1,153.807 total population and 346.142 PIN in Dana. In other hand the light rehabilitation for houses will be implemented in Bulbul with a total severity is 5, 33,268 total population and 19.697 PIN, in Raju with a total severity between 3 and 4, 47.749 total population and 23.771 PIN, in Sharan with a total severity 4, 55.667 total population and 32.873 PIN, in Maaret Tamsrin with a total severity 4, 322.116 total population and 192.844 PIN, in Dana with a total severity 4, 1,153.807 total population and 672.848 PIN and in Ariha with total severity is between 3 and 4, 105,178 total population and 31.756 PIN.

4 - Implementation of activities

Concern will target the main formal and informal camps in the suggested districts by Provision and distribution of core NFIs. The distribution of all NFIs will be designed so that vulnerable camp residents have equal access to assistance by conducting beneficiary registration the day before the distribution and by giving each beneficiary a distribution card which will give them access to the distribution site the next day, Concern will have separate waiting areas for men and women at distribution sites to ensure that women do not feel threatened and can be protected from any harassment. In terms of rehabilitation of unfinished houses, the beneficiary selection in the suggested districts will be used of house-by-house assessments. Selection will be made on the basis of the state of the shelter itself, prioritizing first those households with the worst shelter conditions, and on the vulnerability of households to ensure that only those households that lack the financial means to conduct the rehabilitation themselves, Single headed-households, families with a person living with a disability and the elderly. Concern will select those buildings that can be rehabilitated safely within budget and will only select those shelters for rehabilitation that once completed will meet Shelter SPHERE standards. Concern will verify that the homeowner is the lawful owner of the property before making an agreement with them that specifies the works to be undertaken

8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Risk: Women and girls may face protection risks, including the risk of harassment and abuse, when travelling to/from distribution points or/and when registering their names for accessing assistance.Risk: Sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA), including harassment against affected people, by distributors and male beneficiaries while queuing and before/after distributions; for example, requiring affected people to exchange allotted or additional assistance for sexual favours or other acts, with women and girls, as well as other at risk groups being particularly vulnerable to abuse.Mitigation: Train staff on Do No Harm, protection from SEA (PSEA), and child safeguarding; and sign and adhere to code of conduct that respect and uphold the rights of beneficiaries and PSEA.Set up communication and complaints mechanisms or desks that are clearly marked; for ex. feedback desks, face to face interviews with beneficiaries. 9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Low quality of items expose survivors to further harm, discrimination, and stigmatization. Mitigation measures: Technical speciation on place (standard), samples along with the offer, random check comparing with the offer. Location of service provision (safety of movements for beneficiary, contamination from remnants of war, proximity with sensitive buildings). Mitigation measures: choice of locations done in close consultation and agreement with the community, conduction of safety audits. COVID 19 and Cholera: Not adherence to preventive and IPC related measures can increase the risk of transmission to beneficiaries. Mitigation measures include monitoring of adherence to preventive measures; reduction of beneficiary's numbers for group activities provision of PPE, trainings, suspension of service provision if increase in the number of staff with confirmed/suspected infection occurs. Difficulty in accessing distributions for women, unaccompanied children, survivors of GBV, elderly, people with disabilities (PWD), and their caregivers including mental health disorders. Mitigation measure: Provide seats for pregnant women, PWD, elderly, etc. who cannot stand queuing for long periods, chose location close to beneficiaries, consult communities on the location and time of distribution, Consider house to house or tent to tent distributions with female staff present or transportation support for people who are unable to leave their homes or have movement limitations.
