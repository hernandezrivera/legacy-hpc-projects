Provision of integrated and equitable lifesaving nutrition service for vulnerable children under 5 and pregnant and lactating women in Pariang of Unity and Fangak (Jonglei), South Sudan.

Basic Info

Project Name

Provision of integrated and equitable lifesaving nutrition service for vulnerable children under 5 and pregnant and lactating women in Pariang of Unity and Fangak (Jonglei), South Sudan.

Start Date

01/01/2023

End Date

31/12/2023

Project Summary

The provision of integrated emergency lifesaving Nutrition services will target a total of
19,827 vulnerable individuals; Children U5s; 5,533 Boys, 5,993 Girls, and 8,301 Women, pregnant and Lactating mothers; (Fangak County U5s 6,879, 5,283 PLWs; Pariang County U5s 4,647 and PLWs 3,018)
Through fixed sites and mobile approaches in Fangak (Jonglei), and Pariang of Unity respectively.
During 2022 Humanitarian Response Plan, Hold the Child Organization (HCO) proposes to contribute to the 4 Sectoral objectives; 1- To increase equitable access and utilization of quality preventative nutrition specific services for children, adolescents and women; 2- To increase equitable access and utilization of quality lifesaving nutrition services for early detection and treatment of acute malnutrition for girls and boys under five years of age and PLW affected by acute malnutrition through Active and passive screen for malnutrition at community level through the community nutrition volunteers and Maternal Infant Young Child Nutrition Promoter focusing on the prioritized locations of Pariang (Unity) and Fangak (Jonglei) by the end of 2022; 3- To enhance nutrition situation and program monitoring, analysis and utilization of nutrition information; 4- To increase equitable access to nutrition sensitive interventions from health, WASH, FSL, Education (through Early Childhood Development activities) through conducting malaria screening and treating, Strengthening livelihood skills with kitchen gardening, and demonstration gardens to support on complementary feeding for children under five years both girls and boys. Pregnant and Lactating mothers integrated with Maternal Infant Young Child Nutrition activities in 2023 and Protection sectors through enhanced coordination and integrated programming targeting vulnerable groups in prioritized locations by the end of 2023.
During 2023, Hold the Child Organization is anticipated to utilize 290,338 $ targeting Children below the age of U5s, Pregnant and Lactating children, Internally Displaced people living within communities and returnees seeking refugee provided they meet the admission criteria indicated in the South Sudan CMAM guideline adopted in 2017, Community Mobilization through the involvement of Community Nutrition Volunteers, Mother to Mother Support Groups who will be used to support Vitamin A and Deworming supplementation campaigns during National Immunization Days, (NIDS), timely identification of nutrition status through active and passive surveillance to monitor Proxy GAMs through Community Nutrition workers.

1 - Needs

The IPC ranking of Fangak and Pariang among the Phase 4 counties (issued in April 2022); underscores the observed realities for everyday children and women among communities prone to natural disasters and violence (for the case of Fangak). Protracted flooding the region since 2020 has been characterized with; displacements, supply pipeline breakdowns, submerged service points for health, WASH and Nutrition, and derailed FSL programing for the most at risk population groups. It is anticipated that additional access limits to clean water, access barrier to timely access for health service on top of the recent funding instigated reduction in operational health units, relapses/outbreaks of childhood illnesses e.g. diarrheas, the 2022 cholera outbreak reported in Rubkona south of Pariang county attests to this likelihood, on top of limited production and food supplies with heightened exposure risks to sexual exploitation for women, the primary care providers. These trends will continue to affected home care practices and first line of care in 2023, and wasting trends will spike to unprecedented levels should we maintain the status quo. Recent waves of insecurity in Fangak area characterized with additional displacements in save heavens like Old fangak present an even more precarious reality on the overwhelmed local child survival mechanisms. Our recent MUACH screen in the active project catchments indicated a proxy GAM rate of xxx way beyond the WHO thresholds Based on Hold the Child recent and current works in the targeted counties, 2023 program cycle seeks to ensure lifesaving nutrition services for target underserved pockets, contribute to improvement of nutrition information flow and inter-sector linkages to leverage the limited available resources

2 - Project activities

Output 1: Children under 5 with severe and moderate acute malnutrition, and pregnant & lactating women with moderate acute malnutrition are treated through the CMAM program Activities: 1. HCO will Provide therapeutic and medical treatment to 3,632 Pregnant and lactating Mothers for Fangak, (1,987 Pregnant and 1,645 Lactating); 6,879 Children U5s (3,577 girls and 3,302 boys); For Paring 4,647 U5s and 3,018 pregnant and lactating mothers for moderate acute malnourished and Pregnant. 2. Screening of children in OPD/at entry point (tri-age area) in health facilities and referral to OTP/TSFP services within the health facilities or outside depending the availability of OTP/TSFP services. 3. Screen all new children aged 6-59 months at the OTP for Malaria, using RDT and treated timely, U5s admitted with severe acute malnutrition receiving WASH kits and number of nutrition sites in priority counties with access to WASH services/ facilities. Output 2: Increased nutrition prevention and service awareness and inter-linkages/collaborations with complementary sectors at operational levels. Activities: 1. Training to staff and CNVs on CMAM, MYICN and the use of WASH/Health/GBV on identification and referral of GBV cases at nutrition sites cases identified. 55 Fangak, 25 Pariang. 2. Establish hand washing facilities, latrines and clean drinking water for appetite test at Nutrition sites. 3. Sensitize communities in the priority counties with hygiene and sanitation messages to mothers/caregivers of children under 5 and PLWs at SC/OTP/TSFP sites, as well as, at community level on Nutrition, MIYCN, WASH in Nutrition and promotion of basic health services, as well as existing protection referrals, 4. Organize Mother to Mother Support Groups with a focus on training of Lead Mothers on MIYCN facilitating techniques within the catchment areas. 5. Provide Vit A and deworming to children aged 6-59 months in the nutrition program that were missed during the routine National Immunization Days.Output 3: Improved quality and flow of nutrition information, and utilization of nutrition surveillance date at field levels. Activities: 1) Conduct quarterly (and otherwise scheduled) MUAC screening of children (6-59m) and PLWs at community level for early case detection and timely referrals 2) Ensure availability of cluster indicated reporting tools, and adequately skilled personnel to monitor project implementation 3) Support and coordinate biannual FSNMS assessments in the areas of operation to determine the access and coverage and strengthen routine program monitoring 4) Conduct regular CHDs, supply pipeline managers and stakeholders joint monitoring and supportive supervision visits to project sites 5) Support early pre-positioning of specialized nutrition products for the treatment of SAM and MAM to project sites, 6) Liaise with FSL partners to engage nutrition beneficiaries for home gardening activities, distribution of farm tools and seed to enhance the community diversity and Improve their feeding habits. 7) Enhance psychosocial stimulation of children with severe acute malnutrition by providing Early Childhood Development kits in Nutrition locally made, 8) Form and Strengthen Quality and accountability to affected persons committees including mother support groups in all project areas, 9) Establish a community complaint feedback mechanism with 12 nutrition facilities equipped with suggestions boxes placed nutrition sites; OTP/TSFP sites.

3 - Project indicators

Hold the Child Organization will adhere to South Sudan CMAM standard indicators tailored to Nutrition cluster objectives. Severe Acute Malnutrition and Moderate acute Malnutrition standard Indicators will be monitored through the Monthly reporting templates and reported through the Nutrition Information system to capture data on Cure rate, Non Respondent rates, Defaulters and Death. Joint and Regular supportive visits shall be documented on Quarterly basis, key lessons learnt and challenges will be highlighted for improvement and future Intervention planned appropriately.
