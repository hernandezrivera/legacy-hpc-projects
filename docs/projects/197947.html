Project Summary

The crisis in the Northwest and Southwest regions in Cameroon has caused a lot of damage to the populations, including the displacement of many internally displaced people, the majority of whom live in the West, Center and Littoral regions. In these localities, they are confronted with economic problems to which are added emerging situations of exploitation, gender-based violence, social cohesion and discrimination. These problems particularly affect women and girls and reduce their capacity for resilience. They are exacerbated by the new strategies of non-state armed groups, the Ukrainian crisis and the return of the cholera epidemic.
In the Northwest and Southwest regions actually, the largest number of IDPs are found in Donga-Mantung, Bui and Mezam in the Northwest and in Meme and Fako divisions in the Southwest region. In the meantime, a quite huge number of returnees are back in these localities, the reason for this return being the difficult access to basic services at place of displacement (5%), the difficult access to farmland (23%) and lack of livelihood opportunities (37%).
This population is facing protection issues ranking from discrimination (8%), child marriage (10%), family separation (14%), GBV (18%), drug abuse (22%), economic exploitation (24%), kidnapping (26%), early pregnancy (29%), arbitrary detention (37%), lack of civil documentation (40%). In the GBV, sexual attacks stand at 29%. Showers, latrines and water points are among the most insecure places in these regions. The security concern affecting women include risk of attack within the community (27%), sexual violence (29%), lack of safe spaces (30%), the risk of attack when travelling out of the community (53%).
Women and girls are particularly affected, as they suffer the great responsibility of the household charges because the men having greater risk of abduction by armed forced have limited opportunity for conducting income generating activities. The women also face double jeopardy as in one hand they suffer consequences of GBV and in other hand are prevented from seeking for help to the state services by non-armed groups.
From the need assessment conducted in the NW/SW and West regions, the top priority needs ranked by the men and women include health care, food, WASH, Shelter, education and protection including GBV prevention needs.
The project objective is to ensure that women / young girls, affected by the crisis in the Northwest and Southwest regions or displaced from the Anglophone internal crisis, victims or at risk of GBV, receive an appropriate response and live in a favourable environment. The intervention is organized around 2 main outcomes:
Outcome 1: Women and girls affected by the NW/SW crisis live in safe environment and have access to the necessary services to improve their resilience.
Outcome 2: Women and girls affected by the NW/SW crisis are economically empowered and access decent work through vocational training and provision of start-up kits
Emphasis will be put on women resilience and contribution of local competencies including the councils in the framework of decentralization.
UN Women will build on its long experience in assisting women and girls in humanitarian settings. In respect of its triple mandate, the intervention will consider the aspects of policy, coordination and operation. The project will be implemented in partnership with the Ministry of Women’s Empowerment and the Family, the councils within the framework of decentralization and the civil society organizations.
The project is for 12 months from 1st January 2023, with a total budget of US$ 3,316,298. It will target directly 7 divisions in the Northwest, Southwest and West regions, with 30000 direct beneficiaries and 50 000 indirect beneficiaries. The project target is made up of women, girls, men and boys, host population members and IDPs in the Northwest, Southwest and West regions.

1 - Besoins / Needs

This intervention will focus on protection, with the aim at increasing the resilience of women affected by the crisis through GBV management and economic empowerment. This solution is the most important as it is going to tackle women and girl’s vulnerabilities and provide them with means and income generating activities to care for the other needs expressed such as health care, food, WASH, Shelter and education.The objective of this intervention is to ensure that women / young girls, affected by the crisis and living in the Northwest/ Southwest or displaced in the West regions are protected from GBV and empowered to increase their resilience.
