Project Summary

This project represents an extension of the Foundation’s work in the field of education, where hundreds of students have benefited from educational services. It aims to expand further to provide support to students in a way that contributes to improving the educational reality in the governorate by providing educational courses for ninth grade students, baccalaureate students, students with learning difficulties, and students who have dropped out of school. We seek to target approximately 1,300 students in the first year from all educational categories and 1,200 students in the second year.
The project will involve 120 teachers
The project will ensure, through existing policies, the imposition of protection for children from harassment on all employees, volunteers, and teachers, by signing the institution’s policies and subjecting them to training courses to clarify these policies, in addition to ensuring the establishment of a clear mechanism for reporting any abuse that the child may be exposed to during the implementation stages of the project, and clarifying the mechanisms. Safe communication for them
Through the organization's gender policy, which ensures fair participation of both genders, we will ensure participation in all project activities by both genders
The project will assign an external observer to ensure the process of monitoring, evaluation and follow-up of all activities and to ensure the receipt of accurate information. All project activities will be evaluated and the performance of workers and teachers and the level of improvement of students will be followed according to reports that will be shared with the donor.
