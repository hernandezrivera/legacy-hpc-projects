Project Summary

The overall objective of the project is to provide integrated GBV, child protection, and general protection services to the 70,000 most vulnerable conflict-affected populations, including IDPs, resident communities, and returnees in Darfur, South Kordofan, Jazirah, Red Sea, Gedaref, Northern and Khartoum States. Under GBV sub-sector, UPO aims to reach 30,000 most vulnerable women and young girls affected by conflicts in Al Manaqil, Al Qurashi, Janub Aj Jazirah, Um Algura, Gharb Jabal Marrah, Shamal, Jabal Marrah, Wasat Jabal Marrah, Zalingi, Abu Karinka, Ad Du'ayn, Yassin, Al Galabat Al Gharbyah - Kassab,Al Qureisha, Gala'a Al Nahal, Galabat Ash-Shargiah, Madeinat Al Gedaref, Wasat Al Gedaref, Karrari, Um Durman, Al Fasher, Dar As Salam,Kelemando,Tawila,Ad Dabbah,Dongola,Halfa,Haya,Port Sudan,As Salam - SD,Beliel,Ed Al Fursan,Kas,Kateila,Kubum,Mershi localities with provision of dignity kits, awareness raising, gender sensitive consultation (medical and mental) and capacity building, construction and rehabilitation of women multi use centers to help increase their protection, sharing and participation. The project will also focus on the provision of PSS, GBV community networks and safe channels, livelihood start-up kits for the most vulnerable, training of GBV front-line service providers, and a GBV referral support system. UPO conducts its projects with an inclusive and responsible business model that ensures two-way communication pathways throughout the implementation and exit stages, in addition to providing meaningful access and feedback to all stakeholders. UPO will coordinate all project activities with the GBV across the target states, including regularly sharing the ¾ W. For the child protection sub-sector, UPO aims to reach 20,000 children with CBCPNs PSS, CFS, capacity building, identification of UAC, referral supports, distribution of dignity kits, alternative care, case management, assessment and awareness raising to the public on child protection and development, disaster reduction, and coping mechanisms. 20,000 vulnerable people will be provided with general protection, identification, documentation, and legal aid support. UPO commits to strengthening coordination both at the field and Khartoum across the protection sector and the sub-sectors.
Over ten percent of the activity costs of the project will be implemented through a cash assistance approach for the PSN and extremely affected populations. This CASH will be directly distributed to the target populations. Each family will receive cash support for 12 months, $150 per month, to help support the essential needs of the family. The majority of the activities are designed in a durable, environmentally friendly, and sustainable way for long-term solutions for the IDPs, returnees, and host communities. i.e., women and community centers will be constructed from permanent materials that can last for over 10 years. Community structures, protection committees, and networks should be strengthened and supported to continue taking on their roles beyond the project's lifetime. Women capacity building and training to continue tacking, preventing, and addressing any protection concerns beyond the project duration. UPO uses and focuses on gender-sensitive programming and modalities by designing all activities through gender role frameworks and gender matrix frameworks to ensure safe and inclusive participation and sustainability of the project outcomes by creating a change theory in the co-gender norms and harmful practices and promoting healthy habits and co-existing spheres.

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

This project is designed in a protection-sensitive modality, is essentially based on the gender roles framework and the gender matrix framework, and will consider all protection and gender standards and approaches throughout the humanitarian program cycle, project implementation, and exit strategy while promoting the same approaches and standards in the targeted communities to ensure the sustainability of the project's long-term outcomes. Using UPO protection, SEA, accountability policies, gender-sensitive policies, modalities, and tools. The project ensures that safety, dignity, and anonymity are prioritized while avoiding causing harm.
