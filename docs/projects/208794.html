Project Summary

To deliver life saving, life sustaining interventions and to enhance adaptation of displaced, returned and host households of Ngurosoye ward to shock and systematic constraints within crisis evolving in Bama LGA of Borno State.
Sub purpose is to enable 3,774 people from the most food-insecure, conflict affected IDPs, returnees and host households have access to sufficient, nutritionally adequate and diverse foods.
