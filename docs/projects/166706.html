Project Summary

The project seeks to contribute to reducing morbidity and mortality from COVID-19 by expanding capacity of Sadar District Hospital to manage COVID-19 cases through refurbishment of a 200 bed capacity SARI-ITC facility. This aims at screening and treatment of mild to severe cases with referral services for critical patients. Essential equipment and supplies wll be procured to support operationalization of the facility. Provide acceptable food for patients that meets daily nutient requirement. In addition, mental health and pychosocial support services will be provided to COVID-19 patients and their families. Appropriate IPC measures will be taken to reduce the risk of trasmission.
ACF in collaboration with the health sector and the government will coordinate activities of the facility with other partners.

Needs

According to IEDCR BD June 09, 2020 COVID-19 update, 71,875 cases are confirmed to have COVID-19 from 425,505 tested in Bangladesh so far. The total number of death has reached 975. In the context of cox's Bazar, 1094 cases are confirmed COVID-19 as of June 08, 2020 with 26 deaths and 278 recovered. At the moment , there are Eight isolation and treatment centers in host community with 248 bed capacity. The number of isolation and treatment centers are inadequite considering the current COVID-19 transmission rate and Case Fatality rate. Cox’s Bazar is ranked eighth out of 64 districts in Bangladesh for composite risk of exposure to COVID-19. High population density areas of the district are susceptible to the spread of Covid-19 as social distancing becomes challenging. There is growing need to reduce stigma associated with quarantine and isolation through community engagement and psychosocial support to those affected, their families and the community.This project recognize the gap between health service demand and supply of health services. To increase access to and utilization of COVID-19 services , the project will support establishment of 200 bed capacity SARI-ITC facility that will serve as isolation and inpatient management facility with appropriate gender and protection considerations. The SARI-ITC will provide services to men, women, boys and girls in Cox's Bazar targeting host community and humanitarian workers, ranging from mild/moderate to severe cases.
