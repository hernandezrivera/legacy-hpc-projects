Project Summary

Having identified the critical needs in Education that includes poor enrollment rate, high dropout rate, poor condition of school facilities, poor sanitation facilities and low quality teaching due to unqualified staffing, this project will provide resources to address partly the felt needs. The project will carry out enrolment campaigns just before each school calendar term dates to marshal parents and community leaders to enroll more children to schools. To ensure children cultivate interest in schools, lifeskills activities will be introduced I schools such as school games, playing materials, school clubs that will ensure children find interest in schools as well as improving the schooling environment. The schools infrastructure such as classrooms shall be constructed to improve the coverage that is generally low either through SIP or classroom construction targeting. Sanitation facilities will not be left behind where more gender friendly toilets will be constructed to ensure privacy for boys and girls.

Needs

Sudan has made modest progress in increasing children’s access to education. The enrolment in the basic education increased from around 5.1 million (46 % girls) in 2011/2012 to about 5.9 million 48% girls) in 2016/2017, showing progress in enrolment by 17 per cent. The gender parity index (GPI) has risen from 0.87 to 0.90 during the same period indicating that more girls are enrolling in basic education. However, basic education Gross Enrolment Ratio (GER) remained stagnant at 72% between 2009 and 2016 before increasing by a single point to 73% in 2017. More than 3.1 million children are estimated to be out of school mainly from conflict affected or rural areas, nomadic population and from poor families. Only 2 out of 3 pupils who start grade 1 complete basic education and the numbers are further reduced at secondary school cycle.Protracted conflict in most states in Sudan has also affected access to basic education due to continuous disruption. The enrollment rate is low as alluded above in all states. The dropout rates are high with 40.5% of girls and 41.9% of boys not completing primary school. The poor condition of school facilities is a contributing factor to the dropout rate of children. Only 40% of the classrooms are ‘in good condition’, while 30% needed repairs and 30% requires total replacement in the four states of Blue Nile, South Darfur, East Darfur and South Khordofan. Access to sanitation facilities is also a challenge facing school children in all the four States with only 30% of the schools having complete latrines . The quality of teaching is an important factor contributing to poor education outcomes. Ministry of Education records further establish that only 32% of teachers were trained and qualified, while the remainder have been hired with only secondary school qualifications.

Activities

1. Carry out Enrollment campaign. Before the start of every school calendar, the project will engage the community and the leadership to identify those children who are not accessing education in any school and ensure they get enrolled to a learning institution. Three campaigns will be carried out in each locality at the beginning of the school calendar. 2. Improvement of child safe schools to create children interest in Schools. The schooling environment has become a deterrence to many children accessing schools for education. Dilapidated classrooms and lack of school playing facilities that attract children to school are missing in most schools. This project will create the learning environment to be conducive by providing playing equipment, creation of children clubs and age appropriate games in learning institutions.3. Improve school infrastructure/facilities through construction or rehabilitation. The project shall construct atleast 3 school units in each of the target schools in the localities to increase coverage of classrooms in learning institutions to improve learning environment.4. Construction/Rehabilitation of gender friendly sanitation facilities. Most schools do not have separate toilets for boys and girls and that are available, are not fit for use. This discourages children to attend schooling especially girls. This project will ensure to construct atleast one block of 8 drop holes in each of the target schools. 6. Teacher training. Almost half of the teachers in the schools are not trained. This project seeks to train more teachers on pedagogy and learner centered methodologies to improve learning outcomes.7. Support for School Improvement Plan (SIP). To build the capacity of PTAs to manage the learning institutions, a grant will be given to them to fund proposed set of activities that will improve the learning environment in the school.

Impact on environment/natural resources

Sudan is a semi desert environment under harsh climatic conditions. The education project will factor in rehabilitation of climatic conditions through tree planting by children during environmental clubs activities. The construction of infrastructure will consider cutting down of trees and no destruction will be allowed and where it is done accidentally, re-forestation will be encouraged to ensure regeneration of vegetation.
