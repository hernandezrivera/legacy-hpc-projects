Project Summary

The overall objective of this project is to contribute to the improvement of the access to adequate shelter solutions through the medium and heavy scale rehabilitation works of the premises of the most vulnerable people affected by the conflict. The project will provide support to vulnerable women, men, girls and boys among IDP, returnee and other conflict affected communities of Luhansk and Donetsk oblasts (NGCA).

1 - Needs

- Based on Shelter Cluster estimations 55,000 homes incurred various forms of damage on both sides of the contact line during five years of the conflict. The extent of damage in NGCA remains unknown due to the restricted humanitarian access that prevents from the systematic verifications of the number of the damaged houses. Due to the report “Armed conflict in the East of Ukraine: the damage caused to the housing of the civilian population” made in 2019 since the beginning of hostilities in Donetsk NGCA 26 719 houses were damaged: 21 950 private, 4769 apartment houses, out of them more than 2000 are beyond repair and 7880 private and 2062 apartment houses have been restored. Regarding the Luhansk NGCA the exact number of affected residential facilities is unknown. According to various sources, between 6,000 and 9,000 houses of various forms of ownership have been destroyed or damaged;- According to the National Monitoring System Survey on the Situation of Internally Displaced Persons, June 2019, showed that the majority of the respondents (76%) indicated that the reason behind this return was the possession of private property and that they don’t need to pay for rent. The average profile of those who forced to return to the NGCA and frontline areas are women over 60 years of age. A vast majority of respondents residing in NGCA self-assessed their financial situation as ‘enough funds or food, necessary clothing, footwear, basic needs’ (45%). The main source of income for the largest share if surveyed returnees to the NGCA was retirement and long service pension (53%);- Thirty-nine (39%) per cent of the respondents noted the need for insulation of houses as type of assistance for winter 2019/2020 during the post distribution monitoring (PDM) on coal distribution. Among those highlighted the need for insulation of walls (40%), replacement of windows (35%), roof repair (25%).

2 - Activities & outcomes

Outcome 1: Vulnerable individuals in conflict-affected communities of Donetsk and Luhansk NGCA have been provided with shelter solutions. Output 1: Vulnerable households improved their access to adequate shelter through rehabilitation works of accommodations. Activities for Output 1:- Verification and selection of beneficiary households;- Procurement of construction materials based on the results of the assessment visits;- Select construction company to perform rehabilitation works in the selected households;- Monitor medium and heavy rehabilitation works to be held by the selected construction company.

3 - Indicators & targets

Outcome 1: Vulnerable individuals in conflict-affected communities of Donetsk and Luhansk NGCA have been provided with shelter solutions. - % of households that reported that their living conditions are improved (Target: 75%). Output 1: Vulnerable households improved their access to adequate shelter through rehabilitation works of conflict-affected accommodation in medium to heavy categories.- # of households supported with medium shelter assistance (Target: 750 households, equivalent to an estimated 1,350 individuals, of whom 1,215 adults (729 female and 486 male) and 135 children (81 girls and 54 boys);- # of households supported with heavy shelter assistance (Target: 250 households, equivalent to an estimated 450 individuals, of whom 405 adults (243 women and 162 men) and 45 children (27 girls and 18 boys).
