Project Summary

In fragile South Sudan, protection issues such as sexual violence has increasingly become normalized on both the local level and national level. Further, harmful gender norms and traditions limit the meaningful participation of women which further compromises the effectiveness of peace building processes among communities. Therefore, the project is aimed at addressing the challenges of High rate of general protection issues by promoting awareness session on human rights violations, gender equality, economic empowerment and participation in decision making process. The project also has the component of local peace building social cohesion, root cause analysis of inter and intra community conflict, child protection including general protection, early warning and response mechanism about conflict and disaster.

1. Needs

South Sudan continues to face one of the worst protections concerns since the outbreak of civil war, intercommunal conflict, disaster, sexual exploitations and abuse has been widespread and was widely used as a weapon of war. While the precise number of incidents remains unknown, it is clear that sexual violence, physical abuse has increasingly become normalized at all levels. In addition, harmful gender norms and traditions limit the meaningful participation of women and children which further compromises the effectiveness of peacebuilding and establishment of social cohesion processes among communities. It was noted that men often lure women and girls from returnee, IDP, and host communities into sexual exploitation through grooming in exchange for food/money. The gender analysis and needs assessment conducted by Islamic Relief in 2023, revealed that there are still high incidences of child abuse, human right violations, displacements, insecurity, and general violations of human rights in Kajo keji, Kapoeta East, Kapoeta North, Yei, and Tonj North. According to Protection Cluster HNO these counties have been rated as severity level 4 and areas of prioritization. This requires a bottom-up approach to child and general protection responses and risk mitigation for the affected population. For example, insecurity, peace, gender inequality, and child abuse are the key protection issues faced by women, adolescent girls and children in the targeted counties. Displacement reinforces pre-existing discrimination and socioeconomic disadvantages. The effects of displacement depend on its duration, but immediate manifestations include family separations, exposure to gender violence, trauma associated with the deaths of family members, impaired health, and the loss of the home and possessions. The project aims to improve and promote community response and prevention of protection risks related issues such as child abuse, and human rights violations. This project will be strengthening both returnees/IDPs, and host communities through participation in outreach, advocacy, and peace dialogue organized and facilitated at grassroots level to conflict-affected communities. The project will also establish and building the capacity of Community Based Protection Committees on gender equality, and rights of children and people with disabilities in the target areas. Through the interventions, the project will address the challenges of high rate of human right violations, peace building initiative, women economic empowerment, participation in decision making process, and cultural practices that are harmful to women/girls in the society. The project will be implemented following a Channel of Hope Methodology (CoH) and will address the following: (i) Strengthen local capacities of chiefs, community leaders and local authority on gender equality, conflict prevention, mediation skills, and Disaster Risk Reduction (DRR); (ii) Support communities’ efforts in peacebuilding, protection, conflict transformation, and awareness raising. (iii) Create increased awareness on child and general protection to ensure improved knowledge and access to services for refugees, internally displaced persons and host communities.
