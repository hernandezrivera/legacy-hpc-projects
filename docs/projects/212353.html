Project Summary

Throughout 2024, Violet organization plans to implement comprehensive livelihood support interventions by applying a variety of modalities to address the needs of the different groups with proper modality.
The proposed project will effectively contribute to supporting the self-reliance of the most vulnerable crisis-affected groups in NWS and improving the livelihoods and value chains through income generation activities as follows:
Throughout 2024, Violet organization plans to implement comprehensive livelihood support interventions by applying a variety of modalities to address the needs of the different groups with proper modality.
The proposed project will effectively contribute to supporting the self-reliance of the most vulnerable crisis-affected groups in NWS and improving the livelihoods and value chains through income generation activities as follows:
1. Cash for work for crops and vegetable value chains, targeting 660(3300 individuals, 825 men, 850 women, 800 boys, and 825 girls) CFW workers for one month, and an estimated number of 150 farmers benefit from the service through CFW workers in Idleb and Aleppo governorates. Among the targets under this activity, at least 15% of PWDs and 50 female beneficiaries will be reached.
2. Vocational training for agribusinesses and Food processing, targeting 300 households (1500 individuals, 375 men, 400 women, 300 boys, and 425 girls) in Idleb and Aleppo governorates. Among the targets under this activity, at least 15% of PWDs and 50 female beneficiaries will be reached.
3. Business grants for agribusiness and food processing, targeting 250 households (1250 individuals, 325 men, 300 women, 300 boys, and 325 girls) in Idleb and Aleppo governorates. With an estimated total cost of 1150 USD per household. Among the targets under this activity, at least 15% of PWDs and 50 female beneficiaries will be reached.
Each BNF will receive a cash grant business with a total value of 1000 USD in two installments, the first one (500 USD) after the completion of the full process, including the training attendance, development of the business proposal, as well as the Baseline and verification for his business, and the second installment (500 USD) will be delivered for the beneficiary within one month after the first installment and the verification for the first installment expenditure on the intended items in the signed contract with the beneficiary.
4. Agriculture inputs provision through various modalities (in kind, voucher, cash for inputs) with relevant training for farmers on GAP and climate-smart agriculture, targeting 500 households (2500 individuals, 625 men, 700 women, 500 boys, and 675 girls) in Idelb
governorate with an estimated cost of 1000 USD per each one. Among the targets under this activity, at least 15% of PWDs and 50 female beneficiaries will be reached.
The planned agriculture inputs for distribution to the beneficiaries will be according to the FSL cluster SO2 7 SO3 response package for each type of agriculture input. However, VO is planning to target wheat farmers with agriculture inputs with support for one-hectare wheat cultivation for each farmer, with a seed rate for wheat between 200-250 kg/ha for irrigated cultivation and 150-200kg/ha for rain-fed, with take into consideration of the varieties and/or the agroecological zones, also Wheat fertilizers application: as follows:
• First, during sowing 150-200 kg/ha (NPK 10:10:10), or (15:15:15) or (20:20:20) slow release; and/or (phosphate fertilizers depending on the availability).
• Second, at the tillering stage, 150-200 kg/ha of urea fertilizer.Third: after 15 days at 100 kg/ha Urea
The estimated USD value/cost per beneficiary will be 450-850 USD/ton; however, this will be further determined based on market price assessment or monitoring in specific areas, refer to the Agriculture Inputs and Commodity Bulletins.
