Project Summary

The project has a three-pronged focus that reinforces each other:
Focus one is on empowerment of girls' and women in order for them to participate in the peace building process in the northeast of Nigeria which is affected by the Boko Haram armed insurgency and involve women and girls efforts to prevent and end violence against women and girls in Northeast Nigeria.
The second focus is on the provision of critical protection services for conflict affected children and women by providing reintegration assistance to girls and women who are survivors of conflict related sexual violence and their children born of such violence, as well as the provision of social and economic reintegration assistance to children formerly associated with armed groups (CAAGs) and Women and Girls Formally associated with armed groups (WAAGs).
The third focuses on the provision of immediate GBV prevention and response services to women, girls and boys faced with various forms of gender based violence in Northeast Nigeria.

Needs

The project aim to ensure community and social acceptance of Survivors of Conflict related Sexual Violence including women and girls and their children born out of conflict related sexual violence who because of their situation are stigmatized and ostracized from communities. The project also aim to provide holistic gender based violence response to survivors including case management, psycho-social support, socio-economic reintegration and also linking them with needed multi sector services. Overall, the project has three specific objectives: The role and specific needs of women and girls in the peacebuilding process are recognized and fulfilled. Women and girls associated with armed groups and/or subjected to conflict-related sexual violence (CRSV), who participated in the programme, receive support to successfully reintegrate into their communities and are given a platform/avenue to support peacebuilding and reconciliation in their communities  Communities in target Local Government Areas/IDP camps/host communities provide a supportive, stable and protective environment for women and girls, especially for those who have been associated with Boko Haram.

Activities

Continue to monitor and report on grave child rights violations through the Monitoring and Reporting Mechanism (MRM). Use MRM reports to advocate for enhanced protection of boys, girls and women from recruitment and sexual violence. Supporting community dialogues to discuss the roles and responsibilities of women and girls in peace building and the elimination of gender based violence in communities. Advocacy with religious and community leaders to support in the elimination of violence against women, girls and boys as well as encouraging effective acceptance and reintegration of CRSV survivors and their children. Raising awareness of girls and women and building their capacity to speak up. Empowering girls and women with specific skills that will help them participate in peace building and elimination of violence against women and girls. Socio economic and livelihood support for women and adolescent girls to empower them while also supporting them to mobilize local action to build their resilience and stand strong from negative their situation. Conduct studies and researches on threats faced by Conflict Affected Women, Girls and children in Northeast Nigeria and understand from local perspective ways to foster acceptance and reduce risked and threats faced. This will inform future programming.
