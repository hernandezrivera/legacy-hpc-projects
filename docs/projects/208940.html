Project Summary

Darfur region and Khartoum are the most affected states in Sudan’s ongoing war, with people’s needs increasing day by day due to a lack of assistance provided. The conflict has led to damage to water infrastructure, including wells, boreholes, and water treatment facilities as well as damage to sanitation facilities. Through this project, SUDO aims to provide WASH services to the people of Khartoum and Darfur. The project targets 421,437 individuals across Khartoum and the 5 Darfur states where SUDO is currently have access and operating in all these areas, including its office in Karrai-Khartoum. The target localities are; seven localities of Khartoum, Kulbus and Al-Geneina in WD, Zalingi and Azum in CD, El-Fashir, Tawilla, Kutum and Dar elsalam-ND, Ad-Dein-ED, Belil and Nyala Shamal in SD.
This project will deliver the following Assistance:
• Drill and install new hand pumps
• Install new and rehabilitate water yards
• Upgrading of water supply system
• Rehabilitation of wells
• Rehabilitate water points
• Provide access to water through water trucking
• Distribute water quality supplies at HH level
• Provide water quality monitoring
• Construct communal latrines
• Rehabilitate health and school latrines
• Provide operation cost for water stations
• Training of community committees on maintenance and sustainability of water sources and sanitation facilities

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

The project will improve access to clean water and sanitation facilities. The availability of water in safe and accessible areas to people affected by conflict will reduce the risk associated with fetching water in far and risky areas particularly for women and children as well as the use of unsafe water. Adequate gender-segregated sanitation facilities and hygiene promotion will reduce the practice of open defection and other practices that increase the risk of diseases, thus contribute to the protection of people. Additionally, access of people with disabilities will be considered. Communities in the project locations will be engaged in the project design, implementation and monitoring through establishment of community committees for managing and sustaining of water sources, sanitation facilities, and hygiene practices. Conflict sensitivity and Do No Harm principle will be considered throughout this project, where the focus will be on building connections and reducing the dividers between the groups. The selection of communities and the committees will consider the diversities of gender, religion, ethnicity, and culture. Assessment of the level of satisfaction or complaints from communities will be done as part of the accountability.

4.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will support Inter-Agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including Sexual exploitation and abuse complaints?

SUDO has a safeguarding policy with the objectives to protect beneficiaries of SUDO projects from any form of abuse and exploitation as well as protect staff from the same and any potential false allegation of misconducts. Safeguarding Policy is applicable to; full time and part time SUDO staff, members of SUDO, Board of Directors, Volunteers and interns, consultants, visitors, people acting on behalf of SUDO and – to the relevant extent – to SUDO suppliers and partner organizations. SUDO take all reasonable steps to protect beneficiaries from any harassment, abuse and exploitation potentially perpetrated by SUDO staff or associates. All SUDO staff and associates are required to RESPECT the rights and dignity of individuals, families and communities with whom SUDO work. likewise, SUDO is committed to protecting the rights of the SUDO staff and to maintain its dignified working environment free of any potential harassment, abuse and exploitation. SUDO ensure that safe, appropriate and accessible means of reporting safeguarding concerns are made available to staff and the communities. In the response SUDO follow up safeguarding reports and concerns according to principles and obligations described in the SUDO Code of Conduct and Community Response Mechanism (CRM). The reaction to a complaint or report shall result in a confidential, sensitive, thorough and prompt investigation. Such investigation either may result in the reduction of concern or in further action which includes disciplinary action up to dismissal and/or reporting to relevant authorities.
