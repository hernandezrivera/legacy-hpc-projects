Project Summary

The Project expected to impact positively on the lives of communities in the targeted areas, through indicated activities, capacity building, and raise awareness on the protection, GBV, and food security. CDF’s project will promote economic recovery and protection through a pronged approach that targets leadership and individuals of IDPs, returnees, and host communities in all Locality. This project will provide livelihood activities through vocational training and IGAs, support basic education, establish and training CBCPN, provision awareness sessions, responding to GBV case, and activate of GBV referral system at the state level which will contribute to strengthening the social welfare system at West Darfur,South darfur ,white Nile ,Algazira State and its localities. This project will also include a life skill approach for adolescents and youth
CDF will closely coordinate with UN agencies, INGOs, and government counterparts toavoid duplication. CDF will coordinate with livelihood, protection, education sectors andsubsectors including the GBV Working group , and will attend the GBV Sub Sectormeetings when possible and share information to the sub-sector 4Ws.
This project will provide good opportunities in deferent sector
In education sector quality education for school-aged children from diverse communities in remote conflict-affected into the education sector will support teachers, parents, community leader’s pupils to build capacity education departments to develop and utilize constructive skills, positive attitudes and practices for informal and formal education. This project will support teachers, students, and school PTAs in developing, advocating and delivering on strong practical peace messages, taught in a child-friendly and peace-focused manner by teaching professionals and volunteers. Training workshops will build peace resources at the local level integrated with the education sector as a strong focus of building social cohesion which is weak in many areas due to years of conflict which has resulted in lack of trust within and between many communities. The activity contributes in the Education sector will scale up its multi-sectoral response to support community affected conflict and disaster-affected basic education and secondary school-aged children (ages 6-16) to resume or continue their education in a safe and protective learning environment. Displaced children, out-of-school children, children from nomadic and pastoralist communities, children with disabilities, adolescent girls and children facing severe, emergency, or catastrophic levels in need of this assistance, Currently hosting the highest numbers of school-aged IDP children. Many of these children have endured protracted conflict and continue to be exposed to conflict. Conflict-affected children need psychosocial support, a protective learning environment and the normality of a school routine. However, due to the large influx of IDPs into certain localities, many of the learning spaces in these areas are extremely overcrowded. Children have reduced access to learning supplies, seating, and latrines and the pupil to teacher ratio is often over a hundred to one.
In GVB sector will utilize Age, Gender and Diversity Mainstreaming approach (AGDM) and follow community-based protection approach to generate positive and sustainable outcomes. Analysis and data collection will be segregated by age and gender, which will allow addressing vulnerabilities and socio-economic risks of boys and girls. The planned awareness-raising interventions will ensure that sensitization will tackle gender subordination of women and girls, address gender-based violence and introduce women to peacebuilding approaches and processes. Issues of water fetching and livelihood will be taken into consideration to ensure the protection of women and girls when they have to walk for long-distance.
