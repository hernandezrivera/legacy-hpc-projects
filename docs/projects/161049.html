Project Summary

The aim of LiveWell’s WASH response plan for HRP 2020 will be to “Increase Access to Essential Lifesaving WASH interventions for 25,000 vulnerable IDPs/host populations in Pibor and Pochalla in Jonglei; Yirol East, Yirol West and Awerial in Lakes; Kapoeta East and Magwi in Eastern Equatoria; Juba and Terekeka in Central Equatoria and Raja and Jur River in Western Bahr-al-Ghazal States.
The LiveWell’s HRP budget for LiveWell’s HRP 2020 for WASH project is at US$ 500,000 and this overall budget will aim at supporting the following WASH activities in 5 former states based on the following LiveWell objectives aligned to the WASH Cluster’s objectives and strategies for the Response Plan 2020
1. OBJECTIVE 1: Reduced the occurrence of severe acute malnutrition (SAM/GAM) in under five (5) years through the provision of accessible safe drinking water and good hygiene practices at nutrition centers (OTP/SC) in targeted counties (Yirol east, Yirol west, Pibor and Pochalla):
o Rehabilitate 2 Collective water points in flooded counties such as Pibor, Pochalla in Boma State in order to provide safe drinking water for IDPs' schools, health facilities and nutritional centers for SAM/MAM.
o Distribute 2500 WASH NFI/Hygiene Kits for admitted SAM cases at OTP/SC till the household level.
2. OBJECTIVE 2: Provide quality lifesaving WASH services to prevent or control the occurrence of epidemic prone diseases in measles/cholera/AWDs/EVD hotspots counties (Pibor, Jur River, Yirol east, Magwi etc)
o Reach 25,000 people with basic WASH NFIs and good sanitation/hygiene practices messaging that are geared toward outbreaks prevention and control.
o Train 50 health workers and 50 prefects in schools on handwashing techniques with soap as a good hygiene practices to minimize WASH related disease outbreaks.
o Conduct 3 mass campaigns against cholera, measles, and EVD using IECs materials, streets announcement and other form of media to pass vital WASH information to masses.
3. OBJECTIVE 3: Provide access to timely/equitable basic WASH packages for the most vulnerable groups (women/girls, elderly and disabled) based on needs, protections, and public health-related issues
o Renovate 5 gender-appropriate sanitation facilities to provide safe sanitary facilities to women and girls with appropriate GBV risk analysis
o Distribute 2500 Menstrual Hygiene Management (MHM) kits to girls/women of reproductive age in schools and in the community to improve RH and improve dignity.
o Conduct direct and participatory hygiene promotion messages to reach 25,000 people at public and households’ level

1 - Needs

• Access to clean water and improved WASH services is a major challenge in several parts of Central, Western and Eastern Equatoria, Jonglei & Western Bhar el Ghazal States and it has caused health insecurities to worsen over the second quarter of 2019 leading to measles outbreaks. This is largely attributed to ongoing conflict and impassibility of road network by humanitarian actors (REACH, April-June 2019), hence increased morbidity and mortality in U5R children. • Inadequate provision of essential healthcare services due to inadequate funding led to inadequate access to improved services such as RH and MPSS services. Development partners like World Bank support 27% of the existing health facilities in Jonglei State, 63% of Health Facilities are non-functional due to lack of funding (IRNA report, 2016/2017), resulting into increased morbidity/mortality of common diseases. • IPC report (July, 2019), 10,000 people in Cueibet in Lakes State are likely in Catastrophe (IPC Phase 5), Jonglei State populations estimated to be food insecure and 73% of its population facing crisis (IPC 3). All the proposed counties in Lakes are classified in Emergency phase (IPC 4); Awerial, Cueibet, Rumbek North, Yirol East, Yirol West etc. This is caused by a lean season, delayed rainfall, persistence inflation, displacement, assets depletion and destruction of livelihood as a result of ongoing conflicts in WES, CES, EES, Jonglei, WBS, and Lakes. • Of the 22 SMART surveys conducted between September 2018 and January 2019, eight of them showed GAM rates above the 15% WHO emergency threshold. However, five counties in Jonglei (Ayod, Pibor, Pochalla, and Twic East), Kapoeta East in Eastern Equatoria and Jur River in Western Bahr-al-Ghazal State show Critical levels of Global Acute Malnutrition (GAM 15.0%-29.9%) (IPC Reports 2019). • LiveWell’s PHSA assessment (March, 2019) reported that inadequate WASH services, GAM rate of 19.9%, EPI coverage, inadequate provision of health, nutrition and WASH services are major causes of measles outbreaks in greater Pibor (IDSR, Week 8 of 2019), therefore, HC supported IOM/LiveWell, and MEDAIR/LiveWell to conduct measles vaccination campaign in 5 payams in March and September, 2019. Inadequate provision of primary health care and mental health services to be specific (9%) as a result of inaccessibility, insecurity, attack on health etc. Flooding Assessment in Pibor (Oct, 2019), 13,000 host populations are displaced as a result of flooding, and health services are severely disrupted. • Increased risks of Ebola Virus Disease (EVD) in borders of Yei & Magwi, and measles outbreaks since the beginning of the year have been reported in 15 counties including Pibor, Yirol East, Jur River and including Juba and Wau. 308 suspected measles cases in Jur River have been reported of which 4 tested positive for measles. It was recommended that WHO and humanitarian partners should carry out a reactive campaign in the areas (WHO EVD bulletin & IDSR report of weeks 36 & 37, September, 2019). • Low EPI coverage in Pibor, Jur River, Yirol East, Yirol West (EWARS Week 34, 2019), coupled with flooding in major counties in Jonglei State is anticipated to likely precipitate the outbreaks, malaria outbreaks, acute watery diarrhea (AWDs) and resultant severe acute malnutrition (flooding effect on health report in Pibor, Oct 2019) in under 5 year children and other vulnerable group like disabled, and pregnant women. LiveWell is therefore seeking your approval to recommend our concept note “Increase Access to Essential Lifesaving WASH interventions in Jonglei, Lakes, Western Bhar al Ghazal, Central and Eastern Equatoria States, South Sudan” for the Humanitarian Response Plan (HRP) for WASH Cluster in the coming Year 2020.
