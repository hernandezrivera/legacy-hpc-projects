Project Summary

The proposed project is basically applied for DG Khan because of Muslim Hands presence in this area. The proposed interventions will be focused on capacity building of communities to promote hygiene including hand washing practices and use of toilets in rural communities particularly in most vulnerable parts of the district. Muslim Hands will launch hygiene sessions to community leaders which will further enhance hygiene practices by using Hygiene Kits among other community members within their communities. Moreover use of toilets will be encouraged by construction of low cost toilets in rural areas in disaster prone district of Southern Punjab, Pakistan. Following main activities proposed :
• Provision of appropriate WASH installations in priority health facilities targeted for treating COVID-19 cases: (water supply, sanitation facilities, hand-washing facilities with soap, chlorine powder and lime for disinfection of contaminated surfaces).
• Ensuring access to information on preventive measures, against COVID-19 through social and mass media, radio jingles, leaflets, posters, banners, billboards and radio& television shows.
• Installation of hand washing stations at key public places and distribution of soap.
• Capacity building of key front line workers on measures related to COVID-19
The project is proposed for 09 months and will cover more than 85000 affected population through hard and soft components.
