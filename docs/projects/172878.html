Project Summary

The overall objective of the project is to provide life-saving and sustainable WASH services and facilities to at least 5,000 out-of-camp South Sudanese Refugees (SSRs) and 10,000 vulnerable host communities in El Jabalein and El Salaam localities, White Nile State; and at least 15,000 IDPs, returnee and host communities in Al Fasher and Kutum localities in North Darfur. North Darfur and White Nile are considered one of the states with the greatest humanitarian assistance needs in Sudan due to the presence of refugees, IDPs (both in camps and out-of-camps), and returnees and the over-stretched capacities to address the WASH needs of these vulnerable groups of people, who also include host communities. The project will seek to address the out-of-camp and vulnerable host communities’ life-saving WASH needs through rehabilitation of water systems (water yards, hand pumps, tap stands etc), household latrine construction, provision of household and personal hygiene facilities, hygiene promotion activities and environmental sanitation services.
Women will be engaged in the project by incorporating them into the established WASH committees and hygiene promotion teams. This will ensure women’s needs (including those related to their safety and protection against SGBV) are identified and addressed in the planning and decision-making processes of the project. Separate latrines will be constructed for females, specially designed latrines will be constructed for older and disabled people, and complaints and feedback mechanisms will be set up to ensure all concerns and feedback of the target population are addressed effectively and efficiently.

1 - Needs

Thirty-seven percent of the health facilities do not have sanitation facilities in the country. The health situation is aggravated by limited access to facilties and knowledge by the community on vector control, prevention of cholera and currentlyCOVID 19. The population at risk (who include refugees, IDPs, returnees, and vulnerable host communities) of WASH-related diseases in risk-prone areas do not have access to adequate chlorinated water and soap. Vector control is vital to address malaria's prevailing situation and ongoing outbreaks of chikungunya, dengue, and rift valley fever (RVF) remains a threat to the lives and health of population at risk. There are an estimated nine million school-going children in Sudan, and 48 percent are girls. 5 million children have no sanitation, and more than 70 percent do not have coverage of hygiene services. Open defecation remains a widespread practice in several states in Sudan due to low access and availability of safe and secure latrines. Below is summary of WASH needs: Limited access to safe and clean water due to broken down water sources and systems – water bore holes, water yards and hand pumps.• Limited access to safe and secure latrines due to limited and broken down latrines.• High risks of affected comuunities to water-related morbidity and mortality (especially during rainy seasons) due to widespread open defecation and poor waste management.• Poor personal and household hygiene due to limited knowledge on safe hygiene practices and/or no access to facilities (such as soap, handwashing ibriks).• High risk of women to being victims of SGBV due to limited access to water points and latrines (have to travel for long distances to access the facilities)

2 - Activities/Outputs

• Repairs/rehabilitation of water sources/systems – e.g. repairs of water yards and hafirs (to be fitted with solar pumping system), hand pumps and water stands (considerations for cash for work will be made regarding delivery of repairs/rehabilitation of the water yards and hand pumps)• Support the construction of household latrines ((considerations for cash for work will be made regarding delivery of construction of the household latrines).• Conducting of weekly hygiene promotion activities such as solid waste collection campaigns, household monitoring visits, soap distribution and jerry-can cleaning campaigns, hygiene promotion and environmental awareness raising through use of performance arts.• Capacity building of the WASH Committees and Community Hygiene Promotion teams through trainings and networking
