Project Summary

IOM proposes to support migrants, displaced persons and communities affected by conflict and climate disasters in accordance with humanitarian principles through shelter solutions that include the construction, upgrade or retrofit of shelters and the immediate shelter NFI assistance for the most vulnerable families, while using a people-centered approach and increasing local capacity and knowledge in BBB.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Mozambique faces a triple humanitarian crisis; with a conflict, reoccurring disasters related to natural hazards due to climate shocks, and the impact of Cholera epidemics; that has become incredibly complex. For over four years, the province of Cabo Delgado in Mozambique has experienced brutal attacks by armed groups, while neighboring provinces of Nampula, Niassa, Zambezia and Sofala are seeing a spillover of displacement. As a result of the insecurity, over 850,599 people have been displaced outside places of origin and other 571,468 returnees have been accounted in Northern Mozambique as of August 2023. In this context, the humanitarian situation continues to deteriorate in both hosting and displaced communities, and for those left behind. In total, an estimated 1.2 million people are in need of urgent humanitarian assistance and protection in 2023.In this HRP for 2024, IOM aims to assist 64,340 individuals with essential household items and various shelter solutions of which 20,000 individuals will be supported via the interagency joint response programme that ensures immediate assistance in hard-to-reach areas and districts where there is no shelter partner presence. The programme also aims to support 42,400 individuals with construction, retrofitting and/or upgrading of shelters that withstand harsh weather conditions which will contribute to secure safe, dignified, and healthy living conditions of the population affected by the insecurity crisis, but also that are extremely vulnerable to natural hazards. In addition to these efforts, IOM will ensure that local artisans and population, increases their knowledge and applies the Build Back Better principles.In addition, a common shelter and Non-Food Item (NFI) pipeline stock intending to reach more than 44,340 individuals will continue to be managed by the Shelter Cluster. Since its creation the common pipeline has served more than 13 shelter cluster partners which are able to access this mechanism that offers harmonized and standardized household and shelter items that helps to increase the readiness to respond.

Ensure active participation of the most vulnerable groups in distributions and beneficiary selection, and promote women related working groups and their integration in program activities.9. Carry-out orientation to local artisans, young adults (men and women), and general community members in BBS construction techniques10. Carry-out post distribution monitoring and analysis to inform future programming.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

IOM is implementing the Displacement Tracking Matrix (DTM) which enables the humanitarian community to identify priorities and activities through multi-sectoralassessments that take into consideration gaps in humanitarian service coverage, including health, WASH, protection and shelter services. The DTM displacement monitoring and emergency tracking tools will further inform humanitarian programming of protection risks and needs for better targeted and evidence-based interventions ultimately contributing to more secure settlements for affected populations.Regarding project design, IOM ensures that the shelter Kit content is tailormade to mitigate protection risks and takes into consideration the needs of vulnerable groups.Greater protection concerns are mainstreamed within the Shelter Cluster partners and into the multi-sectoral teams (in addition to having protection representation within IOM structure), who conduct referrals and services specifically targeted for those vulnerable persons. IOM is an active member of the Protection cluster and shelter-protection assessment visits inclusive of HH surveys that focus on vulnerability criteria and protection aspects are performed to ensure integration of these aspects into the design of the program activities.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

Shelter Cluster partners with support from other clusters, will conduct multisectoral community engagement exercises which include focal group discussions, visits to the affected communities, community and household assessments, in order to understand their most immediate pressing shelter and NFI needs and for those displaced, the intentions of the communities to resettle into sites either set up by the Government (to be verified that have meet the minimum standards), or plots provided by the Government within the host community.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Regarding project design, IOM ensures that the shelter Kit content is tailor made to mitigate protection risks and takes into consideration the needs of vulnerable groups. IOM shelter works complements with other sector actions related to disability inclusion which include Individual Protection Assistance (IPA) were vulnerable individuals are referred and assisted based on existing social and health services available. And the specific adaptation of infrastructures for people with disabilities and the set-up of disability inclusion committees at resettlements in order to ensure inclusion and active participation for identification of needs.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

IOM will promote that sustainable construction considerations are integrated into all phases of the project management cycle, these will include assessment and research of the local context for building designs and natural resources while identifying environmental issues on the chain; and that these learnings derive into practices that are applied and monitored throughout the implementation and further use of the affected populations. These may include promotion of green building practices, debris and waste management, and nature-based solutions.
