Project Summary

A.Needs
The residents of Azaz, Ehteimlat, Ma'btali, Alazeezea and Madina Monoura Camp, Balyun, and Bsames suffer from water shortage and the scarcity of clean water and great difficulty in securing it, as these communities suffer from a significant decline in the level of surface water, so the people are forced to buy water from neighboring areas, which leads to an increase in its cost, not to mention the unsuitability of this water for drinking in many cases, and this, in turn, is a danger that threatens the health status of the people and the fear of the spread of WBDs. Also, the situation of sanitation, waste management, and hygiene promotion services in Ehteimlat, Rajo, Deir Hassan-Darhasha, Aqrabat (Dana), Mashhad Ruhin, and Qah communities is very poor, as there are no wastewater treatment plants, and most of the drainage water is discharged into the valleys and natural streams near densely populated areas which lead to increase in diseases and the spread of insects. Furthermore, another high-risk factor is using wastewater for irrigation in agriculture in addition to that, wastewater contaminates the underground water which feeds some wells used for drinking purposes.

B. Intervention and implementation of activities:
IHR aims to address critical WASH gaps in response to the needs of Vulnerable people residing in Balyun, Bsames, Barisha, Ma'btali, Ehteimlat, Raju, Azaz, Deir Hassan - Darhashan, Aqrabat (Dana), Mashhad Ruhin. Qah communities and surrounding camps by providing a package of WASH services that include maintenance of sewers and drinking water systems, securing safe water for people through water networks, constructing reservoirs, constructing sewage systems, and carrying out hygiene awareness activities.
C. Analysis of protection risks:
Risk 1: Health risk associated with construction works and exposure to chemicals.
Likelihood: High
Impact: Medium
Mitigating Measure:
- Ensure staff/partners/contractors involved in construction works or working with chemicals wear appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) and follow established safety regulations; Conduct/repeat safety training for staff/partners/contractors.
Risk 2: Potential exposure to explosive hazard (IED/UXO/ERW) during assessment/ rehabilitation of water supply infrastructure (water stations, towers, distribution networks).
Likelihood: Medium
Impact: High
Mitigating Measure:
Work closely with Mine Action actors in the area and conduct assessments/rehabilitation works only in areas declared as cleared;
Conduct safety training with stuff and contractors; Add explosive hazard awareness material to our programming.
D. PSEA Strategy:
This proposal upholds key principles set out in the HRP such as accountability to affected populations, prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse (PSEA) and do no harm.Reporting SEA Complaints: A confidential hotline is available for anonymous reporting.
