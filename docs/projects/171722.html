Project Summary

Recent climatic shocks and insecurity has significantly impacted to new huge displacements into already crowded IDP sites in Baidoa. The IDPs are living precariously in deplorable conditions and struggle to meet their basic needs owing to inconsistent service provision coupled by cases of exclusion from accessing humanitarian assistance. Of particular concern are women, girls and people with disabilities that are further affected by unplanned nature of the IDP sites that limit them from accessing essential services within the sites hence need for site improvement to enhance their safe and secure access to critical services available within the sites. Their plight is worsened by poor coordination and management mechanisms at the settlements/IDP sites that are quite informal owing to the fact that they are managed by landlords or gatekeepers that are not accountable and further doesn’t adhere to minimum standards. They are further affected by rampant evictions without alternate locations for settlements. Therefore there is a very strong need for site-level coordination and management systems to ensure equitable access to services and increase protection of displaced persons coupled with meaningful consultation and participation of affected IDP communities and ensure a strong foundation with dignified living conditions for durable solutions. The project targets a total of 25,300 person (10,525 women, 9,715men, 2530 boys and 2530 boys) at the informal settlements in Baidoa, Bay Region Somalia.

Mitigation Measures

1. Risk that beneficiaries are exposed to violence during registration/distribution as a result of the distance to or location of humanitarian assistance and service: Mitigation Measure : Enhancing security at the project sites by hiring security staff/ additional guards as well as ensuring that beneficiaries entitlements are provided at a central place to limit long distance and distribution time to enable them get back to their homes before the cover of the night and avoid possible GBV incidendences.2. Risk of women, girls and boys exposed to sexual violence, including sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA), when accessing humanitarian assistance. Mitigation Measure: Ensuring all staff sign PSEA code of conduct and adhering with it as well as conducting awareness on SEA and the reporting procedures that doesnt expose the victims to the perpetrators.3. Risk of no access to or awareness of protection assistance or referral services for survivors of protection incidents (i.e. GBV, child protection, evictions, explosive hazards, etc.)Mitigation measure: Conducting awareness on protection assistance for survivors of protection incidences4. Risk that marginalized groups, persons with disabilities, women, boys and girls, do not participate in consultations or decision-making processes for the project or are excluded from humanitarian services/assistance/activitiesMitigation measure: Conducting comprehensive awareness for persons with disabilities, women, boys and girls and ensuring their inclusion and participation in decision making processes as well as active participation on all project phases/stages right from initiation throughout implementation including mornitoring, till completion5. Risk that beneficiaries are not accessing/using complaint and feedback mechanismsMitigation measure: Establishing beneficiaries complain and feedback mechanisms and conducting awareness targeting the beneficiaries for utilization of the same
