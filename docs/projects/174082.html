Project Summary

The ongoing conflicts in north eastern Nigeria and the recent Covid-19 pandemic which has become a global threat to health have made people to live under lockdown and restriction of movement placed by the government have put stringent measures on women, girls, and children at heightened risk of close partner and other forms of domestic violence as a result. In 2019, 73 per cent of reported incidents of GBV in the BAY States occurred in the context of intimate partner violence, meaning that, of the married/cohabitating survivors who reported an incident of GBV, in at least 98 percent of the incidents, the perpetrator was their partner (former or current) (GBVIMS Annual Report, 2019). Besides, data on GBV incidents within the context of service provision suggests that 54percent of the new incidents of conflict-related sexual violence in 2019 were perpetrated by security personnel (GBVIMS Annual Report, 2019). Going by the above statistics, incidents of abuse and violence are likely to increase as the armed
security personnel are required to enforce travel restrictions and lockdown measures.
This may pose additional burdens on women and adolescent girls as caregivers to the
children, the sick, and the elderly with chronic diseases in the family, and may increase
their risks of COVID-19 infection. The overcrowded housing conditions in the camps and
some of the host communities may also exacerbate the risks of women and adolescent
girls’ caregivers to COVID-19 infection. The closure of schools may expose girls to
additional care giving roles, which may also heighten their exposure to COVID- 19
infection. The situation of children separated from their caregivers may lead to an
increase in the number of female- headed households, which may add a financial burden
on them and expose them to exploitation and abuse.
Life at Best Development Initiative through this project intends to reach out to 3712 direct beneficiaries which include adolescent girls and women while 10000 indirect beneficiaries comprising of women, men, boys and girls including caregivers and parents.

2 - Activities

LABI during implementation will carry out the following activities in line with the sectorobjectives: • Mitigate risks of infection through ensuring adherence to IPC protocols inservice provision facilities; harmonised COVID-19 and GBV awareness creation andinnovative PSS. • Provide with GBV case management and specialized services(psychosocial services, access to justice, safety options and security mechanisms). Adoptmodalities for transitioning to remote service provision to GBV Survivors in the context ofCOVID 19. • Preposition and distribute dignity kits and other critical material support forwomen and girls; Scale up of the local production and assembly of standardized dignitykits and locally made PPE materials. • Strengthening GBV Sub Sector coordination, GBVmainstreaming and capacity building for partners and government stakeholders. • Designcommunication materials, with key GBV and COVID-19 messages to inform the affectedcommunities and activate community network that include women and youth as keycommunity mobilisers.
