Project Summary

the proposed project aims to contribute to strengthening of two-fold SGBV related prevention, protection and life-saving health care response services & psycho-social support for vulnerable population (men, women, children, adolescents, elderly, PWDs, LGBTIs, refugees and host communities). In line with GBV humanitarian and life- saving standards during the interventions would focus to strengthen two pronged immediate basic and specialized health care services including clinical management of domestic violence/IPV against women and children , rape, elder abuse, PWD abuse as well as psycho-social support in target districts of Punjab, Sindh, KP and Balochistan provinces as well as ICT. In collaboration with Provincial Departments of Health innovative telemedicine (tele-tower network) technique would be launched for provision of specialized GBV related health care, clinical management and psycho-social support services through connectivity with target district level local health facilities. the key interventions are;
1. Sexual and Gender based Violence
facility based Provision of survivor centered life saving clinical management and care services for GBV survivors rape and psycho-social support including specialized and basic services through telemedicine approach integrated with emergencies and humanitarian crisis 1.) Multi-sectoral Coordination and referral mechanisms 2). Training and Orientation of health service providers and community health workers for facility based health care provision and clinical management of SGBV; community based case identification and referral to health facilities and other services 3). provision of telemedicine specialized services for clinical management of GBV and Psycho-social support 4). Provision of PPE and medical supplies to front line health care providers 5). Community Outreach Interventions for Prevention and Protection from SGBV
2. Child Protection:
facility based Provision of survivor centered life saving clinical management and care services for child survivors of domestic violence, rape and psycho-social support including specialized and basic services through telemedicine approach integrated with emergencies and humanitarian crisis 1.) Multi-sectoral Coordination and referral mechanisms 2). Training and Orientation of health service providers and community health workers for facility based health care provision and clinical management of SGBV for Children; community based case identification and referral to health facilities and other services 3). provision of telemedicine specialized services for clinical management of GBV and Psycho-social support for children and their care givers 4). Provision of PPE and medical supplies to front line health care providers 5). Community Outreach and School Health Interventions for Prevention and Protection from SGBV and responsive parenting engaging children, teachers and parents/care givers
