Project Summary

GOALPrime aims to address the urgent WASH (Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene) needs of 500,000 flood- and conflict-affected individuals across 23 Local Government Areas (LGAs) in Borno, Adamawa, and Yobe (BAY) States in 2025. The project targets 30% women, 20% men, 30% girls, and 20% boys, with 60% of beneficiaries being Internally Displaced Persons (IDPs) in host communities and 40% from camps. Additionally, 5% of the total target population will include persons living with disabilities.
In Borno State, GOALPrime is targetting the following LGAs Dikwa, Gubio, Jere, Kaga, Kala Balge (Rann), Konduga, Mafa, Maiduguri (MMC), Monguno, Ngala. In Adamawa State, it includes Girei, Madagali, Numan, Yola North, Yola South, Gombi LGAs. while in Yobe State the following locations would be covered Bade, Damaturu, Fune, Geidam, Gulani, Jakusko, Potiskum, Yunusari LGAs.
The project seeks to improve access to safe water, clean latrines, proper sanitation facilities, and sustainable hygiene practices for flood- and conflict-affected communities. By doing so, the project will enhance public health, reduce waterborne disease risks, and strengthen resilience across targeted populations.
Through the Hygiene Promotion activities of this project, 80,000 households/families will receive hygiene kits and door-to-door hygiene education. Via the Menstrual Hygiene Management, 300,000 vulnerable women and adolescent girls in camps and schools will be educated and provided with reusable sanitary pads and laundry soap. For the provision of Safe Water Supply, 300 damaged boreholes will be rehabilitated to produce 25,000 liters each for safe water on daily basis while 100 Dosatron systems will be mounted on some of the rehabilitated boreholes/water systems for an inline-chlorination of water at the sources, and maintaining the WASH sector standard recommended levels. While for the Sanitation Facilities, 1,000 latrine blocks will be rehabilitated, 400 new blocks will be constructed, and 800 filled latrines will be dislodged. Via the Environmental Management, GOALPrime will distribute a total of 1,000 sanitation kits to support daily environmental maintenance in the targeted LGAs.
The expected outcomes of this project will Improved access to clean and safe water for 500,000 individuals, with a minimum of 15 liters per person daily, Enhanced hygiene practices and knowledge among targeted households, reducing waterborne diseases and other health risks, strengthened community resilience through the establishment and training of WASHCOM teams for sustainable water, sanitation, and hygiene facility management, elevated sanitation standards through the rehabilitation and construction of latrines, improving health and dignity for affected populations.
This strategic project leverages community participation, capacity building, and sector collaboration to ensure sustainable and impactful outcomes for the most vulnerable populations in BAY States.

1 - Needs

According to the Humanitarian Needs Overview (HNO) 2024, 5 million people in the BAY States (Borno, Adamawa, and Yobe) require humanitarian WASH assistance, marking a significant increase in severity compared to the previous year (HNO-2024). Women and girls, who make up 52% of those in need, often face protection issues while fetching water from distant sources. Children, accounting for 60% of the population in need, are highly vulnerable to WASH-related diseases and malnutrition. Persons with disabilities require adaptive WASH infrastructure to maintain their dignity and hygiene (HNO-2024). Access to safe and sufficient water remains limited across nearly all BAY States, affecting IDPs, returnees, and host communities. Seventeen percent of assessed households reported having less than the SPHERE standard of 15 liters per person per day, and 5% of households must walk over an hour round trip to access water (HNO-2024). GOALPrime's project aims to reach a total of 500,000 flood- and conflict-affected individuals in BAY State, including 30% women, 20% men, 30% girls, and 20% boys. Of these, 60% will be IDPs in host communities and 40% will be IDPs from camps, with 5% of the total target being people living with disabilities. The project will provide safe and clean water to 1,736,819 people in need through the rehabilitation, upgrading, and reticulation of water points, water quality analysis, water point chlorination, and the provision of common pipeline services (HNO-2024). These efforts are crucial, especially for women and girls who are primarily responsible for fetching water, exposing them to protection risks when water sources are far from their communities (HNO-2024). In the camps, overcrowding and groundwater challenges have left water supply needs unmet. Borno has nine LGAs with critical/severe water needs, Adamawa has twelve, and Yobe has four, totaling 25 LGAs with severe water needs (HNO-2024). According to the WASH sector gap analysis, 1,778,020 people across 10 LGAs selected by GOALPrime in Borno, Adamawa, and Yobe States are affected by shortages of clean water and poor sanitation and hygiene practices (HNO-2024). The WASH Sector’s latest PiN and HRP 2024 show that 5,044,433 people in the BAY States need WASH services (HNO-2024). GOALPrime's project targets 10 LGAs, including Girei (45,765 people in need), Yola North (29,731), Yola South (41,891), Dikwa (93,406), Jere (338,156), Kala/Balge (33,943), Mafa (61,516), Maiduguri (146,362), Geidam (112,154), and Jakusko (50,929) (HNO-2024). Within these LGAs, 789,673 people need safe drinking water at the community or household level through new construction and rehabilitation of water supply systems, including hand pumps and boreholes (HNO-2024). Additionally, 551,997 people need access to safe water for drinking, cooking, and personal hygiene through maintenance and repair of water system parts, batch chlorination, and provision of water treatment tablets (HNO-2024). Furthermore, 300,000 people require gender-segregated sanitation facilities and services (HNO-2024).

2 - Activities

Activity 1: Map Borehole Gaps in Target LGAs GOALPrime will collaborate with community stakeholders and complementary service providers to map out borehole gaps in specified LGAs. This initiative will identify key boreholes for potential rehabilitation, assess their impact on the surrounding population, and determine the extent of damage in those slated for repair. Activity 2: Rehabilitate and Upgrade Non-Functional Boreholes GOALPrime aims to rehabilitate and upgrade non-functional boreholes in the targeted areas. This will address water scarcity issues and enhance access to clean and safe water for the affected population. Activity 3: Water Point Chlorination and Free Residual Chlorine (FRC) Monitoring GOALPrime will enhance the safety of drinking water by chlorinating rehabilitated and government-owned boreholes in camps and host communities. Automated chlorine pumps will be installed for efficient online chlorination, ensuring proper contact time before beneficiaries access clean water. Free residual chlorine levels will be monitored to maintain water safety. Activity 4: Engage Community-Based Staff for Borehole Operations and Hygiene Promotion GOALPrime will identify, coach, and engage community-based borehole operators (CBBO), water point chlorinators (WPC), community hygiene promoters, and local area mechanics (LAM). These community-based staff will conduct door-to-door hygiene promotion, manage borehole operations, and perform minor maintenance tasks. GOALPrime will provide them with the necessary tools and parts to ensure uninterrupted water supply. Activity 5: Construct Reticulation Lines in Camps, Schools, Health Facilities, and Communities GOALPrime will construct water reticulation networks from rehabilitated boreholes to other locations where community members travel long distances to access water. This will ensure sector standards on waiting time and distance are met. Schools with few water points or damaged facilities will be prioritized for construction or rehabilitation to improve learning conditions and sanitation. Activity 6: Rehabilitation, Dislodge, and Upgrade Latrines GOALPrime will rehabilitate, dislodge, and upgrade non-functional and filled-up latrines in camps, schools, health facilities, and child-friendly spaces. This will provide sanitation facilities that meet sector standards, ensuring people live with dignity and reducing open defecation. GOALPrime will coordinate with other partners to achieve Open Defecation Free (ODF) status across all locations.

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

GOALPrime is committed to integrating protection principles into every strategy, ensuring the safeguarding of beneficiaries and stakeholders throughout project execution. This includes a strong emphasis on preventing gender-based violence (GBV) and implementing a robust Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) framework. Our unwavering commitment prioritizes the safety, dignity, and well-being of both beneficiaries and project participants. Equitable access to services, free from obstacles and biases, is a cornerstone of our approach, tailored to the specific needs of the target population. GOALPrime is dedicated to addressing the needs of particularly vulnerable individuals and groups, including those with disabilities and survivors of GBV. We will implement GBV risk mitigation measures to minimize harm and ensure compliance with the Sphere Humanitarian Core Standards and Imperatives. Adhering to the "Do No Harm" protection principle, our methodologies will focus on avoiding additional risks for beneficiaries. To maintain an environment free of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (SEA), all individuals involved in the project—staff, volunteers, contractors, and vendors—will undergo mandatory PSEA orientation. They must affirm their commitment by signing GOALPrime's Zero Tolerance to SEA Code of Conduct. Reporting protocols for PSEA incidents will be established, with utmost confidentiality maintained for the identity of reporters. Anonymous reporting will be encouraged whenever possible. In alignment with the Inter-Agency mechanism for reporting SEA, GOALPrime will systematically engage with affected populations, implementing confidential complaints and investigation procedures at both systemic and individual agency levels. Following recommendations from the IASC Task Force on PSEA, GOALPrime will actively cooperate with other agencies to create and maintain community-based complaints mechanisms (CBCMs) to prevent and respond to SEA. Awareness campaigns will emphasize that humanitarian assistance is unconditional and not contingent on sexual favors. Project beneficiaries will be informed about how to access appropriate complaints mechanisms, and support will be provided to establish community-based mechanisms if SEA occurs. GOALPrime is committed to ensuring the effectiveness of established CBCMs and promoting inter-agency coordination to facilitate broad and straightforward access for potential complainants. Recognizing the importance of environmental sustainability, GOALPrime will ensure that project approaches and tools do not contribute to environmental degradation. Additionally, climate change integration and routine sensitization on climate change prevention will be incorporated into project activities to foster community awareness and resilience.

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

Throughout the project, GOALPrime is committed to a robust monitoring and feedback system. Monthly monitoring visits will be conducted to project locations to oversee progress. Quarterly Focused Group Discussions (FGDs) in intervention communities will gather comprehensive feedback from diverse groups, including males, females, youth, elders, stakeholders, and people with disabilities. Feedback collected from these processes will be carefully analyzed and utilized for continuous learning for better implementation of the project. To ensure project adherence and effectiveness, routine internal spot checks will be performed, and reviews will be organized periodically. Post Distribution Monitoring (PDM) will be carried out after each distribution, with a particular emphasis on feedback from vulnerable communities. This feedback will inform ongoing efforts to enhance service delivery on a monthly basis. Recognizing the importance of open communication, GOALPrime will establish a 24/7 beneficiaries communication and feedback mechanism accessible across all project locations. This platform will be dedicated to addressing queries and complaints promptly, fostering a responsive and accountable project environment.
