3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

Protection will be at the center of the response, and all assistance will be planned and implemented so as to promote the safety, dignity, and rights of affected people. The Child-friendly space and the women-friendly spaces will be guided by the safety audit. In addition, the community will be consulted in the establishment of the center and later provide feedback on the ways the centers are safe for the community. ARCO will consult the Protection Cluster within in the specific area of intervention to have access to the Protection Analysis which identifies threats, vulnerable groups, and existing capacities. In addition, training will be provided to the child protection, women support committees and ARCO protection staff on protection mainstreaming. To create a proper understanding of identified threats, the most vulnerable groups, and the existing capacities of the affected populations as well as services and resources already available. This will help us to identify real and potential risks that may arise in protection programs. Will confirm the threats, vulnerabilities, and negative coping strategies identified in the Protection Analysis Report by consulting with the affected population of each area of intervention. In addition, through analysis, ARCO identifies with beneficiary’s preventive and mitigation measures with the objective of reducing the threats and the vulnerabilities and increasing the existing capacities. In addition, the community through the community-based protection structures will have full participation in the designing and implementation of the program. Furthermore, the community will have full participation including a decision-making role in the program. AGD approach will be used to ensure the inclusiveness of the program to all of the community in the area. Active and effective participation of affected people both in planning, response, and monitoring processes will be ensured through community discussion. ARCO will promote peace by ensuring that all community groups including Refugees ,IDPs, host community members, returnees, pastoralists, and varying tribal and ethnic groups have equal access to project services by selecting project sites in areas accessible to the different groups and hiring diverse staff. This minimizes the risk of conflict due to competition over social services. The possibility of discrimination by locally hired staff against beneficiaries who do not hold the same tribal or political affiliations is mitigated by ensuring that program teams that consist of combinations of locally hired national staff representing all ethnic groups, national staff hired from outside the programmatic areas.
