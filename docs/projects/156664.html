Project Summary

The project aims to increase the utilization of GBV prevention and protection services by women, men and children in selected districts for the improved protection status of women/girls as well as supportive attitude by all community members for the prevention and management of GBV.
Objectives:
1. Availability of comprehensive GBV services increased
2. Awareness and knowledge among women, girls and their families (including men and boys) on availability of comprehensive GBV services increased
1- Activities targeting women and girls are conducted in women and girls safe spaces in both: Idleb/Areha and Aleppo/Afrin as well as in the camps(Deer baloot camp in Afrin) also we will conduct men and boys' activities the young adults, adults, youth, in other facilities through a mobile team, the mobile team well target a rurals in both location(Axel, Iscan, Hamam, Koran, Haltan, Shiekh Abdulrahmanand Tal saloor in Afrin, Shnan Kafrlata, Sarja, Benin, Kafar zeeba Nahle and Korien in Areha)
Generally, the activities will be the same in the centers and outside it with stress on the needs of IDPs and hosting community's differences.
2- Increased awareness and knowledge among women, girls, their families and communities on availability of comprehensive GBV services would be achived via Orientation of existing networks (youth clubs, mothers groups, user groups, watch groups, child and youth clubs) on GBV, Establish resource and information points at community level and Develop and disseminate IEC materials
These activities will not only provide women with protection, psychosocial support and build their skills, but also strengthen the protective environment for women and girls by building individual capacities and resilience, as well as enhancing the overall community support networks. Activities will be based on needs assessments with women from adults and youth to assess their interests, and will identify facilitators from the IDPs and host community for all the gender needs for males and females which are from women, men, boys and girls taking in much consideration the youth needs and adolecents. Activities include vocational, recreational or cultural activities. The program will also represent entry points for identification and referral of GBV concerns. As appropriate, information dissemination regarding services available for survivors and GBV prevention activities will be further integrated.
The specilized services includes Kase managment and referals.
The prevention services includes , awareness raising, life skills sessions, vocational training.

Needs

The humanitarian impact of the Syria crisis remains deep and far-reaching, with the population exposed to significant protection risks. Hundreds of thousands of civilians have been killed orinjured. At least 5.2 million are displaced in neighbouring countries and 6.1 million displaced within Syria; 13.1 million people remain in need of humanitarian assistance, including 5.6 millionpeople with acute needs. After the last movements of displacement toward North-West Idlib, people from women, men, adults, elderly, and young adults and from girls and boys who are from 9 to 17 in addition to the youth and adolescents, were forced to live in a bad situations both in camps and in the new shelter , the previously condensed camps became more crowded, new camps were rapidly and haphazardly established mainly in Euphrates shield and Olive branch areas. , even local communities as usually in such circumstances became affected by shortage of resources and overcrowding as most of them have to host their relatives from nearby villages leading to decrease space and privacy.With the intensification of war lots of causalities took place, more men have to be away for a long time either because of their involvement defending their houses from militant who are involved in such actions or travelling away as breadwinners.So more and more houses are becoming women led and\or under pressure, making women overstressed and more vulnerable. As we all know in most societies, women have limited space to meet, and public spaces are often inhabited largely by men. Traditionally, women’s responsibilities include taking care of children, cooking, carrying out household chores, and generally looking after the family. While these roles may change during crisis, where women may find themselves working or becoming the breadwinner, they remain responsible for the household nevertheless.The last OCHA updates on IDP movements show that people newly displaced in affected areas of Idleb governorate have increased due to fighting and mass shelling. The most Types of GBV identified by community members are respectivly:Intimate Partner Violence, Forced and early child marrige and Forced drop-out of school, further more, No focused support services for survivors of GBV exist in the new cammers communities but a small and inadequate psychosocial support, There is little to no information on the causes and consequences of GBV or importance of seeking services in a timely manner available in the community, key informants indicated that no awareness raising campaigns on the issue of gender based violence or related services had previously or were currently taking place. legal services, or police protection is not currently available for women and girls in these communities. In the Syrian context, women from young adults, adults or even eldery have become more isolated as a consequence of the crisis. Their mobility has been curbed significantly. Women and their family members reported having limited movement of women and girls outside the home due to fear of sexual violence, harassment, and indiscriminate attacks.In addition to, Young boys and men who are not involved in military actions have no alternatives, facing a lot of pressures including change in role, which may increase domestic violence They are not really aware of it.
