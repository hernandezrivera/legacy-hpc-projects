Project Summary

overall objective:
Provision of legal services in the context of protection from domestic violence (GBV) IN IDLEB for 288 women \girls in one year 2023
Launching a legal awareness reference and guide for the Violence Department and the Prevention and Awareness Department
And the implementation of 12 legal awareness sessions regarding legal papers at every stage in the lives of girls and women for 12 women and girls in each session
Implementation of 12 sessions on the merciful separation of domestically abused women and women in the local community
Targeting 12 women and girls per session
Financially helping 40 women in court who need legal consultancy
the project will take place in three location (idleb \ariha\ehsim)
12session in Idleb
6session in Ariha
6session in Ehsem
our work team cosist of (4 lawyers and 9 front line worker)

3 - Needs

Domestic violence, intimate partner violence, exacerbation of social stigmas about divorced women, and its impact on the lives of children lack awareness of the most important identification papers that Women and girls must obtain during their lifetime.Based on previous reports 2022 issued by voices from Syria And reports relief WED The legal awareness and financial support for women in the courts is not available with any educational organization in northwestern SyriaAnd that the legal violations associated with the de-facto forces have become necessary to move towards empowering women legally against gender-based violence.

4 - Implementation of activities

At the beginning of the year, we will complete the previous work through a team of lawyers, partnering with multiple NGOs to build a reference document for raising legal and social awareness related to nonviolent separation in the first and second months. Launching an awareness program for 288 women and girls about legal and social protection produced earlier in Edlib, Ariha, and Ehsim in 12 sessions through 8 months. Provide financial help for 40 women who are registered in Edlib, Ehsim, and Ariha courts and need legal support.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

The reality that girls between the ages of 14 to 17 live is full of risks related to their gender, as they were born a girl who was imposed on social restrictions and norms that prevent her from disclosing any sexual, psychological or material abuse in general. And the attacks from humanitarian workers, as PSEA explains in its reports, and from the family or the surrounding community...


8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Our main goal is to raise awareness of the disguised sexual abuse that girls can be exposed to. and how they could deal with this situation by: Awareness sessions to prevent sexual exploitation and abuse by humanitarian workers for the beneficiaries of the project 288 women and girls 12 sessions synchronized with the sessions allocated to the project Training the staff team on the concepts of preventing sexual exploitation and abuse within the programs of the PSEAH network delivery of educational materials to beneficiaries


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Exposure to bombing (inside Syria): Communicate with observatories through wireless fists to receive warnings in case of flights and to go to shelters. 5 (maximum degree) 3 (medium probability) being bombed (inside Syria) New displacement (Idlib): Establishing and completing activities in the new area, where our activities are flexible to change general thinking and not specific people 3 (maximum degree) 3 (medium probability) new displacement Continuation of the state of emergency due to the Corona epidemic: During the first three months of the year, online training can be done or the workshops can be divided into two machines for each training to take into account social distancing to prevent infection, with the presence of 10 people per training or whatever the need arises. 3 (maximum) 2 (medium probability) Continuation of the state of emergency due to the Corona epidemic Financial risks: It can be solved in two ways, using employee salaries at a certain percentage The other way is to inform the finances of any financial emergency and request an additional amount 3 (maximum degree) 2 (medium probability) Programmatic risks: always trying to coordinate the appropriate time for any activity with the beneficiaries themselves to mitigate any program failure 2 (maximum degree) 2 (medium probability
