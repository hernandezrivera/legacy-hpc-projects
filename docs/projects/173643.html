Project Summary

The project aims at providing life-saving WASH interventions to a total of 55,331 people in eight selected localities in White Nile & Blue Nile states.Women and girls form majority (58%) of the projects’ beneficiaries and ADD will put a special emphasis to address their urgent basic needs and interests in accessing WASH related services by mainstreaming gender and protection in the interventions and involvement of women
and girls in all the stages of project cycle from planning to monitoring . Moreover, presence of project-specific ‘Gender Action Plan’ goes beyond isolated attention on females and analysis of power within a
context of aid delivery to include concerns for men, boys, elderly and people with disability. Apart from a recent inter-agency (UN, line ministries, ADD, NGOs) assessment to the two states, numerous consultations with the IDPs, returnees and host population have led to selection of some activities over others. As usual, ADD will work in close collaboration with the relevant line ministries (mainly ministry of health and ministry of irrigation and water resources), HAC, the community based organizations (CBOs), WES, and the WASH sector lead and partners all through the project's implementation. The proposed activities under this project include :maintenance and upgrading of water systems, hygiene promotion sessions, distribution of WASH non food items( NFIs), household latrine construction through the community-led total sanitation (CLTS) approach, training for water users' committees/community health workers, etc. ADD will be primarily responsible for implementation of the activities while the CBOs will help in the community mobilization activities. ADD will also strengthen the target population’s capacity to self-manage the WASH interventions so that they can still have basic services with little assistance from NGOs. A project specific Monitoring and Evaluation (M&E) framework will guide the achievement of activities and results through SMART indicators. ADD will monitor this project closely and commit to share information n the 5 Ws with the cluster lead as needed. The total budget of the project is 170,000 USD.

1 - Needs

According to the HNO 2019, an estimated 1.3 million people are at risk of migrationdue to water resource depletion because of climatic shocks and could require watertrucking. Water supply infrastructure will also require upgrading and frequent repair. Watersource depletion and floods can have spill-over multiplier effects, including diseaseoutbreak and displacement. To avert disease outbreak, there will be a need for improvedenvironmental sanitation and personal hygiene practices. Of the 3.5 million people in needof WASH assistance, 2.1 million are IDPs and the remainders are residents in areas withpoor access to WASH facilities such as in eastern Sudan, conflict-affected people fromthe Kordofan region, White and Blue Nile States refugees or returnees. Over one third ofSudan’s population is considered vulnerable to the impact of climate shocks. Theanticipated continued impact of climatic shocks on WASH includes water resourcedepletion, disease outbreak and further displacement due to conflict over water resources.Sector analysis shows that over 40 per cent of hand pumps and 30 per cent of motorizedwater schemes in Sudan are at high risk of running dry. Increased operation andmaintenance costs will place a higher burden on vulnerable communities.
