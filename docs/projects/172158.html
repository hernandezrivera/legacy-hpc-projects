Project Summary

The proposed action targets 10,000 individuals/beneficiaries (2,000HH) in Chipinge (7,500 individuals, 1,500 HH) and Chimanimani (2,500 individuals, 500 HH) who are still staying in emergency shelters or staying with host families and have not yet reconstructed their houses followed by households staying in makeshift shelters because the family houses were destroyed or damaged by Cyclone Idai and finally, overcrowded households with compromised sleeping arrangements.
Post cyclone livelihood restoration has been affected by severe food insecurity and harsh economic environment limiting government to support cyclone affected houses with shelter and also COVID-19 protection. The proposed action will provide material for transitional shelter construction and rehabilitation. A set of NFI that include a shelter repair/maintenance kit. The action will provide capacity building on shelter construction to builders and carpenters making use of locally available resources. The process will involve provision of shelter repair tool kits.

1 - Activities

• Beneficiary identification, selection and registration, giving priority to households still staying in emergency shelters or staying with host families and have not yet reconstructed their house followed by households staying in makeshift shelters because the family house was destroyed or damaged by Cyclone Idai and finally, overcrowded households with compromised sleeping arrangements. • Sensitization of selected beneficiaries on the type of shelter that is going to be constructed in line with the international SPHERE standards and the construction materials that they are going to receive.• Mobilze beneficiaries to prepare appropriate sites for the shelter and organize local resources needed such as concrete and stones.• Identification and registration of builders and carpenters to construct the transitional shelters.• Training of local builders and carpenters• Procurement of shelter construction materials• Distribution of materials to beneficiaries. • Monitoring construction of shelters ensuring adherence to SPHERE standards for safety and security• Establishment of a feedback mechanism systems.

8 - Accountability to Affected People
8.1 - Describe how your project was designed in consultation with communities? How will you incorporate communities in project implementation and what feedback mechanisms do you have in place?

During the designing of the project, a consultative methodology will be used through which:• A feasibility study will be carried out to first establish the priorities, capacities and recommendations of the prospective community beneficiaries. • The community will formulate committees responsible for every day running of the project including among others facilitation of distribution of inputs in close collaboration with project team and resolving disputes as they arise with the support of community leaders.
