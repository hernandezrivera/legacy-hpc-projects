Project Summary

Under the proposed project WFP will provide lifesaving nutrition support under CMAM to moderately acute malnourished children 6-59 months of age and acutely malnourished PLW through Targeted supplementary feeding programme.
a) Identification and registration of malnourished children 6-59 months and PLW as per CMAM criteria under TSFP.
b) Provision of Achamum to treat moderately acute malnourished children of 6-59 months and Maamta to treat acutely malnourished PLW.
c) Improve access to nutrition treatment sites and indirectly through MIYCN, health and hygiene care practices by educating and providing awareness to mothers/caretakers.
