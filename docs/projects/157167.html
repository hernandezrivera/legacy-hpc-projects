Project Summary

The proposed project is intended to contribute to improving the health status and nutritional wellbeing of refugees through provision of basic health and nutritional services in five PHC centers across the Locality of El Mairam. The project will target 12,000 individuals (5760 male and 6240 female including 2100 under 5 year-old children) from South Sudan refugees and host community in El Mairam Locality of West Kordofan state.
There is only one functioning health center in El Mairam town serving both the refugees and host community 12,000 with very limited capacity in terms of facilities, equipment and staff. There are other four health centers in the admin units (El Heraika, El Odam, Umm Deries and Umm Senainaiya), not functioning and without even health or nutrition staff. The buildings are well constructed, need some repairs, equipment and furniture and then support with trained staff to deliver the services.
The proposed intervention will aim to rehabilitate the existing centers, provide essential equipment, furniture and train local staff as well as provide support for operation cost and incentives for staff to ensure continuity of services. The project will deliver RH comprehensive package, case management and vaccination services as well as awareness to refugees and host community to increase knowledge on health interventions. It will also provide support to PLW, children and mother groups for supplementary feeding, MUAC and IYCF programme.

Needs

Sudan hosts the largest population of South Sudanese refugees in region, with more than 846,204 individuals reported across the country (as Sudan population Dashboard on 15th January 2019). Among these, nearly 386,471 fled to Sudan since the outbreak of conflict in South Sudan in 2013, and have been registered or recorded at reception areas by UNHCR and the Government’s Commissioner of Refugees (COR). An additional estimated 467,000 South Sudanese have been recorded by various government and UN sources, most of whom were living in Sudan prior to the conflict in South Sudan and are recognized by the Government as refugees because they cannot safely return home. These individuals are yet to be officially registered by UNHCR and COR. Approximately 79% of the current South Sudanese refugee population are living outside of official camps, alongside host communities in more than 100 out-of-camp settlements in South Kordofan, West Kordofan, East Darfur, South Darfur and North Darfur.For refugees in El Mairam of West Kordofan, they are extremely living in one of these out of camp settlements in remote and underdeveloped area, where resources, infrastructures and basic services are very limited. Ministry of Health reports indicate the gaps mainly in low EPI coverage, RH limited supplies and equipment as well as poor skilled reproductive health staff in delivering standard services especially in El Mairam. The report also indicates low coverage for health promotion, community knowledge and awareness on lifesaving health interventions. The report indicated that only 47% of mothers Including pregnant women in selected communities in targeted localities have comprehensive knowledge and capacity to seek appropriate neonatal, child, and maternal health care, including HIV/AIDS and 28% of primary health care facilities providing treatment services for SAM (RWP: 1.1), 136,838 of children 6-59 months affected by Severe Acute Malnutrition admitted to treatment (RWP: 1.1.2), and 2% of mothers support groups providing Infant and Young Child Feeding (IYCF) counseling services in target localities (RWP: 2.1.1)This situation pose them to insecurity and isolation and they can hardly access regular humanitarian response by partners. Due to this critical situation, there is ultimate need to strengthen the provision of limited government services to allow refugees attain self-reliance. The proposed health and nutrition intervention in El Mairam Locality will assist in provision of more sustained support to health and nutrition services for both refugees and host communities

Activities

Responding to the highlighted needs, the proposed project will provide comprehensive PHC services (integrated health and nutrition) to targeted 12,000 people (5760 male and 6240 female) located in 5 health centers’ catchment areas. The project activities are categorized in 2 outputs as follows: Under output 1 Increased access to health services for targeted population in El Mairam, the key activities are, Coordinate with SMoH to deploy and support health staff on incentives basis to ensure continuity of urgently needed services, capacity building (training and awareness sessions) for 50 medical staff, health providers, mothers & caregivers, in case management, new born care, standard precaution and infection prevention control, IMCI, EMOC (emergency of obstetric care), rehabilitation of 2 health centers located at El Odam and Umm Deries, provision of furniture, sufficient drugs and equipment for 5 PHCs in El Mairam, Provision of the health hardware and software services (curative and awareness) via the PHC centers, conduct EPI campaigns. Under output 2 Increased access to nutritional services for malnourished groups among refugees and host communities in El Mairam, the proposed activities are: Coordinate with SMoH to deploy nutrition staff on incentives basis to ensure continuity of urgently needed services, capacity building for nutrition staff, community outreach volunteers in MUAC screening, IYCF, CMAM, conduct MUAC screening campaigns on monthly, provision of adequate and routine nutrition medicines and supplies i.e. SFP & OTP supplies ‘RUSF and RUTF” in coordination with SMoH/UNICEF) and essential nutrition furniture, Provision of the nutrition hardware and software services (supplementary & therapeutic support via distribution of RUTF and RUSF and awareness/counselling on IYCF) via the OTP&SFP centers to 3,960 children of 6-59 month and 4,440 caregivers, refer the complicated malnutrition cases that require curative support to nearest health

Impact on environment/natural resources

Conserving natural resources is a basic requirement for sustainable development and improving the quality of human life. To assure this principle is adopted to target project activities, the project will aim to not reverse the trend towards resource degradation, and will consider giving greater attention and priority to ecological aspects. The project activities were designed in a way as not to impact the environment and will not affect the geographical and context of both the problem statement and nature of target groups. The activities are planned to dig deep into the roots of the problem and search for solutions. The limited health services and the lack of trained medical staff is the critical causes for beneficiaries’ sufferings and the provision of integrated services will lead to entire solution for the causes that lead to permanent and sustainable services.This trend support inclusion of positive environmental aspects and sustainable change.
