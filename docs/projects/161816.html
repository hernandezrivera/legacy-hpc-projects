Project Summary

This project will aim to operationalize a comprehensive, multi-sectoral response to Mafa Town’s Internally Displaced People (IDPs, returnees and Host Communities (HC) using the Nexus approach developed in Nigeria . It intends to address the root causes of the crises, reduce its impact and answer immediate needs.

1 - Needs

, before the NE crisis, State Agencies were struggling to deliver sufficient services to the population. Ministry of Health (MoH) through the State Public Health and Development Agency (SPHDA) were among them. Fluctuant and inequitable medical staff presence, inconstant availability of drugs and consumables, variable level of health data management quality were known problems in MoH facilities. In consequence, health seeking behaviours of northern east Nigeria population were and is still centred on traditional beliefs with lack of information and low level of trust in a failing health system.To illustrate the situation prior to NE crisis, survey supported by international organization in 2006-08 state that “Nigeria which constitutes just one percent of the world population, accounts for 13% of the world maternal and under-five mortality” . Also, only 38% of pregnant women in Nigeria are assisted by a skilled birth attendant during delivery. Infant and young children’s vulnerability is heightened as they are often left in the sole care of already vulnerable women. Since 2009, total or partial destruction health facilities, displacement of population including Public-Servants, increased pressure of population mainly in LGA capitals worthen the situation. The recent Multi sectoral need assessment done by REACH is highlighting and illustrating perception of health services and need by the population including host communities (here called nondisplaced).

2 - Activities

Provision of health care to PLW through a Mobile Hub in GGSS camp in Mafa TownContinuous training and capacity building of Health Community Volunteers/Mobilisers and MoH staff26 N/A N/ACase finding and case management for Pregnant and Lactating Women (PLW), infant and childrenProvision of preventive consultations (ANC, PNC, outpatient follow up after referral etc.)Mother Groups (Mafa Village and Town)Deployment of a dedicated referral pathway team 24/7 to tertiary institutions for PLW, infant and children with complications.Set-up of a community based early warning system involving community leaders and community health volunteers. Organisation of a dedicated Quality and Improvement mechanism to referral pathway activity. Implying experience capitalization and analysis, health data management and analysis ; advocacy at Governance platform level.
