1 - Needs

Protracted political and economic stability has resulted in a significant deterioration in the provision of basic services, including telecommunications and electricity in Libya. The country currently suffers from frequent electricity cuts and a lack of fuel needed to power generators that directly impact the reliance on communication networks. An Emergency Telecommunications Sector (ETS) needs assessment survey conducted in mid-2020 highlighted the need for a strong reliable security telecommunications network in the country to enable the response community to carry out their lifesaving work safely and securely. The ETS plans to continue assessing and monitoring the needs on the ground to address critical communications gaps. As a service cluster, the ETS targets the entire humanitarian community.

2 - Activities

ETS will provide dedicated inter-agency coordination and information management services to ensure a smooth, effective response and to share regular operational information to support advocacy efforts and decision-making. To achieve this, the team will deploy one dedicated coordinator to manage the operation throughout 2021, one ICT specialist and one CFM manager. ETS will assess and explore the potential ways it can provide communications services, such as internet connectivity, to affected communities which will be achieved through close collaboration with the Communication with Communities group and other relevant stakeholders as well as engaging with affected communities themselves.
