Project Summary

Following the Beirut blast, many schools and families lost their ability to connect to learning and part of the infrastructure was severely damaged. The project is to address the short term as well as the mid and long term educational needs of teachers, children and the families affected by this tragedy. The purpose is to support the teaching and learning process of teachers, children (ages 3 to 14) and families affected by Beirut explosion. The project will provide teachers with PSS videos explicitly prepared to assist them in coping with the Beirut blast, distance learning and use of technology entailed. The project will also provide schools with tablets so students can check them out to ensure that distance learning is accessible to all as well will provide the households with connectivity bundles to enable learners follow up on their distance learning. In addition to providing learners with age related educational kits which include materials and games to support the distance learning process amid the effects of the blast. The project will be in coordination with the sector coordination group, UNESCO, MEHE, UNICEF and Beirut Schools District management targeting public and semi-private schools.

1 - Needs

The Beirut blast comes at a difficult time for Lebanon, amid an increase in the number of infections with the novel coronavirus, an economic downfall, and political unrest.The total damage from the blast is estimated at between 10 and 15 billion dollars. Around 159 Beirut schools have been damaged or destroyed in the blast and children have been deeply traumatized by the level of destruction and loss. In spite of tremendous damage caused to the buildings and the trauma as an aftermath to the explosion, we need to keep prioritizing learning and education for children. The launching of the academic year is set for mid-October and many schools and families lost their ability to connect to learning since part of the infrastructure was severely damaged.

2 - Activities

- Produce PSS videos for teachers to help them cope with the blast, improved active distance learning environment and use of technology entailed. The videos will be disseminated on social media and via WhatsApp groups of teachers.- Procure laptops to be provided for public and semi-private school teachers.- Procure tablets for the schools to lend out to children to access learning remotely.- Provide households of children enrolled in public and semi-private schools who lost ability to connect to learning, in areas affected by the blast, with tablets and connectivity as needed.- Provide children enrolled in public and semi-private schools with educational kits which include materials and games to support in the distant learning process.
