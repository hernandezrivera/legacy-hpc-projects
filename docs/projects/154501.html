Project Summary

Introduction: This project is designed with the aim of providing lifesaving emergency integrated healthcare and Nutrition service using the essential clinical package. This is in line with overarching strategic objectives for 2019 humanitarian response plan;
SO1: Save lives by providing timely and integrated multi-sector assistance and services to reduce acute needs among the most vulnerable women, men, girls and boys
SO2: Protect vulnerable women, men, girls and boys through provision of specialized and integrated services. The project will be implemented in compliant to the Health cluster strategy 2019; Provide Lifesaving emergency healthcare access to vulnerable populations focusing on the major causes of morbidity and mortality.
Project Location: WES; Ezo, Mundri East a Counties. CES: Yei and Morobo Counties. EES; Kapoeta East County
Rational of Clinical packages selected: Health Action AiD has selected to implement this project through;
Five (5 ) Mobile clinics
Seven (7) PHCUs
Four (4) PHCCs
The selected packages are in line with cluster objective; ‘’ Improve access and scale up responsiveness’’. The design therefore will increase service coverage, improve access, and enhance referral pathway, increase efficiency and effectiveness of service delivery among vulnerable population in areas where Health Action AiD (HAA) has presence and active projects. HAA emergence intervention has been supported with medical supplies from WHO, UNFPA and the Global Fund through the National Malaria Control Program (NMCP). The project therefore fits into already existing framework of operation and support from partners and it’s intended to enhance scalability of Health care Intervention.

Needs

Based on the September 2018 IPC analysis, The level of acute malnutrition is attributed to severe food insecurity, poor access to health and nutrition services, high morbidity, extremely poor diets and poor sanitation and hygiene. Epidemiological Update W42 2018 (Oct15- Oct21) reported ’’An Ebola virus disease alert was reported from Yambio on 21 October 2018 while IDSR Bulletin RSS_wk 37-2018 recorded Counties in Equatoria with malaria trends that exceed the threshold . In-depth cholera epidemiological report for South Sudan UNICEF April 2018, ‘’ The state of Eastern Equatoria and Juba city in particular seem to play a role in amplification and diffusion of cholera outbreaks’ IRNA report Mar 8th 2018, Greater Mundri is in dire need of emergency health services due to huge gaps in healthcare service accessibility. Out of 13 Health facilities visited, only 2 are functional serving an estimated population of 29494. These reports clearly indicates Access to life saving emergency health care services for the county populations are limited and huge gaps exist because Infrastructures were destroyed. This predisposed IDPs and host communities to high morbidity and mortality due to preventable diseases and under-fives children to preventable outbreaks. the burden of the common illnesses like malaria, diarrhea, pneumonia, ARI, skin infections, eye infections, ear infections, blindness, cough, skin diseases, worm infestation, infant and maternal deaths, STIs, chest/back pains, arthritis. This project will be providing lives saving interventions of essential clinical health packages through, 5 Rapid Mobile teams, 7 PHCUs and 4 PHCC. Rapid mobile response: Community mobilization and sensitization on diseases prevention, health promotion, immunization, and antenatal and postnatal services , screening for Malnutrition for under 5, pregnant and lactating mothers in initiated at community level and management of MAM, RDT for malaria and screening for uncomplicated diseases, active case finding for presumptive TB, provider Initiative Counseling and Testing for HIV, distribution of folic Acid to pregnant mothers Vitamin A supplements to under 5 and deworming. PHCUs: screening for malnutrition with MUAC and management of MAM, screening of pregnant and lactating mothers for malnutrition and management MAM through the OPT, outpatient consultation for common communicable diseases and effective case management, MCH(ante natal care, uncomplicated normal delivery), referral of complicated deliveries to PHCC, Routine EPI and Mass vaccination, Provision of MHPSS services for people in need, referral of SAM to PHCC, condom distribution, family planning services, management of SBVs, routine health Education and conduct refresher trainings to old facility staff on HIV & AIDs and vaccination. PHCC: screening for STI’s and case management, management of complicated MAM, SAM, inpatient care, outpatient consultation, maternal & new born health. Basic new born care resuscitation, warmth and eye prophylaxis, clean cord care, early and exclusive breast feeding, basic obstetric care MVA and post abortion care., management of SGBV (PEP, treatment of STI, Wound care), surveillance of epidemiological diseases and referral services to the County Hospital

Does the project provide beneficiaries with information?

Yes

How does the project provide beneficiaries with information?

Health Action AiD will conduct consultation with local authorities, communities and other stakeholders in the areas of operation. project information will be disseminated to all the interest groups using the local available means based on specific context. the culture of engaging the community from the initiation of the project in order to facilitate ownership and social acceptance. stakeholders workshops will be organized. There will be close collaboration with the County Health Departments, community leaders, women groups, youth and representatives of beneficiaries. the local ways shall be the medium through which communities will be encouraged to encouraged to expressed their concerns, views and provide regular feedback in a regular structured modality. other reasonable modalities will also be considered such as the use of the community score cards. These forms part of the project performance monitoring and reporting.

Are messages context-specific or/and tailored?

Yes

Briefly describe how

Yes, Health Action AiD will conduct assessments, participate in IRNA, surveys and communicate information to specific interest groups based on relevance. assessment reports will be disseminated to the CHDs, Donors and the community. specific models for transmitting information shall be adopted based on standard practices. for example, SGBV information shall be communicated to victims by using the protection code of conduct. Focus groups discussions will also be conducted. community mobilization, IEC campaigns shall be used to disseminate information to the population. quarterly project reviews shall be used as platforms for monitoring performance, adjusting procedures and reviewing appropriate plans to ensure timely attention is paid to address project needs

Protection from sexual exploitation and abuse

Health Action Aid has a Protection policy . all staff will be subjected to training of the protection policy. this will be complemented by other policies from the protection cluster and the sphere standards. whistle blowing policies will also be utilized. identified cases will be investigated, victims counselled and appropriate measures taken to address the issues.

Protection Risk Analysis?

Yes

Briefly describe how

This project is design with a considerable attention to GBV risk factors and thus strive to ensure that participation of men and women in all activities is emphasized for inclusive decision making at all levels . This is done through the incorporation of participatory approach in the project. the project will ensure that; GBV risks factors in facilities, latrines and other infrastructures are thoroughly assessed, the sitting and design reduce such risks, safety and protection of women the protection is ensured in the course of performing their socially construed duties . FGDs will be conducted with men, women, boys and girls on consulting them in detail implementation this is because concerns over safety, privacy pr dignity while using sanitary facilities can lead to the facilities not being used or only being used during hours of darkness

Barriers to Access?

Yes

Briefly describe how

Hard to reach areas will be accessed through the use of rapid mobile response and outreach programs . medical supplies will be pre-positioned in these areas during the dry season. mass community mobilization will be conducted to ensure Information reach the target population. This includes messages about the types of the project, the target beneficiaries, mode of intervention, project timeframe, distribution schedules, complaints redress desk and the use of the community score cards.
