Project Summary

The North West and South West Regions of Cameroon have been in an armed conflict since 2017 leading to the weakening in the functioning of basic services including the civil service activities of municipal councils. During the past years of violent confrontation between Non-State Armed Groups (NSAGs) and state security agents, there have been burning and destruction of civil documents of birth certificate, identity cards amongst others.
During the eight years of the conflict, personnel of outreach civil status center within the communities and health institutions that have died, retired or forced to escape by violence have not been replaced. This has led to reduced coverage for a service that was functioning far below required standard. Similarly, the ongoing action by government to digitalize the civil status sector has been paralyzed by the conflict and only the regional office is functioning.
The conflict has engendered intense security checked through road blocks and occasional community raids. The non-possession of the national identity card makes it impossible for those who have either lost theirs or have attained the age to have one during the period of hostilities to freely travel as they should be. It has equally made them vulnerable to exploitation by state security officers and others vicious actors.
Many young girls have remained trapped in conflict related sexual exploitation particularly in the rural communities because the lack these important civil document that can facilitate their free movement. Similarly, the non-possession of the civil documents of birth certificate and national identity card has limiting consequences on individuals concern to enter into a contract, get employed or even report cases of abuse of their rights to state security agents. IDPs without birth certificates or ID cards cannot take action against those who abuse them.
Birth registration guarantees children to a name and nationality and eliminates statelessness. It also ensures the enjoyment of the right to inheritance as it denotes recognition by parents especially for products of couples living in a traditional marriage.
The project shall facilitate the enjoyment of all rights associated with the Cameroon citizenship, strengthen the confidence and self-esteem of mature beneficiaries while enhancing their security.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The security situation in the North West Region is extremely challenging for young males without identification document and/or birth certificate. Many young people remain trapped in some localities without the ability to move freely as a result. Our project seeks to facilitate the movement of young people and equally ensure the rights of children to a name and nationality within the conflict regions through the establishment of civil documents (birth certificates and ID Cards). It shall also support some children to acquire birth certificates and avoid statelessness.
