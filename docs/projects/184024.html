Project Summary

This project (HA and local partner organizations) intends to provide life- saving specialized quality GBV services, for GBV survivors and vulnerable women and girls (with a focus on women and girls with disabilities and adolescent girls).
The project will focus on most vulnerable IDPs, returnees and host communities vulnerable to GBV and other protection concerns in South Kordofan and Blue Nile state, Specifically, Al-Kurmuk , At Tadamon, Dilling and Rashad localities by providing them with access to services, information about GBV prevention and response, and specific material support and capacity building to reduce GBV vulnerability.
Key interventions will include:
1- Conduct 4 GBV assessments in 4 localities at Blue Nile and South Kordofan including Al-Kurmuk , At Tadamon, Dilling and Rashad
2- Establishment of two women center in Al-Kurmuk and At Tadamon at Blue Nile state in addition to one Rashad and rehabilitation other in Dilling.
2- Reactivate and support 4 community protection networks (CPNs) in 4 target localities (Al-Kurmuk , At Tadamon, Dilling and Rashad) including support Conduct (48) regular meetings with the members of the Community Protection Networks. (12 meeting for each CPN)
3- Conduct (20) training sessions targeting community leaders, religious leaders, NGOs/CBOs, government on GBV prevention ( 5 training for each locality).
4- Organize 4 awareness raising campaigns with local authorities and communities commemorating international days such as 16 Days of Activism and Human Rights .
5- Provide training to women and girls on vocational skills and support them with startup capital for IGAs target 80 women's (20 per locality)
8- Distribution of 400 comprehensive dignity kits that contain menstrual hygiene materials, soap, underwear, and information on available GBV services, including where and how to access those services as well as messages on PSEA. Dignity kits also will include items that may help mitigate GBV risks such as lights and culturally appropriate clothing items. (100 per women center)

1 - Needs

The Al-Kurmuk locality in BNS and Dilling and Locality in SK are high priority areas in need of humanitarian assistance. HA-UK has conducted a needs assessment in the proposed project areas indicates that GBV incidents against children are not adequately reported due to stigma and cultural issues, while various reports confirm GBV/SV incidences and prevalence among girls and women in South Kordofan and Blue Nile. The current humanitarian situation in these localities is already characterized by extreme vulnerability and the protracted crisis and displacement further exacerbates the situation in Dilling and Al-Kurmuk localities resulting in a high risk of violence, abuse and exploitation especially among the most vulnerable group being children and women. Prevention of and response to GBV including provision of psychosocial support services, are priority areas of the protection sector. Other urgent identified concerns include the need to respond to psychosocial trauma, separation of children from parents/guardians and safe access to basic services. Gaps in protection monitoring and population movement tracking also lead to untimely and inadequate assistance. The aim is to increase response capacities of government and local communities to prevent and respond to GBV (legal, medical, psychosocial support and counseling) with a special emphasis on health response as the first entry point for GBV survivors This will be achieved through multi-pronged interventions including capacity building of service providers, advocacy among stakeholders and propositioning GBV emergency supplies (such as personal hygiene kits and RH kits). Advocacy for multi-sector GBV prevention; enhancing utilization of SOPs and effective referral and protection mechanisms creating awareness on children and women’s rights among communities, propositioning of GBV emergency supplies, capacity building of workers from governments, civil society and CBOs to deliver prevention, response and psychosocial support services, are all crucial aspects of HA-UK focus. Therefore identifying and training GBV Emergency response teams to provide rapid interventions, including rapid assessment, is paramount.

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

The proposed project has been designed keeping in veiw the protection asepcts as it will cater to the needs of the women and adolescent girls, the most at protetion risks. The needs' assessment conducted for the project included all the protection aspects. Protection in HA programming is at the centre and has zero tolerance on compromise. HA will put in place and strictly adhere to protection cluster guidelines, international standards and PSEA and HA policies, code of conduct and SoPs. All the staff, service providers and others who come in direct contact with the beneficiaries will be periodically oriented on protection aspects, the contracts will have protection clauses and protection policies and CoC will be signed by all. There will be strict monitoring for protection violations and robust complaint and feedback mechanism in place.
