Project Summary

Until now there are no funding for this project and this project is unsecured, Mercy-USA seek to implement this project from the private fund during 2023.
This action has been designed to address the need for a more integrated comprehensive response across northern Syria while taking into account the unique needs across the country and population per HNO, HRP, and cluster objectives So2. The proposed action aims to build community capacity to contribute to achieving a greater level of resilience, mitigation protection risks, and negative coping mechanism and participate in economic life for 600 HHs including (3000 individuals), through two proposed value chains: Agriculture vegetable value chain:
Mercy-USA in this project will provide the most vulnerable host communities and IDPs in Afrin district Aleppo governorate with garden seed kits for agriculture, tool kit, and training, in order to support their livelihood source for them. Targeted bene ciaries 300 HH can be broken down as follows (Men 210, Women 255, Boys 555, and Girls 480)
Livestock value chain:
To strengthen productivity and pro tability for livestock holders and improve access to quality assets and inputs project will work through distributing E-voucher to the livestock holders to be redeemed against animal feed (fodders) from the participant local suppliers. Total of 300 livestock breeders, 1500 individuals (735 female and 765male) with average of 15 animals per HH (approximately 4500 sheep) will be supported through E-voucher for animal feed (fodder) three times. The E-voucher animal feed will be distributed at the beginning of the project to the targeted BNFs, each BNFs will receive one E-voucher with the PIN code, the distribution will be per site with close coordination with the local councils. Information about the redemption process, CFM, participated shops will be shared with the BNFs during the distribution. The number of rounds is three and each round will cover the needs for one month per round starting from January 2023 to December 2023. The animal feed will be pellet feed and quality will be based on the livestock technical working group (LTWG) under the FSL Cluster support.
As part of the field schools The BENFs will receive training about the best agriculture and livestock good practices and all necessary information to raise the knowledge in the targeted locations. Each group of livestock holders or agriculture trainers, male and female will participated in one school through the project lifecycle, different topics will be covered to improve the heath and productivity status of the livestock or improve the productivity of their garden by the Mercy-USA technical team.
Locations and beneficiaries:

Aleppo governorate - Afrin district- Jandairis sub-district, 300 HH (Men 210, Women 255, Boys 555, and Girls 480)
Idlib (Idlib district – Idlib and Maaret Tamsrin sub-district) 300 HH (Men 210, Women 255, Boys 555, and Girls 480).
Targeted beneficiaries will be both IDPs and host households with 50% each. Also, the beneficiaries will receive training about the best agriculture and livestock practical’s. the training will be for local authority and focal point and as well as through agriculture and livestock farmers eld school, to make sure the farmers and livestock holders receive not only the necessary knowledge but also in practice for that IEC (information education and communication) will be distributed to the targeted beneficiaries.
Eligibility Criteria will be:
1- Livestock holders with a maximum of 20 sheep or 2 caws, for livestock; and a maximum3 dooms for agriculture beneficiaries.
2- No or a low source of income,
3- Do not receive a similar type of assistance, Vulnerability criteria will give for:
1- Women-headed households, 2- Elderly headed households, 3- IDPs,
4- Households with disabled members,
5- Household with more than 5 members,

3 - Needs

Based on HNO 2023, Livestock has also not been spared from the impact of the prevailing water shortage and scarcity, limited access to livestock production inputs especially feed,and the generally unfavourable weather conditions.The unfavourable 2021–2022 rainfall season resulted in a sharp decline in pasture growth and development, increased demand for fodder, limited supply, and high prices, especially imported feed and this trend is likely to continue if the 2022/2023 rainy season is not favourable. The worsening economic crisis affecting Syria and the inability of small livestock keepers to provide adequate fodder for their herds has also resulted in less efficient animal health services. above-mentioned challenges continue to severely undermine the health and nutrition of livestock and reduced the viability of livestock-based food production and livelihood systems. There is a need for emergency provision of agriculture production inputs for vulnerable farmers and livestock breeders affected by the decline in agricultural production during the last two seasons, There is a similar need of livestock production inputs (fodder and veterinary medicines and vaccination) for the agro-stability zones 4 and 5 across Syria.This support should be combined with early recovery and resilience-building actions, to boost local food production and help vulnerable smallholder crop farmers and livestock keepers to be more self-reliant. Interventions include own agriculture input production

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk: Community tension arising from the beneficiary selection • Mitigation measure: Mercy-USA will target both IDPs and host households according to the targeting criteria, and sensitization sessions.• Monitoring: Complaint feedback mechanism, Community feedback mechanisms registration, and Distribution monitoring by the M&E team.Mercy-USA in all of its projects applies the do no harm principle through paying attention to gender differences, checking references in recruiting staff and volunteers and building the capacity of new personnel from the local and/or affected community, providing follow-up supervision and monitoring to ensure that interventions are implemented correctly, use local cultural practices to support local people, use methods from outside the culture where it is appropriate to do so. Also, Mercy USA carries out activities and uses an approach aimed at facilitating individuals and communities to achieve respect for rights in safety and dignity through community-based protection approach. Mercy-USA uses GAM “gender age marker” to tackle whether or not a humanitarian project is designed to effectively respond to the different needs of men, women, girls, and boys within the affected population. Also, Mercy USA In its project separates males and females and people with special needs have special services such as chairs.


8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Overcrowded during E-voucher redemption to support livestock and agriculture inputs which may cause SEA incidentsMixed redemption lines for males and females •All distribution staff are males Redemption site is not accessible to all gender groups No shared clear information about timing and amount of redemption No clear procedure regarding loss of card/voucherTaxation on the assistance •No complaints and feedback mechanism (CFM) in place Risk mitigation:Ensure extended redemption days to avoid overcrowding •Separate lines for males and females in all redemption processes •Ensure gender balance in the hiring process, ideally to be 50%of the team is female.Sensitization about the scope of the project to all communities with highlighting “This assistance is Free” and this statement will be printed on the beneficiary cards.Different CFM channels will be in place such as WhatsApp, hotline, email, phone as well as physical by MEAL team. • PSEA awareness materials (e.g. posters and pamphlets) and reporting hotline will be visible to beneficiaries at the redemption site.Also, Mercy-USA publishing PSEA material and inform BNFs and non-BNFs about their rights and have complaint mechanism in place as well as building the local capacity in terms of protection issues through conducting appropriate training such as PSEA As for Mercy-USA staff, all of its staff once hired Mercy-USA to conduct a PSEA and safe gardening as well as code of conduct and humanitarian principles training.


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk1: Delay in the project after the appropriate season for seeds, Assumption1: all activities start on time without delay, Mitigating measures: start the project in early time, hiring quali ed agronomists. Risk2: No availability for suitable land for planting small gardens, Assumption2: Lands are available, Mitigating measures: Mercy-USA has chosen Afrin and Maaret Tamsrin to ensure the availability of lands for agriculture purposes. Risk 3: Low participant in the training. Assumption3: the bene ciaries will participate in the training Mitigation measure: Mercy-USA technical team to select the appropriate location, time with the targeted community. Risk 4: quality of the animal feed. Assumption4: Animal feed in the market is good quality, Mitigation measure: Mercy-USA technical team will monitor the preparation process of the animal feed.Risks 5: High number of confirmed COVID-19 cases Assumption5: the number ofconfirmed cases is low. Mitigation measure: Mercy-USA will apply the recommended precaution measures across all activities. Risk 6: BNFS may loss the E-voucher and use by other people: Mercy-USA rely on e-voucher online system where the BNFs have to enter the shared PIN by Mercy-USA, at the same time Mercy-USA field team will be present at the shop to double check the BNFs ID.
