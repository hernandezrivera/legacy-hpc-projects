Project Summary

Needs
In 2024, 16M people (8.4M F, 8.3M M) needed assistance across Syria. After 12 years of hostilities, people in Syria saw their situation worsen when a series of EQ hit Syria in Feb 2023. The EQ uprooted hundreds of thousands of families, resulted in almost 5,900 deaths and more than 12,800 injured. The multifaceted crisis in Syria creates continuous cycles of vulnerability, disproportionately impacting those most in need, including individuals with disabilities.15.3 million people need protection services,6.4 CP ,8.5 GBV ,14.4 MA(HNO2024). (Albab, Azaz, Atareb, Idleb Ariha:5), (Daret Azza, Jandairis, Afrin, Harim, Salqin, Bennsh, Dana, Maaret Tamsrin:4)-Subdistrict. (Severity Map 2024).
Intervention, Implementation of activities
Through this Intervention, Bonyan will enhance the prevention of protection risks and respond to the protection needs to most vulnerable group through 2 years intervention (2024 and 2025)
For 2024, Bonyan planning to target 61,100 individuals (B G M W)
(age, gender, diversity, specific needs, elderly, PWD, youth, including IDPs, HCs, and returnees)
Activities will be implemented in 10 CFS (6 centers and 4 tents) ,17 outreach teams in Idlib and Aleppo. Child Protection: CM, CVA, referral, structured& semi-structured PSS, awareness sessions, parenting programs, CPC, Cash CP initiatives and training on CP topics. General Protection: Establishing Protection Help Desks, legal awareness sessions, referral, and protection monitoring. Mine Action: RE sessions and FP training and refer victims to services. Gender-Based Violence: GBV CM, referral, awareness raising using GBV SC toolkit, empowerment activities, training on GBV topics and safety audits, M&E Toolkit and SOPs will be the used by MEAL team. Bonyan is an active member of the (Protection Cluster, GBV&CP& Mine Action sub-cluster, CMTF, CP Awareness raising WG, MHPSS Technical WG, and CP AoR SAG). Regularly sends all reports like 5Ws for the cluster
Protection Risk Analysis
-Lack of services and referrals expose survivors to further harm, discrimination, stigmatization, Likelihood: M. Impact: H
Mitigation: training and supervision of CMW, service mapping including active providers ensuring survivor-centered approach; protected and confidential information systems.
-Resistance from the community on GBV/CP messaging and activities, resulting in low participation, stigmatization or harm to target groups.
Likelihood: M. Impact: M
Mitigation: community and leaders’ engagement in all phases of the project cycle; adapting awareness to the specific context; engagement of child protection committee members.
-Risks related to the location of service provision (safety of movements for beneficiary, contamination from remnants of war, locations are in direct contact with dangerous areas.
Likelihood: M. Impact: H
Mitigation measures: choice of locations done in close consultation with the community, conduction of safety map.
PSEA:
In Bonyan, PSEA Focal Persons are appointed to address allegations promptly, ensuring investigation and reporting follow standard procedures. Persons with disabilities receive PSEA awareness and direct communication access. Call centre numbers and PSEA-related materials are promoted in project areas. Survivor-centered approaches guarantee information access and immediate assistance.
Risk: SEA Violate from one of the staff, Mitigation includes staff training, community feedback, and safe community centers for domestic violence. Mandatory PSEA training, CoC, and complaint mechanisms.
Risks and Mitigations:
Risk: Fraud, aid diversion, and mishandling of supplies. Mitigation: Implement a complaint mechanism with a hotline and complaint boxes. MEAL team handles complaints, and beneficiaries are informed about the mechanism through brochures and community sessions. Risk: Obtaining and publishing images and information for children. Mitigating: Guidelines exist for obtaining and publishing images, written consent is obtained

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The protection team will implement activities within CFSs and through outreach teams in camps and schools as follows:Specialized services will be provided to the most vulnerable children Bonyan will follow the CP cluster instructions related to the selection criteria. Structured parenting sessions will be provided to parents to educate them about positive parenting, parenting without violence, alternatives to punishment, and ways to improve their well-being to enhance interactions between them and their children. Structured, Non-structured PSS and recreational sessions will be provided to children to promote their psychological well-being. Community initiatives will be implemented periodically to achieve integration between the local community and the displaced and to activate the role of community members in decision-making. In addition, CP, MA, GBV, and legal awareness sessions will be provided to people who have problems providing their legal documents to raise community awareness about these topics and mitigate their risks. Focal points and public service providers will be trained on RE topics so that they, in turn, can re-train people in their community, thus disseminating information more widely. Non-GBV&CP actors will be trained on GBV&CP basics to raise community awareness and mitigate risks facing children and women in coordination with clusters.During the implementation of activities, the protection team will rely on the materials, tools, guidelines, and curricula shared by the protection cluster and CP, GBV, and MA sub-clusters.------------2024 Plan - Target: 61,100 - Total Cost: 2,100,000 USD2025 Plan with 30% increase – Target: 80,000 – Total Cost: 2.7M USD
