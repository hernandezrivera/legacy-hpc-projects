Project Summary

DOZ in its protection and education interventions in North East Syria determined the need for urgent WASH services in villages in Rural Hasaka, mainly villages east of Hasaka and urgent need of other WASH services at camps especially for women and girls such hygiene kits.
According to HNO 2018, there is circa 1.5 million people in urgent need to WASH services and this is very true especially for hard-to-reach areas such as Deir Ezzor. On the other hand, most people fleeing form violence in Deir Ezzor are coming to Rural Hasaka villages and to camps around Hasaka.
The purpose of this project is to provide life-saving WASH services to both IDPs and locals most in need in Areeshah and Houl camps in Hasaka province. This includes providing water tanks for newly-arrived families or for families already living in the both camps and their tanks got damaged, and distribution of family hygiene kits on regular rounds to make sure that IDPs especially women, girls, and children have necessary basic hygiene materials each month, in addition to that we organize hygiene promotion and awareness activities within camps in coordination with other actors providing WASH services at the target camps. The whole project will be coordinated with WASH NES cluster to make sure that needs are covered, and potential new needs well assessed during the project.
DOZ submitted this proposal last year to and it was approved by cluster. Based on field local team in Hasaka the needs for this project remain unchanged and therefore DOZ aims to provide the same services of WASH to conflict-affected people especially IDPs in Hasaka.

Needs

Although water and sanitation services and hygiene supplies are available, they are relatively expensive and place further strain on the limited financial resources of Syrian households. The major barrier to accessing hygiene items is therefore the severely reduced purchasing power families in Syria, particularly for IDPs living in camps or marginalized areas. Access to clean water and basic hygiene materials is highly needed in IDPs camps, and especially in new-built collective points or first arrival points of IDPs, in Northern Syria. According to HNO 2018 14.6 Million are in need for water, hygiene and sanitation systems in whole Syria. Only in Hasakeh there are four camps such: Areeshah Camp, Abou Khashab , Al-Houl, and Mabroukah camps that host high number of IDPs from Deir Ezzor and Raqqah and are in need to WASH services. DOZ seek to help preventing spread of disease due to lack of water and hygiene materials and to support local IDPs camps with recent and new arrivals of IDPs. Due to new arrivals of IDPs especially to Areeshah and Houl camps and the damage that happened to Areeshah camp due to floods there is need for more water tanks fixed between tents for daily usage and hygiene materials especially for women, girls, and children. On the other side, the tanks provided required regular maintenance and checks to make sure that it meets protection standards and still usable for drinking or cooking, or other personal daily use. Furthermore, there is need for more hygiene promotion and awareness activities accompanied with these deliveries in coordination with other clusters such as protection and education NES clusters. DOZ will respond in this project to the WASH needs in Areeshah and Houl camps to provide the both camps with 480 water tanks in coordination with NES WASH cluster and exiting actors those provide WASH services to avoid duplication and assure a coordinated cooperation with other actors under NES WASH cluster. Each water tank (2000 liter - family size) total number of tanks estimated to serve circa 7800 persons for water usage for drinking, cleaning, and washing on daily basis – 2 families per one water tank. Also, DOZ distributes circa 10200 hygiene kits where each hygiene kit serves one small family between 4-6 persons. This will be coordinated with existing actors at the targeted camps and will e either one time if the need is high or on regular monthly basis (core versus refill kit). In addition to that, hygiene promotion activities will be implemented each month to raise awareness on critical awareness issues in coordination existing actors at camps from WASH, education, and protection clusters of NES region. The promotion activities will be awareness workshops and distribution of printed information on hygiene awareness, health awareness, and family care especially of children and girls. All project activities will be implemented according to humanitarian principles and SPHERE protection guidelines. DOZ is active in both education and protection clusters and running projects in both sectors in NES. The team dedicated for WASH project will be receiving extra trainings on sphere principles, child protection, and humanitarian principles before getting engaged directly into the filed. DOZ M&E team and senior staff will follow and supervise WASH field teams to assure that the implementation is in accordance with WASH and sphere guidelines.

Protection Risk Analysis

Protection Risk: Risk to purchase materials in Turkey or Iraq to transport them to Syria (Medium). Mitigation Measure: We will secure all materials from inside Syria to avoid the risk of border crossing. Protection Risk: Inequality in distribution physical and psychological harm arising from violence and coercion (Medium)Mitigation Measure: Promote invo9lvemnet communities in distribution planning, Agencies provide feedback and accountability mechanisms to users, Work closely with all partners on the ground. Protection Risk: dominance of male side on distributions as providers or beneficiaries. (Medium). Mitigating Measure: Ensure that half of workers in distribution operations are female and at least 40% of leading staff is female. Send a student and volunteer team from Civil Society to accompany, monitor, and support to avoid GBV in distribution process. Protection Risk: Support to water supply systems. (Medium)Mitigation Measure: Establish strong links with authorities and operators, include communities and authorities in targeting, ensure sanative information is handled with care.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

1. Security escalations or violence against field staff or beneficiaries in the target areas: DOZ works and coordinates all project steps in close coordination with local councils and will be informed in advance of any potential escalations. In addition to that, DOZ Syria makes a security a safety analysis on local and regional levels to make sure that mitigating measures can be planned for any such potential case. Furthermore, the DOZ local team pay regular visits to monitor target areas before, during and after each implementation step. 2. Corruption or Fraud in one or more activities especially for renovation work and materials purchase: DOZ Germany works directly with DOZ Syria and applies its strict financial procedures and policies in each single purchase to make sure that transparency and accountability is part and parcel of each single purchase process of the project. On the other hand, in addition to internal financial controls DOZ Germany hires external auditors to control project financial files and reports. 3. Lack of inclusion and or exclusion of certain individuals or groups: DOZ has zero-tolerance policy against any act of ethnical, sexual, or religious discrimination and its policies are applied on all international staff, local staff, contractors, partners, and volunteers. Furthermore, DOZ filed team in company with local councils will supervise all steps of planning and implementation of the project in the field.
