Project Summary

The proposed project is to improve the well-being and resilience of people affected by accumulated crises through a one health approach that aims to sustainably improve food and nutrition security, increase access to WASH and FSL services, strengthen the target communities’, and promote peaceful co-existence in South Kordofan and Blue Nile States. Most of the current interventions in the target states focus on providing humanitarian assistance, even though this is relevant and needed, however there is a pressing need to consider recovery interventions so as to promote dependency reduction and increase resilience of the communities to withstand future shocks. VSFG and partners have established presence in the target areas and (based on their expertise) have been implementing multi-sectoral, multi-actor relief projects addressing FSL, WASH and , Protection needs of the target people. So, this project builds on the assessment results of VSFG and partners on prioritized needs for intervention in the target areas, and lessons learned from previous and ongoing interventions. The security situation has been mostly stable in the localities targeted by the proposed project; which is conducive to introduce development programs, and it also enables VSFG and partners to closely manage implementation of the project activities. Additionally, the political transition is supportive of implementing development programs, as well as the project is in line with the national and state-level strategic objectives. The protracted conflict and the deterioration of the macroeconomic situation in Sudan have negatively affected the capacity of poor households to secure their basic needs such as food, and protection. In addition, the limited access to livelihood opportunities, increased risks of poverty, social exclusion and violent conflict. The local capacities and infrastructure in the target areas are limited, deteriorated and require strengthening and rehabilitation to cope with the high demand on the available services and resources. The proposed project will enhance resilience through a one health approach and contribute to a humanitarian-development-peace nexus in Sudan through performing the following: implementing a multi-sectoral project to provide holistic response to the needs and priorities of the target communities; establishing a strong project management structure; sharing experience and expertise among VSF G and partners to effectively and efficiently address identified needs; and by accounting for the considerable change in the operational context (political and economic) while developing this project (opportunities and challenges).

1 - Needs

Blue Nile and South Kordofan States have been afflicted by decades of underdevelopment and armed conflict between SAF and SPLA-N (erupted in 2011) which resulted in massive population displacement and protracted humanitarian crises. The security situation is currently stable but fragile and unpredictable especially in South Kordofan which witness more frequent security incidents (e.g. the deadly clash erupted in Kadugli, South Kordofan on 12 and 13 May 2020 between different armed groups affiliated to Nuba Angolo and Arab Hawazma tribes. This led to loss of lives from both parties, physical injuries, burning of houses, looting of household goods and destruction of public infrastructure. Several people IDPs) in settlements in Kadugli town were displaced again having been settled in (camp like settlements) in the Northern and Southern areas of Kadugli town) . Since the end of 2020, new arrivals have been reported in several border locations of Blue Nile State coming from Ethiopia’s Benishangul Gumuz region (7,179 individuals in Wad-Almahi and Gissan Localities) . Following the political changes in Sudan since 2019 until the recent signing of Juba Peace Agreement in October 2020, there has been many changes in the political environment. The transitional government is generally supportive to humanitarian and development actors, however the political instability causes some operational delays. Humanitarian Aid Commission issued a number of directives to facilitate the work of NGOs, and deregistered NNGOs affiliated with the previous regime. As well as VSF-G in 2020 was conducted situation analysis aims to analyze the situation of children in South Kordofan and Blue Nile study indicated that community views that physical violence especially in Russeris about 90%, and psychological violence are 6% as well as Study have shown that lack of community-based child protection network (CBCPNs) as main reasons for violence against children creates devastating effects on a child's life in the short and long term in addition to The high prevalence of children outside education, psychological pressure, and weak economic conditions have led to the large number of violent crimes against children and to the spread of violence among children themselves and other types of violence such as harassment and rape. Referral mechanism very week in the state and there is no reporting mechanism in place to report any types of violence including GBV or S/GBV. According to social welfare department they coordination with local NGOs they started to establish management project system to collect and enhance referral GBV cases in the state but unfortunately these activities had stopped due to lack of trained staff and equipment in GBV unit in social welfare department.

5.2 - Impact on environment/natural resources

The proposed grant will enhance improved resource management and equal access to available resources. Furthermore, VSF G has considered seriously the ‘environmental marker across all the project designed activities. ’The project will protect and restore the natural resource base in the livelihoods systems of the poor and vulnerable households through redressing environmental degradation, increasing efficiency and promoting conservation of soil, water and biomass resources. Due to potential waste generation through medical supplies (vaccination and Treatment campaigns) VSF G will ensure mitigation measures are in place such as proper waste management of the disposable elements of animal vaccination and treatment. The waste will be incinerated safely and each training will include element of environment protection

VSF-g will install suggestion boxes at all the targeted protection and FSL , at WASH main services centers (water distribution points/boreholes) and will establish help desks during exercises such as livestock production and vaccination to receive complaints from the community, and to provide contact information for focal persons from VSF-G, local implementing partner focal points and community leaders to ensure accountability. Assigned VSF-G focal persons will be responsible for regularly opening the boxes.
