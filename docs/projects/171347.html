Project Summary

The project is a response to increasing shelter and NFI needs of newly arrived IDPs and those whose shelter and NFIs were damaged by prolonged flooding, displacements due to conflict, drought, forced eviction and inability to repair/ replace them owing to their poor economic situation.
The project targets 2,600 households with lifesaving assistance in Lower Juba and Gedo. In the target sites, newly arrived IDPs continue to construct shelter using cardboard, clothes, plastic sheets and wooden stick that does not provide privacy and protection against weather elements. Women and girls feel unsafe in such shelters while IDPs living in protracted displacement situations in IDP sites, live in makeshift shelters and need emergency shelter and NFI assistance. The small makeshift structures not only compromise physical security but also privacy and leave women, girls, men, boys, elderly and people with disabilities exposed to rain, temperature fluctuations from cold to extremely hot and diseases.

Under the proposed project, HAPEN will distribute 2,600 ESKs and 2,600 NFIs to newly arrived IDPs, protracted IDPs and in the spirit of inclusion and Do No Harm, to affected hosts who have been affected by drought and floods and have not received any support for the last 1 to 2 years. This will benefit 15,600 people including 5,304 boys, 4,992 girls, 2,496 men and 2,808 women.
The project will also carry out basic site planning to reduce congestion and increase adherence to Shelter Cluster and SPHERE Standards relating to provision of essential services, access, drainage, site planning, fire safety, common areas, etc.

The proposed target IDP sites are located in Kismayo and Afmadow (Lower Juba), Dollow, Garbahaarey, Luuq and Belet Hawo (Gedo).
The selection criteria will include newly arrived IDPs, pregnant and lactating women, women headed HHs, physical and mental disability, child headed households, elderly and households with a large number of children under 5 years. People living with disabilities and hosts may constitute up to 10% and 8% of the beneficiaries respectively.
A participatory process of beneficiary selection will be employed. Activities will be preceded by consultations with the local administration and organizations involved in providing assistance in the respective areas in order to ensure coordination and avoid duplication. HAPEN will team up with site management committees and ensure all teams participating in the selection are trained on the outlined vulnerability approach.
HAPEN has noted that in Kismayo and Belet Hawo, the materials are locally available and will issue vouchers. In the rest of the target sites where the materials are not locally available, HAPEN will source for the materials and provide as in-kind contribution. Distribution of the ESKs and NFIs to the selected IDPs and select hosts will be planned to adhere to Covid-19 prevention guidelines.
HAPEN will empower the beneficiaries to undertake construction of their shelters and repair them in future through onsite training and owner-driven approach by engaging the recipients in constructing emergency shelters.
Protection will be mainstreamed throughout the project process. HAPEN key staff are trained in protection and will in turn train all those involved to ensure integration of all necessary aspects. As participation of women is enhanced, inbuilt protection considerations will address any resulting exposure to sexual exploitation and abuse. Distribution sites will be planned to avoid overcrowding and to ensure women, girls and boys do not cover long distances.
Post Distribution Monitoring (PDM) and Post Construction Monitoring (PCM) will be conducted after distribution and construction respectively to provide beneficiary feedback on the effectiveness of the distribution. 90% of the beneficiaries will take part in the exercise to ensure proper representation. Complaints and feedback desks will be established in addition to hotlines.

Project Activities

1. Procure and distribute 2,600 ESKs to the IDPs and selected host populations to reach 15,600 people including 5,304 boys, 4,992 girls, 2,496 men and 2,808 women and among them 1,560 people living with disability and 789 elders in Kismayo, Afmadow (Lower Juba), Dollow, Luuq, Garbaharrey and Belet Hawo (Gedo).The activity will start with registration of targeted beneficiaries and identify the most vulnerable households with priority focusing on women-headed households, child-headed HHs, elderly, households with PLWDs and households with a high number of children under 5 years. HAPEN will consult with shelter cluster and other partners implementing similar activities in in the project sites. Coordinate with such agencies will ensure common approaches are used, standard ESK's are distributed in-kind and that double-targeting of beneficiaries is avoided. The ESKs will comprise of Locally available sticks for vertical Post ( Philipian Tree), Locally available sticks for truss, door and windows (Philipian Tree) or White wood (8cm x 4cm size each), Locally available sticks (Philipian Tree) for horizontal frames & roofing or white wood (5cm x 2.5 cm size each), Locally available sticks (Philipian Tree) at the base of roof truss or White wood (10cm x 2.5cm size each), 1kg Nails No. 5, 1 kg Nails No.6, 1 Drum door with Frame, 2 Hinges for door, 4 Local plastic sheet (size: 4m x 7m), 4 Outer wall clothing Buul layer. After the distribution of kits, HAPEN will assist IDPs in the construction of emergency shelters to enhance protection levels for women while enhancing overall living conditions for the IDPs. Proper site planning will be done to reduce alarming congestion rates in target settlements. List of beneficiaries along with their phone contacts will be maintained to facilitate M&E processes. 2. Community Response and Feedback Mechanism (CRFM) desks, one in each district, in addition to the already existing hotline will be established with COVID-19 prevention mechanism adopted. Hence there will be 6 CRFM desks. 2 hotlines will be established for the entire project and will be toll free to encourage reporting of complaints and feedback. 3. Conduct a post-construction monitoring (PCM) to cover 90% of the households that have received the ESKs to ensure accountability, gauge appropriateness of materials and effectiveness of distribution and use of the distributed kits.Data will be disaggregated by age, gender and diversity to evaluate whether specific needs of women, girls, boys, men as well as PLWDs will have been met. The results including lessons will be shared with the cluster and the donor to help inform future programming. 4. Procure and distribute NFIs targeting 2,600 households to reach 15,600 people including 5304 boys, 4,992 girls, 2,496 men and 2,808 women and among them 1,560 people living with disability and 789 elders with standard NFIs kits in Kismayo, Afmadow (Lower Juba), Dollow, Luuq, Garbaharrey and Belet Hawo (Gedo).Prior to distribution, registration using the specified criteria will be done in consultation with Shelter Cluster and other partners. The NFI kits will consist of plastic sheet, blanket, sleeping mat, kitchen sets, mosquito net, solar lamp, jerry cans) to the vulnerable IDPs and host community members in the targeted 6 districts. 5. Conduct post-distribution monitoring (PDM) to cover 90% of the households that have received the NFIs to ensure accountability, appropriateness of materials and effectiveness of distribution and use of the distributed kits.Data will be disaggregated by age, gender and diversity to evaluate whether specific needs of women, girls, boys, men and PLWDs were met. The results including lessons learnt will be shared with the cluster and the donor to help inform future programming.

Mitigation Measures

Risk that beneficiaries are exposed to violence during registration/distribution as a result of the distance to or location of humanitarian assistance and services.-HAPEN will ensure the location of and access routes to distribution points and services are safe with enough space for orderly distribution. -The distribution points will be central with beneficiaries not having to traverse long distances or pass through conflict zones to access distribution points. -A mapping of the points will be done in advance to ensure that such points are not near military installations or exposed to threats of attacks, stray bullets, crossfire or violence. -Clear rules will be communicated during registration and sensitization. A key inclusion is the requirement that weapon bearers should are not allowed inside distribution points. Risk of women, girls and boys exposed to sexual violence, including sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA), when accessing humanitarian assistance. -During the distribution of ESKs and NFIs, HAPEN will include clear messages especially to women and girls that services are free and no sexual favours should be given in order to benefit. -HAPEN will ensure that all staff are trained in eliminating protection risks to girls and women. -Distribution sites will be planned to be within safe distance and to ensure the vulnerable groups do not cover long distances and increase exposure. -The planning of the sites will be done in consultation with the local administration and agencies operating in the area. -The distributions itself will take place in daylight hours to allow all beneficiaries to leave and return home during daylight hours. Risk that marginalized groups, persons with disabilities, women, boys and girls, do not participate in consultations or decision-making processes for the project or are excluded from humanitarian
