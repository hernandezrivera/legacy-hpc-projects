Project Summary

The Rehabilitation and Care Center aims to:
1) Providing protection services for children with disabilities, and working to improve the quality of life for them and reduce the risks to which they are exposed. The project’s activities and programs are designed to support these groups from different societies, taking into account the different needs among all groups.
The evaluation visits carried out by Al-Batoul through the ORVs from 2015 to 2022 indicate that there are so many families with a disable child, as the number reached, according to the latest statistics provided by the Directorate of Social Affairs and Labor - The Office of Disability in Tartous & Lattakia Governorate to 10416 in addition to the number of displaced from these families.
PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy
Since 2020 Al-batoul started focusing on PSEA, First there has been a lot of work to train all staff members on PSEA and raising awareness about the expected behaviors and the disciplinary procedures for those who commit it. Trainings have been conducted to WCWs to make sure that the community itself has a main role in fighting this problem and that key persons have the right knowledge and information to deal with this problem since people of the same society may trust each other more than strangers especially in sensitive subjects. Later, there have been a policy and code of conduct for PSEA read and signed by all staff members. The policy includes whole procedures of complaining and what shall be done after a complaint is submitted. Three complaint mechanisms have been established in each of Al-batoul 4 community centers; complaint box, hotline and email address. Also, the policy contains the investigation process and highly stresses the confidentiality and safety of both the survivors and whistleblowers. Awareness sessions targeted different age categories of displaced and host communities. Brochures were distributed, street commercials have been displaced in crowded and common areas in Tartous City, text messages that assured the existence of complaint mechanisms in Al-Batoul centers and encouraged the beneficiaries to use them without hesitation were sent to almost 2500 persons in the areas covered by Al-Batoul centers.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

According to the HNO 2023 project, complex and interrelated protection issues are exacerbated in this ongoing crisis in a general context of limited access to humanitarian services, rights, justice and accountability. Due to the lack of available resources, which led to a lack of awareness of large proportions of families about the health condition of their children and their neglect of them and themselves, in addition to the increase in economic deterioration rates and the spread of poverty resulting from the current crisis, and the increase in domestic violence and early marriage rates and the deprivation of girls from education and the loss of many families to their work due to displacement Especially the families whose displacement has been prolonged since the beginning of the crisis and they were unable to find a suitable job opportunity for their livelihood, which indicates the urgent need for intervention and protection services. - Implementation of activitiesproviding home rehabilitation services for children with disabilities, such as qualifying escorts to deal with disabled children ,in addition to consultations and medical examinations for them. providing a monthly amount of $120 to the escort to cover the special expenses and requirements of the child, in case that he adheres to the rehabilitation .The project also aims to support protection services with livelihood by providing grants for small projects worth $1500that provide opportunities for protection and a welfare safe environment for these families.150POCs targeted in the rehabilitation program, The target population for livelihood services: 25 POCs .risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measuresRisk: Financial risks related to increase in materials prices Likelihood: medium. impact: high this will be mitigated by considering this increase while preparing the proposal budget.
