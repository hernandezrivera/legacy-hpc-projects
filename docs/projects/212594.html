Project Summary

Deir Ezzor, situated in eastern Syria, has faced significant adversities due to both conflict and environmental challenges. These challenges have notably impacted its agricultural sector, with a profound effect on food security. Amid these difficulties, the rehabilitation of irrigation canals emerges as a pivotal element in revitalizing agriculture and enhancing food security in the region.
The Syrian conflict, beginning in 2011, has severely disrupted agricultural practices in Deir Ezzor, damaging critical infrastructure, including the irrigation systems that are vital for farming along the Euphrates River. The destruction of these systems has led to decreased agricultural productivity, contributing to food scarcity and economic hardship for the local population. In response, efforts to rehabilitate the irrigation canals are seen as a crucial step towards agricultural recovery.
The process of rehabilitating these canals involves not only the physical repair and construction work but also the implementation of modern water management practices to ensure efficiency and sustainability. This includes the introduction of water-saving technologies and the training of local farmers in advanced irrigation techniques. Such measures aim to optimize water use, reduce waste, and increase crop yields, thereby supporting the livelihoods of farmers and the food security of the community.
Furthermore, the rehabilitation of irrigation canals is expected to have a ripple effect on the local economy. By improving agricultural productivity, it can stimulate economic activity, create jobs, and encourage the return of displaced populations to their lands. This, in turn, can contribute to the overall stability and resilience of Deir Ezzor, laying the foundation for long-term development and prosperity.
