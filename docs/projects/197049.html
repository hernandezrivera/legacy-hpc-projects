Project Summary

Lebialem Division is one of the most affected communities in the South West Region of Cameroon due to the ongoing crisis across the North West and South West Regions (NW/SW). Being one of the hard to reach areas, boys, girls and persons with disabilities continuously face severe challenges to education and other services.
The gender aspect has been seriously crushed due to several reasons; ranging from: Limited Formal schools in some parts of the Division, Long distance trekking to existing formal centers, over crowded classrooms, rape and sexual assaults, poverty and other predicaments caused as a result of the crisis. Due to these challenges, most girls have been forced into early marriages, persons with disability do not have the physical aid ( Example: Walking aid and others) to take them to school centers. This has kept most teenage girls, young children and persons with disability out of school. Education has become a nightmare for these vulnerable population. The Lebialem People rely on Agriculture and farming for subsistence, this too has been down for years as a result of the crisis in the NW/SW regions. The gap in education to persons with disability is alarming, the gender aspect which has affected girls more in this Division is a major call for concern.
The project has the following objectives:
1. Provide Recreational Devices, mobility aid, sitting and positioning equipments and others to persons with disabilities.
2. Encourage more formal learning centers in distant areas and mainstream gender in all education programs. There is also the need to recruit trained teachers and extend classrooms. This can be realized by renovating dilapidated schools and constructing new school centers across the three sub Divisions of Alou, Fontem and Wabane. This will reduce the high rate of rape and sexual assaults witness among girls who trek for long distances to schools. It will also bring education closer to persons with disability and ensure inclusive education for all, gender inclusion and girls education will also be guaranteed.
3. Empowerment and other skill acquisition programs will be provided to young girls who have been forced to early marriages. This will ensure sustainability and female's participation to economic development will be very visible.
Target Population are:
1. Boys and girls within the ages of 5 to 18years and 18 to 25 years
2. Persons with Disabilities 5 to 18 years and 18 to 25years
3. Young Vulnerable girls of 13 to 18 years and adult girls of 18 to 25years.
