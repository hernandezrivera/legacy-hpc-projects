Project Summary

Project in collaboration with German-based INGO Humedica who assisted with drafting the design of this proposal. Additional on the ground support and partnerships with Mercy Ships, Hope Force International and the Red Cross. Hurricane Beryl Category 4 hit the Southern Grenadines in the Caribbean West Indies on July 1, 2024, devastating the Southern Grenadines islands of Canouan, Mayreau and Union Island. Context: Eden Rest Inc. is working with the Prime Minister Hon. Dr Ralph Gonsalves and the government of St Vincent and Grenadines to provide urgent shelter and assistance to the 5’700 displaced population in collaboration with Reach SVG. Request: We request a gift-in-kind donation of 10’000 cots, 10’000 single mattresses and 10’000 20x40 mid-weight duty tarpaulins. Displaced people 1’100 people are staying in 45 government shelters and an additional 4’400 in private homes, many of them sleeping on the floor. Over 200 children from the Southern Grenadines will have to move to the mainland to go to school
