Project Summary

Amel Association International (Amel) with the support of its medical and social staff will provide outreach services to affected populations including for general health services, acute conditions, chronic diseases, and sexual and reproductive health, and link them to operational PHCs in the area for further follow up. This will be supported by the teams of Amel (doctors, nurses, social workers) working, usually working with its mobile medical units, and skilled to conduct similar outreach activities. A particular focus will be given to the person with specific needs (disabled, elderly) who can't access the PHCCs oustide the damaged areas. The operations will be conducted in the catchment area of Qarantina and will be complemented by the mental health intervention of MdM.

Associated response plan

Lebanon Flash Appeal 2020

Plan fields

1 - Needs

- Lack of access to PHCCs (damaged in the Qarantina catchment area and distance with the Burj Hammoud ones);- Patients not covered by NSSF / private insurance and relying on out of pocket expenditures (not affordable with the loss of livelihood in the damaged areas);- Elderly and persons with specific needs who need home care based;- Need to follow up on basic primary health care for patients who went to the hospital after the blast;- Need to access underserved populations to refer to other sectors through health;

2 - Activities

Provision of outreach services to affected populations including for general health services, acute conditions, chronic diseases, mental health conditions, and sexual and reproductive health, and link them to operational PHCs in the area for further follow up. The activities will be implemented by a social-medical team, in the area of Qarantina.
