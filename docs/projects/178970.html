Project Summary

The project at hand aims to contribute to strengthening emergency life-saving humanitarian aid and protection assistance in IDP sites, reception centers, and camps in northwest Syria. It also aims to collect CCCM relevant data and to provide stakeholders including the CCCM cluster, and the humanitarian community with up-to-date and accurate information in order to contribute to an organized and efficient humanitarian response. the collected data will effectively contribute to organizing the response and avoiding the potential overlap. it also contributes to the timely response through the provision of information in a timely manner.
The collected data will include IDP movements, returnees movement, IDP sites updates in terms of the number of the IDPs inhabiting the camps, number of new arrival, priority needs, humanitarian actors operating in the IDPs sites, and further relevant topics.
Violet seeks to use the technical skills and human resources in the field to cover all accessible areas in northwest Syria, including Idleb, and Aleppo, with data collection according to the ISMI and ISIMM tool provided by the CCCM cluster. 4 - Needs

As the conflict in Syria continues, the number of displaced people increased significantly, with many IDPs forced to seek shelter in planned camps, informal camps or settlements, transit/reception centres, and collective centres. Active conflict and shifting frontlines instigated mass and sudden displacement concentrated in the north-west of Syria, which led to 960,000 people to flee their homes and move closer to the Turkey-Syria border and northern rural Aleppo between December 2019 and March 2020. In North-west Syria, the number of people living in last-resort IDPs sites reached 1.5 million individuals by November 2020, up from 868,000 individuals in November 2019. IDP sites continued to provide a safe space equipped with immediate humanitarian life-saving assistance to the most vulnerable IDPs. The majority of IDP sites listed in the CCCM database are self-settled, meaning that they often lack proper site planning, infrastructure and camp management systems. IDP sites are designed to act as an option of last resort and to be temporary in nature, providing only a minimum level of services. Despite this, as of November 2020, CCCM monitored 1,214 IDP sites in the north-west, of which 1,113 were self-settled, 83 were planned sites, 12 were collective centres and six were reception centres. Services and space are significantly overstretched in most of the existing sites in IDP sites due to overcrowding, in particular in North-west Syria.

5 - Implementation of activities

To achieve the project objectives within the timeframe of 6 months, Violet has designed the project with the following activities: 1) Establish IDP tracking team which will cover more than 300 IDP sites in northwest Syria in both Idleb and Aleppo governorates2) Tracking IDP, returnees' movements, and report through the CCCM Cluster IDP tracking tool (ISMI) to ensure the humanitarian actors are aware of the displacement. 3) Monitoring and updating information on IDP through the CCCM Cluster IDP sites database (ISIMM / ISIMM Plus)4) Data collection on informal sites, including identification of sites to be added to CCCM Cluster database ISIMM Plus and creation of a database of informal sites that cannot be added to ISIMM, but which includes sites that can be targeted for life-saving assistance. 5) Flash updates to CCCM Cluster on any events (conflicts etc.) that might have a direct impact on IDP movements and IDP sites.6) Ad-hoc surveys to inform the CCCM Response, including rapid needs and damage assessments.7) Development of a data collection and information sharing mechanism with IDP site focal points (camp managers, community representatives etc.) through which gaps and emergency needs can be identified and critical information, 8) Development of reports based on the collected data by utilizing Power BI and Tableu for data visualization. 9) Monitoring of all activities, utilizing advanced data collection tools that allow accurate information even in remote data collection settings.
