Project Summary

The proposed project will enable WHO to scale up its ongoing emergency response programme by providing humanitarian health assistance to conflict affected populations in 3 states (Adamawa, Borno and Yobe) in North-East Nigeria to control disease epidemics.

Needs

Although humanitarian and government actors have made progress in reducing morbidity and mortality rates to below emergency thresholds, the affected population remains at high-risk for malnutrition, physical and mental trauma, as well as vaccine-preventable and epidemic-prone diseases (e.g. cholera, malaria, measles, viral hemorrhagic fevers). The risk of disease outbreak is greatly increased in crisis settings, particularly in conflict and displacement, where internally displaced people are often forced to take shelter in overcrowded and unsanitary camps and dwellings with limited or no access to clean water and adequate sanitation. Affected populations across north-east Nigeria remain at significant risk of epidemic-prone diseases such as cholera, measles, meningitis, hepatitis E, and viral haemorrhagic fevers (VHF) including Lassa and yellow fever.

Activities

To expand and strengthen communicable disease surveillance for prevention/mitigation and control of epidemic outbreaks. Capacity Building of LGA DSNOs, Assistants and Health Care workers on disease detection and reporting. Capacity Strengthening for Laboratory Personnel. Mentorship and supportive supervision of DSNOs and assistants by NFELTP residents and graduates. Procurement and Prepositioning of RDT Kits. Conducting Cross-border Coordination Meetings. Environmental Surveillance for Water-borne Diseases. To increase access of vulnerable people to life-saving emergency health assistance in conflict-affected areas of North-east Nigeria. Deployment of H2R Mobile Health Teams and CORPs. 2.3.2.2 Quarterly Review Meetings and Regular Supervision for CORPs and H2R Teams. Risk Communication for Disease Outbreak Prevention. To improve access of conflict-affected communities in North-east Nigeria to treatment of SAM with medical complications. Training of Stabilization Center staff on management of SAM with medical complications. Provision of SAM kits to Stabilization Centers. Regular Supportive Supervisions to Stabilization Centers.
