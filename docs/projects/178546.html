Project Summary

About ten years of continuous armed and economics conflicts in Syria were enough to weaken the Syrian communities. The war has left one of the worst humanitarian crises of all time. Millions of people have been killed or forced to flee their homes. Families are struggling to survive inside Syria, or make a new home in neighboring countries.
The economic and social situation in Syria continues to decline and severely affect everybody especially those who have been displaced from or returned to their homes. The humanitarian needs in Syria are expected to remain high in 2021 as several factors still worsen the economy situation that make people in front of a big daily challenge to secure their basic food needs. The Economic pressures are key drivers of need, with large groups stating that the most widespread challenges are inability to afford needed food items and following negative coping strategies.
The purpose of this project is to provide food parcel to most vulnerable Syrian people in Rural Damascus, Der ez-Zor, and Dar'a, as according to HNO 2021, an estimated 14.2 million people in need of some form of food and agriculture assistance, compared to 9.8 million in the previous year. The food insecurity PiN has risen by 45 per cent in the last year.
Our contribution to this objective is to reduce the vulnerability of affected population through the provision of general food assistance (which its content designed based on SO1 response package) in targeted areas of this project by distributing food parcels three times during project life, with a purpose of assisting the very vulnerable population in these areas.
The food parcels (including wheat, rice, pasta, raw chickpeas, beans, tea, brughul, vegetable oil, sugar, salt, canned tuna ,canned sardin, halawa, canned meat and lentil)
As an outcome for this project The well being of 12,000 vulnerable families (around 60,000 individuals) in need who will receive 12,000 essential food items four times during the project life.

4 - Needs

The need of food is the core need to people in Syria. According to HNO 2021, The situation continues to deteriorate drastically with an estimated 14.2 million people in need of some form of food and agriculture assistance, compared to 9.8 million in the previous year. The food insecurity PiN has risen by 45 per cent in the last year.At least 12.4 million people are estimated to be food insecure, an increase of 57 per cent compared to 2020, of which 1.3 million people are considered severely food insecure. Food prices in Syria have increased dramatically in 2020 and WFP’s national standard reference food basket price in November 2020 was 251 per cent higher when compared to November 2019.The aim of this project is to provide assistance through in-kind, to those in need of assistance and scale up/expand to the newly food insecure caseloads including those impacted socio-economically by the protracted crisis and COVID. FAS will target geographical areas with severity 3 and above with food assistance. So our contribution to this objective is to reduce the vulnerability of affected population through the provision of essential food items.Over 75 per cent of communities reported that food had been unaffordable or unavailable over the last 6 months. Unavailability and unaffordability of individual items are linked, indicating that limited supplies may be driving higher prices. GOPA's mobile team conducted needs assessment visits in these areas at the first 2 months from 2021 year in order to better identify the humanitarian needs and priorities of families affected by the crisis, so that it can design a program that better meets these needs. The needs assessment visits have a clear idea of the population dynamics

5 - Implementation of activities

The distribution process will completed within three phases:1- When we will targeted any area with distribution activities, GOPA team will arrange with the local community leaders FGD meeting in order to explain the project objective to the community, and coordinate with local NGO in the area and community leader to conducts beneficiaries registration operation based on project selection criteria.After explaining the project objectives and the selection criterias of the beneficiaries to the community leaders in each area, they will provide beneficiaries list in coordination with GOPA team,Beneficiaries will invited to register their personal data through inform them either by communities leaders or by calling them by phone if the communication network is available. The distribution and registration location will be in familiar, public and accessible places that take into account the social environment of the target area (GOPA centers, mosque square, school, or municipality building,...)The purpose of beneficiaries registration operation is to collects all personal data of the families, including the families national numbers which will helps to avoid any duplication in providing of services.2- During the registration phase, beneficiaries will be notified of the date,time and distribution point location of assistance delivery.3- On the appointed day the commodities that will be distributed to the target areas will shipped in order to be distributed to the families who were registered in the first phase.
