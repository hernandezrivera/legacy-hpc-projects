Project Summary

UMCOR requests USD 550,500 to implement a Multi-Sector Project WASH and Education in Abugabra ,Adeela ,Bahr El Arab , Abukarinka and Elferdous Localities to benefit 48,289 out of camp refugees integrated with the host communities. This project aims to provide sustainable WASH and Education services in the targeted areas. Under this funding appeal, UMCOR shall do the following:
School construction/rehabilitation, WASH in schools, Provision of education materials, PTA support
Teacher recruitment, trainings/capacity building, teachers' incentives.
WASH activities Borehole drilling/construction
Operation and Maintenance of Water supply facilities, water user committees formation/revival and training, household latrine construction, hygiene Promotion Awareness campaigns/Activities, household jerry can distribution, household soap distribution, chlorination of water sources

Needs

Since the influx of Sudanese refugees in East Darfur in the beginning of 2016, humanitarian agencies continued focusing provision of life-saving activities in the two camps of Kario and Al-Nimir. The out of camp refugees areas are still lacking basic services, since they were not targeted in any intervention. The out of camp refugees are largely dependent on host community resources in their areas, this has resulted in massive pressure on the water points, education, health and other livelihood resources. The UNHCR led inter agency assessment in October 2017 of out of camps in East Darfur in El ferdous,Bahr ElArab Abujabra, Abukarinka and Adilla revealed that boreholes are present in most locations but several are not functioning and needs repairs. The existing boreholes serving the host communities and refugees are in despair conditions leaving refugees and host communities with the option of purchasing water from water tankers with a barrel costing 25 SDG whose quality is also not assured. According to WES no chlorination is taking place. Sanitation coverage was noted to be very low in afro mentioned localities for both refugees and Host communities and open defecation is a common practice; Diarrhea, respiratory infections, malaria and skin allergy was reported as one of the main infections in pregnant women and infants; all are related to poor WASH conditions According to Sudan HNO 2018, Darfur and White Nile as well as South Sudanese refugees in out of camp settlements. Many refugees settle in areas where access to drinking water is limited even for the host communities.
