Project Summary

The project works to secure the basic material for the daily sustenance of the Syrian people in the northern Syrian camps (Al Sad, Safa, Toum and Dabeq) in addition to the 300 families displaced in Al-Bab city by distributing bread daily to 789 families where flour will be purchased (238.14) tons And distribute it to ovens by concluding a service contract by delivering fresh bread on a daily basis for a full year to an Abrar representative in the specified quantities
Then the delegate distributes these quantities to the specified distribution points in each region
Note:
The distribution will be made to the beneficiary families according to the beneficiary card that will be distributed to the families at the beginning of the project.
Flour will be secured from Turkey by a permanent contract
The project is not funded yet, but Abrar believes it can secure 20% of the project’s value

5 - Implementation of activities

- Supply flour or bread directly to households or to bakeries:We will announce tenders for the supply of flour on a daily basis, with the choice of four ovens in addition to a spare oven.We will supply flour daily to individuals and receive the baked quantity packed in a basket.The distribution team will receive the quantities from the ovens and distribute them through approved distribution centers within the mentioned regions, where the distribution centers have prepared cards that determine the beneficiary and the quantity.The M&E team will track all proceduresThe number of beneficiaries is 3969

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Not enough bread distribution points available so people have to wait for extended periods to get bread, fights and harassment could occur; inequality in distribution, Likelihood: high, Impact: high, Mitigating Measure: Ensure agreed context/SPHERE standards and closely monitor these aspects during implementation; Consult separately girls, boys, women and men, including older people and those with disabilities in the assessment, design and implementation phases; Monitoring:Establish complaint & feedback mechanism and FU; Monitor # of bread points and observe if there are queues; Resources Required:Sufficient funds; Allocated teams, resources and staff time; IT support;Staff and partners are trained; Recommendations from CCCM Acceptable Level of Risk: yes
