Project Summary

This project intervention is meant to improve access to emergency clean and safe water, sanitation facilities and hygiene promotion services to the IDPs, Returnees and vulnerable host communities affected by conflict in the hard-to-reach areas of Aweil East in Northern Bahr- Gazal states. Through this project, there will be a greater reduction of transmission of feacal- oral disease and exposure to disease caused by Unsafe water. This emergency project will ensure the Vulnerable Women, Girls, Boys and Men including Disable people living in this hard-to-reach aforementioned areas are provided with safe drinking water, promotion of good hygiene practices and better sanitation facilities to ensure good impact on public health among this Vulnerable communities.
The project is estimated to cost USD 500,000 for the period of one year as from January 2024 to December 2024. SAADO has a well-established presence in these areas with qualified Local staff to ensure timely delivery of services. The beneficiaries for this emergency intervention will be 11111; 2267 Women, 2556 Men, 3000 Girls and 2889 boys respectively.
The main activities that will be implemented through this project will include; Rehabilitation of the existing borehole in communities and Institution facilities, Rehabilitation of the water yard in urban populated area, Distribution of water cleaning Tablet: Aquatab and PUR, Provide MHM kits to adolescent girls in Schools, Provide hygiene WASH Kits to children admitted for SAM/MAM treatment in OTP/SC, Provision of key hygiene promotion messages/sessions to communities including in Health Facilities, OTPs/ SC and facilitate Gender risks analysis, through focus groups discussion with women and girls, construction of gender appropriate and secured sanitation facilities in Institution,
The targeted groups/communities will be Women, Girls, Boys, Men and Person living with disability (PLWD) of IDPs, Returnees, and vulnerable host population living in Aweil East. SAADO has strong presence and well-established office and team in these locations with full capacity to respond in emergency situation and support the cluster to achieve her objective. Through this project, SAADO will maintain a good relationship with other clusters members; Nutrition, Health, Education, GBV and Food security to enable it to provide quality services to the Vulnerable communities through synergies. We will increase our participation in WASH Coordination Meeting in varies level to share and learn from other partners.
All the above is tailored to reduce the burden of vulnerable communities due to inadequate WASH services in Aweil east, that led to disease out-break, increased malnutrition, and increase gender base violence among women and girls, and other vulnerable groups. There is lack of safe and clean water, Improved sanitation and limited knowledge of hygiene practices specially to vulnerable communities; IDPs, Returnees, Host communities affected by conflict and displacement in the targeted counties. For, accountability, the project will be inclusive of all the gender, Age and person with disability. We will work closely with community to form a group that will oversee all the WASH implementation in the field thus enhance Accountability to affected population. We will used monitoring, Evaluation and learning department ensure WASH standards are observed.

1. Needs

The humanitarian situation in South Sudan is in dire state. Vulnerable Girls, Boys, Women and men including Disable person in Aweil east are at heightened risks of shocks and stresses. The likely-hood of water-related disease is extremely high couple with the higher number of returnees, Refugee, IDPs due to sub national conflict and Sudan crisis. As of 27th October 2023, according to the report from UN-OCHA bulleting report, more 341,967 have been recorded crossing into South Sudan from Sudan, putting more pressure to existing limited WASH facilities in this targeted county. Climate change impacts are being felt every year now in South Sudan, with major flooding occurring annually since 2019. Aweil East which is experiencing food insecurity and being rank as one of the priority counties by humanitarian partner also suffer high risks of water borne disease. With all this shock and stresses, Girls and women face gender-based violence (GBV) whilst trying to access scarce water supplies, and their dignity is compromised by poor sanitation and hygiene practices in communities and households. The prolonged crisis in South Sudan coupled with the Sudan crisis has resulted in an expectation by both the government and communities for support from the humanitarian agencies for the provision of WASH services. This anticipated support is growing every year, with very few locations that experience displacement returning to their previous status. The need to build on community resilience to withstand shocks and utilize more sustainable services is growing. Limited access to clean water and sanitation services contributes to the high prevalence of malnutrition, water-borne diseases, poor menstrual and hygiene management, risk of GBV at water points, absenteeism from school, and school dropout. Women and girls have specific WASH risks and needs, for example, they may face increased risk of GBV when collecting water and using communal latrines: in 2020 more than 20% of people using water facilities reported safety concerns, including violence and discrimination, particularly in IDP sites and other crowded settlements according to UNICEF. A stronger framework to support women’s participation in the WASH sector is needed. Persons with disabilities may face barriers due to inaccessible hand washing stations, inaccessible latrines and bathrooms, and long distances to water points. Ninety percent of returnees and 89.7 percent of IDPs live in settlements without segregated latrines, particularly in rural areas of Northern Bahr-el-gazal states. With all above challenges and to prevent the outbreak of disease to the Vulnerable IDPs, Returnees and Host communities in Aweil East, there is an urgent need for safe water, sanitation and hygiene services among the populations of living in this hard- to- reach county thus averting the potential risk to communities. This county often suffers from, poor access and infrastructure which is hampering humanitarian timely response and hence increase suffering of women, children, elderly and other vulnerable groups including PLWD.

2. Project activities

To ensure the project deliver the key needed services to the needy beneficiaries, SAADO will implement activities that are aligned with WASH cluster, to Increase access to Quality WASH services to the vulnerable returnee’s, IDPs and Host communities in Aweil East. This will include; Activities • Provide safe drinking water to (targeted) affected people as per SPHERE guidelines • Rehabilitation of existing borehole in the targeted county more focusing the broken borehole near institution(Health facilities, OTP/SC, Schools and Market)• Upgrading of 1 existing boreholes to water yard in Urban centers of the targeted county to increase access to clean water • Provision and distribution of water purification tablets Aqua tabs (1-67mg tab/10ltrs) or PUR sachets(4mg-1/10ltr) in the communities, School, OTP/SC, and Health facilities • Training of water management committees on management of water points • Construction of segregated emergency temporary latrine (Segregated by sex) as per sphere standards (afterward decommissioning) in Returnees and IDPs camps and under served institution in the targeted county• Construction of semi-permanent segregated latrine in the Vulnerable targeted communities• Construction of temporary bathing places, with drainages, segregated by sex as per SPHERE standards (1 for 100 people in temporary settlements only)• Installation of handwashing facilities in the returnees and IDPs camp, Health facilities, OTP/SC and Schools in the targeted county • Conduct hygiene promotion awareness and campaign for adoption of safe and healthy hygiene practices (focus on developing and dissemination of key massages: Use of clean drinking water, safe disposal of excreta, hand washing with soap at critical times) to the targeted communities including Health Facilities, OTPs/ SC, Market place, Schools, Public Meeting, Church and other gathering• ToT of hygiene promoters on delivery of key hygiene messages in the targeted county who will train others in the county• Provision of WASH kits to household with demonstration: Kit contents: 2-1.5 mtr female hygiene cloth, 6-bars of 100–125-gram body soap; 2-10 ltr Jerry can & 1-20 ltr size bucket with lid /household targeting Malnourish children in the OTP/SC through support of Pipeline partner• Distribution of MHM kit to women and adolescent school girl of reproductive age• Provide WASH Cash assistance to the most vulnerable in the targeted counties
