Project Summary

The proposed project aims to enhance community mental health by leveraging festivals as a platform for addressing cultural norms, community-based outreach, and awareness creation in schools, among community and religious leaders. The project will also focus on maintaining and improving access to nutrition, child protection, WASH (Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene), and strengthening systems and community resilience mechanisms to identify and support vulnerable children and persons suffering from the stigma of mental health conditions in three divisions of the Northwest Region of Cameroon. This project will address cultural norms related to mental health, improve access to essential services such as nutrition, child protection, and WASH (Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene), and strengthen community resilience mechanisms to support vulnerable children and individuals suffering from mental health conditions.
The target of the project includes the communities in the three divisions of Boyo (Bum Subdivision), Momo (Mbengwi and Jikwa), and Menchum (Benade, Benakuma) in the Northwest Region of Cameroon. The project will engage schools, community leaders, and religious leaders as key stakeholders in the intervention. The project aims to create awareness about mental health issues, promote positive cultural norms, and provide support to vulnerable individuals in these communities.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

1). Addressing malnutrition and its impact on children: The project aims to address the issue of malnutrition that arises due to the seasonal farming activities in Menchum division. By focusing on festivals, the project seeks to create a supportive environment for parents and children to receive proper nutrition and care. 2). Reducing stigma and promoting acceptance: The project aims to reduce the stigma associated with body dysfunctions and malnutrition in the community. By organizing festivals, the project seeks to promote acceptance and understanding, creating a more inclusive environment for individuals with disabilities and those affected by malnutrition. 3). Strengthening community support systems: The project aims to strengthen the support systems within the community, particularly for grandparents who are unable to cater for their grandchildren. By organizing festivals, the project seeks to bring together community members and create a network of support for vulnerable individuals. 4). Enhancing mental health and well-being: The project aims to improve the overall mental health and well-being of individuals in the community. By organizing festivals, the project seeks to provide a platform for individuals to express themselves, connect with others, and engage in activities that promote mental well-being. 5). Addressing the impact of unbalanced diet and health complications: The project aims to address the health complications that arise from an unbalanced diet due to the focus on farming. By organizing festivals, the project seeks to promote healthy eating habits and provide education on the importance of a balanced diet for overall wellness and mental health especially for the women and children who are the most vulnerable in the community.
