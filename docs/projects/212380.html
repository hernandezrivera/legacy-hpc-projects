Project Summary

According to the 2024 Humanitarian Needs Overview, the total number of people lacking access to food has increased to 15.4 million. The inability to afford food is the primary driver of this need. It is expected that the limited response to food sector needs will exacerbate the situation as we approach 2024. Through this project, the Takatof Shababe Association will provide life-saving and sustaining food support through two activities for the distribution of food items: 1) Food assistance vouchers for pregnant and nursing women targeting the most vulnerable returnees and internally displaced persons (IDPs), as well as affected host communities. 60% of our interventions are directed towards returnees, and 40% towards IDPs and affected host communities, of which 80% is for IDPs and 20% for affected host communities. This project aims to assist about 25,000 families in Deir ez-Zor province and its countryside with emergency food baskets and food assistance vouchers for pregnant and nursing women.
Activities include providing:
- Provide emergency response to crisis affected vulnerable people (5000 people) with short- term food assistance through appropriate modalities.
- Provide the assessed food insecure people for (10000 people) with monthly food assistance through appropriate modalities.
- Provide people at risk (10000 people) of malnutrition, children, Pregnant and Lactating Women, Elderly, and Persons with Disabilities with supplementary food assistance through appropriate modalities.
aids comprising (food basket - and cash vouchers) for the families in greatest need.
Risks
Risk 1: Abuse and exploitation: Beneficiaries face the risk of abuse and exploitation when traveling to and from food distribution points. Mitigation measure: Takatof Shababe Association ensures distribution points are close to the beneficiaries. Risk 2: Increased community tension due to assistance and/or unclear beneficiary selection criteria leading to clear discrimination. Mitigation measures: Providing standardized assistance packages with consistent and transparent selection criteria. The Takatof Shababe Association will ensure the principle of "do no harm" through conducting a protection risk analysis as part of developing its strategy. - In some urban and crowded areas, humanitarian workers and beneficiaries may face gender-based violence and sexual exploitation. Mitigation measures: 1- Ensure that the staff are well-trained on the basics of sexual and gender-based violence. 2- Adherence to a code of conduct that matches humanitarian work across all sectors and locations.
risk and assumptions
Risk 1: Delays in obtaining official approvals can delay implementation. Probability: Medium. Mitigation measures: 1. Establishing good communication channels with officials at the Ministry of Social Affairs and Labor to ensure all challenges are shared and addressed in a timely manner.
Risk 2: Unavailability of materials in local markets. Probability: Low impact. Mitigation measures: Conducting detailed market analysis in the intervention areas and developing contingency plans to source materials from local markets.
Risk 3: Market instability and exchange rate fluctuations. Probability: High. Impact: High. Mitigation measures: 1. Continuous monitoring of markets.
Risk 4: Fraud and corruption. Probability: Low. Impact: High. Mitigation measures: 1. Clear communication with the target populations about their entitlements using multiple channels.
