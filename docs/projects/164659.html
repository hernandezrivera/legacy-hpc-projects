Project Summary

Syria Relief (SR) aim to continue providing WASH services to populations most impacted by the conﬂict in North Syria mainly in Raqqa and Deir. The project aims to help IDPs in 2 camps in Raqqa governorate (Mahmoudli and Tal Al Sammen) and one in Deir Ezzor Governorate (Abu Khashab) by building new latrines rehabilitating old latrines, install new water tanks, provide water trucking and distribute HKs.
This project focuses on improving access to wash facilities in the camp and to provide needed hygiene supplies to IDPs staying in the camps, it also contribute to strengthening the resilience of the population of camps in Ar-Raqqa, and Der- Azzor as the measures will decrease their vulnerability and invest in strengthening structures that will help the population after project end to meet their needs. SR will coordinate with other INGOs and WWG that are supporting the IDP camps in Raqqa and Deir in order to cover the gabs that exist before the implementation of the project. SR will implement this project based the gabs listed in the WASH tracker in the coming months. SR is ready scale up it intervention.
In Tel-Alssamen camp SR will start its activity since it is new camp.
The intervention will cover the WASH needs as below:
- water tanks installation
- Water trucking
- Water tanks distributing
-Toilets and showers constructing and ﬁxing
- Hygiene kits distribution

4 - Needs

The recent fighting between SDF and TAF along the northern Syrian borders has resulted in massive displacement for more than 180,000 people with more than 4000 HH moving into Raqqa governorate, temporarily living with friends in families in Raqqa city, Tabqa and the Mahmoudli camp. Smaller numbers of HH moved into informal settlements outside the city but will be relocated soon. At the same time, there are still more IDPs spread out across NES area in camps such as Abu Khashabb in Deir Ezzor governorate and also in Hasaka Governorate. A new camp in Tel Al-semen camp was established on 1st Nov 2019 by RCC (Raqqa civil council) and SDC (Syria Democratic Council) and started to receive IDP on 19th Nov 2019. This location was chosen by RCC to receive IDPs from Tal abyad or IDPs from Ein Essa camp, as of Januray 2020 there are 158 HH in the camp but that number is subject to be increased because Raqqa Civil Council (RCC) is planning to relocate more IDPS to that camp. In addition to the above, there is a large number of IDPs (over 4000 HH) living now with friends and relatives in the host community.According to a Wash activities tracker issued by the NES WASH Group on November 2019, there will be a gap in latrines rehabilitation, Hygiene Kits and promotion starting in February 20th on ward. while designing the project SR has taken into consideration the WASH standards in NES that was indorsed by WWG.According to HRP 2020 WASH report, a comprehensive WASH assessments were conducted to inform the 2020 HNO, including a country-wide individual household-level survey at sub-district level (25,644 household level interviews from 268 sub-districts across Syria) and a household-level survey focused on IDP sites in north-west and north-east Syria (2,910 household level interviews in 280 IDP.SR staff from Al Raqqa office made contact with the local authorities and also with camp management to conduct a rapid assessment in relation to the needs of the new IDPs.

5 - Implementation of activities

Overall objective: To deliver humanitarian WASH supplies, services and improve hygienic behavior and practices of most vulnerable people in Ar-Raqqa and Deir Ezzor governorates through improved quality of life, and health status Subobjective 1: Improved access to safe drinking water sources for community in targeted camps, water tanks constructing and distribution, clean water truck. Sub-objective 2: Access to improved sanitation facilities enhanced for community members and school children through rehabilitating existing infrastructure. Beneﬁciaries have increased awareness and adoption of good hygiene and sanitation practices through hygiene promotion measures. -hygiene awareness -hygiene kits distribution

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Syria Relief staff will participate in protection working groups, and child protection and GBV sub-clusters to take lessons learned for mainstreaming of protection in also WASH projects. Measures to protect the safety and dignity of beneﬁciaries will be contextualized to the political and humanitarian situation. In order to reduce risks of Sexual and GenderBased Violence, considerations will be made with respect to the location and design of WASH facilities in order to make sure that there is no sexual exploitation occurs through the implementation process and this issue is going to be managed and monitored through the complaint mechanism and roles of the SR MEAL staff that will always be available over the targeted locations. Moreover, SR team will be coordinating closely with other NGOs in order to develop a special tool for monitoring the issue related to this topic. For example, latrines in schools, clinics, hospitals, and GBV centers will be lit and lockable. SR will focus on protection of beneﬁciaries especially women, and children from sexual abuse and harassment in schools, camps, and communities. In this regard monitoring and reporting tools will be developed and implemented and during the project cycle. Finally, NCA will organize training for partners during the project period on the UN Security Council Resolution 1325 and develop and simpliﬁed checklist to report the progress during the project implementation. SR will focus on enhanced participation of women in project design, implementation and monitoring of activities. All proposed activities are infull alignment. They have also been designed to be culturally appropriate, inclusive, and conﬁdential. In this project, Syria Relief will ensure the safe access to services for all genders and age groups at all times. This measure will protect beneﬁciaries. Likewise, by installing complaint mechanisms in the targeted locations, which will be checked by Syria Relief staff during the time of the project.

Information sharing with other present organizations - Develop the security and contingency plan in syria - Develop a plan for alternative fund transfer routes. - Constant dialogue and advocacy with donors, and NGOs forum. - Transparency in ﬁnance and funding arrangements. - Record staff concerns and address as per HR policy - ensure that ﬁeld and Iraq staff are aware of project steps - Ensure staff well-being - Map potential suppliers and contractors. - Develop alternative supply chain routes in both Syria and neighboring countries. - Develop detailed procurement plans and initiate procurement early. - Regular meetings with LCs and beneﬁciaries represents.
