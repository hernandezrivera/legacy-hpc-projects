Project Summary

The project aims at increasing availability, access and consumption of food and short term livelihood support to the affected communities of Cyclone Kenneth in Metuge District.
The project will include the following activities:
- reinforcement of agricultural activities and increase the production for the rain season 2019-2020: In 5 communities, the project will open a new field crop of 1ha for increasing the production for the next rainy season and balance the loss of production of the current dry season due to Cyclone kenneth. Equipment and technical support will be provided for the activity together with seeds of local varietes of mais produced in loco thanks to the currently ongoing activities of Istituto Oikos in that areas.
- Restoration of the earth dam in Sunni: the dam was built by the end of 1999 in the locality of Miezie, it creates an artificial lake of 2 Km2 and serves 25 ha of crops. It was perfectly working until the flood provoqued by Cyclone Kenneth overlaoded the dam, causing a collapse of 10 metres of it.
- Structural improvements of the local markets in Nacuta, Impiri, Nancaramo and Nanlia: these villages hosts local traditional markets characterized by unproper hygienic standards (no protection of the products from the dust, sun, and insects). Objective of this activity is to realize small stands with locally available materials and to create a proper market space in order to increase safety of products. The activity will also support the local economy.
- empowerment of beekeeping in Nacuta. Trainings and small scale factory implementation: this activity will reinforce the local beekeepers in the honey production and comercialization.
- Installation of local improved solutions for cereals conservation (celerio melhorado) and realization of protected areas for drying of cereals, fruits and vegetables. Loss of cereals following Cyclone Kenneth is exacerbated by bad traditional drying and conservations techniques. With this activity, implemented in 6 villages, the project will realize a proper area for drying and preserving the production. The use of local improved solutions, like the celerios melhorado will be promoted.
- Reintroduction of local fruit trees: Cononuts, Papaya, Guyaba, Lemon. Afforestation of 1 ha in each community with local fuit trees. This activity, implemented along with the other short-term agricultural activities, will guarantee an improvement in fruits availability in the next years.
- improvements of cashew plantations (cleaning of the soil and pruning). Cashew production of 2019 have been heavily damaged by the Cyclone Kenneth. This activity will reinforce the new season production by capacitating local producers on proper and sustainable soil and tree maintenance.
- Fish farming in 25 de junho. The village is close to a lake the population already use for fishing purposes. With the involvemente of the local fishermens, the project will allow the realization of 4 pools of 250 square meters each. The creation of these artificial ponds will increase the production of fresh water fishes, particularly appreciated in the local market.
- Training on market access and food processing for improving the local commercialization of fruits and vegetables and derivated products : fruits (jam and juice) vegetables (drying and tomate paste)
