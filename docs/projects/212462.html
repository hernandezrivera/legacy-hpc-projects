Project Summary

BONYAN has put providing water and sanitation facilities among the top priorities of its interventions in crisis. the designed 2024/25 WASH intervention based on the analysis of humanitarian needs in NW Syrian, need severity for WASH assistance, as well as, in line with SOs of HRP and WASH Cluster.
The project aims to restore functionality or keep water infrastructure at the minimum level of operation; and to carry out life-saving WASH interventions including, provision of water and sanitation facilities and services, and distribution of NFI kits, as well as improvement of hygienic behavior and practices of most vulnerable people. Targeting 25000 persons for 2024 (7500 Boys, 7500 Girls, 5000 Men, 5000 Women)
Starting with the needs, several factors have adversely impacted access to WASH services in Syria, including the February 2023 earthquake, conflict hostilities, economic decline, power shortages, and the impact of climate change. Overall, 13.56 million people need of WASH assistance and Almost 1.5 million more people are in acute need of whom:
51% women/girls - 44% children - 17% have disability
Only 54 per cent of the population in Syria has access to an improved water source
19.2% female-headed households compared with 11.5%of male-headed reported not having access to sufficient water for more than 20 days per month
The project mainly divided into 4 main components:
[1] Provision of Emergency WASH Services for 7000 persons in 8 IDPs sites (Water Trucking, Desludging Septic tanks, Collection transportation and disposal of Solid waste, and distribution of hygiene kits)
[2] Improve WASH facilities and services within schools and child-friendly spaces (15 schools, 7000 children and education actors)
[3] Supporting and restore water, sanitation and solid waste management systems for 10,000 persons (5km of Water Networks, 5 km of Sewage network)
[4] Conducting hygiene promotion initiatives and activities
The details of the activities included in the extended summary paragraph.
[*] Brief on PSEAH prevention measures
To fortify against sexual exploitation and abuse, Bonyan Organization institutes a robust Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) strategy.
PSEA Focal Persons are appointed to address allegations promptly, ensuring investigation and reporting follow standard procedures. Persons with disabilities receive PSEA awareness and direct communication access. Call center numbers and PSEA-related materials are promoted in project areas. Survivor-centered approaches guarantee information access and immediate assistance.
Risk: SEA Violate from one of the staff, Mitigation includes staff training, community feedback, and safe community centers for domestic violence. Mandatory PSEA training, CoC, and complaint mechanisms.
[*] Key Risks and Mitigations:
(1) Risk: Security risks may hinder the safe implementation of activities. - Mitigation: Collaborating with local authorities and humanitarian agencies, conducting thorough security assessments, and maintaining flexibility in project timelines to adapt to changing security situations.
(2) Risk: Toilets present GBV and/or health, safety risk; Access to toilet is not available to all 24/7; Access to toilets not available for all - Mitigation: Ensure agreed context/SPHERE standards and closely monitor these provisions/aspects during implementation. Ensure women and adolescent girls are consulted during design, implementation and monitoring phase of projects. Accountability mechanisms are put in place and received feedback from users is followed up. Lessons from previous experience are shared and learned
(3) Risk: Inadequate coordination may lead to duplication of efforts or gaps in service delivery. -Mitigation: Establishing clear communication channels, active participation in cluster meetings, and regular sharing of project updates to ensure effective collaboration with relevant stakeholders, minimizing the risk of duplication or service delivery gaps.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Bonyan Organization will coordinate with relevant stakeholders to conduct MSNA to collect most recent data about ERL needs of IDPs, HCs and RTRNs (with keeping in mind the specific needs of different age and gender groups), in addition to collect any other necessary data Bonyan will assess the market system’s functionality and capacity to absorb increased demand for goods and services, and potential impact on markets to apply the "DO NO HARM" aspects.IDPs in 8 camps will be provided with different and complementary Emergency WASH Services targeting total of 7000 individuals (Boys:2100 Girls:2100 Men:1400 Women:1400) the activities will follow SPHERE and WASH cluster standards and SOPs.This intervention also will complement Bonyan education project by supporting 15 schools by providing with gender-friendly and disability-adopted WASH facilities and services and distribute hygiene kits to children and education personnel benefiting 7000 individuals (Boys:3200 Girls:3400 Men:200 Women:200)The hygiene kits distributed in both components will be customized to address needed items for all gender and age groups (including toothpaste and toothbrush for children, and sanitary pads, feminine disinfectant ...)On the other hand, the project will support community resilience and access to sustainable WASH services as Communities in NW Syria are overburden to limited functionality and efficiency of water infrastructure and services. Therefore, Bonyan will rehabilitate/expand 5Km of water network and other 5 Km of sanitation network and providing water disinfectants benefiting total of 10000 individuals (Boys:3000 Girls:3000 Men:2000 Women:2000) Within all activities, Hygiene promotion activities will be conducted to improve and encourage beneficiaries to strengthen already well-established hygienic behavior, knowledge, attitude, and practices among the most vulnerable people. Reaching again the same 25000 persons (7500 Boys, 7500 Girls, 5000 Men, 5000 Women)For 2025 Bonyan plans to expand its intervention by 30% reaching 35,000 individuals with an estimated cost of 1.75M usd Bonyan will ensure that the support will be provided to the most vulnerable people through transparent process based on clear vulnerability criteria. Principles of Do No Harm and Accountability to Affected People are incorporated in the project design and will be ensured during the project timeline. Also, MEAL team of BO will conduct ongoing monitoring of project activities to verify achievement of indicators.
