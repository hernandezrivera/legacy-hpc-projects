Project Summary

The project aims to improve the food security situation for the most vulnerable families in northwest Syria by providing live-saving humanitarian food assistance for 10100 families (60601 individuals: 12999 men, 13666 women,16650 Boys,17286 girls)
That will be achieved through two inventions
1-Distribution of cash for food for 1500 vulnerable families (9001 individuals: 1931men, 2030 women, 2473 Boys, 2567 girls) in Idleb and Aleppo governorates while each family will be supported with monthly restricted e-vouchers (60 USD balance) to address their food needs for continuous 8 months.
The e-voucher modality provides beneficiaries with the freedom to choose from a variety of healthy food items.
BINAA will prepare a vendor map and provide the selected vendors with devices and training on how to use these devices. in addition to conducting orientation sessions on using e-voucher cards for the beneficiaries.
2-Free bread distribution (The essential food of the Syrian families) for 8600 vulnerable families (51600 individuals: 11068 men,11636 women, 14177 Boys,14719 girls) on a daily basis for continues 7 months in Idleb and Aleppo governorates while each person will recieve300 g of bread per day based on the FSL cluster recommendations
BINAA will contract with the closest bakeries to the targeted areas and supporting them by the sufficient flour and the operational cost in order to produce the needed amounts of bread, then distribute it to the beneficiaries on a daily basis.
BINAA will take the COVID-19 precautions in all project phases starting from beneficiary selection till the bread distribution according to BNAA COVID-19 Guidance

4 - Needs

The 2021 Humanitarian Needs Overview states that 14.2 million people in need of food and agriculture assistance, at least 12.4 million people are estimated to be food insecure, of which 1.3 million people are considered severely food insecure. Furthermore, 1.7 million people living in camps are deemed to be 100 percent food insecure, and 1.8 million people are estimated to be at risk of food insecurity. BINAA conducted a rapid needs assessment in Aleppo and Idleb in order to Identify needs, affected groups, and geographical areas and interventions requiring immediate assistance and develop an evidence-based strategy and implementation plan for improving the situation for the vulnerable people in these communities. The rapid assessment results indicate the need for Food security intervention and support the families who have no means to secure their daily need of food nor can afford to do so. This has placed a huge burden on the families' shoulders and made them unable to secure their needs.

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

1-Changes in the security situation or authorities/groups in control of project areas Mitigation:-Regular monitoring for INSO security and incident reports.-Assess the access security records for each targeted location.-If it’s during the implementation, will look for other accessible locations with beneficiaries meeting the criteria.-BINAA has regular access to the proposed locations.Exploring different distribution modalities and plans for flexible deliveries in areas that are inaccessible2-Attacks may affect the vendor shops Mitigation:-Implemented works will be reported daily by BINAA filed team -Temporary suspension in the targeted location and reassess the security situation.-Change the targeted communities/locations in coordination with nutrition cluster and HF 3-Fraud in redeeming vouchersMitigation:-Using the E-voucher system will limit this risk.-We will conduct daily monitoring through the dashboard and the control panel.-We will monitor the markets through the field team on a daily base.-We will put the complaint posters in all vendor shops and provide the complaint number to the beneficiaries.-The activities will be implemented according to Cash/ Voucher-based Programs Binaa SOP-MEAL team acts as focal points.4-Bakery malfunctions:Mitigation: The contract between BINA and the supplier will include a topic that the supplier will be committed to secure the daily planned amounts.
