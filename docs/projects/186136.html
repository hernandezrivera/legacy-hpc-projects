Project Summary


The project aims at ensuring the that women, girls, boys and men are able to meet their basic needs; and are safe in their communities and receive support when they experience harm in the targeted locations in the Far North region. The project will provide multipurpose cash assistance, gender-based violence (GBV) and protection programming to beneficiaries to help them meet their basic needs, be safer in their communities, and receive support when they experience harm

1 - Besoins / Needs

In the Far North, the level of violence exacerbated by forced displacement and lack of adequate lifesaving gender-based violence (GBV) services put women and girls at heightened risks of GBV. Between January and September 2020, some 1,648 GBV incidents were recorded, with most incidents related to the denial of resources and physical violence. In 76 per cent of the cases, intimate partners are the perpetrators of violence and abuse against women and girls demonstrating that while the crisis exacerbates the level of violence against women and girls, such risks find their roots causes in pre-existing social and cultural discriminatory norms. (HRP 2021).The protection environment in the Far North region is defined by a combination of pre-existing cultural and traditional norms supportive of gender discrimination, structural gaps in the provision of basic social services and aggravating circumstances created by the conflict. Conflict and incursions by armed groups since 2013 have contributed to the destruction of already insufficient public services facilities. Increasing internal forced movements - caused either by violence, disruption of education services in many affected areas and loss of survival means by most of the households. In addition, protection actors have recorded high numbers of disappearances, especially of men and young men. Older people, people living with disabilities, with chronical illness and, at times, pregnant and lactating women, are often left behind when families flee violence, which increases their vulnerabilities and deprive them of the social safety net on which their survival and wellbeing depend. (HNO 2021).IRC will ensure that Women, girls, boys and men have an improved access to protection assistance when they experience harm in the FN Region as well as supporting them to meet their basic food needs and avoid resorting to negative coping strategies in the Far North Region.
