Project Summary

The population of the NGCA territory has been facing the consequences of the financial and trade blockade for 6 years. In addition, the situation of people is significantly complicated due to quarantine measures caused by COVID-19 and the lack of access to social benefits and pensions. This has a particular impact on the elderly and people with disabilities, as for many families, pensions and social benefits were the only source of income. Available data show that more than half of people in vulnerable NGCA populations are malnourished. Access to food is mainly hampered by rising prices and lack of financial resources. During the cold winter periods, the lack of ability to meet basic nutritional needs creates additional problems, since it is during this period that people need a balanced and high-calorie diet. Currently, the risks associated with food insecurity and malnutrition have become even higher as the situation with the continuation of restrictive measures at the CPV caused by the second wave of coronavirus infection, deprives more than 300,000 vulnerable people of the opportunity to receive social benefits and pensions.The deteriorating socio-economic conditions in the NGCA have a threatening impact on the standard of living of the population and pose a threat to the health of vulnerable people. Therefore, an urgent humanitarian response is urgently needed.
One of the measures to ensure food security of the affected population as a result of the conflict CO "ICF" MIRA" counts the issue of food vouchers. This will help to provide vulnerable benifits and their families with vital food, overcome hunger and survive in difficult socio-economic conditions. Special priority will be given to citizens living in remote localities.
Food vouchers are an alternative to food aid.
The project will provide assistance to 1,500 people of the following vulnerable categories: the elderly 60+, with chronic diseases, with disabilities, and children with disabilities.
CO "ICF" MIRA" plans to issue 9,000 food vouchers to target categories of beneficiaries for the purchase of food products (excluding alcohol and tobacco products) in one of the retail chains of local food stores with a frequency of issue once every 3 months for 6 months. This frequency of issuance is due to the risks caused by COVID-19, the possible death of a potential beneficiary, or their relocation to another region. Each voucher is registered, its size is $ 29 for 1 month. The total amount of cash vouchers provided in the amount of 6 units. within 6 months, it will be $ 174 for one benificiar. During the implementation of the project, all activities will be carried out in compliance with basic humanitarian standards and in coordination with local administrations.
At all stages of project development and implementation, gender analysis will be an integral part of project activities.

1 - Needs

For 4 years, CO "ICF "MIRA" has been responding to the humanitarian crisis in the Donetsk NGCA. Most people are trapped in a conflict zone and their daily food security needs are not met. Poor market access, disrupted market routes and low incomes continue to threaten the ability of conflict-affected people to meet their basic food needs, with particularly vulnerable civilians living along the contact line.More than 600,000 retirees suffer from food insecurity in the uncontrolled territory of the Donetsk region, including more than 100,000 people who are experiencing acute food shortages.In this project, CO "ICF "MIRA" offers an approach to food aid by issuing food vouchers to the most vulnerable categories of the population, which will ensure that the population has access to food security and basic livelihoods, including General stabilization, recovery, vulnerability reduction and sustainability.To provide this type of assistance, certain locations will be selected that are located near the demarcation line, or remote and hard-to-reach areas from large cities. These areas remain with very low levels of social and food security.The project will apply the standards of protection in all humanitarian activities, will be provided equal access for all groups of vulnerable populations. Special attention will be paid to older women and men and people with disabilities. In addition, older people and people with disabilities will be fully involved in all stages of project development and implementation through consultation with local communities.CO "ICF "MIRA" will select the beneficiaries based on the data from the previous assessment and the verification process, which includes public awareness classes and information exchange through calls, face-to-face visits and the issuance of information sheets. At the final stage of selection, household composition, status, income, state of health and capacity, needs and food will be considered. As for the vulnerability criteria and the selection of beneficiaries, as a rule, the CO "ICF "MIRA" will follow the recommendations of the FSL cluster.
