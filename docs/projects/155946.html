Project Summary

The idea of this program is to support vulnerable Syrian families and their children to have improved food security and livelihoods as well as improved nutrition status, As well as and reduce the use of negative coping strategies that impact the wellbeing of children. The ten month project is designed to cover animal herders, farmers and food processors through:
1. Providing householders with the essential needs to make them able to grow up their lands in North Syria (Euphrates shield) in order to resume agricultural activities and improve consumption through provision of agricultural inputs (such as seeds, fertilizers and some agricultural tools 450 HHs).
2. Improved access to irrigation (drop irrigation net system with solar panel).
3. establishment of kitchen gardens 150 HHs (first part: hens, chicken coop, chicken concentrated fodder, vaccines and vitamins, the second part: micro- garden seeds),
4. Support cow herders with concentrated fodder and animal drugs (250 HHs).
5. Then enhance their knowledge and practice through extension service and training program related to the specific activities in order to produce their needed foods.
6. As well as create work opportunities through cash for work activities to rehabilitate the productive assets 50 workers (each worker will receive 10 $ per working day (8 hours)).
7. Targeting the households for restore/establish/expand small –scale business activities through capital infusions (rehabilitate some small dairy products factories after conducting survey for all factories which found in project area, in addition we will support the interest families in dairy products processing with manufacturing equipment and some other tools 100 HHs), and capacity building by training them on entrepreneurship and business practice related to their aspects.
8. Rehabilitate or set up (5) small factories, by providing the selected group of family or HH as small dairy manufacturing owners with basic needed material after need assessment.
Based on these activities we will target 950 HHs as a direct beneficiaries in food processing, agricultural and livestock activities as well as 50 beneficiary from CFW activity and rehabilitate or set up 5 small food processing factories.
Expected Results:
• Improve food security and strengthen resilience of vulnerable households affected by conflict and displacement.
• Livestock breeders (Male and female) in targeted locations are supported through the provision them with fodder.
• Increase the animal production that led to increase herder income through providing them with fodder for their animals will increase animal productions of milk and meat to be sold the surplus in local markets.
• Contribute with child nutrition, and also maternal nutrition as a woman’s nutrition during pregnancy and lactation has a direct effect on her children through increasing in production (Milk, yogurt and other products).
• Economic and income generating options for targeted communities explored and supported based on their commodities through supporting in food processing equipment.
• Create new job opportunities through CFW activity.
Beneficiary Selection criteria:
The beneficiary must be 65% Host Community (HC), 35% IDPs. In total the Minimum 25% women, 15% People with Disabilities (PWD).
• Beneficiary has a land/space suitable for home-gardening.
• Priority to be given to vulnerable households including widows, female headed HHs, Person with disability or have household member with a disability, and households with elderly family members (50+).
• The herders who have up to 25 animals or 5 cows.
• Willingness to participate in training related to the provided livelihoods kit.
• Not eligible if they do not plan to plant crops for the following reasons: no interest, not enough time, not sufficient labour.

Needs

Based on HNO 2019,Page 8 to 15’’ factors affecting yields’’, it is appeared the problems which face the agriculture and livestock sectors, we can summarize it:1. High prices of agricultural inputs such as fertilizers, seeds and pesticides.2. High prices of fuel.3. Absence and lack of source of alternative energy.4. High prices for irrigation.5. The difficulty of importing feeds، medicines and vaccines.6. Worries about the spread of communicable and infectious diseases for inadequate vaccines.7. The cost of feed, production, and veterinary care.8. The difficulty of securing green feeds, especially for cows due to lack of irrigated areas.9. Poor feed composition due to lack of control over the feed produced and on entering the area.Food processing and dairy products:• In target area the diary product factories not available and there are much of products exposed to mess up.• Simple household units for the production of dairy products with very little production capacity and do not meet the technical and health conditions• Food industries for domestic consumption of the household• The Olive mill( oil extract)There are several mills, this year that work but did not cover the needand based on Rapid need assessment was conducted in Qabasin and Bazzaais by IACO team . Qabasin is a town located northeast of the city of Al-Bab in northern Syria. It is administratively part of the Al-Bab sub district, in the Al-Bab District of the Aleppo Governorate.The population is approximately 69,422 / 960,000, distributed in the city of Qabasin and the villages of about 25 villages. The majority of the population works in agriculture and animal husbandry, with a total area of about 18,000 hectares. Common crops in the region are cereals, legumes and fruit trees, The area suffers from lack of water availability after the irrigation project in the area stopped and the water was delivered to it from Shabaa water station. Currently, most irrigated crops depend on irrigation from wells distributed in the region using pumps and generators of different capacity according to the availability of water in these wells, which increases the cost of agricultural production, requires the use of petroleum products in the extraction of water.For agricultural and animal production inputs (seeds, fertilizers, pesticides, fodder, etc.), they are available in the local market but at relatively high prices, which increases the burden on farmers and production costs.With the increase in the unemployment rate and the devaluation of the local currency, most of the residents of the region are suffering from poverty and the minimum daily needs of the individual is estimated at approximately 700 Syrian pounds.Most of the rural families are engaged in agriculture and animal breeding, which may reach more than 80%.Number of unemployed: a very high percentage, especially the youth and women, and cannot be expressed by a specific number.The region is very poor in diary product factories and it need to support this sector in order to improve the market.the area is mainly depend on agriculture and livestock, IACO has full access to this area for agricultural services and full technical capacity to work inside.The people in the villages around this area is mainly depend on this location to get and buy their food and dairy products.The IDPs can be useful for this project due to opening extra numbers of Job vacancies for them through CFW activity.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

1- Area retaken by government of Syria: the likelihood of risk occurring is Low, the effect on achievement of activities probably led to STOP the activity, the risk management and mitigations through Predicting the situation before evacuation.2- Rising of conflict within target area: the likelihood is Medium, probably to Delay some of the activities, the mitigation methods through Secure our field team, prepare security plan3- The Airplanes of Syria government attacking the crowded locations : the likelihood is medium, the effect on activities led to suspend the activities immediately. the procedures of mitigation: We must to regular the queue of beneficiaries and prevent the crowded through identify the benefit HH the time to come to distribution point, then consider distributing door to door if conditions permit. 3- Car Bomb or motorcycle or Suicide: the likelihood of risk occurring is medium, the mitigation procedures Delay the activities The Syrian Free Police carries out intensive security patrols on a 24-hour, making it less likely that any risks or threats will occur, Information gathering and situational awareness, proper security advisories, first aid training, medical plan4- Armed Robbery: the likelihood of risk occurring is Low, the effect on activities achievement led to Delay the activities, the mitigation procedures: Awareness the people on any robbery process through local council, mosques, and farmer union and market owners so we can reported through walki- talki.
