Project Summary

Zimbabwe is currently experiencing a severe El Nino induced drought that has adversely affected the food security, water access and nutritional status of the population. This exceptional drought is an additional burden for a significant part of the population of Zimbabwe who experienced a poor 2018-2019 harvest and endured over the last decade several unprecedented economic, environmental and political shocks and stresses, many of which are having long-lasting impacts.
The project is earmarked at supporting the GoZ line ministries(MoHCC,MAMID, Min of Women Affairs and Gender etc) to ensure effective Integrated Management of Acute Malnutrition(IMAM) program delivery, thus ultimately saving lives. Through strengthening active screening and referral activities and severe acute malnutrition(SAM) treatment and management, NAZ will support the implementation of the IMAM program to ensure adherence to government and World Health Organization(WHO) protocols and an improvement in program indicators. Due to the multifaceted nature of malnutrition, NAZ will ensure it participates in various multi-sectoral platforms to enable linkages with other programs. In addition, NAZ plans to support IYCF program delivery to pregnant and lactating women as well as mothers/caregivers of children with SAM, MAM and U5’s.
This will be done to ensure a continuum of care and or prevent relapse once malnourished children are integrated back into the community. The care group model is to be utilized to deliver the key IYCF behaviours. To support these efforts further, NAZ will support health facility staff to grasp the new ordering mechanism for nutrition commodities to prevent stock-outs and disruptions in program delivery. To enhance food security at household level the project will promote the production and consumption of local diverse foods and biofortified crops.
