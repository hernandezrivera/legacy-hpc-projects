Project Summary

The project aims use a community based approach to reach the protection needs of the most vulnerable children. In Damazine locality at BN state This Project will focus its planned activities in Al Kurmuk, Damazine, Tadamon with HNO 2019 ranking 4 and 5.El ruhama during their implementation indicate that IDPs, and vulnerable host communities in the mentioning targeted area above facing challenges in accessing services to EVI, PSN, PLWD, CAR WAR and SGBV survivors. In the target locations there are still many CAR and SAUM, Survivors of S/GBV and other EVI while systems still are not enhanced to support them. CBPN formed now need to offer more support to their communities at community levels and they need more training to do so including Psychological First Aid (PFA). This project design to protect children from sexual abuse and violence, and to provide adequate assistance to victims to make rapid assessment to map and create child friendly space to identify the children in need and provide them with timely assistants El ruhama will implement this project in close collaboration and partnership with key stakeholders like the community leaders, popular committees, HAC, locality leaders, this is to ensure there is no overlap/ duplication of programs. El ruhama field staffs shall directly lead implementation of all activities.
