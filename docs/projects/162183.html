Project Summary

Provision of core coordination functions to enable a coherent, effective response for Rohingya refugees and their host communities. The aim of the Analysis Hub is to support decision-making through research and analysis which contribute to a shared understanding of the Rohingya crisis. The Rohingya response is a data rich environment. The Analysis Hub will contribute to the provision of impartial, independent research and analysis to aid and support programmatic and operational decision making and contribute to a single and impartial analysis of needs.

Objectives

Support response leadership and coordination to ensure an effective response; Promote a common understanding of context, needs, priorities, response progress and gaps; Lead advocacy and resource mobilization efforts for response; Promote an integrated and multi-sector approach to protection and gender mainstreaming.

Needs

To ensure timely and efficient response, humanitarian access, a common analysis of needs, and a voice for the refugees and affected host communities and of women, men, boys and girls of all age groups and abilities, strategic planning and appeals, consistent and efficient monitoring and information management, day to day coordination (including coordination mechanisms at the Upazila and Camp level), communications and advocacy is needed. Support is currently provided by the ISCG Secretariat to the Strategic Executive Group, the District HoSO, and the Sector Coordinators to ensure inter-sector interaction with close synergies among all these diverse actors, and with the core objective of timely, needs-based, and evidence-driven humanitarian assistance, efficient use of resources and avoiding duplication. The Analysis Hub will seek to meet the response's need for a common and impartial analysis of needs to support programmatic and operational decision making. Additionally, after having the opportuntity to focus on gender and improving the gender analysis capacity of the Analysis Hub in 2019, in 2020 the Analysis Hub will continue its relationship with the Gender Hub and seek to ensure all analysis incorporates a gender lens as well as endeavouring to produce targetted products to fill current gender analysis needs.

Activities or outputs

IOM will provide 16.5 positions to the ISCG Secretariat staffing structure. UNHCR, WFP, UNWomen, ACF and UNICEF currently contribute the balance. The Secretariat will deliver the following key activities: • Support SEG and HoSO efforts to take decisions on key policy, security and operational challenges, informed by protection considerations.• Manage the response programme cycle: needs overviews and analysis, strategic planning and appeals (including contingencies), and response and needs monitoring. • Monitor and analyse access constraints, and engage relevant stakeholders to address them.• Manage data and information in support of humanitarian decision-making, advo¬cacy and public information, resulting in regular information products. • Work with Sectors and humanitarian partners to strengthen accountability and understanding of community capacities and preferences. • Progress on a roadmap to localisation, including building the capacity of national partners to ensure sustainability of the response. • Galvanize agency and Sector capacity in gender and protection to ensure mainstreaming across the response during all stages of the programme cycle. The ISCG will continue to host the Gender Hub, to provide technical support in gender mainstreaming across the response.• Convene and coordinate the PSEA Network; the Transfers Working Group (cash and vouchers); the Emergency Preparedness Working Group; the Information Management and Assessments Working Group.• Ensure adequate monsoon and cyclone preparedness and contingency planning with Sectors and humanitarian partners.• Liaise with key stakeholders inside and outside Bangladesh, including humanitarian partners, donors, authorities and repre¬sentatives of neighboring countries, with a view to mobilize resources, promote humanitarian access, and advocate respect for international law. • Reinforce field coordination mechanisms through the continued management of the humanitarian hub in Ukhiya, and support to Upazila level coordination. In addition, the ACAPS Analysis Hub, attached to the Needs and Population Monitoring, will continue to provide analysis of available data for the response. • Qualitative research to provide greater nuance and insight into contextual variables and quantitative research. •Thematic analysis to close identified gaps in knowledge and understanding • Technical support to the system and engagement in joint initiatives and analysis to support a collective knowledge and evidence base for the response • Support to NPM analysis and methodology
