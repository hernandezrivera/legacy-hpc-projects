Project Summary

In line with Nutrition clusters 2019 HRP response plan, CAO proposes life-saving treatment services targeting children less than five years, pregnant and lactating women as the most vulnerable groups to acute malnutrition due to their increased biological and physiological needs. Prevention activities will also be incorporated in the response to reduce incidence of acute malnutrition and promote good health. An integrated response will be considered with other sectors e.g GBV and Child Protection which CAO is a strong and active member to address the drivers of malnutrition; food insecurity, sub-optional child care and feeding practices, morbidity, lack of safe water and sanitation, internal displacements. The response has prioritized uncovered hard to reach locations in conflict prone areas where population especially target group experience high rates of acute malnutrition. The proposed Nutrition in emergency project for the year 2019 will reach out to 14500 beneficiaries through both static and mobile approaches, working to ensure value for money with the 100,000usd fund support if granted. CAO has existing CP, GBV and Health projects already running with support from SSHF , UNICEF & IOM . All activities throughout the project will be shared with stakeholders and beneficiaries through an existing mechanism for transparency. As required CAO will ensure accountability of resources as well as proper Monitoring and evaluation of activities and reports in Fangak County is well implemented .

Needs

Community Action Development proposes to bridge the gap in all or some of Fangak ( 8 Payams ) will be uncovered in 2019 as IMA exits .CAO if funded, which has capacity to deliver ( as evidenced by the existing donor fund from SSHF under CP & GBV ) will continue treating /supporting the existing OTP & TSFP sites in the above mentioned locations . MIYCN activities will also be incorporated into the proposed programming as a means to prevent malnutrition. As part of Enhanced nutrition situation monitoring, analysis and utilization of nutrition information for early warning timely coordinated response decision making , CAO will also participate in SMART surveys in addition to the routine data collection in the nutrition sites and ensure timely reporting to the cluster . There is a great need to Increased access to integrated nutrition, health, WASH, FSL and protection interventions/responses in proposed locations to promote access and optimize basic services and enhance community resilience. Fangak County in Jonglei state is graded IPC 4 necessitating partners to scale up integrated response in order to help eradicate morbidity and mortality associated with malnutrition in the county. A recent SMART survey conducted by ACF in April 2018 showed a GAM rate of 16.1 % and SAM of 2.5% making this a priority location for Nutrition Cluster in the year 2019 . CAO through an intensive Geographical needs assessment across Fangak County and its neighboring counties has great capacity to implement nutrition projects with the aim of helping already existing partners bring down drivers of malnutrition . This will be done through both static and mobile approaches so we ensure no beneficiary is missed in our quest to provide service to the most vulnerable in the society. There is huge need on ground to integrate other sectors into nutrition programming and as CAO we shall work in with and in line with the cluster and the MOH in ensuring ; • WASH cluster trains nutrition staff who in turn provide WASH messaging at nutrition sites • Nutrition and WASH community volunteers monitor practices related to integrated WASH/Health/FSL and nutrition messages at household level • Joint monitoring of the implementation of the agreed indicators will be monitored by the sectorsCAO will ensure with support from UNICEF & WFP maximum treatment and prevention of SAM &MAM children under 5 Boys and Girls and PLWs in addition to listed community management activities .

Activities

Community Action Development having been an active member of the CP/GBV cluster intends to expand its operations through nutrition integration keen on reaching 14500 beneficiaries through the following activities ensuring synergy with Nutrition clusters strategic objectives . • Treatment of severe acute malnutrition among children 6-59 months • Treatment of moderate acute malnutrition among children 6-59m months • Train health and nutrition care providers on management of acute malnutrition • Provision of quality MIYCN services in nutrition sites and community level• Conduct nutrition SMART surveys in prioritized counties• Train CAO staff on NIS and utilization of nutrition information for early warning and decision making at State level• Support and participate in training and data collection and supervision of FSNMS• Test and treatment of malaria among children boys and Girls with SAM aged 0/6-59 months• Conduct periodic safety assessments at nutrition sites to identify and address women and children’s protection/safety needs• Train CAOs nutrition staff on protection referral pathways (GBV/CP) using CAOs strong CP /GBV background .• Establish kitchen garden at nutrition sites for demonstration

Feedback /complaint mechanisms?

Yes

Briefly describe the mechanisms

CAO's Feedback and complaints mechanism just like in the existing CP /GBV projects will be integrated into its project strategies which include: monitoring and evaluations, recruitment, staff inductions, training and performance management, partnership agreements and are highlighted in reporting. e.g. to partners from national or international NGOs that have strong presence in the areas of intervention in respect to ensure access and good quality of services delivered by this project. CAO provides accessible and timely information to the affected populations on its organizational procedures, structures and processes on solutions to the problem that affects them to ensure that they can make informed decisions, choices and facilitate dialogue between CAO and its affected populations over provision of information. CAO strongly believes that the affected populations are the main stakeholders of humanitarian response, so they have the rights to participate in taking decision regarding their displacement situation and have a voice and opinion on whatever is provided for them. CAO actively seek the views of affected populations to improve policy and practice in programming, ensured that feedback and complaints mechanisms were streamlined, appropriate and strong enough to deal with (communicate, receive, process, respond to and learn from) complaints. CAO has developed a number of strategies to collect feedback and complaints from the affected population as follows:A simplified format was designed by CAO project staff that aimed at recording basic information about the complaints or feedback to be taken by a dedicated project officer for the purpose of collecting complaints and feedback during any type of distribution and during implementation of activities that involved local communities. A complaint and suggestions box will be fixed at each Nutrition site and will be opened by the Nutrition officer in charge on weekly basis.
