Project Summary

The main goal of the proposed project is to deliver immediate humanitarian assistance to the most vulnerable IDP people in camps and member of host communities (HC). In North-West Syria (NWS), the price of basic food commodities remains volatile and is subject to speculation. Disruptive events, specifically displacement and COVID-19 have harmful and immediate consequences on the households’ economy andmay result in reduced consumption and exclusion, requiring the adoption of a framework of priorities and actions that are focused on livelihoods. Proposed intervention has 2 components:(1) distribution of cash to internally displaced persons (IDPs) in camps and vulnerable HC members to cover their urgent lifesaving needs (2) Small grants for 50 women to improve their resilience towards external shocks through a sustainable livelihood. Concerning the FSL component of project, it aims at covering the needs of IDPs in camps in areas of food security and hygiene (multi-sectoral) through cash distributions. The primary objective of the activity is to ensure that vulnerable families have access to food and WASH items to fulfill the immediate needs. Cash will be provided through a money transfer agent following procedures agreed in SoPs including C19 preventive measures. Value of cash transfers will be $90 per HH for 8 months, following cluster recommendations. The activity will be implemented in/off camps in Afrin sub-district and Suran Sub-district. The activities proposed are aligned with ASO2 (FSL part) and ASO3 (WASH part) of this standard allocation.

4 - Needs

This allocation focuses on needs aligned with the following 2021 HRP objective: 1-Provide life-saving humanitarian assistance to the most vulnerable people with an emphasis on those in areas with high severity of needs. Based on WHH’s assessment on HH economic profiles, it is seen that people in Afrin only have around 50% of the financial needs per month to buy needed items. During last 6 months, prices for SMEB increased in Afrin by 28% further hindering access for vulnerable people where food part of SMEB rose in Afrin by 30%, while hygiene related share rose in Afrin by 3%. Humanitarian needs are severe across all sectors, in many cases due to the lack of livelihood opportunities, caused by movement restrictions in/off camps. According to REACH almost 57% of assessed residents and 51% of IDPs in NWS report livelihoods as a priority need. These findings match with WHH MSNA from 04/2021 in A’zaz & Afrin where 53% of respondents mentioned employment opportunities as their main priority. HNAP (03/2021) records livelihood opportunities as a priority need for 57% in Afrin. The lack of livelihood opportunities combined with increase in prices for food and other SMEB related items leads to a further increase in humanitarian needs and dependency on humanitarian assistance & the use of negative coping mechanism; reducing number of meals, borrowing food, relying on debt, child labor and early marriage and others consumption as well as livelihoods based negative coping mechanisms (WHH MSNA 04/2021).These coping mechanisms create a severe risk of malnutrition as well protection risk.
