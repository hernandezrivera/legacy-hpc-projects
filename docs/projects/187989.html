Project Summary

This project aims to continue and increase the accessibility to essential life-saving curative and preventative nutrition services for boys, girls, pregnant and lactating women and other caregivers (other women, and men) with attention to people with special needs without any sexual discrimination, among IDPs and host communities, in NW Syria ,where the project will provide comprehensive quality CMAM services, disseminate and support optimal IYCF-E practices and promote health and nutrition and basic hygienic behaviors within the population in need in northwest Syria, Besides, the project is going to provide harmonized emergency community-based services for children and women of the population in need by applying integrated health, nutrition, child protection, and WaSH approaches.
This project will target 7 sub districts :Afrin, Atareb, Al-Dana, Salqin, Harim ,al Bab and MaaretTamsrin, targeting during 2 years (2022 and 2023) :410000 (IDPs 260000, Host communities 145000 and 5000 returnees) including 12000 PWDs, the number of targeted children under 5 years is 260000 ,140000 PLWs and 10000 of other caregivers including men. the scope of services include:
4 fixed CMAM center within the following health facilities:
1. Al-Hakeem PHC center level 3 in Afrin includes (OTP, TSFP, IYCF-E promotion and micronutrient interventions)
2. Kafr Naseh PHC center in Atareb subdistrict includes (OTP, TSFP, IYCF-E promotion and micronutrient interventions
3. SDI salqin PHC in Salqin includes ( OTP,TSFP, IYCF-E promotion, and micronutrient interventions) ,
4-Harim CMAM center in Harim subdistrict (OTP,TSFP, IYCF-E promotion and micronutrient interventions)
-One Stabilization center within Al Salam hospital located in Harim area providing required intervention and treatment for SAM with complication cases referred from SDI CMAM centers or other NGOs working there.
-16 Rapid Response teams that will be dispatched to the areas of high IDPs (1 RRT in Salqin,2 in Afrin, 3 in Al-Dana,4 in Harim,5 in Al Bab and 1 Maaret Tamsrin sub districts )to provide nutrition, child protection, health, and hygiene promoting services to children under 5 years of age and pregnant and lactating women with including people with disabilities and other caregivers including men.16 RRTs will screen of children 6-59 months and PLWs for malnutrition, referral and follow up who are malnourished, provide emergency IYCF counseling for caregivers including PLWs, disseminating IYCF-E messages on recommended feeding practices, and distributing micronutrients for preventing anemia and malnutrition among children and PLWs.
in addition to referral for CU5 and PLWs with dangers signs and Medical problems to get required health services
Moreover ,they will support hygiene promotion and rising WASH awareness by providing Key messages to prevent the risk of COVID 19 transmission and other infections .
As a part of COVID 19 response, SDI is going to apply adjustments of the nutrition activities to mitigate the risk of infection while ensuring continuity of life-saving nutrition services, confirm on the importance to use PPEs ,strict sterilization, social distancing etc. to ensure from the safety of staff and beneficiaries.
As well as, SDI will conduct training to build capacity of frontline nutrition services providers on IYCF-E and CMAM programs taking into account the current context of COVID-19.
SDI will coordinate and receive the needed nutrition supplies for this project from UNICEF through PAC organization and WFP.

4 - Needs

The project implementation areas are considered one of the most densely populated areas, especially with IDPs where the population figure of NW Syria show that Harim sub district has 71515,Afrin sub-district has 184525,AlDana sub district has1089704,Salqin sub district has 221078and Maret Tamisrin has 272427 populations. And according to 2022HNO/PiN,the project areas like Al Dana subdistrict which has 0.91 million people in need and Mareet Tamisrin has 0.26 M, which are considered ones of the most subdistricts with highest PiN(49% of those PiN is female,52%of them women of reproducive age(15-49),and 11% childen<5,as well as 35% of PiN are displaced people and 28%people with disabilities)which also considered high rates and require intervention to meet their unmet basic health and nutrition needs .The nutrition surveillance report by the nutrition cluster/Sep 2021covering 35 Sub districts across NWS, and involving 8,119 PLW (43.8% of whom were married at an age younger than18)and 26,428 CU5, That report informed that GAM (global acute malnutrition) rate was 1.33 %, SAM (severe acute malnutrition) 0.2 %, MAM (moderate acute malnutrition) 1.12 %, MAM among PLWs 9.6%.. All of the above pieces of information highly inform the need to sustain and scale up the nutrition response to people in need especially in the current context of COVID19 who worsed the already fragile health system and contributed in increase the morbidity and mortality rate.

5 - Implementation of activities

SDI will operate :4 fixed CMAM centers within the health facilities:1-Al-Hakeem PHC center in Afrin includes(OTP,TSFP,IYCF-E )2-Kafr Naseh PHC center in Abin Saman in Atareb subdistrict includes (OTP, TSFP, IYCF-E )3-SDI Salqin PHC center in Salqin includes(OTP,TSFP,IYCF-E )4-And Harim CMAM center within Harim area, SDI will screen all CU5 years and PLW who visit the CMAM clinics, select beneficiaries through MUAC screening and/or(Z-score),provide SAM treatment for children without compliction using Plumpy nut and MAM treatment for CU5 and PLW with MAM using Plumpy sup,and refer SAM with complication to SC to receive the required intervention,also providing essential WASH services in CMAM sites,micronutrient supplementation,one-to-one counseling on WHO-recommended(IYCF-E)and maternal nutrition practices and disseminate IYCF- E messages.As well as,SDI will function one SC in Al Salam hospital located in Harim subdistrict for SAM with complication and 16 RRTs that will be dispatched to Al Bab,Salqin,Afrin,Al Dana ,Harim,and Maaret Tamsrin subdistricts to provide nutrition,child protection,health,and hygiene promoting services and referral.M&E team will conduct field visits to ensure the quality of services, verifying the raw data before preparing HIS report,conducting beneficiaries’ satisfaction surveys,Observational checklists-following complaints.SDI will share the requested reports including 4Ws and others with doners and cluster.

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk related to GBV on CHWs when visiting the homes, also risks of GBV on benefciaries and health staff in health facilities Likelihood: Medium, Impact: High, Mitigation measure: each two CHWs will work together, training of health staff on providing first-line support, identification of GBV cases,Identification of safe referral pathways to ensure comprehensive management of GBVcases ,Risk of sexual exploitation by humanitarian workers of women and girls, Likelihood: Medium, Impact: High, Mitigation measure: Establish a confidential complaint mechanism, including victim assistance, train staff on the code of conduct, including the code of conduct of all staff contracts and address any SEA complaints, SDI is a member of PSEA network. Risk of discrimination or if a service is provided to specific groups or individuals at the exclusion of others. Likelihood: Low, Impact:High Mitigation : the project design encompasses accessibility of services by vulnerable groups (physical accessbility..etc) to ensure from equal access for all beneficires including PWDs through doing rehabilitation work, which includes installation of ramps, handrails and WCs special for PWDs to increas thier access to receive the health service. Risk: Increase outbreak of COVID19 ,Mitigation :SDI will strict apply precautions measures to protect the staff and patients in accordance to IPC guideline and Integrate RCCE to enhance applying all prevention measures.


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Beneficiaries are at risk on being exposed to any sexual misconduct by the humanitarian workers and they might not report the misconduct due to the following reason: 1. lack of knowledge of reporting mechanisms, especially for people with disabilities and women living in far away areas. 2. feeling afraid of retaliation. 3. fear of stigma. 4. fear of deprivation of assistance. 5. being unable to understand written banners for being illiterate and/or for having a visual disability. 6. for children, language might be complicated To mitigate these risks, SDI will : 1. aim at clearly announcing the hotline number and email specialized for PSEA of SDI and UN numbers. through many means, like, banners, social media, and messages to beneficiaries phones through telegram channel to ensure reaching to the biggest number of beneficiaries including women living in far away areas and people with disabilities. 2. Conduct PSEA training for all staff members and contractors/suppliers dealing with beneficiaries and make them read and sign CoC that state Zero Tolerance towards SEA violations. 3. Conduct awareness raising sessions on PSEA for beneficiaries to encourage them to report any violations and stress on the confidentiality and on eliminating any other fears that they might have. 4. Conduct awareness raising sessions for children in an appropriate language. 5. complaint boxes 6. Using PSEA field and organizational checklists tools.


11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumption1: Regular provision of nutrition supplies and available to targeted benefciaries. Risk1: Access to targeted areas becomes hindered . Mitigation: Stock prepositioning in multiple, safe, and close locations to delivery sites Assumption2: Technical skills are available. Risk2: shortage in the specialized technical staff in the targeted areas. Mitigation measure2: More staff workers will be trained to provide appropriate services. Assumption3: No further deterioration of the security situation. Risk3: Attacks on health facilities and health workers during moving between houses. Mitigation measure3: SDI will set up CMAM centers in safe and accessible locations that can be easily reached by beneficiaries. Moving the center to another safe area to avoid the risk of bombing. Assumption 4: All children and women especially those who may have physical limitations are able to reach services. Risk: Traditional habits prevent women from leave homes, physical disabilities prevent some people to access services Mitigation: SDI will bring the services close to communities and camps through running 16 RRTs,to include all people in need with focusing on children, women and adolescent girls,Risk:COVID19 pandimic, mitigation :strict applying IPC, providing PPEs and other safety measures,risk:non renewal of SCR,mitigation: SDI has channels of funding and has cross-border access to pass commodities to Syria with its wide local coordination with local authorities
