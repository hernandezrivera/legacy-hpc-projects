Project Summary

The project main goal is to contribute towards the resilience and cohesion of communities and households by improving housing in the targeted communities through rehabilitation of damaged apartments or providing emergency shelter kits in Aleppo, Rural Damascus, Lattakia, and Deir-ez-Zor.
The project aims to support to sustainably rehabilitate housing to affected and returned families in the most vulnerable areas. The response modalities used will include in-kind and direct assistance to implement small-scale rehabilitation works for private apartments of returnees and affected people in the targeted areas.
Since the con ict in Syria before nine years most of the population has been forced to the flee their homes often multiple times which affected the quality of life and exposed them to multiple risks and violations of their rights, there have been countless deaths and devastating injuries, illness and no humanitarian dignity.
The results of the crisis variables and the secured situations of many areas and cities in Syria have led to various needs.
These results are obvious particularly in areas that are receiving newly returnee families. Despite the large destruction and damage to facilities and buildings, an active returning movement of IDPs has been noticed to Aleppo, Deir ez-Zor, Homs, Hama, Southern area, Rural Latakia and Rural Damascus

4 - Needs

Since the beginning of the crisis in 2011, Syria has witnessed significant challenges in the humanitarian and security situation across the country, an intensification in the targeting of civilian infrastructure. The major cities affected by the crisis that demands urgent help in almost all sectors are targeted areas.Since 2014 GOPA launched her own registration system which is BRMS (beneficiary’s registration management system), and more than 121,350 people originally from targeted areas have been officially registered their request to support them in shelter assistance.Many IDPs returning to their houses in targeted areas discover their properties were looted. These families urgently need additional NFIs. During the past years, the negative impact of the war has left these areas almost deprived of its basic resources. Attacks against schools, hospitals, water networks, electricity plants, places of worship, economic assets and other civilian infrastructure has left these areas in ruins and have contributed to the exacerbation of the humanitarian situation in the city, leaving its returnees in need of all kinds of support, in all sectors.Based on HNO 2021, The shelter situation has continued to deteriorate given the unavailability of adequate and affordable shelter, the limited response for longer-term solutions, new challenges for communal shelters with COVID-19. An estimated 31 per cent of the overall population continue to live in inadequate shelter conditions, with returnees and IDPs being the worst affected. • The Shelter PiN increased to 5.9 million in 2021 which is a four per cent increase from 2020 (5.7 million).

5 - Implementation of activities

The following procedures will be applied to the implementation of the project:1-Location: Important factors should be taken in consideration for the targeted locations. Availability of sewerage, water, electricity.2-Needs assessment: A ﬁeld team have conducted a need assessment in the targeted areas, the report indicates that the top priority needs is the rehabilitation of private houses due to the big number of IDPs. 3-Beneﬁciaries selecting criteria: The following criteria should be applied in selecting beneﬁciaries such as in summary: -Poor household, widows, orphaned children. -Families with disabled, elderly. -Families with a sick member (chronic diseases).-Big number of families. -Household without ﬁxed income, are female-headed household. 4-Building criteria: According to the classiﬁcation of GOPA for the damages of the houses:1-Totally damaged building: it is not accepted. 2-Partially damaged building: it is accepted if the building is saved.3-The building sound: accepted. 5-BOQ: Usually, every house should have a suﬃcient BOQ consisting all the works should be conducted for repairing the house to be able for living. -The kind and quantity should be according To budget. - Rehabilitation should consist of living and bedrooms, kitchen, bathroom, W.C. BOQs are being explained directly with beneﬁciaries to give them the right to share the work. - Some necessary modiﬁcation may be made during the implementation phase as repairing needs. 6- Announcement for bidding in a public newspaper. 8- Implementation: The contractor start the works based on approved a time schedule.9- Handing over the rehabilitated apartment to the beneficiaries, accompanied by the municipality representative during the handover operations

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Protection Risk: Increase tension between beneﬁciaries due to difference in assessments and in providing services, lead to feeling of discrimination. likelihood: Medium.Impact: High. Mitigating Measure: 1- Apply the need assessment to ﬁxed criteria with all sector partners. 2- Sharing the community representative in this stage of intervention. 3- Activate the feedback with beneﬁciaries.Protection Risk: Risk of hurts to family’s community or owners due to unsound buildings or from ERW during preparing BOQ and implementation. likelihood: Medium. Impact: High. Mitigating Measure: 1- Structural assessment for building by qualiﬁed engineers and ERW experts in coordination with army engineers and UNMAS. 2- repair the building after assuring the soundness and safety of building. 3- clarify for workers about the dangers of ERW and to inform about any strange materials. All our activities use a rights-based approach to provide a protective environment that promotes equity and non-discrimination, reinforcing the basic social and human rights of participants while taking into consideration the cultural, historical and social specification of targeted populations. Also, all our volunteers have already received a package of training including: psychological first aid, non-violent communication, protection induction, basics of SGBV, first aid, PSEA and risk management in case of any emergency, as well as other basic information that enables them to act towards any person in need with total respect and most importantly to deliver assistance while protecting the dignity of the person.
