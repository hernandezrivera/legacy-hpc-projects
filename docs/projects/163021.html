Project Summary

This project considers the vulnerabilities of rural and urban populations as a result of multiple hazards, mainly drought, floods and disease outbreaks worsened by the on-going economic crisis. The primary attention is given to children under five years at risk of dying due to acute malnutrition, providing life-saving nutrition treatment to 36,038 children, and protecting the nutritional status of 606,349 children under five years and pregnant and lactating from deteriorating. UNICEF will therefore target all 56 districts, included 25 districts with a high intensity nutrition intervention package, across all the country’s 8 provinces and urban centres of Harare, Bulawayo and Chitungwiza. The High Intensity package will include all the emergency response activities from intensive community level screening, including campaigns, acute malnutrition treatment, IYCF, Micronutrient supplementation as well as cluster coordination at sub-national level.

1 - Activities

• Strengthen coordination through weekly/monthly emergency coordination meetings and joint support visits at national and subnational levels• Procure essential nutrition commodities (RUTF, F75, F100, Micronutrient supplements), and anthropometric equipment (MUAC tapes, weighing scales, height boards) and support nutrition commodity supplies management and distribution in coordination with NatPharm, Pharmacy, Nutrition departments and NGOs• Conduct refresher/trainings for community based and facility-based health workers on IMAM, active screening, IYCF-e, data collection and reporting. • Active screening, for early identification, referral and treatment of acute malnutrition. • Micronutrient supplementation (Vitamin A and Micronutrient Powders (MNPs) for children and Iron and Folic Acid for pregnant women) will be done both in the health facilities and in the communities. • Provide Maternal, Infant and Young Child Feeding (IYCF-e) counselling and support to mothers and caregivers of children below 2 years including communication material and support to Food security sector on food demonstration and use of specialized supplementary food.• Integrated WASH, health, HIV messaging to ensure communities receive accurate and appropriate information to support nutrition and child care practices in humanitarian situation including the mainstreaming of gender, disability, HIV, and protection issues in the response. • Integrated outreach services to improve coverage of the hard to reach areas• Strengthen Information Management and conduct nutrition assessment, to update the information on the nutritional situation of children and women in the most vulnerable districts.• Setting up mechanisms to ensure Accountability to Affected people (AAP) and capacitate sector partners and all stakeholders on the importance of Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse to protect the vulnerable populations.
