Project Summary

Towards increased enrollment and retention of school-age girls and boys, the project seeks to facilitate access to educational services for 10,000 individuals; 7000 girls and 3000 boys, through provision of scholastic materials, enrolment of out of school children, reenrollment of school dropouts and retention of children attending primary schools, towards graduation and progression to secondary school in some selected primary schools of Demsa, Girei and Song LGAs, Adamawa State. The project is also designed to equip the girls and boys with some life skills (creativity, problem solving, communication skills, leadership skills etc.) to help them navigate the huddles and enhance attraction towards regular school attendance. Adolescents aged 10-17 years will be engaged in sexual and reproductive health (SRH) and personal hygiene sessions, towards GBV risk mitigation and prevention.
Stakeholders including; Teachers, School Based Management Committees (SBMCs), Mothers associations, parents and mentors will be trained on education awareness (enrolment drive campaign), PSEA and local resource mobilization for sustainability.

1 - Needs

In line with Humanitarian Response Plan on Education for the year 2023 in Adamawa State, there is an alarming number of school-aged children (especially girls) that are out of school, school dropouts and those not attending schools regularly. This is due vulnerability of guardians, caregivers and threat factors in some communities, including economic vulnerability, gender norms and practices, insurgency, herders-famers clash and poor prioritization for girls child education among caregivers.In the selected LGAs, Demsa Girei and Song, there is a number of People In Need (PIN) of educational services aged 6-17 years in the host communities, 1091 girls and 1005 boys, 2285 girls and 2105 boys and 7962 girls and 7335 boys respectively, giving rise to a total of 21783 (11338 girls and 10445) people in need of educational services.Hence, the project seeks to facilitate access to educational services for 10,000 direct beneficiaries and other indirect beneficiaries through impact saturation.

2 - Activities

The following activities will be conducted:Conduct inaugural session with all G4G Groups : This activity will be conducted to introduce the groups formed to their responsibilities as members of G4G.Carryout Mentoring & hands-on supportive supervision of the G4G Group: This visit is to see how the girls are faring in the groups and what impacts their being part of the group is making on their academic performance. The 200 project schools in selected LGAs will be covered in 9 months.Facilitate G4G Girls participation and conduct supportive supervision of reading hub activities at school level: This visit is to encourage the girls in the G4G groups to participate actively in the reading hub.Support the implementation of life skills such as citizenship, transferable skills etc. in the G4G schools: The project is expected to provide this, and the CSOs would monitor its implementation.Facilitate a bi-annual G4G Leaders Radio Program: 5 Leaders of the G4G groups will be engaged in a radio talk in 2 shows which will be aired 3 times in the first year of the project. They will speak about the impact of the project and other topics that will encourage other girls listening to enroll in schools, be retained and transit to the secondary school.Support Bi-monthly Spelling Bee drill, reading festivals, debates, and drama amongst the G4G groups at school level: HARAF shall support all the 200 schools in preparation for the spelling Bee and the debate competition.Organize Inter-G4G reading festival and drama among G4G schools at the state level: The winners of the competition at the LGA level will assemble to compete at the State level.Random Mentoring session with Mothers Association (MA) members at their monthly meetings: These random visits are to access the activities of the MAs and encourage them to do more.Conduct a Quarterly MA Leaders Peer Learning meeting at the clustered level: The 100 MAs’ leaders are divided into 4 clusters for effectiveness in each of the quarter, the aim of this meeting is for MA leaders to share experiences, challenges and new ideas that will further strengthen the association from their fellow leaders.Facilitate MA Radio Program: This radio programme is meant for the leaders of the MA to reach out to a wider population and encourage other women to join the efforts of encouraging girl child education.Conduct Random supportive supervision to the He4She Boys’ Step down training: When the leaders of the He4She have been trained by HARAF, the leaders are expected to do a step-down training for other members of their group. We will conduct supportive supervision visit randomly to see how the trainings are held.Conduct a 1-day training for selected MA Leaders on resource mobilization and advocacy at the clusters within the LGA: Members of the Mother’s Association need to mobilize resources in order to carry out their supportive role to girl child education and on how to conduct profitable advocacy visits; some of their leaders will be selected for the training which will be conducted in clusters within the LGA (4 clusters shall be used) and they will be expected to step-down the trainings they will receive to other members of the association.Conduct a 3-day vocational training for 200 selected MA members at cluster level: The 3-day vocational trainings for the selected Mothers’ Association will be on re-usable sanitary pads to be held at cluster level.Conduct random supportive supervision to the male champions step down orientation: Just as the He4She leaders will be trained and expected to carry out a step-down training.Training of stakeholders on education awareness (enrolment drive campaign), PSEA and local resource mobilization and monthly stakeholders meeting for sustainability Conduct need assessment for distribution of scholastic materials.Distribution of scholastic materials.
