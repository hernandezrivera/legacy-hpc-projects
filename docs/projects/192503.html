Project Summary

ERP 2022
January 2022- December 2022
The overall objective of the proposed project is to address the urgent protection and emergency needs of communities with specific needs, particularly vulnerable Migrant Workers in Lebanon suffering from socio-economic and health challenges due to the COVID-19 lockdown and the deteriorating socio-economic situation, in order to reduce the risk of evictions, exploitation, abuse and human trafficking through provision of emergency basic assistance of NFIs, Voluntary Return (VR), temporary shelters through Cash for Rent (CfR as a sub form of MPCA), access to protection services (case management and socio-legal services), MHPSS and awareness raising for preventive measures.
The project aims at ensuring under the respective objectives of the ERP 2022:
Objective 1 Protection Services
Activity 1: Social counselling and registration of all migrants requesting support (at least 720) to ensure they meet the vulnerability criteria to be eligible through Amel helplines and referrals from NGOs, migrant communities, embassies, GOs, among others.
Activity 2: Protection to least 3000 vulnerable migrant cases with at least 192 with severe case management faced with violence, abuse, exploitation, human trafficking, smuggling among other challenges that Amel team is specialized in providing for more than ten years.
Activity 3: Legal Counseling, legal aid and access to justice for migrant identified with challenging cases, salary theft, in detention, among other legal challenges by Amel expert lawyers in migrant issues for more than ten years.
Activity 4: Delivery of 42 Psycho-social support and legal awareness sessions to migrants to at least 850 migrants from different nationalities, to ensure psychological support and referral to a psychologist for identified cases for individual therapy.
Objective 2: Temporary Shelters
Activity 1: Ensure support to 1800 Cash for Rent (CfR) to vulnerable migrant workers, especially migrant with children, and community shelters (The indirect target is estimated to 9000) for protection from evictions, homelessness, violence and abuse. The CfR is a sub-form of MPCA that allows to support all the migrants included the undocumented where most are identified with high vulnerability.
Activity 2: Awareness raising material to employers for prevention of abuse in rent increase.
Activity 3: Provision of 1800 hygiene kits, 1440 dignity kits (incl. pads), and 620 baby kits.
Objective 3: Voluntary Return
Activity 1: Counselling and Voluntary Return (VR) to 800 Migrant workers back to their home countries including all the processes of waivers of penalties and documentation to travel (this target including migrants in detention)
Activity 2: Support of PCR tests to migrants before travel.
Activity 3: Legal Support and clearances with the respective general security offices, embassies and employers
After the COVID-19 and COVID-19 Crises Amel already succeeded to support thousands of migrants and their families with relief items, voluntary return, cash for rent, PCR testing, access to health services among others. The processes and coordination with relevant stakeholders are already in place, i.e. channels of communication with General Security, Embassies, relevant ministries, more than 20 migrant communities, among others.
NB:
- It is important to note that the majority of the vulnerable migrants are undocumented, that requires additional effort of supporting documents.
- The project aims to target 70% female migrants, 20% male migrants incl. Sudanese, 10% girls/boys

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

The project is in the first place designed to be responsive and remedial, to counter the protection concerns of the target population, whilst ensuring that they are involved in creating their own safe protection environment. No intervention will be taken without the expressed will and involvement of the targeted individual. Solar Systems are requested in running cost budget line to allow a minimal KVA in the respective migrants’ centers not to jeopardize the flow of the project (i.e. turn on computers and ensure a minimal operation in light) in response to the diesel/electricity crises. -- This project is related to the following sectors: Protection, Shelter, WASH Sector, Migrant Workers and Health Sectors

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

Amel is a member of the CHS alliance and a strong advocate of Humanitarian Accountability to beneficiaries, in addition to donors. Amel has a number of well-practiced quality assurance mechanisms and accountability strategies in place. Constant communication between the project coordination team and field staff, as well as regular field visits ensures good interaction between all staff involved in the project. This allows feedback for rapid responses to changing dynamics in the field. Additionally, this program has a hotline for (P)VoHT, including vulnerable migrants at risk of sexual/labor exploitation. The Do No Harm principles are embedded in the project design, and social stability factors are taken into account. The project builds on existing deep-rooted relationships developed with local communities. Services have been identified by engaging with over 26 migrant communities in Lebanon. The team who developed the proposal has a great understanding of the context & risk mitigation
