Project Summary

The WASH Sector is actively engaged in the national coordination and preparedness planning processes supporting the national Public Health Emergency Operations Centre (PHEOC) in scaling up preparedness and response efforts and implementation of related recommendations suggested by International Health Regulations (IHR) emergency committee. Currently, Zimbabwe is facing multiple hazards driven by drought, economic crisis and the residual impact of the Cyclone Idai, where the humanitarian situation is deteriorating with 6.7M people in need of acute humanitarian assistance and services. COVID-19 is an additional burden further stretching the fragile social systems in place, especially with regards to water, sanitation and hygiene (WASH) services.
This project focuses on the immediate measures that must be undertaken with due priority to ensure preparedness and response actions to mitigate the COVID-19 outbreak in Quarantine Centres and or ports of entry. The response strategy takes cognizance of the emerging needs and gaps identified in quarantine centres and ports of entry and will also build the significant investments which have been made over the past years in supporting WASH Service delivery, The WASH Sector is supporting 3 of the 8 national COVID-19 response plan; namely WASH Infection prevention and control (IPC); (2) Risk communication and community engagement (RCCE); and (3) Coordination, planning and monitoring. The WASH Sector response strategy for Quarantine’s centres include;
• Ensuring adequate supply of safe water to 60 priority public quarantine centres across the country through borehole repairs, rehabilitation of piped networks and new borehole drilling in centres without access to safe water
• Ensuring provision of adequate and appropriate sanitation through quick repairs to existing facilities and or upgrading of plumping networks. This will also entail;
 Provision of WASH Hygiene kits (buckets, IEC materials, PoU water treatment chemicals and soap)
 Provision of MHM kits
 Provision of cleaning and disinfection materials
• Dissemination of key health and hygiene messaging within the population in quarantine centres
• Providing access to critical handwashing facilities including soap and alcohol-based sanitizers,
