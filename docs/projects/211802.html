Project Summary

Intervention and implementation of activities
Objectives/Purpose: Reduce morbidity and mortality rates to access equitable, high-quality health services and enhance protection services for those most in need.
• Operating two (2) fixed medical teams in Al-Hawl camp in the first and second sectors
• Operating a mobile medical team (1) in Arisha camp
• Operating a fixed medical team (1) in the Mahmoudli camp in Raqqa
• Operating (1) mobile medical team in the city of Al-Sabkha in Raqqa
• Providing medical consultations
• Purchasing and providing medicines
• Providing protection and prevention services from sexual exploitation and abuse.
• Providing dignity baskets for women and girls most in need, at a rate of (50) baskets.
Targeting areas (Al-Hawl Camp - Al-Arisha Camp - Al-Mahmoudli Camp - Al-Sabkha City)

Risk analysis
In public health emergencies, the risk of sexual exploitation and abuse increases. Women and children in particular face increased protection risks. The increase in the number of new responders (including non-traditional humanitarian responders) coupled with high demand and inequalities in food supplies, health supplies and protection increases the risks and exposes children to the risk of potential harm. There are greater difficulties in accessing health services, increased burden and separation from caregivers. Quarantine and severe illness/death may increase sexual exploitation and abuse, especially of girls and children, including forced marriage.
Recommended actions
Based on its humanitarian responsibilities, Al Mawaddah Charitable Society is primarily concerned with applying and adhering to the principles of non-harm through project designs, activities and results. The Association will spare no effort in following these principles and mainstreaming protection throughout the life cycle of the project, including coordination with partners, ensuring a safe environment, providing equitable access to project services without discrimination on the basis of gender, color, sect, or race, and promoting social cohesion. This is done by all employees, suppliers, trainers and stakeholders signing the Code of Ethical Conduct. Staff will be trained on protection from sexual exploitation and abuse.
