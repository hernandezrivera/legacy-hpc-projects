Project Summary

Estimates show that about 100,000 IDPs are now living in the Blue Nile region. This is as a result of the current conflict in Khartoum and also as a result of the tribal tension and other security conditions occurred within the region in 2022. IDPs from Khartoum mostly are staying in Damazine and ArRusyaris localities while Inter-communal violence that spread into the Blue Nile have affected Ar Rusyaris ,Wad Al Mahi , Damazine and Geissan localities according to the International Organization for Migration Displacement Tracking Matrix (IOM DTM).
There are increasing reports of gender-based violence (GBV), particularly among internally displaced people fleeing from one state to another, as well as an increased number of domestic violence cases, according to the UN Population Fund.
Access to education have been severely affected by the inter- communal violence happened in 2022 and by the recent flow of IDPs from Khartoum. The November 2022 interagency report indicates that there are 40 basic and 18 secondary schools have been affected by the conflict. For example, 14 have been affected in Wad Al Mahi , 15 have been affected at Ar Rusayris , 21 at Geissan localities, and in AlBan-Jadid (8) village of Ed Damazine town. There are 16,472 students in the assessed areas, including 2,845 in Wad Al Mahi, 5,780 in Ar Rusayris, 4,647 in Geisan localities, and 3,200 in Al-Ban-Jadid village of the Ed Damazine town. Due to the insecurity, 40 schools are closed in 7 villages of the three localities. All schools in Dam Town 5 of Wad Al Mahi locality, Dam Town 9 of Geisan locality, and Al-Azaza village of Ar Rusayris localities schools are occupied by IDPs. About 4,200 children are out of school as IDPs are using the school compound as shelter. No schools have been damaged or destroyed, although some furniture and learning materials have been looted.
The displacement to Blue Nile have added pressure on the already limited resources.
The purpose of this primary education support project in Blue Nile is to Provide improved structured quality of lifesaving education for crisis-affected children and enhance structured lifesaving learning that promotes personal well-being within a protective environment for 10,256 targeted IDPs, returnees, and vulnerable local communities.. 9620 (5700 female)school-aged children, 200 teachers (120 female) and 100 community leaders (45 female) and 50 (20-female) persons with disability and affected children, IDPs and host communities have access to quality education. This will be through providing emergency teaching and learning materials, Support teachers/facilitators with emergency incentives, train Teachers/facilitators on Psychosocial, safe schools, GBV, children’s rights and violence free learning, including child protection issues, PTAs and community training on how to support schools and people with disabilities. The project will also provide learning materials as well as provision of school uniforms, pay exam fees and seating for the poorest students at the eight schools to fill the gap in the educational needs in these eight schools, ( Alshaheed Afendi school, Alagageer school, Medium Tiwek, Bobok schools at Bao locality; and Bodos Sharik, Ajib Almunjulk at Ar Roseires locality, Zaim Alasary and Alyasir schools in Damazine)
Windle Trust International will use its expertise and work experience in Blue Nile for efficient and cost effective implementation of this project

1 - Needs

The following schools have been affected by the recent conflict in Khartoum and the Blue Nile and this project is designed to strengthen the capacity of these schools. The needs of these schools are as follow: 1. Dilapidated latrines and sharing of latrines with boys and girls 2. Poor learning spaces due to destroyed classrooms with no desks as children sit on stones 3. Poverty, as most parents are unable to meet basic school needs such as notebooks, pens and uniforms 4. Lack of teaching and learning materials for teachers and students 5. Inadequate support from parents and community to schools thus all burden left on the government. 6. Poor quality of teaching due to the presence of untrained and demotivated teachers 7. Stigma in the community about the education of children with disability 8 High dropout rates among the girl child due to cultural barriers and early marriages

2 - Activities/Outputs

The objective of this project is to Enhance structured lifesaving learning that promotes personal well-being within a protective environment in the targeted schools to benefit from improved quality of education, through ensuring teachers are well trained and equipped with requisite teaching materials, and that the most vulnerable children are supported. The following activities will be implemented -Rehabilitate latrines and work with schools’ communities to ensure there are separate latrines for boys, girls and teachers. -Renovate eight classrooms two for each school with ramps at the entrance to enable easy access to children with disability. -Procure school desks to improve learning environment for the children in the eight schools - support to girls’ education and children with disabilities. -Provide Hygiene kits for eight schools -Provide sport and recreational kits to eight schools
