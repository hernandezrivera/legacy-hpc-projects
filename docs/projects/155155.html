Project Summary

The rapid and massive increase of the refugee population, concentrated in the south of Cox’s Bazar district, has had an enormous impact on Bangladeshi host communities’ food security, economic vulnerability, market access, labour opportunities and environment. The increasing impact on host communities’ livelihoods is contributing to social tensions and resentment between the local population and refugees, particularly over natural resources such as land, water, fish and firewood. The need to support local communities’ livelihoods is now the supreme need to overcome this situation.
The food and nutrition security of the poorest amongst the host community is a growing concern. Many have lost access to previously farmed lands (often government forest land) and have simultaneously lost work opportunities to cheaper refugee labour market. Forest products on which they previously depended are no longer available. It has also become increasingly clear that the response actors will need to increase their focus on host community food security, particularly in hard-hit communities. In addition, natural disasters and deforestation may prolong and worsen food insecurity situation for both host communities and Rohingya FDMN and exacerbate the likelihood of natural disaster and its impact on both communities.
This one-year project will target 11,000 households from the refugee and host community to reduce food insecurity, and support individuals with livelihoods and nutritional support. The project will also support the Rohingya and host community populations during the lean period through providing cash for multi-purpose activities.

Activities or outputs

Output-1: Enhance household ability to fulfil nutritious food diversity and choice through utilizing homesteadgarden.Project will support establishment of vegetable and fruits gardens, Small scale poultry/livestock in homestead areas. Selected 3,000 beneficiaries will receive 3 days training on comprehensive homestead development (CHD) and also receive money to implement different Comprehensive Homestead Development (CHD) interventions. Output-2: Introduce and establish poor people friendly Income generating activities (IGAs) to increase household level income.Based on the local demand and existing practice project will support the target beneficiary to establish different income generation activities that inspire female engagement. Output-3: Reduce lean period of vulnerable household through CFW. Output-4: Establish beneficial cohesion between the neighbour Host and Rohingya community.Village Development Committee (VDC) members’ training on human rights and refugee rights, linkage workshop between Rohingya consumer group and Host producer group, VDC joint meeting with Rohingya community leader and interactive meeting with farmer group and market actors will be organized to ensure protection and peaceful coexistence between Host and Rohingya community. Output-5: Targeted community members and service providers are better prepared for, mitigate and respond to disaster and adapt to climate change. Food Security Disaster contingency systems are in place and functioning.
