Project Summary

This project is designed to meet the needs of 80% IDPs and 20% host communities of the total beneficiaries targeted, the proposed project will be an extension of the ongoing GBV Prevention and Response, and coordination mechanisms CCCM Project being implemented by PSA and funded by UNHCR in Bosaso, Qardho, Bandarbeyl, Garoowe and Burtinle.
Population displacement due to drought and conflict remains a serious concern, of the total displaced population, 2,341,411 million people are living in IDP sites with the most severe needs. An estimated 351,211 of them are persons with various disabilities while 47% of the displaced are women. IDP sites lack dignified living conditions, sufficient access to basic services, land tenure and adequate resources to sustain their populations.
There are over 2,400 recorded IDP sites across Somalia. An approximate, 85% of the sites are informal settlements on private land and about 74% of them are in urban areas according to the Detailed Site Assessment (DSA) of the CCCM Cluster. Existing coordination and management mechanisms are largely informal, with most sites being managed by landlords or gatekeepers. These mechanisms have limited accountability and little adherence to minimum standards.
In Bosaso there are 128,947 displaced persons, on severity 18,421 are in phase 3, while 47, 895 in phase 4 and 62, 632 in phase 5, in Gardo there are 18, 308 displaced persons, on severity 2562 are in phase 3, while 7594 in phase 4 and 8152 in phase 5, in Garowe there are 31, 565 displaced persons, on severity 4304 are in phase 3, while 8609 in phase 4 and 18, 622 in phase 5, in Bandarbayla there are 1,744 displaced persons, on severity 244 are in phase 723 in phase 4 and 776 in phase 5 and in Burtinle there are 5000 displaced persons and all of them in phase 5 severity.
Marginalized clans and vulnerable groups such as women and people with disabilities are reported to be particularly affected and at risk of discrimination. Persons with disability face several barriers such as separation from family members, loss of assistive devices, access to information and humanitarian assistance during displacement in the informal and unplanned settlements.
GBV Information Management System (GBVIMS) continue to show high prevalence of sexual violence, physical assault, and other forms of GBV among women and girls, particularly those living in the IDP settlements.
The project will ensure that all participants have equal access to resources and training. Monitoring and evaluation will be based on gender, age and diversity dis-segregated data. An additional settlement mapping exercises from the previous one conducted by PSA will be conducted to get the information of all targeted settlements for necessary services in place and gap analysis, with our ongoing project we have noted various gaps that need to be addressed in the next phase of project, during our assessment the IDPs prioritized the following needs as urgent.
• Rehabilitation of solar lights
• Construction of IDP community centres
• Garbage collection and site maintenance
• Advocacy of Land and support relocations to public land when appropriate
In Bosaso, PSA will target 30,000 IDPs, 5000 in Qardo, 10,000 in Garowe, 500 in Bandarbayla and 5,000 in Burtinle.
