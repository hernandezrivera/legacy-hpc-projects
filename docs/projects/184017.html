Project Summary

Darfur and Sudan as a whole is miserably suffering from the recent political changes and instability that occurred since 2021 and the rapid changes of the governance from top to state level and which means services from the government is not to be expected yet soon, furthermore , the current economic situation is hindering majority of Darfurians especially vulnerable to reach the basic needs as of which even food is hard to afford in number of households, however households that cannot afford education rely on humanitarian services. In addition to that , COVID-19 also contributed towards poverty in all areas of the country , restriction of economical activities such as cultivation, livestock and trade.This project targets to increase access to quality education for school aged children and increase the enrollments and Retention rates for school age children in Saraf Omra locality State. are one of the areas that have never witnessed any educational intervention although it is one of the most affected areas by Darfur conflict. The approximate population is over (3,000 household) 15000 Individuals and the assessed 3,000 IDPs,6,000 Returnees and 6,000 Host vulnerable communities. The areas has completely been neglected from Humanitarian services. According to DPI , UNDP , IOM and UNHCR assessment.

1 - Needs

As Sudan remains changing from regime to regime and signing of the Juba Peace Agreement (JPA) continues to progress , transitional government ending and citizens requesting for the government of democracy and civilian rules, within this process Sudan still faces a greater challenge of economic , security , and tensions of what will happen, the scarcity of the population number is rapidly increasing due to the former rebels who signed the peace agreements, However due to economic challenges and inflation of the Sudan prices, education and basic needs still remains the major constrains and challenges especially to the vulnerable communities especially IDPs, Returnees, Refugees and vulnerable host communities living especially in rural areas, the fees of the basic school students is increased to 40% per child per year compared to the year 2020, the fees for food and school nutrition is 36% increase compared to the Last year 2020 , the prices of school essential needs is 53% increase compared to previous year 2020 such as text books, exercise books , pens mathematical sets and etc.The challenges become into the households daily or monthly income to afford basic school fees and needs, during the DPI assessment curried out in the areas of saraf omra of North Darfur indicates that 70% of children (Boys and Girls) left schools due to the unaffordable situation occurring, the whole household is better to be sent for work rather than for education in order to increase the daily income of the household which is child labor. Children become at risk of violence during collection of firewood, working until late night in markets in order to receive pay which will sustain the household, early marriage is increasing with no option household needs to reduce the responsibilities within the household. Furthermore, as children are no longer encouraged to education due to economic situation, this has increased the number of early child recruit into the army due to lack of option, increased number of children involving into drugs

2 - Activities/Outputs

DPI’S intervention will be focusing on implementation of full packaged education and child protection sector through implementation of bellow activities. 1. Construction of 3 schools 2 in saraf omra IDP areas and 1 in Returnee and Host community area with 8 classrooms each. 2. Construction of 2 offices in each school. 3. Construction of 2 kitchen 1 in each school. 4. Construction of 6 units of latrines in each school. 5. Provision of furniture including classes and offices. 6. Provision of full packages of students scholastic materials. 7. Provision of teacher’s kits. 8. Formulation of PTA. 9. Conduct training to PTAS on child protection education in emergency and COVID-19 related issues. 10. Conduct training for the PTAs and community leaders on different topics such as Mine education, Disaster and Risk reduction, health and sanitation , COVID-19 and protection issues as ToT. 11. Provision of WASH facilities such as solid waste management. 12. Provision of water Tank. 13. Establishment of hand washing facilities. 14. Establishment of 1 CFS in each school. 15. Conduct child Sophocles social activities. 16 .distribution of masks to students and teachers
