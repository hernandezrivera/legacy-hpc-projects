Project Summary

AHF has 2 running CCTCs (Harim & Maar Tmasrin by SCHF) & Alsham isolation hospital (Dana by WHO) in response to COVID19 at NW Syria. In all the 3 facilities, there are identified gaps in FSL,
This project focuses on a live-saving food security intervention that meets the FSL cluster strategic objective 1 The proposed activities designed to meet the FSL cluster priority activities "Food Assistance": food voucher in the areas where markets are functional in high severity communities and IDPs hosting areas.
Even health staff and in-quarantine are provided with cooked meals, there is a gap in responding to family’s members who are affected. FSLs interventions will include:
1-Providing life-saving food assistance with one-time RTEs to 3000 HHs .
2-Providing life-saving food assistance with monthly restricted (60$) cash E-voucher to (1200 chronic disease patients with their families members) for 8 months. The 1200 HHs were selected according to specific criteria from the long list of chronic disease patients received from Idlib HD and validated by AHF team. Those 600 HHs are the most vulnerable to being affected by COVID19.
The beneficiaries will be provided with cash E-voucher to buy fresh food from the markets (where accessible) to prevent/reduce the risk of COVID19 infection. They will have freedom to choose from a variety of healthy food items including meat, chicken, vegetables etc.. the beneficiaries will receive cards and training to be able to buy their needs of specified food items .
