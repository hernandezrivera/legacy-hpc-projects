Project Summary

The impact of the protracted conflict, the socio-cultural attitudes towards protecting children or keeping children safe, especially for girls and the difficulties in ensuring a protective environment with adult support in predominantly rural Local Government Areas (LGAs) has been identified as key factors that has weakened the already weak protection system in the North-east . Thousands of children and marginalized girls especially have been exposed to various forms of protection concerns due to the emergency and children are forced to be separated from their parents and caregivers.
Whilst humanitarian efforts are striving to provide help to IDPs and supporting child protection and case anagement efforts for unaccompanied and separated children(UASCs), the rapid spread of COVID-19 in Nigeria has again affected the protection efforts leading children to be exposed to various forms of abuse while they stay home and within their communities. With very few social safety mechanisms provided for marginalised vulnerable groups, girls in IDPs and rural communities are more exposed to high risk of violence,abuse and exploitation within their communities which has negative effects on their physical and psychosocial wellbeing.
Marginalized or Unaccompanied and Separated children living in host communities and IDPs camps are exposed to various forms of abuse such as child labor, survivor sex, abuse and exploitation as they fend for themselves during this pandemic. The fear that schools will eventually open and the majority of girls will be pregnant is a serious concern for most parents while standing the risk of abduction and forced recruitment by local armed groups.
The need to engage in providing a comprehensive program to protect children in rural communities affected by the conflict requires a holistic approach which shall include case management, strengthening the complaint and referral mechanisms as well as strengthening community structures to support the wellbeing of children especially UASCs in the North-East necessitated the need for this project.

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

TPO will ensure that in all stages of the project, the centrality of protection ,ensure to incorporate protection principles and promoting meaningful access, safety and dignity in the project implementation. TPO will ensure to prioritise the safety & dignity of beneficiaries and those associated with the project , and avoid causing harm( Do-no-harm) The project will ensure to prevent and minimize as much as possible any potential challenge in the intervention which can increase people’s vulnerability to both physical and psychosocial risks.In terms of access, TPO and partners will ensure meaningful access to the services provided in proportion to the needs of beneficiaries and without any barriers such as discrimination etc. The project will pay a critical attention to individuals and groups who may be particularly vulnerable or have difficulty accessing assistance and services provided in the project.TPO will also ensure accountability to the affected population by setting up appropriate structures and mechanisms through which affected populations can measure the adequacy of interventions, and address concerns and complaints.TPO will ensure the full and meaningful participation into project activities as well as in accessing the services provided by the project.
