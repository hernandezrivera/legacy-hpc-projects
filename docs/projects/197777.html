1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

GN will provide water and sanitation assistance in the southern part of the Cabo Delgado province, and in the Metuge district, where the basic infrastructure is saturated due to accepting a large number of IDP (internally displaced people). This project plan to improve access to safe water and sanitation services for the target district residents, including the IDP population, and protect the health and livelihoods of the affected population. This project will focus on improving water and sanitation infrastructure where needs have been confirmed. The project will contribute significantly not only to the immediate needs of IDP and host communities affected by the long-recurring internal conflicts in the state, as well as those who are left behind in development, but also to reconstruction and development, thereby promoting a 'humanitarian-development-peace nexus'. It also contributes to human security by contributing to the development of grass-roots communities, conflict prevention, and promoting healthy and dignified individual livelihoods and environments.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

In Impire where communities without access to safe water, GN will construct a high-output water supply system facility that pumps up groundwater using solar panels to generate solar power, installs a 10 000 L (10t) tank on a 3.5m high reinforced steel tower, and delivers safe water through pipes to tap stands located in communities far from the water tower. A high-output water supply system facility will be constructed to deliver safe water via pipes to tap stands to be installed in communities away from the water tower. Additionally, GN will produce concrete floors (slabs), the most important part of a household toilet, and distribute them to residents. This support is essential for the dissemination of household latrines, and residents sensitized by hygiene awareness-raising activities construct their own household latrines from the distributed slabs.
