Project Summary

The project aims to contribute to increased access to meet food for the IDPs and most vulnerable households in Aleppo and Idleb Governorates. To attain this, IhsanRD proposes to distribute cash for food using e-voucher approach. The value of E-voucher transfers will be in an average of 77$/HH/round, for 8 rounds to cover the food needs to the value of 70% based on the coverage of the survival minimum expenditure basket (SMEB) as determined by the CWG and based on REACH price monitoring. Using the SMEB as the basis for a transfer value and conducting market assessments to ensure these items are available in the targeted markets means that households are likely to spend the funds on the intended basic food needs items, something which is monitored by PDMs.

3 - Needs

Syria faces a multitude of challenges that make it one of the world’s most complex humanitarian and protection emergencies. After eleven years of crisis during which the country has lost 42 places on the Human Development Index, most Syrians face a context of continuing humanitarian decline. Based on HNO 2023, Syria still has the largest number of internally displaced people in the world: 6.8 million people, and the highest number of people in need since the beginning of the crisis. Humanitarian and economic indicators continue to deteriorate, basic services are collapsing, and an ongoing cholera outbreak and climatic shocks compound an already dire situation. As a result of these challenges, Today, over 1.8 million IDPs living in 1,421 last resort IDP sites in north-west Syria who need assistance in 2023, an increase from 1.7 million in 2022. Also, as the Turkish lira, which was adopted as an alternative to the Syrian pound in 2020 in north-west Syria, has devalued to its lowest level against the dollar at 18.574 TRY in November 2022. The devaluation is felt by communities who have witnessed additional increases in the prices oIn addition, in some districts in Northwest Syria 12 percent of PLW were in need of treatment for acute malnutrition, while 54 percent of PLW suffered from iron-deficiency anemia (severity classification 5. The poor food and nutrition situation continues to worsen, and the adoption of negative coping strategies has impacted the resilience of household.

4 - Implementation of activities

Contracting with financial service provider (FSP): Procurement process to select the service supplier (for payment services) must be carried out, according to the total value of the fund’s transfer and the value of transfer fee. Contracting with system provider for e-voucher: The service provider will provide IhsanRD with NFC smart cards and POS terminal devices. IhsanRD would assign and distribute the NFC smart cards to BNF, then the project manager could top up BNF balances with approved amount. Market assessment: IhsanRD will conduct a rapid market assessment in the proposed locations and where the response will cover. This will ascertain the ability and capacity of markets to restock if demand increases. Voucher distribution&redemption: BNFs will receive their cards in the first round and use them in the remaining rounds. The BNFs will redeem the voucher at the contracted FSP under supervision of IhsanRD’s team.  Distribution&redemption monitoring: MEAL field team will monitor distribution and redemption points to make sure that processes are managed well besides ensuring that complaint mechanism and selection criteria are shared with the beneficiaries. Arranging BNFs’ payments: Confirming the paid amount of exchanged vouchers and transferring money to the FSP.  PDM: MEAL field team will interview a sample of BNFs including both gender and people with different vulnerable criteria to collect their feedback about the distribution process, and provided aid.

8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

PSEA is at the core of IhsanRD responses, and all IhsanRD staff must comply with it. Effective systems are in place for preventing and responding to SEA acts by integrating PSEA in IhsanRD policies and making sure that all personnel read and sign the code of conduct and that all contracts with local council/hawala agents include clauses on PSEA. IhsanRD also provides awareness training to all staff in the organization on PSEA principles.The proposed program is built taking fully into account: Do No Harm, Age, Gender, Diversity mainstreaming (AGDM), and commitment to providing dignity and equal access to assistance. The project has been designed based on the needs of all groups IhsanRD field team, local councils, community feedback. Before starting the project, IhsanRD will carefully analyze and map all the stakeholders present in the targeted areas, their roles, influences, and potential impact on the intervention. This will allow the project team to set the necessary participatory mechanisms and decide which stakeholders need to be involved in the different implementation stages.


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumptions: 1 Stable security situation during project implementation2 Hawala agents and system providers remained operational3 Local councils welcomed and facilitated the initiatives with no interference in the selection of beneficiaries (exclusion or inclusion of beneficiaries regardless of the criteria designed for the project)4 Timely disbursement of needed funds to start project activities Changing the security situation that may affect the implementation of project activities Mitigation: Monitoring and analysis security situation, temporary suspension of field works to protect the field team and BNFs of the expected risk. Dominant authorities interfered in the exclusion or inclusion of beneficiaries regardless of the selection criteria Mitigation: Early ensuring that the local authorities clearly informed through complete explanation of the objectives, timeline, and criteria for the support. The signed MoUs with local councils will illustrate that IhsanRD team will be fully independent in BNF selection according to the criteria developed, Delay in payments funds, which will delay to paying to the contracted shopkeepers and delay in starting the next rounds.Mitigation: Senior level of coordination with the donor and obtaining advance payments that cover at least direct costs.
