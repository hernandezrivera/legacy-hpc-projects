Project Summary

This project aims to address immediate WASH needs of 58,050 flood affected persons and pupils of 30 villages and 30 government girls schools through immediate provision of safe drinking water through water tankers for 30 days then installation of 200 hand pumps, construction of 200 homebased latrines, construction/repair of 60 latrines in govt. girls schools, provision/repair of drinking water facilities in govt. girls schools, distribution of 40000 soaps and awareness through sessions, IEC and Radio Programs in 5 Union Councils of Taluka Sindhri and Kot Ghulam Muhammad in district Mirpurkhas, Sindh province of Pakistan.
According to the data shared by Additional Relief Commissioner, Sindh 2.2 million people have been directly or indirectly been affected by the flooding with 77,337 houses fully damaged and 137,007 partially damaged in 15,233 villages. In total, 196 relief camps have been established which are providing shelter to 23,629 people. Additionally, it is estimated that 1.9 million acres of crops have been affected and 45,961 loss of livestock.
Mirpurkhas district received over 556 millimeters of rainfall spread over three spells, devastating the already crumbling infrastructure and inundating much of the district. According to PDMA 931,901 population have been forced to leave their homes as more than 73070 houses fully and partially damaged, 30 persons injured and 8 people died, crop on more then 149531 acres destroyed as well as 1078 cattle heads perished in the district.
