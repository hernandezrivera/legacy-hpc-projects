Project Summary

HADY Guiding Services is an NGO passionate in using sports in ensuring that every child in disadvantaged communities in Cameroon (NW/SW) access quality education that will enable them leverage future opportunities (compete for jobs, scholarships on an equal level with other children around the world) HADY’s current project is aimed at using sports as a tool to impart other life skills, educate and motivate learning in conflict affected girls and boys in Fako, South West Region. Our goal for 2020 is to engage over 2000 (1500 girls and 500 boys, special needs included) conflict affected children of age 4 to 17 in safe learning spaces and community schools in Fako whose education have been affected by the current crisis in our sports activities. This will ensure their wellbeing, which also allows them to align to the proper educational skills and opportunities in Cameroon, beyond and in Future. We would use sports disciplines like basketball, table tennis, football and handball, which have proven to be one of the favorite sports for conflict affected children. Our strategy is to provide sports equipment to all safe learning spaces and community schools that we work with and also, send our sports facilitators to engaged these children in our sports programs using this equipment daily. Our sports facilitators are trained on child protection, safeguarding, First Aid and CPR, and how to design sports programs that aligns with “DO NO HARM” and “INCLUSIVE “ principles for better results. Our Sports program is played for fun, as opposed to professional. The primary purpose is participation, with the goal of improved educational preparedness, fun, mental healing and social involvement. To ensure sustainability, we would train over 200 teachers on same skills to continue this process in their various community schools and replicate same skills for greater involvement and positive results in the future. Based on this project, the ratio of trainers to conflict affected children is, 10 trainers are to 100 conflict affected children.

Besoins / Needs

Based on UN TV and UNICEF statistics In 2019 three years of violence and instability in the NW/SW regions of Cameroon has left more than 855,000 school- aged children are out of school, 80% schools are currently closed, 150,000 aged children are internally displaced and has resulted to children staying at home causing them to loose basic life skills like Leadership, Discipline, friendship etc, loosing appetite for learning, causing them to miss out from quality education as a result of the traumatic experiences. This statistics has also been confirmed during our community needs assessment where over 419 girls and 320 boys aged 4-17 totaling to over 739 conflicts affected girls and boys are found in 10 safe learning spaces, and community schools in South West Region Cameroon, Fako coupled with 16 Special Needs concerns who are in dare need of our sports activities. Currently we are only able to engaged 496 children (283 girls and 213 boys) from 5 safe learning spaces with very limited sports equipment and attire for these children. Our target by 2020 is 2000 (1500+ girls and 500+ boys) conflict-affected children in Fako , ensuring they have good sports equipment and attire to better engaged in our sports activities. Children have lost basic opportunistic platforms to be informed on serious issues like Cholera out break, Gender Based Violence and other important educational updates that enrich their wellbeing and quality of education. The level of concern and love for sports that these children have shown during our time in the field cannot be explained by words alone. Just by seeing these sports equipment brings so much joy in the hearts and faces of these children. During their composition writing with title “What Do You Love The Most?” 98% of the children expressed their love for sports and a tool for their motivation to learn and stay in this learning environment.
