Project Summary

The armed clashes between SAF and RSF which occurred on 15 April 2023, in different locations across Sudan, significantly affected the physical safety and security of civilians in the sites where armed conflict erupted including Khartoum state. As a result, many people have been internally displaced to different locations seeking safety and security. Sudan’s health system was under extreme stress prior to the clashed and has been further stretched due to lack of fuel, electrical outages, insecurity, movement restrictions, looting and closure of drug shop and hospitals, inflations, lack of access to money which in turn effected on access of patients to a functional health centers within two hours of their home and the situation is getting worse, as many clinics are closing due to lack of medicine of human capacity. This project aims to enhance the life-threatening conditions of the internally displaced population by providing high-quality life-saving humanitarian services to the internally targeted population in therein high-risk states with fifty-four locations through a comprehensive strategy that integrates the sectors of health, nutrition, WASH and that guarantee a decent life for the target groups. This project will continue in the states where highly effected by new war Khartoum WD in addition to the Department of Public Health has intervened in cooperation and coordination with other partners over the previous years. It includes the states mentioned below: West Kordofan, South Kordofan, South Darfur and North Darfur. Kassala (, the White Nile and Abyei. Despite efforts to shelter the IDPs, some health conditions, hygiene, water and environmental sanitation need intervention to reduce diseases related to the respiratory and urinary tract, diarrhea and others, in addition to the importance of nutrition support, Kidney Solutions, mental and reproductive health. Health interventions include providing a package of PHC- SHC, reproductive health (RH) and maternal and child health by supporting health facilities in the targeted states with the necessary and emergency medical supplies and equipment. Interventions will also focus on provide the trauma package in increasing health capacity, health referral support and community health support through the mobile clinic and provide the necessary kidney solution and other necessary medicine. Nutritional interventions include the provision of preventive package and CMAM services to all targeted population groups focusing on sensitive groups (pregnant and lactating women and children) and treatment of severe acute malnutrition, with or without complications for children under five years of age, moderate acute malnutrition of newborns5 and pregnant and lactating women. The project further aims to improve the public health and environmental health of the target communities through the provision of WASH services through hygiene promotion, construction/rehabilitation of community and institutional latrines (in target health facilities, OTPs, SCs, and schools). In addition to improving the target population's access to safe drinking water to reduce the risk of infection. To reach the estimated targets and planned response scale up, GAH will integrated multi-sectoral health sector, WASH by delivery nutrition and WASH services through existing health facilities. and integrated the outreach teams. and use of simplified protocol and explore rapid response mechanisms for timely delivery of nutrition responses in onset emergencies. all activities in health nutrition and Wash are in service activities with response modalities of in kind represent in supplementary, drug and tools that provide from UN agencies (UNICEF, UNFPA, WFP, UNHCR, WHO etc.) the project target 33932 child and 1500 PLWs at nutrition sector in and (105459 F-73,285M) at WASH sector and (94193.5 F-65,457M) at Health sector. the nutrition activity and WASH are live saving activity

1 - Needs

Sudan’s health system was under extreme stress prior to the clashed and has been further stretched due to lack of fuel, electrical outages, insecurity, movement restrictions, looting and closure of drug shop and hospitals, inflations, lack of access to money which in turn effected on access of patients to a functional health centers within two hours of their home and the situation is getting worse, as many clinics are closing due to lac of medicine of human capacity. IDPs children and adults are distressed because of witnessing violence, loss of family members, displacement and insecurity in addition to the possibility of physical injury and the lack of essential medicines for some patients and are in urgent need of physical, psycho-social support.Despite efforts to shelter the IDPs, some health conditions, hygiene, water and environmental sanitation need intervention to reduce diseases related to the respiratory and urinary tract, diarrhea and others, in addition to the importance of nutrition support, Kidney Solutions, mental and reproductive health. befour th esint crisis The insufficiency of health and nutrition services has led the target localities to be ranked high in terms of severity (5 and 4) according to WASH health and 4 in nutrition sectors 2023 HNO. According to the health 2021 HNO, According to the 2022 MSNA, 62 per cent of the population had to pay in full for medical consultation, 63 per cent paid in full for laboratory diagnostics, and 94 per cent paid for medicines. a situation that is further made complex by the receint crisis, which has also affected the performance and the capacities of the already limited health staff in these health facilities. in addition to in addition to lac of madisin and outbreak of dengue fever and monkeypox many state .The lack in provision of basic SRH services such as antenatal care (ANC), postnatal care (PNC) and family planning (FP) means a shortage of information for pregnant and lactating women across all stages and on all pivotal topics, including promoting a healthy lifestyle and good nutrition, detecting and preventing diseases during pregnancy, and counselling on contraceptive methods and birth control (HNO 2023). The target localities have a nutrition severity and stunting risks of 4 and 5 (HNO2023). According to the S3M 2019, most of these target localities have a high GAM rate. The nutrition situation is also greatly affected by the constrictions imposed as precautionary measures in economic deterioration that significantly affected the food security of many households. The low coverage in immunization this year may also further exacerbate the deterioration in the nutritional health of children under 5. All target localities have a high WASH severity rank of 4 . According to the 2023 HNO, out of the target localities in this project almost localities have a percentage of households with access to basic water services of less than 65%, with the remaining 8 localities having a percentage of less than 85% of households accessing the basic water services, as opposed to the standard of more than 85%.In terms of the percentage of households having access to limited sanitation services, most of target localities have a high percentage and still far from the standard of more than 80%.This has great impacts on the general health of the populations in these localities, and this is more emphasized after the emergence of the war. According to the latest IPC analysis, 17 of the target localities in this project face high levels of food insecurity (IPC phase3), In many states, the recent events of conflict an outbreak of many disease adds more burden on the target localities, which are already considered under crisis (IPC phase3). The analysis also notes that the high inflation rates, the COVID19 outbreak, and the latest flooding events suffered by almost all of Sudan’s states has greatly strained the adaptation abilities of majority of the population.

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

GAH is a protection mandated organization, thus protection mainstreaming is alwayspaid extra attention during project design, and also following the “Do No Harm”principle and maintaining the dignity of all targeted beneficiaries. Therehabilitation of all health and nutrition facilities will also take intoaccount the ease of access to PSN such as construction of wheelchair ramps. GAHwill also incorporate topics of child safeguarding and PSEA in the trainings ofcommunity health promoters CHPs, communitynutrition volunteers CNVs and community health volunteers CHVs, and raiseawareness of school children on sexual exploitation and abuse, and how/to whom canthese violations be reported. Incorporation and linkage of the target facilities (HFs and nutrition centers) and/or formulated groups and structures with protectionservices providers will be prioritized in this project, to enable survivors of violence andvulnerable age groups to receive the needed legal aid, psychosocial and/or medicalcare they need.
