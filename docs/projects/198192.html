Project Summary

The aim is to work with IDPs (including people with disabilities) host communities and families exposed to violence and adversity to access to quality, improved referral and coordination mechanism in the MHPSS sub-sector at local level and to work with Humanitarian Actors (or Partners) to Increase their capacity to mainstream disability and collect relevant data to measure disability inclusion within their activities. The project objective will be achieved through a combination of following outputs:
(i)specific services for PSN with disabilities provided and promote inclusion and participation of persons with disabilities in humanitarian response. (ii) Specific services for PSN with disabilities with psychosocial needs provided. (iii) support to IDPs and affected population with specific needs provided :(iv) specific services for persons with specific needs provided:(v) Protect vulnerable communities and effects of armed conflict trough risk education

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

At the beginning of each project HI conducts a risk assessment , to identify potential risks ( a. Breach of Safeguarding policies and Code of Conduct; b. Sexual exploitation, abuse and harassment ; c. Breach of Child protection policy; etc) . For each risk identified HI set’s up a package of mitigation measures, these include : All staff will receive refresher training on the Code of Conduct and Safeguarding policies, including PSEAH and child protection, and will sign revised documents in their personal files.; ommunity members will be consulted on the design, adaptation and implementation of the complaints mechanism, where appropriate, and will be consulted on the handling of complaints including by confcall sessions; The recruitment and selection of staff to work directly with children will include other child protection elements throughout the process (e.g. child protection mentioned in the work description, reference checks, interview questions, etc.).

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The project has inclusion as a focal point. In this sense, sensitization, awareness-raising and empowerment activities will be carried out for the most vulnerable groups, to guarantee their participation in the project or any other humanitarian action.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

HI will in the first instance conduct an activity to identify people with disabilities and their types, as well as their specific needs.The identification process will be done using the Washington Group Questions for people of more than 5 years old and using the HI identification tool for children of 0 to 5 years old. The identification itself is done by activists to be selected and trained by HI. After identification, people with disabilities are assessed by qualified personnel from different specialties: rehabilitation professionals (occupational therapist, physical therapist, technician in physical therapy, orthoprosthesist, speech therapist) and at a psychosocial professional Based on these data, beneficiaries will receive adequate assistance to respond to their needs, either directly by theservices provided within the project or referring them to the services provided by other stakholders. A follow-up system will be set to receive feedback from beneficiaries referred and confirm if they received adequate assistance. Beneficiaries will be informed about their right to equitable and safe services and assistance as well as on the selection criteria. HI will take into account the factors and barriers limiting the participation of beneficiaries to make sure all have equal opportunities

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

In all its projects HI implements its eco-friendly guide developed to address environment sustainability related issues in all activities . 1. Integrating environment into the project cycle : Thisrequires: a. Continuous reflection: asking key questions at each stage of the project cycle (needs assessment, planning, launch, implementation, closing, monitoring and evaluation). B. A cross-cutting approach that targets every sector of activity (operations, logistics, communication, technical support, etc.). c. An approach that aims to implement best practices, rather than costly technologies. 2. Involving the different teams is also a way to ensure that the process is pertinent and feasible. Involving all the staff in the process also dispels the idea that environmental performance is only important to a few people within the organisation. 3. Appointing an environment adviser and putting together a green team. 4. Implementing training/awareness-raising activities
