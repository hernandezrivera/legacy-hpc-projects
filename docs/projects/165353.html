Project Summary

The intensive shelling and conflicts in southern Idleb and western Aleppo resulted in massive displacement towards western Idleb and northern Aleppo governorates. Most of the IDPs are women, girls, and children who are considered very vulnerable in such fragile contexts.
The project aims to deliver basic PSS services to vulnerable groups among the recent IDPs in camps in three sites according to the erupted needs at the time of implementing the project:
Idleb governorate, Harim district, Harim sub-district.
Aleppo governorate, Azaz district, Azaz sub-district.
Aleppo governorate, Afrin district, Afrin sub-district.

4 - Needs

According to recent reports and updates from the humanitarian clusters in GZT, intensified hostilities between government forces and non-state armed groups in the north-western governorate of Idlib have caused the displacement of more than 300,000 people since the beginning of December 2019. This adds to the 400,000 already displaced between May and August. IDPs are moving further north towards cities and IDP camps (Snapshot IDPs in NW Syria 26.01.2020 - HNAP). The cold and rains exacerbate humanitarian needs with tents flooded and IDPs exposed to low temperatures while sleeping in precarious shelters or in the open air. Increased fuel prices and a fuel shortage hinder transportation for civilians, but also for aid delivery. Other access difficulties are due to the volatile security situation and muddy roads. Protection, food, shelter and NFI, and health are all areas of concern.According to the findings of a needs assessment carried out by IRC in NW Syria, the majority of IDPs are females, and more than half of the surveyed households identified having a head of household who was a person at risk, adding an extra layer of vulnerability to the status of the family. Moreover, 8% of the IDPs reported that they had been separated from at least one member of their household as a result of this recent displacement. Changes in children's behaviors as well as a disability as a factor of vulnerability were among the concerns of the IDPs. The fragile status of the households, especially vulnerable members, increases the need to provide protection services to the affected people.

5 - Implementation of activities

Based on the current and expected situation, and the capacity of IDA to deliver protection services and contribute to the overall efforts to cover the gap with other partners, the scope of activities includes basic PSS services for the vulnerable groups in the target location:1- Provision of quality and integrated protection services with a focus on community-based approaches, including awareness-raising, psychosocial assistance, targeted support to persons with specific protection needs and other community initiatives through community centers and outreach mechanisms. This activity will be implemented by providing awareness sessions through campaigns and community centers. Additionally, the activity includes the provision of PFA support and structures PSS support including case management.2- Capacity building activities targeting humanitarian actors and national/ local authorities to improve the quality provision of protection services.
