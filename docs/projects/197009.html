Project Summary

The project aims at providing meaningful access to safe water, dignified sanitation services and access to proper hygiene means and practices to Internally Displaced Persons (IDPs) hosted in communities and resettlement / expansion areas.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

HELVETAS supports IDPs in resettlement centres and host communities in Ancuabe and Chiure Districts of Cabo Delgado Province. Activities are aimed at an increased access to safe water of appropriate quantity and quality, increased access to appropriate sanitation and hygiene facilities and to implement good sanitation and hygiene practices. It will address the WASH requirements of people with disabilities in the selected communities and centres. HELVETAS will continue its support to local government institutions and will continue to coordinate the WASH cluster activities in the two project districts.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Water activities are:- Distribution of point-of-use water treatment materials / products, including training and monitoring on proper use at household level (all the sites)- Rehabilitation of existing water points with inclusive approach, including support for water committees (host communities, accomodation centers, resettlement areas and schools).- Construction of new water sources (host communities, resettlement areas) with inclusive approach and training of the water committee.To promote a proper sanitation both for IDPs and residents, the project will include:- Installation and management of temporary sanitation and bathing facilities (accommodation centres / transit camps and / or resettlement sites, as first phase)- Promotion activities for self-Construction of household latrines and garbage pits, including the provision of sanitation supplies for most vulnerable households (resettlement sites and hosting communities).- Promotion and construction of some sanitation facilities appropriate for people with disabilities.- Construction and rehabilitation of school sanitation facilities.To guarantee access to proper personal hygiene, the responses are:- Procurement and Distribution of WASH related Non-Food items, with focus on most vulnerable families / households (all sites)- Promote good sanitation and hygiene practices in schools and communities, throughout local actvisits and dramas perfomed In the communities. HELVETAS continues to coordinate the WASH Cluster activities in the two target districts.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Through a participative approach to needs assessment and project’s activity definition, specific situations of potential risks are identified and targeted. For a better understanding of the social context, both local authorities and all the different vulnerable groups are involved.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The project will reinforce the already existing community bonds among the residents and IDPs, supporting their self-solidarity through the implementation of different activities for each group.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Consultations will be done with individuals living with disabilities and the elderly to identify specific adaptation needs for infrastructure. Infrastructure will be adapted for these needs.The project will support this group of vulnerable people by realizing a specifically designed latrine. Adapted hand washing facilities will be tested and implemented.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Fostering the self construction of latrines and realizing special and emergency latrines has a positive impact on the human environment. New water sources are opened only after the results of: i) a hydro-geological survey aimed at guaranteeing enough presence of water in the aquifer; 2) a sanitary survey aimed at guaranteeing the absence of local sources of contamination of the soil in the surrounding area.To prevent over-exploitation of the acquifer, a daily use plan of the new water source will be prepared with the water committee taking into consideration the availability of water in the acquifer.
