Project Summary

Under the sector priorities for the Emergency Response Plan, this proposed project aims to provide and strengthen lifesaving Gender Based Violence (GBV) service provision and contribute to GBV risk mitigation measures in extremely vulnerable communities, especially Lebanese, Palestinians and Syrian refugees, and migrants living in the North, Akkar, and Beirut and Mount Lebanon (BML). The targeted groups will include survivors of GBV as well as persons with disabilities who are often at a heightened risk of GBV. Specifically, Concern will deliver specialized GBV services such as focused and non-focused psychosocial support (PSS) and case management (CM) including external referrals to accompanying services. Protection cash assistance will be provided, where needed, within the case management process to help survivors to access external and/or additional services. In each area, a safe space for women and girls’ will be identified, where the targeted project beneficiaries can receive assistance and strengthen the communities’ peer support networks, with capacity for up to 2,500 beneficiaries with improved access to lifesaving GBV services, recreational activities, including life skills and vocational training information on services and referrals, and awareness-raising sessions. The location of the safe spaces will be in Akkar, Tripoli and BML, for ensuring the accessibility of the beneficiaries. Concern will provide GBV services including, but not limited to, focused and non-focused PSS sessions, emotional support groups, case management including individual PSS, and referrals (as required) to 1,000 women and adolescent girls. Emergency cash assistance will also be provided as needed.
Concern will organize awareness sessions and disseminate information on GBV risks and access to services. Within these awareness sessions, Concern will build the communities’ capacity to identify GBV risks and service referral pathways. Concern’s proposed project will target vulnerable communities in the North, Akkar and BML. In addition, Concern will make a concerted effort to reach persons at heightened protection risk (UNHCR Emergency Handbook) i.e girls and boys, including unaccompanied and separated children; persons with serious health conditions; persons with special legal or physical protection needs; single women; women-headed households; older persons; persons with disabilities; and persons of diverse-sex, sexual orientation or gender identity (LGBTI individuals). During the implementation of all different actions, dignity kits will be distributed to project participants.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

Concern will work with 2,500 beneficiaries to ensure improved access to lifesaving GBV services, recreational activities, including life skills and vocational training information on services and referrals, and awareness-raising sessions. Concern will also provide focused and non-focused PSS sessions, emotional support groups, case management including individual PSS, and referrals to 1,000 women and adolescent girls. Concern will provide emergency cash assistance for up to 100 cases and recurrent cash assistance to 60 cases. The cash will be used when core GBV response services, such as legal services or health services, are not accessible due to financial barriers or to address situations that expose individuals to immediate serious harm and/or GBV risk or its consequences. Therefore, cash could be considered instrumental to a survivor’s recovery. The cash would be provided on a case-by-case basis, complementary to Concern’s over-arching case management services. Emergency cash assistance will be a once-off unconditional cash. Concern will reach up to 4,500 individuals, including men, boys, women and girls, with information on GBV risks (early marriage, violence against women, and access to services), through awareness raising sessions and campaigns. Concern will also engage 150 influential community members, like municipality staff, teachers, gatekeepers, etc. to act as focal points within the project area, to improve their ability to identify or respond to GBV situations.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

Concern worldwide has its own policies for child safeguarding and Protection from Sexual Exploitation, Abuse, and Harassment, as well as the code of conduct. These policies clearly outline Concern’s commitment to preventing and responding to sexual exploitation and abuse, and will be disseminated to all staff, partners, and beneficiaries to ensure everyone is aware of the zero-tolerance approach towards such abuse. Concern will also conduct training and capacity building on PSEA to partner staff and beneficiaries, and engage communities to raise awareness about PSEA. Concern will also ensure that the reporting mechanism are clearly established, especially the Complaints and Response Mechanism (CRM). Concern will also conduct regular monitoring and evaluation activities for the project, to ensure that it is being implemented according to standards.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

As part of the organizational accountability commitments, all project participants will be informed of Concern’s Complaints Response Mechanism (CRM). Concern’s CRM exists to receive complaints and feedback from all project stakeholders including beneficiaries, contractors, and others as relates to the implementation of activities, conduct of Concern’s staff and other issues within Concern’s control. The CRM also contributes to Concern’s M&E and continuous learning. Complaints are received via a dedicated phone line, a complaints box, or in person to Concern field staff. Investigations are carried out where appropriate and all complainants are informed of the outcome. By the end of the programme, there will be evidence that feedback and complaints have been considered within programming, as evidenced by reports, complaints log, and responses given.
