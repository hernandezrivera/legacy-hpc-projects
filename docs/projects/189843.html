Project Summary

The project contributes to increased access to and improved quality of education for IDPs children in Mueda and Meluco districts, with a focus on H2R areas based on ongoing experience in these Districts, through provision of TLS and integration in existing schools, dedicated training for teachers and referral, participation and coordination

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Mueda is a transit district and an area of refuge for at least 91,000 IDPs (IOM/DTM) at the time of submission of this proposal, with recent attacks from insurgents having occurred in the District, particularly in Namatil. The overall number of CIN reached 37577 and currently, stress level for Mueda is “catastrophic”. Meluco has a lesser number of CIN (3285); however the stress level remains “catastrophic” for this District, which has so far received very limited support from humanitarian partners. Helpcode builds on its ongoing operational presence in the two Districts with interventions in protection and WASH (Mueda) and nutrition (Meluco), and capacity to work in H2R areas to propose a coordinated intervention with other education partners in order to ensure improved access to and quality of education for IDPs children through provision of TLS and integration of children in existing schools, provision of learning supplies, teachers training and referall of children to Child Protection services, and through ensuring coordination with other education partners, the Education cluster, other sectoral partners and protection mainstreaming. Helpcode also builds on its peace education curricula in order to promote active participation of children and teachers in design, realization and monitoring of activities in order to develop the humanitarian-development-peace nexus. In both Districts, Helpcode focuses on H2R areas and coordination with other humanitarian partners to avoid overlapping. In Mueda, TLS will be installed in this H2R areas, whereas in Meluco the intervention strategy aims at integrating children in existing schools while providing emergency latrines. Key targets groups are IDPs children (6-17 years old) as well as, for Mueda, teachers in IDPs sites.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Activities to be implemented in the target areas include: Mueda:Provision of TLS with mobile blackboards in H2R areas / campsSensibilization with community activists with regards to IDPs children accessing existing schools and TLS, both with IDPs and hosting communitiesProvision of learning and teaching supplies for children and teachersTraining on VAC, GBV and PSEA for teachers and community activists, with peace education approachReferral of children to Child Protection Services MelucoInstallation of emergency latrines in existing schoolsSensibilization with community activists with regards to accessing accessing existing schools and TLS, both with IDPs and hosting communitiesProvision of learning and teaching supplies for children and teachersTraining on VAC, GBV and PSEA for teachers and community activists, with peace education approachReferral of children to Child Protection Services

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The project aims at mainstreaming protection for IDPs through combining community-based approaches (aimed at ensuring awareness and participation), ad hoc training and improved access to protection (referral) through existing, strengthened services. Protection mainstreaming is also ensured through coordination with institutional and humanitarian partners and through the incorporation of protection principles into aid delivery. The project is built on the (still preliminary) results of protection monitoring activities carried out by Helpcode in the final quarter of 2021 in partnership with UNHCR (Mueda), which aims at identifying specific protection needs and avoid potential risks of exclusion for children.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

Helpcode AAP’s strategy abides to IASC 5 commitments, according to the following: Leadership:- Promote a culture among staff of acceptance of failure and negative feedback from affected people. - Ensure that relevant staff and managers collect feedback from affected populations in appropriate age groups (sensitive to gender & age); respond to this feedback; and report back. - Work with local and national partners, so that there is a coordinated plan and capacity which is sensitive to gender, age, disability and ethnicity. Transparency:- Provide accessible and timely information on organizational procedures, structures and processes that may impact communities in order to support informed decisions, and engage communities in a dialogue as part of information provision Participation:- Work with relevant local and national partners and actors to support and influence effective design, assessment, implementation and monitoring of the response.- Engage all parts of a community, including those who are vulnerable or at risk to draft robust, contextual vulnerability and capacity analyses. Inclusivity is ensured through a thorough analysis of local social dynamics, mobility capacity of beneficiaries, differences in the use of time by women and men, identification of “role models” among community stakeholders that can promote social change. Feedback and complaints:- Provide information to all in affected communities about the organisation, the principles it adheres to, how it expects its staff to behave (including commitments made on PSEA), the programmes it is implementing and what they intend to deliver. - Communicate in languages, formats and media that are easily understood, respectful and culturally appropriate for different members of the community, especially vulnerable and marginalised groups.- Establish and maintain effective feedback systems, using a variety of communication channels that are accessible to all persons of concern and that are appropriate for both sensitive and non-sensitive feedback. The feedback is disaggregated by age and sex at minimum. Design, monitoring and evaluation:- Assess the implementation of the Core Humanitarian Standard (CHS) and other collective standards including the IASC AAP commitments through assessments of organizational performance on accountability to measure and improve accountability to all persons of concern.- Include persons of concern as partners throughout the operation's management cycle and report the results of assessments and follow up actions to them.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The project will work with the District Services of Health and in coordination with the humanitarian partners who have developed guidelines and tools for ensuring disability-inclusive protection in education, in order to go beyond the minimum standards for disability inclusion in humanitarian action.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Among its internal procedure, Helpcode has developed an environmental policy aiming to avoid, minimize and mitigate the environmental impact of its activities in the field, in line with Sphere Humanitarian Standards and global environmental standard, guidelines and tools. In this respect, each project is assessed since design in terms of environmental impact. In this case, environmental concerns will be addressed by:- Limiting and avoiding whenever possible the use of plastic during distribution of learning materials;- Limit the installation of TLS and latrines by realizing a thorough needs assessment and by opting wherever possible for integrating children into existing schoolsEnvironmental assessment will be carried out again at project kick off and at the end.It is also critical to highlight that the overall implementation context is affected by severe vulnerability to climate change and extreme climate events. In terms of mitigation / preparation, the project foresees to provide ad hoc information during teachers’ training activities.
