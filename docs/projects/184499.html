Project Summary

ACTED’s proposed action will response to the needs of IDPS (80% of sites are informal settlements settled on private land with the majority of sites located in urban areas). ACTED will implement the project in Kismayo, Hargeisa, Ceel Afweyn, Laasqoray and Baidoa districts, which are impacted by floods, evictions, drought, and lack of sustainable livelihoods, making the populations increasingly vulnerable and at risk of displacement. ACTED’s selection of areas of intervention is based on the priority geographic areas identified in the CCCM Cluster HRP 2022 partner guidance paper, the areas recording the presence of high numbers of vulnerable IDPs and ACTED’s operational capacity.
In this project, ACTED seeks to improve the living conditions and safe access to services to IDPs, strengthen effective coordination structures at site level, and enhance coordination and information-sharing between humanitarian partners and local authorities while working towards durable solutions for IDP communities and community self-management.
To mitigate site-level protection risks and improve site safety, ACTED will implement site maintenance activities, administer safety audits, install street solar lights, and will work in coordination with active protection actors on ground in order to reduce the risk of GBV. The main outcome will be enhanced coordination, improved information management, increased site safety and strengthened referral with actors including services providers.
The overall project target is 13,759 households across the different areas of intervention.

1. - Projects activities

1) Strengthen safe access to multi-sectorial services at site level through improved site management and coordination.Coordination mechanisms will be established/strengthened in all the areas of intervention, involving local authorities and communities. In the sites where ACTED is not yet involved, the organization will first map existing community governance structures. It will then oversee the establishment/strengthening of Camp Management Committees (CMCs), including the integration of new members in existing committees, making them as inclusive as possible. As such, ACTED will adopt an Age, Gender and Diversity approach during the establishment of CMCs. Terms of Reference for the committees’ members will be developed jointly by ACTED and the CMCs, promoting accountability of the CMC members. Moreover, ACTED will provide capacity building trainings to local authorities, displaced communities and humanitarian partners to promote self-management and sustainable site leadership, and improve coordination and information management. 2) Improve living conditions of displaced people through site development, care and maintenance and decongestion initiatives:ACTED will establish/strengthen site maintenance committees (SMCs) comprised of skilled and unskilled individuals through a cash for work scheme. To facilitate this, ACTED will procure site maintenance toolkits, materials and fire safety equipment, which will be handed over to the SMCs and fire wardens for site improvement works. Prior to the commencement of activities, ACTED will provide capacity building trainings to members of SMCs and fire wardens regarding maintenance standards, fire safety and prevention measures. In addition, ACTED will install solar lights in identified locations based on safety audit findings and focus group discussions with community members including camp management and SMCs. Furthermore, female-only FGDs will be carried out for women to identify heightened protection risks, where lighting will increase access and mitigate GBV threats. Moreover, ACTED will monitor and address female risks and vulnerabilities through regular Safety Audits in each of the target sites. To promote women participation, empowerment and inclusion in decision-making, ACTED will hold a training for female IDP leaders on safety audit in order to develop concrete action plans. 3) Strengthen community self-management and access to information for displaced populations:To support the dissemination of information on multi sectoral service availability, ACTED will use regular service delivery monitoring, site-level coordination meetings with CMCs, and information campaigns including Risk Communication and Community Engagement through outreach activities. Furthermore, information pertaining to service maps, contact lists for emergencies, Common Feedback Mechanism (CFM) hotline numbers, distribution schedules, and referral pathway will be widely disseminated. A multi-sectoral CFM mechanism will also be set up, relying on information desks, hotline numbers, and mobile teams. 4) Support opportunities for displaced people to achieve durable solutions:ACTED will closely coordinate with the relevant government agencies and humanitarian partners to ensure that the necessary social infrastructures and amenities are set up before any relocation occurs. The relocation of IDPs to a new location requires inclusive participation, proper planning, the establishment of effective coordination mechanisms, as well as referral pathways to monitor the process and conditions on the ground. Furthermore, ACTED will support securing of land rights for the displaced population whenever possible. ACTED will also build the capacity of relevant stakeholders to facilitate the transfer of knowledge needed for the delivery of a quality intervention in displacement sites. ACTED will also support the localization agenda and the development of locally led and sustainable solutions.

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

ACTED recognizes differences in vulnerabilities and needs faced by women, men, girls, boys, the elderly, persons living with disabilities, as well as other socio-demographic groups. Through conducting safety audits, ACTED will be able to identify protection risks and assess specific vulnerabilities of women, girls, boys and men to those identified risks. ACTED will also install street solar lights with the location of the solar lights identified based on focus group discussions with community members and meetings with CMCs and newly formed SMCs. Female-only FGDs will be carried out to enable women identify areas that pose risks or areas where lighting will increase access and mitigate GBV threats. In addition, ACTED will seek guidance from other protection/GBV partners that may have knowledge about locations that are known to be problematic. Preference will be given to areas along footpaths, communal areas and latrines. In order to increase safety in the IDPs sites for vulnerable members, as follow up of safety audit activities, ACTED will provide portable solar lights to vulnerable households. Through conducting safety audits, ACTED will be able to identify protection risks and assess specific vulnerabilities of women, girls, boys and men to those identified risks. The analysis of findings from safety audits will be used to inform actions including putting in place risk mitigation measures and advocating for the service providers to improve the physical safety of sites, the distribution of and access to services, or security. Also based on findings, specific site maintenance interventions required to reduce GBV risks will be integrated in the site maintenance plan. ACTED’s internal policies adopts a zero-tolerance policy on sexual exploitation and abuse and fraudulent actions by both staff members and beneficiaries. This internal policy signed by all staff members, ACTED partners, suppliers, site management committees and camp management committees promote the protection from any contrary actions by all stakeholders. The policy strategy is read together with various policies including, conflict of interest policy, anti-fraud and bribery policy, U.N. Inter-Agency Standing Committee (IASC) Task Force on Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse in Humanitarian Crises. ACTED has an existing policy on Sexual Exploitation and Abuse which is readily available to all staff members. This document is part of the code of conduct handbook signed by all ACTED staff and volunteers when working with ACTED. Strict adherence to this policy is expected within the organization and in case of violation, internal reporting mechanisms exists which employees are encouraged to utilize in the event such incidences occur. With regard to employee to beneficiary interaction, the PSEA also provides clauses on this and repercussions in case ACTED employees infringe on this.
