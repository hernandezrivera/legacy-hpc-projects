Project Summary

Oxfam through this action has planned to enable the most vulnerable families in the targeted subdistricts to have access for essential basic needs. This will be done through market feasibility and risk assessment to determine target households preference, delivery mechanism, and modality . A total of 15,000 households will be benefited from this action and 30% of the target households will be female headed households. Vulnerable household will be provided with multipurpose cash assistance to address their immediate basic households need. Oxfam will ensure coordination among various partners operating in the same geographical setting to reduce duplication but rather enhance integration and complementarities . Oxfam will adopt community engagement approach to enhance active participation of the target communities in the entire cycle of the action.

3 - Needs

The food security situation in Syria has drastically deteriorated since last year. In 2023, an estimated 15 million people will need some form of food and agriculture assistance, representing 68 per cent ofthe total population. At least 12.1 million people are estimated to be food insecure, including all 2 million people living in camps who are deemed to be 100 per cent food insecure, and 2.9 million people areestimated to be at risk of food insecurity, an alarming increase of people at risk who could shift to become food insecure during 2023 if the crisis continues at same worsening levels.The impact of Syria’s crisis continues to hinder people’s ability to meet their immediate food needs and livelihoods, in a context marked by very complex drivers and contributing factors. This includes economic worsening and weakened local currency, soaring food and non-food prices, as well as droughtlike conditions and severe agro-climatic fluctuations, water scarcity, and limited energy supply. In addition, food and agriculture commodities prices increase, inflation, lack of purchasing power due to limited livelihood and lack of income sources, population movements that shows no sign of abating, and the Ukraine crisis have had an important economic impact on markets and commodities cost.

4 - Implementation of activities

Oxfam has very experienced and competent staff to implement the multipurpose cash assistance. Market feasibility and risk assessment will be done prior to the start of the project to understand the beneficiaries preference, modality , delivery mechanism and potential risk and mitigation measures . it will further adopt participatory community engagement approach to ensure active participation of the target people and enhance inclusion and reduce exclusion errors. A total of 15,000 households will be benefited from this intervention and 30% of them are female headed households. The action will conduct price monitoring of the basic items in the critical market to understand the changes of price trends and adjust the transfer value accordingly. DDP and Post distribution monitoring will be conducted to measure the impact and beneficiaries satisfaction

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

1. Community tension arising from community perception of unfair eligibility criteria or unfair geographic/beneficiary targeting/selection, or from inclusion/exclusion errors: Community-based beneficiary selection, Effective communication, include communication materials in local language, Conflict-sensitivity analysis, protection risk analysis to inform the final design of the project and mitigation measures 2. Diversion of assets: risk-informed selection criteria, DDM and PDM, CFM 3. SEAH: Oxfam’s PSEA and Child Safeguarding policy, safeguarding focal points in all hubs, training and refreshers on the topic, CFM, reporting lines in place, Manager’s responsibility to provide a healthy working environment. 4. Risk of jeopardizing commitment to meaningful access standard: Market assessments include specific disability, age and gender-sensitive questions; to capture facilitators/barriers to the engagement of certain AGD groups in specific labor markets. This is essential to ensure that female and PWD beneficiaries do not end up unable to join the market after completing the vocational trainings or receiving assistance. 5. People with disabilities will also be identified at the interview stage prior to the engagement in vocational trainings in order to ensure preparedness to accommodate their diverse needs safely, and coordination will take place to ensure recipient of referrals from DPOs.


8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

SEA triggered by unequal power dynamics between e.g. staff responsible for applying beneficiary selection and communities in need of assistance: Oxfam’s PSEA and Child Safeguarding policy; safeguarding focal points in all hubs; training and refreshers on the topic delivered to staff and partners; CFM; reporting lines in place; Manager’s responsibility to secure a working environment conducive to reporting SEA-related concerns; multi-layer verification of the list of selected beneficiaries against Oxfam’s vulnerability criteria and MoVs, in-house capacity to operationalize Oxfam’s reporting and investigation routine.


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risks: - Limited equipment and supplies available in the Syrian market due to instability, sanctions and transactions delays prevent the implementation of project activities. - The USD to SYP Exchange rate fluctuations could result in either big gain at end of project period with no time to appropriate the additional funds, or not enough funds to fulfil commitments and project objectives. - Potential of fraud and aid diversion, amongst Oxfam staff, suppliers and partners - COVID19 spread puts at risk the health and wellbeing of affected communities and of humanitarian workers delivering assistance. - Assumptions: - No interference by conflicting parties in the implementation putting at risk independence, and impartiality. the local authorities will allocate enough production inputs for the rehabilitated bakeries. the community will have the capacity to manage the work in the rehabilitated bakeries sufficiently. The social norms and traditions will enable women to participate in the communities’ decision-making process.Mitigation: Oxfam will perform rapid market assessment to check the availability of the required items and their prices. All Oxfam and partner’s staff will be inducted on Oxfam’s standards on safeguarding and accountability. Very clear accountability mechanism will be put in place prior to the implementation. Oxfam will go for NCEs to absorb the surpluses that it might have as a result to an increase in the exchange rate.
