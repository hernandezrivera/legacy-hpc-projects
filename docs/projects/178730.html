Project Summary

QC through 2021 project is aiming at supporting Education opportunities in north of Syria for the most
vulnerable Syrian children (girls and boys, considering; children with special needs,
adolescents and upper-age youth ).
QC will provide a complementary Education services that are in line with HRP strategic objective:
1- Increase physical access to safe learning environment for all children by:
a- Provision of 165 Classroom caravan (27m and 42m) host (11,890) displaced children (girls and boys) in Aleppo camps, fully equipped with school furniture (20 tables and 40 chairs for students - a table and a chair to the teacher- a whiteboard - First Aid Kits and Fire Extinguishers - Recycle Bin - a heater) and beautified with child/youth friendly design that attract boys and girls to Education.
b- Suppling of 285 Tent-classrooms to both camps and crowded schools in Idleb in order to provide more opportunities for 22,800 children (girls and boys) to access education.
c- Conduct rehabilitation works for 420 classrooms in Azaz, Albab and Afrin in Aleppo NWS. which will benefit 25,000 students (girls and boys).
d- Construction of 16 classrooms school in Albab- Aleppo in NWS. The school will host 1,120 beneficiaries.
d- Ensure that all provided Classroom caravans, Tent-classrooms, rehabilitated as well as constructed classrooms and WASH facilities, support the access of children (girls and boys) with special needs to Education.
e- Ensure that all the provided, rehabilitated and constructed learning spaces ensure good ventilation and provide air circulation to mitigate COVID-19 spread risk.
f- Support access to formal Education to 77,300 students in Aleppo and Idleb (11,880 will benefit school caravans + 19,950 will benefit tent classrooms + 25,000 will benefit 420 rehabilitated classrooms + 1,120 will benefit the 16 classrooms constructed school in Albab + 19,350 student in 21 supported schools by QC in Idleb).
2- Enhance the Quality of Education services by:
a- Provision of learning materials (i.e. School-bags, stationery kit, teacher's kit) for 31,230 students girls and boys (19,350 in QC schools in Idleb+ 11,880 in Aleppo camps - 54%girls, 46%boys,) and 590 Teachers in schools in QC supported schools in Idleb (52%females, 48%males, with taking in consideration the gender sensitivity about colors and design).
b- Enhance the capacity of Teachers and school cadre (females and males) by conducting physical training to 1,500 teachers (50%females, 50%males) about EiE and CPMS (considering WHO recommendation about COVID-19). Training will be conducted in cooperation with EDs in the targeted schools. Training materials will be printed and shared with all trainees.
c- Provision of Teachers' incentives for 590 teachers (52% females, 48% males) for one academic year.
d- Printing and distributing educational curriculum in place to the benefit of 796,980 (SIG curriculum) students (girls and boys), considering the sensitivities of the Syrian crisis.
QC intervention is planned to cover all the Education objectives after 10 years of the Syrian crisis that imposes education as urgent priority to prevent a lost generation.
All activities will be monitored by QC MEAL staff to ensure the quality of provided services. QC has a classroom/teacher observation check-list to evaluate the teacher performance and the environment of the classroom, and to be conducted by the Education supervisor on regular base by physical visits to schools. PDM reports will be issued to measure the satisfaction of beneficiaries about the received kits. Teachers learning cycle and PTA ToRs and meeting documents are developed to be shared on monthly bases.
All required coordination with stakeholders and EDs will done by signing MoUs with partners and education authorities in western rural of Aleppo and Idleb. QC will sign protocols with Turkish EDs as well as EDs in Afrin, Azaz, Albab, Jarablus, Ras Alayn and Tal Abyad, in order to be allowed to work in the area.

4 - Needs

The needs in Education sector in Syria are summarized as follow: 1- An estimated 6.9 million people (97% children) need humanitarian education assistance (HNO 20121).2- COVID-19 has pushed more than 300 schools to close across the North West (NW) of the country (OCHA situation report). Due to the school closure and to the lack of access and availability of new learning modalities, the OOS numbers have ballooned. The Education sector is not able to provide a 2021 estimate of out of school children, however, the numbers of children out of school, and children who are at risk of dropping out, almost certain to increase.3- More than 5,300 teachers working voluntarily mainly in Idlib and rural Aleppo (Edu Cluster). 4- 370 schools are non-supported schools and teachers are working voluntarily in those schools which affects the quality of Education as there are strikes happening by teachers and teachers are not attending the school regularly and trying to look for other job opportunities. 5- Reports show that attacks on education are on the increase. According to reports from the Monitoring and Reporting Mechanism on Grave Violations against Children and Armed Conflict (MRM), 145 attacks on educational facilities and personnel have been verified. 6- School transportation is needed to urge girls and disabled children attending classes.6- The issue of non-recognition of the educational certificate granted to students, especially in the secondary level, remains an important obstacle for adolescents (females and males) to complete their education.7- The protection situation for children and their families is impacted by economic hardships which have been exacerbated by the restrictions to limit the spread of COVID-19.

5 - Implementation of activities

QC will implement Education project complying with HRP strategic objectives to ensure and scale-up safe access to all children to quality education opportunities, by: - Increase access to formal Education to 77,300 students girls and boys by: -Suppling of 285 Tent-classrooms and 165 school caravans (42m2) in addition to 17 WASH caravans (4 WCs in each, one for disabled children) to the camps and crowded schools in Idlib and Aleppo. All tents and caravans are equipped with school furniture. - Conducting rehab works for 420 classrooms in Azaz, Albab and Afrin in Aleppo NWS. The rehab works to include constructions, repair of damaged WASH and electricity networks and to beautify the learning spaces by paintings and designs. - Construction of 16 classrooms school in Albab- Aleppo in NWS. The school will be furnished, beautified and secured to receive all children (including children with special needs). - Conducting BTL and COVID-19 awareness sessions. - - Provision of learning materials (i.e. Schoolbags, stationery kit, teacher's kit) for 31.230 Children and 590 Teachers in schools (54%girls, 46%boys, 52%females, 48%males, with taking in consideration the gender sensitivity about colors and design).- Enhance the capacity of Teachers and school cadre (females and males) by conducting physical training to 1,500 teachers (50%females, 50%males) about EiE and CPMS (considering WHO recommendation about COVID-19).- Provision of Teachers' incentives for 590 teachers (52% females, 48% males) for one academic year. - Printing and distributing textbooks to 796,980 students.- Support running costs for 25 supported schools (2 TECs and 23 public schools). - Forming of 25 PTAs in QC supported schools.- Disinfecting 48 schools

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

R1: Learning facilities unable to put into place and maintain Covid-19 safety measures including positive behavioral changes.Mitigation measures: - QC team is aware of and follow EDs'Covid-19 rules, Edu cluster protocol and WHO recommendations. - Students, school personnel and caregivers should be made aware of the pandemic and were introduced to applied protocols.R2: Teachers or other education personnel participate in physical punishment or bullying of students. Students participate in bullying of students. Mitigation measures:- All learning facilities have a code of conduct and policy on bullying. - Teachers and school personnel are trained on prevention and reporting of, and issues related to, physical punishment and bullying. R3: Learning facilities could be attacked.Mitigation measures: - All attacks will be reported to the Attacks on Education Reporting Mechanism. - Locations of learning facilities are selected based on security analysis, to be in safe areas. Also, all schools/TECs are structurally strengthened to better withstand the type of attack they are most likely to be exposed to.- Learning facilities have contingency safety plans and safety protocols in place. All beneficiaries are trained to apply the safety plan when needed. This Project adopts protection mainstreaming approach in project design and implementation including: 1- Preserve safety and dignity of students and school cadre (females, males, girl and boys) by provision of safe learning spaces (classroom caravans) and separated sanitation caravans to girls and boys ensuring each genders' privacy , 2- Ensuring meaningful access to all beneficiaries including those with special needs by adding ramps which allows children access.

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

A- HLP risk in lands where caravans and tent-classrooms are going to be installed. Mitigation Measures:- Before designing the project, QC will signed a MoU with EDs includes below points: 1- EDs will takeover classroom caravans from QC in the agreed locations. No HLP related problems are there, and if any, ED will solve the problem soon.2- EDs to facilitate QC MEAL team visits as well as third-party monitoring visits to project locations. Risk2: B- Acquisition of educational caravans/ tent-classrooms by armed groups / local councils / camp management and using them for purposes other than education.Mitigation Measure: A MoU to be signed with ED will include:1- All received classroom caravans/tent-classrooms will be used only for Education purposes, and ED commit to this. 2- After installation in agreed locations in camps, ED commit not to change the locations for at least two year. 3- EDs to facilitate QC MEAL team visits as well as third-party monitoring visits to project locations. Risk3:C- Suspension of cross-border assistance from Turkey to NWS.Mitigation Measures:- To work in coordination with AFAD and Turkish Red Crescent to deliver aids to Syria.- To agree with the suppliers to provide all the goods to inside of Syria through border gates as a commercial materials. D- School closures in northwest Syria due to the increasing number of COVID-19 cases.Mitigation Measures: - To commit to WHO recommendations , as well as Education Cluster guidelines (Safe back to school protocol) and strictly to follow all the precaution procedures. - Caravans/tent-classrooms are designed to provide a proper ventilation and air circulation, (42 m2) which will help applying social distancing.
