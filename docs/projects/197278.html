1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

ADRA Mozambique provide food assistance and livelihoods resilience to the most vulnerable and acute food secure households in resettlement areas and host communities of Montepuez and Metuge Districts. The program will target at least 14,000 beneficiaries and will be provided with partly a safety net of food aid to households that are most vulnerable and acutely food insecure, training in agriculture, village lending and savings with TVET, aquaculture and livestock trainings.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Activity 1: Distribution of Food8,000 food kits distributed to 1600 households over 2 months. The food kits distribution with commodity voucher modality and will take into account the needs of different age groups and sex. ADRA will also adopt the kits that are recommended by the Food Security and Livelihoods Cluster. Activity 2: Agricultura1200 smallholder farmers in project regions have improved knowledge of climate-smart farming practices and sustainable access to agricultural inputs with improved post-harvest practices and community storage structures. Activity 3: VSLA and TVET800 households will be trained on Nutrition to facilitate food choices during voucher redemption for balanced diets and dietary diversity. 500 youth (15 to 29 years old) have improved skills and opportunities to access economic activities (21st century technologies). Activity 4: Livestock1200 IDP and host community member households are raising animals better and using improved animal husbandry methods. Activity 5: AquacultureFour community-based tilapia aquaculture ponds are functional and improve the adaptive capacity of 140.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

Consultations will be done with affected populations, including specific discussions with women, gilrs, people living with disabilities and old-aged people to consider the food basket. The food basket created will include items that provide nutrients required by most groups, for instance, iron rich food for young girls and women and protein-prich food for children. Siting of the distribution points will include consultations with women, girls, people living with disabilities and old-aged people to ensure that these groups feel safe in accessing food during voucher distribution and when exchanging vouchers. Consultations will be done with affected populations (not only community leaders but also to boys, men, women, gilrs, people living with disabilities and old-aged people) to define needs and priorities. Updates on the project will similarly be shared with the affected populations through suggestion box and hotline mechanisms. Affected populations will be informed in advance of selection criteria, date/time, and process of the project implementation. All requests for responses to the Linha Verde mechanism will be responed to within 48 hours of receipt from the Food Security and Livelihoods Cluster.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Consultations will be done individuals people living with disabilities and old-aged people in setting up the site for distribution, besides making sure that the sites are accessible to all people including the old-aged and disabled people.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

ADRA plans not to degrade the environment through deforestation, where there will be need for setting up the distribution site. ADRA also planned to preserve the land and the Floresta by not over populate areas that do not have the capacity of accommodating many people for any distribution or open new spaces for distribution. ADRA will avoid this by using areas that already exist in the communities where many people could be accommodated as regular community meeting areas in each community.
