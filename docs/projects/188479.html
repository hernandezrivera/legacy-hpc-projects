Project Summary

Intensive education center to accommodate 320 students (60% male - 40% Female) dropped out in Harim community in Harim sub-district in Harim District in Idleb governorate. The center will contain 8 classrooms (10% Children with disability), 20 students for each classroom. Students will be provided with weekly activities such as citizenship - life skills in a safe area for children and provide weekly awareness-raising sessions for parents during the project period.
There will be an ASER assessment test at first sorting students by their level and students will be sorted into two levels as follows:
• A Therapeutic Reading course which is developed and fully adopted through Chemonics Foundation - Manahel Program.
• The UNICEF curriculum B is an approved curriculum for students who are dropped out of school.
The work will be carried out to bridge the educational gaps of the students and help them to restore the educational gaps and to enable them to enroll in formal education schools like their peers. School bags with stationery will be distributed to students during the project period.

4 - Needs

The national self-reported attendance rate to pre-university education for the 2019-2020 academic year was 81% with nearly all children (96%) attending formal education and learning in classrooms (97%). Boys and girls have overall attendance parity with most children starting school at six years old (78% girls and 78% boys). Attendance peaks at age eight for girls (95%) and nine for boys (95%), then plateaus until the children reach 12 years old when attendance starts to decline. The decline of attendance for girls and boys up to15 years old is relatively similar. As there were displaced around 30,000 people gathered (55%) distributed to Harim Assemble, which is a very important center in the countryside of Idlib province, and around 110,000 people living in Harim now according to our field team coordinating with there. Now, in the northwest of Syria, there are about five million according to recent statistics, as they need great support, and to continue efforts to serve them, we have to work hard to absorb these large numbers. So according to the Education Cluster (UNICEF), there are thousands of students out of school and they need great efforts to help them bring them back to formal education. Thousands of students are now out of school because of the vicious attack on the education sector caused by the bombing of schools which are schools and educational centers are purely humanitarian centers. We are running now the same center as the need is very huge.

A completion certificate at the end of the project will be provided who have passed the final exam by Peace Hands.

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

In fact, we had a current experience for the same project, where the issue of community participation in the region was taken into consideration by holding evening and morning community meetings for men. Where they were listened to and shared their views on the issue of students who dropped out of education. The center is also equipped with facilities to assist students with special needs. Also, the issue of (the attendance of the elderly parents to the awareness-raising sessions) was taken into consideration, in which a young man and a girl from our field team were assigned to help the parents and organize them to attend the monthly sessions (awareness-raising sessions - focused discussion sessions).
