Project Summary

Most public primary schools in the rural communities of the conflict plagued North West Region of Cameroon have either been shut down or function at far below capacity leaving the education of children with limited parental care especially orphans and single parents children in the hands of privately-owned schools. The requirement to pay tuition in these schools inhibits thousands of children from attaining basic primary education. This project shall ensures that fifty (50) deserving children from poor background continue to have access to basic primary education by providing tuition scholarships, vital learning material and basic hygiene and psychosocial support.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The project seeks to respond to the educational needs of children affected by the conflict in the North West Region with particular attention on unaccompanied child IDPs and others who are victims of GBV in Mezam and Momo Division. In this pilot phase the project shall support 250 children with school kits, psychosocial support while engaging them in community safety education. As academic activities gradually begin to be generally accepted in the restive North West Region many children with limited parental care continue to face challenges keeping up with the pace of studies due to no textbooks and tuition fees. To address these challenges, the project shall purchase and donate school kits to 250 children, direct beneficiaries. The kits shall compose of tuition fee, books, bags and sandals for school. It shall work with five schools to equally empower the children in basic personal-security measures especially within school environment in five partner schools in Bamenda. The project shall contribute in reducing the vulnerability of the girls to sexual exploitation, child prostitution and sex trafficking in the project area. It shall carryout outreach campaigns and referral the project shall promote local cooperation and skills sharing within the humanitarian community as the initiative shall liaise with other actors in the project area.
