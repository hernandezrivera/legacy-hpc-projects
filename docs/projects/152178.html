Project Summary

The project is targeted towards Returnees and IDPs. Improve living conditions of Returnees and IDPs by providing Transitional Shelters and Essential Non Food Items to 10,000 households across the three states in the Northeast of Nigeria.

Needs

Over 300,000 New Arrivals, IDPs and Returnees in Borno State and 190,000 IDPs and Returness in Yobe are in need of Shlter and NFI support due to the Bokko Haram insurgent attacks in the past years which had rendered many homeless until of the recent liberation of certain areas by the military, hence Borno and Yobe has seen high number of New arrivals and returnees. As they return they mostly lack conducive shelter and basic NFIs hence the need for this intervention.

Activities

-Provide 10,000 Emergency Shelters-Provide 10,000 Bedding kits-Provide 10,000 Water Storage Kits-Provide 10,000 Kitchen Utensil Kits
