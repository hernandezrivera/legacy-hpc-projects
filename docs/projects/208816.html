Project Summary

The Response is targeting the WASH sector activities in three most affected towns by the displaced people. UNOPS will concentrate the works In Port Sudan as it has high figure of displacement and addition to that it became the alternative airport and all the national Government and International organizations established new offices here. Port Sudan heroically suffers from water scarcity and chronic hygiene due to the lack of the resources. Then Kassala also for its geographic location became crowded and the people displaces in addition to the host communities families from Khartoum. Kassala do not have a lack of water resources, but a lack of capacity to extract and deliver the water. For Wad Medani, it’s the most affected town by the displaced civilians due to its geographical location south of Khartoum and recently it was attacked and captured by RSF forces. The capacity of the service providers like health and water is very low compared to the demand on the basic services. The water and hygiene have the high priority.
The focus is to provide minimal access to the water services by rehabilitation and upgrading of water infrastructures; provide access to safe drinking and domestic water; and capacitating the water administration and IDP’s water committees in order to sustain the service delivery at minimum level in this stage.

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

UNOPS will prioritize raising awareness among affected people on their rights concerning humanitarian assistance, aid workers IASC code of conduct and the CBCM mechanism. The Project will make public existing feedback and complaint channels, while fostering consultations with affected communities on their preferred communication channels (hotline number, email address and WhatsApp) in a culturally, gender and age sensitive manner. UNOPS will strengthen its collaboration with the PSEA Network, its common complaint channels and referral systems to cover for the gaps on partners with fewer capacities. UNOPS will implement its Conflict Sensitivity Guidelines (Version 2.0 | that was set in July 2021 to address conflict sensitivity and implement Peacebuilding approaches within the project.
