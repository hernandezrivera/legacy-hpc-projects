Project Summary

The project interventions are going to address the urgent needs of resident community in Basundah locality in Gedarif state which had been affected by flood , movement of migrants, animal herders and clashes at the border with Ethiopia and movement of families to this locality hindering the limited resources of resident community living in this locality the project is going to target 30,000 individual resident community (5700 men- 6300 women- 8700 boys and 9300 girls), all the project target population (30000) will improve their access to safe drinking water (7 lcd), 300 students will be benefiting from establishment of 5 school clubs (150 boys and 150 girls), 10 teachers in 5 schools (2 per school) will be trained on CHAST. The project will address the needs of the target groups through the WASH 2021 HRP objectives:
the project is going to contribute in Increasing access to safe, adequate water facilities, improved hygiene promotion and sanitation practices and assured the capacity of communities empowered in Basunah Locality of Gedarif state through delivering of WASH services interventions .
the project is going to deliver the below expected outputs:
1. Improve access to safe water and dignified sanitation and clean environment free from open defecation
2. Improve access to hygiene services and promote personal practices .

1 - Needs

Community in Gedarif state is a mixture of different tribes from different parts of Sudan in addition to that the state is hosting a number of Eritrean and Ethiopian refugees and internally displaced population (IDPs) from Darfur, Blue Nile and South Kordofan states, all these vulnerable groups are living in the state and sharing community in their limited resources . Adding to that the communities are facing high impact of economic crisis due to lack of food items, unsafe water resources in some parts of the state, unavailability of fuels, high prices, scarcity of necessary basic commodities and other needs together with poor health and hygiene condition which exacerbate difficulty in sustaining their food security livelihood which in turn affected life health condition.Basundah locality, lies in Gedarif sate and adjacent to Ethiopia country near the borders with total populations: 94710 individuals. Access to safe drinking water is less than 40% with less than 20 L/C/D. While almost all people in the locality practice open defecation which attributed to low hygiene and sanitation awareness.According to the Unicef / MoH S3M survey of 2013 access to improved water source varies from village to village due to local variations in hydro (geo) logy, average access to improved water sources at locality level may differ from the S3M data. Overall however, it is clear that many people still depend on unsafe sources of water. Sanitation coverage in the targeted locality is extremely low, and many people do not practice good hygiene. Part of the reason why sanitation and hygiene practices are poor is socio-cultural in nature: many people in the targeted locality have never been used to good sanitation and hygiene behavior, and p With regard to food security, most the state’s population are not meet the international standards to meet their basic food needs, overall, the food situation in Sudan is facing some difficulties due to economic situation which led to high prices regarding vegetables, Proposed project mainly contribute to the sector objectives by strengthening, reinforcing positive coping strategies & reduce vulnerabilities of targeted communities, empowering beneficiaries with improved human capital and livelihood options. Moreover the importance of FSL activities stem from their ability to enable targeted beneficiaries to pursue goals necessary for their survival and longer-term wellbeing, and thereby reduce the vulnerability refer traditional practices.

2 - Activities/Outputs

based on sector objectives on the HRP 2021 objectives, the project will contributes to cluster/ sector objective priorities by saving lives of the targeted communities through sustain and expand water supply, sanitation and hygiene services to around 30000 people, 5700 out of them are men, 6300 women, 8700 boys and 9300 girls resident communities , in addition to build the capacity of the target groups through conduction of WASH- related training, establishment and support of sanitation clubs and conduction of sanitation awareness campaigns.For food security and livelihood the project will contribute to cluster/ sector objective priorities by saving lives of the targeted communities through sustain and expand emergency agricultural inputs and services through distribution of improved seeds and train target communities on basic agricultural practices, farmers will be provided with tools and agricultural inputs, other training will be conducted to beneficiaries in context to lead them to ownership and sustainability. IRW and its partner will work to sustain and expand FSL services to 3000 target resident and host community community members (300 children; 1440 women and 1260 men) in Basundah locality of Gedarif state output:3000 resident and host communities in Basundah provided with emergency agricultural inputs and services and increase their capacities in agricultural practices.- 30000 targeted most vulnerable resident communities improved their access to basic water and hygiene and sanitation services in Basundah locality .

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

Across all sectors the project is going to implement, IRW will mainstream protection and GBV. The project will ensure that services access points are no cause of protection concerns or give way for GBV occurrence. In all trainings that are rendered by the project, a sessions will be dedicated to cover issues related with protection and GBV to increase alertness and vigilance among the community structures. In addition to the project member’s structures, the project will also have a Protection and GBV concern focal points who will be equipped with the required support so that they are able to observe and identify any concerns and immediately notify the project member’s structures for the required immediate action. IRW will seek a way to closely work with police and other law enforcement bodies for cases that need justice. IR Sudan staff will ensure all IR representatives involved in the project including staff, volunteers, implementing partners, contractors, donors or guests when visiting are aware of and adhere to IR Sudan’s Code of Conduct. The IRS Code of Conduct sets out all steps that are reasonably possible to protect children, vulnerable women and men that are beneficiaries of the project from violence, exploitation, neglect and abuse perpetrated by employees, partner organizations, contractors, official visitors and their communities. Communities and children will be sensitized on the rights of children and made aware of the complaints mechanism in place to ensure they feel comfortable to report and concerns of abuse and confident that appropriate action will be taken and will ensure that they are protected from any repercussions.Regular monitoring and auditing of the IR field offices will be carried out by CO technical staff to ensure that policies and procedures are observed, and participation and feedback from the communities is availed.

7.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will inform and interact with inter-agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including SEA and abuse complaints?

Project implementation will start with launching of the project at the State level to bring on board the relevant government authorities for coordination and support. At the project target area level, IRW will start the intervention by orienting the already existed community members’ structures in East Basundah locality. The community members’ structures will be provided with the details of the project activities, implementation plan and budget. The community members will comprised of different category of community members (male, female, boys and girls) and also with representation of people with disability. The involvement of the beneficiaries as key stakeholders in the project design and implementation helps to ensure transparency and sustainability of project activities IRS has organizational and technical capacity that includes qualified staff and well established offices. All key stakeholders are crucial in our exit strategy to ensure the long term sustainability of the projects. for that reason relevant stakeholders including government counterparts were consulted in the design and implementation of the activities. IRS is bound by our code of conduct in every project we implement which places the needs and rights of those we work with at the center of our planning and strategizing and provision of life saving projects.Regular project review will be conducted together with relevant government bodies and the project target beneficiaries. To further ensure accountability, IRW will establish a compliant response mechanism in the project target area where all forwarded complaints will be handled as per IRW’s compliant response mechanism. Raised complaints and provided response will be recorded and made accessible to any interested stakeholder to maintain transparency and accountability.IRW follows the 5th commitment of the CHS with this regard (Communities and people affected by crisis have access to safe and responsive mechanisms to handle complaints), so, target communities have the right to complain to an agency and to receive an appropriate and timely response, as well target communities should be consulted about how they view complaints mechanism, and their complaints to be welcomed, well received, managed and in confidentiality,
