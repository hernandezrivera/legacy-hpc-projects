Project Summary

Based on the Gap & Need analysis from the health sector; Christian Aid wants to continue health services among the Camp-15 & 19 population. Christian Aid will be continuing 2 fixed health post (FHP) in 2 camps. Through these 2 FHP Outpatient Department services will be conducted. Mostly targeted elderly, children, pregnant and lactating mothers, PWD, Women/Girls who are survivors of gender-based violence (GBV). Also, services will be provided for Adolescent Sexual and Reproductive Health (ASRH) including mental health support conditions (PFA). Door to door Health Education and Family Planning counselling will be provided by trained community health worker (CHW).
Through these 2 FHP; our total OPD target is 13,500 individual and total consultation 47,250 including Sexual Reproductive Health (SRH) services (2-4 times in year). Under total consultation, 10,395-persons under 5Y (including Neonatal and Infant) and 16,538-person adolescents will be received health services. In FHP, normal delivery will be available for people, attendant by Skill Birth Attendant ((SBA). Total 43,200 HH will be received Door-to-door consultations on SRH and family planning issue for young couples in 3 times in a year by trained community health workers (CHW).

Needs

Since the onset of the crisis in August 2017, the Rohingya people arrived in Bangladesh with poor health status. Accessibility of quality essential health services is imperative to reduce preventable death and disease, both in Rohingya well as in the nearby host community. In Camp – 15 total number of Population 49900 (As Need and Population Monitoring report by IOM, 02 October 2018.) – 7 FHP (fixed Health Post) & 2 Primary Health Care (PHC) (24/7) and 1 Other Specialized. 2 FHP running by Christian Aid Partner up-to March-2019. Christian Aid will be continuing One Health Post, Up-to December-2019 by supported Sector. Camp- 19 total num number of Population 22200 (NPM-October 2, 2018) – 2 FHP now available. 1 FHP center running by Christian Aid Partner up-to March-2019. Christian Aid will be continuing One Health Post, Up-to December-2019 by supported Sector. Need Analysis: All FHP in the same area, especially near CiC Hub/Army Office. Rohingya People, who are living far from FHP and who are more vulnerable (elderly, Pregnant women, people with disability, >5Y child with mother, lactating mother); they cannot visit health post. Waiting time in the queue to meet doctor is longer in FHP. Moreover, all people do not know available services in FHP. Therefore, a large number of people do not get the required services. Considerable inequitable distribution of health facilities across the camps, we need to increase FHP services Christian Aid wants to continue ongoing health service until December-2019 in existing Camp (15 & 19) area. Within this area, the health services needs are even greater for the most vulnerable sub-groups- such as the elderly, children, pregnant and lactating mothers, PWD, Women/Girls who are survivors of gender-based violence (GBV). There also need for Adolescent Sexual and Reproductive Health (ASRH) services including mental health support conditions (PFA) etc. Door to door Health Education and Family Planning counselling are very essential in the camp area and nearby community for ensuring better health.

Activities or outputs

1. Provide 47,250 (disaggregated by Sex, Disability and Age) outpatient primary health care consultation through 2 Existing Fixed Health Post (Camp-15-1, and Camp-19-1). 2. Provide psychosocial first aid services by trained professionals including Doctors (male/Female) in FHP with referred and Trained Community Health Worker (CHW) in field level. 3. Provide outpatient consultations services to 10,395 (disaggregated by Sex, Disability and Age) children who are <5 years (including Neonatal and Infant). 4. Provide adolescent sexual and reproductive health (ASRH) services through FHP & Mobile Clinics. 5. Make available Normal delivery services in FHP assist by (SBA) Skill Birth Attendant (Doctor/Nurse/Midwives) with referral facility. 6. SRH and Family Planning Counselling session conducted by 20 health workers. They will be reached 6,000 couples once in a every 3 months and three times in 12 months duration. 7. Door-to-Door consultation and support on PFA, FP Counselling, HIV/AIDS awareness session and SRH Session will be received 3,600 HHs by 20 trained health workers on a monthly basis total 43,200 HHs will be reached in 12 months duration (multiple count). Approximately 60% people will be visited FHP, who will be referred by CHW. 8. Provide Support to women and girls to have access to information, improved health and hygiene practices through door 2 door visit by trained CHW. 9. Provide psychosocial support in order to create an enabling environment for good nutrition and healthcare practices through door 2 door visit by trained CHW. 10. Provide Psychosocial First Aid Training to all Staffs by Christian Pool Master Trainer to using Christian Aid own Manual which has Developed by Church of Sweden. 11. Provide CHW Basic Training to Using Community Health Working Group Materials and Manual by Christian Aid Staffs and Working Group Members.
