Project Summary

Structural barriers and discriminatory social norms continue to limit women's authority in decision-making and political participation in rural households and communities. Women and girls in rural areas lack equal opportunities to access resources, productive assets, and public services such as education, healthcare, infrastructure, and are often subjected to early marriage. Every gender and development indicator reveals that rural women and girls are in a worse situation than rural men and urban women, suffering from poverty, exploitation, and various forms of violations.
The situation in Syria is considered more severe, where the crisis has affected society in its various forms, classes, and individuals. Women have borne the brunt of the negative repercussions of this crisis on all aspects of their economic, social, educational, and even psychological lives. The rise in food insecurity, poverty, and unemployment as a natural result of the harsh conflict and economic deterioration has increased the risk of women and girls being exposed to gender-based violence. Rural women are among the most affected and suffering from the results of the destructive conflict on all levels, especially in Deir ez-Zor province, considering that rural women there are largely involved in agricultural work and animal care on their own land or others, in addition to their household and family duties. The crisis has affected various aspects of the lives of women and girls there, such as increased working hours, lower daily wages, lack of self-confidence, exploitation, violence, and deprivation of their basic rights, as well as bearing other burdens resulting from war and the high prices of essential food items. This has forced many rural girls to leave their education in order to provide for themselves and support their families. It is also worth mentioning that the loss of rural men's jobs due to unemployment and economic contraction, and their spending more time at home, has contributed to an increase in physical and verbal violence against women and girls, reduced self-respect, and increased levels of anxiety and depression among them.
Therefore, providing psychological and social support for women and girls who have been exploited and suffered psychological harm has become necessary to help rebuild their self-confidence and rehabilitate them to live safely and healthily in society. The project aims to empower 800 rural women and girls in the Tabni area in the western countryside of Deir ez-Zor Governorate and engage them in the local community, as well as help them strengthen resilience, positivity, and adapt to difficult circumstances, providing counseling and psychological support. And reintegrating women into society in a proper and balanced manner. The project also aims to encourage achieving financial independence for women and girls by increasing their economic participation and enhancing the culture of women's entrepreneurship in various sectors to contribute to creating suitable job opportunities for women in these sectors. It also supports and enhances the performance of productive groups to include a large number of female workers, increase decent job opportunities for women, and empower them economically, where empowerment is the process through which a woman becomes individually and collectively aware of the way in which she is influenced by power sources in her life, gaining self-confidence and the ability to address and overcome inequality between her and men.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Project Goals:1- Providing psychological and social support services, health awareness, and awareness about gender-based violence to 800 women and girls within 9 months.2- Training and qualifying 150 women and girls through vocational training activities and entrepreneurship training within 4 months.3- Providing financial grants to 100 beneficiaries of vocational training services divided into 25 groups (each group consisting of 4 beneficiaries) where each group receives $5000 to start their own collective projects. Project Activities:The project aims to protect and enhance women's self-confidence and empower them through the following activities:1- Psychological and social support: Through activities providing psychological support to beneficiaries, conducting group awareness sessions, offering individual counseling, initial psychological first aid, and psychological therapy services.2- Awareness about gender-based violence: By implementing workshops on gender and gender-based violence (abuse and exploitation, gender principles and standards, prevention tools, dealing with violence, and assisting violence victims).3- Health and sexual education and promoting health awareness, including important topics such as family planning methods, awareness about the dangers of selective abortion based on gender, unsafe abortion, premarital counseling, awareness about sexually transmitted diseases, in addition to recognizing self-care health methods.4- Training and qualifying 150 women who are breadwinners, widows, and victims of violence by providing vocational training programs, entrepreneurship training, and equipping them with the requirements of the job market. These girls and women will be selected based on criteria of vulnerability and fragility, and based on the outputs of the psychological and abuse assessment through group and individual psychological support sessions.5- Empowering 20 groups of beneficiaries with vocational training and entrepreneurship training to start their own projects by providing financial support to them.Men will be encouraged to participate in awareness sessions and psychological support sessions with the aim of increasing societal awareness and promoting a culture of safe participation for women in decision-making, accepting their integration into society and political life, and reducing violence and unethical acts against them.
