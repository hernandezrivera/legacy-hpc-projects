Project Summary

In response to the most pressing needs in the most underserved areas in Syria, several cities and towns will be targeted in Hasakeh, Deir Ezzor, Raqqa and Aleppo governorate with WASH assistance in light maintenance and rehabilitation of water supplies, sewage and solid waste collection and disposal.
The targeted governorates inherently suffer from severe weakness in the delivery of almost all basic services related to water and sanitation and solid waste management. With the on-going crisis, depleted assets (human, financial and physical) hindered local authorities to respond to the immediate and humanitarian high needs in this area. Additionally, the communities in the target areas of the governorates suffer from seasonal outbreak of water borne diseases and Leishmania which put thousands of inhabitants at risk.
Through this project, UNDP will timely intervene to curb the impact of poor services and ensure access to clean affordable water, reduce water related diseases leishmaniasis incident through waters supplies (including wells) and sewage networks rehabilitation and solid waste collection and removal, insecticide spraying and awareness-raising campaigns to promote the healthy and hygienic environment in the target areas and to help provide decent living conditions for their communities. Activities are in line with WASH strategic objective1.
Under the project, UNDP will expand response to the WASH services by targeting around 24 locations in four governorates(Hasakeh, Deir-Ezzor, Raqqa and Aleppo) to support services for the unserved resident and ensure people have their basic need met.in addition UNDP will provide necessary tools and equipment and technical expertise and labor to support the implementation of project activities.

4 - Needs

According the HNO 2020 findings, access to sufficient and affordable water, adequate sanitation, solid waste management for affected communities are still in need of humanitarian assistance. According to the forecast of the WASH Sector needs of affected populations of WASH services and maintain WASH facilities and infrastructure, will continue to be high in 2020. Years of crisis and insufficient funding to respond to WASH needs have had cumulative effects on water, waste management and sanitation systems across the country. As a result, the need for supporting water systems (operation and maintenance, basic rehabilitation), sewerage and solid waste management systems will remain a priority, focusing on overburdened communities, areas with high return ratio and areas highly dependent on water trucking. The primary WASH need across Syria remains access to enough quantity of safe water as a high need. Despite efforts to restore or maintain water systems’ functionality and countrywide support to water treatment processes still up to 42 per cent of surveyed households are relying on alternative and often unsafe water sources to meet or complement their water needs.The wash assessment conducted in 2019 indicate 11% of communities are unable to access a sufficient quality of water and sanitation. 23% of garbage is not disposed properly and 14% of the population is underserved and/or with low frequency of garbage collection which leads to spread of diseases associated with bad management of solid waste mainly Leishmaniosis which was an endemic disease in Northern Syria. Findings of the rapid assessment conducted by UNDP field team within targeted areas indicates that there is an urgent need to provide wash services in waters supplies (including wells), sewage networks rehabilitation and solid waste collection and removal, and awareness-raising campaigns to promote healthy hygienic environment and help provide decent living conditions for their communities.

5 - Implementation of activities

The project will be implemented under direct supervision of UNDP field staff, activities implementation will include: preparatory phase to set up project team to implement the required tasks, in parallel verify inputs required to undertake the planned activities, prepare technical specs for equipment, tools and launch the procurement process needed to provide the requirements to rehabilitate the targeted wells, pumping station and sewage networks in addition to undertake the solid waste management activities. Procurement will be done by UNDP, some activities will be implemented by private sector, others will be implemented by local workers mainly from targeted communities. Moreover, UNDP will monitor the Installation of procured equipment and ensure efficient operation and maintenance of the rehabilitated supplies and the collection and removal of solid waste.UNDP will coordinate with local municipalities to complement their work and cover the shortage with all partners in the sector on the ground to ensure complementary and synergy of roles needed to maximize the impact of the project and to ensure an integrated package of services to help strengthen the stability of local communities. UNDP Team will be responsible for the overall implementation of the project. A dedicated project team will be in place to follow up on activities. Technical committees will oversee project implementation and will conduct periodic site visits to track progress against indicators and follow up on the process of the executed work; review periodic reports and ensure compliance with the work plan and required technical specifications. The committee includes representation from UNDP project management team, local municipalities and local community. Furthermore, quartly reports 4w will be shared with WASH sector and OCHA. Field Team in Hasakeh, Deir-Ezor, Raqqa and Aleppo will conduct regular site visits. UNDP Damascus will conduct two field missions to track progress against set targets

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

In the case of an intervention by a cross-border organization, the project will have the flexibility to move to another neighborhood or to inform them through the partner to target another neighborhoodOperational: in Hasakeh, there is more than one authority on the ground, so any unplanned intervention may indicate bias and affect the neutral image of interventions and raise sensitivities among society or dominant political forcesMitigation: a geographical targeting mechanism has been developed that takes into account diversity and balanced representation, the adoption of clear and precise targeting criteria that emphasize the neutral image of interventions and their humanitarian role.Political: more violence in the area Mitigation measure : Although there are some political risks which might affect implementation, taking into consideration the criticality of the intervention and to maintain organizations credibility, the risk is acceptable.
