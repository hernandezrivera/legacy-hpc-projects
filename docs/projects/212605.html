Project Summary

In 2024, Mercy-USA will concentrate its efforts on improving Camp Coordination and Camp Management (CCCM) activities in 20 IDPs camps (including the dignified shelter camps established by Mercy-USA) in North West Syria. Through a multi-faceted approach, Mercy-USA aims to establish camp management committees, raise awareness on fire safety, enhance site infrastructure, provide training to community leaders, and foster community-led integration and skills-building initiatives.
Key Activities:
Establishment of Camp Management Committees: Mercy-USA will work closely with relevant clusters to establish camp management committees encompassing key areas such as Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene (WASH), Health, Safety and Security, Education, Shelter and Non-Food Items (SNFI), and Protection. These committees will play a vital role in overseeing camp operations and coordinating various services.
Awareness Sessions on Fire Mitigation/Prevention: Mercy-USA will conduct awareness sessions for the camp population to educate them on fire mitigation and prevention techniques. These sessions will empower residents to identify and address fire risks, ultimately reducing the incidence of fire-related incidents within the camps.
Site Improvements and Maintenance: Mercy-USA will undertake site maintenance activities to enhance the physical infrastructure of the camps. This includes initiatives such as drainage improvements, disability-friendly facilities, flood defenses, lighting poles, and other measures to improve overall camp safety and resilience.
Training for Camp Management Committees and Community Leaders: Mercy-USA will provide comprehensive training to camp management committees and community leaders to enhance their capacity to manage camp operations effectively. This training will cover various aspects of camp management, including coordination, conflict resolution, and community engagement.
Discussing exit /phaseout strategy: mapping relevant stakeholders, capacity building and handover of site management functions.
Conduct community-led integration and skills-building activities including women and youth-led initiatives and start-up grants in laison with ERL
Implement tailored livelihood activities designed to enable HHs to restore their assets and build resilience for better solutions
Establishment of fire points as per the CCCM cluster guidelines'
