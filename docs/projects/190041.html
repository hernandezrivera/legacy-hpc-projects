1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project aims to provide life-saving and life-sustaining food assistance to 4000 insecure IDP households, equivalent to 20,000 individuals; including those in camps and those located within host communities. The project projects to supply a once-month ration of food which would contain: 50 kg of cereal, 10kg of pulses, 5 liters of oil, 1K salt. The work will be done in Metuge and Pemba.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Provide regular food assistance to food-insecure vulnerable households maintaining the WFP nutritional recommendations. Such as, such as wheat flour or rice; lentils, chickpeas, or other pulses; vegetable oil: sugar; and salt. 5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The program has trained counselors that go out with the team. If a situation comes up that is detected as a protection risk for an identified IDP a careful anonymous complaint is encouraged to be made. And proper follow through of the situation is made using the PSEA hotline and a PSEA support personnel. Iris Global’s distribution staff have been trained in PSEA and have signed terms of agreement corresponding with our internal policy/terms.Any complaints IDPs have, they are encouraged to call linha verde.Iris Global is currently training and setting up a post-distribution follow up team for field assessment and surveys on PSEA

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

The project will use the point person of local government and local Chiefs to communicate with their regarded populations that they have authority over; They will announce the date of the distribution to the population and on the day of they will be present helping move things along and ensuring the overall satisfaction of the population he/she oversees. On the day of the Distribution, he/she will help mediate any potential situation.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

There is no discrimination of food assistance on the basis of disability. There are workers on the ground who are prepared to assist the disabled in receiving and carrying the food.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project is mindful of protecting the environment and cleaning up all waste at our distributions. Aside from regular carbon emissions from our truck used to transport the food, there are no concerns beyond that. The workers are instructed to use latrines and to properly dispose of trash in designated trash receptacles.
