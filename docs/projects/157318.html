Project Summary

The protracted crisis in Central Darfur, North Darfur, East Darfur, South Kordofan, West Kordofan and Blue Nile states and Abyei PCA Area, has had major social and economic on the region, making a significant number of peoples displaced, causing significant loses, destruction of livelihoods and infrastructure and negative access to public services which affect negatively on children. The project proposes to use a comprehensive community based and non-community based approaches that enhances local capacities of conflict affected communities to care for the most vulnerable of their communities, this project is designed to enhance protection intervention and response for children of displaced and vulnerable residents. Women and children bear the biggest brunt in such circumstances and children are the epicenter of this intervention. The affected include but are not limited to children at risk (CAR) and other vulnerable children, PSN EVI, vulnerable displaced persons and their host communities.
The project intends to have robust community based and non-community based CP activities that not only target the support to children but also aims at building community capacity to identify respond to and refer CAR and to create self-confidence and assertiveness for them to enhance their protection against violence including Gender Based Violence (GBV). the project will reach children through trained SW, animators, CBPN, PSS mobile teams and peer educators.
To ensure the protection of persons of concern, the particular needs of groups such as, persons with disability, at-risk children, adolescents, survivors of sexual and gender based violence and persons of concern with psychosocial needs need to be addressed in a timely and appropriate manner through targeted programmes.

Needs

In conflict affected communities as is the case in this project, the most vulnerable suffer double tragedy they have problems with their access to service and suffer violations in addition to their displacement especially for those who suffer from protracted displacement or several displacements. According to HNO, inter-agency assessments and focus group discussion/ consultation conducted by GAH in previous and ongoing projects. the total of affected population in South Kordofan are 61,587 IDPs- 58,945 HCs in (Alabassiya, Abujebha, Kadugli), in West Kordofan 1,630 IDPs in (Elnehoud, Elmeiram) , in Blue Nile 20,214 IDPs- 19,305 HCs (Aldamazeen, Geissan), in East Darfur 85,181 IDPs- 59,376 HCs (Ed daein, Adila, Yassin), in North Darfur 293,652- 186,438 HCs (Saraf Omra, Kebkabiya, Alfashir), in Central Darfur(Zalngi) and Abyei PCA Area. the humanitarian situation is critical in all assessed localities is and each of them is ranked high by the child protection sub-sector.60% of the assessed population are women and children, the lack of protection networks, trained PSS staff and huge number of unaccompanied and Separated Children were reported in the assessments and consultations sessions. in addition, to cases of GBV (physically and mentally disorders children and adolescent, FGM and early marriage) are widely practiced. Also, child labor which expose children to even more GBV and SGBV and school drop off. The project aims to establish and support community based child protection networks to identify PSNs/EVI/CAR and, to establish referral mechanism to link those in need with service providers, raising awareness about child protection, GBV and harmful practices. Referral systems will supported\established to ensure their outreach to areas with conflict-affected population, primarily IDPs, not covered previously.This project targets to reach vulnerable children directly through PSS sessions. The project, also targets to reach women, mothers and care givers of the children with CP messages. Children with disabilities will get support through assistive devices and referral to other services. The families of the children will also benefit and the people in target communities will receive key messages in child protection through, mass campaigns and communication. The project also targets to enhance the capacity of Child protection Network members, whom 60% will be women. Also, social workers and community mobilizers to increase their level of knowledge on CP issues (identification of CAR, referral and basic PSS) There are also children and adolescent who will be trained as child leaders to conduct peer counseling and child therapy, recreation and sports in child.

Output2.2: people have equitable access to essential assistance based on vulnerability targeting; 2.1 training service providers on provision of PSS/PFA to children and identification and prevention of family separation.2.2 training teachers, social workers, animators on PSS, case management and mainstreaming integrated child protection, PVHP and other relevant services based. 2.3 Establish/ enhance CBCPN on PSN/ CAR identification, case management and referral pathways.2.4 establish/ enhance community complain and response system.2.5 development of information materials to educate/ inform community members on SEA and how to seek assistance.
