Project Summary

The project will be implemented by CAFOD in partnership with national NGO (Global Aid Hand ( GAH) . The project will tragets IDPs and host community - in South Kordofan/ Rashad locality in three villages communities -west of Rashad town, Um Darawa, Tendimin, and Keleiro, resulted in a wave of displacement towards Rashad town.
The project overall objective is to improve food security and livelihood of the IDPs and host community - The livelihood of the IDPs is depends mainly on farming and non-farming activities. The project will focus on IDPs and host community.
Total targeted groups are the neediest families (2000 HHs) 600 in Darawa, 600 in Tendimin, and K 800 in Keleiro who will directly benefited from the project services.
The community from the selected areas are vulnerable IDPs who felew form homes as resulted on the conflict in South Sudan raised between 2011 and 2016, the Sudan Armed Forces (SAF) and the SPLM-N . The IDPs displaced to seek safety and livelihood opportunities Rashad locality, their livelihood was disrupted hardly hit by current country economic situation and the displacement. Their livelihood is mainly farming, livestock and non-farming activities before the displacement.
Strategy of selection of individual beneficiaries of the project will be based on the level the needs in terms of food security, socio-economic services, considering the active economic farmers, availability of land and women headed household. Community consultation include community forums and the leadership of the villages will be involved in leading the processes of project design and identification of the targeted HHs. Project partner staff and community leaders will be trained in Save, Accessible, Dignify and inclusive programming- SADI to help community in developing community complaint mechanism- and feedback.
Project activities include:- Provision of agriculture services and inputs.
Provision of veterinary services, this will include, conduct refreshment training to 10 CAHWs, Vaccination of animal. Provision of IGAs kits - Train women in Energy efficient stove . Train partner staff and community members in Save, Accessible, Dignify and Inclusive program tools.
- Establish on Community action group (CAG) and carry out DRR training of 20 selected members from Juri HC in DRR , develop Community action plans CAPs. and Implement and monitor the CAP.project team will develop project details implementation plan, which will be main guidance in project monitoring- Partner update reports, progress reports will be sources of project monitoring add to field visit and regular update the project DIP

1 - Needs

The conflict in south Kordofan, raised 2011-2016 between SPLA-N and Sudanese Arm Force ( SAF as result of dis agreement in comprehensive peace agreement - resulted into flow of population of IDPs within and outside of Rashad locality seeking for safety, livelihood and basic services. According to inter agency Assessment Report: [Rashad, South Kordofan State] conducted October 2018], currently there are 22,324 IDPs composed of 3,576 households living in displacement in Rashad. They comprise of 12,520 females, 9,794 males and 4,702 children under five years of age. IDPs originated from Tabdoun, Terre, Tagelbo, Um Darawa, Tendimin, El Beyeera, El Mangala, Serein, Alsaraf, Woroula, Douma, El Mansour, El Moglum and Keleiro. IDPs community generally faces challenges to cope with the displacement, as their livelihood disrupted . Women in this community face most challenges since they are mainly the household livelihoods providers, serving on casual work, petty trading among other limited livelihoods income alternative
