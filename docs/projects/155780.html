Project Summary

The proposed project will fill the gaps in the delivery of essential lifesaving Primary Health Care (PHC) services to the vulnerable returned population of North Waziristan Tribal District of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. For this purpose, EHSAR will target 4 government Health facilities (BHU level) in the targeted District of North Waziristan Tribal District. EHSAR will make transitional arrangements at existing 4 non-functional Government’s Health facilities. The services will include consultations and treatment of infectious diseases, management of acute and chronic health disorders, psychosocial support to mentally handicapped persons, MHCH services, support the government in routine immunization and vaccination, routine investigation through Laboratories, provision of lifesaving drug and other supplements and referrals of complicated cases to tertiary health facilities. EHSAR will supplement these services with health education and Hygiene Promotion activities.
Moreover, the targeted Health facilities will be renovated through minor repair, supplemented by solarization of the targeted health facilities, to ensure the smooth and facilitated operations of the health services.
It is estimated that a total of 69,250 individuals will be benefited from this intervention in the proposed 9 months.

Needs

North Waziristan Tribal District is one of the area, which faced sever militancy followed by military operations against the non-state actors, which resultant around 108,041 registered families to displacement. Along with the registered IDP’s families, more than 35,000 families remained un-registered which were also displaced to the nearby districts and some to Khost in Afghanistan. After the de-notification of the conflict affected areas at North Waziristan Tribal District, the return of the IDPs families were initiated. According to the statistical report of FDMA as of 22nd January 2019, total 92,374 registered families have been returned to North Waziristan Agency, along with 21,962 un-registered families. The report further says that total 6,664 families are also been returned from Khost Province of Afghanistan. The statistics of the returned families shows that heavy population are now settled at north Waziristan tribal District, while on the other hand, the assistance from Humanitarian community and Government are minimal, and the people are facing sever needs for food, Health services, and other sectorial interventions.Due to the military operation in the areas, the entire infrastructure including health facilities, were badly affected and damaged, which are not able to provide standard services to the returnees in the area as well as to support a sustainable return. According to the Multi-cluster assessment report, 58% of the returned population can’t access OR limited access to Health Services. Furthermore, 66% female cannot avail the essential reproductive health services, because of the un-availability of female doctors and other paramedic staff. Moreover, the multi-cluster assessment report indicates that there is no routine laboratory services exist in the area of return, while 33% of the households are suffering with mental health and Psychological disorder. Vaccinations to children are not ensured, and almost all the children under five are not fully vaccinated under Routine EPI mechanism.According to the need prioritization report, the government made functional the THQ Miranshah and Mir li, but still there is need of greater outreach for Primary Health Care services and Reproductive Health including Antenatal and postnatal care services, to far flung villages of the tribal district. Both the hospitals are overburdened, while the shortage of medicine is also observed.Health services are one of the emergency responses to the situation, which is essential to facilitate the returned caseload in the targeted areas. As per EHSAR past experience in the sector of intervention particularly in merged districts (Formerly FATA), health services are essential to assist the vulnerable people in the geographical area, especially women and children. Hence it is extremely important to revitalize the primary health care services to fulfill the needs of men, women, boys and girls and other vulnerable groups on equal basis. It will increase the access of the masses including women and other vulnerable groups to life saving services, including general treatment through qualified doctors, Mother and Child Health care services to overcome the challenges during the pregnancy and deliveries, reproductive health services, free medicine provision for all patients, supplements for pregnant ladies and infants, and other supplies. The proposed intervention will assist 69,250 individuals (11,542 families) consisting 15,235 male, 16,620 female, 18,005 boys and 19,390 girls.

Activities

In the proposed project, the following activities will be carried out during the project period;==> Revitalization of primary health care services in the areas of returns through general outdoor patients (OPD) to strengthen the PHC services at the existing health facilities and ensure that female can access the female paramedic staffs (33% of female & 54% children will be targeted)==> Organizing of Mobile Camps in the far flung areas==> Antenatal & postnatal care for pregnant & lactating women, and ensure safe delivery in the nearby Secondary level hospital, referred through ambulance services.==> Provision of life saving reproductive health services through implementation of MISP in areas of displacement and return==> Mother and child health promotion activities through lady health visitors in the target health facilities==> Provision of mental health and Psycho social support to the affected women, through female psychologist==> Provision of Medical laboratory, to ensure the easy access of the returnee for medical investigations, referred by Medical Officers==> Provision of essential medicine for the entire caseload, and ensure active and well equipped Pharmacy management through professional Pharmacists==> Health education, awareness and Hygiene Promotion in the catchment areas of targeted health facilities through male and female Hygiene Promoters==> Referral mechanism strengthen, and referral of complicated cases/patients to Secondary/tertiary Level hospital through ambulance service, after stabilizing at the basic health unit==> Support to government in routine Immunization and vaccination of children through initiation of EPI services in the targeted health facilities==> Implementation of DEWS in the target health facilities==> Standard response to alerts and outbreaks of priority communicable diseases==> Provision of basic furniture and medical equipment to each targeted health facilities==> Minor repair of the targeted health facilities to ensure the standard health services and sustainable operation==> Installation of solar system at each targeted health facilities to ensure the smooth operation of the health services
