Project Summary

Mercy-USA for Aid and Development will support internally displaced persons (IDPs) and host community in Maarrat An Numan Sub-district in Idleb governorate with winterization support in the form of in-kind, eco-friendly biofuel, and stove kits (in line with SNFI activity 1.2 Provision of seasonal and supplementary NFIs and shelter assistance (e.g. winter clothing, fuel, winter-specific shelter upgrades; including cash/voucher for these items).
Total number of beneficiaries who will be targeted 12,350 individuals (Men 2,346- Women 2470- Boys 3,582- Girls 3,952) by using with in-kind methodology for implementation.
Mercy-USA will target each household with in-kind 900 kg of olive pit fuel throughout the winter months (December, January and February) A distribution of 300 kg per month along with stoves for 25% of the target for new arrived IDPs depending on a preset selection criteria for the most vulnerable households.
On the other hand, Mercy-USA will target 50 HH (Men 48, Women 50, Boys 72, and Girls 80) in Ariha Sub-district with shelter rehabilitation and repair for slightly damaged houses due to fighting on this area, in order to enhance safe access to shelter such as repair windows and wall holes without extreme damage taking in our considerations HLP issue prior to any house selection can be made by using contactor with cost no more than 1000 USD per HH.

Needs

According to HNO 2019, roughly 4.5 million are People in need for NFI assistance.According to the assessment which was conducted by REACH and SNFI Cluster as of September 2018: 66% of households reported inability to afford one or more NFIs in the three months preceding data collection from which (29%) reported inability to afford Heating fuel which alarming with critical NFI need especially for heating fuel during winter. Heating fuel was reported the first priority for both Elderly Men (69%) and Elderly Women (71%) which gives us clear evidence for importance for this life-saving item especially for the most vulnerable groups such as elderly.On the other hand, for shelter 47% of households reported shelter damage (compared with 39% in July 2017). Of the 47% of households reporting shelter damage, 37% were unable to conduct repairs in the three months preceding data collection 37% were unable to conduct repairs in the three months preceding data collection. 73% of households reported possessing legal documentation to prove their occupancy status.Based on the aforementioned information, Mercy-USA see that it is critical to support the most vulnerable people like women, children, and elderly with lifesaving olive-pit fuel and shelter rehabilitation for securing privacy for maintain protection in their houses taking into account verifying HLP documentation before assistance provision for shelter.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumptions: - Cross border access from Turkey is available- Easy and smooth Logistical access- Cooperation from local councils- Facilitation and acceptance from major regional stakeholders/actors- Timely fund dispersal - Targeting of most vulnerable beneficiaries- Recruitment of female staff within distribution teams will be considered-All shipped items will arrive in good physical status- Warehousing process will be smooth without issues- Local qualified contractors are available in the targeted area-HLP documentation are available and maintained Risks: - General daily security threats including robbery and looting - Macro level conflict related threats including aerial bombardment, advancement of hostile actors, shifting alliances, border closure, and chemical attack - Danger to staff and beneficiaries including conflict between beneficiaries - Delays in fund dispersal-Not applying the pre-determined selection criteria for any reason- While female staff can assist logistically it is culturally not accepted for them to take part in the actual distribution for better data and ID verification during distribution process. Presence of female in distribution staff will give advantage for more accurate distribution, and flexibility for female-headed HH- Some shipped items are damaged due to multiple load and unload processes-Fire hazard and/or conflict related activities happen during warehousing process- No enough contractors are available for shelter repair and rehabilitation in the targeted area-There is no HLP documentsRisk Mitigation: - Following Mercy-USA safety and security guidelines and established safety protocols- In the event of major macro level conflict related threats, Mercy-USA will take appropriate action that is commensurate with the scale of the threat. This may include: - Suspending operations temporarily-Shifting resources and operations to more secure locations - Clear steps will be taken to mitigate risks between staff and beneficiaries including gender based violence. - Project staff may be dismissed at any time in the event of endangering beneficiaries or other staff, and this will be clearly outlined in each employment contract. - Contingency stock will be in place for shipments to avoid any impact if Bab Alhawa borders become closed (for items purchased from Turkey) - Gender balance and equal opportunities will be taken into consideration when hiring staff for different position such as and not limited to M&E officer.-All workers will be given the proper needed instructions ahead of any deliveries.All necessary precautions will be taken to prevent a fire incident and no accelerants will be present at the warehouse. -Mercy-USA might use qualified contractors from different area, in order to get shelter rehabilitation done smoothly with high quality. -Mercy-USA will check HLP documents before any beneficiary selection, moreover, HLP will be key criteria for choosing beneficiary.
