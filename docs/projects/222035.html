Project Summary

The project is intended increase equitable access and utilization of quality live saving nutrition services to prevent malnutrition among young children ,adolescents and women in Aweil North ,Aweil East ,Bor South ,Canal/Pigi,Fangak,Manyo ,Panyikang and Fashoda counties of Northern Bahr El Gazal State,Jonglei State and Upper Nile State respectively .The project is targeting a total of Thirty eight thousand six hundred forty eight (38648) people in eight counties.

1. Needs

According to IPC 2024 report , '4.3 million children under five, as well as pregnant and breastfeeding mothers, will need life-saving treatment and preventive care'(IPC 18/112024 report) now up to mid 2025. This is a worrying figure in a country whose population is estimated to be 12millions people .This current situation is expected to deteriorate in 2025 due floods ,influx of more returnees and refugees from Sudan and the ongoing conflicts in some parts of the country .To alleviate current and future effects of malnutrition ,it is paramount to act promptly.

2. Project activities

This nutrition intervention is targeting under five years children ,breastfeeding women ,pregnant women ,adolescent girls and the elderly people through maternal ,infant and young child nutrition(MIYCN) counselling to pregnant women ,lactating women and caregivers. Counselling sessions will be offered to pregnant women ,Breast Feeding women and caregivers at the health facilities and in the communities on how to prevent malnutrition. Community Networking Group(CNG) will conduct screening to identify malnutrition cases among pregnant women, breastfeeding women and under five children .This screening exercise will be conducted in the health facilities and in the community through the help of Community Nutrition Volunteers (CNV).Community Networking Group (CNG) with Support from donors will provide Blanket supplementary Feeding Program(BSFP) to the malnourished children ,pregnant women ,adolescent girls and breastfeeding women .This BSFP feeding program is intended to prevent deterioration in the nutritional status of the identified vulnerable groups. Maternal Infant and Young Child Nutrition(MIYCN) training to Community Nutrition Volunteers(CNVs) and Health Workers to equip the health practitioners with the necessary knowledge needed to curb malnutrition among young children, adolescents girls pregnant women and lactating women.
