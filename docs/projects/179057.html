Project Summary

The project is to establish two fixed safe spaces for women and girls in Idleb and Aleppo governorate, also there will be 2 mobile centers in the camps of Idleb and Aleppo.
The project objective is a provision of GBV awareness-raising within the target communities, further to support and enhance safe access for the protection services including GBV. the project also supports women socio-economic wellbeing by a women empowerment program from PSS and vocational training.
The targeted BNFs will be from women, men, and children from Boys and Girls taking into most consideration the inclusion of people and children with disabilities and the barriers from economic, safety, social and negative norms as shown in HNO 2021, in which women and girls suffer from in the targeted communities. The Project staff capacity building is a major part, as RW has started attending the general protection cluster and GBVSC. Information sharing protocol is one of the first components which will ensure to have from GBVSC to be part in the area service map and being part in providing safe referrals according to the available services in the area.
In addition, RW will coordinate and consulate with GBVSC to ensure the effectiveness of the suggested a list of pieces of training which include: SGBV core concept, protection mainstreaming, PSEA, safe identification and safe referrals, PSS for women and girls, Awareness raising main topics orientation session which will be related to GBV, in addition, any suggestion from the cluster to receive GBV related pieces of training from a certified service provider in the area, will be highly considered to ensure the proposed quality from the activities.
RW confirm that we will enhance formal engagement with and participation to the GBV SC, including attending inter-agency training in order to pass any weakness in our capacity as RW takes the first steps in GBV sector.
The project on the filed will be led by a GBV specialist who will be responsiple of build of capacity of the field staff as well as to insure the quality of the provided services and thier allince with the GBV SC standards.
RW is committed to keep attending the protection and GBVSC meetings and underline their capacity-building programs. The project main activities are awareness-raising for GBV related topics for the targeted communities for both fixed and mobile centers. Provision of safe referral for the project BNFs who are in need and referred them to the available services in the area, taking into most consideration ISP in addition to providing transportation for the needed referred BNFs including disabilities. Provide PSS sessions for women and girls from the young adults, adults, youth, adolescents in addition to the girls who are from 9 to 17yrs to improve their wellbeing and welfare. Provide vocational training for women who are above 18 years. Generally, the activities will be the same in the centers and outside it with stress on the needs of IDPs and hosting community's differences.
Activities will be based on needs assessments and HNO address issues with women from adults and youth to assess their interests, and will identify facilitators from the IDPs and host community for all the gender needs for males and females which are from women, men, boys and girls taking in much consideration the youth needs and adolescents.
in all project stages Covid-19 mitigation and prevention measures will be in place, centre disinfections, PPE for staff and BNFs, Water and soup availabilities, the total number of people per session is no more than 6 with ensuring the social distancing, and increase awareness of Covid-19 prevention/mitigation by WHO brochures.

4 - Needs

A decade of conflict has trapped civilians in a pervasive protection crisis where people’s rights to safety, security and well-being are regularly violated. "Women and girls are particularly vulnerable to GBV because of gender inequality and discrimination in Syria. In the households, women and girls have taken on additional roles traditionally associated with men, including as heads of households and principal breadwinners and although they have more responsibilities," hno2021. Against this background, the COVID-19 pandemic brought a new set of protection risks and consequences for the affected population while compounding existing vulnerabilities. Movement restrictions resulted in disruptions to ongoing protection assistance, prevented protection actors from expanding their reach, and further confined people to situations of violence and vulnerability increasing their distress and exposure to risks, especially of women and children to violence within the households.Protection risks, especially GBV and CP continue their disproportionate impact on women and children. Grave child rights violations remain a critical concern. Multiple protection risks continue to threaten the ability of people with disabilities to engage in basic activities of daily living in addition to beingidentified by humanitarian actors and supported to access vital services.HNO2021.

5 - Implementation of activities

The project activities will be implemented under full coordination with the working actors and coordination admin offices in the areas to avoid any duplication in the efforts. the addressed needs are reflected by the diverse needs of women, men, children boys and girls under 18, PWD and elders. the project is designed and will be implemented under the DO-NO HARM principle and the humanitarian principles and standards to provide humanitarian assistance in quality, equity and gender equality. the project activities will address avoiding any expose for further risks for all BNFs. the proposed activities are reflected by community participation and accountable by the feedback mechanisms. the working staff will be from women and men taking into account social norms and community acceptance for the activities to be tailored and contextualize further to accepted staff to ensure project expected results. the project staff will be from local communities in the targeted areas to help in improving the livelihood situations in the area. the project activities will be implemented after providing all the capacity building needed to the working staff to have quality and safe services, with ensuring the mainstreaming of PSEA- CSG and all related feedback mechanisms and accountability ways. the project meal Tools will be aligned with standards of all the needed tools which ensure the quality of implemented programs, RW committed to monitor and evaluate the project activities by setting a strong MEAL plan includes the site visit benefeciaries satisfaction surveys and complaint hot line. PSS activities will be monitored to address the development of BNFs wellbeing, from the pre and post-test.

Yes

7.1 - Please list any risks related to sexual exploitation and abuse that have been identified during the project design phase and any risk mitigation and prevention measures that have been adopted and built into the project

The project includes SEA risks that could be taken place within working staff, or due to some working staff with BNFs from women or children or even within the BNFs level. SEA risks could occur during the staff or BNFs journey to the project centre location by some people from the community.RW have set many mitigation strategies to address PSEA, which include: the PSEA mandatory training and related COC-PSEA and CSG, which the staff must align with and obtained, with the clear clarification that the RW have zero tolerance with any SEA accidents. RW has PSEA focal points in the working area and there will be focal points in the project centre for any reporting needed or support in PSEA mainstreaming. RW will do awareness-raising for PSEA which include community level, working staff level and centre level which could include: brochures and banners, videos, awareness raining messages and hotlines. RW will ensure that all the working staff is wellknown about the internal reporting and feedback mechanism taken in place in case of SEA occurrence cases. all the project's service providers or contractures who has direct contact with project beneficiaries from children or women must be trained and signed COC. RW will ensure that during any conducted sessions there will be a female member to ensure beneficiaries safety, especially from children and women. RW will be part In PSEA network for NWs and committed to monthly report to the network to ensure the coordination.
