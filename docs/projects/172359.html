Project Summary

The overall objective of the project is to provide lifesaving, safe and sustainable Water Sanitation and Hygiene (WASH) and Food Security and Livelihoods (FSL) services and facilities to at least 10,000 and 2000 vulnerable South Sudanese Refugees and Host communities, respectively, in El Salaam, El Jabalian and Kosti localities , White Nile State. It is expected that at least 60% of target population will be women and girls.
The selected project localities are rated as high priority areas by both HNO Severity Ranking and CRP 2021 priority ranking. Besides providing WASH and FSL services and facilities, the project will also integrate protection of women and children from violence and abuse, thereby strengthening capacities of women and children to better benefit from the services and facilities that will be delivered by the project.

1 - Needs

WASH: Widespread open defecation due to limited access and availability of user/gender friendly latrines; limited access and availability of household and community hygiene and sanitation facilities; poor household and community hygiene and sanitation practices; high risks of outbreak of poor hygiene/sanitation related communicable diseases; limited access by women and girls to personal hygiene materials. FSL: Limited access to tools, materials and finances for food production and livelihood activities; Food insecurity due to limited access to food/food materials; Against the backdrop of the increase in the cost of living due to high inflation (and COVID-19 Pandemic), many refugees and host communities members have reduced purchasing power undermining their capacity to self-reliance, heightening assistance needs and dependency on food/relief assistance. The inability to cover basic needs is also accompanied by several knock-on effects, including increased tensions with host communities, school drop-out and low enrolment, prevalence of child labour, early marriage and other child protection issues, and sexual and gender-based violence (SGBV) risks.
