Project Summary

Al Twaki Development Organization requests 380,000 USD over 12 month to implement WASH services in Adila and Abokarinka locality in East Darfur state. The Total WASH PIN is 39,264 (8,054 in Abukarinka locality and 31,210 WASH PIN in Adilla locality), the project will target 26,030 in both localities. Women represent 18 % of the target beneficiaries, men 17%, girls, 38% and boys 27%. The project aims enhancing access to WASH Services for vulnerable groups. The project interventions include Distribution of jerry can to 1000 HHs ( 500 in Adilla and 500 in Abukarinka locality), Distribution of 2000 soap to HHs affected vulnerable population in Adilla locality, Construction of 50 communal latrines (in camps and other communal locations of 4-5 stances) , Train 150 community leaders to support construction of latrines to the project target through CLTS/ CATS approach in Adilla locality, Rehabilitation of 8 hand pump in Adilla localities , installation for 4 Hand pump in Abu karinka locality ,Water supply by water trucking in Adilla locality.
The project is designed to address the different gender needs of the affected population including disabilities, particular attention will be made to meet the hygiene and sanitation practice and needs for vulnerable households.

1 - Needs

According to the table of the population figures of the draft HNO 2020 and WASH PIN and target, there is total of 8,054 people in Abu karinka 7,001 PIN in sanitation and all total have need to improve situation hygiene, and Adila total people in need in WASH 31,210 (12,638 IDPs), 13,412 PIN of water also all of them need to sanitation and hygiene status improvement, So who are in need of humanitarian assistance in East Darfur State , The project targeted localities host communities and IDPs a total of 26,030 ( Abu karinka 5,000 Adilla 21,030) The government of Sudan reports that only 41.2% of H/Hs in East Darfur State have access to clean water. The WASH Sector WASH field data reveal a dire need of a range of WASH infrastructure, sanitation and hygiene promotion services in East Darfur State in general and in the localities of Adilla and Abu Karinka in particular. The WASH needs have been further exacerbated by the conflict between AL Maalia tribe and Al Rezigat. The access to clean water is limited in part because water infrastructure and efficiency is compromised, with pumps, wells, and generators in general disrepair due to improper usage and management. This circumstance has exacerbated the general infrastructure shortage, compelling populations to access unsafe water sources and purport parasitic infection. The sanitation and hygiene situation is also inadequate. Moreover, Al Twaki Development Organization (TDO) will provide sanitation facilities to the most vulnerable IDPs and to host community. This is in addition to the hygiene promotion activities for all the target population.The scarcity of water resources causes almost half of the conflict cases between agriculturalist and new comers, a part of being among the reasons limiting most of the returnees and the development processes. Common spaces as schools and markets require thus even more attention for their key roles in the social dynamics. The necessities highlighted ask for increasing the delivery of water points as well as improving quality and Productivity. Improvement of WASH facilities is needed. Most of schools’ children have no access to safe drinking water, no access to latrines where programs for ODF is highly needed to raise their awareness and avoid environmental risk hazards to their life’s together with access to information on hygienic practices and promote hand washing with soap and other hygiene messages. Recent inter-tribal conflicts and rebels’ activities have negative impacts on population. together with resident community so providing water, sanitation and hygiene package contributes as effective tool for peace building and conflict mitigation, since most of people move searching for sacristy water.Shortage of service especially, water, sanitation and hygiene practices among school children as well as community residents tend to increasing the risk of disease outbreak, almost all schools and residential areas are without WASH facilities or nonexistent in the targeted areas ,Host communities are negatively affected by poor WASH facilities due to the neglecting of targeted areas by the most of preceding and existing authorities , also the gab is getting wider by the recent inter-tribal conflicts, that led women and the children to be the most vulnerable groups as exposed in addition to the security risks related to the water fetching. Women and children have to walk more than 90 minutes to reach the water source and have to wait for more than 30 minutes in queue, making them the most vulnerable group.

5.2 - Impact on environment/natural resources

Water is a finite although renewable resource that needs careful management to meet the basic needs of populations while respecting the needs of the environment. > Water and sanitation provision have an impact on the health of the environment, through downstream pollution in particular.Water and sanitation are a focus of Millennium Development Goal 7 on environmental sustainability and are important components in meeting all the MDGs. > Good environmental management is critical to the success of water and sanitation programmes where waste treatment relies heavily on natural processes. Risks to service delivery caused by flooding, erosion, depleted or polluted water resources and soil pollution can be mitigated through sustainable management of the natural environment. Water stress and scarcity are likely to intensify in the future, particularly in developing countries vulnerable to the impacts of climate change. Also, by sanitation activities the project enhances the hygiene status and environment surrounding area and good effects on heath people. the improvement of water situation helps communities to create the Jubraka system (cultivation) in their houses and build good point in climate change mitigation.

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

Darfur suffered from years of conflict, exposing them to protection threats including gender-based violence, targeted attacks, and violations of basic human rights. Freedom of movement, especially in conflict-affected areas such as Darfur, remains restricted. Political instability in 2019 had a particular impact on children and adolescents, who were affected by the closure of schools. Sexual violence particularly affects women and girls in displaced persons’ camps and in rural communities, where they are often preyed upon by armed men, especially when they engage in livelihood activities like collecting water or firewood or working in fields.The large refugee population also continues to face specific protection risks and vulnerabilities while in asylum. Refugees’ basic rights persist, including access to registration, documentation, freedom of movement, basic services and the labour market. Some progress has been made since 2018 on policies related to access to work permits and public education for refugees. However, these policies are applied inconsistently at local levels, and refugees still face discrimination when accessing public service.The project will take into account gender considerations during the implementation and the all project cycles to ensure that all targeted groups (women, men and children) will have equitable access to participate, implement and use the facilities that will be provided and supported by the project funds. Special consideration will be extended to female and child-headed households. The selection criteria will focus and consider gender and age which will be developed and agreed by all partners and the community members and this modality will be used in the selection of the beneficiaries under this project. Al Twaki Development Organization ( TDO ) will reinforce the managerial capacity of the community committees in the IDP and host community, to assist them to take more responsibilities during the project implementation, handing over and running, to ensure and build self-reliance, sense of ownership and future sustainability of the project. The approached for the project successes is ensure sustainability, in which all community will actively engage in the all the project cycles.
