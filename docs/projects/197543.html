Project Summary

COHESODEC response activities with regards to community health related interventions in 2023 will be targeting over 220,000 conflict affected populations in the North-West and South-West Regions of Cameroon. These target communities include the following health districts: Bafut, Bali, Bamenda, Bamenda 3, Tubah, Batibo, Mbengwi, Benakuma, Fundong, Mbengwi, Oku, Santa, Tubah, Wum, Kumba-North and Kumba-South Health Districts!
The project seeks to support 5% of epidemic preparedness and response ensuring kits are prepositioned in high risk areas. Moreover, this project shall be enhancing community engagement and adherence to vaccination in affected areas by ensuring that 150 community health workers are trained and/or supported for epidemic surveillance.
Furthermore, the project shall ensure health management, referrals and counter referrals for 250 GBV survivors while also strengthening the mental health and ensuring psychosocial support interventions through the deployment of 2 mental health specialists to ensure that 2,250 effectively receive mental health care.
In addition, this project shall be ensuring the availability of essential healthcare services in areas affected by the crisis with the deployment of 2 health specialists with surgery capacity. The project will as well be ensuring the availability of essential healthcare in areas affected by the crisis involving 150,000 outpatient consultations for main morbidity and mortality causes in 12 supported health facilities.
This project shall equally be ensuring the availability of reproductive health care in areas affected by the crisis by making sure that at least 15% of births are assisted by skilled attendants, while also making sure that 18% of high risk pregnancies are referred.
Also, this project shall ensure the provision of 1,000 dignity kits to pregnant and lactating women and will as well be providing health assistance in hard to reach areas through 8 mobile clinics while equally ensuring that at least 1,200 people effectively receive health care in mobile clinics.
Finally, the project shall ensure the reporting of at least 20% of all attacks on Healthcare in targeted health districts.
