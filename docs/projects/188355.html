Project Summary

The proposed project aims to increase the resilience of affected communities in NWS through supporting wheat value chain .
875 tons of local wheat seeds will be purchased from local farmers in Alrouj valley during harvesting season in June 2022.
175 tons of these wheat seeds will be cleaned, sterilizes and packed to be distributed with agricultural inputs to 1000 vulnerable farmers in the following communities :
1- Idleb - Harim-Armanaz-Milis
2- Idleb - Harim-Armanaz-Sheikh Yousef
3- Idleb - Idleb-Idleb-Kafruhin
4- Idleb - Idleb-Idleb-Bshmaron
5- Idleb - Idleb-Ma’aret Tmasreen-Murin
Selection criteria will be set based on need and vulnerability with prioritizing women and child headed households ,displaced farmers , families with elderly and sick and persons with special needs . beneficiaries names and vulnerability will be identified through registration and verification procedures.
While 700 tons of the wheat crop will be supplied to mills to produce 525 tons of flour which will be baked in the bakery to produce 603,750 bread bundles.
-603750 quality pitta bread bundles will be distributed to 3230 Displaced families in 40 camps in maaret tamasrin subdistrict, 5 days a week for a period of 5 months.
- technical committee from will be formed to determine the quality of 825 MT of wheat seeds that will be purchased from local farmers.
Each ton of flour will produce 1150 bread bundles (1 kg) distributed based on the following criteria :
HH with 1 - 4 individuals will receive one bread bundle.
HH with 5 -8 individuals will receive two bread bundles.
HHs with 9 and more will receive three bread bundles.

4 - Needs

In October, 2021, (ICO) conducted rapid assessment with local council and KII at 8 villages at A Rouj valley .Main agricultural constraints for crop production was : high dependency on food aid, limited access to agriculture inputs, following some poor farming practices, adverse climatic conditions, high production costs and transportation costs as well as lack of quality inputs resulting in less cultivation of land. Neglecting using sterilized seed, cannot cover their operational cost due to the lack of resources; the high cost for seeds, pesticides; cultivation irrigation and harvesting cost, led to the reduction of the net return income to cover the next planting and cultivation cost. Due to the deteriorating of economic activities, COVID19 epidemic, and labor scarcities, led to most people in the areas of study to depend on agriculture as a main source of income. However, the high cost of production, caused the reduction in the economic return for, to pay off their loan, led to the reduction of the net return income to cover the next planting and cultivation cost. In addition to that most of farmers relies on borrowing to purchase agriculture supplies and wait until harvesting(HNO2021). On the other hand, on April 2021, ICO has conducted need assessment in 20 camps in three communities in Maaret Tamsrin 90% of breadwinner are males while10% are female from whom 88% are widowed.• 94 % of the IDPS stated that the most important needs for them is bread while 5% food basket.

5 - Implementation of activities

prepare the beneficiaries list by surveying families in 8 villages, using the criteria to select beneficiaries, purchasing 875 ton of wheat from local farmers .Receive, and distribute agricultural input to 1000 farmers according to beneficiaries list. Distribute irrigation and harvesting voucher to all selected farmers .Organize frequent field trips to random farmers, giving advice, and documenting progress, at least once a week for each village .Project monitoring and evaluation across the target areas. .Conduct survey for tracking IDP’s in the camps and update the database (baseline survey).Construct the final beneficiaries list . Contracting a mill to grind 700 tons of wheat seeds to make 525 tons of flour and transport it to the contracted bakery. Contract bakery to bake 4.75 tons of flour daily to bake good quality. Distribute 5488 bread bundles daily according to work plan (22 days / month) up to 5 months free of charge. Total 603.750 bread bundles to be distributed. Project monitoring and evaluation . PDM

11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

- Beneficiaries protesting for not receiving agriculture input assistant. Mitigation : ICO team will explain clearly the mechanism for selecting the beneficiary list, also we will rely on local authorities, civil society leaders to explain the criterion for selection and resolve any dispute. - Beneficiaries selling agriculture inputs to benefit from cash Mitigation measures: ICO team and local authorities will explain goals for the project, and before distribution beneficiaries will sign an agreement not to sell.In addition, the distribution of agriculture inputs will be done over a period of time and not lump sum, and regular field visit by M & E team will reduce any irregular selling activity. - Misuse of inputs by beneficiaries exposed crops to diseases and insects Mitigation : IC technical team will visit planted fields periodically to conduct agricultural extension sessions. - The area gets exposed to security or military crisis Mitigation: The area that we will work the project at, is considered secure and no major security issues were encountered. However, we will monitor the situation closely to secure our team and the material planned for distribution. In case of security issues arising, operation site will be changed after consultation with donor and FSL Cluster.- Lack of quality agriculture input and in the market to purchase Mitigation: Import agriculture input from Turkey, or buy quality quarries remains from surrounding areas.
