Project Summary

This project aims at increasing access to education and providing education services of good quality in Damascus and Rural Damascus in addition to supporting WASH and hygiene promotion in the same schools and areas in Rural Damascus (Zabadani,babella kafrbatna,Harasta,ghizlanie, einelfigeh, Madaya, Serghaya)
Targeted locations are of severity scale 3, 4 and 5. % of targeted people according to the education severity scale is: 60% for severity 3, 30% for 4, and 10% for 5,As for WASH, severity scale is considered according to the Final Severity Score, the % is: 65% for severity 4, 35% for 3
project will directly contribute to the Education Sector’s HRP objectives 1 (Availability and access) and 2 (Quality), and it will target children (IDPs,host community & returnees) with different kinds of activities in addition to targeting parents, and teachers. Similarly, the project will equally contribute to the WASH Sector SO4 (Improve WASH facilities and services in institutions to minimize sub-standard WASH conditions of the most vulnerable people in Syria) and Activity 4.1. (Support to WASH facilities and services in schools and child friendly spaces in close coordination with Education and Protection sectors). Activities are mentioned in detail with targets in a separate part
Targeted locations are determined according to Tamayoz work and knowledge in those areas where it has completed implementation of some education activities under SHF, and is willing to continue providing services to maintain the sustainability and to expand in new locations where there are no enough schools to encourage re-enroll in Formal education.Tamayoz has access and needed approvals to targeted locations where the need for education and WASH services is so high (as per Tamayoz assessment of wash facilities in some schools in targeted locations in consultation with DoH)
The project will address the beneficiaries education needs according to FGDs made by Tamayoz targeted men, women, boys, and girls and according to the methodology that will include the provision of non-formal education for vulnerable children (boys and girls) from different communities (IDPs, returnees and host community) and by providing a comprehensive package of services: ECE, BTL, TVET and life-skills, NFE, awareness raising related to mine action, COVID-19, AWD / Cholera risk and other hygiene and health issues, support teacher training, provision of PSS, support schools with furniture and solar cells, school feeding, support the most vulnerable students with cash assistance according to specific criteria, support other vulnerable students with winter clothes, establishing PTAs…and will include rehabilitation of wash facilities in schools
Education activities be implemented in one non-formal setting in Damascus and 10 locations in Rural Damascus, while WASH activities will be implemented in the same schools and areas in 8 locations that are under wash severity scale 3and4. Schools selection will be in coordination with DoE. In addition, Hygiene promotion will be implemented in the same schools and non-formal settings in Rural Damascus, and will more focus on women, girls, women headed households and people with disability. NFI hygiene kits will be distributed according to specific criteria.
Activities are planned for the year 2023 to achieve a distinguished impact on children’s education status especially those OOS.
The project will target students (boys and girls) of all ages from 3 to 24 and will support PWD of different ages by engaging them in project cycle (planning, implementation, and monitoring), providing suitable and easy access, deploying trained staff. this project is linked to another protection project where referrals of students with protection risks to case management, It is also linked with livelihood activities. Activities will consider Cholera preventive interventions. the estimate % of budget already secured for Tamayoz planned 2023 WASH Activities is about 5%

3 - Needs

As per Draft 2023 HNO Education chapter: Significantly high percentage (66%) of children do not want to go to school as they find schools are not useful or learning taking place sufficiently, followed by no time due to work/tired (45%) and/or depressed/unmotivated (44%).Improving school condition stays as the highest priority of the family in improving the education services (54%), which is followed by improving the teacher’s ability to teach (44%) and providing cash to support education expenses (38%)In regards to Wash facilities in schools: 56% children going to schools are dissatisfied with the WASH in schools. Top reasons of dissatisfaction: uncleanliness (87%), non-availability of soaps to wash hands (51%), no enough facilities (29%) and toilets not being safe (27%).As per Draft HNO 2023- WASH sector: Access to WASH services is compromised by prolonged decay of the existing systems, caused by damage over years of conflict, limited or no maintenance, continuous drain of technical staff. This is exacerbated by the cascade effect of climate change, economic downturn and imposed coercive measures, putting 13.55 million people in need of WASH assistance. As perTamayoz presence in RD, an increase of OOS rate was observed, this is due to difficult economic situation, lack of needed food, and money, and some kind of disabilities, consequently, a high rate of child labour was also reported. many children work in collecting metal from debris making them at high risk of mine.

4 - Implementation of activities

• The project will be directly implemented by Tamayoz and will be in line with HRP framework and will contribute to HRP objectives1and2 and in line with sectors Response strategies and priorities. It will assure more education Investments through repairing gender sensitive WASH facilities, adequate and provision of solar panels.it will also support distribution of teaching and learning materials, provision of transportation support and school feeding.NFE activities will support bridging the gap of education in locations where there is no or inadequate schools. Students will benefit from a package of services mentioned previously. Wash activities will be implemented in the same schools supported by education activities. A good database and reporting system will be in place, in addition to regular meetings with staff to evaluate the achieved results towards targets. Tamayoz will deploy well-qualified teachers and PSS providers, and will train a group on how to conduct awareness raising regarding hygiene promotion. NFIs hygiene kits distribution objective is to upgrade hygiene and prevent the spread of Cholera and other CD, it will be targeted to the most in need according to clear criteria that includes prioritizing Women headed HHs, families with elderly, PWD. Kits will contain materials that would support sustainable use like water gallon, cleaning tools. Teacher will be provided with capacity building. Monitoring: feedback mechanisms, field visits, pre-post assessment

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk: Inequality in distribution NFI hygiene kits: Mitigation measures: Promote involvement /engagement with communities in distribution planning and feedback from different groups on package for improvementRisk: Timing and process of NFIs distribution put women and girls at higher risk of GBV. Mitigation measures: Distribution will by during day hours, training staff and enforcement of PSEA code of conduct Risk: Gender insensitive contents varieties, MM: kits will be as a standardized gender friendly hygiene kits.Risk: Sale or exchange of NFI kits at lesser value, MM: consult comunity to determine the reasoning behind this and provide solutionsRisk: Tensions within the community due to feeling of being unfairly treated, insufficiently consulted. MM: Teachers and school personnel will be trained on prevention and reporting of, violence, physical punishment and bullying, and the use of positive disciplineRisk: Teachers or other education personnel participate in physical punishment, act violently, bully and neglect students. Students participate in bullying towards other and/or to teachers. Mitigation M: AlTamayoz will set regulations for all staff and teachers that prevent all forms of violence, Children will be closely supervised to prevent any kind of bullying. Risk: Girls are at risk of GBV, MM: locations of Education facilities and routes to them away from actual or potential threats such as violence; especially the risk or threat of gender based violence


8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Risk: Exploitation or abuse perpetrated by service providers when delivering servicesLikelihood: low. impact: mediumMitigating measure: Establishing complaint and feedback mechanism, Providing staff with training on PSEA, Ensuring proper communication with the communities about the transparency in provision of services.Ensuring proper communication with the communities about transparency in the provision of services.AlTamayoz is classified by UN agencies as a "low risk' partner for PSEA.All staff members are trained on PSEA, there is a strong policy in place, a referral system is also in place. PSEA strategy includes several points: - A code of conduct is in place that all employees and volunteers sign - PSEA policy is approved by the board of directors and it is found in the shared file on the association’s local net that is viewed by all employees.- Al staff are trained on PSEA aspect- PSEA focal point is dedicated to following up on PSEA issues.- PEA related awareness-raising sessions are conducted for beneficiaries and staff periodically

Risk: lack of well qualified staff in the area of implementation due to the lack of education opportunities during the crisis years /Likelihood: high. impact: low Mitigating measure:capacity building for people from the implementation area, and deployment of part of thestaff from Damascus Risk:lack of electricity, water and fuel in the Rural areas that might affect the implementation efficiency Likelihood: high. impact: high Mitigating measure:support centers with generator, support centers with tank for water Risk of staff refusal to work in far areas from their homes Likelihood: high. impact: high Mitigating measure:support transportation for staff.Risk: Delays in obtaining the necessary approvals to implement activities and access project areas in a timely mannerLikelihood: Low. impact: mediumMitigating measure: maintain strong communication with all levels of stakeholders to follow-up on necessary approvalsRisk: Financial risks related to increases in materials prices especially for hygiene kits: Likelihood: high. impact: high. It will be mitigated by considering the increase in budget preparationRisk: COVID-19 related restrictions and lockdowns Likelihood: High. impact: high.Mitigating measure: Applying alternative working arrangements and delivery modalities, including online and remote arrangements to ensure continuity of services and communication with people in need, applying social distancing, providing centers with hygiene materials
