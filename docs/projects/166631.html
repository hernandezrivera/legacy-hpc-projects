Project Summary

• Provide a comprehensive understanding of the effect of COVID-19 on mobility at cross-border/inter-regional level through Displacement Tracking Matrix (DTM) as well as in corridors of displacements based on current interventions and capacities on the ground and strenghten muslisector needs assesmsent in areas at risk and of displacement
• Provide a real-time snapshot of mobility restrictions, including the varied and complex forms of mobility restrictions being enacted at location level, through Mobility Restriction Mapping.
• Track information on Stranded Migrants, including numbers of migrants in need, locations where migrants are stranded, and countries of origin, through the IOM Flow Monitoring Portal, which will act as a central repository and dissemination channel for flow monitoring, mobility tracking, border management, movement and other reports, maps and outputs in relation in COVID-19 produced at country, regional and global level.
• Develop a dedicated population mobility portal for COVID-19 preparedness and response.
• Strengthen remote data collection, making use of global network of DTM key informants.
• Establish a network of technical DTM staff, such as GIS, database development, and other areas, including with external partners and technical experts.
• Rolling-out DTM Flow Monitoring tool at some of the Points of Entry (PoEs) with highest movements dynamic in collaboration with the ministry of health and relevant authorities in order to get an average of crossing in both directions (incoming and outgoing) per day as well as origin and destination .
