Project Summary

This project will support the delivery of preventative and curative health care for Vulnerable 3,832 boys and 4,550 girls, 2,674 women and 1,944 males in fangak and Duk during 2019. These will be supported through fixed health facilities, and outreach models. This intervention is based on the existing Hold the Child presence Duk (Health interventions) and Fangak (Nutrition interventions), Consistence coordination through the cluster and local authorities

Needs

The protracted conflict in South Sudan has continued to worsen humanitarian situation in many parts of the country including those that were relatively stable in the initial stages of the conflict. Fangak is one of Jonglei State's counties made of 3 parts. Old Fangak combines both Central and Southern parts is home to over 178,906 people and over 26,200 IDPs. (According to September 2018 report in Old Fangak shared by NBS and OCHA). OCHA projects heavy influx of returnees to Fangak in 2019 from PoCs of Juba, Bor, and Bentiu posing more stress on optimal Health care system available which barely over stretched.The recent IPC analysis (Sept 2018) indicate worrying classification phase with IPC phase 4 (emergency) for Fangak, IPC 3 (Critical) for Duk and likely to worsen if no humanitarian support. ACF-USA’s most recent SMART survey in May 2018 found that; In Duk County, the (GAM) is at 26.1% (22.2-30.5 95% CI) and SAM at 5.5% (3.7- 8.1 95% CI) based on Weight-for-Height. The crude death rate is at 1.18 (0.73-1.90) and under 5 death rate was at 0.96 (0.38-2.38) with the most common causes of death were unknown (32.4%); violence/conflict (29.4%) and Illness (26.5%); therefore, the GAM rate was critical above the WHO emergency threshold. In 2017 November and November 2018, there were similar attack in Duk Payuel resulting into Death and causing population movements with approximately 20 people injured. IDSR Week 28 report indicate suspected case of Meningitis in Ayueldit payam, and week 20 indicated Hepatitis E outbreak in Fangak. Duk and Fangak are more prone to cholera outbreaks with reported flooding. Increased cases of Malaria, Pneumonia, Acute Watery Diarhorea, due to low utilization of basic health care services on maternal and neonatal essential reproductive services. During Immunization campaigns, Coverage is low due to vandalized health facilities, poor roads especially during the rainy season, thus affecting coverage on EPI and full immunization schedules for children below U5 years of age. The only available health facilities are under equipped. Hold the Child is working along with CHD through South Sudan Human Fund and JDF managing Nutrition sector. CHD manage the only referral PHCC in the area Duk Padiet. HCO and CHD supporting the inpatient care as well as conducting referrals for far health facilities of Dorok PHCU, Ayueldit PHCU, conducting Mobile Outreach to Dongchak and Patuenoi. Most of the health facilities, Primary Healthcare Units, lack the capacity to provide the much needed healthcare magnitude looking at the increasing needs in the area due to the recent crises and risk associated with flooding i.e. Cholera; Economic straps due to broken supply chains; and threats of IDP influx from Ayod and Uror of Jiech state. Thus the need to emergency health interventions in the area is to activate the dormant facilities, and cultivate prevention, early detection and treatment.The Duk County is one of the hardest to reach areas of the country, because of poor road conditions and insecurity due to cattle raiding and its proximity to active fighting. Hence majority (41,711) persons of which 8,342 are U5 years of age (males 4,004 and females 4,338) has been displaced (Ayod,Uror and Nyirol) to Pajut in Panyang as well as other payams and the resulting displacement has destabilized the normal cultivation practices resulting to high prevalence of malnutrition due seasonal changes to food security, violence, poor WASH services and disease burden. HCO Sept 2018 Duk reports had 43% females consultations at OPD sites in Duk.

Activities

1- Provide essential primary Health care and emergency clinical package (PHCU, Mobile teams and PHCC) targeting the most vulnerable through static and mobile/outreach models based on contextual demands in our target communities.2- Support immunization for the most vulnerable displaced group of children under 5 (boys and girls), through routine and outreach models working in collaboration with CHDs and other existing development health providers; including training on EPI and Vaccine handling.3- Mainstream Accountability to Affected People throughout the project interventions including working with the exiting Boma health initiatives, CHD among other key stakeholders. 4- Support Integrated Response for Epidemic prone communicable diseases including Diarrheal, Viral, Vector, Haemorrhagic diseases, with Wash/ Nut/FSL.5- Procure 20% of emergency health pipeline.

Accountability to affected population
Does the project provide beneficiaries with information?

Yes

How does the project provide beneficiaries with information?

Through avenues including; project inception sessions with local leaders and stakeholders (including male and females), Key messaging IEC materials, awareness sessions during outreaches, community workers/mobilisers and local gatherings like churches, and health education sessions.

Are messages context-specific or/and tailored?

Yes

Briefly describe how

Messages provided seek to (1) Paint a clear understanding of the project scope and service to set the right expectations in cognizance of the local contextual dynamics, (2) Convey key messages on behavioural changes on hygiene & sanitation, child feeding, immunisation, ANC etc. This therefore makes our messages contextual with tailored cluster generic phrases.

Protection from sexual exploitation and abuse

Yes

Briefly describe how

Our rights based programming recognises Sexual exploitation and abuse as critical issue across the country. In conformity with our relevant policy, this project will integrate recommended measures to Provide optimal awareness about this evil in our workstations and supported health facilities, Ensure compliance to PSEA reporting channels while providing referrals for victims.

Nature of the consultations

Yes

Briefly describe the nature of the consultations

This project is designed with inputs from prior consultations through the ongoing project activities. Such a spirit will be maintained into the new project cycle 2019, and will include measures like: Consultations during project inception sessions, Courtesy calls to local authorities and County health departments and State Ministry of Health offices, Focus group discussion with beneficiaries (including male and females), Donor agencies interactions, Cluster coordination and technical working group session, and Hold the Child’s internal consultation and reflection sessions.

Feedback /complaint mechanisms?

Yes

Briefly describe the mechanisms

Like our ongoing projects, this project will integrate feedback and complaint mechanisms including: suggestion box (at the bases), one on one, focus group discussions (including male and females) among projects sites and catchments.

Community Engagement?

Yes

Briefly describe how

Through the use of community-based structures and the Boma health committee members where they exist; the targeted communities (women, children, and other vulnerable and marginalised populations) will be engaged in assessments, planning, implementation, monitoring and evaluation of project activities

Centrality of Protection in humanitarian action
Protection Risk Analysis?

Yes

Briefly describe how

The project design is based identified needs among target populations attributed to the national crisis, and lifesaving interventions designed to meet the immediate needs of the people.

Barriers to Access?

Yes

Briefly describe how

This project will seek to operationalise health care facilities that are closed or otherwise be closed due to lack of qualified staff, supplies and equipment; through fixed or outreach sites/posts. This action will extend access opportunities to such vulnerable populations that would otherwise be left out. Furthermore the project will advocate for support to rapid responses to fresh IDPs that would otherwise be cut off in hard to reach areas. All health care services will be free to all people in the catchment areas supported under this project.

Addresses inequalities or protection threats?

Yes

Briefly describe how

The project design will address inequalities in limited ways with particular focus on equitable access to health care services for male, females, children, elderly, and disabled within the catchment areas regardless of there ethnicity, and creed. Other measures will include dedicated efforts to include these through the different project aspects from inception to evaluation.

Project activities address inequalities or protection threats?

Yes

Briefly describe how

All project interventions are set to provide health care services for all as hinted above, and thus in ways contribute to addressing inequalities among the targeted communities.

Does the project monitoring measure access of beneficiaries?

Yes

How does the project monitoring measure access of beneficiaries?

Project interventions are to provide critical services to address the identified needs for vulnerable populations among the target areas. Operations will involve beneficiaries taking services from supported Health facilities and data captured in based on these interactions. Project data will capture beneficiaries accessing a Health facility from a given catchment, and reports will seek to analysis the extents of reach Vs targeted beneficiaries.

Does the project monitoring measure safety of beneficiaries?

No

Does the project monitoring measure dignity of beneficiaries?

Yes

How the project monitoring measure dignity of beneficiaries?

Our rights based programming recognises the dignity of beneficiaries to be the essence of our intervention among this project targeted areas. And to this end, the project design carries integrated accountability measure to affected populations, to identify, understand and support their aspirations during the project cycle. The project focus groups and some consultations sessions will deliberately engage different social grouping to provide meaningful engagements on matters that within the project scope that have different impact on their well-being; to inform project design refinement, referrals and future programming.
