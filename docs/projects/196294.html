3 - Needs

Through the monitoring data accompanying the mobile medical teams that provide their services in the governorate of Hama (Al-Ghab Plain - the countryside extending between the cities of Mhardeh and Al-Salqibia) along the Idlib governorate and in the Lattakia governorate (the northern countryside along the international highway Aleppo-Latakia) in addition to the monitoring work that was conducted in schools The sites that were mentioned above within the memorandum of understanding concluded between the association and the Ministry of Education have observed cases of moderate to severe stunting and emaciation, cases of weight loss and a deficiency in micronutrients among children aged (6-13) years, especially in areas of population returnCases of malnutrition were also monitored for pregnant and lactating women due to the deteriorating economic situation and misconceptions about a lot of foods. Through monitoring, it was found that the rate of acute malnutrition among children in the age group from 6 to 59 months ranges between 10-17%, and 90% of the population suffers. Areas of poor food consumption due to the loss of agricultural lands located within the armed conflict areas along Jabal Al-Zawiya and Jabal Al-Akrad and because of forest fires

4 - Implementation of activities

The needs were monitored through field visits, through focused discussion sessions and various activities within schools, in addition to monthly reports from mobile medical teams, health centers, mobile psychological support teams, and community centers affiliated with the association, which are spread in the countryside of Hama and Lattakia governorates, and through exchanging information with the ministries of health and education within a memorandum Understanding between the association and the two ministriesServices will include:------ Awareness activities:Dialogue sessions With the residents of the local community on topics ( breastfeeding, nutrition for pregnant and lactating women)• workshopsWith mothers about (IYCF, .. )• Educational brochures for pregnant and lactating women• Health Care services for pregnants, children and breastfeeding mothers.activities:• Providing micronutrient supplements for pregnant and lactating women.• Providing micronutrient supplements for children (6- 59 months)• Survey campaigns to monitor cases of malnutrition (survey and referral to the concerned authorities) and to activate the measurement of social well-being among pregnant and breastfeeding women.• Nutritional support for malnourished children and malnourished mothers (pregnant and nursing mothers).
