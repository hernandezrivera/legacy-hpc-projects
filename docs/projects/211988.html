Project Summary

In the midst of dire humanitarian conditions in northwestern Syria, the lack of adequate shelter remains a significant issue due to insufficient support and ongoing conflicts, exacerbated by the earthquake in 2023. The region continues to be ravaged by these crises, leaving many vulnerable individuals and families without proper shelter or protection. In 2023, the inadequacy of shelter has increased by 12% in Idleb and 32% in Aleppo governorates, according to MSNA data.
Primary data collected by CARE in November 2023 in 26 sub-districts in Aleppo and Idleb governorates revealed that 24% of people in communities are in need of high/moderate rehabilitation and 73% are in need of light rehabilitation. In IDP sites, 46% of IDP households reported that their shelter required major rehabilitation, while 44% reported a need for light rehabilitation.48% of the access roads were reported as in need of improvement. 11% of respondents indicated that the access roads were so poor that trucks and lifesaving activities were unable to reach the sites. 60% of the drainage systems were rated as bad or very bad, and 57% reported experiencing flood conditions at their site in the past year. Regarding NFIs, the top items identified as urgently required by IDPs are fuel for heating and cooking (96%), winter clothing (80%), blankets (68%), stoves (64%), kitchen utensils, shoes, and hygiene items. 42% of respondents at IDP sites said there were no available services for women and girls.
CARE is planning to reach 40,100 HHs (200,500 individuals) living in 23 sub-districts ranked as high severity scale (3,4)by Shelter cluster in Aleppo and Idleb governorates. CARE plan will include
1- Providing emergency shelter assistance during the rapid responses and natural crisis. This will include;
• Distributing NFIs (5,000 core NFIs, and 30,000 seasonal winter assistance)
• Tents rehabilitation including tents insulation and distributing plastic sheets for 500 tents and installation of 100 tents for the newly displaced people.
• Rehabilitation of collective centers (100 HHs).
• Implementation of site level improvements/ infrastructure for the affected camps and associated with dignified shelter
2- Support transitional shelter interventions by installing dignified shelter. This will include;
• Repairing 2,500 households damaged or unfinished houses in communities to improve shelter conditions and privacy through cash and contractor modalities.
• Construct 2,000 dignified shelter associated with infrastructure
• providing 2,000 solar energy systems at HHs levels.
CARE will ensure protection mainstreaming during the delivery and planning of the shelter intervention through the following actions:
• Conducting community engagement sessions with the target beneficiaries to introduce a complaints system.
Additionally, consultations will be held with communities, particularly females, elderly individuals, and Persons with Disabilities (PwDs), to address their specific needs regarding the modality of the intervention.
• Training the field teams on Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) and protection mainstreaming.
• Ensuring the safety of the targeted beneficiaries, primarily females, during cash intervention modalities by assessing potential risks for females.
• Organizing distribution points with separate queues for the elderly, females, and PwDs, and implementing organized turns.
• Ensuring safe access to shelter facilities for vulnerable groups, mainly females, elderly individuals, and PwDs, during shelter rehabilitation activities. CARE will address their needs based on target groups, including chair toilets, ramps, handles for children, safety equipment like parapets and railings.
• For women's needs, CARE will ensure consultation regarding kitchenette placement and WASH (Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene) facilities.
• Implementing gender separation for women by building separating walls in rooms to ensure privacy for female members of the

0 - Project Summary (extended)

CARE aims to enhance shelter and living conditions for 200,500 individuals (86,215 male, 114,285 female) residing in 23 districts across Aleppo and Idleb governorates. With focusing on mitigating protection, safety, and Gender-Based Violence (GBV) risks by providing safe and dignified shelter and settlements.To address emergency influxes or natural crises during internal movements, CARE will;• Distributing NFIs.5,000 in-kind emergency Non-Food Item (NFI) kits, targeting 5,000 newly displaced households and vulnerable IDPs in informal camps and communal facilities. Additionally, CARE will provide winter assistance to 30,000 households, focusing on the most vulnerable IDPs and host communities (HCs), particularly elderly, female-headed, and child-headed households. Each household will receive $180 USD in cash, sufficient for purchasing essential winterization items, with delivery through cash-based modalities.• Tents rehabilitation including tents insulation and distributing plastic sheets for 500 tents and installation of 100 tents for the newly displaced people.• Rehabilitation of collective centers (100 HHs). • Implementation of site level improvements/ infrastructure for the affected camps and associated with dignified shelter CARE will also strengthen the protection environment by;• Repairing 2,500 households damaged or unfinished houses in communities to improve shelter conditions and privacy through cash and contractor modalities.• Construct 2,000 dignified shelter associated with infrastructure• providing 2,000 solar energy systems at HHs levels.
