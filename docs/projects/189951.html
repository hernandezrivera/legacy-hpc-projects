Project Summary

FHI 360’s response to the emergency in Cabo Delgado through integrated approach (WASH, Health and Protection) in Ancuabe and Metuge district. FHI 360’s goal for the proposed program is to improve the wellbeing of conflict-affected and displaced populations through an integrated, multi-sectoral response. FHI 360 will collaborate 4 essentials elements of protection principles into its operational and all program cycle management. All IRAMP staffs including volunteers in the target area will get the protection mainstreaming training and produce the action plan of protection mainstreaming. There are huge need and gap of MHPSS amongst effected population due to the crisis in Cabo Delgado. FHI 360 therefore embedded MHPSS activities into our activities. Staffs will be equipped with psychosocial support (PSS) and psychological first aid (PFA) skills. Affected population in the FHI 360 target area will be benefited with PSS and PFA services and safe ad ethical referral services to specialized MHPSS.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

FHI 360 conducts situation and context analysis and risk assessment to develop risk mitigation measures. FHI 360 is held accountable for integrating key actions to reduce risks and incorporate 4 essential protection principle elements through out the project cycle management. Prioritize safety, dignity, and avoid causing harm: Protection activities will seek to prevent unintended negative effects of all action such as exposure of the survivors to further forms of harm by maintaining confidentiality. Meaningful Access: As part of protection intervention, FHI 360 will identify affected population with accessibility issues and address issues appropriately to their need and diversity. FHI 360 will arrange for their access to assistance and services. Training will be conducted for staff from all sectors to ensure internal referral is strengthened, which will allow the non-protection sectors to safely refer all program participant including those with accessibility issues to access services. FHI 360 promote program participant’s level of their evolving capacity of participation and empowerment. All actions of our project are based on the result of program participant's feedback. FHI 360 respects the needs and wishes of program participants. We build friendly environment for program participants to share, lead and thrive.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

FHI 360 uphold equity, diversity and inclusiveness. FHI 360 will use inclusive designs to improve access to services for the disabled. FHI 360 will work with the staff at each facility to accommodate physically disabled persons. Each facility will develop simple protocols to physically assist program participants into the facility if necessary and provide care without requiring them to move about the facility. Adequate spaces are provided for people with special needs. IASC Guidelines on the Inclusion of Persons with Disabilities in Humanitarian Action, will be used to assess the specific needs disabled person, so that they receive the necessary support. FHI 360 will collaborated with available district Disabled Persons Organization (DPO) to link with DPO representatives at the field sites who will directly support protection activities by interpreting sign language and assisting the physically challenged and ensuring that their voices are heard, and their needs are integrated into activities.
