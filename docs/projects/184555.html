Project Summary

According Somalia Humanitarian needs overview up to 66 per cent of the population in need of protection services in Somalia are children who continue to be exposed to protection threats of recruitment, family separation, abuse, neglect, abduction, exploitation, and violence.2.3 million children, including 10 per cent of children with disabilities, are in need of immediate child protection services. Compared to the 2021 analysis, the estimated number of people in need increased by 25% in 2022. According to the JMCNA 2021 it is estimated that 607,546 children are in extreme need (26%), 1,449,144 in severe need (61%) and 302,440 in stress (13%), and require humanitarian child protection assistance. Somalia is among the countries recording the highest number of grave violations against children in the world. Abduction, sexual violence, and recruitment and use of children are particularly alarming. Accordingly, children with disabilities are among the most vulnerable, marginalized groups within Somali society because of the attitudinal, environmental, and institutional barriers they face. A recent rapid assessment found 62% of caregivers reporting that communities view children with disabilities as not being able to contribute to the family; 33.7% reported communities view children with disabilities as signs of bad luck, with community members believing that children with disabilities bring drought and poverty to both the family and entire community. Additionally, 28% of caregivers stated they must chain children with disabilities to protect them from harm, such as accidents, discrimination, physical and sexual abuse.The proposed child protection interventions will address the most urgent and critical protection issues in Galgadud region focusing primarily on the internally displaced population as well as host communities. PDA will ensure to intervene on timely, effective and holistic manner to the protection needs of mainly children of the displaced communities in the target regions. The project will address both child right violations through existing inter-agency referral mechanism. PDA currently is implementing food security intervention in these areas; the projection project will be integrated in the existing livelihood projects so that it strengthens both protection and livelihood service provision. PDA has strong and solid community based protection prevention and response programming and this project will fit into that modality of programming. The project will significantly strengthen and significantly scale up the on-going case management response services in targeted communities. Specifically, the project will response to by identifying and responding the needs of separated and unaccompanied children including disable group (UASC), The project will invest in building the capacity of the both protection on provision of psychological first aid to the highly psychological distress community members as part of the first line emergency response; UASC will be provided interim community based care arrangement and foster care. The project aims to provide immediate life-saving assistance to vulnerable populations. Beneficiaries will receive comprehensive case management and psycho-social support (PSS), including emergency care, basic emotional support, and follow up on the services. Capacity building of duty bearers including formal and informal authorities while also taking disable group into consideration to strengthen and improve identification. Mainstreaming child protection to other service providers to ensure compassionate, confidential and competent action for children in emergencies
PDA through this project will ensure to provide adequate supervision and improve the program quality and control, in which PDA will ensure capacity enhancement among its staff on prevention on sexual exploitation and abuse (PSEA)

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

In the refugee camps the following might be some of the main risk facing the target population that may negatively impact the benefices in the camps these include; poor IDPs settlement plan, high cases of GBV, poor data protection, extremist among the youth, as well as military fire, explosion remnant of war and lack or rather limited information sharing and feedback of compliant mechanism in place to ensure safety and dignity of the vulnerable women , girls, boys and men in the violence areas. This is coupled with separation and sexual exploitation as well as undocumented needs of the vulnerable group. PDA will increase social mobilization and awareness aspect within the camps as well as train the community gatekeepers on the proper reporting of these verse hence improve coordination between the community leaders, staff and the local authority for prosecution and cultural solution for the such cases within the camps. However, in the IDPs set up there are extremist violence youth who terrorize vulnerable people hence create community tension as well as causing disharmony among the affected population this will however, be handle through youth awareness creation on their safety and dignity to shun away from engaging from such violence and as well focus their future PDA will accelerate the community active engagement with youth in the IDPs and as well create a better avenue for them to get empowered. To mitigate this PDA, intends to carry out properly IDPs leader’s consultation and involvement of all stakeholders in ensuring the IDPs layout is of one that will ensure easy access to all beneficiary services within the camps, PDA will have trained community leaders in the IDPs on simple surveying of the camps as well as management details of these IDPs hence improve the public relation on the target location. To prevent the rampant and attended sexual exploitation among the vulnerable girls and women in the target communitiesIn terms of services distances on location PDA will ensure that the beneficiaries receive the proposed intervention on timely and within their geographical area with no any challenges in terms of access hence this will ensure better protection of the beneficiaries
