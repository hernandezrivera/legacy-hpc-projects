Project Summary

The Food and Basic Assistance intervention will contribute to saving lives, reducing suffering, and increasing the dignity of crisis-affected Lebanese in Mount of Lebanon by improving food security and resilience through providing food and non-food vouchers for 250 Lebanese families between September 2021 and August 2022, this will contribute as well in the circular economy. Nusaned will be collaborating with Saint-Vincent de Paul in Mount of Lebanon. The project will target communities in underserved areas whose vulnerabilities have been heightened by the pandemic and the far-reaching impact of the worsening socio-economic situation, in particular 250 Lebanese households and individuals allocated in Mount of Lebanon, taking into consideration gender balance and fair distribution. This will be implemented by conducting needs assistance in mentioned areas with the support of our volunteers and our partner. Capacity building and awareness for our volunteers is an integral part of the proposal, in order to ensure a stronger local NGO landscape to respond to the needs.
In response to the worsening socio-economic situation, Food and Basic Assistance is designed to address critical needs with Vouchers' distribution for food and non-food items in most underserved areas. The approach supports food security and basic assistance objectives, while allowing beneficiaries to afford their basic needs using Vouchers in their local food and non-food markets, contributing to economic recovery. Nusaned and its Partner have strong analytical capacities for vulnerability, needs and market assessment and a strong track record of past and existing similar programs. The vouchers assistance will provide vulnerable households coping with a socio-economic shock along with referrals to services that will provide vulnerable households and individuals access to an assortment of food and essential non-food items. The selected Lebanese beneficiaries will be provided with VA in the value of LBP 1.2 million per month for each household of 4 (i.e. 1000 individuals), equivalent to $68 for food assistance and 400,000 LBP for each household for basic assistance, equivalent to $23/month. Each family will receive VA over 10 months.
The VA is designed to provide a protection buffer following a socio-economic shock and, considering that households are impacted differently, allows beneficiaries to make their own choices to cover their most urgent and priority needs while contributing to recovery, decreasing negative stress and crisis related coping strategies. The assistance also directly supports local markets as a multiplier effect through increasing circulation of cash in local shops and its non-restrictive nature also contributes to other positive outcomes related to sanitation and hygene. Adopting a risk reduction model, Nusaned will ensure that those in need are referred to specialized services depending on their specific situation. The duration of 10 months allows households to recover from the shock and find alternative solutions such as employment or specific social services.
This project will be conducted with the support of 15 Nusaned volunteers to conduct needs assessment and support in the implementation of the project.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

Nusaned has developed protection and prevention mechanisms in its “Safeguarding and Protection of children and women (from Sexual Exploitation, Abuse and sexual Harassment, Forced Labor and Trafficking)”. Additionally, Nusaned will be:● Ensuring gender sensitive and gender responsive working environment, based on the human’s rights and fundamental freedoms and free of intimidation, hostility, offence and any form of harassment and abuse of authority.● Encouraging managers and supervisors to act as role models by upholding only the highest standards of conduct.● Familiarization of all those engaged with NUSANED activities under various collaboration arrangements and involvement with the code of conduct, particularly with provisions pertaining to workplace harassment.● Implementation of training and awareness sessions to empower those engaged with NUSANED particularly when involving vulnerable populations.● Identifying risk factors on an on an ongoing basis

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

The key components of accountability to affected people are intrinsically linked and build on each other and they are applicable in all Nusaned operations:- Participation and inclusion.Women, men, boys and girls of diverse backgrounds are able to engage meaningfully and are consulted on protection, assistance, and solutions.- Communication and transparency.Women, men, boys and girls of diverse backgrounds in all operations have access to timely, accurate, and relevant information on (i) their rights and entitlements, and (ii) the programs of Nusaned and its partners.- Feedback and response.Formal and informal feedback from persons of concern is systematically received and responded to, and corrective action taken when appropriate.- Organizational learning and adaptation.Interventions, planning, priority setting, course corrections, and evaluation are informed on an ongoing basis by the views of persons of concern.
