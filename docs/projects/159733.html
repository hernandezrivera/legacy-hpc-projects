Project Summary

The Deyr rains (September-December) started in September in some parts of Somalia and moderate to heavy rains have continued in many parts of the country and within the Ethiopian highlands over the last three weeks, according to the FAO-Managed Somalia Water and Land Information Management (SWALIM). As a result, in Balcad and Jowhar riverine areas, the Shabelle river levels have surpassed the high risk level of flooding and are just a few centimeters to bank full capacity. In response, some residents have already begun relocating to higher ground. In Jowhar, river levels are near bank full, while two major breakages at Maandheere and Dhamasame (Jowhar) have reportedly resulted in flooding. Reports from neighboring areas in Ethiopia indicate overbank flows from the Shabelle with the flood water expected to reach Somalia in a day or two, thus increasing the risk of flooding in the region. Moderate to heavy rains are projected in the Ethiopian highlands that feed the river this week, and the Shabelle River is expected to rise further, leading to overbank spillage.
In response to the increasing humanitarian needs in the Balcad & Jowhar occasioned by the different increase in flood, drought & conflict-induced internally displaced populations (IDPs) & Host communities, since early 2019; the proposed project intends the improving living conditions for prolonged displaced populations in their current locations, NFI needs of 5,500 HH both IDPs and Host communities (male: 2900, female 2600) in coordination with the Tawakal, Towfiq, Jilyale & Bardhere IDPs in Jowhar district and Host communities task force in Balcad (Gololey, Qalimow, Elka-Gelow & Mukay dheere).
Since ORDO has already conducted beneficiary assessments in target locations, prior to project
implementation, a rapid corroboration and vulnerability exercise shall be undertaken to review beneficiary. Female headed households, children headed-households or households with elderly, persistently ill, disabled members or many infant children are always considered a priority when beneficiary selection is being made. The ORDO emergency team has a well-established method of selecting beneficiaries that focuses only on their status, needs and vulnerabilities. ORDO makes these distinctions clear to communities during sensitization meetings at the rollout stage of the project. This project aims to support 5,500 HH both Balcad & Jowhar through NFI kits, ORDO will ensure gender and age awareness including in the disaggregation of data and the targeting of female-headed households, incorporating gender dynamics and the specific needs of different groups into needs assessments and response recommendations. Further, ORDO will ensure that kit contents procured through this project are gender sensitive.

Project Activities

The below listed are our project activities; • Conduct community mobilization for project awareness and selection of beneficiaries through consultation with IDP women and men representatives. • Identification of the beneficiary Vulnerable IDPs within the targeted localities and prepare Distribution Plan and Registers/lists for the targeted locations and inform the IDPs on the planned distribution prior to the set date as required. • The registration data will be disaggregated by gender; Procure and distribute the NFI and undertake distributions as per the Distribution Plan and Beneficiary Registers/List for the Identified IDP locations in the target locations.• Identification and registration of IDP beneficiaries for the NFI. 50% women will form part of the selection committees.• Focus will be on newly displaced IDPs and those whom their shelters have been destroyed by natural or manmade calamities.• Distribution of 5,500 NFI, during distribution each beneficiaries will sing and confirm to have received his entitlement on a distribution, this template that will be developed by ORDO• Follow up PDM will follow to assess the usability and impact of the NFI.

Mitigation Measures

3b. External stakeholder interference in beneficiary selection: Exposure of women and girls to sexual violence, including sexual exploitation and abuse in relation to access to humanitarian assistance.• Onkod Relief and Development Organization (ORDO) will ensure that registration and distribution staffs assigned will be male and female.• Our organization has put in place strong policies on protection which is clearly stated in the staff’s code of conduct and commits every staff member to sign and adhere to it.• We have provided and will always provide trainings to our staff members on Do No Harm from sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA), child safeguarding and adhere to code of conduct that respect and uphold the rights of beneficiaries.• Our organization will assign a female staff who will be available to women and girls to listen to their concerns and complains throughout the registration and distribution process.• We will provide to our staffs jackets, vests and hats to make them visible.• Our organization will clearly and publicly inform our beneficiaries their entitlement, will inform them that the assistance we are providing to be free and don’t require any form of payment or favor. We will ask and advice them to report complaints to the organization any time through our feedback mechanism which is a hotline phone no which is free of charge by printing and affixing it in the IDP Camps and distribution sites. 4b. Protection mainstreaming: Beneficiaries risk exposure to violence during registration/distribution• ORDO will carry out a detailed security assessment before registration and distribution of NFI Kits to ensure the distribution sites, routes, staff and beneficiaries are secure from all sorts of security threats. The assessment will be lead by ORDO’s Security Officer in close coordination with local police, local authority, and humanitarian actors on the ground, the community members and their leaders.• In consultation with beneficiaries and other Aid agencies present on the ground the organization will prepare a detailed distribution/registration plan.• Beneficiaries will be informed of their entitlements before distribution• Beneficiaries selection will be based on the approved beneficiary selection criteria to identify, verify and register the most vulnerable and needy persons/households.• Our organization will ensure maximum use of its resources including registration/distribution staff to ensure smooth registration/distribution process. 4c. Protection mainstreaming: Forced taxation/extortion or theft of humanitarian assistance.• The organization will develop a detailed distribution plan in consultation with stakeholders before distribution to avoid inconveniences that can provide space for theft.• We will closely coordinate with the local police to keep vigil and generally maintain security of the items, beneficiaries and staffs.• The organization will provide clear guidance to the distribution team, who does what, and inform team members that whoever fails to perform his duties will be held responsible and will be accountable for any loss of the items. • Beneficiaries will be informed of their entitlements to avoid theft and inform the staff in case they find someone with more than his entitlement.• During distribution whoever has been assisted and received his entitlements will be recorded to avoid duplication, cheating and theft.
