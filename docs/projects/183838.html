Project Summary

The project will provide assistance for people in needs through provide integrated humanitarian services on protection, child protection, gender based violence and education for IDPs, , returnees, host communities and refugees. the main project purpose is to provide timely multi-sectoral life-saving assistance to crisis affected people to mitigate risk and response to needs through humanitarian action in four conflict affected states.

1 - Needs

This project is designed to the holistic needs of people in need in four conflict affected states ( South Darfur, East Darfur, Blue Nile and South Kordofan) where WVI is targeted to provide humanitarian services. The most vulnerable among them include unaccompanied and separated children, survivors of sexual and gender-based violence (SGBV), women-headed households, elderly women and men, women, girls, boys and men living with disabilities and with chronic health concerns. SGBV continues to affect women and girls especially among the IDP/ returnees/Refugees and host communities.Particularly vulnerable women and girls lack access to quality specialized lifesaving sexual and gender-based violence (SGBV) services, such as the clinical management of rape (CMR), and psycho-social support (PSS), legal aid, case management and referral mechanisms. This gap in services is further exacerbated by the shortage in trained personnel and weak referral pathwaysCommunity structures are weak specially in Blue Nile and South Kordofan with only a few localities with functioning community-based protection networks and women’s centers offering SGBV services. According to a study by UNWOMEN, 80%of women interviewed in Darfur recognized domestic violence as a problem, 70% identified economic violence as an issue, and 5% cited rape as a concern. Lack of services and social norms were identified as some of the exacerbating factors contributing to domestic violence.The children in these four states have faced the worst child rights violation and have been exposed to rape, GBV, risks of UXO, early marriages, abduction and conscription to armed militias. As well as lack of access to high quality education and improved safe school environment.
