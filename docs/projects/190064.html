Project Summary

Plan International (Plan) proposes an eighteen-month project to address critical child protection needs of displaced populations and host communities affected by the ongoing crisis in Northern Ethiopia. The proposed activity will directly benefit an estimated 17,868 children and adults in Tigray and 15,000 children and adults in Amhara through provision of psycho-social support (PSS), case management, family tracing and reunification, positive parenting programming, awareness raising, community-level child protection, and risk and service mapping. The project will contribute to increased resilience among vulnerable children, families, and communities in Northern Ethiopia impacted by displacement and the ongoing conflict
