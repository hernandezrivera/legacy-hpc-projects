Project Summary

The INGO Forum is an independent network of 21 INGOs implementing humanitarian programs / in the process of setting-up operations to respond to the needs of vulnerable population living in Libya.
It provides a joint platform to facilitate the work of INGOs and help members to efficiently and effectively address key issues of common interest.
The Libya INGO Forum exists primarily to serve the needs of its members and to act in their interest for the betterment of serving beneficiaries. The Forum objectives include the following:
Information Sharing: To ensure that there is effective and transparent sharing of information relevant to both Forum members and external stakeholders.
Coordination: To coordinate INGO exchange, learning and resource-sharing through meetings, Working Groups and through relationships with external stakeholders.
Policy/Advocacy: To represent the interests of the members in external meetings, policy discussions, advocacy meetings and documents, as well as to lobby on issues of common interest.
Representation: To consistently represent the interest of members with external stakeholders.

Plan fields

1 - Needs

Members of the LIF have, on a regular basis, expressed the need for increased and improved coordination, as well as the need to advocate for a principled humanitarian response in Libya. In the first half of 2019, the forum group composed of the country directors of each members decided to develop and strengthen three components: security, advocacy and engagement with national NGO and CSO in Libya. Dedicated staff have been recruited to develop these initiatives. As a complement, The LIF, through the M&E department of its hosting agency ACTED, conducted an in depth evaluation in November-December 2019 to further assess the past performance, the needs and expectations in order to define the priorities to be addressed by the Forum in 2020.
