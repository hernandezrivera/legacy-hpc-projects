Project Summary

Bonyan project is intending to expand its provision of integrated community-based and individually targeted protection interventions across Northwest Syria. The Project aims to enhance the prevention and mitigation of protection risks and respond to protection needs providing protection prevention and response services to most vulnerable due to a combination of physical, social, environmental and political factors: age, gender, diversity, specific needs ,displacement status, Health Conditions and Disabilities including IDPs, HCs and returnees in targeted areas. Bonyan will establish two community protection center in Albab and Maret Tamsren , in addition to expand protection services through 5 protection mobile teams (Each mobile team contains 5 members: 1 team leader and 4 team workers in ( Afrin, Albab, Jarablous, Salqin, Maret Tamsren, Idleb , Dana).
