Project Summary


Moderate to heavy rains is foreseen in south and central which is likely to lead to flash floods in low-lying areas while middle Shabelle and Hiran region are at risk of flooding given the highest water level and focused heavy rains in Oct,2020, the situation is likely to trigger displacement and spake of AWD/cholera cases in these regions, however, the reverse is true to Mudug region where a dry spell an /or low rainfall might be expected leading to drought displacement among the poor pastoral livelihoods,Additinally, WASH cluster report (Sept,2020) indicated that a total of 610,034 people were reached with safe access to water(49%) against 1.2m targeted while also a total of 810,451 people were reach with temporary water services against 2.2 M people targeted as well as 211,388 people reached with improved sanitation against the target 980,221.
As per the findings of government and NGOs assessment, the majority of the IDPs settlements in these districts are heavily congested and have also proportionally received the largest number of new arrivals and is faced with a huge task to provide basic social services to population. The increasing new arrivals and returnees are either hosted by other IDP households or staying in makeshift shelter structures. Hosted IDPs are putting pressure on the already inadequate resources for protracted IDPs while those staying in makeshift shelters are exposed to natural whether elements such as cold at night, domestic violence, rains and hot sun during the day. Water is the greatest need in the community and has remained a serious challenge in the target district. Water is mainly supplied by the private individuals, who currently lack the capacity to meet the ever-increasing demand due to booming population with the daily influx of IDPs. IDPs are mostly affected by the water problem and the assessed IDPs cannot also afford the water since they don’t have an income to sustain this. The sanitation situation in the area is 1 latrine per 500 persons. Many people are living with disabilities inflicted from conflict and are unable to access any water consumption points. Protection concerns among women and people with disability are also at an alarming rate where GBV cases are reported daily among the IDP settlements.
Against the backdrops, therefore, PDA intends to provide enhanced water, sanitation and hygiene promotion as well hygiene kits and sanitation too to the affected population including disable group in Middle Shabele, Hiran and Mudug region target district.
PDA will ensure inclusion of disable person in the target location through enhance representation and participation of disable group in project employment, beneficiaries hence improving their representation and decision making within the community, PDA will also ensure accountability to the disadvantage group as well as enhance prevention on sexual exploitation and abuse among its beneficiaries by ensuring its staff are well aware of organization policy on PSEA and code of conduct before the initiation of the program
