Project Summary

Through this project, children with disabilities will be educated and rehabilitated in their homes
Providing them with specialized lessons, assistance, and appropriate auxiliary tools, with the supportive presence of the parents, to work on qualifying the parents for educational follow-up.
This need was evaluated for direct communication with cases at the association’s headquarters during their registration, through the association’s mobile teams.
Field visits and communication with officials in the local community.
The data was collected through the association’s general register, which contains detailed information about the cases (name, national number, type of disability,
Causes of disability, detailed place of residence, needs and phone number). We found that the basic needs of the target group in the proposed project are:
1- Rehabilitating the disabled child and providing him with life and cognitive skills
2- Assisting parents in educating parents in dealing with their child and involving them in the child’s rehabilitation
Among the weaknesses that the organisation decided to address are:
1- There are no specialized centers that offer this qualification
2- The cost of transportation to reach centers that provide a small part of these services
3- In light of the winter season, there is great difficulty in the targeted areas in securing the education and rehabilitation needs of their children
2. Justify the grant application. Home rehabilitation project for children with disabilities, which will address:
Rehabilitating children with disabilities and providing them with life and cognitive skills
Helping parents make children aware of how to deal with their child and involving them in the child’s rehabilitation
It is aligned with OCHA, Humanitarian Sector and Education allocations, so we value and look forward to this partnership.
3. Linkage to the allocation strategy in accordance with OCHA allocations, which prioritizes assistance to the most vulnerable communities, including persons with disabilities, in addition to the education sector.
Allocations that emphasize the provision of education for all.
Our project meets these allocations to reach and meet people with special needs in the Homs countryside.
This group was not served by these services by us or by any other association present in Homs, given the needs shared by the beneficiaries themselves.
With us, we put together this project proposal, as this project is considered to include the education sector, but it has a major role in mitigating the impact of education
The current economic crisis on the families of people with disabilities, but also aims to improve the living standards and mental health of the beneficiary
Through our partnership with UNICEF and providing case management and cash amounts for children with disabilities in a project that started since 2018 until now, a large number of data were collected from the children of Homs and its countryside, and we noticed through our questionnaires to monitor the educational needs of children in the targeted areas and their brothers, with the involvement of the schools, the neighborhood committee and the mukhtars in the monitoring process, we have shown a great need for this type of project, as no organization intervened. Previously in this field, the need was also accurately assessed based on the duration of our long work and our field presence in the targeted areas

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The primary objective is to enhance educational and rehabilitative standards in the targeted areas, where we have confirmed the absence of such projects previously. We have shared our assessment with the education sector and it has been approved. Twenty-five experienced teachers will be selected to provide specialized home lessons for individuals with disabilities up to the age of 17, including their siblings if necessary. Three cars with drivers will be hired to transport the teachers and the specialized educational materials to the children's homes. Each educational session will last approximately one hour and fifteen minutes, with the presence of parents utilizing educational materials. Children will be evaluated during the sessions to assess their progress, mental status, and their receptiveness to the provided information. One educational session will be provided per child per week. Additionally, parents will be trained on the educational materials provided to enable them to continuously support their children's education. This project targets the most affected areas in rural Homs (Al-Qusayr, Al-Farqlas, Tadmur, Quraytayn, Mheen, Mkharram), specifically aiming at 500 children. We relied on our own database formed over the years at the Al-Rajaa Charitable Association, extracting and updating information based on beneficiaries' needs. The project includes a project manager, data entry staff, a protection officer, 25 teachers, and a logistics worker. Each child will be visited about twice a week to give him an educational lesson, while maintaining the participation of the family and siblings in understanding the method of teaching their child. Through our partnership with UNICEF and providing case management and cash amounts for children with disabilities in a project that started since 2018 until now, a large number of data were collected from the children of Homs and its countryside, and we noticed through our questionnaires to monitor the educational needs of children in the targeted areas and their brothers, with the involvement of the schools, the neighborhood committee and the mukhtars in the monitoring process, we have shown a great need for this type of project, as no organization intervened.
