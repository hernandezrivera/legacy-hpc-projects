Project Summary

This project is proposed under the revised 2023 Country Refugee Response Plan appeal submission and has been reviewed by the Refugee Consultation Forum and in line with sectors Strategic Objectives for the HRP 2023 and will focus on lifesaving and protection interventions and has been revised again to accommodate the situation after the irruption of the clashes in Khartoum in 15 of April . The proposed interventions are integrated multi sector for refugees and asylum seekers including South Sudanese refuges in White Nile state and Ethiopian refugees in Gadarif state. This project targeted 59,354 refugees , through multi sector response integrates Protection , Education and WASH and the planned interventions are as follows:
The General Protection response : Targets 19,288 Refugees and will deliver the basic Protection services include formation of protection Community based committees ,awareness session and Capacity building on protection issues.
Child Protection response : Targets 1,000 Refugees children and will deliver the basic Child Protection services include Case Management and referral services , awareness session Capacity building for services providers on child protection issues and provide assistance for PWD children .
Gender Based Violence response : Targets 12,576 Refugees and will deliver the basic GBV services including GBV Case Management and referral services ,GBV awareness session and Capacity building ( CMR,SSP, and other GBV topics ) for services providers
Education response : targets 8,766 (boys 49% and %51 girls and from total targeted children 1% children with disabilities) through improve the absorption of school age children and strengthen capacity of education facilities ,provide scholastic martials , WASH intervention in school , building capacity of teachers ( EiE,SSP ,pedagogue and leadership) , school environment activities
WASH Response : Targets 17,724 Refugees and host communities will deliver the basic WASH service , which include Rehabilitate hand pump, Provision of soap and Jerry cans ,Do vector control, conduct community awareness session on hygiene promotion and community cleaning campaigns , followed by provision of cleaning tools and equipment's , building the capacity of WASH committees on WASH topics .
JASMAR have operation ,presence and good knowledge of targeted communities in targeted state, and will coordinate the project activities with other humanitarian actors and forums (sectors Education , WASH ,CP and GBV) and respective government departments in the project sites .
JASMAR implementation strategy will be direct implementation which include regular community orientation ,mobilization and engagement.

1 - Needs

Sudan one of the largest countries hosting refugee populations ,with 1.16 million refugeesand asylum seekers as of Refugees Response Plan . Since outbreak of armed conflict in Sudan on 15 April 2023, large numbers of civilians have been forced to flee, including people who were already internally displaced because of previous conflicts in Sudan and refugees from other countries who had sought safety in Sudan. And increased vulnerabilities of refugees and asylum seekers across Sudan. In Khartoum it has caused secondary movements of refugees and asylum seekers, including South Sudanese, Eritreans, Ethiopians, Syrians and other nationalities from Khartoum to White Nile, Kassala, Port Sudan, Aj Jazirah, Gedaref and other states. There are further concerns for premature returns to countries of origin, placing refugees at risk of forced and unsafe returns. Mainly in White Nile state Refugee camps is are already overcrowded and neglected, placing stress on available services and limited resources while exposing refugees to heightened protection risks. By mid-May White Nile has received additional 75,000 refugees. Land allocations for expansion of existing camps and establishment of new camps combined with resource mobilization are urgently required to accommodate new arrivals, ensure service provisions that meet basic needs and uphold dignified settlement conditions. The South Sudanese refugees in Sudan about 808,336 Population 25.7% % of SSR located in White Nile state about 293,669 individual .Ethiopian refugees about 72,334 individual about 66,000 in Gadarif state and located in main three camps . Persistent insecurity in countries of origin contribute to protracted refugee caseloads remaining in Sudan and they are affected by current conflict , as opportunities for safe, voluntary, and dignified returns are obstructed.The designed activities under this Project will focus on life-saving assistance to vulnerable people, including refugees, and host communities , and will provide emergency life-saving protection services and access to basic services to address life-threatening conditions. The efforts will be made to mitigate the likelihood of people adopting negative coping mechanisms and who suffer from multi crisis and choke especially affected by conflict in Khartoum and other states . additionally the propose project through planned activities will work to strengthen the protection environment for targeted refugees through provide equitable access to basic services ( Protection , child protection, GBV , Education and WASH ) for refugees in the camps and work to support their opportunities for resilience and self-reliance

2 - Activities/Outputs

The project interventions are designed in a multi-sectoral approach to address the urgent needs of the refugees in White Nile and Gadarif state.For the Protection intervention the planned activities as below : This intervention aims to improve and provide emergency life-saving protection services, the planned activities include Raise awareness on general protection topics in targets 10,800 persons - Establish 8 protection Community-Based committees - Capacity building (targeted 24 service providers) on general protection topics - JASMAR will conduct extensive community engagement and awareness raising activities on protection issues. For Child Protection intervention the planned activities as below : This intervention aims to improve/strengthen child protection environment in targeted camps , the planned activities is : Provide Case Management and referral services for 840 children -Capacity building activities for 140 service providers, community-based child protection committees and will be trained on CP related trainings ,JASMAR will targets and provide assistance to 140 children with Disability and will be reached with direct services to improve their care and wellbeing For GBV intervention the planned activities as below : This intervention aims to improve access to protection services and strengthen protection environment in targeted camps and gathering points , the planned activities include conduct 20 community Awareness raising campaigns to raise awareness about GBV topics - Capacity building on GBV topics for Protection networks and Service providers , targeting 160 participants –JASMAR will work with line ministries to ensure improve of GBV referral pathway and Case Management services and GBV survivors receiving individual case management. For Education intervention the design of this intervention aims to support/ absorption of refugee children to attend schools while improving safety and protection in schools andbuilding the capacity of school teachers , the planned activities is : Building the capacityof 250 school teachers and PTAs on (EiE,SSP ,pedagogue and leadership ) - Provision of scholastic martials( Teacher kits , School Kits, recreational Kits )-Provide school uniforms for students -Teachers (primary or intermediate schools) will received a teacher incentive- Provision of 16 TLSs . and JASMAR will work to improve access to portable water ( water net , Water Tank , Tap stans ),and provision of hand wash facilities in targeted education facilities. For WASH intervention the planned activities as below : This intervention aims to improve access to basic WASH services in targeted camps the planned activities include rehabilitate of 8 hand pump- Provision of soap and provision of Jerry can targeted 6,600 households ,Do 16 vector control (spraying, draining standing water) - conduct community awareness session on hygiene promotion – community cleaning campaigns and provision of cleaning tools and equipment's - building the capacity of community structures on WASH topics
