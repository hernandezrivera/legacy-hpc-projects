Project Summary

ACTED’s proposed action will response to the needs of IDPS and rural communities in Baidoa, Kismayo, El-Afwayne, Badhan, Diinsoor and Buurhakaba districts who are impacted by floods and drought, lack sustainable livelihoods, at risk of displacement and with limited access to adequate WASH and shelter services.
An estimated 2.6 million people have been displaced in Somalia due to conflict, insecurity, drought and floods. Most of the displaced people are not able to meet their basic needs due to inconsistence of service provision, the rapidly developing COVID-19 crisis may lead to further crises, especially for IDPs who often live in overcrowded conditions which lack adequate WASH and health services. ACTED will implement an integrated intervention to address the increased vulnerabilities and improved the strained essential services in IDP sites.
Activities under this action include construction of 40 transitional shelters in Baidoa for 40 households, provision of 692 ESKs (190 in Baidoa, 187 Sool and Sanaag and 315 Kismayo), and also providing NFIs to 1,634 households (400 in Sanaag and Sool, 420 in Baidoa, 630 in Kismayo, 79 in Buurhakaba, 105 in Diinsoor). 6 weeks after distribution of ESKs and NFIs to households, ACTED will conduct a post distribution monitoring. Throughout program implementation, ACTED will utilize the complaints and feedback mechanism in order to grant the beneficiaries an opportunity to share feedback on the project and to also share their complaints.
ACTED’s shelter intervention will respond to the needs of vulnerable IDP populations in Kismayo, Baidoa, Badhan, El-Afwayne, Diinsoor and Buurhakaba. ACTED aims to support new arrivals in these locations who have sub-standard shelters which undermines the living conditions of residents and poses a protection risk for vulnerable households. ESKs and NFIs will be distributed to meet the needs for new arrivals and those with inadequate shelters. In Baidoa, ACTED will construct transitional shelters to provide a sustainable shelter response. This activity is feasible in Baidoa as the government is providing land to IDP communities to cope with the expanding IDP population and ensure sustainability.
Under this Action, priority will be given to newly displaced (because of drought, conflict, evictions or other natural disasters) or household affected by disasters in the last six months. ACTED will use vulnerability based targeting and selection criteria that will prioritize the most vulnerable IDP households who are characterized by: presence of members with psychological condition or physical disabilities, pregnant and lactating women, older person or chronically ill individuals, single headed HHs including female, child, older person headed household, large households and households with many young children, households community acknowledged as poor or with extremely vulnerable shelters, HHs belonging to marginalized groups or clans.
ACTED will use voucher as default modality of implementation for the non-food items intervention. ACTED favors the voucher transfer modality as it:
• enhance beneficiaries’ dignity and participation to the humanitarian response: through the flexible voucher system beneficiaries will be able to decide what to buy within the list of available items, and therefore will be able to choose what to prioritize based on their own assessment;
• support local markets through cash injection for small/medium size vendors.

Project Activities

Shelter: ACTED will construct 40 transitional shelters to the most vulnerable households in Baidoa. The selection of these households will be done in collaboration with the community leaders based on the vulnerability criteria. ACTED will conduct Post Construction Monitoring exercise three months after handover of transitional shelters to the community to ascertain what percentage of the shelters are being used for accommodation, the quality of the shelters and to monitor any modifications which residents may male. These findings are critical to understanding utilization and to improve design for future Shelter and settlement:Provision of ESKs: ACTED will distribute emergency shelter kits to 692 recently displaced households who are new arrivals to IDP camps in Baidoa (190 households), Kismayo (315 households) as well as Sanaag and Sool (187 households). ACTED will also make an additional 315 contingency shelter kits available to recently displaced households who are new arrivals to IDP camps in Kismayo, as well as to flood affected IDP households requiring immediate shelter assistance (315 households). Provision of NFIs: ACTED will provide vouchers for shelter non-food items to 1,634 households (400 in Sanaag and Sool, 420 in Baidoa, 630 in Kismayo, 79 in Buurhakaba, 105 in Diinsoor) to improve living condition.Six weeks after the distribution, post distribution monitoring will be conducted for both ESK and NFI to analyze the impact of the project, the beneficiary’s level of satisfaction and establish the lessons learnt for future programming ACTED has well-established complaint and feedback mechanism for each project, which has toll-free number which beneficiaries can call in case there is an issue or feedback to be provided on the projects or proposed interventions. The complaint and feedback mechanism are being handled by ACTED’s Independent Appraisal, Monitoring and Evaluation unit and feedback to the projects to promote higher accountability.Post Distribution Monitoring- ACTED will conduct Post Distribution Monitoring (PDM) exercise 6 weeks after the ESK and NFI distribution to analyze the impact of the project, the beneficiaries’ level of satisfaction and establish the lessons learnt for future programming.

What is the justification for using cash or vouchers instead of in kind?

The vouchers provided to the beneficiaries will provide them with the independence of purchasing items within the scope of their different needs. ACTED has found that the voucher based system is preferred by beneficiaries as evidenced through a PDM for the current OFDA-funded project. Shelter vouchers were found to be preferred by 98% of beneficiaries due to the ease of the process. However, in kind distributions will be used as back up modalities only whenever markets are too far, not safely accessible for the beneficiaries or NFIs material are not available in the local market, based on market surveys conducted by ACTED. ACTED confirms that the vouchers for shelter NFIs will enable homeowners to obtain appropriate building materials. During the preliminary phase of the project, ACTED will identify suppliers to be engaged for the voucher scheme. Vendors will be identified based on their ability to have the appropriate building materials during the implementation period.

Explain how the project design considered market functionality ?

In the designing of the project, ACTED carried out a market analysis in all districts. Prior to project kick-off, all markets were warranted as functional and the local inflation and market dynamics analyzed in order to provide beneficiaries with adequate amounts according to the prevailing conditions. In the unlikely event of an nation-wide inflation, ACTED would revise the amount accordingly after due consultation with the donor.

Mitigation Measures

Risk 1: ACTED having implemented activities within the areas since 2007, it remains fully aware of the security dynamics and risks involved. Presently with the COVID-19 pandemic, ACTED is conducting all beneficiary registration via telephone, while verification is conducted remotely by ACTED accountability, monitoring and evaluation team (AME). Under usual circumstances, registration will be done at village level and always through door to door so the beneficiary is registered while in his/her home. With regard to the NFI and ESK distribution, will ensure the selected area for distribution will be within close proximity to the communities to ensure safety of the vulnerable beneficiaries. ACTED will also be distributing the NFIs as vouchers and thus, protecting the beneficiaries from theft and protecting their dignity at the same time. Risk 2: In implementing this project, ACTED will conduct all cash transfers through mobile money in order to erase the long distance beneficiaries used to walk in order to receive payment. This previously exposed women, girls and boys to SEA, however, with the mobile money transfer beneficiaries receive money at the comfort of their homes. Lastly, ACTED during implementation will conduct gender and protection sensitization sessions, with practical examples on how involvement of both men and women in said community politics is rewarding. ACTED will further conduct site safety and protection activities through site maintenance works in reducing risks connected to site layout, site environment or presence of dangerous infrastructure. Risk 3: ACTED has an existing policy on Sexual Exploitation and Abuse which is readily available to all staff members. This comprehensive document is part of the code of conduct handbook signed by all ACTED staff upon beginning interaction with ACTED. Strict adherence to this policy is expected within the organization and in case of violation, internal reporting mechanisms exists which grant the employees a voice. With regard to employee to beneficiary interaction, the PSEA also provides clauses on this and repercussions in case ACTED employees infringe on this. Beneficiary hotline numbers exist and shared with all beneficiaries and posted in all sites which ACTED is active. Risk 4: During the selection and construction of the shelters, it is imperative that ACTED collaborate with the local authorities in ensuring the land the shelters are constructed are either community land belonging to the beneficiaries or land donated by the government. If the land is donated by the government, ACTED will ensure that the documentation is properly done and shared with the beneficiaries.
