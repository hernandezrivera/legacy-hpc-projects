Project Summary

The duration of this project will be 12 months staring from 1st January 2024 to 31st December 2024. The education core pipeline supplies project will contribute to the Education Cluster objectives- Children and youth are provided with quality teaching and learning services that support retention and progress through the education cycle and Emergency-affected children and youth have access to safe, protective, inclusive education services that are appropriate to their age, learning level and need. This will be achieved through the provision and management of teaching and learning materials for emergency-affected boys and girls aged 3-17 years (40% girls) to increase equitable access to safe and protective quality education service in South Sudan. Adequate and appropriate education supplies play a key role in ensuring access to equitable quality education and demand for teaching and learning materials is now higher than ever especially currently when South Sudan is facing multiple crisis that are impacting the education service delivery. Through the project, essential education materials will be procured, prepositioned, and distributed and efficiently managed to ensure children's right for equitable access to quality education is safeguarded. The project will have special focus on the most vulnerable populations affected by conflicts and floods such as IDPs, and Returnees. With the deteriorating economic situation in the country, most parents cannot afford to provide learning materials to their children, therefore this project will support learners and teachers with the much needed education supplies. UNICEF has adopted the multi-sectoral approach for project implementation. The provision of supplies will complement integrated WASH and Child Protection interventions in the target schools. Monitoring of the interventions will be conducted jointly with other sectors and lessons learnt documented for future planning. As the Education Cluster lead and one of the most influential actors with field presence in most of the states, UNICEF will provide support to national NGOs with capacity building in supplies distribution, warehousing, and end-user monitoring.

1. Needs

South Sudan continues to face multiple crises including conflicts and climate change induced challenges such as flooding and droughts. The education sector like other sectors has been negatively impacted by these challenges whose far-reaching consequences include damage to school infrastructure and displacement of communities, causing disruption to learning and weakening family livelihood which leads to choosing harmful coping mechanisms that keep children away from school such as early marriages, pregnancies and recruitment in armed groups. These challenges have strained the already weak education system which is grappling with high numbers of out of school children (2.8 million), low gross enrolment rates at 11%, 59% and 12% at preprimary, primary and secondary levels respectively, high numbers of unqualified teachers with one third of teachers being untrained. Adequate teaching and learning materials are in short supply with textbook ratios at 8:1 for primary way below the 1:1 policy guidance (GESP 2023-2027). This deteriorating education landscape in South Sudan is likely to remain worse with these figures being even higher amongst vulnerable populations (IDPs, Returnees, Children with disabilities, girls, and boys in pastoralist communities). Girls are disproportionately affected as they face persistent obstacles that affect impact their access to education opportunities and learning experiences as they transition from adolescence to adulthood. According to education statistics, only 33 per cent of all girls in the country attend primary school, with a high dropout rate of 13 per cent. These trends have led to a low rate of female literacy and inhibit girls from achieving their full potential.

2. Project activities

The UNICEF education core pipeline supplies project will provide teaching and learning materials for emergency-affected children (boys and girls) and teachers (males and females) through implementation of the following activities: 2.1) Procure teaching and learning materials (ECD kits, teacher kits, student kits, recreation kits, dignity kits. 2.2) Preposition and distribute teaching and learning materials for emergency affected boys and girls aged 3-17 years and teachers. 2.3) Conduct end-user monitoring of teaching and learning materials to ensure effective utilization. The affected population will participate in and influence the project through a feedback mechanism. Boys and girls aged 3-17 years will be involved throughout project cycle from needs assessment, design, implementation, and monitoring and evaluation. The project will strengthen SMCs and PTAs especially in management of the education supplies at the school level and conducting end-user monitoring to ensure proper use of the supplies. These activities will directly contribute to the Cluster objective - focusing on ensuring Children and youth are provided with quality teaching and learning services that support retention and progress through the education cycle. To ensure Accountability to the Affected Population is integrated throughout the project, UNICEF in collaboration with other Cluster partners will ensure there is an effective risk management strategy that will help to identify and mitigate risks such as fraud/aid diversion, exposure to sexual exploitation and abuse to ensure maximum results for children. UNICEF will strengthen local capacities and as well coordination and partnership with communities. Schools including teachers and learners and local authorities will be consulted to ensure materials are appropriate and contextually relevant
