Project Summary

This project, with its proposed intervention, is structured along two axes to provide a comprehensive and adapted emergency response. It will be able to adapt to evolving situations and respond to the critical needs faced by the affected population in the target districts (Mocímboa da Praia, Mueda and Muidumbe):
1) Immediate response with sanitation actions that can generate positive impacts on IDPS and returnees affected by insurgent incursions in the target districts and;
2) Immediate response with hygiene promotion activities to benefit IDPS and returnees in critical hygiene situations.
Through this response, APDS plans to cover the immediate sanitation and hygiene needs of IDPs and returnees by implementing the following activities: construction and rehabilitation of emergency latrines; support to communities to increase access to emergency HH latrines; implementation of monthly waste management campaigns; carrying out hygiene promotion for IDPs and returnees through community agents; creation/revitalization of WASH committees; distribution of hygiene kits.
This intervention is justified by the fact that the target audience is in an extremely degraded situation, that is, without the conditions to be resilient after the disruptions caused by the terrorist incursions. It can also be inferred that, if these needs are not met in a short period of time, they may cause more suffering (illness and death) due to the upcoming rainy season.
These interventions, in addition to reducing the suffering that the target group is going through, will also contribute to containing the spread of diseases (cholera, diarrhea) that break out in the communities of these districts during the upcoming rainy season.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project aims to provide immediate access to life-saving sanitation and hygiene services to displaced and returnee populations in the conflict-affected districts of Mocimboa da Praia, Mueda and Muidumbe, with concrete humanitarian interventions that have a positive impact on the sanitation and hygiene of the target groups. Regarding emergency sanitation, in addition to disseminating information about the importance of having good sanitation and its benefits, via door-to-door, as well as en masse in places with larger population agglomerations (markets, mosques, churches, hospitals and schools), we also intend to build and rehabilitate emergency latrines, support communities to increase access to emergency HH latrines, and implement monthly waste management campaigns. With regard to hygiene, the project will place greater emphasis on hygiene promotion in emergency situations, focusing on the following activities: promoting critical hygiene for internally displaced people and returnees through community workers; supporting the creation/revitalization of WASH committees to carry out key humanitarian actions; distributing life-saving hygiene supplies, including menstrual hygiene items to meet the special needs of women and girls.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

SANITATIONRapid construction and maintenance of emergency latrinesIn the event of large-scale population displacements or outbreaks, and to limit health risks and reduce environmental contamination, APDS will focus on building emergency latrines. All community facilities will be maintained and cleaned by dedicated volunteers who are trained and equipped to perform these services. The installation of emergency latrines will temporarily ensure the safe disposal of excreta and design more culturally acceptable solutions in case populations begin to settle in these areas.Rapid construction and maintenance of communal latrines/showersIn existing IDP sites in Mueda, Muedumbe and Mocimboa da Praia districts, emergency community infrastructure (latrine and shower blocks) will be installed in advance next to transit shelters or at the entry point of the identified sites. They are designed with local material, can be easily maintained and reduce the need for frequent maintenance compared to canvas emergency latrines. This design should allow better access to latrines for the newly displaced population with improved privacy. and safety measures to reduce the risk of gender-based violence.Support in the construction and rehabilitation of family latrinesThis activity will allow families living in individual shelters to have family latrines and showers that are in accordance with regional cultural standards. APDS has already identified that many families have started to build their own latrines and showers, but are unable to finish them due to a lack of available materials. Some families are using them even though the structure is not complete, which causes major privacy and protection issues for users. APDS will support communities to improve access to sanitation at the household level. APDS will work in collaboration with the community and local government to ensure it is meet the needs of the community while aligning with the National Sanitation Policy. Implementation of monthly waste management campaignsIn emergency contexts, activities like distributing goods and constructing shelters generate significant waste, often poorly managed, posing environmental health risks such as vector infestations. To address this, APDS aims to raise community awareness about digging strategic pits as traditional landfills and plans monthly waste management campaigns in IDP sites. These initiatives will rely on volunteer efforts, supported by APDS through the provision of cleaning materials and waste area maintenance.HYGIENE PROMOTIONImplementation of emergency hygiene promotion for newly displaced IDPs and ReturneesFor beneficiaries receiving frontline WASH emergency response, APDS will conduct hygiene promotion sessions to address specific needs related to the emergency situation, including cholera outbreak, water treatment, water storage, importance of handwashing, waste management, ensuring that messages are prioritized according to evolving needs. Key hygiene messages will be disseminated by community members called “activists” recruited and trained by APDS.Hygiene promotion through community agentsAPDS intends to deliver critical hygiene promotion messages to all intervention sites through various activities such as Focus Group Discussions (FGDs), mass and door-to-door outreach. Key hygiene messages will be disseminated by community members called “activists” recruited and trained by APDS. There is indeed an urgent need to spread good hygiene practices in the crowded camps, which have never benefited from humanitarian support in terms of hygiene promotion, in the case of Muedumbe and Mocimboa da Praia. Benefiting from the synergy of this proposed project with other projects, wherever possible, APDS will use the KAP survey data to prioritize the messages needed for outreach.Distribution of hygiene kitsIn case of new arrivals or identification of vulnerable families in need within a location, APDS will distribute hygiene kits.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

MITIGATION OF GENDER-BASED VIOLENCE RISKS AND PROTECTIONAlthough APDS does not implement stand-alone protection projects, APDS remains committed to integrating protection into its operations and programming. APDS is therefore working to improve its tools and staff capacities in this regard. In addition to its code of conduct and reporting policy, APDS is strengthening its sexual exploitation and abuse policy which specifically addresses the risk of sexual abuse within the organization and through its programs. Hence the reason why each group will be consulted to mitigate any risk of gender-based violence.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

At this point, it is important to emphasize that the APDS principle is non-discrimination, regardless of color, race, gender, age and nationality. Based on this principle, the APDS will encourage the participation of all beneficiaries expected to benefit from the project (women, children, elderly people). It will create dialogue mechanisms through the establishment of focus groups to allow all beneficiaries to participate and contribute to the identification and redefinition of priority needs.We will also ensure that the latrines to be established under this emergency intervention are segregated according to gender, but also meet the specificities of age groups and people’s needs. We will also seek to ensure adequately designed services, dignity items for menstrual hygiene management, representation of women on water committees, etc.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

This emergency project proposal, focused on the axes of Sanitation and Hygiene, is committed to ensuring that all people, regardless of their physical or cognitive conditions, have equal access to essential services. APDS will adopt an inclusive approach, starting with mapping and identifying needs. To this end, it will involve local leaders and heads of reception/transit centers, forming discussion groups to identify people with disabilities and understand their specific needs. Tools, such as inclusive assessment forms, will be developed to recognize all forms of disability — physical, sensory, intellectual or psychosocial.After this phase, the project will seek to raise awareness in the community through campaigns that raise awareness about the rights of people with disabilities and the importance of their inclusion in emergency actions. Training for volunteers, staff and partners will be provided, with the aim of eliminating stigmas and prejudices that may limit these people's access to services. In addition, the project will work to remove physical and architectural barriers, adapting infrastructure such as latrines and handwashing stations, with the installation of ramps and non-slip surfaces to make them easier for everyone to use.To ensure equitable access, a monitoring system will be established to ensure that people with disabilities receive the necessary support. Focal points will be designated within the team to continuously monitor the inclusion of these groups, addressing potential gaps in the services provided. Community activists, family members and the target group in general will be sensitized to directly support people with disabilities by assisting them in using sanitation and hygiene services. Finally, the project will include an ongoing monitoring and evaluation process, collecting direct feedback from beneficiaries to adjust approaches, ensuring that no one is left behind.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

ENVIRONMENTAs part of its WASH strategy for the period 2020-2025, APDS is committed to taking into account the growing environmental challenges that humanity is currently facing, such as the consequences of climate change, a rapidly growing population and the increasing health risks linked to the environment and urbanization. APDS is therefore striving to integrate, within its support programs and its governance, actions in the areas of Disaster Risk Reduction to strengthen the adaptation of populations to climate change. This is done, for example, by analyzing the environmental impact of its projects and by implementing solar panels for electricity supply whenever possible.
