Project Summary

The project seek to build the resilience and coping mechanisms of youth and youth platforms especially young women including NGOs, youth in government and other community members to increase their active and meaningful participation in humanitarian systems as well as local governance including providing direct support especially for the protection of women. The project will work with a range of stakeholders including local government officials, community youth groups in formal and informal platforms. The project will seek to also increase the visibility and participation of young women.

1 - Needs

The project will support the early recovery efforts of the BAY States Borno Adamawa and Yobe States by providing needed training and other capacity building/mentoring actions for youth in community in government to increase their functionality and participation in humanitarian systems. The project will also seek to support life skill training as well as support for small businesses for most vulnerable young people in selected communities. The project will deploy a community targeting process in the selections of beneficiaries.

2 - Activities

Activities on the project will include 1. Formal training 2. Step down trainings 3. Advocacy intervention targeted at key structures such as the HCT, Government and other actors 4. Budget monitoring and influencing to increase government and donor investments in early recovery and livelihood interventions. 5. Citizens consciousness building actions 6. Community targeting and beneficiary selection 7. Livelihood support to vulnerable young people.
