Project Summary

To upgrade IDP sites and ensure safe and dignified living conditions for the most vulnerable people in Idleb, and north Aleppo with a focus on appropriate preventive measures to alleviate the effects of flooding and prepare for winter, providing decent shelter, and Saed plans to implement a set of coordinated and integrated WASH, Shelter, and CCCM interventions to bridge the gap for 78,605 beneficiaries (15818 men, 17231 women, 20241 boys, 25315 girls including 1101 PWDs) in 34 IDPs sites closely to each other in Dana Afrin, Albab, Jarablus and Maaret Tamsrin sub-districts within a limited geographical space, where gaps were determined based on multi-sectoral needs assessment that Saed conducted considering the current and previous efforts and interventions done by other humanitarian partners in proposal operation area.
Through 3 project proposals Saed suggests implementing:
Conducting improvements to the infrastructure in 10 camps, reported flooded last winter, in Dana through establishment of closed and open rain drainage systems, rehabilitation of roads and pathways inside camps; where Saed has conducted topographical, hydrological, and geotechnical studies for these camps and designed an integrated, and inclusive interventions to drain all rain water in the area through which targeted camps, in addition to 3 other camps where similar interventions will be carried with secured fund by other partners, will benefit.
Activities will be implemented within a timeframe of maximum 6 months where Saed undertakes to complete all works before 15 Dec 2022 winter. Total beneficiaries from this component is: 27419 IDPs (5765 men, 6166 women, 7142 boys, 8346 girls)

4 - Needs

As of February 2021, nearly 1.7 M IDPs were living in 1,385 camps or informal sites (including 24,800 people displaced in Jan and Feb 2021 alone), number of populations in need for assistance has been raised in 2021 to 3.4 m due to the negative effects of deterioration of Syrian Pound and COVID-19. With start of 2021 rains caused damage to nearly 152,700 IDPs in no less than 148 IDPs sites.In a response to people needs, and to adapt integrated multisectoral approach of assistance provision, Saed has conducted a multisectoral needs assessment for two geographic locations in north Syria in 33 camps. Based on the Clusters factsheet data, field needs assessment, FGDs with 7 groups representing populations divided as per age and gender including women, children, elderlies and PWDs, and field notes and watches, results indicated needs over two parts: Providing shelter with associated infrastructure:- Permanent and durable shelter as displacement duration is lasts for longer periods for 500 HHs. - Flood preventive measures for IDPs informal sites and camps, with proper flood drainage systems for 34 IDPs sites. - Tents ground insulation for 1055 tents. - Roads rehabilitation/construction for IDPs sites and camps for better access to services Winterization:- Distribution of heating materials over 3 rounds in winter for 4 planned camps for for 2450 HHs, total of 12443 IDPs, according to cluster guidance. - Distribution of proper heaters and heating materials associated with heating materials distribution.

5 - Implementation of activities

The proposed procedures are directly in line with strategic priority of Syria Humanitarian Response Plan 2021 to increase the resilience and accessibility to services by the most vulnerable people and they are in line with second strategic objective of Shelter sector in infrastructure support for IDPs sites and ensuring access to services by the most vulnerable people by establishing the rain drainage networks system inside the sites that are exposed to flood and conducting the tents ground gravelling for tents that need, besides conducting rehabilitation to affected roads by floods, torrents and wrongful use of roads in ten camps.These interventions will contribute in developing the housing conditions sustainably for 27419 IDPs (5765 men, 6166 women, 7142 boys, 8346 girls) including 369 disabled persons.Activities will be implemented initially by preparing and finalizing all engineering studies, MOUs, and conducting procurement and contracting processes. Then, establishment of rain water drainage system in 33 camps, where closed and open rain water drainage system and drainage canals shall be established with dimensions appropriate to watersheds and according to Flood TWiG guidelines. Establishment of cast canal on the main and subsidiary roads in camps using concrete and covering it with metal cast, and connecting them to drainage network to be established in previous activity 1.1.2 to drain rain water.During designing metal casts canals, it was taken into consideration PWDs, elderlies, and children not being affected, besides not hindering cars, and services vehicles through covering them with metal cast made of font.Saed will contract these services with experienced contractors in the relevant works.
