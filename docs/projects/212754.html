Project Summary

As HNO 2024, it is reported that 16.7 million people in Syria (50% F) need assistance up from 15.3 M in 2023.As HNO 2023, over 85% of households are struggling to meet basic needs due to the rising prices of food and essential goods, followed with insufficient income, where in 51% of households across the country, only one member is earning an income.The socio-economic impact of the 13-year crisis has been adverse effects on the economy and social protection systems. Unemployment rate in Syria witnessed a marginal decrease to 13.50% in 2023 from 13.80% in 2022, while the highest recorded was 15.30% in 2020.The Continuing Education Center (CEC) established by AKF in 2015, and conducted a market assessment to identify employers' needs and skills required. Annually. A forthcoming labor market assessment is planned to validate existing courses,identify new training programs,and explore barriers to fostering a more inclusive economy.CEC's course content is developed by AKF in collaboration with specialized organizations such as(IECD).The focus is on applied Tertiary Short Cycle Education(TSCE), which involves breaking programs into modules lasting from 2 weeks to 6 months, with the objective of cultivating specific skills for immediate employment.It enable learners to swiftly enter employment and return for more advanced modules.Now,the CEC offers 48 courses of different vocations and subjects, household electricity, carpentry, motorcycle repair, solar PV,etc.The project aims to enhance the employable skills of targeted youth(18-35) by increasing access to advanced training programs and new livelihood development opportunities for 400 crisis-affected individuals(50%F) (5% PWD) using baseline evaluations and community-based screening methods that are transparent, inclusive, and gender- and disability-sensitive and will have the opportunity to 1)undertake market-relevant vocational, life skills, English programs, and entrepreneurship training;2)Apply to AKF’s Apprenticeship programmes;3) receive toolkits to facilitate self-employment The implementation of activities will include:-Conduct comprehensive labor market assessment (LMA)to validate selection of vocations opportunities and the required skills and disseminate findings-Update and develop CEC courses based on the outcomes of LMA-Identify extremely vulnerable male and female beneficiaries to participate in the project’s interventions based on score-based selection criteria-Provide vulnerable youth with employable skills, training programs, apprenticeship and self-employment skills that will enable them to meet basic needs and secure future work- Provide vulnerable youth with English and IT training programs to enhance their skills and meet the requirement of new job opportunities -Provide toolkits to youth male and female training graduates to establish or recover their micro-businesses-Provide Career and Entrepreneurship Counselling Services for graduates from TEVET, English, and IT programs.Protection Risk Analysis: Breakdown of social cohesion within the district. Likelihood: L. Impact: M. AKF will ensure diversity by targeting various groups in coordination with CBOs and stakeholders.-Gender-related norms may limit women's participation in some training. Likelihood: Medium. Impact: Medium. AKF will ensure the participation of all community groups in awareness sessions on gender, social roles, and positive results of female participation in this kind of training. AKF will ensure that female beneficiaries are comfortable by choosing what they prefer. AKF will maintain regular monitoring of women training environment and active feedback and complaint mechanisms. In addition, Feedback and Complaint mechanism will provide updates on the implementation and reduce barriers.-LNGOs are accused of bias in selecting beneficiaries, Likelihood: H, Impact: M. AKF will manage the selection process to ensure precise implementation based on the selection criteria
