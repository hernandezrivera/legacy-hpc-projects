Project Summary

This project aims at mitigation and prevention of protection risks through provision of IPA and integrate other protection activities including, Housing, land and property(HLP), mine risk education Programming using community based approaches to ensure conflict sensitivity and accountability to the affected population and the activities will be targeting the most conflict and flood affected communities in four counties of Jonglei State particularly in Duk, Pigi and Twic East., as well as two counties in Unity State that is Mayendit and Leer.
Mobile Humanitarian Agency(MHA) will use the existing community-based structures established under the current and previous SSHF programing, and will establish new community based protection networks(CBPNs) in areas where such structures were non-existent. Working with CBPNs at the community level is proven to be the right approach to better understand various protection needs in the communities including their access to humanitarian and social services.
MHA will carry out routine protection monitoring in all the project locations to identify the potentials protection concern including the use of community members as key informants to identifying protection- related information that will informed our decision to prepare the community, youth and women, men, boys and girls to prevent and minimize the negative impacts of the risks to an acceptable level where a risk is difficult to be avoided or to be eliminated.
Assessments gather data on issues of safety, protection, needs, meaningful access to services, and freedom of movement of beneficiaries to access services and to identify the specific protection concerns that will need to be addressed promptly.. On HLP, MHA will work with the affected communities of returnees and IDPs in various location and assess their both in village and urban setting. IDPs and returnees in the rural areas are in need of shelter construction which will be provided based on critical need. In urban setting, MHA provide legal awareness session to the affected communities, provide legal assistance and legal aid and also ensure communities have access to services such as civil registration and are able to recover lost documents and property.
The purpose of the project is also to engage with youth, family affected by separation and women to achieve a long term protection outcome. MHA believes youths can play key roles in community peacebuilding and peaceful co-existence in including prevention of GBV if they are provided with skills and knowledge. youth make public opinion regarding the essence of peace and humanity, can lead peace movement against racism, and militarism, spread the idea of peace and non-violence, work for democracy, human rights and gender justice.
Strengthen the fight against discrimination, poverty, gender injustices, malnutrition, and hunger, provide voluntary service to the people during manmade and natural disasters, participate in public policymaking, create public awareness about the problems facing their communities and they can make a network of multicultural co-operation in economic, social and cultural areas among others.
MHA has designed unique strategies for sustainable peacebuilding and peaceful coexistence mostly among the big influx of Returnees coming back in Pigi/Canal Panyagoor/Twic East,
Leer and Mayendit Counties of Jonglei and Unity States. MHA will promote dialogue among the conflicting communities, community meetings, and Youth recreation activities utilizing the targeting both Youths in Schools and out of school. MHA will also improve social-economic standards for youth who spend most of their time on
unproductive activities which sometimes force them to engage in bad protection coping mechanisms. They will be trained on skills like carpentry, hairdressing, handcraft making, tailoring, mechanics among others depending on market research survey findings which MHA will
conduct to find out apprenticeship opportunities.

1 - Needs

Reference to the HNO 2022, 5.6 million people are in critical needs of protection services across the country. looking into specific needs in the proposed project locations, there are huge gaps in Pigi, Duk and Twic East and the two counties of Mayendit, and Leer in Unity State. Various humanitarian needs assessments have been conducted in the four counties of Jonglei that have highlighted dire humanitarian situation caused by communal violence and flooding, however continuous displacement by the flood and intercommunal violence keep on increase the caseload and the humanitarian needs and such has overwhelmed the capacity on the ground and additional resources will be needed to cope up with the situation. In locations such as Southern Pigi county, humanitarian need is huge and as seen in Poktap, Duk Payuel, Pajut, Twic East Islands and areas bordering GPAA South East of Bor South County. Accessing place like Duk and Duk Island is one of the major challenge as it is not under UNHAS schedule while there is critical need that include to establish a static presence in those locations for timely response to address the needs of affected population particularly in this hard to reach location with only limited humanitarian partners in protection, GBV, HLP and Child Protection and that has left wide gaps in the basic needs. There are various unverified estimated number of IDPs and returnees in the areas of; Mayendit and, Leer and as of February, huge influx of population has been witnessed in Leer and Mayendit counties due to communal violence, which widely believed to be proxy war by the government against Oppositions controlled areas, several villages in Leer County were burned to ashes, and humanitarian supplies were looted. An estimated of 6479 HHs displaced to Leer Town and most of these IDPs are in Leer town and others are seeking shelter inside Leer Temporary Protection Area with deplorable humanitarian situation. There is need for protection services and advocacy for access to services in the area with humanitarian partners and the local authorities. That is an addition to an already huge existing caseload and will eventually increase demand for services such FSL and other humanitarian services. The same influx of IDPs has beennoticed through our static presence in Pigi/Canal and this an opportunity to switch from the emergency phase to capacity building activities as communities are now seeming to be capable of adapting to new realities due to the changing contexts. They need to be introduced to resilience activities and sustainable projects. MHA wants to take the opportunity to apply a resilience activities approach which shall be built on the existingcommunity-based protection networks (CBPNs) formed under SSHF 2021, where the approach would be to increase the number if IPA compared to any other project indicators. In Pigi/Canal, the allocation will emphasize on the risksthat exist and how they will be tackled through full involvement of the communities in which the communities will be able to solve the issues that are affecting them by themselves. This approach will allow MHA to address risk at multiple levels, it will involve youth to be part of the problem-solving in the issues related to revenge killing, cattle raid, GBV and it will address some negative cultural norms and practices, economic, environmental, and social issues. It will also focus on the individuals to ensure they have the necessary tools to minimize the impacts of shocks and stresses. Moreover, this approach focuses attention on enhancing the capacity of local institutions, communities and individuals to adapt to shifting contexts and manage anticipated as well as unanticipated risks rather than merely focusing on vulnerabilities. 2 - Project activities

MHA will carry out routine protection monitoring with the purpose of gathering critical protection information on the general protection and safety concerns, human rights violation, access to humanitarian service, trend including IDPs, returnees and the host communities in, Duk and Twic East and Pig/Canal counties of Jonglei State as well as in locations such as Leer and Mayendit counties in Unity State. Information gathered will be gazetted and shared with partners for an informed intervention and response. The locations are hotspot for huge potential return of IDPs and returnees due to their unique geographical location and population size. Individual Protection Assistance: IPA is the response granted to a person with specific needs(PSN) after being assessed and need identified. MHA will provide in-kind and cash support to individuals identified to be highly vulnerable and exposed to protection risks. The assistance will alleviate suffering by reducing the level of exposure to the identified risk, improve conditions and ameliorate miserable situations on the individual PSN. In-kind support includes torches, and other Core relief items Cash assistance is offered on individual basis based on specific need to cover the protection desires including non-food items that address urgent short term and long term need such as medication.Referral It is a reality that all humanitarian needs are protection needs as well. Against this background, MHA refers clients who are in need of critical humanitarian services that it cannot provide. Among other referrals, MHA refers individual PSNs to access medical service to partners who can provide them. In addition, referrals are also done to S/NFI, WASH, FSL, Nutrition and other various service providers. In cases where a client is required to move from one location to another to access a certain service such as medication, transportation allowance is provided to facilitate movement of these individuals until the person is assisted. Advocacy and partnership: MHA as protection agency will advocate for the affected communities on their access to humanitarian services. This will be undertaken first by identifying humanitarian need and service gaps in the field locations. Periodic humanitarian need assessments will be done, information collected and reports shared with the respective clusters. To ensure centrality of protection, MHA will train staff from non-protection partners on critical protection topics such as protection mainstreaming and human rights to ensure effective protection and human rights mainstreaming across all sectors.Partnership, community empowerment and capacity building: Key protection leaders will be selected in the communities and form a network known as Community based Protection Network(CBPN). This body will comprise of traditional chiefs, youth representatives, women group, people living with disability and the police or local authority representatives. Housing, Land and Property Legal Counselling: MHA undertakes HLP awareness raising sessions with the person of concerns within the view of dissemination of HLP rights and other critical information. Legal Assistance: MHA will provide legal assistance to the affected clients and accompany them to claim their rights if they are unable to do so by themselves to claim their rights and appear for them in those institutions that have come in contact with them.Legal Aid: MHA will provide legal aid for clients in courts and other quasi-legal and administrative bodies. This will involve pro bro lawyers provided to clients who come into contacts with the law and other administrative authorities.

5.1. Cash and Voucher Assistance (CVA)?

Yes

5.1.1. What is the justification for using cash or vouchers instead of in kind?

Using cash in stead of in-kind gives the beneficiaries a choice of what they needs. Over the last four years, MHA through provision of cash to the person with specific protection needs( IPA) has the most effective way to response to the growing needs of the conflict-affected population especially the vulnerable one. Even though the target is very limited compared to awareness raising and other activities, the limited amount we provided as cash based intervention makes a lot of positive impact in the lives of the most vulnerable individual within shorter time possible. It is very recommendable that Cash and Voucher Assistance is strategic approach and response to alleviate the suffering on timely manner.

5.1.2. Explain how the project design considered market functionality

Our individuals protection assistance package is very small and is only use to target few individuals PSNs to cover the protection needs in order to protect them from negative coping mechanism. There are functional markets in the four geographical locations where the project will be implemented, and such will gives beneficiaries a choice to choose from the variety of commodities in the market, that in include food and other basic needs as may be required.
