Project Summary

HF (previously known as AHA) will provide shelter support by rehabilitating 500 housing Units living in the Aleppo governorate for the earthquake-affected HHs which have been affected either totally or partically.
No. of Beneficiaries/families 2500 in 2024
"(Individuals) 2500
"Male 700
"Female 1600
"Boys (Under 18) 50
"Girls (Under 18) 150
"Persons with disabilities 300
HF will target HHs that have been partially destroyed due to continued damage as a result of aerial/land attacks or the earthquake in addition to general dilapidation from poor living conditions
(HF will verify that by Local Councils and members of the host community that resides in those homes). In addition to the repair of small damages in walls, doors, broken windows, locks, and deteriorated plumbing. Mainly, shelter type grade 2 as Residential housing (single or multiple-story buildings), partially damaged or unfinished. Also, this activity will be implemented through two types of contract modalities, the rest is through a contractor for the procurement and transportation of materials, coordination of works, including hiring of staff, health and safety practices as well as a commitment to that the work is completed on time in addition to the mass works. I. The project will be targeting the most vulnerable HHs, especially women heading HH based on the previous implementation of our work; we focused on reaching them as one of the most vulnerable HH in the communities.
