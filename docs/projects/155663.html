Project Summary

The proposed action aims at supporting the coordination and information of the humanitarian community related to the socio-political crisis occuring in the two English speaking regions. IOM aims at continuing the CERF-funded DTM programme initiated in the South-West/North-West regions end of 2018 (Mobility Tracking - Baseline Assessment), to better inform humanitarian intervention carried out by humanitarian partners.
The DTM is a tool that provides vital information in order to bring efficient and effective provision of humanitarian assistant to conflict-affected populations. It will also allow to understand the magnitude and dynamics of the displacements occuring in these areas.

Needs

The displacement situation in the South-West and North-West regions still needs clarification. The humanitarian community is still facing challenges as to assess the magnitude of the displacements and related needs. IOM is currently conducting the first comprehensive DTM round in the area, covering the two regions and will have reliable data on the number of IDPs and their location within the next few weeks. However, this internal crisis has proven to trigger dynamic displacements of populations. It is therefore crucial to systematically update the information on displacements of the affected populations and their needs. This would allow a better understanding of the displacement trends and the followup on the support displaced populations have received or not.The DTM is a tool that provides vital information in order to bring efficient and effective provision of humanitarian assistant to conflict-affected populations.
