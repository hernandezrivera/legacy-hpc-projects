Project Summary

Under the CCCM cluster, BINAA is implementing a series of targeted interventions across 125 camps, carefully selected based on a comprehensive needs assessment. Drawing insights from ISIMM data, incident report summaries, and CCCM Cluster Meeting minutes, these sites have been identified as lacking robust emergency preparedness and response mechanisms. To address this, BINAA is partnering with other NGOs operating in the camps to complement funding and support these initiatives: 87,500 IDPs will be benefited from the project activities.
Enhanced Contingency Planning, Safety, and Security: This initiative involves the provision and replenishment of fire extinguishers and first aid kits across all 125 locations. Training sessions will empower 25 IDPs from each camp in the proper usage of equipment and best practices for fire prevention and management. Additionally, 70 fire points will be equipped and strategically placed within camps, constructed with durable materials like cement blocks and iron doors.
Capacity Building and Community Engagement: BINAA will establish 125 committees across the targeted camps and conduct 65 needs assessment FGDs. Furthermore, 125 awareness sessions on PSEA, AAP, and other relevant topics will be organized. Through 250 training sessions, BINAA aims to enhance the skills of the most valuable IDPs, supporting 1,250 IDPs in starting their businesses.
Site Maintenance and Emergency Preparedness: Maintenance and care committees will be established in alignment with camp populations, equipped with the necessary tools and resources to ensure effective maintenance and emergency response capabilities. 125 sites will be targeted for improving and maintaining the infrastructure inside the camp, including the following maintenance activities (drainage, lighting, and other related activities)
Camp Management Structure and System Enhancement: Training sessions will be provided to camp management members in all 125 camps to enhance overall camp management structures and systems.
Winter Season Needs Assessment: Ahead of the winter season, a comprehensive multi-sector needs assessment will be conducted in targeted camps to identify gaps and areas requiring intervention.
BINAA's plan is tailored to address urgent needs in 125 camps, strengthening their emergency preparedness and response capacities. Through strategic partnerships and targeted interventions, the project aims to enhance the safety, security, and resilience of affected populations in these areas.
