Project Summary

According to Humanitarian Needs overview(HNO) 2021,the number of people in need of GBV intervention stands at 4 milion. GBV continues to be an issue of great concern in Federal Members States (Galmudug, South West State, Jubaland, Hirashebelle) Puntland and Somaliland worsened by multiple displacements and forced evictions due to flooding and armed conflicts. Women and adolescent girls from 18 – 50 years old (including those living with disabilities) are subjected to Intimate Partner Violence, rape, sexual exploitation, sexual harassment and abuse in Middle Shabele, Lower Shabele,
Gedo, Snaag, Awdal, Biadoa, Bay, Mudug and, Banadir.
Distant water points, markets, health facilities/schools, poor lightning, lack of doors on toilets and lack of disaggregation of sanitary facilities are some of major factors that increase GBV exposure.
Low reporting of GBV cases persist due to stigmatization, victim blaming, shame and interference by clan andreligious leaders, slow access to justice and weak or lack of legal framework for theprotection of women and girls from GBV.
Women and girls living with disabilities are the most impacted during the event of COVID19 because of their severely disadvantages of exclusion and marginalization. Restrictions to movement impacted on their ability to access the limited services that meet their needs due to location, geographical and financially related factors. Isolation and disruption of services for women and girls living with disabilities ensured that they are trapped in physically unsafe and threatening environment.
The objective of the project is to Sustain the lives needs of 4 million people requiring humanitarian assistance, including 2.4 million non-IDPs, 1.6 million IDPs and people with disability across 74 districts, by ensuring safe, equitable and dignified access to food and essential services by the end of 2021. The project will strengthen case management for 1000 survivors of Gender-based
violence (GBV).The project staff who will be providing immediate assistance to the survivors and vulnerable population will be trained on case management and survivor centered approach. WOCCA with the help of community support workers will include persons with disability in the project.1000 survivors,persons with disability and vulnerable person at risk of GBV will be provided with tailored material assistance, basic emotional support and follow up on the services provided. WOCCA will also be providing 5000 solar lanterns to reduce risks to the most vulnerable members of the community and also increase outreach and build networks by awareness raising to 12,500 community members including traditional birth attendants, imams and elders to promote awareness of GBV risks and to mitigate exposure to Gender-based violence. A total of 100 service providers, teachers, community leaders and religious leaders will be trained on GBV risks
and referral pathways. 400 women at risk of GBV will be trained on life skills education to allow them cope up with difficult situations and overcome stress and pressure.

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

-Risk of no access to or awareness of protection assistance or referral services for survivors of protection incidents (i.e. GBV, child protection, evictions, explosive hazards, etc.) as mitigation measure to this risk WOCCA will focus on strengthening of gender inclusive community-based protection mechanisms and robust information campaigns to ensure impartial and timely access to protection services, also in situations of displacement -Risk that marginalized groups, persons with disabilities, women, boys and girls, do not participate in consultations or decision-making processes for the project or are excluded from humanitarian services/assistance /activities as mitigation measure WOCCA will do community engagement of all groups as a key requirement in the response mobilization phase and ensuring that marginalized groups are by default among the prioritized groups. With the help of community support workers WOCCA will carry out inclusive community and authority consultations to identify vulnerable, under-represented HHs, with specific attention to age, gender, minorities, disability and clan representation. -Risk that beneficiaries are not accessing/using complaint and feedback mechanisms as mitigation measure to this risk WOCCA with the help of community support workers will train the community members on how to use and access the complaint and feedback mechanisms so as to get feedback from the beneficiaries.
