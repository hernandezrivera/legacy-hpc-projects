Objectives

Objective 1: Ensure effective, sufficient and continuous provision of life saving water and sanitation services for targeted men, women, boys and girls.Objective 2: Ensure that all targeted women, men, girls and boys have the means and are encouraged to adopt individual and collective measures increasing health seeking behaviours to mitigate public health risks. Objective 3: Ensure that all WASH assistance promotes the protection, safety and dignity of targeted people, and is used equitably to men, women, boys and girls.

Needs

Thirty-three percent (33%) of the targeted men, women and children do not have access to a safe source of water.Repair, operation and maintenance of current tube wells and construction of piped water networks from ground water in Ukhia camps and surface treatment in Teknaf camps and host communities remain a high priority.Hygiene: Ensure optimal hygiene practices for both refugees and host communities, particularly regarding the proper use and maintenance of WASH facilities, handwashing with soap and Household Water Treatment.

Activities or outputs

Safe water supply: Water quality test at househod level. Rehabilitation and maintenance/construction of tube-well. Bucket Chlorination.Sanitation: Rehabilitation, maintenance/construction, decomisison or desludge of latrine. FSM. Hygiene: Rehabilitation, maintenance/construction of washing cubicles. Installation of handwashing station. Provision of hygiene promotion awareness, NFI including aquatabs, soap and MHM. Solid waste mangement.Community approach with WASH committees for operation and maintenance of WASH facilities and for SWM.
