Project Summary

HopeInclusion through this project envisions a transformative change for the Menchum Valley community through a targeted intervention designed to improve health and enhance quality of life by addressing urgent sanitation and hygiene needs. This initiative aim at alleviating the adverse conditions faced by the residents, seeks to renovate and enhance essential sanitation infrastructure across schools, health services and public spaces.
Background and Context
Menchum Valley like many rural communities faces significant challenges in accessing safe and adequate sanitation and hygiene facilities. Many residents lack access to clean, functioning toilets and handwashing stations creating a public health risk that affects all community members. Poor hygiene practices coupled with inadequate waste management systems exacerbate the spread of infectious diseases particularly among vulnerable groups such as school children, elderly persons, patients in health services and persons with disabilities. The effects of these conditions go beyond health impacting education, productivity and overall well-being.
Purpose of the Project
The primary goal of this project is to improve community health and well-being by addressing the most pressing sanitation and hygiene challenges in Menchum Valley. Through comprehensive renovation and construction efforts, HopeInclusion aims to create a sustainable environment where every resident can thrive in dignity and health. The purpose of the project includes:
- Reducing Disease Transmission: Poor sanitation is a known vector for diseases such as cholera, diarrhea and other waterborne illnesses. This project aims to mitigate these risks by providing clean and safe facilities that encourage healthier hygiene practices.

- Improving Access to Education: Children, especially girls, often miss school due to inadequate sanitation facilities, impacting their education and future opportunities. The project seeks to improve school attendance and create a conducive learning environment by installing gender-segregated toilets and handwashing stations in schools.
- Promoting Dignity and Inclusivity: Access to clean and accessible toilets and handwashing stations is a basic human right. By improving these facilities, the project aims to uphold the dignity of Menchum Valley residents ensuring that even the most vulnerable can meet their sanitation needs safely.
- Fostering Sustainable Waste Management: The project addresses the lack of structured waste disposal systems, reducing environmental contamination and promoting sustainable practices. This includes installing waste separation facilities, encouraging composting of organic waste and ensuring proper disposal of non-organic waste.
Targeted Beneficiaries
The Menchum Valley Sanitation and Hygiene Project targets the entire community with special emphasis on vulnerable and high-impact groups such as persons with disabilities, children, women and healthcare workers. By focusing on schools, health services and community centers, the initiative ensures that essential public spaces are equipped to meet the community’s sanitation needs.
- Schools: Inadequate sanitation in schools leads to lower attendance and affects student health and well-being. The project will equip schools with modern toilets, handwashing stations and hygiene education programs which will encourage healthier habits from a young age.
- Health Services: Health facilities need to maintain high hygiene standards to prevent the spread of infections. The project will provide these health centres with adequate toilets and handwashing stations to ensure a safe environment for patients and healthcare workers alike.
- Community Centers: These hubs of social interaction are ideal for instilling good hygiene practices within the wider community. By equipping community centres with modern sanitation facilities, the project will foster a wide culture of cleanliness and health consciousness.

1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

HopeInclusion goal of improving sanitation and hygiene infrastructure in Menchum Valley is underpinned by a comprehensive assessment of community needs. This intervention will focus on critical areas such as inadequate sanitation facilities, lack of clean water and handwashing stations, poor waste management and insufficient hygiene awareness. Each of these factors poses significant health, educational, social and economic challenges for the community contributing to a cycle of poverty, illness and limited opportunitie. The assessment will examine the specific needs the project will address through its planned activities, ultimately contributing to a healthier and more resilient community. 1. Inadequate Sanitation Facilities Current Situation: Menchum Valley suffers from a severe lack of functional and safe toilets both in households and public spaces such as schools, health services and community centers. Existing facilities are outdated, poorly maintained and some are completely unusable. In many cases open defecation remains a common practice further exacerbating public health risks. Need for Intervention: Access to adequate sanitation is a basic human right and its absence has severe repercussions on individual dignity, health and safety. For instance, school children especially girls face challenges in attending school regularly due to a lack of proper sanitation facilities. Women and girls are particularly vulnerable to gender-based violence when forced to seek isolated or unsafe areas to relieve themselves. The project aims to address this need by renovating current facilities and building new ones in schools, health centres and community centers ensuring accessibility for all, including people with disabilities. Project Activities: - Renovate and construct gender-segregated and accessible toilets in public spaces. - Equip schools with adequate sanitation facilities to support student attendance and retention particularly for girls. - Ensure facilities are durable and easy to maintain, fostering long-term usability and sustainability. 2. Lack of Clean Water and Handwashing Stations Current Situation: Handwashing is a simple but effective practice for preventing the spread of diseases yet access to clean water and handwashing stations in Menchum Valley is limited. In schools and health centres where hygiene is especially critical, handwashing facilities are insufficient or absent. This lack of infrastructure leaves community members, including children, healthcare workers and patients vulnerable to infectious diseases. Need for Intervention: Clean water and accessible handwashing stations are essential to maintaining personal hygiene and preventing illness. Inadequate handwashing facilities contribute to the spread of infectious diseases including diarrhea, respiratory infections and skin conditions. The absence of these facilities in schools leads to higher absenteeism among children and impacts their educational experience. Project Activities: - Install handwashing stations equipped with clean water and soap dispensers in strategic locations, including school entrances, health centres, waiting areas and community center. - Educate community members on the importance of handwashing as part of the broader hygiene awareness efforts. - Ensure that handwashing stations are well-maintained and accessible to all, promoting consistent and widespread use. 3.
