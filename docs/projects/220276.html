Project Summary

CORE will deliver multi-sectoral emergency assistance to support recovery efforts in Carriacou, Grenada following widespread, intensive damage across the island in the wake of Hurricane Beryl. Grounded in the priorities of UN-OCHA's Humanitarian Response Plan for Hurricane Beryl, CORE will focus on restoring access to safe and dignified shelter (Strategic Objective 1: Provide life-saving multi-sectoral assistance) and livelihoods recovery (Strategic Objective 2: Support the rapid delivery and resumption of essential services and livelihoods in hurricane-affected areas.) for affected small/micro business entrepreneurs and smallholder farmers. Local CSOs and contractors will be engaged whenever possible across all activities. Project Activities

Debris and Rubble Removal for 400HH, Cash-for-Work emergency livelihoods support for 50 day laborers (15 days of labor each) to support the debris/rubble removal activity., Distribution of Shelter Repair Kits to 150HH that sustained minor to moderate damage., Construction of transitional shelter units to 150HH that sustained moderate to major housing damage to ensure access to dignified temporary shelter while homes are rebuilt., Cash grants to 50 small/micro business entrepreneurs and smallholder farmers to support foods security and livelihoods recovery from damages/loss, increase supply of essential goods and services, and facilitate resumption of agricultural production.
