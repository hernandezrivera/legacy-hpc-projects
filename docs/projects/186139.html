Project Summary

The project aims at ensuring the that women, girls, boys and men are able to meet their basic needs; and are safe in their communities and receive support when they experience harm in the targeted locations in the South West region. The project will provide multipurpose cash assistance, water, sanitation and hygiene, gender-based violence (GBV) and protection programming to beneficiaries to help them meet their basic needs, be safer in their communities, and receive support when they experience harm.

1 - Besoins / Needs

The North West and South West Regions have faced significant levels of violence and insecurity since the escalation of the fighting in 2017 between the Government forces and non-state armed groups. The violence continues to be the highest driver of displacement, with more than 700,000 people displaced, including as the displacement of Cameroonian refugees in Nigeria as well as internal displacement in other regions of Cameroon. The ongoing violence perpetrated by armed actors, combined with pre-existing vulnerabilities, endemic poverty , and cultural/traditional norms, presents significant risks, especially displaced women and girls. Between January and August 2021, 7,534 cases of gender-based violence (GBV) were recorded in the GBVIMS by UNFPA in the three regions, with 1,164 (15%) reported by IRC, along with other human rights violations (e.g., theft, physical violence, kidnapping, looting of properties) which displacement increases vulnerability to. An increase in protection incidents affecting children was also reported in 2021 as the security situation continued to deteriorate. This, combined with already precarious socio-economic conditions faced by many households living in conflict-affected areas limits children and youth’s access to social services, education and livelihood opportunities and further undermines their safety and well-being . According to the HNO, 1.7 million people require humanitarian assistance to access safe drinking water, and adequate basic sanitation and hygiene services. Displaced populations are particularly at risk of diseases as they do not have access to adequate shelter, households, and hygiene items. Food security and populations’ capacities to meet their basic needs as well generating income is severely impacted by insecurity, ongoing conflict and COVID 19; and the NW/SW regions are expected to be in crisis phase (IPC 3) between October 2021 and March 2022 due to higher prices in the market and early depletion of crops, and FN region in stressed phase (IPC2).
