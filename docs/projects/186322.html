Project Summary

According to the MSNA conducted in August 2021 conducted by IOM/OCHA in the North West and South West, West and Littoral regions, there are more than 509,000 persons displaced due to the conflict. According to the Shelter cluster analysis and targets, more than 78,000 IDP need shelters and Core Relief Items (CRIs). In addition to the IDPs, more that 48,000 returnees and 89,000 host communities would also need shelter and CRIs. The project will target both IDPs/returnees (80% of the assistance) and host communities (20% of the assistance).
The category of IDP/returnees includes population self-settled in rural and urban areas who are either living in makeshift shelters with little or no basic household items, displaced/returnees population in host families mostly living in overcrowded and cramped situation (as many as 10 persons in single rooms), displaced/returnees population renting accommodation at risk of eviction as they don’t usually have formal contracts and inability to pay rents due to economic hardship.

Also, of interest are vulnerable host families who are under pressure as family sizes have increased without any corresponding support, there are non-displaced population with partially damaged houses who are in informal settlements, with host families or renting accommodation. Unmet shelter needs worsen protection risks: most of the people require support to restore privacy and dignity, they also have safety and security concerns. UNHCR will be providing inclusive, community-based shelter, CRIs support accompanied by community empowerment initiatives to internally displaced persons and their host in the North West and South West Regions of Cameroon.
The overcrowding of families in the shelters lead to the increase of several risks including Covid-19. To prevention the Covid-19, UNHCR will also distribute some covid-19 protective items.
UNHCR as Shelter cluster lead works with other cluster members and brings together local authorities with UN agencies and NGOs to coordinate and improve the provision of emergency shelters. The result will be a harmonized effort to support affected people to receive adequate housing and live in a dignified manner.
UNHCR will distribute CRIs-basic household items, shelter kits- provision of construction materials for the reconstruction of damaged structures and provision of technical support, support in the construction of transitional shelters through a community-based approach, cash assistance for multipurpose use will also be provided as this will help alleviate the economic pressure on the beneficiaries. It will also have the propensity to give the IDPs the possibility of choice and enhance their economic resilience and purchasing power
The crisis has not only resulted in the displacement of persons but has had a negative impact on the economy of the regions thereby impacting the source of livelihood of IDPs and the host communities. The crisis has particularly affected the agricultural sector with most plantations closing. For the implementation of the project, UNHCR will coordinate and collaborate with state line ministries, local NGOs, and UN agencies with relevant expertise.

1 - Besoins / Needs

The need for shelter and basic household items remains huge with over 509,000 internally displaced within the Littoral, West, North West and South West region according to MSNA conducted in august 2021. In addition to the IDPs UNHCR also intends to support host communities who have become overwhelmed with the presence IDPs in their homes and communities. Given that the economic power of both the IDPs and host communities have been negatively affected there is need for economic support through vocational training thereby increasing the technical skills of the affected population. The support provided will target 80% of IDPs and 20% of host population who will include the most vulnerable like elderly persons without family support, overcrowded households. Children, female headed households, and persons with disabilities. UNHCR intervention will consider displace persons and host living in makeshift shelter, damaged building, and those in overcrowded houses. NFI/Shelter support; UNHCR will provide both in kind and in cash support to 20 000 IDPs and 2000 host community members to support in the construction of damaged structures, extension of accommodation and emergency shelter kits for new displacements and provision of basic household items. In addition, 1000 IDPs will be supported through multipurpose cash assistance. In this manner the beneficiaries The provision of shelter/NFI will improve the living conditions, improve the physical security of IDPs, wellbeing, the dignity of the affected population and reduce the risks of GBV
