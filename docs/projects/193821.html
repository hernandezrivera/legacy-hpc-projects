Project Summary

Environmental Initiative Organization for Sustainable Development (EnvI) focuses on Environment, CC, WASH & Health, is also working on integrated approaches that support live-saving, recovery and sustainable development. EnvI applies participatory approach that engages all stakeholders; ensuring their contribution & participation in all levels of the project design, implementation & monitoring.
This project will help the effected population those exposed to the conflict between the Guamaa' & Shanabla in addition to an estimated 19,000 people who fled the tribal conflict between the Gimir and Arab tribes in West Darfur’s Kulbus locality spread to North Kordofan. So the project is targeting Um Dum IDPs and the neighbouring host communities. The project targets seven villages, which are ElSarafi, Eltekeil, Abugundeia, Um Geregeir, Alzareiba Compund for Nomads, Umbarakat and Um Dum town. The project is planned to promote peaceful and coexistence and reducing any potential tensions and it will provide effective protection to contribute through implementation of WASH interventions. Though EnvI an active member in the WASH coordination sector/ Cluster, where EnvI leader/ CEO is a member of SAG/WASH (Strategic Advisory Committee) and member of AQA Initiative/WASH (TWG).
EnvI and the active stakeholders will keep tracking the project's performance, progress & challenges and will provide the immediate resolutions for any emerging difficulties.
In addition, EnvI will carry consultations with target IDPs and host communities throughout the project cycle to ensure their active participation in planning, implementation and monitoring.
EnvI will ensure the participation of all social categories including; women, men, boys and girls, youths and People with Special Needs, to allow them discuss issues that contribute to their well being.
EnvI will develop effective feedback system to consider all voices of the target population, will activate the complaint mechanism to provide immediate decisions and will document the best practices & learned lessons and will share them with all stakeholders & donors.
EnvI has high calibre WASH team who has good knowledge & linkages of the target area & population, where EnvI implemented Nurseries and Production and Planting of Fruit Seedling ground. As well, EnvI implemented a number of projects and Media Outreach in the States & poses good reputation & trust.
The project aim consider to be a transit Fair Area between the Capital Khartoum and the West of Sudan. The population are ( %) of Males, and ( )% of Females. Most of the Community are Defecated in Open Area. The project will implement Interventions on WASH activities, considering the Climate Change Mitigation and Adaptation, and will apply Hygiene and Mitigatation measurement through inhibit the GHG Emissions from Slaughtering Houses through Rehabilitate them.

1 - Needs

During last September 2022, EnvI carried simple need assessment and the main results were; Um Dum Locality represents reciprocal of IDPs from many areas, especially after last conflict between the Guamaa' & Shanabla tribes on lands. They are mostly farmers and Pastoralists. Those at most needs for sanitation services, water supply for drinking and hygiene knowledge including COVID-19 mitigation measurements. The direct targ5eted population will be 21,600 includes those with special Needs (2%) and indirectly the project will benefit about 20,000 people.

2 - Activities/Outputs

The armed conflict between the Guamaa' & Shanabla in North Kordofan and in addition to an estimated 19,000 people who fled the tribal conflict between the Gimir and Arab tribes in West Darfur’s Kulbus affected Um Dum locality, where more than 1000 families fled the conflict and stayed in UM Dum Locality. Um Dum locality is a pathway for nomads who move across the locality during their routine movement for pastures & water. This puts pressure on the very limited of water sources that creates some conflict over the limited water sources and also increases the open defecation that affect the health of the locality inhabitants. This worsening situation exposes the lives of the people to more risk and created conflict over resources.Improving the water supplies and provision of sanitation & hygiene services will improve the health of the target population & will reduce the conflict over resources.The project aims to achieve three results, which are; target HHs will have access to *basic water services; having access to limited sanitation services and having access to hand washing facilities with soap. The water activities will include; establishment of two water yards, digging and installation of two hand pumps, rehabilitation of two hand pumps, colorization of at least four water sources, distribution of dignity kits for IDPs women & girls, construction /distribution of hand-washing units, distribution Jeri cans & multi-puropse soap to effected poor households, Rehabilitation of three Slaughtering Houses, construction of 30 improved communal latrines, Solid waste Management and Treatment Compost. EnvI will ensure the participation of all social categories including; women, men, boys and girls, youths and People with Special Needs, to allow them discuss issues that contribute to their wellbeing.EnvI will develop effective feedback system to consider all voices of the target population, will activate the complaint mechanism to provide immediate decisions and will document the best practices & learned lessons and will share them with all stakeholders & donors.EnvI has high calibre WASH team who has good knowledge & linkages of the target area & population, where EnvI implemented Nurseries and Production and Planting of Fruit Seedling ground. As well, EnvI implemented a number of projects and Media Outreach in the States & poses good reputation & trust.
