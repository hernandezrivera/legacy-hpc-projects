Project Summary

Al-Birr Hospital was built, operated and owned by Al-Birr Association since 1994 in order to provide all health services (primary and secondary) to all citizens, especially the poor patients and the patients from northern rural Homs, with high quality and in a manner, that preserves their dignity without any discrimination.
The hospital has 150 beds. It is the largest private and charitable hospital in Syria.
Before the crisis in Al-Waer, Al-Birr hospital was visited by a patients from all of Homs city (60% of them from rural Homs) since it's equipped with modern equipment, and has the best staff of doctors and nurses; especially after the damage of most public health centers and public hospitals. Al-Birr hospital was providing all kind of health care including primary, secondary and even tertiary health care services. It was providing all kinds of surgeries and medical care including open cardiac surgery, cardiac catheterization, and specific operations such as neurosurgery.
During the crisis period, the hospital was severely affected because of its location in Al-Waer area, which forced the association to stop working in some departments and limit the services to some services such as dialysis, especially in the last five years.
After the agreement of Al-Waer neighborhood, A lot of people returned to their homes in Al-Waer, so Al-Birr has started rehabilitating this hospital after years of operating another hospitals in Homs city (Farkouh, Al-Kindi and Al-Shawi).
Till now, there is no enough public care centers in Homs city, where most of public hospitals there is just providing some primary services such as outpatient clinics and laboratory, while urgent secondary care services are not provided so patients have to transfer to other private hospitals out of Homs to get services.
Anyway, the kind of services will be provided in Homs city and will help people not just in Homs governorate but also in other governorates since Homs is in the middle of Syria and people do not have to travel to Damascus for obtaining necessary and urgent health services such as catheterization, neurosurgery, lithotripsy and other services.
Because of the great damage in the hospital, Al-Birr has signed an agreement with local donor (chamber of industry in Homs) to provide full rehabilitation of its building, in order to operate other departments in the hospital (ICU, NICU, surgical department, cardiosurgery and catheterization, surgical ICU, hospitalization rooms, gynecology, pediatrics departments.
The rehabilitation process is an urgent issue to integrate the health services that Al-Birr hospital provides. These services will match the critical needs and will present with high quality.
Al-Birr hospital has all the equipment since it did its best to save its equipment. While many of these equipment are working, some of them need maintenance or is old-fashioned and need to be updated. AL-Birr will provide primary and secondary health care to all people in need.
This project is to maintain essential devices, operate the different sections of the hospital and provide various health services for free for people in need.

3 - Needs

With more than 164,000 confirmed cases and close to 5,800 deaths190, COVID-19 continues to disrupt the already-fragile health services and systems . (HNO, 2022). Due to cumulative effects of concurrent crises, cost of health services; lack of medicines, supplies or health staff; and fear of exposure to COVID-19 have replaced distance, over-crowding and long wait times ,Preparedness planning, contingency stocks and emergency operation centers (EOCs) are critical readiness components for public health emergencies. (HNO, 2022) So, reoperating a large hospital, and providing services for needy people is a very important work that may help in enhancing the situation, especially in governorate of Homs. While the crisis has affected all people living in Syria to varying degrees, Displaced populations and returnees, particularly those in northern and eastern countryside of Homs been more acutely affected and all population groups inside these areas are still in-need to get health services with minimal wages. Al-Birr hospital is one of the largest Charity Hospitals in the middle east with a capacity of 150 beds and it consists of 10 floors. Al-Birr Hospital have been damaged severely during the crisis. Al-Birr Hospitals was providing services to all people in Homs and it's rural with minimal wages.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

Al-Birr will make sure that all staff and contractors will sign a code of contact in order to not use any kind of violence while implementing their activities. 2. Al-Birr M&E team will make sure that there is no children labor through rehabilitation work Loss or unauthorized access to data of patients. likelihood: low, impact: medium. mitigating measures: 1. protecting electronic data by username and passwords which show any use of data. 2. coding the data and encrypting them, while unencryption should be through the management of the hospital


8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

risk: sexual exploitation and abuse for people in need to get the benefit of referrals and services. likelihood: medium, impact: high. 1. Al-Birr will implement its PSEA planned strategy where all its staff have to sign on code of conduct for PSEA in order to not use any kind of sexual exploitation and abuse while implementing their activities. 2. According to PSEA proposed strategy, a PSEA focal point will supervise training staff and raising their awareness about PSEA strategy, in addition to his role in management of complains regarding any sexual exploitation or abuse occurred during the implementation


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk of fluctuation of prices, so Al-Birr will contract with contractors at the beginning of rehabilitation process in order to save money and use money for the best targets. 2- Risk of lack of specialized doctors, probability: medium , impact: high, mitigation measures: Al-Birr has its own staff of specialized doctors who have worked in its facilities for years. 3- Risk of lack of access to the hospital especially for vulnerable people, probability: low, impact: medium, mitigation measures: Al-Birr can use its ambulance, and coordinate with its branches and offices located in the wide geography of the governorate
