Project Summary

AMAN (Association for Mass Advancement Network) has formulated the Shelter/NFI plan for JRP 2024 with the following objectives: promoting inclusive engagement, accountability, and community empowerment through participatory approaches and feedback mechanisms; providing safe living conditions to Rohingya refugees/FDMNs and targeted host community assistance, including shelter and LPG assistance, to reduce exposure to physical and protection-related risks.
The planned activities have been targated in total for 4,815 individual Rohingya refugees that focus on constructing 300 New Mid-Term Shelters (MTS) for 1,443 individual out of which Solar and porter support for 958 individuals (200 HHs), and NFI support for all targeted beneficiaries in camp 8W (Block B, C, D), Camp 15 (C, D, E, F), Camp 16 (C, D), Camp 17 (B, C) Coordination with sectors, CiC, and other stakeholders will be key in this process. NFIs, including mosquito net, blankets, hoodies, shawls, palazzo, and skirts, will be provided with special consideration for People with Special Needs (PwSN).
The construction of New MTS will be based on family size, including Site Improvement (SI), targeting 300 households (HHs) and involving labor and porter support. Solar and porter support will be extended to 200 Extremely Vulnerable Households (EVHHs) in 8W (Block B, C, D), and Camp 17 (B, C) following guidelines and strategic objectives of the Energy & Environment Network (EEN) through proper coordination. Clothes distribution will include women, older people, people with disabilities, and other forms of gender-diverse populations, addressing the needs of PwSN and considering the culturally and gender appropriate NFIs. Separate data sheets, questionnaires, and need assessments will be utilized for these initiatives. Proposed activities including community participation and empowement for Rohingya Refugees will be ensured through their active involvement in project implementation providing need based training and sensitization. As per gender age group adolescent and women along with elderly will be provided training on GBV issues and above all social cohesion thus they will be aware of their rights and their roles in the Communities in regards of achieving Shelters and NFI objectives.
To achieve these targets, AMAN has allocated an estimated budget of $ 310,208 for Rohingya refugees (4,815 individuals) in the year 2024. The plan emphasizes gender responsiveness, inclusion of people with disabilities and elderly individuals, and consideration of extremely vulnerable households in all Shelter/NFI activities. EVHHs will be identified through systematic door-to-door assessments in coordination with SM focal points.
AMAN ensures that shelters are accessible and appropriate for persons with disabilities and older persons by following the Protection Mainstreaming Tip Sheet prescribed by the SNFI sector. Capacity building training, attended by AMAN's staff, and training in Safeguard/PSEA contribute to proper protection mainstreaming. AMAN emphasizes the collection, analysis, and use of disaggregated data on gender, age, and diversity, along with consulting women, girls, and marginalized groups during assessments and in the overall response monitoring phase.

1. Needs
1 - Identified Needs

Rohingya refugees in Bangladesh have been grappling with humanitarian challenges for over five years. Despite efforts to meet basic needs, the focus remains on addressing ongoing necessities, particularly Non-Food Items (NFIs). Limited access to income and livelihood opportunities persists, placing a strain on the refugees' resilience. The year 2023 has posed severe tests, including a destructive fire, cyclone (Mocha), and continuous monsoon rains causing significant damage to shelters and belongings. A Need Assessment report highlights shelter as the top priority after Cyclone Mocha, emphasizing the persistent threats from natural disasters like floods and landslides.Joint-Multi Sector Needs Assessment (J-MSNA) Bangladesh, 19 October 2023 report shows that Still for Rohingya Refugee population, having issues with their shelter (72%) and shelter materials/upgrade as their priority needs. Of the 41% of refugee households not improving their shelters, the majority reported not improving it due to lack of humanitarian support (60%) or insufficient support (34%). Refugee households in camps 20 (59%), 18 (58%), 8W (56%), and 4 (55%) were some of the most commonly reporting not improving their shelters for those reasons. Households’ reported top reasons why their shelter is not fully functional for sleeping - 69% Rain water leaking in the sleeping area, 42% Insufficient space. Households’ reported top reasons why their shelter is not fully functional for cooking - 66% Rain water leaking in the kitchen area, 34% Lack of access to cooking facilities. Households’ reported top reasons why their shelter is not fully functional for electricity - 66% Intermittent and insufficient, 35% No electricity and no solar lamp. % of refugee households reporting NFI needs whereas, Mosquito nets 58%, Kitchen sets 53%, Blankets 45%, Solar lamps/panels 43%, Bedding items 37%, Batteries 37%. As per the report “Assessment on NFIs in the Rohingya Camps: May-July 2022 by IOM” High Priority needs show: 30% of the HHs prioritized solar fans, and 24% prioritized HH-level solar systems. Medium Priority needs show: solar-powered fans were mentioned by 23% of HHs. Mosquito nets, kitchen sets and portable solar light were mentioned by 14% of HHs.Based on the above information AMAN would like to construct 300 new MTS and HH level Site Improvement (SI) for 1,443 individual Rohingya Refugees; NFI support including solar lamps for 4,815 individual Rohingya Refugees with porter support including EVHHs; New MTS in camp 8w, 15, 16, and 17 will be built based on the Shelter/NFI Sector assessment to target only completely damaged shelters and if it may not reach the planned target and in this case AMAN will coordinate with the Sector to include other camp based on needs and discussion with the Focal Point organizations as well as operating partners to reach the target.In an effort to address the unequal impact of the normative framework on Rohingya Refugees, this project by AMAN aims to distribute in-kind gifts, including mosquito net, blankets, shawls, solar light & fan, and winter clothes following the sector guidelines whereas, AMAN will ensure activities are coordinated with the operating partners. The project emphasizes gender diversity and the inclusion of people with disabilities. The normative framework disproportionately affects women and girls, limiting their mobility, decision-making power, and access to essential services. The project aims to rectify this by conducting inclusive planning with women leaders and stakeholders, implementing right-based strategies, and providing culturally and gender-appropriate Non-Food Items. Training on gender-based violence issues and promoting social cohesion will empower women, adolescents, and the elderly to be aware of their rights and roles in achieving shelter and NFI objectives within their communities.
