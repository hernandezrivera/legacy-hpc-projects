Project Summary

In proposed critical & live saving primary health care project, the immediate specific health needs & concerns of women, men girls, boys and vulnerable groups of the returning TDPs communities were identified in Vulnerability assessment 2017 report and through HeRAMS assessment in FATA districts, and on the basis of that baseline information, project intervention were designed to cater the needs of most vulnerable populations, especially women, adolescent girls and children, with improved and sustained access to quality and timely Primary health care services in 4 identified non-functional BHU’s in District South Waziristan. Currently these BHU’s buildings need repair & maintenance and are highly non accessible physically for elderly women, men and Person with disabilities. The services proposed will be provided in parallel to necessary repair and physical accessibility work in BHUs. Those revitalized health facilities will provide treatment to the patients from the affected population on prevailing health conditions such as diarrhea, dysentery, fever, cough and cold (air borne and water borne diseases), other communicable and non-communicable diseases, provision of Antenatal and postnatal care, safe delivery, immunization and health and hygiene awareness and education. The Human resource in each health facility will comprise of 1 Doctor, 1 LHV, 1 Medical Technician,1 guard and 1 support staff, while 2 Psychosocial counselors and 1 Pharmacist support will be available on rotation basis. Primary health care medicine including MNCH, RH medicines and supplies will be procured and provided in OPD. Besides this, to reach the unreachable community, medical camps will be held on need basis (1 camp every week). Strong referral mechanism will be established from community to first level care facility (FLCF) & from FLCF to secondary or tertiary level care facility by ensuring dedicated ambulance service. The Women and family members will receive counseling services and psycho-social support. For Person with disabilities the accessibility will be ensured and their data will be recorded and will be provided assistance and referral. The LHV’s will provide awareness session in OPD on Birth preparedness and prevention of complications. The staff will be trained & training material will be provided on antenatal and postnatal care, safe delivery, immunization, use of modern methods of contraceptives, early detection & prevention of childhood disabilities, efficient referral mechanism and disease reporting (DEWS). Training will be provided to TBAs on Nutritional support (including iron and folate supplements), Prenatal recognition of danger signs and treatment or referral as needed, Clean delivery (hand-washing, clean space, clean cord care) and efficient use of safe delivery kits further enabling them to play vital role in reducing morbidity and mortality rate (MMR and IMR) in the areas of intervention. The village Health Advisory Committee comprise of women, men girls, boys and vulnerable groups will be oriented on their role in project implementation and monitoring of PHC and MNCH services and community mobilization. In culturally appropriate manner, trained Social mobilizers/Hygiene Promoter will provide 512 health & hygiene awareness sessions to men and women separately in order to educate them about best hygiene practices i.e. personal and environment hygiene for their own safe health and protection from common communicable and water borne diseases.

Needs

The project will focus on needs of returnees’ and displaced populations’ in tribal districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa have no access to emergency essential health care particularly identified in a. Vulnerability assessment. b. HeRAMS

Activities

1). Making health facilities physically functional through repair, rehabilitation & accessible renovation. 2). Provision of integrated primary health care, maternal and child health (MNCH) and reproductive health services in accessible environment through skilled human resources (50% female, 50% male). 3). Strengthening Referral mechanism and emergency through Ambulance services. 4). Supply chain for free medicines and medical supplies along with necessary medical equipment, furniture & solar system for power backup. 5). Prevention of diseases in communities through Hygiene promotion sessions & Increase access to routine immunization, particularly for children and women. 6). Medical & paramedical human resource (50% female, 50% male) training & reporting on Disease Early warning System for strengthen disease surveillance and response to outbreaks of communicable diseases 7). Provision of specialize assistance to women, men girls, boys with disabilities and low mobility in target communities i.e assistive devices provision or surgeries. 8). Training of LHVs & Midwifes on safe & clean deliveries and mother & neonatal child health. 9). Free Mobile medical camps to reach to unreachable communities or people in need in adjacent areas
