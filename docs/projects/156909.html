Project Summary

This project will support 250 farmers (2 donums per beneficiary), 20% of them at least are women, where they will be provided with seeds and seedlings for ) tomato, pepper, cucumber and zucchini crops (, training, and technical support for farmers, where Silk Road agricultural team will supervise and follow up the crop case during the whole season, supplying the farmer with fertilizer, pesticides, operating fuel and drip irrigation pipes, which are suitable for their crops.
On the other hand, agricultural training will be provided at all stages of the project to educate the farmers with correct scientific methods that must be taken into consideration in the process of agriculture, also agricultural brochures will be distributed for the farmers to raise awareness and guide them to the correct scientific methods which must be applied.
Thus, the project provides integrated services to the farmers so that they are guided, directed, and assisted in all stages during the season to achieve integration in the project and ensure the results.
We will take 12% of the total production by signing contracts with the farmers, and provide 900 households how to meet specific vulnerability criteria like IDPs, women household, families have no income generation with the summer vegetable which is tomato, pepper, cucumber and zucchini where they will receive their rations of fresh vegetables from contracted vendors by paper vouchers way.

Implementation of activities

- Distribution of seeds and seedlings for summer crops: The crops which will be distributed as seedlings are tomato and pepper, while the cucumber and zucchini will be distributed as seeds. Seed and seedlings will be distributed to farmers at the beginning of the planting season, which will be distributed to farmers by the end of March using the vouchers form to deliver the seeds and seedlings, they will receive their rations of seeds and seedlings from sellers were already contracted after meeting the specific criteria.- Distribution the pesticides (insecticides- fungicides) and agricultural fertilizers (urea fertilizers - compound fertilizers - amino acids): Farmers will receive vouchers which contain the all needed pesticides and fertilizers for their crops from contracted suppliers. Where agricultural inputs will be delivered in batches as appropriate to the crop lifecycle, stage by stage.- Distribution the drip irrigation system pipes: 1400 meters of drip irrigation pipes will be distributed to each farmer in order to contribute reducing the waste of water resources. on the other hand, this way of irrigation reduces the crop services, this agricultural input will be received through the voucher system by contracting with selected suppliers chosen after achieving specific criteria.- Distribution of diesel fuel required for the generator operation: Diesel fuel will be distributed to the farmers, where each farmer will take his ration of fuel every month from contracted suppliers, by the vouchers system."the 67 percent of the estimated food insecure people who are in the high severity districts of 4 and 5 as well as the rest of the food insecure people who will need continuous assistance in the less severe districts" according to the HNO2019.- Distribution of agricultural equipment for farmers: The farmers will receive vouchers to receive the agricultural equipment which, axe, pesticides pump and shovel, helping them to serve their crops.A strict contract will be signed with the farmers to give up a portion of the crops they produce for Silk Road Organization to support vulnerable families: The contracted farmers will deliver 12% of their production. The received quantities of fresh vegetables will be distributed to families in the same community meet specific vulnerability criteria where 900 family will receive their rations of vegetable throw the voucher system.- Farmers' awareness will be raised through activities that increase their technical knowledge like field days, brochures distribution and field visits. Field days will be conducted during the all of crop stages. We will design and distribute technical brochures that cover all aspects of crop cultivation according to the seasonal calendar, like fertilization, pests control, harvesting, ploughing.Field visits will be carried out by a specialized technical team to follow the crop through all stages to reach optimal production.Activities to be implemented in Azaz and Qabasin in the Bab due to HNO 2019-HNO-Indicator 1 for Food insecure_CARI Severity score for Azaz' is in level 3.3- HNO-Indicator 1 for Food insecure_CARI Severity score for AlBab-Qabasin is in level 6.
