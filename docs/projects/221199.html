Project Summary

The "Preventing Child Neglect and Supporting Family Unity in the conflict-affected South West and North West Regions of Cameroon" project addresses the profound impact of the ongoing crisis, which has led to displacement, economic instability, and social disruption. Since 2017, over 5,768 individuals have been displaced, resulting in family separation, increased child neglect, and abuse. Children in these conflict-affected areas face malnutrition, limited access to education, and significant emotional and psychological distress (Joint Educational needs assessment in the Conflict affected North West and South West Regions of Cameroon, March 2024). These challenges have weakened family structures, heightened the risk of family disintegration, and left Parents/caregivers struggling to provide adequate care.
A 2023 assessment by PAID-WA revealed that 70% of parents/caregivers are unaware of available child protection resources, while 58% report stress and hopelessness, further diminishing their capacity to meet their children’s needs. This project seeks to address these critical issues by strengthening family resilience and provide much-needed support to vulnerable children and families in these regions by:
(a) Providing mental health and psychosocial support to 1000 conflict affected children in safe child-friendly spaces in 20 communities (10 in the North West and 10 in the South West conflict affected regions);
(b) Providing family-centred psychosocial support to 1000 parents/care givers through established Community Support Networks/safe spaces in 20 communities (10 in the North West and 10 in the South West conflict affected regions);
(d) Organize 20 workshops on Positive Parenting, Discipline, and Family Resilience to improve the parenting skills of 1000 Parents/caregivers (50 parents/ caregivers per each of the 20 communities), strengthening family bonds and fostering resilience and
(c) building the Capacity of 300 parents/caregivers (15 per each of the selected 20 communities in South West and North West Regions of Cameroon) in economic empowerment initiatives to Strengthen family support systems to mitigate the risks of neglect and abuse.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The protracted crisis in Cameroon’s South West and North West regions, marked by violent conflict between non state armed groups and government forces, has had a devastating impact on families and communities. Since the escalation of violence in 2017, over 5,768 persons have been displaced in the North West and South West Region of Cameroon (Cameroon: North West and South West-Situation Report No.62, February 2024 by OCHA). The conflict has resulted in economic instability, and significant disruptions to social structures. Families have been forced to flee their homes, with many separating in the process. The consequences of displacement and economic hardship are deeply affecting family dynamics and child welfare. Parents/Caregivers facing extreme poverty and instability struggle to meet children’s basic needs, leading to a rise in cases of child neglect and abuse.A 2023 assessment by PAID-WA shows that child neglect and abuse are rising in the conflict-affected areas of Cameroon, where over 70% of displaced families struggle to meet their children’s basic needs due to increased poverty and social disruption. Findings from the Joint Educational needs assessment in the Conflict affected North West and South West Regions of Cameroon, March 2024 by the Cameroon educational cluster, revealed that children in these conflict-affected areas not only suffer from malnutrition, limited access to education, but also experience emotional and psychological distress due to the ongoing conflict. These challenges have placed an unprecedented strain on family structures, weakening bonds and increasing the risk of family disintegration.Compounding these issues is the absence of accessible support systems and community networks to aid families in crisis. Communities have lost traditional social safety nets due to widespread displacement leaving children in un safe environment to process their trauma and build resilience. According to an assessment in the North West and South West conflict affected regions by PAID-WA in 2023, many parents/ caregivers (70%) are unaware of available resources for child protection and family support and 58% of parents/caregivers reported symptoms of stress and hopelessness, further impacting their ability to care for children (PAID-WA 2023).These factors exacerbate the risk of child neglect, as well as the emotional and psychological toll on children and their parents/caregivers. In response to these pressing challenges, the "Preventing Child Neglect and Supporting Family Unity" project aims to strengthen family resilience and protect vulnerable children from neglect in the South West and North West regions by: (a) Providing mental health and psychosocial support to 1000 conflict affected children in safe child-friendly spaces in 20 communities (10 in the North West and 10 in the South West conflict affected regions);(b) Providing family-centred psychosocial support to 1000 parents/care givers through established Community Support Networks/safe spaces in 20 communities (10 in the North West and 10 in the South West conflict affected regions); (d) Organizing 20 workshops on Positive Parenting, Discipline, and Family Resilience to improve the parenting skills of 1000 Parents/caregivers (50 parents/ caregivers per each of the 20 communities), strengthening family bonds and fostering resilience and (c) building the Capacity of 300 parents/caregivers (15 per each of the selected 20 communities in South West and North West Regions of Cameroon) in economic empowerment initiatives to Strengthen family support systems to mitigate the risks of neglect and abuse.
