Project Summary

Project Objective:-
- To improve food security to some 10,000 IDPs/ Host communities’ members including vulnerable men and women, through acquisition of various skills to attain food security by 31 December 2025
2500 HH in Fako, 2000HH in Manyu and 3500HH in Meme, 2000HH Kupe Maneguba making a total 10,000 beneficiaries most vulnerable people without discrimination shall receive periodic food assistance.
-Carry out on farm training on to 6000 beneficiaries on the cultivation of short cycle crops such as beans, maize sweet potatoes, Huckleberry, cabbage and poultry to 2500 beneficiaries. Distribution of farm inputs and layer birds, feed and micro poultries
- carry out capacity building and start-up grant to 2500 women, people with disability, youths and men on petit trading and craftmanship, to enable them have a profit of at least 25000 each month to meet up with food security needs.
This project intends improve the food security situation in the North West and South West Region of Cameroon that has been hit by socio- political crises since October 2016 that led to displacement of about 968,477(Cardre Harmonise accessment); These IDPs/host families are faced with the problem of inadequate food on a daily basis in terms of quantity and quality, coupled with the restrictions and hardship cause by the outbreak of the COVID19 pandemic, people with disability, women, children, youths and the elderly are the most affected. The intention of this project is to reduce vulnerability of people affected by the crisis and ameliorate the food insecurity as highlighted by the sector response plan and the FSC objectives. This shall lead to livelihood enhancement/improvement and reduce vulnerability of the beneficiaries.
The project would be carried out in localities Kupe Maneguba, Manyu, Meme, Fako divisions.. CEFORA targets in MANYU 2000 HH, FAKO 2500HH, MEME 3500 and Kupe Maneguba 2000HH beneficiaries making a total of 10000 heads of household ,with the SW region having an HRP food security target of over 239,432 for 2025displaced persons in need of food. This project is focusing women, youths, people with disability and the elderly who are the most vulnerable in the ongoing crisis. This project is an up-scaling of the UNDP SMF/emergency response mechanism (July – December 2019) funded by Japan in Fako division and 2 FAO projects in 2022and 2023 sponsored by UN CERF and the government of Norway.
Project activities and indicators -6000 HH IDPs/ returnees/host community without any discrimination with consideration on gender, age PWD are expected to receive on-farm training in the cultivation of short cycle crops such as beans, maize, soya beans, and vegetables; pesticide usage and post -harvest management. They shall benefit from seeds, farm inputs and farm implements.
2500 persons mainly people with disability, elderly persons, youths, women and men shall benefit from capacity building in livestock production and pens for fowls. They shall have a start-up package of inputs
1500 mainly youths, people with disability, men and women and interested elderly people shall benefit from capacity on entrepreneurship and small business management. They shall have start-up grant package for any business they want to engage in.
There shall be periodic food assistance to 2000 most vulnerable beneficiaries as a copping measure during the early stages of the project.
- ALL beneficiaries and the community at large shall benefit from COVID 19, monkey pox sensitization and awareness campaigns, hygiene packages while observing barrier measures.
-Carry out a baseline survey before the project, and an impact survey after the project
-Carry out monitoring and evaluation during all stages of the different project activities.
Meetings with community leader, local administrators shall be on-going to promote social cohesion, acceptance and protection as a peace building mechanism to guarantee food security. project management committee shall be created

1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

. TARGETED FOCAL AREAS AND FSC STRATEGIC INITIATIVES AND JUSTIFICATION This project is demand driven after several town hall meetings in the project area in the SW REGION as a response to the FSC that has an HRP 2025 target of at least 239,439 internally displaced people and returnee in need of agriculture & livelihood facing food insecurity in the South West region. CEFORA would work with 10000HH directly expecting to touching at least 60000 individuals. This project is going to provide sustainable food and nutrition security through the skills they acquire from the different capacity building and on - farm training they are going to receive. The cultivation of short cycle crops implies from 6 weeks to 3 months beneficiaries would start harvesting their crops, which the will eat and take excess to the market. Most of the elderly, PWD and youths have opted for poultry and petit trading, this would make them self-reliant, attaining recovery in a very short time. This project would also lead to social cohesion reducing tension in these communities as both IDPs/returnees and host families are participating in the project. Also, most host families are now having the burden of taking care of extra persons and this is greatly affecting host communities. It is therefore necessary that the IDPs/returnees (especially women, youths and old people and PWD) produce their own food and poultry, consume nutritive food and have the opportunities to sell excess and even engage in petit trading so as to have some money to buy other basic necessities; this shall improve their livelihood and reduce gender based violence and sexual exploitation. The focus of the project shall be on on-farm training, post-harvest management, of improved varieties of short cycle crops and livestock farming, and carry-out some economic activity) will help to provide a quick and sustainable response to the present crisis by ensuring food and nutrition security (good health) and alleviating poverty and sexual exploitation and abuse. All these align with the national priority areas (alleviate poverty and reinforce resilience during crisis), which in turn align to the sustainable development goals number 1(no poverty), number 2 (zero hunger, number 3 (good health and wellbeing), number 5(gender equality), number 10 (reduce inequalities) and Number 13 (climate action). This thematic area of livelihood recovery also aligns with national agricultural and extension programmes that emphasize on building the capacities of farmers via training and supply of small machines for transformation. We focus on women who are the backbone of households and mostly involved in cultivation and marketing of short cycle crops, the thematic area of female participation is one of our priorities, for if we empower the women, we build sustainable communities. This project will go a long way to complement the UNDP early recovery mechanism small SGF implemented by CEFORA (July-December 2019), The FAO projects of 2022 and 2023 on enhancing food in security. The Youth entrepreneurship program, Gender Climate Change Agriculture Support Programme (GCCASP). CEFORA participated in the implementation of the first phase of this project (January-June 2019).
