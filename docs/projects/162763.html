Project Summary

This project is planned to provide an integrated emergency shelter, non-food and environmentally friendly transitional shelters for protracted IDPs and returnees in West and Centrad Darfur states which is 1 year project. The project will target 12,500 people 2500 household (male, female, elderly, boys and girls) as follows returnees, IDPs and resident communities, in Central (60%) and west Darfur state (40%). The project will ensure that the target communities will have adequate ESNFIs items to ensure their most basic human needs as it relates to the maintenance of health, privacy and dignity and its easily within their reach. SORR will undertake periodic joint inter-agency assessments with special consideration to female headed households to inform the project on the new and emerging situations in the targeted areas. Post-distribution surveys will be an integral part of the project undertaken with ESNFI project or other partners of the sector in the targeted locations to evaluate the effectiveness of the distributions. SORR is a partner in the ESNFI sector and has previously been working in the area distributing NFIs and constructing ES and transitional shelters.
The 12,500 targeted people will receive NFIs and Essential shelter materials for 12500 households (6500 female and 4000 male), and training of 2000 IDPs and returnees vulnerable households in transitional shelter and fuel eﬃciency environmentally friendly.
SORR will conduct needs assessments and verification of beneficiaries of ES/NFI to ensure priority assistance is given to people with specific needs (PSNs), EVIS and the most vulnerable in line with agreed sector guidelines. Assessments and distributions will be done in collaboration with stakeholders, community, women’s associations, on the ground. fire and flood awareness and environmental protection will be undertaken to ensure capacity is built with special consideration to role of gender, skills built and environment is protected. SORC will coordinate with stake holders like UNHCR, NCA, UNOCHA and partners on the ground.
