Project Summary

The Core pipeline project will reach out to 830,000 crisis affected population (IDPs, Returnees, etc) across South Sudan with immediate lifesaving WASH NFIs contributing to the WASH cluster objectives 1, 2, 3. WASH NFIs will be distributed in accordance with the Sphere standard guidelines in Emergency and South Sudan WASH Cluster guidelines. The project will plan, procure, preposition, and manage these critical WASH NFIs. The WASH NFIs will include soap, household water treatment chemicals (PUR and Aqua tabs), filter clothe, buckets (with and without taps), latrine digging kits, latrine slabs (squatting slabs), Tarpaulin for latrine construction, dignity kits, handpump spare parts, amongst others. The project will cover supplies purchase costs, transportation costs to preposition the supplies at the UNICEF warehouse in Wau, Juba, Bor and Malakal and cost the cost of warehousing. These supplies will be made accessible to all WASH Cluster partners through national and state level WASH cluster structures who will approve the release of these supplies by UNICEF to the partners upon satisfactorily meeting the obligations for the supplies release and utilization. The partners will take on the responsibility of transporting these supplies from the UNICEF warehouse to the last mile location of delivery and facilitate the distribution of these supplies to beneficiaries. WASH partners and the cluster will continue to collaborate with the Government at state and national levels and with other clusters (Nutrition, Health, Protection and Education) ensuring maximal utilization of the scarce resources available. Where feasible, and in line with its localization agenda, UNICEF will explore cost effective means to deliver these supplies to the people in need using the voucher system and other modalities, eliminating costs of transportation and storage thus reaching out to more people in need timely. UNICEF is committed to and will deliberately strengthen the capacities of the WASH partners facilitating them to quickly (within 72 hours of crisis occurrence) respond to the needs of vulnerable populations in event of an crisis. The project will deliver rapid assistance informed by critical gaps assessments and analysis to affected populations in accordance with WASH Cluster priorities counties. UNICEF and the WASH partners will undertake post distribution monitoring for supplies distributed to ensure that the supplies reached the crisis affected populations and to gather feedback on the distributed supplies in terms of their relevance, quality, quantity to inform future programming. UNICEF being the provider of last resort, the WASH Core Pipeline project will enable UNICEF to deliver in very complex situation/circumstances where other WASH partners will not be able to deliver in. This is critical in meeting the needs of vulnerable communities indiscriminately.

1. Needs

Extreme climate change shock (floods and drought); diseases outbreak (cholera and monkey pox, etc); protracted communal conflicts in some places in South Sudan over land dispute/border issue between communities; competition over resource utilization; and influx of returnees and refugee as the result of Sudan crisis through the Points of Entry has triggered unprecedent mass displacement and humanitarian catastrophe, led to congestion around water sources, which place much strains on the exiting water points, that culminated in the frequent breakdown of these water sources, conflict as the result of water stress experienced by IDPs and host communities. Girls and women are particularly affected by inadequate WASH services and are more vulnerable/or susceptible to gender-based violence when walking long distance to fetch water, as the people living with disabilities.There are new emerging kinds of emergencies of public health nature, it can be contagious if preparedness and response are inadequate and can cause high fatality rate within a short time span of time. Hepatitis E due to rampant open defection practices, prevalence water borne disease as cholera, typhoid and diarrhea indicates deplorable WASH situation in faecal matter contamination and poor hygiene practices. Some counties with high GAM rate that can be correlated to open defecation being practice, which represent as underlying causes of malnutrition of children under 5 yrs.Core pipeline supplies are our first line of emergency response and serve as the immediate lifesaving option. It is thus critical for WASH items to be prepositioned and made available in strategic locations that are prone to emergency in the country. This will facilitate immediate response by WASH cluster partners to meet the immediate needs of crisis affected communities within the stipulate timeframe as per CCCs/Sphere standards/ 72 hours. The Core Pipeline modality serve to overcome poor road infrastructure networks that complicate supplies transportation at times of need through simple art of prepositioning. In addition, maintaining 25% minimum stock in warehouses help to buffer unpredictable nature of humanitarian situation either man-made/ or natural disaster in the country that often erupt at any time, and cause unprecedent suffering of vulnerable groups (children, elderly and people with disability). UNICEF is the cluster lead for WASH, currently co-managing WASH core pipeline supplies on the behalf of the cluster partners and at the same time playing the role of Provider of the Last Resort (POLR), where necessary, and depending on access and availability of fund and to ensure the gaps identified are fulfilled. Taking the stock of lessons learnt of WASH Preparedness and Response 2024, considering the scenarios of the flood aftermath, on-going cholera outbreak, influx of returnees and refugees and unabated communal conflicts. In 2025, UNICEF WASH projections to support 1,500,000 people with critical WASH supplies.

2. Project activities

UNICEF will use its established procurement mechanisms to ensure the timely procurement of vital WASH core pipeline supplies. Strategic partnerships will be formed, and WASH humanitarian supplies will be strategically positioned within hubs as determined with the WASH cluster placed around the country for seamless access by all qualified WASH actors from the ten states and three administrative areas. UNICEF will work with partners to ensure that WASH core pipeline supplies are delivered to emergency-affected and vulnerable communities on time. The supplies will be available to the WASH actors via the defined cluster’s SOPs. UNICEF will ensure that the supplies are managed safely and efficiently, with accountability to donors, the WASH cluster, and the affected people. UNICEF will continue to capacitate all staff involved in the procurement, transportation, management, and distribution of supplies to the last, milage. Particular attention will be accorded to training of individuals working in supplies management at various warehouses across the country. Routine monitoring will take place on a regular basis at UNICEF and partner warehouses. UNICEF will also invest in training legible WASH cluster partners on reporting on the WASH supplies utilization fostering accountability. End-user monitoring will also be carried out for recipients to ensure that supplies reach the most vulnerable people. UNICEF will strictly effort accountability measures and include in staff contracts clauses on supplies aid diversion to reduce discrepancies that would otherwise result in loss of supplies. Checks and balances will be put in place and monitored regular to ensure that stock reports speak to stock physically available. Partners will continue to be reminded on the supplies governing regulations or release, utilization, and monitoring. UNICEF in collaboration with the Cluster will hold accountable partners that defy the guidelines on the management and use of these supplies. UNICEF will keep updated the Cluster on gaps regarding the supplies, ageing stock and document any issues on quality, relevance, and appropriateness of the WASH NFIs packages to inform project adjustment and to contribute to shaping future projects.
