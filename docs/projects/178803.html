Project Summary

Through this project, SARD intends to support a number of unaccompanied and separated children and families vulnerable to separation through continuation and extension of its specialized and unspecialized protection services which specifically target sensitive child protection issues such as family separation, early child marriage, child labor and recruitment to military services. Through its emergency centres/ Child-Friendly Spaces (CFS) in Sarmada and A’zaz SARD provides unaccompanied and separated children with critical life-saving protection services including shelter, education and PSS, and foster placement. Outside the centres, through its referral system in coordination with the Protection cluster, SARD’s Mobile Case Management team identifies child protection cases and renders or refers specialized and unspecialized protection services. In parallel to protection services provided, SARD supports foster families and families vulnerable to separation with integrated livelihood restoration as a way to promote family kinship and strengthen the family unit as a whole. With the programming grounded in creating sustainability for the family, the likelihood of dependence on the aid in the future is profoundly reduced.

4 - Needs

SARD’s team conducted a rapid needs assessment on child protection issues in its operating areas in April 2021. The assessment aimed at determining the scale of the needs and protection risks for children affected by the conflict in NW and the existing capacity to respond to the needs. A total of 28 KI interviews were conducted with teachers, camp managers, child protection staff, and others. Assessment findings indicated child separation was highly prevalent, as reported by 86% of KI interviews. Reasons for separation were most commonly reported (86% of total KIs) as death of parents or caregivers. Findings and field experience from SARD's team showed that there is a significant gap in the child protection system, mostly due to the lack of a system for responding to child separation. Findings also confirmed cases of violence, child abuse, child labor, early child marriage, and recruitment of boys into the armed forces. According to the assessment, violence rates were significantly higher against boys (71%) than girls (29%). With regard to exclusion from services, the top three groups most likely to be excluded were: children who have lost both parents (61%), children from poor households (50%), and children with a disability (46%). Furthermore, the HNO 2021 highlighted that COVID-19 increased protection risks by confining children to situations of violence and vulnerability and disrupting access to protection assistance, particularly specialized protection services, leading to increased Child Protection and GBV risks. Additionally, according to the HNO, the longer these protection needs are not addressed, the more damage is done through irreversible impacts on particularly women and children.

5 - Implementation of activities

The project will support 1350 individuals (including men, women, boys and girls) across North-West Syria by meeting their protection needs by mitigating protection risks, in line with the Child Protection Minimum Standards in Humanitarian Action. The project will specifically target unaccompanied and separated boys and girls and children/families vulnerable to separation. SARD identifies/receives referral of protection cases (including cases of children with disability) through various channels including: SARD's Mobile Team, the Protection cluster, local authorities (municipalities, local councils), other humanitarian actors, and from the community itself. Following referral / case identification, SARD's Case Management Team actively studies each case for family tracing and reunification or interim care arrangements. Within the emergency shelter, SARD provides full-time caregiver support, education and psychosocial support (PSS), case management, and foster placement, and the shelter is adjusted to serve children with disabilities. Following foster placement, SARD aims to support the sustainability of the family unit as a whole through livelihood recovery support (ERL sector). In situations where children are vulnerable to separation from their families, SARD's Mobile Case Management team intervenes through provision of case management services. Based on the case, an integrated program is developed to strengthen the family unit which includes education re-enrollment and PSS support for the children and a parenting program and livelihood support for the caregivers. In situations where protection services are outside SARD's capacity or expertise, such cases are referred to other humanitarian actors for proper management.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

SARD programming and activities are carefully designed to minimize and mitigate risks associated with operating in sensitive areas. Risk: Protection staff unintentionally perpetrate harm or increase stigma if data confidentiality of sensitive child protection cases is not respected due to lack of training. Mitigation: Staff were trained on data confidentiality, psychological first aid and on communication skills. Staff are closely supervised to ensure compliance with international best practices. SARD regularly updates written guidance and provides refresher sessions.Risk: Protection activities raise expectations of response despite limited service provision availableMitigation: Staff were provided with guidance to communicate with beneficiaries how they can support. All protection staff received training on referrals and regularly update service mappings to support referral pathways.Risk: Community leaders do not see the value of SARD’s protection program and remove permission to operate in the area.Mitigation: SARD field management regularly communicates with local interlocutors to ensure understanding of our programs. SARD ensured that protection programming is rolled out in areas with other programming including health and cash distributions. Risk: With the geographical coverage of Hayat Tahrir al Sham (HTS), there are risks that HTS could interfere with SARD’s processes and prevent activities from taking place. Mitigation: Consistent messaging regarding the SARD’s neutrality and impartiality as a humanitarian actor will mitigate the risk of the interference of non-state actors. Frontline staff will receive training and strict guidance on the application of international humanitarian principles.

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

I. Safety and Security Risks The volatile and often unpredictable nature of the Syrian conflict continues to impose significant risk on humanitarian staff and beneficiaries' safety. Risks could include but not limited to a sudden escalation in hostilities, airstrikes, armed clashes, and kidnapping. The likelihood of increased conflict hostilities is considered moderate in northern Aleppo compared to opposition-controlled areas of Idleb and western Aleppo. The impact of this risk is assessed as high in both areas. SARD has developed and adopted tools to ensure a sufficient level of standard of care to safeguard employees and beneficiaries from a reasonably foreseeable risk of harm is maintained.II. Operations and Program-related Risks The high-risk environment of northwest Syria continues to impose significant risk on the continuation of humanitarian activities. Risks to humanitarian programs implementation in the context of northwest Syria include but not limited to partial or complete closure of border crossings with Turkey; disruption of the supply chain; fluctuating exchange rate; and community acceptance. The likelihood and the impact of operations and program-related risks in areas of northern Aleppo, Idleb, and western Aleppo assessed as high. SARD has developed and adopted tools to ensure its readiness to cope and adjust with sudden unexpected risk as they arise. III. Financial and Reputation Risks The absence of a fully functioning banking system in northwest Syria is a key challenge is how to facilitate fund to northwest Syria with the at most due diligence approach. SARD has developed and adopted tools to minimize the risk and ensure the incorporation of due diligence in its financial transactions.
