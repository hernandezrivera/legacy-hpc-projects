Project Summary

After influx of Rohingya refugee Nabolok has started providing lifesaving services at camp 26 and 23 for reducing their sufferings focusing on WASH, LPG distribution and Solar street light installation as well as for sufferer host community also. Nabolok is implementing a project in camp 26 and 23 under Teknaf upazila. On this project have 3 (three) component like WASH, LPG Distribution and Solar light installation. Under this project to improve site management Nabolok already has installed 250 solar street light at Rohingya camp-26, camp-23 and for nearer host community to ensure safety and security of beneficiaries and will install more 50 solar base street light in 2020 also will maintain & repair all of these light for next five years.

Objectives

Targeted Rohingy and Host Community are illuminated with solar based street lights for ensuring safety and security, especially for women and adolescent girls.

Needs

Solar street light is part of site management, to improve site management means ensuring easy movement at night. Reducing raid of thief. Getting access of all facilities during night especially for women, adolescent girls, Children and old adults. And for all this requirement beneficiaries need solar based street light.

Activities or outputs

1) Installation of solar based street light 30 number of Rohingya refugee and 20 number of Host communities at camp-23 & 26 and Nhilla & Baharchara union and during implementation there will be a consultation with beneficiaries especially women and adolescent girls for site selection. 2) Regular maintain and monitoring of Installed solar based street light for Five (05) years at same areas.

Key project/performance indicators

By the end of the project period women, adolescent girls, children and old adults of targeted household has reported to feel safe and secure at night.Ensuring easy and safe movement during night for nearer host communities.
