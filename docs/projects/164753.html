Project Summary

This project is a continuation of existing education and protection activities by Maram Foundation and funded by OCHA, as well as scaling up education and protection activities in additional schools and locations. the project will contribute to HRP/ASO 1. Helping to create a safer and more secure environment for children. This priority will focus on children under the age of 18 in and out of school by providing an integrated set of services that targets the gaps and needs of the most vulnerable groups while ensuring the centrality of protection. The project will support eight (16) schools, including men, women and children. 100 child have disabilities or special needs. 44% of the beneficiaries are adults: teachers, parents, and community.
Maram conducted a needs assessment that identified need & possible significant impact. The target schools include:

This project seeks to scale up safe and equitable access to formal and non-formal education for school-aged girls and boys between ages 5-17 in a safe environment, with rehabilitation of 102 classes in 16 schools. The project ensures safe, child-friendly and accessible for children of all needs and with disabilities.
the recruitment for the remining 13 school will be through face to face interviews and the certificates will be verified with education directorate.
Maram will increase understanding of value of education among, teachers, students, parents, and the community. 320 educational staff will be trained on education activities (such as accelerated learning programs (ALP), those recommended by (INEE), nonviolent communication, etc.). Maram will also educate parents and community on CP concerns at parent meetings at school.
This project will cover the incentives for teachers at all 16 schools. The project will distribute teacher and student kits, with all needed stationery and teaching tools, and specified by age and gender. Children with low literacy and numeracy will be provided catch-up classes in summer.
Maram will perform rehabilitation projects for classes according to assessments, installing or rehab (whiteboards, desks, lamps, disability accessible facilities, and rehab caravans). This includes an accessible ramp with hand support, accessible toilet with grab bars and long handle taps over a basin at an appropriate height (80 cm).
Back to school (BTS) campaigns will be conducted for one month in summer to encourage the value of education and children to school.

4 - Needs

Maram field staff conducted a technical needs assessment, which identified the target 16 schools. The assessment was conducted as follows: - Assessment methodology: MF education needs assessment adopted a purposive sampling method and consulted selected community leaders/representatives, school principals, as well as project staff to select representative locations for conducting interviews and surveys. Focus group discussions included head teachers, teachers, School Management Committees, parents/community members, women’s groups, and school-age girls and boys by age groups. Maram has been working for over one year in the already-established OCHA-supported schools (AlTawhed and over five years in Al Hikma). Assessment Findings: Based on an analysis of the findings of MF’s assessments listed above, the most commonly cited needs amongst the affected population in the targeted locations are: - Al-Hikma, AlTawhed schools: Maram is already working to provide education and CP services, and there is good satisfaction with the activities provided by Maram. The need is to extend the project at the same level with the same activities, including supporting 34 teacher incentives (19 female, 15 male), rehabilitate 21 classes, education awareness-raising through home visits, and PSS case management. - Bilal Kreim and newly established school (Salah Aldein in Kafer Lusein camp): Needed activities include supporting 72 teacher incentives (35 female, 37 male), rehabilitate 40 classes, awareness-raising through home visits, and PSS case management. - Maaret Elekhwan camp, new school: Needed activities include supporting 16 teacher incentives (12 female, 4 male), CP and protection considerations, and infrastructure. voluntary based schools(Salah Aldin /Al Amal/ Altinmieah/ AlAziziah/ AlNaser/ AlSafa/ AlSadaka/ AlHadaba AlKhadraa / AlOroba / AlBir) need rehabilitating within classes and providing incentives

5 - Implementation of activities

The project will start with coordination with local authorities and Education Directorate to select schools and facilitate the work Then we will register students and provide formal education for student. MF will conduct exams (mid-term and final exams) to measure students’ improvement rate.Provision of structured psychosocial support. And Provision of recreational materials for 16 schools. The sessions contribute to improving the psychological and social well-being of children and reducing the risks of protection they are exposed to as a result of the crisis.And holding PTAs meeting in every semester in each school. the PTAs meeting are important to both parents and school administration as it involves parents in education process to understand the challenges schools face and become part of the solution.at the beginning of project, school bags for children and teachers bags will be provided. As well as stationery supplies for 16 schools and conduct minor rehabilitation for classes and facilities in 16 schools with ensuring accessibility for all children and staff,The M&E team will visit the schools to observe the implementation of rehabilitation.Conduct the training program for teachers for 5 days on the subjects: class preparation, learning styles and multiple intelligences. The training will include pre- and post-evaluation for all trainees to assess their progress and skills improvement.In non-formal education: Conducting BTL campaigns (awareness-raising activities, community mobilization, and community outreach), The BTL campaigns will introduce the project into area and attract students to school,Enrolling children (including CWD) dropped out and underachievers. ASER test will be conducted to determine the level of children, Provision of NF education, including catch-up, literacy, numeracy and remedial classes.Pre and post-test will be conducted to measure the improvement rate for students during cycle. Incentives for teachers will be provided on month

6 - Protection Risk Analysis
