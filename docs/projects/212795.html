Project Summary

Because of the circumstances that occurred in Syria during the crisis that began in 2011 and continues until now, many things and changes occurred that affected people directly in terms of economic and social deterioration, and cases of displacement continue due to the circumstances, i.e. the lack of economic and food security and stability. Which was a huge burden on people and society, not to mention the lack of income or its almost scarcity, the collapse of the currency in terms of exchange, and the high costs of living. One of the most important sectors that was affected during the crisis is the health sector, which constitutes the backbone of life and is associated with all vital sectors and is considered The health sector is one of the most important sectors, and it is a partner with all sectors, and it is a dire need for every human being living on the face of the earth. There are many reasons that made the health sector collapse dramatically, and this sector becomes a dream for many people, and many people lose their lives because of their inability to pay the costs of treatment. With the increase in pandemic diseases, people’s lives have become in great danger. In view of this, after the questionnaires conducted by the Nebras Association, I sensed the urgent and necessary need to establish a health for all project, which is a legitimate right and a duty for every human being. The goals of the project were set: health for all and people’s access to health services with dignity. And not to insult or blackmail to take their right without dispute and to save lives is a duty for all in accordance with international conventions and humanitarian norms. Health is a right and a duty for all. The Nebras Association will, through the Health for All Project, provide health services in partnership with humanitarian partners as much as possible in accordance with the Health for All project program.
