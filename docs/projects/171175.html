Project Summary

As is the case with disasters, flooding heightens the risk of family separation, and causes severe distress for children due to the shock and loss of homes and disruption in daily life, as well as a potential increase in gender-based violence (GBV) due to a breakdown in community structures, law and order, and a lack of physical protection. Violence against children, both inside and outside of the home, has increased as families are in confined spaces, with limited resources and heightened stress. The objective of the project is to strengthen the protection of most vulnerable girls, boys, women and men affected by conﬂict and climatic shocks by reducing risks and vulnerabilities and increasing community capacities. Key to this action will be a focus on improving capacity for immediate response to violations and remedial action as well as strengthening the protective environment in Somalia.
JF will Monitor protection trends in targeted areas, in the framework of the Protection Monitoring System of the Protection Cluster. We will as well with the support of the stakeholders work towards identification through assessment and analysis of persons with disabilities, older persons at-risk and persons from minority clans/communities. Juba foundation within the targeted districts will Promote coexistence and augment collaborative dispute resolution capacity in communities affected by floods. In other proposed intervention JF will Stimulate and support government efforts to safeguard HLP assets left behind by those displaced by flood and establish mechanisms to facilitate repossession on return. JF shall provide information services designed to assist flood victims to access services, navigate complex procedures, and utilize existing remedies in areas of displacement to enhance their own protection.
On environment building, Juba Foundation will reinforce formal and community institutions for better legal and community-based protection of vulnerable groups from violence, coercion and abuse. Key to achieve this objective, activities will include awareness-raising and outreach among communities on protection issues, capacity building and training for duty bearers, service providers and humanitarian workers on HLP. Juba Foundation will assist obtain/access government services such as national identity cards, passports, driver's licenses, title deeds as well as birth and death certiﬁcates. The mapping will also establish the cost of obtaining the documents and issuing agencies at Federal and Regional State level. The information will be disseminated to strengthen governance structures and to support service delivery to affected populations.
On advocacy, Juba foundation will strengthen coordination of protection services and enhance advocacy on protection with relevant stakeholders. Juba Foundation will further identify and provide protection-oriented support to people with disabilities in the target locations.
Juba foundation presence in the area will aid in proper implementation because of the good working relationship with the local administration. Juba foundation will work in coordination with the cluster to ensure that the SPHERE standards are adhered to.
