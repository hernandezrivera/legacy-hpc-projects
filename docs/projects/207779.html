Project Summary

In 2024, DRC Cameroon plans to continue alongside the same line of its ongoing programming approach with DANIDA on the CAR crisis with a focus on capacity building of local actors and transfer of expertise in order to progressively hand over protection activities and diminish its operational presence in the East and Adamawa regions. The light will be put on meeting people’s basic needs, ensuring duty bearers act to respect, protect and fulfil the rights of people and enhancing social cohesion in communities.
In 2024, DRC with DANIDA SPA will contribute to the SOLID project, a multi-country consortium lead by DRC in both Cameroon and the CAR to promote sustainable solutions to displacement situations, by supporting the implementation of Community Development Plans and strengthening social cohesion via micro-projects.
Given that identifying the real capacities of partnering CSOs and building upon them is a long and time-consuming process which affected the 2023 timeline of activity implementation, DRC chose to continue its partnership with two out of the four CSOs identified in 2022. AMS and APROSPEN have both received capacity-building training from DRC and have shown their willingness to learn and teach in a collaborative manner with DRC and its other partners. In 2024, both local partners will thus have all the key in hands to deliver on planned activities, in time and qualitatively.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The project will aim to pursue the DANIDA SPA response to the CAR crisis on the border between the Central African Republic and Cameroon. DRC Cameroon will work with two identified and vetted partners under this grant. Association Mains Solidaires (AMS) and Action pour la Promotion de la Santé, la Production et l’Environnement (APROSPEN), with whom we have already worked under the DANIDA SPA 2023 will continue to be trained and implement protection activities ranging from birth registration to case management and protection monitoring. They will also support CBPCs’ work in peaceful conflict management. Both organisations are based in the East and Adamawa regions of Cameroon. DANIDA SPA 2024 will also contribute to the training of HURDA in charge of protection monitoring within the frame of the SOLID project, in order for DRC to progressively hand over protection activities within its exit strategy. In 2024, DRC is planning to build upon successful activities that has allowed local partners (CSOs, CBPCs, governmental bodies) to become more autonomous and gain expertise. For instance, training sessions on birth registration (awareness-raising, strategic implementation, etc.) have permitted both governmental bodies (BUNEC, MINAS) and CSOs to develop an alternative to what previously existed. The strategies have been absorbed by local partners who are now conducting sensibilization campaigns without the help of DRC. Similarly, AMS and APROSPEN, are still in the process of building their expertise in civil documentation (including birth registration) and in the identification and referral of protection cases, with the help of DRC who will continue supporting them through capacity-building. Moreover, the activities set up in 2023 and continuing in 2024 have allowed for stronger collaboration between local actors, thus helping them diversify their portfolio and learn from one another. In 2023, DRC has learned from challenges faced when implementing micro-projects meant to strengthen social cohesion. Indeed, they are very demanding in terms of resources for vulnerable communities, meaning even if they are willing to participate and get physically involved in the project, they often lack time, material or other necessary resources. DRC is thus adapting its response to the communities’ real capacity.
