Project Summary

Maram intervention is aiming to strengthen access to IDP camps and to livelihood by creating income generating opportunities. It will target the increasing of resilience of affected communities and improving access to production and market infrastructure to restore local economy recovery by improving access to livelihood opportunities and basic services; especially amongst the most vulnerable households and communities; laying the foundations for long-term recovery and decreasing the humanitarian vulnerability not just for beneficiaries profiting from CfW activities. Nevertheless, the work opportunities will target mostly the youths and legally work-eligible adolescents and partially other adults. Moreover, the women and people with disabilities will be partially appropriately targeted.
The rehabilitation works of roads and infrastructure is to be conducted under the scope of flood protection. The rural roads between targeted camps will be rehabilitated and road protecting designs and drainage systems are to be implemented. Work opportunities shall be created through this intervention and the beneficiaries will be identified by developing selection criteria based on objectives of ER Cluster and in coordinating with Protection cluster to identify the most vulnerable cases in camps locating around the activities implementation locations.

4 - Needs

Since 15.01.2020 the new IDPs in NWS experienced new risks and intensifying existing vulnerabilities. Daily bombardments affecting civilian infrastructure in conjunction with winter conditions and the devaluation of the Syrian Pound are causing high prices and unaffordability of essential goods and services in the region. (Situation Report No. 7 - 29 January 2020, OCHA)“Out of around 11 million potential members of the workforce (between 18 and 65 years of age), more than half (3.4 million males and 3.1 million females) are unable to find sustained, reliable and decent work. The assessment also indicates that 55 per cent of total males and 75 per cent of total females report less income from all sources than they need to maintain a moderate living standard.”(ER HNO; 03.12.2019)As a result of this rapid dramatically reduction of households purchasing power, negative coping mechanisms such as child labour, early marriage amongst others impacting differently the livelihood of women, men, girls and boys have emerged. The desired intervention will improve the living conditions of the most vulnerable conflict-affected people including adolescents, youths, mid-skilled people through ensuring restricted work opportunities, nevertheless empower their communities as well enabling better and safe access to labour market.Usually the most common livelihood strategy amongst refugee men is to perform day jobs. To get informed on job opportunities, they have to rely on a good information network and need access to the labour market. It is important for young people to develop the practical, intellectual and social skills that will serve them throughout their lives. Based on “Refugee livelihoods. A review of the evidence; by Machtelt Vriese 2006”Through IDPs involvement in rehabilitation of roads and drainage systems works, which have simultaneously essentially and significantly positive impact on the livelihoods opportunities, a practical internship will be accomplished.

5 - Implementation of activities

1. Short-term work opportunities created: 75 work opportunities created for a period of 3 months and a monthly salary of 200$. The project will be targeting 95% men and 5% women, among which 10% of them will be PWDs. 50% of the targeted communities will be mid-skilled adolescents and youths.Activity 1.1: Profiling of HHs able to be enrolled in cash for work activities: In coordination with the protection cluster and with specific focus on economic vulnerability, the present activity will identify most vulnerable youths, legally eligible adolescents, adults, women and PwDs to be involved in CfW activities according to the targeted percentages.Activity 1.2: Identification of casual jobs in road and drainage system rehabilitationActivity 1.3: Supervision and monitoring of casual workers’ activities. 2. Rehabilitation of roads: Access to and from camps improved all seasons and Increased mobility of people and goods from and to camps also during raining season. This will be achieved by rehabilitation of 7 km.Activity 2.1: Design of road rehabilitation plan: Designing detailed technically rehabilitation plan to address roads flaws.Activity 2.2: Identification and contracting of companies capable of road rehabilitationActivity 2.3: Implementation of road rehabilitationActivity 2.4: Supervision and monitoring of road rehabilitation 3. Rehabilitation of drainage systems: Risk of flooding in IDP camps will be reduced through the rehabilitation of the drainage systems. The targeted drainage systems are those, that are located on the above rehabilitated roads.Activity 3.1: Design of drainage system rehabilitation plan: Designing detailed technically rehabilitation plan to address drainage systems flaws.Activity 3.2: Identification and contracting of companies capable of drainage system rehabilitationActivity 3.3: Implementation of drainage systems rehabilitationActivity 3.4: Supervision and monitoring of drainage system rehabilitation
