Project Summary

AFRYDEV proposes a targeted project aimed at addressing the Gender-Based Violence (GBV) needs in priority Geidam and Yunusari Local Government Areas (LGAs) of Yobe State in Northeast Nigeria. This project will enhance access to lifesaving GBV services, strengthen community-based prevention mechanisms, and mainstream GBV risk mitigation across humanitarian sectors by reaching out to 20,000 community member (Direct-10,000 and indirect 10,000), including survivor of GBV who are affected by crises and the recent flooding . The interventions align with the GBV AoR 2025 Response Plan objectives and leverage a survivor-centered approach to address the critical protection needs of affected populations, including internally displaced persons (IDPs), returnees, and host communities, and also the 2024 Yobe state flood analysis.

1 - Needs

Geidam and Yunusari in Yobe State, Northeast Nigeria, have been severely impacted by conflict, natural disasters, and large-scale displacement, creating fragile settings where the prevalence of gender-based violence (GBV) is significantly heightened. This exacerbates the vulnerability of women, girls, and other marginalized groups who face limited access to quality lifesaving and well-coordinated GBV response services. The absence of effective support systems further deepens the physical, psychological, and social harm experienced by survivors. This project directly addresses these challenges by improving access to comprehensive and lifesaving opportunity, survivor-centered services designed to respond to the needs of GBV survivors and individuals at risk. These services include access to GBV case management and specialized services (CMR, mental health support), Provision of dignity kits and critical material assistance, training for community structures and service providers to improve response quality, Enhancing awareness to address negative social norms perpetuating GBV risks, access to legal aid service. Additionally, GBV risk mitigation measures are integrated into humanitarian response efforts to enhance the well-being of vulnerable individuals and reduce exposure to harm. By establishing community-based GBV response committees, the project strengthens local structures for coordination, case management, and support. These committees provide platforms for effective response mechanisms while ensuring sustainability by empowering communities to take ownership of GBV prevention and response efforts. The holistic approach of this project ensures that survivors and at-risk individuals are supported in a dignified, respectful, and safe manner.This project adopts a comprehensive and integrated methodology to tackle GBV and its interconnected issues. By addressing these interlinked areas, the project enhances the overall well-being of individuals and communities. It leverages awareness campaigns to promote GBV service and mental health literacy while mitigating risks associated with GBV, thereby fostering resilience and improving health outcomes for survivors and vulnerable individuals. Engaging community members, local organizations, and stakeholders ensures the solutions are culturally sensitive, contextually relevant, and tailored to the specific needs of Geidam and Yunusari. This participatory approach builds trust, strengthens community resilience, and promotes local ownership of interventions, which are essential for long-term sustainability. AFRYDEV's local expertise, demonstrated track record in implementing GBV prevention and response initiatives, and strong capacity for community engagement uniquely position it to execute this project effectively. The organization's proven success in delivering high-quality, survivor-centered services ensures impactful outcomes. AFRYDEV also has established networks within the target LGAs, facilitating access and collaboration with key stakeholders and community members.Through robust monitoring, evaluation, and accountability systems, AFRYDEV ensures efficient implementation, effective utilization of resources, and accurate measurement of the project's impact. This commitment to excellence and transparency guarantees that the project achieves its objectives, empowering communities to lead safer and healthier lives while addressing the urgent needs of GBV survivors and vulnerable individuals in Geidam and Yunusari.

2 - Activities

AFRYDEV will provide GBV Case Management and Specialized Services: the project will make provision of individualized case management for 400 women and girls, ensuring timely access to clinical management of rape (CMR), mental health, and psychosocial support (PFA, counseling), also with the distribution of dignity kits to 1,500 for GBV survivor, vulnarble individual, and women and girls in sever needs, ensuring adequate prepositioning and regular replenishment to maintain dignity and hygiene, Also Legal assistance including representation, advocacy, and logistical support for survivors to navigate judicial processes.Community Sensitization and Engagement: Conduct community sensitization outreaches targeting 20,000 both direct and indirect target individuals to challenge harmful social norms and increase GBV awareness and PSEAH, the project will Facilitate dialogues with community leaders, focusing on gender equality and GBV prevention principles.Production of culturally appropriate IEC Materials for GBV clubs to be use by the GBV clubs across the Geidam and Yunusari: the project will engaged a consultant to develop cultural appropriate IEC material in 4 languages and also the consultant will make an audio GBV massages share by the GBV sectored into audio devised that will be used during sensitization in the geidam and Yusufari LGA, with clear message in project location and site on PSEAH.Capacity Building and Livelihood Support: Train 1,00 service providers and community structures on safe service provision, GBV minimum standard, SOP, code of conduct on safeguarding and PSEA, Referral pathway, and AoR coordination. Empower 400 individuals through livelihood activities, linking survivors to income-generation opportunities and providing start-up kits to promote economic independence.Conduct training on PSEAH for all staff and personnel including vendors, volunteers and contractors in the projectEstablishment GBV Club/Desk Office, on GBV Prevention and response including PSEA, Complaints, and feedback mechanisms: the project will establish a 12 member committee of GBV club in Yunusari and activate 12 member committee in Geidam club which will saver and community based mechanism to conduct sensitization, and awareness session on PSEA.GBV Risk Mitigation and Safety Audits: Conduct 4 session of safety audits in institutions and humanitarian sectors to identify and mitigate GBV risks in Geidam and Yunusari. The project will also train 80 humanitarian partners and government patners in Geidam and Yunusari on GBV mainstreaming and safe referral pathways for survivors.Annual Celebration of 16 Days of Activism: The project will support with the celebration of 2025 16 days of activisim to advocate and create more awareness of GBV prevention and response in the Geidam and YusufariConstruction and Renovation of 1 GBV Safe Space in Yusufari and renovation/support Geidam WGSS: The project will construct a new WGSS in Yusufari and activate the existing the WGSS built by AFRYDEV through the 2024 HNF allocation.State and Feild level monitoring visit: the project will ensure to have monthly M&E visit both at the feild level and the state to monitor the all project activity to ensure the progress, and adherence to PSEA in the field.

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

Fundamental to the operation of African Youths for Peace Development and Empowerment Foundation is respect for the dignity and basic human rights of people within Nigeria and throughout the world. AFRYDEV has a PSEA policy that ensures strict adherence. A robust communication strategy has been put in place ensuring every person who represents AFRYDEV reflects the organization's principles and values in their professional conduct, regardless of whom they are dealing with, or where they are working. AFRYDEV PSEA policy aims to provide a safe and trusted environment that safeguards everyone from SEA including beneficiary communities, workers, program participants, vendors, and partner organizations, AFRYDEV will conduct training on PSEAH for all staff and personnel including vendors, volunteers, and contractors in the project. AFRYDEV is committed to safeguarding the people it helps and those it works alongside. We recognize the nature of AFRYDEV’s workplaces our workers and program participants in positions of authority and trust in relation to the communities we work with, especially vulnerable adults and children. AFRYDEV has a clear code of conduct on safeguarding and PSEA will be signed by all staff and any persons who will be involved in the project. AFRYDEV will actively prevent and respond to SEA and maintain an organizational culture that prioritizes safeguarding against SEA so that it is safe for those affected to come forward and report incidents and concerns with the assurance they will be handled sensitively and appropriately, and also Have a clear message in project sites on PSEAH.
