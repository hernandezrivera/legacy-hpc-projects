Project Summary

The project will be implemented by CAFOD and its Caritas Harare, in Chikomba and Caritas Gokwe and Caritas Hwange in Nkayi. A total of 124,509 vulnerable people will benefit from support to contain the spread of the COVID-19 pandemic. The support will include strengthening Health Care Facilities, provision of Hygiene kits and creation and dissemination of hygiene messages.
Determinations of vulnerability criteria will be done in close coordination with the district COVID-19 task force teams. and cross-referenced to data available at district level. Activities will include:
• Support the setting up of hand washing stations at various public places.
• Support mass media campaigns on COVID-19 prevention using various channels of communication (billboards, radio shows, radio jingles etc) taking into account literacy levels and access of different groups.
• Providing access to critical handwashing facilities including soap and alcohol-based sanitizers, WASH-related hygiene kits.
• Ensure field and front-line staff engaged in WASH humanitarian responses have adequate access to PPE and practice key risk reduction practices like social distancing."
Accountability to communities will be through formal and informal mixed methods to attempt triangulation of trends. The project will use the following three interrelated communication channels to the address the feedback and compliance mechanisms:
• Community focal point: (third party person) to address all the types of complaints and feedback
• CAFOD and Caritas partner mobile teams: to address the complaints and feedback regarding programming/ service delivery
• Complaint Office Desk: specifically, for reporting sensitive complaints and feedback.
