Project Summary

this project aims to contribute to the reduction of high mortality and morbidity due to in adequate nutrition services provision as well as water related diseases due to poor Water,Sanitation & Hygiene services within the target areas Jalalqsi district in Hiraan region. Somali Organic Agriculture Development Organization has designed a Nutrition and WASH program that will try and overcome the high malnutrition rates and high morbidity rates in the district., as well as reduce the high rates of AWD/Cholera and diseases related to poor Sanitation, The project will put into consideration the UNICEF causal framework by trying to tackle the immediate cause of disease and going further to tackle the underlying causes of malnutrition by providing a safe nutrition care and safe environment and by tackling inadequate care of children and women. It is therefore anticipated that the Nutrition component to give diagnosis and treatment facilities.
The WASH component will add more clean water sources and provide sanitation facilities to the district and increase hygiene awareness and improve hygiene practices of the IDPs and host communities in the target locations, the combined impact of which will be to reduce the rates of acute watery diarrhoea (AWD), Lower respiratory tract infections i.e pneumonia and other water borne diseases in the district. Community involvement in the programme design and implementation as well as in local capacity building will support resilience in the community and will contribute to a better sustainability of Nutrition and WASH services

Needs

The humanitarian situation in Somalia remains fragile with the latest FSNAU assessment estimating 4.6 million people in need of life-saving. 2.6 of them are internally displaced people, 25 percent of the population, are expected to be in Crisis (IPC Phase 3) or Emergency (IPC Phase 4) through December. Deyr (October-December) rains are expected to be average to below average, but levels of acute food insecurity in Somalia will remain high through the end of the year. Acute and widespread food insecurity increased morbidity and contributed to further deterioration of the overall nutrition situation in Somalia (FSNAU, September, 2018). Hiraan Region faced recently floods resulted in increasing of displacement, this had exacerbated the needs of both displaced communities as well as host community, People left their lands, Home to completely unknown situations, with high probability of facing very bad circumstances in terms of Nutrition and Water, Sanitation and Hygiene services. Based on FSNAU report of Post Gu season of 2018, indicate GAM 15.7 prevalence and SAM 3.3 prevalence in the project target areas. This reveals that there is very critical malnutrition situation under age of five in the target areas. Malnourished children under five are estimated as 9,400 children whereas pregnant and lactating women are 4,200 in the target project areas There is gap in health and WASH service provision in some parts of the district which is contributing factor to the high malnutrition rates in the areas. therefore SOADO will intervene with integrated nutrition and WASH services in the area , through fixed and mobile teams sites, with strong link with ongoing health and food security interventions to ensure reduction of morbidity and mortality related to malnutrition.

Activities

1. Treatment of 470 boys and girls aged between 6-59 months of severe acute malnutrition. 2. Treatment of 8930 boys and girls aged between 6-59 months for moderate acute malnutrition. 3. Treatment of 4,200 pregnant and lactating women for moderate acute malnutrition. 4. Provision of safe water to 46,000 people through chlorination of water for household at water fetching points. 5. Distribution of hygiene kits to persons discharged for cholera/AWD treatment 6. Provision of hygiene promotion sessions for target settlements and communities targeting people. 8. Construction of public latrines in target settlements and communities benefiting people

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

Displaced individuals and households faces critical problems from surrounded environment and host communities sometimes, this problems result in their exclusion or rejection from host communities, which could restrict or reduce their access to different services, this could sometimes rise to security issues and threats to their personal wellbeing,livelihoods or assets. this project is based on all the humanitarian principles, and aim to promote equality and equity in access to services.

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

Risks related to gender and age like, excluding women and girls from some services based on their gender, also excluding or ignoring some categories based on their age whether they are elders or young children and not including them in our services planning . This will be mitigated through ensuring full access and inclusion of all categories of the community equally regardless of their age and gender to the services we are providing.

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

Hiraan region is known with natural disasters and insecurity situation and this could hinder the services flow as well as the security of the staff and community as well, Our organization make efforts to implement its activities in the safer areas, which are in the same time accessible to the beneficiaries. The project will closely work with all stakeholders including local authority, clan elders, youth (male and female) and women. All clans will be represented in relief committee and staffing to avoid conflicts arising from competition for scarce resources and opportunities.
