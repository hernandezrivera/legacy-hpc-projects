Project Summary

The situation in Gadarif State is deteriorating due to the recent conflict erupted in Sudan. According to the conflict large number of people have displaced to Gadarif State and the humanitarian crisis has observed. The conflict resulted in in poor production in agriculture as the prices of fuel is high, the support from the Agricultural bank was out, insecurity hinder the farmers to clean, their farms, some were not able to cultivate, the harvest had impaired.
The invasion of Khartoum and Gazira States by Rabid Support Forces has also led to large influxes of IDPs from Khartoum and Elgazira and other western state to Gadarif State, the caseload of IDPs in Gadarif is high. There are about 542,886 people 19.5% of the total State’s census—in crisis and above levels of food insecurity for the projection period: October 2023 – February 2024, according to the latest Sudan Integrated Food Security Phase Classification (IPC) analysis.
The project aims to enhance food security and livelihood of (23,002) farmers and agropastoral (IDPs, Host communities and non-displaced) through the availability of agricultural inputs and animal health services, thereby improving the agricultural production and productivity and minimizing livestock losses resulting from diseases and ensuring food and nutrition security for vulnerable pastoral communities. The initiative will primarily focus on six localities of Gadarif state namely Elfashaga, El Faw, Gadarif Wasat, Galabat west, Mafaza and Galelnahal localities. The purpose is to restore and improve the food and nutrition security of 55,832 individuals through provision of agricultural inputs, veterinary medicines and vaccines and providing the appropriate capacity building. Special attention shall be given for women-headed households and people with disabilities.

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

The project staff will do their best to ensure that their activities target the most vulnerable, enhance safety, dignity, and promote and protect the human rights of the beneficiaries without contributing to or perpetuating discrimination, abuse, violence, neglect and exploit. Criteria will be determined to ensure that the beneficiaries selected without any bias. The protection could be mainstreamed by including protection principles in the general project management and as core principles in delivering humanitarian aid. Moreover, it could be mainstreamed by incorporating protection principles into project cycle management through protection mainstreaming indicators for the sector. The Organization as a humanitarian actor shares an ethical responsibility for mainstreaming protection across the humanitarian response. The Project staff would be responsible for ensuring that activities within their respective sector and to be carried out with a “protection lens”, and in particular for ensuring that their activities integrate protection principles. The organization will work closely with the Protection's Sector Lead to ensure that the staff has supported with the suitable protection tools and packages.
