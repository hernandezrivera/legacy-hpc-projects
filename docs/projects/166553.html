Project Summary

The Project intend to institute Children with Disabilities Rights Protection through reaching out to 10000 children with disabilities in Zimbabwe affected by COVID-19 Pandemic through Psycho social Support services, establish of Safe Spaces, offer Assistive and Child Stimulation Devices, distribute Primary Care and Hygiene Kits, Sexual Reproductive Health Kits, Emergency Ambulance Services, Gender Based Violence Mainstreaming and offer Emergency Response training to children with disabilities, their families and their communities in the Wake of COVID-19 Disaster in Zimbabwe. The intervention is meant to promote children with disabilities access to information on COVID-19, build their families capacity to prevent primary and secondary infection, improve their hygiene standards and ensure caring and support of the unaccompanied children with disabilities or those displaced and separated by their caregivers due to the COVID-19 Pandemic. The Project will see at least 10000 children living with disabilities from Harare Province, Mashonaland East Province, Masvingo Province, Manicaland Province and Bulawayo Provinces being reached directly while their families and their communities will be as well reached directly and indirectly.
The Project intends to ensure the provision of Hygiene and Primary Caregiver Kits to children with disabilities to prevent infection through caregiving. Children with Disabilities will receive handwashing soap, sanitisers, masks and hand sanitizers for use at home as a way of protecting them from primary and secondary infections. The Organization intends to offer children with disabilities friendly emergency ambulances and referrals on a 24-hour basis to ensure quality and quantity health service provision to disabled children during the COVID-19 Pandemic. The Organization intends to provide temporal shelter and supplementary food and non-food items to children with disabilities who might be isolated from the families or caregivers because of COVID-19 Quarantine/isolation. The Organization intends to offer Counselling and psychosocial support Services to children with disabilities isolated from their caregivers as a result of COVID-19 Disaster including death. The Project will see the establishment of Safe houses and Isolation Centres for homeless children with disabilities, orphaned and those at risk of abuse during the COVID-19 Pandemic period. In addition, the Project will see the provision of Water disinfectants and sexual reproductive health kits to children with disabilities affected and infected by COVID-19. The Organization also intend to establish COVID-19 children with disabilities friendly information centers including virtual platforms, in local languages for the purpose building a culture of information dissemination to children with disabilities.
The success will be measured through: 10000 children with disabilities reached with community-based PSS interventions, reproductive health kits, safe house, supplementally food and nonfood items, emergency ambulance and medical services, COVID-19 information, primary care giver kits and supplementary child stimulation devices/aids. At least 4000 individuals reached with community-based GBViE risk mitigation, PSEA and CP outreach and surveillance (GBV and CP). 500 specialized GBV and CP service providers and community cadres trained in home-based physiotherapy, primary caregiving, GBV and CP in emergencies response

1 - Activities

Zimbabwe Parents of the Handclapped Children Association is an Association of Mothers of Children in Disabilities in Zimbabwe. Children with Disabilities were not spared from the negative impacts of the current COVID-19 Global Epidemic that started in 2019 in China. Zimbabwe Parents of the Handicapped Children Association is working with over 10000 children with disabilities in Harare Province, Mashonaland East Province, Manicaland Province, Masvingo and Bulawayo Province. The Majority of Children with disabilities are on permanent primary and secondary care hence the coming in of COVID-19 greatly expose children with Disabilities through primary and secondary infection from their caregivers. Children with disabilities in Zimbabwe have for a long time living in squalid condition, lacking hygiene kits, homelessness, primary caregiver kits, lacking sanitary wear, quality and quantity medication and medical services, lacking assistive devices and economic opportunities benefiting another general populace. The advent of COVID-19 and the subsequent national lockdown have affected the livelihood sustainability of children with disabilities whose lives have been based upon donations from well-wishers and begging from the streets. As if not enough, Mothers of children with disabilities who had established income generating projects for survival had their activities banned hence reports of hunger, malnutrition’s and risk of children with disabilities exploitation are being received. The Project intends to ensure the provision of Hygiene and Primary Caregiver Kits to children with disabilities to prevent infection through caregiving. Children with Disabilities will receive handwashing soap, sanitisers, masks and hand sanitizers for use at home as a way of protecting them from primary and secondary infections. The Organization intends to offer children with disabilities friendly emergency ambulances and referrals on a 24-hour basis to ensure quality and quantity health service provision to disabled children during the COVID-19 Pandemic. The Organization intends to provide temporal shelter and supplementary food and non-food items to children with disabilities who might be isolated from the families or caregivers because of COVID-19 Quarantine/isolation. The Organization intends to offer Counselling and psychosocial support Services to children with disabilities isolated from their caregivers as a result of COVID-19 Disaster including death. The Project will see the establishment of Safe houses and Isolation Centres for homeless children with disabilities, orphaned and those at risk of abuse during the COVID-19 Pandemic period. In addition, the Project will see the provision of Water disinfectants and sexual reproductive health kits to children with disabilities affected and infected by COVID-19. The Organization also intend to establish COVID-19 children with disabilities friendly information centers including virtual platforms, in local languages for the purpose building a culture of information dissemination to children with disabilities.
