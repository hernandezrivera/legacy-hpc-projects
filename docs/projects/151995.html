Project Summary

INTERSOS aims to continue its emergency response to the most vulnerable segments of the IDPs in Monguno and Ngala. The project will focus on addressing the basic WASH needs and gaps identified by the WASH sector in Monguno and Ngala through the following activities:
Water and Sanitation in Monguno & Ngala Borno State
 Construction of environment friendly gender segregated latrines and showers
 Installation of solar based water supply schemes
 Construction of water collection points
 Installation of solid waste points
 Procurement and Distribution of Hygiene Kits
 Water Quality Analysis and treatment
 Cash for work activities
Hygiene Promotion and Behavior Change Communication (BCC) in Monguno and Ngala
 Delivery of Key Hygiene messages
 Drafting and formation of WASH IEC materials
 Capacity building trainings for the project stakeholders including community members, working group members, SEMA, NEMA, Land department, Education and health department to ensure project sustainability and ownership
 Organization of special events Such as; World water day, World Toilet Day, World Hand Washing Day, World MHM Day
With the financial support of NHF, INTERSOS will implement the aforementioned activities in both projects focusing areas Monguno and Ngala and will scale up its interventions in the proposed locations. In addition, the project will focus on the IDPs hosted at the reception centers and relocation from the congestion in Monguno and Ngala

Needs

As of April 2018, there are still around 2 million people in need of WASH assistance. WASH has been identified as a priority need for IDPs in Borno state. AOGs are estimated to have destroyed 75% of the water and sanitation infrastructure in northeast Nigeria.In Monguno and Ngala, the communities are using and partially are dependent on surface water for drinking and domestic purposes, which ultimately are causing water borne and diarrheal diseases. Furthermore, the stagnancy of water serves as nursery for mosquitos, which leads to high rates of malaria.INTERSOS is present in both projects focusing areas implementing various projects with the support of multiple donors including UNHCR, OFDA, ECHO and OCHA-NHF. As CCCM partner its monitor and assesses the whole areas on daily basis and identifies the needs and gaps in every sector. As per the assessment report there are more than thousands of families in each location who do not have access to safe and clean drinking water and basic sanitation facilities. In addition, due to drinking contaminated water and staying in unhygienic environment they are suffering from the various viral, diarrheal diseases including cholera, scabies etc. furthermore, they do not have adequate pots to store water safely and properly. They are mostly relying on the surface water. Sometimes due to less access and worst security situation they use pond water which brings dangers for their health and they ultimately are suffering from its negative impacts. As result of using contaminated water in the most of the area especially NE Nigeria is experiencing a large cholera outbreak with a total of 21,935 suspected cases and 353 deaths reported from 01 January to 12 August 2018. The outbreak was initially announced in January 2018 in Kano state and to date a total of 207 LGAs in 18 states have been affected in the NE of Nigeria. The breakdown of Borno State shows that 18 LGAs are currently affected since the declaration of the outbreak on 5 September 2018. INTERSOS has been directly responding to the Outbreak in LGA of Magumeri where a CTU was established at Gajiganna PHC supporting between the September 13th to October 8th 103 patients.
