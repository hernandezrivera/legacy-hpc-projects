Project Summary

The project will be implemented by CAFOD and its Caritas partners (listed below) in the rural areas of Binga and Mudzi (6 wards each district). The project is coming in the background of poor crop harvests in 2018 caused by the erratic rainfall of the 2017/2018 agricultural season, the predicted El Nino induced drought of the 2018/2019 agricultural season and the prevailing unfavourable economic conditions in the country. Thus, the project will support farmers with inputs to promote drought resilience, develop skills and capacity for smallholder farmers to withstand drought and increase access to productive water and increase disease surveillance and response.
The interventions will ensure access to drought tolerant agricultural inputs (to include drought resilient crops and animal fodder) to 5200 households (Binga – 2400 and Mudzi - 2800), capacitate stakeholders and farmers in the five on crops and livestock production (with special emphasis to sustainable Integrated Pests and Diseases management) and will also improve access to water for agricultural purposes (cropping and livestock drinking).
The project therefore seeks to achieve the following objectives:
1. To improve agricultural production by improving access to crop and livestock inputs to vulnerable households in the 2 targeted districts.
2. To enhance agricultural productivity through capacity building in crop and livestock production and pests and disease management
3. To improve access to water for crop and livestock production in the 2 targeted districts.

Needs

Due to the recurrent droughts and the prevailing unfavourable economic conditions in the country, and a predicted El-Nino induced drought (which will negatively affect the provision of water for agricultural needs), there are serious needs for;1) providing vulnerable households with drought tolerant agricultural inputs (both nutritious crop varieties and animal fodder)2)Restoring and improving the availability of water in the targeted districts. 2) Improving awareness and knowledge on safe hygiene and sanitation practices 3) increase the capacity of both stakeholders and farmers to sustainably deal with problematic pests and diseases both in corps and livestock production.

Activities

Distribution of drought tolerant and nutritious crop seed varieties, distribution of fodder, training of farmers in crop and livestock production, training of farmers in Integrated crop and livestock pests and diseases, rehabilitation 40 boreholes
