5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Conduct a SWOT AnalysisA SWOT analysis (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats) helps you examine internal and external factors that could impact your project: Strengths: Leverage these to mitigate risks.Weaknesses: Identify potential vulnerabilities or shortcomings that may lead to risk.Opportunities: Look for external opportunities that could pose risks if not capitalized upon.Threats: Recognize external factors that could jeopardize the project’s success.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

Gender-Sensitive and Age-Inclusive Approaches:• Gender and Age Specific Needs: Women, especially adolescents and youth in rural or marginalized communities, may face particular barriers in accessing support. This component project will offer targeted services that address women's unique needs (e.g., maternal health services, support for domestic violence survivors). Similarly, IDP people may require specific assistance.• Gender Equality in Decision-Making: Ensure that adolescents and youth have equal opportunities to participate in decision-making processes, such as community meetings.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Community Engagement: Engage with local disability organizations, healthcare providers, and community leaders who work directly with individuals with disabilities. This will help raise awareness about the project and ensure it reaches the right people.
