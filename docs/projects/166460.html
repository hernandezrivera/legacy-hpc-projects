Project Summary

The proposed project will support the ongoing WASH sector activities in Borno state by
providing a comprehensive WASH approach to be implemented in the hotspot locations
of Gwoza LGA in Pulka Ward specifically in (Camp C and sector 5 in the host community); Konduga LGA in Njimtilo Ward (Ngumari community); Kunduga ALG in Dalori Ward
(Alau Ngururi, Dalori 1 IDP camp, Dalori 2 IDP camp, Konduga boarding school camp and
Kofa community), Jere LGA in Dusman Ward in (Sabon Bolori community), Jere LGA in
Gongulon Ward in (Yerimari community).
MI will provide WASH life-saving services through improving access to safe and sufficient
drinking water supply, safe, adequate and dignified sanitation facilities, hygiene supplies
and information to mitigate risks related to public health with mainstreaming of
protection and gender for vulnerable IDPs, returning refugees and host communities that
meet the differing needs of men, women, boys and girls. Activities will include: Cholera
preparedness and response, construction of water and sanitation infrastructure to newly
arrived IDPs, operation and maintenance of existing WASH infrastructure including water
supply systems, latrines and bathing facilities, solid waste management and distribution
of hygiene kits, which are in line with the WASH sector strategy.
As parts of its plan towards the Corona virus pandemic, Malteser International has proposed adequate measures to curtail the spread of the Corona virus and disruption of WASH services by providing additional water trucking services at Pulka to complement the additional water needs during the pandemic period with a related proper remote management technique put in place, Distribution of hygiene kits and handwashing stands, Sensitization/Mass Messaging of populace across all demography in camps and host communities
The exact beneficiaries in this response will include IDPs, host communities and
returnees, and most vulnerable segments from the communities such as female-headed
households, elderly persons, and persons with disabilities who will be identified and prioritized in collaboration with authorities and community leaders.

1 - Needs

Borno state in Nigeria continues to be amongst those areas with the highest levels ofvulnerability in Nigeria.In the absence of durable solutions,the situation of IDPs dependsheavily on continued humanitarian assistance.Permanent returns of IDPs to their areasof origin are hindered by the prevailing insecurity situation.In September 2019,MalteserInternational (MI) conducted a KAP survey in Gwoza LGA in Pulka Ward (camp A,camp B,camp C,camp D and camp E) and host community in Bokko 2.MI also participated ina multi-sectoral needs’ assessment led by REACH in July 2019, which was conducted inKIVA1, KIVA2,Bulama-Manyatada and Bulabulin.MI also in late October 2019 conducteda Real Time Evaluation for its WASH operation in MMC and Pulka.Additionally, in otherlocations where MI operates,MI has also observed from ongoing operation,there areurgent WASH needs need attention.WASH needs analysis results:In Pulka, specifically in(camp A,camp B,camp C,camp D and camp E),results show beneficiaries have accessto water supply as much as 13l/p/d which is below the sphere standards of 15l/p/d,though not representative for the entire Pulka.WASH needs remain overwhelming insideIDP camps, and host communities where most affected people live.Access to watersupply In Pulka specifically in (camp A,camp B,camp C,camp D and camp E), from theassessment results show that, beneficiaries have access to water supply as much as13l/p/d which is below the sphere standards of 15l/p/d.Many people living outside ofcamps rely on water supply schemes which were constructed by INGOs,but the quantityof water is insufficient either due to the limited number of water schemes installed or unfunctionality of the existing schemes.IDPs and host communities face frequentshortages of water due to intermittent provision of already stretched water facilities or aninability to afford services in a consistent manner.This is exacerbated by increasednumber of IDPs in camps or host communities due to the protracted crisis,with femaleheaded households (FHH) particularly affected.Families cope by reducing waterconsumption, which increases risks to their health.Access to hygiene and sanitationservices in Pulka and other locations where MI operates remain dire.In Pulka from theKAP survey results show; only 17% wash their hands during three critical times and only28% of the total respondents wash their hands with soap due to unavailability.There is a high need for additional new latrines-especially for the newly arrived IDPs,since the existing latrines are over-used.Many existing latrines are in urgent need for rehabilitation and Operation and Maintenance (O&M), since many were vandalized by theharsh weather during the rainy season.Under this action MI is proposing to construct full water supply solar-motorized schemes in Pulka for newly arrived IDPs toimprove access to safe and sufficient water supply.In other operational areas including:Konduga,Jere,Mafa,MMC and Gwoza LGAs,MI is proposing under this action to rehabilitate hand pumps,upgrade existing hand pumps to solar-motorized systems and build capacities of community members to operate and maintain their water facilities.As the novel Corona virus continues to spread in an unpredictable manner around the globe,the impacts of the COVID 19 pandemic will extend further than that on human health as it will also impact on existing services.A greater risk will appear in North-Eastern part of Nigeria with an on-going humanitarian crisis where thousands of displaced persons now take refuge at camps rendering them vulnerable to the spread of infectious diseases. Statistics shows that 40% of households lack access to a facility with soap and water/water storage containers, of which 18% have no facility whatsoever.MI has proposed measures to curtail the spread of the virus by distribution of hygiene kits and handwashing stands, Sensitization/Mass Messaging of populace across all demography in camps and communities.

2 - Activities

Water supply activities: 1- Construction/Upgrading of water supply systems; includingdrilling of boreholes, installation of hybrid motorized pumps, installation of water tanksand solar panels, construction of tap-stands and laundry aprons. 2- Operation andMaintenance of existing water supply systems through provision of Spare parts and ToolKits and capacity building trainings for communities to improve access to safe watersupply 3- Conduct periodic/monthly Water Quality Testing through provision of WaterQuality Testing Kit (H2S strips and Pool Testers) to test Free Residual Chlorine andColiforms bacteria during to ensure water quality. Sanitation activities: 1-Constructgender segregated latrines/bathing facilities to eliminate open defecation and reducepublic health risks. 2-Rehabilitate gender segregated latrines/bathing facilities toeliminate open defecation and reduce public health risks. 3-Support Operation andMaintenance (O&M) of gender-segregated latrines through provision of latrines cleaningkits to WASH Committees to ensure latrines cleanness. 4-Support Drainage Control andSolid Waste Management through provision of Sanitation tool kits to WASH committees.5- Support Dislodging of latrines in IDP camps in Pulka. Hygiene Promotion: 1- Conductnew/refresher hygiene promotion trainings and sessions to new/existing HygienePromoters, and provide Information Education and Communication (IEC) to aid HygienePromoters to facilitate dissemination of hygiene messages. 2- Distribution of EmergencyHygiene Kits which include soap, jerry-cans, sanitary pads for new arrival. 3-Distributionof Hygiene Replenishment Kits (sanitary pads, soap) to ensure improved personalhygiene. 4-Conduct Post Distribution Monitoring (PDM) to monitor quality, quantity anduse of hygiene items distributed in hygiene and replenishment kits. Cholera Preparednessand Response 1- Deployment of Cholera response team and Provision of Choleraoutbreak kits 2-Conduct General hygiene awareness to contain cholera out break. Corona Virus Prevention Activities: Malteser International has proactively engaged in a number of activities and practices that is essential for protecting human health and preventing human to human transmission of the Corona virus during the outbreak. Activities and practices such as;I. General Sensitization/Key Messaging: A team of Hygiene Promoters have been tasked with the responsibility of creating awareness and passing key messages in IDP camps etc. on ways to prevent the spread of Corona virus, basic underlining symptoms and what to do if one is infected. Messaging are to be conducted at household level while maintaining appropriate distancing and avoiding gathering people for mass messaging.II. Distribution of Hygiene Kits: In line with sensitization and awareness campaigns, Malteser International has proposed for a distribution of hygiene kits consisting of soap, sanitiser etc to beneficiaries at IDP camps and host communities for the purpose of improving personal hygiene thereby reducing the risk of human to human transmission of the Corona virus. Additionally, in order to minimize the risk of exposure of staffs and beneficiaries during distribution Malteser International has revised its hygiene kits distribution SOPs by actively managing the flow of traffic while maintaining the required social distancing at waiting areas, providing handwashing station at the entrance and exit of distribution sites.III. Malteser International has prioritize the distribution of hand washing stations and soap, alongside HP messaging posters at certain locations such as; latrines, water collection points, distribution site, entrance to camps etc.

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

Malteser International (MI) feedback arrangements activities shape MIs ongoingresponse improvement to the emergency in Borno state in the IDP camps and hostcommunities in which it operates. Data from feedback mechanism is is collected andanalysed on a regular basis, allowing Malteser to verify progress and improveperformance in accordance with MI and donor guidelines and international standards.During project implementation, MI ensures community participation in theimplementation and monitoring of activities through community feedback/complaintswhich will be used to improve response strategy. To ensure Accountability to AffectedPopulation (AAP), MI establishes anonymous complaint and feedback mechanisms tocapture community complaints and feedbacks to ensure community involvement andto improve project implementation. To do so, Malteser MI will install complaint boxesin its areas of operation in rural communities hosting IDPs and coordinate with CampManagement to gather complaints from already installed boxes. MI also will conductperiodic meetings with community leaders, women groups, water user committeesand any other existing community structure to have their views on projectimplementation and community involvements. Information from these feedbackmechanism boxes will be collected on monthly basis and analyzed with participationof community members to improve project implementation. All recommendationsfrom complaints will be implemented with full community involvement to ensurecommunity participation.
