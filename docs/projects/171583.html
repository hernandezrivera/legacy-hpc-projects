Project Summary

The FSNAU Post Gu 2020 food security and nutrition survey for Somalia show improvement of the nutrition outcomes but still 1.14 million are in need for treatment services and 1.16 million for preventive services. This number includes children under the age of five and pregnant and lactating women and girls. The national median GAM prevalence in children was reported to be around 13.8%. In Gedo the GAM rates are at serious levels of between 9.2% and 12.8%. PLWs and children are prone and vulnerable to the consequences and impacts of the lack of access to basic livelihood and other social facilities such as primary health care services, access to safe drinking water and sanitation services.
Trocaire is implementing an integrated health and nutrition response in Gedo in four districts of Dolow, Luuq, Belethawa and Gabaharey covering host, IDP and marginalized communities in hard to reach areas.
This project will focus on lifesaving management of acute malnutrition through provision of treatment services in line with the Somalia IMAM framework. This project will target approximately 18,453 malnourished boys, girls, pregnant and lactating women with OTP and TSFP services in twenty sites in the four districts as well as address some gaps in other sites in coordination with the nutrition cluster. Children with SAM with complications will also be supported within stabilization centers. Additionally, the project will focus on a preventive approach through education, sensitization and awareness on nutrition with a focus on IYCF targeting beneficiaries as well as their caretakers. Women being the main custodians of the family health will form a key target audience for community sensitization messages on IYCF .They will also form part of community volunteers that effectively manage the community based nutrition activities. Men will be involved in these IYCF community based activities since they are key decision makers in promoting early health seeking behavior.
Activities proposed include the following: Screening and referral for acute malnutrition among children 6-59 months and PLW; treatment for Severe acute malnutrition with complications; treatment of acute malnutrition; treatment of moderate acute malnutrition for both children under 5 and PLW; community mobilization and sensitization at community level to promote adoption of age appropriate infant and young child feeding (IYCF) practices; community mobilization and sensitization at community level to promote adoption of age appropriate infant and young child feeding (IYCF) practices; individual IYCF counselling to both mothers and caretakers including fathers; hiring of one outreach team and staff to support TSFP/OTP services in Garbaharey; hiring of 10 additional community health and nutrition workers; capacity building of staff and community health and nutrition workers; and monitoring and evaluation activities.

Project Activities

Trocaire shall observe Somalia guidelines for integrated management of acute malnutrition in implementation of this project. Within the proposed response, Trocaire nutrition teams will ensure adoption of the basic nutrition service package (BNSP) to guarantee that micronutrient supplementation, immunization, de-worming, promotion and support for IYCF and maternal nutrition and care, prevention and management of common illnesses, monitoring and surveillance are done. This multisectoral approach will be deployed in an integrated health and nutrition program to make the project practical and effective. Accountability mechanism will be put in place to ensure transparency, accountability and instilling ownership of the project. Community members that form the VHC and DHB will be involved in decision making and management of the project which includes beneficiary identification, targeting and service delivery.Summary of ActivitiesI. Mitigation Measures

1. Risk that beneficiaries are exposed to violence during registration/distribution as a result of the distance to or location of humanitarian assistance and services.Mitigation: This risk is medium likelihood with high impact.Trocaire is in constant and regular dialogue with local authorities that inform of any anticipated attacks or insecurity. Should any security issues emerge, Trocaire has in place security protocols that priorities the safety of beneficiaries and staff. Trocaire consults the nutrition cluster and representative community leadership structures, including women groups, in identification of distribution sites. 2. Risk of no access to or awareness of protection assistance or referral services for survivors of protection incidents (i.e. GBV, child protection, evictions, explosive hazards, etc.)Mitigation: This risk is medium likelihood with high impactProtection is central to Trocaire’s work in Somalia and is integrated across project implementation to ensure safe and dignified programming. Trocaire has an active protection team that utilizes robust protection monitoring systems and that works closely with the Trocaire Health sector teams to provide targeted GBV services including Clinical management of Rape (CMR) and Psychosocial First Aid. A toll free GBV line has been established as part of the existing referral mechanisms to ensure women and girls receive assistance in a timely and safe manner. In addition, awareness creation on the services provided by Trocaire is ongoing through both mass media and field teams. As the regional GBV sub-cluster co-lead, Trocaire is well positioned to coordinate with other partners in protection and raise critical issues around needs and gaps in assistance hence better and more planned response. In addition, Trocaire avails resources that are required by survivors to increase their access to protection services, including cash grants and logistical support.3. Risk that marginalized groups, persons with disabilities, women, boys and girls, do not participate in consultations or decision-making processes for the project or are excluded from humanitarian services/assistance/activities. Mitigation: This risk is low likelihood and high impactTrocaire ensures that stakeholders are informed of the project at design stage and at inception. Because nutrition program are mostly targeted, the likelihood of exclusion are very rare since anyone who meets the admission criteria gets included. In addition, Trocaire will adopt a do no harm approach through which various actions will be taken to ensure inclusion of all hard-to-reach groups. This will include the collection of sex, age and disability disaggregated data, ensuring meaningful participation of beneficiaries in the project by consulting them to meet the different identified needs of these diverse groups within the target population. This will be done through Community outreach teams and Trocaire’s comprehensive Feedback and Response mechanism that allows a two- way interaction between Trocaire, its partners and project participants. This will be through face- to- face conversations, suggestion boxes placed at strategic places, the monitoring of information that is collected through a Toll Free line, and community leadership structures. Information will also be collected during regular monitoring activities by both MEAL and program teams.4. Risk that beneficiaries are not accessing/using complaint and feedback mechanisms. Mitigation: This risk is low likelihood and low impact.Trocaire has a complaint and feedback mechanism in place and as such the project will ensure that communities and beneficiaries are sensitized on the available options of raising complaints or giving and receiving feedback. Trocaire’s feedback and response system provides a safe, accessible and effective channel for project beneficiaries to exercise their right to give feedback and raise complaints for a response or redress to be given.
