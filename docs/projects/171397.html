Project Summary

rt community mobilization and screening as well as micro-nutrient supplementation for PLW (multiple micro-nutrients and Iron folate) and Vitamin A supplementation for children between 6-59 months. It will also provide de-worming for children 12-59 months and pregnant women in the 2nd and 3rd trimester as well as counselling and promotion on infant and young child feeding compounded with health and hygiene promotion. Moreover, measles immunization will be provided to infants living within the catchment areas of the target health centers who are not immunized against measles. Systematic defaulter tracing for immunization services will also be conducted at community level.
In order to strengthen the accountability with affected population, we will introduce Mother-led MUAC approach to increase detection, identification and early referral of malnourished children. ANPPCANSOM will train mothers to use MUAC tapes to enable mothers screen, detect oedema and self-referral of their children to nutrition sites.
ANPPCANSOM is committed to ensuring that protection of beneficiaries and do not harm principle is prioritized into all program responses. Protection principles will be integrated into the project in order to improve the nutrition service delivery and guarantee the protection of the population from exposure to threats related to humanitarian assistance by empowering beneficiaries to claim their rights, and to prompt duty-bearers to comply with their obligations.
ANPPCANSOM is accountable to the women, men, boys and girls whose lives it aims to improve, and places this responsibility at the core of its programming. ANPPCANSOM is committed to ensure that people receiving support participate in and influence all steps of the program life-cycle, including initial assessment, project design, beneficiary selection, implementation, monitoring and evaluation. We will open up effective feedback channels as well as complaints and response mechanisms to enable ANPCPANSOM and its partners know what impact programs are having on beneficiaries and intends to incorporate feedback or address problems rapidly, including prevention of sexual assault and exploitation while enabling the deprived, disadvantaged and the marginalized to have a voice.

Project Activities

* Increasing access to and utilization of basic nutrition services package to under-five children and PLW using both fixed an outreach service delivery models. * Supporting treatment of moderate and severe acute malnutrition through 38 health centres and 4 stabilization centers scattered in Lasqoray, Eilafweyn, Oodweine, Sheikh, Berbera, Hargeisa, Gabiley and Zeila districts. * Screening and assessing the nutrition status of children less than five years as well as of pregnant and lactating women using nutrition cluster wasting guidelines in the context of COVID-19. * Treating and rehabilitating estimated 7,362 SAM cases affecting children less than five years through OTP services using nutrition cluster wasting guidelines in the context of COVID-19. * Treating an estimated 29,431 MAM cases affecting children in less than five years using nutrition cluster wasting guidelines in the context of COVID-19 * Treating an estimated 10,624 MAM cases affecting pregnant and lactating women using nutrition cluster wasting guidelines in the context of COVID-19. * Treating and rehabilitating an estimated 1,104 SAM cases with medical complications through stabilization hospitals using nutrition cluster wasting guidelines in the context of COVID-19 * Strengthening local technical capacity (health and nutrition staff and community) to manage and sustain IMAM program. * Preventing malnutrition through behavior change communication using IYCF counselling and support using nutrition cluster IYCF guidelines in the context of COVID-19. * Increasing the knowledge of health and nutrition staff to prevent and respond to GBV and protection and equipping them with the skills to mitigate GBV and protection risks. * Provide training for mothers and on Mother-led MUAC approach to enable mothers identify, detect and diagnose malnutrition in order to improve early identification and early referral of malnourished children.

Mitigation Measures

ANPPCANSOM will effectively handle any risks encountered by integrating and mainstreaming risk management within the institutional management of the organization such as operations, business continuity, human resources, finance, legal and public relations. We will reconcile different perceptions of risk between HQ office and field-based personnel. We will also balance programming needs and adequate security support for our staff by determining ‘acceptable’ risk before a critical incident, not after. ANPPCANSOM demonstrated the added value of investment in risk management and maintaining this perception.
