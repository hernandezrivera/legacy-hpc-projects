Project Summary

The project is aiming to support communities in the Blue Nile, North, West, and South Kordofan, and Kassala states and the project targeted the IDPs, Returnees, and vulnerable host community by Multi-sectorial response "Education, Protection(CP & GBV), WASH, FSL and Nutrition" interventions with a total budget of 2,731,350 USD.
The education activities will target 9,660 children(473 Men, 493 Women, 4260 Boys, and 4434 girls) from IDPs, Returnees, and Vulnerable Host communities in (South Kordofan (Kadugli & Rashad)/Blue Nile (Al Tadamon &Baw)/West Kordofan (El Nuhood)/North Kordofan (Bara&Al Rahad)/Kassala (Reaifi Kassala) through Provide School kits to primary school children Provide teacher kits to primary school teachers Provide recreational kits to secondary schools Provide hygiene kits to upper primary school children Provide temporary Handwashing Stations established Distribution hard copy of COVID19 to School Protocols PTAs (primary school) participating in development and implementation of School Improvement Plan HHs that received a conditional cash grant to support primary students to attend school Construct 8 classrooms in primary school Rehabilitate 16 classrooms in primary school construct Latrines with gender-segregated / rehabilitate Latrines with gender-segregated Teachers/school management members trained on School-Related Gender-Based Violence Prevention Schools with child protection referral pathway established Teachers trained on COVID19 Teacher Preparedness Package.
the WASH interventions targeted 57,750 people(11,319M, 11,781W, 16,979, and 17,672G) from IDPS, Returns and affected HC in the Blue Nile (At Tadamon & Baw), Kassala (Reifi Kassla), South Kordofan (Al Buram&Um Durein), and West Kordofan (Ghubaish&An Nuhud)through the drill of 7 new hand pumps, install of 4 new water yards, rehabilitation of 14 hand pumps. AORD will construct/install 500 new households/s in HHs and operation and maintenance of 1000 existing latrines. AORD will be Constructed 25 handwashing facilities in a communal setup and provide 800 personal hygiene materials.
AORD provides Child protection and GBV services through target 3,280 Women in BNS (Tadamon (Book community)/Baw (Wega community) and SK(Leri(Um Giah Community)/Um Duerian(Karam Community) for GBV and 4,332 children in BNS (At Tadamon - BN Baw), North Kordofan (Bara, Ar Rahad ), West Kordofan (An Nuhud, Ghubaish) and Kassala ( Refi Kassla) for CP, the GBV intervention through construct 4 women Center(semi-permanent), rehabilitate 4 women's centers. AORD will train 4 community protection networks and AORD will conduct 4 community Awareness raising. CP interventions are to Provide rehabilitation and reintegration activities for Children released from the armed groups and armed forces. provide legal assistance Assistance for Children with Disability, procurement, and distribution of tents for emergency CFS Run and maintenance of Child-friendly spaces with Furniture stationaries and equipment.
AORD targets 8,820 children from IPDs and affected HC in (South Kordofan (Kadugli & Rashad)/Blue Nile (Al Tadamon &Baw)/West Kordofan (El Nuhood)/North Kordofan (Bara&Al Rahad)/Kassala (Reaifi Kassala) through Nutrition activities, AORD will provide Outpatient management of severe acute malnutrition without complications for children 6-59 months, vitamin A supplements for 8,820 children, and Infant and Young Child feeding (IYCF) counseling.

1 - Needs

Blue Nile state and Kordofan states classified as war-affected states since the last conflict erupted in Sudan in 2011. The conflict has resulted in massive new displacements and badly deteriorated the basic services and affected the social wellbeing situation in these states. This created vulnerable groups lost their homes and shelters and they have received very limited support from humanitarian actors. There were many cases reported of massive Violations of girls, boys, women, and men's rights. This resulted in massive displacement within and outside of these States.As per the Inter-Agency Rapid Need Assessment conducted in October 2018; There are 38 schools in Rashad locality, only 7 of them in Rashad town, and 31 are in locality villages and towns. The total number of in school children is 6,817 girls and 5,556 boys (12,373) from both IDP and host communities. Most of the IDPs in Rashad live near schools but are unable to access them because of a lack of school fees, textbooks, and other learning materials as well as school uniforms besides the cost of school meals. Also, most of the school-age children from IDP families are working to support their families. There are 6,817 girls and 5,556 boys (12,373) from IDP and host communities attending school in Rashad. The affected population was displaced locally from 14 villages in four localities mainly Abu Kershola, Al Abassiya, Rashad, and Heiban, and were re-settled by the GoS in thirteen locations within Abu Kershola locality. IOM verified 7,007 individuals of IDPs (1,530 men, 1,701 women, 1,964 girls, and 1,812 boys) who remain in displacement and are unable to return to their places of initial displacement. IDPs get part of their food from limited farming in areas where they have access to agricultural land, however, the food production is inadequate due to the poor state of land and lack of farming implements. Others sustain their families through working as farm laborers, cutting firewood for sale, or making charcoal, while there is a third category who hires their boys as herders. Others reduce the number and quantity of food consumed per meal. As per the Blue Nile Joint Needs Assessment conducted in September 2018, a large number of the wells were either not functioning because the pump had broken, had gone dry, or was overcrowded. The situation worsens until the rains come and underground aquifers are replenished. Repairs to damaged pumps are urgently needed. Wells will have to be deepened and new wells drilled, equipped with hand pumps. In terms of personal hygiene, latrines are not widely used and handwashing facilities are almost non-existent. An active and prolonged awareness-raising campaign needs to be implemented that changes people’s understanding of the public health risks associated with open defecation and eating with dirty hands in Bau and Tadamon localities in BNS.Due to the conflict in WK among different tribes and disputes towards the resources and areas exposed by flash floods, the Sudanese nationals migrated from Darfur to WKS, resulting in raising the prices of the entire commodities and pressure on the basic services in some of the localities one of these Nuhood. NK State went through hard times because of natural crisis as other states in Sudan, besides that and because it border areas of conflicts in South Kordofan state that another burden on it by the huge flow of IDPs and HC and with its limited resources that creates high pressure on the basic services and the labor market. All this leads to the rise of socio-economical hazards represented in (internal conflicts, lack of basic services provision, child labor, and unaccompanied children, etc.) Thus there is a critical need to provide multi-sectorial services. Kassala is one of the Sudanese border states that suffer from limited access to basic services especially in the EDU, WASH, FSL, and NUT sectors, there weak enrollment in schools due to many Scio-economic and Cultural factors.
