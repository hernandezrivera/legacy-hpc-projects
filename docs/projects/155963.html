Project Summary

The aim of the project is to reduce the dropout rate in the Kafr Naha area in the southwestern Aleppo countryside by retrieving the students who left school to study in Kafr Naha, which includes 11 schools (elementary, preparatory and secondary) with 5500 students (200 students)high schools. (800 preparatory - 4,500 primary) through the rehabilitation of one class in each school (ie, 11 class in 11 schools) to receive dropouts (850 students including 782 IDPs), rehabilitation and intensive education to continue with their peers Next year.
Applications for students enrolled in 11 educational centers will be received and distributed according to the information survey carried out by the educational supervisor and specialized committee as follows: (1st to 3rd - 4th to 6th - 7th to 9th) inclusive and geographical distribution.
11 classrooms will be distributed to schools, in addition to the separation between males and females starting from the second stage and provide School bags for each stage.
The approved curricula will be provided
And to provide the teachers (male and female) with monthly salaries.
The duration of the stage will be accompanied by the duration of official school and the period (3 months). The student will be registered after the completion of the course in proportion to his age or registered in the next stage immediately.

Needs

According to HNO 2019 ratings reported that protracted crisis in Syria, now in its eighth year, has forced 2.1 million, over one third of children, out-of-school and has put a further 1.3 million children at risk of dropping out. Over 5.8 million school-aged children and about 121,972 education personnel are in need of reached Abar studied the status of dropouts from schools in Kafr Naha. The evaluation included knowledge of the causes of dropouts, which were first and foremost poverty, the need for work and early marriage. The number of dropouts was 850 ( 782 IDPs ) in this region (468 females and 382 males)Distributed as follows 164 females 134 males (7-10) 210 females 172 males (11-15) 94 females 76 males (16-18)The need for 11 classrooms in the 11 schools identified in Kafr Naha was identified, and suggestions were taken from teachers and parents to overcome this phenomenon.Data collection was carried out within two weeks, covering all schools. The Abrar team of 5 people (2 females + 3 males) collected data and sent it to the Information Management Unit at Gaziantep Senior Investigation Office where the data received were analyzed, The need to address this problem in the area of Kafrna.A special questionnaire was developed in three phases:- The Information Management Unit has developed a preliminary draft of the questionnaire covering the points relating to the status of dropouts.- Review and finalize the questionnaire, and new questions have been added in order to come up with a better assessment.- The questionnaire was applied to a small group of the population of the region (male and female), and improvements were made to the questionnaire to achieve the desired goal?Abrar employs 5 researchers (2 females + 3 males). Each researcher is assigned two or more schools according to the number of students.
