Project Summary

In line with SO3, PUI aims at developing and enhancing income-generating capacities of vulnerable populations in Syria through implementing the following activities:
- Supporting people to start re-start a social or business entrepreneurship startup, targeting the following locations:
o Aleppo (Jebel Saman): 130 direct beneficiaries
o Dar’a (city): 50 direct beneficiaries
o Deir ez-Zor (city): 70 direct beneficiaries
o Lattakia (city): 90 direct beneficiaries
o Rural Damascus (city, Duma): 295 direct beneficiaries
- Enrolment of individuals in apprenticeship and on-job training, targeting the following locations:
o Aleppo (Jebel Saman): 90 direct beneficiaries
o Dar’a (city): 35 direct beneficiaries
o Deir ez-Zor (city): 65 direct beneficiaries
o Lattakia (city): 75 direct beneficiaries
o Rural Damascus (Duma): 50 direct beneficiaries

0 - Project Summary (extended)

PUI policies and operational guidelines align with IASC's Commitments to Accountability to Affected Populations. PUI ensures accountability by placing affected populations at the center of all decisions and actions that affect their lives. Thus, targeted populations will be involved throughout all phases of the project to feed into the design of the interventions and guide the identification of both activities and beneficiaries based on PUI’s selection criteria. PUI aims to build a program to meet the needs of girls, boys, women and men living in intervention locations, taking into consideration vulnerabilities, to enhance their access to basic services, foster their self-sufficiency, but also promote social cohesion while ensuring that the program would have no negative side effects and that the risk for girls, boys, women and men to be exposed to any kind of violence when accessing assistance would be mitigated.
