Project Summary

The project aims bridging existing WASH gaps in Middle Shabelle, Lower Shabelle and Bay Regions Somalia through timely provision of Emergency WASH services and well as development of WASH infrastructure. The project targets the most vulnerable populations including IDPs, returnees and marginalized host community members (women,men, boys and girls) including those with disabilities and the elderly that are discriminated from accessing essential WASH services. The plight of the targeted populations is worsened by combined impacts of Covid-19 pandemic, very serious 2019 Deyr and 2020 Gu flooding, past successive drought seasons and intermittent conflicts that has compromised existing limited WASH infrastructures and services and exposed them to dangers of outbreak of AWD/cholera and other sanitation and hygiene related diseases hence need for timely implementation of the project. The project targets a total of vulnerable 15000 persons (6240 women, 5760 men,1500 boys and 1500 girls) in Middle Shabelle, Lower Shabelle and Bay Regions Somalia.

Project Activities

1. Drilling of a new boreholes in Middle Shabelle Region 2. Rehabilitation of 4 boreholes in Lower Shabelle and Bay Region 3. Upgrade of 25 shallow wells in Bay, Lower Shabelle and Middle Shabelle Regions4. Training of 50 water committee members (men and women) 5. Water Distribution by Voucher for 5 weeks in 25 drought affected remote villages in Lower Shabelle and Bay Regions6. Construction of 50 new gender sensitive latrines and suitable for the disabled, fitted with hand washing facilities and lockable doors and are lighted at night in IDP camps in Bay and Lower Shabelle Regions. 7. Distribution of 3000 Hygiene kits in AWD/cholera in Bay, Middle Shabelle and Lower Shabelle Regionsto villages with cases of AWD/Cholera and other water born diseases8. Conducting Emergency hygiene promotion in targeted remote villages prone toAWD/Cholera outbreak and other water borne diseases in Bay, Middle Shabelle and Lower Shabelle Regions

Mitigation Measures

1. Risk that beneficiaries are exposed to violence during registration/distribution as a result of the distance to or location of humanitarian assistance and service: Mitigation Measure : Enhancing security at the project sites by hiring security staff as well as ensuring that WASH services are located at places within clearly defined WASH cluster sphere standards 2. Risk of women, girls and boys exposed to sexual violence, including sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA), when accessing humanitarian assistance. Mitigation Measure: Ensuring all staff sign PSEA code of conduct and adhering with it as well as establishing and strengthening SEA reporting procedures for its effectiveness3. Risk of no access to or awareness of protection assistance or referral services for survivors of protection incidents (i.e. GBV, child protection, evictions, explosive hazards, etc.)Mitigation measure: Conducting awareness on protection assistance for survivors of protection incidences4.
