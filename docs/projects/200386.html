Project Summary

(1) To increase motivation and improve capacity of community based actors in interpersonal communication, community mobilization and coordination of Cholera RCCE activities among front-line health workers, youth, traditional and faith leaders. (2) To engage of community based groups/local governance structures to plan, implement, monitor and utilize evidence-based social and behavioral change communication strategies for increased community awareness, demand, acceptance and utilization of available services and key innovative approaches to advance Cholera response in targeted communities (3) To improve community and interpersonal engagement for behavioral change, demand generation and utilization of integrated health and RCCE services to reduce transmission of cholera outbreak and risk of waterborne and water related diseases in targeted communities in high burden districts in Malawi. (4) To understand the anthropological factors affecting change of behavior and the current knowledge, attitudes, practices, drivers, bottlenecks and communication channels on cholera response to improve the design and implementation of interventions that would improve community based Cholera response in high burden districts in Malawi.
