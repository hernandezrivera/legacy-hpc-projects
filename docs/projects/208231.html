Project Summary

The programme aims to improve living conditions and restore dignity to displaced people and host communities in Cabo Delgado province. Caritas Pemba will help a total of 20,000 households, of which 15,000 will be returnee families and 5,000 will be internally displaced people.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

As mentioned above, the project will benefit 20000 people in the districts of Ancuabe and Macomia, among whom 9000 women are expected to be assisted, equivalent to 45% of the entire project, 50% corresponds to the total number of men and 5% are children of both sexes and disabled people of both sexes.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

- We will build 15,000 shelters in the resettlement centres and return areas and repair 5,000 shelters in the host communities. - Distribution of non-food kits to the target group; -Distribution of materials for repairing and rehabilitating shelters -Provision of shelter repair/rehabilitation services-Improve accessibility of shelters, including installation for people with disabilities;-Train craftsmen in shelter construction and repair;-Dissemination of PSEA-related messages and feedback to beneficiary communities;-Gender-based violence prevention safety audit for planning to avoid trading favors during kit distribution.-Post-distribution monitoring;

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Caritas is committed to integrating protection and gender into all programmes to promote safety, dignity and meaningful access, especially for those who may be most vulnerable and marginalised, including individuals with disabilities and the elderly. Caritas applies a framework to support the integration of protection principles into humanitarian activities. The project will be implemented based on the Global Protection Cluster approach and the principles of ensuring access to impartial assistance and services, especially for the most vulnerable and at-risk groups; accountability to beneficiaries; supporting the development of self-protection capacities; and ensuring "do not harm" by adapting activities to mitigate identified protection risks. Finally, during a monitoring visit, beneficiaries will be consulted on the degree of satisfaction and will be informed to donors and local government.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

In addition, Caritas staff will explain the modality, i.e. the general distribution of shelter kits. general distribution of shelter kits. Dissemination of information on how distribution is adopted under the "Do No Harm" principles, size of guardianship for beneficiaries, and complaints mechanism. The target community will be informed about the project deadline, the quantity of items to be donated, the donors and also the quality of the objects to be distributed will be presented to the communities for approval and a sample will be left in the community for people to compare. Finally, during a monitoring visit, the beneficiaries will be asked about the degree of satisfaction and will be informed of this by the donors and the local government.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

As it is considered an inclusive project, people in need will be the focus of Caritas, including the elderly to identify specific needs for infrastructure adaptation. The infrastructure will be adapted to these needs. Persons with disabilities will be one of the priorities during distributions. Distribution points will be serviced, ensuring ease of access for community members, especially people with disabilities and women. If there are any people with disabilities and if they have some leadership character and ability to do some work, such as registering, organizing beneficiaries, writing reports, they can be included in the field team. For those who cannot walk, some transportation will be provided to transport their NFIs.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The focus of the project is the construction and repair of shelters, which aims to deliver tarpaulins, construction material, in short, the entire shelter kit, depending on the situation of vulnerability. Field agents will be trained to explain to local target communities how to keep the environment clean, avoiding littering in inappropriate places, as well as promoting the use of latrines to avoid infection of the environment. Avoiding digging big holes, throwing garbage anywhere and promoting debates to ensure the well-being of society as a whole.
