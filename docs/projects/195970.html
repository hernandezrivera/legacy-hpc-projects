Project Summary

LPG assistance will be provided to both Rohingya and the host community in camp-25 and Baharchhara Union respectively in Teknaf Upazila from January 2023 to December 2023 for ensuring safe and dignified living conditions and contributing to environmental sustainability and decreasing the dependency on forest wood for cooking purposes. As per our plan, we will distribute LPG refill services and will cover the whole camp among 1769 Rohingya Families throughout the upcoming year. Apart from this, we also plan to continue our LPG refill services among 750 HHs for the host community for all ward of Baharchara Union under the Teknaf Upazila. In this connection, the total beneficiary under the LPG refill service will be 2519 for the next JRP year 2023 and Nabolok will distribute LPG refill services among the targeted beneficiaries of both communities as per pre-prepared schedule on the basis of family size and category as per the project plan. Besides these, to reduce the probability of fire incidents through expired LPG stoves, Nabolok distributed new LPG stoves to 100% of the Rohingya HHs of Camp-25 in 2022. The project conducted a training session on fire safety, especially on LPG safety and security. That's why they can gather some sort of knowledge on fire safety issues, the beneficiaries have increased their knowledge, and applied the knowledge to cooking easily without fear more quickly and safely. It helps them to mitigate the chances to create fire incidents by LPG in our working areas. Nabolok also plans to repair all the existing stoves of the host community HHs and also the distributed stoves in camp-25 of the Rohingya households in 2022. In addition, to address any hazards in our working areas at both camp and host community area, we will allocate some contingency funding in this project proposal which is 2% of the total proposed budget so that we can provide LPG support among approximately 225 the affected beneficiaries by providing new LPG cylinder and stove just after the incidents. This project also has the plan to provide home delivery services by porter support for Extremely Vulnerable Individuals (EVI) i.e. elder persons, pregnant and lactating women, persons with special needs, child-headed families, etc. as a priority basis due to reduce their vulnerability as well as we will provision wide queue place with shed minimize their waiting anguish. We also have a separate queue for women and men so that they won’t face any kind of harassment and allocate disable friendly space. We have also prioritized PWDs, elderly people, and Lactating mothers during distribution. In this connection, we will hire 84 Rohingya people and 36 host community as volunteers, they will perform some small-scale work just crowd controlling & management, maintaining COVID 19 protocol like maintaining social distancing during queues, and ensuring sanitized hands to the beneficiaries before entering the distribution center. These 120 volunteers will get the working opportunity and contribute to their family expenses and create small-scale Income Generating Activities (IGA) by using the wages.

1. Needs
1 - Identified Needs

The Multi-Sector Needs Assessment (MSNA) report- 2021 stated that more than 97% HHs from the Rohingya community have received LPG refill services from humanitarian actors throughout 2021 and these statistics are also very close to our own data which we have collected from our working areas. Up to October 2022, Nabolok had covered 100% of targeted HHs including home delivery service to the Extremely Vulnerable Individuals (EVI), both camp and host community with LPG refill services. We have covered 30% of host communities' households through LPG Refill services of previous years, and we will keep this percentage in the upcoming year as well. We will allocate some contingency funding in this project proposal which is 2% of the total proposed budget. So that, we can distribute LPG cylinders and cooking stoves in case of fully damaged by any incidents in our working areas at both camp and host levels. In this connection, we will hire 84 Rohingya people and 36 host community as volunteers, they will perform some small-scale work just crowd controlling & management, maintaining COVID-19 protocol like maintaining social distancing during queues, and ensuring sanitized hands to the beneficiaries before entering the distribution center. These 120 volunteers will get the working opportunity and contribute to their family expenses and create small-scale Income Generating Activities (IGA) by using the wages.

3. Cross-cutting Themes
3.1 - Protection

Ensuring safety and security of the beneficiaries, the project will select the distribution center consultation with all the respective parties in camp-25. Considering the protection issues, Nabolok will ensure different facilities for beneficiaries like waiting shed, necessary fan for air, safe drinking water, elderly support including disable and pregnant women. The project has a plan to provide home delivery services by porter support for Extremely Vulnerable Individuals (EVI) i.e. elder persons, pregnant and lactating women, persons with special needs, child-headed families, etc. as a priority basis due to reducing their vulnerability as well as we will provide wide queue place with shed minimize their waiting anguish. We also have a separate queue for women and men so that they will not face any kind of harassment and allocate disable friendly space. We will also give priority PWDs, elderly people, and Lactating mothers during distribution.

3.2 - Gender

To ensure safe and dignified living conditions and contribute to environmental sustainability, decrease forest wood dependency for cooking purposes, the project we will distribute LPG refill service and cooking stoves among the selected HHs in our project areas. The females and adolescents have engaged in the cooking work at HH level, so the project team will consider given priority in providing LPG refill for women during distribution. The project will establish breastfeeding corner for lactating women in area of distribution center, provide porter assistance to reach LPG refill to their house which HHs have especially vulnerable i.e. old citizens, pregnant, Lactating women, PWD, etc., and haven’t any other people to receive LPG from the distribution center. In the distribution center ensure different queues for males and females. The project will give priority the older people and pregnant, Lactating women, and PWD.

3.3 - Age including child safeguarding

The project will ensure safeguarding by maintaining safeguarding policies and procedures. To ensure breastfeeding corner for lactating women, porter support for the pregnant mother and safe drinking water for all especially the children. The distribution center will be in a safe and secure place with a shed for all, they will enjoy for receive their services. There will be a seating arrangement for children and elderly people and (EVI). For safety, the project will not distribute LPG cylinders to the under 18 children but will ensure volunteer support for them for carrying cylinders to their transport. Parents need not go to the forest or other areas for collecting firewood, due to the project will ensure LPG cylinders for the targeted Rohingya and host community people so that parents especially women could give time and take care of their children. Mothers can give food support to their family members easily due they will have LPG refills and cooking stoves.

3.4 - Diversity

The project will conduct a household assessment for selecting beneficiary households; those families will be eligible to receive the LPG refill service from Nabolok and have data cards at camp level and have the vulnerability of the host community people. Considering women-headed PW&D families, ethnicity, third gender, different cultural backgrounds, and various lifestyles will be selected as a beneficiary and provide porter support to EVI families for ensuring LPG refills at HH level and necessary porter will be appointed at the distribution point during distribution.

3.5 - Accountability to Affected Populations (AAP)

The project will accountable to ensure dignitary and quality support for the affected people. To address the crisis and reduce the dependency of Rohingya household on the forest for firewood collection in JRP 2023. Providing alternative fuel, i.e. LPG and improved cooking stoves is one of the crosscutting migratory measures to address both physical and environmental impacts. The minimum two accountability types of accountability mechanisms are in place. Projects staffs will play a positive and supportive role to ensure quality support in time. The beneficiary care shares their suggestions, feedback, and complaints with the project staff verbally, and in writing using a complaint box. We will conduct regular monitoring and door-to-door visit for identifying the problem and collect feedback about our services. The project will welcome complaints and feedback for further improvement and will be ready to respond to the complaint, and feedback on a priority basis.
