Needs

• Major hazards affecting health in Sudan droughts, floods, epidemics, internal conflict .There are frequent disease outbreaks of measles, hepatitis E, Shakinvonia, scabies and acute watery diarrhea. The prevalence of non-communicable diseases is on the rise with frequent cross border population movement increases the likelihood of the importation of communicable diseases, including wild polio-virus. Shortage and inequitable distribution of Health Worker, a weak HIS and shortages of drugs and medical supplies exacerbate the overall health situation.• Internally displaced people over 15 years in Darfur, vulnerable refugees and asylum seekers in Kordofan are in high need of humanitarian assistance, as well as host communities in many areas with lack access to services and support. • The 2018 Humanitarian Needs Overview identifies 5.5 million people in need of humanitarian assistance across Sudan including 1.76 million IDPs in need in Darfur.• The health status of newly arrived refugees and those in protracted situations remains a concern, especially among South Sudanese refugees arriving from areas of South Sudan facing emergency levels of acute malnutrition. Success in reducing childhood mortality requires more than the availability of adequate health services with well trained personnel. Management Childhood Illness strategy, promoted by the WHO (CAH)Nearly all the 1.2 million child deaths each year caused by acute watery diarrhea could be prevented by correct home care for diarrhea and health recommendations. Improved hygiene practices, including safe disposal of excreta and hand washing, can reduce the incidence of diarrhea by more than 10%(40.8% of children with diarrhea in the last two weeks did not seek advice for treatment MICS 2014)ARI cause 2 million child deaths annually. • According to SUDAN MICS 2014 under 5 mortality rates at 68 per thousand life births, distributed as highest 91.4 deaths per 1,000 live births in West Darfur, 77.4 in Central Darfur and 82.1 in West Khordofan respectively, Due to acute respiratory infections, diarrhea, measles, malaria or malnutrition – or a combination of these conditions.• Success in reducing childhood mortality requires more than the availability of adequate health services with well trained personnel, As families have the major responsibility for caring for their children, success requires a partnership between Families also need to be able to respond appropriately when their children are sick, seeking appropriate and timely assistance when children need additional care, recommended treatments.• Appropriate care seeking with treatment, could reduce mortality by over 20 %. Malnutrition is associated with more than 50% child deaths. In most places malnutrition is caused by feeding practices that could be improved using existing resources.

Activities

1. Improve the main practices of society and the family through:1.1 Establish 15 health committees in the target communities, to help promote environmental sanitation and reduce diseases transmitted as a result of pollution.1.2 Provide 15 training courses on the main family practices of community health promoters in target communities, to raise awareness and sound health practices.1.3 Conduct coordination with the relevant UN agencies, non-governmental organizations and the Ministry of Health, through coordination meetings at the district level where field reports and updates to the health situation are shared.1.4 Provide training on key family practices for school teachers in finding ways to provide promotional activities in targeted communities to raise awareness and sound health practices.2. Promote basic health practices through:2.1 Production and dissemination of information materials to targeted communities, to be a message that facilitates the interaction and contributes to the provision of correct health information.2.2 Provide health practices awareness sessionsusing health facilities operated through ministry of health, INGOs, through the local language in the target communities.2.3 Support SMOH during Child Day Celebrations, Acute respiratory Day Celebrations, to raise awareness regarding proper health practices, and to instigate the importance of vaccination doses for children.3. Promote emergency preparedness and response among communities and community health promoters:3.1 Through regular reports / reports on needs and early notification.3.2 Build community-based capacity to respond to disease outbreaks through 15 training on a simplified health information system at the community level.
