Project Summary

Project description
This project will offer education plus food for 900 displaced, returnees and vulnerable communities in Lagawa West Kordofan State, and 600 in El Roseres Blue Nile State. Education is offered for those who lost their chances for education. The project will target children and youth from girls and boys, who lost their chances for education, being drop outs, or those who have got low quality few classes' education or not got the chance at all. This project will target also young married girls plus women and men generally with literacy classes using Reflect methodology.
One meal will be offered for all beneficiaries. It will reduce their vulnerability, increase resilience and enhance the success of the whole program.
The Goal of Asmaa is to help the victims of war and vulnerable communities to get education and knowledge, to help them and their communities to be resilient and to fight poverty and hunger. This project will offer Alternative Learning Program (ALP) for children both girls and boy's dropouts or those who didn't go to school, and also for both sexes of youth, offering Unicef curriculum designed for different age groups. Regarding those who have got some classes the project will concentrate on a curriculum for two years to enable them to sit for primary school certificate. The project will offer literacy classes for young married girls mainly and women, plus some circles for men. This project will offer training and sensitization around some lacked knowledge( like gender issues as FGM, child marriage, polygamy, maternity & child health, nutrition, environment, plus civic education ..etc) and vocational training for youth from both sexes and life skills for all the targeted segments, plus establishing cooperatives to enhance nutrition, production and income.
Partners:
This project will be implemented with partnership with Unicef, Ministry of Education, Council of Literacy and adult Education. Experts from these institutions will help the organization to set the curriculum, set- up the schools, training of the teachers, and follow up of the quality of implementation of the project until students being graduated. NGO's and CBO's in the area plus candidates of above partners will be part of planning and implementation of the whole project.
The project will use the formal schools in the area, rent them to offer education for those needy communities living near by the schools, otherwise other places could be used as clubs, kindergartens and community centers …etc.

Needs

Education needs are high in these communities, as some of these communities are returnees due to war and others are displaced or vulnerable communities. These communities face a lot of war hazards and complete insecurity due to war, they lack shelters, food, infrastructure for services generally in their areas, as roads, education and health services, due to lack of local government expenditure on these services, and lack of governmental support generally due to the fact priorities of citizens are not well ranked. International organizations work was restricted due to authorities. Education is not safe in these areas, schools are far from living area due to war, so sometimes children and parents are obliged to leave it. Education if available is not free as usually declared publically; vulnerable people cannot afford paying fees. These areas lack adequate number of schools relevant to population numbers, schools if found are very poor in infrastructure, teachers are few, not trained and with low income. Students lack proper furnished schools, educational materials as books and others, they lack good educational environment. Other children are not enrolled in schools at all. So most of the children doesn’t find chance to be registered or they leave school because cannot pay fees, or they leave it because of no use, due to poor situation.So youth also are either illiterate or with poor education, and this will minimize their choices and chances in the future.Girls face the same situation mentioned, adding to this the discrimination they face in these societies, boys are preferred, so they were married very early, losing their childhood and chances in life. From our experience women are more interested in literacy than men; they try to help their kids in schools, and education of women will improve the whole community.This project will try to offer support for those left behind, by offering them chances of education and offer them choices to continue studying or erase their illiteracy and give them knowledge and life skills plus vocational training mode of production for better chances in life. The project will offer food or a meal for students to lessen their vulnerability, increase their resilience and give them incentive for studying. This project will offer the following:• Open Alternative Learning Programs (ALP) schools for children who are not enrolled in schools or being drop outs, girls and boys• Open ALP classes for youth girls and boys • Open literacy classes for women mainly and some men • Open vocational training for youth• Offer training and sensitization program• Offer life skills• Establish cooperatives to produce food products to decrease vulnerability, increase resilience and support nutrition needs plus productive cooperatives to increase income.
