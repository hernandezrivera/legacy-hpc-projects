Project Summary

The project aims at supporting immediate and long term food needs of crisis affected 18500 persons (7696 women, 7104 men, 1850 boys and 1850 girls) particularly IDPs and most vulnerable non-IDPs (women, men, boys and girls) including those living with disabilities and the elderly in Wajid, Baidoa and Qansax Dheere Districts in Bay and Bakool Regions, Somalia that are facing severe food insecurity owing recurrent climatic shocks specifically combined impacts of poor and erratic Gu 2021 and below average Deyr 2020 rains compounded by previous successive drought seasons as well as floods coupled by impacts of desert locust infestation. The situation is exacerbated by conflict and insecurity that impacts on trade disruptions and further compromise the already depleted coping mechanisms of the vulnerable and marginalized IDPs and Non IDPs (women, men, boys and girls) including the elderly and persons with disabilities in the project targeted locations. Current weather forecast revealed likelihood of poor Deyr 2021 and high likelihood of severe drought till mid 2022 that will severely impact on food insecurity and escalate the already precarious malnutrition status among children (boys and girls) aged under 5 and pregnant lactation women (FSNAU-FEWS NET 2021 Post Gu Technical Release of September 9, 2021).
The above plight calls for timey interventions by providing appropriate safety nets while restoring already dilapidated livelihood conditions and increasing income sources of the crisis affected persons through provision of appropriate livelihood and technical support in rural and peri-urban settings and strengthen targeted community's and IDP's resilience to shocks.

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

1. Inclusion of minority groups such as minority clans, IDPs, women, elderly and persons with disabilities in will be ensured by their thorough participation in the consultation process on projects formulation including needs assessment, identification and prioritization where their concerns will form key decisions of the project planned activities. Moreover, they will actively participate in all stages of project implementation including continuous monitoring where their concerns will form part of major recommendations on the project implementation hence addressing their needs.2. The project will increase protection of women and girls from sexual violence by ensuring their safety during implementation of project activities where distribution of project activities will at centralized locations and at early hours of the day to limit them from covering long under the cover of darkness where most Sexual violence occur to mitigate risk of sexual violence against them.3. The project will be implemented in line with PSEA policy where targeted beneficiaries particularly women and girls will be sensitized of the project to ensure they are fully aware of the exact project activities and exact benefits to them in order to mitigate any form of sexual exploitation and abuse by the project staff and other relevant parties during the project. The project will further ensure that complaints and feedback mechanisms are in place and are easily accessible by the targeted beneficiaries and the community at large such as suggestion boxes and toll free hotlines4. The project will adhere to the principle of "Do no Harm" by ensuring that implemented activities do not became a source of conflict between the targeted beneficiaries and the communities.
