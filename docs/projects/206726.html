Project Summary

This intervention aims to promote the protection, social inclusion and psychosocial well being of the internally displaced people in the resettlement camps of 25 de Junho (Metuge District) and Cujupani (Ancuabe District) through the creation of Community Engagement Centers (CECs) in which children, and women and girls can participate in psychosocial and resilience building activities together with the members of the host communities. The CECs will run daily activities for both groups, children, and women and girls, aiming to improve Child Protection and prevention of GBV efforts within the centers while improving the psychosocial well-being of the participants. Alongside specific activities targeting to improve the Child Protection and Prevention of Gender Based Violence (GBV) efforts at the community level, the CECs also envisions awareness activities with the participation of the whole community on a number of topics such as prevention of GBV, MHPSS, referral pathways, hygiene promotion, nutrition, and more. Additionally, in order to increase the community ownership of the two spaces, it is expected to create a Steering Committee composed by comunnity members and relevant district services that will be in charge of the management and the decision-making of the activities and events that take place within the spaces.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

This intervention aims to promote the protection, social inclusion and psychosocial well being of the internally displaced people in the resettlement camps of 25 de Junho (Metuge District), Cujupani (Ancuabe District) and Mocimboa da Praia (MdP) through the creation of Community Engagement Centers (CECs) in which children, and women and girls can participate in psychosocial and resilience building activities together with the members of the host communities. The CECs will run daily activities for both groups, children, and women and girls, aiming to improve Child Protection and prevention of GBV efforts within the centers while improving the psychosocial well-being of the participants. Alongside specific activities targeting to improve the Child Protection and Prevention of Gender Based Violence (GBV) efforts at the community level, the CECs also envisions awareness activities with the participation of the whole community on a number of topics such as prevention of GBV, MHPSS, referral pathways, hygiene promotion, nutrition, and more. Additionally, in order to increase the community ownership of the two spaces, it is expected to create a Steering Committee composed by comunnity members and relevant district services that will be in charge of the management and the decision-making of the activities and events that take place within the spaces.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

All the activities of the project will be conducted in all districts, namely Metuge, Ancuabe and Mocimboa da Praia. Outcome 1: 13,019 IDP in 25 de Junho and Cujupani resettlement camps, and returnee population in Mocimboa da Praia improve their psychosocial well-being. Output 1.1.: 32 community facilitators’ and community members capacity to enhance the wellbeing and resilience of 760 children, and 240 women and girls is strengthened. Activity 1.1.1.: 4-days initial Protection, MHPSS, Child Protection, GBV prevention, and referral pathways training to 32 activists at the 3 CECs. Activity 1.1.2.: Quarterly 2-days refresher training with the 32 activists facilitating the activities at the CECs, and weekly group supervision sessions to ensure quality-service provision. Activity 1.1.3.: Monthly engagements with the overall communities (13,019 people), including community leaders, local schools, and community-based organizations for awareness raising on relevant issues affecting the participants of the CECs, encourage collaboration and open communication channels.Activity 1.1.4.: Door to door visits at the communities to ensure proper identification, accompaniment and follow up of cases identified at the CECs and to raise awareness on relevant issues related to the CECs.Activity 1.1.5.: Establishment of 3 Steering Committees (1 per CEC) at the community level to manage and make decisions affecting the functioning and the purpose of the CECs to increase the appropriation of the spaces by the communities. Activity 1.1.6.: Maintenance of the infrastructures of the 3 CECs, including the rooms, the perimeter and the WASH facilities (i.e.: hand washing stations and latrines) Output 1.2.: 760 children have access to effective CFSs and related services at the CECs Activity 1.2.1.: Creation and implementation of weekly curriculums to orient resilience building and psycho-social activities for the 760 children, including children with disabilities.Activity 1.2.2.: Assessment of children's needs at the community level and identification of specialized partners that can support and provide specialized services and facilitate workshops and sessions at the CFS for children and caregivers to become an integrated space offering multi-sectoral activities (e.g.: health, WASH, nutrition, civil registration, etc.) Output 1.3.: 240 women and girls, including women and girls at-risk or survivors of GBV, improve psychosocial well being. Activity 1.3.1.: 5-days complementary training on GBV sensitive case care for the 32 activists facilitating the activities for 240 women and girls at the WGSS within the CECs. Activity 1.3.2.: Creation and implementation of weekly curriculums to conduct age and gender appropriate activities to improve women and girls psycho-social well-being, resilience, and life-skills (lectures and guided discussions on diverse topics, beading, play, singing, etc.), especially focusing on the inclusion of women and girls with disabilities. Activity 1.3.3.: Identification, referral and case care of GBV cases following a survivor-centered approach. Acitivity 1.3.4.: Discussions and dialogues involving women and girls, midwives, community and religious leaders, and men and boys to increase awareness and understanding on GBV issues. Activity 1.3.5.: Prevention on GBV, awareness raising activities (e.g.: dramatizations, radio, discussions and dialogues, etc.) with all community members.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

This protection project aims to improve the inclusion of IDPs within the host communities in the resettlement centers where they currently live. However, activities aim to target both host and IDP communities to conduct resilience and peacebuilding activities. It will also especifically hace a Prevention of GBV and Child Protection component, but it will also conduct awareness raising sessions and activities with all the communities as a whole to avoid, partly to avoid creating tensions.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

Within the CECs there are Women and Girls Safe Spaces (WGSS) that will provide a safe space for women and girls to engage in gender, age-appropiate activities: from recreational activities (e.g.: dancing, singing, painting, playing, etc.) for PSS support; to educational activities that improve their life skills (e.g.: basic literacy in Portuguese), to resilience building activities linked to livelihoods (e.g.: sewing, ceramics, etc.). In any case, the women and girls based on their interests as a group will be the ones deciding which will be the specific activities to be conducted. Within the Steering Committees there will be a mandatory 50% of female representation (including women in decision-making roles).

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Some of the community mobilizers (activists) that will be facilitating the activities at the CECs will be people with disabilities, and during the door to door visits, the activists will seek to identify beneficiaries with disabilities that want to participate and that have barriers that impeed their participation. All the activities and sessions conducted at the CECs will have a strong awareness raising on inclusion od people with disabilities and will be adapted to their needs to ensure their active and meaningful participation. Within the Steering Committees there will be a mandatory 20% of people with disabilities representation (including people with disabilities in decision-making roles).

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The CECs are built by local contractors with local materials, therefore having a minimal enviromental impact as all the materials are to be found at the community.
