Project Summary

In response to the growing humanitarian crisis in Lakes State. WAV Gender Based Violence project plan will directly target 10294 vulnerable IDPs and host communities in Wulu county with a total of 6176 females, 1029 males,1029 boys and 2059 girls 5% of the target population will include people with disability,female headed households,pregnant and lactating women and the elderly .The project aims at providing quality GBV services to GBV survivors and reduce suffering of women, girls, men and boys by providing timely and integrated multi-sector assistance and services to reduce acute needs and promote and sustain their resilience to recurrent shocks.
The prioritized objectives to achieve the overall goal will include:
CO 1: Ensure the provision of critical protection-related assistance and specialized services through an
integrated approach to address the priority needs of targeted people among vulnerable women, men, girls and boys in hard-to-reach and priority geographical areas
CO 2: Prevent and mitigate protection risks through enhanced preparedness and resilience.
The main activities to be carried out to realize this objectives and achieve the overall goal will include :
1)Training of the case workers and community protection volunteers on comprehensive case management and psycho social support (PSS) to strengthen safe referrals, including information dissemination on available services.
2) Establishment of Women & girls friendly spaces with PSS ,case management and skill building interventions which will also be supported through women safety committees, women groups and Community-based protection groups to strengthen support in protection, coordination and community awareness raising and sensitization.
3) Conduct community outreach targeting 10294 community members to raise awareness.
4) Establishment and strengthening the referral pathway mechanisms for Wulu county.

1 - Needs

Lakes State has experienced a great deal of conflict in recent years that has caused degenerated and rampant instability issues. Due to the constant upheaval, the capacity of the local social and economic infrastructure is one of the lowest in the country. Furthermore Wulu county has been subjected to numerous displacements with IDPs from BharGhel being integrated into the host community .The displacements are caused by conflicts of inter and intra-communal violence ,revenge killing,farmers pastoralists conflicts, land disputes, clan fighting and many more making women, children, elderly and PSNs victims of circumstances. The June 2019 joint inter-agency needs assessment exercise updates continue to highlight the escalation of insecurity, sexual violence and other forms of GBV against women and adolescent girls becoming wide spread due to continued conflict and displacements. Key concerns include displacements of populations in Wulu county in Dolomoloto,BharGhel and Wulu payams. This recent inter clan conflict among the Bele community of Bar e gel living between Western lakes and Gok state and also farmers and pastoralists conflict has displaced over 1200 people. Sexual violence and other forms of GBV against women and girls are becoming more wide spread in Wulu due to movement of the populations because of conflicts. Through GBV programming and peace building and governance projects WAV has noticed increasing and targeted attack with existing and growing levels of sexual violence perpetrated. In Wulu adolescent girls are particularly vulnerable to multiple forms of violence and have limited support systems and access to information and experience growing levels of sexual violence, abductions ,sexual harassment ,early and forced marriages, sexual exploitation, abuse and restrictions placed on their movements by their families. According to lakes September updates ,conflict between farmers and pastoralists has led to depletion of assets significantly for the county falling into the IPC 4 category. Through this plan atleast 30% targeted beneficiaries will be supported through PSS and lifeskills . Responding to the new crisis requires a targeted emergency response outside the current WAV program sites .WAV will utilize existing internal capacity to respond quickly and effectively to the emerging crisis and will provide emergency GBV services in Wulu county.

2 - Activities

• Support in strengthening and establishment of Women & girls friendly spaces programming, including strengthened integration with child protection to support child survivors.• Training of the community protection volunteers on Comprehensive case management and Psychosocial support (PSS).• Conduct trainings targeting female and male youth as agents of change for gender equality.• Conduct trainings targeting adolescent girls and young women (as priority): empowerment, self-esteem, rights awareness and collective power.• -Conduct trainings targeting male youth (transforming discriminatory attitudes and behaviors that contribute to inequality; negative masculinities, accountable practices, women's rights).• Conducting gender analysis of needs and aspirations of female and male youth in local community. • Conduct gender analysis of community relations and norms affecting peaceful coexistence.• Establishment and strengthening of Safe referrals, including information dissemination on available services through Wulu county referral pathway.• Conduct Community awareness raising and sensitization on women’s rights.• Provision of dignity kits to 400 girls • Capacity building local women's groups and women leaders on mediation and peaceful resolution of conflicts; Involving women in decision-making in Community Dispute Resolution, conflict mitigation, mediation & peace building.• Conduct and support FSL,Nutrition and WASH on safety audits as part of GBV risk mitigation measure• Conduct needs/preference assessment among women and girls on the contents of the dignity kits as well as doing post dignity kit distribution assessment to assess utilization of kits and to obtain feedback on areas of improvement.• Conduct joint development of GBV standard operating procedures (GBV SoPs) in partnership with FSL,WASH and Nutrition to guide provision of coherence of quality GBV services.

6.6 - Will beneficiaries' rights be legally recognised?

To Prevent and minimize as much as possible any unintended negative effects of the project intervention which could increase people's vulnerability to both physical and psychosocial risks.Collaboration within other protection and GBV key actors will be strengthened to ensure that the CMR consultations rooms are friendly to both the survivors and the community in mitigating the current stigma associated with this service points at the same time building on the capacity of the health service providers on the CMR guidelines right to confidentiality and dignity: survivors will receive treatment consistent with the dignity and respect they are owed as human beings. In the context of health services, this means, as a minimum, providing equitable access to quality medical care, ensuring patients' privacy and the confidentiality of their medical information, informing patients and obtaining their consent before any medical intervention, and providing a safe clinical environment. Furthermore, health services will be provided in a language she or he understands and the same strengthened during trainings with the case workers, women groups,volunteers and frontline service providers.

6.5 - Includes perspectives of displacement-affected people?

YESThe project life cycle will commence with the project inception where participatory assessment will be conducted in Wulu County to assess the current GBV concerns being faced by the vulnerable marginalized groups patterns and the risk factors for violence and abuse. WAV will strengthen the existing community-based protection structures and women groups in Wulu county to support programming and activity implementation through trainings to enhance long term sustainability. The intervention is designed to provide lifesaving GBV support to vulnerable IDPs and host communities. In order to strengthen clinical management services .WAV will work closely with health care workers Wulu PHCC to provide CCSAS to expand the health care service contribution to GBV survivors. To strengthen the existing GBV referral pathway mechanisms survivor centered approaches, GBV basic concepts and the key principles in dealing with the GBV survivor will be engraved within the case management interventions. Regular consultations will be provided within the women and girls friendly spaces (WGFS) with women and girls meeting quarterly to discuss key interventions implemented within the period, achievements, challenges, recommendations ,gaps and opportunities within the given intervention to ensure responses are informed by their voices, recommendations and experiences. WAV has existing tools to capture beneficiary feedback on key programming interventions to ensure quality service provision is adapted for this project. WAV will continue to work closely with other partners to harmonize and strengthen responses to ensure integrated programming. The key stakeholders will inform programming and contribute significantly to the responses and adhere to “DO NO HARM” .

6.2 - Is the project linked to government/community systems?

YES-The project will incorporate community involvement and participation as a key ingredient in the delivery of good planning outcomes in Wulu county.There will be county stakeholders consultative meeting that will bring together community opinion leaders ,RRC and the ministry of gender and social services officials this will assist in the identification of local needs,to inform policy-making and provide feedback on service delivery while at the same time fostering a sense of local ownership towards the project .The project will ensure that this issues are mitigated through community participation. The project will engage all the community members as the primary key stakeholders at the design stage, a baseline survey will be conducted and the community members will be involved, focused group discussions (FGD), key informant interviews, HHs questionnaires and Transect walks methods will be used to carry out the baseline to ascertain the priority needs of the community. During implementation phase the community members will participate in the activities within the work plan to achieve the desired results and monitoring progress. WAV will map out other humanitarian actors and other line ministries and collaborate with them throughout the project cycle to promote sustainability.

6.1 - Is the protection environment sufficiently safe/stable?

YES-The project will enhance the capacity of WAV staff and community volunteers on the integration of 4 protection principles (meaningful access, safety and security, accountability to affected population and participation and empowerment) in their work. WAV has developed guidance notes tailored to South Sudan context. During the orientation sessions with community volunteers and front-line WAV staffs will be oriented on the 4 protection principles to enhance safety, dignity and meaningful access to the services provided. The recommendations developed will be supported to ensure quality service is provided for all based on age, gender and diversity. Safety and dignity will be promoted in the following areas: dis-aggregation of gender in distribution centres, installing locks in latrines, ensuring confidential spaces for reports, and building ramps to ensure people with disabilities can have meaningful access to the services in question. Furthermore WAV will carry out awareness raising to the community, local authorities and ministry of gender and social development at county level on protection principles and protection mainstreaming to raise awareness and influence changes in practices, policies, and procedures and to assist in solving the protection concerns through service delivery.”DO NO HARM” principle will be applied within all proposed interventions. WAV will work closely with other actors especially UN OCHA to ensure meaningful access is negotiated and provided for smooth implementation of activities and underpin neutrality of support. Additional efforts are in place to work with marginalized segments of the population (adolescent girls, female headed households, widows, pregnant and lactating women, people with disability and elderly women, child headed households) to strengthen the key principle of empowerment and participation.

YESDuring the inception of this project county consultative meetings will be conducted in Wulu county with the purpose of enlightening the stakeholders on the the project goals ,objectives ,activities and gain a buy-in of the process.The project will also work closely with the local community structures to promote a sense of ownership this is engraved in WAV community based approaches .The community structures will be linked to the ministry of gender and social Services to support in the interventions and promote smooth transition after the exit.WAV will also work closely with other humanitarian actors with projects in GBV and protection to ensure smooth transition this linkages will be promoted during project inception,Implementation and monitoring and evaluation stages of the project cycle .The community structures will also be capacity build through training ,mentor-ship and exchange visits to ensure that knowledge and skill is gained and transferred to the rest of the community to support the sustainability of the project.
