Project Summary

SEMA will implement the following activities:
o Provide specialized GBV services to survivors (Case management)
o Provide activities empowering women and girls and GBV prevention activities include Awareness-raising activities, life skills activities, and vocational training.
o Provide enhanced specialized adolescent girls programming with the aim of addressing child marriage and sexual violence.
o provide income-generating activities in the WGSSs through small grants for women to be able to start new small projects/businesses with the goal of achieving economic independence thus contributing to GBV prevention and mitigation.
o Engage and train humanitarian actors (non-specialist) to reduce risks of GBV and refer to specialized services. SEMA will train humanitarian actors respectively from the Health sector, and Nutrition sector on GBV guidelines to reduce risks of GBV and refer to specialized services. This will be conducted based on Guidelines for Integrating Gender-Based Violence Interventions in Humanitarian Action”
o Conducting safety audits\ community observations to inform the risk of GBV in other sectoral interventions in Idlib and Aleppo.
All activities will be implemented in close collaboration with the GBV sub-cluster and using the materials developed by them and will include GBV SOPs - GBV case management guidelines - the GBV awareness-raising toolkit - My Safety My wellbeing program.
SEMA is an active member in the GBV Sub-cluster since 2016, and is also the GBV referral focal point for A'zaz district, and one of the members of the GBV Capacity Building Initiative.
SEMA was also the leader in developing the GBV M&E toolkit in cooperation with the GBV Sub-cluster, where SEMA is one of the Cluster members who had access to the GBVIMS.
SEMA always follows the updates of the GBV sub-cluster and has high coordination with them and uses all the updated materials and resources.
To ensure response to the accessibility and inclusion of vulnerable persons with disabilities, the WGSS has been rehabilitated to be friendly for women and girls with disability. Moreover, transportation service from/to WGSS center for the disabled will be provided. The team will be trained to conduct group / individual support sessions for the PWD, and they will highly be encouraged to join all activities available in the facility.
SEMA as a part of PSEA network was able to develop a robust PSEA Policy with sufficient compliant mechanisms.

4 - Needs

New GBV trends are identified this year, especially in areas of north-west Syria, are sex-selected abortion to ensure having sons, forced pregnancy to bridge a perceived demographic gap and forced puberty of girls, the process of giving girls hormones so that they reach puberty earlier and get married earlier.And COVID-19 and the economic crisis in Syria has further exacerbated the levels and types of GBV throughout northwest Syria, particularly incidences of domestic violence such as physical and emotional violence, marital rape, and denial of resources. Pregnant women and women and girls with disabilities have been especially vulnerable. As poverty has increasingly impeded the ability of households to meet their basic needs, financially motivated negative coping mechanisms have been increasingly adopted, including forced prostitution, and early and forced marriages. All of this is also contributing to further impediments to women’s access to essential reproductive services.According to the needs assessment conducted by SEMA in November 2020 in four locations (Azaz, Al-Bab, Afrin, and Ariha). Where the assessment tool as Focus group discussions was conducted with twelve groups of Women, Girls, and men to identify the needs and gaps of the protection related issues and GBV within the COVID-19 outbreak from the different age and sex groups in the area. All participants confirmed that GBV continues and that some types of GBV have increased over the past year due to the COVID 19 pandemic and Quarantine, and because the man stays at the home long time that caused more physical violence, and psychological violence against women.

5 - Implementation of activities

In 2021, SEMA will ensure that GBV survivors can access specialized services and the risks of GBV will be prevented and mitigated. Increasing the provision of quality specialized GBV services – with a focus on psychosocial support, case management, strengthening the referral pathways, sustaining women and girls’ safe space, as well as providing emergency case fund for GBV survivors based on the need.After the spread of the COVID-19 pandemic in northwest Syria, SEMA will focus on enhancing the delivery of GBV services through organizing remote services ensuring all infection prevention modalities are in place through physical activities.The project aims to increase the access to awareness services and to enhance its ability to respond via mobile services to reach the most vulnerable and/or survivors of GBV and maximize the outreach during acute emergencies and displacements. Furthermore, outreach visits and awareness sessions will be conducted for men and boys to reduce the GBV in the communities and enhance women accessibility. The quality of services provided and the impact of monitoring will be improved through capacity building of providers, emphasizing Do-No-Harm approach, and ensuring safe referral systems are in place, mapping of available services, and implementing Standard Operating Procedures by the program staff. The project will strive to respond to the need of adolescent girls through providing specific programs and activities that target them. Strategies to prevent gender-based violence will be improved especially by working with communities. The project will continue providing training on GBV Risk Mitigation and to advocate for improved prioritization of GBV and strategies that reduce GBV risks.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

SEMA will follow the risk analysis and mitigation measures that conducted and developed by the cluster and sub-cluster and we will highlight the top issue we may face like:1-lack of quality services and referral expose survivors to harm, enhancing the risk of stigmatization reprisal and further trauma and GBV service providers safety and security because of their role for dealing with violence and we will try to make sure case management with survivors to be conducted by trained personnel only, Service mapping including only those that can provide survivor-cantered services, and for GBV service providers SEMA will focus on Self-care strategies as part of programmes and organization HR policy.2- Resistance or backlash from sections of the community and community leaders on GBV messaging. SEMA will Engagement Community throughout all project cycle. It includes meetings with religious and community leaders and Adaptation of GBV messaging to ensure sensitivity to the context and culture.

7.1 - Please list any risks related to sexual exploitation and abuse that have been identified during the project design phase and any risk mitigation and prevention measures that have been adopted and built into the project

According to MSNA 2021,80% of interviewed KI in Afrin, as well as 70% in Azaz,100% in Albab stated that they do not Know reporting mechanisms in the community to report SEA cases.SEMA is committed to protecting beneficiaries from sexual exploitation and abuse, in compliance with PSEA standard policy and procedures. New staff recruited will have to sign the new version of CoC released by the PSEA network recently and will be fully oriented about PSEA and the available complaint mechanism. Awareness raising of the community about SEA will be a part of activities with full illustration of available compliant mechanism. PSEA focal points are responsible for mainstreaming PSEA into all departments and projects, through developing the measures and practices including feedback and compliant mechanism along with concerned departments. Posters and Banners raising awareness about PSAE will be used in all facilities. Videos released by the network will be constantly shown on partners private social media (Facebook, Instagram,).New modalities of PSEA raising awareness - due to COVID-19 crisis are followed, such as sending text messages and videos to the beneficiaries, online training, using Banners in the distribution locations will be also taken into consideration. The project ensures the community and beneficiaries' engagement in project designing, and planning since the beginning through the need’s assessments and so on during the next phases, and through targeting adolescent girls
