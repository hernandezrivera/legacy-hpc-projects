Project Summary

HRO aims to continue providing GBV services for IDPs & HCs in our already-existing WGSSs in Idleb (Janudiyeh & Badama) & our CCs in Idleb (Jisr Ash Shugur & Darkosh), besides planning to establish new CCs in Idleb (Harim, Salqin, Dana & Maaret Tamsrin), in Aleppo (Afrin & Akhtrin), and new WGSS in Suran & through outreach visits to the homes and camps including widows’ camps. The services include GBV prevention activities such as awareness-raising sessions, empowerment activities, training & campaigns, besides emergency response activities to the new IDPs through providing PFA, referral, sharing information, etc. based on GBV toolkit including early marriage and sexual abuse, reproductive health & information session to all age groups & genders, especially the most vulnerable groups. In addition, some of them will receive case fund, Micro Grant, vocational training (in the WGSSs or widows’ camps), and there will be a capacity-building activity for non-GBV actors on GBV basics and risk mitigation, to reduce the GBV against women. Women committees will be established in these camps to build their capacities, and to ensure that a greater number of people have access to the information related to GBV services in their communities.
HRO will target WLOs with capacity-building training on two levels technically and administratively to ensure their continuity. As a response to the GBV cases, we will provide CVA through case management, IPA & case fund within our projects based on the vulnerability criteria, in addition to signing MoUs with other organizations to refer survivors/at-risk cases to them and ensure that GBV risks are mitigated. The planned number of centers is (12), 8 CCs and 4 WGSS where women and girls can get involved in the activities & interact with their peers & gain access to lifesaving GBV services, including case management, referrals, PSS for survivors focusing on vulnerable groups such as divorced, widows, PwDs and older women (over 50) and ensuring their accessibility and inclusion through providing transportation and adapting the WGSSs to fit their needs (installing the needed tools and facilities, and training the staff on including PwD and OP in the WGSS activities). Also, HRO will target male survivors of sexual violence with specialized GBV services, and adolescent girls (13-19) will be targeted with life skills and AMAL initiative programs.
HRO will target 72,000 BNFs (54,000 F, 18,000 M), (600 Young Children, 15,400 Children, 48,800 Adults, 7,200 older people), of them 3,600 PwDs, through 12 centers and by 60 GBV staff (15 outreach teams, 20 case managers, 3 supervisors).
To ensure gender equality and quality of activities, the M&E plan will include a target-based performance, ITT, and regular site visits by the M&E Field assistants to verify & document project implementation progress including routine meetings with key informants at the local level to generate qualitative data on the effectiveness of project. Depending on beneficiary consent forms, visual evidence that includes photos & videos & MoVs of project activities will be documented. Exit interview questionnaires will be administered to measure the satisfaction of beneficiaries with services being delivered.
Also, HRO will ensure smooth coordination with the protection NGO partners, local councils, stakeholders, and camp managers throughout the project period.
