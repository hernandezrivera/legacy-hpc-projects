Project Summary

In order to prevent the recurrence of famine risk, it is critical that WARDI will not only meet people’s immediate humanitarian needs but also scale up its activities aimed at reducing risks and vulnerabilities and building resilience to future shocks and stresses. To this end, WARDI would like to upscale its WASH activities in Hiran, Lower Shabelle and Banadir regions of southern Somalia to strengthen coherence and complementarity between humanitarian and development efforts, and ultimately aim to reduce needs and vulnerabilities to a point where humanitarian action is no longer required. Thus, Under this project, WARDI proposes to improve access to emergency and sustainable safe water, sanitation facilities and services and promote good hygiene practices in an effort to improve the health status of 36,000 crisis and disaster affected communities and internally displaced men, women, boys and girls including people with disabilities and minorities in Hiran, Banadir and Lower Shabelle regions of south Somalia. Lack of access to safe and adequate water and sanitation facilities coupled with poor hygiene knowledge and practices put these communities at risk for waterborne diseases and loss of life particularly women and children. Key activities that WARDI would like to implement in the target locations include:
WATER: Provision of emergency safe water to new arrived 2,000 IDPs or IDPs affected by floods through voucher for three months; provision of sustainable safe water through rehabilitation of 2 boreholes equipped hybrid system, rehabilitation of 20 shallow wells (15 with hand pumps, 5 equipped with solar system); construction of 6 water kiosks supplied water through piping system to schools and health posts; chlorination of the rehabilitated water sources; capacity building training to 28 WASH committees and 44 well operators shared equally men and women on operation and maintenance. As cross cutting, WARDI will conduct DRR and conflict resolution mechanisms workshop to 200 community members.
SANITATION: To improve the sanitation of the IDP camps and institutions, WARDI will construct 4 twin block and gender segregation institutional latrines in 2 school and 2 health facilities and 200 gender separation IDP latrines with lockable doors and hand washing facilities, special consideration will be given girls/ women privacy and people with disabilities' special needs. WARDI will support 6 IDP camps by providing 24 sanitation sets to clean up the garbage scattered in the settlements, in order to improve the environmental sanitation of the camps and for cleaning the latrines. WARDI will also establish 4 simplified Fecal Sludge Management systems to restore/maintain sanitation systems in outbreak or emergency.
HYGIENE: To reach more than 36,000 crisis affected communities and IDPs, WARDI will train 200 CHPs to carry out comprehensive hygiene promotion campaigns to educate the community better hygiene promotion practices including hand washing with soap practices through visiting house-to-house, schools, feeding/nutrition centers and health centers; in this project, WARDI will provide 1,500 HP kits to 1500 households and IDPs who are at risk of AWD/cholera and malnutrition diseases.
The proposed response took into consideration the impact of the crisis on women, people with disabilities, minorities and children in terms of access to WASH services; it proposed specific actions to prevent risk of gender based violence and hence will contribute to greater gender equality. WARDI top management will monitor the quality and quantity of the WASH intervention during the project implementation and its completion.

Project Activities

Project objective: To reduce people’s WASH needs, risks and vulnerabilities and increase in their resilience. The project is designed to strengthen coherence and complementarity between humanitarian and development efforts.1. Water: 1) Provide emergence safe water to 2,000 new arrived IDPs affected by crisis, each HHs composing 6 people will receive 90 l/day as SPHERE standard for 90 days through voucher, benefiting 12,000 men, women and children including people with disabilities and minorities. WARDI will make sure that the water quality and quantity is available in the market and the price remain steady throughout project duration. 2) Rehabilitate: (1) 2 boreholes equipped with hybrid system complete with its infrastructures, benefiting 12,000 men, women, boys, girls, including people with disabilities and minorities. (2) 20 flood proof shallow wells (15 with hand pumps and 5 with so1alr power), benefiting 10,000 men, women, boys, girls including people with disabilities and minorities. (3) Construct 6 water kiosk supplied water through piping system to 6 IDP camps, benefiting 3,000 men, women, boys, girls, people with disabilities, and minorities.3) conduct training on: (1) 28 WASH committees from 28 water sources; each committee consists 7 members (4men, 3women). The committee will be trained on better water source management, HHWT use, and maintenance of water facilities. As a cross cutting, the WASH committees will also give one day training on DRR and conflict resolution mechanisms. (2) 56 well operators on O&M and collection of water revenues for the sustainability of the water source.4) Chlorinate all the rehabilitated/constructed water sources.2. Sanitation: 1) Construct 4 gender sensitive twin block latrines with lockable doors and hand washing basins to 2 schools and 2 health facilities; benefiting around 3,000 men, women, boys, girls, including people with disabilities and minorities. The action should support the easy use of children and disabilities and will be equipped with menstrual hygiene management facilities.2) Construct 200 flood proof and gender sensitive IDP latrines with lockable doors and hand washing facilities at new arrival IDP camps. Women, children and disabilities will be consulted in the design and the location of the facilities to increase their dignity and avoid the risk of GBV against women and girls.3) Support 6 IDP camps by providing 24 sanitation sets to clean up the garbage scattered in the settlements, in order to improve the environmental sanitation of the camps and for cleaning the latrines. Each set composes (Wheelbarrow (1), Pickaxe (1), Shovel/spade (2), Brooms (4), Rakes (2), Rope and bucket (4). Each IDP camp will receive 4 sanitation sets.4) Establish 4 simplified Fecal Sludge Management systems to restore/maintain sanitation systems in outbreak or emergency. 3. Hygiene:1) Train 200 CHPs (100 men and 100 women) on best hygiene and sanitation practices, hand washing with soap, water storage, environmental sanitation using WC training materials.2) Conduct comprehensive HP and mobilization on AWD/Cholera prevention measures to 25,000 men, women and children through house to house, schools, health and nutrition centers' visits. KAP survey will be done at the inception and the completion of the project to evaluate the effectiveness of the HP intervention.3) Distribute 2000 HP kits to 2000 HHs/IDPs affected/risk AWD or malnutrition diseases. Each HP kit contains (200 aqua tabs, 5 soaps of 800mg, 1 Jerry can of 20-liter capacity, 1 bucket of 20-liter capacity). UNICEF will supply Hygiene promotion kits, while the role of WARDI is to distribute the kits. The project activities will lead to increased security, restore dignity of men, women, boys, girls, people with disabilities, ensure that every group/age have equal access to the WASH facilities and reduce water shortage risks for future shocks of drought, AWD/Cholera outbreaks and strength resilience.

Please provide further explanation

WARDI will hold at the inception of the project, community mobilization, and sensitization meetings with all stakeholders including the local authorities to officially launch the project. During the sensitization meetings, the project objectives, implementation strategies, scope, beneficiary selection criteria, beneficiary entitlement and roles and duties of each stakeholder including M&E roles will be discussed and agreed. WARDI will develop elaborate beneficiary complain and feedback tool, project beneficiaries and stakeholders will be given 1 day workshop on how to use the complaint /feedback system to enhance transparency and accountability. Community ownership of the project and keeping the sustainability of the WASH facilities after the face out of the project will be done through formation of structures such as Beneficiary Representatives, WASH Committees, and Community Hygiene Promoters with equal share of men and women. In order to meet the WASH needs of the affected community, WARDI will involve the target community through its leaders and engendered committees in the first stage of planning of the project to ensure community participation. The organization will establish engendered committees (women participation will be strictly adhered) that will have hand-in-hand working relations with the project team and beneficiary communities; both sides will exchange any information related to the management and implementation of the project activities. Community Feedback and Response Mechanism (CFRM) will also be used to make sure that the beneficiaries are satisfied with services provided and complaints can be channeled to the right direction. WARDI will adhere the principles of "do no Harm" through not creating any partial, nepotism, conflict oriented and sensitive issues within the project beneficiaries. WARDI will adhere to these principles of “do no harm” at all times throughout the project cycle by involving all categories of the community. The project activities has a strategy of emergency and resilience to enable durable solution for the target beneficiaries. WARDI will provide emergency safe water to the vulnerable disaster affected community and IDPs through voucher, to reduce their water need during the rehabilitation/construction of WASH facilities. As an exit strategy, WARDI will also provide the beneficiaries sustainable WASH services to enable durable solutions to the affected communities. To keep the sustainability of the WASH services, WARDI will train WASH committees, well operators and Community Hygiene Promoters, in order to maintain the WASH services after the project is faced out and to keep the ownership of the community to the project. After the project activities is completed, WARDI will hand over the rehabilitated/constructed WASH facilities to the WASH committees with the presence of the local authorities.
