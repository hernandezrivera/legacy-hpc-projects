Project Summary

The project is designed to respond to the WASH needs of 189101 people (Men: 37820, Women: 39601, Girls: 56842, Boys: 54838),( 75640 IDPs, 111459 hosted people, and returnees 2002) with 2316 people of them are people with disabilities during 2023 protracted needs by ensuring good access to sustainable WASH service, The project is designed to respond to the needs of IDPs, Returnees, and hosted people, stayed in the north of Syria in the communities and the IDPs camps .to cover their needs by delivering humanitarian WASH supplies and services to most vulnerable people: men, women, boys, and girls will benefit from the activities which aim to increase access to sustainable water, sanitation, and environmental hygiene solutions, the project will be transferred the water station from working by fuel to working by a hygiene system most of it solar energy.
One of the causes of the spreading of Leishmania is bad solid waste management in place and this is another factor that can increase the threat to vulnerable people SECD is aiming to solve this long-known problem by undertaking supporting the local council to activate good solid waste management which is reducing and eliminating the Leishmania causes.
Through this project in 2023, SECD is planning to support the targeted communities with the
following activities:
-Installation of the solar power system to turn the operating system of the water stations:
36 water stations. and conducting a water safety plan, and establishing a cost recovery system to ensure the suitability of the project. The solar system will be used for both good operation and/or for pumping stations
-Distribution of 10000 consumable hygiene kits (second round) for the most vulnerable people the component of the HKs.
-Conducting 71 hygiene promotion campaigns and Risk Reduction and Community Engagement (RCCE) provision, by raising the awareness of the local community on the cholera outbreak-related health and hygiene messages, including the prevention and treatment techniques on topics like safe water usage, food preparation, open defecation, dehydration, … etc.SECD will target the entire community with at least COVID-19 awareness. The hygiene promotion campaign will focus on Coronavirus-2019, also during these campaigns SECD will distribute PSEA for beneficiaries to increase their knowledge and access to the PSEA reporting hotline.
Supporting sanitation works which include but is not limited to :
-Provision and installation of 8150 garbage bins 0.1m3 size for the small camps.
-Rehabilitation and or /providing of the solid waste truck.
-Support the SWM unit with salaries and fuel for 6 months, uniforms for the workers, masks, and gloves to protect the workers from coronavirus-2019 in this service by Conducting solid waste campaigns to encourage people to participate in this humanitarian effort.
-Rehabilitation of the sewer system which includes but is not limited to Rehabilitation and
extending of Sewer system network ( (HDPE 30cm diameter ):4800m,- (HDPE100 cm diameter ):6600m.
SECD will be in a close position with the local council, the beneficiaries, the IDPs, and the host community and will involve them in all the project stages from the planning till the closure of the project to adhere to the community engagement approach by inviting them to regular FGDs and open all the channels to receive their feedback, complaints, and opinions. the proposed project ensures that the sanitation service would be provided to schools, hospitals, children-friendly centers, Women empowerment centers, and public bakeries.
SECD will conduct a deep KAP assessment prior to the hygiene promotion campaigns to ensure that the chosen subject is not covered by other NGOs / radio / TV (COVID-19 prevention measures)

3 - Needs

As reported by UNICEF, On 10 September 2022, a cholera outbreak was declared in Syria. on the 19th of September 2022, the Early Warning, Alert, and Response Network (EWARN) laboratory in Jarablus confirmed the first cholera-positive case by culture in northwest Syria (NWS). By 30 September, surveillance data showed a total of 10,039 suspected cases reported in 13 of the 19 governorates of Syria. Approximately 22 suspected cases have been reported from overcrowded IDP camps and sites.While the detected outbreak is currently concentrated in the four northern governorates – close to 98 percent of cases are reported from Deir-Ez-Zor, Aleppo, Ar-Raqqa, and Al-Hasakeh – cholera is also rapidly spreading in other governorates, especially in light of the large and high-risk internally displaced population and Syria’s severe water crisis. The proposed interventions depended on the findings of primary data collection by SECD staff during October 2022 who collected the data by using two methodologies: KII and rapid need assessment with IDPs and hosted people and deep technical assessment, The results showed the importance of supporting the targeted communities who are suffering from the lack of the resources to ensure access to the basic life-saving WASH needs, Basic needs versus provided assistance and gaps in the assessed location. will enable its access to clean water while the others in need to connect it to the water network.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

Activity 1: Support for water supply systems.Protection Risk/Threat: Unequal distribution of services inadvertently empowers or strengthens the position of armed groups or other actors.Likelihood: Medium Impact: High.Mitigating Measure: Establish strong links with authorities and operators to promote equity and “do no harm principle” all the time; Organize joint planning culture, and agree on the minimum standard. Activity 2: Supporting solid Waste Management (SWM)Protection Risk/Threat: Movements of garbage trucks in congested.Reliability of services.Likelihood: High.Impact: low. Mitigating Measure: Consult community (separately women and girls) regarding SWM services modality/ frequency, truck routes, and schedule; Establish clear routes and schedule for garbage trucks that use wider access routes, avoiding school areas during the time children travel to/from school. Activity 3:Conduct and rehabilitate sewage systemThe risk: Health risks associated with construction works and exposure to raw sewage and gases.Likelihood: Medium Impact: High Mitigating Measure: SECD will Ensure staff and contractors involved in construction works or working with raw sewage wear appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) and follow established safety regulations; Conduct/repeat safety training for staff and contractors.
