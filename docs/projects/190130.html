Project Summary

Through this modification, World Vision intends to provide life-saving multi-sectoral response to alleviate the impact of recurrent drought on the affected communities in five woredas; Melka Soda, Dugda Dawa, and Suro Berguda in West Guiji Zone and Goro and Seweyna woredas in Bale Zone, as well as ask for an extension of the phase I activities until December 31st, 2022. Access to safe water supply through water system rehabilitation and household water treatment, construction/rehabilitation of household and institution of latrines and handwashing sites, training community leaders on positive hygiene and sanitation practices, and providing urgently needed essential WASH items will help drought affected people in West Guji and Bale.

1. - Projects activities

The proposed project will respond in addressing the most pressing issues related with food security and WASH. Multipurpose cash programming .The project aimed to reduce the negative impacts of drought and deterioration of livelihood assets that has been affected due to recurrent drought in Borena and Liben Zones. In Response to the drought, WV will provide water trucking to 4 HCF,s & 12000 drought affected most vulnerable communities in the four woreda ( Dolo Ado, Bokolmayo, Elwaye, & Teltele) for 60 days to meet the basic minimum demand of 7 litre/person/day for the community. In the 4 HCFs 10m3 water tanker will be installed over a masonry constructed seat in order to enhance the storage capacity of water in HCFs. 16 water schemes ( 13 shallow wells & 3 motorized system) will be rehabilitated. The water trucking and the durable solution will target 27,400 individuals & more than 500,000 cattle’s, goats, seeps, camels. Moreover, it has been planned to procure 8 drums of HTH & 4 pool tester. 16 WASHCO Committee (112 individuals) will establish or strengthen. All the WASCHOs’ will be provided with operation & maintenance kits. 12 latrines( 4 in HCF & 8 in schools) will be also constructed. 23,1000 individuals will be reached through hygiene promotion
