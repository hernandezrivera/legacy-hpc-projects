Project Summary

In response to the Beirut explosion that happened on the 4th of August accompanied by a drastic increase in the number of COVID19 positive cased, HelpAge International is proposing a project to promote better hygiene practices in areas affected by the explosion targeting the most-at-risk older people and persons with disabilities identified by an outreach team of community volunteers through 1) the provision of hygiene kits for 500 households living in the target areas with an older family member and/or person with specific needs, 2) the provision of 250 elderly kits to older people identified to be in need for the kits’ items based on specific assessment criteria, 3) provision of 500 PPE kits to the affected households to reduce the risk of COVID19 transmission, and 4) raising awareness through community volunteers on COVID19 risk mitigation practices emphasizing on good hygiene practices. The project will be linked up with other projects implemented by HelpAge under the Health and Protection sectors to complement the intervention and ensure a comprehensive response to the emerged needs.

1 - Needs

HelpAge international, Amel Association international, Dorcas, and Makassed foundation implemented a joint assessment targeting 802 households in the areas affected by the Beirut explosion on 10-11 August. Findings showed that 66% of the respondents were older people (50 years and above) and 33% of respondents were between 18-50, of which 68% were older person headed households and 32% were adult headed households. This reflects the high number of older people living in the affected areas and the fact that many of them do not wish or are not able to leave their house. The main needs identified as a priority by the affected population were cash assistance (26%), food kits (13.71%), hygiene items (11.93%), and medications (12.65%). The explosion interrupted basic water and sanitation services in many neighborhoods, especially Katarina which experienced intense damages (80% of housing units). Many families require supplies until basic services can be restored. The Water Sector prioritized the WASH response to support vital water facilities, hospitals, PHCs, and affected households, especially in the COVID19 context. At the household level, respondents reported damage to water tanks (18%), especially for Syrian refugees (28%) who experienced higher levels of damage to their water supply (29%). There were also reported cases of the exploitation of older people by others posing as volunteers which raise an alert to the increased risk of abuse.

2 - Activities

Through a community-based intervention, HelpAge International (HAI) will reach out to older people in the affected communities and support them in accessing the information and services they need. An outreach team of community volunteers will identify 500 of the most-at-risk households living in the target areas having at least one older person and/or person with disabilities to provide them with hygiene kits that include detergent, bleach, hand sanitizers, alcohol spray, soap, floor mop, and surfaces mop, to promote COVID19 preventive hygiene practices, in addition to PPE kits to reduce the risk of COVID19 transmission and ensure their access to appropriate protective equipment. Based on specific assessment criteria, older people that will be identified to have further needs for elderly items due to their old-age and/or health conditions will receive elderly kits packages as well. The community volunteers will also raise awareness of the households on COVID19 risk mitigation practices emphasizing good hygiene practices and the correct uses of the distributed kits and refer them to other relevant services as needed.All project staff and volunteers will be trained on PSEA, safeguarding and code of conduct, which will be signed by all staff and volunteers. A complaint and response mechanism will be set up to allow beneficiaries and other people living in target locations to raise any feedback or concerns on the project or related to safeguarding issues. The project will also link up with HelpAge’s projects under the Protection and the Health Sectors:- under the Protection Sector, HelpAge’s community volunteers will reach out to older people in the affected communities and support them in accessing information and services they need.- under the Health Sector, partners AMEL and Makassed’s medical roving team, composed of one doctor and two nurses (one male one female), will visit older people who are not able to reach PHCs by themselves and provide nursing care and medical consultation, as well as refer them to other relevant medical services.
