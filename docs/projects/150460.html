Project Summary

The project is designed to provide urgent support to protect the livelihoods of the small scale farmers' owners of open farms, small-scale poultry breeders and provide rehabilitation work and renewable power supply system for water wells and agricultural ponds based on BBB approach to enhance resilience of agricultural sector. The project will target 250 of dunums of open farms for 125 small-scale farmers (80% males and 20%females) in order restore production and functionality by providing either full and/or partial land rehabilitation, piping and/or irrigation system, seeds and seedlings distribution based on the individual needs assessment of each farm. 3 Agricultural wells will be rehabilitated for the sake of rural communities; 90 farmers " MoA indicates that available wells needs rehabilitation to the north provide water for 30 farmers as average. 3wells * 30 farmers = 90 beneficiary in average". The wells will be supported with renewable power supply system (20 Kw each) to increase its functionality and durability to pump water during day time which will improve farmers access to water resources for irrigation purposes. The project will rehabilitate 50 affected old agricultural ponds (concrete plastering and plastic). The project will also provide rehabilitation works for 50 of small scale poultry farms( 95% males and 5% females) and provide them with renewable power supply system (1.2 kw each) in order to make available the lighting, watering and ventilation required for the chicks. Protection standards and core humanitarian standards will be fully respected.

Needs

Small-scale farmers are of the most affected social groups of food insecurity UNWOMAN 2018: As per the UNWOMEN, farmers and their households were reported as one of the most affected groups in the Gaza strip especially those whose access to land and agricultural inputs have been affected by the barrier, mobility restrictions and the blockade. As per the Ministry of agriculture15,000 dunum of open eld are still in need for land rehabilitation interventions after the war 2014. Land rehabilitation in all governorates of the Gaza strip was set high priority intervention for the Food security sector and the ministry of agriculture "HRP 2018". As per the needs assessment ofPARC for the Organizational plan 2018, land rehabilitation is urgent and is high priority in the north (1500 dunums) and Gaza governorate (2000 dunums). *Power crisis as well has affected farmers ability to sustain their small scale business: The long lasting energy crisis has put an additional burden on farmers who already suffer from an increase in agricultural input costs accompanied by low purchasing power by households. Farmers continue to burden further reduced profitability due to low vegetables and meat production as per UNWOMAN 2018: *agricultural ponds: The bad quality of water in some areas, and power crisis to pump up water for irrigation purposes along with the drought challenges have decreased farmers' resilience and increased farmers' vulnerability to food insecurity and poverty. This has also obliged farmers to resort to negative coping mechanism like selling the land and/or abandoning farming as profession, (PARC and Diakonie Katastrophenhilfe 2016). PARC has identified 2700 Agricultural ponds in the Gaza strip with different functionality conditions (PARC and DKH 2018). Based on past experience, installing of renewable power supply system to water ponds, give the farmers ability to control the irrigation process, when and what to irrigate, as well as to provide assistance to surrounding farmers' community to maximize the bene t and enhance collaboration and social bonds. *Agricultural wells: MoA has set the rehabilitation of agricultural wells as a 2nd high priority for the HRP 2019. Based on the priority intervention matrix of MoA, rehabilitation of agricultural wells in still high in the north and Gaza governorate where 35 and 48 agricultural wells are still in need for rehabilitation. As well, agricultural wells have been set as high priority intervention for PARC organizational plan 2018-2019. *Poultry farmers: 700 broiler poultry farms are at risk due to the electricity shortage, as per last updates of MoA, Oct. 2018. As well, due to the deterioration of the economic situation in the past two-three years, most of animal farms were sold. This is also due to the fact that farmers are unable to sustain the costs of the business and have accumulated debts that they cannot repay, and agro-suppliers are not providing assets/agro-input on credit anymore. Urgent support and protection is required to poultry breeders to protect their livelihood. *Results of the 3 FGDs and 2 workshops organized by PARC for youth with age average (15-20)and adult females ( >40) and farmer males, were as follow: - Due to the fact that land ownership is mainly for men in GS, male farmers focused on the land rehabilitation in the north and GS governorate. - Male farmers focused on the need to get connected to sustainable water resources and provision of agricultural inputs. - Female farmers expressed the need for cash for works programs. They expressed their willingness to work in the field in the harvesting and packaging activities in the olive and other crops harvesting times. - Female farmers focused on small income generating projects like poultry and sheep farms since most of them don't own a land, - In general, female (young and adults) farmers expressed preference to have home gardens with small animal units which will assist them 2 save money & access quality food

Activities

The project is designed to provide urgent rehabilitation support for the farmers and poultry breeders through land rehabilitation for 125 small scale farmers, provide rehabilitation for 50 agricultural ponds (concrete plastering and plastic, and provide rehabilitation and renewable power sully system for 50 poultry farmers, and will create 800 workdays for the sake of 40 unemployed people. The project will. The project will installation renewable power supply system for 3 agricultural wells. Gender mainstreaming will be ensured by targeting/ prioritizing women heads of households and by encouraging women owners of lands/poultries to apply to benefit from the project. Female and people with disabilities will be encouraged to participate in the project field management, in the local committees, especially during the project implementation, follow up, monitoring and evaluation. Women and other project targets will be invited to give lessons learnt for the future design of the project Land rehabilitation intervention: • Conduct a baseline assessment. • Selection of end beneficiaries. • Conduct individual needs assessment. • Cleaning, preparing & ploughing the targeted open fields • Rehabilitating/ installing of local irrigation system and drip line in open fields. • Distributing farming manual tools and accessories for open field farmers (female and male). • Distributing local fertilizers for open field farmers (female and male• Provide cash for work activity for the land cleaning, seedlings planting, fencing etc… Rehabilitation of 50 old damaged agricultural ponds: • Conduct a baseline assessment. • Selection of end beneficiaries. • Conduct needs assessment and individual rehabilitation plan/IRP for 50 affected ponds. • Prepare the technical design and BoQ for every agricultural ponds based on the IRP. • Rehabilitation and installation of renewable power supply system for 3 agricultural wells: • Conduct a baseline assessment. • Selection of specific affected areas. In coordination with MoA • Conduct needs assessment for the damaged agricultural wells and prepare thefinal list of required material/inputs. This requires coordination with the local community, and the ministry of agriculture. • Procure vendors to provide necessary material and service. • Rehabilitate the targeted agricultural wells. Rehabilitation and installation of renewable power supply system for 50 poultry farms: • Conduct a baseline assessment. • Selection of end beneficiaries. • Conduct needs assessment and individual rehabilitation plan/IRP. • Prepare the technical design and BoQ based on the IRP. • Install the renewable power supply system for 50 poultry farms.
