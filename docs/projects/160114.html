Project Summary

this proposed project is intend for improving drought resilience of small holder farmers; agro pastoral and riverline farmers in jalalaqsi district.
this project targets 1,000 HH farmers; 500 in Gu` season and 500 HH in Deyr season.
in each season(Gu` or Deyr) at least 300 HH in river line farmers and 200 HH ago-pastoral farmers beneficiaries participated this project for cultivating of 2Hectars per household, distributing seeds and training for good agricultural practice(GAP) and agribusiness and good agricultural product marketing.
in ago-paspotoral farmers sorghum seeds and sesame seeds will distribute, while river line farmers will distribute vegetable seeds; watermelon, Tomato,red Onion,Carrot and sweet pepper.

Project Activities

the main activities of this project are cultivating 2,000 hectares of rented hourly tractor(6,00 hours) for 1,00 small holder farmers, distributing seeds among selected farmers and training farmers on good agricultural practice(GAP) and agribusiness and good agricultural product marketing. tractor hours without seeds is unproductive but tractor hours with seeds is productive and farmers reach good economic level as project aims.training for will farmers increase the knowledge of farmers on good agricultural practice (GAP) so agricultural production as project aims.

Project Indicators

1. increasing agricultural production and economic level of small holder farmers.2.protecting environment and decrease drought impact.3. producing fresh and organic agricultural products.4. increasing the knowledge of farmers through training.
