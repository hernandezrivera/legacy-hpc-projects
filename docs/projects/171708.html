Project Summary

This proposal aims to address the needs of people affected by the protracted crisis, COVID-19, and droughts in three districts of (Belet Xaawa, Garbaharey, and Geriley in Bardeere of the Gedo region, in the Jubaland state of Somalia through improving access to WASH services for a targeted 25000 vulnerable persons. The needs that call for this intervention has been identiﬁed in community action plans developed by Disaster Risk Reduction (DRR) committees in the target locations through a recently conducted Participatory Disaster Risk Assessment (PDRA) by ASEP.
The project will focus on aspects of inclusion, considering the disabled; the design of all facilities to be constructed will be friendly to the disabled’s needs and appropriate for the use by the disabled category of the beneficiary. In total, 1242 persons with disability will benefit from the ventures, furthermore focus will also be made on the girl child in schools by providing dignity kits to the girl child in ten schools in 2 districts.
The project will undertake consultation with all stakeholders before actual implementation during the initial stages of the project. An event will be organized at the inception to inform all beneficiaries and stakeholders on the project’s scope and extent. enhanced community mobilization will be undertaken to involve all the community in the project area
Our proposed activities fall in line with the three WASH cluster identiﬁed objectives in the WASH cluster 2021 Primary Response Plan. Under Cluster Objective, ASEP proposes 8 activities, including hygiene promotion and COVID-19 awareness campaigns with one campaign per month for 6 months. The second proposed activity is distributing hygiene kits to 1500 Households (HHs) in the three districts. The distribution of these hygiene kits shall support the households during the effects of COVID- 19 as well as the drought that the target affected communities face.
Third, the provision of 600 dignity kits to 300 girls (2 kits per girl) in 10 identiﬁed schools in the districts of Garbaharey and Belet Xaawa.
The fourth intervention includes the proposed procurement and distribution of 4 disinfectant generators to produce sanitizers for 4 schools in Garbehey and Belet Xaawa. These disinfectant generators shall protect the staff and students from the spread of COVID- 19 and improve the schools’ hygiene standards.
Furthermore, in providing life-saving water for the most vulnerable during the effect of COVID–19 and the drought, the proposal through the proposed ﬁfth intervention aims to provide life-saving water to 2100 persons (15 liters per person daily) for 60 days. Sixth, the provision of 300 water containers for the collection and storage of water. Seventh, Household Water Treatment provision for two months for the water catchment community in Geriley for 60 days. In the last proposed intervention, 154 individual women in the three targeted districts will be engaged in environmental cleaning of their surroundings for 6 months, earning USD 30 per month on a Cash for Work (CFW) basis.
On cluster objective, ASEP proposes ﬁve interventions: One is the rehabilitation of 10 existing water kiosks in Belet Xaawa. The second intervention includes the rehabilitation of the water intake in Gawido, in Belet Xaawa. The third is establishing a small water supply from a sand dam water source that will incorporate a solar pumping unit and the reticulation works that will feed into an existing storage tank. The ﬁnal intervention is establishing and training 3 water committees to ensure the proper maintenance and sustainability of water services in Garbaharey, Gawido, and Geriley.
On cluster objective 3, ASEP proposes construction of 150 safe and accessible gender-segregated latrines in the three districts. This will be in line with maintaining safety and equity and accessibility of the vulnerable groups that include women, girls, boys, the elderly, and people with disabilities when accessing WASH services.
