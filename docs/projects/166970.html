Project Summary

Enlargement of Project # 159475: 1. Provision of hygiene kits including masks, soaps to 400 beneficiaries.
2. 50 handwashing jerricans in densely populated IDP settlements. This will serve a total of 1500 IDPs.
3. Update and disseminate multisectoral referral pathways identifying available services and modes of service delivery. The project will conduct service mapping of GBV and COVID sercices in Gedo and L/Juba and will continue to update on monthly basis. This will be done jointly with the relevant ministries and GBV partners.
4. At least 10,000 individuals will be reached with key messaging (images and written) that integrate prevention of GBV and COVID.
5. Support services centers with hygiene materials and protocols in line with government-approved procedures. _x005F_x000B_Support to 20 service centers including health clinics, MCH, GBV-one-stop centers, Madarassas that are opened with hygience materials and protocals that help them prevent the spread of the Covid-19. Graduates from a vocational training program and who have since established textile-based business will be involved in the manufacture of up to 10,000 face masks which will be distributed to the service centers. Soaps and hand washing devices will be distributed to the service centers. , Total estimated beneficiaries = 21900
