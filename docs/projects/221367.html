Project Summary

The project aims to provide comprehensive emergency assistance to vulnerable displacement affected populations in the Northwest and Southwest regions of Cameroon.
Key interventions will focus on:
Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene (WaSH): Improving access to clean water, sanitation facilities, and hygiene promotion.
Shelter and Non-Food Items (NFIs): Providing essential shelter materials and household items.
Food Security and Livelihoods: food assistance with cash transfers, and livelihood activities to enhance resilience.
Education: Ensuring access to quality education for displaced children and youth, including psychosocial support.
All activities will be implemented with a strong focus on protection. This includes:
Safe and Inclusive Programming: Ensuring that protection is mainstreamed in all activities and that the latter are designed and implemented to be sensitive to the specific needs of vulnerable groups, including women, children, the elderly, and persons with disabilities.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The ongoing crisis in the Northwest and Southwest regions of Cameroon has resulted in a complex humanitarian situation, necessitating an integrated response to address the multifaceted needs of the affected population. The crisis has led to significant displacement, destruction of infrastructure, and disruption of livelihoods, resulting in a dire humanitarian situation. A comprehensive approach is imperative to address the interconnected challenges faced by the affected communities. This includes: Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene (WASH): The crisis has disrupted access to clean water and sanitation facilities, leading to increased risks of waterborne diseases. An integrated response should prioritize the rehabilitation and construction of water points, the improvement of sanitation facilities, and the promotion of hygiene practices. Shelter and NFIs: Many displaced persons are living in overcrowded and unsanitary conditions. The provision of adequate shelter, essential household items, and protection services is crucial to ensure their safety and well-being. Education: The conflict has disrupted the education of children and youth, impacting their future prospects. Continued access to education is essential to mitigate the long-term consequences of the crisis. Food Security and Livelihoods: The destruction of livelihoods and agricultural infrastructure has left many people without sustainable income sources. Supporting livelihoods recovery, including through agricultural inputs, vocational training, and microfinance, is essential to ensure food security and economic stability. Civil Documentation: The loss of civil documentation, such as birth certificates and identity cards, hinders access to essential services and rights. Restoring and issuing civil documentation is crucial for ensuring the legal identity and protection of affected individuals. Housing, Land, and Property (HLP): The conflict has led to displacement, land disputes, and property damage. Addressing HLP issues is essential for promoting durable solutions and preventing further displacement. By addressing these interconnected needs through an integrated approach, it is possible to mitigate the impact of the crisis, promote resilience, and support the recovery and development of the affected communities.
