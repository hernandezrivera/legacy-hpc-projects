Project Summary

Child Protection Services to IDPs, Returnees and Host Communities at Ngala, Bama, Konduga and Mobbar L.G.As of Borno State is an initiative designed to address the escalating child protection concerns resulting from the conflict and insecurity in Northeast Nigeria. Focused on vulnerable groups, including children in IDP camps, secondary displaced children, returnees, and those with disabilities, the project aims to implement a holistic approach in the high-severity locations of Bama, Ngala, Mobbar, and Konduga L.G.As of Borno state. The project encompasses Birth Registration, Positive Parenting and Psychosocial Support, Community Awareness, and Inclusion of People with Disabilities.
Objectives:
Birth Registration:
Facilitating formal birth registration processes for all children in the target areas.
Establishing mobile birth registration units in collaboration with local authorities.
Conducting awareness campaigns to highlight the importance of birth registration.
Positive Parenting and Psychosocial Support:
Providing caregivers with positive parenting skills.
Establishing safe spaces and support groups to address psychosocial distress.
Training community volunteers and leaders to identify and respond to signs of distress.
Community Awareness:
Launching community-wide awareness campaigns on child protection risks.
Utilizing various communication channels to disseminate key messages.
Engaging community influencers to advocate for child rights.
Inclusion of People with Disabilities:
Developing inclusive child protection programs tailored for children with disabilities.
Ensuring accessibility to services and support for children with disabilities.
Collaborating with local disability organizations to promote inclusion.
Implementation Strategy:
Partnership Building:
Collaborating with local and international organizations to strengthen the child protection network.
Building partnerships with healthcare providers, education institutions, and disability-focused organizations.
Capacity Building:
Training local child protection workers, community volunteers, and healthcare professionals.
Providing continuous training and supervision for effective program implementation.
Monitoring and Evaluation:
Implementing a robust monitoring and evaluation system to track progress.
Conducting regular assessments to identify emerging challenges.
Soliciting feedback from communities and stakeholders for continuous improvement.

1 - Needs

Child Protection needs are likely to increase in 2024, due to persistent conflict and insecurity, populationmovement, displacement, camp closure and the continued influx of people from NSAG-controlled areas,and other socio-economic conditions. The well-being of children is threatened by multiple child protectionrisks/needs inclusive of early/child marriage, family separation, physical and sexual violence, psycho-socialdistress, recruitment by non-state armed groups, limited access to food, limited access to education, andinadequate parental care, particularly for children with disabilities. Also, the current economic inflation hasfurther exposed children to risk, as care givers do not have enough resources to provide the basic needs ofthe children leading to neglect and harmful child labour. Girls, especially adolescent girls, will be at risk ofearly child marriage and sexual violence continues to significantly affect the physical, mental, emotional,and social well-being of children, and social norms affecting children’s rights.

2 - Activities

Activities:1. Birth Registration: • Activity: Establish mobile registration units in Bama, Ngala, Mobbar, and Konduga.• Target: Register 4000 children under the age of 5 in target areas within the first eight months.• Rationale: Mobile units ensure accessibility, reaching remote and high-risk areas where formal registration services are limited. High registration rates contribute to securing children's legal identities, protecting their rights, and enabling access to essential services.2. Positive Parenting and Psychosocial Support:• Activity: Conduct positive parenting workshops and establish safe spaces.• Target: Reach 4000 caregivers and establish 2 safe spaces in each target area within the first year.• Rationale: Positive parenting workshops equip caregivers with essential skills to create a nurturing environment. Safe spaces provide platforms for psychosocial support, fostering resilience and emotional well-being among children and caregivers.3. Community Awareness:• Activity: Launch community-wide awareness campaigns through various channels.• Target: Community awareness to 8000 caregivers/guardians on child protection risks within the first six months.• Rationale: Community awareness is pivotal in preventing child protection risks. Utilizing diverse communication channels ensures a widespread reach, promoting behavioural changes and advocating for child rights.4. Inclusion of People with Disabilities:• Activity: Develop and implement disability-inclusive child protection programs.• Target: Ensure accessibility to services for 40% of children with disabilities in target areas.• Rationale: Inclusive programs address the unique needs of children with disabilities, promoting equal access to education, healthcare, and psychosocial support. Collaboration with local disability organizations ensures cultural sensitivity and effective implementation. Modalities and Technologies:1. Mobile Registration Units:• Utilize portable devices equipped with data capture capabilities for efficient and accurate birth registration.• Leverage cloud-based technologies for secure data storage and real-time reporting.2. Positive Parenting Workshops and Safe Spaces:• Implement virtual (where mobile network is available) and in-person workshops using online platforms and community centres.• Establish communication channels (hotlines, chat services) for continuous remote support.3. Community Awareness Campaigns:• Employ a multi-channel approach, including radio broadcasts, community meetings, and social media.• Utilize mobile messaging services for targeted outreach to ensure wider coverage.4. Inclusive Programs for People with Disabilities:• Develop accessible materials and information in multiple formats, including braille and audio.• Implement assistive technologies to enhance the participation of children with disabilities.Justification of activities:• Community Engagement: Emphasize strong community engagement strategies, involving local leaders, influencers, and volunteers. This ensures the cultural relevance of activities and enhances community ownership, leading to sustainable impact.• Data Security: Highlight robust data security measures in place, emphasizing the use of secure technologies and adherence to data protection regulations to safeguard sensitive information.• Monitoring and Evaluation: Stress the importance of a rigorous monitoring and evaluation framework, ensuring continuous assessment of activities, early identification of challenges, and the ability to adapt strategies for maximum impact.• Sustainability: Showcase plans for sustainability, including capacity building of local stakeholders, community empowerment, and integration with existing structures and services.
