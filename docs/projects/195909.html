In addition to coordination and learning through improving evidence-based knowledge on community-based ECD and engagement with the Education sector.Our work with the regional ECD center and training a core team of master trainers, then support and follow up with them to transfer this training to 1000 1st grad teachers .The overall goal of EDU is to equip girls, boys,and young adults with the knowledge, skills, attitudes, and values to help them interact effectively with the world and contribute to society.The main objectives for SMILE age7-18 are:1.Scale-up community-based EDU models namely community-based Learning CBLCs for the most marginalized students,2.Improve community support and to increased access to EDU for most marginalized children and youth,3.Continuing the technical support for trained EDU facilitators in the existing CBLCs. In 2023 the project aims to increase opportunities for 10000 students between age 7-18 years out of school or at risk of dropping out with focus on girls,PwDs, and IDPs, to pursue education in a safe environment in Salamieh,Damascus, rural Damascus,Aleppo,Tartous, and latakia.It will work with local teachers and community groups to establish safe learning spaces to deliver non-formal education(NFE) for out of school children or those who are at risk of dropping out.Education gender-sensitive curriculum was developed for target students including child protection,PSS,and special education and conducts awareness-raising campaigns to parents and communities.in addition to life skills curriculum for youth.AKF believes in the importance of working on authorities' levels and continuing professional training for teachers,so the proposed intervention in Formal education for 2023 will be continued in previously proposed activities.In 2022 AKF trained and built capacity of120 supervisors and heads of sectors as a master trainer for ECD regional centers in Hama,Idlib,Raqqa,Damascus,Rural Damascus,and Tartous. Trained supervisors will train on their turn 1800 1st grades teachers.To ensure sustainability, AKF will follow up with the formal and non-formal teachers through Community of practice sessions and continue the professional development training for 3000 male and female supervisors and teachers on the established online courses(Arabic& English)(Creating an inclusive learning environment,developmental Dialogues) focusing on equity, teachers' sensitivity, classroom,differentiation, individual collaborative learning,effective facilitation,and planing for 5 in person training days about blended learning and IT skills.

3 - Needs

The long-lasting complex conflict has devastated its education system which had a catastrophic impact on the availability and quality of education services across the country More than 2M children are out of school.In 2022,children continued to drop out of school to support their families.48% of families with children aged 6-17 reported that the reason their child was not attending school was to work and support the household(HNO2023),so children and youth, mainly females, are growing up unprotected, disconnected, and often with no access to educational opportunities.In 2022 AKF started working on the sustainable module approach designed as an attempt to save the education situation.The rapid assessment studies reported that parents stressed the importance and priority of education for their children and their concerns about the quality of Education provided to children within schools.Also parents reported their inability to support their children with private lessons or private institutes due to economic conditions.AKF response was through internal and external funds and in collaboration with the local NGOs and associations are establishing a number of safe learning spaces were established to provide tutorial support in main subjects(Math, Physics, Chemistry, Arabic, English,and Science) in addition to providing ECD services for the vulnerable children in the most of Syria governorates.

4 - Implementation of activities

Project aims to improve the access to basic services,electricity,water,education,and health, and enhance the quality of formal and non-formal education for children and youth through, Scaling up community-based Learning platforms for marginalized children and youth through following activities:•Enhance community-based and nonformal education platforms as follows:•Follow up the existing 34Community-Based Child Centers (CBCC) in all governorates except Idleb.•Follow up the existing 15Learning spaces to provide non-formal education(NFE)•Scaleup using the curriculum for out-of-school children or at risk of dropping out which is gender-sensitive and based on UNICEF’s standard operating procedure for NFE•Conduct community of Practice session for 300 Community Management Comities members and re-forming them based on their needs•Conduct inperson and remote community of Practice sessions for 225 non-formal teachers at 34CBCCs•Continue conducting in-person and remote community Practice sessions for 170 non-formal teachers at 17safe learning centers•Enhance and continue the technical support for ECE education personnel through cooperation with ECDRC•Continue the training for 60 counselors and social workers from the established learning centers who trained on child protection and referral pathways.•Ensure literacy sessions for parents•Conduct back-to-learning campaigns through community participation•Conduct life skills training and awareness for adolescents,especially girls

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

nsufficient water and sanitation facilities; inability to maintain Covid-19 safety measures including positive behavioral changes. Mitigation: Learning facilities will be improved to ensure sufficient water and sanitation facilities will be provided and maintained. WASH facilities will be sex and age-segregated and accessible to people with disabilities. Students will be trained on proper hygiene practices. All procedures will be coordinated with health authorities. Learning facilities will have Covid-19 safety protocols including identification, prevention measures, awareness-raising and a referral system between the learning facility and the relevant health centre Safety and Security concerns about male and female youth exposure to physical or verbal harassment on their way to the centres. Mitigation: AKF will ensure a safe and secure environment by selecting centre locations in safe and easy-to-access areas, and providing transportation. CBCC facilitators selection committee might face pressure or might be accused of bias in selecting the facilitators. Mitigation: AKF will start with mobilizing communities to consider both qualification and social representation as selection criteria for committees. AKF will expand reach to communities as possible, to increase the probability of qualified and agreed upon facilitators to lead the CBCC, these committees will be responsible for parents’ engagement in Centres management through follow up meetings and sharing feedback.


8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Risk 1: There is a risk that the project activity may lead to safeguarding issues for women involved in the project. Risk 2: The project activities time and locations might be unsafe for the vulnerable groups especially for women and children. In line with AKF safeguarding policy, manual and standards, the project PSEA strategy that will ensure the following mitigation measures are in place: • Train all project staff on safeguarding policies. • Ensure all involved partners adhere to AKF's Safeguarding Policy and Code of Conduct • Develop gender-responsive referral pathways and ensure project beneficiaries and vulnerable groups are aware of safeguarding policies and can access reporting mechanisms. • All data collection will consciously abide by robust ethical standards, guided by AKF’s Codes of Conduct and Safeguarding Policies. • Conduct an assessment of safety aspects in spaces used for activities implementation as well as the surrounding environment. The project will adopt measures and mechanisms to protect beneficiaries and hold superiors and subordinates to account alike. AKF follows specific process and procedures to integrate safeguarding in the Project Cycle which are: • Determine expected safeguarding risk using (Risk Register tool) • Determine safeguarding measures to reduce the risks through (mitigation plan and indicators). • Evaluating safeguarding at the project Cycle before, during and after implementing the project, using Project Cycle Check list.
