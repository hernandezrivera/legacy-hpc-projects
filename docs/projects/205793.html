Project Summary

Kapoeta East is categorized as priority 2 by the 2023 IPCs analysis. The county is therefore recommended for an immediate scale up of food production to mitigate food insecurity by early 2024.
REDO South Sudan aims to promote and improve the farmer’s capacity to produce and harvest enough food for both consummation and increase access to the market where the beneficiaries will be able to sell their produces and maximize profit for sustainability.
To achieve the above aims, REDO through this proposed intervention targets 800 HHs that will be directly involved in the agricultural project activities. To realize its objectives, the project envisage:
1. Provision of Environmentally Friendly Income generating activities (IGA) groups established;
2. Training of Trainers (TOT) refresher training on climate-smart agronomic practices,
3. Provision of business start-up grants to 800 followed by post-heaviest handling technologies,
4. Provide Farmers with high quality crops kits (maize, sorghum, and cowpea, provide Farmers/vegetable group kits with (Amaranth, collard, okra, tomato, onion, watermelon, pumpkin-each one sachet),
5. Provide Vegetable groups with agricultural tools/maloda, and irrigation treadle pumps,6.
6. Conduct awareness raising on community based early warning systems and disaster preparedness, training and or strengthened farmer’s association with refresher training, and support Target community committees to have contingency plans in place and are able to use it in case of emergency and training of partners on gender and protection mainstreaming into food security and livelihood intervention.

1. Needs

An Inter-Agency Food Security Assessment conducted in Kapoeta East in April 2023 shows that Food insecurity is likely to rise by seven percent across the County in the coming months, compared to last year, according to the findings. The latest IPC report projects that from April to July 2023, over 7.7 million people are facing high levels of acute food insecurity (IPC3+) and over 2.9 million people will be in emergency acute food insecurity (IPC4+). Given the high levels of severe acute food insecurity in South Sudan, there is a need for immediate scale-up of multi-sectoral humanitarian assistance to save lives and prevent the total collapse of livelihoods in the affected counties, particularly those with a high share of populations in Emergency (IPC Phase 4) and Catastrophe (IPC Phase 5) acute food insecurity. For populations in Minimal (IPC Phase 1) and Stressed (IPC Phase 2) acute food insecurity, there is a need for resilience-building support and enhanced disaster risk reduction (DRR) strategies to mitigate the effects of climate change. Urgent action is also required for populations classified in Crisis (IPC Phase 3) acute food insecurity to protect their livelihoods and reduce household-level food consumption gaps.REDO South Sudan objective is to revive firming strategies in order to meet the demand of both production and supplies. REDO South Sudan will work in close collaboration with Food Security and Livelihood (FSL) cluster and other partners implementing the agriculture projects in the same location. REDO South Sudan with support from the farmers’ association, will identify together with women groups, community leaders and local authority the target beneficiaries for this particular project. The economic activities will target farmers, women that are involved in agricultural work, those one doing small entrepreneurship to increase food basket for the family and sustain improved productivity. This objective is also in line with the South Sudan Cluster strategies of increasing access and provide capacity development to agriculturalist while focusing on the current technologies used to enhanced production and increase accessibility by farmers to the local and international markets so that both producers and consumers should benefit from the efforts of the local population rather than depending on the food items being import from the neighboring countries. As such, REDO South Sudan will strive to achieve the objectives of the project and work tirelessly with farmer’s associations, women’s group and local authorities to identify the beneficiaries for capacity building, and assess the nearby markets and connect the beneficiaries to those markets so that they are able to sell their products and gain the profit anticipated from the productions and heaviest.
