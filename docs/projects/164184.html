Project Summary

The Syrian war is entering its tenth year, about 11.7 million people they still need humanitarian assistance, because of increased food insecurity, displacement and a complex security situation have limited Syrians' access to diets and livelihood opportunities.
During the past months, the suffering of the displaced has increased as it approaches Idlib
The bombing of Maarrat An Numan caused thousands of people to flee to the borders between Syria and Turkey.
All reports of OCHA affairs, which tracks the movement of the displaced, as well as an assessment of needs from the Mattar team, indicate that 4,798 families have been displaced from Maarrat An Numan to Jisr-Ash-Shugur, and that these families need food
The project aims to distribute food baskets to 4,798 families, or the equivalent of 28788 people in Idleb / Jisr-Ash-Shugur for a period of 6 months.
Objectives:
1- Increased access to monthly emergency food for newly displaced families
2- Contribute to improving the food security situation of people who are food insecure in Idleb - Jisr-Ash-Shugur
3- 4,798 families receive a monthly food basket
