Project Summary

Sanitation focused on participatory health and hygiene education in each district (SAFPHH).
To rightfully ensure that all people at risk of acquiring COVID-19 are identified, reached, and involved.
To proactively communicate, assist and encourage a two-way dialogue with communities, districts, and the public on the risks and preventive measures of COVID-19. perceptions, behaviours and To existing barriers, specific needs, knowledge gaps and provide the identified communities/groups with accurate information tailored to their circumstance
Monitoring and evaluation of the interventions to be implemented.
Sensitization and awareness against stigmatization of children, staff, mothers, caregivers, and the community affected by COVID-19 in each district.
Social protection of children at health facilities, schools, and the community.
Strengthening of caregivers, volunteers, and the COVID-19 Taskforce through Zoom and Skype meetings and the use of messaging strategies to reach partners.
Reduction of impact outbreak and support control measures in respective districts.
Coordinated meetings and discussions on COVID-19 via online platforms ie; Skype, Zoom, and Whatsapp messenger.
To revise our action plan against COVID-19 according to how the situation evolves.
