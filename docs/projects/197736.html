Project Summary

The project aims to create a supportive environment for vulnerable groups including women, children and PWDs in Idleb and Aleppo through provision of Early Recovery intervention in-line with HRP goals, as, the programme objectives and activities explained in detail in this proposal have been designed through practical practice in NW Syria and direct communication and dealing with the women, girls, elderly, PWDs.
Putting into consideration the recent COVID-19 pandemic crisis in NW Syria, strict measures, following the WHO recommendations and the COVID-19: Guidance Note on Gender-Based Violence Service Provision, will be applied to all staff, volunteers, contractors and for sure the beneficiaries.
This intervention will be implemented in the WGSS, CFS of Amal in the Idleb and Aleppo - which targets the IDPs and the host community
Amal organization will seek to support ERL sectoral with 6-month project.
Activities enrolled in this proposal include;
1. Provision of vocational training Providing special training courses to enable women and caregivers to work in the humanitarian organizations: project management, MEAL & Data collection, Operation (HR, Finance, logistic), and writing CV.
2. Apprenticeship: Empowerment of adolescents and youth including the payment of transportation and the distribution of small equipment to these beneficiaries after the end of the followıng trainings
A. Photoshop and graphic design and Digital security
B. Media: principles of media work, text editing, preparing press reports
D. Microsoft Officeç
3. Creating job opportunities after providing the training.

3 - Needs

14M people remain in need of humanitarian assistance, and some of the new challenges with COVID-19. The long-standing needs of 6.9M IDPs remain staggering, particularly for the over 2M people in 1,760 informal settlements and planned camps, often hosted in inadequate shelters and limited access to basic services. Further, the needs of overstretched host communities and those who have returned to their places of origin continue to face inadequate living standards. (HNO 2022) Needs related to basic service delivery, livelihoods and income generation have grown consistently across Syria, these needs require a more comprehensive longer-term response and will remain inadequate and hampered by the lack of infrastructure, investment, human resources and, increasingly.the ERL sector estimates 11.6 million people in need of early recovery and livelihood support. 51 percent are estimated to be women, 45 percent children, and 26 percent persons living with disabilities (PLWD). unemployment is estimated at 50 percent of the working-age population, and over 60 percent of youth.

4 - Implementation of activities

Amal will communicate with the local councils and the local community to explain the project, its objectives and the target groups.In addition, it will advertise the project through social media channels (Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, WhatsApp groups).After signing the agreement, the purchase of equipment necessary to start the implementation of the activities and the preparation of the centre will begin to announce receiving the beneficiaries’ requests.After receiving the applications, the beneficiaries will be selected based on the selection criteria of the project to start benefiting from the activities.After selecting the beneficiaries, they will be divided into groups based on the types of proposed activities, and then the following activities will be implemented:Provision of vocational training, Apprenticeship training, and Creating job opportunities after providing the training.

8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Risk: Risk of exploitation or abuse by staff members/service providers, and low knowledge on PSEA and other types of exploitation and where to report. Impact: high protection riskMitigation:Amal has policies, procedures, and other measures in place that relate to preventing sexual exploitation, abuse, and harassment. So, Amal will work on the following: 1- train the staff on code of conduct, PSEA, CSG and follow them periodically2- Amal ensures to have in a place an accessible mechanism for reporting sexual exploitation and abuse in line with Amal safeguarding policy.3- Activating complaints mechanisms to protect PSEA in particular, placing them in easily accessible places, desigsning awareness-raising banners, placing boxes for complaints and suggestions, and activating the hotline number (WhatsApp)4- Designing special complaints mechanisms for children being children through drawing or other tools5 - provide awareness-raising sessions in the targeted community on PSEA and methods for reporting them and gaining the trust of society and spreading humanitarian principles and stick to them.
