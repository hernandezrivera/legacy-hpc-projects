Project Summary

The proposed project aims to enhance food security of vulnerable conflict-affected IDP, host community and Returnee households in the North West and South West regions of Cameroon by providing Conditional Cash Assistance to create Sustainable agricultural assets. These assets include community farms, poultry farms, small ruminant rearing, and fishponds. Additionally, the project will establish Village Savings and Loans Associations (VSLAs) to enhance financial inclusion and resilience.
The Overall ojective of the project is to enhance the resilience and self-reliance of 2000 vulnerable IDP, Host community and Returnee Households in the Northwest and Southwest regions of Cameroon by improving their food security, promoting sustainable agricultural practices, and fostering financial inclusion through the provision of conditional cash assistance and the establishment of Village Savings and Loans Associations (VSLAs).
The Specific Objectives will be to:
1. To improve food security and livelihoods of vulnerable IDP, Host Communty and Returnee households through the creation of agricultural assets.
2. To enhance community resilience and self-reliance of IDPs, Host Community and Returnees by establishing VSLAs.
3. To provide conditional cash assistance to support the development of sustainable agricultural practices of Vulnerable IDPs, Host Communities and Returnees.
4. To foster community cohesion and cooperation through collective agricultural activities.
Number of Beneficiaries:
- Households: 2,000 vulnerable IDP, Host Communty and Returnee households in the Northwest and Southwest regions.
- Individuals: Approximately 10,000 individuals, assuming an average of 5 members per household.
Agricultural Assets to be created:
- Establish 20 community farms.
- Set up 20 poultry farms
- Initiate 20 small ruminant rearing projects.
- Create 20 fishponds.
Village Savings and Loans Associations (VSLAs):
- Form and operationalize 20 VSLAs.
Training and Capacity Building:
- Train 2,000 individuals in financial literacy, and sustainable agricultural practices.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The project aims to address critical needs of IDPs, Hosts and Returnees, which have been exacerbated by socio-political unrest and economic instability in the Northwest and Southwest regions of Cameroon. Key assessed needs and how the project activities will address them include: 1. Food Security:• Need: Many households face food insecurity due to disrupted agricultural activities and displacement.• Response: The project will establish community farms, poultry farms, small ruminant projects, and fishponds to increase local food production and availability. 2. Livelihoods and Economic Stability:• Need: Vulnerable populations, especially women and youth, lack stable income sources and economic opportunities.• Response: By providing conditional cash assistance and supporting the creation of agricultural assets, the project will help beneficiaries generate income and improve their economic stability. 3. Financial Inclusion:• Need: Many community members lack access to financial services, which limits their ability to save, invest, and manage risks.• Response: The establishment of Village Savings and Loans Associations (VSLAs) will provide a platform for savings, access to small loans, and financial education, enhancing financial inclusion and resilience. 4. Capacity Building:• Need: There is a need for improved knowledge and skills in sustainable agricultural practices, financial management, and business operations.• Response: The project will offer training in financial literacy, business management, and modern agricultural techniques, empowering beneficiaries with the skills needed for sustainable development. 5. Community Cohesion and Social Stability:• Need: The ongoing conflict has strained social ties and community cohesion.• Response: By promoting collective agricultural activities and the formation of VSLAs, the project will foster cooperation, mutual support, and social cohesion within communities. 6. Resilience and Self-Reliance:• Need: Communities need to build resilience to withstand future shocks and reduce dependency on external aid.• Response: The project’s focus on sustainable agricultural practices and financial inclusion will enhance the self-reliance and resilience of beneficiaries, enabling them to better cope with future challenges.
