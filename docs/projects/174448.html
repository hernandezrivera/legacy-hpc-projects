Project Summary

The proposed project is designed in the light of UNOCHA Pakistan 2021 Human Response Plan Sectorial strategy (Protection)in 6 prioritized districts Sibi, Naseerabad, Jaffarabad, Harnai, Killa Abdullah & Musakhail of Balochistan focusing the sectoral objectives of GBV and Child Protection. The main purpose of the project is to establish and strengthen supportive mechanism to Prevent, Protect and respond SGBV and Child Abuse, Violence, Exploitation& neglect survivors at TCH, BHU, DHQ, DCPU & friendly reporting desks at police stations level and improve their access to Mental Health & Psychosocial Support (MPHSS) and protective services through proper case management
DANESH team will conduct formal & informal orientation meetings with district Administrations, Health, Police ,Social Welfare, Women Development departments etc to orient them regarding Project interventions and get their support for smooth implementation of the interventions. DANESH will update its existing service providers mapping directory and link and strengthen the Case Management and Referral Mechanism for GBV and children survivors of violence abuse and Exploitation.
Online or direct specialized psychosocial support sessions will be conducted survivors of Violence, Abuse, Exploitation & SGBV survivors to address their psychosocial needs, reduce their stigma & improve their access to protective services and reintegration in normal life. Front line workers of will be trained & their capacities will be built on psychosocial first aid, stigma prevention associated with Gender, SGBV & Child Protection & Covid-19.
DANESH with mutual consultation of health department identify, officially notify the potential focal persons for focused Tertiary care hospitals, DHQ hospitals & BHUs to respond SGBV, Child abuse, violence, exploitation & COVID-19 cases and built their capacities on psychosocial First aid & stigma associated with prevention of SGBV, Child protection issues including Violence, Abuse & Exploitation and referrals. Similarly with mutual consultation of police department established 18 Friendly reporting desks at 18 police stations. Focal persons will be deputed by Police department at these FRDs to respond SGBV, Child abuse, violence, exploitation cases at police stations level. Further police officials & focal persons of 18 Friendly Reporting Desk,40 Referral partners and Civil society staff members capacities will be built on response, management and documentation of SGBV & child Abuse, Violence and exploitation cases. All SGBV/SRH & VAC cases Data will be managed accordingly. DANESH will initiate community mobilization interventions in 6 DHQs & 24 BHUs catchment area,establish Community Based protection committees (CBPC) to identify , report & refer SGBV/SRH & Child Abuse, violence, Exploitation cases & disseminate information among general community on key messages on prevention & protection of SGBV & child Abuse, Violence, Exploitation & refer the cases for specialized Psychosocial support & protective services. Strong coordination will also be built among CBPCs and all concerned stake holders throughout the project period. The Beneficiary feedback & Complaint mechanism will be established at BHU level for complaint and feedback regarding the implementation of intervention and services. To reduce the stigma associated with SGBV & VAC, DANESH through stigma prevention interventions raise awareness on preventive & protective measures as well as aware community about available services for SGBV & VAC cases. The key strategies to be followed for the effective and efficient project implementation include direct & online psychosocial support service, behavior Change Communication, culturally appropriate techniques & technologies, capacity development of key stake holders & service providers, Age, Gender, diversity mainstreaming, ensuring govt lead/ownership and coordination with other partners
