Project Summary

A period of almost 11 months has passed since the conflict between the RSF and SAF broke out, 8.1 million people have been displaced, with 6.3 million being internally displaced (According to the OCHA’s Sudan Humanitarian Update (23 February 2024)). On the 15th of December, the RSF attacked Al Jazerah state resulting in displacing the IDPs and refugees who came previously to seek refuge in the state in addition to the state residents. The food security state in Sudan is in a dangerous position for Sudanese people with high risk of hunger given the effects the conflict had on Khartoum the economic center of Sudan as well as other states where agriculture is the main source of income and provides food for the rest of Sudan. Now these sectors are almost paralyzed leaving Sudanese people to face the risk of the coming period. According to FAO, the price of stable grains could rise by 500 – 100% in the coming few months. The situation of refugees in Sudan is equally dire, many of the refugees who were in Khartoum and other states were relocated to Gedaref, and some of them had to move to Jazeera and then to Gedaref, while losing their livelihood and belongings.
Additionally, there has been limited support provided to newcomers in Gedaref state given the restricted accessibility due to the security situation. According to UNHCR’s External update #44 SUDAN SITUATION 11-17 January 2024 in Gedaref, humanitarian partners continue to face access constraints because of denial and delayed approvals of travel permits to enter refugee camps. This is affecting the delivery of protection and assistance to the refugee population as partners are compelled to cancel or delay their interventions.
A lot of the refugees fleeing the war were relocated to camps in Gedaref, ZOA has been closely involved with new arrivals and was supporting new arrivals with hot meals and other refugees with agricultural support. Within this project, ZOA aims to assist the refugees to be able to face the upcoming food shortage through providing agricultural support, and vocational training as well as support through WASH activities such as rehabilitating water sources and latrines. It is also known that the influx there is new on the rise need for shelter, and to tackle that this project will support the refugees by providing NFI shelter items and emergency shelter kits. The project aims to address the needs of 35700 Refugees and 6300 host communities through WASH, Education, FSL, and Shelter activities.

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

The project is designed based on the pronounced and observed needs of the project target groups. We will ensure that the needs of refugees, returnees, and vulnerable residents will be addressed while protecting their dignity, and safety while creating access. The project is designed to put community participation as the main pillar. Thus, the project will not be implemented and monitored by ZOA staff only, but rather with the participation of beneficiaries and community members to ensure inclusion, relevancy to the needs of the people, and success of the project. In this manner, ZOA will ensure that people from different groups are part of the community consultations when it comes to determining priorities. Additionally, they would be included in setting criteria for the beneficiary’s selection and reviewing the selected lists. In line with protection principles, the project beneficiaries will be targeted by an inclusive committee representative of all the community groups men, women, youth, elders, persons with disability, etc. We will also work through and closely with protection actors in the targeted areas and make referrals where necessary. During registration and distribution of assistance, we will ensure that people with special needs have very short queue times and separate lines for women, men, and people with special needs.

4 - AAP & PSEA
4.1 - How will the project share information and collect community feedback? Please select as many as relevant.

Face to face with group consultations with aid workers

4.1.1 - How will the project use community feedback to improve and adapt activities? (open-ended/free text, required)4

The project team conducts a needs assessment at the beginning of the project. In this assessment, the team conducted FGDs, interviews, and more with different groups including women, men, men with disability women with disability from different age groups. With attention to localization, the local community is asked to identify a committee that supports the project implementation, monitoring, and evaluation. This committee should include members from all genders, ethnic, ages, and disability groups and they will be the focal people for their different groups. The project will also ensure that the community monitors the performance of the committees and ensures there are safe and open complaint channels. While also keeping the space open for the committee and community to give their complaints and suggestions regarding the project and how it addresses the different needs. These complaints and suggestions are to be seriously taken into consideration during the implementation, monitoring, and evaluation of the project. This is how the project is planned to include all groups and ensure their different needs are being met.

4.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will support Inter-Agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including Sexual exploitation and abuse complaints?

The project will also use interagency approaches through working with UN agencies, HAC, and sector working groups to take best practices and discuss possible challenges. ZOA has a well-designed reporting mechanism internally and externally where it allows beneficiaries to be able to report their complaints or exploitation cases directly from community committees, then our field staff and area managers to the Netherlands government. Complaints are taken seriously and there is a dedicated staff for feedback and complaints in each office, who will ensure proper and confidential investigation and address and escalate the case according to the given situation.
