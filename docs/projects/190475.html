Project Summary

ASDEPO aims to improve access to basic curative and preventive health care services for internally­ displaced and host communities of conflict and drought affected populations in Oromiya, Somali, Tigray, Afar and Gambella regions of 15 woredas. Proposed activities will focus on meeting the health cluster’s strategic plan and Humanitarian Response Plan 2022 objectives Support recovery and restoration of essential health services disrupted or damaged by natural or human induced disasters including minor rehabilitation of health infrastructure; provide quality care for people with physical injuries, disabilities and mental health needs of all forms of conflict and violence including sexual and gender-based violence, during and after health emergency / disaster; Prepare for, detect and respond to health emergencies and epidemic prone disease outbreaks, including COVID-19 pandemic and Provide accessible essential lifesaving health services to targeted populations including internally displaced persons and affected host communities, returnees, children, women of reproductive age and pregnant women
The project will address a total of 246,038(162484IDPs and 83,554 non displaced people of the specific needs and concerns of different gender and age groups through age and gender mainstreaming. The intervention will address the immediate needs of internally displaced and populations affected by the conflict and drought while supporting efforts to build local capacity and strengthen systems to respond better in response of emergency situation.
ASDEPO has planned to provide pediatric and adult, medical consultation, expanded programme for, immunization(EPI), sexual and reproductive health (SRH) services, health education and promotion, nutrition services, referral, and treatment services to needy populations in static health facilities or through MHNTs.

1. - Projects activities

Activity 1: Provide curative and preventative outpatient consultations for a total of 81,982 through deploying MHNT including general medical consultation and early lifesaving referral care in coordination with the respective woreda health offices. Activity 2: Provide Integrate Maternal health service of ANC, PNC, and FP, provide counseling for 3350 PLW and 54,128 FP service for Reproductive age women by ensuring Safe Motherhood Component of reproductive health within maternal health services. Activity 3: provide mental health and psychosocial support, basic emotional support, and confidential referrals to healthcare and other available services for 4,099 individuals of reported cases of GBV through deploying MHPSS officer Activity 4: Provide special need health services for SGBV (sexual and gender-based Violence) survivors, persons with disabilities, and MHPSS (Mental Health and psychosocial support) services fo 665 survivors. Activity 5: MHNT will provide monthly nutritional screening for 31,236 children aged 6-59 months and 21,920 PLW for acute malnutrition and requiring admission to nearby health institutions with stabilization centers. Enhance prevention of acute malnutrition through promotion of infant and young child feeding and micronutrient supplementation in children 0 -59 months, pregnant, and lactating women.Activity 6: Support access to services for the treatment of 2,720 children SAM with medical complications in Stabilization Centers (SCs) and 780 children in Outpatient Therapeutic Programme (OTP) for the Management of SAM without Medical Complications among children aged 6-59 months. Activity 7: Provide health education and counseling for 246,038 people. Provide health education on hygiene and sanitation practice, selected communicable disease, maternal and child health service, prevention of epidemic-prone diseases, optimal nutrition practice, gender-based violence, need for prioritizing children and women and other relevant topics for IDP and host community. Activity 8: Print and disseminate 18,000 IEC materials, to create awareness on health-related issues like child health, maternal health and COVID-19 prevention measures.Activity 9: Support the Expanded Programme on Immunization (EPI) for 5,413 children under one year to ensure that every child in the project areas has access to recommended lifesaving vaccines against diseases such as diphtheria, pertussis, tetanus, poliomyelitis, and measles. ASDEPO also improves COVID-19vaccination coverage as rapidly as possible, especially among populations designated as a high priority who remain unvaccinated or are not yet fully vaccinated.Activity 10: Support Vitamin A supplementation and deworming campaign for 31,236 eligible children during routine health care service follow up and screening sessions as per WHO protocol. Activity 11: Support the emergency response through conducting active case searching in collaboration and coordination of woreda health offices, IPs and communities incase any disease outbreaks occurred. The MHNTs will also integrate with the woreda disease surveillance system and support overall emergencyresponses.Activity 12: Provide capacity building 170 government health facility workers and 102 MHNT staff on PHEM, Measles and scabies and MISP for three days duration.Activity 13: Provide training on Child Safeguarding, GBV, AAP and PSEA policy for, 75 community and religious leaders, 170 health workers, 272 HEWs and 102 MHNT staff working for the project in the targeted Woreda for two days. Activity 14: Procure medicines to support the delivery of essential health and nutrition services such SC and OTP centers through the MHNTs in all the woredas where health facilities are severely damaged. ASDEPO will also mobilize medical and RH kits from UNFPA/WHO and regional health bureau to deliver emergency health care and SRH/GBV service.
