Project Summary

Continued opportunities for meaningful engagement and entrepreneurship improve capacities of young people, and minimize frustration and disempowerment. Empowered youth make positive life choices, safely transit to productive adulthood and contribute to strengthening social cohesion, protection, resilience and economic development. Three factors play a vital role in positive youth development:
• Structured capacity / asset development at home, in school, and in their community;
• Opportunities for adolescents to use their skills learned as participants and leaders in valued activities;
• Positive and sustained mentoring relationships between peers, and between adolescents and adults.

Needs

According to our field teams working in the targeted areas have observed a huge number of youth and adolescents out of schools with no basic education or work. There is currently situation of Deep feeling of frustration among the young people and adolescents in these communities as well as unemployment. The projects activities focus on this population category to engage and participate them in the community through adolescent-led initiatives, awareness campaigns and social mobilization

Implementation of activities

Activity 1: Running services for vulnerable youth and adolescents through mobile teams Activity 2: ADAP teams' management -Program Staff Activity 3: Support scaling comprehensive adolescents and youth friendly services through offering life-skills based education to /5000/ youth and adolescents Activity 4: Promote participation of /5000/ youth and adolescents through promoting basic vocational training Activity 5: Promote participation of /3500/ youth and adolescents through adolescent-led initiatives, awareness campaigns and social mobilization Activity 6: Promote participation of /6500/ adolescents and youth through sports for development

Protection Risk Analysis

Risk 1 : the youth female may exposed to sexual harassment due to mixing between male and females in our activity. risk level: medium Mitigation measures: -we will hire a well-trained team to such cases to control this situation. - we will make different separated session for male and female due to the traditional and religious rule of the targets area risk 2 : since our activities will target different type of community, some individual may expose to the Gender based violence. Risk level: medium Mitigation measures: Conduct awareness sessions about protection affairs and how to face them as well as continuous observation of the activities by our specialized staff.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risks: - As the activities will not cover needs of all in need beneficiaries, this may generate a case of tension among community individuals ( among the beneficiaries and non-beneficiaries) Mitigation measure: concentrating on vulnerable groups ans disabled and giving priority to them and mainstreaming this idea. Risk 2: potential delayed required approval by the local authority resulting in delayed implementing. Mitigation measures: continuous coordination with the relatived authorities and ministries to speeding up the routine procedures.
