Project Summary

The project aims to distribute in-kind materials related to income-generating projects for vulnerable families in the northern and eastern countryside of Homs in the areas of Talbiseh and Mahin, where families were displaced during the war, and after returning to their homes, they lost their sources of income.
Therefore, the project aims to distribute materials that help these families start their own projects to secure the basic needs of their children
The most vulnerable families in the region will be targeted after attending courses in the field of projects and economic feasibility
400 of the most vulnerable families will be targeted in two areas of Homs countryside
250 families in Talbiseh
150 families in Mahin

3 - Needs

11 years into the Syria crisis, humanitarian needs are deepening. Ongoing insecurity and the compounded impact of displacement, combined with worsening socio-economic conditions characterized by sharp currency depreciation, And as the economic situation continues to deteriorate, its impacts are being acutely felt by virtually all populations. By population group, it is the “vulnerable residents” category defined in the HNO - those that have not been recently displaced, that show the greatest increase in humanitarian needs: increasing from 6.4 million in 2020 to 9.2 million in 2021 – suggesting strongly that economic deterioration is now a major driver of needs in areas historically less directly affected by hostilities and displacement. ( 2022 HNO) therefore the Association is aiming at providing grants for families to start generating income projects to help them meet their monthly basic needs and mitigate the devastating effect of their increased poverty due to the current economic situation.
