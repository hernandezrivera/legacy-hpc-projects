Project Summary

Save the Children Zimbabwe (SCZ) intends to support boys and girls from ECD to Grade 7 across Zimbabwe so that they remain in school despite the acute humanitarian crisis. These boys and girls are at a risk starvation and malnutrition and this may lead to school dropouts depriving boys and girls of their right to education. This drought is being attributed to the unfavorable El Nino weather phenomenon. Weather expects have warned that the El Nino phenomenon caused poor crop yield in 2019 and this will cause acute food shortages in 2020. The toll will be great on young boys and girls in school thus compromising their right to uninterrupted education even during emergencies. Save the Children (SC) plans to support 179 234 boys and girls in 6 districts which are namely Rushinga, Gokwe South, Matobo, Binga, Mt. Darwin and Beitbridge. Emergency school feeding will be main activity of this project but to support boys better during this emergency SCZ will also promote an emergency sensitive teaching and learning environment programme. The project will work to improve health and sanitary condition in schools to avoid incidences of the spread of communicable diseases like cholera. The project will work to enhance the capacity of the Ministry of Primary And Secondary Education (MoPSE) to manage coordination of interventions of this nature during emergencies.
Water provision will be a big necessity as this period is associated with water scarcity. Other activities which will be conducted include procurement of dignity kits to retain girls, Counselling, Life Skills, DRR, and Psychosocial (PSS) Support to help build resilience among boys and girls. Education officials, teachers and parents will undergo training in mass feeding, school catch up programmes among other things. Teaching and learning materials will be supported and a school block grant programme will be conducted order to retain vulnerable boys and girls in schools. The whole process in this project will be very participatory in the entire project cycle. Boys and girls, the school development committee, female and male teachers will be involved in decision making. The project will take a particular emphasis on boys and girls with disabilities, living with HIV/AIDS and the disadvantaged orphans and vulnerable children. This project with a budget of $5 000 000 will reach out to 17234 children in the 6 Districts of Zimbabwe stated above. This project is very imperative at this time in the stated districts so that children are returned in school and those who have dropped out return back to school. This is with the understanding that education is a fundamental right for all children under any circumstances. Particularly in this humanitarian crisis SC understands that the provision of quality, basic and inclusive education should not wait even during emergencies. It is with this understanding that SC wants to provide this life saving intervention to boys and girls in Zimbabwe.

1 - Activities

Objective 1: A 75% decrease in the number of boys and girls not benefiting from school feeding from the target of 179 234 in 6 districts in Zimbabwe by December 2020Activities:• School feeding for 179 234 boys and girls in schools in 6 districts. • Capacity building of schools in 6 districts to manage effective school feeding.• School nutrition gardens in 6 districts• Advocacy for Water supply for schools in 6 districts. School feeding will be for all children in the schools in the districts depending on funding. If further targeting is required children from urban settings may be removed. Nutrition gardens will help complement the project by providing relish for the school feeding programme. Provision of water will be critical in this process. Objective 2: A 73% decrease in orphans and vulnerable boys and girls in emergencies from the target of 179 234 not benefiting from any form of support in 6 districts in Zimbabwe by December 2020Activities:• Psychosocial Support in schools for 179 234 boys and girls and their teachers (male and female) in schools in 6 districts• Disaster Risk Reduction in schools for male and female teachers, learners and (male and female) community members in schools in 6 districts.• Child safeguarding and protection in emergencies in schools in 6 districts• School health clubs in 179 234 in schools in 6 districts• Supply of dignity kits in 179 234 in schools in 6 districts• Recreational kits for children in emergencies in 179 234 in schools in 6 districts.• School catch up activities in 179 234 in schools in 6 districts.• 179 234 Boys and girls feedback meetings in schools in 6 districts• Male and female Teacher training on emergency sensitive teaching and learning activities in schools in 6 districts Work will be done to supports orphans and vulnerable particularly addressing barriers to enrollment or attendance. This includes provision of dignity kits and block grants among other things. PSS will be provided to support resilience building among these boys and girls. The children will be supported by recreational activities in safe spaces to help them cope and teachers will be supported with emergency sensitive teaching methodology to offer appropriate support to children. Objective 3: A 65% decrease in the number of boys and girls (ECD to Grade 7) among the target of 179 234 not enrollment in schools in 6 districts in Zimbabwe.Activities:• Community awareness on the importance of education to communities in 6 districts.• Community mobilization of boys and girls particularly orphans and those with disability to return to schools in 6 districts.• School fees payment for boys and girls in 6 districts. • Block Grants in 6 districts.• Purchase of teaching and learning materials Helping children come to school through block grants and the purchase of teaching and learning material will help retain children in school. The block grants will meaningful improve the teaching and learning environment of schools thus making them quality, appropriate and inclusive to all children. This will be followed up by a comprehensive outreach to the community on the importance of education.
