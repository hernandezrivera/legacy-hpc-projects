Project Summary

The Hagaa floods in July/August have had mixed effects. . While the floods disrupted recessional cultivation activities and inundated crops, the rains are supporting late planted Gu main season crops and Gu off-season crops and alleviated fears that the forecast of a below-average Hagaa season would deplete soil moisture and negatively affect off-season crops. In general, farmers have benefitted from improved access to water for irrigation. Worryingly however, the damage to road infrastructure and market access, have adversely affected terms of trade with higher prices of maize, and significantly lowering the prices for goats and daily labour wage rates by up to 30 and 102 per cent below the five-year average.
The current high levels of food insecurity in the country and more within Hirshabelle region, has been driven by drought conditions from late 2018 through mid-May 2019, exacerbated by below-average and erratically distributed rainfall during the 2019 Gu’ season (April-June), which began late and finished early. (FSNAU sept 2019) estimated 4.8 million people across Somalia are expected to face a humanitarian crisis, of these 2.6 are internally displaced people in various part of the country.
Previously, RRP carried out interventions in Hiraan region in partnership with FAO that offered rounds of cash plus agricultural inputs to 2120 farmers both agro-pastoral and the riverine settlements. This was accompanied with trainings done to champion the nutrition sector, and lead farmers to ensure sustainability of the same. This was similar to another project that was almost getting to an end in Beletweyne targeting 1700 beneficiaries with the same kind of intervention. Cash plus agricultural input before the start of the rains in every season has so far yielded fruits.
Therefore, in this proposed plan, beneficiaries will be benefiting from such interventions that are a continuity of what we previously implemented. Enhancing the capacity of the rain-fed and riverine farmers to rebuild their productive assets and reconstruct their capacity to absorb the recurrent climatic shocks. The project provides farm inputs, animal-drawn tools, water pumps, and extension services to the affected farmers. It also provides training on soil fertility amendments and good farming practices for ensuring sustainable food production in agro-pastoral and riverine livelihood zones. Through biometric registration, the identified HH headed with 30% prioritization of women will be done. The registered will within 3 cycles be provision of unconditional cash transfer to 1500 HHs with malnourished children as well as people living with disability. Provision of farming inputs to vulnerable riverine and agro-pastoralist farmers to 1280 HHs considering women headed HHs. RRP will also establish nutrition champions in the program who will be trained as well as lead farmers who will assist even after the stop of the response in guiding the community.
A call center will be in place to ensure that there is a good flow of feedback from the beneficiaries as well as the community.
RRP has on regular basis worked with the CWG to ensure that regular assessments are done to know how viable is the cash based intervention can be, with the intervention from FAO the livelihood situation is currently still necessary to have cash distribution to ensure that there’s no further deterioration in the previous state.
Coordination with other actors will be done to ensure that no duplication of interventions beneficiaries. Monthly, mid-year and end year reports will be done to the respective donors as well as the cluster.

Project Activities

Through provision of both conditional and unconditional cash transfers, selected through inclusive community-based targeting RRP will target riverine and Agro Pastoral settlements of Hirshabelle region. RRP will give seeds and farm inputs like sorghum, maize, cowpeas and vegetable and farm implements like hoe and forks to both Agro Pastoral and riverine communities in target of the rainy seasons. CFW activities will be identified in consultation with participants to ensure nature of work is selected in consideration to specific gender needs. Local authorities will be consulted as well as the relevant partners and clusters. targeting 2500 (1450 men and 1050 women)RRP intends to target populations in IPC3 and 4 especially the newly displaced and worst affected Agro Pastoralist and riverine communities. Payments will be done through mobile cash transfers, equivalent to 70 USD for 3 months of the food component of the Minimum Expenditure Basket. 1200 (650 men and 550 women)Provision of livelihood specific and seasonally appropriate inputs for vulnerable households: Emergency response will be supported through the provision of livelihood specific inputs to both rural and urban populations. For rural beneficiaries, activities will include formation of Agro Pastoral Field Schools, fodder provision for Agro Pastoral communities, livestock restocking and support to community animal health workers. 1200 (650 men and 550 women)For sustainability of the interventions, RRP will train nutrition champions as well as lead farmers who will be able to offer continuous support to the community all year round. targeting 100 (45 women and 55 men)Restoration of community productive assets and disaster preparedness: This will be done through rehabilitation of priority community assets which will be identified by the community through an inclusive process of consultations with Village Committees, the local community and authorities. - targeting 2500 (1450 men and 1050 women)Rehabilitation of assets will be done via the CFW modality to ensure that beneficiaries earn an income from rehabilitating these assets.

Mitigation Measures

RRP through proper community engagement, developed agreed-selection criteria for selecting beneficiaries in our previous FAO projects in Hirshabelle, which was endorsed by the community elders due to its inclusivity. We will ensure women and girls and at larger scale the entire community are made aware that humanitarian assistance is free and no conditions are attached to it. Beneficiaries have a one channel to report concerns through Complaint and Feedback Mechanisms (CFM). RRP shall establish accessible, confidential and well-understood beneficiary feedback mechanism in consultation with different groups. RRP will ensure appropriate equity and quality measures are put in place by ensuring services provided benefit to all community members. During consultation, women, boys and girls will collect accurate information about their specific food and nutritional requirements and preferences for food distribution points and livelihood services. RRP will ensure good working relationship with all stakeholders through genuine participation in the project areas.

Please provide further explanation

For a durable solution, the residence through RRP will be encouraged to work towards having the human rights endorsed as an act of parliament so that any violation will lead to prosecution. Multi-sectoral interventions are often more effective when coordinated, leaving IDPs more resilience to future shocks and able to cope with the grim reality of displacement. On the other hand, the project advocates the local integration of IDPs and enhancing living conditions in their places of origin, for those willing to return, thus providing durable solutions to the protracted IDP crisis in Somalia. RRP will also support communities in IDP sites towards self-reliance in locations where prospects of obtaining land rights is achievable while advocating with local authorities for public land for displaced people. To maximize impact and sustain project outcome, RRP will seek integration with other clusters providing response in areas where proposed activities will be implemented. Details pertaining to project activities will be shared through relevant clusters and 4W reporting matrix to avoid duplication of effort. Local communities will play a key role in different phases of the project to create sense of ownership which will contribute to sustaining of project outcomes. RRP will strive to build local capacities thus ensuring that various community leaders and committees are able to be sustainable maintain resources following project completion and handover.
