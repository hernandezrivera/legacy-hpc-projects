Project Summary

Al-Sham Humanitarian Foundation has a good experience in Humanitarian Action especially bakeries. The foundation owns 4 bakeries and 7 our mills offering bread during the year of 2016 for 1.8 million citizens.The project aims to Distributing bread to 2,100 families on a daily basis for a year
The project aims To provide life-saving humanitarian assistance to the most vulnerable people to alleviate suffering.
through this project, the bread will be offered to targeted families to reduce burden of living, improve their life condition, and support their resilience. The beneciaries will be chosen as per most vulnerable families with the help of hosting camp in the region. Through these procedures, the foundation ensures most benet for vulnerable families as that will be measured by consult those people about the feasibility of the project and its outputs. This project is designed to respond to the needs of the new inux of IDPs from southern Idleb and Aleppo, This project will provide cooked meals, RTERs and food parcels. As per the FSL Cluster recommendations, the cooked meals are going to be implemented for the rst 3 days of the emergency distributions, which will be followed by RTERs for a period of 4 weeks, and nally, we will distribute cash/voucher for food for 8 months to the same IDP households With a monthly value of $ 60 per family. The total number of beneciaries targeted are 5,500 individuals (1,100 households). This project will also help in decreasing severe food insecurities and that may result in malnutrition and other health related problems.
