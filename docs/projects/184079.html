Project Summary

This intervention aims to support primary schools in the targeted locations to develop and implement SIP and to assure the safety of children after their return to school and through the situation of the covid 19 and possible future emergencies.
Also, build the capacity of the teachers and school staff in the filed of EiE and help schools develop and implement preparedness plans and safety protocols. engaging the community in the process.
In addition to building the capacity of teachers while supporting their career development, engaging PTAs and the community, and over all establishing safe welcoming schools for children where they can receive the good quality education they deserve.
This project will benefit around 21,784 beneficiaries directly and over 40,000 in directly.

1 - Needs

Education needs are constantly rising in the different states of Sudan with speciall challenges to reach conflict affected states like Kordofan states, Darfur, blue Nile state and lately the red sea state. the fact that there is 1.5 million people with education needs in Sudan, 60% are boys and girls (0-17) years old and 15% are women (HNO 2020), and over 3M children are still out of school (MoE report/data 2021) raise the alarm that more efforts are needed to breach the gap. All children equally deserve access to education and good quality education, but access to education of poor quality is equivalent to access to no education at all.Good quality education is influenced by what goes on in the class room and beyond, that is why it is important to have a solid school Improvement Plan (SIP) to get effective academic outcomes and achievements as well as contribute in developing of the social and cultural wellbeing of the children and adults within the community and the school. The recent world wide pandemic of the novel corona virus has led to 130 countries to declare complete lock down which affected the education of millions of students. in Sudan school children and teachers are still suffering from the delay that the closure caused in addition to the unstable political and economic status which heavily affected the academic achievement for everyone .the safety of our children in schools and the staff is a high priority that is why it is important to prepare the school staff/ parents/ community with the right knowledge about safety and how to be prepared to deal with different kinds of emergencies while commencing education. Also the schools must be equipped with the right tools and facilities that meet the minimum safety standards to accommodate the needs of the students/teachers and staff with special attention to vulnerable groups like children with disabilities (CWD). Times of emergency call for special measures and procedures, that is why building the capacity of teachers on the different aspects relating to the emergency situations/ building resistance and providing PSS to children and parents is very important and will improve the education experience for them allowing them to develop and prosper.Paying special attention to improving teachers professional development, building the capacity of school staff and a welcoming, safe and improved school environment not only affect the academic path of children but also the teachers, and it helps to reduce drop out rates, increase retention and enrolment rates and decrease teachers migration from public schools to private schools and even teachers migration to out side of Sudan.
