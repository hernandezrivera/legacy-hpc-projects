Project Summary

Maram has designed this proposed to provide the Shelter and NFI assistance for the most affected people in NW Syria to upgrading their living condition through:
- Distribution of essential NFI items 5000 CRI kits.
- Site improvement through land preparation, drainage system and lighting poles for 4,000 shelter units.
- Rehabilitation damaged houses for 1000 houses.
1- Distribution of essential NFI items: MF plans to distribute 5,000 NFI kits to target 25,000 IDPs with different ages and vulnerable cases PWN (23 % men, 25 % women, 26 % Boys, and 27% girls ) taking into consideration Gender and age diversity through NFI kits distribution in Idleb and Aleppo area, where Maram Foundation has full access to the mentioned area and is implementing Emergency Response right now in it.
2- Site improvement through land preparation, drainage system and lighting poles:
The activity aims at upgrading the living conditions of the targeted IDP sites by improving their infrastructure, this will be achieved by implementing graveling rehabilitation activity for the roads and tents of the targeted sites, establishing ramps for elderly people and PLWD and establishing drainage systems inside the sites to avoid any damage to the tents and property of the IDPs during rainy seasons and thus protect the camps from the consequences of the floods.
Maram Foundation will provide graveling for the roads and ground insulation for the family tents for the affected camps in Idleb and Aleppo after Maram teams conduct a detailed rapid needs assessment.
The total target is thus 20,000 IDPs.
3- Rehabilitation damaged houses: MF will implement this activity to target both the host community and IDPs through shelter rehabilitation activities and rehabilitate shelter for 1,000 households (HHs), which include 5000 individuals living in 1,000 damaged houses in Aleppo and Idleb governorate. MF will provide them with shelter repair and rehabilitation activities. This project includes 59% host communities and 41% IDPs.
All activities within the projects will be according to the Shelter cluster objectives and will be designed according to the standards of SPHERE.
Furthermore, the planned intervention will mainstream protection principles especially “Do no harm” throughout the project cycle by considering the different needs of men, women, boys, and girls.
During this project, Maram will consult with the community and individual IDPs taking into account the speciﬁc needs of each group. Maram Foundation also will involve the women and girls through Focus Group Discussions (FGDs) during the community outreach plan.
Summary, this project aims to reach 70,000 individuals targeting most vulnerable men, women, boys and girls and prioritize family members with high vulnerabilities according to the beneficiary selection criteria (female/single/child-headed households, IDP status, no source of income and support, people with disabilities, elderly,...etc.).
Maram Foundation always seeks to high-level coordination with all parties involved in any project (SNFI cluster, Donors, local authorities, other NGOs, and other related parties) to avoid any duplication. So for the activities included in this project and during the designing phase, Maram will coordinate with all the relevant parties and select the locations according to the gaps, which means that there will not be any duplication during implementing the proposed project activities.

4 - Needs

The humanitarian consequences for people suffering the crisis in Syria are wide-ranging and profound. Overall, 11.06 million people are in need of some form of humanitarian assistance in 2020. This includes 4.65 million people estimated to be in acute need. As the crisis enters its tenth year, 6.1 million people remain internally displaced. 5.6 million people have fled their homes, the vast majority to neighbouring countries, with limited prospects for return due to ongoing hostilities in some locations, concerns regarding safety, and the lack of adequate housing, basic services and employment opportunities. Over 1.8 million population movements inside Syria were reported. Crucial civilian infrastructure such as schools, water supply systems, health facilities, and housing infrastructure has sustained extensive damage and much of it remains unrestored or in disrepair.So, MF seeks to save lives, uphold rights and promote access to safe and more privacy shelter through the provision of Shelter and NFI assistance in particular, which include distributing NFI items for the new arrivals, providing ground insulation of their tents in addition to improv the accessibility and the drainage systems in many IDPs sites that affected from flooding during winter seasons, and rehabilitate the houses for those who live in a damaged house where there is no privacy and not protected from the external living conditions. MF is currently implementing site improvement and Tent idistribution& insulation project in Aleppo and Idlep camps and informal settlements, in addition, MF had implemented a Shelter rehabilitation project in partnership with UNHCR, where rehabilitated 1100 damaged houses in Aleppo Governorate.
