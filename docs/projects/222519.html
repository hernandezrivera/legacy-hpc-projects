Project Summary

The project will focus on mechanical clearance (with a MW240) of potential agricultural land to improve food security in Akobo County, Jonglei State. Furthermore, the project will implement EORE to improve knowledge about ERWs among communities with a strong focus on children and youth, as they are disproportionally affected by ERWs in South Sudan. DCA's EORE team is using an innovative approach to ensure knowledge retention among the kids through dramas, music, poems and sports.
DCA will integrate the proposed clearing project will livelihood and resilience projects to ensure complementarity between the improved accessibility to land with improved knowledge and holistic food systems.
The project will clear 650.602 m2 in 2025 and reach 61.446 people with lifesaving messages through EORE, while 34.998 people are expected to directly benefit from the clearing activities.

1. Needs

Approximately 22,3 million m2 are still uncleared in South Sudan, hindering access to vital farming grounds, expansion of infrastructure, and restricting settlements. Furthermore, 67 people have been injured or killed in 2023 alone by ERWs (40 of them are children) and while this has significantly reduced in 2024 with four casualties and 9 injuries so far, the need for both awareness and clearance remains high, as new HA's are being identified resulting in the estimated contamination increasing from 17mio. m2 to over 22 mio. m2 from 2023 to 2024. The high levels of contamination are hindering all other development and humanitarian efforts in South Sudan and should therefore be prioritised to enhance the H-D-P nexus approach and save lives. Approximately 11 million m2 out of the 22,3 million m2 known hazardous areas are mine fields. With a MW240 DCA can efficiently and safely clear mine fields.Akobo County is not only highly contaminated, but also facing high levels of food insecurity, intra- and intercommunal violence, and grappling with the effects of climate change.

2. Project activities

The key activities are mechanical clearance of 650,602 m2 BAC and AP/AT mine fields with the MW240 and providing lifesaving messages to 61,446 community members through innovative approaches to ensure knowledge retention. The EORE activities include trainings of Community Focal Points (CFP) and Peer Risk Educators (PREs), establishment of drama clubs, general awareness in communities and local schools, conducting Sport for Peace activities and the grand final Talent Show, where the children come together to share lifesaving messages through their artistic skills. Mechanical clearance is including all supporting activities including community liaison and stakeholder engagement to ensure access and community support.
