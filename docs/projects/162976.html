4=Fully in place

6.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

Protection and safety of the targeted community will be taken in to consideration throughout the project implementation period .Priority will be given to the protection and security of the beneficiaries who moved along way access the services. Coordination with community leaders, local authorities will be strengthened to ensure safety, protection and security of all beneficiaries in all targeted areas. Protection and safety of the targeted community will be taken in to consideration throughout the project implementation period . To ensure access and participation of all community groups in the targeted location, women, men, children and people with special needs will be consulted for the selection of sites and type of assistant/services in order to ensure access, safety and security. The project will ensure implementing safe programming approaches by taking in to consideration DO NOT Harm principles and approach by making sure that our interventions do not create any conflict or increase risks to the targeted beneficiaries safety and security. KSCS will continue to improve the protection of targeted beneficiaries by addressing issues related to systematic conflict related violence. Sexual Violence will focus on raising men’s awareness about issues of systematic violence. This will focus heavily upon reducing communities risk from sexual violence, especially women’s. Attention will also be placed upon raising awareness among beneficiaries of the available humanitarian services and how to access assistance and securing access to humanitarian assistance for the most vulnerable protracted IDPs, returnees and residents communities. KSCS will work to ensure the following points are applied during the implementation of the project: • Jointly with the targeted communities analysis the protection risks in the area and working together to identify ways to prevent them to extent possible.• Ensure that the services are accessed by targeted community groups.• Full participation of the targeted beneficiaries on the project’s activity delivery.

7.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will inform and interact with inter-agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including SEA and abuse complaints?

As KSCS is signed in Protection from Sexual Exploitation and abuse of Power /PSEA framework committee as a member as a focal point , KSCS committed to adopt PSEA in all of its programming included the beneficiaries, staff and all partners and stakeholders, in addition KSCS standard MEAL system will promote accountability to beneficiaries, donors and stakeholders. To give community members a voice and contribute to effective dialogue with staff during implementation, KSCS will utilize beneficiary accountability that emphasizes the importance of listening and understanding the needs of beneficiaries. Staff will first be oriented on basic principles and practices of beneficiary accountability and then establish appropriate and culture sensitive feedback and response mechanisms that reflect the preferences of the members of targeted communities. This will build stronger relationships with communities as it also makes projects more effective and adaptive to community needs. KSCS will applies do-no-harm approach in designing and implementing its programs and projects, this is mainly to avoid creating or fueling tension and conflict or undermining any group / communities. In order to do this KSCS, will make all efforts to understand the local context, the different stakeholders and their relationships, communities concerns and social/cultural norms and beliefs. In addition, the KSCS will give attention to people’s dignity and treat them as partners and not charity seekers. In the context of Darfur, culturally women cannot speak out in front of men to tackle this situation a separate space for women will be created to talk and express their views on the provided assistance, their concerns and needs and to share ideas on suitable solutions. While meeting donor requirements for MEAL, KSCS will also ensure good communication to the wider stakeholders by sharing successes and challenges, lessons learned and best practices to demonstrate transparency and foster trust, learning and effective decision making.
