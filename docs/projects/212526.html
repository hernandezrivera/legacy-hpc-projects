Project Summary

The assessment report conducted by SR in Jan 2024 in 18 camps in Aleppo and Idleb showed that due to the recent storm in NWS,there was a difficulty in accessing the tents, and many vehicles get stuck in the mud, most participants emphasized that the condition of the roads significantly impacts children when going to and returning from schools.Additionally, it affects the situation of women when going to the toilets, as the road conditions pose significant challenges for them. In other hand, the lack of drainage leads to a significant buildup of mud in front of all the tents, and sometimes the mud even enters the tents, causing considerable difficulty in movement within the camp. Most participants confirmed that the lack of thermal insulation in the tents means they are very cold in winter and excessively hot in summer. All participants confirmed that the lack of lighting leads to the arrival of stray dogs into the camp, in addition to numerous incidents of theft
Aiming to improve the living conditions for IDPs in NWS, SR designed this project to achieve the following outputs
Output1:Increased satisfaction among beneficiaries with their living conditions.
Output2:Reduce the risk of flooding during winter rains
Output3:Improve safety, security, and protection among beneficiaries in the target camps.
The activities
SR will provide life-saving and life-sustaining shelter support to 18 camps by conducting the following activities
1-Conduction of FGDs in the target camps
SR will conduct FGDs session in each target camp to engage the beneficiaries in the project cycle and increase the ownership of the project, and ensure that women and men can provide inputs, access and participate in project activities. The FGDs will be conducted with different group of people with different ages (male, female, PWD and children)
2-Installation and/ or construction of temporary transitional shelters
3-Construction, rehabilitation, and extension of rain-drainage systems
SR will implement a drainage system in 18 camps targeted under this action
4-Road rehabilitation and gravelling
With heavy clay content soil type in most of NWS camps, unsurfaced roads are not usable by vehicles during the rainy season. In order for essential services to continue (like lifesaving activities currently implemented by SR), major roads will need to have their surfaces reinforced. To do so, SR will gravel the main roads that been used by the lifesaving trucks currently. Additionally, SR will make sure that gravelled pathways and roads which connect all camp locations including WASH facilities are straight and wide enough for access including people carrying large packs, or for people with wheelchairs. The narrow and non-straight roads can be places of risk of attack or harassment, straight and wide paths will also allow women and girls to see ahead and identify any threats at a distance and allow people to walk along together. This is particularly important for women and girls with disabilities, who may need extra space for mobility. SR will target 18 camps under this action
5-Assessment for lightening needs
SR will conduct a security assessment to identify key areas for lighting pole installation.
6-lightening installation and supply and installation of HH level solar energy systems
For the main roads: SR will use solar panels in 18camps
For WASH facilities: SR will use smaller lights which are more appropriate than a single strong light on a high pole in 18 camps
7- Capacity building of individuals/ beneficiaries
7-Establishing CRM for any GBV issues with lighting.
Using of WhatsApp, hot line, emails and directly contacting with the team, SR will establish a CRM and feedback mechanism in order to listen from the beneficiaries and vulnerable people particularly and act according to the feedback.This project will incorporates environmentally measures, such as the use of solar panels for lighting and the implementation of rain-drainage systems, promoting sustainability

SR will work closely with local authority to identify the IDP camp site that are suitable for intervention, Planning and implementing mitigating measures, advocate with local authorities to relocate (voluntary) those living in flood zone to safer areas and map out flood risk locations that will cause further displacement.
