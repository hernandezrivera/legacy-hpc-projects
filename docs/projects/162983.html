Project Summary

Landmine (LM) and Explosive Remnants of War (ERW) pose a significant threat to the safety and security of IDPs, returnees, at risk populations and humanitarian actors in Gissan, and Bau localities in Blue Nile state, Abu Jubaiha, Dellami, Kadugli, Rashad and Talodi locality in South Kordofan state. Women and children are particularly vulnerable. The project aim to reduce the risk posed by LM/ERW, the two Manual Clearance Team (MCT) and Two Multi Tasks Teams (MTT) and 4 NTS teams will release 150,000 Square meters of land and 8 Dangerous/ Suspected Hazardous Areas from Landmine and ERW contaminated land and handover to 30,200 (8210 men, 9010 women, 6500 girls and 6500 boys) IDPs, returnees, at risk population and humanitarian workers in traget localities free of LM/ERW. GAH4 MRE teams through Mine Risk Education will enable (100,000 at risk population through direct MRE session ( 20000 women 30000 boys, 30000 girls & 20000 men) and 100,000 at risk population through MRE radio sessions) at risk population to identify and adopt safety measures that reduce the death and injuries posed by landmines/ ERW in the target Localities . the project will implement in coordination with protection sector/MA sub sector in Khartoum/ Blue Nile, in collaboration with National Mine Action Centre (NMAC) for tasking and technical support Humanitarian Aid Commission (HAC) to facilitate the permits and engagement of men and women’s from affected communities throughout the project life cycle. as well as 100 landmine/ERW victim will get assistance.

1 - Needs

As a legacy & ongoing civil strives in Blue Nile and South Kordofan states, landmine and Explosive Remnants of War (ERW) include all categories of mine, bombs, rockets, artillery and rifle projectiles, mortars, and hand grenades, pose a significant threat to the safety and security of civilians and the provision of humanitarian aid. According to IMSMA November 2019 report a total of 200 civilian has been death & 444 injured in South Kordofan state and 145 has been death &302 injuries in Blue Nile state. 52 % of the causality were children & 48% were adults. 80% of these accidents occurred during victim practicing their daily necessary livelihood activities. A total of 127 registered hazard still open in South Kordofan, 17 registered hazards not close in Blue Nile .Landmines and ERW are considered as significant threat to the safety and security of the local population in the area. Contamination in the areas causes social and development disruption preventing the local population from their normal human activities, little emergency assistance can be provided, and economic development is hindered to take place in the area. Women and children are particularly vulnerable either as direct victims or as part of families who become even further economically, socially and psychologically marginalized by the loss of key income earners and burden of care for accident survivors. The presence of landmine & ERW also poses a huge obstacle and threat to humanitarian actors, are exposed to risks while doing their work necessary humanitarian aid cannot be delivered to the most in need populations and Large numbers of nomadic and displaced people in the two states intensifies the danger posed by the presence of landmines and ERW. As many are nomadic, they rarely learn about local landmine or ERW contamination until it is too late, putting themselves at risk of injury or death. Moreover, this vulnerable population’s need for a safe and stable livelihood underscores the urgent demand for survey and clearance activities to release land, as well as risk education for at-risk populations and victim assistance to landmine/ERW survivors .

6.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

The project staff will comply with GAH Code of conduct, PESA and Child protection plice regarding the respect and saving human dignity of beneficiaries. GAH working towards application of the CHS. Through regular meeting the NTS and MRE teams will ensure the consultation/ participation of the affected communities in prioritizing the clearance and technical survey process, during operations and handover of the land. NTS/MRE teams ensures that women opinions on clearance and technical survey prioritization are heard and valued. the teams will liaise to collect and share information from a wide variety of sources (e.g. communities, local authorities, Ministry staff, other NGOs & INGOS) using meetings, interviews, mapping and other participatory activities. GAH will respond to the feedback provided by communities through the Orientation & consultation meetings and complain and feedback box. GAH will make use of the physical presence and expertise in the fields of GBV, CP and GP in this state to raise the awareness of beneficiaries about the danger thereof. To promote the principle of ‘do no harm’ GAH will ensure that the land handover process is widely publicized and involves women and men, ensure that information about what land is safe to use and what remains contaminated is shared widely. GAH will be explicit that handover documentation does not constitute legal evidence of individual (personal) land ownership. Drawing on experience gained in a range of conflict-affected communities, GAH will utilize their technical expertise and capacity to go beyond clearing mines and ERW to addressing wider threats to security posed, by small arms and light weapons (SALW) and ammunition, according to needs on the ground and direct requests for assistance made by local communities in affected localitiesh

GAH is committed to have a feedback and complaints process which is SAFE, EFFECTIVE and ACCESSIBLE.SAFE-: Any feedback or complaints we receive will be treated confidentially If complainer provide his contact details, it is easier for GAH to process the complaint and respond to complainer directly. You can also be anonymous. GAH will never treat complainer badly for making a complaint.EFFECTIVE: GAH promise to follow a clear procedure once receive a complaint, and feedback to complainer (or the community) within 2 weeks. GAH will try to be faster for urgent issues.ACCESSIBLE- GAH are committed to hear complainer voice, so we offer 3 different ways for complainer to contact us. (1) Phone/ SMS. (2) In writing, placed in a complaints box. (3) Direct to any staff member, but one man and one female staff will come at least each month to listen to you.GAH Procedure for handling the feedback or complaint is:1. All verbal or written or SMS feedback and complaints will be documented, logged in a file, and reported to a selected Complaints Committee. If GAH have complainer name, we will acknowledge the complaint.2- The complaints committee will read and priorities the feedback and complaints.3- Responses will be formulated where possible and checked by head of office, before sending back to complainant or the community. If GAH can’t solve the problem, there will still be a response provided in 2 weeks.
