4 - Needs

According to the HNO 2021, The number of people who require health assistance in Syria is estimated to be 12.4 million. Females make up 51 percent of the PiN. Vulnerable groups include 3.32 million women of reproductive age, 1.41 million children under 5 years old, and 536 000 people aged over 60 who are vulnerable to no communicable diseases; also, the report indicates that only 53 percent of Primary Healthcare Centres (PHC) fully functional. The NHAP Report (Dec. 2020) showed that access to health services is the 3rd by importance priority need among female-headed (36%) and vulnerable households (44%) in NWS. In Syria, disability is estimated at 25 percent of the population, 3.05 million. Also, 536,753 older people are onset NCD, which is estimated to account for 45 percent of all mortality. According to the reposts (HeRAMS);The functioning health facilities have been assessed according to whether the health facility exists prior to the crisis or establishedafter the crisis. 79% of health facilities were newly established, while 21% of health facilities existed prior to the crisis in 2011. ((Kelly PHC is one of the crucial health facility exists prior to the crisis providing comprehensive primary health care)) The pandemic of COVID-19 in NWS increased the emergency needs of IDPs and the host community to essential health services, which are now under greater demand than ever. 5 - Implementation of activities

Mercy-USA will be responsible for implementing the program in coordination with the health cluster and stakeholders. This program is aligned with the 2021 HRP by ensuring access to quality, lifesaving, and life-sustaining health services across the community and primary health system. Mercy-USA will provide high-quality essential health services: trauma services, reproductive health, child health and nutrition services, mental health, diagnostic services, and specialized care for persons with disabilities, those with communicable and non-communicable diseases, and children with severe acute malnutrition with complications. MercyUSA ensures effectively and efficiently delivers comprehensive quality health services that are equitable, accessible, and sustainable, ensuring the timely delivery of cost-effective, integrated, and standardized health services tailored to meet the priority health issues faced by most of the population. The quality and accountability will be monitoring by Mercy-USA to ensure meet the standards; furthermore, Mercy-USA will be responsible for all program and relevant financial report to the donor and the cluster ( 4Ws ) and other related reports. 30 doctors and 60 nurses and 10 Midwives will be trained on infection prevention and control protocol IPC for three days. The program will be monitored and managed by the program coordinator, who will work directly from the Turkey office with the field's staff to ensure the program is being correctly implemented. The selected health facilities will start when the fund is received. The Information Management Officer will work with data entries and our health team to produce monthly information on the program's progress.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Protection /Risk/Threat: Violence against Health Facilities and Healthcare Providers Likelihood: High Impact: High Mitigating Measure: - Applying the designated de-confliction mechanism for health facilities, and increasing advocacy efforts by the health sector with all parties to the conflict to engage them on health protection topics and respect of international humanitarian law (IHL). - Continued reporting on attacks against healthcare. Monitoring: Incidence Report Resources Required: security officer. Protection /Risk/Threat: Gender-Based Violence GBV Likelihood: Low Impact: High Mitigating Measure: - Minimum Initial Service Package (MISP) as part of the essential health package. - Trained specialized health staff on providing first-line support, clinical management of sexual assault and rape, identification of GBV cases, and safe referrals. - Safe referral pathways to ensure comprehensive management of GBV cases connecting health services and clinical management with social services, mental health and psychosocial support (MHPSS), in a manner guarantees safety, confidentiality, and privacy of the survivor. - Recruit female staff in health facilities will increase access of women and girls to health services. Monitoring: Case Management / Reports Resources Required: Train the specialized health staff Protection /Risk/Threat: Sexual Exploitation and Abuse PSEA: Likelihood: Low Impact: High Mitigating Measure: -Mercy-USA maintains a ZERO TOLERANCE policy towards SEA in accordance with internationally-established protocols and standards for SEA protection. Mercy-USA staff, interns and volunteers are trained in SEA protection, child safeguarding with clear protocols on reporting potential SEA and child protection concern
