Project Summary

The goal of this intervention is to improve case management for survivors of gender-based violence by providing direct services. This will be accomplished through collaborating and coordinating with other partners, particularly those working on HLP, child protection, CCCM and shelter interventions in Banadir, Galgaduud, Lower and Middle Shabelle regions. The GBV project will be implemented by IRDO in the IDP camps in Banadir, Galgaduud, Lower, and Middle Shabelle districts. In Banadir region, IRDO plans to expand the current SHF project, which will expire in the next three months. Whereas, in the other locations, IRDO will integrate GBV response into ongoing initiatives such as CCCM, particularly in Afgoye District where IRDO is implementing the SHF.
The project will aim to provide immediate lifesaving assistance to 12000 vulnerable populations (9000 women, 2500 girls, 250 men, 250 boys) by providing access to comprehensive case management and psychosocial support (PSS), which includes emergency care and referral to medical teams trained in clinical rape management (CMR). GBV case workers and PSS will distribute dignity kits, to 4000 GBV survivors (3700 women, 300 girls) and vulnerable women and girls who need basic emotional support and psychological first aid. IRDO will also cover the costs of transportation for approximately 6000 GBV survivors (4500 women, 1500 girls) to get compassionate, competent, and confidential clinical care and follow up on services in all of the project's target regions.
IRDO will offer funds for protection to 4000 (3800 Women, 200 Girls) GBV survivors and other vulnerable women in order to reduce GBV risks and contribute to their safety and dignity of women, girls, and anyone at danger of GBV. Existing internal reports from the ongoing GBV project in Kahda/Deynile has shown the need to provide these funds to many survivors of GBV who are more vulnerable to continued abuse and violation. On the other hand, several recent studies have indicated that cash voucher assistance (CVA) has the potential to improve protection outcomes for women and girls by covering the costs of exiting abusive environment, renting secure housing, or finding temporary refuge which IRDO is currently coordinating with other partners. As a result, addressing basic needs like purchasing food or clothing reduces the possibility of dangerous coping mechanisms like transactional sex or forced marriage or even continued rape in sites where there is no lighting and protection in the IDPs.
IRDO will collaborate with Camp Management Committees, local authorities and other NGO actors to identify and target the beneficiaries in all the target regions. IRDO will also mobilize around 100 PSS counsellors in the target regions to deliver one on one and group psychosocial support and counselling for traumatized women and girls in the IDP camps. The counsellor together with community mobilizers will sensitize the community and provide basic psychological support to the GBV survivors, early or forced marriage and vulnerable girls.
Clinical Management of Rape cases and PSS support will be provided to women and girls in safe spaces to support each other by networking, get information regarding GBV available services; risk reduction through engaging community protection groups and distribution of dignity kits to vulnerable women and girls aimed at preserving their dignity which is essential to maintaining self-esteem and confidence to cope in stressful and potentially overwhelming current humanitarian situation. IRDO will also undertake GBV - Training of duty bearers, medical personnel, and humanitarian staff. IRDO will also provide 12,000 GBV/FGM/Sexual Assaults and rape survivors with key GBV/FGM information on service availability, prevention and mitigation of GBV/FGM/Sexual Assaults and rapes cases. Carry out coordination meetings with other partners so as to disseminate information regarding GBV/PSEA issues to different groups

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

To reduce the risk of being exposed to violence as a result of armed actors' targeted killings or as the result of the distance to and location of assistance/services, IRDO pledges to assist CP AoR in monitoring major violations of children's rights, as well as general human rights violations, in order to provide information to appropriate steakhouses interested in advocacy and peacekeeping. IRDO also plans to involve communities in violence and conflict prevention and mobilization. IRDO also anticipates limiting movement for the safety of obtaining services by spreading service distribution to multiple districts within the region.
