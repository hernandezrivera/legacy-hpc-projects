1 - Activities

Within the IDP sites, dissemination of information to affected population to ensure access to available services. Protection presence and monitoring for timely identification and referral to respective actors including security risks as well as community perception on service satisfaction across clusters. Community engagement at all stages of programming while taking into account age, gender and diversity considerations. Persons with special needs including and not limited to women and girls at risk, elderly, persons with disabilities and chronically ill persons will be prioritized. Collecting and analyzing of protection information to inform programming interventions. In collaboration with the Registrar General, community awareness raising sessions on importance of civil status documentation and schedule mobile registration exercises. To mitigate SEA risks, working closely with the PSEA Network will be maintained to ensure wide dissemination of the newly developed SEA reporting mechanisms. Trainings/sensitization sessions with sectorial committees across clusters and or other and working with Protection Focal points across clusters on identified risks. Trainings on Human Rights, protection legal frameworks, principles and standards will be conducted for local authorities and traditional leaders. Coordination meetings with local authorities, traditional leaders and other opinion leaders will be maintained to facilitate information sharing. With the COVID-19 pandemic, appropriate adaptations of the programme including remotely will be pursued. Service delivery for general protection will include establishing protection presence and monitoring in sites for timely identification and referral , dissemination of information on available services via multiple channels , protection mainstreaming across clusters including and not limited to working with protection focal points across clusters, working closely with PSEA Network on SEA mitigation, community engagement including on community perception on services provided, collecting and analyzing of protection information. Trainings on Human Rights, protection legal frameworks, principles and standards for local authorities and traditional leaders. Coordination meetings with relevant stakeholders and mobile civil status documentation issuance and or replacement following sensitization sessions on importance of civil documentation.

8 - Accountability to Affected People
8.1 - Describe how your project was designed in consultation with communities? How will you incorporate communities in project implementation and what feedback mechanisms do you have in place?

Lack of civil status documentation remains a gap raised by communities and Government. Community engagement will comprise participatory assessments with feedback sessions, Focused Group Discussions and key informant interviews among others. Various leadership structures for women, men, youth will be utilized for communicating with communities. Participation of persons with disabilities will be ensured through out the implementation. Sectorial committees, teachers and local leaders including women, men , youth representatives will be sustained. Leadership structures will play key roles during the information dissemination sessions on civil registration. Community consultations will provide opportunity to receive complaints and in collaboration with other clusters ensure that feedback is provided to the affected populations.
