Project Summary

In referring to emergency needs, TAS seeks to provide emergency needs to help the people in need in different locations in Idleb camps with coordination and collaboration by WASH cluster and camps management in the area.
This project will target 168604 IDPs (30450 HHs ) living in 191 camps in Idleb governorate with basic WASH services, drinking water, solid waste removal, de-sludging latrine septic tanks, water tanks construction latrines, and Hygiene kits distribution. In addition to that, ensure functionality of water supply systems and operationalize of sanitation/sewage and solid waste management systems.
The planned intervention will mainstream protection principles especially (Do no harm) throughout the project cycle by considering the different needs of men, women, young children, children, adolescents, young adults, middle-aged adults, and older adults. The provision of sanitation services will be consulted with the targeted camps and communities and take into account the specific needs of each group. The Project also was designed to provide disabilities, elderly and another vulnerable group with accessible handicap infrastructure too by taking these groups’ needs into consideration during the design phase of the infrastructures, with particular focus on vulnerable people such as ‘People with Special Needs’, in addition, to provide access to proper gender-sensitive and disability WASH facilities
All the activities will be implemented according to SPHERE standards and WASH cluster guidelines, through coordination with camp management and local authorities. Furthermore, all the technical studies (construction latrines) will be prepared as per the Cluster recommendations.

The project will achieve following deliverables:
1- Support water, sanitation/sewage and solid waste management systems to ensure regular services for affected people in Syria. This will be ensured through
- Support to water quality assurance, operation and maintenance of water supply systems
- Repair, rehabilitation and operational support to sanitation/sewage and solid waste management systems
Throughout the implementation, TAS will work on construction of collection tanks in the sites and connect them with water sources by establishment a water supply network from the available sources to the high-water tanks in addition to linking it to the sub-tanks via an earth network in the targeted communities in order to increase sustainability and environmental preservation. The operationalizing will be achieved through emerging solar systems and clean energy to decrease the operational cost and build more sustainability aspects in the targeted communities.
Typical WASH activities will be implemented as well, such as provision drinking water, solid waste removal, de-sludging latrine septic tanks, construction latrines blocks, water tanks, where clean water source is not available, or in-efficiency is foreseen.
TAS will work on construction latrines according to cluster standards related to COVID-19, gender sensitive by building separated blocks, safe accessible, and its doors provided with locks, latrine for PwD with special arrangements, septic tanks will be desludging every 20 days.
2- Deliver humanitarian WASH supplies and services, and improve hygienic behavior and practices of most vulnerable people
To enhance the ability of the beneficiaries to hygiene, especially with condition of COVID19, TAS will distribute hygiene kits for each HH in two rounds and the contents will be according to cluster guidelines. Also, hygiene promotion sessions will be conducted in targeted camps, through trained mobile team about the hygiene, COVID-19 preventions and IEC , taking into account the prevention procedures, social distancing and sterilization.

4 - Needs

Access to sufficient and affordable safe water, adequate sanitation, solid waste management and/or hygiene supplies will remain a real challenge for 1.9 million people living in IDP sites. The WASH infrastructure and service provision in many parts of Syria requires significant repair and operational support [HNO 2021].Takaful Al-Sham carried out a WASH&NFI needs assessment selecting locations according to the gap list from WASH cluster in the governorates of Idleb, and Aleppo at the sub-districts of Dana, Maaret Tamsrin, Jisr-Ash-Shugur, Atareb, Agtrin as 32 camps were visited in order to assess the situation of WASH services within them, it was found that camps are not supplied with water or NFI by any party or organization. More training of Water committees is a need for better Water providing/sharing.
