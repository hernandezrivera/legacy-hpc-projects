Project Summary

Kibarouna is a non –profit organization in Latakia that is licensed by MOSAL since 2011 /resolution # 1009 to provide wide range of humanitarian support to vulnerable groups, Especially for the elderly.
Proposal Summary: The title of the proposal is "Provision of Essential Health Care Services, Mental Health, and Psychosocial Support Services for Population in Latakia Governorate (Slaibeh-Al Haffa)." The objectives include reducing suffering, decreasing morbidities and mortalities rates, raising community health awareness, and improving mental health services and psychosocial support activities.
Outputs: The proposed activities include running static medical centers, medical mobile teams, providing medical consultations, procuring medications, conducting RCCE (Risk Communication and Community Engagement) sessions, supporting diagnostic investigations, ultrasound imaging, advanced medical treatment under secondary care, rehabilitation services, and providing MHPss (Mental Health and Psychosocial Support) services inclusive of tailored vocational training.
Expected Outcomes: The expected outcomes include strengthened access to primary health care services for beneficiaries in Latakia and rural areas, enhanced health promotion and education, boosted advanced medical treatment, reinforced provision of assistive devices for people with disabilities, and strengthened provision of MHPss services at the community level.
Overall, the intervention seeks to improve the health outcomes and well-being of the population in Latakia governorate, particularly targeting vulnerable groups and areas with limited access to healthcare services.
Planned Response:
4.1. Project Rationale & Desired Outcome:
The project aims to address the health needs of displaced persons and vulnerable groups in Latakia, Syria, who lack adequate access to healthcare services. Kibarouna association, seeks to facilitate the delivery of essential health services to these populations. By supporting Kibarouna, WHO intends to ensure timely access to necessary healthcare services for vulnerable groups and IDPs.
4.2. Project Beneficiaries:
The project anticipates providing a total of 29,078 services, benefiting approximately 37,200 individuals. The beneficiaries are categorized based on the IASC humanitarian profile into host community members and IDPs, with specific numbers outlined for men, women, boys, and girls.
4.3. Activities:
The project activities include a comprehensive range of medical services such as lab tests, X-rays, medical consultations, medication procurement, diagnostic investigations, and referral services for surgeries and specialized treatments. Additionally, Risk Communication and Community Engagement (RCCE) activities are planned to raise awareness about public health issues. (MHPss) services are also a significant component, encompassing group interventions, counseling sessions, recreational activities, and awareness sessions.The project will improve primary healthcare via static medical centers and mobile teams, offering medical consultations, diagnostics, and awareness activities. Secondary care will involve referrals to hospitals for advanced treatments, while MHPss services will offer group interventions, counseling, and vocational training. Overall, the project seeks to improve access to essential healthcare services, address mental health and psychosocial needs, and ensure accountability and coordination among stakeholders to effectively serve vulnerable populations in Latakia, Syria.
