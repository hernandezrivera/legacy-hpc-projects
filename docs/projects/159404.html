Project Summary

The proposed project takes into account three basic protection mainstreaming principles. As a method of working CF “Child Smile”: 1. Avoids exposing its beneficiaries to further harm as a result of the interventions (safety and dignity); 2. Puts efforts to ensure people’s access to impartial and non-discriminatory assistance; 3. Strives to assist people to claim their rights, access available remedies and recover from the effects of abuse (empowerment, accountability and participation).
4. Accountability in our work involves recognition of the dignity of the local community, its legal capacity and the possibility of self-determination. Accountability will be implemented through a feedback and complaints system for beneficiaries via telephone and e-mail, and the provision of financial and narrative reports to donors, beneficiaries and others
“Child smile” proposes the project which will combine provision of quality services to most vulnerable groups of women, encourage their participation in public events, support groups of local activists and in such a way increase resilience and preparedness of community to mitigate consequences of the conflict.Activities will cover locations in 0- 5 and 5-20 km one, Donetsk oblast GCA, including but not limited to Avdiivka, Toretsk agglomeration, settlements in Yasynuvatskyi and Bakhmutskyi rayons.

1 - Needs

The Fund was earlier providing comprehensive support to the population of mentioned locations under different projects since 2014. Due to this experience more than 75% of beneficiaries who applied for assistance were women. Among them those single and of pre-pension age (45-59) were found most vulnerable. Most of them stay jobless and are not eligible for any social benefits. In July 2019 the Fund conducted 4 focus group discussions with existing groups of active citizens in previously mentioned locations to identify most important issues. Along with poor work of public transport, lack of equipment and specialists in local hospitals/paramedic points, participants mentioned limited possibilities to find job, lack of community events and regular activities for children and adults, feeling of helplessness and isolation of communities’ members. Participants also found that women usually occupy lower positions in local administrations while they are more active both in state sector and non-government organisations work.

2 - Activities & outcomes

In order to make meaningful input in solution of identified issues “Child Smile” Activities of the project will include:● Comprehensive support of beneficiaries (social and legal counselling) in 2 centres created on the basis of municipal structures (libraries, youth centres) and during visits of mobile groups to remote rural areas. ● Cash for work activity for women who do not receive social benefits temporarily or do not meet the criteria of social services and can’t be provided with state assistance. Exact tasks will be approved together with local authorities in order to support communities affected by the conflict (repair of premises, cleaning etc.). Workload will be identified individually depending on the opportunities of beneficiaries.● Awareness raising sessions on social protection, SGBV, women empowerment, advocacy, legal issues and other topics proposed by the beneficiaries. ● Community based activities planned and held together with initiative groups of local women in order to find solution for most important cases identified by community members.

3 - Indicators & targets

At least 4500 women will receive support of beneficiaries (social and legal counselling) in 2 centres created on the basis of municipal structures (libraries, youth centres) and during visits of mobile groups to remote rural areas. ● Cash for work activity for 600 women who do not receive social benefits temporarily or do not meet the criteria of social services and can’t be provided with state assistance. ● 1500 women will take part in awareness raising sessions on social protection, SGBV, women empowerment, advocacy, legal issues and other topics proposed by the beneficiaries. ● 150 community based activities planned and held together with initiative groups of local women in order to find solution for most important cases identified by community members.
