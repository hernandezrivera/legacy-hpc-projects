Project Summary

This project aims at responding to the critical and the basic needs of 4111 Internally displaced families in the Salqin cluster (Salqin sub-district), with a total number of individuals disaggregated approximately 4,223 men, 4,568 women, 7,071 boys, 8,235 girls, and 185 Person with disability living in Salqin Cluster informal IDP sites as well as camp management structure support for 20 IDP sites in the cluster prioritizing the most vulnerable and highly populated locations.
SKT aims to provide the hard component, care, and maintenance of wash and shelter services, supporting the management structure in each IDP site through trainings and establishment of IDP committees, social units’ volunteers, office facilities, and equipment.
The project includes the care & maintenance of latrine blocks, waste holes, toilets lightning, internal roads, a drainage system, and water tanks)and provision of the camp management office building, facilities, and equipment, and provision of capacity building trainings and awareness-raising sessions for the SUVs, IDP committees, and Camp managers on Camp Management toolkit, awareness on COVID-19 risks, fire safety and security, care, and maintenance and provision of fire extinguishers 6 kg for every 20 tents in informal IDP sites and provision of site management for existing 20 informal IDP sites in Salqin cluster. The PSEA activities will be taken into consideration throughout the project to secure the strategy.
This will include registration of newly arrived IDPs, data collection tent to tent on IDP needs and vulnerability mapping, regular information sharing with respective clusters, awareness-raising on accountability to affected population mechanism (compliant reporting) as well as full coordination ion the multi-sectoral assistance through other sectors partners (food, health services, protection, shelter and non-food items, education, and WASH) and provision of dedicated awareness sessions on COVID-19 risks, prevention, and response measures through the SUVs across all IDP sites to reach 4,111 IDP families in camps in Salqin.

Of these, 80% are women and children, and 45,997 IDPs are reported to be persons with specific needs. 11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

1-Risk: Closure of Border Crossing Points.High Impact. Likelihood: low (activities that need to be driven from Turkey will be finished before the specified date. Mitigation: SKT will ensure the possibility of activities implementation inside Syria through continuous market assessment, informing humanitarian actors, and additionally, to advocate through advocacy channels to continue the response in Northwest Syria. Assumption: No interference from any on-ground existing entity. 2-Risk: Interference from any local administrative or military groups entities in the project implementation. Likelihood: Low. Mitigation: SKT will keep coordinating with local councils, camp management, and IDP committees to keep being neutral in its cross-border humanitarian response. SKT keeps advocating for neutrality and always mobilizes communities to foster humanitarian principles. Assumption: all IDPs will collaborate with the implementing organizations to have a smooth implementation. 3-Risk: civil unrest led by IDPs tension resulting from misunderstanding the whole project objectives. Likelihood: Very Low Mitigation: SKT will stop the activities in the tensioned camp until the tension is over by the interference of camp management, IDP committees, and local councils.
