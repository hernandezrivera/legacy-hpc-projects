Project Summary

The project aims to distribute 100 Food Baskets for "food insecure people" displaced to Al Bab Sub-district (monthly distribution for 12 months), priorities will be given for households displaced recently from Idlib, in addition to specific vulnerability criteria (e.g. HH headed by women, People with special needs ...etc.)
The Selection process will be coordinated with FSL Cluster and Al Bab Local Council, and will start in February 2020, Food baskets will be standardized according to FSL Cluster technical recommendations.

4 - Needs

The project activities will address the following assessed needs:1- Targeted households food consumption score2- Targeted households coping strategy3- Targeted households expenditure on foodprimary needs assessment will be carried out by SOH MEAL team starting January 2020 based on displacement flux to Al Bab City. By the date of this revision (7/3/2020), Seeds of Hope approved the following 3 segments based on comprehensive needs assessment in Al Bab City:1- 50 HHs headed by PWD (people with disabilities or with chronic diseases)2- 25 HHs who are categorized as Food insecure (according to CARI principle) in the area covered by NA3- 25 HHs to be kept for (Just-in-time) monthly evaluation in coordination with Al Bab Local Council The project will be implemented for 12 months.

5 - Implementation of activities

SO 1.2 Provide the assessed food insecure people with monthly food assistance through appropriate modalitiesSeeds of Hope will utilize its logistics capabilities in Al Bab city (wharehouse, transportation, work permit ...etc.) , including group of volunteers who will contribute in project implementation. Quality Control for Food ItemsSeeds of Hope will buy the Food Items from trusted suppliers (who are capable of providing attested health certificates), in addition, samples of the Food items purchased will be taken to third party for analysis, to ensure no toxic material or any other harmful substances do exist in the shipment delivered to support food security of beneficiaries.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Protection Risk/Threat 1: Sale or exchange of Food baskets at lesser value, Likelihood: medium, Impact: medium, Mitigating Measure: Consult community throughout program cycle to determine the reasoning behind the sale/exchange of assistance and solicit their feedback with regards to solutions, Monitoring: Enhance data collection: (i) align PDM tool with global standards; where possible, (ii) collect qualitative data through FGDs with different segments of the community; and (iii) consistently review and address contents of feedback mechanisms. Resources: Staff time to: (i) enhance tools; (ii) conduct FGDs; and (iii) regularly monitor feedback mechanism. Protection Risk/Threat 2: Risk of theft of Food baskets or inputs given, Likelihood: medium, Impact: high, Mitigating Measure: Agencies provide feedback and accountability mechanisms to different groups of users; work closely with all partners on the ground, Monitoring: Agency ﬁeld monitoring and consultation with communities; Proper stock management system in place. Resources: Dedicated staff member and good storage management.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

The project took in consideration the critical situation in Idlib based on the escalation reported in January 2020, therefore, provisional measures were taken to control all activities from redundant oﬃce in Al Bab city in Northern Aleppo in case the situation worsen.
