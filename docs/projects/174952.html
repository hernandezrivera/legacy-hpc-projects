Project Summary

IDEA, being a humanitarian organization having strong knowledge of working experience in health sector with the health department, local communities, Govt, Afghan refugees and partnering donor agencies. IDEA has strong team of health professionals, who has deep experience and knowledge of similar health programs. Currently IDEA has an MoU with the KP Health department to curtail the transmission of COVID-19 and enhance the capacities of the said department. IDEA is implementing three projects in Swat and Mardan District in partnership with KP health department, CARE international, WHH and CONCERN Rapid Fund. The PDMA is also in loop and granted NOC for the smooth implementation of the project activities. IDEA is an active member of provincial health cluster and regularly attending meeting that keeps us updated on current health sector issues and interventions. IDEA would like to synergies the proposed intervention with the other current programs and to contribute to the strategic objective one of the Health Sector in 02 districts of KP, Mardan and Swat in which it will to improve the health status. IDEA has presence in both the districts, where it is implementing health and WASH projects in Covid-19 response and before that in Flood Response in Swat districts. Under the proposed project IDEA will target Rehabilitation of health facilities 20 latrines, 40 hand washing points with ensuring the water supply, provision of 2 ambulance on monthly rental basis for the referrering the COVID-19 positive patients, installation of 4 medical waste incinerators, 120 provision of thermal guns, 400 patients beds for the quarantine centers, 10000 disposable bed sheets for the quarantine centers. IDEA will identify the most vulnerable health facilities and catchment areas in consultation with District Health Officer and District Administration. IDEA team will carry out the technical assessment/feasibility for the provision of the assistance. IDEA will also strengthen the health management committees of the respective health facility and will involve its members in the overall implementation of the project activities. Further to add on this, IDEA will also sign an MOU with the provincial health department for the implementation of the proposed project, it will contribute to the removal of duplication of the efforts. The provincial health department will exclude all items to be provided in the proposed project from the provincial ADP fund. IDEA through these services will cater to 400,000 men and women and children at each district.

1- Project Activities

IDEA for physical assessment of health facilities and subsequent consultations with the District Health Officer. The letter of facilitation will be provided to the assessment team in order to have safe visit.1. Training of the paramedical staff on self-protection:IDEA team in consultation with District Health Department will identify the paramedical staff working in the quarantine centers. The targeted 70 paramedical staff will be trained through qualified consultants on Covid-19, precautionary measures, respiratory methods and proper hand washing and hygiene disinfection practices. Addition this training, this will be made mandatory for the staff to complete the online courses of WHO from time to time. They will also be oriented on accessing to the right and reliable information regarding the COVID 19 to better understand and disseminate in their respective community.2. Health messages through different channels/modes i.e. scooters/vehicles, radio and banners in remote areas: Risks communication, community engagement, and health and hygiene promotion activities play pivotal role in reducing Covid-19 transmission and infection. IDEA will systematically communicate with people to encourage and enable them to understand which activities may result in transmission, promote health behaviors and prevent the spread of COVID. IDEA will Identify and engage key influencers in undertaking risk communication, promotion activities on health. 3. Installation of 80 Hand Washing Points (HWPs): IDEA will support the district administration and health department through short term, effective and quick solution through provision of ready to use Hand Washing Stations with soaps and installation of 100+gl water tanks having base of steel for each tank with 2 taps point and soap holder. The HWP will be installed in DC, AC, Courts, Line department offices, EHSAS cash disbursement and other food packages distribution points in Swat and Mardan. Site will be identified in consultation with TMA and DAs. These stations will be connected to the nearby water facility to ensure availability of water all time. These 80 HWP will be handed over to local community and TMAs for proper operation during the response time. Considering the need of regular hand washing in controlling the speared of virus. 4. Installation of 4 medical waste incineration Points: IDEA will consult the DHO Office Mardan for the installation of incineration points in different health facilities, The incineration point will have the capacity to dispose-off up-to 20kg medical waste one time. The staff of the health facilities will be trained on the standard SOPs of the solid waste in COVID-19. All hazardous waste will be collected daily from all dustbins for safe disposal. In addition to this, single chamber incinerator will be installed in each targeted health facility in order to ensure the safe disposal of wastes through combustion process. Operationalization will be made by relevant health facility.5. 400 Patients beds: The increasing number of COVID-19 suspected and confirmed cases exposes in the country and especial including the proposed district Mardan and Swat that there might be a chance of COVID out-breaks being highly affected in the province. IDEA will provide 400 beds to District Administration Mardan through a tripartite MoU to properly deal and cope with the emergency. It will enhance the capacity of Health department to timely and actively respond to the COVID-19 in District Mardan. Similarly, in District Swat. 6. Referral of COVID-19 Cases to health facilities / Quarantine centers:7. Provision 40,000 mask and 40,000 pairs disposable gloves:8. Provision of 80 Oxygen Cylinder with trolley to health facilities and disposable mask: 9. Provision of 120 Infrared Thermometers.
