Project Summary

This project will enhance the livelihood of the communities living rural areas People both host communities and IDP families encountered problems of living in terms of livelihood specifically areas impacted by floods, climate shocks and droughts, considering number of needs assessments conducted by AYUUB on September 2020, it was however noted that people are high in needs to be saved their lives through enhancing their livelihood opportunities and support of Cash for work programs to be sustain their living. Movements of IDPs became higher than the past years, due to different shocks happened in 2020, As a result, a high proportion of the population that is projected to be Stressed (IPC Phase 2) in October-December 2020 could deteriorate to Crisis (IPC Phase 3) in early 2021. As such, for the HNO planning, the FSC is using an average projection of 3.5 million Somalis to be in Crisis (IPC Phase 3) and Emergency (IPC Phase 4) through 2021”. Conflict and insecurity in Lower Shabelle continue to disrupt productive activities. Over the years, livestock herd size among pastoral and agro-pastoral populations in northern and central Somalia has declined significantly due to the effects of frequent and protracted droughts, leading to increased destitution and displacement in rural areas. Wanlaweyn, Qoyroley and Marka district are the are the areas highly affected by movements of IDP families due to floods, insecurity concerns of the past years, people were highly affected by rainy seasons and they are in need of such kind of livelihood support to sustain their lives, fortunately the current Somali government has put efforts to peace negotiations based on the clan conflicts erupted in the districts, AS attacks and other insecurity situation was changed with the help SNA. Families with children of malnutrition, disable people, aged groups, marginalized communities, women headed families of IDPs sites and all other vulnerable groups will be part of such project. The targeted communicates will get help to improve their farming system and enhance livestock production activities, the number of household in which the project will target is 11205 HH, totaling of 67,230 individuals living to the project locations of Awdheegle, Mark and Qoryooley district, 3735HH in each district. It will strengthen the target communities’ income opportunities through provision of agriculture inputs and capacity building support. It is expected that the beneficiaries will continue the livelihood practices, which directly contribute to increased family economy. Further, AYUUB will continue to play an active role to facilitate and guide to strengthen strong links with government schemes and projects so as to generate the required assistance. The project will promote self-reliant opportunities by following the objectives of the food security cluster by promoting farming systems and access to food. The number of direct beneficiaries of this project would be 11205 HH from the above mentioned districts, 67,230 individuals. AYUUB will set a concrete criteria to select the right beneficiaries who will directly benefit from the beneficiaries, below will be some of the criteria’s to be used for Household from Below Poverty Line, Disabled groups must be prioritized, male and female from poor background who do not have any other alternative livelihoods and female headed households.

Project Activities

- Registration of the beneficiaries, conduct assessment and identification of food and money vendors and conduct constant monitoring of the markets to ensure markets are not destabilized, targeting the poor women led households, elderly men and women, disabled men and women and pregnant and lactating mother - Cash for Work activities will be identified in consultation with participants to ensure nature of work is selected in consideration of specific gender needs & cultural practices for women in particular Local authorities will be consulted as well as the relevant parts and actors, bush clearing activities will under such CFW, 1000 HHs from each district will be targeted which counts 3000 household, IDPs youth group both girls and boys will be part such activities to rehabilitate the infrastructures of the towns and support people with CWF - Protect and restore livelihoods, related food and income sources, through provision of seasonally appropriate livelihood inputs and tools including rakes, hoes, wheelbarrow, watering can and other important tools for cultivation and the technical support in the three districts under the project Location for 5000 HHs who do farm cultivations - Provision of farms seeds (sesame, maize and vegetable seeds) to the beneficiaries: Household directly benefitting the project will have support of quality seed to restore the families nutrition while cultivating their farms and AYUUB will help them to practice proper farming system in terms of high production and sustainability - Income generation activities for vulnerable groups/ youth and improving immediate access to food through conducting agricultural trainings - Support rehabilitation and/or restoration of household and community productive assets and capacity to build resilience to withstand future shocks and prevent further deterioration. Canal and water catchment rehabilitation to facilitate agriculture activities to fight against the floods impact - Gardening support: Training to be given to 1000 on family garden to enhance their household level income in association with agricultural and horticultural for their house level, garden crops will be different and vegetable crop seeds will be provided to the beneficiaries to help children to have nutritious
