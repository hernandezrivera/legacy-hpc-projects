Project Summary

Terre des Hommes aims to pursue community-based protection goals for the benefit of displaced, migrants and returnee populations as well as affected host communities in West and South of Libya. The project aims to reduce vulnerabilities, enhance protection and reinforce the resilience of the populations of concern to cope with the effects of the current crisis, through protection as well as cash assistance and health support for PWDs.

Needs

Problem 1: GBV is real present risk and quality of response is inadequate or needs to be maintained Multiple displacements, growing poverty and limited access to services have resulted in an increased level of GBV, while significant challenges remain to ensure that GBV specialized services are widely available. Problem 2: Children face specific protection risks As a result of the crisis, children and adolescents face specific protection risks, including separation from their families, child labour, school drop-out, early marriage, kidnapping, sexual exploitation, trafficking, abuse, neglect and recruitment into armed groups. Problem 3: Services for person with specific needs are insufficientPsychological distress caused by the crisis remains a significant concern among the various communities, in particular among vulnerable groups who have lost their family and social support networks. In addition, persons with disabilities and older persons are particularly affected by the crisis and require targeted support Problem 4: Community mobilization needs regular reinforcement:The displacement caused by the crisis has resulted in weakened community protection mechanisms, which exposes in particular the most vulnerable segments of the population to increased protection risks.

Activities

1) Awareness raising campaigns addressing different types of violence including GBV; 2) community-based women’s committees will be established to strengthen women’s involvement and participation in decision-making;3) Conducting training courses for national NGO staff and members of Communities Committees;4) Awareness raising on Child Protection;5) Standard case management;6) Establishment of Community-based child protection structures;7) Services for persons with specific needs;8 ) In-kind assistance and medical equipment provided; 9) Structured and sustained PSS, and social recreational activities provided;10) Home-based care for elderly and children with disabilities;11) MHPSS referral to specialized mental health/psychosocial supported and services provided if available;12) Mobile units established/supported;13) Community-Based Initiative. Community-based protection services and other activities delivered through the establishment of mobile teams and outreach volunteers, Provision of targeted individual material assistance to persons with specific needs, to address specific protection issues/concerns as identified through protection monitoring/casework, Provision of PFA support and structures PSS support.TdH Italy will work in close coordination with Child Protection and GBV Working Groups

Access, safety and dignity of beneficiaries

TDH Italy has a strong expertise in the region in Protection and Health.The national staff will receive specialize training in Protection and inclusion of People with Disabilities from TDH regional Protection adviser. TDH coordinators, identified for each location, will provide monthly reports to the programme manager. The technical protection adviser will monitor the Protection competence of the field staff. Regional office and HQ will provide continuously technical support and will supervise the intervention. Successful strategies implemented already in Syria and Iraq will be re adapted to the Libyan context. This project will require collaboration from different parties, including the local council, local NGOs and beneficiaries themselves. The targeted communities from the south (Murzuq, Sebha and Ubari) will be coordinated through the southern local councils who will provide access and authorization for Tdh Italy in order to implement the activities. TDH Italy is aware of the general difficulties faced by international actors in accessing and in providing services in the South of Libya. However, TDH It planned to reach those areas addressing principally CP needs, through the potential collaboration with INTALAQ, a local network of volunteers very active in the whole territory of Libya. The project in Tripoli area and Sirt will be coordinated with the Tripoli’s authorities and in collaborations with other agencies. TDH It will participate to the relevant working sectors (CP, SGBV, GP, Health, Cash assistance, etc, in order to avoid duplication and to do the most efficient intervention, in line with sector standards. TDH IT promotes PSEA among its own personnel, and the target population it works with. Personnel has ethical obligations placed upon them, have to respect the Code of Conduct and internal disciplinary procedures.
