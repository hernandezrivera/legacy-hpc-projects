Project Summary

This project will positively contribute to provision of equitable access to learning opportunities for all children of Tonj North County to redress the past inequalities in education due to conflict.
Through the provision of emergency education services the project aim to mobilize all the children in Tonj North and Morobo Counties for back to learning program , in Addition the construction of the Temporary learning spaces and rehabilitation of the destroyed schools in the se Counties meaning the education program will resume for both IDPs , returnees and host community children . The project will support the training of teachers on pedagogy methods to improve their teaching skills
WAO will contribute to increased number of qualified teachers by making sure that teachers are trained and hired due to attrition meeting the minimum qualifications . Short pedagogy training shall be developed to ensure that volunteer teachers are trained.
Given the level conflict, many people including school children are displace and are not in school. Peace building programmes shall be implemented to improve the peaceful co-existence between communities
Furthermore, the inter –Agency assessment agencies conducted for Tonj North and morobo Counties reveals that many children are out of school and many of them are lacking learns materials to improve their education .
Many teachers who are recruited in teaching profession require teachers training since are only but volunteers with no teaching knowledge and skills which this project shall increase to improve the quality of teaching force in the project targeted counties .
Provide and train both children and teachers on psychosocial support services(PSS)
The project takes into consideration the distinct need of female and male particularly the protection concerns.
In relation to water access women and girls in particular are at high risk
The project shall provide children with quality education who are both internally displaced and from host communities .Most of the learning centers / schools lack both teaching and learning materials which in this project will arrange for transportation and distribution to Schools.
On Sanitation and hygiene , latrines and bathing shelters will be constructed in schools which shall be gender segregated and equipped with privacy screens to reduce the risk of gender based violence and ensure privacy

1 - Needs

Morobo County has a population of 103,603 has five payams ie Gulumbi which has 31523 persons, Kumba 31.767, Lujulo 16,786 persons, Payume 11.414 persons and Wudabi 12113 individuals respectively according to South Sudan population census of 2008 with children who need education services since many returnees and host communities will be seeking education facilities which are missing With the 2013, conflict, Morobo was safe and the fighting did not reach there. People remained peaceful doing their economic and social work. Until 2016 conflict which originated in Juba and spread to other places including Morobo County which destroyed all the education structures including learning environment. Currently, the refugees from Uganda and DRC-ongo are returning back home after signing of the Revitalized Agreement on the resolution of conflict in South Sudan (R-TGoNU) ) and the formation of Unity government . Insecurity has been looking at the border of the DRC-Congo where returnees settled resulting into influx to Morobo accommodated in churches, schools and under trees with no humanitarian assistance. There is need to rehabilitated the learning environment to accommodate both the returnees and the host communities The risks of COVID19 remain high among the returnees and the host communities which has made schools to be closed making children miss education . No awareness raising and sensitization of the communities about the pandemic- Awareness raising about the prevention of covid19 in schools and in communities is required. Both host communities and return children need education where they have no proper learning environment, lack of qualified teachers, teaching and learning materials are missing. 98% of educations supporting structures have been destroyed, pre-primary, Primary and secondary schools. There is only one school in Morobo town with no qualified teachers having 3 male and 3 female respectively. The rest of the four Payams of Morobo County have no functional school at the moment. Schools in Lujulo and Wudabi have been completely destroyed and abandoned by the conflict. Children fear to move to nearby and far schools due to insecurity in the County. Water points in schools have been destroyed and therefore, when children open schools may face a big problem of water. Children are malnourished since most of them depend on school feeding programme supported by WFP is no longer there. Based on the inter agency assessment report conducted in August 2020, in Tonj North County in Warrap state confirms that there was population displacements caused by the inter -communal conflict which erupted in May 2020 between communities of Atok and Awan, which intensified when other communities joined like Apuk and Lou Mawien and later on threatened to include all the communities in Tonj North. Most the affected payams by the conflict in Tonj North County were Awul, Rualbet and Kirik with the total population displacement of (3,865)HH (Awul 2201 HH with the total population of 13,202 persons 6,337 male and 6,865 female , Rualbet 932 HH with the total population of 5,592 persons 2684 male and 2908 female and Kirik Payam 732 HH with a total population of 4392 IDPs 2108 male and 2284 female , Several people lost their lives . Schools are destroyed by the conflict in the mentioned locations. There is lack of qualified teachers, water and sanitation facilities disaggregated by gender are missing in the learning centers. Learning and teaching materials were destroyed in the conflict while Parents are not willing to take their children to school but rather look after cattle. As we plan to conduct the back to school campaign, the children will have space to sit and learn in Tonj North. Some locations are not accessed during the rainy season in the county.

12.1.4. Quality, timely and inclusive protection services (including medical care, legal support, safety and mental health psychosocial services to children, women at risk and GBV survivors

This project has an element of Mental health and psychosocial support (MPSS) delivery through capacity building of both teachers and children in schools. Counselling both individual and group s will be delivered to traumatized and stressed teachers and children in school to provide better psychological well being to them. Gender based violence and other protection concerns shall also b addressed by providing legal assistance to those women and girls at risk for have litigation services . Not only that, the majority of the GBV survivors shall be given referral pathway -for further humanitarian assistance to be accorded to them with out discrimination .

12.1.2. Decreased proportion of populations facing IPC 3,4 and 5

The project will deliver services which will contribute to reduction of food insecurity where the locations of the project directly fall under IPC3,4& .School gardening programme initiation in the schools will aim at improving the food security at the household level . School children shall be distributed with Seeds, tools and trained conservation Agriculture to be practiced at their home villages as consequently they will all contribute to improved food security and livelihood in their communities .

12.1.3. Equitable access to cross-sectoral basic services to vulnerable populations (including women, children, the elderly, persons with disability and older persons)

Gender Mainstreaming: Gender is addressed through different stages: acquiring an understanding of the roles and responsibilities of women in relation to household food security and livelihoods; investigating opportunities to increase their access & control over resources are central to this project. An analysis of the role of women in food security in Tonj North and Morobo reveals that women will perceive to have the primary responsibility for food sourcing, storage and rationing as well as engaging in multiple livelihoods activities like petty trade. This analysis is used in this project design to inform targeting and implementation strategies that encourage maximum impact on improving status of women in society.In November 20202 5.3 million people, or nearly half of the population, were already struggling to find enough food each day and were in "crisis" or "emergency" levels of food insecurity (IPC Phases 3 , 4)and 5 ) , according to an Integrated Food Security Phase Classification (IPC) report. all people require equitable distribution of resources and other humanitarian assistance with out discrimination and segregation based on gender, ethnicity and age since all are facing similar needs

12.1.1. Decreased prevalence of global acute malnutrition among children under age 5 in severity levels 3 and 4

The project will asist the school feeding programmes which shall be implemented in various identified schools in the two counties of Tonj North and Morobo targeting children who are malnourished with severe global acute malnutrition indicators . Therefore, the project will implement some of the activities that yield in reduction of global acute malnutrition indicators for example, construction of kitchen , store, for keeping food rations to be cooked for Children meals
