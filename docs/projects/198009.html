Project Summary

PROJECT DESCRIPTION :
The on-going “Protracted Armed Conflict” in the North West/South West (NWSW) Regions of Cameroon which break out since 2016/recent out break of Cholera has resulted in increased displacements of people/deaths.
OCHA sitrep_nwsw_august_and_september_2022_final data : 2.0 M People in need of assistance ; 1.4 M Targeted for assistance ; 600,336 IDP ; 419,734 Returnees (former IDPs and refugees) ; 85,967 Cameroonian refugees in Nigeria. Sources : Humanitarian (Needs Overview/Response Plan) 2022 ; MSNA NWSW, OCHA, August 2022 ; MSNA West, Littoral, OCHA, September 2021 & UNHCR, September 2022.

DEMOGRAPHIC INFORMATION : 05 Subdivisions of 20 communities in rural, urban/semi-urban in Nkambe, Ndu, Ako, Batibo, Bamenda, Wum/Mamfe in 5 Divisions (Donga Mantung, Momo, Mezam, Menchum/Manyu) in the NWSW Regions of Cameroon. Target: 10,000 (dissag. by gender, age, disability of (1,712 Men/1,737 Girls) <18 yrs, (1,012 Men/1,112 Women) > 18 yrs, (1,590 Men/1,612 Women) +60yrs, PWD (600 Men/625 Women)). Targeting 28 Schools.
AIM:
To improve access to learning environment with basic sanitation facilities, hgyiene services and learning material to vulnerable IDPs by 30th June 2023.
Affected children needs an emergency humanitarian assistance for live-saving/alleviate suffering/have the right to (life, dignity, protection, security/to receive humanitarian assistance on the basis of need without discrimination), avoid exposing them to further harm/leave no one behind.
INDICATORS :
-# of students, parents, teachers, supervisors & other Education staff sensitized on COVID-19 prevention
-# of latrines built
-# of girls, boys & adolescents affected by crisis who received learning materials
These will ensure children education/address the physical/mental well-being/living standards of these IDPs.
ACTIVITIES:
-Community awareness of the prevention of COVID-19
-Construction of school and training infrastructure (classrooms, latrines, water points, temporary learning spaces)).
-Purchase & distribution of learning materials (bags, books, pens, pencils, etc.)
OBJECTIVES:
-Schools/education services serve as a platform for accessing basic Water, Sanitation, hygiene/health promotion for crisis affected children/communities.
-Vulnerable children/teachers in schools/communities access basic teaching/learning materials for improved quality EIE by 30th June 2023.
-Reduce mortality by 75% /morbidity by 75% of people affected by crisis.
BENEFICIARIES:
Affected population (IDPs, returnee’s/host communities) of Infants, Boys, Girls, Men, Women, Elderly people/PLWDs of Gender, Age, Disability/Diversity mainstreaming i.e. 50% IDPs, 10% PWDs, 30% host communities/10% Returnees.
METHODOLOGY: Sensitisations.
STRATEGY OF IMPLEMENTATION:
Through community based approach, awareness raising campaign (school-to-school/door-to-door sensitization), radio talks, standard visibility/IEC materials, collaboration with stakeholders, community’s mobilisation/engagement/inclusively school children,. Open space distribution/homes delivery, organized workshops. IDPs/the communities are consulted/participate in the conception of the entire project cycle as decision-makers/local management/ownership. Appropriateness, relevance/feasible. Rapid HH Survey, FGDs, KIIs, field visist/report from communities, AAP/PSEA. Engage MINEDU, MINNE, MINSANT, Municipal Council/advocacy actions.
SUSTAINABILITY:
Trained (CPCs for the continuity of sensitisaton on health and hygiene promotion (HHP); schools administration on how to treat/reuse squatting slabs once the pits are full). Installation of HWFs/preventing COVID-19 is medical needs for wider population. Improve sustainable latrines and ensuring education continuity with learning materials.
JUSTIFICATION:
Latrines reduce GBV cases that are latrines related. Marginalized/vulnerable groups of children are not assisted with learning materials/schools fees.
