Project Summary

MWL proposed project seeks to protect children through establishing community center in Maart An Numan that will provision full child protection specialized services including:1-Structured PSS to the children(B&G) and youth (M&F) through using "l Deal" and “Parenting programme” curriculum for adults to develop their social skills such as cultural and recreation skills 2-Document and provide services for child survivals and at risk cases 3-Providing emergency cases with one - off cash assistant to enable immediate response when it is needed 4-Provision Structured child protection awareness raising campaigns that will covering CP issues (child labor,Family separation,Risk of motorcycles and ERW) through 4 outreach teams 5-Establishing and training 2 Community Child Protection Networks (CPNs) in order to respond to violations affecting children. CPNs will be composed of youth and adults (M&F) volunteering to protect children in their communities 6-Empowering and integrating of PWDs with the community through specialized activities including psychological rehabilitation, and provision PSS activities, life skills, non-formal education, and awareness raising sessions about their rights within the community 7-empowering youth and adolescents in Provision life skills, Psycho-social Support,training bout selection the professional & academic track, planning for success in academic, entrepreneurship skills, sport exercising and inspire them to launch volunteering social initiatives serve their communities 8-Developing contextually relevant (soft - teamwork - volunteering skills) curricula for youth engagement and empowerment based on assessment findings 9-Train NGOs wishing to provide these programs on the developed curricula and conducting mainstreaming workshops with NGOs from other sectors.

Needs

Idelb is home to some two million civilians, about half of whom are displaced, as a result, there have been significant levels of high displacement from Eastern Ghouta, Northern Homs and Hama, and Southern Damascus.Since November 2017, hostilities between Government of Syria (GoS) forces and non-state armed groups (NSAGs) have escalated significantly. In addition to the ongoing armed clashes, aerial and ground-based bombardment was reported on several communities away from the frontlines. This bombardment resulted in destruction and potential explosive hazard contamination of civilian infrastructure, including schools, medical facilities, local markets, housing, IDP settlements, and have disrupted humanitarian activities.The prolonged exposure to war, stress and uncertainty, means that many children are in a state of psychological distress. Findings from the 2018 HNO data collection exercises found 82% of 528 assessed communities reported child labor preventing school attendance.In May 2018, MWL collaborated with the local councils in the project areas to do a needs assessment. High level of psycho social distress among children (B&G) and youth (M&F) due to traumatic experiences, as well as extraordinary smoking practice and violence were among the key findings. Some deaths due to ERW-related causes were also reported. The selected communities have large number of IDPs from Eastern Ghouta As the GoS and allies advanced on Eastern Ghouta in February and March 2018, children and their families lived under ground for weeks without seeing sunlight. They lived with hunger because of the siege as food prices increased due to scarcity. Bed-wetting, aggressive behaviors panic is on their faces with their care givers.MWL Proposed project will targeting Almaa'ra District, in Idlib province, where the displaced people have evacuated from Eastern Ghouta, Northern Homs and Hama, and Southern Damascus.According to the baseline that conducted by MWL in collaboration with the local council in the selected areas, the finding as the following: Total Catchment Population of 13 communities that targeted in Al Ma'ra District, Idleb 277730 person (M 52275, W 98745, B 56175, G 70535) including 34% IDPs.#of Orphan Children 5948 (B 2700, G 3248)#of Children with Disability 891 (B 462, G 429)#of Children out of school 5022 (B 2428, G 2594)#of Children headed house-hold 2381 (B 2219, G 162)#of women Widow (3221)#of Women with Disability (160)#of women headed house-hold (1770)#of women without profession (21760)#of women non-educated (18722)#of Jobless men (17306)#of Men with disability (6420)MWL has selected the Al Mar'a in coordination and consultation with the Child Protection and Mine Action coordinators to ensure that prevent overlapping and select the most need locations.

Implementation of activities

MWL proposed project seeks to protect children through establishing community center that will provision full child protection specialized services including:1-Structured PSS to the children(B&G) and youth (M&F) through using "l Deal" and “Parenting programme” curriculum for adults to develop their social skills such as cultural and recreation skills 2-Document and provide services for child survivals and at risk cases 3-Providing emergency cases with one - off cash assistant to enable immediate response when it is needed 4-Provision Structured child protection awareness raising campaigns that will covering CP issues (child labor,Family separation,Risk of motorcycles and ERW) through 4 outreach teams 5-Establishing and training 2 Community Child Protection Networks (CPNs) in order to respond to violations affecting children. CPNs will be composed of youth and adults (M&F) volunteering to protect children in their communities 6-Empowering and integrating of PWDs with the community through specialized activities including psychological rehabilitation, and provision PSS activities, life skills, non-formal education, and awareness raising sessions about their rights within the community.The project monitoring and evaluation systems will be predicated on the project log-frame and M&E plan focusing specifically on indicator measurement and reporting. Project M&E framework which will include targets based performance indicator tracking table and M&E plan will be developed.At project inception, a light touch joint pre-implementation assessment will be conducted to refine and finalize overall project targets.Regular site visits will be conducted by the M&E Field Officer for verification and documentation of project implementation progress, including routine meetings with key informants at local level to generate qualitative data on the effectiveness of project interventions.MWL M&E team will be responsible to follow-up the partner implementation and observing the quality of activities.Depending on beneficiary consent, visual evidence that include photos and videos of project activities will be documented. Exit interview questionnaires will be administered to measure the satisfaction of beneficiaries with services being delivered at the center. Monitoring data will be shared routinely with project management for informed decision making and necessary adjustments.
