Project Summary

Norwegian Church Aid (NCA) demonstrates unwavering commitment and active engagement in providing vital emergency support, particularly focusing on Shelter and Non-Food Items (NFI) within South Sudan's Aweil East, Baliet, Fashoda, Longchuk, Nasir, Maiwut, and Manyo counties of South Sudan. Our response strategy is founded on our extensive experience in emergency interventions and our ongoing efforts in NFI, Multi-purpose Cash, and Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene (WASH) programs. With this project, NCA's targeted goal is to assist approximately 45,000 individuals, specifically aiming to reach 20,000 males and 25,000 females within these target areas. Our strategic project focuses on cash voucher assistance, the distribution of shelter materials, and household non-food items, integrating these interventions within our broader emergency response initiatives. These efforts are part of NCA's holistic approach, intertwining Gender-Based Violence prevention and WASH programs within South Sudan. By implementing these strategic interventions, our project seeks not only to provide immediate relief but also to instill resilience and contribute to the long-term recovery of communities grappling with these crises. NCA's approach remains guided by the urgency of the situation, ensuring that our interventions are precisely tailored to address the most pressing needs of vulnerable individuals and families. In doing so, we aspire to kindle hope and actively participate in rebuilding lives amid the ongoing crisis in South Sudan.

1. Needs

South Sudan grapples with an unparalleled humanitarian crisis, compounded by persistent conflict and the harsh impacts of climate change. This dire situation has led to approximately 5.83 million people, nearly half the population, facing acute food insecurity, classified as IPC Phase 3 or higher (Crisis or worse) between September and November 2023. Adding to the distress, 1.64 million individuals are in IPC Phase 4 (Emergency), exacerbating the dire circumstances faced by many South Sudanese amidst this ongoing crisis. In the midst of this, Norwegian Church Aid (NCA) is resolutely committed to addressing critical needs, particularly focusing on Shelter and Non-Food Items (NFI) within three pivotal states: Northern Bahr El Ghazal, Upper Nile, and Warrap. Our approach is anchored in extensive experience in emergency interventions, bolstered by ongoing efforts in NFI, Multi-purpose Cash, and Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene (WASH) programs. NCA's targeted goal, within these states, is to assist approximately 45,000 individuals, aiming to reach 20,000 males and 25,000 females. Our strategic project concentrates on cash voucher assistance, shelter material distribution, and household non-food items, all integrated within our broader emergency response initiatives. This crisis is amplified by the influx of displaced individuals, with more than 1 million people fleeing the Sudan conflict to neighboring countries. In South Sudan, as of November 7th, 364,818 individuals have sought refuge, primarily South Sudanese returnees (83%) and Sudanese refugees (16%). The overwhelming majority arrived at specific entry points, with Wunthow/Joda in Upper Nile receiving 83% of arrivals, followed by Panakuach in Unity at 16%, and Kir Adem in Northern Bahr el Ghazal at 1%. The influx, concentrated at entry points, strains limited resources, and poses security risks due to insufficient border control. Most displaced individuals reside in overcrowded camps, enduring unsanitary conditions and insufficient access to essentials like food, clean water, and medical care. This surge in refugees amplifies existing security and stability concerns in South Sudan. Forecasts predict a significant rise in refugee numbers in the coming weeks. NCA's rapid multi-sectoral assessment revealed challenges faced by returnees, especially in securing proper shelter and household essentials. Shockingly, 90% of respondents reported shelters lacking adequate protection from rain and proper walls, while 85% lacked kitchen materials for cooking. Returnees and refugees express a strong preference for cash assistance, followed by in-kind support. NCA responds actively to this crisis by providing Emergency Shelter and Non-Food Items (ES/NFI) to affected refugees and returnees, addressing immediate needs in these vulnerable areas.

2. Project activities

The proposed project has a target of reaching 45,000 individuals, strategically aiming for inclusivity and efficient aid allocation. Utilizing NCA's Emergency Response & Preparedness Unit (EPRU), the project specifically intends to assist 30% women, 20% men, 25% boys, and 25% girls across the impacted regions. It's imperative to highlight that certain beneficiaries will benefit from a range of project activities. This underscores NCA's comprehensive approach, acknowledging the multifaceted needs of vulnerable populations. Such an approach ensures a holistic response, recognizing that individuals may require support across various domains to stabilize their situations effectively. Key activities proposed within the project include initial assessments, thorough verifications, the distribution of Non-Food Items (NFIs), emergency shelter provisions, and importantly, the integration of multi-purpose cash provisions. Additionally, the project emphasizes the significance of offering training opportunities, acknowledging the value of capacity-building initiatives in empowering communities to navigate crises and facilitate effective recovery. Integral to the project's success is the collaboration and joint coordination with essential stakeholders, notably UNHCR and other humanitarian entities operating in South Sudan. This collaboration aims to streamline efforts and guarantee that vulnerable populations, particularly women, children, and individuals with special needs, receive timely and adequate quantities of high-quality NFIs and multi-purpose cash. Through strategic partnerships and coordination, NCA aims to optimize aid delivery efficiency and maximize positive impacts on those in need. The project's approach is not only focused on immediate assistance but also emphasizes the importance of tailored support, acknowledging the distinct vulnerabilities and needs within various segments of the affected communities. Through these concerted efforts, NCA endeavors to provide essential aid while promoting resilience and empowerment among vulnerable populations amidst South Sudan's ongoing crisis.
