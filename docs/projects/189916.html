1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Project proposal aims at reinforcing GBV protection services in the most affected are by conflicts and then desplacements in Capo Delgado Province in favor of around 16.000 persons out of around 3,6 million on People in Need. In line with the objectifs of the Protection Clusters and AOR, the project aims at:- Strengthen mental and psychosocial support (MHPSS) services and case management for survivors;- Reduce the Risk of GBV and improve quality of response for survivors; Objectives, the project will rely on the community protection-based approach, in favor to IDPs and host communities and will be implemented in strict cooperation with the local authorities. Activities will respect and use the approach described above, making use of AVSI specific methodologies: procedure manuals, ethical codes, tools, SOPs, PSEAH and Child Protection Safeguarding. Building up on AVSI solid and multisectorial presence in the area, (CCCM,WASH,Nutrition,Education,Shelter/NFI,Energy) which would allow an area based and multi-purpose response.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The project will ensure protection mainstreaming process in order to esure DO NO HARM approach, promoting meaningful access, safety and dignigy in all actions. In order to guarantee this principles all the staff will be trained on the principles and procedures in place and will sign the reference documents such as the Internal Code of Conduct, the Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse Policy (PSEA) and the AVSI Children Safeguarding Policy documents as well as the AVSI lead protection mainstreaming checklists. In order to avoid conflicts between IDPs (new or extended) and host communities, targeting for individual assistence will be done in consultation with the communities, defining together the criteria of vulnerability and ensuring that all the different groups are represented in the consultations. This will also ensure the use of the principles of impartiality and neutrality: no distinction of nationality, race, religion, social condition and political affiliation will be made and no sides will be taken in any dispute. Protection monitoring, as part of the activities, will be garuatee through routinely monitoring access to services by women, girls, boys and men through spot checks and discussions with communities, and promptly address the obstacles to equal access

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

This project will follow AVSI Foundation SOP described in the AVSI FOUNDATION ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY on ‘feedback and accountability mechanism’. Newly recruited and old staff are always oriented and re-oriented on the need to seek the views of communities concerning program implementation. Beneficiaries and the population at large is also made aware of the forms available to provide suggestions and complaints. This project will adopt a community-based and participatory approach. The selection criteria will be developed in consultation with the communities and will aim to upgrade living-conditions within a settlement and when possible through the construction of new shelters. AVSI will conduct protection assessments in KOBO according to the agreed criteria, which will be reviewed and verified by the local authorities and the representatives of the communities. In AVSI’s experience, this has proven time and again to be particularly productive, as not only people’ concerns and priorities are taken into account.Example of good practices:https://assessments.hpc.tools/assessment/c891c32a-7c60-4aa9-bf6d-a8b48a84730aActivities of MEAL and surveys will be utilized not only for informing program strategies, but also to devise possible and feasible way forward with local communities as per cases described below: https://assessments.hpc.tools/assessment/cded105d-be61-4480-b95c-117ea9fb4013

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

In the present project proposal screening, surveillance and participation of people with disabilities have been included as key activities and indicators.During their implementation, the detection of people and children with disabilities will be reported (disaggregated by gender and age) and given special attention. This translates into prioritizing the abovementioned target population as to the assistance for Shelter, NFI and Protection, especially for those cases having motor disabilities to which AVSI Foundation will provide logistics support.AVSI Foundation will, when possible, organize specific studies on the inclusion and conditions of persons living with a disability and will utilize such studies to devise inclusive strategies on round table with PwD as well as local communities. Examples of such work in similar context can be found at: https://assessments.hpc.tools/assessment/e3f17126-d1f5-4a78-80a4-99dea7c46e69AVSI Foundation will ensure the implementation of sensitization sessions throughout its activities to ensure that these people are supported within the community and given special attention when coordinating and planning activities with local SDSMAS and partners

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

AVSI aims to promote a sustainable and healthy environment in order to improve the wellbeing of the community. Given the experience of AVSI Mozambique in energy effciency-related projects, method to save charcoal and wood during cooking demonstration will be also promoted so that to sensitize the target population on preventing deforestation that in turn impact positively on family finances by saving wood and charcoal. Sensitization activities will include specific thematics on protection of environment, climate change risk and prevention mesures. Recreational activities with children could also include thres plantations in order tu favorite reforestation. In addition of that, it will be prioritazed the use of local materials for construction for Child Friendly Spaces building in order to adopt an environment-friendly approach by trying to increase awareness in the affected communities.AVSI can rely to an internal Policy on environment on May 11th 2020.
