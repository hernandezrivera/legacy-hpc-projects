Project Summary

this project is aimed to provide child-friendly space in 10 locations .for Afrin, the western countryside of Aleppo in addition to the western countryside.
on the other hand, it will support the infant and young child needs by conducting campaigns all the year divided into 4 rounds.
also will be establishing a team of community health workers network.
these CHW community health workers will be touring in the targeted communities providing awareness for the essential package for the community health workers guidelines. Engaging all community, beneficiaries in designing and implementing all activities and make sure to engage them in the monitoring and evaluation process.
The target areas of this project are Aleppo governorate ( A'zaz, Jarablus, Albab, Al-Atareb), as well as Idleb governorate ( Saraqib and Idlib city).
The proposed activities are as following;
1- Implement IYCF campaign in 300 community for 4 rounds.
2- Run 6 IYCF and nutrtional mother and children friendly tents in IDPs camp
The propsoed project is a continuation of already ongoing activities.

Needs

The project is aimed to ensure access of all children and women to the comprehensive quality CMAM services, disseminate and support C-IYCF practices and promote health and nutrition seeking and basic hygienic behaviors within the population in need. Also, the project is going to continue providing harmonized emergency community-based services for all children and women of the population in need by applying the integrated health, nutrition, child protection, and WASH approaches.

Implementation of activities

CFS activities: Child/Mother-Baby-Friendly Spaces are fully functional and accessible to all children and women.1- Provision of key IYCF messages/information 2-Meeting basic needs 3-Psychosocial support (integrate with MHPSS services) 4-Provision of cups/promotion of feeding (for artificial feeding and complementary feeding) 5-Referral to medical, mental health, child protection, and other services 6- key messages about Hygiene awareness CMAM activities: 1- All children under five and PLW in the target communities are screened for malnutrition.2- SAM and MAM children under five are successfully cured/recovered without further relapse and following re-admissions.3- Micronutrient deficiency and MAM among children under five and PLW is prevented and cured through the integrated approaches and Child Health Days.4- The health care facilities have a fully accessible regular quality OTP service for all children under five and PLW.5- Case management of SAM and MAM cases in IDA facilities IYCF activities: 1- Communities are empowered to practice C-IYCF.2- Mothers in prenatal, natal and postnatal period receive comprehensive nutritional support.3- Nutrition programme supply chain is effectively managed to sustain “zero stock out” status at all levels of the health/nutrition service.4- Nutrition programme provides quality service for children and PLW.5- Nutrition programme effectiveness and efficiency are well documented, analyzed, and further improvements are jointly agreed.6- Health and nutrition facilities have regular access to improved WASH solutions.7- The project is effectively and efficiently managed, documented and reported.

Protection Risk Analysis

This project proposal has been designed on the basis of needs assessments consisting ofinterviews with key informants within the target population. The project staff will be hiredwhenever possible from the target area itself, ensuring intimate knowledge of the contextand good acceptability of the staff by the community. IDA has an incorporatedbeneficiaries feedback into their existing feedback system, through suggestion boxes ateach PHC, which are collected and reviewed by the M&E field officer on monthly basis.The M&E Officer will obtain direct patient feedback during the monitoring process. Allpatients feedback will be registered in complaints tracking sheet, the tracking sheet willeasily show the statues for each complaint (such as category, time cycle, decision, extra),the beneficiary will get the result of his/her complaint if possible( since the anonymouscomplaints are acceptable).
