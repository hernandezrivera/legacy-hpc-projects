Project Summary

GSF will implement a 12 month program intervention focusing on GBV response and risk mitigation impacting 1300 survivors and individual at risk of GBV directly and 5000 indirectly in 4 LGAs in Borno State. At the project up, GSF Will priorities access to life saving services for survivors of GBV including those vulnerable and at risk of GBV and will seek to reduce immediate threats of violence including sexual violence and sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA). We will strengthen the capacity of service providers including community base mechanism to prevent and report incidents of GBV and work closely with partners to establish safe and confidential space where survivors including those at risk of GBV can access safety and psychosocial services. IDPs, returnees and hots community members including community base mechanism and local leaders will be engaged to identify and provide appropriate referrals to PSS. GSF will work with it already trained case workers by UNFPA and other agencies through gender consideration (male and female) who will be deploy at the community level to provide integrated case management and psychosocial services for survivors of GBV and to support individual at the risk of GBV especially women and girls. GSF will integrate this project using our mobile protection approach to reach out to hard to reach communities and mobile populations. The mobile approach is design to provide multi sectorial intervention to survivors of GBV in the communities where referral services are practically not possible. The mobile protection operates from pick up bus and compose of heath worker (certified nurse), social worker, Nutrition officer, community mobilisation officer who provide a package of services including case management, counselling, health screening and referral to complex cases. The project will strengthen accountability to beneficiaries through establishment and strengthening of community based complain and feedback mechanism that will allow women, girl, men and boys safely report concerns of gender base violence including sexual exploitation and abuse. GSF will work with the community to identify the preferable and safe method of reporting concerns related to GBV including SEA. This project will target adolescent boys and girls in and out of school and will establish adolescent and youth peer groups that will promote awareness among young people.

1 - Needs

In the North East Nigeria, especially the 3 worst affected states of Adamawa, Borno and Yobe, GBV is widespread perpetrated by Non state Arm group (NSAG) as a tactic of war as well as state security operatives and opportunities community members. of the 1.7 million IDPS in the three states, 54% are women and girls often living in overcrowded condition with inapropraite facilities and weak protection and security measures. Some women and girls are resorting to negative coping mechanism such as survival sex under the strain of prolonged uncertainly and shrinking resources, weakening their resilience as well as coping capacities. Harassment of women and girls have been reported in both formal and temporary IDP sites in the host communities often in the course of conducting daily activities such as food distribution, and water collection. 2 - Activities

Provide individual case management support to 300 survivors of GBV: GSF case worker will identify, document asses the needs and develop case implementation for survivors based on their needs and facilitate referrals to appropriate services. Case workers will provide follow up support to survivors by ensuring services referred for are provided in an appropriate manned as recommended by the minimum initial service package (MISP) for GBV. The 10 case workers will be supervised and supported by 2 GBV Program officers. The officers will work with the case workers to identify capacity building and provide training, mentoring and coaching support base on gaps identified. These officers will receive initial training on basic support and communication skills to ensure support services are immediately available for those in need and will further trained in case management supervision. After the first quarter, key staff will receive additional training to provide full case management services. Establish and equip safe spaces for women and girls to provide opporturnity for safe participation of women and girls in program activities. GSF will construct and equip 2 safe spaces and further support 2 existing safe spaces to facilitate women access to information as well as PSS. These spaces will serve as venue for women to build empowerment skills and social networks and for case management and referrals services. Facilitate Psychosocial and resilience building support to survivors of GBV and individual at the risk of GBV Provide training for GBV service providers on basic concept of GBV and case management. Facilitate the development/updating of LGA level referral pathway Conduct sensitisation and awareness sessions on GBV Mainstream GBV into other sectors of GSF response Distribution of Dignity kits to women and girls Conduct GBV safety audit in project communities and support prevention action Provide safety materials to women and girls at the risk of GBVfacilitate dialogue and awareness sessions with women and girls on risk identified in their communities Provide livelihood and economic support to 200 survivors and women at risk of GBV.
