Project Summary


LABDI is a national and a women led organization that operates across the BAY state and implement activities in areas of nutrition, WASH, Protection …. In this project, LABDI will engage in provision of quality nutrition preventive and curative activities in Yobe and Borno state. Children and PLWs will be the key targets of the project and the beneficiaries will be admitted in to the nutrition programs and receive treatment according to the national guidelines while preventive nutrition services will be provided to caregivers and beneficiaries to prevent and reduce the incidence of malnutrition. LABDI is implementing nutrition project by strengthening the screening, identification and referral of acute malnutrition among children under five, pregnant and lactating women (PLW); ensuring that malnourished children receive timely treatment & quality nutrition services; and ensuring provision of support for appropriate, safe feeding for children 0-23 months (specific focus on <6m) in Yobe and Borno state. In this project, LABDI will also ensure pregnant and lactating women, children under 5, adolescents and other members of the targeted communities receive quality nutrition services by, providing supplies, supporting the daily provision of curative nutrition services in health facilities and community, and other various interventions to reduce morbidity and mortality. The project will also treat children and PLWs with MAM through the use of ToM Brown.
The project will provide capacity building to government healthcare providers and in prevention and treatment of severe acute malnutrition through promotion of appropriate infant and young children feeding (IYCF), community mobilization, identification and referral of SAM cases and treatment of severe acute malnutrition (SAM) among children less than 5 years in Outpatient Therapeutic Program (OTP) and also ensuring those with Medical complications are referred to Stabilization center.

1 - Needs

Records of our current interventions shows worsening nutrition situation. Analysis from the current response intervention locations recorded a steady increase of SAM and MAM cases across all implementation locations. Our record revealed a GAM rate of 16%, SAM rate of 3.2% and MAM rate of 12.4%. The data from Nutrition & Food Security Surveillance (NFSS), admission trends, localized SMART surveys, sentinel surveillance, and humanitarian situation monitoring shows Admissions of SAM with and without medical complications across BAY states increased by 24% and 18% respectively during the first half of 2022. Some locations have seen the worst trends with a fourfold increase in admissions of SAM with medical complications compared to the same period in 2021(Nutrition sector bulletin, July 2022). Therecent IPC analysis also revealed a crisis phase of nutrition situation and projected a worsening situation for 3rd and 4th quarter of the year.Data from LABDI existing project and sector analysis provided a clear idea on the available gaps across locations which suggest the need to increase coverage and scale up nutrition services in BAY states

2 - Activities

Activity 1. Conduct weekly MUAC screening, and checking Oedema of children under five in IDP camps and host communities by Community nutrition volunteer at community level and by CMAM officer/health workers at OTP and TSFP sites. Activity 2: Admit and treat SAM cases with medical complication in inpatient care (SC) according to national guidelines. LABDI will support running of a Stabilization Centers (SC) for the management of SAM with medical complications at general Hospital in Kaga LGA. Activity 3: Admit and treat SAM cases without medical complication and pass appetite test in OTP every week by CMAM officer and health workers. LABDI will also provide OTP services for children with SAM and no medical complications in 5 Health facilities (1 in Borno and 4 in Yobe state). The services will be provided by the facility health workers (MoH staff), whose payment of stipends is covered by this project under system support. Activity 4: Construction and Rehabilitation of OTP and SC site and fixing of minor facilities where there’s needs to support the SC and OTPs. repair of waiting shelters, toilets, furniture's etcActivity 5: Admit and treat under 5 children with MAM using Tom Brown approach. Ingredients and cooking kits, storage containers for preparation of the supplementary feeding will be procured.Activity 6: Admit and treat PLWs with malnutrition using tom brown. Due to correlation between maternal malnutrition and that of their children, LABDI will also screen PLWs across the supported locations. LABDI will enroll the PLW into Tom Brown program where mother to mothers groups are taught on how to produce Tom brown feeds locally to feed the PLWs enrolled. Those with MAM will be referred to and enroll in TSFP implemented by WFP program within the catchment area where LABDI operatesETC.
