Project Summary

The project is to fill the gaps in the FSL sector through Improving the food security status of assessed food insecure people through life-saving and life sustaining food assistance which planned to be achieved by emergency response to crisis affected vulnerable people with short term food assistance through appropriate modalities,and the provision of flour or bread directly to households or to bakeries.
Beyaz Eller aims to:
Distribute 20,000 food basket in 2020
Distribute bread for 3800 HH (200 MT of flour)
Distribute food vouchers for 5,000 HH in 2020
Distribute 20,000 RTE to 10,000 HH in 2020
Distribute cooked meals to 20,000 HH per month

4 - Needs

After more than eight years of conﬂict,the situation in the Syria remains one of the most signiﬁcant humanitarian crises.Based on results from a countrywide household assessment, an estimated 7.9 million people in Syria are food insecure and an additional 1.9 million people are at risk of food insecurity. pregnant and lactating women require nutritional support.Nutrition Surveillance system is showing infant feeding practices to be extremely poor in most of NWS with about 20% of mothers feeding their children properly and this is not because just food insecurity.Suboptimal infant feeding practices is one of the main causes behind increasing acute and chronic malnutrition in Syria.There are some areas with the highest prevalence of malnutrition named as “pockets" of malnutrition.Displacement of population contributes to increased prevalence of malnutrition,which are located in those “pockets” and overburdened communities in Northern Idleb and Aleppo.SMART survey 2019 conﬁrmed the predictions of having at least 5,000 children under the age of ﬁve years could become severely malnourished by Dec 2019 if appropriate prevention measures including early case ﬁnding and referrals are not scaled up. 12 percent of malnourished mothers are younger than 18; and 3 percent of the mothers are younger than 13 years.Food items are available in market,but with limited quantities and IDPs don't have the ﬁnancial ability to buy them.The most urgent food interventions required in the assessed locations are,cash assistance,food baskets and bread.(Care,2019).Women and men each play key roles in food production,“however lacking ownership of,access to,and control of livelihood assets will negatively affect women’s food production.The lower production reduces not only women’s potential income,but also the availability of food for household consumption

5 - Implementation of activities

Beyaz Eller will provide (20,000 food baskets ,bread for 3800 HH per month, food vouchers for 5,000 HH, 10,000 RTE food baskets, cooked meals to 2,000 HH per month) to vulnerable beneﬁciaries(people with disabilities,women and girls caregivers,elders,women headed HHs,IDPs,Poor members of hosting communities and young girls and boys in schools)-setting beneficiaries lists, conducting Rapid Need Assessment, and pre verification of beneficiaries -Cooperation with local councils,community leaders and the FSL cluster by ﬁeld meetings,e-mails-setting distribution plan from pre-agreed distribution points within communities.-Procurement procedures by declaring bids, receiving offers and choosing best prices and best goods.-BE will provide door step delivery when needed-Women and men of different ages will participate in awareness sessions and trainings,they will receive needed support monthly,BE will make sure all beneﬁciaries will have equal chances to access to food and awareness activities

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

In general,women are in charge of food selection,preparation and childcare. If they generate an income, this is most likely to be spent on food and the needs of their children.Although women play a crucial role within the subsistence agricultural sector,their work in producing food is often undervalued. within families some members may be malnourished,while other members are not because they have enough food.Although women play an important role in the food security at the household and communal level, their potential capacity and contribution to a sustainable rural development and food security are often not recognized and due to existing inequalities between men and women not explored.Food distribution within the household can vary according to one’s gender.Although food may be available within the household,but due to hierarchy within the family,may be not as accessible by women compared to men. Also differences in risk and vulnerability between men and women can affect the accessibility to food.Risks-SEA by humanitarian workers male dominated,gate keepers and potential other beneficiaries-Risk of violence from beneficiaries among themselves/with humanitarian workers when huge onset occurring (crowd control)-Diversion of assets-Access to land/assets-Risk to secondary rights holders female HH Mitigating-Establish a feedback mechanism-Recruit female staff and train them-Train staff on code of conduct-All staff contracts to include PSEA-Ensure distribution is well-organized.identification of sites/environment ; set up several distribution spots on sites-Staff work/support the local councils in preparing the beneficiaries list-Meetings with community leaders/gate keepers on targeting criteria etc-Set community feedback mechanism-Use well trained and gender balanced outreach team-Community based beneficiary selection-Integration of food assistance and livelihood projects-CB monitoring-Asset monitoring by CP-Engage with local authorities

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

risk: GBV and SEA due to large gathering and crowd control in FDPs, likelihood: medium, impact: high, mitigation: WHITE HANDS will prevent large gatherings by choosing distribution points close to the beneficiaries' housing, FDPs will be designed as for Sphere standards, there will be seperated places for males and females to wait, seperated distribution lines, awareness posters on GBV,SEA, and how to complaint, first line staff will be trained on PSEA, and will sign on COC to assure thier obligations toward protection of beneficiaries.risk: funds could reach terrorist organizations, likelihood: low, impact: high, mitigating:to ensure control over the use of distributed materials, and in order to ensure that funds do not reach terrorist organizations, White Hands will conduct a security screening of the contracted merchants and ensure that they are not linked.risk: changes in safety due to complex conflict environment,likelihood: low, impact: high, metigating: White Hands will follow up on all security developments, all scenarios and choose alternative places of action, moreover BE will coordinate with the OCHA office and FSL cluster in related issues.
