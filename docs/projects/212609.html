Project Summary

the project will work with the community and stakeholders to ensure that the Community members collectively create change by taking ownership of identifying needs, developing interventions, and challenging power dynamics. the project will increase the ability of the local partner to bring together diverse stakeholders in a common process and identify the stakeholder's needs and how to manage their expectations and needs. by building trust between community members, the community will work together to select their needs and accept each other. the focusing on the community voice and skills, Diverse community members will be better equipped, and working with local leaders to foster peaceful, community-led change.
The communities we are targeting are facing increasing needs due to climate change, as well as challenges regarding securing their drinking water needs. These communities often rely on sources that are not sterile or clean, such as surface wells, or non-sterilized water, which highlights the need to improve their ability to sterilize water sources.
It is crucial to increase awareness of the importance of WASH (water, sanitation, and hygiene) practices, including water conservation, sterilization, and the effects of climate change. Additionally, it is important to enhance the communities' ability to adopt positive adaptation strategies to deal with these variables.
A lack of awareness or interest in personal hygiene, coupled with limited access to sterilizers or sterilization services, contribute to a severe shortage of personal hygiene measures in most members of society. Poor water and sanitation infrastructure also lead to increased pollution and diseases, which in turn greatly affect personal hygiene.
the project through the gender assessment community mapping, community discussion, Facilitation skills will equip the Community members to work collectively to create change by taking ownership of local development processes (common challenges), expanding inclusion of marginalized groups, and fostering the development of stronger social relationships and non-traditional leadership.
Through the participatory need assessments, developing action plan, and project management training, the project will let the community define its needs and design interventions to address the needs or the challenges it faces. also, Enabling the community to collect data and analyse the risks related to it, based on their consideration as the group most knowledgeable about the context related to their community. also, Enhancing the capacity of the community to lead the project itself Develops feelings of ownership of the project in the community, and mobilizes and motivates different groups to engage in similar interventions.
The advocacy training, the project will ensure the centralization of the community’s voice which will lead to a process of building greater trust between the different groups and increasing the community’s confidence towards organizations and humanitarian actors, as the situation shifts from a community that participates in a supposed intervention based on the vision of humanitarian actors to a community that leads the intervention with the support of humanitarian actors.
through the participatory process, Leadership from the community restored trust and relations between the community and the marginalized groups that were isolated.
Selecting committees’ members by following a methodology based on Community mapping, analyzing the nature of social and gender relations, and ensuring the representation of the different groups and the most marginalized among them, it contributes significantly to building and strengthening social cohesion and peace pathways in addition to community participating.
