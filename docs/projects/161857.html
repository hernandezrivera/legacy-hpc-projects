Project Summary

Economic empowerment of vulnerable women affected by the Boko Haram through the Savings and Loans Associations (SLAs) and providing Seed grants to enhance their capital.The aim of the proposed project is to improve access to and control over resources through meaningful and sustainable community driven ventures.The purpose is also to enhance delivery of small-scale enterprises for the vulnerable women whom are victims of the Boko Haram insurgency through cash assistance and entrepreneurial support at community-based level and in groups. The advantages include them providing some form of psycho-social support to each other as they conduct themselves during meetings.
The implementation of the proposed project will include; the mapping, selection, clustering of women into groups; registration of the vulnerable women to make up the target. Electronic /manual capture of bio details of the selected women beneficiaries; Training of the members in groups of the SLAs on the methodology; SLA materials distributed to the groups; GZDI Team provides supportive supervision of the sittings and eventual share-out of the SLA groups’. The training on SLA methodology on group basis is a strategy for GZDI to have them fully understand and continue advocating for supporting other vulnerable households and improving livelihoods in their respective areas.
The project targets vulnerable women whom are affected by the Boko Haram insurgency in locations of the Northeast, Nigeria.

1 - Needs

The Borno, Adamawa and Yobe State level Consultations on 2020 HNO reveals that the overall PiN for BAY states excluding inaccessible population remained the same as 2019 (7.1M) of which:• Seventy-six (76%) percent are under wellbeing and some consequences of living standards (overlap with all wellbeing). This includes consequence related to Nutrition, Health Food Security and livelihoods, Protection, Child Protection, GBV, Mine Action, WASH and Shelter NFI.• Twenty-four (24%) percent are under living standards only. This includes consequences related to Education, CCCM, Shelter NFI, Housing, Land and Property (HLP), Health, Protection, GBV, Early Recovery and WASH. • The vulnerability of women are clearly captured for the BAY States hence the emphasis to support them on priority. Poverty has increased among the affected people due to lack of livelihood opportunities. While needs for IDPs, returnees and host communities apparent, there is no information on those still living in inaccessible areas. The affected communities have lost access to economic assets and livelihood and therefore are solely dependent on humanitarian aid which is insufficient to meet their urgent daily needs is insufficient to meet their urgent daily needs. Summary of needs from the ERL Sector reveals that at present, across Borno, Adamawa and Yobe states, assessments indicate inadequate levels of access to basic services and public infrastructure relative to the size of the population, limited income and livelihood opportunities, and increased resource demands. This situation has led to societal tensions between IDPs, returnees and host communities and resulted in increased hardship for 5.6 million people (comprising 1.8 million girls; 1.5 million boys; 1.2 million women and 1.1 million men) across the three states.Prior to the crisis, poverty, development and socio-economic conditions in northeast Nigeria were very low with relative poverty recorded at 76 per cent in 2010. The conflict has further aggravated the situation. (Consultations on 2020 HNO, 2019),The ERL Sector Objectives: 2 Support livelihood stabilisation and 3: Promote inclusiveness and peaceful co-existence among IDPs in HC, returnees, and host communities, will greatly alleviate most of the mentioned hardships as captured in the ERL sector summarised assessed needs.The proposed project inline with the objectives, will address most of the mentioned needs when the livelihood of the women is enhanced, ultimately the positiveness will reflect on the family members: Youths, children, elderly. Thus the vulnerability of the numerous household members will gradually reduce, they will be better fed, be healthier, attend schools and be more active for progressive development.

2 - Activities

The implementation of the proposed project activities that will address the needs are: • The mapping of the project communities.• Advocacy to relevant Stakeholders.• Mobilisation and awareness creation.• Identifying, selection of vulnerable women with different skills or economic venture. • Grouping the vulnerable women for Savings and Loans Associations.• Registration of the vulnerable women to make up the target. • Electronic /manual capture of bio details of the selected women beneficiaries. Training of the members in groups of the SLAs on the methodology.• SLA materials distribution to the groups.• GZDI Team provides supportive supervision of the sittings and eventual share-out of the SLA groups’. • Empowerment for the women by disbursement of seed grants. • Monitoring and Evaluation of the SLAs.The implementation will be carried out in the various communities of the project LGAs.
