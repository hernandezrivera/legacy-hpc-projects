Project Summary

This project is designed to provide adequate access to food, potable water, skill development, community participation and social integration. The project is targeted mainly at youths both male and female. The program aims at resuscitating 2000 youths with livelihood support and skill development.

Plan fields

1 - Needs

The assessment showed a retarded social status of settlers and a very poor living condition. A little self-sustainability or self-reliance. The community maintain a low status heritage without any means to possible change. The project seeks to revamp the socio economic conditions of idling internally displaced persons, alleviate poverty, create employment and income generating opportunity through micro enterprises and livelihood development. To develop capacity and skills of the target groups on micro enterprise development/management as well as Self Help Groups management.To upgrade vocational skills of the target people on agriculture and agro allied activities, artisan’s activities.To provide initial capital fund for undertaking income generating activities and inculcating self-confidence on running of small business management profitably.

2 - Activities

This project will equip the youth with quality business and vocational skills ranging from Business Registration, Marketing Skills, Business Partnerships, Warehousing, Inventory, Maintenance, and Digital Skills. MODALITIES Self Help Group Management Training:At first a training of 20 potential group leaders, one from each group, are identified for the proposed training of 2 weeks duration. The training will be conducted in 2 batch. These group leaders are literate and capable to read write simple sentences, counting and numerical calculations. The proposed training will be conducted by expert trainers, who are experienced to conduct such training among the target community. Information Education and Communication training materials will be used in the training sessions for better understanding on the subject. Participatory approach will be followed so that all trainees could be vocal and interact with the trainers. Business Management Training:An assessment shows that the target groups have no concept on business startup of management. Their traditional occupation was daily labour with any business orientation or motive. A two weeks business management training is proposed for a down line trainers who will in turn provide a general training for the selected population. TECHNOLOGY Target Beneficiaries will undergo digital skills to market their business products for self-reliance and sustainability.
