Project Summary

CHEF International proposes a one-year project addressing basic curative and preventive health care issues. The project will cater to the immediate needs most vulnerable populations, especially women, adolescent girls, children and persons living with disabilities who have become more vulnerable in the current situation of COVID-19 pandemic. The project goals: a) to improve access of under-served and marginalized socio-economic groups to basic curative and preventive health care services, b) to assist the government of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa in strengthening primary healthcare system in the targeted areas.
CHEF is already closing working with provincial and district health departments. Through this project, CHEF will work with 60 BHU’s of 3 districts (Mardan, Charssada, Bunner) of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. The proposed project will enhance the capacity of targeted health facilities in treatment of the patients in safe & infection prevention & control (IPC) environment on prevailing health conditions such as communicable and non-communicable diseases.
The project will mainly have two components i.e. (1) provision of health care services along with free medicines, delivery kits and free prosthetic and orthotic; (2) Community Outreach through Education and Awareness. To support the women and girls of the target population, a free helpline will be provided for psycho-social support and MNCH support for expecting mothers. The GBV and sexual harassment will also be managed through the helpline by providing them mental psychological support as well as refer them to the relevant specialists’ authorities, if needed. A strong referral mechanism will be established from community to first level care facility (FLCF) & from FLCF to secondary or tertiary level care facility by ensuring dedicated transport ambulance services in target communities specifically for mothers and children.
CHEF will identify Persons with Disabilities within each locality and provide them assistive technology devices as CHEF’s contribution to the project.
Trainings and capacity building activities will target 1500 medical health workers including doctors, paramedics, LHWs, LHVs, DPOs disease surveillance officers, vaccinator & psychologists to prevent, identify and refer the COVID patients to designated COVID treatment centers. Further 300 LHV’s & LHWS will receive training on Birth preparedness and prevention of complications in COVID pandemic situation.
CHEF International will sensitize the local communities on primary health issues through a robust education and awareness campaign. CHEF will develop and publish Brochures and Pamphlets on topics such as how to live a healthy life, MNCH, Hygiene and social distancing, family planning, and managing Stress and anxiety. Radio messages and cable TV adverts will also be utilized to reach out to the masses.
In each Health facility CHEF will provide COVID-19 related PPEs such as gowns, masks, hand sanitizers and disinfection materials for health facilities.

Hiring of Three District Based Medical TeamsCHEF International will hire three district-based Field Teams. Each team will be headed by one District Coordinator, who will work as overall in-charge of the concerned team in respective district. Other members of the team include Female Medical Officer, Male Medical Officer, Psychologists, Pharmacist, Optometrists, Opticians, Lady Health Worker, Community mobilizers and Support Staff. 3. Services Delivery Through BHUs CHEF International will closely work with BHUs to give OPD services to the local communities and outreaching the women and girls though telemedicine services by going door to door and using helpline. CHEF International will pay special attention to referral cases by enhancing itself to be capable enough to handle/manage as many cases as possible within its scope, thus to lower the burden of Tertiary care hospitals.
