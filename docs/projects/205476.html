Project Summary

The outbreak of fighting in Sudan on 15 April 2023 has caused an influx of people fleeing the country to the crisis affected South Sudan. More than 259,451 individuals have fled from Sudan into South Sudan. Nine out of ten of those arriving are South Sudanese-returnees (UNHCR 2023). The increase in the number of refugees and returnees is compounding the already fragile WASH situation in south Sudan and in Greater upper Nile in particular since most of the return locations are in the States bordering Sudan.
In South Sudan, there is still limited access to basic WASH services owing to a chronic lack of investment in WASH infrastructure, conflicts, and repeated Flooding with massive destruction of facilities (HNO 2023). In effect WASH Needs are widespread over the country and most severe in the greater Upper Nile with Malakal, , Koch and Rubkona Counties recording low WASH indices and classified in WASH Severity Classification 4 and 5. The aforementioned counties particularly have unfavorable WASH indicators; such as persons having access to 3 liters of safe drinking water per day, households having access to a safe water source in a distance of 500m or less, Water points being safe from fecal contamination are not met, women, and girls do not feel safe at water points. Humane Aid for Community Organization (HACO), therefore, proposes to support most vulnerable communities in Upper Nile and Unity States; returnees, IDPs, and most vulnerable host communities with emergency WASH services; Distribution of WASH kits to “caretakers/SAM child”, WASH in nutrition facilities; with focus on water supply and hygiene in Communities with high GAM and WASH vulnerability, Provide access to basic WASH services in emergencies, conduct WASH specific activities for mitigating WASH GBV-During Water point rehabilitation, Construction and or rehabilitation of Sanitation facilities and distribution of WASH NFIs, Construction of disaster-resistant WASH structures and infrastructure, Build/rehabilitate water points with flood-resilient designs, Build/rehabilitate toilets with flood-resilient designs and carrying out safety audits before conducting WASH activities. HACO intends to reach 11,111 Individuals; Upper Nile-Malakal (4,111) and Unity States-Rubkona and Koch Counties (7000). The response targets 50% IDPs, 35% vulnerable host community and 15% Returnees in Upper Nile and Unity States.
