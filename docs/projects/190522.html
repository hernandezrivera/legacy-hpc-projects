Project Summary

Several zones in Amhara region have been affected by a continuous influx of displaced people following recent conflict in different parts of the country. North Shewa is among the zones which are highly affected by the conflict. According to the latest multisector assessment report in February 2022, there are approximately 37,000 IDPs in the zone with increasing number of new arrivals in 15 woredas and Debre Birhan city. Most of the displaced people are staying among the host communities, with one third living in Debre Birhan city. There are more than 10,000 IDPs in collective sites of which 9,500 are hosted in IDP sites in Debre Birhan. As per recent assessment, most of the IDPs came from different part of the country, especially from Oromia region (Dano, Jibat, Bako woredas of West Shewa zone; and Limmu, Gobu Sayo, Wayu Tuka, and Sibu Sire, of East Wellega zone). According to the assessment findings, reasons for displacement are attributed to conflict between government forces and Unidentified Armed Groups (UAGs) in Oromia region. As a result, the humanitarian situation has deteriorated with prolonged displacement, a new influx of IDPs, and emerging needs in shelter support. IDP living conditions are incredibly overcrowded in the six collective IDP centers in Debre Birhan Town. An average collective center with just 600m2 capacity accommodates more than 1,200 IDPs. Population groups leading that overcrowded situation includes the most vulnerable people: pregnant and lactating mothers, the elderly, etc.
The level of support provided to the needy IDPs in the proposed target areas has been very low. According to findings of the stated multisectoral assessment, Shelter, Food, NFI, Health, and MPHSS are the top priorities needs identified. Scaling up of current services is highly required to meet the growing needs.
Therefore, Dorcas is applying for immediate funding support to address the critical shelter needs of 1,380 IDP households over a period of with 3 months.
Thanks to the experience and opportunities created because of its currently active humanitarian response interventions in the region in general and in the town in particular, Dorcas has well-functioning project office in the area, to ensure effective implementation of the proposed action. Currently, DAE is implementing cash for rent program for 2,100 HH and emergency wash response for IDPs.
