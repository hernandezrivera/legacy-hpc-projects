Project Summary

Through the project, we aim to:
• Vocational training by distributing unemployed youth to workshops, laboratories and professions for a period of four months for training (with financial compensation provided to the trainee and trainer) with the aim of developing the vocational skills of the trainees and their direct entry into the labor market.
• Reviving the professions affected by the crisis (business support) by providing equipment and tools for each profession with the aim of increasing income and production.
In our project, we target 50 beneficiaries in the city of Khan Sheikhoun in Idlib countryside, of the internally displaced population and returnees whose businesses have been damaged due to the war and displacement and who have lost their equipment completely or partially and thus are unable to continue their work and earn their livelihood with their families.

3 - Needs

- An electronic program for the association has been prepared that includes the entire data of the population residing in the targeted areas, whether (internally displaced persons - host communities - returnees...), the program is designed on Microsoft Access, and it provides each family with its own data form that is entered by a special team in the association . - After entering the forms, the survey and evaluation team conducts field visits covering the project implementation areas (Khan Sheikhoun and its countryside) to verify the degree of need, and the type of intervention needed.

4 - Implementation of activities

- The 50 preselected beneficiaries are divided into two groups (each group extends for a period of 6 months), each group is subjected during the first month to training an economic feasibility study, defining the special bag for each beneficiary, and psychosocial support sessions (violence - gender equality...).- During the following four months, vocational training will begin according to the needs and profession of each beneficiary.- During the sixth and final month, the equipment and supplies for each beneficiary (a professional portfolio) are purchased, which help to start and enter the labor market. - After implementing the necessary interventions, these interventions (activities and operations) are monitored and evaluated by a specialized team that prepares periodic reports through which it measures output indicators both quantitatively and qualitatively. - Reports and results are shared with the Commission using the monthly report form in addition to other forms related to monitoring and evaluation approved by the Commission.
