Objectives

1. Restore access to safe drinking water to pre-crisis level 2. Restore access to sanitation facilities to pre-crisis level 3. Enhance personal hygiene standards and ensure availability of supplies. 4. Ensure excreta free community

Needs

Devastating tropical cyclones hit the coastal areas of Bangladesh almost every year usually accompanied by high-speed winds, sometimes reaching 250 km/hr or more and 3-10m high waves, causing extensive damage to life, property and livestock. Cyclones in the Bay of Bengal occur in two seasons; April-June and October-November; before and after the monsoons. The Bay of Bengal is one of the regions in the world which is frequently affected by storm surges associated with tropical cyclones. 5% of global tropical cyclones form over the Bay of Bengal. The Camp of 921,000 forcibly-displaced Myanmar nationals (FDMN) is very close to the Bay of Bengal thus the FDMNs are under potential Risk of Cyclone. It is assumed that if the Cyclone hit this region, there will be need of additional emergency Water, Sanitation and Hygien (WASH) facilities apart from ongoing support.
