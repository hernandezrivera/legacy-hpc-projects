Project Summary

The project will address the critical need for strengthened child protection (CP) and sexual and gender-based violence (SGBV) prevention and response services in Saida, Wadi Khaled and Akkar Governorate. The project will target at-risk Lebanese girls and boys aged 10-17 and young women and men aged 18-24 at risk or survivors of violence, abuse, neglect and exploitation (especially those engaged in worst forms of child labour, children without legal status or documentation, and girls at risk or survivors of child marriage); and adult women at risk or exposed to GBV, as well as parents and caregivers, partners, community leaders and local service providers.
This project is a consortium Led by Plan Lebanon with IMC and Mouvement social, and its aim is to provide services to address the increasing protection risks amidst the multi-layered crisis in Lebanon. The consortium will utilize psychosocial support (PSS) modules, outreach strategies, community-based initiatives, established networks, trust built with the community, and specialized case management tools to strengthen the protection environment around boys, girls, women and other vulnerable groups
The project will provide a range of services, with each service delivered by a partner. Quality case management will be provided by Mouvement Social (MS), while IMC will handle GBV case management and referral. Both MS and IMC will provide cash assistance, with IMC handling cash for protection. Psychosocial support activities will be conducted by Plan International Lebanon (PIL), in addition to life skills sessions and positive parenting sessions. IMC will handle non-focused PSS activities, cycle of violence sessions, and inter-gender dialogue sessions. Emotional support group sessions will be conducted by IMC, and parenting sessions by PIL. Finally, PIL will conduct trainings to local community leaders on gender equality and inclusion, sexual and reproductive health rights (SRHR), GBV, child protection (CP), and safe identification and referrals, in addition trainings to local PWD organizations will be run to support safe identification and referral of CP and GBV survivors and children at risk. By carefully coordinating the delivery of these services, the project aims to provide comprehensive support to those in need, while ensuring efficient and effective implementation.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

The project includes the provision of 1) basic assistance through the distribution of menstrual health management kits including personal hygiene items covering a period of one year and 2) Cash assistance provision through Emergency cash assistance and Cash for Protection modalities for families included under the case management component, upon the provision of 3) CP and GBV Case management service to high-risk families Moreover, the project focuses on enhancing the wellbeing of targeted adolescents and their caregivers through Psychosocial support interventions including:4) Focused PSS for adolescents 5) Non-focused PSS6) Emotional support in emergencies for caregivers 7) Positive parenting for caregivers8) Cycle of violence sessions for male community membersOn a community level, 9) community based PSS will be an entry point to identify project beneficiaries, their needs and to refer them to internal and external service providers as per the safe referral pathway.Furthermore, 10) intergender dialogues will support adolescents by creating a safe space to discuss gender-related topics with the purpose of combatting gender-based violence. Accordingly, the latter will form peer action groups to mainstream gender key messages. Moreover community stakeholders will be supported to create of a protective safe environment, by 11) implementing community-led initiatives, to disseminate child-protection key messages using the Qudwa approach.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

The project responds to issues of PSEA through the following measures: A comprehensive risk assessment will be conducted to identify potential protection risks and develop measures to prevent and mitigate them. All project (plan and partner) ill also receive comprehensive training on child protection, safeguarding, and PSEA. A robust complaints and feedback mechanism will be established to enable children and their families to report any concerns or complaints they may have about the project or its staff. The project will engage with the local community to build awareness of child protection and safeguarding issues.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

The project will engage affected populations, including children, parents, caregivers, and community leaders, through regular consultations, feedback mechanisms, and community meetings. The project will provide timely and accessible information and establish a complaints and feedback mechanism to address concerns and improve project implementation. Regular monitoring and evaluation will be conducted to assess the effectiveness of the project and collect feedback from beneficiaries and stakeholders through Post activity monitoring calls and post distribution monitoring calls, as well as pre and post surveys to assess project impact and beneficiary satisfaction
