Project Summary

MWL proposed project seeks to provide protective education environment opportunities to vulnerable children (boys and girls)in four sub-districts: (Dana, Maar Tamsarin, Al-Bab, and Afrin). that selected as most priority to ensure expanding ongoing education services by availing inclusive protective learning spaces to ensure that no children absent or dropout of schools' due to lack of learning spaces, learning and teaching materials, economic capacity of the Education Directorate and the parents to cover all the schools & Student’s needs.
MWL proposed project aims to contribute in supporting 15000 (B:9000, G: 6000) aged 5-17 years, & 5000 (M:3000, F:2000) aged 15-24 years, including 150 CWDs and 300 (M:190,F:110) aged 25-59 to get access to Education (formal and non-formal) and raising the quality of education in-line with EiE standards and inclusion Children with Disability through rehabilitation and equip schools, provision transportation and, awareness raising and inclusion activities. This project has no secured fund by any other partners.
That will be achieved through;
1)Rehabilitation for classrooms in targeted schools to raise their capacity to absorb more IDP students
2) providing Schools' furniture / Students' seats to enhance the capacity of schools;
3) Training the targeted teachers and administrative staff on classroom management, modern teaching methods;
4) Provision stationary, teaching & learning materials, textbooks and Student's kit;
5) Using the interim government curricula in formal education and curriculum B for non-formal education in the two semesters during the school year, and Providing literacy and numeracy skills, remedial & catch-up classes for non-formal program
6) Conducting ASER to assess learning;
7) Establishing 2nd shift to providing formal / non-formal education in the school to raise the capacity to absorb new IDPs Boys and Girls;
8) providing incentives for teachers and administrative staff
9) Conduct winterization campaign through distributing of Heating fuel and heaters the through Voucher Scheme to ensure the safety and security for the schools, prevent the fraud & corruption in procurement process
10) Establishing PTAs to follow up with student’s education improvement and issues;
11) Conducting BTL campaigns to target 500 (B:250, G:250) in the targeted locations.
The M&E Coordinator will be responsible to oversight the complaint mechanism where beneficiaries will be able to provide feedback or complaint to ensure satisfied of beneficiaries Evaluation of the project will be conducted by the M&E staff throughout all the project
duration that will started by conducting “base-line survey”, monitoring and compares the progress development and conduct an “end-line survey” to measure the achievements of intervention outputs. A lesson learning event will be conducted at the end of the project life cycle to draw some lessons and best practices from the project. The project will include the CVA modality for the heating liters distribution since MWL followed the same strategy in the previous projects.

4 - Needs

Based on HNO 2022, 14.6 million people are in need of humanitarian assistance, an increase of 1.2 million from 2021. Schools facilities have also suffered immensely, both as a result of attacks and collateral damage. One hundred and eighty-two schools in Idleb and 18 schools in Aleppo Governorates, supporting more than 21,200 students and almost 1,000 educational personnel were also affected. The project was built based on the needs of affected persons in NW Syria since MWL has targeted the locations based on the ASO1. MWL team conducted needs assessment using KII & Filling Questionnaire with Education Directorates, community key members, Camps leaders, and stakeholders. In Afrin 3 communities (Trinda, Qarzihel & Afrin city), severity is 4.30 and school-age children in need is 17264 (B:7754,G:9510). In Dana 5 communities (Sarmada, Burdaqly, Hezreh –Hezri, Qah, & Dana), severity is 5 and school-age children in need is 20059(B:9465,G:10594), while teachers in need is 8281 (M:4027, W:4254). In Maaret Tamsrin children in need is 27015 (B:13272,G:13743).According to the information, in targeted sub-district schools absent due to harsh cold weather last year, with inability to provide fuel for heating for schools. 91% of teachers are working as volunteers. 46% of dropped-out children due to the financial conditions, while 42% due to the child labor. 58% of assessed schools are in need of rehabilitation.

Programmatic reporting provided from team leader on field directly to Program Coordinator & Project Manager. MWL is aware of importance of Accountability to Affected People can improve the intervention.

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

MWL committed to mainstreaming protection throughout its programming. The key to ensure the dignity and voice of the intended beneficiaries is the consistent consultation throughout project implementation .Improve access to quality and inclusive education and protection for children with disabilities is one of priorities of this project, where the target schools, and wash facilities will be friendly and accessible for CWDs, and they will be provided with ramps, special seats for those children. A holistic approach will be adopted to ensure that CWDs are effectively enrolled aa activities through awareness campaigns target the community and parents, and by advocacy to providing the children with hearing devices and wheelchairs to ensure their attendance at school. Transportation for CWDs will be provided to and from school to encourage and facilitate their access to education.MWL will ensure that staff from displaced and host community in recruitment process, MWL will encourage the recruitment of PWD in project teams. The trainers of the workshops will be women and men.PWD will have access to complaint mechanism thorough FGDs, MWL will consider and ensure gender-and age aspects throughout the project cycle.During the assessment, MWL will collect gender-and sex-disaggregated data. MWL will consider the safety of children & teachers as priority through distributing (Safety and security kits).Furthermore, protection mainstreaming measures will be monitored.


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

PSEA Risk:Violate PSEA or CSG, CoC principles.mitigating measures:- Signing PSEA and child safeguarding MWL’s policy by all staff.- getting training on PSEAfor all staff.- PSEA awareness through brochures, videos.- Assigning complaint mechanism for SEA issues.- policies of Code of conduct, PSEA, child safeguarding is mandatory for all staff and vendors working with MWL.MWL will adopt a restrict feedback mechanism to ensure clear AAP. Effective participation by the affected persons will be ensured by getting their feedback via several channels. these channels are accessible for MWBG, PWD. Complaint Box will be fixed in accessible places in addition to email, what’s app number, Mobile complaint boxes for outreach activities and direct contact with MEAL focal point. For BNFs who do not have access to internet, direct contact with MEAL focal point and FGDs on voluntary basis will be used to get their feedback. for illiterates, what’s app voice massage, FGD and direct contact will be used. For PWD, FGDs will be carried out to get their feedback.To protect children, girls and beneficiaries from any exploitation, discrimination and SEA, posters will be published showing that all services are free of charge and MWL adhere to Zero-Tolerance principle, and they can complain directly through the complaint’s mechanism.
