Project Summary

Food assistance needs in Somalia are already high with an estimated 3.5 million people in rural livelihood zones, urban areas, and IDP settlements in need of food assistance. In riverine areas, farmers have suffered from multiple flood events during the Gu and Hagaa 2020 seasons, which damaged Deyr 2019/20 off-season crops and Gu 2020 main season crops. As a result, the main Gu harvest in July/August is significantly below average in these areas. In addition, escalated conflict in Lower Shabelle suspended cropping activities in Qoryooley, Marka, Afgooye, and Wanlaweyn districts and displaced large populations to Mogadishu.
Floods disrupted recessional cultivation activities and inundated crops, the rains are supporting late planted Gu main season crops and Gu off-season crops and alleviated fears that the forecast of a below-average Hagaa season would deplete soil moisture and negatively affect off-season crops. In general, farmers have benefitted from improved access to water for irrigation. Worryingly however, the damage to road infrastructure and market access, have adversely affected terms of trade with higher prices of maize, and significantly lowering the prices for goats and daily labour wage rates by up to 30 and 102 per cent below the five-year average. Juba foundation in the proposed interventions will work towards Improving access to food for 34700 persons facing severe acute food insecurity including 3000 IDPs , non-displaced and returnees and refugees in the proposed districts within selected districts of Lower Juba, Gedo, Bay, Bakool, and Lower Shabelle Region of south central Somalia by the end of 2021 through unconditional and conditional transfers. In order to ensure that livelihood is protected JF will work closely with farmers in order to protect livelihood and related food sources through emergency agriculture, livestock and other livelihood support in line with their livelihood and seasonality.

Project Activities

CO 1: Improving access to food for 3.1 million people facing severe acute food insecurity including IDPs, non-displaced and returnees and refugees by the end of 2021 through unconditional and conditional transfers.Act.1.1: Provide unconditional Cash Transfer through voucher system to improve the livelihoods to to 2500 IDP & Host Community HHs in the selected districts of ,Bakool, Bay, Gedo Middle Shabelle and Lower Juba Regions for a period of 3 months depending on the severity of food insecurity phases, vulnerability, and seasonality of the livelihoods, benefitting around 15,000 men, women, girls, and boys including disabled people and minorities. CO2: Protect livelihood and related food sources for farmers, agro-pastoralists, pastoralists and rural IDPs across Somalia through emergency agriculture, livestock and other livelihood support in line with their livelihood and seasonality in the selected districts of Gedo, Lower Shabelle, Lower Juba, Bay, and Bakool regions.Act.2.1: Distribute 7 goats per HH to 1500 HH from pastoral HHs to improve livelihood to both men, women, boys, and girls, who lost their animals during the crisis and conflicts. Act.2.2: Distribute to 1800HHs of farm inputs and tools (farm tools, fertilizers, seeds and vegetables) and prepare farms before the rainy season in the selected districts of Gedo, Lower Shabelle, Lower Juba, Bay, and Bakool regions benefiting around men, women, boys and girls including people living with disabilities. Act.2.3: Capacity building of 500 lead farmers on crop production, animal husbandry and management of animals and pests control with each of the targeted region training 100 farmers each per region.

What is the justification for using cash or vouchers instead of in kind?

The current use of mobile transfer or E-voucher or cash voucher is more accurate and they can redeem the goods direct without diverting the intended purpose to something else during the assistance, therefore it serves the right purpose. It reduces the chances of fraudsters taking advantage of the vulnerable group.

Explain how the project design considered market functionality ?

JF will as much as possible carry out a market survey and as much as possible acquire the goods locally to reduce on the freight cost as well as promote the local vendors. The locally available services can as well be used to boost the businesses of the locals instead of sourcing it elsewhere.
