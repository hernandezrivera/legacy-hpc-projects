Project Summary

The Project Aims to create and provide essential life-saving integrated primary Health Care Services and emergency response to affected vulnerable IDPS, Returnees and residents of Abyei Administrative Areas, Bor South ,Twic East & Duk Counties of Jonglei state. The Project seeks to bridge significant gaps to the affected communities by floods and inter- communal conflict and insecurity.
The Project Target 35,000 People 53% children and 47% adults, segregated as: Males 49.7%, females 50.3%, 8% elderly persons and people with disabilities represent 15% .Women 25%, Men 22%, Girls 25.3% and Boys 27.7%. By Locations; Duk county (37%), Abyei administrative Area (28%), Bor South (20%) and Twic East (17 %).
The Project will focus on integrated life-saving interventions, reducing morbidity and Mortality by timely detection and response to any outbreak and coordinated health, Nutrition WatSan and Protection services in our targeted locations. Through the supported chain of health facilities, the project will provide the following activities: curative consultation, preventive and promotional and capacity building of health workers, screening, treatment and referral of MAM/SAM cases with medical complications. Training of health workers on MIYCN best practices, conduct WASH-IPCs, distribution of safe reproductive kits and sanitary items and provision of sanitary facilities for both gender. GVB oriented services at the both level static and MMUs. The project will support 14 chain of health facilities in form of 3 PHCCs, 3 PHCUs and 8 MMUs across the three areas we targeting.
SMC project activities budget estimate is 1,095,000 USD for a population of 35000 beneficiaries.

1. Needs

The country's heath system relies heavily on Humanitarian assistance and faces many challenges, such as the luck of funding, staff, medicine and equipment. Is still unable to cope and respond to humanitarian emergencies and provide essential lifesaving intervention in all the sectors and the people suffer from Hunger, violence and poor health. Women, children, the elderly and people with disabilities are the most vulnerable groups, having limited access to health care and face high risks of death and illness. About 8.9 million people in the country need one or more forms of humanitarian assistance, especially in the counties most affected by the floods, conflict, IDPs, returnees and Disease outbreaks (HNO23). The health cluster plans to reach 3.2 million people with essential lifesaving health services in 2024; with focus on IDPs within the country, refugees and returnees from neighboring countries. We need an integrated interventions to prevent and respond to disease outbreaks and other public emergencies, with support from other sectors, such as nutrition, water, Sanitation, Hygiene and Food security. The Current health status of the population is very poor in our targeted location in particular with several heath facilities non-functional or affected by floods and displacements. The current health status of the population is poor Malaria remains the leading cause of morbidity and mortality with 50.2% and 39.4 % in 2023, Extreme low Immunization coverage has made the children vulnerable to vaccines preventable diseases, frequent outbreaks of Measles, Cholera and Hepatitis E remains a major public health concerns in the country (HNO 2023). High infant mortality rate at 84 death per 1000 live births and under –five mortality rate is even higher at 98.7 death per 100 live birth, with the highest maternal mortality rates in the world at 1,223 death per 100,000 live birth (WHO 2023). HNO2023 several health facilities were affected and relocated currently under poor setting and need for rehabilitation and Equipment for their proper functionalities. As of September 2023, only 12% are fully functional, offering the full package of basic package of health and Nutrition services (BPHNS) available and Open at required time and hours (SSD HSF 2023) The Quality of health care faced by inadequate health force, limited supplies, long waiting hours, safe water scarcity, poor maternal health services and lack of follow up care. Twenty counties in eight state reported Measles Outbreaks as of October 2022, which include our targeted locations People in Needs target under this project will be 35,000 in the 3 counties of Jonglei and Abyei administrative area. Adults represents 47% (16,450) and 53% children (18,550) segregated as follows: 8,750 women (25%) 7,700 Men (22%) Boys 26% (9100) and Girls are (9,450) representing 27 %, and people with disabilities represent 15% (5,250). 2. Project activities

The activities will be focusing on delivering life-saving health services contributing to the reduction of preventable morbidity and mortality from infectious, non- communicable and Epidemic-prone diseases related to acute food insecurity, floods and subnational violence in the targeted areas of Abyei Administrative areas And Bor south, Duk &Twic East counties of Jonglei state. 1. Support 16 static and Medical Mobiles clinic to improve access to life-saving essential health services including maternal, child and adolescent health),sexual and reproductive health, treatment of common illnesses, and provision of GBV and MPHSS services to the vulnerable population including those living with disability. 2. Conduct EPI services through Routine/campaigns Immunizations activities against vaccines preventable Diseases targeting children Under 5 Years and mothers in their Child bearing ages.3. Conduct screening, treatment and referral of all under 5 year’s children and pregnant and lactating women (PLW) for SAM/MAM2 cases at our facilities and surrounding villages.4. Training of Health workers on case management, integrated disease surveillance and response & EWARS, CMR, MHPHSS, WASH-IPC, Hygiene promotion health education and MIYCN best practices at the facilities. 5. Provision of basic medical Equipment’s & Supplies and minor construction of 2 PHCCs and 2 PHCUs in Duk, Bor and Abyei Areas. SMC will coordinate his project activities and Provide technical and Operational assistance to the health facilities through supportive supervision visits to ensure service Quality and compliance to the project standards at our operational locations in AAA and targeted Counties in Jonglei state.
