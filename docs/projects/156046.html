Project Summary

The project is compatible with Sector Objective 2: Support the life-saving livelihoods of affected households by increasing agricultural production, protecting and building productive assets and restoring or creating income generating opportunities) as it supports livelihood opportunities, helps to increase agricultural production and protects productive assets by training and grants to such income generating activities.
Those targets are achieved by supporting the whole process starting from training to selling products.
Agriculture grants are supported by trainings and grants to beneficiaries.
The project will establish a women association aiming to support the whole process of winter income generating activities starting from training until selling products.

The project is directed to most vulnerable members especially women who are displaced from Eastern Ghouta inside Rural Damascus; the aim of the project is to help them become self-sufficient by supporting them to start income generating.
The association will have a head office and a training center in Hazano city in Idleb and three training centers in Ma'arrat Tamasrin, Kelly and Ma'arrat An Nu'man
The association will first conduct trainings for women in sewing winter clothes, wool knitting and winter food preparation and storing.

After the end of courses, cash grants will be awarded to women who completed their training successfully after meeting a specific set of requirements and a thorough grant applications studying and will work under the supervision of the trainers; they will also be monitored by M&E team.
The four training centers will be turned into four workshops spaces and a bazaar.
Workshops spaces will be used by grants awarded women to implement their income generating activities.
The association will prepare for a bazaar event to promote products coming from implementing activities.

Needs

Most displaced women in Eastern Ghouta suffer from severe shortage of financial resources and they are forced to work because they are the sole breadwinners especially in cases of widows, wives of detainees, missing persons and disabled family members. Several studies have found that women need help in having a stable income to support their families after years of ongoing war. Our community worker teams on ground found the same results while visiting families. According to HNO 2019 lack of sources of income and employment opportunities continues to heavily affect household purchasing power. In rural communities, households are no longer able to make a living through agriculture alone due to low productivity and incomes, as well as poor access to cultivable areas due to insecurity. Those points mentioned in HNO 2019 shows the need of such a project supporting income generating activities. We have performed a needs assessment by personal interviews as well as electronically using google forms and the results were as follows:14% of the women surveyed were either wives of detainees or wives of missing people.50% of women are widows.More than 60% of women are the breadwinners for their families.More than 80% of the women showed a need to have an association for women that supports income generating activities. Summary of the September 2017 needs assessment and scanned paper examples are attached with the proposal. According to survey conducted by ZBT the following results were found: 100% agree there is a need to have a women association to support income generating activities.100% would support families to join such association.Examples of the results from the survey regarding the income generating activities are as follows: a. 87.5% making food for storage b. 75% wool knitting c. 75% sewing d. 37.5% gardening e. 37.5% cooking75% need financial aid to make a new income generating activity.62.5% need training or experiences development in income generating activity.37.5% need financial aid to continue with existing income generating activity. We have also recently conducted market assessment to study effective of cash grants planned to be awarded to women by the project. The project will help to provide an additional income for families that should have a significant effect on children because mothers will not need to make their children work. This means that children can continue their education. Furthermore, the additional income for families will also lead to a lower rate of marriages among under-age girls because the most common reason for this phenomenon is because families can get money from the husbands or in some cases merely to cut down on the household expenses.Our project is consistent with the three main goals of “No Lost Generation Initiative” Education, Child Protection and more opportunities for youth and adolescents. It is proven that families do prioritize their children education and safety when they have enough money to provide their basic life needs. Estimated percentage of youth in the area targeted by the project is more than 25% according to HNO PiN numbers.
