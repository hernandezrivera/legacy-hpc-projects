Project Summary

The project aims to ensure safe and protective learning environment with a good quality teaching environments and increase access and retention of drought and conflict affected and IDP children in South Central Somalia through an integrated package of school based emergency assistance.
The project sought to increase access and retention to education and will include establishment or rehabilitation of permanent and temporary learning spaces with gender friendly and inclusive WASH facilities ((gender-sensitive Latrines, water points, girl-friendly spaces), provision of school furniture, teacher incentives and teacher training in pedagogue skill, handling special education needs children and psychosocial support, inclusive, protective and socio-emotional teaching and learning, as well as training of Community Education Committees in school management and school safety and Preventing risks of exclusion based on Displacement and disability through advocating for the inclusion of IDPs and people living with disability in decision-making processes and removing barriers associated with accessing education services and facilities for children with disability by setting up a special unit in 5 targeted schools to cater for children with hearing and visual impairment.

Mitigation Measures

Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacementIn most cases IDPs are not included in decision-making processes and have problems accessing education facilities due to barriers associated with cost and distance to near-by schools and in most cases the lack of integration with the host communities. Scarcity of social services causes barriers for the IDPs to access these services. All programmatic and M&E activities will be delivered in partnership with the respective MoEs and in coordination with the Education Cluster. Mercy-USA, in collaboration with the MoEs, will undertake a needs assessment of hard to reach or disadvantaged areas in the regions to identify 20 emergency affected schools in greatest need, thus targeting the most vulnerable children, including IDPs and returnees. Mercy-USA will work with other clusters to maximize the use of the established Teaching learning spaces (TLS), eg. the health cluster members as screening and immunization center, food security cluster as food distribution center and Nutrition cluster as center for screening malnourished children. As an exit strategy CECs will be trained on resources mobilization, management and maintenance of TLS, while the community will be sensitized to take charge for the continuity of the schools. This will enable them manage schools in the absence of external support.Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and ageVulnerable persons experience a cycle of vulnerability and poverty, which exposes them to protection concerns, such as isolation, exploitation and abuse. Children trek long distance in search of water distraction their learning and exposing them to harm’s way. Distance between children’s’ home and the school forces them travel to and from school exposure to further risk of harm. In adequate infrastructure and preference by parent to have their children attend secular schools have made schools to shift the timing of classes to afternoon which compromises the children’s’ safety as classes end late thus giving children sufficient time to travel home before nightfall. Children are usually not cautious when handling sharp objects or during playtime thus may get hurt. Most schools lack properly function WASH facilities and privacy which exposes them to unhygienic condition and the girl-child avoid using communal latrines which may be detrimental to their health and the privacy of the girl thus leading to drop-out. Single mother headed who are faced by economic hardships, opportunity cost takes precedence over the children schooling since children are engaged in child labour to fend for the family. Mercy-USA proposes to scale up and complement its existing education intervention (funded by Educate a Child-EAC) with anticipated funds to improve retention and wellbeing, as well as boost enrollments. Classrooms will be constructed and ensure that the timing of classes are such that the timing of classes give children sufficient time to travel home before nightfall. Single female-headed households will be provided with incentive in the form of conditional cash grants and provision of sanitary kits, girl friendly education kits.
