Project Summary

The project will kick-start and maintain urgently needed protection interventions that can be life-saving to child and their families affected by the ElNino induced Drought, and contribute to restoring dignity of the affected population while ensuring access to safe and confidential child friendly reporting and counseling services. The funds will support activities aimed at protecting women and girls form the consequences of GBV on their health as well as ensuring reduction of exposure to negative coping mechanisms such as transactional sex and sexual exploitation and abuse. The interventions will contribute to mitigating risks of abuse, exploitation, violence and neglect of children. These activities include, provision of psychosocial support services, identification, registration, family tracing and reunification for separated children. Awareness and community education on child protection in emergencies will be prioritized so as to ensure children are safeguarded. The proposed interventions are aligned with the protection humanitarian response priorities and will be sustained through the long term development programs. the aim of the intervention is to Increase access to child friendly reporting and response services for children affected by humanitarian situations. 10, 000 children will be reached and provided with psycho social support and 3,500 adults will be reached with community awareness and education on child protection in emergencies.

Needs

Need to ensure that Child Protection Systems evolve to mainstream humanitarian response. The need is to ensure that children have access to child protection services in humanitarian situations.This includes the need to set up safe and confidential child friendly reporting mechanisms: a free phone 116. a safe reporting platform available 24 hours each day. The need will be set up mobile phone configured to call and text the 116 helpline placed in the communities to enhance reporting of child protection violations. The need is to set up of child friendly safe spaces and provision of therapeutic services including critical incident debrief. Need to strengthen community based structures to ensure sustainability of child protection initiatives.

Activities

Provision of free Child Helpline-including mobile helpline to increase access to child friendly reporting and counselling services. Set up of gazebos for mobile drop in centers; a child friendly safe space for children to access therapeutic services including counselling and psychosocial support. Documentation of all cases in line with the case management principles. Follow up of cases reported to ensure that children receive priority services according to the nature of concerns reported. Community awareness raising on child protection in emergencies to ensure that communities are able to sustain child protection initiatives. Training an capacity development of child protection workers including CCWs and child protection committee members as a way of enhancing their capacity to detect violations.
