Project Summary

This integrated project aims to improve access to quality education to and build resilience of the conflict affected boys and girls in the three selected LGAs especially the children in the Hard To Reach (HTR) areas of Kale Balge, Nganzai and Gwoza LGAs of Borno state.
The boys and girls aged between (3-17) will be provided the following:
• TLS with latrines fitted hand washing facilities and supplies
• Scholastic materials will be provided to New Arrivals or IDPs that have benefited from similar interventions in the past.
• Teachers and School Based Management Committee will be trained resilience and sustainable topics (which include psycho-social support, pedagogy, school/class management, learners protection and school maintenance).
The purpose of the project is to ensure that the children of school age in HTR areas and New Arrival IDPs are quickly given access to quality education and that the interventions are sustainable.

Needs

Due to the displacement caused by the ongoing conflict large populations are compelled to reside in limited spaces resulting in strain on education facilities - classrooms, latrines, teachers and teaching materials. This disruption of impacted the development of children within school age especially children in Hard to Reach (HTR) areas and among New Arrivals. In response to this, the project has identified the following needs:1. Inadequate learning space for children of school age. They either inadequate, damaged or occupied by IDPs2. Limited Latrines (segregated by sex) fitted with handwashing facilities and supplies3. Lack of learning supplies for children of New Arrivals of population in HTR areas of the LGAs4. Resilience skills for teachers needs to be refreshed or built for teachers that are yet to be training in the last one year.5. Lack of validated capacity for parents to adopt protection measures in fending for the education needs of their children.6. Limited participation of parent in the Education Life of your children especially the girl child.

Activities

1. Provision of TLS fitted with mats and blackboards2. Provision of Latrines fitted with handwashing kits and supplies3. Provision of scholastic materials for HTR children and New Arrivals IDPs4. Training of teachers on Pyscho-social support and positive disciplines5. Training of SMBCs on protection of learners and maintenance of school
