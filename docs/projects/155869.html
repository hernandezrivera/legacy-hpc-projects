Project Summary

Al Resala Foundation has been implemented projects since 2013, to provide multi a sector assistance for Conflict-Affected Communities in Northern Syria, the main office of AL Resala foundation is in Kilis with a new one now in Gaziantep.
The project will help people to save and improve living in the camp, and protect IDPs in winter from snow, cold, rain, flood and communicable disease. provide the minimum standard of assist for the camp. IDPs need support on firefighting promotion, special for Women.
The project will be six months’ duration as the followings:
• Invitation tenders, procurement's and contracting with service providers
• Providing services like raising the level of tents from the ground, Tent Installations, fire extinguishers and Solar water heating systems
• Training for firefighting and promotion about use stoves and fuel
• Monitoring and Evaluation by RF staff.

Needs

Based on the CCCM cluster track the displacement in northwest Syria, In 2018, this focus of CCCM Cluster’s displacement tracking activities has concentrated on the northwest of the country, which witnessed more than 1 million population movements since the last HNO, with many of these movements indicating the presence of IDPs that had been displaced multiple times. On average, the cluster tracked more than 96,000 monthly population movements compared to 82,000 the previous year and 40,000 in 2016 in north-west Syria. The main causes of these displacements were renewed military offensives in Idleb, Aleppo, Hama governorates (particularly in September-December 2017). Arrivals were also reported form Ar-Raqqa and Deir-ez-Zor governorates. Protection Risk Analysis

Risks and mitigation (Mitigating Measure) :Risk (1) of overcrowding living conditions and absence of adequate services might expose to risk vulnerable beneficiaries.Likelihood: high ,Impact : high ,Mitigating Measure : Ensure that adequate space is provided by family, Monitor the Shelter living conditions periodically after the completion of the rehabilitation , Raise awareness among the beneficiaries of the dangers of overcrowding, Ensure that a management system is in place for big collective shelters rehabilitation, Consider sphere standards when planning the response,and ensure that management are coordinating services with other actors to cover needs and gaps.Protection Risk/Threat: Eviction from shelter due to lack of approval from property owner and/or exposing beneficiaries at risk due to lack of HLP documentation for the shelter rehabilitated, Likelihood: high, Impact: high, Mitigating Measure:Abide by the HLP guidance document that was developed by the SNFI Cluster, Do not proceed with the Shelter rehabilitation unless with the consensus of the owner, proof of registered HLP ownership, verified ownership claims. If this won’t be available, feasible, refer to the HLP DD, Include HLP caveat into the MoUs or agreements signed ,and Have an HLP section in the M&E assessment form Risk (2): Outbreak of conflict prevents access to project locations, Likelihood of Risk Occurring : low, Effect on Expected Results : Very High, Risk Management Strategy/Mitigating Measure: Lesson learned from 2013 operations found that while conflict occurred, access could be restored within a few days. A cautious approach that plans for these type of delays is expressed in the project work plan.Risk (3) : Airplane shelling, Likelihood of Risk Occurring: medium, Effect on expected Results: Very High. Risk Management Strategy: The risk of Security situation (only heavy shelling) and The risk of the local council buildings being targeted by airstrikes are a very low probability according to The historical information for 7 years ago

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

RF developed Safety and Security Plan that governs operational activities from our base in Gaziantep Turkey, Aleppo and Idleb Offices; as well as protocols for travel, communications, and operations within Syria to let our staff work under above-mentioned conditions; as the staff is selected from people of the same region where we are working; RF works with coordination with local councils while that gives us more legitimacy in the ground and secure our work through the community engagement.RF work also in close coordination with International NGO Safety Organization (INSO) and relief offices of local councils, to share security accidents and recommendations.
