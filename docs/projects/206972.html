Project Summary

Solidarités International's (SI’s) integrated CCCM response is designed to address the unique needs of vulnerable populations residing in formal sites of Cabo Delgado, emphasizing in community ownership and self-governance aiming at creating a sustainable and self-reliant environment that meets essential living standards.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

SI's project aims to enhance the well-being and protection of displaced individuals residing in formal sites of Cabo Delgado, by ensuring effective coordination, community engagement, and information management. The project aims to empower the displaced community, promote self-governance, and facilitate a smooth transition towards more sustainable and durable solutions, while creating a supportive and inclusive environment that meets the immediate and their long-term needs. The key target groups encompass displaced populations and in some cases the host community (for social cohesion purposes) with particular attention to vulnerable households including the ones with persons with special needs (PWSN), women and children.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The project aims to establish and support Site Governance Structures based on Age, Gender, and Diversity (AGD) principles and to implement mechanisms that promote community engagement and participation. The project will collaborate with local authorities to coordinate and monitor humanitarian service delivery, addressing gaps and avoiding duplications. Relocation exercises will be conducted to demarcate plots and allocate shelters to IDP families. The project will create and/or maintain existing referral pathways to address physical, security, protection, and health risks are looked after by relevant partners. Regular recreational activities will also be organized to enhance community cohesion. In terms of effective information management the project will support the regular collection and sharing of statistical information on population movements. This involves head counting and registration exercises, quarterly facility mapping and household surveys, and monthly publication of site profiles and data trends. Capacity building and awareness campaigns on CCCM concepts, prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA), fraud, and deforestation impact will be provided on regular basis. Continuous monitoring and adaptive management mechanisms will enable real-time feedback from the community, fostering a safe and inclusive environment. This holistic approach seeks to empower displaced communities, ensure effective governance, and address their immediate and long-term needs sustainably.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The project prioritizes a comprehensive and proactive approach to identify and mitigate potential risks, particularly in the realm of protection. Anchored in a thorough protection risk analysis, among other approaches (lessons learnt from previous actions and observations) the project engages local communities actively, ensuring their voices shape interventions and addressing specific protection concerns. Protection risk assessments will be conducted in the beginning of the project and will be updated regularly throughout the project's lifecycle, enabling dynamic adjustments to emerging risks and maintaining a responsive stance. Continuous monitoring and adaptive management mechanisms will allow for real-time feedback from the community on protection risks or timely reporting of cases of misconduct aiming to foster an environment of safety and swift corrective action when needed. The project's commitment extends to close coordination with local authorities, humanitarian agencies, and protection-focused organizations, fostering a collaborative environment for early risk identification and collective response.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

Acknowledging the nuanced vulnerabilities and distinct needs of different genders and age groups, SI places paramount emphasis on fostering inclusivity, particularly for women, girls, and elderly individuals. Practical examples include the establishment of Women's and Elderly Committees within the community, providing dedicated platforms for these individuals to actively participate in decision-making processes related to project activities. These committees serve as forums where women and elderly persons can express their needs, contribute insights, and directly influence the design and implementation of interventions. The findings drive the design of adequate interventions that meet the needs and the preferences of the various concerned groups aiming at a safe, meaningful and inclusive service provision.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The project prioritizes inclusivity and endeavors to ensure equitable access to assistance for individuals with disabilities. The project will begin with an inclusive needs assessments, actively engaging disabled individuals and disability organisations to comprehensively understand the unique challenges faced by the most vulnerable groups. Collaboration with disability organizations will serve as a cornerstone, tapping into their expertise to identify and reach individuals with disabilities effectively. Based on the findings, the project will proactively adapt infrastructure when possible or share the information with other actors when adaptations are not possible by SI due to funding constraints or lack of technical expertise.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

SI is committed to minimizing its environmental impact through a proactive and comprehensive approach. Prior to initiation, the project will conduct an Environmental Impact Assessment to identify and address potential risks, ensuring compliance with environmental considerations. Sustainability is at the forefront, with an emphasis on utilizing eco-friendly materials in maintenance and temporary shelters, prioritizing local availability to reduce transportation emissions and bolster the local economy. Environmental education will serve as a proactive strategy, fostering awareness within the camp's population about the significance of environmental conservation. The establishment of robust waste management systems encompassing recycling initiatives and proper disposal methods will alleviate the project's impact on the surrounding environment. This will also empower residents to partake in sustainable practices, thereby contributing to a reduced environmental footprint. The adoption of energy-efficient practices, such as solar-powered installations, will significantly reduce the project's carbon footprint, fostering sustainability and aligning with the broader commitment to environmental responsibility in humanitarian endeavors. The conscientious conservation of water resources will prompt the implementation of water-saving technologies and infrastructure to minimize consumption and preserve local water sources. Adaptive management mechanisms will be concurrently instituted, ensuring real-time monitoring and the ability to promptly address unforeseen environmental risks or changes in the local ecology.
