Project Summary

We are working with children that are displaced by violence and currently residing in the islands, including unaccompanied children and children at risk of forcible recruitment for that reasen we will provide psychosocial support to the children through educational recreational activities focused in the IDPs and activities to prevent young people radicalization/Identification of risk factors for youth and children/Training of activists in the area of protection and gender to identify cases and train the community/Gender dynamics for vulnerable women in training activities/Dynamics focused on men to understand the situation of women and their rights with the help of religious and community leaders/ One CFS in Ibo Two CFS adapted in the two IDP concentrarions in Matemo and one in Quirambo/promoting the green line and Implementing other reporting system with the goverment and the protection cluster for the places where ther is no telephone network. With this activities we will strenghten referral pathways for at risk children,youth and women, strenghten capacity of authorities to provide protection services as well as the community and religious leaders. Through these programs you also mitigate community tensions between host community and IDPs.
