Project Summary

Conflict, drought, flooding and COVID 19 are some of the shocks that have driven displacement. Majority of the displaced population have settled in over 2,400 substandard and un planned IDP sites in urban, semi urban and rural areas more especially in Baidoa, Kismayo and Berdale districts. Majority of these IDPs are living in precarious conditions with many lacking the basic needs. Another major challenge that has been noted from the recent assessment done by the CCCM cluster has identified lack of access to land and weak land tenure system, land disputes, weak judicial system to have largely contributed to the vulnerability of IDPs. This has led to frequent incidents of forceful evictions pushing IDPs into worsened situations requiring both emergency and prolonged assistance.
WRRS will set up in the targeted settlement mobile site management teams for identification, selection, implementation, coordination and for monitoring purposes. Capacity building of the key players in the CCCM on the guiding principles, which will later lead to the formation of a special committee with representation from all the sectors. This will entail Basic training on internationally recognized CCCM training modules and complementary training/workshops on other relevant themes filling gaps identified through assessments Provision of the solar light lamps/ touch to the 24000 beneficiaries (both men and women)identified and registered in the targeted regions. Through the provision of multi-purpose cash transfer address basic site repair and improvements that respond to safety and physical risk concerns. Through cash-for-work, pre-identified skilled and unskilled IDPs are able to repair and maintain basic infrastructures that underpin a safe and risk-free environment. Where possible the organization will embark on settlement de congestion. Provide technical assistance and guidance in establishing or reinforcing existing community representative/committee structures as necessary (including women’s representation) Support and help facilitate IDP governance meetings. Support and help facilitate site coordination meetings with relevant partner’s/service providers. HLP awareness raising interventions to 4000 beneficiaries both men and women on duty bearers on HLP, this will be achieved with the assistance of the formed committee groups.

1. - Projects activities

The most vulnerable settlements in Baidoa,Berdale,Kismayo and Afmadow Districts with large numbers of needy IDPs, including those with recent arrivals who lack services, will be selected. 15 Settlement planning trainings will be conducted for 400 participants and will target community leaders, elders and local authority leaders and equip them with skills that will facilitate participatory management and maintenance of public spaces and fire breaks in the future. The site plan will be developed for the targeted settlements to provide spaces for essential/basic infrastructure services, public/communal spaces, fire breaks, main streets, lighting system and housing. This will be done in close consultation with the CCCM cluster, benefiting women, girls, boys and men, local and central authorities, religious and traditional leaders.In addition, WRRS will conduct 6 coordination meetings during the entire project period, which will include partners working in the same area. Each meeting will consist of 15 participants from different organization. The purpose of these coordination meetings is to avoid any duplication, sharing information and referral mechanisms. Mapping exercise will be conducted to find out the existing resources/infrastructures in the targeted settlements to identify gaps and inform responses in the target 4 districts, which will be shared with CCCM cluster and will be useful in creating master list, which will then be posted online and will be used by different humanitarian partners for data collection and their response analysis. This will enhance the strategic thinking of all stakeholders in terms of needs and gaps in particular settlement. Through this mapping exercises, it will be easy for all stakeholders to plan their activities related to particular settlement. In total, 6 mapping exercises will be conducted in Kismayo, Afmadow, Berdale and Baidoa to evaluate the existing capacity of resources. Community committees will be formed at settlement level to invigorate the existing community structures. Through these committees, coordination at field level will improve and access to information will be easy. These community committee member will be trained on information gathering and sharing. There will be 4W matrix, which will be shared by respective cluster coordinators, which will mainstream information sharing at all levels. Standard Operation Procedures will be adopted for each settlement in terms of information and coordination. Settlement leaders will have information on the figures of in and out, how many new IDPs have come to specific settlement and how many have left the settlement. Capacity Building training will be provided to existing settlement leaders to have improve access to information and accountability to affected populations. Through this training, settlement leaders (community committee members) will have understanding on counting IDPs in and out figures per settlement. Member from local authorities will also be benefited through participating in these training for information sharing (like 4W matrix) and coordination at settlement level. 6 Community centers will be established/constructed at settlement level, which will be utilized for community meetings, training etc.These centers will also be used for focus group discussions and will have multipurpose outputs. The center will improve communication by creating a safe space where the community can meet and discuss their day to day affairs, resolve disputes, and share ideas. Furthermore, the center will also be utilized to strengthen referral path ways (HLP/GBV/CP), relay messages to the community through structured information sessions on HLP, and counsel HLP violation cases. WRRS will also establish a feedback and complaint mechanism that will be accessible to all the community members.

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

The location of the projects will be selected carefully to ensure that beneficiaries are not exposed to any form of violence. The organization is well versed with the risks associated with SEA and therefore will work closely with allthe stakeholders to ensure that the beneficiaries are not exposed to any form of sexual exploitation and especially during the delivery of project activities. Awareness raisingon how to report the incidences of SEA will be done to all the organization stakeholders that is the staff, contractors and other stakeholders involved with the activities of the organization. A reporting mechanism for PSEA will be established to look into any issues related to Sexual exploitation during and even after the end of the project. . WRRS will ensure that all groups of beneficiaries are involved from the onset of projects, normally in Somalia people from marginalized clans and women have been left out in key decision making process and mostly they are not consulted. This is not going tohappen for this project as WRRS will ensure that all the minority and marginalized persons will be involved in major decision making of the project. Women will be given priority and especially those from the marginalized clans. Persons living with disability will also be included in the project activities as in many instances they have been left out. WRRS will work closely with the land owners and the local government in instances where there will be land disputes as Somalia is well known of this kind of disputes. The organization will work with the authorities and more especially in areas where construction is anticipated to secure land tenure. In areas where WRRS work cases of explosives are rare although measures will be put in place to prevent the risk of explosive hazards in the target area.
