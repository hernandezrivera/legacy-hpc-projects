Project Summary

The purpose of this project is to protect the health of conflict victims (Internally displaced people (IDPs) and host communities) living in poor environment due to the armed conflict that broke out in northern , Ethiopia Tigray, Amhara and Afar in November 2020, by providing emergency assistance in regard to water sanitation, health and nutrition.
Project Activities: Rehabilitation of existing water supply systems, Implementation of water management committee training, Distribution of basic hygiene product, Installation of Washrooms, Implementation of health promotion training (including COVID-19 prevention)

3.6 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members using age and gender appropriate, safe and confidential channels?

This project will incorporate protection principles and promote meaningful access, safety and dignity in all phases of the project, by practicing Humanitarian Program Accountability Frame (PAF) with its four major pillars; information sharing, active participation, consultation, compliant handling and feedback mechanism. Accordingly, the project is designed by engaging community members through their representatives such as community leaders and religious leaders, concerned government bodies and other partners. During startup of the program, information about the program, beneficiary selection criteria, and information on their entitlement, the beneficiary’s right to raise any complaints and complaining mechanisms will be provided for beneficiaries and will continue to be communicated to promote transparency. Location, design, safety, appropriateness and convenience of water and sanitation facilities will be decided in consultation with the users particularly women, adolescent girls, elderly and persons with disabilities. Open public meetings and public information boards will be used to share information. Towards employing the DO NO HARM principle prior to implementing project activities a risk assessment will be conducted to ensure beneficiaries and participants do not incur further harm and an action will be taken to mitigate any resulting from an activity being implemented by this project.
