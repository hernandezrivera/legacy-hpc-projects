Project Summary

The project goal is to improve the well-being and self-reliance of refugees and host communities in five states of Sudan. This will be achieved through the provision of integrated, multi-sectorial services including, protection, WASH,.
The overall objective. In WASH African Relief Committee (ARCO) will implement water supply, sanitation and hygiene promotion with NFI distribution, environmental health activities.
In protection (General, child and GBV) ARCO will strive to ensure equitable access to essential services for prevention, mitigation and response (PMR) to protection needs for PoCs. The project will strengthen community engagements for PMR to protection risks against PoC while ensuring change in their attitudes and contribution towards reducing the risks, promoting protective environment and provision of appropriate and quality multi-sectoral services. All PoC, including people with special need and attention, will meaningfully participate /play a central role in community activities and decision-makings on an equal basis with others and while co-existing peacefully with neighboring host communities. Strengthening of community protection structures, promotion of Communication with Communities, strengthening the Community- Based Complaints Mechanisms (CBCMs), ensuring transparency and Accountability to Affected Persons (AAP), reducing the risk of GBV, as well as Engaging Men in Accountable practices (EMAP), Mental Health and Psychosocial Support (MHPSS) are core activities. Protection minimum standards, SoPs and guidelines will be adhered to as well as clear mechanisms for accountability. Child safeguarding policies, PSEAH, Code of Conduct, Anti-Fraud and Anti- Corruption standards will be adhered to while implementing protection interventions. Age, Gender, Diversity sensitive programming will be enhanced to ensure equal and meaningful access to services by diverse groups, disability inclusion is key.
For S/NFI, Alight will monitor situations; preparing hotspot areas and identifying vulnerable communities, and raise awareness of the communities on hazards. We regularly coordinate with COR, HAC, UNHCR and other key partners & exchange information on existing humanitarian situations. Whenever emergency occurs, ARCO will trigger joint need assessment (with state level stakeholders), conduct joint verification exercises and respond to the needs. Prioritization and targeting will be done through cluster conventional processes. Activities that are in line with cluster objective and relevant to the locality culture and traditions are carefully selected and implemented.
Monitoring and evaluation will continue to be reinforced at all levels while people of concern will be engaged at different levels to ensure they remain informed on planned action and any related change and their voices are heard and reflected in implementation and project design.

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

ARCO uses the AGD approach throughout the project design and implementation, especially during beneficiary selection and data monitoring. ARCO follows the IASC Guidelines for Integrating Gender-Based Violence Interventions in Humanitarian Action, the IASC Guidelines for Inclusion of Persons with Disability in Humanitarian Action, the Child Protection Minimum Standards in Humanitarian Action and the Sphere Handbook. ARCO commits to ensure meaningful access across the age categories and vulnerability scenarios, with particular regard for pregnant women/new mothers and infants, older and severely ill and people with disability. In addition, while selecting suitable location for the services, the voices of the vulnerable groups will be prioritized to address barriers related to discrimination and access due to distance. The project will strengthen community-based protection structures and ensure synergies with all the sectors involved, to prioritize safety & dignity and avoid causing harm. In particular, WASH sector will create safe and friendly structures; easily accessible and accommodating to individuals with special needs and appropriate for GBV prevention. Alight will ensure representation of both women, girls, men and girls in community structures including community health, nutrition and WASH volunteers. ARCO also participates in the Sudan Protection Cluster, GBV and CP sub-clusters and local working group to ensure coordination, networking, sharing and learning of best practices, advocacy and protection monitoring. Finally, ARCO ensures accountability through the presence of an inclusive FCRM.

4.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will support Inter-Agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including Sexual exploitation and abuse complaints?

ARCO follows the AAP approach, supporting community participation, feedback and ensuring transparency about the interventions. This is to ensure the project decisions and actions are responsive to the expressed priorities, needs, capacities and views of all persons of concern irrespective of Age, Gender, Diversity and Disability backgrounds. ARCO will ensure a balanced boys, girls, women and men in all project activities with relative 60% female and 40% male. Data for beneficiaries will be disaggregated by gender, age groups and location, as the project activities are tailored for different groups in line with developmental stages, needs and vulnerabilities and basing on relevant parameters appropriate in reverence to heightened protection risks of the persons of concern. Activities like awareness raising and dialogues will be conducted in women and child/youth safe spaces and in the communities ARCO participates in the PSEA Network in Sudan, attending meetings and supporting assessments and coordination with other UN agencies and other relevant international INGOs. ARCO has a PSEAH Policy, Guidelines for Sudan, Child Protection Policy and Code of Conduct and it is undergoing a revision of the PSEAH Implementation structure, ensuring accessibility to Feedback and Complaint Response Mechanism to all the beneficiaries and capacity building for staff, partners and suppliers or contractors about SEAH standards. The PSEAH Implementation structure in ARCO follows the Six IASC Core Principles and the principles of anti-discrimination, accountability, safety security and protection and survivor-centered approach, ensuring the respect of survivor’s will and confidentiality in every step of the reporting and responding process. Different communication channels are available for the beneficiaries (hot line, front desk, suggestion boxes, email...) and confidential data of SEAH incidents will be shared to other agencies or donors based on pre-existing agreements. Under the project, ARCO will conduct Safeguarding risk assessments to mitigate any potential or identified risks of SEA. ARCO will ensure Zero Tolerance for SEAH. Jointly with the PSEA task force, ARCO will develop relevant IEC materials and distribute it in communities, raise awareness on SEAH and ensure appropriate measures are undertaken for investigations. As part of AAP, ARCO will ensure to inform community representatives for planned action at the beginning of project and will remain informed on any change during project implementation, this include awareness raising on referral mechanism and limitations in referrals as per referral SoP. Through MEAL , ARCO will collect community feedback on satisfaction and suggested areas of improvements, where necessary ARCO will investigate the complaints and actions will be taken to address the gaps while the collected information will be used for future project design.
