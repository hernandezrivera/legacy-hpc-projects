Project Summary

Several years ago, primary health care (PHC) was successfully established in Lebanon as an essential approach for the delivery of the Health for all, policy of the World Health Organization (WHO), this approach has increased the accessibility to health care in most regions in Lebanon. However, there are still a considerable number of people for whom this is not yet a reality.
Today, Lebanon is facing a huge crises , starting from the COVID 19 outbreak, to struggling in accessing clean water and electricity , to running out of flour , fuel , medicine , paramedics, baby milk ,struggling in transportation , and obstacles in acquiring hospital rooms .Similarly, all health facilities such as hospitals , dispensaries , primary health cares , pharmacies are all in danger, they are running out of electricity due to the lack of fuel, lack of paramedics and medicine. Let’s not forget about the enormous immigration of the medical staff and the economic crisis. Home based health care center aims to help the vulnerable Lebanese and migrant’s population.
Moreover, from the health point of view, Lebanon is facing a major health challenge from the escalating costs of health services, There is a continuing trend to shorten hospital stay or patients staying at home because they can’t afford it; increased in both burden communicable and non-communicable diseases, an ageing population, thus make the vulnerable and underprivileged groups will be even more deprived.
Let’s not forget about the global outbreak COVID -19, this pandemic is still a major threat both to physical and mental health and to the well-being of entire societies.
Today 1,086,106 have recovered from covid-19, unfortunately little to know about their health status if they are suffering from impairment on independence or from any disabilities that affect their life.
Vulnerable Lebanese and migrants have the right to receive the health care needed , but unfortunately in Lebanon , specifically in Tripoli , patients can’t afford the payment of private hospital and the government hospital are always full of patients.
Therefore, there is an urgent need to extend health services beyond hospitals, Tripoli arteries Association (TAA) is intended to establish an integrated system of care designed to meet the health needs of individuals in their local settings.
It includes primary prevention, i.e. Immunization; secondary prevention, i.e, wound management; and tertiary prevention, i.e. management of chronic diseases, rehabilitation and terminal care.
The goals of home based health care services are to help individuals to improve function and live with greater independence; to promote the patient’s optimal level of well-being; and to assist the patient to remain at home, avoiding hospitalization or admission to long-term care institutions, and Contribute to morbidity and mortality reduction.
Home health care services can be delivered to individuals of all ages with acute illnesses, long-term health conditions, permanent disabilities,limited mobility , COVID 19 cases or terminal illnesses.
To achieve the goal of the home health care:
• Make community assessment to identify people with disability, mothers, children, the elderly, the poor and minority groups.
• Managing the consequences of illness by meeting the needs of those who are in needs of care.
• Serving the needs of the vulnerable and underprivileged by reaching out to them and meeting their health needs.
• Dahr AlMogher Primary health care will assign health personnel, nurses, midwives, physicians, clerical; drivers and administrative workers to manage the homes visit, accompanied with necessary supplies and equipment.
• Monitoring and evaluation will be ongoing with the support of health information system .
Home health care services is crucial for addressing the challenge posed by public health problems particularly to those in the greatest need by establishing a center that integrate into PHC structure

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

Objective 1: Lebanon is a poor country, more than 70% of their population is under the poverty line and are vulnerable citizens, in addition to migrant population, and governments have limited levers that they can pull to help prevent or slow the health threats. It is critical that vulnerable groups receive life-saving assistance, The funds ensure that home care center in Dahr al Mogher PHC can continue to provide life-saving services to thousands of people in Tripoli, by Improving health conditions for vulnerable Lebanese and migrant population suffering from disabilities and limited mobility.The funds will enable home health care to remain open and run critical lifesaving services; especially that COVID-19 is still putting pressure on healthcare system.More than 170 countries now have a COVID-19 preparedness and response plan based on the 10 operational and technical pillars, particularly pillar 7 had defined the case management, clinical operations, and therapeutics of covid 19, approximately 15% of patients with covid-19 develop severe disease that requires oxygen support, and 5% have critical disease with complications such as respiratory failure, acute respiratory distress syndrome. Therefore, there is an urgent need to extend health services beyond hospitals, this is particularly crucial for addressing the challenge posed by public health problems and economic crisis particularly to those in the greatest need by establishing home based health care services .

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

Lebanon is facing the most toughest challenges at all level starting from Health ,transportation, corruption , security , economic crisis to currency collapse , that affecting the entire regions of Lebanon. Thus, certain individuals, groups or communities, face greater marginalization, inequality and violence than others, Such as people living in existing humanitarian emergency settings, Women and adolescent ,girls, Children, older people Persons with disabilities, patient with COVID 19, migrants, and health care workers who are predominantly women . This challenges had seen a rise in Gender-Based Violence GBV and sexual exploitation and abuse of PSEA , a pattern that has been observed in Lebanon . Home health care center will coordinate with trained SGBV focal points and will Identify and refer GBV and PSEA cases to health providers and clinical management of rape services,promote key messages on SGBV and PSEA for women and and girls,ensure all staff and volunteers are trained

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

The escalating of both burden of communicable and non-communicable disease, increase the disability and mobility rate , According to WHO 15% of patients with covid19 are suffering from post COVID-19 condition characterized by impairment on independence, Hence, the importance of establishing home health care center to improve the quality of life for vulnerable Lebanese and migrant population. Therefore, we launched a strategy plan to start the home care center: Listening and talking to communities, is a fundamental part of our association. Establish Call Center to respond to specific needs. Ensure accountability to integrate the home health care project into the countries’ strategies and programs. Evaluate and recruit staff inductions, performed managements, training, partnership agreements, and highlight reporting, design, monitor and evaluate the goals and objectives of programs with the involvement organization and populations.
