Project Summary

Through the proposed interventions HIHFAD is planning to continue the provision of
general and specialized health services through a network of its facilities into a
a comprehensive module including Primary health services with 2 primary health centers
and secondary health services with 4 hospitals 1 of them care about women and child
health while the rest 3 hospitals will be general hospitals. The mentioned facilities are also
putting into consideration the easy access to people with disabilities through the prepared
the infrastructure of those facilities to make sure of full access. in addition, HIHFAD will
continue operating 3 rehabilitation centers to enhance the quality of life to people with
disabilities by the provision of physiotherapy sessions and a solid referral mechanism to
health care points if needed. Adding to the previous interventions HIHFAD is focusing from
a comprehensive perspective on providing specialized health services connected to the
hospitals and outpatient services such as a dialysis unit in Armanaz Hospital and operating a Tuberculosis center which is the only of its type in Idleb governorate. MHPSS services will be connected
to each facility as a complementary component to make sure of covering the health needs
of the people within the targeting areas

4 - Needs

The fragile health system in Syria continues to face concurrent emergencies and chronic challenges which affect the availability and quality of health services across Syria, as well as the physical and mental wellbeing of the population. In 2022, it is critical to ensure investments in health service delivery are not lost: shortages in resources risk contraction of health access at a time when the COVID 19 pandemic is ongoing, other communicable diseases are on the rise, treatment access barriers persist, household-level vulnerability is increasing, and worries about insufficient income to pay for urgent health needs rank 3rd among household concerns. Essential inclusive health services – such as sexual and reproductive health and safe delivery, child health – including routine and expanded immunization; care for communicable and non-communicable diseases; mental health and psychosocial support; emergency services – including trauma and referral; and physical rehabilitation – must be maintained at every level of the health system and ensured to avoid negative outcomes in population mortality and morbidity. Community health services, including outreach and engagement on integrated health messages and evidence-based behavior change interventions, remain an essential component of comprehensive health services. Finally, protection of health care and provision of mental health and psychosocial support for health workers remain critical priorities.2022 SYRIA HNO

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

HIHFAD will make sure that the individuals of vulnerable groups are always accompanied by either their own caregivers or by pre-defined facility staff, so such individuals are additionally protected against any type of harassment or abuse while at the supported facilities. The facilities will be ensured to be well lighted and ventilated and the electric outlets will be rendered either high enough or provided with protecting means, so they are inaccessible to children. HIHFAD will ensure the privacy of the patients, where special attention will be paid to ensure the availability of safe and comfortable examination settings that supports the patient’s privacy. Furthermore, sex-separated toilets will be ensured available as well.To promote the protection environment for the presented beneficiaries, HIHFAD plans to conduct training on psychological first aid for the first-line humanitarian workers that are relevant to the proposed intervention. In addition, HIHFAD will ensure the availability of mhGAP and PSS as gender-focused services integrated into both proposed Health facilities. regarding COVID-19 Spread in NW of Syria during the project implementation HIHFAD focused to provide the protection for both staff and beneficiaries and the mitigations were - Canceling group activities or reducing participant numbers. - Using online platforms for meetings and training. - train staff on IPC and provide PPE kits - worth to mention that all HIHFAD staff are vaccinated by SIG teams


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Considering the high vulnerability of the project beneficiaries, HIHFAD provides allthe hired staff an induction training on the protection principles, PSEA and GBV sothey become oriented enough to provide the services in a dignified and safe wayfor the beneficiaries. A focal point for PSEA and GBV would be appointed perfacility. In addition, the services will be provided on a free-of-charge basis for allthose in need regardless of their individual gender groupings. 11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

below the potential risks and the mitigation depending on the area situation 1-Attacks onthe civilian population and health infrastructure, which may result in the destruction of thefacility or the death of the project staff. Possibility: Low Management: Review and adjustsecurity measures on a daily basis Participation in the UN de-confection mechanism:communicating and pinning project sites as independent neutral humanitarian locations,in order to prevent bombing by international actors. Emphasis on neutrality, independence,and the adherence to humanitarian principles and IHL 2-Intensification of the conflict,leading to delays in implementation, due to access restrictions Possibility: LowManagement: Temporary suspension of project activities and minimization of staffmovement Adjustment of time and the activity plan 3-Drastic changes in the context ofthe Syrian conflict, such as extensive changes in the front lines, leading to massexpulsions and/or access restrictions Possibility: Low Management: - Provision ofemergency plans e.g. through the deployment of personnel in mobile clinics in the eventof a sudden IDP influx - Constant and accurate context analysis and situation assessmentby the security focal point of the project partner HIHFAD 4- interruption of WHO supply line-Temporary closure of the border crossing between Turkey and Syria for humanitarianaid, leading to delays in implementation. Possibility: low management: - Adjustment oftime and activity plan
