Project Summary

Intervention:
One of the outcomes of the Syrian crisis is the emigration of young people and expertise labor, which created a need for some professions in society and the displacement of many families from Eastern Ghouta and several governorates in Syria, to Qutayfah being a safe area, where the number of incoming families reached 2,200 families, and this has adversely affected the economic and livelihood of the host community, the population of which population is 6000 families
Description:
Vocational training courses will be conducted for 200 families with an average of 20 benefiting families, whether the woman is the head of the family or a member of her family; each to be trained as to fulfil the following criteria:
fixed standards:
1. Female-headed households
2. Motivation and ability to work
3. Age between 18 and 50 years
4. No family member has benefited from another livelihood program
5. Families that have sufficient experience in the food industry and lost their work tools such as (dairy industry and municipal ghee) as a result of the crisis
Variable criteria:
6. A family member with special needs
7. They have two or more children under the age of 18
8. Families with one or more elderly people (over 60 years old)
9. The families that do not have more than one stable source of income , taking into account the dependency ratio
10. The families whose children were suspended from education due to the crisis
* The beneficiary must meet 6 or more of the above criteria noting that the first five criteria are fixed
The beneficiary fulfills the first five fixed criteria?: Yes/No or No/Acceptable
If the beneficiary meets the first five fixed criteria, then moves to the variable criteria below, thus targeting the families that score more, as needed:
Changing criteria:
Standard 6:1-0
Criterion 7:1-0
Criterion 8:1-0
Criterion 9: 1-0
Criterion 10: 1-0
The result
Improving knowledge of livelihood activities and their application and new technologies in the field of livelihoods by:
1- Obtaining the required approvals
2- Dissemination of the project through social media and posters in vital areas that include sufficient information
3- Receiving requests and specifying the place of review. The beneficiary will fill in a special form for the profession. With this form, we can conduct a paper filtering based on the criteria.
4- Home visits to collect detailed information about the family's situation that serves as the baseline information for the family (expenses - income - coping strategies)
5- Conducting vocational training courses (opening training centers or contracting with ready-made training centers)
6- Distributing tools and equipment to the successful people in vocational training courses (the successful ones will be evaluated by trainers and supervisors within specific criteria)
7- The ability to grant a certificate certified by the Craftsmen Union
8- Follow-up visits (the beneficiaries will be followed up one week after receiving the tools and equipment to ensure that they have started work, provide them with moral support, and conduct periodic visits for a period of six months)
9- The final evaluation will be conducted to know the impact of the project on the beneficiaries (collecting finish line information)
The trainings targeted by the project are:

boys from 18 to 40 years
Target:
Improving income through the acquisition of knowledge in livelihood related activities and the application of this new knowledge or technologies in the field of livelihoods

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

The target area is 100% safe, so the project does not pose any danger to humanitarian workers. As for the beneficiaries and their protection from gender-based violence, the project targets both genders at an equal rate, 50% of the trainings scheduled to be held target females and 50% target males, and the community has a culture of respect at work between the two sexes without prejudice. In addition, the project workers must carry out a special code of conduct that warns against exploitation and sexual harassment and child protection. In addition, the project aims to end child labor among beneficiary families.

11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumption: all beneficiaries will get the necessary experience during the training - the beneficiaries start working based on the experiences gained during the training phase and the provided equipment bag - the beneficiary’s ability to practice the profession in all parts of the governorateRisks: Some beneficiaries have limited ability to acquire new experiences (flexibility - interaction) - the beneficiary does not employ the experience gained at work and sell the tools - difficulty in accessing and preventing the beneficiaries from workingProbability: Low - Low - LowImpact: high - medium - mediumThe means to reduce the risk: the home visits carried out by the team to ensure the eligibility of the beneficiaries, in addition to subjecting the beneficiary in the training center to a simple acceptance test - the professions were chosen based on a deep analysis of the needs of the local market during the evaluation period and the possibility of changing the results are weak, and job opportunities in The city is large, and the team will make periodic follow-up visits to ensure that the beneficiary is motivated to practice work - at the end of each training, the beneficiaries will be given an experience certificate certified by the Federation of Craftsmen in Latakia, and the beneficiaries do not need licenses to practice the chosen professions
