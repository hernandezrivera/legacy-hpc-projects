Project Summary

The aim of SECD annual plan for 2022-23 is to support IDP resilience and social cohesion between host community and IDP families in camps through conducting certified vocational trainings on basic camp infrastructure rehabilitation, skill trainings for future jobs, roads rehabilitation in camp's communities that host the IDPs.
SECD is planning to engage more with Early Recovery Cluster as needs are exacerbating and the need to implement activities with sustainable impact is obligatory.
Vocational and soft skill trainings will be conducted and dedicated kits will be distributed for 3000 trainees for self maintenance of basic infrastructure in their community/IDP camps.
30000 meters of roads rehabilitation will be implemented over the proposed interventions for the next 2 years, considering CFW modality to generate work opportunities.

4 - Needs

The continues suffering and no root solutions have resulted in exacerbating the needs in north west of Syria. Despite the multi-sector intervention, the needs are still increaseing in the absence of sustainable interventions.The need to increase resilience of IDPs and Host community is a must in which, aid-relaince is obvious and is affecting their daily living to cover the need. Jabal Saman, Jisr Al-shoughour and Idleb districts are ones of the top districts that have high intensity of IDP camps and IDP population. The camp residents are continually suffering from lack of livelihood opportunity to generate income and cover essential needs. Based on the Humanitarian Needs Overview 2022, there are almost 14 million people in need across Syria territories (around 19% increase from last year). 4.6 million of them are residing Idleb and Aleppo governorates in which 950,000 are in need for ERL and ERL with multi sectoral activities.

5 - Implementation of activities

For 2022 and 2023, SECD will focus on increasing resilience and supporting cohesion between IDPs and IDP camp residents and Host Community. Campaign trainings by professional trainers and experts will be conducted for 3000 individuals in 3 districts of Jabal Saman, Idleb and Jisr Al-shoughour.Each campaign will include vocational and soft skill trainings in addition, to distribution of career kit.The selection of trainees will be based on a selection criterial that will be developed with the trainers to make sure most of the possible participants are able to start/continue their career with proficiency with certificate of completion. The campaign will be designed with the trainers and will ensure effective participation from local councilsSoft skill training will be received after the vocational training as a part of the campaign.Based on the selected vocational training interests, SECD will identify what kits to be delivered after successfully completing the training. Vocational trainings on WASH infrastructure maintenance in IDP camps will recive a kit that they can do the job with it.SECD will ensure half of the trainings will benefit the host community and the other half are IDPs. 30000 meters of roads will be rehabilitated within 29 community mainly:- the roads linked between IDP camps and the community.- Within community center affected roads.Lightening will be considered with small led solar lights.
