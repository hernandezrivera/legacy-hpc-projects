Project Summary

Displacement-affected populations, compelled to flee due to conflict, natural disasters, or other severe disruptions, often face the significant challenge of losing crucial legal and civil documentation. The absence of such documentation effectively strips individuals of their recognized identity, legal status, and access to essential services, thereby exacerbating their already difficult circumstances. This project strives to provide a comprehensive and sustainable framework designed to address the documentation gaps afflicting displacement-affected populations, with the overarching goal of restoring and exercise their rights and enabling them to rebuild their lives with dignity.
Legal and civil documentation is foundational to an individual's identity, serving as the gateway to accessing healthcare, education, social protection, financial opportunities, and legal recourse. In the disturbances of crisis, however, these critical documents are frequently lost or destroyed, launching individuals into a state of legal and social invisibility. The absence of proper documentation not only marginalizes these individuals but also exposes them to heightened risks of exploitation, discrimination, and abuse, as they are unable to assert their rights, secure property, or access basic services. This project aims to mitigate these risks, recognizing that the restoration of legal identity is not merely an administrative formality but a vital component of humanitarian response and recovery.
This initiative is not limited to addressing immediate needs but aspires to serve as a catalyst for systemic transformation. It seeks to empower displaced individuals by offering a suite of legal support services, including informational sessions on rights, personalized legal counselling, and direct legal assistance. To ensure financial constraints do not impede access to justice, the project provides financial support to cover the costs associated with legal processes. Additionally, the project facilitates access to civil documentation, such as birth certificates, and national identification cards, through targeted support, guidance, and financial aid.
A critical aspect of the project involves establishing a comprehensive referral network that connects individuals to protection services and other vital resources, including shelter, healthcare, and psychosocial support. By providing accurate and timely information on available services, the project enhances the resilience and protection of vulnerable populations. To ensure lasting impact, the project places a strong emphasis on capacity building and advocacy. It seeks to equip local authorities and humanitarian organizations with the necessary skills and knowledge to prioritize legal documentation in crisis response, while also advocating for policy reforms that enhance access to documentation for displaced populations at national and international levels. The project incorporates rigorous monitoring and evaluation mechanisms to assess its effectiveness, guide continuous improvement, and contribute to the development of global best practices in the field.
The ultimate objective of the project is to restore legal identity, thereby enabling individuals to access essential services, fully exercise their rights, and strengthen their resilience.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

This project aims to restore legal identity and documentation for displacement affected populations. It will provide comprehensive legal support, including information, counselling, and financial assistance, to help individuals reclaim their identity, access essential services, and exercise their rights. A vulnerability assessment will be used to identify the specific needs of various groups, ensuring that support is tailored to those most at risk.Recognizing that the ability to generate income is intricately linked to legal and civil documentation, the project targets individuals who may face additional barriers due to the loss of essential documents. This approach ensures that assistance is directed towards those who are not only grappling with the post effects of crises but also confronting challenges in rebuilding their lives and securing their rights.The project aligns with the principle of inclusivity, striving to leave no one behind. It acknowledges the diverse needs of different demographic groups and tailors its assistance to empower individuals to regain their identities, access essential services, and generate livelihoods for their households. This targeted approach reflects a commitment to holistic and sustainable support for the most vulnerable members of crisis-affected communities

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The project prioritizes activities in Cabo Delgado Province, focusing on areas like Metuge, Mueda, Mocimboa da Praia, Meluco, Muidumbe, Nangade, Quissanga and Pemba, where displacement-affected populations are concentrated. Key activities include providing documentation and legal support services through mobile legal clinics, giving displaced individuals direct access to civil documentation such as birth certificates and national IDs. Informational sessions will educate communities about their legal rights, documentation requirements, and application processes. Individual legal counselling sessions will address specific documentation issues, and the project will work closely with local authorities to streamline these processes and reduce barriers.A referral network will be established to connect individuals with protection services, healthcare, shelter, and psychosocial support, strengthening overall resilience and protection. Additionally, the project emphasizes capacity building and advocacy, training local authorities and partner organizations to prioritize legal documentation in humanitarian responses. It will also advocate for policy reforms at both national and local levels to improve documentation access for displaced populations

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The proposed project identifies potential risks and seeks to avoid causing harm by conducting thorough risk assessments at every stage of its implementation. The project team will work with local experts, community leaders, and protection specialists to identify and assess risks, ensuring that interventions are context-specific and sensitive to the vulnerabilities of affected populations. A comprehensive vulnerability assessment will be used to map the specific needs and risks of individuals, ensuring that no one is left behind and that assistance is provided equitably and without exacerbating existing inequalities.In addition, the project will prioritize safeguarding measures to protect participants, from potential risks such as exploitation, gender-based violence, and discrimination. Adopting a rights-based approach and implementing monitoring mechanisms, the project will identify and mitigate any unintentional harm, such as the potential misuse of resources or further marginalization of already vulnerable groups. All interventions will be designed with the full participation of local communities to ensure that actions align with their needs and that the project avoids reinforcing power imbalances.The proposed project promotes meaningful access to assistance by ensuring that all affected individuals are informed about their rights and the available services. Information will be delivered in accessible formats and through community-based channels to ensure that it reaches those who may otherwise be excluded, particularly marginalized or illiterate groups. The project will make a concerted effort to eliminate barriers such as language, mobility, or financial constraints that might limit access to services, ensuring that everyone can participate in the process.The empowerment and participation of people will be central to the project’s approach. Women will be actively involved in decision-making processes, from planning through to the implementation of activities, ensuring that their voices are heard, and their specific needs are addressed. Gender-sensitive programming will be adopted to tackle the unique challenges faced by women, such as access to documentation, social protection, and legal rights. Special attention will be given to engaging elderly persons, ensuring that their needs are met in a way that respects their dignity and autonomy. The project will provide opportunities for the elderly to participate in community consultations, and it will tailor services to their specific needs, ensuring they are not excluded from vital assistance

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The project focuses on ensuring that individuals with disabilities have equitable access to legal assistance by adopting a multifaceted approach:Conducting inclusive needs assessments, the project aims to understand the unique challenges faced by people with disabilities in accessing legal assistance. This involves direct engagement with communities to gather insights into their specific needs, concerns, and preferences.Community engagement and empowerment form a core aspect of the project. It prioritizes the active participation of people with disabilities in the design and implementation of legal assistance initiatives, ensuring that their perspectives are central to decision-making processes.The project incorporates easy-to-read materials, and alternative formats to disseminate information about available legal assistance services. This ensures that communication methods cater to the diverse needs of individuals with distinct types of disabilities.The project designs legal assistance programs that specifically address the unique legal challenges faced by people with disabilities. This involves specialized services related to disability rights, discrimination, accessibility, and other legal issues impacting this demographic.The proposed project is committed to ensuring that people with disabilities (PWDs) are included in all aspects of its implementation and that barriers to accessing assistance are removed. To identify individuals with disabilities, the project will utilize community-based assessments, engage local disability organizations, and consult with community leaders. This approach ensures that people with disabilities are recognized and included, especially those who may be overlooked through traditional data collection methods.To remove barriers to access, the project will adopt inclusive strategies that ensure all individuals, regardless of disability, can participate in and benefit from the services offered.
