Project Summary

Maram seeks to save lives, uphold rights, and promote access to key institutions and services through providing WASH services in particular. So, MF in Turkey office is remotely managing the delivery of integrated WASH assistance with main-streamed protection and advocacy in NWS. Basic WASH assistance is delivered to the targeted community by taking into account the different needs of men, women, boys, and girls, in addition, to taking into consideration the special needs of disabled people in all the project activities, especially during the infrastructure designs.
According to Syria HNO 2024, Several factors have adversely impacted access to WASH services in Syria, including the February 2023 earthquakes, conflict hostilities, economic decline, power shortages, and the impact of climate change. As a result of the conflict and earthquakes, damage to WASH systems has restricted the population’s access to water and sanitation services in affected communities. Additionally, economic decline has impeded service providers’ technical and financial capacities to operate and maintain water and sanitation systems adequately. This economic downturn has also affected households, limiting their ability to adopt coping mechanisms to maintain basic access to WASH services. Furthermore, the drought, resulting from low precipitation, has negatively impacted the quality and quantity of freshwater in Syria. Together with critically bad sanitation conditions, poor hygiene practices and deterioration of water quality, the WASH conditions caused increased public health risks, including AWD/ cholera outbreak, leishmaniosis, and malnutrition, as well as an impact on the livelihood and protection of households.
Overall, 13.56 million people are in need of WASH assistance, of whom 51 percent are women and girls, 44 percent are children and 17 percent have a disability. Almost 1.5 million more people are in acute need (+23 percent) and 22 percent more subdistricts (43) are now in need, including new geographical areas compared to 2022.
Therefore due to the huge need of WASH intervention in the NWs and referring to emergency needs in addition to camps management and LCs requests to provide the main WASH services in Idleb and Aleppo through INGOs and NGOs, Maram Foundation seeks to provide emergency needs as much as possible to help the people in need in different locations at Idleb and Aleppo with coordination and collaboration by WASH cluster, local authorities, and camps management in the area.
MF is applying for HPC to provide WASH response in many locations in Idleb and Aleppo as a result of the large influx of IDPs and limited access to services and to provide the emergency response as part of Cholera prevention and response in NWS.
MF seeks through this project to provide around 20,000 HHs /100,000 individuals.
The provided WASH activities will be drinking water (by operating the water stations in most locations and using the water trucking in emergency cases only), SWM, de-sludging latrine septic tanks, construction or rehabilitation latrines, rehabilitation of sanitation networks &water networks, wastewater treatment, Hygiene kits distribution (including Cholera kit), and hygiene awareness sessions. The project design will be according to SPHERE standards and WASH guidance in line with current priorities. MF, wherever possible, will provide the proposed WASH activities using the most sustainable solutions such as the establishment of Water pumping stations and water & sewage networks, but nevertheless and when necessary,

Furthermore, the planned intervention will mainstream protection principles especially (Do no harm) throughout the project cycle by considering the different needs of men, women, young children, children, adolescents, young adults, middle-aged adults, and older adults. The provision of sanitation services will be consulted with the targeted camps and communities, and take into account the specific needs of each group. MF also revised the content of hygiene kits by involving the women and girls through Focus Group Discussions (FGDs) during previews implemented projects.The Project also designed to provide disabilities, the elderly, and another vulnerable group with accessible handicap infrastructure too by taking these groups needs into consideration during the design phase of these infrastructures, with particular focus on vulnerable people such as ‘People with Special Needs’, in addition, to provide access to proper gender-sensitive and disability WASH facilities in camps, schools, child-friendly spaces, and health facilities. The main impact of the proposed project will be reducing the cholera outbreak and any other diseases among the beneficiaries by providing potable drinking water and the other WASH activities in NWS in addition to providing sustainable solutions for water supply and sanitation which will lead to reducing the operational cost of supplying water.Maram will take the recommendations of the WASH cluster into consideration throughout the project phases
