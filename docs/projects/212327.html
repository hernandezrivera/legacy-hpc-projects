Project Summary

This project will develop the agriculture situation and address the need of minimizing the bad effects that occurred due to discharging the untreated wastewater directly into the Euphrates River, which is used as the main source of drinking water in North-East Syria (NES). Eventually, benefit from the treated wastewater to irrigate the agricultural lands in the targeted area.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Most of the cities and villages in the province of Ar-Raqqa are located on the banks of the Euphrates River, which is the main source of irrigation and drinking water for the communities living there. Most of the population depends on agriculture and animal husbandry, and the Euphrates River’s water is used to irrigate crops and provide water for the animals.Access to irrigation water in the NES has been further aggravated by unprecedented low water levels of the Euphrates River, as a matter of fact, some of the Irrigation Associations went out of service because of the significant lack of rainfall during the rainy season in 2022, along with subsequent droughts in the area. There are no Decentralised Wastewater Treatment Plants (DWWTPs) in the Al-Raqqa Governorate, and the wastewater is directly discharged into the Euphrates River, which leads to pollution of the water and the spread of water-related diseases, as it is frequently used as drinking water.In addition, wastewater is used in the targeted location to irrigate agricultural lands without any treatment, and this poses a serious threat to public health – farmers do so, because of the proximity of wastewater to their lands. Using this untreated water, close to their lands has a significant operational cost advantage, as irrigating the lands with remote water sources would have surely a higher cost.Also, the farmers in the targeted area still use the old irrigation systems which leads to losing a lot of irrigation water, and also, they are planting the same crops which will affect the soil. So, this project aims to establish a DWWTP for treating the wastewater and utilize the treated water to irrigate agricultural lands of Hawi Elhawa town, and support the farmers in using the new modality of irrigation, and inter some new crops, according to the following information: Photo 1: General location of the DWWTP in Hawi Elhawa on Google Earth Establishing a DWWTP in Hawi Elhawa town:The town of Hawi Elhawa is located 5km east of Al-Raqqa city. Around 10,000 persons live there, 70% of whom are residents and 30% are displaced. Community PCode Community Sub-district District GovernorateC5704 Hawi Elhawa Ar-Raqqa Ar-Raqqa Ar-Raqqa In the village of Hawi Elhawa, there is an Agricultural Association that includes 4.500 dunums of fertile agricultural lands, which were previously irrigated from an irrigation canal, to which irrigation water reached from the artificial lake formed with the Euphrates Dam. Wheat, cotton, barley, maize and some vegetables are grown in these lands.As a result of the extensive damage to the irrigation canals, during the war years, and the lack of maintenance, the clean irrigation water no longer reaches the irrigation canals within the village of Hawi Elhawa.In the past years, due to the difficulty of obtaining clean water, farmers irrigated their lands from agricultural drainage channels, and also untreated sewage water, this inevitably increased health risks.
