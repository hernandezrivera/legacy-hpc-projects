Project Summary

As HNO 2024,16.7 million people(50% female)are in need for assistance across Syria,up from 15.3 million in 2023. the 12 years of crisis have led to negative impacts on all aspectst of life,such as,high inflation,currency depreciation,and increase in commodity prices.This continued to exacerbate the level of vulnerability of millions of people, and lack of access to services.The Aga Khan Foundation in Syria(AKF) community-based Humanitarian Micro-Enterprises(HME) approach has been proven as a sustainable model of establishing and developing local micro-enterprises,and an effective tool to build resilient livelihoods,that is achieved through the blended-finance modality(grants with micro-loans).AKF aims to adopt this approach to support entrepreneurs in Salamieh and Mesyaf districts,Hama governorate by supporting them with financial grants and micro-loans from the First Micro-Finance Bank (FMFB), a sister agency of AKF, so they can purchase enterprise-related assets, or solar-energy systems that will promote green solutions.This is accomplished by the standard rubric for implementing activities:1)Form Local Consultation Group(LCG) representatives of local community to review criteria, develop registration application, and engage in decision-making.2)Conduct a Rapid Needs Assessment(RNA) for the market’s of professions.3)Conduct training courses for 300 business owners on topics about entrepreneurship, business management, market analysis.4)Assess the business plans of the candidates with the Grants Review Committee(GRC) 5)Support 150 final beneficiaries with blended-financing and mentorship services.Protection Risk Analysis:•Collaps of social cohesion Likelihood:Low. Impact: Medium. AKF will ensure diversity by targeting various groups in coordination with CBOs and stakeholders.•Gender-related norms may limit women's participation in some works. Likelihood: Medium. Impact: Medium. AKF will ensure the participation of all community groups in awareness sessions on gender, social roles. AKF will ensure that female beneficiaries are comfortable by choosing what they prefer. AKF will maintain regular monitoring of women working environment and activate feedback and complaint mechanism which provide updates on the implementation and reduce barriers.•LNGOs accused of bias in selecting beneficiaries, Likelihood: High, Impact: Medium. AKF will manage the selection process to ensure precise implementation based on the selection criteria. PSEA:AKF is committed to safeguarding including Prevention from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) and has its Global Safeguarding Manual. HME will develop a specific Risk’ Register (RR) to identify SEA risks and develop relevant mitigation measures to reduce the likelihoods and the impact of the risks on the beneficiaries, especially children and vulnerable adults. RR will be reviewed and examined frequently to evaluate the residual risks and modify the procedures accordingly. AKF has responding measures in place to address SEA cases including speak-up channels, reporting mechanisms, case management, investigation procedures, and disciplinary process that adopt the approach of “Zero Tolerance’’. AKF has developed specific methods and procedures to mainstream Safeguarding, including PSEA, into its all processes and operations including, but not limited to, Recruitment, Contracting, Procurement and Partnerships in order to decrease the risks of SEA incidents and enhance the culture of PSEA during dealing and contracting with partners, individuals, and community groups and members. A mandatory Safeguarding/PSEA training and refresh sessions will be provided to staff, service providers, volunteers and associated members.All staff, volunteers, vendors, contractors and service providers will sign Code of Conduct which outlining the expected behaviors. AKF adopts the Survivor-Focused Approach to consider the survivor’s wishes, safety, and well-being to be a priority in all matters and procedures.
