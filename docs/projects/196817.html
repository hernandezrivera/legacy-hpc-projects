Project Summary

HIA will provide the nutrition services in fixed four health facilities as well RRT. The nutrition integration approach will be in line with nutrition cluster strategic objectives in three PHCs.
These PHCs will covering the main CMAM component as well IYCF program. As such the nutrition services will cover the TSFP, BSFP and OTP. Practically speaking, The nutrition services at community level will cover Household Mid Upper Arm Circumference (MUAC) screening and Edema as well provide Maternal, infant and young child feeding and care practices group counselling in addition to Household Micronutrient supplementation for Children 6-59 are not admitted in SAM or MAM treatment programmes or Blanket Supplementary feeding programmes. furthermore, Household Iron folate acid supplementation for PLWS. as well, support the FMA at the community and PHC levels. moreover, the CHWs will refer Children and women with visible signs of abuse of sexual exploitation and abuse, gender-based violence or/and child neglect/abuse. (Specialized case management sites) in the PHCs. Finally, CHWs will screen the Imunization Zero Dose. on the other hand, the nutrition services in the PHC will cover
- Referrals for specialized services for Children 0-59 having Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM) with medical complications (Stabilization Center)
- Treatment of severe acute malnutrition without medical complications.
- Rapid screening for maternal, infant and young child feeding and care practices complications.
- Maternal, infant and young child feeding and care practices one to one counselling
- Support mother to other support group.
The integration will be located in the following areas:
Governorate, Idleb District Jisir Ash-Shogour Sub-District ,Jisir Ash-Shogour Western Marj Akhdar Meshmehsan PHC
Governorate, Idleb District Harim Sub-District ,Armanaz Armanz community PHC
Governorate, Idleb District Ariha Sub-District ,Ariha Orm eljoz community PHC
Governorate, Idleb District Harem Sub-District ,Salqin AZmarin community PHC

Idleb governorate, Idleb district and Benesh and Maart Masrin sub-districts and
Idleb governorate, Harime distrit. Kafar Takhatem, Qurqanea sub-districts
The RRT will conduct Households visits on daily basis targeting 30-40 PLW and CU5 in average to implement the agreed program mentioned in RRT SOP. The RRT will be in close contact with HIA field team and supply chain central team to ensure that the distribution of nutrition supplies will be monitored on regular basis and the reporting process will be done through supply management monitoring tool in HIA.
Community based Management of Acute Malnutrition (CMAM) Community based screening treatment and referral of SAM and MAM children, pregnant and lactating women to the respective treatment facilities.
Promotion of optimal Infant and Young Child Feeding in Emergency (IYCF-E): targeted one to one counselling on the individualized IYCF subjects and dissemination of IYCF-E messages in the focus groups will be undertaken The RRTs will be integrated with health services by conducting awareness sessions in the health facilities and for those beneficiaries who are seeking the primary health care services along with referring the complicated cases to the health facilities.

3 - Needs

The 2022 HNO indicated increased people in Need (PIN) numbers across different malnutrition parameters since 2020. For instance, chronic malnutrition or stunting increased by 4%, Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM) cases (increased by 29%), MAM cases (increased by 44.7%), for PLWs, the number of PLWs in need for malnutrition treatment was approximately 265,000. SMART survey 18-10-2022 for NWS indicated the following results: - 53.5 % of Women of Reproductive Age (15 – 49 Years) were married less than 18 years with Prevalence of Global Acute Malnutrition in WRA (MUAC < 230 mm) was 11.7% along with prevalence of anemia based on Hemoglobin level among pregnant women was: 58.59%.- prevalence of combined GAM was 5.0% for children under five year and Prevalence of combined SAM 1.7% , Most of the malnutrition cases were in the age group 6 – 17 months, which low IYCF practices could explain these cases.Furthermore, the report revealed malnutrition trends analysis showed nearly the same prevalence of malnutrition in North West of Syria, compared between 2021 and 2022 SMART data we notice that GAM rate by WHZ had been increased from 2.5% in 2021 to 3.3% in 2022, and the SAM rate had been increased from 0.4% to 0.9%. The tow of crystal conclusions of the SMART report were the recommendations in which HIA through this amendment will have full commitment to these recommendations as well, full commitment in amendment guidelines.

8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

HIA's PSEA framework, affirms HIA's commitment to the UN Secretary General’s Bulletin on Special Measures for protection from sexual exploitation and sexual abuse (ST/SGB/2003/13) and to achieving full, ongoing implementation of the IASC Six Core Principles relating to SEA44. ​to create and maintain a safe environment, free from SEA, by taking appropriate measures for this purpose, internally and in the communities where HIA operates, through robust prevention and response work.In order to mitigate SEA cases, HIA has strong training, reporting, and awareness-raising policies in this regard.1- HIA systematically vets all prospective job candidates in accordance with established screening procedures.2- HIA holds mandatory induction and refresher training for all employees and related personnel on the Organization’s SEA policy and procedures.3- HIA has safe, confidential, and accessible mechanisms and procedures for personnel, beneficiaries, and communities, including children, to report SEA allegations and ensures that beneficiaries are aware of these.4- All HIA's contracts agreements include a standard clause requiring contractors, suppliers, consultants, and sub-partners to commit to a zero-tolerance policy on SEA and to take measures to prevent and respond to SEA.5HIA will conduct activities to raise awareness among its staff, beneficiaries, and local communities, including children, and others on PSEA. 6- HIA regularly distributes core PSEA messages for beneficiaries
