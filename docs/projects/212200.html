Project Summary

This program aims to support the resilience of the conflict-affected people in northwest Syria, particularly in Idleb and Aleppo governorates. Shafak's proposed action is designed to contribute to supporting the transition to the early recovery phase, as it helps the targeted communities with long-term assistance activities, programs that focus on building/restoring the communities' infrastructure, local economic empowerment, and initiatives that ensure engagement and participation of the community in recovering from the protracted crisis. This program will achieve its objectives by supporting Men, Women, and PWDs through the following:
a) Vocational Training: 400 affected households’ members are supported through Vocational Training activity to improve their employability opportunities and skills building.
b) On-job Training: 100 Vulnerable Households who received Vocational training will be enrolled in a 3-month training program at workshops/ trainers.
c) Entrepreneurship training: 100 Vulnerable Households who received Vocational training for one month of training program at workshops/ trainers.
d) Life skills for success Training: 200 Vulnerable Households who received VT and Entrepreneurship training for a one-month training program.
e) Basic Literacy and numeracy training: approximately 50 vulnerable individuals among those who attended the above training will be enrolled for two months of training.
f) Establishing or expanding Small-Micro Enterprises: 500. Through business grants and Business development skills training (BDS). The BDS will be for 3-5 Days, and the cash grants will range from 750-1200 USD. The targeted beneficiaries will be from two pathways: those who are not skilled and have passed the different above-mentioned training programs and the other path from those who already have existing businesses that need development or who have skills and need to restart their businesses.
g) Temporary Employment: 1200 vulnerable HHs within the targeted communities are supported through temporary income generation opportunities through Cash for Work activities. Cash-for-Work activities will enable the provision of 1200 temporary labor opportunities focusing on the rehabilitation of community-identified projects and referral to market approach for 1-3 Months.
h) Financial Services: Facilitating the Establishment of 20 Village Savings Loaning Associations (VSLAs) to improve access to financial services. An estimated 20 Savings and Loan Groups, each consisting of approximately 10-15 members, will be facilitated to establish.
i) Graduation Approach: 100 HHs. Support the self-reliance of vulnerable households from those supported with Cash for food. The Graduation approach will also include market-facing skills, transfer of productive assets, financial services through VSLA, mentoring, and Coaching, and a Referral mechanism.
j) Rehabilitating the local economic, public, and social infrastructure (20 Projects) through linking with the Cash-for-Work activity or through service providers. Each project cost will be about 25000 USD. The projects include constructing small markets in the IDP camps to enhance access to the market and creating IGAs within the camp.
k) Supporting local community and social cohesion initiatives: Through forming a community engagement team representing the different segments in the community.
l) Support Establishing or expanding the existing Medium-large enterprises through the provision of business development skills (BDS) training and in-kind/asset support with value reaching up to 3000 USD for 50 selected businesses.
It is worth mentioning that Shafak has secured approximately 12% of the requested funds.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The planned interventions outlined aim to address the complex needs of conflict-affected communities in northwest Syria, particularly in Idleb and Aleppo governorates. Here’s the rationale behind each intervention:• Vocational Training : By providing vocational training to affected households, the program seeks to enhance their employability and skills, thereby improving their chances of finding sustainable livelihood opportunities.• On-job Training : This follows up on vocational training by offering practical, hands-on experience to vulnerable households, further honing their skills and preparing them for employment.• Entrepreneurship Training : Empowering households with entrepreneurship training aims to foster self-reliance and encourage small business development, potentially creating new economic opportunities within the community.• Life Skills for Success Training : Complementing vocational and entrepreneurship training, life skills training equips participants with essential competencies for personal and professional success, enhancing their overall resilience.• Basic Literacy and Numeracy Training : Targeting vulnerable individuals with foundational literacy and numeracy skills ensures they have the basic tools necessary for further education and employment.• Establishing or Expanding Small-Micro Enterprises : Providing financial support and business development training to aspiring entrepreneurs encourages the growth of small businesses, which can contribute to local economic development and job creation.• Temporary Employment: Cash-for-work initiatives provide vulnerable households with temporary income opportunities while contributing to community projects, addressing immediate financial needs and enhancing local infrastructure. • Financial Service: Establishing Village Savings Loan Associations improves community members' access to financial services, empowering them to save, borrow, and invest in their futures.• Graduation Approach: Support vulnerable households with a holistic approach to self-reliance, including market-facing skills, asset transfer, financial services, mentoring, and coaching, to facilitate their transition out of poverty.• Rehabilitating Infrastructure: Rehabilitating local infrastructure addresses immediate needs, stimulates economic activity, and improves community well-being.• Supporting Community Cohesion Initiatives: By fostering community engagement and social cohesion, the program strengthens the social fabric, promotes unity, and enhances resilience in the face of adversity.• Supporting Medium-Large Enterprises: Providing support to medium-large enterprises fosters economic growth and stability, creating opportunities for employment and income generation on a larger scale.
