Project Summary

Project Summary: Empowering Communities Through Comprehensive Protection in Conflict-Affected Regions of NWSW
This intervention aims to strengthen the resilience and protection of vulnerable populations in the conflict-affected North West and South West (NWSW) regions of Cameroon. It targets displaced families, host communities, women, youth, and children, focusing on addressing immediate protection needs and fostering long-term community recovery.
The project seeks to establish and enhance community-based protection mechanisms, empower women and youth as agents of change, and facilitate access to essential services such as education, healthcare, and psychosocial support. By promoting dialogue and reconciliation, it aims to rebuild trust among diverse groups and lay the groundwork for sustainable peace and social cohesion.
Additionally, the intervention will support livelihood initiatives and provide capacity-building in conflict resolution to ensure economic recovery and community stability. Through a participatory approach, the project prioritizes the inclusion of local stakeholders and traditional authorities, ensuring culturally sensitive and context-specific solutions.
This initiative contributes to mitigating the impact of the ongoing crisis while building the foundation for a peaceful and resilient future for conflict-affected communities in the NWSW regions.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The proposed project addresses critical needs resulting from the protracted conflict in the North West and South West (NWSW) regions of Cameroon. These needs, assessed through community consultations and coordination with local stakeholders, encompass the following key areas: Identified Needs:Protection and Safety:High levels of insecurity, displacement, and exposure to gender-based violence (GBV) and child protection risks.Limited access to justice for survivors of violence and abuse.Psychosocial and Mental Health Support:Widespread trauma among conflict-affected populations, particularly children and women.Insufficient availability of mental health and psychosocial support (MHPSS) services.Livelihoods and Economic Stability:Loss of livelihoods and income sources due to displacement and destruction of property.Growing dependency on humanitarian aid without sustainable economic solutions.Education:Interrupted education for children due to school closures and security risks.Lack of safe spaces for learning, particularly for girls and children with disabilities.Social Cohesion and Peacebuilding:Erosion of trust between community members, aggravated by inter-group tensions and displacement.Lack of dialogue and platforms for collaborative problem-solving.Planned Activities and Implementation:Protection Activities: Establish community-based protection mechanisms to monitor and address GBV, child protection, and other safety concerns.Provide legal aid and case management services for survivors of violence and abuse.MHPSS Services: Set up mobile psychosocial support units to deliver counseling and group therapy sessions in hard-to-reach areas.Train community volunteers and leaders in basic psychosocial first aid to extend reach.Livelihood Support: Implement cash-for-work programs and vocational training for youth and women to restore income-generating activities.Distribute start-up kits and grants for small businesses to promote self-reliance.Education Support: Establish temporary learning spaces and distribute school materials to displaced children.Train teachers on conflict-sensitive education methods and psychosocial care.Peacebuilding and Social Cohesion: Organize community dialogues and mediation sessions to rebuild trust and foster reconciliation.Support local peace committees with tools and capacity-building for conflict resolution.Expected Results:Increased safety and reduced exposure to violence for vulnerable populations.Improved mental well-being and strengthened coping mechanisms for trauma-affected individuals.Enhanced livelihoods and reduced dependency on aid through sustainable income opportunities.Access to education for children in a safe and supportive environment.Strengthened community resilience and social cohesion, laying the groundwork for sustainable peace.This holistic approach ensures that immediate humanitarian needs are met while building long-term resilience and recovery for conflict-affected populations in the NWSW regions.
