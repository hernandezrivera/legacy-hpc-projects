Project Summary

WHO will implement the HRP 2022 through the cluster approach, through scaling up disease surveillance, epidemic and pandemic preparedness and response. WHO will work in close coordination with health patners in the delivery of essential health services to populations affected by the conflict especially in access to essential supplies. WHO has the lead agency in health will support health information management including monitoring health interventions and identifying health service gaps. This will be implemented while ensuring the learning and sustainability of critical sector outputs and outcomes by strengthening government ownership and leadership.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

WHO will target to provide life-saving and life-sustaining accessible essential health services to targeted populations, focusing on main causes of morbidity and sexual and reproductive health. The project will also target to prepare for, detect and respond to epidemic-prone disease outbreaks. WHO will provide support and train staff (RRT) to ensure EWARS capacity, the roll-out of a targeted community-based surveillance (CBS) modality using community volunteers. The project will include technical support to the SPS to implement the COVID-19 vaccination campaign reaching the priority population target groups identified through the national strategy. This will also entail providing training for health workers to improve laboratory testing capacity and use of RDTs Also it will support the SPS to optimize institutional and community-based readiness for case management of COVID-19. The project will also be used to strengthen cholera and diarrheal diseases sentinel surveillance, coordinate OCV campaigns, and advocate for improved WASH services in the resettlement sites and host communities. The WHO will work with partners to identify and respond to priority needs and gaps, including participation in joint assessments and revitalization of coordination forums. As the health cluster lead agency WHO will continue to provide steer the coordination meetings and guide humanitarian response. In addition, this HRP proposal aims to improve the availability of information on the status of health resources and health services within Cabo Delgado through the rollout of HeRAMS. WHO will continue to advocate and engage with all health partners, ensuring a people-centred approach for humanitarian response All this will be done in line with the core HRP objectives. This project will be targeting all ages groups (boys, girls, men, women and the elderly) It will be implemented covering 3 provinces (Cabo Delgado, Nampula and Niassa).

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Conduct and enhance e-IDSR training to improve timely detection and response to disease outbreaks & health conditions (e.g., cholera, AWD, measles, malaria, malnutrition, MHPSS). Activation and restoration of the early warning system (EWARS) for cholera and all other acute public health risks. Guide in the implementation of COVID-19 strategic preparedness and response plan. Support of COVID-19, cholera, measles vaccination campaigns reach targeted, vulnerable people Scale-up laboratory testing capacity including rapid diagnostic tests (RDTs) (COVID-19 & cholera) Provide investigation kits to support the rapid investigation of cholera suspected cases both for field testing using RDTs and laboratory confirmation in all hotspot districts. Operationalization of cholera and diarrheal diseases sentinel surveillance supported by district cholera focal points. Guide the province to complete the documentation for the International Coordinating Group (ICG) on vaccine provision request for oral cholera vaccine (OCV) campaign Support to service delivery by working with partners to identify and responding to priority needs and gaps, including participation in joint assessments and revitalization of coordination forums Provide leadership to Health Cluster strategic planning and decision-making to ensure health response needs are appropriately reflected in sectoral and multi-sectoral discussions Monitoring of health response with reliable information management and health system functionality gaps through the roll-out of HeRAMS Advocacy for resources for all health partners, ensuring a people-centred approach

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The project will be implemented in line with UN core principles of equity transparency, fairness and accountability. Gender with age which is a cornerstone for every project implementation has been given due consideration. WHO has considered the IASC Gender with Age Marker (GAM) requirements for all HRP project implementation. It will ensure that all actions address gender- and age-related in humanitarian response. This will be done by analyzing the specific needs of each age group targeted for a response. WHO will champion the needs resources for the right resources for women, girls, boys and men in different age groups. They will be included in decision making during the rapid assessments using the FGD discussions under the AAH approach. Therefore all aspects of gender mainstreaming will be factored in fully during the implementation of the 2022 HRP. A self-assessment for GAM sensitivity of the HRP proposal will be ascertained using the web-based GAM tool to compute the GAM score for the project at its design stage. The following link will be used to generate GAM report

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

The project was designed in line with WHO commitment to inclusivity of all persons in the design and implementation of project activities. The project populations targeted for all the locations have been disaggregated by gender; men, women, boys and girls, old and young and the physically challenged. The affected people will be incorporated into the activities such as the vaccination campaigns against, cholera and measles and would be enlisted to support the exercise. The current platform for community feedback mechanism (CCM) will be used to obtain feedback on the activity implementation. The project will strive to adopt standardized tools and joint implementing strategies with other patners. WHO will work to harmonize different CE/AAP activities that partners have already embedded in their projects. Needs assessments will be conducted jointly with community leaders. Direct observation, key informant interviews and focus group discussions will be used to obtain views from diverse groups. Healthcare workers and community volunteers will be recruited locally and from among the target population when they meet the required qualifications. More women will be encouraged to take up assignments. Complaints will be monitored at service delivery points regularly using complaints boxes and patient exit interviews and through a common feedback mechanism.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The HRP 2022 will be implemented in line with the UN charter of article 25 which outlines the UN convention on the rights of the disabled to attain the highest level of health. WHO will work with the protection cluster and other relevant organizations in conducting need assessments to determine the needs of persons with disabilities in the project locations. The target population for persons with disability has been considered and estimated at 4% .Every effort will be made to ensure data is disaggregated by disability. AAP approach will be implemented during the integrated field mission assessment during which focus group discussion and key informant interview with a person with disabilities will be conducted. This will include identifying persons with disabilities by sex and age to ensure a well-targeted humanitarian response

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

WHO considers environmental protection during implementation of its activities and will advocate for injection and safety measures in the execution of project activities including safe disposal of medical waste. It will guide patners on safe injection waste disposal at the health facilities and during the preventive vaccination campaigns using the Infection Prevention and Safety guidelines.Similarly WHO as the lead health cluster agency will work concertedly with other clusters to identify and mitigate against the environmental risks that portend dangers or asscociated with disease outbreaks.WHO will actively participate in environmental assesements required to be conducted.
