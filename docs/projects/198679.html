Project Summary

The proposed project is titled: - Providing Lifesaving Interventions to IDPs in camps site and out of camp settlements through integrated CCCM interventions in Borno and Yobe state.
Overall objective of the project – To contribute in improving the living conditions and protection of IDPs in sites and ensure equitable access to services and assistance of all persons in need, with a clear focus on moving toward attaining durable solutions and building of community resilient with full participation of all stakeholders in Konduga LGA of Borno state.
GREENCODE will deploy and adopt participatory approach in the implementation of this project, the participatory approach will involve, community engagement, participation and feedback. It will also take into consideration, age, gender and diversity from start to finish of the project in order to encourage resilience and self-sufficiency and reduce dependence among the conflict affected population in both formal and informal IDP camps.
In the implementation of this project, GREENCODE planned to implement in Konduga LGA of Borno state and explore possibility of strengthening CCCM in camp like setting as well as piloting “out of camp setting’ by applying camp management methodologies to ‘outside camp' scenarios in host communities in and around Konduga LGA as Konduga is recorded to be hosting the record largest population of IDPs after Maiduguri Metropolitan Council (MMC).
In the implementation of this CCCM project, GREENCODE will be adopting the standard CCCM model which involves three (3) coordination mechanisms, plus representative community governance structures for persons of concern.
Each has specific roles and responsibilities. To avoid confusion and gaps in camp-based responses, it is important to clearly distinguish and agree the roles and responsibilities of particular actors.
The three standard roles are:
Camp Administration (CA). This role is usually assumed by national or local authorities. It involves the overall supervision of a camp response, including security of the persons of concern.
Camp Coordination (CC). This role involves overall strategic and inter-camp operational coordination, covering issues such as setting strategy, setting standards, contingency planning, and information management.
Camp Management (CM). This role is usually assumed by an NGO partner or by national or local authorities. It involves coordination of a camp's services and maintenance of infrastructure.
In addition to these key functions, we will form representative committees of persons of concern at camp level. These enable persons of concern to exercise their right to participate and influence the design and delivery of humanitarian programmes at all stages.
The approach and methods to be adopted by GREENCODE in camps/site management in Konduga LGA Borno will have the following specific outcomes we seek to achieve through the implementation of the proposed standards are the following:
a) CCCM coordination mechanism/managements are established/reinforced at appropriate levels and are more coordinated and predictable.
b) Site/camps level information is available and shared with stakeholders.
c) Camp management agencies and coordinators become more accountable for the quality of camp management activities.
d) The ability of camp management agencies to provide a standard basic service in emergencies is improved.

1 - Needs

The Boko Haram insurgency in northeastern Nigeria, surging banditry violence in the northwest, incessant farmer/herder conflict in the middle belt and a growing Cameroonian refugee population in the south have all culminated into a complex country crisis for the west African giant. Boko Haram, and its break-out faction ISWAP have continued insurgent activities for over 10 years. The Nigerian military, and the African Union Multinational Joint Task Force have had to contend with suicide bombings, military garrison attacks and arsonic raids on both soft and hard targets. The insurgency has further spread to Niger, Chad and Cameroon in the Lake Chad Basin with over 1.8m people internally displaced in Nigeria alone. 08/12/2020: On 28 November, an attack claimed by Boko Haram killed at least 70 civilians who were harvesting in the village of Koshobo and rural communities in Jere LGA, Borno state. Many others are still missing. Attacks on farmers and fishermen impact access to livelihoods and deteriorate food security. In Borno, over 1.8 million people are currently facing Crisis (IPC Phase 3) or higher levels of food insecurity. 17/11/2020: Food security in Borno state is of high concern due to intensification of violence and significant constraints on humanitarian access. Over 433,000 people faced Emergency (IPC Phase 4) levels of food insecurity in June-August 2020. The economic impacts of COVID-19 and the global crash in oil prices are aggravating factors. The CCCM Sector recent data and needs assessment gap shows that the people in need of CCCM services in Borno state are 1,547,013 and in Yobe state are 139,393. This figure calls for urgent intervention by CCCM sector partners and GREENCODE has proposed the above project for both in camp sites and out-of-camp settlements.

2 - Activities

The planned activities will be focusing on; Outcome 1: CCCM coordination mechanism/managements are established/reinforced at appropriate levels and are more coordinated and predictable. Activity 1: Community mobilization through stakeholders mapping, meetings, community engagement, consultation and alignment of roles. Activity 2: Setting-up of CCCM coordination structures in collaboration with appropriate government counterpart like NEMA and SEMA in sites in Konduga LGA of Borno state. Activity 3: CCCM coordination with other humanitarian actors with the aim of mobilizing support for provision of relevant sectorial assistance to those identified as very vulnerable. Activity 4: Construction/setting up of CCCM coordination points/hall in the site. Activity 5: Procurement of basic CCCM site management and coordination kits and items for smooth implementation of CCCM project. Outcome 2: Sites/camps level information are available and shared with stakeholders. Activity 1: Identification, recruitment and training of site/camp facilitators on basic CCCM principles, standards and data collections. Activity 2: Conduct details Site assessment on monthly and quarterly basis in camps/sites in Konduga LGAs of Borno state. Activity 3: Carry out DTM assessments in newly identified locations and displacement settlement taking key vulnerabilities of men, women, boys and girls into priority Activity 4: Establish/update services mapping of partners operating in the sites and location that GREENCODE is implementing in Konduga LGA. Activity 5: Provide CCCM operation and dedicated IM support. Activity 6: Harmonize feedback mechanisms relevant for sites across all clusters Activity 7: Identify and immediately refer gaps in assistance to relevant clusters, following up to ensure needs are addressed. Outcome 3: Camp management agencies and coordinators become more accountable for the quality of camp management activities. Activity 1: Identify existing governance structures and focal points for sits in Konduga LGA Activity 2: Mobilise and set-up/strengthen site governance structures such as site governance/site committees with representatives of the displaced population Activity 3: Setting up Complaints Response Mechanism (CRM) with due consultation with the stakeholders and preferred mechanism for complaints/feedback either in the form of Complaint Box, Complaints desk focal point, telephone lines etc. Activity 4: Conduct building training and other technical support for exiting and new site governance structures/focal points in Konduga LGA. Activity 5: Coordinate with other partners and stakeholders in establishing/efficient use of referral pathways for both protection (CP/SGBV) and other relief assistance/service delivery to the most vulnerable population among the IDPs. Outcome 4: The ability of camp management agencies to provide a standard basic service in emergencies is improved. Activity 1: Mapping of existing community structures within the site (s) in Konduga LGA Activity 2: Implement community-based activities to promote community cohesion and recovery. : Activity 3: Carry out weekly site environmental sanitation exercise to ensure safe physical, socially and culturally appropriate for inhabitants and habitation. Activity 4: Monitor and conduct mass awareness on cross cutting issues ranging from COVID-19, gender, environment, HIV/AIDS, CP and SGBV etc.) continuously to ensure these issues are addressed.
