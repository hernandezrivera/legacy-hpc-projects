Project Summary

UNICEF will continue to facilitate access to safe learning environment where children can access quality education in areas near the contact line in both GCA and NGCA. Specifically, UNICEF will continue to undertake urgent rehabilitation of education institutions as well as provide education supplies, including Early Child Development (ECD) and educational kits.
In order to increase the protective nature of schools in increasing resilience of children, UNICEF will expand to provide capacity building trainings to a total of 740 teachers, community workers and NGO representatives on the delivery of Life Skills Education to 10,500 children within 347 schools on both sides of the contact line.
Supporting efforts of schools and local authorities to develop policies, interventions and activities which effectively mitigate the impact of the conflict on children, UNICEF will provide trainings on Conflict Sensitive Education (CSE) to a total of 800 education professionals and local authority representatives in areas near the contact line (GCA).
UNICEF will also continue the development of different distance learning modalities to ensure uninterrupted access to all levels of education for children in NGCA as well as in areas near the contact line in GCA

Needs

The ongoing conflict continues to have a access to and of education for children in areas near the contact line as well as being exposed to direct dangers of ongoing military operations. From January to October 2018, there were 57 recorded incidents of education facilities sustaining damages due to shelling (Education Cluster reports : https://goo.gl/hW4Nts ). The September 2018 cluster assessment found 84% of schools requiring some form of infrastructure rehabilitation, 77 of which are located within 20 km of the contact line and nearly half (48%) of education facilities assessed reported having children in need of education supplies. The later figures is corroborated by the REACH survey which found that one in three families (34%)_could not afford school supplies. Exposure to the ongoing conflict impacts the psycho-social functioning of children and adults alike which also affects educational outcomes for children. Nearly two thirds of schools assessed during the cluster survey reported that the conflict impacted on student’s ability to learn while just under half (48%) reported teacher’s personal experiences had impacted on their professional abilities to support their students. Lack of access to all levels of the education curriculum remains a problem particularly for children living in NGCA as well as for some children in areas near the contact line in GCA. The conflict and its consequences impacts boys and girls differently which is compounded by strong gender stereotypes and social norms which can accentuate gender disparities. Activities have been adapted with dedicated processes and modalities to ensure that interventions address the specific needs of girls, particularly for WASH interventions.

Activities & outcomes

UNICEF Ukraine’s Education program will be aiming at: • Rehabilitation of education facilities in GCA and NGCA.• Provision educational supplies, ECD and Educational kits for boys and girls. • Conduct Conflict Sensitive Trainings for education professionals and local authority representatives in areas near the contact line (GCA).• Conduct training on provision of Life Skills Education for educational professionals from secondary schools in GCA and NGCA . • Continued engagement with the Education Cluster around the endorsement by the Government of Ukraine of the “Safe School Declaration” protecting boys and girls from conflict related violence. • Improve the access to distance learning programs for boys and girls from GCA and NGCA.

Indicators & targets

• 7,250 boys and girls befitted from rehabilitation of 35 educational facilities, 15 in GCA (with 150 children enrolled in each) and 20 in NGCA (250 children enrolled)• 14,500 boys and girls benefited from educational supplies, Educational and ECD kits and received by 70 education facilities - 30 in GCA (150 children enrolled in each) and 40 in NGCA (250 children enrolled)• 800 teachers, school administration from 347 secondary schools, as well as representatives of local authorities benefited from conflict-sensitive education• 740 teachers from 347 secondary schools located 0-20 km along the contact line benefited from Life Skills Education reaching 10,500 boys and girls (10,000 children and 690 teachers GCA and 500 children and 50 teachers NGCA)• 4,000 boys and girls benefited from access to distance learning programs
