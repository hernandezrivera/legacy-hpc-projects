Project Summary

The aim of this response plan will be increase access, scale-up response and also strengthen the capacities of primary health care to provide emergency lifesaving health care to 30,639 vulnerable IDPs and host populations in Pibor, Pochalla and Twic East counties in Jonglei; Awerial and Yirol East in Lakes State
LiveWell’s HRP budget for 2021 is estimated at US$ 1,884,270 and will aim at supporting the following 10 health facilities based on the following level.
• 8 Mobile Clinics in Yirol East(3), Awerial (4) and Twic East (1) to reach 18,699 IDPs individuals.
• 1 PHCUs in Pochalla (1), to reach 4401 IDPs/Returnees/Refugees in Alari boma
• 1PHCCs in Pibor county to reach 8,539 IDPs displaced in Pibor due to insecurity and floodings

1 - Needs

• The humanitarian situation across Jonglei State continued to deteriorate as a result of wider range of compounding natural, man-made, and macroeconomic shocks resulting to mass displacement and limited access to crops, livestock, and markets. This has placed Pibor, Twic East, and Bor South counties in Emergency Phase 4 of IPC report. These shocks have simultaneously eroded the capacity of food insecure households to employ coping strategies, exacerbating already widespread, severe, and multisectoral vulnerabilities. Moving forward, humanitarian food assistance will be essential to offset a continued and widespread deterioration that may result into health effect (REACH Assessment in Jonglei, Sept 2020). • The weakened health system compounded by multiple shocks will impact the health and wellbeing of people living in Lakes, Jonglei and South Sudan at large in 2021. There are limited access to optimal health services due to ongoing conflict, flooding and an underdeveloped health care system. According to South Sudan IDSR reports, Malaria remains the top cause of morbidity (64 per cent) and mortality (45 per cent). Between 9 and 15 November 2020, there were 89,403 reported cases of malaria. Measles outbreaks have been recorded in Bor South, Yirol East, Kapoeta East and Pibor in 2020 (WHO IDSR/EWARS Bulletin, 2020) • SMART surveys conducted in April & September 2020 by Action against Hunger in put Duk County at a GAM Rate of 22%. Malaria was reported as the most common health problem for people in 67% of assessed settlements across Jonglei State. (SMART Suvery, April 2020). • According to South Sudan’s HNO, 2021; the ongoing flood situation will further aggravate the health situation, the spread of existing outbreaks and the likelihood of new outbreaks in 2021. This highlights the critical need to strengthen disease surveillance and response system to prevent, detect and respond to future outbreaks. The absence of basic health care including vaccination services has resulted in low immunization coverage in our proposed HRP areas like Pibor, Yirol East & Yirol West amongst others, increasing the risk of vaccine-preventable disease outbreaks. Planned mass vaccination campaigns were disrupted by the COVID-19 containment measures in place, which worsened the vaccination coverage and related health risks (HNO, 2021) • LiveWell is therefore seeking your approval to recommend our concept note “Improve access, scale-up response and strengthen the capacities of health facilities to provide emergency lifesaving health care services to the IDPs/host communities in Jonglei, and Lakes states” for Humanitarian Response Plan (HRP) for Health Cluster in the coming Year 2021.

2 - Project activities

LiveWell is therefore proposing in this humanitarian response plans for the year 2021 to set-up:• Conduct epidemic disease surveillance, prevention and control diseases outbreak detection, investigation and response in all the health facilities • Conduct awareness campaign on COVID-19 at households an community level• Implement WASH in health facilities including building the capacities of health workers to prevent and control COVID-19, Measles and cholera outbreaks.• Conduct Immunization against vaccine preventable diseases and emergency antigens for immunization and cold chain commodities.• Provide BeMONC services in Pibor PHCC • Trained health workers including skilled birth attendants on ANC, and partograph• Conduct mobile and outreach health services in hard to reach locations• Request, transport and distribute IEHK kits including major essential drugs for health facilities• Provide integrate MHPSS and SGBV including services in health facilities • Provide protocols, guidelines and IPC supplies in all health facilities under LiveWell.• Conduct rapid needs assessment and AAP in order to get the view on communities on healthcare providers in all the counties under LiveWell.• Conduct monitoring visits, and support supervision to ensure quality provision in health facilities.• Conduct health education for all patients and households living near health facility• Implement integrated community case management of diseases including boma health initiatives for smallest unit of government.• Provision of rehabilitation services and assertive devices for the disabled. • Conduct curative consultations for inpatients and outpatient services• Conduct MUAC Screening for all the under five years’ children and pregnant & lactating women.• Conduct mass distribution of mosquitoes nets including mass vitamin A supplementation for pregnant women.• Conduct Malaria screening, treatment and tracking • Provide ANC, normal skilled delivery, and post natal care services • Conduct family planning & other reproductive health services
