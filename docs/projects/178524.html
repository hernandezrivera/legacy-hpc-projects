Project Summary

This proposal aims to provide WASH humanitarian life-saving assistance for 27419 newly IDPs (5765 men, 6166 women, 7142 boys, and 8346 girls) including 200 people of special needs in 13 newly established camps or camps that have been recently expanded in Idleb Gove, Harim district Dana and Maaret Tmisreen Sub-districts
Furthermore, this proposal will promote personal and public hygiene and limit diseases spread through insects such as Leishmania, etc. This will be reached through a set of integrated activities that include:
- Water provision for IDPs with an average of 35 liters/day/person
- One-round distribution of hygiene kit that is designed to fulfill HHs needs of hygiene and in pursuance with their culture, whereas we take in consideration family individual number and increasing soap amount to be 2.5 kg according to WASH cluster recommendation.
- Establishing latrine blocks in sites where gaps are considering needs of elderlies, and people with disabilities, whereas we take in consideration WASH cluster and SPHERE guidance: maximum distance from shelter to toilet/latrine is 50 m, family latrine blocks each one consists of two cabin one for females and another for males, Maximum number of persons per public toilet/latrine is 25 persons, latrine blocks for PWD include Seat toilets with metal handles and ramps for PWD
- Waste management deporting to main dumps that confront to daily health conditions. whereas we take in consideration WASH cluster and SPHERE guidance: Number of families per 100-litres garbage container is 5, each person produces 1.1 kg of garbage every day and maximum distance from shelter/tent to garbage container is 100 m
- Septic tanks desludging and deporting them to main sanitation with ensuring that not making pressure to them.
- Establishment and improvement of sewage networks in camps with connecting them to main networks.
- Establishment of water networks and connecting them to water stations with support for operation to reach more sustainable, cost-effective whereas we take in consideration WASH cluster, SPHERE and AISC committee guidance: maximum distance from individual shelters to water taps and distribution points is 200 m and maximum number of people per water tap is 80 persons. Saed will implement all previously proposed WASH activities during the project duration.
The proposed intervention will provide life-saving integrated WASH services through providing safe water, water quality monitoring, construction of gender segregated and safe latrines, solid waste management, desludging, hygiene promotion as well as distribution of hygiene kits in camps and informal settlements for the IDPs who are displaced in the first of December 2019 from the East and south of Idleb, in addition to long-term IDPs living in settlements, seeking to ensure that the households residing in these settlements continue to survive, and in a way that respects their right to water and safe and dignified sanitation services.

5 - Implementation of activities

The project is developed to meet the needs of the most vulnerable people living in 13 camps in Idleb governorate. The project aims to target internally displaced people (IDPs) with lifesaving and integrated WASH intervention which includes the provision of safe water for drinking and personal hygiene, solid waste management activities, desludging, hygiene promotion, distribution of essential WASH NFIs, and construction of latrines in 13 camps in Idleb governorate. Works will start with signing work consent forms the landowners and verifying Camp HLP, with sharing the work-plan with the relevant authorities. Also, prepare tenders for works and contracting with contractors. Then, construction of latrines according to 13 special latrine blocks for PWDs. SAED’s field staff will oversee and supervise the distribution of safe water via water trucks to the IDPs in each camp according to the agreed work plan with the suppliers. In addition, delivery sheets will be made on daily basis and will be signed by the camp management along with water quality monitoring where SAED’s field staff will monitor the water quality at the household level by taking water samples in each camp and properly test the samples with simple pool testers, and conducting monthly water test for water wells (Biology Test for well)For desludging activities, Saed will contract with service providers to implement desludging and solid wastes according to the work plan attached in the budget. and to also procure and distribute hygiene kits to the IDPs in camps one-off round in each camp.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Although all Syrian population has been heavily affected by the current crisis, due to traditional local norms, women are exposed to additional protection related risks. All types of violence are cited as occurring in opposition areas of Syria, with domestic violence, sexual exploitation and child marriage as top three. While sexual exploitation is somehow new, child marriage and domestic violence are often normalized and ingrained in the culture. Women and girls are exposed to structural and societal risks as the lack of safe spaces and public transportation, limited freedom of movement, poverty, unemployment, displacement, stress associated to war, gender role change, lack of documents. All these poses enormous pressure, men feeling they failed in protecting and securing a good life for their families. With men perceiving women as a threat to their control due to gender roles change, domestic violence has increased in Syria. Whereas men actions are explained as a result of stress, women are often blamed for provoking the violence or allowing it to happen. Parental violence against children has also been reported as increasing. Displacement and living in camps are noted as being particularly unsafe environments for women/girls due to the lack of privacy (in tents or in latrines), overcrowding and mixing of people, poverty, financial desperation, and lawlessness.
