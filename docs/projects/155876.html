Overall objective of the project:
The overall objective of the project is to ensure an effective response through the provision of water, sanitation and hygiene services in schools, which will improve hygiene in schools in North Syria.
Specific objective of the project.
The purpose of this project is to improve the hygiene situation in 16 schools in the North of Syria, specifically in the area of Idlib-Jisr Al-Shughur-Bdama. The project will benefit 4866 students of different age groups during the project implementation period (6 months). Services related to the rehabilitation and maintenance of out-of-service health facilities (bath blocks, laundries, water streams) will be provided and re-integrated into the service framework within the96 latrines. And providing cleaning tools and materials in these facilities through delivery to the school administration after coordination with the Directorate of Education located in the region with the provision of awareness sessions for students on personal hygiene and mechanisms and procedures and provide a basket of cleanliness students each student with regard to sex and age groups.

Needs

As the Syrian crisis approaches its eighths year, civilians continue to suffer the burdens of the conflict marked by destruction and disregard for human life. More than 13 million are in need of humanitarian assistance where they face serious threats as more than half of the population has been forced out from their homes. Many people have been displaced several times. Children and youth, millions of whom are known only as conflict, account for more than half of the displaced, as well as half of those in need of humanitarian assistance. The parties to the conflict act with impunity and commit violations of international humanitarian law and human rights law. Because of the conflict, many public institutions and services became out of service as in schools and other facilities within the targeted areas. People, who were previously forced into displacement as in Jisr al-Shughur area in Idleb governorate countryside, are now facing the ramification of these attacks as in the destruction and damage to many facilities in the area. A team from ONSUR’s Monitoring and Evaluation unit was sent to assess the Water and Sanitation situation in schools within the area where the results indicated that 80% of the schools in the district of Bdama in the Jisr Al-Shughur area were out of service and need rehabilitation and that they cannot been used in this state. In addition, less than 25% of the students were aware and committed to maintaining their personal hygiene.These needs were identified through the following sources:- Directorate of Education in the region.- Director schools.Local councils in the region.- Teachers in specific schools.- Monitoring and evaluation team in ONSUR and data collectors.The tools used to determine the needs are:- Personal interview.- Field visits.- Questionnaires.

Implementation of activities

ONSUR will rehabilitate the out-of-service WASH facilities (toilets, water-basins) where a contractor will be hired for the rehabilitation work, as well the hiring of a technical team to oversee the rehabilitation process to ensure its compliance with standards Quality in implementation and quality assurance. Implementation will be in accordance with:1 – Coordinating with the Education Directorate located in the region to give them details about the project and the signing of MoUs with them in addition to the coordination with local councils.2– Appointing a technical team to supervise the work and commitment to the quality of implementation.3- Contracting process for the implementation of rehabilitation and maintenance procedures.4- Procurement and transfer of materials and activities related to the implementation.5- Provision of cleaning tools and materials for the targeted schools6 - Appointing trainers with a strong experience in personal hygiene promotion and procedures.7 – Conducting awareness sessions to students around taking into account age groups and sex. In addition, support the students with specifically designed Hygiene kits. Implementation of activities:Activity .1 Removing damages, rubble, and remains in schools' bathrooms facilities.Activity .2 Cement coating to both the interior of the bathroom facilities (soft coating) and the exterior (hard + soft coating) including the coating of the ceiling Activity .3 Installing of ceramic tiles to the bathroom facilities including the addition of a specific ceramic tile to the water faucets structuresActivity .4 Installing of ceramic tiles to the floors of the water facilities in addition to the installation of stone bindings to the male toilet facilities Activity .5Installation of Aluminium doors and windows to the latrines including the addition of external metal frame to the windowsActivity .6Installation of a metal door to the entrance of the bathrooms facilitiesActivity .7Installation of 3 washbasins with accessories and extensions per block of toilets in schools in addition to the installation of water taps per blockActivity .8Installation of air ventilators with lighting for each block Activity .9Complete maintenance work for all bathroom facilities in each school including (extensions - drainage – rehabilitation and restoring structures) Activity .10 Provision of hygiene and cleaning items to schoolsActivity .11Identifying and selecting the trainers, training materials Activity .12 Conducting the awareness session on location in the schools including the practical exercises Activity.13 Distribution of hygiene promotion materials and brochures in schools and other relative locations Activity.14Distribution of hygiene kits to the students with their families.

Protection Risk Analysis

Activity:1- Provision of NFI packages.Protection Risk: - Inequality in distribution; Physical and psychological harm arising from violence and coercion; Timing and process of distribution put women and girls at higher risk of GBV.- Risk of theft of NFIs and chemicals such as chlorine/Sodium Hypocrite which may be attractive to armed groups inside Syria.- Gender insensitive contents verities of kits with inconsistent items.- (Late) Distribution of expired unhealthy items.Mitigating Measure:- Promote Establishment of adequate management plan; Promote involvement communities in distribution planning and feedback on package for improvement.- Agencies provide feedback and accountability mechanisms to users; Work closely with all partners on the ground, Promote distribution stock between multiple warehouses in the target regions (for large quantities of stock.- Harmonies and standardize gender friendly hygiene kits among the sector members and indicate quantity and instruction for use. - Establish and monitor expiry date of supplies propositioned in warehouse, ensure relevant storage provision and carry out timely disposal of damaged/expired items.2- Emergency sanitation:Protection Risk:- Toilets represent GBV and/or health, safety risk; Access to toilet is not available to all 24/7; Access to toilets not available for all;- Movements of desludging trucks in congested, camp like settings; Reliability of services.- Bathing spaces are installed inappropriately and culturally insensitively.Mitigating Measure:- Ensure context/SPHERE standards and closely monitor these provisions/aspects during implementation. Ensure women and adolescent girls are consulted during design, implementation and monitoring phase of projects. Accountability mechanisms are put in place and received feedback from users is followed up. Lessons from previous experience are shared and learned.- Consult community regarding desludging services modality/ frequency, truck routes and schedule; Establish clear routes and schedule for desludging trucks that use wider access routes, avoiding school areas during the time children travel to/from school; Conduct safety training with truck drivers/operators;- Consult with women and adolescent girls and ensure regular access of water and hanging spaces/options for towel and clothes;

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumptions and Risks.- Price volatility (fuel - other materials in the project implementation areas) which is reflected in dealing with suppliers.- Changing the security situation in the region that impedes the access of suppliers and materials to the implementation areas.- Difficulties in training first-class students (children) about personal hygiene and procedures due to their young age.Provide sufficient satisfaction for the students about the training material or the contents of the student hygiene basket.Risk mitigation.- I support a very strong purchasing team that conducts a full study of the prices during the contract period and makes contracts to comply with the specified price. Contracts include insurance on contracted materials until reaching the agreed place.- Onsur team will study the security situation in the area where implementation will be carried out by contacting local councils or local police in the area. The trainers will be from the middle age group and have experience in dealing with all categories and different methods of communicating the informationThe training material and the contents of the student basket to take their opinion and preferences. - Percentage of beneficiaries will be involved.
