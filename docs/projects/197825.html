Project Summary

This project seeks to address the acute problem of Housing, Land and Property for the conflict affected populations of the North-West and South-West Regions of Cameroon where COHESODEC hopes to implement this project so as to significantly ameliorate on the housing, land and property rights of affected populations of these conflict affected regions.
Vulnerable communities/localities targeted to benefit from our interventions in this HLP project will include the following: Bafut, Bali, Bamenda, Bamenda 3, Batibo, Benakuma, Fundong, Mbengwi, Oku, Santa, Tubah, Wum, Kumba_North and_Kumba_South.
The project seeks to target a total of over 29,000 conflict affected populations badly in need of targeted assistance. The project will be ensuring the training of 500 local authorities, traditional leaders and civil society actors to effectively address HLP related disputes.
Moreover, the project will be ensuring the sensitization of over 23,000 community members, local authorities, and traditional leaders on HLP rights, responsibilities and obligations.
Furthermore, the project will ensure the provision of 2,500 case-specific counseling on HLP related to individual counseling in targeted conflict affected communities.
Finally, the project will be ensuring the provision of 500 case-specific legal assistance involving beneficiaries who constitute victims of the ongoing conflict in the NW/SW regions to ensure the exercise and enjoyment of their HLP rights.

1 - Besoins / Needs

With regards to the OCHA Multi-sector Needs Assessment, conducted by Plan International in the NW/SW of Cameroon, statistics indicate that IDP’s in the North-West Region formally renting stand at-28%, meanwhile those hosted for free constitute-24%. IDP’s having no agreement make up-14%, those formally owning house and land constitute-13%, those informally renting constitute-9%, IDP’s hosted for a fee constitute—9% while those who informally own land and property make up-1%, IDP’s that own house but no formal land agreement constitute-1% and those owning land with informal agreements constitute-1%.On the other hand, in the South-West Region of Cameroon, IDP’s who are formally renting constitute-48%, while those hosted for free constitute-21%, also those having “No agreement” make up 10%, as those formally owning house and land make up -9%. Also, IDP’s informally renting constitute--7%, meanwhile those formally hosted for a fee constitute-2%, IDP’s who informally own property constitue-1%, those owning house but no formal land agreement make up-1% while IDP’s owning houses but having no formal land agreement constitute-1%.The needs required for the effective implementation of this project includes the following: Capacity building to strengthen the capacities of 500 local authorities, traditional leaders and civil society actors to ensure that HLP related disputes are effectively addressed.Moreover, Community Sensitization of local authorities and traditional leaders on HLP rights and obligations shall ensure the restoration of HLP rights to internally displaced persons, resolving ongoing HLP disputes and re-established HLP rights. Also, the strategic sensitization of 23,000 target communities involving local authorities, and traditional leaders on HLP rights and obligations shall constitute the responsibilities and obligations of the project with the use of community sensitization, radio messaging, leaflets-(hand-outs) and posters, etc.)Furthermore, counseling services are designed to help individuals analyze themselves by providing 2,500 case-specific counseling sessions on HLP related issues for victims of HLP rights. Finally, in the implementation of this project, COHESODEC will require the services of legal experts/lawyers who shall ensure the provision of individual legal assistance related to land disputes and access to land which will ensure that 500 affected victims receive legal assistance consisting of professional legal counseling, representation or defense of the persons in court, during pre-trial procedure or elsewhere, and preparation of documents for the persons concerned as well as performing other legal acts in the interests of the persons. Contracts for legal services and assignments shall be signed with experienced practitioners guided by law and professional ethics during the performance of Legal Services.
