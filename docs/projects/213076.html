Project Summary

The socioeconomic situation has continued to deteriorate, In 2024, 16.7 million people are expected to require assistance, the largest number ever since the beginning of the crisis in 2011.Basic services such as education and health have also suffered immensely, both as a result of attacks and collateral damage.
Evolution of People in Need ( Education sector ) is expected to be 7.2 million people ( and it was 6.9 in 2023 ) ,1,312,862 in Aleppo, 1,139,995 in Rural Damascus, 566,507 in Damascus, 439,323 in Homs, 325,953 at Lattakia.
As per 2024 HNO Education chapter: Significantly high percentage (66%) of children do not want to go to school as they find schools are not useful or learning taking place sufficiently, followed by no time due to work/tired (45%) and/or depressed/unmotivated (44%). Improving school condition stays as the highest priority of the family in improving the education services (54%), which is followed by improving the teacher’s ability to teach (44%) and providing cash to support education expenses (38%). As perTamayoz presence in Aleppo and RD, an increase of OOS rate was observed, this is due to difficult economic situation, lack of needed food, and money, and some kind of disabilities, consequently, a high rate of child labour was also reported. many children work in collecting metal from debris making them at high risk of mine.
This project aims at increasing access to education and providing education services of good quality in Aleppo, Homs, latakia, Damascus and Rural Damascus in addition to supporting livelihoods activities in the same targeted governorates
Targeted locations are of severity scale 3, 4 and 5. % of targeted people according to the education severity scale is: 16% for severity 3, 75% for 4, and 8% for 5, As for livelihoods, severity scale is considered according to the Final Severity Score, the % is: 33% for severity 4, 66% for 3
project will directly contribute to the Education Sector’s HRP objectives 1 (Availability and access) and 2 (Quality), and it will target children (IDPs,host community & returnees) with different kinds of activities in addition to targeting parents, and teachers. Similarly, the project will equally contribute to the livelihood Sector SO1 and 2. Activities are mentioned in detail with targets in a separate part
Targeted locations are determined according to Tamayoz work and knowledge in those areas where it has completed implementation of some education activities under SHF, and is willing to continue providing services to maintain the sustainability and to expand in new locations where there are no enough schools to encourage re-enroll in Formal education. Tamayoz has access and needed approvals to targeted locations where the need for education and livelihoods services is so high.
The project will address the beneficiaries education needs according to FGDs made by Tamayoz targeted men, women, boys, and girls and according to the methodology that will include the provision of non-formal education for vulnerable children (boys and girls) from different communities (IDPs, returnees and host community) and by providing a comprehensive package of services: ECE, BTL, and life-skills, NFE, support teacher training, provision of PSS, school feeding, support the most vulnerable students with cash assistance according to specific criteria, support other vulnerable students with winter clothes, …and will include supporting chidren with disabilities with cash assistance under livelihoods activities
Education activities will be implemented in one non-formal setting in Aleppo, Homs, Latakia, Damascus and Rural Damascus. Altamayoz is implementing education activities in partnership with UNICEF in different locations in Rural Damascus which are different from those proposed under this project, Altamayoz will keep coordination with UNICEF to avoid any possible duplication in services.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Activities are planned for the year 2024 to achieve a distinguished impact on children’s education status especially those OOS.The project will target students (boys and girls) of all ages from 3 to 24 and will support PWD of different ages by engaging them in project cycle (planning, implementation, and monitoring), providing suitable and easy access, supporting them with cash assistance deploying trained staff. this project is linked to another protection project where referrals of students with protection risks to case management, It is also linked with food activities.The project will be directly implemented by Tamayoz and will be in line with HRP framework and will contribute to HRP objectives1and2 and in line with sectors Response strategies and priorities. It will assure more education Investments through supporting the economic situation of student's families and encourage them sending their children to school. project will also support provision of transportation support and school feeding. NFE activities will support bridging the gap of education in locations where there is no or inadequate schools. livelihoods activities will be implemented in the same targeted locations. A good database and reporting system will be in place, in addition to regular meetings with staff. Tamayoz will deploy well-qualified teachers and PSS providers,Teacher will be provided with capacity building. Monitoring: feedback mechanisms, field visits, pre-post assessmentPRA Risk: Tensions within the community due to feeling of being unfairly treated, insufficiently consulted. MM: Teachers and school personnel will be trained on prevention and reporting of, violence, physical punishment and bullying, and the use of positive discipline Risk: Teachers or other education personnel participate in physical punishment, act violently, bully and neglect students. Students participate in bullying towards other and/or to teachers. Mitigation M: AlTamayoz will set regulations for all staff and teachers that prevent all forms of violence, Children will be closely supervised to prevent any kind of bullying. Risk: Girls are at risk of GBV, MM: locations of Education facilities and routes to them away from actual or potential threats such as violence; especially the risk or threat of gender based violence. Risk of Interference with beneficiary targeting in livelihoods activities: Mitigation measures: Train staff on targeting/vulnerability criteria as well as community mobilization, Set community feedback mechanismAltamayoz has PSEA strategy that is introduced to all staff membersAll staff has to sign a Code of Conduct related PSEA. All staff will receive training on PESA and periodical awareness raising sessions to beneficiaries.Risk: lack of well qualified staff in the area of implementation due to the lack of education opportunities during the crisis years /Likelihood: high. impact: low Mitigating measure: capacity building for people from the implementation area
