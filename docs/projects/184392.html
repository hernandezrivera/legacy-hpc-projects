Project Summary

According to Protection HNO report 2021, The lack of protection from violence, abuse and neglect remains a key
consequence of the current humanitarian crisis in Somalia for up to 3.2 million people. Out of those, 1.9 million are living in displacement and 1.2 million people belong to host communities. Whether directly exposed to armed conflicts or violence, or the destitution resulting from climatic shocks, affected communities face serious risks to their physical safety and integrity, and access to their rights. The number of people in need of protection has increased in 2021 due to the prolonged drought. Lower Shabelle and Middle Shabelle are most affected areas, due to recurrence of conflicts, droughts and increased military operations led by the SFG
against AS. The Country Task Force on Monitoring and Reporting (CTFMR) verified and documented. 1,473 cases (1,272 boys and 201 girls) of violence against children, with abduction and recruitment/use of children accounting for 41% and 55 % of the overall number of children affected. According to AYUUB Assessments in Marka and Lower Shabelle in June and Sept 2021, above 100,000 of the IDPs displaced in 2020 have fled due to the deteriorating drought conditions. According to AYUUB Multi-Sectoral Assessment report on June 2021, Marka, Qoryooley, and Wanla Weyn were among the most areas hosted highest IPDs movement, mainly escaped from the droughts, military operations and airstrikes in Lower Shabelle region, representing 27% of the total population in each district, due to the ongoing security operation and airstrikes mainly in Bariire and Aw dheegle areas of Lower Shabelle, an
estimated of ( 1500 HHs) (9000 IDPs) fled to Marka and Afgooye, settling in the Afgooye corridor and Marka IDPs camps, lacking the basic of humanitarian assistance.
The Project delivers protection services that encompasses of Child protection case management through reporting of violations affecting the newly displaced people in Jowhar, Marka, Qoryooley and Kurtunwaarey districts due to the ongoing military operations, airstrikes and droughts in Lower Shabelle region, and supporting these IDPs with Child friendly spaces and psychosocial support (CP) and referral responses of GBV and six grave violations affected individuals to the services facilities in the areas, the project will
also enhance the activities with community mobilization through; targeting the various community members, respecting gender equality inclusion, disabled members, women, youths, IMAMs and community leaders to effectively achieve the project goals. Bearing in mind that SNA and AMSIOM forces sometimes backed by US technical troops increased the military operations against AS in Lower Shabelle region, mainly in Bariire, Awdheegle, Shaan Garre, Malable, Moordiinle, Sabiid and Anoole villages, resulting in the IDPs influx, arrived in parts of Marka and Afgooye districts, lacking the primary services, where protection represents the priority one for the affected community.
The project will provide Family tracing & reunification (CP) to the separated children during the displacement and providing Child friendly spaces service awaiting to reunify to the caregivers. The Project targets 2,800HHs of 16,800 people (5,400 girls, 3,540 boys, 5,480 women and 2380 men) in the various districts mentioned above. The project in collaboration with Child Protection sub-cluster will be delivering CP Referral and Response services, Community based CP Awareness activities in the IDP camps.

1. - Projects activities

Activity 1: Assessment, monitoring, awareness raising and outreach (Child Protection) and reporting of displacement and general protection violations from the IDP camps in Jowhar, Kurtunwaarey, Marka and Qoryooley districts over the project period. Activity 2: Access to Basic General Protection Services including Case management, family tracing and reunification for minors lost during conflict including reintegration for ex-child soldiers and referral services to 2,800HHs or16,800 people (5,400 girls, 3,540 boys, 5,480 women and 2380 men) vulnerable and IDPs in Jowhar, Kurtunwaarey, Marka and Qoryooley districts over the project period. Activity 3: Protection oriented assistance ( solar lamps and dignity kits) for 2,333 HHs with specific needs or heightened vulnerability targeting Jowhar, Kurtunwaarey, Marka and Qoryooley districts. Activity 4: Establish Child friendly spaces and psychosocial support in Jowhar, Merka, Qoryooley and Kurtunwarey districts to reach some 8940 boys and girls.
