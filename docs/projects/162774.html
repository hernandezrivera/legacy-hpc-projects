Project Summary

This initiative will ensure that 153,637 children are protected, feed and retained in school during the emergency.Given the underlying vulnerability in the identified emergency districts, World Vision will focus on supporting the most vulnerable children to enable them to enrol and attend school. In view of the GAM rates which have deteriorated over the year to -2% , the project will have deliberate efforts towards school feeding to encourage school attendance and improve the health status of pupils. Complimentary activities for schools to build their ability to provide nutrition foods from school gardens will be supported. The intervention will specifically provide hot meals for children in primary school every day, and additionally drill boreholes and develop community gardens to ensure there is sustainability. The intervention will also support orphans and vulnerable children through training teachers on Disaster Risk Reduction, psycho-social support, school and catch up activities. There will also be provision of support to disadvantaged children in schools ED to Grade 7 to ensure children into school, increase enrolment of children with a disability, to also make sure they receive materials for learning and block granting to support their schools. The above interventions will be implemented to serve children in the following districts, (Mashonaland Central: (Muzarabani, Mbire), Matabeleland North: (Nkayi, Lupane,), Matabeleland South: (Insiza, Bulilima and Mangwe), (Manicaland: (Chimanimani), Midlands (Chirumanzu), and Harare (Chitungwiza).
