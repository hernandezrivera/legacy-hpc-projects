Project Summary

The crisis in the North West and South West Regions (NW/SW) of Cameroon as well as pre-existing vulnerabilities have led to increase in the victimization of vulnerable groups , notably women, widows and orphans who are often faced with issues of destruction of houses and properties, land grabbing, forced and illegal evictions, secondary occupations and disinheritance.
Women, widows and orphans have particularly been rendered very vulnerable by the crisis situation. Women in particular, face double jeopardy: First, they find themselves in an already precarious conflict situation; secondly, customary system does not recognize their rights to Housing Land and Property outside a man. The fact that most of these women are widows, with their husbands having been killed leaves them at the mercy of close relatives, the rich and powerful in the community who most of the time rob them of their rightful inheritance as well as their HLP rights. Also the vast majority of the women, widows and orphans have no information on their HLP rights or adequate civil status documents such as marriage certificate that entitles them as official wives to inherit their husband’s properties after their death.
The conditions of orphans as well are made precarious because some do not have birth certificates to determine their parentage and consequently their succession rights are also forfeited.
This project seeks to carry out an intense advocacy at the level of administration, judiciary and traditional authorities in the South West Region.
At the level of the administration attention shall be drawn to the question of land grabbing and forced evictions of women, widows and orphans and the need for state authorities to intervene.
Advocacy at the level of the judiciary shall focus on the facilitation of civil status documents for orphans in particular and children in general as well as to end the corrupt practices in the courts which leaves widows, women and orphans at the mercy of the rich and powerful who grab their lands and properties. 7 judicial authorities and 7 administrative authorities are targeted. At the level of traditional authorities the focus will be to advocate for a change in gender discriminatory practices against women and widows and to improve their access to land and property. 20 traditional leaders shall be targeted for this advocacy.
The Project will also carry out intensive communication through the publication of flyers, posters, radio and TV campaigns for the promotion of the HLP rights of Women, Widows and Orphans. A hand book on procedures for obtaining a land certificate shall also be produced and distributed to various women groups across the South West Region.
90 women from selected women led CBOs shall be trained on Advocacy and Behavior Change Communication. This will enable them to participate in community sensitization in the promotion of HLP rights of women, widows and orphans. This training will also help in making the project sustainable as local women groups shall take ownership of the promotion of HLP rights at community level. Social Mobilization shall also be carried out through community educative talks and interpersonal communication through household visits in targeted communities across the South West Region.
Furthermore by the trained community women will serve as channels for complaints and feedback at the community level.
In the first phase of this project for 2024, 1000 vulnerable persons including, widows, orphans including those with disabilities in Fako Division are targeted. The targeted population shall directly gain knowledge on their HLP rights and referral pathways for justice and redress for those whose HLP rights have been violated in Fako Division.
