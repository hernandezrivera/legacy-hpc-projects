Project Summary

IR-Sudan is planning to implement this integrated project which aims to enhance access to basic services education, food security and livelihood, WASH, Health & Nutrition, and protection for vulnerable communities including IDPs, Refugees, and host communities and war affected populations in three States, West Kordofan in El Nuhud, Wad Banda and Babanousa Localities with a total target group 47,498 South Sudan Refugees, 4,727 IDPs war affected and 13,738 vulnerable host communities, Blue Nile with total target 26,240 IDPs affected by recent inter-communal conflict in Rosaires & Wadelmahi localities (10,496 & 15,477) respectively, in addition to around 3,788 IDPs from recent war and Gadaref State Babikri refugee camp and Basundah locality with a total target of 30,000 Ethiopian Refugees and host-communities, as well as war affected populations displaced from KHT, estimated around 7,410 individuals. In Education sector and in line with strategic objectives 1&2 of revised HRP, the project will focus on improving access to quality education through increasing access and improving the school environment for target groups, to ensure increasing additional learning spaces to accommodate the new war affected IDPs students whom displaced with their parents from war hotspot areas, in addition to the Eritrean and South Sudan refugees and resident host communities. The proposed activities under this sector will include enrolment of children in child-friendly learning spaces, provision of recreational (kits), and hygiene/Dignity kits, hand washing stations, safe drinking water, rehabilitate gender responsive latrines, rehabilitate temporary learning spaces, and provision psychosocial support.
In WASH, the project will support refugees, war affected IDPs and host communities to improve their access to WASH services that will include sanitation and hygiene promotion services aligned with strategic objectives 1&2 of revised HRP 2023.
Health sector interventions will focus on increasing access to health services by rehabilitating and refurbishing nonfunctioning health facilities, as well as provision of medical equipment, strengthening the capacity of health care providers and community health care workers to provide essential health services in targeted areas.
In FSL and livelihood, the project will aim to enhance the beneficiaries’ resilience and self-reliance through diversifying their livelihoods options, improving access to employment opportunities and income generating activities, especially for those fleeing from war hotspot areas and lost their work opportunities. This will be achieved through provision of simple agricultural tools, provision of seeds, deliver extension trainings to farmers and conduct vocational trainings for youths and support them with income means. The project will support women households in particular with provision of goats restocking to help in promoting their income, and train them on food processing.
The protection interventions will focus on delivering services to acutely vulnerable boys and girls, girls and boys will be provided with specialized child protection response services. Specific sources of threat challenging vulnerabilities of children will be identified and addressed particularly high risk of abuse, neglect, exploitation, or violence. Vulnerable and at-risk children will be provided with gender & age appropriate (structured) psychosocial support and life skills including literacy and numeracy. Communities will be mobilized to support children through activation of child protection networks. Finally the project will enhance and build human resource capacity through targeted groups to respond to child protection concerns at all levels.

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

across all sectors the project is going to implement, IRW will mainstream protection and GBV. The project will ensure that services access points are no cause of protection concerns or give way for GBV occurrence. In all trainings that are rendered by the project, a session will be dedicated to cover issues related with protection and GBV to increase alertness and vigilance among the community structures. In addition to the project member’s structures, the project will also have a Protection and GBV concern focal points who will be equipped with the required support so that they are able to observe and identify any concerns and immediately notify the project member’s structures for the required immediate action. IR Sudan staff will ensure all IR representatives involved in the project including staff, volunteers, implementing partners, contractors, donors or guests when visiting are aware of and adhere to IR Sudan’s Code of Conduct. The IRS Code of Conduct sets out all steps that are reasonably possible to protect children, vulnerable women and men that are beneficiaries of the project from violence, exploitation, neglect and abuse perpetrated by employees, partner organizations, contractors, official visitors and their communities. Communities and children will be sensitized on the rights of children and made aware of the complaints mechanism in place to ensure they feel comfortable to report and concerns of abuse and confident that appropriate action will be taken and will ensure that they are protected from any repercussions. Regular monitoring and auditing of the IR field offices will be carried out by CO technical staff to ensure that policies and procedures are observed, and participation and feedback from the communities is availed.

4 - AAP & PSEA
4.1 - How will the project share information and collect community feedback? Please select as many as relevant.

Face to face with individuals (in homes) through aid workers

4.1.1 - How will the project use community feedback to improve and adapt activities? (open-ended/free text, required)4

IRW has in place a robust complaint response mechanism (CRM) strategy that is fully implemented during project life-cycle and are culturally appropriate, accessible and effective in line with CHS 5. These allows for timely response and feedback to the affected population. Three levels of monitoring beneficiaries feedbacks will followed for the purpose of quality delivery and continuous improvement of services. These are the establishment of complaint boxes, to enable affected populations to report their complaints and provide feedback, The CRM number and email address is printed on each activity implementation banners in local language to allow beneficiaries to report and CRM mobile number is also provided which can be used by majority of the affected populations. These three levels of obtaining community feedbacks is crucial aspect ensured by IRW staff to manage and respond to feedbacks for improvement purposes.

4.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will support Inter-Agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including Sexual exploitation and abuse complaints?

Complaints management is a critical and pivotal aspect of program accountability and continuous improvement of any service delivery program. Islamic Relief worldwide recognizes the need for a standardized approach to establish and strengthen complaints handling mechanism and related processes to be able to demonstrate effectiveness and impact. Special CRSM- Complaint Response Mechanism System for all planned activities will be developed to ensure quality services are delivered to communities in positive manner and feedbacks were collected for improvement purposes on programme delivery. Global guiding document has been developed through a consultative process at local and global levels. Islamic Relief continues to stand firm on zero tolerance for (sexual) misconduct. Therefore, through all delivered interventions IRW, will make sure to create and maintain an environment that prevents sexual exploitation and sexual abuse. IRW staff at all levels have a particular responsibility to support and develop systems that maintain this environment which includes placing and having direct contacts with the target population to manage and deal with such cases if happened. IRW used to share mobile number where beneficiaries can provide feedback, suggestions or complaints on SEA activities or miss appropriateness.
