Project Summary

Empathy Foundation for Africa is a national Non -governmental Organization established since 2017 operating in Awerial County ,Lakes state and currently funded by the UNFAO in Awerial County, Lakes State. The organization have strong field presence in Awerial and in Wau, Western Bahr-El Ghazal.
EFA is proposing in the current HRP 2020 South appeal document a project running from 1st January 2020 to December 31st , 2020. The key objective of the project is to increase vulnerable household access to food through support to increased food production and to reduce the level of vulnerability amongst malnourished children through linkage of nutrition related activities to Food security activities. The project is line with HRP strategic objective 2 ( SO2) and is in support of FSL cluster objective 2.
The project will target mainly IDPs, returnees and vulnerable host population in these areas. A total of 120,000 beneficiaries in the two States. These beneficiaries will be supported through direct input distribution of the inputs, resilience light training, vaccination and treatment.
The project proposing has strong gender consideration , considering protection issues and will integrate nutrition interventions.
Whilst the project will depend on FAO pipeline for its supplies , the project is very much cost effective proposing to reach out to 120,000 people with little resources but then saving lives and creating some stabilization for the food insecure households

1 - Needs

The most recent IPC report In August 2019, an estimated 6.35 million people (54% of the population) are classified in Crisis (IPC Phase 3) or worse acute food insecurity, among whom an estimated 1.7 million people are facing Emergency (IPC Phase 4) acute food insecurity and 10,000 people are in Catastrophe (IPC phase 5). Compared to the same period in 2018, there is a slight reduction in the proportion of people facing Crisis (IPC Phase 3) or worse acute food insecurity by an estimated 5%. However, high levels of acute food insecurity still persist in the country. The main drivers of this conflict and insecurity mainly by cattle raiding, displacement , economic decline, flooding , fall army worm in these areas. The IPC report specifically projects that by April, 2020, the food insecurity is expected to deteoriate further with the depletion of the current harvest in the entire country. In Lakes State , the high food insecurity is linked to cattle raiding which have caused huge displacement this year that have resulted to low production; poor access to food due to restricted movement to productive farmland, limited access to natural food sources, Seasonally poor feeder road conditions causing low market supply and irregular salary payment to the civil servants and outbreak of fall army worm which extensively destroyed most of the crops.In Awerial , movement toward Awerial County and Mingkaman informal settlement sitecontinued in June, driven by access to humanitarian services, perceptions of safety, and presence of relatives. Furthermore,conflict at the border of Yirol West and Rumbek East counties in late May resulted in displacement toward Mapourdit, Tali, Yirol Town, andMingkaman informal settlement site.; Only 30% of assessed settlements in eastern Lakes reported having adequate food access and more than half of the assessed,settlements reported eating only one meal a day. In June, poor harvests in 2018 caused by irregular rainfall and insecurity remained the primary reason for inadequate access to food. (REACH 2019). A total of Nationally, by April, 2020 s per the IPC , The nutrition situation is further expected to deteoriate generally in the Country of which more than 5.50 million people are expected to be in crisis (IPC Phase3) or worse from January to April 2020. In Jur River and Wau Counties the same trend will of having malnourished children will happen. Generally in these two Counties of former Bahr-el ghazal, a total of 293,150 Households are expected to face food insecurity by April, 2020.In light of the above justification, by April 2020, there will be high need to address the food security situation , hence the need for this appeal project.

2 - Activities

The key activities are: Distribution of main agricultural crops seeds, vegetable seeds, Tools and fishing, distribution of dry season vegetable seeds, tools, fishing kits Training of farmers in resilience light intervention, formation and training of Accountability to affected Population committee. Vaccination of livestock Treatment of Livestock. These activities are seasonally realistic and fit the context of the two States.
