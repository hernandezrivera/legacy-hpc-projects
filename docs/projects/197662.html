Project Summary

The project will consist of promoting health, hygiene and sanitation. In the health component, the project will focus on guaranteeing the consumption of drinking water for all displaced people and hosting families. In the hygiene and sanitation component, the project will consist of the distribution of hygiene and sanitation material, promotion and dissemination of messages on construction and proper use of latrines by all project beneficiaries. With this, Caritas intends to work with 5,000 people, including men, women, children and people with disabilities.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project will consist of promoting health, hygiene and sanitation. In the health component, the project will focus on guaranteeing the consumption of drinking water for all displaced people and hosting families, it will also focus on the spread of COVID-19 prevention messages. In the hygiene and sanitation component, the project will consist of the distribution of hygiene and sanitation material, promotion and dissemination of messages on construction and proper use of latrines by all project beneficiaries.The project is aimed at 5,000 displaced people and will be implemented in the districts of Ancuabe. The project will be implemented for the period of 12 months (January 2023 to December 2023).

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

As mentioned above, the project will benefit 5000 people from the Ancuabe district, including adults, men and women, children and people with disabilities, according to the activities below.- Construction of new water sources for host communities and resettlement areas- Promotion activities for self-construction of household latrines and garbage pits in resettlement sites and hosting communities (including the provision of slabs)- Dissemination of WASH related messages - Procurement and/or distribution of WASH related Non-Food items, including menstrual hygiene health materials- PSEA trainings and feedback to communities- Gender based violence prevention safety audit for WASH planning"

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The risks will be identified at the field level and the potential risks to be identified and mitigated (places of provision of the project services; gender balance in the project; The type of product or service to be provided. )-Free and easy access to people: areas or places accessible to all beneficiaries, including people with disabilities, will be selected and conditioned to guarantee non-exclusion without exclusion. Gender balance in the project: this component guarantees that the project has complete data, for example: it is easier for a lady to talk to another woman to report something that is bothering her than with a man, and this prevents cases in the community.Service to provide: identify based on community needs, and avoid giving a non-food product without explaining how to use it.Promote lectures in the community to explain the project and all the possible services to be made available, this avoids a bad perception and creates risks during implementation

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

Consultations will be carried out with individual people, including specific reflections with older women and men, who are the most vulnerable. All kit distributions will be made close to the residences, and there will be a team that will help transport your kits.As a group of identified women will explain to the women in the village, the correct use of menstrual hygiene management kits according to the WASH Cluster hygiene kit standards. Locating WASH infrastructure will include consultation with women and girls to ensure these groups feel involved.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

As it is considered an inclusive project, people in need will be the focus of Caritas, including the elderly to identify specific needs for infrastructure adaptation. The infrastructure will be adapted to these needs.Persons with disabilities will be one of the priorities during distributions. Distribution points will be serviced, ensuring ease of access for community members, especially people with disabilities and women.If there are any people with disabilities and if they have some leadership character and ability to do some work, such as registering, organizing beneficiaries, writing reports, they can be included in the field team.For those who cannot walk, some transportation will be provided to transport their NFIs.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The focus of the project is health, hygiene and sanitation, so field agents will be trained to explain to local target communities how to keep the environment clean, avoiding littering in inappropriate places, promoting the use of latrines to avoid water contamination , obviously respecting the environment, avoiding digging big holes, throwing garbage anywhere and promoting debates on hygiene.Some individual and collective hygiene sessions will be held by the activists to ensure the well-being of the entire society.
