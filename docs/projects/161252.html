Project Summary

APDA is a National NGO, currently operating in Rubkona County Unity State.
In line with the HRP for 2020 WASH cluster strategic objectives 2 , Specific objective 2.1 and 2.2 and WASH strategic objective 3, specific objective 3, APDA targets to support 30000 vulnerable IDPS, host communities and returnees with critical WASH Intervention services for the year 2020.
The project is envisaged to intervene in:

a) Providing timely and Sustainable equitable access to safe water sanitation and hygiene for IDPS, host communities and returnees through safe water supply, improved secured and gender appropriate sanitation facilities and

b) Mitigating WASH related gender –based violence IDPS, host communities and returnees by 31st Dec 2020.
C)To provide timely / sustainable equitable access to safe water sanitation and hygiene for IDPs host communities and returnees.
The project focusses on the acute emergency WASH needs of displaced population, returnees, host communities and those affected by conflicts, floods, disease’s outbreaks and natural disasters.
With the current Large number of nonfunctional water points in these counties with displacement and migration associated with diseases such as diarrhea and cholera, its expected that the affected population will continue facing shortages of safe drinking water and other WASH related supplies. The project is designed to respond and prioritize the emergency WASH needs of these displaced population and their host communities, IDPs, and all other population affected and in critical need for WASH intervention.
Cross cutting and standardization will be promoted through ensuring the provision of emergency , gender balanced and environment friendly emergency sanitation promotion and safe water interventions with adequate quality and quantity (minimum 15 liters/capita/day and other SPHERE standards) for drinking, cooking and hygiene purposes.
Project intervention Strategy prioritizes rehabilitation of water sources over new construction where nonfunctional water points and sanitation facilities are available in for the target populations.
These interventions will include handpump water supplies, pipe schemes, reservoir cleaning and repair, surface water treatment and disinfection, while water transportation will be a last option in case the situation demands.
APDA therefore wishes to seek for support for these emergency WASH interventions in these critical areas from for the period starting from January 1st JAN to December 31st , 2020.

1 - Needs

The crisis and conflicts that has been prevailing in south Sudan for the last several years has continued to drastically affect the living conditions of many south Sudanese. The resultant effect of displacements and conflicts has negatively compromised the community resilience to mitigate their ability on WASH needs. Chronic WASH deficits in the Unity states region have aggravated since the July 2016 conflict and the resulting state in the 4 year period has resulted in many families in dire need of humanitarian support. These major WASH vulnerabilities and nutrition crisis are driven by market failure and the depletion of livelihood assets. In Unity State, the Global acute malnutrition exceeds the emergency threshold. These continuing WASH and food vulnerabilities are likely to result in increased chronic malnutrition and micronutrient deficiencies and disease outbreaks among vulnerable groups. As depicted by the 2019-2020 South Sudan IPC findings. Latest IPC report In August 2019, an estimated 6.35 million people (54% of the population) are classified in Crisis (IPC Phase 3) or worse acute food insecurity, among whom an estimated 1.7 million people are facing Emergency (IPC Phase 4). The main drivers of this conflict and insecurity is mainly by cattle raiding, displacement, economic decline, flooding , epidemic disease outbreaks , fall armyworm and the general lack of knowledge that can mitigate in food insecurity and WASH vulnerabilities . In Unity State, the communities of Rabkona County has continued to experience diverse and serious natural and human catastrophic effects. This has weakened their resilience in the mitigation of their livelihoods and WASH coping capacities as many households are displaced, their means of livelihoods destabilized, markets have been disrupted, supply corridors have remained insecure and many have no financial capacity to purchase their basic needs for WASHs or mitigate on their livelihoods . Majority of the HH are WASH insecure and many result to coping mechanism for survival. These IDPs and their Host communities and all the vulnerable people need WASH support in order to cope with the existing situation. In many instances it’s the Women and girls bear the burden of Water provision and usually suffer the blunt of marginalization to access safe and secure water points . With the current large number of nonfunctional water points in these counties with displacement and migration associated with diseases such as diarrhea and cholera, its expected that the affected population will continue facing shortages of safe drinking water and other WASH related needs. Project intervention Strategy prioritizes rehabilitation of water sources over new construction where nonfunctional water points and sanitation facilities are available in for the target populations. It also en devours to promote durable solutions which will lead to long lasting interventions which will eventually lead to development. These interventions will include, handpump water supplies, motorisisation of water yards, Promotion of community participation and engagement, reservoir cleaning and repair, surface water treatment and disinfection, CTLS. This project will address the vital needs of public health and protection issues through an AAP community approach targeting the most vulnerable with gender balanced and environment friendly emergency sanitation promotion and safe water interventions with adequate quality and quantity (minimum 15 liters/capita/day and other SPHERE standards) for drinking, cooking and hygiene purposes. It will also focus to provide and promote health and lifesaving engineering to stop transmission of water borne diseases in critical contexts e.g in schools, funerals, health facilities, markets and institutions. WASH training will also be conducted for health workers.APDA therefore wishes to seek for support for 30000 populations in Rabkona who are in critical needs of emergency WASH interventions.

8.18 - Please indicate how the risks selected above will be mitigated.

To address protection of the vulnerable populations especially the women Children and the elderly, a risk analysis is usually conducted to inform and to weigh in the best options of inclusiveness. Better Targeting of the beneficiaries and site selection for WASH fairs are carefully considered so that support assistance given mitigates risks and ‘Does No Harm’ to these populations. Special messages with warnings addressing forced recruitment of children or gender-based violence are communicated to ensure that these marginalized and vulnerable groups access assistance; with an understanding WASH support provided does not exacerbate tensions between different social & ethnic groups. In addition, helpdesks shall be established at each WASH NFI distribution point to allow for targeted households and/or other community members to raise complaints, ask questions or provide feedback. Optimization of inclusive participation – as part of the targeting and beneficiary selection process, communities will be consulted through community forums to ensure that all the vulnerable and WASH insecure families are included in the program Information regarding support will be clearly disseminated to ensure that the whole communities including vulnerable groups of women, men, girls and boys, elderly and disabled have a clear understanding of targeting, and selection criteria, entitlements and utilization standards. This will iron out dissatisfactions which could arise from simple misunderstandings. All Wash activities will start early in the day and the disabled, female headed households, the elderly and he children will be given special priority during fair days so that they receive their support early to enable them travel home in time before dusk

9.14 - How will you project ensure feedback from the most vulnerable community members?

AAP needs assessment will be carried out before the project starts . All affected populations will be party and will be included in the PMC and awareness will be created and information given to the beneficiaries while at the same time gathering their expectations on what and how and when as they want for the projects. This will comprise of: how they will access their needs,  the type assistance to be provided,  how they prefer to receive assistance Translation of languages into the local dialect shall be undertaken as well as pictures and images with signs to guide especially those who cant read and write. To monitor the project implementation an M&E, Framework and tools and an inbuilt feedback mechanism (help desks for feedback) to evaluate the progress of the project implementation and impacts for all activities will be undertaken . This will be carried out together with the affected populations and in close collaboration with other partners, to gather information on relevance and satisfaction of beneficiaries. The activity will be desegregated by gender, age and disability i.e, women, men, boys and girls, the disabled and the elderly and will evaluate in terms of timeliness, input distribution, relevance of the inputs and services offered and distributed, quality and quantity of services offered and overall organization and modalities of service provision including security and protection issues. The feedback from the findings will then be used to streamline and restructure the project to align with the needs and preferences of the beneficiaries. The set inbuilt mechanism will be confidential, accessible and responsive and will give information to the beneficiaries about the existing complaint and feedback mechanism. The feedback gotten will be used to adapt, align and redesign project assistance to meet the needs of the affected populations..
