Project Summary

This campaign is CCCL’s major campaign for the year 2023. Through this campaign, we are asking donors and supporters from all over the globe, to help the center’s mission in this critical time where all cancer related medications are scarce with less governmental subsidies and support in addition to the devaluation of the Lebanese Lira; and this is through donating via this campaign to help the center save kids’ lives
it is a part of an on-going project to fund raise for the treatment of children with cancer for the next 12 months. this evidential shortages of cancer medicines; specifically Leukemia related medicine, in the country and sky-rocketing prices which both mean a much needed intervention by CCCL is even more critical than ever and the support of international and national partners and supporters can go long way in fulfilling the goal of the project.
Today, we need to insure the cost of treatments and medication for 1 year so that we can provide to the most vulnerable children with ALL.
At CCCL we have a survival rate of 80% but can reach in some types of cancer the 92%, such as the ALL, It is unacceptable to lower this survivorship percentage because treatments and medications are not secured. It is unacceptable because this percentage is not a number. This percentage is kids with names, parents, siblings and friends at school. It is unacceptable because your support on treatments of ALL and the related medications will permit us to fulfilling this mission.
the exporters, pharmacists, government, hospitals and providers of services such as the CCCL continued to struggle in ensuring the most needed supply of cancer medication and paying the hospitalisation bills. this shortages and the out of pockets payement of hospitalisation is surely published and mentioned by all kind of medias, be it audio and visual, written and social network. Reasons are many but it can be summarized by the lack of foreign currency and cash in the country, movement of capitals internationally and nationally to pay the international pharma and importers of these medication and many more such as the political and socio-economic collapse.
the CCCL have identified a list of most crucial and urgent needs for the next 12 months and is aiming on building international affiliations and partnerships to ensure the needed fund to safe guard the ALL treatment of patients and save their lives. around 15 children with cancer could benefit from this project and hence retain the survivor rate and the quality treatments for our children suffering from all sort of difficulties which will includes treatments such as chemo therapy and beyond such as psychosocial support. this project will ensure this continuum and the quality of care to 50% of children with cancer in Lebanon.
The leukemia’s are the most common cancer in childhood, accounting for about 41 % of all tumors in children less than 15 years of age. Acute Lymphoblastic Leukemia (ALL) and Acute Myeloid Leukemia (AML) account for 77 % and 11 % of cases of childhood leukemia’s, respectively. The single most important prognostic factor in ALL is the treatment. Though fatal without effective therapy, survival rates at 5 years from diagnosis around 90% with current treatment. ALL treatment consists primarily of chemotherapy, while some patients with AML may also need stem cell transplant for cure.
the support of this project ensure the full cost of treatments for 15 childhood cancer patients with leukemia.
