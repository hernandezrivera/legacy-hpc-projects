Project Summary

Drought in Somalia has been a more and more recurring phenomena. During times of severe drought households and communities adapt their activities, roles and responsibilities in order to survive. Difficult decisions are made which consequently change usual patterns, activities and roles among women, girls, men and boys. E.g. movements or abandonment of pastoral areas and moving to urbanized centers; then women becoming breadwinners as men lose their livestock; men contributing more to household work; women being invited to participate in community committees by NGOs, etc. The proposed Protection package aims to increase the resilience, capacities and protective environment of the targeted communities by strengthening community-based protection mechanisms through capacity building, awareness raising, service mapping, and referrals toward mitigating risks (including SGBV), especially for women and girls. Community-based structures, including Community Protection Volunteers, Women’s Forum, Community Committees and duty bearers will play a key role in the project. A protection analysis will be conducted in the target locations to identify existing (strategic and practical) needs and dynamics, vulnerabilities, and threats faced by women, men, boys, and girls. The analyses will inform project activities and will form the basis for the definition of protection messaging. Household solar lamps and wheelbarrows will be provided to contribute to safety and security, and to reduce exposure to SGBV risks. Mapping existing services will improve access to information, establishing protection reporting mechanisms, a referral system, and training community volunteers to reduce protection vulnerabilities while increasing the capacities of the target population, who will be able to make informed decisions.

Project Activities

The intense and persistent drought beginning in 2016 has devastated Somaliland, wiping out millions of livestock, the key to rural livelihoods, and displacing over 700,000 people. Beginning of 2019, hundreds of thousands of people remain displaced, precariously surviving. Recovery remains elusive, as drought conditions continue and people lack the resources to rebuild livelihoods.The longstanding humanitarian crisis has been aggravated by the impact of consecutive poor rainy seasons, including in decades of conflict, triggering large-scale new displacement, and increasing protection concerns. The worst drought affected areas remain Sool, Sanaag in Somaliland. In these regions, drought has continued to exacerbate existing vulnerabilities and further exposed civilians to serous protection concerns, particularly among women, children, elderly, persons with disabilities and marginalized communities. Droughts augmented the responsibility of women and girls and increased activities that expose protection risks. Further, protracted displacement amplifies risks as competition for resources increase, and other tensions grow, such as amongst clans, and between hosts and internally displaced persons (IDPs).The Protection awareness and information dissemination, service mapping/ referral pathways in target areas, showing medical, psychosocial legal services, e.t.c and subject focus of service map will include availability conditions of access, contact details and locations. Carry out protection and gender analysis to identify key protection risks identify gender differentiated needs priorities and inform safe programming and gender mainstreaming during program design and implementation. Establish and train women’s forums to increase participation of women in decision-making and address their protection issues , support them to identify GBV risks and carry out prevention or mitigation measures. A Jointly with women’s forums and protection volunteers, village committees will identify protection and emergency services to facilitate safe timely referrals for survivors. A basic protection training will be provided traditional leaders to strengthen referral of GBV cases to formal system rather than solving traditionally. Trainings for authorities on protection issues and to do advocacy for improvement in better policy making on women protection issues specifically with regards to DRR and disaster response and pre-existing community structures e.g community based committees will be training on basic protection concepts and referral, path ways GBV awareness response committees on basic protection concepts GBV and do no harm. Further material support will be provided the established community protection volunteers and women’s forum’s. Women and girls at child bearing age will receive dignity kits that contain sanitary materials and other items that explicitly tailored towards the local needs of women and girls and have direct impact on their mobility and community involvement and other equipment including wheel barrows and solar lights will be distributed for most vulnerable households to reduce vulnerability on existing protection treats.

Mitigation Measures

Risk:- Members from of marginalized community’s/ minority groups are not included in consultations and/or are excluded from humanitarian services/assistanceMitigation measures:- proper community engagement, the program teams will ensure active participation of different parties of communities including minorities and marginalized groups, protection analysis will be carried out in the program intervention sites to understand different protection risks faced vulnerable target communities and adopt practical mitigation measures, participatory protection risk analysis will be carried out the beginning of the program, the risk analysis will be updated regularly to monitor evolving protection risks and adopt the program accordingly. The selection criteria and process of beneficiaries will be clearly indicated most vulnerable minority groups. Risk :- Women, girls and boys are not included in consultations and decision-making processesMitigation measures :- The program will ensure that all parties of women men boys and girls have access to participation process more specifically the program will create mechanism for women and girls to participate consultation and decision making process by establishing and capacitating women’s forums in each project sites, the women’s forums will be trained on identification of barriers of accessing consultation and decision making process while they will conduct regular meetings to discuss issues specifically affecting women and girls and present identified issue to village committee meeting and will be incorporated committee plans to address those issues. The consultation of women and girls will be the first priority of the program a dedicated technical team will support programs in mainstreaming protection and gender further all Oxfam and partner teams are trained on gender mainstreaming and safe programming. Risk:- Beneficiaries risk exposure to violence during registration/distributionMitigation measures:- Oxfam and partner staff are trained protection mainstreaming. Carry out protection risk analysis of each activity with participation of target women men boys and girls and regularly update to monitor evolving risks. Prioritize safety and dignity of beneficiaries, consult with local community place and time of distribution. Risk :- Barriers for beneficiaries to report concerns through complaint and feedback mechanismsMitigation measures:- strengthen accountability feedback mechanism through continuous induction and sensitizing the community raising feedback is rightfully theirs. Regular promotion of the system through behavioral change communications mainly posters (demonstrated in local language) & organization of accountability sessions at village level to build confidence of the community on the use of the system.Ensure all program teams promote accountability feedback mechanism at all levels and program staff will be trained on accountability feedback mechanisms. ensure a committed staff to be engaged in managing the system including logging in complaints, following up, and closing the feedback loop with the support of the programme/project teams. Enough budget should be allocated for the system and the staff member who is handling the system. In addition to that Oxfam and partner staff receive regular trainings on safe programing and set clear action points and there are dedicated technical protection team to support the process of protection mainstreaming.

Please provide further explanation

The project focuses on building community based protection mechanisms of affected communities by build the capacity of Community Protection Volunteers, community committees and women's forums to identify protection risks in their communities and to take actions to mitigate them which will include practical self-protection measures, advocacy, awareness raising and referrals with the aim of building community resilience and recovery. These actions will increase community resilience and by developing mitigation strategies for solving their own problems without external support. These will also build community cohesion to act or respond the problems together, thus social cohesion will increase in community that promoted and result durable solutions in the long run. These actions also engagement in community action plans that can end up commitments to durable solutions. The Project will build on enhancing community engagements, ownership and capacity building through trainings to act as strong agent of change in their communities. Community-based structures, including Community Protection Volunteers, Women’s Fora, Community Committees and duty bearers will play a key role in the project. A protection and gender analysis will be conducted in the target locations to identify practical and strategic needs of women, men, boys, and girls.
