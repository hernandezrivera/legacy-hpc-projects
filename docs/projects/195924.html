Project Summary

The project will target the IDP’s and Host Community in the targeted locations in order to improve the safe, adequate, and sustainable access for these services along with the improvement for the public health and environment to avoid any outbreaks caused by contamination of the drinking water and lack in the SWM which might cause an outbreak in leishmaniasis either cholera. In this project the rehabilitation for water system, sanitation system, and restoration for the SWM system. Moreover, rehabilitation for water system will target increasing the efficiency of the water station and network by conducting the needed maintenance, replacement, and extension for the existing infrastructure and water station as well along with establishment for water ow meters in order to re-activate the tariff system. Rehabilitation for the sanitation system will target maintenance for the sewage network to repair the leakage points to avoid the mixture between drinking water and wastewater along with conduct needed extension for the network in order to ensure that all shelters and settlements are connected to the sewerage network to ensure the best practice for wastewater disposal along with install for rain drainage in the targeted communities and collective centers to avoid ods which caused by heavy rain. The Solid Waste Management restoration will cover distribution for the containers in the targeted areas along with providing the needed equipment, trucks, and tools to the local council/ authorities to collect and transfer the solid waste to the related landll which will also be rehabilitated. Distribution for Hygiene kits (WASH NFI supplies) will be considered for the IDP’s in Camps and communities one-two times during the project duration the in 2022 and 2023 (104,142 beneciaries) will receive a hygiene kit (WASH NFI's) based on the need along with consideration for age and gender in each family along with hygiene promotion session for each family, where the main topic will be COVID 19 pre-cautionary measures. In addition, each family will receive 20 $ via cash voucher two times per year in order to purchase their needs from the hygiene items, BINAA considers all the precautionary measures against COVID 19. A wastewater treatment plant rehabilitation will take place in Al-Rai, reaching over 40.000 beneficiaries in Al-RAi and Waqf communities by conducting rehabilitation work for Al Rai wastewater treatment plant which has been established by the Syrian government in 2011, in parallel with the wastewater treatment plant in Al Rai, implementation/rehabilitation of the sewage network in Al Rai & Waqf communities will take place to link both communities to the waste after treatment plant.

3 - Needs

Access to sufficient and affordable safe water, adequate sanitation, solid waste management and/or hygiene supplies will remain a challenge for more than 2 million people seeking shelter in IDP Sites. They are highly dependent on continued humanitarian assistance, with 95 per cent of interviewed residents of IDP sites having received some kind of WASH humanitarian assistance. Water trucking services are the only source of water for 73 per cent IDP sites residents and only 39 per cent HHs are connected to (simplified) sewer network, indicating a continued need for medium-term investment in IDP sites infrastructure to provide more sustainable, affordable, and safer way to supply water and dispose wastewater. Despite the sector assistance, 43 per cent of IDPs in sites couldn’t access one or multiple hygiene items, 32 per cent faced barriers to effective handwashing. Also, 17 per cent of HHs reported issues with toilets functionality or wastewater disposal. Disruptions and poor quality of services in IDP sites have also direct negative physical and mental well-being and protection consequences, notably on women and girls, and can ease the spread of waterborne disease, as cholera, with 65 suspected cases reported in 10 IDPs sites by 15th October 2022.
