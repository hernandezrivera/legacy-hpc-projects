Project Summary

The project consists of two main components:
**Component 1 for 24 months - Standard facility-based primary healthcare services through 3 health facilities according to the Gaziantep Health Cluster's EHSP, including the integration of the following services:
-Mental health and psychosocial support
-Screening of acute malnutrition and treatment of moderate acute malnutrition and severe acute malnutrition without complications, referral to care for severe acute malnutrition with complications, and counseling for Infant and Young Child Feeding (IYCF),
-GBV first-line support with referral to specialized GBV services,
-NCD care,
-Provision of facilitated referral to diagnostic services
The PHC locations and codes are:
G0703011 ! Sarmada PHC Center
G0704018 ! Zarzour PHC
G0704049 ! REA Zarzor Mobile Clinic
This component is planned to serve 244,800 beneficiaries.
**Component 2 for 24 months - Outreach community health services through 150 CHWs linked with health facilities to provide the CHWG standard package of services including related social mobilization, active detection and community referrals to standard health facilities for nutrition, CD, NCD, MH, and GBV cases. CHWs will provide additional services to PWDs and vulnerable people including assisted referral to healthcare services using a community-based referral SoP, provision of assistive devise following medical consultation, and active HH follow-up for NCD patients. The 300 CHWs will be linked with UDER and non-UDER supported health facilities.
This component is planned to serve 235,200 beneficiaries.
UDER is sharing the information about this project with members of the coordination clusters in Gaziantep. At the moment, UDER coordinates with a number of actors to provide complementary services at the mentioned facilities.. UDER will continue engaging with the PHC network in Harim to ensure that the facilities are part of the coordinated referral mechanism and applying the same standards and information system (DHIS2) for streamlined data collection.
