Project Summary

This project is equipping small projects for orphan’s families and train them on managing those projects. This aiming at achieving financial revenues, which will benefit families economically. Moreover, increasing endurance, earn a living opportunity and receiving essential services for the affected. These activities will be through 79 small projects as follows:
1- 79 project of animal resources
a. Dairy cows’ projects: dairy cow + barn + feeders for 6 months
b. Dairy cattle projects: 6 dairy cattle + fertilization sheep + barn + feeders for 6 months
2. Training 79 families on how to manage small businesses and best economic practices
5. Training 79 family on best practices to deal with animal resources
Description of the problem/ problems will be solved
- Orphans and their families who suffer from complicated problem. Those families do not have breadwinners, and their economical and livelihood situation affect their social, educational and medical situation negatively.
- The project will enhance economic situation and will encourage family to cooperate and produce
Result/ expected results of the project:
Safeguard orphan’s family independently
Enhance income level of targeted families
Added value of the project for targeted sector
Transfer from production state, self-sufficiency, and enhancement of social, economic, educational and medical aspect for the targeted families

Needs

As per HNO draft report 2019, 9 million people in need . Syrian citizens strive to stay alive after their whole life was destroyed due to the conflict for nearly six years. Millions of people are exhausted from adaptation strategy. Nearly 69% of citizens suffer from poverty and nearly 35% suffer from extreme poverty characterized by severe deprivation from foodstuff required for survival. The potential of having essential services stopped almost completely, including medical care, clean water and education. Some families resort to unsustainable and unsafe means to survive, including compulsory and/or early marriage, child labor and child soldiering to survive.On the other hand, increase of prices and decrease of currency value in 2016 obliged Syrian families to struggle to earn their living. Earn living opportunities decreased more than before; also loss of income affected millions of dependents.Nearly 85% of the Syrian citizens are below poverty line, while 69% of them suffer from poverty as 35% of them lives in extreme poverty. Those who suffer from extreme poverty are not able to afford essential needs for their families. It is likely that, family income may confront more pressure especially after the rise of fuel prices. This reality has created the need for operational solutions instead of temporary rescue solutions. Those solutions proved to be less effective over the long run, in addition to its negative psychological effects on the affected people desires to work and secure their life.
