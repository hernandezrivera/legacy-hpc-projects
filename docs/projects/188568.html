Project Summary

In 2021, the Rescue & Relief Team is planning to Provide Hygiene kits to the most vulnerable HC and IDPs in Northern east Syria in Kisreh and Sur in Dier Ez-Zor as emergency assistance.
The project has been designed based on the assessment that has been done by the end of the year 2021 and January 2022 by the Rescue & Relief Team . in cooperation with its partners and the needs of the people in Northern Syria in Kisreh and Sur in Dier Ez-Zor for basic humanitarian assistance especially the WASH NFIs through direct distribution of Hygiene kits as follows:
- Distribution of 9,899 Hygiene kits for 9,899 crisis-affected groups (3,599 Kits are secured) two times. Through the proposed project, the Rescue & Relief Team aims to contribute to covering the basic WASH needs of the displaced through hygiene kits distribution.
Rescue & Relief Team will target IDPs living within local communities within the of their displacement.
Rescue & Relief Team will take a specific approach by targeting the neediest families within the target communities, especially those headed by a woman, special needs, large number of children or pregnant and lactating women.
The Hygiene kits will be designed in accordance with the Cluster recommended package. while designing the Hygiene kits, the Rescue & Relief Team will take into account the beneficiaries' preference and their feedback through the latest PDMs conducted regarding the Hygiene kits distributed by Rescue & Relief Team. The project implementation will be done through the following steps:
1. signing MOUs with the community representative and receiving the initial list of the beneficiaries
2. Conducting the verification of the beneficiaries in terms of matching the selection criteria and updating beneficiary lists accordingly, taking into account the displacement duration, WASH needs for female community members, children, elderly, or persons with chronic illnesses
3. Distributing Hygiene kits for the selected beneficiaries (IDPs living within local communities) according to the agreed criteria, special requirements or opportunities for women to strengthen their confidence and abilities are considered, RRT team will distributing the kits for two batches.
4. Conducting monitoring activities: check-list within the distribution and PDM, Gender balance within Rescue & Relief Team MEAL team will be insured to allow culturally sensitive questions to be asked of women, by women, so that the program continues to be informed by and respond to the specific needs of women men boys and girls.
During the project implementation, the Rescue & Relief Team will constantly raise awareness for the scope of feedback, expected staff behavior, and selection criteria.

4 - Needs

The majority of displaced (80%) are women and children, who are facing increased protection risks, combined without the ability to cover basic needs. Multiple displacements increase their vulnerability. It also forced them to leave behind belongings. The heightened concentration of people puts an additional burden on already overstretched but also damaged infrastructure. Most urgent is providing shelter to IDPs. However, there is a shortage of accommodation, which is forcing them to stay out of camps. The overcrowding and lack of WASH facilities have led to privacy and protection concerns for women and are increasingly forced early marriage among displaced girls as a coping mechanism. Not only rehabilitation of WASH facilities is needed, but also distribution of hygiene kits. Child protection concerns are heightening, also due to drop out from schools. The winter conditions are increasing the vulnerability further with the need for NFIs, food, shelter, health, and WASH. Basic services, such as health and education, are highly limited due to damaged and directly targeted infrastructure such as hospitals. Besides all lack of basic items and services, IDPs are in high need of psychosocial support and psychological first aid to accommodate them in their current situation.
