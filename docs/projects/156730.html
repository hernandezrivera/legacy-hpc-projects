Project Summary

Sweida has been considered as safe and stable place in Syria, thousands have flocked to Sweida to escape violence in war-shattered cities as Daraa, Hassaka, Raka, Deir El-Zour and Aleppo. And the number of IDPs in Sweida makes up almost 23% of the population, and 27% of them are settled in Sweida city in particular.
The current situation has led to further hardships and needs such as high rents in general and homes in particular, high cost of living, limited or hard to reach services, lack of employment opportunities for host community members due to low labor costs of IDPs, low availability of drinking water, fuel, gas and medical equipment in health centers within.
Also, In light of the new updates of the security situation in Daraa governorate, Syria Trust started its preparations to be ready to intervene directly once the situation has been cleared through wide range of services as a response. Up to this moment, 1,200 person moved from Daraa to Sweida and the number is expected to be increased significantly.
The Trust will activate 7 Nissan Training Center and support it to be a safe, nurturing and welcoming space for all vulnerable people that ensures a quality support through diverse programmes to reinforce self-reliance out of economic enterprise and empowerment, and deliver learning and life skills in order to build strong and engaged communities which have the ability to face life-changing humanitarian solutions
As a result, benefiting all segments in the catchment area of the community center (IDPs, returnees, female headed households, dropouts, elderly, people with disabilities and with specific needs,,,,,,etc). The project will be able to target a total of 1,530 beneficiaries from POC in Swaida city.

Needs

7 Nissan location is considered as one of the most strategic locations in Sweida city, since it will connect two main squares (Al-Sheleh and Sultan Basha Al-Atrash) of Sweida with 2 main roads (Qanawat main road and the one leading to Damascus). Besides, it is close to the centre of the city where many governmental institutions, shops, cafes and 2 schools for superior students (primary and secondary). The training center has 1 main entrance with an area of 700 m2.The catchment areas covered are Doar Al Malaab, Khier Al Dien Al Asaadi St, Swaida City, Al Rahha, and Rasas and after coordination with the Civil Documents Department, The population number is approximately 72,500 people (50% Males & 52 % Females) in addition to 10,000 IDPs (70% is Females& 30 % is Males). The necessary needs in these areas are in the vocational training and Agricultural projects as per the assessments of the needs of the region. In addition, the life skills training will reveal the talent and level of development of all age groups. The population of the area is divided onto two parts; host community and internally displaced people. There are many schools and universities in the region with multiple private institutes, however they all suffer from the common non-interactive teaching methods. All mentioned areas enjoys a cultural and religious diversity and integration at the same time which open many opportunities to serve, support and achieve the positive desired impact.

Implementation of activities

Activities will be implemented in the training center through three categories: - life skills training, vocational training, small business startup training, post-training grants - which will be identified based on the market needs. We will hire expert trainers in addition to the standard staff to work side by side to provide the training and link them to the market needs.

Protection Risk Analysis

R1: Limited access to learning and weakened life skills R2: Self-reliance and livelihood is insufficient for generating incomeR3: Lack of support for small businesses

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

M1: Providing life skills training based on market needs of university students M2: Based on results of market needs study as mentioned previously, the Training center will conduct vocational training which targets mainly profession holders or those interested in having a profession (Mainly youth)M3: Providing project management trainings dedicated to support professional personal skills of fresh graduates looking to launch their startup in a more advanced and specialised manner, and to support anyone with an entrepreneurial mindset from the targeted beneficiaries.
