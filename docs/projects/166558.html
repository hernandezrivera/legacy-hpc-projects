Project Summary

The project will target Manicaland and Mashonaland provinces as they have the highest number of people that will be food insecure at the peak of the lean season at 825 215 (Manicaland) and 577 954 (Mashonaland Central) (ZimVAC, 2019). Further, the project will target the following districts namely Chimanimani, Chipinge, Mbire, and Goromonzi through the provision of small grain seed, garden vegetable seeds, setting up of community, school nutrition gardens, the rehabilitation, mechanization of key livestock infrastructure, PPE and promotion of hygiene to combat COVID-19. Chipinge and Chimanimani districts have been selected due to their experiences with Cyclone Idai that resulted in heavy losses in crop productivity and having more than 6% of households that are supporting an orphan and a physically or mentally challenged individual. The current project will ride on the existing project funded by ADH in Chipinge and Chimanimani. Mbire and Goromonzi districts have been selected as they have a high food insecurity prevalence of 62513 and 141 252 individuals respectively. Help from Germany is already running projects in the aforementioned districts. The total caseload for the project will be 25 000 beneficiaries (5 000 in Chimanimani, 5 000 in Chipinge, 10 000 in Mbire and 5 000 in Goromonzi).
