Project Summary

LPG assistance will be address for Rohingya and Host community at camp-23 and Baharchhara union under Teknaf upazilla from January, 2021 to December, 2021. Considering the camp and component it is the third and continuous project of Nabolok here at camp-23 as we have been working here since October, 2018 to till date. In this project we have plan to continue refill activity as well as we have plan to distribute new 100 nos of LPG cooking sets among 100 Host community’s household whose were not considered at previous Project. Also we have plan for distributing new LPG cooking set with refill among 50 newly migrated families from others camp and among newly separated nuclear family. Then our refilling plan at every month as per family size will be increased 2550 Rohingya HH 750 Host HH instead of 2500 HH and 650 HH respectively. Almost two (02) years have passed since we have distributed the cooking sets among beneficiary as a consequence we have been facing some repair issues of cooking set, that’s why we have a plan to repair those cooking sets whenever it’s require but we do not have any plan for replacement. In the camp-23, for providing LPG refill distribution we will cover both male and female considering gender equality as well no gender discrimination or biasness in our distribution process so that we could cover all age people with ensuring gender equality. In addition, from the very beginning we have been maintain a help desk at distribution center during the distribution time to receive all sorts of quires or claim from the beneficiaries then we try to solve those dispute immediately with consulting the responsible agencies and it will continue in this project also. Considering the COVID-19 pandemic situation, we have been also providing home delivery support by porter assistance especially for the older persons who are more vulnerable by this pandemic of COVID-19 along with we are ensuring separate line for PwSN so that we can provide quick distribution compare to other and these activities has also included in this project. As well as Considering the pandemic COVID-19, we will maintain beneficiaries line up with social distance, installation of hand wash corner with available fresh water and soap so that the beneficiaries can wash their hand before taking LPG refill as well as we will ensure proper safety of our staffs who are engage in the distribution process by providing face mask, gloves and hand sanitizer. So to sum we would say all of the above mentioned activities will be continue in upcoming project in 2021.In addition, our staffs who are engage in the LPG refill distribution process have received several important basic training sessions like PSEA (Preventing Sexual Exploitation and Abuse), Child protection, Safety & security etc so that they can serve to our beneficiaries more efficiently as maintain proper safety & security. As we are covering the 100% beneficiaries regarding LPG cooking set and refill distribution in this camp it helps in reducing the fire wood collection, cut off tree from the reserve forest. Earlier our project in this camp that means before distribution the LPG refill, the beneficiaries had to face many problems like need more time to cook, produce more smoke so that burning eyes their child’s in the time of cooking. After providing this LPG refill those problems are being stopped. As the larger family who have need more cooking in that case we are considering them just before the schedule so that they are no need to go forest for collection fire wood. As a consequence, having our LPG refill activity it have helped to reduce dependency of reserve forest along with increase forestation as well as keep environment more clean & better here. Nabolok always follow any kind of instruction and regulation provided by the sector as well as EEWG committee ensures a smooth coordination, attended all types of meeting (online & manual) with sector and other related agencies and this will continue

1 - Identified Needs

While MSNA2020 stated that only 88% HHs have received LPG cooking facilities, with due respect and acknowledging data collection limitation during COVID-19 restriction, Nabalok's data for the LPG assistance in Camp 23 shows that 93% of HHs has already received LPG assistance in 2020. As Nabolok has established a presence in the camp and will ensure coverage of the remaining 7 %. Esurience of regular refill of LPG cylinder as per family size is the most Important and we will address that in every month to each family. As per MSNA, 2020 some large household were found who use LPG for their cooking and need refill earlier than small family, Nabolok will assess case by case to assist with additional refill when beneficiaries report the early run out of their LPG before their schedule.

1. Provide number of 200 most vulnerable older HHs support LPG home delivery by porter assistance on quarantine time.2. Number of 2500 Rohingya HHs will receive LPG refills on every month as per their family size.3. Number of 750 Host HHs will get LPG refills on every month as per their family size.4. Number of 50 Rohingya HHs will receive new LPG cooking set with continuous refill throughout the project year (newly separated nuclear family and camp transfer's family from others camp to shamlapur, Camp-23).6.
