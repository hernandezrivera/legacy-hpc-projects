1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

IOCC will be reaching out to the vulnerable children aged between 3- and 12-years old attending schools in Tripoli and Akkar and provide them with nutritious and healthy snacks. This intervention will help alleviate hunger, reduce micronutrient deficiencies, prevent malnutrition, improve school enrollment and attendance, and increase cognitive and academic performance of these children.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

All staff involved in the project sign a code of conduct that includes Protection against Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) commitments, and regular protection trainings are provided, such as:-Community-Based Complaint Mechanism (CBCM)-Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA)-SPHERE Standards-Psychological First Aid -Safe Identification and Referral-Gender and Disability Inclusion -Gender Based Violence (GBV) IOCC has assigned a PSEA focal point to coordinate the development and implementation of IOCC PSEA policy and practices. IOCC’s CRM was updated with a focus on sensitive allegations, and CRM policy and procedures, and communication materials were developed. Mandatory trainings on PSEA were rolled out to all IOCC staff and regular sensitization sessions are being carried out. The sessions tackle PSEA core principles, zero tolerance policy to SEA, prevention and response, commitments and personnel obligations.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

IOCC has worked on incorporating PSEA into its work plans on both Prevention and Response level to be able to identify, prevent, and combat sexual exploitation and abuse. IOCC has assigned a PSEA focal point to coordinate the development and implementation of IOCC PSEA policy and practices. IOCC’s CRM was updated with a focus on sensitive allegations, and CRM policy and procedures, and communication materials were developed. Mandatory trainings on PSEA were rolled out to all IOCC staff and regular sensitization sessions are being carried out. The sessions tackle PSEA core principles, zero tolerance policy to SEA, prevention and response, commitments and personnel obligations. A code of conduct that includes PSEA commitments is signed by all IOCC staff, new and existing where the new staff attend an orientation on the PSEA and protection mainstreaming.
