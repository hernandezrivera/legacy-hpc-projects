Project Summary

The project seeks to contribute to reduced morbidity and mortality from COVID-19 or from associated psychosocial and socio-economic impacts in the country. The country is currently faced with a double burden of COVID-19 pandemic and malaria outbreaks. Creating an additional burden and strain to an already weak health delivery system, which had been compromised already by the economic challenges being faced in the country, which had resulted in stock outs of medical supplies, demotivated health staff among many challenges. Malaria cases surge started from week 10 and as at 10 May 2020 a total of 289 071 malaria cases and 19 deaths had been reported. Mashonaland Central, Manicaland and Mashonaland East, which are part of this project target provinces are among the most affected. With regard to COVID-19, as at 20 May 2020 the country had recorded 48 positive cases, four deaths and 18 recoveries with the affected provinces targeted by this project having case loads as follows: Mashonaland East (6), Matabeleland North (1), Midlands (1) and Masvingo ( 1). COVID-19 outbreak has stretched an already compromised health delivery system and the country is still not fully prepared with gaps still existing with regard to; testing capacity, staff capacity, medical supplies, PPE, IPC materials, awareness raising. In addition, the pandemic has significantly affected the delivery of other primary health services that include: immunisation, MNCH services, HIV services, Community Health services, behaviour change and community engagement. For immunization, evidence shows a declining routine immunization coverage due to decreased demand/health seeking behavior; reduced delivery of vaccines and number of outreach services; and lack of conﬁdence of health workers and fear of infection. The further compromised health delivery system will result in increased morbidity and mortality from common illnesses such as malaria, pneumonia, diarrhea, vaccine preventable diseases and TB.
This projects seeks to strengthen preparedness and response to COVID-19 and sustenance of the routine primary health care services. The objective will be pursued through strengthening institutional and community health system, including community engagement. Capacity building trainings will be supported to address the capacity gapsfor MOHCC staff and Village Health Workers (Community Health Workers), especially on case management and Infection Prevention and Control, coupled by supporting them with tools of the trade to enable them to accomplish their duties. To improve health delivery system, the project will strengthen diagnosis through support with diagnostic kits and laboratory reagents. In addition, MOHCC and community health workers will be supported with PPE, IPC materials and patient kits to enable them to undertake their duties safely and support patients adequately. Essential medical support as necessary will be another key component of the project to improve intuitional capacity. To strengthen awareness raising, behavior change and community engagement messages will be disseminated through multiple platforms that include: printed IEC material, radio messages, SMS, Megaphones or mobile media. Integrated Disease Surveillance remains crtical to track the progression of the current outbreaks and detect future outbreaks timely, hence will be strengthened also enhancing utilizing mobile and electronic platforms for reporting.
The project will give special consideration to vulnerable groups that include: elderly, patients with chronic conditions, pregnant and lactating women, children, people living with disabilities, people living with HIV. In addition, the affected populations will be consulted throughout the project lifespan (from this design stage) and feedback mechanisms will be set up to allow for complains to be addressed.

1 - Activities

The project activities will be as outlined below:1. Raise public awareness on COVID-19, promote behaviour change and the public role in preventing its spread (through printed IEC material, bulky SMS, radio messages,).2. Support capacity building of MOHCC staff on case management, IPC, contact tracing and Rapid Resposnse3. Strengthen screening and diagnosis through support with rapid diagnostic kits 4. Procure and distribute Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) and Infection Prevention & Control (IPC) materials 5. Support MOHCC with essential medical equipment and supplies as necessary6. Strengthen the utilisation of electronic and mobile platforms by MOHCC staff and Community Health Workers to strengthen IDSR 6. Strengthen utilisation of mobile platforms (bulk sms) to disseminate information to care givers/general community7. Procure and distribute patient kits for home care (kits include latex gloves, patient surgical masks, hand sanitiser, soap, detergent). 8. Support sustenance of other essential health services e.g immunisation, ANC9. Facilitate community dialogues to ensure participation of the affected community and establish feedback mechanisms
