Project Summary

The project aims to increase the safety and dignity for the most vulnerable people in need in Rural Damascus, Aleppo, Hama, Latakia, Der Ezzor, Raqqa, Qunitra, Daraa, Hasakeh, Qamishly in alignment with the protection sector priorities and severity scale.
The project implementation will focus on increasing awareness and providing immediate support to vulnerable people, The work structure in each location will include:
local Partner mobile team and a stationed teams inside PWDs accessible safe space via soft-rehabilitation with TDH technical supervisors and field Coordinators, in addition to SARC distribution teams.
Improving the protection of populations involves a multifaceted approach, encompassing initiatives and sessions for awareness raising, (PSS) services, and Cash for Protection, tailored to address specific protection needs. TdH will implement these interventions, coordinating with the CASH Working Group for 3 cycles to ensure alignment with sector-specific objectives.
GBV activities:
GBV response services for GBV survivors, mobile teams that provide prevention and intervention services within the standards and Survivor-centered approach, communities will be engaged in awareness sessions topics, such as violence against women and men, early marriage and PSEAH in addition, to have case management for the survivors through referrals, moreover, participate in international events (16 days campaign) and dignity kits to mitigate the risks of GBV- Focused assistance for 100 women via providing MHPSS support, referral to Psychiatric/psychotherapy specialized services.
CP activities:
30350 boys and girls in targeted governorates will be engaged in the following activities :
Awareness-raising sessions and activities in the centers and schools, sessions will cover several topics such as child rights, and child labor, harmful effects of dropping out of school etc., community events to promote the concept of child right and, safety and protection.
Psychosocial Support (PSS) group activities by trained facilitators to promote the well-being and resilience of children. These activities are structured and sustained.
One important aspect of these group activities is to create a safe and supportive environment for children to express themselves and interact with their peers. This can be done through various means such as art play therapy, group discussions, and recreational activities. These activities are designed to help children process their emotions, build coping skills, and develop positive relationships.
Structured group activities also, aim to address specific issues related to child protection. For example, they may focus on topics such as child rights, safety, and prevention of violence or exploitation. These sessions provide children with information and tools to protect themselves and seek help when needed.
Risks and Mitigating measures
R1: Local partners lose access to the project locations and are unable to implement activities, M1: SARC has displayed an outstanding ability to implement humanitarian projects despite constraints and limitations, However, if SARC lost access to project locations, TDH would either identify other suitable partners or, in coordination with them and with local actors, identify other locations with same high needs, where the programming could continue. R2: bureaucracy and delay in getting needed approvals from local authorities, M2: TDH already obtained approvals to work in the project locations and TdH already has and on-going projects and access and access in the targeted locations TDH will advocate through the local partner to have the needed approvals based on the signed MOU/R5: Inflation of Syrian Pound and its effect on the markets and the difference rate between the Syrian central Bank and the USD exchange rate. M6: any significate change will be discussed with the donor and modify the budget via the amendment process.
