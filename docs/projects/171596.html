Project Summary

The proposed project aims to contribute to effectively promote, protect and defend women’s rights in the Gaza Strip .
Its activities will last for 12 months and it outcomes as follows :
Outcomes :
1. 450 women have improved access to legal protection and justice .
Indicators:
1.1 # of women who received legal aid (representation and counselling) by the end of the project. (Target: 450)
1.2 % of increase on Shari’a court rulings in favour of women among the filed cases compared to the baseline of 580 rulings in the last year. (Target: %10)
2. Two young female lawyers are empowered to defend women’s rights in the Gaza Strip.
Indicators:
2.1 # of women legally served by the 2 lawyers by the end of the project.
2.2 # of cases represented by WRU before the Shari'a courts with the assistance of the 2 lawyers by the end of the project. (Target: 450)
2.3 % of Shari’a court rulings in favour of women among the filed cases by the lawyers, by the end of the project. (Target: %50)
3. Duty bearers, community members and international stakeholders increasingly upheld women’s and human rights following PCHR awareness and advocacy efforts.
Indicators:
3.1 % of increase in knowledge of the duty bearers about mechanisms of legal protection and intervention based on International Human rights law. (Target: %40)
3.2 # of Timely high-quality products have been produced and used for national and international advocacy highlighting women rights’ situation in the Gaza Strip by the end of the project. (Target: 2)

1 - Needs

GBV which is already on the rise in Gaza is a direct result of this complex situation which Gaza lives since years , this proposed project will be implemented in a context of increasing pressure on women rights in the opt, as lack of access to resources, waves of conservative norms and rising poverty in the Gaza strip leads to increased frustration among men and women and to more gender-based violence. The Bureau of Statistics report for year 2019, indicated that Palestinian women face many forms of violence, with 29%of the currently married or ever married women experienced “at least once” a form of violence such as (psychological, physical, sexual, social or economic violence) by their husbands in Palestine. 57% of the currently married or ever married women experienced “at least once” psychological violence by their husbands in Palestine. The problem of domestic violence and violence against women in general is not limited to laws that don’t protect women; rather, it is a societal issue. We live in a society where women are treated unfairly and are constantly underestimated and their access to justice is a challenge. Additionally, per a 2015 study on VAW in the Gaza Strip, 39.6 per cent of women reported to have experienced at least one form of domestic violence during the 11 months following the Israeli offensive on Gaza in the summer of 2014. The most common form of violence was of psychological nature. Per data collected by SAWA in 2015, from 950 domestic violence cases, 18 per cent are victims of sexual abuse, 12 per cent of rape Available data reveals a very high percentage of rape committed by perpetrators from outside the victim’s family up to 20 per cent, together with 6 per cent attempted rape, 3 per cent of indecent behaviour and 18 per cent of unspecified abuse. Girl marriage is still a concern, as according to PCBS, early marriage rate of girls under the age of 18 is still high (20 per cent in 2015), a noticeable reduction was witnessed since the year 2000 as the rate stood at 36 per cent. From January to June 2017 data from the GBV Sub-Cluster chaired by UNFPA clearly shows that just less than 3 out of 4 women survivors of violence in West Bank and Gaza are perpetrated by either an intimate partner (36 per cent) or family members (36 per cent). Additionally, more than half of the registered women survivors of violence are married (58 per cent), 16 per cent single, 14 per cent divorced, 9 per cent widowed, and 3 per cent separated. Per the Women’s Centre for Legal Aid and Counselling (WCLAC), 27 cases of the killing of women and girls in the Palestinian territory (West Bank and Gaza) were observed in 2014, and 15 cases in 2015, whereas from the beginning of 2016 until mid-August, 18 cases of femicide were documented.This reality is now overburdened with the situation of COVID19 emergency , data and information received from official and unofficial institutions concerned with women and the family, indicate a noticeable increase in the prevalence of gender-based violence, as closure of workplaces, restrictions on movement and imposing quarantine, have shed a dark light on the lives, safety and health of women, who were forced to stay all the time with family members committing violence against them, unable to escape. According to its own reporting, in light of COVID-19, PCHR’s lines are experiencing increased violence complaints. Since March 2020, PCHR reported providing hundreds of phone call consultations including many in relation to GBV, which is considered as an increase. And starting from January 2020, PCHR received 534 cases for women victims of GBV which indicated the continuity of violence against women.Responding to this reality, Addressing the status of women’s access to justice under the occupation context of the Gaza Strip becomes rooted in PCHR strategic and continuous intervention towards better enhancement of women access to justice. PCHR’s Women’s Rights Unit continued its work in helping Women in having access to justice, especially the marginalized ones, by providing legal aid to them and with the support of its strategic partners. This project will respond to the needs of women through legal protection, building capacities and advocacy. During COVID19 emergency, PCHR adapted its activities to include distribution of dignity kits in cooperation with protection cluster and GBV sub cluster , awareness leaflets, free hotlines, and provision of online legal counselling for women victims of GBV.Furthermore, this project is in line with PCHR’s new strategy 2020-2024 and its on-going interventions to combat violence against women, promote and defend women's rights and also enhance women's access to justice. The project will contribute to effectively promote, protect and defend these women rights through a series of well-designed activities which will last for 12 months. The activities will vary between legal aid, awareness sessions and advocacy efforts. The following sections highlight more details about the outcomes and activities of the project.

2 - Activities

Activity 1.1 Filing 450 Cases before Sharia CourtsPCHR's Women's Rights Unit will receive women complaints relevant to the Family Law and represent them before Gaza Shari'a courts. the most prominent cases will include alimony, house furniture, deferred dowry, child custody, seeing children, and separation.The expected women number is 450 . Activity 1.2 Intervening in order to implement 100 Sharia courts' rulings After Shari'a rulings are issued, the Unit will file executive cases before the executive bodies of the Shari'a courts to help women obtain their financial rightsThe expected number of women who will benefit from the implementation of the rulings are 100. Activity 1.3 Providing 450 Legal Consultations for Women Women unit will provide this service in different forms, including receiving women in PCHR's head office in Gaza City and branches in Khan Yunis and Jabalia, phone calls, or awareness-raising meetings organized by the unit.With regard to COVID-19 related risk mitigation measures, PCHR will activate the online counselling for the women who are in need for the services, hotlines will be activated to allow women reaching the lawyers for the service without the need to come physically to the offices which mitigate the risks of being exposed to danger because of COVID19.In cases of women who have to come to the offices for receiving the service, PCHR adopted some protection measures including providing these women with the gloves, mask faces and keep the social distance between them and the lawyers who are taking the protective measures into account too . Activity 1.4 Providing Legal Aid for Female Prisoners In the project period, PCHR lawyers will pay 8 visits to the women's prison in Gaza City to check female prisoners’ and their imprisonment conditions. The Unit will intensify their visits to provide as much legal aid and consultations as possible for the female prisoners who need them. Activity 2.1 Providing Practical (12 months “on-Job training”) to two Female Lawyers at the women unit.In the project period, PCHR will select 2 young female lawyers to join the women unit as trainees under the supervision of women unit’s staff. The aim of the training is to increase the capacity of these female lawyers in providing responsive legal aid services to women in family court and enable them to conduct community awareness on women’s rights, VAW and women’s access to justice and to enhance their capabilities to be women rights defenders. It will also provide these young lawyers with a gender-sensitive and human rights based approaches to defend women’s rights, the training will adopt the practicing and participation methodology. The lawyers will learn by practicing, they will work under the supervision of the lawyers of the women unit.They will receive cases, build legal files, provide help for the women victims at the courts.Serving women at the courts will enable them to act as women defenders.there will be special focus on PSEA through the training of the lawyers.Activity 3.1 Conducting 4 Training courses targeting 100 participants in women rights In the project period, training unit at PCHR will conduct 4 training courses targeting members and volunteers in civil society organizations; members of youth groups; university students; journalists; media activists; lawyers with 60% women participationThe aim of this to empower the trainees through knowledge of women rights stipulated under national and international laws, 20 training hours for each course (4 days).PCHR will coordinate with CBOs during the project period, to nominate number of PWDs to participate in the trainings , 20% out of the total trainees are planned to be PWDs Activity 3.2 Conducting 2 training courses (20 training hours for each ) for 50 participants from the duty bearers .In the project period, PCHR will conduct 2 training courses targeting the workers at Aman sheletr , rehabilaltion centers and the female police about concepts of human rights, women’s rights, GBV cases , mechanisims of legal protetcion and intervention. This training aims to enhance the capacities of 50 participants (25 pactiaipnts in each course ) in the mentioned areas, which will enable them to apply what they have learned in their organizations to be able to provide women with better services specially victims of GBV. Activity 3.3 producing timely high-quality legal written submission for UN human rights council regarding women in Gaza PCHR’s advocacy officer will work on written submission for UN-human rights council, it will focus on the situation of women rights in the human rights council.PCHR will invest session’s timing to present this submission, in March and June of 2021
