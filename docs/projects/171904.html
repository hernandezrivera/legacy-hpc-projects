Project Summary

The deep humanitarian crisis resulting from the long-running conflict in Eastern Ukraine continues to have a negative impact on the population and infrastructure of the Donetska NGCA territory .
The health system, which is already overloaded due to the fighting, is most affected by the outbreak of the COVID-19 virus.
CO "ICF "MIRA" monitored the situation and needs of medical institutions that are directly identified for receiving COVID-19 patients on the territory of the Donetsk NGCA. As of 26.10.2020, the number of COVID-19 cases is 5943, but the actual figure is probably much higher.
Based on the monitoring of 57 institutions that previously received assistance from CO "ICF "MIRA", it turned out that 30 institutions need assistance to work optimally with a large number of patients. Many of them do not have access to hot water, which makes it impossible to carry out the necessary hygienic hand treatment in COVID-19 conditions. Moreover, the lack of disinfection chambers significantly increases the risk of spreading infectious diseases, including COVID-19, due to the inability to disinfect soft hospital equipment: work clothes, bed linen, blankets, pillows, mattresses, towels.
In order to reduce the risk of COVID-19 spreading in health care facilities and protect medical workers, CO "ICF "MIRA" plans to provide assistance in the form of equipment, disinfection chambers, water heating boilers (boilers), electric hand dryers (dryers), paper towels, dispensers for narrow-panel paper towels and dispensers for liquid soap.
These humanitarian response measures will help to minimize the risk of COVID-19 and other infectious diseases spreading, both among patients of medical institutions and among medical personnel. The implementation of sanitary and hygienic decontamination measures will save the lives of disabled people, people of vulnerable categories (60+), people with weakened immune systems and chronic diseases, and will contribute to preserving the integrity of medical personnel in medical institutions.
This project will be implemented within 5 months and will cover 30 healthcare institutions, including neuropsychiatric dispensaries. The number of potential beneficiaries for the duration of the project will be at least 105,000.
Based on the recommendations of the cluster focused on providing access to hot water, especially in winter conditions COVID-19 and given the magnitude and speed of spread of the new coronavirus infection the project is planned in as soon as possible throughout the NGCA since January 2021.
These types of assistance do not include gender and age differences between beneficiaries.
Monitoring of feedback and complaints from beneficiaries will be provided on a regular basis, verification of each institution will be carried out initially with filling out a detailed questionnaire for each institution and distribution of a hotline and email, PDM surveys will be conducted among patients and staff of the institution. A complaint database will be maintained where all complaints will be classified based on urgency, relevance, and sensitivity and immediately resolved.
All assistance provided as a result of the project is long-term and relevant.
CO "ICF "MIRA" has experience in cooperation with medical institutions and in the provision of humanitarian aid, targeted assistance, and has a clear understanding of the requirements of international partners, both in the implementation of international projects, and gender equality, financial discipline of the project, prevention of corruption and terrorist financing.
The organization has clear standards and internal policies for humanitarian activities on the territory of NGCA, developed in accordance with the requirements of international partners and recommendations of clusters, all employees are familiar with them and strictly adhere to them.

1 - Needs

During 4 years of work of the CO "ICF "MIRA" in the humanitarian sphere, assistance was provided to 57 medical institutions.Based on monitoring and receiving feedback from institutions that had previously received humanitarian assistance, the CO "ICF "MIRA" has determined that 30 institutions need assistance to work optimally with a large number of patients.In many medical institutions, there is a need to provide access to hot water and the devices and facilities needed to perform hand treatment in COVID-19 conditions. Moreover, there is a need to provide medical institutions with disinfection chambers in order to reduce the risk of spreading infectious diseases , including COVID-19, which are necessary for disinfection of soft hospital equipment: work clothes, bed linen, blankets, pillows, mattresses, towels.To prevent the spread of a new coronavirus infection in health care facilities and protect medical workers, CO "ICF "MIRA" plans to provide assistance in the form of equipment, disinfection chambers, water heating boilers (boilers), electric hand dryers (dryers), paper towels, dispensers for narrow-panel paper towels and dispensers for liquid soap.The recommendations of the WASH cluster were used to generate needs for institutions: Water, sanitation, hygiene andwaste management in response to the spread of the virusSARS-Cov-2, which is the causative agent of COVID-19 (https://apps.who.int/iris/bitstream/handle/10665/333560/WHO-2019-nCoV-IPC_WASH-2020.4-rus.pdf)
