Project Summary

The project aims to provide immediate and essential humanitarian assistance by restoring the food security and production capacity of the most affected and vulnerable subsistence farmers and micro value chain actors in the tri-island state of Grenada.
This will be achieved by providing urgent short cycle (e.g. 3 months) smallholder crop farming inputs to address immediate food needs and restart production during the remainder of the wet season; distributing livestock feeds, vaccines and medicines to protect remaining subsistence livestock and prevent the spread of disease; and providing critical food processing tools to the most vulnerable food micro value chain actors.

Project Activities

Provision of urgent short-cycle crop farming inputs to help vulnerable smallholder farmers meet their immediate food needs and restore production capacity. Provision of livestock feeds, vaccines, medicines and other critical animal health supplies to vulnerable subsistence livestock farmers to protect remaining livestock and food security. Distribution of critical food processing tools to the most vulnerable micro food processors (e.g. women groups who process herbs, spices, fruits and other commodities) and spot repairs of food processing spaces such as food cleaning and processing sheds and tables.
