Project Summary

WASH services in Northwest Syria have been severely affected by various challenges, including the February 2023 earthquake, ongoing conflict, economic downturn, and power shortages. Damage to WASH systems from these events has significantly limited access to water and sanitation for affected communities. Economic decline has further hindered service providers' ability to maintain these systems, impacting households' access to essential WASH services. Drought conditions have worsened freshwater quality and availability, exacerbated sanitation issues and increased public health risks such as AWD/Cholera outbreaks, leishmaniosis, and malnutrition. In northern Syria, inadequate toilet facilities, particularly in IDP camps, pose significant challenges, with many lacking gender segregation, lighting, and security, disproportionately affecting women and girls and increasing risks of GBV incidents. Recent conflict and bombardments in northwest Syria have only worsened this already dire situation.
The project aims to implement WASH interventions for the most vulnerable displaced populations in Northwest Syria through the following set of activities:
i) Provision of emergency WASH services IDP sites supporting approximately 173,000 individuals in 2024. Activities include the provision of water services, solid waste management, faecal sludge management, care and maintenance of latrines, provision of water storage tanks, garbage containers, and WASH NFI kits, as well as hygiene promotion, capacity building and community engagement sessions. (Activity 1.1, 1.2, 1.3, 1.4, 1.5, 4.3 and 4.4)
ii) Construction/rehabilitation of sewage networks, including the construction of septic tanks, Anaerobic Buffel Reactors (ABR) or any other suitable and contextual technologies to treat wastewater produced in informal camps/sites. This specific intervention will target informal IDP sites in 2024, supporting over 87,000 individuals in 2024. (Activity 2.3)
iii) Provision of vector-control activities (IRS, Fogging, Larva control, Bed net, curtains distribution) in hotspot and high-risk areas. This specific intervention will target informal IDP camps/sites in 2024, supporting over 87,000 individuals in 2024. (Activity 2.1)
iv) Enhanced water quality assurance, operation, and maintenance of water supply systems through the provision of solar energy, extending the pipe networks to the IDP camps/sites in 2024, supporting over 87,000 individuals (Activity 3.1)
v) Provision of WASH services and rehabilitation of WASH facilities in health facilities in 2024, supporting 115,000 individuals in 2024. (Activity 1.7)
