Project Summary

Boko-Haram insurgency in northeastern Nigeria has disrupted the educational system with the burning of schools, abduction of students, attack, and killing of pupils and teachers. About 2.8 million children are out of school, UNICEF 2019. Recently schools were shut down to contain the COVID 19 pandemic, all these increases marginalized children's risks of sexual violence and early marriage of girl child. This project is targeted at Providing education support in an emergency to 5000 conflict-affected out of school children, through accelerated program and distribution of learning materials in two targeted L.G.A Dikwa and MMC. From the above-mentioned target, 2500 boys and girls within the range of 3-17 years will be reached with a catch-up program for six months and mainstream them back to formal school. And 2500 school children will be supported through back to school campaign and distribution of school learning material in Borno state, the target beneficiaries include, IDP, returnees, and other vulnerable groups in communities, the fund requested for this project would be used to provide learning materials and sustain Temporary Learning Spaces (TLS) to reach engage the identified out of schoolboys and girls by Intercommunity for African Development Initiative, IADI with an accelerated program. The project implementation is targeted at school-age boys and girls within the range of 3-17.
Intercommunity for African Development Initiative will integrate Qur’anic studies curriculum to the accelerated program curriculum to secure the interest of the parents and caregivers whose interest is in having their children attend Qur’anic school. The IADI will support 50 vulnerable Parents and caregivers with livelihood skill training and start-up grant to have them support the mainstreaming of their children back to formal schools after the six months of the accelerated program.
Through this project Intercommunity for African Development Initiative, Nigeria shall carry out disinfection of 50 schools and TLC across the target LGAs to ensure the safety of the school environment and ensure safe learning in the schools. And Carry out Back to School Campaign to support the 5,000 out of school Children excluding the main target to return back to school by the end of coronavirus spread with the support of Ministry of Education, Local Government Education Authorities and other Education in Emergency Working Group and education partners across the project targeted locations

1 - Needs

- The prolonged Boko-Haram insurgency in northeastern Nigeria has disrupted the educational system and impacted on the life of school children which include burning of schools, abduction, attack and killing of pupils and teachers. According to UNICEF 2019 report, 2.8 million children are out of school in the region. Indefinite school shutdown to contain coronavirus pandemic has also increased the marginalized children risks of child exploitation. Although conflict has reduced in the past couple of years in comparison to 2013-2015, as reported by (Jena-2019) it continues to affect the ability of some schools to remain open and provide lessons. This is particularly notable in assessed LGAs in Borno state, where 60% of the schools assessed are closed, compared to 14% in Yobe and none in Adamawa.1 Schools in Adamawa tend to stop functioning temporarily rather than closing: 71% of schools assessed had stopped functioning at some point since 2012 due to conflict, Dikwa and MMC in borno state are filled with many out of school children with need to be assess and enrol back to school, these will help in reducing the rate of child exploitation and early marriage.- The Coronavirus pandemic and its strategic response across Nigeria and particularly northeast affected 4.2million Men, Women, Boys and girls in the education system, which includes school children, parents, education socials and the communities across the state, following the ongoing emergency in the state, it is well noted that government has close down schools and ask everybody to stay at home to reduce the wide spread of Coronavirus and enable easy tracing of the transmission. This has coursed many children backward from schools and learning support.- With this project the identify education need of out of school children in MMC and Dikwa will be supported by Intercommunity for African Development Initiative, Nigeria, through back to school enrolments, distribution of learning materials, provision of (Qur’anic) Integrated accelerated learning, and livelihood skills supports for vulnerable parents and caregivers.
