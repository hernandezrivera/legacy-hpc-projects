Project Summary

MASRRAT has designed the project to contribute to enhancing the stability and rebuilding of the olive value chain through supporting of 200 HHs from IDPs and host communities. The support will target the mid-size olive farmers who own 5-10 dunums of fruitful olive trees (trees > 10 years) in Idleb and Aleppo Governorates (Harim and Afrin district). In addition, the project will support the seasonal workers by targeting 400 workers from (HHS IDPs and Host Communities) for manual harvesting and pruning by cash for work activities. As well as, the project will target the women because they have considerable experience in the care of the olive trees in the targeted areas in addition to their seasonal work in the olive harvest.
Key interventions will include:
- Distributing agricultural inputs, pesticide (insecticide, and fungicide), fuel for irrigation, cost of harvesting, pruning and oil extracting.
- Training all beneficiaries and local communities on recycling the manure and available materials in the local environment to produce organic fertilizer (compost).
- Capacity building to supported olive farmers and increase the technical capacity and knowledge on the best agricultural practices of olive trees by practical and theoretical training for beneficiaries about (production good table olive, pests of olive trees, pruning of olive trees, nutrient requirements for olive trees and fertilization, irrigation of olive trees, marketing of olive for fruits and olive oil, olive propagation).
The proposed action will contribute to strengthening the resilience of the targeted crisis-affected people and their host communities.
This proposal has not funded yet.

4 - Needs

Across Syria,based on HNO2019 and USAID 16 January2020, there are around 11.7 million people in need of multi-sectoral humanitarian assistance. An estimated 83 percent of Syrians live below the poverty line. Since the beginning of the crisis, olives production has been significantly affected, due to the destruction of infrastructure and services, land degradation, migration and the loss of human resources for production, input shortages, high production costs, and the destruction of trees also problems of insecurity such as prolonged lack of harvesting and pruning.Prior to the crisis, olive production used to employ an estimated 100000 Syrian families. However, Harim and Afrin are considered one of the most known areas with high olive production (Special Report: FAO/WFP Crop And Food Security Assessment Mission To The Syrian Arab Republic, 9 October 2018). In addition, they were dependent on agricultural activities especially olives.According to a rapid need assessment that conducted by MASRRAT team in Harim and Afrin (through many meetings with specialists in the agricultural sector and many stakeholders in addition to local councils’ members), the main problems were identified as the following:•The high cost of compost, pesticides, pruning, plowing, and milling services, lead to an increase in production costs.•Spread olive insects and diseases.•Lack of skills of workers for conducting services of pruning and pest management.•Loss agricultural extension about the good ways of harvesting, storing olive fruit and milling caused low oil quality. •Marketing difficulties.All these were caused a deterioration in olive sector, olive oil unavailability, olive oil low quality, as result low farmer incomes, low market movement, and food insecurity accompanied by weakness livelihoods.Through the meetings with the host communities in the targeted areas, the results revealed that the areas included a large number of IDPs which is equal to 33.3% in Harim and 53.8% in Afrin.

5 - Implementation of activities

The project will start implementation on 1 March 2020 by mobilizing the community, conducting visits to targeted areas, conducting an awareness session about the support and activities and signing MoU’s with the LCs in order to facilitate carrying out of the activities. After that, the registration process will take place in a suitable place for potential beneficiaries in terms of safety and access. Beneficiaries will be selected based on selection criteria through a scoring system (200 olive farmers and 400 CFW). Complaints mechanism will be available by many ways, box in LCs, e-mail and WhatsApp number. Selection seasonal workers for pruning and harvesting (cash for work) will be from IDPs and local community from both gender (women and men) wherein for the pruning the workers should have previous experience. The CFW workers will be divided into groups in order to organize the works. The procurement of support will be from Syria. The distribution of support will be in a suitable place and in an appropriate time. During the implementation of the activities, MASRRAT’s Field team will conduct training for beneficiaries about integrated management of olive trees, recycling techniques. During all the phases of the project, the MEAL team will be monitoring the implementation and evaluating the process for ensuring the satisfaction of the beneficiaries. The MEAL team will verify the potential beneficiaries and will conduct PDM after every distribution to ensure the quality and quantity of the received allocation of each beneficiary, in addition to conducting the baseline and end-line survey to measure the impact of the intervention on the community.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

The targeted locations of the project are considered relatively safe as there hasn't been any kind of conflict during this time. MASRRAT team will take into consideration allprecautions, starting with obtaining approvals from the local councils to move freely on the ground, strictly observing security and safety regulations and preventing thebeneficiaries to work if there is any risk. The following consideration will be considered with regard to protection:• Prioritization for safety, dignity, and ‘do no harm’ principles will be concerns in this program; this project has been designed to prevent and mitigate any negative unintentional effects of intervention that might physically jeopardize people (like violence, offence, coercion, and environmental risks), or psychologically threat them (like disrespect, lack of privacy, and disengagement). • Equal and unbiased access to assistance and services: Assistance and services will be provided basing on the needs with no discrimination. • The risk of sexual exploitation by humanitarian workers is considered an average indicator in the project because there is a confidential complaint mechanism, Code of conduct in all staff contracts Address any SEA or GBV complaints and conduct trainings on this subject for the new staff. • There will be no sensitivity in the community during the implementation of the project due to the project will target all the HHs and IDPs in these areas and according to the established criteria.• Enough quantities and types of agricultural inputs will be available and provided equally to beneficiaries with a consideration of the geography of the targeted locations.• MASRRAT will try to strike balance between the implementation of the program and safety and security of the employees who are our paramount priority in safety and security plan. MASRRAT team has been trained on safety and security procedures. Procedures and guidance have been adopted for implementing all other programs.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumptions:- Markets are functioning and all the inputs are available,- The olive varieties are stapled and adapted to the environment, - There is social acceptance for the project and the people are interacting with it. - MASRRAT has great experience in implementing FSL projects - MASRRAT has good access to this region, furthermore currently, is implementing an FSL project in the same region. -The olive tree has special symbolic in the targeted area this gives the program more support.The security situation is safe in the project area.Risks: Some people in the project area do not accept the idea of participation of women in project activities (Mitigation: awareness sessions to the community on women's rights and the importance of their participation in the project and separation between men and women workers.)Corruption and fraud by the traders and providers (Mitigation: clear criteria to select vendors, clear criteria to select vendors, MEAL department will monitor for the local market dealy.)Olive trees infected with spread diseases (Mitigation: MASRRAT will distribute insecticide and fungicide in time)Drought (Mitigation: MASRRAT will distribute fuel for irrigation)Price changes (Mitigating - Sending the money to Syria using PTT centers only and using the Turkish Lira which will led to losing money due to the instable of the exchange rate).
