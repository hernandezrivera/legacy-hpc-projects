Project Summary

The project is addressing all forms of domestic violence against women and girls. The project addresses FGM, domestic violence, forced marriages, girl education drop outs and sexual violence. The project will also provide dignity and hygiene kits and referral services to the targeted communities

1. - Projects activities

The project activities will seek to address identified needs which provide women with promotion and protection of their rights, especially against GBV. This action by ActionAid proposes to address water access problems for women (distances to fetch water, lack of water for domestic and hygiene use). The project will construct Berkeds, in safe spaces, with lighting so that women can easily access water in their communities, safely. The construction designs will also take into consideration people living with disabilities. We will also strengthen women groups for promoting Personal Health and Hygiene Education in the targeted communities through arts, theatre and messaging Given the widespread food insecurity in Somalia, there may be a possibility of vulnerable women and adolescent girls exchanging sex for food. To mitigate this exposure, ActionAid will layer and sequence this project with the other food security and livelihoods project and ensure that at 65% of beneficiaries for cash and livelihood actions are women, including widows, adult women, divorced women, female headed households, pregnant and lactating mothers, and women and girls living with disability. GBV service provision remains low as compared to the needs and geographical landscape response. Limited specialized services such as rape treatment for rape survivors, case management, psycho-social support and higher levels of mental health care for traumatized women and girls are major hindrances to expanding provision of timely, confidential and quality GBV services. Limited GBV specialized service provider continues to impact both quality and reach of services. In that regard, ActionAid proposes to create safe spaces for women (constructed or community identified spaces) which also act as referral service pathways providers. The referral pathways will protect adult married women, adolescent girls, adult married women, widows, divorced women, female headed households, pregnant and lactating women and girls and women living with disability The project also proposes to coordinate policy and legislative reform, especially around the area of women protection and FGM in Somaliland. ActionAid will support local organisations to continue advocacy on ending all forms of violence against women, especially FGM Vulnerable women and girls in IDP camps and host communities express the need for dignity protection and material support (such as dignity and hygiene kits, solar lanterns, torches, mats and mattresses) to meet both basic and protection needs. The need for GBV Shelters for vulnerable women and girls (including GBV survivors) is a prominent concern as they are trapped violent relationships due to lack of secure temporary shelter. ActionAid proposes to provide dignity protection and material support in the temporary shelters/safe spaces ActionAid will also integrate integrate comprehensive strategies to prevent Sexual Exploitation and Abuse while delivering humanitarian services to the communities

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

Protection concerns seems highly increasing since the outbreak of COVID-19 pandemic. Restriction of movement also puts women and girls, and other marginalized groups at a greater risk of violence. AAIS’ experience shows that humanitarian emergencies magnify existing inequalities resulting in long-lasting and detrimental effects on gender equality and women’s rights. COVID –19 has further exacerbated protection risks against women and girls, as GBV incidents such as Intimate Partner Violence (IPV), domestic violence and child marriage are likely to increase during the pandemic, given the additional financial hardship, coupled with restrictions of movement and pre-existing gender discrimination and harmful practices. Inter clan inspired conflicts also happens in the Easter regions (Sanaag and Sool) results displacement and insecurity that can easily affect the delivery of humanitarian assistance and put high risk for our staff. In response, ActionAid has been sensitizing local communities on protection issues such as providing training on protection and women rights general to enhance the local knowledge and awareness on different types of SGBVs. We are also rolling out SHEA and Safeguarding, Child Safeguarding Policy was fully operationalized by AA Somaliland and its partners. All field staff and partners working with the sponsored children are trained with Child Safeguarding policy. the policy is annexed with Partner MOU and regularly assessed on implementation of the stipulated clauses. Conducting social awareness through regular mobilization on the prevailing protection concerns that can particularly affect displaced boys and girls in IDPs where the risk of violence is very high, and level of community awareness is relatively low.
