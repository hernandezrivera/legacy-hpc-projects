Project Summary

The association owns a building consisting of 7 floors (2 underground and 5 above) with a floor area of about 4000 square meters in the Sayyeda Zeinab area in Rural Damascus. The association obtained all official licenses, completed engineering studies, and completed all construction work. During 2024, the association aims to secure the necessary funding to complete the rehabilitating operations and equipping the hospital with the medical equipment.
It is planned that the hospital's capacity will reach 75 beds and include several main departments, including ambulance, intensive care, radiology, laboratories, and other specialized departments, the most important of which are the women's, children's, and dialysis departments and a special section for heart disease. In addition to 3 operating rooms for performing all types of surgeries, an outpatient department, and several logistical departments. All departments will be equipped with the necessary equipment according to the approved standard specifications for hospitals.
Finally, the project aims to provide integrated medical services to the beneficiary families of the poor at nominal fees and free of charge to the families most in need. Regarding the geographical scope, the project aims to provide
services to all residents of the western and Eastern Ghouta regions, reaching the remote areas located in the Syrian desert to the east.

Plan fields

0 - Project Summary (extended)

All areas of the southern Damascus countryside were subjected to large-scale military operations that led to the destruction of many health facilities, including most areas of Western and Eastern Ghouta. The targeted areas are considered to be in high need of medical services. The population exceeds 250 thousand people (75% women and children). The region also hosts dense concentrations of internally displaced persons (IDPs), who constitute 70% of the population. On the other hand, there is no public or private hospital in a circumference of more than 10 km in all directions, while the only hospital (private hospital) in the area was partially destroyed and is out of service. In addition, there are no health centers or specialized clinics in the area, especially dialysis centers, radiology centers for magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) and computed tomography (CT), or an incubator center.Residents of these areas are forced to go to the capital's hospitals to obtain medical services, in conjunction with the lack of means of transportation and high transportation costs. Most of the region's residents are considered poor families in need of health care and unable to afford medical treatment (more than 90% of the population).The association will commit to providing previously prepared studies, engineering plans, and tables of quantities of materials required for cladding. During implementation, periodic follow-up reports will be provided in cooperation with the engineering team. On the other hand, the project will take into account all safety and security standards to protect workers from potential risks while carrying out operations and train them on dealing with hazardous materials. The project is expected to face several economic and logistical risks during implementation, which will be dealt with according to the previously prepared risk matrix.
