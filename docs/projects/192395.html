Project Summary

Since the Alawite Islamic Charity Association (AICA) is known in approaching any humanitarian field in an innovative way AICA is applying to the CP ERP to implement AICA's approach in tackling child protection activities from lessons learnt findings. AICA’s operational team will work alongside with the protection unit where the PSS and MHPSS facilitators will deal with various cases. Providing Focus and non-focus PSS through innovative tailored artistic activities as per age segregation, background and social status where the DO NO HARM and the conflict sensitivity policies are ensured. Beneficiaries will be provided alongside a complementary protection response which is supported under AICA's umbrella within the sectors available: education, health, livelihood and basic assistance.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

1- AICA pre-communicated with focal points in the mentioned areas to ensure availability and facilitate the workflow of the project.2- AICA's field officer has ensured and followed up with the volunteers and outreach team with all the protection policies and implementation plans (PSEA, Code of Conduct, Child Save Guarding, Data protection declaration and declaration consent). IRF signature from the beneficiaries who require further support to be delivered to the school counselor to ensure all safety measures.3- Complaint mechanism through complaint box, hotline or direct follow up from AICA's MEAL officer or if needed by AICA's operational team upon urgency.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

1- Starting with a tailored assessment through KOBO to ensure required criteria as per the projects design.2- Scoring mechanism for the selected beneficiaries to ensure transparency and fairness.3- Ensure gender equality and equity in the selection criteria where all nationalities and social status are taken into consideration to mitigate tension among communities.4- Ensure participation of people with disabilities, by AICA's trained protection team if needed.5- AICA's selected committees, volunteers and / or Focal points are trusted personnel in each community to ensure the ease of outreach and reduce any potential tension or misunderstanding.
