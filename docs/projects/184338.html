Project Summary

The project aims providing timely emergency and sustainable integrated WASH services to the most vulnerable populations including IDPs and marginalized host community members (women,men, boys and girls) including those with disabilities and the elderly in Galgadud, Hiraan, Lower Shabelle, Middle Shabelle and Bay Regions, Somalia by supporting their urgent as well as long term WASH needs that has been seriously affected by devastating impacts of past successive drought seasons, past two consecutive seasons of below average rains, seasonal flash floods, current severe droughts conditions, conflict and insecurity that has impacted to huge displacements of populations .
The above plight is exacerbated by limited access to WASH infrastructures and services and that exposed the vulnerable populations to dangers of acute water diarrhea/ cholera outbreaks and protracted crises, COVID-19 outbreak as well as outbreak of other sanitation and hygiene related diseases. Of particular concern is current forecast that has shown likelihood of below average Deyr 2021 rains and the potential risk of severe drought till mid of 2022 that will significantly worsen vulnerability levels of the populations that are living precariously under comprised access of WASH services. (FSNAU-FEWS NET 2021 Post Gu Technical Release of September 9, 2021)). The project targets a total of 20000 persons (8320 women, 7680 men, 2000 boys and 2000 girls). The Project design will enable for consultation with all community cross-section including the persons with disability to ensure their needs are covered in addition to integration with other relevant clusters . Further the project will ensure increased capacity of WASH committees for management of WASH infrastructure and services beyond the project time in close coordination with the local administration.

Conducting emergency water trucking with temporary distribution systems for 50vulnerable settlements and rural communities in the target locations Galgadud, Hiraan, Lower Shabelle, Middle Shabelle, Banadir and Bay Regions. 2. Conducting household Water Treatment and Safe Storage (HWTSS) in the target locations in Galgadud, Hiraan, Lower Shabelle, Middle Shabelle, Banadir and Bay Regions. 3. Conducting well disinfection and chlorination in the target locations in Galgadud, Hiraan, Lower Shabelle, Middle Shabelle, Banadir and Bay Regions. 4. Extension/rehabilitation of 10 existing water supply system in the target locations in Galgadud, Hiraan, Lower Shabelle, Middle Shabelle, Banadir and Bay Regions. 5. Provision of water containers for transport and storage to vulnerable settlements and communities in the target locations in Galgadud, Hiraan, Lower Shabelle, Middle Shabelle, Banadir and Bay Regions. 6. Construction/improvement/rehabilitation available water sources/boreholes and shallow wells in the target locations in Galgadud, Hiraan, Lower Shabelle, Middle Shabelle, Banadir and Bay Regions. 7. Construction/Rehabilitation of 500 communal latrines to basic sanitation levels in vulnerable settlements in the target locations in Galgadud, Hiraan, Lower Shabelle, Middle Shabelle, Banadir and Bay Regions. 8. Establishment and training of water committee to ensure proper maintenance of water services9. 4.6 - Mitigation Measures

1. Ensuring inclusion of Minority clans/ethnic groups in the consultations on the humanitarian services/assistance right from the commencement of the project at the needs identification stage and assessments and ensuring they are part of key decision made regarding the project. Example: People with Disability (PWD) will be included in the project in all stages of implementation starting consultation meetings on needs identification for the project where their concerns will be taken into consideration such as designing water points/ water kiosks that was suitable for easy access for them as well as designing latrines that user friendly for PWDs. They will in addition be involved in project monitoring processes where their concerns will form basis for key decisions regarding implementation of the project and way forward in terms of any necessary adjustments during implementation till completion2. Ensuring protection of women and girls from sexual violence by ensuring that WASH facilities are located at acceptable distance in line with WASH minimum standards to limit exposing them to Sexual violence when they cover very long distance beyond the safety of their settlements. In addition, designing latrines as per WASH minimum standards to protect them from risk of Sexual violence. Secondly, adhering to PSEA policy and ensuring women and girls are fully aware of the project and their rightful entitlements as per Hygiene kits distribution and other WASH services to mitigate sexual exploitation and abuse during the project. In addition, ensuring that complaints and feedback mechanisms are rightfully in place such as suggestion boxes and toll free hotlines
