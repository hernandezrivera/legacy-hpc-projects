Project Summary

ADA is planning to run a total of 12 OTP sites comprising of 5 static and 7 mobile; thus 4 OTP sites (3 static and 1 mobile sites) in Badhadhe district of Lower Juba, 5 OTP sites comprising of (1 static and 4 mobile sites) in Elwak district of Gedo region and 3 OTP sites that consist of 1 fixed and 2 mobile sites in Abudwak district of Galgadud region.
The overall objective of the project is to provide equitable acess to quality lifesaving curative nutrition services among 10,070 boys and 10,480 girls (6-59) months and 6,344 PLW through systemmatic identification , referrals, and treatment of acutely malnourished cases. The action will directly provide intergreted life-saving nutrition services to communities.The project will target the following settlement; Badhadhe (Raskamboni, Koday,Madawa and Manaraani), Elwak (Elwak Town,Bardhere IDP,Labioor, Dabarbale and Tuulo Burwaqo) and Abudwak (October village, Dayacan IDP & Tawakal IDP).
The children with moderate acute malnutrition (MAM) will be treated with SAM supplies, as such ADA will utilize Extended Admission Criteria(EAC), and in addition those with uncomplicated severe acute malnutrition (SAM) will be treated in the OTPs in line with the national IMAM guideline for admission, treatment and discharge with a target reach of 18,030 children. Complicated cases of SAM will be referred to the nearest stabilization centre (SC) in Badhadhe,Elwak and Abudwak districts. Tracing of children that have defaulted from the OTP programme will be established and carried out by the OTP/SC teams together with the CNWs/CHWs. Provision of therapeutic and supplementary feeding for malnourished children <5 and PLW will be provided during the programme period. Children not progressing well under treatment will be visited at home for counselling for their mothers.
With a target reach of 6,344 PLWs and 2,520 children under 5 for MAM treatment at respective nutrition sites.
ADA will ensure that all nutrition sites will receive uninterrupted therapeutic/supplementary food supplies. ADA will secure agreements with WFP and UNICEF before the start of the program. ADA will provide staff, technical capacity building for staff and additional monitoring to ensure compliance with nutrition protocols. Community mobilization and screening to improve coverage and uptake of nutrition services will be conducted by CNWs/CHWs who will assist in defaulter tracing, follow-up of beneficiaries, community screenings and referrals.
Thus, ADA will train 38 nutrition staff on SAM/MAM and IYCF diagnosis and management. ADA will provide regular joint supervision of the 5 fixed nutrition facilities with MoH and provide refresher trainings for staff.
This programme will promote optimal nutrition and IYCF practices that are beneficial to child survival and development by focusing on the promotion, support and protection of EBF for children and appropriate complementary feeding to build resilience and the nutrition status of infants <6 months (through EBF) and children 6-24 months, thus, a total of 12,344 mothers will benefit from the sessions, mothers with children <6 months will receive messages specific to Exclusive Breastfeeding while mothers with children 6-24 months will receive messages on breastfeeding and complementary feeding. Screening for malnutrition among children and PLW will be conducted primarily at the community level by CNWs, though this screening will also occur at the health facilities by nutrition staff.
Malnourished women and children will be admitted in the relevant OTP programme. Children in the nutrition programme will receive relevant micronutrient supplementation such as iron/folate and Vitamin A according to IMAM guidelines, where a target reach of 32,550. Lastly, ADA will ensure that people with disabilities are given equal chances in access to the nutrition services.

Implementing Partners

ADA will independently implement the project in all specific target locations. to strengthen coordination at the field level, ADA will also work closely with other relief and development organizations that are present in the specific project locations such as Somali Aid, WASDA, IOM, SRCS, Ministry of Health and regional/district health authorities as well as local community members. Given the context, ADA will take an integrated but flexible approach to this program, responding to opportunities as they arise, while maintaining the overall objectives of improved quality, coverage and sustainable management of the health facilities and outreach sites ADA is a member and a partner of the health and Nutrition clusters. Monthly participation at the national and field level in the health and nutrition co-ordination meetings will be maintained in order to represent the project needs and achievements throughout the implementation period, while sharing the ideas and information with other agencies.

1. - Projects activities

Planned Project activities are;-Screening, diagnosis and referral of 32,550 children under 5 years (15,949 boys, 16,601 girls) 6-59 months for acute malnutrition (Badhadhe, Elwak and Abudwak). All children under 5 who will visit the facility and at the outreach sites taking the anthropometric measurement and MUAC by the Community Nutrition Workers (CNWs). Additionally, CNWs will be deployed to carry out community screening and effective referrals of identified cases.-Screening, diagnosis and referral of 12,344 pregnant lactating women for malnutrition at the community and the nutrition facilities.-18,030 children (8,835 boys and 9,195 girls) 6-59 months screened and diagnosed with severe acute malnutrition without medical complication will be treated at the out-patient therapeutic program.-2,520 boys and girls diagnosed with moderate acute malnutrition referred and treated through the EAC program.-Supplementation with vitamin A of All 32,550 children under 5 years (15,949 boys, 16,601 girls) children 6-59 months who will be reached through screening and are eligible for vitamin A supplementation at 6 months interval.-6,344 women who have been diagnosed with moderate acute malnutrition will be treated for MAM.-Community training of 24 (14 men and 10 women) community volunteers who will lead the community in creating awareness on nutrition health hygiene practices. The training duration will be a period of 5 days.-Conducting health nutrition and hygiene promotion sessions in the community for 9,450 (3,106men, 6,344 women) community members.-12,344 women (Pregnant and lactating) who are reached at the nutrition program will benefit from counselling sessions on infant and young child feeding practices. Sessions will be done at the beginning of each day at the antenatal clinics and on individual basis for pregnant lactating women-Conduct refresher training for 24(14 men and 10 women) community health workers volunteers on health, hygiene, sanitation and nutrition. -Conduct 5 days training on Integrated Management of Acute Malnutrition (IMAM) for 38 (21men, 17 women) nutrition workers as per the IMAM protocol. The training duration will last 5 days.In view of the covid protocols, the number of staffs will be strictly limited to 38 and all spaced out 1.5 meters apart. All participants will be required to have masks on and observe the prescribed world health Organization (WHO) COVID19 preventive measures. -Conduct five days training on Infant and Young Child Feeding (IYCF) care messages to 38 health/nutrition volunteers, 11 qualified OTP nurse, 5 IYCF officers and 22 Auxilary Nurses to enable enhanced the effective and best practices of IYCF-E among the target population in Badhadhe, Elwak and Abudwak districts. Project Indicators and Targets;-Number of boys and girls screened for malnutrition by the community and referredTarget:32,550/or 15,949 boys, 16,601 girls.-Number of PLWs screened for acute malnutrition by community workers and referred (Target:12,344)-Number of boys and girls 6-59 months with Severe acute Malnutrition treated (Target:18,030/or 8,835 boys and 9,195 girls .-Number of boys and girls 6-59 months with Moderate Acute Malnutrition treated (Target:2,520)-Number of boys and girls aged 6-59 months reached with Vitamin A supplementation (Target:32,550/or 15,949 boys, 16,601 girls.-Number of PLW with Moderate Acute Malnutrition Treated (Target:6,344)-Number of community nutrition workers/volunteers trained (Target:24-Number persons attending the Nutrition, Health and Hygiene Promotion Sessions conducted ( 9,450)-Number of pregnant and lactating women receiving first individual counselling on appropriate IYCF/IYCF-E (Target:12,344)-Number of persons attended 3 days of refresher training for community health volunteers (24)-Number of nutrition staff trained(IMAM); Target 38-Number of nutrition staff trained (IYCF) target: 16

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

For exclusion of ethnic groups and exposure of women and girls to sexual violence, including sexual exploitation and abuse in relation to access to humanitarian assistance, ADA has mitigation measures in place. First of all, the project provides all target beneficiaries, equal access to project integrated activities with no discrimination, in addition to, creating a safer and more secure environment for children and PLWs; Beneficiaries will be clearly informed that all services are free of charge through creating awareness or and by posters; This project is designed to have monitoring teams in place to assess, whether service delivery locations are adequate and are being used in a dignified way;- A confidential feedback mechanism and protection reporting system will be designed and all beneficiaries will be informed about this mechanism and how to refer or report incidents or violations;- Measures are in place to ensure that beneficiaries understand that it is their right to receive equitable and safe assistance including people with specific needs or vulnerabilities;- All project staff will be trained on GBV prevention and response in emergencies and PSEA, in order to ensure confidentiality and full adherence with the Do No Harm principle with strict dealing with Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (SEA) and GBV issues.Campaigns will be conducted to create awareness regarding stigmatization of people with disabilities (PWD) and GBV victims.Moreover, ADA will ensure to provide a referral and network mechanisms with the partners that are currently implementing protection programs. -Additionally, ADA will give some preferential treatment for the people with disabilities with a target reach of atleast 3% of the total beneficiaries.ADA will ensure that services can be accessed by persons with non-mobility related disabilities.
