Project Summary

QC will increase access to safe drinking water sources for IDPs and host communities in Abujbiha locality including Tagmala village, Rashad, Alabasiya localities in South Kordufan State. The projects will provide access to water yards as well as 33 hand pumps which will serve together 26500 individuals including 12000 IDPs.
The project will be implemented in 5 months started in January 2022. The project will take sustainability in consideration through applying active community engagement. There will be training for community on how to operate water pumps and water yards (community water management) as well as there will be distribution for maintenance tools and devices.
These 33 established water pumps will be as a base for QC for future upgrading to mini water yards by its future projects or other partner projects.
QC will apply a solar system in the water yards to avoid energy source challenges in these areas especially high operating cost for diesel generators.

1 - Needs

According to the information we obtained from Ministry of Irrigation and Water Resources (Dams Implementation Unit), difficulty of obtaining clean water in South Kordofan is still the main issue there. Large parts of the localities of Abu Jubaiha, Rashad and Abbasiya still suffer from difficulty in obtaining clean drinking water, due to the lack of water stations there to meet the increasing demands for water. Due to the scarcity of clean drinking water and the absence of health care, water-borne diseases have become a real threat to the life of people in the Eastern Mountains region, also due to the lack of clean drinking water for humans and animals, and it is still rare and insufficient like previous years. There is an urgent need to dig more dowanki to serve families and communities that share drinking water with their animals from the same water sources. Lack of and limited access to drinking water and sanitation services for the environment, in addition to the lack of care of most of the local population about their physical health, there are great risks that can lead to health disasters. Base on Ministry of Irrigation there are more than 1000 communities lacking water source in 2 kilometer diameters distance.

2 - Activities/Outputs

Activities; QC will drill and install 33 hand pumps and 2 mini water yards in south Kordofan . Also QC will train local communities to be able to operate these water facilities independently through community water management workshops targeting at least one person from each targeted 33 villages/well. Also QC will distribute maintenance set for each trained person. QC will build 4 latrines in one selected school with 4 fixed hand washing facilities for critical time use. Also QC will distribute Jerrycan/bucket and ibrig (2 JC per HH) and 2 buckets with cover and ibrig/HH for handwashing mobile facilities for 100 HH.To implement these activities QC will work closely in coordination with HAC, Government State Water Cooperation, wash sector as well as targeted communities. Geophysics studies will be conducted to select the most suitable sites. After finishing drilling by Rig machine, a water sample will be taken and analyzed to ensure water safety and quality. Qatar Charity has enormous capabilities in implementing this type of projects, for example, regard drinking water projects, QC has implemented more than 250 water wells, including about 210 small wells (HAND PUMPS) in each of the three Darfur states as a part of the voluntary return project for villages affected by the war as well as South Sudan before Separation and North Kordofan. We also have a great history in the construction of integrated water plants (WTP) (Water treatment plant)) in each of the Nile River and Gedaref states. QC has an integrated technical team in addition to a well-digger, equipment, machinery, geological engineers and water engineers. Projects output; About 26500 beneficiaries will have access to safe drinking water including 16500 IDPs in Abujebiha, Rashad and ALabasiya localities. In another words these targeted communities will have a lower risk for water base, water related and water induced diseases. Also they have good opportunity to scale up their income through treating animals as well as home planting. Output summary; 26500 beneficiaries will have access to safe drinking water including 16500 IDPs in Abujebiha, Rashad and ALabasiya localities.
