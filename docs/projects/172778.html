Project Summary

Goal Area 2 of CRS’ Vision 2030 strategy is for all people to survive and thrive in the face of disasters and part of the strategies to outcome this is to make sure that the developmental and mental health of crisis-affected children is protected while ensuring that people affected by crisis have increased resilience capacities to cope with future crises. The COVID-19 pandemic was one such crisis and the associated lockdowns have led to a rise in cases of GBV and SGBV especially in the domestic setting and have further led to new stressors on parents and caregivers as a result of children’s prolonged stay at home due to school closure and loss of livelihood due to COVID-19 induced economic challenges.
The project is aiming for the accomplishment of priority protection needs and improvement in resilience for girls, boys, women and men in dealing with trauma induced by GBV, SGBV, COVID-19. The project also aims to ensure that the mental well-being of all participants is strengthened to ensure that people affected by crisis have increased resilience capacities to cope with future crises. The project will reach out to 10 000 participants in Harare, Chitungwiza, Umguza, Bulilima and Mangwe

1 - Activities

The priority project activities will be as follows in Harare, Chitungwiza, Umguza, Bulilima and Mangwe:- Provision of NFIs to female project participants to ensure their safety and dignity.- Provision of psychosocial support to children through a structured curriculum (Singing to the Lions) which will span over several weeks with follow up meetings and activities- Provision of psychosocial support to Women and Men through a structured curriculum (Rising from Resilient Roots)The project will work closely with other clusters, for example, the health cluster and the social protection cluster to consolidate efforts and buttress its impact.
