Project Summary

The project aims to increase access to hygiene NFIs and to improve WASH infrastructure for the benefit of the most vulnerable IDPs, returnees and other conflict affected communities in Luhansk and Donetsk oblasts (GCA along the contact line and NGCA). The action will be implemented through a comprehensive set of WASH related activities, including distribution of hygiene kits for 16,800 beneficiaries, distribution of diapers for 6,000 beneficiaries, distribution of vouchers for 3,150 household members and 43,750 patient/visitors of social institutions that will undergo the WASH rehabilitation works.

Needs

In the conflict affected area on both sides of the line of contact in Donetsk and Luhansk regions, 4.8 million people are served by centralized water systems. Of those, water supply to 4.2 million is directly affected by the conflict, and greatly at risk, and 3.2 million people require WASH assistance. Water and sanitation systems, as well as most of social facilities are old and outdated and have not been repaired adequately since the conflict started;Based on IOM observations and discussions with beneficiaries and partners in conflict affected areas, the need for diapers and hygiene supplies are confirmed as essential, especially for vulnerable individuals such as: elderly, people in social institutions, women, children, new-borns among others. The limited or lack of access to hygiene materials is mostly determined by the absence of such products in remote rural areas (especially those close to the contact line often requiring vulnerable individuals to travel along unsafe roads to reach the nearest market). According to Joint Market Monitoring, Round III, presented in February 2018, partial/limited availability of hygiene items was mentioned by the respondents residing in Lobacheve, Peredilske and Troitske, while in Bolotne hygiene item are not available;High costs of the hygiene items that was reported by 79% of respondent of the Joint Market Monitoring, makes hygiene items unaffordable for low income families or families that need to prioritize costs for rent, medicines, food, water;Post-distribution monitoring (PDM) conducted by IOM in June 2018 on the multi-purpose cash assistance provided revealed that when it comes to prioritizing the most basic needs, beneficiaries would prioritize winterization needs (purchasing wood or coal) (20%), purchase lifesaving medicines and spend money for healthcare services (24%) or spend money for food (10%);In line with the WASH Cluster priority matrix, the targeted rehabilitation of key WASH sites and structural repairs in social institutions accommodating the most vulnerable and key community sites represent a crucial intervention to ensure adequate access to water and hygiene, as well as decent staying conditions for the most vulnerable individuals and communities in conflict affected areas. Based on IOM’s field monitoring visits in areas close to the contact line (NGCA) numerous social infrastructures, including IDP dormitories, centres caring for the elderly and persons with disabilities and healthcare facilities, are currently unable to respond to the needs of their respective caseload, due to original poor infrastructural conditions, further exacerbated by the hostilities.

Activities & outcomes

Outcome: Vulnerable conflict-affected persons in GCA and NGCA of Ukraine are able to access essential hygiene facilities and critical hygiene items. Output 1: Vulnerable conflict-affected individuals in Luhansk and Donetsk oblasts (NGCA) have improved access to critical hygiene items to improve sanitation practices. - Activities for output 1: 1.1. Coordinate selection criteria and identification of the potential beneficiaries with de facto authorities;1.2. Carry out procurement of hygiene kits and diapers through an open competitive process according to IOM procurement rules and regulations as well as on transparency and accountability;1.3. Distribute hygiene kits to vulnerable households;1.4. Conduct post-monitoring distribution on support provided. Output 2: Vulnerable conflict-affected individuals in Luhansk and Donetsk oblasts (GCA) residing within 20km along the contact line benefited from the distribution of vouchers to address hygiene needs. - Activities for output 2: 2.1. Identify the criteria and the need of affected population on hygiene items, register population, select the beneficiaries according to the criteria;2.2. Identify local suppliers of hygiene items which could provide the vouchers to beneficiaries;2.3. Carry out distribution of the vouchers among the selected households;2.4. Conduct post-monitoring distribution to assess the quality of provided support and identify the needs. Output 3: WASH facilities in targeted social institutions in Donetsk and Luhansk regions (NGCA) are functional and meet the needs of individuals seeking social services - Activities for Output 3:3.1. Assess WASH rehabilitation needs as well as structural repairs of identified public infrastructures such as health and social institutions including, for example, repairs of toilets and showers, heating systems, filtrations systems, internal piping and water connections as well as more structural rehabilitations such as roofing, flooring, tiling, glassing, among others;3.2. Select construction companies through an open competitive process according to IOM rules and regulations;3.3. Conduct works as per identified needs, designs and BoQs, including periodic technical monitoring;3.4. Conduct post-distribution monitoring on WASH rehabilitation activities conducted. The beneficiaries of vouchers and hygiene items will be identified based on agreed upon vulnerability criteria such as: single headed households, presence of elderly or persons with disabilities within the household, multi-child households. Public structures eligible for proposed WASH facilities rehabilitations and repair works will be selected by prioritizing areas close to the contact line, those damaged in the course of hostilities, and those of the greatest importance for the local communities, accommodating the most vulnerable groups of population.

Outcome: Vulnerable conflict-affected persons in GCA and NGCA of Ukraine are able to access essential hygiene facilities and critical hygiene items - % hygiene support recipients confirming their improved sanitation practices (Target:75%). Output 1: Vulnerable conflict-affected individuals in Luhansk and Donetsk oblasts (NGCA) have improved access to critical hygiene items to improve sanitation practices - 1) # of potential beneficiary households verified in target areas during the verification process (Target: 8,000 households);2) # of potential beneficiary social institutions verified in target areas during the assessment visits (Target: 20 social institutions);3) # of hygiene kits procured and distributed (Target: 8,000);4) # of diapers (packs) procured and distributed (Target: 36,000);5) # of individuals benefited from the provision hygiene kits (Target: 16,800 individuals (8,800 female and 8,000 male);6) # of individuals benefitting from the provision of diapers (packs) (Target: 6,000 individuals, of whom 4,000 female and 2,000 male). Output 2: Vulnerable conflict-affected individuals in Luhansk and Donetsk oblasts (GCA) residing within 20km along the contact line benefited from the distribution of vouchers to address hygiene needs - 1) # of vouchers for hygiene items distributes (Target: 1,500);2) # of household members benefited from the provision of vouchers (Target: 3,150 individuals (1,890 female and 1,260 male). Output 3: WASH facilities in targeted social institutions in Donetsk and Luhansk regions (NGCA) are functional and meet the needs of individuals seeking social services - 1) # of social institutions supported with the rehabilitation of WASH facilities (Target: 125 rehabilitations);2) # of individuals served by the social institutions whose WASH facilities were rehabilitated (Target: 43,750 individuals, of whom 26,250 female and 17,500 male).
