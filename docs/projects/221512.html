Project Summary

Empowering Futures: Transforming Schools with Comprehensive WASH Solutions in the Far North Region of Cameroon aims to address critical water, sanitation, and hygiene (WASH) challenges in three schools across three subdivisions. The project focuses on improving WASH infrastructure, promoting hygiene behavior change, and enhancing menstrual health services. By implementing child-focused solutions and incorporating Cash Based Assistance (CBA) to support vulnerable families, the initiative strives to reduce absenteeism, improve health outcomes, and create a supportive learning environment. The primary beneficiaries are approximately 3,000 students and 150 school staff members, ensuring a healthier and more inclusive education system.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The "Empowering Futures: Transforming Schools with Comprehensive WASH Solutions in the Far North Region of Cameroon" project addresses several critical needs through its activities. These needs have been identified based on the challenges faced by schools in the region, exacerbated by natural and health crises. 1. Unsafe Drinking Water: Many schools rely on contaminated water sources, leading to waterborne diseases among students. The project will install commercial-grade water filtration systems and water storage facilities to ensure that students and staff have access to safe and reliable drinking water. This intervention is crucial for reducing health risks and improving overall well-being. 2. Poor Sanitation Facilities: Existing sanitation facilities in schools are inadequate, with many lacking functional toilets and clean, private spaces for students. The project will construct or renovate gender-segregated, child-friendly toilets equipped with water for flushing. Additionally, facilities for menstrual hygiene management will be installed to ensure that girls have the necessary support during menstruation. These improvements will create a healthier school environment and reduce absenteeism, especially among girls. 3. Limited Handwashing Facilities: Inadequate handwashing stations and lack of soap contribute to poor hygiene practices, increasing the spread of illnesses. The project will install durable handwashing stations with soap in all three schools. Hygiene education programs for students and staff will be conducted to promote regular handwashing and other good hygiene practices, ultimately reducing the incidence of communicable diseases. 4. Menstrual Hygiene Management: The lack of menstrual hygiene facilities and support in schools leads to high absenteeism among girls. The project will create girl-friendly sanitation facilities and hold education and awareness events on menstrual health for both boys and girls. Teachers and parents will be trained to provide support, fostering an inclusive environment that empowers girls to attend school regularly. 5. Behavioral Challenges: Changing long-standing hygiene practices requires sustained effort and education. The project will implement behavior change programs that integrate knowledge, norms, emotions, and habits. By leveraging students as hygiene ambassadors and using environmental nudges, the project aims to create lasting behavior change that promotes good hygiene practices. 6. Economic Barriers: Some families face financial constraints that hinder their ability to provide for their children's hygiene and educational needs. Cash Based Assistance (CBA) will be provided to vulnerable families to cover hygiene and education-related expenses, ensuring that all children can benefit from the improved WASH facilities. By addressing these assessed needs, the project aims to create a safer, healthier, and more inclusive educational environment for approximately 3,000 students and 150 school staff members in the Far North Region of Cameroon.
