Project Summary

The Rehabilitation and Care Center aims to:
1) Providing protection services for children with disabilities, in addition to providing protection from gender-based violence for all groups, especially women and girls, and working to improve the quality of life for them and reduce the risks to which they are exposed. The project’s activities and programs are designed to support these groups from different societies, taking into account the different needs among all groups.
The evaluation visits carried out by Al-Batoul through the ORVs from 2015 to 2022 indicate that there are so many families with a disable child, as the number reached, according to the latest statistics provided by the Directorate of Social Affairs and Labor - The Office of Disability in Tartous & Lattakia Governorate to 10416 in addition to the number of displaced from these families.
It was also found that there are large numbers of deprivation of opportunities and services for women and girls; Due to the deteriorating economic conditions of the family, women's needs were considered secondary needs that were not met, including health needs. an increase in cases of physical and sexual violence within the family was monitored.
In addition to the increase in early marriage cases, which was caused by the deterioration of the economic conditions and the high costs of education, which increased the cases of girls dropping out schools and wait for marriage as the only solution to survive. All the risks that we may face through providing case management services would be faced though expert case managers who would know how to react and when it is the right time to react successfully.
All GBV related cases that were detected in the community of Target area were: deprivation of opportunities, neglecting women’s health needs, physical violence, sexual violence, domestic violence and exploitation. All previously mentioned cases would get the needed services from the program and would be referred to get extra specialized services outside the program from health facilities, doctors, lawyers, temporary shelter places and other active actors in the targeted area. Due to the big number of disempowered women who do not have a job or a hobby to make a living, vocational or business trainings would support the community and create better opportunities for better life in addition to the educational services in the program that would also empower the whole community and make it stronger.
2) Regarding the Educational Sector, it is considered an essential factor of response in the field of protection. Actually, the goals of this sector are achieved by providing different types and alternatives of the educational programs in addition to capacity building of teachers in the field of management. All the procedures will support the National Educational System in meeting the needs of many children and adolescents in the field of education.
And many educational services of a higher quality will be provided to many children and adolescents of the primary and secondary stages at school who need to be provided with academic support in the light of the national curriculums provided by the Ministry of Education in Syria.
In addition to that, a safe learning environment will be provided to all students away from any type of exploitation. on monthly basis,4ws reports will be sent to the sectors.
Today, based on a field survey conducted recently it was found that about 12,000 students need remedial lessons in various scientific subjects, in addition to 4,450 students who dropped out and 13,672 are at risk of dropping out.
It must be noted that among the most important factors that have been monitored and that lead to a decrease in the level of motivation towards learning: - The current bad economic conditions suffered by the students' families.
-High prices of stationery and books.-Expatriate children who study in other nearby areas have been subjected to harassment and bullying.

8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Since 2020 Al-batoul started focusing on PSEA, First there has been a lot of work to train all staff members on PSEA and raising awareness about the expected behaviors and the disciplinary procedures for those who commit it. Trainings have been conducted to WCWs to make sure that the community itself has a main role in fighting this problem and that key persons have the right knowledge and information to deal with this problem since people of the same society may trust each other more than strangers especially in sensitive subjects. Later, there have been a policy and code of conduct for PSEA read and signed by all staff members. The policy includes whole procedures of complaining and what shall be done after a complaint is submitted. Three complaint mechanisms have been established in each of Al-batoul 4 community centers; complaint box, hotline and email address. Also, the policy contains the investigation process and highly stresses the confidentiality and safety of both the survivors and whistleblowers. Awareness sessions targeted different age categories of displaced and host communities. Brochures were distributed, street commercials have been displaced in crowded and common areas in Tartous City, text messages that assured the existence of complaint mechanisms in Al-Batoul centers and encouraged the beneficiaries to use them without hesitation were sent to almost 2500 persons in the areas covered by Al-Batoul centers.
