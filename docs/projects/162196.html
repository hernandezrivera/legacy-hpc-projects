Project Summary

The project will operate in the south of Chad country in Moissala, the department of Barh Sarah, one of the departments of the Mandoul Region in southern Chad. This region is hosting a large number of refugees from bordering Central African Republic, CAR, living in refugees sites. Currently Chad is hosting more than 100 000 people running from conflict, many of whom are children. The situation for the refugees as well as host communities is becoming more and more permanent as the Central African Republic is still in crisis. The project will target more than 6500 children - refugees, returness, IDP's and children from host communities - from the villages and refugee sites of Dilingala, Dembo, Gon and Silambi, close to the country border. To mitigate the negative affects on the future lives of these children, the proposed intervention aims to contribute to improved access to formal and non-formal, safe and inclusive quality education, to improve learning but also to improve child protection and child wellness. This will be achieved by improving learning enviroments, providing learning material, build teachers' capacity to work with the target group, sensitize communities on the value of education for both boys and girls, support quality-ensuring eductation structures, amongs other things. The project will also focus on giving opportunity for out-of-school youth, to gain life skills to increase their chances to be economically self-sustained. By providing youth with access to vocational training and economical support, this will allow them to be less exposed to exploitation. To implement this project, SOS CHAD will work in collaboration with local partners and the state technical services to ensure the sustainability of the intervention.
The action targets refugees, returnees, IPD’s and host communities in Moissala. The project end beneficiaries will be:
Direct beneficiaries (6588 persons including 2 841 female)
- Primary school: 6414 schoolchildren aged 6-12 including 2755 girls
- Support four (4) Children Friendly Spaces with 480 children including 280 girls
- 61 teachers (15 women): 51 teachers of primary school and 10 for vocational training program
- Vocational training program: 25 young aged 14-18 including 15 girls
- Women School children parents association: 24 persons including 12 women
- School children mother’s association: 24 Women
- School government: 24 school children including at least 12 girls
- GBV clubs: 16 members including at least 8 women and girls
Indirect beneficiaries: 24,095 people (8251 refugees) including 12,843 women from the host communities and refugees at the Dembo sites; Gon, Silambi and Dilingala. Refugee’s data has been estimated through UNHCR 2019 data exploitation of the population in target area. For the population the data has been provided by the civil registry technical service that update data regularly.
SOS Chad will support four (4) Child Friendly Spaces. These Child Friendly Spaces (CFS) will be built during this project. They will be animated and managed in collaboration with the Community Child Protection Networks, which will be created by SOS Chad. Each CFS will have a maximum capacity of 120 children. He will individually follow each enlisted child through a dashboard developed in his name. Active listening centres led by a team of psychologists and psychosocial assistants will monitor cases diagnosed for referencing. This team will complement the referencing of serious cases by following up at home. SOS Chad's support for these newly created structures in Mandoul will consist of training on EAE management, psychosocial support, animation and awareness techniques, detection and referencing of victims of gender-based violence.
Revitalizing four community structures such as community child protection networks in a vision of empowerment.

Implementing Partners

SOS CHAD will be the major player in the direct implementation of the project activities on the ground. However, it will strongly collaborate with the local technical services in charge of education, with community-based organizations and with the existing community-based child protection mechanisms.
During the implementation of the project, SOS CHAD will be technically supported by the SOS Emergency Response (ER) team, which sits at the International Office Region for West and Central Africa (WCAF) in Dakar, Senegal and thereto by others partners in the fields of child protection and education.
