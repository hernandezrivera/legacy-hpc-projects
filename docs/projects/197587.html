Project Summary

The ongoing conflict in the North-West Region of Cameroon has over the past six years now affected over 2 million people as per OCHA sitrep! Shelter and NFIs needs have remained a major cause for concern to most IDP’s and host communities. In response to the acute Shelter/NFI needs of conflict affected populations of target vulnerable communities of this affected region of Cameroon, COHESODEC seeks to significantly respond to the emergency shelter/NFI needs of vulnerable IDP populations in the following target communities/localities: Bafut, Bamenda, Bamenda 3, Benakuma and Wum localities of the North-West Region of Cameroon in 2023. This targeted response will be aimed at improving both the Shelter/NFI situation of 1,600 Vulnerable IDPs and affected host communities with emergency Shelter/NFI’s support.
In implementing this project, COHESODEC will ensure the provision of 800 core and essential NFIs, such as mattresses, plastic sheeting, blankets, jerry cans, kitchen sets, and solar lamps; including market-based interventions for these items reaching out to the most affected populations with these core and essential Non-Food Items that meet environmental criteria. By ensuring that these items meet environmental criteria, COHESODEC will avoid distributing items that do not conform to international environmental standards. We shall also sensitize the beneficiary populations before effective distribution on best practices while also pointing out harmful practices that are prone to destroy the environment which they must avoid as they use their core and essential NFIs.
Moreover, COHESODEC will ensure the provision of emergency shelter to some 800 affected populations regarding emergency shelter materials and kits; including market-based interventions for these items as these shelter items are distributed / installed while taking into consideration environmental criteria. By ensuring that these items meet environmental criteria, COHESODEC will avoid distributing items that do not conform to international environmental standards. We shall also sensitize the beneficiary populations before effective distribution on best practices while also pointing out harmful practices that are prone to destroy the environment which they must avoid as they install their emergency shelters.
Furthermore, COHESODEC shall equally ensure the provision of rental assistance (free rent, cash-for-rent, or subsidy to 150 most vulnerable people who were forced to flee from their burnt down villages and now squatting in urban areas.
Also, COHESODEC will ensure effective monitoring and evaluation of project activities during the implementation of project activities! Project M&E Staff will constantly and regularly monitor and evaluate project activities to ensure that implementation is in conformity with project action plan and according to deadlines and timelines stipulated.
The Project management overhead cost which includes-(personnel support, transportation, logistics, 7/7 free call-in lines set up and operational cost) will constitute 7% of the total project cost which stands at $43,054. Procurement of Shelter/NFI items to be distributed as usual will be done through a selection process as per COHESODEC procurement policy where the bidding process is advertised, tender files are received, a selection committee is set up to select the best suppliers who meets the procurement criteria in place.
Finally, COHESODEC will ensure proper accountability to affected populations while also making sure that the complaints and feedback mechanism through a free 7/7 Call-in lines are effectively put in place to enable beneficiary communities easily channel in their Complaints while also making sure that management gives timely Feedback to beneficiaries. Accountability to affected populations as in all projects will as usual remain a key component in COHESODEC project implementation strategy!

1 - Besoins / Needs

With regards to Need Assessments conducted by the Shelter Cluster NW/SW and that conducted by COHESODEC involving burnt houses in the North-West Region, there are over 225,516 IDP’s in need of Shelter assistance. These assessments also indicate that 121,165 IDP’s are in urgent need of NFI assistance. According to the assessments, shelter severity needs indicates: 12% extremely inadequate, 48% severely inadequate, 37% moderately inadequate and 4% minorly inadequate. Given the above mentioned disturbing situation of the North-West Region, COHESODEC seeks to ensure that the needs of these affected populations are ameliorated upon in 2023. This project will ensure that the needs of men, women, girls and boys are met as COHESODEC hopes to distribute emergency Shelter and Core relief item kits per household for the use of the entire household. Moreover, in the implementation of this project, COHESODEC shall prioritize persons with disabilities by ensuring that at least 15% of persons living with disabilities are support with emergency shelter and core relief item kits.Furthermore, in the implementation of this project, COHESODEC shall use part of the overhead budget cost to set-up and put in place a 7/7 free call-in lines to beneficiaries for complaints and feedback mechanism with which to receive and address complaints. Also, in implementing this project, COHESODEC shall ensure that overhead cost which includes-(personnel support, transportation, logistics, 7/7 free call-in lines set up and operational cost) will constitute 7% of the total project cost which stands at $43,054.
