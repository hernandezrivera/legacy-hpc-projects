0 - Project Summary (extended)

100 HHs supported restarting or expanding Small-Micro Enterprises through the provision of business skills training and financial or in-kind support. Activities: Almost 100 applicants with disabilities who have existing businesses will have attended three sessions on business skills to improve their skills to write their proposals/ business plans properly and will submit their proposals/ business plans to be reviewed and evaluated in order to select 100 beneficiaries to receive financial or in-kind support.The project steps as below: - A wide call for registration will be conducted in the targeted area for the selected sectors which will be determined according to the market assessment results.- Initial selection of applicants who met the eligibility criteria.- Conducting business skills training (3 sessions).- Receiving the proposals/ business plans from the applicants.- A committee from Sened will develop the scorecard to evaluate proposals/ business plans.- Review and evaluate the proposals/ business plans according to the agreed scorecard and select the eligible grant recipients, in a competitive manner according to their submission score.- Distribution of cash grants or in kind for only 100 beneficiaries, 50% of the beneficiaries will receive in-kind support and 50% of the beneficiaries will receive Cash grants at an average 800 USD.- Monitoring and following up on expanding the beneficiaries' businesses.This project will be implemented in parallel with Edu & Pro project in the same project area and the participants will receive awareness sessions about identifying and reporting any SEA incidents in the project area. Sened will also use new SEA awareness message dedicated to persons who are mute to report SEA incidents. These messages have been developed and shared by IOM and they are provided in Arabic language. Moreover, Sened will consider addressing the distinct needs of men, women, and children, emphasizing persons with disabilities. Sensitive data, primarily related to CP cases, is kept confidential following Sened’s policies and standard procedures for sharing confidential information. An effective feedback and complaint mechanism is in place, and beneficiaries are informed of how to access it, including reporting SEA and child protection safeguarding.
