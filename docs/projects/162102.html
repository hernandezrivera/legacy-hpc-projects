Project Summary

The project will enhance the capacity of humanitarian respondents in community engagement and accountability, ensuring that the needs of the affected population are at the centre of every intervention. Additionally, projects will be implemented through local partners, with adequate capacity building and mentoring support provided throughout. The project also aims to ensure community’s access to timely and lifesaving information on different thematic areas and allow them to participate in decisions that affect their lives through effective community engagement and community participation practices (CHS Commitment 4/ Grand bargain). The community will be provided with information to address their daily life issues presented in a variety of mechanisms including print media, audio content, narrowcast as well as community- led edutainment initiatives. The project also seeks to draw from learning in the response through surveys that will inform on community preferences for information and feedback, awareness of their rights, community participation and feedback mechanisms. The findings from the surveys will inform the partners, sectors and the larger response on appropriate and relevant approaches and strengthen Community engagement and Accountability practice in the Rohingya response.

Needs

-Capacity building of staff and stakeholders on community engagement and Accountability to enable them to mainstream and apply Core principles in service provision. Strengthened capacity will lead to mainstreaming of Accountability in response projects and activities, ensuring that the communities are actively involved and at the centre of every intervention, and their needs and contributions are listened to addressed. Effective communication skills will provide the communities with platform to participate in decisions that affect their lives and be actively involved in project implementation from the design phase to evaluation phase.-Limited access to timely and lifesaving information, especially for women and girls in camps and host community, due to cultural movement restrictions. In some cases, whilst information is available, literacy levels and those living with Special Needs must be factored in. There is need to develop audio and print information to be provided in various ways, relevant to the community context. Women and girls need to be provided with information in settings culturally accessible to them. -Community knowledge on feedback mechanisms and subsequent access to safe and responsive mechanisms remains a huge need. Gaps still exist in the community’s awareness of feedback mechanisms and systems leads to lack of trust for the mechanisms and makes them more comfortable using the traditional community structures, which may take time to respond to community concerns, not respond or not in some cases violate the principles of privacy and confidentiality in resolving complaints and feedback. There is need to raise awareness on feedback mechanisms, and make multiple mechanisms accessible for all groups, especially women, girls and the elderly who have restricted mobility (Physically and culturally) -Gaps still exist in response wide learning and documentation of community engagement practice. There is a need to continuously engage the women, men, boys and girls and conduct different types of surveys in find out their preferences for information provision channels, information needs and feedback mechanisms.

Activities or outputs

1. Capacity Development- Capacity building and refresher trainings for selected community leaders and representatives to strengthen their capacity to advocate for accountability and protection in camp 14, 15 and host community -Capacity strengthening of 400 staff and volunteers on CHS, Accountability, Safeguarding and the use of feedback ensure effective maintenance of systems, responsive platforms and programme quality. (Ongoing)-Monthly Accountability review meetings with partner agencies to review progress against Accountability and CHS Commitments2.Communication resources and Community engagementDetails: -Edutainment activities in the form of songs and interactive theatre to raise community awareness in public community spaces (4 theatre sessions/month), reaching women, men, girls and boys. The These sessions will be conducted in identified spaces at sub block level in the camps, to ensure participation of women, girls and the elderly who could otherwise have cultural restrictions.-Development, design and distribution of IEC materials in the camp and host community to ensure that the community have knowledge on their rights, feedback mechanism use, prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse and other issues affecting their daily lives. Content topics will be influenced by feedback on information needs received from women, men, girls and boys during courtyard meetings and narrowcast sessions. Materials will be developed in different formats and delivered at community and household (door to door) level to ensure access by everyone in the community including those with restricted movements-Conduct weekly bi-monthly community courtyard meetings in the community for awareness on beneficiary rights, and related issues. Separate meetings will be conducted for women, men, boys and girls to ensure active participation by each group. Meetings for women and girls in camps will be conducted in identified spaces at sub block level (where they have access)-Produce and air 30+ Radio messages focussing on community rights, the rights of vulnerable groups including the women, children, PWD, and other social issues affecting their daily living as identified.-Development and airing of 12 thematic Narrowcast messages (1 Monthly) to reach a total of 22,400 listeners in 54 sessions3. Assessment/researches/Studies-Advocate for improved accountability and adaptive programming across the entire humanitarian response, where robust qualitative and quantitative documentation and evidence gathered host community will be disseminated and used as baseline evidence for future response programming. (CHS7) -Conduct 2 Accountability and CWC evaluation surveys and produce a report to inform the organisation and will also be shared across the sectors to support programme design and ensure that programmes delivered are appropriate and relevant to the context (CHS1). Research will be conducted in consultation with CWC WG, preferably following a joint framework-Conduct 1 community Accountability Assessment in Palong Khali to identify community preferences for information dissemination and feedback mechanisms.
