Project Summary

Istituto Oikos aims to support displaced, returnees and hosting communities in Monapo, Mossuril and Nacala Districts focusing on housing needs and enhancing community resilience. Key activities include capacity-building in resilient construction, early recovery solutions adapted to the context and to local materials, and revitalization of community disaster preparedness committees.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

In the coastal areas of the Nampula province, there are no established IDP camps. However, the presence of IDPs and returnees has significantly increased in recent months. According to data from local authorities, approximately 900 households are present across the districts of Monapo, Mossuril, and Nacala. Some of these areas have been adversely affected by recent climatic events, resulting in precarious housing conditions for the local communities. Istituto Oikos is committed to supporting displaced individuals, returnees, and hosting communities in these areas. Our focus is on addressing housing needs and strengthening community resilience keeping into the impact on the environement. Our key initiatives include conducting assessments to identify appropriate construction materials and techniques before intervention. Additionally, we aim to build capacity in resilient construction techniques, provide early recovery solutions tailored to the local context and materials, and rejuvenate community disaster preparedness committees.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The proposed project adopts a comprehensive approach to identify and prevent potential risks, prioritizing the safety and well-being of the communities in Nampula. Collaborating closely with local institutions, the wash cluster, and partners, the project utilizes existing feedback mechanisms like Linha Verde for real-time feedback on service provision, product quality, and information requests, allowing for swift response and resolution of concerns. The project actively engages communities through consultations, focus groups, and participatory data collection to identify and mitigate risks. These participatory processes will not only identify constraints but also empower communities to actively contribute to risk identification and mitigation strategies. Tailored sessions foster understanding and promote new habits to mitigate risks. In addition, Protection mainstreaming is integral to the project's design strategy, focusing on preserving safety, dignity, and equitable access. Oikos adheres to the 'do no harm' principle and Oikos staff is trained in identifying solution that respect these principles. Moreover, Oikos coordinates closely with protection actors, addressing gender-based violence (GBV) and other protection needs through referrals, ensuring a proactive approach to safeguarding vulnerable populations.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

This action plan is designed based on the inputs provided by target group identified through ongoing community engagement and participatory methods. It emphasizes collaboration with communities, valuing their resources and traditional knowledge to ensure community acceptance and active involvement. The project will create spaces for interaction, such as public consultations, focus gender groups and community data collection, to identify constraints and ways forward. While the construction sector has traditionally been male-dominated, our project is committed to enhancing gender balance by ensuring a minimum of 20% representation of women among participants in resilient construction training and in the selection of field personnel. Furthermore, emphasizing women and youth's involvement in the selection of beneficiaries and training groups ensures informed and inclusive practices, recognizing that their participation is crucial for appropriate and accessible shelter services aligned with their needs.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The project ensures inclusion of vulnerable households, especially including people with disabilities, through active participation of local stakeholders in beneficiary selection. Oikos implements proactive measures to prevent risks and ensure inclusion in Shelter activities. Consultations with individuals with disabilities and specific needs allow identify specific construction requirements and adaptation. Oikos actively involves people with disabilities in designing infrastructure for improved accessibility, incorporating features like handrails. Additionally, a community survey ensures inclusive infrastructure, aiming for long-term inclusivity even in the absence of disabled individuals currently.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Environmental conservation is a priority for Istituto Oikos, as per its mandate. Our solutions prioritize an environmentally friendly approach by utilizing locally sourced materials whenever feasible. Additionally, we advocate for a 'reduce, recycle, and reuse' system, particularly in construction activities. Moreover, Istituto Oikos adhere to the country's environmental regulations and to Oikos internal environemental policy. Our project deliberately avoids promoting materials sourced from non-renewable resources, striving to minimize the use of non-reusable materials to curtail excessive waste production. By prioritizing sustainability and adherence to environmental standards, we aim to ensure our initiatives align with the principles of environmental conservation and responsible resource management.
