Project Summary

The conflict in Syria has resulted in disruption of the expanded program on immunization resulting in occurrence of polio and measles outbreaks. Disruption of routine immunization services was significant in areas in northern Syria that are not reachable through cross-line operations for vaccine delivery. Furthermore, the disruption of routine immunization services in northern Syria for the last 7 years has left several birth cohorts susceptible to several of the vaccine preventable diseases. Voices from Syria have urged the need for re-establishing delivery of routine immunization services in those areas.
Syria Immunization Group (SIG) was established late 2013 post the Polio outbreak. SIG is tasked to lead and coordinate immunization activities in northern Syria through planning, capacity building, restoring EPI, implementing campaigns, coordinating with stakeholders including health directorates and also vaccination financing.
The group consists of:
• General Assembly –NGOs/INGOs providing medical aid in north of Syria, it is co-chaired by WHO & UNICEF.
• Technical Committee that is led by WHO and UNICEF.
Many vaccination activities (SIAs, AIRIs and RI) have been done in Northern Syria by SIG to respond to the outbreaks or to prevent occurrence of new ones, including five AIRIs, Two Measles/Rubella campaigns and 19 rounds of ‘House to House Polio vaccination campaigns targeting 800.000 - 1.4 million children depending on the accessibility.
In addition to SIAs and AIRIs, SIG planned to revitalize EPI in the north of Syria; EPI was launched in March 2017 with eighty-seven EPI centers are working now in Aleppo, Idleb and Hama. Fifty-five centers are working in Idleb governorates and 5 centers are in Hama.
The preparation for lunching EPI included; planning, preparing training material guidelines, policies and forms and provide training to service providers and supervisors at governorate and district levels.
EPI services are provided by a team of vaccinator in centers, which are located in primary health centers, these activities are managed and supervised by governorate and districts supervisors in close coordination and support by WHO and UNICEF.
Supervisory team at each governorates level consist of five people, these are: supervisor-data officer, social mobilizer, logistic & cold chain officer and finance officer. The district supervisory team consists of supervisor, data officer, social mobilizer, logistic & cold chain officer. Governorates and district supervisory teams are working under supervision of SIG technical committee considering WHO and UNICEF standards, that will help in capacity building and sustainability of this program.
This proposal is to cover the sustainability of monitoring, evaluation, capacity building in services delivery and data management at both governorate and district levels in Idleb and Hama for one year. These activates are implemented by coordination and collaboration between SIG, NGOs, and health directorates inside Syria. Vaccination departments in governorate and district level in Idleb Hama need support to implement all these activities and this project achieve this target

Needs

Routine immunization activities and campaigns are the most important Health program at emergency and war conditions to decrease morbidity and mortality from vaccine preventable diseases.As Syrian children are still deprived of the basic routine immunization services since the beginning of the crisis which put at high risk for Vaccine Preventable diseases. The re-launch of the routine Immunization Program in northern Syria is substantial, which needs a national Experts team at the Governorate and district levels to follow up the process of planning, service provider selection, training, supervision at the grass root closely and work under the SIG technical committee based on the WHO guidelines.

Implementation of activities

Providing information and data necessary for proper planning of immunization activities in Idleb &Hama (Northern Syria).Participate and provide guidance for macro and micro planning for immunization activities. Follow up refreshing trainings in Idleb &Hama.Conduct necessary refreshing training for service providers at the selected PHCs in coordination with the local NGOs.Support the implementation of activities through communication with the field staff, troubleshooting and providing supportive supervision to ensure proper implementation of the activities.Participate in the data management for the immunization activities through data entry, analysis and interpretation. Suggest corrective actions and recommendations to improve future vaccination activities.Provide logistical technical support and data management of the stocks at the central level.Provide expert support to the communication group at the PHC level to increase the access at the vaccination sites and utilization of services.Conduct advocacy activities at the level of governorate & district leadership and implement proper communication activities at the grass root level to ensure the communityparticipation and ownership.
