Project Summary

Output 1: Enhancing the resilience capacity of 160 most vulnerable HH in Afrin by providing them with vocational, business and life skills training, consumption support and coaching to enhance their capacity to have access to income generation opportunities/employment.
The main vocational training scheme will be the textile scheme for output 1.1, and Electronic devices maintenance for males / Make-up and hairdressing training schema for females under output 1.2.
output 1.1 and output 1.2 will take place at the livelihood center in Afrin. Selected beneficiaries will have access to on-job vocational training and income generation opportunities for vulnerable households over six months for output 1.1 and four months for output 1.2 .
The period of six and four months was designed based on iMDAT experience throughout the last projects, the context, demand, and supply, of the local market at targeted areas.
Over the course of this intervention, two rounds will be conducted under output 1.1 (each round is 6 months) targeting 80 BNFs and output 1.2 (each round is 4 months) targeting 80 BNFs. The selected beneficiaries will be supported by monthly wages, equal to the Survival Minimum Expenditure Basket (SMEB) over the training period.
This support will be conditional as beneficiaries will be asked to commit to 5 months of vocational training under output 1.1 and 3 months under output 1.2, in addition to 1 month of follow-up and monitoring for both outputs 1.1 and 1.2.
It is expected to produce 2,000 children’s jeans clothes sets under output 1.1 to be distributed to the most vulnerable children in the targeted areas, where the time of training will be designed to be 100% for training during 1st and 2nd month, then 50% for training and 50% for production from 3rd to 5th months.
Output 2: IMDAT builds activities under result 2.2 upon past experiences and expertise of the IMDAT team with CfW driven interventions. The focus will be to enable women and FHHs to participate in and have access to income generation activities through home based CFW knitting program. By creating income generation opportunities for 500 HHs via home based CFW knitting scheme over the project period which will include 2 rounds each round will be 4 months in Afrin and Azaz and will target 250 BNFs for each round in both locations, each beneficiary will be provided with 100 USD on monthly basis. Furthermore, the participants at this scheme will be provided with knitting training, and no skills are requested to enroll in this scheme.
Every beneficiary after finishing the four months will be able to knit scarves, hats, gloves, and sweater. These knitting skills will enable her to produce for her family or if she wants to look for job opportunities that require these skills.
At the end of each month, the beneficiaries will deliver the required knitted pieces (first month 5 knitted sets are required “set 4 -12 years includes hat, gloves and scarf ), second month 3 knitted items “4-7 years jacket/sweater, third month 2 knitted items “8 -12 years jacket/sweater, then fourth month 3 knitted sets for 1-3 years baby (include scarf, gloves, hat and sweater). These numbers were calculated based on IMDAT previous experience in knitting projects.
