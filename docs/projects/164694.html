Project Summary

In its eighth year, the conflict inside Syria shows all the signs of a complex protracted humanitarian crisis. Takaful Al-Sham is aiming to support IDPs with WASH assistance in Aleppo and Idleb governorates according to the following sub-districts:
- Atareb 3116 people (Girls 742, Boys 748, Women 722, Men 903) 623 HHs.
- Daret Azza 1072 people (Girls 255, Boys 257, Women 249, Men 311) 214 HHs.
- Jebel Saman 3048 people (Girls 725, Boys 732, Women 707, Men 884) 610 HHs.
- Zarbah 1368 people (Girls 326, Boys 328, Women 317, Men 397) 274HHs.
- Armanaz 3234 people (Girls 770, Boys 776, Women 750, Men 938) 647 HHs.
- Dana 7598 people (Girls 1808, Boys 1824, Women 1763, Men 2203) 1520 HHs .
- Harim 5806 people (Girls 1382, Boys 1393, Women 1347, Men 1684) 1161 HHs.
- Salqin 1570 people (Girls 374, Boys 377, Women 364, Men 455) 314 HHs .
- Maaret Tamsrin 16288 people (Girls 3877, Boys 3909, Women 3779, Men 4724) 3258 HHs.
The total 8620 HHs will be targeted with various WASH services according to family needs where priority will be given to most vulnerable families which include women, children headed household, disabled person, sick, PLW and elderly to enable vulnerable households face severe and harsh displacement conditions and meet their basic hygiene, water and sanitation needs, as verification will take place through visiting their location.
Takaful's WASH interventions will take place as follows:
- Improving access to humanitarian life-saving/emergency WASH facilities and services, and Support to water quality assurance:
1-Water tanks provision and Water trucking: Targeting vulnerable IDPs residing in camps that are not receiving proper quantity of clean water. Takaful will provide 25 liters for each person daily, public water tanks will be provided as well to be adequate to the specified amount of water. Water disinfectants will be added to ensure providing drinkable clean water to beneficiaries.
2-Installation of latrines with the required collective septic tanks: each block of latrines will be consisted of 2 toilets for males and 2 for females, one of each will be provided with special arrangements for PwD. In addition, 2 baths will be specified for each gender.
The volume of the septic tank will be 20 m³.
3-Sewage desludging and solid waste management: the sewage will be removed and transferred to the specified places by the local authorities and actors, twice every month.
As for the solid waste, garbage bins will be distributed in the targeted camps (1 bin for each 10 families). The garbage bins will be emptied and the garbage will be transferred 3 times a week.
- Distribution of essential WASH non-food items (NFI) and hygiene promotion:
1-Hygiene kits distribution: each kit will contain basic required hygiene items according to WASH cluster standards. The distribution will be considering GBV risk-reduction through separating males and females in 2 lines, moreover, include female members in the team of distribution.
2-Hygiene awareness promotion campaigns: brochures will be distributed in the targeted areas with printed flyers, moreover, hygiene awareness raising session will be conducted at each hygiene kit distribution round at the same locations of distribution. female
members in the team of sessions' provision.
In case of sudden displacement during the winter season and during the implementation period, absolute priority will be given to newly arrived IDPs that meet the vulnerability criteria.
At the beginning of the project Takaful will conduct a meeting with local actors to ensure their participation in the project, and take their suggestions to develop the project activities and implementation.
Vendors will be selected through a committee according to specific criteria focus on accessibility, trust in community…etc.
Takaful will coordinate with WASH cluster and with other organization who works in the same sector and field to avoid any duplication

4 - Needs

After nine years of conflict, the WASH infrastructure and service provision in many parts of Syria require significant investments, repair and operational support. 42 per cent of the population is relying on alternative and often unsafe water sources to meet or complement their water needs. (HRP WASH, 2020).Takaful Al-Sham conducted a WASH assessment in 13 camps in the sub-district of Dana, Daret Ezza, Atareb, and Zerbah, It was found that the main source of drinking water in the assessed locations is being purchased through water trucking, where the three most important needs for IDPs were: access to water 54%, functioning latrines 26% soap and washing powder 20%. The most reported challenges to accessing water were unaffordability price of water trucking 62%, the far distance of water sources 27% and the camps don't have enough water tanks to store the water 11%.
