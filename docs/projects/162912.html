Project Summary

The ultimate expected results of the project is that through provision lifesaving assistance and capacity development, to prevent livelihood depletion of vulnerable communities and building services and supplies through responding to immediate food security & livelihood also protection needs of the target population IDPs as well as vulnerable host communities in South and East Darfur as well as White Nile States in Sudan ,through improved access to agricultural and livestock inputs and provision of livelihoods support for most vulnerable affected HHs including IDPs HHs, host community HHs. Those are critically affected and in need of urgent support as all available livelihood resources are under pressure from the overwhelming increase number of demand for livelihood services which poses a threat of conflict out break among different diverse communities in the targeted states.

1 - Needs

According to Humanitarian Need Over view (HNO) 2019, the population movements within & outside the states is a constant, which has led to an exacerbated humanitarian situation. The unabated economic crisis has been going on Sudan since 2018 has negatively impacted of the economic situation of internal displace persons (IDPs), host communities (HCs) living in White Nile, South and East Darfur states ,Which affected deeply entrenched poverty and under investment Livelihoods in the three states are predominantly rural. Regarding to the climate change impact the average of annual rainfall varies and Being so scarce, water is a primary factor that constrains rural livelihoods in the three states., This is driven by lack of productive assets that can be utilized to support their livelihood, insufficient technical and entrepreneur skills, limited employment opportunities and gaps in financial services to support entrepreneurship. As a result most of the IDPs have limited livelihoods options and inadequate access to and consumption of quality food stuffs. Displacements disrupted farming which is the main source of livelihoods leading to poor harvests. Women and girls are particularly vulnerable as they often do not have enough skills required for employment. Youth women are more likely to be among the working poor, at higher risk of unemployment, underemployment, or work.GAH will targeting HHs per each locality (Adila1100-Abujabra825-Bahar alarab 600-Alferdous 500)at East Darfur,(Belil 450-Alsalam 750-Dimsu 750) in South Darfur Also in White Nile(Alsalam1000-Aljableen 850) HHs In Sudan, assessed localities by the IPC in 2019 they are classified as food insecure at crisis and at emergency levels; these localities will be prioritized by GAH for intervention and targeted for assistance according to their humanitarian needs. Specific attention will be given to households with persons with specific needs, including older persons, female-headed, single women, children under five, pregnant and lactating women and people with disabilities. GAH FSL sector strategy will be tailored to integrate and compliment emergency response, recovery and resilience, building activities to save lives, protect and restore livelihoods and initiate longer-term solutions for all vulnerable communities and people living in protracted displacement. These interventions will facilitate achieving longer-term food stabilization, early recovery and create linkages to the Disaster Risk Reduction (DRR) and development programs within the targeted States targeted by Global Aid Hand Organization

2 - Activities / Outputs

GAH currently implementing FSL Project in Alredis1 WN State funded by UNDP, Also In Talodi, Dalami, Abu gubiha SK state funded By SHF, aims to source direct funds to cover other urgent areas to enhance the self-reliance and resilience of the most vulnerable communities to cope with current shocks and stresses. GAH will implement below activities; -provision of agricultural inputs (seeds and equipment.etc…).And services (trainings, community awareness session). Through cash modality, GAH will provide vouchers to the trained food processing trainees in order to access start up materials for their businesses from identified and authorized vendors in targeted localities. - Construction of stores for agricultural products. -Training on the importance of good nutrition - support to small-scale irrigated vegetable cultivation -Training on food processing, production of fuel-efficient stoves, etc. and support to enterprise development, specifically but not exclusively targeting vulnerable household. Building, restoring and maintaining community assets through food or cash for asset programs to crisis effected communities with food insecurity level, Building, restoring and maintaining productive assets, through provision of inputs and capacity building for climate-smart crop production, livestock and veterinary services to vulnerable small-scale farmers, Provision of income generating support and vocational trainings.

7.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will inform and interact with inter-agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including SEA and abuse complaints?

Through community meetings and consultations with community leaders of affected populations, needs of the affected population were identified and possible appropriate and relevant ways of addressing them proposed. Through community meetings bringing together community members, leaders of both Host Communities (HCs) and internal displaced persons (IDPs), government officials, and project implementation agencies, selection criteria will be agreed and verified for identification, selection and verification of beneficiaries. The final list of selected beneficiaries will be verified by GAH, Ministry of Agriculture, SHF/OCHA and other actors and representatives of the affected population through community meetings, to enhance transparency and promote harmony in the community. The set of criteria that will be used by the selection committees will include: HHs with cases of severe livelihood vulnerability; families/households with members who are disability or old age, Female Headed Households (FHH); HHs with under five children.Gender ratio for all activities involving direct beneficiaries. After selection, beneficiary verification will be undertaken through random HH visits to establish the validity of the selected beneficiaries against the selection criteria. Besides the establishment Complaints and Feedback Mechanisms (CFMs) in the target villages, designated GAH staff mobile numbers will be provided to the target affected communities for complaints and feedback. The designated GAH staff members (non-project staff) will keep complaints logs, which will be reviewed weekly by GAH project staff, to address the raised complaints. Sensitive complaints will be forwarded to GAH manager, for further support through use of hot-line email addresses. All responses to complaints raised will be provided within 7 days. Post Distribution Monitoring for supplies distributed and trainings evaluation on trainings to beneficiaries will be conducted to establish how effective, efficient and relevant the support provided to those affected was and feedback shared with targeted populations through community meetings. Reports to be shared with Donor will include lists of complaints received and responses provided/actions taken.Implementation of project activities will mainstream gender and persons with special needs’ priorities and participation. Beneficiary selection of recipients of services and supplies, women, girls, boys, men, and people with disability (PWD) will be consulted and included in community committees overseeing project implementation, to ensure their safety, security, and privacy interests are considered. Trainings to be conducted in the project will provide specific inputs and will be devised to enhance women, girls, PWD and elderly capacities, while ensuring all groups are adequately represented in the groups of trainees.
