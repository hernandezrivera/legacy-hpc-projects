Project Summary

The intervention aims to respond to winterization and NFI needs for the most vulnerable population in NWS, through providing winterization assistance to 30,000 HH of camp residents, and 2,500 NFI Kits as replenishment or response to the new arrivals.

3 - Needs

The estimated number of people requiring NFI support has increased by five per cent to 5.7 million. With funding shortages currently at a historic low, the NFI sectors’ limited response in 2022 is further expected to exacerbate needs as 2023 unfolds. “HNO 2023”Among WoS figures, Dana sub-district is at a noticeable level of vulnerabilities and severity scale and highest level of PIN for NFI, with 346,142 PIN at 3rd level of severity scale. the unstable security environment, and deterioration in economic conditions, compounded by a depreciating Syrian pound, are the main reasons that led to further intensification of the severity of the need for NFI support.According to SNFI cluster advocacy message for winter 2022-23, the winterization funding gap is currently 38.2 M USD, with current funding levels, cluster members won't be able to reach 1.1 million people in need.The most population who lives in camps, are either not heating their shelters, or burning the plastic and clothes for heating themselves and their families, those are the most affected population groups; among those, 28% are female, 44% are children, and 15% PWD.

4 - Implementation of activities

Global Communities will target the most vulnerable population in Dana subdistrict with Winterization assistance through voucher modality reaching 30,000 HH, prioritizing the population who lives in Atmeh camp, as GC is the main actor of SNFI activities there, meanwhile, responding to the critical need of camp residents in Dana SD with 2,500 NFI kits, as replenishment or to the new arrivals HHs.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

Providing CVA may arise risks such as putting vulnerable beneficiaries at risk of robbery or extorsion. Risk of cash and or voucher value diversion to non-humanitarian purposes. This can be mitigated through 1.Distribution methodology with clear protection risks analysed and mitigated. 2.Home delivered rather than at distribution location where beneficiaries could be targeted. 3.Verified beneficiary to prevent cash or voucher value being misused for other non-humanitarian purposes.


8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

In order to mitigate is a high risk of Abuse of Authority by actors (PSEA, GBV, discrimination, monetary coercion). This can be mitigated through 1. Ensuring that GC’s and its implementing partners’ staff have read and signed their Code of Conduct. 2. developing a range of measures and internal procedures to identify staff who are perpetrators of abuse and exploitation and act accordingly. 3. Ensuring active inclusion of complaint mechanism in place. Meanwhile, there is a high risk of Crowded living conditions and lack of lighting and gender segregated sanitation facilities can lead to increase in GBV and sexual exploitation as well as communicable diseases. This can be mitigated through 1. Advocate for provision of alternative accommodation/expansion of existing sites, 2. Work closely with protection and other sectors to understand the issues and take necessary actions together to address them, 3. Extending consultations such as on siting of facilities with all groups of population including women, girls and PWD.
