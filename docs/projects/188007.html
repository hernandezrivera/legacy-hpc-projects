Project Summary

The proposed project will implement as a kind of emergency response to the displaced people in Harim and Salqin camps for 8 months while the first month will be for contracting with vendors, bakeries, hiring field staff, and cars’ rental process. After that, the bread will be distributed for the rest of the project duration (7 months).
Through this project, MWL will be able to continue to support the camps that supported them in its previous project, which ended in April 2021, since those families have not been able to receive any food support. The proposed project will support 3,841 families (M:4033, W:4417, B:5186
, G:5569) including 384 PWDs with 6,933 bread packs per day in 25 camps for 7 months ( where total of loaves baked and distributed will be 1500000 loaves). The weight of the pack was determined based on the recommendations of FSL where the group recommends providing (200 g per person / day)
Families with 1-4 people will receive 1 pack per day
Families with 5-8 people will receive 2 packs per day.
Families with more than 9 people will receive three packs per day
MWL organization is planning to response to those families through free bread distribution, The process will depend on the following modality:
- Procurement of flour and yeast to be provided to the bakeries for making bread.
- Contracting with the baking supplier for baking the bread.
- Distributing the bread to the targeted beneficiaries in non-formal settlements in Dana and Salqin sub-districts in the following camps:

Free bread distribution will be targeting IDPs who are suffering from the poor economical situation due to the recent inflation and crisis of lack of breads in north Syria. The targeted IDPs will be selected from people who had left their communities as a result of the latest escalation of hostilities in northern Syria and are being settled in non-formal camps.
MWL is planned to have 600,000$ for funding this project since there is no secured fund until January 2022; however, MWL is conducting multiple advocacy campaigns and participating in various advocacy meetings to focus on the food security concerns (bread in specific).

4 - Needs

Based on FSL factsheet July 2021, there are 3.3 million people are in need of food security support in NWS while 2.1 million people have limited access to bread. Also REACH November 2021 (Humanitarian Situation Overview in Syria (HSOS) report clarified that 98% of assessed communities reported barriers to access sufficient food. Furthermore, the report of HNO 2021 mentioned that 85% of population living under the poverty line, and 69% below the extreme poverty line and 600,000 children under five suffer from chronic malnutrition. MWL field team conducted need assessment (August 2021) in Salqin and Harim camps in sub-districts through personal interviews and FGDs on voluntary basis. Out of findings, 95% said that the top priority in terms of food was bread, while 82% said that food baskets became as a second priority. In terms of vegetables and fruits, respondents said that it considered the third and fourth priority as 72% and 67% respectively. 54% of the camp residents in Salqin sub-district unable to secure food in most cases, while this percentage exceeds to reach 73% in Dana sub-district. 95% of the focused group participants said that they knew or heard about cases of malnutrition in their camps, and some cases in their families.100% of respondents said that the household income can't cover the cost of fruits and vegetables.

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

MWL remains committed towards mainstreaming protection through all of its projects using several practical mechanisms. Ensuring the integration and voice of the local community throughout every stage of the project is key in commitment to the Do No Harm principle. By conducting regular consultations, as well as establishing mechanisms to gather feedback and complaints, MWL will have the tools to encourage community ownership of the project. Protection Mainstreaming and GBV will be essential principles for MWL planned interventions during all stages of the project taking in consideration equal access, safety and dignity and ensuring accountability to affected newly IDP in temporary camps and host communities. The targeted beneficiaries will be selected based on defined vulnerability criteria to ensure that the needs of the most vulnerable (especially elderly, women, PWDs, pregnant women) had been covered.MWL will ensuring accountability toward the population by applying the different tools set by the M&E team and Compliant Mechanism and ensuring access the vulnerable people to services without any discrimination that is originally not allowed in the MWL Code of Conduct.


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

To protect beneficiaries and Women particularly from any exploitation, discrimination and SEA, posters will be published showing that all services are free of charge and MWL adhere to Zero-Tolerance principle, and they can complain directly through the complaints mechanism.
