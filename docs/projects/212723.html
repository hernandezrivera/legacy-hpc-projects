Project Summary

Number of Ben: SNA: 1000 (1000 PLW, 510 Girls, 490 Boys, 150 PWD) the disaggregation of their families will be (1650 girls, 1550 boys, 1185 women, 615 men) . PSFP: 4071 (1980 B, 2271G) the disaggregation of their families will be (6718 girls, 6310 boys, 4824 women, 2503 men)
According to HNO 2024 findings, almost 16.7 M people are in need in Syria, 5.7 M in need for nutrition assistance. this number witnessed significant increase in comparison to the previous year 15.3 M, this mainly related to the Feb 23 earthquake disaster and its consequences on the economic situation where Syrians are getting poorer as the SYP continue its fall which since the earthquake lost half of its value against USD.
TAS has designed this program aims to improve the access to preventive nutrition items of the most vulnerable IDPs and part of the host community CU5 and PLW in Idleb and Aleppo governorates,
Main interventions: First: Providing supplemental nutrition assistance for vulnerable people to enable the purchase of nutrient-dense foods for children ages 6–59 months and/or PLW to access select micronutrient-rich foods, via voucher modality. TAS will target 1000 individuals from the same targeted families or those who live in the same food assistance targeted locations, who have BLW and CU5. In addition, targeting the families that will be referred from the nutrition and protection clusters. The value of the additional top-up will be 35 USD per family for 8 rounds.
TAS will also contribute to preventing acute malnutrition and micronutrient deficiency among vulnerable children aged 6-23 months, as part of WFP BSFP program. This will involve providing lipid-based nutrient supplements to 4071 children from families receiving General Food Assistance over 10 months in Afrin, Sharan, Harim, Kafir Takharim subdistricts. TAS will contract with shops and e-voucher system. TAS will coordinate with nutrition and with other organizations who works in the same sector and field to reach the right beneficiaries and avoid any duplication. The project staff will include female and male members trained on the protection principles and gender equality.
We already secured 200,000 $ though BHA fund.
Implementation of activities: In-kind BSFP will be received from the donor. The distribution will be conducted in specified food distribution points which will be chosen away from the military centers with enough and safe distance and beneficiaries have easy access to them.
The main activities for SNA will be: Identifying and Verifying Beneficiaries:
as the targeted beneficiaries will be the families that have PLW and/or CU5. TAS will check the health books and health reports to make sure that the targeted families have PLW and/or CU5.
Selecting and Contracting with Vendors: TAS will contract company for both food and nutrition. But two wallets on the system will be created, one for food and the other for nutrition. TAS will contract markets to provide rich micronutrient food items.
Voucher Distribution: female members will be present and will distribute vouchers to women to ensure access and protection. The household must sign beside their names on the distribution list.
Community Outreach: The beneficiaries will receive also an electronic brochure including vendors' addresses, numbers, WhatsApp number for complaints and small descriptions about how to redeem the e-voucher. The beneficiaries will be provided with the specific date and time for voucher redemption. once the beneficiaries’ balances are loaded with the approved amount, the beneficiary will have the capability to redeem the available balance with the approved items from the contracted vendors, where the beneficiary will choose the items he/she needs from the identified list of items, where maximum amount of each item the beneficiary can buy, will be defined.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Redemption of Vouchers: During the redemption process, the project management team manages and monitors the activities of the beneficiaries and the vendors in real-time including beneficiaries’ balances, vendor balances, product activities and much more. Redeemed vouchers will be paid to vendors within 15 days of each phase.Social behavior change communication: “to promote adequate nutrition and health behaviors to contribute to the improvement of nutritional status of children and women, TAS will pilot the implementation of Social behavior change communication in the targeted communities by adopting the Care-group mothers approach. The Care group is composed of 12 women selected by the communities and TAS nutritionist based on their knowledge, willingness, availability, and capacity to disseminate nutrition and health knowledge and promote adequate practices at household and community level. they will be trained from TAS nutritionist according to nutrition cluster guideline to disseminate nutrition knowledge and to train the mothers to do screening for their family members (FMA). Each care group mother was responsible to disseminate messages to 10-12 beneficiary mothers and other influential household and community members. Main target groups will be Pregnant and Lactating Women, mothers of children under 2 years old, household level influencers such as husbands, grand-mothers, mothers in law and community leaders. Four topics were covered: Cholera prevention, Infant and Young Children Feeding (IYCF), Care and nutrition during pregnancy and Prevention of Diseases (diarrhea). Same messages will be disseminated through inter-personal communication and TAS social media, to leverage the effects and avoiding contradictions and confusion. TAS will hire an expertise mobile team to raise awareness of nutrition and to convey the messages to the targeted community. The team will consist of mainly female members as the primary audience will be women. TAS will use the WHO and nutrition cluster material for this purpose.Risk1: The lack of liquidity of the financial service providersMitigations: TAS has FWA with 3 different FSPs and TAS can transfer all the payments through PTT banks, which is more reliable and has a clear plan for money transfer.-Risk2: The fluctuation of the Syrian PoundsMitigations: TAS avoided the risk of Syrian Pounds’ Fluctuation through distributing the cash in USD, Risk3: Fake beneficiaries due to fake vouchers. Expecting that some beneficiaries will forge voucher to benefit from the project.Mitigations: The design of the e-voucher, which includes internal memory, which stores the data of the beneficiary, prevents any forging of the e-vouchers It's worth to mention that 1000 families has PLW and infant will be targeted with CVA supplemental nutrition assistance, and 4071 children will be targeted with WFP PSFP program
