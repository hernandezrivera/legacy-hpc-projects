Project Summary

During the early recovery phase from July 2019 to March 2020, WFP aims at assisting a total of 600,000 people through Food Assistance for Assets (FFA) schemes combined with unconditional food assistance for the most vulnerable groups who are unable to engage in light FFA schemes. The interventions in Cyclone Idai affected areas will be initiated in July 2019 targeting 400,000 people in Sofala and Manica provinces until October 2019. From November 2019 to March 2020, following the expected increases of the food needs during the lean season, WFP plan to increase the number of people assisted to reach 600,000 people. Depending on the food security situation, particularly prior to the lean season, the provision of unconditional food assistance to the most vulnerable communities can be envisaged, covering about 30% of the overall planned numbers. As part of the light FFA approach, food assistance is conditionally provided in exchange of labor for assets rehabilitation and constrution. In this phase WFP will be supporting low-risk, low-cost interventions that requires low technology, and the use of simple, well-defined and light work norms, without compromising the quality of the assets to be created. The assets include soil disalting and cleaning, acess roads rehabilititation, rehabilitation of water points for human and livestock production, rehabilitation of access roads to restablish access to agriculture fields, markets and other social services, rehabilitation of water sources to improve the availability of fresh water for people and livestock, as well as the rehabilitation and construction of houses, latrines or schools. In return to their participation in FFA schemes, households will receive nutritious food basket that will meet 75 percent of their food needs. In terms of modalities, assistance will be provided through a combination of in-kind and CBT modalities. Based on market and retailer assessments, it has been estimated that 30 percent of the targeted beneficiaries could be assisted through the CBT modality and the remaining 70 percent through the in-kind food assistance. While throughout the implementation period, WFP noted an additional need to augment retailers’ capacity, additional retailer assessments will be conducted to widen the retailers base to allow for CBT implementation where and when feasible. Early recovery-oriented FFA/FFT activities in the post Cyclone Idai context consist of supporting communities to re-establish their livelihoods. Early recovery activities are key in paving the way to resilience strengthening of communities who have lost their productive, community and household assets and are at risk of experiencing food insecurity and undernutrition. Based on the achievements made during the relief phase, WFP will therefore continue to support households’ transition from blanket assistance to more targeted needs-based assistance, contributing to foster recovery and resilience strengthening.
