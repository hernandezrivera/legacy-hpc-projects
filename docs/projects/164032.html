Project Summary

Building on UNIDO’s previous work in Syria and based on the undertaken assessment, this project aims to offer Syrian women and men vocational skills training in engineering fields. At least 2 training labs will be restored/upgraded and equipped in Damascus and Aleppo and 3 training modules will be developed in mechatronics fields of Industrial Automation and Information and communications technology (ICT) including but not limited to electrical, sensors and controllers laboratory, electrical systems and machines. At least 200 Syrian women and men from returnees and local communities will be offered training as part of the project.

4 - Needs

Since 2011, Syria’s prolonged crisis has taken an extensive toll on its population, caused massive destruction of physical infrastructure and led to high levels of emigration of qualified personnel. The industrial sector has also been severely affected by the loss in its infrastructure and human capital, undermining its potential to generate jobs and livelihood opportunities for the Syrian returnees and local communities. While a number of industrial establishments resumed their activity, accessibility to jobs is constrained by a lack of training opportunities since about two thirds of TVET centers and complexes are damaged or out of service. As a result, many Syrians are not able to access jobs, thus affecting their livelihoods and exacerbating poverty amongst returnees and local communities in Syria. The Syrian Economy relies heavily on low to medium tech machinery, factories and production lines, especially in the manufacturing and agriculture industries. Thus, providing vocational training in these areas will improve access to jobs and livelihoods in the short and medium term and maximise the impact of the project on local communities and returnees. Sources: Syrian Economic Sciences Society, 2017, Employment and Livelihood Supporting Syria, A Study conducted for UNDP - Syria AL Hasan N, 2018, Mapping of Availability, Variety and Contents of Vocational and Skills Training in Syria, ERL Sector Group, UNDPUNIDO, 2018, Training Assessment, Heavy Plant Operator Demolition and Recycling Machinery in Syria

5 - Implementation of activities

Since 2018, UNIDO has been supporting capacity building efforts in Syria through providing technical and vocational training for disadvantaged groups. Four labs have been rehabilitated at the Vocational Training Centers Complexes in Damascus and Aleppo and the Management Development Productivity Center and more than 200 individuals benefited from training. This project will start with an inception phase in order to finalize the location of labs to be restored and type of training to be offered. This will be followed by civil works and procurement process to equip the labs and develop the training modules, after which training will be offered to beneficiaries. Women will be explicitly encouraged to participate in the project. The training programmes will be designed with gender responsive safety measures in order to be attractive for women and will aim to advance gender equality through taking into consideration the difference in male and female needs, abilities, challenges and concerns. Based on security conditions and access, the project will also encourage the participation of Syrian trainers and trainees from different areas across the country.
