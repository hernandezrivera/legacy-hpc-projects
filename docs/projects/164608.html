Project Summary

Takaful Al-Sham Charity Organization will work through this project on expanding and establishing the targeted camp to raise the capacity to absorb more of IDPs, which keep coming because of the military actions in their villages and cities, to improve their living conditions and help them to cope with harsh conditions of the harsh displacement and winter. Through this intervention, Takaful obtained the HLP documents before starting the project, that proves the lands on which the camp will be established are public property, and then put the blueprints for the camp to include the lands designated for tents and green environmentally friendly areas and pathways, in addition to the other facilities that must be available according to the agreed criteria In CCCM in every camp, then providing clean water, water tanks, and latrines for theses beneficiaries with organic waste disposal and garbage deportation. This intervention will take place in a new location in Aleppo as mentioned on the first page. Total beneficiaries will be 1200 HHs (1428 women, 1295 men, 1647 girls, 1630 boys).
- Establishing, training the camp management committee and establishing the Camp Management system: Takaful Al-Sham will provide the established camps with all required resources such as office and its utilities, Takaful will select people from the displaced themselves to manage the camp, and they will be chosen based on their good reputation and qualifications that help them manage the camp well, Takaful Al-Sham will train them through specialized trainers on the matters They need. Takaful will follow up on the camps' management and provide close monitoring and technical support to make sure that they will be ready to take care of the camp and maintenance activities including reporting tools and respond during emergencies after closing the project. Then Establishing the Camp Management system, which includes the roles and responsibilities of the camp management committee in addition to how to cooperate and with camp residents and local actors in addition to the way to deal with beneficiaries’ data and how to maintain their confidentiality. With regard to the emergency plan, when designing the camp, consideration will be given to the fire breaks in it, in addition to setting fire points that contain fire extinguishers and will be distributed according to the standards followed in the cluster. Also, an integrated emergency plan will be prepared to evacuate the camp in case of need. The plan starts from allocating a special emergency gate in the camp In, to publishing Signboards showing the emergency exit and explaining to the beneficiaries in the camp the safety procedures and the evacuation plan in the event of an emergency, Awareness brochures will be printed, and local actors in the camp will be provided with awareness sessions to familiarize them with the plan of And emergency evacuation and safety procedures. - We will ensure that the principle of Do No Harm is applied in all project's activities through several visits to the market, distribution monitoring, and exit surveys. in the same time, the project will work on tracking and share IDP movements and analyses on displacement trends during the implementation of the project we have teams in different locations who are already trained on data collection and report the data to the main office and we share the reports and finding with cluster member and other actors to response for those IDPs. The estimated average of tracked individuals 100000. Takaful will contract with specialized service providers with a previous experience in service we will provide in all the project, in addition, the contract with the service provider will include all related responsibilities of the contractor including but not limited to; the hours of working, the kind and proprieties of the vehicle used, the exact location. In addition, Takaful will monitor the implementation of the vendor on a daily basis.

4 - Needs

The hostilities in northwest Syria continue to impact heavily on civilians who have endured nine years of crisis. The humanitarian situation, particularly in the Idleb de-escalation area, continues to deteriorate as a result of the hostilities that escalated in late 2019. The number of IDPs from 1 December 2019 to 2 February 2020, by CCCM, almost 586000 people were displaced in northwest Syria, mostly from southern parts of Idleb area, 80 percent of whom are women and children. Many fled their homes within the space of hours without any belongings while others managed to take with them what they needed to survive, believing that they would not be coming back. The majority of those IDPs have stayed within the Idleb area, moving to either urban centers such as Idleb city or Ariha or to areas that already have significant IDP populations such as Dana, Maaret Tamsrin and Atareb according to the situation report from OCHA. - CCCM has tracked another wave of displacements from 15th January to 19th January majorly from the west of Aleppo to the north-west of Aleppo and to the north-west of Idleb. 38,295 IDP movements have been tracked 28% of them were displaced to Atareb; 16% of them are displaced to Dana sub-districts - However, the rapid displacement, as well as the previously existing needs on the ground, means more resources are needed to support all those in need in northern Syria. The current crisis compounds an already dire humanitarian situation in northwest Syria. The main needs of the most recently displaced individuals, as reported by the CCCM, are non-food items, shelter, food and cash assistance. Currently, almost half of those newly displaced are living with host families and rented houses while 32 percent are living in camps or individual tents. Takaful Al-Sham tracked 53000 HHs between 15 Dec 2019 and 7 Feb 2020 in the Sub-district of Muhambal, Ariha, Jisr al-Shughur, Maaret Tamsrin, Salqin, Darkush, Armanaz, Harim, Dana, Atareb, Darat Azzah, Qourqeena.
