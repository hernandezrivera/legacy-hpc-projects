Project Summary

The project to rehabilitate drinking water and other uses in the villages of the Damascus countryside is a project of great importance to the region, as it aims to:
- Improving the quality and quantity of water available to the rural population in the Damascus countryside, which suffers from a severe shortage of clean and potable water due to war, displacement, and sanctions.
- Achieving several sustainable development goals, such as Goal No. 6 (ensuring the availability of water, its sustainable management, and sanitation for all), Goal No. 3 (ensuring healthy lives and promoting well-being for all at all ages), and Goal No. 13 (taking urgent action to combat climate change and its impacts). ).
- Achieving economic, social, and environmental benefits for rural communities, such as increasing income and employment, improving health and nutrition, reducing waterborne diseases, enhancing community participation and accountability, protecting natural resources and biodiversity, and mitigating the effects of climate change.
