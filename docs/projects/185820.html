Project Summary

***TOTAL WFP PROJECT BUDGET IS USD 201,680,000, AND THE APPEALING AMOUNT OF 170,000,000 REFLECTS THE BUDGET GAP***
WFP will provide life-saving food assistance to the entire Rohingya refugee population (approximately 900,000 people) through General Food Assistance. Beneficiaries will receive entitlements that can be redeemed from WFP-contracted local retailers across the camps. E-vouchers provide more flexibility, dignity and greater dietary diversity for the refugees while also creating market linkages with the host community and the wider Bangladeshi economy. To ensure an effective and efficient emergency intervention, WFP will preposition enough in-kind food to rapidly respond to the needs of approximately 30,000 households should they be displaced by monsoon, cyclone or conflict.
In addition to providing large-scale food assistance, WFP makes sure to address the needs of the most vulnerable households through promoting income generating activities in the camps and host communities. WFP will provide complementary food vouchers to 250,000 extremely vulnerable individuals through Fresh Food Corners. WFP will also engage 37,700 Rohingya refugee households in training and provide inputs for camps-based needs such as homestead vegetable production, community aquaculture, recycling project amongst other activities. In cooperation with the Government of Bangladesh, WFP will support 29,100 host community households with agricultural and non-agricultural livelihoods activities, including 10,397 women (head of households) with monthly subsistence allowance and business development, capacity strengthening and follow-up support to ex-beneficiaries through market linkage activities in Ukhiya and Teknaf and 18,703 women (head of households) with capacity strengthening, business support and market linkage opportunities.
