Project Summary

"(Rohingya Refugee Camp)
This project will be implement in Teknaf Rohingya Refugee Camps to enhance self-reliance through training of agricultural techniques, this project aims to Rohingya Refugee learn agricultural techniques suitable for the camp environment and harvest vegetables to build resilience to shocks. Agriculture advisors will support their skill development through field guidance based on their needs.
*Project will be given to priority following; households headed by women or children, elderly, single parents, sick or disabled people who need care, pregnant women, and lactating women
(Host community)
This project aims to improve livelihood for women of vulnerable households through skill development of agriculture as to ensure people are able to sell their agriculture products at Nilla public market. Provide on farm training of vegetable and fruits cultivation and poultry, and off-farm training of marketing and group management. Also to construct Nhilla Public Market corporate with Nhilla union to make market linkage and form advisory board to support management of Nilla public market.
* Project will be given to priority following; (1) Female head of household or children, head of household with many children, or female family member living together who is 15 years old or older, living alone, single parent, sick or disabled person who needs care, 2) Household income less than 5,500 Taka/month.
*Protection mainstreaming/ Gender mainstreaming: Enhancing the quality and maximizing the impact of all assistance activities to enhance protection. Mainstreaming means addressing protection concerns at all levels of programming and obligation to do no harm.
*Accountability for affected people(AAP): IVY will provide accurate, timely and accessible information to affected people about its assistance. Information provided has to be clearly understandable by everyone, irrespective of their age, gender or other characteristics. Through community consultation, monitoring/evaluation/analysis and complaints/feedback process, affected people have a right to be actively involved in the decisions and also engaging affected people in programme decisions makes assistance more effective to improvement.
*Project will promote social cohesion and peaceful existence: Support for self-reliance and livelihood improvement leads to the creation of an economic foundation, which in turn leads to social stability and cohesion between camp and host community.
*In this project Mukti Cox's Bazar shall comply with international standard of safeguarding of children and PSEA to implement the project and manage the project personnel."
