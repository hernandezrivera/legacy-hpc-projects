Project Summary

This action has been designed to address the need for a more integrated comprehensive response across northern Syria while taking into account the unique needs across the country and population per HNO, HRP and cluster objectives SO1 So2 So3. The proposed action aims to build community capacity to contribute to achieve a greater level of resilience, mitigation protection risks and negative coping mechanism and participate in economic life for 16,483 HHs including (84,040 individuals), through three proposed value chains:
1- Supporting Olive farmers through proposed olive value chain including the following activities:
1.1 Provision olive farmers with services and inputs for 400 HHs.
1.2 Strengthening the capacity for farmers on IPM (integrated pests management) 400 Farmers
1.3 Infrastructure rehabilitation for two olive oil extracting mills.
1.4 Capacity building for mills staff on best practice management 20 individuals.
1.5 Provision of inputs for olive home-based production (40 group 200 women).
1.6 Supporting existing association with Bio-Climate station 650 HHs (250 wheat farmers and 400 olive farmers).
2- Support wheat farmers through wheat value chain including the following activities:
2.1 Provision wheat farmers with seeds, services and inputs 250 HHs
2.2 Strengthening the capacity for farmers on IPM (integrated pests management) 250 Farmers
2.3 Improving the purchasing power of 250 farmers through marketing wheat product
2.4 Infrastructure rehabilitation of one wheat mill through providing required equipment.
2.5 Utilizing bakery with required flour to sell bread in fixed prices 1778 HHs.
2.6 Empowering Market functionality through providing 160 ton of (semolina, zero flour) 5600 HHs
2.7 Supporting existing association with soil analysis device 650 HHs (250 wheat farmers and 400 olive farmers).
2.8 Provision of fodder for animal keepers through treating harvested hay with Urea 560 HHs.
3- Supporting livestock breeders through Livestock value chain including the following activities:
3.1 TOT Para vet 30 veterinary.
3.2 Establishment of mobile clinic 1000 cases
3.3 Vaccination campaign (Enterotoxaemia, Foot and mouth disease, Brucella) 6000 HHs
3.4 Raising awareness of transmitted disease through distributing IEC (information education communication) materials 6000 HHs
Provision of inputs for Dairy products (Kursha, cheese, yogurt) 15 group 75 women, 15 selling points.
3.5 Improving purchase power through marketing dairy products 1000 widows.
And the proposed three value chains connected to each other’s through provision of fodder for targeted animal keepers from wheat farmers as well as providing targeted women group with required olive and dairy products.
Also, in order to have friendly environment WATAN proposed alternative energy initiative through:
- Recycling olive by-products (Firewood) 500 HHs
- Establishment of Bio-Gas units imitative 15 HHs.
4.1 By distributing 2000 FB to the people as a general food baskets distribution, the dignity and the resilience of crisis-affected women, men, girls, and boys will be strengthened. The goal of the Project is that participating direct target groups and their households will experience improved medium-term food security, as more calories per day per person will be available as well as food with more and varied nutrients.
The Overall Objective of this project as a direct response to the WFP objectives based on the drafted strategic plan; End hunger [by ensuring access to food], Achieve food security and in alignment with the implementation of activities in the framework of WFP’s.

4 - Needs

Agriculture has also a vital role in providing raw materials for the food manufacturing sector of the food industry.NW Syria has been able to grow a wide variety of agricultural products due to the diversity in the climate of the country.Just development projects like Value chain projects can be able to rebuild all AG cycle and reparation losing of AG assets, irrigation canals, flour mills, olive mills, bakeries, silos, and livestock assets.The households of 200,000 breeders are suffering from the loss of productive assets (livestock) and damaged infrastructure (land, irrigation infrastructure, facilities, etc.), which is affecting both farmers and breeders.WATAN assessment methodology:Watan Field Researchers carried out KI interviews and FGD in Aleppo and Idleb governorate during the month of December 2020. Watan field researchers interviewed 91 community members in Idlib 58 and Aleppo 33.72% of participants reported that there is a serious issue of food in their locations. In Idleb it was higher, reaching 89%. Our field researchers asked more questions about the type of issues and found that: No Food No Market 53%, No income 40%, Loss of agriculture land 28%, no physical access to market 27%, loss of agriculture tools 15%. In addition.Only 33% of the participant also reported that the main source of food is subsistence production. 76% reported that the main source of food is from local markets and imported from other areas. 65% mentioned receiving food assistance from humanitarian organizations. The main source of income was reported as the following, 87% from agriculture, 30% from Agro-pastoralism,82% from small business/trading, and 88% from daily work.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

WATAN in all of its projects applies the do no harm principle through Pay attention to gender differences, Checking references in recruiting staff and volunteers and build the capacity of new personnel from the local and/or affected community, providing follow-up supervision and monitoring to ensure that interventions are implemented correctly, use local cultural practices to support local people, Use methods from outside the culture where it is appropriate to do so.Also, WATAN carrying out activities and using an approach aimed at facilitating individuals and communities to achieve respect for rights in safety and dignity through community-based protection approach. WATAN using GAM “ gender age marker” to tackle whether or not a humanitarian project is designed to effectively respond to the different needs of men, women, girls, and boys within the affected population. Also and in order to target people with special needs, WATAN always includes at less 5% of them in the BNFs list and targeting them through appropriate modalities.WATAN In its project separate between meals and females and people with special needs have special services such as chairs, water tanks. WATAN on regular basis conducting FGD with the local community.Also WATAN publishing PSEA material and inform BNFs and non-BNFs about their rights and have complaint mechanism in place as well as building the local capacity in terms of protection issues through conducting appropriate training such as PSEA As for WATAN’s staff, all of its staff once hired WATAN to conduct a PSEA and safe gardening as well as code of conduct and humanitarian principles training. Also, WATAN an active member in the protection cluster and always applying a referral mechanism.

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

- Overall security risk:Monitoring of the overall security situation will be done through information received from specialized UN agencies international organizations and international NGOs. WATAN will keep close contact with its implementing partners to ensure the feasibility of the project but more important to ensure the safety of the field staff in areas targeted by the action. WATAN will maintain daily contact with local councils of the beneficiary communities to ensure instant communication of any change in the local security situation and prompt reaction.- Environmental contamination and hazards to humans by the LS & AG Activities:To prevent exposure of the local population to left-over injection needles and used vaccine, medicine flasks, and some AG inputs, all hazardous waste is collected by the vaccination teams and destroyed in incinerators.- COVID-19 risk:During the implementation of activities, all protective measures are applied to avoid the spread of COVID-19 infection during vaccination campaigns and training programs (such as avoid crowding, social distancing at least 2 meters, providing masks and gloves for all persons involved in project activities including BNFs, field staff, drivers, trainees, trainers, daily workers). IPs will ensure that all sterilization materials (Alcohol 90 % and Soap) are available during the implementation. In the event of any COVID-19 cases amongst field team members, operations by the affected team are halted immediately and the entire team undergoes a 14 days quarantine before resuming field activities.
