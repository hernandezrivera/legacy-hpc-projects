Project Summary

According to FSNAU post GU 2021 report nearly 3.5 million people in Somalia are expected to face food insecurity or the depletion of livelihood assets indicative of Crisis (IPC Phase 3) by the end of 2021, with nearly 1.2 million children under the age of five likely to be acutely malnourished in the absence of humanitarian assistance. Belet Hawa district of Gedo region district of Lower Juba region, like other parts of Somalia, are plagued by malnutrition and a high level of food insecurity driven by the late start of Gu rainfall season with an early end and uneven rainfall distribution resulting in lower-than-average Gu crop production. Furthermore, floods in Juba's riverine areas resulted in more population displacement and damage to crops and farmland. Moreover, the median prevalence of Global Acute Malnutrition (GAM) has remained serious (11.1%) weight-for-height z-score due to high mobility, low Vitamin A supplementation, low immunization coupled with reduced food security, and access to milk.
Across all population groups, the most vulnerable include households with a significant proportion of
persons with disabilities or medical conditions, children, older persons, and pregnant and lactating women. As families lose their socio-economic safety net and the capacity to cope with shocks, these vulnerabilities
are further increased if those members are the sole household head.
HAPEN will target the vulnerable households in Gedo with emergency livelihood support. The project will seek to provide access to cash through conditional and unconditional transfer for a period of 4 months to improve purchasing power and access to basic needs of the vulnerable households. The project will identify the households that owing to their vulnerability, cannot be engaged in physical works to benefit from cash
relief. These include the disabled, the elderly, pregnant women and lactating women. The rest of the households that have able-bodied members to engage in value adding communal activities will benefit from conditional transfers where they will be engaged in cash for work activities, with the objective of providing short term income support while improving communal assets. Communal infrastructure that will support livelihood recovery and resilience building in the medium and long run will be identified with the help of the local communities for rehabilitation. The cash for both unconditional and conditional cash support will be delivered through the voucher systems. Community-based targeting will be conducted and eligible households identified and registered. The specific criteria for selection of beneficiaries will be developed and agreed on with community representatives but the guiding factor will be vulnerability.

1. - Projects activities

1. Unconditional and conditional cash transfers activities targeting the vulnerable households in Belet Hawa in Gedo for a period of 4 months targeting 50 and 300 households respectively. Community mobilization will be undertaken in the villages to share information about the project including the donor, the purpose, objectives, locations, selection criteria, entitlement to beneficiaries and role of community groups in the project.Beneficiary profiling and registration in the target locations in consultation with the community elders -The criteria for targeting the beneficiaries will ensure that the most vulnerable are not left out. Emphasis for unconditional transfers will be given to the old, disabled and women headed household. -For CFW activities, the communal infrastructures selected for rehabilitation will be discussed prior to selection. Households that are vulnerable but are in a position to work will be selected in cash for work and those households that are vulnerable and unable to engage in community work will benefit from unconditional cash. Household will be engaged on a weekly basis (6 days a week) and paid at the end of each week. -The transfer amount will be established based on the MEB at the time of implementation. It is expected that this values will range between 80-100% of the MEB. -HAPEN has implemented cash transfer activities before through the mobile money system in coordination with the Hawala. The use of mobile money transfer was more secure, effective and faster, however for thehouseholds that do not own a phone or will have no access to a phone, voucher system will be implemented. 2. Distribution of agriculture inputs including tools (a hoe, shovel and panga) and seeds (cow peas-10kgs, beans (10kgs) and maize (10kgs) per HH and training 1,200 farmer households in Belet Hawa. -This activity will mainly support the poor agropastoral households to aid them recover their livelihoods. The beneficiaries will receive a package of agricultural seeds and tools. These kits will be aligned to the recommended packages outlined in the guidance note on the provision of agricultural inputs in Somalia provided by the Food Security Cluster. Happen will identify traders who can supply the inputs to the villages. In order to increase accountability, vouchers will be used to redeem the inputs. Training onpost harvest and handling of grains will further be undertaken by extension officers. Monitoring of the activities will be frequently undertaken to determine theimpact of the cash transfers to the beneficiaries.

2.1.1 - What is the justification for using cash or vouchers instead of in kind?

Cash assistance will offer people in need choice and support individualempowerment, while increasing efficiencies, supporting local actors, andstimulating local economies. CVA is often the most effective way to help peopleget back on their feet and on their way to recovery after devastation occasionedby drought such as the current one. Using cash based programming will provideSOLO with the the ability to spend less per person in need on overhead costs,thus increasing the amount of people that can be helped by removing a numberof supply chain costs and inefficiencies, and reducing staff head counts giventhe ease to which cash can be disbursed.

2.1.2 - Explain how the project design considered market functionality?

Since cash based programming relies heavily on the functionality of markets in targeted areas, HAPEN will rely on its experience to ensure the market is not distorted. Updated market assessments data will be critical to help in making of critical decisions. The organization has staff with expertise in cash based programming to quickly determine the direction the market is taking and set upsystems.
