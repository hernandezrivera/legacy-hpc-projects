Project Summary

To improve nutrition by supplementing food shortages through cultivation of vegetables for self-feeding and fish cultivation in community pond throughout the year, income generation support for buying food (handicraft instruction), and nutrition/protection related training.

Objectives

Promote portable skills development opportunities for Rohingya and enhance the livelihoods and resilience of host communities.

Needs

After influx, food prices have increased but most households rely on market to purchase food. The coping mechanisums are such as relying on less preferred food, borrowing food and relyimg on help from friends or relatives, reducing portion size and number of meals.

Activities or outputs

Below both components is targeted for host community in Nhilla union ward #7, #8, #9. Component 1; Improve nutrition through home gardening and fish cultivation1)Construct community pond for home gardening and fish cultivation. (construct at 25 locations in above area)2)Training for home gardening, fish cultivation and nutrition related. (7 days training for home gardening, and another 7days training for fish cultivation)This component beneficiary will be residents who did not take home gardening training yet Component 2; Income generation through vocational training for youth1)Vocational training. For men, mobile reparing, motocycle reparing, engine pump reparing, home-level electric reparing. For women, tailoring, embroidery. (30 people for each training. Conduct 20days-long training)2)Protection related training(in each 1 week, conduct half day training to all attendances)
