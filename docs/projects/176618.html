1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

ADRA Mozambique will drill boreholes for new resettlement areas in Metuge in Ntocota and the program will target at least 5,000 beneficiaries and accompanied by Hygiene Promotion, distribution of hygiene products and sanitation services.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Metuge District – Ntocota Ressettlement area.Activity 1: Distribution of Hygiene kits5000 hygiene kits distributed to 5000 households. The hygiene kits distributed will take into account he need of different age groups and sex. ADRA will also adopt the kits that are recommended by the WASH Club. Activity 2: Hygiene PromotionThis activity goes hand in hand with the distribution of hygiene kits. ADRA will provide a water purification solution (Certeza) with buckets for water collection and storage to affected households and construct latrines in new resettlement areas, as well as provide trainings in hygiene and health promotion. Due to the cholera outbreak, latrines, water purification and hygiene promotion messaging will be an integral part of the intervention. Activity 3: Borehole drilling10 boreholes constructed to serve 5000 households Activity 4: Latrine ConstructionADRA will provide material for the beneficiaries to make sure that the households have temporary latrines.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

Consultations will be done with affected populations (not only community leaders) to define needs and priorities. Updates on the project will similarly be shared with the affected populations through suggestion box and hotline mechanisms. Affected populations will be informed in advance of selection criteria, date/time, and process. All requests for responses to the Linha Verde mechanism will be responded to within 48 hours of receipt from the WASH Cluster.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Consultations will be done individuals with individuals living with disabilities and the elderly to identify specific adaptation needs for infrastructure. Infrastructure will be adapted for these needs.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

ADRA plans not to degrade the environment through deforestation, where there will be need for setting up the borehole or any sanitation structure. ADRA also planned to preserve the land and the Floresta by not over populate areas that do not have the capacity of accommodating many people for any distribution or open new spaces for distribution. ADRA will avoid this by using areas that already exist in the communities where many people could be accommodated as regular community meeting areas in each community.For all the activities or assets ADRA will carry out an assessment to find out if the activity does not contribute to the degradation of the environment, if it does then alternative ways are done.
