Project Summary

World Vision intends to support learners from infant to upper secondary (3-18 years) across Zimbabwe so that they are protected from COVID-19 infections and continue to learn during this crisis. Currently, there are challenges for learners, teachers and school communities in accessing reliable information about the COVID-19. Furthermore, there is a lack of guidance/support to teachers on alternative education options. Generally, there are many “unknowns” and uncertainties about the COVID-19 that has triggered psychosocial stress and anxiety that negatively affect learners, teachers and school communities. Since the early closure of the schools in March 2020, learners haven’t been receiving learning support from teachers and the challenge is that it’s not yet clear when normal business will resume due to the increase number of cases country-wide (currently at 567).
In order to mitigate the impact of COVID-19 on learners, teachers and school communities, the project will establish a communication system to enhance flow of accurate information on COVID-19 in 5,545 schools. The system will utilize existing communication structures to link education authorities, schools and school communities. The targeted 3.500,000 learners will receive printed COVID-19 IEC prevention materials with up-to-date COVID-19 related information and referral to schools and alternative learning solutions factoring in the language and visual impairments. In the same school communities, WVZ will facilitate the establishment/ strengthening of case management and referral system between education authorities, schools and school communities and health and other social services in the case of COVID-19 case identification. The project will also partner with mobile communication companies to disseminate COVID-19 SMS/text messages. The existing MoPSE information channels such as the website and social media pages will be used to provide information and guidance on COVID-19. Overall, the project will reach out to 2,500 schools and 1,500,000 million learners.
