Project Summary

The clashes between the Sudanese Armed Forces (SAF) and the Rapid Support Forces (RSF) have now lasted for over eight week. The fighting reportedly intensified since the expiry of the ceasefire agreement on 3 June, with Darfur and Khartoum mostly affected, and again after a 24-hour countrywide ceasefire on 10 June that saw a brief lull in the fighting between SAF and RSF. At least 90,000 people have reportedly been killed and over 100,000 injured across the country since the onset of the conflict on 15 April, according to the Federal Ministry of Health (FMoH) as of 13 December. Nearly 5,539,883 individuals have been fled to safer locations inside and outside the country since the conflict began on 15 April. Over 1,102,332 households are internally displaced within Sudan, according to the International Organization for Migration’s Displacement Tracking Matrix (DTM)

Water:
The project will include provision of access to safe drinking water by Rehabilitation of 20 hand
pump SAO will formulate water management
committees that will be trained on operation and maintenance, the committees will be
formulated from all categories of the communities including IDPs, Host
communities and IDPs, representatives (50% of women will be included in the
committees), farmers and Nomads will be included in the committees in
order to strengthen the sustainability of the project. Regular water monitoring will be
conducted as well as water chlorination services.
Hygiene.
SAO will provide hygiene kits full packages to the most vulnerable IDPs , SAO will also conduct hygiene sessions in the 2 targeted Areas
to target community leaders and workers , this will include PTAs committees, mobilization
of hygiene campaigns exercises at community level , public areas such as markets , and
schools , this will be mainly targeting school boys and girls to show examples after
awareness , primary health centers will be targeted and covered to ensure safety of people
in Needs , People Living With Disabilities will put under consideration in the distribution of
hygiene kits

Sanitation.
SAO will implement this project in full WASH component not forgetting sanitation, SAO
will construction emergency household latrines to at least 100 most vulnerable households
including IDPs, , SAO will train the communities on the standard of SLTS
this will involve provision of training and materials such as slabs, plastic sheets for
fencing , and angle bars , the community beneficiaries will dig the latrines and construct
under supervision of SAO WASH program manager, at least one latrines with 4 units of public latrines will
be order to avoid contamination and open deification around markets areas and public places

SAO monitoring and evaluation system involves all key stakeholders, field staff and program staff from head office to follow up on the progress of activities and its contribution towards project results and figure out if there is/are diversion from planned project activities or not. M&E reports always put project progress on the right path.
