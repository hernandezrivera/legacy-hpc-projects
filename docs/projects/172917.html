Project Summary

WHH remains a key strategic partner in providing and supporting humanitarian assistance and response here in Zimbabwe and the world over. During this HRP for 2021 WHH will aim to ensure that WASH humanitarian challenges are responded to within the shortest possible time by ensuring that basic WASH needs are restored and affected communities are supported with life saving interventions. The general target population remains all affected people in Zimbabwe with special emphasis on the vulnerable or disadvantaged population groups as these are seriously affected most when diasters or emergencies occur. Support will be rendered to communities in rural, peri urban and urban areas. Whilst being Provincial Focal Agency (PFA) for Mashonaland Central province, WHH will also maintain its availability to assist all affected communities in the whole country. Engagement with districts and provincial teams will be ensured before, during and after project implementation. Project will be implemented with the stakeholders spearheading and determining appropriate priority areas for intervention. Coordination and feedback mechanisms will be instituted to ensure transparent and effective implementation. All laid down standard humanitarian core values will be earnestly followed and WHH ensure all affected communities are assisted within 48 hrs of any disaster or emergency. WHH will use its available standby Emergency Project Cooperation Agreement with UNICEF to quickly harness needed resources to support affected communities.
During this period WHH will endeavour to improve access to water and sanitation services to those affected by humanitarian crisis. Since access to safe water of appropriate quantity remain a challenge in rural and urban areas of Zimbabwe. This is usually attributed to the adverse effects of the drought, floods, general economic fragility and disease outbreaks (Cholera, Typhoid and the current COVID-19 pandemic.) This project is therefore designed to provide Humanitarian response to save life to all at risk vulnerable communities in the affected areas.
The following are proposed interventions:
The project focuses on improving water and sanitation supplies, promoting safe hygiene practices, awareness raising on the prevention of COVID19 and promoting life saving interventions to communities affected by disaster and emergencies. Implementation will be through local/district stakeholders and WHH. Below listed are proposed activities to attain the proposed project objectives:
Provision of access to sufficient water of appropriate quality and quantity to 316,163 people to fulfil the basic needs through;
• Restoration, rehabilitation or expansion of pre-existing 400 water sources focusing more on Communities, Schools and Other institutions and on upgrading some to solar powered Piped Water Schemes (PWSs) for inclusivity
• Water quality monitoring timeously and sharing with communities.
• Equipping MOH&CC and Local Authority (LA) with water testing knowledge and necessary missing consumables and kits
• Reinforce effective feedback mechanisms for different types of abuse at all public water points.
• Stakeholder and community capacity building to maintain functional water points
• Training of Water Point Committees,
• O&M trainings for established PWS committees
• Facilitate cross learning for sustainable management of water points. VPM and Pump minders refresher trainings
• Installation of water harvesting infrastructure or any possible alternative options in areas with failing drilling exercises
Improving awareness on safe hygiene and sanitation practices to 316,163 people, through;
• Conducting gender and child sensitive hygiene promotion sessions through Sanitation Focused Participatory Health and Hygiene Education (SaFPHHE)
• Promote community engagement and participation in dissemination of key appropriate sanitation, health and hygiene promotion and COVID-19 prevention messages.

8 - Accountability to Affected People
8.1 - Describe how your project was designed in consultation with communities? How will you incorporate communities in project implementation and what feedback mechanisms do you have in place?

In coming up with this project proposal WHH did carry out stakeholder consultations where the respective LA, Government structures and the Communities were enaged to provide their views and plans on handling disasters and emergencies. The prioritized activities were therefore brought up by these consulted entities and hence these activities will resonante well with the community’s expectations. These stakeholder consultaions are an ongoing exercise conducted by WHH during various interactive platforms and opportunities in the targeted areas of operation and usully forms the basis of any proposed intervention. During project implementation, stakeholder involvement and consultation will continue to be interweaved within our approaches at all stages of the project. All Core Humanitarian standards and principles will be upheld ensuring the district stakeholders as well as community members understand the project package and contribute on how this can be improved and no discrimination of any kind will be intertained. Budgetary provisions will be shared through sensitization and review meetings and engagement platforms held Necessary Feedback mechanisms such as toll free lines, feedback meetings and suggestion boxes at all existing and newly established Water points and at most public places will be advocated and promoted to facilitate the reporting of all affected community members and to also provide possible platforms to enhance proper reporting of GBV, PSEA, FRAUD cases and to address the needs of different gender, age and diverse groups. Communities and stakeholders will be able to share their feedback and complaints about the quality and implementation of the project outputs and activities through a variety of methods including phone or SMS on +263 772414076, email to complaints.zimbabwe@welthungerhilfe.de and also directly to field staff and community representatives in the field. Feedback will be communicated with the participants, including the staff, and to all key stakeholders. The project will promote transparent Beneficiary selection and verification processes. Monitoring and Evaluation of strategies implemented will be done to improve on accountability and ensure transparent systems. .

9 - Environmental considerations
9.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

WHH has continued to implement humanitarian projects with a thorough analysis on the environment in which all these projects are done. It has promoted environmentally friendly and climate resilient interventions such as live fencing and stone walled durawalls as part of Water point headworks (fencing) to ensure the environment is protected from further degradation. During implementation, the project will seek to ensure that no environmental degradation is caused by our interventions. Waste management principles will be dealt with in liaison with local Authority and ensure that EMA principles are upheld. In the event of infrastructural repairs to be done, the project will ensure safe disposal of discarded objects. Assessment will be done prior to implementing activities that will disturb the ecosystem in the process.
