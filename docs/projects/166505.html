Objectives

WFP, as the lead agency for Logistics Sector, aims to oversee all the strategic objectives from the sector, namely: 1. Coordination: Provide logistics coordination mechanisms, support and advisory services to identify needs and gaps, and facilitate a collaborative approach amongst humanitarian partners to identify and resolve logistics constraints and bottlenecks. 2. Information Management: Maintain information management and communication platforms to compile and share updates on logistics capacities (sea/airport, transport, storage) and access constraints. 3. Common services: As a provider of last resort, implement timely and reliable temporary common storage, transport and camp access facilitation services, to address the Covid-19 response material supply surge.

Needs

"• Need for coordination between partners and management of logistics-related information to identify and resolve constraints through a collaborative effort, and to avoid duplication.• Strategically located COVID-19 response material storage space during the anticipated supply surge. • A system to navigate camp access for humanitarian partners under governement movement restrictions.

Activities or outputs

"The Logistics Sector strategy responds to the major constraints that currently limit operational capacity and impose significant risks on supply chain continuity, enabling timely and uninterrupted supply of life-saving aid to affected populations.Based on needs and gaps identified by the humanitarian community, the logistics cluster will provide:• Increased frequency of logistics coordination forums in partnership with the health sector during the scale up and initial response phases, aimed at raising and resolving constraints, encouraging partnerships, sharing expertise and resources, and avoiding duplication of effort. • A full logistics information management and communications platform will be maintained. Information products including maps, snapshots, situation updates, meeting minutes, planning tools, supplier information and capacity assessments will continue to be produced and shared through the sector Rohingya response dedicated web-page and mailing lists. A WhatsApp group will be implemented for daily informal communications between logistics functional staff in the field. Logistics capacities, maps and access constraints will be reported and shared through the interactive log.ie online mapping system.• Common storage hubs through WFP, specifically for Covid-19 materials will be set up strategically located outside of the camps, to increase availability of storage space, and improve reporting and accountability management of humanitarian goods.• A dedicated local sanitized truck fleet will be available upon partner request to move Covid materials for delivery to Isolation and treatment centres, and other response locations.• Maintaining available operational standby capacity for rapid deployment based upon identified operational risks, either through physical or virtual stocks of storage and support equipment that can be deployed in support of the humanitarian community. • Facilitation of vehicle movement into the camp area for the humanitarian community under government restrictions, through a service in support of the government to digitally validate and record approved vehicles (both personnel and cargo), to reduce congestion at entry points.
