Project Summary

Mercy-USA will distribute 400 food baskets monthly (68 kg. 1,776 Kcal/day for a family of 5) with monthly food baskets for the most vulnerable IDPs and host communities including female headed households, women and young girls, PLW, the elderly, and disabled in Ma'arrat An Nu'man sub-district in Idleb governorate and Atareb sub-district in Aleppo governorate including informal settlements.
Mercy-USA will target 2000 individuals every month for 5 months can be broken down ( Men 336- Women 406- Boys- 646- Girls 612)

Needs

Drawn from a countrywide household assessment, an estimated 6.5 million Syrians are food insecure and an additional 2.5 million people are at risk of food insecurity, Eight years into the crisis, the main drivers to food insecurity are prolonged displacements, loss and lack of livelihoods and reduced production capacity which in turn resulted in limited financial access to food, high prices and inflation contributing to reduced purchasing power and continuous depletion of livelihoods assets of the most vulnerable populations (FAS HRP 2019 Draft). Based on FSLA factsheets, in Al Ma’ra district Food Security Index (CARI) 69.5% are Marginally and 16.6% are Moderate, which can be expressed in different way for residency status IDP are 67.6% Marginally On the other hand, Arateb IDPs are 57% Marginally CARI. Moreover, as total Aleppo, female CARI 52.6% Marginally, 31.2 Moderate, and 2.3% severe, versus 49.2%, 21.1% and 5.9% for Male respectively. Based on the mentioned figures, there is clear need for general food assistance with targeting the most vulnerable groups such as female headed of households, young girls and children.

Implementation of activities

All components of this project will be implemented by Mercy-USA contracted field staff under the direct supervision of Mercy-USA management from the Turkey officeMercy-USA will attend all FSL Cluster meetings in Turkey, submit 4W's, along with regular information and updates about the project, and the region. Efforts will be made to coordinate with any humanitarian actors on the ground to ensure duplication is avoided. Field staff will coordinate with local councils to emphasize neutrality, and adherence to humanitarian principles while ensuring all stakeholders are informed in the area. When possible, temporary staff will be hired from the beneficiary community to maximize efficiency and impact while building local capacities.- Food Baskets will be purchased inside Turkey, and shipped cross border to the target area. - Food baskets will be delivered to designated distribution points in each targeted location in coordination with the local council in each area. - Beneficiaries will be informed of this location and Mercy-USA staff will ensure protection measures are taken to ensure equitable access particularly for women, and young girls (separate lines, distribution during daylight hours, household delivery, etc.)- ***Household distribution will take place for those vulnerable beneficiaries who cannot receive their allocation at the distribution point.- Distribution schedule will be determined during the vulnerability assessment process in order to reasonably target the most vulnerable households first.- The program coordinator will oversee the project, compile and submit reports, and coordinate with the donor to ensure full compliance.In the field:- The project manager will be responsible for hiring and maintaining field staff with the direct supervision and approval of Mercy-USA management in Turkey. The project manager will be responsible to oversee the implementation of the project in the field according to the workplan.- The field accountant will be responsible for the managing all financial affairs in the field.- The supply logistics officer will be responsible for coordination and oversight of all shipments from the supplier(s) of food baskets from the Turkish border to the appropriate warehouses inside Syria.- The logistics officer will coordinate incoming shipments with the supply logistics officer, and be responsible to coordinate with each of the distribution officers.- Each distribution officer will oversee 2 full time staff for distribution, delivery, data collection, loading and unloading.- MEAL officers will conduct assessments, gather photos, videos, and narrative reports, and will also act as a liaison with vulnerable population groups such as females and young girls.
