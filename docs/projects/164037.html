Project Summary

Proposed project is designed to provide winterization and life-saving services for newly displaced IDPs as response to recent crisis in Idleb that led to lake availability of suitable living places specially in random camps with the following activities :
1- Providing some basic infrastructure Emergency exit, Management office
2- Emergency Fire points
3- Establishing camp representative committees for IDPs based on services ( WASH committee , emergency fire committee .. ) and provide them with required trainings
Th project is planned to serve 3000 HHs of IDPs in a safe area taking into consideration nature of their camp and other factors including selected locations floods possibility in addition HLP issues to avoid any future problems for IDPs.

4 - Needs

According to HNO 2020 and latest CCCM tracking reports , during the first 2.5 months of 2020 more than 600k IDP movement were tracked in NW of syria due to last huge displacement from South and Eastren part of IDLEB, Their movement destination was to communities already over crowded with IDPs from previous displacements, their main needs were shelter , NFI , winterization , food and WASH services .According to HIHFAD field teams no available spaces in camps or communities were available and IDPs had to build some tents (some provided by NGOs and the rest were made by IDPs themselves ) in open grounds without any land preparation or insulation or even WASH infrastructure and facilities. With this way a lot of new established informal camps were built and number of IDPs living in them were doubled , during this winter many of these locations affected by floods or at least witnessed water leaking to inside the tents with weather temperature going below zero making IDPs suffering specially PWD , Women and children with less than 5% of them were covered with fuel and other heating materials , 95% of HHs were suffering and struggling in securing heating source for their families at night.

5 - Implementation of activities

As situation on the ground is escalating rapidly , camps to be targeted will be identified based on location assessment by the start of the project implementation to assess the needs in the area to select camps with highest needs in the area by MEAL team, all activities are being designed according to SPHERE standards and CCCM cluster recommendations.While assessing the needs procurement process and reallocating/hiring required staff for the project will be prepared , tasks to be assigned and technical study to be conducted by specialist engineers for selected camps to make sure no floods could happen in that location. After preparing all BOQs and technical a tender will be announced according to Procurement policy , contracts and service providers will be selected to start the implementation and provide us with required materials and supplies. Technical assessment for each tent will be conducted by project team in order to see if this tent can be insulated or needs to be replaced and depending on the results BNF lists will be prepared so contractors can start the work with close monitoring of our technical engineers and MEAL teams. Camp services' committees will be identified by our team by conducting FGD sessions and providing some awareness sessions before selecting volunteers from the camp to assign them tasks including fire fighting , WASH , and management committees . After each distribution or completion of service MEAL team will conduct PIM/PDM and results will be shared with project teams to make sure highest quality of implementing the project.
