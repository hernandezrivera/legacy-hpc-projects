Project Summary

This project aims to focus on the provision of emergency nutrition services for the prevention and treatment of acute malnutrition amongst vulnerable groups of people (including children, and women) escalated by the ongoing fighting in Sudan. This is aligned to the Nutrition Sector strategic objective 1(Lifesaving preventive and protection intervention focusing among under-two) and Sector objective 2(Treatment of Severe and moderate among under-fives and PLWs). Khartoum, North, West, South Darfur States. Our proposed treatment (both out-patient treatment and in-patient) of severe and moderate acute will target children 6-59 months and PLWs) in prioritized localities and women. The project will scale up access to quality integrated life-saving nutrition preventive and protection minimum package services; Improve access to quality lifesaving nutrition services to treat severe and moderate acute malnutrition among under-fives, pregnant and lactating women; and improve understanding of the nutrition situation in prioritized localities with critical levels of acute malnutrition through evidence generating assessments and inter-sectoral analysis to guide planning and decision making. The nutrition services provided will include nutritional assessment of children under 5 years, pregnant and Lactating Women, infant and young child feeding (IYCF) and other nutrition-related counseling, early years stimulation services for infants/young children, diet management and lifestyle modification for pregnant, lactating women, older people and patient with chronic diseases, distribution of IFA, Vitamin-A, deworming tablets in addition to SBCC activities for positive mindset change. Multi-sectoral nutrition-sensitive interventions (WASH, Health, Food Security, social protection, education, protection) shall be integrated and ensure synergy and collaboration with sector leads aimed at strengthening a holistic approach for addressing multi-sector dimensions affecting food, nutrition and health and social protection security and safety of children and women for improved nutrition outcomes among target population (Vulnerable Residents, IDPs, Refugees, Returnees mainly). The quality of and access to integrated nutrition services for at-risk groups will be improved by strengthening local capacity and linkages for the provision of quality nutrition services and supporting community-based nutrition services at scale to reach the last child in the last miles of this project. The project areas' outcome will be more equitable, accessible, and high-quality, high-impact nutrition services for most at-risk groups such as poor mothers and children.
Activities/Outputs
Under the current prevailing emergency context the HRP 2024 is designed to deliver three activities/outputs under the following four sector objectives;
Objective1: Life saving preventive and protection intervention focusing among under-two.
Objective 2: Treatment of Severe and moderate among under-fives and PLWs.
Objective 3: Broader preventive interventions (FBPM and e-BSFP)
Objective 4: Evidence generation, situation and response monitoring.

Cross-cutting issues including GBV, disability and AAP integrated with minimum multi-sectoral package in WASH, health, FSL and Protection to achieve the expected results under the emergency prention and curative nutrition intervention.
HDPO planned 1) to scale-up emergency preventive and curative nutrition services focusing among under-two; 2) provision of quality treatment services for SAM and MAM among under-fives and PLWs with special focus on maternal Infant and Young Child Feeding practices in emergencies (MIYCF) through strengthening SBC message dissemination; 3) Provision of blanket protective ration for under-fives and PLWs; 4) under evidence gathering through strengthening community based nutrition surveillance and capacity development as well as M&E to correctly collect, analyzed and report and disseminate right statistics and information

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

This nutrition project will ensure the application of the key principles of protection are incorporated in its enforcement and these are prioritizing safety and dignity and avoid causing harm, ensuring meaningful access, accountability, and participation and empowerment. This will be enforced through empowering the community leaders. It will ensure that protection mainstreaming is integrated at the operational level, the protection mainstreaming principles will become part of each phase of the project life-cycle: project development and design; project endorsement; project implementation and monitoring and project evaluation . E.g will ensure that the location of integrated nutrition facilities and access routes are free from threats and protection risks, and necessary safety measures to mitigate the risk of actual or potential threats are taken, Working hours of the facilities will, Project will ensure infrastructure facilities are inclusive for women, girls, person with disability and elderly; and inclusive latrines (separated by gender) so that all individuals and groups can access them in all safety and dignity. HDPO will consult with persons with disabilities and older people in the community to identify other types of adaptations needed. ▪ HDPO will ensure facilities are accessible to children and caregivers seeking support, consult with community-based child protection mechanisms and the child protection actors. Will consider time for beneficiaries to travel to the centers and return home, Community-based needs assessments, community action plans, and direct use of funds by the beneficiaries are the basis of the project design. Involvement of Government lining Ministry will be key like SMOH, SMoG and Social Welfare is key to facilitate workshops, give guidance and direction.Consultation of community leaders for inception and during implementation of the project and for sustainability. Referral pathways will be enshrined in the project especially the HDPO Community Based Protection and Child Protection networks(CBP(C)PNs working in synergy with the Community Nutrition Volunteers and Hygiene promoters
