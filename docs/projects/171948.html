Project Summary

The project aims providing timely emergency and sustainable WASH services to the most
vulnerable populations including IDPs, returnees and marginalized host community
members (women,men, boys and girls) including those with disabilities and the elderly in
Galgadud, Hiraan, Lower Shabelle, Middle Shabelle and Bay Regions, Somalia by
supporting their urgent as well as long term WASH needs that has been seriously affected
by combined impacts of Covid-19 pandemic, devastating 2019 Deyr and 2020 Gu
flooding, past successive drought seasons and intermittent conflicts has significantly
compromised existing limited WASH infrastructures and services and exposed the
vulnerable populations to dangers of outbreak of AWD/cholera and other sanitation and
hygiene related diseases. Of particular concern is anticipated La Niña conditions
associated with and high likelihood of consecutive below-average rainfall seasons, while
there is projected high risk that drought conditions will develop by early 2021 thus
exacerbating the already precarious situation. (FSNAU and FEWS NET report of May,
2020). The project targets a total of 32000 persons (13312 women, 12288 men, 3200 boys
and 3200 girls.

Mitigation Measures

1. Risk that beneficiaries are exposed to violence during registration/distribution as a result of the distance to or location of humanitarian assistance and service: Mitigation Measure : Enhancing security at the project sites by hiring security staff/ additional guards as well as ensuring that WASH services are located at place within WASH cluster sphere standards and that beneficiaries don't queue at water for more than 15minutes. This will limit risk associated with long distances by women and girls that are vulnerable to GBV incidences.2. Risk of women, girls and boys exposed to sexual violence, including sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA), when accessing humanitarian assistance. Mitigation Measure: Ensuring all staff sign PSEA code of conduct and adhering with it as well as conducting awareness on SEA and the reporting procedures that doesnt expose the victims to the perpetrators.3. Risk of no access to or awareness of protection assistance or referral services for survivors of protection incidents (i.e. GBV, child protection, evictions, explosive hazards, etc.)Mitigation measure: Conducting awareness on protection assistance for survivors of protection incidences4. Risk that marginalized groups, persons with disabilities, women, boys and girls, do not participate in consultations or decision-making processes for the project or are excluded from humanitarian services/assistance/activitiesMitigation measure: Conducting comprehensive awareness for persons with disabilities, women, boys and girls and ensuring their inclusion and participation in decision making processes as well as active participation on all project phases/stages right from initiation throughout implementation including mornitoring, till completion5. Risk that beneficiaries are not accessing/using complaint and feedback mechanismsMitigation measure: Establishing beneficiaries complain and feedback mechanisms and conducting awareness targeting the beneficiaries for utilization of the same
