Project Summary

The nutrition emergency response aims to contribute to reduction in the prevalence of Global Acute Malnutrition (GAM) in 6 districts, 3 in Manicaland Province namely Buhera, Chipinge rural and urban and Mutare rural districts and 3 in Masvingo Province namely Bikita, Gutu and Masvingo rural . The intervention will address six objectives that include providing leadership for a coordinated nutrition response, providing life-saving nutrition treatment of children affected by acute malnutrition, providing community level Infant and Young Child Feeding in Emergencies (IYCF-e) support to parents and caregivers of children below the age of two years, providing micronutrient supplements to children 6 to 59 months and pregnant women, improve decision making and effective nutrition response through strengthened data collection, reporting and information systems by at all levels and supporting distribution of essential nutrition commodities for prevention and treatment of acute malnutrition. Key indicators related to the six objectives will be tracked through Monitoring Evaluation, Accountability and Learning mechanisms in GOAL and Ministry of Health and Child Care's health information system. GOAL plan to prioritise key lifesaving actions whilst undertaking the necessary steps to better understand the evolving context, upon which to build supportive actions. In summary, the approach will include treatment for SAM & MAM in young children 6-59m through an expanded approach by supporting capacity building of health workers on treatment of acute malnutrition and supporting delivery of quality treatment services at health facility level. In addition, active screening for identification of acute malnutrition will be undertaken by community-based Village Health Workers (VHWs) following support for their capacity building on active screening. The VHWs then work with community leaders who will be engaged for social mobilisation of community to demand active screening and treatment services. Multiple micronutrient supplementation of high-risk groups, support for improved coordination at the district level and an IYCF-E training for health workers and VHWs will be supported. Review meetings at health facility and district level, to track progress and recommend key actions are an integral component of the response. Protection issues will be mainstreamed by ensuring that children identified with acute malnutrition at community and health facility level are further investigated for underlying protection issues through referral to Department of Social Services at district level and the Community Care Workers (CCWs) at community level and partners that are supporting protection issues directly in the districts of operation. In terms of Accountability to Affected Persons (AAP), GOAL will support the sensitisation of health workers on their role in AAP and support the proposal of mechanisms can be used at health facility level to ensure accountability. The Health Centre Committees (HCC) will be instrumental in handling the community issues. GOAL has a toll free line that will be shared at all levels for feedback to the organisation by affected persons.

1 - Activities

The project will support district level district level monthly cluster coordination meetings in all the six districts. The meetings will be attended all key partners and government departments implementing nutrition interventions to support emergency response in the six districts. Progress towards achievement of key cluster objectives and indicators will be discussed and key actions for the following reporting period agreed. GOAL will support training of health workers (nurses, Environmental Health Technicians and Ward Nutrition Coordinators) on Integrated Management of Acute Malnutrition (IMAM) and Vitamin A and Micronutrient Powders supplementation in the six districts. The trainings will be facilitated by a team from the Ministry of Health and Child Care's Nutrition Department. Protection issues will be discussed in the trainings in order to strengthen referral of cases on treatment for further investigation of underlying protection issues. The trainings will be provided as a TOT and the trained cadres in turn train Village Health Workers (VHWs) on Active Screening and Vitamin A supplementation and Micronutrient Powders. VHWs will receive training in IYCF-e as well. Following the trainings VHWs conduct active screening at community level in their villages and identified cases of SAM and MAM will be referred to health facilities for treatment. VHWs at community level will triage all children due to receive Vitamin A and Micronutrient Powders and give them their Vitamin A dose and distribute Micronutrient Powders to the caregivers. In addition, there will be a link between VHWs and support groups in delivery if IYCF-e messages and the messages will be shared during active screening as well. At health facility level, health workers offer treatment to SAM and MAM cases identified at community level and those identified at health facility level using the agreed guidelines and standards. All children identified at health facility as due for Vitamin A will be supplemented and caregivers of children due for Micronutrient Powders will receive them. GOAL will support the health workers with key data collection tools to strengthen collection of treatment data by reproducing Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM) and Moderate Acute Malnutrition (MAM) Registers which are used at health facility level. VHW will be supported with active screening data collection tools by reproducing tally sheet booklets, monthly return form booklets and referral slip booklets. In order to create demand for screening and treatment services as well as micronutrient supplementation, GOAL will support community dialogue meetings for community leaders who will in turn support VHWs in mobilising communities for active screening and ensure that referred cases receive treatment. Support visits will be conducted to health facilities to provide support to trained workers and check adherence to standards and guidelines so that identified malnutrition cases receive quality treatment services. At the end of each month, monthly review meetings at health facility level for VHWs and at district level for health workers will be conducted to monitor progress on achievement of key indicators and key actions for implementation in the following month will be agreed. Based on clinic requests distribution of essential nutrition commodities will be supported in each of the six districts. All affected persons will be provided with GOAL toll free line for feedback and will use the clinic level mechanism for providing feedback as well.
