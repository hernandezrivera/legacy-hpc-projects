Project Summary

The proposed project aims to respond to water, sanitation and hygiene needs of IDP and host communities in Bossaso, Caluula, Bandar Beyla, Garowe, Gaalkacyo, Goldogob, Baki, Borama, Lughaye, Zeylac, Hargeisa, Burco, Ceel-Afweyn , Ceerigaabo, Belet Weyne, Baidoa, Buur Hakabe, Xudur, Kismayo districts. The project will provide a holistic response to ensure affected families and their children have access to necessary WASH services to prevent water related disease outbreaks and address acute water shortage, sanitation and hygiene needs among disaster affected communities. The WASH activities are designed to be implemented at household and institution levels considering people with disabilities and elderly, safety of children as well as dignity and privacy for women and girls. The household level approach will target families affected by flood, drought and conflict. The health facilities level approach will contribute to improve access to quality healthcare and improve nutrition outcomes. The communities will have access to safe water supply through drilling of new strategic boreholes with hybrid power system, rehabilitation of boreholes, pipeline extensions and water trucking. Moreover, Save the children (SC) will ensure sex-segregated latrines at IDPs by rehabilitation/construction of improved shared household sanitation facilities with hand washing stations. At health facility level, SC will try to cover the main gaps through construction of VIP latrines, pipe line extensions including installing water storage tank, water trucking, regular water quality monitoring and waste management. Both approaches include hygiene promotion activities that will focus on community involvement in hygiene sessions, household visits and community awareness campaigns and provision of hygiene kits to the most vulnerable group and distribution of sanitation tools for waste management. Additionally, the project will build community resilience and increase sustainability of WASH services by enhancing the capacity of village level water and sanitation management committees to manage operate and maintain water and sanitation facilities.SC will ensure participation and active engagement with people with disabilities in all stages of the programme. The project will address barriers and risks faced by people with disability when accessing WASH services through consultations, organizing meeting in physically accessible venues and programme outputs are accessible to children and adults with disabilities, such as WASH infrastructure and distribution of hygiene kit distribution sites are accessible to all members of the community, including the most vulnerable groups. Moreover, Hygiene promoters will deliver key hygiene messages through various communication means to reach people with disability through Door-to-door visit. Total target beneficiaries are 116,000 of which 17, 403 (9572 female, 7831 male ) are people with disabilities. Save the Children has extensive experience targeting and seeking positive impacts for gender equality through its programming. Men, women, boys and girls will be involved in all phases of programming, during need assessment, implementation and monitoring of the projects. Women, men, boys, and girls will get equal opportunities to participate and give them special needs keeping the equity of the beneficiary targeting through monitoring and tracking data and ensuring gender segregation. In Addition to this, at the time of NFI distribution, site selection of the latrines consultation and decision-making was given equal opportunity and mostly giving opportunity to the vulnerable and marginalized groups of the community composed of children and women’s SC will continue to participate in coordination and assess the needs on the ground to ensure that the proposed activities continue to be appropriate to the changing needs and the specific factors that may increase women’s, girls’, individual risk.

Mitigation Measures

SC is committed to ensuring that beneficiaries are able to safely access services, without causing harm. In order to ensure this, and to promote meaningful access, accountability and participation of beneficiaries in the provision of WASH assistance, SC will follow the Somalia WASH cluster guidance on mainstreaming protection into this intervention. The project services will be accessible to avoid exposing the beneficiaries, particularly women, to any risks, Services will be provided at high quality standards and ensure dignity and rights of beneficiaries to WASH services. The hygiene promotion will consider the culture of the beneficiaries and respect their values and any language barriers. The project will ensure that the provided interventions are acceptable to the community and they take part in it through involvement of community committees and community volunteers who are acceptable to the community. The project WASH services will be provided to both IDPs and host communities; all will have access to the services without any discrimination. The project will give special attention to the special groups such as the disabled and elderly and make sure they are prioritized as appropriate. The WASH interventions are designed to protect children and women from risks related to access and use of the water and sanitation facilities. Latrines will be constructed in a way to ensure protection and safety of the users and minimize any risk of collapse. Each latrine will be separate blocks (one for men and one for women) and have lockable door to ensure privacy and protection of women using the facilities. The monitoring of the water quality will ensure protection of the target population form diseases and it will ensure that people, especially women, will access the nearest safe water sources without exposing them to any risks including GBV. Taps stand will be located within a radius of 500m to reduce distance and enhance security of women and girls who often collect water for the household, the water trucking activity, in addition to improving access to safe water, will reduce the additional burden on women and girls. Additionally, Save the Children will assess and ensure its supported service sites are within reasonable walking distances to avoid risk of gender based violence against women and girls. The hygiene promoters are from the same community and most of them women, which eases the communication with HHs, and are culturally acceptable. Home visits will be conducted in appropriate time to ensure privacy of the HHs. The volunteers will be trained to ensure that they have to ensure protection of the HHs, respect their privacy and do not expose them to any risk due to this intervention..

Please provide further explanation

The project design and implementation will be taken into account environmental protection to avoid over exploitation and contamination of the environment and sustainability, the constructed/rehabilitated latrine will used standard designs to avoid contamination of groundwater and surface water. The project is designed in such a way that while providing emergency lifesaving interventions it will also provide longer term sustainable solutions for affected population. To ensure the emergency interventions are linked to durable solutions, the project included construction, rehabilitation, upgrading of water supply systems and the use of solar energy that will reduce the operational cost of the water sources allowing access of water to vulnerable groups who are often closed out due to lack of purchasing power. This project will be linked to government and community systems by employing the Public Private Partnership (PPP) Schemes on water infrastructures. SC will ensure the sustainability of access to clean water at these rehabilitated water sources through training of operators and equip them with Operation and Maintenance (O&M) tool kits.
