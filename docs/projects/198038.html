Project Summary

The proposed project is a combination of the efforts of Choose Life Generation and community members who have been victims of the crisis plaguing the two Anglophone regions of Cameroon. So many people are displaced from our Bafut(about 13,000), more than 807 houses burnt with property and legal documents, close to 400 (0.05% of the population) people dead and many missing. Accessing civil status centers or institutions have met strong resistance from the non-state armed groupsand more the unavailability of the institutions workers who have relocated to relatively safer locations of the Diocese or out. About 90% of children born within these 5 warring years have been deprived of their right to owning birth certificates amongst other issues affecting the civilian that has caused a lot of division among indigenes, hardship, poverty, teenage pregnancies, Gender Based Violence, child labour,trafficking, child soldiers, child marriage, high crime wave and other social ills. The organisation deems it necessary to work with community members and ensure that they actively initiate positive attitudes towards the outlined social ills.
Women go through a lot of discrimination in traditional/cultural setups owing to the fact that they are women. The mourning period and the surrounding conditions are deteriorating as they morn while sitting on the floor and putting on one colour of dress for a year. They are not allowed to move freely in the villages and are they don’t have access to social life unlike the men who can do anything at any given time. There is great need to educate the minds of those who still hang on to this practice and ensure that the situation is improved at every cultural set up.
From appraisals, one of the purpose of the project is to instill the spirit of civil responsibility on the affected population and to ensure that the authorities bestowed with the power to protect and promote the rights of persons in communities take up their responsibility to that effect. Civil status registration has been put to test owning to the fact that children delivered within this crisis period have not been able to establish their birth certificates, those have attained the age of establishing national identity cards have little access to those services, victims who suffered from burning of houses lost their legal documents and till now don’t have access to establish them because of the timid functioning of the local councils. The legal department and the courts are not functional especially in Bafut, they behave been moved to Bamenda and the transport to Bamenda and back has been increased since the crisis started making it difficult for people to move to Bamenda for the purpose.
The non-respect of human rights has been a major concern as the military and the non-state armed group have continuously perpetrated acts of violence and abuse to the already victimized population. There is great need to educate the population on the importance of establishing civil documents, and to respect, protect, promote and fulfil human rights in every set up. It will also be important to monitor human rights activities, document and report cases of abuse and violation and to establish civil documents for vulnerable victims who don’t have access to do so. Since the population living in this part of the country are inclined to their tradition and culture, it will be of utmost importance to involve local and traditional authorities in the process of change.
Children who have not yet establish their birth certificates since 2016, persons who lost their property and legal documents because of the burnings, traditional and civil authorities and victims of the crisis are the main persons to target in this project.
