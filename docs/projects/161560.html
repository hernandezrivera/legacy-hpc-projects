Project Summary

Madagali, Michika, Mubi North, Mubi South and Maiha Local Government Areas of Northern Senatorial district of Adamawa State, Nigeria are the most hit LGAs in the State by the Boko Haram Insurgents. In October 2014, the deadly insurgents overrun the towns and villages of all the 5 LGAs, killing people at sight, burning houses and carting away properties including food items. The inhabitants of these towns and villages found themselves displaced, some in the Republic of Cameroun and Niger Republic while some were within the country. Sometimes in January 2015 when normalcy begun to take place people started returning back home starting a new life all over, People were faced with lack of good health, education, food and housing to mention but a few. Children including adolescents were the most hit with obvious manifestation of malnutrition, inability to have access to school, outbreak of diseases, lack of shelter and protection among others. These areas as a result of the activities of Boko Haram have high rate of out-of-school children because majority of the children have lost their parents, some are separated from their parents, neglected, abused and some are subjected to violence which as a result are placed in alternative care. The children including adolescents need to be given a nutritional support, medi -care likewise quality education in a conducive environment just as their counterparts in other places within the country. The cases of out-of-school children including the neglected once, abused and violently attacked children are common and spread across the 5 Local Government Areas which supposed to be protected and taken care of. Zireenza Support Foundation propose to select 798 affected children including adolescents in all of the 5 Local Government Areas based on the most vulnerable.
Zireenza support foundation intends to conduct profiling of all the children in the affected wards in which a local cluster will be mapped for easy coordination. The full implementation will make use of the profiled population and the clusters created for each ward of the LGA and an Advocacy plan will be developed with goals and objective that will be discussed to solicit community stake-holders involvement for sustainability. Case workers will be trained and use to provide case management services to unaccompanied and separated vulnerable persons affected boys and girls to provide care services for children affected by protection risk such as neglect, family separation, violence and abuse and provide services for those requiring alternative care likewise boys and girls with disability of any form and affected. Case management for those affected or face in one way or the other might be carried out using the developed referral pathways or linkages with other specialized service as palliative to the care givers or families like wise and transport support will be made in cash to cater for treatment to referred sites for services.

1 - Needs

Not only do people have the right to receive quality education now, they also have the right to be equipped with the skills and knowledge that will ensure long-term recognition of and respect for all human rights. Education is the primary vehicle by which economically and socially marginalized adults and children can lift themselves out of poverty and obtain the means to participate fully in their communities. The project will address numerous needs that children including adolescents are facing in the 5 Local Government Areas. Through these, children will be able to enroll in school with all the requisite requirements such as uniforms, books and writing materials and school fees. This will certainly come with reliefs to the children, caregivers and the communities at large. The children will be empowered and relevant to the society, this would check the issues of neglect, right to freedom from discrimination and abuse in the community. The issue of legal documentation will also be addressed where children will have right to say and be heard. When children of school age are out of school for whatever reason, criminality is being bred, the whole society may be thrown into chaos and therefore no body is safe. The project would address this and such aspect like exploitative labor and sexual abuses where mostly girl child fall prey.

2 - Activities

1.1 Conduct Need AssessmentPlanned community mapping to assess the needs will be held where all localities in the wards and district will be visited and management clusters will be formed for ease of coordination and a feedback mechanism will (feedback loop) design to coordinate activities from clusters with support from by Zireenza Support Foundation. To ensure that the most vulnerable out of school children including adolescents are be targeted.1.2 Advocacy plan will be developed to ensure the goals and objective of the project is discussed with stake holders, care providers, CBOs and other local government agencies and partner organizations.1.3 Detailed profiling of targets and their needs, location and form of contact will be carried out in all localities of the project.1.4 A focus group discussion will be held in each cluster to determine the size of the population their ages, sex, location and other information’s will be determine using available software.1.5 Organizing community sensitization in order to bring the project to the attention of the community. 1.6 Organizing awareness to community support and care for children affected and displace and victims of abuse.1.7 Provide nutritional palliative support to children affected livelihood and adolescents to be more motivated and perhaps even overcome the trauma caused by the conflicts. However, in doing so, it is important to bear in mind that proper Identification and selection of needy children including adolescents from the community requires transparency in order to avoid any creation of tensions as a result of discrimination. 1.8 The Target GroupThe target group is out of in school children including adolescents (OOSCs, ages 3-16 years) in the communities in Madagali, Michika, Mubi North, Mubi South and Maiha, Adamawa State, which are also affected by insurgency.

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

The project will ensure feedback loop to collate information and analyses information and respond that is collected from the most vulnerable out of school children and in-school including adolescents, test new primary constituent accountability mechanisms. In most cases, these mechanisms focus on collecting and using feedback from the key constituents of each organization. Feedback is an important element of improving accountability. We Choose feedback collection methods and channels that are appropriate for our primary constituents A good starting point is to decide on the method you will use to collect feedback (e.g. focus group discussions and key informant interviews,) For example, Zireenza Support Foundation we work with young girls and boys, adolescents. To collect feedback from children in an accessible, fun and creative manner, Zireenza Support Foundation uses a number of recreational activities. For example, they have adapted traditional songs and games into feedback collection exercises. They also use other didactic methodologies, like drawing, to make the feedback collection process accessible to their younger constituents. Playfulness has helped them to get active participation and valuable inputs from the children they work with. Figuring out the right mechanism and communications channel is “trial and error” until you find the most suitable approach for your specific context, we will Use simple language when explaining why and how we are collecting feedback in the design stage.
