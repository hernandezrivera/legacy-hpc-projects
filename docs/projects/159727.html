Project Summary

The post Gu 2019 nutrition assessment findings showed
marginal decrease in the median prevalence of Global Acute Malnutrition (GAM) of 13.8%
down from 14% of post Gu 2018 and 17.4% of post Gu 2017. The corresponding median
SAM prevalence are: 2.3% (Gu 2019), 2.2% (Gu 2018) and 3.2% (Gu 2017). The FSNAU report indicated a critical GAM (18.3%) and serious SAM (2.8%) prevalence were recorded in Gedo region while health services in Elwak and Garbaharey districts are facing stock out of emergency medical supplies.The proposed project will be implemented in Gedo region particular Elwak and Garbaharey districts where ARD is implementing health and WASH. ARD implemented health project in Elwak district in partnership with UNFPA, SHF and MOH since 2016 . the heath facilities have been functional through community contribution and ARD in kind of drugs and staff incentives. This has saved many vulnerable children and women affected by drought/AWD at target district. ARD will target a total 30,000 drought-affected people of whom 60% are women/girls with equity access to girls, women, boys and men through one health center and two mobile clinics. ARD will provide free outpatient consultations to 30,000 drought/AWD affected communities including 9000 girls, 9000 boys, 7500 women and 4500 men. Provision of emergency obstetric care including ANC, skilled delivery and PNC to 3000 eligible women and improve referral system through provision of transportation. The organization will conduct routine and supplementary immunization services to 20% of target population approximately 6000 children under the age of five years. In addition, the capacity of 35 staff will be improved through training while 350 community members will also be increased their knowledge and skills on disaster risk reduction, AWD prevention, identification of cases and early referral to health facilities. The organization is active health cluster member in Gedo and Mogadishu and will actively share information with health cluster members. The project will also be integrated into ongoing WASH project as integrated package of intervention. The target district is accessible, and the organization has active PCA with UNFPA in reducing maternal mortality rate through skilled deliveries and strengthening referral system in Somalia. There are reporting system that links to the EWAR system within the national surveillance system. ARD will report weekly/monthly IDSR /HMIS report for earlier detection of acute public health events, earlier and more effective response and reduce impact on health.

Project Activities

1. Provide free outpatient consultations to 30,000 drought/AWD affected people both IDPs and host communities ( including 7500 women, 9000 girls, 9000 boys and 4500 men) through support of one fixed health facility and two mobile clinics in Elwak district . The services to be offered include to treatment of pneumonia, malaria, diarrhea, measles, skin conditions, injury care in children and urinary tract infections, sexually transmitted infections.2. Provision of emergency obstetric care services including ANC, skilled delivery and PNC to 3,000 pregnant and lactating women women in Elwak and Garbaharey districts in Gedo region. In addition, community health worker will Identify, refer complicated deliveries to the health facility. ARD will provide standby transportation from IDPs to health facilities/hospitals and vice-versa in the effort to reduce maternal and child mortality rate in Somalia.3. Conduct routine and supplementary immunization to children under the age of five years in the target population. This is approximately 6000(3000boys and 3000girls) and estimated 3000 women of child bearing age in Elwak and Garabahrey districts. ARD will ensure coverage of 80-90%. There are reporting system that links to the EWAR system within the national surveillance system. ARD will report weekly/monthly IDSR /HMIS report for earlier detection of acute public health events, earlier and more effective response and reduce impact on health.4. Identify women focal points in the IDP settlements to lead the sensitization campaigns on the safe delivery and safe motherhood issues. These women will organize 10 community awareness and education sessions on available SRH services including family planning targeting 200 adolescent girls, 100 women and 50 men at target location. this will be conducted by mobile clinic.. ARD will also develop advocacy materials on safe mother hood.5. Provide training for 20 staff (10 male, 10 female) on Basic Emergency Obstetric Maternal and Neonatal Care for a period of 5 days6. Conduct refresher training for 15 community health workers on prevention and response of communicable diseases ,disease surveillance with emphasis on AWD, malaria, maternal health, child and maternal malnutrition.

Mitigation Measures

ARD has already connection with local community and currently working with community through community-based project where by community members provided a land and ARD established health facilities at target districts to providelife saving health care services including skilled delivery and other reproductive health services. ARD will apply humanitarian principles which includes accountability to affected population by ensuring that beneficiaries involved during assessment, design of the project and project implementation. ARD had discussion with community committee consisting 2 men and 2 women on project needs, design and implementation modalities to ensure accountability to affected population and ensure that quality services are provided to the community, moreover, the beneficiary feedback mechanism will be established to ensure that compliance and effective measures to be taken. During implementation of the project, ARD will organization a meeting with community members together with community committee to explain project objectives, activities, outcomes, target beneficiaries and how the community will actively participate project implementation e.g recruiting ,site monitoring and providing security.
