Project Summary

Due to the circumstances that Syria is going through, the crisis, the economic collapse, the high exchange rate, the lack of income sources that the population in Syria suffers from, and the low standard of living, most of the population has become below the poverty line. Food insecurity is a possible inevitable result, and the inability of the population to buy food. What is required for the human body to be normal, and in addition to supplements, all of this has become a burden on the family, especially those that have fallen below the poverty line. Thus, after the opinion polls conducted by the Nebras Association for Development and through questionnaires, the needs were identified within the Raqqa community. The community was primarily dependent on agriculture. Its sources of income . availability of the basic components, which are land, water, and manpower, and the lack of funding. Nebras Association designed a project for green houses and agricultural plots. Through this project, Nebras Association will achieve many of the goals set for it. Providing financing for the construction of green houses and agricultural tunnels projects
Creating job opportunities for pepole
Supplying vegetables and products from these homes to the local market can reduce prices because they are local production that does not require high transportation costs
Providing a source of income for all workers in the project, which provides money to create a decent life for these people
Save money from the price difference and use it for health care, education, and clothing for the entire community
Sustainability for farmers for long-term projects after their first production season
Thus, the project presented by the Nebras Development Association is one of the most important projects presented to the region.
In the event that any limitations occur, the project directs us to begin work according to the degree of identification and severity and develop an alternative plan directly in the event that we are unable to solve the problems and begin to alleviate the problem and move to alternative solutions.
Exampl: the inability to move from one market to another due to circumstances that make it impossible to secure materials and needs from within the local market

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Nebras Development Association will provide support to 50 farmers according to their project plan and the goals that will be achieved through the project plan. This support will preserve their dignity, ensure safe access to sources of livelihood, secure a decent life for them and their families, and provide the basic components for a decent human life. The project will be implemented according to a decreed plan and step-by-step stages:1- Conduct a questionnaire about the long list of beneficiaries and set criteria by which selection will be made2- Applying the criteria and selecting the short list of beneficiaries after applying the criteria3 - Conduct a field visit to the short list and ensure that the criteria are met and adhered to and develop the final list.4 - Confirming the list of beneficiaries and starting agricultural educational sessions5 - Preparing beneficiaries for the green homes distribution phase6 - Follow up on the work and continuity of the project and submit reports on the project stages7- Closing the projectNebras Development Association has a policy of protection and prevention against sexual exploitation, racial discrimination on social grounds, gender discrimination and equality. This is done by implementing its policy of not tolerating these matters and applying the maximum penalties to those who commit them. Nebras Development Association works within international standards to protect people’s lives and dignity, prevent exploitation or sexual blackmail of beneficiaries, exploit their needs by workers in the humanitarian sector, and prevent the exploitation of children and working children.Nebras Development Association is committed to the process of reporting cases that occur and providing complete reports on incidents encountered in the field of work or sites surrounding the work.Nebras Association has a plan to support these matters through awareness sessions, gender-based violence, sexual exploitation prevention, child exploitation prevention, and periodically to increase awareness and knowledge of their rights in order to preserve their dignity.
