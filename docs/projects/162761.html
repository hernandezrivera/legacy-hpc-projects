Project Summary

This project is aligning with cluster objectives that aiming to provide timely life-saving WASH assistance to crisis affected people to reduce mortality and morbidity, and to contribute to building resilience to recurrent shocks and improving vulnerable people’s access to basic WASH services, as well as enhancing the prevention and mitigation of protection risks and respond to protection needs, strengthen community resilience, and enhance risk mitigation measures. The project will target 92570 (23587 girls,21773 boys, 24077 women, and 23133 men) individuals in need living in 18 localities within 7 SCI priority states including Blue Nile, North Darfur, North Kordofan, South Kordofan, Centeral Darfur, West Darfur, and Red Sea states. The project will be focusing in provision of water supply at community and institutional level including construction, upgrading, improving, and rehabilitation of water sources, in addition to connection of water to schools, health facilities, and child friendly spaces. The project will contribute to elimination of open defecation within our operation areas through enhance the capacity of local communities and mobilizing targeted communities to construct their own latrines, this inline with the construction of emergency latrines during emergencies for people affected by crises. The project will also contribute to solid and medical management efforts through provision of garbage and collection tools, support disposal of solid wastes, construction of medical incinerators at health facilities to facilitate safe disposal of medical wastes. The project will focus on improving hygiene situation and status within the targeted communities to mitigate and reduce the risk of diseases outbreaks as a result of poor WASH services through general hygiene campaigns, awareness sessions, home visits as well as strengthening the capacity of local community committees such as hygiene promotors, conduct training and establishing hygiene clubs at schools and child friendly spaces. The project will create synergies ,complement, and integrate with Health, Nutrition and Food security within the targeted areas and worked with other sectors to address root causes of malnutrition and factors contributing to diseases outbreaks. The project will be implemented in 12 months with estimated budget at $2,314,268.

1 - Needs

As per Humanitarian Need Overview, WASH sector estimates that about 7.6 million people need water, sanitation and hygiene services. These people include IDPs, returnees, non-displaced and host communities who are vulnerable with limited access to WASH services. Out of 7.6 million people in need ,5.3 million lack access to improved water sources, 6.2 million people lack access to improved sanitation and 7.5 million people lack access to Hygiene services. South, North, Central Darfur’s, North and South Kordofan’s, Red Sea, Kassala and Gedaref have very acute as well as chronic WASH needs (severe, very high, high and medium on severity scale). Nile and Sennar state show acute WASH needs (severe and very high on severity scale). Blue Nile and East Darfur shows very acute as well as chronic WASH needs (severe, high and medium on severity scale). West Darfur and West Kordofan shows acute and chronic WASH needs (very high, high and medium). Whereas White Nile and Al Gezira shows acute as well chronic WASH needs (Very high and high on severity scale). 1.59 M people spread in 25 localities are in severe needs of WASH, 1.27 M spread in 37 localities are in very high need of WASH, 2.12 in 39 localities are in high need of WASH, remaining 2. 6 million in 89 localities are in medium to low need of WASH assistance. As per estimates by Joint Monitoring Report , 2019 about 40 per cent of the population do not have access to basic drinking water services, 63 per cent do not have access to basic sanitation, and 23 per cent do not have access to a handwashing facility with soap and water. Taken together just about a third have simultaneous access to water and sanitation in the country with wide disparities between states, between urban-rural and between the richest and poorest.Access to WASH in Institutions (schools and primary health centers) is also limited. Two thirds of the existing schools do not have improved sanitation facilities and a quarter of them do not have access to clean water , this is further compounded by the lack of gender segregated sanitation facilities including for menstrual hygiene management; and ease of access to the physically disabled children. This negatively impacts children’s attendance and enrollment, especially girls. According to HNO by September 2019, over 320, 000 people were directly affected of which 7,648 are. With thousands of houses, latrines, and public facilities destroyed or damaged, the most affected areas were, White Nile, Khartoum, Kassala, North Kordofan, and North Darfur. As a result, people are exposed to water-born diarrheal diseases. 39 percent of the Sudanese population have access to limited sanitation services as per S3MII.As a result of floods, heavy rains and poor sanitation and hygiene status in several parts of the country, the Federal Ministry of Health (FMOH) announced an outbreak of Cholera in Blue Nile state in 8th of September 2019, later cases were reported from Sinnar in 13th of December, Khartoum in 19th of October and Aljazeera in 7th of November and as per 23rd of November cholera cases were reported with 11 deaths (CFR3.3). Dengue fever outbreak was reported from Kassala, Red Sea, Gedaref, North, South, West and East Darfur, North Kordofan and Sennar states. According to WHO and as of 23rd of November 2872 dengue cases were reported with 9 deaths (CFR 0.3). Rift Valley Fever outbreak was reported as well from River Nile and Red Sea states in 28th of September. In 23rd of November WHO and FMoH reported 206 cases with 7 deaths (CFR 3.4). CHIKUNGUNYA cases were reported South, West and East Darfur, Kassala, Sennar and White Nile.

2 - Activities / Outputs

The project will respond to the basic and life-saving needs of IDPs, returnees and host communities while increasing their self-reliance. The planned WASH interventions under this project will include provision of safe drinking water for both households and institutional level through construction, upgrading and/or rehabilitation of water sources, water connections, and installation of water containers, and water quality monitoring including provision of water purifications. Also the project will focus on elimination of open defecation practices among communities through construction of emergency and permeant HHs and intuitions latrines, raising the awareness and build the capacity of local communities to promote hygiene practices and to be more capable to manage their facilities. The project will contribute to solid and medical waste management through support affected communities with appropriate tools and facilities as well as raising the awareness initiatives. Hygiene promotion will be more focusing on improving hygiene status and prevention of diseases outbreaks. The project will contribute to capacity building of WASH community structures through training and strengthening existing committees, and /or establishing new community committees such as hygiene promotion volunteers, water quality monitoring volunteers,WASH committees, and hygiene clubs at schools and child friendly spaces targegting children as an entry points to deliver key hygiene massages for their community and seblings.

5.1 - Impact on environment/natural resources

Environment will be considered as an integral component for this project, and a number of factors will be essential for improving the humanitarian response such as coordination with different actors at the communities, prioritization and awareness raising amongst practitioners, and effective policy frame work. The plan mitigation will through effectiveness of existing waste and medical disposal mechanism as preventive measure for disease caused by insufficient or unsafe waste disposal. Plan the location of emergency waste disposal sites with local authorities to avoid contamination of water sources, disease vectors and odors, latrines construction materials will be as much as possible environmental friendley such as using steel bars insted of bamboo wooden and sticks, as well as minimizing using red bricks in construction through encourching using cement blocks insted.

6.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

The project designed to consider protection issues and concerns especially for female. Latrines will be designed to ensure maximum protection, privacy and dignity for women, and will be located near to dewellins to mitigate exposure to SGBV incidents especially during nights, also the needs of people with disabilities and eldery will be considered during establishing water points and other community facilities such as communal latrines. The project will ensure adherance to child safe guarding,protection, and core humanitarian princibles during implmentation of the project to ensure all community members and especially children were protected, not harmed by our actions.

7.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will inform and interact with inter-agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including SEA and abuse complaints?

The preposition complaint and feedback mechanisms will be used to gauge the satisfaction level of beneficiaries against services provided by SC. Accountability phone is standing by to receive feedback and complaints from the project beneficiaries and stakeholders, for beneficiaries without phones they report to a community focal point. The call-in number will be printed on posters and displayed at service outlet centers to be easily access to callers when interested to deliver their feedback. The MEAL team alongside with program staff will conduct focus group discussions and community meetings to share project information with direct and indirect project beneficiaries and collect their views. All feedback and complaints received will be logged into the Complaints and Response system, analyzed and utilized for project management and planning. To ensure accountability to the affected community, SC will consult communities, government authorities and other relevant partners in the design of this project. The consultation with communities will involve both women and men as well as children. The accountability mechanism will provide opportunity to the affected population to raise their concerns and claim their rights. The project considers conflict sensitivity by understanding the situation, examining how each activity may influence the conflict and work to maximize the positive and minimize the negative impacts of the proposed interventions.
