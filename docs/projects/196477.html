Project Summary

Sadad aims through this program to support the WASH services in 8 communities in Idleb governorate, where the intervention plan will focus on immediate emergency life-saving WASH interventions and support the resilience of existing WASH systems (sewer networks, wastewater treatment, solid waste management) and WASH in institutions, such as schools and healthcare facilities, ensuring coordinated and cohesive response and provide comprehensive WASH services for affected people in northwest Syria living in host communities.
The intervention will focus on providing sustainable services in the long-term to the affected people in Idleb governorate in Sub-districts of Ariha and Ehsem, which scored as high severity (4). Sadad through this intervention will provide emergency WASH services for 46,700 individuals living in 8 communities 20% of them are IDPs and 16% are returnees. Sadad will provide full package WASH emergency services to provide rapid responses and strengthen the community resilience to adapt to living conditions in the affected communities.
The intervention plan will ensure safe access, availability to basic WASH services, and quality assurance based on WASH cluster guidelines, NWS Cholera task force recommendation and COVID-19 mitigation measures for the planned activities. The intervention will comprise the following components:
1- Rehabilitation/upgrade of 8 water pumping stations including equipping the stations with electrical and mechanical equipment and installing renewable energy systems to reduce the running costs benefitting 46,700 Individuals. In addition, support Water Association Units (WAUs) through providing monthly running costs for the stations (fuel, chlorine, engine oil, and incentives for workers) and promote a cost recovery system. As well as train the local communities and apply water safety plans.
2- Rehabilitation of sewage networks to connect the remote sites, public facilities (schools and health centers) in addition to replacing the damaged sewer lines benefiting 21,000 Individuals.
3- Rehabilitation of water networks to connect the remote sites, public facilities (schools and health centers) in addition to replacing the damaged water lines benefiting 21,000 Individuals.
4- Supporting the local operators and municipalities to continue the garbage collection and disposal of the waste from 8 communities (around 46,700 individuals), schools, health centers, and IDPs collective centers and sites. This support will cover providing fuel for garbage vehicles and maintaining/repair of the broken ones in addition to supporting workers’ incentives. Also, Sadad will identify and rehabilitate 3 dumpsites in the surrounding communities.
5- Conducting hygiene promotion sessions about the improvement of hygiene practices and behaviors for 16,600 individuals of the most vulnerable IDPs, returnees and host residents.
6- 16,600 hygiene kits (approx. 27,600 individuals) will be distributed to approx. 5530 households of the IDPs and host communities on three rounds for those who don’t have the capacity to secure hygiene materials.
7- provision of WASH services to 5 education centers and 5 health facilities.
Sadad will ensure support the innovative solutions as possible in its proposed interventions for dealing with the escalation of cholera cases especially during the rainy season, due to sewage systems in disrepair as well as poor sanitation conditions in frontline communities. Sadad also will make sure to support the innovative solution to deal with water drought issues through supporting solar-powered systems, wastewater treatment. In addition to applying environmental improvement projects through developing solid waste management treatment and elevating the bad impacts of neglecting SWM support and improve communities’ resilience to face the spread of cholera, which is a direct result of the deteriorating water situation in NWS.

3 - Needs

The situation in NWS remains grim, with 3.4 million persons in need of humanitarian aid. Out of the total 4.4 million persons residing in NWS, over 70% are considered people in acute need of life-saving assistance.Based on the key findings of the rapid needs assessment which Sadad has conducted in the Ariha and Ehsem Sub-districts, where one of the major issues is the limited access to safe and reliable water sources. Conflicts and displacement have damaged or destroyed water infrastructure, making it difficult for many people to access clean water. In addition, the over-extraction of groundwater resources and pollution of surface water sources has led to a decline in water availability and quality.Another major issue is the lack of proper sanitation and hygiene facilities, which has led to the spread of water-borne diseases, such as cholera and water diarrhea.Climate change also plays a role, as the region has been experiencing an increase in drought and water scarcity. This has led to increased competition for water resources and has made it more difficult for farmers to access the water they need to grow crops.To address these issues, there is a need for comprehensive water management and water infrastructure development. This includes rehabilitation of sewage/water networks, support 8 water pumping stations, establishment of the cost recovery system, support 8 municipalities to maintain garbage collection, hygiene promotion, and WASH services in healthcare facilities.

4 - Implementation of activities

The WASH response will reach 46,700 people, 36% IDPs & returnees. Sadad will provide comprehensive WASH services through implementing the activities directed by their teams in the field in Ariha and Ehsem SD, ranked as 4 WASH severity score, for Safety of Household Potable Water (HH FRC test result). The activities will be targeting 8 water stations and municipalities.●Rehabilitation of sewage/water networks, Sadad will connect the remote neighborhoods with sewage and water networks in addition to replacing the damaged parts.●Support 8 water pumping stations by providing/repairing electrical and mechanical equipment, equipping the boreholes, upgrading the power sources in the station by installing solar-powered systems. ●Establishment of the cost recovery system, through gradually supporting for the water pumping station through secure fuel, oil, chlorine and cover the workers' incentives.●Solid waste management; support 8 municipalities in Ariha and Ehsem Sub-districts to maintain garbage collection and removal from host communities, in addition, to rehabilitate landfills to ensure dispose of the garbage with no harm to the environment. ●Hygiene promotion; distribute 16,600 HKs for the most vulnerable people and implement hygiene promotion awareness sessions.●WASH services in healthcare facilities; support 8 WASH facilities and services in 8 health care facilities and nutrition clinics, including outbreaks dedicated facilities, in close coordination with Health sector.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

In accordance with the risk matrix shared by the WoS WASH sector, the WASH program will ensure a “Do No Harm” principle and has carried out a Protection Risk Analysis as part of its sector strategy development. Potential protection risks that may arise from the implementation of the WASH sector strategy and related activities, along with a description of the relevant mitigation measures and resources required for monitoring those risks have been identified. The risks might include inequality in accessing services during the provision of NFI items, work safety, gender-based violence (GBV) and exposure to explosive hazard. WASH partners will take adequate measures to minimize exposure of the most vulnerable groups (women, adolescent girls, children, people with disabilities, female-headed households) to greater protection risks, and particularly to GBV. WASH staff will be trained on key GBV concepts and referral pathways to properly support survivors. Women, adolescent girls and people with disabilities will be consulted during the design, implementation and monitoring phase of each project, with a need to put the necessary mechanisms in place to facilitate feedback from users.The organization will have zero tolerance for disclosure of child labor in contracts; check and register civil documents submitted by children under 18 years old.


8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Gender remains on the top of Sadad priorities and is highlighted as one of the key objectives outlined in Sadad’s strategy. Sadad adheres to all other international minimum standards, including the SPHERE, IASC, Gender-Based Violence (GBV), Child Protection minimum standards as well as other technical guidelines of the protection cluster and its sub-clusters. Sadad employs a gender policy and procedures to operationalize the application of the Strategic objective for Gender. Key to this is Sadad’s Gender Minimum Standards, which include requirements on 1) Collection of sex and age disaggregated data 2) Inclusion of gender indicators in the monitoring and evaluation plan 3) Inclusion of gender indicators in the employment plan. Consequently, all implemented and planned activities include gender disaggregation of all information collected. This relates to the design of activities addressing women, girls, boys, men, elders, and people with disabilities as well as those activities that entail the participation of community members.
