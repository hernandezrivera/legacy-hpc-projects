Project Summary

The protracted conflict and instability in Somalia continues to cause suffering across all regions in the country. In Gedo region it’s no different and the humanitarian situation is triggered by not only conflict but also drought, inflation and massive displacement that are eroding households’ coping capacities. A recent FWSNET and FSNAU FSNAU-FEWS NET September 2018 Post Gu report indicates that the following populations are considered hotspots and are in need of urgent nutrition and health support interventions. They have Critical prevalence of acute malnutrition (≥15% GAM) or 10.7 percent or more of children have Mid-Upper Arm Circumference (MUAC) below the 125 millimeters (mm) threshold, that is Rural Livelihoods: Northern Inland Pastoral of Northeast, Hawd Pastoral of Northeast, Northwest Guban Pastoral, North Gedo Riverine , Beletweyne District (Reverine & Agro-pastoral), South Gedo Agro-pastoral, South Gedo Riverine. The same areas have done issues with floods and this makes them prone to water and sanitation health issues. Bardhere is situated in the southern region of Gedo, therefore, the proposed emergency response is designed by URDO is to complement other ongoing efforts to avoid any retrogressing any achievements gained before. The program comprises of integrated life-saving health and nutrition interventions, coupled with safe water, sanitation, public health education, distribution of essential WASH NFIs and support to meet the immediate needs of both IDPs and host communities in Bardhere district. While the basic needs and services continue to be an issue in the project area, it is expected that an integrated Health, Nutrition and WASH program with a community-led approach will reduce the current morbidity and mortality risks faced by most vulnerable particularly boys and girls under five and pregnant and lactating mothers, the elderly, the disabled and the destitute, but also work towards improving the community’s capacity to a sustainable, resilient future.

Needs

Bardhere like many parts of Somalia has been majorly affected by the long standing conflict resulting and climatic changes that have had a debilitating effect on all social services infrastructure. Increasingly, gaps in the coverage of essential and life-saving health services are aggravated by inadequate access to safe water and sanitation, mass displacement and eroded livelihoods. FSNAU and FEWSNET September 2018 brief projects that between August and December 2018 an estimated 294,200 children will likely face acute malnutrition, including 55,200 who are likely to be severely malnourished. It is further estimated that of the 8,200,000 people in need, 5,368,274 people will be in need of humanitarian WASH services in 2018. The lack of essential WASH services means serious public health risks and surge of preventable deaths. This program shall promote the equitable participation of both men and women in the planning and decision making processes, this will ultimately help to ensure that the entire affected population has safe and easy access Health, Nutrition services as well as access to better water supply, sanitation facilities. Health and Nutrition posts in the operational areas are one of the cost effective and community owned approach that leads to better outcomes and enhances sustainability. The WASH intervention is meant to complement the Health and Nutrition interventions to further reduced water based and water washed diseases which increase the morbidity and mortality in the area. URDO intends to rehabilitate wells and boreholes and carry out a rigorous health promotion activities. URDO will work closely with the communities and establishing community committees e.g training water management committees to manage infrastructure; and organizing village health teams to model good sanitation and hygiene practices and have a well maintained CHW network who are well trained on Health Hygiene Nutrition Promotion (HHNP) and screening of malnutrition.

Activities

URDO will identify areas where it can find synergy and demonstrate Value for money in implementing the emergency Nutrition program in Bardhere district. All activities in line with the Health and Nutrition services will be provided under one roof. The medical and nutrition team will provide primary health care to children under five and pregnant and lactating mothers and will also carry out screening of malnutrition of the same target groups. URDO in meeting the above focus will recruit and train 20 health care workers (of which 50% are females and 50% males) in basic emergency obstetric care (BemOC), focused antenatal care (FANC) and Essential newborn care (ENC) and Community based management of Acute malnutrition (CMAM) URDO is proposing to implement integrated curative and preventive nutrition interventions under this project through establishment of 6 OTP and TSFP in Bardera The preventive nutrition interventions will mainly focus on behavior change communication targeting mothers and fathers of children less than two years in the target population and routine immunization.In addition, mothers of children in the outpatient therapeutic care program will be enrolled to mother support groups in their area of residence where women in the neighbourhood meet and discuss issue related to child care and IYCF. These sessions are led by community nutrition volunteers and will run 8 10 weeks until all essential IYCF topics are discussed. The mothers who complete these sessions will be encouraged to establish similar groups in their neighbourhood to pass the message to other mothers in the area.Malnutrition levels throughout Bardera district and villages remain difficulty. The high rates are related to variations in right to use to a diet of adequate quantity and quality, poor public health conditions (including water and sanitation), low levels of access to quality Nutrition services for vulnerable groups and lack of basic knowledge on diets.

How is the project linked to durable solutions/resilience?

URDO has designed and will implement its program using community participation and the involvement of the local government and different representatives of the different line ministries in the region. In the program implementation phase URDO will make sure that community will be involved in making sure that every component of the program activity is shared with the community at large through community meetings in which all genders and groups (IDPs, Host communities) are represented. URDO similarly works with the local government and the different line ministry representatives in making sure that the interventions are in need with the communities’ needs. Working closely with the community and local government lead to taking ownership of the structures (Health posts and the Water sources) for sustainability purposes. Training and working with different groups as mentioned in the activity section will increase their resilience because once they know how to maintain the water sources and how to diagnose and deal with certain illness they will know how fast to take action. In addition, URDO is cognizant of the fact that pollution either water, soil or air might occur during the project implementation stages and it might further worsen the human and animal health. These effects might be as a result of the processes of installation of operational activates either as new structures or rehabilitation of health and nutrition centers or the rehabilitation or installation of the WASH structures. The non-biodegradable sachets of the Plumpy nut or supplementary plumpy may also cause environmental an nuisance and may be ingested by the domestic animals and these is a health risk to the animals.

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

URDO is well aware of the clan and community dynamics in Somalia and particularly in Bardhere due to its existence and long term experience in the area. URDO has been making sure that each component of the community is well represented I.e the host community, IDPS and the minority clans in the programe inception and roll out periods. URDO also makes sure that information is made aware to every person i.e Women, men, girls, and boys within target communities through the burners which are written in Somali. The burners will be spelling out what sort of assistance URDO with the support of its donors are to provide to the community in this case the burners will clearly explain the integrated Health, Nutrition and WASH program for the listed settlements but this doesn’t mean that if one is venerable and doesn’t come from the identified settlement they will not be attended to, this is because URDO respects the humanitarian imperative and has knowledge of global initiatives such as Core Humanitarian Standards of Accountability etc. URDO will make sure during its data collection period that beneficiaries will be documented by showing their origin, there IDP or host status, male, female, age and what part of the project have they benefitted from, this will increase accountability to the community as well as to the donor.

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

URDO program management and program team has good understanding that women, men, boys, and girls are affected differently by disasters and displacement, therefore URDO will prioritizes gender equity and the promotion of women's rights in all our humanitarian responses. In so doing URDO will work closely with Women and girls in the health programe through the Primary Health Care service in which maternal child health (MCH) falls. Pregnant and lactating women who are at risk of anemia, hypertension and other life threatening medical condition will be given priority in the ANC and PNC services. Under five Girls and boys will be given priority as they are also the venerable part of the community, the same group will be benefitting rom the Nutrition intervention under the same roof. Older person will be given priority as well. Men with life threatening medical condition will also be treated in the health programe. Women and female Youth will be involved while rehabilitating and reconstruction of boreholes and wells because they are the main users of water for household and personal use.

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

URDO being an indigenous organization is committed to safe programming and will make sure that any of its program activities should not cause benefit and no harm. URDO is aware that limited livelihood opportunities are also a cause of conflict in Somalia, therefore URDO will as much as possible be working with the community and will be cognizant of the fact that the community is subdivided into host and IDPS, women, men and youth and children. Whenever it comes to activities that involve communal structures that might later on bring conflict problems like hiring of staff, reconstruction of health and nutrition posts, Boreholes and wells URDO will make sure that all persons within the community is part of the decision making, this includes even people with disability. The final decision will be shared through public announcement. URDO is also very much aware of the “Heyr” cultural system in which many disputes are settled, therefore to avoid URDOs direct involvement in whenever there are conflicts that might affect the program activities. It should also be appreciated that many Youth and idle Somali men and women will involve themselves in conflict, therefore it is anticipated through the program course that Youth men and women will be engaged in the program through community activities and will receive trainings.
