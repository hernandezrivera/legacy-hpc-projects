Project Summary

RIA initiative is proposing a 12 months project targeted at enhancing the resilience and protection of vulnerable and conflict affected girls and boys in Bama LGA of Borno State, Mubi North of Adamawa state and Potiskum, Nangere and Damaturu LGAs of Yobe State respectively within host communities and Internally Displaced Persons (IDPs) camps. The project will be targeting a total of 11,550 Children ( 7,507 girls and 4,043 boys) who may have been separated or may have been recruited and used by armed actors and those facing protection risks; while 7,700 (5,000 women and 2,700 men) of those targeted will be caregivers, foster parents and other community members in above listed Local Government Areas. About 10% of the targeted beneficiaries will be people living with disability.
Target children mentioned earlier will be reached with continuous community based life-skills services and community based psychosocial support services; using already developed materials by International Rescue Committee, UNICEF and GoalPrime. RIA Initiative will strengthen community-based referral mechanisms via which children with protection concerns shall be identified and referred to case management partners in the selected LGAs as well as other complementary service partners. All services and activities based in the host communities and IDPs Camp will be in compliance with NCDC, WHO and SMoH guidelines on COVID-19 protocols; therefore, RIA Initiative will deploy storytelling, cultural dances and movies, creative arts, cross-cultural activities and edutainment approaches in carrying out her services. RIA Initiative will also work with the Child Protection Sub Sector (CPSS) to strengthen the referral pathways in above listed LGAs and ensure linkage with other complementary services relevant for the well-being of girls and boys in the selected LGAs.
RIAI will undertake awareness raising activities for and with 11,550 children (7,507 girls and 4,043 boys), 7,700 (5,000 women and 2,700 men) on how, where and when to access services in selected LGAs. These activities will include awareness on Child Rights, PSEA, Child Protection, Positive Parenting, Complementary service availability & COVID-19 community transmission prevention. Awareness creation on prevention of S/GBV shall also be integrated into the sensitization package.
There will be collaboration with WASH partners in the mentioned LGAs to provide and procured items for prevention of Cholera among the children in all the sites/communities/locations/facilities that RIA Initiative will be carrying out her activities. In the absence of a WASH partner, there will be direct support through the project.

1 - Needs

The United Nations Children Funds estimates that 60% of the children have experienced some form of violence in Nigeria. Violence against children increases in emergency settings due to the erosion of social protection mechanisms, insecurity, and unmet basic needs.According to the guidance on the Child Protection 2022 Humanitarian Response Plan in northeast Nigeria, an estimated over 2 million people (1,791,557 million children and 253,904 caregivers) require child protection services; this includes an estimated 22,394 children and 3,174 caregivers living with disabilities. Approximately 2 million children need child protection services due to a breakdown/absence of child protection systems and community-based safety networks. Two hundred and seventeen respondents in the Multi-Sector Needs Assessments reported symptoms of child emotional distress, which was most prevalent in IDPs camps and returnee households. It is also worthy to mention that the lingering crisis between government forces (GF) and armed opposition groups (AOG) has a significant impact on the emotional well-being of children. In furtherance, compounding the issue is that 10% of the sites/locations are occupied by IDPs who have been displaced more than once, contributing to even higher levels of stress among children and highlighting the urgent need for mental health and psychosocial support services (MHPSS).It is also estimated that 1 in 50 children have been separated from their family due to the fluidity of insecurities in the region including the selected LGAs, as such; there is an urgent need for family tracing, case management and alternative care. It is also worthy to note that there’s a huge gap in birth registration for children under the age of 5, meaning children who are born within this age group over the last 3 years are at a gross disadvantage of having social security and national identification number.

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

RIA Initiative will continue to mainstream protection throughout the implementation of this project by aligning every approach with the protection principles and promoting meaningful access, safety, and dignity of both the beneficiaries and that of employers and volunteers. RIA Initiative, just like in our ongoing project will continue to prioritize safety, dignity and avoid causing any form of harm to both beneficiaries and workers. RIA Initiative will also strengthen already established mechanisms through which affected populations in selected LGAs can measure the adequacy of interventions, and address concerns and complaints. RIA Initiative will continue to ensure that the target population accesses the service in proportion to needs and without barriers and discrimination. RIA Initiative will continue to pay attention to individuals and groups who may be particularly vulnerable or have difficulty accessing assistance and services. In the Life-skills based psychosocial support services component of this project, RIA Initiative will integrate GBV Risk Mitigation activities to minimize or mitigate GBV risk to beneficiaries. RIA Initiative will continue to work consciously in compliance with the Sphere Humanitarian Core Standards and Imperatives. All approaches will align with the principles of protection as we remain committed to ensuring that this project does not expose beneficiaries to more risk in compliance with the DO NO HARM Protection Principle. RIA Initiative will also ensure that all activities are carried out in compliance with the Nigerian Center for Disease Control (NCDC) and Federal Ministry of Health (FMoH) guidelines against the spread of, and community transmission of COVID-19. In addition, RIA Initiative will be intentional about maintaining an environment where there is zero sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA). All staff, volunteers, contractors, and vendors engaged directly or indirectly to implement this project will be given orientation on PSEA. They will be familiarized with RIA Initiative's policy on PSEA and they must express their unwavering commitment by signing the RIA Initiative's PSEA code of conduct. In addition, RIA Initiative will also lay down reporting protocols for the reporting of PSEA cases if unfortunately, they occur. RIA Initiative will also ensure that the identity of the reporters is kept very confidential. When convenient, RIA Initiative will encourage that reports are made on anonymous grounds.

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

Collection of vital feedbacks from the affected population, which constitutes the vulnerable group to be served in this project, RIA Initiative, will employ amongst other strategies; “Use an anonymous feedback platform where beneficiaries, employees can anonymously ask questions,". This creates a comfort level among employees and targets beneficiaries to be able to communicate freely thereby. Similarly, we will Foster Trust: "It is essential that target beneficiaries or a community member believe that their feedback will or reaches the expected destination and create impact," in the end. It is trust and belief among persons who feel that the same would enable smooth functioning of the feedback mechanism. Acknowledge about the project will also help in no small measure. "It makes a lot of difference when a beneficiary understands that his/her contributions are actually being heard and acknowledged, and not being lost in the organization’s black box. This is always seen as a fulfillment on his/her part. The use of a Two-Way system will also help: A communication system in an organization must always encourage dialogues across the organization and also target beneficiaries of an identified target location. "A combination of top-down pulse surveys and bottom-up employee-generated conversations work well and lastly, responding in a timely and appropriately will help in a great way. On the response and implementation level, all gathered feedback and concerns highlighted by beneficiaries’ will be fully considered due to the fact that it is an expression of what they feel and it will positively influence how activities and services are conducted moving forward.
