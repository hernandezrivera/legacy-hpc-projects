Project Summary

In a bit to ameliorate on the deplorable living conditions of affected populations of the NW/SW Regions of Cameroon, COHESODEC in the implementation of this project hopes to intervene in three-(3) vulnerable divisions i.e Mezam, Momo-(NWR) and Meme division-(SWR). Vulnerable communities identified as beneficiaries of this WASH sector project in 2023 are found within the following target localities: Bafut, Bali, Bamenda, Bamenda 3, Batibo, Mbengwi, Santa, Tubah, _Kumba-North and Kumba-South localities.
This project shall ensure that beneficiaries have access to WASH services and Needs which will for a long time significantly improve on their lives and social well-being regarding Water, Sanitation and Hygiene.
This project shall ensure the construction of 20 water points (solar boreholes) to enable some 10,000 persons in these target vulnerable communities to have access to constructed and rehabilitated water points. The project will also be ensuring that 1 of the 20 solar boreholes are constructed in a health center to enable these community health facilities gained access to safe drinking water points. This project will also ensure the construction of 15 gender-sensitive latrine cubicles in IDP sites of the target localities as well as the construction of 15 gender-segregated family latrine cubicles in IDP sites for a minimum of 1,080 beneficiaries.
Furthermore, the project will enable COHESODEC to distribute a total of 4,050 WASH kits to boys, girls, men, women, families, PWD and old people. These kits shall include Menstrual Hygiene management Kits to 300 women and girls, 250 kits distributed to vulnerable households, 1,500 WASH items (soap, aquatabs) distributed to men, women, boys and girls. The WASH kit intervention of this project will ameliorate on the living conditions of target beneficiaries while the rest will benefit from WASH items (soap, aquatabs) distributed in target communities!
Moreover, the project will be promoting safe hygiene and sanitation practices among vulnerable people reaching out to 10,000 people along awareness raising campaign-lines and the promotion of good WASH practices, ensure that at least 10,000 affected people know how to provide feedback and complaints to humanitarian actors and 300 hand-washing facilities with soap.
In addition, this project seeks to ensure the support of solid waste management in IDP sites through the construction of 6 waste pits and the distribution of 25 waste bins in target IDP sites and the construction of 8 bathroom cubicles in IDP earmarked sites for this project.
Also, the project shall ensure the construction of 2 disability sensitive latrines in health facilities as well as 5 gender sensitive latrines in target health facilities.
Finally, in the implementation of this project, COHESODEC shall conduct 3 emergency WASH needs assessments in affected areas-(Baseline, mid-term evaluation and an end-line assessment) in the 7 divisions of the North-West Region.

1 - Besoins / Needs

In the implementation of this project, COHESODEC will require the following; >Specialized technicians shall be selected and sub-contracted after a tender process ensuring they have a proper mastery of earmarked project activities with regards to borehole drilling and latrine construction, >Specialized suppliers shall be selected and contracted after a tender process to supply WASH kits and hand washing facilities. This will be done through a rigorous tender process to ensure only the best suppliers are contracted to do the job. The WASH kits and hand-washing facilities will be comprised of the following items: (Plastic pail with cover, Plastic dipper, Laundry Soap x4, Bath soap x12, Toothpaste x2, Toothbrush x5, Nail cutter x3, Sanitary Pad packs x3, Malong x2, Water purification tablets-( aqua tabs). >As far as the promotion of safe hygiene and sanitation practices among vulnerable people is concern, COHESODEC shall recruit, train and use 10 sensitization contracted agents, contract a printing press to produce 10,000 hand-outs for awareness raising campaigns and hygiene promotion of good wash practices as well as sensitize the vulnerable population on the provision of feedback and complaints to humanitarian actors. Moreover, COHESODEC shall after due process contract with a specialized contractor to construct waste pits and the supply of waste bins. Finally, COHESODEC shall recruit and train 35 data collectors, 5 per division in all the 7 divisions of the North-West Region to collect data for the WASH need assessments.
