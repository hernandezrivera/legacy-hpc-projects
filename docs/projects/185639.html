Project Summary

Prantic Unnayan Society (PRANTIC), Bangladesh is planning to continue educational access to a total of 1,695 Children of age group (3-24) through conducting a total of 23 LCs in host and Rohingya Communities under Ukhiya Upazila of Cox’s Bazar District. Out of these 23, 17 LCs will be conducted for a total of 1,395 children of age (3-24) in RC and remaining 6 will be conducted for 300 children of age (3-4) in Host community. Out of 17 LCs, in Rohingya community 13 will be conducted following the Education Sector Guidelines and the other 4 LCs in will be conducted under distant learning through digital learning centers (DLCs). The other 6 LCs (out of total 23 LCs) will be conducted in Host Community under distant learning through digital learning centers (DLCs). PRANTIC will strictly follow Edusector curriculum for Rohingya children (4-14).
PRANTIC has been conducting LCs in the both Rohingya and Host community since 2018. At present PRANTIC is conducting a total of 11 LCs (2 in HC and 8 in RC) ensuring education for 785 children (358 girls and 427 boys) of age group (3-24). At present the girl’s participation is 46%. PRANTIC is planning to add more 12 LCs (8 in RC and 4 in HC) to ensure educational access to more 910 children (480 girls and 430 boys) to increase girl’s participation from 46% to 52% making a total of 1,695 children in 23 LCs in the both host and Rohingya communities. In addition to this PRANTIC will accommodate 5% special children (on the basis of availability) with disability and transgender in the both communities.
In order to implement the plan of JRP 2022, PRANTIC will conduct 12 main activities in host and Rohingya Communities. These are 1. Provide basic education, life skills training and organize sports for children (3-24) with COVID- 19 awareness campaign materials from the perspective of prevention, detection and treatment in Rohingya and host community; 2. Conduct Distant Learning through Digital Learning System for (3-5) ECD in Rohingya and Host Community; 3. Recruit required numbers of volunteers and facilitators to conduct LCs and A&Y centres in Rohingya and Host Community; 4. Provide 4 Days Psychosocial Support (PSS) Training to teachers and Supervisory staff; 5. Provide 4 Days Foundation Training on Educational Core Materials to teachers and supervisory staff; 6. Provide 2 days COVID- 19 related Health and Hygiene Training to Teachers and supervisory staff; 7. Provide 2 Days DRM Training to teachers and supervisory staff; 8. Organize day long monthly refreshers training for teachers and supervisory staff; 9. Organize Monthly Guardian meeting, 10. Organize Monthly LCMC Meeting; 11. Organize six monthly FGD with Community for learning outcome assessment; and 12. Organize Annual Community gathering to enhance social cohesion and tolerance in the community.
PRANTIC in parallel to its existing supervision, monitoring and evaluation system, will engage parents, community people, opinion and traditional leaders to strengthen child protection and assessment of progress from the perspective of children's attitude, behavior and life skills. On the basis of assessment, PRANTIC will revise the proposal as per the result of assessment.
PRANTIC is expecting core educational materials for (4-14) age groups from UNICEF as it received in the past. Without calculating this, the estimated average costs per children (3-24) is 125 USD.
