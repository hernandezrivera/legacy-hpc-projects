Project Summary

More than 12 years of conflict and recent events such as February's earthquakes have left healthcare facilities in Northern Syria under-resourced and lacking in adequately maintained medical equipment, limiting access to healthcare for crisis-affected communities. Additionally, the improper disposal of non-functional electronic devices, including medical equipment, releases hazardous materials into the environment, posing a risk to public health and exacerbating environmental degradation.
