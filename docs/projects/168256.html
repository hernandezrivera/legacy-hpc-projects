Project Summary

CFS (Child Friendly Spaces). Potential constraints due to COVID-19 will be overcome through a methodology based on "tent-to-tent" or "door-to-door" recreational activities taking place respectively in the resettlement areas and families hosting IDPs so that to avoid children gathering. Should a lockdown be declared, this methodology will be translated into practice by parents of children acting as activists within their own families. 2 AVSI officers will supervise the daily activities implementation in resettlement areas and create a weekly plan with activists/parents. This methodology has already been adopted in place and is giving positive feedback.
PSS. Training on PSS and mental health for government social workers in partnership with the Alberto Chipande Institute of Science and Technology (ISCTAC) University of Pemba (memorandum of understanding already in place). Owing to constraints due to COVID-19, an hotline for children storytelling and psychosocial support has been created and is operational in local languages.
Reinforce Case Management and Referral System for Child Protection. Cross-cutting activities for capacity building and coordination for Case Management services, namely police (PRM), district attorney office (procuradoria distrital), District Office of Health, Women and Social Welfare (SDSMAS). Particular importance will be given to the Gabinete de Atendimento Familia e Menores Vitimas de Violencia, an institution that could be considered as a connecting point among all institutions mentioned above. These activities include daily on-the-job-training for local authorities and monthly cross-sectoral coordination meetings aimed at fostering the adoption of common tools for streamlining the legal and health follow-up regarding protection issues.
Under this action falls the support to the judicial system logistics. It is worth noting that in some districts does not exist a judge for protection legal issues. It means that judges have to move from one district to another to follow up on such cases. Thanks to AVSI's logistics support, the judge of Macomia reached the districts of Ibo and Quissanga and 4 (cases) went on trial in a 5-month span (2019-2020).
Establishment WGFS (Women Girls Friendly Spaces). The establishment of WGFS in partnership with the local authorities gives to the most vulnerable women and girls a safe place where they can engage in income-generating activities and discuss potential psychological traumas with a specialised staff. Women and girls will be active actors being the managers of the WGFS. The Community leaders and members will be also involved within the establishment process to guarantee support throughout the entire community.

Reinforce Women Protection Mechanism (Case
Management and Referral system strengthening). Similarly to the action described above concerning Child Protection, it will be crucial to render operational the interlinkages among the sectors in charge of preventing and addressing gender-based violence. The action is based on activities integrating both the community and the institutional dimension. Within the community sphere, community courts (tribunais locais) will be either set up or strengthened (whereas existing) with the help of community leaders and through awareness campaigns. At a higher institutional level AVSI commits to enhancing the cross-sectoral coordination and sharing of information to ensure the follow-up of GBV cases both from a legal and clinic perspective.
Linha verde. The hotline will be promoted through radio broadcasts and dedicated awareness sessions at the community level.
Distribution dignity kits. The activity will be carried out to benefit both the increasing inflow of IDPs sheltered both in resettlement areas and within the hosting families. Considering current migration patterns it will be paramount to include host families among beneficiaries.
