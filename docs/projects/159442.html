Project Summary

The proposed intervention is envisioned to improve GBV, shelter and NFIs response through increased access to temporary shelter, NFIs , GBV services and provision of immediate and comprehensive lifesaving services to survivors, and GBV risk mitigation for vulnerable women and girls through the following interventions: Clinical management of Rape (CMR), case management (CM); psychosocial support services (PSS) including psychosocial counselling, psychosocial first aid (PFA), social networking and access to information on available GBV services, through women and girls friendly spaces/safe spaces, and establishment and strengthening of GBV referral pathways (to enable access to comprehensive GBV services including legal, medical and livelihood services) and provision of emergency shelters, NFIs to most vulnerable and displaced female headed and people living with disability special need in the target communities, including IDP, returnees and Host communities in the target locations. The ARC will also strengthen GBV risk reduction through strengthening and engaging community protection groups, distribution of dignity kits, whistles and solar lanterns to vulnerable women, girls and mobility reaching Aids and Grabbers for PLWD, provide capacity building and trainings to community volunteers/workers and GBV service providers to enhance service provision and risk reduction for women and girls, and conduct safety audits for GBV risk assessments to inform the project implementation and decisions, among other interventions. The proposed project will therefore increase accessibility, quality and coordination of GBV, shelter, NFIs and Disability services for conflict and drought affected communities in Banadir, Jubbaland and Somaliland regions of (Kismayo, Afmadow, Dhobley, Sool, Sanaag, Marodi-Jeh and Awdal surrounding villages’ districts). The ARC will work with various stakeholders to ensure women and girls are protected from GBV and receive support when they experience harm . Some of the stakeholders include line ministries at state government level, the local administration, the police, and women leadership at community level, community and religious leaders and will establish protection networks for women and girls. Community action plans and protection mechanisms will be developed and implemented to reduce protection risks for women and girls including those in IDP settings.
The proposed intervention will additionally address Shelter and NFIs through the immediate material needs of newly displaced populations in Sool, Sanaag, Jubbaland and Togdheer including, the most vulnerable by conducting timely protection and shelter assessments and distributing the essential emergency kits (emergency shelter/NFI) as well as, referrals of protection and other needs and some limited direct provision of protection services to affected communities, particularly persons with specific needs. The project will collect information in assessments on general access to services issues and also general protection issues, as well as provide information on the protection concerns in other areas such as CP, GBV and HLP in order to guide the AoRs to address those issues and also other general services clusters to respond to access to food or wash issues. Also address the as well as HLP rights of the beneficiaries by providing HLP information services and capacitating committees and government line authorities and other humanitarian actors on HLP issues. The project will assist in capacity building local authorities, IDP committees etc. on IDP rights/obligations. In addition, the project will also aim to mainstream protection across sectors through advice on effective implementation of protection-sensitive and rights - based approach into the relief assistance for other humanitarian actors/ clusters – PWG/All sectors, training and sensitizing duty bearers and other key stakeholder on protection concerns including GBV, and freedom and safety of movement.

Project Activities

1) Establish women Safe space: Establish of child -friendly spaces and women safe spaces and provide support and resources so they provide a platform for information sharing, awareness raising, peer-to-peer networking and support, training, PSS provision, referrals, and linking to community protection structures.2) Training of duty bearers, medical personnel, and humanitarian staff (GBV): ARC will provide Clinical Care for Sexual Assault Survivors (CCSAS) capacity building opportunities with WHO guidelines to health and GBV staff and provide Health facilities' supplies, guidelines, protocols, and regulations is in support of timely and quality GBV service delivery. 3) GBV and Disability Awareness raising advocacy and public outreach campaigns on GBV prevention, response and mitigation (GBV): ARC will engage communities in discussing GBV, child protection and disability risks including the identified risks and ways of addressing them. Awareness activities will be intensified during the marking of international days like the international women’s day, sexual awareness month, HIV/AIDS day, disability day in relation to GBV and the 16 days of activism.4) Provision of risk mitigation items including Mobility Aid Kits for disabilities, Solar light, whistles and solar lamps installation: The ARC will procure disability mobility kits to respond to the need of people living with special need, disability is estimated 15% of general population in Somalia due to violence prone and longtime conflicts.5) Provision of the emergency temporary shelters and non-food items to newly evicted, displaced IDPs and vulnerable host poor households: The Proposed intervention will distribute the essential NFIs and emergency shelter kits to People in need (PIN) captured under humanitarian need analysis in Somaliland carried out by ARC in support of UNHCR. 6) Strengthen protection assessments, analysis, data collection and reporting7) Capacity building to duty bearers and others: ARC will support train key duty bearers, key stake-holders, IDP committees, leaders and other clusters on GBV basic principles, IDP rights, obligations and principles of camp management. 8) Protection advocacy Initiatives: Based on the assessment data, the project will advocate against forced evictions of displaced population and ensure the freedom of movement, and protection of other key rights in Somaliland in coordination with UNHCR, UNCEF and UNFPA. 9) Strengthen the protection mainstreaming within the humanitarian actors in Somalia: ARC will conduct a series of protection mainstreaming trainings for international NGOs, Local NGOs and CBOs staff from their various sectors. The primary objective of this activity is to ensure that all humanitarian interventions are provided in a safe, dignified and in accountable way.10) Identify and train GBV community activists: In line with the recommendations of the safety audits and protection assessments, ARC will identify and train GBV activists at IDP site level.11) Create and train women vigilant groups: The ARC will identify and train women activist groups in Kismayo, Ceel- Afweyn, Banadir and Afmadow IDP camps. The established groups will be trained for GBV protection, reporting mechanisms and will be the first point of contact for GBV survivors at the regions. The activist groups will monitor GBV incidents and support the survivors to report and seek services. 12) Conduct Safety Audit Assessment for 60 IDP camps.13) Survivor – Centred Psychosocial support Services 14) Provide Case Management capacity strengthening to local actors and GBVWG15) Unconditional Cash Transfer (UCT) to 300 survivors or vulnerable female households 16) Provision of solar lights/lanterns at communal latrines and female headed households at IDPs for risk reduction at night.17) Distribute 200 Mobility Aid Kit to PLWD in the target areas, provide them GBV and protection messages for risk mitigation.18) Procurement of dignity kits.

Please provide further explanation

While the emergency nature of the proposed intervention makes it difficult to propose long-term strategies to reduce humanitarian needs, the ARC strives to create a more sustained approach to reduce vulnerability and risk, and create durable solutions for affected communities. The ARC integrates programmes across different sectors and grants to ensure more complex needs are met and beneficiaries are more stable. Sustainability measures of local projects and emergency responses to crises also increase community resilience. For example, the project will build the capacity of all relevant stakeholders, including GBV WG, health workers, government line ministries, and target community structures to strengthen their capacity to deal with GBV prevention and response. The ARC will also work with local institutions, including water and sanitation committees that the ARC trained in the past through OFDA-funded Actions, as well as the local authorities to ensure the actions are sustained beyond the project. Capacity building of these local institutions will be tailored towards ensuring sound management of services and empowering communities to take primary responsibility for these interventions. This Action also ensures GBV service availability for women and girls to increase the reduction of risks, community awareness and life skill and vocational training enhance women’s economic security. The Action also ensures access to basic commodities and creates a flow of basic goods to the local markets, protecting beneficiaries from the brunt of the drought, reducing negative coping strategies and increasing the household food consumption score through UCT.Finally, if necessary, the ARC will request to shift funds to address any unforeseen emergencies and prevent consequences of shocks while additional funding is secured.
