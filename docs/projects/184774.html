Project Summary


As the countries situation, Conflict, drought, COVID-19 and flooding are some of the main shocks that drive displacement and largely affect at least 2.9 million internally displaced persons in Somalia. The majority of displaced people have self-settled in over 2,400 sub-standard and unplanned IDP sites in urban, semi-urban and rural areas across the country .
Marginalized groups and minority groups especially women are more prioritized in making this kind of small projects to empower women and also the disabilities
Thus our project will help many people living in the four largest camps in Bard here district including the poorest and will benefit the most active job-creating women among the refugees who have little to start a business with. Many women are self-sufficient and do not need much from their husbands and some children can afford to go to school for a small income.
The IDP population of Baardheere continues to demonstrate characteristics that make the group more vulnerable than individuals living in urban or rural host communities. Factors that range from plighted living conditions in settlements, inclusion challenges or overall disenfranchisement in accessing essential services continue to remain prevalent for IDPs creating further difficulty in actualizing material changes for these communities. Amongst the populations that have the most acute needs in IDP sites, persons with disabilities (PWDs), individuals from minority clans, women, and youth face extensive barriers in accessing humanitarian services preventing a dignified living conditions for such demographics.
