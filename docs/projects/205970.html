Project Summary

In recognition of the effect of the disease burden as a result of consumption of unsafe water and poor behavior related to sanitation and hygiene, CIDO in this 2024 Response Plan, proposes to intensify and necessitate equitable access to safe, clean drinking-water, basic sanitation and promote hygiene while enhancing survival of the affected population to address morbidity and mortality related to WASH through integration of WASH intervention with FSL, Protection, NFI, Health and Nutrition. The response targets 12,222 (Akobo-4120 and Twic – 8102) persons with much needed WASH services in line with the following WASH specific objectives for the 2024. The locations targeted falls under Priority 1 and 2 as established by the cluster targeting guidance running 2024. This response attracts a budget of 500,000$ to support 2 counties within a period of one year. During the period, CIDO expects to address SAM cases of children and their care takers as well as Pregnant and Lactating women through nutrition facilities with WASH services (WASH Kits) to reduce disease transmission at household level. The Nutrition facilities such as OTP sites, TSFP and SC including health facilities will be reached with basic water and sanitation package to reduce risk of HAIs transmission due to poor WASH conditions. In addition, communities showing high malnutrition prevalence combined with limited access to safe water will be targeted with lasting solutions for water supply and hygiene. Throughout the response, CIDO will as well mainstream protection and mitigate WASH Gender Based Violence to guarantee that beneficiaries of WASH services, in particular women and children access facilities in a safe manner. In some specific contexts, CIDO will also ensure that water points are not a trigger for conflict by ensuring that water user management committees are well trained to discharge their mandate without biasness. CIDO will exploit its vast experience in implementation of multisector project including Health, Nutrition, WASH, NFI, Protection, and FSL to enhance integration by providing inter sectoral aid for the most vulnerable population with WASH services. Realizing the cross-cutting nature of WASH, CIDO will combine the described activities with other sectors by providing dignity kits and NFIs as a mitigation to improve protection status of women and girls. While improving access to water, The response will take care of irrigation needs for farmers and water for livestock, which contributes to improvement of food production and contain elements of diseases prevention as well as improving sanitation and water access in nutrition sites, health centers and schools

1. Needs

South Sudan continue to face challenging humanitarian context in which peace and security remain fragile. Apparently, the Sudan crisis, Influx of returnees from Ethiopia, Intercommunal violence, food insecurity, and the severe impact of climate change present enormous challenges. It is evident that the operational hand pumps in Akobo and Twic Counties are overused and has led to more breakdown of pumps and increased queuing time at the viable water sources causing reduced per capita water consumption (under 15 L/person/day), hence increased consumption from unprotected water sources like untreated rivers and hand dug wells and also reduced time for other essential tasks at their homes. Inadequate access to clean water, improved sanitation and acceptable hygiene practices within the counties of the response location has significantly contributed to the high levels of malnutrition, disease burdens, food insecurity as well as WASH related protection issues. Access to sanitation facilities is similarly low while access to safe and gender sensitive latrines remains a big challenge during both wet and dry seasons and thus open defecation has become their cardinal right, leading to cycles of water contamination, resulting in acute watery diarrhea, cholera, eye infections, and skin diseases. The drivers of WASH outcomes and needs as mentioned above cut across various sectors including food, nutrition, shelter, protection, health and education. The broken WASH facilities, inadequate hygiene and sanitation facilities and limited access to clean drinking water are pointers to poor health conditions with communities at risk of water-borne diseases such diarrheal diseases, cholera and Hepatitis E. combined with the presence of flood waters vector-borne diseases such as malaria, lymphatic filariasis, schistosomiasis with likelihood is to increase. Protection concerns remain high, with people impacted by violence having limited access to justice and the rule of law. HRP 2024. Further, cites that 5.5 million people exclusive of refugees, will be in need of humanitarian assistance. Continued conflict, instability in the country and within the region coupled with flooding have resulted in large-scale internal and cross-border displacement. Food consumption gaps are extreme, and people have largely exhausted their emergency coping strategies hence putting them at risk of WASH/FSL related morbidities. According to the IPC Acute Food Insecurity and Acute Malnutrition Analysis, February July 2022, issued April 9, 2022, classified Twic Counties as Critical (IPC AMN phase 4. Adolescent school girls are likely to be inconsistent in school attendance or drop out due to lack of menstrual management supplies. This increases their vulnerability and puts them at risk of early marriages (Protection Cluster – UNHCR, 2019). In 2024, the precarious situation is expected to be further exacerbated by the country’s increasing humanitarian caseload, the expected increase in the duration of needs in the most affected areas and the projected further reduction in humanitarian assistance across the country. The current economic crisis is impacting people’s ability to access markets, while conflict, flooding and seasonal effects are limiting the flow of both commercial and humanitarian goods and service as such, there is an urgent need to address WASH gaps which would optimally reduce WASH related morbidity & mortality

Carry out community hygiene promotion activities through house to house visits, sessions, campaigns will be carried out at community and institutions like schools, health and nutrition centers.
