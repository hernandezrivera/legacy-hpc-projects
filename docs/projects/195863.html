Project Summary

According to the 2023 HNO draft, the stats point to increased vulnerability to ongoing hostilities, particularly in the Northwest. Limited access to services and homelessness, resulting in a deterioration of the economy, widespread poverty, lack of livelihoods, destruction and loss of housing and property, lack of natural resources, continued spread of the epidemic, exacerbation of protection needs and increased reliance on negative coping mechanisms. Movement restrictions affect communities due to a variety of issues.
Older people and people with disabilities and chronic diseases face a set of challenges unique to their individual circumstances, particularly in areas affected by conflict or multiple displacement. They face stigma, social isolation, and people face an increased risk of losing civil documents, land, and property.
. The project aims to reduce the aforementioned protection risks for the displaced and the host community in northwestern Syria in the Dana area and the surrounding camps through the presence of a protection center that provides services to the community and is a launching point for awareness teams, as the project will provide the following points:
1. Awareness campaigns through awareness teams to educate the community about public protection risks
2- Implementation of C.M services that address the weaknesses of the elderly and people with disabilities. Who will be monitored by case workers, community protection committees and service delivery workers through referrals to the main service provider, according to inter-agency procedures.
3. Provide Individual Protection Assistance (ECF) to cover protection gaps when service is not available within communities
5. Implementing mental health and psychosocial support activities within the institution's center for people who have mental disorders and psychological distress as a result of the war
6- Legal attention to the beneficiaries by verifying the ownership of homes, lands and civil documents to ensure the validity and credibility of the papers
7- Holding legal awareness sessions on CSD and HLP: The sessions will focus on CSD and HLP, the importance of civil documentation, steps and processes to obtain civil documents, recording life events and explaining the risks associated with not obtaining civil documents in addition to providing information about administrative procedures and risks associated with them .
8- Providing social integration activities for people with disabilities in society through peer-to-peer sessions for people with disabilities periodically and repeatedly every 15 days, which helps exchange coping mechanisms. Also, facilities for people with disabilities will be rehabilitated to improve their access to basic services and educate their families and society. The mechanism of integrating people with disabilities through various seminars at the AHF Center, which will be presented by community workers.
The project will ensure accountability of the affected population and stakeholders through coordination, participation, transparent accountability, complaints mechanism, feedback mechanism, and worker competency monitoring.
The project is designed to promote GQ to include women and girls as an integral part of the project
The monitoring plan will ensure that:
1. Monitoring the quality of the implemented activities and comparing the work plan using monitoring and evaluation tools and knowing the satisfaction of the beneficiaries
2. Tracking project indicators to measure the project's monthly progress at the level of both outputs and activities, (surveys, records, photos...), preparing PIM reports on progress against identified indicators, and writing down lessons learned
The project is designed to reduce protection from sexual exploitation and abuse, by signing the Code of Conduct for Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse and establishing complaints mechanisms.

8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Low level of obligations related to PSEA, disproportionate exposure of women to misconduct by humanitarian workers at risk of SEA, and low level of safe reporting of complaints of SEA.mitigation:1. Impose mandatory PSEA training on all staff, including management, field staff, day laborers, volunteers, contractors and vendors, in each area where there are active processes to ensure that staff are aware of their PSEA obligations and provide Staff with the knowledge and skills to prevent and respond to SEA.2 - Establishing mechanisms for reporting protection from SEA at the community level by adopting unified reporting mechanisms within local councils and active bodies that include reporting mechanisms approved within the network: PSEAprograminSyria@un.org and the network contact number [Whatsapp] 009053091518953 - Engage with communities and key stakeholders in strengthening community complaints mechanisms, including defining the provision of victim assistance services in all targeted locations. 4 - Provide awareness sessions for women, girls, boys and men in reception centers, camps, IDP sites, host communities and the community All project staff sign the Code of Conduct for Protection from SEA5 - Investigate complaints received within PSEA risks, refer the survivor to protection services and legal services, refer the perpetrator to approved sanctions regulations, coordinate with the PSEA network, and provide them with the results of the investigation


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk1: Corona and cholera pandemic and its impact on awareness activities, case management, : high Mitigation: - Maintaining social distance between beneficiaries and the project staff - Use methods of protection, including masks, gloves, and sterilizers -Avoiding meetings that contain many beneficiaries -Transfer project personnel in batches within transportation means to reduce congestion. Risk 2: CPactivities are suspended due to the conflict. probability: medium. Mitigation: in the event of attacks or invasion of the region, the service center will be moved to the JisrAl-Shughour area in coordination with the local authorities to ensure the safety of project personnel and beneficiaries and after acquiring the required approval Risk 3:Council local intervention and impeding the implementation of the project interference by armed groups to prevent the implementation of protection activities situation probability: Medium Mitigation: signing a memorandum of understanding with the local council to avoid obstacles, interference or external dictated by armed groups -Raising awareness of the community and primary caregivers to cooperate mitigating the risks of protection. - Establishing community protection networks that are made up of community leaders and influencers to support unhindered protection activities
