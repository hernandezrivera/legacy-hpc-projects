Project Summary

Basma for relief and development - BRD will establish Two CFS centers in Dana (Sub-District) to reach to the affected people in following villages (Burj Elnumra, Deir Hassan - Darhashan and Kafr Deryan) and Aghtrin(Sub-District) in following villages: (Akhtrein, Aziziyeh (Aghtrin) and Dabeq).
will focus on reducing risks and strengthening protective factors for children to ensure their physical, social and emotional development that will contribute to the cluster priority of protecting children and mitigating the effects of violence and displacement. The proposed interventions are aimed to reduce/mitigate the impact of threats to children’s physical and psychosocial security in Given the seriousness of the protection threats experienced by children affected by conflict and displacement, the child protection interventions proposed are one of the frontline services that are critical to the physical and psychosocial survival of conflict-affected and displaced children. This project will promote girls’ participation in the CFSs and provide them with opportunities to safely talk about sexual violence and other gender-specific CP concerns.
BRD will also enhance the ability of CP staff to detect and support specific vulnerabilities amongst girls and boys through comprehensive case management and referral. CAAFAG (will be approached by BRD case managers), and children with complicated mental health problems will be referred to Other service providers.
Children involved or at risk of hazardous labor and in the same line, the center will provide parenting programs to the parents and caregivers. According to HNO In 81 percent of assessed communities, respondents reported child labor preventing school attendance was an issue of concern. This issue will be approached by BRD case managers to comprehensively be managed by working with children and their caregivers to promote minimal standards and involve children in BRD's CFSs.
The activities will aim at increasing boys’ and girls' knowledge and awareness of possible threats, allowing them to better protect themselves.
BRD will also be involved in raising awareness on the prevention of recruitment of children by armed groups, children involved in hazardous labor and prevention of family separation and will provide cash in case management to who needs regarding set criteria for ( labor, exploitation, recruitment, and who needs some special equipment). The main target will be parents, caregivers and community stakeholders such as religious leaders, local council members, teachers, medical point’s directors, and etc.
BRD will train caseworkers to identify and respond to the needs of such children. BRD will use the CP sub-cluster SOPs and referral pathway to provide the needed services through case management. In the child-friendly space (CFS), dedicated time and space will be created for adolescent girls where they can be together and where they do not have to compete with boys. BRD will ensure that a female facilitator is available to work with the girls, who can offer confidentiality, warmth, and advice.
BRD will seek also to link CFS to other services such as education, health, and social services to enhance referral of children in need of special assistance.
BRD seek to build capacities of child protection staff and community stakeholders to provide protection services for children according to CPMS. These Interventions will aim at addressing vulnerabilities related to child labor, child recruitment, child marriage, and family separation. This project will work with leaders and Community Based Protection to prevent labor, exploitation, and recruitment.

4 - Needs

About nine years of conflict in Syria, have increased the suffering of civilians including the suffering of armed conflict, blockade, homelessness, Recent displacement occurred (total number of IDPs 586K) toward North's Syria, Euphrates shield and Afrin, has exacerbated family stresses, disrupted normal patterns of living and crumbled social support systems exposing children to protection threats and psychosocial distress.Events leading to family separation indicate the interconnectedness and diversity of vulnerability factors with death (24 per cent for boys and 22 per cent for girls), economic need (20 per cent for boys), child marriage (22 per cent for girls) and child recruitment (14 per cent for boys) among top cited reasons for family separations . The absence of a continuum of alternative care options puts some children at increased risk of additional protection concerns. these children will be forced to process their conflict experience alone, which may prevent them from being able to fully engage in daily activities such as playing, learning, or basic social interaction.(HNO2020)the crisis producted child labor, unaccompanied children..etc.In addition to the psychological disorders and distress of both children and caregivers. Child labor section focuses on the proportions of communities that have the phenomenon of child labor, including boys and girls labor, which restricts them from going to school. Focus was on two special types of cases, child-headed household and the families that contain people of special needs, The psychosocial needs section focused on measuring the intensity of the mental distress for both children and caregivers and the consequent type of services required for each level of the psychological distress and The category of separated and unaccompanied children is considered as the most affected by the present conflict.These location are densely populated due to recent displacement, which makes the protection risks very high.

5 - Implementation of activities

BRD will Increase access for boys and girls to quality CP interventions in line with the CPMS according to the principles of CP sub-cluster to establish CFS, according to SOPs and CPMS. Activities will take into consideration the balance and gender between children and PWDs, BRD will hire specialist for each age group and gender (children - M/w) to deal with them depend upon their needs.BRD will secure services either outreach or vehicle which transport them to/from the center.Firstly Establishing children (B:2320,G:2310) and youth (B1600:,G:1500) safe spaces. Activities will focus on mitigating the risks of child labor and encouraging them to return to school, through awareness sessions about the risks of child labor and help them to learn a job that does not affect on their life, development, and education. It will also target caregivers (M:525,W:545) at the same line through awareness sessions parenting skills on the dangers of child labor.BRD will provide structured psychosocial support for groups of children.Activating the program of the resilience of children and youth to measure their adaptation in new situations and psycho-drama activities using puppets theatre to introduce some solutions to their problems, activities will conduct by psychologist.BRD will provide a library, stories, recreational activities.specialized child protection services (CM) for G/B.supervision skills and FTR in emergencies and some specialized training related to focused PSS and PFA for children.BRD will train the adolescents, encourage, build their capacity, develop skills, support them.etcStrengthen HRs capacity to respond to CP concerns in Syria.it will provide training on core CP and some related training to the L Community and form social networks in LA to respond to any CP concerns and to focus on PWDs.Performance and operational monitoring systems will be crucial to ensure that project activities are being implemented as planned and achieving the expected results

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Protection Risk: Girls, boys, women, and men's physical security is put at risk as a result of the violence associated with civilian populations/spaces being targeted by armed parties to the conflict; a lack of consideration for the safety and security to access/receive services; lack of consideration of contextual information and targeting of civilian infrastructure en route to, during and directly after service provision. - Likelihood: High - Impact: High - Mitigation: Consider conflict patterns and contextual analysis as part of decisions for establishing a fixed site or mobile service provision. - Ensure safety audit is conducted to ensure safe access by (B,G,M,W) (due to physical, visual/hearing deficits, psychological issues)- Protection Risk: Risk of exploitation or abuse by staff members/service providers if access to services or information is linked to a fee or limited to/excluded for specific persons/groups. - Likelihood: High- Impact: High - Mitigation: - Communicate regularly and clearly to the community about what services are available and who services are for- Provide regular training to staff and service providers for the code of conduct and PSEA.  Specialized child protection services (case management) for girls and boys are available in prioritized locations - Protection Risk: Risk of doing harm or perpetuating unintended consequences to girls, and boys (and their caregivers) through the provision of specialized child protection services if service providers are not adequately trained/ services are of low quality - Likelihood: High- Impact: High - Mitigation: ensure training packages are in line child protection minimum standards and consider the remotely managed context - Services should not be provided by any staff who have not received and demonstrated proficiency in training modules. - include a practice element to training modules that can be overseen through remote mentorship arrangements to allow for practice prior to service provision
