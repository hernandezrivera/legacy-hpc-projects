Project Summary

In line with the HCT’s recommendations, this project under the Logistics Sector led by WFP aims to ensure the provision of fuel distributions on an ad-hoc basis until end of 2022 as a contingency measure to ensure continuity of the most critical water and health services in the country. A total of 75 facilities including 35 Health facilities and 40 Water pumping stations will be targeted to receive fuel.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

Ensure emergency fuel deliveries on demand, in cases of highly critical and urgent shortfalls targeting Health and WASH facilities in most need. 31 public hospitals, 3 central warehouse locations, and the National AIDS/TB programme building will be prioritized for fuel support to the health sector. For 40 water pumping stations – each providing water to more than 20,000 individuals – will be targeted to receive fuel. This would involve, among others, coordination with the WASH and Health sectors in targeting essential facilities, coordination with OCHA, UNDSS and the relevant Lebanese military authorities to ensure safe fuel distributions, and contracting a superintendent company to monitor fuel distributions and ensure accurate data on the operation is recorded.
