Project Summary

CIAUD has developed an intervention strategy based on community capacities. This strategy takes into account the specificity of the groups as well as community participation mechanisms in the project for better ownership because the community is puting at the center of its interventions. Access to services supposes that members of the community are able to use the services available to obtain solutions to their problems. These are issues of sexual exploitation and abuse. Without access to services, the rights and duties of affected communities are meaningless and vulnerable groups cannot be protected. Access to services is indeed very essential to ensure that rights exist in practice, not just in theory.
CIAUD will contribute, through the implementation of project activities, to the development of sustainable solutions aimed at increasing the protection affected communities against acts of sexual exploitation and abuse, in the Sahel and East regions of Burkina Faso, through access to holistic care services for women, girls, and marginalized groups and to children from EAS. CIAUD will bring its expertise in case management and direct legal and psychological services through its legal and psychological clinics. A referral circuit will be set up in favor of holistic care to the medical service and other services that save lives. As a member of the AoR VBG, the child protection cluster and the PSEA network in Burkina Faso, CIAUD will contribute its expertise in supporting the conduct of training on PSEA, complaint and feedback mechanisms. It will also contribute to the establishment of frameworks and other community dynamics promoting the protection and defense of the interests of the communities and more specifically of its most vulnerable layers.
