Project Summary

O presente plano de resposta a Emergencia, pretente trazer um conjunto de acoes anticipada por forma a melhor responder as novas exigencia conforme os cenarios que forem surgindo.
O plano tenciona atingir um total de 5000 pessoas, corespondente a 1000 familias em dois didtritos, sendo 2500 pessoas em Chiure e igual numero em Montepuez.

Plan fields

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

O plano pretende responder as necessidades causados pelos diferentes cenarios que acontecem de forma ciclica nos distritos de Chiuri e Montepuez, alcancando um total de 5000 pessoas ou criancas, correspondente a 1000 familias.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The main priority activities that will be developed by this project are:-Distribution of point-of-use water treatment materials / products, including training and monitoring on proper use at household level.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The project will identify potential risks through a holistic assessment of all activities and through regular community consultations with primary beneficiaries.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The proposed project will encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of the most vulnerable people, through inclusion and effective participation

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Kukumbi will carry out formative research, with a view to understanding the main barriers and thus aligning its evidence-based interventions.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project will have a positive impact on the environment by ensuring that the activities do not harm the environment, on the contrary, protect it.
