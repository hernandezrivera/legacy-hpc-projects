Project Summary

The project aims to support UNDSS in providing operational security support to UNAFPOs and their Implementing Partners under Saving Lives Together (SLT) Framework in North-East Nigeria. The project supports decision-making process with a solid policy framework with security policies covering all aspects of security management and ensuring cohesion within the UNSMS. With having an increase of humanitarian needs and peace-developing activities in the North-East it is expected that UN personnel and activities will enlarge their footprint in North-East. It will, naturally, ask for more resources to be deployed and a comprehensive security layout and support to be provided.

1 - Needs

The security environment in the BAY States of Nigeria and especially in Borno State where most UN presence and activities are ongoing, continues to be volatile and unstable with ongoing military operations against Non-State Armed Groups (NSAG). This has resulted in generally very dynamic security and operational environments prone to sudden changes. This requires the deployment of enhanced and extended security capabilities to assure continued operational support to the ongoing humanitarian operations. The additional capacity will, therefore, be required for 2022 to enable UNDSS to continue to provide critical security support and coordination of activities for the expected expansion of humanitarian programs and ensuring the safety and security of UNSMS and SLT partners. The UNDSS staff is expected to reach the remote new locations that keep on adding in priority lists and support UN staff and IPs in nine humanitarian hubs out in Borno State, and other UN operational hubs in Adamawa State (Yola and Mubi), and Yobe State (Damaturu, Potiskum). UNDSS staff are expected to assess these locations, provide operational support, liaison with actors on the ground, coordination of security efforts, implementation of Saving Lives Together Framework (SLT), implementation of physical security measures at UN locations, and tailored security trainings to the humanitarian community for their operations in order for them to be carried out safely and securely as well as to establish a physical deployment in eight humanitarian hubs in support of UN operations there.

2 - Activities

a. Conduct security risk assessments and make suitable recommendations on the security measures/conditions of accessible areas/location to UN AFPs and IPs; b. Provide area and ad hoc SRMs and recommendations to the UNSMS and humanitarian community for them to operate safely and securely; c. provide security operational support to UNSMS and IPs personnel and activities at implemented in UN Humanitarian Hubs in Borno State and other UN operational hubs in Adamawa and Yobe States; d. Produce and disseminate regular SITREPs (daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly) and specific analytical products from the area of operations (AoO) to UNAFPOs and IPs; e. Monitoring and tracking of movement of UN personnel and assets official movements within that AoO; f. Establishing and managing functional UN Security Communication System within AoO; g. Maintain liaison, coordination of activities, and collaboration on the security matters with actors on the ground i.e. Nigerian Army units deployed, Nigerian Air Force, MNJTF, DSSS, Police Units, CJTF, and others; h. Implement SLT Framework in AoO and provide every kind of support to SLT partners in line with applicable policies; h. Tailor and facilitate adequate Security Training Plan for UN AFPs and IPs, as well as SLT partners.
