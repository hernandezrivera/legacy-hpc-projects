Project Summary

This project aims to enhance access to quality education for vulnerable children in the Far North Region, addressing critical barriers such as inadequate infrastructure, lack of birth certificates, financial constraints, and teacher shortages. It will focus on providing:
Renovated and newly constructed schools with essential amenities.
Birth certificates for 500 children affected by crises to facilitate school enrollment and exam participation.
Tuition and examination fee support for 300 and 200 vulnerable pupils, respectively, to reduce dropouts.
Training for 50 teachers to improve pedagogy and classroom management.
Community mobilization and the establishment of Parent-Teacher Associations (PTAs) to foster local ownership of education.
The intervention targets over 1,000 students, their families, and educators, aiming to increase school enrollment, improve learning outcomes, and create a sustainable and inclusive education system.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

Inadequate School Infrastructure: Many schools in the Far North Region lack basic infrastructure such as safe classrooms, furniture, clean water, and sanitation facilities.The project will address these needs by renovating and constructing classrooms, installing water points, and building gender-segregated latrines to create conducive learning environments.Lack of Legal Identity (Birth Certificates): Children affected by displacement, armed conflict, and natural disasters often lack birth certificates, preventing their enrollment in schools and participation in official exams.The project will facilitate the issuance of birth certificates for 500 children through mobile registration campaigns and covering administrative costs for vulnerable families.Economic Barriers to Education: Families face financial hardships that make it difficult to pay tuition fees and examination fees, leading to high dropout rates, especially among vulnerable children.The project will provide tuition fee support for 300 pupils and cover examination fees for 200 students, enabling them to stay in school and complete their education.Severe Teacher Shortages and Lack of Training: Rural schools struggle to attract and retain qualified teachers, and many lack proper training in effective teaching methods.The project will train 50 teachers in modern pedagogy, classroom management, and the use of teaching aids, improving the quality of education.Limited Access to Educational Resources: Schools often lack learning tools, textbooks, and access to technology, which hampers teaching and learning effectiveness.The project will equip schools with solar-powered tablets, digital tools, and age-appropriate e-learning materials to enhance learning experiences.Low Community Engagement: Parents and community members are often disengaged from educational initiatives, seeing education as the government’s sole responsibility.The project will mobilize communities through awareness campaigns, establish Parent-Teacher Associations (PTAs), and encourage parental involvement to foster local ownership and support for education.Gender Disparities in Education: Cultural norms, early marriages, and domestic responsibilities disproportionately limit girls’ access to education, resulting in low enrollment and retention rates.The project will target the enrollment of 200 additional girls and establish mentorship programs to empower and encourage female students to stay in school.
