Project Summary

The school meal activity is implemented in the Far North region targeting 72,000 school children in the most crisis affected divisions of Logone and Chari, Mayo Sava and Mayo Tsanaga. The project aims to provide school meals to 72,000 children to encourage and promote school attendance. A consequence of the Lake Chad Basin crisis, many schools were closed leaving thousands of children out of school for many years mainly in the targeted divisions. With the improvement observed in the security situation in the areas and thanks to the humanitarian support from UN agencies, and NGOs many children have been able to go back to school. The project will contribute to maintain those who are already in the school till the completion of the primary school cycle. The project will consist of providing daily hot meals to all the children during 165 days of the school year, to encourage them to attend and stay in schools throughout the school year, thus contributing to improve their nutritional status as well as to protect them enrolment by the terrorist groups.
Food commodities will be delivered to schools and meals will be prepared for the children at school. Parents and communities will complement with various items including water, firewood and cooking services and other locally produced food. The food basket and daily rations are; cereals (140g), Pulses (40g), Oil (15g) and salt (3g). During the implementation special attention will be paid to children with disabilities. Their food will be collected and served in at their sitting place.
Various actions will be put in place to support the implementation of the project. Capacity strengthening sessions will be organized on school feeding management, food storage, gender and Protection, for all actors involved in the school canteens management including other partners operating in the targeted division such as local councils, Parent and Teachers’ Associations, mothers’ associations and Government staff form the Ministries of Basic Education, agriculture, Health, Women affairs and social Affairs. In addition, Mothers' associations will trained on nutrition aspects using locally available food.

1 - Besoins / Needs

The insecurity in the Far North region due to Boko Harm crisis, has prompted the displacement of many households alongside Cameroon Nigeria Borders. The education sector was heavily impacted with schools closed and over 100 thousand of children out of schools in Logone et Chari, Mayo Sava and Mayo Tsanaga divisions in the Far North. Thanks to the Government efforts to maintain security in these divisions, school children are progressively going back to schools and few schools have reopened. Some displaced families are returning to their villages and children are attending school but still hesitant and demotivated. Parents have limited income sources, they are mostly busy reconstructing destroyed farms and properties but they are still fearful with sporadic attacks. Most of the parents’ activities include income generation to sustain households 'feeding, some returnee families are benefiting from humanitarian assistance. Children highly need food support to keep them at school and also to improve their nutritional status as well as to protect from being enrolled or used by terrorist groups. The food assistance at school will encourage them to attend classes and stay in school throughout the school year. The school meals will serve as an incentive to maintain them in class, this alongside various other education assistance and psychological support provided by other humanitarian agencies in the mostly affected divisions.
