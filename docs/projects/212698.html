Project Summary

HRO aims to continue providing GP & MA services for IDPs and HC in our already existing community centers in Idleb (Jisr Ash Shugur, Darkosh) & in Aleppo (Azaz), besides planning to establish new CCs in Idleb (Harim, Salqin, Dana & Maaret Tamsrin), in Aleppo (Afrin & Akhtrin) and through outreach visits to the widows’ camps to target the female widows & children with different GP, MA activities. HRO will also provide integrated protection services through community centers & mobile teams, to ensure access to all age groups, including vulnerable groups from IDPs, HCs, and the most vulnerable groups through risk education awareness sessions, victim assistance services which include PSS, orientation & referral to external services, and case management, GP awareness sessions about older people’s issues. HRO will also provide protection monitoring service through conducting service mapping and establishing referral pathways & reporting on protection concerns and issues, in addition to monitoring the application of protection principles during mass IDPs influxes and IDPs relocation, RE awareness sessions, legal awareness sessions & legal counseling, awareness-raising activities for community and caregivers about Community-based protection services initiatives and structures, recreational activities, CM including CVA & IPA, protection services targeting older People and PwDs with, training the humanitarian workers and local authorities on protection principles and RE. HRO will build the capacity of staff on (GP, MA, Inclusion of PwD & OP).
The MEAL team will be responsible for ensuring the confidentiality of beneficiaries’ data, by ensuring files are stored in a safe place within the facilities, and cases’ names are coded. Monthly reports, key findings, MoVs, and monitoring findings will be shared with the donor and cluster.
HRO ensures the effective participation of IDPs and HC in the project lifecycle from design to implementation and feedback through the already-established protected feedback channels to help them report suggestions, complaints, and PSEA concerns.
45,000 beneficiaries (29,785 F and 15,215 M) (30,245 IDPs and 14,755 HCs) will be targeted with the project’s activities including (340 young children, 10,000 children, 25,000 adults, 9,660 older people), of them 2,250 are PwDs, through 10 community centers and by 40 protection staff and 10 MA staff (20 outreach teams), M&E teams will ensure the quality of activities quality and the application of the accountability mechanisms, considering the gender equality. HRO will ensure smooth coordination with the protection NGO partners civil local bodies, stakeholders, and camp mangers throughout the project period, in addition to the provision of MHPSS services through our ongoing projects to ensure the integration between our protection and MHPSS services.
It is worth mentioning that HRO is a member of the protection cluster and is committed to updating the protection 5WS monthly.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

After ensuring the preparedness of the project logistics and recruiting procedures, staff will be trained on GP, MA basics, inclusion of PwDs & OP, Risk education. Afterwards, teams will proceed with legal awareness raising on CSD & HLP, OP issues, Risk Education awareness sessions: HRO Outreach teams will raise the beneficiaries’ awareness on risk education using MA technical-working group IEC materials, communities reached with protection monitoring, Referrals made for protection & non-protection. Referrals will be done according to the most updated service mapping, victim assistance services including Psychosocial support, orientation & Referral to external services and case management, training the humanitarian workers and local authorities on protection principles, Risk education.M&E plan will include a target-based performance, ITT, regular site visits by the M&E Field assistants to verify & document project implementation progress including routine meetings with key informants at the field level to generate qualitative data on the effectiveness of project interventions, besides beneficiaries’ satisfaction surveys. Depending on beneficiary consent forms, photos & videos & MoVs of project activities will be documented. Complaints’ mechanisms are in place to ensure quality, transparency, and access of vulnerable groups to all available services. Key messages and IEC materials on Cholera and COVID-19 will be disseminated at the end of each session.
