Project Summary

Zimbabwe recorded its first case of COVID-19 on 20 March and had confirmed 42 cases by 17th of May 2020. The Government of Zimbabwe has also declared the COVID-19 pandemic a national disaster and have introduced several containment measures, including a national lockdown. As part of the lockdown, national borders are closed except for formal imports of essential goods such as food and medical supplies, as well as for returning citizens. Additionally, stringent internal movement restrictions are being enforced except for essential services such as the food industry, health, and security.
Prior to the COVID 19 pandemic Zimbabwe was in the grip of widespread food insecurity caused by the poor performance of the 2019/20 agricultural season which experienced delayed onset of rain, resulting in delayed planting and limited availability of water for productive uses. The weather related shocks had a negative impact on livestock due to poor availability of grazing and limited access to water. Approximately 66 000 cattle succumbed to drought related deaths. The Integrated Food Security Phase Classification (IPC) analysis conducted for the period February to June 2020, shows the proportion of rural people facing a Crisis situation (IPC Phase 3-4) is 45 percent (4,3 million). This segment of the rural population currently requires urgent humanitarian assistance.
FAO is proposing an emergency response project that will support the ongoing efforts of the Government and partners to anticipate the impact of COVID-19 on the food security and rural livelihoods economy. The project will be implemented in 4 districts targeting 10, 000 households (50,000 people) in IPC phase 3 & 4. The project aims to minimise disruptions to the food supply chain for vulnerable small holder farmers, through improved post harvest management, provision of crop and livestock inputs, raising awareness among extension workers and farmers to minimise the spread of COVID-19 and provision of PPE for agricultural extension officers (masks at a minimum).
