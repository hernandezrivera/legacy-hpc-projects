Project Summary

Since May 2017, iMMAP has been providing IM support to humanitarian responders in Ethiopia, with the support of the United States Agency for International Development, Bureau of Humanitarian Assistance (USAID/BHA). Therefore, iMMAP is prepared and well positioned to support the humanitarian ecosystem nationwide, including the response in the Northern regions.
iMMAP proposes to support the humanitarian ecosystem in Ethiopia with a 12-month project divided into two geographical areas: a Northern intervention focusing on Tigray/Afar/Amhara and an intervention covering the rest of Ethiopia.
All interventions will aim at enhancing the coordination capacities and the understanding of the humanitarian needs through three main outcomes:
1. Maintaining and scaling up the IM support to humanitarian coordination bodies to meet the increased needs for accurate information management.
2. Providing bi-monthly thematic reports to enhance the understanding and monitor trends of the deteriorating humanitarian situation in Northern Ethiopia through timely and accurate information on a wide range of operational, sectoral and programmatic topics.
3. Training and mentoring of existing INGO/NGO Information Management Officers involved in Inter-Sectoral coordination in Ethiopia.
In line with the principle of localization of the humanitarian response, iMMAP will rely both on International IM officers to palliate the recruitment/movement difficulties in Northern Ethiopia and to face the surge of humanitarian needs. Ethiopian national staff will receive capacity building to ensure that this support is sustainable. These IMOs will lead the data collection, analysis, visualization, and dissemination efforts. They will also support the Humanitarian Needs Overview and Humanitarian Response Plan (HNO / HRP) processes.
