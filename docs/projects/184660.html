Project Summary

The project aims to respond to the needs of internally displaced persons (IDPs), reduce their protection concerns and enhance their living conditions. The project will be implemented in five districts in Galmudug state: Dhusamareb, Hobyo, Celbuur, Galkacyo and Adaado. The driving factors of displacement in Galmudug include recurrent droughts, the Covid-19 pandemic, inter-clan conflicts, and more recently, the war between Galmudug forces and the armed group of ASWJ forces. As a result, the IDPs live in sub-standard, unplanned, and overcrowded sites with poor or no access to essential human services.
DEH’s proposed action seeks to address the immediate needs of the IDPs in the four districts by improving their living conditions, ensuring safe access to multi-sectorial services and strengthening effective coordination structures at the site level. At the initial stage and during the project implementation, DEH will ensure all different communities are equally consulted. The project aims to provide essential basic services to 15,333 households - 80 per cent from the IDPs, and the remaining 20 per cent would be vulnerable persons from the host community. Half of the targeted HHs would be female. During the design stage, consideration would be given to the different needs of women, men, boys and girls in a gender and conflict-sensitive manner. To reduce protection risks at the IDP site level, DEH will undertake site maintenance work, carry out routine safety checks and closely work with other partners on the ground to mitigate the GBV risk.
DEH will conduct settlement mapping exercises to collect data from all targeted settlements to identify facilities gaps in the settlements.
DEH will work closely with authorities and humanitarian partners to achieve a durable solution for the IDPs. DEH will leverage its operation capacity, local expertise, and its relationship with Galmudug authorities to make the project meet its intended goal. DEH will also participate in relevant coordination forums

1. - Projects activities

- To improve community engagement and participation, DEH involves local authorities and communities in all areas of its intervention to establish and strengthen the coordination mechanism. DEH will leverage its local presence and operation capacity to identify the strengths and gaps in the existing community governance structures. Where such gaps exist, DEH will support the formation and strengthening of Camp Management Committees (CMCs), by encouraging and advocating strong CMCs that are representative, (inclusive and diverse). Together with local authorities, CMCs and DEH will participate in the establishment of Terms of Reference to promote transparency and accountability of the committee. DEH will ensure minority groups and women are represented in the CMCs. After its establishment, DEH will deliver CMC Training Package in each targeted IDP communities in the four districts to promote self-management.- To improve the living conditions of IDPs, DEH will undertake site development, care, and maintenance activities. This will be done by procuring site maintenance toolkits, materials, and fire safety equipment. DEH will then set up/ strengthen Site Maintenance Committees (SMCs), train them in maintenance standards, fire safety and preventive measures and through cash for work schemes carry out the maintenance activity.- To mitigate the risk of GBV and other physical threats to vulnerable members of the IDP community, DEH will mount solar powered lights in areas perceived by the community to be of high risk. DEH will maintain Safety Audits in each of the target sites and would be updated (through community engagement) on a regular basis and kept current to reflect the site dynamics.- To ensure affected populations have information they need to access multi-sector services available in their IDP sites, DEH will establish regular outreach activities: coordination meetings with site committees and through mass sensitization- radio programming, signboards, text campaigns etc. Information on multi sectoral service availability would be disseminated in appropriate dialects. Construct community centers that are accessible to all populations with centers used for meetings, designated activities aimed at certain populations, and awareness sessions.- DEH will construct a community center for each IDP site that is accessible to all members without any hindrance. The centers would be used for meetings and awareness sessions.- DEH will work with appropriate government line ministries and humanitarian partners in all efforts to find durable solutions for people affected by displacement. This includes securing the necessary social infrastructures and amenities are in place before any relocation can be undertaken

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

DEH will leverage its vast local knowledge emanating from years of active presence in Galgaduud and Mudug regions and its close relationship with communities in targeted locations and local authorities to effectively implement the project. DEH has in the past resolved issues quickly and effectively. With its robust community feedback mechanism in place, DEH field staff can immediately gather information and resolve problem earlier to mitigate its impact on project operations. To eliminate incidents of exclusion and marginalization, DEH will ensure that the most vulnerable groups such as people with disabilities, women, girls, and boys have access to assistance using community-based approach in their selection. The selection criteria will be based on level vulnerabilities; therefore, priorities will be given to individual from minority communities, female or child-headed households, elderly, the disabled, and people with chronic illness. The organization will also ensure that people from minority communities and marginalized clans are represented in all project decision making process at every stage. Moreover, special attention will be given to women from minority communities who face double vulnerability- being a woman and being from minority communities/clans. DEH will establish accessible and safe complaint feedback mechanism to encourage full participation of all project’s stakeholders. DEH will work closely with local authorities and engage communities to monitor and identify potential sources of risks to mitigate violence against vulnerable communities by armed groups. DEH will conduct regular IDP site safety audits to track and identify protection risks mitigating its impact. DEH will mount solar powered lights in areas perceived by the community to be of high risk. DEH will maintain Safety Audits in each of the target sites and would be updated (through community engagement) on a regular basis and kept current to reflect the site dynamics.
