Project Summary

The response will cover Nugaal Region and target 28,500 beneficiaries (13,965 male and 14,535 female). The project will target mainly internally displaced households, refugee returnees and very poor host community households severely affected by droughts and conflicts across the populations groups of IDPS, Rural Pastoralist, Agro-pastoralist and fishing in the 4 target Districts of Garowe, Eyl, Dangorayo and Godobjiraan. The specific activities will largely include cash based interventions that will target emergency crisis affected households of above IPC 3 level. The planned cash transfers which will include both conditional and unconditional transfers aimed to increase households’ purchasing power and improve access to food and basic need based on the calculated Minimum Expenditure Basket for Nugal (USD 116 according to the latest FSNAU urban mainin markets for September 2020). In addition the response will undertake livelihood protection activities consisting of seasonal farming input, livestock support and diversification of livelihood source to enhance the recovery initiatives of drop out pastoralist, farming and fishing households just emerging from the droughts, COVID-19 pandemic livelihood related disruptions and also cyclones that have hit the Coastal Districts of Nugal Region. The design of the structures and outputs of the project will be required to conform to the required to conform to SPHERE standards including the construction. The project will utilize the Somalia Cash Working Group (CWG) recommended work norms to ensure labour output and compensation based on individual labor input. Women and the disabled shall be prioritized and given suitable jobs within the rehabilitation works. The project is also designed to main stream protection principles and to promote access, safety and dignity for all beneficiaries and in particular women and girls from IDP camps and others who have faced increased risk of SGBV since the outbreak of COVID-19 pandemic. The project is also designed to main stream protection principles and to promote access, safety and dignity for all beneficiaries and in particular women and girls from IDP camps and others who have faced increased risk of SGBV since the outbreak of COVID-19 pandemic. This will include promoting safety and dignity and avoiding to cause harm and ensure impartial and neutral services delivery all the targeted beneficiaries based purely on needs as per the protection requirements for livelihood programming. The project will equally ensure gender mainstreaming approaches remains key to its implementation. Wide consultation will also be made with the community leadership to develop an all accepted selection based on Community Based Targeting (CBT) selection criteria that will prioritize the most vulnerable households as beneficiaries prioritizing women and children access to the support. These will include women headed households, households with elderly and disable individual and large size poor families and IDP households, rural and fishing households with none or minimal sources of income. Provision of Seasonal inputs: The project will provide seasonal inputs consisting of farming seeds, harvest storage bags to cut on post-harvesting losses, fishing inputs for the fishery livelihood group in Eyr District and veterinary and feeds support to pastoralists in the 4 target Districts. The project will put in place a feedback mechanism to help get the beneficiaries share information related to the project. This will be an important component of the project as it will help guide the implementation process, capturing important details relating to changing needs by beneficiaries especially in view of the locusts and drought situation which has the potential to worsen. The feedback mechanism shall also to collect complaints expressed by the beneficiaries for action by the project management. In future the beneficiary data should be encrypted.

Project Activities

Objective 1: To improve immediate food security status for 16,600 persons (2,750 households) affected by the triple crises emergencies that include drought, desert locusts, COVID-19 pandemic effects and coastal typhoons in Nugal Region; 1. Unconditional cash transfer targeting 750 households in IPC 3 and above that need immediate food access assistance.2. Conditional Cash transfer through Cash for Work for 2,000 households in IPC 3 to protect their livelihood households and support recovery initiatives. The Cash-for-Asset will be used in the rehabilitation of the communal water pans selected with the participation of the communities in the 4 target Districts.Objective 2: To protect and restore, farming, pastoralists and fishing livelihoods from negative impact of desert locusts and typhoons destabilized 2,000 households (12,000 persons) to realized enhanced food and income sources.1) Agricultural inputs such as seeds, farming hand tools, bush clearances and modern agriculture training to 1,000 households. Women headed households will be given priority in the selection of beneficiaries. 2) Support for alternative livelihood for 1,000 households drop out households with fishing, vocational training, small scale business startup and training on Village Saving and Loan Association, IDP women taking up alternative livelihood initiatives.InputsUnconditional Cash Transfer: The project will undertake a vulnerability assessment and market assessment survey in the 4 Districts to identify the most vulnerable and also assess the ability of the local markets to provide the required commodities and also absorb the cash provided to the UCT beneficiaries. The project will utilize the nutritional survey returns from the Health and Nutrition centers to target women and under 5-years children from IDP and other host communities in the project locations. The selected beneficiaries will be registered using KoboConnect and a UCT beneficiaries register generated for the monthly cash payments. The project proposes cash payments for 3 months with the amount being determined by the stated Minimum Expenditure Basket for Nugaal Region for the period forecasted. The project will competitively hire a Money Transfer Company (Golis or Sahal) to undertake the monthly payments to the UCT beneficiaries based on verification and validation systems to ensure the payments are to the right households. A Post Distribution Monitoring (PDM) after the disbursement of the last cash payments. Conditional Cash Transfer: The project will support the rehabilitation of water pans that are critical to pastoralists and their livestock to address the need for pasture utilization in areas requiring the proper balance between pasture and water. Many of the water pans in the rangelands have also been affected by the cyclones, with many being buried by eroded soils pushed by the cyclones. The project will use Community Based Targeting (CBT) as recommended by the Somalia Cash working Group (CWG) to undertake the selection of the beneficiaries for the Cash-for Asset labor opportunities. The design of the structures will be required to conform to SPHERE standards in construction. The project will utilize the CWG recommended work norms to ensure labour output and compensation based on individual labor input. Women and the disabled shall be prioritized and given suitable jobs within the rehabilitation works. The project will competitively hire a Money Transfer Company (Golis or Sahal) to undertake the monthly payments to the 2,000 conditional cash beneficiaries. Provision of Seasonal inputs: The project will provide seasonal inputs consisting of farming seeds, harvest storage bags to cut on post-harvesting losses, fishing inputs for the fishery livelihood group in Eyr District and veterinary and feeds support to pastoralists in the 4 target Districts. Feedback Mechanism: The project will put in place a feedback mechanism to help get the beneficiaries share information.

Mitigation Measures

The project will ensure protection measures are in place as required by the protectioncluster for livelihood programming. These will include adhering to the requirements infollowing applicable labour laws and codes, Identify groups at risk of unsafelivelihoods practices, ensuring monitoring and responding to potential exploitativepractices or abuse of power by employers in livelihoods projects supportingcommunity care mechanisms to enable women’s participation in such programs. Theproject will also ensure to collect and use data disaggregated by age, gender anddiversity to effectively inform programming, ensuring that assistance and services arereaching the most vulnerable - Identify and priorities the most vulnerable groups in thecommunity and prevent discrimination or exclusion of marginalized groups. Theproject will also use KoboConnect to register all the project beneficiaries for the varioussupport thus reaching out to vulnerable persons, older people and other mobile andvocal community groups identified for support.
