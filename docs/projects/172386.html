Project Summary

The project is to support Government efforts in providing protection and assistance to refugees and asylum seekers in Zimbabwe while also ensuring containment and prevention of the spread of COVID-19. This will be guided by multi-cluster HRP strategic objectives. The project focuses on preserving unhindered access to territory to refugees and asylum seekers while ensuring fair and efficient asylum procedures. The Response activities will promote, the full enjoyment of rights for refugees and asylum seekers, ensuring provision of support to the Government for protection of refugees and asylum seekers in Zimbabwe. Multisectoral assistance and essential services will be provided to refugees and asylum seekers with particular attention to the most vulnerable individuals, and resilience and self-reliance will be strengthened among refugees and host communities. This will ensure that refugees and asylum seekers are self-reliant and improve in terms of their dietary ad nutritional requirements.
With the negative impact of COVID and the overcrowding prevalent in the refugee camp, health needs of refugees will be prioritised. This will ensure that refugees have access to basic health care services. As preventative measures , WASH in the Tongogara refugee camp will be strengthened through provision of adequate water per persons per day, construction of latrines to reduce the impact of open defecation and promote hygienic practices for refugees and asylum seekers. Education remains a key protection measure for refugees ensuring that refugee children of school going age have access to quality education. Despite the impact of COVID which have left many leaners out of school, there is need to strengthen innovative e-learning opportunities for school going children.
The capacities of national institutions will also be strengthened including supporting the government in creating conducive conditions for sustainable solutions in areas of refugee returns, in collaboration with humanitarian and development actors, as one of the key areas.
The 2021 response will target the overall refugee population, which based on the population projection, is estimated to some 15,405 refugees and asylum seekers. In addition to the provision of life-saving humanitarian assistance, the response will cover the areas of access to territory, reception, registration, refugee status determination and documentation; access to justice, and access to quality education for refugee children.

1 - Activities

The assistance will mainly be focused on refugees and asylum seekers in Tongogara refugee camp. The priority activities will include the following:• Procurement of drugs and medicines to ensure operationalization of the primary health care centre in Tongogara refugee camp• Maintenance of existing WASH infrastructure and extension of water facilities to expanded sections of the refugee camp.• Provision of e-learning facilities for refugee leaners and payment of school fees for refugee leaners to ensure continued access to education in a phased approached during the COVID pandemic• Provision of agricultural inputs for livelihood purpose and establishment of nutritional gardens for targeted refugees in the camp• Provision of domestic fuel to refugees and asylum seekers so as to reduce the impact of environmental degradation and restrict movements in view of the COVID situation.• Provision of shelter repair kits and procurement of NFIs such as soap and sanitary material for refugees and asylum seekers.• Access to general protection services including supporting refugee status determination procedures for refugees and asylum seekers.• Access to SGBV prevention services and sensitizing the community in SGBV issues.

9 - Environmental considerations
9.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project will be aligned to an environmental impact assessment to ensure that mitigation measures such as afforestation are put in place. These interventions will be also be hinged on the Three R principle of waste management in refugee camps promoting Re-use of torn empty bags for vertical gardens and recycling domestic water for micro-irrigation among other applicable practices. The action will raise awareness on water conservation. The project will also promote use of technologies that do not impact negatively on the environment. These will include renewable energy sources were possible for water pumping and biogas for cooking purposes.
