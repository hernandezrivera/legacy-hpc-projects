Project Summary

Childline is a child protection organization that specializes in the provision of child-friendly reporting mechanisms through various platforms including the 116 National Helpline. Childline provides specialized therapeutic and psychosocial support services to children in distress including child survivors of natural and manmade disasters.
Childline Zimbabwe will work at localizing humanitarian action. This project will provide specialized therapeutic services through social workers at the local drop in centres in the affected districts. It will also work through community structures and local child protection structures e.g. child care workers (CCWs) and child protection committees (CPCs). Child Friendly Spaces will be manned and run by local volunteers. The project will also operationalize Childline’s peer to peer support mechanisms in the affected communities where identified adolescents are trained in child protection and activated to provide support services to their peers.
