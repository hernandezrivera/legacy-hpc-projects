Project Summary

People with disabilities are among the most marginalized groups in Gaza strip, especially physical disabilities, hearing impaired, cochlear implant, autism disorder and learning difficulties. These group are losing their right of education and health, which is the gate to include them into the community.
In this regard, Emaar association established educational and rehabilitation centers, and implement many activities, projects, programs that meet students with disabilities needs, Emaar doesn't work independently and recognizes the importance of it's completely role with public, local institutions and ministries, it has developed the idea of this project in coordination and cooperation with Tamer institution for community education which is an active association in participating Emaar of executing educational services.
According to the imposed siege in Gaza strip and Gaza lockdown due to COVID-19 pandemic, the education process become worse and it was stopped after discovering cases inside the crowded neighborhood in 24/8/2020.
This project help students with disabilities to access the educational services and to rehabilitate the internal educational environment in Basma special school for student with disabilities by providing the school with studio and educational mobile clinic.
1-Equipping a new studio for preparing videos of educational lessons. 10 teachers needed to cover the essential subjects lessons. Emaar will prepare the studio room in Emaar complex and it will equipped with: Panasonic UX 90 4K, Traipod Milliboo 609A, Traipod WF 3978, Sun gun- Godox 308 LED 11GHT with battery and charger, 128 GB Extreme Pro, NicMic - Sarmonic Uw10, Outo Cue 19 Inch, GodoxTL-5+soft (2), Laptop HP 16G Cor i7 (8), Hp Monitor N246(4), HP 290 G2
2- equipping an educational mobile clinic which able to reach the disabled students in the marginalized area who unable to access the online learning as a result of their disabilities, their families' economic situation, the lack of electricity. This clinic will provide educational, rehabilitation and psychological services in hot and marginalized area in ways which can increase students' knowledge and make them part of the educational process during emergencies period. The clinic will cover khanyounis and Rafah governorate, the target group will distributed according to their age and their places in coordination and cooperation with schools which work with people with disabilities in the southern .
The clinic need (16 employee to work 3 days in khanyounis and 3 days in rafah (2 psychologist, 2 rehabilitation specialist, 12 teachers for the essential subjects:
-Held group learning sessions in open places in addition to individual session, these sessions will cover the basic subjects for 1st to 9th grade, and students will be supported with printed self-learning material.
-Provide individual rehabilitation sessions for 400 students.
-Provide individual psychological sessions According to students need
-Provide parents' awareness raising session for 2000 ones.
The clinic will be equipped with teaching material, hearing and speech rehabilitation devices.
3000 beneficiary will benefited: 1000 students with disabilities, 2000 students' parents.

1 - Needs

Persons with disabilities face discrimination and barriers that restrict them from participating in society on an equal basis with others. They are denied their rights to be included in the general school system, to be employed, to live independently in the community, to move freely, to vote, to participate in sport and cultural activities, to enjoy social protection, to access justice, to choose medical treatment and to enter freely into legal commitments such as buying and selling property. A disproportionate number of persons with disabilities live in developing countries, often marginalized and in extreme poverty.In recent years, there has been a revolutionary change in approach, globally, to close the gap and ensure that persons with disabilities enjoy the same standards of equality, rights and dignity as everyone else. According to humanitarian needs overview 2020 – Gaza strip:• Psychosocial distress and mental disorders affect both adults and children. Twenty-two per cent of 1.9 million people living in Gaza have been identified in need of MHPSS 270,689 children (50 % girls and 50 % boys). 148,995 adults (55% women and 45% men) are affected by psychosocial distress and mental disorders in Gaza.• A high level of psychosocial stress among both students and adults, with almost one half of adults (48.9 per cent) experiencing poor well-being (63 per cent of these warranting further screening for depression) and almost 30 per cent of children experiencing serious difficulties.• In the Gaza Strip, the deteriorating humanitarian situation and living conditions, have affected 399,000 (193,000F, 206,000M) school and kindergarten (KG) age children and teachers, of whom 15,500 are CWDs.• 983,623 women and 991,428 children who are exposed to public health risks associated with poor water quality, poor wastewater collection and treatment and lack of proper hygiene material.• 399,000 (193,000F, 206,000M) school and kindergarten (KG) age children and teachers, of whom 16,000 are CWDs who face challenges in accessing quality education in a safe environment.• 27 per cent of CWDs (6-17 years) were not enrolled in education (32 per cent in the West Bank, compared to 24 per cent in Gaza). According to the Palestinian Central Bureau of Statistics- 2017 (PCBS) • Around 110 thousand individuals suffer from at least one disability and constitute 2.4% in the Gaza Strip.• 71% of all disabled individuals aged 15 years and above in Palestine had never been enrolled at school; 35.5% in the West Bank and 42.2% in Gaza Strip. In addition, 33.8% of all disabled individuals had dropped out of school; 37.0% in the West Bank and 27.1% in Gaza Strip. Illiteracy rates were 53.1% of all disabled individuals; 51.5% in the West Bank and 56.3% in Gaza Strip. • In Palestine 22.2% of disabled individuals dropped out of school due to environmental and physical obstacles: 23.2% in the West Bank and 19.4% in the Gaza Strip. Of these, 21.7% were male and 22.9% female.• Mobility is the most prevalent disability and constitutes 71.4%, Visual disability is the second most prevalent with 10.6 % while hearing impairment is the third most prevalent with 7.8%. In 2015 Emaar held 5 workshops with specialists and official bodies to identify the needs of people with disabilities in coordination with Khanyounis Municipality and civil institutions working in the field of disability. The most important findings and recommendations is the strategic plan for people with disabilities in khan younis which has been prepared in 2015 in coordination with the official and where focused on education, health, social and economic empowerment. The main objective addressed by the plan is to enhance the role of the educational sector in providing and facilitating access to an inclusion educational service for all age groups according to their capacities in order to contribute to their active participation in the field of education.

It include liquid soup, hand sanitizer 70 % alcohol, face masks, thermometer, wipes, toothbrush and toothpaste. #An equipped educational mobile clinic help to reach students with disabilities8.By using the mobile clinic to move teachers and the teaching materials to the marginalized areas, the teachers will held face to face group learning sessions in an open places in addition to individual learning sessions at student’s homes, these sessions will cover the basic subjects for 1st to 6th grade, and students will be supported with printed self-learning material. 9.Reach 100 out of school student (8-12 years old) in the marginalized area in Rafah and Khanyounis governorate who leave school due to their disabilities and support them with basic education services, Tamer institution team (special education specialist) will participate to reach this group by using the clinic and provide this group with special education services which will empower them to be able to enroll the special schools.10.Provide individual rehabilitation sessions for hearing impaired and cochlear implant students. Rehabilitation sessions is important and show more success with student in young age (6-12 years). The sessions will be held inside the Mobile clinic, the mobile clinic will be supported with hearing and speech rehabilitation devices which already used in Basma center (Emaar has Basma Center for Audiology and Speech Therapy which was established in 2010 and licensed by the Ministry of Health with a registration No. 5/2012 and by the Ministry of Education with No. 937. The center equipped devices for the rehabilitation process: audiometer, tympanometry, nasometer, video voice, autoacousitc, ABR p-25). The rehabilitation sessions ensure a continuous communication between the rehabilitation specialists and children with disabilities and empower students to be able and ready to learn (in the case of stopping the rehabilitation process for a long time, it may cause a setback to the child, which is difficult to remedy later).The target group distribute as (400 girls and boys who suffer from hearing and speech disorder aged between 6-12 years old)11.provide individual and group psychological support sessions according to students and parents’ needs. The individual sessions will be held inside the clinic, and the group session will be held in safe places with a participation from Emaar and Tamer team. (Emaar association has al Amal Center for Care and Psychological Consultations which was established in 2009 and licensed by the Ministry of Health with a registration No. 1/2013. The center aims to provide urgent social needs in terms of counseling and psychotherapy. According to the approved therapy protocol in the center and the standard MHPSS, the specialized team use IQ tests, different type of questionnaires and electroencephalography (EEG) device to evaluate the electrical activity in the brain.)The target group distribute as (50 men and 50 women from students' parent , 200 girls and boys who aged between 10-14 years old)To improve the quality of the psychological therapy during the project:- Provide a training to project staff in basic psychosocial support services and protection need of children- The specialist will use the approved pre and post questionnaire, the pre questionnaire will help the specialist to assess their need. After completing the sessions the specialist will evaluate the cases, using post questionnaire to decide completion of the recommended sessions or to continuity of psychotherapy. #2000 parents benefiting from awareness campaign13.
