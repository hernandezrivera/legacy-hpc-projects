Project Summary

The project aim at revitalization of small businesses that had been crippled totally or partially as a result of current socioeconomic situation in the target locations. In order to address the many gaps in livelihoods opportunities and income generation that have arisen in the target locations due to the ongoing crisis, IhsanRD is proposing to support 864 male and female HHs with vocational training on small businesses. Vocational training program on small businesses aim to provide the target BNFs with technical knowledge on: Business planning based on community need assessment, Budgeting and Economic Feasibility for Small Businesses, Marketing and Value chain, Safety and Security Principles during Implementing of Small Businesses and Respecting the Environmental Principles in Designing and implementing Stages of Small Businesses. Out of 864 HHs who received the vocational training, 719 HHs will receive small grants worth to 1000 USD to start their own projects. The accepted projects for funding must demonstrate a high scientific, technical and managerial quality as mentioned in project activities below.
