Project Summary

The proposed project is complementary to ongoing KAALO WASH project in Nugaal region in Puntland and to response the existing WASH gaps to drought affected people in Puntland, KAALO proposes to provide WASH interventions and resilience strengthening to the target groups men, women and children and institutional facilities during this critical period of water shortage and Acute Water Diarrhea (AWD) risks in providing safe water through water voucher, constructing/rehabilitating WASH infrastructures for sustained access. Hygiene awareness, training on management & maintenance and Disaster Risk Reduction targeting affected people with equal access to boys, girls, men and women.,
The committee need further training on hygiene and sanitation as well as distribution of sanitation tools. Sensitization within members of WASH committees are needed, community education activities on hygiene and proper use of latrines etc. need more emphasis, KAALO intends to complement this with the ongoing WASH project to cover all the existing gaps in the targeted areas. The project will be implemented six villages in Nugaal and Mudug region covering eyl, Garowe, burtinle and Galkacyo districts.
Accountability : KAALO will develop elaborate beneficiary complain and feedback tool. Project beneficiaries and stakeholders will be given 1 day workshop on how to use the complaint /feedback system to enhance transparency and accountability. Community ownership of the project will be done through formation of structures such as Beneficiary Representatives, WASH Committees, and CHPs (consisting of women and men).
implementation plan : KAALO will recruit experienced WASH project staff that will ensure effective community participatory approach in project planning, implementation & management. The WASH Manager will be responsible overall the project implementation while the WASH Engineer will be responsible for the design of the rehabilitation/construction works and ensure the quality of the work is standardized.
Disaster risk reduction : This project objective is to improve WASH sectors resilience to emergencies and disasters while also covering the urgent needs of affected people. The project will invest on improving WASH infrastructure through rehabilitation/ construction of strategic priority boreholes and increase the capacity of WASH committee and staff on disaster risk reduction.

Needs

The 2018 Deyr rains (sept-December) have started late in a number of areas and are projected to be below average. A poor Deyr rain would mark the fifth consecutive season of below-average rainfall in Somalia and Puntland in particular. Poor rainfall performance will limit pasture generation and water availability, reduce rainfed crop harvests, and exacerbate already high levels of acute food insecurity. A risk of Famine continues through the end of the year in the worst affected areas: in a worst-case scenario where there is a significant interruption to current food assistance programs and higher prices. Areas of greatest concern include northeast and some IDP populations (FSNAU Sep. 2018 Report). Depletion of livestock assets due to distress sales and mortality has contributed to increased indebtedness and destitution among many pastoralists. As a result, most rural livelihood zones of Somalia are classified as Crisis (IPC Phase 3) or Emergency (IPC Phase 4). An estimated 5 million people in need of humanitarian assistance, 3.1 million require urgent life-saving assistance. More than 800,000 people are in Emergency (IPC phase 4), one step below famine, reflecting a sharp spike in the number of most vulnerable people, up from 83,000 people in Emergency (IPC4). Displacement from rural to urban areas has significantly impacted the demographic make-up of the country, leading to urban crowding and putting additional pressure on scarce resource. Thus, the main problem, which this project focus, is to address the extensive very limited supply of basic WASH services available to drought driven IDPs and pastoral communities. KAALO implemented WASH program in Nugal and Mudug regions and its recent assessment conducted 3-4 October 2018 revealed limited access to WASH services,. Children who lack access to safe water and the communities’ poor knowledge of good hygiene and sanitation practices are more prone to acute water diarrhea than those who access to safe water.

Activities

1. Water:1.1: Provide safe water to 7,500 (1837men, 2273women, 1530boys and 1860girls) drought affected pastoralists through water voucher system, which they can redeem water at a specified water vendors (45liters/hh) for a period of 30 days.1.2: Rehabilitate 9 berkads: The rehabilitation activities of the protected berkads include de-silting, repairing the cracked parts of the walls, floors and roofs. 3 of the rehabilitated berkads will be equipped with solar powered pumps, construction of water tanks, water kiosks and animal troughs to avoid the contamination of the water and reduce wastage of water. The facilities will be designed for the use of men, women, children, elderly and disabled persons equally. Activity 1.3: purchase and install 5 fiber water tanks (each 10m3 capacity) in five IDP camps and supply water through piping system to reduce the burden of women and girls.Activity 1.4: Train 14 WASH committees composing of 98 members sharing equally men and women on better water resource management & maintenance, HHWT use, O&M and collection of water revenues for the sustainability of the water sources. As cross cutting, held one-day workshop with 40 community members with equal participation of men and women (20men and 20women) on Disaster and Risk Reduction (DRR) and conflict resolution mechanisms to avoid the outbreak of AWD/cholera diseases and conflict caused by the drought respectively.2. Sanitation:Activity 2.1: Construct 12 gender sensitive institutional twin block latrines in 8 schools and 4 in health facilities (each facility will have 1 twin block latrine for female and 1 for male) with lockable doors, septic tanks for desludging purposes when filled and hand washing facilities to encourage washing hands with soap practices. Activity 2.2: Desludge filled 40 gender sensitive IDP latrines. The action includes: desludging the filled septic tanks, repairing the broken parts.

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

The main risks facing the target populations is clan dynamics which causes exclusion and other challenges within the community, and its based racial discrimination of being minority and powerlessness, KAALO will mitigate this challenge through proper participation of members in planing and during the implementation of the project , being local NGO and working in this area for quite sometime will enable us to make sure that both hosts and IDPs are equally participated and coexisted

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

The main risks facing the target populations in terms of gender and age culture of male dominated socity where most of the household are led by males which causes exclusion and other challenges within the community, and its based racial discrimination of being minority and powerlessness, KAALO will mitigate this challenge through proper participation of members in planing and during the implementation of the project. shortage of safe drinking water and other wash facilities also effects women and children in different way as they remain those given least when it comes to access water and other infrastructures during g crises. KAALO will make sure that most mobilizers at the field level will more gender sensitive to fight against this exclusion problem

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

The absence of wash facilities and water shortage in most areas has increased risk of conflict , KAALO will contribute also to reduced armed conflicts and violence in the target areas particularly in Mudug region where conflicts are more likely to happen as result of this. KAALO will work closely with government and other relevant authorities plus UNICEF in the provision of emergency wash services to these vulnerable persons
