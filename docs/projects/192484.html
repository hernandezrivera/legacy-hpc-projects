Project Summary

The protracted impacts of the current socio-economic and health crises in Lebanon continue to hinder the ability of vulnerable Lebanese and Migrants to meet their immediate food needs in a context exacerbated by soaring food prices, lack of income sources and dwindling supplies. A succession of recent shocks and stressors (economic and financial collapse, COVID-19, Beirut port explosions) have incapacitated state institutions to deliver on essential services, plunged ever greater numbers into poverty and vulnerability
and increased people’s reliance on humanitarian assistance to make ends meet. To this end our intervention at SPHERE Building Tomorrow will aim to;
Improve food availability through in kind food assistance and support all for a basic level of survival of the individuals through various modalities.
We aim to outreach a targeted 2000 vulnerable of Lebanese and Migrants families in Beirut city on a biweekly basis. The targeted population is;
1. People with low incomes ( Average of 600,000 LBP)
2. People living with disabilities
3. Survivoes of gender based violence (GBV) survival
4. families impacted by the economic crisis and Beirut Blast
5. Women headed household (HH)
6. Child headed household (HH) of 15 to 18 years
7. Elderly of 65 years above, disabled, chronically sick)
The vulnerable communities will be identified and selected through close consultation with our organization team on ground, to find the most vulnerable; those will then be prioritized to receive tangible in-kind food boxes within our humanitarian field intervention that includes awareness and recreational activities. The food assistance will ensure that vulnerable Lebanese communities and migrants in Beirut city can meet their basic food and nutritional needs as well as survival and minimum living standards, without resorting to negative coping mechanisms or strategies that compromise their health and dignity and essential food assets.
The proposed project will run during 6 months (26 weeks). Each 2 weeks, Aprox. one hundred of boxes (Including the initial and essential food assets) will be distributed to selected and eligible individuals families in focal placements located in Beirut (Ashrafieh & Karm El Zaitoun, Karantina & Mdawar, Tarik Jdideh & Mazraa, Borj Abi Haidar,Barbir, etc...), where awareness and recreational activities will also be implemented. These placements will be ensured with the coordination of stakeholders and humanitarian actors.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

Outcome 1: Improve food availability through in kind food assistance, targeting 2000 Lebanese and Migrants vulnerable individuals and familiesActivities: The Food Consumption Score (FCS) is a composite indicator that considers diet diversity, frequency of consumption and nutrient value of the food groups consumed over a recall period of seven days. According to this score, 2000 targeted households would be classified into three categories: poor, borderline and acceptable food consumption measurements. Outcome 2: Awareness building for 2000 vulnerable individuals and households and targeted communities alongside with the social and recreational designed activities.Activities: Eligible families will be invited to attend social and recreational designed activities while receiving the food assistance in several focal placements located in Beirut

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

• Fuel Crisis and Electricity Interruption: At least one week before the activity, we should ensure that the fuel and gas availability and the transportation for the beneficiaries' is covered to ensure their safe arrivals.• Special attention should be given to prioritize service provision to children with a disability, and females headed-households• COVID 19 Pandemic & PRP: We will apply and commit to the COVID 19 & PRP International measurements or apply remote modalities when deemed necessary.• Safety and Security Conditions: We are ready to reschedule the timeframe of the training or adjust/having access on alternative placements relevant to the training requirements and individuals to ensure individuals safety and secure environments.• The low participation of men participation in CP activities has been a common issue in social work and that has been addressed by proactively seeking to sustain the equivalency between the targeted female and males in our selection process

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

SPHERE Building Tomorrow will conduct a preliminary outreach/vulnerability assessment and the Food Consumption Score (FCS) to benchmark the level of awareness and need in its selected beneficiaries'. We will then employ a month and half re-assessment matrix to track progress as well as verify whether the project is achieving its outcomes on individuals and collective level (pre-posttests, assignments'), as well as conducting a final assessment to track outcome achievability.All activities will be documented with possible audio and video recording, as per the consent of the attendees, to ensure the sustainability of the visibility. All documentation will be retained for the purposes of tracking and transparency. SPHERE Building Tomorrow is committed to providing a final report detailing the project phases and all actions taken throughout the commitment phase, as part of its M&E process.
