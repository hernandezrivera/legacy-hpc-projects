Project Summary

Terre des Hommes Italy response holistically meets the needs of most affected children and their families, whose needs are being identified by TDH IT Teams deployed to the field since the very day after the explosions.
As the assessment is ongoing, TDH IT will deploy teams to track and assist people who moved immediately after the Blast and, according to the first information gathered, are likely to move again during the next weeks.
To provide comprehensive and holistic assistance, activities are designed to impact on children and families on several levels:
Outreach/tracking: Throughout the outreach, teams will undertake the tracking of the individuals to identify who is where and what are their needs, while delivering awareness on CP, COVID prevention, available services etc.
Community-based PSS will address both the psychological and social needs of children and support them in recovering; during CBPSS, teams will identify those in need of Case Management
Psychological First Aid (PFA), Family Counselling and Parenting/Caregiver support will be delivered to children and families to mitigate effects and acute consequences of the explosions, promote adoption of positive coping strategies and give people space to express their fears
Case management: urgent aid will be provided to CP medium and high-risk cases – complementary with the services provided by other humanitarian actors
Multi-purpose cash assistance and delivery of PPEs, hygiene kits etc.: to cushion the socio-economic consequences of the ongoing emergencies MCAP will be provided to most in need families. Beneficiaries will also be provided with Hygiene kits and PPEs as a preventive measure to avoid a further spread of the pandemic.
CP activities will be delivered by professional staff and designed according to SOPs and guidelines developed by Tdh IT in Lebanon in partnership with UNICEF within the Syrian crisis response.

Plan fields

1 - Needs

Psycho-social distress and negative behavioral changes are likely to appear as result of the traumatic event. From the first data collected, Psychological assistance appears to be needed to all affected communities equally for both children and adults. Psychological Trauma is the main CP concern directly connected with the Blast, but several negative behaviors may arise at household and community level: child labor, exploitation, physical violence, and sexual violence are likely to increase in the next future as result of the increased distress and negative coping strategies.Accompanying children and their families in the initial phase of the response and support them in elaborating the trauma will have a durable effects on their wellbeing and provide them with the tools needed to develop positive copying mechanisms.In addition to the above, mainstreaming of Child Safeguarding and PSEA is one of the main component protection actors should focus on. Newly recruited staff from all NGOs, needed in the response to Beirut Blast, need to be adequately informed and trained to mitigate CP and SEA risk. Simultaneously, insuring adequate Self-care for front-liners is extremely needed, especially for staff that has to deal with children’s traumas and have to guarantee their psychological wellbeing.

2 - Activities

In the first six months, Terre des Hommes Italy will implement a comprehensive set of activities aiming at improving the wellbeing of children at increased risk of child protection concerns and at promoting positive copying mechanisms:- NEED ASSESSMENT on the psychological impact of the Blast on the wellbeing of childrenPSYCHOLOGICAL FIRST AID for caregivers and children to release stress and reflect on the trauma experienced;- CREATION OF CHILD-FRIENDLY SAFE SPACES- PSYCHOSOCIAL SUPPORT ACTIVITIES for children affected by the Blast - CAREGIVER PROGRAM; - CASE MANAGEMENT and REFERRAL – Non specialized support for children at medium and high risk of protection concerns; - EMERGENCY and/or MULTIPURPOSE CASH ASSISTANCE – provision of cash assistance addressing or mitigating serious harm to an individual or HH for the most vulnerable families whose children are followed by the case management; - AWARENESS ON CHILD PROTECTION AND PSEA; - PSYCHOLOGICAL SUPPORT FOR TDH-IT STAFF
