Project Summary

The project will improve access and utilization of essential lifesaving interventions including vaccination services for host communities, IDPs, refugees, Returnees, and vulnerable population in priority counties of all the 10 states and 3 Administrative areas through integrated outreaches. The targeted counties were prioritized based on their risk ranking and those in priority one and two respectively. The project locations are:
• Jonglei State: Fangak, Canal/Pigi and Uror, Akobo, Ayod, Bor South, Duk, Nyirol and Twic East.
• Pibor Administrative area: Pibor
• Unity State: Leer, Mayendit, Panyijiar, Rupkona, Guit, Mayom and Parieng.
• Northern Bahr Gazal: Aweil East, Aweil North, Aweil South
• Warrap: Twic, Tonj East, Tonj North
• Abyei Administrative area
• Upper Nile: Renk, Malakal, Luakpiny/ Nasir, Baliet,Fashoda, Maban ,Maiwut, Manyo, Panyikang, Ulang and Longechuk
• Lakes State: Awerial, Rumbek North,
• Eastern Equatoria: Lafon, Kapoeta East.
Activities implementation will be integrated under Health Sector Transformation Project (HSTP):
• Curative and case management for cholera outbreak in those high risk counties
• Emergency measles vaccination for children aged 6 months - 15years
• Scaling up of routine immunization through outreaches, mobiles and periodic intensification of routine immunization.
• Health education and awareness to promote healthy life style and essential health seeking behaviors in regards to cholera and Monkey pox outbreaks.
This emergency project will be integrated into Health Sector Transformation Project (HSTP) and the implementation will take place through UNICEF HSTP partnership in those selected locations.
This project will target 5,949,261 catchment population in 39 counties across the 10 states of South Sudan which are in priority number one and two per cluster coordination. This population includes girls, boys, women, and men in IDP settings and host communities in the 39 vulnerable, disease prone locations. The total number of expected beneficiaries includes 1,701488 females and 1570604 males. Children under five includes 1249344 (Female 649,659 and male is 599684). Eligible children and pregnant women will be reached through outreaches, mobiles, and periodic intensification of routine immunization through implementing partners of health sector transformation project (HSTP). There will be a reactive or preemptive measles vaccination campaigns in an event of outbreak in the targeted 39 locations, whilst supporting Tetanus Toxoid Maternal Newborn Tetanus Elimination (MNTE) campaign implementation in the very hard-to-reach locations. 1. Needs

most vulnerable populations in conflict- and flood-affected areas, prioritizing women, children, the elderly, and people with disabilities. Disease burden data, from District Health Information System and early warning, alert and response system, various initial rapid needs assessment reports and IPC classifications guided the identification of high-need regions. Response prioritization considers factors contributing to community vulnerability, including limited healthcare facilities, access barriers, health facility functionality, flood impacts, and caseloads of returnees and IDPs. The response strategy aligns with the HSTP and integrates community consultations through ISNA. Continuous feedback mechanisms, joint supervision, quarterly reviews, and regular monitoring visits will enable the cluster to adapt to changing needs, focusing resources on the most critical areas.
