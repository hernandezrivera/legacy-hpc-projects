Project Summary

HAPEN has for years been involved in provision of services to displaced populations in camps across Somalia. Through this project, the organization proposes to utilize its engagement with camps to expand its role to include site coordination and management in Lower Juba and Gedo regions. The targeted IDP camps are located in Kismayo, Afmadow, Doolow and Luuq districts.
Specifically, HAPEN will a) Conduct site planning and preparation to increase existing capacity of site committees (b Coordinate and lead improved provision of humanitarian assistance in IDP settlements; (c) Improve communication with affected communities, including through complaint and feedback mechanisms, community sensitization/mobilization, ensure participation of vulnerable groups (women/youth) and that the existing committees are maintained and functional; (d) Information Management including supporting CCCM activities and extending CCCM cluster’s DTM unit to areas where partners are not working. Also included are additional surveys/assessments upon consultation with the cluster to generate information to inform planning and facilitation of services. HAPEN will monitor the displacement conditions in all the target districts especially where there are no CCCM coordination structures in place or no IDP assessment of any kind has been conducted.
The needs of women, girls, men, boys of all ages, the elderly, people living with disabilities and minorities will be identified and addressed throughout the planning, implementation and monitoring processes. The project will take into account variations in gender, ethnic origin, physical ability and age that predispose the target population to sexual exploitation and abuse, extremes of weather and diseases and differently influence their coping strategies. The purpose of this will be to prevent the situation of the already vulnerable groups from deteriorating further.
Contingency planning will be part of the project to ensure that HAPEN and other actors can quickly adapt to and respond to effects of conflict, drought, flooding and disease outbreaks on IDPs.
Relevant consultations will be made with the Protection cluster and GBV sub¬cluster, particularly in site development, site management and the DTM, to enhance this project’s response to, and building of capacity to address the special needs of the IDP populations in Lower Juba and Gedo regions, Jubaland.

Mitigation Measures

Risk of no access to or awareness of protection assistance or referral services for survivors of protection incidents (i.e. GBV, child protection, evictions, explosive hazards, etc.)-HAPEN will at the earliest opportunity and regularly provide information and raise awareness about camp/site rules, security management, facilities and services available equally to women, girls, boys and men.-In order to cater different literacy levels, posters, community boards, camp committees, door-to-door, poster, social media and pictogram will be used to ensure all members in the community are reached.-During planning, ensure the design of the site allows for services and facilities to be accessed by all, including persons with reduced mobility -Work with service providers to ensure that infrastructure where necessary is adapted to accommodate people with reduced mobility including smooth and non-slippery access paths and fit ramps for wheelchair access.-Place persons with impaired mobility and their families close to essential facilities Risk that marginalized groups, persons with disabilities, women, boys and girls, do not participate in consultations or decision-making processes for the project or are excluded from humanitarian services/assistance/activities-Ensure that both women and men are part of management structure and are fully engaged in the management of camp facilities.-Consult women, men, boys, girls, persons with disabilities, older persons and marginalised personsduring assessments to collect accurate information about their specific needs and preferences forthe design and management of camp settings.-Set-up camp committees to represent camp residents in camp governance structures. Ensure members are representative of all groups within the community (ie. gender, age, ethnicity, socioeconomic group,disability).-Ensure the training of camp management teams in coordination and management includes protection mainstreaming. Risk that beneficiaries are not accessing/using complaint and feedback mechanisms-Set up mechanisms for submitting feedback that do not require the beneficiary exposing themselvesto project staff.-RESPOND to complaints, regardless of whether corrective measures can/need to be put in place.-Organize awareness raising sessions so that people know how it works.-Consider a joint feedback mechanism with other sectors to minimize confusion. Risk of land disputes and tenure insecurity in targeted project area-HAPEN will familiarize itself with existing land and property tenure arrangements, including statutory/legislative and customary access rights to land when selecting sites. -Advocate with local authorities and/or property owners to obtain formal agreement to use the land, as a precaution to reduce the risk of eviction, rental costs or conflict erupting due to lack of clarity on these issues.-Ensure camp boundaries and clearly marked and documented to avoid future ownership disputes. -Include host communities in decisions on site location and planning
