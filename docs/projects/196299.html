Project Summary

Based on the 2022 Humanitarian Needs Overview, there are 6.9 million displaced people and 14.7 million people in need. Therefore, the Takatof Youth Association conducted a needs assessment study for the areas most in need of intervention, especially in the protection sector in the city of Deir ez-Zor and its countryside (questionnaires - individual interviews - focused discussion sessions), and accordingly, three areas were identified (Al-Muhajireen neighborhood in Deir ez-Zor city - adoption in the countryside of Deir ez-Zor Al-Gharbi - Al-Salihiyah in the eastern countryside of Deir Ezzor) as areas of recent returnees (adoption 8000 returnees / 2022), and suffer from a clear lack of services encouraging returnees to settle, such as education, livelihoods, legal services and humanitarian aid, especially in the current economic conditions.
Therefore, Takatuf Shabab will work through this proposal with UN agencies to play a role in supporting the return of internally displaced persons, reducing vulnerabilities, strengthening the community, protecting the displaced population, returnees and affected communities, meeting their immediate needs, and improving the situation in the main return area through community centers in return area.
These centers provide safe spaces for women, men, girls, boys, children and people with disabilities from diverse backgrounds to carry out recreational activities and access all protection services, which include legal aid, education, livelihood training, psychosocial support, prevention of gender-based violence, Risk mitigation, child protection intervention.
These centers will provide protection services through the following activities:
• Reducing the risks of sexual violence, exploitation and abuse and improving the quality of response by involving community members in preventing violence, sexual exploitation and gender-based violence through awareness campaigns to support positive transformations in the relationship between the sexes, and ending harmful traditional practices such as early and forced marriage.
• Strengthen child protection through case management, including referrals through the implementation of comprehensive case management to follow up on child protection issues. Also, by targeting community members with awareness-raising and capacity-building sessions to ensure community participation in the prevention of child protection risks.
• Promote service to people Through psychological support activities, clinics and community centers, and involving them in safe places for children, youth and the elderly.
• Enhancing the role of women by granting small projects in the subsistence sector that contribute to enhancing sources of income and self-reliance.
• Enhancing the ability of crisis-affected children to have safe and equitable access to education through online education programmes
• Forming restorative and compensatory classes and building the capacity of the educational staff to prepare them for students

Also, most of the region’s population is in agriculture.There is a large percentage of female breadwinners.It is one of the most prominent social problems that the region suffers from1- Lack of job opportunities2- Early marriage3- Low level of education4- Exposure to domestic violenceand psychological pressures. In addition to a large percentage of people with disabilities, as the number of people with disabilities reached more than 200 people who lack mobility aid services.The region suffers from a lack of interventions by non-governmental organizations, as interventions were limited to the distribution of food baskets.Also in the city of Deir Ezzor, education needs were assessed and a great demand for training courses was observed. so, the association seeks through its training center to provide training services for young people up to the age of 24 years.
