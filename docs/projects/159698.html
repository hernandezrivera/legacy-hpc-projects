Project Summary

The project is intended to address the humanitarian healthcare needs of the vulnerable communities in Garasbaley of Banadir Region, Cadale and Balcad District of Middle Shabelle Region. The target community lack fixed facility with sufficient supplies and human resources to provide basic health care services to serve the vulnerable populations. The direct beneficiaries of the project are 41,595 this include 3744 pregnant and lactating women, 8389 (4278 girls and 411 boys) under five children and 6,655 (3,061 males and 3,594 females) who will benefit from free consultations and treatment for common illnesses and routine health education at the facility level. Through this initiative, Aid Vision will open two health facilities and further scale up Garasbaley MCH that will offer life-saving primary health care services that include but not to limited free Antenatal and Postnatal services, referral for risky pregnancies, free 24 hours skilled deliveries, consultation and treatment of both under and over fives for common illnesses, immunization services, early detection and treatment of communicable diseases both at the community and facility level. The project has also component of community health workers who will do active case finding, defaulter tracing, hygiene promotion and messaging at the community level. For purpose of the sustainability of the project, Aid Vision will employ community based participatory approach through strengthen community capacity by capacity building own staff, local Authority staff, MoH staff and training of CHWs and established community health committees. This approach ensures at the end of the project a strong cadre of community based structures are built for community ownership and sustainability of the program. Further, Aid Vision will closely work with other agencies in the region to better coordinate humanitarian interventions and prevent duplication.

Mitigation Measures

Aid Vision will ensure all minority and majority as well as well as disable groups are involved in the implementation of the project and be able to transparent in terms of employment. Community health workers selection and training will also consider gender sensitivity including men and women. All this will ensure gender and equity issues are addressed among the community members. Aid Vision will collect gender-disaggregated data by sex and age of relevant data related to health activities to ensure they have equal access to quality health care services.Aid Vision will ensure that all segments of the affected population including pregnant and lactating women, children aged 6-59 months have equal access to health assistance. The minority and marginalized communities who are at risk of being neglected/ excluded are also given quality and equal health care services to prevent common illnesses. Aid Vision will also advocate community participation in the implementation of the program. This will help in getting complaints and feedback responses that enhances effective decision making and promote accountability to the affected populationAid Vision proposed project is designed in way that prioritizes the rights, safety and dignity of the beneficiaries. The target communities are fully involved and aware of the roles of each stakeholder where each sees the other as partners and equals. The proposed project will not discriminate against any beneficiary and will enable equal and impartial assistance and services for all. Targeting of vulnerable children and adults will be done on basis of their specific needs. The staff will be inducted on their duties and responsibilities and particularly the rights of the beneficiaries to ensure there are no any form of patient exploitation at the facilities and Aid Vision offices. Watertight mechanisms will be put in place to ensure served populations voices are heard and can file their complaints without being victimized.
