Project Summary

GFO (Great Family Organization) is a voluntary non profitable agency works to contribute in the humanitarian assistance in South Darfur State beside other services providers since 2005
-The current humanitarian situations within both Shattay and Kubum localities (73,000 displaced people recorded in the latest conflict) are passing fatal humanitarian environment, recently received newly displaced those who lacks basic services , raising a flag of risk that might occur to harm the communities in their settlements whatsoever they are (IDPS, Returnees or Host community) forcing the service providers and the entire array of stakeholders to apply the durable solutions as: Aiming to diminish gradually the displacement of the affected communities as well as to address IASC framework that is under pinned the three options obtaining safety, dignity, discrimination, within such environment the ideal groups (children, women, PWD and elderly) were exposed to inaccessibility to protected education and inadequate human rights, so, it addresses gender marker Code 2A (gender mainstreaming that makes concerns and experiences of women, girls ,boys and men on integral dimension of the core elements of the project (gender responsive activities, gender related outcomes), which influencing in promoting and flowing of the gender equality in to the: implementation, monitoring and evaluation of the project.
- To contextualize the critical situation of the targets, the vulnerable groups are extremely missing a lifesaving Components (education and respective response activities)
-The project is aiming to implement activities related to:
Education activities as: (To construct and equipped 18 classrooms, 6 school offices, rehabilitate and equipped 4 classrooms, Construct 6 latrine units, 48 PTA members and 60 teachers trained in Shattaya and Kubum as well as WFP to provide FFE to help creating learning environment to the students).
The project is designed to serve estimated 9,734 pupils entirely (5,123 manage to continue learning after the latest conflict, while 4,611 displaced) , in reference to UNICEF/some survey conducted in 2019 and indirect beneficiaries will be 18,825 person as catchment population.
As of COVID 19, the project will conduct 30 awareness sessions of prevention, distribute 25,000 Face masks to the target communal unities.

1 - Needs

Kubum and Shattay are neighboring localities witnessed the crossing of hug number of IDPs passing toward Kass, Nyala and other IDPs' camps in South Darfur during the Darfur 2003/2004 conflict . Many of these IDPs find refuge in Kubum and Shattay localities since 2004. Humanitarian actor: Internationals such as NCA (ACT-Caritas), CARE Int., WVI, UNICEF, WFP, WHO. And National Actors: GFO, SRCS, provided humanitarian emergency services up to 2009. Some the main INGOs stop giving services because of lack funding.Education sector was one the main sector which affected by the withdrawal of the NGOs beside health and nutrition and Wash.the number of the children IDPs became very high compare to the host community and they have opportunity to go to school because of the lack of premises, teachers and their living situation as poor IDPs.The education Working group including UNICEF,SMoE and NGO survey report of 2019 identified the education as a priority No 1 in South Darfur.- 9,300 children (60% female and 40% male) in school are totally in need of main education materials such as books notebooks. 80% of the existing assessed classrooms need to be rehabilitated, but more than 40% of the pupils have classrooms with no educational materials and shortage of teachers.Children find difficulty to find water for drink and cleaning as the water sources are very far from the school premises. As the COVID 19 Pandemic is now a World Wide threat, the prevention need of awareness among the target population is very high. The project will identify the needed outcomes in the project.

2 - Activities/Outputs

The Activities of this projects designed according to the needs of the project.- Provision of text books and notebooks for pupils from class 1 to class 6- Construction of 24 classrooms (12 girls, 12 boys) by SSB - Construction of 6 teachers offices and 4 stores.- Construction of 8 latrine units (4 girls/4 boys)._ Rehabilitation of 8 classrooms (4 girls/4boys)- Conduct 8 training PTA members in the two localities.-Create 2 trees and vegetable garden to be distributed to the villages and fencing the schools and enhance children nutrition during the school year.- Conducted 20 awareness raising on COVID 19 pandemic prevention through distribution of face masks, informatics diffusion to the community, Organize 12 practical physical trainings on social separation to the affected community. - Organize sport and cultural competitions among classes to enhance reconciliation among the communities.
