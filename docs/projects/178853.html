Project Summary

In its eighth year, conflict inside Syria shows all the signs of a complex protracted humanitarian crisis. Takaful Al-Sham aims to support IDPs with WASH emergency and durable assistance in Aleppo and Idleb according to the following sub-districts:
- Atareb 608 HHs (G 745, B 882, W 730, M 684)
- Daret Azza 734 HHs (G 899, B 1064, W 881, M 826)
- Al Bab 1282 HHs (G 1570, B 1859, W 1538, M 1442)
- Azaz 1740 HHs (G 2132, B 2523, W 2088, M 1958)
- Aghtrin 434 HHs (G 532, B 629, W 521, M 488)
- Afrin 830 HHs (G 1017, B 1204, W 996, M 934)
- Sharan 480 HHs (G 588, B 696, W 576, M 540)
- Jandairis 536 HHs (G 657, B 777, W 643, M 603)
- Sheikh El-Hadid 310 HHs (G 380, B 450, W 372, M 349)
- Dana 1754 HHs (G 2149, B 2543, W 2105, M 1973)
- Harim 824 HHs (G 1009, B 1195, W 989, M 927)
- Salqin 1098 HHs (G 1345, B 1592, W 1318, M 1235)
- Maaret Tamsrin 828 HHs (G 1014, B 1201, W 994, M 932)
- Idleb 442 HHs (G 541, B 641, W 530, M 497)
The total 11900 HHs will be targeted with WASH services according to IDPs location needs, priority will be given to most vulnerable families including all ranks of vulnerability and meet their basic WASH needs.
Takaful's WASH interventions will take place as follows:
- Improve access to humanitarian life-saving/emergency WASH facilities and services, including strengthening Infection Prevention and Control (IPC) measures.:
1-Water tanks provision and Water trucking: Targeting vulnerable IDPs residing in camps that are not receiving proper quantity of clean water. Takaful will provide 35-40 liters for each person daily, public water tanks will be provided as well to be adequate to the specified amount of water. Water disinfectants will be added to ensure providing drinkable clean water to beneficiaries.
2-Installation of latrines with the required collective septic tanks: each block of latrines will be consisted of 2 toilets for males and 2 for females, one of each will be provided with special arrangements for PwD. In addition, 2 baths 1 for each gender.
3-Sewage desludging and solid waste management: sewage will be removed and transferred to the specified areas by the local authorities, twice every month. As for the solid waste, garbage bins will be distributed in the targeted camps (1 bin for 8 families). The garbage bins will be emptied and the garbage will be transferred 3 times a week.
- Distribution of essential WASH non-food items (NFI), hygiene promotion and messaging on COVID-19
1-Hygiene kits distribution (4 rounds a year): each kit will contain basic required hygiene items according to WASH cluster standards containing consumables and supplementary items. The distribution will consider GBV risk-reduction through separating males and females in 2 lines, moreover, include female members in the team of distribution.
2-Hygiene awareness promotion campaigns: posters will be sticked all over the targeted locations, moreover, hygiene awareness raising session will be conducted at each hygiene kit distribution round at the same locations of distribution. female members in the team of sessions' provision. as well as radio awareness bulletin, hanged flexes and billboards.
- Repair and rehabilitation of water systems with repair with operation and maintenance of water supply systems: as Takaful Is planning to assess water systems (water stations - networks - high tanks...etc) In the targeted locations and target them with repair activities to guarantee full accessibility of beneficiaries to water through available water systems. as well as, accompany this with regular maintenance costs, along with operational costs that are required to deliver water.
- Repair, rehabilitation and operational support to sewage systems
as Takaful Is planning to assess sewage systems (networks - manholes - septic tanks …etc) In the targeted locations and target them with repair activities to Improve level of hygienic atmosphere through proper release and transition of sludge away from populations' gathering.

4 - Needs

36% of general population relying on unsafe water supply modalities to meet or complement their water needs, with at least 70% of sewage being discharged untreated and at least half of the sewerage systems not functional, and with about 12% of garbage inappropriately disposed, the WASH infrastructure and service provision in many parts of Syria require significant repair and operational support. Community transmission of COVID-19 is anticipated to continue in 2021. Mainstreaming COVID-19 response throughout all regular WASH activities is crucial to mitigate risks. Depreciation of Syrian currency erodes households' purchasing power and subsequently spending capacity on WASH services, supplies, and consumables,(OCHA- 2021).TASCO conducted a WASH assessment in 21 camps in Aleppo and Idleb, the results indicate a need to install additional water tanks in 6 camps, and a need to install latrines in 15, and 19 camps have no NGO to desludge the septic tanks, also there is a need to install garbage containers in 9 camps, and 17 camps have no party concerned on waste managing.Atareb 608 HHs (G 745, B 882, W 730, M 684), Daret Azza 734 HHs (G 899, B 1064, W 881, M 826), Al Bab 1282 HHs (G 1570, B 1859, W 1538, M 1442), Azaz 1740 HHs (G 2132, B 2523, W 2088, M 1958), Aghtrin 434 HHs (G 532, B 629, W 521, M 488),Afrin 830 HHs (G 1017, B 1204, W 996, M 934),Sharan 480 HHs (G 588, B 696, W 576, M 540), Jandairis 536 HHs (G 657, B 777, W 643, M 603), Sheikh El-Hadid 310 HHs (G 380, B 450, W 372, M 349),Dana 1754 HHs (G 2149, B 2543, W 2105, M 1973),Harim 824 HHs (G 1009, B 1195, W 989, M 927),Salqin 1098 HHs (G 1345, B 1592, W 1318, M 1235),Maaret Tamsrin 828 HH (G 1014, B 1201, W 994, M 932),Idleb 442 HH (G 541, B 641,W 530, M 497)

7.1 - Please list any risks related to sexual exploitation and abuse that have been identified during the project design phase and any risk mitigation and prevention measures that have been adopted and built into the project

- In case the complaint was related to SEA classification we share it with our trained field protection focal point who is in charge of reporting such a case to the General Management immediately in order to investigate of a case and take the required action with the employee according to SEA Code of conduct and refer the case to GBV sub-cluster to follow up with the survivor as well as we report the case to PSEA Network. We classify under the sensitive complaints about sexual exploitation or abuse (SEA), Takaful has zero-tolerance for sexual harassment, exploitation, or abuse by any of its staff members, any report or allegation of verbal or physical sexual exploitation or sexual abuse by organization staff or providers against child or women or disable groups of beneficiaries or people in needs to get humanitarian services, such cases should be reported, where the beneficiaries of Takaful services have the right to report SEA, and TAS staff are required to report SEA by another staff member. - We applied some steps to avoid SEA cases as the following: Most of Takaful management and field staff (males and females) had attended workshops to increase their awareness of PSEA cases.- TAKAFUL has trained a field protection focal point who is in charge of reporting such a case to the General Management immediately in order to follow up on the reported case and take the required measures.- Ensuring that all volunteers who work with TAS signed PSEA CoC

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Safety and security:locations are far from front-lines so change control is not expected.Risk: Changes in the security situation or authorities/groups in control of project areas may make the projects activities less appropriate to the new situation.Likelihood: highImpact: HighMitigation:-In coordination with the Access Working Group, the security officer will help mitigate the risks through collecting information on security updates from field staff, the network of local councils and civil society activists.-Ensure that staff members are trained in safety and security.-All critical documents will be saved as soft copies, so that they will not get lost due to an attack.-If an emergency arises that may threaten the safety of staff and/or beneficiaries, staff is provided with directives to move to a safer location until the area is safe enough to continue with project activities or to ask for location change.- Preparing plan B, where if one of the targeted locations affected by the military operations, it will be replaced with another locations.Programmatic:Risk: Some of targeted locations may be supported from other donor, which lead in duplication of services.Likelihood: LowImpact: MediumMS: -At the beginning of project, we will re-check with related clusters and other networks to make sure that there isn’t any organization implementing similar programs in the targeted location.-If it appears that any organization was responded in one or more of the targeted locations, we will coordinate with cluster to change the location.
