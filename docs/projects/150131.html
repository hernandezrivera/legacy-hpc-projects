Project Summary

The project aims to sustain and enforce the ongoing School Health Program implemented in East Jerusalem schools through providing vaccination, checkup, and screening, provision of emergency first aid and curative services.
There are four types of schools offer education to Palestinian Jerusalemites. The first type is private schools that operate under the supervision of charitable societies and churches. These schools rely on tuition fees or church donations to cover their expenses. However, 90% of private schools receive financial assistance from the Israeli Jerusalem municipality, hence adopt the Israeli curriculum. The second type of schools is run by UNRWA that adopted the Palestinian curriculum in 2000/2001 and is responsible for paying salaries and providing school supplies. However, due to the US government decision to cut funding to UNRWA, the education for 25,000 children is put in jeopardy. The third type is schools supervised jointly by the Israeli Ministry of Education and the Jerusalem Municipality, where the former supervises the technical and logistical processes of the schools and covers expenses of elementary schools, and the latter covers expenses on secondary schools, appoints teachers and administrators and covers their payroll and Israeli curriculum is fully adopted at these schools. The fourth type is Waqf Schools that adopted the Palestinian curriculum in the academic year of 2000/2001 and operate under the Islamic Endowment Department. These schools had become affiliated with the Palestinian Ministry of Education in 1994; therefore the ministry oversees the provision of school supplies and covers the payroll of its employees. The Palestinian Ministry of Health is responsible for the school health in these schools and due to its very limited access to Jerusalem, HWC coordinates with the Palestinian MoE to facilitate its entry to schools and the MoH provides the vaccines for HWC staff to implements the National Vaccination Program. HWC school health services are provided in 62 schools in J1 and J2 areas, it includes services for students’ physical screening, hearing, and vision. HWC worked on providing school community (students, teachers, parents) with health education, emergency preparedness, First Aid, raising awareness among students and their parents about healthy lifestyles, violence, and adolescence risky behaviors. To enhance the students', parents' and teachers’ knowledge, skills and positive attitudes towards healthy practices, violence and risky behaviors.
Specific objective:
1. To increase the accessibility of children and adolescents to preventive, diagnostic, therapeutic and health services through school health program services.
2. To enhance the children and adolescent’s psycho social health.
3. To enhance children, parents, teachers, and adolescents’ knowledge, skills and positive attitudes about healthy practices and risky behaviors and First Aid.

Needs

Palestinian schools in Jerusalem receive only 11% of the education budget. There is a severe shortage of classrooms, infrastructure, and most schools lack a healthy environment, laboratories, libraries,and playgrounds. Most schools operate in rented buildings not designed for school use with unhealthy learning environment especially schools located in the old city, where the majority of the schools are in bad shape, despite this fact, the students' attendance is high where the families are suffering and dedicated to keep their presence in Jerusalem, to maintain the population within the old city. These schools suffer a classroom overcrowding ranging between 0.5-0.9 square meters per student compared with the global ratio ranges from (1.25 - 1.50) square meters per student.Access restriction threatened the students safety ,as they face difficulties in reaching schools,in addition to the violence and fear they are exposed to through the checkpoints and the violence from the settlers causing a state of stress and depression that leads to students’ drop out and delay in academic achievement.The result is that 36% of children in East Jerusalem fail to complete a full 12 years of school which in turn leads to the increase in underage employment specially between boys , where children are subjected to physical, economical and sexual abuse, the exposure to drugs and addiction, and the spread of illiteracy among the Palestinian community. Girls early marriage is increasing as many families feel its not safe to send their girls to schools . Access obstacles also are hindering the teachers’ access to schools, for many of them live outside Jerusalem, also plays a role in threatening the existence and continuity of schools. This in turn forces many of the teachers to go and work for the Municipality schools where they also get higher salaries and forced to teach the augmented curriculum. Restrictions on the right of access to basic education, and the deteriorating economic situation resulted in many social and behavioral problems such as the spread of drug abuse and alcohol consumption, early marriage, lack of security and stress, which is reflected on the individuals and their families in the form of violence that is directed towards women in particular. Social and behavioral problems including drug abuse, alcohol drinking and smoking are very much abundant among Jerusalem youth and at increasing pace. Schools are an entry point to discuss and educate the population in healthy life styles, risky behaviors and violence in all its forms and to keep their steadfastness. The Palestinian Authority has limited control over its schools in Jerusalem, making it a necessity for the Health Work Committees (HWC) to continue providing health services to schools in order to empower the educational process and fill the gap and needs of Palestinian schools in Jerusalem in order to face the systemic Judaization and Israelization of schools, curriculum and the students’ identity.

Activities

1. screening and early detection of diseases for students are provided in 32 schools in East Jerusalem:- provide medical examination and screening services for students of 1st grade in 32 schools. - conduct vision tests for 1st, 5th and 8th grade students in 32 schools. - providing dental checkup for 1st, 4th, 7th, and 10th grade students in 35 schools. 2. The Palestinian Unified National Vaccination Program is implemented in 47 schools - provide students with vaccinations according to the national program 3. Students , teachers and parents increased their skills and information about healthy behaviors , nutrition , smoking , drugs and violence. - Organize 300 health lectures for students in 60 schools. - Organize 6 open days for school students. - Organize 2 campaigns for school students about healthy and risky behaviors(Drugs and Alcohol).Each school has specialized student committees such as the environmental committee, the nutrition committees..etc and these committees are supervised by the teachers . Activities for the campaign will be initiatives by these committees. - Organize 15 health awareness lectures for parents 5 lectures will be about early marriage and its affect on the health and the psychological state of young girls. The health educator and the nurse will organize meeting for the parents with coordination with the parents council. the council will suggest subjects they meet their needs . At least 5 meeting will be on early marriage and if consequences. - Organize training on first aid for school teachers and form emergency preparedness team in 20 schools. - Organize training on psycho social support for school teachers. - Develop and distribute 4000 brochures. - Distribute 20 first aid kits for 20 schools.
