Project Summary

JUSTIFICATION: The National Youth Development Organization (NYDO) whose goal is to support and re-enforce development efforts by promoting, integrating and strengthening human and Socio-economic wellbeing of the Youths/underprivileged communities in Cameroon also has as one of her objectives to arouse community Mobilizations and participation.
In Cameroon, the female youth constitute a very significant portion of the population and according to statistical revelations stands out as the vulnerable age group which is most hit by sexual abuse, exploitation and sex related ills (STI, HIV/AIDS Unwanted Pregnancies).
Paradoxically, this sex remains the most socio economically underprivileged especially during crisis and conflicts. Bearing in mind that the genesis and the bed of these ills during this Anglophone crisis have a relationship with displacement, unemployment, poverty, dependability and illiteracy. They are the most internally displaced, the most sexually exploited and abused, the most stricken by poverty and with a very low school going rate and very high level of school dropouts.
With the above evidence, it is the concern of the National Youth Development Organization guidance in Bamenda to concentrate some efforts on the socio professional insertion of this sex with special attention to the youth bracket. this bearing in mind that the youth age bracket, cutting across all the sexes remains the most highly venerable age group for diverse reasons ranging from political, social, economic, biological, psychological etc.
RATIONAL OF THE PROJECT
The aim is that at the end of the project, NYDO must have succeeded to transform the female youth socially and enable them to be self-reliant leading to the development initiatives and responsible lives that can be replicated elsewhere. The technical support and resources of the support organization (NYDO) will be a case study and a responsibility to reduce the rate of exploitation, sexual abuse, poverty, dependency, irresponsibility, and HIV/AIDS amongst the female youth of the North West Region of Cameroon.
TARGET POPULATION:
The direct beneficiaries of this are the internally displaced and out of school female youths within the area of intervention aged between 11 – 22 years.
3. Program area: the operational base of the project is Bamenda Health District
The goal of this project is to reduce the rate of exploitation as they struggle for sustainability, reduce sexual abuse/violence, HIV/AIDS infection and unwanted pregnancies while promoting, integrating and strengthening their human and socio-economic wellbeing and sustainable livelihood.
7. OBJECTIVES OF THE PROJECT:
 To encourage, promote and intensify self-employment amongst young girls thereby reducing dependability, sexual abuse, jobsite exploitation, prostitution and irresponsible living
 To reduce the rate of unwanted pregnancy and STI, HIV/AIDS amongst young girls
 To promote and intensity micro-savings and micro-enterprise development amongst young girl
 To promote Human Right Education amongst the Girls
8. EXPECTED RESULTS
o Each year, the organization will train/empower at least 100 young girls in a trade.
o Some 100 girls each year should be able to self-employ themselves.
o More than 80% of the trainee should be able to stop prostitution as a means of sustainability and prevent the sex related ills (unwanted pregnancies, STI, HIV/AIDS).
