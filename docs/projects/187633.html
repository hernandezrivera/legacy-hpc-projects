Project Summary

The project aims to improve the food consumption for (1500 HHs) the needy families from IDPs, returnees and host communities in Idleb governorate- Harm district, Dana sub-district.
The project will target the most vulnerable Houshold, with regularly food basket within 8 months, in average one food basket/family/month. the food basket will contain the main food materail according to WFP and FAO recomenadtion. 1,500 HHs
the project activities are continue during two consecutive seasons.
The first season start in May and finish in 31 December 2022, targeted 750 family with estimated budget 658,000 USD$ .While the second season will start after finshing the previous one from January till September 2023 targeted 750 family, with estimated budget 658,000 USD$ .

4 - Needs

The results show a sharp increase from last year, with an increase of 4.5 million people facing acute food insecurity in 2021 compared to 2020. Whereas in 2019, 39 per cent of the total population was food insecure, that figure is now almost 60 per cent in 2020. The number of severely food insecure people has also increased from three percent (0.6 million) of the population in 2019 to six per cent (1.3 million) in 2020. The estimated sector PiN for 2021 – of food insecure plus those at risk of food insecurity – has increased from 9.8 million to 14.2 million. The evidence highlights an alarming deterioration, caused by a combination of factors including currency depreciation and associated loss of purchasing power, considerable hikes in food prices, large population displacements, lack of livelihood and income generating options,losses in agricultural production and assets, all compounded by ongoing conflicts and the COVID-19 pandemic. (UN-OCHA-HNO 2021).Amal finds and through Rapid need assessment FGD, there is a massive need for food supporting in Dana district, where hundred thousand of families from IDPs, Returnees and host community sever food insecure.

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

The project will engage the target benefi ciaries in the design process, planning andactivities implementation. So, the project targeted both host community and IDPs inthe all project stages.the benefi ciaries are from both women and men with priority forthe following families: orphan, female headed household, family with disability person,elderly, huge number of children under five. the project will consider gender issues during the beneficiary selecting, verifiying and basket distributing by inserting females in the project staff, coordinating with the community leaders, and families before and during activities implementation.The project will set and apply complaint mechanism to receiveand solve any issue in manner time.


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

The project will follow strict procedure during the implementation periods, by engage females in the project staff, and engage all stakeholders with targeted families in all project activities especially the distribution process.the project will select office inside the community near the public facilities and main road for easy access.the project will recruit a qualified MEAL staff to ensure high quality of monitoring, evaluating, and transparency.All these steps will conduct by the project are to avoid and prevent any kind of abuse or sexual exploitation.
