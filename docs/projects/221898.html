Project Summary

The proposed intervention will improve vital access to emergency safe water and health promoting hygienic conditions for the most underserved people in Cabo Delgado through a multisectorally-coordinated Area-Based approach, that will empower communities to build resilience and social cohesion in the face of an ongoing conflict.
Activities envisioned, detailed in the next sections, will be as follow:
Objective 1. Provide access to life-saving safe water for drinking, cooking and personal hygiene in emergency:
1.1 Rapid construction/rehabilitation of water points
1.2 Formation and training of Water Management Committees
1.3 Distribution of water purification material and orientation sessions on usage
Objective 2. Organise gender-sensitive hygiene promotion activities
2.1 Capacity building and training to CSOs / women / youth groups to disseminate hygiene promotion messages
2.2 Implementation of hygiene promotion activities and dissemination of educational material
2.3 Wash-in-Schools to spread messages around the 5 primary Hygiene education subjects (Menstrual Hygiene Management, Food, Water, Personal, Environmental)

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The Project’s main objective is to respond with emergency lifesaving water and hygiene interventions for IDPs, returnees and non-displaced populations in context of the ongoing conflict in Macomia district, Cabo Delgado. This will be done through by providing rapid access to sufficient quantity of safe water and by providing support to enhance hygienic conditions for affected groups. The intervention will address critical gaps highlighted by the WASH Cluster (WC), ACTED, and by WASH partners, and implement critical, lifesaving WASH activities with an integrated approach, focusing on immediate needs to ensure access to safe water and hygiene for affected populations in the emergency response. Beneficiaries' involvement will ensure the project builds on existing community structures and facilitates resilient, gender-sensitive coping mechanisms to emergency, while an area-based approach will foster community cohesion by targeting all 3 community groups (IDPs, returnees, and non-displaced) and focusing on specific needs of women, girls, men, boys, elderly, and people with disability. Proactive and tailored engagement of women and youth will be cross-cutting and implemented in all ACTED’s humanitarian project activities, monitoring, and feedback processes. Lifesaving aActivities will actively promote inter-community cooperation and will include, but not limit to, gender-balanced formation of water management committees with a representation of the 3 communities groups, training of hygiene committees of women and youth and washing schools to promote hygiene practice messages, emergency adapted Community Led Total Sanitation Campaigns (CLTS), Child-to-child Approach (Wash-in-Schools). The intervention will also build on ACTED presence and consolidated community acceptance in Macomia district, complementing its blanket approach in-kind food assistance and making use of operational mechanisms in place (distribution points, logistics, warehouses, ...), including existing relations with local governance. Coordination with CCCM Cluster, Shelter, Protection and other service providers will ensure the project targets underserved, hard-to-reach areas and addresses the population multisectoral needs. ACTED will keep flexibility in its response by monitoring information management systems as IOM Displacement Tracking Matrix (DTM), and by coordinating with other intervention mechanisms like RRM, JRP, and other existing interventions, in order to reach the most affected areas in Macomia’s volatile context.

All Acted humanitarian activities will give particular attention to mainstreaming women and youth involvement in the community governance and decision-making mechanisms and will include GBV protection across programming.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The proposed project proactively identifies potential risks and mitigates harm by conducting initial safety audits and protection risk assessments. These evaluations will be updated regularly to address emerging risks and adapt to the rapid changing context. Protection principles, including protection for sexual exploitation, abuse and harassment, are embedded across all project stages. ACTED adopts a zero-tolerance approach towards SEAH. ACTED is committed to the prevention of SEAH both within the organization and within its programmes beneficiary populations. To this end, ACTED’s PSEAH Policy: ▪ Encourages prevention ▪ Promotes detection ▪ Sets out a clear investigation procedure ▪ Sets out a mechanism of sanctions ACTED conducts periodic training for staff and community workers on protection standards, PSEAH, and mainstreams GBV prevention and accessibility for vulnerable groups in its programs. Community consultations are central at all stages, including focus group discussions and the use of Acted Feedback Mechanism, and will guide culturally-gender sensitive and safe WASH service delivery. Additionally, coordination with protection-focused actors and local authorities will strengthen early risk identification and foster a collaborative approach to ensure the safety and dignity of all beneficiaries.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The project ensures meaningful access to assistance and promotes active participation by incorporating gender and age perspectives in all phases. Community engagement will aim at inclusive decision-making, establishing trained, gender-balanced Water Management/Hygiene Promotion Committees and mainstreaming Women’s, Elderly, and Youth participation in community decision-making processes through dedicated platforms. Gender-age-disability disaggregated data collection will guide interventions, ensuring tailored responsiveness to the diversified needs. Additionally, Acted Feedback Mechanism and Post Distribution Monitoring, will inform decisions and empower vulnerable groups to actively shapes the project outcomes.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The project will ensure people with disabilities are identified and supported through inclusive needs assessments and active engagement throughout the project cycle. Initial consultations, surveys and existing data will help identify individuals with disabilities and the specific barriers they face, directly shaping activities design. Planning solution by coordinating with Shelter, CCCM, Protection Cluster and community committees will allow individuals with disabilities to participate fully and access assistance safely without discrimination. Regular monitoring will ensure that project adaptations are responsive and inclusive.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

In line with ACTED’s 3Zero vision—Zero Exclusion, Zero Carbon, Zero Poverty—and Greening Strategy, this project will minimize environmental impact across all activities. Environmental impact assessments data/ rapid environmental screening will be used to identify risks and shape mitigation strategies. The project will prioritize sustainable resource use by incorporating locally sourced, eco-friendly materials and applying a reduce, recycle, and reuse system to WASH activities. Engagement with local communities in awareness activities will emphasize responsible natural resource use to avoid environmental contamination and lower the risk of epidemic outbreaks. By integrating these measures, the project aligns with ACTED’s commitment to sustainable recovery that addresses the specific needs of affected communities while safeguarding the environment. Furthermore, compliance with national environmental regulations and coordination with local authorities will be maintained throughout the Project.
