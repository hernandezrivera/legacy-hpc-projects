Project Summary

The proposed project focus on identifying and addressing the needs of vulnerable groups of both HCs and IDPs in 8 communities in Idleb (Maaret Tamsrin, Dana, Qourqeena) through
Provide quality and integrated protection services, including awareness-raising, psychosocial assistance, assistance in cash, and targeted support to vulnerable groups,
GAN will work at the individual, community, and institutional levels with all relevant entities to promote an environment conducive to the protection of affected populations, with a focus on the most vulnerable,
6 mobile teams will be established to conduct awareness-raising sessions around protection risks encountered by the vulnerable according to the development of the field context and observations of teams within societies
GAN will target 25K individuals
GAN will provide CP CM service and structured PSS for children and life skills for adolescent boys and girls within the equipped CFS in the schools previously supported by GAN (6 CFS) using the curriculums (little fellow-I deal-SHE DEAL-WIN WIN), parenting programs will also be provided (Essential caregiver principles).
Each mobile team will consist of a CP officer +outreach worker.
The PSS team will consist of 12 facilitators, the CM team will include a CM supervisor, and 3 CW
While mobile teams are the safe entry point for society, they will identify vulnerable cases and record throughout the project, after verification of these cases GAN will provide monthly cash grants for 3 months to these cases to meet their needs with autonomy, dignity, and tackling economic poverty which will be reflected positively on the protection factors. This intervention is considered flexible and responsible programming to rapidly adapt to changes
Where poor households might resort to negative/harmful coping strategies, to reduce household expenditures, Covid-19 is further exacerbating economic vulnerability and associated risks.
The monthly value of cash for protection grant is $100 or equivalent TL since this cash modality will give them flexibility in choosing they need and to give the project an ability to meet all changes in beneficiary needs, marginalized groups, minorities, and persons with specific needs will be better able to enjoy their rights and access basic goods and services with cash, 700 families (3500 individuals) out of 25K the total number of BNFs,
Mobile teams’ staff will be trained on the program’s objective eligibility criteria, distribution mechanism so they can assess the adequacy of the program to support each specific case.
The grant program will be linked with CM to serve the most vulnerable cases,
All members of the team will be trained on CSG and PSEA,
The capacity of teachers in schools will be built to improve quality provision of protection services on CSG, PFA, and SIR by GAN employees this training will help teachers to be supportive elements of children, maintain their normal development, and Enhance teachers' ability to deal with them.
GAN will identify the money transfer agents by conducting a competitive process, where the identified vendors will be responsible for delivering the cash amount to the beneficiaries in the distribution points and the payment for the agent will be conducted after finalizing the distribution of each few days which reduce the security risk in delivering the cash in such complex context,
The selected modality aims to use e-cards instead of paper, to reduce the number of papers that need to be signed by the financial department, the beneficiaries, and the suppliers.
To ensure community and beneficiary participation in monitoring and improving the project, several channels will be assigned to solicit stakeholder comments, a dedicated phone number for complaints at HQ, complaints, and suggestions boxes will be installed in each school, an email, and a Facebook page
There will also be a monitoring plan to monitor all project activities, and obligation to report all activities through 4Ws

3 - Needs

The absence of longer-term solutions to displacement, ongoing violations of IHL and human rights law, extensive destruction of infrastructure, and inadequate access to services and to the population in need are likely to continue impacting the protection landscape.The price inflation of basic commodities, shortages of food and fuel, and depreciation of local currency are expected to worsen, driving increased vulnerability and leading to heavy reliance on humanitarian assistance and resorting to harmful coping mechanisms,Approximately 7.8 million people are in need of GBV assistance (a 0.5 million increase from 2022), out of which 51.2 % are women and 37.8 % are girls.More than 6M children, including those with PWD, are at heightened risk of violence, abuse, and exploitation and need critical child protection services. The ongoing hostilities, protracted displacement, and limited access to basic services continue to significantly affect the physical, mental, and social well-being of children and caregivers.considering that approximately 90%of households in Syria consistently experience a gap between their income earned and expenditure required to meet basic needs, severe economic vulnerability poses increasingly higher risks of reliance on negative coping mechanisms, which include begging, illicit economic activities, corruption, child labor, the recruitment of men and children by armed groups, as well as risks of increased GBV.

4 - Implementation of activities

GAN aims to strengthen protection interventions to support the overall protection cluster objectives towards the provision of quality integrated protection services, this will be achieved through 4 pillars of integrated programming1-Will be created 6 mobile teams, and trained on SIR, CSG, and PSEA, after that will start providing awareness sessions about (CP, GBV, MRE, COVID-19, and services information) in targeted communities 2-Children and Adolescents will be provided with structured psychosocial interventions, life skills, and The COVmentoring to improve positive coping, functioning, and self-efficacy 3- specialized protection services (CPCM),4- Caregivers of children and adolescents at risk will be provided with parenting skills and cash assistanceWhere most vulnerable cases will be identified and registered by the mobile team during the provision of awareness sessions in the communities throughout the project period, after that, the field team verifies the applications through another field visit the beneficiaries at their place of residence Parallel will be contracting with money transfer agent through using e-modality. Distribute e-vouchers to beneficiaries accompanied with a term of use and service flyers and redeem them at the distribution points, specific days will be allocated for each group of beneficiaries to avoid crowded at the distribution point.Regular monitoring and baseline, endline and beneficiary satisfaction for all activities will be collected

8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Aid workers or associated people use their power or influence to sexually exploit or abuse the people they should be serving, particularly children. The risk may be heightened for children with disabilities, those unaccompanied, orphans and younger children. mitigation measures: GAN is a member of the PSEA network, attending a monthly meeting and following the guidelines, so to mainstream the protection from sexual exploitation and abuse in the project, GAN has developed a policy stating standards of conduct, including acts of SEA, and a work plan to implement the policy in place, staff members will sign the policy and received systematized and refreshed training on PSEA. GAN Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) policies are in place, practiced and monitored. Polices cover standards of behavior for the organization’s staff and representatives, specifically forbidding sexual exploitation and abuse, and include accountability measures. The Polices address risks at the organizational level and the program level. GAN will provide awareness sessions about the organization’s policies and procedures. GSN ’s staff and targeted communities will have access to safe and effective reporting systems including knowing when they will get a response to their report and having the right to report anonymously.


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measuresAssumption: Access to Syria remains possible and safe through the neighboring countries the good security situation in all targeted area, There is no curfew due to COVID-19, Closure of the borders between Turkey and Syria Risk1-COVID-19 spread in target communities Mitigation:GAN will continue to include COVID-19 considerations and adjust programming to ensure the safety and health of both beneficiaries and humanitarian workers, as well as seek to maximize reach. This includes adapting curricula to online modalities, reducing the number of participants in group activities, as well as home visits for people in extremely vulnerable situations. Risk2 -Risk of money transfer Mitigation: GAN has a prior fundamental condition for money transfer agent: the payment is conducted after distribution Risk3-Risk of shelling by Government of Syrian (GoS) forces and its allies in all or some targeted locations and/or infighting between different armed groups continue causing loss of access to the targeted locationsMitigation: GAN will assess several options like the temporary suspension of activities until the areas become more secure again, Risk4- Risk heavy shelling by the Government of Syrian forces and its allies for a long time (months) or the target locations becomes under its control Mitigation: Shifting program locations to more accessible areas in coordination with the Protection cluster and others.
