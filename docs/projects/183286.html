Project Summary

The outbreak of COVID-19 in Lebanon affected all individuals, particularly the LGBTIQ+ community who already suffered from multi-layered types of violence due to cultural, legal, societal, religious, and socio-economic reasons. In fact, due to the imposed lock-down measures and curfew restrictions, many have lost their jobs since they worked in informal economies with no protection by a work contract. Hence, many were forced to move back with their parents where they were previously rejected and abused, or live with friends, or fail to pay their rent fees.
On top of the pandemic, Lebanon has been suffering from multiple crises, including a political instability, financial and economic crisis, and the Beirut blast that took place on August 4, 2020. The latter also had a direct impact on the LGBTIQ+ individuals as many of them resided or worked in the areas that were most affected by the explosion. Additionally, most of the safe queer spaces were located within the affected area and were severely damaged. This included community centers, cafes, bars, art centers and NGOs. Given the aforementioned factors, these multiple challenges had detrimental consequences on the livelihood, protection, safety, and security, as well as the mental health of LGBTIQ+ individuals.
Through this project, MOSAIC seeks to address the mental needs of individuals and assist them with relevant cash assistance responding to their pressing needs (hygiene and food) arising because of both the health crisis and the economic crisis, with a focus on the following areas:
Mental Health Services,
Legal services
Food Scarcity.
Moreover, this potential fund will allow MOSAIC to cover core costs crucial to the sustainability of support and service provision to the LGBTIQ+ community in Lebanon. These core costs entail a contribution to the social worker’s salary, who is a front-liner in the response to the beneficiaries’ needs, and is responsible for providing case management services, and directing the beneficiaries to the needed services, whether internally or externally. In addition, this fund will contribute in the yearly rental fees of MOSAIC’s center where services and activities take place, as well as essential operational costs, that ensure smooth and safe operations for all MOSAIC’s community, including beneficiaries and staff.

Targeting Criteria:
For this project, MOSAIC aims to supporting 500 individuals, service providers and LGBTIQ Family Members, in Lebanon by providing them with legal, social, mental health/PSS and Trans* health services.
The service at MOSAIC in Beirut aim at targeting the following groups:
1. 82.3% LGBTIQ+ Lebanese
2. 6% LGBTIQ+ Palestinians
3. 11.7% LGBTIQ+ Migrants living in Lebanon (Iraqis Yemenis, Jordanians, Egyptians, …).

Total: 500
Mechanism
The Project seeks to reach out for the most vulnerable individuals of the LGBTIQ+ community residing in Lebanon. The idea is to provide response services to ensure access to hardly afforded services. The services are provided in person at MOSAIC center in Badaro or online.
The project aims to distribute Hygiene and Food kits to 500 beneficiaries registered at MOSAIC. Services are provided to individuals based on case management and social follow up. The project is a 10-month project.

PS: Proportions are estimated based on a rapid analysis done on the registered beneficiaries at MOSAIC.
