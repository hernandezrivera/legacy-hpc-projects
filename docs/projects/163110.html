Project Summary

The proposed response will be implemented by CAFOD and its Caritas partners (listed below) in 7 districts district as follows: Caritas Hwange (Binga, Hwange and Lupane) Caritas Harare (Mudzi and Uzumba-Maramba-Pfungwe) and Caritas Gokwe (Gokwe South and Gokwe North). The Caritas partners, who have permanent presence, deep understanding of the needs and constraints of communities in targeted areas as well as strong relationships with local stakeholders, will be responsible for day to day implementation of activities whilst CAFOD will provide technical guidance and support, ensuring activities are well-planned, supported and monitored with shared responsibility and commitment. CAFOD will have overall responsibility of programme management and financial control. The proposed response is coming on the background of poor crop harvests in 2019 owing to the 2018/2019 drought and a difficult economic environment. The 2019/20 rainfall season is projected to be a ‘normal to below normal’. This proposed response will save lives and sustain and improve livelihoods and be forward-looking in-terms of enhancing both food and nutrition security through supporting agricultural production, food assistance, capacity enhancement and rehabilitation and/ construction of infrastructure for crops and livestock production. The project will target a total of 86,863 people in target areas. The majority (76%) were classified under IPC Phase 3 and 4 in May 2019. Programme monitoring in the target 7 districts by CAFOD and Caritas partners has shown that the numbers of persons in the IPC Phase 3 and 4 has increased since May 2019. Even some households that were considered better off now vulnerable, falling into IPC Phase 3/4. The proposed response will provide emergency food assistance through in-kind for initial 4 months to acutely food insecure persons; provide small livestock through pass on schemes. All persons in the target wards will be trained in nutrition sensitive cooking through cooking demonstrations. The programme will train lead persons each ward who will be assisted to provide training to members in their wards. A summer input package will be provided to households with labour and land. Fodder production will be promoted for small and larger livestock. Species of fodder with multipurpose purposes use, for livestock, fertiliser for crops and environmental management, will be promoted. Stock feeds will be provided for breeding centres for chickens and for improved he-goats following procurement during isolation periods.

1 - Activities

The project activities will include:  -Provide monthly food baskets (in-kind) to acutely food insecure population in rural areas.  -Provide vegetable seed packs and tools for household, community and school nutrition gardens-Provide drought-tolerant seeds and organic fertilizers  -Provide training, extension and advisory services to extension officers and farmers focusing on good agriculture practices, climate-smart agriculture. crop pest management, postharvest handling, storage and processing.  -Restocking of small livestock production  -Establishing livestock breeding centres  -Provide stock feed and support fodder production.  -Support livestock vaccination, disease surveillance and management  -Rehabilitate dip tanks and provide dipping chemicals  -Nutrition sensitive cooking demonstrations  -Environmental management through pasture regenerations. In addition to above activities, the project will conduct community and district entry meetings (launches), participatory beneficiary selection criteria, participatory registration of beneficiaries per intervention, establish complaints handling mechanisms, on-going monitoring and evaluation and closure meetings
