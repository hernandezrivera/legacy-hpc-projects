Project Summary

Central Blood Bank Society aims through this project at the continuing enhancement of the injured and patients in both governmental and non-governmental hospitals with free safe blood units. This target will be achieved by collecting approximately 12000 complete blood units to be used either as a whole unit or by separating them into their derivatives, including Plasma(FFP), platelets, and packed RBCs.
The process of blood units gathering undergoes several stages where at 1st instance that the blood donors should be eligible to donate his/her blood. Donors are classified into three categories; voluntary, familial, and paid donors.
The project, therefore, will target two types of donors: voluntary and familial blood donors.
The access to the familial blood donors is attained through drawing the needed blood units from the family of the patient or the injured to replace the consumed ones. However, the access to voluntary donors is achieved through promoting awareness of the importance of blood donation and its vitality for hospitals ,through outer blood donations campaigns and sponsored advertising on social media which facilitate the process of blood donation for donors and through inviting voluntary donors to donate to the society’s headquarter which cover all the governorates of the Gaza Strip by means of raise society awareness of the CBBS's headquarters, its services and its role. To guarantee the transfer of free safe blood units, they will be checked by viral testing (HIV, HBV, HCV) and blood group testing through reverse group mechanism to a confirmation test for preventing agglutination between the blood cells of blood donor and the antibodies of blood recipient.
The project also aims to strengthen the society’s reserves with blood units sufficient around 200 cases or beneficiaries. The estimated needed units for emergency preparedness are 350-400 units.
The society intends to conduct a documentary video documenting its activities and achievements resulted from the project.

1 - Needs

Gaza strip is a tiny area of land, with almost two million people packed in here. Despite the 2005 Israeli disengagement from Gaza, the United Nations international human rights organizations, and the majority of governments and legal commentators consider the territory to be still occupied by Israeli. Israeli maintains direct external control over Gaza and indirect control over life within Gaza: it controls Gaza’s air and maritime space, and six of Gaza’s seven land crossings. For eleven years, more than two million Palestinians who are living in Gaza facing the blockade imposed over the strip from Israel and Egypt. In recent years, Israel has launched several military operations in the Gaza strip, where the latest was in 2014 and lasted for 51 days. Every time the Gaza strip is exposed to the military action, there are hundreds of causalities, Since the beginning of 2018, the need for blood units has increased because of the rise in several injuries resulted from the Great March of Return demonstrations. The CBBS has contributed to distributing 12322 blood units to the injured and patients in both governmental and private hospitals, which are working on trauma pathways and hemophilia diseases. And therefore, the GRM is still ongoing. Thus the possibility of escalation is expected at any time, and the political situation is unstable as the possibility of war at any moment.Thus there will be an increase in the need for blood units by hospitals for causalities and chronic patients.The process of collecting and distributing blood units is very accurate, so it requires medical examination materials (Reagents), medical Disposable, some other equipment, technical staff, and fuel for power generators that cover the periods of power cuts to draw and store safe blood units.The project activities to collect approximately 12000 units of blood need a capacity building to cover the collection of blood units in outer campaigns and branch, the examination of these blood units and storage to meet the needs of hospitals.The society also needs additional blood donation beds for Rafah and KhanYunis branches, which collect an equivalent of 43% (6994 units of blood) of the overall blood units in the three offices in the Gaza strip. As the existing beds are not suitable for the comfort and safety of the donors, the Humanitarian Fund in the 2nd standard allocation 2018 has supported the two offices by 30% of the needed beds.

9 - Protection mainstreaming
9.1 - Do No Harm

The CBBS’s protocols included polices designated to protect beneficiaries through adopting safety procedures during drawing blood units which dictate the necessity of wearing gloves and other medical consumables once only in each case where beneficiaries can observe that’s consumed in his case exclusively. Also, there is a need for the construction of private paths for persons with disabilities inside society quarters. It is worth to mention that the CBBS respects individuals' privacy manifested by obtaining his/her oral or written acceptance before taking pictures of them for activities’’ documentation. Moreover, society’s services are free and available for everyone in need regardless of ant racial or ethnic discrimination. The existence of the society in southern governorates, namely Rafah and Khanyounis facilitates the access of beneficiaries living there to the provided services. The society is preparing an orientation material of the beneficiaries’ rights and the mechanisms of being provided of service through publishing this material on social media and in society's quarters.

9.2 - Equal and impartial access to aid

The CBBS maintains an open-door policy for complaints, inquiries and proposals by approaching its chairman Dr. Ziad Shaa'th or its project manager Ms.Haneen Alghazaly in the headquarter of the society located in Gaza city , where the complaints, inquiries, or proposals are considered and subsequently issuing the proper decision aimed at promoting performance and satisfying the needs of beneficiaries. Moreover, the policy of the society is based upon serving the people of Gaza Strip without discrimination based on their race, political affiliation or ethnicity. It worth to mention that society conducts an annual survey to assess the level of its service to the beneficiaries and to find out their needs. Besides the existence of complaints box in each branch donation hall, The society also spreads awareness among beneficiaries of their right to request the provided service in such halls.
