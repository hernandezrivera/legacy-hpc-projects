Project Summary

This project will be implemented by Sudan Evangelical Mission (SEM) in Jur River County of South Sudan, reaching 4,560 (810 boys, 810 girls, 1,470 men, 1,470 women). SEM currently has a static presence in the proposed location.
This project aims to achieve the 2025 Humanitarian Response Plan (HRP) objectives by ensuring the static provision of critical protection assistance and services (lifesaving), including specialized response services. Prioritize a collaborative, child-focused approach, with the engagement of local communities, government, and partners to enhance effectiveness. Responses are tailored to address the specific needs of the individuals, considering age, gender, and disability. We shall also ensure that human rights are upheld and promoted for the persons of concern by strengthening coordination, advancing the centrality of protection among humanitarian actors to reduce protection risks, and addressing coordination gaps/issues through close dialogue with partners and adequate security.
We shall conduct regular protection assessments to identify high-risk areas &dynamics exposing civilians to violence and conflicts. We shall collaborate with other partners in the field while carrying out this activity and share the assessment reports with county and national-level cluster and sub-cluster coordinators. The produced assessment and monitoring reports will be used for advocacy in protection with stakeholders and inform protection response.
By raising protection awareness, this project will disseminate accurate information to the community regarding protection, where they will get information regarding violence, exploitation, or environmental hazards, and ultimately contribute to building safer and more resilient communities.
Conducting comprehensive protection case management will provide a structured process for assisting project participants at risk of harm. They will be assessed after identification, supported as per their case plan, and followed up until case closure or transfer to ensure no gaps during service provision.
The above interventions of this project will promote positive coping mechanisms and identify local resources that the communities can use to enhance the protection of all vulnerable groups, such as returnees and internally displaced persons.
Targeting persons with disabilities with individual protection assistance will make this project inclusive, as people with disabilities can participate actively.

1. Needs

The protection cluster South Sudan protection analysis update published in June 2024 explained that the protection environment in South Sudan is characterized by obstacles to access for humanitarian workers and people in need of assistance, limited and unequal service provision, weak or absent protection systems, low awareness of fundamental rights, and discriminatory and harmful socio-cultural norms related to gender. Additionally, discriminatory practices exclude minority clans and marginalized groups, such as persons with disabilities, and disproportionately impact women, children, and older persons.This project will meet the needs of such beneficiaries by creating awareness of protection for the beneficiaries to seek services and conducting referred-to inter-sectoral services for vulnerable people in need. This project has an activity that will build the capacity of men and women in the community to tackle conflict and promote peace on themes like conflict resolution, mitigation, mediation, and peacebuilding.The escalating conflict in Sudan continues to pose substantial challenges to the protection of vulnerable populations in neighboring South Sudan. As the crisis deepens, the need for effective and comprehensive protection strategies becomes increasingly urgent. Displaced individuals, particularly women and children, face heightened risks of violence, exploitation, and abuse, further exacerbating the already fragile humanitarian situation. This project will monitor the returnees arriving in Jur River County from Sudan and Uganda, assess their needs, and refer them to multi-sectoral services provided by other partners in Jur River County. This project shall provide individual protection assistance to persons with disabilities among the returnees, internally displaced persons, and those from the host community to increase their independence, participation in community activities, and inclusion. The protection risk implications of the Sudan conflict on the South Sudan humanitarian response are multifaceted and deeply concerning. As the conflict drives a growing number of refugees and returnees into South Sudan, the demand for protection services increases exponentially. This project will conduct protection risk monitoring to identify the actual or potential exposure of the affected population to violence, coercion, or deliberate deprivation.The South Sudan protection analysis update published in June 2024 further emphasized the need to provide humanitarian and development (re)integration support to Returnees from Sudan across the country to avoid secondary movement and further increase protection risks. This project will, therefore, respond to their individual needs after assessment.During the reporting period (January to May 2024), 45% of KIs interviewed noted a lack of access to humanitarian assistance. The two primary reasons are corruption/bribery, lack of awareness of availability of services, and lack of registration card/food distribution card to access services. For internally displaced persons (IDPs), returnees, refugees, and host communities, tribalism and corruption can also lead to discrimination and create challenges to accessing humanitarian aid and essential services. This project seeks to raise protection awareness so that participants understand the available services and their rights to access humanitarian assistance. Awareness raising will also target humanitarian service providers to reduce the barriers to accessing humanitarian assistance.

2. Project activities

This project will achieve protection cluster objectives by static provision of critical protection assistance and services (lifesaving), including specialized response services through the engagement of local communities, government, and partners to enhance effectiveness. Responses for this project have been tailored to address the specific needs of the individuals, considering age, gender, and disability. To achieve this, we shall implement the following activities:1) Conduct 24 protection awareness-raising sessions in Jur River County through interactive mediums like community public rallies, sports, cultural dances peer, and peer sessions to reach out to the community on protection issues (indicator-# of GP awareness-raising and information sharing activities);2) Carry out identification and referral of 2,400 (450 boys, and 450 girls, 750 males, 750 female) vulnerable community members to inter-sectoral services within their community according to their needs (indicator- # of women, men, girls, and boys with protection concerns or specific needs, identified and referred through static or mobile means);3) Provision of assistive devices, e.g. crutches, wheelchairs, and tricycles, to 2,160 (360 boys, 360 girls, 720 male, 720 female) people with disabilities to promote inclusion and their participation in the community (indicator- # of women, men, girls and boys provided with non-cash assistance through protection case management (other than CP, GBV, and HLP);4) Conduct 480 key informant interviews (KI) to monitor protection risks at the boma level to identify the trends in child marriage, access to humanitarian assistance, access to justice, family separation, sexual violence, restriction of movement, HLP issues, safety and security concerns, explosive hazards, and child labor (indicator- # of PRMS submissions through (KII, Observation, and FGD tools);
