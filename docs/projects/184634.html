Project Summary

The humanitarian situation in Gedo Region remains precarious due to the ongoing multi-layered conflict and insecurity, persistent droughts and famine in subsequent years and continue to increase the susceptibility of women, girls, boys, marginalized, and displaced populations to violence including Child labour other protection concerns like family separations and recruitment and use of children in armed conflict in Ceel waaq and Beled Xaawo Districts.
In response to that, ARIDA is proposing the following interventions to expand lifesaving child protection services to vulnerable populations in Ceelwaaq and Beled Xawo Districts. The complexity of the operating environment requires multifaceted approaches that are tailored to each location including; Strengthen and capacitate community-based child protection mechanisms to prevent and mitigate Child protection risks through multi-layered, community based psychosocial support interventions to reduce vulnerabilities and risks to children by building a protective environment at family and community levels in the targeted districts. The project support for the effected individuals include direct support of identified cases through conducting case management including referral, mental health and psychosocial and counselling support. In addition to this, unaccompanied and separated children in the intervention targeted districts will be reunified with their caregivers/guardians and those children who do not have caregivers/guardians will be supported through community based foster care placements. In addition, the proposed action will ensure inclusion of person with disabilities, mainstreamed in every action of the project, and as well carry out awareness creation on the rights of children and person with disabilities. Child protection frontline staff, Child safeguarding and Child protection focal points, community volunteers as well as staff from local government authority will be capacitated on child safeguarding, PSEA and code of conduct to ensure that all interventions are safe for children and that they can identify.

1. - Projects activities

1. Strengthen and capacitate community based child protection mechanisms (CBCPM) to prevent and mitigate CP risks through multi-layered, community based psycho social support interventions to reduce vulnerabilities and risks to children by building a protective environment at family and community levels2. Peer to peer awareness raising will be led by Child protection communities, youth and community volunteers’ networks to prevent child protection risks.3. Identify vulnerable children in need of support such as case management and emergency child protection care (i.e. alternative care or basic PSS activities) 4. Identify and support children at risk and child survivors including unaccompanied and separated through providing case management services that meets their unique needs, which include family tracing and reunification services, alternative care arrangements. 5. Strengthen and capacitate Project frontline staff and community volunteers to support the case management process, through identification of children at risk, making home monitoring visits and offering peer support to caregivers. 6. Provision of structured mental health and Psychosocial Support services (MHPSS) including individual and group counselling. The nature of the mental health support will be based on each case individual and group need assessment. 7. Support to caregivers and families (MHPSS, positive parenting and other kind of support) of children facing protection risks in order to enhance their capacity to care for and protect their children8. Conduct child safety walks/Mapping exercises to identify risks for children and population in the community
