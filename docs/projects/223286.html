Project Summary

The northern of Mozambique has been facing an extreme humanitarian crisis since 2017 as a result of terrorist incursions with some people displaced and others returning to their areas of origin.
Faced with this scenario, the Foundation for Community Development (FDC), in partnership with the United Nations Children's Fund (UNICEF), implements actions that guarantee Child Protection in the Humanitarian approach to respond with minimum standards, together, to the needs of displaced people, returnees and host communities in need of child protection services, in the districts of Mocímboa da Praia, Nangade and Mueda.
The project's actions focus on the community-based approach, strengthening the participation of children, vulnerable families, community leaders or authorities and community child protection committees (CCPC) in the planning and implementation of activities. The project considers the care of children with specific or special needs, separated and unaccompanied children (UASC), children associated with armed groups and forces (CAAFAG) and children survivors of any type of abuse.
FDC contributes through these actions to the establishment of a functional and comprehensive case management system for the protection of child survivors of any type of abuse, identification, tracking and family reunification of separated and unaccompanied children (UASC), community reintegration of associated children to groups or armed forces (CAAFAG), prevention and protection of GBV and PSEA, provision of individualized or specialized psychosocial support through child-friendly and multipurpose spaces and strengthening the family environment by providing positive parenting to children's parents and caregivers.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The project ensures the early identification of risks at the community level through community-based actions that include dissemination of key messages and the establishment of referral mechanisms based on different humanitarian actors with the capacity to provide different services to different social groups. Community leaders, members of community child protection committees, neighborhood chiefs, case workers and facilitators play a leading role in early identification of any form of risk that may be associated with causing harm.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

FDC strongly supports the fundamental principles of the 2030 Agenda, according to which it is not possible to achieve human potential and sustainable development if half of humanity continues to be denied its full human rights and opportunities. Women and girls must have equal access to quality education, economic resources and political participation, as well as equal opportunities with men and boys in terms of employment, leadership and decision-making at all levels. In this context, women play a leading role in the well-being of the family, nutrition, health and fair distribution of resources is widely recognized. Considering the preponderant role of women in the community, the internally displaced women and girls of Cabo Delgado will be reached by the project providing inclusive approaches in multipurpose centers (MPC), safe spaces for women and girls addressing and disseminating key messages of gender violence, prevention and mitigation of violence and sexual abuse, improved livelihoods, and psychosocial support. FDC will consistently use gender transformative approaches to address structural barriers to gender equality, involving case workers, community facilitators, community activists and paralegals other FDC projects developed in Cabo Delgado province.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The project guarantees assistance to all social groups without exclusion, therefore people with identified disabilities will be involved in processes of integration and establishment of friendships through the different activities developed in favor of psychosocial support in child-friendly or multipurpose spaces. Specialized services will be provided to ensure provision of redress through the case management component. At the same time, FDC must work with different humanitarian actors to ensure the provision of means of compensation to guarantee access to services for people with identified disabilities.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

FDC guarantees the preservation of cultural and traditional environments at a community level. Therefore, all actions that must be developed in favor of behavioral change will be framed within cultural and traditional guidelines in order to establish a synergistic environment and achieve the established project objectives.
