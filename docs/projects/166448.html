Project Summary

As schools and other educational institutions around the world are closed to prevent the spread of COVID-19. Parents, caregivers and educators have responded in stride, finding new ways to keep childrens in a teaching-learning environment. But not all girls and boys have access to the Internet, books or school supplies. We must do something more to ensure all children have equal access to education. Its very necessary to keep children in learning, no matter where they are.
The project is mainly targeted for 4-14 year old learners in Rohingya Refugee camps and host community schools in Ukhiya and Teknaf area.

Objectives

1. Ensure continued and safe return to quality learning for teachers, learners and school communities 2. Mitigate the impact of Covid-19 on learners, teachers and communities through life-saving messaging, mental health and psycho-social support interventions. 3. Prevent spread and transmission of Corona virus among teachers, learners and school communities

Needs

• Capacity development of caregivers to follow up and support teaching and learning at household level;• Capacity development of teachers to cope with remote learning;•Introduction of delightful education materials for happy & effective response; -Development and dissemination of key messages; • Resources for better hand-washing facilities at learning centers and schools;• Disinfectants and cleaning materials for learning centers and schools;

Activities or outputs

# Develop and provide guidance for teachers who are part of the essential services network and who deliver services door-to-door to caregivers. The guidance will be about how caregivers at home can:- Support children's well being and play.- Promote Educational activities - for learners enrolled and non-enrolled in GIEP/LCFA and youth programme # Develop and deliver audio messages for caregivers on parenting for Early Childhood Development (4 to 14 Years) (including COVID-19 awareness) # Provide color pencils, blank drawing books, student workbooks for GIEP and LCFA and pens for encouraging drawing and craft activities for mental health and psycho social support as well as guidance to caregivers on using the materials to support children well-being and play. #Development and delivery of distance learning guidance during COVID-19 response, including the importance of learning at home (especially for vulnerable groups) and tips for caregivers/community volunteers to facilitate learning in Mayanmar languages. Use referrals and self-care in coordination with child protection, WASH and Health.# Child friendly key message/poster development on COVID-19 awareness with help of CP subsector and ensure the delivery# Develop and disseminate messages for caregivers and parents on COVID-19 awareness # Development and delivery of guidance for School Management Committee members and caregivers in the host community for life-saving messages and mental health and psychosocial support during COVID-19 response in line with efforts organized by the MoE and MoPME # Development and delivery of guidance for head teachers and teachers in the host community for life-saving messages and mental health and psychosocial support during COVID-19 response in line with efforts organized by the MoE and MoPME # Clean and disinfect learning spaces# Establish hand washing facilities;

Key project/performance indicators

1. # of (and/or % of targeted) crisis affected [girls and boys 4-14 ] with access to safe and protective learning spaces with adequate gender-sensitive accessible WASH facilities2. # of (and/or % of targeted) school-closure affected [girls and boys 4 -14 ] with access to lifesaving messages regarding COVID-19 3. # of (and/or % of targeted) school-closure affected caregivers who receive guidance on supporting children’s wellbeing and play 4. # of (and/or % of targeted) school-closure affected [girls and boys 4-14] accessing distance learning5. # of (and/or % of targeted) Burmese Language Instructors/ Caregivers/Teachers oriented in distance learning facilitation
