Project Summary

The project aims at enhancing efficiency, leadership and accountability of LNNGOs responding to the increasing needs in Lebanon through the LHDF by means of different outreach, capacity-sharing and advocacy activities, thus contributing to the advancing of the Localisation agenda in Lebanon.
Moreover, the project also aims at reinforcing LNNGOs PSEAH's policies and procedures, through tailored capacity-building, while contributing to collective Accountability to Affected Populations through the LHDF Helpline.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

LHDF will continue to support the engagement and representation of LNNGOs at the main coordination fora, both at national and regional levels. It will facilitate internal coordination of its members as well as external representation and liaison with key stakeholders, developing a unified LNNGO voice and ensuring close linkages with the communities its members serve and are part of. In particular, LHDF will coordinate with other LNNGO networks, notably within the framework of the Localisation Task Force, with the goal of advancing the Localisation agenda. LHDF will maintain its close cooperation with the INGO Forum (LHIF) to ensure, where relevant, a consistent NGO positioning on strategic and operational issues of concern for both fora. LHDF will also ensure adequate coordination with the UN and donor community, as well as with the GoL and relevant regional and local authorities. Finally, LHDF will seek to consolidate and expand its member’s engagement in regional and global policy fora and processes, such as the Grand Bargain.LHDF will facilitate meaningful participation and representation of its membership through timely information-sharing and adequate consultation, as well as through enhanced capacity-sharing. LHDF will also develop its regional outreach to involve members based in remote areas.Finally, LHDF will maintain its dedicated work streams on PSEAH and AAP through tailored capacity-building and through the management of its Helpline.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

The project foresees a dedicated component on PSEAH, which comprises awareness-raising and technical capacity-building of LNNGOs on PSEAH, in coordination with the PSEAH network and other relevant actors. In addition, and in line with LHDF's priorities, additional efforts will be put into strengthening the engagement of member organizations and local NGOs in general in PSEAH.Moreover, the project embeds a participatory and inclusive approach aimed at engaging with and supporting diverse LNNGOs, including those led and/or working with vulnerable and marginalised groups (women, youth, adolescents, children, refugee, migrants, substance users, LGBTQIA+ individuals, persons living with HIV, persons living with disabilities, etc.). Participation of such organisations in external representation will be actively supported by the Forum.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

The project has a dedicated component on AAP, linked to its existing Helpline. The Forum will further strengthen referral to its Helpline for as a means to enhance Accountability to Affected populations. The Helpline is the entry point, at the field level, for information sharing among CSOs and affected populations as well as for referral of cases raised by the affected communities, facilitating knowledge of and access to assistance to the affected communities. LHDF is a RIMS partner, which enables its Helpline to be even further disseminated across humanitarian partners. helpline information across affected communities. Beyond this specific project component, LHDF will maintain its ongoing support to its members through information and resource-sharing, by facilitating exchange of best practices across its members, as well as through the dissemination of capacity-building opportunities. Moreover, LHDF will support its members’ engagement in the AAP Taskforce.
