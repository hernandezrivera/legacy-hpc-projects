Project Summary

This proposal outlines an integrated nutrition response project targeting conflict-affected communities in Adamawa and Borno States, to be implemented by Nkafamiya Rescue Mission. The project aims to address the urgent needs identified during the 2023 Integrated Food Security Phase Classification (IPC) analysis and the Northeast Nigeria Humanitarian Needs Overview of 2024. Through a comprehensive and multi-sectoral approach, the project intends to enhance access to essential nutrition services, improve dietary diversity, and promote sustainable livelihoods to alleviate malnutrition in the region.
The primary objectives of this project are as follows:
i. To reduce malnutrition rates among conflict-affected children Adamawa and Borno States.
ii. To enhance the capacity of healthcare providers, community leaders, and relevant stakeholders to prevent, detect, and treat malnutrition through training and capacity-building initiatives.
iii. To improve access to nutritious food, hygiene, and safe water practices through targeted interventions and behavior change campaigns.
iv. To strengthen community resilience, early warning systems, and response mechanisms to mitigate the impact of future nutrition crises.
The project will prioritize the following vulnerable populations:
a. Pregnant and lactating women, infants, and children under five years old.
b. Displaced persons, refugees, and host communities affected by conflicts and other emergencies.
c. Female-headed households facing food insecurity and limited access to healthcare services.

1 - Needs

BAY states are experiencing a rapid deterioration in the nutrition situation fueled by spikes in food prices and shortages, outbreaks of communicable disease, armed conflict, inaccessibility, displacements, economic crisis, and low coverage of preventive services. Nutrition Sector continues to advocate for resources to accelerate the response to the nutrition crisis in several LGAs. This is based on data from Nutrition & Food Security Surveillance (NFSS), admission trends, localized SMART surveys, sentinel surveillance, and humanitarian situation monitoring. Admissions of SAM with and without medical complications across BAY states increased by 24% and 18% respectively during the first half of 2022. Maiduguri and Bama have seen the worst trends with a fourfold increase in admissions of SAM with medical complications compared to the same period in 2021.

2 - Activities

NRM will implement these project Activities and Deliverables:• Conduct community-wide malnutrition screenings utilizing standardized tools to identify and refer cases for appropriate treatment.• Establish and strengthen outpatient therapeutic programs in health facilities and mobile clinics to provide treatment for malnourished children• Design and implement community-based nutrition education programs targeting women, caregivers, and community leaders.• Promote optimal maternal, infant, and young child feeding practices through counseling sessions and peer support groups.• Conduct hygiene and sanitation campaigns to improve access to safe water, sanitation facilities, and good sanitation practices.• Provide vulnerable households with agricultural training, inputs, and assets to promote sustainable farming practices and diversified crop production.• Facilitate the establishment of farmers' cooperatives and linkages to markets to enhance income generation and improve access to nutritious foods.• Support income-generating activities, such as livestock rearing and agro-processing, to enhance food security and household resilience.• Strengthen community-based surveillance systems for early detection and reporting of nutrition crises, food insecurity, and health emergencies.• Establish and operationalize nutrition monitoring centers to provide real-time data for decision-making and response planning.• Develop and disseminate comprehensive early warning information, enabling timely action and preventive measures.
