Project Summary

In light of the harsh conditions experienced by Rural Damascus Governorate, specifically Eastern Ghouta, after seven years of war, siege and destruction, the health sector in the region was affected, and in light of the spread of epidemics and diseases.
Needs Assessment has emerged through the absence of medical centers that provide comprehensive services for all specialties with high quality and free of charge, serves the surrounding community through consultations and medical services and spreading health awareness in the area in which the center is to be established and the surrounding areas (Arbeen, Zamalka, Kafr Batna, Hammouriyyeh, Zibdeen, Hazzeh, Jisreen, Beit Sawa, Madyara & Misraba) in which approximately 200,000 people live. The majority of whom are women and children, knowing that in those areas there are only a few medical centers that provide Services that are not comprehensive, insufficient and do not cover the high demand/needs, as in Arbeen area there are only 3 medical centers:
• Arbeen (Arbeen) clinic includes very few specialties and is not daily
• A special center (Al-Shifa Center) has some specializations with high costs.
• Mar Ephrem affiliated center offers some specialties for free, but it does not include only five specialties, in addition to simple x-rays
Bearing in mind that the population density in the region, as mentioned previously, includes 200,000 people, and the standard of living is low, and there is a lack of health awareness.
Based on the previous indications and in view of the urgent need, Child Rights Society has taken the decision to establish a comprehensive medical center that provides the following services:
(Internal - Gastroenterology - orthopedic - cardiac - ophthalmic - Otology - Pediatrics - gynecology - neurological - dermatology - radiology - laboratory -department - Psychiatric Clinic -emergency).
To cover the shortage in medical care and all health services free of charge, provide appropriate health awareness for the advancement of the region, Providing appropriate psychological support to cover the area in terms of health (physical and psychological), reduce the burden of overcrowding on medical centers in Damascus governorate, and save the costs of beneficiaries moving to remote places to obtain services.
Noting that Children Rights Society has a center and a field team work in Eastern Ghouta that works on developing the area, conducting professional, developmental, educational and psychological courses, and conducting a full survey of the area on a permanent basis.
It is worth noting that the Children Rights Society is experienced in medical projects execution as it has previously implemented three medical projects with WHO, these projects are:
• The first health project in 2016
• The second health in 2017
• The third health project in cooperation with the Federation of Charitable Societies and funded by WHO
Based on the above, Children Rights Society aims to establish a comprehensive specialized medical center that includes medical consultations and services free of charge, and spreading awareness of health culture in Arbeen region and the surrounding areas (Arbeen, Zamalka, Kafr Batna, Hammouriyyeh, Zibdeen, Hazzeh, Jisreen, Beit Sawa, Madyara & Misraba) ; and this is the main objective of the project.
Project sub-goals:
- Providing high quality, safe health care in all specialties.
- Raising the percentage of health coverage in the affected areas.
- Raising the level of health awareness and spreading a culture of health care.
- Supplying the area to provide comprehensive medical services free of charge to the residents of Irbin area and the residents of the surrounding areas.
- Raising the standard of living by providing job opportunities for a number of individuals residing in the area and the surrounding areas.
- Reducing mortality rates from transmission of infectious diseases and outbreaks of epidemics.
- The Treating psychologically .

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

1- Transmission of infection.solution: Take all preventive measures from wearing masks to sterilizing hands.2- Electrical short circuit or fire.solution: the presence of fire extinguishers that operate automatically when there is a danger.3- Taking a large number of children to patients in the waiting room.solution: the presence of a small recreational area for children.4- Natural disaster risks.solution: appointing a person,will be responsible for making quick decisions and developing alternative solutions.5- A power outage.solution: the presence of an electric generating set to keep work going smoothly. 6- Delaying the provision of health service.solution: Having an emergency doctor examines emergency cases and transfers them to the hospital when needed 7- The presence of a bad clinical condition that requires immediate referral to the hospital.solution: the presence of an ambulance in the area to serve emergency cases.8- Withdrawal of one of the medical center employees without prior notice.solution: Develop an alternative plan and provide the specialized person as soon as possible9- Work injuries.solution: Take all health and safety measures to preserve the health of workers.10- Severe crowding which leads to the transmission of diseases.solution: designing ventilation holes, taking preventive measures.11 - Carrying out unhealthy habits and among the children of the region.solution: Provide awareness seminars through brochures and advice provided .
