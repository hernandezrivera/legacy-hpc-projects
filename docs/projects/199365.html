Project Summary

WFP aims to meet the food and nutrition needs of vulnerable populations in crisis-affected areas, including refugees, internally displaced persons (IDPs), returnees, and host communities, through a package of unconditional food and nutrition assistance provided through food and cash modalities in conflict-affected areas in Burkina Faso.
During 2023, WFP expects to provide food assistance through the food and cash modality to approximately 1,600,000 vulnerable people including during the lean season.
