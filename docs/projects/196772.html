Project Summary

In line with the priorities of the non-food sector for the year 2023 and the need to work to meet the challenges of the cold winter season by supporting the provision of special humanitarian services to confront the severe cold on a large scale, the beneficiary is unable to meet his non-food needs and faces the winter. Therefore, the Good Youth Association will work to improve the provision of basic services to face the winter cold in the village of Maskana, located in the eastern countryside, and the neighboring villages that do not receive any non-food aid, through a mobile team. . . And a fixed point of service that supports women - children - men and women. The elderly and the disabled are among the poorest families who need services to cope with the cold winter. Where the temperatures in winter reach from -0 to -16, as happened previously two years ago, and the frost caused severe damage to the infrastructure of the houses related to the water pipe that broke as a result of the ice. The project aims through the intervention to meet the needs in the winter season to help students go to their schools and provide the necessary protection from the winter cold and disease prevention, the elderly and the disabled, who are the most vulnerable group. As well as protecting women from diseases by providing them with woolen clothes so that they can do various jobs. And securing protection from various financial, psychological and physical exploitation for the poorest groups.
. The population suffers from the deterioration of the economic situation and the great collapse of the economy, which led to the inability to secure the basics related to facing the winter season. In addition to the presence of displaced people from different regions, the lack of livelihoods and the lack of job opportunities necessary to earn a living, which led to a deterioration in the general level of all services, especially non-food items. For groups of children, the elderly and people with special needs. Supports the center and the mobile team to provide humanitarian support. Services to adapt to the cold winter season and for the poorest families, especially those with children, the elderly, school students and people with special needs. This point will support the following services (winter clothes -). Services will be supported through this point based on the special study and the approved questionnaire in the study of families who meet the conditions for benefiting from non-food items in the face of the winter season and who did not receive assistance from any party, and the mobile team will visit areas where transportation is difficult and provide non-food assistance to families The poorest, especially children, women, school students, the elderly and people with special needs.
The center and the mobile team will work 6 hours/day, 5 days a week under the project
The association studied the coping needs during the cold winter and communicated with village officials and villagers during that period

3 - Needs

Theshabab alkhair Association collects and reviews ratings and data on the basic services provided. Situation analysis, risk assessment and then project plan according to the needs of the residents of the area. Through communication and coordination with the authorities and residents directly. The need was assessed through the medical center in the eastern countryside, the village of Tal Zubaydah in 2021/2022, which was implemented by the Good Youth Association with the support of OCHA. The suffering of the people and neighboring villages was noted in the difficulty of coping with the winter season for various reasons, the most important of which is the economic situation and severe weather temperatures. The cold, which makes them vulnerable to diseases and exploitation, especially women and children. The eastern countryside suffers from a great variation in temperatures in winter, with frosts sometimes reaching -16 degrees Celsius, especially in the countryside. 75% of the population is unable to secure and meet the needs of the cold winter due to the collapse of the economic situation and the lack of job opportunities. The high cost of non-food items. Fuel, which means not providing and securing basic needs, especially winter clothes, which constitute a heavy burden on the poorest families. What is required is the opening of a distribution point for non-food items to support the provision of basic services and the operation of a mobile team for remote villages.

4 - Implementation of activities

* The project activities are implemented through the opening of a fixed center for the distribution of winter clothes and a mobile team for remote villages and communication with the elderly, the disabled and the most vulnerable among women as well. Fair and equal distribution is made for all groups and ages * The poorest families are identified through a questionnaire in cooperation with the village head and to ensure that there is no duplication of distribution with other organizations or associations. An employee makes weekly visits to the homes of the beneficiaries of the services, records all information and the quality of services provided from non-food items, then submits the report to study the evaluation results and. The decision is either to continue and develop the work, or to correct errors, if any. * Monitoring the improvement of the beneficiaries of the project services, which reflects positively on their daily practice, reducing diseases, and saving financial expenses. Students return to their schools and their commitment to daily attendance and prevent exploitation in all its forms. Over 200 beneficiaries per month.- Carry out impact assessments on the reach/impact of MHPss- GBV/

9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Difficulty in access: To face these risks, we formed a mobile team, especially for special cases such as disabilities that prevent children or women from reaching the center and obtaining service.Power outages in the distribution center To counter these risks, we will purchase an electric battery- Financial risks: The rate set by the central bank for these transfers shall be at a special rate for United Nations transfers. This system is called the UN exchange, and the central bank does not allow these transfers to be subject to price fluctuations as it happens in the black market.* The risks of a decline in the financial exchange rate are very low and negligible as a result of the direct control and supervision of the Central Bank. * In the event of a change in the exchange rate here, the association communicates and coordinates with the organization to coordinate the problem and solve it in the best possible way.
