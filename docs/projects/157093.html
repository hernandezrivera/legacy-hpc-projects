Project Summary

purpose of the project is to improve the under five children, pregnant women and breastfeeding mother’s nutrition in South Kordofan targeting 497 IDPs and 9,024 host communities (Ab jubaiha 340 IDPs and 3,762 host communities,and El abbasiya locality 157 IDPs and 5,262 host communities) including women and children. In Blue Nile state targeting 22,856 host communities women and children. Targeted localities are, El Roseires(12,044) and El Damazine(10,812) . In Kassala state,targeted host communities are 26,871 women and children .Targeted localities are Kassala (12,243) and Rural Kassala (14,637) . Total target is 59,248 women and children . The project will conduct Initial assessment targeting both women, men, girls and boys as the sample will be 50- 50 in 24 communities which aims to identify the activities in each community and the best strategy for awareness raising, also to analyze the strengths and weaknesses of the community’s initiatives gender relations, practices, activities, the opportunities and the risks in dealing with nutrition services. The assessment will be conducted by a team from the nutrition department,Training for 600 female Community Nutrition Promoters on the basics of nutrition, the external treatments for acute malnutrition, nutrition of pregnant women and breastfeeding mothers, nutrition of neonatal, nutrition of sick children, health and nutritional education and communication for behavior change. The project will admit malnutrition preventing program and malnutrition treatment program for targeted girls ,boys(6-23 month),PLWS and boys and girls(6-59 month).Also the target population will be provided with micro nutrient supplementation. Target localities will adapt and implement multi-sectoral approaches to tackle malnutrition . And also implementing (gender inclusive ) community based food security and livelihood intervention contributing to lowering malnutrition( Cultivation of vegetables fruit and Guddaim trees and provision of solar stoves to ensure good cooking and to safe fuel) .

Needs

Amnesty International noted in its 2018 report on Sudan that (FEWS-NET) reported that the humanitarian situation in SPLM-N-controlled areas in South Kordofan was dire. The rate of chronic malnutrition was estimated at 38.3% due to long-term food deprivation and recurrent illness. Under 5 children and pregnant women as well breastfeeding mothers are vulnerable to poverty as a result of low income and accessibility to basic services and nutrition which lead to malnutrition among under 5 children and mothers. Thousands of IDPs are living in Blue Nile State, mostly in Eldmazein and Elroseries localities seeking security and better access to basic services. The health system in the areas that received IDPs is weak and there is a lack of health and nutrition services for the vulnerable displaces children and women. According to assessment conducted by Labena local NGO, there is risk SAM and MAM among children, lactating mothers and elderly persons particularly in Damazin and ElRosseiris localities. People of these studied areas live below the low standard of living with poor income, deteriorated health and nutrition condition and acute poverty .In Kassala State, Cultural, environmental and behavioral factors contribute to the alarmingly poor nutritional intake among children and pregnant mothers. Awareness of good quality high nutrition foods is very low among the families, sorghum and millet being the primary stable food, use of protein rich animal food is limited .Mothers have low awareness of the need for exclusive breastfeeding for newly born babies. Prevalent gender inequality also contributes to malnutrition. Overall; low status of women in decision making of family and community matters is a significant factor contributing to the problem. Women in the rural areas are very actively involved in the agricultural cultivation process.However they can be oriented towards cultivation of various vegetables and fruit so as to participate in prevention of malnutrition. Women as the primary caregivers in the family are responsible for food preparation and distribution among family members in line with the culture of serving the best food the family could afford to the men and boys of the household.

Activities

1. Initial assessment targeting both women, men, girls and boys as the sample will be 50- 50 in 24 communities which aims to identify the activities in each community and the best strategy for awareness raising, also to analyze the strengths and weaknesses of the community’s initiatives gender relations, practices, activities, the opportunities and the risks in dealing with nutrition services. The assessment will be conducted by a team from the nutrition department. 2. Training for 600 female Community Nutrition Promoters from 24 communities in 6 localities for 15 days on the basics of nutrition, the external treatments for acute malnutrition, nutrition of pregnant women and breastfeeding mothers, nutrition of neonatal, nutrition of sick children, health and nutritional education and communication for behavior change, growth monitoring, vaccination, environmental health and gender equality. 3. Awareness sessions on nutrition through home visits, sessions and broadcast messages, this targets both women and men with proportions of 50 - 50, as pregnant and lactating women needs more information on regular bases to take care of themselves and children 4- Monitoring visits. 5- Demonstration on use of locally available food to the communities with emphasis placed on preparation of balanced diet using low cost food.6- MUAC screening for the targeted children both boys and girls. 7. Production of IEC materials on nutrition, gender equality and environmental hygiene and conservation. 8-Treatment and preventing malnutrition regarding women and children.9-provision of food based nutrition services. Provision of micronutrient.10 provision of seedling,seeds and fences for cultivation of vegetables ,fruit and Guddaim trees.Provision of Solar stoves to 500 households.
