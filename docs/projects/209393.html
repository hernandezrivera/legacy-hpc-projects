Project Summary

Through this project Human Appeal will respond to the gender and age specific needs by distributing lifesaving NFIs and shelter items to vulnerable IDPs mainly in Khartoum, Sennar and Blue Nile States (mainly ‘hard to reach’ areas under coordination with the Cluster and its partner), with possible inclusion of host communities as a way of conflict mitigation measure.
Mainly the project will deliver lifesaving NFI services for (3,000 HHs) 15,000 displaced people through the distribution of NFIs packages. In addition, 150 members of community leaders and government actors will be trained on vulnerability assessment, emergency preparedness, community-based approach, and sustainable use of natural resources for shelter purposes. Moreover, a total number of 1,000 beneficiaries will be targeted by GBV and environmental awareness messages.
The main activities will be included:
• Conduct needs assessment, registration and verification for NFIs distribution to 3000 HHs (15000 Individuals) (family size 5) (5000 women, 3000 men, 7000 boys and girls) in Khartoum, Sennar and Blue Nile states.
• Establishment/strengthening of ES/NFI’s distribution systems through conducting of sustained post-distribution monitoring of NFI distributions.
• Distribution of NFIs to 3000 HHs (15000 Individuals) (family size 5) (5000 women, 3000 men, 7000 boys and girls) with consideration to the specific needs of most vulnerable people including affected women headed households, and persons with specific needs
• Training of 150 members of community leaders, and government actors on vulnerability assessment, emergency preparedness, community - based approach, and sustainable use of natural resources for shelters purposes,(40% female).

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

Through this intervention, HA is committed safeguard beneficiaries from violence and discrimination and is aimed at obtaining full respect for the rights of the individuals. HA will implement the project in impartial and non-discriminatory ways that promote the safety, dignity and integrity of the people receiving assistance during assessment, design of project and delivery of services. The project team will ensure all elements of accountability to affected population to be captured through extensive engagement and consultation with the community. Project is designed to inform all displaced about the availability and location of services. The project ensures safe and equal access to inclusive and non-discriminatory provision of services. Shelter construction is designed to ensure that women, men, girls and boys with specific shelter needs or vulnerabilities are prioritized and supported. The intervention will also carefully apply Do No Harm approach to avoid any negative impact that it might bring to the area or the targeted communities. Manage information in a sensitive manner so that the security of informants or others who may be identifiable is not jeopardized. The assistance and protection measures will not undermine local capacities for self-protection as well as support the efforts of the affected population and local communities to find security and restore dignity.

The feedback received from the communities will be processed as follows:- Collecting Feedback: Through robust complaint and feedback mechanism established for the project, all the complaints and feedback will be received in line with the SoPs and shared with the designated staff for analysis. - Analyzing Feedback: The designated staff will analyze the feedback receive and share with analysis with the project team and the management. - Applying Feedback: If the management deems feedback productive and acting on the feedback may increase efficiency, effectiveness and impact of the project, it will be applied. Where complaints are concerned, all the complaints will be resolved and redressal provided. - Communicating Feedback: The person providing feedback/registering complaint will be communicated back informing the feedback was received fixed.

4.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will support Inter-Agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including Sexual exploitation and abuse complaints?

HA-UK has established multi-sector Community Feedback and Response Mechanisms (CFRM) in all its operational sites including in the proposed project location (South and West Kordofan states) through which the beneficiaries will be informed on their rights to launch complaints or give other forms of feedback in regard to the implementation process and the quality of services they are receiving from the HA-UK. The existing complaint and feedback mechanism will be used during the project period with beneficiaries being sensitized on beneficiary accountability standards. Communities will also be informed about the focal point in the HA-UK to handle any compliant report and feedback. The reporting procedures will be displayed in a visible place in the project location and complains and feedback boxes will be placed at WGSS and project site offices for those who are literate to use. Views and feedback will be collected from women, girls and community beneficiaries including those living with different disabilities and the minority, authorities from the start of the project to the intervention and monthly thereafter. Feedback and complaints will generally be collected using FGD’s with groups of men, women, boys, and girls; during community sensitization and awareness sessions; and through the use of suggestion boxes. HA’s community feedback mechanism allows for a range of complaints to be received and managed, including a mechanism for confidentially fast-tracking concerns of a serious nature such as Sexual Exploitation and Abuse. Through this discussion, the information and the feedback obtained from the beneficiaries of the HA-UK will explain its procedures, decisions and allow women and girls to hold HA-UK accountable. Awareness raising on existing PSEA Networks or Focal Point(s) will be included to ensure that beneficiaries are well informed of existing reporting mechanism
