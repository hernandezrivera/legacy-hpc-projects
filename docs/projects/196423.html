Project Summary

While the crisis has affected all people living in Syria to varying degrees, some segments of the population and locations have been more acutely affected than others. Homs is one of these locations which suffered from hostilities. Despite the very first steps into recovery stage, all population group inside Homs are still with limited access to development services, particularly, adolescents and young people. The needs expand to include countryside areas which suffer from a severe weakness in services. “Adolescents and youth, an age group significantly impacted by the protracted conflict, have had to prematurely assume adulthood responsibilities, missing out on many opportunities especially in learning and development.
We believe that empowering young people as rights holders and preparing them for active citizenship and adulthood initiates a cycle of opportunities that brings positive outcomes for both the individual and the community.” (UNICEF.org)
ADAP projects helped Al-Birr association to provide more support and reach for more youth and adolescents to empower them with life skills and a lot of useful trainings and activities that will help them in building their personalities, careers and more communication lines with the society. The impact of ADAP project was very clear in the life of many young people in the city of Homs, but now, we are looking forward for more empowerment and support to youth in the countryside where there is no much access to services available in the city or even the same amount of technological development.
We believe that working with this age group in such areas will help in building a better future for them and even for their villages and families by giving all the support possible, provide them with life skills that will change their view to their future and make them more aware about the importance of being very well educated, developed and good persons in their societies.
The Projects aims at supporting youth and developing their skills under different programs in 3 fixed locations in Homs City and its northern and western rural, in addition to, 2 mobile teams that will mobilize the provided services in the eastern rural of Homs and AL-Houla.
The project is designed to provide life skills trainings to 5000 beneficiaries from the youths in the targeted areas, technical and vocational education training (TVET) to 2350 beneficiaries, 450 adolescents will be supported for promoting civic engagement and meaningful participation through initiatives led by them, promote participation of 1000 adolescents in social cohesion activities through sport for development activities and equipping 21 trainees with capacity building training on life skills to be a life skills trainers in the targeted areas.
Al-Birr chose those area since their severity scales are severe (the highest severity scale is 3 in eastern rural area of Homs including Qaryatein and Farqalas, and 3 in Dar Kabirah (in Talbiseh district)) or in stress (2) in other areas (in Homs city, and Tall Kalakh) since there is no LSCE program implemented in those area. Al-Birr Association - with budget of 1.5 M USD in 2020, and estimated budget of about 750 K USD in 2021- has reached more than 100 thousand beneficiaries in general during 2020.

3 - Needs

" With an estimated 18 per cent of school age children not attending any form of learning, out of school children remain one of the most affected groups. Over three quarters of 12-17-year-olds who were not in school had dropped out. School dropout exposes children to protection threats such as child labour (mainly for boys) and early marriage (mainly for girls)." (HNO, 2022). Due to the compounding impact of the 2021 situation, many students are either behind in their learning, finding it difficult to return to school or at risk of dropping out. Younger children, adolescents and children with disabilities face further challenges accessing learning services due to limited availability of ECE centers, secondary education services and vocational training opportunities, Because of these problems, it's very important to provide those adolescents with LSCE program, especially skills for learning and personal empowerment. " Lack of access to secondary education in many areas limits the ability of adolescents to continue their education; the lack of vocational education prevents adolescents who will not return to school from learning skills needs for economic opportunities. In nearly a third of households with children not in school it was because children were working."(HNO,2022). In general LSCE program is very important to adolescents and youth, since 11years of crisis in Syria has affected the whole lives of children, especially their self-reliance and their personal skills.

4 - Implementation of activities

The project aims to starting 3 fixed centres in ( Homs city – northern rural of Homs – western rural of Homs) and 2 mobile teams to mobilize the services in villages of Hula and eastern rural of Homs. Al-Birr will implement this project in these areas since it has old branches in these areas, and it's well-known there. The project will provide: 1- Training of 21 life skills facilitator 2- Life skills courses to 5000 adolescents 3- 2350 beneficiaries will be provided with TVET. 4- Sport for development activities for 1000 beneficiaries. 5- Promote 450 adolescents’ and young people’s civic engagement and meaningful participation through 10 initiatives actively led by them. 6- Encouraging drop-out children to back to school through BTL campains. 7. M&E activities will be conducted through an M&E team who will get feedback from beneficiaries through field visits, focus group discussions, and surveys conducted, to evaluate the quality of services provided. All activities will be reported through 4Ws and FTS.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risks: Students are not sufficiently protected from adults or older students while present in a learning centers Likelihood: Medium Impact: High Mitigating measures: Al-Birr will separate people so that opportunities for children to be alone with adults should be avoided. Trainers will be provided with basic PSS, so that they can deal with cases. In addition, trainers will be regularly trained on positive discipline and child safeguarding. 2. Beneficiary and other information is not sufficiently protected. likelihood: low. Impact: High. Mitigation measures: All staff will sign code of conduct including protecting information that may be sensitive or could put people at risk. This should include when equipment, paper and/or electronic data and documents should be relocated or destroyed. Electronic records will be available only for authorized staff, where they can reach it by security codes. Fixed centres will be equipped with needed security cameras, which can give a proof for any illegal behaviour.


8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Risk: sexual exploitation and abuse for people in need to get the benefit of services, or even during sessions of trainings. likelihood: medium, impact: high. 1. Al-Birr will implement its PSEA planned strategy where all its staff have to sign on code of conduct for PSEA in order to not use any kind of sexual exploitation and abuse while implementing their activities. 2. According to PSEA proposed strategy, a PSEA focal point will supervise training staff and raising their awareness about PSEA strategy, in addition to his role in management of complains regarding any sexual exploitation or abuse occurred during the implementation


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

1-One of the potential risks during the implementation stage of the project is -the fluctuation of prices of the needed materials and equipment. Likelihood: medium Impact: high. Mitigation measure: AL-Birr will sign a frame agreement with the suppliers at the beginning of the project to guarantee that all the needed supplies and materials will be purchased at fixed prices during the whole project. In addition some variations are taken into consideration during 2. Impact of COVID-19 Pandemic on people. likelihood: medium, impact: high. Mitigation Measure: Al-Birr is taking into consideration all precautionary measures including lowering the number of beneficiaries in a session, sterilizing, and using sanitizers.
