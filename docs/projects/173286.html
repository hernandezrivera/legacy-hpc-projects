Project Summary

RPN (Reaching People In Need) will continue to support in camp 10 with 2000 TSA2 support and 500 emergency support. The project will be focused on Shelter support to the beneficiaries in regular and emergency basis. The need is aligned with S/NFI sector and respond to the needs reflected in the MSNA 2020. AAP and SADD has also been considered in this project. Also for bringing sustainability, .RPN (Reaching People In Need) will continue providing shelter assistant to improve the physical living conditions for the refugees by reinforcing their shelters with TSA2 assistance. RPN will consult beneficiaries on the household and on the communal level taking into account age, gender and diversity and the specific needs of individuals in camps. This project aims to improve the shelter conditions, Rohingya refugees living in the camp throughTSA2 assistance based on shelter/NFI Sector guidance. As part of TSA II,RPN encouraged to proactively engage girls, boys and women to enhance their role and participation in all programmer activities.. The community mobilization teams include women and are properly trained on communication with the community, and mitigation of power dynamics; - Sensitization messaging with men and women on equitable participation. Girls, women, boys and men may have different priorities due to a diverse use of the living space, and should be given the right to select materials accordingly. Where possible, encourage and support women-led entrepreneurship initiatives for the production of some building components (tiara, gardenia, bamboo mats, etc.). Partners are strongly encouraged to consult the Livelihood Working Group to tap into similar existing livelihood projects. - Consider close cooperation with Women Friendly Spaces and Adolescent Friendly Spaces.
RPN will ensure complaint feedback mechanism by installing help desks in the distribution points. In addition, RPN will ensure that distribution point is accessible to PwSN, priority lines for women, older people and PwSN, shade and space to seat while waiting are organized. TSA2 assistance includes also training on DRR and construction techniques which will be provided with the focus on inclusion of women, youth participation.

1 - Identified Needs

From MSNA 2020 it is stated that 69% Of households reported facing issues with their shelter in the 6 months prior to data collection whereas most of them facing issues with roof, wall, floor, plinth, damaged materials etc. RPN will focuses on those needs of household through transitional shelter assistance and emergency shelter support. The various reports and assessments are quite clear with regards to the lack of sustainability in the materials and construction techniques used for the majority of the shelters in the camps.69% (J-MSNA2020) Issues with shelter remained a common concern for the majority of HHs with almost one third of HHs.(J-MSNA 2020) Reporting having bought shelter materials to make repairs more then a quarter of HHs nt being able to make improvements. The topography and soil composition also demand interventions on a household level to prevent slope failures and improve access. The Rohingya refugee’s main safety concerns are unstable structure Humanitarian crises affect individuals differently depending on their gender, age, disability and other personal characteristics. Humanitarian responses should pay particular attention to persons with specific needs (PSN), as these groups may find it harder than others to access assistance and protection actors. The most vulnerable individuals and families often require additional support with material transportation from distribution points, and repair, maintenance, and upgrading of their shelters. Additional support for Household with Special Needs to be consulted and processes followed in the design and implementation of TSA II. 12 In particular, households without able-bodied male members of 18-60 years old and households with one or more person with physical disabilities need to be provided with further support as described below. This is in addition to the total assistance provided to all families. - Households without able-bodied male member of 18-60 years old. This among others especially focuses on child headed households, single parent households and households with elderly members. To be supported with: Delivery of all materials from distribution point to their shelter; Labor support for implementation of shelter repair, maintenance and upgrading. - Households with one or more person with physical disabilities or/and visual impairment. To be supported with: ▪ Access to the technical support for design and implementation of specific accessibility related shelter interventions; as part of TSA II should not be used for additional accessibility interventions).Regular Monitoring and Reporting: RPN project staff regularly monitor the project activities and report the Shelter sector regularly. RPN closely monitor the activities through field level staff and volunteers. Direct feedback mechanism from beneficiaries to Executive Director via mobile and inspection from will established. Across all interventions, extremely vulnerable individuals will be identified through household-level data collection and consultation with community leaders. RPN will ensure all Shelter staff are trained on GBV and PSEA. To integrate GBV into programming, RPN will actively consult both female and male refugees to seek feedback on key considerations.
