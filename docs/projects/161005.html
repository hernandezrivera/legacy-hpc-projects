Project Summary

Mercy Corps shall be looking for additional funding in addition to the project submitted with reference# HNGA20-SHL-161579-1 that is in pipeline. This additional project shall compliment the pipeline to meet the unmet needs in the same locations where Mercy Corps already intervening with the aforementioned project in the pipeline. Mercy Corps shall be looking for additional funding to address additional shelter/NFIs needs of new arrivals, existing IDPs, returnees and the host community in target LGAs to assist 10,601HHs (53,005 individuals) who lies within Physical and Mental Wellbeing (Category 1). 1000 new arrival HHs shall receive e comprehensive kit comprise on standard Non-food Items (NFIs) and basic emergency shelter kits to avoid letting them wait in open air. 2500 IDPs and host community HHs shall receive repairs and roofing assistance those have completely damage shelters. Mercy Corps also aims to promote ongoing self-building efforts by supporting families to rebuild mud block structures through a CFW intervention. 1000 existing IDPs shall receive improvement kits whose shelters are deteriorated while 500 IDP HHs living in open for long shall receive enhance shelter kits. Mercy Corps shall construct collapsible transportable transitional shelters for IDPs who are expected to return to their locations as well as for those who are at risk of forced eviction. Mercy Corps shall be Provide housing, land and property (HLP) as cross-cutting support to facilitate tenancy agreements in order to avoid forced evictions. Women are particularly vulnerable to forced evictions and MC will utilize lessons learned to prioritize this vulnerable group. All IDP households will receive technical assistance to repair, improve, upgrade or reconstruct their shelters according to global standards i.e. SPHERE and local Shelter CCCM technical guidelines. Beneficiaries unable to work based on the vulnerability criteria will be given additional support. GBV and protection will be mainstreamed in technical designs of shelters, settlement and targeting, ensuring the safety and dignity of women and girls and their equal access. The repairs and upgrades shall be community-based coupled with market-based approach to reinvigorate the local market and vendors. Prepositioned vendors shall fast track the response time. Mercy Corps will distribute the shelter and NFIs kits via in-kind and/or commodity vouchers (based on the local market strength and access). However, for the location where markets are non-functional or lack the capacity to absorb; there shall be prepositioning of stocks. The purpose is to serve the immediate needs of displacement affected households by prepositioning these items in areas with large numbers of expected returnees and new arrivals. The program locations without operational presence, Mercy Corps will preposition the stocks in strategic locations in the shelter sector common storage to move the stocks immediately and respond to an emergency situation. In addition, a master agreement to be signed to top up the available stocks well before the depletion of stocks or mobilize vendors to locations for the trade fair.
Mercy Corps aim for an alternative materials research to find low-cost solutions in collaboration with academic institutions, Shelter sector TWIG for the longevity of the shelter assistance provided and linking research outcome with livelihood and early recovery sector to develop the market. Mercy Corps also aim to design low cost collapsible transitional shelter solution to firefight the land issues and link relief recovery to development. The design shall be replicated with the support of the shelter sector, NEMA, SEMA and CCCM as a pilot.

This project is a proposal for future funding opportunities to be designed based on CCCM and shelter sector gaps and needs analyses in priority LGAs for the year 2020. as a plan for Mercy Corps to conduct a second round of need assessment in January-February 2020 to measure the gaps.

2 - Activities

Mercy Corps shall be providing following assistance through CVA modality a) Providing lifesaving emergency shelter kits and NFIs as comprehensive kit to 1000 HHs (5000 individuals) to new arrivals b) Providing shelter improvement kits to 500HHs (2500 individuals) for the improvement of makeshift shelters and maintaining emergency shelters in camps & informal settlements due to congestion, overcrowding and/or seasonal impacts. Congested camps and informal settlements with a special focus on families with more girls to avoid protection and GBV risks by improving safety and privacy aspects of the deteriorated or poor shelter and providing additional space in overcrowded shelters. c) Lifesaving shelter repairs, up-gradation and improvements in non-camp settings for 500 HHs (2,500 individuals) those in need of life-saving repairs / rebuilding of their burnt / completely damaged shelters to assist extremely vulnerable overcrowded families (IDPs, returns and host community) living in poor sheltering conditions and in critical need of privacy and comfort especially HHs with more girls and women. Additionally CFW shall be introduced for mud block production for the structure d) Improving privacy of 1,000 overcrowded HHs (with more women at family) by providing privacy partitions. e) 2000 Roofing kits for returnees and host community who have self-built structures to support self-recovery f) Enhanced Emergency shelter kits to 500 longer terms displaced IDPs and or returnees HHs belong to inaccessible areas in camps and outside g) Construction of 500 collapsible transitional shelters
