Project Summary

NGO X proposes to provide lifesaving safe water to approximately 1,024,635 vulnerable individuals in Idleb Governorate, North West Syria. This will be implemented via the provision of essential support to 66 Water Stations (WSs) and 4 Water Units (WUs). NGO X has extensive experience delivering water supply programming in Idleb since 2014. In 2020 NGO X provided clean safe water to the homes of over one million people in Idleb via 66 water stations and 4 water units. The proposed project builds upon the experience of having successfully delivered several similar projects with OCHA, FCDO, and BHA funding.
NThe aim of the action is to ensure access to reliable, safe, and clean drinking water to vulnerable conflict-affected communities including host and displaced populations in high priority northern sub-districts of Idleb Governorate. Core activities will include procurement of fuel, minor infrastructure rehabilitation, training to water station operators, coaching to WUs, water quality monitoring and hygiene promotion.
The proposed action will enable people living in the targeted areas to access a sufficient quantity of clean and safe drinking water. Following WASH cluster standards, 35 litres/person/day (LPD) will be piped directly to households (HH) to ensure equitable access for all community members. It should be noted that some water networks have limited capacity, and can not reach the full 35 LPD target.
In target areas, to complement the water supply activities, HHs—including school children, WU staff and individuals benefiting from NGO X food or cash distributions will be reached with essential hygiene promotion sessions adapted to COVID-19 best working practices as outlined by the WASH cluster. Themes covered will include water safety, conservation, and safe hygiene.
NGO X will also provide WASH services in camps. NGO X will install water supply infrastructure provide fuel, oil and other consumables to camp water stations. NGO X will install sewer infrastructure and will undertake regular desludging services to septic tanks located on site. To ensure adequate and appropriate solid waste collection, NGO X will also install bins and undertake solid waste collection.

4 - Needs

In North West Syria, 1.4 million people need safe water systems (WASH Cluster dashboard). 43% of water systems are suspended due to lack of operational support or require some level of rehabilitation and maintenance. Out of the 57% of operational water stations, 21% are functioning without any water disinfection (WASH HNO 2021). In 2020 and until 1 March 2021, NGO X supported 66 water stations in Idleb Governorate, suppling clean water to a catchment population of more than a million people. The projects were funded by OCHA SCHF, FCDO and BHA. However today the projected funding forecast looks bleak. NGO X has only secured funds to cover 14 out of the 66 water stations in Idleb Governorate leaving a gap of 600,000 individuals without access to safe, free, reliable, and equitable water. Without further investment 52 stations will go unfunded leaving HHs without safe reliable water. The alternatives are limited; local authorities do not have the resources required to operate these stations and water trucking services will be placed under enormous pressure and are unlikely to be able to cope with demand. 65% of the key informants reported that the IDP sites do not have adequate sanitation facilities available, 57% of reported not having access to handwashing facilities while 85% of respondents reported that there is no available soap for handwashing. 86% of KIs reported that they are entirely reliant on humanitarian actors to provide their daily household water needs. (CARE RNA 2021)

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Support to water supply systems Risk: Unequal distribution of services inadvertently empower or strengthen the position of armed groups /or theft of rehabilitation/construction materials by groups in order to use in conflict activities Mitigation: Establish strong links with authorities and operators to promote equity and the principle of ‘do no harm’ at all times; Ensure a joint planning culture and agree on minimum standards; Include community and authorities in targeting process; Ensure sensitive information is handled with care. Risk: Improper storage of hazardous supplies (chlorine and other materials) - Possibility of explosion, human casualty, and asset destruction. Mitigation: Ensure proper storage of materials with clear provision of care and safety consideration Emergency sanitation and solid waste management Risk: Toilets present GBV and/or health safety risks Mitigation: Ensure agreed context/SPHERE standards are applied and closely monitor their provision/aspects during implementation; Ensure women and adolescent girls are consulted during design, implementation and monitoring phase of projects; Accountability mechanisms are put in place and received feedback from users is followed up. Risk: Movements of desludging trucks and garbage trucks in congested camp-like settings Mitigation: Establish clear routes and schedules for desludging trucks

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumptions: - Continued access to targeted areas and vulnerable populations, while security conditions remain sufficiently stable; - Sufficient and timely access to required inputs through procurement, available transportation remains; - Strong coordination and collaboration with local authorities and humanitarian actors in the AoO prevails; - NGO X's strategies and mechanisms to prevent armed and sanctioned actors from interference in programmes that violate the JOPs remain effective; Risks: - Remote management presents the risk of international and Syrian staff losing legal status; sudden border closures disrupting operations; other regulatory changes which could affect the way NGO X operates cross-border; - There is a risk of fraud (misappropriation/theft of funds/assets) including banking, cash management, procurement, transport, and programming; - Tools/repair supplies not available: repairs not able to be completed on time due to lack of availability of parts and other supplies; - Fuel theft or misuse at water stations; Aid diversion. - NGO X loses access to some areas in Idleb or Aleppo governorate as a result of shifting political and military control on the ground. Mitigation: - Safety and Security Evacuation Plans and Standard Operating Procedures in place, and an experienced security team on both sides of the border; - Ongoing communication with actors operational across the operational area, and continued adherence to Acceptance Strategies built on community and stakeholder understanding of NGO X’s programming; - NGO X has a strong reconciliation system. Any suspected cases are reported by the WASH team directly to area management team and to SMT to open an investigation.
