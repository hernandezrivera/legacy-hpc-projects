Project Summary

Owing to the absolute need and importance of Housing, Land and Property for the population in conflict situations, COHESODEC seeks to implement this project in the North-West and South-West Regions of Cameroon so as to significantly ameliorate on the housing, land and property rights of affected populations in these regions. Vulnerable communities targeted to benefit from this HLP project will include the following: Bafut, Bamenda 2, Bali, Bamenda 3, Tubah, Batibo, Mbengwi, Kumba 2 Kumba 3, Konye and Mbonge in both affected regions.
This project seeks to target a total of 1,750 conflict affected populations in badly affected areas to ensure the provision of individual legal assistance related to land disputes and access to land ensuring that at least 50 victims receive legal assistance.
Moreover, this project will be ensuring the provision of 50 individual legal assistance related to housing disputes (forced eviction and secondary occupation) as well as making sure badly affected victims among the population are provided with individual counseling on HLP related issues giving 100 affected beneficiaries the privilege of receiving counseling services.
Furthermore, COHESODEC in the implementation of this project will ensure significant sensitization involving 1000 members of local communities, local authorities, and traditional leaders on HLP rights and obligations including community sensitization, radio messaging, leaflets, etc. The project will also be strengthening the capacities of 30 local authorities and traditional leaders to effectively address HLP related disputes, this by effectively training on HLP related matters.
