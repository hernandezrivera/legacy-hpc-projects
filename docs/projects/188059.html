Project Summary

This proposal is planned and not secured.
SCA designed its Food Security interventions to meets the actual food needs in northwest of Syria and comply with the strategic objectives ( SO1) and FSA Cluster Guidelines.
This proposal aims to Improve the food security status of assessed food insecure people through life saving and life sustaining food assistance targeting : 4832 HHs 27418 INDV ( 5765 men, 6166 women, 7142 boys, 8346 girls) , in the 10 camps in Danan sub district :
- Al Safa wa Al Basher/
- Al Etihad(Bab Alhawa)
- Aljihad /
- Basames /
- AlAkraboon
- Bardakli Aljaded2/
- AlFares
- Al Faterah/
- Ktyan /
- TilalHalab
To avoid any overlab, Saed will share information and project activites with FSL cluster and humanitarian partners working in the area , and in the event of a realistic overlab in one or more of the camps, Saed will replace these camps with other camps that suffer from a food gap.
The planned objectives will be achieved through two main activities:
1- Distribution of regular Food Baskets to 4832 HHs over 8 rounds for IDPs in the13 camps to meet 70 % of food needs, the duration oh this activity is 8 months in 2022.
The food baskets has been designed according to FSA recommendation.
2- Distribution of bread to 4832 HHs for 12 months ( 8 months in 2022 , 4 months in 2023) ,At the rate of 1 bread package (1 kg) for a family of less than five people, and 2 bread packages for every family of five or more people.
In order to reinforce COVID-19 protection, soap will be distributed on beneficiaries in average 750 g soap to each beneficiary household monthly.
SCA will ensure the commitment of all staff, labors and related contractors to Humanitarian Principles, Codes of Conduct and PSEA Code of Conduct and Child Protection.

4 - Needs

Acoording to draft HRP 2022-2023 "The FSL Sector has seen an increase in the severity of needs, especially regarding lack of food and agriculture-based livelihoods. The Sector FSA/FSLA assessments for 2020, as well as the mid-year review process for 2021, highlights increased food insecurity across the whole country . This alarming increase in food and agriculture needs is due to a complex combination of inter-linked contextual drivers which can only be addressed through an integrated approach."To understand the real needs for camps' population and figuring out the uncovered gaps and problems , In the second half of 2021 SCA conducted a need assessment in 42 camps under Dana mareet mesrin sub-district using many ways of collecting data in coordination with stakeholders (IDPs, local authorities and clusters), SCA conducted the following: - Rapid survey. - Conducting 13 FGDs that covered different age and sex categories of IDPs - Meeting with camp managers to specify current interventions by humanitarian partners.The summary:-There are 27,418 IDPs(4832 HH) in 13 camps in -Dana sub district suffer from a food gap and do not receive any food assistance .- 80% of IDPs do not have a stable income due to the lack of stable job opportunities, Although most of IDPs have been living in these camps for 5 years.-All targeted IDPs have kitchen tools and stoves, and will benefit from food baskets.- The nearest market to the targeted camps is more than 2km away.

5 - Implementation of activities

Before the start of the actual implementation, SAED will coordinate with the local authorities and obtain the required approvals.- Identify in beneficiaries after data collection and analysis in addition to collect any other necessary data (i.e. market assessment or security assessment).- SAED will develop a plan for the distribution, specify the distribution points and the date of the distributions with the participation of the beneficiaries.In addition, all COVID-19 prevention measures will be taken into consideration when planning distributions.- Starting from 1/5/2022, the distribution of food assistance through the different modalities will start(food basket, bread) as follows:- Each HH will receive one food basket for 8 months (1/5/2022-31/12/2022).In addition to 750 g of soap for each HH as one of the measures to prevent Covid-19-Each HH of less than 5 members will receive 1packeg of bread -1 kg - per day, and each HH of 5 or more will receive 2packeg of bread daily . This activity will continue for 12 months (8 in 2022 and 4 in 2023).- Saed team will implement awareness campaigns about public and personal hygiene and COVID-19 throughout the duration of the project, and the M&E team will also monitor the distribution processes, monitor the food distributed, and ensure the application of humanitarian principles and principles of integrating protection.- Implemented activities and gaps will be shared on a monthly basis by the FSL cluster through 5W reports.
