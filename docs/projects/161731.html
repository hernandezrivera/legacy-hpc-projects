Project Summary


Northeast Nigeria has one of the poorest health indices when compared to other regions especially in the BAY states. Socially, adolescent girls, pregnant and lactating women and children under the age of five years old get a low significant amount of attention in response to humanitarian crises. In times of emergency and such crises, deaths associated with pregnancy, reproductive health, sexual violence, sexual exploitation and malnutrition increase drastically especially amongst females. The timely provision of Reproductive Maternal Newborn Child and adolescent Health and adequate nutrition services can prevent death, disease and disability in the affected population.
This Project's objective is to strengthen and sustain lifesaving health interventions in IDPs camps and host communities and to also facilitate referral services to secondary and Tertiary health institutions. And will target adolescent girls, children under five years old, women of reproductive age, nursing mother and caregivers in IDPs camps and Host communities.
The project is targeting Monguno, Dikwa, and Bama LGAs while the activities include a baseline assessments on the barriers to good nutrition practices and access to reproductive health services, support to trained personnel for provision of outreach and health services for SRH services.
To this end, AHSF intends to train 20 health care workers from some selected facilities in the three LGAs (Nurses and community health extension workers) and 100 community volunteers from communities in which the health facilities are located. The training is aimed at building human capacity of health workers and community volunteers to provide optimum SRH/GBV and nutrition services to residents of these communities.
