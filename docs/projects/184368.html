Project Summary

Somalia’s protracted humanitarian crisis is characterized by ongoing conflicts, climate-related shocks, communicable disease outbreaks and weak social protection mechanisms. The 2022 Humanitarian Needs overview highlights that 7.7Million people in need (5.5M non- internally displaced people, 2.2 Million Internally displaced people, and 0.16M refugees and asylum seekers) an increase from the 5.9 Million recorded in 2021.
The 2022 HNO people in need for the General Protection is 2.7 million, of whom 63 per cent are children, and 49 per cent are women and girls. From January to August of 2021, the Protection Return Monitoring Network (PRMN) reported conflict as the main driver of displacement. Insecurity and conflict contribute to protection challenges such as physical attacks and threat to life, arbitrary arrest and detention, kidnapping, sexual assault, child recruitment, amongst others. This situation is exacerbated by the limited availability of basic services, and support for the victims of abuse.
Oxfam will make direct implementation and at the same time will work local NGO partners in the implementation of the response.
The proposed Protection package aims to increase the resilience, capacities and protective environment of the targeted communities by strengthening community-based protection mechanisms through capacity building, improving access to information and available services, awareness raising, service mapping, and referrals toward mitigating risks (including SGBV), especially for women and girls. Community-based structures, including Community Protection Volunteers, Women’s Forum, Community Committees and duty bearers will play a key role in the project. A protection analysis will be conducted in the target locations to identify existing (strategic and practical) needs and dynamics, vulnerabilities, and threats faced by women, men, boys, and girls.
Though the community based protection approach a community lighting initiatives will be implemented during the action to improve community safety and dignity and reduce protection threats faced by crises affected communities speciﬁcally women and girls in IDPs settlement and vulnerable host communities. Other materials including wheelbarrows will be provided to most vulnerable households to reduce work burden resulted by changing gender roles due to the crises which make the work of women and girls more demanding, e.g fetching water and ﬁrewood collection which in most cases they travel in long distances. In addition this material provision will reduce the frequency of risk exposure and will contribute community cohesion to strengthen community self- protection mechanisms.
Mapping existing services will improve access to information, establishing protection reporting mechanisms, a referral system, and training community volunteers to reduce protection vulnerabilities while increasing the capacities of the target population, who will be able to make informed decisions. Further the action will establish women forums to balance traditional male dominated structures and create safe space for women and girls discuss protection issues. The issues identiﬁed by women groups will then be incorporated systematically into Community Action Plans for further actions and advocacies aiming to strength women’s engagement in community decision making process to address speciﬁc issues affecting them.Oxfam is fundamentally committed to ensuring accountability to affected people. Women, men, girls, and boys within target beneficiaries will be directly involved at all stages of the project cycle in order to encourage community ownership and participation in project activities. Gender protection analysis will be carried out to inform program implementation. Separate consultations will be conducted for different gender groups including persons with disabilities , clan affiliated minorities and other marginalized groups including women and girls.

1. - Projects activities

The proposed protection activities are aimed to address heightened protection risks and contribute to strengthen community self-protection mechanisms. Strengthen protection capacities of existing community structures, supporting community protection committees and Authorities through trainings and material support to enable their protection role at local level. Enable relevant authorities provide protection services to crises affected communities. Community committees, community protection volunteers and authorities will have an increased knowledge on protection concepts risk identiﬁcation and mitigation, service mapping, advocacy and other subjects identiﬁed as relevant during community risk mapping exercises. Capacity building of regional and district authorities such as mayors, regional coordinators, police and other law enforcement agents, to improve knowledge on basic protection concepts and increase the role of authorities to provide better protection services to the crises affected communities of project target areas. Establishment of women forums to balance male dominated structures create safe space for women and girls discuss protection issues and local mechanisms of addressing issues. Women’s Forum will be constituted in all target locations to provide a gender balance to the largely male Community Committees. Women’s Forums will have regular meeting but separate to the largely male-dominated Community Committees, to deﬁne and prioritize issues which affect women speciﬁcally. These issues will then be incorporated systematically into Community Action Plans for further actions and advocacies aiming to strength women’s engagement in community decision making process to address speciﬁc issues affecting them. Carry out protection and gender analysis to identify gendered and protection risks needs and priorities for crises affected communities. Gender and protection analyses will be conducted in the target locations to identify existing needs and dynamics, vulnerabilities and threats faced by women, men, boys and girls. The analyses will inform activities by all sectors in the project and form the basis for deﬁnition of protection messaging. The protection and gender analyses key findings will be disseminated through out the protection cluster to communicate key findings with other member organizations for advocacy purpose. Provide community solar lighting initiatives in hotspot areas to reduce protection threats and increase community safety. I DPs settlements that experience heightened protection risks due to lack of lighting will be targeted. Multifunctional Portable household solar lights will be distributed in target vulnerable IDPs and host communities to contribute to safety and security of the vulnerable populations in the target locations. The household solar lighting is speciﬁcally aimed to reduce possible threats affecting women and girls in their shelter. The solar lamps are multi-functional, with removable ﬂashlight and phone charger to provide women with access to phone charging or even an livelihoods opportunity of earning small income from charging other people’s phones, further reducing their vulnerability to risk exposure. Communal solar lighting will be proved in public spaces identiﬁed as hot spot areas with the facilitations of community protection volunteers, women forums and other community committees. The communal solar lighting initiative will contribute to reduce protection risks increase safety and mobility of crises affected communities. protection awareness raising sessions, develop service maps IEC materials for protection information dissemination and advocacy. Provision of protection material support to the most vulnerable, to reduce speciﬁc protection risks through community protection committees. Oxfam will conduct protection monitoring in 5 districts, community based MHPSS,CFM

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

All Oxfam humanitarian responses will include proactive measures to ensure we do not inadvertently cause harm to people. In practice thus means Safe programming is used as an approach throughout different programs. Safe programming risk analysis is carried out to identify potential risks and updated regular to monitor evolving risks and put in place practical mitigation measures. Safe programming trainings to enable program teams including partners have knowledge and apply across program cycle. Mitigation measures :- The program will ensure that all parties of women men boys and girls have access to participation process more speciﬁcally the program will create mechanism for women and girls to participate consultation and decision making process by establishing and capacitating women’s forums in each project sites, the women’s forums will be trained on identiﬁcation of barriers of accessing consultation and decision making process while they will conduct regular meetings to discuss issues speciﬁcally affecting women and girls and present identiﬁed issue to village committee meeting and will be incorporated committee plans to address those issues. The consultation of women and girls will be the ﬁrst priority of the program a dedicated technical team will support programs in mainstreaming protection and gender further all Oxfam and partner teams are trained on gender mainstreaming and safe programming. During community engagement process maximum diversity of different parities of the community including disabilities will be ensured. Mitigation measures : Oxfam will have accountability and feedback mechanism in place and carries out continuous induction and sensitizing the community raising feedback is rightfully theirs. Regular promotion of the system through behavioral change communications mainly posters (demonstrated in local language) & organization of accountability sessions at village level to build conﬁdence of the community on the use of the system. Ensure all program teams promote accountability feedback mechanism at all levels and program staff will be trained on accountability feedback mechanisms. ensure a committed staff to be engaged in managing the system including logging in complaints, following up, and closing the feedback loop with the support of the programme/project teams. Dedicated personnel is place to ensure the system is place and functional. Further the centrality of protection strategy will also be used as guidance document to ensure key priority protection risks are factored in.
