Project Summary

The project target the most vulnerable IDP and Returnee in Host communities of Northeast Nigeria affected by insurgency to support their HH with soft business skill training, business start-up grant, good agricultural practice training and farming input to improve their livelihood and HH food security.

2 - Activities

Due to insurgency that ravage the communities of Northeast Nigeria, this left thousands of HH with minimal inadequate food for consumption score and they're unable to afford some essential non-food expenditure. The project target to reach 1380 individuals in communities of Borno and Adamawa State. 70% women and 30% men will be reached. The Cash transfer Modality will be through E-voucher while the technology that will be employ will be Red-Rose platform.
