Project Summary

Climatic shocks coupled with conflict, widespread poverty, and vulnerability are among the key drivers that have trapped millions of Somalis in severe hunger (IPC Phase 3, "Crisis" and IPC Phase 4, "emergency") and malnutrition.
According to the latest UNICEF report (September 2019) issued by the United Nations Inter-Agency Group for Child Mortality Estimation (UN IGME), Somalia still remains one of the countries with the highest child mortality caused mostly by preventable causes.
The vast majority of these deaths (85 percent) occur in the first 5 years of life, of which almost half (47 percent​) happen during the first month, largely due to treatable causes such as infectious diseases. These deaths, particularly in Somalia, show the effect of security, conflict, climatic shocks and lack of basic services have on children’s health. Basic health services like vaccination, skilled birth delivery, medical treatment, adequate nutrition, and clean water and sanitation become matters of life and death when children and young adolescents do not have access to them.
Provision of emergency lifesaving and life-sustaining services is essential for ending this preventable child and maternal deaths and for ensuring that no child is left behind.
Qatar Red Crescent Society will improve the access and utilization of Emergency PHC services for women, men, boys and girls living in the most crisis-affected populations in IDP settlements and the vulnerable urban poor in Baidoa, Gardo, Afgoi, and Bal'ad. Static facilities and mobile outreach teams will be set up and strengthened through the provision of medical supplies and the improvement of staff capacity. Remote villages will be reached through mobile clinics; community mobilizers and Community Health Workers (CHWs) will be recruited and trained. Among the activities that QATAR RED CRESCENT SOCIETY will provide include routine and campaign immunization to under 5 children (boys and girls) and Women of childbearing age (WCBA); Provision of antenatal and post-natal care to pregnant and lactating mothers. Treatment and control of communicable diseases targeting which boys, girls, PLW, WCBA and men; Strengthening of referral system between Primary Healthcare facilities, the community (remote villages) and the regional hospitals; Establishment of mobile clinics; Creating Disease Surveillance and Response plan; Training of health workers on clinical management of childhood illness, Basic Emergency Obstetric Care and disease surveillance and response. Training will be conducted for community health workers in​ integrated community case management on communicable diseases​ such as pneumonia, diarrhea, and malaria.

Mitigation Measures

1. Ensure the location and access routes to health facilities and services are safe. QRCS will Identify central locations and bring services closer to vulnerable populations in IDPs and host communities. For example, QRCS will use mobile outreach teams to provide services to populations living further away from static facilities to reduce their risk of exposure to protection issues.2. Consult women, men, boys, girls, persons with disabilities, chronically ill, older persons, pregnant and lactating women, and marginalized persons to collect accurate information about their specific needs and preferences for health center location, design, and services.3. Ensure all health staff, implementing partners and volunteers working with affected populations understand, sign and adhere to a Code of Conduct stating their commitment to respect and foster humanitarian standards and the rights of beneficiaries, including the confidentiality and privacy of patients.4. Ensure all staff, implementing partners and volunteers in direct contact with patients, health staff and beneficiaries have received training in the prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse (PSEA).5. Engage the community and committee representative to play an active role in identifying solutions and in the decision-making processes that affect them, so as to promote a sense of ownership, build their self-esteem and improve the relevance and sustainability of the response.6. QRCS will ensure that the ​mobile teams are set up in a safe space and location with adequate security that does not compromise the safety of beneficiaries as much as possible.On Disability inclusion: -QRCS will ensure people with disabilities are actively sought, mobilized, and engaged. Especially, where available, disability advocacy groups, organizations, and networks owned, led, and run by people with disabilities will be engaged to recommend qualified candidates for suitable staff positions. They will also be engaged in the registration of people with disabilities so that the exercise can be done in a dignified manner and without exposing them to risk.
