Project Summary

A war between the Sudanese Armed Forces (SAF) under and the paramilitary Rapid Support Forces (RSF) , rival factions of the military government of Sudan, began on 15 April 2023 . Fighting has been concentrated around the capital city of Khartoum ,Kurdufan and the Darfur region.
Despite the tremendous efforts to provide humanitarian services, the health and livelihood situation is developing and getting worse due to the expansion of the war zone and the deterioration of the state’s infrastructure in all areas of health, nutrition, and environmental sanitation. as people are displaced to urban cities, which led to the accumulation of garbage, and therefore caused the spread of a number of diseases and has increased. The situation has worsened due to a lack of shelter and the spread of cholera and dengue fever in a number of Sudanese states. The recent conflict that extended to Gezira State and parts of Sennar State led to a new displacement of thousands of citizens and a change in the plan for providing humanitarian aid.
This intervention aims to address the needs of returnees, internally displaced people (IDPs), and their host communities impacted by the violence and instability in WK-SK-NK-GZ-Sinnar-ED-SD-CD-BN-RN-WN-GDRF-and Abyei Rejon using an integrated, multi-sector strategy.
This project aims to enhance the life-threatening conditions of the internally displaced population by providing high-quality life-saving humanitarian services to the internally targeted population in therein high-risk states with fifty-four locations through a comprehensive strategy that integrates the sectors of health, nutrition, WASH and that guarantee a decent life for the target groups.
Health interventions include providing a package of PHC- SHC, (RH) and maternal and child health by supporting health facilities in the targeted states with the necessary and emergency medical supplies and equipment. Interventions will also focus on provide the trauma package in increasing health capacity, health referral support and community health support through the mobile clinic and provide the necessary kidney solution and other necessary medicine. Nutritional interventions include the provision of preventive package and CMAM services to all targeted population groups focusing on sensitive groups (pregnant and lactating women and children) and treatment of severe acute malnutrition, with or without complications for children under five years of age, moderate acute malnutrition of newborns5 and pregnant and lactating women. Poor water quality and lack of access to improved sanitation continue to pose a significant threat to human health, Safe drinking water and improved sanitation of surrounding areas is primary need of people for the healthy life. lack of access to safe water supply, sanitation and hygiene is the third most significant risk factor for poor health in developing countries with high mortality rates. AWD is the leading disease associated with unsafe water supply, sanitation and hygiene and is responsible for the deaths. The health consequences for those living without using toilets are severe. GAH will promoting the awareness program through different health institutions in addition to provide and installation of the safe water source and cholerization the access water in addition to enhance the sanitation behaver and linked it with other health, nutrition and other behaver.
Non-Food Items (NFIs) are essential for addressing immediate household needs and maintaining the well-being of displaced populations. These items contribute significantly to health, dignity, and overall quality of life. GHA will provide integrated WASH and FNI project to provided essential lifesaving intervention.

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

GAH is a protection mandated organization, thus protection mainstreaming is always paid extra attention during project design, and also following the “Do No Harm” principle and maintaining the dignity of all targeted beneficiaries. For mainstreaming protection in this project, GAH will ensure that during the construction/rehabilitation of health facilities and nutrition facilities, that gender segregated and persons with special needs (PSN) friendly WASH facilities are constructed, GAH will also make sure that the design of the latrines includes locks for additional safety measures. The rehabilitation of all health and nutrition facilities will also take into account the ease of access to PSN such as construction of wheelchair ramps. GAH will also incorporate topics of child safeguarding and PSEA in the trainings of community health promoters CHPs, community nutrition volunteers CNVs and community health volunteers CHVs, and raise awareness of school children on sexual exploitation and abuse, and how/to whom can these violations be reported. Incorporation and linkage of the target facilities ( HFs and nutrition centers) and/or formulated groups and structures with protection services providers will be prioritized in this project, to enable survivors of violence and vulnerable age groups to receive the needed legal aid, psychosocial and/or medical care they need.

4.1.1 - How will the project use community feedback to improve and adapt activities? (open-ended/free text, required)4

A feedback mechanism [email-box FGD] will be established by the project to enable community members to express their thoughts, recommendations, and concerns about the project's operations. then the project team will review the input from the community and The comments will be considered by the team when deciding on project activities, timeframes, and resource allocation. - The project team will communicate any modifications made to project activities in response to community feedback.The team will discuss why the modifications were implemented and how they will benefit the community. The project team will assess the effect of improvements depending on community comments. The team will utilize this information to improve project operations and guarantee that they satisfy the community's requirements.

4.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will support Inter-Agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including Sexual exploitation and abuse complaints?

GAH will ensure accountability to affected population (AAP) is fully in place from the inception phase of project by conducting focal group discussions with members and leaders in the target communities, to address their major highlighted needs in a context-suitable approach while ensuring equitable representation of women and girls in the assessment, design and monitoring of all project activities. Prior to implementation, GAH will address the target communities and explain the nature and expected results of the interventions, by different means such as meetings with community leaders, active community members and/or advertisement of the interventions through various methods (campaigns, radio broadcasting). GAH will clearly convey the message of what is to be expected from the interventions and the staff of GAH, and ensure that the target communities have a safe channel to express any violations (sexual exploitation and abuse) without any fear of repercussions. GAH will achieve that by collecting feedback through face to face meetings with individuals, group consultations and community leaders through aid workers. In addition to providing complaints and feedback boxes in the target institutions and explaining to the target communities importance of providing their feedback on quality of provided services and/or any abuse complaints, and how to use the boxes. GAH will regularly check the boxes to ensure the safe running of the project and interventions. GAH will conduct trainings on PSEA and child safeguarding to contracted vendors and all appointed staff in the target locations, and ensure their abiding to the code of conduct. If any complaints of SEA and abuse are reported through the various aforementioned methods, GAH will immediately take appropriate measures to hold the person reported accountable, and ensure that the act never to be repeated. GAH will provide a full report to the interagency on any violation cases, should they occur and the mitigating steps undertaken, in addition to AAP measures.
