Project Summary

Populations exposed to crisis face increased protection concerns, especially in relation to housing, land and property rights. Gender Based Violence and family separation compounded by the challenges in accessing populations hard to reach areas. To response the above situation, WARDI will scale up GBV prevention & response through three interrelated sets of activities: prevention, response (survivor assistance) and coordination among partners.
1) The prevention activities include; training Community Safety & Awareness Mobilizers in approaches and methods to proper survivor centered care, raising awareness in communities about available confidential services, referral networks and dignity kit distributions.
2) The response activities of the project include; provision of medical services quality to women and girls who are survivors of GBV through three GBV one stop centers in (Belet Weyn Hospital, Wanla Weyn and Mogadishu). WARDI will also apply the following principles "privacy, confidentiality, safety of survivors and family” while providing medical assistance to GBV survivors.
3) Since WARDI is focal point of Hiran GBV sub cluster, the organization will strengthen GBV coordination at national and Hiran sub cluster WG to improve coordination among partners, reporting and ensure that mainstreaming of protection across cluster and partners in Hiran region.
Furthermore, the project will also improve the capacity of national staff (8 females, 7 male) on CMR as well improve the capacity of Internal displaced people (IDP) and poor host community on GBV prevention methods.
The project will target 30,000 crisis affected men, women, girls, minorities and people with disabilities with lifesaving GBV services including medical support, psychosocial support, awareness on GBV prevention and capacity building for case workers and community members on GBV prevention and response.
The project is complementary with WASH, health and Nutrition interventions that WARDI is implementing in the targeted locations. The target will include; 21,000 IDPs, 9,000 Host community in Hiran, Lower Shabelle and Banadir regions.
WARDI will consider special arrangements for individuals who cannot physically access the services - mobile counselling services; home visits, establish systems for transportation; distance counselling through mobile technology.

Project Activities

Activity 1: Strengthen three GBV on stop centers within the existing health facilities in Hiran, Banadir and Lower Shabelle (the facilities will be staffed with nurses, midwives).Activity 2: Provide counseling, medical assistance, psychosocial and legal support to 400 GBV survivors in Hiran, Lower Shabelle and Banadir regions of Somalia including IDP girls and women, minorities and people with disability. Activity 3: Strengthen community engagement by organizing 12/month community awareness on GBV prevention in Hiran, Lower Shabelle and Banadir regions of Somalia. The target participants will include religious groups, community leaders, youth groups, women groups and local authority to break barriers and norms that are silent and support GBV. GBV information material developed with the community participation. The community training will lead to make easy the mobilization of the community. Each training will target 50 participants (total participants are 500). Activity 4: Carry out comprehensive GBV awareness campaigns reach 30,000 crisis affected men, women, girls, minorities and people with disabilities with lifesaving GBV services including medical support, psychosocial support, awareness on GBV prevention and capacity building for case workers and community members on GBV prevention and response. Activity 5: In collaboration with GBV Sub Cluster, Ministry of Health (MOH) and Clinical Management of Rape (CMR) task force, organize CMR training for 15 nurse/midwives in Hiran, Lower Shabelle and Banadir regions. Activity 6: Procure and distribute 700 dignity kits and solar to 700 female headed household representing 4,200 women, children, people with disabilities and minorities, who are at risk of GBV or are GBV victims. One dignity kit composes: 3 pieces of shawls, 3 pieces of dress, 3 pieces of petticoat, 3 pieces of head scarf, 3 pieces sanitary towels, 3 pieces of underwear, 3 pieces of soaps, 3 packets of powder soap. The distribution of dignity kits to the women/girl who are at risk of GBV and GBV survivors will contribute to psychosocial and emotional support to GVB survivors and gives heals and wellbeing of survivors.Activity 7: Provide skills development (beauty salon) for 100 female headed households in Banadir region to boost economically the GBV risk/GBV survival women and adolescent girls. Activity 8. Organize community awareness session on FGM practices targeting 200 elders, religious groups and TBA.

Mitigation Measures

2b. Mitigation:The overall goal of the proposed project is to promote protection concerns of women, men, girls and boys including reducing GBV against women and girls. Safety and security measures will be taken into account by close consultation with women and people with disabilities during the project design like project services to be close to their settlements and distribution of dignity kits inside their settlements. In this project women, men, girls and children will give equal opportunity to take part the project design before its implementation and also to be equally heard their voices. Women, men and marginalized communities will equally share the members of the Project committees and also the project staff. All men, women, boys, girls and disables will equal have access to services offered in this project.3c. Mitigation:Insecurity and conflict due to insurgency and clan conflict is now one of the main causes for displacement. Drought, floods and cyclone are also the root cause of the displacement in Somalia. Somalia faces a dire humanitarian situation, having endured drought and famine as well as continued fighting and heavy rains, all of which have aggravated the conditions of IDPs. People denied the rights of the IDPs as human being and they are facing evictions, rape and looting due to the fragility of the central government who was under their responsibility to protect them. Most of the IDPs left behind their livelihood assets and displaced to the big towns with empty hands. Thus, the IDPs have the right to receive necessary service, such as protection. Unfortunately, conflict sometimes is preventing humanitarian agencies from delivering assistance to the IDPs. In this project, WARDI will try to reduce the burden of IDPs (women and children suffer most) and bring the services their WASH requirement to their settlements, in order to reduce the risk of GBV against women/girls for searching needed services. WARDI will also include the IDPs (giving women IDPs their share) to the established committees for the project implementation to get their views and to be heard their complains if arise. The IDP rights will be availed in the course of the project implementation.4d. Mitigation:WARDI will hold at the inception of the project, community mobilization, and sensitization meetings with all stakeholders to officially launch the project. During the sensitization meetings, the project objectives, implementation strategies, scope, beneficiary selection criteria, beneficiary entitlement and roles and duties of each stakeholder including Monitoring & Evaluation roles will be discussed and agreed. WARDI will develop elaborate beneficiary complain and feedback tool, project beneficiaries and stakeholders will be given one day workshop on how to use the complaint/feedback system to enhance transparency and accountability. Hot-line telephone will be established and the community will make aware of the hotline telephone number; the aim of the hotline is: to report the beneficiaries their claims, if they are not satisfied the services that the center offered to them, the hotline care will respect the confidentiality of the beneficiaries' complain. Community ownership of the project will be done through formation of structures such as Beneficiary Representatives, and Camp Committees (consisting of women and men), in order to meet the protection needs of the affected community, WARDI will involve the community through its leaders and engendered GBV community committees, selected members from the beneficiaries and community leaders in the first stage of planning of the project to ensure community participation.
