Project Summary

Needs: The intersectoral severity of needs according to the HNO 2024 is extreme (level 4) for almost all districts in NWS. The top five priority needs reported by households were food, winterization support, household NFIs, access to health care, and hygiene items, linked to sharp price increases for food, rent, and water. Activities: WHH and its partners propose a 12-month intervention in NW Syria to address the ongoing effects of the earthquake and conflict. In Syria, the focus is on vulnerable groups such as IDPs, households living under the poverty line, and PWD. The activities include food support (food vouchers*8 rounds*9,514 HH), cash for food (9,514 HHs*2 rounds*100 USD) and bread in-kind (15,681 HH, 4 rounds, 310 gr/pers/day), setting up food processing workshop (40 women) and NFI (pajamas production, 50 ben), running two social cohesion initiatives (rehab of infrastructure; 500 HH receiving CFW) and one green initiative (planting trees), nutrition services (screening, referrals, vouchers for fresh food; 34,709 ben), support for livestock (1,150 HHs, one time, vouchers), agriculture vouchers (1,045 HHs, 2 rounds-winter and summer), NFI distributions (5227 inv), dignified shelter set up (210 units-1,050 individuals), WASh services (water trucking 35 L/C/D, waste management, wastewater desludging, latrines cleaning, hygiene campaigns, rehab and extension of sewage system to the dignified shelter units) and community based protection (community committees, training, case management, PSS, special needs fund, local initiatives). Approximately 300,000 individuals will receive assistance. The project is multisectoral and addresses comprehensively multiple needs based on an area-based approach. Special attention will be given to women who will make up the main percentage of participants in the income generation activities. Special criteria for targeting will be employed based on the services provided. The most vulnerable HHs will be supported: female, child and elderly headed HHs, families with high number of children under 5, PLW, PWD, destroyed assets, high dependency ratio, etc.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

WHH proposes three main outputs and several activities under each output. The first output will focus on ensuring access to quality food and improving nutritional behaviours. The second output is specifically dedicated to emergencies and response to disasters by providing different types of support based on the needs emerging from the crisis. Shelter rehabilitation and WASH support are included under this component. The third output blends specialized and community-based protection services and community interventions to increase social cohesion. An estimated number of 300,000 individuals will receive a form of assistance under this project, with the potential of overlapping since multi-sectoral and integrated responses will be sought as an inclusive approach.Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating R1)Civil unrest: M1) 1.
