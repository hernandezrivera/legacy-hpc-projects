Project Summary

The project is an Emergency Food security support intervention geared to support mainly vulnerable households in in Gogrial West, Tonj North , Tonj East and Twic Counties in Warrap State that are affected mainly intensive localized fighting and highly affected by flooding in 2020 cropping season. The project will be implemented from February, 2021 to December, 2021. The key objective is to increase people’s access to food and availability of food through support to food production. The project will target 120,000 families ( 20,000 Households in these four most highly food insecure communities . This target population are currently in Phase four(4) of the Emergency Food security phase classification and are likely to phase into Catastrophic Food insecurity Phase Five if urgent action is not taken. The project is line with the current HRP strategic objective 2 ( SO2) and is in support of FSL cluster objective 2 and FSL cluster objective 4.
The main programmatic approach is through direct distribution of inputs, provision of technical agronomic messages, working with accountability to affected committees (AAP) and providing key messaging on how to prevent the spread of COVID-19 to the affected population. The project activities falls within the seasonal calendar and within the livelihood zones of warrap state; hence it is a viable and very much contextualized.
Centre to the project implementation is the consideration given to address protection concern and the involve of the accountability to the affected community. The needs of Women, boys and children are focused and will be robustly addressed in this project. The proposed project will have strong synergy and it will integrate with the nutrition cluster wherein the caretaker or mothers of malnourished children will be trained and supported with high nutritious vegetable seeds for production. The project cash value is 300, 000 USD and the project is expected to get in-kind contribution through FAO pipeline for the core inputs.

1 - Needs

In 2021, South Sudan is expected to experience unprecedented high Food insecurity due to a combination of numerous factors that are related to localized fighting, flooding, hyperinflation, and the current impact of the COVID-19 pandemic. According to the consolidated findings and projection from the integrated Food Security Classification Phase Technical working group ( October 2020- November 2020 indicates that between October 2020 to November 2020 6.3 million people which represents 53% of the people of South Sudan faced a high level of food insecurity. By April to July 2021, 7.2 Million 60% of the people of South Sudan will face a high level of acute food insecurity. This situation and the expected increase of the high food insecure population is worrisome and needs an urgent action which could otherwise lead to catastrophic Food insecure situations and subsequently death. According to UNOCHA, People’s physical and mental wellbeing, living standards, and coping mechanisms are expected to further deteriorate in 2021. Some 8.3 million people in South Sudan are estimated to be in need of humanitarian assistance in 2021Warrap state specifically Gogrial , Twic, Tonj North, Tonj East in 2020 faced two major disasters- localized conflict that has caused huge displacement and flooding. According to the IPC Technical working group report 2021, indicates 2727,000 of the entire population which represents 71% of the total population in the State population are expected to be in Phase three and Phase four. This percentage phase of the State in this high alert Food insecurity phase is likely to move into the Castratrophic Food insecurity situation if urgent humanitarian action is not provided. This is the major problem that HELPO is purposing to address. The project, therefore, aims to Enhance and sustain emergency food production through the complementary vegetable and crop livelihood inputs, fishing, and livestock support in a way that prevents the spread & transmission of COVID 19. This will prevent the expected catastrophic food situation in the State between April and June 2021. HELPO proposes to support 120,000 people ( 20,000 Households).

2 - Project activities

The main project activities: support in the establishment of Accountability to affected Population committee, distribution of main agricultural seeds, tools and fishing; distribution of lean season dry season vegetable seeds, tools, fishing kits Distribution of rapid response kits, provision of technical messages in production of crops, training of farmers in FAW surveillance and detection, vaccination, treatment of livestock, provision of key messages that will help reduce the impact of COVID-19.

3 - Project indicators

The key indicators to be measured include:• Number of HHs (female, child & male-headed) receiving agricultural inputs/ vegetable & fishing kits; • Quantity & variety of seeds distributed; • Quantity & variety of crops produced (outcome); • Percentage Women receiving livelihood kits (crop/ veg/ fish); • Number of SAD returnees receiving livelihood support. • Number of animals vaccinated & treated. The success of the project will be measured through impact assessment studies . The key contributing data to this measurement will be data or reports generated from regular field visits in monitoring the progress of the intervention, beneficiary list, field visit monitoring data, Quarterly and final reports, and Success stories.
