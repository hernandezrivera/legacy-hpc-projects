Project Summary

The project will disseminate information on exclusive and complementary breastfeeding to pregnant women, matrons, traditional birth attendants and carers of children under 5 years old with the aim of improving the nutritional status of the children referred to above. It will also bring health services closer to the population, especially for early childhood development.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project aims to improve the nutritional status of children under 5 years of age, through exclusive breastfeeding and complementary feeding, by means of themes that deal with these subjects. To do so, specially covered methodologies will be used (Emoccoes demostrcionais) in order to allow the caregivers of the target group to understand the subjects in the best way. Importantly, in the nutrition groups that will be established, the participants, despite being involved in the discussions, will also be challenged to prepare recipes that they will learn in order to observe whether or not they really understand the subjects, as well as being a strategy to encourage the participants to consume nutritious foods.With the project we intend to directly benefit about 6000 children from 0-6 months old and 10 000 children from 6 to 59 months old.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The project will cover the geographical areas belonging to Ancuabe districts, specifically the administrative posts of Ancuabe Sede and Metoro respectively and Chiure district in the administrative posts of Chiure sede and Katapua and will have as some activities described below:1. training of PBAs on exclusive breastfeeding and complementary feeding packages ;2. 2. Conducting cooking demonstrations related to complementary feeding;3. Reproduction of IEC material4. Community training on exclusive breastfeeding and complementary feeding (training will be done in nutrition groups and health centres)5. Conducting baseline and end-line of the project;6. Conduct debriefing meetings with the SDSMAS of the districts involved and other relevant actors in the communities7. Encourage the practice of nutrition-sensitive agriculture; 8. 1. 8. Support the SDSMAS in the distribution of supplements.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

As with PSEA, we also have a protection strategy that carefully monitors any suspicion of abuse or harassment at different levels as well as the reporting and protection of whistleblowers. However, all staff will be trained on these matters and the reporting mechanisms will be shared, in order to guarantee the protection of those involved.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The programs will be developed for the most part in the community. Local families will be trained to have a healthy diet taking into account the local diet.For this reason, local activists will be hired and the matrons will work.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Based on the baseline that we will do, as well as investigative work with local state agencies we think it is possible to reach people with some disability of different age groups and they will be treated as a priority in all our intervention.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Bearing in mind that any human intervention in nature has its impact, whether significant or not. In this sense, it is important to state that in this project all harmful aspects to the environment will be taken care of, prioritizing principles of prevention, precaution, conservation, among other aspects that do not endanger the environment, i.e., that protects the environment.
