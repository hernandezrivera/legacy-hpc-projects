Project Summary

Human Appeal (HA) ’s project aims to provide comprehensive humanitarian assistance to 18,484 internally displaced persons (IDPs), equivalent to 3,511 families, across both Idleb and Aleppo governorates. The project focuses on strengthening social cohesion, resilience, and solutions between IDPs and host communities through tailored life skills training, livelihood interventions, and targeted support. Additionally, the project includes care and maintenance activities, as well as site improvements for infrastructure and camp facilities.
HA CCCM (Camp Coordination and Camp Management) interventions align closely with sector objectives. Specifically:
- Social Cohesion and Resilience: Human Appeal aims to enhance social cohesion and resilience by providing life skills training and livelihood support to IDPs and host communities.
- Infrastructure Improvements: The project includes care and maintenance activities for camp facilities, ensuring safe and functional living conditions.
- Urgent Needs: Focusing on 9 IDPs Camps / locations within Maaret Tamsrin, Azaz/Suran sub-districts in Idlib and Aleppo governorates (CCCM IDPs sites file attached), the project serves 18,484 beneficiaries, including 1,391 persons with disabilities (PwDs).
- Key project activities include registration, vulnerability mapping, gender-balanced camp governance, routine site renovations, and fire mitigation measures. Additionally, community participation will be enhanced through capacity-building trainings for staff and committees, fostering accountability mechanisms for the benefit of IDPs.
- Community Participation and Committees: The project aims to enhance community participation and establish committees. These committees will play a crucial role in decision-making, coordination, and ensuring community engagement.
- Capacity Strengthening: Human Appeal will strengthen the capacity of staff and committees by providing relevant trainings. This will empower them to effectively manage camp activities and engage with IDPs.
- Accountability Mechanisms: The project establishes mechanisms for coordinated multisectoral service provision and community engagement. These mechanisms will enhance transparency, accountability, and responsiveness.
- Household and Communal Coping Mechanisms: The project focuses on strengthening coping mechanisms within households and communities. Tailored livelihood activities, including vocational trainings and in-kind grants, will contribute to sustainable solutions.
- Spontaneous Exit Strategies: Human Appeal recognizes the importance of exit strategies. By developing tailored livelihood opportunities, the project aims to facilitate IDPs’ transition out of camps.
- Mainstreaming Protection Principles: Throughout the project cycle, protection principles (including “Do no harm”) will be mainstreamed. Different needs of various groups (men, women, young children, adolescents, young adults, middle-aged adults, and older adults) will be considered.
Together, these efforts contribute to a holistic approach that prioritizes the well-being, dignity, and resilience of IDPs in Northwest Syria.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Human Appeal aims to support 18,484 internally displaced persons (IDPs) through a comprehensive Camp Coordination and Camp Management (CCCM) response in Northwest Syria (NWS). The project aligns with CCCM cluster priorities by providing lifesaving services, enhancing safety provisions, and strengthening infrastructure in IDP camps. The key project activities include: - Lifesaving Assistance: Streamlined humanitarian aid will be provided to affected IDPs, addressing their immediate needs.- Multi-Sectoral Support: The project will offer assistance across various sectors to enhance the well-being of IDPs.- Infrastructure Maintenance: Regular upkeep of camp infrastructure will ensure safe and functional facilities.- Accountability Mechanisms: CCCM mechanisms will be established to enhance accountability to IDPs.- Community Engagement: Community committees, including women’s participation, will be formed.- Capacity Building: Training for camp staff and committees will strengthen their capabilities.- Fire Mitigation and Response: Measures will be taken to prevent and respond to fires.- Mobile CCCM Teams: An expanded mobile approach will facilitate effective coordination. Throughout these activities, Human Appeal will consider the diverse needs of IDPs, including men, women, children, the elderly, and persons with disabilities. Close coordination with the CCCM Cluster and other relevant sectors will ensure seamless information exchange and effective project implementation.
