Project Summary

Generally, the major aim of the Shelter/NFI/CCCM Cluster is to improve living conditions of displaced persons living in camps, camp-like setting and host communities.
Emergency Shelter and Non-Food Item (NFI) assistance, directly to beneficiaries Internally displaced persons in MMC, Jere and Nganzai LGA of Borno state.
Activities will improvement and repairs of Shelters, construction and repairs of type shelters, Emergency shelter kits, distribution of NFI kits and prepositioning of emergency shelter and NFI Kits.
The urgent need for this project is as a result the on going camp closure in Borno state capital, an increasing no of IDPs from the state capital Maiduguri and Jere back to other LGAs. The destruction of shelter by wind-storm, fire outbreaks out breaks and wire and tire of the shelter, shelter is great. A huge gap exist in Shelter and NFIs in areas that are hard to reach eg Nganzai which is with out Shelter intervention for sometime and a large number of the shelters are lost to fire out breaks.

1 - Needs

The State Government of Borno has embark on the closure camps, formal camps across the state capital Maiduguri. Evidence shows that camps are over crowded and shelters are over stretched. The unavoidable fire outbreaks and floods (which are manageable) in some locations affected both shelters and NFI kits as well. In addition, PPGW team will conducted on-the-spot assessment and identify need for camps, the demand for shelters and NFIs for new arrivals, returnees, flood and fire outbreaks victims is TAG urgent. IDPs, Returnees, and Host communities without shelter solutions will be receiving appropriate assistance and trainings to enhance resilience toward durable shelter solutions. IDPs at risk of exposure to harmful elements and those projected coming from inaccessible areas and in need of shelter solutions will be assisted/where necessary advocate for shelter and NFI support. Hundreds of thousands of IDPs have lost their livelihoods and access to basic Services, an increased influx from to rural community due to the camp closure in some locations, humanitarian agencies made efforts to rescue people from appalling humanitarians’ conditions however, due to limited resources and immense needs across the country; it is far from reaching milestone of improving lives of all affected people. Amongst other basic human needs, one of the most immediate needs remains getting access to NFI and shelters for vulnerable people in particular children, lactating and pregnant women, women headed household, newly displaced people, elderly and people with special vulnerabilities. Needs-based assessment will continuously be conducted in coordination with other partners to understanding the gaps and social issues affecting vulnerable communities and triangulate into programmes addressing these needs. Communities are in desperate need of NFI and shelter materials as a step of support improving their lives and build resilience. In order to have effective and efficient response to the needs, PPGW will prioritized to reach many people that are mostly in need of NFI and shelter. PPGW will ensure close coordination with partners throughout its interventions. PPGW activities in target locations will include needs assessments, IES distribution, training, awareness raising on fire prevention, Flood ,gender and environment protection, and NFIs distribution, mobilization of resources, sensitization and coordination of responding to the immediate needs of the people.
