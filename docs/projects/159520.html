Project Summary

A set of measures to support maternity and childhood for the most vulnerable households living in the zone of military conflict, by issuing pharmacy vouchers for purchase baby food and medicines for families with children under 1 year and additional vulnerabilities (disability, chronic diseases, households destruction, large families, single parent, families deprived of livelihood, IDPs / returnees) living in the 20-kilometer zone from the demarcation line in the NGCA.

1 - Needs

After 5 years of armed conflict in the Donbas, OSCE continues to record daily violations of the ceasefire regime, including the use of heavy weapons. In addition to this, the economic blockade of the region remains, as a result, the life and health of citizens living in the conflict zone are under constant threat, damage to housing and critical infrastructure is regularly recorded. The above conditions create not only a direct threat to life and health of the population, but also additional expenses related to the repair and restoration of households, that radically reduces the standard of living of the population, provoking additional morbidity and super-mortality. This makes the region the most depopularized territory on the planet at the moment: annual population reduction reaches 30 thousand people. Against this background, young parents, especially from vulnerable families, find themselves in a very problematic situation. 87% of respondents from among parents with children under 5 years living in NGCA note that they have only a partial opportunity to purchase medical goods, 8% are completely unable to purchase them themselves. 23% of families do not have enough money for basic needs, including food and medicine, 21% can meet their needs for food and medicine, but can no longer buy clothes, 48% do not have the ability to make savings. 9% of respondents are forced to sell their previously accumulated property, 7% - to reduce basic health care costs, 3% are forced to leave their families in search of sources of income. Thus, even outside the target group of the project, among all young parents (about 8 thousand children are born annually in the NGCA of Donetsk region, more than 80% of them live permanently with their parents in the 0-20km zone of NGCA), including those who do not have additional vulnerabilities, except the place of residence, the economic situation of 44% can be described as critical, and 92% - as economically vulnerable. 22% of households do not have a job as a source of income for any of the family members, 29% of respondents have unstable sources of income and are forced to work part-time, constantly looking for a normal job. The organization regularly receives requests to assist the purchasing of medicines, hygiene products and baby food from these categories of the population. Number of this request calculates several thousands that we can not satisfy. Based on the results of the appeals, the approximate needs of the most vulnerable categories of the population in the NGCA of Donetsk region were formed. Targeted issuance of long-term pharmacy cash vouchers (up to $ 300) for the purchase of baby food, hygiene and medicines (by prescription) for vulnerable families with children under 1 year old living in a 20-kilometer zone from the demarcation line is able to satisfy this requests. Involvement of medical specialists at the stage of generating an electronic voucher (special prescription) in the already developed program for this purpose will encourage young parents to interact with pediatricians and will improve the quality of prevention and medical control in the region. The voucher program for NGCA has been implemented since 2016, more than 4,000 people have been provided with medicines. The voucher program provides a rapid response to the requests arising from unexpected incidents, enables beneficiaries to decide on their own which medicines and food they paramountly receive. Also it will reduce the transaction costs associated with logistics and will make humanitarian assistance more targeted and individualize. Besides, allows to reach additional social benefits, such as increasing the chances of successful prevention and timely medical support of children due to encouraging parents to come and consult with doctors.

2 - Activities & outcomes

The proposed project includes the following activities: Activity 1 1) Selection and coordination of settlements of the project. Locations within the 20 km NGCA zone will be selected. At the same time, the help is narrowly targeted, individualized, due to the voucher system distributed through pediatricians;2) Formation, coordination (with the donor and medical institutions) and verification of lists of categories of beneficiaries;3) Create two categories of vouchers: A (equivalent to $ 100) and B (equivalent to $ 300) for severe diagnoses.4) Competitive open selection of the appropriate pharmacy network capable of implementing the voucher component in the designated area;5) Adaptation of the electronic system of registration of individual vouchers (prescriptions), their implementation in the selected network and medical institutions (with the provision of the last missing computers and routers to enable the electronic system), with a fixed limit for all participants, which will allow to keep online records. Activity 21) Development of the layout of the accompanying printing with information about the program, its production and implementation;2) Control of the correctness of the voucher program execution, tracking the list of necessary goods, volumes and intervals between transactions;3) Informing representatives of target vulnerabilities and selected localities about the project and its implementation. Activity 3 1) Providing feedback channels to the beneficiaries in the form of a hotline, receiving and processing appeals through social networks. Monitoring of project mentions, its fixation and work with questions and objections;2) Monitoring throughout the implementation of the voucher program, post-monitoring after its completion, writing and publication of the report in the public domain;3) Evaluation of the implemented project and preparation of the final report.

3 - Indicators & targets

Objective 1: Provide targeted issuance of pharmacy vouchers for the purchase of baby food, hygiene and medicines for vulnerable families with young children under one year (250 people, men - 55, women - 70), large families (250 people, men - 47, women - 78), families with disabled children (130 people, men-23, women - 27), families who have lost a breadwinner (80 people, men - 7, women - 28), families with disabled parents (60 people, men - 8, women-12) and families who lost their homes during the conflict (230 people, men - 45, women – 40). All of them live in a 20-kilometer zone from the demarcation line, including IDPs. The number of beneficiaries is 1,000.
