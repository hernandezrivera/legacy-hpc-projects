Project Summary

Provision of Water, Sanitation and Hygiene services to Flood and Conflict affected IDP's, Returnees and Horst communities of Greater Jonglie (Fangak and Canal/Pigi counties) and Upper Nile (Panyikang and Maiwut counties)
The objectives of the project
1) Contribute to reducing prevalence of malnutrition
2) Contribute to reducing WASH-related morbidity and mortality
3) Access to basic services
Key Planned activities
1) Distribution of standard kits paired with hygiene kits to SAM children/caretakers and PLW
2) Rehabilitation of of nutrition facilities with sanitation
3) Provision of nutrition facilities with access to safe water
4) provision of Aqua-tablets for household water treatment
5) Rehabilitation of mark -2 hand pump boreholes for safe water other than household water treatment
6) Provision of slabs to the people to increase access to sanitation
7) Conduct hygiene Promotion to the people using clean water and soap
8) Rehabilitated/construction of water point's/sources with a flood-resilient design
9) Rehabilitated/construction of latrines with a flood-resilient design

1 - Needs

Jonglie and Upper Nile state's are among the most conflict affected areas and remain highly fragile and unstable due to the armed conflict that erupted in 2013, 2016 and the subsequent intercommunal violence of December 2019 and the recent Kitguang incidence within the SPLA -IO that has seen subsequent displacements; loss of lives, destroyed property, forced thousands of families to flee their homes, their situation is worsened by the impact of severe flooding, hunger, and the COVID-19 pandemic with this it has constrained the available WASH services and infrastructures that can't meet the demand of IDP, Returnees and Horst communitiesWith the recent flooding in Upper Nile and Jonglie in 2021 that destroyed most of the available WASH infrastructure including latrines, water sources and water point thus contaminating water sources causing out break water and sanitation related disease thus contributing to high morbidity and mortality with children, elderly and pregnant mothers mostly affected most; Cases of GBV mostly SBV often occur as women and girls travel long distances in such of clean water as they get rapped on their way to accessing clean water
