Project Summary

The economic and political crisis in Lebanon has had severe consequences for vulnerable migrants, leaving many without work, homeless, and at risk of protection-based violations. To address this, the Migration Services and Development (MSD) will provide comprehensive legal and protection services to the most vulnerable migrants in Lebanon.
Legal services will include counseling, legal assistance, and legal representation, while protection services will include case management and individual counseling and mental health and psychosocial support. These services aim to provide migrants with the necessary support to address their legal issues and protect them from exploitation and abuse.
To ensure the safety and well-being of vulnerable migrants, MSD will conduct individual screenings and offer non-food items (hygine kits and dignity kits) and will allocate 90 USD multipurpose cash assistance over two times for the most vulnerable migrants using the cash in hand modality in order to target the ones who don't have any legal documents. They will also provide Assisted Voluntary Return (AVR) assistance to those who wish to return to their countries of origin. Additionally, MSD will provide trainings on detention and prisons for 20 humanitarians in order to build their intervention capacities with detained migrants.
MSD has been actively supporting migrants since the beginning of the economic crisis in 2019-2020, and they have a strong relationship with the community and other stakeholders, such as embassies, consulates, and Lebanese governmental agencies, including general security. Through this project, MSD aims to reduce the number of migrants who become victims of trafficking and other abuses under the Kafala system.
The provision of legal and protection services, as well as awareness-raising activities, will contribute to building more resilient communities in Lebanon. By improving the well-being of vulnerable migrants, this project will have a positive impact on their mental health, increasing their resilience and capacity to cope with the challenges they face.
In conclusion, this project is of great importance in addressing the needs of vulnerable migrants in Lebanon who have been severely affected by the economic and political crisis. The comprehensive legal and protection services, along with awareness-raising activities, will not only improve the lives of migrants but also contribute to building more resilient communities.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

Under Outcome1, the project will provide 450 vulnerable migrants with awareness sessions on legal topics, community-based protection, access to services to improve their knowledge of protection services. MSD, by its two lawyer, will also provide legal counseling, assistance and legal representation to 200 vulnerable migrants to help them navigate the complex legal and Kafala system in Lebanon. MSD's social workers will provide protection case management and individual counseling, including MHPSS, to help 350 vulnerable migrants overcome the challenges they face, and will also conduct 450 individual screenings to identify those in need of additional assistance and refer to alternative aid providers.Under outcome 2, MSD will provide 450 migrants with NFIs (hygiene and dignity kits)over 3 months and mutipurpose cash assistance over 2 months to help them meet their basic needs, such as shelter, food, and healthcare. MSD will also provide trainings on prisons and detention to increase the intervention capacities of 20 humanitarians.Under Outcome 3, MSD will provide vulnerable migrants with Assisted Voluntary Return to their country of origin, including airplane ticket, travel documentation, and transportation.Under Outcome 4, MSD will conduct one qualitative study specific to migrants in prison. This will enable efficient and evidence-based humanitarian programming across sectors by improving data collection and analysis.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

To implement the project for vulnerable migrants in Lebanon, a protection risk mitigation plan is developed with a focus on preventing sexual exploitation and abuse (PSEA). Implementing a PSEA code of conduct, providing regular training, and setting up community-based protection mechanisms are essential steps. Complaints will be promptly investigated through a robust feedback mechanism that ensures confidentiality and no fear of retaliation. Regular monitoring and evaluation with feedback from beneficiaries and stakeholders will identify areas for improvement. This will ensure safety, protection, and build trust.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

The project aimed at providing services to vulnerable migrants in Lebanon, prioritizes Accountability to Affected Populations (AAP). MSD takes into account the culture and language of the migrants in designing all the project's acticities as well as the feedback of beneficiaries, their needs through focus group discussions, and different assessments, including pre and post-assistance evaluations. Complaints and feedback are also taken seriously, and MSD ensures that a robust complaints and feedback mechanism is in place to address any concerns. By involving beneficiaries in decision-making processes and being responsive to their needs and feedback, MSD is committed to ensuring accountability and transparency in its service deliver
