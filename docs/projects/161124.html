Project Summary

CIDO intends to deliver static life-saving integrated S/NFI activities targeting male and female returnees, IDPs and host communities in 5 Counties of Jonglei State and 2 counties of Lakes State within a span of one calendar year.The counties targeted are (Nyirol, Pibor,Pochalla, Duk and Akobo) of Jonglei State and (Yirol West and Yirol East) of Lakes State. The total number of beneficiaries, men, women, boys and girls targeted by the project are 20,000 approximately 3,500 HHs the mainly composed of new IDPS, the Returnees and 5% of host community.Specifically CIDO will reach the target using in-kind and CBI modalities ; 16 000 individuals will be reached with GIK and 4000 with CBI. The project will be guided by international standards such as IASC and CERF life saving criteria and S/NFI cluster strategy. The main goal of the project is to increase access to S/NFI items, promote resilience of the new and most vulnerable returnees, host communities, and IDPs through integrated service delivery and engaging affected population in discussions for durable solutions building on existing skills and resources. The proposed activities will be implemented in synergy / joint mission with other S/NFI partners on the ground like ADA, ,Medair, Plan International PAH, LWF and HDC who are operating as static and mobile partners in Duk,Pochalla,Nyirol, Akobo, Pibor, Yirol West and Yirol East Counties, Additionally CIDO will work closely with Jonglei and Lakes State Focal Persons (SFP’s) all through the project life to avoid duplication and maximize the use of scarce resources. CIDO’s team will focus on inclusivity of female headed households, persons with special needs, unaccompanied elderly, people with no links to the community, separated children, women at risk (pregnant and lactating mothers), child-headed HH, people living with disability assessed and verified to be in need .CIDO will promote sustainability and durable solutions through coordination with other partners and multi sector integration of protection,WASH, Nutrition, CCM and FSL, CIDO will deliver CBI in coordination with the cluster Cash working Group and cash leading Organizations like, PAH, LWF and ADA. The cash component will be determined by the outcome of market assessments and will be coordinated well with FSL partners implementing cash programming in the proposed counties. S/NFI cluster coordination team both at national and state level will be fully engaged to support the interventions right from assessments, verification, distribution and PDM. CIDO will use clear and useful Complaint Response\Feedback (CRFM) desk which will be established and made operational right from the initial Phase of the analysis all through the beneficiaries identification, registration ,ditribution and during PDM as it will play a key role in resolving beneficiaries’ queries, complaints and compliments in a proactive manner while providing critical information on CBI like transfer value, vendors SOPs, code of conduct and PSEA documents. This will be useful to CIDO’s M&E team as well as provide lessons learnt to be shared with multi-sector partners, cluster and ICWG. CIDO will adhere to the AAP principles both in all the Counties and the respective locations all through the project life span While embracing the policy of Community Communication and Engagement (CCE) and ensuring that communities access clear and useful information to promote positive coping mechanisms and entrench resilience.Additionally CIDO will give support to returnees in understanding their HLP rights. CIDO will make the necessary interventions and consultations with local authorities on the use of the land before NFI distribution. In addition, CIDO will work hand in hand with HLP partners in all the counties to mitigate its operational challenges referring to the cluster formulated laws and policies on HLP to benefit all the marginalized groups.

1 - Needs

In the HNO 2020, S/NFI cluster Projects that out of 2,442,716 individuals approximately 407,120 HHs are in dire need of emergency shelter and NFI items . CIDO is targeting 20,000 individuals , approximately 3,500 HHs who are vulnerable and newly displaced IDPS, returnees and 5 % host community across five counties 5 counties of Pibor, Duk, Akobo, Pochalla, Nyirol , Yirl East and Yirol West. IOM DTM report of October 2019 breaks down the IDP figures as: 21,916 individuals in Akobo, 6,382 individuals in Pibor, 26,508 individuals in Duk, 28,596 individuals in Nyirol, 6, 500 individuals in Pochalla, about 10,000 individuals in Yirol East and about 25,000 individuals in Yirol West. According to S/NFI cluster HNO/HRP sectoral analysis, the cluster assumes that 70 per cent of the displaced population outside POCs are in need of S/NFI assistance. Outside of displacement sites, the targeted caseloads lack access to essential lifesaving NFI items and often times unable to support themselves through communal Connections or coping mechanisms through other resources The October 16th 2019 IRNA assessment conducted in Pibor county highlights critical S/NFI needs caused by conflict and greatly exacerbated by the recent floods in the area. According to the report a total of 146,057, men women boys and girls have been displaced by floods. Another 1200 HH have lost shelter and essential NFI items due to floods in poktap, Duk County .Insecurity related to inter communal and cattle raiding reported across most parts of Jonglei (Pibor, Akobo, Nyirol and Duk State has led to increased numbers of new IDP and returnees negatively affecting the Host Communities who are forced to share the little or no basic needs,shelters and non food items precisely (sleeping mats, mosquito nets, kitchen sets, Blankets and plastic sheets) at their disposals. Prolonged conflict and displacement have eroded people’s livelihoods subsequently eroding their coping mechanism. The health of pregnant mothers and children under 5 years is compromised due to poor living conditions exacerbating exposure to weather elements and disease-causing vectors. Current coping mechanisms across the targeted counties in Jonglei, Yirol East and Yirol west include use of skins and hides as beddings and cowdung as mosquito repellant. This requires a multi sectoral approach to promote durable solutions and sustainability. CIDO will provide intergrated ESNFIs solutions in collaboration with WASH, Protection, FSl, CCM and Nutrition partners. CIDO, will be guided by humanitarian inclusion standards for older people and people with disabilities and ensuring that PWL(pregnant and lactating women), single headed HHs and elderly persons are part of the project. CIDO will give support to returnees in understanding their HLP rights and make the necessary interventions and consultations on the use of the land and before distribution. CIDO will coordinate with protection partners specializing in HLP issues to mitigate challenges and where necessary refer the issues to HLP partners.CIDO will make proper use of the existing and planned expansion of the Humanitarian Hubs as spaces for coordination with other partners on critical response activities and access core pipeline S/NFI items from the nearest Humanitarian hub thus significantly saving time and mitigating logistical challenges normally caused by rain and poor infrastructure. CIDO embraces the area- based approach and being a static partner, the organization will ensure that intervention is geographically targeted, adopt a multi-sectoral and participatory approach. CIDO will work with FSL, Protection, WASH, CCCM, Nutrition and Education to integrate the activities including distribution of survival kits where necessary.

2 - Activities

1.CIDO will do a coordinated joint assessment using the cluster template for a period not less than 7days in each locations in collaboration with Protection, FSL, WASH, Education, and nutrition partners through coordinated , integrated and targeted , shelter and NFIs solutions, plus find unique ways of offering the just in-kind to enhance Health and protection Risks Mitigation of the targeted beneficiaries this will include HLP issues2. Conduct joint planning of response and specific cluster needs analysis based on the joint assessment findings. The needs assessment and analysis will guide identification of the most vulnerable displaced population with critical needs to be prioritized. It will also help to Conduct due diligence by assessing the tenure/ownership arrangements of housing or land prior to the provision of shelter/NFI assistance and identify gaps and approaches to protection mainstreaming.3.CIDO will conduct beneficiaries identification, verification and registration based on the ESNFIs Cluster targeting set criteria (most vulnerable, age and gender)4. CIDO will Conduct Emergency shelter materials/ or loose ESNFIs distribution and at the same time educate the beneficiaries on how to use the S/NFI materials. CIDO will ensure the distribution is coordinated in line with procedures of core pipeline. NFIs will be provided by the core pipeline .CIDO will Consult and agree with local authorities/stakeholders on the use of land prior to the construction of shelter5. CIDO will establish CR\FM (Complains Response\Feedback Mechanism and Conduct training of communities, established committees on their HLP and AAP rights.CFM will help raise awareness on HLP rights among crisis-affected populations, including the services available to support claiming such rights (e.g. legal assistance, dispute resolution and shelter assistance) and local rules and regulations on HLP matters (e.g. rental/lease agreements6. Conduct safety audit – In collaboration with protection partners, CIDO will participate in safety audit exercise to ascertain protection risks including HLP issues to inform Protection mainstreaming which will be guided by protection toolkit through protection partners. As a protection partner CIDO will disseminate GBV pocket guides for non-GBV actors7. Conduct PDM- CIDO’s Post Distribution Management Team will conduct PDMs using the cluster tools to evaluate usefulness, access and level of satisfaction of S/NFI items by the targeted vulnerable beneficiaries and beneficiaries ability to use S/NFIs for supporting results-based health, protection, livelihood, and hygiene needs.8.CIDO will Conduct rapid market assessment and needs analysis for the pilot CBI intervention using the CBI tools, forms and templates eg the CBI M$E template with focus on vulnerability, protection and resilience in targeted counties to ascertain feasibility and risk assessment.-CIDO will carry out the assessment In coordination with PAH, LWF and ADA and other leading agencies.9. CIDO will Conduct selection of the Financial Service Providers (FSP) and vendors using the CWG criteria and guidelines, 10. Conduct verification and registration of target beneficiaries for CBI - CIDO will use the CWG Guidelines and UNHCR Operational Guidelines for Cash-Based Interventions in Displacement Settings to guide the verification and registration exercise, CIDO will Consult and agree with local authorities/stakeholders on the use of land for the provision CBI for construction of Shelter to mitigate HLP risks.11. In coordination with other Cash partners Facilitate piloting of voucher distribution of unconditional but restricted commodity vouchers for locally available shelter materials12. Conduct monitoring of CBI from the beginning to the end of the project and Conduct training of male and female beneficiaries on life skills ,coping strategies and durable solutions to shelter and NFI
