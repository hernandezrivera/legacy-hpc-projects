Project Summary

In 2023, based on the guidance and in line with the suggested curriculum model set by the Skills Development Sector, WFP will transition existing skills development activities into a Combination of foundation and technical trainings. Technical trainings includes TVET (Technical & vocational Education and Training) and upcycling project. WFP will be partner organization under Skills Development Sector and will contribute to the sector by participating in meeting and advocate for portable skills for refugees along with other agencies under its sector. The activities will be conducted in close coordination with the Government of Bangladesh, in particular with RRRC and CiCs for field operation and with other stakeholders in camps (i.e. SWM, FSS). As much as possible, the project also will consider social cohesion between intra-community and refugee and host communities by i.e. having materials produced at camp to be further add value at host community livelihoods.

1. Needs
1 - Identified Needs

In Rohingya refugee camp, mobility of women and girls outside their homes are limited due to community’s social norms, traditional gender roles and practices, leading them of having limited access to services, information and to have little decision-making power in their household. Adolescent girls, young women, and women heads of households are some of the most at-risk, with potential risk of missing opportunity for access to education and vocational skills. With the government restricting economic activities in the camp, risk of human trafficking, as well as child marriage and gender-based violence are high. Persons with disabilities and elderly are likely to face certain physical barriers such as barriers to moving inside shelters, moving around camps, accessing and using latrines and showers, and having access to different services in the camps. To overcome those barriers it is crucial for those to have an opportunity to build their capacity, by developing skills which are portable and support the application and practice of acquired skills and capacities to mitigate challenges in the camp. With this, the technical and foundation skills provided through the project is expected to empower the participants, in particular, women, persons with disabilities and elderly, leading to enhance their self-reliance and participation in decision making.

2. Rationalization
2 - How is your project aligned to the Rationalization Principles (in particular reference # 3 - Adopt an integrated approach, # 4 - Ensure predictability and continuity)?

In 2023, WFP will implement a transition plan to consolidate current Self-Reliance activities, with a combination of foundation and technical trainings and practical skills development. Along with Technical and Vocational Education and Training (TVET), and upcycling activities, the programme will also focus on disability and age inclusion, which engages people with disabilities and older refugees with accessible skills development and production activities, allowing them to contribute to household food security. Through foundaiton trainings beneficiary will come to know basic learnings which they can utilize in their everyday life. Such as: basic on literacy, numeracy, personal hygine, protection, basic on nutrition by concerning everyday food consumption, sign language for person with disabilities, physical exercise session for person with disabilities. Moreover, through technical trainings beneficiary will learn specific skill which they can utilize to prepare product which needed in their home as well as they can support their neighbers. Additionally, by using this sort of portable skill they can get livelihood opportunity when they will return their homeland Myanmar in future.
