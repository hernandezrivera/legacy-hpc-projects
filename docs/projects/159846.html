Project Summary

The project aims at providing life-saving nutrition services for targeted 3962 children (1981 boys and 1981 girls) aged between 6-59 months and 1961 Pregnant Lactating Women (PLWs) that are severely acute and moderately malnourished by providing an integrated nutrition package based on Somali IMAM standards with strong community support through capacity building of nutrition staff, Community Health Workers and Community Nutrition volunteers on case detection through MUAC measurements and weight-height measures to mitigate against sustained and escalating malnutritioncaseloads in Abudwak Districts. Malnutrition prevalence is still very high in Abduwak with GAM rates above 15% threshold. The situation is worsened by poor limit access to health and nutrition services coupled by poor hygiene conditions and practices as wells poor diet diversity and complementary feeding and poor breast feeding patterns among lactating mothers hence need for emergency life-saving nutrition services

Project Activities

1. Screening of 3962 children (1981 boys and 1981 girls) aged between 6-59 months and 1961 PLWs and treating them through SFP program 2. Promotion of IYCF with emphasis to exclusive breastfeeding and complementary feeding targeting 1961 PLWs in project locations in Abudwak District3. Prevention and management of common diseases (anemia, diarrhea, malaria etc) through Vitamin A supplementation, deworming, multiple micro nutrients, supplements, iron/folate supplements and dissemination of hygiene promotion messages and practices to the targeting 3962 children (1981 boys and 1981 girls) aged between 6-59 months and 1961 PLWs 4. Conducting Training/refresher to 40 SFP staff (men and women) as well as volunteers in case detection through MUAC measurements and weight measures 5. Training of 60 Community Health workers (men and women) as well as nutrition volunteers on nutrition education and to enhance their knowledge in Malnutrition and referral procedures6. Participation in nutrition surveys and meeting for early warning information and timely coordinated response
