Project Summary

- Distribution of point-of-use water treatment materials / products, including training and monitoring on proper use at household level.
- Rehabilitation and disinfection of existing water points in host communities, accomodation centers, and resettlement areas, including support for water committees.
- construction of new water sources for host communities and resettlement areas
- Expansion of piped networks to affected communities, resettlement sites, and hosting communities
- Promotion activities for self-construction of household latrines and garbage pits in resettlement sites and hosting communities (including the provision of sanitation supplies for most vulnerable households)
- Dissemination of WASH related messages through mass communication media
- Procurement and Distribution of WASH related Non-Food items, with focus on most vulnerable families / households
