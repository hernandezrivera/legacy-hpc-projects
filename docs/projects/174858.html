Project Summary

With the presence of the ongoing Anglophone crisis, the educational sector has been greatly hit, with sporadic attacks on educational institutions within the affected region, and threat of life to students and children within the region. Prior to this, access to education for persons with disabilities was greatly neglected by most families because of their disabilities. Yet with the increase in advocacy and debates on disability inclusion and ability behind disabilities by the coordination unit of associations of persons with disabilities for the south west, a significant change was registered in most parts of the region, with more and more children with disabilities getting enrolled and being very enthusiastic to acquire education. But with the outbreak of this crisis, much of that effort and excitement has been water down, not just by the crisis due to effects of educational institutions, but also on the families, as the crisis has brought about untold hardship and education for children with disabilities is very huge investment which most families cannot afford to shoulder within the context of the crisis. Disability is not restricted to a particular gender or sex. it is on this standpoint that this intervention shares in its purpose to expedite access to education and protection children and youths with disability of both sex with stress on female children with disabilities against illiteracy, by assisting children with disabilities and families of children with disabilities with educational materials, tuition fee and assistive devices, to enable access to education
