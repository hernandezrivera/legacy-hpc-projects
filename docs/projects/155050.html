Project Summary

Empowering Rohingya refugees and host communities to provide feedback is key to enable a relevant, conflict-sensitive and localised response. Using established feedback systems supplemented with additional feedback through targeted outreach and engagement, such as perception surveys and focus group discussions, will help provide data that can be consolidated and can support sectors’ in decision-making. UNICEF through its project interventions will ensure community engagement , participation and a paradigm shift from messaging to dialogue, by engaging key community stakeholders and wider communities. Some of the key activities: i) TRAINING & CAPACITY DEVELOPMENT: Identify, engage and train community volunteers and staff of partner organanizations on IPC and CwC techniques and methods.
ii) AWARENESS GENERATION & BEHAVIOUR CHANGE: Raise awareness to targeted population (children, women, men) on key behavioral and social change messages on health, nutrition, education, hygiene, sanitation, and protection through door to door visits, community consultations & community dialogue for sustained behaviour change.
iii) RADIO & LOCAL MEDIA PROGRAMMING & BROADCASTING: Develop age-appropriate and culture sensitive local media programme i.e IPT show, folk song and Tarara shows, development of public service announcements, partnership with the ministry of information/Bangladesh Betar, BBC Media Action & community Radio Naf.
iv) SOCIAL MOBILISATION & COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT: Identify community leaders including children and adolescents who have the potential to influence their immediate community; conduct training/ orientation with local community leaders e.g., maji, imams, women leaders, children and adolescent on key Community engagement approaches and messages; and engage local community leaders in designing, development, and implementation of Community engagment activities. To mobilise communities on DRR, enviornment and social cohesion through Inter personal sessions and wider community consultaions. Development of gender sensitive materails and ensuring sex disaggretated data is collated and synthesised through online platforms.
v) COMMUNITY ACCOUNTABILITY MECHANISM: Train staff/volunteers managing IFCs especially in handling feedback and complaints;
UNICEF will: continue to collect disaggregate data by sex, age and disability; ensure gender balanced teams (staff and volunteers); use mechanisms and create opportunities to engage community members and ensure equal access of women, girls, boys and men in the design, implementation and evaluation of integrated programs. Accountability will remain a key UNICEF priority in the years to come as well. UNICEF partners to collect feedback, grievances or complaints and respond to them with effective and timely data sharing and evidence generation with other agencies/sectors. UNICEF will be covering a total population of 825,000 people through different community engagement approaches. Working in line with the CwC vision and objectives , UNICEF will ensure Human rights based interventions across the target groups.

Needs

Empowering Rohingya refugees and host communities to provide feedback is key to enable a relevant, conflict-sensitive and localized response. Using established feedback systems supplemented with additional feedback through targeted outreach and engagement, such as perception surveys and focus group discussions, will help provide data that can be consolidated and can support sectors’ in decision-making.To address the needs and gaps in integration of gender, age and disability perspectives into the response, UNICEF will: continue to collect disaggregate data by sex, age and disability; ensure gender-balanced teams (staff and volunteers); use mechanisms and create opportunities to engage community members and ensure equal access of women, girls, boys and men in the design, implementation and evaluation of integrated programs. Although a majority of refugees state that they are finding it easier to access information about the basics of services (who, what, where and when) and navigate their way around their camp, there are still many information gaps. For example, the elderly, those living with disabilities, adolescent girls, and women who are unable to attend a listening group or walk around the camp are still failing to get access to the basic information about delivery of services. It is essential to maintain and sustain the community engagement interventions at the camp level through community mobilization volunteers. Language-appropriate radio and offline audio programmes and pictorial IEC materials have addressed some of the communication gaps in information provision, but continued to be produced for 2019 . Accountability will remain a key UNICEF priority in the years to come as well. UNICEF partners to collect feedback, grievances or complaints and respond to them with effective and timely data sharing and evidence generation with other agencies/sectors. While there has been some communication, accountability mechanisms are in place e.g Betar Sanglap with the host communities, there are still information gaps on key issues that will also be addressed in the 2019 UNICEF plan.

Activities or outputs

i) TRAINING & CAPACITY DEVELOPMENT: Identify, engage and train community volunteers and staff of partner organanizations on IPC and CwC techniques and methods. ii) AWARENESS GENERATION & BEHAVIOUR CHANGE: Raise awareness to targeted population (children, women, men) on key behavioral and social change messages on health, nutrition, education, hygiene, sanitation, and protection through the door to door visits, community consultations & community dialogue for sustained behavior change. iii) RADIO & LOCAL MEDIA PROGRAMMING & BROADCASTING: Develop age-appropriate and culture sensitive local media programme i.e IPT show, folk song and Tarara shows, development of public service announcements, the partnership with the ministry of information/Bangladesh Betar, BBC Media Action & community Radio Naf. iv) SOCIAL MOBILISATION & COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT: Identify community leaders including children and adolescents who have the potential to influence their immediate community; conduct training/ orientation with local community leaders e.g., maji, imams, women leaders, children and adolescent on key Community engagement approaches and messages; and engage local community leaders in designing, development, and implementation of Community engagment activities. To mobilise communities on DRR, enviornment and social cohesion through Inter personal sessions and wider community consultaions. Development of gender sensitive materails and ensuring sex disaggretated data is collated and synthesised through online platforms. v) COMMUNITY ACCOUNTABILITY MECHANISM: Train staff/volunteers managing IFCs especially in handling feedback and complaints; record and respond to the complaints, queries and feedback from the affected population; and analyze regularly and share with affected population and management a summary progress on feedback/complaints made. Leading and coordinating the Info Hub sub working group. vi) CAMPAIGNS: Conduct issue-specific mobilization campaigns to reach minimum 75% (each round) of settlement popualtion with life saving information. vii)RESEARCH, EVIDENCE & DOCUMENTATION: Multi-sectoral baseline KAPB study on health, nutrition, WASH, education and protection issues (aligned to the RTM); development of programme/ field guidance briefs, development of Language glossary & development of case studies and good practices.
