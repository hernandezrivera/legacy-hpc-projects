Project Summary

(1) To assist in preventing and separation of children from caregivers, and facilitate the identification, registration and medical screening and provision of interim care for separated children, in particular those under five years of age and adolescent girls and registration of caregivers who have lost their children; (2) ensure that family tracing systems are implemented, with appropriate care and protection facilities; (3) prevent sexual abuse and exploitation of women and children by reporting and advocating against instances of sexual violence by military forces, state actors, armed groups and others and providing post-rape health and PSS support; (4) support establishment of safe environments for children and women, including child-friendly spaces, and integrate psycho-social support in protection responses; (5) within established systems, coordinate mine-risk education.

Activities

1) Identification, Documentation, Tracing and Family Reunification and/or placement into alternative forms of care for separated and unaccompanied minors; 2) Provision of Child Friendly Spaces for PSS and safeguarding; 3) Provision of Psychosocial First Aid and Trauma counseling; 4) Strengthening Case Management system - identification and referral for services children with welfare and protection concerns, 5) Strengthening child protection/GBV community surveillance systems within and outside camp settlements; 6) Strengthening Child Safeguarding; 7) Protection Sector Joint Needs Assessment; 8) Support access to civil registration documents for affected people including children, including mobile registration and access to duplicates inside and outside camp settlements; 9) Capacity Building of protection partners in PSS, CPiE, GBV in Emergencies and child safeguarding; 10) Prevention and Response of Sexual Abuse and Exploitation of women and children through engagement with military and service providers, capacity building, access to mobile post-rape care and information; 11) Support protection needs of children at risk including children with a disability, children affected by HIV, adolescent girls, teenage mothers and pregnant girls; 12) support Government coordination of Protection service delivery at national and sub-national level
