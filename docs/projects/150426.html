Project Summary

This is a 12-month responsive project, which aims to increase the accessibility, and quality of life of any 300 disabled older people (55% female, 45% male) in the north Gaza Governorate. This project will respond mainly to the essential health needs of the target group through holistic rehabilitation activities, which include provision of assistive devices and diapers also provision of rehabilitation services including physiotherapy (PT), occupational therapy (OT), speech therapy (ST), psycho-social support (PSS), and home care nursing.

Needs

Ageing has been a challenging issue across the world for decades; either in developing countries or developed ones. The proportion of older persons is growing at a faster rate than the general population. The situation in Palestine in general, and the Gaza Strip in particular has been worsening dramatically than before two decades for external reasons including the repeatedly Israeli aggression, a 10-years strict blockade, backwardness of funding civic organizations, and USA’s decision to stop funding UNRWA, which supports 70% of refugee population in the Gaza Strip, and for internal factors related to the political split, high rate of poverty, unemployment which raised the rate of dependency on UNRWA to 80% as reported by AIDA on Feb, 8th. 2018 (info@aidajerusalem.org). PCBS (2018) indicated that total percentage of older people aged 60 and over reached 5% of the population in Palestine (4.3.% in Gaza Strip, 4.6% M against 5.4% F). Life expectancy increased for both males and females from 67.0 years in 1992 to 72.3 years for males and 75.4 years for females by the midst of 2017. Also it reported about 45.7% of older people in Gaza have disability, 30.6% with mobility difficulties, followed by vision impairment 22.2%. Help Age International (HAI) has conducted situational analysis in 2016, declared that the disability rates increased with age and the highest percentage was among individual aged 75 years and older at 32.1%. The same report indicated that 90% of older people had not taken part in any psycho-social intervention, while 20.7% suffer from depression and 19.8% suffer from anxiety. The same report added, neglect of the older generation is apparent in the absence of any effective national policies or programs tailored to meet the special needs of this sector, or even minimal protection of basic rights to safeguard health, well-being and dignity. The “HI” with its partners (2015) reported that the people with disabilities including the older people in Gaza strip still suffer from lack of rehabilitation services especially in the most vulnerable areas. It was due to several causes such as the insufficient rehabilitation services of MOH in Gaza strip, limited access to community based rehabilitation services for older people in NGOs sector, weakness of referral processing in the local referral network system, and the poor experience of the caregiver's. Baitona Society and International Medical Corps (IMC) conducted needs assessment survey for 300 intentional sample of older people in 2017. The main findings were: 1. 100% of their caregivers needed for training and information about the needs and how to deal with the elderly stage. This is needed to change the negative attitudes of families against elderly stage and protection of human rights of older people including independence and decent life. 2. 70% needed for assistive devices, and 30% of them needed for diapers. This is required to give the beneficiaries more access and benefit from services facilities and increase their social participation.3. 76% needed for rehabilitation sessions (PT, OT, ST, and PSS). This is needed to respond to essential health problems, improving their mobility, alleviating the economic cost over their families, and improving their psycho-social condition. 4. 30% needed for nursing especially for who suffer from diabetic foot and bed sores. This is very important to decrease the possibility of having amputation, and the consequences of infections as well as negative psychological effects.This project coincides with the Goal number 3 and meet the specific objectives number 2, 4, and 6 of Baitona’s Strategic plan. Noting that Baitona has a comprehensive therapy center (PT,OT,ST) and has a profound experience with older people with partnership with HI and IMC. In addition. This project relates to health and nutrition cluster objective number ONE and related to strategic objective number TWO .

Activities

Task/Activity 1: conducting 4 orientation sessions for the staff regarding the older people specific needs. Task/Activity 2: conducting needs assessment survey for 300 older people (55% Female & 45% male) through 300 home visits during the second month of the project.Task/Activity 3: Provision of assistive devices (wheel chairs, walkers, canes, hearing aids, and disposable and screening diapers) to targeted 235 older people (55% Female & 45% male), based on the need assessment survey. Task/Activity 4: Provision outreach sessions of physiotherapy, speech therapy, and occupational therapy for 300 older people (55% Female & 45% male), based on the need assessment survey. Task/Activity 5: provision nursing care for 30% of older people (55% Female & 45% male), based on the need assessment survey. Task/Activity 6: distributing nursing kits for 30% of older people.Task/Activity 7: Provide 600 caregivers (50% Female & 50% male) of older people and/or cares field (home) training on geriatric care, and the importance of wellbeing and mobility.Task/Activity 8: Provide 300 older people (55% Female & 45% male) with individual psychosocial support sessions using evidence based approach (psychoeducational approached)Task/Activity 9: Provide 100 older people (55% Female & 45% male) with group psychosocial support sessions using evidence based approach (psychodrama approach)Task/Activity 10: Provide 100 older people (55% Female & 45% male) with four recreational tripsTask/Activity 11: referring 10% of total Beneficiaries to other health services providers.
