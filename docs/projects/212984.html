Project Summary

RMCO has currently identified a project ready for implementation in two different areas:
1-Hasaka and Deir Ezzor countryside: which are considered the largest in North and East Syria for contaminated lands. The covered land area is divided into two operational areas.
Area A - Hasaka Countryside: Which covers six villages with many estimated explosive hazards.
Area B – Deir Ezzor Countryside: This area has less inhabitants compared to Area A as it was an area of direct conflict during the past years, and large numbers of people were forced to flee their villages and agricultural lands and displaced to safer areas or resided in camps due to their inability to live in peace and secure.
2-Raqqa city: After the liberation from the Islamic State, the city lived a terrifying period due to the spread of mines and ordnance everywhere and the danger hasn't been completely removed.
From here, we have taken upon ourselves till today the task of spreading awareness and activating communication with the population and local councils to facilitate the tasks of removal and reduce losses, and we have identified in this study two of the neighborhoods and some villages which are the most affected by the war ordnance to be targeted by our multi-tasking teams. This requires us, as a local organization RMCO, to carry out our humanitarian tasks to provide emergency awareness lectures to the targeted civilian areas, age groups and both genders to reduce injuries, identify dangerous objects and its extent, and report these confirmed dangerous areas. We intend to start emergency risk education of explosive hazards in populated target areas and move abroad. Our vision is to achieve a safe environment for civilians and families to return to their homes and for farmers to cultivate their fields and children to play safely, and for that we prepared a mechanism of actions for multi-tasking teams:
1_Community Liaison: starts meetings with community leaders and local councils to collect population data (population census - times of conflict - number of victims - types of injuries - places of danger - damaged infrastructure). In case of any victims or injuries, a special report is prepared (Acc V)
2_ Non-Technical Survey: The specialized teams draw the boundaries of the target area and conduct a primary check and based on it when there is any danger, a danger report is prepared. If it's limited to one piece, so they prepare a danger spot task report, but if it extends over a wide area, it would be a Hazard report.
3_ Awareness: The teams lecture people about the types of mines, ordnance and explosives, and explain about their danger (direct _ indirect) impact on them, how to identify it without approaching or touching it, and reporting to the specialist organizations and authorities.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The project’s primary goal is to expand RMCO’s reach into Raqqa, Hasaka, and Deir Ezzor areas of the north and east Syria that are SDF governed areas; and where the Global Coalition personnel currently operate. We intend to build our organizational capacity, establish a regional base of operations, and equip, train and operationalize multitasking field teams (working on NTS, MRE, CL, ACC, VA reports) In addition to demining teams which form the backbone of RMCO’s operations. Our field teams identify, and assist with the safe removal of hundreds of mines from the designated area to ensure that the work is performed to perfection so quality assurance/quality control will be undertaken when delivering risk education activities, including team preparation and reporting of sessions. Evaluation should be done in the field, by the Outreach Coordinator and Outreach Supervisor, for each team at least once a month.Feedback should be provided to the team being monitored immediately upon completion of QA/QC as part of ongoing on-the-job training. Corrective action, which may include additional capacity building, should be taken if poor performance or misconduct is observed. Severe or repeated poor performance should be investigated and may result in disciplinary action in line with RMCO Human Resources policies.
