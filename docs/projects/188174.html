Project Summary

Strengthen lifesaving preventive nutrition services for vulnerable population groups focusing on appropriate infant and young child feeding practices in emergency, micronutrient interventions and optimal maternal nutrition. (Lifesaving, protection and basic services).
Improve equitable access to quality lifesaving curative nutrition services through systematic identification, referral and treatment of acutely malnourished cases for boys and girls under five (Lifesaving and basic services/Resilience).

4 - Needs

2019 SMART surveys indicated pockets of emerging chronic and acute malnutrition. Chronic malnutrition potentially reflect a longer term inadequate dietary intake, including inadequate micronutrient intake as well as repeated infections in younger children, poor feeding practices as reflected by low rates of exclusive breastfeeding and inadequate complementary food in terms of quality and frequency. The nutrition surveillance system is now consisted of a network of up to 968 health centres, with more than 1,016,059 beneficiaries. Further expansion reactivating new centres in Ar-Raqqa, Hassakeh, Rural Aleppo, Dair Ezzor Idleb and Daraa is required. In 2021 an average of 125 U5 children admitted and treated per month compared to 75 in 2018, 90 in 2017 and 65 in 2016. In addition, 19,440 global malnutrition cases were detected compared to 22,670 in 2018 and 18,445 in 2017. 19 stabilization centres (SC) are distributed all over country and cover most of the needs. The SCs will require continued support of nutrition therapeutic supplies, equipment and training of the human resources. When access is permitted, SC will be needed in Ar-Raqqa and Idleb. NES, Aleppo, north west and south of Syria require further expansion of nutrition surveillance sites, screening teams and strengthening health worker capacity to detect and manage malnourished cases. Infant and young child feeding practices remain low and suboptimal (28.5% compared with 25.3 for exclusive breast feeding, in 2015).

5 - Implementation of activities

1. Support facility and community based screening for acute and chronic malnutrition and referral via training and anthropometric equipment2. Improve the treatment of severely acute malnourished children with complications at hospital level through supporting established stabilization centres with training, nutrition therapeutic supplies and equipment.3. Capacity building of health facility staff and community workers on appropriate IYCF practices including implementing the 10 steps to successful breast feeding through BFHI, management of complicated SAM and nutrition surveillance, and early childhood development.4. Support facility and community based counselling and awareness raising on IYCF and optimal maternal nutrition.5- Participate in WHO specific or Nutrition cluster field assessments, and/or nationwide surveys

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

1-Limited geographical coverage of nutrition surveillance sites and outreach screening for the early detection of malnutrition which increases the risk of undetected cases of acute malnutrition at early stages.2-Limited support of stabilization centres especially in hard to reach areas could present a high risk of mortality rate during hospitalization for complicated cases of SAM.3-Reduced rates of exclusive breastfeeding increase the risk of malnutrition in children U5 and morbidity and mortality due to other health implications due to low rates of exclusive breastfeeding.


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Approach to Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA), which includes mitigating activities, including but not limited to the implementation of PSEA policy, standards of behavior for organization’s staff, implementing partners personnel and representatives,; complain PSEA mechanism and training activities for partners and staff of health facilities in all governorates ; PSEA self assessment for partners, coordination with PSEA ICN network to sustain all required measures related to PSEA issues.


11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

1-Expand the nutrition surveillance system and strengthen outreach screening in marginalized and HTR areas including IDPs.2-Conduct refresher courses on updated guidelines and continued supply chain of nutrition therapeutic supplies.3-Strengthen IYCF initiatives at hospital, health centre and community levels
