Project Summary

The proposed project aims to ensure that all children and young people have access to quality education and prevent gendered school drop-outs by addressing emergency related stress factors that affect access to education. Plan International will ensure coordination among provincial and district education structures, monitor school attendance through instituting a robust monitoring system, raise awareness relating to the impacts of disasters on learners, preposition learning materials and temporal learning spaces as well as promote participation of communities in DRR and early warning.

Needs

Need to restore and improve access to quality education for learners during times of distress resulting from Education in Emergencies (EiE) and any other forms of disasters. This proposal proffers a holistic and inclusive approach to response and mitigation. The proposal further highlights the following requirements to ensure achievement of the desired immediate and intermediate impact: Effective partner, stakeholder and other clusters providing protection, food security and nutrition, DRR and WASH services; Positioning of teaching and learning materials, provision of temporal learning facilities as well as rehabilitation of learning facilities that present risks to the teaching and learning processes; capacity enhancement for education structures and systems is also highlighted in the proposal as a prerequisite to for long term sustainability and resilience building.

Activities

Create awareness among parents and community members on the importance of education for all children, especially girls (through Back to School campaigns etc.)• Provision of back to school/student kits for children • Provision of school/classroom kits• Distribution of food rations to schools for children hot meals to increase and retain their enrolment (Food for Education)• Provision of girls incentives for adolescent girls (10 -17 years) to attend school (Conditional Cash transfers) • Training of teachers and teachers’ assistants, for pre-primary and primary education on teaching methodologies, school management inclusive of Child Protection issues. • Provision of psychological support and life skills training (first aids, public and reproductive health education, SGBV, Peace to children as part of the cross-cutting issues.
