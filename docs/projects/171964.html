1 - Needs

Coronavirus remains one of the main threats to public health in the world. The number of COVID-19 cases has already exceeded 46 million 800 thousand. The disease and the consequences of restrictive measures to counteract the pandemic have plunged the world community into a deep crisis. Due to the second wave of the pandemic, health systems around the world are overloaded. As a matter of urgency, countries are introducing new preventive measures to counter the spread of the disease. Providing hospitals with the necessary equipment to effectively deal with the consequences of COVID-19 infection is a priority for each state at the moment. The most vulnerable to the pandemic are residents of territories where the ongoing armed conflicts have created a critical social and economic situation.The ongoing armed conflict in Eastern Ukraine has led to almost complete destruction of the economy, a significant part of medical institutions has been damaged or destroyed during the fighting, and there are no funds in the budget to restore buildings. The number of qualified specialists in medical institutions has also significantly decreased due to the outflow of the population from the war zone and the economic collapse.Funds allocated to provide the remaining medical facilities with facilities and equipment to counter COVID-19 are extremely limited. From open sources, it became known that the number of deaths from the virus in non-government controlled areas of Ukraine (NGCA) of Donetsk oblast, exceeded 578 people, the total number of infected — is about 6873 (taking into account the total population of 2000000 people). The mortality rate from СOVID-19 reached 8,4%, with a norm not reaching 0.5% of deaths from the total number of cases. All of the above indicates a rapid spread of the disease. In addition, the actual number of cases and deaths may be many times higher due to the lack or poor quality of tests for the presence of antibodies to the virus. The situation is also complicated by the fact that in some localities the population does not have safe and fast access to medical services. In a number of villages and towns, ambulatory care centers (rural health posts) were damaged or destroyed as a result of shelling. Due to the presence of check-points and the road surface destroyed during the fighting, and the lack of direct transport links, access to the nearest medical facilities is extremely difficult.In order to more effectively counter the COVID-19 epidemic, it is planned to provide 10 of the most important medical institutions in Donetsk oblast (NGCA) with means and equipment for disinfection, protection from infection with the virus, as well as supplies for conducting examinations, bedding for performing anti-epidemic measures. The humanitarian aid is planned to include:1) equipment:- pulse oximeters- ventilators- oxygen concentrator- mercury thermometers (a great need in hospitals, since contactless ones have a large innacuracy)- bactericidal lamps \ recirculators- touch dispensers- tonometers- stands for droppers - a piece of medical cloth- urinals- panendoscopes- basins for tools and sputum2) supplies:- spatulas for sampling bio material- Logodes- Septosept-FFP-2 respirators- non-sterile gloves- disposable protective suits- protective screens (close-fitting glasses)3) bedding:- blankets- pillows- mattresses- linen.The target audience will be approximately 10,000 people (visitors, patients, and hospital staff)4) posters and leaflets for the public concerning the dissemination of WHO recommendations on counteraction with infection of the COVID-19 virus, explaining the ways of infection and the consequences of the disease.

2 - Activities & Outcomes

The proposed project includes the following activities:Solution 1. Provide medical institutions with the necessary medical equipment and materials to counter COVID-19, including ventilators, oxygen concentrators, and dispensers.a) selection of medical institutions willing to participate in the program;b) purchase of materials and equipment for the implementation of the program, their delivery.c) purchase and delivery of PPEs and disinfection products to hospitals.

Cluster goals within HRP:1) Reduce infectious disease transmission and hospitalization rate by supporting healthcare system, including laboratories and immunization, and COVID-19 incident management system (Surveillance, Infection Prevention and Control, Case Management, EECPs, Risk Communication and Community Engagement). Activity: Provide minimum package of PPE (based on generic PPE estimation tool) that can run health facilities at full capacity to ensure high safety IPC standards among healthcare workers. 2) Reduce infectious disease transmission and hospitalization rate by supporting healthcare system, including laboratories and immunization, and COVID-19 incident management system (Surveillance, Infection Prevention and Control, Case Management, EECPs, Risk Communication and Community Engagement).
