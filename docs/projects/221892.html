Project Summary

The National Protection Consortium of South Sudan Led-by Survivor Aid will provide an integrated protection services to the priority needs identified in HNRP 2025 overview through response to "General Protection, Child Protection, Gender based Violence, and HLP" in Counties affected by conflicts, flooding, violence and displacement.
This is intended to mainstreamed solutions to the existing problems affecting communities, that would poster peaceful coexistence, equal participation and safeguarding and rights consideration. During implementation, the Consortium members (Survivor Aid, OSIL & Agency for Women and Children Development) will extend activities to the hard to reach areas covered under this project through the existing community outreach programme and other mobile modalities outline under this project. This implementation strategy will immensely contribute to achieving the strategic inter-sectoral and cluster objectives 2025.
The proposed sub-sectors and activities will ensure provision of critical protection related assistance through an integrated approach to address priority needs of targeted communities, women, men, girls and boys in hard-to-reach and priority geographical locations. The project will mainstream durable solutions with enhanced protection assessments and monitoring to inform protection planning and overall humanitarian response.
The project Protection teams will respond to emergencies in hard to reach locations with high needs of General Protection as it would be recommended or advised by the Protection Cluster in coordination with other Sub-clusters to ensure a sustainable outcome of the project indicators: -

1. Needs

The aftermath of the conflict in South Sudan coupled with the deeply rooted natural calamities and the negative cultural norms has caused devastating effect on humanitarian situation which entails acute economic depression, increased numbers of people on the move (including IDPs), broken down social fabrics and protection systems, hence increased vulnerability of children, women and girls to gender based violence, child related abuse and exploitation in States targeted under this project.Some of the contributing factors includes the effect of natural disasters caused by climatic changes such as floods which has also inflicted too much suffering, displacement, threaten lives, survival and acerbated too much suffering on the local population estimated at 5.3 million people who are in need of protection assistance including women, girls, elderly and people living with disability in South Sudan.This happened when convergent of Internally Displaced Persons(IDPs) and refugee in the neighboring countries of Uganda, Kenya, Ethiopia, Sudan and Central Africa etc started spontaneous return to their respective original locations in the country, only to be faced with heighten effect of Sexual and Gender Based Violence on Women and Girls, Maiming due to presence of Un-Exploded Ordinance, Child labor and poor infrastructural development and ownership.The proposed project intervention will support 291,677 beneficiaries most of whom are refugees, returnees, IDPs and the Host Communities in the respective locations of Eastern Equatoria State(Kapoeta East County), Jonglei State(Akobo, Canal/Pigi, Fangak, Uror, Ayod Counties), Unity State(Mayiendit, Leer, Panyijier Counties) and Upper Nile State(Fashoda, Maban & Manyo Counties).This projects will be tailored to address specific needs of vulnerable children, women and adolescent girls, People with Disabilities and the elderly who are facing violence, exploitation, abuse, neglect and harmful practices due to absence of comprehensive GBV and child protection services, poor housing and legal rights including lack of sensitization on Explosive Ordinance Risk education thus exposing survivors to further harm. Government, international organizations and national organizations have and are trying to promote child protection in humanitarian action, including gender-based violence services, though the problem is still persisting in the communities, this project aims to address these challenges through offering of holistic approaches such as;Promotion of ‘’protective communities’’ to act as a link between humanitarian response and the communities through the promotion of child friendly centers and gender-sensitive environment for child victims and witnesses. This will be done by strengthening local capabilities rather than substituting the primary actors responsible for child protection through regular trainings in-service, practice training and/or induction on effective child protection.The project will focus on community-based socio-economic reintegration for children and women by coordinating with other programs (i.e. education, health etc.) for preventing and protecting children from violence, exploitation, abuse, neglect and harmful practices. The community-based child protection services will include mental and psychosocial support, restoring family links and re-unification services, mine risk education messages, gender based violence (GBV) prevention and responsive services and integration activities for children formerly separated from their families. Promotion of open discussions and developing, implementing of child-sensitive social protection programs to support and protect families at risk, offering of parenting education and support program.Promotion of social norms which promotes child protection and discourages norms that perpetuates gender based violence which will expose adolescent girls and women to violence, abuse, neglect and harmful practices.

2. Project activities

The following project activities will be provided in each of the targeted locations: 1. Strengthening Mobile and static Protection Response Monitoring System(PRMS) and assessment to understand protection trends for advocacy and response. The activity will target community leaders, church leaders, teachers, groups and local formal and informal institutions and authorities through the use of available tools such as FGD, KII and Direct observations.2. Psychosocial Support and capacity strengthening services. Survivor Aid will provide recreational services, lifesaving skills sessions and livelihoods services in mean of income generating activities (IGA) to the most Vulnerable women, girls, men and boys targeted by the project3. Protection awareness raising and prevention sessions.Awareness creation will be carry out using IEC materials, community mobilizations and focus group discussions to be conducted monthly to address the protection concerns collected during the protection assessment and protection monitoring. 4. Establishment of Peacebuilding and peaceful co-existence CommitteesSA will provide capacity building trainings to local leaders to strengthen the local norms that promote peace and advocate for the right of vulnerable groups such as women, girls, disables and children. The community will be sensitized on pervasive cultural practices and integrate policies that harmonize and mitigate protection risks.5. Prevent and mitigate protection risks through enhanced preparedness and resilience with engagement of community groups and other stakeholders. 6. Community-based dispute resolution mechanisms created and relevant stakeholders trained on HLP and ADR as well. 7. HLP awareness raising and outreach activities engaging and empowering community groups and liaison with government authorities. 8. Provide Comprehensive Legal support / counselling/ assistance / case management. Survivor Aid will provide assistance to individuals affected by conflict, flooding, injustices to claim their rights, provide psychosocial support and referral to GBV survivors and Child Survivors andSurvivors of trafficking.9. Information Dissemination and Awareness raising on rights, entitlements, assistance opportunities on GBV issues, Child protection issues, HLP rights, available services, Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (SEA), Peacebuilding issues, etc.10. Strengthening community-based protection structures: To established women and youth committees, Community Protection Committees, community-based protection networks, Community based child protection structures and networks.11. Capacity building of staff, partners, and local authorities and leaders on GBV core messages, CPiE, Peacebuilding, ADR, HLP, according to available IASC standards, PSEA Cluster SOPs, tools, among others.Evidence-based programming and advocacy across the response will be supported through regular protection monitoring and protection assessments based on pre-identified triggers, with mechanisms to ensure inclusion of persons with specific needs such as persons living with disabilities, older people, young men at risk, adolescent girls at risk, and female headed households.Through this project, Survivor Aid will further provide prevention and response interventions through participatory approach and training of community mobilizers, caseworkers and leaders to support in protection and HLP related activities and in coordinating project implementation on relevant protection concerns.
