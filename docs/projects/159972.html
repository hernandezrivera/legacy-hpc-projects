Project Summary

The project is intended to provide immediate household access to food and safety nets, and will increase purchasing power to the most vulnerable populations in emergency throughout the year and to people in crisis during the livelihood lean season by providing monthly food vouchers and unconditional cash relief/transfers and to improve food access to school going children in vulnerable communities by providing them with daily meals to prevent further food insecurity and malnutrition
Secondly, the project is intended to protect and restore community productive assets through provision of livelihoods inputs like farm inputs, temporary job creation and improved storage capacity of food and seeds for farmers with specific care of Women headed households and other vulnerable groups.
Furthermore, the project is expected to Improve resilience through development of alternative livelihood strategies for vulnerable population, agricultural training, business management training as well as through preparing the population against shocks like drought impacts and floods through infrastructure development.
By the end of the project, approximately, 3, 421 households ( 20,526 vulnerable people) in Awdal and Waqooyi Galbeed regions will have access to food and safety nets, and will increase their purchasing power throughout the project period. Furthermore, the project will facilitate food access to 2,000 HHs (600 HHs receiving food vouchers for 2 months, while 1,400 HHs receiving different seeds for two seasons). Also the project will improve purchasing power of 1,421 HHs through Cash relief worth (800 HHs for 2 months, 400 HHs for 3 months, 100 HHs will get support of 4 hours tillage and 121 HHs will be received food storage training for 2 seasonal).
Cash is selected because of providing purchasing power to the needy beneficiaries. This support is also empowering needy beneficiaries to repay loans they committed during loss of their livestock. It is also contributing to the local economic development of the communities. The cash can reduce prices of basic needs in local markets for the community and reduces local currency inflation.
The market will support the intervention since there is a functional market in the targeted villages. ASAL will use any market assessment and analysis made by humanitarian actors like WFP, OCHA, FSNAU and any other agency. The program will rely on the finding to decide on the transfer modality. Cash and voucher will be given priority where it’s feasible to avoid challenges related to the food commodity pipeline break and community preferences.

Project Activities

Output 1.1: Facilitated food access to 1800hh for 6 months for communities in Waqooyi galbeed region. Activity 1.1: Provision of food vouchers (Maize, Sugar, Oil and pulses) to 1800hh ( 10800 beneficiaries) (806 men headed households and 994 women headed households), for 6 months for communities in waqooyi galbeed and Awdal regions. Output 1.2. Increased minimum household basket for 800hh(352 men headed households and 448 women households) who are extremely vulnerable through cash relief worth 120 USD for 4 Months per year. Activity 1.2.1: Provision of cash relief worth 120 USD per month for 4 months to 800 hh (352 men headed households and 448 women headed households) in communities in crisis focusing more on the most vulnerable individuals both men and women Output 1.3: Reduced malnutrition rates among the 400 school going children through seasonal provision of daily meals in the schools. Activity 1.3.: Provision of daily meals at school (CSB in the morning hours and rice at noon) to 400 school going children for 6 months in the year. Output 2.1: Enhanced food production and assets for 3370 households for 1 year. Provision of vocational training to 45 (20 young men and 25 young women) individuals (unemployed youth in urban area) and provide them with materials to start up a business of their own.
