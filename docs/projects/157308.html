Project Summary

The objective of this project is to restore and sustaining food and livelihood security of the vulnerable population affected by protracted displacements and crisis. The project will target in Abo karinka, EDein, Assalya and Yassen in East Darfur, Alabassya, Kadugli, Rashad and Abu Jebaha in South Kordofan,and the total of in Nayala, Kass, Gereida and Marshang in South Darfur. The activities proposed under this Plan are in line with Outcome 2 aims to enhance the self-reliance and resilience of the most vulnerable IDPs and communities to cope with current and future shocks and stressed and will address access to different food variety of food sources that will have impact on both food insecurity and dietary uptake to assist in the prevention of the prevalence of malnutrition among the different members of the communities, especially children under five, pregnant and lactating women, through the distribution of agricultural inputs such as staple crops, vegetable seeds for backyard gardening enabling families suffering from malnutrition to increase and diversify their food intake, both of which play a role in the prevention of malnutrition, whilst not requiring access to land or significant water availability. Reduction of health hazards through the vaccination and treatment of animals to reduce the transmission of diseases.

Needs

Food Security and livelihoods situations in Sudan has been affected by different factors that trigger acute food insecurity including drought and natural factors such as dry spells, floods coupled with shortage and high agricultural input prices particularly seeds and fuel lead to decrease production of food and cash crops, shortage of feed and water for livestock, flooding that cause damage of cultivated areas and loss of livelihoods assists as well as negative impact on food security in terms of availability and access especially among IDPs and vulnerable host communities.South Kordofan State has been experiencing an influx of IDPs from SPLA/M controlled areas during 2017 and 2018. According to Humanitarian Need Over view (HNO) 2019, are currently displaced within South Kordofan. The population movements within & outside the states is a constant, which has led to an exacerbated humanitarian situation. The lack of an adequate reception mechanism critically affects South Kordofan, as IDPs are mixed within the host community with no formal recognition. The main consequences of this conflict include disruption of basic services, exposure of women, girls, men and boys to further insecurity and violence, disruption of economic and agricultural activities added additional burden placed on host families. South Kordofan Interagency Mission Report Feb 2019, shows that the Inflation and currency devaluation hast resulted in price hike affecting most vulnerable groups purchasing capacity particularly staple foods and basic necessities rendering them unable to meet minimum food requirements and persistent malnutrition. IPC projections for May – July 2018 estimates population under crisis and emergency (IPC 3 & 4) to increase by 14% reaching a total of 6 million including 164,281people across South Kordofan state localities.The needs assessments that carried out in South and East Darfur reflect need to continue assistance to the protracted IDPs in the camps, returnees and host communities in rural areas. Besides basic services (WASH, Health and Education), the target population need assistance to improve food security and restore their livelihoods and develop relative self-reliance and build resilience to both man-made and natural disasters that continue affecting the people. The living conditions in the IDP camps are characterized by overcrowding. Many of the households in the IDP camps lost their livelihoods assets as they escaped from the conflict in their original villages to settle in the IDP camps. As a result most of the IDPs have limited livelihoods options leading to inadequate access to and consumption of quality food stuffs. Displacements disrupted farming which is the main source of livelihoods leading to poor harvests. Women and girls are particularly vulnerable as they often do not have enough skills required for employment. Activities

To provide staple crops and vegetable seeds (standard seeds package – core pipeline) and agriculture tools (standard hand tools package consisting of 1 shovel, 1 rake, 1 planting/weeding hoe and a donkey cart) to 20,000 seriously affected among IDPs and vulnerable communities in the targeted states representing total will directly benefit from the activity (1,300 men, 2700 women 5,700 boys and 10,300 girls) To provide restocking assistance with small ruminants to 1,500 most vulnerable households with special focus on women headed households, to improve accessibility to milk special children to improve the nutritional status of the targeted beneficiaries. In total 8,400 will directly benefit from the activity (450 men, 1050 women 2,700 boys and 4,200 girls)To provide training of the targeted beneficiaries on improved agricultural practices and technologies on soil and water management, designed to maximize productive capacity of the available land and water resources, pest management/control, minimization of the post-harvest and storage losses in total 300 will directly benefit from the activity (150 men, 150women).Provide Free of charge vaccination for 90,000 livestock against endemic and epidemic disease in targeted localities.Provision of innovative vegetable and fruits seeds to 1,500 HHs (women and men) in selected villages of South Kordofan state and 1500 multiple purposes trees seedling in in total 4,800 will directly benefit from the activity (450 men, 1,050women 2700 boys and 4,200 girls).To provide Training to 1000 women beneficiaries on food processing, handcrafts, IGAs and small business and marketing skills and support with kits and assets in total 6,000 will directly benefit from the activity (700 men, 1000 women 1,500 boys and 2,800 girls).
