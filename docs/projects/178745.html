Project Summary

This project aims to contribute to the improved resilience of conflict-affected populations in Northwest Syria by enhancing access to livelihood opportunities and basic services for women and men in Aleppo and Idleb governorates. IYD will achieve this objective by the implementation of the following integrated activities :
IYD will target 30 camps located in Aleppo and Idleb governorates to rehabilitate the main roads between the targeted camps and surrounding communities so that we can prevent the flood and ensure good accessibility to the targeted camps. The graveling process will cover 20,000 M3 between the targeted camps. Based on IYD technical team, the provided thickness of graveling will be 0.3 M.
Likewise, IYD will rehabilitate 5,000 ML of the damaged sewage systems in the targeted urban communities. It is worth mentioning that rehabilitation of the sewage systems will prevent the emergence of swamps where the swamps can be considered as a place for propagating insects and consequently transmit diseases to humans.
To maximize the benefit from the rehabilitation activities between the camps, IYD will provide 200 short-term work opportunities to participate in all rehabilitation activities. IYD will ensure equal access to these opportunities by all gender groups and PWDs. This will be clearly communicated to communities through the project Ads, FGDs, and all other channels.
Furthermore, with the chronic lack of livelihoods and potential impact of COVID-19, the project activities will scale up emergency livelihood support focused on capacity building of the targeted people, restore local manufacturing, mitigate the impact of COVID-19, and enhance food security in NWS by providing vocational training to 900 individuals ( including PWDs) in Aleppo and Idleb governorates. IYD will conduct a vocations assessment to select the most 15 preferable vocations by population. The VT activity will be divided into three rounds, and each round will last for one month by targeting 300 BNFs. By the end of each training, the capacity of the targeted BNFs will be improved so that the trained vocations will help the BNFs to enhance their income. Here we should mention that allowance will be delivered by the end of each training. It is good to note that training centers will be equipped with all necessities which ensure safe and accessible participation of PWDs. For example, entrance has a ramp , PWD toilets, etc.
Following the vocational training activity, IYD will provide business support to 300 new businesses from the best-trained BNFs ( including PWDs) to initiate their new jobs which will lead to protect livelihood sources and restore the workforce. IYD will provide added value in implementing the vocational training activities through its technical guidance and expertise provided by the IYD ERL team, ensuring that the markets will not be negatively impacted, the sustainability of the business support, and comprehensive monitoring of activities, and risk management.
The fund for this project has not been secured yet.

4 - Needs

IYD conducted a comprehensive needs assessment in the governorates of Aleppo and Idlib . the results related to early recovery can be summarized by : - The majority of assessed communities have an unemployment rate of (51-75%). The following trades (Grain, real estate, seeds and building materials) are available in the assessed communities.- Based on the interviewed KIIs, there is a need for training on MS office, data science, photography, and android applications.- The worker's daily average wage is (15 TL), and the average number of working hours is 7.- The most needed support is providing vocational training and providing cash for work activities.- There are local trainers to provide the (theoretical, practical) training with an average wage (80 TL), while to establish a new business, (7,500 TL) is needed as capital.- The preferred professions for females are sewing and food industry, and the preferred ones for males are solar energy, electrical equipment’s and beekeeping- Most assessed communities and camps have dirt roads.- The majority of targeted camps have been affected by the last flood.- Gravelling the roads inside the camps and constructing drainage systems can effectively alleviate the flood incidents' occurrence every year in the assessed camps- The access of the surveyed camps’ inhabitants to the surrounding communities is quite difficult during winter because of the heavy rain and floods, and gravling the roads linking the camps to the nearby communities is an effective solution.

5 - Implementation of activities

As soon as the project fund is secured, IYD, will coordinate with local authorities and leaders, announce the project, and start the activities. 1- Graveling activities: IYD will gravel 20,000 M3 of the main roads between the targeted camps and surrounding communities in Aleppo and Idleb governorates. Based on IYD technical team, the provided thickness of graveling will be 0.3 M. 2- Rehabilitating the sewage systems: IYD will rehabilitate 5,000 ML of the damaged sewage systems in the targeted communities. Here we should mention that 200 CFW workers ( including PWDs) will participate in the implementation of the above two activities, and these workers will be selected from the same targeted camps to have a comprehensive response for the targeted population. 3- Vocational training activities: To mitigate the impact of COVID-19, and enhance food security, IYD will conduct a vocations assessment to select the most 15 preferable vocations by population. Later providing vocational training for 900 individuals. The VT activity will be divided into three rounds, and each round will last for one month by targeting 300 BNFs. 4- Business activity: IYD will provide business support to 300 new businesses from the best-trained BNFs to initiate their new jobs which will lead to protect livelihood sources and restore the workforce.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk 1: Potential discrimination against beneficiaries of PWDs because the staff are not aware of the specific protection risks which persons with disabilities face while implementing the field activities. Mitigation:• IYD will assess staff's current attitudes and knowledge and provide training on disability inclusion measures• IYD will Integrate issues related to persons with disabilities in all existing training topics, e.g. GBV against women and girls with disabilities, Gender and Protection mainstreaming .etcRisk 2: The project activities may cause harm for some women, girls, and PWDs, as well as other vulnerable groups during their participation in these activities either through a cause of action or failing to take necessary actionsMitigation:- Analyze programs from different perspectives will be conducted by asking the local community for feedback on what is not going well and ways to improve it, in addition, a constant assessment will be taken if project activities are having negative consequences on the beneficiaries and may threaten their safety and dignity- Also, IYD will establish robust feedback and complaint mechanisms. Such as:• Customize a text messaging hotline and secure feedback box• Conduct face-to-face interviews with beneficiaries• Organize community meetings to discuss the beneficiary list and gather feedback.• Conduct FGD with beneficiaries, including vulnerable groups (women, PWD) to give them the opportunities to participate and thus, help them protect themselves and claim their rightsRisk 3: SEA by project staff against female beneficiariesMitigation: All staff will sign the Code of Conduct. In addition, to provide training on PSEA to all staff and establish complaint mechanisms
