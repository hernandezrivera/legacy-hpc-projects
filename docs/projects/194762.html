Project Summary

The project aims to minimize the impacts of humanitarian emergencies in WASH sector in targeted localities through improve access to quality WASH services to people in need specifically vulnerable one like PWD, IDPs, Returnees and Host communities in ,Khartoum ,Giezera,White Nile ,North Kurdufan, South Kurdufan, North Darfur and Kassala. Life-saving interventions will be scaled up in the immediate term, water trucking in areas where supply is disrupted including health facilities and the establishment of distribution points, including in gathering and transit points. Essential WASH supplies and NFI emergency sanitation and handwashing facilities will be provided in hotspot locations. Hygiene promotion and household water treatment practices will be promoted as an immediate alternative, especially for those reliant on surface/river water.
This project is contributing to all three sector objectives for crisis-affected population to access basic water services, adequate, protected, and gender-sensitive sanitation services, and to hygiene promotion and other environmental health interventions.
The project interventions are linked to STRATEGIC OBJECTIVES 1: Provide timely multi-sectoral life-saving assistance to crisis affected people to reduce mortality and morbidity and STRATEGIC OBJECTIVES 2: Improve vulnerable people’s access to livelihoods and life-sustaining basic services.
The selected activities aim at improve the access of crisis-affected population to gender and climate change-sensitive basic water services, adequate provision of gender-sensitive sanitation services and to ensure that affected people have access to gender-sensitive Hygiene4 services, risk communication and community engagement (RCCE), menstrual hygiene management (MHM), vector control, and waste management.
The project will be strengthening provision of quality primary quality WASH services across 7 states, in Kassala state , North Darfur North Kurdufan , South Kurdufan ,Khartoum,Gazira states,
Plan will integrate water, sanitation and hygiene interventions in this project, and as well as with education, nutrition and health activities to contribute to reducing malnutrition and epidemics.
The project team will also ensure coordination with other interventions. The project will deliver these activities through community services adopting the sector objectives and approaches, besides integrating some of the WASH interventions into health and nutrition centers to ensure the minimum IPC is applied.

1 - Needs

The MSNA 2022 results indicate that 77 per cent of the population have access to improved water services without factoring the collection time. About 28 per cent of water sources do not have sufficient water, and 25 per cent of water sources are not functioning. Five fold increases in fuel prices has made its operation and maintenance unaffordable . Sanitation coverage in Sudan continues to be amongst the worst in the Middle East and North Africa (MENA) Region. Over 30 per cent of the population has access to basic sanitation with a 33.3 per cent prevalence of open defecation practices which is especially concerning for women and girls. Hand washing with soap and water (as the main indicator for hygienic practices) is estimated at 14.3 per cent.After the armed conflict started in Khartoum WASH needs have increased significantly in urban centres, which were very thinly covered under the 2023 HRP initially. The bulk water treatment and supply systems that can reach a million people are not functional in Khartoum and other conflict-affected states. Existing displaced persons, including the most vulnerable residents, and an increased number of displaced people in new locations, including international borders, are a matter of significant concern.The already limited water sources are under extreme pressure due to increased population concentration in new IDP concentration points, which carries inter-communal tensions and conflict risks; skyrocketing cost of fuel has resulted in a 300 to 400 per cent increase in the operation of fuel-reliant water supply systems as well water trucking. The rainy season will further heighten the risk of outbreaks in the coming months In most of the targeted localities in this project among Plan programme area (Kassala, North Darfur, North Kurdufan, & South Kurdufan) people with limited access to clean water, either this access is physically in terms of distance, time or difficulty to have water or in term of consumption of untreated polluted water are at high risk of water related diseases. Likewise, they don’t have access or limited access to appropriate sanitation activities specially who practicing open defecation and not practicing hand washing with soap area risk of disease at aspect of an outbreak. Humanitarian situation in Darfur is still fragile for all population; frequent tribal conflict is resulting in displacement of villagers from their origin site to towns nearby looking for safety, being displaced is negatively affecting whole live aspect of the fled persons, more over the designated public services (water,Education, Market….) all will be pressured and that lead to deterioration and bad quality of provided services and that will impacted in poor health status for individuals leading to general outbreaks. All these negatively affecting socio-economic status jeopardize the resilience of population. Plan International Sudan, Kordofan Program area has recently completed a comprehensive services mapping and communities’ access to services survey in North Kordofan State, which has provided a reliable data on the current services situation, and the services gaps per sector per each locality. This survey has identified the reality on the ground, and based on the studies Plan International Sudan is proposing integrated intervention to reduce the impact of drought on the lives and stability of peoples in North Kordofan state, which is located between latitude 11.150 and 16.450Nand longitude 27.50 and 32.150 E. It is one of middle state of Sudan and it share boundaries with Northern state, While Nile state, North Darfur, Khartoum state, South Kordofan and West Kordofan.

2 - Activities/Outputs

This project aims at contributing to all three sector objectives for crisis-affected population, Sector Objective I: 4.5 million crisis-affected population have access to gender and climate change-sensitive basic water services2 &sector objective, Sector Objective II: 1.66 million crisis-affected populations have access to adequate gender-sensitive sanitation services, Sector Objective II: 1.66 million crisis-affected populations have access to adequate gender-sensitive sanitation services. The project has been designed and revised to fit within the WASH sector strategy, scope and planned budget for 2023. The project will support the following WASH activities in communities,: Health Facilities and in schools , Construction, rehabilitation/ upgrading of water facilities - Water quality and quantity monitoring - Support operation and maintenance of water systems - Form and train community WASH committees - Construct gender segregated and child friendly communal latrine - Rehabilitate and maintain existing school, communal, health facility latrines - Provide child friendly hand-washing facilities for new and rehabilitated - Conduct hygiene promotion campaigns and provide WASH kits - Support adolescent girls and women in adequate Menstrual Hygiene management, including distribution of hygiene kits - Conduct vector control - Support via hygiene messages and kits to OTP and stabilisation centres, and for households with children having acute malnutrition. The WASH intervention will increase the populations vulnerable to drought access to basic water services, hygiene, and sanitation in key institutions. This will be accomplished through the rehabilitation of hafirs, handpumps, water yards, and sanitation facilities as well as hygiene promotion. It will also ensure that beneficiaries are able to report any issues or concerns through the expansion of Plan's complaints, feedback, and response, mechanism to the target localities.The activities in details include : Water supply by water trucking, Operate and maintain old and new water yards (includes water quality treatment and monitoring, Construction of institutional (Health/nutrition/schools) centres latrines, Rehabilitate emergency household latrines, Rehabilitate communal latrines (in camps, schools, health facilities, markets, Operation and Maintenance of existing latrines (communal), Provision of soap & Jerry can, Construction of hand washing facilities in communal set ups (communal toilets in camps, schools, markets, public places and health /nutrition facilities hand washing mobile facility/ household, Provision of Menstrual Hygiene Materials, Provision of Hygiene Kit (Family) this is also called dignity kit, household hygiene messaging includes HH of severely acute malnutrition(SAM) children, hygiene campaigns in communal set ups (Include Nutrition and Health facilities, schools, or other communal setups ), vector control (equipment’s, chemicals, labour), Provision of cleaning tools, PPE, disinfection and operational support for environmental cleaning at health/nutrition care facilities , Build capacities, provide training, to enhance WASH of affected communities (state/locality level - covers cross cutting health, nutrition, education operations).

This project intends to ensure equal and safe participation of target beneficiaries,particularly girls and women, who are most at risk of being exposed to protectionviolations.
