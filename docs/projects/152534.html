Project Summary

The aim of this response plan will be increase access, scale-up response and also strengthen the capacities of primary health care to provide emergency lifesaving health care to 136,006 vulnerable IDPs and host populations in Pibor; Twic East; and Bor South in Jonglei, Yirol East; and Yirol West in Lakes, Kapoeta East; and Magwi in Eastern Equatoria, and Juba in Central Equatoria States.
The LiveWell’s HRP budget for 2019 at an overall US$ 8,314,666 will aim at supporting the following 13 health facilities based on the following level.
• 7 Mobile Clinics in Pibor, Magwi, Kapoeta East, Yirol East, Yirol West, Twic East and Juba specifically supporting IDPs in Mangateen IDPs) to reached 71,861IDPs/host communities.
• 4 PHCUs in Bor South, Pibor, Yirol East and Yirol West to reach33,050 people
• 2 PHCCs in Pibor and Twic East to reach 31,395 people

Needs

• The conflict in South Sudan had continue to have profound effect on the existence and functionality of health facilities to provide lifesaving healthcare to vulnerable IDPs/host communities due to looting , destruction and killing of health workers (SSA 2017/18).• Inadequate provision of lifesaving health care services due to inadequate funding led to inadequate access to improved services such as RH services .for examples RRHP support 15% of the existing health facilities in Pibor, Twic East and Jebel Boma under LiveWell, 18% of HFs are supported by SSHF SA2 in Gumuruk, Lekuangole and Pibor through LiveWell. 67% of Health Facilities are non-functional due to lack of funding (IRNA report, 2016/2017).• Recent IPC report that 25,000 people in Yirol East, Yirol West and Pibor counties may go into catastrophe (IPC 5) if there are no measures to improve post-harvest in October and December. Also, a critical GAM rate (15%-29.9%)level of acute malnutrition in Jonglei, and Lakes States while Central Equatoria state at alert rate. All is attributed to poor access to health care, nutrition and WASH services. It was recommended that a multi-sectoral humanitarian intervention be scaled up to meet the pressing needs and improved access to basic services such as health (IPC Report, September 2018).• Lack of support for sexual reproductive health and other cross-cutting issues like HIV/AIDS, TB, Mental health, disabilities and NCDs by the current donors supporting LiveWell to runs RRHP activities in PHCCs and County hospitals in Boma hospital and Pibor PHCC in Pibor County, Panyagor hospital, Wernyol/Kongor PHCC in Twic East resulting into inadequate skills birth attendants and poor RH services for suvrivors of a rape.• Increased risks of Ebola Virus Disease (EVD) and cholera outbreaks in Magwi County, Rift Valley Fever (RVF) & measles outbreaks in Yirol East, and malaria endemicity in Twic East, Pibor and Bor South requires stringent health and WASH securities in the hotspots or high risks of transmission araes. (WHO EVD report at week 43, October, 2018).• Low level of immunization coverage in Pibor, Twic East, Kapoeta East, Magwi, Yirol East, Yirol West, and Bor South coupled with flooding from heavy rainfall would precipitate measle outbreaks, malaria outbreaks, severe acute malnutrition• LiveWell is therefore proposing in this humanitarian response plans for the year 2019 to set-up  Seven (7) Mobile Team (MTs) packages in Pibor, Magwi, Twic East, Kapoeta East, Yirol East, Yirol West, and Juba Four (4) PHCUs in Yirol East, Yirol West, Pibor, and Bor South Two (2) PHCCs in Pibor and Twic East • LiveWell is therefore seeking your approval to recommend our concept note “Improve access, scale-up response and strengthen the capacities of health facilities to provide emergency lifesaving health care services to the IDPs/host communities in Jonglei, Lakes, Eastern and Central Equatoria States” for Humanitarian Response Plan (HRP) for Health Cluster in the coming Year 2019. LiveWell has it present in this proposed locations. We are the lead health partner in Twic East, and Pibor under RRHP and SSHF SA2 supporting 19 facilities (2 CHs, 5 PHCCs, and 8 PHCUs, 4 Mobile Teams). LiveWell is also implementing Routine Immunization, and Community-based Surveillance to eradicate polio in Pibor, Boma, Bor South, Twic East, Duk, Ayod, Kapoeta East and Magwi County with support BMGF & USAID. We are also implementing SSHF SA2 WASH Project in Pibor and Magwi complemented with Ebola Virus Disease prevention in the boarder of Nimule and Mugali payams.

Activities

LiveWell will implement emergency lifesaving health care services in 13 health facilities to reach a populations of 35,000 IDPs/host communities in Pibor, Twic East, Yirol East, Yirol West, Kapoeta East, Magwi, Juba and Bor South counties within a year period with the following specific objectives and activities underneath in this HRP for 2019. A. IMPROVE ACCESS AND SCALE-UP RESPONSIVENESS  Establish 7 Mobile clinics,  Support 4 Primary Health Care Units Support 2 Primary Health Care Centers B. PREVENT, DETECT AND RESPOND TO EPIDEMIC PRONE DISEASES Support IDSR and EWARs in identified health facilities Preparedness and response to (Malaria/Measles/ cholera/Meningitis and HepE)  Enhanced EVD surveillance at Nimule Boarder  Integrate WASH response at all identified health services C. IMPROVE RESILIENCE AMONG VULNERABLE POPULATIONS Increase demand for uptake of mental Health & Psychosocial services from 3.5% to 10% and for SGBV services by 25% D. INTEGRATED RESPONSE FOR HEALTH/WASH/NUTRITION/ FOOD SECURITY AND PROTECTION Management of SAM with medical complication  WASH in health facilities and outbreak response
