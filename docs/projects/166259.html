Project Summary

The proposed Hope 360 initiative for peace project is aimed at scaling up timely and equitable access of conflict affected children to quality basic formal education through community mobilization, advocacy, sensitization and specialized capacity building and continuous learning for school boys and girls whose learning has been hampered by Covid-19 through. The project is targeting to reach 2100 conflict affected out of school boys and girls and school boys and girls between the age (6-13 years) whose learning has been cut short due to Covid-19 pandemic in Hawul local government area of Borno state for educational empowerment, people living with disability will also be mainstreamed into both target.
The project objectives are
• To increase awareness through advocacy, sensitization on the importance of formal basic education and its continuation with the aid of the media so as to promote good decision and responsibility actions among the target in Hawul LGAs.
• To increase safe and equitable access of the conflict affected out of school children and regular school boys and girls between the age (6-13 years) affected by Covid-19 in Hawul LGA in Borno state to quality education empowerment on sustainable development at all levels.
• To increase and improve the mental, physical and intellectual capacity of the target through various education empowerment capacity building thereby promoting behavioural change and improving the welbeing of targeted children in Hawul LGA.
In achieving this, Hope 360 Initiative with the OCHA HRP grant will be running educational empowerment in Hawul LGA of Borno state using (English, Hausa and Bura) as the major languages for proper understanding aimed at improving knowledge. Hope 360 initiative will adopt the use of advocacy, sensitization as a tool for increasing community awareness for the target in Hawul LGA.

2 - Activities

The following are activities that will be carried out during the proposed project• Back to school campaign through advocacy, sensitization on the importance of education with the aid of media • Training of staffs and facilitators that will be engaging in this project for successful implementation. • Enrollment of the school boys and girls including those with disabilities• Distribution of learning kits to the targets. IMPACT- increased awareness and improve to capacity building and resilience both of out of school children and the new and regular school boys and girls affected by conflict and covid-19 pandemic in Hawul Local Government Area of Borno state through equitable access to quality basic formal and vocational education OUTCOMES • AWARENESS- on the importance of quality formal education for targets during this period covid-19 increased• ACCESS- to equitable quality education for out of school boys and girls increased• CAPACITY BUILDING- both intellectually and mentally and physically of the school children and out of school within this period of covid-19 is increased• BEHAVIOURAL CHANGE- among the vulnerable children, out of school boys and girls is improved MONITORING AND EVALUATION The proposed project periodic monity which will focus on day to day activities of the project. It will be using clearly stated activity indicators in the plans for monthly, quarterly and annual target to be accomplished. Monitoring activities includes, measuring the physical performance of the targets, budget utilization, quality and quantity of work done, correlation of work done with set objectives and the set plans and time tables. The education officer employed to run the center shall conduct day to day monitoring activities of the project and report to the programme coordinator of Hope 360 initiative. The project evaluation will be jointly helped with all stakeholders including donors and community leaders in the project area, beneficiaries and relevant government counterparts. Indicators for evaluation include:• Financial system:- cost effectiveness and budget utilization • Number of staffs and facilitators who are trained through this project.• Number of both vulnerable out of school children and regular school boys and girls mobilized, sensitized on formal education and capacity strengthening and on Covid-19 preventive means.• Number of boys and girls who have access to the sensitization and advocacy.• Number of out of school boys and girls enrolled back to learning center after the sensitization
