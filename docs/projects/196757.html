Project Summary

The project aims to support the most vulnerable families with bread in the northwest of Syria.
Beneficiaries’ names will be identified through registration and verification procedures in coordination with Local councils.
The M&E team will verify the data on the ground. Up to 3215 families will be selected in 27 camps in the blanket modality.
500 MT of imported Turkish flour (4 trucks monthly) will be provided by IHH as a free contribution to the project.
Every week IHH will import one truck of flour containing 25 MT (4 trucks monthly) on average and it will be stored in the bakeries' warehouse.
Every day IHH will bake and produce 7365 good quality bread bundles weight 600 g to be distributed to 3215 H.Hs of the IDPs in 27 camps in the Afrin subdistrict for 26 days a month, up to 5 months. One day at the beginning of each month will be allocated for replacing beneficiaries' bread cards after verifying their ID documents.
Each ton of flour will produce 1915 bread bundles.
The modality of bread distribution will be the blanket modality that will cover all the H.Hs in the targeted camps based on the following distribution criteria:
HH with 1-3 individuals will receive one bread bundle.
HH with 4-6 individuals will receive two bread bundles.
HH with 7-9 individuals will receive three bread bundles.
HH with 10 individuals and more will receive four bread bundles.
Responsibilities: IHH will be responsible for importing 500 tons of wheat flour, transporting, and storing it in IHH warehouse, and contracting bakery(s). IHH will be responsible fully for preparing the beneficiaries' list and distribution of bread to the beneficiaries on daily basis as well as monitoring the distribution process. IHH will do spot-check monitoring for bread distribution centers and bakeries through field visits to ensure all activities are implemented.
Technical specifications of the flour will be appropriate to the Syrian bread as follows:
Gluten: 27
Index: 92 First Sedimentation:
30-Second Delayed Sedimentation:
42 Fn (max):
320 Energy (min):
108 Ash (max): 0.85
Extensibility: 120
Protein: 11.5
**Quantity of the wheat flour to be baked on daily basis is around 3.8 MT.
**We have 26 working days, 100 MT of flour needed per month
**Distribution Period is 5 months. Production of the bread will be contracted to bakeries and the cost will cover the transportation of the flour from the IHH warehouse to the bakeries and the transportation of bread from the bakeries to the distribution points. The baking cost (including transportation) per ton of flour is 140 USD. Project activities will be coordinated closely with local authorities, the FSL cluster, and other humanitarian actors in addition to the local councils. This is to avoid any duplication and to engage stakeholders and beneficiaries in designing, implementing, and evaluating project activities.

3 - Needs

The camps selected by IHH for this project are not supported by any organization depending on the need analysis done by IHH. Depending on the FSL Gap Analysis Oct 2022, the total number of people receiving free bread is 0 in the Afrin subdistrict. Depending on "IMMAP Wheat to Bread factsheets Nov 2022 Page 1": The total population in Afrin Sub-District is 187868 and the PIN is 95540.Afrin subdistrict is one of the most in-need subdistricts in the northwest of Syria.The daily bread needs of the total population are 62 MT and the daily need of PIN per day is 32MT.Access to subsidized bread for PiN is 0.The region relies heavily on imported flour, as the local flour does not sufficient for the daily need of flour in the region, as 76% of the flour used in the region is imported flour.Local production is only sufficient for 24% of the flour used daily. Depending on "NWS WtB Summary Report Q4 December 2022": The weekly bread needs of the population are 434 MT, the weekly bread needs of PiN is 221 MT and the weekly free bread by NGOs is 86 MT.Just 65% of the population's bread needs are covered by total production.Bread production gap per week 151 MT.The number of the supported bakery is 1 out of 15 (unsupported %93).The weekly full bread production capacity is 728 MT, however, the current productivity is %39.

8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

• The project will contribute to alleviating the suffering of 18326 IDPS (3684 men 4232 women 5397 boys 5013 girls) by supplying them with life-saving services by offering bread which is an essential food item.• IHH will take into consideration the norms of the society regarding gender in each step of implementation, where bread will bedistributed in two queues one for men and one for women. MEAL team of IHH will include female staff to alleviate the sensitivity for families screened, to support society customs (where men are not allowed to enter tents of women), and to receive feedback from the women about the services provided.• Female distributor workers will be hired to make distribution more comfortable and easier for females and to prevent any case of exploitation or abuse that may be done by Male staff.• All project documents and data will be disaggregated by age and gender.• Training sessions about GBV and PSEA will be conducted to IHH before the beginning of the project to ensure full compliance with PSEA and GBV guidelines.• All the staff and suppliers will sign on Code of conduct adopted by IHH.• M&E team will monitor the behavior of workers on-site, and make sure they are abiding by the humanitarian principles and code of conduct.• CRM mechanism includes suggestion and complaints boxes, complaints phone numbers, and emails of IHH will be available on bread cards, banners, posters in the distribution centers for raising concerns regarding SEA or GBV


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumption: Distribution will be implemented smoothly.Risk: conflict between covered and uncovered camps that will not receive humanitarian assistance.Mitigation measures: IHH team coordinated with local authorities and local councils to select the targeted camps. IHH will explain the communities selection criteria.Assumption: Fuel price is stable.Risk: Increasing fuel prices due to the Ukrainian-Russian war and deterioration of the global situation. Mitigation measures: sign a contract with the bakery based on stable prices throughout the project duration.Assumption: The security situation is stable in project locations.Risk: The area gets exposed to security or military crisis.Mitigation measures: The area in which IHH will work on the project, is considered secure and no major security issues were encountered. However, we will monitor the situation closely to secure our team and the material planned for distribution. In case of security issues arise, the operation site will be changed after consultation with OCHA and FSL Cluster.Assumption: No more IDPs displaced.Risk: Increasing the waves of displacement.Mitigation measures: Update beneficiary lists on a monthly basis and prepare a waiting list for filling gaps when beneficiaries move out of the area covered.Assumption: High Bread qualityRisk: Low bread quality.Mitigation measures: IHH has dedicated M&E staff at the bakery sites to monitor the bread quality in terms of raw materials and added materials
