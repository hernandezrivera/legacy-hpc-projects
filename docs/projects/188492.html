Project Summary

overall objective:
the project aims at improving the awerness of Awareness of sexual abuse, empowering, and integrating 50child and girl IDPs of the Syrian orphans living with the host community. It also aims at elevating the economic and social situation of those orphans and their families by providing support on different levels including protection and basic services.
Project goals:
1. Empowering and training (50) children between the ages of 9 and 12through recreational activities to develop and discover skills (sports, music, robotics, language, and targeted cinematic shows) within the first year.
2. Raising the awareness of children between the ages 9 to15 years from the same target group about preventing sexual exploitation and abuse by parents or society.
3. Empowering and training adolescent girls within the target group, (50) girls between the age of 13 and 15 on dialogue skills and community participation.
4. Empowerment and training of the same adolescent girls from the target group (50 girls) on preventing sexual exploitation and abuse by parents and society.

4 - Needs

the effects of prolonged social distancing and confinement measures resulted in increased unemployment and fewer livelihood opportunities for vulnerable families, which will make it more difficult for them to meet their basic needs and could likely to lead to increased negative coping strategies, such as child labor and early marriage. There is also an increased risk of gender-based violence and violence against children, with higher levels of stress within households and many women and children potentially confined at home with their abusers for long periods of time. In Particular orphans suffer double trauma, missing one or both parents on the one hand, and the psychological and social consequences related to being IDPs in a city that have many cultural differences and multiple barriers including traditions. During the preparation of action by survey from our previous work in idleb (based on our community feed back and real stories/ HNAP FROM PSEA ) ,According to the statistics of the Psea Network, between the year of 2019 so far , there are 175 cases of sexual abuse of girls and adolescents

10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Our main goal is to raise awareness of the disguised sexual abuse that girls can be exposed to. and how they could deal with this situation And as we are part of the Psia network, we will adopt the materials of the network, develop and train the work team to work in this field in order to achieve better results.
