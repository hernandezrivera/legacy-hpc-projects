Project Summary

Based on the data mentioned in the HNO report, it has been observed that the number of individuals in need of support in food security and agriculture is increasing. The total number of those in need during 2024 in Syria reached 16.7 million, with more than 2.4 million in need within the Idlib Governorate. This highlights the necessity to expand interventions to mitigate the economic deterioration affecting livelihoods, agriculture, and food security. Additionally, there is a need to establish protection services, especially among internally displaced persons in camps and informal settlements.
AHF has conducted a rapid needs assessment in 7 sub-districts in Idleb and has reported the need for strengthening farmers’ livelihood, providing life-saving assistance, and supporting self-reliance of affected IDPs especially those in the camps AHF is designing a 10-months project in FSL two stages and The number of direct beneficiaries is 25,000 :
1) Purchasing farmers' wheat production(600ton) in Maaret Tamsrin.
2) Produce bread and distribute it to the 18 target IDPs camps in Dana sub-district
AHF is already implementing bread distribution in the same camps for two years and currently seeking for new funding to continue providing life-saving assistance to the PiN through sustaining the bread distribution activity. the selected beneficiaries from the target IDPs who will receive food assistance(bread)
Support wheat value chain with purchasing wheat production from 300 farmers (2 tons from each farmer at a price of $350 per ton) in Maaret Tamsrin.
Bread production at AHF existing bakery & distribution to 18,500 individuals (250g per person of daily bread for 117 days) in the 18 target camps in Dana.
Cash-for-work to 28 individuals for 2 months to support wheat value chain at stage 1.
Cash-for-work to 31 individuals for 5 months through baking and distribution activities at stage 2.
During the year before last, the project has been fully implemented, and currently we have the same plan, and we do not have secure financing
