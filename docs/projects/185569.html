Project Summary

In the crisis affected south west region of Cameroon especially in Fako Division, “It is estimated that Majority of single mothers who are berely surviving and facing hardship as a result of the crisis, receive no and sometime very insignificant financial support,” (PAID-WA 2021). School drop outs in this region also face the same problem. According to PAID-WA: February 2021 assessment report, “47% of students from rural communities in fako division, quit school before going to high school and University for several socio-economic reasons ranging from teenage pregnancy to lack of money for school fees. For all these 47 (in every100) without a decent education or completing school, their prospects of finding a job are bleak. Statistics shows that one in two school drop outs - and two out of three single women - are jobless. The unemployment rate among all 15- to 24-year-olds youth is 60% in rural community in Fako division (PAID-WA 2021),
This project seeks to improve on the welfare of single mothers and school drop outs youth (12-18 years) by enhancing their employability skills through sustainable livelihood pathways and provision of local based revolving startup capital in vulnerable communities in Fako Division in Cameroon. The Pan African Institute for Development West Africa (PAID-WA), Buea, seeks to achieve this through:
a. Capacity building of 200 single mothers and 300 school dropout youths (150 boys and 150 girls, aged 12 to 17) in vocational and entrepreneurial skills for income generation through distance learning mode and field base mode in vulnerable communities in Fako Division;
b. Mentorship and incubation of 500 school drop out with business plan by experts;
c. Provision of Startup capital to single mother and 300 drop out youths(150 boys and 150 girls) through a revolving micro credit schemes

1 - Besoins / Needs

In the crisis affected south west region of Cameroon especially in Fako Division, “It is estimated that Majority of single mothers who are barely surviving and facing hardship as a result of the crisis, receive no and sometime very insignificant financial support,” (PAID-WA 2021) Trends indicate the strong needs for women especially single mothers to take more responsibility for their financial situation. This leaves them financially more vulnerable and generally worse off. As if that is not enough, they have to struggle for gender equity on one hand and for their rights as women on the other. School drop outs in this region also face the same problem. According to an assessment conducted by the pan African Institute for Development, West Africa, (PAID-WA) in February 2021, “47% of students from rural communities in fako division, quit school before going to high school and University or completing school for several socio-economic reasons, ranging from teenage pregnancy to lack of money for school fees. For all these 47 (in every100) without a decent education/employability skills their prospects of finding a job are bleak. Statistics shows that one in two school drop outs - and two out of three single mother - are jobless. The unemployment rate among all 15- to 24-year-olds youth is 60% in rural community in Fako division (PAID-WA 2021). Owing to this, the need to developed the employability skills of single mothers and school dropout youths in Fako Division through vocational Education to build their resilience and provides them with stability is very vitals.
