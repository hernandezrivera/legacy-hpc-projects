Project Summary

Catholic Relief Services (CRS) proposes an emergency assistance program in Mozambique’s Cabo Delgado province. The Proteger e Reconstruir II (Proteger II) project aims to respond to critical shelter needs among conflict-affected internally displaced persons (IDPs) and host community households supporting IDPs with temporary housing. Proteger II will impart participants with knowledge of resilient shelter techniques and provide household items to improve overall living conditions and restore dignity to conflict-affected communities. The project will target specific resettlement sites and host communities in Montepuez district. Proteger II will build on the achievements and lessons learned from 15 months of similar program implementation under the initial Bureau for Humanitarian Assistance (BHA)-supported Proteger e Reconstruir (Proteger I) project which also targeted conflict-affected IDPs in Cabo Delgado with emergency shelter assistance. The objective of the program is to improve the living conditions and restore the dignity of displaced people and host communities in the province of Cabo Delgado. CRS, will assist a total of 7,440 participating direct households, in a total of 37,200 individuals. Among those targeted for support, 20,835 will be internally displaced (4,167 households).

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Regarding project design, CRS will ensure that the Shelter Kit content is tailor-made to mitigate protection risks and takes intoconsideration of the needs of vulnerable groups.Greater protection concerns will be mainstreamed into the multisectoral teams (in addition to having protection representation within the CRS structure), who conduct referrals and services specifically targeted for at the most vulnerable persons. CRS is an active member of the Protection cluster. CRS will conduct joint shelter-protection assessment visits including household surveys that focus on vulnerability criteria and other protection aspects to ensure the integration of these aspects into the design of the program activities. Assessment teams and participants will be notified of the CRS Code of Conduct. Where needed CRS will also establish referral, feedback and complaint mechanisms to complement activities under this project.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

Communities will be engaged in on-the-job training exercises withthe intention is to give guidance on the use of shelter materialsand improved construction techniques. A community-based participatory approach is essential to create meaningfullyinvolvement by displaced residents in protection and assistanceactivities. The community’s leadership, but also other representatives of men, women, youth, elderly, and persons with disabilities will be included in the design of programming and assistance activities.CRS will also set up community complaint desks to ensure anonymous feedback can be received from the affected populations. CRS teams will conduct weekly visits to obtain the complaints and feedback and take action where needed. CRS also closely works with the Linha Verde 1458 complaints hotline to provide regular information and feedback. In sites where a CCCM partner is present, CRS will also closely engagement in their complaints and feedback mechanisms and other community engagement initiatives to ensure relevant feedback for programme implementation can be taken into considerations.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Proteger II will continue to adapt training and social-technical assistance to the education levels and learning styles of participants. People with disabilities will be targeted and assigned a higher score while selecting the most vulnerable houses. In such instances, if with no labour support is available , e technical and labour support for the construction of their shelters will be provided. Under this project CRS aims to target at least 20% of the population with disabilities.Shelter trainings will beconducted in a visual manner to account for low-literacy level community members, some of whom cross other vulnerability criteria as well. To ensure approaches continue to adapt to the needs of populations, the baseline survey will collect information on gender dynamics, resource access, and household decision-making to inform interventions. During registration, CRS will use the Washington Group Short Set of Questions to identify people with disabilities. Project monitoring will include quarterly, sex-disaggregated focus group discussions (FGDs) to serve as a check on the inclusion of vulnerable individuals (where feasible), and all output and quantitative data will be sex and age disaggregated. Where FGDs are not feasible, due to COVID-19 risk mitigation strategies, data will be collected remotely through the KoBo Collect platform and secure messaging services and surveys will be done by project staff keeping two meters away from project participants in small groups adhering to 2 meters of distance and government regulations regarding the number of people in one gathering.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

CRS will seek strict adherence to the Sphere Standards forHumanitarian Assistance which identifies the environment as across-cutting issue to be incorporated to all humanitarianassistance. In that sense, CRS will encourage the use ofassessment tools to assess the environmental impact of theconstruction related program activities. This tools focus from theselection of the shelter sites, the construction of structuresreviewing the possible negative environmental impacts, themanagement considerations while occupied and also considersthe possible decommissioning or dismantling of the structuresto reutilize the resources. The use of such tools will engage localactors and communities to understand the different conditionsparticular to the working areas.
