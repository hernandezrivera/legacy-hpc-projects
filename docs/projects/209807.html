Project Summary

Syria continues to be one of the most complex protracted conflict humanitarian crises globally, exposed to high levels of ongoing conflict, and prone to natural disasters and epidemic outbreaks. These factors have forced an increase of 1.4M of people in need (PIN) since 2023, totaling 16.7M (HNO, 2024) people in need in Syria. Given the myriad needs and vulnerabilities, Concern proposed this action across Northwest Syria (NWS). Concern has proposed intervention areas in line with the severity of the needs identified by Humanitarian Needs Overview where there continues to be lack of, or challenges for NGOs to provide assistance in Afrin and Azaz Districts. Concern will work with local partners to comprehensively address gaps in the response, ensuring the inclusion of diverse beneficiaries, including girls, children with disabilities, and marginalized communities.
The intervention will involve implementing shelter rehabilitation activities, such as repairing walls and cracks or installing windows, doors, lighting, water, and sanitation, to improve the living conditions of the affected families. The project aims to reach the most vulnerable families in the crises affected area, with an average of 5 persons per family who are currently living in unsafe houses and unsanitary conditions. To ensure that the intervention is implemented effectively, Concern and Implementing Partners will work closely with the affected communities and local authorities, conducting assessments to identify the most urgent needs and working with the community to prioritize assistance. The project will also involve providing technical training to the local community, enabling them to participate in the implementation process and build their capacity to maintain the rehabilitated shelters in the future. Furthermore, the shelter rehabilitation activities will be conducted using environmentally friendly and sustainable methods, reducing environmental impact and promoting sustainable practices. This approach will help intervention ensure that the intervention’s benefits are long-lasting and contribute to the sustainable development of the affected communities. Overall, the proposed project will provide immediate and vital assistance to vulnerable families affected by the crisis, improving their living conditions and providing a safer and more secure environment. By working with the affected communities and promoting sustainable practices, the project will also contribute to the long-term development and resilience of the region.
During all project phases, Concern and implementing Partners will consider the AAP measures, the mainstreaming of protection, and PSEA. The project has a risk analysis and mitigation matrix. Along with all activities, monitoring, and evaluation processes will be carried out. At the end of the project, a meeting will be made for the project staff and key stakeholders to evaluate the project results and register the lessons learned and the best practices that have been followed to achieve project results.
So far, Concern was able to secure 50% of the proposed budget covering 100 HHs in Afrin district, these families were affected by the earthquake.
