Project Summary

The project aims at decreasing the high morbidity and mortality rate across Sudan by providing life-saving access to INR consultations and specialised cardiac consultations and surgeries in response to the effect of the Rheumatic Heart Disease (RHD), to decrease the prevalence of chronic cardiac disease across the Country. EMERGENCY Salam Centre for Cardiac Surgery in Khartoum has remained open since the onset of the conflict in Sudan and the closure of it's services may leave thousand of patients without life-saving therapy and treatment. 3,500 cardiological examinations will be provided and 500 admissions will be granted (including cardiosurgery). In light of the above-described scenario, in Khartoum the situation is becoming increasingly difficult.
The ongoing fighting, lasting for over 9 months, has severely damaged most of the city's
infrastructure. As a result, the majority of hospitals are shuttered, due to either physical damage or
an inability to offer assistance owing to the scarcity of medications and essential supplies. In the dire
humanitarian context highlighted, it is of utmost importance to ensure the continuity of high-quality
health services for cardiac patients.
The rheumatic heart disease, endemic in Sudan, is one of the leading causes of mortality among
young people in developing countries. This disease inflicts severe damage to the heart valves,
compromising their proper functioning and leading to the progressive deterioration of the patients'
quality of life, ultimately resulting in death. This issue significantly affects regions in Africa, Southeast
Asia, and the Western Pacific.
Unfortunately, few healthcare facilities have the capacity to provide adequate prevention and
treatment for this infection, making rheumatic fever the primary cause of cardiovascular diseases in
children and adolescents, especially in the age group between 5 and 15 years, in Africa. The root
cause of this critical issue lies predominantly in the limited access to care, especially for children,
stemming from the absence of free services, inadequate healthcare facilities, and a shortage of
qualified medical personnel. In this situation, it is crucial to emphasize the significance of the Salam Centre and its uniqueness.
The Salam Centre for Cardiac Surgery is the only facility in Sudan (and in the Region) that provides
comprehensive cardiac services completely free of charge. Furthermore, for cardiac patients
undergoing surgery at the Salam Centre, receiving the necessary medications, therapies, and post-operative examinations is a matter of life or death. It is essential that the Salam Centre can continue
its operations as planned, as it represents an essential resource in addressing this serious healthcare
issue
