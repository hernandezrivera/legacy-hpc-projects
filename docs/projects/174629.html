Project Summary

COHESODEC within the framework of its emergency response efforts seeks to improve on the deplorable health conditions of I.D.P's and host communities in the North-West Region in 2021. Our interventions in the health sector as usual are very vital and of utmost importance in ensuring that the health conditions of I.D.P's badly affected by the ongoing conflict and its aimed at significantly improving upon the lives of affected populations and host communities.
Our major target in this response will be to reach out and support with regards to health care of at least 115,000 persons in need who have been affected in various areas through the provision of medications, psycho social support services and ensure the availability of our staff who will constantly function within health facilities to ensure accessible health care as well as through mobile clinics in vulnerable communities across major affected areas of the North-West Region to guarantee access to all affected populations.
COHESODEC mobile clinic teams on the ground expected to ensure that affected populations are educated, sensitized as well as get medical consultation and given essential medications while also providing psycho-social support to these vulnerable affected populations most of whom are currently suffering from trauma.
COHESODEC services on the field within the context of this project will ensure accessible and free health care services to these badly affected communities. Our personnel on the field will carry out intensive psycho-social support services and help rape victims as well as victims of sexual and physical assault in both I.D.P and host communities.
COHESODEC interventions during the effective implementation of this project will be centered in the following health districts; Bafut, Bali, Bamenda, Santa, Tubah, Ndop, Mbengwi and Batibo. These health districts it should be noted are among the most affected following this conflict in the North-West and South-West Regions of Cameroon.
COHESODEC will equally provide health care to people who have suffered trauma caused by firearms or white weapons as a result of this conflict. Our psycho-social support Community Health Workers will also ensure strengthening of mental health and psycho social support interventions in all the above mentioned health districts where we plan to intervene.
Moreover, during these interventions, COHESODEC Community Health Workers shall ensure safe childbirth for women affected by this crises. Our interventions in this domain aims to reach out and ensure safe childbirth for 3,400 pregnant women affected by the conflict as well as ensure referral and counter-referral of GBV survivors, provide health care to survivors of GBV and support epidemics prevention in all affected areas within our health districts of intervention.
