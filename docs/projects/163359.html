Project Summary

The project aims at providing access to protective learning routines for 400.000 conflict affected children and adolescents through support to operational formal and non-formal schools (pre-primary, primary and secondary) in North-West, South-West, Littoral, West and Centre regions. The project intends through the blend of interventions proposed (distribution of school inputs, teachers training, studies) to mitigate the effects of possible attacks on education and ensure education in emergencies delivery in conflict and high insecurity context.

Besoins / Needs

The target population consists of pupils, teachers, other education personnell, and community members from five subdivisions in the 5 regions. The intervention zones are the subdivisions hosting the highest numbers of enrolled children from North-West and South-West regions, affected by the Anglophone crisis, which are Bamenda III, Limbe I, Mbanga, Douala IV, Mbouda, Dschang, Yaounde VI.The project benefit 290,000 children aged 4 to 17 in school and in NFE. It will build the capacities of 2,800 teachers from primary and secondary schools on PSS and C/DRR to improve the protective learning environment in and around schools. Teachers will learn to conduct a risk analysis based on the fears of children, and will use the umbrella methodology to prioritize gender specific risks to be addressed through mitigation measures. In order to generate evidence on the perception of safety by school users (parents, students, and teachers) and to strongly advocate for the protection of education against any form of attacks, the project will also conduct a Protective Learning Environment study on selected schools (non-representative sample).
