Project Summary

This project is in line with HRP 2024 objectives and also with cluster objectives, besides, it aligns with the cluster strategy to ensure increased, safe, equitable, inclusive and dignified access to life saving education and strengthen the capacity of the community and education actors from state up to BOMA's level to mitigate the impact of emergencies on children and youth. This project aims to improve access to safe, quality and inclusive protective education services to 5630 children age (3-17) emergency affected IDPs, refugees, returnees and host communities children of Leer and Mayendit Counties. 115 SMCs and 65 volunteer teachers in the two Counties of Leer and Mayendit. This integrated education project by ACR in hard-to-reach areas aims to ensure that children (boys and girls) have access to quality and inclusive education and those in schools are retained. Based on the aims stated, ACR intervention will be in establishing safe learning and recreational facilities in 6 locations (3 in each County). Train 65 teachers in education in emergency and child centered teaching. Train PTAs, SMCs in resource mobilization, importance of education and resources management. Provide monthly incentives for 65 volunteer teachers (33 for Leer and 32 for Mayendit). Organize back to learning campaign (BTL) to encourage children especially girls and children with disabilities to return to school. Response priorities will include gender-segregated WASH facilities as a way of promoting hygiene in schools, Provision of PSS services to the conflict and flood affected children. Provision of learning, teaching and WASH materials to selected schools. Capacity building of SMCs and PTAs. Life-skills and recreational opportunities to support the recovery of children from conflict and flood affected children and promote their overall well being. This project also seeks to address the crucial common cause of school drop-outs rate and this will help to increase and maintain school enrollment and attendance. Teachers, PTAs and SMCs will be trained on life-skills and referral pathways to enhanced the protective aspect of education in emergency (EiE) intervention.
The specific schools will be selected by the community in collaboration with the State Ministry of Education and General Instruction (SMoEI) during the time of launching the project. ACR will engage competent contractors and community members of the selected schools to contribute local labor and materials in all the works from design to completion. The expected activities from the contractors will include Site visitation, Mobilization of materials. Site clearance and levelling, Setting out, Excavation, Planting and casting steel poles, Casting floor slab, Wall hoarding with burnt red bricks/available material, Roof covering with iron sheets. Floor screeding.Fitting metallic doors, windows, and a blackboard, Defect liability period, Completion. During the implementation PTA members will participate full to make sure that the work is done according to the design provided and to make them to own the project.
ACR with the participation of different stakeholders, including State Ministry of Education staff to participate in the inception meeting with the aim of creating awareness about the project, intended beneficiaries as well stakeholder’s role in the implementation of this programme and also engage them in supporting and monitoring the performance of this project for the purposes of building project sustainability. However, during the inception meeting, the roles of each stakeholder will be spelt out clearly. ACR will use this opportunity to emphasis on stakeholder’s participation and timely feedback for effective implementation of the project. ACR will Improve equitable access to safe and protective education service and COVID-19 affected IDPs, returnees, spontaneous refugees, and host community children including children with disabilities.

1. Needs

A functional and properly resourced school should have nutrition, protection, water and sanitation facilities to ensure a child can learn in a safe environment. 185 of these schools have been and are still occupied by IDPs. These school infrastructures including WASH, latrine, furniture, and learning space require urgent rehabilitations and reconstructions. There are also a number of key supply-side barriers to education such as the lack of qualified teachers. The damaged education infrastructure overcrowded classrooms inconsistencies in the provision of teacher incentives, shortage of learning materials, poor sanitation and water facilities in schools, the long distance a child needs to travel to school, and little to no support services in schools for children with disabilities. On the demand side, there are socio-economic and cultural barriers including poverty, low awareness on the importance of education, inadequate support from parents, insecurity in many parts of the country, and inaccessibility to many areas especially during the rainy season due to poor road infrastructure network.In South Sudan, 70% of children aged between 6 and 17 have never set foot in a classroom. Only 10% of these children complete primary education which is the worst completion rates in the world. Shockley, a girl in South Sudan is more likely to die in childbirth than to complete primary education.Education cluster estimated 3.4 million girls and boys between aged 3-17 years old, 53% girls and 4330 teachers and 4330 SMCs and PTAs in areas affected by conflict or displacement.These emergencies ranging from non natural conflicts to natural disaster which some arises yearly with extraordinary intensities that gives complex risk situation and acerbate the struggle of children, women and aged. The local cope up mechanisms have grown-up to be vert insignificant due to the fact that most households had been crushed with the rampant occurrences of lifesaving threatening situation. Based on the situation surrounding the counties, ACR aimed to increase access to inclusive and protective life saving by establishing 6 and rehabilitating 6 TLS in the two counties (each 3 established and 3 renovated), provision of school supplies, WASH materials, Recreational kits, Teaching kits and ECD kits. ACR also aimed to respond by strengthening the capacity of the communities including education actors to mitigate the impact of emergencies on children and youth.

2. Project activities

The activities under this program are:5630 affected children (2915 girls and 2715 boys) be enrolled in schools.6 TLS constructed and 6 TLS rehabilitated (3 per each county).Establish 12 WASH facilities in schools (desegregated by gender).Build the capacity of 20 teaching facilitators on life-skills.Build the capacity of 115 PTAs and SMCs on PSS and referrals.Train 260 youths on life skills and basic vocational training.Provide monthly incentives to 65 volunteer teachers (33 for Leer and 32 for Mayendit).Train 65 volunteer teachers in life skills curriculum, curriculum and guidance for the early childhood development curriculum and materials, Psycho-social-support (PSS), referral pathways, content of and basic pedagogy for primary education curriculum and inter-cluster integration of protection.
