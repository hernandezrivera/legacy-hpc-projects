Project Summary

Based on the HIHFAD need Assessment conducted in October 2023: Child labor is prevalent (63%), primarily driven by poverty (99%), followed by wars and economic crises (60%). Lack of awareness about education importance (38%) and a shortage of accessible schools (24%) also contribute. Regrettably, only 19% report the existence of child protective services, and 81% state their absence. Just 35% believe they can meet their family's daily needs, while 45% express concerns about child protection risks in their community. These issues highlight the complex challenges faced by the targeted areas. Regarding GBV the results stated that 96% of respondents indicated an urgent need for gender-based violence support services,14% of respondents were aware of support services available to survivors of GBV in their community, and 86% said no.
The Project is community based-centered approach aims to ensure community participation in protection prevention and mitigation intervention, HIHFAD seeks to strengthen the resilience of affected communities and the protective environment through providing the integrated response of protection services related to General Protection, Gender Based Violence, Child Protection and Mine Action.
The proposal aims to support and prioritize the most vulnerable groups at the IDP camps and PWD in the communities, in mitigating the impacts of child labor, exploitation, school dropouts, and family separation resulting from financial instability. Additionally, it addresses (GBV) issues encompassing physical, emotional, economic, and sexual violence. Furthermore, it seeks to address concerns related to housing, land, and property (HLP) as well as support mine action survivors. To ensure sustainability, the project will be provided as a complementary in coordination with other sectors by establishing a referral pathway for a comprehensive response like referral to MHPSS, FSL, ERL and shelter
The project activities are designed to increase the resilience of PWDs including children with disabilities and VA including providing assistive devices to Improve access to physical rehabilitation, including and speech therapy for children with speech difficulties through two VA teams.
This project also aims to enhance the psychosocial wellbeing and protection of children and caregivers through conducting inclusive psychosocial support structured group sessions for children, and parenting skills program
as well as Child protection through community participation utilizing outreach modality which will focus on improving resilience through a set of prevention and response activities to mitigate protection risks, the Child protection intervention will be implemented by providing CP case management to decrease identified child protection risks, CVA will be provided to support children to fully recover from their experiences of violence, exploitation, abuse, including accessing services, to mitigate harmful coping mechanisms such as early marriages or child labor, in addition to community’s active participation with child participation, ensure children's voices and rights are heard.
GBV activities GBV team with complementarity with health services in supported heath facility by HIHFAD to ensure, to facilitate the access of IDPs women and children to integration SRH-GBV services,
the availability of GBV services within health facilities, integrating quality GBV Service Provision in SRH Service Delivery based on GBV AoR Guidance Note, to Increase accessibility of health/RH facilities that integrate GBV services
GBV prevention, empowerment will be provided for women, girls, men, boys) to create a safer environment for survivors,
GBV activities will be provided based on community approaches through the support of the GBV committee to strengthen community committees for addressing protection and implementation of community-led GBV risk mitigation initiatives by women or youth, and awareness campaign on GBV risk
