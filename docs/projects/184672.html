Project Summary

Observed levels of the Shabelle River in Somalia have been rising for the past two weeks. This has been attributed to moderate rains in the upper parts of the Ethiopian highlands. Currently, river levels at the upper reaches inside Somalia (Hiraan Region at Belet Weyne and Bulo Burti), are slightly above the normal range, while the mid and lower reaches (Middle and Lower Shabelle regions) are significantly above the normal levels at this time of the year. This has resulted in over bank spillage leading to riverine floods in parts Middle Shabelle. Further, two open river breakages in Jowhar district located in Bodale and Halgan village which are 22 km and 8 km south of Jowhar town respectively, have led to massive flooding in the area since 14 August 2021. According to field reports, several households have been affected and hundreds of crop destroyed.
The rainfall forecast for the coming week (16 to 22 Aug 2021) is pointing towards moderate rains within the upper parts of the Ethiopian highlands. Given the forecast, river levels are expected to continue rising further in the coming week.
Therefore, the flooding in Jowhar is high likely to be sustained throughout the week. Other areas to watch include riverine towns in the lower Shabelle region. Prelimi nary results from analysis of current status of river break ages along the Shabelle River by SWALIM indicate that there are several open and weak points and this could further exacerbate the flood situation. An updated database of the river breakages will be shared within a week.
Since ORDO has already conducted beneficiary assessments in target locations, prior to project
implementation, a rapid corroboration and vulnerability exercise shall be undertaken to review beneficiary. Female headed households, children headed-households or households with elderly, persistently ill, disabled members or many infant children are always considered a priority when beneficiary selection is being made. The ORDO emergency team has a well-established method of selecting beneficiaries that focuses only on their status, needs and vulnerabilities. ORDO makes these distinctions clear to communities during sensitization meetings at the rollout stage of the project. This project aims to support 3,500 HH Jowhar through NFI kits, ORDO will ensure gender and age awareness including in the disaggregation of data and the targeting of female-headed households, incorporating gender dynamics and the specific needs of different groups into needs assessments and response recommendations. Further, ORDO will ensure that kit contents procured through this project are gender sensitive.

1. - Projects activities

The below listed are our project activities;• Conduct community mobilization for project awareness and selection of beneficiaries through consultation with host community’s women and men representatives under riverine households.• Identification of the beneficiary vulnerable host communities within the targeted localities and prepare distribution plan and registers/lists for the targeted locations and inform the host communities’ under riverine household’s on the planned distribution prior to the set date as required.• The registration data will be disaggregated by gender; procure and distribute the NFI and undertake distributions as per the distribution plan and beneficiary registers/list for the Identified host community under riverine households in the target locations.• Identification and registration of host communities under riverine households for the NFIs, 50% women will form part of the selection committees.• Focus will be on host communities under riverine households and those whom their shelters have been destroyed by natural or manmade calamities.• Distribution of 3,500 NFI, during distribution each beneficiary will sign and confirm to have received his entitlement on a distribution, this template that will be developed by ORDO.• Follow up PDM will follow to assess the usability and impact of the NFI.
