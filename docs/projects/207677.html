Project Summary

According the HRP of GBV protection 2023 the crisis in the NWSW regions of Cameroon has caused women and girls to experience multiple forms of GBV, faced with many additional barriers to accessing services and recovering from GBV due to systemic gender inequality and other forms of intersectional discrimination. Most women have loss their sources of livelihood which has resulted to negative coping mechanisms which leads to sexual exploitation and abuse to provide for their basic needs. Many survivors of GBV experience long-lasting psychological and social effects due to the silence and stigma surrounding GBV, a lack of family and community support and appropriate response services, internalized shame, and lack of power and resources to escape continued perpetration of GBV. This project seeks to provide holistic support to enhance the healing, empowerment and recovery process of GBV survivors while reducing the incidence of violence to which they are victims. The objective of the project aligns with the 2023 HRP objective 3; Reduce vulnerabilities and strengthen resilience of 920,000 people affected by crisis.
The specific objectives of this project are to:
1. Provide mental, psychosocial, support to women and adolescents girls including persons with disabilities who are at risk to GBV and GBV survivors
2. Provide medical support to GBV survivors
3. Provide post rape kit to support to GBV survivor
4. Provide legal assistance to GBV survivors
5. Provide referral of GBV survival to other service providers
This project targets 2,000 women, older adolescent girls and people living with disabilities who are at high risk of GBV and GBV survivors as direct beneficiaries, because engaging only GBV survivors as the sole participants of the project. 500 beneficiaries will come from Santa, Ndop (500), Babessi (500) and Mbengwi (500) all in the North west region of Cameroon.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

According to the 2023 HRP of protection GBV AoR, one out of six people living in Cameroon is suffering from the effects of violence, climate chocs, and disease outbreaks. Women, girls, boys, and men are caught in crises that are not of their making. The crisis in the North West Region of Cameroon continue to negatively affect the living conditions of people in the country. Protection needs are rampant; with women and girls are at high risk of gender-based violence. The crisis has heightened the risk of violence, exploitation and abuse. At the same time, national systems, including health and legal systems, and community and social support networks are weaken. This breakdown of systems reduce access to health services, including sexual and reproductive health services, and legal services, leading to an environment of impunity in which perpetrators are not held to account. When systems and services are disrupted or destroyed, women and girls face even higher risk of human rights violations such as sexual violence, intimate partner violence, exploitation and abuse, child marriage, denial of resources and harmful traditional practices. GBV has significant and long-lasting impacts on the health, and psychosocial and economic well-being of women and girls, and their families and communities. According to the HRP GBV protection of 2023, the humanitarian assistance and protection services target 2.7 million of the most vulnerable people in 2023 with aims to ensure that gender, age, and diversity considerations, including on disability, are part and parcel of humanitarian assessments and that humanitarian actors have the capacity to provide a response that is inclusive and adequate. Women and girls including people with disability therefore need access to support services for GBV victims/survivors. Without these support services, women and adolescent girls are at greater risk of GBV, their health is compromised, their mobility is restricted, and they may become increasingly isolated. There is therefore the need to provide protection and services that meet the specific needs of GBV survivors and other women and girls including people with disabilities who are at increased risk of violence, which includes providing psychosocial support, medical services, legal assistance, post rape kit, and referral to livelihood programmes and other services for women in crisis situation to reduce their vulnerability to GBV, including sexual exploitation and abuse. Given the crucial needs, WESAD intends through this project to provide holistic support to reduce vulnerabilities and strengthen resilience of women, adolescents girls and people living with disabilities at risk of GBV and GBV survivors which is in line with the HRP 2023 GBV AoR protection and aligns with objective 3 of the HRP of GBV AoR. It is therefore necessary for any donor agency to partner with WESAD in view of the significant impact the project will make.The following activities will be carried out to attain our objectives:1. Carry out recreational activities such as informal life skills that support development of adaptive and positive behaviors that support women and adolescent girls in dealing with the demands and challenges of everyday life2. Work with health providers and community leaders to identify cases of violence, to inform the community about the urgency of, and the procedures for, referring survivors of sexual violence for medical survivors within 72 hours3. Work with community and health personnel to Identify survivors and provide them with post rape Kit4. Sensitize communities on existing laws and policies that uphold women’s and girls’ rights and protections from GBV, and ensure survivors’ access to care5. Connect survivors to information about their rights, options to report GBV, and care in a safe and confidential manner, case management and sexual and reproductive health care delivered by trained personnel as well as to livelihood support programs
