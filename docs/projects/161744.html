Project Summary

With high level of IDPs movement into Maiduguri, Jere, munguno, mafa and Yola south that lead to high congestion of IDP camps and catastrophic protection gaps and needs, among the IDPs and host communities in these LGAs, GOALPrime Organization Nigeria seek to strengthen community/camp protection structure of the conflict affected people living in IDP camps, and host communities of these LGAs to respond to specific protection needs and risk with timely referrals, distribution of solar lanterns, dignity kits, and hygiene kits, through inspiring integrated community based protection response action, targeting 12,800 vulnerable Women, men, boys and girls within 12 months in Maiduguri, Jere, Monguno, Mafa and Yola south Local Government Area of Borno and Adamawa state respectively .
This project focuses on building capacities of men, women, boys and girls living in IDP camps and host community to become community/camp protection watchers through training of Identified 500 men and women, boys and girls living in the community/IDP camp in protection principles, expected action to take to safe guard life in a threat to life situation and establishment of protection monitoring desk to provide surveillance in the camp and communities to receive immediate protection threat and concern reports in the camps and communities through these trained community/camp protection watchers. GOALPrime Organization Nigeria will localize every action towards resolving any recorded protection risk or concerns to strengthen ownership action and the willingness of the community stakeholders to always respond to the threats seen in their environment. Also GPON shall coordinate with other actors responding in these LGAs, protection sector and all the partners in northeast to secure response to resolve any recorded protection risk and concerns through sharing of the reported protection risk and protection concerns.
Through this project GOALPrime will provide protection assistance to 12,300 vulnerable girls and young women under reproductive age, Men and boys prioritizing people with special need and women and girls headed households to promote GBV risk mitigation, prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse in IDP camps and support them with protection services including distribution of solar lanterns, dignity kits, and hygiene kits. GOALPrime Organization Nigeria will focus to understand the specific protection needs of the identified 12,300 vulnerable young women and girls, Men and boys of different age groups living in IDP camps through protection risk, and protection need mapping across the IDP camps and host communities of Maiduguri, Jere, Monguno, Mafa and Yola south LGA, to enable us provide needed protection services in their respective local and cultural context.
This project is design to be implemented from January 2020 to December 2020 (12 months), the established protection monitoring desk across Maiduguri Jere, Munguno, Mafe and Yola south shall remain functional on the close of the project and GOALPrime Organization Nigeria shall commit to verify every reported cases and through this medium be providing and sharing protection situation update with protection sector, NGOs and advocacy to the global community.
This project shall be implemented through LGA project committees in each of the target LGAs that will involve protection sector, community and IDP camp leaders from the target community and IDP camps. These project committees will be provided with project orientation and consultation to agree on the implementation approach. GOALPrime will through established toll free line receive feedback and concerns from the affected population. GOALPrime Organization Nigeria shall coordinate with the protection sector and partners to provide solutions to every concerns or observation and need identified at the course of the project implementation.

1 - Needs

From the state consultation reports, 6.7 million people across the BAY state are in dire need of humanitarian services, (Borno 3.81M, Adamawa 1.65M, and Yobe 1.28M). The protection situation in Borno state remains fragile and pockets of violent armed conflict/military offensive operations continued and intensified in some parts of the state throughout 2019. NSAG activities including attacks, killings and abductions of civilians increased with risks of suicide bombing. Military operations increased with the launching of operation Yancin Tafki by the Nigerian military and the MNJFT in Abadam, Guzamala, Kukawa, Marte, Mobbar and Ngala LGAs. The LGAs most affected by recent active fighting are Gubio, Magumeri, Nganzai, Dikwa, Monguno. Overall, 24 of the 27 LGAs in Borno have been directly affected by the conflict. Displacement and re-displacement of population continued throughout the year as a result of the attacks, with IDPs moving into congested camps and settlements including in Maiduguri, Jere, Monguno, mafa and Yola south. It is estimated that since January, over 100,000 new arrivals were registered mainly from attacked LGAs. In total nearly 1.5 million people remain displaced in the state. Humanitarian actors have also been impacted by the recent NSAG activities through abductions and killings. New displacements continue to incur serious protection threats both during displacement and in many return areas. Female and child-headed households, unaccompanied and separated children, and adolescent boys and girls are particularly at risk of SGBV, child recruitment, and human trafficking, while adolescent and adult males face threats of harassment, arbitrary arrests and detention, and serious maltreatment during detention. Inadequate access to food, basic services and livelihood opportunities has a direct link to the increasing number of reported cases of sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA), particularly within female-headed households. The protection sector proposed to Enhance civilians’ protection from harm and respond to specific needs and risks with timely and comprehensive protection services, improve affected persons’ access to their rights and strengthen the overall protection environment. Promote the creation of conditions for safe, voluntary and durable solutions and enhance freedom of movement. This project seeks improve affected persons’ access to their right and strengthen the overall protection environment, respond to specific protection needs of vulnerable men, women, boys and girls prioritizing people with special need, young women and girls headed households living in IDP camps in Maiduguri, Jere, Monguno, mafa and Yola south LGA of borno and Adamawa state respectively.

2 - Activities

Project inception meeting with community and camp stakeholders and constitution of project implementation committee.Protection risks and protection need mapping in the communities and IDP camps of Monguno, mafa and Yola south.Identification and profiling 500 of women, men, girls and boys living in IDP camps and host communities of Maiduguri, Jere, Monguno, Mafa and Yola south LGAs for protection principles training.Training of the identified and profiled 500, women, men, girls and Boys in protection principles.Establishment of 10 protection monitoring desk in communities and IDP camps at Monguno, Mafa and Yola south.Recruitment of local camp and community protection monitoring officer for camps and communities in Monguno, Mafa and Yola south to man the protection desk.Identification and profiling of vulnerable women, men, girls and boys, and special need persons living in the IDP camps and communities for support with specific protection need.Support to the identified and profiled women and men, girls and boys, and special need persons with protection services including distribution of solar lanterns, dignity kits, and hygiene kits etc.Responding to reported protection concerns and protection risk in IDP camps and host communities of Maiduguri, Jere, Monguno, Mafa and Yola south through referrals to other service providers in the IDP camps and communities.Continues protection Monitoring, reporting and responding accordingly
