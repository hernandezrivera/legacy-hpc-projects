Project Summary

Acute food crisis (food insecurity) has unfolded in the North East because of years of massive displacement, destruction of agricultural activities, lack of access to farmland and loss of livelihoods. In Borno, Adamawa and Yobe states, 4.7million people are in crisis and emergency phases of food and nutrition insecurity with this number projected to rise to 5.1million by June this year.
Considering the protracted nature of the conflict and the already food insecurity of the region- the conflict has continued to impact on access and availability to food. Currently accesses to farm lands in some locations are not possible as IDPs are warned by security forces about the risk of farming beyond a certain parameter. The food insecurity has threatened many IDPs and host community as such as put them into serious hunger, starvation, malnutrition especially for under 5 children, nursing/lactating mothers in the Borno and other worse hit LGAs in Adamawa and Yobe states.
The project proposed by GREENCODE will sensitize and mobilise community to actively engage in both dry and rainy season’s farming as well as support registered farmers with seeds (these will be seeds familiar with the farmers and as always distributed by FAO and Ministry of Agriculture), cash for labour for farm preparation and harvesting, fertilizers, livestock (cow, sheep goats) and farm tools.
The project is targeting 6,000 households in total (3,000HHs for rainy season and 3,000 for dry season farming). In addition, GREENCODE will undertake advocacy actions for increasing safe access to farmland/grazing area, by going into discussions with community leaders, farmers and government security forces protecting the locations.
As part of protection mainstreaming process, GREENCODE will undertake the following activities community training and outreach event on GBV, Child protection and mine awareness education.
The project will last for 12 months.

Needs

The recent Cadre Harmonisé reports shows that about 3.7 million people are projected to be in crisis or emergency phases of food and nutrition security during the 2018 lean season (Cadre Harmonisé phases 3/4). Borno state and its LGAs are the worse hit as it is the theater of the insurgency with highest number of displacement, destructions and death.Million people in the Borno and other states in the Northeast (Yobe and Adamawa) are projected to be food insecure and considered to be facing crisis or emergency situations. food security remains extremely fragile, particularly in Borno state where many households still depend on assistance from extended families, humanitarian aid organisations and self helps. Despite being displaced both the IDPs and host hosting them require food and improved nutrition; as such access to food has significant impacts on health and wellbeing of displaced populations, host communities, and the surrounding natural environment. The impact of food insecurity is felt by children and pregnant/nursing mothers. Lack of food for these most vulnerable groups will affect the growth and development of such child if they survive dead. Recently heavy rain fall that caused wide spread flooding in most of the northeast states has further affected most of the rainy season crops planted, thus further deepening the food crisis now and in the months to come.In view of the increasing rate of food insecurity, there is an urgent need for humanitarian actors to actively support household agriculture production (crops, livestock) and livelihoods through improved access to agro-based production inputs (including seeds, tools, fertilizer, irrigation facilities, livestock and animal health), asset creation and livelihood support activities (including income generating activities, training/capacity building and conditional resource transfers) will go a long way in supporting IDPs and host community easy access to food and nutrition thereby guarantying improved food security and nutrition. GREENCODE has identified these needs and plans to readdress it with the available support from the FS sector and donors.GREENCODE as an organisation has Staff Code of Conduct is in place and integrate commitment to Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) in the project design. Also in place are Accountability framework and complaint mechanism.
