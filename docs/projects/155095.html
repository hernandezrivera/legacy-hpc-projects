Project Name

Start Date

01/01/2019

End Date

31/12/2019

Project Summary

Needs: The targeted population is still facing great strugle to adapt to the new environment, which brings anxiety and depression. Moreover, as the FDMNs had to endure extraordinary mental pressure during and after their departure from their country, there were significant distress and disappointment, fall of social status as well as the need to adjust to a new social structure on top of having to reprocess major emotional suffering, MHPSS is thus needed. Since the FDMNs are is a relatively more stable but still unpredictable position, they will likely require long term MHPSS services.
Activities or outputs:
1. Action Against Hunger's Mental Health and Care Practices, Gender and Protection (MHCPGP) program implements an array of psychosocial support and mental health services to vulnerable individuals with special focus on vulnarable children, pregnant and lactating women in established outpatient treatment facilities.
2. Specific project activities include providing mental health and psychosocial support within primary and secondary health facilities, ACF's stabilization and nutrition centers. Trained psychologists are placed in primary and secondary health facilities to provide individual and group counseling.
3. Lay counselors are employeed to provide psychological first aid, sensitization, support groups, focused one to one sessions, family support as well as community outreach.
4. Therapeutic activities are also be provided to children to help relieve their stress and encourage social skills.
5. ACF will continue to provide mental health services and psychosocial support in host and refugee communities, including individual and group counseling addressing psychological distress such as depression, anxiety, PTSD and acute stress disorder.
6. The MHCP team continuously analyses the current context as well as factors that could cause potential harm and / or distruption to the refugees and host communities.
7. ACF's MHCP teams will join disaster preparedness and MHPSS key messages and key messages from other sectors to share as part of community awareness raising activities.
8. ACF also conducts prevention activities addressing personal care relating to hygiene, nutrition, physical health and social /emotional practices to enhance the well-being of pregnant and lactating mothers. These activities include adult males and family members. ACF's mental health and psychosocial support are provided to individuals from infancy to adulthood. 28 psychologists, 57 Lay Counselors will be the service providers for MHPSS who are all facility based as they provide the service from ACF centers as well as in field based on need. The qualification for the Psychologist is minimum Masters in Psychology/applied psychology; and the qualification for Lay Counselor is minimum HSC with intensive training on PSS services. The service will cover :
-Camp: 1E and 1W with 6 Staff, 2E with 5 Staff,2W with 3 Staff,3and 4 with 4 Staff,5 with 3 Staff,7 with 3 Staff,9 with 3 Staff,10 with 4 Staff,11 with 5 Staff,13 with 3 Staff,14 with 5 Staff,15and 16 with 3 Staff,21 with 3 Staff,22 with 3 Staff,24 with 5 Staff,26 with 3 Staff, Kutupalong Registered camp with 5 Staff, Nayapara Registered camp with 8 Staff,
- Cox’s Bazar Sadar Hospital with 3 Staff and Moheshkhali Union; Ukhiya, Teknaf, Kutubdia and Pekua Host Community with 8 Staff.
