Project Summary

Through this project, Alpha aims at improving formal education of most vulnerable Lebanese and migrants children through schools. Nine schools have been selected in the governorates of Nabatiyeh (8 schools: Tebnine-1303, Jmeijmeh-Safad-1307, Kafra Primary-1312, Yater-1313, Aita al Shaab-1328, Kafra Secondary-1449, Chakra-1501 and Qabrikha-1509) and South (1 school: Deir Kifa- 1241).
These schools have been chosen because Alpha has already an experience with and knows their situation and their needs. The total number of children enrolled in these schools is 4342 with 808 children coming from private schools and approximately 652 children more than the capacity of these schools (Ref: MEHE). We think that the number will be higher. Nevertheless, and with the available means, a big challenge faces children, parents, teachers and the all concerned by the situation of education in Lebanon.
Alpha will ensure the support through: - Procurement of needed stationary, books, light equipment; - Adapted and inclusive catch-up learning support programs addressing learning losses, based on needs of the most vulnerable children; - Supporting teaching availability and capacity to manage the large increase in Lebanese students transferred from the private sector to the public sector and covering the operational costs of schools to help in hosting additional numbers of children.
The target of each activity above-mentioned will be verified and confirmed in September-October 2021.
Through a rapid assessment, Alpha will verify and confirm or correct this information. In the meanwhile, we will evaluate the needs in order to resolve the problem of the number of children which is more than the capacity of more than 25% of the selected schools. Two to three scenario must be foreseen with one, two or more solutions; opening the school in the afternoon, hire volunteers, set up classrooms...
Inclusive and adapted catch-up learning support programs addressing learning losses based on needs of the most vulnerable children will ensure the necessary balance and answers to the main need: efficiency et effectiveness of our program. We must mention that flexibility and openness, listening and observing, motivating and encouraging will be our key words!

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

The objective of this project is: improving Formal Education of most vulnerable Lebanese and migrant children through schools.Nine schools have been selected; 8 in the governorate of Nabatiyeh and 1 in the governorate of South. As the 9 schools are located in areas where Alpha is active since 2006, and in schools since 2014-2015, the organization’s team has a good knowledge of the strong and weak points and the main needs. In 2021, and regarding the deterioration of the situation there is an urgent need to respond as much as possible; through this project, Alpha will put its know-how to support student, teachers and schools and to manage the influx from private to public schools.The main activities are:- Procurement of needed stationary, books and light equipment to the most vulnerable children; it means between 35% and 40% of children. - Support the most vulnerable children with inclusive and adapted catch-up learning program. - Support teachers in public schools to manage the large increase in Lebanese students transferred from the private to the public sector- Covering the operational costs of schools to help in hosting additional numbers of children With reference to the data already available, in the 9 schools targeted, the total number of children is 4342 with 808 children coming from the private sector and an additional number of at least 652 children.Of course this data must be verified but it gives us an idea of the situation.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

Alpha ensures that all staff involved in this program are aware of the Secretary General Bulletin Special Measures for Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse, and the internal reporting mechanisms for SEA cases, after mandatory trainings and the signature of the code of conduct and the SG bulletin. Alpha have worked on enhancing its internal PSEA minimum standards through participation in trainings, coaching that was provided by UNICEF in 2021; and the roll out of the new UNICEF’s PSEA toolkit developed in 2019 for the work on prevention/ assistance /reporting and investigation capacities.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

Alpha works in both humanitarian and development sectors. It is an independent and not-for-profit organization; it respects a strict political and religious impartiality and encourages pluralism and inter-communal dialogue. The principles of humanity, neutrality, impartiality, transparency and independence are fundamental to Alpha. Concerning the Accountability for Affected Population (AAP) Alpha has the above-mentioned principles as key-words to ensure that communities are meaningfully and continuously involved in decisions that directly impact their lives.
