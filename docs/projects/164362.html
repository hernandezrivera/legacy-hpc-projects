Project Summary

Through this project, Violet organization aims to target the IDPs in Idleb and Aleppo governorate with the following services:
1- Provision of fire safety training IDPs in 17 camps (10 camps in northern Aleppo and 7 camps in Idleb governorate) as well as equipping these camps with the necessary safety means.
2. Establish 12 temporary centers across Idleb and Aleppo governorates to accommodate newly IDPs who are evacuated by Violet Organization.
throughout the project implementation, Violet will ensure the timely reporting for the CCCM cluster and other humanitarian actors through its IDP tracking team. additionally, the Gender Action Plan matrix will be conducted for all activities of projects, from design, recruitment, assessment conducting, implementation, monitoring, and evaluation to put standard plans to make sure the projects meet the minimum standard of gender and protection mainstreaming. Project teams will be gender-balanced and trained on gender awareness. This includes removing barriers that inhibit the recruitment of women to field teams in particular. Furthermore, Violet will ensure the beneficiaries participates in the project, especially women and adolescents to take their suggestions and discuss their needs to make sure the assistants provided suitable for them. Lastly, All activities will require the completion of a participation plan (e.g. accountability mechanisms), in addition to protection risk assessment, monitoring, and modification as part of the project cycle.

4 - Needs

Since November 2019, the humanitarian situation has reached unprecedented levels of needs, with large-scale displacement from southern Idleb up to safer areas in the north. Between 01.12.2019 and 31.01.2020 approx. 520,000 people have been displaced. The majority of displaced (80%) are women and children, who are facing increased protection risks, combined without the ability to cover basic needs. Multiple displacements increase their vulnerability. It also forced them to leave behind belongings. The displaced are heading to northern Aleppo and northern Idleb, the heightened concentration of people puts additional burden on already overstretched, but also damaged infrastructure. Further 280,000 are at immediate risk of displacement. Most urgent is providing shelter to IDPs. However there is a shortage in accommodation, which is forcing them to stay out of camps, collective shelters, in rented houses, but also in unfinished or sub-standard shelters. The overcrowding and lack of WASH facilities has led to privacy and protection concerns for women, and is increasing forced early marriage among displaced girls as coping mechanism. Not only rehabilitation of WASH facilities is needed, but also distribution of hygiene kits. Due to market access, multipurpose cash vouchers are a preferred modality in certain areas. Child protection concerns are heightening, also due to drop out from schools. The winter conditions are increasing the vulnerability further with the need for NFIs, food, shelter, health and WASH. Basic services, such as health and education, are highly limited due to damaged and directly targeted infrastructure such as hospitals. Besides all lack of basic items and services, IDPs are in high need of psychosocial support and psychological first aid to accommodate them in their current situation. Protection concerns are high and thus require a sufficient referral system to address urgent cases of GBV, SEA and child protection.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures
