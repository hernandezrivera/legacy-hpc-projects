Needs

According to Nigerian food security cluster Projection for June to August 2018, shows that Michika, Mubi , and Hong are in crisis stage. Also the joint multi-Sectoral needs assessment of October 2017 also affirms that Madagali is in Crisis, while Michika, Mubi and Hong are under pressure. Meanwhile most of Borno is under crisis. Hence the need to further encourage farmer activities and increase yields. Following recent assessment, locations like madagali ward of madagali LGA were about 80% of the men were killed during the insurgence resulting to high rate of female-headed household who are susceptible to abuses. Some even go out of their to seek succor and survivor sex, and the limited space for farming in those areas due to insecurity, most of the returnees have no full access to their farmland, of which farming is the major livelihood activities carried out in those areas. There is a need to improve on their farming system with the limited space at their disposal to ensuring adequate food is gotten to curb the food crisis in those locations.

Activities

1. Agricultural value chains development by promoting innovative actions to increase efficiency of crop value chains which remain underdeveloped. In locations like Madagali and Michika where Maize and Cowpea is one of the most produced crop. Best practices will be ensured for quality production, this will drastically reduce the strength of the middle men, hence allowing diret access for farmers to meet with buyers and maximise profit. This can be achieved through;i sensitizationii training 2. Promoting sustainable natural resources management amongst farmer groups. e.g land, water, compost etc.i adequate trainingii. sensitization 3. Capacity building and knowledge transfer on best agricultural practice to ensure sustainable agricultural production. This will be achieved through;i micro enterprise fundamentals training on effective marketing of small farming businessii. establishment of farmers group or clusters 4. Facilitation of value addition and processing of farm output to farmers5. Providing better access to markets and linkages with private sector.i. establishment of partnership between private sectors and local farmers6. Enhance household income generation through increased opportunities in food preservation or transformation and marketing training.7. Increasing resilience of small scale farming by encouraging and facilitating formation of cooperative societies among farmer groups.8. Distribution of fertilizers and other farm inputs9. Beneficiaries targeting will be done based on the vulnerability level. Priority will be given to the most vulnerable ( the aged, pregnant women, female headed household, the physically challenged, child headed households and people of concerns).HARAF has child protection and safeguardining policy and staff Code of Conduct is in place and with strong commitment to Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA). The project will mainstream protection ensuring activities target the most vulnerable, enhance safety, dignity. Rights-based approach, Promote and protect human rights of beneficiaries and ensure NOT contributing to or perpetuating discrimination, abuse, violence, neglect orexploitation. About 10% of the budget will be towards the protection mainstreaming activities. Accountability to Affected Population(AAP); HARAF as an organisation is thorough in its activities and accountable and uses power responsibly by taking account of, giving account to, and being held to account by the people they seek to assist combined with effective and quality programming that recognizes the dignity, capacity, and abilities of communities of concern.A strong and periodic Monitoring and Evaluation tool will be ensured, tracking the progress, effectiveness, input, output, outcome and the general impact of the project to the targeted people and location, following the baseline and end-line. Some of the indicators; no of cooperative formed, no of people trained on value chained addition, no of people having access to market and linkage to private sector, no of people reached with fertilizer and farm inputs and market price evaluation. It will be a continuous process throughout the project cycle.
