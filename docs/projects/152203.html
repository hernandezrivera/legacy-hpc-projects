Project Summary

To build resilience among Gender based violence survivors and strengthen returnees
and displace communities through culturally appropriate psychosocial support
interventions. 2. To empower vulnerable women and girls on sustainable economic
livelihood activities to mitigate sexual GBV among the most vulnerable 3. To augment
improved policies and framework on GBV mitigation and prevention through advocacy
and community appraisal platforms

Needs

The protracted crisis in the North East has increase the rate of GBV, with most of theprotection mechanisms out of place, risk mitigation and GBV prevention becomes centralto GBV intervention in the affected communities. 90% of displaced communities areextremely poor with high number of sexual exploitation and abuse, these necessitate theneeds to empower vulnerable population with sustainable livelihood especially in returnedcommunities. Structural Community Resilient building and Community Peace BuildingDialogues engagement through culturally appropriate psycho social support medium isneedful in returned communities and temporary displaced habitation
