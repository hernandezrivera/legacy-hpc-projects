Project Summary

This project is designed especially to address the issue of child protection for those who effected by the conflict in South Darfur state(murshing locality and sharg jebel marra) , the project aim to deliver effective activities to most vulnerable population in targeting areas, the project designed and planned with gender sensitive and gender in age mark this a new tools will allow to redesign the activity of project during any phase ,the project targeted more then 4560 person including most vulnerable women,men.boys girls.
The project will using the methodology such as , training ,session ,therapy group by using arts for children ,drama show and theaters.
The project select all participant in the activities without considering the political,social, cultural ,religion ,age .ethnic background .

Needs

The ongoing conflicts in 5 Darfur states witnessed stability throughout two years ago, although there are armed conflict continue around jebal marra since 2011 and protracted violence and clash especially in center Darfur state namely in Jebal Marra mountain(murshing,leiba.Derbat in south Darfur and Golol, Nertiti,Rerkero and Mu Dakhan in Centeral Darfur) , and other Darfur states. Since 2013, increase the numbers of militia group, rebels, unknown gun man and other banditry group who have used violence and kills .all those conflict , resulted into massive displacement and increase a numbers of internal displaced peoples as indicated in HRP and HNO 2019 5.5 million people in humanitarian need including children 2.6 million require humanitarian assistance and services as well as protection support in Sudan . all those figure include (protection, child protection and GBV & SGBV) these figures categorized by 70% female and desegregated by age 70% children(< 18 years), 30% adult (18-59 years) , elderly (>59 year). The target group of the protection project disturbed two state ,south Darfur ( sharg El jebal … murdhang 44,448 ,El Farian 4,011 and Nyala 869966 specific IDPs camp) and central Darfur (Golo 44,297, Nertiti 54,001 ,Um G ukhan 62,559 and Rekero 59,986). This project designed to cover its activities in 8 Areas in central and south Darfur states . According to the Inter-agency rapid need assessments which was conducted in Deribate & Kidineer on 10 -15 September 2017 and Murshing locality on 10-may -2018 . the results of assessment on D&K found that, at least 65 Unaccompanied children Separated (UASC) 45 in Deribat and 20 in Kidineer all of them are boys, 50% of children at school age are out of school , there are No Friendly Child Spaces (FCS), No social workers in areas protects, no community Based Protection Network (CBPNs),child labor reported ,birth registration issue ,child marriage ,GBV issue and child leading household also reported , in recent year of 2018 the NIDAA organization,GAH and other organization they were response on CP but not enough to cover those areas there still much needs and gaps . According to The Inter-Agency Assessment which was conducted between 25-29 April, 2018. By participated staff from UN (OCHA, UNICEF, WFP, UNHCR and IOM), INGOs (CRS, DRC and WR), line Ministries (SMoE, SMoSA, SMoE), WES and HAC in Rokero town and Gemiza village t, about 2km East of Rokero town . the estimated population is 2,285 households .the assessment found that , the 3 Child Friendly Spaces (CFS) one is completely destroyed by rain, there are no active Community Based Protection Networks(CBPNs) to address child issue , There is only one social worker in the locality department of social affairs, majority of children lack of certification birthday , there are child labour and child marriage among IDPs and community host, no GBV concern and there are only one women center built by DRC funded (UNDP).

Activities

Activity :* 1- A number of 500 volunteers (25 from each village and IDPs camps with 50% female representative ), will be selected and trained for 2 days to work as psych-social and child protection promoter in targets areas .2- Conduct 16 open session of raise awareness on child protection and GBV for community in target areas .3- Conduct 16 session of Drama show to send information and Acknowledge on child protection and Gender Bases Violence for the communities in order to eliminate PC and reduce GBV among target groups. 4- Conduct vulnerability assessment to identify the person with especial needs and their needs in targets areas. 5- Conducting 3 advocacy events gathering community ,government bodies, stakeholders and other partners to demonstrate all parts interest in order to encourage community to stop violence against children.
