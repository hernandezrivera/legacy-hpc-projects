Project Summary

As the Syrian crisis has been continuing for a decade, constant clashes and shelling destroyed most of the previously weak infrastructures. They affected vast plantation areas, irrigation and pest control organized services and seed research is almost absent throughout the war years. These factors and the continuously worsening economic situation and displacement have impacted plantation scale, diversity, and food production.
Orange aims to improve the resilience of the affected Syrian population in Azaz, Mare’ and Souran sub-districts of northern Aleppo countryside through agricultural livelihoods initiative project, which aims to improve the resilience of conflict-affected population at the household level of targeted communities in northwest Syria. This will be carried out through agricultural livelihoods assistance. The project aims to provide vital cultivation technical service by establishing specialized agricultural research laboratories in seed research, integrated pest management IPM, fertilization, and soil, all with equipment and trained technicians to provide the missing services for the project's direct beneficiaries wider community.
The project will also incorporate support through diverse phased agricultural extension support, agriculture inputs, and short-term support that increase access to more effective irrigation techniques and integrated pest management. These will be providing a foundation for market access and creating value chains where poor farmers can be better involved, both directly and indirectly, and strengthen links to markets leading to increasing farm productivity. These interventions will yield higher household-level income and ultimately improve food security. The integration of these activities in a phased and comprehensive approach is essential for ensuring a sustainable impact on the productive capacity and long-term resilience to shocks for communities of Azaz district in northwest Syria.
The project will support 500 farmers providing them with technical knowledge and cultivation best practices, distribute integrated pest management kits and agricultural input vouchers. in addition to 60 households receiving small scale home production equipment and materials

4 - Needs

The continuing crisis has resulted in a substantial reduction in the availability and quality of farm inputs. Insecurity, a dwindling economy, and international import sanctions, decreasing government subvention been severely restricted over the last several years. A summary of the challenges facing the agricultural revitalization is in the following points:- Cost: the increased cost resulted from the war crisis, economic deterioration, and loss of investment due to security.- Access: the road services and infrastructure, transportation, and money handling services have been significantly decreasing since the beginning of the crisis, in addition to the loss of access to besieged areas and clash locations.- Quality: the unavailability of proper control over seed resources, set standards for soil and fertilizer, organized pest management procedures, and loss of knowledge inputs have caused deterioration in the quality and efficiency of cultivation activities.- Assets: farming lands and farming support assets and tools were lost during the war, displacement, and other economic problems that caused a severe need in assets.Orange has conducted a rapid needs assessment in 2019 to answer some key questions regarding farmers, lands, irrigation systems, and other related aspects in northern Aleppo .Key findings: Results showed that most crops from previous times are still being grown, such as winter crops (wheat, barley, and chickpea), cash crops (cumin, black seeds, and coriander), summer and winter vegetables, trees (olive, cherry, and pistachio), lentil, potato. In comparison, onions and garlic are produced in lesser percentages.UNOCHA has listed the agricultural livelihood interventions and the support of accessibility

5 - Implementation of activities

This project intends to provide agricultural livelihood activities via providing knowledge and awareness for farmers on cultivation best practices, agricultural, fertilizer and tools inputs, in addition to the provision of agricultural lab test for soil, integrated pest management and seeds tests.
