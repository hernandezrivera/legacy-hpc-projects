Project Summary

This project aims to increase awareness of explosive ordnance risk education to enhance safety and security for vulnerable groups in the Malakal Upper Nile State. Due to high return rates, safe access to areas must be provided through risk education on explosive ordinance and ERW. The project will target 20,000 children and adults affected by the conflict. In partnership with the local administration, the CAO will implement the project to achieve maximum results. The project activities will increase EORE awareness for the at-risk population, contributing to a safe and secure environment.

1. Needs

South Sudan is currently grappling with significant challenges in humanitarian aid, development, and security. Since gaining independence, the country has witnessed an upsurge in the threat levels posed by landmines and explosive remnants of war (ERW). The conflicts in the disputed area of Jonglei and Upper Nile, as well as Unity, have contributed to this rise in threat levels, which resulted in the displacement of tens of thousands of people both within and outside South Sudan. Despite the conflict in Sudan having a severe impact on the economic outlook of South Sudan, there is still hope for a better future. The northern states, primarily dependent on imports from Sudan, face challenges with skyrocketing essential commodity prices, making life difficult for the population. Malakal was the most highly populated in Upper Nile after the Sudan conflict.CAO intends to continue responding to the needs of children and Adolescent youth as part of its mandate since they have been exposed to armed activities during the crisis in the Upper Nile, referring them for further action to the relevant working groups addressing protection risks issues through a response strategy which includes: Please focus on the provision of Capacities support to communities and staff, strengthening adolescent youth Centres, community-based PSS mechanisms and approaches to respond to their needs when necessary, maintaining a referral system for Land mine Victims, increasing mine Risk education awareness at schools, CFS and in the community with keys messages on the landmine, UXO and ERW, establishment and support of schools club and child help desk and mainstream CP and GBV into Education.

2. Project activities

The following are the proposed activities for the MRE program:1. Educate children in schools about MRE.2. Provide training to teachers on MRE guidelines.3. Raise awareness about MRE in community centers associated with schools.4. Create awareness about MRE among adults in the community.5. Conduct awareness sessions for children, adults, and adolescent youths by the MRE program staff.
