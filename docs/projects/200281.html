Project Summary

Mercy-USA strives to continue its commitment to improving access to healthcare for vulnerable populations, thus, throughout this project, Mercy-USA will provide financial support to vulnerable Lebanese and migrants across Lebanon, in need of surgical intervention for orthopedic or vascular surgeries with medical devices mainly due to their unaffordable cost such as but not limited to the following
• ACL Reconstruction Surgery (Anterior Cruciate Ligament) – major ligament in the knee.
• Knee Replacement Surgery (partial or total knee replacement)
• Shoulder Replacement Surgery
• Hip Replacement Surgery
• Knee/Shoulder Arthroscopy
• Spinal Surgeries
• Joint Fusion
• Pelvic Fracture Surgery
• Angioplasty
• Stent graft placement
• Thrombolytic therapy
• Arterioctomy
• Aneurysm Repair Surgery
The project will support eligible patients admitted to Mercy-USA contracted hospitals for one-time assistance with all diagnostic tests, hospital fees, physician consultations, physician fees, medications, and medical supplies used and required for the surgeries (100% of the hospitalization bill) during the hospital stay.
The activities are summarized below
1- Assessment, shortlisting, and selection of hospitals.
Mercy-USA will map hospitals in the most vulnerable areas identified through the latest MSNA findings (Akkar, El Koura, Aley, El Minieh-Denieh, and Zahleh among others) and then select potential hospitals based on the highest assessment score.
2- MoUs signature with selected hospitals.
3- Review and validation of referrals received from hospitals for Lebanese and migrants on a case-by-case basis based on the eligibility criteria and project budget. Mercy-USA staff will assess the eligibility documents for each patient and inform the hospital and beneficiary on the approval status.
4-Coordination with hospitals and Following Up with Patients throughout the surgical process to ensure timely admission and smooth discharge of patients.
The cases will be tracked using an online tracking tool labeled “ Surgery Cases Tracking Sheet”
5- Issuance of commitment letters to the contracted hospitals.
The assigned staff will prepare commitment letters to the hospitals detailing the financial contribution of Mercy-USA.
6- Monthly payment processing and settlement to the relevant hospitals.
Payments will be made based on the final hospital bill and within 30 days of the presentation of the agreed-upon supporting documentation.
7- Monitoring visits to the contracted hospitals to monitor the quality of services being provided
Activities pertinent to outcome 3:
8- Ensure proper M and E activities, Referral and complaint mechanisms are in place.
10- Coordination with the health sector migrant sector, and other partners
Coordination will be ensured to better map the areas of intervention and guarantee a harmonized coordinated response among partners such as SAMS and La Chaine D’espoir.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

Mercy-USA incorporates the protection mainstreaming principles through its implementation. All newly onboarded staff as well as existing staff will receive training on relevant Mercy-USA policies and procedures including distribution planning and monitoring, CFM, PSEA, child safeguarding, data collection and privacy, code of conduct, time record keeping, and management. Mercy-USA has recently added a new position Protection and Complaints Officer to follow the complaints and referral mechanism across all of Mercy-USA’s Projects. Also, the Protection and Complaints officer will ensure the spreading of protection knowledge among project staff through the implementation of training and sensitization activities and/or taking part in training conducted by the different sectors or different organizations. In terms of Safety & Security, a specialized Security Officer was newly recruited to the project for the 2023 implementation period.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

Mercy-USA will ensure accountability through different mechanisms: M&E activities that will include satisfaction surveys and spot-check visits to contracted hospitals, feedback and complaints mechanism, and Referral. Mercy-USA will ensure community engagement and awareness by informing the beneficiaries about the available services, all details about the project, as well as the feedback and complaints mechanisms through awareness and follow-up on the reporting channels like complaints box, hotline number, and satisfaction survey. This will support the development of self-protection capacities and assist people to claim their rights. The Protection and complaints officer in coordination with the M&E officer will enhance accountability to affected populations while providing guidance, and conducting safe referrals when required. Flyers will be disseminated to partners to enhance the outreach of eligible patients and posters will be hanged inside each contracted hospital.
