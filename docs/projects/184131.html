Project Summary

UNICEF’s WASH humanitarian response will focus on the provision of and sustaining access to lifesaving basic water sources and adequate sanitation facilities and promoting personal and environmental hygiene for 2,250,000 most vulnerable conflict, WASH related epidemics and natural disaster affected non-refugees’ population and hosting communities in North, South, East, West and Central Darfur, South, West and North Kordofan, Blue Nile, White Nile, Sennar, Kassala, Gedarif and Red Sea States and Abyei.

1 - Needs

Despite the threat of COVID-19 and as per Sudan 2022 HNO, 11.07 million population lack handwashing facilities with soap and water. There are also 4.3 million in acute need of basic water services, and 8.4 million require limited/improved sanitation services. As per the 2021 National Multi-Hazard Health Emergency Plan 2021, 3.1 million people are at risk of water-related diseases. More than 11 million people reside in high-risk areas for vector-borne diseases, specifically malaria. In 2022, WASH Sector is planning to target 1.9 m. in need population with basic water, 1.8 m. with adequate sanitation and 4.7 m. with hygiene promotion interventions. Gender inequality is widely existing among the project targeted population, especially with regard to decision making processes, division of labor among family members and gender-based violence

2 - Activities/Outputs

Expected Outputs: - Access to basic gender-sensitive water supply increased and sustained for 350,000 additional unserved emergency-affected populations. - 1,500,000 affected people provided with operation and maintenance support for their existing water sources and/or water treatment/disinfection for their water sources, transportation means and household storage. - Access to gender-sensitive and disabled-friendly adequate sanitation facilities increased and sustained for 90,000 additional unserved emergency affected population; - 1,800,000 emergency-affected people reached with hygiene promotion interventions with focus on COVID-19 prevention including handwashing with soap. Activities: -Strengthening WASH Sector humanitarian coordination forum with focus on the coordination and harmonization of WASH sector planning, advocacy, implementation, monitoring and resource mobilization. -Construction/rehabilitation of gender and disabled people sensitive sustainable durable basic water sources with due consideration to the Integrated Water Resources Management (IWRM) and climate change adaptation. -Operation and maintenance and water chlorination for the existing water supply sources, transportation means and HH level. Empowering affected populations using Community Management for Operation and Maintenance (CMOM) approach with focus on women, girls and adolescents. -Construction/rehabilitation of gender and disabled people sensitive household and community sanitation facilities. - Community mobilization for household latrines construction using Community Led Total Sanitation (CLTS) approach within focus on women, girls and adolescent’s contribution. - Personal and environmental Hygiene promotion campaigns with household visits focusing on COVID-19 IPC related prevention including handwashing with soap and distribution of IPC supplies. -Construction/rehabilitation of schools and health/nutrition centres WASH services with focus on COVID-19 related centres. -Activation and strengthening evidence-based M&E and IM systems with gender sensitive information using up to date, innovative, monitoring tools. -Institutions and human resources capacity building for sector partners with focus on managerial, technical, equipment, logistic and motivation capacities.
