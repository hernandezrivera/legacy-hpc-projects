Project Summary

The project has 3 main objectives:
1- To reduce the risk posed by explosive hazards and the likelihood of exposure to them through implementing the Risk Education pillar of Mine Action Sector and raising the awareness and knowledge of civilians and humanitarian workers through implementing tailored RE programming in 4 subdistricts: Afrin, Jandairis, Bulbul, and Sharan-Afrin district.
This objective will be achieved through conducting RE sessions in the mentioned locations, targeting both children and adults in schools, Bahar's community centres, camps, and public spaces; as well as, conducting training for community focal points and humanitarian workers, as an indirect approach to disseminate the knowledge of RE.
2- Enhance equitable access to quality Child Protection services including (1) community-based psychosocial support and AR, and (2) quality specialized CP services, mainstreaming children with disabilities, in Afrin sub district.
The CP objective entails CFS-based and outreach-based approaches; where CFS-based activities include:
- Establishment of CFS which will be tailored to be inclusive for CwDs i.e. constructing ramps and rehabilitating the wash facilities to be CwDs-friendly, and providing transportation.
- Structured and sustained PSS program
- Parenting program
- Life skills for adolescents >13 years
- Case management & Referrals for children survivors of violence, exploitation, abuse, neglect, child labor, early marriage, children exposed to severe/extreme psychosocial distress, cases of CAAFAG, UASC.
While for outreach-based activities:
- AR for children and adults and IEC campaigns
- Semi-structured PSS
- Referrals of children to the CFS and services in other sectors for a comprehensive response.
- Implementation of emergency response activities and coordinating the protection emergency response amongst Protection organization in Afrin district, given that Bahar is the Protection emergency response FP for the district.
Note that there will be 2 outreach teams covering Afrin SD and implementing these activities and targeting schools, community and IDPs centers, and public spaces.
3- The GBV component aims to provide access for MWBGs survivors to life-saving specialized GBV response services provided at 1 WGSS in Afrin SD, and increase their access to the specialized CM services through disseminating entry points to these services through outreach and statics-based entry points including: - GBV Focal Point in each of Bahar's fixed clinics in Afrin conducting information-sharing sessions on GBV, providing non-focused PSS, targeting the clinics in a room dedicated for GBV services and referring survivors and at risk women & girls to the case managers to be provided with CM services/PSS, coordinate closely with medical staff to strengthen mutual referrals and provide them with orientation sessions on referral pathways.
- Outreach teams covering the SD of Afrin and implementing AR program and information sharing sessions targeting all the community groups, including men and adolescent boys, and referring survivors, to the WGSSs to be provided by CM services, given that Bahar will provide transportation for the referred cases; securing in result, wide coverage of entry points to GBV specialized services.
- Psychosocial support workers based at the WGSS and implementing GBV AR program utilizing the AR toolkit, and My Safety- My Well-being program for adolescent girls. Note that the WGSSs-based GBV activities will also act as an entry point for women and girls to be aware of the importance of seeking help and disclose their cases.
- Quality specialized GBV CM services.
PwDs will be targeted through center and community AR program, specialized services, and supported by transportation from and to the WGSS which will be rehabilitated to be inclusive for PwDs.
Bahar has developed an ethical closure plan and shared it with the GBV SC and will ensure regular updating of the closure plan as needed.

4 - Needs

MA: “Without the mine action response, explosive hazards will continue to pose a threat to civilians” HRP 2021.According to the baseline survey analysis shared by the MASC: The vulnerability of children, teenagers, women, IDPs to EO is high, due to the combination of their exposure to EO (42%) and their inability to recognize the danger putting them at increased risk.According to 2021 HNO, high levels of past and ongoing hostilities have contributed to far-reaching explosive hazard contamination in 2,562 communities in the last 2 years, resulting in serious injury and death, long-term physical and psychological trauma for survivors and their families.CP: according to the PMTF, childing dropout of schools and child marriage are the most common violations occurring.94.7% of the KIs in Aleppo reported that child labour prevents children to attend schools, and this’s not related to COVID-19 as the majority of the KIs stated, also, 37% of KIs in Aleppo reported that children separation exists; Finally, more than 20% of Aleppo KIs confirmed that B&G experiences psychological distress.GBV: “COVID-19 period increased violence because people stayed at home because of ban, streets were empty, and kidnapping and harassment cases increased” (Adult woman in Atareb).As per the HNO 2021, despite the I/NGO efforts invested in raising the community’s awareness on the harmful impacts of early marriage, more than 36% of the KIs said that early marriage is very common in their community, which is more than the double of last year’s percentage. 7.1 - Please list any risks related to sexual exploitation and abuse that have been identified during the project design phase and any risk mitigation and prevention measures that have been adopted and built into the project

Bahar has a Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse PSEA policy that usually is attached to each employee’s job contract clarifying all the related procedures. Bahar will ensure the field teams attend PSEA\safeguarding trainings and refreshing sessions provided by Bahar internally or externally. Bahar’s PSEA focal points responsibility is to ensure training are delivered to the project staff, informative materials (either soft copies in case of online or in-place approach of implementation) are distributed to both staff and beneficiaries, and updating and develop the PSEA policies including the complains pathway/reporting mechanism regularly. The MEAL team will monitor if any danger is threatening the safety of the beneficiaries and will report findings in order to take the appropriate measures. Bahar team will also inform the community, particularly men and youth, about the objective of the project and that its aim in order to mitigate exposing women and girls to any potential harm or violence.

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk 1: COVID-19 pandemic may impose lack of beneficiaries’ access and participation in services due to curfew restrictions, home lockdown, and isolation, or people’s fear of physical interaction, as well as, the in-place approach might expose the participant to the risk of getting infected.Mitigation Measure 1: Bahar will provide sanitizers and Personal Protection Equipment PPE to beneficiaries accessing the facilities and also during the outreach activities and will ensure applying other prevention measures including social distancing, minimizing the groups sessions participants to 5-7 max. Beneficiaries will be informed also about measures taken to encourage them to access the services.Bahar was also a member of the GBV SC COVID-19 Task Force and will implement the assistance according to the guidance provided for GBV COVID-19 guidance and the other Sub sectors’ guidance and instructions as well when feasible. Finally, COVID-19 AR messages will be mainstreamed and disseminated along the course of the project.Risk 2: Breaching Bahar’s CoC, safeguarding, and PSEA policies by the project staff against the beneficiaries in an intended or unintended way.Mitigation Measure 2: Bahar includes these policies in the employees’ contracts and they are being delivered with orientation sessions by the HR department or the FP. In addition, Bahar has assigned 1 FP to conduct trainings on those policies, receive the allegations and act accordingly, ensure timely response and investigate the allegation in order to take suitable action against the person alleged against.
