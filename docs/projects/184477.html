Project Summary

Most part of Lower Juba Region and especially Afmadow District have been experiencing the dry spill like many other locations in Somalia. Majority of the areas in Afmadow have recorded less than
extremely low rains. The depletion of pasture undermined almost all forms of livelihood. In Afmadow, for example, most pastoralists have reported lost on average up to 30to 40% of their goats and sheep due to drought from September to December 2021. Livestock prices have been declining since August as has the terms of trade between goats and cereals . The delay and even total halt of agricultural activities caused by poor rainfall have not only affected income of farmers but also seasonal agricultural wage labour. Worse still, price of necessity has been on the rising. Taking water as an example, 300%increase in cost has been observed since mid 2021 with a 200ltre barrel of water costing $15in some parts of Lower Juba. Milk price have also doubled since February 2021, depriving protein intake of many, especially the most vulnerable . According to an Interagency drought assessment report for Afmadow in September 2021,an estimated 120,000 people of rural Afmadow districts are at risk of food insecurity and need some cushioning against the negative impact of the drought If no assistance is provided, communities’ are facing threat of slipping into famine in the coming months. It is with this that SAAH is proposing an intervention that will see. Provision of essential food items to targeted drought affected populations in Afmadow District Lower Juba Region .SAAH Somalia seek to carry out conditional and non conditonal cash-based food voucher intervention in Afmadow districts in Lower Juba Region, Somalia to increase communities’ ability to access food. The other essential need will be to restore/build key community assets which can benefit the communities’ in the long run. The intervention will target vulnerable households who are able and willing to engage in Food Voucher program. To avoid creating dependency, the project will conditionally provide beneficiaries with 80%of daily food expenditure needs based on the minimum monthly food expenditure basket for 6months. It is expected the beneficiaries are able to access the remaining 20%through their normal market strategies to meet their 2,100Kcal nutrition need recommended daily requirement per person as per SPHERE standard. Women and people living with disability will be given priority.
Provision of agricultural inputs (vegetable seeds) support to farming households in Afmadow district.
Poor seed quality and access: Because of chronic poverty, most farmers have not been able to replace the seeds due to poor financial capacity. As a result the farmers are using recycled seeds whose viability has been reduced due to pests and diseases, poor growing conditions (lack of moisture). This results in under-utilization of their family labour and subsequently plant very small areas before the moisture gets depleted. This failure to utilize the moisture fully when it rains and the subsequent of small cultivation plots results in low yields and food insecurity. To make sure families fully utilize their family labour and take advantage to plant large areas when it rains, and weed their crops, SAAH proposes to support the benefiting communities with seeds. The farmers will be provided with farming inputs which includes seed kit as well as training on good agricultural practices. Key livelihood assets such as livestock water catchments, irrigation canals and access roads will be rehabilitated through the food voucher program. This will not only ensure access to food items but lasting resilience building through the constructed communal assets. Its worth noting that Afmadow has high potential for agriculture which hasn't been fully exploited as a result of dilapidated water canals and lack of certified seeds. Priority will be given to female headed Households and those living with disabilities.

1. - Projects activities

Distribute Food Vouchers to targeted eligible beneficiaries in Lower Juba Region. Conditional and Non Conditional food vouchers will be provided on a monthly basis to 16,600 targeted beneficiaries in Lower Juba for a period of 6 months; unconditional vouchers will be supplied to 10%of the vulnerable targeted caseload/HHs. This assistance will address acute food needs by bolstering the purchasing power of targeted households through the projected lean season. SAAH will involve the community benefiting from the VFW programme in the design and, where possible, administration of the project activities. This includes planning the work to be undertaken, selecting participants, determining wage levels (slightly below market wages), keeping records, supervising works and monitoring. This approach helps to ensure that the community feels ownership for the programme itself and for the assets created by it. Rehabilitation of community assets such as water pans shallow wells ,access roads and irrigation canals through communal labour activities. People Living with disabilities will be given priority including those female headed Households Provide technical support to targeted agro pastoralist through agronomists in Afmadow Districts Lower Juba. The project shall target 50 productive groups in Afmadow(each group made of 20-25people) who are able to participate in on-farm related enterprises. Each group shall be trained in modern agronomic practices and supported with farm inputs (garden tools, seeds). During the selection process women headed household will be given more priority, this will also include people living with disabilities. SAAH will establish a complaint and feedback mechanism that will be used by the beneficiaries to report on any issues regarding the project. The identification of beneficiaries will pay particular attention to gender-related vulnerabilities and where possible, female will represent at least 60% of the beneficiaries. Age, gender, and diversity will be mainstreamed in the identification and registration process, to ensure that women, youth and miniority groups are identified and targeted, and that there is evidence that they benefit from this intervention. Accountability to affected populations in this project is anchored around IASC endorsed via global protection cluster, protection mainstreaming four principles are as follows; Prioritize safety and dignity and avoid causing harm, Meaningful access, Accountability, Participation, and empowerment.

2.1.1 - What is the justification for using cash or vouchers instead of in kind?

Use of cash is likely to be risky and especially when dealing with bulky cash,this might exposure the beneficiaries to insecurity i.e theft, mugging and also expose women to Gender based violence , Vouchers are more efficient and easy to redeem. In kind may cause a lot of inconveniences and especially where transportation of commodities is involve due to bad roads, poor weather conditions and also restriction of movements.
