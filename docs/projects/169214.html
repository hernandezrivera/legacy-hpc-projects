Project Summary

As part of its response to the Beirut explosion, WVL will ensure that affected households in Beirut have access to personal hygiene items including material and awareness related to COVID-19 prevention. WVL will also ensure that affected households have access to water and sanitation services through the rehabilitation of damaged water tanks and pipes as well as the distribution of clean water to households when needed.
WVL will ensure that its WASH activities complement activities under shelter and other sectors for best impact. It will aim to reach 12000 households in Achrafieh, Gemmayze, Mar Mkhayel, Rmeil, Geitawi, Bourj Hammoud, Sin El Fil, Mazraa, Karantina, Basta, Khandaa al Ghamee and Bachoura. Coordination on exact locations will be ensured through the sector coordination mechanism.

1 - Needs

Water services in the neighbourhoods closest to the explosion have been affected, including damage or complete destruction of water tanks. Findings from the LRC assessment showed the need for water storage as well as water trucking services due to damages water networking and shortage in water supplies . Drinking water is also needed until water tanks have been repaired/installed and the damaged water pipes have been rehabilitated. The current destruction and damage to essential water systems has led to a two-fold challenge within the escalating concerns of COVID-19, including increased exposure of vulnerable households to COVID-19 transmission as clean up efforts take place but within the context of limited water and soap to properly wash hands; and secondly, economic constraints are likely to force affected households to prioritize other needs (e.g. repairs in damaged apartment houses) over the purchase of hygiene items. Personal protective equipment (PPE) kits will be required to prevent additional spread of COVID-19 and reduce the impact on an overstretched health system. In fact as per WVL's assessment in affected communities, 45% of surveyed households mentioned that WASH/shelter was a priority need with 70% of them highlighting a need for hygiene kits and 12% highlighting a need for drinking water.

2 - Activities

Distribution of hygiene and disinfection kits WVL will seek to meet the most urgent needs for hygiene and sanitation items particularly considering the ongoing and escalating COVID-19 situation in the country, anticipated to be further exacerbated as a result of this latest disaster. To ensure proper hygiene and COVID-19 prevention measures are in place, the project plans to distribute hygiene and disinfection kits to the communities affected by the recent explosion. Awareness raising on COVID-19 will go hand in hand with the emergency distributions as COVID-19 cases are predicted to increase after the explosion with people unable to maintain proper hygiene or social distancing measures. As part of the rehabilitation works conducted in damaged apartments, WVL's engineers will evaluate the need for water and sanitation rehabilitation. Based on this assessment, households will be provided with essential life-saving water and shelter solutions. WV will use several modalities under this outcome including direct provision of service to the most vulnerable households by hiring skilled labor, provision of cash/value vouchers to support families to purchase required items and conduct their own repairs and cash for work to skilled labourers to complete medium scale repairs. Further, water trucking will be offered to households in urgent need of a safe water source until they can repair damaged water tanks or pipe systems. The intervention that WV will be following while doing the rehabilitation in shelter and WASH will take into consideration the environmental intervention, meaning that the team will be promoting the usage of the water saving and energy saving methodologies while ensuring energy conservation measures whenever applicable. In addition to the above, WV will liaise with the social stability working group to make the needed recommendation for an environmentally friendly interventions of proper waste disposal.
