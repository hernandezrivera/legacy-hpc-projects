Project Summary

Since the start of South Sudan political crisis, there has been mass displacement of populations with the UNHCR projecting more than 3.1 million people to have crossed borders to other countries like Uganda, Kenya, Ethiopia, Democratic republic of Condo and Sudan.
During the crisis, most of the basic infrastructure like roads, health facilities, and schools were destroyed, WASH facilities have not be left out in this crisis pitting the populations in dire need of humanitarian assistance.
Water borne infections have been rampant in the country with sporadic cholera outbreaks being reported over the years. The most recent cholera outbreak was reported in Kapoeta state September 2017 and affected more than 23 counties according to UNICEF September 2017 report.
Open defecation remains one of the major setbacks to proper hygiene and sanitation. This practice has caused contamination of water sources therefore causing major public health concern.
In Ikotos county, water points have been dilapidated by the crisis and need serious rehabilitation to enable the populations get clean water for drinking and domestic use for to the required standards of 15 liters per day per person.
Lack of WASH facilities has encouraged open defecation practices therefore compounding the already dire situation.
Due to lack of disintegrated WASH facilities, women and girls tent to relieve themselves in the bushes therefore increasing the risk of sexual abuse and gender based violence. Lack of menstrual hygiene kits to mothers and school going children also poses a major risk to the mothers and the girls often miss classes during the menstrual period.
This project wishes to address the WASH needs in Ikotos by rehabilitating existing WASH facilities to enable the populations have clean and safe water for drinking.
The project will also ensure that water points in the health facilities, OTP and Stabilization centers have clean and safe water for drinking to enable the malnourished children, pregnant and lactating mothers have clean water for drinking and for domestic use.
The project will also ensure that segregated latrines are rehabilitated and others constructed in schools to ensure that the women and girls have safe places to relieve themselves therefore reducing cases of gender based violence and sexual abuse.
The project will also distribute menstrual hygiene kits to mothers and school going girls to ensure that their dignity is well observed and that the school going girls don’t miss school days due to menses.
Trainings on minimum WASH package to health and nutrition workers will be done and awareness campaigns will be done to improve community knowledge on the importance of proper hygiene and sanitation. Behavioral change communication will be done to improve the sanitation standards of the people in Ikotos county.

Needs

Ikotos county is in dire need of humanitarian assistance. The political crisis in South Sudan affected most of the was facilities. There are 5487 IDPs still in the IDP camps in Ikotos county. With the signing of the peace agreement, it’s expected that there will be influx of returnees in to the county which will further strain the available water points and WASH facilities.Open defecation is a serious concern in the county and its aggravated by lack of latrines in the communities and in schools. This exposes women and school going children to danger of sexual abuse as they go to the bushes to relieve themselves. It also increases the chances of contaminating the water points thus increasing the risk of water borne disease outbreaks. The already existing latrines have been destroyed and need urgent rehabilitation in order to avert disease outbreaks and reduce cases of GBV.Lack of menstrual hygiene kits has exposed the women and school going children to infections by using unhygienic ways of managing menstruation.Health facilities and nutrition treatment centers lack clean and safe water for drinking and therefore water points in the health facilities need to be rehabilitated to provide clean water to this vulnerable populations.Water treatment tablets will also need to be distributed to the community in order to ensure that they take clean and safe water for drinking and for domestic use. This will greatly reduce cases of water borne disease outbreaks and cholera.The Ebola outbreak in DRC puts Ikotos county as a high priority County due to the fact that its bordering Uganda. There will be need to create awareness on hand washing practices and other sanitation practices which can greatly reduce the risk of transmission of this deadly disease.

Activities

1. Construction and rehabilitation of water pointsWater points like boreholes and the other water sources will be constructed and those in bad condition will be rehabilitated in order to ensure that the communities get clean and safe water to the required standards of 15 liters per day per person. Water points in health facilities and nutrition centers will also be rehabilitated to ensure that all the pregnant and lactating mothers and children with acute malnutrition have access to safe water for drinking and domestic use.2. Construction and rehabilitation of latrinesThe project will construct and segregated latrines which will help in reducing cases of open defecation and reduce disease burdens caused by water contamination. The latrines will also help reduce cases of gender based violence and sexual abuse by ensuring that women and girls have a safe place to relieve themselves.3. Distribution of menstrual hygiene kitsMenstrual hygiene kits will be distributed to mothers of childbearing age and school going girls. This will preserve the dignity of the mothers and the girls and will also ensure that the school going girls don’t miss classes during the menses period.4. Distribution of hygiene kitsThe distribution of hygiene kits will ensure that the communities have commodities for safe water storage and also water treatment tablets which will ensure that there is clean and safe water for drinking and for domestic use.5. Training health and nutrition workers on minimum WASH packagesHealth and nutrition workers will be trained on the minimum WASH package activities. They will be encouraged to disseminate the messages to the community and to the patients thus improving the knowledge on key WASH messages6. Formation and training of water management committeesThe water management committees will be formed and trained on how to ensure water safety and how to manage and rehabilitate water points. They will also be trained on how to disseminate key WASH messages to the communities in order to reduce water contamination.7. Promotion of hand washing activities to avert possible cholera and Ebola virus outbreaks.This sanitation and Hygienic practice will be promoted in the county to ensure that the beneficiaries practice hand washing in order to help reduce the risk of disease transmission .8. ReportingAll the project activities will be reported to the relevant ministry and the WASH cluster in order to ensure that proper planning is done to help improve service delivery.

Does the project provide beneficiaries with information?

Yes

How does the project provide beneficiaries with information?

The project will provide the beneficiaries with all the relevant information on the project activities and what they are entitled to. in order to prioritize the needs the community will be involved in identification of the water points to be rehabilitated and the community latrines to be rehabilitated. Through the formation of the water management committees the beneficiaries will be informed of all the necessary information on the project activities.

Are messages context-specific or/and tailored?

Yes

Briefly describe how

Visibility materials printed for the project will have key messages on WASH and some will be translated to the local languages in order to reach as many people as possible. Key messages on menstrual hygiene management will be disseminated to the school going girls and the mothers.

Protection from sexual exploitation and abuse

Yes

Briefly describe how

TADO has policies against sexual exploitation and abuse and every staff working on this project will have to sign this policy. There will be reporting mechanisms put in place to report cases of sexual exploitation and abuse which will protect the identity of the reporter.

Nature of the consultations

Yes

Briefly describe the nature of the consultations

TADO has an ongoing nutrition project in Ikotos county and has identified the needs through focus group discussions with the members of the communities. TADO has also a good relationship with the community leaders and the government and also did consultation with the community leaders and other key stakeholders.

Feedback /complaint mechanisms?

Yes

Briefly describe the mechanisms

TADO will have suggestion boxes within the operation health and nutrition facilities. TADO will also carry out key informant interviews and focus group discussions to find out the impact of the project and what need to be improved. TADO will also engage community leaders who are the spokesmen of the community to find out grievances and ways of addressing them.

Community Engagement?

For proper monitoring and evaluation, community feedback will be put in to consideration to ensure that the project runs as expected. All positive feedback will be improved further and the negative feedback will be rectified to ensure that the community feels ownership of the project.
