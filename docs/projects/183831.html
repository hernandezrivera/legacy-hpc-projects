Project Summary

Gender based violence GBV, still consider as life threatening condition in South Darfour ( Nyala ), by contribution of many factors including conflicts and nature disasters.
The results conducted by RPSDO members on 2021 reflect that sexual and domestic violence is spread out largely, this along with harmful practices for example: child marriage aged between 12 to 17 years and female genital mutilation.
The project is a group of intervention aiming on awareness raising for prevention , mitigation and /or response of GBV in both small and large scale. In addition to support community based protection networks.
Targeting young aged ones, women and community by a collection of awareness sessions, training on peace building and specialized courses (ToT) on GBV. These along with providing trainers by life skills and recreational activities .
Community awareness raising of GBV will lead to pshycosocial support for survivors.
