Project Summary

This project aims to support Saint Vincent and the Grenadines health system's continuity of operations and full recovery in the aftermath of Hurricane Beryl to guarantee availability of and access to essential health services to affected populations. The proper recuperation of health services operations is critical not only to support care delivery to individuals in need of health assistance post-Hurricane Beryl but also to prevent exacerbated damage caused by additional storms that could impact the country in the near future as part of the above-normal 2024 Hurricane season. The project will focus on restoring essential health services, with particular emphasis on the heavily impacted Southern Grenadines, while enhancing the country's capacity to prevent and respond to potential disease outbreaks. Main objectives: Reestablish access to health care services in most affected areas, including mental health and psychosocial support, with a focus on the Smart health facilities in Union Island and Mayreau,

Project Activities

• Perform comprehensive damage assessments of health facilities., • Assist in the clean-up, repairs and rehabilitation of damaged health infrastructure in affected facilities throughout the islands., • Procure, temporarily store and distribute essential medicines, vaccines, and medical supplies to support healthcare delivery in affected islands., • Implement risk communication strategies and disseminate information to prevent communicable and non-communicable diseases, including social mobilization regarding vector control, prevention of waterborne and vector-borne diseases, including dengue., • Procure and distribute NCD kits to support continuity of care for patients with non-communicable diseases in affected areas., • Provide and install equipment to restore the vaccine cold chain in affected health facilities., • Supply generators and water pumps to restore power and water supply to damaged health facilities, ensuring proper functioning of the cold chain., • Enhance capacity of primary healthcare services to provide mental health and psychosocial support to families and community members impacted by Hurricane Beryl., • Strengthen real-time disease surveillance by procuring and distributing wireless communication devices to key public health personnel, enabling immediate reporting and monitoring of health conditions in affected communities., • Provide rapid diagnostic tests for priority diseases, including COVID-19 and dengue, Reproduction and dissemination of good hygiene practices, including food and water safety and sanitation promotion, in shelters and affected communities., • Provision of equipment and supplies for clean water in the affected communities, active shelters, and health centers, such as water tanks, chlorine tablets, water testing kits, etc. • Supply and install water quality monitoring equipment in health facilities and key community locations., • Implement targeted vector control measures in affected communities, including distribution of supplies, environmental management interventions, and community-level vector control activities., • Deployment of members of the Regional Response Team to assist the affected islands with damage and needs assessment in the health sector., • Collection and analysis of information to support evidence-based decision-making., • Logistical support to the reception of external assistance, including coordination of international emergency medical teams as well as management of supplies and equipment., • Provision of support for the management of national and local health coordination mechanisms, as well as coordination with other sectors involved in response operations.
