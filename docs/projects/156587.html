Project Summary

Since the beginning of the crisis in Syria, millions of Syrians were left in great need to all types of aids. Share Foundation has started early recovery interventions in 2017 and provided Micro-Loans to people with startups or existing businesses in order to allow them to create income-generating projects or to allow them to upscale their businesses. This project will be in line with the the third strategic objective of the HRP and ER Cluster priorities, which focus on resilience-oriented approach. The objective of this project is to provide multiple sustainable services to improve the livelihoods of the poor rural communities. The project provides a more sustainable option for the poor by providing a diverse range of services including loans, cash-for-work intervention, vocational training or direct use of the basic services provided.
The Project offers loans with no collateral requirements and at zero interest rates in Ma'arrat Tamasrin. The project will start with a portfolio of 100 thousand US dollar that will be granted to start-ups or existing businesses initiated by women in the selected location. There will be selection criteria done in consultation with the community and the inclusion of individuals from IDP's will be requested in order not to increase the tensions between the two component of the society. The repayment of the loans will be mobilized to other female entrepreneurs. Then the portfolio will be handed to a Committee that will be selected from the community (( mainly from the leaders and the well-performed start-up owners ) that will be trained on portfolio management and will constitute Community Women Fund that will be responsible for the project governance and cash control of the loans in the future.
The project aims to enhance the skills of displaced and hosting households (100 females and 100 males) in the north of Syria by providing a capacity building program on the basics of business planning, financial planning, building a business model and multiple vocational training in order to initiate successful entrepreneurship in their area and start up small businesses that could develop the society and generate new job vacancies for the other refugees ın the area. This intervention will target 200 beneficiaries under predefined criteria, At the end of each training the beneficiaries will be asked to develop project proposals with creative ideas then they will be evaluated and 16 creative start-ups or existing projects will be funded by 2000 $ for each in groups in order to be able to start their income-generating projects, then the new businesses will be supported for 4 months with all management consultancy and coaching in order to manage their businesses and be able to increase the successful of the businesses.

Needs

Close to eight years of crisis which has severely stretched the capacity and resilience of people living in Syria to cope with shocks and stresses brought about by the crisis. It has resulted in a tragic impact on development performance in Syria through destroying economic, social, and human capitals, which resulted in loss of jobs and livelihoods and the loss of productive assets and savings. Therefore the project came to address some of the gaps in the socioeconomic gaps in the targeted groups’ lives which is inline with ER Cluster objective of Strengthening access to livelihood by creating income generating opportunities. It opened the door to more job opportunities and employment in the eight past year .The interventions of providing Micro-loans and Entrepreneurship will be in Maarrat Tamsarin Which has about 30,000 PiN according to HNO 2019 estimation. According to the local council in Maarret Tamsarin the IDPs in Maarrat Tamsarin is about 15,000 to the over all population of about 85,000 population, as the project will target both host community and IDP to the area . According to an assessment done by Takaful Al Sham organization and shared by the ER Clusters showed that about 8061 of families in Maarrat Tamsarin are women-headed families which indicates the acute need to implement programs that target this group. The project intervention will depend on base line data collection which will be a consistent part of the project activities as we will be able to show outcomes of the project and to track the impact of the project on the individual lives and community as a whole.

Implementation of activities

The project activities which will be conducted after developing the project management plan, will be conducted by Share Foundation Field staff: 1- A base-line assessment will be conducted in the targeted locations to assess the exact livelihood needs. 2- Signing MOU with the Local Council. 3- Select the project team, the gender criteria will be taken into consideration. 4- Train the selected field team officers on basics of direct dealing with beneficiaries and on mechanisms and procedures of the program. 5- Announcement of the program intervention in the targeted area. 6- Contracting with the services providers. 7- Initiating the interventions of the project. 8- Continuous follow-up of the beneficiaries. 9- Continuous Monitoring, Evaluation and Learning. 10- Exit the project and handing the project activities to the local council or any related entity 11- Reporting

Protection Risk Analysis

Risks include:•Many women, adolescent girls and other at-risk groups will find work in the informal economy (e.g. engaging in domestic work with receptor or host communities). These activities may force them to travel through unsafe areas or during dangerous times of day or night.•Women and girls at-risk groups are particularly susceptible to exploitation, harassment and abuse from customers, suppliers and market administrators, especially in unregulated markets and when they must borrow money, negotiate prices or manage a shop alone.Mitigation measures•Provide women and girls at-risk groups with safe alternatives or generating income.•Enhance their knowledge and skills base of micro-enterprise, financial management, natural resource management and leadership.•Empower and foster their independence, which may increase their ability to leave exploitative situations.•Enhance economic, physical and psychological well-being of women and girls.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

1- Security situation and access disruption Mitigation : At the beginning of the project the loan officers will be selected and trained from the community 2- Fluctuation of the Exchange rate due to the presence of cash component in the projectThe loans to the families will be provided by the local currency in this case there will be no burden on the beneficiaries to bear the fluctuation.
