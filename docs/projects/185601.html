Project Summary

The Logistics Sector commenced operations in 2017 to support the Rohingya humanitarian response in Bangladesh when the existing national coordination capacity was unable to meet the needs on the ground. Now heading into year five of the response, operations are normalizing, commercial transport and storage is available, and partners have increased the capacity and robustness of their supply chains.
The Logistics Sector is adapting its operations to avoid redundancy and dependency on common services, maximize available resources, focus on locally led sustainable solutions and increase capacity strengthening and emergency preparedness.
The Logistics Sector strategy responds to the major constraints highlighted in a mid-year gaps and needs assessment. To support humanitarian logistics and supply partners in addressing these gaps, the Logistics Sector will support by:
- Continuing provision of coordination and information (amongst humanitarian agencies and with the government of Bangladesh)
- Strengthening capacity (with a focus on national NGO’s) to ensure smooth and sustainable transition to self-sufficient or inter-agency partnerships in supply chain operations
- Strengthening emergency response and preparedness initiatives to enhance supply chain resilience and allow continuous and unimpeded flow of humanitarian supplies in case of sudden onset emergencies.
In addition to the above, particular attention will be placed on initiatives to
- reducing the environmental impacts of logistics activities and increasing sustainability
- promoting and encouraging gender diversity in logistics and supply chain activities within the sector

Plan fields

1 - Identified Needs

An analysis of the logistics gaps and needs was carried out mid-2021: while there is now sufficient transport and storage options on the local market, i) coordination/provision of information, ii) preparedness and iii) capacity-strengthening remain greatly needed. Coordination is needed amongst partners for better utilization of resources and sharing of best practices but also with the government at Cox’s Bazar and Dhaka level for improved understanding of government processes.Preparedness activities have been ongoing however partners still feel there is a gap in their ability to rapidly scale-up for emergency response. They have also stated that physical road access constraints mapping (provided by the Logistics Sector) remained a priority during the cyclone/monsoon season.Last but not least, capacity strengthening is perceived as a continuing gap, and will be increased as part of the 2022 Strategy.

2 - Activities or outputs

Publish regular infographics, assessments, maps, and situation updates on the Logistics Sector website. - Hold regular coordination meetings for partners to exchange information and discuss logistics issues. - Support and advise partners on the logistics-related issues and advocate for streamlined policies and processes that improve logistics performance. - Maintain a dedicated coordination cell to strengthen horizontal cooperation among agencies and the synchronization of logistics efforts. - Maintain standby operational capacity and contingency stocks for rapid scale-up of activities. - Maintain and manage GIS and LogIE platforms for physical road access constraints in order to provide timely information updates during a sudden onset emergency. - Support humanitarian agencies in emergency response and preparedness with trainings, simulation exercises and advisory services. - Enhance resource utilization among humanitarian agencies in order to minimize overlaps and reduce duplication of efforts. - Participate in existing WGs on Environmental initiatives, form a WG to assess and improve practices in humanitarian Logistics operationsConduct and facilitate trainings and workshops to improve logistics performance. - Regularly assess and update logistics market capacity and costs.
