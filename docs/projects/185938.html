Project Summary

The impact of the NWSW crisis since 2016 till date keeps playing a negative role to education system in both regions. Continues slowdown in business, lockdown, loss of businesses by parents not leaving out school closure in some communities especially the hard to reach areas. Also during the outbreak of Covid-19 pandemic, Cameroon as whole came into a standstill in many activates through which additional need for income was at its pick. Many parents lost their jobs and had little or no means to educate their children. Children having 30% of available books with the inability to better assimilate what they learned in school through our need assessment during distribution of school kits. 73.4% were made of IDPs, while approximately 27% as host
We are targeting 5300 children with approximately 53000 exercise books and some other materials. We equally shall mainstream Covid-19 through the distribution so as to mitigate the spread of the virus and make a safer place for children at school. We shall be covering Urban and rural communities with special note of hard to reach areas such as Kupe Muanenguba, Ndian and Lebialem division. The monitoring and evaluation phase will take a community based approach with various stakeholders getting involve in project follow up. The project equally aims at promoting gender equality by getting involve male and female, persons with disabilities in management and monitoring stage still in all a community based approach. The budget of this project is seen to be 42,400USD including direct cost, operational, staff, monitoring and evaluation and logistics.

1 - Besoins / Needs

Since the reopening of schools on 5 October 2020, after a months-long period of school closure due to COVID-19 restrictions, a noticeable increase in school enrolment rates was followed by a spike in attacks against education. These aspects has caused children with special needs to lack access to and inadequate exposure to educational facilities and needs relevant for their academic, social, economic and physical development. This have also caused drop down of income through loss of businesses and jobs by some parents during displacements and lockdown as a result to the crisis and the outbreak of Covid-19. NICEF’s 2020 report stated that about 2000000 children in need of humanitarianAssistance (UNICEF HAC 2020) inclusive of 450,268 IDPs with returnees 203,634 in North West and South West of Cameroon. https://www.unicef.org/media/88216/file/Cameroon-Humanitarian-SitRep-October-2020.pdfAnother problem in addition to the Anglophone crisis which also affected children’sLearning access was the outbreak of Covid-19. Increased lockdown of schools in the entire Country made it a dual issue to both NWSW regions. Parents are facing difficulties to afford basic school need of their children. In 2020/2021 academic year prior to distribution of school kits, Miracle Charity Foundation had a need assessment to better understand the level vulnerability of some IDPs and host children through which 73.4% were made of IDPs, while approximately 27% as host. Many children has about 30% of available books with the inability to better assimilate what they learned in school. Distribution of a total of 7256 books within 7 primary schools within Mutengene was still not enough. The ongoing distribution through which we targeted 15000 books being for 5000 children will give an average of 3 books per child which still not material comparing the level of vulnerability they currently are. 50.6% of these reached out children were female while 49.4% male. Majority were schooling without birth certificates 68.5% while 2.7% were disabled. Miracle Charity Foundation intends to round up with the second phase of school kits distribution by December ending 2021 with a going concern to introduce a phase 3 of this same initiative. We shall be targeting a total of 5300 children with an average of 10 books per child. Not leaving pencils, erasers, pens and other important needs in order to give them a better live. We equally shall mainstream Covid-19 through the distribution so as to mitigate the spread of the virus and make a safer place for children at school. We shall be covering Urban and rural communities of Fako, Meme, Manyu with special note of hard to reach areas such as Kupe Muanenguba, Ndian and Lebialem division. The monitoring and evaluation phase will take a community based approach with various stakeholders getting involve in project follow up. The project equally aims at promoting gender equality by getting involve male and female, persons with disabilities in management and monitoring stage. Extraction of data from the Google sheet (data collected during the project) and run analysis using Excel and Power BI (Microsoft Power Business Intelligence) in order to come out with reports that can be used to understand overall reached and predict future change/sustainability. After overall analysis, a project report will be communicated to donors. This report can be communicated or shared through PowerPoint, words formats, pictures, videos and any other form of available feedback not leaving out proposals from donor. At the end of the evaluation period MICHAFOUND will make an overall reports showing planned, reached and gap analysis. The estimated budget for this project is seen to be 42,400USD including direct cost, operational, staff, monitoring and evaluation and logistics.
