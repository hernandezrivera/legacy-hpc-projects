Project Summary

ASDEPO's is planned to Providing Emergency nutrition service for conflict affected, IDP, drought, and host communities children under 5 years, pregnant and lactating's women and social medical case in five region, 19 woreda of ( Gambela, Oromia, Amhara, Somali and Benishangul-Gumuz, ASDEPO is Experienced organization in area of humanitarian response and presented in area of planning now. Proposed activities will focus on providing nutrition service (TSFP, OTP IYCF, BSFP and awareness creation on nutrition and health relative activities and focused gender based violence prevention and response services.
The project will address the specific needs and concerns of different gender and age groups through age and gender mainstreaming. The intervention will address the immediate needs of internally displaced and populations affected by the conflict and drought while supporting efforts to build local capacity and strengthen systems to respond better in response of emergency situation. The intervention will specifically target vulnerable populations such as women, particularly of child­bearing age, PLW, infant and young children, and children under five elders and disabled communities.
