Project Summary

The persistent threat of mines and cluster munitions hinders the return of land for agricultural and other productive purposes, impairing the socio-economic recovery of the region. MAG Multitask Teams will conduct a combination of Manual Mine Clearance (MMC) and Battle Area Clearance (BAC) in order to clear and release as much land as possible back to the communities. MAG explosive ordnance disposal (EOD) teams are designed to respond to EOD spot tasks reports that are reported outside of MAG’s task sites.
Comprehensive risk education session from MAG community liaisons team are essential to raise awareness among the local population about the dangers posed by mines and cluster munitions. Integrating risk education into the clearance efforts will empower individuals to identify and report hazardous areas, thereby enhancing community safety.

1. Needs

The legacy of decades of conflict in South Sudan has left behind a substantial contamination of mines and cluster munitions, resulting in widespread insecurity and endangering the lives of civilians. These explosive remnants of war not only cause physical harm but also instil fear and restrict access to land crucial for agriculture and other economic activities. South Sudan’s vulnerability to climate change and natural disasters compounds the country’s humanitarian crisis, jeopardizing recovery, and undermining development efforts. These environmental challenges have had a devastating impact on the country's agricultural sector, which is a vital source of livelihood for much of the population. The climate crisis has resulted in decreased agricultural productivity and crop failures, leading to food insecurity and malnutrition among many communities in South Sudan. Landmines and unexploded ordnance still pose a significant threat to farmers, limiting their access to fertile land for cultivation. The presence of landmines and unexploded ordnance not only endangers the lives of farmers but also restricts their ability to expand agricultural activities and maximize their land use. Efforts to clear landmines through mine action can release vast areas of fertile land that can be utilized for agriculture. By releasing cleared land communities can harness the full potential of their land resources, improve agricultural productivity, and build resilience to the adverse effects of climate change. Over recent years the country has suffered repeated cycles of droughts and flooding, this worsens an already critical humanitarian situation marked by severe food insecurity, economic decline, continued conflict, disease outbreaks, and the repercussions of the Sudan conflict. The war in Sudan and the subsequent influx of returnees and refugees into the South Sudan continues to exert additional strain on the fragile humanitarian context. South Sudan’s economy, which is heavily dependent on oil production, has been severely disrupted by the conflict in Sudan along with unrest in the Red Sea region. These disruptions have led to a continued deterioration of macroeconomic conditions, marked by a rapid depreciation of the local currency and increased foreign exchange volatility. In addition to sharp increases in food prices, economic hardship for the local population is further exacerbated by the government’s failure to pay civil servants, along with reduced humanitarian funding. The immediate impact of this worsening macroeconomic situation is expected to be severe in Addressing the clearance of landmines and cluster munitions, implementing risk education programs, and investing in sustainable development initiatives are crucial steps towards mitigating the impact of these hazards and fostering a conducive environment for agricultural growth and socio-economic prosperity in South Sudan. By prioritizing mine action efforts, promoting community resilience, and supporting sustainable development initiatives, South Sudan can overcome the challenges posed by landmines and cluster munitions, paving the way for enhanced agricultural productivity and socio-economic development for vulnerable people.

2. Project activities

The key response activities under this project are: Manual Mine Clearance, Battle Area Clearance, EOD spot tasks, Explosive Ordnance Risk Education sessions to communities and institutes.
