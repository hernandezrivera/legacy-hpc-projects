Project Summary

- Support Government and partners in regular and comprehensive protection assessments to inform continued response and preparedness plans across all areas affected by natural and man-made emergencies in country
- Continued mapping and consolidation of referral pathways including in justice and HLP sectors
- Support mainstreaming efforts including identification of vulnerability criteria for humanitarian assistance, providing added focus on protection needs of vulnerable groups inter alia, persons with disabilities, older persons, child headed households, and persons living with HIV/AIDS
-Provide guidance to humanitarian responders and affected population on relevant legal standards including on house, land and property in the context of resettlement and durable solutions
- Support engagement with justice system and assist humanitarian community with advocacy and referral of cases including around issues reported through relevant hotlines and communicatios with affected population exercises
-Support operationalisation and mainstreaming of protection strategy in the humanitarian response with community involvement and local NGOs. Capacity building of government and national protection entities and local stakeholders on protection in humanitarian settings
