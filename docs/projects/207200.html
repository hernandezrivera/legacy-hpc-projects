Basic Info

Project Name

Start Date

01/01/2024

End Date

31/12/2024

Project Summary

The on-going “Crisis” in the NW/SW Regions of Cameroon which breaks out since 2016/incorporated with Cholera outbreak in 2022/flood disaster in 2023/landslide in 2023 in the NWSW regions due to climate change, has resulted in increased displacements of people, high risk of hygiene-related morbidity/mortality rates. 1.0M among the 1.3M PiN of humanitarian assistance in WASH/Health & 638,421 IDPs/442,580 Returnees.to be targeted including PIN-TARGET WASH HNO-HRP2024 of 20 Nov. 2023 which is 787,8K
DEMOGRAPHIC INFORMATION: Plan intervention in 13 Subdivisions/10 hospitals & health districts in 26 villages in rural, urban/semi-urban in Batibo, Kumbo, Nkambe, Ndu, Fundong, Bamenda 2 & 3, Wum, Buea, Limbe, Tiko, Upper Bayang/Mamfe in 6 Divisions (Momo, Bui, Donga Mantung, Boyo, Mezam, Menchum, Fako/Manyu) in the NWSW Regions of Cameroon. Targeted: 20,000 (dissag. by gender, age, disability of 3,986 Boys/4,014 Girls; 3,667 Men/4,000 Women) >18yrs; (1,320 Men/1,346 Women) +60yrs, PWDs (827 Men/840 Women).
AIM:
To improved safe drinking water, hygiene related services to reduced death rates to children >5yrs and poverty to affected population by 31st December 2024.

-# of water user committees trained

ACTIVITIES
-Promotion of safe hygiene/sanitation practices amongst vulnerable people
-Construction of water points (boreholes, small water supply network..)
-Construction of gender-sensitive latrines in IDP sites/host communities
-Capacity-building of WASH actors in emergency WASH response/relative issues
-Ensuring availability of essential healthcare in areas affected by the crisis
OBJECTIVES
-Improve sustainable access to basic sanitation/safe drinking water to vulnerable people
-Reduce the risk of poor hygiene-related morbidity/mortality of affected population in the crisis
-Reinforce Capacities/coordination of local actor to improve WASH services/resilience of affected populations
BENEFICIARIES
Affected population (IDPs, returnee’s/host communities/ minorities group) of Infants, Boys, Girls, Men, Women, Elderly people/PWDs of Gender, Age, Disability/Diversity mainstreaming i.e. 50% IDPs, 10% PWDs, 30% Host Communities/10% returnees
METHODOLOGY: Sensitisation/drilling.
APPROACH: Community based approach, awareness raising campaign (door-to-door sensitization)/radio talks, standard visibility/IEC materials including PSEA, collaboration with stakeholders/organised workshops, community mobilization/engagement/construction /M&E. IDPs/the communities are consulted/participate in the conception of the entire project cycle as decision-makers/local management/ownership. Rapid HH Survey, FGDs/report from communities, AAP/PSEA. Appropriateness, relevance/feasible. Engage MINEE/MINSANTE/Municipal Council
SUSTAINABILITY
Trained (CPCs for continuos sensitisaton on HP; WMCs/supply with tools box; IDPs on how to treat/reuse squatting slabs). Installation of HWFs/improve sustainable latrines. Out/in patient will have payment of their bills, hospitals and health districts will have access to improve sustainable latrines/safe drinking water.
JUSTIFICATION
Increased # of improved (sustainable latrines/water points) to reduce cholera, diarrhea/deaths/poverty.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

NEEDS: 12,110 people living in poverty are in need of quality and sufficient quantity of sustainable clean water for drinking, laundry, preparing food, bath, washing of hands with soap for delivery of new-born to reduce maternal/neonatal deaths, cholera and diarrhea including deaths to children under 5 years for they collect water from an unimproved water point. Lack of improved sustainable sanitation facility (latrines) to homes. Lack promotion of good wash practices to 20,000 affected population. Trained the affected population on feedback and complaints to humanitarian actors on AAP. Lack of trained water management committees (WMCs) to manage their water supply and supplies of tools box with latrines management. Training of Nurses, community protection committees (CPCs) and community health workers (CHWs), volunteers and animators as health and hygiene promoters. Create community health clubs (CHCs) and trained. 130 most vulnerable people of marginalised/minorities groups, IDPs, returnees and host communities members lack financial assistance for the payment of their hospital bills and buying of medicine. 10 hospitals and health centers are in need of health facilities supported such construction of boreholes with solar power system and improve sustainable latrines. NB: Direct beneficiaries are 12,240 and indirect beneficiaries is 7,760 NB: Construction of borehole underground water scheme is with small solar powered system of 4 taps per borehole. From the sphere handbook, household toilet is 1 toilet for 5 persons or 1 family. i.e. 22 gender-sensitive family HH latrine x 5= 110 people NB: Our project take into account the climate resiliencs in the sense that, from the start of planning, designing, building and operating, we anticipates prepares for and adapt to changing climate conditions.In order that our project should be resilient WASH programming which helps to ensure that our WASH infrastructures (e.g. construct latrines that are climate resilience to withstand thunders storm, floods and land slide including the construction of the borehole tower that carries the water storage tanks and solar plate) and services are sustainable and resilient to climate related risks ; which imply that, WASH contributes in building communities resilience to climate change.
