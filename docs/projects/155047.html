Project Summary

The goal of this project is to ensure improved living conditions to the Rohingya refugees by constructing mid-term shelters and distributing transitional shelter assistance. Capacity development training on disaster risk reduction (DRR) issues and shelter upgradation will also be arranged for the Rohingya community members.

Objectives

Improve living conditions of the Rohingya refugees, contributing to their reduced suffering, enhanced protection, dignity, and safety.

Needs

Recent situation report published in November 1st, 2018, have shown needs of Transitional shelters and Mid-term shelters about 95% Rohingya families. 857 mid-term shelters and 2,962 transitional shelters have been constructed that reached target only 5%. Refugees are residing in makeshift shelters that are severely below basic humanitarian standards, overcrowded, and will not withstand seasonal climatic conditions. The lowquality of the shelters and poor terrain as well as the lack of privacy within these shelters has a serious impact on the physical and psychological well-being of the refugees - especially for women, children and older refugees and refugees with disabilities. Upgrades to shelters and site improvements are urgently required prior to the next monsoon and cyclone season.

Activities or outputs

(1) Construction of 500 mid-term shelters; (2) Distribution of transitional shelter assistance among 2000 Rohingya families; (3) Capacity building training about disaster risk reduction (DRR) and shelter upgradation among 2000 people from Rohingya and host communities;(4) Awareness activities among Rohingya communities on Shelter upgradation and maintenance, with special focus on women
