Project Summary

Project interventions are enabling the easy access of sustainable water services for vulnerable settlements/households include (displaced and none-displaced and persons with disabilities) in floods and severe-drought hotspot districts in Sanaag, Togdheer and Awdal regions of Somaliland, of whom currently accessing temporary water trucking, distant shallow reservoirs, and unprotected shallow wells that are not functional throughout the year. In response, basic water supply systems to be restored through rehabilitation of existing water-facilities and provision of new sustainable water sources to decrease the effects of disproportional affects of droughts, and barriers of accessing sustainable WASH services.

1. - Projects activities

1. Restore the functionality of existing water supply systems; rehabilitation of existing under-resourced shallow wells, surface reservoirs (berkads), and abandoned boreholes in sever-drought hotspot districts in Sool, Sanaag and Togdheer regions, Somaliland. 2. Establishment of of women-led water committees to ensure proper maintenance of water services (service continuity) in arid seasons. 3. Provide well-designed, dignified, and appropriate basic sanitation facilities for affected people (displaced) in sever-drought hotspot districts in Sool, Sanaag and Togdheer regions, Somaliland. 4. Community engagement to adopt accountability and compliant/feedback channels to improve WASH intervention's transparency and community inclusion to ensure provision delivery for communities (displaced, none displaced and people with disabilities).

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

1. Strong community engagement, Impartial selection criteria and engagement with government prior and during activity implementations. 2. Male and female are separated during queuing areas and ensure young girls are accessing safe distributions at hygiene distribution areas; to prevent exposure of displayed boys and girls to violence. 3. Provision of safe WASH services that is visible - with possible distance - to settlements/households that is not more than 500m, to prevent exposure of sexual violence on women and young girls. 4. Provision of gender labelled sanitation services, latrines, with proper ventilations, ramp for disabled and lightening to prevent exposure of boys and girls to violence. 5. Strong engagement of target communities and committee representatives to play an active role in identifying solutions and the decision-making processes that affect them, so as to promote a sense of ownership, and risk of land ownership/land tenure or contamination by explosive hazards in targeted project areas are prevented.
