Project Summary

The country health system is currently strained and compromised mainly due to the debilitating economic environment which has resulted in stock outs of essential medicines and supplies. The private pharmacies that do have medicines sell at exorbitant prices and in US dollars beyond reach of at least two thirds of people. This situation has been worsened by industrial action by medical doctors, the current Cholera and Typhoid outbreaks affecting the country. The impending El Nino will worsen the prevailing situation exposing the majority of the vulnerable populations in the target districts at risk due to diseases outbreaks and inadequate case management. Gaps in the routine disease surveillance systems also compromise timely detection and reporting of disease outbreaks and public health events.
The project will feed into the cluster response, working in collaboration with WHO and MOHCC to sustain access to health services and timely response to diseases endemics. To ensure appropriate responses in the different target districts, the project will facilitate risk mapping exercise to identify the hazard profile, vulnerabilities and response capacities. Through capacity building trainings for MOHCC staff on IDSR and logistical support, the project will strengthen routine surveillance and strengthening of early warning systems to ensure timely detection of disease outbreaks and health events. This will be coupled with strengthening the capacity of local laboratories at district hospitals through support with reagents, trainings and logistics for sample collection and transportation.
Case management usually emerges as a challenge as observed during the current cholera outbreak, in light of this, the project will strengthen case management through capacity building trainings for MOHCC staff and VHWs complemented with provision of job aids. This will improve management of medical consequences of malnutrition, common communicable diseases like cholera, typhoid fever, malaria and measles. For the capacity building to yield fruits there is need for adequate stocks of essential medicines and medical supplies; the project will support distribution of essential medicines (procured by the cluster lead through NatPharm) to health facilities in the target districts.
In an endeavor to enhance coordination, reporting and data use in the target districts, monthly reporting meetings will be supported in the target districts.
