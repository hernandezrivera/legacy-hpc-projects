Project Summary

The aim of the UNICEF’s WASH project is to reduce mortality and morbidity within the affected population through increasing access to and provision of essential WASH services. Water and sanitation are among the immediate priority needs of populations affected by any disasters. It is estimated that up to 50 percent of the affected population in a disaster will require water, sanitation and hygiene services. UNICEF WASH response will target critical needs of the most vulnerable (including those of children, women and people with special needs) and will be guided by humanitarian principles and priorities, including the Sphere Guidelines.
UNICEF’s assistance package for natural disaster will include the following:
Provision of safe water:
Target population of around 530,000 people (119,462 men, 124,338 women, 140,238 boys and 145,962 girls) including people with special needs will have access to sufficient water of appropriate quality and quantity for drinking, cooking and maintaining personal hygiene (benchmark: 5 -15 L/person/day), through water trucking, immediate restoration of damaged water supply systems and provision of household level water filtration solutions where water is available but contaminated.
Provision of Sanitation Facilities:
Target population of around 390,000 people (87,906 men, 91,494 women, 103,194 boys and 107,406 girls) including people with special needs will have access to toilets and washing facilities that are culturally appropriate, secure, sanitary, user-friendly and gender-appropriated (Benchmark: 20 people/hygienic toilet), through installation of temporary latrines, bathing cubicles, and solid waste collection points.
Hygiene Promotion:
Target population of around 560,000 people (126,224 men, 131,376 women, 148,176 boys and 154,224 girls) including people with special needs will receive critical WASH-related information and supplies to prevent child illness, especially diarrhea, through interpersonal hygiene sessions, mass media campaigns, distribution of IEC material and the provision of WASH related non-food items (NFIs).
WASH in Schools:
Around 45,000 children (22,050 boys and 22,950 girls) will have access to safe water and sanitation facilities and follow safe hygienic practices in their learning environment, child friendly spaces through installation of water and sanitation facilities and interpersonal hygiene session
UNICEF’s assistance package for COVID-19 will include the following:
Target population of around 51,000 people (including staff of HCF and patients) will have access to appropriate WASH facilities in health care centers, through new installation and repair/rehabilitation of water and sanitation facilities.
Target population of around 440,000 people (99,176 men, 103,224 women, 116,424 boys and 121,176 girls) including people with special needs will have access to information on preventive measures against COVID-19 through social and mass media campaigns, radio jingles, distribution of IEC materials and television shows.
Target population of around 110,000 people will have access to hand washing stations installed at key public places.
A target of 11,000 people key frontline workers will be trained on measures related to COVID-19, to ensure the safety, comply with SOPs and follow precautionary measures about related to COVID-19.

1- Project Activities

UNICEF will have WASH response for both natural disasters and COVID-19. This will be done through the following activities. For Natural disasters:Repair/Rehabilitation/ installation of damaged water supply systems, along with provision of safe water through water tankering (on need basis)Installation of user-friendly latrines, bathing cubicles and washing places at camps and rehabilitation of latrines at household level. Promotion of critical WASH-related information using interpersonal communication, social and mass media campaigns, along with using of IEC material.Provision of appropriate WASH installations at schools and healthcare facilities along with hygiene education. For COVID-19:Provision of appropriate WASH installations in priority health facilities targeted for treating COVID-19 cases: (water supply, sanitation facilities, hand-washing facilities with soap, chlorine powder and lime for disinfection of contaminated surfaces)Ensuring access to information on preventive measures, against COVID-19 through social and mass media, radio jingles along with use of IEC material.Installation of hand washing stations at key public places.Capacity building of key front-line workers on measures related to COVID-19.
