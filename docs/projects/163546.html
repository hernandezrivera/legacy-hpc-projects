in Misrata and 3 schools in Tawargha.

Plan fields

1 - Needs

For decent living in detention centers, sanitation services must be provided (toilets,washing water, drinking water, and personal hygiene items for women and children). Inschools, in order for the student to continue the study, the appropriate bathrooms mustbe kept that protect the child's safety and dignity. Also, there is a need for washing waterand drinking water. Target number 5000 person, children 70% from total , 15% womenand 15 % Men, Children Ages 05-14 years boys and girls.

2 - Activities

Activities in this project focus on the maintenance and rehabilitation of the bathrooms,the connection of the washing water network and the provision of hygiene kits, especiallyfor children. This project seeks to preserve and protect the dignity of beneficiaries withoutdiscrimination (not on the basis of sex or color). The project will include a healthawareness campaign. Follow-up will be through frequent visits and reports.
