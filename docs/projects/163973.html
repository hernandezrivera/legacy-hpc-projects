Project Summary

The crisis in Syria has resulted in many agricultural infrastructures being out of service. the agricultural roads are one of those negatively affected sectors and urgently in need of rehabilitation.
the IHR is planning to rehabilitate about 2 km of the agriculture roads in Azaz district. that activity aims to facilitate the daily movement from lands to houses, markets, and vice versa. which can reduce the burden of farmers, especially during the winter season. and their families' members, since those roads the children are using to reach for school, and women to reach for the services centers. the rehabilitation works will include:
• Using the backhoe to pave the road and put layer of sands with thickness 40 cm to cover those muddy roads.
• Using the road roller to smooth that layer of sand to be suitable for movement. with Slightly left and right slanting to facilitate rainwater drainage.
• using a tractor to open canal with depth 60 cm on the right and left side along the road to facilitate the rainwater leakage into the canal. therefore, prevent gathering water on the top of the road or on the adjacent agriculture lands that may cause waterlogging.
• installing cement pipes (length 5 m and 0.5 m diameter) on the road to facilitate drainage of excess rainwater. one installing point will be for every 300 meters.
• selecting 100 workers under cash for work activity to remove the weeds from the roadside, pick up the fallen sand while paving roads from farmland. the work will be through one calendar month (26 actual days) and getting 5 $ against per day.
moreover, the IHR is going to distributing 500 agriculture vouchers with cost 150 $ per HH. Each HH has to select seeds enough for cultivating two donums of winter vegetable seeds (50%); Broad Bean, Pea, Onion, Garlic, Chard, Spinach, Lettuce, Parsley, Radish, Garden Cress. And getting on fertilizer, pesticides (30%) or tools based on the value of selected seeds. This means, selecting the seeds is mandatory for all, while the remaining value is optional from the other items. As well, receiving a cash irrigation voucher with cost 30 $ per HH, which is sufficient to provide the initial irrigations needed for plants germination. as well
the IHR technical team will conduct extension visits for beneficiaries’ farms for four months. This aims to provide farmers with technical information about the best agricultural practices and follow up the progress of plants’ growing.
the first intervention aims to improve the agriculture infrastructure, by Improving the farmers’ access to agricultural land and markets, as well the daily movements by women, children and students for school. in additional to Improve the physical properties of the soil of agricultural lands adjacent to the road due to improving the drainage of water and preventing its accumulation on the soil that prevents the growth of plants in it. Meanwhile the cash for work activity will create a temporary source of income for 100 HHs. while the second intervention aims to improve the access to agriculture inputs of vulnerable farmers of men and women, therefore encourage the agriculture process

4 - Needs

The agricultural infrastructure at whole Syria completely or partially damaged due to the war. Perhaps the most notable of them is being some roads out of service due to the lack of necessary ingredients to rehabilitate these roads.IHR team developed need assessment tools; it was designed to target members at local council, women, and men in the targeted area. which aim to focus on the most important risks that people are facing during the current situation, especially with the increasing number of IDPs as a result of the hostilities in Idleb. Data was collected by MEAL team. KIIs sessions were held with 4 key informants (KIIs) from the local councils in each of Azaz, Mare’, Suran and Akhtarin. The results indicate population of Azaz is about 250,000. The HHs number is 42,728, distributed to 15,382 HCs, 14,100 returnee, 13,246 IDPs, 3,250 female-headed household. Around 11,750 HHs have no income, while 2,500 HHs receive income more than 50$. In terms of education level in the targeted area, the average number of women that illiterate is 7,250, 3,250 out of them are girls. Families relied on agriculture as the main resource of their income, which is the most important economic activity in the whole communities in targeted location; the percentage of families who depend on agriculture products was 42%. The average farmland area owned by small farmers was about 5-10 donums. The common crops in the targeted location were winter crops (wheat, barley, lentil) and summer vegetable crops (cucumbers, tomatoes, eggplants, and okra) where the cultivated area reached about 4000 hectares.women are involved in agriculture activities, factories, and selling on the road, and that is due to several problems which include: the need to secure income for living, lack of handcraft market in the area, and the existence of illiteracy in the area.The people suffering to access for their farms and markets due to the rough roads. Where, they select other roads to reach for.

5 - Implementation of activities

once the award agreement is signed, the IHR will recruit the field staff through transparent interviews. MoUs will be signed with local councils of the targeted communities to explain the targeted categories, selection criteria, and the project objectives. through clear application form, the interested participants will be selected against selection criteria like having area of land, experience in agriculture, non-benefiting from similar projects. The supplier will be selected to provide the required items or service. the rehabilitation works will be carried out during summer seasons to ensure that those roads will be ready to serve the people during the next winter. whereas, the IHR will advertise of cash for work activity to reach for 100 qualified vulnerable persons and able to work. the works will be implemented directly be contractor, under supervising of the IHR field team. firstly, the workers, using tools like spade, rake, plastic bucket, and metal wheelbarrow, will remove the weeds of the road. the second stage is paving the road to facilitation putting sands on for 40 cm thickness. in parallel, the worker will move on the both sides of the road to help in paving process. at last stage, a tractor to move on the both sides to open a channel next to the road.for agriculture support activity, before launching the distribution plan, an introducing meeting with selected beneﬁciaries will be conducted to explain the voucher redeem process and raise their awareness about project objectives, focusing on the importance of selecting the Drought-resistant varieties. the beneficiaries will have option to select the identified items during redemption determined days. after getting on the required inputs, cash irrigation voucher with cost 30 $ will be distributed for all targeted beneficiaries. finally, four month of extension visits will be conducted by technical team through field visits to lands and provide the required information about best agricultural practices

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

This project aims to improve access to the agricultural inputs with priority for women, GBV survivor and disabled and enhance their knowledge. as well rehabilitate the agricultural roads which can abbreviate the distances and reduce the potential risks of using distant roadways to people in general, women and children in particular.the IHR has engaged all society groups to define needs, especially women, which aimed giving opportunity to plan and design activities based on needs. the IHR implements the security instructions to keep staff and beneficiaries safe. the vouchers redemption will be from targeted vendors within the market, so no risk of gathering the people. extension visits to beneficiaries’ houses will be. all staff will be known about evacuation plans in case bad situation. the coordination with local authorities will be to prevent any bad interfering. the traditional customs will be taken into consideration, throughout specializing a separated line for women, provide the registration place with chairs for old people, toilet, and singes for Breastfeeding room for women. training field staff about humanitarian and protection principles, are aim to reducing the gender-based violence cases. feedback mechanism will be clear at implementing stages; whats App (writing or audio recording), focus discussion group with targeted women to try discovering any related cases. As the IHR will keep the field staff known about dealing with those cases or raise it to protection cluster. the IHR will respond to persons who are not able to come for registration or voucher distribution/redemption place by field visits to their houses. Registration and distribution points will be in safe and close places.The food processing training will be in special room, separated of men intervention under supervising of female staff. While for cash for work activity, the women workers will be divided into groups separated of men’s groups and under female supervising.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

We assume that: access to agricultural lands is equal for men and women. community members will respect the selection criteria, so only the in-need HHs will apply to benefit from project. no displacement cases will be in the targeted area, so none of the selected beneficiaries will leave their lands. all targeted beneficiaries will use the agricultural inputs to cultivate the agricultural land. we assume that the market assessment results will proceed to the end of project. community engagement with local authorities will conduct clearly at the begging of project, so full commitment from representatives toward the agreed terms. Risk 1:the rehabilitated roads may be sabotaged due to passing the heavy trucks passing on it. Likelihood: low -Impact: medium - Mitigation: IHR program team in coordination with local council will raise the community’s awareness about specializing the road for farmers service only, and take some required actions for that.Risk 2: some crops may not grow in some agricultural lands. Likelihood: low - Impact: medium - Mitigation: IHR program team are going to check on the quality of seeds and test the germination of it to accept the high quality only. The extension visits will be at first day to keep the agronomist close of farmers and provide advices about planting process. Risk 3: Some women in-need but do not have access to agricultural land. Likelihood: medium - Impact: High- Mitigation: during community engagement, the IHR team will hold a meeting with local council and representatives of women, if any to explain the project objectives and confirm the right of female-headed households to have access to lands. Risk 4: cash for irrigation may be used for another purpose. Likelihood: low - Impact: medium -Mitigation1: IHR program team are going to raise the awareness of farmers towards the importance of using the money for irrigation issue. that will be for all selected beneficiaries and before vouchers distribution
