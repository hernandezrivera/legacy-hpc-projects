Project Summary

Garbahare district continues to face multiple and complex socio-economic political challenges. Repeated climatic conditions characterized by below-average rainfall, prolonged drought, delayed rainfall, and widespread livelihood-threatening conflict and constant migration has affected food security. There are 20,910 people in need (PiN) who are in IPC 3 & 4 and need urgent food security support in one way or the other (FSC 2022 Overall Target by District).
FSNAU-FEWSNET Technical Release-September, 2021 for the period July–August 2021 indicates, under its food security overview, that Garbahare district fell under stressed IPC which deteriorates to Crisis IPC during the October-December 2021 projections. The GAM rates stand at between 10-14.9% in August with parts of Garbahare sliding into critical GAM Rate of 15-29.9% for the September-November 2021 projections. The 2021 Post-Gu Food Security and Nutrition Outcomes and Projects indicates the GAM in North Gedo Riverine at 15.1% and 12.9% in North Gedo pastoral areas with a SAM of 2.1% in riverine Garbahare and 1.9% pastoral areas for children <5. The deteriorating food security/ nutrition is further reflected in Morbidity rates of 26.4% in riverine Garbahare and 22.4% in pastoral five. MUAC projections for hard to react areas in Gedo region which largely covers Garbahare due to the presence of insurgency indicates that <5 children MUAC<12.5CM is 15.7% (for riverine communities) and 13.8 for the pastoral areas. The two reports also raise concerns about the reducing browsable pasture in Garbahare districts and the ongoing migrations.
The latest flash assessments and updates from the local authority indicates highly deteriorating cases. Garbaharey has continued to receive an influx of IDPs according to Joint assessment conducted in second week of November 2021.
Most households are thus sliding into food insecurity bracket. As such, the project seeks to support the food insecure and vulnerable households by enhancing access to food. It will increase food security for the at-risk communities in Garbahare host and IDPs by integrated food-related multipronged programming interventions including conditional and unconditional food provision, screening, preventive (blanket), provision of food, referrals, and awareness. It will increase knowledge on food handling, support subsistence farmers, It will provide vouchers, strengthen capacity for food production, rehabilitate assets through for assets and create short term employment opportunities. The project aim is to reduce and minimize exposure to food insecurity among vulnerable households, ameliorate severe effects and stabilize vulnerable families. Primary targets will be the most vulnerable groups including Pregnant and Lactating Women and their children between 6 - 59 months, and women-headed households.
A very important element of the project is building resilience through provision of relevant skills to increases self-reliance, enhance self-sustenance and provide cautionary livelihood activities against shocks. The targeted are the IDPs and those whose assets and sources of livelihood were destroyed affected by prolonged drought and demonstrate susceptibility to food deficiency diseases. Identification is done through standard screening processes and capacity building to PLW to undertake MUAC screening methodologies. Periodic screening, coordination with the local actors including the local administration.

1. - Projects activities

Primarily the project aims to contribute towards addressing food insecurity in Garbahare district. It targets the most vulnerable households including women headed households. Most of the targets are IDP and host communities who have lost their livelihood assets primarily goats, camels to droughts. The project thus provides both rehabilitative, preventive and cautionary interventions. To do this, it aims to coordinate and work closely with the local authority to screen and identify the targeted beneficiaries. Project will involve conditional and unconditional food provision and integrated assistance programming. The project will constitute the following milestones; 1. Conduct social communication, mobilization and community awareness of the project targeting 15,000 persons for 30 days. This involves working with the local authority and community-based workers to identify the most needy households and enlisting them into the programme. The process of joint cooperation and coordination also helps strengthen the capacity of the local administration and build trst in the local administration. 2. Do basic screening to determine the level of food insecurity needs among the targeted at-risk population using standard screening standards targeting 11,000 for the 12 months period. GEWDO current has 8 facilities in Garbaharey and is thus familiar with the population dynamics including right contacts. The mobilization will target the Garbaharey always at-risk persons and populations. 3. Routinely provide food vouchers to 3300 HHs with over 50% going to women-headed households for six months. The aim is to cushion them against the debilitating hunger/food insecurity and enable them stabilize their livelihood. 4. Asses and increase capacity to food production through provision of farm input to 100 households for the three farming seasons. Within Garbaharey, there are agropastoral areas where small-scale subsistence farming takes place. The aim is to enable the communities maximize and strengthen their farm productions. 5. Conduct a one-week training and capacity building on food-safe storage for 150 households. For the most part food wastage has been a major concern in Garbahare subsistence farming. The aim is to enable the communities acquire necessary food storages kills to avoid post-harvest losses. 6. Engage 200 beneficiaries through a one-month rehabilitation of assets and provision of food. This will involve rehabilitating well known infrastructures with primary focus on water catchment facilities. 7. Enhance resilience by provision of marketable vocational skills to 50 youths for three months. The aim is to enable youths acquire skills which can be used to create diversionary sources of livelihood to enhance resilience. 8. Provide basic awareness and capacity building concerning the Coronavirus pandemic targeting to reach 15,000 people and provision of awareness materials and essential sanitation programs including facemasks for 2,000 beneficiaries. For the increasingly food insecure communities, provision of food and food-related support will significantly reduce pressure on daily livelihood and provide the households with the opportunity to build new assets and strengthen household level resilience This is true for most families that have lost livestock and household level livelihood opportunities. It will also expand capacity to enhnace food production besides skills on strengthening nutritious and dietary skills as a resileince component.

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

The organization has a policy of inclusivity, qualitative social mobilization and and community targeted approach. The process ensures that the beneficiaries selection process is inclusive and adequately communicated during the community consultation process. At the core of the project is adhering to both SPHERE Standards and protection requirements. Moreover, several frameworks have been adopted to ensure mainstreaming protection into the activities. All GEWDO activities are conducted in the morning hours to allow beneficiaries have enough time to reach their homes and ensure their safety. Moreover, in all its facilities the organization has separate washrooms and sanitation facilities for both male and female. The planning and location of these facilities are meant to enhance and ensure maintenance of safety and protection from any harm. Primarily GEWDO has operationalized the PSEA policy and CoC which aims to ensure its beneficiaries are safe from any form of sexual exploitation and abuse. Human resource standards include, but not limited to, background checks on employees and ensuring all contractors, consultants, volunteers and staff read, understand and sign the necessary PSEA, CoC besides recommendations from previous employers or clients.Whereas Garbahare is very insecure the organization’s adequate social mobilization policy seeks to ensure adequate communications about project objects to maintain do no harm and put none in the harm’s way. In summary GEWDO’s project will not expose beneficiaries, boys, girls, men and women to any form of harm. GEWDO will progressively monitor its environment and act appropriately where any imminent threat is detected.
