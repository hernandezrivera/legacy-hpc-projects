Project Summary

Through this proposed intervention, shelter and NFI assistance will be provided to vulnerable communities in Sindh province, namely in districts Badin and Sanghar. It is suggested that through the intervention activities, 400 beneficiary households (2800 individuals) with fully damaged houses and 800 households (5600 individuals) with partially damaged homes will be provided with one shelter and NFI kit and one shelter repair kit, following the conduction of the shelter and DRR. ACTED will provide these beneficiaries with a shelter repair kit containing the necessary materials and tools needed for the rehabilitation of a shelter. By providing in-kind material, vulnerable members of the community – particularly those belonging to female-headed households, widows, victims of gender-based violence (GBV), pregnant and lactating women (PLW), persons with disabilities (PWD) as well as child and elderly-headed households – will be able to repair their houses and live in a dignified and sustainable manner, without having to travel long distance to markets and transporting the materials back home.

1- Project Activities

Activity 1: Distribution of Material for Fully and Partially Damaged Shelter for 1,200 vulnerable returnee households One of the foremost needs of vulnerable families is a safe, liveable shelter. The recent floods caused damage to houses severe enough to force families to sleep in the open or cramped tents. Thus, through this activity, 400 beneficiary households (2800 individuals) with fully damaged houses and 800 households (5600 individuals) with Partially Damaged houses will be provided with one shelter repair kit, following the conduction of the shelter and DRR. ACTED will provide these beneficiaries with a shelter repair kit containing the necessary materials and tools needed for the rehabilitation of shelter, enabling households to re-establish adequate shelters that are hazard-resistant, affordable, and have access to portable water supply and sanitation facilities. By providing in-kind material, vulnerable members of the community – such as female-headed households, widows, victims of gender-based violence (GBV), pregnant and lactating women (PLW), persons with disabilities (PWD) as well as child and elderly-headed households – will be able to repair their houses and live in a dignified and sustainable manner without having to travel long distance to markets and transporting the materials back home. Considering the various forms of housing found in these Sindh districts, the kits will contain tools that allow for the repair of different types of shelters. These kits will contain construction material including 16 ft. long steel girder, 10 ft. long bamboo for roof, chicks, lime, polythene sheet, 5-6 mm cotton rope, iron door and iron window. Activity 2: Distribution of Shelter NFI Kits to 1200 Vulnerable Households In order to contribute to the psychological and physical survival of the affected population, ACTED will distribute Shelter NFI kits to 1200 vulnerable HHs, as a means to preserve their health and ensure their welfare, safety and dignity. The shelter NFI kits will assist the vulnerable households with essential items necessary for them to live with a sense of protection and security. The action will provide solar lights to help during these power outages and if the family is sleeping out in the open at night. These cost-effective and reliable lights will also come with charging panels to allow the beneficiaries to charge their mobile phones. This will help keep families in more remote and rural areas connected with the rest of the world despite power outages, which is even more crucial in the aftermath of a disaster. Apart from solar lights, the NFI kit will also contain other essential items such as 2 mosquito nets , 2 jerry cans, steel pan, planner/garmala, measuring tape, steel bucket, spade/kodar and a mason hammer. Activity 3: Provision basic technical training on shelter construction for 1,200 householdsIn consultation with the target communities, a one-day training, separately for male and female, on how to improve shelter conditions will be provided to the target communities. These trainings will be provided to the 1,200 beneficiary households. The trainings will address the overarching DRR considerations and components of the Humanitarian Strategic Plan. Activity 3: Provision IEC material on resilient shelter structure For shelter assistance at household level, ACTED will distribute IEC materials among the shelter beneficiaries. The IEC materials will help the beneficiaries construct safer and resilient shelters using the provided/locally available material (bamboos, rope, lime and tarpaulin sheets). The IEC material include skill development information to ensure improved construction and maintenance of the shelter units following the climate-safe and hazard resilient structures.
