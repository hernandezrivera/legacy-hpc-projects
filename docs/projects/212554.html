Project Summary

In its thirteenth year, Raqqa Governorate suffers from violence, exploitation, homelessness, social and economic deprivation, and psychological pressures. This has deeply affected the mental health of children, adolescents, and caregivers, which has led to a significant increase in the percentage of girls and boys who show signs of distress, fatigue, and psychological and mental pressures.
As there are more than (600,000) people in need of assistance in protection services.
Because their lives are entrusted to us and based on Nebras’ basic principles of protecting the rights of those affected by conflicts by ensuring access to the basics of survival, protection from violence and fear of forced displacement, and working to provide an appropriate environment, Nebras aims through its distinguished and purposeful activities to reduce To protect people from harm or humiliation, to provide vulnerable individuals in targeted communities with protection information and access to specialized protection services, and to provide impartial and impartial assistance without discrimination.
(4) fixed and mobile centers will be opened in Raqqa Governorate to provide protection services through a group of sectors:
(Child Protection / Public Protection Services / Gender-Based Violence / Awareness of Remnants of War) to target them over a period of two years, as the project targets about (124,000) people per year.
At Nebras Association, we strive to follow a community-based approach that works to empower the most vulnerable groups in societies, and we work to generalize protection and focus on the safety and dignity of individuals and families in society.
Nebras seeks to provide support to the issues of children, adults, and women psychologically affected by sexual violence and cases related to mental health. Therefore, Nebras devotes an important part of its work to empowering these groups, reducing the risk resulting from crises, and creating a space of hope that allows them to live in security, as we at Nebras and through the protection sector We give women great importance, give them the right to make decisions, and support them to work on building the capabilities of community members.
The target groups are children, women, girls, adolescent girls, caregivers, displaced people inside and outside camps, and the most vulnerable families.
The sectors include a range of activities:
1- Providing integrated, high-quality protection services
2- Raising awareness, psychological and social assistance, and supporting people with special needs
3- Providing legal services and improving the protection of affected populations through community-based and individually targeted protection interventions
4- Survivors have access to high-quality specialized services in the field of GBV and put in place measures to prevent and reduce the risks of GBV through women’s safe space centers.
5- Increase more equitable access for boys, girls and adults to quality child protection activities for vulnerable children by creating safe spaces for children and adults and providing specialized child protection services (case management) for girls and boys
6- Remnants of War awareness activities reach all members of society and their goal is to enhance the protection of boys and girls from harm, injury and disability through awareness of the dangers of explosive ordnance that is strategically provided to displaced people as well as host communities in schools, IDP camps, sites and communities.
Nebras has a special policy to prevent sexual exploitation and abuse and adopts a “zero tolerance policy” in any violation or case of sexual exploitation or assault. Any violation of this policy must take very firm and strict measures against the violators, and may reach the point of final dismissal and termination of the contract.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Protection activitiesNebras is keen to protect children, reduce the risks of this crisis and its negative effects on the lives of children and caregivers, and ensure that all girls and boys at risk of exploitation, violence and abuse benefit from improved prevention, protection and response interventions through the following activities:Child protectionIt is the sector responsible for providing information and activities that ultimately aim to provide child protection in local and host communities and provide related services. The sector aims primarily to: raise community awareness of child protection issues, respond to child protection cases, and provide child- and adult-friendly spaces that help them return to their homes. Their normal lives under the current circumstances, in addition to providing case management services and providing direct support to children for boys and girls and their families.Public protectionThe services provided by the sector are directed to internally displaced people in local and host communities. In this context, the sector provides the establishment of community centers / fixed / mobile / targeting all segments of society with psychological support activities, awareness sessions and entertainment, in addition to professional training and building the capabilities of protection workers to improve the quality of providing protection services. The sector projects also provide legal consultations, comprehensive case management services for gender-based violence, child protection, the Young Mothers Club program, and the awareness bag program for men and males, in addition to supporting the target groups with in-kind materials. The sector projects then provide activities dedicated to people with special needs and assisting them.Gender-based violence It is the sector responsible for providing response and prevention to survivors of women and girls who have been exposed to danger, to reduce the risks of sexual and gender-based violence and their consequences, improve the quality of the response, and empower the local community. The sector also works to increase awareness about gender-based violence in all targeted areas north of Eastern Syria (Raqqa), especially women and teenage girls, who are considered one of the most vulnerable groups in these societies, as the association’s interventions include gender-based violence by creating safe spaces for women. Awareness of war remnantsThe activities of this sector include raising awareness of war remnants, especially children, caregivers and local communities through community education activities about the dangers of explosive ordnance.
