Project Summary

The humanitarian crisis in Nigeria’s northeast is one of the most severe in the world today, with 7.1 million people in need of humanitarian assistance in 2019, out of which 771428 persons are targeted for WASH assistance. The crisis is adding to the long history of marginalization and chronic under-development as well as high rates of poverty, illiteracy and unemployment. The recent outbreak of the Covid-19 pandemic that started in China has spread over the world and Nigeria is not left out. This has made the situation in the Northeast more complex. As of April 25, 2020, 1,182 cases have been registered with fatalities across Nigeria and over 4,000,000 persons are in need of health services. Of this number, Maiduguri has 30 positive cases with 1 death, Adamawa one case while no case has been recorded in Yobe state. This has caused the government to follow the global preventive measure of hand washing and social distancing. This too becomes a challenge as capacity of primary health care facilities are weak and Water and Sanitation facilities have been destroyed by the insurgents. Moreover, there is low coverage in terms of water supply to the vulnerable population, thus rendering prevention and regular hand washing difficult. In addition, homes with large number of person, congested IDP camps and water points will make it almost impossible to respect social distancing protocols and will help in spreading COVID-19. Also, there are myths surrounding the prevalence of COVID-19 such as traditional beliefs in the intake of some particular food will be effective in the treatment and contentment of the Virus. Due to the lockdown, prices of commodities have skyrocketed making it difficult for IDPs to acquire PPE and maintain proper hygiene practices and ensure the effectiveness of all preventive measures. This puts the already vulnerable population at risk of contracting the Virus.
AHA is proposing to contain the spread of Covid-19 by increasing the knowledge of IDPs, enlarging water and sanitation coverage, preventive measures as well as age , gender and context specific hygiene promotion messages in congested IDPs camps in Borno and Adamawa States.

2 - Activities

-awareness raising and increasing knowledge of decision makers( religious leaders, community leaders ) on corona virus, preventive measures, means of transmission, signs/symptoms of infection, persons at risks.-Provision of water and sanitation as per standards for reduction of crowding and effective appliance of preventive measures.- training of WASHCOM on households hygiene, social distancing and regular hand washing after touching sanitation surfaces.- dissemination of age, gender and context specific messages in Camps, Health care water points, and sanitation on IPC protocol.- distribution of covid-19 preventive Items( disinfectants, face masks,gloves, soaps...)-Hygiene promotion key messages on COVID-19 at water point and sanitaion.-distribution of WASH NFI.
