Project Name

Start Date

01/01/2020

End Date

31/12/2020

Project Summary

The project aims to increase access to hygiene NFIs and to improve WASH infrastructure for the benefit of the most vulnerable IDPs, returnees and other conflict affected communities in Luhansk and Donetsk oblasts (GCA along the contact line and NGCA). As a result of 12 months of project implementation, 40,950 women, men, boys and girls will be able to meet their hygiene needs. The action will be implemented through a comprehensive set of WASH related activities, including distribution of hygiene kits for 16,800 vulnerable individuals, diapers for 6,000 individuals, vouchers among 3,150 individuals and 15,000 patients of key social institutions will benefit from WASH rehabilitation works .

1 - Needs

 Post distribution monitoring (PDM) report conducted by IOM in February 2019 on hygiene kits distribution showed that most of assisted social institutions have a huge need in hygiene items due to the specific type of in-patients under care to be receiving support/residing in social institution and lack of available funds for procurement. Eleven (11) institutions out of 20 supported replenish their stocks of hygiene item with a help of humanitarian assistance provided and in result of usage of the institutions’ funds. Based on IOM observations and discussions with beneficiaries and partners in conflict affected areas, the majority of social institutions (54%) has no money to buy Non-food items (NFI) while the rest (23%) assessed situation with access to NFIs as ‘have limited access and limited or no money at all to buy sanitation and hygiene consumables’. The main reason for lower consumption of the diapers in social institutions was a usage of hygiene items quite rarely and in certain cases due to their unavailability. The complex economic situation in Ukraine and years of ongoing conflict is the reason for the diapers remaining scarcely available for the vulnerable beneficiaries in social institutions located in NGCA. It should be noted that, according to the survey results all institutions (100%) expressed the need for support in the form of diapers for adults in the future. According to PDM report conducted by IOM in April 2019 on rehabilitation works, one hundred per cent (100%) of beneficiaries assessed the living conditions in the social institutions before project implementation as unsatisfactory that is why humanitarian assistance in the form of rehabilitation was relevant and completely met the urgent needs of the institutions. Representatives of all thirteen (13) institutions reported needs in renovation of premises. The second largest need in heating system rehabilitation was reported by participants of five (5) focus-groups. WASH rehabilitations are needed in four (4) social institutions. Based on IOM PDM findings, health facilities covered by the project are located in critical proximity to the contact line and repeatedly subjected to shelling and risk of new damages. Restoration of premises and heating system rehabilitation are the most needed as well.

2 - Activities & outcomes

Outcome: Vulnerable conflict-affected persons in GCA and NGCA of Ukraine are able to access essential hygiene facilities and critical hygiene items Output 1: Vulnerable conflict-affected individuals in Luhansk and Donetsk oblasts (NGCA) have improved access to critical hygiene items to improve sanitation practices. Activities for output 1:  Coordinate selection criteria and identification of the potential beneficiaries with de facto authorities; Carry out procurement of hygiene kits and diapers through an open competitive process according to IOM procurement rules and regulations as well as on transparency and accountability; Distribute hygiene kits and diapers among vulnerable households and social institutions in Donetsk and Luhansk regions (NGCA); Conduct post-monitoring distribution on support provided. The contents of the hygiene kits will be coordinated with the WASH Cluster and based on IOM’s experience carrying out similar programming in the NGCA. The kits will be comprised of the following items: toothbrush, toothpaste, soap, shampoo, washing powder, bleach, dishwasher liquid, sanitary pads, toilet paper and wet wipes, among others. Output 2: Vulnerable conflict-affected individuals in Luhansk and Donetsk oblasts (GCA) residing within 20km along the contact line benefited from the distribution of vouchers to address hygiene needs. Activities for output 2:  Identify the criteria and the need of affected population on hygiene items, register population, select the beneficiaries according to the criteria; Identify local suppliers of hygiene items which could provide the vouchers to beneficiaries; Carry out distribution of the vouchers among the selected households; Conduct post-monitoring distribution to assess the quality of provided support and identify the needs. Based on mapping and market analysis, IOM will create a list of selection criteria that should be taken into account when choosing the vendors. In line with IOM Procurement guidelines, IOM will launch an open call on its official website. Upon the thorough assessment of the received bids, IOM will select several vendors who are qualify to participate in the project. The guidelines to orient vendors on vouchers distribution will be elaborated. IOM Transparency Hotline and E&S Programme Information line currently running by IOM will be used as complain and feedback mechanism during the project implementation. To enhance activities conducted along the contact line in Luhansk and Donetsk regions (GCA), additionally IOM will support the overall obstetric units and ambulatories work, as well as encouraging hygiene promotion in schools and the empowerment of children as agents of change within their families and communities, IOM will conduct hygiene promotion activities. Information, Education and Communication (IEC) materials on key hygiene messages coordinated with the WASH Cluster will be disseminated among the key social infrastructure objects and households. Output 3: WASH facilities in targeted social institutions in Donetsk and Luhansk regions (NGCA) are functional and meet the needs of individuals seeking social services Activities for Output 3: Assess WASH rehabilitation needs as well as structural repairs of identified public infrastructures such as health and social institutions including, for example, repairs of toilets and showers, heating systems, filtrations systems, internal piping and water connections as well as more structural rehabilitations such as roofing, flooring, tiling, glassing, among others; Select construction companies through an open competitive process according to IOM rules and regulations; Conduct works as per identified needs, designs and BoQs, including periodic technical monitoring; Conduct post-distribution monitoring on WASH rehabilitation activities conducted.

3 - Indicators & targets

Outcome: Vulnerable conflict-affected persons in GCA and NGCA of Ukraine are able to access essential hygiene facilities and critical hygiene items % hygiene support recipients confirming their improved sanitation practices (Target:75%). Output 1: Vulnerable conflict-affected individuals in Luhansk and Donetsk oblasts (NGCA) have improved access to critical hygiene items to improve sanitation practices # of potential beneficiary households verified in target areas during the verification process (Target: 8,000 households); # of potential beneficiary social institutions verified in target areas during the assessment visits (Target: 20 social institutions); # of hygiene kits procured and distributed (Target: 8,000); # of diapers (packs) procured and distributed (Target: 36,000); # of individuals benefited from the provision hygiene kits (Target: 16,800 individuals (8,800 female and 8,000 male); # of individuals benefitting from the provision of diapers (packs) (Target: 6,000 individuals, of whom 4,000 female and 2,000 male); Output 2: Vulnerable conflict-affected individuals in Luhansk and Donetsk oblasts (GCA) residing within 20km along the contact line benefited from the distribution of vouchers to address hygiene needs # of vouchers for hygiene items distributes (Target: 1,500); # of household members benefited from the provision of vouchers (Target: 3,150 individuals (1,890 female and 1,260 male). Output 3: WASH facilities in targeted social institutions in Donetsk and Luhansk regions (NGCA) are functional and meet the needs of individuals seeking social services: # of social institutions supported with the rehabilitation of WASH facilities (Target: 47 rehabilitations); # of individuals served by the social institutions whose WASH facilities were rehabilitated (Target: 15,000 individuals, of whom 9,000 female and 6,000 male). IOM will target low-income households that meet at least one of the following vulnerability criteria: people living with disabilities, including disabled children; elderly persons (60+); households with children (3+); households with family members unable to work due to age, disabilities or other causes preventing them from contributing to the household income; single-headed households; and families whose house was destroyed during the conflict.
