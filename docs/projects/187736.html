Project Summary

SECD, during this proposal, aims at increasing resilience and affected people’s access to
basic Education based on the needs assessment conducted by SECD during a full
academic year (9 months) from September to June. Also, the project focuses on the areas with high severity (3,4) and priority schools, through the following HRP objectives: Increased safe and equitable availability and access to in-person and home-based formal and non-formal education for crisis-affected children and youth; SECD, through this proposal, is going to increase enrolment rate to formal and non-formal education, back to learning activities, rehabilitation and maintenance of existing education facilities ,
provision of teaching and learning materials, gender sensitive and disabled friendly WASH interventions, provision of school supplies. SECD is planning to provide access to education for students with disabilities and vulnerable students through providing transportation services and improve school environment and provide assistance within the schools in order to encourage them to continue their education and integrate
them with their peers. Enhanced quality of formal and non-formal education for children and youth; through training activities for educational personnel such as COVID-19, CSG, INEE Minimum Standards and PSEA and also provision of incentives for them. conducting vocational trainings to 400 students
and support 20 schools divided into 10 schools at Idleb governorate, Jisr Ash-shughur district, sub-districts of (Badama, Janudiyeh, and Darkosh) and 10 schools at Aleppo governorate, Jebel Saman district, sub-district of Atareb and Daret Azze. Due to COVID-19, SECD will be committed to do periodic
sterilization process for classrooms and WASH facilities, awareness sessions for students, training for teachers and educational staff, and provision sterilization materials for all schools.
The targeted number of beneficiaries is disaggregated as 15,400 students (7430 boys and
7970 girls) 350 (0.02 %) of them are CWDs, and 200 teachers and educational personnel (90 males, 110 females) will receive education assistance.
SECD, in case the fund is lower than the required, will prioritize the rehabilitation,
construction and that under COVID-19 activities in Badama, Atareb and Daret Azze where
the severity is 5 with focusing on the most vulnerable groups such as PWDs and poor
students.
SECD is going to support 15,400 students (7430 boys and 7970 girls) through rehabilitate 319 classrooms. The rehabilitation will include repairing 20 schools in the 5 districts of Daret Azze, Atareb, Janudiyeh, Badama, and Darkosh. The rehabilitation works will consider the needs of girls and CWDs in regard to WASH facilities; constructing additional latrines blocks to include 1 latrine in each school for CWDs including ramps. Installation of solar power for 20 schools. Furniture schools. Conducting BTL and COVID-19 awareness sessions 15,400 students. Provision of learning materials (Schoolbags, stationery kit, teacher's kit) for 15,400 students (7430 boys and 7970 girls) with regard to the difference in color and design between males and females and 200 teachers (90 males, 110 females).
Enhanced quality of education through providing in-class trainings to the educational
personnel about COVID-19, child safeguarding, CoC, INEE, and PSEA for 200 teachers
(Females 55%, males 45%), also providing vocational trainings to (400) students (150 males,250
females) aged from 18-24 in the non-formal schools. Provision incentives for 200 teachers and educational personnel. Involve parents in the educational process through
conducting monthly meetings for PTA members
The M & E team will be responsible for ensuring the implementation of strict procedures of SECD in all the implementation processes. to Ensure that the activities and learning materials are compatible with BNFs requirement by implementing satisfaction survey after each activity.

4 - Needs

Based on the Humanitarian Needs Overview (HNO 2021) quality education remains acritical need in Syria in general and North West of Syria in particular. Data analysisdemonstrates that 6.9 million children (97%) and school personnel (3%) are estimated tobe in need of humanitarian education assistance, an estimated 2.45 million children areout of school, 1.6 million students are at risk of dropping out in addition to that more thantwo-out- five schools are partially damaged, destroyed sheltering displaced families.. Also, Covid-19 outbreak forced all schools to move between physical, remote, and blended learning modalities.According to all the mentioned above, SECD conducted a needs assessment in 5 sub-districts affiliated from both Idleb and Aleppo; Idleb ( Badama, Janudiyeh, and Darkosh)and Aleppo (Atareb and Daret Azze) to assess the educational situation there, so based onthis needs assessment the proposal is going to address the following needs: back tolearning activities , rehabilitation and maintenance of existing education facilities as wellas establishment of new temporary education centers, provision of teaching and learningmaterials, gender sensitive and disabled friendly WASH interventions, provision of schoolsupplies.According to the need assessment, many schools in the targeted areas are in a dire need for the rehabilitation since it was damaged due to the ongoing crisis and the absence of the regular maintenance

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

Assumptions: Parents are actively participating in the BTL.Risks: Parents do not participate in the BTL as they do not see the value of the campaign and not fully aware about the importance of education for the future of their children. Mitigation Actions: Coordinate and involve some representatives in each camp or community from the parents in planning and implementing the BTL in addition to printing posters clarifying the objectives of the campaigns.Assumptions : The school bags with high quality are available in the local markets northwest SyriaPotential Risk : not having schools bags with high quality in the local markets SyriaMitigation Actions : SECD will search for suppliers who can provide schools bags with high quality from the Turkish markets and send them to Syria. Assumptions : The qualified and experienced trainers are available Potential Risk : not having qualified trainers Mitigation Actions : SECD will expand the selection criteria to find a qualified trainers from Northwest Syria. Assumptions : The education supplies are distributed as planned to children in formal and non-formal education in 20 schoolsPotential Risk : The intervention of some authorities in the distribution of education supplies of schoolsMitigation Actions : SECD will coordinate closely with the local education authorities( Directorates of Education and Education complexes in the areas of operations and will keep them updated on the progress of activities in schools


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) policies are in place,practiced and monitored. Policies will cover standards of behavior for theorganization’s staff and representatives, specifically forbidding sexual exploitationand abuse, and include accountability measures. Policies will address risks at theorganizational level and the program level. Trainings and refreshers on PSEA willbe done systematically. The targeted communities and learning facilities should beaware of SECD policies and procedures. SECD staff and targeted communities willhave access to safe and effective reporting systems including knowing when theywill get a response to their report and having the right to report anonymously. SECDwill ensure a survivor centered approach that ensures the safety and confidentialityof the affected person. The survivor will be an active participant in the processes. Apoor handling of allegations of sexual explanation and abuse can put theaccuser/survivor, alleged perpetrator and others at risk.All SECD staff are receiving regular training on the code of conduct and PSEA policy and they signed on these policies, The contractors have the obligation to sign an ethical declaration with a specific clause on PSEA with an emphasis on mitigation measures and sanctions in case of abuse by an employee, Beneficiaries will receive information on the CRM and the expected behavior of SECD staff and its contractors with an emphasis on how to report any SEA incidents.


11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumptions: The targeted areas are stable, and no large-scale military operations areanticipated, procurement and shipment roads/lines are stable, and relations with theEducation Directorates and local authorities are accessible. Risk 1: COVID-19 pandemicoutbreak in the project targeted areas. Mitigating measures: SECD had already measuresfrom the beginning of the outbreak of the pandemic in the North-west of Syria throughsocial distance, sterilization of the centers and schools. Furthermore, in this proposal willbe distributed PPE kits. Risk 2: Out the blue new wave of displacement, Likelihood:medium Impact: High mitigating measures: The security situation in the project locationswill be followed, and integration with other stakeholders to provide emergency services.Risk 3: Sudden targeting to the project areas: Likelihood: High Impact High mitigatingmeasures: Activation of the e-learning through supporting the teachers and students withinternet packages. Risk 4: Stealing materials such as schools’ assets Likelihood: Low,Impact: high, mitigating measures: MoUs will be signed with the local councils or the localauthorities to protect the materials. Risk 5: Sudden closure of the cross border with TurkeyLikelihood: medium, Impact: high, mitigating measures: Some materials are available inthe local market yet there are missing materials, coordination with the Turkish governmentto allow the humanitarian assistance to be delivered.
