Project Summary

With the passage of nearly 13 years since the Syrian crisis and the economic, social and health repercussions it has caused, it has caused many problems and poor access to many services.
More than 80% of Syria's population suffers from difficulty accessing services due to damage to public facilities and service sectors in general and the health sector in particular.
The idea of the project lies in preparing medical teams consisting of a doctor and 4 nurses from the people of the area in which they are scheduled to work. These teams are subjected to a special course for the project to familiarize them with the conditions and restrictions of the program, which aims to provide home medical services to patients whose condition may develop dangerously if they do not They receive appropriate treatment. How much will these teams contribute to providing home health services, such as giving needles, installing and removing catheters, and providing first aid services, in addition to the possibility of transferring a number of patients, if their need arises, to a contracted pharmacy to give them the appropriate medication based on the doctor’s opinion?
Activating such a program reduces the financial burden on families and facilitates access to health services for them, in addition to contributing to supporting the health sector and relieving pressure on it by providing such home services.
It will also contribute to supporting the stability of nurses and doctors, whose number in the health sector is suffering due to their travel outside Syria with the intention of working to cover their personal expenses through the monthly salaries they will receive.
It will also contribute to the development of medical conditions from acute diseases treated with some medications to diseases that require surgical intervention.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Through its activities, the project seeks to provide support to patients who cannot access medical services in their areas due to their economic situation by providing them with appropriate medical services provided by medical teams, which contributes to their condition not developing into a more dangerous situation.This project also contributes to ensuring the stability of a large number of nurses by providing them with financial support that helps them meet their daily needs.The project seeks to target 50 areas in As-Suwayda Governorate with medical teams consisting of 4 nurses and a doctor who contribute to providing these home medical services and providing medicine through a group of pharmacies for the most needy cases according to prescriptions from the supervising physician.The project seeks to serve at least 100,000 people in As-Suwayda Governorate
