Project Summary

Albustaan handicap Association (AHA) has been operational in Waqooyi galbeed region since 2001 as a non-governmental and non-profit-making organization by contrivuting to the better living conditions of Somaliland Communitties including IDPs. In accordance with Somalia 2019 Post Gu FSNAU FEWS-NET technical release together with FEWS-NET-FSNAU-Somalia food security outlook report, we found that approximately 2.2 million people will experience crisis (IPC3) or emergency (IPC4) outcomes in september 2019 in Somalia. these people are reportedly in urgent need of humanitarian food assistance and interventions to prevent high levels of acute malnutrition. These reports indecated that in Waqooyi galbeed region 159000 people will be in stressed (IPC2), 142000 people will be in Crisis (IPC3) and other 31000 people will be in emergency (IPC4).
To address the critical food insecurity faced by the drought and cyclone affected IDPs and host communities living in and around Hargeisa, Gebiley and Berbera Districts in Waqooyi galbeed region, Albustaan Handicap Association (AHA) is prepared to improve access to basic food needs, enhanced economic opportunity and restored livelihood assets for vulnerable IDPs and host communities in Waqooyi galbeed region. This project was designed to target 5,600 drought and cyclone affected households comprising 33,000 individuals (Men: 13,200, Women: 19,800, Boys:5,280, Girls: 11,880) who are in critical need of humanitarian assistance.
In the project period, the project will achieve the following activities and outputs:
• Most vulnerable households receive unconditional cash transfer to improve their food security
• Agro-pastoralists will be trained in agricultural good practices, supported with high yielding and ecologically suitable seeds and silos.
• Community productive assets will be identified and rehabilitated through conditional cash transfers.
• Peri urban youth will be trained in appropriate alternative income sources and supported with start-up capital (conditional cash) or in-kind kits to start their businesses
