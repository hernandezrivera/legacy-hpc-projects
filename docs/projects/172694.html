Project Summary

Poor rains in the 2019/20 rainy season resulted in poor pastures throughout the district, triggering fear that livestock will succumb to starvation in their thousands before the next rainy season. The targeted activities will include provision of improved indigenous chickens to 500 beneficiaries in wards 2, 3, 6 and goats to 500 beneficiaries in wards 5, 9, and 11.

8 - Accountability to Affected People
8.1 - Describe how your project was designed in consultation with communities? How will you incorporate communities in project implementation and what feedback mechanisms do you have in place?

Guided by CRS’ MEAL Policies and Procedures; Safeguarding Policy and Protection Policy as well as global humanitarian standards, the project will put in place a robust people centered accountability framework that prioritizes the safety and well-being of affected individuals of Beitbridge District. The framework will encompass the participation and consultation of women, men and marginalized populations we serve, information provision and establishment and maintenance of feedback and response mechanisms. The project will employ a participatory approach and project participants will be consulted to inform decision making throughout the life of the project. CRS and partner will ensure the sharing of relevant project information in a timely, accessible and inclusive manner to enable effective participation in project activities by target participants. The project will design an accountability mechanism that allows participants to give feedback on project implementation. The project will likely make use of a Toll-Free line and conventional feedback mechanisms such as suggestion boxes and helpdesks. Project participants will be engaged in a process to identify preferred feedback and response mechanisms. Operational guidelines will be put in place to ensure transparent and systematic handling of complaints and timeous provision of feedback. CRS and partners will reflect on community feedback during monthly meetings, or earlier when need arises, and use the information to inform project decision making and future programing.

9 - Environmental considerations
9.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project will promote less use of agro-chemicals such as soil amendments and will promote use of organic manure for soil nutrition. Training on safe use of agro-chemicals will be done where their use in inevitable. Production of fodder types is currently being done without much use of pesticides by the farmers in the operational area as most if anot all fodder types are pest tolerant. In order to promote soil moisture conservation the project will promote use of improved irrigation methodologies such as drip irrigation to save water. If any chemical fertilizers are used, the project will recommend use of environmentally sound, Eco-friendly and approved types that do not result in pollution of soil or water and incorporate environmentally sound disposal measures of used agro-chemical containers
