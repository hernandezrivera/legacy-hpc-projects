Project Summary

This project is a 2 year project which is aiming at creating long and short term sustainability for vulnerable individuals in the North West region. The first phase of this project will be the drafting of an early recovery framework for youths in the north west and South west region of Cameroon. The objective of this framwork is Assessing the socio-economic impact of the Anglophone crises on youths in NOSO; providing guidance to government, local partners and stakeholders to plan and implement recovery for youths in the short, medium and long term; and charting out a transformational roadmap (policy and institutional reforms, programmatic investments, financing mechanisms etc) to effectively reduce the socio-economic impact of the crises or other current related challenges (like COVID-19) on youths.
The second phase will involve the establishment of a training center to provide a wider range of skills and vocational training both online and onsite to improve livelihoods in the North west region of Cameroon. This project will target 100 youths aged between 18 to 25 years and will focus on building capacity on life skills, economic empowerment and education on their sexuality. A baseline assessment will be conducted in 5 high priority divisions within the North west region to identify 20 youths per division. All 100 youths will be enrolled in a vocational course online and will be attached to an onsite workshop for hands on practical experience. At the end of this drilling, all participants will be provided partial funding which will support them start their ventures. In addition all candidates will be allocated a mentor who will continue to provide technical support to enable them thrive in their respective domains.

Two consultants will be recruited : The consultants will work with other stakeholders on the field to prepare an inception report; a literature review; identification of key data resources; and preliminary consultations with other stakeholders. The inception report will include the proposed methodology, a timeline, a coordination mechanism, an annotated outline of the Framework, and preliminary references. The inception report will provide to the government, stakeholders and partners an high level overview of the current impact of the crises on youths as well as outline the structure of the framework.2. The project will require the development of an online training platform: This platform will support the online training of youths on major courses like entrepreneurship, branding and business development and other soft skills like self resilience, communication, stress management etc. This platform will also be linked to other international training hubs to enable cross collaboration with youths in other countries in Africa. 3. Five Coaches and 5 trainers : They will be recruited to adapt existing curricula to the Cameroon context and further use it to train these youths. They will also develop an assessment tool to support the selection of the vulnerable youths in the different districts.4.
