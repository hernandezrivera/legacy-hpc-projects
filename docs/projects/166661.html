Project Summary

(1) Ensure support on protection matters through the work of community based activists, who identify protection risks and gaps, provide referrals to protection services to the most vulnerable and disseminate information to the community on topics such as GBV, TiP and VaC.The community activists will be trained on COVID-19 prevention, serve as focal points in IDP sites and host communities across Sofala, Manica and Cabo Delgado and have mobile phones and PPE so that the teams can supervise and guide them remotely and safely, promptly delivering new relevant orientations and retrieving information on the situation on site and rumour dissemination so to act accordingly. Local community radios will be supported and Covid-19 related messaging will be broadcasted in the communities through speakers; (2) Support the Government of Mozambique in the operationalization of the Magwaza Transit Centre where returning migrants from South Africa are tested and informed about the procedures and prevention measures in relation to COVID-19.
