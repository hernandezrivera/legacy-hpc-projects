Project Summary

The COVID-19 outbreak in Nigeria and the nationwide suspension of basic services, including air and interstate travels, continue to have negative effects on every sector of human life thus increasing the cost of living for the population. The situation is even worse for a population that depends entirely on humanitarian support. The call for a more improved hand hygiene practices, has further increased the consumption of soap and water within households , and has created the need for household level hand washing buckets. The Government has allowed continuation of humanitarian work but with national guidelines that are domesticated by different sectors. Training sessions on COVID-19 and protective materials for field staff and community volunteers need to be included in every intervention. However, with measures to protect staff and beneficiaries, hence the use of sanitizers to disinfect distribution materials and devices between different users. Due to the demand by the State Emergency Management Agency (SEMA) to restrict gatherings of people for intervention, JDPH will carry out door to door sensitization and awareness on COVID -19 to Prevent the spread of the virus, and properly enlighten the populace with focus on social distancing (emphasized in the President’s address of 13/04/2020), hygiene and awareness. This requires more community-based hygiene promoters to carry out COVID-19 awareness and distribute hand washing materials (soap, buckets). Information Education and Communication ( IEC) materials will be printed and distributed in many cases for awareness. Audio or pre-recorded public address system will be used to conduct awareness and coordinate with health sector on information sharing. JDPH works in Michika, Bazza, Hong, Uba (Adamawa state), Mussa, Tampul, Nwitambaya, Kwajaffa and Monguno (Borno state), Dugum, Garin Bawa,Angwan Rijia, Damagun (Yobe state), we would address the above mentioned needs there. All the mention LGAs are located at the edge of Cameroon, Niger and Chad Républic boarders which are known to have recorded substantial number of cases. With recent reported mass movement of Arm Opposition Groups (AOGs) into Nigeria, the virus could also be transported by AOGs. These locations already have annual history of cholera outbreak due to factors which include, congestion and sanitation challenges, hence the fast spread of COVID-19 is possible.
To Prevent the spread JDPH will Conduct training for staff and volunteers carrying out awareness on COVID-19 key messages, as well as on how to protect themselves as recommended by the WASH Sector in its COVID-19 – Hygiene Promotion Protocols. Six boreholes will be drilled with three rehabilitated to increase access to portable water.
Also, we will conduct hygiene promotion with focus on COVID-19 prevention messages without physical contact with beneficiaries. We will use Governmental and sector approved messages, have pre-recorded messages in various widely spoken languages and have same messages disseminated using megaphones or public address systems by locally recruited field staff. Distribute hand washing materials (soap, buckets, etc.) to targeted beneficiaries as recommended by the WASH Sector. JDPH would also include business owners and work with health actors to share information and training facilitation.
The project aims to target 135 households in each of the thirteen communities listed above with the implementation strategy listed through the effective use of gatekeepers in each of these communities. This intervention will involve a balanced involvement of men, women and people with disabilities with regard to the culture of the host communities thus upholding the do-no-harm principle during intervention. JDPH intends to leverage on its ongoing projects on food security and livelihood, WASH, Peacebuilding and Protection in 13 communities spread across Adamawa, Borno and Yobe states supported by ICCO and Kerk inActie. 1 - Needs

Due to the recent pandemic that is currently ravaging the world, this project will address the following needs: Provision of boreholes and rehabilitation of existing ones: The sedimentary terrain at Monguno and Garin Bawa has made it extremely difficult to have access to portable water for IDPs and host communities because the aquifer (water bearing permeable rock) could go as deep as 200 metres, thus only the provision of a motorized borehole could greatly help cushion the negative effect of limited water supply. Also, other communities like: Uba, Garaha, Michika, Nwitambaya have very limited supply of portable water and will need additional water points,thus the need for hand pump boreholes to help solve this problem especially with the current happenings as it relates to COVID-19. Similarly, Bazza, Kwajaffa and Nwitambaya communities have existing boreholes that are non functional, therefore, the urgent need to rehabilitate them in order to increase water points which will in turn encourage social distancing at these points thereby reducing the spread of COVID-19. Provision of hygiene kits: Most of these IDPs and host communities cannot readily afford and have access to preventive hygiene kits like: Medicated soaps, hand sanitizers and reusable face mask, therefore, the need to include them in the hygiene kits that we will make readily available to curb the widespread of COVID-19.Provision of hand washing points: To encourage hand hygiene, standard hand washing points at strategic locations are needed to be situated for the general populace in order to maintain high level of hygiene. Lack of proper awareness of IDPs and host communities on the nature, effect and dangers of COVID 19, there are insufficient mediums in accessing information, there is high level of ignorance and some are living in denial: There seems to be insufficient awareness and understanding in rural communities on the Coronavirus pandemic which is preventing respect for social distancing. There are also strong religious beliefs that hinder respect for social distancing, certain cultural values are inimical to the success against the fight. There is the age long issue of hygiene associated generally with people, IDP/host communities. insufficient personal hygiene such as washing of hands, open defecation,public hygiene such as poor waste disposal systems that breathe diseases, pre-existing diseases and infections will further aggravate COVID19 and its spreadThe increasing rate of sexual and gender based violence on women and young girls due to the restrictions on movement and total lockdown to prevent the spread of COVID19 .In collaboration with the community stakeholders, we will identify aged persons in these communities with underlying health challenges that makes them more susceptible to the virus whose cases needs further medical attention are referred to other NGOs in the health cluster for immediate medical care.

2 - Activities

To address the above mentioned needs:- We will carry out beneficiary registration with the use of a mobile questionnaire that will be developed in-house by our monitoring and evaluation team- The project will carry out intensive awareness creation and sensitization of IDPs/Host communities on the nature and effect of COVID-19.- Boreholes will be drilled in the aforementioned communities to help mitigate the problems that usually arise as a result of inadequate portable water.- We will equally provide standard hand washing water points to encourage hand hygiene, thereby reducing the spread of this dreaded virus. - Mobilization of 20 community stakeholders in each of the 13 communities on the issue of COVID-19 and the importance of their involvement in ensuring rigorous preventive measures put in place are followed,The WASH officer will coordinate this along side trained volunteers.-We will create and design models that will further enhance our information dissemination through the printing of posters and fliers ( IEC materials) in local languages and distribute same to communities; - Employ the services of mobile public address system in each of the 13 communities for dissemination of information on the Corona virus pandemic. This will be required to run twice weekly for the 7 months of project duration.- Provision of hygiene kits {soap, sanitizers, sanitary towels} one pack to each of the one thousand seven hundred and fifty five households per month for the use of the targeted beneficiaries in these 13 communities.- Identification and management of SGBV cases in the communities. through key informants, focus group discussions. Referring the critical cases to organizations responsible for addressing such issues. This aspect of protection will be coordinated by our staff who is a clinical psychologist alongside trained case workers in each community.- Identification of sick IDPs and those with low immunity for referral to secondary and Tertiary caregivers by collaborating with NGOs offering such services and government health facilities and will be coordinated by our health staff who will work closely with identified NGOs/Government health facilities and also trained volunteers on the necessary tips to check blood sugar and blood pressure with BP apparatus, check temperature of beneficiaries.- We will design and print tallies/vouchers for beneficiaries.- Monitoring will be done throughout the entire life cycle of the project to ensure all activities are effectively and efficiently executed. The organization's capable project team will conduct weekly reviews to review work done and ensure project activities are achieved. JDPH monitoring and Evaluation officer will pay monthly visits to the project implementation communities to conduct monitoring of activities, thus documenting expected results and output in accordance with the monitoring and evaluation schedule.

We are carrying out WASH activities in 12 communities, drilling boreholes, rehabilitating water points, We are rebuilding community trust by promoting dialogue and mediation processes through training and setting up community early warning monitors and facilitating traditional community theatre for dialogue .The above mentioned projects are targeted at IDPs and host communities towards rehabilitation and strengthening socio-economic re-integration.This current project will leverage on the existing above mentioned projects being implemented meeting up the Humanitarian Development Nexus.

Provisions will be made for suggestion and complain boxes at designated locations in each community.3. Certain focal key informants, shall be identified and designated with the responsibility of information gathering from each community and sent to the Accountability officer in the office for assessment and to enable door to door feedback for persons who cannot write into the suggestion box or have access to mobile phones.4.
