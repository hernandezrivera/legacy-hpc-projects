Project Summary

The project aims at supporting vulnerable people (men, women, boys and girls) from different communities (IDPs, returnees and host community) with the needed protection services taking into consideration the variations in needs among all groups.
This project will support the already exist community center in Almidan in order to expand its activities due to the heavy load of beneﬁciaries reaching it (daily number of beneﬁciaries approaching AlMidan center is around 1000 person per day).( This expansion will include General protection and child protection activities, this will not be duplicated by existing GBV activities implemented in partnership with UNFPA and CP ones implemented in partnership with UNHCR and they will be conducting in different times )And to establish 7 community centers, one in Damascus in Rukenaldeen area and 6 centers in Rural Damascus (Harasta, Kafr batna, Arbin, Darayya, Duma, Babella) ( Activities in Harasta and kafr batna will include other activities than GBV related ones, whereas all kinds of protection activities will be implemented in Darayya, Arbin, and Babella, while GP and MRE will only be implemanted in Duma and to establish 3 Women and girls safe spaces (WGSS) in AlZabadani- Maliha, and Qudsiay where there are no other UN prtners of AlTamayoz that support those activities.

4 - Needs

As per draft HNO 2020 protection chapter,The protracted nature of the crisis continues to create increasingly complex and inter-connected protection issues and needs that affect all population groups. Repeated and prolonged displacement, exposure to violence, deepening poverty, disintegration of social support structures, and the persistent challenges in access and availability to services continue to take a huge toll on children.Adolescent boys are more likely to be involved in child labour,while adolescent girls are particularly at risk of child marriage,and other forms of gender-based violence including sexual violence. Widowed and divorced women and girls and those with disabilities continue to face stigmatisation, discrimination, sexual violence, and forced marriages. women and girls have taken on additional roles including as heads of households and principal breadwinners although they have more responsibilities Girls and boys with disabilities are often at heightened risk of forms of violence, abuse, neglect or exploitation. Older people, in particular women, continue to have speciﬁc protection needs.The scale, severity, and complexity of the explosive hazard threat remains a major protection concern. one in two people in Syria live at risk of explosive hazards. According to AlTamayoz presence and assessments in Rural Damascus especially in East Ghota from June 2018 till December2019,many protection concerns have been observed: 90% of women are exposed to protection risks, ratio of female household families is about 73%, Early marriage is observed in 70% of the families. Most women prefer not to talk about their risks and there is a huge need for raising awareness about GBV issues. Elderly percentage is high in East Ghota and old people are at higher risk of being deprived from protection services. Injuries related to explosive materials have been observed.The most important child protection risks observed are child labor,dropping out of school,child marriage

5 - Implementation of activities

Activities in Damascus will be implemented in 2 areas where a density of IDPs and overburdened host community exist: AlMidan in the South and Rukneldin in the North. This project will support the already exist community center in Almidan in order to expand its activities due to the heavy load of beneﬁciaries reaching it (to establish 7 community centers, one in Damascus in Rukenaldeen area where AlTamayoz observed very high need,and 6 centers in Rural Damascus (Harasta, Kafr batna, Arbin, Darayya, Duma, Babella) and to establish 3 Women and girls safe spaces (WGSS) in AlZabadani- Maliha, and Qudsiay Activities will be implemented in all mentioned areas with consideration of coordination with other humanitarian actors in order to provide a complementary services and avoid duplication.
