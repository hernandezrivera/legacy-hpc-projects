Project Summary

CRS and its partner, the Archdiocese of Bamenda, is implementing a 12-month project to meet immediate humanitarian needs and improve food insecurity resulting from the Anglophone Crisis in the Northwest (NW) Region in Cameroon. The Anglophone Crisis Emergency Response II (ACER II) will provide food rations and non-food items (NFIs) through electronic vouchers (e-vouchers) on CRS’ Cash and Asset Transfer (CAT) Platform to vulnerable displaced and host community households in Mezam and Momo Divisions.
ACER II will reach 18,900 individuals in 2,700 HH from April 2021 through March 2022 by providing e-vouchers for food and NFIs. Providing e-vouchers for food under the Food Assistance Sector will directly address people’s barriers to accessing and consuming sufficient, diverse diets to improve food security.
The project will reach vulnerable displaced and host community households living in existing urban and peri-urban communities as well as informal settlements in the intervention area.
Community and vulnerable groups' representatives will actively participate in all activities, including targeting and sensitization exercises. Community input and feedback will be central in gauging the appropriateness of the program strategy; feedback will be used for programmatic decisions, such as changing the frequency or timing of transfers, setting price ceilings and changing the food and NFI baskets. Community participation will be ensured through the Feedback and response mechanism and regular analysis of incoming data, community and individual consultations including focus group discussions, phone interviews, or through M&E activities including Onsite and post distribution monitoring.
