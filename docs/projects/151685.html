Project Summary

In view of the urgent need to improve the quality of education in the Misurata municipality, because of the increasing number of displaced families that provided to Misrata causing overcrowding in schools,
In this sense, it is necessary to seriously consider improving the situation of students by increasing the capacity of schools,The project is expected to benefit 5,000 students, 3,000 girls, 1,500 boys and 500 teachers,The number of prefabricated classroom expected to be implemented is 45 chapters
