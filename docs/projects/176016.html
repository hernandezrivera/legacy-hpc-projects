Project Summary

The recent attacks by insurgents in Cabo Delgado province have caused internally displaced people to move to Nampula province, being economically and socially marginalized. Our plan is to support the activation of agricultural production, guaranteeing food self-sufficiency in Corrane and in the families that receive the displaced.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

This project proposes to support displaced people from Cabo Delgado who live in the Meconta district at the Administrative post of Corrane in Nampula province, through humanitarian intervention to reduce its vulnerability with actions aimed at its immediate and long-term livelihood. KULIMA will distribute agricultural seeds (Orange-fleshed sweet potato seed; corn; rice; cowpeas peanut and cassava stakes), and agricultural production instruments (hoes, knives, machetes, watering cans, and rakes) and the respective fertilizer organic agents for soil enrichment respecting the lines of Conservation Agriculture.The target group of the project includes all displaced people who are in the center of Corrane, but mainly for women and girls in needy situations, such as single mothers and unaccompanied girls and heads of households in a total of 9.000 people and an addition of 1000 local people, to facilitate their integration.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

In coordination with economic activity district services (SDAE) and district planning and infrastructure services (SDPI) at the district level of Meconta and with local authorities, land for cultivation will be defined,for the benefit of the Displaced. After guaranteeing the land to be explored, we can define the various stages of the program as follows: Distribution of agricultural inputs (seeds for 1 season (dry season) and field materials):(1) Support income-generating activities, including training vocational, that is:(i) Agricultural crop management;(ii) Use of fruit plants trees;(iii) Consortium with other species, among other techniques.(iv) Provide farmers with equipment and training to support pest management, post-harvest handling, storage and processing(2) Rehabilitation of agriculture-related infrastructure, including water catchment,(3) post-harvest structures and markets.(4) Food for Work, in order to recover roads and other infrastructures community for the benefit of all,(5) Small breeding species and Pisciculture, in order to enrich and diversify the engagement of producers, especially women.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

For a suffering population like this, who left everything they have and seeks shelter, who even witnessed the brutality of those closest to them, the project will seek to identify specific cases that require more personalized intervention and follow them up. At most, it will be avoided to disclose the identity and location of the displaced to ensure their safety. The target audience's involvement will be made through a participatory and inclusive approach that will allow direct contact with the community.The identification of the target group will be done through a baseline study that will facilitate their involvement, which will have a very important role in the design of assessment instruments for the participation mechanism, with a view to achieving significant community participation in the preparation activities. , reducing the risk of being excluded and neglected in the humanitarian response and, consequently, being disproportionately affected by the impacts of the crises. And also the training of personnel in terms of preventing sexual exploitation and abuse, code of ethics and professional conduct in the area in order to avoid attitudes. Discriminatory in the supply of kits, food products and / or agricultural inputs / seeds.In this context, the humanitarian preparation will count on the participation of Women and the elderly to improve the inclusion results for a diverse group of people, another factor is to see the elderly and / or Women as homogeneous groups, in order to obtain better scores of Number of people who receive subsistence support in resettlement sites and host communities;

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

The success of any activity involves the involvement of the main beneficiaries. Thus, starting from the consultation with the possible beneficiaries where their needs and possible solutions were identified.Beneficiaries are primarily responsible for the success of this project, and must be involved in all phases of the project, whether in community mobilization or in direct intervention actions.Interest groups will be formed among the participants who will make the connection with the project management. This group will be responsible for collecting information about whether or not they are good about project intervention, but any beneficiary will also be free to channel their opinions about the project into a structure that will be created to address these cases. Anonymity will be guaranteed for the most controversial issues of the project, to encourage beneficiaries to report negative cases of the intervention.In addition to the mechanisms mentioned above, complaint boxes will be created in the project areas. It is known that there are complaints boxes in state institutions, but they are rarely, if not, never opened. In the case of this project, there will be a team with the responsibility to regularly watch over these instruments. The project will always seek, directly or indirectly, to collect the level of satisfaction of the beneficiaries

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The Disability for you it is vulnerability and with the current situation the situation is even more complicated.Through leadership in the displaced community, people with disabilities in the center will be identified and given priority in project intervention. Likewise, links will be made with various institutions that specifically care for this group of people through reference services such as Social Action, ADEMO and others.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The issue of respect for the environment is the subject of the moment. In the intervention of this project, we will seek to respect this principle by raising awareness of the correct use of natural resources that avoid harming the environment.We will encourage respect for the sanitation of the environment, because in addition to preserving the environment, it also protects the contamination of various diseases such as diarrhea, cholera and others.
