Project Summary

With this project, UOSSM seeks to contribute to achieving the following objectives:
- Increase access to life-saving and life-sustaining coordinated, equitable humanitarian health services for those most vulnerable and in need.
- Strengthen health sector capacity to prepare for, detect and deliver a timely response to disease outbreaks.
The project aims to address the gap in response to the COVID-19 pandemic in Northwest Syria by supporting 5 CCTCs which are the most prioritized centers recommended by the COVID-19 task force. The centers are ongoing and funded by OCHA until end of August 2021, the CCTCs provide services for treating mild and moderate cases of COVID-19, as they are considered the largest percentage of confirmed cases, ensuring that the severe cases in which the morbidity is high will be referred to the case management hospitals and ICU. The screening will be conducted for all suspected cases of COVID-19 that meet the criteria, the swab (PCR) will be taken and if it is confirmed, the mild and moderate cases will be admitted, isolated, and treated (except those that are asymptomatic with suitable home circumstances for quarantines), thus containing the spread of the COVID-19 pandemic and reducing morbidity and mortality rates. The project also includes educating patients on how to use PPE and training them in practice on healthy behavior.
Each center will be as WHO/HC model that includes 4 areas:
- The screening areas: This area is an entry point, in which under strict IPC precaution measures patients will be checked based on screening questionnaires. Patients who are negatively screened (i.e., they do not fill the case definition criteria) will be referred to the health facilities, while suspected cases matching case definition criteria will be referred to observation areas for further medical assessment.
- The observation areas: For cases that were highly suspected cases of people having been in contact with suspected cases and they underwent laboratory confirmation testing, (the admission, referral, or home isolation) decision will be taken.
- The patients will be sorted according to the preliminary results of the examination, either for isolation and treatment, or for being referred to a higher level of care or sent home, as for very mild cases, and if the conditions in the home are suitable for isolation, the patient will be transferred to their home and followed by the community volunteer in their area as a part of coordination plan of the health cluster with partners
- Isolation and case management section: cases will receive medical assessment and supportive therapy for confirmed cases with mild symptoms and without underlying chronic conditions.
- Recovery area: cases received final assessment and preparation for discharge cases.

5 - Implementation of activities

Provide essential PHC services, by selecting accessible locations, recruiting medical staff, training the staff, providing the following services: conducting PCR for the suspected cases admission for confirmed and mild cases and refer the severe cases to hospitals.Provide health facilities with essential medicines and medical supplies, by preparing the list of essential medical supplies according to the COVID 19 Task Force that meet the needs of the beneficiaries, purchasing process, delivering the supplies to HFs. Strengthen the capacity of health care providers and CHWs, by conducting training need assessment, providing training packages based on the need assessment results, conducting supervision visits to evaluate the improvement in the capacity of the trained staff.Increase access to health services by supporting 5 CCTCs and rehabilitating them to be more suitable for beneficiaries and creating areas that secure the privacy of patients and prevent sexual exploitation as well as to ensure easy access for PWDs, -creating service mapping of the different humanitarian actors, and coordinating with health partners in the area to create referral pathways linked with the CCTCs.Coordinate the humanitarian health response through participating in health sector coordination meetings held across response hubs.M&E activities: inclusion of the M&E team in the designing process and activate their role by conducting regular visits, preparing the satisfaction reports, highlighting the needs and feedbacks of beneficiaries, insuring the reporting(4Ws, HeRAMS, EWARS/EWARN, and the surveillance system of attacks on healthcare).

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

1- Risk-based violence, gender, and sexual exploitation as a result of overcrowding and shared toilets as well as the exploitation of staff to patients in exchange for service delivery: possibility: high, effect: high.Mitigating measures: Establishing separated toilets for men and women -Ensuring that the waiting areas are sufficient for patients –and activating the appointment by phone-Allocating the guard to regulate the flow of patients –Establishing an easy-to-reach complaints mechanism (complaints box, WhatsApp number, exit interviews). Where UOSSM adopted this mechanism in the ongoing PHCs and benefited from the feedback of beneficiaries in redesigning the project to meet the beneficiaries' needs and that shows the engaging of beneficiaries in the in-project designs-Training all employees on the policy of preventing harassment in the workplace and including this policy in their contracts.2- Neglecting the most vulnerable (children, women, the elderly, and the disabled): possibility: moderate, effect: highmitigating measures: -Employing female staff to ensure that services are being provided to women–Ensuring that the logistics are suitable for elderly and disabled people (e.g wheelchairs)- Activating the role of M&E team to evaluate the satisfaction of patients.3- Discrimination in service delivery: possibility: moderate, effect: high. mitigating measures: - A comprehensive services mapping of the area - Activating the role of the CHWs to communicate with the population in the targeted communities and to identify the main needs - Selecting accessible locations of HFs. Monitoring the risks: Post-monitoring/client feedback forms are used. Resources Required: Monitoring staff.
