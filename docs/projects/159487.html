Project Summary

This project is to strengthen the protection and address life-saving needs of IDP families through Shelter/NFIs response in Banadir, Lower Shabelle (Afgoye Corridor) and Bay regions in South and Central Somalia targeting to the new arrivals, forced evictions, and protracted displaced IDP families of those affected by natural hazards. It is designed to mitigate the risk and its consequences on the affected IDP families in the targeting areas. The main objective is to improve the living conditions and safe access to services of IDP families including refugee returns in the targeted locations. HINNA aims at providing 6500 NFI kits to 6,500 HHs (39000 beneficiaries/Individuals) - (18,720 female and 20,280 male), 10% (3,900 individuals) of them will be vulnerable host community, through in-kind distribution to improve the living conditions of the target beneficiaries. 1500 IDP families (9000 individuals – (4,320 female and 4,680 male)) of 6,500 of the project target who are new arrivals and forced evictions will further receive 1500 ESKs through direct construction.
Direct beneﬁciaries of the project is totaled 6500 HHs (39,000 individuals) including men, women, boys, and girls who will automatically be receiving any beneﬁts of the project through selection criteria taking into account gender mainstreaming, 3500 of them (21,000 individuals) for Banadir, 1000 (6,000 Ind.) for Afgoye (Afoye Corridor) of Lower Shabelle region, and 2000 (12,000 Ind.) for Badoa district of Bay region.
