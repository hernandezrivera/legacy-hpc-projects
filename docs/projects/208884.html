Project Summary

The integrated protection project aims to meet life-saving acute, urgent, immediate Child protection (CP) , Gender Based Violence (GBV) and protection needs of individual among newly internal displaced persons( IDPs) and their hosting communities in Aljazera (Gz) North Darfur,(ND) Central Darfur,(CD) White Nile(WN) , Northern ( NS),River Nile (RN) and Khartoum (KRT) states after the clashes between Sudan Army force (SAF) and Rapid Support force (RSF) on April 2023 and the expansion of the conflict to Darfur and Gz states. The project will strengthen community-based prevention mechanisms and improving protection response systems through : conducting protection monitoring exercises , provision of specialized services and enhancing the capacity of the local communities on protections risks, preventions, concerns and needs . The project will provide mental health and Psychosocial support services (MHPSS)including provision of Psychological first aid,, comprehensive case management , social and life skills interventions for vulnerable persons with special needs (PSNs) with focus on unaccompanied and separated children (UASC) ,GBV survivors , elderly , and persons with disabilities (PwDs). This will be provided at individual level such as through provision comprehensive case management, FTR or/and referral services at group level , provision of structured group sessions, peer to peer or /and recreation and life skills sessions .To enhance the local technical capacity, the project will train service providers and relevant stakeholders on protection ,GBV and CP risks, concerns, needs. The project will also provide specialized training on GP,CPand GBV standards and focused topics .Applying the community based approach and ensuring the accountability for affected population ,the project will establish and train of 20 community based protection networks and 20 CB feedback and complaints committees , and support the running and operationalization of 10 women centers. Safety audit exercises will be timely conducted. To enhance the local capacities the project will awareness raising program through organizing structure sessions and public information campaigns . IEC materials will be developed and disseminated to ensure better coverage with protection awareness
To ensure dignified response the project will , procure and distribute of dignity kits to vulnerable women and girls of reproductive age as well as provision of mobility aids, crutches clothes etc to PSNs , The project will conduct protection monitoring Improving community awareness on protection violations, risks and prevention through conducting public awareness campaigns protection‐related issues, during emergencies and availability of services.

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

NADA recruited and will continue to recruit suitable project staff from targeted communities, staff will be involved and fully dedicated to implement and follow up project activities. Project staff will be provided by two days training on PSEA, GBV, protection monitoring and reporting ,code of conduct to avoid potential risks of violence, exploitation and abuse of beneficiaries. The integrated community based approach which will be adopted during the implementation , promotes active engagement and participation of all community segments at all phases to ensure community by-in and sense of ownership. NADA team confirmed that composition of CBPNs considers balanced representation of gender, age, social status and minority to improve t decision making and allow the voices for these groups. CBPNs already received training on different, GBV and protection risks, needs and concerns to be able them to contribute to sustainability of protection education and capacity among local community. This will further support self-protection capacity to be embedded deeply in the community coping mechanisms. Following participatory and community based approaches, targeted communities will be involved in discussion, consultations and decision regarding planned activities. Through this, project intends to ensure full participation and strengthen the ownership of communities. This will contribute to provide the targeted communities with concrete plate forum to identify their concerns, needs, challenges and needed actions to improve the protective environment. APP is core activity, additionally applying Do No Harm principle to clearly done in this project through involvement and inclusion of the communities and hosting communities with equal access to direct assistance based on vulnerability assessment .As part of the Do No Harm approach, NADA will provide training to all project team members and CBPNs on PSEA and code of conduct. This will help to mitigate risk of harassment, abuse and other violation to be committed by staff members against the beneficiaries. NADA will consider the dignity and confidentiality of beneficiaries, particularly when delivering CP and GBV services in particularly provision of case management. NADA will strongly prioritize the best interest of children/ GBV survivors/beneficiary by following protocols and standards of service delivery for the identified GBV cases especially child survivors. The information about these cases will be handled and managed anonymously and confidentially. Counseling for GBV survivors will be done in a designated private space with maximum confidentiality. In addition, the project team will conduct regular consultation with CBPNs, community leaders/ members and local authorities and receive their feedback about project delivered services through complaint and feedback mechanism to identify any negative consequences that might be cause as result of the project implementation.

4.1.1 - How will the project use community feedback to improve and adapt activities? (open-ended/free text, required)4

The project is designed on community based approach where community based complaints ,feedback and response mechanisms(CBCFRM) will be established at the beginning of the project . CBCFRM will be trained on complaints and reporting .Project team will organize regular meeting to receive and address the feedback from community . This will ensure that community inputs and feedback will be used to adapt and better execute the activities.

4.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will support Inter-Agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including Sexual exploitation and abuse complaints?

Project will be implemented on participatory and community based approaches . Regular meetings and information sharing activities will be delivered to community to inform about project services. Targeted communities will continue to have equal access, participate and benefit from project services. Project will highly consider best interest of women and children when handling, sharing data and information about identified cases. Consultations with local community and line ministries will be integral part of this intervention. Project will hold consultations for full engagement of partners as an entry point for smooth implementation with clear exit strategy. Protection interventions have always been sensitive issue for authorities and communities. NADA has incorporated lessons learn from previous intervention in relation to AAP. it has been identified that extensive consultation with local authority and community as is instrumental for smooth and effective project implementation. This is considered as an area of strength for NADA, as all designed intervention have focused on this element as success factor for achieving intended project objectives. Project will also ensure constant feedback and open channels for receiving complaints. NADA will train 100 CRCs whom will receive and respond to complaints and feedback including SEA and abuse complaints .A simple written procedure will be developed, this will be coupled with a complaint box to enable submission of private, confidential or sensitive complaints. A specific tool (Beneficiary Satisfaction Form) has been designed for this purpose. Process, time for response etc should be clearly communicated to beneficiaries. Multi purpose community / Women center and NADA offices are equipped with complain box. Training of CBPNs on protection and GBV , PSEA and how to report its cases will highly contributed to APP. Community based PSS sessions will be conducted by committees and community members. This will ensure that beneficiaries’ opinion are incorporated in future intervention. Information sharing will be articulated in different ways such as readable, watchable IEC material .Awareness raising messages will be disseminated in accessible ways for PWDs. Message will be field tested among targeted groups prior to printing Project will apply Do No Harm approach through considering these sensitivities for successful project implementation without making any harm on communities. To achieve that, NADA has conducted analysis to identify any possible harm that to be caused by the intervention. The project team understands that many communities in the project areas are diversified and explored ways to unite the community; one of the ways is to promote participation of people from various ethnic backgrounds together as members of community networks, with aim to increase coherence and connectedness.
