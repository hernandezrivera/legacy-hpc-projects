Project Summary

The project will provide nutrition services for 8,400 PLWs and 18000 children under five years (1800 PWDs). Through this project, MWL is going to:
*Scale-up and continue the integrated mother and child-centered nutrition life-saving interventions
Scale-up and continue supporting the optimal infant feeding and caring practices in an emergency
Fill the current service gaps in high needs through providing emergency integrated FSL, nutrition, and child protection rapid services through Rapid Response teams (RRT).
Continue and strengthen the existing program for managing acute malnutrition among children under five and PLWs.
The scope of interventions will include:
1st:Scaling up integrated nutrition response support through the Rapid Response teams which will be dispatched to the areas of high IDP flow ( Afrin, AlBab subdistricts in Aleppo governorate, (Dana and Salqin sub-districts in Idleb governorate), to provide the following activities:
- 700 PLWs and 1200 Children will be referred and follow up.
Provide emergency IYCF counseling
Disseminating messages on IYCF-E recommended feeding practices
Distributing micronutrients for preventing anemia and malnutrition
Identify visual signs of abuse/violence against childU5/PLWs during Nutrition screening.
2nd: Continue functioning of the OTP-SFP services through protection centers in Afrin and Salqin subdistricts.
3rd: Capacity building of 50 frontline nutrition services providers on IYCF-E and CMAM programs in the context of COVID-19.
As well as, MWL will train the caregivers on MUAC and edema screening, referral, recognize signs of deterioration.

4 - Needs

HRP 2020 highlighted that an estimated 7.9 million people in Syria are food insecure and an additional 1.9 million people are at risk of food insecurity. HRP clarified that 4.6 million suffer the nutrition insecure. Based on Nutrition cluster, there are at least (148503 children under 5 in Dana) and (34058 in Salqin) is chronically malnourished.The Nutrition Cluster Coordination meeting also declared (137 of 3639 lactating women) and (138 of 2931 Pregnant) are in need for urgent lifesaving nutrition services. The proxy prevalence of acute malnutrition among pregnant and lactating women (PLW) is at 11 percent. A total of 31,444 children 6-59 months were screened of which 516 were MAM and 45 were SAM. There were no oedema cases. A total of 7,919 PLW were screened. MWL has conducted a need assessment targeting 29 random IDPs camps in Dana and Salqin in which MWL distributes free daily bread. MWL has found that there are 936 families with 1 or more PLW, 1456 families with 2 or more children under 5, and 2360 big families (5 or more members).From the collected information, all the respondents suffered from the absence of any kind of nutrition support. More than 95% mentioned that they secure the vegetables and fruits from the local markets. Moreover, 95% of them know about malnutrition cases in their camps and about 75% of them know about stunted disease cases.

5 - Implementation of activities

The scope of interventions will include:1st:Scaling up integrated nutrition response support through the Rapid Response teams which will be dispatched to the areas of high IDP flow ( Afrin, AlBab subdistricts in Aleppo governorate, (Dana and Salqin sub-districts in Idleb governorate), to provide the following activities: Screening 18000 children 6-59 months, and 8400 PLWs, referral and follow up who are malnourished.Disseminating messages on IYCF-E recommended feeding practices, for caregivers including PLWs. Distributing micronutrients for preventing anemia and malnutrition among children and PLWs.Identify visual signs of abuse/violence against childU5/PLWs during Nutrition screening.2nd: Continue functioning of the OTP-SFP services in two protection centers in Afrin and Salqin sub-districts, where MWL Classify SAM and MAM cases, provide SAM treatment for children with uncomplicated severe acute malnutrition using Plumpy nut (provided by UNICEF) and MAM treatment for children and PLW with MAM using LNSL-LQ plumpy sup (provided by WFP) Nutrition status and anthropometric measurements of children under five and PLW are regularly screened, recorded, reported, and required program actions are taken.3rd: Capacity building of 50 frontline nutrition services providers on IYCF-E and CMAM programs in the context of COVID-19.As well as, MWL will train the caregivers on MUAC and edema screening, referral, recognize signs of deterioration, and give them a mobile or WhatsApp number of the CHW to contact in case the child’s condition deteriorates.

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumption:All children and women especially those who may have physical limitations are able to reach services.Risk:Traditional habits prevent women from leave homesMitigation:MWL will bring the services close to communities and camps, to including all people in need with focusing on children, women and adolescent girls, especially those who may have physical limitations and/or are socially isolated, such as women who have experienced divorce, early/forced marriage and widowhood, who may not be able to access basic services including nutrition services. Assumption: No deterioration in the security situation and access to targeted communities and camps remain availableCommunity workers are able to reach the communitiesRisks: Conflict escalation and deterioration in securityIncreased checkpoints and road closures.Mitigation:Assessing the security situation and are up to date with any road closures or changes in road conditionsIf the security situation increasingly deteriorated, we can wait or postpone activities for a few days or go to change targeted areas to another accessible area in need of support, Any major change will be reported and conducted in coordination with SCHF managed by OCHA and nutrition cluster.
