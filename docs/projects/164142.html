Project Summary

Based on the WASH assessments that were conducted in June-August 2019 (HRP 2020), The assessments revealed that, despite overall slight improvement of WASH conditions in Syria, vast WASH-related needs continue to exist in many parts of Syria, affecting various population groups.
Access to sufficient and affordable safe water, adequate sanitation, solid waste management and/or hygiene supplies remain a challenge for newly displaced populations, IDPs living in IDP sites, as well as other most vulnerable households with limited livelihood opportunities and access to basic WASH services. Newly displaced populations require a timely and quality response based on comprehensive preparedness, and smooth transition into regular programming.
in this context, MF in Turkey office is remotely managing the delivery of integrated WASH assistance with main-streamed protection and advocacy in Azaz (Aleppo). Timely humanitarian relief and enhanced protection to vulnerable is delivered to those affected by emergency situations in the settlements. Basic WASH assistance is delivered to the targeted community by taking into account the different needs of men, women, boys, and girls, in addition, to take into consideration the special needs for disabilities people, especially during the infrastructure designs.
Referring to urgent needs and camp management requests to continue in providing a full package of WASH services in Bab Al Noor and Elbil camps where Maram Foundation is working currently in both camps in the same context, Maram will apply to HPC to continue providing WASH assistance in the both camps (Bab Al Noor and Elbil), in addition to that, Maram will respond to Al Resala camp to provide WASH assistance because of the poor WASH condition there and after a request from its camp management.
So, Maram is applying for HPC to implement the WASH project in three camps inside Syria (Bab Al Noor, Elbil camp and Al Resala camp), as a result of a large influx of IDPs and limited access to services, there was a significant lack of the basic WASH services.
Maram will respond to 3,000 families (HHs), 14,380 individuals living in northern camps in Aleppo Governorate with basic WASH services including drinking water, solid waste removal, desludging septic tanks, hygiene promotion, and toilet cleaning, in addition to providing some solutions Sustainable when possible, such as rehabilitation or construction of water or sewage networks. The project is designed according to SPHERE standards and WASH guidelines in line with HRP 2020 priorities.
The planned intervention will mainstream protection principles especially (Do no harm) throughout the project cycle by considering the different needs of men, women, young children, children, adolescents, young adults, middle-aged adults, and older adults. The provision of sanitation services will be consulted with the targeted camps and communities, and take into account the specific needs of each group. Maram Foundation also revised the content of hygiene kits by involving the women and girls through Focus Group Discussions (FGDs) during previews implemented projects.
The Project also designed to provide disabilities, elderly and another vulnerable group with accessible handicap infrastructure too by taking these groups needs into consideration during the design phase of these infrastructures, with particular focus on vulnerable people such as ‘People with Special Needs’. This project fits with the Allocation specific objective of Provide access to proper gender sensitive and disability WASH facilities in camps, schools, child-friendly spaces, and health facilities.

4 - Needs

The needs analysis is based on secondary reference represented with various assessments and reports from WASH Cluster. Assistance and Coordination Unit (ACU), EWARN biweekly reports. The WASH Cluster report shows that there is an increase in the percentage of the WBD especially Typhoid fever (STF) in Azaz district where the Severity Scales is 4 in Azaz camps.Furthermore, as a basic data resource MF field team has conducted a rapid need assessment as following;- Bab Al Noor and Elbil: Since MF is working to provide the WASH services in both camps (Bab Al Noor for two years while in Elbil around one year). and to assess the current situation during Maram intervenstion or any additional needs not taken into account in the camps, MF conducted the assessment. It has been confirmed that there is good satisfaction with the activities provided by Maram, in Addition, MF has received a written request from the camp administration of Bab Al Noor, grateful for the good intervention, and request to extend the project at the same level. So MF is applying for HPC to extend the project in both camps by providing full WASH services including; water supply, garbage collection, desludging septic tanks, cleaning latrines, and hygiene assistance.- Al Resala Camp: After a request and suggestion from camp management, MF visited the camp in Dec-19 by using Maram WASH assessment tool to conduct a rapid need assessment with coordination by AFAD.The objective of the assessment to assess the current situation of the camp and have an idea about the number of IDPs, the living condition, security/safety, and WASH needs, in addition to assessing the infrastructure condition. It has been confirmed that access to adequate WASH services and regular access to safe drinking water is very important for most IDPs living there.The WASH needs were: Garbage collection, hygiene assistance, desludging septic tanks, and garbage bins, in addition, to rehabilitate the sewage and water network in the camp.

5 - Implementation of activities

As mentioned in Needs paragraph, Maram will implement the following activities: - In Bab Al Noor and Elbil camps: Full WASH services including; water supply, garbage collection, desludging septic tanks (only in Bab Al Noor), cleaning latrines, and hygiene assistance, in addition, to provide all the required maintenance for water or sanitation networks.- In Al Resala camp: Maram will implement garbage collection, hygiene assistance, desludging septic tanks, cleaning latrines, and garbage bins, in addition, to rehabilitate the sewage and water network in the camp.All the activities will be implemented according to SPHERE standards and WASH cluster guidelines, through coordination with camps management and local authorities.As well as, all the technical studies (rehabilitation water and sanitation networks) will be prepared after confirming the project including construction studies, drawings, BOQs.. etc according to the needs priorities, while the daily activities (water supply, garbage collection...etc.) all PRs will be prepared before starting the project in order to start providing the service at beginning of the project period. Maram will hold regular coordination meetings with the target camps management to assess Maram's WASH intervention and update the needs and IDPs numbers.In addition and during the project life, monitoring will be conducted at every stage of the project duration, by collecting routine data and analysis them on a continuous basis.Maram M&E team will be carrying out data collection and regular monitoring of all activities, the field staff will be using the designed M&E surveys and questionnaires to collect all needed data and will report them to the M&E in Turkey, Turkey team always try to make sure that data collection tools are appropriate and tailored to the project needs. In addition to evaluating the services provided by Maram to evaluate the service and find out how it met the needs of beneficiaries of different ages.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

There are several protection risks that Maram can face during the implementation of the project, including:1-Protection risk: Toilets represent GBV and/or health, safety risk; Access to toilet is not available to all 24/7; Access to toilets not available for all; Likelihood: High, Impact: High Mitigating Measure: Ensure context/SPHERE standards and closely monitor these provisions/aspects during implementation. Ensure women and adolescent girls are consulted during the design, implementation and monitoring phase of projects. Accountability mechanisms are put in place and received feedback from users is followed up. Lessons from previous experience are shared and learned. 2- Potential exposure to explosive hazard (IED/UXO/ERW) during assessment/ rehabilitation of wastewater infrastructure (WWTP, sewer networks). Likelihood: Medium, Impact: High Mitigating Measure: Work closely with Mine Action actors in the area and conduct assessments/rehabilitation works only in areas declared as cleared; Conduct safety training with staff and contractors; Add explosive hazard awareness material to your programming. 3- Protection risk: Health risks associated with construction works and exposure to raw sewage and gases. Likelihood: High, Impact: Medium Mitigating Measure: Ensure staff/partners/contractors involved in construction works or working with raw sewage wear appropriate PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) and follow established safety regulations; Conduct/repeat safety training for staff/partners/contractors. 4-Protection risk: Inequality in distribution; Physical and psychological harm arising from violence and coercion; Timing and process of distribution put women and girls at higher risk of GBV. Likelihood: High, Impact: High Mitigating Measure: Promote Establishment of adequate management plan; Promote involvement communities in distribution planning and feedback on package for improvement.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumptions, risks, and mitigations:Risk 1: Organizations Acceptance by beneficiaries, communities and other stakeholders:CONTINGENCY / MITIGATION MEASURE: Maram Foundation will prepare a community outreach plan, will attending and conduct coordination meetings with target community members and the local authorities to explain project activities, targets, and purposes.Risk 2: Deterioration of safety and security context:CONTINGENCY / MITIGATION MEASURE: In general, the security situation will be acceptable because of the project located in the demilitarized zone, but any way Maram Foundation, in addition to the procedures that are always taken into consideration in all projects:- MF maintains a security risk management system for its areas of operation in order to adapt and respond to changes in context- MF maintains a full stakeholder and power analysis for its areas of operation in order to better understand the context- MF on a daily basis reviews security information related to its areas of operation and monitors staff movement as per the current security conditions.Risk 3: Exchange rates change significantly and therefore the budget is insufficient to reach the targeted number of beneficiariesCONTINGENCY / MITIGATION MEASURE: - MF will maintain a regular dialogue with its donor in the event of a significant change- MF conducts regular price monitoring, utilizing existing information where available- MF will contract with the contractor in the dollar to avoid the risk.Risk 4: MF unable to maintain access to the most vulnerable populations despite changes in conflict dynamics inside SyriaCONTINGENCY / MITIGATION MEASURE: MF has adopted a Whole-of-Syria structure in order to provide multiple entry-points to vulnerable affected populations and to enable a more flexible response.Risks 5: HLP clear documentsCONTINGENCY / MITIGATION: MF staff will verify all the HLP documents available through the local council and actual community members.
