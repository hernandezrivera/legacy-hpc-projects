Project Summary

Although many of the damaged water supply infrastructures in re-taken areas such as Ninewa, Kirkuk and Anbar have been partially or fully rehabilitated in 2017-2018, several locations across Iraq still have non-functional water infrastructure that requires rehabilitation. Restoration of basic level of water and sanitation services in areas of return is essential in facilitating dignified return of the population. This also includes improving waste water management at household level.
The project will address these basic WASH needs, through rehabilitation of water and sanitation infrastructure. As these infrastructures mainly belong to host populations and have been over exploited, due to the returnees, the project aims as well to improve social structures in the communities, in order to avoid social tensions and conflicts in the communities.
Hygiene practices will also be addressed and for the most vulnerable populations, basic hygiene kits will be provided through MPC distributions.

Needs

In the areas where populations have returned in 2018, needs assessments show a need for rehabilitation of WASH infrastructure. This incus as well an improvement of waste water management systems, as in many villages there is no proper community system in place. Many vulnerable returnee and host populations can also not afford basic hygiene items, which has a negative influence on sanitation & hygiene practices.

Activities

1. Rehabilitation: rehabilitation of waste water management inside the villages through cash for work, and reconstruction of sewerage line system.Cordaid will conduct hygiene promotion campaigns in the targeted schools. Cordaid would like to expand the role of school teachers in actively spreading hygiene promotion information during morning class, and within the community and other community/ religious events or gatherings. Therefore, teachers will be trained in hygiene promotion, and will be engaged in advocacy community awareness raising events organized in the schools and villages. Campaigns are focused on issues. The hygiene promotion will be complemented through a cash-for-hygiene (MPC) distribution.

Cordaid project team will work with partners hand to hand through regular technical support visits. All funds received are closely budgeted for, monitored, and accounted for through final reporting and controls at the field, national and international level. Finally anti-fraud policy in place, and Cordaid making sure that newly hired staff and partners are aware of this policy and Cordaid finance and logistic team will provide a regular visit through a monthly partner visit.3. Finally, Cordaid is expected to get the registration within 2 months in Iraq and the proposed design is in line with authorities’ regulations, community needs and Cordaid vision and mission.4. Capacity building of staff is being built in to the response planning and master budget to ensure sufficient expertise across MEAL and quality programming, although CO. Will include the partners in the training as part of the exit strategy and to keep the humanitarian team ready to act when needed.

How is your project accountable to affected population

Cordaid will work from the onset of the project together with the community, as the primary stakeholders of the project; this also to ensure that actual needs of the community are addresses. Cordaid targets a community & household level approach, to improve social structures, and will work where possible with local organisations. A complaints and feedback mechanism will also be put in place. Cordaid employees will be regularly in the area, to have clear communication and respect the dignity of the population.
