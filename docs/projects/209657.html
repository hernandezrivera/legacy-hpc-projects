Project Summary

According to the 2024 Humanitarian Needs Report issued by the Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs (OCHA), statistics indicate increased exposure to ongoing hostilities, particularly in northwest and northeastern Syria. Also limited access to services; The repercussions of the earthquake that struck the region on February 6, which often separates children from family members, including primary caregivers, have affected
There are 2.45 million children between the ages of 5 and 17 out of school, increasing reported protection risks, cases of torture, detention, kidnapping, sexual violence, deprivation, and lack of access to humanitarian assistance due to lack of civil documentation, among other factors. Wars and the obstacles resulting from them
. The project aims to reduce the risks of war remnants and enhance community awareness to deal with them by expanding the services provided at the AHF Center for Communities through increasing the following protection activities interventions: 1. Educating women, men and children. Regarding the remnants of war through mobile teams.
2. Implement case management services that address the vulnerabilities of infected people and primary care providers associated with immediate risks.
These services will be provided by specialized labor in case of management through the AHF team
3- Building the capabilities of workers in the field of case management.
The number of war remnants awareness team will be 9 awareness teams, each team including (one awareness worker
Outreach employee)
The number of victim assistance team will be 5 awareness teams, each team including (one awareness worker
Outreach employee)
The number of beneficiaries of the project will reach 34,300 beneficiaries, including men, women, girls and boys
The Al Sham Humanitarian Foundation has good access to the mentioned places, and has implemented multiple projects with many donors, the International Organization for Migration, and the SCHF organization in the mentioned places. It also has a good coordination network with actors in the region, and work will be done to sign memorandums of understanding. In order to reach polluted places in order to survey and identify them.
Cham Humanitarian Affairs also has a qualified work team with good experience regarding protection services related to awareness of war remnants, as well as survey operations. It has also previously implemented projects to protect the wounded from war remnants and the mechanism of integrating them from society.
There will be direct and regular coordination with the MA Cluster to take the required directions and notify the extent of polluted places only
Protection activities will be implemented in Jisr al-Shughur and the surrounding area, and the project will target host communities, displaced people, returnees, community centers and camps. The center will serve the most vulnerable people. All activities are consistent with the Humanitarian Response Plan as well as the Protection Cluster's strategic objectives. Services will be provided for one year. The project will coordinate with affected populations and stakeholders and is designed to promote gender balance. The project aims to include children with special needs, and all activities will be implemented in accordance with the recommendations of the Protection Cluster and the World Health Organization in order to the necessary preventive measures to protect beneficiaries and humanitarian workers. The project will be monitored by the Monitoring and Evaluation team, in order to monitor the progress of the project and ensure that activities are implemented according to the work plan and agreed indicators. The project is designed to reduce protection from sexual exploitation and abuse, through signing a code of conduct and training on the risks of sexual exploitation and exploitation.
