Project Summary

The intervention protection monitoring in grass root communities is systematically and regularly collecting, verifying and analyzing information over an extended period of time in order to identify violation of rights and protection risk for population of concern for the purpose of informing effective responses in this communities. Since the inception of the Anglophone crises, arm conflict in grass root communities have lead to serious violation of right of persons over better health care, shelter and NFI, education, food security and WASH facilities etc. In these divisions they have been constant protection issues of torture, illegal detention, targeted killing, Gender Base Violence violations, lost of property and civil status registration documents. As a result of the problem mention above IYEC a UN ECOSOC Accredited grass root NGO came out with this project in order to provide quality and timely data and information to enable evidence, inform action for quality protection out comes. It is inherently action oriented. The information collected will directly support decisions and actions that will ultimately help achieve the protection and solution objectives define by the relevant operations in the grass root communities. Every data point collected will be tied to a specific action.The objective of this project is to collect information on need of life-saving assistance or immediate support, to obtain protection trends, to access protection coping mechanisms and capabilities, Get the site location info, Map multiple services and structures, Obtain priority needs from multiple key informants, report an incident, make an individual or group referral, make observations and collect photos. The target beneficiaries are women, youth, and young girls, internally displace persons, stakeholders in the community. IYEC plan to intervene in Fako division ( west coast crick, Limbe III crick,Tiko crick), Manyu ( Akwaya, Tinto, Eyumudjock), Ndian (Toko, Mondemba,Ekondotiti, Mbenmong, Akwa, Kombo itindi). In the project IYEC will encourage IDPs in these communities to return to their place of origin in order to increase the number of returnees’ in these grass root communities.

Besoins / Needs

After an assessment carried out by IYEC. The project will address the following assessment needs.-The protection monitoring project will address the needs of qualitative and quantitative data and information on the evolution of the protection situation which include. a) Protection issues and concern in the specific location or context, such as access to adequate shelter, health, livelihoods or civil documentation.b) Protection risk and threats. c) Protection incident and human right violation and abuses such as SGBV, child protection.d) The capacities and self protection and coping mechanism of the specified population groups.-The protection monitoring project will address the assessment needs of providing quality and timely data and information to enable evidence-informed action for quality outcomes. its inherently action oriented. The information collected will directly support decisions and actions that ultimately help achieve the protection and solution objectives defined by relevant operation in the division. Every data point collected will be tied to a specific purpose.-The protection monitoring project will address the needs of trends of information flow which will help to ensure that programming is responsive to changes in the protection environment. More specifically the protection monitoring will generate trends information-evidence that can be use by humanitarian actors to a) Inform the response (protection and assistance need) to prevent and respond to protection incidents, and address protection issuesb) Inform communication messages to IDPsc) Inform advocacy messages and identify relevant authorities and humanitarian actors to prevent and address protection issues and incidents. d) Identify immediate or urgent protection cases in need of life-saving assistance, immediate support, and/ or referral.e) Plan the response ( protection and assistance need) at the border and in the CoA for person of concern/ plan the response in the expected host community,f) Identify profile of individuals who are mostly at risk of incident, to inform prevention and mitigation measures.g) Inform future protection and solution strategies, including decisions about resource mobilization and allocation, prioritization and partnerships.
