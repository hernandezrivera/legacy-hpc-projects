Project Summary

This project aims to contribute improved health standards for the prevention of cholera among 10,000 individuals living in the villages of Mundri west, Mundri East and Mvolo counties through provision of safe water supply for conflict affected populations in Mundri
Based on the existing cholera risks, project activities will be geared towards reinforcing gains made in previous WASH interventions and recent assessment needs for reduction of vulnerabilities.
Major effort will be on improving the access to basic WASH services for mitigation of morbidity and mortality related to diarrheal-illness by at list 85% among target group. Main activities will focus on behavior change that ensures water, food and hand safety at household levels, schools, public and community gathering places are at a good standard.
The project will also ensure protection of women and girls by creating access to safe water supply which is secure to reduce further risk of Sexual Gender Based incidences in Mundri West, Mundri east and Mvolo In these locations, girls and women spend most hours of the night at wells or move long distances to bushy streams to collect water. In the target areas, incidences of sexual Gender based violence were reported in the course of accessing water.
Capacity of community members will be strengthened alongside provision of sustained access to basic WASH services; and to ensure active participation of community members, key stakeholders will be involved at grass-root level. Recent needs assessments will jointly be reviewed at each project to tackle existing gaps.
15 Community Hygiene Promotion will be trained (5 per location) to improve access to hygiene knowledge with practical demonstration of messages of food hygiene, handwashing with soap, water treatment and storage. 5 Community Hygiene Supervisors will be recruited and trained to trace and report cases of acute watery diarrhea; and also to supervise bucket chlorination, disinfect households of persons with confirmed cholera cases.
Homes of malnourished children benefiting from feeding programmes will be also be traced and mothers helped to observe good hygiene habits.
Supervisors will also support events of Focused Group Discussions that are aimed at involving the community members to play active role in cholera prevention and elimination of open defecation through community based approach to curb water contamination at the sources.
Three(3) boreholes will be drilled one per location to reduce risks associated to consumption of contaminated surface water; and further risk of sexual Gender Based Violence women/girls face in the course of collecting water from isolated wells and streams in Mundri west, Mvolo and Mundri East
Three (3) boreholes will be rehabilitated in Mundri, Mvolo and Mundri West .
Six (6) water user committees will be formed and trained in each of the locations where at least 3 females and 2 males in each group); refresher training will be given for water user committees of 3 rehabilitated boreholes.
Three (3) School hygiene promotion clubs will be formed and teacher, PTA and pupils will be trained to support sustainability school hygiene.
RWDSS has own capacity to complete activities and will collaborate with the Ministry of Water Resources and irrigation during Hydro-geological survey, actual drilling, quality tests, and training of water user committees and ensure handover is properly done.
During project period RWDSS will keep participating in WASH cluster activities; and provide weekly and monthly reports. Support will also be given for other cholera mitigation activities under the
WASH/HEALTH cluster especially mobilization of target communities during Oral Cholera Vaccination campaigns, World Water Day, Global Hand washing Day and World Toilet Day.

1 - Needs

Conflicts in South Sudan have displaced about 2 million people living both in urban and rural areas. In 2016- 2017, the conflicts spread through the Equatoria region which resulted to multiple displacements, causing influx of affected population to Mundri west from Mundri east and surrounding areas; and most vulnerable affected populations are left to the bear risks associated to consequences of conflicts especially Sexual Gender Based Violence and preventable diseases. Community members are forced to bare high costs of living and struggle with poor health standards due to limited WASH services.WASH needs of an already vulnerable population are now stretched; and coping abilities are further eroded by prolonged food insecurity, economic collapse, malnutrition and diseases. Investment in WASH services declined significantly in the country; and access to improved sanitation facilities and safe drinking water is estimated to be less than 10% and 60% respectively.The affected areas in Mundri west, Mundri east and Mvolo are accessible but recent assessments indicates that the humanitarian WASH, Health, food and protection needs in the area remain at the highest level due to continued influx of displaced persons. Affected populations are those living in Mundri where there is poor access to safe and secure water and sanitation services.Open defecation, use of surface water and poor hygiene conditions are the main risks causing vulnerability. hund-dug wells are isolated in bushy environment; and in most cases open defecation fields.During recent joint assessments with County, local authorities and school authorities, more than 25,000 individuals in Mvolo, Mundri West and Mundri east are in dire need of WASH services. Community members report suffering from Acute Watery Diarrhea and blood in urine especially among the children during certain months of the year. Most of the affected populations are persons displaced during the 2016 conflicts and returnees living with host communities. In Mvolo women spend their nights at wells during dry season waiting for water, a situation which is unbearable with the current insecurity in the country. Because there were reported incidences of sexual harassment at water points, women and girls are in constant fear of sexual assault during time of fetching water. Some community members reported cases of snake bites in the process of going to the river and streams.During rainy season, surface water is highly contaminated; and high contamination and turbidity reduces the effectiveness of treating water with chlorine. During dry season, community members from different areas struggle to get water.

2 - Activities

•• Training and recruitment of 30 community hygiene promoters 10 per location and , 9 Hygiene Promotion Supervisors three per location and provision of hygiene promotion kits.• 12 Hygiene promotion sessions at households, water points, community gathering places, schools, markets including places for sale of animals.• Tracing cases of acute watery diarrhoea, water quality monitoring at water points and households and disinfection of households of persons with confirmed cholera cases.• Training of three (3) school Hygiene Promotion Clubs formed in 3 primary schools including teachers, PTA and pupils to support school hygiene promotion• Distribution of WASH NFIs including Acquatabs, PUR, Buckets and Soap; and monitoring use at households, schools and public places• Drilling of 3 new boreholes in Mundri West , Mundri east and Mvolo Counties • Rehabilitation of 3 Non-functional boreholes • Training of 3 water user committees formed and refresher training for water user committees of 3 rehabilitated boreholes. (At list 5 members in each Water User Committee Group given Level 1 and Level 2 training• Training of 9 pump mechanics (three per location )on maintaining of water facilities and distribution of spare kits to 6 water points mechanics• Joint Monitoring and Evaluation of activities with local authorities and partners on the ground; and reporting (daily, Weekly and Monthly).

Community members will be supported with WASH Non-food items to improve quality of water, support handwashing at schools, public places and community gatherings.This project will maximize opportunities through Focused Group Discussions with community members and utilize trainees who are community members to equip other beneficiaries with the knowledge of living healthy lives. This will also reduce the pressure on health facilities and costs of treating diseases related to WASH.Community members will be provided with better options of sustainable water points by drilling and rehabilitation of boreholes in villages with incidences of GBV in process of accessing water (sites where community members reported cases of sexual gender based violence caused by marginalized groups). The safe water supply source will reduce risk of depending on contaminated surface water sources where assessments were carried out..This will entirely focus on achieving WASH cluster objectives by providing lifesaving WASH services among community members who are conflict affected Mundri west Mundri East and mvolo
