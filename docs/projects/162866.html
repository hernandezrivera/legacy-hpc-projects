Project Summary

IRW intends to intervene in East Sudan and WK addressing urgent needs of refugees and host communities through multi sector projects that includes: Education, WASH, Livelihood, Health, Nutrition and Environment mainstreamed by protection activities. The plan will target a total of 47,644 persons including 15% host communities (17,587 (13,182 refugees, and 4,407 host community) in East Sudan and 30055 in WK)
Education sector intervention will contribute in increasing refugee children enrollment in primary school and secondary school. In WK the intervention will focus on construction/rehabilitation of primary classrooms in WK and secondary classes in Gadaref equipped with essential student and teacher seating, construction/rehabilitation of gender sensitive latrines with water facilities and hygiene promotion in school/ learning spaces, where as in East Sudan the intervention will also focus on school retention and provision of refugee teachers’ incentives. In both East Sudan and WK the sector will form and train student clubs, support them with recreation materials and promote their skills to enhance awareness in schools and community on hygiene issues. Trainings will conducted for teachers and PTAs in schools.
WASH intervention will contribute to improving access to safe sufficient water supply, promote and enhance access to hygiene services and support improving access to safe dignified sanitation and clean environment free from open defecation in WK. In East Sudan the response will focus on health promotion.
Livelihood sector intervention will consider strengthening the refugees and host community self-reliance. IN WK the intervention will focus on improving livelihood opportunities through provision of agricultural inputs, building their capacity on climate-smart crop production and vocational trainings. Refugees will be supported with income generating activities/business opportunities or employment and support with cash or in kind. In Sudan the intervention will focus vocational training for both men and women y, business skills training such as entrepreneurship, business management and simple book keeping. In kind supports will be provided to the trained targets to enable them start small business in their community.
Health and Nutrition sector intervention will be carried out in WK only and will contribute to provision of standard primary health care and nutrition services through construction/rehabilitation of health facilities, conduct trainings to health staff to ensure delivery of standard services. Nutrition staff will be trained on CMAM, community outreach volunteers will identified and trained on CMAM to carry out regular MUAC screening, mother to mother groups will be established and train on IYCF promotion. Nutrition supplies will be availed and distributed on monthly basis.
Energy and Environment this sector intervention will be carried out in East Sudan only and will focus on providing fuel efficient cooking stove to 2197 HH.

1 - Needs

West Kordofan State, lies in the west of Sudan and adjacent to South Sudan country near the borders with total populations: 1,945,520 individuals. The State is hosting 67,893 refugees scattered in different towns causing tension and pressure to vulnerable infrastructures and sharing with host community his limited resources. Through this intervention IRW will target 30,055 refugees in 5 areas (El Mairam, Kailak, Lagawa, El Sunut and Kharasana). Gadaref State is bordering Ethiopia and Eritrea (please insert the direction of the borders). Through this intervention a total of 13,182 refugees from Ethiopia and Eritrea in Elfashaga refugee camp and 4407 host communities will be addressed. The refugees in both East Sudan and West Kordofan have particular vulnerabilities according to recent participatory assessments conducted jointly by NGOs during 2018/2019 and led by UNHCR. The assessment findings revealed the gaps in terms of services delivered across all sectors and also identified the portion of assistance delivered by some humanitarian actors in the area and highlighted the remaining gaps. Based on those findings of the assessments and Islamic Relief World Wide’s experience, urgent needs of refugees and host community in different sectors were identified. The refugees, mostly share the host communities’ limited resources. Be settled in camps or mixed in host communities, often causes tensions around water sources, health units, overcrowd classrooms and limited business and employment opportunities especially during marketing.The refugees normally receive international protection from the Government of Sudan, however they do not have the same rights as Sudanese citizens, and often have specific barriers to accessing services on par with nationals. As a result, refugees are largely reliant on humanitarian assistance to meet their basic needs, a situation that is compounded by limited access to livelihoods. Additionally, refugees are situated in some of the most remote and economically disadvantaged areas that places and added additional strains on limited host community resources.

5.1 - Impact on environment/natural resources

Conserving natural resources is a basic requirement for sustainable development and improving the quality of human life. To assure this principle is adopted to target project activities, the project will aim to not reverse the trend towards resource degradation, and will consider giving greater attention and priority to ecological aspects. The project activities were designed in a way as not to impact the environment and will not affect the geographical and context of both the problem statement and nature of target groups. The activities are planned to dig deep into the roots of the problem and search for solutions. This trend support inclusion of positive environmental aspects and sustainable change.

6 - Protection mainstreaming

4=Fully in place

6.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

protection will be a key feature within the intervention. In all the awareness session woman and girls will be sensitized about the protection issues and to reduce the risk of such incidents. For example, facilities and water points will be constructed in safe locations that minimize risks of various types of GBV. Pitching water in most part of remote areas in Sudan is the responsibility of women and young girls. However, this project will spare women an ample time and give them the opportunity to carry out other activities and participate in the development process of their communities, state and country.

7.1 - How will the project share information and collect community feedback and use it to improve your activities? Please select as many as relevant.

Face to face with individuals (in other venues/locations) through aid workers

7.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will inform and interact with inter-agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including SEA and abuse complaints?

To deal with GBV and other cases in the field IR will establish a referral mechanism with the health facility at the locality level to treat the GBV patients, referral mechanism with the social welfare department to lead if there is any protection issue reported. IR field teams with the support of the CHPs/volunteers will refer the victims to the health facilities or the social welfare.IR Sudan’s long presence, understanding and knowledge of the area ensure transparency by following all the humanitarian principles. Efforts will be made to ensure that all supported activities are conflict sensitive and in line with the Do No Harm concept and other planning tools adapted to the field of peace building. The health committees and water user groups will be supported in improving participatory conflict sensitive approaches to implementation of activities with an Islamic perspective. WASH committee will have a sub committees of water management and repair and maintenance committees that represent all stakeholders in the community including women and youth, and will promote inter dependencies.
