Project Summary

This project aims to rehabilitate the building of Sfira bakery and install a new production line with 12 tons of production power. The main need for this project is that Sfira area and its surrounding villages depends on Waha bakery now that is working in double shifts (20 hrs daily) to barely provide the needs of some villages. All those areas suffer without bread (which is considered as the primary food component for the Syria family) when Waha bakery breaks down due to over load
The project target is to provide 70% (almost 100,000 person) of Sfira population with fresh bread.

Needs

- Bread is considered the most essential food for the Syrian families, and it is the primary component of the Syria meal all over the county.- The bakery will be run by the government after the rehabilitation, which also will provide the diesel and flour needed for bread production• Sfira city population lacks fresh bread.• Usually dealers bring bread from Aleppo to redistribute it in Sfira, and this seriously affects the bread quality.• Due to the overload on Waha bakery, villages shares of bread is decreased and the price of bread is increased because private distributes take advantage of that.- Needs assessment sources:• Key persons from local community (Sfira Mukhtar, and municipality director)• Livelihood team members on direct assessment mission.• Waha bakery manager.• Local community (affected and hosted personnel).

Implementation of activities

- beside the previously mentioned implementation steps we would like to add that SSSD has its own shelter unit which consists of experienced team of engineers who implemented many shelter and rehabilitation projects all over Syria including schools rehabilitation, bakery rehabilitation in Southern of Syria and house oriented interventions.- Based on previous experience, we recommend to obtaining project approvals from concerned authorities, along with their commitment to provide the flour and diesel to the bakery after rehabilitation is completed.- SSSD vision is to employ local expertise and assets within its projects, and this will support development of the local communities and increase their capacities. The project activities will be done as the following:• Obtaining project approvals from concerned authorities, which will include the government to provide the flour and diesel to the bakery after rehabilitation.• Procuring needed materials to start the soft rehabilitation of the bakery building.• Rehabilitating the building.• Procuring the required production line based on administration of mill and bakeries administration.• Installation of production line.• Monitoring and assessment of project progress will be a continues process through over the implementation.

Protection Risk Analysis

- The protection risks may include:• Child labour may be witnessed during the project activities.• Sexual abuse or harassment to teen ager workers may occur if working hours were extended after dawn. • Misuse of power might happen by work leader.• Female beneficiaries may be violated through SGBV risks during standing in bread ques. - Related mitigation measures should include:• Regular monitoring visits of SSSD staff to identify child labour, SGBV or misuse of power and advise the contractor to act properly.• Three lines of ques will be installed as fixed partitions according to Syrian bakery standards. One for females, one for males and one for military personnel.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

- The risks and assumptions may include:1. Changes in the exchange rate (US $ against the Syrian pound).2. Lack of needed materials related to rehabilitation works.3. Delaying in obtaining the needed approvals from authorities.4. Work injuries may occur during the project implementation. To mitigate them we will:1. Buy the materials as soon as possible (regarding to the materials availability).2. Seek unavailable materials form another near governorate. (if needed)3. Request approvals from the starting day of the project and coordinating with concerned authorities to accelerate this issue.4. Commit the workers to follow work safety measures.
