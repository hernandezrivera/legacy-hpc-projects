Project Summary

The ongoing conflict in the North-West and South-West Regions of Cameroon has displaced over a million citizens in the country and has rendered most of these vulnerable populations helpless and food in-secure. Due to the protracted separatist conflict in these regions, the displacement of populations continues. As of March 2022, about 579,000 people were still displaced in these two regions.
The internal displacement has further worsened the deplorable living conditions of a significant proportion of the populations of these regions rendering many going to bed on empty stomachs. It is for these pathetic reasons and much more that COHESODEC hopes to carry out interventions in some of the most vulnerable targeted areas of Mezam and Menchum divisions of the North-West Region which includes the following: Bali, Bafut, Bamenda, Bamenda 3, Benakuma, Tubah and Wum to improve on the food in-secure situation of these displaced populations.
COHESODEC through this intervention will ensure the provision of Agriculture assistance to IDPs and host communities to improve their food security situation through agricultural livestock and fishery support (small ruminant, local poultry, table birds, piggery, fishery and agriculture) to some 1,000 targeted people (female and male).
Moreover, COHESODEC will ensure the provision of unconditional food, Cash transfers/Cash+ (Emergency food Production) to some 3,000 IDPs and local vulnerable communities of targeted people. We strongly believe this emergency food production will go a long way to ameliorate on the basic food security needs of these affected populations in 2023. The assistance at this point in time is of utmost importance to the affected populations given that the agricultural support activities as a livelihood enhancement will empower them economically and will sustain them significantly.
Furthermore, COHESODEC will in this intervention be providing seasonal Food in kind through voucher transfers to 3,000 I.D.Ps and targeted local vulnerable populations in a bit to temporally ameliorate on the food security situation of Conflict affected populations of these targeted areas of the North-West Region.

1 - Besoins / Needs

The deplorable living conditions of conflict affected communities of the North-West Region remains a cause for concern. As per the recent Cadre Harmonisé results of 2022, statistics show that over 11% of the population in Cameroon face acute food insecurity (2,865,906 people) with about 1% in the Emergency phase (253,853 people) and 10% classified in the Crisis phase (2,612,054 people). In total, 6,082,234 people are under pressure, representing 23% of the total population. For the projected period from June to August 2023, over 9% of the Cameroon's population is expected to face acute food insecurity (2,413,288 people), with less than 1% under Emergency phase (185,150 people) and 8% under Crisis phase (2,228,138 people). Also, 5,689,589 people will be under pressure phase, representing 21% of the total population.The main outcomes of this project will include the following: 1) Ensure that at least 85% of the beneficiary households increase their consumption of vegetable, eggs and mushrooms in targeted communities; 2) Ensure that at least 75% of the beneficiary households improve their livelihoods from selling their production surplus; 3) 100% of beneficiaries (male and female) IDPs and local vulnerable targeted populations receive unconditional food assistance; 4) 100% of beneficiaries (male and female) IDPs and local vulnerable targeted populations receive cash assistance;5) 8% of the total targeted beneficiary are persons living with disabilities; Key project outputs: • Provide agricultural assistance to 1, 000 IDPs and host population to improve their food security situation through agricultural livestock and fishery support (small ruminant, local poultry, table birds, piggery, fishery and agriculture. • Provide unconditional food, Cash transfers/Cash+ (emergency food production) to 3,000 IDPs and local vulnerable population. • Provide Food-in kind seasonal food transfers to 3,000 targeted local vulnerable populations.
