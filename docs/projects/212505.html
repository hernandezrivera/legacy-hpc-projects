Project Summary

The project aims to strengthen access of IDP and Host Communities to lifesaving and life-sustaining essential primary health services, Secondary and referral response for the most vulnerable people with high-severity needs, and limited access to regular services in underserved urban settings and camps in Afrin and Azaz districts in Aleppo Governorate. The targeted BNFs will receive a comprehensive Essential Health Service Package through the supported Health Facilities as follow:
1 primary healthcare centre establishment in Afrin
1 mobile clinic (B0203088! Mobile Clinic-Sharan).
and 2 Ambulances.
There will be integrated outreach services, including health promotion activities and home-based specialized medical care focused on people with chronic conditions, people living with disabilities (PwD), and at-risk pregnancies. A total of 50,000 individuals are expected to directly benefit in 2024-2025.
The project is designed according to the needs, health cluster Recommendations, and funding gaps identified by the health cluster and Aleppo Health directorate. The project will ensure the provision of equitable life-saving and preventive healthcare services, enhance the well-being and welfare of people and strengthen the capacity of local health professionals. The project goal is to contribute to decreasing the morbidity and mortality of the people living in NWS.
In addition to ensuring the continuity of care and increasing the mobility of PWDs during the challenging of the earthquake-affected population the Cholera outbreak.
