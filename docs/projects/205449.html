Project Summary

The food insecurity in South Sudan in 2024 is likely to be driven by different factors, varying in degree across South Sudan, and are likely to continue such as influx of the refugees and returnees from Sudan crisis, persistent flooding incidence in most of the States and counties of which Renk, Malakal, Mayom, Koch, and Panyijiar have been grossly affected. This is likely to cause food insecurity due to low crop yields, market access constraints, and high rates of inflation due to the economic crisis, protracted conflict, and flooding limiting the flow of both commercial and humanitarian goods and services. The main factors affecting food security are food availability, access, utilization, stability, and seasonality which all have significant effects on the dimensions of food security. Unity and Upper Nile States have been severely hit by multiple impediments and constraints, this worsened over the last three years by widespread flooding, recurrent intra state violence, localized dry spells, indirect effects on the supply chain and production, and high food prices, thus exacerbating people’s food security situation and livelihoods. Based on the IPC findings September 2023, the most food insecure states between December 2023 and March 2024 where more than 50% of their populations are likely to face Crisis (IPC Phase 3) or worse acute food insecurity are Unity State (61%) and Upper Nile State (57%). The counties most affected are Renk, Manyo Koch, Mayom and Rubkona counties. The severe food insecurity situation therefore calls for livelihoods and food security support. Humane Aid for Community Organization (HACO) proposes to Intervene through provision and distribution of Agriculture inputs (crop seeds, vegetable seeds, fishing kits, and agriculture hand tools). training surpport to targeted households in agriculture; good agronomic practices, good fishing practices, i.e. seedbed and nursery preparation, best agricultural practices, fishing, and fish postharvest handling, preservation, nutrition, and preservation techniques, livestock vaccination, and training in climate change effects downscaling and adaptation. The total target population is 20,000 HHs (120,000 individuals) in the counties of Manyo and Renk of Upper Nile State; Mayom, Koch, and Rubkona in Unity State for both dry, and main planting season of 2024. The Proposed Project will target Returnees (20%), IDPS (35%) and most vulnerable Host Community (45%). Families hosting the elderly and persons with disabilities will be targeted and prioritised and 60% of the targeted farming Households will be Female Headed Households.

2. Project activities

In order to reduce the food security crisis, Humane Aid for Community Organization (HACO) proposes to enhance food production and carry out support training to farmers. The livelihoods Project activities are meant to improve crop, vegetable production, livestock yields in order to reduce the food insecurity outlook in Renk and Manyo Counties in Upper Nile; Koch and Rubkona Counties in Unity state. The project activities will be the distribution of high quality Agricultural tools and inputs to 20,000 Households (120,000 individuals); vegetable and crop seeds, fishing kits, Agricultural tools/Implements, Vaccination of 50,000 livestock in order to improve the yield of the farmers. Other activities will be training of 25,000 farmers in best agronomic practices, best fishing, fish handling, and fish preservation techniques to improve the knowledge of the farmers in fishing methods that are time tested and result in good harvests and Training farmers in climatic change shocks and adaptation in order to help famers cope with climatic change to increase their resilience to; crops and livestock diseases, dry spells, floods and displacements.
