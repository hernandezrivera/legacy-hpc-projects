Project Summary

SCA through this project aims at mitigating protection risks and responding to the critical protection needs in Northern Idlib, by providing life-saving and specialized Child Protection services in Schools. Through Outreach teams in northern of idlib taking into consideration all the technical guidance notes of COVID 19,
SCA has a presence in Northern Idlib through protection & Education projects in the area for more than 4 years ago and managed to build trust with the community. Our past protection interventions had a positive impact on the community in general and especially on girls, boys including PWD. The exacerbated needs and the staggering protections risk in this district have been identified through rapid assessments and stressed by the local councils& Camps Manager in Northern Idlib that welcomed our return to the area to provide our usual services.
Through this project, SCA will work to achieve the following outcomes, through operating static centers and mobile teams:
1) The safety, dignity, and well-being of vulnerable boys and girls are improved.
2) Address the specific protection needs of children who are survivors or at risk of violence, abuse, and exploitation through providing quality-specialized child protection services.
3)The resilience of the local community is enhanced by providing lifesaving emergency protection services and protection monitoring.
SCA will target in Northern Idlib district 8 communities (Sarmada, Atma, Qah, Bab El Hawa, Hezreh - Hezri,Maaret Tamasrin,Kelly and Kafr Aruq) .
The eight mobile outreach teams, who will works in schools, and communities, each team will be composed of 6 staff members( 3 facilitators and 3 animators) (responsible for PSS, recreational and awareness-raising sessions, and assistance with community protection networks), and They will be trained to identify of vulnerable cases, including Protection, CP, etc.).by hiring gender balanced staff.(1Facilitator,1 animator for each school).
Main CP project activities are:
• Running recreational, psychosocial, and resilience activities in learning spaces.
• Conducting awareness-raising activities for children, parents, Teachers and community members on child protection concerns, focusing on following topics (Child Labor, Family tracing and reunification, Basic CP, Child Rights), using IEC materials, home visits and advocacy with service providers
• Providing referral services.
• Training humanitarian workers on PFA, Confidentiality and referral mechanisms; and Case identification of psychosocial distress.
• Implementing PSEA awareness activities.
SCA will be providing PSS programs (Children) & awareness Raising for Children and Parents (on CP, FTR , CAAFAG,MRE,UASC, child labor, disability, physical/psychological injuries.etc) utilizing the guidance shared by the CP SC, SCA will target children and caregivers with an awareness-raising program on main CP related concerns. In addition, SCA Outreach Teams will be equipped with a PSS kits To be used in sessions with children and adolescents and will take into account COVID-19 related considerations.
also SCA Outreach Team will be providing Referral services to Specialized Actors..
SCA will conduct protection monitoring in collaboration with PMTF, SCA field team will collect information on the protection risks in Northern Idlib, identify risks like the restriction of movement, lack of civil documentation, lack of social structures and so on. Activities will emphasize equity and non-discrimination, reinforcing the basic social and human rights of participants while taking into consideration the cultural, historical and social specificities of targeted populations.All activities in Field will be monitored by M&E team on a monthly basis, Use the kobo tool in M&E.Complaints system.traning staff.FGDs.RNA. after hiring gender balanced staff who will be chosen from the IDPs and host community in the served area with a good reputation and act as key persons in their surrounding.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

1. Risk of having harmful impact on children in School if the children were mistreated. Level: low. Impact: High. Mitigation: conduct sensitization training for all Teachers dealing with children, and having regular visits by M&E team and making a clear well-announced complaint receiving mechanisms. 2. Risk of children receiving/referred to specialized PSS/psychological assistance feeling shame and stigmatized. Risk: low. Impact: high. Mitigation: Staff trained to how to refer children in need for assistance in a manner that protect their privacy and security.3-Protection risk: In areas with IDPs, potential tension between host and IDPs Likelihood: Medium. Impact: High Mitigation measure: Targeting both groups in project activities, and ensure work aims at reducing tensions.4-Protection risk: No inclusion and/or participation of disadvantaged groups Likelihood: Medium. Impact: High Mitigation measures: To increase equitable access to the facilities, an inclusion checklist will be applied, so that the needs of disadvantaged groups are considered into program design (IDPs, CwDs, adolescents, girls). 5-Protection risk: Unsafe learning spaces Likelihood: High. Impact: High Mitigation measures: Each of the project locations will be identi ed and set up using SCA CFS guidelines, which will also be used to train Teachers and parents on minimum standards for assessing the risk and establishing safe spaces.

7 - Does the project include a PSEA strategy ?

Yes

7.1 - Please list any risks related to sexual exploitation and abuse that have been identified during the project design phase and any risk mitigation and prevention measures that have been adopted and built into the project

To prevent any PSEA issue from occurring during the implementation period of the project activities, especially the activities in which the project staff and service providers are in direct contact with the beneficiaries,such as Provide physical abuse & PSS sessions.all Project Staff activities have been reviewed and assessed for risk factors for PSEA with the use of the PSEA Network Field and Organizational Mitigation and Prevention Checklists and the following prevention and mitigation measures will be taken:1-SAED will make sure that all Project staff, understand and signed the Code of Conduct.2-Outreach staff with direct contact with beneficiaries carries will receive PSEA training 3-SAED Charity will review internal reporting mechanisms and ensure all staff know about reporting mechanisms 4-Contractors and their workers will not have direct contact with beneficiaries. 5-PSEA messaging and physical abuse sessions will be done though pasting PSEA posters and placing PSEA banners at the Project sites(24 Schools).SCA has developed child-friendly PSEA materials that can be displayed in classrooms, as well as child-friendly identication and reporting mechanisms, to ensure the safety of children. These are constantly monitored through the project lifecycle by the Child Protection team at SCA, and regular training and updates are conducted to staff. To avoid inecient referrals that do not result in service provision, referral pathways will be mapped for all project locations.

While external attacks may occur, we are more likely to face internal ﬁghting and civil unrest. Our teams receive up to-date guidance and information from our dedicated security team.Potential Risk 3: that existing traditional practices and community structures conﬂict with child protection principles (e.g child rights messaging is perceive as a threat) Likelihood: mid Impact on Activities : high Mitigation Actions : Liaise with "neutral" parties as much as possible and triangulate all information from all sources prior to decision making.
