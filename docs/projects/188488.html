Project Summary

A project / honorable work and self-sufficiency / that supports livelihoods and food security in Raqqa and will continue for 2 years in Raqqa governorate and its countryside
To contribute to reducing vulnerability and enhancing the resilience of families affected by the crisis in Raqqa governorate by creating sustainable livelihoods and improving access to basic services
By carrying out and implementing a number of activities that help in this:
1- The first activity: Training 100 beneficiaries in apprenticeships in different professions such as (blacksmithing - carpentry - manufacturing and installing aluminum - shaving - hairdressing - sewing - embroidery - weaving - Crochet- mobile maintenance and programming) as part of their distribution to existing professional workshops that supervise the Training and developing their skills to be able to practice these professions in the future and establish their own businesses or engage in existing business partnerships or make partnerships among themselves and establish joint businesses
2- The second activity: Training 25 people who have skills but need capital and training in the field of managing small projects, preparing their budgets and marketing their projects. After their training, they are given the necessary equipment according to the proposed budgets for 3 months to help them launch their own project.
3- The third activity: training 40 people with certificates on computer systems and working on a suite of Office programs (Word - Excel - PowerPoint) to be able to apply for jobs in the public and private labor market
4- Fourth activity: Training 100 beneficiaries in the field of cheese and dairy industry in terms of bringing raw materials and carrying out the appropriate production processes for their manufacture and then marketing methods in the local market and providing the beneficiaries with the number and tools necessary to carry out production and supervise them at all stages

4 - Needs

After 11 years of conflict in Syria and the war, people's ability to adapt to the most affected communities has been severely diminished. In Syria, 83% of the estimated 83% live below the poverty line and the monthly food quota, which includes basic items, is equivalent to approximately 80% of the monthly income of workers and employees, indicating that there is general poverty in society.People in Syria are increasingly at risk because of the loss or lack of sustainable means of actionIncreased spontaneous return to conflict-affected areas in part, lack of productive assets and personal savings and destruction of production infrastructure have a significant impact on the population and in many neighbourhoods have led to a high level of deprivation and people's adoption of harmful adaptation strategies such as food consumption shortages, delays in access to necessary medical care, reduced hygiene practices, which in turn increases public health risks or spends more. people thus accumulating debt, child labour and early marriage that affects teenage girls in particular and the community in general
