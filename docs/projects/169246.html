Project Summary

In response to the Beirut explosion that happened on the 4th of August, HelpAge International is proposing a project that will contribute to improved access to primary healthcare services for most-at-risk older people and persons with disability living in the affected areas through 1) two roving medical teams of general physicians and nurses providing home-based essential healthcare services in partnership with Amel Association International and Makassed Association, 2) referring older people and persons with disabilities to relevant services based on their needs and ensuring their access, and 3) equipping three of the affected primary healthcare centers in Bachoura, Msaytbeh, and Horj with appropriate PPEs to mitigate the risk of COVID19 infection. The project will be linked up with other projects implemented by HelpAge under the Protection and WASH sectors to complement the intervention and ensure a comprehensive response to the emerged needs.

1 - Needs

HelpAge international, Amel Association international, Dorcas, and Makassed foundation implemented a joint assessment targeting 802 households in the areas affected by the Beirut explosion on 10-11 August. Findings showed that 66% of the respondents were older people (50 years and above) and 33% of respondents were between 18-50, of which 68% were older person headed households and 32% were adult headed households. This reflects the high number of older people living in the affected areas, and the fact that many of them do not wish or are not able to leave their house.54.23 % of the surveyed households reported having a person with chronic disease with prevalence of diabetes and hypertension. Furthermore, those affected people with underlying medical conditions reported having limited to no access to needed health care and/or medication as 73.55% reported not receiving the medical attention needed. Some older people have reported that they are unaware of the services provided by the humanitarian response or that they do not have access to the services.Although the concerns around COVID-19 as a major health concern was quite low among the respondents (6%), the intersection between COVID-19, the diminished health system and this emergency (not able to physically distance, no masks, no hygiene kits) will exacerbate the necessary steps that need to be taken to prevent the transmission of COVID-19.Moreover, there were also reported cases of the exploitation of older people by others posing as volunteers which raise an alert to the increased risk of abuse.

2 - Activities

Through a community-based intervention, HelpAge International (HAI) will reach out to older people in the affected communities and support them in accessing the information and services they need. Two medical outreach teams from Makassed and Amel will provide home-based nursing care and medical consultations for 500 older people. Each medical team will be composed of 2 nurses (a team of one male and one female nurse) and one medical doctor. The nurses will provide nursing care for older men and women in the affected areas, including bedsore care, wound care, dressing, shower assistance, and follow up on any health condition as well as health awareness on the prevention of diseases with a focus on Covid-19. The medical doctor will be conducting home visits for older people and prescribing medication/treatment needed. The medical team will move around the target areas and will be equipped with needed medical equipment and supplies to support the affected older people. Older people with other needs identified will be referred to relevant services using an updated service mapping. HelpAge will follow-up on the referral mechanism and ensure that older people and persons with disabilities have access and receive the services they need.For further primary healthcare services, older people will be referred to the closest PHCs in the area of intervention. In addition, Makassed primary healthcare centers (PHCs) in Bachoura, Msaytbeh, and Horj areas, which were also affected by the explosion, will be supported with appropriate PPEs to mitigate the increased risk of COVID19 infection.All project staff and volunteers will be trained on PSEA, safeguarding and code of conduct, which will be signed by all staff and volunteers. A complaint and response mechanism will be set up to allow beneficiaries and other people living in target locations to raise any feedback or concerns on the project or related to safeguarding issues. The project will also link up with HelpAge’s projects under the Protection and the WASH Sector:- under the Protection Sector, HelpAge’s community volunteers will reach out to older people in the affected communities and support them in accessing information and services they need, including health services.- Under the WASH sector, hygiene kits will also be distributed to older people at risk who are receiving awareness sessions on Covid-19.
