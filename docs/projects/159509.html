Project Summary

This project seeks to address dire humanitarian needs of protection for the IDPs and poor host communities in Banadir (Kahda, Daynile and Dharkaynley districts) districts) and Kisamayo of Lower Jubba Region. The project is planned to include the protection mainstreaming guidelines to ensure that the beneficiaries access humanitarian aid at convenient time and points considering place of distribution as well time. The project will involve all the target stakeholders to avoid conflicts and to facilitate effective targeting and inclusion of the most vulnerable households in the target locations.
SCC proposed project will be implemented in a period of 12-months to benefit 2250 women, girls and men in Banadir and Kismaiyo. The project aims to sustain the provision of emergency protection and lifesaving services to reduce risks for women and girls at the target locations. The intervention will contribute to build resilience and restore dignity for the beneficiaries to gain control of their lives through the provision of case management and material support for the affected women and children in the target villages and IDP Camps.
The proposed project resulted to recurrent conflicts and drought that is widespread in rural-urban forcing the target communities to dislodge in search of services and livelihood opportunities hence increasing displacement in Banadir region and Kismayo . While there is a considerable number of refugees returning to Kismayo villages and IDPs that presents significant protection challenges, particularly for women and children, as households lose traditional socio-economic safety nets and frequently experience extremely poor living conditions in Internally Displaced Persons (IDP) sites. There are significant information gaps on the specific protection vulnerabilities faced by IDPs and host communities in these regions and SCC outlined the need for strengthened system-wide data collection, analysis and data management across the protection sectors in banadir and Lower Jubba regions. The intervention will include the following activities:
Activity 1.1: Awareness raising forums and campaigns for individuals targeted with rights-based public outreach and awareness-raising activities.
Activity 1.2: Capacity building on protection for duty bearers, service providers and community leaders.
Activity 1.3: train and establish Community-based protection committees in Banadir and Lower Jubba regions.
Activity 1.4: Provision of training to GBV/Health workers to ensure clinical care for sexual assault survivors accesses health facilities with trained GBV/Health staff.
Activity 1.5: conduct gender forums involving local authorities, law enforcement, women representatives, traditional community leaders, state duty bearers, service providers and community leaders engaged on protection.
Activity 2.1. provision of life-saving assistance to rape survivors through Cash voucher support to cope with post-trauma stress.
Activity 2.2: Dignity kit distribution to women and girls of reproductive age (15-49Yrs) including rape survivors.
Activity 2.3: Provision of solar lanterns to female households in the IDP camps.
