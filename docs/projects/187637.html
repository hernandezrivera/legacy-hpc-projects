Project Summary

4 - Needs

Based on HNO/21 findings, as the Syria crisis approaches its twelve year, health system remains heavily disrupted. in general, 26% of healthcare centers are totally non-functional and only 58% of hospitals and 53% of primary healthcare centers are fully functional, 50% of healthcare workers have fled the country.In Syria, not only persons with disabilities require specialized services and measures to ensure access, also persons with injuries and with major-to-complete functional difficulty in at least one WGQs are in need for due to financial barriers to accessing health services.Geographically, health sector severity scale reveals that areas of catastrophic need to all of medicines, supplies, health staff, health centers, Prosthetics and physiotherapy centers are primarily located in parts of Aleppo, Idleb and Rural Damascus, while areas of extreme need have remained primarily in north-west and North-east Syria. Areas of North-east Syria are at heightened risk of outbreaks due to frequently interrupted water supply.COVID-19 has also impacted an already debilitated health system in which half of health facilities are partially or non-functional which in turn had pushed health system to the brink. so, strengthening of all COVID-19 response pillars must continue in both 2022 and 2023, in addition to planning for holding awareness raising sessions, distributing PPE Kits and vaccine rollout, particularly in access constrained areas and areas of disputed control.

5 - Implementation of activities

ATAA has a good experience in working in health sector, ATAA is managing 3 PHCs, 3 MC, one Prostheses and physical therapy center; one Gynecological hospital in Azaz and one more PHC in Jarablus will be managed by ATAA as of mid of FEB 2022; the hospital and Jarablus PHC were managed by Syria Relief and will be handed over to ATAA.ATAA is implementing their projects in Different areas in IDLEB and ALEPPO Governorates an expert staff with medical background are available in the field.Based on the need assessment or the recommendations coming from health cluster, ATAA are selecting the implementation area; and implementing the activities in a timely manner.the physical access to the field for the Turkey based team is consider an advantage in implementation, were the close monitoring practice is in place.Generally, ATAA provides high quality primary health services through the PHCs and the mobile clinics to NCD patients and gynecological services (ANC and PNC).in addition to these services, another services are provided to the clients, such as Nutrition services through assessing MAM and SAM cases using different tools such MUAC Screening and weighing weight,…etc; and provide nutrition supplies when it is required, for sever cases we activate the referral system for these cases and follow up respectively; MHPSS Services are provided to the clients based on doctors recommendations, theses services are implemented and managed by PSS specialists; all consultations reported

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

R1: Partial access to Project services; ATAA Team may not reach out one of the most vulnerable groups within the population i.e., womenLikelihood: mediumImpact: highMitigation1: ATAA will be able to reach both genders (male and female) within targeted communities, by recruiting gender-mixed Project staffR2: ATAA staff member applies sexual exploitation or abuse towards a Project BNF. This might result in hurting/killing the staff member or the BNF. L2: lowImpact: HighM2: ATAA will training all Project staff on PSEA and CSG. Then they will sign ATAA related policies before reaching out Project BNFs. R3: Lack of quality services and referral expose survivors to harm, enhancing risk of stigmatization reprisal and further trauma. Lack of quality services also lead to fewer disclosures and lack of trust in services.R4: Discrimination from project staff toward vulnerable groups (PWDs, women, children)Likelihood: Low Impact: High, Mitigation: all staff is trained on protection principles and will be signed the COC and PSEA policy with related training and refresher sessions.R5: ATAA contractors and suppliers violate PSEA against the project BNFsLikelihood: M, Impact: H, Mitigation: all contractors and suppliers must sign the contract including PSEA policy attachment and will be fully aware according to PSEA and related procedures in addition to conduct local vetting for suppliers.


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

ATAA updated its PSEA policy in December 2021 and was shared with donors, and set a dedicated PSEA committee including (PSEA focal point, HR focal point, MEAL focal point, Grants and partnership focal point).ATAA adopted a core concept in all intervention to make PSEA and CSG as mandatory in all projects and activities, in this regard, all ATAA staff, volunteers and contractors will got orientation sessions about PSEA and CSG policies and sign both PSEA and CSG code of conducts.All project locations will include banners and complaint boxes with delivering messages from accountability teams regarding the complaints and feedback channels in addition to ATAA Feedback and Complaints system regarding reporting and response of any PSEA case starting from receiving the report, to investigation process, and provide aid and support to the survivor till closure of the case after achieving the objectives of investigation.ATAA is PSEA network member participate in all regular network meetings, in addition to submit monthly reports to the network, in addition to conduct training for all staff about PSEA and target humanitarian workers in the intervention location to support the role of PSEA network.Gender balance will be a core point in the project within all teams (programs, media, MEAL) and locations will be accessed by women and girls safely.


11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risks1. Safety and security: continuing shelling could produce new IDPs waves, the thing that will impact projects implementation through increasing number of consultations, increasing in pharma and medical consumables consumption and develop more needs for new medical equipment; also, this could affect the HR where more medical staff are required.The shelling could produce more people with disabilities, the thing that will make new burden on project implementation and will affect the implemented target.2. COVID 19: due to COVID 19 the medical staff could be affected the thing which will lead to under staffing; also, more PPE and IPC materials are required; in addition to this, more trained staff is required.3. Cross Border deliveries: stopping cross border movement could affect the availability of medical commodities the thing that will impact negatively the medical practices due to low availability of medical commodities. Mitigations Preparing a buffer stock for medical commodities to do an emergency response when it is needed Preparing a buffer stock of PPE and IPC materials to do an emergency response when it is required Training the staff about COVID 19, signs and symptoms, protection and mitigation measures in order to train the community Plan the medical commodities procurements to avoid any shortage.COVID 19 Risk mitigation measures are always in place through:(comments section)
