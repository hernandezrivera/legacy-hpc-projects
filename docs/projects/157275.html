Project Summary

The project designed to respond to HRP 2019 Outcome 1 and 2 to respond major basic education and child protection concerns raised inter agency assessment in Al abbassiya, Toladi and Kadugli localities in South Kordofan states which indicated that;
Almost 60% of the visited IDP’s are women and children. Community members stated that the numbers of child and women headed households are high. Information was not available on the actual number of unaccompanied minors due to lack of proper registration and population statistics. Separated and unaccompanied minors were reported to be living with members of the extended families. Some under-age children had been orphaned through the death of one or both parents during the conflict or while living in displacement. Identified UASC are living with extended members of the family or people of same tribe. Lack of a proper child welfare staff/services was observed in all the locations visited by the team. While parents and peers were identified as the major support groups for children, several cases of physically and mentally challenged children and adolescents were reported by informants. Most of these cases have never been treated or examined by a specialized physician.
The classrooms are not sufficient in relation to the total number of the school children, an estimated 2000 school aged children in the IDP community are out of school. The high drop-out is partially related to the long distance that children walk to school, School administrations have been instructed by the MoE not to register more than 50 children per class in grade 1, and this will consequently deny more children; including IDPs; the opportunity to access basic education, The schools visited by the mission have 170 teachers (36 males and 134 females); 97 are trained, Some villages have very low numbers of girls in upper classes, there high school drop-out rate for boys though it is not as high as the girls.
The child protection component focused on Immediate need for PSS activities, Conduct FTR for Identification, and registration of unaccompanied and separated children through CBCPN volunteers and social workers, provide individual assistance to 405 UASC to facilitate reunification and in alternative care, rehabilitate CFS together with TLS, Conduct home based visit and focus group discussions for children in need of additional support, train animators and social workers on management of CFSs, code of conduct, child protection policy and PSS package, setting up a referral system, Community based PSS, and monitoring, registration and documentation.
Education component enrollment for children out of school children, provision of teaching, learning supplies and recreational materials, Construction of classrooms and furnishing semi-permanent classrooms, Rehabilitation of permanent classrooms and temporary learning spaces (TLS) including green spaces, Construction of WASH facilities for boys and girls, training for teachers and parent’s association including school management committees and support PTA meetings.
For gender and youth mainstreaming, given the traditional context, many women face systematic restriction as majority limited participation, for this reason we will make every effort to promote the involvement of women and girls as well as provide them with opportunities to gain skills and cash for building assets. We will conduct gender mobilization, sensitization and awareness rising at community level which will continue to be built into training program.
AAP; We will make information available on the need for participation, consultation and use feedback mechanisms which will determine beneficiary preference for project interventions and feedback mechanism will be developed to ensure that the voices of the beneficiaries at all levels are integrated from the design phase, implementation and through to the evaluation phase of which grievance mechanisms are an integral part of this system.

Activities

Education Activities • Provision of teaching, learning supplies and recreational materials for 5800 enrolled children and 2000 Out of school children. • Construction of 32 classrooms, Rehabilitation of 48 permanent classrooms and 4 TLS. • Construction of 17 gender sensitive WASH facilities. • Capacity building for 16 PTAs including school management committees. • Advocacy, PTA meetings to support community engagement and establishment of complaints and feedback mechanism. • Train 80 teachers (43 men, 37 women) to provide accelerated learning classes for 2000 out of school boys and girls age 7-14 to support their transition into formal basic and secondary education. • Train 120 teachers (67 men, 53 women) EIE, teaching, child protection issues, health and hygiene education etc Child protection Activities • Support PSS activities, child protection, GBV prevention and referral.• Conduct Identification, and registration of unaccompanied and separated children through CBCPN volunteers and social workers.• Trace biological families and mediate reunification, Provide individual assistance to 405 UASC to facilitate reunification and alternative care for 45 boys and girls.• Reunify UASC and Conduct follows up visits of children reunified or placed in alternative care.• Rehabilitate 3 CFS in and build 4 new CFS together with TLS, and 1 additional CFS in the permanent school.• Organize daily PSS activities, Conduct home based visit and focus group discussions for children in need of additional support.• Train 30 animators and 10 social workers on management of CFSs, code of conduct, child protection policy and PSS package.• Train 5 CBCPNs on CRC, CP and community based CP response, including setting up a referral system, Community based PSS, and FTR • Set up monitoring, registration and documentation system at the CFS to keep records of children participation in project activities.
