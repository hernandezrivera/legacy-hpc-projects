Project Summary

According to the humanitarian gaps in all sectors especially in WASH 3.1 million people in
need of WASH assistance 1.4 million are IDPs in Darfur.
The high population density in Darfur’s IDP camps increases the rate of water extraction
and the frequency of breakdown of water systems, Improved WASH conditions are key to
cutting malnutrition rates, as exemplified in the diarrhea-malnutrition cycle that is
prevalent in vulnerable populations.
, El Sireaf & Saraf Omra localities have been prioritized for multi-sector interventions and
as critical needs in the WASH sector. The needs assessment conducted by Almassar with
the participation of HAC, SMoH, in 2019 (report not finalizing). It is found that the
population increase in the two localities has created pressure in the already limited WASH
facilities. It was found that most of the hand pump out of service and water yards is very
need for constriction/rehabilitation.
El Sireaf & Saraf Omra localities have been prioritized for WASH interventions according
to A Massar rapid assessment (the methodology used to collect data is an interview with
affected people and key informants, physical visits to facilities and observation.
The analysis determined that the human per capita for water consumption (based on
sphere standard 12 hours of operation and 10 taps per water point)
There are a small number of hygiene promoters to disseminate hygiene messages to the
communities and poor hygiene practices among school children. There is a need to
establish community structures and train them on health and hygiene education and
water source management and maintenance. Almost 68% of households have no access
to proper latrine facilities creating a serious health threat to the communities. Hence
urgent intervention to improve access to safe drinking water highly needed to contribute to
the health status of target communities, Almassar will promote CATS /CLTS approaches
& will work for effective wash performance for target communities.
