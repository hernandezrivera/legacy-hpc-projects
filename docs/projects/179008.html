Project Summary

Maram Foundation's (MF) active involvement in Aleppo area since 2013 has allowed building solid knowledge and credibility within the local communities to be able to analyze and respond to the needs in the area.The Proposed Intervention will support the most affected population by providing food security in the most in need areas in Albab district. the intervention will be through the distribution
of wheat seeds and sheep to the most affected and needy farmers to ensure their food
security and support their livelihoods in the long term. The project will also support the
local market by purchasing all project inputs from the target areas.
Selection priorities:
1. most affected farmers from host communities and have more than 10 dunums. As we
cannot target IDPs because of their lack of adequate land ownership
2. Agriculture is the main source of income for beneficiary households
3. Farmers directly or indirectly affected by the ongoing crisis
4 - family affected and headed by a woman
the project will enable most affected population to resist poverty and gain resilience to
fight the severe economic conditions, to overcome challenges caused by conflict, to
become independent from humanitarian aid and move towards self-reliance.
this project will provide an opportunity to female-headed households and engaging them
to create sustainable income and lives in dignity.

4 - Needs

Based on HNO 2021,the protracted crisis in Syria has intensified needs over the past year through a complex and deepening socio-economic crisis, degradation of livelihoods, knock-on effects of COVID-19, the ongoing conflict in the North of the country and major losses in the food production sector (due to conflict and climate change).These have combined to severely compromise thenational food security situation. The worsening economicenvironment with year-on-year inflation hovering between 200to 300 per cent is heavily impacting purchasing power, as thecountry imports most of its staple needs, as well as essentialagricultural inputs.314 The results of the national assessmentshowed that 50 per cent of Syrian household reported spendingat least 75 per cent of their income on food, while 28 per centare adopting crisis or emergency strategies.This is extremely high and such families are unable to saveand are barely surviving.As abasic reference, Maram field team conducted a field visit to AlBab countryside to verifyfrom the needs and take a close look about livestook and agriculture situation whereMaram field staff visited (Borj, Hadath, Hazwan, Maqri, Noman, Olan, Qdeiran, QemmetDaghelbash, Saflaniyeh, Sheikh Elwan, Sheikh Jarrah, Sosyan, Su Sinbat, Tal Jerji andWaqqah) More than 3000 HHs in needed to food security and they practicing copingstrategies. Maram’s programme will target vulnerable populations affected by conflict anddisplacement living in the most sub-standard living-conditions in Al Bab district withthrough wheat and sheep distribution. As FSL cluster, the most vulnerablepopulations include female headed household the project will target female3 headedhousehold with sheep distribution.

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumptions, risks, and mitigations: Risk 1: Organizations Acceptance by beneﬁciaries, communities and other stakeholders: CONTINGENCY / MITIGATION MEASURE: Maram Foundation will prepare a community outreach plan, will attending and conduct coordination meetings with target community members and the local authorities to explain project activities, targets, and purposes. Risk 2: Access prevented (Armed groups occupying the area; active conﬂict, disgruntled locals; access/transport routes blocked by huge number of IDPs ﬂeeing to north of Idleb) likelihood: Likely impact: critical mitigation: Avoid checkpoints whenever possible. Conduct a safe approach to the checkpoint. Deal with main actors. Try to comply. follow policies and procedures/ SOPs etc/ staff training. Risk 3: Front Line Shifting likelihood: Likely impact: critical mitigation: Avoid targeted locations Prepare for evacuation Risk 4: Exchange rates change signiﬁcantly and therefore the budget is insuﬃcient to reach the targeted number of beneﬁciaries CONTINGENCY / MITIGATION MEASURE: - MF will maintain a regular dialogue with its donor in the event of a signiﬁcant change - MF conducts regular price monitoring, utilizing existing information where available - MF will contract with the contractor in the dollar to avoid the risk. Risk 5: MF unable to maintain access to the most vulnerable populations despite changes in conﬂict dynamics inside Syria CONTINGENCY / MITIGATION MEASURE: MF has adopted a Whole-of-Syria structure in order to provide multiple entry-points to vulnerable affected populations and to enable a more ﬂexible response.
