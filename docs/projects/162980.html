Project Summary

The project will be operated in the five districts (Umguza, Umzingwane, (in Matabeleland south), Sanyati, Kariba, (Mashonaland West) and Gweru (Midlands) where Caritas is operating in. The project aims at assisting in maintaining boys and girls to remain attending school in the face of the food insecurity situation in their areas. The project targets to reach out to 106447 boys and girls of schools going age including, orphans and vulnerable boys and girls in the five districts. The focus will be on reducing the number of dropouts of boys and girls in the five districts by feeding the beneficiaries. The specific objectives will be to:
reduce by at least 60% the risk of school drop outs for children between the ages of 3 to 12 years in Gweru, Kariba Sanyati, Umguza and Umzingwane by December 2020. This will be achieved through:
-Conducting Education awareness campaigns
-Provision of Block grants to support boys and girls with learner needs.
-Provision of PSS in emergencies to the boys and girls in the targetted areas.
-Training of males and females committee members and staff
-Provision of Sanitary packs for girls
The project also aims at assisting boys out of school to be able to enroll back in school through support with food provision.
A significant decrease in the number of vulnerable boys and girls particularly orphans not attending school is expected to be realized as a result of the interventions which aims at providing food packs, seed support and establishment of nutrition gardens for the vulnerable boys and girls. The interventions are targeted at improving learner wellbeing by strengthening the school feeding program in schools by December 2020.
The project focuses on the following activities as the means to achieve these set goals:
-School nutrition gardens
-School feeding Committees Training
-Provision /rehabilitation of Clean Water Sources in schools
-training on warehouse management and food preparation.
The project also focuses on improving orphans and vulnerable children’s attendance in schools and at the household level through supplementary feeding. The following activities are earmarked for the achievement of this goal:
-Feeding of boys and girls ( orphans) and other vulnerable children
-Provision of Low input nutrition gardens for the boy or girl headed hh.
-Garden inputs distributions for school gardens. The project will engage community members in the execution of the project. The community will assist with the identification of some of the boys vulnerable and verification of beneficiaries. The community is expected in the establishment of gardens at schools and at the HH level. Assist with land preparation and management of crops. They will also assist with the preparation and security of foodstuffs brought to schools. The community is expected in monitoring and evaluation of the project.
