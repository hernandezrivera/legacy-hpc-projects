Project Summary

The project In 2021, communities in the province of Bujumbura were severely affected by heavy rainfalls and floods. This has led to large numbers of internally displaced people, many of which sought shelter in one of the country’s IDP camps. As of today, KINYINGA IDP camp located in the Mutimbuzi commune, hosts approximately 1495 individuals. The WASH situation in the larger commune, particularly in the camp, remains alarming and humanitarian interventions are urgently needed to not only address the primary needs for survival and contribute to the physical and mental well-being of people but also the protection and dignity of vulnerable people.
The proposed project will deliver a rapid emergency assistance to people affected by humanitarian shocks and seeks to contribute to improving hygiene and sanitation conditions for 962 households in Kinyinya IDP camp and reduce occurrence and persistence of waterborne diseases and epidemics such as cholera in a fragile humanitarian context.
In order to ensure access to improved hygiene and sanitation services in regards to the SPHERE standards, the project will construct 3 blocs of emergency latrines (10 doors per bloc) that will serve 1495 persons living in Kinyinya IDP considering that one door will be used by 50 persons. In addition, the project will construct 3 blocs of emergency bathrooms with 10 doors each and which are friendly to persons living with disability. Hygiene kits (bucket, 2 jerry cans, soaps, potty for children, aquatab) will be distributed to 962 households to promote safe water transport and storage but also reduce the open defecation practices especially for children.
To promote adoption of best hygiene practices, the project will collaborate with the ministry of health department in charge of information, education and communication (IEC) and hygiene promotion to develop/adapt messages that encourages behavior change for hygiene which will be diffused inside and around Kinyinya through radio spots. Onsite sensitization sessions will also be conducted.
Moreover, 897 women and girls will be supported with menstrual hygiene management kits.
To prevent sexual abuses in the site, the project will also provide lightening around the constructed latrines and bathrooms and will ensure there are separated but also the project will organize sensitization session around prevention of sexual abuses.
