Project Summary


Nile Hope (NH) is a national non-governmental Organization founded in 2004. It operates in South Sudan in the states of; Central Equatoria, Upper Nile, Unity, Jonglei, Western Bahr El Ghazal States and Abyei and Pibor Administrative Areas. The organization has its headquarters in Akobo County, Jonglei State and as well as satellite coordination offices in Juba and Nairobi. Nile Hope is an important player in the Education Sector, Active Member of SS Education Cluster, NEC, Inter-Agency for Education in Emergencies (INEE) Network and NEAR Global network and continues to participate in critical forums and policy gatherings to improve Education Intervention in South Sudan and Beyond. Education is a basic human right and as such, all children are empowered, regardless of gender or disability to live healthier and sustainable lives to access Education.
Political instability, violence, climate change leading to widespread flooding and draught, disease outbreaks and high inflation are compounding the complex, chronic needs of the most vulnerable people in the country, especially children. Livelihoods are severely impacted and access to education and other services are hampered. Children are at a high risk of abuse, violence, exploitation and psychosocial distress; and 2.8 million children (52 per cent girls) out of school are especially vulnerable to risks. The 2024 South Sudan Humanitarian Needs Overview (HNO) and Education Cluster Objectives focuses on the humanitarian needs of children and adolescents (3-17 years) including children and youth with Disabilities of the host, IDPs and Returnees Communities are enrolled in school, Teachers are trained on EiE, subject matter, and pedagogical skills, Children and Youth (3-17 years old) benefited from distribution of learning materials and school management committees (SMC) members, Parent Teacher Associations (PTA), and education authorities trained on school management, governance, climate change and Disaster Risk Reduction (DRR). Akobo, Nyirol, Uror and Pibor of greater Jonglei all falls under priority locations based on the HRP priority Locations map. The intervention will target 7,059 ( 4235 M, 2824 F) beneficiaries across the respective locations.
