Project Summary

As Syria grapples with a crisis now entering its 13th year, marked by extensive conflict and recognized globally as one of the most severe humanitarian disasters, WATAN Foundation has emerged at the forefront of efforts to address the profound needs of its population. The conflict's toll has been devastating, with hundreds of thousands of lives lost and the widespread destruction of civilian and agricultural infrastructure. Homes, schools, health facilities, and critical water supply and irrigation systems have been decimated, leaving approximately 13.4 million people in dire need of humanitarian aid and underscoring the urgency for comprehensive interventions.
In response to this critical situation, WATAN Foundation has launched an ambitious project focused on economic empowerment as a cornerstone for recovery and sustainable development. This initiative is meticulously designed to rehabilitate and repair essential local economic infrastructure, aiming to revive the economy from the grassroots level and improve the overall living conditions for the Syrian people.
Key components of the project include:
Rehabilitation of Education Facilities: Recognizing education as a fundamental pillar for future growth, the project prioritizes the restoration of schools and educational institutions. This effort aims to provide safe and conducive learning environments for children and young adults, facilitating the continuity of education disrupted by the conflict.
Rehabilitation of Health Facilities: With the health system in shambles, restoring health facilities is critical to addressing the immediate health needs of the population and laying the groundwork for a resilient public health system. This initiative focuses on repairing damaged hospitals and clinics, ensuring access to basic healthcare services, and addressing the long-term health challenges posed by the crisis.
Access to Basic Utilities: The project places a high priority on restoring access to basic utilities, including electricity, gas, water, and sewage systems. The rehabilitation of these utilities is vital for improving living conditions, ensuring public health, and facilitating other aspects of economic and social recovery.
Creation of Short-term Work Opportunities: To address the immediate economic fallout and high unemployment rates resulting from the conflict, the project includes generating short-term employment opportunities. This strategy aims to inject cash into the economy, provide livelihoods for families, and stimulate local markets.
Support for Entrepreneurship and Rural Enterprises: Understanding the importance of entrepreneurship and rural enterprises in driving economic growth, the project offers targeted support to entrepreneurs and small business owners. This includes providing training, financial assistance, and resources needed to start and sustain businesses, with a particular focus on empowering women and youth.
By focusing on these critical areas, WATAN Foundation's project goes beyond mere relief efforts, aiming to rebuild the fabric of Syrian society and economy. It seeks to empower communities with the skills, resources, and infrastructure needed to overcome the challenges posed by the conflict and pave the way for a stable and prosperous future. Through collaborative efforts with local communities and international partners, the initiative aims to create a lasting impact, helping Syria to recover, rebuild, and eventually thrive in the post-conflict era.
