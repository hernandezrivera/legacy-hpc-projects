Project Summary

This project proposes a sectoral strategy aims to address Early Recovery sector objectives in Dana sub-district (Qah, Atma, Aqirbat, Kafer Lussein, Deir Ballut) camps in Idleb governorate, which is to provide critically needed rehabilitation - upgrades- construction to an alternative access road, and to support both economic growth and IDPs resilience, where the road is considered the lifeline for IDPs in camps as well as host community populations the neighbor areas.
The project formulation has been based on needs identified by the Early Recovery cluster, supported with rapid needs assessments conducted by IMAAR in April 2021 and firsthand consultations with camps management and local authorities/stakeholders and aims to rehabilitate and maintenance of basic infrastructures, including roads, related drainage systems, and sewage systems, to ensure safe and dignified living conditions for 150,952 most vulnerable people (38,805 women, 38,921 men, 33,332 girls, and 39,894 boys), currently living in 358 IDP sites/camps and 3 nearby host communities in the sub-districts of Dana (Idleb Governorate) and Jandairis (Aleppo Governorate), which affected by poor weather conditions to alleviate the effects of flooding and prepare for winter.
The project will promote the following:
i. Improve access to camps and basic services for IDPs throughout upgrading/rehabilitation 30 Km of main roads outside the camps in need of rehabilitation (new asphalt layer) with all the manholes and drainage infrastructure.
ii. Improve the technical situation for 30 Km of the road network and reducing the damage in winter (flood response/prevention) by upgrading/rehabilitation of main roads in need of rehabilitation (asphalt/gravelling), excavate the open channels (rain drainage systems) and filling the pits.
iii. Expansion/cleaning/construction of sanitation networks, drainage system out the camps in need of expansion, and installation of sewer pipes.
iv. Increase the resilience of IDPs and host community populations by generating new employment for 150 skilled and unskilled workers including women and PWDs (through supervisory roles) through the rehabilitation and maintenance of road infrastructure.
v. Institutional reform and capacity building:
1. Strengthening the road construction industry by establishing a laboratory for soil analysis and training staff.
2. Defining environmental standards for road works, and road transport issues (such as road safety, axle-load limit enforcement).
3. contacting the union of contractors for coordination and cooperation, and provide them with technical advisory services, if needed.
vi- Establishing 5 rescue teams and providing them with training and equipment in order to assist in responding to the flood risks.
The project was designed to restore sections of the road network which is in poor condition, and at the same time to strengthen the planning, implementation, and management capacity of the local authorities, moreover, to develop further the emerging local road construction and maintenance industry, using mainly labor-based methods and generating job opportunities to assist with poverty alleviation.

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumption1: IMAAR has full access to the targeted communities & the beneficiaries regularly throughout the life cycle of the project. Risk: Escalation of conflict exacerbates insecurity in the targeted area affecting the IMAAR’s ability to operate and access it. Likelihood: M/Impact: H MS: IMAAR will commit to ongoing monitoring to ensure the operational context is secure enough to ensure access to the targeted communities, hiring of field staff and volunteers from the local communities of the targeted area, purchase of materials from the local market with guarantee the Do No Harm principles. Assumption2: The activities can be completed without delay. Risk: -Adverse weather conditions will affect the implementation of rehabilitation works, and at certain times all site works may be stopped. Probability: H/Impact: M MS: A fast track approach shall be followed to maximize the implementation of the rehabilitation works period, so there will be a flexible schedule. The rehabilitation works will be divided into 3 parts to be performed simultaneously by IMAAR, hence ensuring fast delivery before winter arrives around November. Risk3: Spread of COVID-19 pandemic cases in NW Syria. Probability: M/Impact: M MS3: 1. Follow strict preventive measures, building on the ERL guidelines and recommendations. Those will be reflected in each activity. 2. All project teams and staff will receive sensitization on COVID-19 precautions. Risk4: Aid diversion by armed groups, including looting of warehouses. Probability: L/Impact: M MS4: Invest on sound security measure at the warehouses, Train the staff on how to deal with aid diversion, Establish timely reporting mechanism & info sharing with other actors on the ground, donors and clusters.
