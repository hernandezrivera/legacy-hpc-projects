Project Summary

Balad Organization has been active as a humanitarian NGO and continuously implementing in Idlib, Aleppo for the past 5 years.
Balad has offices and access both in northwest side of Syria as well as in Euphrates shield area. Balad has gained widespread acceptance where it operates and has established good relations with stakeholders such as local communities, council structures, and IDP sites during implementing several projects and responding to newly IDPs in different camps in targeted areas. This facilitates a proper access for all of our teams into all the location of implementing the project and monitoring them.
Balad Organisation will consult and coordinate with the local authorities and CCCM cluster to select targeted sites and make sure there is no duplication the in the selected sites.
Project will target 20 camps in several areas in Northwest of Syria (A’zaz, salqin, Maaret Tamsrin, Harim, Sarmada, Dier Hassan).
Before starting the activities, we will take into consideration the HLP issues if necessary by making both the camps’ managers and the landlords sign consent forms which expressly provide the permission to conduct the project activities without any legal consequences.
Activities will be implemented according to the agreed criteria in CCCM cluster, with stress on prevention measures of COVID 19.
At the onset of project, field teams will meet with camps representatives to explain project and its goals, take any suggestions which help in success of project and ensure their support.
The key administrative and critical humanitarian infrastructure will be developed including admin offices, distribution points, and warehouse, safe spaces for women and children, supplying camps with necessary safety equipment, firebreaks & fire points, in
addition to support the camps' management by providing trainings and supplies, and supporting forming the committees inside camps, in addition to contingency planning.
- Reinforcing areas of the sites impacted by flooding or other incidents through small scale intervention such as , replacing damaged tents, cleaning of sanitation and communal facilities
- Ensuring access to facilities (latrines, showers) for women and children, mitigating protection risks by improving and maintaining including repairing of lighting, fence, organize camp gate in sites, and other camp facilities, and providing fuel for running generators and other maintenance cost.
Balad will take over the camps management for the first 3 months, in parallel with training and preparing the camps management committees to take over the management for the rest of the project duration. Balad organization will train the camps management committees on the necessary skills that will facilitate the care and maintenance activities including reporting tools and respond during emergencies.
Camp management committees will be strengthened through through capacity development, and trainings on aspects of camp governance, gender, inclusion, and protection mainstreaming on both management and field level.
Field officers will send daily reports to project officer at HQ on project activities, the M&E field officers also will send daily reports to M&E officer at HQ to produce monthly reports including implemented activities, challenges, learned lessons, and planned activities for following month. Monitoring tools include surveys, interviews, pictures, recordings, and direct observations. M&E officer is also responsible for for managing complaints mechanism so users can submit complaints and suggestions anonymously through complaint boxes , phone number, an email, or by direct communication.
Project will target 20,000 individuals in 20 IDP sites of Northwest of Syria by developing and reinforcing the key administrative and critical humanitarian infrastructure.
in case of underfunding, the number of targeted sites and beneficiaries will be reduced, and thus the expected results of the project will be reduced as well.

4 - Needs

According HNO 2021 13.4 million people in Syria are in need of humanitarian assistance a 21 per cent increase compared to 2020, with needs increasingly being exacerbated by economic decline.During 10 years of conflict thousands of civilians have fled their homes most of them are are women and children without taking with them what they need during displacement, they've gone to safer areas, but these areas already crowded with displaced people, and most of them are living with host families or in temporary shelters, and they are in urgent need of humanitarian support.According to CCCM cluster report on march 2021 "As of February 2021, the CCCM database includes 1,379 IDP sites that host 1,673,680 IDPs or 318,577 families. Of these, 80% are women and children, and 23,660 IDPs are reported to be persons with specific needs. In February, 31 new sites were added, and 26 sites were inactivated in the database due to duplicated reporting of sites, as well as the depopulation and subsequent closure of some sites. Between 10 February and 18 March 2021, incidents affected 127 IDP sites in north-west Syria, which caused one death, three injuries, the destruction of 2,049 shelters, and partial damage in 1,475 shelters. According to the EWARN report shared by ACU, there are 2,220 confirmed COVID-19 cases in IDPs sites in NWS, with 1,480 in Idleb and 741 in Aleppo IDP sites"Balad team have implemented rapid need assessment in many locations in A'zaz, salqin, Maaret Tamsrin, Harim, Sarmada, Dier Hassan to assess main needs of IDPs.The main findings of this RNA indicates that most of IDP sites are self-settled, and lack proper site planning, infrastructure and camp management systems and providing only a minimum level of services.

5 - Implementation of activities

Project will target 20 camps in several areas in NWS (A'zaz, salqin, Maaret Tamsrin, Harim Sarmada, Dier Hassan).Before starting project's activities, we will take into consideration HLP issues if necessary by making both camps’ managers and landlords sign consent forms which expressly provide permission to conduct project activities without any legal consequences, the activities will be implemented according to agreed criteria in CCCM cluster, with stress on prevention measures of COVID 19.Project's activities:- Establish CM committees.- Help development for committees members selection.- Training the CM members on camp managment.- Help in development for each committees member.- Establishment of contingency planning with fire fighting system and evacuation plan.- Reinforcing areas of the sites impacted by flooding or other incidents through small scale intervention such as, replacing damaged tents, cleaning of sanitation and communal facilities.- Providing camp operational and regular maintenance activities for camps including repairing of lighting, fence, organize camp gate in sites, and other camp facilities, and providing fuel for running generators and other maintenance cost.- Establishment of feedback/complaint (AAP) mechanism- Reporting and monitoring the provided services in camps.- Conducting needs assessments in camps for all services provided (3Ws) tool.- Through trainings and sessions , raise awareness of the CM and IDPs communities on gender, inclusion, and protection mainstreaming and risks related.- Balad will take over camps management for first 3 months, in parallel with training and preparing camps management committees to take over the management for the rest of project duration.

7.1 - Please list any risks related to sexual exploitation and abuse that have been identified during the project design phase and any risk mitigation and prevention measures that have been adopted and built into the project

To prevent any PSEA issue from occurring during the implementation period of the project activities, especially the activities in which the project staff and service providers are in direct contact with the beneficiaries, all field activities have been reviewed and assessed for risk factors for PSEA with the use of the PSEA Network Field and Organizational Mitigation and Prevention Checklists and the following prevention and mitigation measures will be taken:1- Balad will make sure that All staff, contractor, and volunteers understand and sign the Code of Conduct.2- Field staff with direct contact with beneficiaries will receive PSEA training 3- Balad will review internal reporting mechanisms and ensure all staff know about reporting mechanisms4- female staff will always be present at the distribution site and involved in the distribution process5- Beneficiaries will be given information that aid is free6- Contractors and their workers will not have direct contact with beneficiaries7- PSEA messaging and awareness raising will be done though pasting PSEA posters and placing PSEA banners at the distribution site or other service place.

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Balad conducts a security assessment for its projects by receiving information on security situations from its network of local councils, camps admins and civil society activists, as well as by remaining continuously apprised of security situations through bulletins released by the international community. For emergency relief projects, when a security situation arises that threatens the safety of staff, the staff is redirected to a nearby, safer location until the area is safe enough to conduct the project activates. Assumption, Risks and mitigation:Assumption 1: All papers and permissions on matters related to the camp's land are available at the camp administration in the area.Risk 1: lack of ownership papers of the camp land and no permission to carry out activities.Mitigation measure: Obtaining land ownership papers and having camp managers and landlords sign approval forms that which expressly provide permission to conduct project activities without any legal consequences before starting project's activities.Assumption 2: All needed materials are available and in sufficient quantity. Risk 2: As most items and materials are imported from Turkey, any closure of the border crossing points with Turkey will result in a lack of such items and materials. Mitigation measures: Balad will ensure to launch procurement directly after the signature of the award for this project. In addition, contractors’ in-house stock capacity will be considered as a key criterion for selection during the bidding process to ensure bulk deliveries.
