Project Summary

The aim of the project is to empower advocacy task force in the hotspot areas in order to be able to document WASH violations within international standards to be accepted legally as advocacy tools for Palestinian WASH rights.
The documentation process of the most urgent and prioritize cases will be followed by advocacy campaign targeting the international community and the main international actors such as the UN, EU parliament, and different embassies/representative offices in Palestine. Through this project, PENGON will work on assessment report about Palestinians limited access to their water resources in the West Bank, and another report for Gaza, these 2 reports will be prepared and discussed within the WASH cluster through different round table discussions in West Bank and Gaza. These reports will be supported by maps, and infographics. The report will be submitted to the Special Rapporteur on the human rights to safe drinking water and sanitation in the UNHRC, and other related human right rapporteurs. in addition to that based on the report PENGON will target the EU parliaments with online negotiations in order to highlight the violations of the WASH rights in West Bank and Gaza, in order to come up with Parliamentary questions that addressed by Members of the European Parliament to other European Union Institutions and bodies in order to take formal actions and declarations.
PENGON will work on advocacy and awareness materials about Israeli measures against WASH sector in Area C including the demolition and confiscation of WASH structures. these materials include Fact sheets, and video infographic.
As a continuity of an advocacy campaign " Make Noise for Gaza" that was launched as Gaza has entered the 15th year of illegal blockade, PENGON has worked in 2021 in cooperation with the active environmental, legal and human right organizations in Gaza, and other international networks like Friends of Earth International, La via Campensena , so through this project PENGON will advocate and mobilize local, regional and international allies about Israeli restrictions against the flow of WASH material to Gaza. Different meeting will also be held among different national and international stakeholders with the aim of bringing the issue of flow of WASH material to Gaza and human rights abuses to the spotlight, to spark discussion and open networking channels among environmental human rights defenders and local and international activists.
Another part of this project will be developing the capacities of Palestinian institutions in WASH sector in West Bank and Gaza. The capacity building will be trainings and community engagement for accountability mechanisms and tools that Palestinian institutions in the WASH sector needs to develop this sector and raise their demands and needs. the environmental human right defenders to better enable them to defend Palestinian environmental rights through activities and actions at both the national and international levels.

1 - Needs

The project demonstrated the existing urgent interventions needed for the communities that are vulnerable to Israeli measures against WASH sector in Area C including the demolition and confiscation of WASH structures in West bank , this issue needs to be raised and advocated internationally using well documented cases within international documentation standards, based on these cases different advocacy tools and materials will be used to target the main actors like EU, UN- Human Right Council and other agencies, in order to take actions and declarations in relation to the violations against WASH access right. In 2020 based on PENGON documentation, the Israeli forces demolished 78 cisterns and pools (with a total storage capacity of 9398 m3, benefiting at least 2367 people most of them are children, and irrigating more than (1545) dunums. Gender considerations in securing WASH needs during humanitarian emergencies and in long term interventions ensure protection of women, where all demands will be raised by different lobbying tools at national and international level.To improve the conditions of women in different societies, several guidelines have been developed worldwide aiming to provide direction and recommendations for gender mainstreaming in water, sanitation and hygiene (WASH) projects. Societal and political constraints in the Palestinian context make gender mainstreaming in WASH projects a challenging task.It is needed to raise these facts in different lobbying materials like fact sheet, reports, and infographics that can be used widely. Another part which is the urgent need for advocating the WASH rights in Gaza and list the restrictions on the flow of WASH materials to Gaza Strip while it entered the 16th year of enclosure, in which Israel controls what goes in and what goes out of the strip. The impact of this on the economic, political and social crisis in Gaza is reaching an urgent scale. Half of Palestinians in Gaza now live in poverty, 80% depend on some form of humanitarian aid, 68% are food insecure, and 69% of the youth are unemployed, 12% of water born diseases in Gaza are women. The lack of vital supplies such as water and electricity ( electricity runs from two to four hours a day), together with the deterioration of the environment, have made Gaza an uninhabitable place. Only 4% of water is fit for human use. Gaza’s health service is completely inadequate. While the world responds to COVID-19, Palestinians in Gaza are particularly susceptible to the pandemic as they live in one of the world’s most densely populated regions and lack essential medicines and equipment for tackling the virus. Medical instruments, devices and medicines have always been in inadequate supply in Gaza, as have parts of machinery for diagnostic departments and laboratories. Critically life-saving medicines for infants or to treat cancers and other progressive diseases are often unavailable. Based on a campaign “Make Noise for Gaza” that PENGON planned in cooperation with different stakeholders, it is recommended to make the year 2022, a year of working on ending the Siege on Gaza. PENGON has incorporated building the capacity of Palestinian institutions in WASH sector to develop and strengthen the accountability skills and mechanisms in order to develop the WASH services and advocate the community technical and financial demands by using effective tools, and active actors who are able to lead the change and present the needed support. Based on PENGON work in accountability work in environmental sector with Palestinian organizations, CBOs, and communities, it was founded that there is lack in accountability skills and tools and lack of awareness in targeted bodies and actors. there is a need to integrate WASH organization with legal and human right organizations to come up with integrative and comprehensive work
