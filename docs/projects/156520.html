Project Summary

The project aims to establish a Prosthetics and Orthotics center to provide prosthetics and orthotics for people in need. All patient will be trained and rehabilitated after getting the prosthetic or orthotic. And the project will provide the necessary medicines and assistive devices for all patients as needed.
In addition, the project will provide MHPSS services for all patients, support includes assessment, follow up cases and PSS activities.

Needs

Within the overall 13.1 million people in need, and notwithstanding individual vulnerabilities related to age, gender, disability and socioeconomic status, there are 5.6 million facing particularly acute needs.While technical capacity continues to grow, there remains a need for long-term capacity building, particularly in areas such as cash-based programming, resilience and early recovery, shelter rehabilitation, protection, including HLP technical capacity. 11.3 Million people in need for healthcare services in Syria, high percentage of them are in the NW of Syria, (5.5 M Male, 5.8 M Female – 1.3 M Children 0-4 years, 3.4 M Children 5-17 years, 6.1 M Adults 18-59 years, and 0.5 M Elderly >59 years).(HNO 2018) As a result of the conflict, 70 000 individuals have lost their lives and an estimated further 350 000 persons have been injured. The health system has been severely disrupted, along with the health care infrastructure, with the workforce and availability of essential medicines and supplies being most affected. This has had a direct impact on the provision of primary and secondary health care (preventive and curative), including support for chronic diseases, reproductive health, infant and child health, nutrition, mental health services, and support for people with disabilities.(Syrian Arab Republic - Situation Report Issue 12|15 March 2013) 30,000 people each month are estimated to be injured by the Syrian conflict, many of whom will suffer lifelong disabilities. 30% of trauma cases are estimated to result in permanent disabilities requiring long-term rehabilitative care (WHO 2016 a). A study by Handicap International (2016 b) focused on reported injuries and psychological trauma that they observed while working with Syrians affected by the conflict. The key findings from the Handicap International (2016b) study were:• Among the injuries sustained as a result of the crisis, 53% are due to the use of explosive weapons. 17% of the direct victims injured by explosive weapons were children (0-17 years), 9% are elderly (over 60 years old) and 21% are women and girls.• Among the sample of people with injuries caused by explosive weapons:o 47% had fractures or complex fractures, including open fractures of lower and/or upper limbs.o 15% have undergone amputation.o 10% are facing peripheral nerve damage.o 5% of the victims from explosive weapons suffered from a spinal cord injuryo leading to the paralysis of lower limbs or paralysis of lower and upper limbs.o 89% of people with injuries due to the use of explosive weapons have permanent or temporary physical impairments.In the NW of Syria there is only two centers providing prosthetics and orthotics, one in Azaz and the other in Idlib, these two centers are working hardly to cover the need for this service, and their waiting lists are already full and a lot of patients are waiting. And even this project will have a long waiting list when started because of the huge need for prosthetics and orthotics in the area.This situation along with the almost absence of health facilities that provide such services, had led to the huge need for this kind of services to provide the patients with prosthetics and orthotics and rehabilitation services to help them overcome their disability feeling and help them gain their position in their society. This need was clear from the large number of PWDs presented in Bab Al Hawa Hospital, with very few centers that provide this type of services. Beside that the majority of the people can’t afford the cost of such devices on their own, and they can’t be referred to Turkey unless their condition is critical.

Implementation of activities

The project aims to establish a fully equipped center for Prosthetics and Orthotics along with Rehabilitation services for people in need, whether their condition is war-related or not. The center will provided with the required equipments and devices to produce the prosthetics for all beneficiaries as required with the appropriated measures for each patient, and it will be provided with the orthotics to be delivered for patients who need such devices. The project will also provide medicines and assistive devices to facilitate the rehabilitation services that will be provided as well. People will be trained and get the rehabilitation services to be able to regain their position in the society as soon as possible. In addition, MHPSS services will be engaged within the rehabilitation services provided in the center since provision of MHPSS services will include PSS activities, self-care component and awareness sessions for patients, caregivers and families by the psychosocial worker in addition to initial assessments and referral of beneficiaries to different community resources upon the need of beneficiaries by the social worker. The center provide its services to patients of all ages, from children to elderly people, men and women. And it take into consideration the high need of young patients for a special care in terms of the quality of devices provided for them, and the intensity of the rehabilitation services they need to get, to insure they can continue their life without any negative side effects of their disability on their normal day to day life.Part of the implementation activities is to collect feedback data from the beneficiaries throughout simple and clear complaint mechanisms, the data collected through these mechanisms are analysed and reviewed by the quality team, and the necessary actions are undertaken to solve any issues faced by the beneficiaries regarding the provision of the service.The center will receive all referred cases from the NW of Syria in addition to the local area the project presented in.
