Project Summary

Work to improve communities’ food security through supplementary food
assistance to vulnerable host communities and bread distribution to vulnerable people
living in camps and their surrounding host communities in Al-Hasakeh, Ar-Raqqa, and
Deir ez-Zor governorates in NES. will continue to implement food security
inventions supporting over 270,000 Syrians living in camps and host communities,
including providing daily bread to all camp and informal settlement populations,
supplementary food vouchers to provide fresh foods to WFP standard food basket
recipients and Malnutrition cases.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

we will work to improve communities’ food security through supplementary foodassistance to vulnerable host communities and bread distribution to vulnerable peopleliving in camps and their surrounding host communities in Al-Hasakeh, Ar-Raqqa, andDeir ez-Zor governorates in NES. We will continue to implement food securityinventions supporting over 227,000 Syrians living in camps and in host communities,including providing daily bread to all camp and informal settlement populations,supplementary food vouchers to provide fresh foods to WFP standard food basketrecipients. farmers and households.We will continue to work with local communities, LAs, and INGOs in target locations to mitigate protection risks to beneficiaries and any unintended consequences of program activities. Field staff will consult with LAs and community leaders (including sharing beneficiary criteria) for assistance in communicating program activities, beneficiary selection criteria, and conflict resolution. the M&E team will also help ensure distributions do not create inter-community conflict and do not take place in areas of armed conflict. For bread distributions, we will mitigate intra-community conflicts by making bread accessible to all participants. Supplemental food vouchers will also target food-insecure host community members. Vulnerable female, child, PwD, and elderly-headed HHs will receive selection priority, and bread distribution points will be located near underserved and hard-to-reach areas to ensure equitable coverage and reduce protection risks for vulnerable HHs. Camp distributions will be organized to ensure that lines for women, children, PwDs, and elderly-headed HHs are separate. We understand and accept the responsibility to help ensure SEA does not take place through the proposed project. We have a dedicated PSEA strategy that covers the following elements: recruitment policy; induction and training; performance management; whistleblowing; discipline and grievance/complaints; partnership agreements; policy implementation, monitoring, and review; and promotes an organizational culture concerned about these issues coupled with top-down leadership from its Board of Directors and Executive Management Team. We also sensitize vendors on its Code of Conduct and its intolerance for gender discrimination and sexual harassment or abuse.
