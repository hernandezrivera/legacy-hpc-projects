Project Summary

The project aims to improve access to drinking water services in three places as following:
The area of Ariha is fed by the Mastoma station, which is located north of the city. It is 8 km. away. The station has been in need of rehabilitation for a long time. There has been no renovation and rehabilitation of diesel engines and wells since it was being operated by electricity provided by a general network. It has been out of business since the beginning of the year.
Currently, drinking water is supplied by tankers from the private sector without monitoring of water quality.
Our project in Ariha includes starting to solve the problem by preparing a well within the area under the supervision of the local council to secure safe drinking water for the population
In Hass, the drinking water is currently supplied by two wells to a collection tank within the building of the water unit of the city and from there to a high reservoir in the center of the city with a capacity of (300) m3 the capacity of the two wells is (70 m3 / h). The city is currently running the water wells and the horizontal pump on the human power line Station 2 hours a day. The local council collects the fees from the residents at a rate of 1000 SP per month (about $ 2.5), which was invested in the regular maintenance of the water network and the repeated maintenance of the station which was recently discharged from the service. And the local counsel has funded an improvement program to dig a well within the area in September last year to provide the necessary water for the city, but it needs to be equipped with the necessary equipment.
Our proposal includes the preparation of the third well in the city with mechanical and electrical equipment, in addition to securing a new generator instead of the inoperative generator, and working to restore the trust between the local council and the beneficiaries of drinking water services by operating for three months with coverage of the operating costs in the first month by 80% (60%) in the second month and 40% in the third month to reactivate the collection by the local council so that it can continue operating in the following months.
.
In Kansafra the water was supplied to the town from the Allej water station to the city's reservoir and then pumped into the main reservoir, the main network that served the whole town.
The project has been suspended since 2012, and the city's high reservoir is completely destroyed. The town provides its water through private water tanks without supervision and at high prices.
Our proposal includes the installation of the well located in the village with electrical and mechanical equipment, with control room and storage capacity of about 50 m3 so that the city council can secure the safe water for the village residents and displaced persons located at an acceptable price.

Needs

Due to HNO 2019, Comprehensive WASH assessments were conducted in mid-2018 to inform the 2019 HNO, involving individual household level surveys and the assessment of existing water systems, Based on WASH assessments there are an estimated 15.6 million people who require WASH assistance (people who are at elevated risk of consumption of unsafe water, including people vulnerable due to displacement), 6.2 million of whom are considered in acute need (displaced people in camps, informal settlements and host communities).Idleb Governorate as well as surrounding NSAG controlled-areas have become extremely crowded amidst the significant displacement to and within those areas. Increased pressure on already overstretched WASH services has resulted in an increased dependency on alternative and often unsafe water sources that significantly increase the risk of waterborne diseases. In addition, the continuous drilling of wells in search for water has negatively affected groundwater resources, with a significant depletion of water aquifers observed especially around camps near the Turkish border. The extremely high levels of displacement combined with the increase in the number of people seeking shelter in IDP sites has presented significant challenges to WASH sector partners, in view of the increased cost and complexity to deliver in IDP site settings than in host communities. Cross-border and cross-line access have particular importance for the WASH sector, especially for people with acute WASH needs as this type of programming constitutes the bulk of WASH emergency response. In the targeted sub-districts of Ariha, the WASH need severity rating is 2, HAS and Jeb Kas is 5, kansafra is 2.The lack of access to clean water impacts the lives and health of all communities’ members and on women in particular, because women have many daily tasks depending basically on water such as washing dishes, clothes, cleaning houses and cooking, In these circumstances.Households are relying on trucked water which is unchlorinated and financially unsustainable. Limited access to WASH services is also connected with poor food security status and increasing reliance on a negative coping mechanism such as drinking surface water for children, in particular, reducing water consumption for personal hygiene, reducing expenditure on other basic needs such as food. With better access to clean water for the entire population, women and girls will not be forced to travel long distances to access water and will increase their access to self-hygiene

Implementation of activities

In Ariha the project will solve the problem by preparing a well within the area under the supervision of QRC field staff and the local council to secure safe drinking water for the population. The project includes the following activities:1-Tender the needed Equipment for Three wells (Q=50m3/h).2- Install and test the equipment's then operate the wells as the pumping scheme of town.3- Operating the wells via the provided stand by Generator and fuel (108000 liters) for 10 hours a day for a total duration of 3 months.4-Monitoring the quality of water, by providing the needed chlorine for water purification. In Hass the project's activities will be :1- Tender the needed Equipment for one well.2- Install and test the equipment then operate the well as the pumping scheme of town.3-Monitoring the quality of water, by providing the needed Sterilization materials for water purification.4-Monitor the efficiency of the collection work by the Local Council for drinking water costs to maintain the operation of drinking water networks and the sustainability of the project5- Operating the well via the provided stand by Generator and fuel (32400 liters) for 10 hours a day for a total duration of 3 months. In Kansafra the project will equip the well located in the village with electrical and mechanical equipment, with control room and storage capacity of about 50 m3 so that the city council can secure the safe water for the village residents and displaced persons located at an acceptable price.the activities will be:1- Tender the needed Equipment for one well.2- Install and test the equipment then operate the well as the pumping scheme of town.3- Tender the construction works for the two rooms and the tank.4- Tender the construction works for the sewage networks5- The implementation of construction works and testing of the reservoir.6-Monitoring the quality of water, by providing the needed chlorine for water purification.7- Operating the well via the provided stand by Generator and fuel (37800 liters) for 12 hours a day for a total duration of 3 months.
