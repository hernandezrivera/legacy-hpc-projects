Project Summary

In response to the expected El Nino drought 2018/19, the International Rescue Committee (IRC) seeks to integrate Gender Based Violence (GBV) programming in the IRC's existing and planned drought response and resilience work in Chipinge, districts.

Needs

The projected El Nino period poses great risk for women and girls whose vulnerability will be further exacerbated by drought. The drought coupled by the prevailing economic challenges will diminish women and girls’ ability to meet their basic needs including food resulting in women and girls’ increased exposure to gender based violence and intimate partner violence (IPV). Women and girls will be further subjected to sexual exploitation and abuse in exchange for basics as they venture outside their homes in search of food and other basic necessities. The most pressing needs in the 3 Districts are to ensure that women and girls are safe from different forms of GBV and receive effective services and support to reduce their vulnerability to GBV during the projected El Nino- exacerbated drought and the prevailing economic crisis.

Activities

Focus will be on inclusive access to humanitarian assistance for women, , girls , boys and men in the targeted districts. The IRC will carry out the following activities: 1) Train Community focal persons to provide basic psychosocial support to GBV survivors and provide information on available clinical care services and specialized support services 2) Support community focal persons to conduct GBV awareness raising and sensitization in target communities in Chipinge District . 3) Support women groups to support village savings and landings associations (VSLAs) alongside and gender discussions to improve women’s access to economic opportunities, increase their decision making power and reduce IPV. 4) Support stakeholder coordination meetings at District level to support a coordinated and effective GBV response in Chipinge District.
