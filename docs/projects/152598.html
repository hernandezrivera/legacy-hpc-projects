Project Summary

Aid Support Community Organization will focus on households and community level as delivery platform with emphasis on gender base violence prevention by awareness rising on the accountability to affected population
Based on September 2018 IPC findings, the project focused locations Wau, Aweil South and Yirol East are facing emergency and crisis phase 4 and 3. ASCO will increasingly recognize the response need of the IDPs, vulnerable host population taking into account the livelihoods of the affected population by saving their lives, protecting and strengthening their livelihoods through increasing their long term resilience and reduce their vulnerability to future shocks.
The project will contribute to three cluster objectives: Unconditional food assistance, Food production for the most vulnerable households, transitional emergency food assistance and dependency reduction on food and agricultural input support.
ASCO will engage closely with Nutrition, health and WASH partners on ground to integrate Food security sensitive intervention into other sectors. Food security and livelihood status is compromised where people are exposed to high level of infection due to unsafe and insufficient water supply, limited access to basic healthcare, poor food security and inadequate sanitation.
ASCO will carry out integrated respond by supporting the households within the communities with livelihood kits, dry season response fishing and vegetable kits and tools. ASCO will coordinate with WASH partners on ground to ensure all targeted households follow water treatment and storage, hand washing promotion with soap distribution and hygiene promotion for small stock open defecation free at proximity domiciliary level around the households and the nutrition sites.
Women will involve in participation in project design implementation of activities and information sharing to express their views. The project will also increase availability of GBV prevention and response services inWau,Aweil South and Yirol East Counties where many women are suffering from different forms of gender base violence.
ASCO will collaborate and coordinate with health partners on ground at households’ level distributed with livelihood kits to ensure management of medical complications among children with SAM, provision of infant and young child feeding to children with SAM and vaccination of children under 5years on polio and measles and pregnant and lactating mothers.
Based on September IPC report released by food and security cluster, SMART surveys, areas affected by conflict people including children under 5years and PLW are severely food insecure and have increased nutritional requirements. ASCO will integrate by advocating for prevention and treatment of malnutrition to children under the age of 5years and nursing mothers, provision of nutrition education at nutrition sites, provide micronutrients supplementation to children under 5 years and PLW and other vulnerable groups including elderly, HIV/AIDS and TB Patients male and female
Project focus on three humanitarian assistance priorities, food availability, ensuring continued regular access to food, production and trade, nutritional diversity and safe access to natural resources. ASCO will scale up further the provision of main and dry season livelihood kits. Fishing kits, crop, and vegetable kits to the most severely affected households targeting host community beneficiaries men, women, boys and girls including elderly people and disabled by involving or participating in seed distribution.
Right based approach will be used in selecting targeted 20000 beneficiaries constitute of Women 7000, Men 3000, Girls 6000 and boys 4000 to undertake different roles and activities of the project. Local Authorities of Wau, Aweil South and Yirol East Counties will be involved in the identification and selection of beneficiaries.

Needs

South Sudan continues to face enormous developmental challenges as it embarks on a socio economic transformation after the protracted civil war. Food security in South Sudan is likely to deteriorate to unprecedented level affecting 7.1 million people facing severe food insecurity based on IPC September 2018findings. FSL cluster targeting 5.3 million for humanitarian assistance. 2.3 million Internally displaced people reference by UNOCHA. September IPC more than 7.1 million people are likely to be severely food insecure in the absence of sustained humanitarian assistance. Priority will be given to the most vulnerable, including women headed households, internally displaced persons, returnees and host communities. People at risk of malnutrition in crisis affected areas, especially children under 5 years and pregnant and lactating women, are not able to meet their basic nutrition requirements. ASCO will Support in Crop, vegetable and fisheries production to increase food production to improve on their nutrition status. ASCO, focusing in Wau in Western Bahr el Ghazal, Aweil South in Northern Bahr el Ghazal and Yirol East in lakes State with IPC phase 4 targeting 20000 direct beneficiaries male and female. Ensure this by provision of livelihood kits, training on market skills and fishing methods to rehabilitate livelihood for vulnerable population who are at risk of hunger and malnutrition by improving self-reliance and coping capacitiesFood insecurity in focused Counties has increased significantly to the unprecedented level due to a combination of inter related factors conflict, insecurity, low food production attributed by insecurity and climatic shock, severe economic crisis and the exhausting of coping capacities. Lower purchasing power in the current protracted economic crisis, reduced trade flows, livestock raiding and disease morbidity and mortality, Nutrition situation is high in Wau in Western Bahr el Ghazal, Aweil South in Nbeg and lakes states where the prevalence of Global Acute Malnutrition is above emergency thresholds. High level of malnutrition is related to inadequate food consumption, morbidity and sub-optimal complementary feeding practices.The multiple threats have caused a decline in purchasing power affecting households, reduced trade flow, decreasing agricultural production and expanding insecurity, making food security situation highly precarious to Phase 3 and 4 based on September 2018 integrated phase classification IPC report released September 2018 , FAO /WFP operational reports and updates and SMART Surveys for September by Nutrition Cluster.The situation have compelled the affected population the IDPs and vulnerable host communities to adopt negative coping strategies for survival. Targeted 20000 beneficiaries were also severely affected by floods destroying the existing crops.Aid Support Community Organization focusing in Wau, Aweil South and Yirol East Counties in greater Bahr el Ghazal States with IPC phase 4 targeting 20000 direct beneficiaries male and female, Ensure this by provision of livelihood kits crop, vegetable and fishing kits, training on market skills and fishing methods to rehabilitate livelihood for vulnerable population who are at risk of hunger and malnutrition by improving self-reliance and coping capacities.Need to save lives for IDPs and host community, women and children to protect and rehabilitate livelihoods for the most vulnerable, by sustainably restoring and maintaining livelihood production capacities, essential livelihood kits. With these distress strategies, Focused Counties continues to face significant food consumption deficits.

Activities

ASCO South Sudan will focus on lifesaving activities during project implantation to meet the immediate food security and livelihood needs in the focused counties.• Unconditional In-kind food assistance by general food distribution assistance by Provide emergency regular life sustaining and life-saving food assistance to prevent famine, improve food consumption, improve dietary diversity and improve coping strategies for the most vulnerable population• Emergency food production: main season agriculture seeds and tool distribution: fish, vegetable and crop kits.• Emergency food and livelihood support: fishing kits or rapid response kits fishing kits or cow pea and three variety of fast maturing vegetable seed• Reducing pest and crop disease e.g Fall Army Worms surveillance, detection, prevention and eradication• Livestock vaccination; disease prevention and treatment especially zoonotic e.g. Rift Valley Fever disease.• Sustaining lives through conditional in-kind food assistance, Conditional food assistance through food for assets food for assistance. Focusing on seasonal food assistance with other multi sector integrated actions notably with Protection, WASH, Nutrition and Health actors. • Transitional emergency food assistance• In addition, ASCO will provide emergency training and build capacity to work with community beneficiaries on post-harvest techniques involving women, men, and youth girls and boys in terms of improving the value chain for food products through better processing, storage preservation of seeds and marketing of agricultural products.• Support emergency fisheries and aquaculture activities for communities living along the rivers and dependent on fisheries for their livelihood. This will be achieved by distribution of fishing gears to vulnerable households for fish catch to supplement their diet.• Train targeted selected beneficiaries and build capacity fishermen and women on fishing methods, preparation, preservation and marketing skills to supports the building of resilience to improve food security and livelihoods.• Train selected beneficiaries and build capacity the vulnerable households on marketing skills to supports the building of resilience and empower the vulnerable beneficiaries comprising of women and men, youth Boys and Girls including government local authorities carry out local trade which will enable them generate income to Support their livelihood.• ASCO will also train the selected beneficiaries and build capacity household’s beneficiaries on appropriate emergency crop and vegetable production new technology and modern methods of agricultural farming to increase food production• ASCO will carry out monitoring and evaluation and post distribution impact crop, and fishing assessment and reporting.
