Project Summary

The objectives of this project in Monguno LGA, Pulka Ward of Gwoza LGA, & Maiduguri Metropolitan City are:
To influence duty-bearers so that they support protection of the affected civilians
To provide access to general protection services for people at-risk
To provide access to safe space & services for GBV survivors & individual at risk
To support the resilience & wellbeing of the population & to recreate social networks
To support establishment of community-led protection mechanism.
In each location, PUI expects to implement the project in at least 1 sub-ward – preferably those that are not yet sufficiently reached by humanitarian services. Upholding integrated approach, PUI expects the same individuals could receive the following protection & psychosocial services or participate in these activities:
Protection:
• Protection Assessment & Safety Audit
• Protection Monitoring
• Protection Training for Community Leaders
• Protection Awareness & IEC Material Production
• Case Management
• Safe Referrals

• Support Group Sessions
• Information & Guidance
• Training on Psychological First Aid
• Recreational & Creative Activities
• Referrals
• Psychological First Aid & Single-Session Therapy.
In total, PUI targets 13,995 individuals (6,717 in Maiduguri, 3,779 in Monguno, & 3,499 in Pulka).

1 - Needs

Per September-October 2023 Protection Sector Northeast Nigeria (PSNE) Protection Monitoring Report, the situation in the BAY States is characterized by heightened insecurity perpetrated by the members of the NSAG who sustained predatory attacks on farmers in the peripheries of the garrisoned towns including Gwoza & Monguno. Members of the NSAG also mounted illegal vehicle checkpoints on the main supply routes linking Maiduguri-Damasak, Maiduguri Damboa, & Damaturu-Gujba – targeting military & civilian commuters travelling to various locations in the BAY States. Data available from IOM DTM indicates that 10,455 individuals were displaced in September & October 2023. Those displaced were recorded as new arrivals in Borno State including from Gwoza, Pulka & Monguno. The protection incidents reported included: attacks on civilians & other unlawful killings & attacks on civilian infrastructure; abduction, kidnapping, forced disappearance arbitrary or unlawful arrest &/or detention; SGBV; theft, extorsion, eviction or destruction of personal property; torture or inhuman cruel degrading treatment; forced family & child separation; incident due to presence of mines & other explosive ordnance; psychological/emotional abuse or inflicted stress. The locations where the protection incidents were recorded in Borno State include Maiduguri, Monguno & Gwoza. In terms of the LGAs (in Adamawa & Borno states) with the highest number of GBV incidents, Pulka (6%) is one of the highest where GBV incidents were reported after Madagali ranked the highest at 26%, Mubi South at 20%, Michika at 12%, Mubi North at 10%, Bama at 9%, & Ngala at 8%, Pulka 6%, Banki 5%, & Yola 4%. Pulka is also the location where there have been overcrowded & congested camps. In Monguno, Pulka & Katsina, there's a clear gap in understanding & accessibility regarding mental health & psychosocial support (MHPSS), leading people to harmful coping mechanisms & unscientific treatments. The OCHA HRP Nigeria 2023 report highlights how insecurity, movement restrictions, & violence exacerbate mental health issues like stress, anxiety, depression, & substance abuse among conflict-affected populations. The Mental Health Psychosocial Support Technical Working Group Nigeria & PUI's consultations reveal the psychological impact of these adversities. Humanitarian efforts to provide community-based psychosocial support face challenges like a lack of specialized care & referrals, resulting in relapses among those treated. Sociocultural issues, including gender dynamics, family separation, & forced relocations, intensify the psychological distress. PUI's engagement through protection monitoring, surveys, & counselling aims to understand the community's needs & collaboratively design resilience-building PSS activities. A 2021 assessment in NorthEast Nigeria showed widespread psychosocial distress among conflict-affected children, with symptoms like anxiety & aggressiveness. The situation is worsened by structural & resource gaps in MHPSS services.

2 - Activities

Protection Assessment & Safety Audit: Assess how well protection principles are applied in humanitarian programs & the safety of facilities for women & girls against GBV. PUI will adapt the process used in Pulka in Maiduguri & Monguno. Freelancers will collect data & analyse it, as well as write the report. Safety audit will be done in all PUI facilities in 3 locations. Protection assessment will invite 12 adult men & 12 adult women from each sub-wardProtection Monitoring: Gather current information about protection risks. PUI will use PSNE harmonized tools. Protection monitoring volunteers & officers, supervised by a supervisor or project manager, are involved. Target 300 households.Protection Training for Community Leaders: Train community & religious leaders on protection & GBV prevention. PUI can adapt the curriculum based on current issues. Training in small groups, led by a protection supervisor or project manager. Target 375 individuals.Protection Awareness & IEC Material Production: Raise community awareness on protection risks, referral pathways, & safety plans. Sessions are to be conducted in single-sex, age-specific small groups, led by a volunteer or an officer. Proven effective in Monguno. Target 9,000 individualsCase Management: PUI is part of GBVIMS+ in Nigeria, with a strong team in Monguno (case management officers supervised by a protection supervisor & technically supported by protection coordinator). PUI aims to expand services to other areas due to community demand & increasing GBV cases, especially in Maiduguri with a big gap of GBV services. Target 180 female survivors per locationSafe Referrals: Protection officers in health facilities refer people needing protection services in Monguno. They also update service mapping monthly. This is an important activity to get survivors & persons at-risk the needs quickly as less & less protection services available due to funding issues. Anticipate referring 240 individualsIndividual Protection Assistance: one-off cash assistance for immediate protection threats. PUI is now updating its standard operating procedures to adapt to changing contexts & rising inflation. Important in the context where basic needs prices is rising. 225 indivuduals will receive min 55,000 NGNAdolescent Girls’ Safe Space Activities: a 12-week program using IRC’s curriculum My Safety & My Wellbeing, focusing on support networks & challenging GBV norms. Include vocational training. There has been a demand to deliver for adolescent boys in Monguno which shows the success of this programme. Invite 270 girlsEngaging Men through Accountability Practices (EMAP): Use IRC’s curriculum for adult men in small groups. The 16-week program covers healthy masculinity & supporting women in GBV prevention. There has been a demand to deliver for adolescent boys in Monguno which shows the success of this programme. Invite 150 men & boys.Cash for Gender-Based Violence: Provide cash support as part of case management to help survivors avoid harmful practices like transactional sex. PUI will train the case management workers to decide cash distribution depending on each client’s case plan & to guide recipients in using funds effectively. Target 90 case management clients for 3 monthsDignity kit distribution: the recipient assessment & verification will be based on protection vulnerability. The content items will follow the guidelines from GBV SS. Target 180 women & girls at-risk. In additions, PUI will procure 180 dignity kits in additions for rapid emergency response.PUI will also provide PSS: lay Counselling for 576 individuals; support group sessions for 600 participants; awareness raising for 3,000 individuals; training on psychological first aid and referrals for 100 community stakeholders; recreational activities for 2,880 individuals; referrals for severe cases; psychomotor stimulations for mothers/caregivers and children at mother-to-baby corner; and single-session therapy during emergency.

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

PUI will use the findings from the monthly Community Committee Meeting that has been on-going in all PUI LGAs (MMC, Monguno & Pulka) to design a project or an approach based on the demand or request of the community members. If the on-going CCM lack of participation of women, girls, & youth, additional FGDs to consult about project design will be conducted. PUI has protection mainstreaming global guidelines in place that guide on how the project implementation upheld safety & dignity, participation, empowerment of & accountability to the affected populations. This global guideline monitored by the global protection & gender advisor will be used in this project. Additionally, the protection assessment & safety audit proposed for this project can be used to develop a protection mainstreaming action plan for each specific location.Moreover, PUI has Prevention of Sexual Exploitation & Abuse as well as Child Safeguarding Global Policy in place that must be signed by all staffs & stakeholders of PUI activities. In addition, there is a safe email address to where survivors or witness or SEA (and fraud) can report directly to the headquarter. At the community level, the MEAL Assistant situated in the PUI facilities will be in charge in listening to the complaints including such a report from the community members.
