Project Summary

hrough this project, Violet aims to establish Camp Management Mobile Team to Build the Capacity of Site Representatives and improve living conditions by implementing minor infrastructure and technical maintenance in 10 IDPs Sites in Northwest Syria.
The intervention aims to is to ensure the capacity of the targeted 10 IDPs sites in order to continue the provision of the humanitarian assistance in the targeted IDPs sites and access for humanitarian response by enhance the capacity and knowledge of the camps management about the standards and guidelines for the activities related to the CCCM includes (but not limited) coordination mechanism in accordance to SPHERE standards, dealing with the disasters and reducing the negative impact of it such as fire incidents and the first aid sessions.

A large proportion of IDP sites in Northwest Syria are informal settlements, also known as “spontaneous” or “self-established” camps. These usually consist of groups of tents or other shelters that were set up by IDPs themselves or other actors. Permission to use the site chosen is usually informal and requires renegotiation. They are usually too dense and sometimes too large, requiring phased upgrading to meet international standards and local and international good practices, including introducing firebreaks, surface water drainage, and infrastructures such as schools, distribution centers, water supplies, and recreational areas. Crucially, these sites are not managed by anyone and often have no legal claim to the land on which they were established. In these sites, the delivery of dignified protection and multi-sectoral assistance cannot be guaranteed by humanitarian actors, a critical issue for vulnerable groups, and particularly for women- and child-headed households. Planned camps, on the other hand, are managed by accountable humanitarian organizations that are responsible for these services. The same applies to transit and reception centers, which are temporary structures established for very short-term displacements. Accordingly, Violet is aiming to support the camp management in 10 informal settlements in Northwest Syria through CCCM mobile teams with a particular focus on CCCM approaches and community participation trageting 2000 HHs ( 10,000 individuals).
4 - Implementation of activities
Violet will start implementing the project by reaching out to the agencies that are managing the targeted camps in order to coordinate the access to the IDP sites and initiate the discussion process with the Camp Management Team inside each site and discuss the project approach and the planned achievement during the project implementation. The activities which will be carried out during the project duration are as follows: 1. Violet will coordinate with all concerned stakeholders to obtain the needed documents and inform them about the project. 2. Establish CCCM mobile team that will be responsible for covering all the identified sites and conduct online CCCM, Sphere Standards, disaster risk reduction and community mobilization; fire safety and first aid training and practical training on fire safety. 3. Conduct detailed assessments for the 10 targeted IDP sites in Northwest Syria in order to identify gaps and existing capacities of camp management and fire safety in coordination with the MEAL team and come up with detailed response plan. 4. Upon the completion of the assessments in the IDP sites, Violet will establish site management committees in a participatory approach through nomination or election and conduct focus group discussions with the camp management to discuss the TOR for the Site Management Committee. Each site management committee will consist of 12-28 member. 5. Conduct minor infrastructure and technical maintenance proposed by the Site Mana
