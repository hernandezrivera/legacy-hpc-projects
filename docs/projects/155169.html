Needs

Since 25th August 2017 Violence in Rakhine State had been driven and Rohingya people crossed over the border through pushing which make a large scale and escalating humanitarian crisis. Many NGOs were responding to reach more people in need of life-saving assistance. Hereafter, they are settled in different camps under Ukiah & Tehnaf Upazilas. They are under emergency food facilities, tent/shelter, emergency pure drinking water, hygiene kits & medical facilities etc. The speed and scale of the influx had resulted in a critical humanitarian emergency. The people who had arrived in Bangladesh came with very few possessions. They have used the majority of their savings on transportation and constructing a shelter, often out of no more than bamboo and thin plastic. The Rohingya population in Cox’s Bazar is highly vulnerable, having fled conflict and experienced severe trauma and now living in extremely difficult conditions. Children represent 55% of Rohingya refugee population in Cox’s Bazar District. Many refugee children are in need of continuous protection support. They are at high risk of child trafficking, abuse & exploitation. Girls are considered particularly vulnerable to abuse, violence, sexual exploitation & neglect.

Activities or outputs

Children and adolescence location will be identified by staff members and volunteers who will discuss with their families to create direct links between the children and the project. Children will be attending daily the no. of Child Friendly Spaces. Each of the centers will host 03 groups of 30 children and adolescence and will get awareness session in the center. Adolescence will also have 01 additional meeting per week to receive life skill development training, reproduction rights and sexual health, trafficking, exploitation and abuse awareness, and adolescent girls will receive dignity kits package after every 06 months. Each center will with have 02 groups of 30 adolescents/group and each group receives different types of Awareness session, Skill development & Vocational training, Life skill, Safe guarding and DRR (30 Adolescence x 06 centers *03 Batch/CFS). In to 540 adolescents will be directly beneficial by the project. The project will also establish community based structures that will create a protective environment for children and their community’s Case management (leaders, Maji, Imam, Teachers etc.) who will be trained to detect and report cases of trafficking abuse and exploitation. Project Coordinator (PC) of this task along with his team will supervise the programme. An inclusive child protection program will be implemented through:• Establish and strengthen child protection community based structures by activating child protection committees, children’s clubs and caregivers support groups.• Establishment of a child friendly referral mechanism through Legal assistance. • Organize community driven initiatives against identified harmful cultural practices.• Organize awareness raising activities on child protection & child friendly referrals to services for children at risk.• Organize Court yard Session (bi Monthly) at camp level (Rohingya) on child protection (Parents etc). • Establishment of an individual case management system for children at risk. Establishment of innovative targeted programmes for adolescents and youth to promote resilience and positive coping mechanisms.• Establishment and running of integrated child friendly spaces to provide psycho social activities, early childhood development, safe adolescent spaces and safe identification and referral of children with protection needs.• Mass awareness on Child protection at camp level through leaflet, fostering etc. • Awareness Training on protection to adolescent group. • Establish Legal assistance support. • Provide input support to adolescent on reproductive health related Dignity Kits.• Formation and support of community based psycho-social support structures. • Capacity building of child protection actors and stakeholders including Government agencies and host communities.• Organize cultural activities, show and theater on Child protection, adolescent sexual reproductive health & rights (Art competition, Debit Competition)• Indoor games materials for CFS.• Outdoor Games among adolescent Group• Final Out door Games among adolescent Group• Adolescent drama on anti Trafficking, Abuse, Exploitation etc.• To arrange Refreshment facilities for CFS children• Training on safe guarding, protection & DRR (Adolescent & Child groups) Agrajattra are currently running 03 Child Friendly Spaces at different camps covering 400 Children. Have been working in the Rohingya emergency since the beginning of crisis in different sectors like SHELTER/NFI, PROTECTION, EDUCATION, FOOD SECURITY, HEALTH, WASH, ENVIRONMENT ETC. Agrajattra having sufficient qualified staffs and a large number of Volunteers and has developed and maintains an excellent correlation with local authorities, Community Based Organization (CBO), Youth clubs, voluntarily institution and teacher of Child Friendly Space (CFS) will play a vital role along with different stakeholders for sustainability.
