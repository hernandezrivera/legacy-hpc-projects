Project Summary

The project is aimed to ensure access of all children and women to the comprehensive
quality CMAM services, disseminate and support C-IYCF practices and promote health
and nutrition seeking and basic hygienic behaviors within the population in need. Also, the
project is going to continue providing harmonized emergency community-based services
for children and women of the population in need by applying the convergence health,
nutrition, child protection and Wash approaches
14951 children under five will benefit from screening for malnutrition, 8970 of them will receive micronutrients, 360 will be treated from MAM and 76 will be treated from SAM.
6143 pregnant and lactating women will benefit from screening for malnutrition, 3686 will receive micronutrients and IYCF key messages.

Needs

Over the last seven years of the Syrian crisis, there has been a collapse in the health sector inside Syria, and most of the health and nutrition services are provided by non-governmental organizations in health centers. However, due to continuous attack on health facilities and the lack of awareness on the availability of health and nutrition services, many cases which needs medical care or consultation will not visit the health facilities to seek the services, especially women and children who are the most vulnerable groups and at more risk of malnutrition. Many changes took place during 2017 and 2018, which led to an increase in the population in Idlib governorate, as most of the displaced people who came from besieged areas like Eastern Ghouta, Southern Damascus and Northern Homs whom most of them moved towards Idleb governorate, according to United Nations HNO 2019, population of Idlib governorate is 2,379,650 people, 1,125,234 of them are IDPs. Idleb city is considered as the center of the north Syria with more than 175000 people live in the city of Idlib. Many displacements from several other areas reached Idlib and Ariha sub-districts such as Bloudan, Zabadani, Madaya and Alwaer ...etc. Most of them were living in a severe siege therefore they are in a critical need for health and nutritional care services. All of the above necessitates the need to scale up nutrition activities at Idlib city and train qualified Community Health Workers (CHWs) who will be able to provide a better awareness to community on nutrition, communicable diseases, non-communicable diseases and family health screening, besides the availability of the nutrition services in health facilities in their catchment areas, CHWs will also provide Hygiene promotion activities including providing key messages and distribution of water purifying sachets, this will be carried out through home visits, referralof child protection cases to specialists, community awareness sessions and MUAC screening for children under 5 years and PLWs and referral of malnourished children & PLWs for treatment, in addition to newly established mother to mother support activities.Orient, at Medical Complex in Idlib, receives cases of malnutrition on a daily basis from other health centers in the city and nearby areas in addition to the screening activities for malnutrition, awareness, distribution of preventive food supplements, and referral of some cases to the center if needed. In 2018, 12617 children under five have been screened for malnutrition, 4588 women have been counselled on IYCF, and 316 MAM and 92 SAM cases have been treated. Orient’s nutrition team is one of the most important teams who covers the city of Idlib and some communities in Rural Ariha during the past three years.This work has achieved many successes both in the treatment of malnutrition for children under 5 years and for pregnant and lactating women, in addition to relactation. Orient along with other few partners operate in the city of Idlib which is classified as intensity level 4 as per nutrition cluster levels.

Implementation of activities

The nutrition team at Orient is already working within Orient Medical Complex and the Orient Mobile Clinic. Through this project, the nutrition activities will be developed according to the strategic plan for the nutrition sector. Nutrition technicians and community health workers will conduct a thorough survey for all children under 5 years, pregnants and lactating women to detect cases of malnutrition and refer all cases of malnutrition detected at CMAM Center at Orient Medical Complex in Idlib throughout the project period.In addition to malnutrition survey, the nutrition team will stimulate and disseminate C-IYCF and best practices in hygiene behavior in the community through mother-to-mother sessions and community health workers. Each community health team will be supervised by an experienced team leader to organize, coordinate and implement the action plan.The program includes capacity development program for all nutrition and health workers on child health, IYCF and CMAM. It also includes training of several mothers, midwives and community leaders on how to provide C-IYCF stimulation sessions.Primary health care will be provided to all women and children who visit ORIENT Medical Complex through a specialized medical staff working within the center.The implementation of the activities will be monitored by the project manager and M&E team in addition to a technical follow-up through a technical supervisor on the program

Protection Risk Analysis

Risk: Ignorance of safety measures and response to risks by staff Probability: moderate.Effect: high Mitigation measures: Staff will be trained on safety measures and jointoperational principles (JOP) during emergency response. Patients will be educated onsafety guideline through posters and brochures distributed throughout communityoutreach, and in waiting rooms inside the facilities. Risk: no access or high risk to access Orient’s health and nutrition facilities for beneficiaries, especially for women. Probability: weak. Effect: high Mitigation measures: - Advocacy for safe access for beneficiaries, and for a safe environment for staff members, will be expanded and shared with coordination bodies at all levels. - Orient’s health and nutrition facilities’ locations are fully accessed to all beneficiaries and multiple routes exist, Orient medical complex is at Idlib city which is accessible to all people in the area and multiple routes are available, besides Orient mobile clinic reaches the beneficiaries at their community.Risk: gender groups discrimination when providing services Probability: weak. Effect: highMitigation measures: - In this project, Tailored services are provided to vulnerable gender groups (children and women) - Dedicated female staff is recruited to provide sensitive health and nutrition services to women e.g. IYCF. project manager, security and safety officer, M&E team will follow up the measures of mitigation.
