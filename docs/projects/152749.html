Project Summary

Although South Sudan fervent hopes of an end to a conflict (Revitalized Peace Agreement) that has killed hundreds of thousands of people was signed and celebrations done on the 31 October 2018 still that extensive doubt that the fragile agreement will hold the deal, which is meant to end a civil war that began in 2013, Humanitarian calamity continued growing across the Country, with Over one third of South Sudan’s estimated 12 million citizens are displaced, while more than half of the population is affected by the crisis, and nearly two million IDPs are in need of urgent humanitarian assistance.
Confident Children out of Conflict is proposing the following interventions to protection cluster strategic and indicators on lifesaving Child protection response services to most vulnerable populations (boys, Girls, women, men) in Juba Central Equatorial State Rokon, Luri Serimon This proposed child protection project will endure to strengthen the resilience of Children, caregivers and their families as Children affected by conflict and Need to be assisted to use and rely on adequate coping mechanisms, which include developing self-confidence and expression of emotions. CCoC own experience affirms to Real post -traumatic distress, depression, Phobia occurring in high numbers on children.
An integrated approach to ensure broad community psychosocial support to both children and their caregivers is critical to ensure children and young people are adequately cared for in all aspects and CCoC’s ongoing effort through child friendly spaces will continue but this project will ensure they are more deeply rooted and owned by the community.
The complexity of the operating environment requires multi-layered approaches that are tailored to each location including: comprehensive case management services, School bases PSS, safe space for both children and women besides maintaining a functional interim care Centre for UASC and most vulnerable children in need of Protection and emergency response services in Juba Central Equatorial State. The proposed CPiE intervention is intended to: Foster families and community’s access to life saving child protection services i.e.
Comprehensive case management package (FTR), psychosocial support (PSS) services through CFS including Non CFS psychosocial support (CBPSS). Integrate protection monitoring and mainstreaming into the Child Protection in emergency actions to improve the overall context analysis and integration of protection principles in the intervention that will be scaled up to respond to the identified needs and Enhance understanding and analysis among staff and communities and other actors on the child protection context and needs.
CCoC will utilize and increase on the existing internal emergency response capacity to staff and be able to provide timely, effective and efficient Child protection (FTR), PSS and ICC services for UASCs during the project period within a six-month response in Central Equatorial State.
This will offer an opportunity to provide much needed CPiE humanitarian services in Juba IDP urban settlements to areas with without child protection services and in addition, strengthen Child Protection interventions in Lemongaba, Luri, Rokun, Serimon, Gumbo, Mahad IDP camps and Lologo and Korbou Host communities in Juba Central Equatoria State communities.
The child protection intervention has been specifically designed to be integrated within CCoC’s existing child protection program in Juba, which are aimed at strengthening community-based protection mechanisms (CBPM) and facilitate access to information and appropriate services. Through existing Community Protection Committees (CPC) and children/Adolescent groups to facilitate the identification of and response to child protection concerns, the development of community-based mitigation plans, prevention messaging (e.g. on child abuse, Exploitation, early marriages etc.) and child protection surveillance mechanisms. Needs

According to the assessment conducted between 4th and 5th October by the team of Humanitarians partners following reports of displacements as a result of cattle raids and inter communal conflict which had taken place during the 3rd Week of August2018, the raid, where Houses were set ablaze, fleeing residents were shot, women and girls raped. 13 people were reportedly killed including 5 children who were thrown in burning houses and over 20 injured. This created a lot of fear among the residents hence they have to flee their homes such as Tijor and to locations deemed safe for them, Rokon and Kuda. Some are said to have fled to Lemon Gabba on the outskirts of Juba, where their humanitarian needs remain dire. Rokon County of Jubek State is currently made up of about 8,700 according RRC residents, besides 5,040 members of the displaced communities currently registered and being hosted in the county. The displaced communities originally came from Amadi area of the former Western Equatoria State, However, communities from Dolo, Gwor, including those displaced from the outskirts of Rokon itself left their areas due to insecurity caused by cattle keepers. Local authorities further reported the existence of about 883 HH, 3852 individuals in Kuda with some occupying the school .These displacements have led to family splits leaving many children separated from their parents and families more than 6303 people moved and settled in Lemon gaba and kworojik, where separated and an unaccompanied children were identified and registered by CCC, Under these circumstances, abandoned and neglected children, especially girls have become vulnerable to SGBV, Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (SEA). Economic hardship and the lack of survival opportunities, has also forced children and the care givers to engage in negative coping mechanisms, such as child marriage, survival sex, substance abuse, and violence this was reported during the focus group discussion with key community stake holders which include religious leaders, teachers, community leaders (chief), Women group leaders, PTA members, social worker, caregivers and adolescent children, there are no or limited CP intervention in these counties. The objective of the project is to improve access and scale up prevention and response mechanisms to most at risk and vulnerable children and the caregivers in these payams of Rokon, Kunda and Luri under jubek state and this objective will be achieved by conducting awareness raising on child protection and available services and referrals, for the identified most vulnerable children and provide support through comprehensive case management and focused PSS and referring to other services. CCOC will also provide psychosocial support to children through structured activities and CFS for different age group this will act as an avenue for the children to express themselves and report child protection concerns, not only this but CCOC will establish community based child protection networks to act as key focal points to identify, report, support and refer the most at risk and most vulnerable children in the IDP site and nearby conflict affected communities of Rokon , Luri Kunda and juba,
