Project Summary

This intervention is aimed at strengthening the capacity and knowledge of 50 associations of persons with disabilities in the south west region, to be able to champion the crusade on disability mainstreaming through effective advocacy at all levels within the society. the complexities that surrounds disability and the need for persons with disabilities to be taken into direct consideration during humanitarian response programmes is paramount amongst humanitarian organization as as well as local and regional administrative authorities. This intervention is aimed at strengthening and advocating for the meaningful and effective inclusion of persons with disabilities at all levels into humanitarian response programmes and to fast track a rapid change in status for persons with disabilities from beneficiaries to participants and implementing partners.
The aim of this project is to train 50 associations of persons with disabilities within the south west region with the objective of developing and strengthening their capacities and knowledge for more effective and productive advocacy on disability mainstreaming at all levels and involving all stakeholder .
