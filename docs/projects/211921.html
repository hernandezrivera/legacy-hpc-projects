Project Summary

a. Needs:
The ongoing conflict in Syria, which will soon enter its fourteenth year, has caused massive destruction and unimaginable losses to the Syrian people and in all aspects of their lives. Residential buildings were severely damaged as a result of the war and the earthquake that occurred in February 2023, as a large percentage of inhabited homes were completely or partially destroyed. Most of the population has been displaced to other homes or areas, resulting in the loss of household items and living essentials that they previously owned. Residents face difficulty in purchasing basic household supplies due to high prices, low income, and deteriorating living conditions. These conditions exacerbate the tragedy and lead to economic, health and social consequences that exacerbate the suffering of the people and their need for humanitarian aid.
After stability returns to the liberated areas of rural Idleb, people continue to return to their villages and homes there. The return of more than 3,000 families in Sanjar and its countryside and more than 2,200 families in the municipality of Al-Tamanaah was recorded, and most of them are struggling to secure basic life necessities such as cooking utensils, lighting, and heating in the winter or cooling in the summer, as the war and earthquake in February 2023 caused the destruction of homes, the displacement of residents, and the loss of necessities. Basic life. There is an urgent need to provide basic relief materials quickly and urgently to ensure the minimum standard of living in the region for families and meet their basic humanitarian needs. There are currently no programs or entities providing assistance in the targeted area in this direction. These difficult conditions hinder the return of residents to their areas and hinder early recovery efforts in the region.
to intervene:
The areas (Saree - Seraa- Maksar - Abu Dali - Khwein - Al-Msheirfah - Bashkun) were chosen because they are the most recent areas of return and lack many of the necessities of life compared to other areas. The project will seek to provide relief aid to 500 families in the targeted areas, including basic tools, household supplies, and summer and winter equipment, through mechanisms that ensure fair and effective distribution.
Risks:
Workers exploit beneficiaries to obtain aid. Mechanism: Publishing and circulating the numbers and methods of complaints to beneficiaries and subjecting workers to training on protection policies and the code of conduct specified in the association. Imposing strict penalties on exploiters.
Al-Birr and Social Services in Idleb adopts a code of conduct and a protection policy that all workers and volunteers in the society must adhere to. It prohibits any behavior or action that indicates sexual exploitation, harassment or abuse and does not tolerate any behavior or action that may harm the dignity or privacy of beneficiaries. Help offices and protection offices receive any complaints according to confidential procedures that guarantee the safety and security of complainants. Training on safeguarding policies and procedures is provided to individuals and beneficiaries as needed.
Increasing the rate of families returning to the region and increasing needs. Mechanism: Sending new lists to supporting organizations to meet needs according to capabilities.
A fire or theft occurs in the warehouse. Mechanism: Appointing a guard and installing fire extinguishers and surveillance cameras.
Variation in the distribution of aid packages between target groups. Mechanism: Publishing the mechanism for beneficiary acceptance, distribution, objections and complaints.
Difficulty in reaching the main registration center for beneficiaries. Mechanism: Formation of mobile teams for distribution and registration within rural areas.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Through our activities, our association will seek to provide immediate, decisive, and life-saving assistance to 500 families (2500 benificiaries) in the mentioned areas, including basic tools for each person (sponge, two blankets, a 15-liter water container, and a mosquito net where each family's share does not exceed 5 sponges, 10 blankets, 3 water containers, and 3 mosquito nets), in addition to providing each family with cooking utensils, pots, some dishes, tableware, glasses, dignity baskets, and distributing fans with batteries that charge on a solar panel along with a solar lamp. Thick blankets, a 1x2-meter carpet, and winter clothing will also be provided for each family member for winter preparation, with no more than 5 beneficiaries per family.
