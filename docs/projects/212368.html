Project Summary

During 2024, Violet aims to implement projects to improve the livelihoods of the most vulnerable people in NWS and infrastructure rehabilitation through the implementation of the following activities:
1. Creating temporary employment opportunities through implementing Cash for work related to the ERL sector to rehabilitate and restore the community infrastructure. Targeting 1000 people
2. Youth capacity building through the provision of Vocational training to enable them to start their businesses targeting 800 peoples
3. Provision of MSMEs cash grants to allow the vocational trainees to start their businesses targeting 400 people
4. Establishment of VSLA groups within targeted communities to provide increased access to business, emergency, or social funds for its members in distress. Targeting 40 groups of 600 people
5. Improve the access of the farmers to farmlands through road rehabilitation to support the farmers in marketing crops, transportation, and marketing their products as well as improving their access to the farmlands; the proposed target for this activity is to rehabilitate 5 KM of roads serving 3000 people within targeted communities.
6. Rehabilitation of 10 basic productive infrastructures (Markets/warehouses) with targeted communities serving 3100 people within targeted communities.
7. Rehabilitation of schools targeting 30 schools and serving 14551 individuals (students, teachers, and other school staff including cleaners, guards, etc.)
During the project implementation, Violet will prioritize women-headed households, PWD, youth, and other vulnerable groups to participate in the program. Moreover, the project will be designed and implemented to ensure the inclusion of PWD, mainly vocational training and business grants, in the program implementation. To this end, Violet will ensure the training halls are PWD-friendly spaces and easy to access.
VO will ensure CVA coordination and harmonization with other aid providers at the area level through ERL cluster and field-level coordination meetings and FGDs before each activity to avoid any possible overlapping with other aid providers and also reach the most vulnerable households with support.
Furthermore, through this project, VO aims to foster social cohesion within targeted communities and build a sense of ownership by local communities by including different community groups, including the most vulnerable, in the project activities' design and implementation.
Regarding CFW activity: referring to the Cash for Work Minimum Recommended ActionsGuidance for NWS developed by CWG and ERL partners:https://www.humanitarianresponse.info/en/operations/stima/document/nws-cash-work-recommended-minimum-actions-mar-2021 Violet will ensure Do No Harm approach/principles in this specific activity through consult community/local stakeholders at the area of implementation on project design, especially on the daily wage rate, number of work hours per day, and project duration to ensure CFW daily wage rates do not distort local labor market and draw workers away from local employment opportunities, therefore harming local producers and arbitrarily introducing non-sustainable practices.
All proposed project activities will be delivered in adherence to humanitarian and do-no-harm principles and will ensure equal access to all beneficiaries in need without discrimination while inclusively engaging them in decision-making throughout the project phases. Violet will work to ensure gender and protection are mainstreamed into all activities by prioritizing the specific and often sensitive issues related to gender, disability, age, and origin among the communities. The data has been analyzed with age, sex, and disability perspectives.
Collect and monitor data disaggregated by sex, age, and disability condition and monitor and oversee barriers to access to services. Violet has an active feedback and response mechanism. There are many channels to receive and deal with complaints.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

This project aims to respond to emergency needs by the implementation of different activities with appropriate modalities based on the needs and beneficiaries' preference availability; this project includes CVA, so before each activity, Violet will assess market systems functionality/capacity to absorb increased demand for goods and services and potential impact on markets and non-cash recipients accessing the same markets. Moreover, it will engage project stakeholders in the design and implementation to ensure the local ownership of the project. Thus, Violet will sign MOUs with the LCs, demonstrating their roles and responsibilities. Moreover, Violet will ensure close coordination with the ERL cluster and humanitarian actors in the field to avoid potential overlapping. During the implementation, Violet will ensure the BNF's protection and do No Harm principle by consulting the BNFs on the distribution location, Timing and arranging the priority ques for elderlies, women, and, but also through training the staff on the Organization code of conduct and PSEApolicy. Additionally, to avoid tension between the communities and different groups, Violet will include a % of the most vulnerable HC members in the project. It will also announce the project criteria, ensure they're clear to the BNFs, and activate the CRM mechanism. 40% of the project budget is already secured.
