Project Summary

The project aims to scale up safe and equitable access to formal education for crisis affected children (IDPs and Hosts), through restoration and preparation of schools by
providing students with learning materials (stationary, pens, notebooks), school bags, fixing all damaged components of affected schools like windows, doors, painting and remodelling the buildings of 15 targeted schools, establish, expand and rehabilitate new classrooms, and to enhance the quality of formal education for children and youth (aged5 –17 years) within a protective environment through providing teachers and education personnel with incentives, furnishing schools, provide children with recreational materials and Provide teachers and education personnel with teaching resources, kits, and guides in targeted schools. All the project activities are funded, when the project was approved, the target locations were fully accessed.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Changes in the security situation, particularly in project target areas, affect the safety/mobility/ displacement of beneficiaries/staff and results in being unable to participate in project.This includes the risk of targeted attacks and bombing of schools/ communities and mines, airstrikes and crossfire.GVB and SGBV Due to long-distance walking alone,children may get lost, be attacked or lose interest in learning. Not having enough WASH facilities for boys and girls might cause lack of privacy to the children.Tensions due to different/inadequate standards being used insufficient safety and health standards at schools.Mitigating measures:Closely monitor the security situation,ensure that staff members and teachers are trained in safety and Security best practices, including mine risk educationIf and when the local security situation deteriorates:a.Immediately inform internal emergency focal point on the situation;b.Coordinate with partners,cluster and hibernate/suspend the operations if necessary;c.Examine the possibilities of adopting different modalities to implement the project,communication with donors to accept flexibility and support relocation of activities to safer spaces whenever needed,alternative locations(community centers,homes,etc)and methodologies of education should be considered to avoid attack-related risks,Training of partners and local teams on Child safety,development of schools specific child safety policies;establishment of complaint mechanisms in school.Duty of Care policies are in place,and the staff are informed on the responsibilities as well as limitations of the organization.Work in collaboration with WASH section who will establish WASH facilities in schools.Tension/competition over incentive due to different scales, interruption of payments,etc.Offer incentives that are in line and comparable with public school teacher incentives.Standardize teacher's incentives at the levels of ESWG/cluster and other major donors

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risks: - Suspension of financial support. - Attacks on Schools. - Harassment and exploitation. Mitigating: - Continued support for the coming years for students to continue in their schools. - Develop a quick evacuation plan for schools until the danger ends, Security/safety training for the organization, students and school staff.Establish ECCE within the neighbourhoods whereby children will be dropped to the centre by their parents.- distribution activities take place inside target schools under the supervision of teachers and managers, Establishment of COC addresses GBV and PSEA, raising staff and beneficiaries awareness on PSEA,GBV and complaint mechanism
