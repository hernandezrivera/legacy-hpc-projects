1 - Needs

Boko Haram incessant attacks has had devastating consequences on the communities of Borno state. Their activities has displaced the farmers, business owners and causing them to abandon their farms and business to seek safety in the state headquarters (Maiduguri and Jere LGAs). After the closure of camps in Maiduguri and Jere, most IDPs still remain in the host communities. This displacement among family members and caregivers has caused devastating emotional and financial effects on already vulnerable households. These communities during the crisis period have experienced livelihood losses in addition to loss of life, housing and health coupled with the COVID-19 pandemic, which has devastated the livelihood of the populations. These unexpected situations have driven families to rely on desperate coping measures of cutting back on daily meals and pulling children from school. These extreme measures have increased risky behaviors, such as early marriage among young girls or transactional sex to help offset a family’s financial burdens, put children at risk of child labor, and cause households to spiral deeper into debt. Under this situation, Sure Aid Foundation proposes to implement a set of interventions to reduce the economic risks and increase livelihood opportunities for Married and young women through life skills training, sustainable enterprise development, saving and internal lending groups and access to essential services such as health in Maiduguri and Jere LGA.

2 - Activities

Under this project, the beneficiaries will be trained on certain activities like:1. Saving and internal lending community2. Training on business skills3. Vocational training The beneficiaries will be trained on saving and internal lending communities, which will help to gain knowledge and skill and formation of SILC activities.Sure Aid Foundation will facilitate the recruitment of field agents at the implementing communities. These field agents will support the SILC members in the various SILC groups. Each field agent will form 7 groups in each of the implementing communities, with each of the groups comprising 15-25 members. The group will be internal lending communities in which self selected groups make regular contributions to and borrow from it. The beneficiaries will be trained on business skills to prepare their mind for running sustainable businesses. During the business skill training, beneficiaries will begin to prepare a market study, with guidance from promoters. The aim of the market study is to map existing local businesses and assess the potential for starting up the small businesses in which they are interested. Based on the market study, beneficiaries who are interested in starting their own business will choose a business plan based on the market assessments for their proposed business, and accordingly identify their vocational training needs.Vocational training will start after the business skills training element. With the help of local training institutes and businesses, beneficiaries will receive a variety of training options, in activities such as accessory making, sewing, hairdressing, livestock raising, dairy-product making, perfume making, cleaning supplies production and food catering services. Beneficiaries at this stage will be supported to start their own businesses and they will be advised and encouraged to employ one or two people from the community as their business start taking shape. Concurrent with the vocational training and business start-up, beneficiaries will attend life skills training courses on health starting from sexual and reproductive health. The project will be implemented during a period of 12 months
