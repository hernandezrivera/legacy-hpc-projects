Project Summary

UNHAS will aim to maintain an air-transport service to all humanitarian organisations operating across Libya, with a focus on main hubs such as Tripoli, Misratah, Sebha and Benghazi, based on operational bottlenecks, humanitarian access and presence, in order to facilitate movements and projects implementation within the country.

Needs

With the prevailing and volatile security situation inside Libya, travelling by road is often not possible, and access to main operational areas and population in need remains a major bottleneck for humanitarian actors.

Activities

UNHAS will provide a fast, safe and flexible air access which is crucial for humanitarian organizations, including UN agencies, NGOs and donor entities to: access vulnerable people within Libya; transport cargo to remote locations; and provide capacity for evacuation of staff.
