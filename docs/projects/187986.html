Project Summary

The project supports IDPs and host communities in Idleb and Rural Aleppo with WASHservices, both emergency services for IDPs in camps and out of camps. People in camps will receive drinking water through water trucking or water pumping where possible, water storage tanks, constructing septic tanks, latrine blocks, desludging, garbage bins and garbage collection in addition to conducting hygiene promotion sessions. Hygiene kits and diapers for the elderly and PWD will be distributed three times.
In communities out of camp, both IDPs and host communities will benefit from rehabilitation and operation of water pumping stations, operation of station on solar power wherenever possible, and the rehabilitation of sewage network. This project will contribute to the improvement of the COVID-19 preparedness and prevention capacity of IDPs and host community residents, through ensuring the continuity of the WASH services and provide hygiene and protective materials as per the recommendation of the COVID-19 Global Humanitarian Response Plan to prevent the transmission of COVID-19.

4 - Needs

It is estimated that approximately 4.1 M people live in the northwest of Syria. 2.8 million people in need. Nearly 1.7 million people live in 1,360 camps/sites.. The lack of basic services put the survival of the majority of people at risk, given the waves of displacement and the outbreak of COVID-19. Water borne diseases and Leishmaniasis are also a threat especially in Al Bab districts, according to ACU, there are currently 1622 cases (1112 of them in Albab subdistrict and 510 in Al Raee subdistrict, in addition to 6945 Leishmaniasis cases in Al Bab subdistrict (ACU dashboard). Shortage of electricity remains the root cause for water supply systems underperformance or cessation and is mostly related to the significant shortages of fuel for power plants "HNO 2022"HIHFAD conducted a need assessment in Nov 2021 in Al-Bab sub-district; the assessment aimed to identify WASH and Health needs in order to find out the required interventions.The findings show that girls, boys, and women remain the most vulnerable groups affected by the water needs, with 59% of the population are under 18 and women representing 22% of beneficiaries from Shwieha water station, while 21% of the population are from high vulnerable such as PWD, pregnant and lactating women and the elderly. The majority pay for water due to the limited amount of water trucking while they already suffer from diminished purchasing power due to deterioration of local currency and the limited livelihood opportunities.

5 - Implementation of activities

The intervention will be based on the technical needs assessments by HIHFAD, thetargeted camps will receive the following services (water trucking, water storage tanks,Latrine blocks and septic tanks desludging of septic tanks, garbage bins, hygiene promotion and hygiene kits. A sewage line will be installed in Abnaa Mahein camps in DeiHassan. Moreover, garbage bins will be distributed in all targeted camps, and they will bedischarged three times per week (for every 10 HHs 1 garbage bin of half a barrel). HIHFADfi eld team will be responsible of implementing the activities of the projects. Latrines willbe purchased from Turkey according to the design which includes three latrines+1 forpeople with disabilities and are gender segregated.The project will take into consideration factors that hinder PWD from benefiting and participating in the project, by ensuring representatives of PWDs in water committees in the camps, and tailoring the activities to reach them as the location of PWD latrines, and tent to tent distribution of kits for them.

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

Children below 18 years old conduct works on behalf of contractors: HIHFAD inform allcontractors and included a paragraph in the contract to forbid any supplier fromcontracting children in daily labour. Movements of garbage trucks and water trucks incongested camp: HIHFAD discuss with benefi ciaries and camps managers the best routesleading to the water storage tanks, latrines and garbage bins in order to avoid anypotential risk.Health risk associated with garbage removal: HIHFAD ensures that contractors involved in SWM activities wear appropriate Personal Protective Equipment like gloves and face mask during work. Inequality in distribution; Physical and psychological harm arising from violence and coercion.Water distribution not safe toaccess or not accessible, especially for women, girls (exposure to GBV risk) and children elderly and people with disabilities; inequality in distribution. Mitigation: Consult separately girls, boys, women and men, including older people and those with disabilities in the assessment, design and implementation phases; Make sure that distance of water collection points remains at minimum distance.


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Timing and process of distribution may put women and girls at higher risk of GBV. Mitigation: HIHFAD will promote the establishment of adequate management plan; engagement with communities in distribution planning and feedback from different groups on package for improvement; training of staff and enforcement of code of PSEA code of conduct among partner staff from different groups on package for improvement; training of staff and enforcement of code of PSEA code of conduct among partner staff.


11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

There are several risks that threaten the safety and security, these are the major risks:1 –IEDs and remnants of war, to be considered one of the biggest and most dangerous threats at all since the region witnessed long-term fighting and clashes which were the main cause of spreading these materials among the villages and the towns. 2 - Disputes between the factions of the Syrian Free Army from time to time, these problems often end quickly and on the same day without escalation of the military position. 3 -Retaliatory military operations and assassinations by the sleeper cells belonging to the Kurdish militias located in the remote areas. HIHFAD maintains detailed security protocol for itsfi eld staff and patients.
