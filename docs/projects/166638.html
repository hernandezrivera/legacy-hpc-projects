Project Summary

• Use of IEC material from the Ministry of Health, translating into local languages i.e Xuangana, Ronga, Lomue, Chuabo, Manhaua.Spreading COVID19 messages through community radios on its spread and prevetion, male engagement messages in SBCC for positive masculinities, SGBV prevention and positive parenting, equitable distribution of resources, shared decision making and gender equality, existing GBV and child protection referral pathways to reflect changes in available services and ensure women have access to resources, hotlines , shelters and the WhatsApp platform that has been created by WHO and the Ministry of Health to get accurate information Dissemination of key messages through media , radio spots, OUTDOOR Technology and use of mega phones • IEC materials printed and distributed in intervention areas.
