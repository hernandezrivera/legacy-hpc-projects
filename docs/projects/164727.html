Project Summary

Silk Road proposes to support communities with water trucking and solid waste collection covering 7,984 individuals, (Around 1300 HH) located in Tell Abiad and Suluk sub-district in Ar-Raqqa governorate ( Suilk Road will targte Tell Abiad, Restam, Al zaibakia, Al Doughania, Sadek, Al Doughania-Ashman, Al Far city, Kssas, Ranin, Al Homaira, Shreian, Kherbet Elroz, Atshana Sluk, Sokkariyeh, Suluk, Bir Mohmmed Elkhader, Khatuniya Sluk, Hammam At- Turkman and Ein Al-Arus ) as an emergency response to the recent conflict escalation in Northeast Syria. The target population are IDPs, returnees and host community living in those selected communities; the majority of the IDPs are from Ain Issa camp, Ar-Raqqa countryside and some of them from Menbij.
Through this project, Silk Road will support target beneficiaries through providing potable water to all IDPs in selected communities through blanket water trucking service where they will be distributing 30 l/p/d. Silk Road will hire a contractor to conduct water sources mapping, and water trucking to the target communities in coordination with the WASH Working Group.
In addition to providing other basic WASH services, that will include solid waste collection management, public hygiene promotion activities and hygiene kits distribution, with a specialized provision hygiene kits that include menstrual hygiene products, particularly for women. The hygiene promotion campaigns will focus on awareness of water-borne diseases, basic guidance for personal hygiene, environmental cleanliness and the specific activities and inputs of the overall project and will aim to encourage communities to adopt safer hygiene practices while also providing the necessary hygiene items to reduce the risk of occurrence of health threats. Good hygiene in a community is achieved through the promotion of hygiene messages in complementary with the provision of basic hygiene items to enable the communities to practice good hygiene. Due to low literacy levels of women, and most men, hygiene messaging and mass information sharing will be undertaken using Banners, brochures and publications that will include messages and to promote lifesaving behaviour change.
Silk road identified that some IDPs families living within host communities and collective shelters need water tanks as they do not have access to enough water storage. Silk road will hire a contractor to procure and install communal water tanks. The size of each water tank is 1m3 which can serve five families (considering that the average family size is 6. Silk road will assess the situation at the project start to assess the number of IDPs changed and they need to install additional tanks.
Furthermore, the proposed WASH interventions in this proposal will ensure that they do not create additional risks for the affected population (especially IDPs women and children and the newly IDPs) but will provide the best solutions to the suffering population in the targeted areas, also Silk Road will ensure that the health impact in this emergency WASH intervention will reduced disease risk and reduced transmission risk and it adhering to internationally recognized standards and best practices, ensuring that services are provided in line with the Do No Harm framework, Sphere standards, and humanitarian principles, and that services are of the highest quality.

4 - Needs

Reports issued by international community indicate that more than 75 000 displaced people are unable to return to their homes or camps (According to mobility and needs monitoring - December overview, 2,567 returned to Tell Abiad subdistrict during December). Displaced people are facing an unknown future especially in Tel Abiad & Ras AL Ain, where the relocation dynamic is very frequent due to the current situation.According to need assessment conducted by Silk Road, many families returned to Tell Abiad and Ras Al Ain districts and their countryside, in addition to the displacement of families from Ain Issa camp, Menbij and Ain Al Arab cities to Tell Abiad. Most of the 18,000 people who were living in Tell Abiad returned back to Tell Abiad city or areas sounding. Safety and security issues are the most important requirements in the current situation, especially after incidents occurred such as a cars bomb. Families continue to flow from the border areas and clashing lines to the safer areas in Tell Abiad, Ras Al Ain, Sukkariyeh, Karmazat and the northern countryside of Abiad. Tell Abiad sub-district is suffering from a massive lack of drinking water supply, currently, it receives water in a limited time and during specific days of the week as this kind of services is not adequate to all families need and even it doesn’t reach faraway houses who had to buy water from tanks owners with a 1000-1500 SYP for each barrel 220 litres. NES Populated areas are essentially a series of small towns and villages, with limited infrastructure. Due to a disruption to the supply from the Al Hisha water station, residents resorted to using local wells. The percentage of income spent on securing drinking water is around 15 to 17 % per family. Many families, especially IDPs, have to face difficulties in reaching the needed water where there are limited private sources with a high price and not available at all times.

5 - Implementation of activities

Silk Road proposes to support communities with integrated WASH services include water trucking, solid waste collection, hygiene kits distribution and hygiene promotion campaigns covering Approximately 11,522 individuals, (Around 2164 HH) located in Tell Abiad district in Ar-Raqqa governorate as an emergency response to the recent conflict escalation in Northeast Syria. Silk Road will support target beneficiaries through providing safe and clean water to all host communities, returnees and IDPs in the selected communities through water trucking service where they will be distributing 30 l/p/d. Silk Road will hire a contractor to conduct water sources mapping, and water trucking to the target communities in coordination with the WASH cluster. In addition to providing other basic WASH services, that will include solid waste collection management (Including maintenance of dumpsites), public hygiene promotion activities and hygiene kits distribution, with a specialized provision hygiene kits that include menstrual hygiene products, particularly for women. Also, Silk Road will rehabilitate the Sewage Network in Hammam Al Turkmen. The hygiene promotion campaigns will focus on awareness of water-borne diseases, basic guidance for personal hygiene, environmental cleanliness and the specific activities and inputs of the overall project and will aim to encourage communities to adopt safer hygiene practices while also providing the necessary hygiene items to reduce the risk of occurrence of health threats. Silk Road identified that some IDPs families living within host communities and collective shelters need water tanks as they do not have access to enough water storage. Silk road will hire a contractor to procure and install communal water tanks. Silk Road will ensure that the health impact in this emergency WASH intervention will reduce disease risk and reduced transmission risk and it adhering to internationally recognized standards.
