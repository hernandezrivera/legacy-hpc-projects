1 - Needs

One of the foremost needs of the humanitarian community in northeast Nigeria is ensuring access to remote and hard-to-reach areas where the people in need are located. The ongoing conflict and security challenges often make ground transportation for aid workers impossible, making air services the only alternative for the movement of humanitarian personnel to provide support, assess needs, and coordinate relief efforts. This is especially critical in medical emergencies for the transportation of patients and medical workers in locations with limited medical facilities. Storage infrastructure in the locations where humanitarian response is being conducted is extremely limited or non-existent. Therefore, the need for common warehouse services in these locations where a high level of humanitarian needs and thus response is located, is extremely critical. Delivery of critical and time-sensitive humanitarian cargo to hard-to-reach locations has also been a challenge for the humanitarian community. The ability to deliver this critical aid in an organized and timely manner has been a vital support to the humanitarian community. These humanitarian needs are underpinned by the need for coordination between humanitarian actors and the need for sharing information among the humanitarian community to ensure effective and efficient delivery of the response.

2 - Activities

The Logistics Sector will facilitate access to secured common storage facilities on across six humanitarian hubs in Bama, Banki, Damasak, Dikwa, Monguno, and Ngala. The sector will also maintain a limited stock of logistics assets for loan to humanitarian organizations requiring additional storage space and for response to sudden onset emergencies. The Sector will also conduct dedicated logistics trainings in the areas of storage, transportation, and core humanitarian supply chain management to help improve operations of humanitarian actors. Effective humanitarian assistance requires actors to reach affected communities to provide timely life-saving assistance. Thus, UNHAS will continue to serve humanitarians with fixed wing operating to/from Abuja, Maiduguri and Yola and helicopter flights operating from Maiduguri 11 locations: Bama, Banki, Damasak, Damboa, Damaturu, Monguno, Rann, Pulka, Gwoza, Ngala and Dikwa.
