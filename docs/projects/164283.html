Project Summary

Al-Birr Hospital was built, operated and owned by Al-Birr Association since 1994 in order to provide all health services (primary and secondary) to all citizens, especially the poor patients and the patients from northern rural Homs, with high quality and in a manner, that preserves their dignity without any discrimination.
The hospital has 150 beds. It is the largest private and charitable hospital in Syria.
Before the crisis in Al-Waer, Al-Birr hospital was visited by a patients from all of Homs city (60% of them from rural Homs) since it's equipped with modern equipment, and has the best staff of doctors and nurses; especially after the damage of most public health centers and public hospitals. Al-Birr hospital was providing all kind of health care including primary, secondary and even tertiary health care services. It was providing all kinds of surgeries and medical care including open cardiac surgery, cardiac catheterization, and specific operations such as neurosurgery.
During the crisis period, the hospital was severely affected because of its location in Al-Waer area, which forced the association to stop working in some departments and limit the services to some services such as dialysis, especially in the last five years.
After the agreement of Al-Waer neighborhood, A lot of people returned to their homes in Al-Waer, so Al-Birr has started rehabilitating this hospital after years of operating another hospitals in Homs city (Farkouh, Mahattah, Al-Kindi and Al-Shawi).
Till now, there is no enough public care centers in Homs city, where most of public hospitals there is just providing some primary services such as outpatient clinics and laboratory, while urgent secondary care services are not provided so patients have to transfer to other private hospitals out of Homs to get services.
Anyway, the kind of services will be provided in Homs city and will help people not just in Homs governorate but also in other governorates since Homs is in the middle of Syria and people do not have to travel to Damascus for obtaining necessary and urgent health services such as catheterization, neurosurgery, lithotripsy and other services.
Because of the great damage in the hospital, the difficulty of full rehabilitation of its building, and the desire to provide health services to the increasing number of returnees Al-Birr has rehabilitated a dialysis department, emergency department which includes an operation room and ICU, out-patient clinics, radiology and laboratory departments. Al-Birr plans to rehabilitate other departments in the hospital (ICU, NICU, surgical department, cardiosurgery and catheterization, surgical ICU, hospitalization rooms, gynecology, pediatrics departments.
process of those floors will include architectural modification according to the terms of the Ministry of health, interior wood work, exterior Aluminum work, floor installation for the surgery rooms and maintenance the elevator of surgery and services, medical gas network, generators, heating, and air-conditioning devices and its networks. The rehabilitation process is an urgent issue to integrate the health services that Al-Birr hospital provides. These services will match the critical needs and will present with high quality.
Al-Birr hospital has all the equipment since it tested its best to save its equipment. These equipment are working, or are maintained by Al-Birr, and these project will rehabilitate and rebuild the infrastructure of these floors including wood, aluminum, plumbing, electricity, painting and flooring.
AL-Birr will provide primary and secondary health care to all people in need.

4 - Needs

According to (HNO) 13.2 million people in Syria are in need for health services ( 9.5 million of them are female and 5.1 million are children). In Homs there are 878,527 in need for health services (HNO).During the past years, the city of Homs and its inhabitants have experienced harsh conditions that have negatively affected all aspects of life. the city is still suffering from severe weakness in the infrastructure, especially in the health field. Reference to WHO Health Resources Availability Monitoring System( HeRAMs )the situation of the public hospitals remains the same: the National hospital of Homs is partially to fully damaged and non-functioning. Urgent secondary care services are not provided enough in care centers, and the number of these public centers is not suitable for number of population in Homs city. so patients have to transfer to other private hospitals or to travel out of Homs to get services. According to (HeRAMS)out of 23 public health centers in Northern Rural Homs; 11 were reported as not functioning ,11 as partially functioning (provide vaccination services only) and 1 as fully functioning. In addition to identified barriers to access healthcare: cost of services; cost of transportation; safety and security restricting access; low coverage; lack of personnel; lack of medicine; and lack of adapted services. While the crisis has affected all people living in Syria to varying degrees, Displaced populations and returnees, particularly those in northern and eastern countryside of Homs been more acutely affected and all population groups inside these areas are still in-need to get health services with minimal wages.Al-Birr hospital is one of the largest Charity Hospitals in the middle east with a capacity of 150 beds and it consists of 10 floors.Al-Birr Hospital have been damaged severely during the crisis. Al-Birr Hospitals was providing services to all people in Homs and it's rural with minimal wages.

5 - Implementation of activities

Al-Birr will rehabilitate the infrastructure for all floors.Al-Birr will decorate all floors and rooms.Al-Birr will provide needed equipment for all rooms.and will provide primary and secondary health care to people in Homs and it's rural through it's Al-Birr Hospital out-patient clinics and Hospital.Al-Birr will provide medical consultation to 50,000 beneficiaries in all specializations through it's out-patient clinics which includes all the medical specializations.Also MRI, CT scan, lab Tests, echo in addition to physical rehabilitation sessions through it's hospital physiotherapy center.Al-Birr will support general and orthopedic surgeries and also will support normal and cesarean deliveries in addition to cardiovascular operations and cardiac catheterization.All of the above mentioned services will be 100% free of charges. Al-Birr M&E team will monitor all the services provided by the project and will conduct surveys and evaluate the processes and collect the beneficiaries feedback on the provided services and send it to the management to take the corrective actions in case there is a need to, or to help them take the right decisions.the M&E team will also monitor the process of applying the corrective actions and measure their impact on the project and the satisfaction of the beneficiaries.Since the project is designed to serve all people in need in Homs and in order to provide the services to people who deserve it Al-Birr will conduct a socio-economic study for every application which the people submit to benefit from the project services especially in providing mechanism of secondary health care.
