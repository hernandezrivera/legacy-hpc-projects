Project Summary

This Project main objective is to reduce excess to morbidity and mortality of epidemic prone diseases and health insecurities among the vulnerable population of returnees and host communities in Terekeka and Kajo keji Counties classified under (IPC4) with 49000 and 12981 people in emergency, due to various circumstances affecting ,Including: conflict, flooding & drought leading, long period of stress of food insecurity, exposing communities into negative coping strategies, of the already poor health indicators as
1. Malaria (accounted for the majority of the daily consultations); 2. Acute watery diarrhea; 3. Acute respiratory tract infections/pneumonia; 4. Typhoid; 5. Cough; 6. Skin infections; 7. Eye infections; 8. Ear infections; 9. High rates of malnutrition; 10. Suspected Whooping cough reported in Kiu PHCU in Jaming Payam of Tali , Tijor ,of tereke as well as Kajo keji Counties
2. high global acute malnutrition (GAM) rate in Tali was at 17.3% of the children aged 6-59 months, increase of children mortality,
3. acute shortage of many essential medicines,
4. acute shortage of trained health personnel,
5. very week health referrals system in the two counties for eg in terekeka there is (only 1 ambulance covering 250,493) population, in vast geographic administration, and very challenging topographic, (streams and sump)
6. with very limited logistic, for EPI, with very small coverage,
7. acute clean water shortage up to 80% of population practicing wide spread open defecation;
8. No EWARS report due to SMART phones; yet no logistic available
9. Very Limited supportive, supervision being conducted to the peripheral health facilities by the CHD/SMOH officials; due to capacity challenges
10. There is outbreak in Tindilo a mysterious illness presenting with generalized body swelling (edematous) and the body becomes heavy. So far 10 people were reported to have died in the last 4 months; and
11. suspected measles cases were reported in the facilities, like of Tijor,Tali, tindlo and Kajo keji
Therefore this project will employ mixture approaches, Mobile /Rapid Response Mechanism, and Temporary & Static PHCU
The project deploy 4 qualified health teams to support Integrated Mobile Health care and 1PHCU team in reaching 40% (26,679) of Health Clusters targets, PIN, the project is targeting vulnerable population of returnees and host communities. (Including under-fives girls, and boys, men and women elderly and disabilities)
Main project activities, includes
1. Rapid Screening/detection, among the affected population for case finding treatment and referrals
2. provision of essential medical supplies for essential health care package in the remote population to reduces access to morbidity and mortality, in hard to reach areas under IPC4
3. Special Management of uncomplicated, common illnesses/Treatment causing high morbidity and mortality, including SGBV through (CMR)
4. to conduct Community Management of common illnesses/Treatment, this will reduces access to morbidity and mortality, in wider range, hard to reach areas, due to present of essential drugs at hands CDD.
5. to provide Basic Nutrition services in nutrition education and basic Management of uncomplicated MAM
6. to Provide immunization against vaccine preventable diseases.
7. to provide emergency access, to clean Water through provision water purification facilities, and Hygiene promotion, education,
8. to conduct Capacity building for health care workers, Boma Health Committee, and Community Drugs distributors CDDs
9. to provide continues Health Education and promotion distribution of IEC ,
10. to Strengthen disease surveillance /EWARN reporting platform, through and outbreak response.
