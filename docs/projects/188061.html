Project Summary

In December 2021, the Borno state government closed all IDP camps within Maiduguri the state's capital in an effort to return IDPs to their respective home towns and bring back normalcy to life and enjoying continued peace in the state.
The government repatriation process is only applicable to the state's capital, which means IDPs are still living in camps and camp-like communities with a dire need for support to coordinate and manage these camps as well as providing shelter and NFI support to these IDPs in the host communities and camps.
This project is aimed at providing support to IDPs in Magumeri with specific objectives of coordinating intervention among implementing partners in Magumeri to avoid duplication of work; provide out of camp activities to facilitate support among the IDPs; to provide shelter support to the IDPs having poor or no shelter in Magumeri; to provide NFI to IDPs in need.

2 - Activities

CAMP COORDINATION CAMP MANAGEMENT ACTIVITIES-Work with ETT/DTM teams on collating new arrival information and bi-monthly site assessments. -Monitoring of service provision and humanitarian standards in the site, and regularly providing this information to partners at camp-level for service planning and delivery. -Setting-up or reinforcing governance and community participation structures, ensuring meaningful representation and voice for men/women, boys/girls, minority groups, and persons with specific needs. -Working with relevant partners to ensure that the displaced families are informed on the assistance and services available in the camps/site and how to access them, including through establishment of information desks and through community outreach, and are aware of mechanisms to make complaints and receive feedback. -Promoting and facilitating accountability towards target population including through the establishment of complaints and feedback mechanisms, and the promotion of two-way communication. -Working with the community to identify site improvement and mitigation needs (such as levelling of pathways, slope protection, drainage, installation of communal facilities, and lighting), and implementing works in line with the Area Coordination focal point site plans and in line with the Shelter and Site Improvement Technical Working Group guidelines, including the consideration of GBV mitigation measures, coordinated with other infrastructure works as needed. -Supporting the development of effective referral mechanisms in close collaboration with relevant stakeholders and Protection actors. -Developing and updating contingency and emergency response plans at site-level in collaboration with the CiC/relevant authorities and humanitarian partners, following overall contingency and emergency response plans and guidance developed by the Site Management Sector/ISCG with the local authorities. -Supporting the allocation of land for shelters and set up of service infrastructure. In line with the SOP on land allocation for shelters and services infrastructure, work with the CiC and local authorities to ensure land allocation follows site plans and relevant Sector strategies for service coverage, and to ensure non-duplication of services. -Promoting Protection and GBV mainstreaming, Accountability to Affected Populations, and Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) at site level. -Engage with host communities around sites and settlements to promote peaceful coexistence. -Adopting a participatory approach in all stages of the response (needs assessment, planning, implementation, monitoring and evaluation). NFI AND SHELTER ACTIVITIES-Conduct Needs Assessment for Emergency Shelter and NFI Kits in the specified project area.-Conduct Beneficiary Selection, registration and token card distribution-Verification of the lists of beneficiaries provided by the representatives or collected by the field team by the team through field visits and to ensure compliance with vulnerability criteria.-Verification of the beneficiaries by asking them to confirm their names using the coded tokens/registration cards, to confirm them against the registration list. -Distribution Process of the Shelter and NFI kits-Transport the Emergency Shelter (ES) and NFI Kits to distribution site or sites. Estimate volume of 500 ES kits is 70 metric ton.-Ensure Protection Mainstreaming: Promote Protection and GBV mainstreaming, Accountability to Affected Populations, and Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA).-Establishing beneficiary feedback mechanisms to identify issues related to the Emergency Shelter and NFI Kits: targeting and registration of beneficiaries, beneficiary receipt of planned assistance, security at distribution sites, issues related to beneficiary access to the planned assistance and protection issues related to the entire distribution process.
