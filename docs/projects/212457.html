Project Summary

This project began in 2021 based on needs identified at that time, and the NGO will continue this project until July 31, 2024 to ensure out-of-school children and those at risk of dropping out are able to access safe and quality learning opportunities. The project targets three governorates with continuing high needs according to the 2024 HNO: Al-Hasakeh (100% SDs at severity 4), Ar-Raqqa (30% SDs at severity 3, 70% at severity 4), and Deir-ez-Zor (22% at severity 3, 71% at severity 4, and 7% at severity 5).
The objective of ensuring access to safe and quality learning opportunities will achieved through strengthening educational pathways by investment in non-formal education services, integrated community protection services (PTAs, CBCP, etc.) and quality learning environments. In addition to ensure that teachers are trained on well-being and life skills to support the psycho-social well-being of both non-formal teachers and students, so to achieve project target the children aged 3-5 years will enrolled in ECCD centers in locations of project implementation (Hasakeh city, Shadadeh subdistrict, Areesha camp, Raqqa City and Kisreh subdistrict in Deir-ez-Zor governorate), those children will gain early literacy and math skills, also children aged 6-17 years will enrolled in TLS centers in mentioned locations and will supported with non formal education program in addition to participating in remedial education interventions, while the parents and caregivers will receive parenting program and community activities to enhance their parenting skills, in the same time teachers will receive trainings on Life Skills Education, Psycho-Social Support and Violence Prevention, to ensure this project will implemented as planned , the children will receive student kids and teachers will provided with teacher kits, and the caregivers will receive ELM kits, teachers and caregivers will attend the parent-teacher association (PTA), in addition to monthly teacher learning cycle (TLC) meeting.
Targets: the project will reach 792 children aged 3-5 with ECCD programming, including psychosocial support and educational recreation activities. It will also reach 12,870 children aged 6-17 with non-formal education, providing them with NFE classes and school supplies. The project will also provide training to 5,973 teachers in non-formal education.
Protection Risk Analysis:
Risk 1: Learning facilities can be attacked by parties to the conflict or by communities. Attacks may include airstrikes, bombs/explosions/shelling, fighting/gunshots, arson/fire, looting/theft; kidnapping/arrest of teachers or students, direct attacks on teachers or students, intimidation of school staff / students or military use of the school, arson, theft or looting. Likelihood: Medium, impact: high. Mitigation: learning spaces should have safety protocols in place. Risk 2: Teachers, or other education personnel participate in physical punishment. Students participate in bullying or act violently towards students and/or to teachers. Likelihood: High, impact: high. Mitigation: learning spaces should have an enforced code of conduct against all forms of violence. School personnel should be trained on prevention and reporting of. Risk 3: Learning facilities are unsafe, does not provide protection form adverse weather condition, inadequate WASH facilities or lack of sex disaggregated WASH facilities. Likelihood: Medium, Impact: High. Mitigation: Improve safety of learning facilities through rehabilitation, repairs and provision of additional WASH facilities.
Coordination:
The NGO has leadership in various roles within the cluster system, with Education Working Group (WG) Coordinators based in Northeast and Northwest Syria. The NGO actively participates in the Protection WG, the Child Protection WG the GBV Task Force, MHPSS TWG, the Child Protection Case Management Task Force, and the CAAC Task Force.
