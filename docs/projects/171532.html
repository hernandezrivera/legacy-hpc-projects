Project Summary

Dollow, Luuq, Garbaharey and Belet Hawa districts in Gedo region constantly face flare-ups of humanitarian crises including flooding, conflict and displacement, which puts women and girls at a heightened risk of GBV. The socio- cultural constraints that continue to impede access to basic services especially for girls, and present more grave consequences for women and girls such as early and forced marriages make Gedo Region a difficult environment for women and girls to enjoy their health and sexual rights. Protection risks are particularly high during stresses and shocks, which unfortunately are frequently experienced in Gedo. COVID-19 pandemic has heightened protection risks for women and girls who are most at risk of GBV including FGM, Intimate Partner Violence and sexual abuse and exploitation due to disrupted livelihoods. Services are currently available in main town centers leaving women in rural areas such as Gedo, IDP camps and smaller towns without access to lifesaving care. Furthermore the non-existence of strong referral systems and a lack of awareness by community members compounds access constraints to services that would otherwise be very beneficial, even life- saving to survivors (Gedo GBV Rapid Assessment, 2020)
Trocaire has been working in Gedo Region since 1992, and has more recently (in 2017 onwards) integrated its health service provision with protection/ GBV services. As a co-coordinator of the Gedo sub-GBV working group, Trocaire has since collaborated with local and international stakeholders to strengthen protection and GBV services in Gedo and will work with local organisations to strengthen nclusivity in service delivery especially for people living with disabilities. These efforts have been scaled up in the worrying context marred by negative economic and social impacts of COVID-19 pandemic. This project will continue the COVID-19 scale up response by strengthening health referrals through supporting a GBV hotline, referral transport, provision of clinical management of rape services and case management with provision of dignity kits to 500 survivors, including people living with disabilities. Trocaire will work closely with community structures, including local NGOs who have been working directly with this group, to identify people with disabilities and ensure their access to basic life-saving services, and advocate for inclusive targeting criteria within the GEDO GBV sub- cluster. Capacity building sessions will be conducted for staff and community volunteers across all areas of intervention. COVID-19 preventive and control measures will continue to be implemented in existing service delivery points such as women centers, static health facilities and mobile outreach. This will be done through a GBV integration approach.
This project will reach approximately 9090 women and girls, including those with disabilities (approximately 100 girls and women with various forms of disabilities) with lifesaving GBV services in a safe, timely, confidential and coordinated manner. This will be in line with the cluster objectives one and two.

Project Activities

• Clinical management of rape (CMR) serviceTrocaire has protocols and standard operating procedures related to informed consent, history taking and examination, information management, referral and reporting and medical certificates in line with the Somali National Protocol on Clinical Management of Rape (2015) and international standards (WHO, 2007 and 2019 forthcoming). CMR service provision is undertaken in adherence to these SoPs. Some of the key CMR related activities in this project include: Providing survivors with comprehensive care and medication within recommended period of time (72-120hours); post exposure prophylaxis, emergency contraceptive pill, antibiotics, prophylaxis for sexually transmitted diseases. Clinical and non-clinical staff capacity building on Clinical Management of Rape, including survivor centered skills, compassionate care and first-line Psychosocial Support. 20 clinical staff (4 doctors, 13 midwives and 3 nurse) were trained on CMR in 2019, a refresher training will be conducted for them Coordination with other available GBV service providers for referrals to and from other services and from communities. This will include provision of transport for survivor to and from health facilities.• Provision of psychosocial support through women centersThis project will provide psychosocial support through structured psychosocial support sessions. Women who require more support will be referred to case managers based at women centers.Trocaire shall work closely with a local protection partner to renovate, equip and manage four women centres, each in all the four districts of intervention. GBV case management (GBV)Trocaire will primarily provide GBV case management primarily through women centers designed with private case management rooms. • Training of health staff on Clinical Management of Rape (Refresher),Management of FGM complications and GBV response particularly survivor centered approaches• Continued training for health and nutrition staff on psychosocial first aid and referrals• Awareness raising on GBV services available and prevention of GBV will be done for community members through radio, the use of contextualized IEC material. Awareness will be raised by other trained outreach groups, guided by appointed and trained protection focal points. Monitoring and evaluation activitiesThese will include regular program team monitoring sessions, but also deliberate safety audits within static and mobile facilities.

Mitigation Measures

Risk 1:Likelihood: MediumImpact: HighMitigation measures: • Risk monitoring and safety audits in project sites to ensure project sites are not located within areas of potential attacks• The service will have mobile outreach to ensure services are taken closer to address access barrier and that people are not leaving their locations to access services. - Trocaire will work closely with representatives of people living with disability to ensure that girls and women affected can access GBV and other services provided by partners in a convenient, safe and timely manner. • A strong referral system will be put in place to refer cases requiring advanced services Risk 2Likelihood: LowImpact: HighMitigation measures: • A detailed analysis has been conducted to exclusion and marginalization risks• Staff who speak language of minority groups will be hired to provide services • Number of female staff will be increased to facilitate better participation, access and utilization by women and girls, including those with disabilities.• Information sharing on services will be provided in language of minority groups, feedback and complaints mechanisms have also been translated into the two main dialects of Somali including Afmaay widely spoken by minority groups• Platforms for women and girls participation will be enhanced, requiring gender representation in committees, involving women and girls at all stages of the project (female specific forums to enable women raise their concerns). Risk 3Likelihood: MediumImpact: HighMitigation measures:• Safety audits will be conducted in sites where GBV services are provided and urgent routine actions taken as per findings• The protection interventions will take into account SGBV risk mitigation throughout the project cycle in line with IASC Guidelines for Integrating Gender-Based Violence Interventions in Humanitarian Action• Mobile services will be provided to displaced women and girls within IDP sites to reduce GBV risks associated with travelling to access services. Measures will be put in place to ensure deliver of services at household level for girls and women living with disabilities.
