Project Summary

The intervention aims to achieve and sustain stability for access on an equitable basis and offer continuity to learners, girls and boys. This means ensuring that every child targeted in the 64 selected municipalities can access education on an equitable basis without discrimination. This will be achieved using several approaches that will contribute directly to this outcome:
1. Improved inclusive infrastructural capacity: To promote access to learning opportunities for school aged boys and girls in Cameroon, the capacities of the public schools that are most affected by the crises in North/South West, East and Far North will be increased.
2. Inclusive Education offer improvement and promotion of free access: Inclusive education strategies remove barriers and create safe learning environments for all children, and promote free access for all children regardless of nationality, legal status, gender or disability.

1 - Besoins / Needs

The project is complementary of the ECW seed fund received in the framework of the Multi-Year Resilience Programme (MYRP) designed with the different stakeholders, targeting 64 municipalities in the Country. Improved inclusive infrastructural capacity: To promote access to learning opportunities for school aged boys and girls in Cameroon, the capacities of the public schools that are most affected by the crises in North/South West, East and Far North will be increased. Rehabilitation of schools (classrooms, equipment, gender separated latrines and water points) will be undertaken. Rehabilitation of schools and safe temporary learning spaces (ETAPE) will comply with regulations for water, sanitation and hygiene (WASH) standards, gender equity standards, and accessibility for students with physical disability. Addition of new classrooms, including temporary learning spaces and boreholes will also be undertaken based on needs-assessments, to increase the availability of spaces and better equip the schools to accommodate for larger number of students, including children with disabilities. Provision of alternative and distance learning opportunities: the inte aims to continue expanding alternative mechanisms to widen access to education beyond formal schooling; complementary, alternative, and flexible pathways to learning need to be examined and made available to out of school children in order to improve their intellectual development. This will include the use of innovative technology to promote access to different forms of remote learning through digital devices (tablets, mobile centers equipped with nano server, nano projector and solar battery), radio programmes (solar radio, USB keys), print (Training Manual) and other media. It will also entail the construction and equipment of safe temporary learning spaces (ETAPE) for the provision of non-formal education that is accessible by learners and educators in the regions most affected by the crisis. Inclusive Education offer improvement and promotion of free access: Inclusive education strategies remove barriers and create safe learning environments for all children, and promote free access for all children regardless of nationality, legal status, gender or disability. Barriers to enrollment include the attached negative stigma regarding children with disabilities which leads to social exclusion. The intervention aims to increase access to learning for children with all types of disabilities.
