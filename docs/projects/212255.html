Project Summary

Needs :
The areas in this project were chosen based on HNO 2024 severity scale greater than 3 of the protection and education sectors, and based on the evaluation visits by the SSSD team in December 2023 in Rural Damascus, Aleppo, Idleb, Deir-ez-Zor, Al-Hasakeh, Dar'a, As-Sweida, Hama, Tartous and Latakia in cooperation with the key people, the mayor, school principals, and based on the home visits by the MTs, the needs in the regions were similar to some extent (education, schools rehabilitation, child protection, psychosocial support ..) Most of the population in these areas are vulnerable residents, with various needs.
Intervention, Implementation of activities :
Accordingly, this project will meet part of the humanitarian needs through the provision of integrated education and protection services in 9 governorates, through rehabilitating 12 schools ( WASH, solar power systems ), and providing 124 classrooms and 5 ECE rooms with furniture, where 10,000 students and 2,500 out-of-school children will be offered education sessions, also 12,500 children will get a school bag and school supplies, as well as 6,400 students will benefiting from life skills and citizenship education programs, and 17,000 beneficiaries the implementation of back-to-school campaigns and various awareness-raising and CP campaigns.
Also, 630 teachers and members of the community will be trained and their role will be activated through 18 PTAs will be established. 1,500 students will support with cross-line exams in Aleppo and Deir-ez-Zor.
In addition to provide a protection prevention and response activities (GBV &CP) in the targeted schools.
GBV Response activities will be implemented in schools targeted for educational activities, outside working hours in private rooms (it could be the administration room or other room to be agreed with the school management), with an emphasis on maintaining confidentiality and privacy in all case management procedures, these activities will be carried out by a specialized staff.
As for prevention activities, students and caregivers will be targeted within schools outside working hours.
PRA:
Risks: 1. Lack of quality services and referral expose survivors to harm, enhancing risk of stigmatization reprisal and further trauma. Lack of quality services also lead to fewer disclosures and lack of trust in services. 2.Privacy concerns along with confidentiality and safety might prevent women & girls to access GBV response services
Likelihood: High Impact: High
Mitigating measures: 1. Service mapping including only those that can provide survivor centered services
2. Include supervisory structures in response services
3. GBV case management service providers ensure confidentiality principles are in line with global standards and the GBV SOPs, ensure data protection protocols and private physical space are in place.
Risks: Risk of inter or intra-community tension due to service delivery locations or types of services offered if one community is served and a neighboring community not served. Protection risk faced by girls and boys may persist if community members do not play an active role in the delivery of protection services.
Likelihood: Medium Impact: High
1. Ensure a comprehensive service mapping is undertaken so that risk mitigation and communication with communities can be proactive and informed.
2. Ensure consultation with communities and neighboring communities related to location of services to understand perspectives of communities are all understood and addressed. Including access barriers for those with injuries/disabilities and communication mechanisms for persons with visual/hearing impairments.
3. Support national actors and community-based groups to ensure sustainability of protection intervention.
4. Established community-based groups/committees to enhance community-based protection mechanisms.
4. Ensure information sharing with community members on services available.
