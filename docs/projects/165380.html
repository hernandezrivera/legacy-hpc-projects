Project Summary

The project will be directly implemented by German INGO Deutsche e.V Welthungerhilfe (WHH). This project aims to respond to the needs of conflict-affected newly IDPs in/off camps by providing live-saving assistance through multi-purpose voucher to cover the basic needs of IDP households in A'zaz since this modality offers flexibility and freedom of choice while restoring some dignity and are less stigmatizing than the assistance through in kind assistance. The project targets the IDPs who have arrived in A’zaz in/off camps within the last two months and already reside in a shelter; along with ones who are already camp residents and have not received any humanitarian assistance over the last six months. Each family will receive voucher worth of 120 USD per month for three months period to cover monthly basic needs in food, hygiene and non-food items. Targeted Household (HHs) will be able to redeem food, hygiene, non-food items from the contracted vendors/shops. Additionally, the proposed project will support people to increase their income by enabling them to save up and use it in other economic activities. As this project also aims to enhance the resilience of the targeted HHs, the negative coping strategies will be mitigated. In total, 1,000 HHs will benefit from the activity. The specific location and camps will be agreed on in a coordination with AFAD and local authorities in order to avoid duplication.

4 - Needs

This allocation strategy is developed based on the Northwest Syria Humanitarian Readiness and Response Plan (31 January 2020). As the 2020 HRP is still under preparation, this allocation will align with last year’s 2019 HRP which remains largely relevant with the current context. The allocation focuses only on the most urgent needs aligned with the first and second 2019 HRP objectives; 1. To provide life-saving humanitarian assistance to the most vulnerable people to alleviate suffering; 2. To respond to protection needs with an emphasis on those in areas with high severity of needs. According to WHH's assessments from February 2020, there are estimated 13 formal and 26 informal camps in A’zaz district and 134,139 IDPs residing in A’zaz in total. Among these IDPs 79.836 individuals live in formal camps, while 54.303 individuals live in informal camps. Azzaz is the district with the highest number of IDPs. The Turkish authorities directly addressed WHH with regard to the high needs in the region. A rapid assessment has been conducted by WHH in A’zaz, Aleppo governorate, between the dates January 2020 – February 2020. The main objective of the assessments was to gain an overview of the humanitarian situation in the informal camps in the targeted areas. The assessments focused on specific sectors which are food security, NFI and WASH based on the scope of WHH activities. Key Informant Interviews have been conducted in 23 informal tented settlements in A’zaz with camp managers, camp sector managers and some camp residents, and in 6 central retailer-shops in A’zaz. According to the recently conducted rapid needs assessment on the February 2020, people living in IDP camps mainly prone to access to basic needs and services due to lack of financial inputs. The situation got worsened in Azaz after the recent displacements. The majority of the people are working as daily workers and earning generally less than 3 $ per day. The methodology of RNA tool was used in assessment.

5 - Implementation of activities

The project targets the IDPs who have arrived in A’zaz in/off camps within the last two months and already reside in a shelter; along with ones who are already camp residents and have not received any humanitarian assistance over last six months. Among people who matched with these initial criteria, women-headed/child-headed households, PLW, disabled people &elderly people will be prioritized. The process in camps will be in a coordination with targeted camp managers and LC.Criteria &activity details will be shared with local authorities, BNFs & Non-BNFs to promote transparency and avoid any conflicts among them. MEAL team will conduct verification.Vouchers will be printed in Syria; each voucher will have a reference code assinged for each HH.Distribution will be conducted once per month for three months. The redemption will be done in the specified dates. Daily workers/monitors will monitor the process. Vendors/shops will be selected following vendor assessment based on their proximity to settlements, supply capacity, price of items and willingness to join activity. Contract explains both WHH and vendor’s responsibilities clearly will be jointly signed. The vendors will receive briefing on the activity details.Voucher distribution points will be agreed together with camp managers/LCs in a point where people can reach by walking. The distribution time can take 1-3 days depends on the number of BNFs. In case, there are people with special needs, the field staff can deliver the assistance to them. The field staff will post brochures and explain the details of the activities.Price of the items which will be purchased/redeemed ll be in $, will remain stable throughout the project. In case of a significant fluctuation in the price of items and the vendors request a change on the agreed prices, WHH will consider making price change following a new market assessment done by WHH in the area.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Since Welthungerhilfe has a wide range of NGO network, it can do either referral or receive referral from other peer partners. The proposed activity will not only enhance targeted group's dignity but also decrease their dependence on other community members and individuals for their well-being. This independence will strengthen their resilience and ability to adapt to other challenges such as health risks, unemployment and poor sanitation. There is scope for greater complementary between multisectoral voucher distribution activities and protection programming and services to maximize positive protection outcomes and reduce risks. The project will make sure through distributing multisectoral vouchers that beneficiaries can cover their basic needs based on their own preferences by contributing to more of sector-specific interventions which can also have positive results for children as well as IDPs at most vulnerable situation through its indirect impact on nutrition and health. In the activities, cultural sensitivities shall be considered; separate lines will be formed both for female and male BNFs and female staff shall be available as one of the contact persons in the field. Furthermore, inclusion of disabled people will be taking into account by bother selecting the distribution point as a disabled and elderly friendly space and delivering the assistance to their settlement in case they have difficulty to reach the distribution point.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumption(A)1:Security situation shall be stable.Risk(R)1:Military clashes may hinder activity;it may not be conducted for a while.Mitigation(M)1: WHH security staff constantly monitor project areas and inform staff. WHH staff is contacted and withdraw from targeted areas immediately if a potential security risk comes up.A2:WHH staff can do their job without any interference.R2:Threats to WHH staff.M2: In case of any possible threat situation, project team will be directed to carry out an immediate evacuation.Donors will be reported immediately on any occurred incidents.WHH will involve in AFAD and camp managers.A3:People received multisectoral vouchers shall be used based on specific needs of beneficiaries.R3:Vouchers may be exchanged for cash at a lower value.M3:contract with vendors will be terminated.WHH monitoring team shall be closely monitoring activities.A4: People can exchange MPV from the contracted vendors at agreed prices.R4: Vendors may try to charge more than the agreed list of prices; or manipulate the beneficiaries.M4:Different monitoring mechanisms shall be pursued;ghost buyers.Field team shall be closely monitoring prices,redemption process.A5:WHH staff work honestly during MPV distribution/redemption.R5:Staff shall be Corrupted.M5: We will be open with community about project.Use clearly verifiable beneficiary targeting criteria and check beneficiary list.Work closely with camp managers and sector managers.A6: Prices of goods remain stable during project period. R6:Prices of goods can rise due to inflation which will negatively impact both HHs and vendors.M6:WHH will have market assessment in catchment area based on the results.A7:Paper vouchers cannot be replicated.R7:Vouchers shall be replicated and redeemed during redemption process L(L), I(H), Risk Classification(L).M7:For each distribution, new vouchers shall be designed (differing color).As precaution, unreplicatable hologram shall be used during designing process.
