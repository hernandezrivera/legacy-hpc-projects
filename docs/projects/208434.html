Project Summary

Through the project WFP will provide multi-purpose cash transfer to 50 000 IDPs in the targeted regions of the NW and SW regions, in line with WFP Cameroon Country Strategic Plan (CSP 2022-2026). Providing cash assistance for food security and nutrition can promote additional dignity as compared with in-kind food assistance and can reduce the risks to exploitative labor, early marriage, and sale of food assistance to get cash for other lifesaving needs. The protection component can enable them to pay for transportation, reducing time exposure moving to resort to negative coping strategies including survival sex, child recruitment an in dangerous areas. Protection principles will be mainstreamed (non-discrimination, participation, meaningful access, risk reduction, and safety and dignity).

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The escalation of armed conflict between the Government of Cameroon and the non-state armed groups (NSAGs) in in the North-West and South-West Regions (NWSW), amplified humanitarian needs, triggering the displacement of over 1.3 million persons in the last 7 years. The eruption of armed conflict in 2017 resulted in the displacement of over 700,000 civilians and close to 600,000 refugees into Nigeria. Active armed clashes as well as serious criminal incidents such as kidnapping-for-ransom continued to prevail over the years resulting in the killing of belligerents, civilians, destruction of properties and in the displacement of members of local communities. In 2021-2022, 66% of schools in both regions were closed, causing the displacement of about 200,000 school-aged children, most of who remain out of school. In July 2023, 3,655 persons were displaced in the NW and SW due to violence, with Meme, Manyu and Fako divisions in the SW and Mezam and Bui divisions in the NW recording the highest number of displacements and causing frequent pendular displacements to nearby bushes and towns for safety and resulted in humanitarian needs.The majority of the IDPs fled the North-West and South-West regions in 2020 or before. Short-term, pendular movement continues within the two regions. Over 94 per cent of these displacements are related to violence and insecurity, driven by fear of attacks (38 per cent), military operations (29 per cent), and confrontations between armed groups (18 per cent). Often people move back and forth between their homes and their places of refuge.According to October 2023 Cadre Harmonisé realized by Government and partners in all the 10 regions, the NWSW regions have about 508,480 persons in phase 3 to 5 of the Integrated Food Security Phase Classification (IPC). 03 of the 07 divisions in NW and 05 of the 06 divisions in SW are under food insecurity crisis phase.
