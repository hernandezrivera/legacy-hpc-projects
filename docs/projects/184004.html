Project Summary

Through this project WV will adopt a more focused on supports integration of health, nutrition and at OTP and PHC facilities to reduce morbidity and mortality towards malnutrition. The existing social norms within any community shapes and impacts on parents’ practices on childcare, affecting children’s health and nutritional status. WV’s activities shall work on to influence the existing norms and encourage more child-friendly practices in health, nutrition sectors .. All children under 5 and pregnant and lactating women arriving at the outpatient consultations will be screened and referred for immunization ANC, and to the CMAM and IYCF programs . WV will treat 10,239 severely acute malnourished children,WVI will closely monitor the nutrition supplies and put more emphasis to prevent supplies leakages. Good hygiene practices will be promoted in Mother Support Group (MSGs) also, referral to health services will be made including CMAM, IYCF, ANC and vaccination .WVI will work through existing SMoH systems and structures, building their capacity as well as strengthening community networks and referrals between communities and health facilities.

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

World Vision will ensure that the project promotes safety and dignity of beneficiaries, particularly vulnerable groups such as women and girls. Responsibility of child care, feeding and maternity in the household is primarily done by women (mothers and grandmothers). The project will ensure that women have direct access to information and CMAM services at convenient hours with the targeted OTPs open from morning to afternoonWorld Vision and Pan Care will also take deliberate steps to seek the view of women and girls on the suitability of services and act on the feedback to ensure their needs are metHouseholds with people living with disabilities whenever identified, will be supported through the community and Nutrition care Group lead mothers to access services and information.CORPs and NCG lead mothers will encourage women candidates to participate so as to make the interaction with mothers culturally appropriate and minimize likelihood protection concerns

7.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will inform and interact with inter-agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including SEA and abuse complaints?

Suggestion boxes will be set up at health facilities ,Complaint and suggestion sheet to be filled out by the community members will be developed and placed at suggestion boxes. Assigned WV focal persons will be responsible for regularly opening the boxes. The local committee will read the complaint in front of the committee and transfer the complaint to the project focal person, who will either directly respond or transfer the complaint to a higher-level of management. The local committee will also ensure that the boxes and sheets are in place and are regularly opened by WV focal persons. WV focal persons are also responsible for submitting monthly accountability reports, to be shared with the Blue Nile Program & Khartoum Offices.Feedback and complaints are welcomed, and will be recorded (using a logbook or similar) and analysed, with action taken and feedback provided. WV will sensitize communities on their rights to services under this project, and our adherence to World Vision's Code of Conduct and the Code of Conduct for the Red Cross and NGOs in emergencies as well as WV Child Protection (safeguarding policies). Feedback mechanisms will use the local language, Arabic, so it is contextually appropriate and well-understood by the intended beneficiaries. WV will also ensure that the format to share information considers education and literacy levels, and will be easy and quick to read. In addition, the project will adopt some of UNICEF AAP indicators. The project considers traditions and other factors that hinder participation of certain community groups and that procedures/system exist to ensure free and open discussion- Documentation of Evidence of community participation in decision making throughout the project cycle- The project is designed based on consultation with community members represented by different groups; women, men, girls, boys and different age groups and abilities- The project maintains information system on beneficiaries disaggregated by age, gender and disability- Feedback and complaints mechanisms are tailored to the operational context, visible, known and accessible to all individuals (including children and adolescents) and groups in the community- Documentation of evidence of responses to complaints following the system specified steps
