Project Summary

Analysis by tribal Districts shows that Orakzai had the worst food consumption situation, where 34% of the surveyed households had poor and 31% had borderline consumption. The proposed project will improve food availability to the most vulnerable returned families including widows, orphans, Older People and PWDs in tribal district Orakzai. HOPE will provide critical agriculture return package (agriculture inputs and Tool kits) to 5000 families. Germination tests will be conducted and only those certified seed will be distributed among farmer which has more than 82 % result.
The project will organize well planned 170 capacity building training sessions for the 5000 beneficiaries of the project. The themes of the training will consist of practical demonstrations in the fields, establishment of demo plots, farmer field school, farmer business school, field days, workshops, seminars and exposure visits etc.
While conducting these activities in Tribal District Orakzai, HOPE will keep close coordination with all Implementing Partners to avoid duplications and overlapping.

Needs

The Food Consumption Score (FCS) is a composite score based on dietary frequency, food frequency and relative nutrition importance of different food groups. The FATA In-depth Food Security and Livelihood Assessment conducted in February/March 2017 revealed that 64% of households had acceptable food consumption, 29% had borderline consumption and remaining 6% had poor consumption. Analysis by tribal Districts shows that Orakzai had the worst food consumption situation, where 34% of the surveyed households had poor and 31% had borderline consumption. The situation was better in Khyber, Kurram and South Waziristan, where no household were in the poor category and 13%, 17% and 19%, respectively, had borderline consumption. Livelihood of the people is badly affected at the course of displacement. People have lost their livelihood means when they had to flee to other areas in KPK as a result of the military operation against the militants. Agriculture tools were either sold out or lost during displacement and upon return, they are not in a position to restart agricultural activities and other means of livelihoods Because of no purchase power to purchase agriculture tools and seed /fertilizer to prepare their land for the upcoming crop seasons. The livelihood of the residents of Orakzai is totally dependent on agriculture and livestock but during the displacement IDPs lost most of their livestock which was the main source of their livelihoods. Animals were seen in the area and they were wild, infected by different diseases. Agriculture land is totally barren due to non-cultivation for past six years. Orchards are completely ruined in the area. Following the main needs of the peoples living in the Tribal District Orakzai: -a) Preparations agricultural livelihoods for the upcoming season. b) Provision of agricultural toolkits c) Livestock restocking d) Livestock medicines including de-wormers, antibiotics, external parasitizes, mobile veterinary clinic and rehabilitation of veterinary centers e) Rehabilitation of de-siltation of katcha (non-cemented) and Pakka (cemented) irrigation channels f) Provision of fruit trees and nurseries.

Activities

Distribution of Agriculture Package (inputs and tool kits): Prior to the distribution of critical agriculture return package (agriculture inputs and Tool kits to 5000 families), beneficiaries will be identified by involvement of Village Development Committees (VDC). VDCs members will be oriented on the assessment tools. During the orientation session, vulnerable beneficiaries’ criteria will be developed with consultation with VDCs members. The project agriculture package and tool kits will only be provided after verification of 5000 selected beneficiaries according to criteria. The project Social Mobilizers Team (SMT) will verify selected beneficiaries according to the set criteria. Prior to the distribution of agriculture inputs, germination tests will be conducted and only those certified seed will be distributed among farmer which has more than 82 % result. Capacity Building: The project will organize well planned 170 capacity building training sessions for the 5000 beneficiaries of the project. It will include trainings like one- day orientation sessions on assessment tools, one -day training workshop at the time of distributions of all inputs, technical trainings along with practical demonstrations in the fields, establishment of demo plots, farmer field school, farmer business school, field days, workshops, seminars and exposure visits etc. Capacity building sessions on various topic will enhance knowledge and skill of the farmer and will empower male and female farmers with for the adoption of modern crop production, post-harvest losses management/handling technologies, water management, harvesting, nursery raising and orchard management, marketing and efficient use of irrigation water. The capacity sessions will ultimately benefit the small farmers and other stakeholders in adopting modern agriculture practices which will result good quality more production. While conducting these activities in Tribal District Orakzai, HOPE will keep close coordination with all Implementing Partners to avoid duplications and overlapping.
